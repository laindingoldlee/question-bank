Use vectors with origin $O$, and let $r$ be the radius, so $|A|=|B|=|M|=r$. Let $P$, $Q$, $R$, $T$ be the midpoints of $OA$, $MB$, $OB$, $MA$:
\[
P=\dfrac{A}{2},\quad Q=\dfrac{M+B}{2},\quad R=\dfrac{B}{2},\quad T=\dfrac{M+A}{2}.
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s=0.2){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
real r=3;
pair O=(0,0),A=r*dir(0),B=r*dir(60),M=r*dir(22);
pair P=A/2,Q=(M+B)/2,R=B/2,T=(M+A)/2;
draw(O--A,linewidth(0.9)); draw(O--B,linewidth(0.9)); draw(arc(O,r,0,60),linewidth(0.9));
draw(M--A,gray); draw(M--B,gray);
pair X=extension(P,Q,R,T);
draw(P--Q,red); draw(R--T,blue);
draw(ra(X,Q-X,T-X,0.16));
dot(O);dot(A);dot(B);dot(M);dot(P);dot(Q);dot(R);dot(T);
label("$O$",O,SW); label("$A$",A,SE); label("$B$",B,N); label("$M$",M,E);
label("$P$",P,S); label("$Q$",Q,dir(190)); label("$R$",R,NW); label("$T$",T,dir(225));
draw(arc(O,0.6,0,60)); label("$60^\circ$",0.85*dir(30),fontsize(8pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Then
\[
Q-P=\dfrac{M+(B-A)}{2},\qquad T-R=\dfrac{M-(B-A)}{2},
\]
so
\[
(Q-P)\cdot(T-R)=\dfrac{|M|^2-|B-A|^2}{4}=\dfrac{r^2-AB^2}{4}.
\]
Triangle $OAB$ has $OA=OB$ and $\angle AOB=60^\circ$, so it is equilateral and $AB=r$. Hence the dot product is $0$.

The two vectors are nonzero: $Q=P$ would need $M=A-B$ and $T=R$ would need $M=B-A$, but these two points of the circle make angles of $60^\circ$ with $OA$, respectively $OB$, on the far side from $B$, respectively $A$, so neither lies on the arc $AB$. Therefore $\boxed{PQ\perp RT}$. $\blacksquare$
