Five congruent spheres of radius $r$ form a square pyramid resting on a horizontal table: the four base spheres lie on the table with their centres at the corners of a square, each tangent to its two neighbours, and the fifth sphere sits on top, tangent to all four base spheres. Find the height of the top of the pyramid (the highest point of the top sphere) above the table, in terms of $r$.
