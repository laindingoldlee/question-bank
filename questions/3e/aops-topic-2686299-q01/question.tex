Positive integers $x$ and $y$ have greatest common factor $27$, and $2y$ is divisible by $x$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] If $x$ is not a multiple of $y$, what is the smallest possible value of $x$?
\item[(b)] If instead $y$ is not a multiple of $x$, what is the smallest possible value of $x$?
\end{enumerate}
