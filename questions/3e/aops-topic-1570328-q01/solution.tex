First $(a+b)(b+c)=ab+ac+b^2+bc$. Multiplying by $c+a$:
\[
\begin{aligned}
(ab+ac+b^2+bc)(c+a)&=abc+a^2b+ac^2+a^2c+b^2c+ab^2+bc^2+abc\\
&=\boxed{a^2b+a^2c+ab^2+b^2c+ac^2+bc^2+2abc}.
\end{aligned}
\]
A useful compact form is $(a+b)(b+c)(c+a)=(a+b+c)(ab+bc+ca)-abc$.
