Let the edge be $s$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair o=(0.45,0.32);
pair p(real x,real y,real z){return (x,z)+y*o;}
draw(p(0,0,0)--p(1,0,0)--p(1,0,1)--p(0,0,1)--cycle,linewidth(1));
draw(p(1,0,0)--p(1,1,0)--p(1,1,1)--p(1,0,1),linewidth(1));
draw(p(0,0,1)--p(0,1,1)--p(1,1,1),linewidth(1));
draw(p(0,0,0)--p(0,1,0)--p(1,1,0),dashed);draw(p(0,1,0)--p(0,1,1),dashed);
label("$s$",p(0.5,0,0),S);label("$s$",p(1,0.5,0),SE);label("$s$",p(0,0,0.5),W);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The cube has $6$ square faces, so its surface area is $6s^2$, and its volume is $s^3$. Then $6s^2=s^3$ with $s>0$ gives $s=6$, and the volume is $6^3=\boxed{216}$ (and the surface area is $6\cdot36=216$ as well).
