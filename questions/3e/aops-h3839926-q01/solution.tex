For $k$ digits in both bases, the intersection of the two integer intervals is $10^{k-1}\le n\le 5^k-1$. It is nonempty exactly when $2^{k-1}<5$, giving $k=1,2,3$. The respective intervals are $[1,4]$, $[10,24]$, and $[100,124]$, with $4,15,25$ integers. Thus the answer is $\boxed{44}$. If zero is admitted and written as one digit, the count would be $45$.
