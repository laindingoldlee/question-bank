The two-digit numbers beginning with $8$ are $80,\dots,89$. Since $81=9\cdot9$, the one congruent to $4$ modulo $9$ is $85$. So Tim has $85$, Jim has $87$ and Kim has $87+9=\boxed{96}$ apples.
