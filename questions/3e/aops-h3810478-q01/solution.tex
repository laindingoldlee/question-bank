The equation implies $a<b$ and $a<\dfrac{2026}{3}$. Rearranging gives
\[
(2026-3a)(2026+3b)=2026^2=4\cdot1013^2.
\]
Put $u=2026-3a$. Then $0<u<2026$ and $u\equiv1\pmod3$. The positive divisors of $4\cdot1013^2$ below $2026$ are $1,2,4,1013$, of which only $1,4$ are $1$ modulo $3$.
For $u=1$, we get $a=675,b=1367550$; for $u=4$, $a=674,b=341381$. Both satisfy the original equation, so
\[
\boxed{(a,b)=(675,1367550),(674,341381)}.
\]
