If the expression contains $k$ positive-integer blocks, each block ends in 2, so the sum is congruent to $2k$ modulo 10. Thus divisibility by 10 requires $k$ to be a multiple of 5. Since $1\le k\le9$, this means $k=5$. Every block is congruent modulo 3 to its digit sum, so every resulting total is congruent to 18, hence 0, modulo 3. Therefore exactly the placements making five blocks work. Choose their four separating gaps in $\boxed{\binom84=70}$ ways.
