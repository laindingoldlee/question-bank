Mr.\ Cook bought a box of Halloween candies. He ate half of the candies before the first child came and took her share. He ate half of what was left before the second child came and took her share, and half of what was left again before the third child came and took all of the remaining candies. Each child received the same number of candies. Which of the following statements is certainly true about the number of candies Mr.\ Cook bought?

$\textbf{(A)}$ It is a multiple of $3$\qquad $\textbf{(B)}$ It is a multiple of $4$\qquad $\textbf{(C)}$ It is a multiple of $6$\qquad $\textbf{(D)}$ It is a multiple of $7$\qquad $\textbf{(E)}$ It is a multiple of $11$
