Let $P_N=\displaystyle\prod_{n=1}^{N}i^n=i^1\cdot i^2\cdots i^N$, where $i^2=-1$. Find all the values taken by $P_N$ and decide whether the infinite product $\displaystyle\prod_{n=1}^{\infty}i^n$ converges to a finite value.
