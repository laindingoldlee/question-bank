Every right-hand side is a multiple of $3$: $36=3\cdot12$, $39=3\cdot13$, $45=3\cdot15$, $48=3\cdot16$, $33=3\cdot11$. And $12,13,15,16,11$ are the sums of all the digits on the left:
\[3+4+5=12,\quad5+6+2=13,\quad2+7+6=15,\quad4+7+5=16,\quad2+8+1=11.\]
So the rule is ``$\overline{ab}+c$ means $3(a+b+c)$'', which fits all five lines. Then
\[41+5=3(4+1+5)=\boxed{30}.\]
