Try rational roots. At $x=4$: $2048-4864+3712-1072+224-48=0$, so $x-4$ is a factor; dividing gives $2x^4-11x^3+14x^2-11x+12$, which also vanishes at $x=4$ ($512-704+224-44+12=0$), leaving $2x^3-3x^2+2x-3=x^2(2x-3)+(2x-3)=(2x-3)\left(x^2+1\right)$. Hence
\[2x^5-19x^4+58x^3-67x^2+56x-48=\boxed{(x-4)^2(2x-3)\left(x^2+1\right)}.\]
