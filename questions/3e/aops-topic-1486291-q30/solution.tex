Number the vertical grid lines $0,1,\dots,2003$ and the horizontal ones $0,1,2$. A rectangle is determined by $2$ of the $2004$ vertical lines and $2$ of the $3$ horizontal lines, so there are $\dbinom{2004}{2}\cdot3$ rectangles.

The shaded squares form column $1002$, between vertical lines $1001$ and $1002$, and every row has a shaded square in it. So a rectangle contains a shaded square exactly when its left side is on one of the lines $0,\dots,1001$ and its right side on one of the lines $1002,\dots,2003$: $1002\cdot1002$ choices of vertical sides, with any choice of horizontal sides. Hence
\[
P(\text{contains a shaded square})=\dfrac{1002^2\cdot3}{\dbinom{2004}{2}\cdot3}=\dfrac{1002^2}{1002\cdot2003}=\dfrac{1002}{2003},
\]
and the probability that the rectangle contains no shaded square is
\[
1-\dfrac{1002}{2003}=\boxed{\dfrac{1001}{2003}} .
\]
The poster's $\dfrac{1002}{2003}$ is the probability of the opposite event.
