Let the compass cost $c$ dollars. Then the watch costs $c+100$, and $c+(c+100)=110$, so $2c=10$ and $c=\boxed{5}$ dollars. (The tempting answer $10$ is wrong: then the watch would cost $110$ and the total would be $120$.)
