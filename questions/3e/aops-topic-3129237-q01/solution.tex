In any two consecutive days the total is at most $9$. Thus $22$ days yield at most $99$, and fewer days cannot do better. In $23$ days, start with alternating $5,4,5,4,\ldots,5$, which totals $104$. Change two nonadjacent $5$ entries to $3$; each remains between $4$ entries, so the consecutive-day restriction still holds, and the total becomes $100$. Therefore the least is $\boxed{23}$.
