If $2013=a+(a+1)+\cdots+(a+k-1)$ with $a\ge1$, then
\[2013=\dfrac{k(2a+k-1)}{2},\qquad k(2a+k-1)=4026=2\cdot3\cdot11\cdot61.\]
The factors $k$ and $2a+k-1$ have opposite parity (their difference $2a-1$ is odd), and $2a+k-1>k$, so $k<\sqrt{4026}<64$. The divisors of $4026$ below $64$ are $1,2,3,6,11,22,33,61$; the largest is $k=61$, with cofactor $66$ of opposite parity, giving $2a+60=66$, $a=3$. Indeed
\[3+4+\cdots+63=\dfrac{61\cdot66}{2}=2013.\]
So the largest $k$ is $\boxed{61}$.
