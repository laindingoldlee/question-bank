Let $e_1=a+b+c$, $e_2=ab+bc+ca$ and $e_3=abc$. We have $e_1=\dfrac{3}{2}$, and
\[
e_2=\dfrac{(a+b+c)^2-(a^2+b^2+c^2)}{2}=\dfrac{\dfrac{9}{4}-\dfrac{3}{2}}{2}=\dfrac{3}{8} .
\]
The identity
\[
a^3+b^3+c^3-3abc=(a+b+c)\left(a^2+b^2+c^2-ab-bc-ca\right)
\]
gives
\[
\dfrac{3}{2}-3e_3=\dfrac{3}{2}\left(\dfrac{3}{2}-\dfrac{3}{8}\right)=\dfrac{3}{2}\cdot\dfrac{9}{8}=\dfrac{27}{16},
\]
so $3e_3=\dfrac{24}{16}-\dfrac{27}{16}=-\dfrac{3}{16}$ and $abc=\boxed{-\dfrac{1}{16}}$.

Such numbers exist: they are the roots of $t^3-\dfrac{3}{2}t^2+\dfrac{3}{8}t+\dfrac{1}{16}=0$, i.e. of $16t^3-24t^2+6t+1=0$, which has three real roots.
