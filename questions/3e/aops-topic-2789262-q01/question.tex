Let $ABC$ be a triangle with altitudes $AD$ and $BE$. Let $P$ and $Q$ be the reflections of $D$ over $A$ and of $E$ over $B$. Line $\ell$ through $P$ is parallel to $BC$ and line $m$ through $Q$ is parallel to $AC$; they meet at $O$. Find $\dfrac{[OAB]}{[ABC]}$.
