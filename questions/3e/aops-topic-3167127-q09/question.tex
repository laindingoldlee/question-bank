A frog starts at pad $1$ and chooses uniformly among forward jumps of one or two pads that do not go past pad $7$. Landing on pad $2$ or $5$ counts as a death and immediately restarts it at pad $1$. What is the expected number of deaths before first reaching pad $7$?
