Let $C$ be a point on a circle with diameter $AB$. The line through $B$ perpendicular to $BC$ meets the bisector of $\angle ACB$ at $D$. Let $E$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $B$ to $AD$, and let $M$ be the midpoint of $CD$. Prove that $B$, $D$, $E$ and $M$ are concyclic.
