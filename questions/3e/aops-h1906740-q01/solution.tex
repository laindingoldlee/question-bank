Choose $A=(0,0),B=(b,0),D=(0,d)$ with $b,d>0$. Convexity places $C$ in the first quadrant. Its distances from the horizontal and vertical axes are its ordinate and abscissa, respectively. The hypotheses therefore give $C=(d,b)$.

The diagonal direction vectors are $AC=(d,b)$ and $BD=(-b,d)$. Their dot product is $-bd+bd=0$, so $\boxed{AC\perp BD}$.

\end{asy}
