Adding $6y$ to both sides gives $\boxed{x=6y+4}$. Every choice of $y$ gives a solution; for example
\[
y=0:\ (x,y)=(4,0),\qquad y=1:\ (10,1),\qquad y=-1:\ (-2,-1).
\]
(Check: $4-0=4$, $10-6=4$, $-2+6=4$.)
