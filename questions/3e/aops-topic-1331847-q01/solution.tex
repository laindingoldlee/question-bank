If you draw $t$ TREAT cards and $8-t$ TRICK cards, the order does not matter and you finish with $28.16\left(\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^t\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{8-t}=28.16\cdot\dfrac{3^t}{2^8}=0.11\cdot3^t$ dollars. Since only $5$ TRICK cards exist, $t\ge3$, and $P(t)=\dfrac{\binom7t\binom{5}{8-t}}{\binom{12}{8}}$ with $\dbinom{12}{8}=495$:
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
t&3&4&5&6&7\\\hline
\binom7t\binom5{8-t}&35&175&210&70&5
\end{array}
\]
The expected value is
\[
\dfrac{0.11}{495}\left(35\cdot27+175\cdot81+210\cdot243+70\cdot729+5\cdot2187\right)=\dfrac{0.11\cdot128115}{495}=\boxed{28.47\text{ dollars}}.
\]
