A pile of $12$ face-down cards has $7$ marked TREAT and $5$ marked TRICK. Drawing a TRICK card halves your money, and drawing a TREAT card increases your money by $50$ percent. You draw $8$ cards at random without replacement, starting with $28.16$ dollars. What is the expected amount of money you have at the end?
