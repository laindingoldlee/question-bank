Let $O$ be a point, and let $\ell$ be a ray starting at $O$. For each positive integer $n$, point $P_n$ is chosen above $\ell$ so that the foot of the perpendicular from $P_n$ to $\ell$ is $n$ units from $O$ along $\ell$.

If the perpendicular distance from $P_n$ to $\ell$ is

\[
\begin{cases}
1, & n\text{ is odd},\\
2\sqrt3, & n\text{ is even},
\end{cases}
\]find the degree measure of

\[
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\angle P_nOP_{n+2}.
\]
