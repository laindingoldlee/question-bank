Take the ray as the positive horizontal axis. Within each parity class, the heights are constant and the angles from the ray strictly decrease. Thus the angle between $OP_n$ and $OP_{n+2}$ is the difference of their inclinations.

The odd-indexed sum telescopes to $\arctan(\dfrac{1}{1})=45^\circ$, since the final inclination tends to zero. The even-indexed sum telescopes to $\arctan(2\dfrac{\sqrt 3}{2})=60^\circ$. All terms are positive, so these two convergent sums can be added. The answer is $\boxed{105^\circ}$.

\end{asy}
