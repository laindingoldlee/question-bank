The vowels are O, O, E, and the consonants are C, T, B, R. The first letter must be one of the $4$ consonants. The remaining $6$ letters, including both O's, can be arranged in $\dfrac{6!}{2!}=360$ ways. So there are $4\cdot360=\boxed{1440}$ arrangements.
