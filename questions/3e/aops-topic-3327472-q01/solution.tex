The smallest possible remaining mean is $\dfrac{n}{2}$, obtained by deleting $n$, and the largest is $\dfrac{n+2}{2}$, obtained by deleting $1$. Thus $\dfrac{n}{2}\le22\le\dfrac{n+2}{2}$, giving $42\le n\le44$. Since $n$ is odd, $n=43$. The original sum is $\dfrac{43\cdot44}{2}=946$, while the remaining sum is $42\cdot22=924$. Hence $k=22$ and $\boxed{n+k=65}$.
