A song has chart peaks grouped by country as follows: Australia $51$; Canada $41,2$; Costa Rica $8$; Czech Republic $4,1$; Germany $18$; Ireland $70$; Latvia $18$; New Zealand $9$; UK $28$; US $62,26,13,10,1,1$. (a) How many charts peaked at $1$? (b) Which country has the smallest mean peak? (c) Average all listed chart peaks from countries whose first letter has odd position in the English alphabet, counting each chart separately.
