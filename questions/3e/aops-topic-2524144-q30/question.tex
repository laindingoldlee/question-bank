A user befriends $5$ of the $N$ users who are online. Later, $100$ different online users, chosen at random, post in a thread, and exactly $2$ of them are the user's new friends. For which value of $N$ is the expected number of friends among $100$ randomly chosen online users equal to the observed number $2$? (This is the usual ``capture-recapture'' estimate of $N$.)
