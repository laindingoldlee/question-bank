Let the smallest be $s$; the largest is $2s+5$, and $(2s+5)-s=35$ gives $s=30$ and the largest $65$. The sum is $3\cdot47=141$, so the middle number is $141-95=46$. The numbers are $\boxed{30,46,65}$.
