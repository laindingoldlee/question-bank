The least common multiple increases exactly when $n$ is a prime power. Indeed a prime power introduces a new exponent; if $n$ has at least two distinct prime factors, each of its maximal prime-power factors is smaller than $n$ and already represented. The even prime powers at most 1000 are $2,4,\ldots,512$. Their sum is $2^{10}-2=\boxed{1022}$.
