Vieta gives $r+s+t=-8$. Thus the new roots are $-8-r,-8-s,-8-t$. If $P(x)=x^3+8x^2+5x+3$, their monic polynomial is $-P(-8-x)$. Expanding gives $x^3+16x^2+69x+37$. Therefore $A+B+C=122$ and the answer is $\boxed{14884}$.
