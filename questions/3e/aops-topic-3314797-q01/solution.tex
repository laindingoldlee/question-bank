Ignoring caps, stars and bars counts nonnegative solutions in seven variables. Inclusion--exclusion subtracts violations of the four upper bounds 6 and three upper bounds 10. If $i$ women and $j$ men violate their caps, subtract 7 or 11 from their contributions, leaving total $20-7i-11j$. Thus the answer is\[\sum_{\substack{0\le i\le4,\ 0\le j\le3\\7i+11j\le20}}(-1)^{i+j}\binom4i\binom3j\binom{26-7i-11j}{6}=\boxed{112567}.\]The terms are $230230-108528+5544-15015+336$, which sum to 112567.
