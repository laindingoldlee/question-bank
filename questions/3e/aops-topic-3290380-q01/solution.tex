As $\operatorname{Log}i=\dfrac{\pi i}{2}$, we have $i^i=\exp(i\operatorname{Log}i)=\boxed{e^{-\dfrac{\pi}{2}}}\approx0.208$, a positive real number. Other logarithm branches give $e^{-\dfrac{\pi}{2}-2\pi k}$ for integers $k$, so the principal convention matters.
