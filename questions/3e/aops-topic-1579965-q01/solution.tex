The point $(x,y)$ is uniform in the $1\times2$ rectangle, of area $2$. The points with $x^2+y^2\le1$ form a quarter of the unit disc, of area $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$, which lies inside the rectangle. So
\[
P(x^2+y^2>1)=\dfrac{2-\dfrac{\pi}{4}}{2}=\boxed{1-\dfrac{\pi}{8}}\approx0.607 .
\]
