The sum $|x+2|+|x+4|+|x+5|$ is the total distance from $x$ to the three points $-5,-4,-2$ on the number line.

The distance to the two outer points satisfies $|x+5|+|x+2|\ge(-2)-(-5)=3$, with equality exactly when $-5\le x\le-2$ (triangle inequality). The middle term satisfies $|x+4|\ge0$, with equality exactly when $x=-4$. Both equalities hold at $x=-4$, which lies in $[-5,-2]$, so the minimum is
\[
|{-4}+2|+0+|{-4}+5|=2+0+1=\boxed{3}.
\]
In general, a sum of distances to several points is smallest at the median of the points; here the median is $-4$.
