Place $ B=(0,0),A=(3,0),C=(0,4)$. The midpoint is $ D=(\dfrac{3}{2},2)$. Therefore $ BD=\sqrt{\dfrac{9}{4}+4}=\dfrac{5}{2}$. Since $ D $ is the midpoint of the length 5 hypotenuse, $ AD=CD=\dfrac{5}{2}$ as well. Thus $\boxed{AD=BD=CD=\dfrac{5}{2}}$.
