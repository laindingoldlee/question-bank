If $4$ is not chosen, we choose a multiset of size $4$ from $\{1,2,3\}$: by stars and bars there are $\dbinom{4+2}{2}=15$ ways. If $4$ is chosen once, the other $3$ numbers form a multiset of size $3$ from $\{1,2,3\}$: $\dbinom{3+2}{2}=10$ ways. In total $15+10=\boxed{25}$ selections.
