Set $t=2^{111x}>0$. Multiplying the equation by $4$ gives
\[
t^3-8t^2+16t-4=0.
\]
This cubic has three positive roots: its signs change on $(0,1)$, $(2,3)$, and $(4,5)$. By Vieta their product is $4$. Since $x=\dfrac{\log_2t}{111}$, the sum of the original roots is
\[
\dfrac{\log_2(t_1t_2t_3)}{111}=\dfrac{2}{111}.
\]
Thus $\boxed{m+n=113}$.
