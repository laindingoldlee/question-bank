A father's childhood lasted one sixth of his life. After a further one eighth of his life he married and immediately left for the army. After another one twelfth of his life he returned from the army, and $5$ years later his son was born. The son lived half as many years as his father lived, and died $4$ years before his father. How many years did the father live, and how old was he when his son was born?
