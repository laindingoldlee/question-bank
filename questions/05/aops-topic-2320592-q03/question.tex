A pianist, a cellist, a mathematician and a flutist are on an island and must all cross to a nearby island in a boat that holds at most two people. The boat cannot cross empty, and nobody can swim across. Three pairs must never be in the boat together: the cellist and the flutist (the cellist would throw the flute overboard), the flutist and the pianist (the flutist would throw the pianist into the sea), and the pianist and the mathematician (the mathematician would fall off the boat). On land there is no trouble. How can they all get across, and what is the smallest number of crossings needed?
