In triangle $ABC$, $AB=1$ and $AC=2$. Side $BC$ and the median from $A$ to $BC$ have the same length. What is $BC$?

$\textbf{(A) }\dfrac{1+\sqrt2}{2}\qquad\textbf{(B) }\dfrac{1+\sqrt3}{2}\qquad\textbf{(C) }\sqrt2\qquad\textbf{(D) }\dfrac{3}{2}\qquad\textbf{(E) }\sqrt3$
