Multiples of $4$ from $8$ to $256$: $64-1=63$ numbers. Odd squares plus $6$ in the range: $s^2+6$ with $s=3,5,\dots,15$, giving $15,31,55,87,127,175,231$: $7$ numbers, all odd, so none is a multiple of $4$. Thus $N=70$ and $N^2=\boxed{4900}$.
