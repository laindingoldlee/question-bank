A linear function has the form $f(x)=mx+c$. From $f(0)=7$ we get $c=7$, and $f(2)=2m+7=17$ gives $m=5$. So $f(x)=5x+7$, and
\[
\sum_{x=0}^{127}(5x+7)=5\cdot\dfrac{127\cdot128}{2}+7\cdot128=40640+896=\boxed{41536}.
\]
