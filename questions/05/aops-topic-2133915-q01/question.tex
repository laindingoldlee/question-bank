Let $ABCD$ be a rectangle, and write $\mathbf u=\overrightarrow{AB}$ and $\mathbf w=\overrightarrow{AD}$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that a point $P$ lies inside or on the boundary of the rectangle if and only if
\[0\le\overrightarrow{AP}\cdot\mathbf u\le\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf u\qquad\text{and}\qquad0\le\overrightarrow{AP}\cdot\mathbf w\le\mathbf w\cdot\mathbf w,\]
where $\cdot$ is the dot product. (This test works even when the sides are not parallel to the axes.)
\item[(b)] The points $A(0,0)$, $B(4,2)$, $C(1,8)$, $D(-3,6)$ are the vertices of a rectangle. Use (a) to decide whether $P(1,4)$ and $Q(4,4)$ lie inside it.
\end{enumerate}
