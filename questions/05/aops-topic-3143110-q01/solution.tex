The smallest factor is at most $\sqrt[3]{36}<4$, hence 2 or 3. Listing factor pairs of 18 or 12 gives the unordered triples $(2,2,9),(2,3,6),(3,3,4)$. They yield $3+6+3=\boxed{12}$ ordered triples.
