By the sum-to-product formula,
\[\sin3x+\sin x=2\sin\dfrac{3x+x}{2}\cos\dfrac{3x-x}{2}=2\sin2x\cos x.\]
So we need $\sin2x=0$ or $\cos x=0$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $\sin2x=0$ means $x=\dfrac{k\pi}{2}$; in the interval this gives $x=-\dfrac\pi2,0,\dfrac\pi2$.
\item $\cos x=0$ gives $x=\pm\dfrac\pi2$ in the interval.
\end{itemize}
The solutions are $\boxed{x=-\dfrac{\pi}{2},\ 0,\ \dfrac{\pi}{2}}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
real f(real x){return sin(3x)+sin(x);}
draw((-1.9,0)--(1.95,0),Arrow(5)); draw((0,-1.75)--(0,1.85),Arrow(5));
label("$x$",(1.95,0),E); label("$y$",(0,1.85),N);
draw(graph(f,-pi/2,pi/2,200),blue+linewidth(0.9));
dot((-pi/2,0),red); dot((0,0),red); dot((pi/2,0),red);
label("$-\dfrac{\pi}{2}$",(-pi/2,0),NW,fontsize(8pt)); label("$\dfrac{\pi}{2}$",(pi/2,0),SE,fontsize(8pt));
label("$0$",(0,0),SW,fontsize(8pt));
for(real yy=-1;yy<=1;yy+=2){draw((-0.05,yy)--(0.05,yy)); label("$"+string(yy)+"$",(0.05,yy),E,fontsize(7pt));}
label("$y=\sin3x+\sin x$",(1.0,-0.75),blue+fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}
