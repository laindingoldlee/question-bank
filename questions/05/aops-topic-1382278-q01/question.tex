Find the circumradius of triangle $JKL$ with $JK=JL=25$ and $KL=40$.

A student lets $M$ be the midpoint of $KL$, notes that the circumcentre $O$ lies on line $JM$, and solves $R^2=(15-R)^2+20^2$, getting $R=\dfrac{125}{6}$. But then $15-R$ is negative. Is $R=\dfrac{125}{6}$ correct, and what does the negative value mean?
