Three congruent circles $A,B,C$ are mutually externally tangent and internally tangent to a unit circle. A circle $D$ fills their central gap. The circle $E$, distinct from $C$, lies in the gap bounded by $A,B,D$ and is tangent to all three. Its radius is $\dfrac{a\sqrt b-c}{d}$ in lowest radical form with positive integers and squarefree $b$. Find $a+b+c+d$.
