The hikes form an arithmetic sequence with first term $5$ and common difference $\dfrac14$. After $n$ hikes the total distance is
\[5n+\dfrac14\bigl(0+1+\cdots+(n-1)\bigr)=5n+\dfrac{n(n-1)}{8}\]
kilometers. We need the least $n$ with $5n+\dfrac{n(n-1)}{8}>1000$, that is, $40n+n^2-n>8000$, or
\[n(n+39)>8000.\]
The left side increases with $n$. Since $n^2\approx8000-39n$ suggests $n$ a little above $70$, test: $n=72$ gives $72\cdot111=7992<8000$, and $n=73$ gives $73\cdot112=8176>8000$. So the total first exceeds $1000$ kilometers on the $\boxed{73}$rd hike, and he walks down $73$ roads.
