In the tribe of Zimmer, a boy becomes a man only after a sequence of hikes. The first hike is $5$ kilometers long, and each following hike goes down a different road and is a quarter of a kilometer longer than the previous one (so the second hike is $5\dfrac14$ kilometers). The rite of passage is completed at the end of the hike during which the total distance walked on all his hikes first exceeds $1000$ kilometers. How many roads must a man walk down before he is called a man?
