Since $AB\parallel CD$, triangles $ABX$ and $CDX$ are similar with ratio $AB:CD=20:30=2:3$. So $AX:XC=BX:XD=2:3$.

Let $[ABX]=4k$. Then:
\begin{itemize}
\item $[CDX]=\left(\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^2\cdot4k=9k$ (similar triangles);
\item $[BXC]=\dfrac{3}{2}[ABX]=6k$, since triangles $ABX$ and $CBX$ share the height from $B$ to line $AC$ and $XC=\dfrac{3}{2}AX$;
\item likewise $[AXD]=6k$.
\end{itemize}
The whole trapezoid has area $4k+6k+6k+9k=25k=300$, so $k=12$ and
\[
[BXC]=6k=\boxed{72}.
\]

usepackage("amsmath");

pair X=extension(A,C,B,D);
fill(B--X--C--cycle,rgb(0.85,0.9,1));
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(A--C^^B--D);
dot(X);
label("$A$",A,NW);label("$B$",B,NE);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,SW);label("$X$",X,1.4*N);
label("$20$",(A+B)/2,N);label("$30$",(C+D)/2,S);
label("$48$",(A+B+X)/3);label("$108$",(C+D+X)/3);label("$72$",(B+C+X)/3);label("$72$",(A+D+X)/3);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

(The four triangles have areas $48$, $72$, $108$ and $72$; the shaded one is $BXC$.)
