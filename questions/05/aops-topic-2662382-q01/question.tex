Six people, numbered $1$ through $6$, sit in a row of six chairs. Each person must sit next to at least one person whose number has the opposite parity (odd next to even, even next to odd). All seatings satisfying this condition are equally likely. The probability that persons $1$ and $2$ sit next to each other is $\dfrac mn$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $(m+6)(n+8)$.
