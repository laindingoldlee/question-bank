During one day, $20$ cars drive past Jake's house, one at a time. When a driver smells Jake's burnt donuts, he or she independently drives on with probability $50\%$, turns back with probability $30\%$, or stops and parks in Jake's driveway with probability $20\%$. However, once there are $5$ or more cars parked in the driveway, each later driver drives on with probability $65\%$, turns back with probability $25\%$, or stops with probability $10\%$. The driveway is empty at the start of the day, and cars that stop stay for the rest of the day. What is the probability that exactly $12$ cars stop and exactly $5$ cars turn back? Give the exact value and a decimal approximation.
