For an integer $k\ge2$, let $N_k$ be the smallest positive integer $n$ that leaves remainder $y-1$ when divided by $y$, for every $y=2,3,\dots,k$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $N_k=\operatorname{lcm}(2,3,\dots,k)-1$.
\item[(b)] Find $N_k$ for $k=2,3,\dots,10$.
\item[(c)] A student conjectures that all these numbers are prime. Is the conjecture true?
\end{enumerate}
