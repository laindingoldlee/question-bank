Partition the exterior according to the nearest face, edge, or vertex. The six face prisms contribute the surface area $ 2(6+6+4)=32 $. Each edge contributes a quarter-cylinder of radius 1; total edge length is $ 4(3+2+2)=28 $, giving $ 7\pi $. Eight vertex octants form one unit ball, contributing $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$. These regions overlap only at boundaries, so the desired volume is $\boxed{32+\dfrac{25\pi}{3}}$.
