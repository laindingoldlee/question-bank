Two points are chosen independently and uniformly by arclength on the boundary of a unit square. The probability their Euclidean distance is at least $\dfrac{1}{2}$ is $\dfrac{a-b\pi}{c}$ in reduced positive-integer form with $\gcd(a,b,c)=1 $. Find $ a+b+c $.
