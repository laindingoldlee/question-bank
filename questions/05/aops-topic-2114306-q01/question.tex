At Luke's birthday party, a colony on Mars is $140{,}000{,}000$ miles away, and Luke wants to send a piece of cake to his friend there. His rocket flies at a constant $18{,}000$ miles per hour in a straight line, aimed at the place where Mars will be when the rocket arrives. Mars moves at a constant $53{,}858$ miles per hour along a straight line that is perpendicular to the rocket's line of flight. How many hours after the launch does his friend receive the cake? Round your answer to the nearest hour.
