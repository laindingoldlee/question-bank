During Caroline's first circuit, $0\le t\le2\pi$, her position is $(-2+2\cos t,2\sin t)$, whose first coordinate is nonpositive. John's first coordinate is positive for every $t>0$, so no meeting occurs then.
During Caroline's eastward segment, $2\pi\le t\le2\pi+1$, her first coordinate runs from zero to two. John's first coordinate is two throughout this interval. Equality is therefore possible only at $t=2\pi+1$, when Caroline is at $(2,0)$. John is then on his southward leg with height $6-t=5-2\pi\ne0$.
For $2\pi+1<t\le10$, Caroline is on her second circle and has first coordinate $2\cos(t-2\pi-1)<2$, whereas John's first coordinate remains two. Thus $\boxed{\text{there is no meeting for }0<t\le10}$.
