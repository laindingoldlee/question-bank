Two walkers start at $(0,0)$ at time zero. John moves continuously at one unit per second: two units east, two north, four south, and two north, then repeats. Caroline moves at two units per second: one counterclockwise circuit of a circle of radius two, starting at its rightmost point, followed by two units east, then repeats from the new location. Do they occupy the same point at any time $0<t\le10$?
