Four equally spaced points $A,B,C,D$ on a circle are joined to an interior point $P$, dividing the disk into four regions. The regions adjoining minor arcs $AB$ and $BC$ occupy fractions $\dfrac{1}{5}$ and $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of the disk. Find the fraction occupied by the second-largest region.
