Bottle $1$ holds $1$ litre of a solution that is $80\%$ acid, and bottle $2$ holds $1$ litre that is $50\%$ acid. We fill an empty one-litre bottle completely with a mixture from the two bottles that is $60\%$ acid. If the volumes poured from bottle $1$ and bottle $2$ are in the ratio $m:n$ in lowest terms, find $m+n$.
