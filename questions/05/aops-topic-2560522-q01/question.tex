A counter starts at $500$. At each step it goes up by $1$ or down by $1$, each with probability $\dfrac12$, independently of earlier steps. The process stops when the counter reaches $0$ or $1000$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the expected number of steps.
\item[(b)] More generally, for a counter that starts at $k$ and stops at $0$ or $N$ ($0\le k\le N$), prove that the expected number of steps is $k(N-k)$.
\end{enumerate}
