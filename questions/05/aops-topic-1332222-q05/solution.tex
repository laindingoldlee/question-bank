Without restriction there are $\dbinom{12}{2}^2=66^2=4356$ ways. If a particular basket is empty, the apples go into the other two baskets in $11^2=121$ ways; if two particular baskets are empty, there is $1$ way. By inclusion-exclusion the number of ways with no empty basket is
\[
4356-3\cdot121+3\cdot1=\boxed{3996}.
\]
