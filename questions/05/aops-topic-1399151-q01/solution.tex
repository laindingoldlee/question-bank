Let the parallel sides be $p>q$ and the legs $r$, $s$. Translating one leg along the bases (draw through an end of the shorter base a line parallel to the other leg) produces a triangle with sides $p-q$, $r$, $s$, which must be non-degenerate. Check the six choices of bases:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{bases} & \text{triangle } (p-q,r,s) & \\ \hline
4,1 & 3,2,3 & \text{valid}\\
4,2 & 2,1,3 & 1+2=3,\ \text{degenerate}\\
4,3 & 1,1,2 & \text{degenerate}\\
3,1 & 2,2,4 & \text{degenerate}\\
3,2 & 1,1,4 & \text{impossible}\\
2,1 & 1,3,4 & \text{degenerate}
\end{array}
\]
So the bases are $AB=4$ and $DC=1$ and the legs are $AD=2$ and $BC=3$. Let $Q$ on $AB$ with $AQ=1$; then $AQCD$ is a parallelogram, so $QC=2$, and triangle $QBC$ has sides $QB=3$, $QC=2$, $BC=3$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real h=4*sqrt(2)/3;
pair A=(0,0),B=(4,0),C=(5/3,h),D=(2/3,h),Q=(1,0),F=(5/3,0);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(1));
draw(C--Q,red+dashed); draw(C--F,gray+dashed);
draw(F+(0.15,0)--F+(0.15,0.15)--F+(0,0.15),gray);
dot(A);dot(B);dot(C);dot(D);dot(Q,red);
label("$A$",A,SW); label("$B$",B,SE); label("$C$",C,NE); label("$D$",D,NW); label("$Q$",Q,S,red);
label("$1$",(D+C)/2,N); label("$2$",(A+D)/2,W); label("$3$",(B+C)/2,NE);
label("$1$",(A+Q)/2,S); label("$3$",(Q+B)/2+(0.5,0),S);
label("$2$",(Q+C)/2,W,red); label("$h$",(F+C)/2,E,gray);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Triangle $QBC$ is isosceles with $BQ=BC=3$ and base $QC=2$, so its height to $QC$ is $\sqrt{9-1}=2\sqrt2$ and its area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot2\cdot2\sqrt2=2\sqrt2$. Its height to the side $QB=3$ is the height $h$ of the trapezoid:
\[
h=\dfrac{2\cdot2\sqrt2}{3}=\dfrac{4\sqrt2}{3}.
\]
The area of the trapezoid is $\dfrac{4+1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{4\sqrt2}{3}=\dfrac{10\sqrt2}{3}$, so $a=10$, $b=2$, $c=3$ and $a+b-c=\boxed{9}$.
