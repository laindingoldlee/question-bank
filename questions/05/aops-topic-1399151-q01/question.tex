A trapezoid has side lengths $1$, $2$, $3$ and $4$, in some order. Its area can be written as $\dfrac{a\sqrt b}{c}$, where $a$, $b$, $c$ are positive integers, $\gcd(a,c)=1$ and $b$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. Find $a+b-c$.
