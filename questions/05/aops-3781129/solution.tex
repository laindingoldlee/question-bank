Write $P_n=A_n+B_nx+C_nx^2+\cdots$. The initial polynomial gives $A_1=4,B_1=-4,C_1=1$. The recurrence yields $A_n=4$, $B_n=4B_{n-1}=-4^n$, and $C_n=4C_{n-1}+B_{n-1}^2=4C_{n-1}+16^{n-1}$. Dividing by $4^n$ and summing the resulting geometric progression gives $\boxed{C_n = \dfrac{16^n-4^n}{12}}$. The formula also checks directly by induction from $C_1=1$.
