A circle has centre $T$ and radius $2$. A point $K$ outside the circle is such that the segment $KT$ meets the circle at a point $Y$ with $YK=6$ (so $YT=2$).
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the length of a tangent segment from $K$ to the circle.
\item[(b)] A student uses the power of a point and claims that the tangent has length $\sqrt{6\cdot8}=4\sqrt3$. What is the error?
\end{enumerate}
