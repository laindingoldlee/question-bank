There are $\binom{15}{2}\binom{13}{2}=8190$ ordered pairs of hands. Distinct two-element subsets with equal sum cannot share a ball, since that would force their other balls to coincide. Let $k_s$ be the number of pairs summing to $s$. The pair counts $1,2,\ldots,6$ each occur for four sums, and count $7$ occurs for three sums. Thus favorable ordered pairs total $4\sum_{k=1}^6k(k-1)+3\cdot7\cdot6=4(70)+126=406$. The probability is $\dfrac{406}{8190}=\dfrac{29}{585}$, giving $m+n=\boxed{614}$.
