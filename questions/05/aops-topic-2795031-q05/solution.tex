(a) Use a $4\times4$ square grid. Its 4 rows,4 columns, and 2 main diagonals give 10 lines. (b) Use points $(0,0),(2,0),(4,0)$; $(1,1),(2,1),(3,1)$; and $(0,2),(2,2),(4,2)$. The three horizontal rows are three lines. Of the 9 pairs joining a bottom-row point to a top-row point, exactly 7 have their midpoint among the middle-row points: exclude the two vertical lines at $x=0$ and $x=4$. These 7 lines are distinct and each contains a middle point, giving 10 lines total.

usepackage("amsmath");

\end{asy}
