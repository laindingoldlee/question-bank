The forbidden patterns are exactly pairs of $B$ s two positions apart, regardless of the intervening letter. Thus the five odd positions and five even positions each form an independent binary string with no adjacent $B$ s. If $f_n$ counts length $n$ strings with that rule, $f_0=1,f_1=2$, and $f_n=f_{n-1}+f_{n-2}$ by conditioning on the last letter. Hence $f_5=13$, and the answer is $13^2=\boxed{169}$.
