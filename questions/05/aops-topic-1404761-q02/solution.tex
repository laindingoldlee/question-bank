Let the failing students' mean be $x$. The overall mean is the weighted average of the two groups' means, with weights $0.6$ and $0.4$:
\[
0.6\cdot8+0.4\cdot x=6\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad 0.4x=1.2\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad x=\boxed{3}.
\]
