\textbf{(a)} Since $2=0\cdot22+2$ with $0\le2<22$, the quotient is $0$ and the remainder is $\boxed{2}$. The integers congruent to $2$ modulo $22$ are those that differ from $2$ by a multiple of $22$: $x=22k+2$ for integers $k$, that is, $\dots,-20,2,24,46,\dots$.

\textbf{(b)} Reduce each term modulo $23$:
\[121=5\cdot23+6,\quad 939=40\cdot23+19,\quad -94=-4\cdot23-2,\quad 938=40\cdot23+18,\quad 81=3\cdot23+12,\quad 23\times2\equiv0 .\]
So
\[S\equiv6+19-2+18+12+0=53=2\cdot23+7\equiv7\pmod{23},\]
and the remainder is $\boxed{7}$. (Directly: $S=2031=88\cdot23+7$.)
