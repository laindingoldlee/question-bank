Let $S=\displaystyle\sum_{k\ge1}\dfrac{7k}{8^k}$; the series converges because the terms shrink geometrically. Then
\[
\dfrac{S}{8}=\dfrac{7}{64}+\dfrac{14}{512}+\dfrac{21}{4096}+\cdots=\sum_{k\ge1}\dfrac{7(k-1)}{8^k}.
\]
Subtracting term by term,
\[
S-\dfrac {S}{8}=\sum_{k\ge1}\dfrac{7}{8^k}=7\cdot\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{8}}{1-\dfrac{1}{8}}=1 .
\]
Hence $\dfrac{7}{8}S=1$ and
\[
S=\boxed{\dfrac{8}{7}}.
\]
