For fixed $y$, possible values have parity $y$ and range from $3y$ through $3y+18$ in steps of $2$. Even $y=0,2,4,6,8$ cover all even values from $0$ to $42$, because their step-two intervals overlap. Odd $y=1,3,5,7,9$ similarly cover all odd values from $3$ to $45$. Thus every integer from $2$ to $43$ is possible, but $44$ is not: it is even, requiring even $y$, whose maximum is $2\cdot9+3\cdot8=42$. The answer is $\boxed{44}$.
