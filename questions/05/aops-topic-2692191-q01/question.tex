For $a>0$, $a\ne1$, say that the graphs of $y=a^x$ and $y=\log_ax$ are \emph{tangent} if they have a common point at which they have the same tangent line. A student claims: ``The graphs are tangent exactly when $a=\sqrt[e]{e}$.'' Prove or disprove this claim by finding all $a$ for which the graphs are tangent.
