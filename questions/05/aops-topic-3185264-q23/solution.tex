Use $A=(0,0),B=(4,0),C=(4,4),D=(0,4)$, so $M=(2,0)$ and $P=(0,1)$. Reflect $P$ across $CD$ to $P_1=(0,7)$, then reflect $P_1$ across $BC$ to $P_2=(8,7)$. Reflecting the last two path pieces in the same way unfolds $MN+NO+OP$ into a broken path from $M$ to $P_2$, whose length is at least $MP_2=\sqrt{85}$. The straight segment crosses $x=4$ at $(4,\dfrac{7}{3})$ and then the reflected top side at $(\dfrac{38}{7},4)$, corresponding in the original square to $N=(4,\dfrac{7}{3})$ and $O=(\dfrac{18}{7},4)$. These lie on the required sides, so equality is achieved. Thus $\boxed{x=85}$.

\end{asy}
