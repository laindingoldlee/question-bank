Let $x$ gallons of cream be used; then $30-x$ gallons of milk are used. Count the gallons of butterfat:
\[
0.23x+0.03(30-x)=0.04\cdot30 .
\]
This gives $0.2x+0.9=1.2$, so $x=1.5$. The farmer needs
\[
\boxed{1.5\text{ gallons of cream and }28.5\text{ gallons of milk}}.
\]
Check: $0.23\cdot1.5+0.03\cdot28.5=0.345+0.855=1.2=0.04\cdot30$.
