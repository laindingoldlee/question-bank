Each round is a permutation matching the three labelled players on one team to the other. Split the six permutations into three even and three odd ones. Each player-pair occurs in exactly one even and one odd permutation, and each even--odd pair shares exactly one player-pair. Thus the requirement that each player-pair occur twice says $e_i+o_j=2$ for all $i,j$. All even multiplicities are a common $t$ and all odd multiplicities are $2-t$, where $t=0,1,2$. The numbers of ordered six-round schedules are $\dfrac{6!}{(2!)^3},6!,\dfrac{6!}{(2!)^3}$, summing to $\boxed{900}$.
