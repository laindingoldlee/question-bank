The multiples of $2$ are $2,4,\dots,100$, with sum $2(1+2+\cdots+50)=2\cdot1275=2550$. The multiples of $3$ are $3,6,\dots,99$, with sum $3(1+2+\cdots+33)=3\cdot561=1683$. The difference is $2550-1683=\boxed{867}$.
