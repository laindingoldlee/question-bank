Two of the digits $1,2,3,4$ differ by more than $2$ only for the pair $\{1,4\}$. So we count the arrangements in which $1$ and $4$ are not adjacent. There are $4!=24$ arrangements in all. Treating $1$ and $4$ as a block, there are $3!$ arrangements of the block and the other two digits, and $2$ orders inside the block: $12$ arrangements with $1$ and $4$ adjacent. Hence the answer is $24-12=\boxed{12}$.
