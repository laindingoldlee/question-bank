The terms in odd positions are $3,5,7,9,11,13,\dots$ (common difference $2$), and the terms in even positions are $7,4,1,-2,-5,\dots$ (common difference $-3$). The next three terms are the $12$th, $13$th and $14$th: the next even-position term $-5-3=-8$, the next odd-position term $13+2=15$, and then $-8-3=-11$:
\[\boxed{-8,\ 15,\ -11}.\]
