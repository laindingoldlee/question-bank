The knight has eight potential destinations, none the bishop's square since the pieces share a row. Its expected count is $8(1-p)$.

Along each bishop diagonal ray, reaching the square at distance $k$ requires all first $k$ squares to be meteor-free, with probability $(1-p)^k$. The knight's square is on none of these rays. Linearity of expectation and a geometric series give bishop expectation
\[
4\sum_{k=1}^\infty(1-p)^k=\dfrac{4(1-p)}{p}.
\]
Equating the expectations and dividing by $1-p>0$ yields $\boxed{p=\dfrac{1}{2}}$.
