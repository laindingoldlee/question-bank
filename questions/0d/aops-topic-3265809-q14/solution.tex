The extended sine rule and area formula give $K=\dfrac{abc}{4R}$. Substituting $R=abc>0$ yields $\boxed{K=\dfrac{1}{4}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(4,0),C=(1.1,2.7);pair O=(2,0.759259259);real R=abs(O-A);draw(circle(O,R),gray);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(O--A,dashed);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,N);label("$O$",O,E);label("$R$",(A+O)/2,NW);label("$c$",(A+B)/2,S);label("$a$",(B+C)/2,NE);label("$b$",(A+C)/2,NW);label("Schematic shape; numerical scale suppressed",(2,-2));
\end{asy}
