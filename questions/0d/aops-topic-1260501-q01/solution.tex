\emph{Method 1.} Using $\sum_{k=1}^{24}k=300$ and $\sum_{k=1}^{24}k^2=\dfrac{24\cdot25\cdot49}{6}=4900$,
\[\sum_{k=1}^{24}k(25-k)=25\cdot300-4900=\boxed{2600}.\]
\emph{Method 2 (counting).} $\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}k(n-k)=\dbinom{n+1}{3}$: choosing $3$ numbers from $\{0,1,\dots,n\}$, if the middle one is $k$ there are $k$ choices below it and $n-k$ above. With $n=25$ this is $\dbinom{26}{3}=2600$.
