Eight students, numbered $1$ to $8$ counter-clockwise, sit in a circle. A teacher has $2013$ erasers. Starting with student $1$ and going counter-clockwise, he gives $i$ erasers to student $i$; after student $8$ he returns to student $1$ and repeats, until he does not have enough erasers for the next student. How many erasers does the teacher have left?
