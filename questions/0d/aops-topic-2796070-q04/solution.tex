One full round uses $1+2+\cdots+8=36$ erasers. Since $2013=55\cdot36+33$, after $55$ rounds $33$ remain. Then he gives $1,2,\dots,7$ erasers (total $28$), leaving $5$, which is fewer than the $8$ needed by student $8$. He has $\boxed{5}$ erasers left.
