Diana has played everyone. Amy's only game is therefore with Diana. Catherine needs two more opponents besides Diana and cannot use Amy, so she has played Betty and Elliana. Betty's games are then exactly Diana and Catherine, leaving no Betty--Elliana game. Elliana has played Diana and Catherine, hence two games. The sum of degrees is $1+2+3+4+2=12$, counting each game twice, so $\boxed6$ games have occurred.
