Let $d_0,\ldots,d_{11}$ be the nonnegative distances traveled in the successive half-minute intervals. The observations require
$d_j+d_{j+1}=1$ for $0\le j\le10$.
Subtracting successive equations shows $d_{j+2}=d_j$, so the distances alternate $t,1-t$ for some $0\le t\le1$. The six pairs therefore sum to $6$ meters. Constant speed realizes these constraints, so both the minimum and maximum are $\boxed6$ meters; their sum is $\boxed{12}$.
