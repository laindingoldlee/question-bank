Each radio cost $\dfrac dn$ dollars. Selling two at half cost loses $2\cdot\dfrac{d}{2n}=\dfrac dn$ dollars, and the other $n-2$ radios gain $8(n-2)$ dollars. So
\[8(n-2)-\dfrac dn=72\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad d=8n^2-88n=8n(n-11).\]
Since $d>0$, we need $n>11$. For $n=12$, $d=96$: each radio costs $8$, two are sold for $4$ each and ten for $16$ each, giving revenue $8+160=168=96+72$. The least value is $\boxed{12}$.
