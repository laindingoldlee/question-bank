A machine processes $20$ barrels of cider per minute with quality control and $35$ without. A rival team processes $27$ barrels per minute. In a $60$-minute competition, the machine runs with quality control at first and then switches it off. After how many minutes must quality control be switched off for the competition to be a tie?
