Inverse proportionality means that (weight)$\times$(area) is constant:
\[120\cdot200=24000=240\cdot A,\qquad A=\boxed{100}\text{ square inches}.\]
(Under this unrealistic assumption, a heavier object needs \emph{less} glue.)
