Count the complements: $15$ men are unmarried, $30$ have no telephone, $25$ own no car and $20$ own no home. A man who lacks at least one of the four is in at least one of these groups, so at most $15+30+25+20=90$ men lack something, and at least $100-90=10$ men have all four.

The bound is attained when the four complementary groups are disjoint (for example, men $1$--$15$ unmarried, $16$--$45$ without a telephone, $46$--$70$ without a car, $71$--$90$ without a home); then exactly men $91$--$100$ have all four. The minimum is $\boxed{10}$.
