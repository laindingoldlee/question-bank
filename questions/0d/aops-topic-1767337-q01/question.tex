Four bugs sit at the vertices of a unit square. At the same moment each bug begins to crawl directly towards its counterclockwise neighbour, always heading straight at it, at a constant speed of $1$ unit per minute. How long does it take for the bugs to meet?

Also explain the following ``radial'' argument: each bug's velocity makes a $45^\circ$ angle with the line to the centre, so it approaches the centre at speed $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}$, and it starts at distance $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}$ from the centre.
