Completing squares: $(x-1)^2+(y+2)^2=1$, the unit circle about $(1,-2)$. Its lattice points are $(0,-2),(2,-2),(1,-1),(1,-3)$:
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw(circle((1,-2),1));dot((0,-2));dot((2,-2));dot((1,-1));dot((1,-3));draw((-0.5,0)--(2.5,0),Arrow);draw((0,-3.5)--(0,0.5),Arrow);label("$(1,-2)$",(1,-3.3),S);dot((1,-2));
\end{asy}

so there are $\boxed{4}$ pairs.
