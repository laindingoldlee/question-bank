$a\equiv b\pmod{12}$ means that $a-b$ is a multiple of $12$; for $0\le n\le11$ this says that $n$ is the remainder when $10389$ is divided by $12$. Since $12\cdot865=10380$, we get $10389=12\cdot865+9$, so $n=\boxed{9}$.

(Without long division: $10389=10000+389$; $10000=12\cdot833+4$ and $389=12\cdot32+5$, so $10389\equiv4+5=9$.)
