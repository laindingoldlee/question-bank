A catering business makes three kinds of cakes, each needing a decorator, a baker and a design consultant. Cake A takes $9$ decorator hours, $6$ baker hours and $1$ consultant hour; cake B takes $10$, $4$ and $2$ hours; cake C takes $12$, $4$ and $1$ hours. There are exactly $398$ decorator hours, $164$ baker hours and $58$ consultant hours available. How many cakes of each kind can be made if all the available hours are used?
