Use $B=(0,0)$, $C=(1,0)$, and $A=(x,y)$. Then $M=(\dfrac{x}{2},\dfrac{y}{2})$ and $G=(\dfrac{x+1}{3},\dfrac{y}{3})$. The condition is $3\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\le\sqrt{(x-2)^2+y^2}$. Squaring and completing the square gives $(x+\dfrac{1}{4})^2+y^2\le\dfrac{9}{16}$. Thus the region is a disk of radius $\dfrac{3}{4}$, with area $\boxed{\dfrac{9\pi}{16}}$. Excluding degenerate triangles only removes points on a line and does not change this area.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
filldraw(circle((-.25,0),.75),lightblue);pair B=(0,0),C=(1,0),A=(-.4,.5),M=A/2,G=(A+C)/3;draw(A--B--C--A);draw(A--M--G,blue);label("A",A,N);label("B",B,S);label("C",C,E);label("M",M,W);label("G",G,NE);dot((-.25,0));label("centre",(-.25,0),S);
\end{asy}
