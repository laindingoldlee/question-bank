A test for a disease gives the correct result $95\%$ of the time: it is positive for $95\%$ of the people who have the disease and negative for $95\%$ of the people who do not. Suppose $2\%$ of the population has the disease. A randomly chosen person tests positive. What is the probability that this person actually has the disease? Explain in words why the answer is so low.
