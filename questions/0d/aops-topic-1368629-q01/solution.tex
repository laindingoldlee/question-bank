The ratio $2:3$ corresponds to the fraction $\dfrac{2}{3}$. Its reciprocal is $\dfrac{3}{2}$, which corresponds to the ratio $\boxed{3:2}$: the reciprocal of a ratio $a:b$ (with $a,b\ne0$) is $b:a$, obtained by swapping the two terms. Check: $\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot\dfrac{3}{2}=1$.
