A symmetric lens is bounded by two minor circular arcs with common endpoints $A,B$, each subtending $60^\circ$ at its circle centre. If $AB=8$, find the largest inscribed circle radius $a+b\sqrt c$, with $c$ squarefree, and compute $a+b+c$.
