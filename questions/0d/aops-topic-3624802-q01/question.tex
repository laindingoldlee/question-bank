An urn contains S special balls and N normal balls, where $S\ge 1$ and $N\ge 0$. Alice and Bob alternately draw uniformly without replacement, Alice first. The first special ball wins. Show Alice is at least as likely to win, and determine exactly when their probabilities are equal.
