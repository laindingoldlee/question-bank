Let $p_k$ be the probability that the first special ball is drawn in position $k$. For $1\le k\le N+1$, $p_k=\dfrac{\binom{N+S-k}{S-1}}{\binom{N+S}{S}}$. Alice wins at odd $k$, Bob at even $k$. Since $p_k$ is nonincreasing, pairing $p_1-p_2$, $p_3-p_4$, and so forth shows $P(A)-P(B)\ge 0$. If $S>1$ and $N\ge 1$, the first pair is strictly positive; if $N=0$, Alice wins surely. For $S=1$, all $N+1$ positions are equally likely, so equality occurs precisely when $N+1$ is even, or $\boxed{S=1\text{ and }N\text{ odd}}$. In all other cases Alice is strictly more likely to win.
