$|x-a|$ is the distance from $x$ to $a$ on the number line, so we want the point $x$ whose total distance to $2$, $4$, $5$ is least.

Pair the outer points: $|x-2|+|x-5|\ge|(x-2)-(x-5)|=3$ by the triangle inequality, with equality exactly for $2\le x\le5$; and $|x-4|\ge0$, with equality exactly for $x=4$. Both equalities hold at $x=4$, so the minimum is
\[
3+0=\boxed{3}.
\]
In general, $|x-a_1|+\dots+|x-a_n|$ with $a_1\le\dots\le a_n$ is minimised at a median of the $a_i$: pairing $a_1$ with $a_n$, $a_2$ with $a_{n-1}$, and so on, each pair contributes at least $a_{n+1-i}-a_i$, with equality when $x$ is between them, and a median lies between every pair.
