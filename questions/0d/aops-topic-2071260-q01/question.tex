The positive integers $a$ and $b$ are both divisible by $6$, but neither is divisible by any perfect square other than $1$. Which of the following is always true about the value of $\dfrac{a}{2}+\dfrac{b}{3}$?

$\textbf{(A)}$ It is even and composite. \quad
$\textbf{(B)}$ It is odd and composite. \quad
$\textbf{(C)}$ It is even and prime. \quad
$\textbf{(D)}$ It is odd and prime. \quad
$\textbf{(E)}$ None of the above.
