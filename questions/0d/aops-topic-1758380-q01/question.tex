A machine in a chocolate factory squirts out drops of molten chocolate. When the machine is set to $x$, the drops have mean mass $x$ grams with a standard deviation of $0.01$ grams. Nearly all chocolate chips produced have a mass within $2$ standard deviations of the mean. To make (nearly) no chocolate chips of mass less than $2$ grams, what is the lowest setting $x$ that should be used?
