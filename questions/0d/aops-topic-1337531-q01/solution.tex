Every cross-section is the same, so we compare areas of the circular face and the square. The largest square inside a circle of radius $r$ is the inscribed square, whose diagonal is a diameter $2r$; its side is $r\sqrt2$ and its area is $2r^2$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real r=1; pair O=(0,0);
pair[] Q={dir(45),dir(135),dir(225),dir(315)};
fill(circle(O,r),paleyellow);
fill(Q[0]--Q[1]--Q[2]--Q[3]--cycle,white);
draw(circle(O,r),linewidth(0.9));
draw(Q[0]--Q[1]--Q[2]--Q[3]--cycle,blue+linewidth(0.9));
draw(O--Q[0],red+dashed); dot(O);
label("$O$",O,W); label("$r$",(O+Q[0])/2,SE,red);
label("$r\sqrt{2}$",(Q[2]+Q[3])/2,N,blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The circle has area $\pi r^2$, so the discarded fraction (the four shaded circular segments) is
\[
\dfrac{\pi r^2-2r^2}{\pi r^2}=\boxed{1-\dfrac{2}{\pi}}\approx0.363,
\]
about $36\%$ of the cheese.
