Let the common ratio be $r$, so $b=ar$, $c=ar^2$, $d=ar^3$. Then
\[
b-a=a(r-1)=3,\qquad c-b=ar(r-1)=9 .
\]
Dividing (note $a(r-1)=3\ne0$) gives $r=3$, and then $a\cdot2=3$, so $a=\dfrac{3}{2}$. The sequence is $\dfrac{3}{2},\dfrac{9}{2},\dfrac{27}{2},\dfrac{81}{2}$, and indeed $\dfrac{9}{2}-\dfrac{3}{2}=3$, $\dfrac{27}{2}-\dfrac{9}{2}=9$. Hence
\[
d=\boxed{\dfrac{81}{2}} .
\]
