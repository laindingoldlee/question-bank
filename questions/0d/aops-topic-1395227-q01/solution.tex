Let the digits be $x>y>z$ (distinct, from $1$ to $9$). For fixed digits, the difference between an arrangement and a rearrangement is largest when we take the largest arrangement $\overline{xyz}$ minus the smallest $\overline{zyx}$:
\[
\overline{xyz}-\overline{zyx}=(100x+10y+z)-(100z+10y+x)=99(x-z).
\]
This is largest when $x-z$ is largest, namely $x=9$, $z=1$ (any middle digit $y$ from $2$ to $8$), giving $99\cdot8=792$; for example $981-189=792$. The answer is $\boxed{792}$.
