First seat the $6$ girls in some $6$ of the positions of a row of girls only: $6!$ orders. The girls create $7$ gaps (one at each end and five between neighbours). No two boys are adjacent exactly when the boys are placed in $3$ different gaps; choose the gaps in $\dbinom73$ ways and put the $3$ boys into them in $3!$ orders. So the number of seatings is
\[
6!\cdot\dbinom73\cdot3!=720\cdot35\cdot6=\boxed{151200}.
\]
