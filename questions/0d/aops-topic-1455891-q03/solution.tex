\item The triangle has sides $12$, $11$, $12$: it is isosceles with base $11$. The altitude to the base bisects it, so its height is
\[
h=\sqrt{12^2-\left(\dfrac{11}{2}\right)^2}=\sqrt{144-\dfrac{121}{4}}=\dfrac{\sqrt{455}}{2},
\]
and the area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot11\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt{455}}{2}=\dfrac{11\sqrt{455}}{4}$. (Heron's formula with $s=\dfrac{35}{2}$ gives the same value.)
