This is a relay: in each part, $T$ denotes the answer to the previous part.
\begin{enumerate}
\item A circular pizza is cut into slices whose central angles are all $30^\circ$. How many slices are there?
\item A triangle has sides $T$, $11$ and $12$. What is its area?
\item What is $T^4$?
\item What is $T\cdot\dfrac{1}{T}$?
\item What positive value of $x$ satisfies $x^4+T=1297$?
\end{enumerate}
