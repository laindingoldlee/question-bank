Long division gives $\dfrac{1}{7}=0.\overline{142857}$, $\dfrac{2}{7}=0.\overline{285714}$, and $\dfrac{3}{7}=0.\overline{428571}$. Equivalently, multiplying each block by $7$ gives $999999$, $1999998$, and $2999997$, respectively; dividing by $999999$ verifies the expansions. Multiplication by $10$ modulo $7$ cycles the nonzero remainders $1,3,2,6,4,5$, so starting with remainder $2$ or $3$ rotates the same long-division cycle.
