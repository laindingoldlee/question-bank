Let $u=\log_a b$; then $\log_b a=\dfrac{1}{u}$, and $u+\dfrac{1}{u}=\sqrt{29}$. Hence
\[
\left(u-\dfrac{1}{u}\right)^2=\left(u+\dfrac{1}{u}\right)^2-4=29-4=25,
\]
so $\left|\log_a b-\log_b a\right|=\boxed{5}$.
