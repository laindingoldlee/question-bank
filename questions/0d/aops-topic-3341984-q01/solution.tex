Let $K=\max(0,\min(M,T-1)-\max(1,T-M)+1)$ be the number of ordered pairs $(u,v)$ in the range with $u+v=T$. Let $\delta=1$ when $T$ is even and $1\le\dfrac{T}{2}\le M$, and $0$ otherwise. For three pair-events, each has $KM$ ordered outcomes; each intersection of two has $K$, since the two other entries are forced; the triple intersection has $\delta$. Inclusion--exclusion gives probability $\boxed{\dfrac{3KM-3K+\delta}{M^3}}$. For $M=1000,T=500$, $K=499,\delta=1$, so the answer is $\dfrac{1495504}{1000000000}=\boxed{\dfrac{93469}{62500000}}$.
