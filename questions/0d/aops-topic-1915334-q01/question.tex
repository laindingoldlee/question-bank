Moldy Potatoes is a website that collects reviews for a film, designates each review as either positive or negative, and then reports the percentage of reviews that are positive, rounded to the nearest whole percent. If a film has a Moldy Potatoes rating of $85\%$, what is the minimum number of reviews that the website could have collected for that film?
