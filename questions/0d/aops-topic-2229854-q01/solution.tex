\textbf{(a)} The boxes hold $6\times2=12$ basketballs, and there are $3$ more, so the total is
\[3+6\times2=3+12=\boxed{15}.\]

\textbf{(b)} The multiplication $6\times2$ counts the balls in the boxes as one group, and only then are the $3$ loose balls added; this is why multiplication is done before addition. Computing from left to right, $(3+6)\times2=18$, would describe a different situation: $9$ boxes with $2$ balls each, which is not what Sam has.
