Move everything to one side: $(x+2)^2(x+1)-(x+2)(x-2)=(x+2)\left(x^2+2x+4\right)\le0$. Since $x^2+2x+4=(x+1)^2+3>0$, this is $x\le-2$. The integers are $-9,\dots,-2$, with sum $\boxed{-44}$.
