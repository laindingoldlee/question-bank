Trailing zeroes count factors of $10$, hence equal the smaller of the exponents of $2$ and $5$. Each factorial here has at least as many factors of $2$ as of $5$, so only factors of $5$ need counting. Their total is $6!+8!+2\cdot10!=720+40320+7257600=7298640=10137\cdot720$. Thus $k!=720$, so $\boxed{k=6}$.
