By reflection in the main diagonal, after reading BA there is one walk at each A adjacent to B. After BAN, the counts on the N cells (top right, centre, bottom left, bottom right) are $(1,2,1,0)$. After BANA the counts on the A cells (top middle, middle left, middle right, bottom middle) are $(3,3,3,3)$. After BANAN the N counts are $(6,12,6,6)$. Each noncentral N has two neighbouring A cells, whereas the centre has four, giving $2(6+6+6)+4(12)=\boxed{84}$ walks.
