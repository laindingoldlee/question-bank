Given a positive integer $n$ and a prime $p$, there is are unique nonnegative integers $a$ and $b$ such

that $n = p^b \cdot  a$ and $gcd  (a, p) = 1$. Let $v_p(n)$ denote this uniquely determined $a$. Let $S$ denote the set of the first 20 primes. Find $\sum_{ p \in S} v_p (1 + \sum^{100}_{i=0} p^i )$.
