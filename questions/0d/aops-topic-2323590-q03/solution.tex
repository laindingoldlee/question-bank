By Vieta's formulas, $a+b=-\dfrac{10}{3}$ and $ab=\dfrac{14}{3}$. Hence
\[a^2+b^2=(a+b)^2-2ab=\dfrac{100}{9}-\dfrac{28}{3}=\dfrac{100-84}{9}=\boxed{\dfrac{16}{9}}.\]
(The roots are not real here, but the identity holds for complex roots as well.)
