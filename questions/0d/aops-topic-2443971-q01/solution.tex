Since $64=2^6=2^5\cdot2$,
\[\sqrt[5]{64}=\sqrt[5]{2^5}\cdot\sqrt[5]{2}=\boxed{2\sqrt[5]{2}}.\]
(Check: $\left(2\sqrt[5]2\right)^5=32\cdot2=64$. The number $b=2$ cannot be reduced, since $\sqrt[5]{64}$ is not an integer: $2^5=32<64<243=3^5$.)
