Let the distance be $d$ miles. The times are $\dfrac{d}{100}$ and $\dfrac{d}{110}$ hours, and $9$ minutes is $\dfrac{9}{60}=\dfrac{3}{20}$ hour, so
\[
\dfrac{d}{100}-\dfrac{d}{110}=\dfrac{3}{20}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\dfrac{d}{1100}=\dfrac{3}{20}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad d=\boxed{165\text{ miles}}.
\]
Check: $1.65$ h $-1.5$ h $=0.15$ h $=9$ min. (The poster's expressions $\dfrac{110}{x-9}$ and $\dfrac{100}{x}$ mix up distance and time and minutes and hours.)
