For $j\mid i$, the summand is $\dfrac{i}{j}+1$ when $j=1$, and $\dfrac{i}{j}$ otherwise. Thus the inner sum is $\sigma(i)+1$, where $\sigma$ sums positive divisors. Since 210 is squarefree with primes 2, 3, 5, 7, multiplicativity gives $\sum_{i\mid210}\sigma(i)=\prod_{p\mid210}(1+(1+p))=4\cdot5\cdot7\cdot9=1260$. There are 16 divisors, so the result is $\boxed{1276}$.
