By Vieta's formulas $r+s+t=-\dfrac63=-2$, so $r+s=-2-t$, $s+t=-2-r$ and $t+r=-2-s$. Since $3x^3+6x^2+6x+3=3(x-r)(x-s)(x-t)$,
\[(r+s)(s+t)(t+r)=(-2-r)(-2-s)(-2-t)=\dfrac{3(-2)^3+6(-2)^2+6(-2)+3}{3}=\dfrac{-24+24-12+3}{3}=\boxed{-3}.\]
(Check: the polynomial is $3(x+1)\left(x^2+x+1\right)$ with roots $-1,\omega,\omega^2$, and $(\omega-1)\left(\omega+\omega^2\right)\left(\omega^2-1\right)=-(\omega-1)\left(\omega^2-1\right)=-3$.)
