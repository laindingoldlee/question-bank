Place the vertices at $A=(0,0)$, $B=(u,0)$, $D=(v,w)$, and $C=(u+v,w)$, with $u>0,w\ne0$. Then $AC^2+BD^2=(u+v)^2+w^2+(u-v)^2+w^2=2u^2+2(v^2+w^2)$. The side squares sum to precisely the same quantity because opposite sides are equal. This establishes the identity for either sign of $v$ or $w$.

size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(4,0),D=(1,2),C=(5,2);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C,dashed);draw(B--D,dashed);label("$A=(0,0)$",A,SW);label("$B=(u,0)$",B,SE);label("$C=(u+v,w)$",C,NE);label("$D=(v,w)$",D,NW);
\end{asy}
