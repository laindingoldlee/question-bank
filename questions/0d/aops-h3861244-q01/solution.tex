The one-digit blocks occupy $1+\cdots+9=45$ positions. The two-digit blocks occupy $2(10+\cdots+99)=9810$ more, ending at position $9855$. Thus the next blocks are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{repeated number}&\text{positions}\\\hline
100&9856\text{--}10155\\
101&10156\text{--}10458\\
102&10459\text{--}10764\\
103&10765\text{--}11073
\end{array}
\]
The chosen interval includes a tail of the $100$ block and all of the $101$ block; their nonzero digits are all ones. It includes all $102$ copies of $102$, contributing $2^{102}$. Finally, it includes the first $307$ digits of the $103$ block, namely $102$ complete copies and the next leading $1$, contributing $3^{102}$. Therefore $N=6^{102}$ and $\boxed{\log_6N=102}$.
