Let $F(x)=x^3-x^2-1$. Modulo $3$, direct checking gives the unique root $x\equiv2$. Suppose $r$ is a root modulo $3^k$. Its three possible lifts are $r+3^kt$, $t=0,1,2$, and expansion gives
\[
F(r+3^kt)\equiv F(r)+3^kt(3r^2-2r)\pmod{3^{k+1}}.
\]
Since $r\equiv2\pmod3$, the derivative factor is $2\pmod3$, hence exactly one of the three $t$ works at each stage. The successive unique residues are
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
\text{modulus}&3&9&27&81&243\\ \hline
\text{root}&2&5&23&77&158
\end{array}
\]
Each entry is checked by substitution, and the lift argument proves uniqueness. Therefore $\boxed{x\equiv158\pmod{243}}$.
