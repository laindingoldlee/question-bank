Take $A=(0,0),B=(w,0),C=(w,h),D=(0,h)$. Then $M=(\dfrac{w}{2},0)$ and $N=(w,\dfrac{h}{2})$, so $MN=\sqrt{(\dfrac{w}{2})^2+(\dfrac{h}{2})^2}=\dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{w^2+h^2}=\dfrac{1}{2}AC$.

\end{asy}
