The sides are $AB=8$, $BC=\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5$, $CD=11$ and $DA=4$. Let $E=(e,4)$ with $0\le e\le11$. The segment $AE$ is part of both perimeters, so it cancels:
\[
\underbrace{AB+BC+CE}_{\text{quadrilateral }ABCE}=\underbrace{AD+DE}_{\text{triangle }ADE}\iff 8+5+(11-e)=4+e\iff e=10 .
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(8,0),C=(11,4),D=(0,4),Ee=(10,4);
fill(A--B--C--Ee--cycle,paleyellow);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));draw(A--Ee,blue+linewidth(0.9));
dot(Ee);
label("$A(0,0)$",A,SW);label("$B(8,0)$",B,SE);label("$C(11,4)$",C,E);label("$D(0,4)$",D,NW);label("$E$",Ee,N);
label("$8$",(A+B)/2,S);label("$5$",(B+C)/2,SE);label("$4$",(A+D)/2,W);label("$10$",(D+Ee)/2,N);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

So $E=(10,4)$. The trapezoid has area $\dfrac{8+11}{2}\cdot4=38$ and triangle $ADE$ has area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot4\cdot10=20$, so
\[
[ABCE]=38-20=\boxed{18}.
\]
(Check with the shoelace formula on $(0,0),(8,0),(11,4),(10,4)$: $\dfrac{1}{2}\left|0+32+(44-40)+0\right|=18$.)
