In $2.5$ hours the ship travels $2000\cdot2.5=5000$ miles, so its distance becomes $10000-5000=5000$ miles, half the original distance. Since the apparent area is proportional to $\dfrac{1}{d^2}$, halving the distance multiplies the apparent area by $2^2=4$. The apparent area is $\boxed{4\text{ square millimetres}}$.
