The apparent area of a distant object, as seen by an observer, is inversely proportional to the square of its distance from the observer. An alien spaceship flies straight toward an observer at a constant speed of $2000$ miles per hour. When it is $10000$ miles away, its apparent area is $1$ square millimetre. What is its apparent area $2$ hours and $30$ minutes later?
