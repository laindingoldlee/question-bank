Factor out $4^{x-1}$:
\[
4^x-4^{x-1}=4^{x-1}(4-1)=3\cdot4^{x-1}=24\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 4^{x-1}=8 .
\]
Writing both sides as powers of $2$, $2^{2x-2}=2^3$, so $2x-2=3$ and $x=\dfrac{5}{2}$. Then
\[
(2x)^x=5^{\dfrac{5}{2}}=5^2\cdot5^{\dfrac{1}{2}}=\boxed{25\sqrt5}.
\]
