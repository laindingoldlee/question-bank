Group the terms as squares:
\[(x+y+z)^2=x^2+y^2+z^2+2xy+2yz+2zx,\qquad(y-1)^2+(z-1)^2=y^2+z^2-2y-2z+2.\]
Their sum is exactly the left side, so the equation says
\[(x+y+z)^2+(y-1)^2+(z-1)^2=0.\]
A sum of squares of real numbers is $0$ only if each is $0$: $y=z=1$ and $x+y+z=0$. Hence $\boxed{x=-2}$.
