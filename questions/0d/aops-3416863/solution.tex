Let the diagonals meet at $O$. Applying strict triangle inequality in $AOB,BOC,COD,DOA$ and summing gives $u<2(AO+BO+CO+DO)=2(e+f)$. Each diagonal is shorter than either boundary route between its endpoints. Thus $2e<u$ and $2f<u$, and adding yields $e+f<u$. Combining proves $\boxed{u<2(e+f)<2u}$.
