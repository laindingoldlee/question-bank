If the first number is $a$, the sum is
\[
a+(a+1)+\cdots+(a+737)=738a+\dfrac{737\cdot738}{2}=738a+369\cdot737 .
\]
Since $738=6\cdot123$, the first term is a multiple of $6$. And $369\equiv3$, $737\equiv5\pmod6$, so $369\cdot737\equiv15\equiv3\pmod6$. The remainder is $\boxed{3}$, whatever the integers are (the given $178$th term is not needed; with it, $a=4{,}256{,}638$).
