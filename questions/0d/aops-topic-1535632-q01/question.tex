The lines $AB$ and $DC$ containing two opposite sides of a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ meet at a point $P$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of the diagonal $AC$ and $R$ the midpoint of the diagonal $BD$. Prove that
\[
4\,[PMR]=[ABCD],
\]
where $[\,\cdot\,]$ denotes area.
