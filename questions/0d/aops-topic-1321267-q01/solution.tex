Let garden two have $y$ bushes, so garden one has $y+9$. After the move, $y+12=1.5(y-3)$, so $y+12=1.5y-4.5$, $0.5y=16.5$ and $y=33$. Garden one has $\boxed{42}$ bushes. Check: $45=1.5\cdot30$.
