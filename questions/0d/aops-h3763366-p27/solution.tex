Let $t$ be the time to the meeting, and let the riders\textquotesingle s speeds be $u,v$. The first rider\textquotesingle s remaining distance is $vt$, taking 27 minutes, so $27u=vt$. The second\textquotesingle s remaining distance is $ut$, taking 12 minutes, so $12v=ut$. Multiplying gives $t^2=27\cdot12=324$, hence $t=18$. The first rider\textquotesingle s total time is $18+27=\boxed{45}$ minutes.
