A tile has inradius $\dfrac{20\sqrt3\cdot\sqrt3}{6}=10$. The centre must lie at least 1 from each edge, in a smaller similar equilateral triangle of inradius 9. Its area ratio to the tile is $(\dfrac{9}{10})^2=\boxed{\dfrac{81}{100}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(-sqrt(3),-1),B=(sqrt(3),-1),C=(0,2);draw(A--B--C--cycle);filldraw(.9*A--.9*B--.9*C--cycle,lightgray);draw(circle((0,0),.1));label("allowed centre region",(0,.5));
\end{asy}
