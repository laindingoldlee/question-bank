\textbf{(a)} \[P(\text{Friday})=0.8\cdot0.8+0.2\cdot\dfrac1{15}\cdot\dfrac1{10}=\dfrac{16}{25}+\dfrac1{750}=\dfrac{480+1}{750}=\boxed{\dfrac{481}{750}}\approx0.641.\]

\textbf{(b)} Given the Friday goal, the Sunday goal is reached with probability $\dfrac56+\dfrac16\cdot\dfrac1{10}=\dfrac{51}{60}=\dfrac{17}{20}$. So
\[P(\text{both})=\dfrac{481}{750}\cdot\dfrac{17}{20}=\boxed{\dfrac{8177}{15000}}\approx0.545.\]
