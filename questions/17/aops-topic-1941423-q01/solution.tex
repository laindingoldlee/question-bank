Let the numbers be $a,a+d,a+2d,a+3d,a+4d$. Their sum is $5(a+2d)=310$, so $a+2d=62$. The last three sum to $3(a+3d)=231$, so $a+3d=77$. Hence $d=15$, $a=32$, and the numbers are $\boxed{32,\ 47,\ 62,\ 77,\ 92}$.
