Interpret combinations as unordered scoops with repeats allowed. Ben has $\binom73=35$ choices and Jerry $\binom62=15$, giving 525 total. A forbidden choice shares two flavours: Jerry must choose two distinct flavours, in $\binom52=10$ ways. Ben must use both, either with a third flavour in three ways or by repeating one of them in two ways. Thus 50 choices are forbidden, leaving $\boxed{475}$.
