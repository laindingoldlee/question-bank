Rowing $1$ mile at $4$ miles per hour takes $\dfrac14$ hour $=15$ minutes, during which $150$ gallons leak in. At most $30$ gallons may remain, so Roy must bail at least $120$ gallons in $15$ minutes: $\boxed{8}$ gallons per minute.
