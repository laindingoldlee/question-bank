The monic quotient must be $x^2+cx+1$. Comparing coefficients gives $a+c=-1$ and $a-3c=-5$, so $a=-2,c=1$. The quadratic coefficient is $b=ac-2=-4$. Hence $|a+b|=\boxed6$.
