$120=2^3\cdot3\cdot5$, so it has $(3+1)(1+1)(1+1)=\boxed{16}$ positive divisors. A divisor divisible by $3$ has the form $3\cdot d$ with $d$ a divisor of $40=2^3\cdot5$, so there are $(3+1)(1+1)=\boxed{8}$ of them.
