A company's revenue is a quadratic function $R(x)$ of the number $x$ of items sold, and $R(2)=R(12)=0$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that this information does not determine $R(20)$.
\item[(b)] If the largest value of $R$ is $M$, find $R(20)$ in terms of $M$.
\end{enumerate}
