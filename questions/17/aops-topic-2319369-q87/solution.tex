\textbf{(a)} Since $2$ and $12$ are the roots, $R(x)=a(x-2)(x-12)$ for some $a\ne0$, and $R(20)=a\cdot18\cdot8=144a$, which depends on $a$. For instance $a=-1$ and $a=-2$ give different values.

\textbf{(b)} For $R$ to have a largest value, $a<0$, and the maximum is at the midpoint $x=7$ of the roots: $M=R(7)=a\cdot5\cdot(-5)=-25a$, so $a=-\dfrac M{25}$. Then
\[R(20)=144a=\boxed{-\dfrac{144M}{25}},\]
a loss of $5.76$ times the maximum revenue (the model is only realistic between $2$ and $12$ items).
