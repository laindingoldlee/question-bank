A line segment $AB$ from a point $A$ on the positive $x$-axis to a point $B$ on the positive $y$-axis cuts off a triangle of area $54$ square units in the first quadrant. The line $y=0.6x$ divides this triangle into two triangles of areas $40$ and $14$ square units. If $A$ has coordinates $(a,0)$, find all possible values of $a$. Express your answers in simplest radical form.
