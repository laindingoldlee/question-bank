$ 343=7^3 $. Each nonzero decimal digit in such a product must be $ 1 $ or $ 7 $, and exactly three must be $ 7 $. The four possibilities are $ 1777,7177,7717,7771 $. Exactly the last three are in the interval, so the answer is $\boxed3 $.
