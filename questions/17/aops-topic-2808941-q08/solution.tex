Use $A=(0,0),B=(2,0),C=(2,2),D=(0,2)$, so the circle is $(x-1)^2+(y-1)^2=1$ and $M=(1,0),N=(1,2)$. Parameterize $DM$ from $M$ by $(x,y)=(1-t,2t)$. Substitution gives $5t^2-4t=0$, so $P=(\dfrac{1}{5},\dfrac{8}{5})$. By central symmetry $Q=(\dfrac{9}{5},\dfrac{2}{5})$. The diagonal $MN$ has length 2, and the distances of $P,Q$ from its line are both $\dfrac{4}{5}$. Thus the area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot2\left(\dfrac{4}{5}+\dfrac{4}{5}\right)=\boxed{\dfrac{8}{5}}$.

\end{asy}
