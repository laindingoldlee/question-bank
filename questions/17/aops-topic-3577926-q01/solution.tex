The left side is nonnegative, so $x\ge0$. Squaring gives $x+2=x^2$, or $(x-2)(x+1)=0$. The candidates are $2$ and $-1$. Only $2$ satisfies the nonnegative requirement and the original equation. Therefore $\boxed{x=2}$. Squaring erases sign information, which is why $-1$ appears as an extraneous candidate.
