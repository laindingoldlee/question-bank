The unhired people form a contiguous interval in age order. Conversely every nonempty interval can remain by removing people outside it from the two ends. There are $10+9+\cdots+1=55$ nonempty intervals, plus the empty remainder for hiring everyone. Thus there are $\boxed{56}$ teams.
