Number the original positions $1$ through $50$. Let $S_o,S_e$ be the sums in odd and even positions. The first player commits to whichever parity has the greater total. Initially the two endpoints have opposite parity, so a chosen-parity coin is available. After that choice the two endpoints have the other parity. Whichever the opponent takes exposes an endpoint of the chosen parity again. Repeating lets the first player take all $25$ coins of that parity, with total $\max(S_o,S_e)\ge\min(S_o,S_e)$.
