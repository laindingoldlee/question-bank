Any common factor divides $8(5n+16)-5(8n+29)=128-145=-17$, so it is $17$ (a prime). It does occur: for $n=7$ the numbers are $51=3\cdot17$ and $85=5\cdot17$. The common factor is $\boxed{17}$.
