Use $B=(0,0),C=(8,0),A=(4,4\sqrt3),D=(2,0)$, so $E=(3,2\sqrt3)$. Then $BE^2=9+12=21$ and $CE^2=25+12=37$. Their sum is $\boxed{58}$.

\end{asy}
