Use mass points. Put masses $m_A,m_B,m_C$ at the vertices so that $P$ and $Q$ are the balance points of $AB$ and $AC$:
\[m_A\cdot AP=m_B\cdot PB\iff 3m_A=9m_B,\qquad m_A\cdot AQ=m_C\cdot QC\iff 15m_A=6m_C.\]
Taking $m_A=6$ gives $m_B=2$ and $m_C=15$. Then $P$ carries mass $m_A+m_B=8$, and the centre of mass of the whole system lies on $CP$ (the balance point of the masses $8$ at $P$ and $15$ at $C$) and also on $BQ$. So it is $S$, and on segment $PC$
\[PS:SC=m_C:m_P=\boxed{15:8}.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0), B=(12,0), C=21*dir(62);
pair P=A+3*unit(B-A), Q=A+15*unit(C-A);
pair Sx=extension(C,P,B,Q);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
draw(C--P,blue); draw(B--Q,blue);
dot(P);dot(Q);dot(Sx);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,N);label("$P$",P,S);label("$Q$",Q,W);label("$S$",Sx,dir(20));
label("$3$",(A+P)/2,S,fontsize(8pt)); label("$9$",(P+B)/2,S,fontsize(8pt));
label("$15$",(A+Q)/2,W,fontsize(8pt)); label("$6$",(Q+C)/2,W,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}

(As a check by Menelaus' theorem for triangle $APC$ and the line $B,S,Q$: $\dfrac{AB}{BP}\cdot\dfrac{PS}{SC}\cdot\dfrac{CQ}{QA}=\dfrac{12}{9}\cdot\dfrac{15}{8}\cdot\dfrac{6}{15}=1$.)
