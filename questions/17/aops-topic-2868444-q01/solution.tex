We would need $a^2+b^2\equiv6\pmod8$. Squares are $0,1$ or $4$ modulo $8$, so a sum of two squares is $0,1,2,4$ or $5$ modulo $8$, never $6$. So there are $\boxed{\text{no solutions}}$.
