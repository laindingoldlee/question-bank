If digits include 9, their sum must be a multiple of 9 for the integer to be divisible by 9. No three distinct odd digits including 9 have such a sum. Without 9 the digits come from 1,3,5,7. Of these four possible triples, $\{1,3,7\}$ has sum 11, so its numbers are not divisible by 3; $\{1,5,7\}$ requires ending 5 and divisibility by 7 and 13, but neither 175 nor 715 qualifies. The remaining triples give 135,315 from $\{1,3,5\}$, and 735 from $\{3,5,7\}$ (375 fails divisibility by 7). All three pass every condition. Sum $\boxed{1185}$.
