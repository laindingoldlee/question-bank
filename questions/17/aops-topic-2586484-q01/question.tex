The infinite continued fraction
\[1+\dfrac{1^2}{3+\dfrac{2^2}{5+\dfrac{3^2}{7+\dfrac{4^2}{9+\cdots}}}}\]
is defined as the limit of its convergents
\[c_0=1,\qquad c_1=1+\dfrac{1^2}{3},\qquad c_2=1+\dfrac{1^2}{3+\dfrac{2^2}{5}},\qquad\dots\]
Find its value.
