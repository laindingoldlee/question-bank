Each die shows $5$ or $6$ with probability $\dfrac26=\dfrac13$, and the dice are independent, so the probability is $\dfrac13\cdot\dfrac13=\boxed{\dfrac19}$. (Equivalently, $4$ of the $36$ equally likely outcomes work.)
