Let there be $r$ red, $w$ white and $b$ blue chips. The conditions are
\[b\ge\dfrac {w}{2},\qquad b\le\dfrac {r}{3},\qquad w+b\ge55.\]
From the first, $w\le2b$, so $55\le w+b\le3b$, giving $b\ge\dfrac{55}{3}$, i.e.\ $b\ge19$ since $b$ is an integer. Then $r\ge3b\ge57$.

The value $57$ is possible: $b=19$, $w=36$, $r=57$ satisfies $19\ge18$, $19\le19$ and $36+19=55$. The minimum is $\boxed{57}$, answer $\textbf{(E)}$.
