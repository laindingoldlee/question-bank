A box contains chips, each of which is red, white or blue. The number of blue chips is at least half the number of white chips, and at most one third the number of red chips. The number of chips that are white or blue is at least $55$. The minimum number of red chips is

$\textbf{(A) }24\qquad\textbf{(B) }33\qquad\textbf{(C) }45\qquad\textbf{(D) }54\qquad\textbf{(E) }57$
