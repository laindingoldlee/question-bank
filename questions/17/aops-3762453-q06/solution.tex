The tangent-chord theorem and the shared angle at $T$ show that triangles $TAB$ and $TCA$ are similar (with the appropriate exterior ordering of $B,C$). Corresponding sides give $\dfrac{T A}{T C} = \dfrac{T B}{T A}$, hence $\boxed{TA^2=TB\cdot TC}$. This is the tangent-secant power identity.

import graph;
usepackage("amsmath");
import geometry;
size(7cm);
pair O=(0,0),T=(3,0),A=(1/3,sqrt(8)/3),B=(1,0),C=(-1,0);draw(unitcircle);draw(T--A);draw(T--C);draw(A--B);draw(A--C);label("$T$",T,(1,0));label("$A$",A,(0,1));label("$B$",B,(0,-1));label("$C$",C,(-1,0));
\end{asy}
