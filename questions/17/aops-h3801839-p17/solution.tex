Any initial number at most 1999 that is only decremented ends at at most $1999-32=1967$. If it is divided by 100 at any stage, its new value is at most 20 and it never increases. Every initial number from 2000 through 2025 reaches 2000 by time 25 and is divided by the next step, so it also finishes at most 20. Starting from 1999 gives 1967 without encountering a multiple of 100 during these steps. Thus the maximum is $\boxed{1967}$.
