First multiply the first two factors:
\[
\left(-2x^2yz^2+3x^2y\right)\left(-5y^2z^3-4z\right)=10x^2y^3z^5+8x^2yz^3-15x^2y^3z^3-12x^2yz .
\]
Now multiply each of these four terms by $xy^2$ and by $z$:
\[
\begin{aligned}
\left(10x^2y^3z^5+8x^2yz^3-15x^2y^3z^3-12x^2yz\right)xy^2&=10x^3y^5z^5+8x^3y^3z^3-15x^3y^5z^3-12x^3y^3z,\\
\left(10x^2y^3z^5+8x^2yz^3-15x^2y^3z^3-12x^2yz\right)z&=10x^2y^3z^6+8x^2yz^4-15x^2y^3z^4-12x^2yz^2 .
\end{aligned}
\]
No two of the eight terms are alike, so the product is
\[
\boxed{10x^3y^5z^5-15x^3y^5z^3+8x^3y^3z^3-12x^3y^3z+10x^2y^3z^6-15x^2y^3z^4+8x^2yz^4-12x^2yz^2}.
\]
A quick check: at $x=y=z=1$ the original product is $(1)(-9)(2)=-18$, and the eight coefficients sum to $10-15+8-12+10-15+8-12=-18$.
