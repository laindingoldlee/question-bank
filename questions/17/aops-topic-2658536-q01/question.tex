Let $\sigma(n)$ denote the sum of the positive divisors of $n$. Prove that if $n$ is an \emph{even} positive integer with $\sigma(n)=2n$, then $n=2^{p-1}(2^p-1)$, where $p$ and $2^p-1$ are both prime.
