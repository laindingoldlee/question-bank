Clearing denominators, $nb+na=ab$, i.e.\ $ab-na-nb+n^2=n^2$, so
\[
(a-n)(b-n)=n^2 .
\]
Since $\dfrac{1}{a}<\dfrac{1}{n}$, we have $a>n$ and likewise $b>n$; so $a-n=d$ and $b-n=\dfrac{n^2}{d}$ for a positive divisor $d$ of $n^2$. Then
\[
a+b=2n+d+\dfrac{n^2}{d}\ge 2n+2\sqrt{d\cdot\dfrac{n^2}{d}}=4n
\]
by AM--GM, with equality for $d=n$, i.e.\ $a=b=2n$ (and indeed $\dfrac{1}{2n}+\dfrac{1}{2n}=\dfrac{1}{n}$). The minimum is $\boxed{4n}$.
