Let Linda take $x$ minutes per mile on the first day, so $x+5$, $x+10$, $x+15$ minutes per mile on the following days. In $60$ minutes she travels $\dfrac{60}{x}$ miles, and this must be an integer, so $x$, $x+5$, $x+10$ and $x+15$ must all be divisors of $60$.

The divisors of $60$ are $1,2,3,4,5,6,10,12,15,20,30,60$. The only $x$ for which $x,x+5,x+10,x+15$ are all in this list is $x=5$ (giving $5,10,15,20$). So the distances are
\[\dfrac{60}{5}+\dfrac{60}{10}+\dfrac{60}{15}+\dfrac{60}{20}=12+6+4+3=25,\]
and the answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(C)}\ 25}$.
