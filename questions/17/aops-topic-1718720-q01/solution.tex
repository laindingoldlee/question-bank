MAXIMUM has three M's and the four different letters A, X, I, U. First arrange A, X, I, U in a row: $4!=24$ ways. This creates $5$ gaps (before, between and after them), and the M's must go into $3$ different gaps: $\binom53=10$ ways. Total $24\cdot10=\boxed{240}$.
