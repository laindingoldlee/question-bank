In how many ways can a committee of $4$ and a committee of $3$ be formed from $12$ people, if nobody may serve on both committees? A student writes $\dfrac{\dbinom{12}{3}\dbinom94+\dbinom{12}4\dbinom83}{2}$. Is this correct, and is the division by $2$ needed?
