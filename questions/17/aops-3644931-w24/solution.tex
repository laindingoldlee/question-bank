The reciprocal sum is $[\dfrac{1}{(1-\dfrac{1}{p_1})}][\dfrac{1}{(1-\dfrac{1}{p_2})}]-1=\dfrac{(p_1+p_2-1)}{[(p_1-1)(p_2-1)]}$. Its reduced denominator does not determine the numerator. For distinct primes 3 and 1013 the sum is $\dfrac{1015}{2024}$, whereas for distinct primes 2 and 4049 it is $\dfrac{2025}{2024}$. Trial division verifies these primes. Both satisfy the stated denominator condition and give different numerators. Thus the question has no unique numerical answer.
