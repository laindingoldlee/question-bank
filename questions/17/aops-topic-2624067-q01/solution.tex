We prove more generally: if $a\ge1$ and $b\ge0$ are integers with $2^a-1\mid2^b-1$, then $a\mid b$.

Write $b=qa+r$ with $0\le r<a$. Since $2^a\equiv1\pmod{2^a-1}$,
\[2^b=\left(2^a\right)^q\cdot2^r\equiv2^r\pmod{2^a-1},\qquad\text{so}\qquad 2^b-1\equiv2^r-1\pmod{2^a-1}.\]
Hence $2^a-1\mid2^r-1$. But $0\le2^r-1<2^a-1$, so $2^r-1=0$, i.e. $r=0$ and $a\mid b$.

Taking $a=2p$ and $b=n-1$ gives the statement. $\blacksquare$
