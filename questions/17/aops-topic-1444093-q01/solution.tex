Let the numbers be $x<y$. The sum plus the difference is $(x+y)+(y-x)=2y$, so
\[
2y+xy+\dfrac{y}{x}=450 .
\]
Multiplying by $x$ gives $y(x^2+2x+1)=450x$, that is,
\[
y=\dfrac{450x}{(x+1)^2}.
\]
Since $x$ and $x+1$ have no common factor, $(x+1)^2$ must divide $450=2\cdot3^2\cdot5^2$. The square divisors of $450$ are $1,9,25,225$, and $x\ge1$ means $x+1\in\{3,5,15\}$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $x=2$: $y=\dfrac{900}{9}=100$; check $102+98+200+50=450$.
\item $x=4$: $y=\dfrac{1800}{25}=72$; check $76+68+288+18=450$.
\item $x=14$: $y=\dfrac{6300}{225}=28$; check $42+14+392+2=450$.
\end{itemize}
So there are three pairs: $\boxed{(2,100),\ (4,72),\ (14,28)}$. (In each, the quotient happens to be an integer: writing $y=kx$, the condition is $k(x+1)^2=450$.)
