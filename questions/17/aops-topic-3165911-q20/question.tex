For positive integers $N$, define $S_N=\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^N\sum_{m=1}^N\dfrac{n\sec^2m-m\tan^2n}{3^{m+n}(m+n)}$, with trigonometric inputs in radians. Find $\lim_{N\to\infty}S_N$.
