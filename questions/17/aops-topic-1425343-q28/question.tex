Twins Cara and Dara drove from their house to the beach in exactly $5$ hours, not including rest stops. First Cara drove at an average speed of $40$ miles per hour and stopped at a rest area located halfway between their house and the beach. Then Dara drove from that location to the beach at an average speed of $60$ miles per hour. For how many hours did Dara drive?
