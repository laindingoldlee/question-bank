Let each half of the trip be $d$ miles. Then
\[
\dfrac{d}{40}+\dfrac{d}{60}=5\;\Longrightarrow\;\dfrac{3d+2d}{120}=5\;\Longrightarrow\;d=120 .
\]
Dara drove $\dfrac{120}{60}=\boxed{2}$ hours (and Cara $3$ hours).
