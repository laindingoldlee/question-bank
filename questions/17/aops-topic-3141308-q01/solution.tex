Choose the seven digits in $\binom{10}{7}$ ways. The smallest must be $d$. Choose which three of the other six go before it in $\binom63$ ways. Both their descending order and the remaining ascending order are forced. The leading digit cannot be zero. Total: $\binom{10}{7}\binom63=\boxed{2400}$.
