The non-water part does not change. In $100$ pounds of plums it is $8\%$, i.e. $8$ pounds. In the prunes it is $80\%$ of the weight, so the prunes weigh $\dfrac{8}{0.8}=\boxed{10}$ pounds.

The attempted solution assumed that $72\%$ of the water evaporates; but the percentages $92\%$ and $20\%$ refer to different totals, so subtracting them is meaningless.
