Use $Y=(0,0),O=(10,0),N=(10,10),G=(0,10)$. Then $A=(5,0)$, $B=(5,10)$, $C=(\dfrac{5}{2},5)$ and $D=(\dfrac{15}{2},5)$. The rhombus is in cyclic order A,C,B,D, with diagonals ten and five and area 25. Its side is $\dfrac{5\sqrt5}{2}$, so semiperimeter is $5\sqrt5$. Thus inradius is area divided by semiperimeter, $\dfrac{25}{(5\sqrt5)}=\boxed{\sqrt5}$.

import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);

\end{asy}
