A chord divides a circle into areas in the ratio $1:2$. The same chord is the complete segment cutting off a $45^\circ$-$45^\circ$-$90^\circ$ corner triangle from a square, again dividing the square in ratio $1:2$. Find the circle-to-square area ratio; an exact expression using the unique root of an equation is acceptable.
