Kevin picks a positive integer less than $1000$. Alex tries to find it by asking questions that Kevin answers with yes or no; each question must be either ``Is the number at most $a$?'' or ``Is the number at least $a$?'', where Alex chooses $a$ each time. What is the minimum number of questions that guarantees Alex can determine Kevin's number?
