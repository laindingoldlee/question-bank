Five cups are labelled $1,2,3,4,5$ and five lids are labelled $1,2,3,4,5$. The five lids are put on the five cups, one lid per cup. In how many of the ways of doing this do at least two lids land on the cups with the same numbers? What is the corresponding probability if the lids are put on at random?
