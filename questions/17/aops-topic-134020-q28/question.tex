Jeff picks a card at random from ten cards numbered $1$ through $10$; the number on the card is his starting point on a number line of the integers from $-1$ to $12$. He then spins a fair spinner with three equal sectors: one says ``move $1$ space left'' and two say ``move $1$ space right''. He follows the instruction, then spins again and follows the second instruction. What is the probability that he ends at a multiple of $3$? Express your answer as a common fraction.
