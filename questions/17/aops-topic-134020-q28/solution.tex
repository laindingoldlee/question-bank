Two spins move him by $-2$ (left, left) with probability $\dfrac{1}{9}$, by $0$ (one of each) with probability $2\cdot\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}=\dfrac{4}{9}$, and by $+2$ (right, right) with probability $\dfrac{4}{9}$.

For the starting points $1,\dots,10$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $s-2$ is a multiple of $3$ for $s=2,5,8$ ($3$ values);
\item $s$ is a multiple of $3$ for $s=3,6,9$ ($3$ values);
\item $s+2$ is a multiple of $3$ for $s=1,4,7,10$ ($4$ values).
\end{itemize}
(Note $0$ counts as a multiple of $3$.) Hence
\[
P=\dfrac{1}{10}\left(3\cdot\dfrac{1}{9}+3\cdot\dfrac{4}{9}+4\cdot\dfrac{4}{9}\right)=\dfrac{3+12+16}{90}=\boxed{\dfrac{31}{90}}.
\]
