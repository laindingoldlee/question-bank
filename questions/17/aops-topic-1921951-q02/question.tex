In $1997$, Everett exercised on every day with an even-numbered date (the day of the month is even), beginning with January $2$. On January $2$ he did $22$ jumping jacks. Each time he exercised, he did one more jumping jack than he had done the previous time he exercised. How many jumping jacks did Everett do in $1997$?
