The sum
\[
H=1+\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{3}+\dots+\dfrac{1}{1000}
\]
has no simple closed form. Find $\lfloor H\rfloor$, the greatest integer not exceeding $H$. (You may use $\ln10\approx2.3026$.)
