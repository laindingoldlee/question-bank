The altitude to the side of length 24 bisects it into two lengths 12. Pythagoras gives height $\sqrt{20^2-12^2}=16$, hence area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot24\cdot16=192$. Each altitude to a side of length 20 is $\dfrac{2\cdot192}{20}=19.2$. Their sum is $16+19.2+19.2=\boxed{54.4}$, choice A.

\end{asy}
