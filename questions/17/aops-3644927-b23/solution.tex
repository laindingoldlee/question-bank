Write the ratio in lowest terms as $\dfrac{b}{a}>1$. Integer sides then have the form $ka^2,kab,kb^2$, with coprime positive integers $a<b$. The triangle condition is $a^2+ab>b^2$, and $kb^2<30$. Hence $b\le5$. Checking coprime pairs gives $(a,b)=(2,3),(3,4),(4,5)$, with respectively $k=1,2,3$; $k=1$; and $k=1$. Thus the five triangles are $(4,6,9),(8,12,18),(12,18,27),(9,12,16),(16,20,25)$, giving $\boxed5$.
