Among the $6^3=216$ face sequences, those hitting the original six face number $216-5^3=91$. If six is never hit, success can arise from at least two hits on the original five: exactly two gives $\binom32\cdot4=12$, and three gives one. The only remaining success is three hits on the original four, giving one more. These cases are disjoint, so the probability is $\dfrac{91+12+1+1}{216}=\boxed{\dfrac{35}{72}}$.
