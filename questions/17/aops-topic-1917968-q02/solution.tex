There are $\left\lfloor\dfrac{1000}{12}\right\rfloor=83$ multiples of $12$. A multiple of $12$ is also a multiple of $15$ exactly when it is a multiple of $\operatorname{lcm}(12,15)=60$, and there are $\left\lfloor\dfrac{1000}{60}\right\rfloor=16$ of those. The answer is $83-16=\boxed{67}$.
