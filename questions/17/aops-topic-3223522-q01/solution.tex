Let the counts be $s,m,l$. Then $s+m+l=10$ and, in ten-cent units, $12s+13m+18l=149$. Subtracting $12$ times the first equation gives $m+6l=29$. Since $m\ge0$, $l\le4$. Since $s=10-m-l=5l-19\ge0$, $l\ge4$. Hence $\boxed{l=4}$, with $m=5,s=1$.
