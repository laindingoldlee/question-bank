In triangle $ABC$, the external bisector of $\angle A$ meets line $BC$ at $D$. Point $F$ lies on segment $CD$, and points $E$ and $G$ lie on segment $AC$, so that $AD\parallel FG\parallel BE$ and $AB\parallel EF$. If $AB=4$, $BC=5$, $BD=12$ and $CD=17$, then $CG=\dfrac mn$ for relatively prime positive integers $m$ and $n$. Find $m+n$.
