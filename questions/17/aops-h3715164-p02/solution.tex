A radius to a point of tangency is perpendicular to the tangent, so $\angle OAP=\angle OBP=90^\circ$. The interior angles of quadrilateral $OAPB$ total $360^\circ$, giving $\angle AOB=360^\circ-90^\circ-90^\circ-70^\circ=\boxed{110^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair O=(0,0),A=dir(55),B=dir(-55),P=(1/cos(55pi/180),0);draw(circle(O,1));draw(O--A--P--B--O);label("$O$",O,(-1,0));label("$A$",A,(0,1));label("$B$",B,(0,-1));label("$P$",P,(1,0));label("$70^\circ$",P+(-.35,0));label("$110^\circ$",(.3,0));
\end{asy}
