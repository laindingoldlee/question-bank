A maximum has no $1$, since combining it with another part increases the product. A part $k\ge5$ can be replaced by $3,k-3$ since $3(k-3)>k$. A $4$ can be replaced by two $2$s with equal product. Three $2$s can be replaced by two $3$s, increasing $8$ to $9$. Consequently a maximum consists of $3$s and at most two $2$s, with a $4$ interchangeable with two $2$s when that case occurs. Since $1337\equiv2\pmod3$, here there is exactly one $2$ and $445$ threes. Thus $\boxed{t=446}$.
