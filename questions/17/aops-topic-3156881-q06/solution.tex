The five binary parenthesizations have values $\dfrac{1}{24},\dfrac{3}{8},\dfrac{2}{3},\dfrac{3}{8},6 $. They exhaust the placements because the last division separates the four ordered inputs after position 1, 2, or 3, with the remaining triple parenthesized in its two possible ways. The largest is $\boxed6 $, obtained by $ 1\div((2\div3)\div4)$, or simply $ 1\div(2\div3\div4)$ with left-to-right division inside.
