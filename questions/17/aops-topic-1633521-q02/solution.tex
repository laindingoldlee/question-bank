First, $2018=13^2+43^2$ (as $169+1849=2018$). Let $u=2018^{1009}$; then
\[
2018^{2019}+2018=2018\left(2018^{2018}+1\right)=\left(13^2+43^2\right)\left(u^2+1^2\right).
\]
By the identity $\left(a^2+b^2\right)\left(c^2+d^2\right)=(ac+bd)^2+(bc-ad)^2$ (expand both sides: the cross terms $2abcd$ cancel on the right),
\[
2018^{2019}+2018=\boxed{\left(13\cdot2018^{1009}+43\right)^2+\left(43\cdot2018^{1009}-13\right)^2}.
\]
