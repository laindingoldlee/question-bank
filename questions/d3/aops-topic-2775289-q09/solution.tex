Unrolling the cylinder makes the helix a straight segment. Its horizontal displacement is three circumferences, $3\cdot2\pi\cdot\dfrac{2}{\pi}=12$, and vertical displacement 5. Pythagoras gives $\sqrt{12^2+5^2}=\boxed{13}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw((0,0)--(12,0)--(12,5)--(0,5)--cycle);draw((0,0)--(12,5),blue);draw((4,0)--(4,5),dashed);draw((8,0)--(8,5),dashed);label("three circumferences: $12$",(6,0),S);label("$5$",(12,2.5),E);label("unrolled path",(6,2.5),NW);
\end{asy}
