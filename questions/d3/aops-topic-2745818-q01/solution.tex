Place $A=(0,0)$, $B=(8,0)$, $C=(8,6)$, $D=(0,6)$, so $E=(3,6)$ and $F=(6,6)$.

\begin{asy}

\end{asy}

Line $AF$ is $y=x$, and line $BE$ is $y=\dfrac65(8-x)$. They meet where $x=\dfrac65(8-x)$, i.e. $x=\dfrac{48}{11}$, so the height of triangle $AHB$ above $AB$ is $\dfrac{48}{11}$. (Equivalently, triangles $HEF$ and $HBA$ are similar with ratio $EF:AB=3:8$, so the height is $\dfrac{8}{11}\cdot6$.) The area is
\[\dfrac12\cdot8\cdot\dfrac{48}{11}=\dfrac{192}{11},\]
so $x=\boxed{192}$.
