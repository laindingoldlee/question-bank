In trapezoid $ABCD$, sides $AD$ and $BC$ are parallel and $\angle ABC=90^\circ$. A circle is inscribed in the trapezoid, tangent to all four sides. Its point of tangency $E$ with side $DC$ satisfies $DE=4$ and $EC=16$. Find the area of $ABCD$.
