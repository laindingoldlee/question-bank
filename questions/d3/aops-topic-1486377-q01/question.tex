Square $ABCD$ has side length $1$. A quarter circle with centre $D$ passes through $A$ and $C$, and a quarter circle with centre $C$ passes through $B$ and $D$. The two quarter discs bounded by these arcs are shaded. Find the area of the region inside the square that is not shaded.
