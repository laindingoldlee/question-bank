Place $D=(0,0)$, $C=(1,0)$, $B=(1,1)$, $A=(0,1)$. The two unit circles centred at $D$ and $C$ meet inside the square at $X=\left(\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\right)$, since $X$ is at distance $1$ from both. Triangle $DCX$ is equilateral, so $\angle XDC=\angle XCD=60^\circ$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,1),B=(1,1),C=(1,0),D=(0,0),X=(0.5,sqrt(3)/2);
fill(D--arc(D,1,0,90)--cycle,rgb(0.86,0.91,1));
fill(C--arc(C,1,90,180)--cycle,rgb(0.86,0.91,1));
fill(arc(D,1,60,90)--A--B--arc(C,1,90,120)--cycle,orange);
draw(arc(D,1,0,90),blue); draw(arc(C,1,90,180),blue);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
dot(X); label("$X$",(0.5,0.945));
label("$A$",A,NW); label("$B$",B,NE); label("$C$",C,SE); label("$D$",D,SW);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The unshaded region (orange) is bounded by $AB$, the arc $BX$ of the circle centred at $C$ and the arc $XA$ of the circle centred at $D$. Its area is the area of the square minus the area of the union of the two quarter discs:
\[
\text{unshaded}=1-\big(\text{area of the union of the two quarter discs}\big).
\]
Each quarter disc has area $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$. Their overlap is the region below both arcs, made of the sector $DCX$ of the circle centred at $D$ (angle $60^\circ$) and the sector $CXD$ of the circle centred at $C$ (angle $60^\circ$), which overlap in the equilateral triangle $DCX$:
\[
\text{overlap}=2\cdot\dfrac{\pi}{6}-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}=\dfrac{\pi}{3}-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}.
\]
So the union has area $\dfrac{\pi}{4}+\dfrac{\pi}{4}-\dfrac{\pi}{3}+\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}=\dfrac{\pi}{6}+\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}$, and
\[
\text{unshaded}=\boxed{1-\dfrac{\pi}{6}-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}}\approx0.0434 .
\]
No calculus is needed: only sectors and a triangle.
