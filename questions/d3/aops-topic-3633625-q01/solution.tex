A quotient $\dfrac{a}{b}$ (with $b\ne 0$) means the unique real number $q$ satisfying $bq=a$. For $a=b=0$, every real $q$ satisfies $0q=0$, so no unique quotient exists. Therefore $\boxed{\dfrac{0}{0}\text{ is undefined}}$.
