For $x\le1$, $x^2(x-1)\le0$, so any solution has $x>1$. On $x>1$, both positive factors $x^2$ and $x-1$ strictly increase, so their product is strictly increasing. At $x=4$ it is $48$; at $x=5$ it is $100$. Continuity therefore gives a unique solution in $\boxed{(4,5)}$.
