There are $\binom{10}{2}=45$ games in total. If $g$ games have been played, their point total is at most $3g$, so $131\le3g$ and $g\ge44$. Since at least one game is unplayed, $g\le44$. Thus $g=44$, leaving $\boxed1$ game. One draw among those games gives $3\cdot44-1=131$, so the conditions are feasible.
