Dividing by $27$ gives $x^2-2x+4=0$, with roots $1\pm i\sqrt3=2(\cos60^\circ\pm i\sin60^\circ)$. De Moivre's theorem gives the sixth power of either root as $2^6=64$. Hence the difference is $\boxed0$, regardless of root order.
