There are $6\cdot8=48$ ordered first-two outcomes. Exactly three have sum exceeding 12: $(5,8),(6,7),(6,8)$. Each of the remaining 45 has one matching twelve-sided outcome. Thus the probability is $\dfrac{45}{6\cdot8\cdot12}=\dfrac{5}{64}$, giving $\boxed{59}$.
