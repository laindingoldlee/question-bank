A path consists of $3$ right steps and $4$ up steps in some order, so there are $\dbinom73=35$ paths in all. A path through $(2,2)$ goes from $(0,0)$ to $(2,2)$ in $\dbinom42=6$ ways and then from $(2,2)$ to $(3,4)$ in $\dbinom31=3$ ways, so $18$ paths pass through $(2,2)$. Hence
\[
35-18=\boxed{\textbf{(B)}\ 17}
\]
paths avoid it.
