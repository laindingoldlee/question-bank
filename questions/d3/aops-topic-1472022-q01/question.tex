Prella and Bodo find a treasure map. First, the map says ``walk $600$ meters due northwest to find your next clue.'' After walking the $600$ meters, they find a clue that says ``walk $1{,}000$ meters due north and then $300$ meters due southeast to find another clue.'' After following the directions, they find a final set of instructions: ``Walk $600$ meters due south and you will find your treasure.'' How far is the treasure from their original location? Give the exact distance and the distance to the nearest meter.
