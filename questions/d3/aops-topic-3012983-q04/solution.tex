Write $ z=a+bi $. Equating real and imaginary parts gives $ a^2-b^2=0 $ and $ 2ab=1 $. Thus $ b=a $ (the alternative $ b=-a $ would give $ 2ab\le0 $), and $ 2a^2=1 $. Consequently $\boxed{z=\pm\dfrac{1+i}{\sqrt2}}$, and squaring verifies both.
