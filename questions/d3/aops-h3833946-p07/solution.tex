The integer points on the circle are $(\pm5,0),(0,\pm5),(\pm4,\pm3),(\pm3,\pm4)$. Joining in circular order gives the natural convex dodecagon. In the first quadrant the cross-product sums are $15+7+15=37$. By the shoelace formula and fourfold symmetry, area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot4\cdot37=\boxed{74}$.
