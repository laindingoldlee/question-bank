For $n$ sides the angle is $180-\dfrac{360}{n}$ degrees. It is integral exactly when $n$ divides 360. The largest possibility is $\boxed{360}$, giving angle 179 degrees.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw(polygon(12));label("regular n-gon",(0,0));label("$180-\dfrac{360}{n}$",(0,-1.5));
\end{asy}
