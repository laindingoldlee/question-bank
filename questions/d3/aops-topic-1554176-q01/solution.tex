All $\dbinom{20}3=1140$ sets of three marbles are equally likely. The favourable sets consist of $2$ of the $9$ blue marbles and $1$ of the $4$ red ones: $\dbinom92\cdot4=144$. So the probability is
\[
\dfrac{144}{1140}=\boxed{\dfrac{12}{95}}.
\]
The poster's $\dfrac{4}{20}\cdot\dfrac{9}{19}\cdot\dfrac{8}{19}$ has two errors: the last denominator should be $18$, and $\dfrac{4}{20}\cdot\dfrac{9}{19}\cdot\dfrac{8}{18}=\dfrac{4}{95}$ is the probability of the order red, blue, blue only. The red marble can be drawn first, second or third, so the correct answer is $3\cdot\dfrac{4}{95}=\dfrac{12}{95}$.
