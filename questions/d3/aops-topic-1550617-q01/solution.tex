The radius to a point of tangency is perpendicular to that side, so the apothem of the hexagon is $1$. Joining the centre $O$ to the six vertices cuts the hexagon into six equilateral triangles of side $s$, each with height $1$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
real R=2/sqrt(3);
pair[] V; for(int i=0;i<6;++i) V.push(R*dir(60*i));
pair O=(0,0);
fill(O--V[0]--V[1]--cycle,paleyellow);
draw(V[0]--V[1]--V[2]--V[3]--V[4]--V[5]--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(unitcircle,blue);
pair M=(V[0]+V[1])/2;
draw(O--M,red);draw(ra(M,O-M,V[0]-M,0.09),red);
draw(O--V[0],dashed);draw(O--V[1],dashed);
dot(O);label("$O$",O,SW);
label("$1$",0.5*M+0.13*dir(-60),red);
label("$s$",(V[1]+V[2])/2,N);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The height of an equilateral triangle of side $s$ is $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}s$, so $s=\dfrac{2}{\sqrt3}$. The area of the hexagon is
\[
6\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot s\cdot1=3s=\dfrac{6}{\sqrt3}=\boxed{2\sqrt3}.
\]
