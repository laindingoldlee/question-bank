The egg block and chilaquiles block overlap only at the mixed dish. Therefore the six non-bean dishes must form one block, with the three egg-only dishes on one side of the mixed dish and the two chilaquiles-only dishes on the other. Choose which side holds each group in two ways, then order the groups in $3!2!$ ways. The bean dish can go before or after this six-dish block, giving two more choices. The total is $\boxed{2\cdot3!\cdot2!\cdot2=48}$.
