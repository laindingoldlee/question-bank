Let current age be $ a $ and elapsed time $ t $, with $ a>t>0 $. Since $\gcd(a,a-t)=\gcd(a,t)=4 $, write $ a=4u,t=4v $ with coprime $ u>v $. The lcm condition gives $ 4uv=48 $, so $ uv=12 $. The coprime factor pairs with $ u>v $ are $(12,1)$ and $(4,3)$. The smaller current age is $ 4\cdot4=\boxed{16}$, with elapsed time 12 and meeting age 4.
