A sim-racing setup consists of one wheelbase, one rim, one set of pedals, one rig and one shifter, together with at most $2$ accessories. There are $3$ wheelbases, $11$ rims, $2$ sets of pedals, $4$ rigs, $5$ shifters and $9$ different accessories to choose from. The accessories are optional (a setup may have $0$, $1$ or $2$ of them), and a setup cannot contain the same accessory twice. How many different setups are possible?
