The first $40$ miles take $\dfrac{40}{64}=0.625$ h and the remaining $80$ miles take $\dfrac{80}{74}\approx1.0811$ h, for a total of about $1.7061$ h. The average speed is $\dfrac{120}{1.7061}\approx\boxed{70.3\text{ mph}}$ (exactly $\dfrac{120}{\dfrac{505}{296}}=\dfrac{7104}{101}\approx70.34$).
