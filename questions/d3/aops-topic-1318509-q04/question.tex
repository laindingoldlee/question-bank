On a $120$-mile drive from St. Louis to Columbia, a driver averaged $64$ mph from St. Louis to Wentzville and $74$ mph from Wentzville to Columbia. Wentzville is one third of the way from St. Louis to Columbia. What was the average speed for the whole drive, to the nearest tenth of a mile per hour?
