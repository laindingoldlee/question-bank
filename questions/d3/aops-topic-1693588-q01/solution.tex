The removed cards are $k$ and $k+1$ with $1\le k\le n-1$, so
\[
\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}-(2k+1)=1224,\qquad 3\le2k+1\le2n-1 .
\]
Hence $1227\le\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}\le1223+2n$. Now $\dfrac{49\cdot50}{2}=1225<1227$, and for $n\ge51$, $\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}-2n=\dfrac{n(n-3)}{2}\ge1224>1223$. So $n=50$, $\dfrac{50\cdot51}{2}=1275$, $2k+1=51$ and $k=25$. Then $k-20=\boxed{5}$.
