Each entry is one modulo 30. A subset of size $k\ge2$ therefore has sum congruent to $k$ modulo 30. For $k=2,4,6$ its sum is even; for $k=3$ divisible by three; and for $k=5$ divisible by five. Each sum is larger than that divisor, so all $2^6-1-6=57$ subsets of size at least two qualify. Among singleton sums, only $91=7\cdot13$ and $121=11^2$ are composite: $31,61,151$ are prime and one is neither. Hence the answer is $\boxed{59}$.
