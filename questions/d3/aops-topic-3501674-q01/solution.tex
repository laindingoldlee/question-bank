The expression is $(2x+\dfrac{3}{x})^3$. AM-GM gives $2x+\dfrac{3}{x}\ge2\sqrt6$, with equality when $2x=\dfrac{3}{x}$, or $x=\sqrt{\dfrac{3}{2}}$. Cubing yields minimum $(2\sqrt6)^3=\boxed{48\sqrt6}$.
