Pair the $5$ with an even factor: $4.6\times5=23$. Then $23\times2.1=48.3$ and
\[
48.3\times3.8=48.3\times4-48.3\times0.2=193.2-9.66=183.54 .
\]
The exact value is $183.54$, and the nearest integer is $\boxed{184}$.
