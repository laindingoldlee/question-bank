Order the set increasingly. Its two smallest sum to $-5$, and its two largest to $12$. For at least four entries, these four sum to $7$. The second-largest integer is at most $5$, so all additional middle entries are distinct integers at most $4$. Their sum is at most $1+2+3+4=10$, even if zero or negatives are included. Thus the total is at most $17$. A three-entry set has middle entry at least $-2$, so its total $7-\text{middle}$ is at most $9$. The set $\{-5,0,1,2,3,4,5,7\}$ attains $17$ and has the required extreme pair sums. Hence $\boxed{17}$.
