Two runners race over $420$ metres. Oron runs at $8$ metres per second. Independently on each of seven $60$-metre segments, Salmon runs at $5$ metres per second with probability $\dfrac{3}{5}$ and at $10$ metres per second with probability $\dfrac{2}{5}$. What is the probability Salmon wins?
