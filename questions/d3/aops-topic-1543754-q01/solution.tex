Let $m=n+1\ge2$, so the number consists of $m$ fours, $m-1$ eights and a final $9$. Using $\underbrace{11\ldots1}_{k}=\dfrac{10^k-1}{9}$, the number is
\[
X=4\cdot10^{m}\cdot\dfrac{10^m-1}{9}+8\cdot10\cdot\dfrac{10^{m-1}-1}{9}+9 .
\]
Simplify:
\[
9X=4\cdot10^{2m}-4\cdot10^m+8\cdot10^m-80+81=4\cdot10^{2m}+4\cdot10^m+1=\left(2\cdot10^m+1\right)^2 .
\]
Since $2\cdot10^m+1$ has digit sum $3$, it is divisible by $3$, and
\[
X=\left(\dfrac{2\cdot10^m+1}{3}\right)^2=\big(\underbrace{66\ldots6}_{m-1}7\big)^2
\]
is the square of an integer. For example $4489=67^2$, $444889=667^2$, $44448889=6667^2$. $\blacksquare$
