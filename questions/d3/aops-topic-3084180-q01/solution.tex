The left side minus the right side equals \[\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}\left(a_i-\dfrac{a_n}{2}\right)^2+\dfrac{5-n}{4}a_n^2.\]It is always nonnegative for $ n\le5 $. For $ n>5 $, set $ a_1=\cdots=a_{n-1}=1 $ and $ a_n=2 $. The difference becomes $ 5-n<0 $. Hence exactly $\boxed{n=2,3,4,5}$ work.
