Three trolls, $AA$, $BB$, and $CC$, divided the stones they had collected during the day in a cave. $AA$ and $BB$ each received $12$ stones, while the remaining stones went to $CC$, who could not count. During the night, $AA$ stole one stone from $BB$, $BB$ stole one stone from $CC$, and $CC$ stole one stone from $AA$.  As a result, the average mass of the stones belonging to $AA$ decreased by $1$ gram, the average mass of the stones belonging to $BB$ decreased by $2$ grams, while the average mass of the stones belonging to $CC$ increased by $4$ grams. How many stones did $CC$ receive?
