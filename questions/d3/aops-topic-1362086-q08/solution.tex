Let the width be $w$, so the length is $3w$. Equal areas give
\[
(3w-9)(w+4)=3w\cdot w\iff 3w^2+3w-36=3w^2\iff w=12 .
\]
The original rectangle is $36$ by $12$ (area $432$) and the new one is $27$ by $16$ (area $432$).

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((0,0)--(36,0)--(36,12)--(0,12)--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw((0,0)--(27,0)--(27,16)--(0,16)--cycle,red+dashed);
label("$36$",(18,0),S); label("$12$",(36,6),E);
label("$27$",(13.5,16),N,red); label("$16$",(27,8),W,red);
label("solid: original $36\times12$;\quad dashed: new $27\times16$",(18,-5.5),fontsize(8pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The perimeter of the original rectangle is $2(36+12)=\boxed{96}$ feet. (The new rectangle's perimeter is $86$ feet.)
