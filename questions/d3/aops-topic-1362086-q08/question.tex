The length of a rectangle is three times its width. A new rectangle is created by decreasing the length of the original rectangle by $9$ feet and increasing its width by $4$ feet. The area of the new rectangle is the same as the area of the original rectangle. What is the perimeter of the original rectangle, in feet?
