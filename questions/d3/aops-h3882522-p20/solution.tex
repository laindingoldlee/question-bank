The map $\omega\mapsto\omega^6$ takes the primitive $396$th roots onto the primitive $66$th roots, with six preimages each: there are $\phi(396)=120$ and $\phi(66)=20$ roots respectively, and reduction of the exponents coprime to $396$ onto residues coprime to $66$ has constant fiber size $6$.

For $c_N(i)=\sum_{\zeta\text{ primitive }N}\zeta^i$, the finite geometric-sum identity and inclusion-exclusion on divisors give
\[
c_N(i)=\sum_{d\mid\gcd(N,i)}d\,\mu(\dfrac{N}{d}),
\]
where $\mu(m)=0$ if a square prime factor divides $m$, and otherwise $\mu(m)=(-1)^r$ when $m$ has $r$ distinct prime factors. With $N=66=2\cdot3\cdot11$, the values for $1\le i\le11$ are
\[
\begin{cases}
-1,&i=1,5,7,\\
1,&i=2,4,8,10,\\
2,&i=3,9,\\
-2,&i=6,\\
10,&i=11.
\end{cases}
\]
Thus the requested sum is
\[
6[-(1+5+7)+(2+4+8+10)+2(3+9)-2(6)+10(11)]
=\boxed{798}.
\]
