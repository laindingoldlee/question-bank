Set $A=0,E=tC,F=sB$. Since $D$ internally divides $EF$ in ratio $3:5$, $D = \dfrac{5tC+3sB}{8}$. Its membership in $BC$ gives $5t+3s=8$. Also $E^{\prime}=(1-t)C,F^{\prime}=(1-s)B$, so membership in their line gives $\dfrac{5 t}{1-t} + \dfrac{3 s}{1-s} = 8$. Solving yields $t = \dfrac{4}{5} , s = \dfrac{4}{3}$. Thus $E E^{\prime} = (\dfrac{3}{5}) A C$ and $F F^{\prime} = (\dfrac{5}{3}) A B$, implying $\dfrac{A C}{A B} = \dfrac{25}{27}$. Hence $\dfrac{A E}{A F} = (\dfrac{t}{s}) (\dfrac{A C}{A B}) = \boxed{\dfrac{5}{9}}$.
