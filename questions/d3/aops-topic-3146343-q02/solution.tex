Each entry is at most 118, so none is divisible by 121. Thus at least two are divisible by 11. All three cannot be, since 120 is not divisible by 11. Choose the one not divisible by 11 in 3 ways. Write the other two as $11u,11v$, with $u,v\ge1$ and $u+v\le10$. There are $1+2+\cdots+9=45$ ordered pairs. The remaining entry $120-11(u+v)$ is positive and automatically not divisible by 11. Total: $\boxed{135}$.
