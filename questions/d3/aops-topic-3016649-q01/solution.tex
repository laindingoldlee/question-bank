Choose $A=(0,0,0),B=(b,0,0),D=(0,d,0),C=(b,d,0),M=(0,0,h)$ with $b,d,h>0$. Projection onto $MB$ gives $P=(\dfrac{bh^2}{b^2+h^2},0,\dfrac{hb^2}{b^2+h^2})$. Its position vector has dot product zero with $\overrightarrow{MC}=(b,d,-h)$. Since $AP$ and $MC$ are nonzero, they are perpendicular.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(5,0),D=(1.5,2),C=B+D,M=(0,4),P=(80/41,100/41);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(M--A^^M--B^^M--C);draw(A--P,blue);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$M$",M,N);label("$P$",P,E);
\end{asy}
