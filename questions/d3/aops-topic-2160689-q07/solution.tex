The area of a trapezoid is the average of the bases times the height:
\[\dfrac{12+6}{2}\cdot9=9\cdot9=\boxed{81}.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0), B=(12,0), C=(8,9), D=(2,9);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(D--(2,0),dashed); draw((2,0)--(2.7,0)--(2.7,0.7)--(2,0.7));
label("$12$",(A+B)/2,S); label("$6$",(C+D)/2,N); label("$9$",(2,4.5),E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
