Long division by place value: $1035=1000+0\cdot100+30+5$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $10$ hundreds divided by $5$ is $2$ hundreds, with nothing left over.
\item The next digit is $3$ tens, and $3$ tens divided by $5$ gives $0$ tens. This $0$ must be written in the quotient to hold the tens place.
\item The $3$ tens and $5$ ones make $35$ ones, and $35\div5=7$ ones.
\end{itemize}
So $1035\div5=2\text{ hundreds}+0\text{ tens}+7\text{ ones}=\boxed{207}$. Writing $27$ means $2$ tens and $7$ ones; leaving out the $0$ shifts the $2$ from the hundreds place to the tens place. Check: $5\cdot207=1035$, while $5\cdot27=135$. Also $1035\div5$ must be more than $1000\div5=200$, so $27$ is far too small.
