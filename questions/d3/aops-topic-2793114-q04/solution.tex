Units give $d=0$ with no carry. Tens give $a+c=b+10u$, where $u=0$ or 1. Let $v$ be the carry from hundreds. Thousands give $2a+v=10c$. Since $v=0$ or 1, parity forces $v=0$; hence $a=5,c=1$. Tens then give $b=6,u=0$, and hundreds give $e=7$. The unique solution is $\boxed{(a,b,c,d,e)=(5,6,1,0,7)}$, verified by $5610+5151=10761$.
