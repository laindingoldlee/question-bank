Colour the grid like a chessboard with the corners black; there are $13$ black and $12$ white squares, and the centre is black, so the remaining $24$ squares are balanced. Count tilings by scanning the squares in reading order and always covering the first uncovered square by a domino going right or down (a transfer-matrix count over the $5$ rows, keeping track of which squares of the next row are already covered). Carrying this out row by row gives
\[\boxed{196}\]
tilings.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(2.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
for(int i=0;i<5;++i) for(int j=0;j<5;++j) draw(shift(i,j)*unitsquare,gray); fill(shift(2,2)*unitsquare,gray);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
