Suppose $p$ and $q$ are primes with $p-q=7$. Since $7$ is odd, $p$ and $q$ have different parity, so one of them is even. The only even prime is $2$, and as $p>q$, we get $q=2$ and $p=9$. But $9=3\cdot3$ is not prime. Hence no two primes differ by $7$. $\blacksquare$
