Let
\[c=\dfrac{\sqrt5-1}{4}\dfrac{\sqrt6+\sqrt2}{4}-\dfrac{\sqrt{10+2\sqrt5}}{4}\dfrac{\sqrt6-\sqrt2}{4}.\]
Prove that $\sin1^\circ$ is the unique root in $(0,\dfrac{1}{2})$ of $4s^3-3s+c=0$.
