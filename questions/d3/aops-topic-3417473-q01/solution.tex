Announce publicly: ``At least one person has green eyes.'' We prove that with $n$ green-eyed people, they leave at the $n$th midnight. If $n=1$, that person sees no green eyes and immediately infers their own colour, leaving at the first midnight. For the induction step, each green-eyed person sees $n-1$ green-eyed people. If their own eyes were not green, those others would leave at midnight $n-1$ by the induction hypothesis. When they do not, the observer deduces that their own eyes are green. All make this deduction together and leave at midnight $n$. Therefore all $100$ leave at the $\boxed{100\text{th midnight}}$. The public announcement establishes the common knowledge needed for the nested reasoning, even though each person initially sees green eyes.
