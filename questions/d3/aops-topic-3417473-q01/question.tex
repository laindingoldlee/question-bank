On an island, $100$ perfect logicians all have green eyes. Each sees everyone else's eye colour but not their own. They may leave at midnight only once they know their own eye colour; all departures are observed by everyone. The rules and everyone's perfect reasoning are common knowledge. Give a truthful public announcement about eye colour that enables everyone to leave, and determine when they leave after the announcement.
