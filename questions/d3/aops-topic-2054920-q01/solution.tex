The relation is linear with slope $\dfrac{5}{9}$, so a change $\Delta F$ produces the change $\Delta C=\dfrac{5}{9}\Delta F$, and conversely $\Delta F=\dfrac{9}{5}\Delta C$.
\begin{itemize}
\item I: $\Delta F=1$ gives $\Delta C=\dfrac{5}{9}$. True.
\item II: $\Delta C=1$ gives $\Delta F=\dfrac{9}{5}=1.8$. True.
\item III: $\Delta F=\dfrac{5}{9}$ gives $\Delta C=\dfrac{25}{81}\ne1$. False.
\end{itemize}
The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(D)}\ \text{I and II only}}$.
