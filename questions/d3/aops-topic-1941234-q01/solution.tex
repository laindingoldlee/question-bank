The multiples of $3$ that are $1$ more than a multiple of $7$ are $15,36,57,78,99,\dots$ (they repeat every $21$). Among these, $78$ is the only one with $x\equiv3\pmod5$ in the range up to $105$. By the Chinese remainder theorem the solutions are $x\equiv78\pmod{105}$, so the solutions below $100$ are $78$ and the negative numbers $78-105k$. The largest is $\boxed{78}$.
