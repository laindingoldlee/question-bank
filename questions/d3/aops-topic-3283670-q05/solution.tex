For one triangle area $T$, $1-4T=\dfrac{4}{3}T$, so $T=\dfrac{3}{16}$ and $ab=\dfrac{3}{8}$. Since $a^2+b^2=1$, we have $a+b=\dfrac{\sqrt7}{2}$ and $a-b=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Adding gives $\boxed{a=\dfrac{\sqrt7+1}{4}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
real a=(sqrt(7)+1)/4,b=(sqrt(7)-1)/4;pair C=(a*a,a*b);path t=(0,0)--(1,0)--C--cycle;for(int i=0;i<4;++i)filldraw(shift(.5,.5)*rotate(90*i)*shift(-.5,-.5)*t,lightgray);label("$1$",(.5,0),S);label("$a$",C/2,NW);label("$b$",((1,0)+C)/2,NE);
\end{asy}
