$15^2+13^2=225+169=394$, $14^2+12^2=196+144=340$, $10^2+11^2=100+121=221$. So $a\#b=a^2+b^2$ fits, and $15\#16=225+256=\boxed{481}$. (Other rules could also fit three values; this one is the natural choice.)
