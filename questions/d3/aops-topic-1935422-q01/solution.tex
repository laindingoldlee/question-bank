The deck has area $10\cdot15=150$ square feet, so at $c$ dollars per square foot it costs $150c$ dollars. The budget gives
\[
150c\le900\iff c\le6 .
\]
So you can pay at most $\boxed{\$6}$ per square foot (any price $0<c\le6$ is affordable).
