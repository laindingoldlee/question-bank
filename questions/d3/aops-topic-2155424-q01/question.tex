Two circles with centres $S$ and $T$ touch each other externally. A common tangent of the two circles, not passing through their point of contact, touches the circle with centre $S$ at $A$ and the circle with centre $T$ at $B$. Let $AP$ be a diameter of the circle with centre $S$, and let the tangent from $P$ to the circle with centre $T$ touch it at $Q$. Prove that $AP=PQ$.
