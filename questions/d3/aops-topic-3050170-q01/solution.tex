Writing $ z=a+bi $ gives $ a^2-b^2=0 $, $ 2ab=1 $. Therefore $ a=b=\pm\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}$, with the same sign. The two roots are $\boxed{\pm\dfrac{1+i}{\sqrt2}}$. The root with positive real part, $\dfrac{1+i}{\sqrt2}$, is the principal square root.
