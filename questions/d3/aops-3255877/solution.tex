Put $u_n=F_n^2$. The Fibonacci recurrence implies $u_{n+3}=2u_{n+2}+2u_{n+1}-u_n$: expand $F_{n+2}=F_{n+1}+F_n$ to verify it. Since $u_0=0,u_1=u_2=1$, the generating function is $U (t) = \dfrac{t-t^2}{1-2t-2t^2+t^3}$. This identity follows by multiplying the recurrence by $t^{n+3}$ and summing. The series converges at $t = \dfrac{1}{9}$, since $F_n\le2^n$. Substitution gives $S = \dfrac{36}{275}$, already reduced. Thus $\boxed{m+n=311}$.
