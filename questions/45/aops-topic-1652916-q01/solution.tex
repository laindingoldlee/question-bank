There are $32-x$ twenty-dollar bills, so
\[
5x+20(32-x)=430\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 640-15x=430\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x=14 .
\]
The teller has $\boxed{14}$ five-dollar bills (and $18$ twenty-dollar bills). Check: $14+18=32$ bills and $5\cdot14+20\cdot18=70+360=430$ dollars.
