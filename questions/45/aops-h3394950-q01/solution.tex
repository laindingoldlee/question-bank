From the sum of twentieth powers, each of $|a|,|b|,|c|$ is at most $1$. For $0\le t\le1$, $t^{20}\ge t^{24}$, with equality only at $t=0$ or $1$. Equality of the two sums therefore forces every absolute value to be $0$ or $1$. Their twentieth powers sum to $1$, so exactly one coordinate is $1$ or $-1$ and the other two are zero. There are $\boxed6$ ordered triples.
