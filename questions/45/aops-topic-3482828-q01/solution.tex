Use coordinates $A=(0,0),B=(8,0),D=(0,h),C=(12,h)$ with $h>0$. Then $M=(6,h)$ and $N=(10,\dfrac{h}{2})$. Perpendicularity at $M$ gives $(-6,-h)\cdot(4,-\dfrac{h}{2})=0$, or $h^2=48$. Therefore $AM=\sqrt{36+48}=\boxed{2\sqrt{21}}$.

usepackage("amsmath");

pair A=(0,0),B=(8,0),D=(0,4sqrt(3)),C=(12,4sqrt(3)),M=(C+D)/2,N=(B+C)/2;draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--M--N,dashed);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$M$",M,(0,1));label("$N$",N,E);
\end{asy}
