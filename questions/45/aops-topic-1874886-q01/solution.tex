The cars approach each other at $40+50=90$ miles per hour, so they meet after
\[
\dfrac{210}{90}=\dfrac{7}{3}\text{ hours}=2\text{ hours }20\text{ minutes}.
\]
They meet at $\boxed{2{:}20\text{ p.m.}}$ (Check: car $A$ has gone $40\cdot\dfrac{7}{3}=\dfrac{280}{3}$ miles and car $B$ has gone $50\cdot\dfrac{7}{3}=\dfrac{350}{3}$ miles, a total of $210$.)
