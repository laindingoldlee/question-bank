Points $O$, $A$, $B$ lie on one line, with $OA=12$ cm and $OB=9$ cm. Find the distance between the midpoints of the segments $OA$ and $OB$ if
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] the point $O$ lies on the segment $AB$;
\item[(b)] the point $O$ does not lie on the segment $AB$.
\end{enumerate}
