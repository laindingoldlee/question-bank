Let $S_n=\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^n\dfrac{k}{2^k}$. Then
\[
S_n-\dfrac{1}{2}S_n=\sum_{k=1}^n\dfrac{k}{2^k}-\sum_{k=1}^{n}\dfrac{k}{2^{k+1}}=\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{4}+\cdots+\dfrac{1}{2^n}-\dfrac{n}{2^{n+1}}=1-\dfrac{1}{2^n}-\dfrac{n}{2^{n+1}} .
\]
So $S_n=2-\dfrac{2}{2^n}-\dfrac{n}{2^n}=2-\dfrac{n+2}{2^n}$. As $n\to\infty$, $\dfrac{n+2}{2^n}\to0$, so the series converges and
\[
\sum_{k=1}^\infty\dfrac{k}{2^k}=\boxed{2}.
\]
