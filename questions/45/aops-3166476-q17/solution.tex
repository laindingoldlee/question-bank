The triangle is right at $A$. Its altitude to hypotenuse $BC$ is $AE=\dfrac{15\cdot20}{25}=12$, and $CE=\dfrac{15^2}{25}=9$. Since $CD=4$, $DE=5$. The exterior point $F$ lies seven beyond $A$, so $EF=19$. The right triangle $DEF$ gives $DF^2=5^2+19^2=\boxed{386}$.
