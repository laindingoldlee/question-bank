Gabriel and Isabel each start with a pile of $20$ coins, each coin a nickel, a dime or a quarter. Gabriel gives Isabel $2$ coins, and then Isabel gives Gabriel $5$ coins. Afterwards Gabriel's pile is worth twice as much as Isabel's pile. Among all the ways this can happen, consider those in which the combined value of the two piles is as large as possible. In those cases, what is the least number of dimes Isabel could end up with?
