The domain excludes $0,-1,-2$. Multiplying by $x(x+1)(x+2)$ gives $(x+1)(x+2)+x(x+2)=x(x+1)$, hence $x^2+4x+2=0$. Thus $\boxed{x=-2\pm\sqrt2}$. Neither root is excluded, so both solve the original equation.
