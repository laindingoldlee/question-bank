For a square with $A=(0,0)$, $B=(s,0)$, $C=(s,s)$, $D=(0,s)$, expansion of the four squared distances shows $PA^2+PC^2=PB^2+PD^2$. Thus $PC^2=1+49-25=25$, giving $\boxed5$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
real s=sqrt(32);pair A=(0,0),Bp=(s,0),C=(s,s),D=(0,s),P=((s^2+24)/(2*s),(s^2-24)/(2*s));draw(A--Bp--C--D--cycle);draw(A--P--Bp);draw(C--P--D);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",Bp,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$P$",P,N);
\end{asy}
