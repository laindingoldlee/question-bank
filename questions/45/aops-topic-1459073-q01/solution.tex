Express everything through $a$ and $b$: $c=a+b$, $d=b+c=a+2b$, and then $a=c+d=2a+3b$, so $a=-3b$. Hence $c=-2b$, $d=-b$ and
\[
a+b+c+d=-3b+b-2b-b=-5b .
\]
Since $b$ is a positive integer, this is largest when $b=1$, giving $\boxed{-5}$ (with $(a,b,c,d)=(-3,1,-2,-1)$).

The value $0$ would need $b=0$, which is not allowed.
