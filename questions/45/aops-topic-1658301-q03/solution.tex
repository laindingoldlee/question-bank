Let there be $r$ red marbles; then there are $r+2$ blue, $r+4$ white and $3r+6$ in all. The probability of two reds without replacement is
\[
\dfrac{r}{3r+6}\cdot\dfrac{r-1}{3r+5}.
\]
The condition $\dfrac{r(r-1)}{(3r+6)(3r+5)}<\dfrac{1}{15}$ becomes (the denominators are positive)
\[
15r^2-15r<9r^2+33r+30\iff6r^2-48r-30<0\iff r^2-8r-5<0\iff r<4+\sqrt{21}\approx8.58 .
\]
So $r\le8$ and the bag holds at most $3\cdot8+6=\boxed{30}$ marbles. Check: for $r=8$ the probability is $\dfrac{8\cdot7}{30\cdot29}=\dfrac{28}{435}<\dfrac{29}{435}=\dfrac{1}{15}$, while for $r=9$ it is $\dfrac{72}{1056}=\dfrac{3}{44}>\dfrac{1}{15}$.
