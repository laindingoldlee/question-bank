A bag contains only red, blue and white marbles. There are two more blue marbles than red marbles, and two more white marbles than blue marbles. Given that the probability of drawing two red marbles in a row (without replacement) is less than $\dfrac{1}{15}$, what is the largest possible number of marbles in the bag?
