Put $m=n-1$. The binomial theorem gives $(m+1)^{2016}-1\equiv2016m\pmod{m^2}$. Thus the divisibility holds exactly when $m\mid2016$. We need the largest divisor $m\le997$. Since $2016=2^5\cdot3^2\cdot7$, the greatest divisor below $1008=\dfrac{2016}{2}$ is $672=\dfrac{2016}{3}$: any divisor larger than $672$ has complementary divisor less than $3$, hence is $1008$ or $2016$. Both are too large. Therefore $n=672+1=\boxed{673}$.
