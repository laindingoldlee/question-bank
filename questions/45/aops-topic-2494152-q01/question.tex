Individually, Dave needs $8$ hours to shovel all of Kevin's snow, John needs $6$ hours to shovel all of Kevin's snow, and Allison needs $4$ hours to shovel all of Kevin's snow. If Kevin, Dave, John and Allison all work together, how many minutes do they need to shovel all of Kevin's snow?
