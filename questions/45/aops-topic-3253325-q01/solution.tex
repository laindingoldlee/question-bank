The identity $(z+w)^2=(z-w)^2+4zw$ gives $(z+w)^2=-4+104=100$. Thus $\boxed{z+w=10\text{ or }-10}$. Both occur: use $z=5+i,w=5-i$, or $z=-5+i,w=-5-i$. Complex numbers do not have a usual positive-difference ordering, so the specified directed difference is used.
