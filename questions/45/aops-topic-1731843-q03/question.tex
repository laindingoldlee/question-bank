Don's signature coffee blend is $60\%$ dark roast and $40\%$ light roast. He has $10$ kg of blend A, which is $80\%$ dark roast and $20\%$ light roast, and $10$ kg of blend B, which is $20\%$ dark roast and $80\%$ light roast. How many kilograms of blend A will Don need to use to make $10$ kg of his signature blend? Express your answer as a mixed number.
