We have $\dfrac{1}{7}=0.\overline{142857}$ and $\dfrac{2}{7}=0.\overline{285714}$. Prove that for every $k=1,2,\dots,6$ the repeating block of the decimal of $\dfrac{k}{7}$ is a cyclic shift of $142857$ (the same digits in the same cyclic order, starting at a different place).
