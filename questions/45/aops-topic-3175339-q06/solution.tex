The condition is exactly $n\mid2016$ with $n>2$. Since $2016=2^5\cdot3^2\cdot7$, its divisor sum is $(1+2+\cdots+32)(1+3+9)(1+7)=63\cdot13\cdot8=6552$. Remove divisors $1,2$ to obtain $\boxed{6549}$.
