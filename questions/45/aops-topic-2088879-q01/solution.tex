Rationalising,
\[a=\dfrac{1}{10-\sqrt{99}}=\dfrac{10+\sqrt{99}}{(10-\sqrt{99})(10+\sqrt{99})}=\dfrac{10+\sqrt{99}}{100-99}=10+\sqrt{99}.\]
Since $9.5^2=90.25<99<100=10^2$, we have $9.5<\sqrt{99}<10$, so $19.5<a<20$. To the nearest integer, $a\approx\boxed{20}$.
