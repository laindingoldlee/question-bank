Since $AD=DC$, the rectangle is a square, and $ACD$ is an isosceles right triangle with hypotenuse $AC=20\sqrt2$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(1,0),C=(1,1),D=(0,1);
fill(A--C--D--cycle,lightgray);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.8));draw(A--C,dashed);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);
label("$20\sqrt2$",(0.5,0.5),SE);label("$20$",(0,0.5),W);label("$20$",(0.5,1),N);
\end{asy}

Its legs are $\dfrac{20\sqrt2}{\sqrt2}=20$, so its area is $\dfrac12\cdot20\cdot20=\boxed{200}$.
