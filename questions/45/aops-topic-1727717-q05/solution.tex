There are $8\cdot7\cdot6\cdot5=1680$ placements. The cube has $24$ rotations. A rotation other than the identity fixes at most $2$ vertices (a rotation about an axis through two opposite vertices fixes those two; the other rotations fix none), so it cannot fix a placement, which needs four vertices to stay in place. Hence each placement has exactly $24$ different images, the placements fall into classes of $24$, and the number of different arrangements is $\dfrac{1680}{24}=\boxed{70}$.
