In each case look for two numbers with the given sum and product.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $5+7=12$ and $5\cdot7=35$, so $x^2+12x+35=(x+5)(x+7)$ and $\boxed{x=-5\text{ or }x=-7}$.
\item[(b)] $44+100=144$ and $44\cdot100=4400$, so $x^2+144x+4400=(x+44)(x+100)$ and $\boxed{x=-44\text{ or }x=-100}$. (The discriminant is $144^2-4\cdot4400=3136=56^2$, giving $x=\dfrac{-144\pm56}{2}$, the same roots.)
\item[(c)] $12+13=25$ and $12\cdot13=156$, so $x^2+25x+156=(x+12)(x+13)$ and $\boxed{x=-12\text{ or }x=-13}$.
\end{enumerate}
