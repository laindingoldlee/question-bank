Heather places $5$ red balls and $5$ green balls in any combination into two identical boxes (each box gets at least one ball). A box is chosen at random, and then a ball is chosen at random from that box. Heather wins if a green ball is drawn. What is the probability that Heather wins if she uses the distribution of balls that maximizes her chance of winning? Express your answer as a common fraction.
