Triangle $ABC$ has $AB=4$, $BC=5$, $CA=6$. Points $D\ne B$ and $E\ne C$ lie on lines $AB,AC$ and satisfy $BD=CE$. Assume triangles $ADE$ and $ABC$ have distinct circumcircles, meeting again at $P\ne A$. Find the sum of the distinct possible values of $AP^2$, and express it as $\dfrac{p}{q}$ in lowest terms; find $p+q$.
