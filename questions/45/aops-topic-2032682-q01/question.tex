Find all $m$ such that both $\dfrac{1}{m+3}$ and $\dfrac{m+5}{m+3}$ are integers,
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] if $m$ is required to be an integer;
\item[(b)] if $m$ may be any real number.
\end{enumerate}
