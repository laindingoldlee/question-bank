One swimmer covers $\dfrac{9}{8}$ times another's distance in $\dfrac{5}{9}$ times the other's time. The second swimmer's speed is 3 kilometres per hour. Write the first's speed as $\dfrac{m}{n}$ in lowest terms and find $m+n$.
