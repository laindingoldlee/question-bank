Let the number be $10a+b$ with digits $a$ (tens) and $b$ (units). Then $a+b=14$ and
\[
(10a+b)+2(10b+a)=12a+21b=222 .
\]
Substituting $b=14-a$: $12a+294-21a=222$, so $9a=72$, $a=8$ and $b=6$. The number is $\boxed{86}$.

Check: $8+6=14$ and $86+2\cdot68=86+136=222$.
