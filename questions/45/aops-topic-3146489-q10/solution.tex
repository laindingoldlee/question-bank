There are $\binom93=84$ equally likely subsets. With $1$ included, the other two numbers must sum to $2$ modulo $3$. They can both be residue $1$, which forces the pair $4,7$, or one residue $0$ from $3,6,9$ and one residue $2$ from $2,5,8$, giving nine pairs. Thus ten subsets win, and the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{10}{84}=\dfrac{5}{42}}$.
