Let $H,T,U$ be the sums of the hundreds, tens, and units digits. Then $H+T+U=45$, and the sum of the primes is $100H+10T+U=90H+450-9U$. The three units digits must be distinct members of $\{1,3,7,9\}$, so $U\ge11$. Also $H\le23$: the only three-digit set with sum greater than $23$ is $\{7,8,9\}$, which would leave only $1,3$ available as prime endings. Thus the total is at most $90\cdot23+450-9\cdot11=2421$. This is attained by $641,827,953$. Trial division by primes up to their square roots verifies that all three are prime. Hence $\boxed{2421}$.
