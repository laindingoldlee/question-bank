Triangle $ABC$ has a right angle at $A$, $AB=c$ and $AC=b$. Points $P$ on $AC$ and $Q$ on $AB$ satisfy $\angle APQ=\angle ABC$ and $\angle AQP=\angle ACB$. Let $E$ and $F$ be the feet of the perpendiculars from $P$ and $Q$ to $BC$. Find $PQ+PE+QF$ in terms of $b$ and $c$.
