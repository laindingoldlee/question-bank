Define $x\otimes y=\dfrac{x+y}{1-xy}$. Let $u=2+\sqrt3$, $v=\sqrt3$ and $w=2-\sqrt3$. Compute
\[
\left(\left(\left(u\otimes v\right)\otimes w\right)\otimes v\right)\otimes u
\]
without using trigonometry.
