Let $n=\overline{yz}=10y+z$. Then $p=\dfrac{n}{99}$ and $q=\dfrac{n}{100}$, so
\[
275n\left(\dfrac{1}{99}-\dfrac{1}{100}\right)=\dfrac{275n}{9900}=\dfrac{n}{36}=1,
\]
giving $n=36$: $y=3$, $z=6$. Then $r-p=\dfrac{63-36}{99}=\dfrac{27}{99}=\boxed{0.\overline{27}}=\dfrac{3}{11}$.
