Consider the equation
\[
\sqrt{4-\sqrt{4+x}}+\sqrt{4+\sqrt{4+x}}=6 .
\]
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that the equation has no real solution.
\item[(b)] Squaring both sides twice suggests the ``solution'' $x=-184$. Explain why this value does not solve the equation in the real numbers.
\end{enumerate}
