By Heron's formula, with $s=\dfrac{10+17+21}{2}=24$, the area is
\[
K=\sqrt{24\cdot14\cdot7\cdot3}=\sqrt{7056}=84 .
\]
The circumradius is
\[
R=\dfrac{abc}{4K}=\dfrac{10\cdot17\cdot21}{4\cdot84}=\dfrac{3570}{336}=\dfrac{85}{8}.
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0), C=(21,0), A=(6,8), O=(10.5,-1.625);
real R=10.625;
draw(circle(O,R),blue);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(O--C,red+dashed);
dot(A);dot(B);dot(C);dot(O);
label("$A$",A,N); label("$B$",B,W); label("$C$",C,E); label("$O$",O,S);
label("$21$",(10.5,0),N); label("$10$",(3,4),SE); label("$17$",(13.5,4),NE);
label("$R$",(15.75,-0.8),S,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

(The triangle is obtuse, since $21^2>10^2+17^2$, so the centre $O$ lies outside it, below the longest side.) The circumference is
\[
2\pi R=\boxed{\dfrac{85\pi}{4}},\qquad\text{with radius } R=\dfrac{85}{8}.
\]
