Let Carlos's speed be $c$ mph, so Yuri's is $c-20$. Equal times give
\[
\dfrac{40}{c}=\dfrac{15}{c-20}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 40c-800=15c\quad\Longrightarrow\quad c=32 .
\]
So Carlos rides at $\boxed{32\text{ mph}}$ and Yuri at $\boxed{12\text{ mph}}$ (each takes $\dfrac{5}{4}$ hours).
