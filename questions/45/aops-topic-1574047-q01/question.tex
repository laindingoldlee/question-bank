Which two of the following four statements, labelled $A$ through $D$, are true?
\begin{itemize}
\item[$A$:] Statement $B$ is false, but statement $C$ is true.
\item[$B$:] Statement $C$ is true, but statement $D$ is false.
\item[$C$:] Statement $D$ is false and statement $A$ is false.
\item[$D$:] Statement $A$ is true, and statement $B$ is true.
\end{itemize}
