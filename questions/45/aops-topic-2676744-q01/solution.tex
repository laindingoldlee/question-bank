Since $n\ge1$, $3^n+1\equiv1\pmod3$, so $3\nmid3^n+1$. If $3\mid m$, then $3\mid m\mid3^n+1$, a contradiction. Hence $m\not\equiv0$, i.e. $m\equiv1$ or $2\pmod3$. $\blacksquare$
