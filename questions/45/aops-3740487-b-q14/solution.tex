Each panda is chosen to guard with probability $\dfrac{4}{20}$, and then wins with probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$, so each is the winner with probability $\dfrac{1}{20}$. With independent nights, this is the coupon-collector problem. After $k$ distinct winners have appeared, the probability that the next night gives a new winner is $\dfrac{20-k}{20}$, so the expected wait is $\dfrac{20}{20-k}$. Adding these waiting times gives $20\sum_{j=1}^{20}\dfrac{1}{j}\approx71.9548$, which rounds to $\boxed{72}$, choice C.
