In triangle $ABC$, $\angle B=90^\circ$, $BC=16$ and $AC=20$. Triangle $DEF$ is similar to triangle $ABC$, with $D$, $E$, $F$ corresponding to $A$, $B$, $C$ respectively, and each side of triangle $DEF$ is $\dfrac13$ the length of the corresponding side of triangle $ABC$. What is the value of $\sin F$?
