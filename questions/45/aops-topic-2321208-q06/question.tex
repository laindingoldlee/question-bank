A player's race time $T$ is uniformly distributed between $4$ minutes $30$ seconds and $5$ minutes. The average time $A$ of the whole field is independent of $T$ and uniformly distributed between $7$ minutes $30$ seconds and $14$ minutes. What is the probability that $T$ is less than half of $A$?
