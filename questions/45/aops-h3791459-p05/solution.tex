Each of the four events has probability $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^3=\dfrac{1}{8}$. Left and right events cannot overlap except on a probability-zero boundary, nor can top and bottom. Each of the four remaining pairs means all points lie in one quarter, with probability $\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^3=\dfrac{1}{64}$. Triple intersections have probability zero. Inclusion--exclusion gives $\dfrac{4}{8}-\dfrac{4}{64}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{16}}$.
