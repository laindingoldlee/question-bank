Observe $2029=45^2+2^2$, so $45^2\equiv-4\pmod{2029}$. Dividing by $4$ gives $\left(45\cdot2^{-1}\right)^2\equiv-1$. Since $2^{-1}\equiv1015$, this root is $1037$, and its negative is $992$. Both are roots. There can be at most two roots: if $a^2\equiv b^2$, primality implies $a\equiv b$ or $a\equiv-b$. Thus the only root in $1\le n<1000$ is $\boxed{992}$.
