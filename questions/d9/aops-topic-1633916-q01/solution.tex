The quadratic factors as $x^2-ax-bx+ab=(x-a)(x-b)$, so its roots are $a$ and $b$, which are positive integers, with $p=a+b$ and $q=ab$. Then
\[
p+q+1=ab+a+b+1=(a+1)(b+1)=21 .
\]
Both factors are at least $2$, and $21=3\cdot7$ is the only such factorisation, so $\{a,b\}=\{2,6\}$. Hence $p=8$, $q=12$ and $pq=\boxed{\textbf{(D)}\ 96}$.
