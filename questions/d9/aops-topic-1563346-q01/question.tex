A $6\times6$ grid of $36$ unit squares is to be completely covered by T-shaped pieces, each made of $4$ unit squares (three in a row and one attached to the middle square on one side). The pieces may not overlap each other, but they may stick out over the edge of the grid. What is the fewest number of T-shaped pieces needed to cover the grid completely?
