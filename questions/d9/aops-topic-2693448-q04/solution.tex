For Tim and Bill there are $3$ options (neither, only Tim, only Bill). For John and Betsy there are $3$ (neither, Betsy only, both). The other $6$ people are free: $2^6$. That gives $3\cdot3\cdot64=576$ subsets, of which one is empty, so $\boxed{575}$ committees.
