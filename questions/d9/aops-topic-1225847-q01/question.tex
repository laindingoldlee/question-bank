A student conjectures: ``If the units digit of the positive integer $p$ is not $2$ or $7$, then $p^2+p-1$ is prime.'' Explain where the exception for units digits $2$ and $7$ comes from, and disprove the conjecture by finding the smallest counterexample $p\ge2$.
