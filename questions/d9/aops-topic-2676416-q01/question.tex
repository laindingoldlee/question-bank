A test tube of negligible thickness consists of a hemisphere of diameter $1$ cm attached to a cylinder of diameter $1$ cm and height $8$ cm. It contains $3$ mL of liquid and is held at rest with its axis at an angle of $30^\circ$ to the horizontal, closed end down. Assuming the surface of the liquid is flat and horizontal, what is the vertical depth of the liquid (from the lowest point of the tube to the surface)? ($1$ mL $=1$ cm$^3$.)
