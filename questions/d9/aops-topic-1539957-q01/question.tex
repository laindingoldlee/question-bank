Two vertical flagpoles stand on level ground $12$ feet apart; one is $45$ feet tall and the other $41$ feet tall. A straight wire is stretched from the top of the taller pole, passes over the top of the shorter pole (touching it) and is fixed to the ground on the far side of the shorter pole. How far from the foot of the shorter pole does the wire touch the ground?
