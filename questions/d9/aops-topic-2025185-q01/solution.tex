The Ducks are equally likely to be paired with any of the other three teams, so they meet the Beavers in the first round with probability $\dfrac{1}{3}$.

Otherwise (probability $\dfrac{2}{3}$) they are in different first-round games, and they meet in the final exactly when both win their games, which has probability $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{1}{4}$.

So the probability is
\[
\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{6}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}.
\]
(A quicker view: exactly $3$ games are played among the $\dbinom42=6$ pairs of teams, and by symmetry each pair is equally likely to play, so the probability is $\dfrac{3}{6}$.)
