Since $\left\lfloor\dfrac{9k}{8}\right\rfloor=k+\left\lfloor\dfrac{k}{8}\right\rfloor$, the sum equals $\dfrac{32\cdot33}{2}+[7(0)+8(1)+8(2)+8(3)+1(4)]=528+52=\boxed{580}$.
