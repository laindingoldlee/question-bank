Let the total be $N$. Both $N$ divisible by $9$ and by $6$, so $N$ is a positive multiple of $18$. The number of Pablo-correct but Pedro-wrong questions is $7-\dfrac{N}{6}$, which lies between $0$ and $\dfrac{N}{9}$. Thus $N\le42$ and $7\le\dfrac{5N}{18}$, giving $N\ge\dfrac{126}{5}$. The only multiple of $18$ in this interval is $36$. Pedro got $\dfrac{8}{9}\cdot36=\boxed{32}$ correct.
