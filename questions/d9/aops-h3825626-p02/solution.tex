Modulo $2$, $a^2-b^2\equiv a+b$. Thus the parity of the sum of all remaining numbers is invariant. Initially the sum is $2080$, which is even. Just before the last operation the two remaining numbers therefore have the same parity. Their squares are congruent modulo $4$ (both $0$ if even, both $1$ if odd), so their difference is divisible by $4$.
