Order the side lengths $a\le b\le c$. Perimeter 30 and strict triangle inequality imply $10\le c\le14$. For each c, $a+b=30-c$ and $30-2c\le a\le\lfloor\dfrac{(30-c)}{2}\rfloor$. The numbers of possibilities for c=10,11,12,13,14 are respectively 1,2,4,5,7. Their sum is $\boxed{19}$.
