Comparing windows ending at $i$ and $i-1$ cancels two scores, leaving $a_i>a_{i-3}$ for $i=4,\ldots,10$. Thus the position chains $(1,4,7,10)$, $(2,5,8)$ and $(3,6,9)$ must increase. Choose the four scores of the first chain and the three of the second; all internal orders are forced. The count is $\dfrac{10!}{4!3!3!}=\boxed{4200}$.
