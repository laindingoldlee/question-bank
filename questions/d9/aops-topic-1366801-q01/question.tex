A club has $10$ boys and $10$ girls. A $4$-person committee is chosen at random. What is the probability that the committee has at least $1$ boy and at least $1$ girl? A student computes $\dfrac{\dbinom{10}{1}\dbinom{10}{1}\dbinom{18}{2}}{\dbinom{20}{4}}$ and gets a number greater than $1$. What went wrong?
