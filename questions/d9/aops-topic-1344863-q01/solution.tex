Check the multiples of $7$ greater than $7$ in order, with their digit sums:
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccccccc}
n&14&21&28&35&42&49&56&63&70\\ \hline
\text{digit sum}&5&3&10&8&6&13&11&9&7
\end{array}
\]
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccccccc}
n&77&84&91&98&105&112&119&126&133\\ \hline
\text{digit sum}&14&12&10&17&6&4&11&9&7
\end{array}
\]
The digit sum is a multiple of $7$ for $70$ (excluded: divisible by $10$), $77$ (excluded: divisible by $11$) and then $133$. Since $133=7\cdot19$ is divisible by neither $10$ nor $11$, the answer is $\boxed{133}$.
