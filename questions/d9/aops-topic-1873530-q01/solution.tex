Put $u=f(x)=3-2x$; as $x$ runs over all reals, so does $u$, and $x=\dfrac{3-u}{2}$. Then
\[
g(u)=2\left(\dfrac{3-u}{2}\right)^2-3\cdot\dfrac{3-u}{2}+5=\dfrac{(3-u)^2-3(3-u)}{2}+5=\dfrac{u^2-3u}{2}+5 .
\]
So $\boxed{g(x)=\dfrac{x^2-3x+10}{2}}$.

Check: $g(3-2x)=\dfrac{(9-12x+4x^2)-(9-6x)+10}{2}=\dfrac{4x^2-6x+10}{2}=2x^2-3x+5$.

(The observation $2x^2-3x+5=-x\cdot f(x)+5$ is true, but it does not give $g$, because the right side still contains $x$ separately from $f(x)$; one must write $x$ itself in terms of $u=f(x)$.)
