Let $S_N=\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{N}\dfrac{1}{n^2}$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $S_N<2-\dfrac{1}{N}$ for every $N\ge2$; deduce that the infinite series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{n^2}$ converges to a number at most $2$.
\item[(b)] Euler proved that $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{n^2}=\dfrac{\pi^2}{6}$. Assuming this, find $\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{(2k+1)^2}=1+\dfrac{1}{9}+\dfrac{1}{25}+\cdots$.
\end{enumerate}
