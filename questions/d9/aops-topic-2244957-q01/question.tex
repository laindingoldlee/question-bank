Let $a$, $b$, $c$ be real numbers in the open interval $(0,1)$ with $ab+bc+ca=1$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $a+b+c+abc<2$.
\item[(b)] Show that $a+b+c+abc$ can be made as close to $2$ as we like, so that it has no largest value (its least upper bound is $2$).
\end{enumerate}
