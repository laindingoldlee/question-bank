A quantity changes from $a$ to $b$, where $a>0$. Let
\[X=\dfrac{b-a}{a}\cdot100\%,\qquad Y=\dfrac ba\cdot100\%.\]
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Which of $X$ and $Y$ is the percentage change, and what does the other one measure? Show that $Y=100\%+X$.
\item[(b)] Find $X$ and $Y$ when a price goes from $80$ to $100$, and when it goes from $80$ to $60$.
\end{enumerate}
