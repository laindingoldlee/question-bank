Collect terms: $4x^2+2x-\dfrac x2=0$, i.e. $4x^2+\dfrac32x=0$, i.e. $x\left(4x+\dfrac32\right)=0$. The nonzero solution is $x=\boxed{-\dfrac38}$. (Check: $4\cdot\dfrac{9}{64}-\dfrac34=\dfrac{9}{16}-\dfrac{12}{16}=-\dfrac{3}{16}=\dfrac12\cdot\left(-\dfrac38\right)$.)
