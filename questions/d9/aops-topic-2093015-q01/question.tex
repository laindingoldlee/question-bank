In how many ways can the nine digits $1,2,\dots,9$ be placed in the nine cells of a $3\times3$ grid, one digit per cell, so that every two consecutive digits ($1$ and $2$, $2$ and $3$, \dots, $8$ and $9$) are in cells that share a side?
