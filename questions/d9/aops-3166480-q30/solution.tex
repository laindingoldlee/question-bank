There are sixteen people and nine empty chairs. Arrange the family blocks in $5!$ ways and their members internally in $(4!)^2(3!)^2 2!$ ways. Put one empty chair in each of the four internal gaps, then distribute the five remaining empties into the six gaps including ends in $\binom{10}{5}$ ways. Thus $N=5!(4!)^2(3!)^2 2!\binom{10}{5}=2^{14}3^7\cdot5\cdot7$. The divisor count is $\boxed{15\cdot8\cdot2\cdot2=480}$.
