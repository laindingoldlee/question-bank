Let the numbers be $x,y$, with $m=\lceil x\rceil$ and $n=\lceil y\rceil$, so $mn=100$. For each number, $\lfloor x\rfloor=m$ if $x$ is an integer and $\lfloor x\rfloor=m-1$ otherwise.

\textbf{$m,n>0$.} Then $0\le\lfloor x\rfloor\le m$ and $0\le\lfloor y\rfloor\le n$, so $X\le mn=100$.

\textbf{$m,n<0$.} Then $\lfloor x\rfloor$ and $\lfloor y\rfloor$ are negative with $|\lfloor x\rfloor|\le|m|+1$ and $|\lfloor y\rfloor|\le|n|+1$, so
\[
X\le(|m|+1)(|n|+1)=|m||n|+|m|+|n|+1=101+|m|+|n| .
\]
Among the factor pairs of $100$, the sum $|m|+|n|$ is largest for $\{1,100\}$, so $X\le202$.

\textbf{Attained.} Take $x=-1.5$ and $y=-100.5$: rounding up gives $(-1)(-100)=100$, and rounding down gives $(-2)(-101)=202$.

So the largest possible value is $\boxed{202}$.
