For side $s$, the conditions are $4s<s^2<12s$, that is, $4<s<12$, so $s=5,6,\dots,11$. The sum of the possible areas is
\[
5^2+6^2+\dots+11^2=\dfrac{11\cdot12\cdot23}{6}-\dfrac{4\cdot5\cdot9}{6}=506-30=476,
\]
and the product of its digits is $4\cdot7\cdot6=\boxed{168}$.
