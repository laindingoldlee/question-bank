Put the circle centres at $(-2,2)$ and $(2,2)$. Symmetry places the square centre horizontally at $0$. If its side is $s$, its upper-left vertex is $(-\dfrac{s}{2},s)$, so $(2-\dfrac{s}{2})^2+(s-2)^2=4$. This simplifies to $(5s-4)(s-4)=0$. The value $s=4$ makes the square overlap the circles and does not put its upper corners on the lower arcs. Hence $s=\dfrac{4}{5}$, and its area is $\boxed{\dfrac{16}{25}}$.
