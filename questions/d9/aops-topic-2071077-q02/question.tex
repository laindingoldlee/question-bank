Let \(a,b,c\) be positive numbers with \(a<b<c\), \(a+b>c\) and \(a^2+b^2>c^2\). Prove that there exist positive numbers \(x,y,z\) such that
\[
x^2+y^2=a^2,\qquad x^2+xz+z^2=b^2,\qquad y^2+\sqrt3\,yz+z^2=c^2 .
\]
