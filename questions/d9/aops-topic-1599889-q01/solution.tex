Since $s^3t^4u^3w>0$ and $w<0$, we get $s^3t^4u^3<0$. In particular $t\ne0$, so $t^4>0$, and therefore $s^3u^3=(su)^3<0$. A cube is negative exactly when the number is negative, so $su<0$: the answer is $\boxed{\text{(d)}}$.

The others need not hold: $s=1$, $u=-1$, $t=-1$, $w=-1$ satisfies the condition ($1\cdot1\cdot(-1)\cdot(-1)=1>0$) but violates (b), (c) and (e); and $s=-1$, $u=1$, $t=1$, $w=-1$ satisfies it but violates (a) and (c).
