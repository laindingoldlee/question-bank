Put $u=\log_xy$ and $v=\log_yz$. The first equation gives $u+v=uv$, and the second gives $- v = \dfrac{1}{uz}$, so $zu^2+u-1=0$. Since $u$ is rational and $z$ is an integer, its discriminant $1+4z$ is an integer square, say $(2k+1)^2$. Thus $z=k(k+1)$; the smallest greater than 67 is $72$. The roots for $u$ are $\dfrac{1}{9}$ and $- \dfrac{1}{8}$. In both cases $u v = - \dfrac{1}{z} = - \dfrac{1}{72}$, so $x=z^{-72}$. The two $y$ values are $72^{-8}$ and $72^9$. Both triples satisfy the logarithmic equations, and the product of their $xyz$ values is $72^{-141}$. Since $72=2^3 3^2$, the requested sum of prime factors with multiplicity is $141(3\cdot2+2\cdot3)=\boxed{1692}$.
