Positive rationals $x,y,z$, none equal to 1, satisfy $\log_xy+\log_yz=\log_xz$ and $\log_x (\dfrac{y}{z}) = \dfrac{\log_y x}{z}$. Additionally, $z$ is an integer greater than 67 and $\log_xy$ is rational. Minimise $z$, then multiply all possible values of $xyz$. Express the product as $\dfrac{m}{n}$ in lowest terms and find the sum of prime factors of $mn$, counted with multiplicity.
