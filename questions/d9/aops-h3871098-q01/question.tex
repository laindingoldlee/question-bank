Let $s=3$, $d=1$, and suppose $0\le\theta\le\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ satisfies
\[
\sin\theta-\cos\theta=-\dfrac{1}{s-d}.
\]
Find exact radical expressions for $\sin\theta$, $\cos\theta$, and $\tan\theta$. More generally, for real $s,d$ with $s\ne d$, determine when the equation has at least one real solution $\theta$ without the first-quadrant restriction.
