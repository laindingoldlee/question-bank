Let the numbers be $a\le b$. The condition $ab=a+b+2020$ becomes
\[ab-a-b+1=2021\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad (a-1)(b-1)=2021.\]
Since $a,b\ge1$, both factors $a-1$ and $b-1$ are nonnegative, and their product is positive, so they are positive divisors of $2021$ with $a-1\le b-1$.

Now $2021=43\cdot47$ with $43$ and $47$ prime, so the factorisations are $1\cdot2021$ and $43\cdot47$. They give
\[(a,b)=(2,2022)\qquad\text{and}\qquad(a,b)=(44,48).\]
Check: $2\cdot2022=4044=2+2022+2020$ and $44\cdot48=2112=44+48+2020$.

So the pairs are $\boxed{\{2,2022\}\text{ and }\{44,48\}}$.
