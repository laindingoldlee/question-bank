Ghosts are turned into candy: each pink ghost gives $8$ pieces, each green ghost $3$ pieces and each white ghost $9$ pieces. The ghostbusters catch $3$ pink ghosts and $2$ more green ghosts than white ghosts, and get $114$ pieces of candy. How many white ghosts did they catch?

$\textbf{(A) }5\qquad\textbf{(B) }9\qquad\textbf{(C) }11\qquad\textbf{(D) }7\qquad\textbf{(E) }8$
