Let the square have side $s$ and the strip width $w$. Cutting a strip of width $w$ from the left, right and top sides leaves a rectangle of width $s-2w$ and height $s-w$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=8,w=2;
fill((0,0)--(w,0)--(w,s-w)--(s-w,s-w)--(s-w,0)--(s,0)--(s,s)--(0,s)--cycle,palegray);
draw((0,0)--(s,0)--(s,s)--(0,s)--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw((w,0)--(w,s-w)--(s-w,s-w)--(s-w,0),linewidth(0.9));
label("$w$",(w/2,s/2));label("$w$",(s-w/2,s/2));label("$w$",(s/2,s-w/2));
label("$s$",(s/2,0),S);label("$s$",(0,s/2),W);
draw((w+0.15,1.0)--(s-w-0.15,1.0),Arrows(TeXHead));label("$s-2w$",(s/2+0.4,1.0),N);
draw((w+0.6,0.15)--(w+0.6,s-w-0.15),Arrows(TeXHead));label("$s-w$",(w+0.6,(s-w)/2+0.5),E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Put $r=\dfrac {w}{s}$, with $0<r<\dfrac{1}{2}$ so that the rectangle exists. The condition is
\[
(s-2w)(s-w)=\dfrac{3}{8}s^2\iff(1-2r)(1-r)=\dfrac{3}{8}\iff16r^2-24r+5=0\iff(4r-1)(4r-5)=0 .
\]
The root $r=\dfrac{5}{4}$ is too large (the strip would be wider than the square), so $r=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{4}}$. Check: $\left(1-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)\left(1-\dfrac{1}{4}\right)=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{3}{4}=\dfrac{3}{8}$.
