Reverse the progression if necessary, so its ratio is $\dfrac{p}{q}>1$ with coprime integers $p>q\ge1$. A length-$n$ integer progression must have the form
$sq^{n-1},spq^{n-2},\ldots,sp^{n-1}$
for a positive integer $s$, because coprimality forces $q^{n-1}$ to divide its first term.
If $q=1$, the ratio is at least $2$, so $100\cdot2^{n-1}\le2014$, giving $n\le5$.
If $q\ge2$, then $p\ge3$ and $3^{n-1}\le2014$. Since $3^7=2187$, this gives $n\le7$.
Length seven is attained by
\[
128,192,288,432,648,972,1458
\]
with ratio $\dfrac{3}{2}$. Therefore $\boxed{n_{\max}=7}$.
