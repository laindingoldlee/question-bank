Factoring gives $(A-B)(A+2B)=0 $. If $ A=B $, then $ A=\dfrac{1}{5}$. If $ A=-2B $, the sum $ A+B=\dfrac{2}{5}$ gives $ A=\dfrac{4}{5}$. Thus the required sum is $\boxed1 $.
