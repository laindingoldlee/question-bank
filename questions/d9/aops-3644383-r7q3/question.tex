Let $f(x) = \dfrac{3}{8}x - \dfrac{5}{4}$ . Define $f^n(x)$ recursively by $f^n(x) = f^{n-1}(f(x))$ and $f^1(x) = f(x)$. Let $m$ be the smallest positive integer such that $f^7(m)$ an integer. The prime factorization of $m$ can be written as $m = p^{e_1}_1 \cdot p^{e_2}_2 \cdot p^{e_3}_3 \cdot p^{e_4}_4 \cdot p^{e_5}_5 \cdot p^{e_6}_6$, where $p_1$, $p_2$, $...$, $p_6$ are distinct primes, and $e_1$, $e_2$, $...$, $e_6$ are their corresponding exponents. Find the positive difference between the sum of the primes and the sum of their exponents.
