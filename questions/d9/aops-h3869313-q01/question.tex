At a school library, books are classified as fiction, nonfiction, or reference books. During a reorganization, the librarian first moved \(36\) fiction books into the nonfiction section because they were mislabeled. Then she moved \(24\) nonfiction books into the reference section because they were actually encyclopedias or dictionaries. Finally, she moved \(15\) reference books into the fiction section because they were story collections.

Initially, the percentage of books in the fiction section minus the percentage of books in the nonfiction section was \(X\%\). After the reorganization, this difference became \((X-6)\%\). Compute the total number of books in the library.
