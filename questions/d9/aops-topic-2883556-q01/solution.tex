$90=9\cdot10$, $91=7\cdot13$, $92=4\cdot23$ and $93=3\cdot31$ are all valid dates. But $94=2\cdot47=1\cdot94$ needs a day of $47$ or $94$, which is impossible. The answer is $\boxed{\text{(E)}\ 1994}$.
