Write
\[
\dfrac{n+143}{n-1}=1+\dfrac{144}{n-1}.
\]
So the condition is that $n-1$ divides $144$, i.e. $n-1=\pm d$ for a positive divisor $d$ of $144$. Since $144=2^4\cdot3^2$ has $5\cdot3=15$ positive divisors, there are $30$ values of $n-1$, and they cancel in pairs $\pm d$; so their sum is $0$ and the sum of the values of $n$ is
\[
0+30\cdot1=\boxed{30}.
\]
(If only positive $n$ were allowed, the values would be $n=d+1$ for the $15$ positive divisors $d$ (the negative divisors give $n\le0$), with sum $\sigma(144)+15=403+15=418$.)
