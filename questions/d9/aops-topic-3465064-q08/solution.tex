Put $A=\log_5a,B=\log_5b,C=\log_5c$. Then $A+B-C=2$, $B+C-A=3$, $A+C-B=4$. Adding the first and third gives $A=3$, and the second gives $B+C=6$. Therefore $\log_{25}(bc)=\dfrac{\log_5(bc)}{\log_525}=\dfrac{6}{2}=\boxed3$.
