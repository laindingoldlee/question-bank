The double-angle formula changes the expression to $\tan20^\circ\dfrac{\tan40^\circ}{\tan10^\circ}$. The triple-angle product identity
\[
\tan3u=\tan u\,\tan(60^\circ+u)\,\tan(60^\circ-u)
\]
follows by dividing $\sin3u=4\sin u\sin(60^\circ+u)\sin(60^\circ-u)$ by the analogous cosine identity. At $u=10^\circ$ it gives
\[
\tan10^\circ\cot20^\circ\cot40^\circ=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}.
\]
Taking reciprocals yields the requested value $\boxed{\sqrt3}$.
