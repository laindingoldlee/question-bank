In acute triangle $ABC$, $AB=45$ and $BC=60$. Points $M$ and $N$ are the midpoints of $AB$ and $AC$, respectively. The angle bisector $BT$ (with $T$ on $AC$) meets $MN$ at $P$. Compute $\dfrac{MP}{PN}$.
