Glue John and Betsy into a block ($2$ internal orders): $9$ units around a circle give $8!\cdot2=80640$ seatings. Among these, those with Tim next to Bill as well: two blocks, $8$ units, $7!\cdot2\cdot2=20160$. The answer is $80640-20160=\boxed{60480}$.
