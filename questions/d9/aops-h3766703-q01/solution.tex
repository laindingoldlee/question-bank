The first player wins. Positions $0$ and $2$ are losing; positions $1,3,4$ are winning. Thereafter every odd number at least $5$ is losing and every even number at least $6$ is winning: an odd pile has only a move to the preceding winning even pile, while an even pile can remove one to reach the preceding losing odd pile. This proves the pattern inductively starting at $5$. The first player removes one candy, leaving $2021$. Thereafter move from each even pile to a losing one; at $4$, remove half to leave $2$.
