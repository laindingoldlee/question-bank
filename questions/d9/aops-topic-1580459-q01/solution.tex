The graph of $y=|x|$ is a V with vertex at the origin. Replacing $x$ by $x+5$ shifts it $5$ units to the left, and subtracting $6$ shifts it $6$ units down. So the vertex moves from $(0,0)$ to $\boxed{(-5,-6)}$.

Directly: $|x+5|\ge0$ with equality only at $x=-5$, so $f$ has its minimum value $-6$ there, and the graph changes from slope $-1$ to slope $+1$ at $(-5,-6)$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
draw((-13,0)--(4,0),gray,Arrow(TeXHead));draw((0,-7.5)--(0,7),gray,Arrow(TeXHead));
label("$x$",(4,0),E,gray);label("$y$",(0,7),N,gray);
real f(real x){return abs(x+5)-6;}
draw(graph(f,-12,1.5),blue+linewidth(1));
draw((-5,0)--(-5,-6),dashed);
dot((-5,-6));label("$(-5,-6)$",(-5,-6),S);
dot((-11,0));dot((1,0));label("$-11$",(-11,0),SW);label("$1$",(1,0),SE);
label("$-5$",(-5,0),N);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

(The graph crosses the $x$-axis where $|x+5|=6$, at $x=-11$ and $x=1$.)
