It is not determined: a chain of 15 regions needs only two colors, whereas three mutually adjacent regions require three, and a planar arrangement with four pairwise adjacent regions requires four. Extra regions can be added by subdividing one boundary region without removing these requirements. The four-color theorem states that every planar map can be colored with at most four colors. Therefore the least universal guarantee is $\boxed 4$, while the stated information does not identify the minimum for a particular map.
