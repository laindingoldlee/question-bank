The roots are $x=\dfrac{2\pm\sqrt{4-12}}{2}=1\pm i\sqrt2$ (they are not real, since the discriminant is $-8$). Their sum is $\boxed{2}$, as Vieta's formula $-\dfrac {b}{a}=2$ also gives.
