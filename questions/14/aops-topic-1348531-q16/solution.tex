$2016\equiv6\pmod{30}$, and $6^2=36\equiv6\pmod{30}$, so every positive power of $6$ is $\equiv6\pmod{30}$. Hence $n=6$, $x=\dfrac{36+4}{4}=10$, and $\left(x^2\right)^2=10^4=\boxed{10000}$.
