Rewrite the equation as $mn-m-n+1=4$, that is,
\[(m-1)(n-1)=4.\]
So $m-1$ and $n-1$ are integers whose product is $4$:
\[(m-1,n-1)\in\{(1,4),(4,1),(2,2),(-1,-4),(-4,-1),(-2,-2)\}.\]
The solutions are
\[(m,n)=\boxed{(2,5),\ (5,2),\ (3,3),\ (0,-3),\ (-3,0),\ (-1,-1)}.\]
(Check, for example: $0\cdot(-3)=0=0-3+3$ and $(-1)(-1)=1=-1-1+3$.)
