If two entries satisfy $a\ge b+2$, replacing them by $a-1,b+1$ multiplies the factorial product by $\dfrac{b+1}{a}<1$. Thus at a minimum all entries differ by at most one. Since the mean is $\dfrac{57}{20}$, there must be seventeen threes and three twos. Hence $M=(3!)^{17}(2!)^3=2^{20}3^{17}$, which has $(20+1)(17+1)=\boxed{378}$ divisors.
