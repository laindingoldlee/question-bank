The diagonals of a rhombus bisect each other at right angles. Their half-lengths are $5$ and $12$, so each side is $\sqrt{5^2+12^2}=13$. The perimeter is $4\cdot13=\boxed{52}$, choice D.
\begin{asy}
import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
pair A=(-12,0),B=(0,5),C=(12,0),D=(0,-5);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C^^B--D,dashed);label("$A$",A,(-1,0));label("$B$",B,(0,1));label("$C$",C,(1,0));label("$D$",D,(0,-1));label("12",(12/2,0),(0,-1));label("5",(0,5/2),(1,0));
\end{asy}
