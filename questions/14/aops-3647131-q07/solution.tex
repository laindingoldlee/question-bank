Each new person chooses a uniformly random earlier person, producing a random recursive tree. Person $j\ge2$ already has one edge when arriving and remains a leaf if no later person chooses them. That probability is $\dfrac{\prod_{t=j+1}^{51}(t-2)}{(t-1)}=\dfrac{(j-1)}{50}$. Person one is also initially a leaf after person two arrives; its probability of receiving no further edge is $\dfrac{1}{50}$. Linearity of expectation therefore gives $\dfrac{(1+\sum_{j=2}^{51}(j-1))}{50}=\dfrac{1276}{50}=\boxed{\dfrac{638}{25}}$.
