Banknotes of values $1,2,5,10,20,50,100,200,500,1000,2000,5000$ lie on a desk (any number of each value, possibly none). Two players move alternately, the first player starting. A move consists of taking one banknote of value other than $1$ from the desk and replacing it with one or more banknotes of smaller values with the same total. A player who cannot move loses. Determine which player has a winning strategy, depending on the starting position, and describe the strategy.
