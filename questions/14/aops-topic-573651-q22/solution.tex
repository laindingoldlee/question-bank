There are $20\cdot20=400$ equally likely outcomes. A sum of $24$ needs two numbered faces $a+b=24$ with $1\le a\le19$ and $b\in\{1,\dots,8,10,\dots,20\}$. Since $b\le20$, $a\ge4$, so $a\in\{4,\dots,19\}$ ($16$ values) and $b=24-a$ runs over $5,\dots,20$; we must exclude $b=9$, that is $a=15$. This leaves $15$ outcomes, and the probability is
\[
\dfrac{15}{400}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{80}}.
\]
