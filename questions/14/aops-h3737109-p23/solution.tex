The intersections with $y=-1$ and $y=2$ have horizontal distances $\dfrac{1}{|m|}$ and $\dfrac{4}{|m|}$ from the $y$-axis. The trapezoid height is 3, so its area is $\dfrac{1}{2}(\dfrac{1}{|m|}+\dfrac{4}{|m|})3=\dfrac{15}{2|m|}$. Setting this to 45 gives $m=\pm\dfrac{1}{6}$, whose product is $\boxed{-\dfrac{1}{36}}$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
real s=1;draw((0,-2)--(0,3),Arrow);filldraw((0,-1)--(6*s,-1)--(24*s,2)--(0,2)--cycle,lightblue);label("$y=-1$",(3*s,-1),S);label("$y=2$",(12*s,2),N);label("$m=\dfrac{1}{6}$",(12*s,.8));
\end{asy}
