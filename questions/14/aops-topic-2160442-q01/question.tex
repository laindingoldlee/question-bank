In triangle $ABC$, $\angle BAC>90^\circ$ and $AB\ne AC$. The bisector of $\angle BAC$ meets the perpendicular bisector of $BC$ at a point $X$ outside the triangle. Let $M$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $X$ to line $AB$, and suppose that $M$ lies on ray $AB$ beyond $B$. Prove that the foot $N$ of the perpendicular from $X$ to line $AC$ lies on the segment $AC$, strictly between $A$ and $C$.
