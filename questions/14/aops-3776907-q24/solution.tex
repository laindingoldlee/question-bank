Put the centroid at the origin. The vertex vectors of the two given medians have lengths $2$ and $4$, because a centroid divides each median in a $2:1$ ratio. If these vectors are $u,v$, the third is $-u-v$, and the triangle area is $\dfrac{3}{2}|\det(u,v)|\le\dfrac{3}{2}\cdot2\cdot4=12$. Perpendicular $u,v$ attain equality, so the maximum is $\boxed{12}$.

import graph;

import geometry;
size(7cm);

\end{asy}
