Triangle $ABC$ has area $1$. Points $D$, $E$, $F$ lie on the sides $BC$, $CA$, $AB$ respectively, such that $AD$ passes through the midpoint of $FC$, $FC$ passes through the midpoint of $BE$, and $BE$ passes through the midpoint of $AD$. The three segments $AD$, $BE$, $CF$ bound a small triangle inside $ABC$. Find its area, in the form $\dfrac{a-b\sqrt c}{4}$ for positive integers $a$, $b$, $c$.
