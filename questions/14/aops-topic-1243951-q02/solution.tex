$\lfloor\log_2n\rfloor=2k$ means $2^{2k}\le n<2^{2k+1}$. For $k=1,2,3,4$ this gives the ranges $4$--$7$, $16$--$31$, $64$--$127$, $256$--$511$, with $4+16+64+256=\boxed{340}$ integers ($2^{10}=1024>1000$).
