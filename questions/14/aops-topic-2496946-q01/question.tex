Triangle $ABC$ has a right angle at $C$, with $AC=24$ and $BC=7$. Let $D$ be the foot of the altitude from $C$ to $AB$, and let the bisector of angle $BAC$ meet $CD$ at $E$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find $DE$.
\item[(b)] A student found $CD=\dfrac{168}{25}$ correctly, then used the Pythagorean theorem in triangle $BCD$ and wrote $BD=7^2-\left(\dfrac{168}{25}\right)^2=3.8416$, and so $AD=25-3.8416=21.1584$. Using the angle bisector theorem with this value he obtained $DE=\dfrac{551}{175}$. Find his mistake.
\end{enumerate}
