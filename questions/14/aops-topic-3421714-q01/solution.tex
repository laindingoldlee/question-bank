Let $u=2^{111x}>0$. The equation becomes $\dfrac{u^3}{4}+4u=2u^2$. Multiplying by $4$ and dividing by $u$ gives $u^2-8u+16=(u-4)^2=0$. Hence $2^{111x}=4=2^2$, giving the unique root and sum $\boxed{\dfrac{2}{111}}$.
