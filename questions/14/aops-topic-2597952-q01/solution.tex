Steps 1 to 4 are correct. Step 5 divides both sides of step 4 by $a^2-ab$. But $a^2-ab=a(a-b)=0$ because $a=b$, and dividing by $0$ is not allowed.

From $2X=1\cdot X$ one may only conclude $X=0$, and indeed $X=a^2-ab=0$. So the error is $\boxed{\text{division by }a^2-ab=0\text{ in the last step}}$.
