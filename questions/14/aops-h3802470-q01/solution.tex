If all four primes were odd, the second equation would have an even left side and odd right side. Hence $p=2$. The second equation factors as $rs=(2q+1)(4q^2+1)$. Since $r,s$ are primes and both factors exceed one, the two factors must be $r,s$ in increasing order: $r=2q+1$, $s=4q^2+1$. Substituting in the first equation gives $4+q+4q^2+1=2q(2q+1)$, hence $q=5$. Thus $(p,q,r,s)=(2,5,11,101)$, which satisfies both equations, and the answer is $\boxed{4\cdot5\cdot101-1=2019}$.
