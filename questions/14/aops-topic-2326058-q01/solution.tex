Let $S=a+b+c+d$. By the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality (or the QM-AM inequality),
\[a^2+b^2+c^2+d^2\ge\dfrac{(a+b+c+d)^2}{4}=\dfrac{S^2}{4}.\]
Each child received $S+a^2+b^2+c^2+d^2\le360$ candies, so
\[S+\dfrac{S^2}{4}\le360\iff S^2+4S-1440\le0\iff(S-36)(S+40)\le0,\]
and since $S\ge0$, $S\le36$. Equality is possible: $a=b=c=d=9$ gives $36+4\cdot81=360$. So the maximum is $\boxed{36}$.
