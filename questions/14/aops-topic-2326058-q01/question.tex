Children go trick-or-treating. On one street the four houses give each child $a$, $b$, $c$ and $d$ candies, and on the next street the four houses give each child $a^2$, $b^2$, $c^2$ and $d^2$ candies, where $a,b,c,d$ are nonnegative integers. Each child received at most $360$ candies in total. Find the maximum possible value of $a+b+c+d$.
