Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle with orthocentre $H$, and let $D$, $E$, $F$ be the midpoints of $BC$, $CA$, $AB$, respectively. A circle centred at $H$ is drawn so large that triangle $ABC$ lies in its interior. The line $EF$ meets the circle at $P$, the line $FD$ meets it at $Q$, and the line $DE$ meets it at $R$ (for each line, either of the two intersection points may be taken). Prove that $AP=BQ=CR$.
