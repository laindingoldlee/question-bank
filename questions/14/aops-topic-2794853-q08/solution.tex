Unroll the cylinder. A full turn has horizontal displacement $24\pi$. The smallest permitted displacement to the opposite point is $2.5\cdot24\pi=60\pi$, while the vertical displacement is $80\pi$. A straight segment is shortest on the unrolled surface and has length $\sqrt{(60\pi)^2+(80\pi)^2}=\boxed{100\pi}$. Larger allowed winding gives a longer displacement.
