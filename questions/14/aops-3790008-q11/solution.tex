For any subset of $j$ bit positions, the expected sign of its parity after $t$ steps is $(1 - \dfrac{2 j}{9})^t$. Expanding the indicator that all nine parities are even gives return probability $2^{-9} \sum_{j=0}^9 \binom{9}{j} (1 - \dfrac{2 j}{9})^t$. At even $t\to\infty$, only $j=0,9$ survive, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{256}}$. The probability that some bit has never been selected is at most $9 (\dfrac{8}{9})^t \to 0$, so the conditional limit is the same. In the original $2^{-x}$ notation, $x=8$.
