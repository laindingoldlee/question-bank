(a) The proper divisors of $12$ sum to $1+2+3+4+6=16>12$, so it is abundant. Those of $8$ sum to $1+2+4=7<8$, so it is deficient. A perfect number has proper-divisor sum equal to itself. (b) If $n=ab$ with $a,b>1$, then $2^n-1=(2^a-1)(1+2^a+\cdots+2^{a(b-1)})$, a product of two integers greater than one. Thus prime $2^n-1$ requires prime $n$.
