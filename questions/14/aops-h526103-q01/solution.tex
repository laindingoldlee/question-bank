Repeated use of $\sin2t=2\sin t\cos t$ gives
\[
\cos12^\circ\cos24^\circ\cos48^\circ
=\dfrac{\sin96^\circ}{8\sin12^\circ}.
\]
Since $\sin96^\circ=\cos6^\circ$ and $\cos84^\circ=\sin6^\circ$, multiplication by $\cos84^\circ$ leaves $\dfrac{1}{16}$. Also
\[
\sin36^\circ=\sin144^\circ=2\sin72^\circ\cos72^\circ
=4\sin36^\circ\cos36^\circ\cos72^\circ,
\]
so $\cos36^\circ\cos72^\circ=\dfrac{1}{4}$. The required product is therefore $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{64}}$.
