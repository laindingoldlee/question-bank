Necessity follows by taking $x=1$: $k$ itself must be $u^2+v^2$ for integers $u,v$. Conversely, if $k=u^2+v^2$ and $x=a^2+b^2$, the identity\[(u^2+v^2)(a^2+b^2)=(ua-vb)^2+(ub+va)^2\]gives the required construction. Therefore the valid $k$ are exactly the integers that are sums of two integer squares, including $0$.
