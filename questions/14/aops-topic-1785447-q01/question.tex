Timmy goes fishing from $3$ PM to $7{:}30$ PM. Assume that fish are caught at random so that the probability of catching no fish during any period of $t$ hours is $(0.8)^t$, independently for non-overlapping periods; in particular, the chance of catching at least one fish in any given hour is $20\%$. What is the probability that Timmy catches at least one fish? Give the exact value and a decimal approximation.
