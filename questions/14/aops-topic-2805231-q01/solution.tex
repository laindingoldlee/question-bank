(a) For example $1,3,4,5,2,6$ clockwise. The windows of five starting at each position give $1+3+4=8>7=5+2$, $3+4+5=12>8$, $4+5+2=11>7$, $5+2+6=13>4$, $2+6+1=9>7$, $6+1+3=10>9$.

(b) $\boxed{\text{Yes}}$: for example $1,3,6,2,4,5$. Clockwise: $10>6$, $11>9$, $12>6$, $11>4$, $10>9$, $9>8$; and reading counterclockwise, $1,5,4,2,6,3$: $10>8$, $11>9$, $12>4$, $11>6$, $10>9$, $9>6$. (The condition says, for each number left out, that the three before it beat the two after it.)
