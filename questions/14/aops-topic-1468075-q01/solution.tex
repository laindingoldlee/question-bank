Let $m$ be his average on the first nine quizzes. Then
\[
\dfrac{9m+80}{10}=m+2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad9m+80=10m+20\quad\Longrightarrow\quad m=60 .
\]
The new average is $m+2=\boxed{62}$. (Shortcut: the $80$ is $18$ above the new average, and those $18$ points spread over all $10$ quizzes raise each of the old $9$ by $2$.)
