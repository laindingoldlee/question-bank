The terms are $a$, $a+7$, $a+14$, which are congruent to $a$, $a+1$, $a+2$ modulo $3$ (since $7\equiv1$ and $14\equiv2$). Three consecutive integers modulo $3$ cover all residues, so one of the terms is $\equiv0\pmod3$. $\blacksquare$ (The same works for any common difference not divisible by $3$.)
