$n^2=2^{200}3^{200}$ has $201^2=40401$ divisors. They pair up as $d\leftrightarrow\dfrac{n^2}{d}$, with exactly one of each pair less than $n$, except $d=n$, which is paired with itself. So $\dfrac{40401-1}{2}=20200$ divisors of $n^2$ are less than $n$.

Every divisor of $n$ divides $n^2$, and $n$ has $101^2=10201$ divisors, of which $10200$ are less than $n$. Hence the answer is
\[20200-10200=\boxed{10000}.\]
