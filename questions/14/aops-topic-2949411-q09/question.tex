A cheetah starts at mile 11 and runs toward increasing mile markers at constant maximum speed $u$. Its owner, also starting at mile 11, simultaneously drives down to mile 10 at half his maximum speed $v$, then reverses and drives at speed $v$, meeting the cheetah at mile 20. Find $v:u$.
