The owner drives one mile at speed $\dfrac{v}{2}$ and ten miles at speed $v$, taking $\dfrac{2}{v}+\dfrac{10}{v}=\dfrac{12}{v}$ hours in compatible units. The cheetah travels nine miles in $\dfrac{9}{u}$. Equating gives $\dfrac{v}{u}=\boxed{\dfrac{4}{3}}$.
