Five pirates, Amar, Beth, Charlie, Dan and Emily, ranked in this order, must split $100$ gold coins. The highest-ranked pirate still alive proposes a split, and all living pirates (including the proposer) vote on it. If at least half of the votes are in favour (a tie is enough), the split is carried out; otherwise the proposer walks the plank and the next pirate in rank makes a proposal, with the same rules. Each pirate is a perfect logician and wants, first, to stay alive; second, to get as many coins as possible; and third, other things being equal, to see the proposer walk the plank (so a pirate votes yes only if the proposal gives him strictly more than he would get after the proposer walks the plank). What should Amar propose so that he survives and keeps as many coins as possible, and how many coins does he keep?
