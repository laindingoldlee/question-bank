The geometric sum is $\dfrac{2^{24}-1}{15}=\dfrac{(4095)(4097)}{15}$. Factor $4095=3^2\cdot5\cdot7\cdot13$ and $4097=17\cdot241$. Thus the original number is $3\cdot7\cdot13\cdot17\cdot241$. Trial division by primes at most $\sqrt{241}<16$ verifies that $241$ is prime. The largest prime factor is $\boxed{241}$.
