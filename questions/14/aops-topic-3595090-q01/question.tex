Let $0<a<\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. The ray from the origin making angle $a$ with the positive $x$-axis meets the unit circle at $P$ and the line $x=1$ at $Q$. Let $R=(\cos a,0)$ and $S=(1,0)$. Find the area of trapezoid $RPQS$.
