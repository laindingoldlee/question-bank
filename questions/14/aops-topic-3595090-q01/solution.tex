The vertical parallel sides have lengths $RP=\sin a$ and $SQ=\tan a$. Their horizontal separation is $1-\cos a$. Hence the area is\[\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}(1-\cos a)(\sin a+\tan a)}.\]All lengths are positive because $0<a<\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
\begin{asy}
import geometry;
size(240);
real a=40;pair O=(0,0),P=dir(a),Q=(1,tan(a*pi/180)),R=(P.x,0),S=(1,0);draw(arc(O,1,0,90));filldraw(R--P--Q--S--cycle,lightgreen,black);draw(O--Q);draw(O--(1.2,0));label("$O$",O,SW);label("$P$",P,NW);label("$Q$",Q,NE);label("$R$",R,S);label("$S$",S,SE);label("$a$",(.25,.1));
\end{asy}
