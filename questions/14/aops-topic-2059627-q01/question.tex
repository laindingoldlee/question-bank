Ben, Fang and Venetia play a game in which a card numbered $2$, $3$, $4$, $5$, $6$, $7$ or $8$ is stuck to each of their foreheads (several players may have the same number), so that each player sees the other two numbers but not their own. Joey walks in and announces: ``The three numbers are not all different, and the product of the three numbers is a perfect square!'' How many of the three players can now deduce their own number?
