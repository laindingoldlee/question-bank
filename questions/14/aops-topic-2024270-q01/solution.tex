Let $L$ be the lesser and $G$ the greater number. Multi and Divi agree:
\[GL=\dfrac{G}{L}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad L^2=1\quad(\text{as }G\ne0),\]
so $L=1$ or $L=-1$.
\begin{itemize}
\item If $L=1$, Addi and Multi give $G+1=G$, impossible.
\item If $L=-1$, Addi and Multi give $G-1=-G$, so $G=\dfrac{1}{2}$, which is indeed greater than $-1$.
\end{itemize}
Check: Addi gives $\dfrac{1}{2}-1=-\dfrac{1}{2}$, Multi gives $-\dfrac{1}{2}$, Divi gives $\dfrac{1}{2}\div(-1)=-\dfrac{1}{2}$. Subbi's result is
\[\dfrac{1}{2}-(-1)=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{2}}.\]
