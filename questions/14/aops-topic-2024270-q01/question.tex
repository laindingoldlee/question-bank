Addi, Subbi, Multi, and Divi are given the same two distinct nonzero numbers. Addi adds the two numbers. Subbi subtracts the lesser number from the greater. Multi multiplies the two numbers. Divi divides the greater number by the lesser. If the results obtained by Addi, Multi, and Divi are all the same, what is Subbi's result? Express your answer as a common fraction.
