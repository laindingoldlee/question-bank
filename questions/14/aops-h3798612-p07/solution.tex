Scale $AB=BC=CD=1$. Triangle $BCD$ has equal sides $BC,CD$ and base angles $15^\circ$, so $\angle BCD=150^\circ$. Because $C,D,E$ are in that order, $\angle BCE=150^\circ$. Put $\theta=\angle CBE$; then $\angle BEC=30^\circ-\theta$ and $0<\theta<30^\circ$. Since $A,B,E$ are collinear in that order, $AC^2=2+2\cos\theta$, hence $AC=2\cos\dfrac{\theta}{2}$. The sine law in $BCE$, together with $CE=AC$, gives
\[\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\sin(30^\circ-\theta)}=2\cos\dfrac{\theta}{2},\quad \sin\dfrac{\theta}{2}=\sin(30^\circ-\theta).\]
Both angles are acute, so $\theta=20^\circ$. Therefore $\angle AED=\angle BEC=\boxed{10^\circ}$.
