Divisibility by 4 forces $B=2$ or 6. The alternating digit sum for divisibility by 11 is $A+3-B$. If $B=2$, this is between 1 and 10, impossible. If $B=6$, it vanishes exactly at $A=3$. The number is 553476, with digit sum 30, so its remainder is $\boxed3$.
