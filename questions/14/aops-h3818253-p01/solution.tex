Positivity implies $1\le y\le7$. Modulo $5$, $x+y\equiv0$, so put $x=5k-y$. Then $z=20-7k-y$. The inequalities $x,z\ge1$ imply $k\ge1$ and $k\le2$. For $k=1$, $y=1,2,3,4$; for $k=2$, $y=1,2,3,4,5$. Thus the nine triples are
\[
\boxed{(4,1,12),(3,2,11),(2,3,10),(1,4,9),
(9,1,5),(8,2,4),(7,3,3),(6,4,2),(5,5,1)}.
\]
