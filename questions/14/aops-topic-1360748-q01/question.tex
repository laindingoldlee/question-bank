A rational number is one that can be written as $\dfrac{p}{q}$ with integers $p,q$ and $q\ne0$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that the product of two rational numbers is rational.
\item[(b)] Prove that every rational number is a product of two rational numbers. Is a product of two irrational numbers always irrational?
\end{enumerate}
