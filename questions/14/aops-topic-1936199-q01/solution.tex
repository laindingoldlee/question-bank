We have $F(n)=\dfrac{10^n-1}{9}$. Since $\gcd(7,9)=1$, $7$ divides $F(n)$ exactly when $7$ divides $10^n-1$, that is, when $10^n\equiv1\pmod7$.

Modulo $7$, $10\equiv3$, and the powers $3^1,3^2,\dots,3^6$ are $3,2,6,4,5,1$; after that they repeat with period $6$. So $10^n\equiv1\pmod7$ exactly when $6\mid n$. (For example $F(6)=111111=7\cdot15873$.)

The multiples of $6$ below $1000$ are $6,12,\dots,996$, and there are $\boxed{166}$ of them.
