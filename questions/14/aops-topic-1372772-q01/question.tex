In a $10$-mile race, Janet covered the first $2$ miles at a constant rate. She then sped up and rode the last $8$ miles at a rate that was $0.5$ mile per minute faster. Her overall time would have been $2$ minutes less had she ridden the whole race at the faster rate. What was Janet's average speed, in miles per minute, for the whole race?
