Let the first rate be $r$ miles per minute. Riding the first $2$ miles at $r+0.5$ instead of $r$ saves $2$ minutes:
\[
\dfrac{2}{r}-\dfrac{2}{r+0.5}=2\iff\dfrac{1}{r}-\dfrac{1}{r+0.5}=1\iff 0.5=r(r+0.5)\iff 2r^2+r-1=0 .
\]
So $(2r-1)(r+1)=0$ and, as $r>0$, $r=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Her times were $2\div\dfrac{1}{2}=4$ minutes for the first part and $\dfrac{8}{1}=8$ minutes for the rest, $12$ minutes in all, so her average speed was
\[
\dfrac{10}{12}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{6}}\text{ mile per minute}.
\]
