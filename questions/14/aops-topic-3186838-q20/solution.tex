The probabilities of scores $0,2,5,8,10$ are respectively $\dfrac{4}{20},\dfrac{7}{20},\dfrac{5}{20},\dfrac{3}{20},\dfrac{1}{20}$, because the annular areas are proportional to $7,5,3,1$ and the hit probability is $\dfrac{4}{5}$. The only score multisets totalling $15$ are $\{0,5,10\}$, $\{2,5,8\}$, and $\{5,5,5\}$. For independent shots the probability is $\dfrac{6\cdot4\cdot5\cdot1+6\cdot7\cdot5\cdot3+5^3}{20^3}=\dfrac{7}{64}$. Hence $m+n=\boxed{71}$.
