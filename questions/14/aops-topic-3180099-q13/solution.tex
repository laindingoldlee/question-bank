Place the large centre at $(0,0)$ and $M=(10,0)$. The square vertices are $A=(-10,0),B=(0,-10),C=(0,10)$. The small centre is $(14,0)$ and its triangle vertices are $O=(16,-2\sqrt3),P=(16,2\sqrt3)$. The pentagon shoelace sum is $\dfrac{1}{2}(100+160+64\sqrt3+160+100)=260+32\sqrt3$. Thus $\boxed{a+b+c=295}$.
