Two circles of radii 10 and 4 touch externally at $M$. Square $ABMC$ is inscribed in the larger circle, and equilateral triangle $MOP$ in the smaller. The vertices occur so that $ABOPC$ is the convex outer pentagon. Write its area as $a+b\sqrt c$ with squarefree $c$, and find $a+b+c$.
