\textbf{$n$ is even.} If $n$ were odd, every $a_i$ would be odd (they divide the odd number $n$), and a sum of an odd number of odd integers is odd, not $2012$.

\textbf{$4\mid n$.} If $n\equiv2\pmod4$, exactly one factor of $2$ appears in the product, so exactly one $a_i$ is even and the other $n-1$ are odd. Since $n-1$ is odd, the sum is even $+$ odd $=$ odd, again not $2012$. Hence $4\mid n$. $\blacksquare$
