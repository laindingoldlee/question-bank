The legs of a $45$-$45$-$90$ triangle are equal, and the hypotenuse is $\sqrt2$ times a leg, so each leg is $\dfrac{16}{\sqrt2}=8\sqrt2$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair C=(0,0), B=(8,0), A=(0,8);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw((0.6,0)--(0.6,0.6)--(0,0.6));
label("$16$",(A+B)/2,NE); label("$8\sqrt2$",(A+C)/2,W); label("$8\sqrt2$",(B+C)/2,S);
label("$45^\circ$",B+(-1.45,0.5)); label("$45^\circ$",A+(0.75,-1.9));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\left(8\sqrt2\right)^2=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot128=\boxed{64}$ square inches.
