From $10{:}47$ a.m.\ to $7{:}47$ p.m.\ is $9$ hours $=540$ minutes, and $7{:}32$ p.m.\ is $15$ minutes earlier. So he slept $540-15=\boxed{525}$ minutes ($8$ hours $45$ minutes).
