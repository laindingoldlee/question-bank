(a) $(n+50)^2-n^2=100n+2500$ and $(100-n)^2-n^2=10000-200n$ are multiples of $100$. (b) Expanding gives $(50+k)^2=2500+100k+k^2$ and $(100+k)^2=10000+200k+k^2$, with ordinary carrying when $k^2\ge100$. (c) $(10k+5)^2=100k(k+1)+25$: multiply consecutive integers $k,k+1$ and append $25$. For example $95^2=100(9)(10)+25=9025$.
