Let a soda cost $c$ cents; then a hot dog costs $2c$ cents. The total in cents is $100d$, so
\[
2ch+cs=100d\quad\Longrightarrow\quad c=\boxed{\dfrac{100d}{2h+s}}\text{ cents}.
\]
