The upper half of the large disk has area $2\pi$. Each small disk contributes an upper semicircle of area $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ inside that half. The remaining area is $2\pi-\pi=\pi$. Dividing by the area $\pi$ of a unit circle gives $\boxed1$.
