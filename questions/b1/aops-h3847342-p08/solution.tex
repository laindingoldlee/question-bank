The base angles are $40^\circ$, and the sine rule or the altitude from $A$ gives $BC=2AB\sin50^\circ>AB=AC$, so $D$ lies beyond $C$.

Construct a ray from $B$ making angle $50^\circ$ with $BA$ on the side toward ray $AC$, meeting that ray at $D'$. Triangle $ABD'$ has angles $100^\circ,50^\circ,30^\circ$. Hence
\[
AD'=AB\,\dfrac{\sin50^\circ}{\sin30^\circ}
=2AB\sin50^\circ=BC.
\]
There is only one point of ray $AC$ at this distance from $A$, so $D'=D$ and $\boxed{\angle ABD=50^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(1,0),C=dir(100),D=2sin(50pi/180)*dir(100);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(C--D--B,blue);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,W);label("$D$",D,N);label("$100^\circ$",(.1,.15));
\end{asy}
