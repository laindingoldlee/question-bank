Write $a=3x$ and $b=3y$ with $\gcd(x,y)=1$. Then $\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=3xy=60$, so $xy=20$ with $x,y$ coprime: $\{x,y\}=\{1,20\}$ or $\{4,5\}$. The sums $x+y$ are $21$ and $9$, so the minimum of $a+b=3(x+y)$ is $3\cdot9=\boxed{27}$, for $\{a,b\}=\{12,15\}$.
