Prove that, as polynomials with coefficients taken modulo $13$,
\[
(x-1^2)(x-2^2)(x-3^2)(x-4^2)(x-5^2)(x-6^2)\equiv x^6-1\pmod{13},
\]
that is, the two sides have coefficients that agree modulo $13$.
