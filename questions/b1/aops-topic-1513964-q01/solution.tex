Split it into two conditions that must both hold:
\begin{itemize}
\item $|x-3|\le5\iff-5\le x-3\le5\iff-2\le x\le8$;
\item $|x-3|>2\iff x-3>2$ or $x-3<-2\iff x>5$ or $x<1$.
\end{itemize}
(Geometrically, $x$ is more than $2$ but at most $5$ units from $3$ on the number line.) Combining,
\[
\boxed{-2\le x<1\ \text{ or }\ 5<x\le8}.
\]
