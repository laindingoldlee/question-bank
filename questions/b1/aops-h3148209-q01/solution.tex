The count is $\lfloor\sqrt{4n+1000}\rfloor-\lfloor\sqrt{4n}\rfloor$. It cannot reach $30$: thirty successive squares beginning at $r^2$ with $r\ge3$ span $(r+29)^2-r^2=58r+841\ge1015$, exceeding the interval\textquotesingle s span $999$. At $n=1$, squares $3^2$ through $31^2$ give $29$, so $a=29$. For the minimum, put $k=\lfloor\sqrt{4n}\rfloor\le63$. If at most six squares occurred, then $4n+1000<(k+7)^2$, hence $1000\le14k+48\le930$, impossible. Thus at least seven occur. At $n=1000$, they are $64^2$ through $70^2$, exactly seven. Therefore $\boxed{a-b=29-7=22}$.
