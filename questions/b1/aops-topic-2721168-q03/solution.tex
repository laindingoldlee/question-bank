Factor: $x^3-3x+2=(x-1)^2(x+2)$ and $x^2-3x+2=(x-1)(x-2)$, so $x\ne1,2$ and
\[y=\dfrac{(x-1)(x+2)}{x-2}=x+3+\dfrac{4}{x-2}.\]
So $x-2\in\{\pm1,\pm2,\pm4\}$, i.e. $x\in\{3,1,4,0,6,-2\}$, and $x=1$ is excluded. The solutions are
\[(x,y)=\boxed{(-2,0),\ (0,1),\ (3,10),\ (4,9),\ (6,10)}.\]
