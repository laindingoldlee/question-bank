The sum of the coefficients of a polynomial in $x$ and $y$ is its value at $x=y=1$:
\[(2\cdot1+1)^4=3^4=\boxed{81}.\]
(Directly, with the row $1,4,6,4,1$ of Pascal's triangle: $(2x+y)^4=16x^4+32x^3y+24x^2y^2+8xy^3+y^4$, and $16+32+24+8+1=81$.)
