The first three examples are $ 3=1+1+1 $, $ 17=1+8+8 $, and $ 29=1+1+27 $. To show no prime is missed below $ 29 $, each cube is at most $ 27 $, so use only $ 1,8,27 $. A sum containing $ 27 $ is at least $ 29 $. Sums of three entries from $ 1,8 $ are $ 3,10,17,24 $, only the first and third prime. Therefore the third smallest is $\boxed{29}$.
