A $12\times8$ rectangular grid is made of $13$ vertical lines and $9$ horizontal lines. Point $A$ is the top left corner and $B$ the bottom right corner. Not counting $A$, the diagonal $AB$ meets the grid lines at $16$ points. Not counting $A$, at how many points does the diagonal $AB$ of a $230\times80$ grid (with $231$ vertical and $81$ horizontal lines) meet the grid lines?
