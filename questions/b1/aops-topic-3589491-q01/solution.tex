The degree must be even, since the constant term is the product of the negated positive roots. If $1$ is a root, then $P(1)=0$, which is possible, for example for $(x-1)(x-1001)$. Otherwise every root exceeds $1$. Since $1001=7\cdot11\cdot13$, there can be at most three roots, hence exactly two. Their unordered pairs are $(7,143),(11,91),(13,77)$; the pair $(1,1001)$ was already covered. They give $P(1)=852,900,912$, respectively. The distinct values are therefore $0,852,900,912$, with sum $2664=2\cdot1001+662$. The answer is $\boxed{662}$.
