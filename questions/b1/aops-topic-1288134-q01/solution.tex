Both numbers are positive, and $\left(\sqrt2\right)^4=\left(\left(\sqrt2\right)^2\right)^2=2^2=4$. The positive fourth root of $4$ is unique, so $\sqrt[4]4=\sqrt2$. Equivalently, $\sqrt[4]{4}=\sqrt[4]{2^2}=2^{\dfrac{2}{4}}=2^{\dfrac{1}{2}}$. The simplified form uses the smallest index, because the radicand $4=2^2$ is a perfect square; so $\boxed{\sqrt2}$ is the simplified form.
