In parallelogram $ABCD$, point $E$ lies on side $AD$ and point $F$ lies on side $AB$ so that $BE=DF$. Prove that $[FDC]=[BEC]$, and deduce that $C$ is equidistant from the lines $BE$ and $DF$. (Here $[\,\cdot\,]$ denotes area.)
