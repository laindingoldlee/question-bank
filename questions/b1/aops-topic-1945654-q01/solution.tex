Let $h_1$ be the distance between the parallel lines $AD$ and $BC$, and $h_2$ the distance between the parallel lines $AB$ and $DC$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),Bv=(5,0),C=(4,3),D=(-1,3);
pair E0=0.5*D; real f=-1+sqrt(abs(E0-Bv)^2-9); pair F=(f,0);
fill(Bv--E0--C--cycle,opacity(0.25)+blue);
fill(F--D--C--cycle,opacity(0.25)+red);
draw(A--Bv--C--D--cycle,linewidth(1));
draw(Bv--E0,linewidth(0.9));draw(D--F,linewidth(0.9));draw(E0--C);draw(F--C);
dot(E0);dot(F);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",Bv,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$E$",E0,W);label("$F$",F,S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Triangle $BEC$ has base $BC$, and its vertex $E$ lies on line $AD\parallel BC$, so its height is $h_1$ and $[BEC]=\dfrac{1}{2}\,BC\cdot h_1=\dfrac{1}{2}[ABCD]$.

Triangle $FDC$ has base $DC$, and its vertex $F$ lies on line $AB\parallel DC$, so its height is $h_2$ and $[FDC]=\dfrac{1}{2}\,DC\cdot h_2=\dfrac{1}{2}[ABCD]$.

Hence $[FDC]=[BEC]$. (This part does not use $BE=DF$.)

Now let $d_1$ and $d_2$ be the distances from $C$ to the lines $BE$ and $DF$. Using $BE$ and $DF$ as bases,
\[
\dfrac{1}{2}\,BE\cdot d_1=[BEC]=[FDC]=\dfrac{1}{2}\,DF\cdot d_2 ,
\]
and since $BE=DF$ we get $d_1=d_2$. $\blacksquare$
