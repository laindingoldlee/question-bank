With $c$ correct and $u$ blank answers ($c,u\ge0$, $c+u\le12$) the score is $5c+2u$.

For a fixed $c$, the reachable scores are $5c,5c+2,\dots,5c+2(12-c)=24+3c$, all of the same parity as $c$.

\textbf{Even scores} come from even $c$: $c=0$ gives $0,2,\dots,24$; $c=2$ gives $10,\dots,30$; $c=4$ gives $20,\dots,36$; $c=6$ gives $30,\dots,42$; $c=8$ gives $40,\dots,48$; $c=10$ gives $50,52,54$; $c=12$ gives $60$. So every even score from $0$ to $54$ is reachable, and $56$ and $58$ are not.

\textbf{Odd scores} come from odd $c$: $c=1$ gives $5,7,\dots,27$; $c=3$ gives $15,\dots,33$; $c=5$ gives $25,\dots,39$; $c=7$ gives $35,\dots,45$; $c=9$ gives $45,\dots,51$; $c=11$ gives $55,57$. So every odd score from $5$ to $51$ is reachable, together with $55$ and $57$; the odd scores $1$, $3$, $53$ and $59$ are not.

So the impossible scores are $1,3,53,56,58,59$, with sum $1+3+53+56+58+59=\boxed{230}$.
