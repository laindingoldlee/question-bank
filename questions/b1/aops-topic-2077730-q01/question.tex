A monthly electricity bill is a fixed charge of $\log343$ dollars (for public lighting) plus $0.56$ dollars for each kilowatt-hour used, so the bill for $x$ kilowatt-hours is
\[V(x)=0.56x+\log343 .\]
In one month the bill was $125.16$ dollars. How many kilowatt-hours were used? (Use $\log7=0.84$.)
