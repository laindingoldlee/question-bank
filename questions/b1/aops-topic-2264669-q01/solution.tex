Don has $4+2=6$ shirts and $3$ pairs of pants, so $6\cdot3=18$ shirt--pants combinations in all. The only forbidden combination is the one with two black items: the black shirt with the black pants. So he can make $18-1=\boxed{17}$ outfits.
