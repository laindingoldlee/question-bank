The expected unripe count per bunch is $8\cdot\dfrac{1}{3}+6\cdot\dfrac{1}{6}+2\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{14}{3}$. Linearity gives $12\cdot\dfrac{14}{3}=\boxed{56}$.
