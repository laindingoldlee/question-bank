Factor the denominators: $105=3\cdot5\cdot7$ and $175=5^2\cdot7$. The least common denominator takes each prime to its highest power: $3\cdot5^2\cdot7=525$. Then $525=105\cdot5=175\cdot3$, so
\[
\dfrac{x+5}{105}+\dfrac{3x-4}{175}=\dfrac{5(x+5)+3(3x-4)}{525}=\boxed{\dfrac{14x+13}{525}} .
\]
The numerator and denominator have no common factor ($14x+13$ is not a constant multiple of a factor of $525$). In general, the least common denominator is the LCM of the denominators, found from their prime factorisations.
