The intersections are $ D=(1,\sqrt3)$ and $ E=(\sqrt3,1)$, at polar angles 60 and 30 degrees. Sector $ EOD $ therefore has area $\dfrac{30}{360}\pi(2)^2=\dfrac{\pi}{3}$. The quadrilateral $ OEBD $ has area $\sqrt3-1 $: its shoelace sum is $ 2\sqrt3-2 $. Subtracting this quadrilateral from the sector leaves the desired area, $\boxed{1+\dfrac{\pi}{3}-\sqrt3}$.
