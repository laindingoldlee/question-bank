The gap between the two Tuesdays is $L-100$ days, where $L$ is the number of days in year $N$. Only $366-100=266$ is divisible by $7$, so $N$ is a leap year. Its preceding year has $365$ days. From day $100$ of year $N-1$ to day $300$ of year $N$ is $365-100+300=565\equiv 5\pmod 7$ days. Five days before Tuesday is $\boxed{\text{Thursday}}$.
