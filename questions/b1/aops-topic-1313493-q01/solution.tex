As a quadratic in $b$: $b^2-ab-(2a^2+3a-12)=0$. Its discriminant
\[
a^2+4(2a^2+3a-12)=9a^2+12a-48=(3a+2)^2-52
\]
must be a perfect square $k^2$ ($k\ge0$). Then $(3a+2-k)(3a+2+k)=52$, with both factors of the same parity, hence both even: $2\cdot26$ is the only option with the smaller factor positive and $3a+2\ge5$. So $3a+2=14$, $k=12$, $a=4$, and $b=\dfrac{a+k}{2}=8$ (the other root $\dfrac{a-k}{2}<0$). Check: $4(8+8+3)=76=64+12$. The only pair is $\boxed{(a,b)=(4,8)}$.
