When the circuit containing blinking lights $A$ and $B$ is turned on, lights $A$ and $B$ blink together. From then on $A$ blinks once every $5$ seconds and $B$ blinks once every $11$ seconds. Lindsey looks at the two lights just in time to see $A$ blink alone. What is the probability, as a percent, that the next light to blink will be $A$ blinking alone?
