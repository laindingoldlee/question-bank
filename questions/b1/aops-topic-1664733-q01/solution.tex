Let $u=\sqrt5+\sqrt3$ and $v=\sqrt5-\sqrt3$. Then $uv=5-3=2$ and
\[
u^2+v^2=\left(8+2\sqrt{15}\right)+\left(8-2\sqrt{15}\right)=16 .
\]
So
\[
\dfrac {u}{v}+\dfrac {v}{u}=\dfrac{u^2+v^2}{uv}=\dfrac{16}{2}=\boxed{8}.
\]
(The trick: the two fractions are reciprocals, and putting them over the common denominator $uv$ makes the $\sqrt{15}$ terms cancel.)
