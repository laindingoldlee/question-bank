The units digits of $3^1,3^2,3^3,3^4,3^5,\dots$ are $3,9,7,1,3,\dots$: each units digit is determined by the previous one (multiply by $3$ and keep the last digit), so they repeat with period $4$. Since $2020$ is a multiple of $4$, the units digit of $3^{2020}$ is the same as that of $3^4=81$, namely $\boxed{1}$.
