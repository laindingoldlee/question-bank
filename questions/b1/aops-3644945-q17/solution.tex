The full product is 720, so maximise $720-a[b(1+c(d-1))-1]$. Fix $e$; the other four numbers have only 24 orders. Evaluating that small exact expression gives maxima $465,585,620,640,652$ for $e=2,3,4,5,6$, respectively. The corresponding orders are $(5,4,6,3,2)$, $(5,4,6,2,3)$, $(5,3,6,2,4)$, $(4,3,6,2,5)$, $(4,3,5,2,6)$. Thus the maximum is $\boxed{652}$. The table is reproducible by choosing $a$ from the four available numbers, $b$ from the remaining three and $c$ from the remaining two; $d$ is then forced.
