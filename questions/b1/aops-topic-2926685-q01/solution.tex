Burnside over the $24$ rotations. Identity: $3^6=729$. Face rotations by $\pm90^\circ$ ($6$): cycles $1,1,4$, giving $3^3$ each. Face rotations by $180^\circ$ ($3$): cycles $1,1,2,2$, giving $3^4$ each. Vertex rotations ($8$): two $3$-cycles, $3^2$ each. Edge rotations ($6$): three $2$-cycles, $3^3$ each.
\[\dfrac{729+6\cdot27+3\cdot81+8\cdot9+6\cdot27}{24}=\dfrac{1368}{24}=\boxed{57}.\]
