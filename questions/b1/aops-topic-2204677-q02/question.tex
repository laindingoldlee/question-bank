A squirrel family has fewer than $60$ members. When the squirrels are arranged in $3$ rows of equal length, $2$ are left out; in $4$ rows of equal length, $3$ are left out; in $5$ rows of equal length, none are left out. How many squirrels are in each row when they are arranged in $5$ rows?
