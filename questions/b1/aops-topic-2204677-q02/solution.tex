Let the family have $n$ members. Then $n\equiv2\pmod3$, $n\equiv3\pmod4$ and $n\equiv0\pmod5$. The first two say $n+1$ is divisible by $3$ and by $4$, so $n+1$ is a multiple of $12$: $n\in\{11,23,35,47,59\}$. The only one divisible by $5$ is $n=35$. In $5$ rows there are $\dfrac{35}{5}=\boxed{7}$ squirrels per row.
