Divide numerator and denominator by $13!12!11!10!$. The expression becomes $155\cdot\dfrac{1+14+14\cdot13+14\cdot13\cdot12}{14\cdot13\cdot12\cdot11+1}=155\cdot\dfrac{2381}{24025}$. Since $24025=155^2$, the answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{2381}{155}}$.
