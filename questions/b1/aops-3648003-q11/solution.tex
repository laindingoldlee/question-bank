Use $A=(6,0)$, $C=(-3,3\sqrt3)$, $D=(-6,0)$, $E=(-3,-3\sqrt3)$. The square containing $E$ has $G=C+(-3\sqrt3,-9)$. Write $X=A+u(G-A)=D+v(E-D)$. Comparing coordinates gives $v=(\sqrt3-1)u$ and $12=6(1+\sqrt3)u$, so $u=\sqrt3-1$. Since $AG=6\sqrt6$, $AX=6\sqrt6(\sqrt3-1)=\sqrt{648}-\sqrt{216}$. Hence $\boxed{a+b=864}$.
