Find all triples of positive integers $(a,b,c)$ such that each of the equations
\[
x^2-ax+b=0,\qquad x^2-bx+c=0,\qquad x^2-cx+a=0
\]
has integer roots.

(A student claims that $a=b=c$ is forced, by subtracting the equations pairwise and solving for a common root $x$, and concludes that $(4,4,4)$ is the only triple. Find the error.)
