The expression is defined exactly when the radicand is nonnegative: $\dfrac{1+3a}{25}\ge0\iff1+3a\ge0\iff a\ge-\dfrac{1}{3}$. The domain is $\boxed{a\ge-\dfrac{1}{3}}$, that is, the interval $\left[-\dfrac{1}{3},\infty\right)$.
