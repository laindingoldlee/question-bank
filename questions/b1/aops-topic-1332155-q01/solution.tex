Complete squares one variable at a time:
\[
a^2+b^2+c^2+d^2-ab-bc-cd-d+\dfrac{2}{5}=\left(a-\dfrac {b}{2}\right)^2+\dfrac{3}{4}\left(b-\dfrac{2c}{3}\right)^2+\dfrac{2}{3}\left(c-\dfrac{3d}{4}\right)^2+\dfrac{5}{8}\left(d-\dfrac{4}{5}\right)^2 .
\]
(Expanding the right side gives back the left side.) A sum of nonnegative terms is $0$ only if each term is $0$, so $d=\dfrac{4}{5}$, $c=\dfrac{3d}{4}=\dfrac{3}{5}$, $b=\dfrac{2c}{3}=\dfrac{2}{5}$, $a=\dfrac {b}{2}=\dfrac{1}{5}$. The only solution is
\[
\boxed{(a,b,c,d)=\left(\dfrac{1}{5},\dfrac{2}{5},\dfrac{3}{5},\dfrac{4}{5}\right)}.
\]
