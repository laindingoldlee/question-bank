Let the side lengths be $ a,b,c $ and inradius $ r $. The two small triangles based on the two halves of a side each have area $\dfrac{ra}{4}$, or the analogous expression. Put $ x=\dfrac{ra}{4},y=\dfrac{rb}{4},z=\dfrac{rc}{4}$. The quadrilateral areas are $ x+y,x+z,y+z $, in some order. Since their sum is 55, $ x+y+z=\dfrac{55}{2}$. Subtracting 16,19,20 gives $ x,y,z $ in some order as $\dfrac{23}{2},\dfrac{17}{2},\dfrac{15}{2}$. The side ratio is therefore $\boxed{15:17:23}$.
\begin{asy}

real cx=593.0/46,cy=sqrt(289-cx*cx),scaleFactor=sqrt(110/(23*cy));pair A=(0,0),B=scaleFactor*(23,0),C=scaleFactor*(cx,cy);pair I=(15*A+17*B+23*C)/55;draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(I--(A+B)/2^^I--(B+C)/2^^I--(C+A)/2);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,N);label("$I$",I,S);
\end{asy}
