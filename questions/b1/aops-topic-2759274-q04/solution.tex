Subtract the three corner triangles. A corner triangle with sides along the triangle's sides has area equal to the product of the two side fractions times $[ABC]$:
\[[AZY]=\dfrac34\cdot\dfrac13\cdot120=30,\quad[BXZ]=\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac14\cdot120=15,\quad[CYX]=\dfrac23\cdot\dfrac12\cdot120=40.\]
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(1.5,4),B=(0,0),C=(6,0);pair X=(B+C)/2,Y=C+(2/3)*(A-C),Z=A+(3/4)*(B-A);
fill(X--Y--Z--cycle,rgb(0.85,0.9,1));draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(X--Y--Z--cycle);
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$X$",X,S);label("$Y$",Y,NE);label("$Z$",Z,W);
\end{asy}

So $[XYZ]=120-30-15-40=35$. Answer $\boxed{\textbf{(A)}\ 35}$.
