Factor out $14!$: since $\dfrac{20!}{14!}=15\cdot16\cdot17\cdot18\cdot19\cdot20=27907200$,
\[
20!+14!=14!\cdot27907201,\qquad 20!-14!=14!\cdot27907199 .
\]
Every prime up to $13$ divides $14!$, hence both numbers. Suppose a prime $p>13$ divides both. Then $p\nmid14!$, so $p$ divides both $27907201$ and $27907199$, hence their difference $2$, which is impossible. So the largest common prime factor is $\boxed{13}$.
