For $k\ge2$ take $m=2^k-2$ and $n=2^k\left(2^k-2\right)$. Then
\[m=2\left(2^{k-1}-1\right),\qquad n=2^{k+1}\left(2^{k-1}-1\right)\]
have the same prime divisors ($2$ and those of $2^{k-1}-1$), and
\[m+1=2^k-1,\qquad n+1=2^{2k}-2^{k+1}+1=\left(2^k-1\right)^2\]
have the same prime divisors. Since $m\ge2$, $n=2^km\ne m$, and different $k$ give different pairs. $\blacksquare$
