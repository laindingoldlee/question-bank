Suppose we make $m$ moves. Each move uses three of the four colours, so it leaves out exactly one colour. Let $o_R,o_U,o_G,o_K$ be the numbers of moves that leave out red, blue, green and black. Then $o_R+o_U+o_G+o_K=m$, and red is used in $m-o_R$ moves, one ball each time, so $m-o_R\le10$. In the same way $m-o_U\le15$ and $m-o_G\le20$. Therefore
\[
m=o_R+o_U+o_G+o_K\ge(m-10)+(m-15)+(m-20)+0=3m-45,
\]
so $2m\le45$ and $m\le22$.

$22$ moves are possible: make $12$ moves with blue, green, black, then $7$ with red, green, black, then $2$ with red, blue, black, and $1$ with red, blue, green. This uses $7+2+1=10$ red, $12+2+1=15$ blue, $12+7+1=20$ green and $12+7+2=21\le25$ black balls.

The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(D)}\ 22}$.
