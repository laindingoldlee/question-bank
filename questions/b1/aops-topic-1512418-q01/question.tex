The siding of a house has an area of $2250$ square feet, and it needs $2$ coats of paint. Paint is sold in two sizes:
\begin{itemize}
\item a quart costs $\$18$ and covers $80$ square feet;
\item a gallon costs $\$29$ and covers $320$ square feet.
\end{itemize}
What is the minimum cost of the paint needed for the job, and with that purchase, how much paint is left over?
