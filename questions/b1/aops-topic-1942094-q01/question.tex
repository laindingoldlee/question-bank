Find all integers $x$ such that
\[
x^x+x^{x^2-1}=x^{x^3-x^2}-x^2 .
\]
(Powers of a nonzero integer to a negative integer exponent are reciprocals, and $x=0$ is not allowed since $0^0$ is undefined.)
