\textbf{Positive $x$.} For $x=1$ the left side is $2$ and the right side is $0$. For $x=2$: $2^2+2^3=12$ and $2^{8-4}-4=12$, so $x=2$ works. For $x\ge3$ we have $(x^3-x^2)-(x^2-1)=x^3-2x^2+1\ge10$, so
\[
x^{x^3-x^2}\ge x^{10}\,x^{x^2-1}>3x^{x^2-1}\ge x^{x^2-1}+x^x+x^2,
\]
using $x^x\le x^{x^2-1}$ and $x^2\le x^{x^2-1}$. So the right side exceeds the left side and there is no solution.

\textbf{Negative $x$.} For $x=-1$: the left side is $(-1)^{-1}+(-1)^0=-1+1=0$ and the right side is $(-1)^{-2}-1=0$, so $x=-1$ works. Now let $x=-m$ with $m\ge2$. Then $x^2-1=m^2-1\ge3$ is a positive exponent, so $x^{x^2-1}$ is an integer with $|x^{x^2-1}|=m^{m^2-1}\ge m^3$. The exponents $x=-m$ and $x^3-x^2=-m^3-m^2$ are negative, so $|x^x|<1$ and $|x^{x^3-x^2}|<1$. Hence
\[
|\text{left side}|>m^{3}-1\ge m^2+1>|\text{right side}|,
\]
because $m^3-m^2\ge4>2$. No solution.

Therefore the solutions are $\boxed{x=2\text{ and }x=-1}$.
