The last digit of an integer cube ending in $5$ forces the root to end in $5$. Trying $455$, the binomial theorem gives $(450+5)^3=91125000+3037500+33750+125=94196375$. Since the cube function is strictly increasing on the real numbers, the unique real cube root is $\boxed{455}$.
