Put $\theta=\angle APC$. In right triangle $ACP$, $\angle A=90^\circ-\theta$. The given relation gives $\angle A+\angle B=\theta$, so $\angle ACB=180^\circ-\theta$. Subtract $\angle ACP=90^\circ$ to get $\angle PCQ=90^\circ-\theta$. The right triangles $CPQ$ and $ACP$ are therefore similar, with $\dfrac{PQ}{CP}=\dfrac{CP}{AC}$. Hence $PQ=\dfrac{20^2}{25}=\boxed{16}$.

\end{asy}
