Pentagon angle $EAB$ is $108^\circ$ and square angle $BAG$ is $90^\circ$, on opposite sides of $AB$. Thus the smaller angle $EAG$ is $360^\circ-108^\circ-90^\circ=162^\circ$. Because $AE=AG$, triangle $AEG$ has base angles $9^\circ$. Ray $EA$ lies inside angle $GED$ in this configuration, so $\angle GED=\angle GEA+\angle AED=9^\circ+108^\circ=\boxed{117^\circ}$.
