If both prime legs are odd, the sum of their squares is $2$ modulo $4$, which is not a square. Otherwise one leg is $2$, and if the other is $q$, then $c^2-q^2=4$. Thus $(c-q)(c+q)=4$. The factors are positive of the same parity, forcing both to be $2$, hence $q=0$, a contradiction.
