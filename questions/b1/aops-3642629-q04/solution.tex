After five days the total score is 25. Let Daphne's score be s. The leader has at most five points and the other eight players at most $s-1$, so $25\le5+s+8(s-1)=9s-3$. Therefore $s\ge4$. Her final score is at least four. If all nine others finished strictly above her, each would have at least five, making the total at least $4+9\cdot5=49$. But nine days contain 45 games, hence only 45 points. Contradiction.
