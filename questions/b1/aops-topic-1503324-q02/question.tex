In trapezoid $ABCD$, $AB\parallel CD$. Let $E$ be the midpoint of $CD$, and suppose that line $AE$ is the perpendicular bisector of $DB$. Let $F$ be the intersection of $AE$ and $DB$, and $G$ the intersection of $AC$ and $DB$. Show that $FG\cdot GC=AG\cdot GB$.
