Let $E$ be the event that the last red occurs after the last blue, and $F$ the event it occurs after the last white. Among red and blue pellets, the last is red with probability $\dfrac{5}{15}=\dfrac{1}{3}$; similarly $P(F)=\dfrac{5}{20}=\dfrac{1}{4}$. Their intersection means the final pellet overall is red, of probability $\dfrac{5}{30}=\dfrac{1}{6}$. The requested complement is $1-P(E)-P(F)+P(E\cap F)=1-\dfrac{1}{3}-\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{6}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{12}}$.
