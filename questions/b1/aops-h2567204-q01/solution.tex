The equilateral triangle has side $2\sqrt3$, so its area is $3\sqrt3$. A regular dodecagon of side $s$ has apothem $\dfrac{s}{(2\tan15^\circ)}$ and area
\[
\dfrac{1}{2}(12s)\dfrac{s}{2\tan15^\circ}=3s^2\cot15^\circ.
\]
Equating the areas gives
\[
\boxed{s=\sqrt{\sqrt3\tan15^\circ}}.
\]
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
real s=sqrt(sqrt(3)*tan(pi/12)),R=s/(2sin(pi/12));for(int j=0;j<12;++j)draw(R*dir(30j)--R*dir(30(j+1)));draw((0,0)--R*(dir(0)+dir(30))/2,red);label("$s$",R*(dir(0)+dir(30))/2,NE);label("regular dodecagon",(0,-R-.3));
\end{asy}
