Put $t=a^2+b^2$. Values with $t\ge9$ are nonpositive, so a positive maximum has $0<t<9$. Cauchy-Schwarz gives $(a+7b)^2\le50t$. By AM-GM applied to $t,\dfrac{9-t}{2},\dfrac{9-t}{2}$, we have $t(9-t)^2\le108$. Thus the square of the positive expression is at most $50\cdot108=5400$. Equality holds at $t=3$ and $b=7a$, with both variables positive. Consequently $\boxed{M^2=5400}$.
