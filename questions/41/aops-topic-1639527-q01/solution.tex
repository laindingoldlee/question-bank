Let the team play $x$ games after the first $100$. It won $80$ of the first $100$ and $\dfrac{x}{2}$ of the rest, so
\[
80+\dfrac{x}{2}=\dfrac{70}{100}(100+x)=70+\dfrac{7x}{10}.
\]
Then $10=\dfrac{7x}{10}-\dfrac{5x}{10}=\dfrac{x}{5}$, so $x=50$ and the team played $100+50=\boxed{150}$ games. (Check: it won $80+25=105$ games, and $\dfrac{105}{150}=70\%$.)
