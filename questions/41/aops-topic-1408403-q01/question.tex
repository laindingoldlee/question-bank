There are $50$ equally spaced points on a circle, numbered $1$ to $50$ clockwise. Starting at $1$, Sara draws connected segments, each joining two points $8$ spaces apart and moving clockwise from the end of the previous segment: the first segment goes from $1$ to $9$, the second from $9$ to $17$, and so on. What is the sum of the numbers at the endpoints of the $23$rd segment?
