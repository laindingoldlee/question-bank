After $k$ segments Sara has moved $8k$ spaces from point $1$, so she is at the point whose number is congruent to $1+8k$ modulo $50$ (with $50$ in place of $0$). The $23$rd segment goes from the point after $22$ segments to the point after $23$ segments:
\[
1+8\cdot22=177\equiv27,\qquad 1+8\cdot23=185\equiv35\pmod{50}.
\]
So it joins $27$ and $35$, and the sum is $\boxed{62}$.
