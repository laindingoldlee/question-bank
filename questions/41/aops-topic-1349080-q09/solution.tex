Each term splits as $\dfrac{1}{k(k+2)}=\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{1}{k}-\dfrac{1}{k+2}\right)$, so the sum telescopes:
\[
\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{1}{1}-\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{3}-\dfrac{1}{5}+\dots+\dfrac{1}{1997}-\dfrac{1}{1999}\right)=\dfrac{1}{2}\left(1-\dfrac{1}{1999}\right)=\boxed{\dfrac{999}{1999}}.
\]
