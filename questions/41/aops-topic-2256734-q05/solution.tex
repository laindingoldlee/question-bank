Time is distance over rate: $\dfrac{x}{y+7}=\dfrac13$ and $\dfrac{2y}{x+5}=\dfrac12$, so $y=3x-7$ and $4y=x+5$. Then $12x-28=x+5$, so $x=3$ and $y=2$. The new walk is $8\cdot3+10=34$ miles at $5\cdot2+7=17$ miles per hour, which takes $2$ hours $=\boxed{\textbf{(E) }120}$ minutes.
