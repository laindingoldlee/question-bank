For $k\ge2$, $k^2<k(k+1)$, so $\dfrac{1}{k^2}>\dfrac{1}{k(k+1)}=\dfrac{1}{k}-\dfrac{1}{k+1}$. Therefore
\[
\sum_{k=1}^{100}\dfrac{1}{k^2}>1+\sum_{k=2}^{100}\left(\dfrac{1}{k}-\dfrac{1}{k+1}\right)=1+\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{101}>1+0.5-0.01=1.49>1.4 .
\]
$\blacksquare$ (The sum is about $1.635$.)
