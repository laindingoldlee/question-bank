Any three of the points form a triangle unless they are collinear. By the condition, three of the points are collinear exactly when all three are among the $5$ points on the line. Hence the number of triangles is
\[\dbinom{11}{3}-\dbinom{5}{3}=165-10=\boxed{155}.\]
