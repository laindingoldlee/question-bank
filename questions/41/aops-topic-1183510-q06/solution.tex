An amount is $10a+25b$ cents with $a,b\ge0$. Since $25b\le99$, $b\in\{0,1,2,3\}$.
\begin{itemize}
\item Even $b$ (the amount is a multiple of $10$): $b=0$ gives $10,20,\ldots,90$, and $b=2$ gives $50,60,\ldots,90$, already listed. That is $9$ amounts.
\item Odd $b$ (the amount ends in $5$): $b=1$ gives $25,35,\ldots,95$ ($8$ amounts), and $b=3$ gives $75,85,95$, already listed.
\end{itemize}
In total $9+8=\boxed{17}$ amounts.
