Ten people have birthdays in a non-leap year; assume that each birthday is equally likely to be any of the $365$ days, independently of the others. Find the probability that no two of them have the same birthday. Is it
\[\dfrac{365\cdot364\cdot363\cdots356}{365^{10}}\ ?\]
Give the probability to three decimal places.
