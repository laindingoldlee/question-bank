In the worst case you first take all $11$ red and all $7$ yellow balls ($18$ balls) without a blue one, so $18$ is not enough. With $19$ balls, at most $18$ can come from the two largest colour groups. More precisely, missing a colour means all chosen balls come from two colours, which have at most $11+7=18$ balls. So $\boxed{19}$ balls guarantee all three colours.
