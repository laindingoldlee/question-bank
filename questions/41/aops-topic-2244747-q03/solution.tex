The graphs meet where $|3x-2|=|x-3|$, i.e. $3x-2=x-3$ or $3x-2=-(x-3)$. This gives $x=-\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $x=\dfrac{5}{4}$, and the points
\[P=\left(-\dfrac{1}{2},-\dfrac{7}{2}\right),\qquad Q=\left(\dfrac{5}{4},-\dfrac{7}{4}\right).\]
The vertex of $y=-|3x-2|$ is $V=\left(\dfrac{2}{3},0\right)$.

\begin{asy}

filldraw(V--P--Q--cycle,rgb(1,0.9,0.7),black);
dot(V); dot(P); dot(Q);
label("$\left(\dfrac{2}{3},0\right)$",V,N,fontsize(8pt)); label("$\left(-\dfrac{1}{2},-\dfrac{7}{2}\right)$",P,W,fontsize(8pt)); label("$\left(\dfrac{5}{4},-\dfrac{7}{4}\right)$",Q,E,fontsize(8pt));

\end{asy}

By the shoelace formula,
\[[VPQ]=\dfrac{1}{2}\left|\dfrac{2}{3}\left(-\dfrac{7}{2}+\dfrac{7}{4}\right)-\dfrac{1}{2}\left(-\dfrac{7}{4}-0\right)+\dfrac{5}{4}\left(0+\dfrac{7}{2}\right)\right|=\dfrac{1}{2}\left|-\dfrac{7}{6}+\dfrac{7}{8}+\dfrac{35}{8}\right|=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{49}{12}=\boxed{\dfrac{49}{24}}.\]
