Glue the four Democrats into one block. Then we arrange $6$ objects (the block and the $5$ Republicans) in a row, in $6!=720$ ways, and arrange the Democrats inside their block in $4!=24$ ways. The total is
\[
6!\cdot4!=720\cdot24=\boxed{17280}.
\]
