Each die has $21$ even outcomes out of $42$, so an even result has probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. The count $X$ is binomial with parameters $69,\dfrac{1}{2}$. Thus $\boxed{\Pr(X\ge27)=\dfrac{1}{2^{69}}\sum_{k=27}^{69}\binom{69}{k}}$, equivalently $1-\dfrac{1}{2^{69}}\sum_{k=0}^{26}\binom{69}{k}$.
