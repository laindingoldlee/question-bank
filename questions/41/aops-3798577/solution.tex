Each monomial has exponents $(a,b,c)$ with $0\le a,b,c\le20$ and $a+b+c\ge20$, because each of the twenty chosen factors contains at least one variable. Conversely, any such triple is realizable: take twenty slots and mark $a$ of them with $x$, then place $b$ marks for $y$ in unmarked slots first, and finally $c$ marks for $z$ in still-unmarked slots first. The sum condition ensures every slot is marked, and each variable occurs at most once per slot. Thus every slot corresponds to an available factor. There are $21^3$ exponent triples, of which $\binom{22}3$ have sum at most $19$ by stars and bars. The answer is $9261-1540=\boxed{7721}$.
