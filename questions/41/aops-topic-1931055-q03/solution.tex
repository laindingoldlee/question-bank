The two wrong-coloured tiles occupy a random pair of the $144$ positions, and all $\dbinom{144}{2}=10296$ pairs are equally likely.

Pairs of positions sharing an edge: in each of the $12$ rows there are $11$ horizontally adjacent pairs, and in each of the $12$ columns $11$ vertically adjacent pairs, so $2\cdot12\cdot11=264$ pairs.

The probability is $\dfrac{264}{10296}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{39}}$.
