A $12$-foot by $12$-foot square bathroom floor is tiled with $1$-foot square tiles. Two of the tiles are the wrong colour. If the tiles are placed randomly, what is the probability that the two wrong-coloured tiles share an edge? Express your answer as a common fraction.
