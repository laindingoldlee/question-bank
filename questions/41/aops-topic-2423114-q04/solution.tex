Build the board in rings around the central tile. The central tile is ring $0$. Ring $k$ (for $k\ge1$) is a hexagonal ring with $k+1$ tiles along each of its six sides; counting each corner tile once, it has $6k$ tiles. A board with $N$ tiles along each edge consists of rings $0,1,\dots,N-1$, so it has
\[1+6\left(1+2+\cdots+(N-1)\right)=1+6\cdot\dfrac{(N-1)N}{2}=\boxed{3N^2-3N+1}\]
tiles. Check: $N=3$ gives $27-9+1=19$, and $N=2$ gives $7$. (Counting by rows also works: the rows have $N,N+1,\dots,2N-1,\dots,N+1,N$ tiles.)
