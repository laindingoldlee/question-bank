A $90^\circ$ clockwise rotation about the origin sends $(x,y)$ to $(y,-x)$. The images are $A'(1,1)$, $B'(-1,0)$, $C'(1,-2)$, $D'(2,-2)$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;draw((-3,0)--(3,0),gray,Arrow);draw((0,-3)--(0,3),gray,Arrow);
pair A=(-1,1),B=(0,-1),C=(2,1),D=(2,2);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);
pair A2=(1,1),B2=(-1,0),C2=(1,-2),D2=(2,-2);draw(A2--B2--C2--D2--cycle,dashed);
label("$A$",A,W);label("$B$",B,S);label("$C$",C,E);label("$D$",D,N);label("$A'$",A2,N);label("$B'$",B2,W);label("$C'$",C2,S);label("$D'$",D2,E);
\end{asy}

The sum of the coordinates is $(1+1)+(-1+0)+(1-2)+(2-2)=\boxed{0}$.
