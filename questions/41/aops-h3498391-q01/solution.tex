More generally write the endpoint as $(m,m,m)$ and let $N=3m$. A uniform path is a uniformly shuffled word with $m$ copies of each of three step types. After $t$ steps, the expected product of the first two coordinate counts is
\[
\mathbb E[ab]=t(t-1)\dfrac{m^2}{N(N-1)}.
\]
Indeed, $ab$ counts ordered pairs of distinct step positions among the first $t$ whose types are first-coordinate then second-coordinate, with probability $\dfrac{m}{N}\cdot\dfrac{m}{N-1}$. The same holds for $bc,ca$. By linearity of expectation,
\[
A=\dfrac{3m^2}{N(N-1)}\sum_{t=0}^{N}t(t-1)
=\dfrac{3m^2}{N(N-1)}\cdot\dfrac{N(N+1)(N-1)}{3}
=m^2(3m+1).
\]
For $m=2024$, the remainder is $24^2\cdot73\equiv\boxed{48}\pmod{1000}$.
