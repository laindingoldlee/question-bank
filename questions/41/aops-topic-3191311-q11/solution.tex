The Reuleaux region is the equilateral triangle together with three circular segments. Each segment has area $\dfrac{\pi s^2}{6}-\dfrac{\sqrt3s^2}{4}$. The total is $\dfrac{\pi-\sqrt3}{2}s^2$. The circle has radius $\dfrac{s}{2}$ and area $\dfrac{\pi s^2}{4}$. Their ratio is $\boxed{2-\dfrac{2\sqrt3}{\pi}}$.

\end{asy}
