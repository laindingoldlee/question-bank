For certain real values of $a,b,c$ and $d$, the equation $x^4+ax^3+bx^2+cx+d=0$ has four non-real roots. The product of two of these roots is $13+i$ and the sum of the other two roots is $3+4i$, where $i=\sqrt{-1}$. Find $b$. In your solution, explain why the product of the other two roots must be exactly $13-i$.
