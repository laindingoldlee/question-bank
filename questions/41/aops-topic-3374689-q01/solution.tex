If the two perpendicular sides have length $x>0$, the third fenced side is $20-2x>0$. Area is $x(20-2x)=50-2(x-5)^2\le50$. Equality occurs at $x=5$ and the other side $10$. Thus the maximum is $\boxed{50\text{ m}^2}$.
