The eight available cells form an 8-cycle under knight moves. The two specified corners are opposite vertices of that cycle. If $j$ of the twelve moves go clockwise, the displacement is $2j-12\equiv4\pmod8$. Thus $j\in\{0,4,8,12\}$. The count is $\binom{12}0+\binom{12}4+\binom{12}8+\binom{12}{12}=\boxed{992}$.
