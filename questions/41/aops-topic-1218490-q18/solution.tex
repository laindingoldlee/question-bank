Out of $500\cdot50=25000$ people, $50$ have the disease and test positive; of the $24950$ healthy people, $2\%$, i.e. $499$, test positive. So
\[p=\dfrac{50}{50+499}=\boxed{\dfrac{50}{549}}\approx0.091.\]
