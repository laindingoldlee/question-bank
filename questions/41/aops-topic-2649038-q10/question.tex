Four students, Adria, Bill, Carl and Daniel, each either always tells the truth or always lies.
\begin{itemize}
\item Adria: ``I only got $75\%$ on the test.''
\item Bill: ``You got $90\%$, liar.''
\item Carl: ``Bill is lying.''
\item Daniel: ``Exactly one of us four is honest.''
\item Bill: ``Daniel is telling the truth.''
\end{itemize}
How many of the four are honest?
