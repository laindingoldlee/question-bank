If three side lengths of a cyclic quadrilateral and the radius of its circumcircle are known, the fourth side can be found. Do this in the following case: a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ is inscribed in a circle of radius $5$, and $AB=BC=CD=6$. Find $DA$.
