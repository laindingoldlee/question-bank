The product of all six labels is $720=2^4\cdot3^2\cdot5$. Deleting any one label from 1 through 6 removes at most two factors of 2 and at most one factor of 3. The remaining product therefore still has both a factor of 2 and a factor of 3, so it is always divisible by 6. The probability is $\boxed1$, choice E.
