There are $1000$ students who have been absent, $300$ who have been tardy and $750$ freshmen. The students in at least one of these groups number at most
\[
1000+300+750=2050,
\]
with equality exactly when the three groups do not overlap. So the number of students in none of the groups is at least $3000-2050=950$, and it equals $950$ when the groups are disjoint (which is possible). The least possible number is $\boxed{950}$.
