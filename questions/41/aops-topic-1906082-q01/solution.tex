The $100$ kg contain $50$ kg of copper, $30$ kg of zinc and $20$ kg of nickel. Adding only nickel leaves copper and zinc at $50:30=5:3$, so nickel must become $30$ kg. Hence $30-20=\boxed{10}$ kg of nickel must be added.
