An isosceles trapezoid $ABCD$ with $AB\parallel CD$ has $AB=24$, $CD=16$ and $AD=BC=8$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the area of $ABCD$.
\item[(b)] Find the radius of the circle through $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$.
\end{enumerate}
