$20^{20}=2^{40}\cdot5^{20}$, so its divisors are $2^a5^b$ with $0\le a\le40$ and $0\le b\le20$: there are $41\cdot21=861$ of them. Such a divisor is a multiple of $10^6=2^65^6$ exactly when $a\ge6$ and $b\ge6$, which gives $35\cdot15=525$ divisors. The probability is
\[\dfrac{525}{861}=\dfrac{35\cdot15}{41\cdot21}=\boxed{\dfrac{25}{41}}.\]
