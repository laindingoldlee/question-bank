Two natural numbers $n$ and $m$ have $\gcd(n,m)=6$ and $\operatorname{lcm}(n,m)=600$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Which primes occur in the prime factorisation of $n$ or of $m$?
\item[(b)] For each of those primes, what is the smaller of its exponents in $n$ and $m$?
\item[(c)] For each of those primes, what is the larger of its exponents in $n$ and $m$?
\item[(d)] How many unordered pairs $\{n,m\}$ of positive integers have greatest common divisor $6$ and least common multiple $600$? How many ordered pairs $(n,m)$?
\end{enumerate}
