A cube of side $5$ is divided into $125$ unit cubes. A plane passes through a vertex $O$ of the big cube, through the opposite vertex $Q$, and through the midpoints of the two vertical edges of the big cube that contain neither $O$ nor $Q$ (the big cube is standing on a face, with $O$ on the bottom face and $Q$ on the top face). How many unit cubes does the plane cut (meet in interior points)?
