Let $a$ be the number of subsets of $\{1,2,\ldots,9\}$, including the empty set and full set. Let $b$ be the number of triangles whose vertices are vertices of a regular octagon. Let $c$ be the sum of the squares of the roots of $x^2=5x-6$. Find $(a+b+c)^2$.
