The passenger always moves forward relative to the ground, at $310$ or $290$ kilometres per hour. The forward and backward legs have equal lengths and equal speeds relative to the train, hence equal durations. The ground average speed is therefore $\dfrac{310+290}{2}=\boxed{300\text{ kilometres per hour}}$. Equivalently the net displacement relative to the train is zero, while all ground motion is forward.
