Pair the first and last factors, and the middle two. Since $r^2=-2r-6$, they become $r^2+7r+10=5r+4$ and $r^2+7r+12=5r+6$. Hence $a=25r^2+50r+24=-150+24=-126$. Therefore $a+200=\boxed{74}$, independently of the root chosen.
