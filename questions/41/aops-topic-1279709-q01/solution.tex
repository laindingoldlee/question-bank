Multiply by $20!$: each term becomes $\dfrac{20!}{(2k+1)!\,(19-2k)!}=\dbinom{20}{2k+1}$. The sum of the odd-index binomial coefficients of row $20$ is half of $2^{20}$, i.e. $2^{19}$. So the sum is
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{2^{19}}{20!}}.
\]
