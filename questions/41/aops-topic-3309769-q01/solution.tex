An integer $x$ can be any of $-7,-6,\ldots,7$, and each gives two nonzero values of $y$: $30$ points. Likewise there are $30$ points with integer $y$. Both coordinates are integers precisely for $(\pm1,\pm7),(\pm7,\pm1),(\pm5,\pm5)$, giving $12$ overlaps. Inclusion--exclusion gives $\boxed{30+30-12=48}$.

\end{asy}
