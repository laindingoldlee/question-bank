Let $k=\lfloor z\rfloor$ and $m=\lfloor yz\rfloor$. Dividing the first two equations gives $z=\dfrac{100k}{99}$; requiring $k\le z<k+1$ gives $1\le k\le98$. Similarly $yz=\dfrac{50m}{49}$ and $1\le m\le48$. Each pair yields exactly one positive triple: $x=\dfrac{98}{m}$, $y=\dfrac{99m}{(98k)}$ and $z=\dfrac{100k}{99}$. These formulas satisfy all equations and floor restrictions, so the total is $98\cdot48=\boxed{4704}$.
