For $n=1$ and $n=2$ we get $\sqrt[3]{11}$ and $\sqrt[3]{138}$, not integers ($2^3<11<3^3$ and $5^3<138<6^3$). For $n=3$: $3^7+10=2187+10=2197=13^3$. So $n=\boxed{3}$.
