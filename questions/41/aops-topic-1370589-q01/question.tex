For positive numbers $x$ and $y$ the operation $\spadesuit(x,y)$ is defined as
\[\spadesuit(x,y)=x-\dfrac{1}{y}.\]
What is $\spadesuit\left(2,\spadesuit(2,2)\right)$?

$\textbf{(A) }\dfrac{2}{3}\qquad\textbf{(B) }1\qquad\textbf{(C) }\dfrac{4}{3}\qquad\textbf{(D) }\dfrac{5}{3}\qquad\textbf{(E) }2$
