Work from the inside out. First
\[\spadesuit(2,2)=2-\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{3}{2} .\]
Then
\[\spadesuit\left(2,\dfrac{3}{2}\right)=2-\dfrac{1}{\dfrac{3}{2}}=2-\dfrac{2}{3}=\dfrac{4}{3} .\]
The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(C) }\dfrac{4}{3}}$.
