$511=8^3-1$ and $63=8^2-1$, so
\[511\cdot63=\left(8^3-1\right)\left(8^2-1\right)=8^5-8^3-8^2+1.\]
Now $8^5-8^3=63\cdot8^3=77000_8$, and subtracting $8^2=100_8$ and adding $1$ gives
\[511\cdot63=76701_8.\]
(Check: $7\cdot4096+6\cdot512+7\cdot64+1=32193=511\cdot63$.) The digit sum is $7+6+7+0+1=\boxed{21}$.
