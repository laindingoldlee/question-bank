Four balls are put into three boxes; a box may stay empty. Count the possible outcomes in each of the following models, and explain why the four answers are not simply related by dividing by $4!$ or $3!$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] The balls are distinguishable (numbered $1$ to $4$) and the boxes are distinguishable (labelled $A$, $B$, $C$).
\item[(b)] The balls are identical and the boxes are distinguishable.
\item[(c)] The balls are distinguishable and the boxes are identical.
\item[(d)] The balls are identical and the boxes are identical.
\end{enumerate}
