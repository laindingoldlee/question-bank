There must be at least $3$ men.
\begin{itemize}
\item $3$ men and $2$ women: $\dbinom43\dbinom{10}2=4\cdot45=180$ committees.
\item $4$ men and $1$ woman: $\dbinom44\dbinom{10}1=10$ committees.
\end{itemize}
The total is $\boxed{190}$.
