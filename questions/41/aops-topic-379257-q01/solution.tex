An average of $10$ hours over $6$ weeks means $6\cdot10=60$ hours in total. In the first five weeks she worked $8+11+7+12+10=48$ hours, so in the final week she needs
\[
60-48=12\text{ hours},
\]
answer $\boxed{\textbf{(D)}\ 12}$.
