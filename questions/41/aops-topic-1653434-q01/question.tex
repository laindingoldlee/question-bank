The symbol $\dbinom{n}{k}$ (read ``$n$ choose $k$'') denotes the number of ways to choose $k$ objects from $n$ different objects when the order of choosing does not matter.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find $\dbinom52$ by listing the choices of $2$ letters from $\{A,B,C,D,E\}$.
\item[(b)] Explain why $\dbinom nk=\dfrac{n!}{k!\,(n-k)!}$, and use it to compute $\dbinom{10}{4}$.
\end{enumerate}
