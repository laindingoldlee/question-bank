Write $t=\dfrac{AE}{AC}$ and $K=[ABC]$. Since $AD$ is a median and $F\in BE$, barycentric ratios give $[ABF]=\dfrac{t}{1+t}K$ and $[DEF]=\dfrac{t(1-t)}{2(1+t)}K$. These can also be obtained by taking $A=(0,2),B=(0,0),C=(2,0)$ and using determinants. Dividing the given areas yields $\dfrac{16}{44}=\dfrac{1-t}{2}$, hence $t=\dfrac{3}{11}$. Finally $[AEF]=t[ABF]=\dfrac{3}{11}\cdot44=\boxed{12}$, choice B.
