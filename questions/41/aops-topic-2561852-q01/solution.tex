\textbf{(a)} Use the rotation to fix Ron's seat. Harry's seat is then forced (opposite Ron), and the other $6$ boys fill the remaining $6$ seats in $6!=\boxed{720}$ ways.

\textbf{(b)} Without restrictions there are $7!=5040$ seatings (fix one boy's seat and arrange the other seven). Count those in which Snape and Malfoy are neighbours: fix Snape's seat; Malfoy sits on his left or right ($2$ ways), and the other $6$ boys can be seated in $6!$ ways, giving $2\cdot720=1440$. So $5040-1440=\boxed{3600}$ seatings keep them apart.
