Eight boys, among them Ron, Harry, Snape and Malfoy, sit at a round table with eight equally spaced seats. Seatings that differ only by a rotation are considered the same.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] In how many seatings do Ron and Harry sit directly opposite each other?
\item[(b)] In how many seatings are Snape and Malfoy not next to each other?
\end{enumerate}
