The data give $f(1)=1,f(2)=4,f(3)=9$. Therefore the monic cubic $f(x)-x^2$ has roots $1,2,3$, so $f(x)-x^2=(x-1)(x-2)(x-3)$. At $4$, $f(4)=16+6=22$. The product is $36\cdot22=\boxed{792}$.
