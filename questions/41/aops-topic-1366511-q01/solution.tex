The exponent of $2$ in $N!$ is (Legendre's formula)
\[
\left\lfloor\dfrac{N}{2}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{N}{4}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{N}{8}\right\rfloor+\cdots,
\]
since $\left\lfloor\dfrac{N}{2^i}\right\rfloor$ counts the multiples of $2^i$ among $1,\dots,N$, and a number divisible by exactly $2^j$ is counted once for each $i=1,\dots,j$. For $N=2^k$ every term is exact:
\[
n=2^{k-1}+2^{k-2}+\cdots+2+1=\boxed{2^k-1}.
\]
For example, $4!=24=2^3\cdot3$ and $8!=40320=2^7\cdot315$. So $(2^k)!=2^{2^k-1}\cdot m$ with $m=\dfrac{(2^k)!}{2^{2^k-1}}$ odd. (If $m$ is not required to be odd, the pairs are exactly $\left(m,n\right)=\left(\dfrac{(2^k)!}{2^n},n\right)$ with $0\le n\le2^k-1$.)
