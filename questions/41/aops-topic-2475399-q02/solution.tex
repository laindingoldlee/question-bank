The $k$th term is $1+2+\cdots+k=\dfrac{k(k+1)}2=\dbinom{k+1}{2}$. By Pascal's rule, $\dbinom{k+2}{3}-\dbinom{k+1}{3}=\dbinom{k+1}{2}$, so the sum telescopes:
\[\sum_{k=1}^{300}\dbinom{k+1}2=\sum_{k=1}^{300}\left(\dbinom{k+2}3-\dbinom{k+1}3\right)=\dbinom{302}{3}-\dbinom23=\dbinom{302}3.\]
Hence the sum is
\[\dbinom{302}{3}=\dfrac{302\cdot301\cdot300}{6}=\boxed{4545100}.\]
