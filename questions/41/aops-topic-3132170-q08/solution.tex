Let its leading coefficient be the nonzero integer $a$ and its prime roots $p,q$. The polynomial is $a(x-p)(x-q)$, so the coefficient sum is $a(p-1)(q-1)$. This is a positive prime, forcing $a>0$. The distinct factors $p-1,q-1$ are nonnegative positive integers; one must be 1, hence one root is 2. The other root $q$ is odd, so $q-1$ is even. To be prime after multiplication it must be 2, and $a=1$. Thus the roots are 2,3 and their sum is $\boxed5$.
