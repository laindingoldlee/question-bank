If $p\ne3$, then $p$ is not divisible by $3$, so $p\equiv\pm1\pmod3$ and $p^2\equiv1\pmod3$. Then $p^2+2\equiv0\pmod3$, and since $p^2+2>3$, it is not prime. So the condition forces $p=3$. Then $p^2+2=11$ is indeed prime, and $p^3+2=29$ is prime. $\blacksquare$
