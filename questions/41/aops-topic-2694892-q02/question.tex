Two straight train tracks, one running north--south and the other east--west, cross at point $X$. Train $A$ starts $15$ miles west of $X$ and travels east toward $X$ at a steady $44$ miles per hour. At the same moment train $B$ starts $10$ miles north of $X$ and travels south toward $X$ at a steady $26$ miles per hour. Which train reaches $X$ first, and how many minutes earlier?
