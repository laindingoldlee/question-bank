In a $30$-$60$-$90$ triangle the side opposite the $30^\circ$ angle is half the hypotenuse and the side opposite the $60^\circ$ angle is $\sqrt3$ times that. So the legs are $6$ and $6\sqrt3$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair C=(0,0), B=(6*sqrt(3),0), A=(0,6);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw((0.6,0)--(0.6,0.6)--(0,0.6));
label("$12$",(A+B)/2,NE); label("$6$",(A+C)/2,W); label("$6\sqrt3$",(B+C)/2,S);
label("$30^\circ$",B+(-2.1,0.45)); label("$60^\circ$",A+(0.75,-1.5));
label("$A$",A,N); label("$B$",B,E); label("$C$",C,SW);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot6\cdot6\sqrt3=\boxed{18\sqrt3}$ square inches.
