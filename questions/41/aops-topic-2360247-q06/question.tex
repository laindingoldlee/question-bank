Twenty students, among them a pair of twins and a student Sally (who is not one of the twins), take part in a class math competition, and all $20!$ possible final rankings are equally likely. The student in first place wins a gift card. Then all $20$ students enter a raffle for a math book: the student in last place gets $2$ raffle tickets, every other student gets $1$, and one of the $21$ tickets is drawn uniformly at random. The probability that one of the twins wins the gift card and Sally wins the raffle can be written as $\dfrac mn$ in lowest terms. What is $m^2+n$?
