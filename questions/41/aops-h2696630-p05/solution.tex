The first player places a horizontal domino in the two central cells: row $3$, columns $3,4$. Thereafter, respond to every move by its image under a half-turn about the board center.

After each first-player turn the occupied cells are half-turn symmetric. Thus the image of a newly placed bar is unoccupied unless it overlaps that same new bar. A vertical bar and its rotated image lie in different columns, because the board has an even number of columns. A horizontal bar can overlap its image only in the middle row and only if it reaches one of the central two cells; those cells are already occupied. Hence every response is legal and disjoint.

The opponent can never fill the board, since the corresponding rotated bar remains available. The game cannot stop with vacant cells because a single-cell piece is always legal. Therefore the first player necessarily makes the final move.
