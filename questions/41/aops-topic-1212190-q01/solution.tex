The sum of $1,\dots,26$ is $351$, so $ab=351-a-b$, i.e. $ab+a+b+1=352$, or
\[(a+1)(b+1)=352=2^5\cdot11.\]
Both factors are between $2$ and $27$; the only such factorisation is $16\cdot22$. So $\{a,b\}=\{15,21\}$ and $|a-b|=\boxed{6}$.
