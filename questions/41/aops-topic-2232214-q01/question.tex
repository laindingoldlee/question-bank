Let $a\ne0$, $b$, $c$ be real numbers.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that if $b^2-4ac\ge0$, the solutions of $ax^2+bx+c=0$ are $x=\dfrac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}$.
\item[(b)] Prove the ``short'' quadratic formula: if the middle coefficient is even, $b=2b'$, and $(b')^2-ac\ge0$, then the solutions of $ax^2+2b'x+c=0$ are
\[x=\dfrac{-b'\pm\sqrt{(b')^2-ac}}{a}.\]
\end{enumerate}
(Part (b) may use (a), but (a) must be proved from scratch.)
