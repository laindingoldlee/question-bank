They are $101,108,\dots,997$, since $101=7\cdot14+3$ is the first three-digit one and $997=7\cdot142+3$ the last ($997+7>999$). These form an arithmetic sequence with difference $7$, with $\dfrac{997-101}{7}+1=128+1=\boxed{129}$ terms.
