In regular hexagon $ABCDEF$ with side length $1$, a point $P_1$ is chosen uniformly at random on segment $\overline{AB}$, $P_2$ on $\overline{BC}$, $P_3$ on $\overline{CD}$, $P_4$ on $\overline{DE}$ and $P_5$ on $\overline{EF}$, all independently. (No point is chosen on $\overline{FA}$.) Find, with proof, the expected value of the area of pentagon $P_1P_2P_3P_4P_5$.
