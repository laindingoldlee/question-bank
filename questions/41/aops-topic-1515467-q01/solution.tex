Subtracting a number means adding its negative, so
\[
x-y=x+(-y)=-\big((-x)+y\big)=-(y-x),
\]
using $-(u+v)=(-u)+(-v)$ and $-(-x)=x$. Hence $x-y=-(y-x)=\boxed{-7}$. (Both $y-x=-(x-y)$ and $x-y=-(y-x)$ hold; they are the same statement.)
