Ariceli walks $x_1$ yards from home, then turns right and walks $x_2$ yards, then turns right again and walks $x_3$ yards, and continues in this way, turning right after each stretch, until she has turned $99$ times and then walked $x_{100}$ yards. The numbers $x_1,x_2,\dots,x_{100}$ are consecutive positive odd integers in increasing order. She ends up exactly $d$ yards from home. What is $d$? Express your answer in simplest radical form.
