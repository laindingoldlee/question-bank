How many permutations of $(1,2,\dots,8)$ have no two consecutive integers $i,i+1$ appearing next to each other in increasing order (that is, $i$ immediately followed by $i+1$)? For example, $(2,1,4,3)$ is such a permutation of $(1,2,3,4)$ but $(3,1,2,4)$ is not.
