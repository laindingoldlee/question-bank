Bring everything to one side (do not multiply by $4x-5$, whose sign is unknown):
\[
\dfrac{x^2-9x+7-x(4x-5)}{4x-5}\le0\iff\dfrac{-3x^2-4x+7}{4x-5}\le0\iff\dfrac{3x^2+4x-7}{4x-5}\ge0\iff\dfrac{(3x+7)(x-1)}{4x-5}\ge0 .
\]
The critical points are $-\dfrac{7}{3}$, $1$ (zeros of the numerator, allowed) and $\dfrac{5}{4}$ (zero of the denominator, excluded). Signs of the factors:
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
 & x<-\dfrac{7}{3} & -\dfrac{7}{3}<x<1 & 1<x<\dfrac{5}{4} & x>\dfrac{5}{4}\\ \hline
3x+7 & - & + & + & +\\
x-1 & - & - & + & +\\
4x-5 & - & - & - & +\\ \hline
\text{quotient} & - & + & - & +
\end{array}
\]
So the solution set is
\[
\boxed{-\dfrac{7}{3}\le x\le1\ \text{ or }\ x>\dfrac{5}{4}},\qquad\text{i.e. } x\in\left[-\dfrac{7}{3},1\right]\cup\left(\dfrac{5}{4},\infty\right).
\]
