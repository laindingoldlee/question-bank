Regardless of the first person's type, the second has the opposite type: a first liar makes the second claim true, and a first truth-teller makes it false. Thus the first two contain one of each. Whenever a prefix has equal numbers, the next claim is true, adding a truth-teller. The following prefix then has one more truth-teller, making the next claim false and adding a liar. Therefore positions 3, 5,...,125 are truthful and positions 4, 6,...,124 are liars. There are $1+61=\boxed{62}$ liars in total.
