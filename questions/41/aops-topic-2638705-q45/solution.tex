Group the terms with $w$ and without it. Both groups share the factor $xyz+2xy-3xz-2yz-6x-4y+6z+12$. This equals $(x-2)(yz+2y-3z-6)=(x-2)(y-3)(z+2)$. Therefore the full factorization is $\boxed{(w+1)(x-2)(y-3)(z+2)}$.
