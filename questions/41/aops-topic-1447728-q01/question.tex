A box contains $7000$ red balls, $5000$ green balls and $2000$ white balls. People draw balls one at a time at random, without replacement.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that the probability $p$ that the first $7000$ people all draw red balls is $p=\dfrac{1}{\dbinom{14000}{7000}}$.
\item[(b)] Prove that $\dfrac{4^n}{2n+1}\le\dbinom{2n}{n}\le4^n$ for every positive integer $n$.
\item[(c)] Use (b) and $\log_{10}4\approx0.60206$ to show that $10^{-4215}<p<10^{-4210}$.
\end{enumerate}
