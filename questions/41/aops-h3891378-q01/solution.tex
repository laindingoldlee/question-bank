An odd $n$ makes the expression nonintegral, so $n$ is even. Factor:
\[\dfrac{n^4}{4}-2026n^2+1=
\left(\dfrac{n^2}{2}-45n-1\right)
\left(\dfrac{n^2}{2}+45n-1\right).\]
For $2\le n\le90$, the first factor is $\dfrac{n(n-90)}{2}-1<0$, while the second is positive. Thus no such even $n$ works. The next candidate is $n=92$, giving factors $91$ and $8371$:
\[91\cdot8371=7\cdot13\cdot11\cdot761.\]
The number 761 is prime: none of $2,3,5,7,11,13,17,19,23$, the primes at most $\sqrt{761}<28$, divides it. These four primes are distinct, so the smallest $n$ is $\boxed{92}$.
