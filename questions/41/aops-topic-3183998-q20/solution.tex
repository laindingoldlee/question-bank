Modulo $100$, $33^5\equiv93$ and $33^{10}\equiv49$, so $33^{20}\equiv1$. All three exponents $33,333,3333$ are congruent to $13$ modulo $20$, and all three bases are congruent to $33$. Repeated squaring gives $33^{13}\equiv13$. Hence the product is congruent to $27\cdot13^3\equiv\boxed{19}\pmod{100}$.
