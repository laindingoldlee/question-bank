Circle \(C\) has diameter \(12\) feet and centre \(O\). A straight path enters the circle, runs to \(O\), turns through \(90^\circ\) there, and runs straight out of the circle. Let \(P\) be the area of the smaller of the two regions into which the path divides the disc. Find \(\dfrac{P}{\pi}\).

\(\textbf{(A) } 6 \qquad\textbf{(B) } 9 \qquad\textbf{(C) } 18 \qquad\textbf{(D) } 36 \qquad\textbf{(E) } 48\)
