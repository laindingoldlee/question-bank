Let him fall asleep $x$ hours after $4$ AM and wake $y$ hours after $11$ AM, with $(x,y)$ uniform on the square $[0,2]\times[0,2]$. He sleeps $(7+y)-x$ hours, so he gets less than $6$ hours exactly when $x-y>1$, the shaded triangle of area $\dfrac{1}{2}$.

label("$x$",(1,0),S); label("$y$",(0,1),W); label("$0$",(0,0),SW); label("$2$",(2,0),S); label("$2$",(0,2),W);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The probability of at least $6$ hours is $1-\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{2}}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{8}}$.
