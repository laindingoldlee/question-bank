Let $q$ be the probability of no arrival in five minutes. No arrivals in twenty minutes has probability $q^4=1-\dfrac{609}{625}=\dfrac{16}{625}=(\dfrac{2}{5})^4$. Thus $q=\dfrac{2}{5}$ and the requested probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{3}{5}}$. Raising the arrival probability to the fourth power instead counts an arrival in every interval.
