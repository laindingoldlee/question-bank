Let the legs be $a,b$ and hypotenuse $c$. Area equals both $\dfrac{ab}{2}$ and $r\dfrac{a+b+c}{2}$, so $r=\dfrac{ab}{a+b+c}=\dfrac{a+b-c}{2}$; the last identity follows from $c^2=a^2+b^2$. Modulo $2$, squares equal their bases, so $a+b\equiv c\pmod2$. Hence $a+b-c$ is even and $r$ is integral.
