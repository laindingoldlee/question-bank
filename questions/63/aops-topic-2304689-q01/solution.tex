By the law of total probability, average the three conditional probabilities:
\[\dfrac13\cdot\dfrac14\left(1+\dfrac45+\dfrac{16}{25}\right)=\dfrac1{12}\cdot\dfrac{61}{25}=\boxed{\dfrac{61}{300}}\approx20.3\%.\]
