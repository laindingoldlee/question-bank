A pig is trying to cross a road. If there are no cars, he crosses successfully with probability $25\%$. Each car on the road multiplies his chance by $\dfrac45$; for example, with $1$ car the probability is $25\%\cdot\dfrac45$. The number of cars is $0$, $1$ or $2$, each with probability $\dfrac13$. What is the probability that the pig crosses the road?
