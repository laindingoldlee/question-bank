Since
\[k(k+1)=\dfrac{k(k+1)(k+2)-(k-1)k(k+1)}3,\]
the sum telescopes:
\[\sum_{k=1}^{99}k(k+1)=\dfrac{99\cdot100\cdot101-0}3=33\cdot100\cdot101=\boxed{333300}.\]
(Equivalently, $\sum k(k+1)=\sum k^2+\sum k$, with $\sum k^2$ found by telescoping $x^3-(x-1)^3=3x^2-3x+1$, as in the post.)
