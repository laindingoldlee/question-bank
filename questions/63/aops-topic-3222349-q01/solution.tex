Integer multiplication, for example repeated squaring, gives $19^{25}=93076495688256089536609610280499$ and $3^{67}=92709463147897837085761925410587$. Their difference is the positive integer $367032540358252450847684869912$. Hence $\boxed{19^{25}>3^{67}}$. One reproducible exact procedure forms powers by repeatedly squaring a base and multiplying the powers corresponding to the $1$ bits of the exponent; no logarithmic approximation or rounding is involved.
