The angle bisectors of a triangle are concurrent, so $I$ is the incentre and $AI$ bisects $\angle A$. Here $\angle A=180^\circ-63^\circ-51^\circ=66^\circ$, so $\angle IAB=33^\circ$ and $\angle IBA=\dfrac{63^\circ}{2}=31.5^\circ$. In triangle $AIB$,
\[
\angle AIB=180^\circ-33^\circ-31.5^\circ=\boxed{\dfrac{231}{2}^\circ=115.5^\circ} .
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0),C=(6,0);
pair A=extension(B,B+dir(63),C,C+dir(129));
real a=abs(C-B),bb=abs(A-C),c=abs(B-A);
pair I=(a*A+bb*B+c*C)/(a+bb+c);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(B--I,linewidth(0.8));draw(C--I,linewidth(0.8));
draw(A--I,blue+dashed);
draw(arc(B,0.7,0,63),linewidth(0.6));
draw(arc(C,0.7,180-51,180),linewidth(0.6));
draw(arc(I,0.45,degrees(A-I),degrees(B-I)),red+linewidth(0.8));
label("$115.5^\circ$",I+0.95*dir((degrees(A-I)+degrees(B-I))/2),red);
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$I$",I,1.3*S);
label("$63^\circ$",B+1.2*dir(13),fontsize(9pt));label("$51^\circ$",C+1.2*dir(168),fontsize(9pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

(This agrees with the formula $\angle AIB=90^\circ+\dfrac{\angle C}{2}=90^\circ+25.5^\circ$.) The key's $123^\circ$ is a different angle: $\angle BIC=90^\circ+\dfrac{\angle A}{2}=180^\circ-31.5^\circ-25.5^\circ=123^\circ$.
