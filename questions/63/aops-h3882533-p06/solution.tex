Let $s=x+y$ and $t=xy$, so $s=1-t$. Combining fractions gives
\[
\dfrac{s(1-t)}{(1+t)^2-s^2}=\dfrac{(1-t)^2}{4t}=\dfrac{25}{24}.
\]
Hence $6t^2-37t+6=0$, whose roots are $6$ and $\dfrac{1}{6}$. Since $s=1-t>0$, only $t=\dfrac{1}{6}$ is possible, with $s=\dfrac{5}{6}$. Thus
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{1}{x}+\dfrac{1}{y}=\dfrac{s}{t}=5}.
\]
Indeed $\{x,y\}=\{\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{1}{3}\}$ works.
