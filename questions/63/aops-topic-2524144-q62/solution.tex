The triangle lies in the first quadrant, so $b>0$. The line meets the axes at $(0,b)$ and $(2b,0)$, so the triangle has legs $b$ and $2b$ and area $\dfrac12\cdot b\cdot2b=b^2=4$. Hence $b=\boxed{2}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((-0.5,0)--(5,0),Arrow(4));draw((0,-0.5)--(0,3),Arrow(4));
fill((0,0)--(4,0)--(0,2)--cycle,lightgray);
draw((0,2)--(4.5,-0.25),linewidth(0.8));
dot((0,2));dot((4,0));
label("$(0,b)$",(0,2),W);label("$(2b,0)$",(4,0),SW);
label("$y=-\dfrac12x+b$",(2.6,1.0),NE,fontsize(8pt));
label("$x$",(5,0),S);label("$y$",(0,3),W);
\end{asy}
