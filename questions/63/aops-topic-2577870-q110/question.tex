A shop sells packs containing 1, $X$, or $Y$ pairs of socks at nonnegative integer prices $a,b,c$. Given integers $0\le N\le100000$ and $X,Y>1$, find the least cost of buying exactly $N$ pairs. Give a correct algorithm and evaluate it for $(N,X,Y,a,b,c)=(42,5,10,3,5,45)$.
