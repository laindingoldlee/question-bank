A game has six suspect cards, six weapon cards and nine room cards. One uniformly chosen card of each category is hidden. The remaining eighteen cards are dealt uniformly into three labelled hands of six. If a player guesses one suspect, one weapon and one room uniformly from all possibilities, ignoring their hand, what is the chance of identifying all three hidden cards?
