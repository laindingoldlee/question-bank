Let the starting price be $P$. After the first year it is $1.5P$, and after the second year $0.5\cdot1.5P=0.75P$. So the final price is $\boxed{75\%}$ of the original price. (The changes do not cancel, because the $50\%$ decrease is taken of a larger amount.)
