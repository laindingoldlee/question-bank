Choosing the term $x^{a_i}$ from the $i$th factor ($1\le a_i\le6$), the coefficient of $x^8$ counts the solutions of $a_1+\dots+a_5=8$ with $1\le a_i\le6$. With $b_i=a_i-1\ge0$ we need $b_1+\dots+b_5=3$, and the bound $b_i\le5$ is automatic. By stars and bars the count is
\[\binom{3+4}{4}=\binom74=\boxed{35}.\]
