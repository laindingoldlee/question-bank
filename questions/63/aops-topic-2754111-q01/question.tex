A car drives four laps of a circular track of circumference $10$ km, at a constant speed during each lap. From the second lap on, each lap's speed (in km per hour) is the square of the previous lap's speed; for example, $2,4,16,256$. The average speed over the whole race is $\dfrac{13122}{1499}$ km per hour. What is the speed on the first lap, and what is the speed on the last lap?
