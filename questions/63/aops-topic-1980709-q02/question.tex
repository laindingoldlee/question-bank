Ana, Bruno, Celia and Dirce are in a park playing with a ball. Each child who receives the ball must pass it to one of the other three children. Ana makes the first pass. In how many different ways can the ball come back to Ana after exactly $4$ passes? (For example: Ana to Bruno, Bruno to Ana, Ana to Celia, Celia to Ana.)
