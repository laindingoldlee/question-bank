A salesman's sales depend on the hour of the day: at hour $h$ (on a $24$-hour clock, so $10$ am is $h=10$ and $6$ pm is $h=18$) he sells
\[S(h)=\left|\dfrac{15}{h}-2h\right|\]
widgets. Find, to the nearest tenth, the average of $S(8),S(9),\dots,S(18)$, his sales at the eleven whole hours from $8$ am to $6$ pm.
