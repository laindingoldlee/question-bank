Let $\theta=\angle CAD$. Triangle $ACD$ is isosceles, so $\angle ADC=\dfrac{180^\circ-\theta}{2}$. The trapezoid has equal base angles, so $\angle BAD=\angle ADC$. As $\angle BAD=45^\circ+\theta$, we obtain $\theta=30^\circ$.

The height is $h=AC\sin30^\circ=5$. Drop perpendiculars from $B,C$ to $AD$, with equal overhangs $x$. The horizontal projection of $AC$ is $AD-x=10\cos30^\circ=5\sqrt3$. Since $BC=AD-2x$, this is also the average of the bases. The area is $5\sqrt3\cdot5=\boxed{25\sqrt3}$.

\end{asy}
