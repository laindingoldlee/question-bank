The equal legs force the fourth side to repeat one of 1, 10, 20. If the legs are 1, the bases 10 and 20 differ by more than twice a leg, impossible. Legs 10 with bases 1 and 20 work because $10>\dfrac{19}{2}$; legs 20 with bases 1 and 10 also work because $20>\dfrac{9}{2}$. Thus the possible fourth sides sum to $\boxed{30}$.
