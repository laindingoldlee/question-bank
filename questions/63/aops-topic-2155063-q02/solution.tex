Let Eric remove $r$ red and $6r$ white balls, with $r\ge1$ (he removed some balls). Then $58-7r$ balls remain, and if $w$ of them are white, $5w$ are red, so $58-7r=6w$. Hence $7r\equiv58\pmod6$, that is, $r\equiv4\pmod6$. Also $7r\le58$, so $r\le8$. Therefore $r=4$: he removed $7\cdot4=\boxed{28}$ balls, of which $\boxed{4}$ were red (and $24$ white).

Check: $30$ balls remain, $w=5$ white and $25$ red, so the bag originally held $29$ red and $29$ white balls.
