Prime factorization gives $65520=2^4\cdot3^2\cdot5\cdot7\cdot13$. A divisor chooses each prime exponent independently from $0$ through its exponent in this factorization. Thus the count is $(4+1)(2+1)(1+1)^3=\boxed{120}$.
