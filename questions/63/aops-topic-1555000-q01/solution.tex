Let $x$ quarts be drained. Draining removes $0.1x$ quarts of antifreeze, and replacing by water keeps $200$ quarts in the radiator with $20-0.1x$ quarts of antifreeze. Adding $100$ quarts of $5\%$ solution adds $5$ quarts of antifreeze, and the result has $0.06\cdot300=18$ quarts:
\[
20-0.1x+5=18\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x=\boxed{70}\text{ quarts}.
\]
