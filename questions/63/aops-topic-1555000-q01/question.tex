A car radiator contains $200$ quarts of a $10\%$ antifreeze solution. A certain amount of the solution is drained off and replaced by pure water, and then $100$ quarts of a $5\%$ antifreeze solution are added, making $300$ quarts of a $6\%$ antifreeze solution. How many quarts were drained off?
