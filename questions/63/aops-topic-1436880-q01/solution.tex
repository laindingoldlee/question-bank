Let $S=\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n}k\,2^k$. Then $2S=\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n}k\,2^{k+1}=\sum_{k=2}^{n+1}(k-1)2^k$. Subtracting,
\[
2S-S=n\,2^{n+1}-\sum_{k=1}^{n}2^k=n\,2^{n+1}-(2^{n+1}-2).
\]
So
\[
\boxed{S=(n-1)2^{n+1}+2} .
\]
Check: $n=1$ gives $2$, $n=2$ gives $2+8=10=1\cdot8+2$, $n=3$ gives $10+24=34=2\cdot16+2$.
