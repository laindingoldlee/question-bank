Induction shows that the first row and first column of every $H_n$ contain only $1$'s. If $S_n$ is the sum of all entries, the block recurrence gives $S_{n+1}=2S_n$, so $S_n=2^n$. Let $N=2^{10}=1024$. Deleting the first row and column removes $2N-1$ ones, counting their shared corner only once. The remaining sum is $N-(2N-1)=-(N-1)$ and the remaining number of entries is $(N-1)^2$. Hence
\[
M=-\dfrac{1}{N-1},\qquad \boxed{k=1023}.
\]
