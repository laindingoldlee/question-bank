By Vieta's formulas, $x,y,z$ are the roots of
\[t^3+17t^2+46t-168=0.\]
$t=2$ is a root ($8+68+92-168=0$), and $t^3+17t^2+46t-168=(t-2)(t^2+19t+84)=(t-2)(t+7)(t+12)$. So $x=2$, $y=-7$, $z=-12$, and
\[x^9-y^3+z^2=512+343+144=\boxed{999}.\]
