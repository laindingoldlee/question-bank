A regular $100$-gon is drawn. At each vertex a nonnegative real number is written in blue, such that any three consecutive blue numbers have sum at most $1$. Then at each vertex the product of the blue numbers at its two neighbouring vertices is written in red. What is the maximum possible sum of the $100$ red numbers?
