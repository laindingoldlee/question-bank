The minute hand is $20\cdot6=120^\circ$ clockwise from twelve. The hour hand is $8\cdot30+20\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=250^\circ$. Their smaller separation is $250-120=\boxed{130^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw(circle((0,0),2));draw((0,0)--1.8dir(-30),blue);draw((0,0)--1.2dir(-160),red);label("12",(0,2),N);label("minute",1.8dir(-30),E);label("hour",1.2dir(-160),W);label("$130^\circ$",.7dir(-95));
\end{asy}
