They made $85-22=63$ field goals worth $184-22=162$ points. If $t$ of them were two-pointers and $h$ three-pointers, then
\[t+h=63,\qquad2t+3h=162.\]
Subtracting twice the first equation from the second gives $h=162-126=\boxed{36}$. (Then $t=27$, and $22+2\cdot27+3\cdot36=184$.)
