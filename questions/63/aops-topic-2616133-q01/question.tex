Grace places a pencil in a cylindrical cup and finds that it fits exactly diagonally: one end touches the edge of the bottom and the other end touches the opposite edge of the top. The pencil is $17$ units long and of negligible thickness, and the cup is $8$ units tall. The volume of the cup is $k\pi$ cubic units. Find $k$.
