The pencil is the diagonal of a rectangle through the axis of the cylinder, whose sides are the height $8$ and the diameter $d$. By the Pythagorean theorem $d=\sqrt{17^2-8^2}=\sqrt{225}=15$, so the radius is $\dfrac{15}{2}$ and the volume is
\[\pi\left(\dfrac{15}{2}\right)^2\cdot8=450\pi.\]
So $k=\boxed{450}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real w=15,h=8,e=1.6;
path top=ellipse((w/2,h),w/2,e), bot=ellipse((w/2,0),w/2,e);
draw(top,linewidth(0.7));
draw(subpath(bot,2,4),linewidth(0.7)); draw(subpath(bot,0,2),dashed);
draw((0,0)--(0,h),linewidth(0.7)); draw((w,0)--(w,h),linewidth(0.7));
draw((0,0)--(w,h),linewidth(1.6)+gray(0.35));
draw((0,0)--(w,0),dashed);
label("$17$",(w/2,h/2),NW); label("$8$",(w,h/2),E); label("$15$",(w/2,-e),S);
\end{asy}
