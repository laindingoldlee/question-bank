In a class of $40$ students, $27$ like Math and $25$ like English. The question ``at most how many students do not like at least one of these subjects?'' can be read in two ways. Answer both:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] At most how many students like neither subject?
\item[(b)] At most how many students fail to like at least one of the two subjects (that is, do not like both)?
\end{enumerate}
