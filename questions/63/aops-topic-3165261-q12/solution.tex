Place $B=(-8,0)$, $C=(8,0)$, $A=(0,6)$, so $M=(0,0)$ and $N=(4,3)$. Write $P=(0,t)$ with $0\le t<6$. The distance condition gives $4(16+(t-3)^2)=64+t^2$, or $3(t-2)(t-6)=0$. The permitted value is $t=2$. Hence $[PBC]=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot16\cdot2=\boxed{16}$.
