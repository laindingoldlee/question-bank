$321_5=75+10+1=86$ and $321_4=48+8+1=57$, so the sum is $143$. Dividing by $3$ repeatedly: $143=3\cdot47+2$, $47=3\cdot15+2$, $15=3\cdot5+0$, $5=3\cdot1+2$, $1=3\cdot0+1$. Reading the remainders upward, $143=\boxed{12022_3}$.
