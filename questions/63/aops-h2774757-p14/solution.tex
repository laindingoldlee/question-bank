In polar coordinates the two radii are independent with density $2r$ on $[0,1]$, so each has expectation $\int_0^1 2r^2\,dr=\dfrac{2}{3}$. The relative angle is uniform on $[0,2\pi]$ and independent of the radii, with mean absolute sine $\dfrac{2}{\pi}$. Thus the expected area is $\dfrac{1}{2}(\dfrac{2}{3})^2\dfrac{2}{\pi}=\boxed{\dfrac{4}{9\pi}}$.
