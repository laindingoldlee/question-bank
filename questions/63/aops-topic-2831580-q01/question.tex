A regular $n$-gon $P_1P_2\cdots P_n$ ($n\ge3$) has circumradius $1$. Prove that for every point $P$ on its circumcircle,
\[PP_1^3+PP_2^3+\cdots+PP_n^3\le2\sqrt3\,n.\]
