The original denominators require $r\ne0$, $r\ne s$, and $s\ne1$. Factoring first gives $\dfrac{8(r-s)}{2r(r-s)}+\dfrac{3r^2}{r(s-1)}=\dfrac{4}{r}+\dfrac{3r}{s-1}$. A common denominator yields $\boxed{\dfrac{3r^2+4s-4}{r(s-1)}}$, with all three original restrictions retained even though the factor $r-s$ canceled.
