Each queen changes its column by $-4,0$, or $4$. Thus the four residue classes modulo 4 are independent chains of lengths $7,6,6,6$. A permutation of a chain moving each entry by at most one step consists only of fixed points and disjoint swaps of adjacent entries: consider the leftmost moved entry to force such a swap. For a chain of length $n$, the count $T_n$ satisfies $T_n=T_{n-1}+T_{n-2}$ with $T_0=T_1=1$, hence $T_6=13,T_7=21$. The answer is $21\cdot13^3=\boxed{46137}$.
