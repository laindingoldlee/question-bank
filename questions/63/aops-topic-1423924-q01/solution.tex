Upstream the ship's speed is $\dfrac{113.4}{9}=12.6$ mph, so its speed in still water is $12.6+1.9=14.5$ mph, and downstream it moves at $14.5+1.9=16.4$ mph. The return trip takes
\[
\dfrac{113.4}{16.4}=\dfrac{1134}{164}=\boxed{\dfrac{567}{82}\text{ hours}}\approx6.91\text{ hours},
\]
about $6$ hours $55$ minutes.
