A yield sign is a triangle $ABC$ with a red border of constant width $6$ inches: the inner white triangle $A'B'C'$ has each side parallel to the corresponding side of $ABC$ and at distance $6$ inches from it, on the inside. Prove that the inner triangle $A'B'C'$ is similar to the whole sign $ABC$. (The usual yield sign is equilateral, but the argument works for any triangle whose inradius is greater than $6$ inches.)
