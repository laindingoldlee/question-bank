Points $M$ and $N$ are the midpoints of sides $PA$ and $PB$ of $\triangle PAB$. As $P$ moves along a line that is parallel to side $AB$, how many of the four quantities listed below change?
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] the length of the segment $MN$
\item[(b)] the perimeter of $\triangle PAB$
\item[(c)] the area of $\triangle PAB$
\item[(d)] the area of trapezoid $ABNM$
\end{enumerate}
(A) $0$\qquad (B) $1$\qquad (C) $2$\qquad (D) $3$\qquad (E) $4$

Explain in particular why the perimeter changes.
