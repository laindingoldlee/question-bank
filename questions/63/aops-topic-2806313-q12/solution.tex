In alphabetical order the letters are $A,B,D,N,R,U$. Words before $DURBAN$: starting with $A$ or $B$: $2\cdot5!=240$. Starting with $D$, second letter before $U$ ($A,B,N,R$): $4\cdot4!=96$. Starting $DU$, third letter before $R$ ($A,B,N$): $3\cdot3!=18$. Starting $DUR$, fourth letter before $B$ ($A$): $1\cdot2!=2$. Starting $DURB$: $DURBAN$ is the first. So its position is $240+96+18+2+1=\boxed{357}$.
