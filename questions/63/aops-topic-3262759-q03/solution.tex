Only B sums $3,4,5,6$ can win. Their counts are $20,15,6,1$ out of $64$, and the numbers of A outcomes strictly below them are $1,3,6,10$. Thus
\[\boxed{\dfrac{20(1)+15(3)+6(6)+1(10)}{64\cdot36}=\dfrac{37}{768}}.\]
