Number the rows of Pascal's triangle from $0$, so that row $n$ consists of $\dbinom n0,\dbinom n1,\dots,\dbinom nn$. Let $F_1=F_2=1$ and $F_{m+1}=F_m+F_{m-1}$ be the Fibonacci numbers. Prove that the sums along the ``shallow diagonals'' of Pascal's triangle are Fibonacci numbers:
\[\sum_{k=0}^{\left\lfloor\dfrac n2\right\rfloor}\dbinom{n-k}{k}=F_{n+1}\qquad\text{for every integer }n\ge0.\]
(For example, for $n=4$: $\dbinom40+\dbinom31+\dbinom22=1+3+1=5=F_5$.)
