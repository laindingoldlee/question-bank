The active lines have slopes $-11,1,9$ on $x<-3$, $-3<x<-\dfrac{1}{2}$, and $x>-\dfrac{1}{2}$. Write the quadratic as $p(x)=ax^2+bx+c$. At a tangency to a line $mx+\beta$,
\[
t=\dfrac{m-b}{2a},\qquad \beta=c-\dfrac{(m-b)^2}{4a}.
\]
Subtracting the intercept equations for slopes $-11,1$ gives $b=6a-5$; using slopes $1,9$ gives $b=a+5$. Hence $a=2,b=7,c=\dfrac{7}{2}$. The three contact coordinates are $-\dfrac{9}{2},-\dfrac{3}{2},\dfrac{1}{2}$, which lie in their required intervals. Their sum is $\boxed{-\dfrac{11}{2}}$.
