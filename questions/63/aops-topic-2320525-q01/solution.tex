There are two ways to finish the piece.
\begin{itemize}
\item Stop, then go back and finish: probability $\dfrac27\cdot\dfrac{1}{11}=\dfrac{2}{77}$.
\item Force myself to keep playing, and not give up: probability $\dfrac37\cdot\dfrac{1}{10}=\dfrac{3}{70}$.
\end{itemize}
These cases are disjoint, so the probability is
\[\dfrac{2}{77}+\dfrac{3}{70}=\dfrac{20}{770}+\dfrac{33}{770}=\boxed{\dfrac{53}{770}}.\]
