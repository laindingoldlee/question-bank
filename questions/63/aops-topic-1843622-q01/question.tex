Trains leave New York for Atlanta every $20$ minutes, starting at 1:00 a.m., and trains leave Atlanta for New York every $30$ minutes, starting at 1:10 a.m., throughout the day. Every trip takes exactly $10$ hours. The train that leaves New York at 1:00 p.m.\ arrives in Atlanta at 11:00 p.m. How many trains travelling from Atlanta to New York does it pass on the way?
