Each weighing has three outcomes, so four weighings distinguish at most $3^4=81$ coins, insufficient. Five suffice: weigh $43$ coins against $43$, leaving $42$ aside; the outcome identifies a suspect group of at most $43$. Repeat, splitting suspects into three groups as evenly as possible with equal-sized groups on the pans. After five total weighings, at most $\lceil\dfrac{128}{3^5}\rceil=1$ suspect remains. The minimum is $\boxed5$.
