There are $\dfrac{n(n-3)}{2}$ diagonals in total. Each red vertex is incident to $n-3$ diagonals, and the red-to-red diagonal has been counted twice. Therefore $137=\dfrac{n(n-3)}{2}-2(n-3)+1=\dfrac{(n-4)(n-3)}{2}+1$. Thus $(n-4)(n-3)=272=16\cdot17$, giving $\boxed{n=20}$.
