For positive die results $a+b+c=7$, put $u=a-1$, $v=b-1$, $w=c-1$. Then $u+v+w=4$, giving $\binom62=15$ nonnegative solutions. No die bound is violated since each original result is at most 5. Of the $6^3=216$ equally likely outcomes, 15 qualify, giving $\dfrac{15}{216}=\dfrac{5}{72}$. Thus $\boxed{a+b=77}$.
