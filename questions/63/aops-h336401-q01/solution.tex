If the outer side is $s$, the four removed corner triangles each have legs $\dfrac{s}{2},\dfrac{s}{2}$ and area $\dfrac{s^2}{8}$. Their total area is $\dfrac{s^2}{2}$, leaving half the original area. Thus the smaller square has area $\boxed{30}$, choice \textbf{D}.
