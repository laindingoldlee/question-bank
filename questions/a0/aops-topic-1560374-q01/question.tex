Evaluate
\[
1+\dfrac{8}{2+\dfrac{8}{2+\dfrac{8}{2+\cdots}}}
\]
where the value of the infinite continued fraction is defined as the limit of the sequence $x_1=2$, $x_{k+1}=2+\dfrac{8}{x_k}$, and the expression is $1+\dfrac{8}{x}$ with $x=\lim x_k$.
