The equation says that $x$ is equidistant from $7$ and $-1$ on the number line, so $x$ is their midpoint, $3$. (Algebraically, squaring gives $x^2-14x+49=x^2+2x+1$, so $16x=48$.) There is $\boxed{1}$ solution.
