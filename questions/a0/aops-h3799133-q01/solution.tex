The ratio gives $\dfrac{AE}{AC}=\dfrac{3}{5}$. Triangles $ABE,ABC$ have the same altitude from $B$ to line $AC$, so $[ABE]=\dfrac{3}{5}[ABC]$. Likewise $[AED]=\dfrac{3}{5}[ACD]$. Adding,
\[
[ABED]=[ABE]+[AED]
=\dfrac{3}{5}([ABC]+[ACD])
=\dfrac{3}{5}(80)=\boxed{48}.
\]
In fact the argument works for every convex quadrilateral.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

\end{asy}
