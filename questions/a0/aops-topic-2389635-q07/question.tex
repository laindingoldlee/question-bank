Mrs. Puff bakes fish pies for a fundraiser. A koi pie takes $4$ cups of flour, a salmon pie $5$ cups and a trout pie $6$ cups, and they sell for $\$6$, $\$7$ and $\$9$ respectively. If she has $25$ cups of flour and sells every pie she bakes, what is the maximum amount of money she can raise?
