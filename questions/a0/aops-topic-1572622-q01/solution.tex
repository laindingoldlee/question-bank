The yellow sector is $360^\circ-72^\circ-60^\circ-108^\circ=120^\circ$. The probabilities of the colors are the fractions of the full turn:
\[
P(R)=\dfrac{72}{360}=\dfrac{1}{5},\quad P(B)=\dfrac{60}{360}=\dfrac{1}{6},\quad P(G)=\dfrac{108}{360}=\dfrac{3}{10},\quad P(Y)=\dfrac{120}{360}=\dfrac{1}{3} .
\]
The two spins are independent, so
\[
P(\text{same})=\dfrac{1}{25}+\dfrac{1}{36}+\dfrac{9}{100}+\dfrac{1}{9}=\dfrac{36+25+81+100}{900}=\dfrac{242}{900}=\boxed{\dfrac{121}{450}}.
\]
