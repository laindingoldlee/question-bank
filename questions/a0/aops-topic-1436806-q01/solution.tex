We need $N\equiv3\pmod5$ and $N\equiv6\pmod9$, with $N\ge6$. List Bob's numbers and check them modulo $5$:
\[
6\equiv1,\quad 15\equiv0,\quad 24\equiv4,\quad 33\equiv3\pmod5 .
\]
So the first common number is $\boxed{33}$ ($33=3+6\cdot5=6+3\cdot9$). Since $5$ and $9$ are coprime, the common numbers repeat every $\operatorname{lcm}(5,9)=45$: they are $33, 78, 123, \dots$, that is, $33+45t$ for $t\ge0$.

(In general: list the terms of the sequence with the larger step and stop at the first one with the right remainder for the other step; this is the Chinese remainder theorem in action.)
