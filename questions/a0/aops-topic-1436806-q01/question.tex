Annie starts with $3$ jelly beans and adds jelly beans $5$ at a time, so she has $3, 8, 13, \dots$ jelly beans. Bob starts with $6$ jelly beans and adds them $9$ at a time, so he has $6, 15, 24, \dots$ jelly beans. What is the first number of jelly beans that both of them reach? Which numbers do both of them reach?
