Write digits $a,b,c$ with $a\ge 1$. Put $u=a-1\ge 0$. Then $u+b+c=3$, and all digits are automatically at most $9$. Stars and bars gives $\binom{3+3-1}{3-1}=\binom 52=\boxed{10}$.
