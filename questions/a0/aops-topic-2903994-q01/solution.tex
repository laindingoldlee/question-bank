$45$ litres of $X$ remain, and $90$ litres of milk and $90$ of water are added, giving $225$ litres with milk $125$ and water $100$. So the remaining $X$ has $35$ milk and $10$ water, i.e. water is $\dfrac29$ of $X$. Initially the water was $\dfrac29\cdot180=\boxed{40}$ litres.
