If the vertex angle is $\theta$, then $72\sin\theta=36$. The acute condition gives $\theta=30^\circ$. The cosine rule gives base squared $288-288\cos30^\circ=144(2-\sqrt3)$. The base is $12\sqrt{2-\sqrt3}\approx\boxed{6.21}$.
