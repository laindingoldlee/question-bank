What is the maximum value of $xy$ if
\[
x+\dfrac{1}{y}=\dfrac{7}{2}\qquad\text{and}\qquad y+\dfrac{1}{x}=\dfrac{7}{5} ?
\]
Express your answer as a decimal to the nearest tenth.
