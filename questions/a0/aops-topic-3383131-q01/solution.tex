Choosing $x=0,1$ gives $(0,1),(1,5)$; choosing $x=-1,2$ gives $(-1,-3),(2,9)$. Each pair determines the same line. Any two distinct real $x$ values work when their $y$ values are calculated from the equation; merely choosing a line of the same slope without the correct intercept is insufficient.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw((-1.5,0)--(2.5,0),Arrow);draw((0,-4)--(0,10),Arrow);draw((-1.25,-4)--(2.25,10),blue);dot("$(0,1)$",(0,1),W);dot("$(1,5)$",(1,5),E);label("$x$",(2.5,0),S);label("$y$",(0,10),W);
\end{asy}
