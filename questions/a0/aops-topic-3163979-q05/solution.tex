If $a,b$ families have one and two children, then $\dfrac{2b}{a+2b}=\dfrac{2}{5}$. Hence $10b=2a+4b$, so $a=3b$. The required fraction is $\dfrac{b}{a+b}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{4}}$.
