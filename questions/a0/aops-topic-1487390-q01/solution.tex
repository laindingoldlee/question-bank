In one turn the wheel rolls a distance equal to its circumference $2\pi r$. One mile is $5280$ feet, so each turn covers
\[
2\pi r=\dfrac{5280}{240}=22\ \text{feet}.
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real r=1; real L=2*pi*r;
draw((-1.4,0)--(L+1.4,0),gray);
draw(circle((0,r),r),linewidth(0.9));
draw(circle((L,r),r),gray+dashed);
dot((0,r)); draw((0,r)--(0,r)+r*dir(40),blue);
label("$r$",(0,r)+0.5*r*dir(40),NW,blue);
dot((0,0)); dot((L,0));
draw((0,-0.35)--(L,-0.35),red,Arrows(6));
label("one turn: $22$ ft",(L/2,-0.35),S,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Hence
\[
r=\dfrac{22}{2\pi}=\boxed{\dfrac{11}{\pi}\ \text{feet}}\approx3.5\ \text{feet}
\]
(about $42$ inches; with $\pi\approx\dfrac{22}{7}$ the radius is exactly $3.5$ feet).
