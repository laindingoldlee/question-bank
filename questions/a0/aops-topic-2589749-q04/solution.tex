Let the distance be $d$ miles. The two trips differ by $6+15=21$ minutes, i.e. $\dfrac{7}{20}$ hour:
\[\dfrac d6-\dfrac d9=\dfrac{7}{20}\iff\dfrac{d}{18}=\dfrac{7}{20}\iff d=\dfrac{63}{10}.\]
Walking takes $\dfrac{63}{60}=\dfrac{21}{20}$ hours, so the on-time trip takes $\dfrac{21}{20}-\dfrac{1}{10}=\dfrac{19}{20}$ hours. The required speed is
\[\dfrac{\dfrac{63}{10}}{\dfrac{19}{20}}=\boxed{\dfrac{126}{19}}\text{ miles per hour}.\]
