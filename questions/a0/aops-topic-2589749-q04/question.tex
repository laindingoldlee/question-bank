If I walk from my house to work at $6$ miles per hour, I arrive $6$ minutes late, but if I run at $9$ miles per hour, I arrive $15$ minutes early. At what constant speed, in miles per hour, should I travel to arrive exactly on time? Express your answer as a common fraction.
