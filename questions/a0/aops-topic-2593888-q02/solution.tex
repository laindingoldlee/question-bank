Put $b_i=-a_i$; the $b_i$ are positive integers with $b_1+\cdots+b_{24}=95$. Writing $95$ as a row of $95$ ones, a solution corresponds to choosing $23$ of the $94$ gaps between them as dividers (stars and bars). So the number of solutions is
\[\boxed{\dbinom{94}{23}}=4\,945\,588\,740\,588\,647\,843\,640.\]
