Split the fraction on the left:
\[\dfrac{2^{2n}+\dbinom{2n}{n}}{2\cdot(2n)!}=\dfrac{2^{2n}}{2\cdot(2n)!}+\dfrac{1}{2\cdot(2n)!}\cdot\dfrac{(2n)!}{n!\,n!}=\dfrac{2^{2n-1}}{(2n)!}+\dfrac{1}{2\left(n!\right)^2}.\ \blacksquare\]
So the two forms are equal; the second one shows the two contributions separately and avoids the binomial coefficient.
