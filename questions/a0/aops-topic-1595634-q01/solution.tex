There are $4!=24$ equally likely numbers. A number is divisible by $4$ exactly when the number formed by its last two digits is. The two-digit endings with distinct digits from $\{1,2,3,4\}$ that are multiples of $4$ are
\[
12,\quad 24,\quad 32,
\]
and each can be completed by the remaining two digits in $2$ ways. So $3\cdot2=6$ of the $24$ numbers are divisible by $4$, and
\[
P(\text{not divisible by }4)=1-\dfrac{6}{24}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{4}}.
\]
