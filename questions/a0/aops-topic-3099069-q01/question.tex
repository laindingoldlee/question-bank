Three circles have noncollinear complex centres $z_1,z_2,z_3$ and pairwise distinct positive radii $r_1,r_2,r_3$. For each pair let $h_{ij}=\dfrac{r_i z_j-r_j z_i}{r_i-r_j}$ be its external homothety centre. Prove that $h_{12},h_{23},h_{31}$ are collinear.
