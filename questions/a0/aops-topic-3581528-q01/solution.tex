Let $S=a+b+c$. Each ratio $u=\dfrac{a}{S},\dfrac{b}{S},\dfrac{c}{S}$ lies strictly between $0$ and $1$, so $u^r<u$ for $r>1$. Summing yields $\dfrac{a^r+b^r+c^r}{S^r}<\dfrac{a+b+c}{S}=1$. Thus $a^r+b^r+c^r<(a+b+c)^r$. Taking $a=2,b=3,c=5,r=10000$ proves the claim.
