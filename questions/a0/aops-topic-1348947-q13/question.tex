A box contains $3$ black books and $1$ red book. At the end of every minute, beginning with the end of the first minute, a book is chosen at random from the box and returned to the box together with another book of the same colour. The probability that after exactly one hour $43$ of the books in the box are black can be written as $\dfrac {a}{b}$ with $a$ and $b$ coprime positive integers. Find $a$.
