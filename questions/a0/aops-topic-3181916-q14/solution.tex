A monomial survives exactly when the sum of the exponents of $c,d$ is even; otherwise its two coefficients cancel. Write that sum as $2k$, with $0\le k\le50$. There are $2k+1$ choices for the two exponents and $101-2k$ choices for the exponents of $a,b$. Thus the count is $\sum_{k=0}^{50}(2k+1)(101-2k)=\sum_{k=0}^{50}(101+200k-4k^2)=5151+255000-171700=\boxed{88451}$.
