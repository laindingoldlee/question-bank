Place the larger rectangles at $[0,12]\times[0,4]$ and $[12,24]\times[0,4]$. Place the smaller ones at $[18,24]\times[4,6]$ and $[24,30]\times[4,6]$. Their interiors are disjoint, they form a connected region without a hole, and the boundary vertices are
\[(0,0),(24,0),(24,4),(30,4),(30,6),(18,6),(18,4),(0,4).\]
There are eight corners, so it is an octagon. Its perimeter is $24+4+6+2+12+2+18+4=\boxed{72}$.
