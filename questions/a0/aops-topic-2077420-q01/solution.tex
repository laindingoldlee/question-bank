Take side length \(3\): \(B=(0,0)\), \(C=(3,0)\), \(A=\left(\dfrac{3}{2},\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}\right)\), so \(E=(1,0)\) and \(D=(2,0)\). Let \(H=\left(\dfrac{3}{2},0\right)\) be the foot of the altitude from \(A\).

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0), C=(3,0), A=(1.5,1.5*sqrt(3));
pair Ept=(1,0), D=(2,0), H=(1.5,0);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(A--D,blue); draw(A--H,dashed);
draw(H--(H+(0.12,0))--(H+(0.12,0.12))--(H+(0,0.12)));
dot(A);dot(B);dot(C);dot(D);dot(Ept);dot(H);
label("$A$",A,N); label("$B$",B,SW); label("$C$",C,SE); label("$D$",D,S); label("$E$",Ept,S); label("$H$",H,S);
label("$\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}$",(1.5,1.1),W); label("$\sqrt7$",(A+D)/2,NE,blue);
label("$\dfrac{1}{2}$",(H+D)/2,N,fontsize(8pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Then \(HD=\dfrac{1}{2}\), \(AH=\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}\), and
\[
AD=\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{27}{4}}=\sqrt7 .
\]
The angle \(ADE\) equals \(\angle ADH\) (\(E\) and \(H\) are on the same side of \(D\)), so in right triangle \(AHD\),
\[
\sin\angle ADE=\dfrac{AH}{AD}=\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2\sqrt7}=\dfrac{3\sqrt{21}}{14}.
\]
Thus \(a=3\), \(b=21\), \(c=14\), and \(a+b+c=\boxed{38}\).
