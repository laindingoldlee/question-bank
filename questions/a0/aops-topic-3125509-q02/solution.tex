The product of the given sums is $\dfrac{ab}{(1-r)(1-s)}$. Let $t=r+s-rs=1-(1-r)(1-s)$. Since $0<(1-r)(1-s)<1$, $0<t<1$. Therefore $ab+abt+abt^2+\cdots=\dfrac{ab}{1-t}=\dfrac{ab}{(1-r)(1-s)}$, proving equality of the sums; the claim does not assert equality term by term.
