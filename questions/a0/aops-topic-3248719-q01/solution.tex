A one-digit positive even number is not divisible by $9$. A two-digit all-even number has even digit sum at most $16$, which cannot be a positive multiple of $9$. For a three-digit number, the least possible leading digit is $2$. Its total digit sum must be $18$, forcing the other two digits to be $8,8$. Thus the least is $\boxed{288}$.
