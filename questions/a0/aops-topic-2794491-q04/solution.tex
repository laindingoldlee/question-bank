The middle two counts are each 6 or 7. The total number of performances by individuals is between 25 and 27 and is divisible by 3. Thus it is 27, the two middle counts are both 7, and there are $\boxed9$ songs. Feasibility follows by having Mary absent from 1 song, each middle girl from 2, and Susan from 4.
