Let $x=\dfrac{\sqrt5-1}{2}$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $x=\dfrac{1}{1+x}$.
\item[(b)] Define $c_1=1$ and $c_{n+1}=\dfrac{1}{1+c_n}$, so that $c_2=\dfrac{1}{1+1}$, $c_3=\dfrac{1}{1+\dfrac{1}{1+1}}$, and so on. Prove that $c_n\to x$; that is, $x$ is the value of the infinite continued fraction
\[
\dfrac{1}{1+\dfrac{1}{1+\dfrac{1}{1+\cdots}}} .
\]
\end{enumerate}
