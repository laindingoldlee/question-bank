Triangle $ABC$ has $AB=BC$ and lies on circle $O$. Another circle $P$ passes through $A,B$ and meets segment $AC$ again at $D$. The line through $D$ parallel to $AB$ is perpendicular to the tangent to $O$ at $C$; they meet at $E$. If $EC=\dfrac{16}{3}$ and the longest side of $ABC$ is $16$, find the ratio of the areas of circles $O$ and $P$.
