There are $\left\lfloor\dfrac{169}{8}\right\rfloor=21$ multiples of $8$ and $\left\lfloor\dfrac{169}{16}\right\rfloor=10$ multiples of $16$, and every multiple of $16$ is a multiple of $8$. So the answer is $21-10=\boxed{11}$.
