Since $i^2=-1$, we have $i^4=\left(i^2\right)^2=1$, so powers of $i$ repeat with period $4$.

\textbf{(a)} $i^4=\boxed{1}$. \quad \textbf{(b)} $i^5=i^4\cdot i=\boxed{i}$. \quad \textbf{(c)} $2011=4\cdot502+3$, so $i^{2011}=i^3=\boxed{-i}$.
