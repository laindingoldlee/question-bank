Call the numbers $a$ and $b$, so $a+b=7$ and $ab=25$. Then
\[
\dfrac{1}{a}+\dfrac{1}{b}=\dfrac{a+b}{ab}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{25}} .
\]
Remark: $a$ and $b$ are the roots of $t^2-7t+25=0$, whose discriminant $49-100$ is negative, so the two numbers are not real; they are the complex numbers $\dfrac{7\pm\sqrt{51}\,i}{2}$. The identity $\dfrac{1}{a}+\dfrac{1}{b}=\dfrac{a+b}{ab}$ holds for any nonzero complex numbers, so the answer is still $\dfrac{7}{25}$.
