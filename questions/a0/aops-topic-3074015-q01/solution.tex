Convert $20$ minutes to $\dfrac{1}{3}$ hour. Equal distances give $\dfrac{x}{3}=\dfrac{12}{x}$, so $x^2=36$ and $x=6$. The speeds are $6$ and $\dfrac{1}{6}$, differing by $\boxed{\dfrac{35}{6}}$ miles per hour.
