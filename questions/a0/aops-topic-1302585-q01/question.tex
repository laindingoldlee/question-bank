In the game Threes, a number chosen at random from $\{1,2,3\}$ is written in each square of a $4\times4$ grid (independently and uniformly). A board is \emph{dead} if no $1$ is next to a $2$ and no $3$ is next to another $3$ (``next to'' means sharing a side). What is the probability that a random board is dead?
