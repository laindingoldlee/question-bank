Circles $A$, $B$, $C$ and $D$ all have radius $1$ (each circle is named after its centre). The centres of circles $B$ and $C$ lie on circle $A$; the centres of circles $A$ and $D$ lie on circle $B$ and on circle $C$; the centres of circles $B$ and $C$ lie on circle $D$. In addition, the four centres $A$, $B$, $D$, $C$ form a square, in this order. What is the area of the region that lies inside all four circles?
