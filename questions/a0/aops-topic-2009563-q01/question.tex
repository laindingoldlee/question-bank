Instead of using two standard cubical dice in a board game, three standard cubical dice are used so that the game goes more quickly. In the regular game, doubles are needed to get out of the ``pit''. In the revised game, doubles or triples will get you out (that is, at least two of the three dice show the same number). How many times as likely is it for a player to get out of the pit on one toss under the new rules as compared to the old rules?
