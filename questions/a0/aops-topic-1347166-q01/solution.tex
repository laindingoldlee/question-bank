Number the days so that John's haircut is day $0$ (a Tuesday) and Billy's is day $1$. John's haircuts fall on days $\equiv0\pmod{33}$ and Billy's on days $\equiv1\pmod{64}$. Since $\gcd(33,64)=1$, there is exactly one such day modulo $33\cdot64=2112$.

Write the day as $33m$; we need $33m\equiv1\pmod{64}$. Since $33^2=1089=17\cdot64+1$, we have $33\cdot33\equiv1\pmod{64}$, so $m\equiv33\pmod{64}$ and the first common day is day $33\cdot33=1089$. (It is the first one after day $1$, since $m=33$ is the least positive solution.)

Finally $1089=7\cdot155+4$, so day $1089$ is $4$ days of the week after Tuesday:
\[
\boxed{\text{Saturday}}.
\]
