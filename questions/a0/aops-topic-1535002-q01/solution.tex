The odd numbers from $2$ to $450$ are $3,5,7,\dots,449$, an arithmetic sequence with common difference $2$. It has $\dfrac{449-3}{2}+1=224$ terms, so its sum is
\[
224\cdot\dfrac{3+449}{2}=224\cdot226=\boxed{50624}.
\]
(Equivalently, $1+3+\cdots+449=225^2=50625$, and removing the term $1$ leaves $50624$.)
