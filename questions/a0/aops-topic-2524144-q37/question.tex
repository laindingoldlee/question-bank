On a $25$-question test, each correct answer earns $6$ points, each unanswered question earns $1.5$ points, and each wrong answer earns $0$ points. In how many ways can the numbers of correct, unanswered and wrong answers be chosen so that the total score is exactly $120$? (Only the three numbers matter; for example, $20$ correct and $5$ wrong is one way.)
