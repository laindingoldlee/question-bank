Let $f(x)=x^2+1$ with domain $x>0$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the range of $f$ and a formula for $f^{-1}$, with its domain and range.
\item[(b)] Explain how a restriction on the domain of a function affects its inverse, and why $x^2+1$ needs a restriction to have an inverse at all.
\end{enumerate}
