From $80^x=4$ we get $80=4^{\dfrac1x}$, so $\dfrac1x=\log_480$. Similarly $\dfrac1y=\log_41280$. Hence
\[\dfrac1x-\dfrac1y=\log_4\dfrac{80}{1280}=\log_4\dfrac1{16}=\boxed{-2}.\]
