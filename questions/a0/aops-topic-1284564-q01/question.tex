An ant performs a random walk on the vertices of a $4$-dimensional unit hypercube (which has $16$ vertices, each joined by edges to $4$ others). At each step it moves along one of the $4$ edges at its current vertex, chosen uniformly at random (it may go back along the edge it just used). The walk starts at a vertex and ends when the ant first returns to it. What is the expected length of the walk?
