Let the series be $S$. Multiplying by $\dfrac{1}{5}$ and subtracting gives $S-\dfrac{S}{5}=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{5^n}=\dfrac{1}{4}$. Hence $S=\dfrac{5}{16}$, so $\boxed{p+q=21}$.
