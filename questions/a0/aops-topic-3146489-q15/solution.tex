Put $t=\sqrt{x^2+20x+1}\ge0$. Then the equation becomes $t^2-24=2t$, or $(t-6)(t+4)=0$. Only $t=6$ is allowed. Thus $x^2+20x+1=36$, or $x^2+20x-35=0$. Its discriminant is $540>0$, so it has two real roots, both valid because the radicand equals $36$. Their product, by Vieta, is $\boxed{-35}$.
