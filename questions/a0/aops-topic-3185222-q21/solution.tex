The midpoint theorem makes $PQRS$ a parallelogram with adjacent side lengths $\dfrac{AC}{2}=14$ and $\dfrac{BD}{2}=\dfrac{15}{2}$. If adjacent side vectors are $u,v$, its diagonal vectors are $u+v,u-v$, and $|u+v|^2+|u-v|^2=2|u|^2+2|v|^2$. Thus $PR^2+QS^2=2\left(14^2+\left(\dfrac{15}{2}\right)^2\right)=\boxed{\dfrac{1009}{2}}$.
