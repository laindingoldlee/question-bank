With $a,j,r$ people, $aj+jr+ra=191$. Since $(a+j+r)^2\ge3(aj+jr+ra)=573$, we get $a+j+r\ge24$ (as $23^2=529$). And $7\cdot8+8\cdot9+9\cdot7=191$, so the minimum is $\boxed{24}$.
