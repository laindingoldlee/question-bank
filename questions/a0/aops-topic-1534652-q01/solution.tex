Let Zeno's speed be $v$ miles per hour. Zeno takes $\dfrac{10}{v}$ hours. Achilles takes
\[
\dfrac{5}{2v}+\dfrac{5}{\dfrac {v}{2}}=\dfrac{5}{2v}+\dfrac{10}{v}=\dfrac{25}{2v}
\]
hours. The difference is $\dfrac{25}{2v}-\dfrac{10}{v}=\dfrac{5}{2v}=1$, so $v=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{2}}$ miles per hour. (Zeno takes $4$ hours, Achilles $1+4=5$ hours.)
