Achilles and Zeno compete in a $10$-mile race. Zeno walks at a constant speed. For the first $5$ miles, Achilles runs with a hare that goes twice as fast as Zeno. For the last $5$ miles, Achilles walks with a tortoise that goes half as fast as Zeno. Achilles finishes the race one hour after Zeno. How fast does Zeno walk, in miles per hour?
