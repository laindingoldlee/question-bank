Each interior angle of the decagon is $144^\circ$, so $\angle PCD=144^\circ-60^\circ=84^\circ$. Since $CP=CB=CD$, triangle $PCD$ is isosceles with $\angle CPD=\dfrac{180^\circ-84^\circ}2=48^\circ$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair[] V;for(int k=0;k<10;++k){V.push(dir(90+36*k));}for(int k=0;k<10;++k){draw(V[k]--V[(k+1)%10]);}
pair A=V[4],B=V[5],C=V[6],D=V[7];pair P1=rotate(60,B)*C,P2=rotate(-60,B)*C;pair P=(length(P1)<length(P2))?P1:P2;draw(B--P--C);draw(P--D,dashed);
label("$A$",A,W);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,S);label("$D$",D,SE);label("$P$",P,N);
\end{asy}

Hence $\angle BPD=\angle BPC+\angle CPD=60^\circ+48^\circ=\boxed{108^\circ}$.
