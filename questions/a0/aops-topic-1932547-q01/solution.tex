Let $r$ be an integer root. Then $b=-r(ar+c)$, so $r$ divides $b$, and $r\ne0$. For $r>0$ every term of $ar^2+cr+b$ is positive, so $r<0$. As $b$ is prime, $r=-1$ or $r=-b$.

\textbf{$r=-1$:} $a-c+b=0$, so $c=a+b$. If $a$ is odd, then $a+b$ is even and greater than $2$, not prime. So $a=2$, $b=3$ (the prime after $2$) and $c=5$, which is indeed the prime after $3$. This gives $2x^2+5x+3=(2x+3)(x+1)$ with root $-1$.

\textbf{$r=-b$:} $ab^2-cb+b=0$, so $c=ab+1$. If $a$ is odd, $ab+1$ is even and greater than $2$, not prime. If $a=2$, then $b=3$ and $c=7$, but the prime after $3$ is $5$, not $7$.

So the only possibility is $(a,b,c)=(2,3,5)$, and $b$ has $\boxed{1}$ possible value, namely $b=3$.
