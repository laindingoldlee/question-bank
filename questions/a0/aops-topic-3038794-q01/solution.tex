A seven-digit palindrome is determined by its first four digits; increasing that four-digit prefix increases the whole palindrome. The prefixes immediately below and above $ 2017 $ are $ 2016 $ and $ 2018 $, giving $ B=2016102 $ and $ A=2018102 $. Therefore $\boxed{A-B=2000}$.
