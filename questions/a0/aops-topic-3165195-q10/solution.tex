Randomly losing two and randomly ordering the rest is equivalent to a uniformly random permutation of all six marbles, using the first four for draws and the last two as lost marbles. Conditional on first yellow and then red, the four unseen marbles consist of three blue and one yellow, exchangeable among their positions. The next is therefore blue with probability $\dfrac{3}{4}$, giving $\boxed{m+n=7}$.
