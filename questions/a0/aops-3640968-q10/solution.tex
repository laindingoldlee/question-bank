For odd k let $\chi(k)=(-1)^{\dfrac{(k-1)}{2}}$, a multiplicative function. Expand $\arctan(2^{-k})=\dfrac{\sum_{j\ge1,\ j\text{ odd}}\chi(j)2^{-kj}}{j}$. Absolute convergence permits regrouping by n=kj. The original sum becomes $\dfrac{\sum_{n\ge1,\ n\text{ odd}}\chi(n)2^{-n}\,[\sum_{k\mid n}\mu(k)]}{n}$. The M\"obius divisor sum is zero unless n=1, where it is one. Only that term remains, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$.
