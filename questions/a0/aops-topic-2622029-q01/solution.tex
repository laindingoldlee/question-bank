Expanding both sides proves the identity
\[(ac-bd)^2+(ad+bc)^2=a^2c^2+b^2d^2+a^2d^2+b^2c^2=\left(a^2+b^2\right)\left(c^2+d^2\right).\]
So $\dfrac14+(ad+bc)^2=1$, i.e. $(ad+bc)^2=\dfrac34$. Since all four numbers are positive, $ad+bc=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}}$.

(Such numbers exist: $a=c=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$ and $b=d=\dfrac12$ give $ac-bd=\dfrac12$.)
