\emph{Case $x\le30$.} Then $|60-2x|=60-2x$ and the equation is $x=|4x-60|$. If $x\ge15$: $x=4x-60$, $x=20$. If $x<15$: $x=60-4x$, $x=12$.

\emph{Case $x>30$.} Then $|60-2x|=2x-60$ and the equation is $x=|60|=60$, which satisfies $x>30$.

The solutions are $12,20,60$, with sum $\boxed{92}$.
