Given $12$ heads, all $\binom{16}{4}=1820$ positions of the $4$ tails are equally likely.

The $4$ tails split the heads into $5$ runs (some possibly empty) of lengths $x_1,\dots,x_5\ge0$ with $x_1+\dots+x_5=12$; this is a bijection with the arrangements. We need every $x_i\le4$. By inclusion-exclusion on the runs with $x_i\ge5$ (substitute $x_i'=x_i-5$):
\[
\binom{16}{4}-\binom51\binom{11}{4}+\binom52\binom64=1820-5\cdot330+10\cdot15=320.
\]
(Three runs of at least $5$ would need $15>12$ heads.) Hence the probability is
\[\dfrac{320}{1820}=\boxed{\dfrac{16}{91}}.\]
