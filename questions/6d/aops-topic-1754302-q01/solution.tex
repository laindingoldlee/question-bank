$0$ is a perfect square ($0=0^2$), but it is not a factor of $36$: a factor $t$ of $36$ must satisfy $36=t\cdot k$ for some integer $k$, and $0\cdot k=0\ne36$. The positive square factors of $36=2^2\cdot3^2$ are $2^{2i}3^{2j}$ with $i,j\in\{0,1\}$, namely $1,4,9,36$. So the answer is $\boxed{4}$, and the student is wrong only in counting $0$.
