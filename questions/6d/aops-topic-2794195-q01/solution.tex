Each shared edge reduces the initial perimeter 12 by 2. A cluster of four triangles has at most 3 shared edges: its adjacency graph is a subgraph of the hexagonal honeycomb graph, whose shortest cycle has 6 edges, so a four-vertex subgraph is a forest. Therefore perimeter is at least $12-2\cdot3=6$. Four unit triangles tile an equilateral triangle of side 2, attaining $\boxed6$.

\end{asy}
