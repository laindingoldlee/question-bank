The exponent of a prime $p$ in $P(n)$ is $V_p (n) = \sum_{j\ge1} p^j \dfrac{\lfloor \dfrac{n}{p^j} \rfloor (\lfloor \dfrac{n}{p^j} \rfloor + 1)}{2}$: each multiple of $p^j$ contributes its index once. Since $2025^{2025}=3^{8100}5^{4050}$, both thresholds must hold. At $n=179$, $V_3 = 3 (59 \cdot \dfrac{60}{2}) + 9 (19 \cdot \dfrac{20}{2}) + 27 (6 \cdot \dfrac{7}{2}) + 81 (2 \cdot \dfrac{3}{2}) = 7830 < 8100$, and $V_5=3975<4050$. At $n=180$, the values are $V_3=8190$ and $V_5=4155$. The exponents are nondecreasing in $n$, so the least value is $\boxed{180}$.
