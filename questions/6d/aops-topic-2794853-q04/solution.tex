The radius is the altitude from $B$ to the hypotenuse: $r=\dfrac{15\cdot20}{25}=12$. Since 12 is smaller than both legs, the portion inside the triangle is precisely a quarter-disk. Its area is $\dfrac{1}{4}\pi12^2=\boxed{36\pi}$.
