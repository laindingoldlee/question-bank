Let the painted rectangle be $x$ by $y$ inches, so $xy=72$. The canvas is $(x+8)$ by $(y+8)$, and since its sides are whole numbers, so are $x$ and $y$. The perimeter of the canvas is
\[
2(x+8)+2(y+8)=2(x+y)+32 ,
\]
so we must minimise $x+y$ over the factor pairs of $72$: $1+72$, $2+36$, $3+24$, $4+18$, $6+12$, $8+9$. The least sum is $8+9=17$, for a $9\times8$ painted region on a $17\times16$ canvas.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((0,0)--(17,0)--(17,16)--(0,16)--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
filldraw((4,4)--(13,4)--(13,12)--(4,12)--cycle,lightgray,linewidth(0.8));
label("painted",(8.5,8.6));label("$9\times8$",(8.5,7.2));
draw((13,8)--(17,8),dashed);label("$4$",(15,8),N);
draw((8.5,12)--(8.5,16),dashed);label("$4$",(8.5,14),E);
label("$17$",(8.5,0),S);label("$16$",(17,8),E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The least possible perimeter is $2\cdot17+32=\boxed{66}$ inches.
