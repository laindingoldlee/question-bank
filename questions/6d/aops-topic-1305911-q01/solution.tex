We need the number of derangements of $5$ objects. By inclusion-exclusion over the set of students who do get their own paper,
\[
D_5=5!-\dbinom51 4!+\dbinom52 3!-\dbinom53 2!+\dbinom54 1!-\dbinom55 0!=120-120+60-20+5-1=\boxed{44}.
\]
