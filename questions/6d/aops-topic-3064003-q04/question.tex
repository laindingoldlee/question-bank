An isosceles trapezoid $ABCD$ has longer base $AD$ parallel to $BC$, base angles $60^\circ$ at $A,D$, and diagonals of length $10\sqrt{21}$. A point $E$ satisfies $EA=10\sqrt7$ and $ED=30\sqrt7$. Let $F$ be the perpendicular foot from $C$ to $AD$. Find $EF$. Write $EF=m\sqrt n$, where $n$ is squarefree, and find $m+n$.
