The numerator factors as $(t-1)(t-2)(t-3)(t-4)$: indeed, with $u=t^2-5t$,
\[(t-1)(t-4)\cdot(t-2)(t-3)=(u+4)(u+6)=u^2+10u+24=t^4-10t^3+35t^2-50t+24.\]
The denominator is $(t-3)(t-4)$. So $f(t)$ is undefined for $t=3$ and $t=4$, and otherwise
\[f(t)=(t-1)(t-2).\]
The remaining values are $t=1,2,5,6,7$, with $f(t)=0,0,12,20,30$. The multiples of $4$ are $0$, $0$, $12$ and $20$ (zero is a multiple of $4$), so the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac45}$.
