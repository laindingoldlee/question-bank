Can a quadratic equation $ax^2+bx+c=0$ ($a\ne0$) have exactly one real root and one non-real root?
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that this is impossible when $a$, $b$, $c$ are real.
\item[(b)] Show that it is possible when the coefficients may be non-real complex numbers.
\end{enumerate}
