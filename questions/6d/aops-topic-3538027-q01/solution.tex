The denominators are $91,243,189,341$. Clearing denominators gives $243x+91y=22113$ and $341x+189y=64449$. The determinant is $243\cdot189-91\cdot341=14896\ne0$. Elimination yields $\boxed{x=-\dfrac{17199}{152},\quad y=\dfrac{82863}{152}}$. Substitution in the cleared equations verifies both values.
