For all real $x,y$,
\[25\left(x^2+y^2\right)-(3x+4y)^2=16x^2-24xy+9y^2=(4x-3y)^2\ge0 .\]
(This is the Cauchy--Schwarz inequality $(3x+4y)^2\le\left(3^2+4^2\right)\left(x^2+y^2\right)$.) Hence
\[\dfrac{(3x+4y)^2}{x^2+y^2}\le25,\]
with equality exactly when $4x=3y$, for example $x=3$, $y=4$, which gives $\dfrac{25^2}{25}=25$. The maximum is $\boxed{25}$.
