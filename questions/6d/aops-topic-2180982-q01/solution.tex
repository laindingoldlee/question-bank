Write $2020^{4040}=202^{4040}\cdot10^{4040}$. So the last $4040$ digits are zeros, and the two digits before them are the last two digits of $202^{4040}$ (which has many more than two digits). Since $202\equiv2\pmod{100}$, these are the last two digits of $2^{4040}$.

Let $N=2^{4040}$. Then $N\equiv0\pmod4$. Modulo $25$: $2^{10}=1024\equiv-1$, so $2^{20}\equiv1$ and $N=\left(2^{20}\right)^{202}\equiv1\pmod{25}$. The numbers below $100$ that are $\equiv1\pmod{25}$ are $1$, $26$, $51$, $76$, and the only multiple of $4$ among them is $76$. So $N\equiv76\pmod{100}$.

The last $4042$ digits of $2020^{4040}$ are therefore $7$, $6$ and then $4040$ zeros, and their sum is $7+6=\boxed{13}$.
