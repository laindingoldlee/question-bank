In the prime factorisations of positive integers $a$, $b$ and $c$: $a$ and $b$ have exactly $4$ prime factors in common, $a$ and $c$ have exactly $5$ prime factors in common, and $b$ and $c$ have exactly $6$ prime factors in common. (Prime factors are counted with multiplicity: for example, $24=2\cdot2\cdot2\cdot3$ and $36=2\cdot2\cdot3\cdot3$ have the $3$ prime factors $2,2,3$ in common.) What is the least possible value of the product $abc$?

A book gives the answer $1{,}327{,}104=2^{14}\cdot3^4$, from $a=2^5$, $b=2^4\cdot3^2$, $c=2^5\cdot3^2$. Is it correct?
