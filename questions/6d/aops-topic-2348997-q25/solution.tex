Work backwards: before giving $8$ to Jessica she had $12$. These $12$ are the $30\%$ left after giving $70\%$ to Mara, so she then had $\dfrac{12}{0.3}=40$, which is half of her original number. She started with $\boxed{80}$ marbles.
