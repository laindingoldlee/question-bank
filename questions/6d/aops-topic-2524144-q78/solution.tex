If the roots $r,s$ satisfy $r+s=rs$, then $(r-1)(s-1)=1$. With positive integers this forces $r-1=s-1=1$, so $r=s=2$ and the quadratic is $(x-2)^2=x^2-4x+4$. Its vertex is $\boxed{(2,0)}$.
