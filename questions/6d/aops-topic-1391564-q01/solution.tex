Since $CE=9$ and $OC=15$, the point $E$ is at distance $OE=6$ from the centre. A chord through $E$ at distance $h$ from $O$ has length $2\sqrt{225-h^2}$, and $h$ takes every value from $0$ (the diameter $CD$) to $OE=6$ (the chord perpendicular to $CD$ at $E$, dashed below).

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0), C=(-15,0), D=(15,0), Ep=(-6,0);
pair P1=(-6,sqrt(189)), P2=(-6,-sqrt(189));
real t=aTan(0.75); pair u=dir(t);
real s1=-dot(Ep,u)+sqrt(dot(Ep,u)^2-(dot(Ep,Ep)-225));
real s2=-dot(Ep,u)-sqrt(dot(Ep,u)^2-(dot(Ep,Ep)-225));
pair A=Ep+s1*u, B=Ep+s2*u;
draw(circle(O,15),linewidth(0.9));
draw(C--D,linewidth(0.9));
draw(A--B,blue);
draw(P1--P2,red+dashed);
draw(Ep+(0,1.2)--Ep+(-1.2,1.2)--Ep+(-1.2,0));
dot(O);dot(C);dot(D);dot(Ep);dot(A);dot(B);
label("$O$",O,S); label("$C$",C,W); label("$D$",D,E); label("$E$",Ep,SE);
label("$A$",A,NE); label("$B$",B,SW);
label("$9$",(C+Ep)/2,S); label("$6$",Ep+0.72*(O-Ep),N);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

So the lengths of chords through $E$ fill the interval from $2\sqrt{225-36}=2\sqrt{189}\approx27.5$ to $30$. The integer lengths are $28$, $29$, $30$.
\begin{itemize}
\item Length $30$: only the diameter, $1$ chord.
\item Lengths $28$ and $29$: each is strictly between the minimum and the maximum, and there are two chords through $E$ of each such length (mirror images in line $CD$): $2+2=4$ chords.
\end{itemize}
The number of chords is $1+4=\boxed{5}$.
