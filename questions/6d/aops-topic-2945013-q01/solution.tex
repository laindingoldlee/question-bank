Each numerator term is twice the corresponding denominator term, so the numerator is $2(62+52+\cdots+12)$ and the quotient is $\boxed{2}$ (the sums are $444$ and $222$).

Division distributes over a sum in the numerator, $\dfrac{a+b}c=\dfrac ac+\dfrac bc$, but not in the denominator: $\dfrac{a+b}{c+d}\ne\dfrac ac+\dfrac bd$ in general.
