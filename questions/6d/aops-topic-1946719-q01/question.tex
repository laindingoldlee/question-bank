A function machine gives the outputs $9,36,81,144$ for the inputs $1,2,3,4$. Find the polynomial $f$ of degree at most $3$ with $f(1)=9$, $f(2)=36$, $f(3)=81$, $f(4)=144$, prove that it is the only one, and find $f(5)$.
