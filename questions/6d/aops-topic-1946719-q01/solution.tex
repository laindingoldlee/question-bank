The outputs are $9\cdot1,\,9\cdot4,\,9\cdot9,\,9\cdot16$, i.e. $(3n)^2$, so $f(n)=9n^2$ works.

It is the only such polynomial: if $g$ also has degree at most $3$ and the same four values, then $f-g$ has degree at most $3$ and the four roots $1,2,3,4$, so $f-g$ is the zero polynomial.

Hence $f(n)=\boxed{9n^2}$ and $f(5)=225$.
