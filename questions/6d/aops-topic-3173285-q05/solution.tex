Write $c=ak$ with positive integer $k$. Then $a(a+b+k)=53$. Since $53$ is prime, $a=1$ or $53$. The latter would require $a+b+k=1$, impossible. Hence $a=1$ and $b+k=52$, yielding exactly $\boxed{(a,b,c)=(1,b,52-b)\quad(1\le b\le51)}$.
