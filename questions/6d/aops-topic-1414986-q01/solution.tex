Let the rectangle be $x$ by $15-x$ metres (the perimeter is $2x+2(15-x)=30$). Its area is
\[
x(15-x)=\dfrac{225}{4}-\left(x-\dfrac{15}{2}\right)^2\le\dfrac{225}{4}=56.25,
\]
with equality only for $x=7.5$. So the best rectangle is the square $\boxed{7.5\text{ m by }7.5\text{ m}}$, with area $56.25\text{ m}^2$; for example a $10$ by $5$ rectangle (dashed) gives only $50\text{ m}^2$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((0,0)--(7.5,0)--(7.5,7.5)--(0,7.5)--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
label("$7.5$",(3.75,0),S); label("$7.5$",(7.5,3.75),E);
label("area $56.25$",(3.75,3.75));
draw((10,0)--(20,0)--(20,5)--(10,5)--cycle,blue+dashed);
label("$10$",(15,0),S,blue); label("$5$",(20,2.5),E,blue); label("area $50$",(15,2.5),blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

If any shape is allowed, a circle does better: with circumference $30$ its radius is $\dfrac{15}{\pi}$ and its area is $\pi\cdot\dfrac{225}{\pi^2}=\dfrac{225}{\pi}\approx71.6\text{ m}^2$. (By the isoperimetric inequality the circle encloses the most area of all shapes with a given perimeter.)
