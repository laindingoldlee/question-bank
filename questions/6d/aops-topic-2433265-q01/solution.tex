For $a>0$, let $t=a^{\dfrac13}=\sqrt[3]{a}>0$, so $a=t^3$. By the definition of rational exponents, $a^{\dfrac53}=\left(a^{\dfrac13}\right)^5=t^5$ and $a^{\dfrac23}=t^2$. Hence
\[a^{\dfrac53}\div a=\dfrac{t^5}{t^3}=t^2=a^{\dfrac23}.\]
This is the rule $a^m\div a^n=a^{m-n}$ with $m=\dfrac53$ and $n=1=\dfrac33$: $\dfrac53-\dfrac33=\boxed{\dfrac23}$.
