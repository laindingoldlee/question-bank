James and Charles each pick two random points on distinct sides of a square (each person's two points are chosen uniformly on the boundary, conditioned on lying on different sides), and each joins his two points with a segment. The probability that the two segments intersect is $\dfrac mn$ for relatively prime positive integers $m,n$. Find $m+n$.
