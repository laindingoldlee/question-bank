Jack and Jill live on opposite sides of a symmetric hill, at the same walking distance from the top, and each of them walks up at a constant speed. Jack leaves home at 6:00 PM and reaches the top at 8:00 PM. Jill leaves home at 7:00 PM and reaches the top $13.5$ minutes after Jack does. How many times as fast as Jack does Jill walk? Give the exact ratio and its value rounded to the nearest hundredth.
