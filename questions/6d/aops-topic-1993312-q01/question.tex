Two points on the circumference of a circle of radius $r$ are selected independently and uniformly at random. From each point a chord of length $r$ is drawn in a clockwise direction (that is, the other endpoint of the chord is reached from the chosen point by moving clockwise around the circle). What is the probability that the two chords intersect?

$\textbf{(A) } \dfrac{1}{10}\qquad\textbf{(B) } \dfrac{1}{5}\qquad\textbf{(C) } \dfrac{1}{4}\qquad\textbf{(D) } \dfrac{1}{3}\qquad\textbf{(E) } \dfrac{1}{2}$
