Jerry has several identical strings of brand $X$, each taking exactly $1$ hour to burn, and several of brand $Y$, each taking exactly $2$ hours, and matches. The strings burn unevenly (different pieces may burn at different speeds), but a string lit at both ends always burns out in half its usual time. Once lit, a fire cannot be put out. How can he measure exactly $22$ minutes and $30$ seconds?
