The ratio of the rightmost to leftmost column widths is $\dfrac{15}{6}=\dfrac{5}{2}$, since those areas share the bottom-row height. The same width ratio applies to the top row, so the top-right area is $10\cdot\dfrac{5}{2}=\boxed{25}$.

\end{asy}
