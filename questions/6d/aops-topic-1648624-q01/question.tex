In a group of $18$ girls, every girl likes reading at least one of three kinds of publication: $10$ girls like women's magazines, $7$ like health magazines and $5$ like newspapers. (A girl may like more than one kind.)
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Explain why these numbers do not determine how many girls like \emph{only} women's magazines.
\item[(b)] Find the least and the greatest possible number of girls who like only women's magazines.
\end{enumerate}
