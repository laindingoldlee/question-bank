A semicircle with radius $4$ has centre $O$, and $A$ and $B$ are distinct points on its curved part. Suppose that at least one angle of $\triangle OAB$ has an integer measure in degrees, and that, up to congruence, each triangle $OAB$ satisfying this is equally likely. What is the probability that the area of $\triangle OAB$ is at most $4$?
