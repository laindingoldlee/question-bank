Winning in $4+k$ games means winning the last game and exactly three of the first $3+k$. Sum the disjoint possibilities:
\[\sum_{k=0}^3\binom{3+k}{k}\left(\dfrac{3}{8}\right)^4\left(\dfrac{5}{8}\right)^k=\boxed{\dfrac{127413}{524288}}.\]
