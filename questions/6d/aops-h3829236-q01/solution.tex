Reality requires $|x|,|y|\le1$. Apply Cauchy-Schwarz to the vectors $(x,\sqrt{1-x^2})$ and $(\sqrt{1-y^2},y)$, each of length $1$. It gives
\[
\left|x\sqrt{1-y^2}+y\sqrt{1-x^2}\right|\le1.
\]
The left side of the proposed equation therefore cannot equal $3$. Thus $\boxed{\text{there are no real solutions}}$.
