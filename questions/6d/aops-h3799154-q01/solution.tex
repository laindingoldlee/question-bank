The $N$th edition is in year $2000+N$, and $N\mid2000+N$ exactly when $N\mid2000$. Since $2000=2^4\cdot5^3$, its twenty divisors give the years
\[\boxed{\begin{gathered}2001,2002,2004,2005,2008,2010,2016,2020,2025,2040,\\2050,2080,2100,2125,2200,2250,2400,2500,3000,4000.\end{gathered}}\]
There are no others because the edition number must be a positive divisor of $2000$.
