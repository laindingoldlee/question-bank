Let $a_n$ be the number of allowed length-$n$ strings. A string ends in $0$ after any allowed length-$n-1$ prefix, or in $01$ after an allowed length-$n-2$ prefix; for $n\ge2$ this gives $a_n=a_{n-1}+a_{n-2}$. With $a_0=1,a_1=2$, the values through $n=7$ are $1,2,3,5,8,13,21,34$. Thus $\boxed{34}$.
