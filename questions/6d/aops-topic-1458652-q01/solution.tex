Since $\angle AFC=\angle ADC=90^\circ$, the points $F$ and $D$ lie on the circle with diameter $AC$, so $AFDC$ is a cyclic quadrilateral. Hence its exterior angle at $D$ equals the opposite interior angle:
\[
\angle BDF=180^\circ-\angle FDC=\angle FAC=\angle A .
\]
In the isosceles triangle $SBC$ ($SB=SC$) the central angle is $\angle BSC=2\angle A$ (the triangle is acute, so $S$ lies inside it, on the same side of $BC$ as $A$). So
\[
\angle SBC=\dfrac{180^\circ-2\angle A}{2}=90^\circ-\angle A .
\]
Let line $BS$ meet $DF$ at $X$. In triangle $BDX$ the angles at $B$ and $D$ are $\angle XBD=\angle SBC=90^\circ-\angle A$ and $\angle BDX=\angle BDF=\angle A$, so
\[
\angle BXD=180^\circ-(90^\circ-\angle A)-\angle A=90^\circ .
\]
Therefore $SB\perp DF$. $\blacksquare$
