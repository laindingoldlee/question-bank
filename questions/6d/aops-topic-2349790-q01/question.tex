Let
\[n=a\cdot1!+b\cdot2!+c\cdot3!+d\cdot4!+e\cdot5!,\]
where $a,b,c,d,e$ are integers with $0\le a\le1$, $0\le b\le2$, $0\le c\le3$, $0\le d\le4$ and $0\le e\le5$. Find the sum of all possible values of $n$ if $c=0$.
