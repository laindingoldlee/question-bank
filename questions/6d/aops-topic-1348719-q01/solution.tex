Multiply by $3xy$: $3y+18x=xy$, that is $xy-18x-3y=0$, and adding $54$ gives
\[
(x-3)(y-18)=54 .
\]
Because $\dfrac{1}{x}<\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $\dfrac{6}{y}<\dfrac{1}{3}$, we need $x>3$ and $y>18$, so both factors are positive. Conversely, each factorisation $54=uv$ into positive integers gives the solution $x=u+3$, $y=v+18$.

Since $54=2\cdot3^3$ has $(1+1)(3+1)=8$ positive divisors, there are $\boxed{8}$ ordered pairs, for example $(4,72)$, $(5,45)$, $(6,36)$, $(9,27)$, $(12,24)$, $(21,21)$, $(30,20)$, $(57,19)$.
