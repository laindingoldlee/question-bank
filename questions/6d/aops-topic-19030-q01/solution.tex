Let the square have side $1$, and let $M$ be the midpoint of $AB$.

\textbf{The angles at $A$ and $B$.} Since $E$ lies inside the square, the ray $AE$ lies between the rays $AB$ and $AD$, so $\angle EAB<90^\circ$; similarly $\angle EBA<90^\circ$. So the triangle is obtuse exactly when $\angle AEB>90^\circ$.

\textbf{The angle at $E$.} By the law of cosines, $\angle AEB>90^\circ$ exactly when $AE^2+BE^2<AB^2$. By the median length formula (Apollonius), $AE^2+BE^2=2EM^2+\dfrac{AB^2}{2}$, so the condition becomes $EM<\dfrac{AB}{2}=\dfrac{1}{2}$: the point $E$ must lie inside the circle with diameter $AB$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0), B=(2,0), C=(2,2), D=(0,2), M=(1,0);
fill(arc(M,1,0,180)--cycle,paleblue);
draw(arc(M,1,0,180),blue+linewidth(0.9));
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
pair Ept=(1.25,0.55);
draw(A--Ept--B,red); dot(Ept,red); dot(M);
label("$A$",A,SW); label("$B$",B,SE); label("$C$",C,NE); label("$D$",D,NW); label("$M$",M,S);
label("$E$",Ept,N,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The part of this circle inside the square is a half-disc of radius $\dfrac{1}{2}$, with area $\dfrac{1}{2}\pi\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^2=\dfrac{\pi}{8}$. The probability is therefore
\[\dfrac{\pi}{8}\approx0.3927\approx\boxed{0.39}.\]
