Let $a$ be the limiting probability that $4$ integers chosen uniformly and independently from $\{1,\dots,n\}$ have greatest common divisor $1$, as $n\to\infty$. Find $\lfloor300a\rfloor$, using $\zeta(4)=\sum\dfrac1{k^4}=\dfrac{\pi^4}{90}$.
