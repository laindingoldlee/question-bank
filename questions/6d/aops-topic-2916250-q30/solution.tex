For each prime $p$, all four are divisible by $p$ with probability $p^{-4}$, independently across primes in the limit. So $a=\prod_p(1-p^{-4})=\dfrac1{\zeta(4)}=\dfrac{90}{\pi^4}$. Then $300a=\dfrac{27000}{\pi^4}\approx277.2$ and the answer is $\boxed{277}$.
