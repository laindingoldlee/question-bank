Let her usual speed be $v$ mph. The distance is $\dfrac {v}{2}$ miles, and at $v-10$ mph the trip takes $40$ minutes $=\dfrac{2}{3}$ h:
\[
\dfrac {v}{2}=\dfrac{2}{3}(v-10)\ \Longrightarrow\ 3v=4v-40\ \Longrightarrow\ v=40 .
\]
So she drives $\dfrac{40}{2}=\boxed{20}$ miles. (Check: $20$ miles at $30$ mph takes $40$ minutes.)
