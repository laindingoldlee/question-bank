With $n$ people there are $\dbinom n2=\dfrac{n(n-1)}{2}$ photos, and John is in $n-1$ of them (one with each other person). So
\[
\dfrac{n-1}{\dfrac{n(n-1)}{2}}=\dfrac{2}{n}=\dfrac{1}{12},
\]
giving $n=\boxed{24}$.
