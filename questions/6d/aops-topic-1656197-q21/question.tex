At a middle school, $25\%$ of the students are in sixth grade, $35\%$ in seventh grade and $40\%$ in eighth grade. Of the sixth graders, $n\%$ like math. Of the seventh graders, $\dfrac{100+n}{2}\%$ like math. Of the eighth graders, $(n+20)\%$ do \emph{not} like math. If $51\%$ of all the students like math, what is the value of $n$?

(a) $10$\quad (b) $15$\quad (c) $20$\quad (d) $25$\quad (e) $30$\quad (f) $40$\quad (g) $50$\quad (h) $60$\quad (i) $70$\quad (j) $80$
