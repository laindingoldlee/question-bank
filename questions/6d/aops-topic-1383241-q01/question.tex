Let $b$ and $d$ be relatively prime positive integers, and let $a$ and $c$ be integers.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Let $b'$ and $d'$ be integers with $bb'\equiv1\pmod d$ and $dd'\equiv1\pmod b$ (they exist because $\gcd(b,d)=1$). Show that $x_0=a\,dd'+c\,bb'$ satisfies $x_0\equiv a\pmod b$ and $x_0\equiv c\pmod d$, and that the integers satisfying both congruences are exactly those with $x\equiv x_0\pmod{bd}$.
\item[(b)] Use (a) to solve the system $x\equiv2\pmod5$, $x\equiv3\pmod7$.
\end{enumerate}
