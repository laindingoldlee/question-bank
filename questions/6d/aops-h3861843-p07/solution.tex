Let $N$ be the midpoint of $AB$. Triangle $AMB$ has sides $3,4,5$, hence is right at $M$, so its median to $AB$ has length $MN=\dfrac{5}{2}$. In a parallelogram the segment joining the midpoints of opposite sides equals and is parallel to either other side; hence $BC=MN=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{2}}$.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

\end{asy}
