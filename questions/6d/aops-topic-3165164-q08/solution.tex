The identity $L_n^2+L_nL_{n+1}-L_{n+1}^2=5(-1)^n$ follows by checking $n=0$ and observing that replacing $(a,b)$ by $(b,a+b)$ negates the left side. Put $a=L_{2019}$, $b=L_{2020}$, so $a^2+ab-b^2=-5$, and $L_{2023}=2a+3b$. Expanding gives $(2a+3b)(7a-4b)=(a^2+b^2)+13(a^2+ab-b^2)=a^2+b^2-65$. Thus $a^2+b^2\equiv65\pmod{L_{2023}}$. Since $L_{2023}>65$ by the increasing recurrence (already $L_9=76$), the remainder is $\boxed{65}$.
