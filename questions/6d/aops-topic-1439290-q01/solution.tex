A two-digit number has digit sum at most $18$, so the prime has at least three digits. The three-digit numbers with digit sum $20$, in increasing order, begin
\[
299,\ 389,\ 398,\ \dots
\]
(a three-digit number $\overline{1xy}$ would need $x+y=19$, impossible, and $\overline{2xy}$ needs $x+y=18$, so only $299$; then $\overline{3xy}$ with $x+y=17$ gives $389$ and $398$). Now $299=13\cdot23$ is not prime, while $389$ is prime: it is not divisible by $2,3,5,7,11,13,17,19$, and $19^2=361<389<400=20^2$, so these are all the primes we need to test. The answer is $\boxed{389}$.
