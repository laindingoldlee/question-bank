Put $d=x+22>22$. Since $x\equiv-22\pmod d$, the condition is $d\mid22^{20}=2^{20}11^{20}$. This number has $21^2=441$ divisors. Its divisors at most $22$ are $1,2,4,8,11,16,22$, seven in total. Thus the answer is $\boxed{434}$.
