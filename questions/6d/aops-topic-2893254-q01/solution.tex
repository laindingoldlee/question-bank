$x^{2014}-x^{2004}=x^{2004}(x^{10}-1)=x^{2004}(x^5-1)(x^5+1)$
\[=\boxed{x^{2004}(x-1)(x+1)(x^4+x^3+x^2+x+1)(x^4-x^3+x^2-x+1)}.\]
The claimed form is wrong: at $x=2$ it gives $2^{2014}\cdot31$, but the true value is $2^{2004}\cdot1023=2^{2014}-2^{2004}$.
