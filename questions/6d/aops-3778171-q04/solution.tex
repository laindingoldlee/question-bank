Label each city\textquotesingle s residents by residues modulo $10$. Have resident $i$ of the first city call residents $i,i+1,i+3$ of the second. This gives thirty distinct calls. The six nonzero ordered differences of $\{0,1,3\}$ modulo $10$ are $1,2,3,7,8,9$, all distinct. Therefore two different first-city residents have at most one common contact: two common contacts would express their label difference as two distinct ordered differences of that set. A completely connected pair of residents in each city is consequently impossible, which proves the required property.
