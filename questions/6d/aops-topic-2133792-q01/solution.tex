Write $11=10+1$ and expand with the binomial theorem:
\[11^{56}=\sum_{k=0}^{56}\binom{56}{k}10^k .\]
Every term with $k\ge3$ is a multiple of $1000$. The remaining terms are
\[\binom{56}{0}+\binom{56}{1}\cdot10+\binom{56}{2}\cdot100=1+560+1540\cdot100=1+560+154000 .\]
Since $154000$ is also a multiple of $1000$, we get $11^{56}\equiv561\pmod{1000}$, so the last three digits are $\boxed{561}$.
