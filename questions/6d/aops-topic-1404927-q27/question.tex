Nonzero real numbers $a,b,c$ with $b+c$, $c+a$, $a+b$ all nonzero satisfy
\[
k=\dfrac{a}{b+c}=\dfrac{b}{c+a}=\dfrac{c}{a+b}.
\]
How many possible values of $k$ are there?

(A) $1$\quad (B) $2$\quad (C) $3$\quad (D) $4$\quad (E) $6$
