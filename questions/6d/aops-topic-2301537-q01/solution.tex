Label the angles $\angle1=\angle CAB$, $\angle2=\angle DAC$ and $\angle3=\angle DCA$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0), B=(4,0), D=4*dir(55), C=B+D;
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); draw(A--C,blue);
label("$A$",A,SW); label("$B$",B,SE); label("$C$",C,NE); label("$D$",D,NW);
draw(arc(A,0.9,0,27.5),red); draw(arc(A,1.1,27.5,55),red); draw(arc(C,0.9,180,207.5),red);
label("$1$",A+1.4*dir(14),fontsize(8pt)); label("$2$",A+1.5*dir(41),fontsize(8pt)); label("$3$",C+1.4*dir(194),fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}

Since $AC$ bisects $\angle DAB$, $\angle1=\angle2$. Since $AB\parallel DC$ (opposite sides of a parallelogram), the alternate angles along the transversal $AC$ are equal: $\angle3=\angle1$. Hence $\angle3=\angle2$, that is, in triangle $ACD$ the angles at $A$ and $C$ are equal, so the triangle is isosceles with $DA=DC$.

In a parallelogram opposite sides are equal: $AB=DC$ and $BC=DA$. Together with $DA=DC$ this gives $AB=BC=CD=DA$, so $ABCD$ is a rhombus. $\blacksquare$
