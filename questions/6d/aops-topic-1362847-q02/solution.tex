Let the three take $16+x$, $17+y$ and $20+z$ cans with $x,y,z\ge0$. Then
\[
x+y+z=100-53=47 .
\]
By stars and bars, the number of solutions in nonnegative integers is
\[
\dbinom{47+2}{2}=\dbinom{49}{2}=\boxed{1176}.
\]
