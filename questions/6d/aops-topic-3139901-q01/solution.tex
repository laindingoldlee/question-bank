Events $ U,V $ are independent exactly when $ P(U\cap V)=P(U)P(V)$. Here $ P(A)=\dfrac{1}{2}$, $ P(B)=\dfrac{1}{3}$, and $ A\cap B=\{6\}$ has probability $\dfrac{1}{6}$, equal to the product. Thus $ A,B $ are independent. But $ P(C)=\dfrac{1}{2}$ while $ A\cap C=\{4,6\}$ has probability $\dfrac{1}{3}\ne\dfrac{1}{4}$. Thus $ A,C $ are not independent.
