The $k$-th term is $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt[3]{k^2}+\sqrt[3]{k(k+1)}+\sqrt[3]{(k+1)^2}}$, for $k=1,2,\dots,10$. Put $u=\sqrt[3]{k+1}$ and $v=\sqrt[3]{k}$. The denominator is $u^2+uv+v^2$, and
\[
(u-v)(u^2+uv+v^2)=u^3-v^3=(k+1)-k=1 .
\]
So the $k$-th term equals $u-v=\sqrt[3]{k+1}-\sqrt[3]{k}$, and the sum telescopes:
\[
\sum_{k=1}^{10}\left(\sqrt[3]{k+1}-\sqrt[3]{k}\right)=\sqrt[3]{11}-\sqrt[3]{1}=\boxed{\sqrt[3]{11}-1}.
\]
