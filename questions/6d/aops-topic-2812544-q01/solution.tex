Let the roots be $\dfrac ar,\ a,\ ar$ with $a,r$ real and nonzero. By Vieta: the sum is $a\left(\dfrac1r+1+r\right)=30$, the sum of pairwise products is $a^2\left(\dfrac1r+1+r\right)=-k$, and the product is $a^3=-20k$.

Dividing the second by the first: $a=-\dfrac k{30}$. Then $a^3=-20k=600a$, so $a^2=600$ and $a=\pm10\sqrt6$. Real $r$ requires $\left|r+\dfrac1r\right|\ge2$. From $\dfrac1r+1+r=\dfrac{30}a=\pm\dfrac{\sqrt6}2$, only the minus sign works ($r+\dfrac1r=-1-\dfrac{\sqrt6}2<-2$), so $a=-10\sqrt6$ and $k=-30a=\boxed{300\sqrt6}$. (The roots are about $15.31$, $-24.49$, $39.18$.)
