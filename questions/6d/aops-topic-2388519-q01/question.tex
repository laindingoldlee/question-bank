A game of dots and boxes is played on a board of $10\times10$ unit boxes, that is, on an $11\times11$ square array of dots. On each turn a player draws one unit segment joining two neighbouring dots. A player who draws the fourth side of a box completes it. What is the largest number of segments that can be drawn so that no box has three or four of its sides drawn (so that nobody can complete a box on the next turn)? Show how this number can be reached.
