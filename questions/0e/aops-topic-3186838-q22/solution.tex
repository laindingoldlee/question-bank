The identity $f(x,y)=\dfrac{y^4-x^4}{y-x}$ gives $f(k,k+2)=\dfrac{(k+2)^4-k^4}{2}$. Summing from $k=0$ to $9$ telescopes to $\dfrac{10^4+11^4-0^4-1^4}{2}=\boxed{12320}$.
