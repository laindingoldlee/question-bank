Let $n=\overline{abc}$. From $n\equiv4\pmod8$ write $n=8k+4$. Then $n\equiv7\pmod9$ gives $8k\equiv3\pmod9$; as $8\equiv-1$, this is $k\equiv-3\equiv6\pmod9$. So $k=9j+6$ and $n=72j+52$, i.e. $n\equiv52\pmod{72}$.

Next, $n\equiv1\pmod7$: since $72\equiv2$ and $52\equiv3\pmod7$, we need $2j+3\equiv1$, i.e. $2j\equiv-2$, $j\equiv6\pmod7$. So $j=7i+6$ and
\[
n=72(7i+6)+52=504i+484 .
\]
The three-digit values are $\boxed{484\text{ and }988}$. (Check: $484=7\cdot69+1=8\cdot60+4=9\cdot53+7$, and $988=484+504$.)
