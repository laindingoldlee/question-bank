Model a star system as follows. The Sun is a ball, and ten planets are points outside it. A planet is \emph{visible} from a point $P$ on the Sun's surface if it lies strictly on the outer side of the tangent plane to the Sun at $P$. Prove that there is a point on the Sun's surface from which at most four of the ten planets are visible (so fewer than five).
