The books inside the informational pile can be ordered in $5!$ ways, the practice pile in $3!$ ways and the solutions pile in $1!$ way, and the three piles can be ordered in $3!$ ways. By the multiplication principle the total is
\[
3!\cdot5!\cdot3!\cdot1!=6\cdot120\cdot6=\boxed{4320}.
\]
