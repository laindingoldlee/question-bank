Bobby has $5$ different informational books, $3$ different practice-test books and $1$ solutions book. He puts them in $3$ piles, one pile for each kind of book, and places the piles in a row on his desk. If the order of the books within each pile matters and the order of the piles in the row matters, in how many ways can he do this?
