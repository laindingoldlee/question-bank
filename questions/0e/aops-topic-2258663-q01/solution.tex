With simple interest, each month adds $7\%$ of the amount lent, $C$. After $8$ months the interest is $8\cdot0.07\,C=0.56\,C$, so the payment is
\[C+0.56\,C=1.56\,C=780,\qquad C=\dfrac{780}{1.56}=\boxed{500}\ \text{dollars}.\]
