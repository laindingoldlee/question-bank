$N$ must be divisible by $4$, so the number formed by its last two digits is a multiple of $4$. The possible endings are $33,34,43,44$, and only $44$ is a multiple of $4$. So $N$ ends in $44$.

$N$ must be divisible by $3$, so its digit sum is. If $N$ has $p$ threes and $q$ fours, its digit sum is $3p+4q\equiv q\pmod3$, so $3\mid q$. Since $q\ge2$ (the ending $44$), $q\ge3$, and $p\ge1$. So $N$ has at least $4$ digits, and a $4$-digit $N$ has exactly one $3$ and three $4$s and ends in $44$: it is $3444$ or $4344$. The smaller is
\[N=\boxed{3444}=12\cdot287.\]
