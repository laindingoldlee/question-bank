Use the complement: count the three-digit numbers whose digits are all even. The hundreds digit can be $2,4,6,8$ ($4$ choices; it cannot be $0$), and the tens and units digits can each be $0,2,4,6,8$ ($5$ choices each). That gives $4\cdot5\cdot5=100$ numbers out of $900$. Hence
\[
P(\text{at least one odd digit})=1-\dfrac{100}{900}=\boxed{\dfrac{8}{9}}.
\]
The answer $\dfrac{209}{225}$ comes from allowing only $4$ even digits in the tens and units places; but $0$ is an even digit and is allowed there.
