Let Nemo's speed in still water be $v$ mph and the distance $d$ miles. Then
\[
d=10(v+10)=15(v-10).
\]
So $10v+100=15v-150$, giving $v=50$, and $d=10\cdot60=\boxed{600\text{ miles}}$. Check: upstream $\dfrac{600}{40}=15$ hours.
