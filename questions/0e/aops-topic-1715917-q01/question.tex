Let $n$ be a positive integer, $a=2^n\cdot7^{n+1}+11$ and $b=2^{n+1}\cdot7^n+3$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that the fraction $\dfrac {a}{b}$ is in lowest terms.
\item[(b)] Prove that $a+b-7$ is not a perfect square for any positive integer $n$.
\end{enumerate}
