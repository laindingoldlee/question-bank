Since $D=2r$, $\tau=\dfrac{C}{r}=2\dfrac{C}{D}=\boxed{2\pi}$. The definition also gives $\boxed{C=\tau r}$. Both conventions describe the same circumference.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

pair O=(0,0),A=(-1,0),B=(1,0);draw(circle(O,1));draw(A--B);dot(O);label("$O$",O,N);label("$r$",(.5,0),N);label("$D=2r$",(0,-.25),S);label("circumference $C$",(0,1),N);
\end{asy}
