By Bayes' theorem,
\[
P(E\mid T)=\dfrac{P(E)P(T\mid E)}{P(E)P(T\mid E)+P(\overline E)P(T\mid\overline E)}=\dfrac{0.2\cdot0.75}{0.2\cdot0.75+0.8\cdot0.4}=\dfrac{0.15}{0.15+0.32}=\boxed{\dfrac{15}{47}}\approx0.319 .
\]
(Out of $100$ typical years: $15$ have an earthquake and a tsunami, $32$ have a tsunami without an earthquake; $15$ of the $47$ tsunami years had an earthquake.)
