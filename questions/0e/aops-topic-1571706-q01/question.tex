In the town of Disasterland, the probability of an earthquake in any given year is $20\%$. If there is an earthquake in a year, the probability of a tsunami in the same year is $75\%$; if there is no earthquake, the probability of a tsunami that year is $40\%$. Given that there was a tsunami in $2019$, what is the probability that there was an earthquake in $2019$?
