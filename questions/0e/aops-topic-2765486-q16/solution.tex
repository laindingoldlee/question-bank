$2017=(11111100001)_2$ has 11 digits and seven 1 s. Multiplication by $2^k$ appends $k$ zeros without changing the seven 1 s. The total digit count is $\sum_{k=0}^{100}(11+k)=101(61)$, and the 1 count is $101(7)$. Thus probability $\boxed{\dfrac{7}{61}}$.
