The condition says that $X,Y$ have the same parity. Conditional on both being odd, they are independent uniform choices from $1,3,\ldots,2025$, each with mean $1013$. Conditional on both being even, they are independent uniform choices from $2,4,\ldots,2024$, again with mean $1013$. Thus in either case $\mathbb E[XY]=1013^2$, and unconditionally $\mathbb E[X]=\mathbb E[Y]=1013$. The covariance is zero. Both variances are positive, so the correlation is $\boxed0$.
