Choose the even number ($3$ ways) and the odd number ($3$ ways), and choose which two of the four dice show the even number ($\dbinom42=6$ ways). That gives $3\cdot3\cdot6=54$ of the $6^4=1296$ equally likely outcomes, so the probability is
\[
\dfrac{54}{1296}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{24}}.
\]
