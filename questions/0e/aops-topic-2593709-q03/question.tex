Define
\[P(x)=\left(x-1^2\right)\left(x-2^2\right)\cdots\left(x-100^2\right).\]
How many integers $n$ are there such that $P(n)\le0$?

$\textbf{(A) }4900\qquad\textbf{(B) }4950\qquad\textbf{(C) }5000\qquad\textbf{(D) }5050\qquad\textbf{(E) }5100$
