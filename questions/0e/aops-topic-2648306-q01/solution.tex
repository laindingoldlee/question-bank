A square of the form $8n+1$ is odd, say $(2k+1)^2=4k^2+4k+1$ with $k\ge1$. Then $8n=4k(k+1)$, i.e. $n=\dfrac{k(k+1)}{2}$, a triangular number; conversely $8\cdot\dfrac{k(k+1)}2+1=(2k+1)^2$. Since $\dfrac{63\cdot64}{2}=2016\le2021<2080=\dfrac{64\cdot65}{2}$, the answers are
\[\boxed{n=\dfrac{k(k+1)}{2}\text{ for }k=1,2,\dots,63}\quad(1,3,6,10,\dots,2016),\]
a total of $63$ values.
