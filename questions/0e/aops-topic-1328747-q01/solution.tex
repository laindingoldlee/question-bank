Write $a=\dfrac{kb}{c}$. From $10=\dfrac{6k}{3}$ we get $k=5$. Then $a=\dfrac{5\cdot4}{10}=\boxed{2}$.

(The guesses $\dfrac{20}{3}$ and $3$ come from changing only one of $b$ and $c$; both changes must be applied: $10\cdot\dfrac{4}{6}\cdot\dfrac{3}{10}=2$.)
