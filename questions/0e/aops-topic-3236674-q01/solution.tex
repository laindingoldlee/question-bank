Evaluate the inner expression first: $3^2-4=5$. Hence $5^2-5^2=\boxed0$. In general $(a-b)^2=a^2-2ab+b^2$, so squaring does not distribute over subtraction. In this case $(9-4)^2=81-72+16=25$, not $81-16$.
