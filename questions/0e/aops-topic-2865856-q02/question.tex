In a two-player game, each player independently receives an integer score chosen uniformly from $0$ to $10$, and the higher score wins; a tie leads to a rematch, repeated until someone wins. Calvin has rigged the game so that his score is chosen uniformly from $7,8,9,10$ instead, while Alex's score is still uniform from $0$ to $10$. What is the probability that Alex wins?
