\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] At 8:30 the Reindeer Rail train is $1.5\cdot40=60$ miles out. The Elf National train gains $80-40=40$ mph, so it catches up after $1.5$ hours, at $\boxed{\text{10:00 A.M.}}$ (both are $120$ miles out).
\item[(b)] At 10:00 the Reindeer Rail train is $120$ miles out and the Glacier train gains $80$ mph, so it catches up after $1.5$ hours, at $\boxed{\text{11:30 A.M.}}$
\item[(c)] At 10:00 the Elf National train is also $120$ miles out, and the Glacier train gains $40$ mph, so it catches up after $3$ hours, at $\boxed{\text{1:00 P.M.}}$
\end{enumerate}
