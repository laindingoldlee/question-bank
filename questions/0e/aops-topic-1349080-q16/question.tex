Three trains leave the same station along the same track in the same direction: the Reindeer Rail train at 7 A.M.\ at $40$ mph, the Elf National train at 8:30 A.M.\ at $80$ mph, and the Glacier Transportation train at 10 A.M.\ at $120$ mph. At what time does
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] the Elf National train catch up with the Reindeer Rail train;
\item[(b)] the Glacier Transportation train catch up with the Reindeer Rail train;
\item[(c)] the Glacier Transportation train catch up with the Elf National train?
\end{enumerate}
