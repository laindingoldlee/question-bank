$2^{n+1}-2^n=2^n(2-1)=2^n$. So the sequence of differences of $1,2,4,8,\dots$ is $1,2,4,\dots$, the same sequence. (More generally, the differences of $r^n$ are $(r-1)r^n$; $r=2$ is the only base for which they reproduce the sequence exactly.)
