If the second 264 were 364, the sum would be $\sum_{n=5}^{14}\dfrac{1}{2n(n+1)}=\dfrac{1}{2}(\dfrac{1}{5}-\dfrac{1}{15})=\dfrac{1}{15}$. Restoring the stated term gives $\dfrac{1}{15}+\dfrac{1}{264}-\dfrac{1}{364}=\dfrac{2711}{40040}$. Euclid's algorithm gives $\gcd(2711,40040)=1 $, so $\boxed{a+b=42751}$.
