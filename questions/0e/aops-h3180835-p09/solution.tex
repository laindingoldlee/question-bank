Use $A=(0,0)$, $B=(7,0)$, $C=(7,7)$, $D=(0,7)$. Then opposite vertices of the first inner square are $E=(3,0)$ and $G=(4,7)$, giving centre $(\dfrac{7}{2},\dfrac{7}{2})$ and other vertices $(7,3),(0,4)$. Its side length is $5$ and a unit edge direction is $(\dfrac{4}{5},\dfrac{3}{5})$. The axis-aligned inscribed square shares the same centre by symmetry of opposite supporting lines. If its side is $t$, the projection of a corner displacement onto that unit direction is $\dfrac{4}{5}\cdot\dfrac{t}{2}+\dfrac{3}{5}\cdot\dfrac{t}{2}=\dfrac{7t}{10}$. Tangency to the inner square\textquotesingle s sides requires this to equal $\dfrac{5}{2}$. Hence $\boxed{t=\dfrac{25}{7}}$.
