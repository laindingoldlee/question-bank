The point $(x,y)$ satisfies the equation exactly when $(\lfloor x\rfloor,\lfloor y\rfloor)=(m,n)$ with $mn=2021$, that is, when it lies in the unit square $[m,m+1)\times[n,n+1)$. Since $2021=43\cdot47$, the integer pairs with $mn=2021$ are
\[(1,2021),\ (2021,1),\ (43,47),\ (47,43)\]
and their negatives, $8$ pairs in all. The solution set is $8$ disjoint unit squares, of total area $\boxed{8}$.
