Ports $D$ and $E$ are $90$ miles apart on a river. A yacht needs $6$ hours to go from one port to the other downstream and $10$ hours to go upstream. A boat needs $5$ hours to go downstream between the ports. Assuming the current is the same for both and each vessel has a constant speed in still water, how long does the boat take to go upstream?
