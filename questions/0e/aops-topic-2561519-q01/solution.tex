Since $\tan A=\dfrac45$, we may take the leg opposite $A$ to be $4$ and the adjacent leg $5$. Then $A=\arctan0.8\approx38.66^\circ$. To confirm the rounding: $\tan$ is increasing on acute angles, and $\tan38.65^\circ\approx0.7999<0.8<0.8027\approx\tan38.75^\circ$, so $38.65^\circ<A<38.75^\circ$ and $A\approx\boxed{38.7^\circ}$.
