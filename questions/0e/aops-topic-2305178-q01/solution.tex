Split by who among Harold and Julian is on the committee; the other $4$ people have no restrictions.
\begin{itemize}
\item Neither: $4\cdot3\cdot2=24$ ways.
\item Harold but not Julian: Harold is vice-president or vice-vice-president ($2$ ways), and the other two posts are filled from the $4$ others in $4\cdot3=12$ ways: $24$ ways.
\item Julian but not Harold: Julian is president or vice-vice-president ($2$ ways), and $4\cdot3=12$ ways for the others: $24$ ways.
\end{itemize}
The total is $24+24+24=\boxed{72}$.
