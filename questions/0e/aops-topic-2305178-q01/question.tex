A math club of $6$ people, including Harold and Julian, chooses a president, a vice-president and a vice-vice-president (three different people). Harold and Julian refuse to serve on the committee together. Moreover, Harold refuses to be president and Julian refuses to be vice-president. In how many ways can the three officers be chosen?
