A hexagon inscribed in a circle has three consecutive sides of length $3$ and three consecutive sides of length $5$. The chord that divides the hexagon into two trapezoids, one with three sides of length $3$ and the other with three sides of length $5$, has length $\dfrac mn$ with $m,n$ coprime positive integers. Find $m+n$.
