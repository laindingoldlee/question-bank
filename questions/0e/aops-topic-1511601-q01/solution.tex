\textbf{Perimeter.} By the distance formula,
\[
AB=\sqrt{7^2+2^2}=\sqrt{53},\quad BC=\sqrt{2^2+9^2}=\sqrt{85},\quad CD=\sqrt{6^2+1^2}=\sqrt{37},\quad DA=\sqrt{1^2+8^2}=\sqrt{65}.
\]
So the perimeter is $\boxed{\sqrt{53}+\sqrt{85}+\sqrt{37}+\sqrt{65}}\approx30.65$.

\textbf{Area.} By the shoelace formula, with the vertices in the order $A,B,C,D$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
2K&=\bigl|(-3)(6)-4\cdot4+4(-3)-2\cdot6+2(-4)-(-4)(-3)+(-4)(4)-(-3)(-4)\bigr|\\
&=\bigl|-34-24-20-28\bigr|=106,
\end{aligned}
\]
so the area is $K=\boxed{53}$.
