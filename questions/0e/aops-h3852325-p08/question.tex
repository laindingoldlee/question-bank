The examination marks $(x,y)$ of $16$ students have
\[
\bar x=35.75,\quad\bar y=25.75,\quad\sigma_x=7.79,\quad\sigma_y=11.91,\quad\sum xy=15837.
\]
\begin{enumerate}
\item Compare the average and spread of the two papers.
\item The point $(38,0)$ is being investigated as a possible outlier. Suggest a reason it may warrant investigation and one possible explanation; state what further evidence would be needed.
\item If this point is omitted provisionally, find the means for the other $15$ students.
\item Explain why $\sum xy$ is unchanged and show that the new $S_{xy}=1169.8$.
\item Given $S_{xx}=965.6$ and $S_{yy}=1561.7$ for these $15$ students, calculate the correlation coefficient and the regression line of $y$ on $x$.
\item The correlation for all $16$ was $0.746$. Does an increase after omission alone justify permanently deleting the point?
\item Use the fitted line to estimate $y$ for $x=38$.
\end{enumerate}
