Circle $\omega_A$ has centre $A$ and circle $\omega_B$ has centre $B$; each circle passes through the centre of the other, and $AB$ is horizontal with $A$ to the left of $B$. Let $C$ be the lower intersection point of the two circles. The line $L$ through $C$ parallel to $AB$ meets $\omega_A$ again at $D$ and $\omega_B$ again at $E$. Consider the diagonal of trapezoid $ABED$ that has positive slope and the leg of trapezoid $ABED$ that has positive slope. The sum of the slopes of these two segments can be written as $\dfrac{a\sqrt b}{c}$, where $a,c$ are relatively prime positive integers and $b$ is squarefree. Find $abc$.
