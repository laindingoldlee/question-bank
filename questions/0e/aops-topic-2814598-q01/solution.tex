A $6$-digit palindrome is $\overline{abccba}=100001a+10010b+1100c=11(9091a+910b+100c)$, so it is always divisible by $11$. It is divisible by $5$ iff its last digit $a$ is $0$ or $5$; since $a$ is also the first digit, $a=5$. Then $b,c$ are arbitrary: $\boxed{100}$ palindromes.
