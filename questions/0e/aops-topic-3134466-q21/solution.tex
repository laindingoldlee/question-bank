The four required side-midpoint vertices are pairwise at least distance 2 apart, so a route needs at least 8 edges. Such routes exist around the outside. Every 8-edge route visits these four vertices in a cyclic order with four length 2 legs. There are two orders going around the square; each has four adjacent-endpoint legs, each with two choices (via center or via a corner). Excluding the all-center choice gives $2(2^4-1)=30$. The other four orders have two opposite-endpoint legs (forced through center) and two adjacent legs, giving $4(2^2-1)=12$. These are disjoint routes distinguished by visit order and leg choices. Total $\boxed{42}$.
