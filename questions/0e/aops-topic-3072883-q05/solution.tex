Choose the repeated digit in $10$ ways. If it occurs exactly $k$ times, choose its positions in $\binom5k$ ways and fill the other positions with any of the other nine digits. No sequence has two different digits each occurring three times. Hence the disjoint count is $10\sum_{k=3}^5\binom5k9^{5-k}=10(810+45+1)=\boxed{8560}$.
