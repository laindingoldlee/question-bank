The extra three-fifths of a jar of water weighs $180$ grams, so a full jar's water alone weighs $180\div\dfrac{3}{5}=300$ grams. One-fifth weighs $60$ grams, leaving empty-jar mass $560-60=\boxed{500\text{ g}}$.
