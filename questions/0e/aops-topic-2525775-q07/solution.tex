There are $\dbinom83=56$ triangles in all. Subtract the bad ones:
\begin{itemize}
\item three consecutive points: $8$ triangles;
\item exactly one pair of adjacent vertices: choose the adjacent pair ($8$ ways), then the third vertex among the $8-4=4$ points that are not in the pair or next to it: $32$ triangles.
\end{itemize}
So $56-8-32=\boxed{16}$ triangles have no two adjacent vertices.
