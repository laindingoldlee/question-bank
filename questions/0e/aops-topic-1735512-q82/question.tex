Circles $A$ and $B$ (centred at points $A$ and $B$) intersect, and points $E$ and $F$ lie on circle $B$ so that quadrilateral $AEBF$ is a square. What is the probability that a randomly chosen line through point $A$ intersects both circles? Express your answer as a common fraction.
