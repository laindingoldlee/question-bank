Multiplying by the denominators gives $mn+2=9n=m$. Hence $m=9n$ and $9n^2-9n+2=0$, whose roots are $n=\dfrac{1}{3},\dfrac{2}{3}$. The corresponding $m$ values are 3, 6. The requested sum is $3+6+\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{2}{3}=\boxed{10}$.
