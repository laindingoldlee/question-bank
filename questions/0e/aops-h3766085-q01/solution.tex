If there are $N$ pages and sheet $(2k-1,2k)$ is removed, then $\dfrac{N(N+1)}{2}-(4k-1)=2025$, so $\dfrac{N(N+1)}{2}=2024+4k$. The removed sum is between $3$ and $2N-1$, which implies $N\ge64$ and $N\le65$ by testing the increasing triangular totals at $63,64,65,66$. Since $N$ is even, $N=64$. Then $2080-2025=55=27+28$. Thus $\boxed{64\text{ pages, with }27,28\text{ removed}}$.
