By the distributive law,
\[
(a+b)(a-b)=a^2-ab+ab-b^2=a^2-b^2 .
\]
This is the \emph{difference of squares} formula, $a^2-b^2=(a+b)(a-b)$. With $a=100$ and $b=3$,
\[
103\cdot97=(100+3)(100-3)=100^2-3^2=\boxed{9991}.
\]
