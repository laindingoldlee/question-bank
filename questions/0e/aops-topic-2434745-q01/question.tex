$ABCD$ is a cyclic quadrilateral (its vertices lie on a circle in this order). Let $x$, $y$, $z$ be the distances from $A$ to the lines $BD$, $BC$, $CD$ respectively. Prove that
\[\dfrac{BD}{x}=\dfrac{BC}{y}+\dfrac{CD}{z}.\]
