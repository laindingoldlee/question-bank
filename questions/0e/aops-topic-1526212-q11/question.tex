Colleen rolls a standard fair six-sided die. If she rolls a number with an odd number of positive integer divisors, she steps $1$ metre to her right; otherwise she steps $1$ metre to her left. After four rolls of the die, what is the probability that Colleen ends up right where she started? Express your answer as a common fraction.
