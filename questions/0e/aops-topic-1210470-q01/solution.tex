All $4!=24$ placements are equally likely. Exactly two wrong means two CDs are in their own cases and the other two are swapped. Choose the two misplaced CDs in $\dbinom42=6$ ways; they must be exchanged (one way). So the probability is $\dfrac{6}{24}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{4}}$.
