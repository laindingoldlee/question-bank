Count according to the unique run $99$ (runs $999$ or longer are forbidden). From $2025$ to $9999$, its starting positions give respectively $90,63,72$ numbers. From $10000$ to $19999$, the three possible starts after the leading $1$ give $90,81,90$ numbers: any digit immediately beside the run must differ from $9$. From $20000$ to $20250$, only $20099,20199$ work. These cases are disjoint, so the total is $90+63+72+90+81+90+2=\boxed{488}$.
