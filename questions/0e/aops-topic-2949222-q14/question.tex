An equilateral triangular prism has height 2 and contains four unit-radius balls. Their centres all lie in the mid-height plane: three are placed symmetrically, each tangent to the two vertical faces near a different base vertex, and the fourth centre is the triangle centre. Find the least possible prism volume under this arrangement.
