Pair the outer and inner factors: $x(x+3)=x^2+3x$ and $(x+1)(x+2)=x^2+3x+2$. With $u=x^2+3x$ the equation is $u(u+2)=24$, i.e. $u^2+2u-24=0=(u+6)(u-4)$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $u=4$: $x^2+3x-4=0$, so $x=1$ or $x=-4$.
\item $u=-6$: $x^2+3x+6=0$, so $x=\dfrac{-3\pm i\sqrt{15}}{2}$ (not real).
\end{itemize}
The real solutions are $\boxed{x=1\text{ and }x=-4}$ (with the two complex solutions above).
