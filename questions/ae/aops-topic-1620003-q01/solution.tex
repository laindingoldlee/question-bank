The units digit of a power of $107$ depends only on the units digit $7$. The units digits of $7^1,7^2,7^3,7^4,7^5,\dots$ are $7,9,3,1,7,\dots$, repeating with period $4$. So we need the exponent $107^2$ modulo $4$. Since $107\equiv3\equiv-1\pmod4$, $107^2\equiv1\pmod4$. Hence the units digit is the same as that of $7^1$, namely $\boxed{7}$.
