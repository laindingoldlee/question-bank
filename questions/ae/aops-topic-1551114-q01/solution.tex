The $k$-th triangular number is $1+2+\cdots+k=\dfrac{k(k+1)}{2}$, so the terms are, for $k=2,\dots,2017$,
\[
\dfrac{2}{k(k+1)}=2\left(\dfrac{1}{k}-\dfrac{1}{k+1}\right).
\]
The sum telescopes:
\[
\sum_{k=2}^{2017}2\left(\dfrac{1}{k}-\dfrac{1}{k+1}\right)=2\left(\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{2018}\right)=1-\dfrac{1}{1009}=\boxed{\dfrac{1008}{1009}}.
\]
