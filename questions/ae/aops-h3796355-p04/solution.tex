The possible integer $x$ values are $2$ through $10$. For each, $\left\lceil\dfrac{2x}{3}\right\rceil\le y\le2x$, giving $2x-\left\lceil\dfrac{2x}{3}\right\rceil+1$ points. For $x=2,\ldots,10$ these counts are $3,5,6,7,9,10,11,13,14$. Their sum is $\boxed{78}$.
