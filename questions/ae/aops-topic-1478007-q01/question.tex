Find an increasing sequence of positive integers $a_1<a_2<a_3<\cdots$ such that
\[
\dfrac{4}{7}=\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{a_1}+\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{a_2}+\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{a_3}+\cdots,
\]
and prove that it is the only such sequence (finite or infinite).
