A regular hexagon is inscribed in a circle of radius 1, which is inscribed in the square $[-1,1]^2$. A larger regular hexagon centred at the origin has vertices $(R,0),(\dfrac{R}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt3R}{2}),(-\dfrac{R}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt3R}{2}),(-R,0),(-\dfrac{R}{2},-\dfrac{\sqrt3R}{2}),(\dfrac{R}{2},-\dfrac{\sqrt3R}{2})$; the four square vertices lie on its sloping edges. Find the smaller-to-larger hexagon area ratio.
