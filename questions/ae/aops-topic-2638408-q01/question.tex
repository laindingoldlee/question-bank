Let $a_1,a_2,\dots,a_k$ be any finite sequence of real numbers. Prove that there are infinitely many polynomials $P$ with $P(n)=a_n$ for $n=1,2,\dots,k$, and that any two of them take different values at $n=k+1$ (so they are genuinely different ``rules'' for continuing the sequence).
