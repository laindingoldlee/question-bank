Let $CA=u,CB=v$ and $\angle ACB=\theta$. Because $P$ is the midpoint of $ED$, its coordinates in the basis of unit vectors along the rays $CE,CD$ are $(\dfrac{7}{2} , \dfrac{7}{2})$. Since it lies on intercept line $AB$, $\dfrac{7}{2u}+\dfrac{7}{2v}=1$, giving $u+v=\dfrac{2uv}{7}$. The area condition gives $uv\sin\theta=70$. Hence $h_a+h_b=(u+v)\sin\theta=\dfrac{2}{7}\cdot70=\boxed{20}$.
