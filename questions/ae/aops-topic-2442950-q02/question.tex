A sequence satisfies $f(1)=f(2)=1$ and
\[f(n)=f(n-1)+f(n-2)+2n\qquad\text{for }n\ge3.\]
Express $f(2021)$ in terms of Fibonacci numbers $F_k$ (with $F_1=F_2=1$ and $F_{k}=F_{k-1}+F_{k-2}$).
