A number $m\ge2$ can be split into at least two equal groups of at least two people exactly when $m$ has a divisor $d$ with $2\le d\le\dfrac {m}{2}$, that is, exactly when $m$ is composite. So the number $n$ of students and the number $n+2$ (with the two teachers) must both be prime: $n$ and $n+2$ are twin primes.

The primes from $30$ to $50$ are $31,37,41,43,47$, and $n+2$ must also be prime:
\[
31+2=33=3\cdot11,\quad 37+2=39=3\cdot13,\quad 41+2=43\ \text{prime},\quad 43+2=45,\quad 47+2=49=7^2 .
\]
So $\boxed{41}$ students came.
