Wilma and Betty run an archery class that usually has between $30$ and $50$ students, whom they split into equal groups (at least two groups, each with at least two students). Today the students who came could not be split into such equal groups, and they still could not be split into equal groups when Wilma and Betty joined in as two more archers. How many students came today, if the number was between $30$ and $50$?
