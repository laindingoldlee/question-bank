By Vieta's formulas for $cx^2+ax+b$ (leading coefficient $c$, linear coefficient $a$, constant $b$), the sum of the roots is $\boxed{-\dfrac ac}$ and the product is $\boxed{\dfrac bc}$. (The trick is that the letters are not in the usual positions.)
