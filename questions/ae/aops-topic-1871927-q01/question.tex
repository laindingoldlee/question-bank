The centre of the cue ball on a rectangular pool table is directly above point $A$ on the table. I wish to bounce the cue ball off a straight rail so that after the bounce, the centre of the ball passes directly over point $B$ on the table. The radius of the cue ball is $1$ inch, $A$ is $6$ inches from the rail, $B$ is $9$ inches from the rail, and $A$ is $5$ inches from $B$. How far from the point of the rail nearest to $B$ should the ball hit the rail? (Assume the ball rebounds like a light ray: angle of incidence equals angle of reflection.)
