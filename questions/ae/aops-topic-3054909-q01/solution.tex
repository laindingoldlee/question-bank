For digits $ a,b,c,d $, put $ u=a-1 $. Then $ u+b+c+d=22 $, with $ 0\le u\le8 $ and $ 0\le b,c,d\le9 $. Unrestricted stars and bars gives $\binom{25}{3}$. Subtract $\binom{16}{3}$ for $ u\ge9 $ and $ 3\binom{15}{3}$ for another digit at least 10. Add back $ 3\binom63 $ for an intersection of the first type with one other, and $ 3\binom53 $ for two other digits. Three simultaneous violations are impossible. Thus the count is $ 2300-560-1365+60+30=\boxed{465}$.
