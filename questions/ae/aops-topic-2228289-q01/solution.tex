Write $z=a+bi$ with $a,b$ real. Then $z^2=a^2-b^2+2abi$, so
\[a^2-b^2=10,\qquad ab=-2\sqrt6 .\]
Then $b=-\dfrac{2\sqrt6}{a}$ and $a^2-\dfrac{24}{a^2}=10$, i.e. $a^4-10a^2-24=0$, $(a^2-12)(a^2+2)=0$. So $a^2=12$, $a=\pm2\sqrt3$ and $b=\mp\sqrt2$:
\[z=\boxed{\pm\left(2\sqrt3-i\sqrt2\right)}.\]
(Check: $(2\sqrt3-i\sqrt2)^2=12-2-2\cdot2\sqrt3\cdot\sqrt2\,i=10-4\sqrt6\,i$.) The principal square root, with positive real part, is $2\sqrt3-i\sqrt2$.
