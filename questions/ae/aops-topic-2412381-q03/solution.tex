The $30\%$ of participants who do not cheat are $60$ people, each rolling the weighted die. For one of them, the probability that the vote equals the organiser's roll is
\[\sum_{k=1}^6p_k^2=0.3^2+0.2^2+0.4^2+0.05^2+0.02^2+0.03^2=0.09+0.04+0.16+0.0025+0.0004+0.0009=0.2938.\]
By linearity of expectation (the wins of different participants are not independent, as they all depend on the same roll, but expectation still adds), the expected number of honest winners is
\[60\cdot0.2938=\boxed{17.628}.\]
