A troublemaker runs an online poll. He has a weighted $6$-sided die that shows $1,2,3,4,5,6$ with probabilities $30\%$, $20\%$, $40\%$, $5\%$, $2\%$ and $3\%$, respectively. Each of $200$ participants is told to roll a die of exactly this kind and vote for the number rolled; then he rolls his own die once, and a participant wins if his or her vote equals his roll. However, $70\%$ of the participants cheat by rolling an ordinary fair die instead, and they are disqualified. All rolls are independent. What is the expected number of participants who win and are not disqualified?
