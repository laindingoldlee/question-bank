The probability is the ratio of the areas, so
\[\dfrac{\pi r^2}{25}=\dfrac12,\qquad r^2=\dfrac{25}{2\pi},\qquad r=\dfrac{5}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\approx1.995.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw(box((0,0),(5,5)),linewidth(1));
real r=5/sqrt(2*pi);
filldraw(circle((2.5,2.5),r),paleblue,blue);
draw((2.5,2.5)--(2.5,2.5)+r*dir(20),blue); dot((2.5,2.5),blue);
label("$r$",(2.5,2.5)+0.5*r*dir(20),N,blue);
label("$5$",(2.5,0),S); label("$5$",(5,2.5),E);
\end{asy}

(The circle fits: $r<2.5$.) Since $1.5^2=2.25<\dfrac{25}{2\pi}\approx3.98<6.25=2.5^2$, the radius lies between $1.5$ and $2.5$, so rounded to the nearest integer it is $\boxed{2}$.
