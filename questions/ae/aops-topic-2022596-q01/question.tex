Two distinct lines $\ell_1$ and $\ell_2$, neither horizontal nor vertical, meet at a point $P$. A line of slope $1$ through $P$ bisects one of the angles formed by $\ell_1$ and $\ell_2$. Prove that the product of the slopes of $\ell_1$ and $\ell_2$ is $1$. What happens instead if $\ell_1$ is horizontal?
