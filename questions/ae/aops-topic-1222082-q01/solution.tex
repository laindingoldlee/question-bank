Let the square have side $1$, with $A=(0,0)$, $B=(1,0)$, $C=(1,1)$, $D=(0,1)$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(1,0),C=(1,1),D=(0,1); real e=2-sqrt(3);
pair E=(0,e),F=(1-e,1);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(1)); draw(B--E--F--cycle,blue);
fill(D--E--F--cycle,lightred); fill(A--B--E--cycle,lightgreen); draw(B--E--F--cycle,blue); draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(1));
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$E$",E,W);label("$F$",F,N);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Since $BE=BF$, the right triangles $ABE$ and $CBF$ are congruent ($AB=CB$), so $AE=CF=e$ and $DE=DF=1-e$. The condition $BE=EF$ gives
\[1+e^2=2(1-e)^2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad e^2-4e+1=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad e=2-\sqrt3.\]
Then
\[\dfrac{[DEF]}{[ABE]}=\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{2}(1-e)^2}{\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot1\cdot e}=\dfrac{\left(\sqrt3-1\right)^2}{2-\sqrt3}=\dfrac{4-2\sqrt3}{2-\sqrt3}=\boxed{2}.\]
