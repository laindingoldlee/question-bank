A data set has $n$ values listed in increasing order. Explain why the median is the value in position $\dfrac{n+1}{2}$ when $n$ is odd, and the mean of the values in positions $\dfrac {n}{2}$ and $\dfrac {n}{2}+1$ when $n$ is even. Then find the median of
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $3,9,4,12,7,15,1$;
\item[(b)] $3,9,4,12,7,15,1,10$.
\end{enumerate}
