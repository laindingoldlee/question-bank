Modulo $n+1$, substitute $n\equiv-1$ to get $n^2-n-2019\equiv-2017$. Hence the gcd equals $\gcd(2017,n+1)$. The number $2017$ is prime (trial division by primes at most $43$ suffices), so the only possible gcds are $1,2017$. Both occur, for example at $n=1$ and $n=2016$. Their sum is $\boxed{2018}$.
