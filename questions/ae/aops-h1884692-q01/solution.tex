Let the side length be $s$. Then $AC=s\sqrt2$. The angle bisector theorem in triangle $ABC$ gives
\[
\dfrac{BM}{MC}=\dfrac{AB}{AC}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}.
\]
Since $BM+MC=s$, we obtain $BM=\dfrac{s}{1+\sqrt2}=s(\sqrt2-1)$. Therefore $BC+BM=s+s(\sqrt2-1)=s\sqrt2=AC$.
