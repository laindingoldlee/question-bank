A glip is worth $a$ points and a glop is worth $b$ points, where $a$ and $b$ are positive integers.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that if $\gcd(a,b)=1$, then the greatest total that cannot be scored (as $ax+by$ with nonnegative integers $x,y$) is $ab-a-b$.
\item[(b)] What happens if $\gcd(a,b)>1$?
\end{enumerate}
