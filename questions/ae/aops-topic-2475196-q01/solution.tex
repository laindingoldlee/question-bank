\textbf{(a)} $37\times3=111$, so $37\times3k=111k=100k+10k+k=\overline{kkk}$ for every digit $k$.

\textbf{(b)} $1332=36\times37$ and $333=9\times37$, so
\[1332+333=45\times37=1665,\qquad 1665\div3=15\times37=37\times(3\cdot5),\]
which is $\boxed{555}$ by (a). In general, for positive integers $m,n$ with $m+n=9k$ and $1\le k\le9$, we get $\dfrac{37m+37n}{3}=37\times3k=\overline{kkk}$; here $m+n=36+9=45=9\cdot5$.
