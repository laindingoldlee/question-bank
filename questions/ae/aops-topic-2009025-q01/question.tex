You meet a stranger who says: ``I have three children, and at least one of them is a boy.'' Assume that each child is independently a boy or a girl with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$ each, and that the stranger is a uniformly random parent of three children among those who have at least one boy. What is the probability that all three children are boys?
