Let $s=x+y+z$. Since $29s\le29x+30y+31z\le31s$, with equality in either place only if two of the variables are $0$ (impossible here),
\[
29s<366<31s\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad \dfrac{366}{31}<s<\dfrac{366}{29},\quad\text{i.e.}\quad 11.8<s<12.7 .
\]
So $s=12$. Such numbers exist: from $29x+30y+31z=30s+(z-x)$ we need $z-x=6$ and $y=12-x-z=6-2x\ge1$, giving
\[
(x,y,z)=(1,4,7)\quad\text{or}\quad(2,2,8)
\]
(the first is the calendar of a leap year: one month of $29$ days, four of $30$, seven of $31$). In every case $x+y+z=\boxed{12}$.
