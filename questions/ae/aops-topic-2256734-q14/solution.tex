Let the cost be $C>0$. Then $\dfrac{C}{54+k}=0.9\cdot\dfrac{C}{54}$, so $54+k=60$ and $k=6$. With $48$ students the share is $\dfrac{C}{48}=\dfrac{54}{48}\cdot\dfrac{C}{54}=1.125\cdot\dfrac C{54}$, an increase of $h=12.5$ percent. So $h+k=\boxed{\textbf{(B) }18.5}$.
