$54$ students plan to split the cost of a field trip equally. If another $k$ students join and the cost is split evenly again, each student's share is $10\%$ less. If instead $k$ students are removed from the original $54$, each student's share increases by $h\%$. If the cost is positive, what is $h+k$?

$\textbf{(A) }16\qquad\textbf{(B) }18.5\qquad\textbf{(C) }21.5\qquad\textbf{(D) }26\qquad\textbf{(E) }29$
