Put the incenter at the origin and let the equal vertex vectors be $v_i$, with $|v_i|^2=R^2$ and $v_i\cdot v_j=-\dfrac{R^2}{3}$ for i not j. A point with barycentric coordinates $\lambda_i$ is closer to the centre than to vertex i exactly when $x\cdot v_i\le \dfrac{R^2}{2}$, or $\lambda_i\le\dfrac{5}{8}$. Thus remove each corner tetrahedron where $\lambda_i>\dfrac{5}{8}$. Each is similar to the original with linear scale $\dfrac{3}{8}$, and the four removed pieces are disjoint since two barycentric coordinates cannot both exceed $\dfrac{5}{8}$. The retained volume is $1024[1-4(\dfrac{3}{8})^3]=\boxed{808}$.
\begin{asy}
import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
pair[] V={(0,3),(-3,-1),(3,-1),(1,0)};for(int i=0;i<4;++i)for(int j=i+1;j<4;++j)draw(V[i]--V[j],gray);for(int i=0;i<4;++i){pair[] W;for(int j=0;j<4;++j)if(j!=i)W.push(V[i]+3/8*(V[j]-V[i]));draw(W[0]--W[1]--W[2]--cycle,blue);}label("four corner cuts, each scale $\dfrac{3}{8}$",(0,-2));
\end{asy}
