Square $ABCD$ has side length $10\sqrt{15}$. Point $E$ lies on side $BC$ with $\dfrac{BE}{EC}=\dfrac12$, point $F$ lies on side $CD$ with $\dfrac{CF}{FD}=\dfrac12$, point $G$ lies on side $DA$ with $\dfrac{DG}{GA}=\dfrac12$, and point $H$ lies on side $AB$ with $\dfrac{AH}{HB}=\dfrac12$. Find the area of the region bounded by the lines $AE$, $BF$, $CG$ and $DH$.
