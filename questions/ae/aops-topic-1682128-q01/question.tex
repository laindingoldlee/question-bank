Let $a$, $b$ and $x$ be points of the plane (viewed as vectors) with $a\ne b$, let $m=\dfrac{a+b}{2}$, and let $L$ be the perpendicular bisector of segment $ab$. Prove that the distance from $x$ to $L$ is
\[
d=\dfrac{\bigl|\,|x-a|^2-|x-b|^2\,\bigr|}{2|b-a|}=\dfrac{|(x-m)\cdot(b-a)|}{|b-a|}.
\]
