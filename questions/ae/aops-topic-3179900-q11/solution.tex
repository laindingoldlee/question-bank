Including leading-zero strings, there are 12 arrangements. At each place, the sum of appearing digits is $\dfrac{12(2+0+2+1)}{4}=15$, so the total is $15\cdot1111=16665$. The forbidden leading-zero strings contribute 221+212+122=555. Subtracting gives $\boxed{16110}$.
