Let $x=\overline{ab}$ be a two-digit positive integer (digits $a\ge1$ and $b$) and let $y$ be a one-digit positive integer such that $x\cdot y=\overline{cd}$ is also a two-digit number. Suppose that for every positive integer $n$,
\[\overline{a\underbrace{bb\cdots b}_{n}}\times y=\overline{\underbrace{cc\cdots c}_{n}\,d}.\]
For example, $(x,y)=(16,4)$ works, since $16\cdot4=64$, $166\cdot4=664$, $1666\cdot4=6664$, and so on. Find all pairs $(x,y)$ with this property.
