Let $A_1A_2\cdots A_{12}$ be a regular dodecagon. Three frogs start at $A_4$, $A_8$ and $A_{12}$. At the end of each minute, simultaneously, each frog jumps to one of the two vertices adjacent to its current position, chosen at random and independently, with equal probability. All jumping stops as soon as two frogs are at the same vertex at the same time. The expected number of minutes until the frogs stop is $\dfrac mn$ in lowest terms. Find $m+n$.
