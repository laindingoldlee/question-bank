First simplify the fourth roots of powers of $3$:
\[
\sqrt[4]{3^4}=3,\qquad \sqrt[4]{3^2}=3^{\dfrac{2}{4}}=3^{\dfrac{1}{2}}=\sqrt3 .
\]
So the expression is
\[
\dfrac{2\sqrt5\cdot\sqrt[4]{2}\cdot\sqrt3}{6\cdot3}=\dfrac{2\sqrt{15}\,\sqrt[4]{2}}{18}=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt{15}\,\sqrt[4]{2}}{9}} .
\]
(Equivalently $\dfrac{\sqrt[4]{450}}{9}$, since $\sqrt{15}\,\sqrt[4]{2}=\sqrt[4]{225\cdot2}$.)
