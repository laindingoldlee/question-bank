Ask: If I asked whether the left road leads to the village, would you answer yes? A truth-teller answers yes exactly when the left road is correct. A liar lies to the inner question and then lies about that answer, so the two reversals also produce yes exactly when the left road is correct. Take the left road after yes and the right road after no.
