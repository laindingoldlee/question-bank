An equilateral triangle of side $t$ has area $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}t^2=16\sqrt3$, so $t^2=64$ and $t=8$. Its perimeter is $24$, so the square has side $6$, and its diagonal is $6\sqrt2$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real t=8, q=6;
pair A=(0,0), B=(t,0), C=(t/2,t*sqrt(3)/2);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
pair o=(11,0);
draw(o--o+(q,0)--o+(q,q)--o+(0,q)--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(o--o+(q,q),red+dashed);
label("$8$",(A+B)/2,S); label("$8$",(A+C)/2,NW); label("$8$",(B+C)/2,NE);
label("$6$",o+(q/2,0),S); label("$6$",o+(q,q/2),E);
label("$6\sqrt{2}$",o+(q/2,q/2),NW,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The diagonal is $\boxed{6\sqrt2}$ cm.
