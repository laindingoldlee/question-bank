The substitution axiom says equal inputs may be replaced within any function. Apply it to $ F(t)=t+c $: $ a=b $ implies $ F(a)=F(b)$, which is $ a+c=b+c $. Transitivity instead combines $ u=v $ and $ v=w $ to obtain $ u=w $; by itself it does not assert that an arbitrary operation preserves equality. The required operation-compatibility is supplied by substitution.
