A positive integer $M$ has $d$ digits exactly when $10^{d-1}\le M<10^d$, so $d=1+\lfloor\log_{10}M\rfloor$. Substituting $M=\dfrac{n(n-1)}{2}$ gives $\boxed{1+\left\lfloor\log_{10}\left(\dfrac{n(n-1)}{2}\right)\right\rfloor}$.
