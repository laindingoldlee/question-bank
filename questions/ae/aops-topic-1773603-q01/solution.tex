Within each column the order is forced (bottom to top), so an order of breaking is determined by the sequence of column choices: a word with three $A$'s, three $B$'s and two $C$'s, and every such word is possible. The number of words is
\[
\dfrac{8!}{3!\,3!\,2!}=\dfrac{40320}{72}=\boxed{560}.
\]
