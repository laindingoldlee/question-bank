\textbf{No.} Multiply by $8$ and complete the square:
\[
64a^2+16a+56=8k^2\iff (8a+1)^2+55=8k^2 .
\]
Let $u=8a+1$. Then $u^2=8k^2-55$, so modulo $5$,
\[
u^2\equiv 8k^2\equiv3k^2\pmod5 .
\]
The squares modulo $5$ are $0,1,4$. If $5\nmid k$, then $k^2\equiv1$ or $4$, and $3k^2\equiv3$ or $2$, which are not squares modulo $5$. So $5\mid k$, and then $u^2\equiv0$, so $5\mid u$ as well. But then $25$ divides $8k^2-u^2=55$, which is false.

Hence there are no integer solutions.
