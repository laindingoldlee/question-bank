There are $99999-10000+1=90000$ five-digit integers. Those ending in $5$ are $10005,10015,\dots,99995$: the first four digits can be anything from $1000$ to $9999$, giving $9000$ numbers. The probability is $\dfrac{9000}{90000}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{10}}$.
