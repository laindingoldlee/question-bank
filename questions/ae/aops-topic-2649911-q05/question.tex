A block of wood has the shape of a right circular cylinder with radius $6$ and height $8$, and its entire surface has been painted blue. Points $A$ and $B$ are chosen on the edge of one of the circular faces of the cylinder so that the arc $AB$ on that face measures $120^\circ$. The block is then sliced in half along the plane that passes through point $A$, point $B$, and the centre of the cylinder, revealing a flat, unpainted face on each half. The area of one of these unpainted faces is $a\pi+b\sqrt c$, where $a$, $b$ and $c$ are integers and $c$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. Find $a+b+c$.
