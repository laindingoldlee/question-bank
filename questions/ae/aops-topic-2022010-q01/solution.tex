Factor out $x$: $x\left(x^{2019}-x-3\right)=0$. So $x=0$ is a root.

Any other rational root is a rational root of the monic integer polynomial $x^{2019}-x-3$. By the rational root theorem it must be an integer dividing $3$, i.e. one of $\pm1,\pm3$. Check them:
\[
1-1-3=-3,\quad (-1)+1-3=-3,\quad 3^{2019}-3-3=3^{2019}-6\ne0,\quad -3^{2019}+3-3=-3^{2019}\ne0 .
\]
None is zero. Hence the only rational root is $\boxed{x=0}$.
