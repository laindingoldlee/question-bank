Count the complement. Write the numbers $0$ to $999$ with three digits (leading zeros allowed). Those with no digit $6$ number $9^3=729$, and this includes $0$; so among $1$ to $999$ there are $728$ numbers without a $6$, and $999-728=271$ with a $6$. The number $1000$ has no $6$.

So $\boxed{271}$ integers from $1$ to $1000$ contain a $6$ (the answer is the same whether or not the endpoints are included).
