Walking across a park at constant speed takes $60$ minutes. Think of the walk in stages: first half the distance, then half of the remaining distance, then half of what remains, and so on forever.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that the first $n$ stages take $60\left(1-\dfrac1{2^n}\right)$ minutes.
\item[(b)] There are infinitely many stages. Explain why the whole walk still takes a finite time, and find it.
\end{enumerate}
