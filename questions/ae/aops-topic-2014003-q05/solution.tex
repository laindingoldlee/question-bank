Multiplying $x+\dfrac{1}{x}=2$ by $x$ (note $x\ne0$) gives $x^2-2x+1=0$, that is $(x-1)^2=0$, so $x=1$ is the only solution, real or complex. Then
\[
x^{29292934}+\dfrac{1}{x^{29292934}}=1+1=\boxed{2}.
\]
