The parity of the sum is invariant, because replacing $a,b$ by $|a-b|$ changes the sum by twice their minimum. The initial sum $2015\cdot1008$ is even. No operation increases the largest number, so a final value must be one of $0,2,\ldots,2014$.

Every positive even $k\le2014$ is attainable. Put $v=k-1$, an odd number between $1$ and $2013$, and temporarily retain $v,2014,2015$. Pair consecutive numbers in $1,\ldots,v-1$ and $v+1,\ldots,2013$. This makes $1006$ copies of $1$, which can be paired to make zeroes and then combined into one zero. Now replace $2014,v$ by $2014-v$, and combine this with $2015$ to obtain $v+1=k$. Zeroes do not affect the result.

To obtain zero, reduce $1,2,3$ to zero via $3-2=1$ and $1-1=0$. Group the remaining $4,\ldots,2015$ into blocks of four consecutive numbers; each block reduces to zero by taking two adjacent differences of $1$ and subtracting them. Thus all candidate values occur, giving $\boxed{1008}$ possibilities.
