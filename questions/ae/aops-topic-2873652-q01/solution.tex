True. For $n=\prod p^{e}$ we have $\varphi(n)=n\prod_{p\mid n}\left(1-\dfrac1p\right)$, and for $k\ge1$ the primes dividing $12^k$ are exactly $2$ and $3$. So
\[\varphi(12^k)=12^k\left(1-\dfrac12\right)\left(1-\dfrac13\right)=\dfrac{12^k}{3}=4\cdot12^{k-1}=12^{k-1}\varphi(12),\]
since $\varphi(12)=4$. $\blacksquare$

(For $k=0$ the identity fails: $\varphi(1)=1\ne\dfrac4{12}$.)
