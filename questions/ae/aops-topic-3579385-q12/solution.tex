Define the degree-at-most-five polynomial $P(t)=a+b\binom{t+1}{1}+c\binom{t+2}{2}+d\binom{t+3}{3}+e\binom{t+4}{4}+f\binom{t+5}{5}$. The six equations specify $P(t)=2^t$ for $t=0,1,2,3,4,5$, so there is at most one such polynomial: a difference of two would have six roots but degree at most five. Its basis polynomials have distinct degrees and nonzero leading coefficients, so the coefficient sextuple is also unique. Direct substitution into the six equations verifies $(a,b,c,d,e,f)=(0,3,-6,7,-4,1)$, producing right sides $1,2,4,8,16,32$. Therefore the requested expression is $0+9-54+189-324+243=\boxed{63}$.
