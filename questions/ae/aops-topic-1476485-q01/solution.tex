Factor out $n-1$ and expand; the sum telescopes:
\[
(n-1)\left(n^{k-1}+n^{k-2}+\cdots+n+1\right)=\left(n^{k}+n^{k-1}+\cdots+n\right)-\left(n^{k-1}+\cdots+n+1\right)=n^k-1 .
\]
Every term except $n^k$ and $-1$ cancels. $\blacksquare$

This is the factorisation $x^k-1=(x-1)\left(x^{k-1}+\cdots+x+1\right)$, which is the same as the formula for the sum of a finite geometric series, $1+x+\cdots+x^{k-1}=\dfrac{x^k-1}{x-1}$.

In base $n$, the numeral with $k$ digits all equal to $n-1$ stands for
\[
(n-1)n^{k-1}+(n-1)n^{k-2}+\cdots+(n-1)n^0 ,
\]
which by the identity is $n^k-1$, that is, one less than $100\ldots0_n$ (a $1$ followed by $k$ zeros). For example $666_7=6\cdot49+6\cdot7+6=342=343-1=7^3-1$, just as $999_{10}=10^3-1$. A useful consequence in number theory is that $n-1$ divides $n^k-1$.
