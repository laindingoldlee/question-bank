In one revolution the tire rolls a distance equal to its circumference, $2\pi r\approx2\cdot\dfrac{22}{7}\cdot1=\dfrac{44}{7}$ feet. In one minute it makes
\[
4400\div\dfrac{44}{7}=4400\cdot\dfrac{7}{44}=\boxed{700}
\]
revolutions.
