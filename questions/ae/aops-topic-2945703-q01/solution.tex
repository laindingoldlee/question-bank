Measure the time $t$ in hours after 12:00 ($0\le t<12$) and positions as fractions of a full turn: hour $\dfrac t{12}$, minute $t$, second $60t$ (mod $1$).

Hour and minute coincide iff $t-\dfrac t{12}=\dfrac{11t}{12}$ is an integer, i.e. $t=\dfrac{12k}{11}$ for $k=0,1,\dots,10$. Then the hour hand is at $\dfrac k{11}$ and the second hand at $\dfrac{720k}{11}$. They coincide iff $\dfrac{719k}{11}$ is an integer. Since $719=11\cdot65+4$, this needs $11\mid4k$, i.e. $k=0$.

So the hands coincide only at $\boxed{12{:}00{:}00}$.
