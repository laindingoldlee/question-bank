The integer legs satisfy $ab=120=2^3\cdot3\cdot5$. There are $\dfrac{(3+1)(1+1)(1+1)}{2}=8$ unordered factor pairs. Distinct pairs give distinct hypotenuses: their squares are $a^2+b^2=(a+b)^2-240$, and a fixed sum and product determine the pair uniquely. Hence $\boxed8$ lengths.
