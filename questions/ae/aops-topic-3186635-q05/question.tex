At the North Pole, ice tends to grow in parallelogram structures of area $60$. On the other hand, at the South Pole, ice grows in right triangular structures, in which each triangular and parallelogram structure have the same area. If every ice triangle $ABC$ has legs $\overline{AB}$ and $\overline{AC}$ that are integer lengths, how many distinct possible lengths are there for the hypotenuse $\overline{BC}$?
