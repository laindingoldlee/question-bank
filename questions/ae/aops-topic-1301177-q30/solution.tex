Look for $(a+b\sqrt2)^2=a^2+2b^2+2ab\sqrt2=17+12\sqrt2$, i.e. $a^2+2b^2=17$ and $ab=6$. Then $a=3$, $b=2$ works. So $\sqrt{17+12\sqrt2}=\boxed{3+2\sqrt2}$ (positive, as it should be).
