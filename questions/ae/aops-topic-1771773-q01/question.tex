George stands on a number line labelled with the integers from $0$ to $5$. Whenever he is at $n$ (with $1\le n\le4$), he moves one unit to the right with probability $\dfrac{1}{n+1}$ and one unit to the left with probability $\dfrac{n}{n+1}$. If he reaches $5$ he wins, and if he reaches $0$ he loses. Given that he starts at $2$, what is the probability that he wins?
