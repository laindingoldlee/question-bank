Let $B$, $C$, $W$ be the three sets. A student outside $B\cap C\cap W$ misses at least one of the three properties. The numbers of students missing each property are $30$, $40$ and $25$, so at most $30+40+25=95$ students miss at least one. Hence at least $100-95=5$ students have all three.

Five is possible: let $30$ students lack only brown hair, $40$ lack only school colours, $25$ lack only walking, and $5$ have all three. Then $|B|=100-30=70$, $|C|=100-40=60$, $|W|=100-25=75$. The minimum is $\boxed{5}$.
