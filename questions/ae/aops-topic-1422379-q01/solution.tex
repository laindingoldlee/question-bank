\textbf{(a)} By the dot product,
\[
\cos\theta=\dfrac{\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf v}{|\mathbf u|\,|\mathbf v|}=\dfrac{1\cdot3+2\cdot5}{\sqrt5\cdot\sqrt{34}}=\dfrac{13}{\sqrt{170}}\approx0.997054 .
\]
A faster way: $\mathbf u$ and $\mathbf v$ make angles $\arctan2$ and $\arctan\dfrac{5}{3}$ with the $x$-axis, so
\[
\tan\theta=\tan\left(\arctan2-\arctan\dfrac{5}{3}\right)=\dfrac{2-\dfrac{5}{3}}{1+2\cdot\dfrac{5}{3}}=\dfrac{1}{13},
\]
and $\theta=\arctan\dfrac{1}{13}\approx4.3987^\circ$. Since $0.3987\cdot60\approx23.9$, $\theta\approx\boxed{4^\circ24'}$.

draw(O--U,blue+linewidth(0.9),Arrow(TeXHead)); draw(O--V,red+linewidth(0.9),Arrow(TeXHead));
label("$\mathbf{u}$",U,2*W,blue); label("$\mathbf{v}=(3,5)$",V,E,red);
draw(arc(O,3.6,a2,a1)); label("$\theta$",3.6*dir(a2),E);
draw(arc(O,0.7,a1,a2+360),dashed,Arrow(TeXHead)); label("$360^\circ-\theta$",0.75*dir(215),SW);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

\textbf{(b)} Turning from $\mathbf u$ to $\mathbf v$ the long way round gives
\[
360^\circ-\theta\approx360^\circ-4^\circ23.9'=\boxed{355^\circ36'} .
\]

(The answer choices in the original post, $184^\circ39'$, $94^\circ23'$, $85^\circ36'$, $175^\circ36'$ and $184^\circ24'$, contain neither of these values; they are $180^\circ\pm\theta$ and $90^\circ\pm\theta$ and similar, so they do not match the question as stated.)
