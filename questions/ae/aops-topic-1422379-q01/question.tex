Let $\mathbf{u}=(1,2)$ and $\mathbf{v}=(3,5)$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find, to the nearest minute, the angle $\theta$ between $\mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{v}$.
\item[(b)] Find, to the nearest minute, the larger angle with initial side $\mathbf{u}$ and terminal side $\mathbf{v}$, that is, the reflex angle $360^\circ-\theta$.
\end{enumerate}
