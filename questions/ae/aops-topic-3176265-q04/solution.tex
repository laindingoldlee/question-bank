The minute hand sweeps the upper semicircle of radius $10$, area $50\pi$. The hour hand sweeps $15^\circ$, from $7.5^\circ$ below the leftward horizontal to $7.5^\circ$ above it. Its upper half is already inside the minute-hand sweep. Only a $7.5^\circ$ sector of radius $6$ adds new area: $\dfrac{7.5}{360}\pi6^2=\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$. The union area is $\boxed{\dfrac{203\pi}{4}}$.

\end{asy}
