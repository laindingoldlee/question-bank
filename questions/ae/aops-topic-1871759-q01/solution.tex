Since $8\equiv-1\pmod9$, we have $8^k\equiv(-1)^k$, so
\[
17523_8=1\cdot8^4+7\cdot8^3+5\cdot8^2+2\cdot8+3\equiv1-7+5-2+3=0\pmod9 .
\]
The remainder is $\boxed{0}$. (Directly: $17523_8=4096+3584+320+16+3=8019=9\cdot891$.)
