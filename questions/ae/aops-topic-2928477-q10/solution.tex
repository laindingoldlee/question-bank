Let $S_k$ be the number of posts just after the cleanup at time $20k$, with $S_0=2$. During $(20(k-1),20k]$, the first account creates four posts and the second creates $\lfloor\dfrac{5k}{2}\rfloor-\lfloor\dfrac{5(k-1)}{2}\rfloor$. Thus\[S_k=\max\left(0,S_{k-1}+4+\left\lfloor\dfrac{5k}{2}\right\rfloor-\left\lfloor\dfrac{5(k-1)}{2}\right\rfloor-\left\lfloor\dfrac{k}{3}\right\rfloor-10\mathbf1_{3\mid k}\right).\]Here the indicator equals one when $3$ divides $k$, and zero otherwise. Iteration gives the following post-cleanup counts, grouped by minute, at that minute's $20$-, $40$-, and $60$-second marks:\[\begin{array}{c|rrr}\text{minute}&20&40&60\\\hline1&8&15&10\\2&16&21&16\\3&20&25&18\\4&22&25&18\\5&20&23&14\\6&16&17&8\\7&8&9&0\end{array}\]Every earlier cleanup leaves a positive count. At $420$ seconds, the pre-cleanup count is $15$; the moderator deletes $7$ and the administrator deletes the remaining $8$. Hence both accounts can first be banned at $\boxed{420\text{ seconds}}$.
