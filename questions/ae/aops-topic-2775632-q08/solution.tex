For $x\le4$, the median is 4, so $\dfrac{29+x}{5}=4$ gives $x=-9$. For $4\le x\le6$, the median is $x$, giving $x=\dfrac{29}{4}$, outside that interval. For $x\ge6$, the median is 6, giving $x=1$, outside that interval. Hence the only value is-9, and the answer is $\boxed9$.
