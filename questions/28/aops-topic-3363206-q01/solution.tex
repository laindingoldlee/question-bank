The possible dates are July 23--31, 2006; every July date in 2007--2011; and July 1--22, 2012. For a date July $d$ in year $y$, the age in days is $365(2024-y)+L_y+22-d$, where $L_y$ counts February 29 dates after that birthday and through July 22, 2024. These counts are $5$ for $2006,2007$, $4$ for $2008$--$2011$, and $3$ for $2012$. Modulo $365$, the contributions are $\sum_{d=23}^{31}(27-d)=0$, $\sum_{d=1}^{31}(27-d)=341$, four copies of $\sum_{d=1}^{31}(26-d)=310$, and $\sum_{d=1}^{22}(25-d)=297$. Their sum is $1878\equiv\boxed{53}\pmod{365}$.
