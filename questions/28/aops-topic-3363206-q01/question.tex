Chara was born in July in a year of the form $20cd$. On July 22, 2024, Chara is between $12$ and $17$ years old inclusive, where age in years means completed birthdays. Sum the ages in days for every possible birth date. Find the remainder of this sum modulo $365$. Use the Gregorian calendar and ordinary date differences.
