An equilateral triangle of side $s$ has area $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}s^2$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0),C=(2,0),A=(1,sqrt(3));
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
label("$s$",(A+B)/2,NW);label("$s$",(A+C)/2,NE);label("$s$",(B+C)/2,S);
label("area $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}s^2$",(1,0.55));
\end{asy}

So $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}s^2=s$ gives $s=\dfrac4{\sqrt3}=\dfrac{4\sqrt3}3$, and the perimeter is $3s=\boxed{4\sqrt3}$ cm.
