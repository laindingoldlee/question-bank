From $m=n+16$, $n(n+16)=36$, so $n^2+16n-36=(n-2)(n+18)=0$. As $n>0$, $n=2$ and $m=18$. The roots of $x^2+18x+2=0$ are
\[x=\dfrac{-18\pm\sqrt{324-8}}{2}=\boxed{-9\pm\sqrt{79}}.\]
