For the second claim, suppose $r_1,r_2,\ldots$ listed all reals in $(0,1)$. Choose each decimal expansion not to end in repeating $9$s. Construct $s=0.d_1d_2\ldots$ by setting $d_n=2$ if the $n$th digit of $r_n$ is $1$, and $d_n=1$ otherwise. Then $s\in(0,1)$, and its nonterminating expansion using only $1,2$ differs from $r_n$ at position $n$ for every $n$. It cannot have an alternative terminating expansion. Thus $s$ is missing from the list, a contradiction. Infinity alone does not imply equal cardinality.
