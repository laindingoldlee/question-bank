\textbf{(a)} If $a=0$ every term is $0$, and if $r=1$ every term is $a$; in both cases the sequence is constant. Conversely, if the sequence is constant then $a=ar$, so $a(1-r)=0$, i.e. $a=0$ or $r=1$.

\textbf{(b)} By (a), a non-constant geometric sequence has $r\ne1$. Multiply $S_n=a+ar+\cdots+ar^{n-1}$ by $r$:
\[rS_n=ar+ar^2+\cdots+ar^{n}.\]
Subtracting, all terms but two cancel: $rS_n-S_n=ar^n-a$, so $(r-1)S_n=a\left(r^n-1\right)$. Since $r-1\ne0$ we may divide, which gives the formula. $\blacksquare$

(For $r=1$ the formula would divide by zero; then simply $S_n=na$. This is why the formula is stated for non-constant sequences.)

\textbf{(c)} Here $a=3$ and $r=2$, so $S_{10}=\dfrac{3\left(2^{10}-1\right)}{2-1}=3\cdot1023=\boxed{3069}$.
