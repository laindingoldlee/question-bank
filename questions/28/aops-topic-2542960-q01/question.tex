A geometric sequence has first term $a$ and common ratio $r$, so its terms are $a,\ ar,\ ar^2,\ \dots$. It is called \emph{non-constant} if not all of its terms are equal.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that a geometric sequence is constant if and only if $a=0$ or $r=1$.
\item[(b)] Prove that for a non-constant geometric sequence the sum of the first $n$ terms is
\[S_n=a+ar+\cdots+ar^{n-1}=\dfrac{a\left(r^n-1\right)}{r-1}.\]
\item[(c)] Find the sum of the first $10$ terms of the non-constant geometric series $3+6+12+\cdots$.
\end{enumerate}
