Amelia and John have $64$ cards, numbered $1$ to $64$. The cards are shuffled, and then Amelia and John are each dealt seven cards. Let $p$ be the probability that the sum of the numbers in Amelia's hand is greater than the sum of the numbers in John's hand.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $p=\dfrac{1-q}{2}$, where $q$ is the probability that the two sums are equal.
\item[(b)] Deduce that $p<\dfrac{1}{2}$.
\end{enumerate}
