The cake is $30$ cubic hams, so $1$ cubic ham $=8$ cubic feet and $1$ ham $=\sqrt[3]8=\boxed{2}$ feet.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair x=(3,0),y=(1.2,0.9),z=(0,5);draw((0,0)--x--x+z--z--cycle);draw(z--z+y--x+z+y--x+z);draw(x--x+y--x+y+z);label("$3$",x/2,S);label("$5$",z/2,W);label("$2$",x+y/2,SE);
\end{asy}
