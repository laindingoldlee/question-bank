Let there be $x$ single-leg dragons, $y$ double-headed dragons, $z$ cats and $2y$ snakes. Counting heads and legs,
\[
x+2y+z+2y=58\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x+4y+z=58,
\]
\[
x+4y+3z+8y=160\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x+12y+3z=160 .
\]
Subtracting the first equation from the second gives $8y+2z=102$, so $4y+z=51$, and then
\[
x=58-(4y+z)=\boxed{7}.
\]
The numbers of the other animals are not determined, but such fields exist: for example $y=0$, $z=51$ (and no snakes) gives $7+51=58$ heads and $7+153=160$ legs.
