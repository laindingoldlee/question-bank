On a field there are four kinds of animals:
\begin{itemize}
\item single-leg dragons ($1$ leg, $1$ head);
\item double-headed dragons ($4$ legs, $2$ heads);
\item three-legged cats ($3$ legs, $1$ head);
\item four-legged snakes ($4$ legs, $1$ head).
\end{itemize}
There are twice as many four-legged snakes as double-headed dragons, and altogether there are $58$ heads and $160$ legs. How many single-leg dragons are there?
