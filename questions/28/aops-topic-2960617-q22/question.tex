A bus drives between consecutive stops in $2$ minutes and waits $1$ minute at each stop. Zia walks between consecutive stops in $5$ minutes. On reaching a stop, she waits if the bus is at the preceding stop or has already departed it; otherwise she immediately walks on. At time $0$, both depart toward the library, with the bus three stops behind Zia. At what time does she board?
