Number Zia's initial stop $0$. The bus departs stop $-3$ at time $0$, reaches stop $k$ at time $3k+8$, and leaves at time $3k+9$. Zia reaches stops $1,2,3$ at times $5,10,15$. At time $5$ the bus is at stop $-1$, so she walks on from stop $1$. At time $10$ it is traveling from stop $0$ to stop $1$, so she walks on from stop $2$. At time $15$ it is at stop $2$, the preceding stop, so she waits at stop $3$. The bus reaches stop $3$ at time $3(3)+8=\boxed{17}$ minutes.
