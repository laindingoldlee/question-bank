\textbf{Substitution.} Replacing $y$ by $4x$ gives $2x^2+16x^2=360$, so $18x^2=360$ and $x^2=20$.

\textbf{Solving for $x$ and $y$ first.} From $18x^2=360$ we get $x=\pm2\sqrt5$, and then $y=4x=\pm8\sqrt5$. Check: $2\cdot20+320=360$. Either solution gives $x^2=20$.

\textbf{Without finding $x$.} Squaring $y=4x$ gives $y^2=16x^2$, so $2x^2+y^2=18x^2$ directly.

In every case $\boxed{x^2=20}$.
