Let the numbers be $50+d$ and $50-d$; every pair of real numbers with sum $100$ has this form, with $d$ real. Their product is
\[(50+d)(50-d)=2500-d^2\le2500,\]
with equality exactly when $d=0$, i.e. when both numbers are $50$. So the maximum product is $\boxed{2500}$.

(Note that this argument works for all real numbers, including negative ones; the AM-GM inequality alone would only cover nonnegative numbers.)
