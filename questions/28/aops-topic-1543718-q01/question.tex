A bag contains $12$ letter tiles: P, H, Y, S, I, C, S, K, I, D, D, O. Seven tiles are drawn at random without replacement.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What is the probability that the seven tiles drawn are exactly the letters of the word PHYSICS (in any order)?
\item[(b)] If the tiles are drawn one at a time, what is the probability that, in the order drawn, they spell PHYSICS?
\end{enumerate}
