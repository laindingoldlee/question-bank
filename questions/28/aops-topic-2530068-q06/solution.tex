Write $f(x)=ax^2+bx+c$. Then $c=f(0)=0$, $a+b=42$ and $4a+2b=187$. Subtracting twice the second equation from the third gives $2a=103$, so $a=\dfrac{103}{2}$ and $b=-\dfrac{19}{2}$:
\[f(x)=\dfrac{103x^2-19x}{2}.\]
The equation $f(x)=665$ becomes $103x^2-19x-1330=0$. Its discriminant $19^2+4\cdot103\cdot1330$ is positive, so it has two real roots, and by Vieta's formulas their product is
\[\boxed{-\dfrac{1330}{103}}.\]
