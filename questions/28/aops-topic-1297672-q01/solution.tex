Married men and women are equally numerous: $\dfrac{3}{5}M=\dfrac{5}{7}W$, so $21M=25W$ and $M=25k$, $W=21k$ (then $\dfrac{3}{5}M=15k$ is a whole number). The population is $46k>1100$, so $k\ge24$, and the smallest population is $46\cdot24=\boxed{1104}$.
