Measure angles clockwise from $12$, in degrees. The minute hand moves $6^\circ$ per minute and the hour hand $\dfrac{1}{2}^\circ$ per minute. At $1{:}00$ the minute hand is at $0^\circ$ and the hour hand at $30^\circ$, so $m$ minutes later the minute hand is ahead of the hour hand by
\[6m-\left(30+\dfrac {m}{2}\right)=\dfrac{11m}{2}-30\ \text{degrees}.\]
The hands first point in opposite directions when this equals $180$:
\[\dfrac{11m}{2}=210\quad\Longrightarrow\quad m=\dfrac{420}{11}=38\dfrac{2}{11}.\]
Since $\dfrac{2}{11}$ of a minute is $\dfrac{120}{11}\approx10.9$ seconds, the time is
\[\boxed{38\dfrac{2}{11}\text{ minutes after }1{:}00}\approx1{:}38{:}11,\]
so the student's answer is correct to the nearest second.

(If ``in a straight line'' also allowed the hands to overlap, that would happen earlier, when $\dfrac{11m}{2}=30$, at $m=\dfrac{60}{11}=5\dfrac{5}{11}$, about $1{:}05{:}27$.)
