The raw-count ratio is $\dfrac{20}{10}=2$, while the reported scores are 110 and 120 with ratio $\dfrac{120}{110}=\dfrac{12}{11}$. Both scales give a difference of 10 because adding the same constant preserves differences. Ratios are not preserved by adding 100, so twice a reported score cannot be interpreted as twice the raw count. Neither comparison alone measures a ratio of underlying ability.
