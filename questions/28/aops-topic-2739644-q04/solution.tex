Compare with $(x^2+x+1)^3$. Since $(x^2+x+1)^2=x^4+2x^3+3x^2+2x+1$, multiplying by $x^2+x+1$ gives $x^6+3x^5+6x^4+7x^3+6x^2+3x+1$. So the polynomial is $\boxed{(x^2+x+1)^3}$, and $x^2+x+1$ has no real roots.
