The two roots imply $P(x)=k(x-1)(x-2)$ with $k\ne0$. Thus $P(3)=2k$, $P(4)=6k$, and $P(5)=12k$. The relation gives $4k^2=18k$, so $k=\dfrac{9}{2}$. Therefore $P(6)=20k=\boxed{90}$.
