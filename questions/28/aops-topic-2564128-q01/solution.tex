The prime factorisations are
\[500=2^2\cdot5^3,\quad 1200=2^4\cdot3\cdot5^2,\quad 1800=2^3\cdot3^2\cdot5^2,\quad 2520=2^3\cdot3^2\cdot5\cdot7.\]
Taking the smallest exponent of each prime gives $2^2\cdot3^0\cdot5^1\cdot7^0=\boxed{20}$.
