Use coordinates with $x$ east and $y$ north. A bearing of N$\,\theta\,$E points in the direction $(\sin\theta,\cos\theta)$. The desired ground velocity is
\[
\vec g=10(\sin70^\circ,\cos70^\circ)\approx(9.397,\ 3.420),
\]
the current is $\vec c=(4,0)$, and the ship's velocity relative to the water is $\vec v=\vec g-\vec c\approx(5.397,\ 3.420)$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0), G=10*(Sin(70),Cos(70)), Cc=(4,0), V=G-Cc;
draw(O--(0,4.5),gray,Arrow); label("N",(0,4.5),N,gray);
draw(O--V,blue,Arrow); draw(V--G,red,Arrow); draw(O--G,linewidth(1),Arrow);
label("ship $\vec v$",V/2,NW,blue); label("current $4$",(V+G)/2,N,red); label("course $10$",G/2,SE);
draw(arc(O,1.2,90,90-70)); label("$70^\circ$",1.5*dir(60),fontsize(8pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Its speed is $|\vec v|=\sqrt{5.397^2+3.420^2}\approx\sqrt{40.82}\approx6.39$ mi/h, and its bearing N$\,\theta\,$E satisfies $\tan\theta=\dfrac{5.397}{3.420}\approx1.578$, so $\theta\approx57.6^\circ$.

The captain should steer at about $\boxed{6.4\text{ mi/h on a bearing of N}\,58^\circ\,\text{E}}$.
