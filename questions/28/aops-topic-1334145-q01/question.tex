A ship's captain plans to sail where there is a current of $4$ mi/h moving due east. What speed and bearing should the captain maintain (relative to the water) so that the ship's actual course, taking the current into account, is $10$ mi/h on a bearing of N$\,70^\circ\,$E? Round the speed to the nearest tenth and the direction to the nearest degree.
