A dog's leash is tied to a corner of a shop at ground level. The shop is a cube with side length $6$ feet, and the leash is $8$ feet long. What is the area of the ground that the dog can reach? The dog must stay outside the shop, and the leash cannot pass through the shop. Express your answer in terms of $\pi$.
