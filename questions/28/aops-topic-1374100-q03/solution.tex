Look at the ground from above: the shop is a $6\times6$ square and the leash is tied at its corner $P$. Let $Q$ and $R$ be the two neighbouring corners.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=6,L=8;
filldraw((0,0)--(s,0)--(s,s)--(0,s)--cycle,lightgray,black+linewidth(0.9));
path big=(0,0)--arc((0,0),L,90,360)--cycle;
draw(arc((0,0),L,90,360),blue+linewidth(0.9));
draw((0,L)--(0,s),blue+linewidth(0.9)); draw((L,0)--(s,0),blue+linewidth(0.9));
draw(arc((s,0),L-s,0,90),red+linewidth(0.9)); draw(arc((0,s),L-s,0,90),red+linewidth(0.9));
draw((0,0)--L*dir(225),blue+dashed);
dot((0,0)); dot((s,0)); dot((0,s));
label("$P$",(0,0),W); label("$Q$",(s,0),S); label("$R$",(0,s),W);
label("$8$",0.55*L*dir(225),NW,blue);
label("$2$",(s,0)+2*dir(45),NE,red); label("$2$",(0,s)+2*dir(45),NE,red);
label("shop",(s/2,s/2));
label("$6$",(s/2,0),S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

\begin{itemize}
\item Without bending around the shop, the dog reaches the three quarters of the disc of radius $8$ about $P$ that lie outside the square: area $\dfrac{3}{4}\pi\cdot8^2=48\pi$.
\item To get behind the shop, the leash must bend around $Q$ or $R$. After the $6$ feet to that corner, $2$ feet remain, so the dog reaches a further quarter disc of radius $2$ beyond each of these corners (along the far sides of the shop): area $2\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}\pi\cdot2^2=2\pi$.
\end{itemize}
These regions do not overlap, and the dog cannot reach the far corner (that needs $12$ feet of leash). The total area is
\[
48\pi+2\pi=\boxed{50\pi}\ \text{square feet}.
\]
