$ 2024=8\cdot11\cdot23 $. The five distinct integers $ 2,8,11,23,2022 $ all appear as factors in $ 2023!$, and their product is $ 2\cdot2022\cdot2024 $. Therefore the fraction is already an even integer. Taking the floor leaves it unchanged, so the answer is $\boxed{\text{even}}$.
