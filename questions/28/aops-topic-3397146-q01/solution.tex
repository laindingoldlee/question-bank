Dalya covers $30$ miles in two complete flight-rest cycles, taking $90$ minutes. The final $10$ miles take $20$ more minutes, so she finishes at $110$. Phoebe covers $35$ miles in five full cycles, taking $100$ minutes. The final $5$ miles take $\dfrac{75}{7}$ minutes, giving finish time $\dfrac{775}{7}=110\dfrac{5}{7}$ minutes. Thus $\boxed{\text{Dalya wins by }\dfrac{5}{7}\text{ minute}}$. No rest is charged after finishing.
