The numbers are $u=x-1$ and $v=2x-1$. The condition $uv=u+v$ gives
\[
(x-1)(2x-1)=3x-2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 2x^2-6x+3=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x=\dfrac{3\pm\sqrt3}{2}.
\]
Since the product equals the sum, the requested quantity is $(u+v)\cdot uv=(u+v)^2=(3x-2)^2$. Here
\[
3x-2=\dfrac{9\pm3\sqrt3}{2}-2=\dfrac{5\pm3\sqrt3}{2},\qquad (3x-2)^2=\dfrac{52\pm30\sqrt3}{4}=\dfrac{26\pm15\sqrt3}{2}.
\]
So the quantity is $\dfrac{26+15\sqrt3}{2}$ or $\dfrac{26-15\sqrt3}{2}$, depending on which root $x$ is, and neither value is among (A)-(D). The answer is $\boxed{\text{(E) None of the above}}$.
