The quantity $n-2\left\lfloor\dfrac {n}{2}\right\rfloor$ is the last binary digit of $n$, and $\left\lfloor\dfrac {n}{2}\right\rfloor$ is $n$ with that digit removed. So by induction $f(n)$ is the number of $1$'s in the binary representation of $n$.

Since $2^{11}=2048>2010$, every $m<2011$ has at most $11$ binary digits. Eleven $1$'s would mean $m=2^{11}-1=2047>2010$, so $f(m)\le10$. The value $10$ is reached, e.g.\ by $m=1023=1111111111_2$ (or $m=1535=10111111111_2$). The greatest value is $\boxed{10}$.
