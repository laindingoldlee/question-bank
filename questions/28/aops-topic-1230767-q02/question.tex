A function $f$ on the nonnegative integers satisfies $f(0)=0$ and
\[f(n)=f\left(\left\lfloor\dfrac {n}{2}\right\rfloor\right)+n-2\left\lfloor\dfrac {n}{2}\right\rfloor\]
for every positive integer $n$. Find the greatest value of $f(m)$ over positive integers $m<2011$.
