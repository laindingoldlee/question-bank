In an isosceles trapezoid the diagonals are equal and symmetric about the axis, so $\angle EGD=\angle FDG=45^\circ$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair D=(0,0),G=(6,0),Ep=(1.5,4.5),F=(4.5,4.5);
draw(D--G--F--Ep--cycle);draw(D--F,dashed);draw(G--Ep,dashed);
label("$D$",D,SW);label("$G$",G,SE);label("$F$",F,NE);label("$E$",Ep,NW);label("$45^\circ$",D+0.9*dir(22),fontsize(8pt));label("$a$",(D+F)/2,NW);
\end{asy}

The diagonals meet at $45^\circ+45^\circ$ to form a right angle, i.e. they are perpendicular, each of length $a$. A quadrilateral with perpendicular diagonals has area half their product: $\boxed{\dfrac{a^2}2}$.

(Directly: the height is $a\sin45^\circ$ and the midline is $a\cos45^\circ$, giving $\dfrac{a^2}2$.)
