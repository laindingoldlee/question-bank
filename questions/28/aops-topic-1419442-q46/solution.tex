The Shoelace Formula needs the vertices in order around the polygon. Taken in the listed order, the path $(-9,1)\to(-1,-5)\to(3,-4)\to(-4,4)\to(7,1)\to(-9,1)$ crosses itself (dashed red below), so the formula does not give the area of the pentagon; that is where $22.5$ comes from.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair[] P={(-9,1),(-1,-5),(3,-4),(7,1),(-4,4)};
string[] L={"$(-9,1)$","$(-1,-5)$","$(3,-4)$","$(7,1)$","$(-4,4)$"};
pair[] dirs={W,S,SE,E,N};
draw((-10.5,0)--(8.5,0),gray,Arrow(TeXHead)); draw((0,-3.2)--(0,5.5),gray,Arrow(TeXHead));
draw((-9,1)--(-1,-5)--(3,-4)--(-4,4)--(7,1)--cycle,red+dashed);
draw(P[0]--P[1]--P[2]--P[3]--P[4]--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
for(int i=0;i<5;++i){dot(P[i]); label(L[i],P[i],dirs[i]);}
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

In counterclockwise order around the convex pentagon the vertices are
\[
(-9,1),\ (-1,-5),\ (3,-4),\ (7,1),\ (-4,4).
\]
The shoelace sums are
\[
\sum x_iy_{i+1}=(-9)(-5)+(-1)(-4)+3\cdot1+7\cdot4+(-4)\cdot1=45+4+3+28-4=76,
\]
\[
\sum y_ix_{i+1}=1\cdot(-1)+(-5)\cdot3+(-4)\cdot7+1\cdot(-4)+4\cdot(-9)=-1-15-28-4-36=-84 .
\]
So the area is
\[
\dfrac{|76-(-84)|}{2}=\dfrac{160}{2}=\boxed{80}\text{ square units},
\]
and the answer key is right.
