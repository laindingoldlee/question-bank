Yes: $a\equiv b\pmod n$ means $n\mid a-b$, and $10-(-1)=11$. Then $10^{2023}\equiv(-1)^{2023}=-1\equiv\boxed{10}\pmod{11}$. (This congruence is the reason behind the alternating-sum test for divisibility by $11$.)
