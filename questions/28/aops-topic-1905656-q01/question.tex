Josh has four pairs of pajamas; each pair consists of a shirt and a pair of pants, so there are $8$ items. He takes the items from a pile one at a time, in a uniformly random order. The first item starts a stack. After folding each later item, he puts it either on top of the stack or at the bottom of the stack, as he chooses. His goal is a final stack in which every shirt is next to the pants of its own pair, for example (pairs $Aa$, $Bb$, $Cc$, $Dd$, top to bottom) $AabBdDcC$. Assuming Josh chooses well, what is the probability that he is unable to reach his goal?
