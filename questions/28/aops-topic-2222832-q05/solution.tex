On the way to the river Momo flies against the wind at $10-5=5$ miles per hour, taking $\dfrac{1}{5}$ hour. On the way back the wind helps: $10+5=15$ miles per hour, taking $\dfrac{1}{15}$ hour. In total
\[\dfrac{1}{5}+\dfrac{1}{15}=\dfrac{4}{15}\text{ hour}=\boxed{16\text{ minutes}}.\]
(Note that this is longer than the $12$ minutes the trip would take with no wind: the slow leg lasts longer than the fast one.)
