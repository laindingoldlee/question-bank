In trapezoid $ABCD$ with $\overline{BC}\parallel\overline{AD}$, we have $AB=4$, $BC=6$, $CD=8$ and $DA=12$. The angle bisectors of $\angle A$ and $\angle B$ meet at $X$, and the angle bisectors of $\angle C$ and $\angle D$ meet at $Y$. What is $XY^2$?

$\textbf{(A) }6\qquad\textbf{(B) }7\qquad\textbf{(C) }8\qquad\textbf{(D) }9\qquad\textbf{(E) }10$
