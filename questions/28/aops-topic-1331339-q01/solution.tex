Choose the pair of languages and then one book in each:
\[
4\cdot5+4\cdot6+5\cdot6=20+24+30=\boxed{74}.
\]
Check by complement: $\dbinom{15}{2}-\dbinom42-\dbinom52-\dbinom62=105-6-10-15=74$.
