$8$ cups is $64$ ounces. With $s$ small and $b$ big paintings, $4s+10b=64$, i.e. $2s+5b=32$, with $b\ge1$. Since $2s$ and $32$ are even, $5b$ is even, so $b$ is even and $b\ge2$. Then $2s=32-5b\le22$, so $s\le11$, and $s=11$, $b=2$ works ($44+20=64$). The answer is $\boxed{11}$.
