Let $k$ be the common value: $2x=3y=4z=k$. Then
\[
x=\dfrac {k}{2},\qquad y=\dfrac {k}{3},\qquad z=\dfrac {k}{4} .
\]
Hence
\[
\dfrac {x}{6}=\dfrac{k}{12},\qquad\dfrac {y}{4}=\dfrac{k}{12},\qquad\dfrac {z}{3}=\dfrac{k}{12},
\]
so the three fractions are equal. $\blacksquare$

(Equivalently, $x:y:z=\dfrac{1}{2}:\dfrac{1}{3}:\dfrac{1}{4}=6:4:3$, after multiplying by $12$. If $k=0$ then $x=y=z=0$ and all three fractions are $0$.)
