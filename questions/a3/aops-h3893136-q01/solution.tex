For $x<0$, oddness gives $f(x)\le0$, and $f(0)=0$, so every root is positive. Put $u=|\log_2x|\ge0$. If $a<0$, or if $m>a>0$, only $u=a+m$ can contribute, giving at most two roots. Thus $a>0$ and $m\le a$.

For $0<m<a$, the four logarithms of the roots, in order, are $-a-m,-a+m,a-m,a+m$. Positive numbers form a geometric sequence exactly when their logarithms form an arithmetic sequence. The consecutive gaps are $2m,2a-2m,2m$, equal exactly when $m=\dfrac{a}{2}$.

For $m=a$, the three logarithms are $-2a,0,2a$, which always form an arithmetic sequence. Both cases are attainable, so the values are $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2},\ 1}$.
