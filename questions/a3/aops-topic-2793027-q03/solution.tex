The centroid divides each median in the ratio $2:1$. The six triangles formed by the three medians have equal area, each $\dfrac{24}{6}=4$. Quadrilateral $AMGN$ consists of two of them, so its area is $\boxed8$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(1,4),B=(0,0),C=(6,0),M=(A+B)/2,Nn=(A+C)/2,G=(A+B+C)/3;draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--M--G--Nn--cycle);draw(A--(B+C)/2,dashed);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$M$",M,W);label("$N$",Nn,E);label("$G$",G,S);
\end{asy}
