In the game Snake Eyes, you bet an amount and roll two standard dice. If exactly one die shows a $1$, you win your bet (you get your stake back plus the same amount). If both dice show a $1$, you win twice your bet. If neither die shows a $1$, you lose your bet. What is the probability that you win money? If you bet $\$100$, what is the expected value of your net gain?
