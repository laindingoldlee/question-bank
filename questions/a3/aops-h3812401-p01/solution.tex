Write a palindrome as $10001a+1010b+100c$, where $1\le a\le9$ and $0\le b,c\le9$. If $\Delta a,\Delta b,\Delta c$ are the digit changes from $x$ to $y$, then
\[10001\Delta a+1010\Delta b+100\Delta c=1820.\]
Modulo $10$, $\Delta a=0$. Dividing by $10$ gives $101\Delta b+10\Delta c=182$, so $\Delta b\equiv2\pmod{10}$. Its possible values are $2$ and $-8$; the latter would force $\Delta c=99$, impossible. Thus $\Delta b=2$, $\Delta c=-2$. There are $9$ choices for $a$, $8$ for the starting $b\in\{0,\ldots,7\}$ and $8$ for $c\in\{2,\ldots,9\}$. The answer is $\boxed{576}$.
