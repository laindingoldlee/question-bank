The sum is at most $999+9999=10998$, so $H=1$ and $E=0$. Then $\overline{SHOT}=1000S+100+10O+T$ and the equation is
\[100F+10L+U+1000S+100+10O+T=10000+100L+10P+S.\]
If $S\le8$ the left side is at most $999+8000+199<10000$, so $S=9$, and the equation simplifies to
\[100F+10O+U+T=909+90L+10P.\]
The left side is at most $900+90+18=1008$, so $90L+10P\le99$: either $L=0$ and $P\in\{0,\dots,9\}$, or $L=1$ and $P=0$. Each case occurs: with $F=9$, take $O=P$ and $U+T=9$ when $L=0$, and $O=9$, $U+T=9$ when $(L,P)=(1,0)$.

So $\overline{HELPS}=\overline{10LP9}$ is one of $10009,10019,\dots,10099$ or $10109$, and there are $\boxed{11}$ possible values (confirmed by exhaustive search).
