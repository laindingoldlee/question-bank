The map $x+\dfrac{1}{x}$ has image $(-\infty,-2]\cup[2,\infty)$. A root below $-2$ contributes two real values of $x$; roots $-2$ or $2$ contribute one each; roots in $(-2,2)$ contribute none. Thus either one cubic root is in $[-20,-2)$ and the other two are in $(-2,2)$, or the roots include both $-2,2$ and one root in $(-2,2)$. In the first case, the factors of $P(3)$ range over $w\in(5,23]$ and distinct $u,v\in(1,5)$. Their product fills $(5,575)$: the products $uv$ fill $(1,25)$, since each interior square root can be perturbed to distinct factors with the same product. Multiplying by $w$ yields the stated interval. The second case gives $P(3)=5(3-r)\in(5,25)$, already inside it. Hence $b-a=575-5=\boxed{570}$.
