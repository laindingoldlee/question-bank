Let $x\in[0,1)$ be chosen uniformly at random, with continued fraction expansion $x=\dfrac{1}{a_1+\dfrac{1}{a_2+\cdots}}$ where the $a_i$ are positive integers. Find, with proof, the expected value of $a_1$.
