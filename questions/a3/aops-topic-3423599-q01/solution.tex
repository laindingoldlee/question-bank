The climbing speed is $\dfrac{2500}{20}=125$ feet per minute, and the descending speed is $\dfrac{2500}{30}=\dfrac{250}{3}$. Their difference is $125-\dfrac{250}{3}=\boxed{\dfrac{125}{3}\text{ feet per minute}}$.
