$ABC$ is an equilateral triangle, and each of its sides is trisected. Let $P$ be the trisection point on side $AB$ nearest to $A$, $Q$ the trisection point on side $BC$ nearest to $B$, and $R$ the trisection point on side $CA$ nearest to $C$. What is the ratio of the area of triangle $PQR$ to the area of triangle $ABC$?
