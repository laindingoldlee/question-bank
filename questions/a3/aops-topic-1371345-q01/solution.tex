(a) Work modulo $8$, where $2016\equiv0$, $3^b\equiv1$ or $3$ (as $b$ is even or odd) and $7^c\equiv1$ or $7$ (as $c$ is even or odd). Since $3^b+7^c$ is even, $2^a$ is even, so $a\ge1$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $a=1$: we need $3^b+7^c\equiv6\pmod8$, but the possible sums are $2,0,4,2$. Impossible.
\item $a=2$: we need $3^b+7^c\equiv4$, so $b$ odd and $c$ even. Then $3^b+7^c=2012$ with $c\in\{0,2\}$ (as $7^4>2016$) gives $3^b=2011$ or $1963$, neither a power of $3$.
\item $a\ge3$: we need $3^b+7^c\equiv0$, so $b$ even and $c$ odd, $c\in\{1,3\}$, $b\in\{0,2,4,6\}$. Then $2^a=2009-3^b\in\{2008,2000,1928,1280\}$ or $2^a=1673-3^b\in\{1672,1664,1592,944\}$, and none of these is a power of $2$.
\end{itemize}
So there are no solutions.

(b) $2016=2^5\cdot3^2\cdot7$, so $(a,b,c)=(5,2,1)$ by unique factorisation, and $a+b+c=\boxed{8}$.
