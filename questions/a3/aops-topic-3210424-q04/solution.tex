Let $T_n$ count unlabelled domino tilings of a $2\times n$ board. The leftmost part is either one vertical domino or two horizontal dominoes, so $T_n=T_{n-1}+T_{n-2}$ with $T_0=T_1=1$. Thus $T_4=5$. Each tiling has $4!$ assignments of the distinct labels, giving $\boxed{120}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
for(int x=0;x<=4;++x)draw((x,0)--(x,2));for(int y=0;y<=2;++y)draw((0,y)--(4,y));draw((0,0)--(4,0)--(4,2)--(0,2)--cycle,linewidth(1.2));label("$4$",(2,0),S);label("$2$",(4,1),E);
\end{asy}
