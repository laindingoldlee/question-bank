An $n\times n\times n$ cube, where $n\ge2$, is built from $n^3$ unit cubes. Prove that exactly $8$ of the unit cubes have three faces on the surface of the large cube, $12(n-2)$ have exactly two, $6(n-2)^2$ have exactly one and $(n-2)^3$ have none, and check that these numbers add up to $n^3$.
