Let the roots be $r_1,r_2,r_3,r_4$ with $r_1r_2=-32$, and let $p=r_1+r_2$. By Vieta's formulas for a quadratic, $(x-r_1)(x-r_2)=x^2-px-32$, so the quartic factors as
\[x^4-18x^3+kx^2+200x-1984=\left(x^2-px-32\right)\left(x^2-qx+c\right),\]
where $x^2-qx+c=(x-r_3)(x-r_4)$. Comparing constant terms, $-32c=-1984$, so $c=62$ (that is, $r_3r_4=62$), and
\[x^4-18x^3+kx^2+200x-1984=\left(x^2-px-32\right)\left(x^2-qx+62\right).\]
Expanding the right side,
\[x^4-(p+q)x^3+(pq+30)x^2+(32q-62p)x-1984 .\]
Comparing coefficients: $p+q=18$ and $32q-62p=200$. Substituting $q=18-p$ gives $576-94p=200$, so $p=4$ and $q=14$. Therefore
\[k=pq+30=56+30=\boxed{86}.\]
Check: $\left(x^2-4x-32\right)\left(x^2-14x+62\right)=x^4-18x^3+86x^2+200x-1984$, and the roots are $8$, $-4$ (product $-32$) and $7\pm i\sqrt{13}$.
