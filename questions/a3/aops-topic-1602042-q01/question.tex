Let $a$ and $b$ be positive numbers with $a\ne b$. Point $P$ lies on segment $AB$ with $AP=a$ and $PB=b$; let $O$ be the midpoint of $AB$ and draw the semicircle with diameter $AB$. The perpendicular to $AB$ at $P$ meets the semicircle at $Q$, and $H$ is the foot of the perpendicular from $P$ to $OQ$. Prove that
\[
OQ=\dfrac{a+b}{2},\qquad PQ=\sqrt{ab},\qquad HQ=\dfrac{2ab}{a+b},
\]
the arithmetic, geometric and harmonic means of $a$ and $b$, and deduce that $\dfrac{a+b}{2}\ge\sqrt{ab}\ge\dfrac{2ab}{a+b}$.
