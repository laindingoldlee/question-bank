Let $k$ be the number of nines. The median condition gives $2\le k\le4$. For $k=2$, all other values are distinct, so their sum is at least $1+2+3+4+5+6+7=28$, giving total at least 46. For $k=3$, every other value occurs at most twice. The first four sorted values sum to at least $1+1+2+2=6$; the fifth and sixth are at least 5, giving total at least $6+10+27=43$. For $k=4$, the first five sum to at least $1+1+1+2+5=10$, giving total at least 46. Finally $(1,1,2,2,5,5,9,9,9)$ attains $\boxed{43}$.
