The powers of $i$ repeat with period $4$: $i,\ -1,\ -i,\ 1,\ i,\ -1,\dots$, and each block of four consecutive powers adds up to $i-1-i+1=0$. Since $301=4\cdot75+1$, the first $300$ terms form $75$ complete blocks with sum $0$, and
\[i+i^2+\cdots+i^{301}=i^{301}=\left(i^4\right)^{75}\cdot i=\boxed{i}.\]
