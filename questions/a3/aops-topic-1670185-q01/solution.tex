The $k$-th car is finished $6k$ minutes after production begins, so the $40$th car is finished after $6\cdot40=\boxed{240}$ minutes.

(If instead the first car were already finished at time $0$, there would be only $39$ six-minute gaps between the first and the fortieth car, giving $6\cdot39=234$ minutes; the wording ``every six minutes a new car is produced'' from an empty start means $240$.)
