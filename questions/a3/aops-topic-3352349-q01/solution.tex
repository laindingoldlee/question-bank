Let the common payment be $q$ and the amount available before child $j$ be $R$. Then $q=\dfrac{R}{10}+900j$. The next payment is $\dfrac{R-q}{10}+900(j+1)=q-\dfrac{q}{10}+900$, so equality implies $q=9000$. If $n$ is the last child, its remaining estate is $9000$, giving $9000=900n+900$, hence $n=9$. The answer is $\boxed9$.
