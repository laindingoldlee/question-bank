Let $p=x+y+z$, $q=xy+yz+zx$ and $r=xyz=-2$. The sum of the two cyclic sums is $pq-3r=4$, so $pq=-2$. Their difference is $\pm(x-y)(y-z)(z-x)$, whose square $36$ equals the discriminant
\[p^2q^2-4q^3-4p^3r+18pqr-27r^2=4-4q^3+8p^3+72-108.\]
Hence $q^3-2p^3=-17$. With $q=-\dfrac2p$: $-\dfrac8{p^3}-2p^3=-17$, i.e. $2p^6-17p^3+8=0$, so $p^3=8$ or $p^3=\dfrac12$.

Both occur: $p=2$ gives $x,y,z=-1,1,2$ (in a suitable order), and $p=\dfrac1{\sqrt[3]2}$ also gives three real roots, since the discriminant $36$ is positive. The answers are $\boxed{x+y+z=2\text{ or }\dfrac{\sqrt[3]4}2}$.
