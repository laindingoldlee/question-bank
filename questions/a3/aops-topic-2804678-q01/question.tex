At noon on the summer solstice there are no shadows in Syene. At the same moment in Alexandria, $800$ km away (taken as due north along the Earth's surface), a vertical stick casts a shadow showing that the sun's rays make an angle of $7.2^\circ$ with the vertical. Treating the Earth as a sphere and the sun's rays as parallel, estimate the Earth's volume in km$^3$ in scientific notation (three significant figures).
