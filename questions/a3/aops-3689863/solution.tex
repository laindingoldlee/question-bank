The centroid and incenter are
\[G=\dfrac{A+B+C}{3},\qquad I=\dfrac{aA+bB+cC}{a+b+c}.\]
The first follows by the median division ratio. For the second, the three subtriangle areas with vertex $I$ are proportional to their bases $a,b,c$, since their common altitude is the inradius; area coordinates therefore give the stated weighted average.
The circumcenter is
\[O=\dfrac{A\sin2\alpha+B\sin2\beta+C\sin2\gamma}{\sin2\alpha+\sin2\beta+\sin2\gamma}.\]
Indeed its signed subtriangle areas are proportional to $\sin2\alpha,\sin2\beta,\sin2\gamma$, using circumradius $R$ and area $\dfrac{R^2\sin2\alpha}{2}$ opposite $A$. The denominator equals $4\sin\alpha\sin\beta\sin\gamma>0$, so the formula remains valid for obtuse and right triangles.
A formula valid in all cases is $\boxed{H=A+B+C-2O}$. To verify it, $(H-A)\cdot(B-C)=|B|^2-|C|^2-2O\cdot(B-C)=0$ because $O$ is equidistant from $B,C$; similarly for the other altitudes. When no angle is right, trigonometric simplification also gives $H=\dfrac{A\tan\alpha+B\tan\beta+C\tan\gamma}{\tan\alpha+\tan\beta+\tan\gamma}$. For a right triangle that tangent expression is undefined, while $H$ is simply the right-angle vertex.
