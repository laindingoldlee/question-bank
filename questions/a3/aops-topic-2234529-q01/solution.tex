Evaluate the tower from the top: $2^2=4$, $2^4=16$, $2^{16}=65536$, so $b=2^{65536}$.

Each of the $2020$ terms of $a$ is at most $2020^{2020}$, and $2020<2048=2^{11}$, so
\[a\le2020\cdot2020^{2020}=2020^{2021}<\left(2^{11}\right)^{2021}=2^{22231}<2^{65536}=b .\]
Hence $\boxed{b>a}$.

(If the exponents were applied from the left instead, $\left(\left(\left(2^2\right)^2\right)^2\right)^2=2^{16}=65536$, which is far smaller than the single term $2020^{2020}$ of $a$; so the reading of the tower matters.)
