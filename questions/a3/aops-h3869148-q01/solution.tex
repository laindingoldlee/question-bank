Adding $1$ factors the condition as $(a+1)(b+1)\equiv 0\pmod 3$. This holds when $a\equiv 2$ or $b\equiv 2$. Each residue occurs equally often and the choices are independent. The complementary probability is $(\dfrac{2}{3})^2$, so the desired probability is $1-\dfrac{4}{9}=\dfrac{5}{9}$. Hence $\boxed{u+v=14}$.
