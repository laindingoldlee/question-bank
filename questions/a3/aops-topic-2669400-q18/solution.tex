Replacing $x$ by $-x$: $-xf(-x)-2xf(x)=-1$, i.e. $2xf(x)+xf(-x)=1$. With $u=xf(x)$ and $v=xf(-x)$: $u+2v=-1$ and $2u+v=1$, so $u=1$. Hence $f(x)=\boxed{\dfrac1x}$, which indeed works: $1+2x\cdot\left(-\dfrac1x\right)=-1$.
