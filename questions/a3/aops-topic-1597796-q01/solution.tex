If $a\equiv2$ and $b\equiv3\pmod7$, then $ab\equiv6\pmod7$. The three-digit numbers congruent to $6\bmod 7$ are at most $993$, since $994=7\cdot142$ gives $999\equiv5$, $998\equiv4$, $997\equiv3$, $996\equiv2$, $995\equiv1$, $994\equiv0$ and $993\equiv6$.

The value $993$ is attained: $993=3\cdot331$ with $3\equiv3$ and $331=7\cdot47+2\equiv2\pmod7$. So the greatest possible product is $\boxed{993}$.
