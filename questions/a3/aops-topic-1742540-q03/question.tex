A solid cube is formed from $125$ unit cubes. A set of unit cubes is removed, leaving behind a smaller solid cube. In how many different ways can this be done? (For example, there are $8$ ways to remove $61$ unit cubes from the original $5\times5\times5$ cube to leave a cube with edges of $4$ units.)
