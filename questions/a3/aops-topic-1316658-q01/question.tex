Find constants $A$ and $B$ such that
\[\dfrac{2x}{x^2-5x+6}=\dfrac{A}{x-3}+\dfrac{B}{x-2}\]
for all $x$ other than $2$ and $3$. One method clears denominators to get $2x=A(x-2)+B(x-3)$ and then substitutes $x=3$ and $x=2$. Explain why this substitution is legitimate even though $x=2$ and $x=3$ are excluded from the original equation.
