Point $P$, which is not the centre $O$ of a circle, is the intersection point of five distinct chords of the circle. The distance from $P$ to the circle along each of the ten segments of these chords (from $P$ to the two ends of each chord) is an integer. What is the least possible integer radius of the circle?
