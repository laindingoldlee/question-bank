Since $x^3=x(x^2+1)-x$,
\[
\dfrac{x^3}{1+x^2}=\dfrac{x(x^2+1)-x}{x^2+1}=\boxed{x-\dfrac{x}{x^2+1}}.
\]
The remaining fraction cannot be simplified further, since $x$ and $x^2+1$ have no common factor.
