Since $b+c=6-a$, $a+c=6-b$ and $a+b=6-c$,
\[
a^2(b+c)+b^2(a+c)+c^2(a+b)=a^2(6-a)+b^2(6-b)+c^2(6-c)=6(a^2+b^2+c^2)-(a^3+b^3+c^3).
\]
This equals $6\cdot40-200=\boxed{40}$.

(Such numbers exist: they are the roots of $t^3-6t^2-2t+\dfrac{52}{3}$, which are real, since $ab+bc+ca=\dfrac{36-40}{2}=-2$ and $abc=\dfrac{200-216-36}{3}=-\dfrac{52}{3}$.)
