Let the age sum in 2024 be $s$ and their age difference be $d$. Their candy difference is proportional to total candy times $\dfrac{d}{s}$. Equal differences in the next year give $\dfrac{1.2s}{s+2}=1$, so $s=10$. The 2026-to-2025 difference ratio is $\dfrac{1.2\cdot12}{14}=\dfrac{36}{35}$. Thus the new difference is $700\cdot\dfrac{36}{35}=\boxed{720}$, choice B.
