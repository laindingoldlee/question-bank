At the beginning of each of the last three years, a mother gave her children Ayşe and Zehra amounts of candy proportional to their ages.



Each year, the total amount of candy she gave increased by \(20\%\) compared to the previous year.



If Ayşe received \(700\) more candies than Zehra in both \(2024\) and \(2025\), how many more candies will Ayşe receive than Zehra in \(2026\)?



\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{a)}\ 700 & \text{b)}\ 720 \\
\text{c)}\ 750 & \text{d)}\ 770 \\
\text{e)}\ 780
\end{array}
\]
