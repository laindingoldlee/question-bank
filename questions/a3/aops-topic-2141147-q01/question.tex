In the ``four fours'' puzzle one writes numbers using exactly four $4$s and mathematical operations. Suppose the allowed operations include addition, subtraction (also as a minus sign in front of an expression), multiplication, division, square roots (which may be applied any number of times) and logarithms, where the base of a logarithm must itself be one of the four $4$s. Show that $3764376$ can be written in this way. More generally, show that every positive integer $n$ can.
