A closed cylindrical soda can has height $12$ and radius $3$, and it is full of soda. A circular hole of radius $1$ is cut in the top, concentric with the top. Bob drinks some of the soda. Afterwards he notices that the soda just reaches the edge of the hole when the can is tilted so that its axis makes an angle of $45^\circ$ with the vertical. How much soda did he drink? (The surface of the soda is a horizontal plane, and the soda does not splash.)
