Circles centered at $O,P$ have radii $2,3$ and touch externally at $A$. An equilateral triangle $ABC$ is inscribed in the first circle. Extend $AB,AC$ beyond $B,C$ to $D,E$ with $BD=CE=\sqrt3$. For each of the two tangent points $F$ from $D$ to the second circle, find $EF^2$.
