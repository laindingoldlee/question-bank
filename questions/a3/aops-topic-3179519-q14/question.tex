A particle starts moving on the number line at a time $t = 0$. Its position on the number line, as a function of time, is $$x = (t-2012)^2 -2012(t-2012)-2013.$$Find the number of positive integer values of $t$ at which time the particle lies in the negative half of the number line (strictly to the left of $0$).
