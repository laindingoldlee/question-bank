The first person receives mixed colors with probability $\dfrac{3\cdot3}{\binom62}=\dfrac{3}{5}$. Given this, the second receives mixed colors with probability $\dfrac{2\cdot2}{\binom42}=\dfrac{2}{3}$, after which the last pair is forced mixed. The probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{2}{5}}$.
