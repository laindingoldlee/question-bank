There are $n$ hideouts numbered $1$ to $n$ in a straight line. A robber hides in one of them. Every night he must move to a neighbouring hideout (from hideout $k$ to $k-1$ or $k+1$; from $1$ he must go to $2$, and from $n$ to $n-1$). Every day a cop may search exactly one hideout, and catches the robber if he is there. Find a strategy that is guaranteed to catch the robber in fewer than $2n$ searches, and prove that it works.
