By the binomial theorem, the term in $x^k$ is $\dbinom5k(2x)^k3^{5-k}$, with coefficient $c_k=\dbinom5k2^k3^{5-k}$:
\[c_0=243,\quad c_1=5\cdot2\cdot81=810,\quad c_2=10\cdot4\cdot27=1080,\quad c_3=10\cdot8\cdot9=720,\quad c_4=5\cdot16\cdot3=240,\quad c_5=32 .\]
(The ratio $\dfrac{c_{k+1}}{c_k}=\dfrac{2(5-k)}{3(k+1)}$ is greater than $1$ only for $k=0,1$, so the coefficients rise up to $k=2$ and then fall.) The term with the largest coefficient is $\boxed{1080x^2}$.
