Number the positions $1$ to $18$ from the left. The last exchange that touches a position decides its coin.
\begin{itemize}
\item Positions $9$ and $18$ (multiples of $9$) end with pennies: $2$ coins, $2$ cents.
\item The other even positions $2,4,6,8,10,12,14,16$ end with nickels: $8$ coins, $40$ cents.
\item The other multiples of $3$, namely the odd positions $3$ and $15$, keep their quarters: $50$ cents.
\item The remaining positions $1,5,7,11,13,17$ still hold dimes: $60$ cents.
\end{itemize}
The total is $2+40+50+60=\boxed{152}$ cents.
