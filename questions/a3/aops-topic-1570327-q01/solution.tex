Let the digits be $d_1d_2\cdots d_9$. For each $k$,
\[
d_k+d_{k+1}+d_{k+2}=14=d_{k+1}+d_{k+2}+d_{k+3},
\]
so $d_{k+3}=d_k$: the digits repeat with period $3$. Hence
\[
d_1=d_4=d_7,\qquad d_2=d_5=d_8,\qquad d_3=d_6=d_9 .
\]
We know $d_1=6$ and $d_9=6$, so $d_3=6$ as well, and $d_1+d_2+d_3=14$ gives $d_2=2$. The fifth digit is $d_5=d_2=\boxed{2}$ (the passcode is $626626626$).
