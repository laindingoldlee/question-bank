With $n$ passengers ($4\le n\le12$) the price per person is $35-2.5(n-4)=45-2.5n$ dollars, so the revenue is
\[
R(n)=n(45-2.5n)=-2.5\left(n^2-18n\right)=-2.5(n-9)^2+202.5 .
\]
This is largest when $n=9$: $R(9)=9\cdot22.50=202.50$ dollars (compare $R(8)=R(10)=200$, $R(12)=120$, $R(4)=140$). The answer is $\boxed{9}$, choice $\textbf{A}$.
