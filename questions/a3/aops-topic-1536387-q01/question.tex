A deep-sea fishing boat charges $\$35.00$ per person for an expedition, with a $4$-person minimum. For each person beyond the first $4$, the price per person (for everyone on the trip) is reduced by $\$2.50$. The boat can take at most $12$ passengers. For what number of people does the boat receive the maximum revenue?

$\textbf{A.}\ 9\qquad\textbf{B.}\ 8\qquad\textbf{C.}\ 12\qquad\textbf{D.}\ 4$
