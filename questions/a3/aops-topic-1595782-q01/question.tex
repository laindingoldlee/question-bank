You start with the numbers $\dfrac{1}{12}$, $\dfrac{1}{8}$ and $\dfrac{1}{6}$. At each step you may multiply a number you already have by $1$ or by $\dfrac{1}{2}$, or add or subtract two numbers you already have; every result can be used again. Prove that you can never obtain $\dfrac{3}{10}$.
