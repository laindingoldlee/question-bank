Rotation pairs the $24$ noncentral positions into $12$ pairs and fixes the centre. An invariant removed set of size four must comprise two whole pairs; it cannot include the centre, since the remaining three removals could not be paired. Hence the probability is $\dfrac{\binom{12}{2}}{\binom{25}{4}}=\dfrac{3}{575}$ and $\boxed{m+n=578}$.
