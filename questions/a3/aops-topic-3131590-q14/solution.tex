The uniform density cancels in the conditional ratio. Weight each parameter value by its head probability: $\dfrac{\int_{\dfrac{1}{2}}^{\dfrac{2}{3}}p\,dp}{\int_{\dfrac{1}{3}}^{\dfrac{2}{3}}p\,dp}=\dfrac{\dfrac{7}{72}}{\dfrac{1}{6}}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{12}}$.
