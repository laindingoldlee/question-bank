The first ship finishes after $\dfrac{500}{30}=\dfrac{50}{3}$ hours. By then the second covers $ 20t+45(\dfrac{50}{3}-t)=750-25t $. Requiring this to be at least 500 gives $ t\le10 $. Equality is attainable, so the maximum is $\boxed{10}$ hours.
