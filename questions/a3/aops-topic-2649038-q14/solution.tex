Number the months $0,1,\dots,599$. John visits in the months divisible by $6$ ($100$ of them), Jacob in those divisible by $8$ ($75$), and both in those divisible by $\operatorname{lcm}(6,8)=24$ ($25$). Exactly one of them visits on $100+75-2\cdot25=\boxed{125}$ days. (Including the first day of the $51$st year would add a joint visit, which does not change the count.)
