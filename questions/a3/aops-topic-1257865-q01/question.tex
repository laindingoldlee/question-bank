The expression $\sqrt{0+\sqrt{0+\sqrt{0+\cdots}}}$ is defined as the limit of the sequence $a_1=\sqrt0$, $a_{n+1}=\sqrt{0+a_n}$. The equation $x=\sqrt{0+x}$ has the two solutions $0$ and $1$. What is the value of the expression? Compare with $\sqrt{6+\sqrt{6+\cdots}}$, defined by $b_1=\sqrt6$, $b_{n+1}=\sqrt{6+b_n}$.
