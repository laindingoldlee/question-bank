Positive integers $x$ and $y$ satisfy
\[
\sum_{i=0}^{12}\left[\dbinom{12}{i}\dbinom{12}{i}+\dbinom{11}{i}\dbinom{13}{12-i}\right]=2\dbinom{x}{y}.
\]
What is the smallest possible value of $\dfrac {x}{y}$?
