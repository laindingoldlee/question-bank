Given $K$, the expected score is $0.9K$. Therefore the unconditional expectation is $0.9\cdot\dfrac{4+25}{2}=\boxed{13.05}$. For 25 known questions, the ratio of the probabilities of ten mistakes to one is $\dfrac{\binom{25}{10}(0.1)^{10}(0.9)^{15}}{25(0.1)(0.9)^{24}}=\dfrac{\binom{25}{10}}{25\cdot9^9}\approx0.0003375$. Ten mistakes are much less likely; the number of arrangements alone cannot determine probability.
