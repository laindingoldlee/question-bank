The three positive ratios have product 1. AM–GM gives $\dfrac{\dfrac{a}{b}+\dfrac{b}{c}+\dfrac{c}{a}}{3}\ge\sqrt[3]{1}=1$. Equality holds exactly when all three ratios are 1, that is,$a=b=c$.
