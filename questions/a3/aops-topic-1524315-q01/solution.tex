$1.5$ chickens lay $1.5$ eggs in $1.5$ days, so $1$ chicken lays $1$ egg in $1.5$ days, i.e.\ $\dfrac{1}{1.5}=\dfrac{2}{3}$ of an egg per day. Then $9$ chickens in $9$ days lay
\[
9\cdot9\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}=\boxed{54}
\]
eggs.
