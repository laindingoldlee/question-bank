Triangle $ABC$ has a right angle at $B$. Regular hexagons $P_1$, $P_2$, $P_3$ are constructed outside the triangle so that $AB$ is a side of $P_1$, $BC$ is a side of $P_2$ and $CA$ is a side of $P_3$. If the area of $P_1$ is $16$ and the area of $P_2$ is $36$, find the area of $P_3$.
