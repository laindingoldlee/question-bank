The remainder has degree less than two, so write it as $ax+b$. Since the divisor is $(x-1)(x-2)$, substitution at its roots gives $a+b=1$ and $2a+b=2^{100}$. Thus $a=2^{100}-1$ and $b=2-2^{100}$. The remainder is $\boxed{(2^{100}-1)x+2-2^{100}}$.
