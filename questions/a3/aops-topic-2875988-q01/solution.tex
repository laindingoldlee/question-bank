Let the rates be $b,j,s$. Then $2b+3j+4s=74$ and $4b+2j+3s=91$. Twice the first minus the second gives $4j+5s=57$, so $5s\equiv57\equiv1\pmod4$ and $s\equiv1\pmod4$. With $s\in\{1,5,9\}$ we get $j=13,8,3$, and $b=\dfrac{74-3j-4s}{2}$ is $15.5$, $15$, $14.5$. Only $(b,j,s)=(15,8,5)$ is integral, so the answer is $225+64+25=\boxed{314}$.
