For example,
\[\boxed{4\times4+10-2=16+8=24}\qquad\text{and}\qquad\boxed{(10+4)\times2-4=28-4=24}.\]
The first adds $16$ and $8$, the second subtracts $4$ from $28$; so they are genuinely different.
