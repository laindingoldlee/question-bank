In the school the ratio is $\boxed{1:1}$, as given. If there are $n$ boys and $n$ girls, the bus carries $\dfrac35n$ boys and $\dfrac23n$ girls, a ratio of $\dfrac35:\dfrac23=\boxed{9:10}$.
