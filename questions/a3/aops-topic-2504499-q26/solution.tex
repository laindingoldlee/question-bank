$t^2+\dfrac1{t^2}=9-2=7$ and $t^3+\dfrac1{t^3}=27-3\cdot3=18$. Then $\left(t^2+\dfrac1{t^2}\right)\left(t^3+\dfrac1{t^3}\right)=t^5+\dfrac1{t^5}+t+\dfrac1t$, so $t^5+\dfrac1{t^5}=126-3=\boxed{123}$.
