In a regular decagon, choose five diagonals whose interiors do not intersect. Count the choices up to rotations of the decagon; reflections remain distinct. You may use the fact that the number of ways to choose $k$ noncrossing diagonals in a labelled convex $n$-gon is
\[D(n,k)=\dfrac{1}{k+1}\binom{n+k-1}{k}\binom{n-3}{k}.\]
