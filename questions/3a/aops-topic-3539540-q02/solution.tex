Write $n=2^a m$ with m odd, and let d be the number of divisors of m. Every odd divisor is odd, so their sum has parity d. The total divisor count is $(a+1)d$. Their difference has parity $ad$. Replacing n by 4 n increases a by 2, changing this parity expression by $2 d$, an even number. Thus the R-type property is preserved. Starting from 1 produces the infinite sequence $1,4,16,64,\ldots$.
