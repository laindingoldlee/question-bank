None exists. A product of $n$ distinct primes is at least the product of the first $n$ primes, which is at least $2^n$ for $n\ge1$. But $2^n>n$ for all $n\ge1$, so the product is larger than $n$. ($n=1$ fails too, since $1$ is not prime.) $\blacksquare$
