The first digit is not $0$, so $a\ge1$, and then all five digits are at least $1$: they come from $1,2,\dots,9$. A nondecreasing list of five digits is determined by how many times each digit $1,\dots,9$ is used, so we count the ways to choose $5$ digits from $9$ kinds with repetition allowed. By stars and bars ($5$ stars and $8$ bars separating the $9$ kinds) there are
\[\dbinom{5+8}{8}=\dbinom{13}{5}=\boxed{1287}\]
such numbers.
