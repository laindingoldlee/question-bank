Place $A=(0,0),B=(4,0),C=(4,4),D=(0,4)$. Then $Q=(2,0),R=(4,1),S=(1,4),P=(0,2)$. Intersecting their six side lines gives the common hexagon, in cyclic order, with vertices $(\dfrac{8}{5},\dfrac{2}{5}),(\dfrac{16}{7},\dfrac{4}{7}),(3,2),(2,3),(\dfrac{4}{7},\dfrac{16}{7}),(\dfrac{2}{5},\dfrac{8}{5})$. Each vertex satisfies both triangles'side inequalities. The shoelace formula gives area $\boxed{\dfrac{291}{70}}$.

\end{asy}
