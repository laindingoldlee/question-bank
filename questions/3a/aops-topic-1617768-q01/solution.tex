Each term is twice the previous one, so the sequence is geometric with first term $2$ and ratio $2$.
\begin{itemize}
\item Recursive: $a_1=2$ and $a_n=2a_{n-1}$ for $n\ge2$.
\item Explicit: $a_n=2\cdot2^{n-1}=\boxed{2^n}$.
\end{itemize}
