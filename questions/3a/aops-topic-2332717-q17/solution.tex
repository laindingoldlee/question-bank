Reading and Writing occupy two adjacent class slots within a block: $3$ positions in the morning block of four and $2$ in the afternoon block of three, $5$ in all, with $2$ orders. The other five classes fill the other slots in $5!$ ways. Total $5\cdot2\cdot120=1200$. \textbf{(C)}
