Find the value of
\[
\dfrac{(2+3)\left(2^2+3^2\right)\left(2^4+3^4\right)\cdots\left(2^{1024}+3^{1024}\right)\left(2^{2048}+3^{2048}\right)+2^{4096}}{3^{2048}},
\]
where each exponent in the product is twice the previous one.
