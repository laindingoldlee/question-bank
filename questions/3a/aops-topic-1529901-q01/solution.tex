Let $P=(2+3)\left(2^2+3^2\right)\cdots\left(2^{2048}+3^{2048}\right)$. Multiply by $1=3-2$ and use the difference of squares repeatedly:
\[
(3-2)(3+2)=3^2-2^2,\quad \left(3^2-2^2\right)\left(3^2+2^2\right)=3^4-2^4,\quad\dots,\quad \left(3^{2048}-2^{2048}\right)\left(3^{2048}+2^{2048}\right)=3^{4096}-2^{4096}.
\]
So $P=3^{4096}-2^{4096}$, and
\[
\dfrac{P+2^{4096}}{3^{2048}}=\dfrac{3^{4096}}{3^{2048}}=\boxed{3^{2048}}.
\]
