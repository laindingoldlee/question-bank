Let the age be $a$ and $k=\left\lfloor\dfrac{364}{a}\right\rfloor$. The multiples of $a$ below $365$ are $a,2a,\dots,ka$, with sum $S=a\cdot\dfrac{k(k+1)}{2}$. Since Cedric was born in $2016-a$ (or $2015-a$, if his birthday is later in the year), $S$ must be just below $2016$. For the factors of $2016$ near the possible answer:
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
a&28&32&36&42&48\\ \hline
k&13&11&10&8&7\\
S&2548&2112&1980&1512&1344
\end{array}
\]
For smaller factors $a\le28$ the sum is even larger ($k$ grows roughly like $\dfrac{364}{a}$, so $S$ is about $\dfrac{364^2}{2a}$), and for larger factors $a\ge48$ it is smaller still. Only $a=36$ fits: $36+72+\cdots+360=36\cdot55=1980=2016-36$. Cedric was born in $\boxed{1980}$.
