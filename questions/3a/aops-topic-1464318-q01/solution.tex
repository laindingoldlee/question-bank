The second digit is one of $1,3,5,7,9$. If it is $3$ or more, the last digit would have to be at least $12$, which is impossible. So the second digit is $1$, and the last digit is one of $4,5,6,7,8,9$: $6$ choices. The first digit has $9$ choices ($1$ to $9$) and the third and fourth digits $10$ each. The total is
\[
9\cdot1\cdot10\cdot10\cdot6=\boxed{5400}.
\]
