Expanding,
\[
u^2+v^2=(mx+ny)^2+(nx-my)^2=m^2x^2+2mnxy+n^2y^2+n^2x^2-2mnxy+m^2y^2=(m^2+n^2)(x^2+y^2).
\]
So $(m^2+n^2)(x^2+y^2)=x^2+y^2$, and since $x^2+y^2>0$ we get $m^2+n^2=\boxed{1}$.
