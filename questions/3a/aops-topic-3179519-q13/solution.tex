One cut can at most double the number of pieces, even when pieces are stacked, so at least $\lceil\log_2 32\rceil=5$ cuts are needed. Halve the length $8$ three times, stacking equal pieces, then halve the length $4$ twice. This creates all $32$ unit squares in $\boxed5$ cuts.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
for(int i=0;i<=8;++i)draw((i,0)--(i,4));for(int j=0;j<=4;++j)draw((0,j)--(8,j));label("$8$",(4,0),S);label("$4$",(0,2),W);
\end{asy}
