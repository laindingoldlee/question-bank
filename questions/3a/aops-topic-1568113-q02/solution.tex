Since $n^2+n=n(n+1)$,
\[
f(n)=\dfrac{1}{n(n+1)}=\dfrac{1}{n}-\dfrac{1}{n+1}.
\]
The sum telescopes:
\[
\left(1-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)+\left(\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{3}\right)+\cdots+\left(\dfrac{1}{10}-\dfrac{1}{11}\right)=1-\dfrac{1}{11}=\boxed{\dfrac{10}{11}}.
\]
