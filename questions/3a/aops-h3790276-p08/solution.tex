The total rises from $25$ to $32$, a factor of $\dfrac{32}{25}=1.28$. If the number of boys rises by $10\%$, its multiplier is only $1.1$. Therefore the fraction of boys is multiplied by $\dfrac{1.1}{1.28}<1$: it decreases, so it cannot rise by ten percentage points. The proposed situation is $\boxed{\text{impossible}}$.
