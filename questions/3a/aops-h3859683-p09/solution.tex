To have Manhattan distance five after five moves, no horizontal direction may be canceled by its opposite and no vertical direction by its opposite. Choose one horizontal and one vertical direction in four ways, then any five-step word in those directions in $2^5$ ways. Purely horizontal or vertical paths have been counted twice; there are four such paths. Thus $4\cdot 32-4=124$ favorable paths out of $4^5=1024$, giving $\dfrac{31}{256}$ and $\boxed{m+n=287}$.
