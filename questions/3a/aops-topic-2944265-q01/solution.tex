One-digit palindromes sum to 45. Two-digit palindromes sum to $11(1+\cdots+9)=495$. Three-digit palindromes $101a+10b$, with $1\le a\le9$, $0\le b\le9$, sum to $101\cdot10\cdot45+10\cdot9\cdot45=49500$. Four-digit palindromes below 2017 are $1001+110b$ for $b=0,\ldots,9$, and 2002. Their sum is $10010+110\cdot45+2002=16962$. Total $45+495+49500+16962=\boxed{67002}$.
