Let my allowance be $x\ge0$. The sum of the five allowances is $84+x$, so the average is $\dfrac{84+x}{5}$. The median depends on where $x$ falls.
\begin{itemize}
\item $x\le17$: the median is $17$, so $84+x=85$ and $x=1$, which is indeed at most $17$.
\item $17\le x\le24$: the median is $x$, so $84+x=5x$ and $x=21$, which is in the range.
\item $x\ge24$: the median is $24$, so $84+x=120$ and $x=36$, which is in the range.
\end{itemize}
So my allowance is $\boxed{\$1,\ \$21\text{ or }\$36}$. The poster's $\$36$ is only one of the three possibilities.
