In right triangle $ABH$, the midpoint $D$ of the hypotenuse satisfies $DH=\dfrac{AB}{2}$. Similarly, $EH=\dfrac{BC}{2}$. The midpoint theorem gives $DE=\dfrac{AC}{2}$. Equality of the three sides of $DEH$ therefore implies $AB=BC=AC$, proving the claim.

\end{asy}
