The arc of a sector is the same fraction of the whole circumference as its central angle is of $360^\circ$. Here the fraction is $\dfrac{20}{360}=\dfrac{1}{18}$, so
\[
\text{circumference}=18\cdot\pi=\boxed{18\pi}\text{ units}.
\]
(The radius is $9$, since $2\pi\cdot9=18\pi$.)

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real R=3;pair O=(0,0);
fill(O--arc(O,R,80,100)--cycle,palegray);
draw(circle(O,R),linewidth(0.9));
draw(O--R*dir(80));draw(O--R*dir(100));
draw(arc(O,R,80,100),red+linewidth(1.6));
draw(arc(O,0.9,80,100));
draw(0.93*dir(86)--1.45*dir(62),gray);
label("$20^\circ$",1.45*dir(62),E,fontsize(9pt));
label("$\pi$",R*dir(90),N,red);
dot(O);label("$O$",O,S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
