In triangle $ABC$, extend side $CA$ beyond $A$, side $AB$ beyond $B$ and side $BC$ beyond $C$. This gives one exterior angle at each vertex: $x$ at $A$, $y$ at $B$ and $z$ at $C$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $x+y+z=360^\circ$, using only the facts that two angles forming a straight line add up to $180^\circ$ and that the angles of a triangle add up to $180^\circ$.
\item[(b)] A convex polygon with $n$ sides has its sides extended in the same way, going around the polygon in one direction, giving one exterior angle at each vertex. Prove that these $n$ exterior angles also add up to $360^\circ$.
\end{enumerate}
