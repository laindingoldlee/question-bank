The total is $45$, so each triple sums to $15$. The triple containing $9$ must be either $\{1,5,9\}$ or $\{2,4,9\}$. In the first case consider the triple containing $8$ would have to contain $2,5$ or $3,4$ or $1,6$, but only $3,4$ are available and the complement $\{2,6,7\}$ also sums to $15$. Thus this case gives $\{1,5,9\},\{3,4,8\},\{2,6,7\}$. In the second case the triple with $8$ can only be $\{1,6,8\}$ (the other pairs require $2$ or $4$); its complement is $\{3,5,7\}$. Hence there are $\boxed 2$ divisions, choice C.
