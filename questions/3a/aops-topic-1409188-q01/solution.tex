A number is divisible by $11$ exactly when the alternating sum of its digits is. For $\overline{3d41}$ this sum is $3-d+4-1=6-d$. Since $0\le d\le9$, we need $-3\le6-d\le6$, and the only multiple of $11$ in this range is $0$. So $d=\boxed{6}$, and indeed $3641=11\cdot331$.
