If the units digit is $u$, then $95u$ has two digits only when $u=1$. If the tens digit is $t$, then $100\le95t\le199$, forcing $t=2$. Thus the multiplication is $\boxed{95\cdot21=1995}$, with partial products 95 and 190 (the latter shifted one place).
