Joe has two identical sets of cards labelled $1$ to $8$ ($16$ cards in all). He puts all the cards into $3$ distinct piles, each pile arranged in strictly increasing order from top to bottom (so no pile contains two cards with the same label). Each pile must contain a card labelled $1$ or $2$, and any two adjacent cards in a pile must differ by at most $2$. In how many ways can he do this?
