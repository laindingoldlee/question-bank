Right triangle $ABC$ has $AB=3$, $AC=4$ and $BC=5$. Points $D$, $E$ and $F$ are located inside triangle $ABC$ so that $D$ lies on $\overline{BE}$, $E$ lies on $\overline{AF}$, and $F$ lies on $\overline{CD}$. If $BD=DE$, $AE=EF$ and $FC=2DF$, what is the area of triangle $DEF$? Express your answer as a common fraction.
