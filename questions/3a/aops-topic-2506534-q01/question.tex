For a non-degenerate triangle $ABC$, let $f(\triangle ABC)$ be the number of intersection points in the figure formed by drawing the sides of $\triangle ABC$, the circle with diameter $AB$, the circle with diameter $AC$ and the circle with diameter $BC$. The average value of $f(\triangle ABC)$ over all non-congruent triangles $ABC$ whose side lengths are three distinct integers between $20$ and $30$ inclusive can be written as $\dfrac xy$ in lowest terms. Find $x+y$.
