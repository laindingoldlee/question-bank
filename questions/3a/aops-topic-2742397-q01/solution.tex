If the sequence has common difference $d$ and $k\ge1$ steps, then $kd=4060-20=4040$. Since the terms increase, $d$ is a positive divisor of $4040=2^3\cdot5\cdot101$, and each divisor gives exactly one sequence. There are $4\cdot2\cdot2=\boxed{16}$ sequences.
