There are $3^3=27$ equally likely colour sequences for the three flowers. The sequences with three different colours are the orderings of red, white and purple, of which there are $3!=6$. The probability is
\[
\dfrac{6}{27}=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{9}}.
\]
(Equivalently: the second flower differs from the first with probability $\dfrac{2}{3}$, and the third differs from both with probability $\dfrac{1}{3}$, giving $\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot\dfrac{1}{3}=\dfrac{2}{9}$.)
