Insert arithmetic operations and brackets into the sequence $2222222222$ (ten $2$s, whose order may not be changed, and adjacent $2$s may be joined into multi-digit numbers) to obtain $999$. For example, from $22222$ one can obtain $45$ as $22\cdot2+\dfrac22$.
