Put $A=(0,0)$ and the rectangle in $0\le x\le3,0\le y\le6$. The circle meets $x=3$ at height $3\sqrt3$. The portion inside the rectangle consists of the triangle of base three and height $3\sqrt3$, plus a $30^\circ$ sector of radius six. Its area is $\dfrac{9\sqrt3}{2}+3\pi$. Subtracting from $36\pi$ gives $\boxed{33\pi-\dfrac{9\sqrt3}{2}}$, choice A. 
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(220);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(3,0),C=(3,6),D=(0,6),P=(3,3*sqrt(3));draw(circle(A,6));draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--P,dashed);label("$A$",A,(-1,-1));label("$B$",B,(1,-1));label("$C$",C,(1,1));label("$D$",D,(-1,1));label("$P$",P,(1,0));
\end{asy}
