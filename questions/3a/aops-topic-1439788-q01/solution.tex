The third angle is $\angle C=180^\circ-30^\circ-60^\circ=90^\circ$, so $ABC$ is a $30$--$60$--$90$ right triangle with hypotenuse $AB=c$. The side opposite the $30^\circ$ angle is half the hypotenuse, and the side opposite the $60^\circ$ angle is $\sqrt3$ times that:
\[
BC=c\sin30^\circ=\boxed{\dfrac {c}{2}},\qquad AC=c\sin60^\circ=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}c}.
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s=0.2){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
pair A=(0,0),Cc=(sqrt(3),0)*2,B=(sqrt(3)*2,2);
draw(A--Cc--B--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); draw(ra(Cc,A-Cc,B-Cc,0.25));
draw(arc(A,0.8,0,30),red); draw(arc(B,0.5,210,270),red);
label("$A$",A,SW); label("$C$",Cc,SE); label("$B$",B,N);
label("$30^\circ$",A+0.85*dir(15),E,red); label("$60^\circ$",B+0.55*dir(240),dir(240),red);
label("$c$",(A+B)/2,NW); label("$\dfrac{c}{2}$",(Cc+B)/2,E); label("$\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}c$",(A+Cc)/2,S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The legs are perpendicular, so the area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac {c}{2}\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}c=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt3}{8}c^2}$.
