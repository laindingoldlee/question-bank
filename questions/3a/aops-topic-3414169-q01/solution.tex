At time $2490$, $249$ pins have been sold but only $11$ batches have been completed (two initial and nine more), so $11n\ge249$ and $n\ge23$. For $n=23$, during $250j\le t<250(j+1)$, $j=0,\ldots,9$, supply is $23(j+2)$ and demand is at most $25j+24$. Their difference is at least $22-2j>0$. At the final sale, time $2500$, supply is $12\cdot23>250$. Thus $\boxed{23}$ suffices and is minimal.
