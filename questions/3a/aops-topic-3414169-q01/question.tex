A maker produces a whole batch of $n$ identical pins every $250$ seconds. At time $0$ he has two completed batches, and continues making batches. Starting at time $10$, one new customer arrives every $10$ seconds until $250$ customers have each bought one pin. If a batch completion and a sale coincide, completion happens first. Find the least $n$ ensuring every sale can be filled.
