For $n\ge1$ let
\[
A_n=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt1}+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt5}+\cdots+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2n-1}},\qquad
B_n=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt4}+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt6}+\cdots+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2n}} .
\]
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that $A_n$ and $B_n$ grow without bound as $n\to\infty$ (so the infinite sums diverge and their ratio is not defined).
\item[(b)] Nevertheless, show that the ratio $\dfrac{A_n}{B_n}$ of the partial sums has a limit as $n\to\infty$, and find it.
\end{enumerate}
