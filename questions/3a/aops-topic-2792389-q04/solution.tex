The bottom four earn 6 points in their six mutual games, so the second-place score is at least 6. The top two together can earn at most 13 points:1 in their mutual game and 12 against the other six players. If second place exceeded 6, it would be at least 6.5 and first place at least 7, contradicting that bound. Thus second place has 6 and the bottom four combined have exactly 6. They earned all 6 internally and therefore lost every game against the top four. Hence $\boxed{\text{third place beat fifth place}}$.
