Expand:
\[
(1-p)(1-q)(1-r)=1-(p+q+r)+(pq+qr+rp)-pqr .
\]
From $(p+q+r)^2=p^2+q^2+r^2+2(pq+qr+rp)$ we get $4=30+2(pq+qr+rp)$, so $pq+qr+rp=-13$. Hence
\[
(1-p)(1-q)(1-r)=1-2-13-10=\boxed{-24}.
\]
(Such numbers exist: by Vieta's formulas $p,q,r$ are the roots of $t^3-2t^2-13t-10=(t+1)(t+2)(t-5)$, namely $-1,-2,5$, and indeed $(1+1)(1+2)(1-5)=-24$.)
