The first bag contains the one-digit primes $2,3,5,7$ and the second bag contains the digits $0,1,2,\dots,9$. A digit from the second bag is placed in the tens place and a prime from the first bag in the units place, forming a two-digit number. How many different two-digit numbers divisible by $3$ can be formed in this way?
