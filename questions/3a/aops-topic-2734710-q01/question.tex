The upper-bound test for real roots (synthetic division by $c>0$ giving all nonnegative entries shows $c$ is an upper bound) is stated for polynomials with positive leading coefficient. How can it be used for $p(x)=-x^3-x^2-4x-4$? Find all real roots of $p$.
