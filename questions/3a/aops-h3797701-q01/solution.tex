Since $y=4t^2+4t+1=4x-27$, the image is a straight segment. Completing the square gives $x=(t+\dfrac{1}{2})^2+\dfrac{27}{4}$, ranging from $\dfrac{27}{4}$ to $13$. The bounded region is a right triangle with base $13-\dfrac{27}{4}=\dfrac{25}{4}$ and height $25$. Its area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{25}{4}\cdot25=\dfrac{625}{8}=78.125$, rounding to $\boxed{78}$.
