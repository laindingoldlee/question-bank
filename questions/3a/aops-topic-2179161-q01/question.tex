In how many ways can $10$ identical toys be given to $3$ dogs, if every toy is given away and some dogs may get no toys? A student argues that the answer is $\dbinom{11}{3}$, because there are $11$ places (before, between and after the $10$ toys) to put the dividing sticks. What is wrong with this argument?
