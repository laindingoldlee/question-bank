Modulo $18$, $5^{-2}$ means the inverse of $5^2=25$: the residue $u$ with $25u\equiv1\pmod{18}$. It exists because $\gcd(25,18)=1$. Since $25\equiv7\pmod{18}$ and $7\cdot13=91=5\cdot18+1$, we get $u=13$.

Equivalently, $5^{-1}\equiv11$ (as $5\cdot11=55=3\cdot18+1$), and $5^{-2}=\left(5^{-1}\right)^2\equiv11^2=121=6\cdot18+13\equiv13$. So $5^{-2}\equiv\boxed{13}\pmod{18}$. (Check: $25\cdot13=325=18\cdot18+1$.)
