A restaurant has $2$ appetizers, $3$ main dishes and $4$ desserts. At lunch, everything except the desserts is available; at dinner, everything is available. Al eats lunch and dinner at the restaurant. At lunch he orders one appetizer and one main dish; at dinner he orders one appetizer, one main dish and one dessert. He never orders the same dish twice during the day. In how many ways can he order his two meals? (The order in which the dishes of a meal are served does not matter.)
