Lunch: $2$ choices of appetizer and $3$ of main dish, so $2\cdot3=6$ lunches.

Dinner: the appetizer must be the other one ($1$ choice), the main dish one of the $2$ not eaten at lunch, and the dessert any of the $4$ desserts: $1\cdot2\cdot4=8$ dinners for each lunch.

In total there are $6\cdot8=\boxed{48}$ ways.
