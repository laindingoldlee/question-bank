A chain begins at $1$ and contains exactly $16$ moves. Eight moves multiply the current number by $2$, and the other eight multiply it by $3$. An ordering of the moves is called admissible if no three consecutive moves multiply by the same number. One admissible ordering is selected uniformly at random. What is the probability that $2^4\cdot3^4$ appears somewhere in the chain? Express the probability as $\dfrac{m}{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $m+n$.
