We use induction on $n$.

\textbf{Base case $n=4$:} $4!=24>16=2^4$.

\textbf{Inductive step:} suppose $n!>2^n$ for some $n\ge4$. Then, since $n+1\ge5>2$,
\[(n+1)!=(n+1)\cdot n!>(n+1)\cdot2^n>2\cdot2^n=2^{n+1}.\]
So the statement holds for $n+1$, and by induction for every $n\ge4$. $\blacksquare$

(The condition $n\ge4$ is needed: $1!<2$, $2!<4$ and $3!<8$.)
