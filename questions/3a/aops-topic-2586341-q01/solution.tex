A grandmother has a child who has a child, so there are at least three generations and hence at least $3$ people.

Three people suffice: a grandmother, her daughter, and her daughter's daughter. The mothers are the grandmother and the middle woman ($2$ mothers), the daughters are the middle woman and the youngest ($2$ daughters), and there is $1$ grandmother. So the smallest possible number is $\boxed{3}$.
