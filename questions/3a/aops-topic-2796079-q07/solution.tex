By the binomial theorem, $(1+10)^n\equiv1+10n+100\binom n2\pmod{1000}$, since later terms contain $10^3$. With $n=2014$: $10n=20140\equiv140$, and $\binom{2014}2=1007\cdot2013=2027091$, so $100\binom n2\equiv100$. Thus
\[11^{2014}\equiv1+140+100=\boxed{241}\pmod{1000}.\]
