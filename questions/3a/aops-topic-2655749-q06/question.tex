A regular dodecahedron (the polyhedron consisting of $12$ congruent regular pentagonal faces) floats in empty space with two horizontal faces. There is a ring of five slanted faces adjacent to the top face, and a ring of five slanted faces adjacent to the bottom face. (Each face of the top ring shares an edge with its two neighbours in that ring and with exactly two faces of the bottom ring.) How many ways are there to move from the top face to the bottom face via a sequence of adjacent faces so that each face is visited at most once and moves are not permitted from the bottom ring to the top ring?
