Triangles $MBD$ and $MCD$ have equal bases $MB=MC$ on the same line and the same altitude from $D$, so $[MCD]=6$. Thus $[MAC]=[MAD]+[MDC]=11$. Since $M$ is the midpoint of $BC$, triangles $MAB$ and $MAC$ have equal areas. Therefore $[ABC]=2\cdot11=\boxed{22}$.
