If $3\nmid x$ and $x$ is even, then $3\mid\dfrac{x}{2}$ would imply $3\mid x$, a contradiction. If $x$ is odd, its successor is $3x+1\equiv1\pmod3$. Thus each successor of a nonmultiple of three remains a nonmultiple. Induction proves the claim for all later terms; this does not require any assumption about eventual arrival at one.
