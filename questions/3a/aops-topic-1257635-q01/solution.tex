We need two numbers with product $6$ and sum $5$: these are $2$ and $3$. Hence
\[
x^2+5x+6=\boxed{(x+2)(x+3)}.
\]
(Check: $(x+2)(x+3)=x^2+3x+2x+6=x^2+5x+6$.)
