Alice and Bob play a game in which Bob repeatedly flips a weighted coin: on heads Bob gets a point, and on tails Alice gets a point. They play until one of them has $5$ points. If the probability of heads is $\dfrac{2}{3}$, what is the probability that Bob reaches $5$ points first?
