With $n$ arrows, the number of hits is binomial$(n,\dfrac12)$, and we need $P(\text{hits}\ge15)\ge0.95$. Computing, $n=39$ gives about $0.940$ and $n=40$ about $0.960$. So $\boxed{40}$ arrows.
