The imaginary part is $(m+2)(m+3)$, so it vanishes only at $m=-2$ in the domain. Thus (a) $m=-2$, where $z=0$. The real numerator factors as $(m-3)(m+2)$, so the real part is zero at $m=3$ or $-2$. Excluding $z=0$ leaves (b) $\boxed{m=3}$, for which $z=30i$. If zero is allowed as purely imaginary, $m=-2$ would also qualify.
