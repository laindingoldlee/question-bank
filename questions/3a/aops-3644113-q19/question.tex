Alice starts at the origin $(0, 0)$ and walks east (along the positive x-axis) at a constant speed of 2 units per second. Bob starts at the point $(0, 3)$ and walks south (along the negative y-axis) at a constant speed of $1$ unit per second. Let $s$ be the number of seconds after which the distance between Alice and Bob is minimized, and let $d$ be that minimum distance. The value of $25 \cdot d \cdot s$ can be expressed as $a\sqrt{b}$, where $a$ and $b$ are integers such that $b$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. Find $a + b$.
