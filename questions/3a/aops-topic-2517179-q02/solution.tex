Both numbers are powers of $3$: the gcd of $3^u$ and $3^v$ is $3^{\min(u,v)}$, since the common divisors of two powers of the prime $3$ are exactly the powers $3^j$ with $j\le\min(u,v)$. Here $u=3^{333}+1<3^{334}+1=v$, so
\[\gcd\left(3^{3^{333}+1},\ 3^{3^{334}+1}\right)=\boxed{3^{3^{333}+1}}.\]
