Let $w,x,y,z$ be four distinct points in the complex plane, no three of which are collinear. Prove that $w,x,y,z$ are the vertices of a parallelogram (in some order) if and only if the sum of two of them equals the sum of the other two. Show also that the hypothesis "no three collinear" cannot be dropped.
