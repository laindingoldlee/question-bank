A private jet made a trip from Denver to Los Angeles in $3$ hours, flying against a steady headwind. On the return trip the wind speed doubled and became a tailwind. The return trip took only $2.5$ hours. If the plane's ground speed on the return trip was $450$ mi/h, what was the speed of the original headwind?
