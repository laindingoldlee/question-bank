Let the plane's airspeed be $p$ and the headwind $w$ (mi/h). The return ground speed is $p+2w=450$, and the distance is $2.5\cdot450=1125$ miles. Going out, the ground speed was $\dfrac{1125}{3}=375=p-w$. Subtracting, $3w=75$, so the headwind was $\boxed{25}$ miles per hour (and $p=400$).
