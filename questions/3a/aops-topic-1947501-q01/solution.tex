Scoring at least $80\%$ means getting at least $8$ of the $10$ questions right. Each of the $2^{10}=1024$ patterns of right and wrong answers is equally likely, and the number of patterns with exactly $k$ right answers is $\dbinom{10}{k}$. So the probability is
\[\dfrac{\dbinom{10}{8}+\dbinom{10}{9}+\dbinom{10}{10}}{1024}=\dfrac{45+10+1}{1024}=\dfrac{56}{1024}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{128}}.\]
