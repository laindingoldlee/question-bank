Enclose the origin in the cube $[-1,1]^3$. By rotational symmetry each of its six faces receives one-sixth of all directed rays from the center. An unoriented line meets the specified top face exactly when one of its two directions exits there. These are disjoint equal-probability events, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{2}{6}=\dfrac{1}{3}}$. Edges and corners have probability zero.
