The two-digit multiples of $17$ are $17,34,51,68,85$, and those of $23$ are $23,46,69,92$. So each digit determines the possible next digits:
\[1\to7,\quad 2\to3,\quad 3\to4,\quad 4\to6,\quad 5\to1,\quad 6\to8\text{ or }9,\quad 8\to5,\quad 9\to2,\]
and $7$ has no possible successor.

Starting from $3$, the digits are forced as $3,4,6$; at each $6$ we may go on with $9$ or $8$. After $9$ the digits are $9,2,3,4,6$, back to $6$. After $8$ they are $8,5,1,7$ and then the number must end (it may also end earlier, at $8$, $5$ or $1$). So the number is the block $34692$ repeated, possibly followed by a tail $8517$ (or part of it) after a $6$:
\[\underbrace{34692\,34692\,\cdots}_{\text{repeating}}\qquad\text{or}\qquad 34692\cdots346\,\underbrace{8517}_{\text{tail}}\ \text{(cut short as needed)}.\]

\textbf{No tail.} The digit in position $n$ depends on $n\bmod5$: positions $\equiv1,2,3,4,0$ hold $3,4,6,9,2$. Since $2000\equiv0\pmod5$, the last digit is $2$.

\textbf{With a tail.} The $6$s are in positions $\equiv3\pmod5$. If the tail starts after a $6$ in position $j\equiv3\pmod5$, the tail digits $8,5,1,7$ are in positions $j+1,\ldots,j+4$, so position $2000=j+t$ with $t\in\{1,2,3,4\}$ and $t\equiv2000-3\equiv2\pmod5$. So $t=2$ and the last digit is $5$ (the number ends $\ldots3468\,5$).

Both are possible, so the possible last digits are $2$ and $5$, and their sum is $\boxed{7}$.
