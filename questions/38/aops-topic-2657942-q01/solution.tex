A tower of exponents is evaluated from the top down, so
\[x^{2^3}=x^{(2^3)}=\boxed{x^8}.\]
On the other hand, $(x^2)^3=x^2\cdot x^2\cdot x^2=x^{2\cdot3}=\boxed{x^6}$. They are not equal in general: for $x=2$ they are $256$ and $64$.
