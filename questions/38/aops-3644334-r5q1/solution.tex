For a nonnegative current amount, doing an addition before a multiplication by an integer greater than one never decreases the final amount. Thus the maximum uses all additions first, then all multiplications: $(1+3+5)\cdot2\cdot4\cdot6=432$. Since each island may be visited at most once, Pat may visit none, or only even islands, and finish with zero. Hence $M-m=\boxed{432}$. Requiring every island would instead make the minimum nine and the difference 423.
