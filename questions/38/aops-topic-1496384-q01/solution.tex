There are $4\cdot5=20$ equally likely pairs of results. Every number on Spinner II is odd, so the product is even only when Spinner I shows $2$ or $4$.
\begin{itemize}
\item Spinner I shows $2$: the products are $2,6,10,14,18$; three of them ($10,14,18$) have two digits.
\item Spinner I shows $4$: the products are $4,12,20,28,36$; four of them have two digits.
\end{itemize}
So $3+4=7$ pairs work, and the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{7}{20}}$.
