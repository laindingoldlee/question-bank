Spinner I is divided into four equal sections labelled $2$, $3$, $4$ and $5$. Spinner II is divided into five equal sections labelled $1$, $3$, $5$, $7$ and $9$. If each spinner is spun and the resulting numbers are multiplied, what is the probability that the product is a two-digit even number? Express your answer as a common fraction.
