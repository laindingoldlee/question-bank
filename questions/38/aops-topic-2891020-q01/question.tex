In a line of $100$ people, the first has $100$ litres of water and the rest have none. The first gives $\dfrac12$ of his water to the second, the second gives $\dfrac23$ of hers to the third, and so on; the $99$th gives $\dfrac{99}{100}$ of his to the $100$th. How much water does the $100$th person end up with?
