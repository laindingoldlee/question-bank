Extend four alternate sides of the octagon to form a square. Each corner of the square is cut off along an isosceles right triangle whose hypotenuse is a side of the octagon, so its legs are $\dfrac{x}{\sqrt2}$. The square has side $x+2\cdot\dfrac{x}{\sqrt2}=x\left(1+\sqrt2\right)$. Hence
\[[\text{octagon}]=x^2\left(1+\sqrt2\right)^2-4\cdot\dfrac12\cdot\left(\dfrac{x}{\sqrt2}\right)^2=x^2\left(3+2\sqrt2\right)-x^2=\boxed{2\left(1+\sqrt2\right)x^2}.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real a=1/sqrt(2),s=1+sqrt(2);
pair[] v={(a,0),(a+1,0),(s,a),(s,a+1),(a+1,s),(a,s),(0,a+1),(0,a)};
fill((0,0)--v[0]--v[7]--cycle,palegray);fill((s,0)--v[2]--v[1]--cycle,palegray);fill((s,s)--v[4]--v[3]--cycle,palegray);fill((0,s)--v[6]--v[5]--cycle,palegray);
draw((0,0)--(s,0)--(s,s)--(0,s)--cycle,dashed);
draw(v[0]--v[1]--v[2]--v[3]--v[4]--v[5]--v[6]--v[7]--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
label("$x$",(v[0]+v[1])/2,N,fontsize(8pt));label("$x$",(v[7]+v[0])/2,NE,fontsize(8pt));
label("$\dfrac{x}{\sqrt2}$",(a/2,0),S,fontsize(7pt));label("$\dfrac{x}{\sqrt2}$",(0,a/2),W,fontsize(7pt));
\end{asy}
