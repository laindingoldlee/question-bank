Factor the second equation:
\[
x^2y+xy^2-x-y=xy(x+y)-(x+y)=(xy-1)(x+y)=6(x+y)=24,
\]
so $x+y=4$. Then
\[
x^2+y^2=(x+y)^2-2xy=16-14=\boxed{2}.
\]
(Here $x$ and $y$ are the roots of $t^2-4t+7=0$, namely $2\pm i\sqrt3$; they are not real, but the computation is valid for complex numbers too.)
