Write $u=|y|>0$. Then
\[
f(4,y)=4-u+4u-\dfrac{4}{u}=4+3u-\dfrac{4}{u} .
\]
Setting this equal to $5$: $3u-\dfrac{4}{u}=1$, i.e.\ $3u^2-u-4=0$, i.e.\ $(3u-4)(u+1)=0$. Since $u>0$, $u=\dfrac{4}{3}$. So $\boxed{y=\dfrac{4}{3}\text{ or }y=-\dfrac{4}{3}}$.
