When I play with our dog, it jumps on me with probability $\dfrac27$. If it jumps on me, I scream with probability $\dfrac89$. If I scream, my brother reminds me to turn my back on the dog with probability $\dfrac{11}{12}$, and, independently, my mom comes running with probability $\dfrac{6}{11}$. If my mom comes running, the dog gets overwhelmed with probability $\dfrac{36}{a}$, where $a$ is the smallest integer greater than $1$ that is both a perfect square and a perfect cube. After all this, I remember to turn my back and calm the dog down with probability $\dfrac23$.

What is the probability that either this whole chain happens (the dog jumps on me, I scream, my brother reminds me, my mom comes running, the dog gets overwhelmed and I calm it down), or the dog does not jump on me at all?
