The small triangle has each side half the original, so its area is one-quarter of 100, namely 25. The other piece has area $100-25=\boxed{75}$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(0,sqrt(3)),B=(-1,0),C=(1,0),M=(A+B)/2,N=(A+C)/2;filldraw(M--B--C--N--cycle,lightblue);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(M--N);label("$A$",A,(0,1));label("$B$",B,(-1,-1));label("$C$",C,(1,-1));label("$M$",M,(-1,0));label("$N$",N,(1,0));
\end{asy}
