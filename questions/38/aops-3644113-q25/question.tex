Let $a, b, c, d$ with $a \ne 0$ be single-digit numbers. A four-digit year abcd is lucky if \[\underline{abcd} = (\underline{ab} + \underline{cd})^2.\]Notably, $2025 $ is a lucky year since $2025 = (20 + 25)^2$. Find the sum of the other two lucky years.



Note: The notation $\underline{abcd}$ denotes the number $1000a+100b+10c+d$, not the product a×b×c×d. Similarly, the notation $\underline{ab}$ and $\underline{cd}$ denote the numbers $10a + b$ and $10c + d$, respectively.
