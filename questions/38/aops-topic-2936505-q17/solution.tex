Let $D$ be Alec's sum minus James's. Complementing every face ($a\mapsto7-a$ and $b\mapsto13-b$) sends $D$ to $7m-13n-D$. The distribution therefore has symmetry centre $c=\dfrac{7m-13n}{2}$ and positive probability at every integer between its extreme values. If $c>0$, symmetry gives $\Pr(D>0)=\Pr(D<2c)$, strictly larger than $\Pr(D<0)$: the interval $[0,2c)$ contains support values, unless all support is positive, which already makes the inequality clear. The reverse holds for $c<0$. Equal chances are therefore equivalent to $7m=13n$. The smallest positive choice is $m=13,n=7$, giving $\boxed{91}$.
