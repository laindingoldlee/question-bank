Since $n<2024$, its digit sum is at most $36$, so $1988\le n<2024$. If $1988\le n\le1999$, its digit sum is at least $26$, and direct substitution using $n=1990+u$ or $1980+u$ gives no solution. For $n=2000+10t+u$, $t=0,1,2$, the equation becomes $11t+2u=22$. The only digit solution is $t=2,u=0$; $t=0$ requires $u=11$ and $t=1$ a noninteger. Thus $\boxed{n=2020}$.
