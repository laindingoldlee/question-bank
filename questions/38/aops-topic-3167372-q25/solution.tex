The count $X$ is binomial with parameters $10,\dfrac{1}{2}$. For an integer guess $g$, the expected score is $\dfrac{10\binom{10}{g}+3\binom{10}{g-1}+3\binom{10}{g+1}}{1024}$, treating out-of-range binomial coefficients as zero. For $g=0,1,2,3,4,5$, the numerators are $40,238,840,1965,3216,3780$, respectively; for $g=6,\ldots,10$ they repeat in reverse order by symmetry. Guesses outside $0,\ldots,10$ have still smaller expectation. Thus guess $\boxed5$, with expected score $\dfrac{3780}{1024}=\boxed{\dfrac{945}{256}}$, exceeding the safe score $2$.
