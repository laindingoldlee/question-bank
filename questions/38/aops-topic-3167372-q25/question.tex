Ten other teams independently choose a safe option with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. You may take a guaranteed score of $2$, or guess the number $X$ of those teams choosing the safe option. An exact guess scores $10$, an error of one scores $3$, and any other guess scores zero. Which choice maximizes your expected score?
