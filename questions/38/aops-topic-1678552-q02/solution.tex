Fill the $5$-gallon bucket. Pour from it into the $2$-gallon bucket until the small one is full, leaving $3$ gallons; empty the small bucket into the river. Pour again from the large bucket to fill the small one, leaving $5-2-2=\boxed{1}$ gallon in the large bucket.

In equation form, $1=5\cdot1-2\cdot2$: one fill of the large bucket minus two fills of the small one.
