A placement is determined by the numbers $x_1,\dots,x_7\ge0$ of objects in the boxes, with $x_1+\cdots+x_7=5$. Represent it as a row of $5$ stars and $6$ bars: the bars cut the row into $7$ groups, and $x_i$ is the number of stars in group $i$. Every arrangement of $5$ stars and $6$ bars gives exactly one placement, so the number of placements is
\[
\dbinom{11}{5}=\boxed{462}.
\]
