Jalacia erases some integers from the list
\[
5,\ 9,\ 2,\ 3,\ 7,\ 8,\ 6,\ 5,\ 6,\ 4,\ 1,\ 7,\ 9,\ 8 .
\]
When she is done, the range of the remaining list is $2$. What is the least possible number of integers that Jalacia could have erased?
