If the remaining list has range $2$, its values lie in some window $\{m,m+1,m+2\}$ (with both $m$ and $m+2$ present). To erase as few numbers as possible, keep every number in the best window. The list has $14$ numbers, with counts: $1,2,3,4$ once each and $5,6,7,8,9$ twice each. The windows hold
\[
\{1,2,3\}:3,\ \{2,3,4\}:3,\ \{3,4,5\}:4,\ \{4,5,6\}:5,\ \{5,6,7\}:6,\ \{6,7,8\}:6,\ \{7,8,9\}:6 .
\]
At most $6$ numbers can remain, for example $5,7,6,5,6,7$ (range $7-5=2$). So at least $14-6=\boxed{8}$ numbers must be erased.
