Modulo $4$, the square residues are $0,1$. Modulo $25$, the nonzero square residues are $1,4,6,9,11,14,16,19,21,24$, together with $0$, giving $11$. Every combination of a residue modulo $4$ and modulo $25$ comes from some integer by the Chinese remainder theorem. Therefore the number of square residues modulo $100$ is $2\cdot11=\boxed{22}$. The given hundred consecutive integers represent every residue class.
