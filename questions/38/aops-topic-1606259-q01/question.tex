Let $0\le k\le n$ be integers and let $\dbinom nk$ denote the number of $k$-element subsets of an $n$-element set.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $\dbinom nk=\dfrac{n!}{k!\,(n-k)!}$.
\item[(b)] Prove that $\dbinom nk$ is the coefficient of $x^ky^{n-k}$ in the expansion of $(x+y)^n$.
\end{enumerate}
