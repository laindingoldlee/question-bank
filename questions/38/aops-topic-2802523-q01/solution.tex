Choose $3$ of the $7$ days arranged in a cycle with no two adjacent. Between the chosen days there are $4$ rest days, at least one in each of the $3$ gaps, so the gaps are $(1,1,2)$ in some cyclic order. Fix which gap is the double gap and where the pattern starts: each plan is determined by the day that begins the double gap, giving $\boxed{7}$ plans.
