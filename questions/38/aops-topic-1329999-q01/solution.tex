Multiply both sides by the positive number $x!\,y!\,z!$. The inequality becomes
\[
(x+y)\,y!\,z!+(y+z)\,x!\,z!+(z+x)\,x!\,y!>5 .
\]
Each factorial is at least $1$ and each sum such as $x+y$ is at least $2$, so each of the three terms is at least $2$, and the left side is at least $6>5$. $\blacksquare$
