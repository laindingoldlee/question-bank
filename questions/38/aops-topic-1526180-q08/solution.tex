Every date uses $8$ digits (MMDDYYYY). Since $2018=8\cdot252+2$, the $2018$th digit is the $2$nd digit of the $253$rd date.

Count the days of 2018: January to August have $31+28+31+30+31+30+31+31=243$ days (2018 is not a leap year). So the $253$rd day is September $10$, written $09102018$, whose $2$nd digit is $\boxed{9}$.
