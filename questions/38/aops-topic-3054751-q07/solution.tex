Such a number is either $p^3$ or $pq$ for distinct primes $p<q$. The cubes are 8 and 27. For products, the smaller primes 2,3,5,7 allow respectively 14,9,5,2 choices of the larger prime (bounded by 49,33,19,14, respectively). No larger smaller prime is possible, since $11\cdot13>100$. Thus the total is $2+14+9+5+2=\boxed{32}$.
