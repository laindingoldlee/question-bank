For length $n$ and first term $a$, $n(2a+n-1)=200$, hence $a=\dfrac{\dfrac{200}{n}-n+1}{2}$. Thus $n$ divides 200 and $\dfrac{200}{n}-n+1$ is even: precisely one of $n$ and $\dfrac{200}{n}$ is even. Testing the divisors yields $n=1,5,8,25,40,200$, with first terms $100,18,9,-8,-17,-99$ respectively. Therefore the sequences run from $\boxed{100\text{ to }100;\ 18\text{ to }22;\ 9\text{ to }16;\ -8\text{ to }16;\ -17\text{ to }22;\ -99\text{ to }100}$.
