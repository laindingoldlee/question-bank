$-(4+5)$ is the additive inverse of $4+5$, the number that gives $0$ when added to $4+5$. And $(-4)+(-5)$ has this property: using commutativity and associativity,
\[
(4+5)+\bigl((-4)+(-5)\bigr)=\bigl(4+(-4)\bigr)+\bigl(5+(-5)\bigr)=0+0=0 .
\]
Since a number has only one additive inverse, $-(4+5)=(-4)+(-5)$; both equal $-9$. (Equivalently, $-(4+5)=(-1)(4+5)=(-1)4+(-1)5$ by the distributive property.)
