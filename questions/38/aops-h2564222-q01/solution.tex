The triangle inequality gives $d_P\ge\sqrt{8^2+12^2}=4\sqrt{13}$, with equality exactly when $P$ lies on the joining segment. Its equation is $3x+2y=24$. Positive integer solutions require even $x$ with $0<x<8$, giving
\[
(x,y)=(2,9),(4,6),(6,3).
\]
These attain the lower bound, so there are $\boxed3$ minimizing points.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

\end{asy}
