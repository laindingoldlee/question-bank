A fraction in lowest terms is a terminating decimal exactly when its denominator has no prime factors other than $2$ and $5$. Since $700=2^2\cdot5^2\cdot7$, the fraction $\dfrac {n}{700}$ terminates exactly when the factor $7$ cancels, that is, when $7\mid n$. (If $7\mid n$, then $\dfrac {n}{700}=\dfrac{n\cdot 1}{7\cdot100}$ has denominator dividing $100$; if $7\nmid n$, the reduced denominator still contains $7$.)

The multiples of $7$ with $0<n<699$, i.e.\ $1\le n\le698$, are $7,14,\dots,693$: there are $\left\lfloor\dfrac{698}{7}\right\rfloor=\boxed{99}$ of them.
