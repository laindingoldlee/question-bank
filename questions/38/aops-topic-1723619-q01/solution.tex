Three nonzero numbers are consecutive terms of a geometric sequence exactly when the middle one squared equals the product of the other two:
\[
(x+4)^2=2x(2x-7)\ \Longrightarrow\ x^2+8x+16=4x^2-14x\ \Longrightarrow\ 3x^2-22x-16=0\ \Longrightarrow\ (x-8)(3x+2)=0 .
\]
So $x=8$ (terms $16,12,9$, ratio $\dfrac{3}{4}$) or $x=-\dfrac{2}{3}$ (terms $-\dfrac{4}{3},\dfrac{10}{3},-\dfrac{25}{3}$, ratio $-\dfrac{5}{2}$). Both give nonzero terms, so both are valid, and the sum is $8-\dfrac{2}{3}=\boxed{\dfrac{22}{3}}$.
