Choose the two digits.

\emph{Both nonzero:} $\binom92=36$ choices. Each of the $4$ positions takes either digit, excluding the two constant strings: $2^4-2=14$. Subtotal $504$.

\emph{One digit is $0$:} $9$ choices for the nonzero digit $a$, which must lead. The other three positions are $a$ or $0$, not all $a$: $2^3-1=7$. Subtotal $63$.

Total: $504+63=\boxed{567}$.
