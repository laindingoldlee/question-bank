Let $X$ and $Y$ be the two rolls. Summing over all $64$ equally likely outcomes,
\[E[X+Y]=\sum_{x,y}(x+y)P(x,y)=\sum_{x,y}x\,P(x,y)+\sum_{x,y}y\,P(x,y)=\sum_xx\,P(X=x)+\sum_yy\,P(Y=y)=E[X]+E[Y],\]
since summing $P(x,y)$ over $y$ gives $P(X=x)$, and over $x$ gives $P(Y=y)$. This linearity of expectation holds for any two random variables, independent or not. Hence $E[X+Y]=4.5+4.5=\boxed{9}$.

(A symmetry check: pairing each outcome $(x,y)$ with $(9-x,9-y)$ pairs a sum $s$ with $18-s$, so the average sum is $9$.)
