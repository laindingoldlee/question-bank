Harold runs at a constant $800$ metres every $5$ minutes and Jim at a constant $700$ metres every $4$ minutes. They start together at the same point of a $400$-metre circular track and run in opposite directions without stopping. How many times do they pass each other during the first $5$ hours? (The start does not count as a pass.)
