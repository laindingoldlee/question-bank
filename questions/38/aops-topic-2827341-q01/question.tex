The ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1$ has semi-axes $a$ and $b$. Using the fact that a circle of radius $r$ has area $\pi r^2$, prove that the ellipse has area $\pi ab$.
