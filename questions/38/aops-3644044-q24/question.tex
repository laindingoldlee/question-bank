Triangle $\vartriangle ABC$ has $AB = 4$, $AC = 5$, and $BC = 6$. Let the midpoint of $\overline{BC}$ be $M$, and let the intersection of the angle bisector at $B$ with the line  $\overleftrightarrow{AC}$ be $N$. The shortest distance from $C$ to $\overline{MN}$ can be expressed as $\dfrac{1}{r} (p\sqrt{q})$, where $p$, $r$ are relatively prime and $q$ is not divisible by any perfect square other than $1$. Find $p + q + r$.
