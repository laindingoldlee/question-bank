A closed bottle in the shape of a right circular cylinder with radius $A$ and height $B$ is half full of water. It is tilted so that its axis makes an angle $C$ with the vertical, where $0\le C\le\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. Find the area of the water's surface in terms of $A$, $B$ and $C$.
