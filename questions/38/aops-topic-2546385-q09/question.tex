Shrek and $2$ friends sit in a circle and take turns, starting with Shrek and passing to the right. On a turn, the player rolls a fair $20$-sided die (faces $1$ to $20$). If the roll is not composite, the player wins and the game ends. If the roll is a multiple of $5$ other than $5$ itself, the same player rolls again. Otherwise the turn passes to the player on the right. What is the probability that Shrek wins? Round to the nearest percent.
