The non-composite numbers are $1,2,3,5,7,11,13,17,19$ ($9$ numbers); the re-roll numbers are $10,15,20$ ($3$ numbers); the other $8$ numbers pass the turn. A re-roll does not change the situation, so each turn ends in a win with probability $\dfrac{9}{17}$ and in a pass with probability $\dfrac{8}{17}$.

Shrek wins on his $k$th turn with probability $\left(\dfrac8{17}\right)^{3(k-1)}\dfrac9{17}$. Summing the geometric series,
\[P=\dfrac{\dfrac9{17}}{1-\left(\dfrac8{17}\right)^3}=\dfrac{9\cdot17^2}{17^3-8^3}=\dfrac{2601}{4401}=\dfrac{289}{489}\approx0.591.\]
So Shrek wins with probability about $\boxed{59\%}$.
