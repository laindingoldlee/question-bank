A regular octagon with side length $1$ unit has area $2+2\sqrt2$ square units. The area of a regular octagon with side length $3$ units can be written as $a(b+\sqrt c)$ square units, where $a$, $b$, $c$ are positive integers and $c$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. What is $a+b+c$?
