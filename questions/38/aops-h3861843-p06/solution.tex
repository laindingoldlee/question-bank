For positive $x,y$, equality would require $\dfrac{\dfrac{x}{2}+\dfrac{6y}{5}}{x+2y}=\dfrac{\dfrac{9y}{5}}{x+3y}$. Cross multiplication and cancellation gives $\dfrac{1}{2}x^2+\dfrac{9}{10}xy=0$, impossible. Thus there is no positive solution and no minimum $x+y$ under that interpretation. Formally allowing $x=0$ makes the equality hold, but then the stated initial shooting percentages have no defined denominator. The source needs correction before a numerical minimum is meaningful.
