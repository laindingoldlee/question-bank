For first roll $a$, the second must be a multiple of $\dfrac{12}{\gcd(a,12)}$ and has $\gcd(a,12)$ choices among $1$ through $12$. For $a=1,\ldots,12$, the counts are $1,2,3,4,1,6,1,4,3,2,1,12$, totaling $40$. Hence the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{40}{144}=\dfrac{5}{18}}$.
