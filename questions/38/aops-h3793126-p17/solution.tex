Let $S$ be the cost of one week and $u_0,u_1,u_2$ the fees on July 10, 11, 12. Periodicity gives
\[S+u_0+u_1+u_2=7000,\quad 2S-u_0-u_1=7000,\quad 3S-u_2=10000.\]
Adding all three yields $6S=24000$, so $S=4000$. The requested 24 days consist of three weeks plus the first three day types, costing $3S+u_0+u_1+u_2=2S+7000=\boxed{15000\text{ tenge}}$.
