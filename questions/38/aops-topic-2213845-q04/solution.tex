The three lines contain $5$, $6$ and $7$ points, and $A,B,C$ each lie on two of them, so they contain $5+6+7-3=15$ points: all the points, as stated.

Three points form a triangle unless they are collinear, which happens exactly when all three lie on one of the three lines. So the number of triangles is
\[\dbinom{15}{3}-\dbinom53-\dbinom63-\dbinom73=455-10-20-35=\boxed{390}.\]
