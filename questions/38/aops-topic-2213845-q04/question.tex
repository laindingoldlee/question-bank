There are $15$ points in a plane, three of which are called $A$, $B$ and $C$. Apart from $A$ and $B$, exactly three of the points lie on line $AB$; apart from $B$ and $C$, exactly four lie on line $BC$; apart from $C$ and $A$, exactly five lie on line $CA$. Every one of the $15$ points lies on one of these three lines, and no three points are collinear except on these three lines. How many triangles (of positive area) have all three vertices among the $15$ points?
