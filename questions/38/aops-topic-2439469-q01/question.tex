A student claims that any two equal numbers add up to zero, with the following argument. Let $a=b$. Then
\begin{align*}
a^2-b^2&=b^2-a^2\\
(a+b)(a-b)&=(a+b)(b-a)=(a+b)\bigl(-(a-b)\bigr)\\
a+b&=-(a+b)\\
2a+2b&=0\\
a+b&=0.
\end{align*}
Find the error.
