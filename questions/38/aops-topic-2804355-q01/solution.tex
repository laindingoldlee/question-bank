Let $Y$'s rate be $y$ jobs per day; then $X$'s is $1.4y$ and $2.4y=\dfrac1{10}$, so $y=\dfrac1{24}$ and $X$'s rate is $\dfrac7{120}$. Time for $0.35$ of the job: $0.35\cdot\dfrac{120}7=\boxed{6}$ days.
