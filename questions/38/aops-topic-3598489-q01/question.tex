For each positive integer $n$, let $F_n=\dfrac{n!}{(n-2)!(n-4)!\cdots}$, ending with $0!$ when $n$ is even and $1!$ when $n$ is odd. For $n=1$, the empty denominator is $1$. How many $n$ make $F_n$ an integer?
