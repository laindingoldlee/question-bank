The values for $n=1,\ldots,10$ are respectively $1,2,6,12,20,15,7,\dfrac{7}{6},\dfrac{1}{10},\dfrac{1}{384}$. Thus exactly $n=1,\ldots,7$ work among these. Also\[\dfrac{F_{n+2}}{F_n}=\dfrac{(n+2)(n+1)}{n!}.\]For $n\ge5$ this ratio is less than $1$: it is so at $5$, and the denominator grows by a factor $n+1$ while the numerator grows by the smaller factor $\dfrac{n+3}{n+1}$. Consequently the odd and even subsequences remain between $0$ and $1$ after $n=9$ and $n=10$, respectively. No further integers occur. The answer is $\boxed7$.
