A circle of radius $4$ rolls without slipping around the inside of a circle of radius $8$, always internally tangent to it. A point $P$ is marked on the small circle.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that $P$ moves back and forth along a diameter of the large circle.
\item[(b)] When the small circle rolls once around the inside of the large circle (so that its centre goes once around), what is the total distance travelled by $P$?
\end{enumerate}
