The equality is $110x+y=yb^2+xb+x$, or $(109-b)x=(b^2-1)y$, with $1\le x,y<b<10$. Reduce $\dfrac{y}{x}=\dfrac{109-b}{b^2-1}$ for each $b=2,\ldots,9$. The reduced numerator-denominator pairs are $(107,3),(53,4),(7,1),(13,3),(103,35),(17,8),(101,63),(5,4)$. All but the last violate the digit bounds. For $b=9$, the only admissible multiple is $(x,y)=(4,5)$. Thus $\boxed{n=445}$.
