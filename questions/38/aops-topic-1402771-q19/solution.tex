Let a pencil cost $p$ cents and an eraser $e$ cents. Then
\[
20p+16e=\dfrac{6}{5}(16p+20e)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad100p+80e=96p+120e\quad\Longrightarrow\quad p=10e .
\]
The smallest positive whole-number prices are $e=1$, $p=10$. Then $20$ pencils and $20$ erasers cost $20(p+e)=20\cdot11=220$ cents, that is $\boxed{\$2.20}$.
