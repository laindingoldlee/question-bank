At a store, the cost of $20$ pencils and $16$ erasers is $20\%$ more than the cost of $16$ pencils and $20$ erasers. All pencils cost the same and all erasers cost the same. If the price of each pencil and of each eraser is a whole number of cents, find the minimum possible price of $20$ pencils and $20$ erasers, in dollars.
