The square sides give $ BE=BA $ and $ BH=BC $. Their external orientations make $\angle EBH $ supplementary to $\angle ABC $, so its sine is the same. Hence $[BEH]=\dfrac{1}{2} BA\cdot BC\sin\angle ABC=[ABC]$. By Heron's formula, with semiperimeter 21, this equals $\sqrt{21\cdot8\cdot7\cdot6}=\boxed{84}$.
