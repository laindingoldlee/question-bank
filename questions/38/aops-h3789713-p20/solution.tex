A $6\times12$ rectangle has perimeter $36$ and area $72$, while a $5\times20$ rectangle has perimeter $50$ and area $100$. In both cases perimeter is half the area. Thus all piece perimeters sum to $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot300^2=45000$. This counts the outer boundary $1200$ once and every internal cut twice. If the total cut length is $L$, then $45000=1200+2L$, so $\boxed{L=21900}$.
