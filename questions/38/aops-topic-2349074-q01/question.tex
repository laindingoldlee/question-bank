$ABC$ is a right triangle with the right angle at $A$. $AH$ is its altitude and $AK$ is the bisector of the right angle, with $H$ and $K$ on $BC$. If $CK:KB=1:3$, what is $CH:HB$?

$\textbf{(A) }1:3\qquad\textbf{(B) }1:9\qquad\textbf{(C) }1:\sqrt3\qquad\textbf{(D) }1:6\qquad\textbf{(E) }1:4$
