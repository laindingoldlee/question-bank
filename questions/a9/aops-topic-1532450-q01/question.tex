In a convex cyclic pentagon $ABCDE$, $\angle AEB+\angle DEC=\angle BEC$. The diagonal $AC$ meets $BE$ and $BD$ at $X$ and $Y$ respectively, and $CE$ and $BD$ meet at $Z$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $X$, $Y$, $Z$, $E$ lie on a circle.
\item[(b)] Prove that if $XY=YZ$, then $EY\cdot XZ=XY\cdot(EX+EZ)$.
\item[(c)] Show that $XY=YZ=1$, $XZ=\dfrac{3}{2}$ and a perimeter of $10$ for quadrilateral $XYZE$ cannot all hold.
\end{enumerate}
