\textbf{(a)} Completing the square, $f(t)=40000-16(t-50)^2$. So his greatest distance from Earth is exactly $40000$ miles, reached at $t=50$, and $f(t)=40000$ has the single (double) root $t=50$. He just reaches the moon, after $\boxed{50\text{ hours}}$.

\textbf{(b)} No. Fifteen minutes is $\dfrac14$ hour, and $f\left(\dfrac14\right)=-1+400=399$ miles, far short of $40000$; he needs $50$ hours. So he cannot reach the moon on one breath.
