Let $a_n$ count sequences avoiding consecutive eats. Such a sequence either ends with a noneat symbol (two choices after any valid length-$n-1$ prefix), or ends with eat preceded by a noneat symbol (two choices after length $n-2$). Thus $a_n=2a_{n-1}+2a_{n-2}$, with $a_0=1,a_1=3$. It gives $a_2=8,a_3=22,a_4=60,a_5=164,a_6=448$. Subtracting from all $3^6=729$ sequences gives $\boxed{281}$.
