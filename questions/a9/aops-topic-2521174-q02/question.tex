Points $P$, $X$ and $Q$ lie on a line in this order, and $PQ$ is the diameter of a large semicircle. A semicircle with diameter $XQ$ is drawn on the same side of the line as the large semicircle (so it lies inside the large one), and a semicircle with diameter $PX$ is drawn on the other side of the line. The shaded region is bounded by these three semicircular arcs: it consists of the part of the large half-disc outside the half-disc on $XQ$, together with the half-disc on $PX$. The semicircle on $PX$ is the smallest of the three semicircles and has radius $8$, and the shaded region has area $180\pi$. Find the diameter of the large circle.
