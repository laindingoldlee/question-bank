With $c=-33$ the equation reads $m^3+n^3+c^3-3mnc=0$, and
\[m^3+n^3+c^3-3mnc=\dfrac12(m+n+c)\left((m-n)^2+(n-c)^2+(c-m)^2\right).\]
So either $m+n=33$ or $m=n=c=-33$. With $m+n=33$ and $mn\ge0$, both are nonnegative: $m=0,1,\dots,33$, giving $34$ pairs. Adding $(-33,-33)$ gives $\boxed{35}$.
