Both cars are at the same place, $75$ km past $A$, at the same moment. The first car took $\dfrac{75}{60}$ hours $=75$ minutes to get there from $A$, and the second car took $\dfrac{75}{75}$ hour $=60$ minutes. So the second car passed $A$ $75-60=\boxed{15}$ minutes after the first car.
