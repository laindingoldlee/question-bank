A car passes point $A$ driving at a constant rate of $60$ km per hour. A second car, travelling at a constant rate of $75$ km per hour, passes the same point $A$ a while later and then follows the first car. It catches the first car after travelling a distance of $75$ km past point $A$. How many minutes after the first car passed point $A$ did the second car pass point $A$?
