Infinitely many jars stand in a row; the $n$th jar from the left contains $n$ red marbles and $2$ blue marbles. For a positive integer $k$, Karate draws one marble uniformly at random from each of the first $k$ jars, independently. If the probability that all $k$ drawn marbles are red is strictly less than $\dfrac{1}{2020}$, what is the least possible value of $k$?
