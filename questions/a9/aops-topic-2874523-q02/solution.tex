Comparing two adjacent length-20 windows shows that positions $i$ and $i+20$ have the same letter. A length-22 window consists of a balanced length-20 window followed by two letters; these last two must be equal. Periodicity then shows that positions $i$ and $i+1$ agree for every $1\le i\le L-21$, where $L$ is the total length. If $L\ge 31$, the first eleven letters agree, contradicting balance of the first twenty. Thus $L\le 30$. The sequence $A^{10}B^{10}A^{10}$ has length $30$; every twenty-letter window is balanced, and each twenty-two-letter window adds two As to a balanced twenty-letter window. Hence the maximum is $\boxed{30}$.
