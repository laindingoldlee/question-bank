Big Ben, a $12$-hour clock in London, sounds $4$ notes on the quarter hour, $8$ notes on the half hour, $12$ notes on the three-quarter hour and $16$ notes on the hour. In addition, on the hour it strikes one note for each hour; for example, it strikes $5$ additional notes at $5$ o'clock. How many notes does Big Ben sound in a $24$-hour period?
