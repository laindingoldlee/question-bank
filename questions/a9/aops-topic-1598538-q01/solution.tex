\textbf{Chimes.} Every hour contains one quarter, half, three-quarter and full hour, giving $4+8+12+16=40$ notes; over $24$ hours that is $24\cdot40=960$ notes.

\textbf{Hour strikes.} In each $12$-hour cycle the strikes are $1+2+\dots+12=78$, and a day contains two cycles: $2\cdot78=156$ notes.

Total: $960+156=\boxed{1116}$ notes.
