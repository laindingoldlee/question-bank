Can the polynomials
\[
a^2-b^3,\qquad a^2+b^3,\qquad a^3-b^2,\qquad a^3+b^2
\]
be factored? Prove that none of them is a product of two non-constant polynomials in $a$ and $b$ with real coefficients.
