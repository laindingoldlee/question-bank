Against the stream the speed is $\dfrac{1880}{4}=470$ mph, and with it $\dfrac{5820}{6}=970$ mph. So $j-w=470$ and $j+w=970$, giving a jet speed of $\boxed{720\text{ mph}}$ and a jet stream of $\boxed{250\text{ mph}}$.
