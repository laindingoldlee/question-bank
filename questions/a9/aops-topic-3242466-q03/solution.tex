The minute hand at twelve means zero minutes past the hour; the hour hand gives three. Thus it shows $\boxed{3{:}00}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw(circle((0,0),2));draw((0,0)--(0,1.8),blue);draw((0,0)--(1.2,0),red);label("12",(0,2),N);label("3",(2,0),E);label("6",(0,-2),S);label("9",(-2,0),W);
\end{asy}
