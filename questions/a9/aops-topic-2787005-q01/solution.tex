Yes. Choose six different rays from a common point $O$ and take a unit segment along each. Every pair intersects exactly at $O$, with no common positive-length interval.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
for(int k=0;k<6;++k){pair P=2*dir(60*k);draw((0,0)--P);label(string(k+1),P,dir(60*k));}dot((0,0));label("$O$",(0,0),SW);
\end{asy}
