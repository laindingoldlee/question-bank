Every $n\ge3$ is such a sum: $3$, $4$ and $5$ are, and every $n\ge6$ is $3$ plus a smaller one of $3,4,5,\dots$, so by induction it is a sum of $3$s, $4$s and $5$s. The number $0$ is the empty sum. But $1$ and $2$ are smaller than every allowed term, so they cannot be written. The answer is $\boxed{2}$.
