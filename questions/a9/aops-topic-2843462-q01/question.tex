A row of $56$ cells is coloured at random: each cell independently is red with probability $0.1$ and blue with probability $0.9$. Call a blue cell \emph{special} if the two cells immediately to its left and the two cells immediately to its right are all red (so the first two and last two cells cannot be special). The expected number of special cells is $\dfrac mn$ in lowest terms. If $m=\overline{abc}$ and $n=\overline{defgh}$ in base $10$, find the three-digit number $\overline{ead}$.
