Bryan visits a carnival booth where Carl shows him $10$ boxes. Exactly one of the boxes contains a gold coin; the other boxes are empty. Bryan randomly takes one of the boxes, but he does not open it. Carl, who knows where the coin is, then opens five of the other boxes that he knows are empty and shows Bryan that they are empty. Carl then tells Bryan that he can either keep his chosen box or return it and choose one of the remaining closed boxes instead. If Bryan returns his box and chooses one of the other closed boxes at random, what is the probability that he chooses the box with the gold coin? Express your answer as a common fraction.
