Equal remainders mean $\overline{abc}$ divides $4044-2025=2019=3\cdot673$, with $673$ prime. The divisors of $2019$ are $1,3,673,2019$, and the only three-digit one is $\boxed{673}$. Check: $2025=3\cdot673+6$ and $4044=6\cdot673+6$.
