In an isosceles triangle $ABC$ with $AB=AC$, let $D$ and $E$ be the perpendicular projections of $C$ onto line $AB$ and of $B$ onto line $AC$, respectively. Which statement always holds?
\begin{enumerate}
\item $BC^3<BD^3+BE^3$.
\item $BC^3=BD^3+BE^3$.
\item $BC^3>BD^3+BE^3$.
\item None of the preceding statements always holds.
\end{enumerate}
