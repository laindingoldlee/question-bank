The two altitudes $CD$ and $BE$ have equal lengths because the equal sides $AB,AC$ give the same triangle area. Pythagoras in $BCD$ therefore yields $BC^2=BD^2+BE^2$. Both $BD$ and $BE$ are positive in a nondegenerate isosceles triangle, so $BC$ exceeds each of them. Hence
\[
BC^3=BC(BD^2+BE^2)>BD^3+BE^3.
\]
Thus the correct statement is $\boxed{\text{3}}$.

\end{asy}
