Both terms are nonnegative, and their sum is $0$, so both are $0$:
\[
x+2y=0\qquad\text{and}\qquad 4x-7y-3=0 .
\]
From the first, $x=-2y$; substituting, $-8y-7y=3$, so $y=-\dfrac{1}{5}$ and $x=\dfrac{2}{5}$. Therefore
\[
x-y=\dfrac{2}{5}+\dfrac{1}{5}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{5}}.
\]
