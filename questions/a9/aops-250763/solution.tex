There are eight choices for the first digit (2 through 9), and ten choices for each remaining digit, giving $8\cdot10^6$ possible numbers. Requiring first digit 9 and last digit 0 leaves five free digits, giving $10^5$ numbers. Thus the fraction is $\dfrac{10^5}{8\cdot10^6}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{80}}$, choice B.
