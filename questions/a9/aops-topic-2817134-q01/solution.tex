By Bezout's identity there are integers $x,y$ with $ax+by=g_2$. Since $g_1$ divides $a$ and $b$, it divides $ax+by=g_2$. $\blacksquare$

Without the assumption $g_2=\gcd(a,b)$ the claim is false: with $a=b=6$, $g_1=2$ and $g_2=3$ are both common divisors, but $2\nmid3$.
