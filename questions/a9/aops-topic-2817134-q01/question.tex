Suppose $g_1\mid a$ and $g_1\mid b$, and let $g_2=\gcd(a,b)$, where $a,b$ are positive integers. Prove that $g_1\mid g_2$. Show by example that the conclusion can fail if $g_2$ is merely some other common divisor of $a$ and $b$.
