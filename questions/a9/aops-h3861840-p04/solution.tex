Before Jerry first turns at $t=30$ minutes, he stays ahead of Ben. At that time Ben has covered three quarters of the pool, so their separation is one quarter. They now approach at combined speed $\dfrac{1}{40}+\dfrac{1}{30}=\dfrac{7}{120}$ pool lengths per minute. The additional time is $\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{4}}{\dfrac{7}{120}}=\dfrac{30}{7}$ minutes. They meet at $\boxed{\dfrac{240}{7}}$ minutes, before Ben reaches his first end.
