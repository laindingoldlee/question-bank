The triangle inequality for vectors $(2k-1,a_k)$ gives
\[
\sum_{k=1}^n\sqrt{(2k-1)^2+a_k^2}\ge
\sqrt{\left(\sum_{k=1}^n(2k-1)\right)^2+17^2}
=\sqrt{n^4+289}.
\]
Equality holds by choosing $a_k=\dfrac{17(2k-1)}{n^2}$, which are positive and sum to $17$. Hence $S_n=\sqrt{n^4+289}$.

If this is an integer $m$, then $(m-n^2)(m+n^2)=289=17^2$. Both factors are positive integers. The pair $(17,17)$ gives $n=0$, whereas $(1,289)$ gives $n^2=144$. Therefore $\boxed{n=12}$ and $S_{12}=145$.
