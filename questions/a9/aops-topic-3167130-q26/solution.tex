Write $x^x=e^{x\ln x}$. The derivative of $x\ln x$ is $1+\ln x$, negative for $x<e^{-1}$ and positive for $x>e^{-1}$. Thus the minimum is attained at $x=e^{-1}$ and equals $e^{-\dfrac{1}{e}}$. Since $10000e^{-\dfrac{1}{e}}\approx6922.00628$, the answer is $\boxed{6922}$.
