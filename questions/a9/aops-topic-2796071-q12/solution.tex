Let the terms be $a-d,\ a,\ a+d,\ \dfrac{(a+d)^2}{a}$. Then $2a+d=24$ and $a-d+\dfrac{(a+d)^2}a=28$.

Substituting $d=24-2a$: $3a-24+\dfrac{(24-a)^2}{a}=28$, so $3a^2-24a+576-48a+a^2=28a$, i.e. $4a^2-100a+576=0$, $a^2-25a+144=0$, $a=9$ or $16$.

$a=9,d=6$: $3,9,15,25$. $a=16,d=-8$: $24,16,8,4$. Both consist of positive integers, so the sequences are $\boxed{(3,9,15,25)\text{ and }(24,16,8,4)}$.
