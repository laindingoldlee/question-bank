The numerator is always positive, so a positive quotient requires $ x>0 $. For $ 0<x\le17 $, it is $\dfrac{37}{x}-2 $, decreasing to $\dfrac{3}{17}$. For $ 17\le x\le20 $, it is $\dfrac{3}{x}$, decreasing to $\dfrac{3}{20}$. For $ x\ge20 $, it is $ 2-\dfrac{37}{x}$, increasing from that same value. Thus the attained minimum is $\boxed{\dfrac{3}{20}}$, at $ x=20 $.
