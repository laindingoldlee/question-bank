The medial triangle has one-quarter of the original area. The three corner triangles cut off by $PQR$ each have area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}$ times the corresponding side product and sine, hence $\dfrac{2}{9}[ABC]$. Thus $[PQR]=(1-3\cdot\dfrac{2}{9})[ABC]=\dfrac{1}{3}[ABC]$. The ratio is $\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{4}}{\dfrac{1}{3}}=\dfrac{3}{4}$, giving $\boxed7$.
