Alvin picks a random integer from $1$ to $100$ inclusive. Every minute he does the following. First, if the number is divisible by $4$, he divides it by $2$. Next, if the result has one digit he multiplies it by $7$, and if it has two digits he reverses its digits (a two-digit number ending in $0$ becomes a one-digit number; for example $30$ becomes $3$). After four minutes, what is the probability that Alvin's number is a one-digit prime?
