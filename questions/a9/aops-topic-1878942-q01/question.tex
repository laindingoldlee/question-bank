Real numbers $a$, $b$, $c$ satisfy
\[
\dfrac{abc}{a+b}=-\dfrac{1}{2},\qquad\dfrac{abc}{b+c}=\dfrac{1}{2},\qquad\dfrac{abc}{c+a}=1 .
\]
Find all possible values of $abc$.
