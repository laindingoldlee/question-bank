Let $k=abc$. Then $k\ne0$ (otherwise the first fraction would be $0$), and
\[
a+b=-2k,\qquad b+c=2k,\qquad c+a=k .
\]
Adding, $2(a+b+c)=k$, so $a+b+c=\dfrac {k}{2}$ and
\[
c=\dfrac {k}{2}+2k=\dfrac{5k}{2},\qquad a=\dfrac {k}{2}-2k=-\dfrac{3k}{2},\qquad b=\dfrac {k}{2}-k=-\dfrac {k}{2} .
\]
Then $k=abc=\left(-\dfrac{3k}{2}\right)\left(-\dfrac {k}{2}\right)\left(\dfrac{5k}{2}\right)=\dfrac{15k^3}{8}$, so $k^2=\dfrac{8}{15}$ and
\[
abc=\boxed{\pm\dfrac{2\sqrt{30}}{15}}.
\]
Both signs occur: for either value of $k$ the numbers $a=-\dfrac{3k}{2}$, $b=-\dfrac {k}{2}$, $c=\dfrac{5k}{2}$ satisfy all three equations (the denominators $-2k,2k,k$ are nonzero).
