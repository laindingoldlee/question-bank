Since $2008=8\cdot251=2^3\cdot251$ and $251$ is prime (it is not divisible by $2,3,5,7,11,13$, and $17^2>251$),
\[2008^5=2^{15}\cdot251^5,\]
a product of $15+5=20$ primes; by unique factorisation this is the only way. The sum is
\[15\cdot2+5\cdot251=30+1255=\boxed{1285}.\]
