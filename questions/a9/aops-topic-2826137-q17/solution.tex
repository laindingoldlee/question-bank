The nine non-A letters include two Ms and two Ts, so have $\dfrac{9!}{2!2!}$ arrangements. There are ten gaps around these letters; choose three distinct gaps for the three identical As, giving $N=\dfrac{9!}{2!2!}\binom{10}{3}=10886400$. Therefore $\dfrac{N}{100}=108864$, whose last three digits are $\boxed{864}$.
