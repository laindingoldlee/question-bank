Two children play a game with a box of $17$ sweets. They take turns, and on each turn a child eats $1$, $2$ or $3$ sweets. The child who eats the last sweet wins. The first child eats $3$ sweets on his first turn. How many sweets should the second child eat on his first turn to make sure that he wins, whatever the first child does afterwards?
