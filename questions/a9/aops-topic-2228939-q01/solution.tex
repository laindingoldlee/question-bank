Since $10\equiv3\pmod7$ and $3^6=729\equiv1\pmod7$ (Fermat's little theorem), $10^{N}\bmod7$ depends only on $N\bmod6$.

For $k\ge1$, $10^k$ is even and $10^k\equiv1\pmod3$, so $10^k\equiv4\pmod6$. Hence
\[10^{10^k}\equiv3^4=81\equiv4\pmod7\qquad\text{for every }k\ge1 .\]
The sum of the $52$ terms is congruent to $52\cdot4=208=7\cdot29+5$, so the remainder is $\boxed{5}$.
