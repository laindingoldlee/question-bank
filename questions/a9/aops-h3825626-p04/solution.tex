(a) Pair a designated player with any of the other $2n-1$, then repeat with the remaining $2n-2$ players. This gives
\[
\boxed{(2n-1)(2n-3)\cdots3\cdot1=\dfrac{(2n)!}{2^n n!}}.
\]
It is a product of odd integers and is therefore odd.

(b) By symmetry Bo's partner is uniform among the other $2n-1$ players, so the probability is $\dfrac{1}{2n-1}$. Hence $2n-1=23$ and $\boxed{n=12}$.
