A line $\ell$ and two points $A$ and $B$ not on $\ell$ are given in a plane, such that $A$ and $B$ are at different distances from $\ell$. Find the point $P$ on $\ell$ for which $|AP-PB|$ is as large as possible. Treat separately the cases where $A$ and $B$ are on the same side of $\ell$ and on opposite sides.
