Reduce coefficients modulo 2. The factors become $x$, $x$, $(x+1)^2=x^2+1$, $x^3$, and $x+1$. Their product is $x^5(x^2+1)(x+1)=x^8+x^7+x^6+x^5$ modulo 2. Exactly these four coefficients are odd, so the answer is $\boxed4$.
