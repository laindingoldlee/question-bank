The LCM condition makes the numbers pairwise coprime. Hence $\tau(a^2bc)=48\tau(a^2)$. The exponent patterns for $\tau(a)=12$ are $(11),(5,1),(3,2),(2,1,1)$, so $\tau(a^2)$ is respectively $23,33,35,45$. Each is attainable using disjoint primes for $a,b,c$. Their required sum is $48(23+33+35+45)=\boxed{6528}$.
