A contains $25$ mL of water; B contains $22.5$ mL. Their difference is $2.5$ mL. Relative to B, the percentage increase is $\dfrac{2.5}{22.5}\cdot100\%=\boxed{\dfrac{100}{9}\%}\approx11.1\%$. This differs from the $0.5$ percentage-point difference between concentrations.
