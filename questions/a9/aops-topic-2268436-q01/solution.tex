Removing the two points $-2$ and $2$ splits the number line into three open pieces: the numbers less than $-2$, the numbers strictly between $-2$ and $2$, and the numbers greater than $2$. Round brackets exclude the endpoints, and $\pm\infty$ always get round brackets. The set is
\[\boxed{(-\infty,-2)\cup(-2,2)\cup(2,\infty)}.\]
(For $f$: $x^2-4=0$ exactly when $x=\pm2$, so these are the only excluded values.)
