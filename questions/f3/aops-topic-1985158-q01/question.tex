A railroad and a highway cross at right angles at a point $O$. At a certain moment a train on the railroad is $10$ km from $O$ and a car on the highway is $6$ km from $O$. Both travel toward $O$ at $\dfrac{1}{2}$ km per minute and keep going straight at that speed. After how many minutes are they $4$ km apart for the first time?
