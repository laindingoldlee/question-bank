Victor is $17-12=5$ years old. In $x$ years Kate will be $17+x$ and Victor $5+x$, so
\[
17+x=2(5+x)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x=7 .
\]
Check: in $7$ years Kate is $24$ and Victor is $12$. The answer is $\boxed{7}$ years. (The poster's answer $10$ would give ages $27$ and $15$, and $27\ne30$.)
