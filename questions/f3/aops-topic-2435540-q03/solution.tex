Let the area be $A$ square inches and the perimeter $P$ inches, so $\dfrac{A}{P}=\dfrac54$. Since $1$ foot $=12$ inches, $1$ square foot $=144$ square inches. In feet, the area is $\dfrac{A}{144}$ square feet and the perimeter is $\dfrac{P}{12}$ feet, so the ratio is
\[\dfrac{\dfrac{A}{144}}{\dfrac{P}{12}}=\dfrac{A}{144}\cdot\dfrac{12}{P}=\dfrac{1}{12}\cdot\dfrac{A}{P}=\dfrac{1}{12}\cdot\dfrac54=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{48}}.\]
(The ratio area : perimeter is a length, so it is divided by $12$ when inches are replaced by feet.)
