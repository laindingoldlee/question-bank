Suppose $a,b>0$ and $c=a+b$. By the binomial theorem,
\[c^n=(a+b)^n=a^n+b^n+\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}\dbinom nk a^k b^{n-k}.\]
For $n\ge2$ the sum has at least one term ($k=1$), and every term is positive because $a,b>0$. Hence $c^n>a^n+b^n$, so $a^n+b^n=c^n$ is impossible. $\blacksquare$
