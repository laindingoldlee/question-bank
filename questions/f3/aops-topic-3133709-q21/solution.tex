There are at least 29 distinct directed fooling relationships ending among 14 recipients. Pigeonhole gives a recipient with at least $\lceil\dfrac{29}{14}\rceil=3$ incoming relationships. To show no higher guarantee, label students 0 through 29. For each $i=0,\ldots,28$, let $i$ fool recipient $(i+1)\bmod14$, and give student 29 no outgoing relationship. No edge is a self-edge; all 14 recipients appear, and each receives at most 3 edges. Thus $\boxed n=3$.
