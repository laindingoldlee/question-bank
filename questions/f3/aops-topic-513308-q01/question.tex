Automobile license plates for a state consist of four letters followed by a dash and two single digits. How many different license plates are possible if exactly one letter is repeated exactly once, but digits cannot be repeated?

A student argues: ``There are $26\cdot25\cdot24$ ways to choose the three letters (one of them used twice), $\dfrac{4!}{2!\,1!\,1!}=12$ ways to arrange the chosen letters and $10\cdot9=90$ ways to choose the digits, so there are $26\cdot25\cdot24\cdot12\cdot90=16\,848\,000$ plates.'' Find the error and give the correct count.
