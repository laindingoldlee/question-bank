The launch direction gives derivative $y'(22)=-1$, and the release point gives the convenient form
\[
y=a(x-22)^2-(x-22)+8.
\]
At $x=0$ the height is $10$, so $484a+30=10$ and $a=-\dfrac{5}{121}$. Expanding yields
\[
\boxed{y=-\dfrac{5}{121}x^2+\dfrac{9}{11}x+10}.
\]
