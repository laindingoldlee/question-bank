Let $H_n=1+\dfrac12+\dfrac13+\cdots+\dfrac1n$. Prove that $H_{2^k}\ge1+\dfrac k2$ for every integer $k\ge0$, and deduce that the harmonic series $1+\dfrac12+\dfrac13+\cdots$ diverges.
