For $j\ge1$, the block $\dfrac1{2^{j-1}+1}+\dfrac1{2^{j-1}+2}+\cdots+\dfrac1{2^j}$ has $2^{j-1}$ terms, each at least $\dfrac1{2^j}$, so the block is at least $2^{j-1}\cdot\dfrac1{2^j}=\dfrac12$. Splitting $H_{2^k}$ into the first term $1$ and the blocks $j=1,\dots,k$,
\[H_{2^k}\ge1+k\cdot\dfrac12.\]
For example $\dfrac13+\dfrac14\ge\dfrac14+\dfrac14=\dfrac12$ and $\dfrac15+\cdots+\dfrac18\ge4\cdot\dfrac18$.

Given any bound $B$, choose $k>2B$; then the partial sums eventually exceed $B$. Since all terms are positive, the partial sums increase without bound and the series diverges. $\blacksquare$
