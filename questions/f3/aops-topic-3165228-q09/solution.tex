For $n=6$, the number $111111=3\cdot7\cdot11\cdot13\cdot37$ is divisible by $11$ and $13$, so it works. For $n=1,2,3,4,5$, the repunits factor respectively as $1$, $11$, $3\cdot37$, $11\cdot101$, and $41\cdot271$, with the listed factors prime. The positive divisors of each contain no pair greater than one differing by two: for the semiprime cases, the only divisors besides one are the two prime factors and their product. Thus none of the earlier values works, and the minimum is $\boxed6$.
