Since $4=2^2$, replace each base 4 digit with its two-bit form: $1\mapsto 01$, $3\mapsto 11$, $2\mapsto 10$. Thus $(132)_4=(011110)_2=(11110)_2$. Since $8=2^3$, group in threes from the right: $011\,110$, giving $\boxed{(36)_8}$. Grouping works because moving one base 4 position multiplies by $2^2$, and moving one base 8 position multiplies by $2^3$.
