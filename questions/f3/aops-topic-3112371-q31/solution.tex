The initial surface area is $2(4s^2+4s^2+s^2)=18s^2=81$, so $s^2=\dfrac{9}{2}$. Every final face has smaller side $2s$, so any contained circle has diameter at most $2s$. This is attained on either a $2s\times2s$ or $4s\times2s$ face. The maximum disk area is $\pi s^2=\boxed{\dfrac{9\pi}{2}}$.

\end{asy}
