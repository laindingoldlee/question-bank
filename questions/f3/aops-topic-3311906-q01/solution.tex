For a monic quadratic with roots $r,s$, $(x-r)(x-s)=x^2-(r+s)x+rs$. Comparing with $x^2-5x+6$ gives $\boxed{r+s=5,\ rs=6}$. More generally, for $ax^2+bx+c$ with $a\ne0$, dividing by $a$ gives $r+s=-\dfrac{b}{a}$ and $rs=\dfrac{c}{a}$.
