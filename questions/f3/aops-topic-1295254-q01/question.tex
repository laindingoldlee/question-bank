Jamie, Linda and Don bought bundles of roses at a flower shop, all paying the same price per bundle. They took the bundles to a fair, where all three sold bundles at the same fixed price per bundle, higher than the shop's price. At the end of the fair they gave their unsold bundles away. Jamie bought $20$ bundles, sold $15$ and made a profit of $\$60$. Linda bought $34$ bundles, sold $24$ and made a profit of $\$69$. Don bought $40$ bundles and sold $36$. How many dollars of profit did Don make? (Profit is money received minus money paid.)
