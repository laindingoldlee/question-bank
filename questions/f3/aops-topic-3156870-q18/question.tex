For integers 1 through 99, let $ f(x)$ be twice the number of letters in the standard English name of $ x $, ignoring spaces and hyphens. Find the unordered pair of distinct integers $ x,y $ in this range with $ f(x)=y $ and $ f(y)=x $, and give $ x+y $.
