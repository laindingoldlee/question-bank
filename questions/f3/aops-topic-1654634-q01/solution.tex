Divide each number by $20$: the problem becomes choosing $x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4$ uniformly from $[0,1]$ and asking for $P(x_1+x_2+x_3+x_4\le1)$. The point $(x_1,\dots,x_4)$ is uniform in the unit $4$-dimensional cube, which has volume $1$, so the probability is the volume of the region
\[
x_i\ge0,\qquad x_1+x_2+x_3+x_4\le1 .
\]
This region is a $4$-dimensional simplex. Its volume is $\dfrac{1}{4!}$: in general the region $x_i\ge0$, $x_1+\cdots+x_n\le1$ has volume $\dfrac{1}{n!}$ (for $n=2$ it is a triangle of area $\dfrac{1}{2}$, for $n=3$ a tetrahedron of volume $\dfrac{1}{6}$, and each extra dimension integrates $\dfrac{(1-t)^{n-1}}{(n-1)!}$ over $0\le t\le1$, giving $\dfrac{1}{n!}$).

Hence the probability is
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{1}{24}}.
\]
