Pages 1 through 9 use 9 digits; pages 10 through 99 use $ 90\cdot2=180 $; pages 100 through 999 use $ 900\cdot3=2700 $. Altogether the first 999 pages use 2889 digits. The remaining 100 digits label $ 100\div4=25 $ four-digit page numbers, so the last page is $ 999+25=\boxed{1024}$.
