In how many ways can $m$ balls be put into $n$ boxes (boxes may be empty) if
(a) the balls and the boxes are distinguishable;
(b) the balls are identical and the boxes distinguishable;
(c) the balls are distinguishable and the boxes identical;
(d) the balls and the boxes are identical?
Check your answers for $m=4$, $n=3$.
