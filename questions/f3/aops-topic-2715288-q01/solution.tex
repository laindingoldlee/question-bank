\textbf{(a)} Each ball chooses a box: $\boxed{n^m}$.

\textbf{(b)} Stars and bars: $\boxed{\dbinom{m+n-1}{n-1}}$.

\textbf{(c)} We split the balls into at most $n$ nonempty unlabelled groups: $\boxed{\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n}S(m,k)}$, where the Stirling number $S(m,k)$ counts partitions of an $m$-set into $k$ nonempty blocks. It satisfies $S(m,k)=S(m-1,k-1)+kS(m-1,k)$, and $S(m,k)=\dfrac1{k!}\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{k}(-1)^j\dbinom kj(k-j)^m$ (inclusion--exclusion on surjections).

\textbf{(d)} We need the number $p_n(m)$ of partitions of $m$ into at most $n$ parts; it satisfies $p_n(m)=p_{n-1}(m)+p_n(m-n)$ (either fewer than $n$ parts, or subtract $1$ from each of exactly $n$ parts). There is no simple closed form.

For $m=4$, $n=3$: (a) $81$; (b) $\dbinom62=15$; (c) $S(4,1)+S(4,2)+S(4,3)=1+7+6=14$; (d) $4$ ($4$, $3+1$, $2+2$, $2+1+1$).
