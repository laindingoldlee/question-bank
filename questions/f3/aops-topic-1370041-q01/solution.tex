By the remainder theorem, the remainder when a polynomial $p(x)$ is divided by $x-a$ is $p(a)$. Here is why: write $p(x)=(x-a)q(x)+r$ with $r$ a constant (the remainder has degree less than $1$); putting $x=a$ gives $p(a)=r$.

With $p(x)=x^{13}+1$ and $a=1$, the remainder is $p(1)=1+1=\boxed{2}$.
