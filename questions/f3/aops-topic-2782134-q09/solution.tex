Numbers have square product exactly when their squarefree parts agree. Among 1 through 6, the classes are $\{1,4\},\{2\},\{3\},\{5\},\{6\}$. Hence $ 2^2+1+1+1+1=8 $ of 36 ordered outcomes work, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{2}{9}}$.
