Both sides are nonnegative, so the inequality is equivalent to the one between their squares:
\[
(x-3)^2<(x+5)^2\iff x^2-6x+9<x^2+10x+25\iff -16<16x\iff x>-1 .
\]
Geometrically, $|x-3|$ and $|x+5|$ are the distances from $x$ to $3$ and to $-5$ on the number line, and $x$ is closer to $3$ exactly when it lies to the right of the midpoint $-1$. The solution set is $\boxed{x>-1}$.
