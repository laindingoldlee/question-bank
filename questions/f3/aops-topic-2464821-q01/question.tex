A fair coin is tossed and a fair six-sided die is rolled.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the probability of getting heads and a $6$.
\item[(b)] Find the probability that the die shows a $5$ or a $6$.
\item[(c)] Find the probability of getting heads or a $6$ (or both).
\end{enumerate}
In each part, explain why the probabilities of the simpler events are multiplied, added, or combined in another way.
