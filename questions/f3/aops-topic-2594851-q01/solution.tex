Between the day before yesterday (age $25$) and his birthday next year (when he turns $28$), he must have birthdays on which he turns $26$, $27$ and $28$. The birthday on which he turns $28$ is next calendar year, so he turns $27$ this calendar year and turned $26$ last calendar year. But he was still $25$ the day before yesterday, so he turned $26$ yesterday or today, and this happened last calendar year. That is only possible if today is January $1$ and his birthday was yesterday, December $31$.

Check: on December $30$ he was $25$; on December $31$ he turned $26$; on December $31$ of the current year he will turn $27$; and on December $31$ of next year he will turn $28$.

He was born on $\boxed{\text{December }31}$, and the statement was made on January $1$.
