If an ordinary face has probability $p$, then $3p+1.2p=1$, so $p=\dfrac{5}{21}$ and $P(O)=\dfrac{6}{21}$. The requested probability is $p^4P(O)=\dfrac{5^4\cdot6}{21^5}=\boxed{\dfrac{1250}{1361367}}$.
