The triangle has area 6 and semiperimeter 6, so its inradius is 1. Reflecting the incenter across each side creates an outward triangle with that side as base and altitude 1. Their interiors are disjoint, and the hexagon is their union with $ABC$. Thus its area is $6+\dfrac{1}{2}(3+4+5)\cdot1=\boxed{12}$.
