A travelator moves at 1.5 metres per second. Grandma steps onto it at time 0 and stands still. Grandpa steps onto it 2 seconds later and walks forward at 1 metre per second relative to the travelator. At time 4 seconds, Grandson starts jogging alongside it at 2 metres per second. When Grandma is halfway between Grandpa, who is ahead, and Grandson, how far is Grandma from the start?
