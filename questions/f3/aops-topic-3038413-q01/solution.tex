Let $ t\ge4 $ be seconds since Grandma started. Their positions in metres are $\dfrac{3}{2}t $, $\dfrac{5}{2}(t-2)$, and $ 2(t-4)$. The midpoint condition is $ 2(\dfrac{3}{2}t)=\dfrac{5}{2}(t-2)+2(t-4)$, hence $ 3t=\dfrac{9}{2}t-13 $ and $ t=\dfrac{26}{3}$. Grandma is then $\boxed{13}$ metres from the start. The other positions are $\dfrac{50}{3}$ and $\dfrac{28}{3}$, respectively, confirming that Grandpa is ahead and Grandma is between them.
