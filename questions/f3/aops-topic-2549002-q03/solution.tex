The condition is $100x+10y+z+10x+y=100z+77$, i.e. $110x+11y=99z+77$, or
\[10x+y=9z+7.\]
Since $\overline{z77}$ is a three-digit number, $z\ge1$. For $z=1,\dots,9$, the two-digit number $\overline{xy}=9z+7$ is
\[16,\ 25,\ 34,\ 43,\ 52,\ 61,\ 70,\ 79,\ 88,\]
which determines $x$ and $y$. The numbers are
\[\boxed{161,\ 252,\ 343,\ 434,\ 525,\ 616,\ 707,\ 798,\ 889}.\]
(Check: $798+79=877$.)
