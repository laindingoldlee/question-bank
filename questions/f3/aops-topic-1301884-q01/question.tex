$ABCD$ is a convex quadrilateral with $AD=BC$ and $\angle BCD=\angle ADC$, and $E$ is the midpoint of $CD$. Which of the following must be true?

(A) $E$ lies on the perpendicular bisector of $AB$.\quad(B) $ABCD$ is a trapezium.\quad(C) $\angle CBA=\angle BAD$.\quad(D) Lines $AD$ and $BC$ meet at a right angle.
