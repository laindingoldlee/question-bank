Without loss of generality let $x<y$. Since $\ln2>0$, $(y-x)\ln2>0$, and the exponential of a positive real exceeds $1$. Thus $\dfrac{2^y}{2^x}=e^{(y-x)\ln2}>1$, giving $2^y>2^x$. Hence the two values cannot be equal.
