We need $N\equiv3\pmod7$ and $N\equiv5\pmod{11}$. The numbers congruent to $5$ mod $11$ are $5,16,27,38,\ldots$, with remainders $5,2,6,3,\ldots$ modulo $7$. The first with remainder $3$ is $38$. By the Chinese remainder theorem the solutions are exactly $N\equiv38\pmod{77}$, so the least positive number of students is $\boxed{38}$.
