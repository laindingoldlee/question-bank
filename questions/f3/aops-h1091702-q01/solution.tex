Completing the squares gives
\[
144(x+3)^2-25(y+2)^2=3600,
\qquad
\dfrac{(x+3)^2}{25}-\dfrac{(y+2)^2}{144}=1.
\]
The center is $(-3,-2)$ and the focal distance is $\sqrt{25+144}=13$. Thus the foci are $(10,-2)$ and $(-16,-2)$, while the conjugate axis is $x=-3$.

For focus $(10,-2)$, equality of squared distances to the focus and directrix gives
\[
(x-10)^2+(y+2)^2=(x+3)^2,
\]
hence $(y+2)^2=26x-91$. For focus $(-16,-2)$ the same calculation gives $(y+2)^2=-26x-247$. Therefore the two parabolas are
\[
\boxed{(y+2)^2=26x-91\quad\text{and}\quad(y+2)^2=-26x-247}.
\]
