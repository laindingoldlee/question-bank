The product is positive, so either all three integers are positive or exactly two are negative; two negative consecutive odd integers next to a positive one would be $-3,-1,1$, with product $3$. So all three are positive. Factor:
\[
4845=5\cdot969=3\cdot5\cdot17\cdot19=15\cdot17\cdot19 .
\]
The integers are $15,17,19$ (the middle one is close to $\sqrt[3]{4845}\approx16.9$), and the largest is $\boxed{19}$.
