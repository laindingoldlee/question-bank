Joining the point to the vertices partitions the triangle into three triangles with common base length $s$ and heights 1,3,5. Thus its area is $\dfrac{1}{2}s(1+3+5)=\dfrac{9}{2}s$. Comparing with $\dfrac{1}{2}sh$ gives altitude $h=9$, hence $s=6\sqrt3$. The area is $\boxed{27\sqrt3}$.
