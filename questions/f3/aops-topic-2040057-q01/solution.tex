By the Euclidean algorithm, \(\gcd(936,588)=12\):
\(936=588+348\), \(588=348+240\), \(348=240+108\), \(240=2\cdot108+24\), \(108=4\cdot24+12\), \(24=2\cdot12\).
Dividing by \(12\), the equation becomes
\[
78x+49y=1 .
\]
A particular solution: \(78\cdot22=1716=49\cdot35+1\), so \((x,y)=(22,-35)\) works. If \((x,y)\) is any solution, then \(78(x-22)=-49(y+35)\); since \(\gcd(78,49)=1\), \(49\mid x-22\). Writing \(x=22+49t\) gives \(y=-35-78t\). Hence all solutions are
\[
\boxed{(x,y)=(22+49t,\ -35-78t),\quad t\in\mathbb{Z}}.
\]
