$\angle C=180^\circ-30^\circ-105^\circ=45^\circ$. By the law of sines,
\[BC=\dfrac{AB\sin A}{\sin C}=\dfrac{3\cdot\dfrac12}{\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}}=\dfrac{3\sqrt2}{2}.\]
With $\sin105^\circ=\sin\left(60^\circ+45^\circ\right)=\dfrac{\sqrt6+\sqrt2}{4}$,
\[[ABC]=\dfrac12\cdot AB\cdot BC\cdot\sin B=\dfrac12\cdot3\cdot\dfrac{3\sqrt2}{2}\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt6+\sqrt2}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{9\left(\sqrt3+1\right)}{8}}.\]
