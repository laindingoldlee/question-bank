(a) Since $e>2$, we have $e^3+e^5+e^7<3e^7<e^{19}$, as $e^{12}>2^{12}>3$. Thus the sum is less than $2e^{19}<e^{20}$.
(b) Divide by $e^{17}$. We must compare $2+2e+2e^2$ with $e^3$. The function $F(t)=t^3-2t^2-2t-2$ is increasing for $t\ge2$, since $F'(t)=3t^2-4t-2\ge2>0$ there. Moreover $2<e<\dfrac{11}{4}$: the exponential series gives
\[e<1+1+\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{6}+\dfrac{1}{24}\sum_{j=0}^\infty\left(\dfrac{1}{5}\right)^j=\dfrac{87}{32}<\dfrac{11}{4}.\]
Hence $F(e)<F(\dfrac{11}{4})=-\dfrac{117}{64}<0$. Therefore the sum in (b) is $\boxed{\text{greater than }e^{20}}$, whereas that in (a) is $\boxed{\text{less}}$.
