The chords are equal, so they are symmetric about the centre $O$, each at distance $4$ from $O$. Each chord has half-length $\sqrt{8^2-4^2}=4\sqrt3$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real R=8; draw(circle((0,0),R)); real h=4,w=sqrt(R^2-h^2);
fill((-w,h)--(w,h)--arc((0,0),R,30,-30)--(w,-h)--(-w,-h)--arc((0,0),R,210,150)--cycle,lightblue);
draw((-w,h)--(w,h),blue); draw((-w,-h)--(w,-h),blue); draw((0,0)--(w,h),red); draw((0,0)--(w,-h),red); draw((0,0)--(-w,h),red); draw((0,0)--(-w,-h),red);
label("$8$",(w/2,h/2),NW); dot((0,0)); draw((0,-h)--(0,h),gray+dashed); label("$8$",(0,-h/2),W,gray);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Join $O$ to the four endpoints. The radii to the ends of one chord make an angle of $120^\circ$ (since $\cos\dfrac{\theta}{2}=\dfrac{4}{8}$), so the region consists of
\begin{itemize}
\item two isosceles triangles with base $8\sqrt3$ and height $4$, total area $2\cdot16\sqrt3=32\sqrt3$;
\item two circular sectors of angle $60^\circ$ on the left and right, total area $\dfrac{120}{360}\cdot64\pi=\dfrac{64\pi}{3}$.
\end{itemize}
The area is $\boxed{32\sqrt3+\dfrac{64\pi}{3}}$ square inches. The poster's answer is correct.
