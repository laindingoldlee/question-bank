Three students start at the same time. $A$ starts at $1509$ and adds $3$ each step; $B$ starts at $2019$ and subtracts $7$ each step; $C$ starts at $X$ and adds $1$ at the first step, $2$ at the second, $3$ at the third, and so on. After $N$ steps all three have the same number. Find $X$.
