Measure meat in deer. By (5) a tiger eats $1$ deer, and by (3) a lion eats $2$. With $n$ lions and $n$ tigers, (1) and (2) give $2n+n=18$, so $n=6$. There are $\boxed{6\text{ lions and }6\text{ tigers}}$.

Fact (4) is not needed; it only says that a buffalo provides $3$ deer of meat, which is consistent.
