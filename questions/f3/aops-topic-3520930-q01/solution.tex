If year $N$ has $L$ days, the two given dates are $L-100$ days apart. This must be divisible by $7$. Of $365-100=265$ and $366-100=266$, only $266$ is divisible by $7$. Thus $N$ is leap and $N-1$ is common. From day $100$ of $N-1$ to day $300$ of $N$ there are $365+200=565\equiv5\pmod7$ days. Five days before Tuesday is $\boxed{\text{Thursday}}$.
