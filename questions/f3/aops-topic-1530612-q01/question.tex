Two right circular cones share a common vertex and a common axis, and their bases are parallel: the smaller cone is the part of the larger cone cut off by a plane parallel to the base. The ratio of the base radius of the smaller cone to the base radius of the larger cone is $3:5$. What is the ratio of the volume of the smaller cone to the volume of the larger cone? Express your answer as a common fraction.
