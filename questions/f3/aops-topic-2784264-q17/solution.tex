Enumerate the $7!$ permutations of the seven distinct objects. Each permutation is counted exactly once and retained precisely when all three stated conditions hold. The following complete calculation gives $\boxed{128}$ valid orders.\begin{verbatim}
from itertools import permutations
count=0
for p in permutations("HFSMQXY"):
    if (p.index("F")==p.index("H")+1
        and p.index("S") in (3,4)
        and abs(p.index("M")-p.index("Q"))!=1):
        count+=1
assert count==128
\end{verbatim}
