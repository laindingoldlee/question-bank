Expanding, $(x+m)^2=x^2+2mx+m^2$. Two polynomials are equal exactly when their coefficients are equal, so
\[
b=2m,\qquad c=m^2 .
\]
From $c=m^2$ we get $c\ge0$ and $m=\pm\sqrt c$, hence $b=2m=\pm2\sqrt c$. $\blacksquare$

(Equivalently, $b^2=4c$: the discriminant $b^2-4c$ is zero. For example, $x^2-6x+9=(x-3)^2$ has $b=-6=-2\sqrt9$.)
