Let $p$ be the chosen vertex vector from the center and $v_1,\ldots,v_{22}$ all the vertex vectors. They have length $3$ and sum to zero by symmetry. Including the zero distance from $p$ to itself,
\[
\sum_{j=1}^{22}|p-v_j|^2
=22|p|^2+\sum_{j=1}^{22}|v_j|^2-2p\cdot\sum_{j=1}^{22}v_j
=22\cdot9+22\cdot9=\boxed{396}.
\]

\end{asy}
