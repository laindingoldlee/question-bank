Rearranging and factoring gives $(a-1)(b-2)(c-3)=2018=2\cdot1009$. Since $a$ is positive, $a-1$ must be positive. If the other two factors are positive, the independent placement of the primes $2$ and $1009$ among three factors gives $3^2=9$ ordered triples. If both are negative, then $b-2=-1$ and $c-3=-1$ or $-2$; each case determines a positive integer $a-1$, giving two more triples. Zero factors or opposite signs are impossible. The answer is $\boxed{11}$.
