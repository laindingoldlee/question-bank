Bring the difference $D=\dfrac {a}{b}+\dfrac {b}{c}+\dfrac {c}{a}-\dfrac{a+b}{a+c}-\dfrac{a+c}{b+c}-\dfrac{b+c}{a+b}$ to the common denominator $abc(a+b)(b+c)(c+a)>0$. Expanding (a routine but careful computation), the numerator is
\[
N=\bigl(a^4c^2+a^2b^4+b^2c^4-3a^2b^2c^2\bigr)+\bigl(a^3b^3+b^3c^3+c^3a^3-a^3bc^2-a^2b^3c-ab^2c^3\bigr).
\]
The first bracket is nonnegative by AM-GM, since the product of $a^4c^2$, $a^2b^4$, $b^2c^4$ is $(a^2b^2c^2)^3$.

For the second bracket put $x=ab$, $y=bc$, $z=ca$. Then $a^3bc^2=xz^2$, $a^2b^3c=x^2y$, $ab^2c^3=y^2z$, and the bracket is
\[
x^3+y^3+z^3-xz^2-x^2y-y^2z .
\]
By AM-GM, $xz^2\le\dfrac{x^3+2z^3}{3}$, $x^2y\le\dfrac{2x^3+y^3}{3}$ and $y^2z\le\dfrac{2y^3+z^3}{3}$; adding gives $xz^2+x^2y+y^2z\le x^3+y^3+z^3$.

So $N\ge0$, hence $D\ge0$, which is the inequality. Equality holds when $a=b=c$. $\blacksquare$
