Write $a=AB,c=BC$. The angle-bisector length formula is $BD=\dfrac{2ac\cos60^\circ}{a+c}=\dfrac{ac}{a+c}$. It follows either by adding the areas of $ABD,BCD$ and using the angle-bisector theorem, or directly from the sine rule. Substituting gives $10=\dfrac{ac}{49}$, hence $\boxed{ac=490}$.
