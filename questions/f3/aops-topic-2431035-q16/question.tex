As Emily is riding her bicycle on a long straight road, she spots Emerson skating in the same direction $\dfrac{1}{2}$ mile in front of her. After she passes him, she can see him in her rear mirror until he is $\dfrac{1}{2}$ mile behind her. Emily rides at a constant rate of $12$ miles per hour, and Emerson skates at a constant rate of $8$ miles per hour. For how many minutes can Emily see Emerson?
