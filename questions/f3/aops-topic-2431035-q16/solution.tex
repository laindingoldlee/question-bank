Relative to Emerson, Emily moves forward at $12-8=4$ miles per hour. She sees him from the moment he is $\dfrac{1}{2}$ mile ahead of her until he is $\dfrac{1}{2}$ mile behind her, so in that time she gains $\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{2}=1$ mile on him. This takes $\dfrac{1}{4}$ hour, which is $\boxed{15}$ minutes.
