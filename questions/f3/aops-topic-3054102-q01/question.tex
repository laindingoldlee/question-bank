A rectangle has width 1 and length $\sqrt3 $. On its two long edges, mark points $(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3},0)$ and $(\dfrac{2\sqrt3}{3},1)$. Fold the right-hand part flat across the line joining these points. If the original area is $ A $ and the area covered after folding is $ B $, find $\dfrac{B}{A}$.
