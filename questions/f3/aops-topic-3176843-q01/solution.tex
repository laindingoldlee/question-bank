On day $d$, the probability all 100 sensors remain quiet is $\left(1-\dfrac{1}{200\cdot2^{d-1}}\right)^{100}$. Multiply these independent no-alarm probabilities over the seven days and take the complement. The answer is $\boxed{1-\displaystyle\prod_{d=1}^{7}\left(1-\dfrac{1}{200\cdot2^{d-1}}\right)^{100}}$.
