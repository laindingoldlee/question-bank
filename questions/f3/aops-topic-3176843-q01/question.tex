There are 100 independent sensors. On day $d=1,2,\ldots,7$, each sensor independently triggers a false alarm with probability $\dfrac{1}{200\cdot2^{d-1}}$. All sensor-day events are independent. Find the probability of at least one false alarm during the seven days.
