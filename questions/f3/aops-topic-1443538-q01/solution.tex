The horizontal change from $B$ to $A$ is $4-(-1)=5$ and the vertical change is $-7-5=-12$. By the distance formula (the Pythagorean theorem in the right triangle shown),
\[
AB=\sqrt{5^2+12^2}=\sqrt{169}=\boxed{13}.
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(4,-7),B=(-1,5),Cc=(4,5);
draw((-2.5,0)--(5.5,0),gray,Arrow); draw((0,-8)--(0,6.5),gray,Arrow);
draw(A--B,blue+linewidth(0.9)); draw(B--Cc--A,red+dashed);
dot(A);dot(B);dot(Cc,red);
label("$A(4,-7)$",A,SE); label("$B(-1,5)$",B,NW); label("$(4,5)$",Cc,NE,red);
label("$5$",(B+Cc)/2,N,red); label("$12$",(4,-2.5),E,red); label("$13$",(A+B)/2,SW,blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
