Three fair coins are tossed. What is the probability that all three show the same face (all heads or all tails)? A student argues: "Whatever happens, two of the coins show the same face. The third coin matches them with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, so the answer is $\dfrac{1}{2}$." Find the error.
