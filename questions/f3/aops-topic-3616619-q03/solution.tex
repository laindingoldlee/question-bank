Scale the common short side to 1. Symmetry of the isosceles trapezoid gives $A=(0,0)$, $D=(2,0)$, $B=(\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt 3}{2})$, $C=(\dfrac{3}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt 3}{2})$. Now $AC^2=3$ and $CD^2=1$, while $AD^2=4$. Thus $AC^2+CD^2=AD^2$, so triangle ACD is right at C and $\boxed{\angle ACD=90^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
import geometry; size(220);
pair A=(0,0),B=(0.5,sqrt(3)/2),C=(1.5,sqrt(3)/2),D=(2,0);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C,dashed);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,NW);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,SE);
\end{asy}
