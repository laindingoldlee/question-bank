Let $w$ and $z$ be complex numbers with $\operatorname{Re}(z)\ne\operatorname{Re}(w)$, plotted as points in the complex plane. Prove that the line through them has slope
\[
\dfrac{\operatorname{Im}(z-w)}{\operatorname{Re}(z-w)} .
\]
