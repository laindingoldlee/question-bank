Write $N=7a+2$. Then $7a\equiv2\pmod9$, so $a\equiv8\pmod9$, since $7\cdot8\equiv2$. Thus $N\equiv58\pmod{63}$. The largest three-digit possibility is $58+63\cdot14=\boxed{940}$; the next is $1003$.
