Numbers $a,b,c$ satisfy $a+b+c=6$, $a^2+b^2+c^2=26$ and $a^3+b^3+c^3=84$. Find the value of
\[
P=\left(a^2+ab+b^2\right)\left(b^2+bc+c^2\right)\left(c^2+ca+a^2\right).
\]
