Since $2002=2\cdot7\cdot11\cdot13$, a qualifying number can use only digits $1,2,7$, with at most one $2$ and at most one $7$. Zero is forbidden because zero is not a positive divisor.
For lengths one, two, and three, the counts are respectively $3$, $1+2+2+2=7$, and $1+3+3+6=13$, according to whether a $2$ and/or $7$ occurs. A four-digit number below $2002$ must start with $1$: any permitted number starting with $2$ is at least $2111$. Its remaining three positions have the same thirteen choices. Thus the total is $\boxed{3+7+13+13=36}$.
