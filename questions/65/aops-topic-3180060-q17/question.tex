Two integer bases are used by a club. A speaker using base $b$ says: ``13 people use my base;23 use the other base, which I write as 12.'' A second speaker, using one of those two bases, says: ``There are 34 members;10 use both bases and 9 use neither.'' Every numeral is interpreted in its speaker's base, and both statements are true. Find the total club membership in decimal.
