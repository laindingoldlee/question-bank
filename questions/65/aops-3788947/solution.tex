An exact finite recurrence is obtained by retaining the last two entries. For a subset $S\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\}$ and distinct $a,b\in S$, let $D(S,a,b)$ count valid orderings of $S$ ending in $a,b$. Initialise $D(\{a,b\},a,b)=1$. For $|S|\ge3$, use $D(S,a,b)=\sum_{c\in S\setminus\{a,b\},\ \neg(c<a<b)}D(S\setminus\{b\},c,a)$. Every valid ordering has exactly one predecessor obtained by deleting its last entry, and the only new forbidden triple is $(c,a,b)$, proving the recurrence. The desired answer is $\boxed{f(n)=\sum_{a\ne b}D(\{1,\ldots,n\},a,b)}$. It yields $f(1)=1,f(2)=2,f(3)=5,f(4)=17$.
