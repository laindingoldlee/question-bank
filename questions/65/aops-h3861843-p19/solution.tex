Each of the four terms is nondecreasing on $[0,6]$, and the graph is a step function. Its horizontal pieces cover total width six. At every jump the shortest connection is vertical, and all jumps are upward, so their total length telescopes to $f(6)-f(0)=10+20+40+80=150$. Hence the minimum pen travel is $\boxed{156}$. Values at isolated endpoints of steps lie between the one-sided limits and add no extra distance.
