One- and two-digit integers occupy $9+90\cdot2=189$ places. Thus positions 2024 through 2026 correspond to positions 1835 through 1837 in the three-digit block. Since $1835=3\cdot611+2$, the first is the second digit of $100+611=711$. The next is its third digit, and the last is the first digit of 712. The answer is $\boxed{117}$.
