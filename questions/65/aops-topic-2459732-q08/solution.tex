Only one face is hidden. The faces $3$ and $6$ cannot both be hidden, so a multiple of $3$ is always visible; likewise the even faces $2,4,6$ cannot all be hidden, so an even number is always visible. Hence the product of the visible faces is always a multiple of $2\cdot3=6$. (Directly: the product of all six faces is $720$, and $\dfrac{720}{k}$ is $720,360,240,180,144,120$ for $k=1,\dots,6$.) The probability is $\boxed{1}$.
