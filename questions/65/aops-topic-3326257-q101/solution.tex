The side lengths are proportional to the reciprocals of the altitudes. The triangle inequalities are therefore $\left|\dfrac{1}{18}-\dfrac{1}{24}\right|<\dfrac{1}{h}<\dfrac{1}{18}+\dfrac{1}{24}$, giving $\dfrac{1}{72}<\dfrac{1}{h}<\dfrac{7}{72}$, or $\dfrac{72}{7}<h<72$. Thus the greatest integer possibility is $\boxed{71}$. Conversely the reciprocal lengths for $18,24,71$ satisfy strict triangle inequalities and define a triangle; scaling it appropriately makes its altitudes exactly those three values.
