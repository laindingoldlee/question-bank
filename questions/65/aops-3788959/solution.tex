The total coefficient sum is $P(1)=\left(\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}\right)^2$. For $2\le k\le n$, the coefficient of $x^k$ is $a_k=\sum_{i=1}^{k-1}i(k-i)=\dfrac{k(k^2-1)}{6}$. Consequently $\sum_{k=0}^{n}a_k=\dfrac{1}{6}\left(\sum_{k=1}^n k^3-\sum_{k=1}^n k\right)=\dfrac{n(n+1)(n-1)(n+2)}{24}$. Subtracting from the total yields $\boxed{\sum_{k=n+1}^{2n}a_k=\dfrac{n(n+1)(5n^2+5n+2)}{24}}$.
