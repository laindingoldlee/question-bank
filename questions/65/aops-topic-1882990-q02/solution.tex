In a $30^\circ$-$60^\circ$-$90^\circ$ triangle the shorter leg is half the hypotenuse and the longer leg is $\sqrt3$ times the shorter leg. So the legs are $6$ and $6\sqrt3$ centimetres.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(6*sqrt(3),0),C=(0,6);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(1));
draw((0.6,0)--(0.6,0.6)--(0,0.6));
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,N);
label("$6$",(0,3),W);label("$6\sqrt3$",(3*sqrt(3),0),S);label("$12$",(B+C)/2,NE);
label("$30^\circ$",B+(-2.4,0.5));label("$60^\circ$",C+1.6*dir(-60));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The area of the triangle is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot6\cdot6\sqrt3=18\sqrt3$ square centimetres. So the circle has diameter $18\sqrt3$ cm and radius $9\sqrt3$ cm.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0);real r=9*sqrt(3);
draw(circle(O,r),linewidth(1));
draw((-r,0)--(r,0),dashed);dot(O);dot((-r,0));dot((r,0));
label("$d=18\sqrt3$",(0,0),N);label("$r=9\sqrt3$",(r/2,0),S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Its area is $\pi\left(9\sqrt3\right)^2=\boxed{243\pi}$ square centimetres.
