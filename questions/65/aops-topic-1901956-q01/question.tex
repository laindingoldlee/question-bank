Let $f(x)=\sqrt{x-2}$ on its natural domain.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the domain and the range of $f$.
\item[(b)] Find the inverse function $f^{-1}$, including its domain and range.
\item[(c)] Explain why the function $g(x)=x^2+2$ defined for all real $x$ is not the inverse of $f$.
\end{enumerate}
