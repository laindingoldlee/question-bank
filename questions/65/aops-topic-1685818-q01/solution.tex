Add $1$ to both sides and factor:
\[
xy+x+y+1=(x+1)(y+1)=20 .
\]
Both factors are at least $2$, so $(x+1,y+1)$ is one of $(2,10)$, $(4,5)$, $(5,4)$, $(10,2)$. Hence
\[
(x,y)=\boxed{(1,9),\ (3,4),\ (4,3),\ (9,1)}.
\]
