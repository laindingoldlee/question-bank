The ratio does not depend on the size of the square, so let the side be $4$: $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $C=(4,4)$, $D=(0,4)$, $M=(4,2)$.

\textbf{Locating $S$.} Since $\angle AMS=90^\circ$, $\angle AMB+\angle SMC=90^\circ=\angle AMB+\angle BAM$, so $\angle SMC=\angle BAM$. The right triangles $ABM$ and $MCS$ are therefore similar, and
\[\dfrac{CS}{MC}=\dfrac{BM}{AB}=\dfrac12,\qquad CS=1,\qquad DS=3.\]

\textbf{The two right triangles.} Triangle $AMS$ has a right angle at $M$, legs $AM=\sqrt{4^2+2^2}=2\sqrt5$, $MS=\sqrt{2^2+1^2}=\sqrt5$ and hypotenuse $AS=\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5$. Triangle $ADS$ has a right angle at $D$, legs $4$ and $3$, hypotenuse $5$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0), B=(4,0), C=(4,4), D=(0,4), M=(4,2), Sp=(3,4);
real a=abs(M-Sp), bb=abs(Sp-A), c=abs(A-M);
pair I1=(a*A+bb*M+c*Sp)/(a+bb+c); real r1=(3*sqrt(5)-5)/2;
pair I2=(1,3);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
draw(A--M--Sp--cycle,blue+linewidth(0.8));
draw(circle(I1,r1),red); draw(circle(I2,1),deepgreen);
pair e1=unit(A-M), e2=unit(Sp-M); real s=0.22;
draw(M+s*e1--M+s*e1+s*e2--M+s*e2);
dot(M); dot(Sp); dot(I1); dot(I2);
label("$A$",A,SW); label("$B$",B,SE); label("$C$",C,NE); label("$D$",D,NW); label("$M$",M,E); label("$S$",Sp,N);
label("$O$",I1,S,red); label("$R$",I2,S,deepgreen);
label("$2$",(4,1),E); label("$2$",(4,3),E); label("$1$",(3.5,4),N); label("$3$",(1.5,4),N);
\end{asy}

In a right triangle with legs $u,v$ and hypotenuse $w$, the inradius is $\dfrac{u+v-w}{2}$ (the tangent lengths from the right-angle vertex both equal the inradius). So
\[r_O=\dfrac{2\sqrt5+\sqrt5-5}{2}=\dfrac{3\sqrt5-5}{2},\qquad r_R=\dfrac{4+3-5}{2}=1.\]
The ratio of the areas is
\[\dfrac{\pi r_O^2}{\pi r_R^2}=\left(\dfrac{3\sqrt5-5}{2}\right)^2=\dfrac{70-30\sqrt5}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{35-15\sqrt5}{2}}\approx0.729.\]
