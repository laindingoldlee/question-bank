Extend four alternate sides to form a square. Each corner cut off is an isosceles right triangle whose hypotenuse is a side of the octagon, so its legs are $\dfrac{5}{\sqrt2}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=1; real t=s/sqrt(2);
pair[] P={(t,0),(t+s,0),(2t+s,t),(2t+s,t+s),(t+s,2t+s),(t,2t+s),(0,t+s),(0,t)};
draw((0,0)--(2t+s,0)--(2t+s,2t+s)--(0,2t+s)--cycle,gray+dashed);
draw(P[0]--P[1]--P[2]--P[3]--P[4]--P[5]--P[6]--P[7]--cycle,linewidth(1));
label("$5$",(P[0]+P[1])/2,S); label("$\dfrac{5}{\sqrt2}$",(t/2,0),S,gray); label("$\dfrac{5}{\sqrt2}$",(0,t+s+t/2),W,gray);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The square has side $5+2\cdot\dfrac{5}{\sqrt2}=5+5\sqrt2$, and the four corners have total area $4\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{25}{2}=25$. Hence
\[
[\text{octagon}]=(5+5\sqrt2)^2-25=25(3+2\sqrt2)-25=\boxed{50+50\sqrt2}\approx120.7 .
\]
(In general a regular octagon of side $s$ has area $2(1+\sqrt2)s^2$.)
