MATHCOUNTS has ten letters: eight distinct non-T letters and two copies of T. With zero Ts there are $8\cdot7\cdot6\cdot5=1680$ strings; with one T there are $4\cdot8\cdot7\cdot6=1344$; with two Ts there are $\binom42\cdot8\cdot7=336$. Thus the total is $\boxed{3360}$. The three cases are disjoint and exhaust every permitted string.
