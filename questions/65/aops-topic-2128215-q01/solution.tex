Note that $3003=3\cdot1001$, $7007=7\cdot1001$ and $10010=10\cdot1001$. Dividing by $1001$ and multiplying by $3$ gives the equivalent equation
\[9x^2-15x+7=0 .\]
By Vieta's formulas the roots $r,s$ satisfy $r+s=\dfrac{15}{9}=\dfrac{5}{3}$ and $rs=\dfrac{7}{9}$. Hence
\[r^3+s^3=(r+s)^3-3rs(r+s)=\dfrac{125}{27}-3\cdot\dfrac{7}{9}\cdot\dfrac{5}{3}=\dfrac{125}{27}-\dfrac{105}{27}=\boxed{\dfrac{20}{27}}.\]
(The discriminant $225-252$ is negative, so the roots are non-real complex conjugates; the identity and Vieta's formulas still apply.)
