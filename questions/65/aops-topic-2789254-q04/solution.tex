$\boxed{\text{Yes}}$. Weighing 1: $Q_1,Q_2,D_1$ against $Q_3,Q_4,D_2$ (each side holds the same kinds of coins).
\begin{itemize}
\item If the left is lighter, the fake is $Q_1$, $Q_2$ or $D_1$: weigh $Q_1$ against $Q_2$; the lighter is fake, and if they balance $D_1$ is fake. The right lighter is symmetric.
\item If they balance, the fake is $Q_5$, $D_3$ or the nickel $N$, and $Q_1,D_1$ are genuine. Weigh $Q_5+D_1$ against $Q_1+D_3$: left lighter means $Q_5$ is fake, right lighter means $D_3$, and balance means $N$.
\end{itemize}
