A bag contains $10$ game consoles, each of which is either a PlayStation 5 or a Nintendo Switch, and at least one of them is a Switch. Two consoles are drawn at random without replacement. The probability of drawing two Switches is greater than or equal to the probability of drawing one Switch and one PlayStation 5. How many different numbers of PlayStation 5's could be in the bag?
