Let $f:\mathbb Z\to\mathbb Z$, $a\in\mathbb Z$, and $c$ be a positive integer. (a) Prove that two of $a,f(a),\ldots,f^c(a)$ have the same residue modulo $c$. (b) If $u\equiv v\pmod c$ always implies $f(u)\equiv f(v)\pmod c$, prove that the residue sequence is eventually periodic.
