A number divisible by $5$ ends in $0$ or $5$; there is no $5$ among the digits, so the last digit is $0$. The first four digits are then an ordered choice of $4$ of the remaining $5$ digits $1,3,4,7,9$ (none of which is $0$, so the leading digit is fine): $5\cdot4\cdot3\cdot2=\boxed{120}$.
