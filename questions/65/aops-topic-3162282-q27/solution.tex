Put $ a=xy,b=yz,c=zx $ and label the largest $ a $. Then $ a+b+c=20 $ and $ ab+bc+ca=100 $. Hence $ b+c=20-a $ and $ bc=100-a(20-a)=(a-10)^2 $. The inequality $ 4bc\le(b+c)^2 $ gives $ 4(a-10)^2\le(20-a)^2 $, or $ a\le\dfrac{40}{3}$. Equality is attainable at $ a=\dfrac{40}{3}$, $ b=c=\dfrac{10}{3}$; positive pair-products always recover positive $ x,y,z $. The smallest variable is then $\sqrt{\dfrac{bc}{a}}=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt{30}}{6}}$.
