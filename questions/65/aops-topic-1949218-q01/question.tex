Let triangle $ABC$ have side lengths $a=BC$, $b=CA$, $c=AB$, semiperimeter $s=\dfrac{a+b+c}{2}$, area $K$, inradius $r$ and circumradius $R$. Prove that
\[
K=rs\qquad\text{and}\qquad K=\dfrac{abc}{4R}.
\]
