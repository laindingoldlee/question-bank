Let a pencil cost $p$ and a pen $q$. From $2p+3q=5$ and $13p+8q=21$: multiplying the first by $8$ and the second by $3$ and subtracting gives $16p-39p=40-63$, so $p=1$ and then $q=1$. The cost is $34+55=89$. Answer $\boxed{\textbf{(D)}\ 89}$.
