A set $S=\{a_1,a_2,\dots,a_r\}$ of positive integers with $a_1<a_2<\dots<a_r$ is called $m$-\emph{separated} (where $m$ is a positive integer) if $a_i-a_{i-1}\ge m$ for each $i=2,3,\dots,r$. Let $X=\{1,2,\dots,n\}$ and let $r\ge1$ with $r\le n-(m-1)(r-1)$. Find the number of $r$-element subsets of $X$ that are $m$-separated.
