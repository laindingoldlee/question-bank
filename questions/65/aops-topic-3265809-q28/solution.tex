Pair divisors around $\sqrt k$ to get $d(k)\le2\sqrt k$. The condition therefore implies $\dfrac{k}{2}\le2\sqrt k$, hence $k\le16$. The divisor counts for $k=1,\ldots,16$ are
\[1,2,2,3,2,4,2,4,3,4,2,6,2,4,4,5.\]
Comparing them with $\dfrac{k}{2}$ gives exactly $k=1,2,3,4,6,8,12$. Thus $\boxed7$ integers work.
