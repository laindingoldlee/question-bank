Since $9^2+12^2=81+144=225=15^2$, triangle $DEF$ is right-angled, with legs $9$ and $12$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s=0.2){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
pair D=(0,0),Ep=(12,0),F=(0,9);
draw(D--Ep--F--cycle); draw(ra(D,(1,0),(0,1),0.8));
label("$D$",D,SW); label("$E$",Ep,SE); label("$F$",F,NW); label("$12$",(6,0),S); label("$9$",(0,4.5),W); label("$15$",(6,4.5),NE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Its area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot9\cdot12=\boxed{54}$. (Alternatively, triangle $ABC$ has area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot6\cdot8=24$, and the scale factor is $\dfrac{3}{2}$, so the area is $24\cdot\dfrac{9}{4}=54$.)
