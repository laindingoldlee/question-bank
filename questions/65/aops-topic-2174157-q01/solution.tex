There are $\dbinom{11}{3}=165$ equally likely choices. Exactly $2$ powerful memes: $\dbinom32\dbinom81=24$ choices; all $3$ powerful: $1$ choice. So the probability is
\[\dfrac{24+1}{165}=\dfrac{25}{165}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{33}}.\]
