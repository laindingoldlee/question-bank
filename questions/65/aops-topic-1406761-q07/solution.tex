The set is $\{4+3k: k=0,1,\dots,14\}$ ($15$ numbers). A sum of three distinct elements is $12+3(i+j+k)$ with distinct $i,j,k\in\{0,\dots,14\}$. The sum $i+j+k$ of three distinct numbers from $0$ to $14$ is at least $0+1+2=3$ and at most $12+13+14=39$, and every value in between occurs: starting from $\{0,1,2\}$, we can repeatedly increase the largest element that can be increased by $1$ without a clash, raising the sum by $1$ at each step until we reach $\{12,13,14\}$. So $i+j+k$ takes the $37$ values $3,4,\dots,39$, and the sums $12+3(i+j+k)$ are $37$ different integers ($21,24,\dots,129$). The answer is $\boxed{37}$.
