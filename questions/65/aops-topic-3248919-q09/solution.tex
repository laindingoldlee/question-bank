The central circle has radius $1$. The entire window has area $9\pi$, so the outer radius is $3$. A sector pane has two radial edges of length $3-1=2$, an outer arc of length $\dfrac{1}{8}(2\pi\cdot3)$, and an inner arc of length $\dfrac{1}{8}(2\pi\cdot1)$. Its perimeter is $\boxed{4+\pi}$.

\end{asy}
