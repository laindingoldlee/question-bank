The equal sides cannot both be $4$, since $4+4<9$. Thus they are $9$, with base $4$. The altitude bisects the base and has length $\sqrt{9^2-2^2}=\sqrt{77}$. The area is $\dfrac{1}{2}(4)\sqrt{77}=\boxed{2\sqrt{77}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair[] pts={(-2,0),(2,0),(0,sqrt(77))}; string[] names={"$A$","$B$","$C$"}; path boundary=pts[0]; for(int j=1;j<pts.length;++j)boundary=boundary--pts[j];draw(boundary--cycle); for(int i=0;i<pts.length;++i) label(names[i],pts[i],dir(90+360*i/pts.length));
\end{asy}
