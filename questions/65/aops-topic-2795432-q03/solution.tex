When $A$ reaches 900 metres, the elapsed time is $\dfrac{900}{400}$ times as long. Thus $B,C$ have run $680\cdot\dfrac{900}{400}=1530$ metres. Neither has finished because both are slower than $A$. Their combined remaining distance is $2000-1530=\boxed{470\text{ metres}}$.
