Let $ABC$ be a triangle. Points $E$ and $F$ lie on sides $AC$ and $AB$ respectively. Ray $EF$ meets line $CB$ at $D$. Point $X$ lies on segment $AF$ such that $EX$ is parallel to $BC$. Given that $CE=4$, $EA=2$, $AF=3$ and $FB=1$, find
\[
AX,\quad XF,\quad \dfrac{XE}{BC},\quad \dfrac{XE}{DB},\quad \dfrac{CB}{BD},\quad \dfrac{DF}{FE}.
\]
