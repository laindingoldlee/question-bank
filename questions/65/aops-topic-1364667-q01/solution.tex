\textbf{$AX$, $XF$, $\dfrac{XE}{BC}$.} Since $EX\parallel CB$, triangle $AXE$ is similar to triangle $ABC$ with ratio $\dfrac{AE}{AC}=\dfrac{2}{6}=\dfrac{1}{3}$. So $AX=\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot4=\dfrac{4}{3}$, $XF=3-\dfrac{4}{3}=\dfrac{5}{3}$ and $\dfrac{XE}{BC}=\dfrac{1}{3}$.

\textbf{$\dfrac{XE}{DB}$.} Since $XE\parallel BD$, triangles $FXE$ and $FBD$ are similar (vertical angles at $F$), so
\[
\dfrac{XE}{BD}=\dfrac{FX}{FB}=\dfrac{\dfrac{5}{3}}{1}=\dfrac{5}{3} .
\]
\textbf{$\dfrac{CB}{BD}$.} Combining, $\dfrac{CB}{BD}=\dfrac{CB}{XE}\cdot\dfrac{XE}{BD}=3\cdot\dfrac{5}{3}=5$. (Menelaus' theorem for the line $DFE$ gives the same: $\dfrac{AF}{FB}\cdot\dfrac{BD}{DC}\cdot\dfrac{CE}{EA}=1$, so $\dfrac{BD}{DC}=\dfrac{1}{6}$ and $CB=5BD$.)

\textbf{$\dfrac{DF}{FE}$.} From the same similar triangles, $\dfrac{DF}{FE}=\dfrac{FB}{FX}=\dfrac{1}{\dfrac{5}{3}}=\dfrac{3}{5}$.

Answers: $\boxed{AX=\dfrac{4}{3},\ XF=\dfrac{5}{3},\ \dfrac{XE}{BC}=\dfrac{1}{3},\ \dfrac{XE}{DB}=\dfrac{5}{3},\ \dfrac{CB}{BD}=5,\ \dfrac{DF}{FE}=\dfrac{3}{5}}$.
