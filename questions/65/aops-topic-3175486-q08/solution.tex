Factoring yields $(a-2006)(b-2006)=2006^2=2^2\cdot17^2\cdot59^2$. Both factors are positive. Put $t=a-2006$. Since $b\ge100000$, $t\le\dfrac{4024036}{97994}<42$. The positive divisors of $2006^2$ below $42$ are $1,2,4,17,34$, so the largest possible $t$ is $34$. It gives $a=2040$ and $b=2006+\dfrac{4024036}{34}=\boxed{120360}$, satisfying the digit restrictions. Larger $t$ would be required to reduce $b$, so this is minimal.
