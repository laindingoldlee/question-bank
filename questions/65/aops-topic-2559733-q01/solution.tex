If $p\ne3$, then $3\nmid p$, so $p^2\equiv1\pmod3$ and $p^2+8\equiv0\pmod3$. Since $p^2+8>3$, it is then not prime. Hence $p=3$. Indeed $3^2+8=17$ is prime, and $p^3+8p+2=27+24+2=53$, which is prime. $\blacksquare$
