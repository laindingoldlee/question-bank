Put $DE=h$, $AC=BE=u$, $BC=v$. Similarity of right triangles $BDE$ and $BAC$ gives $\dfrac{h}{u}=\dfrac{u}{v}$, hence $u^2=hv$. In triangle $BDE$, $u^2+h^2=120^2=14400$. Since $h+v=288$, this is $h(h+v)=288h=14400$. Therefore $\boxed{DE=50}$.
