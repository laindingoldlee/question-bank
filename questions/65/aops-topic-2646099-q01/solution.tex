The first sequence consists of the numbers $\equiv1\pmod3$ from $1$ to $298$; the second of the numbers $\equiv2\pmod8$ from $2$ to $794$. A common number satisfies $x\equiv1\pmod3$ and $x\equiv2\pmod8$, i.e. $x\equiv10\pmod{24}$, and $x\le298$. These are $10,34,\dots,298=10+12\cdot24$, so there are $\boxed{13}$ common numbers.
