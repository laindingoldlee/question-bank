The first $15$ are all nonzero strings of at most four binary digits, viewed in decimal. Among the $16$ four-position strings including $0000$, each position contains $1$ eight times, so their sum is $8(1000+100+10+1)=8888$. The sixteenth positive number is $10000$, giving $\boxed{18888}$.
