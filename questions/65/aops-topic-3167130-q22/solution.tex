Use $\binom{v+1}{2}=1+2+\cdots+v$. Interchanging the finite sums, each prime power $p^k\le50$ contributes $k$ for every multiple of $p^k$, giving $\sum_p\sum_{k\ge1}k\left\lfloor\dfrac{50}{p^k}\right\rfloor$. For $p=2$ this is $25+24+18+12+5=84$; for $p=3$ it is $16+10+3=29$; for $p=5$ it is $10+4=14$; for $p=7$ it is $7+2=9$. Primes $11,13,17,19,23$ contribute $4,3,2,2,2$, and the six primes $29,31,37,41,43,47$ each contribute $1$. The total is $\boxed{155}$.
