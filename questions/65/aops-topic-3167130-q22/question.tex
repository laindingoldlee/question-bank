For a prime $p$ and positive integer $n$, let $v_p(n)$ be the exponent of $p$ in $n$. Evaluate $\sum_{n=1}^{50}\sum_{\substack{p\le n\\p\text{ prime}}}\binom{v_p(n)+1}{2}$.
