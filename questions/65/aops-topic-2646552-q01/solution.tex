Let $N=2^{100}$. Each of the $N$ factors of $N!$ is at most $N$, so
\[N!<N^N=2^{100\cdot2^{100}}.\]
On the other hand, $100!=100\cdot(99\cdot98\cdots4)\cdot3\cdot2\cdot1>100\cdot4^{96}=100\cdot2^{192}>100\cdot2^{100}$. Hence
\[2^{100!}>2^{100\cdot2^{100}}>\left(2^{100}\right)!,\]
and $\boxed{2^{100!}}$ is larger. $\blacksquare$
