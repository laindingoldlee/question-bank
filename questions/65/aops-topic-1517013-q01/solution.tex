First, $2^{17}+2^{14}=2^{14}(8+1)=9\cdot2^{14}=\left(3\cdot2^7\right)^2=384^2$. So we need
\[
384^2+2^n=m^2\qquad\text{for a positive integer }m ,
\]
that is, $2^n=(m-384)(m+384)$. Both factors are positive and their product is a power of $2$, so both are powers of $2$: $m-384=2^s$ and $m+384=2^t$ with $s<t$ and $s+t=n$. Subtracting,
\[
2^t-2^s=768=2^8\cdot3,\qquad\text{i.e.}\qquad 2^s\left(2^{t-s}-1\right)=2^8\cdot3 .
\]
Since $2^{t-s}-1$ is odd, $2^s=2^8$ and $2^{t-s}-1=3$. So $s=8$, $t=10$ and $n=s+t=18$.

Check: $2^{17}+2^{14}+2^{18}=131072+16384+262144=409600=640^2$. The only such $n$ is $\boxed{18}$.
