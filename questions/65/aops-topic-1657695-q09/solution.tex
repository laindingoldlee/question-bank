From $x^2=18x-1$ we get $x\ne0$ and $x+\dfrac{1}{x}=18$. Let $s_n=x^n+x^{-n}$. Then $s_{2n}=s_n^2-2$, and every $s_{2^k}$ is an integer: $s_1=18$, $s_2=18^2-2=322$, and each later term is the square of the previous one minus $2$. If $s_n$ ends in $2$, then $s_n^2$ ends in $4$ and $s_{2n}$ ends in $2$. Since $s_2$ ends in $2$, so do $s_4,s_8,\dots,s_{2048}$ ($2048=2^{11}$). The units digit is $\boxed{2}$.
