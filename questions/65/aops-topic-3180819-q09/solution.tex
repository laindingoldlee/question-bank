Let the positive common difference be $d$. Then $(5+2d)^2=5(5+8d)$, giving $4d(d-5)=0$. Since $d>0$, $d=5$. The first nine terms run from $5$ to $45$, with sum $\dfrac{9}{2}(5+45)=\boxed{225}$.
