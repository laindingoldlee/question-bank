A $40$ km trip consists of $40\div\dfrac{1}{5}=200$ fifths of a kilometre, costing $200\cdot0.45=\$90$. The fare is $3.20+90=\$93.20$, and each person pays $\dfrac{93.20}{4}=\boxed{\$23.30}$.
