If the distances are $2d$ and $d$, total time is $\dfrac{2d}{15}+\dfrac{d}{20}=\dfrac{11d}{60}$ seconds. Dividing total distance $3d$ by this gives $\boxed{\dfrac{180}{11}}$ meters per second.
