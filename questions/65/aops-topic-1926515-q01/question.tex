The diagonals of a trapezoid have lengths $17$ and $15$, and the segment connecting the midpoints of the two bases has length $4$. The height of the trapezoid can be written as $\dfrac{x\sqrt y}{y}$, where $x$ and $y$ are positive integers and $y$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. Find $x+y$.
