Convergence requires the summands to tend to zero. For the first series this requires $ar^n\to-1$. A real geometric sequence can have a nonzero limit only when $r=1$: if a nonzero limit $L$ exists, taking limits in $ar^{n+1}=r(ar^n)$ yields $L=rL$. Thus $r=1,a=-1$ are necessary, and they make every summand zero, so they are sufficient. Similarly the second series converges exactly when $r=1,a=1$. Both sums are $\boxed{0}$.
