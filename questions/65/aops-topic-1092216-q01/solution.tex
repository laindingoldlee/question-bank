There are $\dbinom82=28$ equally likely pairs. The pairs who do not know each other are exactly the pairs of one bridesmaid and one groomsman: $4\cdot2=8$ pairs. So $28-8=20$ pairs know each other, and the probability is
\[\dfrac{20}{28}=\boxed{\dfrac57}.\]
