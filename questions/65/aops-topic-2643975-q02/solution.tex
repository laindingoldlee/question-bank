Since $0<A+B<\pi$ and $\cos(A+B)>0$, the angle $A+B$ is acute and $\sin(A+B)=\dfrac35$. Since $0<2A+B<\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$ and $\cos(2A+B)>0$, the angle $2A+B$ is also acute, so $\sin(2A+B)=\dfrac45$. Now $A=(2A+B)-(A+B)$, so
\[\cos A=\cos(2A+B)\cos(A+B)+\sin(2A+B)\sin(A+B)=\dfrac35\cdot\dfrac45+\dfrac45\cdot\dfrac35=\boxed{\dfrac{24}{25}}.\]
(Check: $A\approx16.26^\circ$ and $B\approx20.61^\circ$ are indeed acute.)
