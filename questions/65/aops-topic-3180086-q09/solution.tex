The intersection consists of two 120-degree circular segments. Its area is $2(27\pi-\dfrac{81\sqrt3}{4})=54\pi-\dfrac{81\sqrt3}{2}$. Subtract this from the summed disk area $162\pi$, obtaining $108\pi+\dfrac{81\sqrt3}{2}=\dfrac{216\pi+81\sqrt3+0}{2}$. Thus $\boxed{a+b+c+d+e=302}$.
