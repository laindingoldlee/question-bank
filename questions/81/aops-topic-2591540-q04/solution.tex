If the numbers are $\overline{ab}=10a+b$ and $\overline{ba}=10b+a$, both are two-digit, so $a,b\in\{1,\dots,9\}$. Their sum is $11(a+b)$, and $a+b$ takes every value from $2$ to $18$. So there are $\boxed{17}$ distinct sums.
