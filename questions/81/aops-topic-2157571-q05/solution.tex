MINNEAPOLIS has $11$ letters: I and N appear twice each, and M, E, A, P, O, L, S once each. The number of arrangements is
\[\dfrac{11!}{2!\,2!}=\dfrac{39916800}{4}=\boxed{9979200}.\]
