The penny count is a positive multiple of 5. If there were at most 10 coins, there would be at least 5 pennies and at most 5 other coins, including a dime and nickel. Their value is at most $5+3\cdot25+10+5=95$ cents, impossible; with 10 or more pennies the required other denominations already exceed 10 coins. Eleven coins suffice: three quarters, one dime, two nickels, and five pennies total 100 cents. For the maximum, use one quarter, one dime, one nickel, and 60 pennies, giving 63 coins; replacing any penny value by additional larger denominations can only reduce the count. The sum is $11+63=\boxed{74}$.
