Grogg picks two different integers at random from $1$ to $10$ (all pairs equally likely). Lizzie writes down $3$ different numbers from $1$ to $10$. She wins if at least one of her numbers is one of Grogg's numbers. What is the probability that Lizzie wins, and what is the expected number of her numbers that are among Grogg's?
