From left to right, $x\div y\times3\div2=\dfrac xy\cdot\dfrac32$. Setting this equal to $3$ gives $\dfrac xy=2$. So the solutions are exactly the pairs with
\[\boxed{x=2y,\ y\ne0},\]
for example $(2,1)$, $(4,2)$, $(-3,-1.5)$. One equation cannot determine two unknowns; it only fixes the ratio $x:y=2:1$.
