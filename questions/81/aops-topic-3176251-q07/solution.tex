Since $3^2+4^2=5^2$, the triangle is right at $B$ and has area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot3\cdot4=6$. Also area equals $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot5\cdot BH$, so $\boxed{BH=\dfrac{12}{5}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair B=(0,0),A=(3,0),C=(0,4),H=A+dot(B-A,C-A)/dot(C-A,C-A)*(C-A);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(B--H,blue);label("$A$",A,SE);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,N);label("$H$",H,NE);label("$3$",(A+B)/2,S);label("$4$",(B+C)/2,W);label("$5$",(A+C)/2,NE);
\end{asy}
