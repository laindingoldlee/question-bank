Modulo $11$ the powers of $2$ are $2,4,8,5,10,9,7,3,6,1$, so $2^n\equiv-1$ exactly when $n\equiv5\pmod{10}$. Modulo $13$, $3^3=27\equiv1$ and $3,3^2\not\equiv1$, so $3^n\equiv1$ exactly when $3\mid n$. The smallest $n\equiv5\pmod{10}$ divisible by $3$ is $\boxed{15}$.
