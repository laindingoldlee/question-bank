Each two-hour cycle completes $\dfrac16+\dfrac1{10}=\dfrac4{15}$ of the job. After three cycles ($6$ hours), $\dfrac{12}{15}=\dfrac45$ is done. In hour $7$ Alex does $\dfrac16$, bringing the total to $\dfrac45+\dfrac16=\dfrac{29}{30}$. Bob finishes the last $\dfrac1{30}$ at rate $\dfrac1{10}$ per hour in $\dfrac13$ hour. Total: $\boxed{\dfrac{22}{3}\text{ hours}}$, i.e. $7$ hours $20$ minutes.
