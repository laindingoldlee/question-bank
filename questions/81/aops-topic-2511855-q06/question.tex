The phrase MENTAL MATH is written with its usual space between the two words. In addition, you may insert a single space into some of the gaps between two consecutive letters of the same word (for example, M ENTAL MATH), using at most $2$ extra spaces in total. In how many different ways can the phrase be written, counting the way with no extra spaces?
