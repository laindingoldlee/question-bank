The running sum must first reach $+3$ at time $3$ or $5$. At time $3$ this requires $HHH$, after which the last three tosses are arbitrary: $8$ sequences. At time $5$, there must be four heads and one tail among the first five, and the tail must be in one of the first three places to avoid an earlier hit. The final toss has two choices, giving $3\cdot2=6$ sequences. The probability is $\dfrac{8+6}{64}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{32}}$.
