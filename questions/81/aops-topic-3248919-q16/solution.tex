The top and bottom faces of each die contribute $7$, giving $9(7)=63$. Across any three-die row, matching internal faces and the opposite-face rule force the two outside end faces to sum to $7$. Three rows contribute $21$ and three columns contribute another $21$. Every exposed face has been counted once, so the total is $\boxed{105}$.

usepackage("amsmath");

\end{asy}
