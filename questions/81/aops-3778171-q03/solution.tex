The second equation gives $y=\dfrac{x}{3x-4}$, with $x\ne0,\dfrac{4}{3}$. Divide the first equation by $x$ and substitute to get $2x+\dfrac{x}{3x-4}=1+\dfrac{8}{3x-4}$. Clearing the denominator gives $6x^2-10x-4=0$, or $(3x+1)(x-2)=0$. Hence $(x,y)=(2,1)$ or $(-\dfrac{1}{3},\dfrac{1}{15})$. Both have nonzero original denominators and satisfy the equations, so these are exactly the solutions.
