One of the three is the average of the other two.
\begin{itemize}
\item $a$ in the middle: $2a=1+\dfrac a3+4$, so $a=3$; the terms are $1,3,5$, difference $\pm2$.
\item $1$ in the middle: $2=a+\dfrac a3+4$ gives $a<0$, rejected.
\item $\dfrac a3+4$ in the middle: $\dfrac{2a}3+8=1+a$, so $a=21$; the terms are $1,11,21$, difference $\pm10$.
\end{itemize}
So the common difference is $\boxed{2}$ or $\boxed{10}$ in absolute value (the sequences $1,3,5$ and $1,11,21$).
