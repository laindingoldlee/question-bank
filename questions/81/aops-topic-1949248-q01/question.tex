Let $ABEF$ be a rectangle, and let $C$ and $D$ be points on side $BE$ such that $B$, $C$, $D$, $E$ lie in that order with $BC=2$, $CD=3$ and $DE=7$. The lines $AC$ and $FD$ meet at $G$. Compute $BG^{2}-CG^{2}-DG^{2}+EG^{2}$, and show that the value does not depend on the height of the rectangle.
