Use coordinates with $B=(0,0)$, $E=(12,0)$, $A=(0,h)$, $F=(12,h)$, where $h>0$ is the height; then $C=(2,0)$ and $D=(5,0)$.

Line $AC$ consists of the points $(2t,\,h(1-t))$ and line $FD$ of the points $(12-7u,\,h(1-u))$. Equal $y$-coordinates give $t=u$, and then $2t=12-7t$ gives $t=\dfrac{4}{3}$. So
\[
G=\left(\dfrac{8}{3},\,-\dfrac {h}{3}\right),
\]
which lies below line $BE$: the segments $AC$ and $FD$ do not meet, but the lines do.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real h=4;
pair B=(0,0),C=(2,0),D=(5,0),E0=(12,0),F=(12,h),A=(0,h),G=(8/3,-h/3);
draw(A--B--E0--F--cycle,linewidth(1));
draw(A--C,linewidth(0.9));draw(C--G,dashed);
draw(F--D,linewidth(0.9));draw(D--G,dashed);
draw(B--G,blue+dotted);draw(E0--G,blue+dotted);
dot(B);dot(C);dot(D);dot(E0);dot(G);
label("$A$",A,NW);label("$B$",B,W);label("$C$",C,SW);label("$D$",D,SE);label("$E$",E0,SE);label("$F$",F,NE);label("$G$",G,S);
label("$2$",(B+C)/2,N);label("$3$",(C+D)/2,N);label("$7$",(D+E0)/2,N);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Write $G=(g,k)$. Since $B,C,D,E$ all lie on the $x$-axis, each squared distance is $(g-x_P)^2+k^2$, and the four $k^2$ terms cancel in $BG^2-CG^2-DG^2+EG^2$ (two plus signs, two minus signs). Hence
\[
BG^2-CG^2-DG^2+EG^2=g^2-(g-2)^2-(g-5)^2+(g-12)^2=-10g+115 .
\]
With $g=\dfrac{8}{3}$, which does not involve $h$, the value is
\[
115-\dfrac{80}{3}=\boxed{\dfrac{265}{3}} .
\]
