View an order as a cyclic string. The move reverses the cyclic orientation, then rotates the resulting string; it preserves the circular adjacency of the nine digits. Thus every reachable order is a rotation of $123456789$ or of its reversal, at most $18$ orders. Reversal itself is the move $n=9$. A move $n=1$ followed by reversal $n=9$ rotates the original string left by one position. Repeating this rotation reaches all nine rotations, and reversal reaches the other nine. They are distinct because all digits differ. The maximum is $\boxed{18}$.
