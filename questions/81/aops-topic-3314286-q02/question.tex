Begin with the digits $1$ through $9$ in increasing order. A move chooses $n\in\{1,\ldots,9\}$ and reverses positions $1$ through $n$ and separately positions $n+1$ through $9$. What is the maximum number of distinct orders, including the initial order, that can ever occur?
