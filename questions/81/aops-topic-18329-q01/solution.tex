Factor: \(436995=5\cdot87399=3^4\cdot5\cdot13\cdot83\).

Exactly one of the numbers is divisible by \(5\); a number of the form \(\overline{aba}\) divisible by \(5\) ends in \(0\) or \(5\), and it cannot start with \(0\), so it is \(\overline{5b5}=505+10b=5(101+2b)\). The other number is then
\[
\dfrac{87399}{101+2b},
\]
so \(101+2b\) is an odd divisor of \(87399=3^4\cdot13\cdot83\) between \(101\) and \(119\). Checking \(101,103,\dots,119\), the only such divisor is \(117=9\cdot13\), giving \(b=8\). Then the numbers are \(585\) and \(\dfrac{87399}{117}=747\), and \(747\) indeed has equal first and last digits.

\[
\boxed{585\text{ and }747}
\]
