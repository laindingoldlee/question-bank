In a single-elimination lacrosse tournament, $64$ teams play $6$ rounds and $63$ games. Before the tournament you must predict the winners of all $63$ games (your predictions must be consistent: a team you predict to win a game must be one you predicted to reach it). You score $32$ points for correctly predicting the champion, $16$ points for each correctly predicted winner of a semifinal, and so on, down to $1$ point for each correctly predicted winner of a first-round game; the maximum score is $192$. Knowing nothing about the teams, you decide every prediction by flipping a fair coin. What is the expected number of points you score?
