If a set has $k$ elements, its least possible sum is $\dfrac{k(k+1)}{2}$, so average ten requires $1\le k\le19$. Write its increasing elements as $a_i=i+b_i$, where $0\le b_1\le\cdots\le b_k$. Their sum condition is $\sum b_i=10k-\dfrac{k(k+1)}{2}$. Thus we count partitions of that integer into at most $k$ parts, equivalently partitions with parts at most $k$. A reproducible recurrence starts with $d(0)=1$ and other $d(s)=0$; for each allowed part $j=1,\ldots,k$, update $d(s)\leftarrow d(s)+d(s-j)$ in increasing order for $s\ge j$. The counts for $k=1,\ldots,19$ are $1,9,61,297,1115,3331,8033,15765,25331,33401,36043,31570,22142,12186,5096,1530,297,30,1$. Their sum is $\boxed{196239}$.
