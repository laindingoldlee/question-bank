Set $P=(0,0,0)$, $A=(2,0,0)$, $B=(0,3,0)$ and $C=(0,0,4)$. Interior containment means $D=(-u,-v,-w)$ with $u,v,w>0$ and $u^2+v^2+w^2=25$. The determinant formula gives $V=\dfrac{2\cdot3\cdot4}{6}\left(1+\dfrac{u}{2}+\dfrac{v}{3}+\dfrac{w}{4}\right)$. By Cauchy--Schwarz, $\dfrac{u}{2}+\dfrac{v}{3}+\dfrac{w}{4}\le5\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{9}+\dfrac{1}{16}}=\dfrac{5\sqrt{61}}{12}$. Equality occurs for $(u,v,w)=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{61}}(30,20,15)$, all positive and satisfying the length condition. Hence $\boxed{V=4+\dfrac{5\sqrt{61}}{3}}$ and $\boxed{\lfloor V\rfloor=17}$.
