They do not: we show $\sqrt x>\ln x$ for all $x>0$. Let $h(x)=\sqrt x-\ln x$. Then $h'(x)=\dfrac1{2\sqrt x}-\dfrac1x=\dfrac{\sqrt x-2}{2x}$, which is negative for $x<4$ and positive for $x>4$. So $h$ has minimum $h(4)=2-\ln4=2-1.386\ldots>0$ (as $e^2>4$). Hence $\boxed{\text{no intersection points}}$. $\blacksquare$
