Dividing by the degree-one polynomial $x-a$ gives $p(x)=(x-a)q(x)+r$ with a constant remainder $r$. Substituting $x=a$ kills the first term: $p(a)=r$.

For $p(x)=-3x^3-4x^2+10x-7$ and $a=2$: $p(2)=-24-16+20-7=\boxed{-27}$.
