Define a sequence by $a_0=0$ and
\[
a_n=a_{\left\lfloor\dfrac {n}{2}\right\rfloor}+n-2\left\lfloor\dfrac {n}{2}\right\rfloor\qquad(n\ge1).
\]
Let $N=\dfrac{5}{7}\left(2^{24}-1\right)$. Compute $a_N$.
