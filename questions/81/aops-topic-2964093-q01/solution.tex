The complement is that both of Blanca's cards are smaller than both of Amira's. Given the set of four cards drawn, every split into Amira's pair and Blanca's pair is equally likely, and exactly one of the $\binom42=6$ splits gives Blanca the two smallest. So the probability is $1-\dfrac16=\boxed{\dfrac56}$.
