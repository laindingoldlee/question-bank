The mean is $\dfrac{27+x}{6}$.

\textbf{Case 1: $x\notin\{4,6,7\}$.} Then $5$ is the only mode (it appears at least twice and every other value once, or three times if $x=5$). So we need mean $=5$: $27+x=30$, $x=3$. Then the data $3,4,5,5,6,7$ have median $5$. So $x=3$ works. ($x=5$ fails: the mean would be $\dfrac{32}{6}\ne5$.)

\textbf{Case 2: $x\in\{4,6,7\}$.} Then $5$ and $x$ are both modes, with mean $\dfrac{5+x}{2}$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $x=4$: modes' mean $4.5$, mean $\dfrac{31}{6}$: fails.
\item $x=6$: modes' mean $5.5$, mean $\dfrac{33}{6}=5.5$, and the data $4,5,5,6,6,7$ have median $5.5$: works.
\item $x=7$: modes' mean $6$, mean $\dfrac{34}{6}$: fails.
\end{itemize}
So $x=3$ or $x=6$, and there are $\boxed{2}$ possible values.
