After $k$ rotations the disk has turned $150k$ degrees, and $A$ is back in its position exactly when $360\mid150k$, i.e.\ $12\mid5k$, i.e.\ $12\mid k$. The first rotation after 10 AM Saturday is at noon on Saturday, so the $12$th is at noon $11$ days later. Since $11=7+4$, that is four days of the week after Saturday: $\boxed{\text{Wednesday}}$ (at noon), as posted.
