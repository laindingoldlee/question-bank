Multiply by $10$: $30x^2-40x\le6x^2-3x+5$, i.e. $24x^2-37x-5\le0$. The roots are
\[x=\dfrac{37\pm\sqrt{37^2+4\cdot24\cdot5}}{48}=\dfrac{37\pm43}{48},\]
namely $-\dfrac{1}{8}$ and $\dfrac{5}{3}$. The inequality holds for $-\dfrac{1}{8}\le x\le\dfrac{5}{3}$, which contains the integers $0$ and $1$: $\boxed{2}$ integers.
