A knave's chosen statement must falsely call a neighbour a knave, so every knave has at least one knight neighbour. Each knight can neighbour at most two knaves. Thus $L\le2K=2(60-L)$, giving $L\le40$. Repeating the block knave, knave, knight twenty times works: each knave points to an adjacent knight, and each knight points to an adjacent knave. Hence the maximum is $\boxed{40}$.
