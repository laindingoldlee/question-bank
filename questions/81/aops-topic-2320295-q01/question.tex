Apollo and Artemis each shoot once at each of $5$ targets, all shots independent. Apollo hits each of $3$ of the targets with probability $97\%$ and each of the other $2$ with probability $94\%$. Artemis hits each of $4$ of the targets with probability $99\%$ and the last one with probability $94\%$. What is the probability that Apollo hits exactly four targets and Artemis hits all five?
