Let $T(n)$ be the number of $3$-element subsets $\{a,b,c\}$ of $\{1,2,\dots,n\}$ whose elements are the side lengths of a (non-degenerate) triangle. Show that
\[
T(n)=\sum_{k=1}^{n-2}\left\lfloor\dfrac{k^2}{4}\right\rfloor,
\]
and find $T(10)$.
