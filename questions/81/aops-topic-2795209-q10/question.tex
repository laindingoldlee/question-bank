An Olympic triathlon consists of a $1.5$ km swim, a $40$ km bike ride and a $10$ km run. Cori bikes $3$ times as fast as she runs and runs $3$ times as fast as she swims. Danielle bikes $2$ times as fast as she runs and runs $4$ times as fast as she swims. Cori and Danielle finish in exactly the same total time. Find the ratio of Cori's biking speed to Danielle's biking speed, as a fraction in lowest terms.
