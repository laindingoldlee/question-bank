Let Cori swim at $v$, so she runs at $3v$ and bikes at $9v$. Her time is
\[\dfrac{1.5}{v}+\dfrac{10}{3v}+\dfrac{40}{9v}=\dfrac{13.5+30+40}{9v}=\dfrac{83.5}{9v}.\]
Let Danielle swim at $w$, run at $4w$ and bike at $8w$. Her time is $\dfrac{1.5}{w}+\dfrac{10}{4w}+\dfrac{40}{8w}=\dfrac{9}{w}$.

Equal times give $\dfrac{83.5}{9v}=\dfrac9w$, so $\dfrac vw=\dfrac{83.5}{81}$. The required ratio is
\[\dfrac{9v}{8w}=\dfrac{9}{8}\cdot\dfrac{83.5}{81}=\dfrac{83.5}{72}=\boxed{\dfrac{167}{144}}.\]
