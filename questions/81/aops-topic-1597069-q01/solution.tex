Use the difference of squares and then the sum and difference of cubes:
\[
3^{12}-2^{12}=\left(3^6-2^6\right)\left(3^6+2^6\right),
\]
\[
3^6-2^6=\left(3^3-2^3\right)\left(3^3+2^3\right)=19\cdot35=5\cdot7\cdot19,
\]
\[
3^6+2^6=\left(3^2\right)^3+\left(2^2\right)^3=\left(3^2+2^2\right)\left(3^4-3^2\cdot2^2+2^4\right)=13\cdot(81-36+16)=13\cdot61 .
\]
Hence
\[
3^{12}-2^{12}=531441-4096=527345=\boxed{5\cdot7\cdot13\cdot19\cdot61}.
\]
It has five prime factors, so it is \emph{not} a product of two primes.
