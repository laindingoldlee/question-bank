The board has $8$ symmetries. Classify by the first move.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{X in the centre.} The symmetries fixing the centre act on the other $8$ squares with two orbits: corners and edges. So O has $2$ essentially different replies.
\item \textbf{X in a corner}, say top left. The symmetries fixing this corner are the identity and the reflection in the main diagonal. The other $8$ squares fall into the orbits: the opposite corner; the centre; the two other corners; the two edge squares next to X; the two far edge squares. That is $5$ replies.
\item \textbf{X on an edge}, say top middle. The symmetries fixing it are the identity and the reflection in the vertical axis. The orbits of the other squares: the two top corners; the centre; the bottom middle; the two side edges; the two bottom corners. Again $5$ replies.
\end{itemize}
In total $2+5+5=\boxed{12}$.
