For $(x,y)=(9,2)$, $x^6-y^6=531377=7\cdot11\cdot67\cdot103$. These four primes sum to $188$, and $x^2+x+y^2=94$. Scaling both $x,y$ by $7$ gives $(63,14)$ and multiplies the sixth-power difference by $7^6$, introducing no new prime factor. The four distinct primes still sum to $188$, but now $x^2+x+y^2=3969+63+196=4228$. Thus the requested value is not unique.
