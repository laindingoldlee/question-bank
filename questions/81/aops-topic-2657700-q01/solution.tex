Since $n^2=(n-3)(n+3)+9$, we have $\dfrac{n^2}{n-3}=n+3+\dfrac{9}{n-3}$, which is an integer exactly when $n-3$ divides $9$:
\[n-3\in\{\pm1,\pm3,\pm9\},\qquad n\in\{4,2,6,0,12,-6\}.\]
Their sum is $4+2+6+0+12-6=\boxed{18}$.
