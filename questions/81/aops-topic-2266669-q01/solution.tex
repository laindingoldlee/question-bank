For $n\ge3$, $\dbinom n3=\dfrac{n(n-1)(n-2)}{6}$, so with $m=n-2$ the series is $\displaystyle6\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{m(m+1)(m+2)}$. Use
\[\dfrac{1}{m(m+1)(m+2)}=\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{1}{m(m+1)}-\dfrac{1}{(m+1)(m+2)}\right),\]
which is checked by putting the right side over the common denominator: $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{(m+2)-m}{m(m+1)(m+2)}$. The partial sums telescope:
\[\sum_{n=3}^{N}\dfrac{1}{\dbinom n3}=6\sum_{m=1}^{N-2}\dfrac{1}{m(m+1)(m+2)}=3\left(\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{(N-1)N}\right)=\dfrac{3}{2}-\dfrac{3}{N(N-1)}.\]
As $N\to\infty$, $\dfrac{3}{N(N-1)}\to0$. So the series \textbf{converges}, and its sum is $\boxed{\dfrac{3}{2}}$.
