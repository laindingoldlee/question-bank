Let the numbers be $n,n+1,\dots,n+9$ and let $n+k$ be erased ($0\le k\le9$). Then $10n+45-(n+k)=2011$, so $9n=1966+k$. Since $1966\equiv4\pmod9$, $k=5$ and $n=219$. The erased number is $\boxed{224}$.
