Yes. Let $F$ be the sum of squared club sizes. Suppose a club of size $k\ge15$ empties, and the destination clubs previously have sizes $b_1,\ldots,b_k$. Then
\[\Delta F=-k^2+\sum_{i=1}^k((b_i+1)^2-b_i^2)=-k^2+2\sum b_i+k.\]
Since $\sum b_i\le119-k$, we have $\Delta F\le238-k^2-k\le238-225-15=-2$. Thus the nonnegative integer $F$ decreases at each step, and only finitely many steps are possible.
