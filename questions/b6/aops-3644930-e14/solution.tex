There are $\dfrac{\binom63}{2}=10$ unordered splits of the six cyclic vertices into two triples. The two convex triangles are disjoint exactly when each triple consists of three consecutive vertices. There are three such splits, corresponding to the three separating pairs of opposite gaps. Hence seven splits intersect, with probability $\boxed{\dfrac{7}{10}}$.
