The total is $31$. Every match contributes $10$ result points and at least two goal points, so at most two matches occurred. Since every team has positive points, exactly two occurred. Only Dins can have won a match, because the others have fewer than ten points. Two wins by Dins would already give twenty points, impossible. Two draws would force Dins to be the middle team (the others have fewer than ten); then the Hubs and Rungs single-match goals would be $3,4$, giving Dins $10+3+4=17$, impossible. Thus there was one win and one draw. Dins cannot be the middle team because win-plus-draw points total fifteen. If Hubs were in the middle, its draw against Rungs would require four Hubs goals while its total goal allowance is only $8-5=3$, impossible. Therefore Rungs is in the middle. The Hubs--Rungs draw is $3$--$3$; Rungs has one additional goal in its loss, while Dins has $14-10=4$ goals. The matches are $\boxed{\text{Hubs--Rungs }3\!:\!3,\quad\text{Dins--Rungs }4\!:\!1}$; Hubs and Dins do not play.
