Let the integers be $a,b<10^6$. Then $\gcd(a,b)\cdot\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=ab<10^{12}$, and $\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)\ge10^9$, so
\[
\gcd(a,b)<\dfrac{10^{12}}{10^9}=10^3 ,
\]
i.e.\ the gcd has at most $3$ digits.

This is attained: $a=999900=100\cdot9999$ and $b=999800=100\cdot9998$ have $\gcd=100$ (as $\gcd(9999,9998)=1$) and $\operatorname{lcm}=100\cdot9999\cdot9998=9997000200$, which has $10$ digits. The answer is $\boxed{3}$.
