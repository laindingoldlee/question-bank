A system $N\equiv a\pmod m$, $N\equiv b\pmod n$ is solvable exactly when $a\equiv b\pmod{\gcd(m,n)}$, and then the solution is unique modulo $\operatorname{lcm}(m,n)$ (two solutions differ by a common multiple of $m$ and $n$).

Here $\gcd(8,12)=4$ and $1\equiv5\pmod4$, so solutions exist and are unique modulo $24$. From $N=1+8k\equiv5\pmod{12}$: $8k\equiv4\pmod{12}$, i.e. $2k\equiv1\pmod3$, so $k\equiv2\pmod3$ and $N=1+8(2+3m)=17+24m$. Thus $\boxed{N\equiv17\pmod{24}}$.
