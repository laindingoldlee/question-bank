Let $c$ be the hypotenuse. Pythagoras gives $a^2+b^2=c^2$, so the supplied sum is $2c^2=200$, hence $c=10$. From $abc=260$, $ab=26$. The area is $\dfrac{ab}{2}=\boxed{13}$.
