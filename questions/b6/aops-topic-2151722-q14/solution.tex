The facing pages are consecutive numbers $n$ and $n+1$, so $n(n+1)=315282$. Then $n^2<315282<(n+1)^2$, and since $561^2=314721$ and $562^2=315844$, we get $n=561$. Indeed $561\cdot562=314721+561=315282$. The sum is
\[561+562=\boxed{1123}.\]
