\emph{Base case} $n=1$: the left side is $2$ and the right side is $2^2\cdot0+2=2$.

\emph{Inductive step.} Assume the formula for $n$. Then
\[\sum_{k=1}^{n+1}k\cdot2^k=2^{n+1}(n-1)+2+(n+1)2^{n+1}=2^{n+1}\cdot2n+2=2^{n+2}n+2,\]
which is the formula for $n+1$. By induction it holds for all $n\ge1$. $\blacksquare$
