$\sum a_i^2=\left(\sum a_i\right)^2-2\sum_{i<j}a_ia_j=0-0=0$. A sum of squares of reals is $0$ only if every $a_i=0$, so $\sum a_i^3=0$. $\blacksquare$

(The poster's intermediate claim $\sum a_i^2=\sum a_i$ is a slip; the correct identity gives $\sum a_i^2=0$ directly.)
