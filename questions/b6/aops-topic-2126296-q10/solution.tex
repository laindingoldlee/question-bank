Drawing continues as long as no colour has $4$ balls and some colour has at most $1$ ball. Such a state has at most $3+3+1=7$ balls, and $7$ is possible: for example $3$ blue, $3$ red and $1$ white (the jar has enough balls of each colour).

On the other hand, after $8$ draws one of the stopping conditions must hold: if no colour has reached $4$, then each colour has at most $3$ balls, so each colour has at least $8-3-3=2$ balls. So the drawing always stops by the $8$th ball, and it may take that long. The answer is $\boxed{8}$.
