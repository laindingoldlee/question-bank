Take as known that a circle of radius $r$ has circumference $2\pi r$ (this is the definition of $\pi$). For $n\ge3$, let $P_n$ and $h_n$ be the perimeter and the apothem (distance from the centre to a side) of the regular $n$-gon inscribed in the circle, and let $Q_n$ be the perimeter of the regular $n$-gon circumscribed about it.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that the inscribed $n$-gon has area $\dfrac{1}{2}P_nh_n$ and the circumscribed $n$-gon has area $\dfrac{1}{2}Q_nr$.
\item[(b)] Using the facts that $P_n$ and $Q_n$ both approach $2\pi r$ and $h_n$ approaches $r$ as $n$ grows, prove that the area of the circle is $\pi r^2$.
\end{enumerate}
