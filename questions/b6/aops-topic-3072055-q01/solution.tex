Write the angles as $x,x+d,x+2d$. Their sum is $180^\circ$, so $x+d=60^\circ$. The largest is $x+2(60-x)=\boxed{120-x}$ degrees. The possible range is $0<x\le60$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

pair A=(0,0),B=(4,0),C=(1,sqrt(3));draw(A--B--C--cycle);label("$60^\circ$",(.45,.2));label("$x=30^\circ$",(3.2,.2));label("$120^\circ-x=90^\circ$",C,N);
\end{asy}
