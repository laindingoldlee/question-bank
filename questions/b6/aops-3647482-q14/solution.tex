If $p$ proteins and $t$ toppings are safe, then $p\binom t3=2800$, with $1\le p\le6$ and $3\le t\le24$. Thus $\binom t3\ge\dfrac{2800}{6}>466$. Because $\binom{15}{3}=455$, we need $t\ge16$. The values for $t=16,17,\ldots,24$ are $560,680,816,969,1140,1330,1540,1771,2024$. Only 560 divides 2800 with integer quotient between 1 and 6; it gives $p=5$, $t=16$. Therefore the combined count is $\boxed{21}$.
