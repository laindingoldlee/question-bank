Fix Ethan at angle zero. Each girl can be attracted exactly when her angle is between $-\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ and $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, since a unit chord subtends $60^\circ$. Conditional on both angles lying there, the allowable square of side $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$ has two corner triangles where their angular separation exceeds $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$. These occupy one quarter of that square. The chance both girls are in range is $\dfrac{1}{9}$, so the desired probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{36}}$.

\end{asy}
