For $S=\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{8}+\cdots$ one argues: $2S=1+\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{4}+\cdots=1+S$, so $S=1$. For $T=1-1+1-1+\cdots$ the same kind of argument, $T=1-(1-1+1-\cdots)=1-T$, gives $T=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Explain why the first argument is valid and the second is not.
