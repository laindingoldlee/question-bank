Put $m=2n+1$ and $v=S(m)=d(m)=2t$. Then the final condition is $S(m+v^2)=v$. We seek the least odd $m\ge3$ with these properties. The odd values $m\le1911$ satisfying $S(m)=d(m)$ with even value are $11,101,301,525,1003,1111,1425,1575,1911$. Their corresponding $v$ are $2,2,4,12,4,4,12,18,12$, and their values $S(m+v^2)$ are $6,6,11,21,11,11,21,27,12$. Thus the first success is $m=1911=3\cdot7^2\cdot13$, with $v=12,t=6$, and $m+144=2055$ has digit sum 12. Therefore $\boxed{n=955}$. The short exhaustive table is obtained by looping through odd $m\le1911$, summing its decimal digits, and computing $d(m)=\prod(e_i+1)$ from trial-division factorisation.
