The common ringing interval is $\operatorname{lcm}(2,4,6,8,10,12)=120$ seconds. Thirty minutes is $1800$ seconds, so the times are $0,120,240,\ldots,1800$. There are $\dfrac{1800}{120}+1=\boxed{16}$ such times. Excluding the initial ring would instead give $15$.
