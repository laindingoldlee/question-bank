Let $a=\sqrt{11}+\sqrt{13}$, $b=\sqrt{11}+2\sqrt{13}$ and $c=\sqrt{11}+3\sqrt{13}$. The expression
\[a^2(b-c)+b^2(c-a)+c^2(a-b)\]
can be written in the form $p\sqrt q$ for integers $p$ and $q$, where $q$ is square-free. Find $p+q$.
