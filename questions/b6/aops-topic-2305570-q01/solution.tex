The expression factors: for all numbers $a,b,c$,
\[a^2(b-c)+b^2(c-a)+c^2(a-b)=-(a-b)(b-c)(c-a).\]
Indeed, expanding,
\[(a-b)(b-c)(c-a)=-a^2b+a^2c+ab^2-b^2c-ac^2+bc^2,\]
which is the negative of $a^2(b-c)+b^2(c-a)+c^2(a-b)=a^2b-a^2c+b^2c-ab^2+ac^2-bc^2$.

Here $a-b=-\sqrt{13}$, $b-c=-\sqrt{13}$ and $c-a=2\sqrt{13}$, so
\[-(a-b)(b-c)(c-a)=-\left(-\sqrt{13}\right)\left(-\sqrt{13}\right)\left(2\sqrt{13}\right)=-26\sqrt{13}.\]
So $p=-26$, $q=13$, and $p+q=\boxed{-13}$.
