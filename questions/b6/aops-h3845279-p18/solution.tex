The peak is $10$. Choose the nonempty proper subset of $1,\ldots,9$ to go to its left, in increasing order; the complement goes to the right in decreasing order. There are $2^9-2=510$ permutations.

The two endpoint values are the minima of the two subsets. One is $1$, so their sum is even exactly when the other minimum is an odd number $j\in\{3,5,7,9\}$. Choose the side containing $1$ in $2$ ways. All numbers below $j$ must be on that side, $j$ must be on the other, and the $9-j$ larger values may go to either side. The favorable count is
\[
2(2^6+2^4+2^2+1)=170.
\]
The fraction is $\boxed{\dfrac{170}{510}=\dfrac{1}{3}}$.
