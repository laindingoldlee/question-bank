The numbers are $\overline{20cd}$ with two-digit $n=\overline{cd}$ from $10$ to $99$, and we need $20n=2^2\cdot5\cdot n$ to be a square, i.e. $5n$ a square. That means $n=5k^2$, and $10\le5k^2\le99$ gives $k=2,3,4$, i.e. $n=20,45,80$ ($20\cdot45=30^2$, $20\cdot80=40^2$). Apart from $2020$ the numbers are $2045$ and $2080$: $\boxed{2}$ numbers.
