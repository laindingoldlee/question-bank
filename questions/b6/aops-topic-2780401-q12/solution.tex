(a) For $ N=1 $, there are no triples to check, so assigning weight 1 works: the answer is $\boxed1 $. (b) Partition the line into consecutive blocks of 3, with a possible final block of 1 or 2. Every block has sum at most $\dfrac{1}{2019}$, including the short final block because it can be extended to a triple and weights are nonnegative. Thus $ 1\le\dfrac{\lceil \dfrac{N}{3}\rceil}{2019}$, requiring $ N\ge6055 $. Equality is attained at $ N=6055 $ by putting weight $\dfrac{1}{2019}$ at positions $ 1,4,7,\ldots,6055 $ and 0 elsewhere. Every triple contains at most one nonzero weight. Hence the second answer is $\boxed{6055}$.
