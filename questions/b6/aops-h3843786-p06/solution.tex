For one bit, let $p$ be its stationary probability of being $1$. Then $p=(1-p)+\dfrac{p}{2}$, so $p=\dfrac{2}{3}$. Independent bit transitions make the product distribution stationary, with probability
\[
\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^k\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^{7-k}
\]
for a specified floor with $k$ ones. It is the unique stationary distribution: every floor can reach the all-ones floor, and from there every floor is reachable, while the all-ones floor also has a self-loop.

For three independent people, the probability their bits all agree at a specified position is
\[
\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^3+\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^3=\dfrac{1}{3}.
\]
The seven positions are independent, so the coincidence probability is $\dfrac{1}{3^7}=\dfrac{1}{2187}$. Therefore $\boxed{m+n=2188}$.
