The sum is $45n$, which is divisible by $5$ and by $9$. A number with all digits equal to $d\ge1$ that is divisible by $5$ must have $d=5$. The number $55\cdots5$ with $k$ fives has digit sum $5k$, so it is divisible by $9$ exactly when $9\mid k$. The smallest choice is $k=9$:
\[45n=555555555,\qquad n=12345679.\]
The digit sum of $n$ is $1+2+3+4+5+6+7+9=\boxed{37}$.
