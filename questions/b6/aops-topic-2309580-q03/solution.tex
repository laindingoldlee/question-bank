Write the odd integer as $2a+1$ and the even integer as $2b$ with integers $a,b$. Then
\[(2a+1)(2b)=2\bigl(b(2a+1)\bigr)\]
is even, and
\[(2a+1)+2b=2(a+b)+1\]
is odd. $\blacksquare$
