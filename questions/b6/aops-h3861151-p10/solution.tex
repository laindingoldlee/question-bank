The two segments are medians, so $F$ is the centroid. Let the altitude of $ABC$ be $h$ and its base be $b$. The segment $DE$ has length $\dfrac{b}{2}$ and height $\dfrac{h}{2}$ above $BC$, while $F$ has height $\dfrac{h}{3}$. Thus $[DEF]=\dfrac{1}{2}\dfrac{b}{2}\dfrac{h}{6}=\dfrac{bh}{24}=\dfrac{1}{12}[ABC]=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{12}}$.
