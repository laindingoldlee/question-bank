We need $N\equiv0\pmod3$ and $N\equiv2\pmod7$, i.e. $N\equiv9\pmod{21}$: $N=9,30,51,72,93,114,135,\dots$. Their remainders mod $11$ are $9,8,7,6,5,4,3$, so the first with remainder $3$ is $\boxed{135}$.
