Angle $C$ is 70 degrees. The circumcenter is inside this acute triangle, and isosceles triangle $AOB$ gives $\angle OAB=90^\circ-C=20^\circ$. The altitude gives $\angle DAB=90^\circ-B=46^\circ$. Both rays are inside angle $A$, so their difference is $\boxed{26^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair B=(0,0),C=(6,0),A=6*sin(70*pi/180)/sin(66*pi/180)*dir(44),D=(A.x,0);pair O=(3,(abs(A)^2-6*A.x)/(2*A.y));draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--D,dashed);draw(A--O,blue);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,S);label("$O$",O,E);
\end{asy}
