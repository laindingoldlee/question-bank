Since $3$ is prime and $3\mid ab$, we have $3\mid a$ or $3\mid b$; by symmetry say $3\mid a$. Then $b=(a+b)-a$ is a difference of two multiples of $3$, so $3\mid b$ as well. $\blacksquare$
