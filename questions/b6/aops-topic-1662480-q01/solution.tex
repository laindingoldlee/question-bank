Distribute each product, taking care with the sign in front of the second bracket:
\[
12(3+x)-4(5x-6)=36+12x-20x+24 .
\]
Now collect like terms: $12x-20x=-8x$ and $36+24=60$. So the expression equals
\[
\boxed{60-8x}.
\]
(Check with $x=1$: $12\cdot4-4\cdot(-1)=52$ and $60-8=52$.)
