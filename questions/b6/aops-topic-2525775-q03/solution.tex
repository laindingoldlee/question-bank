Write $f(x)=x(x-1)(x+1)Q(x)+r(x)$ with $r(x)=ax^2+bx+c$ of degree at most $2$. Substituting $x=0,1,-1$ (where the first term vanishes):
\[c=5,\qquad a+b+c=10,\qquad a-b+c=10.\]
So $a+b=5$ and $a-b=5$, giving $a=5$ and $b=0$. The remainder is $\boxed{5x^2+5}$.
