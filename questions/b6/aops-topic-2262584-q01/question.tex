Let $a,b,c$ be positive integers with $a+b=c$. Prove that $ab\ge c-1$, with equality exactly when $a=1$ or $b=1$. (So, for fixed $c$, the product $ab$ is smallest at $(a,b)=(1,c-1)$ or $(c-1,1)$.)
