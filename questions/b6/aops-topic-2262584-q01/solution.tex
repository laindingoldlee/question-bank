Since $c=a+b$,
\[ab-(c-1)=ab-a-b+1=(a-1)(b-1).\]
Both $a-1$ and $b-1$ are nonnegative integers, so $(a-1)(b-1)\ge0$, that is, $ab\ge c-1$. Equality holds exactly when $(a-1)(b-1)=0$, i.e. when $a=1$ or $b=1$.
