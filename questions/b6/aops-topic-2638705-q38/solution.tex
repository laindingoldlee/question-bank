If the digit sum is $s$, then $1000\le s^4\le9999$, so $s=6,7,8,$ or $9$. Their fourth powers are $1296,2401,4096,6561$, whose digit sums are $18,7,19,18$. Only $2401$ has the required digit sum. Thus $\boxed{2401}$.
