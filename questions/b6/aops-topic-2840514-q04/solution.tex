$4004=2^2\cdot7\cdot11\cdot13$ has $3\cdot2\cdot2\cdot2=24$ divisors. Of these, $1$ is neither prime nor composite and $4$ are prime ($2,7,11,13$), leaving $19$ composite. The probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{19}{24}}$.
