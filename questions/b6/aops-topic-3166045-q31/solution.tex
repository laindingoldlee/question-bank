Put $u=xy$. Multiplication of the equations gives $u+2+\dfrac{1}{u}=96$, hence $u^2-94u+1=0$. Its two distinct roots are positive. Each is attainable by $x=\dfrac{u+1}{8}$ and $y=\dfrac{8u}{u+1}$, since the quadratic ensures both original equations. By Vieta, their sum is $\boxed{94}$.
