Let $Y,B,P$ be the sets of students in the three classes, $x$ the number of students in exactly two classes and $t$ the number in all three, so $x+t=9$.

\textbf{Inclusion-exclusion} for three sets says
\[|Y\cup B\cup P|=|Y|+|B|+|P|-\left(|Y\cap B|+|B\cap P|+|P\cap Y|\right)+|Y\cap B\cap P|.\]
A student in exactly two classes lies in exactly one of the pairwise intersections, and a student in all three lies in all three of them, so the sum of the pairwise intersections is $x+3t$. Hence
\[20=10+13+9-(x+3t)+t=32-x-2t=32-(x+t)-t=32-9-t,\]
so $t=\boxed{3}$.

\textbf{Check by counting enrolments.} With $u$ students in exactly one class, $u+x+t=20$ gives $u=11$, and the total number of enrolments is $u+2x+3t=11+12+9=32=10+13+9$.
