There are $20$ students in an after-school program offering classes in yoga, bridge and painting. Each student takes at least one of the three classes, and may take two or all three. There are $10$ students taking yoga, $13$ taking bridge and $9$ taking painting, and $9$ students are taking at least two classes. How many students are taking all three classes? Solve the problem using the inclusion-exclusion principle for three sets.
