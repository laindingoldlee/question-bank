The bases are roots of $t^2-12t+1$, so $a_n=12a_{n-1}-a_{n-2}$ with $a_0=2,a_1=12$. This proves integrality by induction. Modulo $7$, the sequence $2(-1)^n$ has the same initial values and obeys the same recurrence, since $12\equiv5$ and $5(-1)-1=-6\equiv1$. Induction gives $a_n\equiv2(-1)^n$. For odd $2025$, the remainder is $\boxed5$.
