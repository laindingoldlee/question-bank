Let the legs be $a,b$. Pythagoras gives $a^2+b^2=169$, while $(a+b)^2=289$. Subtract to get $2ab=120$, so the area is $\dfrac{ab}{2}=\boxed{30}$, choice D. In fact the legs are 5 and 12, since they have sum 17 and product 60.
