At time zero two inhabitants learn the news. New groups double every five minutes: $2,4,8,\ldots$. After $5k$ minutes, the cumulative capacity is $2+4+\cdots+2^{k+1}=2^{k+2}-2$. At 35 minutes this is 510, and at 40 minutes it reaches 1022, sufficient for the 1000 inhabitants with the last group curtailed. Thus the required time is $\boxed{40\text{ minutes}}$.
