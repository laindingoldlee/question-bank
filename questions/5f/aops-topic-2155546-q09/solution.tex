Adding the first two equations gives
\[(a+b+c)^2=a^2+b^2+c^2+2ab+2ac+2bc=16,\]
but the third equation gives $(a+b+c)^2=9$. This is a contradiction, so the system has \textbf{no solutions} (not even complex ones).
