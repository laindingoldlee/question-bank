Write K for a knight and L for a liar. A K immediately followed clockwise by an L is impossible: the knight’s next-four group begins with that liar; moving one place forward removes a liar and adds one person, so the number of knights cannot decrease. If the knight’s statement is true, the liar’s statement would also be true. A mixed cyclic sequence necessarily contains a K-to-L transition, so no mixed sequence works. Both all-knight and all-liar configurations do work. Thus the possible counts are $\boxed{0\text{ and }19}$.
