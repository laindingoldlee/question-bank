The tetrahedron is obtained by extending the four alternating face planes of the octahedron. Equivalently, start with a regular tetrahedron of side 2 and cut off the four corner tetrahedra at its edge midpoints; the remaining octahedron has side 1. Each removed tetrahedron has half the outer edge length and hence one eighth its volume. The octahedron fraction is $1-4(\dfrac{1}{8})=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$.

\end{asy}
