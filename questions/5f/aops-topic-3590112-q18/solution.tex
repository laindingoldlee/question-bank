Let their initial holdings be $T,J$. The conditions are $J+n=2(T-n)$ and $T+2=n(J-2)$. Eliminating $J$ gives $(2n-1)T=3n^2+2n+2$. Multiplying by $4$ gives $(2n-1)(4T-6n-7)=15$, so $2n-1$ is a positive divisor of $15$. Thus $n=1,2,3,8$. The corresponding $(T,J)$ are $(7,11),(6,6),(7,5),(14,4)$, all allowing the stated transfers. Their sum is $\boxed{14}$.
