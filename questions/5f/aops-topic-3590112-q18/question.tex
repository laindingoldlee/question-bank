Tom and Jerry have positive integer numbers of coins. For a positive integer $n$, if Tom gives Jerry $n$ coins, Jerry has twice as many as Tom has left. If instead Jerry gives Tom $2$ coins, Tom has $n$ times as many as Jerry has left. Find the sum of all possible $n$.
