The first condition places $A,B,C$ on one line, and the second puts $D$ on that same line because $B\ne C$. Choose a coordinate increasing from $A$ toward $C$. Then $a<b<c$. Since $C$ is between $B$ and $D$ and $b<c$, the second condition implies $b<c<d$. Hence $a<b<c<d$, proving both required betweenness claims.
