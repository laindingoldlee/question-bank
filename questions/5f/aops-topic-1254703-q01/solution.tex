We need $25q+10d=1000$, i.e. $5q+2d=200$, with $q,d\ge0$. Then $q$ must be even, $q=0,2,\dots,40$, and each gives one $d=\dfrac{200-5q}{2}$. That is $\boxed{21}$ ways (every way uses at least one coin).
