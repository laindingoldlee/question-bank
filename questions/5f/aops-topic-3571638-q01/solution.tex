Set $s=a+b+c$. Clearing denominators gives $(100a+10b+c)s=1000a$. Since $100a\le100a+10b+c$, we have $1\le s\le10$. For each $s$, the integer $\dfrac{1000a}{s}$ must have hundreds digit $a$ and digit sum $s$. If $s<10$, write $b+c=s-a$ and substitute $c=s-a-b$ to obtain $9bs=1000a-99as-s^2$. Testing $s=1,\ldots,10$ and $1\le a\le\min(9,s)$, retaining integral $0\le b,c\le9$, gives just $(a,b,c)=(1,2,5)$. Direct checking gives $\dfrac{1}{1+2+5}=\dfrac{1}{8}=0.125$. Thus $\boxed{(1,2,5)}$.
