\textbf{Two players left.} Suppose $X$ spins first and $Y$ second. $X$ is the first to spin red with probability
\[\dfrac13+\left(\dfrac23\right)^2\cdot\dfrac13+\left(\dfrac23\right)^4\cdot\dfrac13+\cdots=\dfrac{\dfrac13}{1-\dfrac49}=\dfrac35.\]
So the first spinner wins with probability $\dfrac25$ and the second with probability $\dfrac35$.

\textbf{Three players.} The first red spin is Bob's, Julie's or Hak's with probabilities proportional to $1:\dfrac23:\dfrac49=9:6:4$, i.e. $\dfrac9{19},\dfrac6{19},\dfrac4{19}$.
\begin{itemize}
\item If Bob is eliminated, the next spinner is Julie, then Hak: Julie wins with probability $\dfrac25$.
\item If Julie is eliminated, she loses.
\item If Hak is eliminated, the next spinner is Bob, then Julie: Julie wins with probability $\dfrac35$.
\end{itemize}
Hence
\[P(\text{Julie wins})=\dfrac9{19}\cdot\dfrac25+\dfrac4{19}\cdot\dfrac35=\dfrac{18+12}{95}=\boxed{\dfrac6{19}}.\]
