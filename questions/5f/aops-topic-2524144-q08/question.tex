Bob, Julie and Hak play a game, taking turns in this cyclic order, starting with Bob. On a turn, the player spins a wheel that is $\dfrac13$ red and $\dfrac23$ green. A player who spins red is eliminated; a player who spins green stays in. Turns continue in the same cyclic order among the players still in the game, and the last player remaining wins. What is the probability that Julie wins?
