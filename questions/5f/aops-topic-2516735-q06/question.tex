A loan of $\$49.30$ is charged $1\%$ interest per day: at the end of each day, the amount owed is multiplied by $1.01$ and then rounded to the nearest whole number of dollars (amounts ending in exactly $.50$ are rounded up). No repayments are made. How much is owed after $30$ days, and how much of that is interest?
