The iterates imply $P(\dfrac{1}{j})=\dfrac{1}{(j+1)}$ for $j=1,\ldots,125$. Let $Q(x)=(x+1)P(x)-x$, a polynomial of degree at most 125. It vanishes at all 125 points $\dfrac{1}{j}$, so $Q(x)=C\prod_{j=1}^{125}(x-\dfrac{1}{j})$. At $x=-1$, $Q(-1)=1$, giving $C=-\dfrac{1}{126}$. Consequently $P(2)=\dfrac{2}{3}-\dfrac{[1\cdot3\cdots249]}{(378\cdot125!)}$. The numerator of the second term is odd, while its denominator has two-adic valuation $1+\lfloor\dfrac{125}{2}\rfloor+\lfloor\dfrac{125}{4}\rfloor+\cdots=1+62+31+15+7+3+1=120$. The first term has odd denominator, so subtracting it cannot cancel this factor of two. Thus the requested valuation is $\boxed{120}$.
