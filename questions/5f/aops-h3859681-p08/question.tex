Angela is standing on the bottom left square of a $3\times 3$ grid. She wants to reach the top right square by moving only right or up one square at a time. Find the number of ways Bill can fill each square with the letter $\mathtt{a}$, $\mathtt{b}$, or $\mathtt{c}$, such that every square contains exactly one letter and no matter what path Angela takes she will always encounter the letters $\mathtt{a}$, $\mathtt{b}$, $\mathtt{c}$ consecutively, in that order.
