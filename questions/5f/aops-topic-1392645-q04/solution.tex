By Vieta's formulas the sum of all four roots is $-1$ (the negative of the coefficient of $x^3$), whatever $c$ is. Since one root is $15$, the other three sum to $-1-15=\boxed{-16}$. (Here $c=-\dfrac{15^4+15^3+15+1}{15^2}=-\dfrac{54016}{225}$; the other roots are $\dfrac{1}{15}$ and two further real roots, since the polynomial is palindromic.)
