Measure times in minutes after 9:00. Ann arrives at $a\in[0,45]$ and Bob at $b\in[30,60]$; the point $(a,b)$ is uniform in a $45\times30$ rectangle of area $1350$. Ann is there during $[a,a+15]$ and Bob during $[b,b+5]$. The intervals overlap exactly when $a\le b+5$ and $b\le a+15$, that is
\[
b-15\le a\le b+5 .
\]

\begin{asy}

fill((15,30)--(35,30)--(45,40)--(45,60)--cycle,paleblue);
draw((15,30)--(45,60),blue);draw((35,30)--(45,40),blue);
draw((0,30)--(45,30)--(45,60)--(0,60)--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
label("$0$",(0,30),S);label("$15$",(15,30),S);label("$35$",(35,30),S);label("$45$",(45,30),S);
label("$30$",(0,30),W);label("$40$",(0,40),W);label("$60$",(0,60),W);
draw((0,40)--(0.8,40));
label("$a$",(22.5,27),S);label("$b$",(-6,45),W);
label("$a=b-15$",(20,50),blue+fontsize(9pt));label("$a=b+5$",(45,40),E,blue+fontsize(9pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline

For each $b$ the allowed $a$ form an interval (the shaded band):
\begin{itemize}
\item $30\le b\le40$: $a$ runs from $b-15$ to $b+5$, length $20$; this strip has area $10\cdot20=200$.
\item $40\le b\le60$: $a$ runs from $b-15$ to $45$, length $60-b$; this part is a triangle of area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot20\cdot20=200$.
\end{itemize}
The probability is
\[
\dfrac{200+200}{1350}=\boxed{\dfrac{8}{27}}.
\]
