Ann arrives at a coffee shop at a time chosen uniformly at random between 9:00 AM and 9:45 AM and stays for $15$ minutes. Bob arrives at a time chosen uniformly at random between 9:30 AM and 10:00 AM, independently of Ann, and stays for $5$ minutes. What is the probability that Ann and Bob are in the coffee shop at the same time? Express your answer as a common fraction.
