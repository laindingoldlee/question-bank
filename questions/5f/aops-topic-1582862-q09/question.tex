As you sit in your chair, you are whirling through space with Earth as it moves around the Sun. Assume Earth moves on a circle of radius $1.4947\times10^{11}$ metres around the Sun and completes one revolution every $365.25$ days. What is your ``sitting'' speed relative to the Sun, in kilometres per hour, rounded to the nearest $100$ km per hour? Use $3.14159$ for $\pi$.
