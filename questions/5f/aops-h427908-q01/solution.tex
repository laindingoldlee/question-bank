Let the center be $c$. Add the sums on the middle row, middle column, and both main diagonals. Each noncentral entry is counted once, and the center is counted four times. The result is
\[
1+2+\cdots+9+3c=45+3c.
\]
It is divisible by $9$, so $3\mid c$.
