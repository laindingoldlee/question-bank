A child without a candy must see every other candy-less child, if any, inside their own pair. Thus all candy-less children belong to one pair. There cannot be none: everyone's statement would then be true although everyone has candy. There cannot be just one: that child's candy-holding partner would also make a true statement. Exactly two in one pair work, since both tell the truth and all children in other pairs see candy-less children outside their pair. Therefore $26-2=\boxed{24}$ received candy.
