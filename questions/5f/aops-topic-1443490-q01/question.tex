Suppose
\[
2\sqrt[3]{4}=w+\dfrac{1}{x+\dfrac{1}{y+\dfrac{1}{z+\cdots}}}
\]
is the simple continued fraction of $2\sqrt[3]{4}$, where $w,x,y,z,\dots$ are positive integers. What is the value of $x$?
