Note $2\sqrt[3]4=\sqrt[3]{8\cdot4}=\sqrt[3]{32}$. Since $27<32<64$, $w=3$, and
\[
x=\left\lfloor\dfrac{1}{\sqrt[3]{32}-3}\right\rfloor .
\]
We have $5<\dfrac{1}{\sqrt[3]{32}-3}<6$ exactly when $3+\dfrac{1}{6}<\sqrt[3]{32}<3+\dfrac{1}{5}$, that is, $\left(\dfrac{19}{6}\right)^3<32<\left(\dfrac{16}{5}\right)^3$. Both hold:
\[
\left(\dfrac{19}{6}\right)^3=\dfrac{6859}{216}\approx31.75<32,\qquad \left(\dfrac{16}{5}\right)^3=\dfrac{4096}{125}=32.768>32 .
\]
Hence $x=\boxed{5}$.
