Choose which $4$ of the $8$ positions hold the maths and science books: $\binom84=70$ ways. In those positions the top two must hold the maths books ($2!$ orders) and the bottom two the science books ($2!$ orders). The same applies to the other four positions for history and English. The total is $70\cdot2!\cdot2!\cdot2!\cdot2!=\boxed{1120}$.
