The first coach can choose a set of $5$ starters in $\binom{12}{5}=792$ ways, and independently the second coach can choose in $792$ ways. Different choices give different sets of $10$ players (the two rosters are disjoint), so the number of possible sets is
\[
792^2=\boxed{627264}.
\]
``Without regard to position'' means that the five starters are not assigned to particular positions (point guard, center, \dots); if they were, each team would have $12\cdot11\cdot10\cdot9\cdot8=95040$ ordered choices and the count would be $95040^2=9032601600$.
