Expand the product $\prod_i\left(1+p_i+\cdots+p_i^{a_i}\right)$: each term of the expansion is obtained by choosing one power $p_i^{e_i}$ ($0\le e_i\le a_i$) from each factor, giving $p_1^{e_1}\cdots p_k^{e_k}$. By unique factorisation these are exactly the divisors of $n$, each appearing once. So the product is the sum of all divisors. Each factor is a geometric series, $1+p+\cdots+p^a=\dfrac{p^{a+1}-1}{p-1}$.

For $720=2^4\cdot3^2\cdot5$:
\[
\sigma(720)=(1+2+4+8+16)(1+3+9)(1+5)=31\cdot13\cdot6=\boxed{2418}.
\]
