Four equal balls of radius $r$ lie on a flat table, with their centres at the corners of a square, each ball touching its two neighbours. A fifth ball of the same radius is placed on top of the four so that it touches all of them. Find the distance from the farthest point of the fifth ball to the plane of the table.
