Use the difference of squares twice:
\[
x^4-y^4=(x^2-y^2)(x^2+y^2)=\boxed{(x-y)(x+y)(x^2+y^2)}.
\]
The factor $x^2+y^2$ cannot be split further: a factorisation into two linear integer factors $(px+qy)(rx+sy)$ would vanish at a point with $(x,y)\ne(0,0)$ on the line $px+qy=0$, but $x^2+y^2>0$ there.
