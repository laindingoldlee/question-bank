In triangle $ABC$, the points $E$ and $F$ lie on the sides $AB$ and $AC$ (with $E\ne B$ and $F\ne C$) so that $AE=AF$. Let $D$ be the midpoint of $BC$, and suppose that $\angle EDB=\angle FDC$. Prove that triangle $ABC$ is isosceles with $AB=AC$.
