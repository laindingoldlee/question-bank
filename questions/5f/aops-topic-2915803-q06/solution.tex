Each side is $2+4=6$, so the triangle is equilateral. Its altitude is $3\sqrt3$, giving area $\dfrac{1}{2}(6)(3\sqrt3)=\boxed{9\sqrt3}$.

pair A=(0,3*sqrt(3)),B=(-3,0),C=(3,0),D=(1,0),Ee=A+(C-A)/3,F=B+(A-B)/3;draw(A--B--C--cycle);dot(D);dot(Ee);dot(F);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,S);label("$E$",Ee,E);label("$F$",F,W);
\end{asy}
