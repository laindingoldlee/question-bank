A covfefe number $\overline{pepe}=101\cdot\overline{pe}$ is determined by the digits $p$ and $e$. It is less than $8000$ exactly when $p\le7$. So $p\in\{1,\dots,7\}$ (7 choices) and $e\in\{0,\dots,9\}$ (10 choices), giving $7\cdot10=\boxed{70}$ covfefe numbers.
