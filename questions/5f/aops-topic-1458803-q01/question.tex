Call a positive integer a \emph{covfefe number} if its decimal representation has the form $\overline{pepe}$: four digits, where the first and third digits are both $p$ and the second and fourth digits are both $e$ ($p$ and $e$ are digits, not necessarily distinct, and $p\ne0$). How many covfefe numbers are less than $8000$?
