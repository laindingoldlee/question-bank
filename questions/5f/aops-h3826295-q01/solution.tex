$12!=2^{10}3^55^27\cdot11$, so it has $11\cdot6\cdot3\cdot2\cdot2=792$ divisors. A divisor is divisible by $24=2^33$ precisely when its exponent of $2$ is at least $3$ and its exponent of $3$ at least $1$. There are $8\cdot5\cdot3\cdot2\cdot2=480$ such divisors. The required fraction is $\boxed{\dfrac{480}{792}=\dfrac{20}{33}}$.
