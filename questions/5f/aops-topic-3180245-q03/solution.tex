Since $\sqrt{x^2}=|x|$, positive $x$ would make the right side 0, impossible. For negative $x$, the equation is $\dfrac{1}{x}=2x$, so $x=-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$. Hence $\boxed{p+q=4}$.
