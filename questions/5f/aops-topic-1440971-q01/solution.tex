Let the length be $l$ and the width $w$. Increasing the length by $1$ adds a strip of area $1\cdot w$, so $w=12$. Increasing the width by $2$ adds a strip of area $2l$, so $l=21$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real l=21,w=12;
fill((l,0)--(l+1,0)--(l+1,w)--(l,w)--cycle,pink); fill((0,w)--(l,w)--(l,w+2)--(0,w+2)--cycle,lightcyan);
draw((0,0)--(l,0)--(l,w)--(0,w)--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw((l,0)--(l+1,0)--(l+1,w)--(l,w),red+dashed); draw((0,w)--(0,w+2)--(l,w+2)--(l,w),blue+dashed);
label("$l$",(l/2,0),S); label("$w$",(0,w/2),W);
label("$1$",(l+0.5,0),S,red); label("$2$",(0,w+1),W,blue);
label("$12$",(l+0.5,w/2),E,red); label("$42$",(l/2,w+1),blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The original area is $21\cdot12=\boxed{252}$.
