There are $\dbinom52=10$ equally likely pairs of cards, and $\dbinom32=3$ of them consist of two blank cards. The probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{3}{10}}$. (Equivalently, $\dfrac35\cdot\dfrac24=\dfrac{3}{10}$.)
