For digits $a,b$ with $a\ne0$, let $N=\overline{ababab}$. Find the smallest positive integer $n$ such that, for some choice of the digits $a$ and $b$, the product $nN$ is a perfect square. For which digits $a,b$ is this smallest $n$ achieved?
