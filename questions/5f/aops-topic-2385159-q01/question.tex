Points $D$, $E$ and $F$ are chosen inside triangle $ABC$ such that $E$ lies on $\overline{AD}$, $F$ lies on $\overline{BE}$, $D$ lies on $\overline{CF}$, and $\angle ABE=\angle BCF=\angle CAD$. If $BC=2DF$ and the areas of triangles $ABE$, $BCF$ and $CAD$ are $30$, $35$ and $40$ respectively, what is the area of triangle $ABC$?
