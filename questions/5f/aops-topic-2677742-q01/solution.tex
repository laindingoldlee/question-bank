The numbers $1$--$9$ use $9$ digits and $10$--$99$ use $180$, a total of $189$. The remaining $2020-189=1831=3\cdot610+1$ digits are the numbers $100$ to $709$ followed by the first digit, $7$, of $710$.

A number is congruent mod $9$ to its digit sum, and so is each block. So the number is congruent to
\[(1+2+\cdots+709)+7=\dfrac{709\cdot710}{2}+7=251695+7\pmod 9.\]
Since $251695\equiv2+5+1+6+9+5=28\equiv1$, the remainder is $1+7=\boxed{8}$.
