Alan has been running around a large park for the past $30$ minutes, in no particular direction, at a constant speed of $3$ metres per second, and he now notices that his keys are missing. When he checked $10$ minutes ago, he still had them. Alan can search the ground at a rate of $1$ square kilometre per hour. In the worst case, how long will it take him to search every point where the keys could be? (The park contains every point that Alan could have reached.)
