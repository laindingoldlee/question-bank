The five numbers $b-a_i$ are different integers with product $2009=7^2\cdot41$. At most two of them have absolute value $1$ (namely $1$ and $-1$), so at least three have absolute value at least $7$, the smallest prime factor of $2009$. Since $2009$ has only three prime factors counted with multiplicity, exactly three factors have absolute values $7$, $7$, $41$, and the other two are $1$ and $-1$. The two of absolute value $7$ are different, so they are $7$ and $-7$. The product $1\cdot(-1)\cdot7\cdot(-7)\cdot(\pm41)=\pm2009$ must be positive, so the last factor is $41$.

Hence $\{b-a_i\}=\{1,-1,7,-7,41\}$, and adding,
\[
5b-9=1-1+7-7+41=41,
\]
so $b=\boxed{10}$.
