Write $n=a^2+b^2$ with $a>b\ge1$. List the values of $a^2+b^2$ below $100$ (these need $a\le9$) and look for repeats. They are
\[
\begin{array}{l}
5,10,13,17,20,25,26,29,34,37,40,41,45,50,52,53,58,61,65,68,73,74,80,82,85,89,90,97
\end{array}
\]
and the first values that occur twice are
\[
65=8^2+1^2=7^2+4^2,\qquad 85=9^2+2^2=7^2+6^2 .
\]
(Note $50=7^2+1^2=5^2+5^2$ does not count, since $5$ and $5$ are not distinct.) Every number below $85$ other than $65$ appears at most once with distinct parts, so the second smallest such integer is $\boxed{85}$.
