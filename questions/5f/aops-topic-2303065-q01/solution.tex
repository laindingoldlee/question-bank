Let $S$ be the sum. Then
\[2S=2^{101}+2^{102}+\cdots+2^{10001},\]
and subtracting $S=2^{100}+2^{101}+\cdots+2^{10000}$, all terms except two cancel:
\[S=2S-S=\boxed{2^{10001}-2^{100}}.\]
(This is the geometric series formula: the first term is $2^{100}$, the ratio is $2$, and there are $9901$ terms, so $S=2^{100}\cdot\dfrac{2^{9901}-1}{2-1}$.)
