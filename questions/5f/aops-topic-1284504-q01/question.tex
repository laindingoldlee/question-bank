A student notices that roughly half of the primes are congruent to $5$ modulo $6$ and conjectures that among any two consecutive primes, both at least $5$, at least one is congruent to $5$ modulo $6$. Prove that every prime $p\ge5$ is congruent to $1$ or $5$ modulo $6$, and show that the conjecture is false.
