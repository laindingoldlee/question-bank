The numbers $4,6,8,9$ cannot be so written. The smallest sum of distinct composites is $4+6=10$, so all composites below $10$ fail. Every even integer $n\ge10$ is $4+(n-4)$, a sum of distinct positive composites. Every odd composite is either $9$ or at least $15$; for odd $n\ge15$, write $n=9+(n-9)$, where $n-9\ge6$ is even and distinct from $9$. Thus the complete list is $4,6,8,9$, and its product is $\boxed{1728}$.
