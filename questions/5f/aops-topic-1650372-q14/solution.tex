If she read $x$ pages on the first day, then she read $x,\dfrac {x}{2},\dfrac {x}{4},\dfrac {x}{8},\dfrac {x}{16}$ pages, in total
\[
x\left(1+\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{8}+\dfrac{1}{16}\right)=\dfrac{31x}{16}=248,
\]
so $x=\dfrac{248\cdot16}{31}=\boxed{128}$. (Days: $128+64+32+16+8=248$.)
