The six letters of ``TIN CAN'' (T, I, N, C, A, N) are arranged in a row and a single space is placed in one of the five gaps between consecutive letters, so that two groups of letters are formed. For example, ``NIT NAC'' is one arrangement, ``NAC NIT'' is a different one, and ``NITNAC'' (no space) does not count. How many arrangements are possible?
