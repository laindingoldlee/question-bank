The leap years from $1990$ to $2017$ are $1992,1996,2000,2004,2008,2012,2016$: seven of them ($2000$ is a leap year, being divisible by $400$). So the number of days is
\[
28\cdot365+7=10220+7=\boxed{10227}.
\]
(Check: $10227=7\cdot1461$ is a multiple of $7$, which is why the days of the week repeat after these $28$ years.)
