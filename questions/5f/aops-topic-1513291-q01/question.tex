The $1990$ and $2018$ calendars are identical in the number of days in each month and the day of the week on which each month begins; in fact the calendar repeats in this way every $28$ years until the year $2100$. How many days are there in the $28$ years preceding $2018$ (that is, the years $1990$ through $2017$)?
