A domino is an \emph{unordered} pair of numbers from $\{0,1,\dots,6\}$, possibly equal (the domino $2|5$ is the same as $5|2$). There are $\dbinom72=21$ dominoes with two different numbers and $7$ doubles $0|0,1|1,\dots,6|6$, so $28$ dominoes in all. The probability of a double is
\[
\dfrac{7}{28}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{4}}.
\]
(The tempting answer $\dfrac{1}{7}$ comes from fixing one side and asking for the other side to match, which treats the $49$ \emph{ordered} pairs as equally likely; but each non-double domino corresponds to two ordered pairs while a double corresponds to only one.)
