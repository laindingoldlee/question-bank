The $k$th product is $(10^k-2)(10^k+2)=10^{2k}-4$, for $k=1,\ldots,2024$. Thus the sum is $\sum_{k=1}^{2024}10^{2k}-8096$. Its last two terms contribute $100+10000-8096=2004$, and all higher terms start at the millions place. Hence the decimal has 2022 isolated leading digits 1, separated by zeros, followed by the six-digit block $002004$. The digit sum is $2022+2+4=\boxed{2028}$.
