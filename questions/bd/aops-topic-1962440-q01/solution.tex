An inscribed angle is half the arc it subtends, the arc lying on the opposite side from the vertex.

$\angle ABC=130^\circ$ subtends the arc $AC$ not containing $B$, so that arc is $260^\circ$, and the arc $A\to B\to C$ is $360^\circ-260^\circ=100^\circ$. Likewise $\angle CDE=110^\circ$ gives arc $C\to D\to E$ equal to $360^\circ-220^\circ=140^\circ$. The remaining arc from $E$ back to $A$ (not containing $B,C,D$) is
\[
360^\circ-100^\circ-140^\circ=120^\circ .
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real[] t={150,70,10,-90,-170};
string[] nm={"$A$","$B$","$C$","$D$","$E$"};
pair[] P;
for(int i=0;i<5;++i) P.push(dir(t[i]));
draw(unitcircle,linewidth(0.8));
draw(P[0]--P[1]--P[2],blue);
draw(P[2]--P[3]--P[4],deepgreen);
draw(P[0]--P[2]--P[4],red+linewidth(0.9));
for(int i=0;i<5;++i){dot(P[i]);label(nm[i],P[i],1.4*dir(t[i]));}
label("$130^\circ$",P[1],3.2*dir(t[1]+180),blue+fontsize(8pt));
label("$110^\circ$",P[3],3.2*dir(t[3]+180),deepgreen+fontsize(8pt));
label("$60^\circ$",P[2]+(-0.56,0.0),red+fontsize(7pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

$\angle ACE$ subtends exactly this arc $EA$, so $\angle ACE=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot120^\circ=\boxed{60^\circ}$.
