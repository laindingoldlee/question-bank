The rows have $3,7,15,\dots$ boxes: row $k$ has $2^{k+1}-1$ boxes (true for $k=1$, and $2\left(2^{k+1}-1\right)+1=2^{k+2}-1$). The total is
\[\sum_{k=1}^{20}\left(2^{k+1}-1\right)=\left(2^{22}-4\right)-20=2^{22}-24=8\left(2^{19}-3\right).\]
Since $2^{19}-3$ is odd, the largest power of $2$ dividing the total is $\boxed{8}$.
