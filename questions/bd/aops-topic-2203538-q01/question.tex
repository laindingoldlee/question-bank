There are $51$ pigeons in a flock. Every pigeon dislikes exactly $3$ of the other pigeons and does not want to be in the same group as any of them. (Dislike need not be mutual: pigeon $A$ may dislike pigeon $B$ while $B$ does not dislike $A$; still, $A$ and $B$ may not share a group.) The flock is to be divided into $n$ groups, each pigeon in exactly one group, so that no pigeon is in a group with a pigeon it dislikes. Find the smallest $n$ such that this is possible for every pattern of dislikes.
