\textbf{$1010$ are not enough.} The $1010$ even numbers $2,4,\dots,2020$ have the common factor $2$, so no two of them are relatively prime.

\textbf{$1011$ are enough.} Split the set into the $1010$ pairs $\{1,2\},\{3,4\},\dots,\{2019,2020\}$. If $1011$ numbers are chosen, two of them lie in the same pair, by the pigeonhole principle. They are consecutive integers, and consecutive integers are relatively prime: a common divisor of $k$ and $k+1$ divides $(k+1)-k=1$.

So the answer is $\boxed{1011}$.
