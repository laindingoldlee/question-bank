(a) With $ J(t)=1 $ during the pulse and 0 afterward, the specified balances are $ CV\prime=I_pJ-g(V-E)$ and $ s\prime=\rho J-\lambda s $. (b) Solving the two first-order linear equations on $ 0\le t\le T $ gives \[V(t)=E+\dfrac{I_p}{g}(1-e^{-\dfrac{gt}{C}}),\qquad s(t)=\dfrac{\rho}{\lambda}(1-e^{-\lambda t}).\]After the pulse, the deviations decay: \[V(t)=E+\dfrac{I_p}{g}(1-e^{-\dfrac{gT}{C}})e^{-\dfrac{g(t-T)}{C}},\quad s(t)=\dfrac{\rho}{\lambda}(1-e^{-\lambda T})e^{-\lambda(t-T)}.\](c) Integrate only over the pulse, since $ u=0 $ afterward: \[\boxed{\Delta w=\dfrac{\eta I_p}{g}\left[T-\dfrac{C}{g}(1-e^{-\dfrac{gT}{C}})\right]}.\]These formulas solve the explicitly defined toy system; they do not infer unspecified biological channel or learning dynamics.
