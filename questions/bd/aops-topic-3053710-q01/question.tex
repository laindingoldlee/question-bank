Consider a toy membrane model with capacitance $ C>0 $, conductance $ g>0 $, rest potential $ E $, and voltage $ V(0)=E $. Input current is $ I_p $ on $ 0\le t\le T $ and zero afterward. A chemical quantity $ s $ starts at 0, is released at rate $\rho $ during the pulse, and decays at rate $\lambda s $, with $\lambda>0 $. (a) Write charge and chemical balance equations. (b) Solve for $ V(t)$ and $ s(t)$. (c) A weight follows $ w\prime=\eta u(t)(V-E)$, where $ u=1 $ during the pulse and 0 afterward. Find its total change.
