When an equation has no constant term, every term contains the factor $x$, so we can factor it out. A product is $0$ exactly when one of its factors is $0$. (Do not divide by $x$ instead: that loses the solution $x=0$.)

\textbf{(a)} $2x^2-8x=2x(x-4)=0$, so $x=0$ or $x=4$: the solutions are $\boxed{x=0\text{ or }x=4}$.

\textbf{(b)} $x^3-5x^2+6x=x\left(x^2-5x+6\right)=x(x-2)(x-3)=0$, so the solutions are $\boxed{x=0,\ x=2\text{ or }x=3}$.

(After the factor $x$ is removed, what remains is a quadratic, which can be factored as here or solved with the quadratic formula.)
