Alice, Bob, Carl, and Dave know which of them holds a coin. Alice says she holds it. Bob says Carl holds it. Carl says exactly one of the four statements is true. Dave says a male holds it. Alice is female; the other three are male. Who holds the coin, if each statement has its usual truth value?
