Vieta gives $\sum r_i=0$, $\sum_{i<j}r_ir_j=4$, and $r_1r_2r_3=1$. Thus $\sum r_i^2=-8$, and summing $r_i^3+4r_i-1=0$ gives $\sum r_i^3=3$. The common denominator is $\prod(1+3r_i)=1+9(4)+27=64$, which is nonzero. The numerator is $2\sum r_i^2(1+3r_i)=2(-8+9)=2$. Therefore the value is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{32}}$.
