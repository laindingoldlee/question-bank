Put the large centre at $(1,1)$ and a corner circle centre at $(r,r)$. External tangency gives $\sqrt2(1-r)=1+r$. Solving yields $r=\dfrac{\sqrt2-1}{\sqrt2+1}=3-2\sqrt2=3-\sqrt8$. Therefore $\boxed{a+b=11}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
real r=3-2*sqrt(2);draw((0,0)--(2,0)--(2,2)--(0,2)--cycle);draw(circle((1,1),1));for(int i=0;i<2;++i)for(int j=0;j<2;++j)draw(circle((i==0?r:2-r,j==0?r:2-r),r));draw((r,r)--(1,1),dashed);label("$r$",(r,r),SW);
\end{asy}
