The conditions give $\dfrac{BD}{BA}=\dfrac{BA}{BC}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Triangles $BDA$ and $BAC$ share the included angle at $B$. They are similar by SAS, with scale factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Their corresponding sides $DA,AC$ therefore satisfy $\dfrac{AD}{AC}=\dfrac{1}{2}$, proving $AC=2AD$.

\end{asy}
