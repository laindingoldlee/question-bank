The largest sphere is the inscribed sphere. The tetrahedron has volume $V=\dfrac{a^3}{6\sqrt2}=\dfrac{216}{6\sqrt2}=18\sqrt2$ and total surface area $S=4\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}a^2=36\sqrt3$. Splitting it into four pyramids from the incentre, $V=\dfrac{1}{3}Sr$, so
\[r=\dfrac{3V}{S}=\dfrac{54\sqrt2}{36\sqrt3}=\dfrac{\sqrt6}{2}.\]
