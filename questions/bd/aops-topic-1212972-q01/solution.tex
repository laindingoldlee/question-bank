Let $X_i=1$ if the $i$-th yak is defeated and $0$ otherwise. Then $E[X_i]=\dfrac{1}{5}$, and by linearity of expectation the expected number of defeated yaks is
\[E[X_1+\cdots+X_7]=7\cdot\dfrac{1}{5}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{5}}.\]
