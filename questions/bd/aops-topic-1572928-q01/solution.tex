Test whether $H+1$ is a factor of the numerator by substituting $H=-1$ (factor theorem):
\[
-4.9(-1)^2+15.2(-1)+20.1=-4.9-15.2+20.1=0 .
\]
So $H+1$ is a factor. Dividing (or matching coefficients in $(H+1)(pH+q)=pH^2+(p+q)H+q$, which gives $p=-4.9$ and $q=20.1$; indeed $p+q=15.2$):
\[
-4.9H^2+15.2H+20.1=(H+1)(20.1-4.9H).
\]
Hence, for $H\ne-1$,
\[
\dfrac{-4.9H^2+15.2H+20.1}{H+1}=\boxed{20.1-4.9H}.
\]
