Write the augmented matrix and reduce:
\[
\left(\begin{array}{cc|c}2&4&12\\12&6&14\end{array}\right)
\xrightarrow{R_1\div2}
\left(\begin{array}{cc|c}1&2&6\\12&6&14\end{array}\right)
\xrightarrow{R_2-12R_1}
\left(\begin{array}{cc|c}1&2&6\\0&-18&-58\end{array}\right).
\]
Dividing the second row by $-18$ gives $y=\dfrac{58}{18}=\dfrac{29}{9}$. Then $R_1-2R_2$ gives $x=6-\dfrac{58}{9}=-\dfrac{4}{9}$. So
\[
\boxed{x=-\dfrac{4}{9},\ y=\dfrac{29}{9}}.
\]
Check: $2\cdot\left(-\dfrac{4}{9}\right)+4\cdot\dfrac{29}{9}=\dfrac{108}{9}=12$ and $12\cdot\left(-\dfrac{4}{9}\right)+6\cdot\dfrac{29}{9}=\dfrac{-48+174}{9}=14$.
