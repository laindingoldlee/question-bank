Since $AC$ is a diameter, $\angle ADC=90^\circ$. Pythagoras gives $AD=\sqrt{12^2-6^2}=6\sqrt3$. Also $\sin \angle D A C = \dfrac{D C}{A C} = \dfrac{1}{2}$, so $\boxed{\angle DAC=30^\circ}$ and $\boxed{AD=6\sqrt3}$.

\end{asy}
