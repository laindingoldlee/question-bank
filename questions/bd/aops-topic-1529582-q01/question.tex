Ted has entered a buffet line in which he chooses two different kinds of meat, three different vegetables and four different desserts. The choices are:
\begin{itemize}
\item meat: beef, chicken, pork, duck, fish;
\item vegetables: baked beans, corn, potatoes, broccoli, chives;
\item desserts: brownies, chocolate cake, chocolate pudding, ice cream, apricot pops.
\end{itemize}
If the order of the food items is not important, how many different meals might he choose?
