Let $A_h(j)$ count unordered rooted binary-tree colourings of height $h$ with $j$ black leaves. At height zero, $A_0(0)=A_0(1)=1$. A tree is an unordered pair of child trees. Thus for $u<v$ contribute $A_{h-1}(u)A_{h-1}(v)$ to $A_h(u+v)$, and for $u=v$ contribute $\dfrac{A_{h-1} (u) (A_{h-1}(u)+1)}{2}$. This recurrence incorporates every automorphism, since each vertex may interchange its two subtrees. The root value is the fraction of black leaves, so the desired count is $A_5(16)$. Applying the recurrence successively for $h=1,2,3,4,5$ gives central entries $1,2,5,35,2682$. Consequently $N=2682$ and the remainder is $\boxed{682}$.
