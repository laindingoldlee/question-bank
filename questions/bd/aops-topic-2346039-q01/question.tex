Ralph takes the AMC 8, which has $25$ questions, each with $5$ answer choices of which exactly one is correct. He knows none of the answers and guesses on every question, choosing one of the $5$ choices uniformly at random and independently for different questions. What is the probability that he gets exactly $23$ questions right?
