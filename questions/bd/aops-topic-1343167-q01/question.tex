A ``triangle'' $ABC$ has side lengths $AB=3$, $BC=5$ and $CA=8$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Use Heron's formula to compute its area.
\item[(b)] Explain what the result says about the points $A$, $B$, $C$. Is $ABC$ a genuine triangle?
\end{enumerate}
