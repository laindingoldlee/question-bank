The divisor factors as $(x^2-x+1)(x^4+x^3+x^2+x+1)$, whose roots are the primitive sixth roots and the nonidentity fifth roots, all simple. At such a root $\zeta$, divisibility requires $\zeta^{10n}+\zeta^{9n}+\zeta^{8n}+\zeta^{7n}+\zeta^{6n}=5$. Each summand has modulus one, so equality forces all five summands to equal one. Consecutive powers then give $\zeta^n=1$. For primitive roots this is equivalent to $6\mid n$ and $5\mid n$, hence $30\mid n$. Conversely that condition makes every root a zero of the dividend. The count is $\lfloor\dfrac{2023}{30}\rfloor=\boxed{67}$.
