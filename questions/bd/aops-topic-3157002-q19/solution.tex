Write $ f(7)=7^K $, where $ K=6^E $ and $ E=f(5)\ge3 $. The numbers 7 and 14 cannot divide $ 7^K-1 $. For every $ d\in\{2,3,4,5,6,8,9,10,12,13,15\}$, $\gcd(7,d)=1 $ and Euler's $\varphi(d)$ is one of $ 1,2,4,6,8,12 $, each dividing $ 6^E $. Thus all these $ d $, and also 1, divide the number. Modulo 11, the order of 7 is 10; since $ 6^E\equiv6\pmod{10}$ and $ 7^6\equiv4\pmod{11}$, 11 does not divide it. Hence the answer is $ 120-7-11-14=\boxed{88}$.
