Both comparisons reduce to comparing positive numbers through their squares (for positive numbers, $u<v$ exactly when $u^2<v^2$).

\textbf{Upper bound.} $7-4\sqrt3<1\iff6<4\sqrt3$. Both sides are positive and $6^2=36<48=(4\sqrt3)^2$, so $6<4\sqrt3$ and $7-4\sqrt3<1$.

\textbf{Lower bound.} $7-4\sqrt3>0\iff7>4\sqrt3$, and $7^2=49>48=(4\sqrt3)^2$.

So $0<7-4\sqrt3<1$. (In fact $7-4\sqrt3=(2-\sqrt3)^2=\dfrac{1}{7+4\sqrt3}\approx0.072$.)
