Two drivers travel the same route from Pittsburgh to Columbus and back, each at the same constant speed and taking 3 hours total. Their independent uniformly random departure times are in 12–3 p.m. and 1–4 p.m. respectively. Find the probability of an on-road meeting closer to Pittsburgh than Columbus, and give the sum of its reduced numerator and denominator.
