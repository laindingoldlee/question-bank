Write $A=kB$ with a positive integer $k$. Complementary means $A+B=90$, so $(k+1)B=90$. Thus $k+1$ is a divisor of $90$ with $k+1\ge2$, and every such divisor gives a valid pair $B=\dfrac{90}{k+1}$, $A=90-B$.

Since $90=2\cdot3^2\cdot5$ has $2\cdot3\cdot2=12$ divisors, there are $12-1=11$ choices for $k+1$. Different choices give different values of $B$ and hence of $A$, so there are $\boxed{11}$ possible measures for $A$: with $k+1=2,3,5,6,9,10,15,18,30,45,90$ we get $A=45,60,72,75,80,81,84,85,87,88,89$.
