Let $\varepsilon$ be an ellipse centered around the origin, such that its minor axis is perpendicular to the $x$-axis. The length of the ellipse's major and minor axes is $8$ and $6$, respectively. Then, let $ABCD$ be a rectangle centered around the origin, such that $AB$ is parallel to the $x$-axis. The lengths of $AB$ and $BC$ are $8$ and $3\sqrt2$, respectively. The area outside the ellipse but inside the rectangle can be expressed as $a\sqrt{b}-c-d\pi$, for positive integers $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$ where $b$ is not divisible by a perfect square of any prime. Find $a + b + c + d$.
