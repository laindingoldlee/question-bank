The ellipse is $\dfrac{x^2}{16}+\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$ and the rectangle has $|x|\le4$, $|y|\le\dfrac{3}{\sqrt2}$. Its area is $24\sqrt2$. The part of the ellipse inside the rectangle has area $16\int_0^{\dfrac{3}{\sqrt2}}\sqrt{1-\dfrac{y^2}{9}}\,dy$. Substitute $y=3\sin u$: this becomes $48\int_0^{\dfrac{\pi}{4}}\cos^2u\,du=6\pi+12$. The desired area is $24\sqrt2-12-6\pi$, giving $a+b+c+d=\boxed{44}$.
