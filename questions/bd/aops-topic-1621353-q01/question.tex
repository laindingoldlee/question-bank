A rectangle is inscribed in an isosceles right triangle so that two of its vertices lie on the hypotenuse and the other two lie on the legs, one on each leg. The hypotenuse has length $45$ inches, and two adjacent sides of the rectangle are in the ratio $5:2$. Find the side lengths of the rectangle. (There are two cases.)
