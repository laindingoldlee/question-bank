There are 21 possible lines and seven parallel classes of three lines, classified by the sum modulo seven of their endpoint indices. Choosing three distinct directions gives $\binom73 3^3=945$ unordered triples. Exactly 140 are concurrent: at each of the seven vertices, choose three of its six lines. To verify that there are no other triple concurrences, use $\zeta^7=1$, $1+\zeta+\cdots+\zeta^6=0$ and the exact line equation $z+\zeta^{a+b}\overline z=\zeta^a+\zeta^b$. Intersect each pair of different directions and substitute in the third equation; the only solutions are the stated vertex triples. Thus 805 unordered triples bound triangles. With independent selection and replacement, the probability is $\dfrac{6\cdot805}{21^3}=\dfrac{230}{441}$, giving $\boxed{671}$.
