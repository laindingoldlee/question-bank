In regular heptagon $\mathcal{H}$, three pairs of vertices are randomly selected, and a line is drawn between every pair. The probability that the three lines drawn bound a triangle with a positive finite area can be written as $\dfrac{m}{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Compute $m+n$.

Note: the same pair of vertices can be selected multiple times. For example, one could draw lines $AB$, $AB$, and $AC$.
