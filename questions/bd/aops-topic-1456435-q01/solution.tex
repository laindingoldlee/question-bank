We have $p=\dfrac{\pi}{ie}$. Since $\dfrac{1}{i}=\dfrac{-i}{i\cdot(-i)}=-i$,
\[
p=-\dfrac{\pi}{e}\,i .
\]
With $\dfrac{\pi}{e}\approx\dfrac{3.14159}{2.71828}\approx1.1557$, this is $p\approx\boxed{0-1.16\,i}$, that is $p\approx-1.16i$.
