A group of $25$ people consists of knights, serfs and damsels. Knights always tell the truth, serfs always lie, and each damsel alternates: if she answers one question truthfully she lies on the next, and vice versa. Each of the $25$ people was asked, in order: ``Are you a knight?'' ($17$ said yes), ``Are you a damsel?'' ($12$ said yes), and ``Are you a serf?'' ($8$ said yes). How many knights are in the group?

$\textbf{(A)}\ 4\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ 5\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ 9\qquad\textbf{(D)}\ 13\qquad\textbf{(E)}\ 17$
