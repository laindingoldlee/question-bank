Let $u,v,w$ be the three real roots of $t^3-3t+1=0$. There are three distinct roots, as its signs change on $(-2,-1),(0,1),(1,2)$. Since $u^3=3u-1$, substitution shows that $u^3,v^3,w^3$ are the three roots of the given cubic. Thus $a^{\dfrac{2}{3}} , b^{\dfrac{2}{3}} , c^{\dfrac{2}{3}}$ are $u^2,v^2,w^2$ under real cube-root convention. Vieta gives $u+v+w=0,uv+vw+wu=-3,uvw=-1$, so the squared values have elementary sums $6,9,1$. Therefore the sum of reciprocal pair-sums is $\dfrac{6^2+9}{6\cdot9-1} = \dfrac{45}{53}$, and $\boxed{m+n=98}$.
