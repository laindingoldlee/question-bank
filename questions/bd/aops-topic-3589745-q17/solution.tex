If the chosen integers are $a,b$, then $ab=153-a-b$, so $(a+1)(b+1)=154=2\cdot7\cdot11$. Both factors lie between $2$ and $18$. The only pair is $7,22$ (invalid), $2,77$ (invalid), or $11,14$ (valid). Thus $a,b=10,13$ and the sum is $\boxed{23}$.
