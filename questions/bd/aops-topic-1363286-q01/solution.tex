In one round, all four miss with probability
\[
\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot\dfrac{3}{4}\cdot\dfrac{4}{5}=\dfrac{1}{5},
\]
and Jill wins in that round (the first three miss and she hits) with probability
\[
\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot\dfrac{3}{4}\cdot\dfrac{1}{5}=\dfrac{1}{20}.
\]
Jill wins in round $k$ with probability $\left(\dfrac{1}{5}\right)^{k-1}\dfrac{1}{20}$, so
\[
P(\text{Jill wins})=\dfrac{1}{20}\left(1+\dfrac{1}{5}+\dfrac{1}{25}+\cdots\right)=\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{20}}{1-\dfrac{1}{5}}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{16}}.
\]
