Hillary, Donald, Gary and Jill take turns throwing rocks at a tin can on a wall, in that order: Hillary first, then Donald, then Gary, then Jill. Each time Hillary throws, she hits the can with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$; Donald hits with probability $\dfrac{1}{3}$, Gary with probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$ and Jill with probability $\dfrac{1}{5}$, independently. If all four miss, they take another round in the same order. The first one to hit the can wins. What is the probability that Jill wins?
