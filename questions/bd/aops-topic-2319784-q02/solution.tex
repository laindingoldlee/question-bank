The conditions on the first three and on coins $6$ to $8$ (``heads or tails'') are always satisfied. The coins are independent, and each of the four remaining coins must show one particular side, with probability $\dfrac12$ each. So the probability is
\[\left(\dfrac12\right)^4=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{16}}.\]
