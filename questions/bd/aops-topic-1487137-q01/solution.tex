If the word has $j$ B's, it has length $5+j$ and is determined by the positions of the B's, which can be chosen in $\dbinom{5+j}{j}$ ways. With $j=0,1,2,3$:
\[
\dbinom{5}{0}+\dbinom{6}{1}+\dbinom{7}{2}+\dbinom{8}{3}=1+6+21+56=\boxed{84}.
\]
(By the hockey-stick identity this is $\dbinom{9}{3}=84$.)
