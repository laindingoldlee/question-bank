Nine players draw from the nine available numbers 30 through 38, so every number is used. The three numbers 30, 31, 32 must go to linebackers and 36, 37, 38 to linemen. Choose two of the shared numbers 33, 34, 35 for the other linebackers in 3 ways. Then assign the five chosen numbers to linebackers in $ 5!$ ways and four remaining to linemen in $ 4!$ ways. Thus $\boxed{3\cdot5!4!=8640}$.
