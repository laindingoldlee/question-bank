Cross-multiplication and completing a product give $(m-30)(n+24)=-720$. Set $d=30-m$; the conditions are equivalent to $d$ being a positive divisor of 720 with $d<24$. Each gives $m=30-d$ and $n=\dfrac{720}{d}-24$, both greater than 6. The divisors are $1,2,3,4,5,6,8,9,10,12,15,16,18,20$, giving $\boxed{14}$ pairs.
