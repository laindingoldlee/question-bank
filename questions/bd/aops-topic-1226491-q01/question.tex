There are four bags of coins. All coins in one bag have the same weight, which is $1$, $2$ or $3$ ounces. Taking $1$ coin from bag 1, $3$ from bag 2, $9$ from bag 3 and $27$ from bag 4 gives $40$ coins weighing $95$ ounces in total. Find the weight of a coin from each bag.
