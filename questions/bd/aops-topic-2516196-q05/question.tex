Triangle $ABC$ has an incircle with centre $I$ and radius $2$, and $\angle ACB$ is acute. Let $\ell$ be the line through $I$ parallel to $AC$, and let $M$ and $N$ be the points where $\ell$ meets $AB$ and $BC$ respectively. If $MN=7$ and $AM=4$, the area of the trapezoid $AMNC$ can be written as $a+b\sqrt3+c\sqrt5$ with integers $a,b,c$. Find $a+b+c$.
