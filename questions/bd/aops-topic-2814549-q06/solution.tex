The left side counts unordered pairs of distinct $2$-element subsets of an $n$-set. The two subsets cover either $4$ people or $3$ people. With $4$ people: choose them ($\binom n4$), then split them into two pairs ($3$ ways). With $3$ people the two pairs share one person: choose the $3$ people ($\binom n3$) and the shared person ($3$ ways), which determines both pairs. $\blacksquare$
