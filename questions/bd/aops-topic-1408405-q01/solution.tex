Let $C,G,F$ be the sets of players. Inclusion-exclusion gives
\[
|C\cup G\cup F|=4+10+10-1-3-4+1=17,
\]
which agrees with the total, so the data are consistent. The players who tried out for guard and something else are those in $G\cap C$ or $G\cap F$: $1+4-1=4$ players. So
\[
\text{only guard}=10-4=\boxed{6}.
\]
