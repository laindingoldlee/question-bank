Tryouts were held for three positions on a school basketball team: centre, guard and forward. Four players tried out for centre, $10$ for guard and $10$ for forward. These numbers include $1$ player who tried out for centre and guard, $3$ who tried out for centre and forward, and $4$ who tried out for guard and forward; all six of these counts include $1$ player who tried out for all three positions. If $17$ players in all tried out, how many tried out only for guard?
