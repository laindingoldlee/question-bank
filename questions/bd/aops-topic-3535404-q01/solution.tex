Vieta gives $p_1=e_1$ and $\sum_{i<j}r_ir_j=e_2$. Squaring the sum gives $p_2=e_1^2-2e_2$. Expanding its cube gives $e_1^3=p_3+3e_1e_2-3e_3$, because multiplying the pairwise-product sum by the root sum counts each mixed square term once and the triple product three times. Thus $\boxed{p_1=e_1,\quad p_2=e_1^2-2e_2,\quad p_3=e_1^3-3e_1e_2+3e_3}$.
