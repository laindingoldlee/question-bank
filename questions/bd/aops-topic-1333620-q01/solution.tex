As in a regular hexagon of side $s$ with centre $O$, $\angle AOE=120^\circ$ and $OA=OE=s$, the short diagonal is $AE=\sqrt3\,s$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair[] V; string[] L={"$A$","$B$","$C$","$D$","$E$","$F$"};
for(int k=0;k<6;++k) V[k]=dir(120+60*k);
draw(V[0]--V[1]--V[2]--V[3]--V[4]--V[5]--cycle,linewidth(1));
for(int k=0;k<6;++k) label(L[k],V[k],dir(120+60*k));
draw(V[0]--V[4],red+dashed); draw(V[0]--(0,0)--V[4],gray); dot((0,0)); label("$O$",(0,0),S);
draw(arc((0,0),0.2,0,120),gray);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

So $s=\dfrac{x}{\sqrt3}$ and
\[
[ABCDEF]=\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}s^2=\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}\cdot\dfrac{x^2}{3}=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}x^2}.
\]
For example, $x=5$ gives $\dfrac{25\sqrt3}{2}$.
