Before a trip, a car's odometer reads $13{,}810$ miles and its tank is full. At $14{,}105$ miles the driver refills the tank with $12$ gallons, and at the end of the trip, at $14{,}465$ miles, refills it with $15$ gallons. To the nearest whole number, how many miles per gallon did the car get on the trip?
