The numerator is a difference of squares:
\[a^2+2ab+b^2-a^2b^2=(a+b)^2-(ab)^2=(a+b-ab)(a+b+ab).\]
Since $a+b+ab\ne0$, the fraction equals $a+b-ab$, and the whole expression is
\[(a+b-ab)+ab=a+b=\boxed{\sqrt5}.\]
