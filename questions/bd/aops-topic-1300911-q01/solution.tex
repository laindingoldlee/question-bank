$\sqrt{\ }$ denotes the principal (nonnegative) square root, a single number. Both $x$ and $-x$ square to $x^2$, and the nonnegative one is $|x|$. So $\sqrt{x^2}=\boxed{|x|}$; for example $\sqrt{(-3)^2}=3$. The ``$\pm$'' appears when \emph{solving} $y^2=x^2$, which has the two solutions $y=\pm x$; that is a different question.
