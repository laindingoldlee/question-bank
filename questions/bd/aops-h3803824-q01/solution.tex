The horizontal displacement is $16-4+1-\cdots=\dfrac{16}{1+\dfrac{1}{4}}=\dfrac{64}{5}$. The vertical displacement is $8-2+\dfrac{1}{2}-\cdots=\dfrac{8}{1+\dfrac{1}{4}}=\dfrac{32}{5}$. Thus the limiting position is $\boxed{(12.80,6.40)}$, and the displacement magnitude is $\sqrt{(\dfrac{64}{5})^2+(\dfrac{32}{5})^2}=\dfrac{32\sqrt5}{5}\approx\boxed{14.31}$.
