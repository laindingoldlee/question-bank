Both first radicands must be nonnegative, giving $-2\le x\le2$. On this interval their product is nonnegative and $\sqrt{2+x}\sqrt{2-x}=\sqrt{(2+x)(2-x)}=\sqrt{4-x^2}$. Thus $f(x)=0$ throughout its domain, and both the minimum and maximum are $\boxed{0}$.
