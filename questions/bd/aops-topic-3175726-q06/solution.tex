Let $d$ be this gcd. It divides $q=f(x+1)-f(x)=3x^2+3x+1$. It also divides $3f(x)-xq+q=2x+7$. Finally $4q-(2x+7)(6x-15)=109$, so $d\mid109$ and $d\le109$. At $x=51$, $f(51)=109\cdot1217$ and $f(52)=109\cdot1290$, so the gcd is at least $109$ and hence exactly $109$. The maximum is $\boxed{109}$.
