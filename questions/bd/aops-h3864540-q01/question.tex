There are $21$ distinguishable students. Exactly one stole the answer key to the final exam (the thief) and a different student witnessed the theft (the witness).

In each round, you choose a subset $  S  $ of the students to bring into a private room. If the witness is in $  S  $ and the thief is not, the witness identifies the thief. Otherwise, no information is given.

What is the smallest number of rounds sufficient to guarantee that you can identify the thief?
