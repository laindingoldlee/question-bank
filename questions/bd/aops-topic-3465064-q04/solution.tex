We have $z=1+i$, $z^2=2i$, and $z^3=-2+2i$. The last two vertices are $(0,2)$ and $(-2,2)$, so their side is horizontal with length 2. The first vertex $(1,1)$ is one unit from that line. The area is $\dfrac{1}{2}(2)(1)=\boxed1$.
\begin{asy}
import geometry; usepackage("amsmath"); size(240);
pair A=(1,1),B=(0,2),C=(-2,2);draw((-3,0)--(2,0),Arrow);draw((0,-.2)--(0,3),Arrow);draw(A--B--C--cycle,blue);label("$z$",A,SE);label("$z^2$",B,N);label("$z^3$",C,NW);
\end{asy}
