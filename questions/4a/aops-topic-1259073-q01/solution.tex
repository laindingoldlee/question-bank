Let $r=\dfrac{2}{3}$. Multiplying the first $k$ factors by $1-r$ and using the difference of squares repeatedly,
\[(1-r)(1+r)(1+r^2)\cdots\left(1+r^{2^{k-1}}\right)=1-r^{2^k}.\]
As $k\to\infty$, $r^{2^k}\to0$, so the product equals $\dfrac{1}{1-r}=\boxed{3}$.
