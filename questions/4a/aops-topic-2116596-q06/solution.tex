The numbers $1,2,\dots,2020$ contain $1010$ odd and $1010$ even numbers, so each of $S$, $E$, $O$ is odd with probability exactly $\dfrac{1}{2}$, independently.

Whatever the value of $SE$, exactly one parity of $O$ makes $SE+O$ odd, and $O$ has that parity with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Hence the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$.

In detail: $SE$ is odd only when both $S$ and $E$ are odd, with probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$, so
\[P(SE+O\text{ odd})=P(SE\text{ odd})\,P(O\text{ even})+P(SE\text{ even})\,P(O\text{ odd})=\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{3}{4}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{1}{2} .\]
(The posted answer $\dfrac{3}{8}$ comes from taking $P(SE\text{ even})=\dfrac{1}{2}$ instead of $\dfrac{3}{4}$.)
