The two polygons share the vertices $S$ and $R$.

\textbf{In the hexagon.} Going around $S,Q,U,A,R,E$, the vertices $R$ and $S$ are both adjacent to $E$, so $RS$ is a short diagonal of a regular hexagon of side $1$. Triangle $RES$ has $ER=ES=1$ and $\angle RES=120^\circ$, so
\[
RS=2\cdot\sin60^\circ=\sqrt3 .
\]

\textbf{In the pentagon.} Going around $P,R,I,S,M$, the vertices $R$ and $S$ are both adjacent to $I$, so $RS$ is a diagonal of the regular pentagon and $IS$ is a side. In a regular pentagon the ratio of a diagonal to a side is the golden ratio $\varphi=\dfrac{1+\sqrt5}{2}$ (triangle $RIS$ has angles $108^\circ,36^\circ,36^\circ$, and $\dfrac{RS}{IS}=2\cos36^\circ=\varphi$). Hence
\[
IS=\dfrac{RS}{\varphi}=\dfrac{2\sqrt3}{1+\sqrt5}=\dfrac{2\sqrt3\left(\sqrt5-1\right)}{4}=\dfrac{\sqrt{15}-\sqrt3}{2}\approx1.070 .
\]

\textbf{All configurations.} Once $R$ and $S$ are fixed, the pentagon can lie on either side of the line $RS$ (with $I$ on the same side as $E$, or on the side of the hexagon's centre), as in the two sketches; and the labels of the hexagon could run either way round. In every case $RS=\sqrt3$ is a diagonal of the pentagon and $IS$ is a side, so $IS$ takes only one value:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(9cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real d2r=pi/180;
picture hexpic(bool inside){
  picture pic;
  pair Sx=dir(210),Qx=dir(270),Ux=dir(330),Ax=dir(30),Rx=dir(90),Ex=dir(150);
  draw(pic,Sx--Qx--Ux--Ax--Rx--Ex--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
  real side=sqrt(3)/((1+sqrt(5))/2);
  pair m=(Rx+Sx)/2; pair nrm=unit(rotate(90)*(Rx-Sx));
  // nrm points away from the hexagon centre (towards E)
  if(dot(nrm,Ex-m)<0) nrm=-nrm;
  real hI=(sqrt(3)/2)/tan(54*d2r);
  pair Ix= inside ? m-hI*nrm : m+hI*nrm;
  // the other two vertices: rotate about R and S
  pair Px=Rx+rotate(inside? -108: 108)*(Ix-Rx);
  pair Mx=Sx+rotate(inside? 108: -108)*(Ix-Sx);
  draw(pic,Rx--Ix--Sx--Mx--Px--cycle,blue+linewidth(0.9));
  draw(pic,Rx--Sx,red+dashed);
  label(pic,"$S$",Sx,SW);label(pic,"$Q$",Qx,S);label(pic,"$U$",Ux,SE);label(pic,"$A$",Ax,NE);label(pic,"$R$",Rx,N);label(pic,"$E$",Ex,inside? NW: 1.3*dir(-15));
  label(pic,"$I$",Ix,inside? dir(0): dir(180),blue);label(pic,"$P$",Px,inside? dir(90):dir(0),blue);label(pic,"$M$",Mx,inside? dir(180):2.2*dir(105),blue);
  return pic;
}
add(hexpic(false));
add(shift(3.6,0)*hexpic(true));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The only possible value is $IS=\dfrac{\sqrt{15}-\sqrt3}{2}$, so the sum of all possible values is $\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt{15}-\sqrt3}{2}}$.
