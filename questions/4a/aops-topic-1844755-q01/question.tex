The absolute value $|x|$ of a number $x$ is its distance from $0$ on the number line. Decide whether each statement is true or false, and explain.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $\left|-\dfrac{1}{4}\right|>\dfrac{1}{4}$
\item[(b)] $|-10|>7$
\item[(c)] $\left|-\dfrac{1}{4}\right|\ge\dfrac{1}{4}$
\end{enumerate}
