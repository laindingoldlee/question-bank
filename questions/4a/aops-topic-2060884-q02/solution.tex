There are $11$ integers from $4$ to $14$. For each difference $d\in\{1,2,3,4\}$, the pairs $\{a,a+d\}$ have $4\le a\le14-d$, giving $11-d$ pairs. Total: $10+9+8+7=\boxed{34}$.
