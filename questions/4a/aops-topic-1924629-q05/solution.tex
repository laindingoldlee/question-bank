Let the fraction be $\dfrac {a}{b}$. Then $\dfrac{a+27}{b+36}=\dfrac {a}{b}$, so $a(b+36)=b(a+27)$, i.e.\ $36a=27b$ and $\dfrac {a}{b}=\dfrac{27}{36}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{4}}$. (Check: $\dfrac{3+27}{4+36}=\dfrac{30}{40}=\dfrac{3}{4}$.)
