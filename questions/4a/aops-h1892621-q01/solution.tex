Team A completes $\dfrac{4}{12}=\dfrac{1}{3}$ of the job in the first four days. In the next two days, the teams jointly complete $2(\dfrac{1}{12}+\dfrac{1}{36})=\dfrac{2}{9}$. Thus $\dfrac{4}{9}$ remains. At team B's rate $\dfrac{1}{36}$ job per day, the remaining time is $\dfrac{4}{9}\cdot36=\boxed{16\text{ days}}$.
