Twenty percent of M\&M candies are yellow, and a sample of $120$ candies is selected. The number $Y$ of yellow candies in the sample is modelled by a binomial distribution. Use the rule of thumb that $Y$ will usually lie within two standard deviations of its mean.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the mean and the standard deviation of $Y$.
\item[(b)] Find the approximate range of values of $Y$ (round to whole numbers), and its width.
\end{enumerate}
A student computed $\sigma\approx4.38$ and gave the width as $2\sigma\approx9$. What went wrong?
