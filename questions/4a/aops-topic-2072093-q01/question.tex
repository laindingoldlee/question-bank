Patrick enjoys number puzzles. If you give Patrick a number, he flips a coin: on heads he multiplies your number by $2$, on tails he divides it by $2$. He then flips again: on heads he adds $5$ to the new number, on tails he subtracts $5$. Then he announces the result.

Amy gives Patrick her favourite number and he replies $23$. She gives him the same number again and this time he replies $12$. What is Amy's favourite number?
