The mirror line is the perpendicular bisector of the segment joining a point to its image. That segment has slope $\dfrac{5-3}{2-6}=-\dfrac{1}{2}$, so $\ell$ has slope $\boxed{2}$ (and passes through the midpoint $(4,4)$).

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair P=(6,3),Q=(2,5),M=(P+Q)/2; draw(P--Q,dashed); draw((M-1.6*(1,2))--(M+1.6*(1,2)),blue+linewidth(1)); dot(P);dot(Q);dot(M);
label("$(6,3)$",P,E); label("$(2,5)$",Q,W); label("$(4,4)$",M,W); label("$\ell$",M+1.6*(1,2),N,blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
