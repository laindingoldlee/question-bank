An urn contains $20$ tokens: there are $10$ colours, and exactly two tokens of each colour. Tokens of the same colour are indistinguishable. How many different ordered arrangements (sequences) of $10$ tokens can be formed from these $20$ tokens? Give also a general formula for sequences of length $r$ when there are $n$ colours with two tokens each.
