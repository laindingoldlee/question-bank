Bloggy puts $12$ lamps equally spaced around the circular border of a park, so that the lamps are the vertices of a regular dodecagon. A magic orb is then placed at a point chosen uniformly at random inside the park. The dodecagon lights up only if the orb lies inside it. What is the probability that the dodecagon lights up?
