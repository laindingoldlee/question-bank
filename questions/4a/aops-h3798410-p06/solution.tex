Let the depth be h and the side parallel to the wall be w. Since $hw=2160$, AM-GM gives $2h+w\ge2\sqrt{2hw}=2\sqrt{4320}>131$ metres. Posts every twelve metres including both endpoints force the fenced length to be a multiple of twelve, so it is at least 132. This is attained with $h=36,w=60$, fitting within the 100-metre wall and placing posts also at both corners. The number of posts is $\dfrac{132}{12}+1=\boxed{12}$.
