Let $a,b,x,y$ be integers with $a\ne0$ and $ax+by=1$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $by\equiv1\pmod a$, and explain why the signs of $a$ and $b$ do not matter.
\item[(b)] Deduce that $\gcd(a,b)=1$ and that $y$ is an inverse of $b$ modulo $a$.
\item[(c)] Use $7\cdot(-2)+5\cdot3=1$ to find the inverse of $5$ modulo $7$.
\end{enumerate}
