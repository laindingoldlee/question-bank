Assume their choices are independent, each speaking with probability $p$. Then $(1-p)^2=\dfrac{25}{36}$, so $p=\dfrac{1}{6}$. Bill's expected years are $9(\dfrac{5}{6})(\dfrac{1}{6})+3(\dfrac{5}{6})^2+6(\dfrac{1}{6})^2=\dfrac{7}{2}$; speaking alone contributes zero. Multiplying by twelve gives $\boxed{42\text{ months}}$. Equal marginal probabilities alone would not justify independence.
