Reflection in the altitude pairs all qualifying points except the midpoint. An odd count forces the midpoint to qualify, so write $BC=2k$ and the altitude as an integer $h>0$. Here $k$ is an integer, the area is $kh\le500$, and obtuseness is equivalent to $k>h$.

For an interior point at integer offset $t$ from the midpoint, $AD=\sqrt{h^2+t^2}$. Besides the midpoint, exactly one positive offset $t<k$ must give a square. Let $t_1<t_2$ be the first two positive offsets with $h^2+t^2$ square, taking $t_2=\infty$ if there is only one. They are found exhaustively from
\[
(c-t)(c+t)=h^2
\]
by positive factor pairs of the same parity. The allowed range is
\[
\max(h+1,t_1+1)\le k\le\min(\lfloor\dfrac{500}{h}\rfloor,t_2).
\]
Since $h<k$ and $hk\le500$, only $1\le h\le21$ need checking. The nonempty ranges are
\[
