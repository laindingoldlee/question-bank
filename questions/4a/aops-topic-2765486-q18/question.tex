All birth years are integers from 1818 through 2017. A son’s birth year $s$, his father’s $f$, and grandfather’s $g$ form a geometric sequence, with $g<f<s$. His sister is five years younger, born in year $s+5$. Her year, their mother’s year $m$, and the same grandfather’s year form an arithmetic sequence, with $g<m<s$. Find $m$.
