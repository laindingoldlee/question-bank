Since $1,3,5,7$ are roots of $P(x)-a$, we can write $P(x)-a=(x-1)(x-3)(x-5)(x-7)Q(x)$ with $Q$ having integer coefficients (dividing by monic integer polynomials keeps integer coefficients). Substituting $x=2,4,6,8$:
\[-2a=-15\,Q(2),\qquad-2a=9\,Q(4),\qquad-2a=-15\,Q(6),\qquad-2a=105\,Q(8).\]
So $2a$ is a multiple of $15$, $9$ and $105$, hence of $\operatorname{lcm}(9,15,105)=315$. As $315$ is odd, $315\mid a$.

$a=315$ is attained: we need $Q(2)=Q(6)=42$, $Q(4)=-70$, $Q(8)=-6$. Try $Q(x)=42+(x-2)(x-6)R(x)$; the conditions become $-4R(4)=-112$ and $12R(8)=-48$, i.e. $R(4)=28$, $R(8)=-4$, met by $R(x)=60-8x$. Then
\[P(x)=315+(x-1)(x-3)(x-5)(x-7)\left(42+(x-2)(x-6)(60-8x)\right)\]
has integer coefficients and the required values. The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(B) }315}$.
