The units digits of $2^1,2^2,2^3,2^4,2^5,\dots$ are $2,4,8,6,2,\dots$, repeating with period $4$ (each digit depends only on the previous one). Since $2021=4\cdot505+1$, the units digit of $2^{2021}$ equals that of $2^1$, which is $\boxed{2}$.
