The odd integers available are $3,5,\dots,19$. The smallest sum is $3+5+7=15$ and the largest $15+17+19=51$, and every sum is odd. Every odd number from $15$ to $51$ occurs: starting from $(3,5,7)$, raise the largest number by $2$ until it is $19$, then the middle one, then the smallest, increasing the sum by $2$ each step, until $(15,17,19)$. So the answer is the number of odd integers from $15$ to $51$: $\boxed{19}$.
