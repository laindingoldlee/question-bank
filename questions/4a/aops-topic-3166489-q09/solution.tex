The product is $\dfrac{4041!}{2020!}$. Its exponent of five is $(808+161+32+6+1)-(404+80+16+3)=1008-503=505$. There are $1010$ even factors in the interval, already supplying more than $505$ twos. Therefore the number of factors of ten, and hence trailing zeros, is $\boxed{505}$.
