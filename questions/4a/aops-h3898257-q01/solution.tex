Points $A,N$ on the same arc $BC$ subtend the same opposite arc, so $\angle BNC=\angle BAC=80^\circ$. Since $N$ is on the perpendicular bisector, $NB=NC$. The base angles of triangle $BNC$ are therefore $\dfrac{180^\circ-80^\circ}{2}=\boxed{50^\circ}$.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair B=dir(190),C=dir(350),N=dir(90),A=dir(130);draw(unitcircle);draw(A--B--C--A);draw(B--N--C,blue);draw((0,-1.2)--(0,1.2),dashed);label("A",A,NW);label("B",B,SW);label("C",C,SE);label("N",N,(0,1));
\end{asy}
