Completing the squares, $(x-3)^2+(y+4)^2=-6+9+16=19$: a circle with centre $C=(3,-4)$ and radius $\sqrt{19}$. The origin $O$ is at distance $OC=5>\sqrt{19}$ from the centre, so it lies outside the circle. For any point $P$ on the circle, $OP\ge OC-CP=5-\sqrt{19}$ by the triangle inequality, with equality where segment $OC$ crosses the circle. The minimum distance is $\boxed{5-\sqrt{19}}\approx0.64$.

pair C=(3,-4);real r=sqrt(19);
draw(circle(C,r),linewidth(0.8));
draw((0,0)--C,dashed);
pair Pn=C+r*unit((0,0)-C);
dot((0,0));dot(C);dot(Pn);
label("$O$",(0,0),NW);label("$(3,-4)$",C,SE);label("$P$",Pn,1.6*dir(-8));
label("$x$",(8.5,0),S);label("$y$",(0,1.5),W);
\end{asy}
