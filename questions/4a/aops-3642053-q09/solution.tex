Every palindrome of length at least four contains a central palindrome of length two or three. Only O and L repeat, so a qualifying pair must occupy positions at distance one or two. There are $\dfrac{8!}{(2!2!)}=10080$ strings total. For the complement, choose two O positions at distance at least three and two remaining L positions at distance at least three. Enumerating the position pairs gives 122 choices; the four other letters have $4!=24$ orders. Thus the answer is $10080-122\cdot24=\boxed{7152}$. The computation audit independently checks all distinct strings.
