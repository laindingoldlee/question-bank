Let the smaller number be $s$. Division with quotient $3$ and remainder $12$ means the larger number is $3s+12$, with $12<s$. Then
\[
s+(3s+12)=144\ \Longrightarrow\ 4s=132\ \Longrightarrow\ s=\boxed{33}.
\]
Check: the larger number is $111=3\cdot33+12$, and $12<33$, so the remainder is valid.
