The cube root of the negative number $1-\sqrt5$ is negative, and the other two factors are positive, so the product is negative. Its absolute value is the sixth root of
\[(3+\sqrt5)^3(1-\sqrt5)^2(7-3\sqrt5).\]
Since $(1-\sqrt5)^2=6-2\sqrt5=2(3-\sqrt5)$ and $(3+\sqrt5)(3-\sqrt5)=4$, this is $8(3+\sqrt5)^2(7-3\sqrt5)=8(14+6\sqrt5)(7-3\sqrt5)=8(98-90)=64$. So the product is $-\sqrt[6]{64}=\boxed{-2}$.
