The top and bottom each contribute 9. The outside boundary has 12 unit segments, each with mean tower height 5, contributing 60. There are 12 interior neighboring pairs; each contributes the absolute height difference. For two distinct uniform heights 1 through 9, the mean difference is $\dfrac{\sum_{d=1}^8 2d(9-d)}{9\cdot8}=\dfrac{10}{3}$. Thus internal exposed walls contribute 40. Total $\boxed{118}$.
