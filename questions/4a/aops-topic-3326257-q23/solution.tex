Let $p_i$ denote the winning probability from room $i$. The boundary values are $p_1=0,p_{300}=1$, and $p_i=\dfrac{1}{2}(p_{i-1}+p_{i+1})$ for interior rooms. Thus consecutive differences are constant, so $p_i=\dfrac{i-1}{299}$. Hence $\boxed{p_8=\dfrac{7}{299}}$. Absorption occurs with probability one, since in any 299-step block there is a uniformly positive probability of consecutive steps reaching a boundary.
