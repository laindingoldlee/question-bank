Let the article have $2w$ words. Felipe types his $w$ words in $\dfrac{w}{60}$ minutes and Chung types hers in $\dfrac{w}{40}$ minutes, so the whole article takes
\[
\dfrac{w}{60}+\dfrac{w}{40}=\dfrac{2w+3w}{120}=\dfrac{w}{24}\text{ minutes}.
\]
The average rate is
\[
\dfrac{2w}{\dfrac{w}{24}}=\boxed{48\text{ words per minute}},
\]
the harmonic mean of $60$ and $40$, not their arithmetic mean $50$. (If instead they typed their halves at the same time, the article would be finished when Chung finishes, also at an average of $48$ words per minute.)
