Factor: $4^x-1=\left(2^x-1\right)\left(2^x+1\right)=3^y$. So $2^x-1$ and $2^x+1$ are both powers of $3$. They differ by $2$, and two powers of $3$ that differ by $2$ must be $1$ and $3$ (if the smaller were at least $3$, both would be multiples of $3$ and their difference would be a multiple of $3$). Hence $2^x-1=1$, $x=1$, and $3^y=3$, $y=1$. The only solution is
\[\boxed{(x,y)=(1,1)}.\]
