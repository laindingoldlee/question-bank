There are $8\cdot8=64$ equally likely ordered outcomes, giving the numbers $10a+b$ with $a,b\in\{1,\dots,8\}$. A prime other than $2$ and $5$ ends in $1$, $3$, $7$ or $9$, so the units digit must be $1$, $3$ or $7$ (the die has no $9$). The primes are:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{tens digit }1: & 11,\ 13,\ 17\\
\text{tens digit }2: & 23\\
\text{tens digit }3: & 31,\ 37\\
\text{tens digit }4: & 41,\ 43,\ 47\\
\text{tens digit }5: & 53\\
\text{tens digit }6: & 61,\ 67\\
\text{tens digit }7: & 71,\ 73\\
\text{tens digit }8: & 83
\end{array}
\]
(The rejected candidates are $21=3\cdot7$, $27$, $33$, $51=3\cdot17$, $57=3\cdot19$, $63$, $77$, $81$ and $87=3\cdot29$.) That is $15$ primes, so the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{15}{64}}$.
