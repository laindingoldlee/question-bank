For $p=2$, the result is $0$, not prime. For $p>2$, factor $q=(p-2)(p^2+2p+4)$. Both factors are positive and the second exceeds $1$, so primality requires $p-2=1$. Thus $p=3,q=19$, and $19$ is prime. The only pair is $\boxed{(3,19)}$.
