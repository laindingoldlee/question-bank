Whatever the first card is, the second must be one of the $39$ cards of other suits among the remaining $51$, the third one of $26$ among $50$, and the fourth one of $13$ among $49$:
\[\dfrac{39}{51}\cdot\dfrac{26}{50}\cdot\dfrac{13}{49}=\dfrac{13182}{124950}=\boxed{\dfrac{2197}{20825}}\approx0.105.\]
