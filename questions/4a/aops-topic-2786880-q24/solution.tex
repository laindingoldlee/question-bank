For a positive integer with binary length $ L $ and $ w $ ones, the process takes $(L-1)+(w-1)=L+w-2 $ turns: each nonleading one is removed by subtraction, and every nonleading digit is removed by halving. For $ n<500 $, $ L\le9 $. Values with $ L\le8 $ need at most 14 turns. For $ L=9 $, all nine ones would give 511, which is excluded, so at most 15 turns are possible. Exactly eight ones require changing one bit of 511 to 0. Keeping length 9 and obtaining a value below 500 requires removing bit 128, 64, 32, or 16, giving 383,447,479,495. Thus the maximum is $\boxed{15}$, attained by $\boxed4 $ values.
