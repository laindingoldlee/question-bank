\textbf{(a)} The flips are independent, so a particular sequence with $k$ heads and $4-k$ tails has probability $\left(\dfrac38\right)^k\left(\dfrac58\right)^{4-k}=\dfrac{3^k5^{4-k}}{8^4}$, and there are $\dbinom4k$ such sequences. On the other hand, expanding $(3h+5t)^4$ means choosing $3h$ or $5t$ from each of the four factors, exactly like choosing a result for each flip; the choices with $k$ factors $3h$ give
\[(3h+5t)^4=\sum_{k=0}^{4}\dbinom4k3^k5^{4-k}h^kt^{4-k}.\]
So the coefficient of $h^kt^{4-k}$ is $\dbinom4k3^k5^{4-k}$, and dividing by $8^4=(3+5)^4$ (the sum of all coefficients) gives exactly the probability above.

\textbf{(b)} With $k=2$: $\dfrac{\dbinom42\cdot3^2\cdot5^2}{8^4}=\dfrac{6\cdot225}{4096}=\boxed{\dfrac{675}{2048}}$.
