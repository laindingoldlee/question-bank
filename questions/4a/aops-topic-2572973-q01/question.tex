A biased coin lands heads with probability $\dfrac38$ and tails with probability $\dfrac58$. It is flipped $4$ times.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Explain why the probability of exactly $k$ heads equals the coefficient of $h^kt^{4-k}$ in the expansion of $(3h+5t)^4$, divided by $8^4$.
\item[(b)] Find the probability of exactly $2$ heads.
\end{enumerate}
