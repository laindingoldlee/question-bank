There are $36$ equally likely outcomes, of which $30$ show different numbers. Among these, the sum is $6$ for $(1,5),(5,1),(2,4),(4,2)$ (the outcome $(3,3)$ is excluded). So the probability is $\dfrac{4}{30}=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{15}}$.
