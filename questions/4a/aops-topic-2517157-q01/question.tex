A fair six-sided die is rolled and $X$ is the number shown.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the expected value $E(X)$.
\item[(b)] The die is rolled $600$ times and each face happens to come up exactly $100$ times. What is the average of the $600$ results?
\item[(c)] Show that $\displaystyle\sum_{x=1}^{6}\left(x-E(X)\right)=0$, and explain why it does not matter that $X$ can never equal $E(X)$.
\end{enumerate}
