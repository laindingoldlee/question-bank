Work in the frame of the water, in which the raft is at rest. In this frame the boat moves away from the raft for $60$ minutes at constant speed, turns, and returns at the same speed, so the return also takes $60$ minutes. Thus the boat meets the raft again $2$ hours after passing it at $A$.

During those $2$ hours the raft, moving with the current, travelled $6.0$ km relative to the bank. So the current's speed is
\[
\dfrac{6.0\text{ km}}{2\text{ h}}=\boxed{3\text{ km per hour}}.
\]
