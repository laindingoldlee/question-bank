Eight suspects numbered $1$ through $8$ sit around a circular table. Each suspect simultaneously points either to the person immediately clockwise or to the person immediately counterclockwise.

A pointing arrangement is called stable if exactly four suspects are pointed to by exactly one person. A stable arrangement is selected uniformly at random.

What is the probability that suspects $1$ and $5$ point in the same direction? Express the probability as $\dfrac{m}{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $m+n.$
