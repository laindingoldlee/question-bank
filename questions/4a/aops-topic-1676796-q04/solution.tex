Count the three-digit numbers without the digit $1$, then remove those that also have no $5$.
\begin{itemize}
\item No digit $1$: the hundreds digit has $8$ choices (not $0$ or $1$), and the tens and units digits $9$ each: $8\cdot9\cdot9=648$.
\item No $1$ and no $5$: $7\cdot8\cdot8=448$.
\end{itemize}
So $648-448=\boxed{200}$ numbers have no $1$ and at least one $5$.
