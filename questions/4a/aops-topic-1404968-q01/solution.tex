\textbf{(a)} We arrange the multiset $\{F,F,S,S,S,S,S,R\}$ of $8$ letters. Choose the $2$ positions of the first-class cars, $\binom82=28$ ways, then the position of the restaurant car among the remaining $6$, $6$ ways; the second-class cars fill the rest. So there are $28\cdot6=\boxed{168}$ orders, which is $\dfrac{8!}{2!\,5!\,1!}$.

\textbf{(b)} With all cars different, every permutation of the $8$ cars counts: $8!=\boxed{40320}$ orders.
