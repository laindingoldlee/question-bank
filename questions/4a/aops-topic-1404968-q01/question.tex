A European high-speed passenger train is made up of two first-class cars, five second-class cars and one restaurant car, coupled in a row behind the locomotive.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] In how many orders can the cars be arranged if cars of the same class are regarded as identical?
\item[(b)] In how many orders can they be arranged if all eight cars are regarded as different?
\end{enumerate}
