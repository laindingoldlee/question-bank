If the rate is $r$, then $1000(1+r)^5=1500$, so
\[1+r=1.5^{\dfrac15}\approx1.08447,\qquad r\approx\boxed{8.4\%}.\]
Check: $1.084^5\approx1.497$ and $1.085^5\approx1.504$. (With simple interest instead, the rate would be $\dfrac{500}{1000\cdot5}=10\%$.)
