In an imaginary contest system, exactly the top $5\%$ of AMC 8 participants take the AMC 10, the top $2.5\%$ of AMC 10 participants take the AMC 12, the top $5\%$ of AMC 12 participants take the AIME, the top $10\%$ of AIME participants qualify for the USAJMO, and the top $3\%$ of USAJMO participants qualify for the USAMO.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What percent of the AMC 8 participants reach the USAMO?
\item[(b)] If $39000$ students take the AMC 8, how many of them would reach the USAMO? Interpret your answer.
\end{enumerate}
