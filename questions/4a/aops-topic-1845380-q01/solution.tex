Multiply the equation by $4$: $4x+\dfrac{1}{4x}=4$. Put $u=4x$, so $u+\dfrac{1}{u}=4$ and $u^3=64x^3$. Then
\[
u^3+\dfrac{1}{u^3}=\left(u+\dfrac{1}{u}\right)^3-3\left(u+\dfrac{1}{u}\right)=4^3-3\cdot4=52 .
\]
So $64x^3+\dfrac{1}{64x^3}=\boxed{52}$.
