Degenerate triangles $ABC$ and $DEF$ have $AB=5$, $AC=7$, $BC=12$ and $DE=11$, $DF=4$, $EF=15$. A student argues: ``$\angle A=\angle D=180^\circ$, so the other angles are all $0^\circ$; by AA, $\triangle ABC\sim\triangle DEF$ (or $\triangle ACB\sim\triangle DFE$).'' But the side ratios do not match. Find the flaw in the argument.
