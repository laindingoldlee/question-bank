Write $m=2n\ge2$; the integers from $0$ to $2^m-1$ are exactly the $m$-bit strings.

Fix one bit position. The strings with an even number of $1$s and a $1$ in that position correspond to free choices of the other $m-1$ bits with an odd number of $1$s among them, and there are $2^{m-2}$ of these. So each place value $2^j$ ($0\le j\le m-1$) is counted $2^{m-2}$ times, and
\[
N=2^{m-2}\left(2^0+2^1+\cdots+2^{m-1}\right)=2^{m-2}\left(2^m-1\right).
\]
In binary, $2^m-1$ is $m$ ones, and multiplying by $2^{m-2}$ only appends zeros. So the digit sum of $N$ is $m=\boxed{2n}$. (For $n=1$: the numbers are $0$ and $3$, $N=3=11_2$, digit sum $2$.)
