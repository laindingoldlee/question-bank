By Vieta's formulas, $a+b+c=3$ and $ab+bc+ca=3$. Hence
\[a^2+b^2+c^2=(a+b+c)^2-2(ab+bc+ca)=9-6=3.\]
Each root satisfies $x^3=3x^2-3x-1$. Adding these three equations,
\[a^3+b^3+c^3=3(a^2+b^2+c^2)-3(a+b+c)-3=9-9-3=\boxed{-3}.\]
(Check: the polynomial is $(x-1)^3+2$, so the roots are $1+u$ with $u^3=-2$; the three values of $u$ have sum $0$ and sum of squares $0$, so $\sum(1+u)^3=3+0+0+(-6)=-3$.)
