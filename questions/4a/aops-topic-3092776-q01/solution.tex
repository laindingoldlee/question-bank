Use sides $6k,8k,10k$, a scaled 3--4--5 triangle. Area is $24k^2$ and perimeter $24k$, so the ratio is $\boxed k$. Varying $k$ gives infinitely many examples.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw((0,0)--(8,0)--(0,6)--cycle);label("$8k$",(4,0),S);label("$6k$",(0,3),W);label("$10k$",(4,3),NE);
\end{asy}
