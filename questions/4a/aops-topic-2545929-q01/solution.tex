In base ten the equation reads
\[\left(5a^2+5a+3\right)+\left(4a^2+4a+2\right)=a^3+3a^2+2a+5,\]
i.e. $a^3-6a^2-7a=0$, or $a(a-7)(a+1)=0$. Since the digit $5$ requires $a\ge6$, $a=\boxed{7}$. (Check: $553_7=283$, $442_7=226$, $1325_7=509=283+226$.)
