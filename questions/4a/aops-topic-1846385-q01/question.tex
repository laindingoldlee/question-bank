A crane carries cargo from a hatch and swings it to the side before lowering it onto the dock. The crane's jib is $10$ m long and pivots about its lower end. The operator raises the jib from an angle of $30^\circ$ above the horizontal to $60^\circ$ above the horizontal. Find the vertical distance by which the end of the jib rises, in surd form.
