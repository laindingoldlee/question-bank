The only other possibilities are that it rains on all three days, with probability $\left(\dfrac13\right)^3=\dfrac1{27}$, or on none, with probability $\left(\dfrac23\right)^3=\dfrac8{27}$. So the probability is
\[1-\dfrac1{27}-\dfrac8{27}=\dfrac{18}{27}=\boxed{\dfrac23}.\]
(Directly: exactly one rainy day has probability $3\cdot\dfrac13\cdot\dfrac49=\dfrac{12}{27}$ and exactly two have $3\cdot\dfrac19\cdot\dfrac23=\dfrac{6}{27}$.)
