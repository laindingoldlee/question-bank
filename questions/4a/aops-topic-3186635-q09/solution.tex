The sphere volume is $\dfrac{4}{3}\pi12^3=2304\pi$ and the original cone volume is $\dfrac{1}{3}\pi2^2\cdot18=24\pi$. A new radius-$2$ cone of height $h$ has volume $\dfrac{4}{3}\pi h$. Setting this equal to $2328\pi$ gives $\boxed{h=1746}$.
