Since 1331=$11^3$, expand $(1+11)^{2022}$. All terms of degree at least 3 vanish modulo 1331, leaving $1+2022\cdot11+\binom{2022}2\cdot121$. Now $2022\equiv86\pmod{121}$ and $\binom{2022}2\equiv\dfrac{9\cdot8}{2}\equiv3\pmod{11}$. The remainder is $1+946+363=\boxed{1310}$.
