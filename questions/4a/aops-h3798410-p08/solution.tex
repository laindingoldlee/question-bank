The required volume is $4\cdot300\cdot0.1=120$ cubic metres. In seven full hours the robot lays $20+19+\cdots+14=\dfrac{7(20+14)}{2}=119$, not enough. The eighth hour brings the total to $119+13=132$, sufficient. Thus the least whole number of hours is $\boxed8$.
