Let the first two numbers be $x$ and $y$. Then
\[
a_3=6,\quad a_4=x+y+6,\quad a_5=x+2y+12,\quad a_6=2x+3y+24 .
\]
Continuing in the same way (each term is the sum of the three before it),
\[
a_7=4x+6y+42,\quad a_8=7x+11y+78,\quad a_9=13x+20y+144,\quad a_{10}=24x+37y+264,\quad a_{11}=44x+68y+486 .
\]
The conditions $a_6=11$ and $a_{11}=14$ give
\[
2x+3y=-13\qquad\text{and}\qquad 44x+68y=-472,\ \text{i.e. } 11x+17y=-118 .
\]
Multiplying the first equation by $11$ and the second by $2$: $22x+33y=-143$ and $22x+34y=-236$, so $y=-93$, and then $2x=-13+279=266$, $x=133$.

Hence the sequence is
\[
\boxed{133,\ -93,\ 6,\ 46,\ -41,\ 11,\ 16,\ -14,\ 13,\ 15,\ 14,\ 42}.
\]
Check: $6+46-41=11$, $13+15-14=14$, and every term from the fourth on is the sum of the three before it.
