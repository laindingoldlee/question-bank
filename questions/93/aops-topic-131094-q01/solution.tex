Each term splits into a difference:
\[\dfrac{1}{(2k-1)(2k+1)}=\dfrac12\left(\dfrac1{2k-1}-\dfrac1{2k+1}\right),\]
since $\dfrac1{2k-1}-\dfrac1{2k+1}=\dfrac{(2k+1)-(2k-1)}{(2k-1)(2k+1)}=\dfrac{2}{(2k-1)(2k+1)}$. The terms run from $k=1$ to $k=100$ (as $2\cdot100+1=201$). Adding, the sum telescopes:
\[\dfrac12\left[\left(1-\dfrac13\right)+\left(\dfrac13-\dfrac15\right)+\cdots+\left(\dfrac1{199}-\dfrac1{201}\right)\right]=\dfrac12\left(1-\dfrac1{201}\right)=\boxed{\dfrac{100}{201}}.\]
