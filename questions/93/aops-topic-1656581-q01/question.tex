How many of the following statements are always true?
\begin{enumerate}
\item[i.] Any two points in three-dimensional space (not necessarily distinct) determine a unique line.
\item[ii.] The centroid of a triangle lies inside the triangle.
\item[iii.] The intersection of a right circular cone with a horizontal plane (perpendicular to its axis) is a triangle.
\item[iv.] Any three distinct points in three-dimensional space determine a unique plane.
\end{enumerate}
