When $(a+b+c+d+5)^{2020}$ is expanded and like terms are combined, the resulting expression contains exactly $N$ terms that include all four variables $a$, $b$, $c$ and $d$, with the power of $a$ at least $5$, the power of $b$ at least $4$, the power of $c$ at least $3$ and the power of $d$ at least $2$. Find $N$ (in terms of a binomial coefficient, and as a number).
