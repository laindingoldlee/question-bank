Zeke and Mary approach each other at $50+40=90$ mph, starting $12$ miles apart, so they meet after $\dfrac{12}{90}=\dfrac{2}{15}$ hour. In that time Harry and Mary close their gap at $20+40=60$ mph, by $60\cdot\dfrac{2}{15}=8$ miles. They are then $12-8=\boxed{4}$ miles apart.
