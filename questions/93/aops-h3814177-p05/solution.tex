Adding the three side sums counts each midpoint once and each vertex twice. Thus $3S=75+V$, where $V$ is the sum at the vertices. Since $V\le13+14+15=42$, we get $S\le39$. Put $13,14,15$ at the vertices and $12,10,11$ on the sides joining $(13,14),(14,15),(15,13)$ respectively. Every side sums to $39$, attaining the bound. Hence $\boxed{S=39}$.
