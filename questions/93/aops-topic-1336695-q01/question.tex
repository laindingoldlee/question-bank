Let $ax^2+bx+c$ with $a\ne0$ have roots $r$ and $s$ (counted with multiplicity, possibly complex).
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove Vieta's formulas $r+s=-\dfrac {b}{a}$ and $rs=\dfrac {c}{a}$.
\item[(b)] Without solving, find the sum and the product of the roots of $2x^2-7x+3$, and the value of $r^2+s^2$ for these roots.
\end{enumerate}
