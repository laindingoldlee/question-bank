\textbf{(a)} Since $r$ and $s$ are the roots, the quadratic factors as $ax^2+bx+c=a(x-r)(x-s)$. Expanding the right side gives $ax^2-a(r+s)x+ars$. Comparing coefficients, $b=-a(r+s)$ and $c=ars$, so
\[
r+s=-\dfrac {b}{a},\qquad rs=\dfrac {c}{a} .
\]

\textbf{(b)} For $2x^2-7x+3$: $r+s=\dfrac{7}{2}$ and $rs=\dfrac{3}{2}$. Then
\[
r^2+s^2=(r+s)^2-2rs=\dfrac{49}{4}-3=\boxed{\dfrac{37}{4}},
\]
with sum $\boxed{\dfrac{7}{2}}$ and product $\boxed{\dfrac{3}{2}}$. (Check: the roots are $3$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}$, and $9+\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{37}{4}$.)
