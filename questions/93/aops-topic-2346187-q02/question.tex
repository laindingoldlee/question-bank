Every day, Mary's mom drives from home along a straight avenue to Mary's school, arriving exactly at dismissal time, picks Mary up and immediately drives her home along the same avenue, arriving home at 4:20. One day Mary leaves school $69$ minutes before dismissal and starts walking home along the avenue. Her mom leaves home at the usual time and drives at her usual constant speed. When she meets Mary, she picks her up on the spot, turns around at once and drives home, and they arrive home at 4:00. For how many minutes had Mary been walking when she was picked up?
