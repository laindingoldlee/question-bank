Shift the display by one: $m=n+1$. The buttons now multiply $m$ by 2 or 5, starting from $m=2$. Thus all reachable values are $n=2^u5^v-1$, $u\ge1,v\ge0$. Below 100 they are $1,3,7,15,31,63$ for $v=0$; $9,19,39,79$ for $v=1$; and $49,99$ for $v=2$. Their sum is $\boxed{414}$. If at least one button press is required, exclude the initial display 1 and obtain 413.
