Harry has a strange calculator from Husdon River Trading that has two buttons. Button A multiplies a number by $2$ and adds $1$ while button B multiplies a number by $5$ and adds $4$. For example, if the number $5$ is on the screen, pressing A would turn the number into $11$ and pressing B would turn the number into $29$. If the initial number is $1$, find the sum of all possible numbers less than $100$ that the calculator can produce.
