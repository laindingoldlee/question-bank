The third side equals one of the given sides.
\begin{itemize}
\item It cannot be $10$: sides $10,10,20$ fail the triangle inequality, since $10+10=20$ is not greater than $20$.
\item So the sides are $20,20,10$, which is a valid triangle ($20+10>20$):
\end{itemize}

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real hh=sqrt(400-25); pair B=(-5,0),C=(5,0),A=(0,hh);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(1));
dot(A);dot(B);dot(C);
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);
label("$20$",(A+B)/2,W,fontsize(9pt));label("$20$",(A+C)/2,E,fontsize(9pt));label("$10$",(B+C)/2,S,fontsize(9pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Its perimeter is $50$ and its shortest side is $10$. The similar triangle has shortest side $50$, so the scale factor is $5$. Its perimeter is
\[
5\cdot50=\boxed{250\text{ inches}}.
\]
