Count the complement. Write each of $0,1,\dots,999$ with three digits (leading zeros allowed). The strings with no digit $1$ have $9$ choices per digit: $9^3=729$, one of which is $000$. So $728$ positive integers below $1000$ contain no $1$, and $999-728=\boxed{271}$ contain at least one.
