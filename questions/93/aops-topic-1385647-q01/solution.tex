Simon fails exactly when he does not have both a green apple and a green pear, and does not have both a yellow apple and a yellow pear. So a failing collection misses one green kind and one yellow kind, and it lies inside one of four groups:
\begin{itemize}
\item no green apples, no yellow pears: at most the $7$ green pears and $5$ yellow apples, $12$ fruits;
\item no green pears, no yellow apples: at most $3+2=5$ fruits;
\item no apples at all: at most $7+2=9$ fruits;
\item no pears at all: at most $3+5=8$ fruits.
\end{itemize} So $12$ fruits may still fail, but any $13$ fruits must include a matching apple and pear. The answer is $\boxed{13}$.

(The poster's count of $12$ is the size of the worst failing collection; one more fruit is needed to be certain.)
