For each bit, the numerical contribution satisfies $ u\mathbin{\mathrm{OR}}v=u+v-(u\mathbin{\mathrm{AND}}v)$: bits present twice in the sum are reduced to one copy. Summing the two identities yields $\boxed{2x+y+z-(x\mathbin{\mathrm{AND}}y)-(x\mathbin{\mathrm{AND}}z)}$. Because the per-bit identity is summed as integer place values, ordinary carrying does not invalidate it.
