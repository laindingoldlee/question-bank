Each new group of three hexagons shares two edges with the previous end hexagon and three further edges within the group: one between the upper and lower pair and two connecting them to the new end. Its eighteen side lengths therefore increase the outer perimeter by $18-2\cdot5=8$. Starting from perimeter six and adding 674 groups yields $6+674\cdot8=\boxed{5398}$. No additional shared edges occur between nonconsecutive groups, as their centres are farther than the neighbour spacing.
