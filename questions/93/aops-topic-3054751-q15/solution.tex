The midpoint of the hypotenuse is the circumcentre, so $MB=MC$. Also $\angle MCB=\angle ACB=180^\circ-90^\circ-49^\circ=41^\circ$. The equal base angles of $\triangle MBC$ give $\boxed{\angle MBC=41^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair B=(0,0),A=(3,0),C=(0,3tan(49*pi/180)),M=(A+C)/2;draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(B--M,blue);label("$A$",A,SE);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,N);label("$M$",M,NE);
\end{asy}
