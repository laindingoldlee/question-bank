The conditions require $n\mid12648$, $n\mid5797$, and $n>3$. The Euclidean algorithm gives $\gcd(12648,5797)=527=17\cdot31$. Its positive divisors are $1,17,31,527$, and all except $1$ exceed $3$. Thus $\boxed{n\in\{17,31,527\}}$.
