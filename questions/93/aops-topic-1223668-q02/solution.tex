$5+2\sqrt6=\left(\sqrt3+\sqrt2\right)^2$, so the first factor is $\sqrt[4]{6x}\,\sqrt{\sqrt3+\sqrt2}$. Also $3\sqrt{2x}-2\sqrt{3x}=\sqrt{6x}\cdot\dfrac{3\sqrt2-2\sqrt3}{\sqrt6}=\sqrt{6x}\left(\sqrt3-\sqrt2\right)$, so the second factor is $\sqrt[4]{6x}\,\sqrt{\sqrt3-\sqrt2}$. The product is
\[\sqrt{6x}\cdot\sqrt{\left(\sqrt3+\sqrt2\right)\left(\sqrt3-\sqrt2\right)}=\boxed{\sqrt{6x}}.\]
