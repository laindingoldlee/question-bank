The first expression is $\dfrac{a}{2}+b $, giving $ 3+4=\boxed7 $. The second is $\dfrac{a+b}{2}$, giving $\boxed5 $. Parentheses distinguish the scope of taking one half; the bare phrase half of a plus b is ambiguous.
