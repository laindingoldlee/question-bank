Nonzero real numbers $a$, $b$, $c$ satisfy
\[
\dfrac{a+b-c}{c}=\dfrac{a+c-b}{b}=\dfrac{b+c-a}{a}.
\]
Find all possible values of $\dfrac{(a+b)(b+c)(c+a)}{abc}$.
