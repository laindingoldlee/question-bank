Since $29$ is prime and $29\nmid 8$, Fermat's little theorem gives $8^{28}\equiv1\pmod{29}$. As $900=28\cdot32+4$,
\[
8^{900}=\left(8^{28}\right)^{32}\cdot8^4\equiv8^4=4096\pmod{29}.
\]
Finally $4096=29\cdot141+7$, so the remainder is $\boxed{7}$.
