Let $a,b,c,d,e$ be real numbers, not all zero, and let $m,n,k,l,f$ be positive even integers. Prove that the sum of all real solutions of
\[
ax^m+bx^n+cx^k+dx^l+ex^f=0
\]
is $0$.
