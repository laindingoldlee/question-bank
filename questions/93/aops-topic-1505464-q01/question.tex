It is a deep theorem (the Gelfond--Schneider theorem) that $\sqrt2^{\sqrt2}$ is irrational. Without using this fact:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that there exist irrational numbers $a$ and $b$ such that $a^b$ is rational, by considering $\sqrt2^{\sqrt2}$.
\item[(b)] Give an explicit example: show that $\log_2 9$ is irrational and that $\sqrt2^{\log_2 9}$ is rational.
\end{enumerate}
