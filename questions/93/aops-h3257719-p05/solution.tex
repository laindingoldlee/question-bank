Let the vertex vectors from the center be $v_0,\ldots,v_{11}$. Symmetry gives $\sum v_j=0$, and each squared length is $25$. Including the zero distance to the fixed vertex,
\[
\sum_{j=0}^{11}|v_j-v_0|^2
=\sum_{j=0}^{11}(|v_j|^2+|v_0|^2-2v_j\cdot v_0)
=12(25+25)=\boxed{600}.
\]
