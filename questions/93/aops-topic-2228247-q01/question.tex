In convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, $\angle ABC=140^\circ$ and $\angle BCD=110^\circ$. A point $O$ inside the quadrilateral satisfies $\angle DAO=\dfrac{1}{2}\angle DAB$ and $\angle ADO=\dfrac{1}{2}\angle ADC$. Find $\angle AOD$.

(The original problem also says that $ABCD$ is inscribed in a circle with centre $O$. Show that this is impossible.)
