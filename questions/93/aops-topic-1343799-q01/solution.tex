Take a cross-section. The centres $O_1$ (radius $9$) and $O_2$ (radius $3$) are $9+3=12$ apart. The band consists of two common external tangent segments and two arcs.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(8cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O1=(0,0), O2=(12,0);
pair T1=9*dir(60), T2=O2+3*dir(60), U1=9*dir(-60), U2=O2+3*dir(-60);
draw(circle(O1,9),gray); draw(circle(O2,3),gray);
draw(arc(O1,9,60,300),red+linewidth(1.2)); draw(arc(O2,3,-60,60),red+linewidth(1.2));
draw(T1--T2,red+linewidth(1.2)); draw(U1--U2,red+linewidth(1.2));
draw(O1--O2,dashed); draw(O1--T1,blue+dashed); draw(O2--T2,blue+dashed);
draw(O2--6*dir(60),dotted);
draw(arc(O1,1.8,0,60),blue); label("$60^\circ$",O1+2.7*dir(30),blue+fontsize(9pt));
dot(O1);dot(O2);dot(T1);dot(T2);
label("$O_1$",O1,SW); label("$O_2$",O2,SE); label("$T_1$",T1,N); label("$T_2$",T2,NE);
label("$9$",(O1+T1)/2+0.9*dir(150),blue); label("$3$",(O2+T2)/2,E,blue);
label("$6\sqrt{3}$",(T1+T2)/2,NE,red); label("$12$",(6,0),S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Let $T_1T_2$ be one tangent segment. The radii $O_1T_1$ and $O_2T_2$ are both perpendicular to it, so $O_1T_1T_2O_2$ is a right trapezoid. Dropping a perpendicular from $O_2$ to $O_1T_1$ gives a right triangle with hypotenuse $12$ and one leg $9-3=6$; its other leg is
\[
T_1T_2=\sqrt{12^2-6^2}=\sqrt{108}=6\sqrt3 .
\]
In that right triangle the leg $6$ is half the hypotenuse, so $\angle T_1O_1O_2=60^\circ$. By symmetry the band leaves the large pipe at $60^\circ$ above and below the line of centres, so it wraps $360^\circ-120^\circ=240^\circ$ of the large pipe. The radii $O_2T_2$ are parallel to $O_1T_1$, so the band wraps $120^\circ$ of the small pipe.

The length is therefore
\[
2\cdot6\sqrt3+\dfrac{240}{360}\cdot2\pi\cdot9+\dfrac{120}{360}\cdot2\pi\cdot3=12\sqrt3+12\pi+2\pi=14\pi+12\sqrt3 .
\]
So $a=14$, $b=12$, $c=3$ and $a+b+c=\boxed{29}$.
