If $x=1$, the equation gives $y=16$; similarly $y=1$ gives $x=16$. If both are at least $3$, the sum is at least $3^3+3^3=54$, too large. Thus in the remaining cases one is $2$. The function $2^y+y^2$ strictly increases for positive integer $y$, and equals $17$ at $y=3$. Therefore all pairs are $\boxed{(1,16),(16,1),(2,3),(3,2)}$, and the least sum is $\boxed5$.
