In an idealized replacement-cost model, an untreated railroad tie costs $\$11$, lasts 10 years and has scrap value $\$1$ at replacement. Treatment raises the purchase cost to $\$14$, extends its life to 15 years and leaves no scrap value. The annual discount rate is $5\%$, replacement continues forever, and all prices stay fixed. Which option has lower present cost per tie position?
