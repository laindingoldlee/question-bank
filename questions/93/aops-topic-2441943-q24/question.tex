The floor of a room is the shape obtained from a rectangle measuring $24$ ft $6$ in by $20$ ft $5$ in by removing a smaller rectangle at one corner, and adjacent walls meet at right angles. Each wall is $8$ feet tall. A door and a window have a combined area of $50\text{ ft}^2$. If a can of paint covers $400\text{ ft}^2$, how many whole cans of paint must be bought to paint the interior walls of the room, not counting the door and the window?
