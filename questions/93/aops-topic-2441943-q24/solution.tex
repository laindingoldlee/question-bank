Removing a rectangle of size $a\times b$ at a corner replaces two boundary segments of lengths $a$ and $b$ by two others of the same lengths, so the perimeter equals that of the full rectangle:
\[2\left(24\dfrac12+20\dfrac{5}{12}\right)=2\cdot44\dfrac{11}{12}=89\dfrac56\text{ ft}.\]
The walls have total area $89\dfrac56\cdot8=718\dfrac23\text{ ft}^2$, and without the door and window $668\dfrac23\text{ ft}^2$ must be painted. One can covers only $400$, while two cover $800$, so $\boxed{2}$ cans are needed.
