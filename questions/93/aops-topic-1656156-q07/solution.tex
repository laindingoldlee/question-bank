A number divisible by $20=2^2\cdot5$ and $18=2\cdot3^2$ is divisible by $\operatorname{lcm}(20,18)=2^2\cdot3^2\cdot5=180$. The three-digit multiples of $180$ are $180,360,540,720,900$ (the next is $1080$), so the largest is $\boxed{\textbf{(d)}\ 900}$. ($990$ is divisible by $18$ but not by $20$.)
