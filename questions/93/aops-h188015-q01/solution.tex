Since $15^2+20^2=25^2$, the triangle is right and its area is $\dfrac{1}{2}(15)(20)=150$. An altitude to a side of length $s$ has length $\dfrac{300}{s}$. The shortest altitude corresponds to the longest side, giving $\boxed{12}$, choice $\boxed{\text{B}}$.
