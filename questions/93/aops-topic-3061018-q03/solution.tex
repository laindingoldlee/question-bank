Factor $3240=2^3\cdot3^4\cdot5$. An odd divisor has zero exponent of 2 and thus $(4+1)(1+1)=10$ choices. An even divisor has three positive choices for the exponent of 2 and therefore $3\cdot5\cdot2=30$ choices. The difference is $\boxed{20}$.
