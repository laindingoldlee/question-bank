Let $a$ and $b$ be positive integers. Greg chooses an integer uniformly at random from $1$ to $a$ inclusive, and independently Nikhil chooses an integer uniformly at random from $1$ to $b$ inclusive. The expected value of the product of their numbers equals three times the expected value of their sum. Find the sum of all possible values of $a$.
