(a) If two unknown three-digit positive integers are given only through their ratio, can they always be recovered uniquely? (b) Suppose instead that the integers are coprime and their ratio, less than $1$, is given truncated to nine decimal places. Prove that the pair can then be recovered uniquely and describe a finite procedure.
