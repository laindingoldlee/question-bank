Vincent the Bug starts at $0$ on a number line. Every minute he jumps $2$ units right with probability $\dfrac23$ or $3$ units right with probability $\dfrac13$. The probability that he never lands exactly on $15$ is $\dfrac pq$ in lowest terms. Find $p+q$.
