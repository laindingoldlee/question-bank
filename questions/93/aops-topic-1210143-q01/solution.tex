Let $A$ (who waits $10$ minutes) arrive at $a$ and $B$ (who waits $15$ minutes) at $b$, in minutes after 12:00. If $A$ arrives first ($a\le b$) they meet when $b-a\le10$; if $B$ arrives first ($b\le a$) they meet when $a-b\le15$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((0,0)--(60,0)--(60,60)--(0,60)--cycle); fill((0,0)--(15,0)--(60,45)--(60,60)--(50,60)--(0,10)--cycle,lightblue);
draw((15,0)--(60,45)); draw((0,10)--(50,60)); label("$A$",(30,0),S); label("$B$",(0,30),W); label("$60$",(60,0),S); label("$60$",(0,60),W);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

In the $60\times60$ square of possible $(a,b)$, the region where they fail to meet consists of two right triangles with legs $50$ and $45$. So
\[P(\text{meet})=1-\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot50^2+\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot45^2}{3600}=1-\dfrac{2262.5}{3600}=\boxed{\dfrac{107}{288}}\approx0.372.\]
