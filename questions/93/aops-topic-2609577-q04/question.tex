Team A has $7$ members and team B has $5$ members. Team A has a stack of $7$ CDs: $4$ with team A songs and $3$ with team B songs. Team B has a stack of $5$ CDs: $2$ with team B songs and $3$ with team A songs. In each round, each team's CDs are dealt out at random, one to each of its members; then one member of each team is chosen at random, and these two members swap their CDs; finally all CDs are returned to their team's stack. What is the probability that after $3$ rounds each stack contains only its own team's songs?
