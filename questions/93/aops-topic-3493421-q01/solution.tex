We have $11^7=19487171>18874368=\dfrac{9}{8}\,2^{24}$. Also $(\dfrac{9}{8})^6=\dfrac{531441}{262144}>2$. Therefore $11^{84}>(\dfrac{9}{8})^{12}2^{288}>4\cdot2^{288}=2^{290}>2^{289}$. Thus $\boxed{11^{84}}$ is larger.
