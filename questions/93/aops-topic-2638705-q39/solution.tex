Subtracting $30$ and factoring gives $(x+1)(y-5)(z+6)=18$. Since $x+1>0$ and $z+6\ge6$, we must have $y-5>0$. The divisor $z+6$ of $18$ is therefore $6,9,$ or $18$. For $z=0$, $(x+1)(y-5)=3$, giving $(x,y)=(0,8),(2,6)$. For $z=3$, the product is $2$, giving $(0,7),(1,6)$. For $z=12$, the product is $1$, giving $(0,6)$. Thus the complete list is $\boxed{(0,8,0),(2,6,0),(0,7,3),(1,6,3),(0,6,12)}$.
