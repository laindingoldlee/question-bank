Let $T=a+b+\dots+z$ and let $x_k$ be the value of the $k$th letter. The condition says $T-x_k=k$, that is, $x_k=T-k$. Adding these $26$ equations,
\[
T=26T-(1+2+\dots+26)=26T-351,\qquad\text{so}\qquad T=\dfrac{351}{25}.
\]
Hence the $k$th letter equals $\dfrac{351}{25}-k=\dfrac{351-25k}{25}$:
\[
\boxed{a=\dfrac{326}{25},\ b=\dfrac{301}{25},\ c=\dfrac{276}{25},\ \dots,\ y=-\dfrac{274}{25},\ z=-\dfrac{299}{25}}
\]
(each letter is $1$ less than the previous one). Check: the total is $26\cdot\dfrac{351}{25}-351=\dfrac{351}{25}=T$, and removing the $k$th letter leaves $T-x_k=k$.
