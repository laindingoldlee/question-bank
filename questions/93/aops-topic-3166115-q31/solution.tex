Factor $2200=2^3\cdot5^2\cdot11$. A divisor has form $2^a5^b11^c$ and divisor count $(a+1)(b+1)(c+1)$. Summing independently over exponents gives $(1+2+3+4)(1+2+3)(1+2)=\boxed{180}$.
