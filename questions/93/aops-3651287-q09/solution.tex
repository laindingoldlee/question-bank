Testing palindromes of one or two digits gives no match with their sum with 2025. For three digits write $n=101a+10b$, $1\le a\le9$, $0\le b\le9$, and test these 90 possibilities in increasing order. The first satisfying the reversed-digit test is $\boxed{747}$, since $747+2025=2772$. A reproducible minimality check is: for $a=1$ through 7 and $b=0$ through 9, form $101a+10b$ and accept exactly when its sum with 2025 equals its digit reversal; the first hit occurs at $(7,4)$.
