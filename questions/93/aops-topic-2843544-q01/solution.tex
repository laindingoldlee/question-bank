By the quadratic formula the roots are $r,s=\dfrac{-b\pm\sqrt{D}}{2a}$ with $D=b^2-4ac$ (any square root of $D$ if it is negative). Then
\[r+s=-\dfrac ba,\qquad rs=\dfrac{b^2-D}{4a^2}=\dfrac{4ac}{4a^2}=\dfrac ca.\]
Hence $a(x-r)(x-s)=a\bigl(x^2-(r+s)x+rs\bigr)=ax^2+bx+c$. $\blacksquare$
