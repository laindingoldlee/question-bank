No. Take an outer rectangle of width $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and height $\dfrac{3}{4}$, whose perimeter is $\dfrac{5}{2}$. Its top part is a $\dfrac{1}{2}\times\dfrac{1}{2}$ square of perimeter 2. Divide the bottom strip into two $\dfrac{1}{4}\times\dfrac{1}{4}$ squares, each of perimeter 1. Thus all three tile perimeters are integers but the outer perimeter is not.
