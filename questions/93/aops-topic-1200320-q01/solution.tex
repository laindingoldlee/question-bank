We need three vertices no two of which are adjacent on the octagon. Fix one chosen vertex (8 ways); the other two must come from the $5$ vertices not adjacent to it, which form a path of $5$, with no two adjacent: $\dbinom42=6$ ways. Each triangle is counted $3$ times, giving $\dfrac{8\cdot6}{3}=16$ triangles, out of $\dbinom83=56$. The probability is $\dfrac{16}{56}=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{7}}$.
