Dividing both sides gives $4x+7y=5$ and $7x+4y=17$. Eliminating yields $x=3,y=-1$, verified in both original equations. Multiplying an entire equation by a nonzero constant preserves its solution set; changing just one term need not, because it alters only part of one side.
