Eric has $1$ head and $2$ feet, so the chickens and pigs have $20$ heads and $50$ feet. If there are $c$ chickens and $p$ pigs, then $c+p=20$ and $2c+4p=50$. Subtracting twice the first equation, $2p=10$, so $p=5$ and $c=\boxed{15}$.
