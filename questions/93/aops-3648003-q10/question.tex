Angela has an unfair six-sided dice such that each odd number has probability p of being rolled while each even number has probability q of being rolled. After rolling the dice twice, the probability that the product of her rolls is a perfect square is $\dfrac{3}{4}(p + q)$. If pq can be expressed as $\dfrac{a}{b}$ , where $a$ and $b$ are relatively prime positive integers, find $a + b$.
