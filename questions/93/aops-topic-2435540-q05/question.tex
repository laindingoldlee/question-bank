Al and Bob play a game of Bottle Flip. They take turns flipping a water bottle, and the first person who fails to land the bottle loses. Al has a $\dfrac{4}{5}$ chance of landing the bottle every time he flips it, and Bob has a $\dfrac{3}{4}$ chance of landing the bottle every time he flips it, independently of all other flips. Given that Al goes first, what is the probability that he wins the game?
