Use six states recording the last letter and, for $A$ or $C$, whether its required neighbor is still pending. Denote them $A_0,A_1,T,C_0,C_1,G$, where subscript $1$ means pending. Starting from $(0,1,0,0,0,0)$, appending a letter gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
A'_0&=T,& A'_1&=C_0+G,&T'&=A_0+A_1+C_0+G,\\
C'_0&=G,& C'_1&=A_0+T,&G'&=A_0+T+C_0+C_1.
\end{aligned}
\]
These transitions forbid equal neighbors and force a pending letter's next neighbor. The totals of the four nonpending states for lengths $1$ through $9$ are
\[
0,1,2,5,12,29,70,169,408.
\]
At length nine the state vector is $(60,85,144,60,84,144)$, so the required count is $\boxed{408}$.
