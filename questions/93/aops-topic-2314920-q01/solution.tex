Add the probabilities of the ways in which he gets them back:
\[0.5\cdot0.9+0.25+0.25\cdot(0.2+0.6)=0.45+0.25+0.2=0.9.\]
The probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{9}{10}}$. (He loses them for good with probability $0.5\cdot0.1+0.25\cdot0.2=0.1$.)
