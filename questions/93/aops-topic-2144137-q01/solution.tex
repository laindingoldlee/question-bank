Neither $x$ nor $y$ is $0$, since $5^0=6^0=1$. Raising the equations to the powers $\dfrac{1}{x}$ and $\dfrac{1}{y}$ gives
\[5=30^{\dfrac{1}{x}},\qquad 6=30^{\dfrac{1}{y}}.\]
Multiplying,
\[30=5\cdot6=30^{\dfrac{1}{x}+\dfrac{1}{y}},\qquad\text{so}\qquad\dfrac{1}{x}+\dfrac{1}{y}=\boxed{1}.\]
(With logarithms: $\dfrac{1}{x}=\log_{30}5$ and $\dfrac{1}{y}=\log_{30}6$, whose sum is $\log_{30}30=1$.)
