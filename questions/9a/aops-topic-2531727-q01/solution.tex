Measure arrival times in minutes after 1:00: the boss at $X$ and the client at $Y$, with $(X,Y)$ uniform on the square $[0,120]^2$. They meet exactly when $|X-Y|\le40$. The other region consists of two right triangles with legs $120-40=80$, of total area $80^2=6400$ out of $120^2=14400$. So
\[P(\text{they do not meet})=\dfrac{6400}{14400}=\dfrac49.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real L=120;
fill((0,0)--(80,0)--(120,40)--(120,120)--(40,120)--(0,80)--cycle,rgb(0.85,0.85,0.85));
fill((40,120)--(0,120)--(0,80)--cycle,rgb(0.75,0.85,1));
fill((80,0)--(120,0)--(120,40)--cycle,rgb(0.75,0.85,1));
draw(box((0,0),(L,L)),linewidth(0.8));
draw((0,80)--(40,120)); draw((80,0)--(120,40));
string[] tl={"1:00","1:40","2:20","3:00"};
for(int i=0;i<4;++i){label(tl[i],(40*i,0),S,fontsize(7pt)); label(tl[i],(0,40*i),W,fontsize(7pt));}
label("boss arrives",(60,-14),S,fontsize(8pt));
label(rotate(90)*"client arrives",(-30,60),W,fontsize(8pt));
label("meet",(60,60),fontsize(9pt));
label("no",(16,108),fontsize(8pt)); label("no",(104,14),fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}

The cat is saved by the boss exactly when it falls, survives the fall, and the boss and the client do not meet:
\[\dfrac{9}{13}\cdot\dfrac{13}{14}\cdot\dfrac49=\boxed{\dfrac27}.\]
