A cat on a slippery roof falls with probability $\dfrac{9}{13}$. If it falls, it dies at once with probability $\dfrac{1}{14}$; otherwise it survives the fall, but it will die unless it is rescued. The only possible rescuer is a boss who has arranged to meet a client at a coffee shop. The boss and the client each arrive at a time chosen uniformly at random between 1:00 and 3:00, independently of each other and of the cat, and each waits $40$ minutes for the other before leaving. If they do not meet, the furious boss walks off his anger near the cat, finds it and saves it; if they meet, nobody saves the cat. What is the probability that the cat falls but is saved by the boss?
