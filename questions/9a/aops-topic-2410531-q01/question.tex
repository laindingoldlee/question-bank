A student reasons: ``Since $16\equiv4\pmod{12}$, we have $8^{16}\equiv8^4\pmod{12}$.''
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Is the conclusion $8^{16}\equiv8^4\pmod{12}$ true? Find the remainder of $8^{16}$ on division by $12$.
\item[(b)] Show that the reasoning is not valid in general: give an example where $j\equiv k\pmod m$ but $a^j\not\equiv a^k\pmod m$.
\end{enumerate}
