We claim that
\[
78n^2+72n+2018=(7-n)^3+(7-n)^3+(n+1)^3+(n+11)^3 .
\]
Expand each cube:
\[
2(7-n)^3=686-294n+42n^2-2n^3,\qquad(n+1)^3=n^3+3n^2+3n+1,\qquad(n+11)^3=n^3+33n^2+363n+1331 .
\]
Adding, the $n^3$ terms cancel and we get $(42+3+33)n^2+(-294+3+363)n+(686+1+1331)=78n^2+72n+2018$. Since $7-n$, $n+1$ and $n+11$ are integers, this writes $78n^2+72n+2018$ as a sum of four cubes for every integer $n$. $\blacksquare$

(The identity was found by looking for cubes of the form $(\pm n+c)^3$ whose $n^3$ terms cancel: two with $-n$ and two with $+n$.)
