A mechanical grasshopper is placed $42$ inches from the door of its cage. Each time a button is pressed, the grasshopper jumps one third of the remaining distance towards the door.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] How far from the door is the grasshopper after the third jump?
\item[(b)] What total distance has the grasshopper travelled after five jumps?
\end{enumerate}
