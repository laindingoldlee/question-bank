Let the intercepts be $u,v$. The lines are $y=2(x-u)$ and $y=-(x-v)$. Their intersection has height $\dfrac{2(v-u)}{3}$, up to sign. Thus if the intercept distance is $d=|v-u|$, the triangle area is $\dfrac{1}{2}d\cdot\dfrac{2d}{3}=\dfrac{d^2}{3}=108$. Hence $\boxed{d=18}$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(18,0),C=(6,12);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(C--(6,0),dashed);label("$u$",A,SW);label("$v$",B,SE);label("slope 2",(A+C)/2,NW);label("slope -1",(B+C)/2,NE);label("$d$",(9,0),S);
\end{asy}
