Place $B=(0,0),C=(7,0),A=(1,h),D=(6,h)$ with $h>0$. Reflection across $BD$ sends $A$ to the horizontal axis, so $BD$ bisects the angle between $BA$ and that axis. Writing $\theta=\angle DBC$ gives $\tan\theta=\dfrac{h}{6}$ and $\tan2\theta=h$. The double-angle identity gives $\dfrac{\dfrac{h}{3}}{1-\dfrac{h^2}{36}}=h$, hence $h^2=24$. Therefore the area is $\dfrac{5+7}{2}\cdot2\sqrt6=\boxed{12\sqrt6}$.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

\end{asy}
