\textbf{(a) True:} $x+2=3y$, so $x+2=ky$ with $k=3$.

\textbf{(b) False:} $x=3y-2$. If $x=ky$ for a constant $k$, then $y=0$ would force $x=0$, but $y=0$ gives $x=-2$.

\textbf{(c) True:} $3y-2=x$, so $3y-2=kx$ with $k=1$.

So both descriptions in the question are correct: $x+2$ and $y$ are directly proportional (constant $3$), and so are $3y-2$ and $x$ (constant $1$). It is $x$ and $y$ themselves that are not proportional; they are related linearly, not proportionally.
