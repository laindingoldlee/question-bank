Suppose $3y=x+2$ for all the pairs $(x,y)$ under consideration. (A quantity $u$ is \emph{directly proportional} to $v$ if $u=kv$ for some nonzero constant $k$.) Decide whether each statement is true:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $x+2$ is directly proportional to $y$;
\item[(b)] $x$ is directly proportional to $y$;
\item[(c)] $3y-2$ is directly proportional to $x$.
\end{enumerate}
