In each of the first nine cases the remainder is one less than the group size, so $N+1$ is divisible by $2,3,\dots,10$, hence by
\[
\operatorname{lcm}(2,3,\dots,10)=2^3\cdot3^2\cdot5\cdot7=2520 .
\]
So $N=2520k-1$ for a positive integer $k$. Since $2520=11\cdot229+1\equiv1\pmod{11}$, we need $k-1\equiv0\pmod{11}$, and the smallest choice is $k=1$:
\[
N=\boxed{2519}=11\cdot229 .
\]
