The square root is a three-digit prime palindrome below $\sqrt{100000}\approx316$: $101,131,151,181,191,313$, with squares $10201$, $17161$, $22801$, $32761$, $36481$, $97969$. Only $36481$ starts with a square ($36$); its middle digit is $4$ and it ends in $81$. The number is $\boxed{36481}=191^2$.
