Among three-character strings from 000 through 999, let A_i mean digit 7 occurs in position i. If that position is fixed, the other two digits must sum to 2 modulo 3. Their residue-class sizes are 4,3,3, so there are $4\cdot 3+3\cdot 3+3\cdot 4=33$ possibilities. For two fixed 7 s, the remaining digit is 1 modulo 3, giving 3 possibilities. With all three fixed,777 is divisible by 3, giving 1. Inclusion--exclusion gives $3\cdot 33-3\cdot 3+1=91$. Padding shorter integers with zeros changes neither their digit 7 occurrence nor divisibility. Neither 000 nor 1000 qualifies. Thus the fraction is $\boxed{\dfrac{91}{1000}}$.
