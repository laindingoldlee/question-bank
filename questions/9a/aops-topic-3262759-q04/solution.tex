For sums $3,\ldots,12$, B has outcome counts $1,3,6,10,12,12,10,6,3,1$, obtained by expanding $(x+x^2+x^3+x^4)^3$. A has counts $2,3,4,5,6,5,4,3,2,1$ at those sums. Their dot product is $282$, so the tie probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{282}{36\cdot64}=\dfrac{47}{384}}$.
For B sums $3,\ldots,12$, A has respectively $33,30,26,21,15,10,6,3,1,0$ outcomes strictly above them. Their weighted sum is $870$, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{870}{2304}=\dfrac{145}{384}}$.
