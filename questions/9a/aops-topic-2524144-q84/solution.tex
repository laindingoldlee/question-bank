A four-digit base-$x$ number has digits $d_1\ge1$ and $d_2,d_3,d_4\ge0$. If their sum is $x-1$, each digit is automatically at most $x-1$, so we only need nonnegative solutions of $(d_1-1)+d_2+d_3+d_4=x-2$. By stars and bars,
\[N_x=\binom{x-2+3}{3}=\binom{x+1}{3}.\]
(For example, $N_{10}=\binom{11}3=165$.) By the hockey-stick identity,
\[\sum_{x=10}^{20}\binom{x+1}3=\sum_{m=11}^{21}\binom m3=\binom{22}4-\binom{11}4=7315-330=\boxed{6985}.\]
