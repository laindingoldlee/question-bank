Five consecutive days within one month give $d+(d+1)+\dots+(d+4)=5d+10$, which is never $63$. So the days run over the end of a month: the last $k$ days of a month with $L$ days and then days $1,\dots,5-k$ of the next. Trying month lengths $L=28,29,30,31$ and $k=1,\dots,4$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $L=31$: totals $41,\ 67,\ 93,\ 121$; \quad $L=30$: $40,\ 65,\ 90,\ 117$;
\item $L=29$: $39,\ 63,\ 87,\ 113$; \quad $L=28$: $38,\ 61,\ 84,\ 109$.
\end{itemize}
Only $L=29$, $k=2$ works: $28+29+1+2+3=63$. So the dates are $\boxed{\text{February }28,\ 29\text{ and March }1,\ 2,\ 3\text{ of a leap year}}$.
