A convex cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$ has perpendicular diagonals meeting at $E$. Let $W,X,Y,Z$ be the perpendicular feet from $E$ to $AB,BC,CD,DA$. Given $EW=2EY$ and $EW\cdot EX\cdot EY\cdot EZ=36$, find the minimum of $\dfrac{1}{[EAB]}+\dfrac{1}{[EBC]}+\dfrac{1}{[ECD]}+\dfrac{1}{[EDA]}$.
