The diameter-circle theorem gives $CD\perp AB$ and $BE\perp AC$. Thus $AD=AC\cos45^\circ$ and $AE=AB\cos45^\circ$. The two triangles share angle $A$, so their area ratio is $\dfrac{AD\cdot AE}{AB\cdot AC}=\cos^245^\circ=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$.

\end{asy}
