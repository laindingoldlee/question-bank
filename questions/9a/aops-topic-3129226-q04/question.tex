There are 100 food trucks arranged in a circle and ten gnomes. Initially the gnomes eat at different trucks. After each course, gnome $i$ moves exactly $i$ trucks clockwise or counterclockwise before eating again. If every truck eventually serves a gnome, prove that some gnome visits a truck twice.
