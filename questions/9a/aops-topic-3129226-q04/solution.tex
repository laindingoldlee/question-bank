Assume no gnome revisits a truck. Gnome 10 stays within one residue class modulo 10, which contains only ten trucks, so there can be at most ten courses. Covering 100 trucks needs at least ten courses, since there are ten gnomes. Consequently there are exactly ten courses and all 100 visits must occur at different trucks. Label the trucks 0 through 99. Each odd-numbered gnome changes parity at every move, so among its ten visits exactly five are at odd trucks. The five such gnomes contribute 25 odd visits. Each even-numbered gnome keeps parity and contributes either zero or ten odd visits. The total odd visits is therefore 25 modulo 10. But visiting every truck once requires exactly 50 odd visits, a contradiction.
