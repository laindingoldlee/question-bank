The interior angles of a $20$-gon add up to $(20-2)\cdot180^\circ=3240^\circ$, so each interior angle of the regular $20$-gon measures $\dfrac{3240}{20}=162$ degrees. (Equivalently, each exterior angle is $\dfrac{360^\circ}{20}=18^\circ$ and $180^\circ-18^\circ=162^\circ$.)

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair[] V; for(int k=0;k<20;++k) V.push(dir(-99+18*k));
for(int k=0;k<20;++k) draw(V[k]--V[(k+1)%20],linewidth(0.8));
pair Q=V[1];
draw(Q--Q+(0.42,0),red+dashed);
draw(arc(Q,0.2,0,18),red); label("$18^\circ$",Q+(0.46,0.05),E,red+fontsize(8pt));
draw(arc(Q,0.12,18,180),blue); label("$162^\circ$",Q+0.3*dir(100),blue+fontsize(8pt));
dot(Q);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}


In lowest terms $162=\dfrac{162}{1}$, so $m=162$, $n=1$ and $m+n=\boxed{163}$.
