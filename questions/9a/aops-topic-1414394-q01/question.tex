Let $ABC$ be a triangle with inradius $99$ and circumradius $200$, and let $H$ be its orthocentre. The reflections of $H$ in the midpoints of the sides $AB$, $BC$, $CA$ are $H_1$, $H_2$, $H_3$, respectively. What is the average of the lengths $CH_1$, $AH_2$, $BH_3$?
