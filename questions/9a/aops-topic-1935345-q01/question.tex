A student writes the number of permutations of $p$ objects chosen from $n$ as $\dfrac{n!}{n-p!}$ and the number of combinations as $\dbinom np=\dfrac{n!}{n!\,(n-p)!}$. Find and correct the errors in both formulas, explain the difference between the two counts, and compute both for $n=5$, $p=2$.
