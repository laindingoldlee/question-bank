Because $AE\parallel DF$, the corresponding angles in triangles $AEP$ and $FDP$ agree; their angles at $P$ are also vertical angles. Therefore the triangles are similar, with corresponding bases $AE$ and $DF$.

Areas of similar triangles are in the square of the corresponding side ratio. Hence
\[
\left(\dfrac{AE}{DF}\right)^2=\dfrac{[AEP]}{[DFP]}=5.
\]
Both lengths are positive, so $\boxed{AE:DF=\sqrt5:1}$.
