Any one of the three points can be the common vertex of two adjacent sides. Vector addition gives possible fourth vertices $(7,12),(1,-2),(-1,2)$. Their $x$-coordinates sum to $\boxed7$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(3,7),C=(4,5);draw(A--B--(B+C)--C--cycle,blue);draw(B--A--(C-B)--C--cycle,red+dashed);draw(C--A--(B-C)--B--cycle,green+dotted);dot(A);dot(B);dot(C);
\end{asy}
