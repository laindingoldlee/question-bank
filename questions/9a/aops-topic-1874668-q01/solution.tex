Write $a=5m$ and $b=5n$ with $\gcd(m,n)=1$. Then $\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=5mn=55$, so $mn=11$, and $\{m,n\}=\{1,11\}$. Hence $\{a,b\}=\{5,55\}$ is the only possibility, and $a+b=\boxed{60}$.
