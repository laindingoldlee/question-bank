An integer ending in $7$ is $10k+7$; it is divisible by $3$ when $10k+7\equiv k+1\equiv0\pmod3$, i.e. $k\equiv2\pmod3$. So the numbers are $30j+27$: those between $2020$ and $2400$ are $2037,2067,\dots,2397$, i.e. $30j+27$ for $j=67,68,\dots,79$. That is $79-67+1=13$ numbers: $\boxed{\textbf{(D)}\ 13}$.
