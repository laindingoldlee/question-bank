In trapezoid $ABCD$ with $AB\parallel CD$, the diagonals meet at $O$, and the area of triangle $AOB$ is $k$ times the area of triangle $COD$. Assuming $AC\ne BD$, find
\[
\dfrac{AD^2-BC^2}{AC^2-BD^2}
\]
in terms of $k$.
