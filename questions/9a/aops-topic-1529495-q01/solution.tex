Because the $5$ cm blocks all go below the $3$ cm blocks, a tower is determined by the numbers $a$ of $5$ cm blocks and $b$ of $3$ cm blocks, with
\[
5a+3b=2016,\qquad a,b\ge0 .
\]
Since $3\mid2016$ and $3\mid3b$, we need $3\mid5a$, so $3\mid a$. Conversely, for every multiple $a$ of $3$ with $5a\le2016$, $b=\dfrac{2016-5a}{3}$ is a nonnegative integer. As $5a\le2016$ means $a\le403$, the possible values are $a=0,3,6,\dots,402$, which is $\dfrac{402}{3}+1=\boxed{135}$ ways. (The example has $a=12$, $b=652$.)
