There are $\left\lfloor\dfrac{50}{3}\right\rfloor=16$ multiples of $3$, $\left\lfloor\dfrac{50}{5}\right\rfloor=10$ multiples of $5$, and $\left\lfloor\dfrac{50}{15}\right\rfloor=3$ multiples of both (that is, of $15$). By inclusion-exclusion the answer is $16+10-3=\boxed{23}$.
