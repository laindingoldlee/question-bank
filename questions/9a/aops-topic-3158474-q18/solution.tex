The zero triple works. Otherwise $ab+bc+ca\ge a(a+b+c)$ implies $a\le2$. If $a=0$, the equation is $(b-2)(c-2)=4$; with $0\le b\le c$, solutions are $(b,c)=(0,0),(3,6),(4,4)$. If $a=1$, it becomes $(b-1)(c-1)=3$, giving $(b,c)=(2,4)$. If $a=2$, it becomes $bc=4$, giving $b=c=2$. Thus the complete list is $\boxed{(0,0,0),(0,3,6),(0,4,4),(1,2,4),(2,2,2)}$.
