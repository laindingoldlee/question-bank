The square and circle have at most eight isolated crossings, the hexagon and circle at most twelve, and the square and convex hexagon at most eight. Thus the total is at most $\boxed{28}$. Concentric regular polygons with slightly differing rotations and a circle whose radius lies between both apothems and both circumradii attain all three bounds; a generic small perturbation separates any coincident crossings.
\begin{asy}
import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
draw(circle((0,0),.94));path h=dir(0);for(int i=1;i<6;++i)h=h--dir(60*i);draw(h--cycle,blue);draw((1.1,0)--(0,1.1)--(-1.1,0)--(0,-1.1)--cycle,red);label("8 + 12 + 8 crossings",(0,-1.5));
\end{asy}
