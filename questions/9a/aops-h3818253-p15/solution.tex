For $x=0$ there is no solution; $x=1$ gives $y=1$. If $x\ge2$, modulo $4$ forces $y=2k$ with $k\ge1$. Hence
\[
(3^k-1)(3^k+1)=2^x.
\]
Both factors are powers of $2$ differing by $2$. Their greatest common divisor is $2$, so the smaller is $2$ and the larger $4$. Thus $k=1,x=3$. The solutions are $\boxed{(1,1),(3,2)}$.
