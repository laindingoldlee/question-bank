A decreasing integer between $2000$ and $7000$ has four digits and leading digit $d\in\{2,3,4,5,6\}$ ($7000$ itself is not decreasing). Its other three digits are three different digits less than $d$, and once they are chosen they must be written in decreasing order. So there are $\dbinom d3$ such numbers with leading digit $d$:
\[
\dbinom23+\dbinom33+\dbinom43+\dbinom53+\dbinom63=0+1+4+10+20=\boxed{35}.
\]
