Since $ 231=3\cdot7\cdot11 $, its three prime-exponent contributions to the divisor count are of the form $ 5e+2 $, while any other prime dividing $ x $ contributes $ 5e+1 $. Now $ 3213=3^3\cdot7\cdot17 $ is odd, so the three former contributions are odd and at least 7. Any nontrivial divisor of 3213 congruent to 1 modulo 5 is at least 21, but $ 21\cdot7^3>3213 $; thus there are no other primes. A factor of the form $ 5e+2 $ cannot exceed $\dfrac{3213}{49}<66 $. The divisors of 3213 below 66 congruent to 2 modulo 5 are 7,17,27. Their only triple with product 3213 is $ 7\cdot17\cdot27 $. Therefore the exponents of 3,7,11 in $ x $ are 1,3,5 in some order. Hence $\tau(x)=2\cdot4\cdot6=\boxed{48}$.
