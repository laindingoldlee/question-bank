Completing squares gives $(a+2)^2+(b-3)^2=234$. The only square residues modulo $3$ are $0,1$. Since $234$ is divisible by $3$, both squares must be divisible by $3$, so $3\mid a+2$ and $3\mid b-3$. Their squares and their sum must then be divisible by $9$, but $234\equiv 0\pmod 9$ (indeed $234=9\cdot 26$). Put $a+2=3u,b-3=3v$ to obtain $u^2+v^2=26$. As $|u|,|v|\le 5$, the squares available are $0,1,4,9,16,25$; only $1+25=26$. Thus $(a+2,b-3)=(\pm 3,\pm 15)$ or $(\pm 15,\pm 3)$, with independent signs. The eight pairs are $\boxed{(1,18),(1,-12),(-5,18),(-5,-12),(13,6),(13,0),(-17,6),(-17,0)}$.
