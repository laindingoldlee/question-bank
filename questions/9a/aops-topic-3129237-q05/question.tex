A captain and $100$ crew members start with one gold coin each. The captain may make at most two proposals redistributing the $101$ indivisible coins among them. The captain does not vote; a crew member votes yes if gaining coins, no if losing, and abstains otherwise. A proposal passes only when yes votes outnumber no votes. What is the maximum number of coins the captain can finish with?
