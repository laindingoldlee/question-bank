The four-digit cubes up to $9000$ are $10^3,\dots,20^3$ (since $21^3=9261$):
\[1000,\ 1331,\ 1728,\ 2197,\ 2744,\ 3375,\ 4096,\ 4913,\ 5832,\ 6859,\ 8000.\]
For each, count how many of the first three digits are $0$, $1$, $4$ or $9$, and compare with the last digit:
\[1000:3\ne0,\quad 1331:1=1,\quad 1728:1\ne8,\quad 2197:2\ne7,\quad 2744:1\ne4,\quad 3375:0\ne5,\]
\[4096:3\ne6,\quad 4913:3=3,\quad 5832:0\ne2,\quad 6859:0\ne9,\quad 8000:2\ne0.\]
$1331$ is a palindrome, so the unique orz number is $\boxed{4913}=17^3$.
