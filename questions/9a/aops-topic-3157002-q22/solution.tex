Let $ S(n)$ denote the sum through $ n $. The odd numbers through $ 2n $ sum to $ n^2 $, while the even numbers have the same odd parts as 1 through $ n $. Thus $ S(2n)=n^2+S(n)$ and $ S(1)=1 $. Hence $ S(256)=1+1+4+16+\cdots+16384=1+\dfrac{4^8-1}{3}=\boxed{21846}$.
