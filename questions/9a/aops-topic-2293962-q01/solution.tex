Collect terms on each side: the left side is $10+2x$ and the right side is $2x+3$. If some number $x$, real or complex, satisfied $10+2x=2x+3$, then subtracting $2x$ from both sides (which is allowed for complex numbers too) would give $10=3$, which is false. So $\boxed{\text{there is no solution}}$, real or complex. Complex numbers create solutions for equations like $x^2=-1$; they cannot help when the variable cancels and leaves a false statement about numbers.
