A prime $p<100$ is chosen uniformly. A player wins if an integer $n$ makes $n^6+2n^5+2n^4+n^3+(n^2+n+1)^2$ divisible by $p$. Write the winning probability as $\dfrac{a}{b}$ in lowest terms and find $a+b$.
