Let $A=(0,0),B=(4,0),C=(0,3)$. The angle-bisector theorem gives feet $D=(\dfrac{12}{7},\dfrac{12}{7})$ on $BC$, $E=(0,\dfrac{4}{3})$ on $CA$, and $F=(\dfrac{3}{2},0)$ on $AB$. The determinant of $D-F$ and $E-F$ is $\dfrac{20}{7}$, so $[DEF]=\boxed{\dfrac{10}{7}}$.
