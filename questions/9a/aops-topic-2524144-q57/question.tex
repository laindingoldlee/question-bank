In a strategy game, Team B's chance of winning a battle is $100\%$ if Team A has no allies and $80\%$ if Team A has one ally. Each additional ally of Team A multiplies Team B's chance of winning by $0.95$. What is the least number of allies Team A needs so that Team B's chance of winning is less than $50\%$?
