With $k\ge1$ allies, Team B's chance is $0.8\cdot0.95^{k-1}$. We need $0.95^{k-1}<\dfrac{0.5}{0.8}=0.625$. Now
\[0.95^9\approx0.6302>0.625,\qquad0.95^{10}\approx0.5987<0.625,\]
and the powers decrease, so the least $k-1$ is $10$. Team A needs $\boxed{11}$ allies (then B's chance is about $47.9\%$; with $10$ allies it is about $50.4\%$).
