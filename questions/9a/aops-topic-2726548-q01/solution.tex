Since $\dfrac{1}{k(k+7)(k+14)}=\dfrac1{14}\left(\dfrac{1}{k(k+7)}-\dfrac{1}{(k+7)(k+14)}\right)$, the sum telescopes:
\[S_n=\dfrac1{14}\left(\dfrac{1}{3\cdot10}-\dfrac{1}{(n+7)(n+14)}\right).\]
Hence $S_n<\dfrac{1}{14\cdot30}=\dfrac1{420}<\dfrac1{60}$ for every $n$, so $\boxed{S_n=\dfrac1{60}\text{ never holds}}$. (The post's computation drops the factor $\dfrac1{14}$ and then gets $n=-2$, which is not of the required form anyway.)
