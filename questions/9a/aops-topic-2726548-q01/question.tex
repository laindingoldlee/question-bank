Find a closed form for
\[S_n=\dfrac{1}{3\cdot10\cdot17}+\dfrac{1}{10\cdot17\cdot24}+\cdots+\dfrac{1}{n(n+7)(n+14)}\]
(where $n\equiv3\pmod7$), and decide whether $S_n=\dfrac1{60}$ for some $n$.
