Altogether $B$ is $2+2=4$ meters north and $3$ meters east of $A$. By the Pythagorean theorem,
\[AB=\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=\boxed{5\text{ meters}}.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0), P1=(0,2), P2=(3,2), Bp=(3,4);
draw(O--P1--P2--Bp,blue+linewidth(0.9),Arrow(6));
draw(O--Bp,red+dashed);
draw((0,0)--(3,0)--(3,2),dotted);
dot(O); dot(Bp);
label("$A$",O,SW); label("$B$",Bp,NE);
label("$2$",(0,1),W,blue); label("$3$",(2.3,2),N,blue); label("$2$",(3,3),E,blue);
label("$5$",(0.9,1.2),SE,red);
label("$3$",(1.5,0),S); label("$4$",(3,1),E);
\end{asy}
