For ordered queen--king positions, horizontal and vertical attacks give $2\cdot8\cdot8\cdot7=896$ outcomes. Diagonal lengths in each direction are $1,2,\ldots,7,8,7,\ldots,1$, giving $2[8\cdot7+2\sum_{j=1}^7j(j-1)]=560$ ordered attacks. These directions are disjoint for distinct squares. Divide $1456$ by $64\cdot63=4032$ to obtain $\boxed{\dfrac{13}{36}}$.
