On any given day a person is hit by a car with probability $p$, and the events on different days are independent.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What is the probability that the person is hit both today and tomorrow?
\item[(b)] Given that the person is hit today, what is the probability that he is hit tomorrow?
\end{enumerate}
A friend argues that the answer to (b) is $p^2$, because being hit twice in a row has probability $p^2$. Explain the error.
