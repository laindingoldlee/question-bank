In triangle $DAB$, the midpoint theorem gives $XY\parallel DB$. Hence $DB\perp AB$, so triangle $ABD$ is right-angled at $B$. Its hypotenuse midpoint $X$ satisfies $XA=XB=XD$.

Since $Z$ is the midpoint of $BC$ and $XZ\perp BC$, $X$ lies on the perpendicular bisector of $BC$, giving $XB=XC$. Thus all four vertices lie on the circle centered at $X$, and $AD$ is a diameter. Therefore $\angle ACD=90^\circ$.

By convexity ray $CA$ is inside angle $BCD$, so
\[
\angle ACB=\angle BCD-\angle ACD=109^\circ-90^\circ=\boxed{19^\circ}.
\]
