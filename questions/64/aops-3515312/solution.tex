Put $M=2025$, $N=M^2$. If the selected pile has size $2k+1$, its remaining $2k$ balls form a uniformly random subset of all $N$ balls: each such subset has exactly $N-2k$ possible removed balls, so no subset is favoured. From one type the generating polynomial for an even selection is $E(x)=\sum_{j=0}^{1012}\binom M{2j}x^{2j}=\dfrac{(1+x)^M+(1-x)^M}{2}$. Thus the number of valid $2k$-subsets is $[x^{2k}]E(x)^M$, out of $\binom N{2k}$ subsets. Averaging the $2025$ equally likely pile sizes gives the exact answer $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2025}\sum_{k=0}^{2024}\dfrac{[x^{2k}]\left(\dfrac{(1+x)^{2025}+(1-x)^{2025}}{2}\right)^{2025}}{\binom{4100625}{2k}}}$. The coefficient notation counts subsets of physically distinct balls, even when their types agree.
