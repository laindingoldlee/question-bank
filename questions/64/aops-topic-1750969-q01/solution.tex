Choose the two actors: $\dbinom82=28$ ways (they play the same role, so their order does not matter). Arrange the remaining $6$ students around the circle up to rotation: $\dfrac{6!}{6}=5!=120$ ways. The total is $28\cdot120=\boxed{3360}$.
