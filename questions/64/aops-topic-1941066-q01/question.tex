At $h$ hours and $m$ minutes ($0\le h\le11$, $0\le m<60$), show that one of the angles between the hands of a clock is
\[
\left|30h-\dfrac{11m}{2}\right|\text{ degrees},
\]
so the smaller angle is this number or $360^\circ$ minus it, whichever is at most $180^\circ$. Use the formula to find the smaller angle between the hands at $7{:}24$.
