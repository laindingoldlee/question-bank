Measure angles clockwise from the $12$. The minute hand turns $360^\circ$ in $60$ minutes, so it is at $6m$ degrees. The hour hand turns $30^\circ$ per hour and $\dfrac{30}{60}=\dfrac{1}{2}$ degree per minute, so it is at $30h+\dfrac {m}{2}$ degrees. The angle between them is the difference
\[
\left|30h+\dfrac {m}{2}-6m\right|=\left|30h-\dfrac{11m}{2}\right|,
\]
which lies between $0^\circ$ and $360^\circ$; if it exceeds $180^\circ$, the smaller angle is $360^\circ$ minus it.

At $7{:}24$: $|210-132|=78$, which is at most $180$, so the smaller angle is $\boxed{78^\circ}$.
