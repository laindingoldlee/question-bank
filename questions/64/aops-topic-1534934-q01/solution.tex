The three winners must be Andy, Barbara and Christie in some order. There are $3!=6$ orders, and each has probability $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{1}{6}=\dfrac{1}{36}$. So the probability is
\[
6\cdot\dfrac{1}{36}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{6}}.
\]
