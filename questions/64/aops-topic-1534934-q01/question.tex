Andy, Barbara and Christie play a game repeatedly. In each game exactly one of them wins: Andy with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, Barbara with probability $\dfrac{1}{3}$ and Christie with probability $\dfrac{1}{6}$, independently of the other games. What is the probability that each of them wins exactly one of the first three games played? Express your answer as a common fraction.
