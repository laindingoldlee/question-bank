$6^6=46656>3382$, so $z\le5$; and if $z\le4$ the sum is at most $3\cdot4^4=768$. Hence $z=5$, $5^5=3125$, and $x^x+y^y=257$. With $x\le y\le5$: $y=4$ gives $x^x=1$, $x=1$; $y\le3$ gives at most $54$. So $(x,y,z)=\boxed{(1,4,5)}$.
