Put $u=\dfrac{1}{9}$. The finite product identity $(1-u)\prod_{k=0}^{N}(1+u^{2^k})=1-u^{2^{N+1}}$ follows by repeated difference of squares. Since $0<u<1$, taking the limit gives product $\dfrac{1}{1-u}=\dfrac{9}{8}$. Thus $K=2^{\dfrac{9}{8}}$ and $\boxed{K^8=512}$.
