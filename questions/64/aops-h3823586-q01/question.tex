Let $d=\tau(n)$ for a positive integer $n$. Compute $r$, the remainder of $180(3d-1)$ on division by $10$, then $c=\binom{r+5}{2}$, then the digit sum $s$ of $c$. An algorithm next asks for the smallest prime factor of $s^2$. Explain why this step fails for every $n$. If that step is changed to the smallest prime factor $p$ of $s^2+3$, and the output is $\dfrac{p^2}{2}$, what is the result after $10^{11}$ iterations starting at $1$?
