Multiplying gives $(y+y^{-1})(y^n+y^{-n})=x_{n+1}+x_{n-1}$. Thus $x_0=2$, $x_1=3$, and $x_{n+1}=3x_n-x_{n-1}$ for $n\ge1$. Solving $y^2-3y+1=0$ gives reciprocal roots $\dfrac{3\pm\sqrt5}{2}$. Whichever root is chosen, $\boxed{x_n=\left(\dfrac{3+\sqrt5}{2}\right)^n+\left(\dfrac{3-\sqrt5}{2}\right)^n}$. More generally replace 3 by $k$ to obtain $x_{n+1}=kx_n-x_{n-1}$ and $\boxed{x_n=\left(\dfrac{k+\sqrt{k^2-4}}{2}\right)^n+\left(\dfrac{k-\sqrt{k^2-4}}{2}\right)^n}$. For $k=2$, both bases are 1 and $x_n=2$, as required.
