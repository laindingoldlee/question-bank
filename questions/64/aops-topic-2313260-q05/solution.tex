The digit sum is $1+8+7+8+7+8+0+1+4+1=45$, which is divisible by $9$. So $1878780141$ is divisible by $9$ (it equals $9\cdot208753349$), and since it is larger than $9$, it is $\boxed{\text{composite}}$.
