Let $F$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $B$. The regions are the triangles $ABF$, $BCF$ and $ACD$, where $[ACD]=[ABC]=[ABF]+[BCF]$. Triangles $ABF$ and $BCF$ are similar to triangle $ACB$ (they share its angles), with hypotenuses $AB$ and $BC$, so $\dfrac{[BCF]}{[ABF]}=\left(\dfrac{BC}{AB}\right)^2>1$.

The areas in increasing order are $[ABF]<[BCF]<[ABF]+[BCF]$, and the arithmetic progression condition gives $[BCF]-[ABF]=[ABF]$, i.e. $[BCF]=2[ABF]$. Hence $\left(\dfrac{BC}{AB}\right)^2=2$ and $\dfrac{AB}{BC}=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$. So $a=b=2$ and $ab=\boxed{4}$.

fill(A--B--Fp--cycle,mediumgray);fill(B--C--Fp--cycle,lightgray);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.8));draw(A--C);draw(B--Fp,dashed);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,NW);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,SE);label("$F$",Fp,SE);
\end{asy}
