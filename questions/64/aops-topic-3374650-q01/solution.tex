For base $b>0$, the height is $6b+8$. Thus $\dfrac{1}{2} b(6b+8)=132$, or $3b^2+4b-132=(b-6)(3b+22)=0$. Only $b=6$ is positive, giving $h=44$. Hence $\boxed{b=6\text{ yd},\ h=44\text{ yd}}$.
