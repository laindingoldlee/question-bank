In $30$ real minutes the car clock advances $35$ minutes, so it always shows $\dfrac{35}{30}=\dfrac{7}{6}$ times the real time elapsed since noon. When the car clock shows 7:00 it has advanced $420$ minutes, so the real time elapsed is $420\cdot\dfrac{6}{7}=360$ minutes. The actual time is $\boxed{6{:}00\text{ p.m.}}$
