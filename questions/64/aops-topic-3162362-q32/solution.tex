Put $ B=(0,0),C=(14,0),A=(5,12)$. The altitude from $ A $ is $ x=5 $, and the altitude from $ B $ has slope $\dfrac{3}{4}$, so $ H=(5,\dfrac{15}{4})$. The circle centres lie vertically above their tangent points. Substitution of $ H $ yields centres $(0,\dfrac{125}{24})$ and $(14,\dfrac{507}{40})$. Subtracting their circle equations gives the common chord line $ x=7-\dfrac{8}{15}y $. Substituting into $ x^2+y^2-\dfrac{125}{12}y=0 $ gives roots $ y=\dfrac{15}{4}$ and $ y=\dfrac{2940}{289}$. Thus $ R=(\dfrac{455}{289},\dfrac{2940}{289})$, and
\[AR=\dfrac{\sqrt{990^2+528^2}}{289}=\dfrac{66\cdot17}{289}=\boxed{\dfrac{66}{17}}.\]
