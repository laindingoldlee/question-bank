Each angle is an inscribed angle intercepting the minor arc $ AH $, which has measure 45 degrees. Each therefore equals $\dfrac{45^\circ}{2}=22.5^\circ $. Their sum is $ 6\cdot22.5^\circ=\boxed{135^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair[] P;for(int k=0;k<8;++k)P.push(dir(90-45*k));path p=P[0];for(int k=1;k<8;++k)p=p--P[k];draw(p--cycle);string[] L={"A","B","C","D","E","F","G","H"};for(int k=0;k<8;++k)label("$"+L[k]+"$",P[k],P[k]);draw(P[0]--P[3]--P[7],blue);
\end{asy}
