Write $z=x+iy$ with $x,y$ real. Then $\overline z=x-iy$ and
\[z\,\overline z=(x+iy)(x-iy)=x^2+y^2=|z|^2=1 .\]
In particular $z\ne0$, and dividing by $z$ gives $\overline z=\dfrac{1}{z}$. $\blacksquare$
