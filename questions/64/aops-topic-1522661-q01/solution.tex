By the binomial theorem,
\[
\left(3+\sqrt5\right)^n=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\dbinom nk3^{n-k}\left(\sqrt5\right)^k,\qquad\left(3-\sqrt5\right)^n=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\dbinom nk3^{n-k}\left(-\sqrt5\right)^k .
\]
When we add, the terms with odd $k$ cancel and the terms with even $k=2j$ double:
\[
\left(3+\sqrt5\right)^n+\left(3-\sqrt5\right)^n=2\sum_{0\le 2j\le n}\dbinom{n}{2j}3^{n-2j}5^{j},
\]
which is a sum of integers. $\blacksquare$

(Alternatively, $3\pm\sqrt5$ are the roots of $t^2-6t+4=0$, so $S_n=\left(3+\sqrt5\right)^n+\left(3-\sqrt5\right)^n$ satisfies $S_n=6S_{n-1}-4S_{n-2}$ with $S_0=2$, $S_1=6$, and induction gives integers $2,6,28,144,\dots$)
