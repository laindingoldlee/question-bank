Put $m=2000$. The numerator is $(m+1)((m+1)^2-400)=m^3+3m^2-397m-399$. Dividing by $m$ gives $m^2+3m-397-\dfrac{399}{m}=4005603-\dfrac{399}{2000}$. Its floor is $N=4005602$, whose digit sum is $4+0+0+5+6+0+2=\boxed{17}$.
