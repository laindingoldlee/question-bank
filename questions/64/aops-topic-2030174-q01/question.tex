A dessert chef prepares a dessert for each day of a week, Sunday through Saturday. Each day's dessert is cake, pie, ice cream, or pudding. The same dessert may not be served on two consecutive days, and there must be cake on Friday because of a birthday. How many different dessert menus for the week are possible?
