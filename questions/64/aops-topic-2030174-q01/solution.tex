Build the menu outward from Friday, which is fixed as cake.
\begin{itemize}
\item Saturday: anything except Friday's cake, $3$ choices.
\item Thursday: anything except Friday's cake, $3$ choices.
\item Wednesday, Tuesday, Monday, Sunday, in that order: anything except the dessert of the day just after, $3$ choices each.
\end{itemize}
Every menu satisfying the rules arises exactly once this way, so the number of menus is
\[3^6=\boxed{729}.\]
(Going forward from Sunday instead is awkward, because Thursday's options depend on whether Wednesday was cake; that is the source of the confusion in counting $3$ choices for every day from Sunday on. An alternative check: without the Friday rule there are $4\cdot3^6$ menus, and by symmetry among the four desserts exactly a quarter of them have cake on Friday.)
