The involution condition makes $f$ injective: applying $f$ to equal outputs recovers equal inputs. Thus the second identity implies $2f(2x+16)=\dfrac{1}{x+8}$. Setting $t=2x+16$ gives $f(t)=\dfrac{1}{t}$ for every $t>16$, and hence $f(2018)=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2018}}$. The assumptions are consistent, since $f(t)=\dfrac{1}{t}$ on all positive reals satisfies both identities.
