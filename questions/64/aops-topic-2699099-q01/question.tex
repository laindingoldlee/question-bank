Players numbered $1$ to $16$ cross, in order, a bridge of $18$ consecutive pairs of glass tiles. In each pair, one tile (chosen at random, independently) is safe and the other breaks, eliminating the player who steps on it. Each player walks on tiles already known to be safe and guesses (with probability $\dfrac12$ of success) at the first pair not yet revealed; any guess, right or wrong, reveals that pair. What is the probability that player $7$ crosses without having to guess?
