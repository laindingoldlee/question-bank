Factor out $15^{121}$:
\[
15^{121}+15^{122}+15^{123}=15^{121}\left(1+15+15^2\right)=15^{121}\cdot241=3^{121}\cdot5^{121}\cdot241 .
\]
The number $241$ is prime: $15^2=225<241<256=16^2$, and $241$ is not divisible by $2,3,5,7,11,13$. So the greatest prime factor is $\boxed{241}$.
