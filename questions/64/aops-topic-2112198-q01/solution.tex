The term $\dfrac{21x}{7x}$ requires $x\ne0$, and then it equals $3$. The equation becomes
\[3x^2+3x+3=20x+34\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad 3x^2-17x-31=0 .\]
By the quadratic formula,
\[x=\dfrac{17\pm\sqrt{289+372}}{6}=\boxed{\dfrac{17\pm\sqrt{661}}{6}}.\]
Neither root is $0$ (their product is $-\dfrac{31}{3}\ne0$), so both are solutions. Numerically, $x\approx7.118$ or $x\approx-1.452$.
