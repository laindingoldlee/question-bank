A student claims: ``five points are needed to determine a unique parabola; four points are not enough.''
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that the four points $(-2,6)$, $(1,-3)$, $(2,-2)$ and $(3,1)$ lie on both parabolas $y=x^2-2x-2$ and $6x=18+y-y^2$. So four points do not always determine a parabola.
\item[(b)] Show that infinitely many ellipses pass through the four points $(1,1)$, $(-1,1)$, $(-1,-1)$, $(1,-1)$. So four points do not determine a conic section either.
\end{enumerate}
