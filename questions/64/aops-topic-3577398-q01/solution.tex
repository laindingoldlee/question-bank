The recurrence is $3p_{k+1}-5p_k+2p_{k-1}=0$, whose characteristic roots are $1$ and $\dfrac{2}{3}$. Thus $p_k=A+B(\dfrac{2}{3})^k$. A probability distribution has $p_k\to0$, so $A=0$. From $p_0=\dfrac{1}{3}$, $B=\dfrac{1}{3}$. These probabilities do sum to $1$. Therefore $p_4=\dfrac{1}{3}(\dfrac{2}{3})^4=\boxed{\dfrac{16}{243}}$.
