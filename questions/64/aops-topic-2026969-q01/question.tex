Let $n\ge 2$ be an integer. There is a unique positive integer $k$ with $k!\le n<(k+1)!$. Call $n$ \emph{somewhat prime} if $n$ is not divisible by any of the integers $2,3,\dots,k$. (When $k=1$ this list is empty.) How many somewhat prime integers are there from $2$ to $1440$, inclusive?
