Place $P=(-4,0)$ and $Q=(4,0)$. The area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot8\cdot h=12$, where $h$ is the distance from $R$ to line $PQ$, so $h=3$: $R$ lies on one of the lines $y=3$, $y=-3$.
\begin{itemize}
\item Right angle at $P$: $R$ is on the line $x=-4$, giving $(-4,3)$ and $(-4,-3)$.
\item Right angle at $Q$: similarly $(4,3)$ and $(4,-3)$.
\item Right angle at $R$: by Thales' theorem $R$ lies on the circle with diameter $PQ$, i.e.\ $x^2+y^2=16$. With $y=\pm3$ we get $x=\pm\sqrt7$, giving $4$ points.
\end{itemize}

usepackage("amsmath");

draw(circle((0,0),4),gray);
draw((-6,3)--(6,3),dotted);draw((-6,-3)--(6,-3),dotted);
draw((-4,-3.6)--(-4,3.6),dashed);draw((4,-3.6)--(4,3.6),dashed);
draw(P--Q,linewidth(1));
real xr=sqrt(16-9);
pair[] R={(-4,3),(-4,-3),(4,3),(4,-3),(xr,3),(-xr,3),(xr,-3),(-xr,-3)};
for(pair r:R) dot(r,red);
draw(P--(xr,3)--Q,red);
dot(P);dot(Q);label("$P$",P,W);label("$Q$",Q,E);label("$8$",(0,0),S);
label("$y=3$",(6,3),E);label("$y=-3$",(6,-3),E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

These $8$ points are distinct, so the answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(D)}\ 8}$.
