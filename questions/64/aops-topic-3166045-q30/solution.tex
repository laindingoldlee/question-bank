Repeated difference of squares gives $(1-t)\prod_{j=0}^{m}(1+t^{2^j})=1-t^{2^{m+1}}$. At $t=\dfrac{1}{2}$, the finite product equals $2(1-2^{-2^{m+1}})$, which tends to $\boxed2$.
