Deleting the units digit replaces $n$ by $\lfloor \dfrac{n}{5}\rfloor$. The new sum is $5(0+1+\cdots+9)+10=235$, while the correct sum is $50\cdot\dfrac{51}{2}=1275$. The difference is $\boxed{1040}$.
