The terms involving $x$ factor as $x(x+1)(x+2)$, which is divisible by $3$. Also $3y$ is divisible by $3$, whereas $2018\equiv2\pmod3$. Thus $z^2\equiv2\pmod3$, impossible because a square is $0$ or $1$ modulo $3$. There are $\boxed{\text{no solutions}}$.
