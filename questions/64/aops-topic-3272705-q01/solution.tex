Let the counts be $(q,n,d,p)$. They satisfy $25q+5n+10d+p=100$ and the stated bounds. Reduction modulo $5$ forces $p=0$ or $5$. Testing $q=0,\ldots,4$ in $5q+n+2d=20-\dfrac{p}{5}$ gives precisely $(2,3,3,5),(3,0,2,5),(3,1,2,0),(3,2,1,5),(3,3,1,0),(4,0,0,0)$. Thus there are $\boxed6$ combinations.
