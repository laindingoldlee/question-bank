Add the first three copies and divide by the fourth:
\[(\pi+\pi+\pi)\div\pi=\dfrac{3\pi}{\pi}=3.\]
So $\boxed{(\pi+\pi+\pi)\div\pi=3}$ works. The brackets matter: without them, $\pi+\pi+\pi\div\pi=2\pi+1$.
