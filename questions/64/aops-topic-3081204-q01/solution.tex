Write the second number as $(013)_6$. Borrowing one unit from the $6^2$ place gives six units in the $6$ place; borrowing one of those gives six extra units in the units place. The columns are now $0,5,7$ in ordinary decimal counts. Subtracting $0,1,3$ gives digits $0,4,4$, hence $\boxed{(44)_6}$. A check in base ten is $37-9=28=4\cdot6+4$.
