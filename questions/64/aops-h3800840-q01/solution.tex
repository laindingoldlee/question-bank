A four-digit year has digit sum at most $36$, so the birth year lies between $1974$ and $2009$. For a year $1900+10a+b$, the condition is $110-10a-b=10+a+b$, or $11a+2b=100$. With digits $a,b$ and the stated range, the only solution is $a=8,b=6$, giving year $1986$ and age $24$. For a year $2000+10a+b$, the equation is $10-10a-b=2+a+b$, or $11a+2b=8$, forcing $a=0,b=4$. This gives year $2004$ and age $6$. Thus $\boxed{6\text{ or }24}$.
