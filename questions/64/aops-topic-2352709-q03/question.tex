In triangle $ABC$, points $X$, $Y$, $Z$ lie on the sides $AB$, $BC$, $CA$ respectively, so that
\[AX:XB=BY:YC=CZ:ZA.\]
Given that $AB=7$, the area of triangle $XYZ$ is $7$ and the area of triangle $ABC$ is $12$, compute $|AX-BX|$.
