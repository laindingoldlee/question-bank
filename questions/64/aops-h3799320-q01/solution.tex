One construction is
\[
\boxed{\texttt{123412314231243121342132413214321}}.
\]
Its permutation substrings begin at positions $1,2,3,4;\ 6,7,8,9;\ 11,12,13,14;\ 17,18,19,20;\ 22,23,24,25;\ 27,28,29,30$. Grouped in that order they are
\[
\begin{array}{rrrr}
1234&2341&3412&4123\\
2314&3142&1423&4231\\
3124&1243&2431&4312\\
2134&1342&3421&4213\\
1324&3241&2413&4132\\
3214&2143&1432&4321
\end{array}
\]
These are $24$ distinct permutations, so all are present. A length-$L$ string has only $L-3$ four-character windows, giving the elementary lower bound $\boxed{L\ge27}$. This bound alone does not establish optimality of the displayed length-$33$ construction.
