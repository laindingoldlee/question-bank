The quantity $y$ is jointly proportional to the $100$ numbers $x_1,x_2,\dots,x_{100}$; that is, $y=k\,x_1x_2\cdots x_{100}$ for a nonzero constant $k$. Jiri multiplies $25$ of the $x_i$ by $3$. By what positive number must Vlatko multiply each of the other $75$ numbers $x_i$ so that $y$ is unchanged?
