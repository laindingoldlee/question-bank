Tripling $25$ of the factors multiplies $y$ by $3^{25}$. Multiplying each of the other $75$ factors by $m$ multiplies $y$ by $m^{75}$. For $y$ to be unchanged we need
\[3^{25}m^{75}=1,\qquad m^{75}=3^{-25},\qquad m^{3}=3^{-1},\]
so
\[m=\boxed{3^{-\dfrac{1}{3}}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt[3]3}}\approx0.693 .\]
(The reading $\dfrac{y}{x_1x_2\cdots x_{100}}=k$ in the post is the right meaning of ``jointly proportional'', but the multiplier is not $3^{-25}$: that would be the factor for a single one of the remaining numbers, not for each of the $75$.)
