Since $7\equiv3\pmod4$, the condition is $x\equiv3\pmod4$. The integers greater than $1$ of this form are
\[
3,7,11,15,19,23,27,\dots,
\]
so $a_1=3$, $d=4$ and $a_n=3+4(n-1)$. Then $a_4=15$, $a_7=27$, and
\[
a_1+a_4+a_7+d=3+15+27+4=\boxed{49}.
\]
