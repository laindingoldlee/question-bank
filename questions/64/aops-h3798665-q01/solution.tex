Non-one digits must multiply to $20=2^2\cdot5$. The only possibilities are the multisets $\{2,2,5\}$ and $\{4,5\}$. Each has digit sum nine, so exactly fourteen ones must be added. The first multiset gives $\dfrac{17!}{14!2!}=2040$ arrangements; the second gives $\dfrac{16!}{14!}=240$. No zero can occur. Thus the answer is $\boxed{2280}$.
