There are $\dbinom{10}{3}=120$ equally likely groups, of which $\dbinom73=35$ consist of men only. So the probability of at least one woman is
\[1-\dfrac{35}{120}=\dfrac{85}{120}=\boxed{\dfrac{17}{24}}.\]
