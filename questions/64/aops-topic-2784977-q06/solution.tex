Reflect $\dfrac{1}{5}$ across $\dfrac{1}{6}$: the other coordinate is $2\cdot\dfrac{1}{6}-\dfrac{1}{5}=\dfrac{1}{3}-\dfrac{1}{5}=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{15}}$.

\end{asy}
