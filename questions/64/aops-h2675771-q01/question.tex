Let $a,b,c,d$ be consecutive terms of a real geometric progression. Numbers $u,v,w$ satisfy
\[
u+2v+3w=6,\qquad4u+5v+6w=12,\qquad6u+9v=4.
\]
Prove that the roots of
\[
\left(\dfrac{1}{u}+\dfrac{1}{v}+\dfrac{1}{w}\right)x^2+
\bigl((b-c)^2+(c-a)^2+(d-b)^2\bigr)x+u+v+w=0
\]
are the reciprocals of the roots of $20x^2+10(a-d)^2x-9=0$.
