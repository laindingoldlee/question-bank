Solving the linear system gives $(u,v,w)=(-\dfrac{1}{3},\dfrac{2}{3},\dfrac{5}{3})$. Hence $u+v+w=2$ and $\dfrac{1}{u}+\dfrac{1}{v}+\dfrac{1}{w}=-\dfrac{9}{10}$.

Write $(a,b,c,d)=(t,tq,tq^2,tq^3)$. Factoring gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
(b-c)^2+(c-a)^2+(d-b)^2
&=t^2(q-1)^2\bigl(q^2+(q+1)^2+q^2(q+1)^2\bigr)\\
&=t^2(q-1)^2(q^2+q+1)^2=(a-d)^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
Thus the first quadratic, multiplied by ten, is $-9x^2+10(a-d)^2x+20=0$. Its roots are nonzero. Substituting $x=\dfrac{1}{z}$ and multiplying by $z^2$ gives exactly $20z^2+10(a-d)^2z-9=0$, proving the reciprocal-root claim.
