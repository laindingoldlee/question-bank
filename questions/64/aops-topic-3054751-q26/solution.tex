Substitution gives $a^2=10(c+1)$. Hence 5 divides $a$ and $a$ is even, so $a=10k$ for a positive integer $k$. Then $c=10k^2-1$ and $b=10k^2+1$. Since $a>35$, $k\ge4$, and all three positive factors increase with $k$. At $k=4$ the scores are 40,161,159, all admissible. The minimum product is $\boxed{40\cdot161\cdot159=1023960}$.
