Stanley and Andrew decide to meet between 2:00 pm and 3:00 pm to play rock-paper-scissors. Each arrives at a random time in that hour, uniformly and independently of the other. The first to arrive waits $15$ minutes and then leaves if his opponent has not appeared (no one stays after 3:00 pm). What is the probability that the match takes place?
