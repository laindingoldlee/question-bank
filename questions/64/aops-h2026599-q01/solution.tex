Scale to $A=(0,0),B=(1,0),C=(u,v)$ with $v>0$. Write $D=tC$ and $E=B+s(C-B)$, where $0<t,s<1$, and put
\[
U=u^2+v^2,\qquad V=(u-1)^2+v^2.
\]
The power of $C$ in the circle $ABED$ gives $(1-t)U=(1-s)V$.

Let the coordinates of $X,Y$ on $AB$ be $x,y$. Their circle meets $AC$ at $D,C$ and $BC$ at $E,C$. Taking powers of $A,B$ gives
\[
xy=tU,\qquad(1-x)(1-y)=sV.
\]
Subtracting yields
\[
x+y=1+tU-sV=1+U-V=2u.
\]
Thus the midpoint of $XY$ is $(u,0)$, precisely the perpendicular projection of $C=(u,v)$ onto $AB$.
