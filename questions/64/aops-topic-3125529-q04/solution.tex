The possible lengths are $2,4,6$. For a palindrome to be even, its first and last digit must be one of $2,4,6,8$. All other digits in its first half are arbitrary and determine the second half. The counts are $4,4\cdot10,4\cdot10^2$, respectively, totaling $\boxed{444}$.
