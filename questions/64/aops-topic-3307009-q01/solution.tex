Write the terms as $4+5j$, $0\le j\le4$. Three-element sums are $12+5(i+j+k)$. The index sums attain every integer from $3$ to $9$, witnessed by triples $012,013,014,024,034,134,234$. Thus the values are $27,32,37,42,47,52,57$, giving $\boxed7$.
