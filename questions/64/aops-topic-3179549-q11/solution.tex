Initially the attempts and successes are $20t$ and $13t$ for a positive integer $t$. With $k$ additional successes, $\dfrac{13t+k}{20t+k}=\dfrac{7}{10}$, yielding $3k=10t$. Thus $t$ is a positive multiple of $3$, and the smallest possible $k$ is $\boxed{10}$, attained at $t=3$.
