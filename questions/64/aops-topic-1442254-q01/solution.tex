Each question is correct with probability $\dfrac{1}{3}$, independently. Choosing which $7$ are correct,
\[
P=\dbinom{10}{7}\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^7\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^3=\dfrac{120\cdot8}{3^{10}}=\dfrac{960}{59049}=\boxed{\dfrac{320}{19683}}\approx0.0163 .
\]
