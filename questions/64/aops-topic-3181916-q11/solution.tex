The angle bisector theorem gives $BD:DC=4:7$, so in vector notation $D=\dfrac{7B+4C}{11}$. Since $AP:PD=3:1$, we have $P=\dfrac{A+3D}{4}=\dfrac{11A+21B+12C}{44}$. On line $BM$, the coefficients of $A,C$ have the same ratio as in $M$, namely 11:12. Thus $M=\dfrac{11A+12C}{23}$ and $AM:MC=12:11$, giving $\boxed{23}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(4,0),C=7*dir(60),D=(7*B+4*C)/11,M=(11*A+12*C)/23,P=(A+3*D)/4;draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--D^^B--M);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,S);label("$M$",M,NE);label("$P$",P,W);label("Schematic",(3,-1));
\end{asy}
