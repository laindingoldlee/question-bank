At a turkey-pardoning festival, $20$ turkeys are ranked with no ties, all rankings equally likely. The first-place turkey is pardoned for $3$ years, the second for $2$ years and the third for $1$ year; the others are not pardoned. Ella and Bella are two of the turkeys. What is the probability that both of them are free for at least the next two years?
