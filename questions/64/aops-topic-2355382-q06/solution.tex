Being free for at least two years means finishing first or second. So Ella and Bella must take the first two places, in either order. The pair of turkeys in the first two places is equally likely to be any of the $\dbinom{20}{2}=190$ pairs, so the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{190}}$. (Equivalently, $\dfrac{2}{20}\cdot\dfrac{1}{19}=\dfrac{1}{190}$.)
