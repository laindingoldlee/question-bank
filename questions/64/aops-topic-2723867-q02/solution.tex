With circumradius $R$, the chord from $A$ to the vertex $k$ steps away has length $2R\sin\dfrac{k\pi}7$. Using $\sum_{k=1}^{6}\sin\dfrac{k\pi}{7}=\cot\dfrac{\pi}{14}$,
\[AB+AC+\cdots+AG=2R\cot\dfrac\pi{14}=\dfrac{n\cot\dfrac\pi{14}}{\sin\dfrac\pi7},\]
since $n=2R\sin\dfrac\pi7$. So the sum equals $1$ when
\[n=\sin\dfrac\pi7\tan\dfrac\pi{14}=2\sin^2\dfrac\pi{14}=\boxed{1-\cos\dfrac\pi7}.\]
(Any positive multiple $m\left(1-\cos\dfrac\pi7\right)$ with $m$ a positive integer also works.)
