Let $ABCD$ be a rhombus with $\angle ABC$ obtuse, and let the circle with diameter $BD$ meet $AB$ and $BC$ again at $E$ and $F$, respectively. Given that $AE=1$ and $AF=2$, find the area of $ABCD$.
