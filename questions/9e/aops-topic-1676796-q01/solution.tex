She needs $2$, $3$ or $4$ heads. Out of the $2^4=16$ equally likely sequences, $\dbinom42+\dbinom43+\dbinom44=6+4+1=11$ qualify. (Complement: $0$ or $1$ head occurs in $1+4=5$ sequences.) The probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{11}{16}}$.
