Every row is the same, so the ratios for the whole garden equal those for one row: carrot to tomato is $\dfrac{10}{5}=2$, and tomato to spinach is $\dfrac54$. The positive difference is $2-\dfrac54=\dfrac34$, choice $\boxed{\textbf{(D)}\ \dfrac34}$.
