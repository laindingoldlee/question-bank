The initial factor $2^m$ is removed by successive halvings, leaving $2^d-1$ or $2^d+1$, where $d=n-m\ge1$. For $d\ge2$,
\[
(2^d-1)^2-1=2^{d+1}(2^{d-1}-1),
\]
\[
(2^d+1)^2-1=2^{d+1}(2^{d-1}+1).
\]
After the odd step and all resulting halvings, the exponent $d$ decreases by one. Eventually the minus case reaches $1$, which goes to $0$, and the plus case reaches $3$, which follows $3,8,4,2,1,0$.

Thus both signs terminate for every allowed pair. The count is $\boxed{\binom{11}{2}=55}$.
