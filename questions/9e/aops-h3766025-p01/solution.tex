Yes. Use the powers of $2$ modulo $11$ in order: $\boxed{1,2,4,8,5,10,9,7,3,6}$. These are all ten nonzero residues, and the next residue is again $1$. Every successive term is twice the previous one modulo $11$, including the wraparound. Therefore $b\equiv2a$ and $c\equiv2b\equiv4a$, yielding $b^2-ac\equiv4a^2-4a^2=0$.
