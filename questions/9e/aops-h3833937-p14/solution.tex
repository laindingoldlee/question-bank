For a positive base other than $1$, the exponent must be zero, giving $x=5,12$; the bases are nonzero there. Base $1$ gives $x^2+3x=0$, so $x=0,-3$. For a negative base to have a real power equal to $1$, its absolute value must be $1$. Base $-1$ gives $x=-1,-2$, with exponents $78,98$, both even integers. Base zero cannot give $1$. Thus the six solutions sum to $0-3+5+12-1-2=\boxed{11}$.
