The first congruence says $x=7k+3$ for some integer $k$. Substitute this into the second:
\[
7k+3\equiv2\pmod5\iff 2k\equiv-1\equiv4\pmod5\iff k\equiv2\pmod5 ,
\]
where we divided by $2$, which is allowed because $\gcd(2,5)=1$ (indeed $2\cdot3=6\equiv1$, so multiplying by $3$ undoes multiplication by $2$). So $k=5m+2$ and
\[
x=7(5m+2)+3=35m+17 .
\]
Check: $17=2\cdot7+3$ and $17=3\cdot5+2$. Conversely every $35m+17$ works, since adding a multiple of $35$ changes neither remainder. This is the Chinese remainder theorem in action: because $7$ and $5$ are coprime, the two conditions together are equivalent to one condition modulo $7\cdot5=35$.

The solution set is $\boxed{\{x: x\equiv17\pmod{35}\}=\{\dots,-18,17,52,87,\dots\}}$.
