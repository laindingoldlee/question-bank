Positive integers $a$ and $b$ satisfy
\[
a+b=\dfrac {a}{b}+\dfrac {b}{a} .
\]
Find $a^2+b^2$. Then show that if $a$ and $b$ are only required to be nonzero real numbers, the value of $a^2+b^2$ is not determined.
