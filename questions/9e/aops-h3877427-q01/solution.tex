Set $B=(0,0)$ and $R=(30,0)$. Then $M=(-10,10\sqrt3)$ and $X=(12,0)$. The equation of $MR$ is
\[
y=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}(30-x).
\]
Hence $Y=(12,9\dfrac{\sqrt 3}{2})$, giving $\boxed{XY=9\dfrac{\sqrt 3}{2}}$ metres. Also $MR=\sqrt{40^2+(10\sqrt3)^2}=10\sqrt{19}$. The horizontal displacement from $M$ to $Y$ is $22$ out of the total $40$, so
\[
\boxed{MY=\dfrac{22}{40}\cdot10\sqrt{19}=\dfrac{11\sqrt{19}}{2}}\text{ metres}.
\]
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair B=(0,0),R=(30,0),M=(-10,10sqrt(3)),X=(12,0),Y=(12,9sqrt(3)/2);draw(B--R--M--cycle);draw(X--Y,red);draw(X+(1,0)--X+(1,1)--X+(0,1));label("$B$",B,(0,-1));label("$R$",R,SE);label("$M$",M,NW);label("$X$",X,(0,-1));label("$Y$",Y,NE);
\end{asy}
