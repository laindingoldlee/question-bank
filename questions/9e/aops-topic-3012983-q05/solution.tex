The perpendicular from the centre bisects the chord. Its half-length is $ 7 $, so the required distance is $\sqrt{16^2-7^2}=\boxed{3\sqrt{23}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
real h=3*sqrt(23);pair O=(0,0),A=(-7,h),B=(7,h),M=(0,h);draw(circle(O,16));draw(A--B^^O--A^^O--M);label("$O$",O,S);label("$A$",A,NW);label("$B$",B,NE);label("$M$",M,N);label("$16$",A/2,W);label("$7$",(3.5,h),N);
\end{asy}
