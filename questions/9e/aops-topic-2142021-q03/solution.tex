Both $a$ and $b$ are positive, so $\sqrt a$ and $\sqrt b$ are real, and
\[\dfrac{a+b}{2}-\sqrt{ab}=\dfrac{\left(\sqrt a\right)^2-2\sqrt a\sqrt b+\left(\sqrt b\right)^2}{2}=\dfrac{\left(\sqrt a-\sqrt b\right)^2}{2}\ge0 .\]
So $\dfrac{a+b}{2}\ge\sqrt{ab}$; in fact the inequality is strict, because $a\ne b$ ($a$ is odd and $b$ is even). This is the AM--GM inequality for two numbers; the particular values of $a$ and $b$ play no role. $\blacksquare$
