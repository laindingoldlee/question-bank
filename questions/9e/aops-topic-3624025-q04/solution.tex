The first two conditions give $x=35k+26$: among $5,12,19,26,33$ modulo 35 only 26 is 1 modulo 5. The last condition is $x\equiv 2\pmod 3$, so $35k+26\equiv 2k+2\equiv 2\pmod 3$ and $k\equiv 0\pmod 3$. Thus $x\equiv 26\pmod{105}$, whose only representative from 1 through 100 is $\boxed{26}$.
