Per minute, Tobey mows $\dfrac{1}{50}$ of the lawn and together they mow $\dfrac{1}{30}$. So Nick mows $\dfrac{1}{30}-\dfrac{1}{50}=\dfrac{5-3}{150}=\dfrac{1}{75}$ per minute, and alone he needs $\boxed{75}$ minutes ($1$ hour $15$ minutes), agreeing with the poster's method.
