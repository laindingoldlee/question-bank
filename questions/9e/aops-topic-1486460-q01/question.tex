Let $x$ be a nonzero number and $t=x+\dfrac{1}{x}$. Prove that for every positive integer $n$, the number $x^n+\dfrac{1}{x^n}$ can be written as a polynomial in $t$ with integer coefficients (the same polynomial for all $x$).
