For every integer $d$, $0=d\cdot0$. So every integer divides $0$ under this definition, and $0$ has infinitely many divisors. (Many texts require $d\ne0$ for divisors, giving all nonzero integers.) Unlike every nonzero integer, $0$ has no largest divisor, so ``the factors of $0$'' are not a finite list.
