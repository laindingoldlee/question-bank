Let $p$ be the fraction of adults who are parents. Then $65\%$ of these parents are grandparents, so $0.65p=0.50$. Thus $p=\dfrac{10}{13}$, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{1000}{13}\%\approx76.923\%}$.
