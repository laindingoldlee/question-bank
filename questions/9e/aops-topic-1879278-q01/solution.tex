All denominators are nonzero, so $a,b\notin\{0,-1\}$. Multiply both sides by $ab(a+1)(b+1)$:
\[
b(a+1)+a(b+1)=ab\quad\Longrightarrow\quad ab+a+b=0 .
\]
Dividing by $ab$ gives $1+\dfrac{1}{b}+\dfrac{1}{a}=0$, so $\dfrac{1}{a}+\dfrac{1}{b}=\boxed{-1}$. (Such numbers exist, e.g.\ $a=1$, $b=-\dfrac{1}{2}$.)
