The area of a circle of radius $r$ is $\pi r^2$, so the area is $\pi\cdot37^2=\boxed{1369\pi}\approx4300.8$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(2.6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0); draw(circle(O,37)); draw(O--37*dir(30),blue); dot(O); label("$O$",O,S); label("$37$",18.5*dir(30),NW,blue);
\end{asy}
