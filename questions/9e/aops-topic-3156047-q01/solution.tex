AM--GM gives perimeter $ 2(a+b)\ge4\sqrt{ab}=16\sqrt2 $, lying strictly between $ 22 $ and $ 23 $. Thus any integer perimeter is at least $ 23 $. This is attainable: take $ a,b=\dfrac{23\pm\sqrt{17}}{4}$, whose sum is $\dfrac{23}{2}$ and product $ 32 $. Hence $\boxed{23\text{ cm}}$. 
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
real a=(23+sqrt(17))/4,b=(23-sqrt(17))/4;draw((0,0)--(a,0)--(a,b)--(0,b)--cycle);label("$a$",(a/2,0),S);label("$b$",(a,b/2),E);label("Area $32$",(a/2,b/2));
\end{asy}
