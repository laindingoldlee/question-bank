Choose $A=(0,0),B=(b,0),D=(0,d),C=(c,e)$ with $b,c,d,e>0$. Convexity places $C$ in the first quadrant. The midpoint is $M=\left(\dfrac{b+c}{2},\dfrac{e}{2}\right)$. Angle $BAM$ is acute; therefore the equal angle $ADC$ is acute and $e<d$. Taking tangents gives
\[
\dfrac{c}{d-e}=\dfrac{e}{b+c},\qquad c(b+c)+e^2=de.
\]
Since ray $AM$ has smaller slope than ray $AC$, their angle difference is acute. Its tangent is
\[
\tan\angle CAM
=\dfrac{\dfrac{e}{c}-\dfrac{e}{b+c}}{1+\dfrac{e^2}{c(b+c)}}
=\dfrac{eb}{c(b+c)+e^2}
=\dfrac{b}{d}.
\]
But $\tan\angle ADB=\dfrac{b}{d}$ as well. Both are acute, proving the desired equality.
