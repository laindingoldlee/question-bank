In triangle $ABD$, $E$ is the foot of the altitude from $B$ and $C$ is the foot of the altitude from $A$. Suppose $AC=BE$ and $DE=DC$. A worksheet asks you to prove that triangle $DEC$ is equilateral.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $AD=BD$ and that triangle $DEC$ is isosceles.
\item[(b)] Show that the claim ``$DEC$ is equilateral'' cannot be proved: give a triangle satisfying the hypotheses for which $DEC$ is not equilateral.
\end{enumerate}
