The digit $5$ appears, so $b\ge6$. Writing the numerals in powers of $b$,
\[
(4b^3+b^2+3)-(2b^3+3b^2+b+4)=b^3+3b^2+4b+5 ,
\]
which simplifies to $b^3-5b^2-5b-6=0$, i.e. $(b-6)(b^2+b+1)=0$. Since $b^2+b+1>0$, the base is $b=\boxed{6}$.

Check by column subtraction in base $6$: units $3-4$ needs a borrow, $9-4=5$ (as $3+6=9$); tens $0-1-1$ needs a borrow, $6-2=4$; hundreds $1-3-1$ needs a borrow, $7-4=3$; thousands $4-2-1=1$. The result is $1345_6$.
