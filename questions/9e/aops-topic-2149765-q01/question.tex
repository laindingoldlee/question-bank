$PS$ is a line segment of length $4$, and $O$ is its midpoint. A semicircular arc is drawn with $PS$ as diameter, and $X$ is the midpoint of this arc. Points $Q$ and $R$ on the arc $PXS$ are such that $QR$ is parallel to $PS$ and the semicircular arc drawn with $QR$ as diameter (on the side of $QR$ towards $PS$) is tangent to $PS$. Find the area of the region $QXROQ$ bounded by the two semicircular arcs.
