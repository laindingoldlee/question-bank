Let the wholesale price be $W$, so the old selling price is $1.6W$. Now the cost is $1.15W$ and the price $1.1\cdot1.6W=1.76W$. The profit rate is $\dfrac{1.76}{1.15}-1=\dfrac{61}{115}\approx\boxed{53.04\%}$.
