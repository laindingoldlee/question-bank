Suppose $f$ varies directly as $g$ and inversely as $h$ (that is, $f=\dfrac{kg}{h}$ for a constant $k$). If $g=24$ when $h=2$ and $f=6$, find $g$ when $f=18$ and $h=-3$.

A student objects: ``If $f$ varies inversely with $h$, then $fh$ should always be the same, so this makes no sense.'' Explain what is wrong with the objection.
