In a circle with centre $O$ and radius $r$, the arc $AB$ measures $60^\circ$. The two rays that trisect angle $AOB$ meet the circle at $C$ and $D$, and the chord $AB$ at $C'$ and $D'$, with $C$ and $C'$ on the same ray. The region bounded by arc $CD$ and the segments $C'D'$, $C'C$ and $D'D$ has area $xr^n$, where $x$ and $n$ are constants. Find $x$ and $n$, and then find $\dfrac{\lfloor100x\rfloor}{n}$.
