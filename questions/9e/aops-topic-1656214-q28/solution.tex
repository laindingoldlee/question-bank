The conditions say that within each of the three classes
\[
\{1,4,7,10,13,16\},\qquad\{2,5,8,11,14,17\},\qquad\{3,6,9,12,15,18\}
\]
the numbers appear in increasing order, and there is no condition between different classes. A permutation is therefore determined by choosing which $6$ of the $18$ positions hold the first class and which $6$ of the remaining $12$ hold the second:
\[
N=\dbinom{18}{6}\dbinom{12}{6}=18564\cdot924=17153136 .
\]
The remainder is $\boxed{\textbf{(f)}\ 136}$.
