\textbf{(a)} A square is $\equiv0$ or $1\pmod4$, so $a^2+b^2\equiv0,1$ or $2\pmod4$. But $10^{100}+3\equiv3\pmod4$. So no integers work. $\blacksquare$

\textbf{(b)} For example $a=10^{50}$, $b=\sqrt3$ (indeed any point on the circle of radius $\sqrt{10^{100}+3}$ works).
