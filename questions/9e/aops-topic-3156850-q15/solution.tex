Take $ B=(0,0),A=(4,0),C=(0,3)$. The hypotenuse is $ 3x+4y=12 $. Reflection gives $ B'=(\dfrac{72}{25},\dfrac{96}{25})$: it is twice the perpendicular projection $(\dfrac{36}{25},\dfrac{48}{25})$ of $ B $. The slope of $ AB'$ is $-\dfrac{24}{7}$, so $ D=(0,\dfrac{96}{7})$. Consequently $ AD=\sqrt{16+\dfrac{9216}{49}}=\boxed{\dfrac{100}{7}}$.
