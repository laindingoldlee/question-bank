Joseph lives $2$ miles from school. It took him $15$ minutes to bike from school to home. He biked the first half of the distance at a speed of $12$ miles per hour.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What was his speed for the remaining distance?
\item[(b)] What was his average speed for the whole trip?
\end{enumerate}
