\textbf{(a)} The first half of the distance is $1$ mile. At $12$ miles per hour it takes $\dfrac{1}{12}$ hour $=5$ minutes. So the second mile took $15-5=10$ minutes $=\dfrac{1}{6}$ hour, and his speed for it was
\[
\dfrac{1\text{ mile}}{\dfrac{1}{6}\text{ hour}}=\boxed{6\text{ miles per hour}}.
\]

\textbf{(b)} Average speed is total distance over total time:
\[
\dfrac{2\text{ miles}}{\dfrac{1}{4}\text{ hour}}=\boxed{8\text{ miles per hour}}.
\]
(Note that this is not the average $9$ of the two speeds $12$ and $6$: he spends more time at the slower speed.)
