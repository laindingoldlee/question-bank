Since $\dfrac1{(2k-1)(2k+1)}=\dfrac12\left(\dfrac1{2k-1}-\dfrac1{2k+1}\right)$, the sum telescopes:
\[\dfrac12\left(1-\dfrac1{2n+1}\right)=\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac{2n}{2n+1}=\dfrac{n}{2n+1}.\ \blacksquare\]
