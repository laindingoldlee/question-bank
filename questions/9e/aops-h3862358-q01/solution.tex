Define $F(t)=\sum_{j=0}^{99}a_{j+1}t^j$. Expanding $F(u-1)$, its coefficient of $u^r$ is precisely the left side of the given equation. Therefore
\[
F(u-1)=\sum_{r=0}^{99}(100-r)^2u^r,
\qquad
F(t)=\sum_{r=0}^{99}(100-r)^2(t+1)^r.
\]
Taking the coefficient of $t$ gives
\[
a_2=\sum_{r=1}^{99}r(100-r)^2
=10000\sum r-200\sum r^2+\sum r^3
=\dfrac{100^2(100^2-1)}{12}=8332500.
\]
Thus the requested remainder is $\boxed{500}$.
