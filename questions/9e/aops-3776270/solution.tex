For any four ordered digits $w<x<y<z$, exactly five permutations have the required alternating pattern: $wyxz,wzxy,xywz,xzwy,yzwx$. This may also be checked by placing the largest digit in position $2$ or $4$, yielding respectively three and two orders. With no zero, choose four of nine digits and use any of the five orders, giving $5\binom94=630$. With zero, choose the other three digits in $\binom93$ ways. Exactly two alternating orders start with the smallest digit, so three give valid four-digit numbers. Total: $630+3\binom93=\boxed{882}$.
