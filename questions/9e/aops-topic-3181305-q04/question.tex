One playlist contains 15 happy and 5 sad songs; a disjoint second playlist contains 10 happy and 10 sad songs. A uniformly selected song from their union is known to be sad. Write the probability it came from the first playlist as $\dfrac{p}{q}$ in lowest terms and find $p+q$.
