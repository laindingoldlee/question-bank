An odd integer is $2k+1$, and $(2k+1)^2=4k(k+1)+1\equiv1\pmod4$. So the sum of two odd squares is $\equiv2\pmod4$. But every perfect square is $\equiv0$ or $1\pmod4$ (even squares are multiples of $4$, odd squares are $\equiv1$). Hence the sum is not a perfect square. $\blacksquare$
