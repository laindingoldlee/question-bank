Count the integers without a $7$. Write $0,1,\dots,999$ as three-digit strings with leading zeros; those without a $7$ have $9$ choices for each digit, giving $9^3=729$ strings. Removing $000$ and adding $1000$ (which has no $7$), there are again $729$ integers from $1$ to $1000$ without a $7$. So
\[1000-729=\boxed{271}\]
contain at least one $7$.
