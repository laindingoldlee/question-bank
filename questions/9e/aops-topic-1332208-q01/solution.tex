By the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality in Engel form (Titu's lemma), $\sum\dfrac{x_i^2}{y_i}\ge\dfrac{(\sum x_i)^2}{\sum y_i}$ for positive $y_i$, so
\[
\dfrac{4^2}{a}+\dfrac{2^2}{b}+\dfrac{1^2}{c}+\dfrac{1^2}{d}\ge\dfrac{(4+2+1+1)^2}{a+b+c+d}=\dfrac{64}{a+b+c+d}.
\]
Equality holds when $\dfrac{4}{a}=\dfrac{2}{b}=\dfrac{1}{c}=\dfrac{1}{d}$, i.e. $a:b:c:d=4:2:1:1$. $\blacksquare$
