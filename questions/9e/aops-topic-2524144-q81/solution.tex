Count among the $6^3=216$ equally likely outcomes.
\begin{itemize}
\item Exactly one $3$: choose its die ($3$ ways), and the other two dice avoid $3$ ($5^2$ ways): $75$.
\item At least one $4$ and at least one $6$: by inclusion--exclusion, $216-2\cdot5^3+4^3=216-250+64=30$.
\item Both: the dice show $3$, $4$, $6$ in some order: $3!=6$.
\end{itemize}
So $75+30-6=99$ outcomes are favourable, and the probability is $\dfrac{99}{216}=\boxed{\dfrac{11}{24}}$.
