(a) After $k$ more wrong answers the score is $\dfrac{34}{53+k}$, and we need $\dfrac{34}{53+k}<\dfrac{1}{2}$, i.e. $68<53+k$, $k>15$. So $k=\boxed{16}$. (With $15$ the score is $\dfrac{34}{68}$, exactly one half.)

(b) After $m$ more right answers the score is $\dfrac{34+m}{53+m}$, and we need $\dfrac{34+m}{53+m}>\dfrac{3}{4}$, i.e. $136+4m>159+3m$, $m>23$. So $m=\boxed{24}$. (With $23$ the score is $\dfrac{57}{76}=\dfrac{3}{4}$ exactly.)
