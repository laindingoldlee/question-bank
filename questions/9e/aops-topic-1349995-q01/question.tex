On a practice topic I have answered $53$ problems and got $34$ of them right.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What is the smallest number of further problems I must get wrong in a row so that fewer than half of all my answers are right?
\item[(b)] Starting again from $34$ right out of $53$, what is the smallest number of further problems I must get right in a row so that more than $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of all my answers are right?
\end{enumerate}
