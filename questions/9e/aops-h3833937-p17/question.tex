At noon, an airplane takes off from point $A$, maintaining a $30^{\circ}$ angle with the ground and traveling north. One hour later, a train leaves point $A$ at the same speed and direction as the plane, but parallel to the ground. The time in hours after noon that it will take for the plane to be exactly above the train can be written as $a+b\sqrt{c}$, where $a,$ $b$, and $c$ are positive integers and $b\sqrt{c}$ is fully simplified. What is $a+b+c$?
