At time $t$ hours after noon, the plane has traveled horizontal distance $vt\cos30^\circ$, while the train has traveled $v(t-1)$. Equality gives $t\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}=t-1$, hence $t=\dfrac{2}{2-\sqrt3}=4+2\sqrt3$. Therefore $\boxed{a+b+c=9}$.
