In triangle $ABC$, $\angle A=65^\circ$ and $\angle B=80^\circ$, and $D$ is the incentre. The line $BD$ meets side $AC$ at $E$. A ray of light travels from $B$ to $E$ and is reflected by the side $AC$ (angle of incidence equals angle of reflection); it then hits side $BC$ at $F$, is reflected there and hits side $AB$ at $G$, is reflected there and hits side $AC$ at $H$. Let $x$ be the area of triangle $ABC$ and $y$ the area of quadrilateral $EFGH$. Find $\dfrac xy$, rounded to the nearest thousandth.
