Adding the three equations, $2(C+J+K)=1238+1572+1384=4194$, so $C+J+K=2097$. Hence
\[C=2097-1384=713,\qquad J=2097-1572=525,\qquad K=2097-1238=859 .\]
So $\boxed{\text{Karl}}$ weighs the most ($859$ pounds).
