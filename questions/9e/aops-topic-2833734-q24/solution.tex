The letters G,U,I,F each occur twice, and W,D once. Choose the two repeated letters whose copies will be adjacent in $\binom42=6 $ ways. Merge those two pairs into blocks, leaving eight objects, with two other letter types still repeated. There are $\dfrac{8!}{2!2!}=10080 $ arrangements. Subtract those in which either further pair is adjacent: $ 2\cdot\dfrac{7!}{2!}=5040 $. Add back those with both further pairs adjacent: $ 6!=720 $. Thus the count is $ 6(10080-5040+720)=\boxed{34560}$.
