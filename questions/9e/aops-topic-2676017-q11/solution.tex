Each block of six, $(6k+1)+(6k+2)+(6k+3)-(6k+4)-(6k+5)-(6k+6)$, equals $-9$. The numbers $1$ to $2016$ form $336$ blocks, giving $-3024$. Since $2017=6\cdot336+1$, the remaining terms are $+2017+2018+2019-2020=4034$. The total is $-3024+4034=\boxed{1010}$.
