The altitudes are $\dfrac{1200}{a},\dfrac{1200}{b},\dfrac{1200}{c}$. Their sum is $1200\cdot\dfrac{ab+bc+ca}{abc}=1200\cdot\dfrac{4700}{60000}=\boxed{94}$. The data are consistent: a right triangle with sides $30,40,50$ has area $600$, pairwise-product sum $4700$, and product $60000$.
