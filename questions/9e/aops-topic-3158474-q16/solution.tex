The identity $a^3+b^3+c^3-3abc=(a+b+c)(a^2+b^2+c^2-ab-bc-ca)$ gives the expression $3|abc|$. For positivity none of $a,b,c$ is zero. Two have the same sign; write their magnitudes as distinct positive integers $u,v$, so the third magnitude is $u+v$. Since $u,v$ are distinct, $uv(u+v)\ge1\cdot2\cdot3=6$. Equality occurs at $(1,2,-3)$, so the answer is $\boxed{18}$.
