I must fill $10$ slots on my hockey team: $2$ Center, $2$ Right, $2$ Left, $2$ D1 and $2$ D2. There are two tryouts with different people: $10$ people come to the first tryout and $8$ to the second. At each tryout I pick one Center, one Right, one Left, one D1 and one D2. In how many ways can I make my team?
