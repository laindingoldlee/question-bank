Ash's $15$ friends stand in five columns, with $n$ friends stacked in the $n$-th column ($n=1,2,3,4,5$). Each time Ash whacks a friend with his magic newspaper, that friend disappears and the rest of that column drop down one place. Ash can only whack the bottom person of a column. In how many different orders can he whack all $15$ friends?
