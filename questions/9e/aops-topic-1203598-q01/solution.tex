Expanding the product gives
\[\left(bc-a^2\right)\left(ca-b^2\right)\left(ab-c^2\right)=\left(a^3b^3+b^3c^3+c^3a^3\right)-abc\left(a^3+b^3+c^3\right)\]
(check with $a=0$: both sides equal $b^3c^3$). The numbers $ab,bc,ca$ have sum $q$, pairwise-product sum $abc(a+b+c)=pr$ and product $r^2$, so by the identity $x^3+y^3+z^3=e_1^3-3e_1e_2+3e_3$,
\[a^3b^3+b^3c^3+c^3a^3=q^3-3pqr+3r^2,\qquad a^3+b^3+c^3=p^3-3pq+3r.\]
Therefore the expression equals
\[q^3-3pqr+3r^2-r\left(p^3-3pq+3r\right)=\boxed{q^3-p^3r}.\]
