An equilateral paper triangle $ABC$ has side 12. On each side mark points 3 units from its endpoints: $A_B,B_A$ on $AB$, $B_C,C_B$ on $BC$, and $C_A,A_C$ on $CA$. Let $A_1=A_CB_C\cap A_BC_B$, and define $B_1,C_1$ cyclically. Remove the corner rhombi $AA_BA_1A_C$, $BB_CB_1B_A$, $CC_AC_1C_B$, then fold along the sides of $A_1B_1C_1$ to form a tray with its three side panels meeting along their exposed edges. Find the tray volume.
