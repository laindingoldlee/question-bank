Use $A=(0,0),B=(12,0),C=(6,6\sqrt3)$. Then $A_1=(\dfrac{9}{2},\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2})$, $B_1=(\dfrac{15}{2},\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2})$, $C_1=(6,3\sqrt3)$. Thus the central equilateral base has side 3. Each remaining side panel is an isosceles trapezoid with parallel edges 3 and 6 and slant height $\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}$. Folding the three panels together yields a right regular triangular frustum with top side 6. Its two base apothems differ by $\dfrac{(6-3)\sqrt3}{6}=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$. Hence the vertical height is $h=\sqrt{(\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2})^2-(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2})^2}=\sqrt6$. The parallel areas are $A_b=\dfrac{9\sqrt3}{4}$ and $A_t=9\sqrt3$. The frustum formula gives $V=\dfrac{h}{3}(A_b+A_t+\sqrt{A_bA_t})=\dfrac{\sqrt6}{3}\cdot\dfrac{63\sqrt3}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{63\sqrt2}{4}}$.
