A bishop on the square in row $i$, column $j$ ($1\le i,j\le8$) reaches $\min(i,j)-1+\min(i,9-j)-1+\min(9-i,j)-1+\min(9-i,9-j)-1$ squares. Going from the edge inward, the squares form rings: the outer ring gives $7$, the next $9$, then $11$, and the central $2\times2$ gives $13$ (e.g. corner $7$, square $(2,2)$: $1+1+1+6=9$, $(3,3)$: $2+2+2+5=11$, $(4,4)$: $3+3+3+4=13$). Every square in the same ring has the same count, so $n\in\{7,9,11,13\}$: $\boxed{4}$ values.
