The first $65$ km take $\dfrac{65}{13}=5$ hours. An average of $19$ km per hour over $75$ km would mean a total time of $\dfrac{75}{19}$ hours, which is less than $4$ hours. But $5$ hours have already been used. So no speed works. $\blacksquare$
