The base angles are $\angle BAC=\angle BCA=\dfrac{180^\circ-36^\circ}2=72^\circ$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=dir(90),A=dir(90+144),C=dir(90-144),O=(0,0);draw(circle(O,1));draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(B--O--C,dashed);dot(O);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,N);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$O$",O,W);label("$36^\circ$",B+0.3*dir(-120),W,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}

$\angle BAC$ is an inscribed angle subtending arc $BC$ (the arc not containing $A$), so the central angle is $\angle BOC=2\cdot72^\circ=\boxed{144^\circ}$.
