$I$ is the incentre. Let $r$ be the inradius and $h$ the altitude from $A$ to $BC$. The line $KL$ is parallel to $BC$ at distance $r$ from it.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0),C=(8,0),A=(-5,sqrt(56));
pair I=(8*A+15*B+9*C)/32;real r=I.y;
pair K=B+(r/A.y)*(A-B),L=C+(r/A.y)*(A-C);
draw(circle(I,r),gray+dashed);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(K--L,blue+linewidth(0.9));
draw(B--I,red+dashed);draw(C--I,red+dashed);
dot(I);
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$K$",K,W);label("$L$",L,NE);label("$I$",I,N);
label("$9$",(A+B)/2,SW);label("$15$",A+0.3*(C-A),NE);label("$8$",(B+C)/2,S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Triangle $AKL$ is similar to triangle $ABC$ (since $KL\parallel BC$), with ratio $\dfrac{h-r}{h}$, so $AK=\dfrac{h-r}{h}\,AB$ and
\[
KB=AB-AK=\dfrac {r}{h}\cdot AB .
\]
The area of $ABC$ is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot BC\cdot h$ and also $r\cdot s$, where $s=\dfrac{9+15+8}{2}=16$ is the semiperimeter. Hence $\dfrac {r}{h}=\dfrac{BC}{2s}=\dfrac{8}{32}=\dfrac{1}{4}$ and
\[
KB=\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot9=\boxed{\textbf{(D)}\ \dfrac{9}{4}}.
\]
(Another view: $\angle KIB=\angle IBC=\angle KBI$, so triangle $KBI$ is isosceles with $KB=KI$.)
