Bart thinks of a nonzero number $b$ and Lisa thinks of a nonzero number $\ell$. They compute the square of the sum of their numbers, subtract the square of Bart's number and the square of Lisa's number, and divide the result by the product of their two numbers. Show that the result does not depend on the numbers chosen, and find it.
