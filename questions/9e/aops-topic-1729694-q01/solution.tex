$N-1$ is divisible by $2$, $3$ and $4$, hence by $\operatorname{lcm}(2,3,4)=12$. So $N=12k+1$. The largest three-digit such number: $999=12\cdot83+3$, so $12\cdot83+1=\boxed{997}$.
