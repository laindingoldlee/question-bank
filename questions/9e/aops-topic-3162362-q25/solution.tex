The sum counts pairs of positive integers $(i,j)$ with $ ij\le n $. Swapping the coordinates pairs all unequal pairs, leaving one unpaired pair for each square $ i^2\le n $. Its parity is therefore that of $\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor $. The even values $ 2,4,\ldots,44 $ contribute intervals $ k^2\le n<(k+1)^2 $, each of length $ 2k+1 $, except the last ends at 2017. For $ k=2j $, $ j=1,\ldots,21 $, the total is $\sum(4j+1)=945 $. The last interval 1936 through 2017 contributes 82. Hence $\boxed{1027}$.
