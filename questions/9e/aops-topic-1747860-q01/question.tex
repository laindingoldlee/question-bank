In a hockey-themed board game, players start in the penalty box, and a player escapes from the penalty box by rolling the same number on both of two standard six-sided dice. Piper, Quincy and Riley take turns, in that order, each rolling the pair of dice on her turn; a player who has escaped no longer rolls. What is the probability that Piper is the last of the three to escape? Express your answer as a common fraction.
