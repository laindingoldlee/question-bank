Factor $2010=2\cdot3\cdot5\cdot67$, a product of four distinct primes.

\textbf{(a)} A triple of positive integers with product $2010$ is determined by deciding, for each of the four primes, which of $a$, $b$, $c$ it divides (each prime appears exactly once in the product). That gives $3^4=\boxed{81}$ triples.

\textbf{(b)} The absolute values $(|a|,|b|,|c|)$ form one of the $81$ positive triples. The product is positive, so an even number of $a,b,c$ are negative: none, or one of the $\dbinom32=3$ pairs. That is $4$ sign patterns, so there are $81\cdot4=\boxed{324}$ triples.
