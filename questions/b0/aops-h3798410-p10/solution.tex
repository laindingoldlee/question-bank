(a) An occupied seat can cover at most itself and its two neighbours. To cover all 120 positions requires at least $\dfrac{120}{3}=40$ occupied seats. Occupying seats $2,5,8,\ldots,119$ attains this, since every empty seat then neighbours an occupied one.
(b) With 118 occupied seats, the two empty seats might be positions one and two; a person choosing seat one would have no occupied neighbour. With 119 occupied, the sole empty seat necessarily has an occupied neighbour. Thus the answers are $\boxed{40\text{ and }119}$.
