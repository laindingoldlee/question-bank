Set A at zero. Then $B=8x,C=12x,M=6x,E=15x$. Since $CE=3CD$, $CD=x$. The two possible positions for P are $14x$ and $16x$, so $112=14x$ or $16x$, yielding $x=8$ or seven. Hence the sum of the two possible CD values is $\boxed{15}$.
