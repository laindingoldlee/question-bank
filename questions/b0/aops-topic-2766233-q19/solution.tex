Expanding the prescribed factors gives coefficient $-2(a+b+c)$ on $x^2$ and $-8\sqrt{abc}$ on $x$. Thus $a+b+c=11$ and $abc=36$. The only ordered positive triple with these properties is $(2,3,6)$: checking $a\le\sqrt[3]{36}<4$ leaves $a=1,2,3$, and the required remaining sums and products yield only $a=2,b=3,c=6$. Its constant term $a^2+b^2+c^2-2(ab+bc+ca)=49-72=-23$ also agrees. Hence $\boxed{30}$.
