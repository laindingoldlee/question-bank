A small shed consists of a cube with surface area $648$ square feet and, on top of it, a roof in the shape of a triangular prism lying on one of its rectangular faces. That face coincides with the top face of the cube, and the cross-section of the roof is an isosceles triangle whose base is an edge of the cube and whose height is $9$ feet. The shed is painted entirely beige except for its bottom and four non-overlapping square windows of side $2$ feet, each lying entirely in a side face of the cube. The paint is applied $\dfrac14$ inch thick and comes in cans of $2$ cubic feet. How many cans are needed?
