Multiply by $ab$: $a^2b+b^2+ac=2024ab$. Since $\gcd(b,c)=1$, divisibility by $b$ forces $b\mid a$; write $a=kb$. Then $b(2024k-k^2b-1)=kc$. The parenthesised factor is coprime to $k$, so $k\mid b$; write $b=kt,a=k^2t$. We get $c=t(2024k-k^3t-1)$ and $\gcd(b,c)=t$, hence $t=1$. Therefore $a=k^2,b=k,c=k(2024-k^2)-1$. Positivity requires $k^2<2024$, so $k\le44$. The largest $a+b$ is $44^2+44=\boxed{1980}$, with $c=3871$ and gcd one.
