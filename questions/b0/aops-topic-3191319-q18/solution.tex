Total area is $320$, requiring at least $\dfrac{320}{5}=64$ pieces. Each break increases the piece count by one, so at least $63$ breaks are needed. Cut into $16$ strips of size $1\times20$, then split each strip into four $1\times5$ pieces. This uses $15+16\cdot3=63$ breaks, attaining the bound. The answer is $\boxed{63}$.

\end{asy}
