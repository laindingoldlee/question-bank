Taking reciprocals gives $a+\dfrac{1}{b+\dfrac{1}{c}}=\dfrac{8}{3}$. The fractional term is positive and at most one, so $a=2$. It follows that $b+\dfrac{1}{c}=\dfrac{3}{2}$. Since $b$ is a positive integer and $0<\dfrac{1}{c}\le1$, we obtain $b=1,c=2$. Therefore $\boxed{abc=4}$.
