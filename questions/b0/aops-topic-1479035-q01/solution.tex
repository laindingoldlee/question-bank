If the width $w$ and length $\ell$ become $w\left(1-\dfrac{p}{100}\right)$ and $\ell\left(1+\dfrac{p}{100}\right)$, the new area is
\[
w\ell\left(1-\dfrac{p}{100}\right)\left(1+\dfrac{p}{100}\right)=w\ell\left(1-\dfrac{p^2}{10000}\right).
\]
So the area decreases by $\dfrac{p^2}{10000}$ of itself, that is, by $\dfrac{p^2}{100}$ percent. We need
\[
2<\dfrac{p^2}{100}<3\qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad 200<p^2<300 .
\]
Since $14^2=196$, $15^2=225$, $16^2=256$, $17^2=289$ and $18^2=324$, the possible values are $p=\boxed{15,\ 16,\ 17}$, giving area decreases of $2.25\%$, $2.56\%$ and $2.89\%$.
