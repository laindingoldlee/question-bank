Put $u=a+1$ and $v=b+1$. The first equation says $uv=270$. The second gives $uc=243-v$, hence $u\mid243-v$. Substituting $c=\dfrac{243-v}{u}$ in the third gives $v(243-v)+u(u-1)=284u$. Equivalently $u^2-285u+243v-v^2=0$. Testing the positive divisors $u$ of 270 with $u,v\ge2$, $v=\dfrac{270}{u}$, leaves only $(u,v)=(15,18)$; this can be checked over $u=2,3,5,6,9,10,15,18,27,30,45,54,90,135$. It yields $(a,b,c)=(14,17,15)$, directly satisfying all equations. Therefore $\boxed{a+b+c=46}$.
