The criteria are AA (two corresponding angle pairs equal), SAS (two pairs of corresponding sides proportional and their included angles equal), and SSS (all three corresponding side pairs proportional). The third angle in AA is forced by the angle sum; the side criteria determine the same shape up to scale. 
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(220);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(2,0),C=(.5,1.5),v=(4,0);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(v--v+1.5*B--v+1.5*C--cycle);label("$a$",(B+C)/2,(1,0));label("$ka$",v+1.5*(B+C)/2,(1,0));
\end{asy}
