We have $x\equiv1\pmod7$ and $y\equiv2\pmod7$, and congruences can be added: $x+y\equiv1+2=3\pmod7$. Since $0\le3<7$, the remainder is $\boxed{3}$. (In other words, $x=7a+1$ and $y=7b+2$ give $x+y=7(a+b)+3$.)
