At a conference, six people place their name badges in a hat, which is shaken; one badge is then given to each person, every distribution being equally likely. Each turn, every person who does not yet have their own badge finds the person whose badge they have and takes that person's badge (all at the same time). For example, if Alice has Bob's badge and Bob has Charlie's badge, Alice has Charlie's badge after one turn. Compute the probability that everyone eventually ends up with their own badge.
