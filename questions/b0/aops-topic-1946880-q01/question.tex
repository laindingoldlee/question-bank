A shopper bought only apples, bananas and pears, at least one fruit of each kind: five pieces of fruit in all, including exactly one banana, for a total of $2$ euros $75$ cents. Apples cost $50$ cents each, pears $60$ cents each, and a banana costs a whole number of cents less than a pear. How many cents could the banana cost? Give all possibilities.
