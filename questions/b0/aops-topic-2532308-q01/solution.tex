Every prime factor of $n$ divides the product, so $n=2^a3^b$ with $a,b\ge1$ (both primes do occur). Let $\tau=(a+1)(b+1)$ be the number of divisors. Pairing each divisor $d$ with $\dfrac nd$ shows that the product of the divisors is $n^{\dfrac{\tau}{2}}$. Comparing exponents,
\[\dfrac{a\tau}{2}=75,\qquad\dfrac{b\tau}{2}=60,\]
so $\dfrac ab=\dfrac{75}{60}=\dfrac54$ and $a=5k$, $b=4k$ for a positive integer $k$. Then
\[\dfrac{5k(5k+1)(4k+1)}{2}=75\iff k(5k+1)(4k+1)=30.\]
$k=1$ gives $1\cdot6\cdot5=30$, and $k\ge2$ gives at least $2\cdot11\cdot9>30$. So $a=5$, $b=4$ and
\[n=2^5\cdot3^4=\boxed{2592}.\]
