Until 12:40 Jerry runs at $9-1.5=7.5$ mph for $2\dfrac{2}{3}$ hours, covering $20$ miles. Tom runs at $10.5$ mph for $1\dfrac{1}{3}$ hours, covering $14$ miles, so he has not caught up and is $6$ miles behind. After 12:40 Jerry runs at $9$ mph and Tom at $11$ mph, so Tom gains $2$ mph and catches up after $3$ hours, at $\boxed{\text{3:40 P.M.}}$
