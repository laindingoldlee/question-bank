Jerry steals a heavy Christmas present from Tom and runs away at 10:00 A.M. Jerry's normal speed is $9$ miles per hour, but the present slows him down by $1.5$ miles per hour. Tom runs at $10.5$ miles per hour and starts chasing Jerry along the same path at 11:20 A.M. At 12:40 P.M.\ Jerry drops the present but keeps running, and at the same moment Tom puts on super shoes that increase his speed by $0.5$ miles per hour. At what time does Tom catch Jerry?
