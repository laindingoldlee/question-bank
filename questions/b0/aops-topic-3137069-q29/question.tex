An isosceles trapezoid has legs of length 1. Its diagonals divide each other in ratio $ 2:1 $, but either base may be the longer. Fix the length $ L $ of the base named $ AB $. Find the smallest $ L $ for which there is exactly one nondegenerate trapezoid up to congruence.
