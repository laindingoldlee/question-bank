Triangle $ABC$ has side lengths $a=BC$, $b=CA$, $c=AB$ and area $T$, and let $x>0$. On each of the three lines $AB$, $BC$, $CA$, mark the two points that lie outside the side, at distance $x$ beyond each of its endpoints: on line $AB$ the points $A_1$ (beyond $A$) and $B_1$ (beyond $B$), on line $BC$ the points $B_2$ (beyond $B$) and $C_1$ (beyond $C$), and on line $CA$ the points $C_2$ (beyond $C$) and $A_2$ (beyond $A$). Find the area of the hexagon $A_1A_2B_2B_1C_2C_1$ in terms of $T$, $a$, $b$, $c$ and $x$.
