Let $k>0$. Show that the infinite nested radical $\sqrt{k+\sqrt{k+\sqrt{k+\cdots}}}$ (the limit of $c_1=\sqrt k$, $c_{n+1}=\sqrt{k+c_n}$) equals $\dfrac{1+\sqrt{1+4k}}{2}$.
