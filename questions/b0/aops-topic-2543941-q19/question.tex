In how many different ways can the digits $1,2,3,4,5,6$ be placed in the $2\times3$ array of boxes below, one digit per box, so that the numbers in each row decrease from left to right and the numbers in each column decrease from top to bottom?

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(2.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
for(int i=0;i<3;++i) for(int j=0;j<2;++j) draw(shift(i,j)*unitsquare);
\end{asy}
