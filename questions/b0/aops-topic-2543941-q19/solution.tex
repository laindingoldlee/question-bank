The $6$ must be in the top left box (it is larger than everything to its right and below), and the $1$ in the bottom right box. The other four digits $2,3,4,5$ fill the top middle $t_2$, top right $t_3$, bottom left $b_1$ and bottom middle $b_2$. The conditions left are $t_2>t_3$, $b_1>b_2$ and, in the middle column, $t_2>b_2$ (the others hold automatically).

Choose which two of $2,3,4,5$ go in the top row ($6$ ways); each row is then in decreasing order. Check $t_2>b_2$:
\[\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{top }(t_2,t_3)&\text{bottom }(b_1,b_2)&t_2>b_2?\\\hline
(5,4)&(3,2)&\text{yes}\\
(5,3)&(4,2)&\text{yes}\\
(5,2)&(4,3)&\text{yes}\\
(4,3)&(5,2)&\text{yes}\\
(4,2)&(5,3)&\text{yes}\\
(3,2)&(5,4)&\text{no}
\end{array}\]
So there are $\boxed{5}$ ways.
