In triangle $ABC$, $AB=8\sqrt{3}$, $AC=12-4\sqrt{3}$, and $\angle BAC=10^{\circ}$. Suppose point $D$ is on line $AB$ and point $E$ is on line $AC$. Let $m$ be the minimum possible value of $CD+DE+EB$. Compute $m^2$.

Note: $D$ is not necessarily between $A$ and $B$, and $E$ is not necessarily between $A$ and $C$.
