A hunting dog chases a rabbit at a constant speed of $v$ km per hour. When the chase begins the rabbit is $2$ km away, and the rabbit starts running directly away from the dog at $18$ km per hour at the same moment. Assuming $v>18$, how long does it take the dog to catch the rabbit? Give the answer for $v=20$, $v=24$ and $v=30$, and explain what happens if $v\le18$.
