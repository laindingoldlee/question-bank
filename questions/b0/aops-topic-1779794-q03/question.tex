A bowl contains $100$ g of salt water with a salt concentration of $20\%$. We take out $x$ g of the mixture and then add $x$ g of water. Then we again take out $x$ g of the (well-mixed) salt water and add $x$ g of water. Now the concentration is $5\%$. Find $x$.
