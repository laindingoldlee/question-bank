Suppose that a sphere is intersected by $3$ equally spaced parallel planes and that the cross sections of the sphere have areas $4\pi$, $9\pi$, and $6\pi$, respectively, with the plane that makes a cross section with area $9\pi$ in the middle of the other two. Then, if the surface area of the solid determined  by the sphere and the upper and lowermost planes is $(m+p\sqrt{n})\pi$, and $m$ and $n$ share no common factors, find $m + n + p$.
