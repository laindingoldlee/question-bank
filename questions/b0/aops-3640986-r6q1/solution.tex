Let the plane heights be t-d,t,t+d relative to the sphere centre, and let its radius be R. Subtracting the squared cross-section radii gives $d^2-2td=5$ and $d^2+2td=3$. Hence d=2,t=-$\dfrac{1}{4}$ and $R^2=9+\dfrac{1}{16}=\dfrac{145}{16}$. The slab height is four, so its curved area is $2\pi R\cdot4=2\pi\sqrt{145}$. Add its two flat ends of areas $4\pi$ and $6\pi$, giving $(10+2\sqrt{145})\pi$. Thus $m+n+p=\boxed{157}$.
