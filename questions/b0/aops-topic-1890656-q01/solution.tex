Place the square as $A=(0,0)$, $B=(3,0)$, $C=(3,3)$, $D=(0,3)$, and let $X=(x,y)$ with $0<x,y<3$. The segment $AX$ splits quadrilateral $ABXD$ into triangles $ABX$ and $AXD$ ($B$ and $D$ lie on opposite sides of line $AX$). Triangle $ABX$ has base $AB=3$ and height $y$; triangle $AXD$ has base $AD=3$ and height $x$. So
\[
[ABXD]=\dfrac{3}{2}y+\dfrac{3}{2}x=\dfrac{3}{2}(x+y),
\]
and $[ABXD]>4$ exactly when $x+y>\dfrac{8}{3}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(3,0),C=(3,3),D=(0,3),X=(2.2,1.8);
fill((8/3,0)--B--C--D--(0,8/3)--cycle,paleblue);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw((8/3,0)--(0,8/3),blue+linewidth(0.8));
draw(A--B--X--D--cycle,red+linewidth(0.9));
draw(A--X,red+dashed);
dot(X,red);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$X$",X,NE,red);
label("$x+y=\dfrac{8}{3}$",(1.05,1.62),SW,blue+fontsize(9pt));
label("$3$",(1.5,0),S);label("$3$",(0,1.5),W);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The part of the square with $x+y\le\dfrac{8}{3}$ is a right isosceles triangle with legs $\dfrac{8}{3}$, of area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{64}{9}=\dfrac{32}{9}$. The shaded part has area $9-\dfrac{32}{9}=\dfrac{49}{9}$, so the probability is
\[
\dfrac{\dfrac{49}{9}}{9}=\boxed{\dfrac{49}{81}} .
\]
