MISSISSIPPI has $11$ letters: one M, four I's, four S's and two P's. Arranging $11$ letters and dividing by the orders of the repeated letters,
\[
\dfrac{11!}{1!\,4!\,4!\,2!}=\dfrac{39916800}{1152}=\boxed{34650}.
\]
