The summand decomposes as $\dfrac{1}{n}+\dfrac{1}{n+1}+\dfrac{1}{n+2}$. It is therefore at least $\dfrac{1}{n}$, so its positive partial sums dominate the divergent harmonic partial sums. The series diverges to $\boxed{+\infty}$.
