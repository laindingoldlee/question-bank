Nine different sketchbooks are put on a shelf in a row, except that three of them are identical. The three identical ones must be next to each other. Two particular sketchbooks $X$ and $Y$ must have one or two sketchbooks between them (so they are not next to each other). Another particular sketchbook $Z$ must be at one of the two ends of the row. How many arrangements are possible?
