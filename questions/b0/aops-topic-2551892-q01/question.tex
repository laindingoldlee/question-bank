For every prime $p\ge7$, the decimal expansion of $\dfrac1p$ is purely repeating: $\dfrac1p=0.\overline{a_1a_2\cdots a_n}$, where $n$ is the length of the shortest repeating block. Let $N_p$ be the integer whose digits are $a_1a_2\cdots a_n$ (leading zeros allowed; for example $\dfrac1{11}=0.\overline{09}$ gives $N_{11}=9$). Find the greatest common divisor of all the numbers $N_p$ for primes $p\ge7$.
