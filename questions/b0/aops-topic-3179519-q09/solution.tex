Area bounds the number by $\left\lfloor\dfrac{28}{6}\right\rfloor=4$. Four prints fit by splitting the sheet into two strips of height $2$, each containing two width-$3$ prints. Thus the maximum is $\boxed4$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw((0,0)--(7,0)--(7,4)--(0,4)--cycle);for(int i=0;i<2;++i)for(int j=0;j<2;++j)draw((3*i,2*j)--(3*i+3,2*j)--(3*i+3,2*j+2)--(3*i,2*j+2)--cycle);label("$7$",(3.5,0),S);label("$4$",(0,2),W);label("$3\times2$",(1.5,1));
\end{asy}
