The amounts are proportional to rate $\times$ time: $5\cdot6:3\cdot8:2\cdot5=30:24:10$. These add up to $64$ parts, so one part is $\dfrac{320}{64}=5$ plates and Sam cooked $30\cdot5=\boxed{150}$ plates.
