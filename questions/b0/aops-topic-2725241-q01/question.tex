Sam, Tom and Una cooked $320$ plates of spaghetti in one day. Sam cooked for $6$ hours, Tom for $8$ hours and Una for $5$ hours, and their rates are in the ratio $5:3:2$ (Sam cooks $5$ plates for every $3$ by Tom and $2$ by Una in the same time). How many plates did Sam cook?
