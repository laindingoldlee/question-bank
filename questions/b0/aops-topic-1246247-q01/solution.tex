We need $s\mid360$, $s\ge3$, and $d(s)=d\left(\dfrac{360}{s}\right)$, where $360=2^3\cdot3^2\cdot5$. Write $s=2^a3^b5^c$; then $\dfrac{360}{s}=2^{3-a}3^{2-b}5^{1-c}$ and the condition is
\[(a+1)(b+1)(c+1)=(4-a)(3-b)(2-c).\]
Checking the $24$ divisors (the condition is symmetric under $s\leftrightarrow\dfrac{360}{s}$), the solutions are $s=18=2\cdot3^2$ and $s=20=2^2\cdot5$ (both have $6$ divisors). The answer is $18+20=\boxed{38}$.
