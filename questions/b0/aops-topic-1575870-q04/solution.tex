In $7$ hours the truck covered $7\cdot40=280$ miles, so the van covered $529-280=249$ miles in $7$ hours. Its average speed was
\[
\dfrac{249}{7}\text{ mph}=\boxed{35\dfrac{4}{7}\text{ mph}}\approx35.57\text{ mph}.
\]
