$(x-y)^2=x^2+y^2-2xy=8-8=0$, so $x=y$ and $x^2=4$. Thus $x=y=2$ or $x=y=-2$, and
\[\dfrac1x+\dfrac1y=\dfrac{x+y}{xy}=\dfrac{\pm4}{4}.\]
The possible values are $\boxed{1\text{ and }-1}$.
