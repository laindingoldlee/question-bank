A person initially knows the word exactly when their letter occurs in just one candidate word. LEEK has unique letters L,K; BEAN has B,N; HAMS has H,M; TACO has T,C. Each would make two people know, contrary to the condition. PEAS has no unique letter, so nobody would know. SOUP has precisely one unique letter, U, while S,O,P each appear elsewhere. Thus the only possible word is SOUP, and the required letter is $\boxed{U}$. The others can then also infer SOUP.
