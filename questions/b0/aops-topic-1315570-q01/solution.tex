A string looks the same after the reflection exactly when each letter does. Among $M,U,D,K,I,P$, the letters symmetric about a horizontal line are $D$, $K$ and $I$; $M$, $U$ and $P$ are not (they become $W$-like, $\cap$-like and $b$-like shapes). So the under-words are the strings over $\{D,K,I\}$, and there are
\[
3+3^2+3^3+3^4=3+9+27+81=\boxed{120}
\]
with at most $4$ letters.
