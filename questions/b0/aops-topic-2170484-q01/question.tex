Many numbers of the form $6n+1$, where $n$ is a positive integer, are prime: $7$, $13$, $19$, \dots
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the smallest positive integer $n$ for which $6n+1$ is not prime.
\item[(b)] Prove that $6n+1$ is composite for infinitely many positive integers $n$.
\item[(c)] Prove that every prime greater than $3$ is of the form $6n+1$ or $6n-1$ for some positive integer $n$, and use this to explain why primes are so common among the numbers $6n+1$.
\end{enumerate}
