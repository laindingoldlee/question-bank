If the integer roots are $r$ and $s$, then $x^2+bx+30=(x-r)(x-s)$, so by Vieta's formulas
\[
rs=30,\qquad r+s=-b .
\]
Since $b>0$, we need $r+s<0$; with $rs=30>0$ both roots are negative. Write $r=-p$, $s=-q$ with positive integers $p\le q$, $pq=30$, $b=p+q$. The factor pairs of $30$ give
\[
1+30=31,\qquad 2+15=17,\qquad 3+10=13,\qquad 5+6=11 .
\]
These are four different values of $b$, so there are $\boxed{4}$ possibilities.
