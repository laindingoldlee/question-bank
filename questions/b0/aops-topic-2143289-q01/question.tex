A student solved $n$ problems during a $31$-day period, solving a whole number of problems (possibly zero) each day. From day $1$ to day $14$ inclusive he solved an average of $5$ problems per day, and from day $12$ to day $31$ inclusive he solved an average of $3$ problems per day. Find the number of different values of $n$ for which this is possible.
