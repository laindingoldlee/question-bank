In a classroom, $9$ students are talking, $5$ are standing and $4$ are reading (a student may do more than one of these things, for example stand while reading or talking). Exactly one student is standing and not talking, and exactly one student is reading and not talking. Every student is talking, standing or reading. What is the smallest possible number of students in the room?
