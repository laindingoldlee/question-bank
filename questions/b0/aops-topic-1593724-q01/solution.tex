The real cube root is a one-to-one function, so the equation is equivalent to the equation obtained by cubing both sides (no extraneous roots can appear):
\[
x^3-x^2-10=(x-1)^3=x^3-3x^2+3x-1 .
\]
This simplifies to
\[
2x^2-3x-9=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad(2x+3)(x-3)=0,
\]
so $x=3$ or $x=-\dfrac{3}{2}$.

Check: for $x=3$, $\sqrt[3]{27-9-10}=\sqrt[3]{8}=2=3-1$. For $x=-\dfrac{3}{2}$, $x^3-x^2-10=-\dfrac{27}{8}-\dfrac{18}{8}-\dfrac{80}{8}=-\dfrac{125}{8}$, whose cube root is $-\dfrac{5}{2}=x-1$. Both work:
\[
\boxed{x=3\quad\text{or}\quad x=-\dfrac{3}{2}}.
\]
