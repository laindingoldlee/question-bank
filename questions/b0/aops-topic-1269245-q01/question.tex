The sides of a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ are extended: $A'$ lies on ray $AB$ beyond $B$ with $BA'=AB$, $B'$ on ray $BC$ beyond $C$ with $CB'=BC$, $C'$ on ray $CD$ beyond $D$ with $DC'=CD$, and $D'$ on ray $DA$ beyond $A$ with $AD'=DA$. If the area of $ABCD$ is $1$ square centimetre, find the area of quadrilateral $A'B'C'D'$.
