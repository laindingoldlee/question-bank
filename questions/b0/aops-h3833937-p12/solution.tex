A triangular layer with side count $k$ contains $\dfrac{k(k+1)}{2}$ balls. The first five layer sizes are $1,3,6,10,15$, totaling 35. Thus the bottom layer has fifteen balls and the second from bottom has $\boxed{10}$. 
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(220);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
for(int j=0;j<4;++j)for(int i=0;i<=j;++i)draw(circle((i-j/2,-j*.87),.45));label("second layer from bottom",(0,1),(0,1));
\end{asy}
