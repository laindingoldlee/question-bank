If she makes $k$ cookies, then $k$ must divide $80$, $112$ and $128$, so the largest possible $k$ is $\gcd(80,112,128)$. Since $80=2^4\cdot5$, $112=2^4\cdot7$ and $128=2^7$, the gcd is $2^4=16$. So she can make $\boxed{16}$ cookies, each with $5$ chocolate chips, $7$ pecans and $8$ M\&Ms.
