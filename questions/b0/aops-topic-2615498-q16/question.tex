Let $n$ be an odd positive integer, and let $y=-x^2-nx$ (so $x^2+nx+y=0$). The expression $\displaystyle\prod_{i=0}^{n}(x+i)^2$ can be written as $y^{n+1}+a_ny^n+a_{n-1}y^{n-1}+\cdots+a_2y^2$ with constants $a_i$. Prove that $a_n=\dfrac{n-n^3}{6}$ and $a_2=\bigl((n-1)!\bigr)^2$.
