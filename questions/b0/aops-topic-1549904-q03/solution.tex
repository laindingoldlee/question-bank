Number the chairs first. The two students take one of the $7$ pairs of adjacent chairs, in $2$ orders; the other three students then take $3$ of the remaining $5$ chairs in $5\cdot4\cdot3=60$ ways. That gives $7\cdot2\cdot60=840$ seatings. Each rotation class has $7$ elements, so the answer is $\dfrac{840}{7}=\boxed{120}$.

(Equivalently: rotate so that the pair occupies chairs $1$ and $2$; the $2$ orders and $60$ placements of the others give $120$.)
