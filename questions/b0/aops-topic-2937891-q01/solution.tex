Let $t_i=2^{2^{-i}}-1>0$. Since $2^{2^{-(i+1)}}=\sqrt{1+t_i}$,
\[t_{i+1}=\sqrt{1+t_i}-1=\dfrac{t_i}{\sqrt{1+t_i}+1}<\dfrac{t_i}2.\]
So $t_i\le2^{-i}$, with strict inequality for $i\ge1$, and $S<\sum2^{-i}=2$. By comparison the series converges. Also $e^u-1>u$ for $u>0$, so $t_i>2^{-i}\ln2$ and $S>2\ln2\approx1.386$. (Numerically $S\approx1.7818$.) $\blacksquare$
