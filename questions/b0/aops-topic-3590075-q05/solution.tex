The contribution is $(m+11)(n+9)+46$. Therefore both $m+11$ and $n+9$ divide $46$. Since they are at least $12$ and $10$, respectively, each belongs to $\{23,46\}$. The class-B contribution per student is $m+11+\dfrac{46}{n+9}$, so its possible values are $23+1,23+2,46+1,46+2$, namely $\boxed{24,25,47,48}$. Each pair of chosen divisors gives positive $m,n$, so all four occur.
