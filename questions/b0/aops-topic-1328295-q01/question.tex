Let $T_n=1+2+\dots+n$ be the $n$-th triangular number.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $\dbinom{n+1}{2}=\dbinom{n+1}{n-1}$.
\item[(b)] Prove algebraically that $T_n=\dbinom{n+1}{2}$.
\item[(c)] Give a counting (committee-forming) proof of $T_n=\dbinom{n+1}{2}$.
\end{enumerate}
