\textbf{(a)} There are $100\cdot100=10000$ equally likely ordered pairs (length, width), and the rectangle is a square for the $100$ pairs with equal entries. The probability is $\dfrac{100}{10000}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{100}}$.

\textbf{(b)} A shape corresponds to a pair $a\le b$ with $1\le a,b\le100$. There are $\dbinom{100}{2}=4950$ shapes with $a<b$ and $100$ squares, so $5050$ shapes in all, and the probability is $\dfrac{100}{5050}=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{101}}$.
