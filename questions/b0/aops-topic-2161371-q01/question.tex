A rectangle has whole-number side lengths, each between $1$ and $100$ inches inclusive.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] The length and the width are chosen independently and uniformly at random from $1,2,\dots,100$. What is the probability that the rectangle is a square?
\item[(b)] Suppose instead that each differently shaped rectangle is equally likely, where an $a\times b$ rectangle and a $b\times a$ rectangle count as the same shape. What is the probability that the rectangle is a square?
\end{enumerate}
