Since $14=2\cdot7$, the limiting prime is seven: its exponent in $2012!$ is $\lfloor\dfrac{2012}{7}\rfloor+\lfloor\dfrac{2012}{49}\rfloor+\lfloor\dfrac{2012}{343}\rfloor=287+41+5=333$. The exponent of two is at least $\lfloor\dfrac{2012}{2}\rfloor=1006>333$. Hence division by $14$ is integral $333$ times, and the first failure is division $\boxed{334}$.
