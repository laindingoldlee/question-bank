Let $S$ be a square and $T$ be an equilateral triangle. Suppose that

\[
[\text{area of }S]=[\text{perimeter of }T]
\]

and

\[
[\text{area of }T]=[\text{perimeter of }S].
\]

Here, all measurements are taken using the same base unit, and equality refers to their numerical values.

A rectangle is constructed whose side lengths are equal to the side lengths of $S$ and $T$, respectively.

1. Prove that the side lengths of $S$ and $T$ are uniquely determined.

2. Find the area of the rectangle.

Can you find the rectangle's area without first calculating either side length individually?
