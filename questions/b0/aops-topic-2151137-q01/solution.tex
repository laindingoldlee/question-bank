Collect terms: $3=4ix$, so
\[x=\dfrac{3}{4i}=\dfrac{3\cdot(-i)}{4i\cdot(-i)}=\boxed{-\dfrac{3i}{4}}.\]
Check: $ix=\dfrac{3}{4}$, and $2-\dfrac{3}{4}=\dfrac{5}{4}=3\cdot\dfrac{3}{4}-1$.
