For positive integers $x$ and $y$, let $\overline{xy}$ denote the number formed by writing the digits of $x$ followed by the digits of $y$ (for example, if $x=12$ and $y=345$, then $\overline{xy}=12345$). Find all pairs of positive integers $(x,y)$ such that
\[xy+x+y=\overline{xy}.\]
