If $y$ has $k$ digits, then $\overline{xy}=10^kx+y$. The equation becomes
\[xy+x+y=10^kx+y\iff x(y+1)=10^kx\iff y+1=10^k,\]
since $x>0$. So $y=10^k-1=99\cdots9$ ($k$ nines), which indeed has $k$ digits. Hence the solutions are
\[\boxed{(x,y)=(x,\ 9),\ (x,\ 99),\ (x,\ 999),\ \dots\quad\text{for every positive integer }x}.\]
For example $6\cdot9+6+9=69$ and $12\cdot99+12+99=1299$.
