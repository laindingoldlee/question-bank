Eliminate $c$: $\dfrac{\rho-\rho_1}{x_1}=\dfrac{\rho-\rho_2}{x_2}$ (if $x_1,x_2\ne0$; otherwise multiply out directly), so $x_2(\rho-\rho_1)=x_1(\rho-\rho_2)$. Then $\rho(x_2-x_1)=\rho_1x_2-\rho_2x_1$, and dividing by $x_2-x_1$ gives the formula. $\blacksquare$
