Two identical loaded dice each show $1,2,3,4,5,6$ with probabilities
\[\dfrac12,\quad\dfrac14,\quad\dfrac18,\quad\dfrac{1}{16},\quad\dfrac{1}{32},\quad\dfrac{1}{32},\]
respectively, independently of each other. The two dice are rolled.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What is the probability that each die shows a $7$?
\item[(b)] What is the probability of ``rolling a seven'', that is, that the two numbers add up to $7$?
\end{enumerate}
