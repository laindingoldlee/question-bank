\textbf{(a)} No face shows a $7$, so the probability is $\boxed{0}$ (this is the trick in the question).

\textbf{(b)} The total is $7$ for the ordered pairs $(1,6),(2,5),(3,4),(4,3),(5,2),(6,1)$. By independence,
\[P=2\left(\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac{1}{32}+\dfrac14\cdot\dfrac{1}{32}+\dfrac18\cdot\dfrac{1}{16}\right)=2\left(\dfrac{2}{128}+\dfrac{1}{128}+\dfrac{1}{128}\right)\]
\[=\dfrac{8}{128}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{16}}.\]
(The six probabilities add up to $1$, as they should.)
