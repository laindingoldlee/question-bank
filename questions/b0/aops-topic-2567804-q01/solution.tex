A palindrome is determined by its first half (including the middle digit for odd length), whose first digit is nonzero.
\begin{itemize}
\item $4$ digits, $\overline{abba}$: $9\cdot10=90$.
\item $5$ digits, $\overline{abcba}$: $9\cdot10\cdot10=900$.
\item $6$ digits, $\overline{abccba}$: $9\cdot10\cdot10=900$.
\end{itemize}
So there are $\boxed{90,\ 900\text{ and }900}$ of them ($1890$ in total).
