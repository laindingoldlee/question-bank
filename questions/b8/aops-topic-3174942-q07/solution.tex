Let $V=3\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{n^2}$ liters. Summing the first ten terms gives $3\sum_{n=1}^{10}\dfrac{1}{n^2}=\dfrac{1968329}{423360}$, approximately $4.649303$. The remaining sum is positive and at most $3\int_{10}^{\infty}\dfrac{dx}{x^2}=\dfrac{3}{10}$, because $x^{-2}$ decreases. Thus $4.62<V<4.949304<4.95$. Fourteen bottles provide only $4.62$ liters, whereas fifteen provide $4.95$ liters. Exactly fifteen bottles suffice, costing $\boxed{30}$ dollars.
