Three people each drink $\dfrac{1}{n^2}$ liters in minute $n$, for every positive integer $n$. Juice is bought in $330$-milliliter bottles costing $2$ dollars each, and unused portions may be shared and saved. What is the smallest total cost sufficient for all their drinking?
