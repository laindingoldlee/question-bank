The smallest three-digit multiple of $13$ is $104=13\cdot8$ and the largest is $988=13\cdot76$ (since $13\cdot77=1001$). So the multiples are $13k$ for $k=8,9,\dots,76$, and there are $76-8+1=\boxed{\textbf{(C) }69}$ of them.
