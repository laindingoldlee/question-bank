A player at $(0,0)$ throws a marker that flies in the direction of the vector $(3,4)$, toward a hidden target. The player then walks to $(100,0)$ and throws again; this time the marker flies in the direction $(-4,3)$. Both markers fly straight toward the target. Find the coordinates of the target and its distance from $(0,0)$.
