The first line is $y=\dfrac43x$. The second passes through $(100,0)$ with slope $-\dfrac34$: $y=-\dfrac34(x-100)$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((-10,0)--(110,0),Arrow);draw((0,-10)--(0,70),Arrow);pair T0=(36,48);
draw((0,0)--T0,dashed);draw((100,0)--T0,dashed);draw((0,0)--(12,16),Arrow);draw((100,0)--(88,9),Arrow);
dot((0,0));dot((100,0));dot(T0);label("$(0,0)$",(0,0),SW);label("$(100,0)$",(100,0),S);label("$(36,48)$",T0,N);
\end{asy}

Equating, $\dfrac43x=-\dfrac34x+75$, so $\dfrac{25}{12}x=75$ and $x=36$, $y=48$. The target is at $\boxed{(36,48)}$, at distance $\sqrt{36^2+48^2}=60$ from the origin. (The two directions are perpendicular, since $(3,4)\cdot(-4,3)=0$.)
