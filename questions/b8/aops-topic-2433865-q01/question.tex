What is $(-1)^{\dfrac{2}{3}}$?
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Evaluate it as a real number, using the real cube root ($x^{\dfrac{2}{3}}=\left(\sqrt[3]{x}\right)^2$).
\item[(b)] Find all complex numbers $w$ with $w^3=(-1)^2$, and identify the principal value $e^{\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot i\pi}$ of $(-1)^{\dfrac23}$ among them.
\end{enumerate}
