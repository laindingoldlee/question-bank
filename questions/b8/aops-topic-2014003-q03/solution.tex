Complete the square in $x$ and in $y$:
\[
\left(x^2-2x+1\right)+\left(y^2-16y+64\right)=149+1+64,
\qquad (x-1)^2+(y-8)^2=214 .
\]
This is the circle with centre $(1,8)$ and radius $\sqrt{214}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
xaxis("$x$",-16,20,Arrow(4)); yaxis("$y$",-9,27,Arrow(4));
pair Cc=(1,8); real r=sqrt(214);
draw(circle(Cc,r),blue+linewidth(0.9));
draw(Cc--(Cc+r*dir(30)),red);
dot(Cc); label("$(1,8)$",Cc,SE);
label("$\sqrt{214}$",Cc+0.55*r*dir(30),NW,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Its area is $\pi r^2=\boxed{214\pi}$.
