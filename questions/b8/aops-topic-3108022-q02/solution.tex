The numbers $ 1-3^{-n}$ terminate in base three, but their reduced denominator $ 3^n $ prevents termination in decimal, where reduced denominators may have only factors $ 2 $ and $ 5 $. They approach $ 1 $, which is ineligible because it terminates in both bases. Consequently the supremum is $\boxed1 $ and there is no greatest eligible number.
