Let the original distance of the base from the wall be $x$ feet and the ladder length be $L$ feet. The two right triangles give $L^2=x^2+24^2=(x+8)^2+20^2$. Subtraction yields $16x+64=176$, so $x=7$. Thus $L=\sqrt{7^2+24^2}=\boxed{25\text{ feet}}$.
