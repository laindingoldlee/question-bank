Winning all three has probability $0.84\cdot0.58\cdot0.88=0.428736$. Winning exactly two has probability
\[0.84\cdot0.58\cdot0.12+0.84\cdot0.42\cdot0.88+0.16\cdot0.58\cdot0.88=0.058464+0.310464+0.081664=0.450592.\]
