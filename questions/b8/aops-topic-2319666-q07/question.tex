In each round you roll one fair die and flip three fair coins. You repeat this until either the coins show three heads in two consecutive rounds, or the die shows a $6$ in two consecutive rounds. What is the probability that three heads in two consecutive rounds happens first (strictly before the die shows a $6$ in two consecutive rounds)?
