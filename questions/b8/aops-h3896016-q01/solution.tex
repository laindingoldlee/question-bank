The last term must be $1$ or $11$, since it is an extreme of the entire permutation. Removing it leaves a permutation with the same property on ten ordered numbers. Conversely, either extreme may be appended to any such shorter permutation. Thus the counts satisfy $a_n=2a_{n-1}$ and $a_1=1$, giving $a_{11}=2^{10}=1024$. Its digit sum is $\boxed7$.
