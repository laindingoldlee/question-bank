The one-digit pairs $(1,2),(3,4),(5,6),(7,8)$ give four. For larger pairs $(k,k+1)$, the increment must change every place; hence all digits of $k$ after its leading digit must be 9. The eligible two-digit-or-transition pairs have smaller number $9,19,\ldots,99$ (ten choices). The remaining eligible pairs have smaller number $199,299,\ldots,999$ (nine choices). Thus the answer is $4+10+9=\boxed{23}$.
