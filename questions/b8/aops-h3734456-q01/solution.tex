Let $L$ be the least common multiple. The four numbers $\dfrac{L}{a},\dfrac{L}{b},\dfrac{L}{c},\dfrac{L}{d}$ are distinct positive integers. In increasing order they are at least $1,2,3,4$. Consequently
\[
\dfrac{a+b+c+d}{L}\le1+\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{25}{12}.
\]
Equality occurs for $(a,b,c,d)=(12,6,4,3)$, whose least common multiple is $12$. Hence $\boxed{m+n=37}$.
