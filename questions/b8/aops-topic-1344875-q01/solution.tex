Let $\sqrt{k^2+323}=m$, a positive integer with $m>k$. Then
\[
m^2-k^2=323\quad\Longrightarrow\quad (m-k)(m+k)=323=17\cdot19 .
\]
The factors $m-k<m+k$ are positive with product $323$, so $(m-k,m+k)=(1,323)$ or $(17,19)$. These give $k=\dfrac{323-1}{2}=161$ and $k=\dfrac{19-17}{2}=1$. Check: $1+323=18^2$ and $161^2+323=25921+323=26244=162^2$.

The sum is $161+1=\boxed{162}$.
