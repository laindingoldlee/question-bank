Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle with $D$, $E$, $F$ the feet of the altitudes lying on $BC$, $CA$, $AB$, respectively. One of the intersection points of the line $EF$ and the circumcircle of $ABC$ is $P$. The lines $BP$ and $DF$ meet at the point $Q$. Prove that $AP=AQ$.
