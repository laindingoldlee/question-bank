Bob's claim is that at least $9$ of the $18$ bahbs are menaed. It is false exactly when at most $8$ are menaed, i.e. when at least $10$ of the $18$ are not menaed.

The bahbs that are shown include exactly $n-1$ that are not menaed; about the $18-n$ bahbs that are not shown nothing is known, so they might all be menaed.
\begin{itemize}
\item If $n\le10$, the shown bahbs contain at most $9$ that are not menaed. Then it is possible that all the unshown bahbs are menaed, so that exactly $18-(n-1)\ge9$ bahbs are menaed and Bob's claim is true. So $n\le10$ does not disprove the claim.
\item If $n=11$, Bob sees $10$ bahbs that are not menaed, so at most $8$ of the $18$ are menaed, fewer than half. The claim is false.
\end{itemize}
The least such $n$ is $\boxed{11}$.
