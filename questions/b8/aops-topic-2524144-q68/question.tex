Bob has a group of $18$ huelled bahbs, and he claims that at least half of the bahbs in this group are menaed. To prove him wrong, an audience member shows Bob $n$ of the $18$ bahbs. Bob can tell at a glance whether a bahb is menaed, and all but one of the $n$ bahbs shown are not menaed. What is the least $n$ for which this certainly proves Bob's claim false, whatever the bahbs that were not shown are like?
