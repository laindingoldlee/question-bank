If $xyz=0$ both sides are $0$. Otherwise divide by $xyz$: we need $x^4+y^4+z^4\ge x^2yz+xy^2z+xyz^2$. By AM-GM on four terms,
\[x^2yz=\sqrt[4]{x^4\cdot x^4\cdot y^4\cdot z^4}\le\dfrac{2x^4+y^4+z^4}4,\]
and similarly for the other two. Adding the three inequalities gives exactly $x^4+y^4+z^4$ on the right. $\blacksquare$
