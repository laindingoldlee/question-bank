An interior point equidistant from the two side lines lies on the internal angle bisector, so $BD:DC=13:14$. The area is the sum of triangles $ABD,ACD$, namely $\dfrac{1}{2}(13+14)\dfrac{56}{9}=84$. Thus $\sin A=\dfrac{2(84)}{13\cdot14}=\dfrac{12}{13}$, giving $\cos A=\pm\dfrac{5}{13}$. By the cosine rule, $BC^2=13^2+14^2-2(13)(14)\cos A$ is 225 or 505. Both angles are nondegenerate and yield the required distance. Therefore the largest $BD$ is $\boxed{\dfrac{13\sqrt{505}}{27}}$.
