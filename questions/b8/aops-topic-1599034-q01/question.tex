Three congruent semicircles of radius $1$ are drawn so that each two of them are externally tangent and their centres form an equilateral triangle; each semicircle bulges towards the centre of that triangle, with its diameter perpendicular to the line joining its centre to the centre of the triangle. A circle passes through the six endpoints of the three diameters. Find the area of the region inside this circle but outside the three semicircles. Express your answer as a common fraction in terms of $\pi$.
