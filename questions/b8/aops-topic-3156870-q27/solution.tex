Three gender changes mean four nonempty runs, alternating genders. There are two orders, GBGB or BGBG. Split the five girls into two positive run lengths in 4 ways and four boys into two positive run lengths in 3 ways. Then order the girls in $ 5!$ ways and boys in $ 4!$ ways within the run positions. The count is $\boxed{2\cdot4\cdot3\cdot5!4!=69120}$.
