Pair the first and last terms, the second and second-to-last, and so on: each of the $500\,000$ pairs sums to $1\,000\,001$. So the sum is
\[500\,000\cdot1\,000\,001=\boxed{500\,000\,500\,000}\]
(this is the formula $\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$ with $n=10^6$).
