The incircle of triangle $ABC$, with centre $I$, touches $BC$, $CA$, $AB$ at $M$, $N$, $P$ respectively. Let $E=BI\cap MN$, $F=CI\cap MP$, $S=AE\cap BC$ and $R=AF\cap BC$. Assume $BC$ is the longest side of the triangle.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that $\angle BEM=\dfrac{1}{2}\angle BAC$.
\item[(b)] Express the lengths $RS$ and $EF$ in terms of the side lengths $a=BC$, $b=CA$, $c=AB$.
\end{enumerate}
