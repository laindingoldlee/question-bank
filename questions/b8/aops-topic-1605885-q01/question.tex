The $96$ members of a club stand in a circle and count off $1,2,3,\dots,96$ in order around the circle. Every member who says an even number steps out of the circle. The remaining members continue counting around the circle, the next member (the one who said $1$) starting the second round with $97$, and again everyone who says an even number steps out. This continues until only one member is left. Which number did this member say in the first round?
