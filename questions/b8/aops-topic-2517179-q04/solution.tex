Let $x=3^{3^{333}}$. Then
\[3^{3^{334}}=3^{3\cdot3^{333}}=\left(3^{3^{333}}\right)^3=x^3,\]
and $x^3+1=(x+1)\left(x^2-x+1\right)$. So $x+1$ divides $x^3+1$, and the gcd is
\[\gcd\left(x+1,\ x^3+1\right)=x+1=\boxed{3^{3^{333}}+1}.\]
