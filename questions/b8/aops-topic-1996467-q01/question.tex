Without calculus, prove that a ball of radius $R$ has volume $\dfrac{4}{3}\pi R^3$. You may use Cavalieri's principle (two solids whose cross-sections by every horizontal plane have equal areas have equal volumes) and the formulas for the volumes of a cylinder and a cone.
