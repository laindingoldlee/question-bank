The factors of $n$ always include $n$ and, if $n>1$, also $1$. The sum is exactly $n+1$ when there are no other factors, that is, when $n$ is prime. For $n=1$ the sum is $1$, not $2$. The one-digit primes are $2,3,5,7$, so the answer is $\boxed{4}$.
