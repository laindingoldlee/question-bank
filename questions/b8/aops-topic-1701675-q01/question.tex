On a dark and stormy night Snoopy suddenly saw a flash of lightning. Ten seconds later he heard the sound of thunder. The speed of sound is $1088$ feet per second and one mile is $5280$ feet. Taking the speed of light to be $3\times10^8$ metres per second (and $1$ foot $=0.3048$ metres), how far was Snoopy from the flash? How much does taking the speed of light into account change the answer?
