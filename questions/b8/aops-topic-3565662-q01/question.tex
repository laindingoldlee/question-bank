Pipes $A,C$ fill a tank and $B$ drains it at constant rates. Pipe $B$ empties a full tank one hour faster than $A$ fills it. All three together fill it in two hours. If $B,C$ operate for one hour from empty and then $B$ is turned off, $C$ takes another $1$ hour $15$ minutes to fill it. Pipe $A$ fills it in less than five hours. Find the time in minutes for $C$ alone to fill the tank.
