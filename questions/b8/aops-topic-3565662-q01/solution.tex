Let $A$ take $t>1$ hours, and let $c$ be $C$'s filling rate. Then $\dfrac{1}{t}-\dfrac{1}{t-1}+c=\dfrac{1}{2}$, and $\dfrac{9}{4}c-\dfrac{1}{t-1}=1$. Substitution of $c=\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{t(t-1)}$ gives $t^2-9t+18=0$, so $t=3$ or $6$. The condition $t<5$ selects $3$. Hence $c=\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{6}=\dfrac{2}{3}$ tank per hour, so $C$ takes $\dfrac{3}{2}$ hours, or $\boxed{90\text{ minutes}}$. Its initial net rate with $B$ is positive, consistent with starting empty.
