The first terms look like a binomial expansion: $(3x-3)^3=27x^3-81x^2+81x-27$. Hence
\[f(x)=(3x-3)^3+75=27(x-1)^3+75.\]
This is strictly increasing, so $f$ has an inverse, and $f^{-1}(9336)$ is the unique $x$ with $f(x)=9336$:
\[27(x-1)^3=9336-75=9261=21^3\quad\Longrightarrow\quad(x-1)^3=343=7^3\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x=8.\]
So $f^{-1}(9336)=\boxed{8}$. (Check: $27\cdot512-81\cdot64+81\cdot8+48=13824-5184+648+48=9336$.)
