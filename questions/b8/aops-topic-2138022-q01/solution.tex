The first equation says $x^2+z^2=4$.

\textbf{(a)} If $x$ and $z$ are positive integers, then $x^2$ and $z^2$ are among $1,4,9,\dots$, and no two of these add up to $4$ ($1+1=2$, $1+4=5$, $4+4=8$). So there are \textbf{no} solutions in positive integers.

\textbf{(b)} In integers, $x^2+z^2=4$ forces one of $x,z$ to be $0$ and the other $\pm2$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $x=\pm2$, $z=0$: then $y^2-5y+4=0$, so $y=1$ or $y=4$.
\item $x=0$, $z=\pm2$: then $y^2-5y=0$, so $y=0$ or $y=5$.
\end{itemize}
So the integer solutions $(x,y,z)$ are the eight triples
\[\boxed{(\pm2,1,0),\ (\pm2,4,0),\ (0,0,\pm2),\ (0,5,\pm2)} .\]
