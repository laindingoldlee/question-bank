There are $\dfrac{6!}{2!}=360$ words. We count the words that come before IRVINE. The letters in alphabetical order are E, I, I, N, R, V.
\begin{itemize}
\item Words starting with E: the other five letters I, I, N, R, V can be arranged in $\dfrac{5!}{2!}=60$ ways.
\item Words starting with I and then E, I or N: in each case the remaining four letters are distinct, so there are $3\cdot4!=72$ words.
\item Words starting with IR and then E, I or N: the remaining three letters are distinct, giving $3\cdot3!=18$ words.
\item Words starting with IRV: the remaining letters are E, I, N. Before IRVINE come IRVEIN, IRVENI (starting IRVE) and IRVIEN, which is $3$ words.
\end{itemize}
So $60+72+18+3=153$ words come before IRVINE, and IRVINE is in position $\boxed{154}$.
