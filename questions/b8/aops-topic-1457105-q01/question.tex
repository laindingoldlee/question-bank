Trapezoid $ABCD$ has $AB\parallel DC$, $AB=b_1<DC=b_2$ and height $h$. The legs $AD$ and $BC$, extended beyond $A$ and $B$, meet at $V$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Using the similar triangles $VAB$ and $VDC$, prove that the area of $ABCD$ is $\dfrac{1}{2}h(b_1+b_2)$.
\item[(b)] Why does this argument not work for a parallelogram?
\end{enumerate}
