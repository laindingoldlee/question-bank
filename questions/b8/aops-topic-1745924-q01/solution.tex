Look at Alex's group. Its other two members are a uniformly random pair of the remaining $23$ students, and there are $\dbinom{23}{2}=253$ such pairs, of which exactly one is $\{\text{Bill},\text{Cody}\}$. The probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{253}}$.
