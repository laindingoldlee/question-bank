The polynomial $g(x)=f(x)+x^2$ is monic of degree $4$ and vanishes at $-1,2,-3,4$, so $g(x)=(x+1)(x-2)(x+3)(x-4)$. Then
\[f(1)=g(1)-1=2\cdot(-1)\cdot4\cdot(-3)-1=\boxed{23}.\]
