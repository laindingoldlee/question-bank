The inradius is $r=\dfrac{3+4-5}{2}=1$. Since $CI$ bisects the right angle, $CI=\sqrt2$. Write $\theta=\angle ACM$. The median direction has $\cos\theta=\dfrac{4}{5}$ and $\sin\theta=\dfrac{3}{5}$, because the midpoint has half the horizontal and vertical displacements of $A$ and $B$. Thus the required distance is $CI\sin(45^\circ-\theta)=\sqrt2\dfrac{4-3}{5\sqrt2}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{5}}$.
