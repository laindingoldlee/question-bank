Let $s=a+b+c$, $q=ab+bc+ca$, $r=abc$. Adding the equations gives $q=2s$. Multiplying them by $c,a,b$, respectively, and adding gives $3r=s^2-2s$. Multiplying the original equations in the forms $ab=a+c$, $bc=a+b$, $ca=b+c$ gives
\[
r^2=(a+b)(b+c)(c+a)=sq-r=2s^2-r.
\]
Substitute $r=\dfrac{(s^2-2s)}{3}$ to obtain $s(s-6)(s+1)^2=0$. The expression $1+s+q+r=1+3s+r$ can therefore only be $1,27,-1$.

All occur. The triples $(0,0,0)$ and $(2,2,2)$ give the first two. For the third, choose a real root $t$ of $t^3+t^2-2t-1=0$ in $(1,2)$, and set
\[
a=t,\quad b=t^2-2,\quad c=1-t-t^2.
\]
Reducing products by the cubic verifies all three original equations, and $a+b+c=-1$. Hence the requested sum is $1+27-1=\boxed{27}$.
