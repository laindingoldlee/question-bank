The $k$th line has at most $k-1$ previous intersections and hence at most $k$ pieces, each adding one region. Thus the maximum is $1+\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$, attained by lines with no parallels or triple intersections. For $n=55$ this is $1541$; for $n=56$ it is $1597$. Therefore $\boxed{56}$.
