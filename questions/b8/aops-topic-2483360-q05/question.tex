Bob, Cob and Dob are three of the $30$ students in a class, and every student presents a project today, one per presentation period. For each period the teacher picks the presenter uniformly at random from the students who have not yet presented and are in the room. Bob is in the bathroom during the first two periods, so he cannot be picked for them; everyone else is always available. What is the probability that Dob presents before both Bob and Cob?
