Let the digit sum be $s$, and write $N=39ks$. Since $N\equiv s\pmod9$, $(3k-1)s\equiv0\pmod9$. The factor $3k-1$ is coprime to $9$, so $9\mid s$. Therefore $351\mid N$. The possible multiples below $2024$ are $351,702,1053,1404,1755$. The first four have digit sum $9$ and are divisible by $351$. The last has digit sum $18$ but is not divisible by $702$. Hence the answer is $\boxed{351,702,1053,1404}$.
