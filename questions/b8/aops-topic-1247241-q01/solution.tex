$391^2-389^2=(391-389)(391+389)=2\cdot780=1560$. Its greatest proper divisor is $\dfrac{1560}{2}=\boxed{780}$ (dividing by the smallest prime factor). Of the quoted wrong answers, $7$ does not divide $1560$, and $2$ is its smallest prime divisor rather than its largest proper divisor.
