All roots are positive real, negative real, or purely imaginary. Positive roots comprise 90 integers, 81 new square roots (exclude the 9 perfect squares), and 81 new fourth roots (a fourth root coincides with an integer or square root precisely when its radicand is square). This gives 252. Negative roots comprise 90 negative square roots plus 81 new negative fourth roots, giving 171. The purely imaginary roots $\pm i\sqrt[4]{k}$ give 180 more, with no overlap with real roots. Thus the total is $252+171+180=\boxed{603}$.
