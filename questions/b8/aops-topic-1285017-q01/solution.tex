The surface area of a sphere of radius $r$ is $4\pi r^2$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real r=1.4; draw(circle((0,0),r),blue); draw(ellipse((0,0),r,0.35*r),blue+dashed); draw((0,0)--(r,0),red); label("$5$ m",(r/2,0),N,red); dot((0,0));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Here $4\pi\cdot25=100\pi\approx314.16$, so the surface area is about $\boxed{314\text{ m}^2}$.
