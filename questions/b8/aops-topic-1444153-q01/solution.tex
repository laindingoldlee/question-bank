The first person has $5$ chairs to choose from, the second $4$, the third $3$ and the fourth $2$, so there are $5\cdot4\cdot3\cdot2=\boxed{120}$ ways. (Equivalently, choose the empty chair in $5$ ways and seat the four people in the other chairs in $4!=24$ ways.) The poster's $120$ is correct.
