Squaring $\sqrt{x+R_1}=\sqrt x+1$ gives $R_1=2\sqrt x+1$, where $R_1=\sqrt{4x+R_2}$. By induction, $R_k=2^k\sqrt x+1$ requires $R_{k+1}=2^{k+1}\sqrt x+1$. At the innermost level, $\sqrt{4^{2008}x+3}=2^{2008}\sqrt x+1$. Squaring, $3=2^{2009}\sqrt x+1$, so $\sqrt x=2^{-2008}$ and $x=\boxed{\dfrac1{4^{2008}}}$. (Every step reverses, since all quantities are positive.)
