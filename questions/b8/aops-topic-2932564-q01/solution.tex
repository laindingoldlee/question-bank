Dividing by $ab$, $bc$, $ac$ and writing $x=\dfrac1a$, $y=\dfrac1b$, $z=\dfrac1c$:
\[2x+y=3,\qquad5y+4z=6,\qquad8x+7z=9.\]
From the first, $y=3-2x$. Then $15-10x+4z=6$ gives $z=\dfrac{10x-9}4$, and $8x+\dfrac{7(10x-9)}4=9$ gives $102x=99$. So $x=\dfrac{33}{34}$, $y=\dfrac{18}{17}$, $z=\dfrac3{17}$ and
\[abc=\dfrac1{xyz}=\dfrac{34\cdot17\cdot17}{33\cdot18\cdot3}=\dfrac{4913}{891}\approx5.51403,\]
so $\lfloor1000abc\rfloor=\boxed{5514}$.
