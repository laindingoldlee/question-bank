List the partitions with parts $a\ge b\ge c\ge1$ by the largest part:
\[8+1+1,\ 7+2+1,\ 6+3+1,\ 6+2+2,\ 5+4+1,\ 5+3+2,\ 4+4+2,\ 4+3+3.\]
($a\le8$, and $a\ge4$ since $3a\ge10$.) There are $\boxed{8}$.

(There is no simple closed formula as for stars and bars; for three parts the number is the integer nearest to $\dfrac{n^2}{12}$, here $\dfrac{100}{12}\approx8.3$.)
