The Pythagorean theorem can be pictured with squares built on the three sides of a right triangle. Instead, build equilateral triangles outward on the legs $a$, $b$ and on the hypotenuse $c$ of a right triangle, and let $[a]$, $[b]$, $[c]$ be their areas. Find the ratio $\left([a]+[b]\right):[c]$.
