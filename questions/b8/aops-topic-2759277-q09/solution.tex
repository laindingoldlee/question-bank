Numbers without a $0$: $9$ one-digit and $9\cdot9=81$ two-digit, so $90$ up to $99$. Among $100,\dots,199$ the zero-free numbers are $1ab$ with $a,b\ne0$: $111$--$119$ ($9$ numbers, total $99$) and $121$--$129$ ($9$ numbers, total $108$); $130$ is removed; then $131,132,133$ bring the total to $111$. Since $134$ would make $112$, $N=133$. Answer $\boxed{\textbf{(C)}\ 133}$.
