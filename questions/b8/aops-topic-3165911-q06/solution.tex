The condition allows $36-4^2=20$ ordered outcomes. Among them the desired ones are $(4,6),(5,5),(5,6),(6,4),(6,5)$; $(6,6)$ is excluded by the condition. Thus the conditional probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{5}{20}=\dfrac{1}{4}}$.
