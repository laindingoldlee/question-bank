Convex hexagon $ABCDEF$ satisfies $AB\parallel DE$, $BC\parallel EF$, $CD\parallel AF$, and
\[
BC-EF=ED-AB=AF-CD>0 .
\]
Prove that all interior angles of $ABCDEF$ are equal.
