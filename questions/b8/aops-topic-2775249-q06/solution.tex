Opposite vertices are separated by the space diagonal $\sqrt{25+16+4}=3\sqrt5$, so any enclosing sphere has radius at least $\dfrac{3\sqrt5}{2}$. The sphere centred at the box centre with that radius contains every vertex and hence the box. Its volume is $\dfrac{4}{3}\pi(\dfrac{3\sqrt5}{2})^3=\boxed{\dfrac{45\sqrt5\pi}{2}}$.
