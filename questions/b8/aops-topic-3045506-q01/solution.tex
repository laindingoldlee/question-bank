If Red is innocent, the truthful Red statement excludes Blue and Orange because they stayed in the lounge. If Red is the impostor, those two are already excluded by uniqueness. Thus neither Blue nor Orange can be the impostor. If Pink is innocent, Pink’s two implications say that Yellow and Green have the same innocence status. Since there is only one impostor, both must be innocent. If Pink is the impostor, uniqueness again excludes Yellow and Green. Therefore only Red, Pink, or Lime remain. All three are possible: let Blue and Orange stay in the lounge for the whole round, and let each of Red, Yellow, Green, Pink, and Lime have been there but leave three minutes before the present. Then every statement is true, including Pink’s when the impostor is Red, Pink, or Lime. Any one of those three may be the impostor, since none stayed throughout. Hence the complete answer is $\boxed{\text{Red, Pink, or Lime}}$.
