\textbf{Proof.} Since $\log3>0$ and $\log5>0$, the AM-GM inequality gives
\[
\log3\cdot\log5\le\left(\dfrac{\log3+\log5}{2}\right)^2=\left(\dfrac{\log15}{2}\right)^2<\left(\dfrac{\log16}{2}\right)^2=(\log4)^2 ,
\]
using that $\log$ is increasing and $\log16=2\log4$. Finally $0<\log4<\log10=1$, so $(\log4)^2<\log4$. Hence $\log3\cdot\log5<\log4$.
