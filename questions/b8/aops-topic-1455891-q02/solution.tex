\begin{enumerate}
\item $2293760=2^{16}\cdot35=2^{16}\cdot5\cdot7$ (indeed $2^{16}=65536$ and $65536\cdot35=2293760$). The largest prime factor is $T=7$.
\item $\dfrac{7!}{4!}=7\cdot6\cdot5=210$.
\item Let $y=\sqrt{42+\sqrt{42+\cdots}}$, the limit of the increasing sequence $\sqrt{42}$, $\sqrt{42+\sqrt{42}}$, $\dots$ (all terms are below $7$, since $\sqrt{42+t}<7$ whenever $t<7$). Then $y=\sqrt{42+y}$, so $y^2-y-42=(y-7)(y+6)=0$ and $y=7$. The answer is $\dfrac{210}{7}=30$.
\item $\dbinom{30}{2}=\dfrac{30\cdot29}{2}=435$.
\item $435=3\cdot5\cdot29$, so the smallest prime factor is $\boxed{3}$.
\end{enumerate}
Answers: $7,\ 210,\ 30,\ 435,\ 3$.
