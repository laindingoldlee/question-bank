This is a relay: in each part, $T$ denotes the answer to the previous part.
\begin{enumerate}
\item What is the largest prime factor of $2293760$?
\item What is $\dfrac{T!}{4!}$?
\item What is $\dfrac{T}{\sqrt{42+\sqrt{42+\sqrt{42+\cdots}}}}$\,?
\item There are $T$ people in a room, and each pair of them shakes hands once. How many handshakes are there?
\item What is the smallest prime factor of $T$?
\end{enumerate}
