Both summands have the same expectation by symmetry. For the first, the unordered numerator pair is equally likely to be any of six two-element subsets. Pair each subset with its complement. The products $ab(a+b)^2$ for pairs $23,24,25,34,35,45$ are $150,288,490,588,960,1620$. Consequently the required expectation is\[\dfrac{1}{3}\left(\dfrac{150}{1620}+\dfrac{1620}{150}+\dfrac{288}{960}+\dfrac{960}{288}+\dfrac{490}{588}+\dfrac{588}{490}\right)=\dfrac{4471}{810}=5.519753\ldots.\]To the nearest hundredth it is $\boxed{5.52}$.
