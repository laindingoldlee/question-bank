Three consecutive natural numbers are $n$, $n+1$, $n+2$ for some natural number $n$, so whatever the order of $a$, $b$, $c$,
\[
a+b+c=n+(n+1)+(n+2)=3n+3=3(n+1).
\]
Hence $\dfrac{a+b+c}{3}=n+1$, which is an integer (indeed it is the middle number of the three). $\blacksquare$
