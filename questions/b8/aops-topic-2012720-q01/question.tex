Gracie starts at her house and walks one block east. After each block she walks, she flips a fair coin: she walks one block east if the coin lands heads, and one block west otherwise. She stops her walk the first time she arrives back at her house. What is the probability that she stops after walking exactly eight blocks? Express your answer as a common fraction.
