$65=1^2+8^2=4^2+7^2$. Checking smaller values: the sums of two distinct positive squares below $65$ are $5,10,13,17,20,25,26,29,34,37,40,41,45,50,52,53,58,61$ (with $50=1+49$ only, since $25+25$ repeats). Each appears once, so the answer is $\boxed{65}$.
