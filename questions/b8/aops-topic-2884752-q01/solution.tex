A sum of $7$ consecutive integers is $7a+21$, a multiple of $7$. A sum of $8$ is $8a+28\equiv4\pmod8$. A sum of $71$ is $71(a+35)$, a multiple of $71$. Conversely, any such number works (integers may be negative). We need $497k\equiv4\pmod8$ with $497\equiv1$, so $k\equiv4$, giving the answer $\boxed{1988}$.
