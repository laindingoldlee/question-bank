The radius of the sphere is $5$. The perpendicular from the centre $O$ to the plane meets it at the centre of the cross-section, and any point of the cross-section is at distance $5$ from $O$. By the Pythagorean theorem the cross-section has radius $\sqrt{5^2-3^2}=\boxed{4}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0);
draw(circle(O,5),linewidth(0.8));
draw((-4,-3)--(4,-3),linewidth(0.8));
draw(ellipse((0,-3),4,0.7),dashed);
draw(O--(0,-3),dashed);draw(O--(4,-3));
draw((0,-3)+(0.35,0)--(0,-3)+(0.35,0.35)--(0,-3)+(0,0.35));
dot(O);label("$O$",O,N);
label("$3$",(0,-1.5),W);label("$5$",(2,-1.5),NE);label("$4$",(1.3,-3),N);
\end{asy}
