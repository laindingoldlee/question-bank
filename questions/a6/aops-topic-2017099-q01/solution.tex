Measure time $t$ in hours from Jill's start, so Jack starts at $t=-\dfrac{1}{6}$.

Jack reaches the top after $\dfrac{5}{15}=\dfrac{1}{3}$ hour of running, at $t=\dfrac{1}{3}-\dfrac{1}{6}=\dfrac{1}{6}$. Jill reaches the top at $t=\dfrac{5}{16}>\dfrac{1}{6}$, so they pass while Jack is coming down and Jill is still going up. For $t\ge\dfrac{1}{6}$, Jack's distance below the top is $20\left(t-\dfrac{1}{6}\right)$ and Jill's is $5-16t$. They meet when
\[
20t-\dfrac{10}{3}=5-16t\iff 36t=\dfrac{25}{3}\iff t=\dfrac{25}{108}.
\]
This is between $\dfrac{1}{6}$ and $\dfrac{5}{16}$, as required. Their distance from the top is then
\[
5-16\cdot\dfrac{25}{108}=5-\dfrac{100}{27}=\boxed{\dfrac{35}{27}\text{ km}}.
\]
