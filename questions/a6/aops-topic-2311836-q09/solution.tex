A positive integer $N$ has $\lfloor\log_{10}N\rfloor+1$ digits, so we need $\log_{10}(2020!)$ accurately enough to find its integer part.

With $n=2020$: $\ln2020\approx7.610852790$, so $\left(n+\dfrac12\right)\ln n\approx2020.5\cdot7.610852790\approx15377.728063$. Also $\dfrac12\ln(2\pi)\approx0.918939$. Hence
\[L_{2020}\approx15377.728063-2020+0.918939=13358.647002.\]
The two correction terms are between $0$ and $\dfrac{1}{24240}<0.00005$, so
\[13358.64700<\ln(2020!)<13358.64706,\]
and dividing by $\ln10\approx2.302585093$,
\[5801.5866<\log_{10}(2020!)<5801.5868.\]
The integer part is $5801$, with plenty of room on both sides, so $2020!$ has $5801+1=\boxed{5802}$ digits.
