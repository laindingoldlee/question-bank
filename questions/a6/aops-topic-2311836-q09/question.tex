How many digits does the decimal representation of $2020!$ have? You may use a calculator for logarithms, together with Stirling's bounds: for every positive integer $n$,
\[L_n+\dfrac{1}{12n+1}<\ln(n!)<L_n+\dfrac{1}{12n},\qquad\text{where }L_n=\left(n+\dfrac12\right)\ln n-n+\dfrac12\ln(2\pi).\]
