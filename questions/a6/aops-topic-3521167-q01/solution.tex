After subtracting $1$ from every result, count nonnegative solutions to $x_1+x_2+x_3+x_4=65$ with $x_i\le41$. Without upper bounds there are $\binom{68}{3}$. For each specified coordinate at least $42$, subtracting $42$ leaves sum $23$, giving $\binom{26}{3}$ solutions. Two violations cannot occur because $84>65$. Thus the probability is $\dfrac{\binom{68}{3}-4\binom{26}{3}}{42^4}=\boxed{\dfrac{9929}{777924}}$.
