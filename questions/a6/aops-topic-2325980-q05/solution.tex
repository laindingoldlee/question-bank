The number $N$ of pieces satisfies $N\equiv1\pmod3$ and $N\equiv0\pmod5$. The positive multiples of $5$ are $5,10,15,\dots$, with remainders $2,1,0,\dots$ modulo $3$. The first one with remainder $1$ is $\boxed{10}$. (All solutions are $N\equiv10\pmod{15}$.)
