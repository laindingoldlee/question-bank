Let the integers be $n,n+1,n+2,n+3$ with $n\ge1$. Pair the outer and the inner factors:
\[
n(n+3)=n^2+3n,\qquad (n+1)(n+2)=n^2+3n+2 .
\]
With $t=n^2+3n+1$ the product is
\[
P=(t-1)(t+1)=t^2-1 .
\]
Since $n\ge1$, $t\ge5$, and
\[
(t-1)^2=t^2-2t+1<t^2-1<t^2 ,
\]
because $2t-1>1$. So $P$ lies strictly between the consecutive squares $(t-1)^2$ and $t^2$, and is therefore not a perfect square. $\blacksquare$

(The word ``positive'' matters: with $n=0$ the product is $0=0^2$.)
