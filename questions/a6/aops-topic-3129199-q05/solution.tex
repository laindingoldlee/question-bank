There are $\binom{2n}{2}=2n^2-n$ unordered pairs. Their positive differences take at most $n^2-n$ values, from 1 through $n^2-n$. If every difference occurred at most twice, there would be at most $2n^2-2n$ pairs, which is smaller than $2n^2-n$. The pigeonhole principle therefore gives a difference occurring at least three times.
