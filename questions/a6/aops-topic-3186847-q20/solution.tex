If occupied chairs are $1\le s_1<\cdots<s_5\le 14$ with gaps at least 2, subtract $0,1,2,3,4$ respectively. This gives any five distinct positions from 1 through 10, so there are $\binom{10}{5}$ seat sets. Arrange the five people in $5!$ ways, giving $\binom{10}{5}5!=\boxed{30240}$.
