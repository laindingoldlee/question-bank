Both equations are homogeneous of degree $2$. Doubling the first and subtracting the second (to eliminate the constants: $2\cdot1=2$):
\[
2(2x^2-y^2)-(xy+x^2)=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad3x^2-xy-2y^2=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad(3x+2y)(x-y)=0 .
\]
If $y=x$: $x^2=1$, giving $(1,1)$ and $(-1,-1)$, and both satisfy $xy+x^2=2$. If $y=-\dfrac{3}{2}x$: $2x^2-\dfrac{9}{4}x^2=-\dfrac{x^2}{4}=1$, impossible. The solutions are $\boxed{(1,1)\text{ and }(-1,-1)}$.
