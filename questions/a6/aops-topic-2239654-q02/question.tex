Numbers $a$ and $b$ satisfy $3a+3b=123$ and $a^3+b^3=82$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find $\dfrac{1}{a}+\dfrac{1}{b}$.
\item[(b)] Show that $a$ and $b$ are not real numbers.
\end{enumerate}
