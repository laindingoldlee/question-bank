Let the integer side lengths be $a\le b\le c$, with sum ten. Triangle inequality gives $c<5$, hence $c\le4$. If $c\le3$, the sum is at most nine, so $c=4$. Then $a+b=6$, giving $(a,b)=(2,4)$ or $(3,3)$. Both are nondegenerate, so $\boxed2$ triangles are possible.

\end{asy}
