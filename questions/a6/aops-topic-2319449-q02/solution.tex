Glue the three Hunger Games books into one block. Then there are $5+7+1=13$ objects to arrange, in $13!$ ways, and the three books inside the block can be ordered in $3!$ ways. The number of arrangements is
\[13!\cdot3!=6227020800\cdot6=\boxed{37362124800}.\]
