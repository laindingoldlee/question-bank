Look at parities: odd $+$ odd $=$ even, odd $+$ even $=$ odd, even $+$ odd $=$ odd. So the parities go
\[
\text{odd, odd, even, odd, odd, even},\dots
\]
and repeat with period $3$ (after an odd, odd, even block, the next block starts again with even $+$ odd $=$ odd, odd $+$ even $=$ odd). Exactly $2$ of every $3$ consecutive terms are odd, so among the first $60=3\cdot20$ terms there are $2\cdot20=\boxed{40}$ odd terms.
