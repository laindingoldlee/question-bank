Let x, y, z be the overlap areas of adjacent napkins AB, BC, CD. The areas covered only by each are $144-x$, $81-x-y$, $49-y-z$, and $16-z$. Their alternating sum is
\[(144-x)-(81-x-y)+(49-y-z)-(16-z)=\boxed{96\text{ cm}^2}.\]
All overlap areas cancel, so their individual shapes or sizes are unnecessary.
