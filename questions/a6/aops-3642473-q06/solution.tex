Let there be m children. After each loss Naomi waits exactly $m-2$ games before playing again. If she finished as king, all eight losses must be followed by another appearance, and after her first appearance every game she misses belongs to one of these eight waiting periods. Before her first appearance she waits some $t$ with $0\le t\le m-2$. Since she plays $12+8=20$ games, $75=20+8(m-2)+t$, or $55=8(m-2)+t$. If $m\le8$, the right side is at most $9\cdot6=54$; if $m\ge9$, it is at least $8\cdot7=56$. Both are impossible. Thus she cannot be the final king.
