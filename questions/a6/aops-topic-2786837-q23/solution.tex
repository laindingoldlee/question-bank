Let the terms be $a,ar,ar^2,ar^3$. Then $a(1+r)(1+r^2)=255$ and $ar(1+r^2)=204$. Dividing, $\dfrac{1+r}r=\dfrac{255}{204}=\dfrac54$, so $r=4$. Then $a\cdot5\cdot17=255$ gives $a=3$, and the sequence is $3,12,48,192$. The smallest number is $\boxed{3}$.
