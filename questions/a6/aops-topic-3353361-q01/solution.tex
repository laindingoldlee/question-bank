The rates in rooms per hour are $\dfrac{1}{15},\dfrac{1}{10},\dfrac{2}{15}$. Abe first paints $\dfrac{1}{10}$. Abe and Bea together work at $\dfrac{1}{6}$, so painting the next $\dfrac{2}{5}$ takes $\dfrac{12}{5}$ hours. All three work at $\dfrac{3}{10}$, so the remaining half takes $\dfrac{5}{3}$ hours. Total time is $\dfrac{3}{2}+\dfrac{12}{5}+\dfrac{5}{3}=\dfrac{167}{30}$ hours, or $\boxed{334}$ minutes.
