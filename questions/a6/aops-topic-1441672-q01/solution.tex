From the equations, $\sqrt y=40-x$ and $\sqrt x=22-y$ are rational. Write $a=\sqrt x\ge0$ and $b=\sqrt y\ge0$ (rational), so
\[
a^2+b=40,\qquad a+b^2=22 .
\]
Substituting $b=40-a^2$ into the second equation gives $a+(40-a^2)^2=22$, that is,
\[
a^4-80a^2+a+1578=0 .
\]
A rational root of this monic integer polynomial must be an integer dividing $1578=2\cdot3\cdot263$. Trying $a=6$: $1296-2880+6+1578=0$. Dividing,
\[
a^4-80a^2+a+1578=(a-6)\left(a^3+6a^2-44a-263\right),
\]
and the cubic has no rational roots: its candidates are $\pm1,\pm263$, and none works ($1+6-44-263\ne0$, $-1+6+44-263\ne0$, and $\pm263$ are far too large in size). So $a=6$, $b=40-36=4$, giving $x=36$, $y=16$ (check: $36+4=40$, $6+16=22$), and
\[
x+y=\boxed{52}.
\]
