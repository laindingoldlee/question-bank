Place $B=(0,0),C=(21,0),A=(u,v)$ with $v>0$. Subtracting $u^2+v^2=100$ from $(u-21)^2+v^2=289$ yields $u=6$, then $v=8$. Hence $AD=8$. Since $\angle AED=\angle AFD=90^\circ$, all four vertices lie on the circle with diameter $AD$. Its disk contains the quadrilateral. Conversely any disk containing $A,D$ has radius at least $\dfrac{AD}{2}=4$, by the triangle inequality. The minimum area is $\boxed{16\pi}$.
