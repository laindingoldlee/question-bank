A line $\ell$ and two distinct points $A$ and $B$ not on $\ell$ are given in a plane, such that $AB$ is not perpendicular to $\ell$. Find the point $P$ on $\ell$ for which $|AP-PB|$ is as small as possible. Treat separately the cases where $A$ and $B$ are on the same side of $\ell$ and on opposite sides.
