Expand and collect the terms containing $w$:
\[
s=2lw+2lh+2wh\ \Rightarrow\ s-2lh=2w(l+h)\ \Rightarrow\ \boxed{w=\dfrac{s-2lh}{2(l+h)}},
\]
which is valid because $l+h>0$.
