Let the sticks have length $1$. Every piece has length at most $\dfrac{1}{3}$, while all the pieces together have length $4$; so the longest piece is always shorter than the sum of the others, and the pieces can always be laid out as a polygon with one side per piece. Hence the pieces form a $14$-gon exactly when there are $14$ pieces in total.

Write the numbers of pieces as $3+e_1,\dots,3+e_4$ with each $e_i\in\{0,1,2\}$. The total is $14$ exactly when $e_1+e_2+e_3+e_4=2$: either one $e_i$ is $2$ ($4$ ways) or two of them are $1$ ($\dbinom42=6$ ways). That is $10$ of the $3^4=81$ equally likely outcomes, so the probability is
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{10}{81}}.
\]
