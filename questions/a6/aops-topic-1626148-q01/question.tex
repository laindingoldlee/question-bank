Four spaghetti sticks of equal length are dropped. Each stick, independently, breaks into $3$, $4$ or $5$ equal pieces, each with probability $\dfrac{1}{3}$. What is the probability that the pieces can be used as the sides of a $14$-sided polygon, using every piece as one whole side? Express your answer as a common fraction.
