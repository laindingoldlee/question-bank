Abhi, Bryan, Meghna and Noreen are each assigned a different integer from $1$ to $10$, inclusive. Abhi's number is prime and Noreen's number is a perfect square. Bryan's number is half of the number assigned to another person, while Meghna's number is the sum of the numbers assigned to two of the other people. The ordered quadruple $(A,B,M,N)=(2,1,5,4)$ is one possible assignment. How many such assignments are there?
