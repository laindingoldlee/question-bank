If the prime exponents are $e_i$, then $\prod(e_i+1)=16$. The possible exponent multisets are $\{15\},\{7,1\},\{3,3\},\{3,1,1\},\{1,1,1,1\}$. Assigning larger exponents to smaller primes minimizes each case. The corresponding minima are $32768,384,216,120,210$. Therefore the least is $\boxed{120=2^3\cdot3\cdot5}$.
