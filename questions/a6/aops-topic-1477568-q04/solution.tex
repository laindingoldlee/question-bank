Time is distance divided by speed, so $K$'s time for the $30$ miles is
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{30}{x}\text{ hours}} .
\]
$M$'s speed is $x-\dfrac{1}{3}$, so $M$'s time is $\dfrac{30}{x-\dfrac{1}{3}}=\dfrac{90}{3x-1}$ hours. Since $K$ takes $30$ minutes $=\dfrac{1}{2}$ hour less,
\[
\dfrac{90}{3x-1}-\dfrac{30}{x}=\dfrac{1}{2} .
\]
(Clearing denominators gives $3x^2-x-60=0$. The question only asks for $K$'s time in terms of $x$, which is $\dfrac{30}{x}$; the other information is not needed for that.)
