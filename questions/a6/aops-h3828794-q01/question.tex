There are $2023$ people living on an island. Each person is either a knight, who always tells the truth, or a knave, who always lies. One day, every knight declared: "I am friends with exactly one knave," while every knave declared: "I have no friends who are knights." Which group is larger, the knights or the knaves? Justify your answer.

Note: Friendship is always mutual: if $A$ is a friend of $B$, then $B$ is a friend of $A$.
