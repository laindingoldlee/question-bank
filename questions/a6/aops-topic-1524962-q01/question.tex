An Euler Airline flight is getting ready to take off. The pilot starts from rest at the edge of the runway and needs to accelerate to a speed of $300$ km/h in $30$ seconds. Acceleration is defined as the change in speed per unit time. What is the pilot's average acceleration, in metres per second per second (metres per second squared), during takeoff? Express your answer as a decimal to the nearest tenth.
