Let $P_n$ count one-peak permutations and $Z_n$ count zero-peak permutations. A zero-peak permutation decreases to its minimum then increases; assigning each of the other $n-1$ entries to one side gives $Z_n=2^{n-1}$. Insert largest entry $n$ into a permutation of length $n-1$. For a one-peak permutation, the two end slots and two slots beside its peak preserve the peak count, giving four choices. For a zero-peak permutation, the $n-2$ interior slots create one peak. Therefore $P_n=4P_{n-1}+(n-2)2^{n-2}$, with $P_2=0$. Induction yields $P_n=2^{n-2}(2^{n-1}-n)$. At $n=100$, $N=2^{98}(2^{99}-100)=2^{100}(2^{97}-5^2)$, giving $\boxed{a+b+c=202}$.
