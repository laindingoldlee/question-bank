Let $N$ count permutations of $1,\ldots,100$ with exactly one interior peak: an entry exceeding both neighbours. Write $N=2^a(2^b-c^2)$ with $c$ odd and positive, taking the representation obtained from the peak-count formula. Find $a+b+c$.
