With $k$ nonempty classes there are $m=2024-k$ positive differences. Their sum is at least $\dfrac{m(m+1)}{2}$. Within each class the differences telescope to its largest minus its smallest element. The sum is therefore at most the sum of the $k$ largest integers minus that of the $k$ smallest, which is $k(2024-k)=km$. For $m>0$, we get $m+1\le2k$, hence $k\ge675$. The case $m=0$ is no better.
Set $k=675$. For $1\le i\le k-1$, use the class $\{i,2k-i,2k+i-1\}$, and add $\{k,3k-1\}$. These partition $1,\ldots,3k-1$. Triple gaps are $2k-2i$ and $2i-1$, giving all even numbers $2,\ldots,2k-2$ and odd numbers $1,\ldots,2k-3$ once. The pair contributes $2k-1$. Thus all gaps are distinct and the answer is $\boxed{675}$.
