If $5\mid a$, then $xa^3+ya^2+za+t\equiv t\not\equiv0$, impossible; so $5\nmid a$, and $a$ has an inverse $b$ modulo $5$ ($ab\equiv1$). Then
\[b^3\left(xa^3+ya^2+za+t\right)\equiv x+yb+zb^2+tb^3\pmod5,\]
and the left side is divisible by $5$. $\blacksquare$
