Measure work in "jobs". Mr.\ Wiggins does $\dfrac{1}{20}$ of the job per day and Mr.\ Moradiajam $\dfrac{1}{30}$. If they work together for $x$ days, then Mr.\ Wiggins works alone for $5$ days, doing $5\cdot\dfrac{1}{20}=\dfrac{1}{4}$ of the job (this is why the $5$ is multiplied by $\dfrac{1}{20}$: days times rate gives work done). The whole job is $1$:
\[
x\left(\dfrac{1}{20}+\dfrac{1}{30}\right)+\dfrac{5}{20}=1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\dfrac{x}{12}=\dfrac{3}{4}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x=9 .
\]
So Mr.\ Moradiajam works $\boxed{9}$ days and Mr.\ Wiggins works $9+5=\boxed{14}$ days.

Check: $\dfrac{14}{20}+\dfrac{9}{30}=\dfrac{7}{10}+\dfrac{3}{10}=1$.
