An $H$-hour analogue clock has the numbers $1$ to $H$ equally spaced around its dial. The hour hand makes one full turn every $H$ hours and the minute hand one full turn every $60$ minutes, and each hour has $60$ minutes. At $0{:}00$ both hands point straight up. Consider the time $h$ hours and $m$ minutes, where $h\in\{0,1,\dots,H-1\}$ and $0\le m<60$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that one of the two angles between the hands is
\[\theta=\left|\dfrac{360h}{H}-\dfrac{6(H-1)m}{H}\right|\ \text{degrees},\]
so that the angle between the hands (the one at most $180^\circ$) is $\theta$ if $\theta\le180^\circ$ and $360^\circ-\theta$ otherwise.
\item[(b)] Write $\theta$ for a $10$-hour, a $6$-hour, a $5$-hour and a $24$-hour clock.
\item[(c)] Find the angle between the hands at $7{:}30$ on a $10$-hour clock and at $17{:}45$ on a $24$-hour clock.
\end{enumerate}
