\emph{Lower bound.} Suppose Ali does $p$ of the P tasks and $q$ of the Q tasks (even allowing fractions of tasks), and Budi does the rest. If they finish after $T$ minutes, then
\[T\ge3p+5q,\qquad T\ge6(10-p)+4(12-q)=108-6p-4q.\]
Adding $4$ times the first and $5$ times the second,
\[9T\ge540-18p\ge540-180=360,\]
so $T\ge40$.

\emph{Achieving it.} Ali does all $10$ P tasks and $2$ Q tasks ($30+10=40$ minutes), and Budi does the other $10$ Q tasks ($40$ minutes).

The earliest finishing time is $\boxed{07{:}40}$.
