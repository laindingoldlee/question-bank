The centroid gives $AG=2GM=10$. Since $G$ lies inside $\angle A$, the two angles made by $AG$ with $AB,AC$ have sines $\dfrac{8}{10},\dfrac{6}{10}$. Because the perpendicular feet lie on the side segments, both angles are acute; their sines show that they are complementary. Thus $\angle A=90^\circ$. The median to the hypotenuse is $AM=15$, hence $BC=30$. Put $A=(0,0),B=(b,0),C=(0,c)$. Then $G=(\dfrac{b}{3},\dfrac{c}{3})$, so $c=24,b=18$. The sides are $\boxed{18,24,30}$.
