Let $a,b,m,d$ be integers with $m>0$ and $d>0$. Prove that if $a\equiv b\pmod m$, $d\mid a$ and $d\mid b$, then
\[\dfrac {a}{d}\equiv\dfrac {b}{d}\pmod{\dfrac{m}{\gcd(d,m)}}.\]
