Similarity gives $\dfrac{BQ}{BC}=\sqrt{\dfrac{64}{100}}=\dfrac{4}{5}$. Thus $\dfrac{CQ}{CB}=\dfrac{1}{5}$, and triangle $CQR$ has area $100\left(\dfrac{1}{5}\right)^2=4$. Subtracting the two corner triangles yields $100-64-4=\boxed{32}$.
