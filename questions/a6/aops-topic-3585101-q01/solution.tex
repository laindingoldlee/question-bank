Put $b_n=a_n-1$. Then $b_{n+2}=b_{n+1}b_n$ and $b_1=b_2=2$, so $b_n=2^{F_n}$ where $F_1=F_2=1$ are Fibonacci numbers. Modulo $11$, powers of $2$ have period $10$. Computing Fibonacci residues modulo $10$ gives $(F_{60},F_{61})\equiv(0,1)$, the initial pair; the recurrence then repeats. Since $2020\equiv40\pmod{60}$ and $F_{40}\equiv5\pmod{10}$, $a_{2020}\equiv2^5+1\equiv0\pmod{11}$. Each $a_n$ is odd, so among $0,11$ modulo $22$ only $\boxed{11}$ works.
