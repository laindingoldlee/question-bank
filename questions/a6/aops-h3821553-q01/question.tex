In a fictional game, a positive-strength token leaves its holder ``pending''. Receiving a strictly stronger token while pending cancels both tokens and returns the holder to a safe state. A strength-zero token does nothing. A player who remains pending when the timer ends loses.
Two players each secretly take a strength-$1$ token, then each presents a strength-zero token. They receive the opponent's presented token and then their own. Explain the outcome. Why might either player have expected this plan to work if the opponent had instead presented a token of strength greater than $1$?
