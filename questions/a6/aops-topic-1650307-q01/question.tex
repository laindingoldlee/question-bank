A round table has radius $4$. Six rectangular place mats are placed on the table. Each place mat has width $1$ and length $x$. They are positioned so that each mat has two corners on the edge of the table, these two corners being the end points of the same side of length $x$. Further, the mats are positioned so that the inner corners each touch an inner corner of an adjacent mat. What is $x$? (Explain also why the guess $x+1=4$, that is $x=3$, is wrong.)
