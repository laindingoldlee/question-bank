Let $p$ and $q$ be the roots of $x^2+ax+b=0$ (if the quadratic has a double root, take $p=q$ equal to it). Prove that
\[a=-(p+q)\qquad\text{and}\qquad b=pq,\]
explaining in particular why two polynomials that agree for every $x$ must have the same coefficients.
