A solid cylinder has length $8$ cm and diameter $6$ cm. Two identical cones are removed from it: each cone has one end of the cylinder as its base, and the two vertices meet at the centre of the cylinder. Find the surface area of the remaining solid, using $\pi\approx3.142$.
