\textbf{Parity.} Modulo $4$: $x^2+7y^2\equiv x^2-y^2\equiv2011\equiv3\pmod4$. Squares are $0$ or $1\pmod4$, so $x^2\equiv0$ and $y^2\equiv1$: $x$ is even and $y$ is odd.

\textbf{Range.} $7y^2<2011$ gives $y\le16$, so $y\in\{1,3,5,\dots,15\}$.

\textbf{Check.} $2011-7y^2$ for $y=1,3,5,7,9,11,13,15$ equals
\[
2004,\ 1948,\ 1836,\ 1668,\ 1444,\ 1164,\ 828,\ 436 .
\]
Only $1444=38^2$ is a perfect square; the others lie strictly between consecutive squares: $44^2<1948<2004<45^2$, $42^2<1836<43^2$, $40^2<1668<41^2$, $34^2<1164<35^2$, $28^2<828<29^2$, $20^2<436<21^2$. So the only solution is $\boxed{(x,y)=(38,9)}$. Check: $1444+7\cdot81=1444+567=2011$.
