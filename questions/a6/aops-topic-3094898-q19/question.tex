A player has exactly 40 possessions. Each possession ends in a turnover with probability 0.05. Otherwise the player chooses a two-point attempt with probability 0.8 and a three-point attempt with probability 0.2. The respective success probabilities are 0.6 and 0.35. Find the expected total points.
