Conditional on attempting a shot, the expected score is $0.8\cdot0.6\cdot2+0.2\cdot0.35\cdot3=1.17$. Including turnovers gives $0.95\cdot1.17=1.1115$ per possession. Linearity of expectation gives $40\cdot1.1115=\boxed{44.46}$ points.
