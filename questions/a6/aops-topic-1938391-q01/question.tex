Let $f(x)$ be the area of the parallelogram with vertices $(0,0)$, $(x,1)$, $(1,x)$ and $(x+1,x+1)$. Compute
\[
\dfrac{1}{f(2)}+\dfrac{1}{f(3)}+\dfrac{1}{f(4)}+\cdots .
\]
