Work in cents and let $n$ be the number of letters.
\begin{itemize}
\item If $n\le15$: the prices are $150$ and $160$, never equal.
\item If $15<n\le21$: the prices are $150$ and $160+7(n-15)>160$, never equal.
\item If $n>21$: the prices are $150+8(n-21)$ and $160+7(n-15)$. They are equal when
\[
8n-18=7n+55\iff n=73 .
\]
\end{itemize}
Check: $150+8\cdot52=566$ and $160+7\cdot58=566$ cents. The prices agree for $\boxed{73}$ letters (both \$5.66).
