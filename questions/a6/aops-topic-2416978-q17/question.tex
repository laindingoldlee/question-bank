Equilateral triangle $ABC$ has side length $4$. A circle $\omega$ has centre $B$ and radius $4$. Point $D$ lies on $\omega$, outside triangle $ABC$, so that the minor arc $DA$ of $\omega$ measures $60^\circ$. The bisector of angle $DBA$ meets arc $DA$ at $M$. Find the area of the region bounded by arc $MA$, segment $AC$ and segment $MC$.
