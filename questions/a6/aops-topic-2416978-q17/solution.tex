Put $B=(0,0)$, $A=(4,0)$ and $C=\left(2,2\sqrt3\right)$. The circle $\omega$ passes through $A$ and $C$. The two points of $\omega$ that make a $60^\circ$ arc with $A$ are at angles $\pm60^\circ$; the one at $+60^\circ$ is $C$, a vertex of the triangle, so $D=\left(2,-2\sqrt3\right)$. The bisector of the $60^\circ$ angle $DBA$ is at angle $-30^\circ$, so $M=4\left(\cos30^\circ,-\sin30^\circ\right)=\left(2\sqrt3,-2\right)$, and $\angle MBC=30^\circ+60^\circ=90^\circ$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0), A=(4,0), C=(2,2*sqrt(3)), D=(2,-2*sqrt(3)), M=4*dir(-30);
fill(arc(B,4,-30,0)--C--cycle,rgb(1,0.85,0.55));
draw(arc(B,4,-75,75),gray);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
draw(B--D,linewidth(0.6)); draw(B--M,dashed);
draw(M--C,red); draw(arc(B,4,-30,0),red+linewidth(1));
draw(arc(B,0.7,-60,-30)); draw(arc(B,0.8,-30,0));
dot(D); dot(M);
label("$A$",A,E); label("$B$",B,W); label("$C$",C,N); label("$D$",D,S); label("$M$",M,SE);
label("$4$",(B+C)/2,NW);
\end{asy}

The sector $BMA$ (radius $4$, angle $30^\circ$) together with triangle $BAC$ forms the region bounded by $BM$, arc $MA$, $AC$ and $CB$. Removing triangle $BMC$ from it leaves exactly the required region. (Triangle $BMC$ lies inside this region: the ray $BA$ lies inside the right angle $MBC$, so it meets the chord $MC$ at a point $X$; as $X$ is inside $\omega$, $BX<4=BA$, so $X$ is on the segment $BA$. Thus triangle $BMC$ splits into triangle $BMX$, which lies in the sector $BMA$, and triangle $BXC$, which lies in triangle $BAC$.) Hence the area is
\[[\text{sector }BMA]+[BAC]-[BMC]=\dfrac{30}{360}\cdot16\pi+\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}\cdot16-\dfrac12\cdot4\cdot4=\boxed{\dfrac{4\pi}{3}+4\sqrt3-8}\approx3.12.\]
