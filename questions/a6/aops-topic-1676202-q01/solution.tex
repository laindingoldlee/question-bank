The base is a perfect square: $x^2-2+\dfrac{1}{x^2}=\left(x-\dfrac{1}{x}\right)^2$. Hence the expression equals $\left(x-\dfrac{1}{x}\right)^{20}$, whose general term is
\[
\dbinom{20}{j}x^{20-j}\left(-\dfrac{1}{x}\right)^{j}=(-1)^j\dbinom{20}{j}x^{20-2j}.
\]
The constant term has $j=10$: it is $(-1)^{10}\dbinom{20}{10}=\boxed{184756}$.
