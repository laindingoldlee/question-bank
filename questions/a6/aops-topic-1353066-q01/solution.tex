Let $s_n$ and $p_n$ be the numbers of sniffles and spuffles after $n$ nights. Each sniffle produces one sniffle and one spuffle, and each spuffle produces two sniffles, so
\[
s_{n+1}=s_n+2p_n,\qquad p_{n+1}=s_n .
\]
Hence $s_{n+1}=s_n+2s_{n-1}$, with $s_0=3$ and $s_1=3+2\cdot0=3$. Computing:
\[
s_0,\dots,s_{10}=3,\ 3,\ 9,\ 15,\ 33,\ 63,\ 129,\ 255,\ 513,\ 1023,\ 2049 .
\]
(In fact $s_n=2^{n+1}+(-1)^n$: this fits $s_0=3$, $s_1=3$, and $2^{n+2}+(-1)^{n+1}=\left(2^{n+1}+(-1)^n\right)+2\left(2^n+(-1)^{n-1}\right)$, so it satisfies the recurrence.) After ten nights Danny owns $\boxed{2049}$ sniffles.
