Danielle and Rachel have the same number of berries. Danielle eats $2$ of her berries; then Celine takes $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of Danielle's remaining berries, and Rachel eats $80\%$ of her own berries. In the end, Danielle and Rachel again have the same number of berries. How many berries did Danielle have at the beginning?
