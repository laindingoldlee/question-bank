Choose the repeated face in $ 6 $ ways and its two positions in $\binom42=6 $ ways. Fill the other ordered positions with distinct different faces in $ 5\cdot4 $ ways. The probability is $\dfrac{6\cdot6\cdot5\cdot4}{6^4}=\dfrac{5}{9}$, so $ 7m+6n=35+54=\boxed{89}$.
