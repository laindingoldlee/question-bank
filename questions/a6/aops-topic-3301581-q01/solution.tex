Set $k_0=-\lfloor\dfrac{a}{d}\rfloor$ and $r=a+k_0d$. The defining floor inequalities give $0\le r<d$. Every other member is $r+jd$. If $j<0$ it is negative, while if $j\ge0$ it is at least $r$. Thus $r$ is the least nonnegative member. Alternatively, any such least member at least $d$ could be reduced by $d$, contradicting minimality.
