Write $x=3k+2$. The second congruence gives $3k\equiv1\pmod5$, so $k\equiv2\pmod5$ since $2$ is the inverse of $3$. Therefore $\boxed{x\equiv8\pmod{15}}$. Substitution verifies this entire residue class.
