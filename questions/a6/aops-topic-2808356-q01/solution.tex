$1023=3\cdot11\cdot31$ and $2014\equiv991\equiv-32\pmod{1023}$. Note $32=2^5$ and $2^{10}=1024\equiv1\pmod{1023}$. So $2014^{2022}\equiv(-1)^{2022}2^{5\cdot2022}=2^{10110}=\left(2^{10}\right)^{1011}\equiv\boxed{1}\pmod{1023}$.
