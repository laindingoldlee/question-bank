The adjacent comparisons must alternate. Complementing values by $v\mapsto6-v$ exchanges patterns beginning with an increase and a decrease. Count up-down-up-down. The maximum 5 lies in position 2 or 4. If in position 2, choose the first entry in 4 ways; the last three must rise then fall, giving 2 orders. This gives 8. Reversal shows position 4 also gives 8. Doubling for the other comparison pattern gives $2(8+8)=\boxed{32}$.
