(a) Partition the entries into 674 triples. Each triple has integer sum, so their total is an integer. (b) Any two entries differ by an integer: choose four other entries and subtract the two corresponding five-entry sums. Thus the entries can be written $t+m_i$ with integers $m_i$. One five-entry sum shows $5t$ is an integer. Therefore five times the total, $2022(5t)+5\sum m_i$, is an integer. However, taking every entry equal to $\dfrac{1}{5}$ makes every five-entry sum equal to 1, whereas the full sum is $\dfrac{2022}{5}$, which is not an integer.
