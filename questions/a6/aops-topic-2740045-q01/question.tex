A country's licence plates consist of $3$ letters (from A to Z) followed by $4$ digits. The plates are issued in order, starting with AAA0001 and ending with BEZ9999, and within each letter block the numbers run from $0001$ to $9999$ (the number $0000$ is never used). How many plates are issued?
