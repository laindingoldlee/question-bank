Let $x$ be a nonzero real number and let $p=x+\dfrac{1}{x}$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that $|p|\ge2$. For which $x$ is $p\ge2$?
\item[(b)] Find all possible values of $x^2-\dfrac{1}{x^2}$ in terms of $p$, and explain why the value is not determined by $p$ alone.
\end{enumerate}
