Let $R=\underbrace{11\ldots1}_{2000}$. Then $2R=\underbrace{22\ldots2}_{2000}$ and
\[
2002R=1001\cdot2R=2R\cdot1000+2R .
\]
Add the two numbers column by column. $2R\cdot1000$ is $2000$ twos followed by three zeros, and $2R$ is $2000$ twos. In the last three columns we add $0+2$; in the next $1997$ columns we add $2+2=4$; in the three leftmost columns only the shifted number has digits, $2$ each. No column exceeds $9$, so there are no carries:
\[
2002R=222\underbrace{44\ldots4}_{1997}222 .
\]
Its digit sum is $3\cdot2+1997\cdot4+3\cdot2=12+7988=\boxed{8000}$.
