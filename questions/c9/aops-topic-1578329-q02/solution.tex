Each person independently finds it bitter with probability $\dfrac{3}{4}$. Use the complement: "fewer than two" means $0$ or $1$ person.
\[
P(0)=\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^5=\dfrac{1}{1024},\qquad P(1)=\dbinom51\cdot\dfrac{3}{4}\cdot\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^4=\dfrac{15}{1024}.
\]
So
\[
P(\text{at least }2)=1-\dfrac{16}{1024}=1-\dfrac{1}{64}=\boxed{\dfrac{63}{64}}.
\]
