We count ordered triples $(a,b,c)$ of positive integers with $a+b+c=10$. Write $10$ as a row of ten $1$s; there are $9$ gaps between them, and choosing $2$ of the gaps to cut splits the row into three nonempty blocks, whose sizes are $a$, $b$, $c$. This is a bijection, so the number of ways is
\[
\dbinom92=\boxed{36}.
\]
