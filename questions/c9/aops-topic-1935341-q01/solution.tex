(a) $b=(a+b)-a$ is the difference of two integers, so it is an integer. $\blacksquare$

(b) No. If $b$ were an integer, then by part (a) with the roles of $a$ and $b$ exchanged, $a=(a+b)-b$ would be an integer, a contradiction. (For example $a=b=\dfrac{1}{2}$ gives the integer $a+b=1$ with neither $a$ nor $b$ an integer.) So the answer is $\boxed{\text{no}}$.
