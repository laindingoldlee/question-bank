The base angles are 30 degrees, so $AB=AC=s$ and $\angle A=120^\circ$. Set $A=(0,0)$, $B=(\dfrac{s}{2},-\dfrac{\sqrt3s}{2})$, $C=(\dfrac{s}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt3s}{2})$, and $O=(x,y)$. From $OA^2=1,OB^2=4,OC^2=1$, subtract to get $s^2-sx+\sqrt3sy=3$ and $s^2-sx-\sqrt3sy=0$. Hence $x=s-\dfrac{3}{2s}$, $y=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2s}$. Substituting in $x^2+y^2=1$ gives $s^4-4s^2+3=0$, so $s=1$ or $\sqrt3$. The first gives $O=(-\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2})$, outside the angle sector between rays $AB,AC$, contrary to the angle-addition condition. Thus $s=\sqrt3$, and $BC=\sqrt3s=\boxed3$ cm.

\end{asy}
