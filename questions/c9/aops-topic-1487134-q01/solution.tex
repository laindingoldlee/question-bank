A way of doing it is a sequence of nine fruits containing $2$ apples, $3$ pears and $4$ oranges. The number of such sequences is the multinomial coefficient
\[
\dfrac{9!}{2!\,3!\,4!}=\dfrac{362880}{2\cdot6\cdot24}=\boxed{1260}.
\]
(Equivalently, choose the $2$ apple days in $\dbinom{9}{2}=36$ ways and then the $3$ pear days among the other $7$ in $\dbinom{7}{3}=35$ ways: $36\cdot35=1260$.)
