For vertices $(x_1,y_1),\dots,(x_n,y_n)$ listed in order around the polygon, the shoelace formula gives the signed value
\[
\dfrac{1}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(x_iy_{i+1}-x_{i+1}y_i\right)\qquad(\text{with }x_{n+1}=x_1,\ y_{n+1}=y_1),
\]
which is $+$(area) when the vertices go around counterclockwise and $-$(area) when they go clockwise. The area is its absolute value.

\textbf{Counterclockwise order} $(0,0),(4,0),(4,4),(0,4)$:
\[
\dfrac{1}{2}\left[(0\cdot0-4\cdot0)+(4\cdot4-4\cdot0)+(4\cdot4-0\cdot4)+(0\cdot0-0\cdot4)\right]=\dfrac{1}{2}(0+16+16+0)=16 .
\]

\textbf{Clockwise order} $(0,0),(0,4),(4,4),(4,0)$: every product pair is swapped, so each term changes sign and the result is $-16$.

So $-16$ just means the vertices were listed clockwise; the area is $|-16|=\boxed{16}$. (The vertices must be listed in order around the polygon, in either direction; listing them in a crossing order such as $(0,0),(4,4),(4,0),(0,4)$ gives a wrong value.)
