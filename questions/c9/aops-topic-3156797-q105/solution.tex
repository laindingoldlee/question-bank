The possible prime sums are $ 3,5,7,11,13,17 $. Their ordered outcome counts are respectively $ 1,6,15,27,21,3 $, from coefficients of $(x+x^2+\cdots+x^6)^3 $. These can also be found by stars and bars with upper-bound corrections. They total $ 73 $ of $ 216 $ equally likely outcomes, so $\boxed{\dfrac{73}{216}}$.
