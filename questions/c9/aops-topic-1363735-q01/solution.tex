Compute each ratio and its distance from $1$:
\[
\text{Infernape: }\dfrac{190}{230}\approx0.826,\ \ |0.826-1|\approx0.174;\qquad
\text{Empoleon: }\dfrac{95}{100}=0.95,\ \ |0.95-1|=0.05;
\]
\[
\text{Boombloxer: }\dfrac{1.15}{1}=1.15,\ \ |1.15-1|=0.15 .
\]
Pikachu and Charizard are not in the battle. Empoleon's ratio is closest to $1$, so the answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(B)}\text{ Empoleon}}$. (A common mistake is to pick Boombloxer because its numbers are closest to each other, but $0.15$ of its health is a large fraction.)
