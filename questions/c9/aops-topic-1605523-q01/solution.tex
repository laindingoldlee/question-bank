The powers of $3$ modulo $5$ are $3,4,2,1,3,4,2,1,\dots$, repeating with period $4$ since $3^4=81\equiv1\pmod5$. As $2003=4\cdot500+3$,
\[
3^{2003}=(3^4)^{500}\cdot3^3\equiv27\equiv2\pmod5 .
\]
The remainder is $\boxed{2}$.
