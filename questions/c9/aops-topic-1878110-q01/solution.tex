The servant bought $x+3$ oranges for Rs $15$, so the market price is $\dfrac{15}{x+3}$ per orange, while Amit pays Rs $15$ for $x$ oranges, i.e.\ $\dfrac{15}{x}$ per orange. Hence
\[
\dfrac{15}{x}-\dfrac{15}{x+3}=\dfrac{1}{4}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\dfrac{45}{x(x+3)}=\dfrac{1}{4}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x^2+3x-180=0 .
\]
This factors as $(x+15)(x-12)=0$, and $x>0$, so $x=\boxed{12}$. (Check: $15$ oranges at Re $1$ each; Amit gets $12$ for Rs $15$, i.e.\ Rs $1.25$ each.)
