Amit sends his servant to the market to buy oranges worth Rs $15$. On the way back the servant eats $3$ of the oranges, so Amit effectively pays Rs $\dfrac{1}{4}$ more per orange than the market price. Let $x$ be the number of oranges Amit receives. Form a quadratic equation in $x$ and hence find $x$.
