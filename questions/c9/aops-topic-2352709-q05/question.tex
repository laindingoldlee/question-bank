Six tribespeople argue about who gets credit for three theorems. Each theorem must be credited to exactly two of the six people. In how many ways can the credits be assigned
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] if each person gets credit for exactly one theorem;
\item[(b)] if there is no restriction on how many theorems a person gets credit for?
\end{enumerate}
