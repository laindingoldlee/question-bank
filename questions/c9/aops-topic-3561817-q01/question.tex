Each day in Trichotomy is sunny, cloudy, or rainy. Conditional on today's weather, tomorrow matches it with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and is each other type with probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$, independently of earlier weather. Given Friday is not rainy, what is the probability that neither Saturday nor Sunday is rainy?
