An isosceles right triangle is placed on top of a square of side $4$, so that one leg of the triangle lies along the top side of the square; the legs of the triangle have the same length as the sides of the square. The perimeter of the resulting figure can be written as $a+b\sqrt c$, where $a$, $b$, $c$ are integers and $c$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. What is $a+b+c$?

$\textbf{(A) }18\qquad\textbf{(B) }20\qquad\textbf{(C) }22\qquad\textbf{(D) }24\qquad\textbf{(E) }26$
