Note that $1314=2\cdot657$. Let $N=657^{315}$. Then $N$ is divisible by $657$, and $N$ is odd (a power of an odd number). The multiples of $657$ that are odd are exactly the numbers $\equiv657\pmod{1314}$, because the multiples of $657$ alternate between $\equiv0$ and $\equiv657\pmod{1314}$ according to whether the cofactor is even or odd. Here $N=657\cdot657^{314}$ with an odd cofactor, so the remainder is $\boxed{657}$.
