Let $t=2^{17}$, so $2^{289}=t^{17}$. Since $17$ is odd,
\[\dfrac{t^{17}+1}{t+1}=t^{16}-t^{15}+t^{14}-\cdots-t+1=\sum_{i=1}^{8}t^{2i-1}(t-1)+1.\]
Now $t-1=2^{17}-1=2^0+2^1+\cdots+2^{16}$, so
\[t^{2i-1}(t-1)=2^{17(2i-1)}+2^{17(2i-1)+1}+\cdots+2^{17(2i-1)+16}\]
is a sum of $17$ distinct powers of $2$, with exponents from $17(2i-1)$ to $34i-1$. These ranges for $i=1,\dots,8$ do not overlap and none contains $0$, and the final $1=2^0$ adds one more power. So the binary expansion has
\[k=8\cdot17+1=\boxed{\textbf{(C) }137}\]
terms (the representation as a sum of distinct powers of $2$ is unique).
