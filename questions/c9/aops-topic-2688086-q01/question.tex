Let $D$ be a point on side $AB$ and $E$ a point on side $AC$ of triangle $ABC$, with $AD=r\cdot AB$ and $AE=s\cdot AC$. Prove that $\dfrac{[ADE]}{[ABC]}=rs$, where $[\cdot]$ denotes area.
