dot(D);dot(Ep);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,N);label("$D$",D,S);label("$E$",Ep,W);
\end{asy}

Triangles $ADE$ and $ABE$ have bases $AD$ and $AB$ on the same line and the same apex $E$, hence the same height; so $\dfrac{[ADE]}{[ABE]}=\dfrac{AD}{AB}=r$. Similarly triangles $ABE$ and $ABC$ have bases $AE$ and $AC$ on line $AC$ and the common apex $B$, so $\dfrac{[ABE]}{[ABC]}=\dfrac{AE}{AC}=s$. Multiplying,
\[\dfrac{[ADE]}{[ABC]}=\dfrac{[ADE]}{[ABE]}\cdot\dfrac{[ABE]}{[ABC]}=rs.\ \blacksquare\]
