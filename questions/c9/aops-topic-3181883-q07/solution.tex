Expected wasted time per minute is $0.6(0.9)(30)+0.4(0.3)(10)=17.4$ seconds. Expected study time is therefore $100(60-17.4)=4260$ seconds, or $\boxed{71}$ minutes. Independence between different minutes is unnecessary for this expectation.
