For fixed $ j $, the value $ 2^j $ can be selected only at stages $ i\ge j $. Its absence probability is $\prod_{i=j}^{2017}\dfrac{i}{i+1}=\dfrac{j}{2018}$, including zero when $ j=0 $. Its presence indicator therefore has expectation $ 1-\dfrac{j}{2018}$. Summing these 2018 indicators gives $ 2018-\dfrac{1}{2018}\dfrac{2017\cdot2018}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{2019}{2}}$.
