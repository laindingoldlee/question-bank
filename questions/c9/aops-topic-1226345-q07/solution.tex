From $p(16)=1296$: $p(11)=216$, $p(6)=36$, $p(1)=6$. A rectangle with integer sides and perimeter $6$ is $1\times2$, so $a(1)=2$. Then $a(2)=4,\dots,a(7)=14$ (using $x=1,\dots,6$), $a(9)=12\cdot14=168$ and $a(11)=12\cdot168=2016$. So the sides of $R(11)$ satisfy $a+b=108$, $ab=2016$: roots of $t^2-108t+2016=0$, $t=\dfrac{108\pm60}{2}$. The answer is $\boxed{(24,84)}$.
