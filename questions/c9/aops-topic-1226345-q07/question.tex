$R(x)$ is a rectangle for each natural number $x$; $R(1)$ has integer side lengths. Let $p(x)$ and $a(x)$ be the perimeter and area of $R(x)$. Suppose $p(x+5)=6p(x)$ for all $x$; $a(x+2)=12a(x)$ for all $x>6$; and $a(x+1)=a(x)+2$ for $x\le6$. If $p(16)=1296$ and $R(11)$ has sides $a\le b$, find $(a,b)$.
