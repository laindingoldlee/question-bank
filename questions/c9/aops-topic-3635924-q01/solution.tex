Let $X$ be the number of A-heads in its first six tosses and $Y$ the number of B-heads. By symmetry $P(X>Y)=P(X<Y)$; write $t=P(X=Y)$. A wins automatically when $X>Y$, never when $X<Y$, and wins half the ties through its seventh toss. Thus $P(\text{A wins})=\dfrac{1-t}{2}+\dfrac{t}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$.
