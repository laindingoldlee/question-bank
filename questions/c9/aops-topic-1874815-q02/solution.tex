We have $101010101=1+10^2+10^4+10^6+10^8$, so
\[
101010101\times57=57+5700+570000+57000000+5700000000 .
\]
The copies of $57$ occupy different pairs of digit places, so there is no carrying, and the product is $57$ written five times: $\boxed{5757575757}$.
