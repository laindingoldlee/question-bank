The slant height is the radius of the semicircle, $10$. The arc of the semicircle, $10\pi$, becomes the circumference of the base, so the base radius is $5$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw(arc((0,0),2,0,180));draw((-2,0)--(2,0));draw((0,0)--(0,0),linewidth(2));label("$10$",(1,0),S);
pair a=(4,0),b=(7,0),p=(5.5,2.6);draw(a--p--b);draw(ellipse((5.5,0),1.5,0.3));draw((5.5,0)--p,dashed);label("$10$",(6.3,1.3),E);label("$5$",(6.25,0),S);label("$h$",(5.5,1.3),W);
\end{asy}

The altitude is $\sqrt{10^2-5^2}=\boxed{5\sqrt3}$.
