A circular lake has radius $1$ mile. On a pitch-black night Chris lands in the lake at a point $L$ at distance $r$ from its centre, where $0<r<1$. He can swim at most $1$ mile. He picks a direction uniformly at random and swims in a straight line; he survives if he reaches the shore after swimming at most $1$ mile. Let $\mathcal P$ be the probability that he survives; it depends on $r$. Let $\mathcal B$ be the least upper bound and $\mathcal A$ the greatest lower bound of $\mathcal P$ over all landing points (so $\mathcal A<\mathcal P<\mathcal B$). Find $77\mathcal B-7\mathcal A$.
