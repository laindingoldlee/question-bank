In the city of Townsville, population $10000$, each person has a $1\%$ chance of having a certain disease. A test for the disease gives the correct result for $98\%$ of people who do not have the disease and for $97\%$ of people who do have it. I am tested and the test reads positive. What is the probability that I actually have the disease? A classmate adds that this probability decreases as the population increases; is that right?
