Group the thousands and the tens separately:
\[(1000+1000+1000+1000)+(20+30+30+20)=4000+100=\boxed{4100},\]
where $1030$ has been split as $1000+30$. (A common slip is to carry the last $100$ into the thousands and answer $5000$.)
