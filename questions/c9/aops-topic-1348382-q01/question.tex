Consider the equation
\[
(12a^2+b)(12b^2+c)(12c^2+a)=2016\,a^2b^2c^2
\]
in positive real numbers $a$, $b$, $c$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find all solutions with $a=b=c$.
\item[(b)] Show that the equation has infinitely many solutions in positive reals, so that ``find all $a$, $b$, $c$'' has no finite answer.
\end{enumerate}
