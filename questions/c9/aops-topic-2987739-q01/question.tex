In each post, a forum troll independently does each of the following: criticises the post above (probability $\dfrac12$), insults the original poster (probability $\dfrac15$), and types ``e'' $200$ times (probability $\dfrac3{10}$); the three actions are independent, so it may do none, some or all of them. Two trolls post, independently. What is the probability that the first troll criticises the post above and the second troll insults the original poster or types ``e'' $200$ times (or both)?
