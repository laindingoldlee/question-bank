For the second troll, the probability of doing neither of the two actions is $\dfrac45\cdot\dfrac7{10}=\dfrac{14}{25}$, so it does at least one with probability $\dfrac{11}{25}$. By independence the answer is $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac{11}{25}=\boxed{\dfrac{11}{50}}$.
