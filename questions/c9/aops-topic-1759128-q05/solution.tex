Let a lap have length $L$. To average $2x$ over $2L$ he must take a total time of $\dfrac{2L}{2x}=\dfrac {L}{x}$. But the first lap alone already takes $\dfrac {L}{x}$ hours. So the second lap would have to take no time at all: \textbf{it is impossible}; no finite speed works. (In general, with second-lap speed $v$ the average is the harmonic mean $\dfrac{2xv}{x+v}<2x$ for every $v>0$.)
