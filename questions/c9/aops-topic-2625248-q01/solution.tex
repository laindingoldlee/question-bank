Let the distance be $D$, Danny's speed $w$ and the car's speed $v$, and suppose they meet after time $t$. Then $wt+vt=D$. The drive home covers the car's outward distance $vt$ again, which takes another time $t$, so the trip takes $2t$. Hence
\[2t=\dfrac13\cdot\dfrac Dw,\qquad\text{so}\qquad D=6wt.\]
Then $wt+vt=6wt$ gives $v=5w$, and the ratio is $w:v=\boxed{1:5}$.
