Danny phones his mother when he gets off the train. He then starts walking home, and at the same moment his mother leaves home in her car. She drives until she meets Danny, picks him up and drives him home. Danny finds that his total trip home from the station took $\dfrac13$ of the time it would have taken to walk the whole way. Assuming constant speeds, what is the ratio of Danny's walking speed to the car's speed?
