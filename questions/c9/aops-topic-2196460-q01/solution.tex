\textbf{(a)} The divisors come in pairs $d$ and $-d$, which cancel, so the sum is $\boxed{0}$.

\textbf{(b)} We have
\[1234567890=2\cdot3^2\cdot5\cdot3607\cdot3803,\]
where $3607$ and $3803$ are prime (neither is divisible by a prime up to $61$, and $62^2=3844$ exceeds both). Since the sum of divisors is multiplicative,
\[\sigma(1234567890)=(1+2)(1+3+9)(1+5)(1+3607)(1+3803)=3\cdot13\cdot6\cdot3608\cdot3804=\boxed{3211610688}.\]
