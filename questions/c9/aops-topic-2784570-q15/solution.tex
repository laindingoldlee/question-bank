If the roots are $u,v$, then $u+v=-2$ and $uv=3$, so $u^2+v^2=(-2)^2-2\cdot3=-2$. Therefore $u^4+v^4=(u^2+v^2)^2-2u^2v^2=4-18=\boxed{-14}$.
