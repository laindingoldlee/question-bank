Mrs. Frank's classroom clock has the school colours, green and purple. The clock is a regular dodecagon with radius $14$ cm (the distance from its centre to each vertex). Centred on the clock's face is a green circle of radius $9$ cm, and the region outside the circle is purple. Which colour has more area?
