$ABCD$ is a quadrilateral. Each side is extended past its endpoint by its own length: $A'$ is on ray $AB$ with $BA'=AB$, $B'$ on ray $BC$ with $CB'=BC$, $C'$ on ray $CD$ with $DC'=CD$, and $D'$ on ray $DA$ with $AD'=DA$. Given only the points $A',B',C',D'$, show how to reconstruct $ABCD$.
