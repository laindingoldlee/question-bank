Jessy has many cubes with edges $1$ cm, $2$ cm and $3$ cm. She builds larger solid cubes by fitting some of them together without gaps or overlaps (a single cube also counts). Two constructions of cubes of the same volume count as different if and only if they use different numbers of cubes. For example, there are two ways to get a cube of volume $8\text{ cm}^3$: with eight $1$-cm cubes, or with one $2$-cm cube. In how many different ways can she get a cube of volume $27\text{ cm}^3$?
