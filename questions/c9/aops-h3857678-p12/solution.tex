At most three points are awarded per match, so the total is at most $3\binom{18}2=459$. Eighteen successful teams would need at least $468$ points, impossible. For a construction, let seventeen teams form a cyclic tournament in which each beats the next eight teams and loses to the other eight. Let all seventeen beat team eighteen. Each has nine wins and 27 points, so $\boxed{17}$ teams can succeed, choice E.
