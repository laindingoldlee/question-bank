In a football tournament with \(18\) teams, every pair of teams plays exactly one match.



A winning team receives \(3\) points, a losing team receives \(0\) points, and teams that draw receive \(1\) point each.



A successful team is a team that earns at least \(26\) points.



What is the maximum possible number of successful teams?

\begin{array}{ll}
\text{a)}\ 9 & \text{b)}\ 11 \\
\text{c)}\ 13 & \text{d)}\ 15 \\
\text{e)}\ 17
\end{array}
