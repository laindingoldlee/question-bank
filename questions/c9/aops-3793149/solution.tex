Expand each denominator geometrically: $\dfrac{x^{2n}}{1-x^{2n+1}}=\sum_{j=0}^\infty x^{2n+(2n+1)j}$. All terms are nonnegative and the series converges, bounded above by $\dfrac{1}{1-x}\sum_nx^{2n}$. For a term $x^m$, the exponent condition is $m+1=(2n+1)(j+1)$. Every positive odd divisor $d$ of $m+1$ gives exactly one pair $n = \dfrac{d-1}{2} , j = \dfrac{m+1}{d} - 1$, and vice versa. Hence the coefficient is $\boxed{\text{the number of odd divisors of }m+1}$.
