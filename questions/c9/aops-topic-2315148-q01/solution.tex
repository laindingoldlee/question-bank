Subtract $6$: $7j\equiv-2\equiv3\pmod5$. Reduce the coefficient: $7\equiv2$, so $2j\equiv3\pmod5$. The inverse of $2$ modulo $5$ is $3$, since $2\cdot3=6\equiv1$. Multiplying by $3$,
\[j\equiv9\equiv4\pmod5.\]
So $\boxed{j\equiv4\pmod5}$. (Check: $7\cdot4+6=34\equiv4\pmod5$.)
