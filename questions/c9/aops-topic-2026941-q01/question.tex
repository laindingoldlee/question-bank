Justin buys $m$ large packs of water bottles at $5$ dollars each and $n$ huge packs at $9$ dollars each, where $m$ and $n$ are nonnegative integers. What is the greatest total cost $T$ (in dollars) for which there is exactly one pair $(m,n)$ with $5m+9n=T$?
