If $5m+9n=T$ and $5m'+9n'=T$, then $5(m-m')=9(n'-n)$, so $9\mid m-m'$ and $5\mid n'-n$. Hence all representations of $T$ are obtained from one of them by repeatedly replacing $(m,n)$ by $(m+9,n-5)$ or $(m-9,n+5)$, as long as both entries stay nonnegative.

Therefore a representation $(m,n)$ is the only one exactly when neither move is possible, that is, when $n\le 4$ and $m\le 8$. So $T$ has a unique representation if and only if $T=5m+9n$ with $0\le m\le 8$ and $0\le n\le 4$. The largest such value is
\[
5\cdot 8+9\cdot 4=\boxed{76}\text{ dollars},
\]
attained only by $(m,n)=(8,4)$.
