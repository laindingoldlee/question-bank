An ISBN is a $10$-digit code $d_1d_2\ldots d_{10}$. The check digit $d_{10}$ is the remainder of $1d_1+2d_2+\cdots+9d_9$ on division by $11$. The ISBN $0$-$8217$-$7483$-$8$ was copied with two of its first nine digits swapped (the check digit is in its correct place). Which two digits could have been swapped? Find all possibilities.
