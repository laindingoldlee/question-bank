Compare Ching's $23$ coins with Chong's first $23$ coins. By symmetry, Chong is ahead, behind, or level with probabilities $p$, $p$, $q$ where $2p+q=1$. Chong's last coin decides the level case: he wins it with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. If he is already ahead he still wins, and if he is behind he can at best tie. So
\[P(\text{Chong wins})=p+\dfrac {q}{2}=\dfrac{2p+q}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}.\]
