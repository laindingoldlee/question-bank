This question is about the expression $0^0$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0^+}x^0$ and $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0^+}0^x$.
\item[(b)] Prove that $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0^+}x^x=1$. (You may use $\ln u\le u-1$ for $u>0$.)
\item[(c)] Show that for every number $c$ with $0<c<1$ there are functions $f$ and $g$ with $f(x)\to0^+$ and $g(x)\to0^+$ as $x\to0^+$ but $f(x)^{g(x)}\to c$.
\end{enumerate}
Explain what (a)--(c) show about $0^0$.
