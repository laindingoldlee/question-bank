With three such digits there are $3^3=27$ integers. With exactly two, if the first digit is the low one it has $6$ choices ($1$--$6$), giving $6\cdot3^2=54$. If the second or third is low, it has $7$ choices ($0$--$6$), giving $2\cdot7\cdot3^2=126$. Total: $27+54+126=\boxed{207}$.
