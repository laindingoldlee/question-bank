In triangle $ABC$, $\angle ABC=40^\circ$ and $\angle ACB=80^\circ$. The bisector of angle $ABC$ meets $AC$ at $D$. The point $E$ on the ray $BD$ satisfies $BE=BA$. Prove that $AD=CE$.
