The two kinds of candy are distributed independently.

By stars and bars, the number of ways to give $5$ identical cinnamon candies to $4$ children is the number of nonnegative solutions of $c_1+c_2+c_3+c_4=5$, which is $\binom{5+3}{3}=\binom83=56$. Similarly, the butterscotch can be distributed in $\binom{6+3}{3}=\binom93=84$ ways.

By the multiplication principle, the total is
\[56\cdot84=\boxed{4704}.\]
