Lines $L_1\colon y=\sqrt3x$ and $L_2\colon x=\sqrt3y$ are given. A set $S$ of points has the property that for every point $P$ in $S$, the reflections of $P$ in $L_1$ and in $L_2$ are also in $S$. If $(6,5)\in S$, what is the smallest possible number of points in $S$?
