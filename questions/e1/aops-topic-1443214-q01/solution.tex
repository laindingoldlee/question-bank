List the heights in increasing order. If the number of players is odd, the median is the middle height, and the tallest player is at least as tall as the middle one. If it is even, the median is the average of the two middle heights, so the taller of these is at least the median, and the tallest player is at least that tall. Either way the tallest player is at least $6$ feet $4$ inches.

This is attained, for example, if every player is $6$ feet $4$ inches tall (or the players are $6$ ft $0$ in, $6$ ft $4$ in, $6$ ft $4$ in). So the answer is $\boxed{6\text{ feet }4\text{ inches}}$.
