The cooling system of a car holds $15$ litres. It is full of a mixture that is $40\%$ antifreeze. Some of the mixture is drained and replaced by pure antifreeze, so that the system is again full and now contains a $60\%$ antifreeze solution.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] How many litres must be drained and replaced?
\item[(b)] A student lets $x$ be the number of litres replaced and writes
\[0.4(15-x)+1\cdot x=0.6(15),\quad\text{then}\quad40(15-x)+10x=60(15),\quad4(15-x)+x=6(15),\quad60-3x=90,\quad x=-3 .\]
Find the errors.
\end{enumerate}
