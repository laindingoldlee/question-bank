First the values of $n?$:
\[
1?=1,\quad 2?=2!\cdot1!=2,\quad 3?=3!\cdot2!\cdot1!=12,\quad 4?=4!\cdot3!\cdot2!\cdot1!=24\cdot6\cdot2=288 .
\]
Then the values of $n\#$:
\[
1\#=1,\quad 2\#=2\cdot1=2,\quad 3\#=12\cdot2\cdot1=24,\quad 4\#=288\cdot12\cdot2\cdot1=6912 .
\]
Hence
\[
4\#\cdot3\#\cdot2\#\cdot1\#=6912\cdot24\cdot2\cdot1=\boxed{331776}\ \left(=24^4\right).
\]
