$360000=36\cdot10^4=2^6\cdot3^2\cdot5^4$. A factor $2^a3^b5^c$ is divisible by $4$ exactly when $a\ge2$, so $a\in\{2,\dots,6\}$ ($5$ choices), $b\in\{0,1,2\}$ ($3$ choices) and $c\in\{0,\dots,4\}$ ($5$ choices). There are $5\cdot3\cdot5=\boxed{75}$ such factors. (Equivalently, they are $4$ times the factors of $90000=2^4\cdot3^2\cdot5^4$, which has $5\cdot3\cdot5=75$ factors.)
