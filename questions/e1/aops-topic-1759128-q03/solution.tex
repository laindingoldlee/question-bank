Since $\pi$ radians equal $180^\circ$, $540$ radians equal $540\cdot\dfrac{180}{\pi}=\boxed{\dfrac{97200}{\pi}}$ degrees, about $30940^\circ$. (If the intended quantity was $540^\circ$, that is $3\pi$ radians.)
