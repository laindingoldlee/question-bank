\textbf{Yes.} Start both glasses at time $0$.
\begin{itemize}
\item At minute $5$ the 5-minute glass runs out; turn it over at once.
\item At minute $8$ the 8-minute glass runs out. The 5-minute glass has now run for $3$ minutes since it was turned, so it has $2$ minutes of sand left. Start the interval now.
\item At minute $10$ the 5-minute glass runs out; turn it over at once.
\item At minute $15$ it runs out again. End the interval.
\end{itemize}
The interval from minute $8$ to minute $15$ lasts $2+5=\boxed{7}$ minutes.
