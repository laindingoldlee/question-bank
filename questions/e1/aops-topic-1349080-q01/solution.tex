The area of a trapezoid is the height times the average of the bases. If the other base is $x$,
\[
8\cdot\dfrac{15+x}{2}=136\;\Longrightarrow\;15+x=34\;\Longrightarrow\;x=\boxed{19}.
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(19,0),C=(13,8),D=(-2,8),H=(13,0);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(C--H,dashed); draw((13,0)--(13,0.9)--(12.1,0.9)--(12.1,0));
label("$15$",(D+C)/2,N); label("$x$",(A+B)/2,S); label("$8$",(13,4),W);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
