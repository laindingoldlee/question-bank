Let the total be $T$. Jasmine and Jessica paid $0.4T+80$ together, and Juliet paid half of that, so
\[T=\dfrac32(0.4T+80)=0.6T+120,\]
which gives $0.4T=120$ and $T=\boxed{300}$.
