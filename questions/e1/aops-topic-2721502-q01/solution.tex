\emph{Bounds.} The blocks $p_1\dots p_4$ and $p_5\dots p_8$ together sum to $36$, so one of them is at least $18$: $M\ge18$. Four distinct entries sum to at most $5+6+7+8=26$: $M\le26$.

\emph{Every value occurs.} For example:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{cc|cc}
$M$ & permutation & $M$ & permutation\\\hline
$18$ & $2\,3\,6\,7\,1\,4\,5\,8$ & $23$ & $1\,2\,3\,7\,4\,5\,6\,8$\\
$19$ & $1\,3\,6\,7\,2\,4\,5\,8$ & $24$ & $1\,2\,3\,6\,4\,5\,7\,8$\\
$20$ & $1\,2\,6\,7\,3\,4\,5\,8$ & $25$ & $1\,2\,3\,5\,4\,6\,7\,8$\\
$21$ & $1\,2\,5\,7\,3\,4\,6\,8$ & $26$ & $1\,2\,3\,4\,5\,6\,7\,8$\\
$22$ & $1\,2\,4\,7\,3\,5\,6\,8$ & &
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
(For the first, the window sums are $18,17,18,17,18$.) So $N=18+19+\cdots+26=\boxed{\textbf{(D)}\ 198}$.
