For a permutation $p_1p_2\dots p_8$ of $1,2,\dots,8$, let $M$ be the largest of the five sums $p_k+p_{k+1}+p_{k+2}+p_{k+3}$ ($1\le k\le5$) of four consecutive entries. Let $N$ be the sum of all distinct possible values of $M$. Find $N$.

$\textbf{(A)}\ 141\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ 161\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ 180\qquad\textbf{(D)}\ 198\qquad\textbf{(E)}\ 215$
