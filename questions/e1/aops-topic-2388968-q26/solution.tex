Since $t^6=\left(t^2\right)^3$, we get $\sqrt[3]{100t^6}=\sqrt[3]{100}\cdot\sqrt[3]{\left(t^2\right)^3}=\boxed{t^2\sqrt[3]{100}}$. (No absolute value is needed: the real cube root of $\left(t^2\right)^3$ is $t^2$ for every real $t$, and $100=2^2\cdot5^2$ has no cube factor to take out.)
