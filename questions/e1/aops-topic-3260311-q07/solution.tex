After $d$ days Eddie has $15d$ fish, and Bob has $\sum_{n=1}^d(10n-1)=5d^2+4d$. Bob leads when $5d^2-11d>0$, equivalently $d>\dfrac{11}{5}$ for positive $d$. The first integer is $\boxed3$.
