Let $p$ be a positive integer.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] How many solutions does $x+y+z=p$ have in nonnegative integers?
\item[(b)] How many in positive integers?
\item[(c)] Which of the two does the basic stars-and-bars picture ($p$ stars and $2$ bars in a row) count, and how does the other count follow? Give both numbers for $p=10$.
\end{enumerate}
