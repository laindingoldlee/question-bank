Reflection in $AB$ interchanges $P,Q$, so $\angle APB=\angle AQB$. They are opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral and sum to 180 degrees; each is 90 degrees. Thus triangle $APB$ is right with hypotenuse 20 and leg 16, and $BP=\sqrt{20^2-16^2}=\boxed{12}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(20,0),P=(12.8,9.6),Q=(12.8,-9.6);draw(circle(A,16),gray);draw(circle(B,12),gray);draw(circle((10,0),10),blue);draw(A--P--B--Q--cycle);draw(A--B,dashed);label("$A$",A,W);label("$B$",B,E);label("$P$",P,N);label("$Q$",Q,S);
\end{asy}
