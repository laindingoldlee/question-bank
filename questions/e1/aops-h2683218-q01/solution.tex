Pair each odd number with its following even number. If that even endpoint has $t$ trailing zeroes, the difference of digit sums is $-1+9t$, because the increment erases $t$ nines. There are $1006$ pairs. Among the endpoints, $201$ are multiples of $10$, $20$ of $100$, and $2$ of $1000$. Counting these gives the total trailing-zero contribution, so
\[
-1006+9(201+20+2)=\boxed{1001}.
\]
