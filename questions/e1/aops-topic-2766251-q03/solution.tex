The tree array spans $4\cdot5=20$ by $11\cdot5=55$ feet. Adding 12 to each dimension gives 32 by 67, area $\boxed{2144}$ square feet.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw((-6,-6)--(61,-6)--(61,26)--(-6,26)--cycle);for(int i=0;i<12;++i)for(int j=0;j<5;++j)dot((5*i,5*j));label("$67$",(27.5,-6),S);label("$32$",(-6,10),W);
\end{asy}
