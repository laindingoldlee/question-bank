For any one of your chosen numbers, a competing team avoids it with probability $\dfrac{37}{42}$. All $m$ teams avoid it with probability $(\dfrac{37}{42})^m$. By linearity of expectation over your five numbers, your expected score is $\boxed{15(\dfrac{37}{42})^m}$. The formula does not depend on the numbers chosen, so every five-element choice has the same expected score.
