Each can holds $\dfrac{14}{2}=7$ ounces, and three gallons are $3\cdot128=384$ ounces. Since $\dfrac{384}{7}=54\dfrac67$, $54$ cans hold only $378$ ounces, while $55$ cans hold $385$ ounces. The minimum is $\boxed{55}$ cans.
