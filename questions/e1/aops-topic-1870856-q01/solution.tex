\textbf{Horner's scheme.} Write the polynomial as $\bigl(((5x+7)x+8)x+9\bigr)x+10$ and multiply by $1.98$ as ``double, then subtract $1\%$ of the double'':
\begin{align*}
5\cdot1.98+7&=9.9+7=16.9,\\
16.9\cdot1.98+8&=(33.8-0.338)+8=41.462,\\
41.462\cdot1.98+9&=(82.924-0.82924)+9=91.09476,\\
91.09476\cdot1.98+10&=(182.18952-1.8218952)+10=190.3676248 .
\end{align*}
\textbf{Check by expanding about $2$.} With $x=2-h$, $h=0.02$, one finds
\[
5x^4+7x^3+8x^2+9x+10=196-285h+170h^2-47h^3+5h^4,
\]
which gives $196-5.7+0.068-0.000376+0.0000008=190.3676248$.

The value is $\boxed{190.3676248}$.
