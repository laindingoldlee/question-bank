Let $f(x)=a_2x^2+a_1x+a_0$, where $a_2$, $a_1$ and $a_0$ are odd integers. Prove that $f$ has no rational root, and deduce that $f$ cannot be written as a product $(px+q)(rx+s)$ with integers $p,q,r,s$.
