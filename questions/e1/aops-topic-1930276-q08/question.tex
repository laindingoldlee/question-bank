Row $n$ of Pascal's triangle ($n=0,1,2,\dots$) consists of the numbers $\dbinom n0,\dbinom n1,\dots,\dbinom nn$. Prove that for all integers $n\ge1$ and $0\le k\le n-1$,
\[
\dbinom nk=\dbinom{n-k-1}{0}+\dbinom{n-k}{1}+\dbinom{n-k+1}{2}+\cdots+\dbinom{n-1}{k}.
\]
(In the triangle, the terms on the right lie on the diagonal parallel to the right side of the triangle that passes through $\dbinom{n-1}{k}$, the upper right neighbour of $\dbinom nk$. They run from the left edge of the triangle down to $\dbinom{n-1}{k}$, which lies on the same diagonal parallel to the left side as $\dbinom nk$.)
