A rectangle with side lengths $137$ and $1806$ is divided into unit squares, and $137$ sentries are placed, each in its own unit square. Each sentry guards every unit square in the same row and the same column as itself, and no sentry may stand in a square guarded by another sentry. There are $k$ ways to place the sentries. What is the remainder when $k$ is divided by $1807$?
