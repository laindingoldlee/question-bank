(a) Let $p_r=\prod_{j=1}^r\dfrac{2j}{2j+3}$ and $p_0=1$. Since $(2r+1)p_{r-1}-(2r+3)p_r=p_{r-1}$, summing gives $\sum_{r=1}^{50}p_r=2-102p_{50}<2$. Its first six terms already exceed $1$ (successive terms are $\dfrac{2}{5} , \dfrac{8}{35} , \dfrac{16}{105} , \dfrac{128}{1155} , \dfrac{256}{3003} , \dfrac{1024}{15015}$). Thus its floor is $1$. (b) The product is $\dfrac{4^{50}}{\binom{100}{50}}$. Exact integer comparison gives $12\binom{100}{50}<4^{50}<13\binom{100}{50}$, so its floor is $12$. (c) The first three factors give $\dfrac{135}{64} > 2$, while $\log(1+t)<t$ gives the full product less than $e<3$; its floor is $2$. (d) The first five factors multiply to $\dfrac{221}{72} > 3$. For $j\ge2$, $1 + \dfrac{1}{j^2} < \dfrac{j^2}{j^2-1}$, and telescoping gives the whole product less than $2 \cdot \dfrac{200}{101} < 4$. Its floor is $3$.
