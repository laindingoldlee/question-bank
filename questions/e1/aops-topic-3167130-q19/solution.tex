By Vieta, $r+s+t=0$ and $rs+rt+st=-B$, so $A=2B$. The tangent--chord theorem gives $\angle XPY=\angle XYQ$ and $\angle XYP=\angle XQY$ (using the corresponding nondegenerate triangles), hence triangles $PXY$ and $YXQ$ are similar. Their corresponding sides give $\dfrac{PX}{YX}=\dfrac{XY}{XQ}$, so $B^2=AC$. Combining with $A=2B$ gives $B=2C$ and $A=4C$. Put $C=p$, an odd prime. Then $A^B=2^{4p}p^{2p}$ has $(4p+1)(2p+1)$ divisors, while $B^A=2^{4p}p^{4p}$ has $(4p+1)^2$ divisors. Thus $D=2p(4p+1)$ and the last condition becomes $p=4p+1-40$, giving $p=13$. Therefore $\boxed{(A,B,C)=(52,26,13)}$. The displayed circle construction verifies that these lengths are realizable.
