Find positive integers $A,B,C$ satisfying all three conditions: (i) the roots $r,s,t$ of $x^3-Bx-C$ obey $r^2+s^2+t^2=A$; (ii) two circles meet at $X,Y$, with $P$ on the first circle and $PY$ tangent to the second at $Y$, and $Q$ on the second circle and $QY$ tangent to the first at $Y$, where $PX=A$, $QX=C$, and $XY=B$; (iii) $C$ is an odd prime and $C=\dfrac{D}{B}-40$, where $D$ is the positive difference between the divisor counts of $A^B$ and $B^A$.
