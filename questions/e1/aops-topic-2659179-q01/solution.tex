Number the places $1$ to $13$ from left to right. At the start the empty places are $2$, $5$ and $9$; in the target they are $5$, $9$ and $12$.

Moving one heart from place $p$ to an empty place $q$ empties $p$ and fills $q$, so the only possible move is $q=2$, $p=12$, and place $12$ does hold a heart. After moving it, the hearts occupy places $1$--$4$, $6$--$8$, $10$--$11$ and $13$, which are groups of $4,3,2,1$.

So the answer is $\boxed{\text{yes}}$: move the second-to-last heart into the first gap. (The target is the mirror image of the start, so only one gap has to move.)
