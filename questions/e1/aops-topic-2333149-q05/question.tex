Mike goes outside every $4$ days (on days $4,8,12,\dots$), goes to the grocery store once a week (on days $7,14,21,\dots$) and reads a book every $2$ days (on days $2,4,6,\dots$). On how many of the days $1,2,\dots,365$ of a year does he do at least one of these things?
