Every multiple of $4$ is even, so the days on which he goes outside are among the days on which he reads. So we count the days $n\le365$ divisible by $2$ or by $7$. By inclusion-exclusion,
\[\left\lfloor\dfrac{365}{2}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{365}{7}\right\rfloor-\left\lfloor\dfrac{365}{14}\right\rfloor=182+52-26=\boxed{208}.\]
