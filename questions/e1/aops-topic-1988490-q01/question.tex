Let $a_1=1$ and, for $n\ge2$, $a_n=\dfrac{a_{n-1}+2}{a_{n-1}+1}$. (Equivalently, if $a_{n-1}=\dfrac {p}{q}$ then $a_n=\dfrac{p+2q}{p+q}$, giving $\dfrac{1}{1},\dfrac{3}{2},\dfrac{7}{5},\dfrac{17}{12},\dots$.) Prove that $a_n$ converges to $\sqrt2$.
