Orion chooses a positive number $a$ and Michelle chooses a positive number $b$. Joshua then chooses a positive number $c$ smaller than $a$. No matter which such number Joshua chooses, the three numbers $a,b,c$ are the side lengths of a triangle.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that $a=b$.
\item[(b)] A student objects: take $a=6$ and $b=4$; Joshua can choose $c=5$, and $6,4,5$ satisfy the triangle inequality, yet $a\ne b$. What is wrong with this objection?
\end{enumerate}
