A convex octagon $ABCDEFGH$ has consecutive side lengths $6,10,8,5,12,11,7,9$. It starts above the horizontal ground, with $A$ at coordinate 0 and $B$ at $-6$. It tips clockwise, successively laying sides $HA,GH,FG,EF,DE,CD,BC,AB$ on the ground to its right. A roll means one such tip. How many rolls are first needed for the contact segment to contain ground coordinate 1434, and which side is then on the ground?
