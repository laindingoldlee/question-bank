\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that there is no smallest positive real number.
\item[(b)] Prove that for every positive real number $\varepsilon$ there is a positive integer $N$ such that $\dfrac{1}{n}<\varepsilon$ for every integer $n>N$.
\end{enumerate}
Explain how these facts answer the question ``what is $\dfrac{1}{\infty}$?''.
