The remaining volume is $\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}\pi r^3=\dfrac{4}{9}\pi r^3$. The cone volume is $\dfrac{1}{3}\pi r^2\cdot12=4\pi r^2$. Equating and dividing by positive $\dfrac{4}{9}\pi r^2$ gives $\boxed{r=9}$.
