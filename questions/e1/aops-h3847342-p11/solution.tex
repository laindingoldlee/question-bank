Write the side lengths as $a,b,c$, the circumradius as $R$, and the area as $\Delta$. Projection and the cosine rule give $EF=a\cos A$, $FD=b\cos B$, and $DE=c\cos C$ (the acute condition makes these positive). By the sine rule,
\[
p(DEF)=2R(\sin A\cos A+\sin B\cos B+\sin C\cos C).
\]
Using $A+B+C=\pi$, the identity $\sin2A+\sin2B+\sin2C=4\sin A\sin B\sin C$ gives $p(DEF)=4R\sin A\sin B\sin C$. Meanwhile $\Delta=\dfrac{abc}{4R}=2R^2\sin A\sin B\sin C$. Thus $\Delta=\dfrac{R\,p(DEF)}{2}$. With $R=\dfrac{25}{2}$,
\[
\boxed{\Delta=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{25}{2}\cdot32=200}.
\]
