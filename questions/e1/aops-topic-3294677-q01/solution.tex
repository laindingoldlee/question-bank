The two segment lines are $y=\dfrac{3}{2}(x-1)$ and $y=-\dfrac{3}{2}(x-2)$. Their intersection is $Z=(\dfrac{3}{2},\dfrac{3}{4})$. Since $XY=1$, the triangle area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot1\cdot\dfrac{3}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{8}}$ square units.

\end{asy}
