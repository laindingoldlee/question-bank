Find the value of
\[
\sqrt{5+\sqrt{6+2\sqrt{7+3\sqrt{8+4\sqrt{9+5\sqrt{10+\cdots}}}}}}\,,
\]
where the $k$th radical (counting from the outside, $k=1,2,3,\dots$) is $\sqrt{(k+4)+k\sqrt{\cdots}}$. Interpret the expression as the limit of the truncations obtained by replacing the innermost radical by $0$.
