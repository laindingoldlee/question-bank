A gumball machine contains $r$ red, $b$ blue and $g$ green gumballs, where $r,b,g\ge1$. Gumballs come out one at a time in an unknown order. What is the least number of gumballs you must get to be sure of having at least one of each colour? Give the answer in terms of $r$, $b$, $g$.
