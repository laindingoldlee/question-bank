Scale $AB$ to one and set $A=(0,0)$, $B=(1,0)$. The angle bisector is $y=x$, so $Q=(1,1)$ and the height is at least one. The diagonal makes $60^\circ$, not $30^\circ$, with $AB$, hence $C=(1,\sqrt3)$ and $O=(\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2})$. Ray $OB$ makes angle $-60^\circ$ with the positive horizontal. Ray $OQ$ has slope $2-\sqrt3=\tan15^\circ$, so its angle is $15^\circ$. Their separation is $\boxed{75^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(1,0),C=(1,sqrt(3)),D=(0,sqrt(3)),Q=(1,1),O=(.5,sqrt(3)/2);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C^^A--Q^^B--O--Q);label("$A$",A,(-1,-1));label("$B$",B,(1,-1));label("$C$",C,(1,1));label("$D$",D,(-1,1));label("$Q$",Q,(1,0));label("$O$",O,(-1,0));
\end{asy}
