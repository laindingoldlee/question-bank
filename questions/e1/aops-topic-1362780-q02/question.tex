A travel agency charges an initial fee of $100$ dollars plus a constant fee for each vertical metre climbed. A trip climbing $3000$ vertical metres costs $400$ dollars. Write a formula for the fee $F(d)$, in dollars, for a trip that climbs $d$ vertical metres.
