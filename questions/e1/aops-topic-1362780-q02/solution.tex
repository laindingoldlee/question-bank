The climbing part of the $3000$ m trip costs $400-100=300$ dollars, so the fee per metre is $\dfrac{300}{3000}=0.1$ dollar. Therefore
\[
\boxed{F(d)=100+0.1d}\qquad\left(\text{equivalently } F(d)=100+\dfrac{d}{10}\right).
\]
Check: $F(3000)=100+300=400$.
