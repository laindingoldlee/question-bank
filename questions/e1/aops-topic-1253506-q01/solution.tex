Let $e_1=x+y+z=9$, $e_2=xy+yz+zx$, $e_3=xyz$ and $p_k=x^k+y^k+z^k$. Then $p_2=e_1^2-2e_2$ gives $e_2=\dfrac{81-29}{2}=26$. Newton's identity $p_3=e_1p_2-e_2p_1+3e_3$ gives $99=261-234+3e_3$, so $e_3=24$. Finally
\[p_4=e_1p_3-e_2p_2+e_3p_1=9\cdot99-26\cdot29+24\cdot9=891-754+216=\boxed{353}.\]
(Indeed $x,y,z$ are the roots of $t^3-9t^2+26t-24=(t-2)(t-3)(t-4)$, and $2^4+3^4+4^4=353$.)
