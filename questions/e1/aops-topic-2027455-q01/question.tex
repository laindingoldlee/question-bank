A regular icosahedron is a solid with $20$ equilateral triangle faces, $30$ edges, and $12$ vertices. A single plane cuts off a pentagonal pyramid from the icosahedron. The remaining solid has $A$ vertices, $B$ edges, and $C$ faces. Find the maximum possible value of $A+B+C$.
