Four consecutive odd numbers are $2n+1,2n+3,2n+5,2n+7$ with $n\ge0$; their sum is $8n+16=8(n+2)$, always a multiple of $8$. The sums for $n=0$ and $n=1$ are $16$ and $24$, whose greatest common divisor is $8$, so no larger number divides every such sum. The answer is $\boxed{8}$.
