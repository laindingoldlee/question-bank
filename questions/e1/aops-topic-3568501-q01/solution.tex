(a) If $r=1$, all terms are $5$, giving $100\%$. If $r\ge2$, all terms after the first are products of two integers greater than $1$, giving $\dfrac{1}{16}=6.25\%$. (b) $x=r^2<7.5$ leaves $r=1,2$. At $r=1$ the second term is $5$ and $5+1$ is composite. At $r=2$ it is $10$ and $11$ is prime. Hence $\boxed{x=4}$.
