Scale AB to one and choose $A=(0,0),B=(1,0)$, with C,D,E above AB. Let $\varphi=\dfrac{1+\sqrt5}{2}$. Standard pentagon coordinates give
\[D=(\dfrac{1}{2},\sin72^\circ+\sin36^\circ),\quad E=(-\cos72^\circ,\sin72^\circ),\quad P=(-\varphi,0).\]
The exterior rhombus has Q=E-(1,0) and R=D-(1,0). Thus
\[\overrightarrow{PQ}=(\cos72^\circ,\sin72^\circ),\quad \overrightarrow{PR}=(\dfrac{\sqrt5}{2},\varphi\sin72^\circ).\]
The first vector has direction 72 degrees. For the second, $\dfrac{2\varphi\sin72^\circ}{\sqrt5}=\cot36^\circ$, using $4\varphi\sin^2 36^\circ=\sqrt5$, so its direction is 54 degrees. Their angle is $72^\circ-54^\circ=\boxed{18^\circ}$.
