The vertex on the line is $(x,4-2x)$ with $0<x<2$, so the rectangle has width $x$, height $4-2x$ and area
\[x(4-2x)=-2x^2+4x=2-2(x-1)^2\le2,\]
with equality exactly when $x=1$. The largest rectangle is $1$ by $2$, with perimeter $2(1+2)=\boxed{6}$.

label("$x$",(2.6,0),E); label("$y$",(0,4.6),N);
draw((-0.1,4.2)--(2.1,-0.2),blue+linewidth(0.9));
fill((0,0)--(1,0)--(1,2)--(0,2)--cycle,rgb(1,0.88,0.7)); draw((0,0)--(1,0)--(1,2)--(0,2)--cycle,red+linewidth(0.9));
dot((1,2)); label("$(1,2)$",(1,2),NE); label("$(x,4-2x)$",(1.6,0.8),E,fontsize(8pt));
dot((1.6,0.8));
label("$4$",(0,4),W); label("$2$",(2,0),S); label("$y=4-2x$",(0.3,3.4),E,blue);
