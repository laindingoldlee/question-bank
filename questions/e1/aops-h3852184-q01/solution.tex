Let $A=(0,-2)$, $B=(0,2)$, $C=(2\sqrt3,-2)$, and $P=(a,b)$. Then $f=PA+PB+PC$. Rotate the plane clockwise $60^\circ$ about $A$, sending $P$ to $P'$ and $C$ to $C'=(\sqrt3,-5)$. The equilateral construction gives $PP'=PA$, while $P'C'=PC$. Hence
\[
f=BP+PP'+P'C'\ge BC'=\sqrt{3+49}=2\sqrt{13}.
\]
Equality occurs when $B,P,P',C'$ lie on one line in that order. Write $P=B+t(C'-B)=(t\sqrt3,2-7t)$, and require its clockwise rotation about $A$ to lie on the same line. Substitution in the rotation matrix gives $t=\dfrac{6}{13}$ and $P'=B+\dfrac{8}{13}(C'-B)$. Both parameters lie strictly between $0$ and $1$ in the required order, so equality is attained. This linear calculation also gives the only equality configuration. Thus
\[
P=\left(\dfrac{6\sqrt3}{13},-\dfrac{16}{13}\right),\qquad
\boxed{a+b=\dfrac{6\sqrt3-16}{13}}.
\]
