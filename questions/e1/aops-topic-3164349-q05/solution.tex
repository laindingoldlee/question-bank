Let the carries from the units, tens, and hundreds columns be $u,v,w$. Column addition gives $3C=E+10u$, $I+M+u=10v$, $P+M+v=10w$, and $E+w=10P$. Since $w\le2$ and $E\le9$, the last equation forces $P=1$. The hundreds equation then cannot have $w=2$, since $1+M+v\le12$. Thus $w=1,E=9$, and $M+v=9$. The units equation forces $u=0,C=3$. If $v=2$, then $M=7,I=13$, impossible. Therefore $v=1,M=8,I=2$. The smallest unused nonzero digit for $A$ is $4$, giving $9123+9833+483=\boxed{19439}$.
