At a county fair a vendor is offering a ``fair special'' on sandals. If you buy one pair of sandals at the regular price of $50$ dollars, you get a second pair at a $40\%$ discount and a third pair at half the regular price. Javier took advantage of the ``fair special'' to buy three pairs of sandals. What percentage of the $150$ dollar regular price did he save?
