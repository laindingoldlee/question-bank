Consider the $98$ repunits $R_1=1,R_2=11,\dots,R_{98}$. By pigeonhole, two have the same remainder mod $97$: $R_j\equiv R_i$ with $i<j$. Then $97\mid R_j-R_i=R_{j-i}\cdot10^i$. Since $\gcd(10,97)=1$, $97\mid R_{j-i}$. $\blacksquare$ (The least such repunit has $96$ digits.)
