The distances $\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{1}{4},\dfrac{1}{8},\dots$ add up to the whole field, and the times form a geometric series with first term $1$ and ratio $\dfrac{2}{3}$:
\[
1+\dfrac{2}{3}+\dfrac{4}{9}+\dots=\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{2}{3}}=\boxed{3}\text{ minutes}.
\]
