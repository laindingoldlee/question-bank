Xena runs halfway across a field in $1$ minute. The next fourth of the field takes her $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of a minute to cross, the next eighth takes $\dfrac{4}{9}$ of a minute, and so on, each half of the previous distance taking $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of the previous time. How many minutes does it take Xena to cross the field?
