One factor 45 requires two factors 3 and one factor 5. In $ 45!$, $ v_3=15+5+1=21 $ and $ v_5=9+1=10 $. Therefore the greatest exponent of 45 dividing it is $\min(\lfloor\dfrac{21}{2}\rfloor,10)=\boxed{10}$.
