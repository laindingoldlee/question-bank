There are $5$ different pairs of gloves, and left and right gloves are distinguishable. In how many ways can we select $4$ of the $10$ gloves so that some $2$ of the $4$ form a pair? Also explain why the count $5\cdot\dbinom82=140$ (choose a pair, then any $2$ of the other $8$ gloves) is wrong.
