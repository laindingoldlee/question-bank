There are $\dbinom{10}{4}=210$ selections in all. A selection with no pair uses gloves from $4$ different pairs, one glove from each: $\dbinom54\cdot2^4=80$ ways. So $210-80=\boxed{130}$ selections contain a pair.

The count $5\cdot\dbinom82$ counts a selection consisting of two complete pairs twice, once for each of its pairs. There are $\dbinom52=10$ such selections, and $140-10=130$.
