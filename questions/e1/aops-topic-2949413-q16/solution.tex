The sum and difference satisfy $a_{n+1}+b_{n+1}=(a_n+b_n)^2$ and $a_{n+1}-b_{n+1}=(a_n-b_n)^2$. Therefore $a_{10}+b_{10}=6^{1024}$ and $a_{10}-b_{10}=2^{1024}>0$. The larger number is $\boxed{\dfrac{6^{1024}+2^{1024}}{2}}$.
