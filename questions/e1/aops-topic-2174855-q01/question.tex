In the PowerTen lottery, balls numbered $0$ to $9$ are drawn one at a time, each ball equally likely to be any of the ten digits, independently of the others. You pick digits, and you win if your digits match the drawn balls in the same order. There are three kinds of tickets:
\begin{itemize}
\item a \$1 ticket: pick $2$ digits; win \$10 if they match the first $2$ balls in order;
\item a \$10 ticket: pick $3$ digits; win \$10{,}000 if they match the first $3$ balls in order;
\item a \$100 ticket: pick $4$ digits; win \$10{,}000{,}000 if they match the first $4$ balls in order.
\end{itemize}
Every winning ticket is paid in full. You have exactly \$100, and you may also pool your money with friends (sharing the winnings in proportion to the money put in). Which of these is the best strategy?
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(A)] Don't buy any tickets.
\item[(B)] Buy $100$ tickets at \$1.
\item[(C)] Buy $10$ tickets at \$10.
\item[(D)] Buy $1$ ticket at \$100.
\item[(E)] Pool with friends to buy \$1{,}000{,}000 worth of \$1 tickets.
\item[(F)] Pool with friends to buy \$1{,}000{,}000 worth of \$100 tickets.
\end{enumerate}
