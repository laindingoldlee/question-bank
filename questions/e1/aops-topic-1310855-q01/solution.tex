In base $b$, $523_b=5b^2+2b+3$, and the digit $5$ requires $b\ge6$. So
\[
5b^2+2b+3=262\iff 5b^2+2b-259=0\iff (b-7)(5b+37)=0 .
\]
The positive root is $b=\boxed{7}$. Check: $5\cdot49+2\cdot7+3=245+14+3=262$.
