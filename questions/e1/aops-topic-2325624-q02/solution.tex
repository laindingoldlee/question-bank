Let $X$ be the number of items he gets. By linearity of expectation, $E(X)=69\cdot\dfrac{1}{69000}=\dfrac{1}{1000}$. Each item brings $40\,000\,000$ coins, so the expected profit is
\[\dfrac{1}{1000}\cdot40\,000\,000=\boxed{40\,000\text{ coins}}.\]
