The polygon angles at $A$ are $120^\circ$ and $108^\circ$, so the smaller angle $FAI$ outside their interiors is $360^\circ-120^\circ-108^\circ=132^\circ$. Since $AF=AI$, triangle $AFI$ has base angles $24^\circ$. Ray $FA$ lies inside angle $IFE$, so $\angle IFE=\angle IFA+\angle AFE=24^\circ+120^\circ=\boxed{144^\circ}$.
