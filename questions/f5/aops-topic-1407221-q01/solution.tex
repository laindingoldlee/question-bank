$83\dfrac{1}{3}\%=\dfrac{250}{3}\cdot\dfrac{1}{100}=\dfrac{5}{6}$. So the whole trip is $90\div\dfrac{5}{6}=108$ miles, and Bob still had $108-90=\boxed{18}$ miles to go. (Equivalently, the remaining $\dfrac{1}{6}$ of the trip is one fifth of the $90$ miles already travelled.)
