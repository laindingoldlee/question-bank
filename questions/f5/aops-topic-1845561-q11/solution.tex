The minute hand points at the $3$, i.e. $90^\circ$ from the $12$. The hour hand moves $30^\circ$ per hour, so $0.5^\circ$ per minute; at $3{:}15$ it is at $3\cdot30^\circ+15\cdot0.5^\circ=97.5^\circ$. The angle between the hands is $97.5^\circ-90^\circ=\boxed{7.5^\circ}$.
