First take out the common factor $2$:
\[4x^4-6x^2+2=2\left(2x^4-3x^2+1\right).\]
With $u=x^2$ the bracket is $2u^2-3u+1=(2u-1)(u-1)$, so
\[4x^4-6x^2+2=2\left(2x^2-1\right)\left(x^2-1\right)=\boxed{2\left(2x^2-1\right)(x-1)(x+1)}.\]
The factor $2x^2-1$ cannot be split further over the integers (its roots $\pm\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}$ are irrational); over the real numbers it would be $\left(\sqrt2\,x-1\right)\left(\sqrt2\,x+1\right)$.
