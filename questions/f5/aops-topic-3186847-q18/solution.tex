Write $f(x)=(x-h)^2-u^2$, with $u\ge 0$, since its minimum must be a real root and hence nonpositive. The root condition at $-u^2$ gives $(-u^2-h)^2=u^2$, so $h=-u^2\pm u$. Therefore $f(2022)=(2022+u^2\mp u)^2-u^2$. In either sign, $2022+u^2\mp u\ge 2021+u$, because the smaller expression differs from $2021+u$ by $(u-1)^2$. It follows that $f(2022)\ge(2021+u)^2-u^2\ge 2021^2>1$. Thus $\boxed{\text{no such quadratic exists}}$.
