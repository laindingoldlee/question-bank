Let $E$ be the expected number of rolls. The first roll is a $3$ with probability $\dfrac{1}{6}$, and then we stop; otherwise, with probability $\dfrac{5}{6}$, we are back where we started, having used one roll. So
\[
E=1+\dfrac{5}{6}E\quad\Longrightarrow\quad E=\boxed{6}.
\]
(Equivalently, $\sum_{k\ge1}k\cdot\left(\dfrac{5}{6}\right)^{k-1}\dfrac{1}{6}=\dfrac{1}{\dfrac{1}{6}}=6$.)
