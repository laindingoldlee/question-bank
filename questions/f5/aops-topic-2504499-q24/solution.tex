Let $a=\sqrt[3]x$, $b=\sqrt[3]y$: $a-b=2$ and $a^3-b^3=(a-b)(a^2+ab+b^2)=12$, so $a^2+ab+b^2=6$. Also $a^2-2ab+b^2=4$; subtracting, $3ab=2$. Hence $xy=(ab)^3=\boxed{\dfrac8{27}}$.
