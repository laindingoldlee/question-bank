Positivity forces $a=5$, while $b\in\{4,6\}$ and $c\in\{4,12\}$. The expression is twice the range of the three numbers. Its smallest range is one, attained at $(a,b,c)=(5,4,4)$. Therefore the minimum is $\boxed2$.
