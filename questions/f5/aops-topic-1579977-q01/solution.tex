\textbf{(a)} Choose the colour and then two socks of that colour:
\[
\dbinom42+\dbinom42+\dbinom22=6+6+1=\boxed{13}.
\]

\textbf{(b)} Choose the two colours and one sock of each:
\[
4\cdot4+4\cdot2+4\cdot2=16+8+8=\boxed{32}.
\]
Check: $13+32=45=\dbinom{10}{2}$, the total number of pairs.
