I have $10$ distinguishable socks in my drawer: $4$ white, $4$ brown and $2$ blue. In how many ways can I choose a pair of socks if
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] the two socks have the same colour?
\item[(b)] the two socks have different colours?
\end{enumerate}
