The determinant is $ad-bc=2\cdot21-5\cdot17=42-85=-43\ne0$, so the inverse exists and
\[
\begin{pmatrix}2&5\\17&21\end{pmatrix}^{-1}=\dfrac{1}{-43}\begin{pmatrix}21&-5\\-17&2\end{pmatrix}
=\boxed{\begin{pmatrix}-\dfrac{21}{43}&\dfrac{5}{43}\\[2mm]\dfrac{17}{43}&-\dfrac{2}{43}\end{pmatrix}}.
\]
Check: $\begin{pmatrix}2&5\\17&21\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}21&-5\\-17&2\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}-43&0\\0&-43\end{pmatrix}$.
