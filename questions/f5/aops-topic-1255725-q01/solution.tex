Let $x$ be the value (the sequence $\sqrt{10},\sqrt{10+\sqrt{10}},\dots$ is increasing and bounded by $4$, so it converges). Then $x=\sqrt{10+x}$, so $x^2-x-10=0$ and, since $x>0$,
\[x=\boxed{\dfrac{1+\sqrt{41}}{2}}\approx3.70.\]
The poster's equation $x^2-10=x$ is correct and does have real roots; it simply does not factor over the integers.
