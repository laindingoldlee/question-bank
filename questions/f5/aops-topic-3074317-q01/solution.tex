The hypothesis is $a+c-2b=0$. Substitution and factorization give $0=2y^2-x^2-z^2+xy+yz-2xz=(2y-x-z)(x+y+z)$. The second factor is nonzero, so $2y=x+z$, exactly the arithmetic-progression condition.
