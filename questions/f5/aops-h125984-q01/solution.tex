Let $x_1<\cdots<x_{10}$ be a ten-element subset without the triangle property. Then necessarily
\[
x_{i+2}\ge x_i+x_{i+1}\qquad(1\le i\le8),
\]
or the corresponding consecutive triple would form a triangle. Conversely these inequalities rule out every triple, because the two largest entries before $x_j$ have sum at most $x_j$.

The smallest possible first entries are $4,5$. The recurrence therefore gives successive lower bounds
\[
4,\ 5,\ 9,\ 14,\ 23,\ 37,\ 60,\ 97,\ 157,\ 254.
\]
Thus no triangle-free ten-element subset fits below $254$, while this displayed sequence itself is one at $n=254$. Therefore the largest desired value is $\boxed{253}$.
