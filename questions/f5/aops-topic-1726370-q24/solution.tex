Factor: $(a-b)(a+b)=100$. The factors $a-b$ and $a+b$ have the same parity (their sum $2a$ is even), and they cannot both be odd since $100$ is even, so both are even. To make $a=\dfrac{(a-b)+(a+b)}{2}$ as large as possible, take the factor pair with the largest sum: the even pairs with product $100$ are $2\cdot50$ and $10\cdot10$ (and their negatives, which give negative $a$). The pair $a-b=2$, $a+b=50$ gives $a=26$, $b=24$, and indeed $26^2-24^2=676-576=100$. The greatest value is $\boxed{26}$.
