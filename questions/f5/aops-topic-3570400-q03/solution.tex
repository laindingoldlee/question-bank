Let $F(r,b,g,L)$ count valid completions with the indicated remaining tile counts and previous color $L$. Set $F(0,0,0,L)=1$. Otherwise, sum $F$ over choices of the next color other than $L$, reducing that color's count by one; terms with negative counts are omitted. This partitions rows uniquely by their next tile and therefore neither misses nor repeats a row. The first-tile contributions are $F(3,3,2,R)=45$, $F(4,2,2,B)=21$, and $F(4,3,1,G)=13$. Their sum is $\boxed{79}$.
