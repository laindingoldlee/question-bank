Among the five known faces, two complete opposite pairs must have equal sums. Listing pair sums, the only repeated sum from disjoint pairs is $13+24=15+22=37$. Therefore the face opposite $3$ is $37-3=\boxed{34}$. The displayed cube net verifies that these three pairs can be opposite.
