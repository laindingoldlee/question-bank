Bob bumped his head, and now whenever he tries to plot a point $(a,b)$ he plots $(b,a)$ instead. His textbook asks him to graph the line $y=3x+2$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Sketch the graph Bob produces together with the correct line. What are the slopes of the two lines?
\item[(b)] Find an equation of the line Bob draws.
\item[(c)] If Bob tries to graph a line with slope $m\ne0$, what is the slope of the line he draws, in terms of $m$? What happens if $m=0$, or if he tries to graph a vertical line?
\end{enumerate}
