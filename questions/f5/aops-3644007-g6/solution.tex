The triangle area is always $3\cdot\dfrac{2}{2}=3$. Its inradius is area divided by semiperimeter, so maximise it by minimising $AE+BE$. Reflection or convexity shows this sum is least when E is the midpoint of CD. Then both legs are $\dfrac{5}{2}$, the semiperimeter is four and the inradius $\dfrac{3}{4}$. Thus the largest incircle area is $\boxed{\dfrac{9\pi}{16}}$.
