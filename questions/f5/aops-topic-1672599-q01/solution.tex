Let the number be $\overline{abc}_7=\overline{cba}_9$, with digits $0\le a,b,c\le6$ and leading digits $a,c\ge1$. Then
\[
49a+7b+c=81c+9b+a\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad48a=2b+80c\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad b=24a-40c=8(3a-5c).
\]
So $b$ is a multiple of $8$ between $0$ and $6$, hence $b=0$ and $3a=5c$. With $1\le a,c\le6$ this forces $a=5$, $c=3$. The number is
\[
\boxed{503_7}=5\cdot49+3=248=3\cdot81+5=305_9 ,
\]
and it is the only one.
