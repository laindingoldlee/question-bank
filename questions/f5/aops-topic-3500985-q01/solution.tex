Cross-multiplication gives $(40+3a)(24+b)=(48+3b)(20+a)$, hence $24a=20b$, or $6a=5b$. Coprimality of $5,6$ gives $a=5k,b=6k$ for a positive integer $k$. The minimum sum is $\boxed{11}$.
