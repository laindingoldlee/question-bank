Since $\angle B=60^\circ$, the sine rule gives $BC=6\sqrt2\,\dfrac{\sin75^\circ}{\sin60^\circ}=6\sqrt2\,\dfrac{\sqrt6+\sqrt2}{2\sqrt3}=\boxed{6+2\sqrt3}$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(0,0),C=(6*sqrt(2),0),B=intersectionpoint(A--(10*dir(75)),C--(C+10*dir(135)));draw(A--B--C--cycle);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,N);label("$C$",C,SE);
\end{asy}
