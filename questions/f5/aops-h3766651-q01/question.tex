A quadruple of nonnegative integers $(a,b,c,d)$ is called $81$-compact when $\lfloor\dfrac{a}{81}\rfloor=\lfloor\dfrac{b}{81}\rfloor=\lfloor\dfrac{c}{81}\rfloor=\lfloor\dfrac{d}{81}\rfloor$. Let $N$ be the number of such ordered quadruples with every entry at most $3^{10}$ and
\[
a^9+b^9+c^9+d^9\equiv0\pmod{3^{10}},\qquad
a^8+b^8+c^8+d^8\equiv0\pmod{81}.
\]
Find $N$ modulo $1000$.
