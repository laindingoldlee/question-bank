Put $t=\sqrt[4]5>1$. Using $t^4=5$, expansion gives
\[
(t^3-t^2+t-1)^2=4(4-3t+2t^2-t^3).
\]
The expression $t^3-t^2+t-1=(t-1)(t^2+1)$ is positive, so
\[
x=t^3-t^2+t-1,\qquad (t+1)x=t^4-1=4.
\]
Hence $t=\dfrac{4}{x}-1$. Raising to the fourth power gives $(4-x)^4=5x^4$. Expanding and dividing by $-4$ yields
\[
x^4+4x^3-24x^2+64x-64=0.
\]
The desired value is $\boxed{64}$.
