\textbf{Yes.} Start both hourglasses at time $0$.
\begin{itemize}
\item At minute $7$ the 7-minute glass runs out: put the pizza in the oven. The 11-minute glass has $4$ minutes left.
\item At minute $11$ the 11-minute glass runs out. The pizza has cooked $4$ minutes. Turn the 11-minute glass over at once.
\item At minute $22$ it runs out again. Take the pizza out.
\end{itemize}
The pizza cooked from minute $7$ to minute $22$, which is $4+11=\boxed{15}$ minutes.
