The coefficients are Catalan numbers $C_n=\dfrac{\binom{2n}n}{n+1}$. Their generating function satisfies $C(z)=1+zC(z)^2$ by first-return decomposition, hence $C(z)=\dfrac{1-\sqrt{1-4z}}{2z}$ for $0<z<\dfrac{1}{4}$. As $z$ increases to $\dfrac{1}{4}$, the nonnegative series increases to the requested series, while the formula tends to 2. Thus the sum is $\boxed2$. This argument also establishes convergence at the endpoint.
