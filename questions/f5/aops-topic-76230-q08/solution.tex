\textbf{Total.} The hundreds digit is any of the $6$ nonzero digits, then the tens digit any of the $6$ remaining digits, then the units digit any of the $5$ remaining: $6\cdot6\cdot5=180$ numbers.

\textbf{Favourable.} A number is divisible by $4$ exactly when its last two digits form a multiple of $4$. The multiples of $4$ from $00$ to $99$ that use two different digits from the list are
\[
08,\ 16,\ 36,\ 60,\ 68,\ 76,\ 80,\ 96 .
\]
The number exceeds $300$ exactly when its hundreds digit is $3,6,7,8$ or $9$ (no number $300$ can be formed), and the hundreds digit must differ from the last two digits. The numbers of choices are $4,4,3,4,3,3,4,3$ for the eight endings above (for example, ending $36$ allows $7,8,9$), in total $28$.

The probability is $\dfrac{28}{180}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{45}}$. (The answer $\dfrac{3}{20}=\dfrac{27}{180}$ suggested in the post is one short.)
