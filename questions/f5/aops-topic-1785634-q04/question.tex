A fly is at the centre of a cubical room whose walls (edges) have length $3$ feet. The fly travels $8$ inches up, then $6$ inches to its left (horizontally, parallel to two of the walls), then $2\sqrt2$ inches down. It never hits a wall. After it moves, the distance from the fly to each of the six faces of the room (four walls, floor and ceiling) is measured. What is the average of these six distances?
