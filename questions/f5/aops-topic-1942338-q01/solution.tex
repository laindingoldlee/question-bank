The base is $100\text{ cm}\times100\text{ cm}=10000\text{ cm}^2$, which is $100$ regions of $10\text{ cm}\times10\text{ cm}$. So the pan receives $2\cdot100=200$ cubic centimeters per second. Converting the time with unit fractions,
\[
200\ \dfrac{\text{cm}^3}{\text{second}}\times60\ \dfrac{\text{seconds}}{\text{minute}}\times60\ \dfrac{\text{minutes}}{\text{hour}}=720000\ \dfrac{\text{cm}^3}{\text{hour}} .
\]
(Each factor $\dfrac{60\text{ seconds}}{1\text{ minute}}$ equals $1$, so multiplying by it changes the units, not the amount.) The depth is the volume divided by the base area:
\[
\dfrac{720000\text{ cm}^3}{10000\text{ cm}^2}=\boxed{72}\text{ cm}.
\]
