Assume that a raindrop has volume $1$ cubic centimeter. Rain falls at a rate of $2$ raindrops per second on each $10$ cm by $10$ cm region. What is the depth of water, in centimeters, in a pan with a square base measuring $1$ meter by $1$ meter (and vertical sides) after $1$ hour of rain?
