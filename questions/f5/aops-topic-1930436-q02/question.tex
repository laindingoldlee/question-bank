Sarah is making a sandwich. She has $5$ types of meat (ham, salami, turkey, baloney and roast beef) and $3$ types of cheese (Swiss, American and provolone). She wants $2$ different meats and $1$ cheese, but she hates salami together with American cheese, and roast beef together with baloney. How many different sandwiches can she make that she will like?
