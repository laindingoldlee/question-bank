Without restrictions there are $\dbinom52\cdot3=10\cdot3=30$ sandwiches.

Sandwiches with salami and American cheese: salami plus one of the other $4$ meats, with American: $4$.

Sandwiches with roast beef and baloney: that pair of meats with any of the $3$ cheeses: $3$.

No sandwich is in both groups (the second group has no salami). So she likes $30-4-3=\boxed{23}$ sandwiches.
