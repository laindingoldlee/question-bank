A student solves $x^{x^{x^{\cdot^{\cdot^{\cdot}}}}}=4$ by substitution: since the tower in the exponent is the whole tower again, $x^4=4$, so $x=\sqrt2$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Let $a_1=\sqrt2$ and $a_{k+1}=\sqrt2^{\,a_k}$, so that the tower with $x=\sqrt2$ is the limit of $a_k$. Prove that $a_k$ is increasing and $a_k<2$ for all $k$, and find the value of the tower.
\item[(b)] Conclude that the equation $x^{x^{x^{\cdot^{\cdot^{\cdot}}}}}=4$ has no positive real solution. Where does the student's argument go wrong?
\end{enumerate}
