The reciprocal denominators must agree. On the left, the inner fraction equals $\dfrac{2}{3}$. On the right, $2+\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{5}{2}$, then $1-\dfrac{2}{5}=\dfrac{3}{5}$, and the full denominator is $2+\dfrac{5}{3}=\dfrac{11}{3}$. Thus $x+\dfrac{2}{3}=\dfrac{11}{3}$ and $\boxed{x=3}$.
