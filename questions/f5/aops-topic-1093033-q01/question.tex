Alice, Bob and Carol each think of an expression of the form $\dfrac{1}{kx^n}$, where $k$ is a positive integer and $n$ is a nonnegative integer. The simplest common denominator of Alice's and Bob's expressions is $4x^2$, that of Bob's and Carol's expressions is $12x^3$, and that of Alice's and Carol's expressions is $6x^3$. Find all possible expressions that could be Carol's expression.
