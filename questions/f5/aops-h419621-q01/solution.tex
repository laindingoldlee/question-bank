Take $CD$ as base. Its length is $6$ miles and the perpendicular distance from $A$ to its line is $3$ miles. Hence the triangular area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot6\cdot3=\boxed9$ square miles, choice E.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(0,3),C=(3,0),D=(3,6),H=(3,3);draw(A--C--D--cycle);draw(A--H,dashed);label("A",A,W);label("C",C,SE);label("D",D,NE);label("H",H,E);label("3",(A+H)/2,N);label("6",(3,4.5),E);
\end{asy}
