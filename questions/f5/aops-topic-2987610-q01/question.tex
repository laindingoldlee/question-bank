Rachel decorates a closed rectangular box measuring $12$ in by $16$ in by $8$ in. She covers five of its six faces exactly with congruent, non-overlapping square pieces of fabric; each square lies flat on a single face and pieces are not cut or folded. What is the fewest number of fabric squares she needs?
