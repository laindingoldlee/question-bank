Compute the sequence modulo $21$:
\[F_0,F_1,\dots,F_{17}\equiv1,1,2,3,5,8,13,0,13,13,5,18,2,20,1,0,1,1.\]
Since $F_{16}\equiv F_0$ and $F_{17}\equiv F_1$, and each term is determined by the two before it, the sequence modulo $21$ repeats with period $16$. As $2021=16\cdot126+5$, we get $F_{2021}\equiv F_5=\boxed{8}\pmod{21}$.
