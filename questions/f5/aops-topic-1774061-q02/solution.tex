Iguanas 1 and 4 make opposite statements, so exactly one of them tells the truth. Since there is only one truthful iguana, iguanas 2, 3, 5 and 6 all lie. Iguana 2 lying means road $3$ leads to the airport (and indeed iguanas 3, 5, 6 then lie, as roads $5$, $2$, $4$ do not). Then road $1$ does not, so iguana 4 is the truthful one. The sum is $4+3=\boxed{7}$.
