The sum of two dice is at least $2$, while the sum of three is at most $18$. The required two-die sum must therefore be $2$, and the three-die sum $16$. The two dice are both $1$; the other three form either $(4,6,6)$ or $(5,5,6)$ in any order. There are $\binom52=10$ choices of the ones and $3+3=6$ orders of the other values, giving $60$ outcomes. The probability is $\dfrac{60}{6^5}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{648}}$.
