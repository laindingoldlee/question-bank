The three new terms are symmetric about the middle term, which is the average of the ends, $\dfrac{3}{8}$. So their sum is $3\cdot\dfrac{3}{8}=\boxed{\dfrac{9}{8}}$. (They are $\dfrac{5}{16},\dfrac{3}{8},\dfrac{7}{16}$.)
