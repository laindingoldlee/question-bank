At a party there are $20$ married couples. Every man shakes hands with everyone except himself and his wife. Ten of the women shake hands with no other woman, and the other ten women all shake hands with each other. A student counts $\dbinom{20}{2}$ handshakes among the men, $\dbinom{20}{2}$ between men and women, and $\dbinom{10}{2}$ among the women, getting $425$. Find the error and the correct total.
