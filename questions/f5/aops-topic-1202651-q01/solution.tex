The man--woman count is wrong: each of the $20$ men shakes hands with $19$ women (all but his wife), giving $20\cdot19=380$ handshakes, not $\dbinom{20}{2}=190$. The other counts are right: $\dbinom{20}{2}=190$ among the men and $\dbinom{10}2=45$ among the ten sociable women. The total is
\[190+380+45=\boxed{615}.\]
