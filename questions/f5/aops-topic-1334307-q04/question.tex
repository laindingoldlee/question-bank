A circular running track is $10$ metres wide and is divided into ten concentric lanes, each $1$ metre wide. Each runner runs along the centre line of their lane, and the runner in the innermost lane runs exactly one lap. For the race to be fair, how much of a head start should the runner in the outermost lane be given compared with the innermost runner? Express your answer in terms of $\pi$.
