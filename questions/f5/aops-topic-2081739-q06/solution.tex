A third of the cup is \(\dfrac{16}{3}\) ounces. Track the amount:
\[
\dfrac{16}{3}\ \xrightarrow{\text{Benny}}\ \dfrac{8}{3}+\dfrac{16}{3}=8\ \xrightarrow{\text{Becky}}\ 4+\dfrac{16}{3}=\dfrac{28}{3}\ \xrightarrow{\text{Bob}}\ \dfrac{14}{3}+\dfrac{16}{3}=10.
\]
The cup holds \(\boxed{10}\) ounces.
