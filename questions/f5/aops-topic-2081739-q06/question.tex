A \(16\)-ounce cup is filled \(\dfrac{1}{3}\) of the way with water. Benny pours out half of the water and then adds water filling another \(\dfrac{1}{3}\) of the cup. Becky does the same (pours out half, adds \(\dfrac{1}{3}\) of a cup), and then Bob does the same. How many ounces of water are in the cup now?
