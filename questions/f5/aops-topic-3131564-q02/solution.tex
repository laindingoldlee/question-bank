Place $A=(0,0),B=(18,0)$ and the other vertices above $AB$. The angle conditions give $D=(6,6),C=(13,5)$. Hence $CD=\sqrt{7^2+1^2}=\boxed{5\sqrt2}$.

usepackage("amsmath");

\end{asy}
