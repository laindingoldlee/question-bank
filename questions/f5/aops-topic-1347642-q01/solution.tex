Use the complement. The probability that a given friend does not win is $\dfrac{7}{8}$, so by independence the probability that none of them wins is $\left(\dfrac{7}{8}\right)^4=\dfrac{2401}{4096}$. Therefore
\[
P(\text{at least one wins})=1-\dfrac{2401}{4096}=\boxed{\dfrac{1695}{4096}}\approx0.414 .
\]
