Put $g=a_7,h=a_8,k=a_{11}$ and $S=g+h+k$. The first six terms sum to at most $(g-6)+\cdots+(g-1)=6g-21$, and $a_9+a_{10}\le2k-3$. Hence $995\le7g+h+3k-24=3S+4g-2h-24\le3S+2g-26$. Also $h\ge g+1$ and $k\ge g+4$, so $S\ge3g+5$ and $g\le\dfrac{S-5}{3}$. Therefore $995\le\dfrac{11S-88}{3}$, giving $S\ge\dfrac{3073}{11}>279$. Since $S$ is integral, $S\ge280$. This is attained by $(84,86,87,88,89,90,91,92,95,96,97)$, whose sum is $995$ and whose designated terms sum to $280$. The minimum average is $\boxed{\dfrac{280}{3}}$.
