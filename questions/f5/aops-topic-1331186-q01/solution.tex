Expanding, $x^2-y^2=12$ and $2xy=-5$. Taking moduli, $x^2+y^2=|12-5i|=13$. Hence $x^2=\dfrac{25}{2}$ and $y^2=\dfrac{1}{2}$, so
\[
\dfrac{x^2}{y^4}=\dfrac{\dfrac{25}{2}}{\dfrac{1}{4}}=\boxed{50}.
\]
(The two square roots of $12-5i$ are $\pm\left(\dfrac{5}{\sqrt2}-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}i\right)$, and both give the same value.)
