Find the least common denominator of the fractions
\[
\dfrac{ax^2}{a^2-x^2}\qquad\text{and}\qquad\dfrac{2ax}{a^2+ax},
\]
and write $\dfrac{ax^2}{a^2-x^2}-\dfrac{2ax}{a^2+ax}$ as a single fraction in lowest terms. (Assume $a\ne0$ and $x\ne\pm a$.)
