\textbf{Factor the denominators.}
\[
a^2-x^2=(a-x)(a+x),\qquad a^2+ax=a(a+x).
\]
The least common denominator must contain every factor to the highest power in which it appears: $a$, $a+x$ and $a-x$. So the LCD is
\[
\boxed{a(a+x)(a-x)} .
\]

\textbf{The difference.}
\[
\dfrac{ax^2}{(a-x)(a+x)}-\dfrac{2ax}{a(a+x)}=\dfrac{a\cdot ax^2-2ax(a-x)}{a(a+x)(a-x)}=\dfrac{a\left(ax^2-2ax+2x^2\right)}{a(a+x)(a-x)}=\dfrac{ax^2-2ax+2x^2}{(a+x)(a-x)} .
\]
The numerator $x(ax-2a+2x)$ has no factor in common with $(a+x)(a-x)$ in general, so this is in lowest terms:
\[
\dfrac{ax^2}{a^2-x^2}-\dfrac{2ax}{a^2+ax}=\dfrac{x(ax+2x-2a)}{a^2-x^2}.
\]
