Dr.~Pisani exercises his dog Sam on a walk by throwing a stick that Sam chases and brings back. Dr.~Pisani walks in a straight line at a constant speed $v$. Each time, he throws the stick to land at the same distance $d$ from where he stands; Sam starts from Dr.~Pisani at the moment of the throw, runs at a constant speed $u>v$ straight to the stick, and then runs straight to the point where he meets Dr.~Pisani again. To keep Sam running for the longest time per throw, in which direction should Dr.~Pisani throw the stick?

$\textbf{(A) }$In front of him$\qquad\textbf{(B) }$Behind him$\qquad\textbf{(C) }$Sideways$\qquad\textbf{(D) }$Any direction, all are equivalent

A student argues for (C): ``When the stick is thrown sideways, Sam runs sideways and then along the hypotenuse of a right triangle, which is longest.'' Find the correct answer and explain what is wrong with this argument.
