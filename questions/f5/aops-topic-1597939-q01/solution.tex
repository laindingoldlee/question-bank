From the second equation, $y=54-7x-z$. Substitute into the other two:
\[
2x+3(54-7x-z)+5z=64\iff-19x+2z=-98,
\]
\[
4x-6(54-7x-z)+3z=24\iff46x+9z=348 .
\]
From the first of these, $z=\dfrac{19x-98}{2}$. Then
\[
46x+\dfrac{9(19x-98)}{2}=348\iff92x+171x-882=696\iff263x=1578\iff x=6 .
\]
So $z=\dfrac{114-98}{2}=8$ and $y=54-42-8=4$. The unique solution is
\[
\boxed{(x,y,z)=(6,4,8)}.
\]
Check: $12+12+40=64$, $42+4+8=54$, $24-24+24=24$.
