Tanish and Ryan are having an archery battle! Tanish goes first and then they alternate turns. On each turn, both Ryan and Tanish have a $\dfrac{1}{5}$ probability of hitting the bullseye and winning, and a $\dfrac{1}{10}$ probability of missing the target and losing the battle. The probability that Tanish wins the archery battle can be expressed as $\dfrac{a}{b}$, where $a$ and $b$ are relatively prime. Find $a + b$.
