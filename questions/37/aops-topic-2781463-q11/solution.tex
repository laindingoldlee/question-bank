Axis-parallel squares contribute $3^2+2^2+1^2=14$. A tilted square has a uniquely chosen upward-right side vector $(a,b)$ with positive integers $a,b$ and $a+b\le3$. Its bounding box has side $a+b$, allowing $(4-a-b)^2$ translations. The pairs $(1,1),(1,2),(2,1)$ contribute $4+1+1=6$. Total $\boxed{20}$.
