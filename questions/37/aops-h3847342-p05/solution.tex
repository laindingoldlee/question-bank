Write $AB=c$, $AC=b$, $BC=a$. Projection onto the two rays gives $AE=c\cos60^\circ=\dfrac{c}{2}$ and $AF=b\cos60^\circ=\dfrac{b}{2}$, whether or not a foot lies beyond a side. The included angle $EAF$ is $60^\circ$, so triangle $AEF$ is similar to triangle $ABC$ with the two adjacent sides interchanged and scale factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$. In particular $EF=\dfrac{a}{2}$. Thus the perimeter ratio is $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and the answer is $\boxed{105}$.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

\end{asy}
