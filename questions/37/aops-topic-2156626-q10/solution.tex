Let the ages be $a$ and $b$, with $a+b=17$. Since
\[a^3+b^3=(a+b)^3-3ab(a+b),\]
we get $1241=4913-51ab$, so $51ab=3672$ and $ab=\boxed{72}$. (The ages are $9$ and $8$: $729+512=1241$.)
