There are $25+80=105$ pieces. Each non-vertex straight cut adds four to the total side count: two new cut edges plus two splits of old edges. The final side count is $25\cdot4+80\cdot4=420$. If $H$ pieces are hexagons, the remaining $105-H$ are triangles, so $6H+3(105-H)=420$. Therefore $\boxed{H=35}$.
