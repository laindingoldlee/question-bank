The purity of gold is measured in karats: $24$-karat gold is pure gold, and $k$-karat gold is $\dfrac{k}{24}$ gold by mass. A piece of $16$-karat jewellery has a mass of $30$ grams and a piece of $10$-karat jewellery has a mass of $40$ grams. Find the positive difference, in grams, between the amounts of pure gold in the two pieces. Express your answer as a mixed number.
