The first piece contains $\dfrac{16}{24}\cdot30=20$ grams of gold, the second $\dfrac{10}{24}\cdot40=\dfrac{50}{3}=16\dfrac{2}{3}$ grams. The difference is
\[
20-16\dfrac{2}{3}=\boxed{3\dfrac{1}{3}}\text{ grams}.
\]
