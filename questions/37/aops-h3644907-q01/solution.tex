For even $n$, pair complementary numbers summing to $2n+1$; the product $(2n+1)^n$ is a square. For odd $n\ge3$, first pair $(1,5),(2,4),(3,6)$, whose sums have product $6\cdot6\cdot9=18^2$. Pair the remaining numbers complementarily with sum $2n+7$; there are $n-3$ such pairs, an even number. For $n=1$, the only sum is $3$, not a square. Thus precisely $n=2,\ldots,37$ work: $\boxed{36}$.
