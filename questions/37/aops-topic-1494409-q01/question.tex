Triangle $ABC$ has an obtuse angle at $C$. An \emph{altitude} is the segment from a vertex perpendicular to the line containing the opposite side; let $D$, $E$, $F$ be the feet of the altitudes from $A$, $B$, $C$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that $D$ lies on line $BC$ beyond $C$ (not on side $BC$), and $E$ lies on line $AC$ beyond $C$.
\item[(b)] Prove that the lines $AD$, $BE$, $CF$ pass through one point $H$ (the orthocentre), and that $H$ lies outside the triangle.
\item[(c)] Show that $C$ is the orthocentre of triangle $ABH$.
\end{enumerate}
