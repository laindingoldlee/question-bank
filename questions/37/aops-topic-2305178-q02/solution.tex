Write $z=a+bi$ with real $a,b$. Then $z^2=a^2-b^2+2abi$, so
\[a^2-b^2=33,\qquad 2ab=-56.\]
Also $|z|^2=|z^2|$, i.e. $a^2+b^2=\sqrt{33^2+56^2}=\sqrt{4225}=65$. Adding and subtracting, $a^2=49$ and $b^2=16$, so $a=\pm7$ and $b=\pm4$, and $ab=-28<0$ means the signs are opposite. Hence
\[z=\boxed{7-4i\ \text{ or }\ -7+4i}.\]
(Check: $(7-4i)^2=49-56i-16=33-56i$.) The principal square root, the one with positive real part, is $7-4i$.
