Let $A=\sum_{k=1}^{100}\lfloor\dfrac{10000}{k}\rfloor$ and $T=\sum_{k=1}^{10000}\lfloor\dfrac{10000}{k}\rfloor$. The latter counts ordered positive integer pairs $(u,v)$ with $uv\le10000$. Every such pair has $u\le100$ or $v\le100$; each of these sets has size $A$, and their intersection is the entire $100\times100$ square. Thus $T=2A-10000$. The requested difference is $A-(T-A)=2A-T=\boxed{10000}$.
