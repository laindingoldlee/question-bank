Apply the rule step by step:
\[
f(1)=1+2=3,\quad f(2)=3+3=6,\quad f(3)=6+4=10,\quad f(4)=10+5=15,\quad f(5)=15+6=\boxed{21}.
\]
(In general $f(n)=1+2+\cdots+(n+1)=\dfrac{(n+1)(n+2)}{2}$, a triangular number.)
