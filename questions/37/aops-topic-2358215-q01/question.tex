A tribe has $50$ members, ranked $1$ (least worthy) to $50$ (most worthy). In each round, all current members vote on whether to expel the least worthy current member. If more than half of the current members vote yes, that member is expelled and a new round begins; otherwise the game ends. Every member's first priority is to stay in the tribe; subject to that, each member wants the final tribe to be as small as possible. All members reason perfectly, and all of this is known to everyone. How many members remain at the end?
