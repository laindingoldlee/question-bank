The $k$th line meets at most $k-1$ previous lines, dividing its chord into at most $k$ pieces and creating at most $k$ new regions. The bound is attained by choosing lines with every pairwise intersection inside the disk, no parallel lines and no three concurrent. Thus the answer is $1+\sum_{k=1}^{67}k=1+\dfrac{67\cdot68}{2}=\boxed{2279}$.
