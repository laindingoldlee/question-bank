Ten ants are placed anywhere on a thin bar $1$ metre long, each facing either end, and all walk at $1$ metre per minute. When two ants meet, both turn around; when an ant reaches an end of the bar, it falls off. Show that all the ants are guaranteed to have fallen off after $1$ minute, and that no shorter time works for every starting arrangement.
