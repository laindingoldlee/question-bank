When two ants meet and turn around, the picture is the same as if they had passed through each other, except that the ants exchange labels. So the set of positions at every moment is the same as for ten ``ghost'' ants that walk straight without turning. Each ghost reaches an end within $1$ minute (it walks at most the full length of the bar), so after $1$ minute no ant is left on the bar.

No smaller time works in general: an ant starting very close to one end and walking toward the other end (with all other ants arranged arbitrarily) corresponds to a ghost that needs almost the full minute. So the guaranteed time is $\boxed{1\text{ minute}}$.
