Both gift counts are integers, so the initial total is $55k$. The remaining fraction is $1-\dfrac{2}{5}-\dfrac{6}{11}=\dfrac{3}{55}$, leaving $3k$ balls. This is a positive multiple of 9 exactly when $k$ is a positive multiple of 3. The minimum is $55\cdot3=\boxed{165}$.
