Let $ABCD$ be a unit square. If $E$ is the midpoint of $AB$ and $F$ lies inside $ABCD$ such that $CFD$ is an equilateral triangle, the positive difference between the area of $CED$ and $CFD$ can be expressed in the form $\dfrac{a-\sqrt{b}}{c}$ , where $a,b,c$ are positive integers, $b$ is squarefree, and $\gcd(a,c)=1$. What is $a + b + c$?
