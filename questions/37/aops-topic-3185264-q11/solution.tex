Triangle $CED$ has base $CD=1$ and altitude $1$, so its area is $\dfrac{1}{2}$. The equilateral triangle $CFD$ has area $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}$. Their positive difference is $\dfrac{2-\sqrt3}{4}$, so $a+b+c=\boxed9$.

\end{asy}
