William's clock breaks at exactly 12:00 PM, when its minute hand points the same way as that of a correct clock. From then on its minute hand turns at half the normal speed, and in addition, at the end of every real hour (1:00 PM, 2:00 PM, \ldots) the minute hand instantly jumps forward by $10$ minutes on the dial (that is, by $60^\circ$); at those moments the position just after the jump counts. Not counting 12:00 PM itself, through how many degrees in total (jumps included) has the broken minute hand turned at the moment when its minute hand points the same way as the minute hand of a correct clock for the $20$th time?
