Collect by degree. $x^3$: $9+162+72-243=0$. $x^2$: $2+81-2+162-243=0$. $x$: $-46-614+69+209+162-23+243=0$. The constant is $-23\cdot(-23)=529$. The value is $\boxed{529}$ for every $x$.
