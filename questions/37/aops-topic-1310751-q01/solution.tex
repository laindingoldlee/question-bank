$72=2^3\cdot3^2$, and the sum of divisors is multiplicative:
\[
\sigma(72)=(1+2+4+8)(1+3+9)=15\cdot13=\boxed{195}.
\]
(Expanding the product lists every divisor $2^i3^j$ exactly once.)
