In an equilateral triangle $ ABC $ of side 4, $ M $ is the midpoint of $ AB $ and $ BN:NC=1:2 $. Write $[BNM]=\dfrac{k\sqrt m}{n}$ in reduced form with $ m $ squarefree, and find $ k+m+n $.
