The triangle is right at $A$. Its perpendicular projection $H$ onto $BC$ satisfies $BH=\dfrac{AB^2}{BC}=\dfrac{25}{13}$ and $HC=\dfrac{144}{13}$. The two square regions have common height $13$, so their areas are $25,144$. The difference is $\boxed{119}$.
