If the sides are $x$ and $40-x$, the area is $x(40-x)=400-(x-20)^2\le400$, with equality when $x=20$. So the best rectangle is a $20$ by $20$ square, with area $\boxed{400}$ square feet.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((0,0)--(20,0)--(20,20)--(0,20)--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); label("$20$",(10,0),S); label("$20$",(20,10),E);
draw((26,4)--(56,4)--(56,14)--(26,14)--cycle,gray); label("$30$",(41,4),S); label("$10$",(56,9),E); label("area $300$",(41,9));label("area $400$",(10,10));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
