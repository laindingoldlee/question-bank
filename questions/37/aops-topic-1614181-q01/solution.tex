The units digit of a product depends only on the units digits of the factors. Starting from $7$ and multiplying by $7$ each time, the units digits are
\[
7,\quad 9\ (49),\quad 3\ (343),\quad 1\ (2401),\quad 7,\ \dots
\]
and the cycle $7,9,3,1$ repeats with period $4$. So the units digits $1,3,7,9$ all occur, but $5$ never does (also: $7^n$ is not divisible by $5$). The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(C)}\ 5}$.
