We need $a\ne0$ and $b\ne0$. Multiply by $4ab$:
\[
4b-4a=ab\iff ab+4a-4b=0\iff (a-4)(b+4)=-16 .
\]
(Check: $(a-4)(b+4)=ab+4a-4b-16$.) So $a-4=d$ and $b+4=-\dfrac{16}{d}$ for a divisor $d$ of $16$, positive or negative. There are $10$ such divisors, $\pm1,\pm2,\pm4,\pm8,\pm16$:
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccccccccc}
d&1&2&4&8&16&-1&-2&-4&-8&-16\\\hline
a&5&6&8&12&20&3&2&0&-4&-12\\
b&-20&-12&-8&-6&-5&12&4&0&-2&-3
\end{array}
\]
The case $d=-4$ gives $a=b=0$, which is not allowed. The other $9$ pairs all work, so the answer is $\boxed{9}$.
