Write the digits as $abc dcba$. Divisibility by 11 requires $2a-2b+2c-d$ to be a multiple of 11. Try the lexicographically largest first three digits $a=b=c=9$. Then $18-d$ must be a multiple of 11 with $0\le d\le9$, forcing $d=7$. Hence $\boxed{9997999}$ qualifies and is largest: any smaller first-three-digit prefix produces a smaller number, and no larger middle digit works with prefix 999.
