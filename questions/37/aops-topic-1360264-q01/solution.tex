The odd integers $17,19,\dots,99$ form an arithmetic sequence with $\dfrac{99-17}{2}+1=42$ terms and average $\dfrac{17+99}{2}=58$, so their sum is $42\cdot58=2436$. After one is removed, $41$ numbers with average $59$ have sum $41\cdot59=2419$. The removed integer is $2436-2419=\boxed{17}$.
