By the order of operations the expression is $\dfrac{83x}{31}+7x$, and the two terms are like terms:
\[\dfrac{83x}{31}+7x=\dfrac{83x}{31}+\dfrac{217x}{31}=\dfrac{300x}{31}.\]
With a calculator, $x=e^{e}\approx15.15426$, so
\[\dfrac{300x}{31}\approx\dfrac{300\cdot15.15426}{31}\approx146.654,\]
which is $\boxed{146.7}$ to the nearest tenth.
