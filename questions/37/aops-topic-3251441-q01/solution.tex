The remaining fraction after day $k$ is $\dfrac{k}{k+1}$. Their product telescopes:
\[\prod_{k=1}^{99}\dfrac{k}{k+1}=\dfrac{1}{100}.\]
Thus the initial amount is $\boxed{200}$ units. However, after day one exactly $100$ candies would remain, and day two calls for removing $\dfrac{100}{3}$ candies, not an integer. Therefore $\boxed{\text{the indivisible-candy version is impossible}}$.
