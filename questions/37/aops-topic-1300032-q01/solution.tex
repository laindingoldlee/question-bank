Organise by the larger coins; the pennies are then determined.
\begin{itemize}
\item One quarter: $4$ cents remain, only pennies: $1$ way.
\item No quarter, $d$ dimes ($d=0,1,2$): the remaining $29-10d$ cents use $0$ to $\left\lfloor\dfrac{29-10d}{5}\right\rfloor$ nickels: $6+4+2=12$ ways.
\end{itemize}
Total: $\boxed{13}$. In general, nest the counts from the largest coin down (or use the generating function $\dfrac{1}{(1-x)(1-x^5)(1-x^{10})(1-x^{25})}$).
