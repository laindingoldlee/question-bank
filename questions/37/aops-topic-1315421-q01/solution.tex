Since $2^3=8\equiv1\pmod7$, the powers of $2$ repeat with period $3$: $2,4,1,\dots$. As $2005=3\cdot668+1$, $2^{2005}=(2^3)^{668}\cdot2\equiv2\pmod7$. The remainder is $\boxed{2}$. (The poster's ``$1$'' was for $2^{2007}$.)
