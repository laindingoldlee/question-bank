Rotate so that Albert is in a fixed seat. Then the other four people fill the remaining four seats in $4!=24$ ways. David is next to Albert in $2\cdot3!=12$ of these. So there are $24-12=\boxed{12}$ arrangements. (Directly: David has $2$ non-adjacent seats and the others $3!$ arrangements, giving $12$.) The poster's answer of $12$ is correct.
