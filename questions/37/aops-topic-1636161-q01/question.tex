Prove that for integers $n\ge1$ and $1\le k\le n$,
\[
\dbinom{n+1}{k}=\dbinom{n}{k-1}+\dbinom{n}{k},
\]
by showing that both sides count the same thing. In particular, explain why $\dbinom72=\dbinom61+\dbinom62$.
