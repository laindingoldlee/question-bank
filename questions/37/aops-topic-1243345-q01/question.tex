Let $N$ be a positive integer with at most $n$ digits. Explain why $\dfrac{N}{10^n-1}=\dfrac{N}{\underbrace{99\cdots9}_{n}}$ is the repeating decimal $0.\overline{d_1d_2\cdots d_n}$, where $d_1d_2\cdots d_n$ are the digits of $N$ written with leading zeros to make $n$ digits.
