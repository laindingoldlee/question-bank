We have
\[\dfrac{N}{10^n-1}=\dfrac{N}{10^n}\cdot\dfrac{1}{1-10^{-n}}=\dfrac{N}{10^n}+\dfrac{N}{10^{2n}}+\dfrac{N}{10^{3n}}+\cdots.\]
Since $N<10^n$, the term $\dfrac{N}{10^{kn}}$ places the $n$-digit block $d_1\cdots d_n$ in decimal places $(k-1)n+1$ to $kn$, and the blocks do not overlap. So the sum is $0.d_1\cdots d_nd_1\cdots d_n\ldots=0.\overline{d_1\cdots d_n}$. (Example: $\dfrac{37}{999}=0.\overline{037}$.) $\blacksquare$
