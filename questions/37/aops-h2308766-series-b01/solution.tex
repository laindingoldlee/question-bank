After $j$ losses, the losing rating is $1200(\dfrac{5}{6})^j$. It first drops below 100 after fourteen losses. The winner\textquotesingle s gain over those fourteen games is $\dfrac{7200}{6}(1-(\dfrac{5}{6})^{14})$. After that every win adds 25. The number required is therefore $14+\left\lceil\dfrac{5000-1200-\dfrac{7200}{6}(1-(\dfrac{5}{6})^{14})}{25}\right\rceil=\boxed{122}$.
