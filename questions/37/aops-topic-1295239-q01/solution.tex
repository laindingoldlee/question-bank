An isosceles triangle has two equal sides (legs) of length $a$ and a third side (base) of length $b$, where $b=a$ is allowed. Different pairs $(a,b)$ give non-congruent triangles, because the side lengths $\{a,a,b\}$ determine $a$ (the repeated length) and $b$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0), C=(3,0), A=(1.5,3.2);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
label("$A$",A,N); label("$B$",B,SW); label("$C$",C,SE);
pair m1=(A+B)/2, m2=(A+C)/2;
label("$a$",m1+0.34*(rotate(90)*unit(A-B))); label("$a$",m2+0.34*(rotate(-90)*unit(A-C))); label("$b$",(B+C)/2,S);
draw(m1+0.12*dir(degrees(A-B)+90)*(-1)--m1+0.12*dir(degrees(A-B)+90));
draw(m2+0.12*dir(degrees(A-C)+90)*(-1)--m2+0.12*dir(degrees(A-C)+90));
\end{asy}

The conditions are:
\begin{itemize}
\item triangle inequality: $a+a>b$, i.e. $b\le2a-1$ (the other inequality $a+b>a$ is automatic);
\item perimeter: $2a+b\le19$, i.e. $b\le19-2a$.
\end{itemize}
So for each $a\ge1$ the number of bases is $\min(2a-1,\ 19-2a)$ (when positive):
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccccccc}
a&1&2&3&4&5&6&7&8&9\\\hline
\#b&1&3&5&7&9&7&5&3&1
\end{array}
\]
For $a\ge10$ there is no base. The total is
\[1+3+5+7+9+7+5+3+1=\boxed{41}.\]
