The pattern R, O, Y, Y, G, B has $6$ beads. Since $74=12\cdot6+2$, the first $72$ beads are $12$ complete patterns, and beads $73$ and $74$ are the first two beads of a new pattern: red, orange. The last bead is $\boxed{\text{orange}}$.
