After $n$ presses, the probability of no repetition is $P_n=\prod_{j=0}^{n-1}(1-\dfrac{j}{1728})$ for $n\le1728$. These products decrease with $n$. Direct evaluation gives $P_{49}\approx0.503076>\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $P_{50}\approx0.488811<\dfrac{1}{2}$. Thus the least number of presses is $\boxed{50}$. The product formula follows by conditioning on each previous result being distinct; comparing the two neighbouring exact rational products to one half certifies the threshold.
