For every integer $k$, $k^5\equiv k\pmod{10}$ (check the ten last digits, or note $k^5-k$ is divisible by $2$ and by $5$). Multiplying by $k^{m-1}$ gives $k^{m+4}\equiv k^m\pmod{10}$ for $m\ge1$, so the last digit of $k^m$ depends only on $m$ modulo $4$. Since $2015=4\cdot503+3$,
\[
k^{2015}\equiv k^3\pmod{10}.
\]
Therefore
\[
A\equiv1^3+2^3+\dots+2015^3=\left(\dfrac{2015\cdot2016}{2}\right)^2=2031120^2\pmod{10},
\]
and $2031120^2$ ends in $0$. The last digit of $A$ is $\boxed{0}$.
