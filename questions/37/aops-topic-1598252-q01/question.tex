Let
\[
T=2^{3^{4^{5^{6^{7^{8^{9^{0}}}}}}}},
\]
where the tower is evaluated from the top down, as usual. A calculator gave $T=1$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Explain why $T\ne1$ and what the calculator computed instead.
\item[(b)] Find the units digit of $T$.
\end{enumerate}
