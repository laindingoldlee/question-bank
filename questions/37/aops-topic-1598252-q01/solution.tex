\textbf{(a)} A tower of exponents is evaluated from the top: $9^0=1$, then $8^1=8$, then $7^8=5764801$, then $6^{5764801}$, and so on. Every exponent from $8^1$ downwards is a positive integer, so $T$ is $2$ raised to a positive integer power, an even number at least $2$, not $1$. The calculator evaluated from the left, $\left(\left(\left(2^3\right)^4\right)^{\cdots}\right)^0$, and anything (nonzero) to the power $0$ is $1$.

\textbf{(b)} Write $T=2^N$ with $N=3^M$ and $M=4^{5^{\cdots}}$. Since $M$ is a positive power of $4$, it is even, say $M=2L$, so $N=3^M=9^L\equiv1^L=1\pmod 4$. The units digits of $2^1,2^2,2^3,2^4,\dots$ repeat with period $4$: $2,4,8,6,\dots$. As $N\equiv1\pmod4$, the units digit of $T$ is the same as that of $2^1$, namely $\boxed{2}$.
