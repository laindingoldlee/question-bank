Subtracting, $y^2-x^2-(y-x)=24$, i.e. $(y-x)(y+x-1)=24$. The factors have opposite parity (their sum $2y-1$ is odd), and $y+x-1>0$ forces $y-x>0$. The factor pairs of $24$ with opposite parity are $(1,24)$, $(3,8)$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $y-x=1$, $x+y=25$: $(x,y)=(12,13)$, $z=144+13-100=57>0$ \checkmark;
\item $y-x=3$, $x+y=9$: $(x,y)=(3,6)$, $z=9+6-100<0$ \texttimes.
\end{itemize}
So there is exactly $\boxed{1}$ triple, $(12,13,57)$.
