Consider the following multiple-choice question.
\begin{quote}
If you choose an answer to this question at random, what is the chance that you will be correct?\\
(a) $25\%$\qquad (b) $50\%$\qquad (c) $75\%$\qquad (d) $25\%$
\end{quote}
Show that none of the four choices can be the correct answer.
