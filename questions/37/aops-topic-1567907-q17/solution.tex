Let $f(n)=n^{n-1}+(n-1)^n$. Then
\[
f(1)=1+0=1,\quad f(2)=2+1=3,\quad f(3)=9+8=17,\quad f(4)=64+81=145,\quad f(5)=625+1024=1649 .
\]
Both terms increase with $n$ (for $n\ge2$ the bases and the exponents both increase), so $f$ is increasing and $n=\boxed{4}$ is the only solution.
