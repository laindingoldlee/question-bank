Let $a,b,c$ be the fractions of the job done per minute. Then $a+b=\dfrac1{36}$, $a+c=\dfrac1{45}$, $b+c=\dfrac1{60}$. Adding, $2(a+b+c)=\dfrac{5+4+3}{180}=\dfrac1{15}$, so $a+b+c=\dfrac1{30}$ and $c=\dfrac1{30}-\dfrac1{36}=\dfrac1{180}$. Chris alone takes $\boxed{180\text{ minutes}}=3$ hours.
