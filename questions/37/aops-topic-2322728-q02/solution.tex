Let me keep $m$ pieces and the sisters get $s_1$ and $s_2$, with $m+s_1+s_2=10$, $m\ge2$ and $s_1,s_2\ge1$. Put $m'=m-2$, $s_1'=s_1-1$, $s_2'=s_2-1$; then $m'+s_1'+s_2'=6$ in nonnegative integers. By stars and bars there are $\dbinom{6+2}{2}=\boxed{28}$ ways.
