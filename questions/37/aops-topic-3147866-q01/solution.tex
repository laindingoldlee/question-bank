For temporarily labelled committees, the first has $\binom62^2 $ choices and the second $\binom42^2 $ choices. Each unlabelled pair is counted exactly twice, once in each order; the committees cannot be identical because they are disjoint and nonempty. Thus the answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{\binom62^2\binom42^2}{2}=4050}$.
