Suppose $N$ is such a number. Then $N=123a+1$ and $N=321b+2$ for some integers $a,b$. Both $123=3\cdot41$ and $321=3\cdot107$ are multiples of $3$, so
\[
N\equiv1\pmod3\qquad\text{and}\qquad N\equiv2\pmod3,
\]
which is impossible. Hence no such positive integer exists. $\blacksquare$

(The third condition is not even needed; it would give $N\equiv3\equiv0\pmod 3$, since $333=9\cdot37$, contradicting both.)
