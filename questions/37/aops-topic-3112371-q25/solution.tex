The lateral area is $2\pi x\cdot4=16\pi$, so $x=2$. Ball volumes are proportional to cubed radii. Their interiors do not overlap, so the probability is $\dfrac{1^3}{2^3+1^3}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{9}}$.
