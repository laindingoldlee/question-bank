Let $w_n$ be the number of such words of length $n$. A good word of length $n\ge3$ either ends in A (preceded by any good word of length $n-1$) or ends in AB (preceded by any good word of length $n-2$). So
\[
w_n=w_{n-1}+w_{n-2},\qquad w_1=2,\ w_2=3 .
\]
Then $w_3=5$, $w_4=8$, $w_5=\boxed{13}$.
