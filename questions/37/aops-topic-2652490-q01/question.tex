In triangle $ABC$, let $D$ be a point on side $\overline{BC}$ and let $T$ be a point on segment $\overline{AD}$. Prove that
\[\dfrac{TD}{AD}=\dfrac{[BTC]}{[ABC]},\]
where $[\,\cdot\,]$ denotes area.
