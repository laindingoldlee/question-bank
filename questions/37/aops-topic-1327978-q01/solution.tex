Put $x=a+b$, $y=b+c$, $z=c+a$. Conversely $a=\dfrac{x-y+z}{2}$, $b=\dfrac{x+y-z}{2}$, $c=\dfrac{-x+y+z}{2}$, which are integers exactly when $x+y+z$ is even. So we count ordered integer triples $(x,y,z)$ with $xyz=1800$ and $x+y+z$ even.

Write $1800=2^3\cdot3^2\cdot5^2$. The sum is even iff an even number of $x,y,z$ are odd. Since the product is even, either all three are even (the three factors of $2$ go one to each: $1$ way) or exactly one is even (it gets all three $2$s: $3$ ways). So there are $4$ admissible distributions of the $2$s. The factors $3^2$ and $5^2$ can be distributed in $\dbinom42=6$ ways each. Finally the signs: an even number of $x,y,z$ are negative, $4$ ways, and signs do not affect parity. The total is
\[
4\cdot6\cdot6\cdot4=\boxed{576}.
\]
