A straight log is to be cut. Jack marks it with red lines that would cut it into $24$ equal pieces, and Jill marks it with yellow lines that would cut it into $18$ equal pieces (some yellow lines fall on red lines). A lumberjack cuts along every painted line, making one cut at each marked position. How many cuts does he make?
