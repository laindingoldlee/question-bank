Take the log to have length $72$ (a common multiple of $24$ and $18$). The red lines are at the multiples of $3$ strictly between $0$ and $72$: $23$ lines. The yellow lines are at the multiples of $4$ strictly between $0$ and $72$: $17$ lines. A red line and a yellow line coincide at the multiples of $\operatorname{lcm}(3,4)=12$: $12,24,36,48,60$, which is $5$ positions. So the number of different marked positions, and of cuts, is
\[
23+17-5=\boxed{35}.
\]
