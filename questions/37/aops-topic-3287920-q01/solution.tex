Each kilometre consumes total life $\dfrac{2}{3200}+\dfrac{2}{4000}=\dfrac{9}{8000}$. Six units of life bound distance by $\dfrac{6\cdot8000}{9}=\dfrac{16000}{3}$. Attain this by rotating three pairs through front, rear, and rest over three equal legs. Every tyre spends one third of the distance in each role and has total wear $\dfrac{D}{3}(\dfrac{1}{3200}+\dfrac{1}{4000})=1$. The maximum is $\boxed{\dfrac{16000}{3}\text{ km}}$.
