A monomial $x^ay^bz^c$ with $a+b+c=2006$ survives exactly when $b+c$ is even; otherwise its two coefficients cancel. For $b+c=2k$, there are $2k+1$ choices of $(b,c)$, with $0\le k\le1003$. All surviving coefficients are positive. Hence the count is $\sum_{k=0}^{1003}(2k+1)=1004^2=\boxed{1008016}$.
