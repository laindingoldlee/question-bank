\emph{$10$ suffice:} $4$ pennies, $1$ nickel, $2$ dimes, $3$ quarters. Any amount $25q+r$ with $r<25$ uses $q\le3$ quarters, and $r=10d+5n+p$ with $d\le2$, $n\le1$, $p\le4$.

\emph{$9$ do not.} Paying $99$ cents needs at least $9$ coins, and the only $9$-coin way is $3$ quarters, $2$ dimes and $4$ pennies. That set cannot pay $5$ cents. So at least $\boxed{10}$ coins are needed.
