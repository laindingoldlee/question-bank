There are $(7\cdot6\cdot5\cdot4\cdot3)(10\cdot9\cdot8\cdot7\cdot6)$ equally likely sequence pairs. Four agreements use all four shared designs. Choose the unmatched day in $5$ ways, order the common designs in $4!$ ways, and choose the remaining shirts in $3\cdot6$ ways. Thus
\[P=\dfrac{5\cdot4!\cdot3\cdot6}{2520\cdot30240}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{35280}}.\]
