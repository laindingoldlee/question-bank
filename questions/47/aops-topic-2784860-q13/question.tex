Choose one of six distinct sock pairs, exactly one pink and one black, and one of four distinct pairs of pants, exactly one blue and one red. Pink socks cannot be worn with blue pants, and black socks may only be worn with red pants. All other combinations are allowed. How many outfits are possible?
