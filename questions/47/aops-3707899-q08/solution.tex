Comparing the first and third perimeters gives $AB+BC=CD+DA$. Comparing the second and fourth gives $BC+CD=DA+AB$. Adding and subtracting yields $BC=DA$ and $AB=CD$. A convex quadrilateral with both pairs of opposite sides equal is a parallelogram: the diagonal $AC$ produces congruent triangles by SSS, giving opposite-side parallelism. Comparing the first and second perimeters then gives $AC=BD$. In a parallelogram with adjacent side vectors $u,v$, equality $|u+v|^2=|u-v|^2$ implies $4u\cdot v=0$. Thus adjacent sides are perpendicular, proving it is a rectangle.
