Without the frequency restriction, there are $5\cdot4^4=1280$ words. A letter occurring at least 3 times without equal adjacency must occur exactly at positions 1,3,5; four occurrences cannot fit. Choose that letter in 5 ways and the two other positions independently from the other 4 letters, giving $5\cdot4^2=80$ invalid words. A word cannot have two letters each occurring 3 times, so there is no overlap. Thus the answer is $1280-80=\boxed{1200}$.
