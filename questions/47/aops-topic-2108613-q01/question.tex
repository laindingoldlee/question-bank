A cheerleading squad has $14$ cheerleaders, all of different heights. In how many ways can they line up for a photo in two rows of seven, so that each cheerleader in the back row is taller than the cheerleader immediately in front of her, and the heights of the cheerleaders in the back row decrease from the middle towards each end?
