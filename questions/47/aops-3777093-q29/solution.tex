Since $4444^{4444}<10^{17776}$, its digit sum is at most $159984$. Its next digit sum is at most $54$, and the digit sum of a positive integer at most $54$ is at most $13$. Digit sums preserve residues modulo $9$. Now $4444\equiv7\pmod9$ and $7^3\equiv1$, so $4444^{4444}\equiv7\pmod9$. The only positive integer at most $13$ congruent to $7$ is $\boxed7$.
