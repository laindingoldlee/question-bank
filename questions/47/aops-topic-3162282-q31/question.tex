Let $ t $ be the positive value of the infinite periodic continued fraction $\dfrac{1}{\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{\dfrac{1}{2}+\cdots}}}$. Find the real number $ A $ satisfying $ A=\dfrac{A}{6}+t $.
