Self-similarity gives $ t=\dfrac{1}{\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{\dfrac{1}{3}+t}}$. Clearing denominators yields $ 3t^2+t-2=0 $, with roots $\dfrac{2}{3},-1 $. Positivity selects $ t=\dfrac{2}{3}$. Hence $\dfrac{5}{6}A=\dfrac{2}{3}$ and $\boxed{A=\dfrac{4}{5}}$. The positive periodic continued fraction converges: its two-level map is an increasing fractional linear map with a unique positive fixed point, and its even and odd convergents bound that point monotonically.
