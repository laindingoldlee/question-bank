The conditions say $-1<b<1$ and $2<a+b<3$. At $x=2$,
\[
y=2a+b=2(a+b)-b .
\]
Put $u=a+b$; then $u$ and $b$ can be chosen independently with $2<u<3$ and $-1<b<1$ (and then $a=u-b$). Since $4<2u<6$ and $-1<-b<1$, we get $3<2u-b<7$.

Every value in between is possible: for $3<t<7$ take $u=\dfrac{t+5}{4}$ and $b=\dfrac{5-t}{2}$, which satisfy $2<u<3$, $-1<b<1$ and $2u-b=t$. So the range is $\boxed{3<y<7}$.
