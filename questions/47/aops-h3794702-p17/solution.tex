Write $OX=a$, $OY=b$. Pythagoras in $XON$ and $YOM$ gives $a^2+\dfrac{b^2}{4}=19^2$ and $b^2+\dfrac{a^2}{4}=22^2$. Adding yields $\dfrac{5}{4}(a^2+b^2)=845$, so $XY^2=a^2+b^2=676$. Hence $\boxed{XY=26}$.
