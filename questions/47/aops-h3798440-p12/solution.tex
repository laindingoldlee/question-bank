Set $AD=x$ and let the altitude above $BC$ be $h$. The angle-bisector and parallel-line angles make triangle $ABP$ isosceles, so $BP=AB=1$. Choose affine coordinates $B=(0,0),C=(x,0),A=(u,h),D=(u+x,h),P=(1,0)$. Intersecting $AP$ with $BD$ gives $Q=(\dfrac{u+x}{x+1},\dfrac{h}{x+1})$. Thus $[BPQ]=\dfrac{h}{2(x+1)}$, while $[BCD]=\dfrac{xh}{2}$. Since $CDQP$ is $BCD$ minus $BPQ$, their area ratio is $x(x+1)-1=55$. Hence $(x-7)(x+8)=0$ and the positive length is $\boxed{AD=7}$.
