For $y=Ax^2+cx+d$, a tangent of slope $m$ has intercept $d-\dfrac{(m-c)^2}{4A}$. For $y=-Ax^2+dx+c$, its intercept is $c+\dfrac{(m-d)^2}{4A}$. A common tangent therefore requires
\[
4A(d-c)=(m-c)^2+(m-d)^2.
\]
Substitute the known slope $m=c$ and let $u=d-c\ne0$. Then $4Au=u^2$, so $u=4A=8104$. Thus $\boxed{c-d=-8104}$. Conversely with this value the slope equation factors as $2(m-c)(m-d)=0$, giving exactly the two distinct required tangents.
