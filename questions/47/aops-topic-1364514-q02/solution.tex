Let the escalator move $e$ steps per second, and let $N$ be the number of steps from top to bottom (the number one would walk on a stopped escalator). While a person rides, the steps he or she walks plus the steps the escalator carries him or her add up to $N$:
\[
N=30+72e,\qquad N=40+60e .
\]
Subtracting, $12e=10$, so $e=\dfrac{5}{6}$ and $N=30+72\cdot\dfrac{5}{6}=30+60=90$.

So each of them would have to walk down $\boxed{90}$ steps.
