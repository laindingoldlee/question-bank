Justin and Shelby board an escalator as it is moving down. Justin walks down $30$ steps and reaches the bottom in $72$ seconds, while Shelby walks down $40$ steps and reaches the bottom in $60$ seconds. Each walks at a constant rate. If the escalator were not moving, how many steps would each of them have to walk down to reach the bottom?
