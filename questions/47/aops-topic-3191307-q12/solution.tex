Let the three counts be $a,b,c$. Then $a+b+c=10$ and $6a+9b+2c=49$, yielding $4a+7b=29$. Modulo $4$, $b\equiv3\pmod4$, and $7b\le29$ forces $b=3$. Thus $a=2$ and $c=\boxed5$.
