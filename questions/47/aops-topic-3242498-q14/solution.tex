The prime $p$ is not $11$. Fermat's little theorem gives $p^{10}\equiv1\pmod{11}$, while $p^4\equiv1$. Therefore $p^2\equiv1$ (divide $p^{10}$ by $(p^4)^2$), so $p\equiv\pm1\pmod{11}$. Among primes below $23$, namely $2,3,5,7,11,13,17,19$, none has either residue. The number $23$ is prime and is $1$ modulo $11$. Its digit sum is $\boxed5$, choice B.
