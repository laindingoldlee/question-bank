Complete the square:
\[y=x^2+x+1=\left(x+\dfrac12\right)^2+\dfrac34.\]
So the graph is an upward parabola with vertex $\left(-\dfrac12,\dfrac34\right)$ and axis of symmetry $x=-\dfrac12$. Its least value is $\dfrac34>0$, so it has no $x$-intercepts. The $y$-intercept is $(0,1)$, and by symmetry $(-1,1)$ is also on the graph.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
real sx=2, sy=0.6;
real f(real x){return sy*(x^2+x+1);}
real g(real t){return f(t/sx);}
draw((-5.6,0)--(3.8,0),Arrow(6)); draw((0,0)--(0,3.6),Arrow(6));
label("$x$",(3.8,0),E); label("$y$",(0,3.6),N);
draw((-1,0)--(-1,3.3),dashed+gray(0.4));
draw(graph(g,-5.2,3.2),blue+linewidth(0.9));
dot((-1,f(-0.5))); dot((0,f(0))); dot((-2,f(-1)));
label("$\left(-\dfrac12,\dfrac34\right)$",(-1,0),S); draw((-1,-0.05)--(-1,f(-0.5)),dotted);
label("$(0,1)$",(0,f(0)),E); label("$(-1,1)$",(-2,f(-1)),W);
label("$x=-\dfrac12$",(-1,3.3),NW,gray(0.3));
\end{asy}
