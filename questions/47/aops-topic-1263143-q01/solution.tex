Since $a^{2017b}$ is a power of the prime $p$, $a=p^k$ for some positive integer $k$, and the equation becomes $2017^p+p=2017bk$. So $2017\mid2017^p+p$, hence $2017\mid p$; as $2017$ is prime, $p=2017$. Then $bk=2017^{2016}+1$, and $a^p=2017^{2017k}$ is smallest when $k=1$ (with $b=2017^{2016}+1$). The minimum is $\boxed{2017^{2017}}$.
