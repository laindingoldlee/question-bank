(a) Every possible hidden number is among the ten guesses, so the probability is $\boxed1 $. (b) Each guess misses with probability $\dfrac{9}{10}$, and the misses are independent. Thus the success probability is $\boxed{1-(\dfrac{9}{10})^{10}}$.
