By Vieta's formulas, $x_1+x_2=8$ and $x_1x_2=k$. Then
\[
3x_1+4x_2=3(x_1+x_2)+x_2=24+x_2=29,
\]
so $x_2=5$ and $x_1=3$. Hence $k=x_1x_2=\boxed{15}$. (Check: $x^2-8x+15=(x-3)(x-5)$ and $3\cdot3+4\cdot5=29$.)
