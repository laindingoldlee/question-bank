Let Dan's speed be $v$ mph. At the meeting Dan has driven $3+2=5$ hours and Sally $2$ hours, over the same distance:
\[
5v=2(v+46)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 3v=92\quad\Longrightarrow\quad v=\boxed{30\dfrac{2}{3}\text{ mph}}\approx30.67\text{ mph}.
\]
