A point $P$ is located in the interior of rectangle $ABCD$ such that $PB=25$ cm. A point $Q$ is located on segment $AB$ such that segment $PQ$ is perpendicular to segment $AB$. All lengths are measured in centimetres, $AQ=PQ$, segment $PQ$ has the smallest integer length possible, and segment $BQ$ also has integer length. What is the length of segment $AB$?
