Triangle $PQB$ has a right angle at $Q$, so $PQ^2+QB^2=25^2=625$ with $PQ$ and $QB$ positive integers. The solutions are $(PQ,QB)=(7,24),(15,20),(20,15),(24,7)$. The smallest possible $PQ$ is $7$, with $QB=24$. Then $AQ=PQ=7$ and
\[
AB=AQ+QB=7+24=\boxed{31}\text{ cm}.
\]
(The rectangle's other side only needs to exceed $7$, so that $P$ is inside.)

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(31,0),C=(31,12),D=(0,12),Q=(7,0),P=(7,7);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(P--Q,blue+linewidth(0.8));draw(P--B,red+linewidth(0.8));draw(A--P,gray+dashed);
real q=1;draw(Q+(q,0)--Q+(q,q)--Q+(0,q),blue);
dot(P);dot(Q);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$Q$",Q,S);label("$P$",P,N);
label("$7$",(A+Q)/2,S);label("$24$",(Q+B)/2,S);label("$7$",(P+Q)/2,W,blue);label("$25$",(P+B)/2,NE,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
