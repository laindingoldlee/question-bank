The middle prime $q$ is about $\dfrac{173}{3}\approx57.7$. The primes near there are $\dots,47,53,59,61,67,\dots$
\begin{itemize}
\item If the middle prime is $53$ or less, the sum is at most $47+53+59=159<173$.
\item If the middle prime is $61$ or more, the sum is at least $59+61+67=187>173$.
\end{itemize}
So the middle prime is $59$, and indeed $53+59+61=173$. The largest prime is $\boxed{61}$.
