Split $-4bc=-2bc-2bc$ and regroup into two perfect squares:
\[
(b^2c^2-2bc+1)-(b^2+2bc+c^2)=(bc-1)^2-(b+c)^2 .
\]
By the difference of squares,
\[
b^2c^2-4bc-b^2-c^2+1=\boxed{(bc-b-c-1)(bc+b+c-1)}.
\]
