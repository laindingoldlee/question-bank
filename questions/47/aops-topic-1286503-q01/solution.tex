$\dfrac{A+B}{2}=\sqrt{AB}$ means $A+B-2\sqrt{AB}=0$, i.e. $\left(\sqrt A-\sqrt B\right)^2=0$, so $A=B$. Conversely $A=B$ works. The possible values of $A+B=2A$ are $2,4,\dots,1998$, with sum
\[
2(1+2+\cdots+999)=999\cdot1000=\boxed{999000}.
\]
