Squares are $0$ or $1$ modulo $4$, so their differences cannot be $2$ modulo $4$. Thus $2026$ is impossible. The other two work: $2024=507^2-505^2=(507-505)(507+505)$ and $2025=1013^2-1012^2=(1013-1012)(1013+1012)$. Therefore $\boxed{2024\text{ and }2025}$ are possible.
