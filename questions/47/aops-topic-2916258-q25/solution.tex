Let the centre be $O$ and let $h$ be the distance from $O$ to $CD$ (and to $AB$), so $R^2=h^2+25$. The point $P$ lies on the axis of symmetry at distance $\dfrac{R^2}h$ from $O$, beyond $CD$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real h=5/sqrt(2),R=sqrt(h^2+25);pair O=(0,0);draw(circle(O,R));pair C=(5,h),D=(-5,h),A=(-5,-h),B=(5,-h);pair P=(0,R^2/h);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(C--P--D);dot(P);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,E);label("$D$",D,W);label("$P$",P,N);
\end{asy}

The distance from $P$ to $AB$ is $\dfrac{h^2+25}h+h=2h+\dfrac{25}h\ge2\sqrt{50}=10\sqrt2$ by AM--GM, with equality at $h=\dfrac5{\sqrt2}$. The minimum is $\boxed{10\sqrt2}$.
