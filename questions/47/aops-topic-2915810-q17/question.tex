Ten labelled points lie in cyclic order on a circle. Join them in five chords so each point belongs to one chord and no chords cross. Additionally, there must not be six distinct points $A,B,C,D,E,F$ in cyclic order with chords $AB,CD,EF$. How many matchings satisfy this?
