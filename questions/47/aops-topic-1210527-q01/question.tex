An equilateral triangle is moved by a sequence of rotations about its centre, all clockwise: at step $1$ by $3^\circ$, at step $2$ by a further $9^\circ$, at step $3$ by a further $27^\circ$, and in general at step $n$ by a further $3^n$ degrees. How many different positions, including the starting one, does the triangle occupy?
