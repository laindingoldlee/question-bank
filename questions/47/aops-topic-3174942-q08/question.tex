Let $q$ be the probability that four consecutive heads occur before two consecutive tails when a fair coin is repeatedly tossed. Choose uniformly a positive perfect square at most $46656$, and let $r$ be the probability its remainder modulo $14$ is $11$. Find $\dfrac{q}{r}$.
