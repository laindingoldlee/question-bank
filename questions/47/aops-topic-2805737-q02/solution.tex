A larger side faces a larger angle, so $C>A=58^\circ$. Since $B=122^\circ-C>0$, the interval is $0<B<64^\circ$. Every angle in this interval defines a valid triangle satisfying the condition. Thus $\boxed{n-m=64^\circ}$.
