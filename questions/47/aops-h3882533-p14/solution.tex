Write $\deg P=d\ge7$ and leading coefficient $c\ne0$. If $d>7$, then $9d>d+56$, so the expression has degree $9d>61$, impossible. Hence $d=7$. Write $P(x)=cx^7+ex^6+Q(x)$ with $\deg Q\le5$.

The coefficient of $x^{63}$ in the expression is $c^9-c$. Its vanishing requires $c^8=1$, so $c=\pm1$ over the reals. Since the $x^6$ coefficient of $P(x+5)$ is $35c+e$, the coefficient of $x^{62}$ in the difference is
\[
9c^8(35c+e)-e=315c+8e.
\]
Thus $e=-315\dfrac{c}{8}$. These two cancellations are sufficient, since all remaining terms have degree at most $61$. The complete family is
\[
\boxed{P(x)=\varepsilon\left(x^7-\dfrac{315}{8}x^6\right)+Q(x),\quad
\varepsilon\in\{-1,1\},\quad\deg Q\le5}.
\]
