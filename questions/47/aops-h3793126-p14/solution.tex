For fixed denominators, use the two smallest available digits as numerators; globally an exchange placing a smaller digit in a numerator and a larger one in a denominator strictly reduces the sum. Thus the numerators are 1 and 2 and the denominators 8 and 9. Compare $\dfrac{1}{8}+\dfrac{2}{9}=\dfrac{25}{72}$ with $\dfrac{1}{9}+\dfrac{2}{8}=\dfrac{26}{72}$. The minimum uses $(W,X,Y,Z)=(1,8,2,9)$ or exchanges the fractions, yielding $WZ+XY=9+16=\boxed{25}$.
