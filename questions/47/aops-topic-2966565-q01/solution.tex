With $c$ correct and $b$ blank, $6c+1.5b=114$, i.e. $4c+b=76$, with $b\ge0$ and $c+b\le25$. Then $b=76-4c\ge0$ gives $c\le19$, and $c+76-4c\le25$ gives $c\ge17$. So $c\in\{17,18,19\}$ (with $b=8,4,0$), and the sum is $\boxed{54}$.
