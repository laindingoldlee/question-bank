By the binomial formula the probability is
\[\binom{100}{99}(0.99)^{99}(0.01)=0.99^{99}.\]
Now $\ln0.99^{99}=99\ln0.99\approx99(-0.01005)=-0.995$, so $0.99^{99}\approx e^{-0.995}\approx\boxed{0.37}$.
