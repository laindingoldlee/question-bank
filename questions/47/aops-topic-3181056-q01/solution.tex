If the slower runner completes $m\ge2$ laps, the faster completes $m+1$, because this is their first later meeting. Their lap times are $\dfrac{270}{m}$ and $\dfrac{270}{m+1}$, so both consecutive integers $m,m+1$ divide $270=2\cdot3^3\cdot5$. Its divisors are $1,2,3,5,6,9,10,15,18,27,30,45,54,90,135,270$. The consecutive pairs with $m\ge2$ are $(2,3),(5,6),(9,10)$. The differences are $135-90=45$, $54-45=9$, and $30-27=3$. Thus $\boxed{3,9,45\text{ seconds}}$.
