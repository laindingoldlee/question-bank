Look at numbers just above $500$, where the digit sums are small and grow by $1$ from one number to the next:
\[
510=6\cdot85,\qquad 511=7\cdot73,\qquad 512=8\cdot64,\qquad 513=9\cdot57 .
\]
The digit sums are $6,7,8,9$, and each number is a multiple of its digit sum. So $\boxed{510,511,512,513}$ are four consecutive special numbers. (A computer search shows that this is the smallest such run.)
