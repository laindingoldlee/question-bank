Yes: a power of a fraction is the same power of the numerator over the same power of the denominator, because $\left(\dfrac{a}{b}\right)^2=\dfrac{a}{b}\cdot\dfrac{a}{b}=\dfrac{a^2}{b^2}$. So
\[
\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^3=\dfrac{8}{27},\qquad \left(\dfrac{2}{9}\right)^2=\dfrac{4}{81},
\]
and
\[
\dfrac{\dfrac{8}{27}}{\dfrac{4}{81}}=\dfrac{8}{27}\cdot\dfrac{81}{4}=\dfrac{8\cdot81}{27\cdot4}=\boxed{6}.
\]
