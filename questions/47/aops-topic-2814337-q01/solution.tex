By Bezout's identity there are integers $x,y$ with $ax+by=1$. Multiplying by $c$: $c=acx+bcy$. Here $a\mid acx$, and $a\mid bc$ gives $a\mid bcy$. Hence $a\mid c$. $\blacksquare$
