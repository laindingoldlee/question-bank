Factor $2016=2^5\cdot3^2\cdot7$. For a prime with exponent $e$, the exponents in $m,n$ are nonnegative with sum at most $e$, giving $1+2+\cdots+(e+1)=\dfrac{(e+1)(e+2)}{2}$ choices. Multiplying independent choices gives $21\cdot6\cdot3=\boxed{378}$.
