Adjacent sums odd means digit parities alternate. Divisibility by 4 forces the last digit even, so the parity pattern is even--odd--even--odd--even--odd--even--odd--even. All five even digits occur. Since the tens digit is odd, the last digit must be 2 or 6. Choose it in 2 ways. Arrange four of the five odd digits in their four positions in $5\cdot4\cdot3\cdot2=120$ ways.
