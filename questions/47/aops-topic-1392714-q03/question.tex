How many ordered pairs of nonnegative integers $(a,b)$ are there with $a+b=999$ such that each of $a$ and $b$ consists of at most two different digits? (These distinct digits need not be the same in $a$ and $b$; for example, we might have $a=622$ and $b=377$.)
