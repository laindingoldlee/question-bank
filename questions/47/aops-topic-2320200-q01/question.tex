Prove the hockey stick identity
\[\dbinom rr+\dbinom{r+1}{r}+\dbinom{r+2}{r}+\cdots+\dbinom nr=\dbinom{n+1}{r+1}\qquad(0\le r\le n)\]
by a block-walking argument, that is, by counting lattice paths made of unit steps to the right and up.
