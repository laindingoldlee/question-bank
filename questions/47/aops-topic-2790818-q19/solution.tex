Whoever yells $99$ wins, since the opponent then has no legal yell below $100$. The second teacher can yell $89,91,93,95$ or $97$ (the numbers in $89$--$99$ coprime to $88$; $99=9\cdot11$ is not). After $89,91,95$ or $97$ the first teacher could yell $99$ (coprime to each) and win. After $\boxed{93}$ the first teacher must yell $94,95,97$ or $98$ ($96$ and $99$ are multiples of $3$), and each of these is coprime to $99$, so the second teacher answers $99$ and wins.
