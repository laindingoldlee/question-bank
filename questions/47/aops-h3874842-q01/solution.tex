Write $s=\sin x$, $c=\cos x$, and $u=\sqrt3s+8c$. Using $s^2+c^2=1$, the expression becomes
\[
256-4u^2+12\sqrt3u
=283-4\left(u-\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}\right)^2.
\]
It is therefore at most $283$. As $x$ varies, $u=\sqrt3\sin x+8\cos x$ takes every value in $[-\sqrt{67},\sqrt{67}]$. Since $3\dfrac{\sqrt 3}{2}$ lies in that interval, equality is attained. The maximum is $\boxed{283}$.

\end{asy}
