The conditions say
\[
N\equiv1\pmod 2,\qquad N\equiv2\pmod 3,\qquad N\equiv3\pmod 5,\qquad N\equiv5\pmod 7 .
\]
The first two give $N\equiv5\pmod 6$. Combined with $N\equiv3\pmod5$: among $5,11,17,23,29$ only $23$ works, so $N\equiv23\pmod{30}$. Finally $23,53,83,113,143,173$ have remainders $2,4,6,1,3,5$ modulo $7$, so $N\equiv173\pmod{210}$. The least positive value is $\boxed{173}$.
