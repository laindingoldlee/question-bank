The smallest divisor of $a$ greater than 1 is a prime $p$, so $\gcd(a,b)=p=e_6$. There are five divisors of $b$ below $p$, forcing $p\ge7$. Write $b=p^k m$ with $p\nmid m$ and $k\ge1$. All five divisors below $p$ divide $m$, so $m$ has at least five divisors. Every positive integer below 12 has at most four divisors (checked directly), hence $m\ge12$. Consequently $a+b\ge p+12p\ge91$. Equality is attained by $a=7,b=84$, whose first six divisors are $1,2,3,4,6,7$. Thus $\boxed{91}$.
