It is enough to work modulo $2$ and $5$. Modulo $2$, any positive power of $x$ equals $x$. Modulo $5$, if $5\mid x$ the claim is immediate; otherwise Fermat gives $x^4\equiv1\pmod5$. Since $4\mid2^n$, $x^{2^n+1}\equiv x\pmod5$. The difference is divisible by both $2$ and $5$, hence by $10$, proving equality of units digits.
