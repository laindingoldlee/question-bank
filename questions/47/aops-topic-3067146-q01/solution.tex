Since $7^4=2401\equiv1+400\pmod{1000}$, the binomial theorem gives $7^{20}\equiv(1+400)^5\equiv1+2000\equiv1\pmod{1000}$; terms involving $400^2$ are divisible by 1000. Therefore $7^{402}=(7^{20})^{20}7^2\equiv\boxed{49}\pmod{1000}$.
