Triangles $PAB$ and $PCD$ are similar, so $AB:CD=PA:PC=PB:PD=4:5$. Triangles $PBC$ and $PDC$ share an altitude from $C$ to line $BD$, giving $[PBC]:[PDC]=PB:PD=4:5$. Thus $[PBC]=20$. Likewise $[PAD]=20$. Summing the four component areas gives $16+20+25+20=\boxed{81}$.

\end{asy}
