Three painters can paint $3$ apartments in $3$ days. All painters work at the same constant rate.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] How many apartments can $6$ painters paint in $6$ days?
\item[(b)] How many days do $9$ painters need to paint $9$ apartments?
\item[(c)] How many painters are needed to paint $12$ apartments in $12$ days?
\end{enumerate}
