Set $A=(0,0)$, $B=(1,0)$, $C=(1,1)$ and $D=(0,1)$. Then
\[
E=\left(1,\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}\right),\qquad F=\left(\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3},1\right),\qquad
AE=AF=\dfrac{2}{\sqrt3}.
\]
Triangle $ABE$ is right-angled at $B$. Its inradius is
\[
r=\dfrac{AB+BE-AE}{2}=\dfrac{1-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}}{2}.
\]
The point of tangency on $AB$ is distance $r$ from $B$. Tangents from $A$ to the incircle have equal lengths, so
\[
AG=1-r=\dfrac{1+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}}{2}.
\]
Finally $\angle GAF=30^\circ$, giving
\[
[AGF]=\dfrac{1}{2}\,AG\,AF\sin30^\circ
=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt3+1}{12}}.
\]
