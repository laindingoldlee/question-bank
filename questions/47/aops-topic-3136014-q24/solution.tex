Since $2013=3\cdot11\cdot61$, the allowed prime factors are 2,5,7,13. Every member is uniquely $2^a5^b7^c13^d$ with nonnegative exponents, including 1. The convergent geometric series multiply, giving $\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{1}{2}}\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{1}{5}}\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{1}{7}}\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{1}{13}}=2\cdot\dfrac{5}{4}\cdot\dfrac{7}{6}\cdot\dfrac{13}{12}=\boxed{\dfrac{455}{144}}$.
