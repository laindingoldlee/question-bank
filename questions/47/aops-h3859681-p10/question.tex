Let $k>1$ be the unique real number such that the graph of $(|x|-k)^2+(|y|-k)^2=k$ in any $3$ of the $4$ quadrants share a common tangent line. The area of the region bound by the graph of $(|x|+k)^2+(|y|+k)^2=k^4$ can be written in the form $\dfrac{a-b\sqrt{n}+c\pi}{d}$, where $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$, $n$ are positive integers, $n$ is square-free, and $\gcd(a,b,c,d)=1$. Compute $a+bn+c+d$.
