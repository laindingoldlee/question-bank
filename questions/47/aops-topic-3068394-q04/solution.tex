In $r$ rounds a championship bracket can accommodate at most $2^r$ entrants: trace the binary tree of preceding games. Since $128<156\le256$, at least 8 rounds are needed. Put 156 entrants into a 256-slot eight-round bracket with byes in round 1; this attains 8. Thus the answer is $\boxed8$.
