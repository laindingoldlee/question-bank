Write the sequence as $0<a_2<a_3<a_4<a_5\le8$ and let $d_i$ be the four successive differences. Consecutive terms have opposite parity, so every $d_i$ is odd: $d_i=2e_i+1$ with $e_i\ge0$. The last term is
\[a_5=d_1+d_2+d_3+d_4=4+2(e_1+e_2+e_3+e_4),\]
and it is automatically even. The condition $a_5\le8$ becomes $e_1+e_2+e_3+e_4\le2$. Adding a slack variable $e_5\ge0$, we count nonnegative solutions of $e_1+\cdots+e_5=2$, which by stars and bars is $\dbinom{6}{4}=\boxed{15}$.
