It need not hold. Take $\alpha=2,\beta=1,\gamma=3$. The three quadratics are $x^2-3x+2$, $x^2-4x+3$, and $x^2-5x+6$, so $(m_1,m_2,m_3)=(3,4,5)$ satisfies $m_1^2+m_2^2=m_3^2$. But the proposed left side equals $(4+1+9)(2+1+3)=84$, while the right side is $3\cdot2\cdot1\cdot3=18$. Thus the assertion is $\boxed{\text{false}}$.
