Take out the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$:
\[\dfrac{25}{2}x^2-4xy+\dfrac{8}{25}y^2=\dfrac{1}{2}\left(25x^2-8xy+\dfrac{16}{25}y^2\right).\]
Inside the bracket, $25x^2=(5x)^2$, $\dfrac{16}{25}y^2=\left(\dfrac{4}{5}y\right)^2$ and $8xy=2\cdot5x\cdot\dfrac{4}{5}y$, so the bracket is the perfect square $\left(5x-\dfrac{4}{5}y\right)^2$. Writing $5x-\dfrac{4}{5}y=\dfrac{25x-4y}{5}$,
\[\dfrac{25}{2}x^2-4xy+\dfrac{8}{25}y^2=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{(25x-4y)^2}{25}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{50}(25x-4y)^2}.\]
Check: $\dfrac{1}{50}\left(625x^2-200xy+16y^2\right)=\dfrac{25}{2}x^2-4xy+\dfrac{8}{25}y^2$.
