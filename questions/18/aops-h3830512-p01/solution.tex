The west route is $\sqrt{15^2+8^2}+8+x=25+x$. The east route is $\sqrt{15^2+(x+9)^2}+9$. Equality gives $\sqrt{225+(x+9)^2}=x+16$. Squaring and simplifying yields $14x=50$, so $\boxed{x=\dfrac{25}{7}}$. This positive value satisfies the unsquared equation.
