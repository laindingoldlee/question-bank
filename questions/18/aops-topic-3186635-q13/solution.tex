The relative angular speed is $330^\circ$ per hour. Successive right-angle configurations differ by $180^\circ$ in relative angle, so are spaced $\dfrac{6}{11}$ hour apart. A seven-hour interval can contain at most $\left\lfloor\dfrac{7}{\dfrac{6}{11}}\right\rfloor+1=13$ such instants, attained by starting at one of them. Thus $\boxed{13}$ points.
