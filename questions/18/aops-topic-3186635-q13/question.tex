For an assignment in his math class, Michael must stare at an analog clock for a period of $7$ hours. He must record the times at which the minute hand and hour hand form an angle of exactly $90^\circ$, and he will receive $1$ point for every time he records correctly. What is the maximum number of points Michael can earn on his assignment?
