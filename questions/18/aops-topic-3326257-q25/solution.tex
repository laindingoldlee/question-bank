Factor the denominator as $(k-2)(k+4)$. Then $\dfrac{1}{(k-2)(k+4)}=\dfrac{1}{6}\left(\dfrac{1}{k-2}-\dfrac{1}{k+4}\right)$. The infinite sum telescopes to $S=\dfrac{1}{6}\left(\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{5}+\dfrac{1}{6}+\dfrac{1}{7}+\dfrac{1}{8}\right)$. Therefore $1680S=280\left(\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{5}+\dfrac{1}{6}+\dfrac{1}{7}+\dfrac{1}{8}\right)=\boxed{341}$.
