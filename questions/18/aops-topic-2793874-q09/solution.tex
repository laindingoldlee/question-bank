For positive integers $a+b+c=10$, stars and bars gives $\binom92=36$. Exclude choices with a specified variable at least 7: subtract $\binom32=3$ for each of three variables. Two cannot simultaneously exceed 6. Thus 27 of 216 outcomes work, probability $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{8}}$.
