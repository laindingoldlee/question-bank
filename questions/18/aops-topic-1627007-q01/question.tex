Two trains travel towards each other at constant speeds. The first leaves city $A$ at 08:50 heading for city $B$; the second leaves city $B$ at 09:20 heading for city $A$. The first train arrives at $B$ $40$ minutes after the trains pass each other, and the second train arrives at $A$ $100$ minutes after they pass. At what time do the trains pass each other?
