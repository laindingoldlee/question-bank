Let the trains pass $t$ minutes after 08:50; the second train has then travelled for $t-30$ minutes. Let the speeds be $v_1$ and $v_2$.

After the meeting the first train covers, in $40$ minutes, the stretch the second train covered before the meeting in $t-30$ minutes; and the second train covers, in $100$ minutes, the stretch the first covered in $t$ minutes:
\[
40v_1=(t-30)v_2,\qquad 100v_2=tv_1 .
\]
Multiplying, $4000v_1v_2=t(t-30)v_1v_2$, so
\[
t^2-30t-4000=0\ \Longrightarrow\ (t-80)(t+50)=0\ \Longrightarrow\ t=80 .
\]
The trains pass $80$ minutes after 08:50, at $\boxed{10{:}10}$. Check: $v_1:v_2=100:80=5:4$, and the first train's $40$ minutes after the meeting covers what the second did in $50$ minutes (09:20 to 10:10), consistent with $40\cdot5=50\cdot4$.
