Using each of the numbers $1$, $5$, $5$, $7$ exactly once, together with the operations $+$, $-$, $\times$, $\div$, exponents, square roots, decimal points and writing digits next to each other to form numbers (as in $80=17\times5-5$ and $16=75\div5+1$), express
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $46$;
\item[(b)] $66$.
\end{enumerate}
Factorials and repeating-decimal bars are not allowed.
