Adding the common overlap to both equal exclusive areas shows the figures have equal total areas. Thus the square has area 169 and side $\boxed{13}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
path sq=(0,0)--(3,0)--(3,3)--(0,3)--cycle;path cr=circle((2.7,1.5),sqrt(9/pi));filldraw(sq,lightgray);draw(cr,blue);label("square",(0.7,1.5));label("other figure",(3.4,1.5),E);
\end{asy}
