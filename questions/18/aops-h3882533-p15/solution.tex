The equations reduce to $a+b+c+d+e=0$, $a+2b+3c+4d+5e=0$, and $c+d+e=0$. Hence their complete solution family is
\[
(a,b,c,d,e)=(s+2t,-s-2t,-s-t,s,t),\qquad s,t\in\mathbb R.
\]
In particular there are infinitely many solutions.

The binomial theorem makes the inner sum $(\ln2+i\pi)^n$. Absolute convergence follows by bounding it by $(\ln2+\pi)^n$ and comparing both factorial series with exponentials. We may therefore separate the sums:
\[
S=(e^{\ln3}-1)(e^{\ln2+i\pi}-1)=(3-1)(-2-1)=-6.
\]
Thus $\boxed{|S|=6}$.
