Consider the real homogeneous system
\[
\begin{cases}
a+3b+5c+7d+9e=0\\
2a+5b+8c+11d+14e=0\\
3a+7b+11c+15d+19e=0\\
4a+9b+16c+21d+26e=0\\
5a+11b+17c+23d+29e=0\\
6a+13b+20c+27d+34e=0.
\end{cases}
\]
Describe all its solutions. Then evaluate the absolute value of the convergent series
\[
S=\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}
\dfrac{(\ln3)^m}{m!n!}\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom nk(\ln2)^k(i\pi)^{n-k}.
\]
