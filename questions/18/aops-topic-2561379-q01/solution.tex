Multiply numerator and denominator by the conjugate $\sqrt{4+h}+2$:
\[\dfrac{\sqrt{4+h}-2}{h}\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt{4+h}+2}{\sqrt{4+h}+2}=\dfrac{(4+h)-4}{h\left(\sqrt{4+h}+2\right)}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{4+h}+2}}\qquad(h\ne0).\]
The simplified form makes sense at $h=0$, where it equals $\dfrac{1}{2+2}=\boxed{\dfrac14}$. (This is the value the original expression approaches as $h$ approaches $0$; it is the slope of the graph of $y=\sqrt x$ at $x=4$.)
