Write the number as $100a+10b+c$. Deleting the units digit requires $10a+b$ to divide $c$, so $c=0$. The other two deletions give $b\mid10a$ and $a\mid b$. Write $b=ka$, where $k$ divides ten. With nonzero digits, $k=1,2,5$ give respectively nine, four and one choices of $a$. Thus there are $\boxed{14}$ numbers.
