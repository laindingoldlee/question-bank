A number is divisible by $9$ exactly when its digit sum is. The digit sum of $x$ is its number of $1$s, so $x$ has at least nine $1$s, and it also has at least one $0$: at least $10$ digits. The smallest $10$-digit number with nine $1$s and one $0$ puts the $0$ as early as possible, in the second place:
\[
x=\boxed{1011111111}.
\]
