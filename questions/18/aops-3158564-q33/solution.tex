Let $r_1=1$ and $r_j=\sqrt{j+r_{j-1}}$. The expression is $n+r_{n-1}$ and increases with $n$. Induction gives $r_j<\sqrt j+1$ for $j\ge1$, since $j+\sqrt{j-1}+1<(\sqrt j+1)^2$. Thus $r_{40}<\sqrt{40}+1<8$, giving $r_{41}<7$ and $42+r_{41}<49$. But $r_{42}>\sqrt{42}>6$, so $43+r_{42}>49$. Therefore $\boxed{n=42}$.
