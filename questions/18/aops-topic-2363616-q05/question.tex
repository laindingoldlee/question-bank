Let $A$ be a vertex of a unit tesseract and $B$ the vertex opposite $A$. An ant starts at $A$ and wants to get to $B$, but it can only walk on the square ($2$-dimensional) faces of the tesseract. How long is the shortest path for the ant? (Assume the path consists of finitely many pieces, each lying in a single square face.)
