An isosceles trapezoid $ABCD$ has $AB\parallel DC$, $AB=4$, $DC=10$, and $AD=BC=5$. The vertices $D,C,F$ lie in this order on a line. Triangle $DEF$ is right at $F$, and $B$ is the midpoint of hypotenuse $DE$. Find $CF$. Choices: (A) $3.25$; (B) $3.5$; (C) $3.75$; (D) $4.0$; (E) $4.25$.
