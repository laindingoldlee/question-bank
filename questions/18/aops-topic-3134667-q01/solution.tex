Choose the four positions occupied by even numbers in $\binom94 $ ways. Their relative order must begin with 6, leaving $ 3!$ arrangements of the other evens. The five odd positions must end with 9, leaving $ 4!$ arrangements of the other odds. Thus the count is $\binom94\cdot3!\cdot4!=\boxed{18144}$.
