Indistinguishable: a selection is determined by the numbers $r\in\{0,\dots,5\}$ and $b\in\{0,\dots,4\}$ of red and blue marbles, not both zero: $6\cdot5-1=\boxed{29}$.

Distinguishable: any nonempty subset of $9$ marbles: $2^9-1=\boxed{511}$.

Problems usually intend indistinguishable marbles of a colour when they ask about ``combinations of colours'', and distinguishable objects when they ask about choosing specific objects; when unclear, the problem should say.
