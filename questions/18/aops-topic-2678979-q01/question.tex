A rook and a knight stand on an infinite chessboard so that neither attacks the other. An earthquake hits: each cell other than the two occupied cells collapses independently with probability $p$, where $0<p<1$. A piece cannot move onto or across a collapsed cell (the knight jumps, so for it only the target cell matters). For which $p$ are the expected number of cells the rook can move to and the expected number of cells the knight can move to equal?
