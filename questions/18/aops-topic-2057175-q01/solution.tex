Let the circle touch $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, $DA$ at $W$, $X$, $Y$, $Z$ respectively. The two tangent segments from an external point to a circle are equal, so
\[
AW=AZ,\qquad BW=BX,\qquad CX=CY,\qquad DY=DZ .
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real[] th={250,10,105,175};
pair tp(real t){return dir(t);}
pair vx(real t1,real t2){return dir((t1+t2)/2)/Cos((t2-t1)/2);}
pair Av=vx(th[3],th[0]+360), Bv=vx(th[0],th[1]+360), Cv=vx(th[1],th[2]), Dv=vx(th[2],th[3]);
draw(circle((0,0),1),blue);
draw(Av--Bv--Cv--Dv--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
pair T1=tp(th[0]),T2=tp(th[1]),T3=tp(th[2]),T4=tp(th[3]);
dot(T1);dot(T2);dot(T3);dot(T4);dot((0,0));
label("$A$",Av,SW); label("$B$",Bv,SE); label("$C$",Cv,NE); label("$D$",Dv,NW);
label("$W$",T1,S); label("$X$",T2,E); label("$Y$",T3,N); label("$Z$",T4,W); label("$O$",(0,0),SE);
draw(Av--(0,0),gray(0.6)+dashed);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Each tangency point lies on its side, so
\[
AB+CD=(AW+WB)+(CY+YD),\qquad BC+AD=(BX+XC)+(AZ+ZD).
\]
Replacing $AW$ by $AZ$, $WB$ by $BX$, $CY$ by $XC$ and $YD$ by $ZD$ turns the first sum into the second. Hence $\boxed{AB+CD=BC+AD}$. $\blacksquare$
