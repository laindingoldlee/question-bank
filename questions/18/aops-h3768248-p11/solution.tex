The hour hand lies in the two specified sectors for 4 hours per day. The minute hand lies there for 10 minutes per hour, totaling another 4 hours. During the four hour-hand-active hours, both hands qualify for 10 minutes per hour, or 40 minutes. Inclusion--exclusion gives $4+4$ hours minus 40 minutes, which is $\boxed{7\text{ h }20\text{ min}}$.
