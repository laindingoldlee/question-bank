The floor must be 1, so $ 1\le\sqrt[17]{x}<2 $, equivalent to $ 1\le x<2^{17}$. Thus the endpoints are 1 and $ 2^{17}-1 $, with difference $\boxed{2^{17}-2=131070}$.
