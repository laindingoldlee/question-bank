We claim $a_n=2^n-1$. It holds for $n=0,1$. Substituting the two preceding formulas into the recurrence gives $3(2^{n-1}-1)-2(2^{n-2}-1)=2^n-1$, completing induction. Therefore
\[\sum_{j=0}^na_j=(2^{n+1}-1)-(n+1)=\boxed{2^{n+1}-n-2}.\]
