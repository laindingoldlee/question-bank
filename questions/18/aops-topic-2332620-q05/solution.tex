Each rally multiplies the share by $\dfrac43$, so after $n$ rallies the share is $20\cdot\left(\dfrac43\right)^n$ percent. The shares are
\[20,\quad\dfrac{80}{3}\approx26.7,\quad\dfrac{320}{9}\approx35.6,\quad\dfrac{1280}{27}\approx47.4,\quad\dfrac{5120}{81}\approx63.2,\]
so $3$ rallies are not enough and $\boxed{4}$ rallies are needed.
