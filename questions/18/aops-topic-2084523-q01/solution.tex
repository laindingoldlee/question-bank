By the binomial theorem,
\[\left(2y+3x^2\right)^4=\sum_{k=0}^{4}\dbinom4k(2y)^{4-k}\left(3x^2\right)^k.\]
The power of $x$ is $2k$, so $x^6$ needs $k=3$:
\[\dbinom43(2y)\left(3x^2\right)^3=4\cdot2y\cdot27x^6=216x^6y.\]
The term is $\boxed{216x^6y}$, with numerical coefficient $216$ (as a polynomial in $x$, the coefficient of $x^6$ is $216y$).
