Dave rolls a fair six-sided die until a six appears for the first time. Independently, Linda rolls a fair six-sided die until a six appears for the first time.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] For a positive integer $k$, find the probability that Linda rolls her die exactly $k$ times and Dave rolls his die $k-1$, $k$ or $k+1$ times.
\item[(b)] The probability that the number of times Dave rolls his die is equal to or within one of the number of times Linda rolls her die can be written as $\dfrac{m}{n}$ in lowest terms. Find $m+n$.
\end{enumerate}
