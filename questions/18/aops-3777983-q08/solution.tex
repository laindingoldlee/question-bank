The lcm is a multiple of $n$, and the gcd lies between 1 and $n$, so necessarily $\operatorname{lcm}(n,x)=2n$ and $n\mid y$. Thus $x$ is even and $\dfrac{x}{2} \mid n$, implying $\dfrac{x}{2} \mid y$. Conversely those conditions work with $n = \dfrac{x}{2}$. Write $x=2k,y=jk$; then $k\ge1,j\ge1$ and $(j+2)k\le20$. For $k=1,\ldots,6$, the counts are $18,8,4,3,2,1$, totaling $\boxed{36}$.
