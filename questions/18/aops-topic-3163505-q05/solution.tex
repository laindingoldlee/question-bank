Let their prices be $p,s$. The equations reduce to $p+2s=3$ and $p+s=\dfrac{5}{2}$. Thus $s=\dfrac{1}{2},p=2$, and the difference is $\boxed{\dfrac{3}{2}\text{ dollars}}$.
