\textbf{(a)} The steps give
\[\left(5(2B+3)+S\right)\cdot10+G+125=(10B+15+S)\cdot10+G+125=100B+10S+G+275.\]
Subtracting $275$ leaves $100B+10S+G$. Since $S$ and $G$ are digits, this number's units digit is $G$, its tens digit is $S$, and the number formed by the remaining digits is $B$.

\textbf{(b)} The steps give
\[\left(50(2F+5)+n\right)\cdot4-1000=(100F+250+n)\cdot4-1000=400F+4n.\]
Dividing by $4$ gives $100F+n$ with $0\le n\le99$, so $F$ is the number formed by all but the last two digits (equivalently, $F$ is the quotient of the result divided by $400$). The random number $n$ only affects the last two digits. $\blacksquare$
