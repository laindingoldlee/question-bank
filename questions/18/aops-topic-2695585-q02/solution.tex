Group by powers of $y$: $9y^2(x^2+6x+9)=9y^2(x+3)^2$, $4(x^2+6x+9)=4(x+3)^2$ and $12y(x^2+6x+9)=12y(x+3)^2$. So LHS $-$ RHS $=(x+3)^2(9y^2-12y+4)=(x+3)^2(3y-2)^2\ge0$. $\blacksquare$
