At most five one-digit terms can occur: partition digits 1 through 9 into divisibility chains $\{1,2,4,8\}$, $\{3,6\}$, $\{5\}$, $\{7\}$, $\{9\}$, and use at most one from each. If there were at least eight terms, at least three would have two or more digits, consuming at least $ 5+2\cdot3=11 $ digits even with five one-digit terms. Only ten distinct decimal digits exist, so at most seven terms are possible. The sequence $ 4,6,7,9,10,23,58 $ uses each digit exactly once and has no divisibility relation between distinct terms. Thus the maximum is $\boxed7 $.
