Let the counts be $p,n,d,q,h$. Subtracting the ten-coin equation from the value equation gives $4n+9d+24q+49h=43$, so $h=0$. If $q=1$, $4n+9d=19$ has no nonnegative solution; $q\ge2$ is impossible. Thus $4n+9d=43$, forcing $d=3,n=4,p=3$. The favourable triples number $3\cdot4\cdot3=36$, out of $\binom{10}3=120$. The answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{3}{10}}$.
