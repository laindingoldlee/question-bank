Let $F(2^k)$ be the odd part of $(2^k)!$, and let $P_k$ be the product of odd positive integers below $2^k$. The even factors, after a factor $2$ is removed from each, give the factorial at the preceding level; thus $F(2^k)=P_kF(2^{k-1})$. Directly, $P_1,P_2,P_3,P_4$ are congruent to $1,3,9,17$ modulo $32$. The odd residues below $32$ pair as $a,32-a$ for $a=1,3,\ldots,15$, giving $P_5\equiv P_4^2\equiv1$. Every later $P_k$ consists of complete blocks of these residues, so is also $1$. Therefore the answer is $1\cdot3\cdot9\cdot17=459\equiv\boxed{11}\pmod{32}$.
