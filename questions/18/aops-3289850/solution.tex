If $a=1$, every term is zero for all $x>0$ with $x\ne1$. If $a\ne1$, let $t=\log_ax$. The excluded bases give $t\ne0,-1,-2$. Change of base reduces the equation to $\dfrac{2}{t}+\dfrac{1}{t+1}+\dfrac{3}{t+2}=0$. Multiplying gives $6t^2+9t+4=0$, whose discriminant is $81-96=-15<0$. Thus there are no solutions for $a\ne1$. The full answer is $\boxed{a=1:\ x>0,x\ne1;\quad a\ne1:\ \text{no solutions}}$.
