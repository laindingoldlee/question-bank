The four groups do not overlap and together make up the whole school, so there are $15+50+45+140=250$ students. Those who play basketball are the $45$ who play only basketball and the $15$ who play both: $60$ students. The percentage is
\[
\dfrac{60}{250}\cdot100\%=\boxed{24\%}.
\]
