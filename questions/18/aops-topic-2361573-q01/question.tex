In the real or complex numbers, division $\dfrac{a}{b}$ is defined using the multiplicative inverse of $b$. Explain (a) why $\dfrac{0}{1}=0$ but $\dfrac{1}{0}$ is undefined; (b) why neither $\dfrac{1}{0}$ nor $\dfrac{0}{0}$ is a rational number; (c) why the usual identity $\dfrac{a}{a}=1$ requires $a\ne0$; and (d) why adjoining an inverse of zero while retaining the ordinary field laws would cause a contradiction.
