Let $ABC$ be a triangle and let $D$ be the point on side $BC$ such that $AD$ bisects angle $BAC$. Suppose that $AD=AC$. Let $E$ be a point on side $AD$ such that $\angle CED=\angle ABD$. Prove that $BD=CE$.
