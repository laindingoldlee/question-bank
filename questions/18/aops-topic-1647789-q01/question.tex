In the first quadrant (points $(x,y)$ with $x\ge0$ and $y\ge0$; the coordinates need not be integers), a flea jumps as follows: from a point $(x,y)$ it can jump either to $(x-4,\,y+6)$ or to $(x+1,\,y-1)$, but it may never jump outside the first quadrant. Find the area of the set of starting points from which the flea can never get farther than $2018$ from the origin.
