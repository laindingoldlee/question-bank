(a) Precisely the $\phi(120)=120(1-\dfrac{1}{2})(1-\dfrac{1}{3})(1-\dfrac{1}{5})=32$ invertible residues have an inverse, each unique. Thus the probability is $\dfrac{32}{120^2}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{450}}$. (b) Remove the self-inverse residues. Since $120=8\cdot3\cdot5$ with coprime factors, the Chinese remainder theorem gives $4\cdot2\cdot2=16$ solutions to $x^2\equiv1$: all four odd residues modulo 8, and $\pm1$ modulo 3 and 5. Therefore there are $32-16=16$ successful unequal ordered pairs out of $120\cdot119$, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{2}{1785}}$.
