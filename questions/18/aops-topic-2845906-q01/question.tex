Three logicians walk into a bar. The bartender asks, ``Beer for all of you?'' The first says ``I don't know.'' The second says ``I don't know.'' The third says ``Yes!'' Assuming each logician knows only whether \emph{he} wants a beer and answers truthfully and logically, explain the answers.
