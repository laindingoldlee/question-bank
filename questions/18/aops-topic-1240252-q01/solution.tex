Let the number be $\dfrac {p}{q}$ with $q>0$. Long division of $p$ by $q$ produces each new digit from the current remainder, which is one of $0,1,\dots,q-1$, and the next remainder depends only on the current one. If a remainder $0$ occurs, the expansion terminates. Otherwise, among the first $q+1$ remainders two are equal (pigeonhole principle), and from then on the digits and remainders repeat in a cycle of length at most $q-1$. $\blacksquare$
