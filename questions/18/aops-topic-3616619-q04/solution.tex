Assign weights 12 to the blue sock,6 to each white sock,4 to each red sock, and 3 to each yellow sock. Total weights by color are $12,2\cdot 6,3\cdot 4,4\cdot 3$, all 12. Thus yellow or white carries 24 of total 48, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$.
