Each dollar performs an independent three-step random walk. Fix a frog. Only dollars starting at distances $1$ and $3$ to either side can arrive there after three steps. Their arrival probabilities are $\dfrac{3}{8},\dfrac{3}{8},\dfrac{1}{8},\dfrac{1}{8}$. Independence is valid because every bill makes its own independent choice even when bills share a frog. The probability this frog gets no bill is
\[
(\dfrac{5}{8})^2(\dfrac{7}{8})^2=\dfrac{1225}{4096}.
\]
By linearity of expectation, the desired number is
\[
2048\left(1-\dfrac{1225}{4096}\right)=\dfrac{2871}{2}.
\]
Hence $\boxed{a+b=2873}$.
