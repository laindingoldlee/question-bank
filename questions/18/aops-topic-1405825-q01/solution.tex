Since $256=2^8$,
\[
\dfrac{1}{256}=\dfrac{1}{2^8}=\dfrac{5^8}{2^8\cdot5^8}=\dfrac{5^8}{10^8}=\dfrac{390625}{100000000}=0.00390625 .
\]
So the expansion terminates after $8$ decimal places, and its last digit is the units digit of $5^8$. Every power of $5$ ends in $5$ (because $5\cdot5=25$ ends in $5$, and multiplying a number ending in $5$ by $5$ again gives a number ending in $5$). The last digit is $\boxed{5}$.
