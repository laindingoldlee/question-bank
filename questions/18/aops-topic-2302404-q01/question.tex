Alejandro and Mia split the check at a restaurant. Alejandro ordered $\$12$ of food and Mia ordered $\$18$. Alejandro paid his $\$12$ plus a $20\%$ tip on his part, and Mia paid her $\$18$ plus a $15\%$ tip on her part. The total tip is $r\%$ of the total check for the table, not including the tip. What is $r$?
