Alejandro's tip is $0.20\cdot12=2.40$ dollars and Mia's is $0.15\cdot18=2.70$ dollars, so the total tip is $5.10$ dollars. The total check is $12+18=30$ dollars, and
\[\dfrac{5.10}{30}=0.17=17\%,\]
so $r=\boxed{17}$.
