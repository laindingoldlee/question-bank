Only $1$ is neither prime nor composite, so on each day he rolls twice with probability $\dfrac15$ and once otherwise. The expected number of rolls per day is $1+\dfrac15=\dfrac65$. The year $2021$ has $365$ days, so by linearity of expectation the expected total is $365\cdot\dfrac65=\boxed{438}$.
