Every morning in $2021$, Joe rolls a fair die with faces $1$ to $5$. If the number shown is neither prime nor composite, he rolls once more (and then stops, whatever the second roll shows); otherwise he stops after the first roll. What is the expected total number of rolls in $2021$?
