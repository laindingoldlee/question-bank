The condition says that, from left to right, the three people appear in the order Daniel, Jeff, Max. In a random arrangement the relative order of these three people is equally likely to be any of the $3!=6$ orders (the other people do not matter). So the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{6}}$.
