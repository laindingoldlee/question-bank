The $8$ equally likely birth orders with at least two boys are
\[
BBG,\ BGB,\ GBB,\ BBB .
\]
Each has probability $\dfrac{1}{8}$, and in three of these four the family has a girl. So the conditional probability is
\[
\dfrac{3}{4}\ \Longrightarrow\ \boxed{\dfrac{3}{4}} .
\]
