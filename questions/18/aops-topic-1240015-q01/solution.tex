The equation is $f(x)=x^3-5x^2+2=0$. Since $f'(x)=3x^2-10x<0$ on $\left(0,\dfrac{10}{3}\right)$, $f$ is strictly decreasing on $[0,2]$, from $f(0)=2>0$ to $f(2)=-10<0$. So it has exactly one zero there: $\boxed{\text{(C)}}$.
