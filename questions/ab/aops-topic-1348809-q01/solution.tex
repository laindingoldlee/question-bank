First choose the $16$ relatives who sit: $\dbinom{18}{16}=\dbinom{18}{2}=153$ ways. Then seat $16$ distinct people around a round table up to rotation: fix one person's seat and arrange the other $15$ in the remaining seats, $15!$ ways. In total
\[
153\cdot15!=153\cdot1307674368000=\boxed{200074178304000}
\]
ways (equivalently, $\dfrac{18!}{2!\cdot16}$).
