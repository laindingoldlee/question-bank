Call the speaker $X$ and his brother $Y$. All groups of $4$ from $7$ number $\dbinom74=35$. The forbidden groups are those containing $X$ but not $Y$: choose the other $3$ members from the $5$ people other than $X$ and $Y$, in $\dbinom53=10$ ways. So the number of allowed groups is
\[
35-10=\boxed{25}.
\]
(Check: groups with neither brother, $\dbinom54=5$; with $Y$ but not $X$, $\dbinom53=10$; with both, $\dbinom52=10$; total $25$.)
