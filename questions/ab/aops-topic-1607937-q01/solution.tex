The sum of the four numbers already works:
\[
\boxed{10+9+5+1=25}.
\]
Any expression that uses each of $1,5,9,10$ exactly once and equals $25$ is a correct answer. (A computer search over all expressions shows that here every solution is just a rearrangement of this sum; no expression with $\times$ or $\div$ gives $25$.)
