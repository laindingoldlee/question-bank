Two positive integers have square product exactly when their squarefree parts agree. The classes here are $\{1,4,9\}$, $\{2,8\}$, and the five singletons $\{3\},\{5\},\{6\},\{7\},\{10\}$. Choose at most one element of each class, including the option to choose none. The count is $(3+1)(2+1)2^5=\boxed{384}$.
