We have $\overline{ababab}=10101(10a+b)$ and $10101=3\cdot7\cdot13\cdot37$. Thus 37 divides one of $2m+1,2m+3,2m+5$. Also $5(2m+1)^3<10^6$, so $m\le28$. These three factors are at most 61, so the divisible factor must equal 37. The only possibilities are $m=16,17,18$, yielding products $213675,252525,295815$. Only the middle product has the required form. Therefore $(m,a,b)=(17,2,5)$ and $m+a+b=\boxed{24}$.
