Write the five terms as $m-2d,\ m-d,\ m,\ m+d,\ m+2d$, where $d<0$ because the progression decreases. The three middle terms add up to $3m=33$, so $m=11$. The product of the extreme terms is
\[
(11-2d)(11+2d)=121-4d^2=57,\qquad d^2=16,
\]
and $d=-4$. The five terms are $19,15,11,7,3$, so the progression starts $a_1=19$ with common difference $-4$, and
\[
a_{100}=19+99\cdot(-4)=19-396=\boxed{-377}.
\]
