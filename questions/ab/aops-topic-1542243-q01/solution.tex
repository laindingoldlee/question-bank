Since $2^4=16\equiv1\pmod{15}$, the powers of $2$ repeat with period $4$ modulo $15$. As $2015=4\cdot503+3$,
\[
2^{2015}=\left(2^4\right)^{503}\cdot2^3\equiv1^{503}\cdot8=8\pmod{15}.
\]
The remainder is $\boxed{8}$.
