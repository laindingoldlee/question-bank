Exactly $k$ misses before success has probability $(\dfrac{1}{2})^{k+1}$ and score $50(0.8)^k$. Therefore the expectation is $25\sum_{k=0}^\infty(0.4)^k=\dfrac{25}{1-0.4}=\boxed{\dfrac{125}{3}}$. The probability of never succeeding is zero, so these cases exhaust the outcomes.
