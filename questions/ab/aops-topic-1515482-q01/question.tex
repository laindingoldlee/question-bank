It takes one man $5$ seconds to paint one face of a cube. Three men are to paint all six faces of each of $188$ cubes. A face is always painted by one man, and two men can never work on the same cube at the same time, but a man may paint some faces of a cube and leave the others for someone else. What is the shortest possible time for the job?
