A quadratic polynomial $P(x)$ has leading coefficient $1$, and both of its roots are positive integers. The number $\dfrac{P(0)}{20^{20}}$ is a positive integer, and among all such polynomials, $P$ has the smallest possible value of $P(-1)$. Then $P(1)$ can be written as $\left(20^k-1\right)^2$ for a positive integer $k$. Find $k$.

$\textbf{(A) }10\qquad\textbf{(B) }15\qquad\textbf{(C) }20\qquad\textbf{(D) }25\qquad\textbf{(E) }30$
