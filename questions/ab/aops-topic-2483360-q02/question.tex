A four-dimensional hypercube with edge length $7$ is the set of points $(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)$ with $0\le x_i\le7$ for each $i$, where the distance between two points is $\sqrt{(x_1-y_1)^2+(x_2-y_2)^2+(x_3-y_3)^2+(x_4-y_4)^2}$. What is the length of the longest line segment that fits inside this hypercube?
