For any two points $(x_i)$ and $(y_i)$ of the hypercube, each coordinate difference satisfies $|x_i-y_i|\le7$, so their distance is at most
\[\sqrt{7^2+7^2+7^2+7^2}=\sqrt{196}=14.\]
Equality holds for the opposite corners $(0,0,0,0)$ and $(7,7,7,7)$, and the segment joining them lies in the hypercube (its points $(t,t,t,t)$ with $0\le t\le7$ satisfy all the inequalities). So the longest segment has length $\boxed{14}$.

(This continues the pattern of diagonals: a square of side $7$ has diagonal $7\sqrt2$, a cube $7\sqrt3$, and the hypercube $7\sqrt4=14$; each step applies Pythagoras once more.)

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair e1=(1,0), e2=(0,1), e3=(0.42,0.3), e4=(1.25,0.55);
pair P(int a,int b,int c,int d){return a*e1+b*e2+c*e3+d*e4;}
for(int a=0;a<2;++a) for(int b=0;b<2;++b) for(int c=0;c<2;++c) for(int d=0;d<2;++d){
  if(a==0) draw(P(0,b,c,d)--P(1,b,c,d),gray);
  if(b==0) draw(P(a,0,c,d)--P(a,1,c,d),gray);
  if(c==0) draw(P(a,b,0,d)--P(a,b,1,d),gray);
  if(d==0) draw(P(a,b,c,0)--P(a,b,c,1),gray);
  dot(P(a,b,c,d),gray);
}
draw(P(0,0,0,0)--P(1,1,1,1),red+linewidth(1));
dot(P(0,0,0,0),red); dot(P(1,1,1,1),red);
label("$(0,0,0,0)$",P(0,0,0,0),SW,fontsize(8pt));
label("$(7,7,7,7)$",P(1,1,1,1),NE,fontsize(8pt));
label("$7$",(P(0,0,0,0)+P(1,0,0,0))/2,S,fontsize(8pt));
label("$14$",(0.2,0.62),red);
\end{asy}
