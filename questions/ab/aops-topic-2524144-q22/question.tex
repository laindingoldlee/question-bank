For positive integers $x$ and $y$ let $f(x,y)=\gcd(x,y)\cdot\operatorname{lcm}(x,y)$. What is the minimum value of $f(x,y)$ over integers $x$ and $y$ with $5\le x\le10$ and $5\le y\le10$?
