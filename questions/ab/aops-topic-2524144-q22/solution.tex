For every prime $p$, if $p$ appears to the powers $\alpha$ and $\beta$ in $x$ and $y$, then it appears to the power $\min(\alpha,\beta)$ in the gcd and $\max(\alpha,\beta)$ in the lcm, and $\min+\max=\alpha+\beta$. Hence $\gcd(x,y)\cdot\operatorname{lcm}(x,y)=xy$. Its minimum for $x,y\ge5$ is $5\cdot5=\boxed{25}$.
