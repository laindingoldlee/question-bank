You have $10$ litres of milk in a full $10$-litre container, and two empty containers with capacities $7$ litres and $3$ litres. The containers have no markings, and you may only pour milk from one container into another until the first is empty or the second is full. Without wasting any milk, how can you divide it into two portions of exactly $5$ litres each?
