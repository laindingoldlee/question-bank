There are $\binom{17}2=136$ matches, at most 34 decisive. Every decisive match contributes at most two to the sum of absolute final scores, while tied matches contribute zero. Thus that sum is at most 68. Seventeen distinct integer scores, however, have absolute values summing to at least $0+2(1+2+\cdots+8)=72$, since the seventeen integers closest to zero minimize this sum. This contradiction proves two scores coincide.
