The overall average rate is the total number of words divided by the total time (not the average of the two rates):
\[
\dfrac{540{,}000+1200}{(45+5)\cdot60}=\dfrac{541{,}200}{3000}=\boxed{180.4\text{ words per minute}}.
\]
(The two separate rates are $200$ and $4$ words per minute; their plain average, $102$, would weight the two sessions equally, although the novel took nine times as long.)
