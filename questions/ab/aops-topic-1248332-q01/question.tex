In a video game, each press of a button moves your character one unit right or one unit up, each with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. You start at $(0,0)$ and want to reach $(6,6)$, but neither coordinate can exceed $6$: if a press would push a coordinate past $6$, nothing happens. What is the expected number of presses needed to reach $(6,6)$?
