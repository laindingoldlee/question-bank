Think of each press as a fair coin flip (right $=$ heads, up $=$ tails). You reach $(6,6)$ at the first moment you have seen at least $6$ heads and at least $6$ tails; extra heads after the sixth are the wasted presses.

Let $E(x,y)$ be the expected number of further presses from $(x,y)$. On the top or right edge only one direction helps, and each press succeeds with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, so $E(6,y)=2(6-y)$ and $E(x,6)=2(6-x)$. Inside,
\[E(x,y)=1+\dfrac{1}{2}E(x+1,y)+\dfrac{1}{2}E(x,y+1).\]
Filling in the table backwards from $(6,6)$ gives
\[E(0,0)=\dfrac{3765}{256}=\boxed{14.70703125}.\]
(Equivalently, $E=12+2\sum_{k=0}^{5}\dbinom{5+k}{k}\dfrac{6-k}{2^{5+k}}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot2$ counts the expected wasted presses; the recursion is the easiest way to evaluate it.)
