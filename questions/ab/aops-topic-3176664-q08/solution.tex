Writing elementary symmetric sums $e_1,e_2,e_3$, the first two equations give $e_1=3$ and $3=e_1^2-2e_2$, hence $e_2=3$. The third gives $3=e_1^3-3e_1e_2+3e_3=3e_3$, so $e_3=1$. Thus $x,y,z$ are roots of $t^3-3t^2+3t-1=(t-1)^3$. Their only value is 1, and $\boxed{12000N=12000}$.
