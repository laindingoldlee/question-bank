An isosceles triangle with equal sides of $5$ inches and a base of $6$ inches is inscribed in a circle. What is the radius of the circle, in inches? Express your answer as a mixed number. A student argued: ``The altitude to the base is $4$; the centroid divides the median in the ratio $2:1$, so the radius is $\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot4=2\dfrac{2}{3}$.'' Explain the error and find the correct radius.
