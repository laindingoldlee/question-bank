\textbf{The error.} The centre of the circle through the three vertices is the \emph{circumcentre} $O$, the point equidistant from $A$, $B$ and $C$. The centroid $G$ is where the medians meet; it is $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of the way down the median, but its distances to the vertices are in general not equal. They coincide only in an equilateral triangle. Here $GA=\dfrac{8}{3}$ but $GB=\sqrt{3^2+\left(\dfrac{4}{3}\right)^2}=\dfrac{\sqrt{97}}{3}\ne\dfrac{8}{3}$.

\textbf{Correct radius.} Let $M$ be the midpoint of the base $BC$, so $AM\perp BC$, $BM=3$ and $AM=\sqrt{5^2-3^2}=4$. The circumcentre lies on $AM$ (the perpendicular bisector of $BC$), say at distance $R$ from $A$, so $OM=4-R$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real R=25/8;
pair O=(0,0),A=(0,R),B=(-3,R-4),C=(3,R-4);
pair M=(0,R-4),G=(0,R-8/3);
draw(circle(O,R),linewidth(0.9));
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(1));
draw(A--M,dashed);draw(O--B,blue);
real s=0.22; draw(M+(s,0)--M+(s,s)--M+(0,s));
dot(O,blue);dot(G,red);
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$M$",M,S);
label("$O$",O,SE,blue);label("$G$",G,NE,red);
label("$5$",(A+B)/2,NW);label("$5$",(A+C)/2,NE);label("$3$",(B+M)/2,S);
label("$R$",(O+B)/2,dir(80),blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

From $OB=R$ and the right triangle $OMB$,
\[
R^2=3^2+(4-R)^2=25-8R+R^2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad R=\dfrac{25}{8}=\boxed{3\dfrac{1}{8}}\text{ inches}.
\]
(Check: $R=\dfrac{abc}{4K}=\dfrac{5\cdot5\cdot6}{4\cdot12}=\dfrac{25}{8}$.)
