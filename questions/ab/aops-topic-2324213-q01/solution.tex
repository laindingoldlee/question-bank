As with $\dfrac{17}{5}=3+\dfrac25$, we take out as many whole copies of the denominator as possible. Since $3(n-2)=3n-6$,
\[3n-1=3(n-2)+5,\]
so
\[\dfrac{3n-1}{n-2}=\dfrac{3(n-2)+5}{n-2}=\boxed{3+\dfrac{5}{n-2}}.\]
This is polynomial division: $3n-1$ divided by $n-2$ gives quotient $3$ and remainder $5$. For example, $n=7$ gives $\dfrac{20}{5}=4=3+\dfrac55$.
