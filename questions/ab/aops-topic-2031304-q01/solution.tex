Collect the $x$ terms: $1=\sqrt[3]9\,x-x=\left(\sqrt[3]9-1\right)x$. Since $\sqrt[3]9\ne1$, we can divide:
\[x=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt[3]9-1}.\]
To rationalise, let $t=\sqrt[3]9$, so $t^3=9$, and use $t^3-1=(t-1)(t^2+t+1)$:
\[x=\dfrac{t^2+t+1}{(t-1)(t^2+t+1)}=\dfrac{t^2+t+1}{t^3-1}=\dfrac{t^2+t+1}{8}.\]
Since $t^2=\sqrt[3]{81}=3\sqrt[3]3$,
\[x=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{\sqrt[3]9-1}=\dfrac{1+\sqrt[3]9+3\sqrt[3]3}{8}}.\]
