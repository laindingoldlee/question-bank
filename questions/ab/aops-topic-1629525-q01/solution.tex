Bring everything to one side: $4x^2+4x-40x=4x^2-36x=0$, and factor:
\[
4x(x-9)=0 .
\]
A product is zero only if a factor is zero, so $x=0$ or $x=9$. Do not divide both sides by $x$ at the start: that would lose the solution $x=0$. The solutions are $\boxed{x=0\text{ or }x=9}$.
