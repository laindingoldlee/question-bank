Initially there are $n-4$ apples, so the event means selecting two apples. Thus $\dfrac{(n-4)(n-5)}{n(n-1)}=\dfrac{1}{3}$. Rearrangement gives $2n^2-26n+60=2(n-3)(n-10)=0$. Since four oranges and at least two apples are needed, $n\ge6$, leaving $\boxed{n=10}$.
