The perimeter is $4\cdot BC=16$, so $AB=16-BC-CA=16-4-6=\boxed{6}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0),C=(4,0),A=(2,sqrt(32));
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(1));
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);
label("$4$",(B+C)/2,S);label("$6$",(A+C)/2,E);label("$6$",(A+B)/2,W,blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The sides $4,6,6$ satisfy the triangle inequality ($4+6>6$), so such a triangle exists; it is isosceles with $AB=CA=6$.
