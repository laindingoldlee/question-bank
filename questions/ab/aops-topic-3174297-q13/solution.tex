Three explosions in one player's hand require at least three rounds. To finish by round three, all three rounds must choose the same player and all three cards must explode. For either named player this has probability $\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^3=\dfrac{1}{216}$. The two events are disjoint, so the answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{108}}$.
