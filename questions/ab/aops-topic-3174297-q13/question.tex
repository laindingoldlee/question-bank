Each round independently chooses one of three equally likely actions: player $F$ draws a card, player $G$ draws a card, or nobody draws. Every drawn card independently explodes with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. A player loses on their third exploding card. Find the probability the game ends within three rounds.
