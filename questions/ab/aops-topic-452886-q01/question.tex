Two circles of different sizes overlap. Region $B$ is the part of the larger circle outside the smaller one, region $C$ is the part of the smaller circle outside the larger one, and $X$ is their region of overlap. The circles are moved, without changing their sizes, so that the area of $X$ increases by $50$\%, while the two circles still overlap without either containing the other. By what percent does the difference of areas $B-C$ change?
