The endpoint $ 10^{100}$ has digit sum $ 1 $ and is ineligible. Pad all smaller numbers to one hundred digits. The desired strings correspond to nonnegative solutions of $ d_1+\cdots+d_{100}=3 $. The digit upper bound $ 9 $ is automatic. Stars and bars gives $\binom{102}{3}=\boxed{171700}$.
