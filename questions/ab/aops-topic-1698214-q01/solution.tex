Put $u=x^3$. Multiply by $4$ and complete the square:
\[
4u^2+20u=4y^8+412\ \Longrightarrow\ (2u+5)^2-(2y^4)^2=437=19\cdot23 .
\]
So $(2u+5-2y^4)(2u+5+2y^4)=437$. The two factors $d\le e$ (since $2y^4\ge0$) are integers with $de=437$ and $e-d=4y^4$. The factor pairs are $(1,437)$, $(19,23)$, $(-437,-1)$, $(-23,-19)$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $e-d=436$: $y^4=109$, not a fourth power.
\item $e-d=4$: $y^4=1$, so $y=\pm1$. For $(19,23)$: $2u+5=\dfrac{19+23}{2}=21$, so $u=8$ and $x=2$. For $(-23,-19)$: $2u+5=-21$, so $u=-13$, which is not a cube.
\end{itemize}
The solutions are $\boxed{(x,y)=(2,1)\text{ and }(2,-1)}$. Check: $64+40=104=1+103$.
