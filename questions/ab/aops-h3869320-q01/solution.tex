Two real numbers have the same distance to the nearest integer exactly when their sum or difference is an integer. Thus the desired $x$ satisfy at least one of
\[
x^2+3x\in\mathbb Z,\qquad x^2-3x\in\mathbb Z.
\]
The first function increases from $0$ to $460$ on $(0,20)$, giving $459$ solutions. The second has minimum $-\dfrac{9}{4}$ at $x=\dfrac{3}{2}$. The values $-2,-1$ each have two preimages, $0$ has the one allowed preimage $3$, and the integers $1,\ldots,339$ each have one. This gives $344$ solutions.

At an overlap, $6x$ is an integer. Write $x=\dfrac{k}{6}$, where $1\le k\le119$. The condition $x^2-3x\in\mathbb Z$ becomes $36\mid k(k-18)$. Modulo $4$ this forces $k$ even, and modulo $9$ it forces $3\mid k$, so $6\mid k$. Conversely every integer $x=1,\ldots,19$ works. There are $19$ overlaps, and the total is
\[
\boxed{459+344-19=784}.
\]
