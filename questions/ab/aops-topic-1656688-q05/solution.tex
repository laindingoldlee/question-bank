There are $4!=24$ arrangements, of which $3!=6$ begin with $0$, leaving $18$ numbers.

\textbf{Thousands place.} Each of $2,1,8$ leads in $3!=6$ numbers: contribution $6(2+1+8)\cdot1000=66000$.

\textbf{Each other place.} Fix a nonzero digit $d$ in, say, the hundreds place. The thousands digit is one of the other two nonzero digits ($2$ ways) and the remaining two digits fill the last places ($2$ ways), so $d$ appears there $4$ times. ($0$ contributes nothing.) Each of the hundreds, tens and units places contributes $4(2+1+8)=44$ times its place value:
\[
44\cdot(100+10+1)=4884 .
\]
The sum is $66000+4884=\boxed{70884}$.
