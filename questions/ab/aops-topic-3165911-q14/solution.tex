Track the score difference, stopping at $\pm3$. After two rounds, surviving counts at differences $-2,0,2$ are $1,2,1$. If after $2t$ rounds they are $v,2v,v$, then after two more rounds, discarding paths that hit $\pm3$, they are $3v,6v,3v$: from the middle there are two returns and one route to each side, while a side has one surviving route to the middle and one back to itself. By induction, at round ten the side counts are $3^4$ each. The eleventh round ends the game exactly when it steps outward from one of these sides. Hence the answer is $\boxed{2\cdot3^4=162}$.
