We have $2020=2^2\cdot5\cdot101$ and $2021=43\cdot47$, so the two numbers have no common prime factor, and
\[M=2020^{2020}\cdot2021^{2020}=2^{4040}\cdot5^{2020}\cdot101^{2020}\cdot43^{2020}\cdot47^{2020}.\]
The number of trailing zeros is the number of factors $10=2\cdot5$, which is $\min(4040,2020)=\boxed{2020}$.
