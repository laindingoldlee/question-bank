Use $A=(0,0),B=(2,0),C=(2,2),D=(0,2)$. Then $P=(1,\sqrt3),Q=(2-\sqrt3,1),R=(1,2-\sqrt3),S=(\sqrt3,1)$. The perpendicular diagonals $PR,QS$ both have length $2(\sqrt3-1)$. Thus $[PQRS]=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot4(\sqrt3-1)^2=\boxed{8-4\sqrt3}$.

\end{asy}
