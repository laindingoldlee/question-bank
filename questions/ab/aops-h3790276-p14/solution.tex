Let the original side lengths be $a,b$. Equal piece perimeters force each cut to bisect one side. The two cuts use different orientations, since their piece perimeters differ. Thus $2a+b=40$ and $a+2b=50$. Adding gives $3(a+b)=90$, so the original perimeter is $2(a+b)=\boxed{60}$.
