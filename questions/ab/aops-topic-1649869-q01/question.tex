Let
\[
D=\{d\mid -5<d\le6,\ d\in\mathbb{R}\},\qquad E=\{e\mid 5<e\le6,\ e\in\mathbb{R}\},\qquad F=\{f\mid -6\le f<3,\ f\in\mathbb{R}\},
\]
with universal set $\mathbb{R}$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Determine $(F\cup D)\cap(D\cup E)$ and draw a Venn diagram of it.
\item[(b)] Determine $(F\cap D\cap E)^c$ and draw a Venn diagram of it.
\end{enumerate}
