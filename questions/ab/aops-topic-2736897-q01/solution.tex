Drawing continues while every colour has at most $3$ balls and some colour has at most $1$. Such a state has at most $1+3+3=7$ balls, and $7$ is possible (for example $1$ blue, $3$ red, $3$ white). After $8$ draws one of the stopping conditions must hold: if no colour has $4$, then all have at most $3$, and with $8$ balls each colour has at least $8-6=2$. So the largest number is $\boxed{8}$.
