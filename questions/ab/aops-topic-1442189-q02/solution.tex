First find the order of $7$ modulo $1000$. We have $7^4=2401\equiv401$, and
\[
401^2=160801\equiv801,\qquad 401^4\equiv801^2=641601\equiv601,\qquad 401^5\equiv601\cdot401=241001\equiv1 \pmod{1000}.
\]
So $7^{20}\equiv1\pmod{1000}$, and we need $7^7$ modulo $20$. Since $7^2=49\equiv9$ and $7^4\equiv81\equiv1\pmod{20}$,
\[
7^7=7^4\cdot7^3\equiv343\equiv3\pmod{20}.
\]
Therefore $7^{7^7}\equiv7^3=343\pmod{1000}$, and the remainder is $\boxed{343}$.

(If the expression were read as $\left(7^7\right)^7=7^{49}$, the same method gives $7^{49}\equiv7^{9}\equiv607$; the usual convention is that towers are evaluated from the top, giving $7^{\left(7^7\right)}$.)
