Let there be $N$ vases, each costing $1$ unit. Revenue: $0.8N\cdot1.3=1.04N$ from the first sale and $0.9(0.2N-17)$ from the last. Overall profit $5\%$ means total revenue $1.05N$:
\[1.04N+0.18N-15.3=1.05N\quad\Longrightarrow\quad0.17N=15.3\quad\Longrightarrow\quad N=\boxed{90}.\]
