Use $A=(0,0),B=(6,0),C=(6,6),D=(0,6),E=(6,3)$. Lines $AC$ and $DE$ have equations $y=x$ and $y=6-\dfrac{x}{2}$, meeting at $F=(4,4)$. Shoelace on $A,B,E,F$ gives area $\dfrac{1}{2}(18+24-12)=\boxed{15}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(6,0),C=(6,6),D=(0,6),Ep=(6,3),F=(4,4);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C);draw(D--Ep);filldraw(A--B--Ep--F--cycle,lightblue+opacity(.4),blue);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$E$",Ep,E);label("$F$",F,N);
\end{asy}
