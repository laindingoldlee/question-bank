On the streets, a shortest route uses only blocks towards the destination.
\begin{itemize}
\item From home to the southwest corner she rides $2$ blocks east and $1$ block north in some order: $\dbinom31=3$ routes.
\item The diagonal path through the park is fixed.
\item From the northeast corner to school she rides $2$ blocks east and $2$ blocks north in some order: $\dbinom42=6$ routes.
\end{itemize}
By the multiplication principle there are $3\cdot6=\boxed{\textbf{(E) }18}$ routes.
