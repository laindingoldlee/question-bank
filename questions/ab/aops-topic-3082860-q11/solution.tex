Increasing $ 14 $ to $ 16 $ gives area $ 16\cdot12=192=14^2-4 $. Increasing $ 12 $ to $ 14 $ gives $ 196 $, and $ 196+4=200 $ is not a square. Thus the $\boxed{14\text{-inch dimension was increased to }16\text{ inches}}$.
