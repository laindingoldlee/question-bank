For odd length $2k+1$, the conditions force the alternating string $101\cdots01$. For even length $2k$, exactly $k$ ones occur; $k-1$ zeroes separate them, and one extra zero may go in any of the $k-1$ internal gaps or the final gap, giving $k$ choices. The possible lengths are $1$ through $11$. The sole length-eleven string is $1365$ in decimal, so it and all admissible shorter strings are allowed. There are six odd-length strings and $1+2+3+4+5=15$ even-length strings, for $\boxed{21}$.
