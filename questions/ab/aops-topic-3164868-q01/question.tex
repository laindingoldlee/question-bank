A secret starts with one informed person. At the end of each hour, every person who first learned it during the preceding hour tells two previously uninformed people, and nobody is told twice. A city has $524300$ people. If Beth is the last person informed, and an announcement occurs exactly $19$ hours after the process starts, does she learn the secret before that announcement?
