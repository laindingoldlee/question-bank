After $h$ complete hours, the cumulative number informed is $1+2+\cdots+2^h=2^{h+1}-1$, until the city is exhausted. At hour $18$ this is $2^{19}-1=524287$, fewer than $524300$. The remaining $13$ people hear at hour $19$. Thus Beth first hears at exactly $19$ hours, not before. If the announcement is made at that same instant, the model gives a tie and does not determine which simultaneous message she notices first.
