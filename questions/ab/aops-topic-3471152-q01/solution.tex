Valid constructions are $0=0(2+2+5)$, $1=5-2-2+0$, $2=0(5-2)+2$, $3=0\cdot2+5-2$, $4=0\cdot5+2+2$, $5=0(2+2)+5$, and $6=2(5-2)+0$. Each uses exactly the stipulated multiset of digits.
