Each entry is at least five. In any triple, the largest exceeds the sum of the other two and is therefore at least eleven. Every triple sum is at least $5+5+11=21$. Summing over all 42 cyclic triples counts each entry three times, giving total at least $\dfrac{42\cdot21}{3}=294$. Repeating the block $5,5,11$ fourteen times attains the bound. Thus the minimum is $\boxed{294}$.
