Every citizen wears a green or blue hat and belongs to exactly one neighborhood. Every citizen in a neighborhood, including oneself, counts as a neighbor. Averaged over green citizens,65\% of neighbors are green; averaged over blue citizens,80\% are blue. Neighborhood sizes may differ. Find the ratio of green to blue citizens.
