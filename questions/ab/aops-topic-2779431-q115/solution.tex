For neighborhood $i$, let $g_i,b_i$ be its green and blue counts and $n_i=g_i+b_i$. Its contribution to the sum, over green citizens, of the blue-neighbor fraction is $\dfrac{g_ib_i}{n_i}$. This is also its contribution to the sum, over blue citizens, of the green-neighbor fraction. Therefore $0.35G=\sum_i \dfrac{g_ib_i}{n_i}=0.20B$, where $G,B$ are total populations of the two colors. Hence $\boxed{G:B=4:7}$.
