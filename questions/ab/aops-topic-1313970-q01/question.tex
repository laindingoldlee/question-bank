Jonathan, his brother, their father and $4$ indistinguishable boxes of cupcakes ride in a car with $7$ seats: $2$ in the front row (one is the driver's seat), $2$ in the middle row and $3$ in the back row. Each box takes a whole seat and boxes are not stacked. The father drives, and Jonathan refuses to sit in the back row. How many arrangements are there?
