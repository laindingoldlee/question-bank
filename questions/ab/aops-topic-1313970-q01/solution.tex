The father takes the driver's seat. The remaining $6$ seats hold Jonathan, his brother and the $4$ identical boxes, one per seat. Jonathan has $3$ allowed seats (front passenger or either middle seat), his brother then has $5$ choices, and the boxes fill the other $4$ seats in $1$ way. The total is $3\cdot5=\boxed{15}$.
