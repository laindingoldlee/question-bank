For every real pair $(x,y)$ satisfying $\log_2(2x+y)=\log_4(x^2+xy+7y^2)$, a real $K$ may satisfy $\log_3(3x+y)=\log_9(3x^2+4xy+Ky^2)$. Find the product of the possible $K$ values arising from such pairs.
