Bring everything to one side over the common denominator $a^2b^2>0$:
\[\dfrac{a^2}{b^2}+\dfrac{b^2}{a^2}-2=\dfrac{a^4-2a^2b^2+b^4}{a^2b^2}=\dfrac{\left(a^2-b^2\right)^2}{a^2b^2}\ge0 .\]
So $\dfrac{a^2}{b^2}+\dfrac{b^2}{a^2}\ge2$, with equality exactly when $a^2=b^2$, that is, $\boxed{a=b}$ (as $a,b>0$).

(Equivalently, by AM-GM, $\dfrac{a^2}{b^2}+\dfrac{b^2}{a^2}\ge2\sqrt{\dfrac{a^2}{b^2}\cdot\dfrac{b^2}{a^2}}=2$.)
