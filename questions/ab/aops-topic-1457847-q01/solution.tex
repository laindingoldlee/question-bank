\textbf{(a)} With $p=3x$ and $q=2y$:
\[
(3x-2y)^2=(3x)^2-2(3x)(2y)+(2y)^2=\boxed{9x^2-12xy+4y^2}.
\]
\textbf{(b)} With $p=x$, $q=6$: $(x-6)(x+6)=\boxed{x^2-36}$. The identity holds because $(p-q)(p+q)=p^2+pq-qp-q^2$ and the middle terms cancel.

\textbf{(c)} $97^2=(100-3)^2=10000-600+9=\boxed{9409}$, and $94\cdot106=(100-6)(100+6)=10000-36=\boxed{9964}$.
