\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Use the identity $(p-q)^2=p^2-2pq+q^2$ to expand $(3x-2y)^2$ without multiplying out $(3x-2y)(3x-2y)$ term by term.
\item[(b)] Use the identity $(p-q)(p+q)=p^2-q^2$ to expand $(x-6)(x+6)$, and explain why the identity is true.
\item[(c)] Use these identities to compute $97^2$ and $94\cdot106$ without a calculator.
\end{enumerate}
