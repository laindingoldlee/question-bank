Use $A=(0,0),B=(1,0),C=(1,1),D=(0,1)$. Then $P=(\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{1}{2}),D^{\prime}=(2,1),C^{\prime}=(1,-1)$. The listed quadrilateral is simple and concave. Shoelace in the indicated order gives $\dfrac{1}{2}|\dfrac{1}{2}+0-1+\dfrac{1}{2}-(1+1+0-\dfrac{1}{2})|=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{4}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(1,0),C=(1,1),D=(0,1),P=(.5,.5),Dp=(2,1),Cp=(1,-1);filldraw(P--Dp--B--Cp--cycle,lightblue,blue);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);label("$P$",P,W);label("$D^{\prime}$",Dp,E);label("$C^{\prime}$",Cp,S);label("$B$",B,E);
\end{asy}
