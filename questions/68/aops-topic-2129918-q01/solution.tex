Raise both sides of $4^{x_1}=5$ to the power $x_2$:
\[4^{x_1x_2}=\left(4^{x_1}\right)^{x_2}=5^{x_2}=6 .\]
Continuing in the same way with $x_3,x_4,\dots,x_{124}$ (there are $127-4+1=124$ equations),
\[4^{x_1x_2\cdots x_{124}}=128 .\]
Since $4=2^2$ and $128=2^7$, this says $2^{2x_1x_2\cdots x_{124}}=2^7$, so $x_1x_2\cdots x_{124}=\dfrac{7}{2}$ and
\[4x_1x_2\cdots x_{124}=\boxed{14}.\]
(Equivalently, $x_k=\log_{k+3}(k+4)$ and the product of these logarithms telescopes to $\log_4128=\dfrac{7}{2}$.)
