Iniki has large, medium and small metal bars. The large bars each weigh $8$ kg, the medium bars $6$ kg and the small bars $3$ kg. Iron, nickel and lead are present in the ratio $4:1:3$ in each large bar, $2:1:3$ in each medium bar and $1:1:1$ in each small bar. If Iniki wants to melt together a combination of bars to make an alloy that contains $40$ kg of iron, $20$ kg of nickel and $40$ kg of lead, how many small bars will she have to use?
