A higher seed wins a series with probability $p=(\dfrac{2}{3})^3[1+3(\dfrac{1}{3})+6(\dfrac{1}{3})^2]=\dfrac{64}{81}$. Put $q=\dfrac{17}{81}$. Team $3$ first beats $2$ with probability $q$, then faces $1$ with probability $p$ or $4$ with probability $q$. Its final winning probabilities are $q,p$, respectively. Thus the answer is $q(pq+qp)=\boxed{\dfrac{36992}{531441}\approx6.96\%}$.
