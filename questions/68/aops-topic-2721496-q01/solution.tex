Divisibility by $4$ needs $4\mid\overline{5c}$, so $c=2$ or $6$. Divisibility by $9$ needs $9\mid a+b+5+c$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $c=2$: $a+b\equiv2\pmod9$, and $a+b=2$ is impossible with distinct digits avoiding $2$ and $a\ge1$; so $a+b=11$, avoiding $2$ and $5$: $(a,b)=(3,8),(4,7),(7,4),(8,3)$.
\item $c=6$: $a+b=7$ or $16$, avoiding $5$ and $6$: $(3,4),(4,3),(7,0)$ and $(7,9),(9,7)$.
\end{itemize}
The numbers are $3852,4752,7452,8352,3456,4356,7056,7956,9756$: $\boxed{9}$ of them.
