Let $x=0.973636\ldots$. Then $10000x=9736.3636\ldots$ and $100x=97.3636\ldots$, so $9900x=9639$ and
\[x=\dfrac{9639}{9900}=\dfrac{1071}{1100}.\]
Since $1071=3^2\cdot7\cdot17$ and $1100=2^2\cdot5^2\cdot11$ share no prime factor, the answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{1071}{1100}}$.
