The angle bisector theorem gives $\dfrac{BD}{DC}=\dfrac{AB}{AC}=\dfrac{13}{15}$. Since $BD+DC=14$, we obtain $BD=14\cdot\dfrac{13}{28}=\boxed{\dfrac{13}{2}}$. The unequal sides rule out a symmetry argument for $BD=7$.

\end{asy}
