A rectangle in the strip is determined by its left and right edges, two of the $m+1$ vertical grid lines. So there are $\dbinom{m+1}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{m(m+1)}{2}}$ rectangles. This is the $m$th triangular number $1+2+\cdots+m$ (there are $m$ rectangles of length $1$, $m-1$ of length $2$, ..., $1$ of length $m$), and it also counts handshakes among $m+1$ people: both are choices of $2$ objects out of $m+1$.
