Let the axis vertices be $A=(u,0)$ and $B=(0,v)$, with $u,v>0$. Rotating the side vector by $60^\circ$ shows that the third vertex in the first quadrant is $\left(\dfrac{u+\sqrt3v}{2},\dfrac{v+\sqrt3u}{2}\right)$. The opposite orientation has at least one negative coordinate, since $u>\sqrt3v$ and $v>\sqrt3u$ cannot both hold. Therefore $u+\sqrt3v=40$ and $\sqrt3u+v=50$, giving $u=25\sqrt3-20$, $v=20\sqrt3-25$. The side squared is $u^2+v^2=4100-2000\sqrt3$. Area is $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}(u^2+v^2)=1025\sqrt3-1500\approx\boxed{275.35}$.
