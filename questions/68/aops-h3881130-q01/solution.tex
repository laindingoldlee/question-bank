The identity $\sum_{j=0}^{n}F_j=F_{n+2}-1$ follows by telescoping $F_j=F_{j+2}-F_{j+1}$. Summing this identity twice more gives
\[
\sum_{j=0}^{n}\binom{n-j+2}{2}F_j
=F_{n+6}-\dfrac{n^2+7n+16}{2}.
\]
The binomial coefficient counts how many times $F_j$ appears in three successive partial sums. Setting $n=18$ matches the required sum, giving
\[
F_{24}-\dfrac{18^2+7(18)+16}{2}=46368-233=46135.
\]
Thus the last two digits are $\boxed{35}$.
