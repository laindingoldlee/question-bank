By Legendre's formula, $v_2(24!)=12+6+3+1=22$ and $v_2(52!)=26+13+6+3+1=49$. Write $52!+24!=24!\left(\dfrac{52!}{24!}+1\right)$. Here $\dfrac{52!}{24!}=25\cdot26\cdots52$ is even, so the bracket is odd. The highest power is $\boxed{2^{22}}$.
