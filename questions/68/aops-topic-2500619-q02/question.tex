Every day in an eight-day interval, the Cents Lord puts a whole number of cents into her piggy bank. The numbers of cents she puts in on consecutive days form an increasing arithmetic progression, and she puts $2008$ cents into the piggy bank in total over the eight days. What is the least possible number of cents she could have put into her piggy bank on any one of the days?
