The other two angles sum to at most $136^\circ$, leaving at least $180^\circ-136^\circ=44^\circ$. The triangle with angles $44^\circ,68^\circ,68^\circ$ attains this bound. Thus $\boxed{44^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(-1,0),B=(1,0),C=(0,tan(68*pi/180));draw(A--B--C--cycle);label("$68^\circ$",A+(0.3,0.2));label("$68^\circ$",B+(-0.3,0.2));label("$44^\circ$",C+(0,-0.4));label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,N);
\end{asy}
