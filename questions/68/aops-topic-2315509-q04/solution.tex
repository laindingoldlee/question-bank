Write $[\cdot]$ for area and $T=[ABC]$. The ratios give $AZ=\dfrac78AB$, $ZB=\dfrac18AB$, $BX=\dfrac78BC$, $XC=\dfrac18BC$, $CY=\dfrac78CA$, $YA=\dfrac18CA$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0), B=(8,0), C=(2.5,6);
pair X=B+7/8*(C-B), Y=C+7/8*(A-C), Z=A+7/8*(B-A);
fill(X--Y--Z--cycle,rgb(0.85,0.9,1));
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); draw(X--Y--Z--cycle,blue+linewidth(0.9));
dot(A); dot(B); dot(C); dot(X); dot(Y); dot(Z);
label("$A$",A,SW); label("$B$",B,SE); label("$C$",C,N); label("$X$",X,NE); label("$Y$",Y,W); label("$Z$",Z,SW);
label("$7$",(Z+A)/2,S,fontsize(8pt)); label("$1$",(Z+B)/2,S,fontsize(7pt));
label("$7$",(B+X)/2,NE,fontsize(8pt)); label("$1$",(X+C)/2,NE,fontsize(7pt));
label("$7$",(C+Y)/2,NW,fontsize(8pt)); label("$1$",(Y+A)/2,E,fontsize(7pt));
\end{asy}

Two triangles sharing an angle have areas in the ratio of the products of the sides enclosing that angle. So
\[[AZY]=\dfrac{AZ}{AB}\cdot\dfrac{AY}{AC}\,T=\dfrac78\cdot\dfrac18\,T=\dfrac{7}{64}T,\]
and likewise $[BXZ]=\dfrac78\cdot\dfrac18T=\dfrac{7}{64}T$ and $[CYX]=\dfrac78\cdot\dfrac18T=\dfrac{7}{64}T$. Removing these three corner triangles leaves $XYZ$:
\[\dfrac{[XYZ]}{[ABC]}=1-3\cdot\dfrac{7}{64}=\boxed{\dfrac{43}{64}}.\]
