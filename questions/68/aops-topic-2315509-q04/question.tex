In $\triangle ABC$, points $X$, $Y$, $Z$ lie on sides $BC$, $CA$, $AB$ respectively, such that
\[\dfrac{XB}{XC}=\dfrac{YC}{YA}=\dfrac{ZA}{ZB}=\dfrac71.\]
Find the ratio of the area of $\triangle XYZ$ to the area of $\triangle ABC$.
