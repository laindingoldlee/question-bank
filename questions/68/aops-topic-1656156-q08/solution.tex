Let $x=0.\overline{18}$. Then $100x=18.\overline{18}$, so $99x=18$ and $x=\dfrac{18}{99}=\dfrac{2}{11}$. Hence
\[
20.\overline{18}=20+\dfrac{2}{11}=\boxed{\textbf{(a)}\ \dfrac{222}{11}} .
\]
