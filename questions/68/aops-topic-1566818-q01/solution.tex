Factor out $17^5$:
\[
17^6+17^5=17^5(17+1)=18\cdot17^5,
\]
which is a multiple of $18$. $\blacksquare$

(Alternatively, $17\equiv-1\pmod{18}$, so $17^6+17^5\equiv1+(-1)=0\pmod{18}$.)
