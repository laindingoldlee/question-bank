ARG is in the top half and ESP in the bottom half, so they meet only in the final, which requires each to win $3$ matches; these matches involve disjoint sets of games. The probability is $\left(\dfrac12\right)^3\left(\dfrac12\right)^3=\boxed{\dfrac1{64}}$.
