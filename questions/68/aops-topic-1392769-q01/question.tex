A number $m$ is chosen at random and removed from the set $\{0,1,2,\dots,9\}$. Then a number $n$ is chosen at random from the remaining nine numbers. What is the probability that $x^2+2mx+n^2=0$ has two distinct real solutions? Express your answer as a common fraction.
