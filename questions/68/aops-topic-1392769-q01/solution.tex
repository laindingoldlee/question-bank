The equation has two distinct real solutions exactly when its discriminant is positive:
\[
(2m)^2-4n^2>0\iff m^2>n^2\iff m>n ,
\]
since $m,n\ge0$. All $10\cdot9=90$ ordered pairs $(m,n)$ of distinct numbers are equally likely, and swapping $m$ and $n$ pairs each outcome with $m>n$ with one with $m<n$. So exactly half of them have $m>n$, and the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$.
