Split $ABCD$ into the four triangles $PAB$, $PBC$, $PCD$, $PDA$. A triangle with two sides $x$, $y$ and included angle $\theta$ has area $\dfrac{1}{2}xy\sin\theta\le\dfrac{1}{2}xy$, with equality only if $\theta=90^\circ$. Hence
\[
2002=[ABCD]\le\dfrac{1}{2}\bigl(PA\cdot PB+PB\cdot PC+PC\cdot PD+PD\cdot PA\bigr)=\dfrac{1}{2}(PA+PC)(PB+PD)=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot52\cdot77=2002 .
\]
So equality holds, and all four angles at $P$ are right angles. Then $A,P,C$ are collinear, $B,P,D$ are collinear, and the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ are perpendicular at $P$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair ft(pair P,pair U,pair V){return U+dot(P-U,V-U)/dot(V-U,V-U)*(V-U);}
path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s=0.2){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
pair P=(0,0),A=(-24,0),B=(0,-32),C=(28,0),D=(0,45);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(A--C,blue);draw(B--D,blue);
draw(ra(P,C-P,D-P,2.6));
dot(P);dot(A);dot(B);dot(C);dot(D);
label("$A$",A,W);label("$B$",B,S);label("$C$",C,E);label("$D$",D,N);label("$P$",P,dir(225));
label("$24$",(A+P)/2,N,blue);label("$28$",(C+P)/2,S,blue);label("$32$",(B+P)/2,E,blue);label("$45$",(D+P)/2,W,blue);
label("$40$",(A+B)/2,SW);label("$4\sqrt{113}$",(B+C)/2,SE);label("$53$",(C+D)/2,NE);label("$51$",(D+A)/2,NW);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

By the Pythagorean theorem,
\[
AB=\sqrt{24^2+32^2}=40,\quad BC=\sqrt{32^2+28^2}=\sqrt{1808}=4\sqrt{113},\quad CD=\sqrt{28^2+45^2}=53,\quad DA=\sqrt{45^2+24^2}=51 .
\]
The perimeter is $40+53+51+4\sqrt{113}=\boxed{144+4\sqrt{113}}$.
