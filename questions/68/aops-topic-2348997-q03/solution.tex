The minor arc $AB$ is $\dfrac{150}{360}=\dfrac5{12}$ of the circumference:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0);pair A=dir(15),B=dir(165);
draw(unitcircle,gray);draw(arc(O,1,15,165),linewidth(1.6));
draw(A--O--B);dot(O);
label("$O$",O,S);label("$A$",A,E);label("$B$",B,W);
draw(arc(O,0.18,15,165));label("$150^\circ$",(0,0.18),N,fontsize(8pt));
label("$10\pi$",dir(90),N);label("$r$",(A+O)/2,SE);
\end{asy}

\[\dfrac5{12}\cdot2\pi r=10\pi\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad r=12.\]
The area is $\pi\cdot12^2=\boxed{144\pi}$ square units.
