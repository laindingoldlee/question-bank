The arc length is $r\theta=10\cdot\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ inches. One revolution rolls the wheel through its circumference, $3.5\pi$ inches. Thus the number of revolutions is $\dfrac{\dfrac{10 \pi}{3}}{3.5\pi} = \boxed{\dfrac{20}{21}}$. Specifying rotation relative to the local travel direction avoids conflating rolling distance with absolute orientation around a curved track.
