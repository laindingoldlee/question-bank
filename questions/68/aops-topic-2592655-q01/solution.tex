Group the last three terms:
\[-m^2+2mn-n^2=-\left(m^2-2mn+n^2\right)=-(m-n)^2.\]
So the expression is a difference of squares:
\[x^2-(m-n)^2=\boxed{(x-m+n)(x+m-n)}.\]
Check: $(x-m+n)(x+m-n)=x^2-(m-n)^2=x^2-m^2+2mn-n^2$.
