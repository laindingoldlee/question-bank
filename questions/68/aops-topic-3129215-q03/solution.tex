(a) The possible positive payments below 15 are $5,7,10,12,14$. Their required changes $4,6,9,11,13$ cannot be made from 5 and 7. Paying 15 works, with 14 returned as two 7-dollar bills. The minimum is $\boxed{15}$. (b) Directly listing $5a+7b$ for $a,b\ge0$ up to 23 leaves $\boxed{1,2,3,4,6,8,9,11,13,16,18,23}$. All larger amounts are possible: $24=2\cdot5+2\cdot7$, $25=5\cdot5$, $26=5+3\cdot7$, $27=4\cdot5+7$, and $28=4\cdot7$ give five consecutive amounts, and repeatedly adding 5 covers every integer at least 24.
