Use $B=(0,0),C=(12,0),A=(0,3),D=(12,8)$. Then $AD$ lies on $5x-12y+36=0$. The distance from $M=(6,0)$ is $\dfrac{|30+36|}{\sqrt{25+144}}=\boxed{\dfrac{66}{13}}$. Its projection lies on the segment because its parameter from $A$ is $\dfrac{(6,-3)\cdot(12,5)}{169}=\dfrac{57}{169}\in(0,1)$.
