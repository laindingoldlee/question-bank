Write it as $10a+b$, where $1\le a\le9$ and $0\le b\le9$. The equation is $10b+2a=10a+b$, or $9b=8a$. Coprimality of $8,9$ forces $a=9$, then $b=8$. The score is $\boxed{98}$.
