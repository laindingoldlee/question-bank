A game requires each team to submit a prime from $2$ through $2023$. A nonprime submission or a duplicate of another team's submission scores zero. Otherwise its score is $\min(30,\lfloor4\ln d\rfloor)$, where $d$ is its distance from the closest other valid submission. Exactly one other team submits $13$. Find your score if you submit (a) $997$, (b) $13$, (c) $2023$.
