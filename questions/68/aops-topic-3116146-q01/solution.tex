Long division or cross multiplication gives $3.1415929<\dfrac{355}{113}<3.1415930$ and $\dfrac{22}{7}>3.1428$. Thus $0<\dfrac{355}{113}-\pi<0.0000004$, whereas $\dfrac{22}{7}-\pi>0.0012073$. The former error is strictly smaller.
