The midpoint of $a$ and $b$ is $\dfrac{a+b}{2}$. Here $-1\dfrac{5}{6}=-\dfrac{11}{6}$, so
\[
\dfrac{1}{2}\left(-\dfrac{11}{6}+\dfrac{3}{5}\right)=\dfrac{1}{2}\left(-\dfrac{55}{30}+\dfrac{18}{30}\right)=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\left(-\dfrac{37}{30}\right)=\boxed{-\dfrac{37}{60}}.
\]
Since $\left|-\dfrac{37}{60}\right|<1$, this number cannot be written as a mixed number with a nonzero whole part.
