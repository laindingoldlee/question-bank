The two blue beads split the five red beads into two gaps. Rotation forgets the choice of first blue bead, and reversal also interchanges the gaps. Thus only the unordered pair of nonnegative gap sizes matters. The possibilities are $\{0,5\}$, $\{1,4\}$, and $\{2,3\}$. Each gives one necklace, so the answer is $\boxed3$.
