If the radius is $r$, then $\pi r^2=100\pi$, so $r^2=100$ and $r=10$ cm.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw(circle((0,0),1),linewidth(0.9));
dot((0,0)); label("$O$",(0,0),SW);
draw((0,0)--dir(30),red); label("$10$",0.5*dir(30),NW,red);
\end{asy}
