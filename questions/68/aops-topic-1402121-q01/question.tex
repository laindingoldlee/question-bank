Let $x$ and $y$ be positive integers with $y\ge x$. In how many ways can $x$ balls be put into $y$ boxes (boxes may be left empty) when
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] the balls are distinguishable and the boxes are distinguishable;
\item[(b)] the balls are distinguishable and the boxes are not;
\item[(c)] the balls are indistinguishable and the boxes are distinguishable;
\item[(d)] neither the balls nor the boxes are distinguishable?
\end{enumerate}
Explain each answer, and evaluate all four for $x=4$, $y=5$.
