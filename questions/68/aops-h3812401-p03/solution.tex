For $k=59$, $279k=16461$, which qualifies. To prove minimality, a smaller qualifying multiple is between $279$ and $16182$, so it must have three or five digits. The three-digit multiples of $279$ are $279,558,837$, none qualifying. A five-digit palindrome with middle digit $4$ below $16461$ must be one of $10401,11411,12421,13431,14441,15451$. Their remainders modulo $279$ are $78,251,145,39,212,106$, respectively. None is divisible by $279$. Therefore $\boxed{k=59}$.
