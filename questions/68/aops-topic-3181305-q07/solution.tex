Choose the destination for the together-pair in 2 ways. Choose which member of the separated pair joins them in 2 ways. Complete that group by choosing 2 of the remaining 6 students, in $\binom62=15$ ways. The other group is forced. Thus $2\cdot2\cdot15=\boxed{60}$.
