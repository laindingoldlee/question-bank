Let $e_1=x+y+z=6$, $e_2=xy+yz+zx$, $e_3=xyz$. Then $e_2=\dfrac{36-26}{2}=5$, and
\[x^3+y^3+z^3=e_1^3-3e_1e_2+3e_3=216-90+3e_3=90,\]
so $e_3=-12$. Hence $x,y,z$ are the roots of $t^3-6t^2+5t+12=(t+1)(t-3)(t-4)$. The solutions are the $6$ orderings of $\boxed{(-1,3,4)}$.
