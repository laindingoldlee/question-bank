We need $x^2-8x+15>0$ (the radicand must be nonnegative and the denominator nonzero). Since $x^2-8x+15=(x-3)(x-5)$, this holds exactly when $x<3$ or $x>5$. The domain is $\boxed{(-\infty,3)\cup(5,\infty)}$.
