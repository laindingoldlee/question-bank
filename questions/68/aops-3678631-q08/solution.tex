The daily change in Jessie's lead is her daily page count minus 16. This is positive from day 7 through day 23, and negative from day 24 onward. Before day 7 the lead decreases; at day 23 her total is $285+\dfrac{(31+17)8}{2}=477$, whereas Melinda has read 368 pages, so the later peak exceeds the initial lead. Thus the maximum occurs at the end of $\boxed{\text{day }23}$.
