The reciprocal of a nonzero number $x$ is the number $y$ with $xy=1$. Since $\left(-\dfrac{1}{3}\right)(-3)=1$, the reciprocal of $-\dfrac{1}{3}$ is $\boxed{-3}$. (Taking a reciprocal does not change the sign.)
