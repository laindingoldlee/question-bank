Let the cylinder have radius $r$ and the cone have height $h$. The volumes are equal:
\[\pi r^2\cdot12=\dfrac13\pi(2r)^2h=\dfrac43\pi r^2h.\]
So $12=\dfrac43h$ and $h=\boxed{9\text{ m}}$.
