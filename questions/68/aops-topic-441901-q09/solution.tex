Let the sides be positive integers $a\le b$. The condition is
\[
ab=2\cdot2(a+b)=4a+4b\iff ab-4a-4b+16=16\iff(a-4)(b-4)=16 .
\]
If both factors were negative, then $a-4\ge-3$ and $b-4\ge-3$ would make the product at most $9<16$; so both factors are positive divisors of $16$ with $a-4\le b-4$:
\[
(a-4,b-4)\in\{(1,16),(2,8),(4,4)\}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad (a,b)\in\{(5,20),(6,12),(8,8)\}.
\]

The areas are $100$, $72$ and $64$ (check: $5\cdot20=100=2\cdot50$, $6\cdot12=72=2\cdot36$, $8\cdot8=64=2\cdot32$), and their sum is $\boxed{236}$.
