To get from her home to a friend's house, Georgia walks along a route that goes past a school and then a post office, in that order. The distance from her home to the school is $\dfrac78$ mile, and the distance from the school to the post office is $\dfrac56$ mile. The whole walk from her home to her friend's house takes $42$ minutes, and Georgia walks one mile in $18$ minutes. How many miles is it from the post office to her friend's house?
