Put $T=a^2+b^2+c^2$ and $U=ab+bc+ca$. Adding gives $T+2U=89$, and hence $(a+b+c)^2=89$. Squaring the three equations and adding gives $54^2+21^2+14^2=T^2+2U^2$: the cross terms combine because $U^2=a^2b^2+b^2c^2+c^2a^2+2abc(a+b+c)$. Thus $3553=T^2+\dfrac{(89-T)^2}{2}$, or $3T^2-178T+815=0$. Its roots are $5$ and $\dfrac{163}{3}$. But $3T\ge(a+b+c)^2=89$ by Cauchy--Schwarz, ruling out 5. Therefore $\boxed{T=\dfrac{163}{3}}$.
