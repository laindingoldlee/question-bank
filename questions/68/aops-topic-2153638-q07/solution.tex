Complete the square: $(z-1)^2=i-1$. Write a square root of $-1+i$ as $a+bi$ with $a,b$ real. Then $a^2-b^2=-1$ and $2ab=1$, and taking absolute values, $a^2+b^2=\left|-1+i\right|=\sqrt2$. Hence
\[a^2=\dfrac{\sqrt2-1}{2},\qquad b^2=\dfrac{\sqrt2+1}{2},\]
and $a$, $b$ have the same sign (since $2ab=1>0$). So the square roots of $-1+i$ are $\pm\left(\sqrt{\dfrac{\sqrt2-1}{2}}+i\sqrt{\dfrac{\sqrt2+1}{2}}\right)$, and
\[\boxed{z=1\pm\left(\sqrt{\dfrac{\sqrt2-1}{2}}+i\sqrt{\dfrac{\sqrt2+1}{2}}\right)}\approx1.455+1.099i\ \text{ or }\ 0.545-1.099i .\]
