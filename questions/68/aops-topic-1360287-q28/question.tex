Bus $A$ is $150$ miles due east of Bus $B$. Both buses start driving due west at constant speeds at the same time. It takes Bus $A$ $10$ hours to overtake Bus $B$. If they had started at the same time and driven at the same constant speeds but toward each other, they would have met in $2$ hours. What is the speed of Bus $A$, in miles per hour?
