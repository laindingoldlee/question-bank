Let the speeds be $a$ and $b$. Driving the same way, $A$ closes the $150$-mile gap at $a-b$ mph in $10$ hours, so $a-b=15$. Driving toward each other, they close it at $a+b$ mph in $2$ hours, so $a+b=75$. Hence $a=\boxed{45}$ mph (and $b=30$).
