Multiply both sides by $y+3$, which is nonzero for the fraction to be defined:
\[
2\sqrt x=8(y+3),\qquad y+3=\dfrac{\sqrt x}{4},\qquad \boxed{y=\dfrac{\sqrt x}{4}-3}.
\]
Here $x>0$: we need $x\ge0$ for $\sqrt x$, and $x=0$ would give $y+3=0$, where the original fraction is undefined.
