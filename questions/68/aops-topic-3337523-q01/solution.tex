Put $t=\dfrac{x}{y}$. Then $\dfrac{t^3+4t}{4t^2+1}=\dfrac{15}{16}$, with positive denominator. Cross-multiplication gives $16t^3-60t^2+64t-15=(2t-3)(8t^2-18t+5)=0$. Hence $t=\dfrac{3}{2}$ or $t=\dfrac{9\pm\sqrt{41}}{8}$. The three ratios are $\boxed{3:2,\ (9+\sqrt{41}):8,\ (9-\sqrt{41}):8}$.
