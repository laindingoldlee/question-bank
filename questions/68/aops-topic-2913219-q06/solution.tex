There are $46$ odd multiples of $3$ among $3,6,\ldots,273$, and $27$ odd multiples of $5$ among $5,10,\ldots,270$. Their overlap consists of the $9$ odd multiples of $15$ below $274$. Inclusion-exclusion gives $46+27-9=\boxed{64}$.
