\textbf{Any two terms have gcd $2$.} Let $m<n$. Repeatedly using $u^2-1=(u-1)(u+1)$,
\[
3^{2^n}-1=\left(3^{2^m}-1\right)\left(3^{2^m}+1\right)\left(3^{2^{m+1}}+1\right)\cdots\left(3^{2^{n-1}}+1\right),
\]
so $a_m=3^{2^m}+1$ divides $3^{2^n}-1=a_n-2$. Hence any common divisor of $a_m$ and $a_n$ divides $a_n-(a_n-2)=2$. Every $a_k$ is even ($3^{2^k}$ is odd), so $\gcd(a_m,a_n)=2$.

\textbf{Three terms.} The gcd of $a_{1999},a_{2009},a_{2010}$ divides $\gcd(a_{1999},a_{2009})=2$, and $2$ divides all three. The answer is $\boxed{2}$.
