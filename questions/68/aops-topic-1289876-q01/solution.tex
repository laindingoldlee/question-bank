MOONDUST has the seven distinct letters M, O, N, D, U, S, T, with O appearing twice.

\textbf{No repeated letter:} $7\cdot6\cdot5=210$ arrangements.

\textbf{Two O's:} choose the third letter from the other $6$ letters and its position among the $3$ places: $6\cdot3=18$ arrangements.

Total: $210+18=\boxed{228}$.
