Some chords are drawn in a circle so that no three of them pass through a common point inside the circle. Let $n$ be the number of chords and $I$ the number of points inside the circle where two of them cross.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that the chords divide the disc into exactly $1+n+I$ regions.
\item[(b)] Find the largest number of regions into which $n$ chords can divide a disc, and show that it can be attained.
\end{enumerate}
