Five people, Sally, Bob, Methuselah, Mary and Susan, are to be seated in six seats arranged in two rows of three seats each; one seat stays empty. Two people are \emph{adjacent} if they sit next to each other in the same row. Sally may not sit adjacent to Bob, Bob may not sit adjacent to Methuselah, and Mary may not sit adjacent to Susan. In how many ways can they be seated?
