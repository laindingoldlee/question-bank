Going around the circle from $15$ to $49$ (through $16,17,\dots$) takes $49-15=34$ steps, and since they are opposite, this is half of the full circle of $n$ steps. (The other way round also takes $n-34$ steps, which must also be half.) So $\dfrac{n}{2}=34$ and
\[
n=\boxed{68}.
\]
(Check: $49\le68$, and the number opposite $k$ is $k+34$ for $k\le34$.)
