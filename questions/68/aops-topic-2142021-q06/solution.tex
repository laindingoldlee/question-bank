Let Alice run $a$ laps per minute and Bob $b$ laps per minute. The number of complete laps after $t$ minutes is $\lfloor at\rfloor$ for Alice and $\lfloor bt\rfloor$ for Bob.

\textbf{Alice.} $46\le8a<47$, so $\dfrac{46}{8}\le a<\dfrac{47}{8}$ and
\[166.75=\dfrac{29\cdot46}{8}\le29a<\dfrac{29\cdot47}{8}=170.375 .\]
As $a$ runs through its interval, $29a$ runs through $[166.75,\ 170.375)$, so Alice's laps after $29$ minutes can be any of $166,167,168,169,170$.

\textbf{Bob.} $41\le7b<42$, so
\[169.857\ldots=\dfrac{29\cdot41}{7}\le29b<\dfrac{29\cdot42}{7}=174,\]
and Bob's laps can be any of $169,170,171,172,173$.

The two speeds can be chosen independently, so $x$ can be any common value: $x\in\{169,170\}$. For example, $a=b=\dfrac{170}{29}$ gives $x=170$ (this $a$ is less than $\dfrac{47}{8}$, and this $b$ is at least $\dfrac{41}{7}$), and $a=\dfrac{169}{29}$, $b=\dfrac{41}{7}$ give $x=169$. The sum is $169+170=\boxed{339}$.
