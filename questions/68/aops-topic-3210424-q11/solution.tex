Each term is $5^k-2^{-k}$. Geometric summation gives $S=\dfrac{5^{2025}-5}{4}-(1-2^{-2024})=\dfrac{5^{2025}-9}{4}+\dfrac{1}{2^{2024}}$. Hence $a=2025,b=9,c=2024$ and $\boxed{a+b+c=4058}$. The power-of-two denominator in lowest terms fixes $c=2024$; the one-digit restriction on $b$ then fixes $a,b$.
