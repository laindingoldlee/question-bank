Choose the fixed number ($6$ ways); the other five must all be out of place, a derangement of $5$ objects. By inclusion-exclusion, $D_5=5!\left(1-1+\dfrac{1}{2!}-\dfrac{1}{3!}+\dfrac{1}{4!}-\dfrac{1}{5!}\right)=60-20+5-1=44$. The answer is $6\cdot44=\boxed{264}$.
