Choose the center uniformly modulo the unit grid. By summing indicator functions for intersection with each cell, the expectation equals the area of possible centers for which the disk meets one fixed unit cell. This region is the square expanded by radius $\dfrac{1}{2}$: it comprises the unit square, four strips of area $\dfrac{1}{2}$, and four quarter-disks of radius $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore the expectation is $\boxed{3+\dfrac{\pi}{4}}$. Boundary contacts have probability zero.
