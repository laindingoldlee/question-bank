$4+2\sqrt3=\left(1+\sqrt3\right)^2$ and $49+8\sqrt3=\left(1+4\sqrt3\right)^2$ (since $1+48=49$ and $2\cdot4\sqrt3=8\sqrt3$). So the expression is $\left(4+4\sqrt3-1-4\sqrt3\right)^2=3^2=\boxed{9}$.
