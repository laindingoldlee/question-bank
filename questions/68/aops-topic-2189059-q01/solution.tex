On day $k$ (counting February $1$ as day $1$) Alex eats peanuts number $7k-6$ to $7k$. Since $161=7\cdot23$, the $161$st peanut is the last one eaten on day $23$, which is February $23$, $2020$.

February $1$ is a Saturday, so February $1+21=22$ is also a Saturday, and February $23$ is a $\boxed{\text{Sunday}}$. (The leap day, February $29$, comes later and does not matter.)
