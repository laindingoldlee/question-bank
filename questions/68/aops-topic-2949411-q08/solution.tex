Place $J=(0,1),A=(0,0),M=(1,0)$, traversing counterclockwise. Successive exterior turns give $E=(1+\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2})$ and $S=(1,\sqrt2)$. Side $SU$ is horizontal. Since the exterior turn at $J$ is 45 degrees, side $UJ$ has slope 1; through $J$ its line is $y=x+1$. It meets $y=\sqrt2$ at $U=(\sqrt2-1,\sqrt2)$. Therefore $US=1-(\sqrt2-1)=\boxed{2-\sqrt2}$.

\end{asy}
