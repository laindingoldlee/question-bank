Count the complement: four-digit strings with no ``low'' digit ($0,1$) immediately followed by a ``high'' digit ($8,9$). Track the type of the last digit: low ($L$), high ($H$) or other ($O$, $6$ choices). Let $l_k,h_k,o_k$ be the counts of good $k$-digit strings. Then $l_{k+1}=2(l_k+h_k+o_k)$, $h_{k+1}=2(h_k+o_k)$, $o_{k+1}=6(l_k+h_k+o_k)$. The first digit is $1$ (low, $1$ way), $8,9$ (high) or $2$--$7$ (other). Starting from $(1,2,6)$: $(18,16,54)$, $(176,140,528)$, $(1688,1336,5064)$, total $8088$. So $9000-8088=\boxed{912}$ numbers are uplifting.
