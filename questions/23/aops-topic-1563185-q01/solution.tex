Let the integers be $a\le b\le c$. Then $a+b+c=72$ and the condition is $c-a=b$, i.e.\ $c=a+b$. So $2c=72$, $c=36$ and $a+b=36$ with $a\le b\le36$, which means $0\le a\le18$ and $b=36-a$.

A mode exists only if two of the numbers are equal:
\begin{itemize}
\item $a=b$: $a=b=18$, giving $\{18,18,36\}$ (range $18$, median $18$);
\item $b=c$: $36-a=36$, so $a=0$, giving $\{0,36,36\}$ (range $36$, median $36$).
\end{itemize}
(All three equal would need $a=b=c=36$ and $a+b=36$, impossible.) The possible sets are
\[
\boxed{\{18,18,36\}\ \text{and}\ \{0,36,36\}}.
\]
