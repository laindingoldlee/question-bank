Both numbers are positive, so we may compare their squares, $3^{\sqrt5}$ and $5^{\sqrt3}$, and then take the power $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}$ of both (which preserves order for positive numbers). This compares
\[
3^{\sqrt{\dfrac{5}{3}}}\quad\text{with}\quad 5 .
\]
\textbf{Bound the exponent.} $\dfrac{5}{3}<\dfrac{16}{9}$ (as $15<16$), so $\sqrt{\dfrac{5}{3}}<\dfrac{4}{3}$. Since $3>1$, larger exponents give larger powers:
\[
3^{\sqrt{\dfrac{5}{3}}}<3^{\dfrac{4}{3}}=3\cdot\sqrt[3]{3}.
\]
\textbf{Bound the cube root.} $\sqrt[3]{3}<\dfrac{5}{3}$ because $\left(\dfrac{5}{3}\right)^3=\dfrac{125}{27}>3$. Hence $3\cdot\sqrt[3]3<5$.

Therefore $3^{\sqrt{\dfrac{5}{3}}}<5$, so $3^{\sqrt5}<5^{\sqrt3}$ and
\[
\boxed{\sqrt5^{\sqrt3}>\sqrt3^{\sqrt5}} .
\]
(Numerically $\sqrt5^{\sqrt3}\approx4.03$ and $\sqrt3^{\sqrt5}\approx3.42$. With calculus: $\dfrac{\ln x}{x}$ increases for $0<x<e$, and $\sqrt3<\sqrt5<e$.)
