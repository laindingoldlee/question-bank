Lillian has many $3\times3$ cm tiles and $2\times2$ cm tiles. She uses them to cover a $7\times18$ cm tray exactly, with no overlaps, no gaps, no tiles sticking out and no broken tiles. How many tiles does she use? Show that the answer does not depend on how she does it.
