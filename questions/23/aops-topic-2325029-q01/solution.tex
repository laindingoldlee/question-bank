We count solutions of $h_1+h_2+\cdots+h_{10}=24$ in positive integers. Line up $24$ hours and choose $9$ of the $23$ gaps between consecutive hours to cut; this splits them into $10$ nonempty groups, given in order to the accounts. So there are
\[\dbinom{23}{9}=\boxed{817190}\]
ways.
