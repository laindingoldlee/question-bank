The first two areas share the altitude from $P$ to $AB$, so $AF:FB=2:1$. Since $C,P,F$ are collinear, this also gives $[APC]:[BPC]=2:1$. Put $[BPC]=t$; then $[APC]=2t$ and $[ABP]=189$.

Similarly, because $B,P,E$ are collinear, $AE:EC=[ABP]:[CBP]=189:t$. Triangles $APE$ and $CPE$ have this same ratio. Since $[CPE]=24$, $[APE]=2t-24$ and
\[\dfrac{2t-24}{24}=\dfrac{189}{t}.\]
Thus $t^2-12t-2268=0=(t-54)(t+42)$, giving $t=54$. The total area is $189+2t+t=189+162=\boxed{351}$.
