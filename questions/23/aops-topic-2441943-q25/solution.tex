With $q$ quarters and $2q$ dimes, $25q+10\cdot2q=45q=1890$ cents, so $q=42$ and there are $84$ dimes. At the convenience store he spends $5$ quarters and $10$ dimes, leaving $37$ quarters and $74$ dimes. Paying exactly $55$ cents with dimes and quarters requires $25u+10v=55$; $u$ must be odd and $u\le2$, so $u=1$, $v=3$. He uses one more quarter and has $\boxed{36}$ quarters left.
