The limiting sight line passes through both tops. Its drop from tower top to wall top is $250$ feet over $50$ horizontal feet, so its drop per horizontal foot is $5$. Dropping the remaining $50$ feet beyond the wall requires $\dfrac{50}{5}=10$ feet. Thus the least distance is $\boxed{10}$ feet. Points closer are blocked.

\end{asy}
