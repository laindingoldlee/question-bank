Let $C_p(s)$ be the coefficient of $t^s$ in $(t+t^2+\cdots+t^9)^p$, the number of admissible ordered digit strings. Across all such strings the sum of every digit in every position is $sC_p(s)$. Symmetry makes each position contribute $\dfrac{sC_p(s)}{p}$. Weighting positions by $1,10,\ldots,10^{p-1}$ gives $\boxed{S_p(s)=\dfrac{s(10^p-1)}{9p}C_p(s)}$. Since nonzero digits force $1\le p\le s$, the sum over all lengths is $\boxed{\sum_{p=1}^s\dfrac{s(10^p-1)}{9p}C_p(s)}$. The coefficient can be computed by the finite recurrence $C_p(s)=\sum_{d=1}^9 C_{p-1}(s-d)$ with $C_0(0)=1$ and other $C_0$ values zero.
