Here $ac=24\cdot(-105)=-2520$. We need two numbers with product $-2520$ and sum $166$: $180$ and $-14$. Splitting the middle term,
\[24x^2+180x-14x-105=12x(2x+15)-7(2x+15)=\boxed{(12x-7)(2x+15)}.\]
