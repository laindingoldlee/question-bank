After exchanging the legs if needed, use $B=(0,0)$, $A=(a,0)$, $C=(0,b)$ with $a>b$. The total area is $18$, so $ab=36$. The perpendicular bisector of $AC$ has equation $ax-by=\dfrac{a^2-b^2}{2}$ and meets $AB$ at $D=(\dfrac{a^2-b^2}{2a},0)$ and $AC$ at its midpoint $M=(\dfrac{a}{2},\dfrac{b}{2})$. The small triangle $ADM$ has area $\dfrac{b(a^2+b^2)}{8a}=5$. Dividing by total area $\dfrac{ab}{2}=18$ gives $\dfrac{1+(\dfrac{b}{a})^2}{4}=\dfrac{5}{18}$, so $\dfrac{b}{a}=\dfrac{1}{3}$. Thus $a=6\sqrt3$, $b=2\sqrt3$, and $AC=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}=\boxed{2\sqrt{30}}$.
