Since $g(u)=3-\dfrac{4}{u+3}$, the equation becomes $3-\dfrac{4}{h(x)+3}=3-\dfrac{1}{x-1}$. Thus $h(x)+3=4(x-1)$ and $\boxed{h(x)=4x-7}$ for $x\ne1$. This never equals $-3$ on that domain, so composition is defined. No value at $x=1$ is required.
