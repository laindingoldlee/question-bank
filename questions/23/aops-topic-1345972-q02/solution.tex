After $n$ showings, the person is still not a customer with probability $0.7^n$, so we need
\[
1-0.7^n\ge0.6\iff0.7^n\le0.4 .
\]
Now $0.7^2=0.49>0.4$ and $0.7^3=0.343\le0.4$, and $0.7^n$ decreases in $n$, so the least number is $\boxed{3}$.
