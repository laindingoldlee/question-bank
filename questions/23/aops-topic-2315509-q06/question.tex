Spherical scoops of ice cream are placed in an ice cream cone (a right circular cone with its vertex at the bottom). Each scoop is tangent to the sides of the cone and rests on the scoop below it (consecutive scoops touch), each scoop has twice the radius of the one below it, and the top of the largest (final) scoop is exactly level with the rim of the cone, so that it does not poke out. Given that the largest scoop has a diameter of $4$ inches, determine the height of the cone.
