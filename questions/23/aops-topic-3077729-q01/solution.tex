Two distinct lines perpendicular to the same line are parallel, so they cannot intersect at the stated third point. Thus the original geometric claim is impossible. Without that claim, the points form a triangle with horizontal base of length $1$ and height $2$, giving area $\dfrac{1}{2}(1)(2)=\boxed1$.
