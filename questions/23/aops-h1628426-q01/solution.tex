Take $P$ as vector origin and let the plane have equation $\ell(X)=1$ for a linear functional $\ell$. Since the base is a parallelogram, $A+C=B+D$. A point at fraction $t$ of an edge from $P$ lies in the plane precisely when $t\ell(X)=1$. Here $t_A=1$, $t_B=\dfrac{3}{4}$, and $t_D=\dfrac{4}{5}$, so
\[1+\dfrac{1}{t_C}=\dfrac{4}{3}+\dfrac{5}{4},\qquad t_C=\dfrac{12}{19}.\]
This lies in $(0,1)$, as required. The requested complementary fraction is $\boxed{\dfrac{7}{19}}$.
