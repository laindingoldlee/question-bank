There are $6\cdot5=30$ equally likely pairs. The pairs with $a+b\le2$ are
\[(0,0),\ (0,1),\ (0,2),\ (1,0),\ (1,1),\ (2,0),\]
six in all. So the probability is $1-\dfrac{6}{30}=\boxed{\dfrac45}$.
