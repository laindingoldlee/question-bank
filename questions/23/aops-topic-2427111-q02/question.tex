Circle $\omega$ has centre $P$ and radius $4$. Points $B$ and $C$ lie on $\omega$ with $PB$ vertical and $PC$ horizontal, so that $BC$ is a quarter of the circle. Let $M$ be the midpoint of $PC$. Line $L$ passes through $B$ and $M$, and line $K$ passes through $B$ and $C$. Line $L$ meets $\omega$ again at $D$. The line $T$ through $D$ parallel to $PC$ meets line $K$ at $E$. What is the area of triangle $BDE$?
