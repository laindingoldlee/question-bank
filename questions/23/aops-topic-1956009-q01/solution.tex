We have $2^{1009}+4^{2019}=2^{1009}+2^{4038}=2^{1009}\left(1+2^{3029}\right)$, and $1+2^{3029}$ is odd. So the exponent of $2$ in the prime factorisation is exactly $1009$, which is odd. In a perfect square every prime appears to an even power, so the number is not a perfect square. $\blacksquare$
