You stand at the centre of a circle of radius $100$ metres. Every second you move as follows: first you choose a direction to face, and then an adversary decides whether you take a $1$-metre step forward or backward. You escape if you ever reach the edge of the circle. Do you have a strategy that guarantees your escape? Prove your answer.
