Scan left to right, waiting first for $L$, then $M$, then $T$. Until all three have been found, exactly one letter advances the scan and two do not. A string fails precisely when it has $k=0,1,$ or $2$ advances. For a fixed $k$, choose the advance positions in $\binom6k$ ways and fill all others in $2^{6-k}$ ways. Thus the successful count is $3^6-\sum_{k=0}^2\binom6k2^{6-k}=729-64-192-240=\boxed{233}$.
