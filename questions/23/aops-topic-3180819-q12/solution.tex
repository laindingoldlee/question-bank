A number is congruent to its digit sum modulo $9$, so $B\equiv\pm14\equiv4\text{ or }5\pmod9$. Consequently $B\ge4$. Equality is possible with $A=96$: $s(96)=15$ and $s(100)=1$. Thus the minimum is $\boxed4$.
