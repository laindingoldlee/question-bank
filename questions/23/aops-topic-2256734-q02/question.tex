Alice has $4$ cats and $60$ cans of cat food. She gives the first cat $20\%$ of the cans, the second cat $25\%$ of what is left, the third cat $\dfrac49$ of what is left after that, and the rest to the fourth cat. How many cans does the fourth cat get?

$\textbf{(A) }16\qquad\textbf{(B) }\dfrac{55}{3}\qquad\textbf{(C) }20\qquad\textbf{(D) }\dfrac{182}{9}\qquad\textbf{(E) }24$
