Positive reals $x,y,z$ satisfy
\[
\dfrac{9\sqrt{x^2+4}}{x}=\dfrac{11\sqrt{y^2+400}}{y}=\dfrac{12\sqrt{z^2+81}}{z}\qquad\text{and}\qquad\dfrac{xyz}{360}=\dfrac {x}{2}+\dfrac{y}{20}+\dfrac {z}{9} .
\]
If $x=\dfrac{a\sqrt b}{c}$, $y=\dfrac{d\sqrt b}{e}$, $z=\dfrac{f\sqrt b}{g}$, where $a$ and $c$, $d$ and $e$, $f$ and $g$ are coprime pairs of positive integers and $b$ is squarefree, find $a+b+c+d+e+f+g$.
