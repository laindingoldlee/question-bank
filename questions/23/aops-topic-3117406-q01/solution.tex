It does not. Put $a=2t,b=c=t$, with $t>0$. The proposed equation becomes $t^4=(2+\sqrt2)t^3(\sqrt5t-1)$. It holds for $t=\dfrac{2+\sqrt2}{(2+\sqrt2)\sqrt5-1}>0$, obtained by solving the resulting linear equation after division by $t^3$. But $b^2=t^2$ while $ac=2t^2$, which are unequal. This is an exact positive counterexample.
