We have $5^2=25$, and if a number ends in $25$, then $5$ times it ends in $25$ as well: $5(100m+25)=100(5m+1)+25$. So every power $5^n$ with $n\ge2$ ends in $25$, and the last two digits of $5^{2003}$ are $\boxed{25}$.
