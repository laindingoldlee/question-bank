Of the nine other integers, exactly four are in the set containing $1$. Symmetry gives probability $\boxed{\dfrac{4}{9}}$ that $2$ is among them. Equivalently the count is $\dfrac{\binom83}{\binom94}=\dfrac{4}{9}$.
