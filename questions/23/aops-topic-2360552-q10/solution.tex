Put the centres at $A(-1,0)$, $B(1,0)$ and $C(0,1)$. Circle $C$ meets circle $A$ at $(0,0)$ and $(-1,1)$; the common part of the two discs is a lens made of two quarter-circle segments, of area $2\left(\dfrac\pi4-\dfrac12\right)=\dfrac\pi2-1$. The same holds for $B$, and the two lenses meet only at the origin. So the required area is
\[\pi-2\left(\dfrac\pi2-1\right)=\boxed{2}.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(-1,0),B=(1,0),C=(0,1);
fill(circle(C,1),lightgray);fill(circle(A,1),white);fill(circle(B,1),white);
draw(circle(A,1));draw(circle(B,1));draw(circle(C,1));draw(A--B,dashed);
dot(A);dot(B);dot(C);dot((0,0));label("$A$",A,S);label("$B$",B,S);label("$C$",C,N);
\end{asy}
