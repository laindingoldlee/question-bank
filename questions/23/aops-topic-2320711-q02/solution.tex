Among $1,\dots,97$ there are $\left\lfloor\dfrac{97}{5}\right\rfloor=19$ multiples of $5$, of which $\left\lfloor\dfrac{97}{25}\right\rfloor=3$ are multiples of $25$ (and none of $125$). So the exponent is $19+3=22$, and the highest power is $\boxed{5^{22}}$.
