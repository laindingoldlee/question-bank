Using $\cos2\theta=2\cos^2\theta-1$, a student writes $\cos4x=2\cos^2 2x-1$, so $\cos^2 2x=\dfrac{\cos4x+1}{2}$, and then offers two continuations:
\[
\text{(i) }\ \cos^2 2x=2\cos^4x-1,\qquad\text{(ii) }\ \cos^2 2x=(2\cos^2x-1)^2.
\]
Which is correct? Express $\cos4x$ as a polynomial in $\cos x$.
