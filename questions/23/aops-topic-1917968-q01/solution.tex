$f(3)$ is the remainder of $27$ modulo $7$, which is $6$. Then $f(6)$ is the remainder of $216=7\cdot30+6$, which is again $6$ (indeed $6\equiv-1$ and $(-1)^3=-1\equiv6$). So $f(f(f(3)))=f(f(6))=f(6)=\boxed{6}$.
