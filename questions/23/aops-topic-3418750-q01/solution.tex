Write $y=4x-8$ and solve for the input: $x=\dfrac{y+8}{4}$. Renaming the inverse input $x$ gives $\boxed{f^{-1}(x)=\dfrac{x+8}{4}}$. Substitution shows $f(f^{-1}(x))=x$ and $f^{-1}(f(x))=x$. Swapping variable names and solving is just a convenient way to express this input-output reversal.
