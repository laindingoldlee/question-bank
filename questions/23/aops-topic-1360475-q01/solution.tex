$123456^3$ is even and $123^5$ is odd, so $d$ is odd and $2\nmid d$. The digit sums of $123456$ and $123$ are $21$ and $6$, both multiples of $3$, so $3\mid123456$ and $3\mid123$, and therefore $3$ divides both terms and divides $d$. The least prime factor is $\boxed{3}$.
