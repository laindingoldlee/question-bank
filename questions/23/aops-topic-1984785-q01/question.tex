Eight people have distinct skill levels. A poll compares two of them and tells you which one is better, and the results are consistent with a single ranking (so the transitive property can be used). What is the least number of polls that guarantees that you can determine the complete ranking of all eight people, however the polls turn out? Prove your answer.
