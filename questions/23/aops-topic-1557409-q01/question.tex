Ralph and Waldo each simultaneously hold out some fingers on one hand (from $1$ to $5$). Ralph always holds out a prime number of fingers, each possible prime being equally likely; Waldo always holds out an odd number of fingers, each possible odd number being equally likely; their choices are independent. What is the probability that the total number of fingers they hold out is even?
