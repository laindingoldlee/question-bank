Ralph holds out $2$, $3$ or $5$ fingers and Waldo holds out $1$, $3$ or $5$, each with probability $\dfrac{1}{3}$. Waldo's number is always odd, so the sum is even exactly when Ralph's number is odd, i.e.\ when Ralph holds out $3$ or $5$. The probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{2}{3}}$.
