The sum of individual surface areas is $6\sum_{k=1}^7k^2=840$. Each interface hides two faces of the smaller cube, so subtract $2\sum_{k=1}^6k^2=182$. Total $\boxed{658}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
real y=0;for(int k=7;k>=1;--k){real x=(7-k)/2.0;draw((x,y)--(x+k,y)--(x+k,y+k)--(x,y+k)--cycle);label(string(k),(x+k/2,y+k/2));y+=k;}label("front view; cube side lengths",(3.5,-2));
\end{asy}
