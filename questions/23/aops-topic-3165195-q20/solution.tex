Place $A=(0,0),B=(7,0),C=(\dfrac{16}{7},\dfrac{24\sqrt5}{7})$, as verified by the three side lengths. The altitude from $C$ is vertical. The slope of $BC$ is $-\dfrac{8\sqrt5}{11}$, so the altitude from $A$ has slope $\dfrac{11}{8\sqrt5}$. Their intersection is $H=(\dfrac{16}{7},\dfrac{22\sqrt5}{35})$. Reflecting gives $A^{\prime}=(\dfrac{32}{7},\dfrac{44\sqrt5}{35})$, $B^{\prime}=(-\dfrac{17}{7},\dfrac{44\sqrt5}{35})$, and $C^{\prime}=(\dfrac{16}{7},-\dfrac{76\sqrt5}{35})$. Intersecting the six side half-planes yields the parallelogram with vertices $(0,0)$, $(\dfrac{56}{15},0)$, $(\dfrac{32}{7},\dfrac{44\sqrt5}{35})$, $(\dfrac{88}{105},\dfrac{44\sqrt5}{35})$. Each vertex lies in both triangles; its four edges lie on $AB,A^{\prime}B^{\prime},AC,A^{\prime}C^{\prime}$, with the remaining two side inequalities redundant. The area is base times height: $\dfrac{56}{15}\cdot\dfrac{44\sqrt5}{35}=\dfrac{352\sqrt5}{75}$. Thus $\boxed{a+b+c=432}$.
