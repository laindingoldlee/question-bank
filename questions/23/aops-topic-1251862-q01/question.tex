A man has three sons of different ages, all positive integers. He tells each of three mathematicians the age of one son: Jorge gets the youngest son's age, Brett the middle son's, and Matt the oldest son's. They then say, truthfully:

Brett: ``This information is useless! I don't know any other age at all!''

Jorge: ``Of course you don't! I knew that from the start.''

Matt: ``Jorge, if you knew that from the start, is your number even?''

Jorge: ``No.''

Matt: ``Thanks, now I know all the ages.''

What are the ages?
