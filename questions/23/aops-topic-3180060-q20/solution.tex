Place the chord at $B=(-5,0),C=(5,0)$ and choose the orientation so $O=(0,\sqrt{39})$. The new centre is $K=(5,k)$, with radius $|k|$. Since it passes through $O$, $25+(k-\sqrt{39})^2=k^2$, so $k=\dfrac{32}{\sqrt{39}}$. Therefore $r^2=\dfrac{1024}{39}$ and the requested value is $\boxed{1024039}$.
