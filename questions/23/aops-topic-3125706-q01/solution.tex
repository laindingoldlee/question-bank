The factors equal $4(x+\dfrac{1}{2})^2+2\ge2$ and $(y-3)^2+4\ge4$. Their product is at least $8$, with equality only when both squares vanish. Thus the unique solution is $\boxed{(-\dfrac{1}{2},3)}$.
