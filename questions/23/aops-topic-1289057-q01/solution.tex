We count with Burnside's lemma over the $16$ symmetries of a regular octagon (8 rotations and 8 reflections), counting arrangements of $4$ red and $4$ yellow beads fixed by each.
\begin{itemize}
\item Identity: $\dbinom84=70$.
\item Rotations by $1,3,5,7$ steps: the beads form one cycle of $8$, so $0$.
\item Rotations by $2,6$ steps: two cycles of $4$; choose one red: $2$ each.
\item Rotation by $4$ steps: four cycles of $2$; choose two red: $\dbinom42=6$.
\item $4$ reflections through two opposite beads: cycles are $2$ fixed beads and $3$ pairs. Four reds need either both fixed beads and one pair ($3$ ways) or two pairs and no fixed bead ($3$ ways): $6$ each.
\item $4$ reflections through two opposite edges: $4$ pairs; choose two red: $6$ each.
\end{itemize}
The number of bracelets is $\dfrac{70+0+2\cdot2+6+4\cdot6+4\cdot6}{16}=\dfrac{128}{16}=\boxed{8}$.
