Let the numbers be $a$ and $b$, with $g=\gcd(a,b)$ and $L=\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)$. Since $g\mid a$ and $a\mid L$, we have $g\le a\le L$; similarly $g\le b\le L$. If $g=L$, these inequalities force $a=g=b$. $\blacksquare$
