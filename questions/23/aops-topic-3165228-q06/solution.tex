Put $A=(-8,0)$ and $B=(8,0)$. Then $C=(0,\pm6)$ and $D=(0,\pm15)$ by the Pythagorean theorem. If the two are on opposite sides of $AB$, the quadrilateral area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot16(15+6)=168$. If they are on the same side, the smaller triangle is removed from the larger and the concave quadrilateral has area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot16(15-6)=\boxed{72}$. These exhaust all possible positions.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(-8,0),Bp=(8,0),C=(0,6),D=(0,15);draw(A--D--Bp--C--cycle,blue);draw(A--Bp,dashed);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",Bp,SE);label("$C$",C,S);label("$D$",D,N);
\end{asy}

\end{asy}
