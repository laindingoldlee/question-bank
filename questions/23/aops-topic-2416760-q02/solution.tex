\[V_{\text{cylinder}}=\pi\cdot5^2\cdot10=250\pi,\qquad V_{\text{cone}}=\dfrac13\pi\cdot5^2\cdot5=\dfrac{125\pi}{3}.\]
So the silo holds
\[250\pi+2\cdot\dfrac{125\pi}{3}=\dfrac{1000\pi}{3}\approx1047.2\text{ cubic feet},\]
and the answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(D) } 1047.2}$. (The other choices are the two cones alone, $\dfrac{250\pi}{3}\approx261.8$, the cylinder alone, $250\pi\approx785.4$, and the cylinder with one cone, $\dfrac{875\pi}{3}\approx916.3$.)
