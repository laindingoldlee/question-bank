A company makes two types of bicycle: mountain bicycles and foldable bicycles. A mountain bicycle requires $4$ hours to assemble and $1$ hour for finishing; a foldable bicycle requires $6$ hours to assemble and $1$ hour for finishing. At most $108$ hours are available for assembling and at most $24$ hours for finishing. Let $x$ and $y$ be the numbers of mountain and foldable bicycles produced.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Write the system of inequalities and find the vertices of the feasible region in the $xy$-plane.
\item[(b)] How many production plans $(x,y)$ of whole numbers of bicycles are feasible?
\end{enumerate}
