There are $3^d$ such numbers with exactly $d$ base-$7$ digits. A number with fewer digits is smaller. Among numbers with the same number of digits, the order is the dictionary order of the digit strings.

Replace the digits $1,3,5$ by $0,1,2$. This matches the strings with $d$ digits, in increasing order, with the base-$3$ numerals $0,1,\dots,3^d-1$ (written with $d$ digits).

There are $3+9+27=39$ such numbers with at most $3$ digits, and $81$ with $4$ digits. So the $100$th number is the $61$st four-digit one, which corresponds to the base-$3$ numeral of $60$:
\[
60=2\cdot27+0\cdot9+2\cdot3+0=2020_3 .
\]
Changing back $0,1,2\to1,3,5$ gives
\[
\boxed{5151_7}=5\cdot343+1\cdot49+5\cdot7+1=\boxed{1800}.
\]
