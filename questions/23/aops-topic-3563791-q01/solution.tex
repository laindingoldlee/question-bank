A number with distinct decimal digits has at most ten digits, so $n^5<10^{10}$ and $n<100$. There are $99$ candidates $1\le n\le99$. The nine multiples of $10$ fail because their fifth powers end in at least five zeros. Also $6^5=7776$ fails. These ten distinct exclusions leave at most $99-10=\boxed{89}$ candidates.
