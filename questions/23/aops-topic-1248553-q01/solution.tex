Count solutions of $a+b+c+d=21$ with $0\le a,b,c,d\le10$. Without the upper bound there are $\dbinom{24}{3}=2024$. If some score is at least $11$, subtract $11$ from it: $\dbinom{13}{3}=286$ solutions for each of the $4$ scores. Two scores of at least $11$ would total $22>21$. So the count is
\[2024-4\cdot286=\boxed{880}.\]
