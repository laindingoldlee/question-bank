The multiples of $n$ in this range are
\[0,\ n,\ 2n,\ \dots,\ kn,\qquad\text{where }k=\left\lfloor\dfrac {N}{n}\right\rfloor\]
is the largest integer with $kn\le N$. These $k+1$ numbers form an arithmetic sequence, and the average of an arithmetic sequence is the average of its first and last terms. So the average is
\[\boxed{\dfrac{n}{2}\left\lfloor\dfrac{N}{n}\right\rfloor}.\]
For example, for $n=3$ and $N=10$ the multiples are $0,3,6,9$, with average $\dfrac{18}{4}=4.5=\dfrac{3}{2}\cdot3$.
