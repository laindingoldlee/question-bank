Equal coefficient sums mean $p(1)=q(1)$. Since $2021\equiv1\pmod{2020}$, their value difference is divisible by 2020. This positive lower bound is attained by $p(x)=2x+1$ and $q(x)=x+2$, both of degree 1, whose difference at 2021 is 2020. Hence $\boxed{2020}$.
