The equation has no real solutions exactly when its discriminant is negative: $a^2-4\cdot3\cdot6=a^2-72<0$, i.e. $|a|<\sqrt{72}\approx8.49$. The largest such integer is $a=\boxed{8}$ (discriminant $64-72<0$, while $a=9$ gives $81-72>0$).
