The coefficients are real, so $3\sqrt2-5i$ is also a root, and
\[\left(x-3\sqrt2-5i\right)\left(x-3\sqrt2+5i\right)=x^2-6\sqrt2x+43.\]
If $\rho$ is the third root, the product of the roots is $43\rho=-2021$, so $\rho=-47$. Hence
\[x^3+Ax^2+Bx+2021=\left(x^2-6\sqrt2x+43\right)(x+47)=x^3+\left(47-6\sqrt2\right)x^2+\left(43-282\sqrt2\right)x+2021,\]
so $A+B=90-288\sqrt2$ and $p+q+r=90+288+2=\boxed{380}$.
