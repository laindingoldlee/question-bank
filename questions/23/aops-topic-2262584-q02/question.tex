Let $n$ be a positive integer. Prove or disprove that
\[\dbinom{n+1}{1}+\dbinom{n+2}{2}+\dbinom{n+3}{3}+\cdots+\dbinom{2n+1}{n+1}-1\]
can be expressed as the sum of $n$ positive perfect squares.
