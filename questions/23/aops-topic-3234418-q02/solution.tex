The two known distances from the start are $10,5$ with included angle $30^\circ$. The cosine rule gives $d^2=100+25-100\cos30^\circ=125-50\sqrt3$. Thus $\boxed{d=5\sqrt{5-2\sqrt3}}$ miles.
