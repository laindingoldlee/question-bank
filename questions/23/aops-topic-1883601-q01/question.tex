A spinner is divided into $4$ equal sections, and $2$ non-adjacent sections are painted black (the other two are white). Alex then divides every white section into $3$ equal regions and paints the middle one black. Kelvin then divides every black region, including the ones Alex painted, into $3$ equal regions and paints the middle one white. Alex then spins the spinner and wins if it lands on a black region. What is the probability that he wins?
