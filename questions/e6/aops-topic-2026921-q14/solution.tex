Since $x^3+y^3=(x+y)(x^2-xy+y^2)$, for $x+y\ne0$ we get $x\diamondsuit y=x^2-xy+y^2$. Hence
\[6\diamondsuit5=36-30+25=\boxed{31}.\]
(Directly: $\dfrac{216+125}{11}=\dfrac{341}{11}=31$.)
