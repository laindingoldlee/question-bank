A student solves $x^2-8x-9=0$ as follows:
\[
x=\dfrac{8+\sqrt{8^2-4\cdot1\cdot(-9)}}{2}=\dfrac{8+\sqrt{100}}{2}=\dfrac{18}{2}=9 .
\]
What is wrong with this solution? Give the correct set of solutions.
