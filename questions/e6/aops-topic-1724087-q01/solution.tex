The quadratic formula has a $\pm$ sign: $x=\dfrac{8\pm\sqrt{100}}{2}=\dfrac{8\pm10}{2}$. The student used only the $+$ sign and lost the root $\dfrac{8-10}{2}=-1$. Indeed $x^2-8x-9=(x-9)(x+1)$. The solutions are $\boxed{x=9\text{ and }x=-1}$.
