Let $p$ be the probability that the first player wins. On the first flip she wins with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. If her flip and the second player's flip are both tails (probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$), the game starts over, with her flipping first again. So
\[
p=\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{4}p ,\qquad\text{hence}\qquad p=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{3}}.
\]
(Equivalently, $p=\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{8}+\dfrac{1}{32}+\cdots=\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{2}}{1-\dfrac{1}{4}}=\dfrac{2}{3}$.)
