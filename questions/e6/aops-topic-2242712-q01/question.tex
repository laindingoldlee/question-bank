For positive rational numbers, a \emph{common multiple} of $r_1,\ldots,r_k$ is a positive rational number $L$ such that each $\dfrac{L}{r_i}$ is an integer, and the \emph{least common multiple} is the smallest such $L$. A \emph{common divisor} is a positive rational $d$ such that each $\dfrac{r_i}{d}$ is an integer, and the \emph{greatest common divisor} is the largest such $d$. Find the least common multiple of $\dfrac{1}{2}$, $\dfrac{3}{2}$ and $\dfrac{5}{2}$.
