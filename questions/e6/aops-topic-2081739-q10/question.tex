I can throw a ball \(40\) yards. Benny throws half as far as I do. Jane throws \(2\) yards farther than Benny. Joe throws half as far as Jane. Bill throws \(20\) times as far as Joe. My sister throws \(\dfrac{1}{11}\) as far as Bill. How much farther can I throw than my sister?
