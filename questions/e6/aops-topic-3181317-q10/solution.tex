The maximum degree is 19. To lose exactly one degree, either choose one $-x^2$ among the five first factors, giving coefficient $-5\cdot6^2=-180$, or choose one $5x$ among the two second factors, giving $2\cdot6\cdot5=60$. Their sum is $\boxed{-120}$.
