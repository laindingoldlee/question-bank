The number of lit lamps increases only when a newly lit lamp has two unlit neighbours. That requires at least three unlit lamps beforehand, so no move can raise the total above 98. To attain 98, maintain one consecutive block of k lit lamps. While at least three unlit lamps remain, light the second unlit lamp after the block; both its neighbours are unlit, so the count increases. Then light the gap between this lamp and the old block, turning off the just-created lamp as required. This leaves a consecutive block of length k+1. Starting from one lit lamp, repeat until k=98. Thus the maximum is $\boxed{98}$.
