The triangle is right at $B$ and has area $6$. Hence $[PAC]=4$, so $P$ lies on the line parallel to $AC$ at two-thirds of the altitude from $AC$ to $B$. The distance from $B$ to $AC$ is $\dfrac{12}{5}$, leaving distance $\dfrac{1}{3}\dfrac{12}{5}=\dfrac{4}{5}$ from $B$ to the allowed line. The perpendicular foot lies inside the triangle, so this lower bound is attained. Thus $\boxed{BP_{\min}=\dfrac{4}{5}}$.
