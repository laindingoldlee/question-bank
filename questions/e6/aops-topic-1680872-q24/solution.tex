In alphabetical order the letters are E, R, S, U. The $3!=6$ words starting with E are numbers $1$ to $6$. Then come the words starting with R, in order:
\[
\text{RESU }(7),\ \text{REUS }(8),\ \text{RSEU }(9),\ \text{RSUE }(10),\ \text{RUES }(11),\ \text{RUSE }(12).
\]
So RUSE is number $\boxed{12}$.
