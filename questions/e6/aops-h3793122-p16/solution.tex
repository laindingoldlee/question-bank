For a trapezoid, the area between parallel sections of lengths $u,v$ is proportional to $u^2-v^2$: extend its legs to their intersection and subtract similar triangle areas. Writing $x=MN$, the given ratio gives $\dfrac{13-x^2}{x^2-1}=3$. Thus $x^2=4$ and $\boxed{MN=2}$. 
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(220);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(-sqrt(13)/2,0),D=(sqrt(13)/2,0),B=(-.5,3),C=(.5,3);real t=(sqrt(13)-2)/(sqrt(13)-1);pair M=A+t*(B-A),N=D+t*(C-D);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(M--N);label("$A$",A,(-1,-1));label("$B$",B,(-1,1));label("$C$",C,(1,1));label("$D$",D,(1,-1));label("$M$",M,(-1,0));label("$N$",N,(1,0));
\end{asy}
