Let the two sides perpendicular to the river be $x$; the side parallel to it is $240-2x$. The area is
\[
x(240-2x)=-2(x-60)^2+7200,
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((-1,0)--(9,0),blue+linewidth(1.5)); label("river",(4,0),S,blue);
draw((1,0)--(1,3)--(7,3)--(7,0),linewidth(0.9)); label("$x$",(1,1.5),W); label("$x$",(7,1.5),E); label("$240-2x$",(4,3),N);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

which is largest at $x=60$: a $60$ by $120$ rectangle with area $\boxed{7200\text{ m}^2}$.
