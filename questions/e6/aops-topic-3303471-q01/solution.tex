Let the original speeds be $a>r$ and the lap length $L$. The relative speed remains $a-r$ after both increase speed equally. Therefore
\[\dfrac{a}{a-r}=3,\qquad \dfrac{a+6}{a-r}=6.\]
Subtracting gives $a-r=2$. Then $a=6$, so $\boxed{r=4\text{ metres per second}}$.
