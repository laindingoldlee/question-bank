Factor $(x-y)(x+y)=69$. Both factors are positive odd integers with the first smaller than the second. Their possible pairs are $(1,69)$ and $(3,23)$, giving $(x,y)=(35,34),(13,10)$. Thus there are $\boxed2$ solutions.
