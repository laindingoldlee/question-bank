A contest has an opening round of 12 questions, awarding 10,9,...,1 points to the ten fastest correct solvers of each question. It then has eight matches with at most five questions each, followed by four matches with at most seven questions each. (a) If ten solvers answer each opening question correctly, how many points are awarded in that round? (b) What is the maximum number of questions used overall?
