For a positive integer $n$, let $\operatorname{rad}(n)$ be the product of the distinct primes dividing $n$ (so $\operatorname{rad}(40)=10$). Let $m$ and $n$ be positive integers with $\gcd(m,n)=1$, and let $b\ge2$ be an integer. Prove that if $\dfrac {m}{n}$ has a terminating expansion in base $b$, then $\operatorname{rad}(n)\le b$.
