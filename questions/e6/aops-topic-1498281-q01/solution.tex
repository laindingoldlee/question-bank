The square uses $16160-31=16129$ men. Since $127^2=16129$, the square has $127$ rows and $127$ columns, so there are $\boxed{127}$ men in the front row. ($31$ left over is less than $2\cdot127+1=255$, the number needed to enlarge the square by one row and one column, so $127$ is indeed the largest square.)
