$\angle ACE$ and $\angle DCB$ are vertical angles, so $y-15=x-2y$, i.e. $x=3y-15$. $\angle ACE$ and $\angle ECB$ form a straight angle, so $(y-15)+3x=180$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair C=(0,0); draw(-2*dir(20)--2*dir(20)); draw(-2*dir(130)--2*dir(130));
label("$A$",-2*dir(20),W); label("$B$",2*dir(20),E); label("$D$",-2*dir(130),SE); label("$E$",2*dir(130),NW); dot(C); label("$C$",C,S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Substituting, $y-15+9y-45=180$, so $y=24$ and $x=57$. Then $x+y=\boxed{81}$.
