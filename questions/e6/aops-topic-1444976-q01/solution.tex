The minute hand turns $6^\circ$ per minute and the hour hand $0.5^\circ$ per minute, so the minute hand gains $5.5^\circ$ per minute on the hour hand. In $24$ hours $=1440$ minutes it gains $1440\cdot5.5=7920^\circ=22\cdot360^\circ$, that is, exactly $22$ full laps.

Measure the angle from the hour hand to the minute hand, going clockwise; it increases steadily from $0^\circ$ at midnight. The hands are perpendicular when this angle is $90^\circ$ or $270^\circ$ (mod $360^\circ$). During each of the $22$ laps the angle passes through $90^\circ$ once and through $270^\circ$ once, and at the start and end (midnight) it is $0^\circ$. So the hands are perpendicular
