The complementary event has probability $\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^4\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^4=\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^4=\dfrac{1}{16}$. Therefore the desired probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{15}{16}}$.
