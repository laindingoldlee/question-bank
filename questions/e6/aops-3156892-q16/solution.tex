Let $C_n$ count triangulations of a polygon with $n+2$ vertices. Choosing the triangle incident to a fixed side gives $C_n=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}C_jC_{n-1-j}$, $C_0=1$. This produces $1,1,2,5,14,42,132$ through $C_6$. An octagon therefore has $\boxed{132}$ triangulations.

\end{asy}
