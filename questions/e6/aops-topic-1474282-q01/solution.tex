The terms are the odd numbers $1,3,\dots,2007$, which are $\dfrac{2007+1}{2}=1004$ numbers, with signs alternating $+,-$. Group them in consecutive pairs:
\[
(1-3)+(5-7)+\cdots+(2005-2007).
\]
There are $\dfrac{1004}{2}=502$ pairs, each equal to $-2$, so the sum is $502\cdot(-2)=\boxed{-1004}$.

In general, pairing gives $1-3+5-\cdots-(4m-1)=-2m$; if the last term has a plus sign, add it to the sum of the pairs before it.
