Let the tunnel have length $L$. If I run back, I cover $\dfrac{2}{5}L$ and meet the train exactly at the entrance. If instead I run ahead, in that same time I cover $\dfrac{2}{5}L$ and reach the point $\dfrac{4}{5}L$ of the tunnel just as the train arrives at the entrance. From then on, I cover the remaining $\dfrac{1}{5}L$ while the train covers the whole tunnel $L$, and we arrive together at the exit. So my speed is $\dfrac{1}{5}$ of the train's: $\dfrac{60}{5}=\boxed{12}$ mph.
