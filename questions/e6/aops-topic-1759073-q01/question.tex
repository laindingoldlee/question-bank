I am jogging in a train tunnel. When I am $\dfrac{2}{5}$ of the way through it, I hear a train approaching the tunnel entrance behind me at $60$ mph. Whether I run back towards the entrance or ahead towards the exit, I will reach that end of the tunnel at exactly the same moment as the train. What is my speed, in mph? (I run at a constant speed.)
