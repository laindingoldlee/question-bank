For an integer $k\ge3$, let $D(k)$ be the number of degrees in an interior angle of a regular $k$-gon. Which of the following ordered pairs $(m,n)$ satisfy $180^\circ\le D(m)+D(n)<240^\circ$? (More than one choice may be correct.)

(a) $(3,6)$\quad (b) $(5,8)$\quad (c) $(4,9)$\quad (d) $(6,8)$\quad (e) $(8,11)$
