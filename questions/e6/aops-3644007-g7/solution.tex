Let $PB=m$, $AB=n$, with positive integers $mn=10$, and put $t=\cos\angle PBA$. In coordinates $P=(0,0),B=(m,0)$, the altitude of A is $n\sqrt{1-t^2}$ and $PA^2=m^2+n^2-2mnt$. Tangency gives $PC=\dfrac{PA^2}{m}$, so area is $\dfrac{n(m^2+n^2-20t)\sqrt{1-t^2}}{(2m)}$. Replacing t by -u, the maximum for a fixed ratio $\rho=\dfrac{n}{m}$ is the maximum of $5(\rho^2+1+2\rho u)\sqrt{1-u^2}$. One may restrict $u\ge0$, and this expression increases with positive rho. Hence choose $m=1,n=10$, the largest available ratio. Differentiate $(101+20u)\sqrt{1-u^2}$: its interior maximum solves $40u^2+101u-20=0$, giving $u=\dfrac{(\sqrt{13401}-101)}{80}$. Thus the exact maximum is $\boxed{\dfrac{5}{4}(303+\sqrt{13401})\sqrt{1-(\dfrac{\sqrt{13401}-101}{80})^2}}\approx514.46368$. This configuration has $PC>PB$ as required.
