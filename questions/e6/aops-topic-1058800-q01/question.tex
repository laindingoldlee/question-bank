The arithmetic progressions $2,5,8,11,\dots$ and $3,10,17,24,\dots$ have some common values. What is the largest value less than $500$ that they have in common?

(A student writes the terms as $3n-1$ and $7x-4$, sets $3n-1=7x-4$, gets $3(n+1)=7x$ and concludes that $x$ is divisible by $3$. Is this step correct?)
