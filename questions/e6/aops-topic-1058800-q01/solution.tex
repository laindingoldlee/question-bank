The terms of the first progression are $3n-1$ ($n\ge1$) and those of the second are $7x-4$ ($x\ge1$). A common value satisfies
\[
3n-1=7x-4\iff 3(n+1)=7x .
\]
\textbf{The student's step is correct.} $3$ divides $7x$, and since $\gcd(3,7)=1$, $3$ divides $x$ (by unique prime factorisation, the prime $3$ must divide $x$). Write $x=3j$; then $n+1=7j$, and the common value is
\[
7x-4=21j-4\qquad(j\ge1),
\]
that is, $17,38,59,\dots$, the numbers that are $\equiv2\pmod3$ and $\equiv3\pmod7$.

\textbf{Largest below $500$.} $21j-4<500\iff j<24$, so $j=23$ gives $21\cdot23-4=\boxed{479}$ (here $x=69$ and $n=160$: $3\cdot160-1=479$ and $7\cdot69-4=479$).
