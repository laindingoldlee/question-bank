$x=2$ is a root. Dividing gives $4x^4+8x^3-23x^2-39x+63$. This has root $\dfrac32$, and further division gives $(2x-3)^2(x^2+5x+7)$ for the complete factorisation
\[(x-2)(2x-3)^2(x^2+5x+7).\]
The quadratic has discriminant $25-28<0$. So the distinct real roots are $2$ and $\dfrac32$, with product $\boxed{3}$.
