A student claims that $n^3+3^n$ is a perfect square for every positive integer $n$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that the claim is false.
\item[(b)] Prove that $n^3+3^n$ is not a perfect square for any even positive integer $n$.
\end{enumerate}
