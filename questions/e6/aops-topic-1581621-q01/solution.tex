Let Justin catch $j$ fish and his father $f$. Then
\[
j+f=12,\qquad 3j-12=5f-8 .
\]
Substituting $j=12-f$ in the second equation: $36-3f-12=5f-8$, so $32=8f$ and $f=4$, $j=8$.

Check: $3\cdot8=24$ exceeds $12$ by $12$, and $5\cdot4=20$ exceeds $8$ by $12$. So Justin caught $\boxed{8}$ fish and his father caught $\boxed{4}$.
