For positive rational numbers, a \emph{common multiple} of $r_1,\ldots,r_k$ is a positive rational number $L$ such that each $\dfrac{L}{r_i}$ is an integer, and the \emph{least common multiple} is the smallest such $L$. A \emph{common divisor} is a positive rational $d$ such that each $\dfrac{r_i}{d}$ is an integer, and the \emph{greatest common divisor} is the largest such $d$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the least common multiple of $\dfrac{2}{5}$, $\dfrac{9}{4}$, $\dfrac{6}{7}$, $\dfrac{3}{8}$.
\item[(b)] Find their greatest common divisor.
\end{enumerate}
