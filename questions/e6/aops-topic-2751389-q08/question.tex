Square $ABCD$ has side length $4$. Circle $\omega_A$ has centre $A$ and radius $AB$, and circle $\omega_C$ has centre $C$ and radius $CA$, the diagonal of the square. Let $T$ be one of the intersection points of $\omega_A$ and $\omega_C$. If the ratio of the area of triangle $CAT$ to the area of square $ABCD$ is $\dfrac{\sqrt a}{b}$ in simplest form, find $a+b$.
