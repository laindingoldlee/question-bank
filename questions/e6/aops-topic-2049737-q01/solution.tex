Repeatedly multiply the fractional part by $9$ and take the integer part as the next digit:
\[
\dfrac{1}{7}\cdot9=\dfrac{9}{7}=1+\dfrac{2}{7},\qquad \dfrac{2}{7}\cdot9=\dfrac{18}{7}=2+\dfrac{4}{7},\qquad \dfrac{4}{7}\cdot9=\dfrac{36}{7}=5+\dfrac{1}{7} .
\]
The remainder $\dfrac{1}{7}$ has returned, so the digits $1,2,5$ repeat:
\[
\dfrac{1}{7}=\boxed{0.\overline{125}_9}.
\]
Check: $0.\overline{125}_9=\dfrac{125_9}{9^3-1}=\dfrac{81+18+5}{728}=\dfrac{104}{728}=\dfrac{1}{7}$.
