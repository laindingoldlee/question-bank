There is a pile of apples on the ground. Bob takes $\dfrac16$ of the apples. Mary takes $\dfrac16$ of the remaining apples. Joe eats $9$ apples. Then Mary takes as many apples as the square root of the number of apples remaining. Now the number of apples left is a highly composite number less than $100$ (a positive integer with more divisors than every smaller positive integer). How many apples were there at the start?
