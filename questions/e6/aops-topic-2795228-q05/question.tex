Four villages have undirected trails $AB,BC,CD,BD,AD$ and no others. Trail hill counts are nonnegative. Routes $ABC$ and $BCD$ each have 10 hills; $ABD$ has 22; $ADB$ has 45. Find a route from $A$ to $D$ guaranteed to minimize the hill count.
