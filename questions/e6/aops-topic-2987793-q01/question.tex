Kelsey sits through a one-hour class. The probability that she eats a potato chip at some time during the class is $64\%$. Assume that chips are eaten ``at a constant rate'' in the sense that whether she eats one in the first half-hour and whether she eats one in the second half-hour are independent events with equal probabilities. What is the probability that she eats a chip during the first $30$ minutes? Explain why the answer $\dfrac12\cdot64\%=32\%$ is wrong.
