\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] A grocer has two kinds of tea, one selling for $80$ cents per pound and the other for $60$ cents per pound. How many pounds of each must he use to make $50$ pounds of tea selling for $74$ cents per pound?
\item[(b)] A pharmacist has a $12\%$ boric acid solution and a $20\%$ boric acid solution. How much of each must he use to make $80$ grams of a $15\%$ boric acid solution?
\item[(c)] A wine must have a $12\%$ alcohol content. How many gallons of wine with $9\%$ alcohol must be mixed with $3000$ gallons of wine with $15\%$ alcohol to achieve this?
\item[(d)] Alex estimated that it would take him $26$ minutes and $15$ seconds to cook pasta, but it actually took less time. If his percent error (relative to the actual time) was $5\%$, how long did it actually take?
\end{enumerate}
