\textbf{(a)} Let $x$ pounds be the $80$-cent tea and $50-x$ the $60$-cent tea:
\[
80x+60(50-x)=74\cdot50\ \Longrightarrow\ 20x=700\ \Longrightarrow\ x=35 .
\]
\boxed{35\text{ pounds of }80\text{-cent tea and }15\text{ pounds of }60\text{-cent tea}}.

\textbf{(b)} Let $x$ grams be the $12\%$ solution and $80-x$ the $20\%$ solution:
\[
0.12x+0.20(80-x)=0.15\cdot80=12\ \Longrightarrow\ 16-0.08x=12\ \Longrightarrow\ x=50 .
\]
\boxed{50\text{ g of the }12\%\text{ solution and }30\text{ g of the }20\%\text{ solution}}.

\textbf{(c)} With $x$ gallons of $9\%$ wine,
\[
0.09x+0.15\cdot3000=0.12(x+3000)\ \Longrightarrow\ 450-360=0.03x\ \Longrightarrow\ x=\boxed{3000\text{ gallons}} .
\]
(This makes sense: $12\%$ is exactly halfway between $9\%$ and $15\%$, so equal amounts are needed.)

\textbf{(d)} The estimate is $26$ min $15$ s $=26.25$ minutes. With actual time $t$, the percent error is $\dfrac{26.25-t}{t}=0.05$, so $26.25=1.05t$ and
\[
t=\dfrac{26.25}{1.05}=\boxed{25\text{ minutes}} .
\]
