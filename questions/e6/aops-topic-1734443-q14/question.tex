A torus is formed by revolving a circle of radius $r$ about a line in its plane at distance $R>r$ from its centre. Using Pappus's centroid theorems (the surface area of a surface of revolution equals the length of the generating curve times the distance travelled by its centroid, and the volume of a solid of revolution equals the area of the generating region times the distance travelled by its centroid), find the surface area and the volume of the torus.
