The membership total is $30+35+42=107$. If $t$ students do all three, the total is at most $3t+2(52-t)=104+t$, so $t\ge3$. Equality is possible: take $3$ doing all three, $10$ doing only swimming and soccer, $17$ doing only swimming and basketball, and $22$ doing only soccer and basketball. These give the required totals and $52$ students. Thus the minimum is $\boxed3$.
