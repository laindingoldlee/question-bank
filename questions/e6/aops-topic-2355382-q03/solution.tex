Each turkey is in two of the three pairs, so twice the total weight is $26+43+45=114$, and the total is $57$ pounds. The heaviest turkey is the one not in the lightest pair, so it weighs $57-26=\boxed{31}$ pounds. (The three weights are $57-45=12$, $57-43=14$ and $31$.)
