The period contains the years $2005$ to $2010$, of which only $2008$ is a leap year, so it has $6\cdot365+1=2191$ days, i.e. $2191\cdot24=52584$ hours. The rate is
\[
\dfrac{1600000}{52584}\approx\boxed{30.4}\text{ showers per hour}.
\]
