Three-digit multiples of $8$ run from $104=8\cdot13$ to $992=8\cdot124$: $124-12=112$ of them. A multiple of $8$ is a multiple of $6$ exactly when it is a multiple of $\operatorname{lcm}(8,6)=24$; three-digit multiples of $24$ run from $120=24\cdot5$ to $984=24\cdot41$: $37$ of them. The answer is $112-37=\boxed{75}$.
