In triangle $ABC$, points $D$, $E$, $F$ lie on sides $AB$, $BC$, $CA$ with
\[\dfrac{AD}{DB}=\dfrac{BE}{EC}=\dfrac{CF}{FA}=\dfrac23.\]
The cevians $AE$, $BF$ and $CD$ bound a small triangle inside $ABC$. Find the ratio of the area of triangle $ABC$ to the area of this small triangle.
