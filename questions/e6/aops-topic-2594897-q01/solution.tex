The coin values are $1,5,10,25,50,100$. Two of them add up to $11$ only as $1+10$: every coin other than a penny or a nickel is worth at least $10$ cents, and the sums using only pennies and nickels are $2$, $6$ and $10$, while $5+10=15$ and any coin of $25$ cents or more is already too large.

So the coins must be a penny and a dime. This does not contradict the statement: ``one of them is not a dime'' is true of the penny. The other one is a dime. The answer is $\boxed{\text{a dime and a penny}}$.
