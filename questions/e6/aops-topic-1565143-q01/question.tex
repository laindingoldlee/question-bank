The prime factorization of $1995$ is $3\times5\times7\times19$, which uses each odd digit ($1,3,5,7,9$) exactly once, and $1995$ is the smallest positive integer with this property. What is the next smallest positive integer whose prime factorization, written as a product of primes, uses each odd digit exactly once (and no other digits)?
