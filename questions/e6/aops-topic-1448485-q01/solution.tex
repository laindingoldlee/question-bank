Write the polynomial as $x^3+px^2+2017x+q$ with roots $a,b,c$. By Vieta, $ab+bc+ca=2017$ and $p=-(a+b+c)$. Then
\[
p^2=(a+b+c)^2=a^2+b^2+c^2+2\cdot2017 .
\]
Since $a^2+b^2+c^2-(ab+bc+ca)=\dfrac{1}{2}\left[(a-b)^2+(b-c)^2+(c-a)^2\right]\ge0$, we have $a^2+b^2+c^2\ge2017$, so
\[
p^2\ge3\cdot2017=\boxed{6051},
\]
with equality when $a=b=c=\sqrt{\dfrac{2017}{3}}$, that is, for $\left(x-\sqrt{\dfrac{2017}{3}}\right)^3$.
