Before the birthday correction and birth-year subtraction, the value is $(2k+5)50+1773=100k+2023$. If $B$ is the birth year and $e=1$ before the birthday, otherwise 0, the result is $100k+(2023-B-e)$. The parenthesis is the current age. For an age from 0 to 99, it occupies the final two decimal places, while $k\in\{3,\ldots,9\}$ is the hundreds digit. In another year $Y$, replace 1773 by $Y-250$.
