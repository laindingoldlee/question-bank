In the year 2023, choose an integer $k$ with $2<k<10$, double it, add 5, multiply by 50, add 1773, subtract 1 if your birthday has not occurred this year, and subtract your birth year. Prove that for an age between 0 and 99, the result has first digit $k$ and last two digits equal to your age.
