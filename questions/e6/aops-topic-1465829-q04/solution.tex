Write the triple as $ n-1,n,n+1 $. Its square sum is $ 3n^2+2=1111d $, where $ 1\le d\le9 $. Modulo $ 3 $, this forces $ d\equiv2 $, hence $ d=2,5,8 $. The respective values of $ n^2 $ would be $ 740,1851,2962 $. But $ 27^2<740<28^2 $, $ 43^2<1851<44^2 $, and $ 54^2<2962<55^2 $. None is a square. Therefore there are $\boxed{\text{no such triples}}$.
