Alice wins exactly when $\boxed{4\nmid N}$. If $4\nmid N$, she takes $N\bmod4$ cards, leaving a multiple of $4$. Whatever Bob takes ($k$ cards), she then takes $4-k$. This keeps a multiple of $4$ after each of her turns, so she takes the last card. If $4\mid N$, Bob uses the same strategy against her.
