Expanding, $(7+bi)(c+4i)=(7c-4b)+(28+bc)i$. So $7c-4b=5$ and $bc=12$. From the first, $b=\dfrac{7c-5}4$; substituting, $c(7c-5)=48$, i.e. $7c^2-5c-48=0$, so $(c-3)(7c+16)=0$. As $c$ is an integer, $c=3$ and $b=4$. Then $\left|b-ci\right|=\left|4-3i\right|=\sqrt{16+9}=\boxed{5}$.
