Consider the regular octahedron, cube and icosahedron, each with edge length $1$. The shortest distance an ant on the surface of the octahedron must walk to get from a vertex to the opposite vertex is $\sqrt3$; for the cube it is $\sqrt5$. How far must the ant walk on the surface of the icosahedron to get from a vertex to the opposite vertex?
