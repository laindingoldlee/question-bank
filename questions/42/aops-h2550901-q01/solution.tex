Write the line equations as $a_ix+b_iy+c_i=0$. The condition is
\[
\dfrac{|a_1x+b_1y+c_1|}{\sqrt{a_1^2+b_1^2}}
=2\dfrac{|a_2x+b_2y+c_2|}{\sqrt{a_2^2+b_2^2}}.
\]
Removing absolute values gives either of two linear equations:
\[
\dfrac{a_1x+b_1y+c_1}{\sqrt{a_1^2+b_1^2}}
=\pm2\dfrac{a_2x+b_2y+c_2}{\sqrt{a_2^2+b_2^2}}.
\]
They define two distinct lines through $\ell_1\cap\ell_2$. Thus the locus is $\boxed{\text{a pair of straight lines}}$.
