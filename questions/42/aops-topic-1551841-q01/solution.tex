A roll is $1000$ cm long. Since $7\cdot135=945\le1000<8\cdot135=1080$, each roll yields at most $7$ ropes (the leftover $55$ cm cannot be joined to make a rope). Then $3$ rolls give at most $21<22$ ropes, while $4$ rolls give $28\ge22$. So they need $\boxed{4}$ rolls.

(Dividing total lengths, $22\cdot135=2970$ cm, would suggest $3$ rolls, but that would require joining leftover pieces.)
