Factor the left side as $(\sin t+\cos t)(\sin^2t+\cos^2t)=\sin t+\cos t$. Hence
\[
\sin\left(t+\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}.
\]
The argument belongs to $[0,\dfrac{\pi}{2}]$, where sine is strictly increasing, so it equals $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$. Thus $\boxed{t=\dfrac{\pi}{12}}$.

\end{asy}
