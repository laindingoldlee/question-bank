By Vieta, $b=-(u+v)$ and $c=uv$, so $uv-u-v=298$ and $(u-1)(v-1)=299=13\cdot23$.

The factorizations with $u-1<v-1$ are $(1,299)$, $(13,23)$, $(-299,-1)$, $(-23,-13)$, giving $v-u=298,10,298,10$. The minimum is $\boxed{10}$ (e.g. $u=14$, $v=24$, $b=-38$, $c=336$).
