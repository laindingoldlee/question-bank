I have $n$ friends. Every day, each friend games for exactly $3$ hours, in one continuous block lying within that day (from 00:00 to 24:00). At no moment are more than $5$ friends gaming at once. Find the least $n$ such that, however the friends plan their gaming (subject to these rules), at every moment of the day at least one friend is gaming.
