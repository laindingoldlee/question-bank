Cancel $k!$ in each factor. The numerator becomes $1+(k+1)+(k+1)(k+2)=(k+2)^2$, while the denominator becomes $1-3(k+1)+(k+1)(k+2)=k^2$. Thus the product is $\left(\dfrac{3\cdot4\cdots100}{1\cdot2\cdots98}\right)^2=\left(\dfrac{99\cdot100}{2}\right)^2=4950^2=\boxed{24502500}$.
