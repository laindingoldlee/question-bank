Both radicands are perfect squares:
\[
4-2\sqrt3=(\sqrt3-1)^2,\qquad 97-56\sqrt3=(7-4\sqrt3)^2 ,
\]
since $3+1-2\sqrt3=4-2\sqrt3$ and $49+48-56\sqrt3=97-56\sqrt3$. Both bases are positive ($\sqrt3>1$ and $4\sqrt3=\sqrt{48}<7$), so
\[
4\sqrt{4-2\sqrt3}+\sqrt{97-56\sqrt3}=4(\sqrt3-1)+(7-4\sqrt3)=\boxed{3},
\]
an integer.
