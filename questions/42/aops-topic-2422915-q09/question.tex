Every day Bob bounces on his trampoline for either $1$ hour or $2$ hours, each with probability $\dfrac12$, independently of everything else. His sister bounces only on Saturdays: for half an hour, plus an extra $15$ minutes if Bob bounces for less than $1.1$ hours that day.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What is the expected total time that Bob and his sister bounce on a Saturday?
\item[(b)] What is the expected total time that they bounce on a day chosen uniformly at random from the seven days of a week?
\end{enumerate}
