If the hundreds digit were three, matching 308 and 324 exactly once would forbid tens digits zero and two and units digits eight and four. Then 904 could not match at all. Hence the hundreds digit is not three. Matching 308 uses either tens zero or units eight. Tens zero would force units four to match 324, but then 904 would match twice. Therefore units is eight, tens is two, and matching 904 requires hundreds nine. Thus $\boxed{N=928}$.
