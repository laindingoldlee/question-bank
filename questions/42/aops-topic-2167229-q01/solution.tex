Let $k=\lfloor\log_a b\rfloor$; since $b\ge1$, $k\ge0$ is an integer, and
\[k\le\log_a b<k+1,\qquad\text{so}\qquad a^k\le b<a^{k+1}.\]
Both $b$ and $a^{k+1}$ are integers, so $b<a^{k+1}$ gives $b+1\le a^{k+1}$. Together with $b+1>b\ge a^k$,
\[a^k<b+1\le a^{k+1},\qquad\text{so}\qquad k<\log_a(b+1)\le k+1 .\]
Hence $\lceil\log_a(b+1)\rceil=k+1=\lfloor\log_a b\rfloor+1$. $\blacksquare$

(Both sides count the digits of $b$ when it is written in base $a$.)
