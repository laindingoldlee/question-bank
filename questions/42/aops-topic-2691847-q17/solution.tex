Powers of $9$ end in $9$ for odd exponents and in $1$ for even ones; the exponent $19474729347293747$ is odd, so the first term ends in $9$. Every positive power of $5$ ends in $5$. The sum ends in the last digit of $9+5=14$, i.e. $\boxed{4}$.
