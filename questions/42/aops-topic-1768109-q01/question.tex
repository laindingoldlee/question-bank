In a right trapezoid $ABCD$, the bases are $AB=a$ and $DC=b$, and the leg $AD$ is perpendicular to both bases. The diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ are perpendicular. Prove that the altitude $AD$ equals $\sqrt{ab}$.
