The last replacement leaves $\lfloor\dfrac{1001}{4}\rfloor=250$ quarters. Dimes occupy multiples of three that are not multiples of four: $333-83=250$. Nickels occupy multiples of two that are multiples of neither three nor four: $500-166-250+83=167$. The other $1001-250-250-167=334$ coins are pennies. In cents the total is $25\cdot250+10\cdot250+5\cdot167+334=9919$, or $\boxed{99.19\text{ dollars}}$.
