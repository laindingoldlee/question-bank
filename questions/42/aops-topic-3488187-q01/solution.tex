There are $9^3=729$ numbers. Each digit position has average $5$, so their mean is $555$ and their sum is $729\cdot555$. If $n$ is erased, integrality requires $729\cdot555-n\equiv0\pmod{728}$, or $n\equiv555\pmod{728}$. Among three-digit numbers $111\le n\le999$, the only candidate is $555$: adjacent congruent values are $-173,1283$. It is allowed, so the requested sum is $\boxed{555}$.
