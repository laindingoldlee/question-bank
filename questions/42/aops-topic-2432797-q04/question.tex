Harry folds origami flowers: a rose takes him $6$ minutes and a lily $8$ minutes. Before starting each new flower he chooses, independently and with equal probability, whether to fold a rose or a lily. Exactly one hour after he started, Harry has just finished folding a flower. Given this, what is the probability that he has folded at least $9$ flowers?
