The formal equation at $T=8$ gives $x^9=8$, so $x=2^{\dfrac{1}{3}}$. Hence $y=x^{24}-x=256-2^{\dfrac{1}{3}}$, lying strictly between $254$ and $255$, and $\boxed{\lfloor y\rfloor=254}$. However, the infinite-tower iteration does not converge to $8$. The function $f(t)=x^{1+t}$ is increasing, satisfies $f(2)=2$, and has $1<T_1<2$. Induction makes $T_n$ increasing and bounded above by $2$, so its limit $L\in[1,2]$ satisfies $L=f(L)$. On $[1,2]$, $\ln t-(1+t)\ln x$ has derivative $\dfrac{1}{t}-\dfrac{\ln2}{3}>0$, so its only zero there is $2$. Thus the iteration converges to $\boxed2$, not $8$. The second relation is the valid fixed-point equation for the repeated 24th-root expression.
