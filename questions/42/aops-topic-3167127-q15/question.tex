A formal fixed-point equation $T=x^{1+T}$ is required to have the value $T=8$, with $x>0$. A positive number $y$ is then defined by $x^{24}=y+x$. Find $\lfloor y\rfloor$. Also determine whether iteration $T_0=1$, $T_{n+1}=x^{1+T_n}$ converges to $8$.
