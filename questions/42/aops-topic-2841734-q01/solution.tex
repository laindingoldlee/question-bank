Choose the co-presidents in $\binom{10}2=45$ ways. The committee is a nonempty subset of size at most $4$ of the other $8$ members: $\binom81+\binom82+\binom83+\binom84=8+28+56+70=162$ ways. The total is $45\cdot162=\boxed{7290}$.
