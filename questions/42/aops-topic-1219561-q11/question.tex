Fifty children numbered $1$ to $50$ stand in a circle. Child $1$ holds a bag of chocolates, gives one chocolate to child $2$, who then leaves the circle, and passes the bag to child $3$; child $3$ gives a chocolate to child $4$, who leaves, and passes the bag to child $5$; and so on around the circle, skipping children who have left, until one child remains. Which child never receives a chocolate?
