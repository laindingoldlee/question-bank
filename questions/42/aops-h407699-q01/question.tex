Emily cycles at $12$ miles per hour and Emerson skates in the same direction at $8$ miles per hour. Emily first sees him when he is $\dfrac{1}{2}$ mile ahead and can continue seeing him until he is $\dfrac{1}{2}$ mile behind. For how many minutes can she see him? Choices: (A) $6$; (B) $8$; (C) $12$; (D) $15$; (E) $16$.
