On the first day, Natasha puts one penny in each of two piles. On each following day she doubles the number of coins in the first pile and triples the number of coins in the second pile. How many fewer days does it take the tripled pile than the doubled pile to reach at least $500{,}000$ coins? (A calculator is allowed.)
