For legs $ a,b $, comparing areas gives $ ab=25\cdot12=300 $. Also $ a^2+b^2=625 $. Therefore $(a+b)^2=625+600=1225 $, so $ a+b=35 $. Adding the hypotenuse gives perimeter $\boxed{60}$.
