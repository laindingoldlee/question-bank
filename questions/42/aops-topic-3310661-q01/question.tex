A gathering has $25$ people who each eat six items. With probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, six additional guests arrive and each eats twelve items. Grilling $g$ items leaves $85\%$ available after losses. (a) Find expected demand. (b) If attendance is known before cooking, find the expected minimum integer number grilled. (c) How many must be grilled in advance to guarantee enough?
