Demand is $150$ or $222$, each with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Thus (a) expected demand is $\boxed{186}$. (b) The required integer amounts are $\lceil\dfrac{150}{0.85}\rceil=177$ and $\lceil\dfrac{222}{0.85}\rceil=262$. Their average is $\boxed{\dfrac{439}{2}}$. (c) To cover the larger possible demand requires $\boxed{262}$. Rounding the expected demand before computing a ceiling would answer a different question.
