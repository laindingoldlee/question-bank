Let a loaf have $L$ slices and a package $M$ slices. Mike made $s$ sandwiches: $M=5s$ and $L=2s+4$. Joey made $j$ sandwiches: $L=2j$ and $M=4j+4$. From $2s+4=2j$, $j=s+2$, so $5s=4(s+2)+4$ and $s=12$. Hence $L=\boxed{28}$ (and $M=60$).
