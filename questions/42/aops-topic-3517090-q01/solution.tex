Let Bob's stepping rate be $v$, Al's $3v$, and the escalator's upward rate $u$. Al takes time $\dfrac{150}{3v}=\dfrac{50}{v}$, so the escalator length is $N=150-\dfrac{50u}{v}$. Bob takes $\dfrac{75}{v}$, giving $N=75+\dfrac{75u}{v}$. Equating yields $75=\dfrac{125u}{v}$, so $\dfrac{u}{v}=\dfrac{3}{5}$. Therefore $N=75+45=\boxed{120}$ steps.
