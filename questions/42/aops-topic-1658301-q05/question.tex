Call a sequence \emph{Fibonacci-like} if every term after the second is the sum of the two previous terms. For integers $i,j$ let $F_{i,j}$ be the Fibonacci-like sequence with first term $i$ and second term $j$, and let $F_{i,j}(k)$ be its $k$th term. For each pair $(i,j)$ with $1\le i\le j\le2$ (that is, $(1,1)$, $(1,2)$ and $(2,2)$) and each $k=1,2,\dots,2018$, take the remainder of $F_{i,j}(k)$ on division by $5$. Find the remainder when the sum of all these $3\cdot2018$ remainders is divided by $1000$.
