Put $\ell_1:y=0$, centres $A=(0,2)$ and $B=(2\sqrt2,1)$, since their horizontal separation squared is $3^2-1^2=8$. Let $C=(x,8-r)$, with its disk below $\ell_2:y=8$. Tangency to $A$ gives $x^2=16r-32$, and tangency to $B$ gives $x^2-4\sqrt2x+8=16r-48$. Subtracting yields $x=3\sqrt2$. Hence $18=16r-32$, so $r=\dfrac{25}{8}$ and $\boxed{10a+b=258}$.

\end{asy}
