Segments $AC$ and $BD$ are parallel and lie on the same side of line $AB$, with $AC=a$ and $BD=b$. Lines $CB$ and $DA$ meet at $E$, and the line through $E$ parallel to $AC$ meets $AB$ at $F$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $EF=\dfrac{ab}{a+b}$, that is, $\dfrac{1}{EF}=\dfrac{1}{a}+\dfrac{1}{b}$.
\item[(b)] One painter can paint a fence in $x$ hours and another in $y$ hours. How long do they take working together? Explain how the answer corresponds to part (a).
\end{enumerate}
