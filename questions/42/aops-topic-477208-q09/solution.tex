The sphere touches all six faces of the big cube, so its diameter equals the edge of the big cube: $9$ (left figure, the cross-section through the centre parallel to a face). The small cube has all eight vertices on the sphere, so its space diagonal is a diameter of the sphere, also $9$. The right figure is the cross-section of the small cube through two opposite edges: a rectangle with sides $s$ (an edge) and $s\sqrt2$ (a face diagonal), whose diagonal is the space diagonal.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(9cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=3*sqrt(3);
draw(box((-4.5,-4.5),(4.5,4.5)),linewidth(0.9)); draw(circle((0,0),4.5),blue);
draw((-4.5,-5.3)--(4.5,-5.3),Arrows(TeXHead)); label("$9$",(0,-5.3),S);
label("face section of the big cube",(0,5.2),N,fontsize(8pt));
pair c=(13,0);
draw(circle(c,4.5),blue);
pair P=c+(-s*sqrt(2)/2,-s/2),Q=c+(s*sqrt(2)/2,-s/2),R=c+(s*sqrt(2)/2,s/2),T=c+(-s*sqrt(2)/2,s/2);
draw(P--Q--R--T--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); draw(P--R,red);
label("$s\sqrt{2}$",(P+Q)/2,S); label("$s$",(Q+R)/2,E); label("$9$",c+(-0.9,0.6),red);
label("diagonal section of the small cube",c+(0,5.2),N,fontsize(8pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

So $s^2+2s^2=81$, i.e. $s\sqrt3=9$ and $s=3\sqrt3$. The volume of the small cube is
\[
s^3=\left(3\sqrt3\right)^3=27\cdot3\sqrt3=\boxed{81\sqrt3}
\]
