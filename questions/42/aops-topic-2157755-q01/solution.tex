Write the equation as $x^2(x+1)=392=7^2\cdot8$, which suggests $x=7$: indeed $343+49=392$. By the factor theorem $x-7$ divides $x^3+x^2-392$, and division gives
\[x^3+x^2-392=(x-7)\left(x^2+8x+56\right).\]
The quadratic factor has roots
\[x=\dfrac{-8\pm\sqrt{64-224}}{2}=-4\pm\dfrac{\sqrt{-160}}{2}=-4\pm2i\sqrt{10}.\]
So the solutions are $\boxed{x=7\ \text{ and }\ x=-4\pm2i\sqrt{10}}$; $x=7$ is the only real one.
