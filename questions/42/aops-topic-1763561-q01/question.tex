Planes $\mathbf{P}_1$ and $\mathbf{P}_2$ are perpendicular and meet along the line $\ell$. Circle $\omega_1$ with radius $2$ and centre $O_1$ lies in $\mathbf{P}_1$. Unit circle $\omega_2$, centred at $O_2\in\mathbf{P}_1$, is internally tangent to $\omega_1$ at $T_1$, where the ray $\overrightarrow{T_1O_1}$ meets $\ell$ at an acute angle of $30^\circ$. Unit circle $\omega_3$, centred at $O_3\in\mathbf{P}_1$, is externally tangent to $\omega_1$ at $T_2$, where the ray $\overrightarrow{T_2O_1}$ is perpendicular to $\ell$. The minimum possible length of $\overline{O_2O_3}$ is $\sqrt m$ for a positive integer $m$. Compute $m$.
