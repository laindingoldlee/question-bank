Rotations by 120 degrees about $ O $ permute the three triangles $ AOB,BOC,COA $, so they have equal areas and partition $ ABC $. Thus $[AOC]=\dfrac{300}{3}=\boxed{100}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=dir(90),B=dir(210),C=dir(330),O=(0,0);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(O--A^^O--B^^O--C);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$O$",O,S);
\end{asy}
