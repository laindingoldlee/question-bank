Eight friends, Jeff, Alex, Rachel, Marcus, Sara, William, Ryan and Andrew, must get home in two different cars, each with five seats. Each car needs exactly one designated driver, chosen from the people in that car. Andrew and Ryan cannot drive; everyone else can. Rachel cannot share a car with Alex or with Jeff. A \emph{configuration} records who rides in which car and who drives each car (the seats of the passengers do not matter). How many configurations are there?
