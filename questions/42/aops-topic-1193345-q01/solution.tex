We need $-16t^2+150t+0.1>100$, i.e. $16t^2-150t+99.9<0$. The roots of $16t^2-150t+99.9=0$ are
\[t=\dfrac{150\pm\sqrt{22500-6393.6}}{32}=\dfrac{150\pm\sqrt{16106.4}}{32}\approx\dfrac{150\pm126.91}{32},\]
so about $0.72$ and $8.65$. The ball is above $100$ ft for $\boxed{0.72<t<8.65}$ seconds. (The poster's inequality used $>0$ instead of $>100$.)
