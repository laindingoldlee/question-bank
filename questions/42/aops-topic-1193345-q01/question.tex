A ball is thrown upward from a height of $0.1$ ft with initial velocity $150$ ft/s, so its height after $t$ seconds is $h=-16t^2+150t+0.1$. During what time interval is the ball higher than $100$ ft? Give the endpoints to the nearest hundredth of a second.
