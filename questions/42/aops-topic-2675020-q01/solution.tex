Let the prices be $a,b,c,d$ with total $T$. The six pair sums split into three complementary pairs, $(a+b)+(c+d)=(a+c)+(b+d)=(a+d)+(b+c)=T$. The five amounts are different, so they are five different pair sums; only one pair sum is unknown, so two complete complementary pairs are among the known amounts, both adding to $T$.

Among $13,15,19,20,22$ the only two disjoint pairs with equal sums are $13+22=15+20=35$ (the other pair sums $28,32,33,34,37,39,41,42$ are all different). So $T=\boxed{35}$, and the missing pair sum is $35-19=16$. (For example, the prices $6,7,9,13$ give the pair sums $13,15,19,16,20,22$.)
