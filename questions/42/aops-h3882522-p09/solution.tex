Let the decagon circumradius be $r$ and its side length be $R=2r\sin18^\circ$. Centers one step apart are distance $R$ apart and centers two steps apart are distance $2R\cos18^\circ<2R$ apart. Centers three or more steps apart have distance at least $2r\sin54^\circ>2R$, because $\dfrac{\sin54^\circ}{\sin18^\circ}=\dfrac{(3+\sqrt5)}{2}>2$.

There are ten unordered center pairs at each of the first two separations, and each pair gives two intersections. No point lies on three circles: otherwise its equal distances $R$ from three noncollinear decagon vertices would make it their circumcenter, whose circumradius is $r\ne R$. Thus all pairwise intersection points are distinct, giving $\boxed{40}$.
