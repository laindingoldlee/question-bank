Let the leading digit be $d$, with $3\le d\le9$. Then
\[
n=1000d+100(d-1)+10(d-2)+(d-3)=1111d-123.
\]
Since $1111\equiv1\pmod{37}$ and $-123\equiv25\pmod{37}$, the remainders are $d+25$, namely $28,29,\ldots,34$. These are all distinct and already in the correct remainder range. Their sum is
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{7(28+34)}{2}=217}.
\]
