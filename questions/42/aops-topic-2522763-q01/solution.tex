Use $abc=1$ to rewrite the second and third denominators. Multiplying the numerator and denominator of a fraction with denominator $b+bc+1$ by $a$, and of one with denominator $c+ca+1$ by $ab$:
\[b+bc+1=\dfrac{ab+abc+a}{a}=\dfrac{1+a+ab}{a},\qquad c+ca+1=\dfrac{abc+a^2bc+ab}{ab}=\dfrac{1+a+ab}{ab}.\]
Let $D=1+a+ab\ne0$. Then
\[\dfrac1{a+ab+1}+\dfrac1{b+bc+1}+\dfrac1{c+ca+1}=\dfrac{1+a+ab}{D}=1,\]
\[\dfrac a{a+ab+1}+\dfrac b{b+bc+1}+\dfrac c{c+ca+1}=\dfrac{a+ab+abc}{D}=\dfrac{a+ab+1}{D}=1.\]
Hence
\[E=20\cdot1+2021\cdot1=\boxed{2041}.\]
