\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] The sequence $1,2,4,5,7,8,10,\dots$ is formed by alternately adding $1$ and adding $2$. Find a formula for its $n$th term, its $100$th term, and the sum of its first $100$ terms.
\item[(b)] The sequence $1,2,8,16,64,128,512,\dots$ is formed by alternately multiplying by $2$ and multiplying by $4$. Find the sum of its first $20$ terms.
\end{enumerate}
