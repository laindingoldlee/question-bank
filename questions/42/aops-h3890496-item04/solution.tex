Let $t_i$ be child $i$\textquotesingle s number of taffies. Then $\sum t_i=7$ and $0\le t_i\le 3$; the licorice allocation is forced. Without upper bounds there are $\binom{11}{4}=330$ solutions. If a specified $t_i\ge 4$, subtract four to obtain $\binom 74=35$ solutions. Two such violations are impossible because they would require at least eight taffies. Thus the total is $330-5\cdot 35=\boxed{155}$.
