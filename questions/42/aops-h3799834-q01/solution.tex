Each weighing has three outcomes, so a four-weighing decision process has at most $3^4=81$ possible outcome sequences. Identifying the heavy object requires at least one distinct outcome sequence per candidate, giving $n\le81$. For $81$ objects, divide into three groups of $27$ and compare two groups. The heavy group is the heavier side, or the unweighed group if they balance. Repeat on that group with sizes $9,3,1$. Four weighings identify the object, so the maximum is $\boxed{81}$.
