In a row the first and last centres must be at least 1 from the short edges, leaving a centre span at most 26; spacing at least 2 permits at most 14 columns. Similarly at most 7 rows fit across the dimension 14. Thus at most $14\cdot7=98$ circles fit, and a tangent $7\times14$ grid attains it. The answer is $\boxed{98}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw((0,0)--(28,0)--(28,14)--(0,14)--cycle);for(int i=0;i<14;++i)for(int j=0;j<7;++j)draw(circle((1+2*i,1+2*j),1));label("28",(14,0),S);label("14",(0,7),W);
\end{asy}
