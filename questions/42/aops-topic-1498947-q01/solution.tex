Treat the equation as a quadratic in $x$:
\[
x^2-2(y+1)x+\left(4y^2-4y-8\right)=0 .
\]
For an integer solution $x$ the quarter-discriminant must be a perfect square:
\[
\dfrac{\Delta}{4}=(y+1)^2-\left(4y^2-4y-8\right)=-3y^2+6y+9=-3(y-3)(y+1).
\]
It is nonnegative only for $-1\le y\le3$, and then $x=y+1\pm\sqrt{\dfrac{\Delta}{4}}$:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
y & \dfrac{\Delta}{4} & x\\\hline
-1 & 0 & 0\\
0 & 9 & 4,\ -2\\
1 & 12 & \text{none}\\
2 & 9 & 6,\ 0\\
3 & 0 & 4
\end{array}
\]
The solutions are $(0,-1)$, $(4,0)$, $(-2,0)$, $(6,2)$, $(0,2)$, $(4,3)$, so there are $\boxed{6}$ pairs.
