Let $S=\sum a_i$ and $Q=\sum a_i^2$. We need $\dfrac QS\ge\sqrt{\dfrac Qn}$, i.e. $\dfrac{Q^2}{S^2}\ge\dfrac Qn$, i.e. $nQ\ge S^2$. That is the Cauchy--Schwarz (or QM--AM) inequality $(1^2+\cdots+1^2)(a_1^2+\cdots+a_n^2)\ge(a_1+\cdots+a_n)^2$. Equality holds iff all $a_i$ are equal. $\blacksquare$
