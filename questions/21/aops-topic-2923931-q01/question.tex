For positive reals $a_1,\dots,a_n$, prove that the contraharmonic mean $\dfrac{a_1^2+\cdots+a_n^2}{a_1+\cdots+a_n}$ is at least the root mean square $\sqrt{\dfrac{a_1^2+\cdots+a_n^2}n}$.
