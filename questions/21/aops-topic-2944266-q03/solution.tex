Write $t=\tan\theta$, so $0<t<1$ and $\cot\theta=\dfrac{1}{t}>1$. Both powers of $\dfrac{1}{t}$ exceed 1, and the larger exponent $\dfrac{1}{t}$ gives the larger power. Both powers of $t$ are below 1, and the smaller exponent $t$ gives the larger power. Hence the decreasing order is $\boxed{(\cot\theta)^{\cot\theta}>(\cot\theta)^{\tan\theta}>(\tan\theta)^{\tan\theta}>(\tan\theta)^{\cot\theta}}$.
