This is a multiset permutation: $\dfrac{5!}{2!\,2!\,1!}=\dfrac{120}{4}=\boxed{30}$. (Equivalently, choose the positions of the orange vases, $\dbinom52=10$ ways, then of the purple ones among the remaining three, $\dbinom32=3$ ways.)
