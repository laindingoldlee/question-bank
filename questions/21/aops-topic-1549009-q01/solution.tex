Each of the $10$ lines (rows, columns and the two diagonals) consists of $4$ squares with entries in $\{-1,0,1\}$, so its sum is an integer between $-4$ and $4$. There are only $9$ such integers:
\[
-4,-3,-2,-1,0,1,2,3,4 .
\]
By the pigeonhole principle, two of the $10$ line sums are equal. So the sums can never all be different. $\blacksquare$

(This is why an exhaustive computer search over all $3^{16}$ fillings finds nothing.)
