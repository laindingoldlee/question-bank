The even power is $z^{2024}=|z|^{2024}$. Since the power function increases for nonnegative inputs, $\boxed{0\le |z|\le(\dfrac{25}{8})^{\dfrac{1}{2024}}}$. The inequality gives an interval, not a unique value.
