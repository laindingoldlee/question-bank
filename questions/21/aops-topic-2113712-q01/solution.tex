The equation is \textbf{false}:
\[(1+i)(1+i)=1+2i+i^2=1+2i-1=2i,\]
and $2i\ne1+i$ because their real parts, $0$ and $1$, differ.

In general, $z=z^2$ means $z^2-z=z(z-1)=0$, and a product of complex numbers is $0$ only if a factor is $0$. So $z=z\cdot z$ holds only for $\boxed{z=0\text{ and }z=1}$; in particular it fails for $z=1+i$.
