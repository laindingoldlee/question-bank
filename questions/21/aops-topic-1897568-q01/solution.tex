$A$ walks $\dfrac{1}{4}$ round per minute and $B$ walks $\dfrac{1}{5}$ round per minute, so $A$ gains $\dfrac{1}{4}-\dfrac{1}{5}=\dfrac{1}{20}$ round per minute on $B$. Walking in the same direction, $A$ next meets $B$ when $A$ has gained one full round, which takes $20$ minutes ($A$ has then walked $5$ rounds and $B$ $4$ rounds). The time is $\boxed{7{:}20\text{ a.m.}}$
