A circle with centre $(h,k)$ and radius $r$ has equation $(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2$. Here $r^2=20$, so $r=\sqrt{20}=\boxed{2\sqrt5}$ (and the centre is $(5,7)$).

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((-1,0)--(11,0),Arrow(6)); draw((0,-1)--(0,13),Arrow(6));
label("$x$",(11,0),E); label("$y$",(0,13),N);
draw(circle((5,7),4.47214),blue+linewidth(0.9));
dot((5,7));draw((5,7)--(5+4.47214*cos(pi/6),7+4.47214*sin(pi/6)),red);
label("$(5,7)$",(5,7),SW); label("$2\sqrt5$",(5+2.236*cos(pi/6),7+2.236*sin(pi/6)),NW,red);
\end{asy}
