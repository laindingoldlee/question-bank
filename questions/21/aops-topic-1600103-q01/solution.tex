One lap takes $3\cdot10=30$ minutes, so the $10+5+15=30$ laps take $900$ minutes. Adding the break and the fall,
\[
900+13+\dfrac{10}{60}=913\dfrac{1}{6}\text{ minutes}=\dfrac{5479}{6}\text{ minutes}=\dfrac{5479}{360}\text{ hours}\approx15.21944\text{ hours}.
\]
To the nearest thousandth, the outing takes $\boxed{15.219}$ hours.
