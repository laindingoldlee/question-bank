Jenny rides her horse around a circular track at a constant rate; it takes her $10$ minutes to go one third of the way around. She rides $10$ laps, takes a $13$-minute break, rides $5$ laps, falls off for $10$ seconds, and then rides $15$ more laps. Her riding speed is not affected by the fall. How many hours does the whole outing take? Express your answer to the nearest thousandth.
