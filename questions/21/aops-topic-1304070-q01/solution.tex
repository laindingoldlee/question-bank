Two distinct lines meet in at most one point, so there are at most $\dbinom n2$ points. This is attained when no two lines are parallel and no three pass through a point: then every pair gives its own point. The maximum is $\boxed{\dbinom n2=\dfrac{n(n-1)}{2}}$.
