GLOBALHELLFRY has exactly $10$ distinct letters, G, L, O, B, A, H, E, F, R, Y, so they use all ten digits $0,\dots,9$ once each. The letter L appears $4$ times and every other letter once. So the digit sum of the number is
\[
(0+1+\dots+9)+3\cdot L=45+3L,
\]
a multiple of $3$. Hence the number is divisible by $3$, and being far larger than $3$, it is not prime. \textbf{No}, it can never be prime.
