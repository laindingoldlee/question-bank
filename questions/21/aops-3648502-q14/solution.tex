Let $A$ be the limiting point nearer home and $B$ the limiting point nearer school, measured from home. An outward leg ends at $B=A+(\dfrac{4}{5})(30-A)=24+\dfrac{A}{5}$. Returning four fifths of the distance home gives $A=\dfrac{B}{5}$. Solving yields $A=5,B=25$. The distance is $\boxed{20\text{ miles}}$. The two-step position map has contraction factor $\dfrac{1}{25}$, so the limits exist.
