Let $p$ be the smallest prime not dividing $n$. Every composite coprime to $n$ is at least $p^2$, while $p^2$ itself is coprime to $n$. Thus the property is equivalent to $n\le p^2$. If $p=2$, $n$ is odd and at most 4, giving 3. If $p=3$, $n$ is even, not divisible by 3, and at most 9, giving 2, 4, 8. If $p=5$, $n$ is divisible by 6, not 5, and at most 25, giving 6, 12, 18, 24. If $p=7$, it is divisible by 30 and at most 49, giving 30. If $p\ge11$, it must be divisible by $2\cdot3\cdot5\cdot7=210$, impossible below 201. The complete list sums to $2+3+4+6+8+12+18+24+30=\boxed{107}$.
