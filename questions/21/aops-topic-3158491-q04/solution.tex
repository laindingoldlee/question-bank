The sums are $ n_1x_1 $ and $ n_2x_2 $, so their ratio is $\dfrac{7}{5}\dfrac{15}{14}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{2}}$. These conditions are consistent, for example with side counts 14 and 10. 
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
for(int k=0;k<14;++k)draw(dir(90+360*k/14)--dir(90+360*(k+1)/14));for(int k=0;k<10;++k)draw((3,0)+dir(90+36*k)--(3,0)+dir(90+36*(k+1)));label("14 sides",(0,-1.3));label("10 sides",(3,-1.3));
\end{asy}
