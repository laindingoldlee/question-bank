The power of a point relative to a circle of center $O$ and radius $r$ is its squared distance from $O$ minus $r^2$. Find the radical axis (the set of points of equal power) of the circles $x^2+y^2=9$ and $(x-4)^2+y^2=4$, and describe its direction relative to the line of centers.
