Convert each rate to taps per minute:
\[4\text{ per minute},\qquad \dfrac{1}{\dfrac12}=2\text{ per minute},\qquad \dfrac{3}{\dfrac32}=2\text{ per minute}.\]
So her fastest rate is $4$ taps per minute. If she spends parts of a minute at different rates, say $t_1$, $t_2$, $t_3$ minutes with $t_1+t_2+t_3=1$, she makes $4t_1+2t_2+2t_3\le4(t_1+t_2+t_3)=4$ taps. Therefore $\boxed{\text{No}}$: in one minute she makes at most $4$ taps, never $5$.
