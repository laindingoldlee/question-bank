The equation is a constant multiple of $(x-2)(x-4)=x^2-6x+8$. Since the coefficients have no common factor and $A>0$, it is exactly $x^2-6x+8=0$, so $A+B+C=1-6+8=\boxed{3}$. (In general $A+B+C$ is the value of the polynomial at $x=1$, here $(1-2)(1-4)=3$.)
