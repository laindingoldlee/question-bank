With $6$ days to go before school starts, Amelia has already done $60$ problems of her summer assignment, while her niece Susan has not started the same assignment. By the time school starts both have finished. In these $6$ days Susan did $3$ times as many problems as Amelia. On average, how many problems per day did Amelia do in these $6$ days?
