If $c-a\le4$, then $a\le13$ from $200\ge a(a+1)+a+2$, while $200\le a(a+3)+a+4=(a+2)^2$ forces $a\ge13$. For $a=13$, $b=14,15,16$ give $c=18,5,-8$, none satisfying the bound and order. Thus the difference is at least 5. At difference 5, $c=a+5$ gives $a(b+1)=195$. Checking the positive divisor pairs with $a<b<a+5$ yields only $a=13,b=14,c=18$. Therefore the unique answer is $\boxed{(13,14,18)}$.
