Write the sum forwards and backwards and add: each of the $n$ columns sums to $n+1$, so $2T_n=n(n+1)$ and
\[
T_n=\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}.
\]
Hence $T_{160}=\dfrac{160\cdot161}{2}=80\cdot161=\boxed{12880}$.
