Let the band have $n$ members. Then
\[
n=mr+2=(m+1)(r-2)=mr-2m+r-2,
\]
so $r=2m+4$ and
\[
n=m(2m+4)+2=2m^2+4m+2=2(m+1)^2 .
\]
We need $2(m+1)^2<100$, that is $(m+1)^2<50$, so $m+1\le7$. The largest possibility is $m=6$, $r=16$:
$n=2\cdot49=\boxed{98}$. Check: $6\cdot16+2=98$ and $7\cdot14=98$.
