Write the first quadratic's negative integer roots as $-r,-s$, where $rs=20$ and $a=r+s$. The positive factor pairs $(1,20),(2,10),(4,5)$ show that the least possible $a$ is 9. Write the second quadratic's roots as $-u,-v$, so $u+v=17$ and $b=uv$. Among positive integers with sum 17, the minimum product is $1\cdot16=16$. These choices are independent and simultaneously attainable. Thus $\min(a+b)=9+16=\boxed{25}$.
