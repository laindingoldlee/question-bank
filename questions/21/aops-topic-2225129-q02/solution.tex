\textbf{(a)} The trick is to use a fraction:
\[\left(3+\dfrac{3}{7}\right)\times7=\dfrac{24}{7}\times7=\boxed{24}.\]

\textbf{(b)} For example
\[(10\times3-6)\times1=\boxed{24}\qquad\text{or}\qquad10\times3-6\times1=24 .\]
