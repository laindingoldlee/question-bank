Let the counts be $x,y,z\ge1$. Then $x+y+z=100$ and $\dfrac x2+3y+7z=100$, i.e. $x+6y+14z=200$. Subtracting, $5y+13z=100$. Then $5\mid13z$, so $5\mid z$; $z=5$ gives $y=7$, while $z\ge10$ is too large. So $x=100-12=\boxed{88}$.
