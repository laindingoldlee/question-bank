The prime factorization is $1430=2\cdot5\cdot11\cdot13$. If the four consecutive positive integers start at $n$, the product of any three lies between $n(n+1)(n+2)$ and $(n+1)(n+2)(n+3)$. For $n\le9$ even the upper bound is at most $10\cdot11\cdot12=1320$, and for $n\ge11$ the lower bound is at least $11\cdot12\cdot13=1716$. Thus the four are $10,11,12,13$. Since $10\cdot11\cdot13=1430$, the omitted number is $\boxed{12}$.
