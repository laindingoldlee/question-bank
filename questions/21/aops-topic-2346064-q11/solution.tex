Naomi travelled $6$ miles in $10$ minutes, i.e. $\dfrac16$ hour, so her average speed was $6\div\dfrac16=36$ miles per hour. Maya travelled $6$ miles in $30$ minutes, i.e. $\dfrac12$ hour, so her average speed was $12$ miles per hour. The difference is $36-12=24$. The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(E) }24}$.
