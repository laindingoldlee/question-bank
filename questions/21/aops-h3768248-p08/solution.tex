For totals at most 6, the largest fraction strictly below one half is at most $\dfrac{2}{5}$ (check even totals 2,4,6 and odd totals 1,3,5). Thus none lies strictly between $\dfrac{2}{5}$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}$. With seven students, three girls give $\dfrac{3}{7}$ in that interval. The minimum is $\boxed7$.
