The square root of the integer $q$ is rational and hence integral; write it as $s$. Then $p^3r^2s=50$. Since no prime cube divides 50, $p=1$. The possibilities for $r$ are 1 and 5. They give $(q,r)=(2500,1)$ and $(4,5)$, whose products are 2500 and 20. Their sum is $\boxed{2520}$.
