The addition formula gives $\sin(x+\dfrac{\pi}{2})=\sin x\cdot0+\cos x\cdot1=\cos x$. Setting $x=0$ in any such identity requires $\sin c=1$, whose positive solutions are $\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2k\pi$ for nonnegative integers $k$. Therefore the least is $\boxed{\dfrac{\pi}{2}}$.
