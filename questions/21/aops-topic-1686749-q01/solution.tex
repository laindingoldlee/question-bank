The whole circle has area $\pi\cdot12^2=144\pi$. A $30^\circ$ sector is $\dfrac{30}{360}=\dfrac{1}{12}$ of the circle.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0);real R=12;
filldraw(O--arc(O,R,0,30)--cycle,lightgray,linewidth(0.9));
draw(circle(O,R),linewidth(0.7));
draw(arc(O,2.5,0,30),linewidth(0.6));
label("$30^\circ$",O+4.3*dir(15));
label("$12$",(R/2,0),S);
dot(O);label("$O$",O,W);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

So its area is $\dfrac{144\pi}{12}=\boxed{12\pi}$ square centimetres.
