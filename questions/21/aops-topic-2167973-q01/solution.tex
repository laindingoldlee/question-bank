Introduce a slack variable $w=23-x-y-z\ge0$. The triples $(x,y,z)$ correspond exactly to the solutions of
\[x+y+z+w=23\]
in nonnegative integers. By stars and bars there are
\[\dbinom{23+3}{3}=\dbinom{26}{3}=\boxed{2600}\]
of them.
