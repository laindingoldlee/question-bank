Writing the syllable count of a line as $2a+3b$: $5=2+3$ is the only option, so each $5$-syllable line uses one two-syllable and one three-syllable word; and $7=2+2+3$ is the only option, so the middle line uses two two-syllable words and one three-syllable word. Altogether that is $4$ two-syllable and $3$ three-syllable words, so every word is used exactly once.

Assign the three-syllable words to the lines in $3!=6$ ways. Assign the two-syllable words: $4$ choices for line $1$, $3$ for line $3$, and the remaining two go to line $2$: $12$ ways. Order the words within lines: $2$ ways for line $1$, $3!=6$ for line $2$ and $2$ for line $3$. The total is
\[6\cdot12\cdot2\cdot6\cdot2=\boxed{1728}.\]
