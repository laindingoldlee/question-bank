A haiku is a poem with three lines: the first line has $5$ syllables, the second $7$ and the third $5$. The available words are the two-syllable words UNKNOWN, MEASURE, COUNTING, LOGIC and the three-syllable words ALGEBRA, TRIANGLE, REASONING. If each word is used at most once, how many different haiku can be made? (Two haiku are different if some line differs, including in the order of its words.)
