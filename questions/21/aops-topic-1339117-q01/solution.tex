\textbf{Proof.} By induction on $n$. For $n=1$: $1=1^2$. If $1+3+\dots+(2n-1)=n^2$, then adding the next odd number gives
\[
1+3+\dots+(2n-1)+(2n+1)=n^2+2n+1=(n+1)^2 .
\]
(Picture: an $n\times n$ square of dots grows into an $(n+1)\times(n+1)$ square by adding an L-shaped border of $2n+1$ dots.)

The numbers $1,3,\dots,99$ are the first $50$ odd integers ($99=2\cdot50-1$), so their sum is $50^2=\boxed{2500}$.
