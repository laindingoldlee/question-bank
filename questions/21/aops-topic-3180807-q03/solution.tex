Multiplying by $10a$ gives $b(10-a)=10a^2$. Since $b\le9$, $10a^2\le9(10-a)$, which forces $a\le2$. For $a=1$, $b=\dfrac{10}{9}$ is not an integer. For $a=2$, $b=5$. Therefore the decimal is $\boxed{2.5}$.
