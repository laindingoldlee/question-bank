\textbf{Yes.} Since $13=3\cdot15-2\cdot16$, three fillings of the $15$-litre bucket and two emptyings of the $16$-litre bucket suffice. Write the contents as ($15$-litre bucket, $16$-litre bucket):
\[
(15,0)\to(0,15)\to(15,15)\to(14,16)\to(14,0)\to(0,14)\to(15,14)\to(13,16).
\]
In words: fill the 15-litre bucket and pour it into the 16-litre bucket. Fill it again and pour until the 16-litre bucket is full, which takes $1$ litre, leaving $14$. Empty the big bucket and pour the $14$ litres into it. Fill the 15-litre bucket and pour until the big bucket is full, which takes $2$ litres. Exactly $\boxed{13}$ litres remain in the 15-litre bucket.
