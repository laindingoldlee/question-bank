The $k$-th cut crosses each earlier cut at most once, so it is divided into at most $k$ parts, each adding one piece. So $4$ cuts give at most $1+1+2+3+4=11$ pieces, and $11$ is reached when every two cuts cross inside the pizza and no three meet at a point. The answer is $\boxed{11}$.
