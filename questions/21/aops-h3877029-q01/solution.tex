Multiplication by five gives $3y^5x^2+25x+375+10y=0$. Since $y\ne0$, the quadratic formula gives $\boxed{x=\dfrac{-25\pm\sqrt{625-12y^5(375+10y)}}{6y^5}}$. Real solutions occur precisely for nonzero real $y$ satisfying $625-12y^5(375+10y)\ge0$. There is no uniquely specified pair $(x,y)$ from one equation alone.
