A square $ABCD$ and a circle are drawn so that the line $BD$ is tangent to the circle at the point $D$, and the centre of the circle and the point $A$ lie on the same side of line $BD$. The tangents from $A$ to the circle touch it at $K$ and $M$, and $\angle KAM=2\arcsin\sqrt{\dfrac{2}{5}}$. The radius of the circle is $3\sqrt2$. Find the side length of the square.
