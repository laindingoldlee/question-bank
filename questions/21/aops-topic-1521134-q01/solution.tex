The price is the same each month, so costs are proportional to litres. Let the August cost be $A=3125$. Then September costs $A\left(1+\dfrac{x}{100}\right)$ and October costs
\[
A\left(1+\dfrac{x}{100}\right)\left(1-\dfrac{x}{100}\right)=A\left(1-\dfrac{x^2}{10000}\right)=3080 .
\]
So $1-\dfrac{x^2}{10000}=\dfrac{3080}{3125}=0.9856$, $x^2=144$ and $x=12$ (as $x>0$). The September cost is
\[
3125\cdot1.12=\boxed{\$3500}
\]
(that is, $2800$ litres).
