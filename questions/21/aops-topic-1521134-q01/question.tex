In August, September and October, a company paid a constant price of $\$1.25$ per litre of fuel. In September it used $x\%$ more litres than in August, where $x>0$. In October it used $x\%$ fewer litres than in September. The company paid a total of $\$3125$ for fuel in August and $\$3080$ in October. Determine the company's total fuel cost for September.
