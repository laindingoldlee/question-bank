Factor $n^3-1=(n-1)(n^2+n+1)$ and $n^3+1=(n+1)(n^2-n+1)$. The product splits into two telescoping products. First,
\[
\prod_{n=2}^{1000}\dfrac{n-1}{n+1}=\dfrac{1\cdot2\cdot3\cdots999}{3\cdot4\cdot5\cdots1001}=\dfrac{1\cdot2}{1000\cdot1001}.
\]
Second, $n^2+n+1=(n+1)^2-(n+1)+1$, so the factor $n^2+n+1$ in the numerator for $n$ equals the factor in the denominator for $n+1$. Hence
\[
\prod_{n=2}^{1000}\dfrac{n^2+n+1}{n^2-n+1}=\dfrac{1000^2+1000+1}{2^2-2+1}=\dfrac{1001001}{3}.
\]
Multiplying,
\[
\dfrac{2}{1000\cdot1001}\cdot\dfrac{1001001}{3}=\dfrac{2002002}{3003000}=\boxed{\dfrac{333667}{500500}}\approx0.6666673,
\]
which is very close to $\dfrac{2}{3}$. (In general the product up to $N$ is $\dfrac{2(N^2+N+1)}{3N(N+1)}=\dfrac{2}{3}\left(1+\dfrac{1}{N(N+1)}\right)$, which tends to $\dfrac{2}{3}$.)
