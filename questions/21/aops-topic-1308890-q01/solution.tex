Write $A=a(1-bc)+(b+c)\cdot1$. By the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality,
\[
A^2\le\left(a^2+(b+c)^2\right)\left((1-bc)^2+1\right).
\]
Put $t=bc$. Since $a^2=2-b^2-c^2$, the first factor is $a^2+(b+c)^2=2+2t$, and the second is $t^2-2t+2$. So
\[
A^2\le2(1+t)(t^2-2t+2)=2\left(t^3-t^2+2\right)=4+2t^2(t-1).
\]
Also $t=bc\le\dfrac{b^2+c^2}{2}\le\dfrac{2}{2}=1$, so $2t^2(t-1)\le0$ and $A^2\le4$, i.e. $-2\le A\le2$.

Both bounds are attained: $(a,b,c)=(1,1,0)$ gives $a^2+b^2+c^2=2$ and $A=2$, and $(a,b,c)=(-1,-1,0)$ gives $A=-2$. So
\[
\boxed{\max A=2,\qquad \min A=-2}.
\]
