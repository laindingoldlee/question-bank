Let the motorcycle be ridden for $t$ hours, so the rider walks for $2-t$ hours. The distances add up to $25$ miles:
\[
45t+6(2-t)=25\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 39t=13\quad\Longrightarrow\quad t=\dfrac{1}{3} .
\]
So the motorcycle went $45\cdot\dfrac{1}{3}=\boxed{15\text{ miles}}$ before it broke down. (Check: the remaining $10$ miles take $\dfrac{10}{6}=\dfrac{5}{3}$ hours, and $\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{5}{3}=2$.)
