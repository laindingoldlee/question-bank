A motorcycle breaks down and the rider has to walk the rest of the way to work. The motorcycle had been driven at a speed of $45$ miles per hour, and the rider walks at a speed of $6$ miles per hour. The distance from home to work is $25$ miles, and the total time for the trip was $2$ hours. How far did the motorcycle go before it broke down?
