After the transfer jar $B$ has $53$ balls. Let $R$ be the number of red balls moved. The probability of red, given $R$, is $\dfrac{30+R}{53}$, so the overall probability is $\dfrac{30+E[R]}{53}$. By linearity of expectation $E[R]=3\cdot\dfrac{35}{50}=2.1$. Therefore the probability is $\dfrac{32.1}{53}=\boxed{\dfrac{321}{530}}$.
