The difference of two integers is divisible by $7$ exactly when they leave the same remainder upon division by $7$. There are $7$ possible remainders.

$70$ integers are not enough: take $10$ integers with each remainder; then no $11$ of them share a remainder. With $71$ integers, since $7\cdot10=70<71$, some remainder occurs at least $11$ times (pigeonhole principle), and those $11$ integers work. The answer is $\boxed{71}$.
