The fence is $2(60+20)=160$ feet long, so the square has side $40$ feet.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw(box((0,0),(6,2)),linewidth(0.8)); label("$60$",(3,0),S); label("$20$",(6,1),E);
draw(box((8,-1),(12,3)),blue+linewidth(0.8)); label("$40$",(10,-1),S,blue); label("$40$",(12,1),E,blue);
label("$1200$",(3,1)); label("$1600$",(10,1),blue);
\end{asy}

The old area is $60\cdot20=1200$ square feet and the new area is $40^2=1600$ square feet, an increase of $\boxed{\textbf{(D)}\ 400}$ square feet.
