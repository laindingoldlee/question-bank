A student evaluates $32\times35\times2^{-2}\times\left(\dfrac52\right)^{-3}$ as follows:
\begin{align*}
32\times35\times2^{-2}\times\left(\dfrac52\right)^{-3}&=32\times35\times\dfrac14\times\left(\dfrac25\right)^3\\
&=32\times35\times\dfrac14\times\dfrac{6}{125}\\
&=1620\times\dfrac14\times\dfrac{6}{125}=405\times\dfrac{6}{125}=81\times\dfrac{6}{25}=\dfrac{486}{25}=19\dfrac{11}{25}.
\end{align*}
Find the errors and evaluate the expression correctly.
