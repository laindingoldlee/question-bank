Write $s=\sin\theta,c=\cos\theta$, so $\tan\theta=\dfrac{s}{c}$. Then $n=s^2+cs+s+\dfrac{s^2}{c}$. Division by $s$ and subtraction of $1$ gives $\boxed{\sin\theta+\cos\theta+\tan\theta}$. The stated domain makes all divisions valid.
