From $(A+B)^2=A^2+2AB+B^2$ we get $64=20+2AB$, so $AB=22$. Then
\[A^3+B^3=(A+B)^3-3AB(A+B)=512-3\cdot22\cdot8=\boxed{-16}.\]
(Here $A$ and $B$ are non-real, since $(A-B)^2=20-44<0$; the identity holds all the same.)
