Let $D$ be the number of previous false statements minus the number of previous true statements. A speaker tells the truth precisely when $D=2$, then decreases $D$ by $1$; otherwise the statement is false and increases $D$ by $1$. Starting at $D=0$, the first two statements are false, after which truth and falsehood alternate. The true statements occur at positions $3,5,\ldots,29$, numbering $\boxed{14}$.
