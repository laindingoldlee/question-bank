The graph of $y=|x|$ is a V with vertex $(0,0)$, and the graph of $y=-|x|+4$ is an upside-down V with vertex $(0,4)$. For $x\ge0$ they meet where $x=-x+4$, at $(2,2)$, and by symmetry also at $(-2,2)$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
fill((0,0)--(2,2)--(0,4)--(-2,2)--cycle,palegreen);
draw((-3.2,0)--(3.2,0),Arrow(TeXHead)); draw((0,-1)--(0,5),Arrow(TeXHead));
label("$x$",(3.2,0),E); label("$y$",(0,5),N);
draw((-3,3)--(0,0)--(3,3),blue+linewidth(0.9)); draw((-3,1)--(0,4)--(3,1),red+linewidth(0.9));
dot((0,0));dot((2,2));dot((0,4));dot((-2,2));
label("$(0,0)$",(0,0),SE); label("$(2,2)$",(2.3,2),E); label("$(-2,2)$",(-2.3,2),W); label("$(0,4)$",(0,4),dir(30));
label("$y=|x|$",(3,3),E,blue); label("$y=-|x|+4$",(3,1),E,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The bounded region is the quadrilateral with vertices $(0,0)$, $(2,2)$, $(0,4)$, $(-2,2)$. Its sides have slopes $\pm1$, so it is a square, with diagonals of length $4$. Its area is
\[
\dfrac{4\cdot4}{2}=\boxed{8}.
\]
