Column by column, $A$ appears in the hundreds place once, $B$ twice and $C$ twice, and similarly for the other places. The sum is $(100+20+2)A+(200+10+2)B+(200+20+1)C=122A+212B+221C=1223$. Trying small digits, $A=3$, $B=3$, $C=1$ works: $366+636+221=1223$. It is the unique solution, since $C\ge2$ forces $122A+212B\le781$ with no solutions. $\overline{ABC}=\boxed{331}$.
