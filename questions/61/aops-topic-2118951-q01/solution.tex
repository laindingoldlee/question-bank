For every integer $k\ge0$, $\left(x^3\right)^k-(-1)^k$ is divisible by $x^3-(-1)=x^3+1$ (since $a-b$ divides $a^k-b^k$). So, modulo $x^3+1$, we may replace $x^{3k}$ by $(-1)^k$:
\[x^{81}=\left(x^3\right)^{27}\to-1,\qquad x^{48}=\left(x^3\right)^{16}\to1,\qquad 2x^{27}=2\left(x^3\right)^9\to-2,\qquad x^6=\left(x^3\right)^2\to1 .\]
Hence
\[x^{81}+x^{48}+2x^{27}+x^6+3=(x^3+1)\,Q(x)+(-1+1-2+1+3)\]
for some polynomial $Q$, and the remainder is $\boxed{2}$ (a constant, so of degree less than $3$).

(The step-by-step method of subtracting multiples such as $x^{78}\left(x^3+1\right)$ performs the same reductions: it turns $x^{81}$ into $-x^{78}$, then $x^{75}$, and so on, and also gives $2$.)
