Let $x^2+3x+147=k^2$ with $k>0$. Multiply by $4$: $(2x+3)^2+579=(2k)^2$, so
\[(2k-2x-3)(2k+2x+3)=579=3\cdot193.\]
Both factors are positive and odd, with the second larger: $(1,579)$ gives $2x+3=289$, $x=143$; $(3,193)$ gives $2x+3=95$, $x=46$. The sum is $143+46=\boxed{189}$.
