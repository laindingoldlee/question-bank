At 8:48 the minute hand is at $288^\circ$ clockwise from twelve and the hour hand is at $264^\circ$, so the smaller angle is $24^\circ$. The minute hand gains $6-\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{11}{2}$ degrees per minute. The first increase to $36^\circ$ needs $12^\circ$, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{24}{11}\text{ minutes}}$. This occurs before the separation reaches $180^\circ$, so it is indeed the first such time.
\begin{asy}
size(7cm);
draw(circle((0,0),1));pair H=0.65dir(90-264),M=0.9dir(90-288);draw((0,0)--H,linewidth(1));draw((0,0)--M);label("hour",H,SW);label("minute",M,NW);label("12",(0,1),N);label("$24^\circ$",(-0.4,0),E);
\end{asy}
