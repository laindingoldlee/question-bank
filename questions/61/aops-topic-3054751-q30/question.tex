In right triangle $ABC$, $\angle B=90^\circ$ and $AB:BC=3:4$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of $BC$ and $N$ an interior point of $AB$. Lines $AM,CN$ meet at $P$, and ray $BP$ meets $AC$ at $Q$. Suppose $(\dfrac{CM}{CB})(\dfrac{BN}{NA})(\dfrac{AP}{PM})=1$. Is $\dfrac{BP}{BQ}$ uniquely determined? If not, find its possible range.
