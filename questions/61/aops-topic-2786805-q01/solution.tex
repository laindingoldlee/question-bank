From February $22$, $2022$ to February $22$, $2222$ there are $200$ years. The leap days in this span are February $29$ of the years $2024,2028,\dots,2220$, which is $50$ years, minus the century years $2100$ and $2200$, which are not leap years: $48$ leap days. The number of days is $200\cdot365+48$. Since $365\equiv1\pmod7$,
\[200\cdot365+48\equiv200+48=248\equiv3\pmod7.\]
Three days after Tuesday is $\boxed{\text{Friday}}$.
