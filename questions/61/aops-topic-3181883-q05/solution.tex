The possible sets of three distinct digits summing to $5$ are $\{0,1,4\}$ and $\{0,2,3\}$. A prime exceeding $5$ must end in $1$ or $3$ here. The only resulting three-digit candidates are $401$ and $203$. The latter is $7\cdot29$; $401$ is divisible by none of $2,3,5,7,11,13,17,19$, the primes at most $\sqrt{401}$. The required sum is $\boxed{401}$.
