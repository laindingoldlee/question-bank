A $30^\circ$-$60^\circ$-$90^\circ$ triangle $OAB_1$ has its right angle at $A$, its $30^\circ$ angle at $O$, and shortest side $AB_1=1$. A second $30^\circ$-$60^\circ$-$90^\circ$ triangle $OB_1B_2$ is built outside the first one, with its $30^\circ$ angle at $O$ and its longer leg $OB_1$ equal to the hypotenuse of the first triangle. The process is repeated, each new triangle $OB_kB_{k+1}$ having the hypotenuse of the previous triangle as its longer leg and turning around $O$ in the same direction, until the hypotenuse of the last triangle lies along the longer leg $OA$ of the first triangle. What is the length of the part of the last hypotenuse that is not on $OA$?
