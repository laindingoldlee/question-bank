The ray $OB$ lies inside the angle $\angle AOC$ (with $\angle AOC<180^\circ$). The rays $OX$, $OY$, $OZ$ are the bisectors of $\angle AOB$, $\angle BOC$ and $\angle COA$ respectively. Prove that
\[
\dfrac{\angle XOY}{\angle COA}+\dfrac{\angle XOZ}{\angle BOC}+\dfrac{\angle YOZ}{\angle AOB}=\dfrac{3}{2} .
\]
