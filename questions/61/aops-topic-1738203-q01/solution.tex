He needs exactly $4$ correct guesses out of $9$, each correct with probability $\dfrac{1}{5}$ independently. By the binomial formula,
\[
\dbinom94\left(\dfrac{1}{5}\right)^4\left(\dfrac{4}{5}\right)^5=\dfrac{126\cdot4^5}{5^9}=\boxed{\dfrac{129024}{1953125}}\approx0.066 .
\]
