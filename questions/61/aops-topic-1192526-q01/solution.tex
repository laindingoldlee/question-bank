If $x\le0$, the first equation gives $y=8>0$, and then the second gives $x=14$, a contradiction. So $x>0$ and $2x+y=8$.

If $y\ge0$, the second equation gives $x=14$, and then $y=8-28<0$, a contradiction. So $y<0$ and $x-2y=14$.

Solving $2x+y=8$ and $x-2y=14$ gives $x=6$, $y=-4$, which satisfy the sign conditions. Hence $x+y=\boxed{2}$.
