She needs a total of at least $6\cdot85=510$, and she already has $2\cdot80=160$, so the last four tests must total at least $350$. Tests $3$, $4$ and $5$ give at most $300$, so the last test needs at least $50$; and $100,100,100,50$ works. The minimum is $\boxed{50}$.
