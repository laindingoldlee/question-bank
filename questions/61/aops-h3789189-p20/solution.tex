Work in a frame moving with the water, where the mattress is stationary. After passing it, the swimmer goes away from it for ten minutes at his fixed speed relative to water; returning takes another ten minutes at the same speed. Thus the mattress drifts for twenty minutes between the two meetings. It moves the $400$ metres from the first meeting point to the starting point, so the current speed is $\dfrac{400}{20}=\boxed{20}$ metres per minute.
