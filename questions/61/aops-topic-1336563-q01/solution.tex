Think of $15$ ones in a row with $14$ gaps between them. Choosing $2$ of the gaps to cut splits the row into three nonempty blocks of sizes $x,y,z$, and every solution arises exactly once. So there are $\dbinom{14}{2}=\boxed{91}$ ordered triples.
