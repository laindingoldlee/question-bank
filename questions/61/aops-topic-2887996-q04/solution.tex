The altitudes are $15$, $8$ and $\dfrac{8\cdot15}{17}=\dfrac{120}{17}$, and the area is $60$. So the answer is $23+\dfrac{120}{17}-60=\boxed{-\dfrac{509}{17}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(15,0),C=(0,8);draw(A--B--C--cycle);pair F=C+dot(A-C,B-C)/dot(B-C,B-C)*(B-C);draw(A--F,dashed);label("$15$",(7.5,0),S);label("$8$",(0,4),W);label("$17$",(7.5,4),NE);
\end{asy}
