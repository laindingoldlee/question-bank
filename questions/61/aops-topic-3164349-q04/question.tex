A floor is tiled by regular hexagons of side $14$. A circular coin of radius $\sqrt3$ is dropped with its center uniformly distributed modulo one tile. The probability that the coin lies wholly within a single tile is $\dfrac{p}{q}$ in lowest terms. Find $p+q$.
