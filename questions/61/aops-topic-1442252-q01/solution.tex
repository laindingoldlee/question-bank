The roots are $\dfrac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-8c}}{4}$, which differ by $\dfrac{\sqrt{b^2-8c}}{2}$. So $b^2-8c=1600$, i.e.\ $c=\dfrac{b^2-1600}{8}$. For $c$ to be a positive integer we need $b>40$ and $8\mid b^2$, i.e.\ $4\mid b$. Since $c$ increases with $b$, the least $b+c$ comes from the least $b$, which is $b=44$, with $c=\dfrac{1936-1600}{8}=42$. So the least value is $b+c=\boxed{86}$ (the roots are $-1$ and $-21$).
