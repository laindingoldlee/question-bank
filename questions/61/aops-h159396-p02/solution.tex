Set $w=z^2$. The quadratic $w^2+4w+8=0$ gives $w=-2\pm2i$. If $z=a+ib$, then $a^2-b^2=-2$, $a^2+b^2=|w|=2\sqrt2$. Consequently
\[
\boxed{z=\pm\sqrt{\sqrt2-1}\ \pm i\sqrt{\sqrt2+1}},
\]
where the signs are independent. The values of $w$ have modulus $2\sqrt2$ and arguments $\dfrac{3\pi}{4},\dfrac{5\pi}{4}$. Taking both square roots of each gives
\[
\boxed{z=2^{\dfrac{3}{4}}(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta),\quad
\theta\in\left\{\dfrac{3\pi}{8},\dfrac{5\pi}{8},\dfrac{11\pi}{8},\dfrac{13\pi}{8}\right\}.}
\]
