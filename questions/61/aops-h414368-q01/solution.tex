Triangle $ABD$ has half the parallelogram's area, namely $40$. Since $AE=\dfrac{1}{2}AD$, triangles $ABE,ABD$ share the altitude from $B$ and have area ratio $1:2$. Thus $[ABE]=20$. Removing it leaves $[BEDC]=80-20=\boxed{60}$, choice \textbf{C}.

\end{asy}
