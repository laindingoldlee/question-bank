A hidden integer lies from 1 through 10000 inclusive. You choose in advance a list of moduli from 1 through 100 and receive the remainder modulo each. First minimize the number of moduli required to guarantee identification of every hidden integer. Subject to that, minimize the sum of the moduli. Find both minima.
