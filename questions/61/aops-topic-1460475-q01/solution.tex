Factor $2016!$ out of the denominator: $2016!+2017!=2016!\,(1+2017)=2016!\cdot2018$. Also $2018!=2018\cdot2017\cdot2016!$. Hence
\[
\dfrac{2018!}{2016!+2017!}=\dfrac{2018\cdot2017\cdot2016!}{2018\cdot2016!}=\boxed{2017}.
\]
