Use $x^3+y^3=(x+y)^3-3xy(x+y)$:
\[
144=216-18xy,\qquad xy=4 .
\]
So $x$ and $y$ are the roots of $t^2-6t+4=0$, that is, $t=3\pm\sqrt5$. The pairs are
\[
\boxed{(x,y)=(3+\sqrt5,\,3-\sqrt5)\ \text{ or }\ (3-\sqrt5,\,3+\sqrt5)}.
\]
