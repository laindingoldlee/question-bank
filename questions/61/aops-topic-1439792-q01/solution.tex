Either the $54^\circ$ angle is one of the two equal base angles, or it is the vertex angle.
\begin{itemize}
\item Case 1: the base angles are $54^\circ$, and the vertex angle is $180^\circ-108^\circ=72^\circ$. Angles: $54^\circ,54^\circ,72^\circ$.
\item Case 2: the vertex angle is $54^\circ$, and the base angles are $\dfrac{180^\circ-54^\circ}{2}=63^\circ$. Angles: $54^\circ,63^\circ,63^\circ$.
\end{itemize}

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(8cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A1=(0,0),B1=(6,0),C1=(3,3*Tan(54));
pair A2=(9,0),B2=(15,0),C2=(12,3*Tan(63));
draw(A1--B1--C1--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); draw(A2--B2--C2--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(arc(A1,0.8,0,54),red); draw(arc(B1,0.8,126,180),red); draw(arc(C1,0.8,-117,-63),blue);
draw(arc(A2,0.8,0,63),blue); draw(arc(B2,0.8,117,180),blue); draw(arc(C2,0.8,-117,-63),red);
label("$54^\circ$",A1+1.5*dir(27),red); label("$54^\circ$",B1+1.5*dir(153),red); label("$72^\circ$",C1+1.35*dir(-90),blue);
label("$63^\circ$",A2+1.5*dir(31.5),blue); label("$63^\circ$",B2+1.5*dir(148.5),blue); label("$54^\circ$",C2+1.35*dir(-90),red);
label("Case 1",(3,-0.3),S); label("Case 2",(12,-0.3),S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

\textbf{(a)} The greatest possible angle is $\boxed{72^\circ}$ (case 1). \textbf{(b)} The least possible angle is $\boxed{54^\circ}$ (it occurs in both cases; no angle is smaller).
