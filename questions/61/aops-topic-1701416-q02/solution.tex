Suppose $e$ and $f$ are both identities. Compute $e\,\#\,f$ in two ways:
\begin{itemize}
\item since $f$ is an identity, $e\,\#\,f=e$ (use $b\,\#\,f=b$ with $b=e$);
\item since $e$ is an identity, $e\,\#\,f=f$ (use $e\,\#\,b=b$ with $b=f$).
\end{itemize}
Hence $e=e\,\#\,f=f$, so any two identities are equal, and an operation has at most one identity. $\blacksquare$
