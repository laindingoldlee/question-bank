Call a pile size losing if the player to move loses with best play. A pile of $1$ is losing (you must take the last chip). From $2,3,4,5$ you can leave $1$, so these are winning; from $6$ every move leaves $2$ to $5$, so $6$ is losing. Inductively, the losing sizes are exactly those $\equiv1\pmod5$: from such a size every move leaves a size $\not\equiv1$, and from any other size you can move to one $\equiv1$.

$2014\equiv4\pmod5$, so remove $\boxed{3}$ chips to leave $2011\equiv1\pmod5$, and afterwards always restore a multiple of $5$ plus one.
