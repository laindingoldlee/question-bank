In the tetrahedron $ABCD$, the three faces meeting at $A$ are right triangles with their right angles at $A$; that is, $AB$, $AC$ and $AD$ are mutually perpendicular. If $AB=2$, $AC=3$ and $AD=4$, find the distance from $A$ to the plane of face $BCD$. Express your answer as a common fraction in simplest radical form.
