Yes. Dividing powers of the same base subtracts exponents:
\[\dfrac{2^3}{2^6}=2^{3-6}=2^{-3}=\dfrac{1}{2^3}.\]
Directly: $\dfrac{2^3}{2^6}=\dfrac{2^3}{2^3\cdot2^3}=\dfrac{1}{2^3}$, and numerically $\dfrac{8}{64}=\dfrac{1}{8}$.
