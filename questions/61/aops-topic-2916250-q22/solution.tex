$\angle BMC=180^\circ-60^\circ-60^\circ=60^\circ$ and $MB=MC$ (both equal $AM=MD$), so triangle $BMC$ is equilateral with side $BC=4$. The trapezoid is three equilateral triangles of side $4$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),M=(4,0),D=(8,0),B=dir(60)*4,C=M+dir(60)*4;draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(B--M--C);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,N);label("$C$",C,N);label("$D$",D,SE);label("$M$",M,S);
\end{asy}

Its area is $3\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}4\cdot16=\boxed{12\sqrt3}$.
