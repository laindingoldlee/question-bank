Let $a$ and $b$ be positive numbers and $n$ a positive integer, and let
\[
t_k=\dbinom{n}{k}a^{n-k}b^k\qquad(k=0,1,\dots,n)
\]
be the terms of the expansion of $(a+b)^n$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that $\dfrac{t_{k+1}}{t_k}=\dfrac{n-k}{k+1}\cdot\dfrac{b}{a}$, and deduce that the terms first increase and then decrease (one of the two stages may be empty, and at most two consecutive terms can be equal).
\item[(b)] Find the largest term in the expansion of $(2+3)^{12}$, that is, the value of $k$ for which $t_k=\dbinom{12}{k}2^{12-k}3^k$ is largest, and the value of that term.
\end{enumerate}
