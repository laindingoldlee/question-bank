Let $a$ and $b$ be positive integers.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that if $\gcd(a,b)+\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)$ is a prime number, then $a$ and $b$ are relatively prime.
\item[(b)] Is the converse true?
\end{enumerate}
