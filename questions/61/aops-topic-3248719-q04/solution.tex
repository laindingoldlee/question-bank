The speed ratio is $\dfrac{v_B}{v_D}=\dfrac{186}{200}=\dfrac{93}{100}$. Daria travels $214$ metres in the second race, so Benny travels $214\cdot\dfrac{93}{100}=199.02$ metres. Daria leads by $0.98$ metre, or $\boxed{98\text{ cm}}$.
