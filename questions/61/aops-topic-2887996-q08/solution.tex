The number of ways to roll sum $s$ with four dice is the coefficient of $x^s$ in $(x+\cdots+x^6)^4$: $104$ for $s=11$ and $140$ for $s=13$. The probability is $\dfrac{244}{1296}=\boxed{\dfrac{61}{324}}$.
