Let $f$ and $t$ be the numbers of fireworks and t-shirts, so $19f+15t=159$ with $f,t\ge0$. Then $f\le8$, and $159-19f$ must be a multiple of $15$:
\[159,\ 140,\ 121,\ 102,\ 83,\ 64,\ 45,\ 26,\ 7\qquad(f=0,1,\ldots,8).\]
Only $45$ (for $f=6$) is a multiple of $15$, giving $t=3$. So Kevin bought $6+3=\boxed{9}$ items.
