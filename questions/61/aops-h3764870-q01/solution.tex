If the sum is prime $p$, divisibility of the product by $p$ forces some element to be divisible by $p$. Since all elements are positive and their sum is $p$, that element must be $p$ itself and there can be no others. Conversely each singleton $\{p\}$ works. The primes at most $20$ are $2,3,5,7,11,13,17,19$, so the answer is $\boxed8$.
