Since $\gcd(6\cdot8,49)=1$, the inverses exist, and $(6\cdot8)^{-1}\equiv6^{-1}8^{-1}$. Hence
\[(8+6)(6\cdot8)^{-1}\equiv8\cdot6^{-1}8^{-1}+6\cdot6^{-1}8^{-1}\equiv6^{-1}+8^{-1}\pmod{49},\]
exactly as with ordinary fractions $\dfrac16+\dfrac18=\dfrac{8+6}{48}$. Now $48\equiv-1$, so $48^{-1}\equiv-1$ and
\[6^{-1}+8^{-1}\equiv-14\equiv\boxed{35}\pmod{49}.\]
(Check: $6^{-1}\equiv41$ because $6\cdot41=246=5\cdot49+1$; $8^{-1}\equiv43$ because $8\cdot43=344=7\cdot49+1$; and $41+43=84\equiv35$.)
