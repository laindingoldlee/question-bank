Substitute $t=y+2$ into $x^2+y^2=t^2$. This gives $x^2=4y+4$, so $f(x)=\dfrac{x^2}{4}-1$. Conversely this gives $y\ge-1,t\ge1$ and valid intersections for every real $x$. The coefficient sum is $f(1)=\boxed{-\dfrac{3}{4}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
import graph;real f(real x){return x*x/4-1;}draw(graph(f,-4,4),blue);draw(circle((0,0),2),gray);draw((-3,0)--(3,0),dashed);label("$y=\dfrac{x^2}{4}-1$",(3.5,2.5),NE);label("$t=2$",(-2,0),NW);
\end{asy}
