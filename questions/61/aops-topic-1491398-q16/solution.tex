Pair each divisor $d$ of $8!$ with $\dfrac{8!}{d}$. Since $8!=8\cdot7!$,
\[
d>7!\iff\dfrac{8!}{d}<\dfrac{8!}{7!}=8 .
\]
So the divisors larger than $7!$ correspond to the divisors of $8!$ less than $8$, namely $1,2,3,4,5,6,7$. All of them divide $8!$. Hence there are $\boxed{7}$ such divisors: $8!$, $\dfrac{8!}{2}$, $\dfrac{8!}{3}$, $\dots$, $\dfrac{8!}{7}$.
