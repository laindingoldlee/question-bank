At least nine hexadecimal digits are required, so at least seventeen base-four digits are needed. A seventeen-digit base-four palindrome is determined by its first nine digits. Enumerate these prefixes in increasing order, from $100000000_4$ through $333333333_4$, append the reverse of the first eight digits, and convert each result to base sixteen. The first passing the two hexadecimal tests is $\boxed{163498725_{16}}=11203102120130211_4$. Its nine hexadecimal digits are distinct. All shorter integers have at most eight hexadecimal digits, and all earlier seventeen-digit palindromes were tested, proving minimality.
