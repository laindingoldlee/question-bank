Two territories initially contain $n$ units each. In a battle, if the attacker has $A\ge2$ units and the defender has $B\ge1$, they roll $\min(3,A)$ and $\min(2,B)$ independent fair six-sided dice. Pair the highest dice, then the next highest if both sides have another die. In each pair the defender loses one unit if the attacking roll is higher; otherwise the attacker loses one. Repeat until $B=0$ (attacker wins) or $A<2$ (attacker loses). Give an exact recurrence for the victory probability for arbitrary $n$, and compute it for $n=2$.
