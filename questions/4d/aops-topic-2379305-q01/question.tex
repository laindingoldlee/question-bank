King Arthur and his $359$ knights sit down at a round table with $360$ chairs, numbered $1$ to $360$ in order around the table; every seating of the $360$ people is equally likely. Then everybody changes places by a fixed rule: the person in chair $k$ moves to chair $361-k$ (so the people in chairs $1$ and $360$ swap, those in chairs $2$ and $359$ swap, and so on). You are one of the knights. What is the probability that after the swaps you sit immediately to King Arthur's right?
