Let $ABCDEFGH$ be a cube with bottom face $ABCD$ and top face $EFGH$, where $E,F,G,H$ lie directly above $A,B,C,D$, and the front face is $ABFE$. The edge $AB$ is the bottom edge of the front face, and the top edge $FG$ joins the front face to the back face. Are the lines $AB$ and $FG$ parallel? If not, do they intersect? Find the angle between their directions.
