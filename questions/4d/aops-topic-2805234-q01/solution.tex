Suppose $k$ players score exactly $5$. The $\binom k2$ games among themselves distribute $\binom k2$ points to them, so $\dfrac{k(k-1)}2\le5k$, giving $k\le11$.

Eleven is possible: let $11$ players draw all their games with each other ($10$ games, $5$ points each) and lose every game against the other $19$ players. (The other players then score at least $11$ each, so they do not score exactly $5$.) The answer is $\boxed{11}$.
