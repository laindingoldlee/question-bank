Number rows and columns so Cinderella’s colour is $C(i,j)=i+j\pmod3$. Any proper three-colouring D of a rectangular grid has an integer height lift H: across an edge choose the unique increment $+1$ or $-1$ matching the colour change modulo three. Around a unit square the four increments sum to an even multiple of three between -4 and four, hence zero; therefore the lift is path-independent. Set $F(i,j)=H(i,j)-i-j$. Along a step right or up, F changes by zero or -2, so it is nonincreasing, and all its values have the same parity. Agreement with Cinderella is exactly $F\equiv0\pmod3$. Follow the bottom and right boundary from the lower-left to upper-right corner. Every boundary value avoids zero modulo three. Such a sequence of zero or -2 changes can decrease at most once: two decreases would pass through all three residues. Thus its endpoint values differ by at most two. Monotonicity traps every interior F between those same corner values; parity permits only the endpoint values. Neither is zero modulo three, so no interior cell agrees either.
