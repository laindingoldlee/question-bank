Running in opposite directions, together they cover $\dfrac{1}{50}+\dfrac{1}{60}=\dfrac{11}{300}$ of a lap per second. They first meet when they have together covered half a lap, at $t=\dfrac{150}{11}\approx13.6$ s, and then every time they cover another full lap together, i.e. every $\dfrac{300}{11}\approx27.3$ s. The meetings in $600$ seconds are at $t=\dfrac{150}{11}+\dfrac{300}{11}k\le600$, i.e. $k\le\dfrac{6450}{300}=21.5$, so $k=0,\dots,21$: $\boxed{22}$ meetings, answer $\textbf{(c)}$.
