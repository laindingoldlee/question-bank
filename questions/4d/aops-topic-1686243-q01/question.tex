Let $c>|d|$. Simplify
\[
\dfrac{\sqrt[3]{c^{3}+3c^{2}d+3cd^{2}+d^{3}}}{\sqrt{c^{2}-d^{2}}\cdot\sqrt{c^{2}+d^{2}}}
\]
and choose the equal expression.

$\textbf{(A)}\ \dfrac{\sqrt{c^{4}+d^{4}}}{(c^{2}+d^{2})(c-d)}\qquad
\textbf{(B)}\ \dfrac{\sqrt{c^{2}+d^{2}}}{(c^{2}+d^{2})(c-d)}\qquad
\textbf{(C)}\ \dfrac{\sqrt{c-d}}{(c^{2}+d^{2})(c+d)}\qquad
\textbf{(D)}\ \dfrac{\sqrt{c^{4}-d^{4}}}{(c^{2}+d^{2})(c-d)}\qquad
\textbf{(E)}\ \dfrac{\sqrt{c^{2}-d^{2}}}{c^{2}+d^{2}}$
