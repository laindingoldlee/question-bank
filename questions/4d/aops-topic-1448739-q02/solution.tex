Using $xy=4$: $3xy=12$ and $12x^3y^5=12(xy)^3y^2=768y^2$. So the expression is $12+6x^2+768y^2$. By AM-GM,
\[
6x^2+768y^2\ge2\sqrt{6\cdot768\,x^2y^2}=2\sqrt{4608\cdot16}=2\cdot192\sqrt2=384\sqrt2,
\]
with equality when $6x^2=768y^2$, that is $x^2=128y^2$; together with $x^2y^2=16$ this gives $y^4=\dfrac{1}{8}$, which has real solutions (with $x=\dfrac{4}{y}$). The minimum is
\[
\boxed{12+384\sqrt2}\approx555.06 .
\]
