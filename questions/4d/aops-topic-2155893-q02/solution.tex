Apply the difference of squares twice:
\[x^4-81=\left(x^2-9\right)\left(x^2+9\right)=\boxed{(x-3)(x+3)\left(x^2+9\right)}.\]
The factor $x^2+9$ has no real roots, so it does not factor further over the integers.
