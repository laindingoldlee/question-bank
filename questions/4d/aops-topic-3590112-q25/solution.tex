From $(a-3)^2\ge0$, $(b-4)^2\ge0$, and $(c-5)^2\ge0$, we get $b\le\dfrac{4a}{3}$, $c\le\dfrac{5b}{4}$, and $a\le\dfrac{3c}{5}$. Multiplying gives $abc\le abc$. If any individual inequality were strict, the product would be strict, impossible. Thus all are equalities, forcing $a=3,b=4,c=5$. These satisfy the original equations, so the sum is $\boxed{12}$.
