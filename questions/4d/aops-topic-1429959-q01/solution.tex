First write $x$ as a cube by splitting each exponent into three equal parts:
\[
2^{15}\cdot3^6=\left(2^5\right)^3\cdot\left(3^2\right)^3=\left(2^5\cdot3^2\right)^3 .
\]
So the number is $2^5\cdot3^2=32\cdot9=\boxed{288}$. (In general, a product of prime powers is a perfect cube exactly when every exponent is a multiple of $3$, and its cube root is found by dividing each exponent by $3$.)
