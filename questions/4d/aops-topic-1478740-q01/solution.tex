Since $1'=\left(\dfrac{1}{60}\right)^\circ$, we have $40^\circ20'=\left(40+\dfrac{20}{60}\right)^\circ=\left(\dfrac{121}{3}\right)^\circ$. As $180^\circ=\pi$ radians,
\[
\dfrac{121}{3}\cdot\dfrac{\pi}{180}=\boxed{\dfrac{121\pi}{540}}\approx0.704\text{ radians}.
\]
