Joining the centre $O$ to the six vertices divides the hexagon into six triangles. Each has two sides equal to the radius $10$ and a central angle of $60^\circ$, so it is equilateral with side $10$. The area is
\[6\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}\cdot10^2=\boxed{150\sqrt3}\ \text{cm}^2.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw(circle((0,0),10));
pair[] V;for(int k=0;k<6;++k)V.push(10*dir(60*k));
draw(V[0]--V[1]--V[2]--V[3]--V[4]--V[5]--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
for(int k=0;k<6;++k)draw((0,0)--V[k],dashed);
dot((0,0));label("$O$",(0,0),S);
label("$10$",V[0]/2,S);label("$10$",(V[0]+V[1])/2,E);
\end{asy}
