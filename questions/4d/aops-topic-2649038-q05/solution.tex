From the centre the diagonal neighbours are the four corners, and from a corner the only diagonal neighbour is the centre. So the caterpillar is at a corner (an outer square) after an odd number of days and at the centre after an even number. Of the integers $1$ to $99$, $50$ are odd, so the probability is $\dfrac{50}{99}$ and $b-a=\boxed{49}$.
