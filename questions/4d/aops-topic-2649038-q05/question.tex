A caterpillar starts in the centre square of a $3\times3$ grid. Each day it moves to a randomly chosen square that is diagonally adjacent to its current square. The number of days it does this is chosen uniformly at random from the integers $1$ to $99$. The probability that after the last day the caterpillar is on one of the outer $8$ squares is $\dfrac ab$ in lowest terms. Find $b-a$.
