Factor by grouping:
\[
x^3+x^2+x+1=x^2(x+1)+(x+1)=(x+1)(x^2+1).
\]
So $x+1=0$ or $x^2=-1$, and the solutions are $\boxed{x=-1,\ x=i,\ x=-i}$. The only real solution is $x=-1$. (Another view: multiplying by $x-1$ gives $x^4-1=0$, so the roots are the fourth roots of unity other than $1$.)
