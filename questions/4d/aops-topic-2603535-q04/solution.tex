The area is length times breadth, and only one factor is multiplied by $1.3$. So the area is multiplied by $1.3$, an increase of $\boxed{30\%}$. (If both sides were increased by $30\%$, the area would be multiplied by $1.3^2=1.69$, an increase of $69\%$.)
