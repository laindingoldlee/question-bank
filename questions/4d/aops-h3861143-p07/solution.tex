The four triangles formed by the diagonals have areas in the ratio $[ABX]:[BCX]:[CDX]:[DAX]=1:2:4:2$, from the base ratio $AB:CD=1:2$ and the corresponding altitude ratios. Since $[BCX]=1$, their areas are $\dfrac{1}{2},1,2,1$. The pentagon $ABCDX$ omits triangle $DAX$, leaving area $\boxed{\dfrac{7}{2}}$.
