The chord has length $BC=32\sin120^\circ=16\sqrt3$. Put $B=(-8\sqrt3,0),C=(8\sqrt3,0)$ and the large center at $(0,8)$. Since $\angle BAC=120^\circ$, $A$ lies on the minor arc below the chord. The small center is thirteen-sixteenths of the way from the large center to $A$, and its vertical coordinate is $-3$ by tangency to $BC$. Thus $y_A=-\dfrac{72}{13}$ and $x_A=\pm\dfrac{64\sqrt3}{13}$. Area comparison gives $AB\cdot AC=\dfrac{2304}{13}$, and the distance formula gives $AB^2+AC^2=\dfrac{7680}{13}$. Hence $(AB+AC)^2=\dfrac{12288}{13}$ and $AB+AC=\dfrac{64\sqrt{39}}{13}$. Therefore $\boxed{a+b+c=116}$.
