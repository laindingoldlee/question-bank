Two circles $\omega_1$ and $\omega_2$ have radii $16$ and $3$, respectively. are internally tangent at point $A$. Let $B$ and $C$ be points of $\omega_1$ such that $BC$ is tangent to $\omega_2$ and $\angle BAC=120^{\circ}$. The value of $AB+AC$ can be expressed as $\dfrac{a\sqrt{b}}{c}$ where $a$ and $c$ are relatively prime positive integers and $b$ is square-free. Find $a+b+c$.
