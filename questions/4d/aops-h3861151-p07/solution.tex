If $20,25$ were both legs, the area would be $250$, not $150$. Therefore $25$ is the hypotenuse, and the remaining leg is $\sqrt{25^2-20^2}=\boxed{15}$. Its area with leg $20$ is indeed $150$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(220);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(20,0),C=(0,15); draw(A--B--C--cycle); label("$A$",A,(-1,-1)); label("$B$",B,(1,-1)); label("$C$",C,(0,1)); label("$20$",(A+B)/2,(0,-1));label("$15$",(A+C)/2,(-1,0));label("$25$",(B+C)/2,(1,1));
\end{asy}
