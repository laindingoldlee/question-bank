We need $3n+4\equiv0\pmod5$, i.e. $3n\equiv1\pmod5$. Multiplying by $2$ (the inverse of $3$ mod $5$), $n\equiv2\pmod5$. So the answer is $\boxed{n=5k+2,\ k\in\mathbb Z}$.
