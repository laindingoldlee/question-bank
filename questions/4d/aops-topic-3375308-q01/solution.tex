Write the integers as $n,n+1$, with $n\ge1$. The difference is $(n+1)^2-n^2=2n+1$, equal to their sum. Thus $1\le n\le49$, giving all odd integers $3,5,\ldots,99$. In particular $n=39$ gives $40^2-39^2=\boxed{79}$.
