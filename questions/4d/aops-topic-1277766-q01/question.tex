For a two-digit number $\overline{ab}$ define $f(\overline{ab})=\overline{ab}+a+b$, and let $g(x)=f(x)+\operatorname{rev}f(x)$, where $\operatorname{rev}$ reverses the digits. Show that $g(42)=g(69)$, and explain why this does not imply $42=69$.
