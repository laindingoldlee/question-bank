The first player can be paired with any of the other $5$. Of the remaining four, the first can be paired with any of the other $3$, and the last two form the final pair. So there are $5\cdot3\cdot1=\boxed{15}$ pairings. (Equivalently, $\dfrac{6!}{2^3\cdot3!}=15$.)
