The phrase means that some two neighbouring letters are equal. Count the complement. There are $26^4=456976$ words in all. A word with no two adjacent letters equal has $26$ choices for the first letter and $25$ choices for each later letter (anything except the letter just before it), giving $26\cdot25^3=406250$ words. Hence the number of words with at least one pair of equal adjacent letters is
\[456976-406250=\boxed{50726}.\]
