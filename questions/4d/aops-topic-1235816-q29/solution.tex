The total time is
\[\dfrac{n}{n+1}+\dfrac{2n+1}{n(n+1)}=\dfrac{n^2+2n+1}{n(n+1)}=\dfrac{n+1}{n}=6,\]
so $n=\dfrac{1}{5}$. The distance is $n+2n+1=3n+1=\dfrac{8}{5}$ miles, and the average speed is $\dfrac{8}{5}\div6=\boxed{\dfrac{4}{15}}$ mph.
