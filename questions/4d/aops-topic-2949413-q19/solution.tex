Since $2019!<10000^{2019}=10^{8076}$, its digit sum satisfies $0<a_2\le9\cdot8076=72684$. Thus $a_2$ has at most five digits and $0<a_3\le45$. Every term is divisible by 9 because digit sum preserves residue modulo 9 and $9\mid2019!$. The given parity forces $a_3=18$ or 36. Hence $a_4=9$ and $a_5=9$. No earlier equality is possible: a positive integer equals its digit sum only if it has one digit, whereas $a_3$ has two, and $a_1$ is larger still. Therefore $\boxed{n=5}$.
