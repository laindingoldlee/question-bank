Write $n=2^a m$ with $a\ge0$ and $m$ odd. Every positive factor of $n$ has the form $2^i d$ with $0\le i\le a$ and $d$ a factor of $m$.

\begin{itemize}
\item The odd factors are those with $i=0$; there are $\tau(m)$ of them, where $\tau(m)$ is the number of positive factors of $m$.
\item The even factors are those with $1\le i\le a$; there are $a\,\tau(m)$ of them.
\end{itemize}
Since $\tau(m)\ge1$, the two counts are equal exactly when $a=1$, that is, when $n$ is even but not divisible by $4$, i.e. $n\equiv2\pmod4$.

The numbers $n\equiv2\pmod 4$ with $100<n<1000$ are $102,106,\dots,998$, which is $4k+2$ for $k=25,26,\dots,249$. There are $249-25+1=\boxed{225}$ of them.
