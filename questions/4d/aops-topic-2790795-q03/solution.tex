All three sides of $ AOB $ have length 1, so it is equilateral. Therefore $\boxed{\angle AOB=60^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair O=(0,0),A=dir(0),B=dir(60);draw(circle(O,1));draw(O--A--B--cycle);label("$O$",O,SW);label("$A$",A,E);label("$B$",B,N);
\end{asy}
