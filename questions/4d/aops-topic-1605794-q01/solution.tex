$4=2^2$ and $18=2\cdot3^2$. The greatest common factor takes the lower power of each common prime, $\gcd(4,18)=2$; the least common multiple takes the higher powers, $\operatorname{lcm}(4,18)=2^2\cdot3^2=36$. The sum is $2+36=\boxed{38}$.
