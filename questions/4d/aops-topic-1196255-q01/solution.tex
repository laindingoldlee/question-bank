Let $h_A,t_A$ and $h_B,t_B$ be the numbers of heads and tails of the two coins. Since $h_A+t_A=9000$ and $h_B+t_B=9001$, we cannot have both $h_B\le h_A$ and $t_B\le t_A$ (adding would give $9001\le9000$), and we cannot have both $h_B>h_A$ and $t_B>t_A$ (adding would give $9001\ge9002$). So exactly one of the events ``$B$ has more heads than $A$'' and ``$B$ has more tails than $A$'' occurs. Swapping the roles of heads and tails on every flip (which does not change the probabilities, as the coins are fair) exchanges these two events, so they have equal probability, so each is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$.
