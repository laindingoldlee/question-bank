At the end of night $n$ it is $3n$ feet up, so during day $n+1$ it reaches $3n+5$ feet. It reaches the top on day $n+1$ when $3n+5\ge40$, i.e. $n\ge\dfrac{35}{3}$, so $n=12$. After $12$ days and nights it is at $36$ feet, and on day $\boxed{13}$ it climbs to $41\ge40$. (On day $12$ it only reaches $38$ feet.)
