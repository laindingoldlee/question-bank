Triangles $ABD,ACD$ have equal area because $BD=DC$. For $P\in AD\setminus\{A\}$, replacing $D$ by $P$ scales both areas by the same positive factor $\dfrac{AP}{AD}$. Hence the ratio is always 1, and the requested difference is $\boxed0$.

pair A=(2,4),B=(0,0),C=(6,0),D=(3,0),P=(2.5,2);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--D);draw(B--P--C);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,S);label("$P$",P,E);
\end{asy}
