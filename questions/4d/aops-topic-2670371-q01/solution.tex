Let the break points be at $x$ and $y$. By symmetry we may assume $x<y$; the outcomes then fill the triangle $0<x<y<8$ of area $32$. The pieces $x$, $y-x$, $8-y$ form a non-degenerate triangle exactly when each is less than half the total length $4$ (the triangle inequality, since each piece must be shorter than the sum $8$ minus itself):
\[x<4,\qquad y-x<4,\qquad y>4.\]
This is the shaded triangle with vertices $(0,4)$, $(4,4)$, $(4,8)$, of area $8$. The probability is $\dfrac{8}{32}=\boxed{\dfrac14}$.
