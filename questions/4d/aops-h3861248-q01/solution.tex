For any three numbers $u,v,w$ with sum $S$, Newton's identity gives
\[
6uvw=S^3-3S(u^2+v^2+w^2)+2(u^3+v^3+w^3).
\]
Apply this to every row and sum. The result depends only on $S$, the sum of the squares of all nine entries, and the sum of their cubes. Applying it to every column gives exactly the same expression. Therefore the sum of the three row products equals the sum of the three column products.

The double sum $K$ is three times the difference of these sums, hence always zero. Thus both extrema are zero, $\Delta=0$, and $\boxed{2025\Delta=0}$.
