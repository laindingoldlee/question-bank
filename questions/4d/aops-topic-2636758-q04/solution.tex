Let $u=x^2-9x$. Then $u^2=x^4-18x^3+81x^2$ and
\[x^4-18x^3+119x^2-342x+360=u^2+38u+360=(u+18)(u+20).\]
So the polynomial is $\left(x^2-9x+18\right)\left(x^2-9x+20\right)=(x-3)(x-6)(x-4)(x-5)$, and the roots are $\boxed{3,\ 4,\ 5,\ 6}$.
