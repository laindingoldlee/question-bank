Rays $\overrightarrow{AB}$ and $\overrightarrow{AC}$ form a $30^\circ$ angle, and a circle with centre $O$ is tangent to the two rays at $B$ and $C$. A segment $PQ$, parallel to $BC$ and shorter than $BC$, is tangent to the circle and meets the rays at $P$ (on $\overrightarrow{AB}$) and $Q$ (on $\overrightarrow{AC}$). If $AP=1$, find (a) the radius of the circle, and (b) the length $BC$.
