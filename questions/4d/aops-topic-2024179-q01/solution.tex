Colour the grid like a chessboard, with the four corners black; there are $13$ black and $12$ white cells, and every pair of neighbours consists of one black and one white cell.

Neighbouring numbers differ by $1$, so they have opposite parity. Any two black cells are joined by a path in the grid with an even number of steps, so all black cells have the same parity, and all white cells have the other parity. The only odd number available is $1$. Hence either
\begin{itemize}
\item every black cell is $1$ and every white cell is $0$ or $2$, or
\item every white cell is $1$ and every black cell is $0$ or $2$.
\end{itemize}
Conversely, every such filling works: each pair of neighbours is a $1$ next to a $0$ or a $2$. The two families do not overlap. So the number of arrangements is
\[
2^{12}+2^{13}=4096+8192=\boxed{12288}.
\]
