Let $A=(0,0)$, $B=(w,0)$, $C=(w,h)$, $D=(0,h)$ form a rectangle. A circular arc from $A$ to $C$ lies inside it and is strictly increasing in both coordinates. A horizontal line meets $AD,BC$, and the arc at $Q,P,R$, respectively. Consider the curvilinear region bounded by $AQ,QR$, and arc $RA$, together with the region bounded by $CP,PR$, and arc $RC$. Construct the horizontal line that minimises their total area.
