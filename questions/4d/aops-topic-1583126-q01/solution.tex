\textbf{(a)} The squares $1,4,9,16,25,36,49,64,81$ have units digits $1,4,9,6,5,6,9,4,1$, and $1+4+9+6+5+6+9+4+1=45$.

\textbf{(b)} The units digit of $n^2$ depends only on the units digit of $n$. So in every block of ten consecutive integers $10k+1,\dots,10k+10$ the units digits of the squares are $1,4,9,6,5,6,9,4,1,0$, with sum $45$.

The numbers $1,\dots,2010$ form $201$ such blocks, and $2011,\dots,2018$ end in $1,\dots,8$, contributing $1+4+9+6+5+6+9+4=44$. The total is
\[
201\cdot45+44=9045+44=\boxed{9089}.
\]
