The sum of the integers from $1$ to $9$ is $45$, and the units digits of $1^2,2^2,\dots,9^2$ also add up to $45$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Verify the second fact.
\item[(b)] Find the sum of the units digits of $1^2,2^2,3^2,\dots,2018^2$.
\end{enumerate}
