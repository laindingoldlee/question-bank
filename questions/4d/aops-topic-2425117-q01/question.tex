A box in the shape of a rectangular prism measuring $1\times2\times3$ inches is coated with a layer of titanium $2$ inches thick. The coated solid consists of all points within $2$ inches of the box, so the coating has flat faces, rounded edges and rounded corners. What is the volume of the titanium?
