Let $a$, $b$, $c$ be real numbers with $ab+bc+ca\ne0$. Find a necessary and sufficient condition on $a$, $b$, $c$ for the equation
\[
\sin x=\dfrac{a^2+b^2+c^2}{ab+bc+ca}
\]
to have a real solution $x$.
