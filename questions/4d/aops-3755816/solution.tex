Multiply by $81!$ and apply the multinomial theorem to $(1+1+1)^{81}$. The sum is $\dfrac{3^{81}}{81!}$. The exponent of $3$ in $81!$ is $\lfloor \dfrac{81}{3} \rfloor + \lfloor \dfrac{81}{9} \rfloor + \lfloor \dfrac{81}{27} \rfloor + \lfloor \dfrac{81}{81} \rfloor = 27 + 9 + 3 + 1 = 40$. After cancellation the remaining exponent is $\boxed{k=41}$.
