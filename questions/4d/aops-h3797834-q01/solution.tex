Write the ordered integer abscissas as $a,a+u,a+u+v$ with $u,v>0$. The determinant area formula gives $\dfrac{uv(u+v)}{2}=24$. By reflection we may take $u\le v$, so $2u^3\le48$ and $u=1$ or $2$. For $u=1$, $v(v+1)=48$ has no integer solution. For $u=2$, $v(v+2)=24$ gives $v=4$. The sum of ordinates is
\[a^2+(a+2)^2+(a+6)^2=3a^2+16a+40=3\left(a+\dfrac{8}{3}\right)^2+\dfrac{56}{3}.\]
Its integer minimum occurs at $a=-3$ and equals $\boxed{19}$. Points $(-3,9),(-1,1),(3,9)$ attain it; reflecting them gives the other minimizing triple.
