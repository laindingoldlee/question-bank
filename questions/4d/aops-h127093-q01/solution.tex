The other vertices are $B=(v,u)$, $C=(-v,u)$, $D=(-v,-u)$, $E=(v,-u)$. The rectangle $BCDE$ has area $4uv$. Triangle $ABE$ has vertical base $BE=2u$ and horizontal height $u-v$, so its area is $u(u-v)$. The pentagon is their union, giving
\[
451=u^2+3uv=u(u+3v).
\]
Now $451=11\cdot41$. Since $u+3v>u>v>0$, the possible ordered factor pairs are $(1,451)$ and $(11,41)$. The first gives $v=150$, violating $v<u$. The second gives $u=11,v=10$. Thus $\boxed{u+v=21}$.

\end{asy}
