Let $P(x)=a_n(x-r_1)(x-r_2)\cdots(x-r_n)$ with $a_n\ne0$, and let $S_k$ be the $k$th elementary symmetric sum of the roots (so $S_1=\sum r_i$, $S_2=\sum_{i<j}r_ir_j$, \dots, $S_n=r_1\cdots r_n$). Express $S_1+S_2+\cdots+S_n$ in terms of $P$. Then compute it for $P(x)=x^3-6x^2+11x-6$.
