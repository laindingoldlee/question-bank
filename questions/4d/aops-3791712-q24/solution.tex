Write $C=\theta$, so $A=180^\circ-3\theta$. By the sine rule, $\dfrac{13}{5} = \dfrac{\sin 3 \theta}{\sin \theta} = 4 \cos^2 \theta - 1$. Thus $\cos^2 \theta = \dfrac{9}{10}$, so $\sin 2 \theta = \dfrac{3}{5}$. The area is $(\dfrac{1}{2}) A B \cdot B C \sin B = (\dfrac{1}{2}) 5 \cdot 13 \cdot \dfrac{3}{5} = \boxed{\dfrac{39}{2}}$.
