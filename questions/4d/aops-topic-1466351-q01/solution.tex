Let $A_i$ be the event that card $i$ is in spot $i$. For any $k$ of the cards, the probability that all $k$ are in their own spots is $\dfrac{(13-k)!}{13!}$, since the other $13-k$ cards can then be arranged in any way. By inclusion--exclusion,
\[
P(A_1\cup\cdots\cup A_{13})=\sum_{k=1}^{13}(-1)^{k+1}\dbinom{13}{k}\dfrac{(13-k)!}{13!}=\sum_{k=1}^{13}\dfrac{(-1)^{k+1}}{k!}
=1-\dfrac{1}{2!}+\dfrac{1}{3!}-\cdots+\dfrac{1}{13!}.
\]
Equivalently, the number of arrangements with no card in its own spot is the derangement number $D_{13}=13!\sum_{k=0}^{13}\dfrac{(-1)^k}{k!}=2290792932$, which can also be computed from $D_1=0$, $D_2=1$, $D_n=(n-1)(D_{n-1}+D_{n-2})$. So the probability is
\[
1-\dfrac{D_{13}}{13!}=1-\dfrac{2290792932}{6227020800}=\dfrac{3936227868}{6227020800}=\boxed{\dfrac{109339663}{172972800}}\approx0.632 .
\]
The value is extremely close to $1-\dfrac{1}{e}$, because the alternating series converges very quickly.
