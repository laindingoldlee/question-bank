The arithmetic mean gives $x+y=19$. The harmonic mean is $\dfrac{2xy}{x+y}=16$, so $xy=8\cdot19=152$. Then $x$ and $y$ are the roots of
\[
t^2-19t+152=0,
\]
whose discriminant is $19^2-4\cdot152=361-608=-247<0$. So there are \emph{no} real numbers $x,y$ with these means (the only solutions are the complex numbers $\dfrac{19\pm i\sqrt{247}}{2}$).

This also follows from the HM--AM inequality: for positive $x,y$,
\[
\dfrac{x+y}{2}-\dfrac{2xy}{x+y}=\dfrac{(x-y)^2}{2(x+y)}\ge0,
\]
so the harmonic mean can never exceed the arithmetic mean, while here $16>9.5$. (If $x+y=19>0$ and $xy=152>0$, both numbers would have to be positive.) $\blacksquare$
