After a $40\%$ discount the price is $60\%$ of the original price $p$, so $0.6p=375$ and $p=\dfrac{375}{0.6}=625$. The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(C)}\ \$625}$. (Check: $40\%$ of $625$ is $250$, and $625-250=375$.)
