She paid $x^2$ dollars. Since her change was less than $\$10<\$20$, she handed over the least number of $\$20$ bills needed, so the change is the amount needed to bring $x^2$ up to the next multiple of $20$. Its value depends only on $x^2\bmod 20$. Working modulo $20$, as $x$ runs through $0,1,\dots,19$,
\[
x^2\bmod20\in\{0,1,4,9,16,5\}.
\]
The corresponding change is $0$, $19$, $16$, $11$, $4$, $15$ dollars. She did receive change, so the change is positive, and it is less than $10$; the only possibility is $x^2\equiv16\pmod{20}$ and the change is $\boxed{\$4}$. (For example, $x=4$: $16$ dollars, paid with one $\$20$ bill.)
