The condition $ 10k\equiv1\pmod{109}$ is sufficient, since $ 10(a+kd)\equiv10a+d\pmod{109}$. It is necessary: take $ d=1 $ and a nonnegative integer $ a\equiv-k\pmod{109}$; the implication then forces $-10k+1\equiv0 $. Because $ 10\cdot11=110\equiv1 $, all valid $ k $ are congruent to 11 modulo 109, and the least is $\boxed{11}$.
