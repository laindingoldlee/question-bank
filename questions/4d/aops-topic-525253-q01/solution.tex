Let $\ell_1$ and $\ell_2$ be the lines $2x+5y=10$ and $3x+4y=12$. The allowed region is bounded below by the broken line through $(0,3)$, the intersection point of the two lines, and $(5,0)$. Solving $2x+5y=10$, $3x+4y=12$ gives the corner $P=\left(\dfrac{20}{7},\dfrac{6}{7}\right)$.

label("$\ell_1$",(0,2),W); label("$\ell_2$",(4,0),S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline

A linear function on such a region takes its least value at a corner (its level lines are slanted between the slopes of the two boundary lines: $-\dfrac{8}{13}$ lies between $-\dfrac{2}{5}$ and $-\dfrac{3}{4}$). At $\left(\dfrac{20}{7},\dfrac{6}{7}\right)$ the value is $\dfrac{160+78}{7}=34$; near the axes it is larger (at $(0,3)$ it is $39$, at $(5,0)$ it is $40$). So the least value is $\boxed{34}$.
