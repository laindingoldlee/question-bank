A round bracket means the endpoint is excluded and a square bracket that it is included; $\cup$ joins two pieces, and $\pm\infty$ always get round brackets.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $(1,2)$ \quad (the same set as (b), written in the other direction)
\item[(b)] $(1,2)$
\item[(c)] $[1,2)$
\item[(d)] $[1,2)$
\item[(e)] $[1,2]$
\item[(f)] No real number is both at least $2$ and at most $1$, so the set is empty: $\varnothing$.
\item[(g)] $(-\infty,-2)\cup(1,\infty)$
\item[(h)] $(-\infty,-2]\cup[1,\infty)$
\item[(i)] $(-\infty,-2)\cup[1,\infty)$
\item[(j)] $(-\infty,-2]\cup(1,\infty)$
\end{enumerate}
