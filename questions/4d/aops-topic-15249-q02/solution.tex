Note that $f(x)=f(x-1)\bigl(f(x-2)-1\bigr)$. Then
\begin{align*}
f(3)&=5(3-1)=10,\\
f(4)&=10(5-1)=40,\\
f(5)&=40(10-1)=360,\\
f(6)&=360(40-1)=\boxed{14040}.
\end{align*}
