The vertices are the pairwise intersections of the lines: $y=0$ and $y=x$ meet at $(0,0)$, $y=0$ and $x=8$ meet at $(8,0)$, and $x=8$ and $y=x$ meet at $(8,8)$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
xaxis(-1,10,Arrow(TeXHead)); yaxis(-1,10,Arrow(TeXHead));
fill((0,0)--(8,0)--(8,8)--cycle,paleblue);
draw((-0.8,-0.8)--(9.5,9.5),blue); draw((8,-1)--(8,9.8),red);
draw((0,0)--(8,0)--(8,8)--cycle,linewidth(1));
dot((0,0)); dot((8,0)); dot((8,8));
label("$(0,0)$",(0,0),SE); label("$(8,0)$",(8,0),SE); label("$(8,8)$",(8,8),E);
label("$y=x$",(9.5,9.5),SE,blue); label("$x=8$",(8,9.8),W,red);
label("$8$",(4,0),S); label("$8$",(8,4),E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

This is a right triangle with legs $8$ (along the $x$-axis) and $8$ (along $x=8$), so its area is
\[\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot8\cdot8=\boxed{32}.\]
