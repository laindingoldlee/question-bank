$\gcd(n,36)=2$ means $2\mid n$, $4\nmid n$ and $3\nmid n$. Write $n=2m$ with $m$ odd and $3\nmid m$, where $50\le m\le499$.

Odd $m$ in $[50,499]$: $51,53,\dots,499$, i.e. $225$ numbers. Odd multiples of $3$ among them are $3j$ with $j$ odd and $17\le j\le166$: $j=17,19,\dots,165$, i.e. $75$ numbers. The count is $225-75=\boxed{150}$.
