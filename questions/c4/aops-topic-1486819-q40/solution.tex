Adding $p$ purple marbles leaves $40$ green out of $60+p$ marbles. We need
\[
\dfrac{40}{60+p}=\dfrac{5}{12}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 60+p=\dfrac{40\cdot12}{5}=96 ,
\]
so $p=\boxed{36}$.
