Add $1$ to both sides and factor:
\[
xy+x+y+1=(x+1)(y+1)=35 .
\]
Since $x,y\ge1$, both factors are at least $2$, so $\{x+1,y+1\}=\{5,7\}$ and $\{x,y\}=\{4,6\}$. Hence $x+y=\boxed{10}$. (Check: $4+6+24=34$.)
