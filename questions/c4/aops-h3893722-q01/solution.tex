A product fails to be divisible by 6 only if it has no factor 2 or no factor 3. There are 50 odd cards and $100-\left\lfloor\dfrac{100}{3}\right\rfloor=67$ cards not divisible by 3. Thus 68 cards include both an even card and a multiple of 3. Drawing the 67 cards not divisible by 3 shows that 67 is insufficient. The answer is $\boxed{68}$.
