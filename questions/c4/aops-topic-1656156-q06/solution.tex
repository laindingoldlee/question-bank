The distance from $\dfrac{1}{20}$ to $\dfrac{1}{18}$ is $\dfrac{1}{18}-\dfrac{1}{20}=\dfrac{10-9}{180}=\dfrac{1}{180}$. One third of the way along is
\[
\dfrac{1}{20}+\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{1}{180}=\dfrac{27}{540}+\dfrac{1}{540}=\dfrac{28}{540}=\boxed{\textbf{(a)}\ \dfrac{7}{135}} .
\]
