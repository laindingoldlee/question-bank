Every point in the disk is at most four units from each defender, so the wide-open probability, under the stated fourteen-unit rule, is zero. Adjacent unit defender disks overlap in lenses of area $2\cdot\dfrac{\pi}{4}-1=\dfrac{\pi}{2}-1$. The four lenses are disjoint apart from boundary points, and opposite defender disks only touch. Thus the heavily-contested area is $2\pi-4$, giving the requested difference $\boxed{\dfrac{4-2\pi}{9\pi}}$.
