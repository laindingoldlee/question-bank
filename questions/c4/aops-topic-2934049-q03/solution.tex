Let $O$ be the centre. The two quadrilaterals lie on the indicated sides of diameters $ZI$ and $MG$, and their intersection is triangle $IMO$. Joining the centre to all six vertices partitions the hexagon into six congruent equilateral triangles, one of which is $IMO$. The fraction is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{6}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair[] p;path q;for(int k=0;k<6;++k){p.push(2*dir(60*k));q=q--p[k];}draw(q--cycle);draw(p[0]--p[3]^^p[2]--p[5]);filldraw(p[2]--p[3]--(0,0)--cycle,lightgray);string[] names={"Z","U","M","I","N","G"};for(int k=0;k<6;++k)label("$"+names[k]+"$",p[k],dir(60*k));label("$O$",(0,0),SE);
\end{asy}
