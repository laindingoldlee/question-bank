Eight unit cubes are stacked to form a $2\times2\times2$ cube, and $A$ and $B$ are opposite vertices of the large cube. A \emph{short path} from $A$ to $B$ consists of six one-unit moves, each to the right, up or back, along edges of the unit cubes that lie on the surface of the large cube.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] How many short paths are there?
\item[(b)] A student argues: ``We need two moves in each of the three directions, so there are $\dbinom62\dbinom42=90$ paths.'' What does $90$ count, and why is it too large?
\end{enumerate}
