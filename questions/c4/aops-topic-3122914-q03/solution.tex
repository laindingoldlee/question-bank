After $n$ assignments the mean is $\dfrac{90\cdot50+45n\cdot100}{90+45n}=100-\dfrac{100}{n+2}$. This is less than $100$ for every finite $n$. It is at least $90$ exactly when $n+2\ge10$, so $\boxed8$ assignments suffice and are necessary. The answer to the first question is $\boxed{\text{no}}$.
