One root of the equation $x^2-px+q=0$ is $m$ times the other, where $m$ is real and the roots are nonzero. Then $\dfrac{m}{1+m^2}$ is equal to:

$\textbf{(A) }\dfrac{q}{p^2-2q}\qquad\textbf{(B) }\dfrac{p}{q^2-2p}\qquad\textbf{(C) }\dfrac{q}{q^2-2p}\qquad\textbf{(D) }\text{none of these}$
