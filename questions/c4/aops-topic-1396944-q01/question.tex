Dana needs $300$ pickets for her colourful picket fence, with equal numbers of each of her $4$ colours: red, green, yellow and blue. She already has $32$ red, $26$ green, $9$ yellow and no blue pickets.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] How many more pickets of each colour does Dana need?
\item[(b)] Pickets cost $25$ cents each. If she buys $50$ or more of one colour she gets $20\%$ off those pickets, and if she buys $60$ or more of one colour she gets $30\%$ off them instead. How much does Dana spend if she buys exactly the pickets she needs?
\item[(c)] What is the least Dana could spend if she is allowed to buy extra pickets?
\end{enumerate}
