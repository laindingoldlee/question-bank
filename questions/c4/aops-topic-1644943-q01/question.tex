Let $f$ be a differentiable function from the positive real numbers to the real numbers, with $f(y)\ne0$ for all $y>0$, such that
\[
f\!\left(\dfrac {x}{y}\right)=\dfrac{f(x)}{f(y)}\qquad\text{for all }x,y>0,
\]
and $f'(1)=1$. Find $f$.
