Write $3^{107}=3\cdot9^{53}=3(10-1)^{53}$. Modulo $1000$ only the terms of $(10-1)^{53}$ with $10^0$, $10^1$ and $10^2$ matter:
\[(10-1)^{53}\equiv-1+\dbinom{53}{1}10-\dbinom{53}{2}100=-1+530-137800\equiv-1+530-800=-271\equiv729\pmod{1000}.\]
Hence $3^{107}\equiv3\cdot729=2187\equiv187\pmod{1000}$, and the last three digits are $\boxed{187}$.
