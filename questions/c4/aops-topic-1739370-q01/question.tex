Three red beads, five blue beads, four green beads and three white beads are put on a necklace so that all beads of the same colour are next to each other. Beads of the same colour are identical, and two necklaces are the same if one can be turned into the other by rotating it or flipping it over. How many different necklaces are there?

A student says the answer is $6$, the circular arrangements of $4$ objects; another says $24$, because rotations should not be counted. Who, if anyone, is right?
