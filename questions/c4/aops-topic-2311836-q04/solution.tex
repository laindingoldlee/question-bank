The $6$-digit integers are $100000,\dots,999999$. The smallest multiple of $4$ among them is $100000=4\cdot25000$ and the largest is $999996=4\cdot249999$. So the multiples of $4$ are $4k$ for $k=25000,\dots,249999$, and there are
\[249999-25000+1=\boxed{225000}\]
of them (one quarter of the $900000$ six-digit integers).
