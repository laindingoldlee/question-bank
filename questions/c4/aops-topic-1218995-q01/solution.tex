This is the classical eight queens problem. Since each row has exactly one queen, a placement is a permutation $(c_1,\dots,c_8)$ of columns with $|c_i-c_j|\ne|i-j|$ for $i\ne j$. A systematic backtracking search (row by row, trying columns not attacked by earlier queens) finds exactly $\boxed{92}$ solutions; they fall into $12$ classes under the symmetries of the board ($11$ classes of size $8$ and one of size $4$: $88+4=92$).
