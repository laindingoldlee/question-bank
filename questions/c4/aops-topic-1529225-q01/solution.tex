\textbf{A team wins $16$ to $k$.} Here $0\le k\le14$, the match lasts $16+k$ rounds, and the last round is won by the winner. The first $15+k$ rounds contain exactly $15$ wins of the winner, so there are $\dbinom{15+k}{15}$ sequences. Summing over $k$ with the hockey-stick identity,
\[
\sum_{k=0}^{14}\dbinom{15+k}{15}=\dbinom{15}{15}+\dbinom{16}{15}+\cdots+\dbinom{29}{15}=\dbinom{30}{16}=145422675 ,
\]
and either team can be the winner.

\textbf{A tie.} All $30$ rounds are played with $15$ wins each: $\dbinom{30}{15}=155117520$ sequences.

The total is
\[
2\dbinom{30}{16}+\dbinom{30}{15}=290845350+155117520=\boxed{445962870}.
\]
(If instead only the final score is counted, there are $2\cdot15+1=31$ possible results: $16$-$k$ for either team with $0\le k\le14$, and $15$-$15$.)
