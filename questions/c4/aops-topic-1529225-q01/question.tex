Two teams play a match made of rounds; each round is won by exactly one of the teams. The first team to win $16$ rounds wins the match. At most $30$ rounds are played: if each team has won $15$ rounds after $30$ rounds, the match ends in a tie. Two matches are considered different if the sequences of round winners are different. How many different matches are possible?
