The letters are $M,M,M,A,A,U,H,D$. First arrange $A,A,U,H,D$: $\dfrac{5!}{2!}=60$ ways, of which $4!=24$ have the two $A$'s together and $36$ do not. Then place the three $M$'s in different gaps among the $6$ gaps (including the ends).
\begin{itemize}
\item $A$'s apart: any $3$ of the $6$ gaps: $36\cdot\dbinom63=720$.
\item $A$'s together: one $M$ must go between them, and $2$ more in the other $5$ gaps: $24\cdot\dbinom52=240$.
\end{itemize}
Total: $\boxed{960}$.
