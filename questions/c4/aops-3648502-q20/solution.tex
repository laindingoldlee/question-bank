The two fixed centres differ horizontally by 27 and vertically by 8, so their separation is $L=\sqrt{793}$. The intermediate centre of radius $r$ lies at height $r$, and collinearity puts its distances from the fixed centres at $\dfrac{L(r-2)}{8}$ and $\dfrac{L(10-r)}{8}$. Nonoverlap requires these to be at least $r+2$ and $r+10$. Thus $r\ge\dfrac{2(L+8)}{(L-8)}$ and $r\le\dfrac{10(L-8)}{(L+8)}$. Both endpoints are feasible since the lower bound is less than the upper. Their product is $\boxed{20}$.
