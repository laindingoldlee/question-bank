Let $S$ be the sum. Then $\dfrac{1}{2}S=\dfrac{1}{2^2}+\cdots+\dfrac{1}{2^{11}}$, and subtracting,
\[S-\dfrac{1}{2}S=\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{2^{11}},\qquad\text{so}\qquad S=1-\dfrac{1}{2^{10}}=\boxed{\dfrac{1023}{1024}}.\]
The posted attempt made a sign error: subtracting the shifted series leaves $\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{2^{11}}$, not $\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{2^{11}}$. (A sum of positive terms each at most $\dfrac{1}{2}$ of the remaining gap to $1$ can never exceed $1$.)
