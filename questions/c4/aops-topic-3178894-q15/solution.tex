Count by first coordinate. For $x=0$ there are 7 values of $y$. For each of $x=\pm1,\pm2$ there are 5. For each of $x=\pm3$ there is 1. Thus $7+4\cdot5+2=\boxed{29}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw(circle((0,0),3));draw((-3.5,0)--(3.5,0),Arrow);draw((0,-3.5)--(0,3.5),Arrow);for(int x=-3;x<=3;++x)for(int y=-3;y<=3;++y)if(x*x+y*y<=9)dot((x,y));label("$O$",(0,0),SW);label("$x$",(3.5,0),E);label("$y$",(0,3.5),N);label("radius 3",(2.4,2.6));
\end{asy}
