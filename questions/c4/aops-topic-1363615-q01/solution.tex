Let $x$ ounces of the $20\%$ solution be used; then $45-x$ ounces of the $30\%$ solution are used. Counting ounces of acid,
\[
0.20x+0.30(45-x)=0.24\cdot45\iff 13.5-0.1x=10.8\iff x=27 .
\]
So $\boxed{27}$ ounces of the $20\%$ solution are needed (with $18$ ounces of the $30\%$ solution). Check: $5.4+5.4=10.8$ ounces of acid in $45$ ounces is $24\%$. The value $16.6$ is wrong.
