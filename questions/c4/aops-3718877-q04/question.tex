A firefly starts at the origin. At the start of each second it independently chooses uniformly a displacement from $\{-1,0,1\}^3$ (including the zero displacement), and travels along that segment at constant speed for one second. A point is illuminated whenever its distance from the firefly is strictly less than $2$. Find the expected fraction of lattice points in $[-10,10]^3$ illuminated at least once by time $10$.
