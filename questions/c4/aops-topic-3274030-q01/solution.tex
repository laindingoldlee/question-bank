Use inclusion--exclusion on missing each required set. There are $2^{12}=4096$ subsets. Missing the respective sets gives $2^6,2^8,2^7$ subsets. Missing pairs gives $2^4,2^2,2^4$, and missing all three gives $2^1$. Thus the answer is $4096-64-256-128+16+4+16-2=\boxed{3682}$.
