Use $A=(0,0),B=(10,0),C=(10,10),D=(0,10),P=(3,0)$. The distance condition gives $R=(3,10)$, $Q=(10,\sqrt{51})$, and $S=(0,\sqrt{91})$. Diagonal $PR$ has length 10. The perpendicular distances from $Q,S$ to its line are 7 and 3, so the two triangles on that diagonal have total area $\dfrac{1}{2}(10)(7+3)=\boxed{50}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(10,0),C=(10,10),D=(0,10),P=(3,0),Q=(10,sqrt(51)),R=(3,10),Ss=(0,sqrt(91));draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);fill(P--Q--R--Ss--cycle,lightblue);draw(P--Q--R--Ss--cycle);draw(P--R,dashed);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$P$",P,S);label("$Q$",Q,E);label("$R$",R,N);label("$S$",Ss,W);
\end{asy}
