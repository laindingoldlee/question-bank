For a fixed $y$, the product $xy$ is a linear function of $x$, so on $-8\le x\le2$ its extreme values occur at $x=-8$ or $x=2$; similarly, for fixed $x$ the extremes in $y$ occur at $y=-4$ or $y=10$. So the maximum and minimum of $xy$ are among the four corner products
\[(-8)(-4)=32,\qquad(-8)\cdot10=-80,\qquad2\cdot(-4)=-8,\qquad2\cdot10=20 .\]
The maximum is $32$ and the minimum is $-80$, and their difference is $32-(-80)=\boxed{112}$.
