The even-denominator terms sum to $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{(2n)^2}=\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot\dfrac{\pi^2}{6}$. Removing them from the full sum gives $\left(1-\dfrac{1}{4}\right)\dfrac{\pi^2}{6}=\boxed{\dfrac{\pi^2}{8}}$.
