Let the walker's speed be $w$ mph; the biker's is $1.5w$. Moving in the same direction, the biker gains $1.5w-w=0.5w$ miles per hour, so after $1.5$ hours
\[
0.5w\cdot1.5=12.5\iff0.75w=12.5\iff w=\dfrac{50}{3}.
\]
So the walker moves at $\boxed{\dfrac{50}{3}\approx16.7\text{ mph}}$ and the biker at $\boxed{25\text{ mph}}$.

(These speeds are fast for walking. If ``$1.5$ times faster'' is understood as ``$2.5$ times as fast'', the same method gives $1.5w\cdot1.5=12.5$, i.e.\ walker $\dfrac{50}{9}\approx5.6$ mph and biker $\dfrac{125}{9}\approx13.9$ mph.)
