John stays up from 6:00 PM to 6:00 AM. Every hour on the hour, starting at 6:00 PM, he is either \emph{unlucky} (and then he falls asleep and the process stops) or \emph{lucky}. At 6:00 PM the probability that he is unlucky is $\dfrac{1}{4}$. Each time he is lucky, the probability that he is unlucky at the next hour is multiplied by $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What is the probability that John is still awake after the 9:00 PM hour?
\item[(b)] Explain why the rule cannot be applied as stated at 10:00 PM, and what this means for the probability that John falls asleep at some point during the night.
\end{enumerate}
