Write $5^{51}=125^{17}=2^{119}(1-\dfrac{3}{128})^{17}$. Bernoulli's inequality gives $(1-\dfrac{3}{128})^{17}\ge1-\dfrac{51}{128}=\dfrac{77}{128}>\dfrac{1}{2}$. Thus $5^{51}>\dfrac{2^{119}}{2}=2^{118}$. Therefore $\boxed{5^{51}>2^{118}}$. For completeness, the Bernoulli bound $(1-u)^m\ge1-mu$ for $0\le u\le1$ follows inductively by multiplication by $1-u$ and adding the nonnegative term $mu^2$.
