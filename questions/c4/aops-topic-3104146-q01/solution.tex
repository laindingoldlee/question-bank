The three angles at $ P $ are $ 120^\circ $. Put $ PC=z $. The cosine rule gives $ AB^2=196 $, $ BC^2=z^2+6z+36 $ and $ AC^2=z^2+10z+100 $. Pythagoras at $ B $ yields $ 196+z^2+6z+36=z^2+10z+100 $, so $ 4z=132 $ and $\boxed{z=33}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

pair P=(0,0),A=10*dir(0),B=6*dir(120),C=33*dir(240);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--P--B^^P--C,dashed);label("$P$",P,E);label("$A$",A,E);label("$B$",B,NW);label("$C$",C,SW);label("$10$",A/2,N);label("$6$",B/2,W);label("$z$",C/2,E);
\end{asy}
