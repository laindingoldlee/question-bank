Each digit appears once in each place, so $S=1111(A+B+C+D+E+F+G)=11\cdot101(A+\cdots+G)$. The digit sum is positive and at most $63$, so all its prime factors are below $101$. Since $101$ is prime and always divides $S$, the answer is $\boxed{101}$.
