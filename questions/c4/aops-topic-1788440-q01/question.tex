The game of Connex contains one $4$-unit piece, two identical $3$-unit pieces, three identical $2$-unit pieces and four identical $1$-unit pieces. How many different arrangements of pieces (in a row) will make a $10$-unit segment? The $10$-unit segments made of the pieces $4$-$3$-$2$-$1$ and $1$-$2$-$3$-$4$ count as two different arrangements.
