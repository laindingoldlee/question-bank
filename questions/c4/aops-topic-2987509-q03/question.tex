Two trains are $100$ miles apart and move toward each other, each at $10$ miles per hour. An ostrich starts at the front of one train and flies at $15$ miles per hour toward the other train; whenever it reaches a train it immediately turns back toward the other one. It keeps doing this until the trains collide. How far does the ostrich fly?
