From $f(0)=4$ we get $c=4$. Then $f(2)=4a+2b+4=2$ gives $2a+b=-1$, and
\[
f(4)-f(3)=(16a+4b+c)-(9a+3b+c)=7a+b=4 .
\]
Subtracting, $5a=5$, so $a=1$ and $b=-3$. Hence $f(x)=x^2-3x+4$ and $f(1)=1-3+4=\boxed{2}$.
