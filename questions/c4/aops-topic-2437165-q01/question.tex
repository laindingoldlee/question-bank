A rectangular grid consists of $n\times m$ unit squares ($n\le m$), so it has $(n+1)(m+1)$ grid points. How many squares, of any size and in any orientation (tilted ones included), have all four vertices at grid points? Find a formula in terms of $n$ and $m$.
