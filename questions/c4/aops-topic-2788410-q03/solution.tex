The product is $\dfrac{4014!}{2007!}$. Since $(2n)!=2^n\,n!\cdot\big(1\cdot3\cdot5\cdots(2n-1)\big)$, with $n=2007$ we get $\dfrac{4014!}{2007!}=2^{2007}\cdot1\cdot3\cdots4013$, and the second factor is odd. The answer is $\boxed{2^{2007}}$.
