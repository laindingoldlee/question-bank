Convex octagon $ABCDEFGH$ has eight sides of equal length, and $ACEG$ is a square. The area of square $ACEG$ is $\dfrac{7}{9}$ of the area of the octagon. What is the ratio of the area of square $BDFH$ to the area of the octagon? Express your answer as a common fraction.
