The centre distances are $3,4,5$, forming a right triangle of area $6$. The angles at the radius-one, radius-two, and radius-three centres are respectively $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, $\beta=\arccos(\dfrac{3}{5})$, and $\gamma=\arccos(\dfrac{4}{5})$, measured in radians. Remove the three sectors inside the centre triangle. Their areas are $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$, $2\beta$, and $\dfrac{9}{2}\gamma$. Thus the gap area is
\[\boxed{6-\dfrac{\pi}{4}-2\arccos(\dfrac{3}{5})-\dfrac{9}{2}\arccos(\dfrac{4}{5})}.\]
