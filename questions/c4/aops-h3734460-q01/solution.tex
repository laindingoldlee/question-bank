The polynomial $L=5f-4g$ has degree at most one because its quadratic coefficients cancel. At either intersection, $f=g$ and $L=f$, so its graph is the line through both intersections. Thus $5f(0)-4g(0)=27$. The midpoint condition gives $f(0)+g(0)=18$. Solving yields $f(0)=11,g(0)=7$, and $\boxed{f(0)g(0)=77}$.
