Each of the $6$ women shakes hands with $6-2=4$ others. That counts every handshake twice, so there are $\dfrac{6\cdot4}{2}=\boxed{12}$ handshakes. (Equivalently, $\dbinom62-3=15-3=12$, removing the three partner pairs.)
