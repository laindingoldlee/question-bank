Add up the seat numbers of Bea, Ceci, Dee and Edie before and after.

Before, the five friends filled seats $1$ to $5$, so the four of them occupied seats with total $15-a$, where $a$ is Ada's seat. Afterwards they occupy all seats except the free end seat $f$, with total $15-f$. Bea's move adds $2$, Ceci's move subtracts $1$, and the swap changes nothing, so
\[
15-f=(15-a)+2-1\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad a=f+1 .
\]
The free end seat is $f=1$ or $f=5$; $f=5$ would give $a=6$, impossible. So $f=1$ and $a=\boxed{2}$.

This really happens, for example: before, Bea $1$, Ada $2$, Ceci $3$, Dee $4$, Edie $5$; after, seat $1$ free, Ceci $2$, Bea $3$, Edie $4$, Dee $5$. (So the poster's answer $4$ is not possible.)
