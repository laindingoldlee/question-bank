Triangle $CDE$ is isosceles with $CD=DE=4$ and vertex angle $108^\circ$, so its base angles are $36^\circ$. In right triangle $DPE$, the angle at $E$ is $36^\circ$, giving $PE=\dfrac{4}{\cos36^\circ}$. Also the pentagon diagonal $CE=8\cos36^\circ$. Since $P$ lies on $CE$,\[PE+2PC=2CE-PE=16\cos36^\circ-\dfrac{4}{\cos36^\circ}.\]Using $\cos36^\circ=\dfrac{1+\sqrt5}{4}$, the expression simplifies to $4(1+\sqrt5)-4(\sqrt5-1)=\boxed8$.

\end{asy}
