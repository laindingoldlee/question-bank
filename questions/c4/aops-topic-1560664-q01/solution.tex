\textbf{(a)} If $f(x)=0$, then $x^2=12x+540=3(4x+180)$, so $3\mid x^2$. Since $3$ is prime, $3\mid x$. (If $x$ were not divisible by $3$, then $x^2$ would leave remainder $1$ on division by $3$, while $12x+540$ is a multiple of $3$, so $f(x)$ could not be $0$.) In the same way $x^2=2(6x+270)$, so $2\mid x^2$ and $2\mid x$. $\blacksquare$

\textbf{(b)} First, every rational root of the monic polynomial $f$ is an integer (by the rational root theorem), so we look for integer roots, which by (a) have the form $x=6m$. Then
\[
36m^2-72m-540=0\iff m^2-2m-15=0\iff(m-5)(m+3)=0 ,
\]
so $m=5$ or $m=-3$, i.e.\ $x=30$ or $x=-18$. Indeed $f(x)=(x-30)(x+18)$, and the roots are $\boxed{30\text{ and }-18}$.
