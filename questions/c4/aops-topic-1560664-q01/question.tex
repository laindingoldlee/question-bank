Let $f(x)=x^2-12x-540$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that if $x$ is an integer with $f(x)=0$, then $x$ is divisible by $3$ and by $2$.
\item[(b)] Use (a) to find the roots of $f$.
\end{enumerate}
