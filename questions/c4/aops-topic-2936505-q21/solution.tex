The coefficient sum is $g(1)=\prod(1-a^2)$. Since $f(1)=2\prod(1-a)=11$ and $f(-1)=-2\prod(1+a)=13$, we obtain $g(1)=-\dfrac{f(1)f(-1)}{4}=\boxed{-\dfrac{143}{4}}$.
