The digits must be $0,1,2,3,4,5,6$, whose sum is $21$, so divisibility by $3$ is automatic. Divisibility by all $1,\ldots,6$ means divisibility by $60$, requiring a final zero and divisibility by $4$. With final digit zero, the tens digit must be even: $2,4,$ or $6$. Each choice permits arbitrary ordering of the remaining five nonzero digits in $5!$ ways. Thus the answer is $3\cdot 120=\boxed{360}$.
