Independent choices of positive factors minimize the final product by choosing the smaller factor each time. For $n=1,2$, use $\dfrac{4}{3}$; at $n=3$ the two agree; for $n\ge 4$, use $\dfrac{n+1}{n}$. Thus the minimum is $(\dfrac{4}{3})^3\prod_{n=4}^{99}\dfrac{n+1}{n}=(\dfrac{64}{27})(\dfrac{100}{4})=\boxed{\dfrac{1600}{27}}$, choice B.
