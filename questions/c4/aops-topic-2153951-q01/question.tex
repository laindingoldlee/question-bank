Amy draws all the triangles whose perimeter is $26$ cm and whose side lengths are whole numbers of centimetres. Triangles with the same three side lengths in a different order, such as $(4,5,6)$ and $(4,6,5)$, count as the same type. How many different types of triangle does she draw?
