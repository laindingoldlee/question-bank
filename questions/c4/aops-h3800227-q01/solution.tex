Write the sorted digits as $a<b<c<d$. The possible middle pairs $(b,c)$ are $(2,8),(3,7),(4,6)$; the pair $(1,9)$ leaves no smaller or larger digit, and $(5,5)$ repeats a digit. Their counts of choices for $(a,d)$ are $1\cdot1$, $2\cdot2$, and $3\cdot3$. Thus there are $1+4+9=14$ digit sets. Each gives $4!$ numbers, for a total of $\boxed{336}$.
