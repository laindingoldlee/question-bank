Let the directional counts be $ E,W,N,S $. The constraints are $ E-W=2 $, $ N-S=1 $, and total 5, so $ W+S=1 $. If $(W,S)=(1,0)$, the counts are $(3,1,1,0)$ and have $\dfrac{5!}{3!}=20 $ orders. If $(W,S)=(0,1)$, they are $(2,0,2,1)$ and have $\dfrac{5!}{2!2!}=30 $ orders. Hence the probability is $\dfrac{50}{4^5}=\boxed{\dfrac{25}{512}}$.
