The square has side $14$. Let $X$ and $Y$ be the feet of the perpendiculars from $E$ to the lines $AB$ and $CD$. The heights of the two triangles are
\[
EX=\dfrac{2\cdot105}{14}=15,\qquad EY=\dfrac{2\cdot91}{14}=13 .
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair o(real x,real y,real z){return (x+0.42*y,z+0.3*y);}
pair A=o(0,0,0),B=o(14,0,0),C=o(14,14,0),D=o(0,14,0),Ep=o(3,9,12),F=o(3,9,0);
pair X=o(3,0,0),Y=o(3,14,0);
draw(D--A--B--C,linewidth(0.9)); draw(C--D,dashed);
draw(A--Ep,linewidth(0.9)); draw(B--Ep,linewidth(0.9)); draw(C--Ep,linewidth(0.9)); draw(D--Ep,dashed);
draw(X--Ep,red); draw(Y--Ep,red+dashed); draw(Ep--F,blue+dashed); draw(X--Y,gray+dashed);
dot(A);dot(B);dot(C);dot(D);dot(Ep);dot(F,blue);dot(X,red);dot(Y,red);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,E);label("$D$",D,W);label("$E$",Ep,N);
label("$X$",X,S,red);label("$Y$",Y,SE,red);label("$F$",F,S,blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The lines $EX$ and $EY$ are perpendicular to $AB\parallel CD$, so the plane $EXY$ is perpendicular to $AB$; it meets the base in the segment $XY$ of length $14$ (perpendicular to both $AB$ and $CD$), and it contains the foot $F$ of the perpendicular from $E$ to the base. So the height $EF$ of the pyramid is the height to side $XY$ of triangle $EXY$ with sides $14$, $15$, $13$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s=0.2){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
pair X=(0,0),Y=(14,0),Ep=(9,12),F=(9,0);
draw(X--Y--Ep--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(Ep--F,blue+dashed); draw(ra(F,X-F,Ep-F,0.7),blue);
dot(X);dot(Y);dot(Ep);dot(F,blue);
label("$X$",X,SW);label("$Y$",Y,SE);label("$E$",Ep,N);label("$F$",F,S,blue);
draw((0,-1.9)--(14,-1.9),gray,Arrows(TeXHead)); label("$14$",(7,-1.9),S,gray);label("$15$",(X+Ep)/2,NW);label("$13$",(Y+Ep)/2,NE);label("$12$",(9,6),W,blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

By Heron's formula ($s=21$) its area is $\sqrt{21\cdot7\cdot6\cdot8}=84$, so $EF=\dfrac{2\cdot84}{14}=12$. (Equivalently, $XF=9$ and $YF=5$, since $15^2-9^2=13^2-5^2=144$.) The volume is
\[
\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot196\cdot12=\boxed{784}.
\]
