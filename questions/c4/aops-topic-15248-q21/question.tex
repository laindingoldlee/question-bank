Jeff the coat boy takes coats at a party, numbering them $1,2,3,\ldots$ in the order they arrive. Every $2$nd coat (coats $2,4,6,\ldots$) is black, every $5$th coat (coats $5,10,15,\ldots$) is an overcoat, and every $7$th coat (coats $7,14,21,\ldots$) is leather; a coat may have several of these properties. So far there have been exactly $9$ overcoats and $6$ leather coats. What are the possible numbers of black coats so far?
