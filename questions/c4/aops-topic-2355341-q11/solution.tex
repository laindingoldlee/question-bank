Max has the smallest number, so $L>M$, and $|L-M|=1$ gives $L=M+1$. Then $|L-J|=1$ gives $J=L+1=M+2$ or $J=L-1=M$; the second is impossible because the numbers are different. So $J=M+2>L=M+1>M$, and the order is $J,L,M$: $\boxed{\textbf{(B)}}$.
