A quadratic with roots $r_1$, $r_2$ is $(x-r_1)(x-r_2)=0$, i.e.\ $x^2-(r_1+r_2)x+r_1r_2=0$. Here $r_1+r_2=6$ and $r_1r_2=-21.25=-\dfrac{85}{4}$, so
\[
x^2-6x-\dfrac{85}{4}=0\qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad\boxed{4x^2-24x-85=0}.
\]
(Equivalently $(2x+5)(2x-17)=0$.) Any nonzero multiple of this equation also works.
