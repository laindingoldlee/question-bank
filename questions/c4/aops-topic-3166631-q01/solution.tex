Euclidean division gives $c=\lfloor\dfrac{n}{12}\rfloor$ and $b=n-12c$. The range of $n$ gives $0\le c\le7$. If $12c+b=12c^{\prime}+b^{\prime}$, then $12(c-c^{\prime})=b^{\prime}-b$, whose absolute value is at most $11$; therefore it is zero and both coordinates agree. Conversely any $b\in\{0,\ldots,11\}$ and $c\in\{0,\ldots,7\}$ gives a valid $n$. There are $12\cdot8=\boxed{96}$ pairs.
