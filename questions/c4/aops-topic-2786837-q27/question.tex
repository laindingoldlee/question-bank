Guang is working on a project. From $1{:}00$ AM Saturday to $1{:}00$ AM Sunday, the probability that he has not finished $x$ hours after $1{:}00$ AM Saturday is $\dfrac1{x+1}$ (for $0\le x<24$). If he has not finished by $1{:}00$ AM Sunday, he finishes immediately at that moment. Find the exact expected number of minutes after $1{:}00$ AM Saturday at which he finishes, and give it to two decimal places.
