Let $T$ be the finishing time in hours. Then $P(T>x)=\dfrac1{x+1}$ for $0\le x<24$ and $P(T>x)=0$ for $x\ge24$. For a nonnegative random variable, $E[T]=\displaystyle\int_0^\infty P(T>x)\,dx$, so
\[E[T]=\int_0^{24}\dfrac{dx}{x+1}=\ln25\ \text{hours}.\]
In minutes this is $60\ln25=\boxed{120\ln5\approx193.13}$.
