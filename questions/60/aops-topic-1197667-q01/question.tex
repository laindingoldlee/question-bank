Find the sum
\[
1\text{ cm}+2\text{ cm}+3\text{ cm}+\cdots+11\text{ cm}+12\text{ m}+13\text{ cm}+\cdots+20\text{ cm},
\]
in which every term is in centimetres except the twelfth, which is $12$ metres. Give the answer in centimetres and in metres.
