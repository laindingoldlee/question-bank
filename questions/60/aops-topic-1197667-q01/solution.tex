Without the twelfth term, the centimetre terms add up to
\[
(1+2+\cdots+20)-12=210-12=198\text{ cm}.
\]
The twelfth term is $12\text{ m}=1200$ cm. The total is
\[
198+1200=\boxed{1398\text{ cm}=13.98\text{ m}}.
\]
