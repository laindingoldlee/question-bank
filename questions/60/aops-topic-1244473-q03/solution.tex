Give each marble its target rank G$=0$, B$=1$, R$=2$, so the row reads $2,0,1,1,0,1,2,0,2$. A switch of two adjacent marbles of different colours changes the number of inversions (pairs out of order) by exactly $1$, and switching equal colours is useless. The target has $0$ inversions, so the minimum number of switches equals the current number of inversions, and it is attained by repeatedly switching an adjacent out-of-order pair (bubble sort).

Count inversions: the R in position $1$ precedes the six G's and B's in positions $2,3,4,5,6,8$: $6$; the B's in positions $3$, $4$ each precede the G's in positions $5$ and $8$: $2+2=4$; the B in position $6$ precedes the G in position $8$: $1$; the R in position $7$ precedes the G in position $8$: $1$. Total $6+4+1+1=12$. The answer is $\boxed{12}$.
