Write $a=gu,b=gv$ with $\gcd(u,v)=1$. The equation reduces to $1+uv=u+v$, or $(u-1)(v-1)=0$. Thus one of $a,b$ must divide the other.

There are $21$ equal pairs. The strict divisible pairs with smaller entry first are
\[
(10,20),(10,30),(11,22),(12,24),(13,26),(14,28),(15,30).
\]
There are no others because a smaller entry above $15$ has no larger multiple in the interval. Each gives two orders, so the answer is $\boxed{21+2(7)=35}$.
