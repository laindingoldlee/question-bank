With rolls $a,b$: $ab+a+b=(a+1)(b+1)-1$. We need $(a+1)(b+1)\equiv1\pmod3$, i.e. $a+1\equiv b+1\equiv1$ or $a+1\equiv b+1\equiv2\pmod 3$. So $a\equiv b\equiv0$ or $a\equiv b\equiv1\pmod3$.

On the $6$-sided die, residues $0$ and $1$ each occur twice. On the $20$-sided die, residue $0$ occurs $6$ times and residue $1$ occurs $7$ times. The probability is
\[\dfrac{2\cdot6+2\cdot7}{6\cdot20}=\dfrac{26}{120}=\boxed{\dfrac{13}{60}}.\]
