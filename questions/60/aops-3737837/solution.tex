Transform every tetrahedral die result $a$ into $5-a$, and every ordinary die result $b$ into $7-b$. This bijection preserves the probability of each outcome. If the original score difference is $D=A-B$, the transformed difference is $(15-A)-(14-B)=1-D$. Since $D$ is an integer, exactly one of $D$ and $1-D$ is positive. Thus outcomes pair into one Ana win and one non-win, giving probability $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$.
