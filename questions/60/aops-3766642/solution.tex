Let $(F_k,W_k,G_k)$ count choices after the first creature and $k$ further battles. Starting from $(1,0,0)$, appending any next type gives $F_{k+1}=2F_k+G_k$, $W_{k+1}=2W_k+F_k$, $G_{k+1}=2G_k+W_k$. For example a Fire survivor stays Fire against Fire or Grass, while a Grass survivor becomes Fire against Fire. Successive triples are $(2,1,0),(4,4,1),(9,12,6),(24,33,24),(72,90,81),(225,252,252)$. Therefore $\boxed{225}$ assignments yield Fire.
