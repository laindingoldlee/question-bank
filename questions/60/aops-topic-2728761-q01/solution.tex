Let $n=0.\overline{xy}$. Then $100n=xy.\overline{xy}$, and subtracting, $99n=\overline{xy}$, the two-digit number $10x+y$. So
\[0.\overline{xy}=\dfrac{10x+y}{99}.\]
For example $0.\overline{27}=\dfrac{27}{99}=\boxed{\dfrac3{11}}$. (In general a block of $k$ repeating digits gives the block divided by $10^k-1$.)
