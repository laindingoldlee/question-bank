For every positive integer $t$, the coefficient bounds imply
\[
(t+1)^3<P(t)<(t+3)^3.
\]
Therefore the only possible cube is $(t+2)^3$, and the condition becomes
\[
(a-6)t^2+(b-12)t+(c-8)=0.
\]
The sole excluded polynomial is $(x+2)^3$, with coefficients $(6,12,8)$, leaving $5\cdot23\cdot25-1=2874$ equally likely choices.

Only $1\le t\le18$ need be considered. For $t\ge19$, if $a-6\ge1$ the expression is at least $t^2-8t-6>0$; if $a-6\le-1$ it is at most $-t^2+14t+18<0$. If $a=6$, a nonzero coefficient $b-12$ gives a nonzero expression of its sign, while $b=12$ leaves a nonzero constant because the identically zero case was excluded.

For each $t=1,\ldots,18$, count $a,b$ for which $c=8-(a-6)t^2-(b-12)t$ lies in $[2,26]$, then remove the excluded triple. The counts are
\[
74,55,39,25,19,18,10,7,5,3,3,3,3,3,2,1,1,1,
\]
whose sum is $272$. By linearity of expectation,
\[
E=\dfrac{272}{2874}=\dfrac{136}{1437}.
\]
Thus $\boxed{m+n=1573}$.
