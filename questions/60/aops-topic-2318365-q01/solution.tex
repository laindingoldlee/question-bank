She eats it at once with probability $\dfrac12$, or leaves it and then goes back and eats it with probability $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac15=\dfrac{1}{10}$. These cases are disjoint, so the probability is
\[\dfrac12+\dfrac{1}{10}=\boxed{\dfrac35}.\]
