A spinner is a disc with $20$ dots equally spaced around its edge, labelled $1$ to $20$ in order around the circle. Each dot labelled with a prime number is joined to the centre by a segment. These segments divide the disc into sectors, which are shaded alternately red and blue going around the disc. The sector between the dots labelled $2$ and $3$ (the two smallest primes) is blue. The spinner's arrow stops at a uniformly random direction; Alice wins if it lands in a blue sector. What is the probability that Alice wins?
