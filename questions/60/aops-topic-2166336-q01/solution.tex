By the difference of squares,
\[2^{22}-1=\left(2^{11}-1\right)\left(2^{11}+1\right)=2047\cdot2049 .\]
Now $2047=23\cdot89$, and $2049=3\cdot683$ (note $2^{11}+1$ is divisible by $3$ because $2\equiv-1\pmod3$). The factors $3$, $23$ and $89$ are prime, and so is $683$: it is not divisible by any prime up to $\sqrt{683}<27$, namely $2,3,5,7,11,13,17,19,23$. Hence
\[2^{22}-1=\boxed{3\cdot23\cdot89\cdot683}\ (=4194303).\]
