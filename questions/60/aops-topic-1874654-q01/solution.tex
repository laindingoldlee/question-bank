There are $\left\lfloor\dfrac{99}{3}\right\rfloor=33$ multiples of $3$ below $100$. Those divisible by $2$ as well are the multiples of $6$, and there are $\left\lfloor\dfrac{99}{6}\right\rfloor=16$ of them. So the answer is $33-16=\boxed{17}$ (the odd multiples of $3$: $3,9,15,\dots,99$).
