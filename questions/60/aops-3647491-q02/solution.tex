Let widths be $x,y$ and common height $h>0$. Then $x^2+h^2=9$, $y^2+h^2=16$, and $x+y=5$. Subtracting gives $5(y-x)=7$, so $x=\dfrac{9}{5}$, $y=\dfrac{16}{5}$, and $h=\sqrt{9-\dfrac{81}{25}}=\dfrac{12}{5}$. The total area is $h(x+y)=\boxed{12}$.
