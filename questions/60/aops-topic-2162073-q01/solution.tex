The terms are
\[7,\ -6,\ -13,\ -7,\ 6,\ 13,\ 7,\ -6,\ \dots\]
In general $a_{n+1}=a_n-a_{n-1}$ and $a_{n+2}=a_{n+1}-a_n=-a_{n-1}$, so $a_{n+3}=-a_n$ and $a_{n+6}=a_n$: the sequence has period $6$. The six terms of a period add to $7-6-13-7+6+13=0$. Since $2000=6\cdot333+2$, the first $1998$ terms add to $0$, and the sum of the first $2000$ terms is $a_{1999}+a_{2000}=a_1+a_2=7-6=\boxed{1}$.
