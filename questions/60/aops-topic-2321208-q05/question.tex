A problem says: ``$30\%$ of players use client $L$, $40\%$ use client $B$, and $60\%$ use both.''
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Explain why these numbers are impossible.
\item[(b)] Suppose instead that $30\%$ use $L$, $40\%$ use $B$ and $x\%$ use both. Which values of $x$ are possible, and what percentage of players use neither client, in terms of $x$?
\end{enumerate}
