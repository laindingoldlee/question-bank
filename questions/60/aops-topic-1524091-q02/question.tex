In circle $O$, the chords $AB$ and $BC$ have equal lengths and $\angle ABC=90^\circ$. Given that circle $O$ has a radius of $3$ metres, what is the length of arc $ABC$ (the arc from $A$ to $C$ passing through $B$)? Express your answer in terms of $\pi$.
