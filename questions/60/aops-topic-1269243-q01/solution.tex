A year has Friday the $13$th in February, March and November exactly when it is a common (non-leap) year in which February $13$ is a Friday; this happens when January $1$ is a Thursday. (Feb $13$ and Mar $13$ are $28$ days apart; Mar $13$ and Nov $13$ are $245=35\cdot7$ days apart.) Checking all fourteen possible calendars (seven starting days, common or leap), a year has three Friday the $13$ths exactly when it is a common year starting on Thursday or a leap year starting on Sunday (January, April, July), and never more.

January $1$, $1998$ was a Thursday. Each common year moves the weekday of January $1$ forward by $1$ and each leap year by $2$. So January $1$ falls on: $1999$ Friday, $2000$ Saturday, $2001$ Monday, $2002$ Tuesday, $2003$ Wednesday, $2004$ Thursday, $2005$ Saturday, $2006$ Sunday, $2007$ Monday, $2008$ Tuesday, $2009$ Thursday ($2000$, $2004$, $2008$ are leap years).

A common year has three Friday the $13$ths only if it starts on Thursday, and a leap year only if it starts on Sunday (then January, April and July). None of $1999$--$2008$ qualifies ($2004$ is a leap year starting on Thursday, which gives only February and August). So the next such year is $\boxed{2009}$, with Friday the $13$th in February, March and November.
