With $A=(0,0)$: $B=(5,4)$ and $C=(4,3)$. By the shoelace formula,
\[[ABC]=\dfrac12\left|5\cdot3-4\cdot4\right|=\boxed{\dfrac12}\text{ cm}^2.\]
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
for(int i=0;i<6;++i)for(int j=0;j<5;++j)dot((i,j),gray);draw((0,0)--(5,4)--(4,3)--cycle);label("$A$",(0,0),SW);label("$B$",(5,4),NE);label("$C$",(4,3),S);
\end{asy}
