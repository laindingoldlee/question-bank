Given a circle and five lines $\ell_1,\ell_2,\ell_3,\ell_4,\ell_5$ in its plane, construct a pentagon $A_1A_2A_3A_4A_5$ inscribed in the circle whose sides satisfy
\[
A_1A_2\parallel\ell_1,\quad A_2A_3\parallel\ell_2,\quad A_3A_4\parallel\ell_3,\quad A_4A_5\parallel\ell_4,\quad A_5A_1\parallel\ell_5,
\]
and prove that the construction works.
