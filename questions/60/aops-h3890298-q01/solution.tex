Use inches. The table has radius 6 and the disk radius $\dfrac{1}{2}$. The disk fits exactly when its center is at most $6-\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{11}{2}$ inches from the table center. Thus the probability is the ratio of disk areas,
\[\dfrac{\pi(\dfrac{11}{2})^2}{\pi6^2}=\boxed{\dfrac{121}{144}}.\]
