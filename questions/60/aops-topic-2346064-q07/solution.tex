Such a number starts with $2$. Its second digit is greater than $2$ (increasing digits) and at most $3$ (the number is below $2400$), so it is $3$. The last two digits are two different digits from $\{4,5,6,7,8,9\}$, written in increasing order, so each choice of two digits gives exactly one number: $\dbinom62=15$ numbers. The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(C) }15}$.
