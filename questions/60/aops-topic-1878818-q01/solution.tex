(a) Every route consists of exactly $3$ moves right and $3$ moves down, in some order, and every such order gives a route that stays in the grid. So a route is determined by choosing which $3$ of the $6$ moves are ``right'':
\[
\dbinom63=\boxed{20}.
\]
(b) With $n$ rows and $m$ columns, a route has $m-1$ moves right and $n-1$ moves down, $n+m-2$ moves in total, so the number of routes is
\[
\boxed{\dbinom{n+m-2}{n-1}=\dfrac{(n+m-2)!}{(n-1)!\,(m-1)!}} .
\]
For $n=m=4$ this gives $\dbinom63=20$.
