Induction on the number of players; one player is trivial. Line up all but one player $P$ as required: $Q_1,Q_2,\dots,Q_m$ with $Q_i$ beating $Q_{i+1}$. If $P$ beat $Q_1$, put $P$ in front. If $Q_m$ beat $P$, put $P$ at the back. Otherwise $Q_1$ beat $P$ and $P$ beat $Q_m$, so there is a first index $i$ with $P$ beating $Q_{i+1}$; then $Q_i$ beat $P$, and $P$ can stand between $Q_i$ and $Q_{i+1}$. $\blacksquare$
