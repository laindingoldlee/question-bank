First, $5^{49}>4^{49}=2^{98}>2^{81}$.

Next, $5^{49}=\dfrac{10^{49}}{2^{49}}$, and $2^{49}>2^{40}=\left(2^{10}\right)^4=1024^4>1000^4=10^{12}$. Hence $5^{49}<\dfrac{10^{49}}{10^{12}}=10^{37}$.

So the order from greatest to least is $\boxed{10^{37},\ 5^{49},\ 2^{81}}$. (Numerically, $2^{81}\approx2.4\times10^{24}$ and $5^{49}\approx1.8\times10^{34}$.)
