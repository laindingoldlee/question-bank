A standard deck of $52$ cards is shuffled $4$ times. During each shuffle, independently of the other shuffles, exactly one card falls out with probability $16\%$, and otherwise no card falls out. A card that falls out is equally likely to be any of the $52$ cards, and it lands face up with probability $20\%$, independently of which card it is. After each shuffle the card is put back into the deck. What is the probability that in every one of the $4$ shuffles a card falls out, lands face up, and is an ace?
