Since $n>2$, the integer $n-1$ is at least $2$, so it has a prime factor $p$. Then $p\le n-1<n$. If $p$ also divided $n$, it would divide $n-(n-1)=1$, which is impossible. So $p$ is a prime less than $n$ that does not divide $n$. $\blacksquare$

(For example, $n=6$: $n-1=5$, and $5\nmid6$; $n=12$: $n-1=11$, and $11\nmid12$.)
