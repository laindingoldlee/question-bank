Renu's hourly rate is $\dfrac{1}{60}$ task and Seema's is $\dfrac{1}{40}$. If Seema works $d$ days, Renu works $2d$ days. Their completed work is $\dfrac{4d}{60}+\dfrac{4d}{40}=\dfrac{d}{6}$. Setting this equal to one gives $\boxed{d=6}$.
