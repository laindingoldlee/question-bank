\emph{The fee.} The first repair lasts $2.5$ hours, charged as $3$ daytime hours: $x+3(23.50)=105.50$, so $x=35$.

\emph{The night rate.} The second repair lasts $5$ hours $20$ minutes, charged as $6$ hours starting at 6:30, 7:30, 8:30, 9:30 (daytime, before 10:13) and 10:30, 11:30 (night). So $35+4(23.50)+2h=191.60$ and $h=31.30$.

\emph{Today.} The start $s$ is between 2:00 and 5:00 p.m., and the seven hours start at $s,s+1,\dots,s+6$. Only the last two can start after dusk (9:02 p.m.): the last one does when $s$ is after 3:02, and the last two do when $s$ is after 4:02. So there are $0$, $1$ or $2$ night hours, and the cost is
\[35+7(23.50)+n(31.30-23.50)=199.50+7.80n,\qquad n\in\{0,1,2\}.\]
The difference between the most and the least is $2(7.80)=\boxed{\$15.60}$.
