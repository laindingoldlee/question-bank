A sentence is encrypted letter by letter. If a letter is the $\theta$-th letter of the alphabet ($a$ is $1$, $b$ is $2$, \dots, $z$ is $26$), it is replaced by the $r$-th letter, where $r$ is the remainder when $2^\theta$ is divided by $26$; spaces are kept. For example, ``abcde'' becomes ``bdhpf''. After encryption a sentence reads
\[\text{vbddb vbdvbh hvfd}.\]
How many possible original sentences are there?
