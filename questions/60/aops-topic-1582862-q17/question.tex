Eratosthenes, a Greek mathematician of the 3rd century BCE, estimated the circumference of Earth. The distance from Syene (Aswan) to Alexandria, which is due north of it, is about $500$ miles ($5000$ stadia). At noon on the summer solstice the Sun cast no shadow on a vertical pole in Syene, while at the same moment a vertical pole in Alexandria cast a shadow, and the angle between the pole and the ray from its top to the end of its shadow was $7.2^\circ$. Assuming the Sun's rays are parallel, estimate the circumference of Earth in miles.
