A dartboard is a regular hexagon. Its six vertices are marked blue and the midpoints of its six edges are marked red. A dart lands at a uniformly random point of the board; the throw is \emph{bluish} if the nearest of the twelve marked points is blue. What is the probability that the throw is bluish? Express your answer as a common fraction.
