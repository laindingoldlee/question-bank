For first digit $a$ ($1\le a\le9$), the last two digits $(b,c)$ with $b+c=a$ can be chosen in $a+1$ ways. The total is $\displaystyle\sum_{a=1}^{9}(a+1)=2+3+\cdots+10=\boxed{54}$.
