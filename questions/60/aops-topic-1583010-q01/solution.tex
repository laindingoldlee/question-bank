Let the package weigh $w$ kilograms. Then
\[
w=\dfrac{9}{10}w+\dfrac{9}{10}\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad \dfrac{1}{10}w=\dfrac{9}{10}\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad w=9 .
\]
In words: the missing tenth of the weight is the $\dfrac{9}{10}$ kg, so the whole is ten times that. The package weighs $\boxed{9\text{ kg}}$.
