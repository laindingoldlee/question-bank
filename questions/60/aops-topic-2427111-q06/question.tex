A box contains nine cards numbered $1$ to $9$. Alice draws two cards at random; she wins if the sum of her two numbers is prime. She puts both cards back and shakes the box. Then Bob draws two cards at random; he wins if the sum of his two numbers is greater than $11$. What is the probability that both Alice and Bob win?
