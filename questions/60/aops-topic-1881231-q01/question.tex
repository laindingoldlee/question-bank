A train travels from $X$ to $Y$ at a constant speed. After travelling for $1$ hour it has an accident and stops for $\dfrac12$ hour. It then continues at $\dfrac45$ of its original speed and reaches $Y$ $2$ hours late. If the accident had happened $80$ km further along the route, the train would have reached $Y$ only $1$ hour late. Find the original speed of the train and the distance from $X$ to $Y$.
