The deficits from six sum to 5. Since they are nonnegative, the upper bound of five is automatic, so stars and bars gives $\binom{10}{5}=252$ ordered outcomes. There are $6^6=46656$ total outcomes. Thus the probability is $\dfrac{252}{46656}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{1296}}$.
