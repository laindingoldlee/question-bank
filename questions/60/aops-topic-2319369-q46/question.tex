Peanuts cost $1.20$ dollars per pound, almonds $2.20$ dollars per pound and cashews $3.20$ dollars per pound. A shop wants $280$ pounds of a mixture costing $2.59$ dollars per pound, in which the weight of almonds and cashews together is three times the weight of peanuts. Find the weight of each kind of nut, rounded to the nearest pound.
