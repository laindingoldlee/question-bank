For $x>0$, AM--GM gives $x+\dfrac{5}{x}\ge2\sqrt{x\cdot\dfrac{5}{x}}=2\sqrt5$, with equality at $x=\sqrt5$. So the minimum over positive $x$ is $\boxed{2\sqrt5}$.

For negative $x$ the expression is negative, and it is unbounded below (for example $x=-t$ with $t$ large gives $-t-\dfrac{5}{t}$), so over all nonzero reals there is no smallest value.
