At each $(x,0)$, $x=1,\ldots,100$, a vertical ray points upward; at each $(0,y)$, $y=1,\ldots,100$, a horizontal ray points right. A ray detects only the first vertex it encounters. Consider triangles with three distinct lattice vertices in $\{1,\ldots,100\}^2$ such that exactly one vertex is detected by neither of its two rays. Find the sum of their areas.
