Right triangle OAC has OA=5 and angle ACO=30 degrees, so OC=10 and $AC=5\sqrt3$. If the smaller radius is r, its centre lies 2r from C along CO. External tangency gives $10-2r=5+r$, hence $r=\dfrac{5}{3}$. Quadrilateral OACB has area $2\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot5\cdot5\sqrt3=25\sqrt3$. The portion of the large disk inside it is a 120-degree sector, of area $\dfrac{25\pi}{3}$; the small disk is entirely inside it and has area $\dfrac{25\pi}{9}$. Thus the requested area is $\boxed{25\sqrt3-\dfrac{100\pi}{9}}$.
