The year digit sum is $11$. For month $m$, list valid days whose digit sum is $11-s(m)$. In months January through December the counts are respectively $2,3,3,3,3,3,4,4,3,2,3,3$. For example, January contributes $19,28$, February contributes $9,18,27$, and July contributes $4,13,22,31$. The usual month lengths, with February having $28$ days, give total $\boxed{36}$.
