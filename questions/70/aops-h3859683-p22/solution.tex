Put $d_i=k-(f(i)-f(i-1))$. Then $0\le d_i\le2k$ and $\sum d_i=2n$, so $G(n,k)$ is the coefficient of $z^{2n}$ in $(1+z+\cdots+z^{2k})^n$. For a transparent finite search, define $D_0(0)=1$ and $D_i(s)=\sum_{j=0}^{\min(2k,s)}D_{i-1}(s-j)$, with other initial values zero. Evaluate $D_n(2n)$ for each divisor $n$ of each candidate product $kn=1,2,\ldots$. No product at most 13 gives a value above 2025. At product 14, $(n,k)=(7,2)$ gives 8135. Hence the minimum is $\boxed{14}$.
