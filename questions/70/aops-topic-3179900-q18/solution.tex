By symmetry, the probability sought is half of one minus the tie probability. If both have $j$ guards, the third helicopter has $6-2j$, so the tie count among $3^6=729$ assignments is $\sum_{j=0}^{3}\dfrac{6!}{j!j!(6-2j)!}=1+30+90+20=141$. Thus the desired probability is $\dfrac{1}{2}(1-\dfrac{141}{729})=\dfrac{98}{243}$. Hence $\boxed{a+b=341}$.
