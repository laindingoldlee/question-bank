Expand as $\sum_{k\ge0}(x+x^2+\cdots+x^6)^k$. Choosing one monomial from each of the $k$ factors records an ordered list of $k$ parts, each between $1$ and $6$; its exponent is the sum of those parts. Thus the coefficient $a_n$ counts ordered compositions of $n$ with allowed parts $1,\ldots,6$, including one empty composition for $n=0$. Classifying by the last part gives $a_n=\sum_{j=1}^6a_{n-j}$, with $a_0=1$ and $a_n=0$ for $n<0$. The first coefficients are $1,1,2,4,8,16,32,63$.
