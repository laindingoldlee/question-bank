Let $N$ be an integer with $1\le N\le13$, and let $S(N)$ be the number of distinct values that can be obtained as the sum of $N$ distinct elements of $\{1,2,3,\dots,13\}$. Find the sum of all values of $N$ for which $S(N)$ is as large as possible.
