The age difference is always $41-14=27$. If Brian's age is $\overline{ab}=10a+b$ and his father's is $\overline{ba}=10b+a$, then $9(b-a)=27$, so $b=a+3$. Brian's possible ages are therefore $14,25,36,47,58,69$, and the next one after $14$ is $\boxed{25}$ (his father is then $52$).
