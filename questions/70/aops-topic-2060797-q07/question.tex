A function $f$ satisfies $f(x)=\left(f(x+1)\right)^2-1$ and $f(x)\le0$ for all real $x$, and $f(0)=0$. Let $g(x)=\left(f(x)\right)^2-x$. Determine $\left|g(10)\right|$.

$\textbf{(A) }10\qquad\textbf{(B) }17\qquad\textbf{(C) }29\qquad\textbf{(D) }42\qquad\textbf{(E) }70$
