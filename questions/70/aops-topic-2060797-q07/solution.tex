The equation gives $\left(f(x+1)\right)^2=f(x)+1$, and since $f(x+1)\le0$,
\[f(x+1)=-\sqrt{f(x)+1}.\]
Starting from $f(0)=0$: $f(1)=-\sqrt1=-1$, $f(2)=-\sqrt0=0$, $f(3)=-1$, and so on; by induction $f(n)=0$ for even $n\ge0$ and $f(n)=-1$ for odd $n$. In particular $f(10)=0$, so
\[g(10)=0^2-10=-10,\qquad|g(10)|=10,\]
answer $\boxed{\textbf{(A)}\ 10}$.

(Such a function exists, for example $f(x)=0$ when $\lfloor x\rfloor$ is even and $f(x)=-1$ when $\lfloor x\rfloor$ is odd: then $\left(f(x+1)\right)^2-1$ equals $1-1=0=f(x)$ or $0-1=-1=f(x)$.)
