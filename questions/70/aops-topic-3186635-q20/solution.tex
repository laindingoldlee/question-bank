The conditioning event consists of five $2$s and one $3$ (six outcomes), or four $2$s, one $3$, and one of $1,4,5,6$ ($4\cdot30=120$ outcomes). All prime faces occur in the first six and in the thirty outcomes with the remaining face $5$. Thus the conditional probability is $\dfrac{36}{126}=\dfrac{2}{7}$, giving $m+n=\boxed9$.
