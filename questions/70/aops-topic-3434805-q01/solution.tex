The trip distance is $57060-56200=860$ miles. Since the tank is full at both the beginning and end, the fuel consumed during the trip equals the later refills, $12+20=32$ gallons. The initial six gallons were added before measuring trip consumption. Thus the average is $\dfrac{860}{32}=26.875$, or $\boxed{26.9\text{ miles per gallon}}$.
