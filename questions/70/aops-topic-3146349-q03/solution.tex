The area is 28. If the bounding grid rectangle has dimensions $w,h$, then $wh\ge28$, so the integer $w+h$ is at least 11. The boundary must span the full width twice and height twice, giving perimeter at least $2(w+h)\ge22$. This bound is attainable: the displayed tiling fills a $5\times6$ rectangle except the two lower corner cells. Removing either corner leaves perimeter unchanged, so the perimeter is 22. The seven labelled pieces are each one of the required shapes. Therefore $\boxed{22}$.
