Let $k$ be a positive integer. Prove that the number of ordered pairs of positive integers $(a,b)$ with
\[
\dfrac{1}{a}+\dfrac{1}{b}=\dfrac{1}{k}
\]
equals $d(k^2)$, the number of positive divisors of $k^2$.
