You buy a dozen donuts at a shop with $4$ flavours: chocolate, lemon, jelly and glazed. There are unlimited glazed and chocolate donuts, but only $5$ lemon and $3$ jelly donuts left. How many different combinations of $12$ donuts can you get? (Donuts of the same flavour are identical.) A student computed $\dbinom{15}{3}-\left(\dbinom93+\dbinom{11}3-\dbinom53\right)$ and got $346$. Is this right?
