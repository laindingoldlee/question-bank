At the Carnation Coronation of Queen Flora, each flower girl in the procession wears lilies, tulips or violets (or a combination of them). A guard counts $48$ flower girls wearing lilies, $15$ wearing lilies and violets, $9$ wearing lilies and tulips, and $3$ wearing all three flowers. How many flower girls are wearing only lilies?
