Among the $48$ girls with lilies, $15$ also wear violets and $9$ also wear tulips, and the $3$ girls wearing all three are counted in both groups. So the number wearing lilies together with another flower is $15+9-3=21$, and the number wearing only lilies is $48-21=\boxed{27}$.
