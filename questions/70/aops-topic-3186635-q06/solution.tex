If there are $s$ squares and $t$ triangles, the angle totals give $360s+180t=1800$, or $2s+t=10$. Perimeters give $4s+3t=24$. Eliminating $t$ gives $30-2s=24$, so $\boxed{s=3}$.

\end{asy}
