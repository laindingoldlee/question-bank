Let $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$ be numbers with $c\ne0$ and $d\ne0$, and let
\[
M=\dfrac{ax+by}{cx+dy}.
\]
Suppose that $M$ has the same value for all numbers $x$, $y$ with $cx+dy\ne0$. Prove that $\dfrac {a}{c}=\dfrac {b}{d}$.
