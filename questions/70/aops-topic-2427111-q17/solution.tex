The rectangle has sides $AB=x-1$ and $BC=2x+3$, so
\[(x-1)(2x+3)=18\iff2x^2+x-21=0\iff(x-3)(2x+7)=0.\]
Since $x-1>0$, $x=3$, so $AB=2$ and $BC=9$.

A rectangle inscribed in a circle has its diagonals as diameters (each angle of the rectangle is a right angle, inscribed in a semicircle). The diagonal is $AC=\sqrt{2^2+9^2}=\sqrt{85}$, so the circumference is $\pi\sqrt{85}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(2,0),C=(2,9),D=(0,9),O=(1,4.5);
draw(circle(O,sqrt(85)/2),blue);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
draw(A--C,dashed);
draw(A--B,red+linewidth(1.4)); draw(B--C,red+linewidth(1.4));
draw(B+(-0.3,0)--B+(-0.3,0.3)--B+(0,0.3));
dot(A); dot(B); dot(C);
label("$A$",A,SW); label("$B$",B,SE); label("$C$",C,NE); label("$2$",(A+B)/2,S); label("$9$",(B+C)/2,E); label("$\sqrt{85}$",(-1.3,6.2),fontsize(8pt)); draw((-0.6,6.0)--(1.3,5.85),gray+0.4);
\end{asy}

The total distance is
\[2+9+\pi\sqrt{85}=\boxed{11+\sqrt{85}\,\pi}\approx39.96.\]
