An ant starts at point $A$ and walks $x-1$ units to a point $B$. It then turns through $90^\circ$ and walks $2x+3$ units to a point $C$. The points $A$, $B$, $C$ are vertices of a rectangle of area $18$, and the rectangle is inscribed in a circle. The ant then walks once around the circle, starting and ending at $C$. What is the total distance the ant walks?
