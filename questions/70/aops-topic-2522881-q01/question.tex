The Fibonacci numbers are defined by $F_0=0$, $F_1=1$ and $F_{n+1}=F_n+F_{n-1}$ for $n\ge1$. Let
\[\varphi=\dfrac{1+\sqrt5}{2},\qquad\psi=\dfrac{1-\sqrt5}{2}.\]
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that $\varphi$ and $\psi$ are the roots of $x^2=x+1$, and explain where the factor $\sqrt5$ in the formula below comes from.
\item[(b)] Prove that for every $n\ge0$,
\[F_n=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt5}\left(\varphi^n-\psi^n\right).\]
\end{enumerate}
