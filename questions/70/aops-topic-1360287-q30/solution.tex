Let $s$ be the digit sum and $n$ the number. Then $n=2s+2$ (with remainder $2<s$) and $ns=112$, so $(2s+2)s=112$, $s^2+s-56=0$, $(s+8)(s-7)=0$, and $s=7$. Then $n=16$, whose digit sum is indeed $1+6=7$; $16=2\cdot7+2$ and $16\cdot7=112$. The integer is $\boxed{16}$.
