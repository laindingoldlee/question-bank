\textbf{(a)} Each of the $10$ positions can show any of $6$ numbers, independently: $6^{10}=\boxed{60466176}$ sequences.

\textbf{(b)} A record is a list $(n_1,\dots,n_6)$ of nonnegative integers with $n_1+\cdots+n_6=10$, where $n_i$ is the number of blocks showing $i$. By stars and bars there are
\[\dbinom{15}{5}=\boxed{3003}\]
records.
