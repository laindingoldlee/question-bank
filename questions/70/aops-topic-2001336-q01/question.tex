Richard has $10$ identical Integer Blocks: cubes whose six faces show the integers $1$ to $6$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] He puts the blocks in a row and reads the numbers on their top faces from left to right. How many different sequences of $10$ numbers can he obtain?
\item[(b)] He throws the blocks in a pile and records only how many blocks show each number on top. How many different records are possible?
\end{enumerate}
