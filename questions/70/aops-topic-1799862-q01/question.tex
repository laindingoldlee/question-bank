Two players play tic-tac-toe on a $3\times3$ board, X moving first, but neither player uses any strategy: at each move the player chooses one of the empty squares, all empty squares being equally likely. The game stops as soon as a player completes a row, a column or a diagonal of three of his own marks, or when the board is full. What is the probability that the game ends in a tie?
