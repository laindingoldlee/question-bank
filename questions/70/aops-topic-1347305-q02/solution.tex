Since $z=-y$ makes the expression zero ($(-y)^5+y^5=0$), $z+y$ is a factor:
\[
z^5+y^5=\boxed{(z+y)\left(z^4-z^3y+z^2y^2-zy^3+y^4\right)}.
\]
This follows from part 1 by replacing $y$ with $-y$.
