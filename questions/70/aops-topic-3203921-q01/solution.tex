$v(1)=5-10-\dfrac{1}{1000}=\boxed{-\dfrac{5001}{1000}}$. A view count cannot be negative, so this single permitted input disproves the claimed universal interpretation. The formula may be studied as a real-valued function, but it needs a different interpretation or domain restriction to model nonnegative counts.
