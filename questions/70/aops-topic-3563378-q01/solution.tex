Place $A=(0,0)$, $B=(c,0)$, $C=(b\cos A,b\sin A)$. The distance formula gives $a^2=(b\cos A-c)^2+b^2\sin^2A=b^2+c^2-2bc\cos A$, valid for acute, right, and obtuse $A$. If the area is $K$, the cross-product area formula gives $2K=bc\sin A=ca\sin B=ab\sin C$. Divide by $abc$ and invert to obtain the sine-ratio identity. These steps use no assumption that an altitude lies inside the triangle.
\begin{asy}
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(4,0),C=(1,3);draw(A--B--C--cycle);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,N);label("$c$",(A+B)/2,S);label("$b$",(A+C)/2,NW);label("$a$",(B+C)/2,NE);
\end{asy}
