Modulo $8$, $9\equiv1$ and $15\equiv-1$, so
\[
9^5+15^5\equiv1^5+(-1)^5=0\pmod8 .
\]
The remainder is $\boxed{0}$. (In fact $9+15=24$ divides $9^5+15^5$, since $a+b$ divides $a^5+b^5$; and $9^5+15^5=818424=8\cdot102303$.)
