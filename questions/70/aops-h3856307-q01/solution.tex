The powers $3^{n+1}$ cancel in $b_n$. The finite partial sum is
\[
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{n=0}^N\dfrac{b_n}{3^n}
&=\sum_{n=0}^N\left(
\dfrac{\lfloor3^{n+\dfrac{1}{2}}\rfloor}{3^n}
-\dfrac{\lfloor3^{n-\dfrac{1}{2}}\rfloor}{3^{n-1}}\right)\\
&=\dfrac{\lfloor3^{N+\dfrac{1}{2}}\rfloor}{3^N}
-3\lfloor3^{-\dfrac{1}{2}}\rfloor
=\dfrac{\lfloor3^{N+\dfrac{1}{2}}\rfloor}{3^N}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Since $0\le3^{N+\dfrac{1}{2}}-\lfloor3^{N+\dfrac{1}{2}}\rfloor<1$, the final expression differs from $\sqrt3$ by less than $3^{-N}$. Thus the series equals $\boxed{\sqrt3}$.
The final term of each finite telescope tends to $\sqrt3$, not zero. Infinite cancellation that discards it is invalid.
