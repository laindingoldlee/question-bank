For a real number $x$ let $[x]$ denote the integer nearest to $x$ (for example $[5]=5$, $[5.3]=5$, $[6.7]=7$), and let $\lceil x\rceil$ denote the least integer greater than or equal to $x$. How many positive integers $n<100$ satisfy $\left[\dfrac {n}{7}\right]=\left\lceil\dfrac {n}{7}\right\rceil$?
