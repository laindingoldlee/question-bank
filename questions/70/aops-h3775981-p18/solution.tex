The seven increasing digits must be chosen from $1,\ldots,9$, with their order then forced. Since all nine sum to $45$, the chosen sum is divisible by $3$ exactly when the two omitted digits have sum divisible by $3$. There are $\binom32=3$ pairs from $\{3,6,9\}$ and $3\cdot3=9$ pairs with one digit from each of $\{1,4,7\}$ and $\{2,5,8\}$. The answer is $\boxed{12}$.
