Let $p$ be the smallest prime factor of $N$. Then the second divisor is $p$ and the second-to-last is $\dfrac{N}{p}$. Their given ratio implies $\dfrac{N}{p}=2025p$, hence $N=2025p^2=3^45^2p^2$. Because $3$ is a factor, the smallest prime factor satisfies $p\le3$. Thus $p=2$ or $p=3$, giving $\boxed{N=8100\text{ or }18225}$. In each case the indicated $p$ really is the smallest prime factor, so both values work.
