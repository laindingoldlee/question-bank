Let $u=2^x$, $v=3^x$. The left side is $\dfrac{u^3+v^3}{uv(u+v)}=\dfrac{u^2-uv+v^2}{uv}=t-1+\dfrac1t$ with $t=\dfrac uv=\left(\dfrac23\right)^x$. So $t+\dfrac1t=\dfrac{13}6$, i.e. $t=\dfrac23$ or $\dfrac32$, giving $\boxed{x=1\text{ or }x=-1}$.
