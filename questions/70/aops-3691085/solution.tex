There are $\dfrac{11!}{2!2!}$ equally likely distinct strings. First require word lengths at least two: the space cuts the ten-letter string after positions 2 through 8, giving $\dfrac{7\cdot10!}{4}$ arrangements. For dd adjacent inside a word, choose their starting position $i=1,\ldots,9$ in the letter-only string. There are seven space cuts except that the cut between them is forbidden when $2\le i\le8$. This gives $\dfrac{(9\cdot7-7)8!}{2}=1128960$ arrangements; the same holds for ee. For both pairs adjacent, their ordered starting positions $i,j$ must be nonadjacent and unequal. There are 56 choices. Among these, 42 have $i$ internal and 42 have $j$ internal, so the number of allowed cuts sums to $7\cdot56-84=308$. The remaining six distinct letters give $308\cdot6!=221760$ arrangements. Inclusion--exclusion therefore gives $\dfrac{6350400-2\cdot1128960+221760}{\dfrac{11!}{4}}=\boxed{\dfrac{214}{495}}$.
