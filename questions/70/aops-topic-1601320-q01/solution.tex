For $n\ge2$, $5^n$ is divisible by $25$ and is odd, and $5^n\equiv1\pmod4$ (since $5\equiv1\pmod 4$). The number between $0$ and $99$ that is divisible by $25$ and $\equiv1\pmod4$ is $25$. Hence $5^{n}\equiv25\pmod{100}$ for every $n\ge2$; in particular the last two digits of $5^{347}$ are $\boxed{25}$. (Equivalently: $5^2=25$, and $25\cdot5=125$ ends in $25$ again, so the last two digits stay $25$.)
