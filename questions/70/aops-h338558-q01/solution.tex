The pair $(x,y)$ is uniform in a rectangle of area $2$. Its region $y\ge x$ is a right triangle with legs $1$, hence area $\dfrac{1}{2}$. The diagonal boundary has probability zero, so
\[
\boxed{\Pr(x>y)=1-\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{2}}{2}=\dfrac{3}{4}}.
\]
