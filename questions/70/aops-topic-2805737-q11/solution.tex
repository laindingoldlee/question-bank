Triangles $BEF$ and $BAC$ have the same altitude to line $AC$, and $EF=\dfrac{1}{3}AC$. Therefore $[BEF]=\dfrac{1}{3}[BAC]=\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{8\cdot3}{2}=\boxed4$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(8,0),C=(8,3),D=(0,3),E0=C/3,F=2*C/3;draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C);filldraw(B--E0--F--cycle,lightgray);label("A",A,SW);label("B",B,SE);label("C",C,NE);label("D",D,NW);label("E",E0,NW);label("F",F,NW);
\end{asy}
