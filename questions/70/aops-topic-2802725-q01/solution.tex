The exterior angles of a convex polygon are positive and sum to $360^\circ$. Each of the $n-4$ non-obtuse interior angles has exterior angle at least $90^\circ$, and the four obtuse ones have positive exterior angles, so $90(n-4)<360$, i.e. $n\le7$. For $n=7$ take three right angles and four interior angles of $157.5^\circ$ (exterior angles $90,90,90,22.5,22.5,22.5,22.5$, summing to $360$); such a convex heptagon exists. So the answer is $\boxed{7}$.
