The number is $ 32u $ with $ u $ odd. Since it is divisible by 3, $ u $ is an odd multiple of 3. The bound $ 32u<150 $ gives $ u<\dfrac{75}{16}<5 $, so $ u=3 $ and the number is $\boxed{96}$.
