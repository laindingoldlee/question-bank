Twenty-three players may agree on a strategy, then communicate only by visiting a room containing a statue pointing north, east, south, or west. Its initial direction is unknown. On each visit the selected player must rotate it by $90^\circ$ in either direction, or end the game. Devise a strategy ensuring that ending the game certifies everyone has visited. Assume every player is selected infinitely often if the game has not ended, and show that the strategy eventually ends.
