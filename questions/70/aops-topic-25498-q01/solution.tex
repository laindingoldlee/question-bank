Numerators and denominators are consecutive Fibonacci numbers ($F_1=F_2=1$): the $n$th term is $\dfrac{F_n}{F_{n+1}}$. If the limit is $L$, then $\dfrac{F_{n+1}}{F_{n+2}}=\dfrac{F_{n+1}}{F_{n+1}+F_n}=\dfrac1{1+\dfrac{F_n}{F_{n+1}}}$, so $L=\dfrac1{1+L}$, i.e. $L^2+L-1=0$ and $L=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt5-1}2}$. (Convergence follows from Binet's formula.)
