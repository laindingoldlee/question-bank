The expression factors as $(21!+14)(14!+21)$. Now $2\mid21!+14$, $7\mid21!+14$, and $3\mid14!+21$. Neither factor is divisible by $5$: $21!+14\equiv4$ and $14!+21\equiv1\pmod5$. So the three smallest prime factors are $2,3,7$, with product $\boxed{42}$.
