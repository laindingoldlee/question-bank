A right triangle with sides $3$, $4$, $5$ is drawn. Then a right triangle with sides $5$, $12$, $13$ is drawn so that its leg of length $5$ is the hypotenuse of the first triangle. Prove that this can be continued forever: at every stage there is a right triangle with integer sides one of whose legs is the hypotenuse of the triangle drawn last.
