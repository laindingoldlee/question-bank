$n\equiv4\pmod{10}$ means $n$ ends in $4$. Then $n-8$ ends in $6$ and is a multiple of $11$. Since $10\le n\le99$, $n-8$ lies between $2$ and $91$, and the only multiple of $11$ there ending in $6$ is $66$. So $n=\boxed{74}$. (Indeed $74=6\cdot11+8$.)
