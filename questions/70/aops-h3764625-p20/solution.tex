Every pair of corner cells shares a row, column, or main diagonal, so all four corners need different colors. The center shares such a line with every corner and needs a fifth color. The displayed coloring meets all requirements with five colors, proving the minimum is $\boxed5$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
string[][] a={{"1", "3", "2"}, {"2", "5", "1"}, {"3", "1", "4"}};
for(int r=0;r<a.length;++r)for(int c=0;c<a[r].length;++c){draw(box((c,-r),(c+1,1-r)));label(a[r][c],(c+.5,.5-r));}label("five colors, represented by numbers",(1.5,-3.2));
\end{asy}
