For a rooted full binary subtree of depth $h$, let $g_h$ count choices that may include its root but never include an ancestor and descendant. Either choose the root alone or independently choose from its two child subtrees, so $g_h=1+g_{h-1}^2$, with $g_0=2$ (choose the leaf or not). This gives $g_1=5,g_2=26,g_3=677$. The nonempty strings split into independent subtrees starting in 0 and 1, each of depth 3. Therefore the answer is $g_3^2=\boxed{458329}$.
