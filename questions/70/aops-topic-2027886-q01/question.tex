Thomas the bug starts at $(0,0)$ in the coordinate plane. He travels by making moves: on his $k$th move he turns $30^\circ$ counterclockwise and then walks forward $2k$ units. His first move takes him from $(0,0)$ to $(\sqrt3,1)$. Through how many unit squares of the grid (squares with corners at lattice points) will his path have passed, by the end of his $5$th move? (A square counts if the path passes through its interior.)
