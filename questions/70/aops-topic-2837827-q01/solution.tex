The roots of $x^2-x-1$ are $\varphi,\psi=\dfrac{1\pm\sqrt5}2$, and each satisfies $x^n=F_nx+F_{n-1}$; in particular $x^4=3x+2$ and $x^5=5x+3$. So at each root,
\[a(5x+3)+b(3x+2)+1=(5a+3b)x+(3a+2b+1)=0.\]
A linear polynomial with two distinct roots is zero, so $5a+3b=0$ and $3a+2b+1=0$. Solving, $\boxed{a=3,\ b=-5}$. (Check: $3x^5-5x^4+1=(x^2-x-1)(3x^3-2x^2+x-1)$.)
