The original polynomial is $P(x)=x^2-(6+2i)x+(11+10i)$. Conjugating a root equation shows that the conjugate roots are precisely the roots of the coefficientwise conjugate polynomial. Thus the answer is $\boxed{x^2-(6-2i)x+11-10i}$.
