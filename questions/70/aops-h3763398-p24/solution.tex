Write the annual counts as $a<b<c<d<3a$. Their sum is $4a+b+c+d=31$. Since $b+c+d\ge3a+6$, $7a+6\le31$, so $a\le3$. Since $b+c+d\le9a-6$, $13a-6\ge31$, so $a\ge3$. Hence $a=3$, and $b,c,d$ are three distinct numbers from 4 through 8 with sum 19. The possibilities are $(4,7,8)$ and $(5,6,8)$, both giving $\boxed{d=8}$.
