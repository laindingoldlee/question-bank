Peter and Dave play a game. $n$ coins are placed in a line, and each coin has a value that is a positive real number. Starting with Peter, they alternately take one of the two coins at the ends of the line, until all coins are taken. Peter wins if the total value of his coins is less than or equal to the total value of Dave's coins; otherwise Dave wins. For how many integers $n$ with $2\le n\le2020$ does Peter have a winning strategy for every possible assignment of values?
