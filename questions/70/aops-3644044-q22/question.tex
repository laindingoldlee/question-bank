Danny and Jonny are playing a game. To begin, Danny chooses a number between $2$ and $n$, and after this all of that number's divisors other than $1$ become restricted, meaning that they or any of their multiples cannot be picked for the rest of the game. Next, Jonny chooses a number that is not restricted or a multiple of a restricted number, and the process repeats until someone cannot choose numbers anymore. Let $S$ be the set of integers between $2$ and $30$ such that Danny has a winning strategy. Find the sum of all elements in $S$.
