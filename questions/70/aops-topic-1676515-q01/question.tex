Here is a ``proof'' that an elephant weighs as much as a mosquito. Let $x$ be the weight of the elephant and $y$ the weight of the mosquito, and let $x+y=2v$. Then $x-2v=-y$ and $x=-y+2v$. Multiplying these two equations gives $x^2-2vx=y^2-2vy$. Adding $v^2$ to both sides, $x^2-2vx+v^2=y^2-2vy+v^2$, that is, $(x-v)^2=(y-v)^2$. Taking square roots, $x-v=y-v$, so $x=y$. What is wrong?
