By symmetry, $P=\dfrac{1-\Pr(R=B)}{2}$. For one cell, the generating function of red count minus blue count is $\dfrac{z+2+z^{-1}}{4}$. For $49$ independent cells, the zero-difference probability is the constant coefficient of $4^{-49}z^{-49}(1+z)^{98}$, namely $\dfrac{\binom{98}{49}}{2^{98}}$. Hence $2^{100}P=2^{99}-2\binom{98}{49}$. Integer evaluation modulo $1000$ gives $\boxed{488}$.
