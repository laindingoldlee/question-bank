While doing Math Kangaroo homework, Angeline is painting a $7\times 7$ grid where all of the squares are initially white. For every square on the grid, there is a $\dfrac{1}{4}$ probability that Angeline paints the square blue, a $\dfrac{1}{4}$ probability that she paints the square red, a $\dfrac{1}{4}$ probability she paints the square purple, and a $\dfrac{1}{4}$ probability she leaves it white. Let $P$ be the probability that there are more red squares than blue squares. Find the remainder when $2^{100}\cdot P$ is divided by $1000$.
