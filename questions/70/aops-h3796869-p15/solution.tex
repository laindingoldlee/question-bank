Use a unit circumradius temporarily. Each regular hexagon has area $\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}$. Two consecutive hexagons overlap in a rhombus with diagonals one and $\sqrt3$, hence area $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$, one-third of a hexagon. Each overlap therefore has actual area four. The first and third hexagons meet only at one vertex. Their union area is $3\cdot12-2\cdot4=\boxed{28\text{ cm}^2}$.
