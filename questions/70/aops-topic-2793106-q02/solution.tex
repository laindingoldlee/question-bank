Five suffice. Accumulate boxes into a truck until its load first exceeds 20 tons; that load is at most 30 because the final box weighs at most 10. Repeat while the remaining total exceeds 20. There can be at most 4 completed loads exceeding 20, since 5 would exceed the total 100; any remainder fits a fifth truck. Four do not always suffice: take 13 boxes each of weight $\dfrac{100}{13}$. Four boxes weigh more than 30, so each truck carries at most 3 boxes, requiring 5 trucks. Thus $\boxed5$.
