In a geometric sequence of positive terms, $a_2=5$ and $a_8=320$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find $a_4$.
\item[(b)] Show that in general, for a geometric sequence of positive terms and indices $m<k<n$,
\[
a_k=a_m^{\dfrac{n-k}{n-m}}\,a_n^{\dfrac{k-m}{n-m}} ,
\]
so that a term between two known terms is found by ``weighting'' them according to how far it is from each.
\end{enumerate}
