Let $n$ be a positive integer and $k$ a nonnegative integer. Prove that
\[
\sum_{i=0}^{k}(-1)^i\dbinom ni=(-1)^k\dbinom{n-1}{k},
\]
where $\dbinom{m}{j}=0$ for $j>m$.
