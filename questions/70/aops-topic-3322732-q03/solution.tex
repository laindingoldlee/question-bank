In the initial six positions the B count is zero and the A count positive, so no prefix qualifies. During the B block, the A count is $6$: B must occur $3$ or $12$ times, giving $n=9,18$. During the final A block, the B count is $24$: the A count must be $12$ or $48$, requiring $6$ or $42$ additional A's after position $30$. This gives $n=36,72$. The sum is $9+18+36+72=\boxed{135}$.
