Electricity costs $\$200$ per $25$ kWh. Solar panels cost $\$18500$ for a $1500$ sq.\ ft.\ house, a one-time payment, and the cost is proportional to the area of the house. A $2500$ sq.\ ft.\ house uses $900$ kWh per month. After how many whole months will installing solar panels have cost less than paying for electricity?
