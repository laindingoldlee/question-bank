Electricity costs $\dfrac{200}{25}=\$8$ per kWh, so $900$ kWh costs $\$7200$ per month. The panels for $2500$ sq.\ ft.\ cost $18500\cdot\dfrac{2500}{1500}\approx\$30833$. After $4$ months the bills total $\$28800$ (less than the panels); after $5$ months they total $\$36000$ (more). So after $\boxed{5}$ months the panels are the cheaper option.
