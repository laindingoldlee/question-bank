In $8(4c)$ the only operation is multiplication: $8\cdot(4\cdot c)=(8\cdot4)\cdot c=32c$ by the associative law. No addition is involved, so the distributive law is not needed.

In $8(4+c)$, $8$ multiplies a sum, and the distributive law $a(b+c)=ab+ac$ is exactly what lets us multiply each term: $8(4+c)=8\cdot4+8\cdot c=32+8c$.

So $8(4c)=\boxed{32c}$, $8(4+c)=\boxed{32+8c}$, and the distributive law is needed for $8(4+c)$. (A common mistake is to ``distribute'' in $8(4c)$ and get $32\cdot8c$; check with $c=1$: $8(4\cdot1)=32$, not $256$.)
