The required heads and required tails are disjoint sets of coins, so $\dfrac{2}{3}x+\dfrac{4}{5}x\le2024$. Hence $x\le\dfrac{2024\cdot15}{22}=1380$. This is attained by placing $920$ heads followed by $1104$ tails: the first $1380$ include $920$ heads and the last $1380$ include $1104$ tails. Therefore $\boxed{1380}$.
