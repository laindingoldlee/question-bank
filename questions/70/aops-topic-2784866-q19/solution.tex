The combined primitive ratio is $ A:B:C=15:40:88 $. Since these three integers have gcd 1, every integer triple in this ratio is $(15t,40t,88t)$ for a positive integer $ t $. Its sum is 143 t, and $ 33\mid143t $ is equivalent to $ 3\mid t $. The least $ t $ is 3, giving $\boxed{B=120}$.
