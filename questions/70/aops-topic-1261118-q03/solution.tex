There are $\dbinom53=10$ ways to choose three coins. Their sums (in cents) are
\[16,\ 31,\ 36,\ 40,\ 106,\ 111,\ 115,\ 126,\ 130,\ 135,\]
all different (the sums without the dollar coin are below $100$, and those with it are $100$ plus a different pair-sum $6,11,15,26,30,35$). So there are $\boxed{10}$ possible sums.
