Inverting the given equation, $\dfrac{a^2+1}{a}=a+\dfrac1a=\dfrac{20}{\sqrt2}=10\sqrt2$. Then
\[\left(a-\dfrac1a\right)^2=\left(a+\dfrac1a\right)^2-4=200-4=196,\]
and $a-\dfrac1a>0$ because $a>1$, so $a-\dfrac1a=14$. Hence
\[\dfrac{14a}{a^2-1}=\dfrac{14}{a-\dfrac1a}=\dfrac{14}{14}=\boxed{1}.\]
