Write the integers in decreasing order $a_1\ge\cdots\ge a_7$. If $a_1+a_2+a_3\le43$, then $a_3\le14$, so the total is at most $43+4(14)=99$, a contradiction. Thus the three largest have sum at least $44$. The list $15,15,14,14,14,14,14$ has total $100$ and no three have sum greater than $44$. Therefore $\boxed{L=44}$.
