On $|z|=1$, the leading term $81z^3$ dominates the other terms, whose total modulus is at most $19+18+40=77$. Rouch\'e's theorem therefore places all three roots inside the unit disk, justifying the geometric series. Their product is $\dfrac{1000}{[(1+a)(1+b)(1+c)]}$. Evaluating the polynomial at $-1$ gives $-81(1+a)(1+b)(1+c)=-40$, so the expression is $1000\cdot\dfrac{81}{40}=\boxed{2025}$.
