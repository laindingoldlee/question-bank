Let $y$ be the value. The expression repeats after two levels, so
\[y=\sqrt{2\sqrt{3y}}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad y^2=2\sqrt{3y}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad y^4=12y.\]
Since $y>0$, $y^3=12$, so $y=\sqrt[3]{12}$ and $x=\boxed{12}$.
