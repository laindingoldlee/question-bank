Since $7$ is prime, $z$ is primitive. Set $t_j=z^j+z^{-j}$ for $j=1,2,3$. The six nontrivial roots occur in these three inverse pairs. Dividing $1+z+\cdots+z^6=0$ by $z^3$ shows that $t=z+z^{-1}$ satisfies
\[
1+t+(t^2-2)+(t^3-3t)=0,
\]
or $t^3+t^2-2t-1=0$. The same holds for the distinct values $t_1,t_2,t_3$. By Vieta, their pairwise-product sum is $-2$ and their product is $1$. The required sum is consequently
\[
\sum_{j=1}^3\dfrac{1}{t_j}=\dfrac{-2}{1}=\boxed{-2}.
\]
