Adding the equations $x_k-x_{k-1}=k$ for $k=1,2,\dots,n$, the left side telescopes:
\[
x_n-x_0=1+2+\cdots+n=\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}.
\]
Hence
\[
\boxed{x_n=7+\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}=\dfrac{n^2+n+14}{2}} .
\]
Check: $x_1=8$, $x_2=10$, $x_3=13$, as the recursion gives.
