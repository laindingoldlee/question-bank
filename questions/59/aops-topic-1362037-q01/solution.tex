We have $999{,}999{,}999=10^9-1$. Since $10^9=2^9\cdot5^9$ is a multiple of $2^5=32$, $10^9\equiv0\pmod{32}$, so
\[
999{,}999{,}999\equiv-1\equiv\boxed{31}\pmod{32}.
\]
(The general trick: a number is congruent modulo $2^k$ to the number formed by its last $k$ digits, because $10^k$ is a multiple of $2^k$. Here the last five digits give $99999=32\cdot3124+31$.)
