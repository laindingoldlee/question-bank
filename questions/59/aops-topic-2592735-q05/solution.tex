Let $n=\overline{CD}$. Then $n^2=100\cdot\overline{AB}+n$, so $100\mid n(n-1)$, and $n^2\ge1000$ gives $n\ge32$.

As $n$ and $n-1$ are coprime, $25$ divides $n$ or $n-1$, and $4$ divides $n$ or $n-1$. Modulo $100$ this leaves $n\equiv0,1,25$ or $76$. The only two-digit $n\ge32$ among these is $n=76$, and indeed $76^2=5776$. So $A=5$, $B=7$, $C=7$, $D=6$, and
\[A+B+C+D=\boxed{25}.\]
