Billy has built a straight fence $60$ feet long, with a post at each end and one every $10$ feet. He starts at a randomly selected post and paints it completely. He then walks to another unpainted post (not necessarily a neighbouring one) and paints it, and so on, until all posts are painted. What is the maximum distance, in feet, that Billy could walk while painting the posts? (A student got $210$; the given answer is $230$.)
