The trains approach each other at the relative speed $20+40=60$ mph $=60\cdot\dfrac{5280}{3600}=88$ feet per second. From the moment the fronts meet until the backs separate, the total relative distance covered is the sum of the two lengths. In $30$ seconds this is $88\cdot30=2640$ feet, so the second train is $2640-1000=\boxed{1640}$ feet long.
