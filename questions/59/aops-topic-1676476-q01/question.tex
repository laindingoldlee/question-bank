A student writes
\[
-1=\sqrt{-1}\cdot\sqrt{-1}=\sqrt{(-1)(-1)}=\sqrt{1}=1
\]
and concludes that a positive number can equal its negation. Find the error, and prove that no positive number equals its negation.
