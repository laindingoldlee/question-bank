In a unit circle with centre $O$, points $D$ and $A$ are opposite ends of a diameter. Let $R$ be the midpoint of $DO$, and let $S$ be the midpoint of $DR$. A line perpendicular to $DA$ through $S$ meets the circle at $B$ and $C$. Is triangle $ABC$ equilateral? Would the answer change if the perpendicular were drawn through $R$ instead? Justify both answers.
