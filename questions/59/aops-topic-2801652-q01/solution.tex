On $y=x$ the equation becomes $\dfrac{x^2-x}{x+2}=a$, i.e. $x^2-(1+a)x-2a=0$ (with $x\ne-2$; $x=-2$ is never a root since it gives $6=0$). Its roots $x_1,x_2$ give points $(x_i,x_i)$ at distance $\sqrt2|x_1-x_2|=6$, so $(x_1-x_2)^2=18$. By Vieta, $(x_1-x_2)^2=(1+a)^2+8a$, so $a^2+10a-17=0$ and $a=\boxed{\sqrt{42}-5}$ (the positive root).
