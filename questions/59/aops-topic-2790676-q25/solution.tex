The odd part must be an odd prime $ p $: if composite, its smallest prime divisor would be smaller than the odd part. Thus the numbers are $ 2^ap $ with $ a\ge1 $ and $ p $ odd prime. For $ a=1 $, there are 8 choices $ p=3,5,7,11,13,17,19,23 $; for $ a=2 $, four choices 3, 5, 7, 11; for $ a=3 $, two choices 3, 5; and for $ a=4 $, one choice 3. Larger $ a $ are impossible. The count is $\boxed{15}$.
