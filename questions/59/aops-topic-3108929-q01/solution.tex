Place the triangle at $(0,0),(3,0),(0,4)$. Its hypotenuse has equation $ 4x+3y=12 $. The opposite square vertex is $(s,s)$, so $ 7s=12 $ and $\boxed{s=\dfrac{12}{7}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(3,0),C=(0,4);real s=12/7;draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--(s,0)--(s,s)--(0,s)--cycle);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,N);label("$3$",(1.5,0),S);label("$4$",(0,2),W);label("$s$",(s,s/2),E);
\end{asy}
