Let $n$ be a positive integer such that $\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor-2$ divides $n-4$ and $\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor+2$ divides $n+4$. Find the greatest such $n$ less than $1000$. (Here $\lfloor x\rfloor$ is the greatest integer less than or equal to $x$; a divisor must be nonzero.)
