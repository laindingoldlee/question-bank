Let $AC=t$ and $\angle BAC=\angle CAD=\theta$. The cosine rule in ABC and ADC gives $t^2+25-10 t\cos\theta=16$ and $t^2+64-16 t\cos\theta=25$. Subtracting gives $6 t\cos\theta=30$, so $t\cos\theta=5$. Substituting back gives $t^2=41$, hence $t\sin\theta=\sqrt{41-25}=4$. Since convexity puts B and D on opposite sides of AC, the area is the sum of the two triangle areas:\[\dfrac{1}{2} t(5+8)\sin\theta=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot 13\cdot 4=\boxed{26}.\]
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
import geometry;size(240);
real t=sqrt(41);pair A=(0,0),C=(t,0),B=(25/t,20/t),D=(40/t,-32/t);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C,dashed);label("$A$",A,W);label("$B$",B,N);label("$C$",C,E);label("$D$",D,S);label("$5$",(A+B)/2,NW);label("$4$",(B+C)/2,NE);label("$5$",(C+D)/2,E);label("$8$",(A+D)/2,SW);
\end{asy}
