The surface is an ellipsoid centered at the origin. Setting two coordinates to zero gives intercepts $x=\pm2$, $y=\pm3$, and $z=\pm4$. Thus its semiaxis lengths are $\boxed{2,3,4}$. It is obtained by scaling a unit sphere independently by these factors along the three axes.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
path e=ellipse((0,0),2,3);draw(e);draw(ellipse((0,0),2,.65),dashed);draw(ellipse((0,0),.75,3),dashed);draw((0,0)--(2,0));draw((0,0)--(0,3));draw((0,0)--(-.6,-.6));label("$2$",(1,0),S);label("$4$",(0,1.5),E);label("$3$",(-.6,-.6),SW);label("schematic projection",(0,-3.5));
\end{asy}
