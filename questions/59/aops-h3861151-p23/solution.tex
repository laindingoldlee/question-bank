Let the common side length be $s$. The vectors $\overrightarrow{AB}$ and $\overrightarrow{AC}$ have dot product $\dfrac{s^2}{2}$, since their angle is $60^\circ$. Their projected components are perpendicular, so their dot product comes entirely from the height changes: $(23-37)(16-37)=294$. Thus $\boxed{AB^2=588}$. The diagram shows the orthogonal projection; labels specify the three heights.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(sqrt(392),0),C=(0,sqrt(147));draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw((1,0)--(1,1)--(0,1));label("$A\prime\ (h=37)$",A,(-1,-1));label("$B\prime\ (h=23)$",B,(1,-1));label("$C\prime\ (h=16)$",C,(0,1));
\end{asy}
