\textbf{(a)} $97=0\cdot144+8\cdot12+1$, so $97=81_{12}$. The alien raises $0$ fingers on the left hand, $8$ on the middle hand and $1$ on the right hand.

\textbf{(b)} The largest three-digit base-$12$ number has all digits $11$:
\[11\cdot144+11\cdot12+11=12^3-1=\boxed{1727}.\]
