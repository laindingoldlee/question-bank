Quadrilateral $ABCD$ is inscribed in circle $O$ with $AB=3$, $BC=2$, $CD=6$ and $DA=8$. Points $X$ and $Y$ lie on $\overline{BD}$ with $\dfrac{DX}{BD}=\dfrac14$ and $\dfrac{BY}{BD}=\dfrac{11}{36}$. Let $E$ be the intersection of line $AX$ with the line through $Y$ parallel to $\overline{AD}$, and $F$ the intersection of line $CX$ with the line through $E$ parallel to $\overline{AC}$. Let $G$ be the point of circle $O$ other than $C$ on line $CX$. What is $XF\cdot XG$?

$\textbf{(A) }17\qquad\textbf{(B) }\dfrac{59-5\sqrt2}{3}\qquad\textbf{(C) }\dfrac{91-12\sqrt3}{4}\qquad\textbf{(D) }\dfrac{67-10\sqrt2}{3}\qquad\textbf{(E) }18$
