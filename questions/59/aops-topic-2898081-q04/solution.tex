Let $S=A+B+C+D+E$. The equations say $3A=S$, $4B=S$, $5C=S$, $6D=S$. Then $S=\dfrac S3+\dfrac S4+\dfrac S5+\dfrac S6+6$, so $S\left(1-\dfrac{19}{20}\right)=6$ and $S=120$. Hence $\boxed{A=40,\ B=30,\ C=24,\ D=20,\ E=6}$.
