$10a+b\equiv0\pmod7\iff-20a-2b\equiv0\iff a-2b\equiv0\pmod7$, since $-20\equiv1\pmod7$ and multiplying by $-2$ is reversible ($\gcd(2,7)=1$).

$10a+b\equiv0\pmod{13}\iff40a+4b\equiv0\iff a+4b\equiv0\pmod{13}$, since $40\equiv1\pmod{13}$ and $\gcd(4,13)=1$. $\blacksquare$

(The same method works for any $m$ coprime to $10$: multiply by an inverse of $10$ modulo $m$, e.g. $17\mid N\iff17\mid a-5b$ and $19\mid N\iff19\mid a+2b$.)
