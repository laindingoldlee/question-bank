Convex hexagons $H_n$ and squares $S_n$ are nested in the order $H_1\supset S_1\supset H_2\supset S_2\supset\cdots$, each new polygon inscribed in the preceding one. Their areas are $h_n,s_n$. Given $h_1s_1=24\sqrt3-41$, does $(\sum h_n)(\sum s_n)$ have a finite maximum? Hexagons need not be regular.
