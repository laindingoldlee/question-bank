A cyclist rides over a route which is $\dfrac13$ uphill, $\dfrac13$ on level ground and $\dfrac13$ downhill. He covers the uphill part at $16$ miles per hour and the level part at $24$ miles per hour. At what speed, in miles per hour, must he ride the downhill part so that his average speed for the whole route is $24$ miles per hour?
