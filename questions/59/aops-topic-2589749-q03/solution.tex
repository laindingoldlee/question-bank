Let each third of the route be $d$ miles and the downhill speed $v$. The total time must be $\dfrac{3d}{24}=\dfrac d8$ hours, so
\[\dfrac{d}{16}+\dfrac{d}{24}+\dfrac dv=\dfrac d8\iff\dfrac1v=\dfrac18-\dfrac1{16}-\dfrac1{24}=\dfrac{6-3-2}{48}=\dfrac1{48}.\]
So he must ride downhill at $\boxed{48}$ miles per hour.
