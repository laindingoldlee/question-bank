Let the set cost $T$ cents. If the first brother paid $x$, the others paid $T-x$, and $x=\dfrac{T-x}{2}$ gives $x=\dfrac {T}{3}$. In the same way the second paid $y$ with $y=\dfrac{T-y}{3}$, so $y=\dfrac {T}{4}$, and the third paid $z$ with $z=\dfrac{T-z}{4}$, so $z=\dfrac {T}{5}$. The fourth paid the rest:
\[
T-\dfrac {T}{3}-\dfrac {T}{4}-\dfrac {T}{5}=\dfrac{13T}{60}=130 ,
\]
so $T=600$. The ball set cost $\boxed{600\text{ cents}}$, that is $\$6$. (The brothers paid $200$, $150$, $120$ and $130$ cents; for example, $200$ is half of $150+120+130=400$.)
