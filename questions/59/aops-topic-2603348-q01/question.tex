Give an example of two triangles $ABC$ and $XYZ$ such that
\[\dfrac{AB}{XY}=\dfrac{BC}{YZ}\qquad\text{and}\qquad\angle BCA=\angle YZX,\]
but the triangles are not similar. Prove that your example works. (So ``SSA'' is not a similarity test.)
