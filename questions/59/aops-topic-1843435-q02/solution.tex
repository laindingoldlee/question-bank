Factor: $x^2+5x-84=(x+12)(x-7)$, since $12\cdot(-7)=-84$ and $12-7=5$. So $g(x)=0$ exactly when $x=-12$ or $x=7$, and $g(0)=-84$. The $x$-intercepts are $\boxed{(-12,0)\text{ and }(7,0)}$ and the $y$-intercept is $\boxed{(0,-84)}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
size(7cm,5cm,IgnoreAspect);
real g(real x){return x^2+5*x-84;}
draw((-15.5,0)--(10.5,0),gray(0.6),Arrow(5));
draw((0,-100)--(0,50),gray(0.6),Arrow(5));
draw(graph(g,-14.2,9.2),blue+linewidth(0.9));
dot((-12,0));dot((7,0));dot((0,-84));
label("$(-12,0)$",(-12,0),SW);label("$(7,0)$",(7,0),SE);label("$(0,-84)$",(0,-84),SE);
label("$x$",(10.5,0),E);label("$y$",(0,50),N);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

(The graph is drawn with a compressed vertical scale.)
