With $n$ people, each in $3$ handshakes, there are $\dfrac{3n}{2}$ handshakes. So $n$ is even and $\dfrac{3n}{2}<15$, i.e. $n<10$; hence $n\le8$. Eight people suffice: label them as the vertices of a cube and let each shake hands with the three people on adjacent vertices ($12$ handshakes). The maximum is $\boxed{8}$.
