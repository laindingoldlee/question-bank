Work from the inside out. First $x-\dfrac{30x}{100}=0.7x$. Then
\[
0.7x-\dfrac{20\cdot0.7x}{100}=0.7x-0.14x=0.56x ,
\]
and finally $0.56x-0.5x=0.06x$. So the expression equals $\boxed{\dfrac{3x}{50}}$ (that is, $6\%$ of $x$).
