The first condition gives the first elementary symmetric sum $e_1=0$. Multiplying the reciprocal condition by $abcd\ne0$ gives the third symmetric sum $e_3=0$. The polynomial with these four roots is therefore
\[
Q(t)=(t-a)(t-b)(t-c)(t-d)=t^4+e_2t^2+e_4,
\]
an even polynomial. Hence $Q(-a)=Q(a)=0$, so $-a$ is one of the other three roots (because $a\ne0$). This supplies the required pair.
