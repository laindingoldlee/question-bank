Lines $AB$ and $DC$ are parallel, and transversals $AC$ and $BD$ intersect at a point $X$ between the two lines such that $\dfrac{AX}{CX}=\dfrac57$. Points $P$ and $Q$ lie on segments $AB$ and $DC$, respectively. The segment $PQ$ intersects transversals $BD$ and $AC$ at points $M$ and $N$, respectively, so that $PM=MN=NQ$. What is the ratio $\dfrac{AP}{BP}$? Express your answer as a common fraction.
