The numbers $100$ and $600$ contain no $3$, so it does not matter whether they are included; count $100$ to $599$.
\begin{itemize}
\item Hundreds digit $3$: all $100$ numbers from $300$ to $399$.
\item Hundreds digit $1$, $2$, $4$ or $5$: the last two digits $00$ to $99$ contain a $3$ in $100-9\cdot9=19$ cases, so $4\cdot19=76$ numbers.
\end{itemize}
The total is $100+76=\boxed{176}$. The student counted $19$ for all five hundreds, including the $300$s, and then added the $100$ numbers of the $300$s again; the $19$ numbers from $300$ to $399$ were counted twice.
