Every vertical cross-section has length 5, over horizontal width 10. By Cavalieri's principle, or integration, area is $\int_0^{10}5\,dx=\boxed{50}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
import graph;real f(real x){return sin(x*pi/5);}draw(graph(f,0,10),blue);real g(real x){return f(x)+5;}draw(graph(g,0,10),blue);draw((0,0)--(0,5));draw((10,0)--(10,5));label("$5$",(0,2.5),W);label("$10$",(5,-1),S);
\end{asy}
