For tens digit $d$ the units digit can be $d-1$, $d$ or $d+1$, if it is a digit. That gives $3$ choices for $d=1,\dots,8$ and $2$ choices for $d=9$ (units $8$ or $9$): $8\cdot3+2=\boxed{26}$. (For $d=1$, units $0,1,2$ are all allowed.)
