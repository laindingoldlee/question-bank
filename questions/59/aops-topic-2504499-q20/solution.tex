Split $108=5\cdot21+3$. Each of the $21$ blocks of five days has at most $3$ games, and the last three days at most $2$ (not all three), so at most $63+2=65$.

The pattern $XOXOX$ repeated $21$ times, then $XOX$ (where $X$ is a game day) achieves $65$: there are never three consecutive game days, and every window of five days contains exactly three game days. So the answer is $\boxed{65}$.
