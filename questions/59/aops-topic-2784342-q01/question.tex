Xera and Yeta use this method to decide who sits in the front passenger seat. Xera throws a standard six-sided die, after which Yeta picks a card from a standard $52$-card deck, with replacement. They keep alternating in this way until either Xera wins by rolling a four or Yeta wins by picking a four. What is the probability that Yeta wins? Express your answer as a common fraction.
