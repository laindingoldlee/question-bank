In each round Xera wins with probability $\dfrac16$; if not, Yeta wins with probability $\dfrac4{52}=\dfrac1{13}$. A round with no winner has probability $\dfrac56\cdot\dfrac{12}{13}=\dfrac{60}{78}$. So
\[P(\text{Yeta})=\sum_{j\ge1}\left(\dfrac{60}{78}\right)^{j-1}\dfrac56\cdot\dfrac1{13}=\dfrac{\dfrac5{78}}{1-\dfrac{60}{78}}=\dfrac5{18}.\]
The answer is $\boxed{\dfrac5{18}}$.
