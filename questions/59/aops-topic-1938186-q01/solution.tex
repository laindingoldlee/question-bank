The $k$th repunit is $\dfrac{10^k-1}{9}$, so
\[
S=\dfrac{1}{9}\sum_{k=1}^{100}(10^k-1)=\dfrac{1}{9}\left(\dfrac{10^{101}-10}{9}-100\right)=\dfrac{10^{101}-910}{81}.
\]
Since $81\cdot12345679=999999999=10^9-1$, the number $\dfrac{10^{99}-1}{81}$ has the $99$-digit expansion $\underbrace{012345679\,012345679\cdots012345679}_{11\text{ blocks}}$ (with a leading zero). Then
\[
S=\dfrac{100(10^{99}-1)+100-910}{81}=100\cdot\dfrac{10^{99}-1}{81}-10 .
\]
Multiplying by $100$ appends $00$, and subtracting $10$ changes the final $\ldots67900$ into $\ldots67890$. So $S$ consists of ten blocks $123456790$ followed by $1234567890$, i.e.
\[
S=123456790\,123456790\cdots123456790\,1234567890 .
\]
Each block $123456790$ has digit sum $37$, and $1234567890$ has digit sum $45$. The digit sum of $S$ is $10\cdot37+45=\boxed{415}$.
