The centre distance is 33 and the difference of radii is 7, so the common external tangent length is $AB=\sqrt{33^2-7^2}=\sqrt{1040}=4\sqrt{65}$. Equal tangents from $X$ to the first circle give $XA=XT$, and to the second give $XB=XT$. Thus $X$ is the midpoint of $AB$, yielding $\boxed{AX=2\sqrt{65}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

pair O=(0,0),P=(33,0),T=(20,0);real c=7/33,s=sqrt(1-c*c);pair A=20*(c,s),B=P+13*(c,s),X=(A+B)/2;draw(circle(O,20));draw(circle(P,13));draw(A--B);draw(T--X);label("$O$",O,W);label("$P$",P,E);label("$T$",T,S);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,N);label("$X$",X,N);
\end{asy}
