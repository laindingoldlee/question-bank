The allowed digits are $\{0,1,4,5,6,9\}$. Pad each value from $0$ to $999$ to three digits. The allowed digits have residue counts $3,2,1$ modulo $3$. A triple has sum divisible by $3$ if all residues agree or all are distinct, giving
\[
3^3+2^3+1^3+6\cdot3\cdot2\cdot1=72.
\]
A sum divisible by both $3$ and $8$ must be $0$ or $24$, since the largest sum is $27$. Sum zero gives $000$; sum $24$ gives exactly the three permutations of $9,9,6$. Excluding these yields $\boxed{68}$ positive integers.
