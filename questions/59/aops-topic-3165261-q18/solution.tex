Cassini's identity gives $F_{2j-1}F_{2j+1}=F_{2j}^2+1$. It follows that $\dfrac{1}{F_{2j}+\dfrac{1}{F_{2j}}}=\dfrac{F_{2j}}{F_{2j-1}F_{2j+1}}=\dfrac{1}{F_{2j-1}}-\dfrac{1}{F_{2j+1}}$, since $F_{2j+1}-F_{2j-1}=F_{2j}$. Thus partial sums telescope to $\dfrac{1}{F_5}-\dfrac{1}{F_{2N+1}}$. The final term tends to zero as the Fibonacci numbers grow, giving $S=\dfrac{1}{5}$ and $\boxed{420S=84}$. Cassini's identity itself follows by induction because $F_{n+1}F_{n-1}-F_n^2$ changes sign at each step.
