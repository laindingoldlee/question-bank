Put the southwest corner at $(0,0)$, so the start is $S=(10,0)$, the midpoint of the north wall is $M=(5,10)$, and the west wall is $x=0$. The leg $MS=\sqrt{5^2+10^2}=5\sqrt5$ is fixed, so we minimise $SP+PM$ over $P$ on the west wall. Reflect $S$ in the west wall to $S'=(-10,0)$; then $SP=S'P$, and
\[SP+PM=S'P+PM\ge S'M=\sqrt{15^2+10^2}=5\sqrt{13},\]
with equality when $P$ is on segment $S'M$, i.e. $P=\left(0,\dfrac{20}3\right)$, which is on the wall.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair SW_=(0,0),SE_=(10,0),NE_=(10,10),NW_=(0,10);pair M=(5,10),P=(0,20/3),R=(-10,0);
draw(SW_--SE_--NE_--NW_--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
draw(SE_--P--M--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
draw(R--P,dashed);draw(R--SW_,dotted);dot(R);dot(P);dot(M);dot(SE_);
label("$S'(-10,0)$",R,S);label("$P$",P,NW);label("$M$",M,N);label("$S$",SE_,SE);label("$(0,0)$",SW_,S);
label("$5\sqrt{13}$",(R+M)/2+(-3.2,1.2));label("$5\sqrt5$",(M+SE_)/2,E);
\end{asy}

The shortest lap is $5\sqrt{13}+5\sqrt5$, so $a+b+c+d=5+13+5+5=\boxed{28}$.
