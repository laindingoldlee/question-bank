A square room is $10$ meters by $10$ meters and has a north, south, east and west wall. You start at the southeast corner, run to some point on the west wall, then from that point to the midpoint of the north wall, then back to the southeast corner. This is one lap. The length of the shortest lap can be written as $a\sqrt b+c\sqrt d$, where $b$ and $d$ are squarefree. Find $a+b+c+d$.
