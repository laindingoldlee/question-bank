The number must be divisible by $\operatorname{lcm}(2,3,5,11)=330$. It lies between $1\,997\,000$ and $1\,997\,999$. Since $1\,997\,000=330\cdot6051+170$, the multiples of $330$ in this range are
\[
330\cdot6052=1\,997\,160,\qquad 330\cdot6053=1\,997\,490,\qquad 330\cdot6054=1\,997\,820 .
\]
The smallest is $1\,997\,160$, so $\boxed{a=1,\ b=6,\ c=0}$. Check: it ends in $0$ (divisible by $2$ and $5$), its digit sum is $1+9+9+7+1+6+0=33$ (divisible by $3$), and its alternating digit sum $0-6+1-7+9-9+1=-11$ is divisible by $11$.
