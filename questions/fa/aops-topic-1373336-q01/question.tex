Alice and Bob agree to meet for lunch at some time during a $2$-hour period. Each arrives at a time chosen uniformly at random during the period, independently of the other, waits $10$ minutes for the other (or until the end of the period), and leaves if the other has not arrived. What is the probability that they meet?
