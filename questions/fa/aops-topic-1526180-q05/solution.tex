Jim owns $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of the farm. Each of John's heirs owns $\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{1}{3}=\dfrac{1}{9}$, so after Paul sells his share the family owns $1-\dfrac{1}{9}=\dfrac{8}{9}$ of the original farm. In the final sale Jim's part of what is sold is
\[
\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{3}}{\dfrac{8}{9}}=\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{9}{8}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{8}}.
\]
