Some people are hiding in a secret place. The police think they are missing and offer a reward of at most $\$1{,}000{,}000$ for finding them. Johnny knows where they are. For each full year that he keeps the secret he receives $\$200{,}000$, and these yearly payments stop as soon as he tells the police. He can tell the police now, tell them $6$ years from now, or never tell; if he never tells, he will live for $15$ more years. Which choice is sure to give Johnny the most money in total, whatever the actual reward turns out to be, and how much does he receive with it?
