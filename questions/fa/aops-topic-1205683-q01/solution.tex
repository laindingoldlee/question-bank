Square repeatedly modulo $1000$:
\[7^4=2401\equiv401,\quad 7^8\equiv401^2=160801\equiv801,\quad 7^{16}\equiv801^2\equiv601,\]
\[7^{32}\equiv601^2\equiv201,\quad 7^{64}\equiv201^2=40401\equiv401.\]
Then $7^{72}=7^{64}\cdot7^8\equiv401\cdot801=321201\equiv\boxed{201}\pmod{1000}$.
