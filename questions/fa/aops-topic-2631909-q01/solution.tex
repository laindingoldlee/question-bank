Since $3^2=1^2+2^2+2^2$ and $2019=3\cdot673$,
\[2019^2=673^2\left(1^2+2^2+2^2\right)=\boxed{673^2+1346^2+1346^2}.\]
(Check: $452929+2\cdot1811716=4076361=2019^2$.) $\blacksquare$
