Move the $b$ terms to the right:
\[
a+8c=4+7b,\qquad 8a-c=7-4b .
\]
Square both equations and add. On the left, the cross terms $16ac$ and $-16ac$ cancel:
\[
(a+8c)^2+(8a-c)^2=65a^2+65c^2 .
\]
On the right, the cross terms $56b$ and $-56b$ cancel:
\[
(4+7b)^2+(7-4b)^2=16+49+65b^2=65+65b^2 .
\]
Hence $65(a^2+c^2)=65(1+b^2)$, so
\[
a^2-b^2+c^2=\boxed{1}.
\]
(The value does not depend on which of the infinitely many solutions is taken; for example $b=0$ gives $a=\dfrac{60}{65}=\dfrac{12}{13}$, $c=\dfrac{5}{13}$ and $a^2+c^2=1$.)
