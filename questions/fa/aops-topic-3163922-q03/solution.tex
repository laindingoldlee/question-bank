Let $V$ be the volume after the first addition. Acid comprises $0.7V$. After five more liters, acid comprises $1-\dfrac{4}{11}=\dfrac{7}{11}$ of $V+5$, because $36\dfrac{4}{11}$ percent is $\dfrac{4}{11}$. Thus $\dfrac{7}{10}V=\dfrac{7}{11}(V+5)$, so $11V=10V+50$ and $V=50$. Acid volume is $\boxed{35}$ liters.
