Each block of four digits is worth $2018$ times a power of $10^4$:
\[
N=2018\left(1+10^4+10^8+\cdots+10^{96}\right)=2018\sum_{i=0}^{24}\left(10^4\right)^i .
\]
Work modulo $7$. First $2018=7\cdot288+2\equiv2$. Next $10\equiv3$, so $10^4\equiv3^4=81\equiv4$. The powers of $4$ modulo $7$ repeat with period $3$: $4^0\equiv1$, $4^1\equiv4$, $4^2\equiv2$, $4^3\equiv1,\dots$, and each full period contributes $1+4+2=7\equiv0$. The $25$ terms $i=0,1,\dots,24$ form $8$ full periods ($i=0$ to $23$) and the single term $i=24$, which is $\left(4^3\right)^8\equiv1$. So
\[
N\equiv2\cdot1=2\pmod 7,
\]
and the remainder is $\boxed{2}$.
