Five friends plan a party badly: at noon, every friend leaves their own house and travels to one of the other four friends' houses, chosen uniformly at random and independently. The probability that every friend meets at least one other friend at the house they chose is $\dfrac mn$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $m+n$.
