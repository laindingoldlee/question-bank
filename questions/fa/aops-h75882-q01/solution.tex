Set $f(t)=\sum_{j=1}^7(j+t)^2x_j$, a polynomial of degree at most two. Its second differences at equally spaced arguments are constant. From $f(0)=1,f(1)=12,f(2)=123$, the first differences are 11 and 111, differing by 100. The next first difference is 211. Therefore $f(3)=123+211=\boxed{334}$. Equivalently, $f(3)=3f(2)-3f(1)+f(0)$ for every quadratic.
