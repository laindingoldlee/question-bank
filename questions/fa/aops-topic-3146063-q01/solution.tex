The pool holds $3\cdot12=36$ hose-hours. By noon, $3\cdot5=15$ have been supplied, leaving $21$. Two hoses need $\dfrac{21}{2}=10.5$ more hours. The pool is full at $\boxed{10{:}30\text{ p.m.}}$.
