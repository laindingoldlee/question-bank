Rearrange and complete the product: $(a-6)(b-6)=36 $. The original equation $ b(a-6)=6a>0 $ forces $ a>6 $, and similarly $ b>6 $. Thus $ a=6+d,b=6+\dfrac{36}{d}$ for any positive divisor $ d $ of 36. The complete set is \[\boxed{(7,42),(8,24),(9,18),(10,15),(12,12),(15,10),(18,9),(24,8),(42,7)}.\]
