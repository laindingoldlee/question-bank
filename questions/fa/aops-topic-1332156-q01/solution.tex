Two men do $2$ jobs in $5$ hours, so one man does $1$ job in $5$ hours. Then $3$ men working at once do $3$ jobs (one each) in $\boxed{5\text{ hours}}$.

In general, with the rate of $\dfrac{2}{2\cdot5}=\dfrac{1}{5}$ job per man-hour, $m$ men do $j$ jobs in $\dfrac{5j}{m}$ hours.
