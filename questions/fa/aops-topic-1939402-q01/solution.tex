Order does not matter, so count sets. Whatever Doug's set is, Regina has $\dbinom53=10$ equally likely sets. To share exactly two numbers with Doug she picks $2$ of his $3$ numbers and $1$ of the $2$ numbers he did not pick: $\dbinom32\dbinom21=6$ ways. The probability is $\dfrac{6}{10}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{5}}$. (Counting ordered choices, as with the denominator $5\cdot4\cdot3=60$, gives the same answer: $\dfrac{36}{60}$.)
