Both objects complete one revolution in the same time $T$, so they have the same angular speed. In one revolution $A$ travels $2\pi\cdot2=4\pi$ and $B$ travels $2\pi\cdot4=8\pi$. Their speeds are $\dfrac{4\pi}{T}$ and $\dfrac{8\pi}{T}$, so $B$ really is moving faster: the ratio of speeds is $\boxed{2}$. (Equal angular speed does not mean equal linear speed; linear speed is proportional to the distance from the centre.)
