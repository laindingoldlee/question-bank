Expand: $f(a)=a^2-(a^2-2a+1)=2a-1$. Now $3\mid2a-1$ exactly when $2a\equiv1\pmod3$, i.e.\ (multiplying by $2$, since $2\cdot2\equiv1$) $a\equiv2\pmod3$.

Among $1,2,\dots,309$ (that is, $103$ complete blocks of three consecutive integers) exactly one number in each block is $\equiv2\pmod3$, so $103$ numbers work. The probability is
\[
\dfrac{103}{309}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{3}}.
\]
