An integer $a$ is chosen uniformly at random from $1,2,\dots,309$. Let $f(a)$ be the difference between the square of $a$ and the square of the integer just before $a$, that is, $f(a)=a^2-(a-1)^2$. Find the probability that $f(a)$ is divisible by $3$.
