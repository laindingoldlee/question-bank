Infinity is not a real number on which all field operations are defined. In the extended reals, expressions such as $\dfrac{\infty}{\infty}$ are undefined. In limits the notation is indeterminate: $\dfrac{t}{t}\to1$, $\dfrac{t^2}{t}\to\infty$ and $\dfrac{t}{t^2}\to0$ although each numerator and denominator tends to infinity. Thus the division step has no valid meaning.
