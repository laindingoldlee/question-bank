The two directions from the house are perpendicular. Hence the school-to-tournament distance is $\sqrt{5^2+12^2}=13$ miles, giving $\dfrac{13}{0.5}=\boxed{26}$ hours.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair H=(0,0),Sp=(-5/sqrt(2),-5/sqrt(2)),T=(-12/sqrt(2),12/sqrt(2));draw(H--Sp--T--cycle);label("house",H,E);label("school",Sp,S);label("tournament",T,N);
\end{asy}
