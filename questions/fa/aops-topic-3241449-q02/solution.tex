For $u\ge5$, the map $u\mapsto u(u-4)$ fixes $5$ and strictly increases every integer $u\ge6$; for $u\ge6$ its increase $u(u-5)$ is at least six. Thus once such a value occurs by time 2, the 97 th term exceeds 100. If $k\ge6$ or $k\le-2$, then $a_2\ge12$. The remaining $k=-1,0,1,2,3,4,5$ give respectively $a_2=5,0,3,4,3,0,5$, all eventually fixed at $0,3$, or $5$. Exactly $\boxed7$ values work.
