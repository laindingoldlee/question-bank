Scale the outer side to 3. Coordinates can be $P=(2,0),Q=(3,2),R=(1,3),S=(0,1)$. Adjacent edge vectors $(1,2),(-2,1)$ are perpendicular and have squared length 5, so the inner area is 5 versus outer area 9. The fraction is $\boxed{\dfrac{5}{9}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(3,0),C=(3,3),D=(0,3),P=(2,0),Q=(3,2),R=(1,3),Ss=(0,1);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);filldraw(P--Q--R--Ss--cycle,lightgray);label("$P$",P,S);label("$Q$",Q,E);label("$R$",R,N);label("$S$",Ss,W);
\end{asy}
