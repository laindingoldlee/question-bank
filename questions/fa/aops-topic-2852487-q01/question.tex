The probability that a student fails a test taken $x$ days after his birthday is $\dfrac1{x^2}$. His birthday is May 24 and the test is May 27. On that day, because of stress, he is $\dfrac89$ \emph{more} likely to fail than this formula says (that is, the probability is multiplied by $1+\dfrac89$). What is the probability, as a percentage, that he fails?
