The domain is $x\ge\dfrac{1}{2}$: on this domain $x\ge\sqrt{2x-1}$ because $x^2-(2x-1)=(x-1)^2\ge0$.
Squaring the nonnegative left side gives
\[
2x+2\sqrt{x^2-2x+1}=2x+2|x-1|
=\begin{cases}2,&\dfrac{1}{2}\le x\le1,\\4x-2,&x\ge1.\end{cases}
\]
Therefore (a) every $\boxed{x\in[\dfrac{1}{2},1]}$ works; (b) there is $\boxed{\text{no real solution}}$; and (c) $4x-2=4$, giving $\boxed{x=\dfrac{3}{2}}$.
