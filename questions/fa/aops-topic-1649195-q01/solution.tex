Let Helen start with $N$ candy bars. The bars she shares are $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of all her bars, and they are exactly the $18$ bars she gives away, one to each friend:
\[
\dfrac{3}{4}N=18\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad N=18\cdot\dfrac{4}{3}=\boxed{24}.
\]
Check: $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of $24$ is $18$, and she keeps the other $6$.
