The first seven terms are $\dfrac{1}{2},1,4,5,\dfrac{3}{2},\dfrac{1}{2},1$. Since each subsequent term is determined by the preceding pair, the repeated pair proves period five. One period has product $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot1\cdot4\cdot5\cdot\dfrac{3}{2}=15$. As $2023=5\cdot404+3$, the full product is $15^{404}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot1\cdot4=2^1 3^{404}5^{404}$. The requested sum is $2+1+3+404+5+404=\boxed{819}$.
