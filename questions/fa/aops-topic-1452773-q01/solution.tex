Let there be $x$ students in 9th grade. Then there are $2x$ in 12th grade and $10\cdot2x=20x$ in 11th grade. The 9th and 10th graders together number half of the 11th graders, $10x$, so there are $9x$ in 10th grade. In total
\[
x+9x+20x+2x=32x=32,
\]
so $x=1$ and there are $\boxed{2}$ students in 12th grade.
