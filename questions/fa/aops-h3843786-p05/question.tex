Let
\[
S=\{23^a5^b7^c:a,b,c\in\mathbb Z,\ 1\le|a|,|b|,|c|,\ |a|+|b|+|c|\le16\}.
\]
For a positive integer $u$, let $\Omega(u)$ count its prime factors with multiplicity. Let $N$ be the sum of $\Omega$ of the reduced numerator over all entries of $S$. Choose distinct $X=\dfrac{m_1}{n_1}$ and $Y=\dfrac{m_2}{n_2}$ from $S$, in reduced form, and replace their two entries in the list by $\dfrac{m_1}{n_2}$ and $\dfrac{m_2}{n_1}$, reducing each. Repeated resulting entries are retained. Suppose each of the primes $23,5,7$ cancels at least once across the two reductions, and the new numerator-factor sum is $28557$. How many ordered pairs $(X,Y)$ are possible?
