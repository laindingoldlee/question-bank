Let line $WB$ meet line $ZY$ at $T$. Since $WX\parallel ZY$, the triangles $BXW$ and $BYT$ have equal alternate angles at $X$ and $Y$, equal vertical angles at $B$, and $BX=BY$; so they are congruent and $YT=XW$. In a parallelogram $XW=YZ$, so $YT=YZ$ and $Y$ is the midpoint of $ZT$.

Now $\angle ZAT=90^\circ$, so in right triangle $ZAT$ the midpoint $Y$ of the hypotenuse is equidistant from all three vertices (it is the centre of the circle with diameter $ZT$). Hence $AY=YZ$. $\blacksquare$
