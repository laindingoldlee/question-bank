In convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, the points $E$, $F$, $G$, $H$ are the trisection points of $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, $DA$ nearer to $A$, $C$, $C$, $A$, respectively (so $AE=\dfrac{1}{3}AB$, $CF=\dfrac{1}{3}CB$, $CG=\dfrac{1}{3}CD$, $AH=\dfrac{1}{3}AD$). Let $K$ be the intersection of $EG$ and $FH$. Show that $EFGH$ is a parallelogram, and that $K$ is the midpoint of both $EG$ and $FH$.
