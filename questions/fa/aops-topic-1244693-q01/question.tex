A cricket tournament has two rounds and a playoff. In Round $1$ there are two groups of $4$ teams, and in each group every team plays every other team once; one team from each group advances. In Round $2$ there are two groups of $5$ teams (each containing one team from Round $1$ and four new teams), and in each group every team plays every other team twice. Then $2$ teams from each Round $2$ group enter a playoff of two semifinals and a final. How many matches are played in total?
