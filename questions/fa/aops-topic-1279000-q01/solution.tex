$251=1^3+5^3+5^3=2^3+3^3+6^3$. A complete check of all sums $a^3+b^3+c^3$ with $1\le a\le b\le c\le6$ (larger cubes exceed $251$) shows no smaller number has two representations, so the answer is $\boxed{251}$.
