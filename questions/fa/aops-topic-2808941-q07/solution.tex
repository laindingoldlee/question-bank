Its centre lies on the angle bisector. If its radius is $r$ and distance from the sector vertex is $d$, tangency to the sides gives $r=d\sin30^\circ=\dfrac{d}{2}$. Internal tangency to the radius 3 circle gives $d+r=3$, so $r=1$. The sector area is $\dfrac{1}{6}\pi3^2=\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$, hence the ratio is $\boxed{\dfrac{2}{3}}$.
