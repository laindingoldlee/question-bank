Divisibility by $8$ requires $8\mid 111B$, and $111$ is coprime to $8$, so $B=0$ or $8$. Divisibility by $9$ requires $9\mid A+3B$. For $B=0$, $A=9$, giving $9000$. For $B=8$, $A+24$ is divisible by $9$, giving $A=3$ and $3888$. Both numbers are divisible by $72$, so the possible sums are $\boxed 9\text{ and }\boxed{11}$. A unique answer requires an additional condition, such as $B\ne 0$.
