Since $A+B+C=\pi$, the tangent addition identity gives $\tan A+\tan B+\tan C=\tan A\tan B\tan C$. Therefore the right side is
\[
\tan A\tan B+\tan B\tan C+\tan C\tan A.
\]
Put $q=\cos A\cos B\cos C$. From $\sin A\sin B=\cos A\cos B+\cos C$, the numerator over the common denominator $q$ is
\[
3q+\cos^2A+\cos^2B+\cos^2C.
\]
To evaluate the square sum, use $\cos C=\sin A\sin B-\cos A\cos B$ and expand:
\[
\cos^2C+2q
=\sin^2A\sin^2B-\cos^2A\cos^2B
=1-\cos^2A-\cos^2B.
\]
Thus the numerator is $3q+1-2q=1+q$, and division by $q$ proves the identity.
