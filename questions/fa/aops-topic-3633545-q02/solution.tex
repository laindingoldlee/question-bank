If $p$ is the per-draw win probability, the expected score is $\sum_{j=1}^5(6-j)p(1-p)^{j-1}$. It is increasing in $p$: use the same independent uniform random variables for two probabilities $p_1<p_2$; the larger probability produces a first win no later, hence a prize no smaller, on every outcome. Thus enter all ten tickets, giving $p=0.1$. The weekly expectation is $0.1(5+4(0.9)+3(0.9)^2+2(0.9)^3+(0.9)^4)=1.31441$. Over ten weeks it is $\boxed{13.1441}$ points.
