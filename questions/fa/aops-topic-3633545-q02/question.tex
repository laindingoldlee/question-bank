Each week for ten weeks you receive ten tickets, which cannot be saved. Entering $k$ tickets gives a total probability $\dfrac{k}{100}$ of winning each successive prize, with independent draws until your first win; after that win you are ineligible for later prizes that week. The prizes, in order, are worth $5,4,3,2,1$ points. How many tickets should you enter per week to maximize expected points, and what is the total expected score?
