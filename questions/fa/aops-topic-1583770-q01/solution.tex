Count the days from April $14$, $2012$ to January $1$, $2013$: $16$ more days in April, then May through December,
\[
16+31+30+31+31+30+31+30+31=261 ,
\]
reaches December $31$, and one more day reaches January $1$: $262$ days in all. Since $262=7\cdot37+3$, January $1$, $2013$ falls $3$ days of the week after Saturday, on a $\boxed{\text{Tuesday}}$.
