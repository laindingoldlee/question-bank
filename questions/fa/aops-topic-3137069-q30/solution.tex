On the intervals cut by 1, 2, 3, the expression respectively equals $-z $, $ z-2 $, $ 3z-6 $, and $ z $. It decreases up to 1 and is strictly increasing thereafter. The unique minimizing value is $\boxed{z=1}$, at which the minimum is $-1 $.
