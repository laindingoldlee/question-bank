Every term has the form (positive coefficient)$\cdot a^ib^jc^kd^l$ with $i+j+k+l=6$, $i,j,k,l\ge0$, and every such monomial occurs; since all coefficients are positive, nothing cancels. By stars and bars the number of such monomials is $\dbinom{6+3}{3}=\boxed{84}$.
