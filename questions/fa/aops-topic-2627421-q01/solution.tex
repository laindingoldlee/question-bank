Since $\left(x+\dfrac1x\right)\left(x^n+\dfrac1{x^n}\right)=x^{n+1}+\dfrac1{x^{n+1}}+x^{n-1}+\dfrac1{x^{n-1}}$, we have $a_{n+1}=3a_n-a_{n-1}$, with $a_0=2$, $a_1=3$:
\[2,\ 3,\ 7,\ 18,\ 47,\ 123,\ 322,\ 843,\ 2207,\ 5778,\ 15127,\ 39603.\]
So $a_{11}=39603$, with digit sum $3+9+6+0+3=21$: $\boxed{\textbf{(E) }21}$.
