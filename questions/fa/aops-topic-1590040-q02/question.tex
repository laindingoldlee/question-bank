A solid right circular cone has a base radius of $5$ meters and a slant height of $10$ meters. A top cone will be removed, by a cut parallel to the base, so that the volume of the remaining frustum will be $\dfrac{1}{3}$ that of the original cone. In square meters, what is the total surface area, including both bases, of the frustum? Express your answer to the nearest whole number.
