Jordan plays Minesweeper on a $4\times6$ board ($24$ squares) with $22$ mines. His first click is always safe: the $22$ mines are placed uniformly at random among the $23$ squares other than the first square he reveals. A revealed safe square shows how many of its (up to $8$) neighbouring squares contain mines. Jordan wins if he reveals both safe squares without ever clicking a mine. What is his best strategy, and what is his probability of winning with it?
