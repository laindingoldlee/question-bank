Let $K$ be the number of knaves. A knight sees $K$ knaves among the other $178$ residents, so truth requires $K>89$. A knave sees $K-1$ knaves, and falsehood requires $K-1\le89$. Both kinds must exist: an all-knight island would make every statement false, and an all-knave island would make every statement true. Hence $K\ge90$ and $K\le90$, giving $\boxed{90}$ knaves.
