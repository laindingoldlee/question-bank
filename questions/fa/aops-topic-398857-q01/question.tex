A gecko is in a room that is $12$ feet long, $10$ feet wide and $8$ feet tall. The gecko is on a side wall ($10$ by $8$), one foot from the ceiling and one foot from the back wall ($12$ by $8$). It spots a fly on the opposite side wall, one foot from the floor and one foot from the front wall ($12$ by $8$). What is the length of the shortest path the gecko can take to reach the fly, if it does not jump and can walk only across the ceiling and the walls? Express your answer in simplest radical form.
