Ed usually drives to work at a constant speed $S$ mph. One day, halfway to work, he turns around, speeds up by $8$ mph and drives home; then he immediately drives to work at $6$ mph faster than he drove home. His total driving time is exactly $67\%$ greater than usual. Find $S$.
