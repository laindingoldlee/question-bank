Let the distance be $d$. The times are $\dfrac{d}{2S}$, $\dfrac{d}{2(S+8)}$ and $\dfrac{d}{S+14}$, and they add up to $1.67\cdot\dfrac {d}{S}$:
\[\dfrac{1}{2S}+\dfrac{1}{2(S+8)}+\dfrac{1}{S+14}=\dfrac{167}{100S}.\]
Multiplying by $200S(S+8)(S+14)$:
\[100(S+8)(S+14)+100S(S+14)+200S(S+8)=334(S+8)(S+14),\]
\[400S^2+5200S+11200=334S^2+7348S+37408,\qquad 66S^2-2148S-26208=0,\]
i.e. $11S^2-358S-4368=0$. The discriminant is $358^2+4\cdot11\cdot4368=320356=566^2$, so $S=\dfrac{358+566}{22}=\boxed{42}$ (the other root is negative).
