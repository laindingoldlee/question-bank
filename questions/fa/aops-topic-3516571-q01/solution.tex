Only $a=2,4,8,16$ can occur. Fix $a,d$, and put $M=31-a-d$. Write $b=2j$ with $j\ge1$. For each $j$ satisfying $2j<M$, the choices of positive $c$ number $M-2j$. Their sum is $\left\lfloor\dfrac{(M-1)^2}{4}\right\rfloor$ when $M\ge3$, and zero otherwise. Sum this over the primes $d\le28-a$. For $a=2,4,8,16$ the subtotals are respectively $751,608,376,100$. Their sum is $\boxed{1835}$. The primes needed in these finite sums are $2,3,5,7,11,13,17,19,23$.
