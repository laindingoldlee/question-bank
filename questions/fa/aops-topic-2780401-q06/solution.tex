Digit sum 1 would make a positive integer a power of 10, never prime. Digit sums 6 and 9 force divisibility by 3 but cannot represent the prime 3 itself. The other sums $ 2,3,4,5,7,8,10 $ occur for primes $ 2,3,13,5,7,17,19 $, respectively. Thus the count is $\boxed7 $.
