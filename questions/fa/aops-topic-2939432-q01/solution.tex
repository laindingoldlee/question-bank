The successive forward-difference rows are\[\begin{array}{rrrrrr}2&4&7&11&20&28\\2&3&4&9&8\\1&1&5&-1\\0&4&-6\\4&-10\\-14\end{array}\]For a polynomial of degree at most 5, fifth differences are constant. Extend upward: the next fourth difference is $-10-14=-24$; the next third is $-6-24=-30$; the next second is $-1-30=-31$; the next first is $8-31=-23$; and the next value is $28-23=\boxed5$. The degree bound makes the interpolation unique.
