No. The symbol $\sqrt{a}$ (for $a\ge0$) denotes the \emph{nonnegative} number whose square is $a$, so $\sqrt1=1$, even though both $1$ and $-1$ square to $1$. (The two square roots of $1$ are written $\pm\sqrt1$.) Hence $\sqrt1+x=2$ gives $1+x=2$, so $x=\boxed{1}$ is the only solution; $x=3$ is not.
