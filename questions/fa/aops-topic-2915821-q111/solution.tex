There are 20 consonants and 6 vowels, so the number is $ 20^4\cdot6^2 $. It is 0 modulo 5 and, since $ 20\equiv-1 $ and $ 6\equiv-1\pmod7 $, it is 1 modulo 7. Among multiples of 5 modulo 35, the unique one congruent to 1 modulo 7 is 15. Thus the answer is $\boxed{15}$.
