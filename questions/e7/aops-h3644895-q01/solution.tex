Each pair has four allowed envelopes, giving $4^3=64$ assignments before imposing distinctness. For any specified two pairs to collide, their common envelope has two choices and the third pair has four, giving $8$. There are three possible colliding pairs. A triple collision is impossible, because every envelope is forbidden to one of the pairs; hence these three collision events are disjoint. The answer is $64-3\cdot8=\boxed{40}$.
