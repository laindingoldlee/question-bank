Choose $B=(0,0)$, $C=(7,0)$. Subtracting $AB^2=25$ from $AC^2=64$ gives $A=(\dfrac{5}{7},\dfrac{20\sqrt3}{7})$, and $D=(\dfrac{7}{2},0)$. The equation $|X-A|^2=|X-D|^2$ of the perpendicular bisector simplifies to
\[
\dfrac{39}{7}x-\dfrac{40\sqrt3}{7}y=-\dfrac{51}{4}.
\]
Intersecting with $AB$ (where $y=4\sqrt3 x$) and $AC$ (where $y=\dfrac{5\sqrt3}{11}(7-x)$) gives
\[
E=\left(\dfrac{17}{84},\dfrac{17\sqrt3}{21}\right),\quad
F=\left(\dfrac{613}{196},\dfrac{345\sqrt3}{196}\right).
\]
Both points lie on the stated segments. The determinant area formula yields
\[
[DEF]=\dfrac{1}{2}\left|(E_x-D_x)F_y-E_y(F_x-D_x)\right|
=\boxed{\dfrac{1849\sqrt3}{672}}.
\]
