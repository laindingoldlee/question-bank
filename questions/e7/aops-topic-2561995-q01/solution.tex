Write $b=ak$ with $k$ a positive integer and put $x=2^a$. Then
\[2^b-1=x^k-1=(x-1)\left(x^{k-1}+x^{k-2}+\cdots+x+1\right),\]
and the second factor is an integer. So $x-1=2^a-1$ divides $2^b-1$. $\blacksquare$
