Let $E,O$ count tuples with even and odd sums. There are $E+O=\prod_i(a_i+1)$ total tuples. Also $E-O=\prod_i\left(\sum_{j=0}^{a_i}(-1)^j\right)$ by multiplying and collecting signs. Each inner sum is $1$ when $a_i$ is even and $0$ when it is odd. Thus $\boxed{E=\dfrac{\prod_i(a_i+1)+\varepsilon}{2}}$, where $\varepsilon=1$ if all $a_i$ are even and $0$ otherwise.
