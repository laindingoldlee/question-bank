There is a pile of apples on the ground. Bob takes $\dfrac15$ of the apples. Mary then takes as many apples as the square root of the number of apples remaining. Then Joe eats $9$ apples. Now the number of apples left is a prime number less than $10$. How many apples were there at the start?
