The alloy contains $0.10\cdot150=15$ g of copper, and adding silver does not change that. If $x$ grams of silver are added, the copper must be $7.5\%=\dfrac{3}{40}$ of the total:
\[
\dfrac{15}{150+x}=\dfrac{3}{40}\;\Longrightarrow\;150+x=200\;\Longrightarrow\;x=\boxed{50}\text{ g}.
\]
