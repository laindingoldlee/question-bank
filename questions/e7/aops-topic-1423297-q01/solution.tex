The conjecture is false. Take $a=11$ and $b=2$: they are coprime, $a$ is odd and $b$ is even, but
\[
11^2-\dfrac{2^2}{2}=121-2=\boxed{119=7\cdot17},
\]
which is composite. Another counterexample is $a=9$, $b=8$: $81-32=49=7^2$. (The expression can even fail to be positive: $a=1$, $b=2$ gives $-1$.)

In fact there are infinitely many counterexamples: with $b=2$ the expression is $a^2-2$, and for $a=7k\pm3$ with $k$ even (so that $a$ is odd) we get $a^2-2\equiv9-2\equiv0\pmod7$, so $a^2-2$ is a multiple of $7$ larger than $7$ once $a>3$.
