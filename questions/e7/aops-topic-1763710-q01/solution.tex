(a) Each of $a,b,c$ is the tens digit of $3$ of the nine numbers and the units digit of $3$ of them, so
\[
S=10\cdot3(a+b+c)+3(a+b+c)=33(a+b+c),\qquad \dfrac {S}{3}=\boxed{11(a+b+c)}.
\]
(b) $S=165$ means $a+b+c=5$ with nonzero digits. The multisets of digits are $\{1,1,3\}$ and $\{1,2,2\}$, giving
\[
\boxed{113,\ 131,\ 311,\ 122,\ 212,\ 221}.
\]
