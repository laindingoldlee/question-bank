Let $n\ge1$ be an integer. Numbers $x_1,x_2,\dots,x_n$ are chosen independently and uniformly at random from the interval $[0,1]$. Prove that the probability that $x_1+x_2+\cdots+x_n<1$ is $\dfrac{1}{n!}$.
