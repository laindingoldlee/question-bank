Since $33^2=1089<1139<34^2$, the smaller prime is at most $33$. Trying primes: $1139$ is odd, its digit sum $14$ is not a multiple of $3$, it does not end in $0$ or $5$, and $1139=7\cdot162+5=11\cdot103+6=13\cdot87+8$, but $1139=17\cdot67$. Both $17$ and $67$ are prime, so the sum is $17+67=\boxed{84}$.
