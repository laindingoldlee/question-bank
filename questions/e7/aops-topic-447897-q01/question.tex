A unit circle is inscribed in an outer square and circumscribed about an inner square. Shade the part of the disk outside the inner square. Which is closest to the ratio of this shaded area to the area inside the outer square but outside the inner square: $\dfrac{1}{2},1,\dfrac{3}{2},2,\dfrac{5}{2}$?
