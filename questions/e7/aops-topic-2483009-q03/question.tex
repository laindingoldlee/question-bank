King Arthur has $20$ knights, and $13$ of them are chosen at random (all choices equally likely) to accompany him to the dragon's lair. If Lancelot is chosen, Arthur survives no matter what. If Lancelot is not chosen but Mordred is, Mordred kills Arthur. If neither is chosen, the dragon kills Arthur with probability $\dfrac13$. What is the probability that Arthur survives?
