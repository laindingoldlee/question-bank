Ash's bow is drawn in the coordinate plane with $A(-25,25)$, $B(25,25)$, $C(25,-25)$, $D(-25,-25)$ and $O(0,0)$. It consists of a circle of radius $4$ centred at $O$ and two ``triangle portions'': triangle $OAD$ and triangle $OBC$, each with the part inside the circle cut off. What is the area of one triangle portion?
