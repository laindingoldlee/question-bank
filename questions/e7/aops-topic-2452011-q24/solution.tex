Triangle $OAD$ has base $AD=50$ (on the line $x=-25$) and height $25$ (the distance from $O$ to that line), so its area is $\dfrac12\cdot50\cdot25=625$.

The vectors $\overrightarrow{OA}=(-25,25)$ and $\overrightarrow{OD}=(-25,-25)$ have dot product $625-625=0$, so $\angle AOD=90^\circ$. Since the distance from $O$ to $AD$ is $25>4$, the part of the disc inside the triangle is exactly a quarter of the disc, of area $\dfrac14\pi\cdot4^2=4\pi$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=0.1;
pair A=s*(-25,25), B=s*(25,25), C=s*(25,-25), D=s*(-25,-25), O=(0,0);
fill(O--A--D--cycle,rgb(1,0.8,0.85)); fill(O--B--C--cycle,rgb(1,0.8,0.85));
fill(arc(O,0.4,135,225)--O--cycle,white); fill(arc(O,0.4,-45,45)--O--cycle,white);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,gray); draw(A--C,gray); draw(B--D,gray);
draw(O--A--D--cycle,linewidth(0.8)); draw(O--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
draw(circle(O,0.4),blue);
dot(O); label("$O$",(0,0.42),N,fontsize(8pt));
label("$A(-25,25)$",A,NW,fontsize(8pt)); label("$B(25,25)$",B,NE,fontsize(8pt));
label("$C(25,-25)$",C,SE,fontsize(8pt)); label("$D(-25,-25)$",D,SW,fontsize(8pt));
draw(O--0.4*dir(-90),blue); label("$4$",(0,-0.4),S,blue+fontsize(7pt));
\end{asy}

So one triangle portion has area $\boxed{625-4\pi}$. (By symmetry, triangle $OBC$ gives the same.)
