An equilateral triangle has side $a$. A second equilateral triangle is formed by joining the midpoints of its sides, a third by joining the midpoints of the second's sides, and so on forever. The limit of the sum of the perimeters of all these triangles is

(A) infinite \quad (B) $5\dfrac{1}{4}a$ \quad (C) $2a$ \quad (D) $6a$ \quad (E) $4\dfrac{1}{2}a$
