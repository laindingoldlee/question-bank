For real $t$, $\sqrt{t^2}=|t|$. Since $(2\sqrt2)^2=8<9$, we have $2\sqrt2<3$, so $\left|3-2\sqrt2\right|=\left|2\sqrt2-3\right|=3-2\sqrt2$. The sum is $2\left(3-2\sqrt2\right)=\boxed{6-4\sqrt2}$.
