Triangle $XYZ$ has $\angle X=60^\circ$ and $\angle Y=45^\circ$. A circle with centre $P$ cuts each side in two points, making chords $AB$ on $XY$, $CD$ on $YZ$ and $EF$ on $ZX$, with $AB=CD=EF$. Find $\angle XPY$.
