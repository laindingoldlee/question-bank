The side lengths are a factor pair of $48$: $1\times48$, $2\times24$, $3\times16$, $4\times12$, $6\times8$, with perimeters $98,52,38,32,28$. The perimeter is largest for the long thin rectangle and smallest for the one closest to a square:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((0,0)--(48,0)--(48,1)--(0,1)--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
draw((0,4)--(8,4)--(8,10)--(0,10)--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
label("$48$",(24,0),S);label("$1$",(48,0.5),E);label("$8$",(4,10),N);label("$6$",(8,7),E);
label("perimeter $98$",(24,1),N);label("perimeter $28$",(8,7),5E);
\end{asy}

The difference is $98-28=\boxed{70}$.
