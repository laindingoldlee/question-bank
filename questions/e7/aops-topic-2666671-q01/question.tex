Let $\diamondsuit$ be a binary operation on the nonzero real numbers (so $a\diamondsuit b$ is a nonzero real number whenever $a$ and $b$ are) such that
\[a\diamondsuit(b\diamondsuit c)=(a\diamondsuit b)\cdot c\qquad\text{and}\qquad a\diamondsuit a=1\]
for all nonzero real numbers $a,b,c$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $a\diamondsuit b=\dfrac ab$ for all nonzero $a,b$.
\item[(b)] Solve $2016\diamondsuit(6\diamondsuit x)=100$.
\end{enumerate}
