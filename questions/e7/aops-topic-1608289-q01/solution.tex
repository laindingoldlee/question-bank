Count the three-digit numbers with no digit $5$, and subtract those that also have no digit $3$ (complementary counting).

\textbf{No $5$.} The hundreds digit can be any of $1,2,3,4,6,7,8,9$ ($8$ choices), and each of the other two digits any of the $9$ digits other than $5$: $8\cdot9\cdot9=648$ numbers.

\textbf{No $5$ and no $3$.} Hundreds digit: $7$ choices; the other two digits: $8$ choices each: $7\cdot8\cdot8=448$ numbers.

Hence $648-448=\boxed{200}$ numbers contain a $3$ but no $5$.
