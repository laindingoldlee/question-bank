Put $B=(0,0),C=(14,0)$. Solving $AB=13,AC=15$ gives $A=(5,12)$. The centroid is $G=(\dfrac{19}{3},4)$. The altitude from $A$ is $x=5$; the altitude from $B$, perpendicular to $AC$, is $y=\dfrac{3x}{4}$. Hence $H=(5,\dfrac{15}{4})$. Thus $GH^2=(\dfrac{4}{3})^2+(\dfrac{1}{4})^2=\dfrac{265}{144}$, so $GH=\dfrac{\sqrt{265}}{12}$. Since 265 is square-free, $\boxed{m+n=277}$.

\end{asy}
