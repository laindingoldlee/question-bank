Let $n>1$ be an integer. Prove that if no prime $p$ with $p\le\sqrt{n}$ divides $n$, then $n$ is prime. Explain why, when testing whether $n$ is prime (or looking for its smallest prime factor), it is enough to try the primes up to $\lfloor\sqrt{n}\rfloor$.
