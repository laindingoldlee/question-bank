Scale the square to unit side. If two points lie on the bottom side, every centroid has height at most one third. Conversely the entire bottom strip is attained: using the third point on the left side gives centroid x in [0,$\dfrac{2}{3}$], while using the right side gives x in [$\dfrac{1}{3}$,1], independently of height in [0,$\dfrac{1}{3}$]. Rotating covers the four boundary strips of width one third. Their complement is the central square of side one third, so the covered fraction is $1-\dfrac{1}{9}=\dfrac{8}{9}$. Rescaling area 99 gives $\boxed{88}$.
