For a finite set $X$ let $|X|$ be its number of elements. Define $S_n=\sum|A\cap B|$, summed over all ordered pairs $(A,B)$ of subsets of $\{1,2,\dots,n\}$ with $|A|=|B|$. (For example $S_2=4$.) Let $\dfrac{S_{2022}}{S_{2021}}=\dfrac pq$ in lowest terms. Find the remainder when $p+q$ is divided by $1000$.
