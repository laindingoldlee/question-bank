Let $a$, $b$, $c$ be the fractions of the project that $A$, $B$, $C$ complete per day. Then
\[
12(a+b)=\dfrac{1}{3},\qquad 4(b+c)=\dfrac{1}{6},\qquad 10(a+b+c)=1-\dfrac{1}{3}-\dfrac{1}{6}=\dfrac{1}{2} ,
\]
so $a+b=\dfrac{1}{36}$, $b+c=\dfrac{1}{24}$ and $a+b+c=\dfrac{1}{20}$. Hence
\[
c=\dfrac{1}{20}-\dfrac{1}{36}=\dfrac{1}{45},\qquad a=\dfrac{1}{20}-\dfrac{1}{24}=\dfrac{1}{120},\qquad b=\dfrac{1}{36}-\dfrac{1}{120}=\dfrac{7}{360}.
\]
$B$ worked all $12+4+10=26$ days and did $26\cdot\dfrac{7}{360}=\dfrac{91}{180}$ of the project, so $B$ is paid
\[
360\cdot\dfrac{91}{180}=\boxed{\$182}.
\]
Check: $A$ worked $22$ days and did $\dfrac{22}{120}=\dfrac{66}{360}$ ($\$66$); $C$ worked $14$ days and did $\dfrac{14}{45}=\dfrac{112}{360}$ ($\$112$); $66+182+112=360$.
