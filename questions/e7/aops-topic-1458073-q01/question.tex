A project took $26$ days for three people $A$, $B$ and $C$ to finish, each working at a constant rate whenever they worked.
\begin{enumerate}
\item For the first $12$ days, $A$ and $B$ together completed $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of the project.
\item For the next $4$ days, $B$ and $C$ together completed $\dfrac{1}{6}$ of the project.
\item All three worked together for the last $10$ days and completed the project.
\item The total payment of $\$360$ was shared in proportion to the amount of work each person did.
\end{enumerate}
How much money should $B$ be paid?
