Use the source convention: unpadded month and day, and the final two year digits. The latest possible year is 2999. A palindrome ending in 99 must start in 99. No two-digit month begins 99, so the month is 9, and the day must begin 9. The only valid such day is 9. Thus $\text{9--9--99}$ is palindromic, giving $\boxed{\text{September 9, 2999}}$, and no later date that year can work.
