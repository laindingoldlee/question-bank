The square has side $\sqrt9=3$ m, so $AC=3$ and the equilateral triangle also has sides $3$. The perimeter of $ABCDE$ consists of $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, $DE$ and $EA$ (the common side $AC$ is inside), so it is $5\cdot3=\boxed{15}$ m.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),C=(3,0),B=(1.5,1.5*sqrt(3)),D=(3,-3),Ep=(0,-3);
draw(A--B--C--D--Ep--cycle,linewidth(0.8));draw(A--C,dashed);
label("$A$",A,W);label("$B$",B,N);label("$C$",C,E);label("$D$",D,SE);label("$E$",Ep,SW);
label("$3$",(A+B)/2,NW);label("$3$",(B+C)/2,NE);label("$3$",(C+D)/2,E);label("$3$",(D+Ep)/2,S);label("$3$",(Ep+A)/2,W);
label("area $9$",(1.5,-1.5));
\end{asy}
