Let $a_1,a_2,b_1,b_2,c_1,c_2$ be nonzero real numbers. Suppose $a_1x^2+b_1x+c_1=0$ has real roots $r_1,s_1$ and $a_2x^2+b_2x+c_2=0$ has real roots $r_2,s_2$. Must the equation
\[(a_1+a_2)x^2+(b_1+b_2)x+(c_1+c_2)=0\]
have the roots $\dfrac{r_1+r_2}{2}$ and $\dfrac{s_1+s_2}{2}$? Prove your answer.
