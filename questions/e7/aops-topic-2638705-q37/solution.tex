Divide by $abcd$ modulo $13$ and write $u=a^{-1},v=b^{-1},w=c^{-1},z=d^{-1}$ and $S=u+v+w+z$. The equations are $S+2z=0$, $S+2w=4$, $S+2v=5$, $S+2u=9$. Adding gives $6S=18$, so $S=3$. Therefore $(u,v,w,z)=(3,1,7,5)$ modulo $13$. Taking inverses gives $(a,b,c,d)\equiv(9,1,2,8)$, with sum $20\equiv\boxed7\pmod{13}$.
