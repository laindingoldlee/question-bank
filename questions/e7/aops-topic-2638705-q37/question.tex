Integers $a,b,c,d$ are nonzero modulo $13$ and satisfy $3abc+abd+acd+bcd\equiv0$, $abc+3abd+acd+bcd\equiv4abcd$, $abc+abd+3acd+bcd\equiv5abcd$, and $abc+abd+acd+3bcd\equiv9abcd$, all modulo $13$. Find $a+b+c+d\pmod{13}$.
