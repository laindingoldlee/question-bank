The generating function $\sum_{n\ge1}\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}z^n=\dfrac{z}{(1-z)^3}$ follows by differentiating the geometric series twice or multiplying three geometric series and shifting once. It converges for $|z|<1 $. At $ z=\dfrac{1}{3}$, the sum is $\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{3}}{(\dfrac{2}{3})^3}=\boxed{\dfrac{9}{8}}$.
