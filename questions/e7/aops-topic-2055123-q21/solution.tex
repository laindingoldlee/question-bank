A uniformly random subset includes each number $i$ independently with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Let $X_i=i$ if $i$ is chosen and $X_i=1$ otherwise; then $P=X_1X_2\cdots X_{2020}$, the factors are independent, and $E[X_i]=\dfrac{1+i}{2}$. Hence
\[E[P]=\prod_{i=1}^{2020}\dfrac{i+1}{2}=\dfrac{2\cdot3\cdots2021}{2^{2020}}=\dfrac{2021!}{2^{2020}}.\]
(Equivalently, the sum of the products over all subsets is $\prod_{i}(1+i)$.) By Legendre's formula, the exponent of $2$ in $2021!$ is
\[1010+505+252+126+63+31+15+7+3+1=2013 .\]
So $E[P]=\dfrac{m}{2^{2020-2013}}=\dfrac{m}{2^7}$ with $m=\dfrac{2021!}{2^{2013}}$ odd. Thus $n=7$, answer $\boxed{\textbf{(B)}\ 7}$.
