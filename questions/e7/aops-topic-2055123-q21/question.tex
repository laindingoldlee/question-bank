A subset of $\{1,2,3,\dots,2020\}$ is chosen uniformly at random from all $2^{2020}$ subsets, and $P$ is the product of the numbers in the subset (if the subset is empty, $P=1$). The expected value of $P$ can be written as $\dfrac{m}{2^n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are positive integers and $m$ is odd. Find $n$.

$\textbf{(A)}\ 6 \qquad\textbf{(B)}\ 7 \qquad\textbf{(C)}\ 8 \qquad\textbf{(D)}\ 9 \qquad\textbf{(E)}\ 10$
