Work modulo $100$ by repeated squaring:
\[
12^2=144\equiv44,\qquad 12^4\equiv44^2=1936\equiv36,\qquad 12^8\equiv36^2=1296\equiv96 .
\]
Then
\[
12^{10}=12^8\cdot12^2\equiv96\cdot44=4224\equiv24\pmod{100}.
\]
The remainder is $\boxed{24}$. (Indeed $12^{10}=61{,}917{,}364{,}224$.)
