Since $2023=7\cdot17^2$, we have $\phi(2023)=2023\cdot\dfrac{6}{7}\cdot\dfrac{16}{17}=1632$. Therefore $\phi(2023^{1632})=7^{1632}17^{3264}\cdot\dfrac{6}{7}\cdot\dfrac{16}{17}=2^5\cdot3\cdot7^{1631}\cdot17^{3263}$. The exponent sum is $5+1+1631+3263=\boxed{4900}$.
