Adding the three equations gives $2(x+y+z)=12$, so $x+y+z=6$. Subtracting each equation from this:
\[z=6-(-2)=8,\qquad x=6-3=3,\qquad y=6-11=-5.\]
So $\boxed{(x,y,z)=(3,-5,8)}$. Check: $3+(-5)=-2$, $-5+8=3$, $3+8=11$.
