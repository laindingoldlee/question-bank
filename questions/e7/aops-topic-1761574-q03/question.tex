Four children (Jessica, John, and the twins Sam and Joseph) and three adults (Mr.\ Smith, Mrs.\ Smith and Grandmother Annie) sit around a round table with ten equally spaced chairs, one person per chair. Jessica must sit at least three chairs away from John: for example J1, empty, empty, J2 is acceptable, but J1, empty, J2 is not. The twins must sit in adjacent chairs. Going clockwise around the table, the three adults must appear in the order Mr.\ Smith, Mrs.\ Smith, Grandmother Annie (not necessarily next to each other). Arrangements that are rotations of each other are considered the same. How many seating arrangements are there?
