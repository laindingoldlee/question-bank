The polynomial $Q(x)=x(P(x)+1)-2$ has the four roots $1,\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{1}{3},\dfrac{1}{4}$. Its degree is at most four and $Q(0)=-2$, so
\[
Q(x)=-48(x-1)\left(x-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)\left(x-\dfrac{1}{3}\right)\left(x-\dfrac{1}{4}\right).
\]
At $x=-1$, the product is $5$, giving $Q(-1)=-240$. But $Q(-1)=-P(-1)-3$, hence $\boxed{P(-1)=237}$.
