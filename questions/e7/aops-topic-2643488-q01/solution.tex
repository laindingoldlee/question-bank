Let $a_n=6^{n+2}+7^{2n+1}$. For $n=0$, $a_0=36+7=43$. If $43\mid a_n$, then
\[a_{n+1}=6\cdot6^{n+2}+49\cdot7^{2n+1}=6\left(6^{n+2}+7^{2n+1}\right)+43\cdot7^{2n+1}=6a_n+43\cdot7^{2n+1},\]
which is divisible by $43$. By induction, $43\mid a_n$ for all $n\ge0$. $\blacksquare$
