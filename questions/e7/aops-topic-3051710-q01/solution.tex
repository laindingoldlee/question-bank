One Oren is $\dfrac{2}{5}$ Oran, and one Orin is $\dfrac{3}{10}$ Oran. The total is $9+10(\dfrac{2}{5})+21(\dfrac{3}{10})=\dfrac{193}{10}$ Orans. One Oran is $\dfrac{5}{12}$ Orun, so the answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{193}{24}\text{ Oruns}}$.
