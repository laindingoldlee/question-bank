A time on a digital clock is written with the hour without a leading zero followed by two digits for the minutes (for example 4:04 or 12:21). Reading the digits without the colon gives a whole number. Call a time \emph{excessive} if this number is a palindrome, and \emph{interesting} if this number is a multiple of $4$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] On a $12$-hour clock (times $1{:}00$ to $12{:}59$), how many times are both excessive and interesting?
\item[(b)] On a $24$-hour clock (times $0{:}00$ to $23{:}59$), how many times are both excessive and interesting?
\end{enumerate}
