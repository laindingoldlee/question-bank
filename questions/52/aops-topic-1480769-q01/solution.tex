Put $y=5^x$. Then $25^x=\left(5^2\right)^x=y^2$, and the equation becomes the quadratic
\[
y^2+y-650=0,\qquad (y-25)(y+26)=0 .
\]
Since $y=5^x>0$, only $y=25$ is possible. So $5^x=25=5^2$, and (because $5^x$ is increasing) $\boxed{x=2}$ is the only solution.
