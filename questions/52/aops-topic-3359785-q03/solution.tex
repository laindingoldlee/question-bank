At $x=10$, $10f(20)+5f(\dfrac{1}{5})=60$. At $x=\dfrac{1}{10}$, $5f(20)+10f(\dfrac{1}{5})=\dfrac{3}{5}$. Twice the first equation minus the second gives $15f(20)=\dfrac{597}{5}$, so $\boxed{f(20)=\dfrac{199}{25}}$.
