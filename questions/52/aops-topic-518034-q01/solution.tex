The last digit of $2013$ in base $b$ is the remainder of $2013$ on division by $b$. So we need $2013\equiv 3\pmod b$ with $3$ a legal digit, that is, $b\mid 2010$ and $b>3$.

Since $2010=2\cdot3\cdot5\cdot67$, it has $2^4=16$ divisors:
\[1,2,3,5,6,10,15,30,67,134,201,335,402,670,1005,2010.\]
Removing $1$, $2$ and $3$ leaves $\boxed{13}$ bases.
