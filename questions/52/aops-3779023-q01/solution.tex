Expanding gives $Q=(a^2-ac+2b^2)x^2+2b(a+c)x+(c^2-ac+2b^2)$. Its discriminant is $4(ac-b^2)((a-c)^2+4b^2)$, while the original discriminant is $4(b^2-ac)$. The second factor is strictly positive under the stated exclusion, so nonzero discriminants have opposite signs; if one is zero, so is the other. Thus two distinct real roots for one correspond to no real roots for the other.
