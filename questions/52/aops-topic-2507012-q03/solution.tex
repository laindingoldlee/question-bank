If the sides are $x$ and $50-x$ ($0<x<50$), the area is
\[x(50-x)=625-(x-25)^2\le625,\]
with equality for $x=25$, the $25\times25$ square. The largest area is $\boxed{625}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw(box((0,0),(1,4)),linewidth(0.9)); label("$10$",(0.5,0),S,fontsize(8pt)); label("$40$",(0,2),W,fontsize(8pt)); label("$400$",(0.5,2),fontsize(8pt));
draw(box((2.5,0),(5,2.5)),linewidth(0.9)); fill(box((2.5,0),(5,2.5)),rgb(0.85,0.9,1)); draw(box((2.5,0),(5,2.5)),linewidth(0.9));
label("$25$",(3.75,0),S,fontsize(8pt)); label("$25$",(5,1.25),E,fontsize(8pt)); label("$625$",(3.75,1.25),fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}

For example, the $10\times40$ rectangle (left) has area $400$, less than the area $625$ of the square (right) with the same perimeter.
