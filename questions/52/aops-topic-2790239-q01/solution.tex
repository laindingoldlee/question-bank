Compare consecutive numbers. $TI<IN$ gives $T<I$ (different first digits). $IN<IT$ gives $N<T$. $IT<ON$ gives $I<O$. $ON<AM$ gives $O<A$. $AM<AN$ gives $M<N$. $AN<ME$ gives $A<M$. Chaining: $N<T<I<O<A<M<N$, a contradiction. $\blacksquare$
