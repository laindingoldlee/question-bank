Let $X$ and $Y$ be the two numbers. Each is equally likely to be $1,\dots,6$, so
\[
E[X]=E[Y]=\dfrac{1+2+\cdots+6}{6}=\dfrac{21}{6}=3.5 .
\]
By linearity of expectation the expected payout per round is $E[X+Y]=3.5+3.5=7$ dollars. If the casino charges $c$ dollars, its expected gain per round is $c-7$. This is at least $0$ exactly when $c\ge7$, so the least charge is $\boxed{7\text{ dollars}}$; any charge above $7$ gives an expected profit.
