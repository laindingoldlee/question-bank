Four players play bridge. The dealer deals a $52$-card deck one card at a time, clockwise, starting with the player on the dealer's left, so the dealer receives the last card. Partway through, the dealer is interrupted and nobody remembers who received the last card dealt. Without counting any cards, how can the dealer deal the rest so that every player receives exactly the cards they would have received without the interruption?
