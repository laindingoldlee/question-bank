The three volume-price ratios are $\dfrac{4\cdot\dfrac{4}{3}\pi3^3}{3}=48\pi$, $\dfrac{\dfrac{4}{3}\pi4^3}{2}=\dfrac{128\pi}{3}$, and $5\cdot\dfrac{4}{3}\pi=\dfrac{20\pi}{3}$. The first is greatest, giving $n=48$ and $\boxed{3n=144}$. Any mixture of offers has a weighted-average ratio no greater than the best individual offer.
