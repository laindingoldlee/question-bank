The guarantee fails exactly when every box $k$ has at most $k^2-1$ balls, which allows up to $\sum_{k=1}^6(k^2-1)=91-6=85$ balls. With $85$ balls placed this way, no box qualifies; with $86$ balls, some box must have at least $k^2$ (pigeonhole). The least $N$ is $\boxed{86}$.
