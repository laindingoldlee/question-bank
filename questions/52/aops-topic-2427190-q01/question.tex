Billy wants to draw an equilateral triangle on a given segment $AB$, but he cannot measure angles. He can only
\begin{itemize}
\item draw the straight line through two points,
\item draw a circle with a given centre passing through a given point,
\item mark the midpoint of a segment, and draw the line through a given point perpendicular to a given line.
\end{itemize}
Describe two different ways for Billy to construct an equilateral triangle with side $AB$, and prove that each construction works.
