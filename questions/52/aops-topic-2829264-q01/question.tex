Prove the Chinese remainder theorem for two moduli: if $\gcd(m,n)=1$, then for any integers $a,b$ there is an integer $x$ with $x\equiv a\pmod m$ and $x\equiv b\pmod n$, and $x$ is unique modulo $mn$.
