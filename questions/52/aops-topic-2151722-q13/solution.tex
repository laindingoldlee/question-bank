Factor as a quadratic in $x^2$:
\[x^4-5x^2+4=\left(x^2-1\right)\left(x^2-4\right)=(x-1)(x+1)(x-2)(x+2).\]
The solutions are $\pm1$ and $\pm2$, so $G=1-1+2-2=0$ and $\sin0^\circ=\boxed{0}$.
