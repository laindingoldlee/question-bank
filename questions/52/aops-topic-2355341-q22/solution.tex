Since $1323=27\cdot49=3^3\cdot7^2$, the equation is $3^A7^B=3^{3C}7^{2C}$, so by unique factorisation $A=3C$ and $B=2C$. Then $C$ divides $A$, $B$ and $C$, so $C=1$, $A=3$, $B=2$, and $A+B+C=6$: $\boxed{\textbf{(A)}\ 6}$.
