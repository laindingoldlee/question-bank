Subtracting their equations gives $(a+4)(x-1)=0$. If $a=-4$, the polynomials are identical and have two distinct roots, so this is excluded. Thus the common root is $1$, giving $a=3$. At $a=3$, the roots are $1,-4$ and $1,3$, respectively, so the answer is $\boxed3$.
