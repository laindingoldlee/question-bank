Let $m$ miles be through the mountains and $300-m$ through the plains. The total time is $3$ hours $48$ minutes $=3.8$ hours, so
\[
\dfrac{300-m}{90}+\dfrac{m}{37.5}=3.8 .
\]
Multiply by $450$ (note $\dfrac{450}{37.5}=12$): $5(300-m)+12m=1710$, so $1500+7m=1710$ and $m=\boxed{30}$ miles. Check: $\dfrac{270}{90}+\dfrac{30}{37.5}=3+0.8=3.8$ hours.
