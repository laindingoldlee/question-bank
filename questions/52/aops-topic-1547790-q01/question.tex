Let $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$ be the four roots (real or complex) of
\[
f(x)=3x^4-120x^3-11x^2+6x+30 .
\]
Find the value of
\[
\dfrac{1}{a}+\dfrac{1}{b}+\dfrac{1}{c}+\dfrac{1}{d}+2^{abcd}.
\]
Express your answer as a common fraction.
