By Vieta's formulas for $3x^4-120x^3-11x^2+6x+30$,
\[
abcd=\dfrac{30}{3}=10,\qquad abc+abd+acd+bcd=-\dfrac{6}{3}=-2 .
\]
Hence
\[
\dfrac{1}{a}+\dfrac{1}{b}+\dfrac{1}{c}+\dfrac{1}{d}=\dfrac{abc+abd+acd+bcd}{abcd}=-\dfrac{2}{10}=-\dfrac{1}{5} ,
\]
and $2^{abcd}=2^{10}=1024$. The value is
\[
1024-\dfrac{1}{5}=\boxed{\dfrac{5119}{5}}.
\]
