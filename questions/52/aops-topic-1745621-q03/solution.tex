Let $x$ be the number in both. By inclusion-exclusion, $460+571-x\le985$, so $x\ge46$; and $x\le460$. Both extremes occur ($x=46$: every student is in at least one group; $x=460$: every athlete is in a club). The difference is $460-46=\boxed{414}$.
