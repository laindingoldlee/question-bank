The first four terms of a sequence are $2,\ 34,\ 246,\ 1028$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] The $n$th term has the form $n^k+n$ for a fixed positive integer $k$. Find $k$, and find the fifth term under this rule.
\item[(b)] Find the fifth term if instead the $n$th term is given by the polynomial of degree at most $3$ in $n$ that fits the four given terms.
\end{enumerate}
