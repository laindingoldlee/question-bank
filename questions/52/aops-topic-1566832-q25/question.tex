There are twelve different mixed numbers $a\dfrac {b}{c}$ that can be created by substituting three of the numbers $1$, $2$, $3$ and $5$ for $a$, $b$ and $c$, where $b<c$ (and $a$, $b$, $c$ are different). What is the mean of these twelve mixed numbers? Express your answer as a mixed number.
