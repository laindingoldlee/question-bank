The first must lie; otherwise its truthful statement would make itself a liar. Thus not all three are liars. If the second is a knight, its statement directly makes the third a knight. If the second is a liar, the first two are liars, and the first statement being false forces the third to be a knight. Therefore the third is $\boxed{\text{a knight}}$.
