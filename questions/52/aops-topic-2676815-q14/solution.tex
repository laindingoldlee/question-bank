The conditions say $n\equiv0\pmod4$, $n\equiv4\pmod5$ and $n\equiv4\pmod6$. The last two mean $30\mid n-4$, so $n=30m+4$; then $4\mid30m$ means $m$ is even. So $n\equiv4\pmod{60}$: $n=4,64,\dots,964$, which is $\boxed{17}$ numbers.
