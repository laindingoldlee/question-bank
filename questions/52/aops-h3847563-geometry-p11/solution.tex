Extend $BA$ beyond $A$ to $D$ so that $AD=AC$. Then triangle $ACD$ is isosceles, so $\angle ADC=\angle ACD$. In triangle $BCD$, $\angle BCD>\angle ACD=\angle BDC$, hence its opposite side $BD$ is greater than $BC$. Since $BD=BA+AC$, the result follows. The other two inequalities follow by relabeling. 
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(220);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(1,2),B=(0,0),C=(4,0); draw(A--B--C--cycle); label("$A$",A,(-1,-1)); label("$B$",B,(1,-1)); label("$C$",C,(0,1)); pair D=A+abs(A-C)*unit(A-B);draw(A--D--C);label("$D$",D,(0,1));
\end{asy}
