The condition is $xy=n-1$ with $x<y$, so three choices mean three unequal factor pairs. For a nonsquare, this requires six divisors, with form $p^5$ or $p^2q$ for distinct primes; their least possible values are 32 and 12. For a square it requires seven divisors, giving $p^6\ge64$. Thus the least product is twelve, with pairs $(1,12),(2,6),(3,4)$. Therefore $\boxed{n=13}$.
