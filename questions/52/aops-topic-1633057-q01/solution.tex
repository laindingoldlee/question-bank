A point $(x,y)$ is on the parabola exactly when its distance to the focus equals its distance to the directrix:
\[
\sqrt{x^2+y^2}=|y-4|\iff x^2+y^2=y^2-8y+16\iff8y=16-x^2 .
\]
So the parabola is
\[
\boxed{y=2-\dfrac{x^2}{8}}\qquad\left(\text{equivalently }x^2=-8(y-2)\right),
\]
opening downwards, with vertex $(0,2)$ midway between the focus and the directrix.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
real f(real x){return 2-x^2/8;}
draw((-7,0)--(7,0),gray,Arrow(TeXHead));draw((0,-4.5)--(0,5.2),gray,Arrow(TeXHead));
label("$x$",(7,0),E,gray);label("$y$",(0,5.2),N,gray);
draw(graph(f,-6.5,6.5),blue+linewidth(0.9));
draw((-7,4)--(7,4),red);label("$y=4$",(7,4),E,red);
pair F=(0,0),P=(4,0),M=(4,4);
draw(F--(P+(0,-0.05)),deepgreen+dashed);draw(P--M,deepgreen+dashed);
dot(F);dot(P);dot((0,2));
label("$F(0,0)$",F,SW);label("$P(4,0)$",P,NE);label("$(0,2)$",(0,2),NE);
label("$4$",(2,0),S,deepgreen);label("$4$",(4,2.2),E,deepgreen);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

(For example, $P(4,0)$ is on the parabola: it is $4$ from the focus and $4$ from the line $y=4$.)
