Write $t_i=1$ for a knight and $0$ for a knave. The statement is true precisely when the two neighbouring values differ, so $t_{i-1}+t_i+t_{i+1}\equiv0\pmod2$. Subtract adjacent relations to get $t_{i+3}=t_i$. A circular sequence of length $31$, coprime to $3$, must then be constant. The constant value $1$ violates the relation, while $0$ satisfies it. Hence all $\boxed{31}$ residents are knaves.
