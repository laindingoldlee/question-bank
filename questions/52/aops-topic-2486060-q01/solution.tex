The tortoise needs $1\div\dfrac13=3$ hours $=180$ minutes. Running, the hare needs $\dfrac13$ hour $=20$ minutes in total, $10$ minutes for each half. If it sleeps $m$ minutes, it finishes after $20+m$ minutes, and it wins exactly when $20+m<180$, i.e. $m<160$. The largest whole number of minutes is $\boxed{159}$.
