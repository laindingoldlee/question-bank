A tortoise races a hare on a $1$-mile track. The tortoise moves at $\dfrac13$ mile per hour and never stops; the hare moves at $3$ miles per hour. When the hare is halfway along the track, it takes a nap, and when it wakes up it immediately continues at the same speed. What is the largest whole number of minutes the hare can sleep and still win (not tie) the race?
