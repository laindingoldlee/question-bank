For cube side $a$ and sphere radius $r$, $a^3=\dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3$. Thus $k=\dfrac{a\sqrt3}{2r}$ and $k^6=\dfrac{27a^6}{64r^6}=\dfrac{27}{64}\left(\dfrac{4\pi}{3}\right)^2=\boxed{\dfrac{3\pi^2}{4}}$.
