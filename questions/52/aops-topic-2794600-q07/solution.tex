The probabilities of a true positive and false positive are $ 0.05\cdot0.99=0.0495 $ and $ 0.95\cdot0.05=0.0475 $. Conditional on a positive result, the probability is $\dfrac{0.0495}{0.0495+0.0475}=\dfrac{99}{194}$. Thus $\boxed{p+q=293}$.
