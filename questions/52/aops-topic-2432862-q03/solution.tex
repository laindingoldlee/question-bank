If the price is raised by $c$ cents ($0\le c\le197$), the income in cents is
\[I(c)=(100+c)(197-c)=-c^2+97c+19700=19700+\dfrac{97^2}{4}-\left(c-\dfrac{97}{2}\right)^2.\]
This is largest for the whole numbers closest to $\dfrac{97}{2}$, namely $c=48$ and $c=49$, which give
\[148\cdot149=149\cdot148=22052\text{ cents}.\]
The maximum income is $\boxed{\$220.52}$, at a price of $\$1.48$ or $\$1.49$. (Lowering the price, with one more cup sold per cent, would follow the same formula with $c<0$ and gives less.)
