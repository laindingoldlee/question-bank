LemonBerry sells Berry-Lemonade at $1$ dollar per cup, and $197$ cups are sold per day. For every $1$ cent increase in the price, $1$ fewer cup is sold (so at $\$1.10$ only $197-10=187$ cups would be sold). The price is a whole number of cents. What is the maximum daily income LemonBerry can get?
