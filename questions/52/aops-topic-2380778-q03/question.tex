Starting with an empty box, the elf and the reindeer add candy canes to it: $5$ purple candy canes at the end of every week, $4$ green candy canes at the end of every five days, and $8$ red candy canes at the end of every two weeks. How many green and purple candy canes (together) are on the box after one year of $365$ days?
