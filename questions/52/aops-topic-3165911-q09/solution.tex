Triangle $ABE$ is isosceles right with hypotenuse $AB=10$ and legs $5\sqrt2$. Thus $R=5$ and $r=\dfrac{10\sqrt2-10}{2}=5\sqrt2-5$. Therefore $\boxed{R-r=10-5\sqrt2}$.

pair A=(0,0),B=(10,0),C=(10,10),D=(0,10),Ep=(5,5),O=(5,0);real r=5sqrt(2)-5;draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C^^B--D,dashed);draw(circle(O,5),gray);draw(circle((5,r),r),blue);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$E$",Ep,N);label("$10$",(5,0),S);
\end{asy}
