Two lengths $L_1$ and $L_2$ satisfy
\[(L_1+0.05):(L_2+0.05)=15:1.\]
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Express $L_1$ in terms of $L_2$.
\item[(b)] Can $L_1$ and $L_2$ both be determined from this information alone? Explain.
\item[(c)] Find $L_1$ if $L_2=0.2$.
\end{enumerate}
