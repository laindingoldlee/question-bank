The first radicand needs $2x-2\ge0$, i.e. $x\ge1$, and the second needs $\dfrac{1-x}2\ge0$, i.e. $x\le1$. Both hold only at $x=1$, where the denominator is $0$. So $g(x)$ is not defined at any real $x$.

Hence the ``function'' $f$ has domain $\{1\}$, and there is nothing to make continuous: the question as posed is vacuous (no limit of $g(x)$ as $x\to1$ exists, since $g$ is defined nowhere near $1$). $\blacksquare$
