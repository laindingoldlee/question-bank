A $2$ by $41$ rectangle consists of unit squares. The middle unit square of each row is shaded. If a rectangle (with sides along the grid lines) is chosen at random from all the rectangles in the figure, what is the probability that it does not include a shaded square? Express your answer as a common fraction.
