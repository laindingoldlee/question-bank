Twin primes are primes that differ by $2$, such as $5$ and $7$, whose average is $6$. A student notices that for small primes $p$, the number $6p$ is often the average of a pair of twin primes, that is, $6p-1$ and $6p+1$ are both prime.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that the average of any pair of twin primes other than $3,5$ is a multiple of $6$.
\item[(b)] Prove that if $p$ is a prime with $p\equiv1\pmod5$ or $p\equiv4\pmod5$, then $6p-1$ and $6p+1$ are not both prime.
\item[(c)] Find the smallest prime $p$ for which $6p-1$ and $6p+1$ are not both prime.
\end{enumerate}
