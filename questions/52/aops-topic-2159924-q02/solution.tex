Let the train have length $L$ m and speed $v$ m per second. To pass the lamp post, the train moves its own length, so $L=10v$. To cross the bridge (from the front reaching the bridge until the back leaves it), it moves $L+360$ m, so $L+360=30v$. Subtracting, $360=20v$, so $v=18$ and $L=10\cdot18=\boxed{180}$ m.
