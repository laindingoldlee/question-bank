Your rating $r$ in an online game is a real number chosen uniformly at random from the interval $[1000,1500]$. Put $u=r-1000$ and $p=\dfrac{u}{230+u}$. You play a game against me and win with probability $p$. If you win, I challenge you to a rematch with probability $1-p$, and you accept with probability $p$. You win the rematch with probability $p$, and if you have won both games, I get so angry that with probability $1-p$ I summon a demon to haunt your dreams. What is the probability that the evening ends with the demon being summoned?
