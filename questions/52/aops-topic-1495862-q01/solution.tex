Write $z=a+bi$ with $a,b$ real. Then $z^2=a^2-b^2+2abi=i$, so
\[
a^2-b^2=0,\qquad 2ab=1 .
\]
From the second equation $a$ and $b$ have the same sign, and the first gives $a=\pm b$, so $a=b$. Then $2a^2=1$, and $a=b=\pm\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$. The two square roots of $i$ are
\[
z=\pm\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}(1+i).
\]
The principal square root is the one with positive real part (argument $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$, half of the argument $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ of $i$):
\[
\sqrt i=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}+\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\,i}.
\]
Check: $\left(\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}(1+i)\right)^2=\dfrac{1}{2}(1+2i-1)=i$.
