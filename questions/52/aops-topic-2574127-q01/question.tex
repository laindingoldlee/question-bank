I am driving at $60$ miles per hour.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Can I drive fast enough that each mile takes one minute less than it does now?
\item[(b)] How fast must I drive so that each mile takes $10$ seconds less than it does now?
\end{enumerate}
