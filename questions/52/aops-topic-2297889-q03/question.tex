Prove the Congruent Complements Theorem: if $\angle1$ and $\angle2$ are complementary, $\angle3$ and $\angle4$ are complementary, and $\angle2\cong\angle4$, then $\angle1\cong\angle3$. (In particular, two angles complementary to the same angle are congruent.)
