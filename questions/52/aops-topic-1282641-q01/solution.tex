When the diesel train catches up, both trains have covered the same distance from Miami. The diesel train covered $45\cdot4=180$ km in $4$ hours; the cattle train covered the same $180$ km in $14+4=18$ hours. So its average speed was $\dfrac{180}{18}=\boxed{10}$ kilometres per hour.
