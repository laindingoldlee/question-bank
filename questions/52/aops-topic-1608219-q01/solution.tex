Unconditionally the second marble is blue with probability $\dfrac{4}{10}$. Conditional on a red first marble, it is blue with probability $\dfrac{4}{9}$, which differs, so the events are dependent. The probability of one of each color is $\dfrac{6}{10}\dfrac{4}{9}+\dfrac{4}{10}\dfrac{6}{9}=\boxed{\dfrac{8}{15}}$. Drawing simultaneously and then randomly labeling the two marbles gives the same distribution.
