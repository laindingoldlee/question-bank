The students who liked both subjects are those who liked math minus those who liked math but not science: $84-23=61$. By inclusion-exclusion, the number who liked at least one subject is
\[
84+73-61=96,
\]
so $120-96=\boxed{24}$ students disliked both. (Check: $23$ math only, $61$ both, $73-61=12$ science only, and $23+61+12+24=120$.)
