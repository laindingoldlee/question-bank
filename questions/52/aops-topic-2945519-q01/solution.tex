For a positive integer $n$ with $\sin n>0$, $f(n)=n^2(0-0)=0$. Since $\sin1,\sin2,\sin3,\sin9>0$, we get $f(1)=f(2)=f(3)=f(9)=0$, and $f(0)=0$. Also $f(6)=36$, since $\sin6<0$ gives $\lfloor\sin6\rfloor=-1$.

Then $g(3)=3^0\cdot f\!\left(0-0\cdot f\!\left(\dfrac{f(9)}{3^{36}}\right)\right)=f(0)=0$. Next $3^{g(3)}=1$, and $g(1)=1^0\cdot f\bigl(0-0\cdot f(0)\bigr)=f(0)=0$. Hence $h(3)=3^{g(1)}=3^0=\boxed{1}$.
