A captain and 100 crew members each initially have one gold coin. The captain proposes redistributions of the 101 coins into nonnegative integer holdings. Each crew member votes yes if gaining coins, no if losing, and abstains otherwise. A proposal passes when yes votes strictly outnumber no votes; the captain does not vote. Construct a sequence of successful proposals that leaves the captain with 25 coins.
