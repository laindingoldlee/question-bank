First take the coins of 49 crew members and give one additional coin to each of 50 other crew members, using the captain’s initial coin to supply the difference. There are 50 yes votes and 49 no votes. The state is: captain 0, 49 crew with 0, 50 crew with 2, and one crew with 1. Now repeatedly choose two crew holding 2 coins, take their four coins, give one to each of three currently empty-handed crew, and retain one for the captain. Each such proposal receives three yes votes and two no votes. Repeat 25 times, using a fresh pair of two-coin holders each time. The number of zero-coin crew decreases by one per step, from 49 to 24, so three recipients are always available. The captain finishes with $\boxed{25}$ coins.
