The minimum is $\boxed{35}$. To attain it, take $\{0,\pm1,\pm2,\pm2^2,\ldots,\pm2^8\}$. Its distinct products are $0$ and $\pm2^j$ for $0\le j\le16$, totaling $35$. For a lower bound, let there be $p$ positives, $q$ negatives, and $\delta\in\{0,1\}$ zeros. For positive sets of sizes $r,s$, their products include the increasing chain $a_1b_1<\cdots<a_1b_s<a_2b_s<\cdots<a_rb_s$, so there are at least $r+s-1$ products. If both signs occur, negative products number at least $p+q-1$, and products within the larger same-sign group number at least $2\max(p,q)-1$. Including zero gives at least $p+q+2\max(p,q)-2+\delta\ge35$, since $p+q+\delta=19$. If only one nonzero sign occurs, the same chain gives at least $2(19-\delta)-1+\delta\ge36$. This proves the bound.
