Let $H_m=\sum_{k=1}^m\dfrac{1}{k}$. In the alternating sum through $2n$, start with $H_{2n}$ and subtract twice the sum over even denominators. That even sum is $\dfrac{1}{2}H_n$, so the result is $H_{2n}-H_n=\sum_{k=n+1}^{2n}\dfrac{1}{k}$. Taking $n=1000$ proves the stated case.
