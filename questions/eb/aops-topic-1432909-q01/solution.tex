Let $O$ be the centre. Since $CD$ is a diameter, $\angle CHD=90^\circ$ (angle in a semicircle). Also $\angle COK=90^\circ$ because the diameters are perpendicular.

Triangles $COK$ and $CHD$ share the angle at $C$ and both have a right angle, so they are similar, and
\[
\dfrac{CK}{CO}=\dfrac{CD}{CH}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad CK=\dfrac{5\cdot10}{8}=\dfrac{25}{4}.
\]
By Pythagoras in triangle $COK$,
\[
OK=\sqrt{CK^2-CO^2}=\sqrt{\dfrac{625}{16}-\dfrac{400}{16}}=\dfrac{15}{4}.
\]
So $K$ is $\dfrac{15}{4}=3.75$ from the centre, and the two segments of $AB$ are
\[
KB=5-\dfrac{15}{4}=\dfrac{5}{4}=1.25,\qquad AK=5+\dfrac{15}{4}=\dfrac{35}{4}=8.75 .
\]
The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(A)}\ 1.25,\ 8.75}$.
