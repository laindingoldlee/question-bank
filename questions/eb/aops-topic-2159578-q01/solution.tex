Factor each number into primes:
\[300=2^2\cdot3\cdot5^2,\qquad 450=2\cdot3^2\cdot5^2,\qquad 875=5^3\cdot7 .\]
So the product under one square root is $2^3\cdot3^3\cdot5^7\cdot7$, and
\[\sqrt{2^3\cdot3^3\cdot5^7\cdot7}=2\cdot3\cdot5^3\sqrt{2\cdot3\cdot5\cdot7}=\boxed{750\sqrt{210}}.\]
