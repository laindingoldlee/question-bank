Two parallel lines are ``everywhere the same distance apart''. Make this precise and prove it: if $\ell\parallel m$, $P$ and $Q$ are any two points of $\ell$, and $P'$ and $Q'$ are the feet of the perpendiculars from $P$ and $Q$ to $m$, prove that $PP'=QQ'$.
