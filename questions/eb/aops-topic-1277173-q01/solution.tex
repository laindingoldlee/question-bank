The statement can only fail on a card with an even number and a consonant.
\begin{itemize}
\item $4$ must be turned: a consonant on the back would break the rule.
\item $b$ must be turned: an even number on the back would break the rule.
\item $a$ need not be turned: a vowel is allowed with anything.
\item $5$ need not be turned: the rule says nothing about odd numbers.
\end{itemize}
So $\boxed{2}$ cards ($4$ and $b$) must be turned.
