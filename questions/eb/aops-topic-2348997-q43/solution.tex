Let the edge lengths be $x,y,z$ with $xz=50$, $yz=81$, $xy=128$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real a=2.4,b2=1.5,c=1.9;pair dz=(0.75,0.5);
pair O=(0,0);
draw(O--(a,0)--(a,c)--(0,c)--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
draw((a,0)--(a,0)+dz--(a,c)+dz--(0,c)+dz--(0,c),linewidth(0.8));
draw((a,c)--(a,c)+dz,linewidth(0.8));
draw(O--dz,dashed);draw(dz--(a,0)+dz,dashed);draw(dz--(0,c)+dz,dashed);
label("$x$",(a/2,0),S);label("$z$",(0,c/2),W);label("$y$",(a,0)+dz/2,SE);
label("$xz=50$",(1.55,1.25));label("$yz=81$",(a,c/2)+dz/2,fontsize(8pt));label("$xy=128$",(a/2,c)+dz/2,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}

Multiplying, $(xyz)^2=50\cdot81\cdot128=518400=720^2$, so the volume is $xyz=720$ (with $x=\dfrac{80}9$, $y=\dfrac{72}5$, $z=\dfrac{45}8$). The surface area is $2(50+81+128)=518$. The difference is $720-518=\boxed{202}$.
