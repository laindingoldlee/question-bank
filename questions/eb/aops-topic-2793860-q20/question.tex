Let $P=(10^{100},0)$ and $\ell_0$ be $y=x$. For $n\ge1$, $\ell_n$ bisects the acute angle between $\ell_{n-1}$ and the positive $x$ axis. Find the smallest $n$ for which some $X\in\ell_n$ satisfies $PX\le1$.
