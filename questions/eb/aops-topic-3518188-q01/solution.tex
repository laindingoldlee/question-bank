Since $6^2+8^2=10^2$, the triangle is right at $B$, with area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot6\cdot8=24$. If $h$ is the distance from $B$ to $AC$, then $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot10h=24$, giving $h=\dfrac{24}{5}$. This distance is the circle radius: $\boxed{\dfrac{24}{5}}$. 
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
import geometry;
size(260);
pair B=(0,0),A=(6,0),C=(0,8),H=foot(B,A,C);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(circle(B,4.8));draw(B--H,dashed);label("$A$",A,E);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,N);label("$H$",H,NE);label("$r$",H/2,NW);
\end{asy}
