Every day of one week (all seven days) David earns some amount of money; on day $k$ he earns $a_k$ dollars. He decides to give $10\%$ of the week's earnings to charity. Is there a difference between taking $10\%$ of each day's earnings and adding these up, and taking $10\%$ of the total at the end of the week? Explain. (Ignore rounding to whole cents.)
