Since $2^4=16\equiv1\pmod5$ and $3^4=81\equiv1\pmod5$, for all positive integers $a,b$
\[2^{4a}=16^a\equiv1^a=1\pmod5\qquad\text{and}\qquad3^{4b}=81^b\equiv1\pmod5 .\]
Hence $5\mid2^{4a}-3^{4b}$, and in the same way $5\mid2^{4c}-3^{4d}$. A product of two multiples of $5$ is a multiple of $5\cdot5=25$. $\blacksquare$
