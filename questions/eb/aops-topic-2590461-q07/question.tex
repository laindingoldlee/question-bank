Let $a$ and $b$ be positive numbers.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that the product $ab$ is smaller than both $a$ and $b$ if $a<1$ and $b<1$; lies between $a$ and $b$ if one of them is less than $1$ and the other greater than $1$; and is larger than both if $a>1$ and $b>1$. (For example, $0.016\times0.15=0.0024$ is smaller than both factors.)
\item[(b)] Prove that the quotient $a\div b$ is larger than $a$ exactly when $b<1$.
\end{enumerate}
