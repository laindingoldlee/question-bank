\textbf{(a)} Since $a>0$, multiplying the inequality $b<1$ by $a$ gives $ab<a$, and $b>1$ gives $ab>a$. In the same way, $ab<b$ if $a<1$ and $ab>b$ if $a>1$.
\begin{itemize}
\item If $a<1$ and $b<1$: $ab<a$ and $ab<b$.
\item If, say, $a<1<b$: $ab>a$ (because $b>1$) and $ab<b$ (because $a<1$), so $a<ab<b$.
\item If $a>1$ and $b>1$: $ab>a$ and $ab>b$.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{(b)} Since $a>0$ and $b>0$,
\[\dfrac ab>a\iff\dfrac1b>1\iff b<1.\]
So dividing by a number less than $1$ makes a positive number larger (for example $0.32\div0.16=2$), and dividing by a number greater than $1$ makes it smaller. $\blacksquare$
