Separate the product into $\prod\dfrac{n}{n+1}$ and $\prod\dfrac{n+2}{n+1}$. These telescope to $\dfrac{1}{2022}$ and $\dfrac{2023}{2}$ respectively. The result is $\boxed{\dfrac{2023}{4044}}$.
