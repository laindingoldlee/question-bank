Let the semiaxes be $a\ge b$. For an ellipse rotated by angle $\theta$ inside an axis-parallel rectangle, its squared half-width and half-height are
\[
a^2\cos^2\theta+b^2\sin^2\theta=64,\qquad
a^2\sin^2\theta+b^2\cos^2\theta=25.
\]
These formulas follow by maximising the coordinate of $(a\cos t,b\sin t)$ after rotation. Thus $a^2+b^2=89$. The focal distance gives $a^2-b^2=7^2=49$, so $a^2=69,b^2=20$. The area is
\[
\pi ab=\pi\sqrt{1380}=2\pi\sqrt{345}.
\]
Since $345=3\cdot5\cdot23$ is squarefree, $\boxed{m+n=347}$. The orientation exists because $\cos2\theta=\dfrac{39}{49}$ is between $-1$ and $1$.
