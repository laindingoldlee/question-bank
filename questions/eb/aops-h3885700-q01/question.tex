Ellipse $\mathcal{E}$ is tangent to all four sides of a rectangle with length $16$ and width $10$. The distance between the foci of $\mathcal{E}$ is $14$. The area of $\mathcal{E}$ can be written as $\pi m\sqrt{n}$ for positive integers $m$ and $n$ with $n$ not divisible by the square of any prime. Find $m+n$.
