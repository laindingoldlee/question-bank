There are $66$ porcupines, $104$ meerkats, and $130$ bunnies. Each is either a truth-teller or a liar and makes exactly one statement, of type A, B, C, or D:
\begin{itemize}
\item A: Exactly $120$ animals are truth-tellers.
\item B: If at least $50$ porcupines tell the truth, then at least $80$ meerkats are liars.
\item C: At least $40$ bunnies are liars if and only if the total number of truth-tellers is divisible by $4$.
\item D: More meerkats than porcupines tell the truth, and exactly $30$ bunnies tell the truth.
\end{itemize}
The respective counts of speakers of A, B, C, D are $(16,20,18,12)$ for porcupines, $(28,26,30,20)$ for meerkats, and $(36,34,40,20)$ for bunnies. Every liar contributes one token; eight tokens buy a whole smoothie. How many whole smoothies remain after buying as many as possible and serving $11$ people one each?
