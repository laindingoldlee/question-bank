The units digits of $2^1,2^2,2^3,2^4,\ldots$ are $2,4,8,6$ and then repeat with period $4$. Since $241=4\cdot60+1$, the units digit of $2^{241}$ is the same as that of $2^1$, namely $\boxed{2}$.
