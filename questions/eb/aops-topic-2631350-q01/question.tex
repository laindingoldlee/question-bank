There are $4$ cards on a table, each with a number on one side and a letter on the other. The visible faces show $4$, $7$, E and K. Consider the proposition: ``If a card shows a vowel on one side, then it has an even number on the other side.'' Which cards must you turn over to test whether the proposition is true? Explain your answer.
