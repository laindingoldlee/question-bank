Count factors of $3$ among the odd numbers up to $99$ (Legendre-style). Odd multiples of $3$: $3,9,\dots,99$, which is $17$ numbers. Odd multiples of $9$: $9,27,45,63,81,99$, which is $6$. Odd multiples of $27$: $27,81$, which is $2$. Odd multiples of $81$: $81$, which is $1$. So $n=17+6+2+1=\boxed{26}$.
