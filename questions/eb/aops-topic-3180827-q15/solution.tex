Triangle inequalities give $AP+BP\ge AB=5$ and $CP+DP\ge CD=2$. Both equalities hold when $P$ lies on both segments $AB$ and $CD$. These intersect at $P=(\dfrac{9}{4},4)$, which lies inside both. Thus the minimum is $\boxed7$.

pair A=(0,1),B=(3,5),C=(1,4),D=(3,4),P=(2.25,4);draw(A--D--B--C--cycle,gray);draw(A--B^^C--D);dot(P);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,N);label("$C$",C,W);label("$D$",D,E);label("$P$",P,S);
\end{asy}
