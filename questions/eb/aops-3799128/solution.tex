If Enrique is truthful, then Pablo is not, since Pablo would require $R<P<E<R$. Francisco also cannot be truthful, since that would give $R<F<M<E<R$. To have three truthful students, Raul and Manuel must then be truthful, forcing $F<M<E<R<P$, so Pablo has $10$, contradiction. Thus Enrique is not truthful. Pablo and Raul cannot both be truthful because they require opposite orders of $R,E$. Therefore Francisco and Manuel must be truthful, giving $R<F<M<P$. If Raul is the third truthful student, then $E<R<F<M<P$, again giving Pablo $10$. Hence Pablo is truthful, so $R<F<M<P<E$. The grades are $\boxed{R=6,F=7,M=8,P=9,E=10}$.
