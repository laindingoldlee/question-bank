Six teams beat Blair in boys and four in girls. Among the other nine teams, these sets overlap in at least $ 6+4-9=1 $ team, which necessarily beats Blair's total. Thus Blair cannot rank first. Rank 2 is achievable: give Blair scores $(100,100)$; one team $(200,200)$; five teams $(101,1),(102,2),\ldots,(105,5)$; and three teams $(1,101),(2,102),(3,103)$. All individual scores are distinct, Blair has the stated ranks, and only the $(200,200)$ team has total above 200. The answer is $\boxed2 $.
