Start with a $ 4\times4 $ square. Attach a $ 1\times1 $ square at the centre of each side, outside it. Repeatedly attach a square one quarter as long onto each of the three exposed sides of every square just added. Find the sum of the areas of all squares as a reduced fraction $\dfrac{p}{q}$, and give $ p+q $.
