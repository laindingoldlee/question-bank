Let $M$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $O$ to $AB$, so $OM=7$. The triangles $OMA$ and $OMB$ are congruent right triangles ($OA=OB$, common leg $OM$), so $M$ is the midpoint of $AB$ and $MB=6$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real R=sqrt(85);
pair O=(0,0), A=(-6,-7), B=(6,-7), M=(0,-7);
draw(circle(O,R),linewidth(0.9));
draw(A--B,linewidth(1)); draw(O--M,blue); draw(O--B,red);
draw(M+(0.6,0)--M+(0.6,0.6)--M+(0,0.6));
dot(O); dot(A); dot(B); dot(M);
label("$O$",O,N); label("$A$",A,SW); label("$B$",B,SE); label("$M$",M,S);
label("$7$",(O+M)/2,W,blue); label("$6$",(M+B)/2,S); label("$r$",(O+B)/2,NE,red);
\end{asy}

By the Pythagorean theorem in triangle $OMB$, the radius is
\[r=OB=\sqrt{7^2+6^2}=\boxed{\sqrt{85}}.\]
