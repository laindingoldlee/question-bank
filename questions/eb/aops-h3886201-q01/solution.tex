Write the bounding rectangle dimensions as $w,h$, where $w+h=10$ and $1\le w\le9$. There are nine unnotched rectangles. For fixed $w,h$, a proper notch has $(w-1)(h-1)$ choices of dimensions and four choices of corner. Each produces a different polygon, because the bounding box and missing corner recover its construction. Therefore the total is $9+4\sum_{w=1}^9(w-1)(9-w)=9+4\cdot84=\boxed{345}$. Every such notch replaces two boundary segments by equally long new ones, so the perimeter remains $20$.
