Alex and Bob run around a $900$ m circular track in opposite directions, starting at the same time from the same point. Alex runs a lap in $100$ seconds and Bob in $150$ seconds, both at constant speeds. When they meet for the ninth time (not counting the start), how many more metres must Bob run to reach the starting point?
