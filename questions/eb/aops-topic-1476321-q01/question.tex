At a party, each man dances with exactly $3$ women and each woman dances with exactly $4$ men (each pair dances at most once).
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that there cannot be exactly $9$ men.
\item[(b)] For which numbers of men is such a party possible, and how many women are there then?
\end{enumerate}
