We need $\dfrac{N}{100}\cdot880=\dfrac{44N}{5}=k^2$ for a positive integer $k$ with $k^2<880$. Then $5k^2=44N$, so $44=4\cdot11$ divides $5k^2$, hence $4\cdot11\mid k^2$ and therefore $2\cdot11=22\mid k$. Writing $k=22j$, the condition $k^2=484j^2<880$ forces $j=1$, so $k^2=484$ and
\[N=\dfrac{5\cdot484}{44}=55.\]
(Check: $55\%$ of $880$ is $484=22^2$.) The sum of the digits of $N$ is $5+5=\boxed{10}$.
