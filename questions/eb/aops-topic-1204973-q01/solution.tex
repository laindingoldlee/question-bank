$6^{101}+10^{101}=2^{101}\left(3^{101}+5^{101}\right)$. Since $101$ is odd,
\[3^{101}+5^{101}=(3+5)\left(3^{100}-3^{99}\cdot5+\cdots+5^{100}\right).\]
The second factor is a sum of $101$ odd terms, hence odd. So $3^{101}+5^{101}$ is exactly divisible by $8=2^3$, and $n=101+3=\boxed{104}$.
