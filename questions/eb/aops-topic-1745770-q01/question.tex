A car's faulty odometer never shows an odd digit: it counts $0,2,4,6,8,20,22,\dots$, so a distance of $1$ mile is recorded as $2$ and a distance of $5$ miles is recorded as $20$. The car starts a trip with the odometer at $0$, and at the end of the trip the odometer shows $8866$. What is the actual distance travelled, in miles?
