Each barn has positive numbers $g,h$ of goats and geese with $4g+2h=10$, or $2g+h=5$. The only possibilities are $(g,h)=(1,3),(2,1)$. If $t$ barns have the latter pattern, the goat count is $2t+(5-t)=8$, giving $t=3$. Put two goats and one goose in three barns, and one goat and three geese in two barns. This uses exactly eight goats and nine geese.
