Expand the sum as $11001A+10001M+1100T+100I+10E+10K$. Leading digits $A,M$ must be nonzero. A swap argument assigns smaller digits to larger positive weights: the minimum uses digits $0,1,2,3,4,5$, assigns $A=1,M=2$, then $T=0,I=3$ and $\{E,K\}=\{4,5\}$. To justify the placement of zero, moving it from any lower-weight nonleading position to $T$ only decreases the sum; moving a smaller positive digit from a lower weight to $A$ or $M$ also decreases it. The resulting minimum is $11001+20002+300+90=\boxed{31393}$.
