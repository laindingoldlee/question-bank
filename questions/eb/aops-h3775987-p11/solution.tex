The cumulative probabilities imply that each exact failure percentage from $1$ to $99$ has probability $\dfrac{1}{100}$, as does success. Reaching the start of $97\%$ rules out the first $96$ failure outcomes, leaving four equally likely possibilities: failure at $97,98,99$, or success. Therefore the conditional chance of failure at $98\%$ is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{4}}$.
