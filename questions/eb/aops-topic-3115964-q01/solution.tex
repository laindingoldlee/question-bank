Distribute $m-1$: $(m-1)\sum_{k=0}^n m^k=\sum_{k=0}^n(m^{k+1}-m^k)=m^{n+1}-1$, because all intermediate terms cancel. Adding $1$ gives the identity. This also covers $m=1$, without dividing by $m-1$.
