Every student in Jack's class has exactly $\$1.05$, made up of nickels, dimes and quarters (not necessarily all three kinds). No two students have the same combination of coins, and every possible combination is held by some student. Jack is one of the students. What is the probability that Jack cannot give his friend Jenny change for a dollar bill, that is, cannot hand her coins worth exactly $\$1.00$?
