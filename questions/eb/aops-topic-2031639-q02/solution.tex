The first head occurs on flip $n$ with probability $\dfrac{1}{2^n}$, so
\[
E=\sum_{n\ge1}\dfrac{n}{2^n}=\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{2}{4}+\dfrac{3}{8}+\dfrac{4}{16}+\cdots.
\]
Then
\[
\dfrac{E}{2}=\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{2}{8}+\dfrac{3}{16}+\cdots,
\]
and subtracting term by term,
\[
E-\dfrac{E}{2}=\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{8}+\cdots=1.
\]
(The series converges, so these manipulations are valid.) Hence $\dfrac{E}{2}=1$ and the expected winnings are
\[\boxed{2}\ \text{dollars}.\]
