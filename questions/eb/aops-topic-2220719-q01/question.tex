A certain country uses only bills worth $\$15$ and $\$44$. Its ATMs pay out any amount you request, as long as at least one bill of each kind is used. Show that, for every integer $x$, you can withdraw $\$x$ if and only if you cannot withdraw $\$y$, where $x+y=719$.
