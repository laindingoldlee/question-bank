In square $BECF$ the vertices $B$ and $C$ are opposite, so $BC=12$ is its diagonal. Hence triangle $BCF$ is an isosceles right triangle with hypotenuse $BC=12$ and legs $BF=CF=6\sqrt2$. The inradius of a right triangle is $\dfrac{a+b-c}{2}$, so
\[r=\dfrac{6\sqrt2+6\sqrt2-12}{2}=\boxed{6\sqrt2-6}.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(12,0),C=(12,12),D=(0,12),Ep=(6,6),Fp=(18,6);real r=6*sqrt(2)-6;pair I=(18-r*sqrt(2),6);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.8));draw(B--Ep--C--Fp--cycle,linewidth(0.8));draw(circle(I,r),dashed);dot(I);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,S);label("$C$",C,N);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$E$",Ep,W);label("$F$",Fp,E);
label("$12$",(A+B)/2,S,fontsize(8pt));label("$r$",I+(0,-r/2),E,fontsize(7pt));draw(I--(I.x,6-r),dotted);
\end{asy}
