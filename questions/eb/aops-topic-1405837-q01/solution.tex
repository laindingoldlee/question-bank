Rewrite the equation as $x^2-4bx-45b^2=0$ and factor:
\[
(x-9b)(x+5b)=0 .
\]
So $x=9b$ or $x=-5b$, giving $\dfrac{b}{x}=\dfrac{1}{9}$ or $\dfrac{b}{x}=-\dfrac{1}{5}$. The largest possible value is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{9}}$.
