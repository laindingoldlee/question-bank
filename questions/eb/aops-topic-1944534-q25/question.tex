There are $10$ cards laid face down in a row: $8$, $9$, $10$, J, Q, K, A, $2$ and two Jokers. Every $15$ seconds (the first time $15$ seconds after the start) Matt chooses three distinct cards uniformly at random from all $10$ cards and turns them over to look at them, then turns them face down again. Find the expected time until he turns over a Joker for the first time.

$\textbf{(A) }\dfrac{225}{8}\qquad\textbf{(B) }\dfrac{225}{7}\qquad\textbf{(C) }45\qquad\textbf{(D) }\dfrac{225}{4}\qquad\textbf{(E) }\dfrac{450}{7}$
