In one turn the probability of seeing no Joker is $\dfrac{\binom83}{\binom{10}3}=\dfrac{56}{120}=\dfrac{7}{15}$, so a turn shows a Joker with probability $p=\dfrac{8}{15}$, independently of the other turns. The number of turns $X$ until the first Joker is geometric, with
\[
E[X]=\dfrac{1}{p}=\dfrac{15}{8} .
\]
Each turn takes $15$ seconds, so the expected time is $15\cdot\dfrac{15}{8}=\boxed{\textbf{(A) }\dfrac{225}{8}}$ seconds.
