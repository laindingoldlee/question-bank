Two circles with radii $10$ and $7.5$ intersect at $A$ and $B$, their common chord $AB$ has length $12$, and their centres lie on opposite sides of the line $AB$. A segment $EF$ passes through $A$, with $E$ on the first circle and $F$ on the second. Find the greatest possible length of $EF$.
