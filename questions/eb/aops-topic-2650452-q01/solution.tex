There are $9000$ four-digit numbers. Those without a $1$: $8\cdot9^3=5832$ (first digit from $\{2,\dots,9\}$, the others from $9$ digits). Likewise $5832$ have no $2$. Those with neither: $7\cdot8^3=3584$. By inclusion--exclusion the number containing both is
\[9000-5832-5832+3584=\boxed{920}.\]
