Find
\[
\sqrt{1+\dfrac{1}{1^2}+\dfrac{1}{2^2}}+\sqrt{1+\dfrac{1}{2^2}+\dfrac{1}{3^2}}+\cdots+\sqrt{1+\dfrac{1}{2011^2}+\dfrac{1}{2012^2}},
\]
and generalise the result to the sum of the first $n$ such terms.
