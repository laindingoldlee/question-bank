There are nine two-digit palindromes $ 11,22,\ldots,99 $ and ninety three-digit palindromes, with nine choices for the first digit and ten for the middle. The only four-digit palindrome below $ 1009 $ is $ 1001 $. Hence there are $\boxed{100}$.
