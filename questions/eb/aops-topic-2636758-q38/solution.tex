The totals are the numbers $5a+8b+7c$ with $a,b,c\ge0$. The representable numbers up to $17$ are
\[0,\ 5,\ 7,\ 8,\ 10,\ 12,\ 13,\ 14,\ 15,\ 16,\ 17,\]
for example $12=5+7$, $13=5+8$, $14=7+7$, $15=7+8$, $16=8+8$. Since $12,13,14,15,16$ are all representable, adding $5$s gives every number from $12$ on. And $11$ is not representable: $11-5=6$, $11-7=4$ and $11-8=3$ are not representable. So the answer is $\boxed{11}$.
