Duncan has $12$ congruent equilateral triangles made of paper. He uses six of them to make a regular hexagon. Then he attaches each of the other six triangles along a different side of the hexagon, pointing outwards, so that the outline becomes a $12$-sided star-shaped polygon. By what percent does the perimeter increase from the hexagon to the $12$-sided polygon?
