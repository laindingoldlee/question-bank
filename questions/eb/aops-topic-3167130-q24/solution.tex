Scale the five-hour arrival interval to $[0,1]$. The condition is that the largest arrival minus the smallest is at most $r=\dfrac{1}{5}$. For five independent uniform points, condition on which is the minimum $m$. With probability density $5\,dm$, all four others must lie in $[m,\min(m+r,1)]$, giving probability $5\int_0^{1-r}r^4\,dm+5\int_{1-r}^1(1-m)^4\,dm=5r^4-4r^5$. At $r=\dfrac{1}{5}$ this equals $\boxed{\dfrac{21}{3125}}$. Ties have probability zero.
