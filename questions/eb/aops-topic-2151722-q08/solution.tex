We need $\dbinom{4}{M}=\dbinom{4}{1}=4$ with $0\le M\le4$. The values of $\dbinom{4}{M}$ for $M=0,1,2,3,4$ are $1,4,6,4,1$, so $M=1$ or $M=3$ (by the symmetry $\dbinom{4}{M}=\dbinom{4}{4-M}$). Since $M\ne1$, we get $M=\boxed{3}$.
