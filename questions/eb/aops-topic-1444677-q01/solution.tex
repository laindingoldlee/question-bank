By symmetry you win the series with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. (Imagine all five games are always played; the winner of the series is the player who wins at least three of them.)
\begin{itemize}
\item You win in $3$ games: $HHH$, probability $\dfrac{1}{8}$.
\item You win in $4$ games: you win game $4$ and exactly $2$ of the first $3$: $\dbinom32\cdot\dfrac{1}{16}=\dfrac{3}{16}$.
\item You win in $5$ games: you win game $5$ and exactly $2$ of the first $4$: $\dbinom42\cdot\dfrac{1}{32}=\dfrac{6}{32}=\dfrac{3}{16}$.
\end{itemize}
(Check: $\dfrac{1}{8}+\dfrac{3}{16}+\dfrac{3}{16}=\dfrac{1}{2}$.) Dividing by $P(\text{you win})=\dfrac{1}{2}$, the conditional probabilities are
\[
P(3\text{ games})=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{4}},\qquad P(4\text{ games})=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{8}},\qquad P(5\text{ games})=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{8}}.
\]
