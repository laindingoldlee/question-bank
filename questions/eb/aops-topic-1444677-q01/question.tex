You and a friend play a best-of-five series: each game is decided by a fair coin flip (heads you win the game, tails your friend wins), and the first player to win three games wins the series. Given that you win the series, what is the probability that the series lasted exactly $3$ games? exactly $4$ games? exactly $5$ games?
