$141=3\cdot47$ and $161=7\cdot23$ are composite. For $151$, the primes at most $\sqrt{151}<13$ are $2,3,5,7,11$; none divides it, so it is prime. Thus the classifications are $\boxed{\text{composite, prime, composite}}$.
