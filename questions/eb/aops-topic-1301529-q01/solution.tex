Let $x=2^{2009}$, so $A=\dfrac{x+1}{2x+1}$ and $B=\dfrac{2x+1}{4x+1}$. Both denominators are positive, so compare the cross products:
\[
(x+1)(4x+1)-(2x+1)^2=\left(4x^2+5x+1\right)-\left(4x^2+4x+1\right)=x>0 .
\]
Hence $\boxed{A>B}$. (Intuitively $A=\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{2(2x+1)}$ and $B=\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{2(4x+1)}$, and $A$ is further above $\dfrac{1}{2}$.)
