Since $f=4c$ and the digits are distinct, $c=1$ or $2$. If $c=1$, then $f=4,e=3$; but $40a+4b=10d+3$ equates an even and odd integer, impossible. Thus $c=2,f=8,e=6$. Since $d=8-a$, the block equation becomes $40a+4b=86-10a$, or $25a+2b=43$. With $a\ge1$ and $0\le b\le9$, this forces $a=1,b=9$, then $d=7$. All digits are distinct, so the integer is $\boxed{192768}$.
