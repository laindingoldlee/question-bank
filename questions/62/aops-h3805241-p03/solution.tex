The triple-angle identity identifies $r=\cos40^\circ$ and $t=\cos50^\circ=\sin40^\circ$; the other roots correspond to angles $160^\circ,280^\circ$ and $170^\circ,290^\circ$, respectively, and are smaller. Factoring the expression gives $r^2(r+1)(r^2-t^2)^2=\cos^240^\circ(1+\cos40^\circ)\cos^280^\circ$. Repeated double-angle identities give $\cos20^\circ\cos40^\circ\cos80^\circ=\dfrac{1}{8}$, while $1+\cos40^\circ=2\cos^220^\circ$. Thus the value is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{32}}$.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

pair O=(0,0),P=dir(40),H=(P.x,0);draw(arc(O,1,0,90),gray);draw(O--H--P--cycle,blue);label("$r=\cos40^\circ$",midpoint(O--H),S);label("$t=\sin40^\circ$",midpoint(H--P),E);label("$40^\circ$",(.23,.08));
\end{asy}
