Square the unknown quantity:
\[
\left(x+\dfrac{1}{x}\right)^2=x^2+2+\dfrac{1}{x^2}=3+2=5 .
\]
So $x+\dfrac{1}{x}=\pm\sqrt5$. Both values occur for real $x$: $x+\dfrac{1}{x}=\sqrt5$ gives $x^2-\sqrt5\,x+1=0$, with discriminant $5-4=1>0$, and $x=-\left(\text{such a root}\right)$ gives $-\sqrt5$. Hence the possible values are $\boxed{\pm\sqrt5}$ (and $\sqrt5$ if $x$ is known to be positive).
