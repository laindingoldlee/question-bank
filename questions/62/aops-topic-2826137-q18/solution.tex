Factor the difference as $\dfrac{1}{2}(a+b+c)((a-b)^2+(b-c)^2+(c-a)^2)$. Thus either $a+b+c=0$ or $a=b=c$. For a fixed $a$, the number of pairs $b,c$ in the range with sum $-a$ is $201-|a|$. Summing gives $201^2-2(1+\cdots+100)=30301$. There are $201$ diagonal triples, with only $(0,0,0)$ counted already. The total is $30301+201-1=\boxed{30501}$.
