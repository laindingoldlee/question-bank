A circle with centre $O$ has diameter $AB$. Circles with diameters $AO$ and $BO$ are drawn. A fourth circle is externally tangent to the two smaller circles and internally tangent to the largest circle. Let $p$ be the ratio of the area of the smallest of the four circles to the area of the largest. Compute $1000p$, rounded to the nearest integer.
