The least common denominator is $168$:
\[
\dfrac{5}{7}+\dfrac{3}{8}+\dfrac{1}{6}=\dfrac{120+63+28}{168}=\dfrac{211}{168}=1+\dfrac{43}{168}.
\]
Long division of $43$ by $168$: $430=2\cdot168+94$, $940=5\cdot168+100$, $1000=5\cdot168+160$, $1600=9\cdot168+88$, so $\dfrac{43}{168}=0.2559\ldots$ and the sum is $1.2559\ldots$. The fourth digit after the decimal point (the ten-thousandths digit) is $\boxed{9}$, answer $\textbf{(D)}$.

(A quicker route with decimals: $\dfrac{5}{7}=0.714285\ldots$, $\dfrac{3}{8}=0.375$, $\dfrac{1}{6}=0.166666\ldots$; the sum is $1.255952\ldots$, and the digits beyond the fourth place are far from causing a carry.)
