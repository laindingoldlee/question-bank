For each three-element subset, there are 2 cyclic orders and $(2014)!$ permutations of the remaining elements. The probability that this subset is a 3-cycle is $\dfrac{2(2014)!}{2017!}$. Sum its indicator over $\binom{2017}3$ subsets to get $\binom{2017}3\dfrac{2(2014)!}{2017!}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{3}}$.
