Triangle $ABC$ is isosceles with $A=(2,7)$, $B=(-2,0)$ and $C=(3,-1)$. Triangle $ABC$ is reflected over $BC$ to form triangle $A'BC$. Triangle $A'BC$ is reflected over $A'C$ to form triangle $A'B'C$. Triangle $A'B'C$ is reflected over $B'C$ to form triangle $A''B'C$. What is the slope of line $AA''$?
