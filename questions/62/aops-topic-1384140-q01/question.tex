Let $r$ and $k$ be positive integers. A student claims: ``If $rk$ has $y$ positive divisors, then there are exactly $y$ ordered pairs of positive integers $(m,n)$ with $\gcd(m,n)=r$ and $\operatorname{lcm}(m,n)=k$, because $mn=rk$.''
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that the claim is false.
\item[(b)] Find the correct number of such ordered pairs in terms of $r$ and $k$.
\end{enumerate}
