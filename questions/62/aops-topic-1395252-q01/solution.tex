Look for multipliers $p,q,r$ such that $p(x+2y+3z)+q(2x+3y+z)+r(3x+y+3z)$ has equal coefficients of $x$, $y$, $z$:
\[
p+2q+3r=2p+3q+r=3p+q+3r .
\]
The first equality gives $p+q=2r$ and the second gives $p=2q-2r$; together $3q=4r$, so $(p,q,r)=(2,4,3)$ works. Then
\[
2(x+2y+3z)+4(2x+3y+z)+3(3x+y+3z)=19x+19y+19z=2\cdot6+4\cdot8+3\cdot10=74,
\]
so $x+y+z=\boxed{\dfrac{74}{19}}$. (Indeed the system has the unique solution $x=\dfrac{46}{19}$, $y=\dfrac{16}{19}$, $z=\dfrac{12}{19}$.)
