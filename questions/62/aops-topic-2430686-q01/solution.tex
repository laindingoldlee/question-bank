At the start the cookies (diameter $8$ cm) fill the $200$ cm shelf exactly, so there are $\dfrac{200}{8}=25$ cookies.

With $k$ cookies, the centres of the end cookies are $4$ cm from the ends of the shelf, so the first and last centres are $200-8=192$ cm apart, and there are $k-1$ equal gaps: each gap is $\dfrac{192}{k-1}$ cm. The second and fifth centres are $3$ gaps apart, so
\[\dfrac{3\cdot192}{k-1}=82\dfrac27=\dfrac{576}{7}\iff k-1=7\iff k=8.\]
So $25-8=17$ cookies have been eaten, one per day, the first on a Monday. The $17$th cookie is eaten $16$ days after the first, and $16=2\cdot7+2$, so today is two days after Monday: $\boxed{\text{Wednesday}}$.
