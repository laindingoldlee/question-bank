A chef has a shelf $200$ cm long. He arranges circular cookies of radius $4$ cm in one straight row along the shelf, touching each other, from one end of the shelf to the other. Every day he takes one cookie away and eats it, and then spaces out the remaining cookies so that the first and last cookies are again at the two ends of the shelf and the distances between the centres of neighbouring cookies are all equal. After some time he measures the distance between the centres of the second and the fifth cookie: it is $82\dfrac{2}{7}$ cm. He had already eaten that day's cookie before measuring, and he ate the first cookie on a Monday. What day of the week is it?
