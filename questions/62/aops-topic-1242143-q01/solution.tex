The first three places are an ordered selection of $3$ of the $22$ horses, all equally likely: $22\cdot21\cdot20=9240$ ways, of which $3!=6$ use the three females. The probability is $\dfrac{6}{9240}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{1540}}$.
