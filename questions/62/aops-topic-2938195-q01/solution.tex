Let $t=\tan A$ (so $t\ne0,1$). The left side is
\[\dfrac t{1-\dfrac1t}+\dfrac{\dfrac1t}{1-t}=\dfrac{t^2}{t-1}-\dfrac1{t(t-1)}=\dfrac{t^3-1}{t(t-1)}=\dfrac{t^2+t+1}t=1+t+\dfrac1t.\]
Finally, $t+\dfrac1t=\dfrac{\sin^2A+\cos^2A}{\sin A\cos A}=\sec A\csc A$. $\blacksquare$
