From $BE^2=OB^2+OE^2$, obtain $OB^2=20-4=16$, so $B=(4,0)$ and $C=(8,0)$. In right triangle $BCD$, the side opposite $30^\circ$ is $BC=4$, giving $CD=4\sqrt3$. Thus $D=(8,4\sqrt3)$ and
\[DE=\sqrt{64+(4\sqrt3-2)^2}=\sqrt{116-16\sqrt3}\approx9.40.\]
It rounds to $\boxed9$.
