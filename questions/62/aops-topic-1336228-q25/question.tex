A house has $10$ rooms. Ten boys each stay in a different room and count the doors in their room. Adding up all their counts they get $25$. Let $N$ be the number of doors that lead outside the house (every door either joins two rooms or joins a room to the outside). Which of the following cannot be true?

$\textbf{(A) }N=7\qquad\textbf{(B) }N=5\qquad\textbf{(C) }N=3\qquad\textbf{(D) }N=2$
