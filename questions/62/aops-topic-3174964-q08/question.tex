A runner completes eight laps of a $300$-meter track: the first two at $2$ meters per second, then two each at $1.5$, $1$, and $0.5$ meters per second. A dog runs continuously at $12$ kilometers per hour during the entire run. Find the dog's total distance, rounded to the nearest $100$ meters.
