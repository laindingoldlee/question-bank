Both primes are odd ($2+98$ fails). Checking $p\le50$: $3+97$, $11+89$, $17+83$, $29+71$, $41+59$, $47+53$ work, and $5,7,13,19,23,31,37,43$ give the composites $95,93,87,81,77,69,63,57$. So there are $\boxed{6}$ sets.
