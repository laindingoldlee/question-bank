Partition by odd part into the eleven chains $(1,2,4,8,16)$, $(3,6,12)$, $(5,10,20)$, $(7,14)$, $(9,18)$, and singletons 11, 13, 15, 17, 19, 21. At most one may be selected per chain, so an 11-element set selects exactly one from each. The fixed 15 and 21 exclude 1, 3, 5, 7; hence 14 is forced. This also excludes 2. The remaining choices are one of 4, 8, 16; one of 6, 12; one of 10, 20; and one of 9, 18. If 4 is selected, 12 and 20 are excluded, forcing 6 and 10; then 18 is excluded by 6, giving one choice. If 8 or 16 is selected, the only remaining cross-chain conflict is the pair 6, 18. Among the $2\cdot2\cdot2=8$ choices in the other chains, two contain that pair, leaving 6. Thus there are $1+6+6=\boxed{13}$ sets.
