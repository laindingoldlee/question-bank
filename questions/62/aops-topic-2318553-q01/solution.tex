\textbf{(a)} The throne needs $75\cdot9=675$ diamonds. Each trade gives $5$ diamonds, so $\dfrac{675}{5}=135$ trades are needed, using $135\cdot2=\boxed{270}$ stacks of mycelium (that is, $270\cdot64=17280$ blocks).

\textbf{(b)} $\dfrac{270}{27}=\boxed{10}$ shulker boxes, exactly full.
