A strictly increasing geometric sequence of positive integers $a_1, a_2, a_3,...$ satisfies the following properties:

(a) Each term leaves a common remainder when divided by $7$

(b) The first term is an integer from $1$ to $6$

(c) The common ratio is an perfect square

Let $N$ be the smallest possible value of $\dfrac{a_{2021}}{a_1}$. Find the remainder when $N$ is divided by $100$.
