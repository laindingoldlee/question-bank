Let the ratio be the integer square $r>1$. Since $a_1$ is nonzero modulo $7$, equality of the first two residues forces $r\equiv1\pmod7$. The least square greater than $1$ with that property is $36$ (the squares $4,9,16,25$ do not qualify). Hence $N=36^{2020}$. Modulo $100$, powers of $36$ cycle as $36,96,56,16,76$ and then repeat. Since $2020$ is divisible by $5$, the answer is $\boxed{76}$.
