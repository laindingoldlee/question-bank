Choose $2j$ endpoints and pair them without crossings. The number of such pairings of selected cyclic endpoints is the Catalan number $C_j$. Thus the total is $\sum_{j=0}^3\binom6{2j}C_j=1+15+30+5=\boxed{51}$. The empty drawing is the $j=0$ term.

import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);

\end{asy}
