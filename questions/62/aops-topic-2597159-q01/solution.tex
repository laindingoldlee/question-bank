A player of skill $p$ beats a player of skill $q$ with probability $\dfrac p{p+q}$. The three pairings are equally likely.
\begin{itemize}
\item $\{1,2\},\{3,4\}$: $3$ and $4$ meet in the first round, so the probability is $0$.
\item $\{1,3\},\{2,4\}$: both must win: $\dfrac34\cdot\dfrac46=\dfrac12$.
\item $\{1,4\},\{2,3\}$: $\dfrac45\cdot\dfrac35=\dfrac{12}{25}$.
\end{itemize}
The probability is
\[\dfrac13\left(0+\dfrac12+\dfrac{12}{25}\right)=\dfrac13\cdot\dfrac{49}{50}=\dfrac{49}{150},\]
so $m+n=\boxed{199}$.
