Taking logarithms, $A^B=B^A$ means $\dfrac{\ln A}{A}=\dfrac{\ln B}{B}$. The function $\dfrac{\ln t}{t}$ increases on $(0,e)$ and decreases on $(e,\infty)$, so the smaller number satisfies $B<e$: $B=1$ or $2$. $B=1$ gives $A=1$, not distinct. $B=2$ needs $2^A=A^2$ with $A>2$; $A=3$ fails ($8\ne9$), $A=4$ works, and $2^A>A^2$ for $A\ge5$. So $A=4$, $B=2$, $X=16$, and $X^2=\boxed{256}$ (the only possible value).
