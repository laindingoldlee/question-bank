A way of taking the vitamins is an ordered sequence of $1$s and $2$s with sum $10$. Let $a_n$ be the number of such sequences with sum $n$. Looking at the first day, the first term is $1$ (leaving sum $n-1$) or $2$ (leaving sum $n-2$), so
\[
a_n=a_{n-1}+a_{n-2},\qquad a_1=1,\ a_2=2.
\]
Hence $a_3=3$, $a_4=5$, $a_5=8$, $a_6=13$, $a_7=21$, $a_8=34$, $a_9=55$, and $a_{10}=89$.

(Directly: with $k$ two-vitamin days there are $10-2k$ one-vitamin days, giving $\binom{10-k}{k}$ orders, and $1+9+28+35+15+1=89$.)

The answer is $\boxed{89}$.
