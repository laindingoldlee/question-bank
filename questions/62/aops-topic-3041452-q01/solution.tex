Let $w$ and $s$ be the walking and skating speeds in distance units per minute. Equal route lengths give $25w+13s=11w+20s$, hence $14w=7s$ and $s=2w$. The route length is $25w+13(2w)=51w$, so walking alone takes $\boxed{51\text{ minutes}}$.
