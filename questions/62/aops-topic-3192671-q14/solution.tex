The roots have the form $50-d,50+d$ for a positive integer $d$, giving $k=2500-d^2$. Divisibility by $14$ requires $d^2\equiv8\pmod{14}$. In particular $d$ is even. The first possibilities $d=2,4,6$ have squared residues $4,2,8$, so the smallest valid $d$ is $6$. Thus the largest $k$ is $2500-36=\boxed{2464}$.
