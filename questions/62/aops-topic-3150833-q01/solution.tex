By unique prime factorisation, every divisor has a unique exponent tuple $(f_1,\ldots,f_r)$ with $ 0\le f_j\le e_j $. Conversely every such tuple constructs a divisor. The independent choices multiply to $\boxed{\prod_{j=1}^r(e_j+1)}$. For $ N=1 $, the empty product equals one, its single positive divisor.
