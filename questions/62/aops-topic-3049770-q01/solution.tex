The equation is $10A+B=6A+6B+8$, giving $4A-5B=8$. Modulo 5, $A\equiv2\pmod5$, so $A=2$ or 7. These give $B=0$ or 4, respectively. Therefore $A-B$ can be $\boxed2$ or $\boxed3$; the valid numbers are 20 and 74.
