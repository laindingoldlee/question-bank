The six angles total $90^\circ$, so $[ABC]=12\cdot\dfrac{18}{2}=108$. Each small triangle has area one half the corresponding adjacent-ray product times $\sin15^\circ$. Adding their areas gives $K\dfrac{\sin15^\circ}{2}=108$. Therefore
\[
K=\dfrac{216}{\sin15^\circ}=216(\sqrt6+\sqrt2).
\]
Thus $\boxed{a+b+c=216+6+2=224}$.
