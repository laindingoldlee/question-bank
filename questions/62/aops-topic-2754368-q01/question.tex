Twelve chairs are equally spaced around a round table. The chairs are fixed in place, so two seatings that differ by a rotation are different. Seven different people are in the room, and each person either sits in one of the chairs or stands. No two seated people may be in adjacent chairs. How many arrangements are possible? (The arrangement in which everyone stands counts.)
