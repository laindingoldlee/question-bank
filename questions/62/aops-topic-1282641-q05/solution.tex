The total weekly salary of all $20$ people is $20\cdot1275=25500$ dollars, and the teachers earn $9\cdot1000=9000$ of it. The $11$ assistant principals earn $25500-9000=16500$ dollars, a mean of $\dfrac{16500}{11}=\boxed{\$1500}$.
