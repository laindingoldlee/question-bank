The T positions are uniform among $\binom62=15$ pairs. The pair $\{3,4\}$ wins regardless of the coin. Exactly two other pairs, $\{2,4\}$ and $\{3,5\}$, can become $\{3,4\}$ in one adjacent swap; they win only on heads. Thus $\dfrac{1}{15}+\dfrac{2}{15}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{15}}$.
