Triangle $ACM$ is right at $C$, and $\angle CAM=15^\circ$, so $AC=6\cos15^\circ$. By the sine law in $ABC$,
\[BC=AC\dfrac{\sin15^\circ}{\sin30^\circ}=12\sin15^\circ\cos15^\circ=6\sin30^\circ=\boxed3.\]
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(0,0),M=(6,0),C=6cos(15*pi/180)*dir(15),B=(6cos(15*pi/180)*sqrt(2),0);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(C--M,blue);label("$A$",A,(-1,-1));label("$B$",B,(1,-1));label("$C$",C,(0,1));label("$M$",M,(0,-1));label("$6$",(3,0),(0,-1));
\end{asy}
