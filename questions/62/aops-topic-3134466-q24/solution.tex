All $3^4=81$ action sequences are equally likely. At least three actual moves are needed. With exactly three moves, all must have the same direction; choose one of two directions and one of four positions for the stay, giving 8 sequences. With four moves, either all share a direction (2 sequences), or three share one direction and the opposite move occurs first or last (4 sequences). An opposite move in a middle position fails to cover the square, and two moves in each direction also fail. Thus 14 sequences work, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{14}{81}}$.
