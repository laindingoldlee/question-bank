The multiples of $6$ in this range are $-96,-90,\dots,0,\dots,198$. The terms from $-96$ to $96$ cancel in pairs, leaving
\[102+108+\cdots+198 .\]
This arithmetic sequence has $\dfrac{198-102}{6}+1=17$ terms with average $\dfrac{102+198}{2}=150$, so the sum is $17\cdot150=\boxed{2550}$.
