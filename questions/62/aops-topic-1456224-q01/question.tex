Find the sum of the infinite series
\[
\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{3}{16}+\dfrac{5}{64}+\cdots+\dfrac{2n-1}{4^n}+\cdots ,
\]
by writing each term as a difference $f(n-1)-f(n)$ so that the partial sums telescope.
