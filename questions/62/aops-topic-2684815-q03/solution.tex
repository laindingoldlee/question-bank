Let $u=\sqrt[3]2$, so $u^3=2$. Since $(u-1)(1+u+u^2)=u^3-1=1$, we get $M=(1+u)(u-1)=u^2-1$. Also $\sqrt[3]{16}=2u$, so $N=\dfrac{2u-1}{u^2+1}$. Then
\[\dfrac MN=\dfrac{(u^2-1)(u^2+1)}{2u-1}=\dfrac{u^4-1}{2u-1}=\dfrac{2u-1}{2u-1}=\boxed{1}.\]
