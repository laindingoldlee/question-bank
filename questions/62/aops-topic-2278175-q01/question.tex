Triangle $ABC$ has $\angle BAC=90^\circ$, and $AD$ is the altitude from $A$ to $BC$. The circle with centre $A$ and radius $AD$ meets the circumcircle of $ABC$ at $X$ and $Y$. Prove that the line $XY$ passes through the midpoint of $AD$.
