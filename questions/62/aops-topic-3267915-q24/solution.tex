Since $\sin161^\circ=\sin19^\circ$, product-to-sum gives $2\sin19^\circ\sin38^\circ=\cos19^\circ-\cos57^\circ$. Also $\cos57^\circ=\sin33^\circ$, so the left side is $\cos19^\circ=\sin71^\circ$. The positive-angle solutions are $n\equiv71,109\pmod{360}$; hence the least positive integer is $\boxed{71}$.
