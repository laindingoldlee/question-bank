Coordinates $A=(0,0)$, $B=(1,0)$, $D=(0,1)$ give $E=(\dfrac{2}{3},\dfrac{2}{3})$. Split along $AE$: triangles $ABE$ and $ADE$ each have area $\dfrac{1}{3}$. The quadrilateral area is $\boxed{\dfrac{2}{3}}$.
\begin{asy}
import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(1,0),C=(1,1),D=(0,1),E=(2/3,2/3);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(B--E--D);draw(A--C,dashed);label("$A$",A,(-1,-1));label("$B$",B,(1,-1));label("$C$",C,(1,1));label("$D$",D,(-1,1));label("$E$",E,(1,0));
\end{asy}
