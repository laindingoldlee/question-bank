With $q$ quarters and $d$ dimes, in cents: $25q+10d=615$ and $10q+25d=750$. Adding, $35(q+d)=1365$, so $q+d=39$; subtracting, $15(q-d)=-135$, so $q-d=-9$. Hence $q=\boxed{15}$ (and $d=24$).
