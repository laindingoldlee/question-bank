First arrange the four genre blocks in $4!=24$ ways. Then arrange the books inside the blocks in $x!$, $2!$, $3!$ and $3!$ ways. By the multiplication principle the number of arrangements is
\[24\cdot x!\cdot2\cdot6\cdot6=1728\cdot x! .\]
Setting $1728\cdot x!=3456$ gives $x!=2$, so $x=\boxed{2}$.
