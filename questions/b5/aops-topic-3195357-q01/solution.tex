Each monomial is $a^ib^jc^k$ with $i,j,k\ge0$ and $i+j+k=3$. Every such choice occurs with positive multinomial coefficient. Stars and bars gives $\binom52=\boxed{10}$. Before collecting like terms there are $3^3=27$ products, which is a different count.
