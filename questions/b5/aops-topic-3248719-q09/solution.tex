The graph of $f^n$ has $2^n$ successive linear branches, alternating between increasing from zero to one and decreasing from one to zero. Each branch crosses the diagonal $y=x$ exactly once. No shared interior endpoint is a diagonal intersection, since those endpoints have height zero or one but interior abscissa. Thus $f^n(x)=x$ has exactly $2^n$ solutions. A point fixed by $f^{12}$ has least period dividing $12$. Every proper divisor of $12$ divides $6$ or $4$, and their common fixed points have period dividing $\gcd(6,4)=2$. Inclusion--exclusion gives $2^{12}-2^6-2^4+2^2=\boxed{4020}$.
