Given which five numbers are selected, all $5!=120$ orders are equally likely. In a mountain order the largest number must be in the middle. The other four numbers are split into the two on the left, which must be increasing, and the two on the right, which must be decreasing; there are $\dbinom42=6$ ways to choose the left pair, and then the order is forced. So $6$ of the $120$ orders work, and the probability is $\dfrac{6}{120}=\boxed{\dfrac1{20}}$.
