Use $A=(0,3),B=(0,0),C=(4,0)$. The angle-bisector theorem gives $B T = \dfrac{3}{2}$. The circumcircle is $(x-2)^2 + (y - \dfrac{3}{2})^2 = \dfrac{25}{4}$. Intersecting $A + t (\dfrac{3}{2} , - 3)$ with it gives the second intersection $X=(2,-1)$, so $Y=(2,0)$. Intersecting $A+t(2,-3)$ gives $t = \dfrac{17}{13}$ and $P = (\dfrac{34}{13} , - \dfrac{12}{13})$. Thus $\boxed{P B = \dfrac{10 \sqrt{13}}{13}}$; in the original radical format, $m+n+p=36$.

\end{asy}
