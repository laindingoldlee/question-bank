Let the square be $n^2$ with $n$ a positive integer. Since $24=2^3\cdot3$, we need $2^3\mid n^2$ and $3\mid n^2$. The exponent of $2$ in $n^2$ is twice that in $n$, so it is at least $3$ exactly when $4\mid n$; and $3\mid n^2$ exactly when $3\mid n$. So the condition is $12\mid n$. Also $n^2<10^6$ means $n\le999$. The multiples of $12$ from $12$ to $996$ number $\dfrac{996}{12}=\boxed{83}$.
