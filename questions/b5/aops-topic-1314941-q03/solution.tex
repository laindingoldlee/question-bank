A boy has both eyes closed only if he is one of the third of the boys who closed the left eye; a girl has both eyes closed only if she is one of the third of the girls who closed the right eye. So exactly one third of the boys and one third of the girls, $\dfrac{33}{3}=11$ students in all, had both eyes closed, and $33-11=\boxed{22}$ students could peek.
