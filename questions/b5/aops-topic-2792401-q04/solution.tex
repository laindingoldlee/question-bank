Compare two paths differing only in the order of one horizontal and one vertical step through a $2\times2$ block. The first condition makes the two off-diagonal cell values equal. The other direction condition makes the other diagonal pair equal. Thus throughout the board all cells of one checkerboard colour have a common value $a$, and all cells of the other have a common value $b$: diagonal moves connect each colour class. There are 8 cells of each colour, so the total is $8(a+b)$, divisible by 8. But 500 is not divisible by 8.

\end{asy}
