For positive integers $n$ and $m$, each exterior angle of a regular $n$-sided polygon is $45$ degrees larger than each exterior angle of a regular $m$-sided polygon. One example is $n=4$ and $m=8$, because the exterior angles of a square and a regular octagon are $90^\circ$ and $45^\circ$. What is the greatest possible value of $m$?
