Five socks can have counts $(3,1,1)$, allowing only one disjoint matching pair. Conversely if there were at most one pair, one colour could have at most three socks and each other colour at most one, totaling at most five. Thus six socks always contain two pairs, and the minimum is $\boxed6$.
