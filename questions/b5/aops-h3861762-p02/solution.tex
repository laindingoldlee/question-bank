Let the total be $5k$, since after any redistribution the other five must each have $k$ pencils. No initial count can exceed $k$, because any student may be a recipient. Six distinct nonnegative integers at most $k$ have sum at most
\[
k+(k-1)+\cdots+(k-5)=6k-15.
\]
Thus $5k\le6k-15$, giving $k\ge15$ and total at least $75$.

The counts $10,11,12,13,14,15$ attain $75$. If the donor starts with $a$ pencils, the other five need a total of $5\cdot15-(75-a)=a$ pencils to reach $15$ each; every individual need is nonnegative. Therefore each possible donor can perform the redistribution. The minimum is $\boxed{75}$.
