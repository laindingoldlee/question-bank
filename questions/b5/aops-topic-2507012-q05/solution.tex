Since $121=11^2$ and $11$ is prime, $121$ divides the product $uv$ exactly when the total exponent of $11$ in $u$ and $v$ is at least $2$. No number from $1$ to $100$ is divisible by $121$, so each die contributes at most one factor $11$, and we need both $u$ and $v$ to be multiples of $11$. There are $9$ multiples of $11$ in $1,\dots,100$ ($11,22,\dots,99$), so the probability is
\[\left(\dfrac{9}{100}\right)^2=\boxed{\dfrac{81}{10000}}.\]
