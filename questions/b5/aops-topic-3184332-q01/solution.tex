The invalid step is distributing the square root over that quotient. The familiar quotient rule requires suitable domains (in the real version, nonnegative numerator and positive denominator); it is not universally valid for principal complex roots. Here $\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{-1}}=\sqrt{-1}=i$, while $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{-1}}=\dfrac{1}{i}=-i$. Thus those two expressions differ, and there is no contradiction.
