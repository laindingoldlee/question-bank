(a) The tens digit is $1,\dots,7$ and determines the units digit, so there are $\boxed{7}$: $16,25,34,43,52,61,70$.

(b) Write the digits as $d_1,\dots,d_6$ with $d_1\ge1$. Put $d_1'=d_1-1\ge0$; then $d_1'+d_2+\dots+d_6=6$ in nonnegative integers, and no digit can exceed $9$ automatically. By stars and bars there are $\dbinom{6+5}{5}=\boxed{462}$ such numbers. (The same method gives (a): $\dbinom{6+1}{1}=7$.)
