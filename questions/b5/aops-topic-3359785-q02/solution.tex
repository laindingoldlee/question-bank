There are $80$ adjacent answer pairs. For any specified pair, the probability of BA is $\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{1}{16}$. Linearity of expectation, which does not require these pair indicators to be independent, gives $80\cdot\dfrac{1}{16}=\boxed5$.
