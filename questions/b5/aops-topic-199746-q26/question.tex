Kevin writes the integers $1$, $2$, $3$ and $4$, each exactly once, in a row in some order. Then he writes the sums of adjacent pairs to make a new row, and repeats until one integer is left. For example, starting with $3,2,1,4$ he gets $5,3,5$, then $8,8$, and ends with $16$. Over all possible starting orders, how many different final numbers are possible?
