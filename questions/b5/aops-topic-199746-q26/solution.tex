If the row is $a,b,c,d$, the next rows are $a+b,\ b+c,\ c+d$, then $a+2b+c,\ b+2c+d$, and the final number is
\[
a+3b+3c+d=(a+b+c+d)+2(b+c)=10+2(b+c).
\]
So the final number depends only on the sum of the two middle numbers. The possible sums of two of $1,2,3,4$ are $3,4,5,6,7$ ($5$ arises twice, as $1+4$ and $2+3$), giving the final numbers $16,18,20,22,24$. There are $\boxed{5}$ possible final numbers.
