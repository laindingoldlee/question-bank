The integer with four divisors is either $ p^3 $ or $ pq $ for distinct primes. The divisor sums are $(p+1)(p^2+1)$ and $(p+1)(q+1)$. For a prime cube, $ p\le11 $ gives a sum at most $ 1464 $, while $ p\ge13 $ gives at least $ 2380 $, so none lies in the interval. For $ pq $, if one prime is $ 2 $, the sum is $ 3(q+1)$; the multiples of $ 3 $ in the interval give $ q=669,670,671,672 $, all composite ($ 671=11\cdot61 $). Otherwise both primes are odd and the sum is a multiple of $ 4 $, leaving $ 2012,2016 $. Since $ 2012=4\cdot503 $ with $ 503 $ prime, its only pair of positive even factors is $ 2,1006 $, which would give the nonprime $ p=1 $. But $ 2016=(3+1)(503+1)$, and $ 503 $ is prime (trial division by primes through $\sqrt{503}<23 $ verifies this). Thus exactly $\boxed1 $ number is nice.
