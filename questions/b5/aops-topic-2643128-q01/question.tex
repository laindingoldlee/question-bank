Two numbers are chosen independently and uniformly at random from $\{1,2,\dots,n\}$ (they may be equal).
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that the probability that they are relatively prime is
\[\dfrac{2\left(\varphi(1)+\varphi(2)+\cdots+\varphi(n)\right)-1}{n^2},\]
where $\varphi$ is Euler's totient function.
\item[(b)] Evaluate this probability for $n=10$.
\end{enumerate}
