For every positive integer $k$, rationalization gives $\sqrt{k+1}-\sqrt{k}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{k+1}+\sqrt{k}}$. Its denominator increases with $k$, so the differences strictly decrease. The largest is $\boxed{\sqrt2-1}$.
