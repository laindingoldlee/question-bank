Six axes pass through pairs of opposite vertices, and six through midpoints of opposite sides. These exhaust its reflection axes, giving $\boxed{12}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
for(int k=0;k<12;++k)draw(3*dir(30*k)--3*dir(30*(k+1)));for(int k=0;k<12;++k)draw(-3.3*dir(15*k)--3.3*dir(15*k),dashed+gray);
\end{asy}
