Write the square side as $ s $, so $ s^2=40 $. Symmetry puts the two upper vertices at $(\pm\dfrac{s}{2},s)$ relative to the circle centre. Hence $ R^2=s^2+\dfrac{s^2}{4}=50 $. The semicircle area is $\boxed{25\pi}$.
