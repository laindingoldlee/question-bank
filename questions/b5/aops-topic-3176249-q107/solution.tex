Use $B=(0,0),C=(2,0),A=(1,\sqrt3),D=(1,0)$. Reflect $B$ across $AC$ to $B\prime=(3,\sqrt3)$. Then $BP+PD=B\prime P+PD\ge DB\prime=\sqrt7$. The segment $DB\prime$ meets $AC$ at $P=(\dfrac{5}{3},\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3})$, within the side, so equality is attainable. Adding $BD=1$ gives $\boxed{1+\sqrt7}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(1,sqrt(3)),B=(0,0),C=(2,0),D=(1,0),Bp=(3,sqrt(3)),P=(5/3,sqrt(3)/3);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(B--P--D^^D--Bp);draw(B--Bp,dashed);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,S);label("$P$",P,E);label("$B^{\prime}$",Bp,NE);
\end{asy}
