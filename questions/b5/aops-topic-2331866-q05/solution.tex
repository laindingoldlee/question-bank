Since $2020^3=8\cdot1010^3$ is divisible by $1000$, so is $2020^{2021}$. Hence
\[2020^{2021}-2019\equiv-2019\equiv-2019+3000=981\pmod{1000},\]
and the number is positive, so its last three digits are $9$, $8$, $1$, with sum $\boxed{18}$.
