Look for $a+b\sqrt{29}$ whose cube is $182+41\sqrt{29}$:
\[
\left(a+b\sqrt{29}\right)^3=a^3+87ab^2+\left(3a^2b+29b^3\right)\sqrt{29}.
\]
With $b=1$ we need $3a^2+29=41$, so $a=2$, and then $a^3+87a=8+174=182$. So
\[
\left(2+\sqrt{29}\right)^3=182+41\sqrt{29},\qquad \left(2-\sqrt{29}\right)^3=182-41\sqrt{29},
\]
the second by changing the sign of $\sqrt{29}$ throughout. (The second number is negative, since $41\sqrt{29}\approx220.8$, and its real cube root is $2-\sqrt{29}<0$.) Therefore the expression equals
\[
\left(2+\sqrt{29}\right)^2-\left(2-\sqrt{29}\right)^2=\left(33+4\sqrt{29}\right)-\left(33-4\sqrt{29}\right)=\boxed{8\sqrt{29}} .
\]
(Directly: $(u+v)^2-(u-v)^2=4uv$ with $u=2$, $v=\sqrt{29}$.)
