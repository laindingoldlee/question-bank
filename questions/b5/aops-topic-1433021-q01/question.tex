Find
\[
\left(182+41\sqrt{29}\right)^{\dfrac{2}{3}}-\left(182-41\sqrt{29}\right)^{\dfrac{2}{3}},
\]
where, for a real number $t$, $t^{\dfrac{2}{3}}$ means $\left(\sqrt[3]{t}\right)^2$ with the real cube root.
