Pair each divisor $d$ with $\dfrac{48}{d}$: $\sum\dfrac{1}{d}=\dfrac{1}{48}\sum\dfrac{48}{d}=\dfrac{\sigma(48)}{48}$. Since $48=2^4\cdot3$, $\sigma(48)=31\cdot4=124$, and the sum is $\dfrac{124}{48}=\boxed{\dfrac{31}{12}}$.
