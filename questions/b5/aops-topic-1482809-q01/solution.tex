There are $6$ black, $14$ green and $8$ blue socks. In the worst case we draw all socks of the two most numerous colours first: $14+8=22$ socks, with no black sock. So $22$ socks are not enough.

With $23$ socks we are certain: if some colour were missing, all $23$ socks would come from the other two colours, but any two colours together have at most $14+8=22$ socks. So the answer is $\boxed{23}$.
