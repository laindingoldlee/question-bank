The divisor is $11_7=8$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $45_7=33$; $33\div8=4$ remainder $1$. Quotient digit $4$.
\item Bring down $5$: $15_7=12$; $12\div8=1$ remainder $4$. Quotient digit $1$.
\item Bring down $4$: $44_7=32$; $32\div8=4$ remainder $0$. Quotient digit $4$.
\end{itemize}
So $4554_7\div11_7=\boxed{414_7}$ exactly. Check: $414_7=207$, $4554_7=1656=207\cdot8$.
