A base-$5$ numeral uses the place values $1,5,25,125,\ldots$. So $31_5=3\cdot5+1=16$, and the product is $27\cdot16=432$.

To write $432$ in base $5$, take out the largest multiples of the place values: $432=3\cdot125+57$, $57=2\cdot25+7$, $7=1\cdot5+2$. Hence
\[
432=3\cdot125+2\cdot25+1\cdot5+2=\boxed{3212_5}.
\]
