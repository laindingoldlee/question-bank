The second equation says $|x|+|y|=3$ or $5$. The first gives $y=3-\dfrac{x^2}3\le3$ and is symmetric in $x\mapsto-x$, so count solutions with $x\ge0$ and reflect.

Case $y\ge0$ (so $0\le x\le3$): $x+3-\dfrac{x^2}3=3$ gives $x=0$ or $x=3$, i.e. $(0,3)$ and $(3,0)$; $x+3-\dfrac{x^2}3=5$ gives $x^2-3x+6=0$, no real roots.

Case $y<0$ (so $x>3$): $x-3+\dfrac{x^2}3=3$ gives $x^2+3x-18=0$, $x=3$, not $>3$; $x-3+\dfrac{x^2}3=5$ gives $x^2+3x-24=0$, $x=\dfrac{-3+\sqrt{105}}2\approx3.62>3$, one solution.

With $x\ge0$ we have $(0,3)$, $(3,0)$ and one point with $y<0$. Reflecting the two with $x>0$ gives $\boxed{\textbf{(D) }5}$ solutions in all.
