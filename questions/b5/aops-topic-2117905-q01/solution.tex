Since $x\ne0$, multiplying $x+\dfrac{1}{x}=1$ by $x$ gives
\[x^2-x+1=0 .\]
Multiplying this by $x+1$ gives $(x+1)(x^2-x+1)=x^3+1=0$, so
\[x^3=-1 .\]
Since $2019=3\cdot673$,
\[x^{2019}=\left(x^3\right)^{673}=(-1)^{673}=-1,\qquad\text{so}\qquad \dfrac{1}{x^{2019}}=-1\]
as well. Therefore
\[x^{2019}+\dfrac{1}{x^{2019}}=\boxed{-2}.\]
(The discriminant of $x^2-x+1$ is $-3<0$, so no real $x$ satisfies the condition; $x=\dfrac{1\pm i\sqrt3}{2}$ are the two sixth roots of unity with $x^3=-1$, $x\ne-1$. Without complex numbers one can also use $P_n=x^n+\dfrac{1}{x^n}$, which satisfies $P_n=P_{n-1}-P_{n-2}$ and repeats with period $6$: $P_0,P_1,\dots=2,1,-1,-2,-1,1,2,\dots$; since $2019\equiv3\pmod 6$, $P_{2019}=P_3=-2$.)
