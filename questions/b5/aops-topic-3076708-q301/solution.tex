The inverse $\dfrac{1}{b}$ is the unique number whose product with $b$ is $1$. Multiplying $a$ by it gives the unique solution $q$ of $bq=a$, and both $a\div b$ and $\dfrac{a}{b}$ denote this quotient. Zero has no multiplicative inverse, since $0q=0$ for every real $q$ and cannot equal $1$.
