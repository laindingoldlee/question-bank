Let $x$, $y$, $z$ be positive real numbers with $x\ge y\ge z$ and $x+y+z=20.1$. Which of the following statements is true?

(A) Always $xy<99$.\quad (B) Always $xy>1$.\quad (C) Always $xy\ne75$.\quad (D) Always $xy\ne25$.\quad (E) None of the statements (A)-(D) is true.
