Samantha has a sock cupboard with $8$ pairs of striped socks, $10$ pairs of polka-dotted socks and $4$ pairs of fuzzy black socks. Only the black socks are fuzzy, and each sock is separate (not attached to its mate). With her eyes closed and with gloves on, so that she cannot tell the socks apart, Samantha wants to pull out socks until she is sure to have a matching pair that is either fuzzy or striped. What is the minimum number of socks she must pull out to guarantee this?

\textbf{(A)} $4$\qquad\textbf{(B)} $23$\qquad\textbf{(C)} $13$\qquad\textbf{(D)} $8$\qquad\textbf{(E)} Answer not given
