There are $16$ striped, $20$ polka-dotted and $8$ fuzzy black socks.

\textbf{$22$ socks are not enough.} In the worst case she pulls out all $20$ polka-dotted socks, one striped sock and one fuzzy sock: $22$ socks without two striped or two fuzzy socks.

\textbf{$23$ socks are enough.} Among any $23$ socks at most $20$ are polka-dotted, so at least $3$ are striped or fuzzy. Two of these $3$ are of the same kind (pigeonhole principle), giving a striped pair or a fuzzy pair.

So the answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(B)}\ 23}$.
