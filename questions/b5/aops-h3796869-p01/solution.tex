If a small cube has side $s$, the large cube has side $2s$. The surface-area difference is $8\cdot6s^2-6(2s)^2=24s^2=216$. Thus $s^2=9$ and the positive side length is $\boxed{3\text{ cm}}$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(2,0),C=(3,1),D=(1,1),E=(0,2),F=(2,2),G=(3,3),H=(1,3);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(E--F--G--H--cycle);draw(A--E);draw(B--F);draw(C--G);draw(D--H,dashed);label("$s$",(1,0),(0,-1));
\end{asy}
