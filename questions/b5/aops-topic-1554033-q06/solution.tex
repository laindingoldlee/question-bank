The whole cone has volume $V=\dfrac{1}{3}\pi\cdot27^2\cdot54=13122\pi$. The cuts are at distances $54-18=36$ and $54-36=18$ from the apex $V$, so they cut off cones similar to the whole one with scale factors $\dfrac{36}{54}=\dfrac{2}{3}$ and $\dfrac{18}{54}=\dfrac{1}{3}$; their volumes are $\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^3V$ and $\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^3V$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real H=54,R=27; pair V=(0,H);
void ell(real y,real r,pen p){ path f=(-r,y),bk=(r,y); for(int i=1;i<=40;++i){ real t=pi+pi*i/40; f=f--(r*cos(t),y+0.18*r*sin(t)); } for(int i=1;i<=40;++i){ real t=pi*i/40; bk=bk--(r*cos(t),y+0.18*r*sin(t)); } draw(f,p); draw(bk,p+dashed); }
real r1=R*(H-18)/H, r2=R*(H-36)/H;
path band=(-r1,18)--(-r2,36)--(r2,36)--(r1,18)--cycle; fill(band,paleblue);
draw((-R,0)--V--(R,0),linewidth(0.9));
ell(0,R,linewidth(0.9)); ell(18,r1,blue); ell(36,r2,blue);
draw((0,0)--V,dashed);draw((0,0)--(R,0),dashed);
label("radius $27$",(0,-0.18*R),S);
draw((R+5,0)--(R+5,18),Arrows(TeXHead));label("$18$",(R+5,9),E);
draw((R+5,18)--(R+5,36),Arrows(TeXHead));label("$18$",(R+5,27),E);
draw((R+5,36)--(R+5,54),Arrows(TeXHead));label("$18$",(R+5,45),E);
dot(V);label("$V$",V,N);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The middle piece (shaded) is the difference:
\[
\left(\dfrac{8}{27}-\dfrac{1}{27}\right)V=\dfrac{7}{27}\cdot13122\pi=\boxed{3402\pi}.
\]
