$5\cdot98=490\equiv-1\pmod{491}$, so $5^{-1}\equiv-98$. Then $x\equiv12\cdot(-98)=-1176\equiv-1176+3\cdot491=297\pmod{491}$. The largest negative solution is $297-491=\boxed{-194}$. (Check: $5\cdot(-194)-12=-982=-2\cdot491$.)
