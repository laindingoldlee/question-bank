A Pythagorean triple is a triple $(a,b,c)$ of positive integers with $a^2+b^2=c^2$. Prove that every Pythagorean triple can be written, after swapping $a$ and $b$ if necessary, as
\[a=k\left(m^2-n^2\right),\qquad b=k\cdot2mn,\qquad c=k\left(m^2+n^2\right)\]
for some positive integers $k$, $m$, $n$ with $m>n$.
