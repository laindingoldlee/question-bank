After $x$ hours the base $EF$ has length $10x$ and the height from $D$ is $5x$. Thus the area is $25x^2=100$. Since time is positive, $\boxed{x=2}$ hours.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(220);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair E=(-4,0),F=(6,0),D=(0,5);draw(E--F--D--cycle);draw((0,0)--D,dashed);label("$E(-4x,0)$",E,(-1,-1));label("$F(6x,0)$",F,(1,-1));label("$D(0,5x)$",D,(0,1));
\end{asy}
