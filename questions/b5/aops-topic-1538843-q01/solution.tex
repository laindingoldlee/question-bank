Let there be $s$ seventh-graders and $e$ eighth-graders. Then
\[
\dfrac{0.3s}{0.4e}=\dfrac{21}{32}\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad \dfrac {s}{e}=\dfrac{21}{32}\cdot\dfrac{4}{3}=\dfrac{7}{8} .
\]
So we may take $s=7k$ and $e=8k$. The students who stayed home are $0.7s=4.9k$ seventh-graders and $0.6e=4.8k$ eighth-graders, in the ratio
\[
4.9:4.8=\boxed{49:48}.
\]
