Write $K=21\cdot22\cdot23\cdot24=255024$. The number is $(20!)^2(1+K^2)$. Since $K\equiv-1\pmod5$, the second factor is $2\pmod5$ and adds no factors of $5$. The exponent of $5$ in $(20!)^2$ is $2 \lfloor \dfrac{20}{5} \rfloor = 8$, and its exponent of $2$ exceeds this. Thus there are $\boxed8$ trailing zeros.
