Each compounding period multiplies the balance by $1+\dfrac{1}{n}$, so after one year the balance is $(1+\dfrac{1}{n})^n$. Its limit is $\boxed e$ by definition. The natural logarithm $\ln$ is the inverse of $t\mapsto e^t$, so $e^t=2$ has solution $\boxed{t=\ln2}$ years. This illustrates why the exponential base $e$ and its inverse arise in continuous proportional growth.
