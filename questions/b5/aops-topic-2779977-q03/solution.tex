Counting outcome strings gives $ n^n-(n-1)^n=175 $. For $ n=1,2,3,4 $, the left side is 1,3,19,175. It strictly increases with $ n $: inject every length-$ n $ string over 1 through $ n $ containing 1 into a length-$ n+1 $ string by appending 1; there are also further strings, for example the string consisting of $n$ copies of $(n+1)$ followed by 1. Thus there are no larger solutions, and $\boxed{n=4}$.
