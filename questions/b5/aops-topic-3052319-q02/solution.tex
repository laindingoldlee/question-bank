If $ \dfrac{P_1}{Q_1} $ and $ \dfrac{P_2}{Q_2} $ agree at all those inputs, then $ P_1Q_2-P_2Q_1 $ vanishes there. Its degree is at most $ n+m $, so it is identically zero. The two rational expressions therefore represent the same rational function. Numerator and denominator coefficients need not be unique, since common factors or a common nonzero scalar can change the representation. In particular the bound applies to $\dfrac{x^n+k}{x^m+l}$, though its restricted form can sometimes be determined from fewer samples.
