Let $ P,Q $ be polynomials of degrees at most $ n,m $, with $ Q $ nonzero. Prove that a rational function $ \dfrac{P}{Q} $ is uniquely determined as a function by its values at $ n+m+1 $ distinct inputs where its denominator is nonzero, among candidates of these degree bounds defined at those inputs.
