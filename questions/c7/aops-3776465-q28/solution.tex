For a $d$-digit candidate, $10^d\mid k(k-1)$. Consecutive factors are coprime. If $k$ is even, it must satisfy $k\equiv0\pmod{2^d}$ and $k\equiv1\pmod{5^d}$. These have exactly one residue modulo $10^d$ by the Chinese remainder theorem. For $d=1,2,3$, the residues are $6,76,376$, each with the required digit length. Their sum is $458$, whose digit sum is $\boxed{17}$.
