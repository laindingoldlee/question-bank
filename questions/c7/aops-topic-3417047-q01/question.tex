A function on integers $n\ge2$ has $f(2)\ne0$ and satisfies $(n-1)f(n+1)=(n+2)f(n)$ for every $n\ge2$. Let $a=\dfrac{f(2024)}{f(2)}$. Find its prime factorization, its number of distinct prime factors, and its total number of prime factors counted with multiplicity.
