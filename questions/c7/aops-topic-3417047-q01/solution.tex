Telescoping gives $a=\prod_{n=2}^{2023}\dfrac{n+2}{n-1}=\dfrac{2023\cdot2024\cdot2025}{6}$. The factorizations are $2023=7\cdot17^2$, $2024=2^3\cdot11\cdot23$, and $2025=3^4\cdot5^2$. Hence $\boxed{a=2^2 3^3 5^2 7\cdot11\cdot17^2\cdot23}$. There are $\boxed7$ distinct prime factors and $2+3+2+1+1+2+1=\boxed{12}$ counting multiplicity.
