Only primes 2 and 3 can divide x or y. For either prime, its exponents in x and y sum to 5. The exponent in the gcd is the smaller one, which can be 0,1,2, and every such choice occurs. The primes are independent, so the gcd is $2^a 3^b$ for $a,b\in\{0,1,2\}$, giving $\boxed 9$ values.
