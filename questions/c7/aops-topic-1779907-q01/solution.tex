Choose the digits from left to right. The hundreds digit can be any of $1,\dots,9$: $9$ choices. The tens digit can be any of the $10$ digits except the hundreds digit: $9$ choices. The units digit can be anything except the tens digit: $9$ choices. By the multiplication principle there are $9\cdot9\cdot9=\boxed{729}$ such numbers.
