Cauchy--Schwarz gives $1=(a+b+c)^2\le(3a^2+4b^2+12c^2)\left(\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{12}\right)=\dfrac{2}{3}(3a^2+4b^2+12c^2)$. Thus the expression is at least $\dfrac{3}{2}$. Equality requires $(a,b,c)$ proportional to $(\dfrac{1}{3} , \dfrac{1}{4} , \dfrac{1}{12})$, and the sum condition gives $(\dfrac{1}{2} , \dfrac{3}{8} , \dfrac{1}{8})$. The minimum is $\boxed{\dfrac{3}{2}}$.
