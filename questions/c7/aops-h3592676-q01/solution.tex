A width-$i$ interval in the horizontal grid has $m-i+1$ positions; a height-$j$ interval has $n-j+1$ positions. Thus the total area factors as
\[
\sum_{i=1}^m\sum_{j=1}^n ij(m-i+1)(n-j+1)
=\left(\sum_{i=1}^m i(m-i+1)\right)
 \left(\sum_{j=1}^n j(n-j+1)\right).
\]
Using the formulas for the sums of integers and squares,
\[
\sum_{i=1}^m i(m-i+1)
=(m+1)\dfrac{m(m+1)}{2}-\dfrac{m(m+1)(2m+1)}{6}
=\binom{m+2}{3}.
\]
The analogous second factor proves the result.
