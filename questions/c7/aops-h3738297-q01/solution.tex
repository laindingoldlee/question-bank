Algebraically, each side simplifies to $\dfrac{a!}{c!(b-c)!(a-b)!}$ after cancelling factorials.
Combinatorially, count pairs of subsets $C\subseteq B\subseteq A$ of a fixed $a$-element set, where $|B|=b,|C|=c$. Choosing $B$ first and then $C$ gives the left side. Choosing $C$ first and the $b-c$ additional members of $B$ from outside $C$ gives the right side. Both procedures count every pair exactly once.
