Since $\angle AA_1B=\angle AB_1B=90^\circ$, the points $A_1$ and $B_1$ lie on the circle with diameter $AB$, whose centre is $M$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real a=70,bb=50;
pair A=(0,0),B=(6,0);
pair C=extension(A,A+dir(a),B,B+dir(180-bb));
pair A1=C+dot(A-C,unit(B-C))*unit(B-C), B1=C+dot(B-C,unit(A-C))*unit(A-C);
pair M=(A+B)/2;
draw(circle(M,3),gray+dashed);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(A--A1,linewidth(0.7));draw(B--B1,linewidth(0.7));
draw(A1--M--B1,blue+linewidth(0.8));draw(A1--B1,blue+dashed);
real r=0.3;
pair u=unit(C-A1),v=unit(A-A1);draw(A1+r*u--A1+r*(u+v)--A1+r*v,linewidth(0.6));
u=unit(C-B1);v=unit(B-B1);draw(B1+r*u--B1+r*(u+v)--B1+r*v,linewidth(0.6));
draw(arc(C,0.7,degrees(B-C),degrees(A-C)),linewidth(0.6));label("$60^\circ$",C+1.15*dir((degrees(B-C)+degrees(A-C))/2),fontsize(9pt));
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,N);label("$M$",M,S);
label("$A_1$",A1,NE);label("$B_1$",B1,NW);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

In right triangle $AA_1C$, $\angle A_1AC=90^\circ-\angle C=30^\circ$, so $\angle A_1AB_1=30^\circ$ (the triangle is acute, so $B_1$ lies on side $AC$ and $A_1$ on side $BC$). This is an inscribed angle of the circle subtending arc $A_1B_1$, and the central angle on the same arc is twice as large:
\[
\angle A_1MB_1=2\cdot30^\circ=\boxed{60^\circ}.
\]
(So triangle $A_1MB_1$ is equilateral, since $MA_1=MB_1=\dfrac{AB}{2}$.)
