Write the terms as $a,ar,\ldots,ar^5$, where $a,r\ne0$. Every symmetrically placed pair has product $K=a^2r^5$. Consequently the reciprocal of each term is the opposite term divided by $K$, so the sum of reciprocals is $\dfrac{20}{K}=25$. Hence $K=\dfrac{4}{5}$. Pairing the six terms gives total product $K^3=\boxed{\dfrac{64}{125}}$. This argument allows negative terms or a negative ratio; the given sums force $K>0$.
