We have $\overrightarrow{AB}=(3,4)$ with $AB=5$, and $\overrightarrow{AC}=(-8,6)$ with $AC=10$. The bisector of angle $A$ points along the sum of the unit vectors:
\[
\dfrac{(3,4)}{5}+\dfrac{(-8,6)}{10}=\left(-\dfrac{1}{5},\dfrac{7}{5}\right)\parallel(-1,7).
\]
The line through $A(4,1)$ with direction $(-1,7)$ is $7(x-4)+(y-1)=0$, that is,
\[
7x+y-29=0 .
\]
(Check with the angle bisector theorem: the line meets $BC$ at $D=\left(\dfrac{10}{3},\dfrac{17}{3}\right)$, which divides $BC$ in the ratio $BD:DC=1:2=AB:AC$.)

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(4,1),B=(7,5),C=(-4,7),D=(10/3,17/3);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(A--D,blue+linewidth(0.9));
draw((4.43,-2)--(2.86,9),blue+dashed);
draw(arc(A,1.0,degrees(B-A),degrees(D-A)),red);
draw(arc(A,1.15,degrees(D-A),degrees(C-A)),red);
dot(A);dot(B);dot(C);dot(D,blue);
label("$A(4,1)$",A,SE);label("$B(7,5)$",B,E);label("$C(-4,7)$",C,W);
label("$D$",D,NE,blue);
label("$5$",(A+B)/2,SE);label("$10$",(A+C)/2,SW);
label("$7x+y=29$",(3.2,8.4),E,blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Every equation of this line with integer coefficients is $k(7x+y-29)=0$ for a nonzero integer $k$, so $a+b=8k$. The least positive value is $\boxed{8}$.
