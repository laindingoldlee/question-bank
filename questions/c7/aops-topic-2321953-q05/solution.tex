Completing the squares, $(x-5)^2-25+(y-5)^2-25=10$, i.e.
\[(x-5)^2+(y-5)^2=60.\]
So the circle has centre $(5,5)$ and radius $r=\sqrt{60}$. A circumscribed square has side equal to the diameter $2r$, so its area is
\[(2r)^2=4r^2=4\cdot60=\boxed{240}.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((-3,0)--(14,0),Arrow(6)); draw((0,-3)--(0,14),Arrow(6));
label("$x$",(14,0),E); label("$y$",(0,14),N);
real r=sqrt(60);
draw(box((5-r,5-r),(5+r,5+r)),linewidth(0.9));
draw(circle((5,5),r),blue+linewidth(0.9));
dot((5,5)); label("$(5,5)$",(5,5),SW);
draw((5,5)--(5+r,5),red); label("$\sqrt{60}$",(5+r/2,5),N,red);
label("$2\sqrt{60}$",(5,5+r),N);
\end{asy}
