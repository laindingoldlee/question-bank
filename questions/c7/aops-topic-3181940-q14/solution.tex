If $5\mid n$, then $\phi(5n)=5\phi(n)$, so $\phi(n)=8$. If $5\nmid n$, then $\phi(5n)=4\phi(n)$, so $\phi(n)=4$. The complete solutions of these totient equations are respectively $15,16,20,24,30$ and $5,8,10,12$: to verify completeness, every prime divisor must have $p-1$ dividing 8 or 4, so only 2,3,5 occur, and their exponents are bounded by the totient product. Keeping the required divisibility by 5 leaves $15,20,30$ from the first list and $8,12$ from the second. Their sum is $\boxed{85}$.
