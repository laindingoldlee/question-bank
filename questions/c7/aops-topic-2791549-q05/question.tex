Two girls play a game. The first writes $A$ or $B$ at the right end of a row, one letter per turn. After each of her moves, the second girl may swap any two letters of the row (not necessarily adjacent) or do nothing. After each has made $2011$ moves, can the second girl make sure the row is a palindrome?
