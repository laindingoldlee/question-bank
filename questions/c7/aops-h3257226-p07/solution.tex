Triangle inequalities give $PA+PC\ge AC=\sqrt5$ and $PB+PD\ge BD=\sqrt5$. Equality in both occurs when $P$ lies on both segments $AC$ and $BD$. Solving their parametric equations gives their intersection $P=\left(\dfrac{1}{5},\dfrac{3}{5}\right)$, interior to each. Hence the minimum is $\boxed{2\sqrt5}$.

\end{asy}
