In the upper semicircle of a radius 5 circle, draw: an equilateral triangle with horizontal base on the diameter and upper vertex on the arc; a square with its bottom side on the diameter and both top vertices on the arc; and a circle tangent to the diameter and internally tangent to the semicircle at its highest point. All shapes are symmetric about the vertical centre line. Find the sum of their perimeters. Write it as $a\sqrt b+c\sqrt d+\dfrac{e}{f}\pi$, with $b<d$ squarefree and $e,f$ coprime positive integers, and find $a+b+c+d+e+f$.
