The triangle height is 5, so its side is $\dfrac{10}{\sqrt3}$ and its perimeter is $10\sqrt3$. If the square side is $s$, its upper corner satisfies $s^2+(\dfrac{s}{2})^2=25$, giving $s=2\sqrt5$ and perimeter $8\sqrt5$. The smaller circle has radius $\dfrac{5}{2}$, so its circumference is $5\pi$. The sum is $10\sqrt3+8\sqrt5+5\pi$, and $\boxed{a+b+c+d+e+f=32}$.

\end{asy}
