Write $ x=m+r $, with $ m\ge0 $ integral and $ 0\le r<1 $. Then $ 2015r=2m $. Positivity and $ r<1 $ give $ m=1,\ldots,1007 $. Therefore $ s=\sum m+\dfrac{2}{2015}\sum m $, whose fractional part equals that of $\dfrac{1007\cdot1008}{2015}=503+\dfrac{1511}{2015}$. Thus $\boxed{\{s\}=\dfrac{1511}{2015}}$.
