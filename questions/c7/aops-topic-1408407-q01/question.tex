When ice melts and becomes water, its volume decreases by $8\%$. A cylindrical block of ice completely fills a cylindrical container of height $10$ cm and radius $4$ cm. When all of the ice melts, what will be the height of the water in the container? Express your answer as a decimal to the nearest tenth.
