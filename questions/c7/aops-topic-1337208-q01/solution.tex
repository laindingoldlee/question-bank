The volume of a prism is (area of base)$\times$(height). An equilateral triangle with side $6$ has height $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\cdot6=3\sqrt3$, so its area is
\[
\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot6\cdot3\sqrt3=9\sqrt3 .
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(6,0),C=(3,3*sqrt(3)); pair d=(2.2,1.5);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(B--B+d--C+d--C,linewidth(0.9));
draw(A+d--B+d,dashed); draw(A+d--C+d,dashed); draw(A--A+d,dashed);
label("$6$",(A+B)/2,S); label("$6$",(A+C)/2,W); label("$6$",(B+C)/2,NE);
label("$3$",B+d/2,SE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

With height $3$ ft the volume is $9\sqrt3\cdot3=\boxed{27\sqrt3}$ cubic feet ($\approx46.8$ ft$^3$).
