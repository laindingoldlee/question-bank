$ABCD$ is a parallelogram that is not a rhombus. The bisectors of its four angles bound a quadrilateral: the bisectors at $A$ and $B$ meet at $P$, at $B$ and $C$ at $Q$, at $C$ and $D$ at $R$, and at $D$ and $A$ at $T$. Prove that $PQRT$ is a rectangle.
