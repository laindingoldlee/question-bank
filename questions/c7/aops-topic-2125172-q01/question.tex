An open-top box is made from a rectangular sheet of cardboard $6$ inches long and $3$ inches wide. A square of side $x$ inches is marked at each corner. Each corner square is cut along one of its sides and folded, so that it becomes a flap glued to a neighbouring side, and the four sides of the box are folded up.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the value of $x$ that maximises the volume of the box, and the maximum volume.
\item[(b)] A student argued: ``The volume is $V(x)=x(6-2x)(3-2x)$, which is zero at $x=0$ and at $x=1.5$, so the maximum is halfway between, at $x=0.75$.'' Explain why this is wrong.
\end{enumerate}
