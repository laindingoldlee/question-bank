Let $u(n)$ be the units digit of a positive integer $n$, and let $t(n)$ be its tens digit if $n\ge10$ and $0$ otherwise. What is $\displaystyle u\left(2019^{2019^{t\left(2019^{u(t(2019^{2019}) \times 2019^{t(u(2019^{2018})})}\right)}}+u\left(2019^{u(u(2019)^{u(2019^{2019 u(2019)})}) \times u(u(2019^{2018})^{2019})}\right)\right)$?
