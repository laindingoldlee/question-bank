Infinity is not a real number that can be substituted into ordinary field arithmetic. Although both $t$ and $\pi t$ tend to infinity, this does not make the two functions equal, nor permit cancellation from a formal expression involving infinity. For every finite $t>0$, $\dfrac{\pi t}{t}=\pi$ and $\dfrac{t}{t}=1$, so the limits are respectively $\boxed{\pi}$ and $\boxed1$.

There is a second error: $\dfrac{22}{7}$ is an approximation to $\pi$, not an equality. The alleged conclusion results from invalid premises and operations, not a contradiction in arithmetic.
