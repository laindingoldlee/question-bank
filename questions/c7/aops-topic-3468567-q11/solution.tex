Because $(n+2)^2-n^2=4n+4$, each term is $\dfrac{1}{n^2}-\dfrac{1}{(n+2)^2}$. The partial sum through $N$ is $1+\dfrac{1}{4}-\dfrac{1}{(N+1)^2}-\dfrac{1}{(N+2)^2}$. The last two terms tend to zero, leaving $\boxed{\dfrac{5}{4}}$.
