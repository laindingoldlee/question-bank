A positive integer $N$ has $\lfloor\log_{10}N\rfloor+1$ digits. Here $\log_{10}2^{67}=67\cdot0.30103\approx20.169$, so $2^{67}$ has $20+1=\boxed{21}$ digits.

Without logarithms: $2^{10}=1024$, so $2^{67}=2^7\cdot(1024)^6=128\cdot 1.024^6\cdot10^{18}$. Since $1<1.024^6<1.2$, this lies between $1.28\cdot10^{20}$ and $1.6\cdot10^{20}$, again $21$ digits.
