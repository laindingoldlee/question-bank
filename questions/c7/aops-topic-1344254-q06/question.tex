A semicircle and a circle are placed inside a square with sides of length $4$ cm. The diameter of the semicircle is a side of the square. The circle is tangent to the two sides of the square that meet at a vertex not on that diameter, and it is tangent to the semicircle. In centimetres, what is the radius of the circle? Express your answer as a decimal to the nearest hundredth.
