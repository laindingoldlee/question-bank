Put the square at $(0,0)$, $(4,0)$, $(4,4)$, $(0,4)$ with the semicircle on the bottom side: centre $M=(2,0)$, radius $2$. The circle sits in the top-left corner, tangent to the top and left sides, so its centre is $Q=(r,4-r)$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=4; real r=8-4*sqrt(3);
pair P0=(0,0), P1=(4,0), P2=(4,4), P3=(0,4), M=(2,0), Q=(r,4-r);
draw(P0--P1--P2--P3--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(arc(M,2,0,180),blue+linewidth(0.9));
draw(circle(Q,r),red+linewidth(0.9));
pair T=M+2*unit(Q-M);
draw(M--Q,gray+dashed); draw(Q--(0,4-r),red+dashed); draw(Q--(r,4),red+dashed);
dot(M);dot(Q);dot(T);
label("$M$",M,S); label("$Q$",Q,dir(-20)); label("$2$",(M+T)/2,dir(-20),gray); label("$4$",(2,4),N); label("$4$",(4,2),E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The circles touch externally, so $MQ=2+r$:
\[
(r-2)^2+(4-r)^2=(2+r)^2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad r^2-16r+16=0 .
\]
The root less than $2$ is $r=8-4\sqrt3\approx\boxed{1.07}$ cm. (The other root, $8+4\sqrt3$, is far too big for the square.)
