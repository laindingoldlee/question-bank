Let $x$, $y$, and $z$ be real numbers that satisfy the following system of equations:

\begin{align*}
\sin x\cos y&=\dfrac{1}{2}\\
\sin y\cos z&=\dfrac{1}{3}\\
\sin z\cos x&=\dfrac{1}{4}
\end{align*}
The product of all possible values of $\sin x\sin y\sin z$ can be written as $\dfrac{m}{n}$ for relatively prime positive integers $m$ and $n$. Find $m+n$.
