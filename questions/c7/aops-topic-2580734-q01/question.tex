A piece stands on the lower-left cell of an $n\times n$ board ($n\ge2$). A move takes the piece to the adjacent cell above, to the right, or diagonally up-right. Two players move alternately, and the player who puts the piece on the upper-right cell wins. Which player has a winning strategy?
