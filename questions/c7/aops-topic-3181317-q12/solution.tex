The median $BM$ halves triangle area, so $[ABC]=20$. Parameterize $B=0,C=1,D=t$ along their line. The ratio condition is $|t|:|t-1|=3:2$, whose solutions are $t=\dfrac{3}{5}$ and $t=3$. Thus $DC:BC$ equals $\dfrac{2}{5}$ or 2. Since triangles $ADC,ABC$ share the altitude from $A$ to this line, their areas have the same ratio. The possible areas are $\boxed{8\text{ and }40}$.

\end{asy}
