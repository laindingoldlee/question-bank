Subtract exponents of the same base:
\[\dfrac{3^{\dfrac{1}{2}}\cdot2^{\dfrac{2}{3}}}{3^{\dfrac{1}{6}}\cdot2^{\dfrac{3}{2}}}=3^{\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{6}}\cdot2^{\dfrac{2}{3}-\dfrac{3}{2}}=3^{\dfrac{1}{3}}\cdot2^{-\dfrac{5}{6}}=\dfrac{3^{\dfrac{1}{3}}}{2^{\dfrac{5}{6}}}.\]
Multiply the numerator and denominator by $2^{\dfrac{1}{6}}$, which makes the denominator $2^{\dfrac{5}{6}+\dfrac{1}{6}}=2$:
\[\dfrac{3^{\dfrac{1}{3}}\cdot2^{\dfrac{1}{6}}}{2}=\dfrac{9^{\dfrac{1}{6}}\cdot2^{\dfrac{1}{6}}}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt[6]{18}}{2}}.\]
