Zeros do not affect any sum, so let there be $a$ copies of $-1$, $b$ of $1$ and $c$ of $2$. Then $-a+b+2c=19$ and $a+b+4c=99$. Subtracting, $a+c=40$, so $b=59-3c\ge0$ gives $c\le19$. Now
\[\sum x_i^3=-a+b+8c=19+6c,\]
so the maximum is $19+6\cdot19=\boxed{133}$ (at $a=21$, $b=2$, $c=19$) and the minimum is $\boxed{19}$ (at $c=0$, $a=40$, $b=59$).
