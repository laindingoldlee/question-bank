Scale the circumradius to one and put $A=(1,0)$, with successive vertices at angles $\dfrac{2\pi j}{7}$. Let $s=\sin\dfrac{\pi}{7}$. Then $D=(-\cos\dfrac{\pi}{7},s)$ and $E=(-\cos\dfrac{\pi}{7},-s)$, so the perpendicular line is $y=-s$. Also $BC=2s$. The distance equation gives $P=(1\pm\sqrt3s,-s)$. The plus choice lies to the right of the whole polygon. The minus choice lies inside: the horizontal chord of the heptagon at this height runs from $E$ to the point on $AG$ of abscissa $1-s\tan\dfrac{\pi}{7}$, and $-\cos\dfrac{\pi}{7}<1-\sqrt3s<1-s\tan\dfrac{\pi}{7}$. These inequalities follow from $\cot\dfrac{\pi}{14}>\sqrt3$ and $\tan\dfrac{\pi}{7}<\sqrt3$. Thus the outside point uses the plus sign. From $P$, the vector to $E$ points left, while the vector to $A$ is $(-\sqrt3s,s)$, making an angle $\boxed{30^\circ}$ above that direction.

\end{asy}
