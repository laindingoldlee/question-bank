Let Alice's rank sum be $ T=10S $. In each category her rank plus another contestant's rank is at least 3, so the other contestant's total is at least $ 30-T $. For $ T\le14 $, this exceeds $ T $, guaranteeing a strict win. Thus $ S=\dfrac{14}{10}$ is attainable and sufficient. For $ 15\le T\le19 $, assign Alice ranks 1 or 2 with $ T-10 $ twos, and another contestant the complementary 2 or 1 in every category. That contestant's sum is $ 30-T\le T $. For $ T\ge20 $, choose any ten admissible Alice ranks summing to $ T $, and give another contestant rank 1 wherever Alice is not 1, and rank 2 otherwise. Its total is at most 20, hence at most $ T $. Remaining ranks can always be assigned to the other contestants. Therefore no larger score alone guarantees a strict win, and $\boxed{S=\dfrac{7}{5}}$.
