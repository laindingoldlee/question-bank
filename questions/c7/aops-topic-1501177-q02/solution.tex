Since $2^x>0$ for every real $x$, $f(x)=0$ only when $x=0$. Moreover $f(x)<0$ for $x<0$ and $f(x)>0$ for $x>0$, so the graph really crosses the axis at $x=0$ (it does not just touch it). The graph crosses the $x$-axis exactly $\boxed{1}$ time.
