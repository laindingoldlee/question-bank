Place $B=(0,0),C=(14,0)$. The side lengths give $A=(5,12)$ and $D=(5,0)$. The circumcentre has x-coordinate 7; equating distances to $A,B$ gives $O=(7,\dfrac{33}{8})$. Along line $AO$, descending 12 units to the x-axis changes x by $\dfrac{2\cdot12}{12-\dfrac{33}{8}}=\dfrac{64}{21}$. Thus $DE=\dfrac{64}{21}$ and $\boxed{m+n=85}$.

\end{asy}
