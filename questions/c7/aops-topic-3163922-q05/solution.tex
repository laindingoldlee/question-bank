Since $21^2+20^2=29^2$, triangle PEA is right at E. Since $15^2+20^2=25^2$, triangle EAR is right at A. Thus PE and AR are both perpendicular to EA and therefore parallel. Convexity places P and R on the same side of EA, making PEAR a trapezoid of parallel sides 21 and 15 and height 20. Its area is $\dfrac{1}{2}(21+15)20=\boxed{360}$.
\begin{asy}
import geometry; size(220);
pair E=(0,0),A=(20,0),P=(0,21),R=(20,15);draw(P--E--A--R--cycle);draw(P--A,dashed);draw(E--R,dashed);label("$P$",P,N);label("$E$",E,SW);label("$A$",A,SE);label("$R$",R,NE);
\end{asy}
