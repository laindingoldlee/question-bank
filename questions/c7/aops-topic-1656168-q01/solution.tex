Since $44^2=1936<2018<2025=45^2$, the condition $x^2<2018$ holds exactly for $|x|\le44$, i.e.\ $x=-44,-43,\dots,44$. That is $2\cdot44+1=\boxed{\textbf{(g)}\ 89}$ integers.
