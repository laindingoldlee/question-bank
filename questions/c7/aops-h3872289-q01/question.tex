In $\triangle ABC$, $\angle A = 60^\circ$. Let $D$ be a point on $BC$ such that $AD$ bisects $\angle A$. If $AB = 6$ and the area of $\triangle ABC$ is $10\sqrt{3}$, let the length of the angle bisector $AD$ be in the form $\dfrac{m\sqrt{n}}{p}$ where $m$ and $p$ are relatively prime and $n$ is fully simplified. What is $m+p$?
