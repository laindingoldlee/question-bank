Writing $AC=b$, the area formula gives
\[
10\sqrt3=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot6b\sin60^\circ=\dfrac{3b\sqrt3}{2},
\]
so $b=\dfrac{20}{3}$. For an internal angle bisector with adjacent sides $b,c$ and included angle $A$,
\[
AD=\dfrac{2bc\cos(\dfrac{A}{2})}{b+c}.
\]
Using $c=6$ and $A=60^\circ$ gives $AD=60\dfrac{\sqrt 3}{19}$. This is in lowest radical form, so $\boxed{m+p=79}$.
