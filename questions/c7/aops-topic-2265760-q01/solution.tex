\textbf{(a)} $\dfrac{18}{4.5}=\dfrac{180}{45}=4$, so the giraffe is $\boxed{4}$ times as tall as Casey.

\textbf{(b)} The giraffe is $18-4.5=\boxed{13.5}$ feet taller, and $\dfrac{13.5}{4.5}=3$, so the difference is $3$ of Casey's heights.

This explains the two answers suggested for ``how many times taller'': the giraffe is $4$ times \emph{as tall as} Casey, while the amount by which it is taller is $3$ times Casey's height. The usual meaning of ``how many times taller'' in such worksheet questions is ``how many times as tall'', with answer $4$.
