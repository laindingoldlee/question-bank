Clearing denominators, $7ab=26(a+b)$. Multiply by $7$ and add $26^2$ to both sides:
\[49ab-182a-182b+676=676\quad\Longrightarrow\quad(7a-26)(7b-26)=676=2^2\cdot13^2 .\]
Since $\dfrac{1}{a}<\dfrac{7}{26}$ forces $a\ge4$, both factors are positive: $7a-26\ge2$. Both factors must be $\equiv-26\equiv2\pmod7$. The positive divisors of $676$ are $1,2,4,13,26,52,169,338,676$, with residues $1,2,4,6,5,3,1,2,4$ modulo $7$. The ones that are $\equiv2$ are $2$ and $338$, and $2\cdot338=676$. With $a\le b$ this gives $7a-26=2$ and $7b-26=338$, so $a=4$ and $b=52$.

Check: $\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{52}=\dfrac{13+1}{52}=\dfrac{14}{52}=\dfrac{7}{26}$. The only representation is
\[\boxed{\dfrac{7}{26}=\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{52}}.\]
