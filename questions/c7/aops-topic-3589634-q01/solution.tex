Pythagoras in $ABC$ gives $AC=5$. The side lengths of $ACD$ are $5,12,13$, so the converse of Pythagoras shows that $\angle CAD=90^\circ$. Convexity puts $B,D$ on opposite sides of $AC$, so the areas add: $[ABCD]=\dfrac{1}{2}(3)(4)+\dfrac{1}{2}(5)(12)=6+30=\boxed{36}$.

\end{asy}
