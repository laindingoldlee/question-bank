Write $a=dx$, $b=dy$, where $d=\gcd(a,b)$ and $\gcd(x,y)=1$. Then $d(d-11)xy=406+7d$, so $d>11$ and $d\mid406=2\cdot7\cdot29$. The candidate divisors are $14,29,58,203,406$. For each, $xy=\dfrac{\dfrac{406}{d}+7}{d-11}$; only $d=14$ makes this a positive integer, namely $12$. The coprime unordered factor pairs of $12$ are $(1,12)$ and $(3,4)$. Their sums give $a+b=182$ and $98$. The minimum is $\boxed{98}$.
