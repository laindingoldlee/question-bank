Suppose $ax^2+bx+c=0$ has a rational root $\dfrac{u}{v}$ in lowest terms, where $v\ne0$ and $a,b,c$ are odd integers. Clearing denominators gives $au^2+buv+cv^2=0$. Modulo $2$, this becomes
\[
u^2+uv+v^2\equiv0.
\]
Since $u,v$ are not both even, their residue pair is $(1,0),(0,1)$, or $(1,1)$. In each case the left side is $1$ modulo $2$, a contradiction.
