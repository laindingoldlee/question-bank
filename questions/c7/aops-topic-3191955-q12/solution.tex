From $c=\dfrac{1000-b}{4}$, $b$ must be a multiple of $4$. The inequality becomes $250+\dfrac{19b}{4}<1000$, or $b<\dfrac{3000}{19}$. The largest multiple of $4$ below this bound is $156$; it gives $c=211$ and $c+5b=991<1000$. Thus $b=\boxed{156}$.
