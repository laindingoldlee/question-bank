For side length $ s $, coordinates $ A=(0,0),B=(s,0),C=(s,s),D=(0,s)$ give $ P=(\dfrac{20s}{37},0)$ and $ Q=(\dfrac{20s}{37},s)$. Thus $ PQ=s=1 $, so the area is $\boxed1 $. 
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(4,0),C=(4,4),D=(0,4),P=(80/37,0),Q=(80/37,4);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(P--Q,blue);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$P$",P,S);label("$Q$",Q,N);label("$1$",(P+Q)/2,E);
\end{asy}
