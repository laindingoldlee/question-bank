Let the integers be $a<b<c<d<e$. Each integer appears in $4$ of the $10$ sums, so
\[
4(a+b+c+d+e)=16+20+22+23+25+28+29+30+34+37=264,
\]
and $a+b+c+d+e=66$. The smallest sum is $a+b=16$ and the largest is $d+e=37$, so $c=66-16-37=13$. The second smallest sum is $a+c=20$, so $a=7$ and $b=9$; the second largest is $c+e=34$, so $e=21$ and $d=16$.

Check: the pairwise sums of $7,9,13,16,21$ are $16,20,23,28,22,25,30,29,34,37$, which are the given sums. The product is $7\cdot9\cdot13\cdot16\cdot21=\boxed{275184}$.
