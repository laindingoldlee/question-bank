Let $P$ be the product of the nonreal roots of $x^4-4x^3+6x^2-4x=2005$. Find $\lfloor P\rfloor$.

A student reasons: ``By Vieta the product of all roots is $-2005$; the equation is $(x-1)^4=2006$, so the product of the real roots is $\sqrt[4]{2006}\cdot\left(-\sqrt[4]{2006}\right)=-\sqrt{2006}$; hence $P=\dfrac{2005}{\sqrt{2006}}$ and $\lfloor P\rfloor=44$.'' The correct answer is $45$. Find the error.
