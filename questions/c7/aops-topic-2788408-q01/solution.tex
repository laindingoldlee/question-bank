Take $k$ coins from bag $k$ ($k=1,\dots,10$), $55$ coins in all, and weigh them. With no fakes they would weigh $550$ g; the excess, $W-550$ grams, equals the number of coins taken from the fake bag, which is its label. $\blacksquare$
