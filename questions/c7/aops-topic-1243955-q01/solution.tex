If $B$ answers are blank, $6N+2B=102$, i.e. $B=51-3N$, with $B\ge0$ and $N+B\le25$. So $N\le17$ and $51-2N\le25$, i.e. $N\ge13$. Each of $N=13,\dots,17$ works, so there are $\boxed{5}$ values.
