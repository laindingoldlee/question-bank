Zero cannot appear. The distinct appearing digits must sum to six, because digit $d$ uses $d$ positions. The possibilities are $\{6\},\{5,1\},\{4,2\},\{3,2,1\}$. The corresponding multiset-permutation counts are $1$, $\dfrac{6!}{5!}=6$, $\dfrac{6!}{4!2!}=15$, and $\dfrac{6!}{3!2!}=60$. Their sum is $\boxed{82}$.
