The volume is $ V=\dfrac{1}{3}\pi r^2h $. Replacing $ r $ by $ 3r $ multiplies $ r^2 $ by 9, so the volume is multiplied by $\boxed9 $.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw((-1,0)--(0,3)--(1,0));draw(ellipse((0,0),1,0.25));draw((3,0)--(6,3)--(9,0));draw(ellipse((6,0),3,0.5));label("$r$",(0.5,0),S);label("$3r$",(7.5,0),S);draw((0,0)--(1,0)^^(6,0)--(9,0));label("same height",(4,3.5));
\end{asy}
