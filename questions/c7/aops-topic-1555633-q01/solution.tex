Let the upstream speed be $x$ miles per hour, so the downstream speed is $x+3$. The times are $\dfrac{16}{x}$ and $\dfrac{16}{x+3}$ hours, and the difference is $16$ minutes $=\dfrac{16}{60}$ hour:
\[
\dfrac{16}{x}-\dfrac{16}{x+3}=\dfrac{16}{60}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\dfrac{3}{x(x+3)}=\dfrac{1}{60}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x^2+3x-180=0 .
\]
So $(x-12)(x+15)=0$ and, as $x>0$, the boat travelled upstream at $\boxed{12\text{ miles per hour}}$. Check: $\dfrac{16}{12}$ h $=80$ min and $\dfrac{16}{15}$ h $=64$ min differ by $16$ min.
