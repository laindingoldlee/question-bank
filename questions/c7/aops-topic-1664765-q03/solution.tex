Each card gets one of $-2,-1,0,1,2$. A product greater than $1$ is nonzero, so no card shows $0$: there are $4^6=4096$ such choices.

\textbf{Positive products.} Changing the sign of the first card is a bijection between choices with positive product and choices with negative product, so $\dfrac{4096}{2}=2048$ choices have a positive product.

\textbf{Product exactly $1$.} The absolute value of the product is $2^j$, where $j$ is the number of cards showing $\pm2$; it equals $1$ only when every card shows $\pm1$ and the number of $-1$'s is even: $\dfrac{2^6}{2}=32$ choices. Every other positive product is at least $2$.

Hence the number of ways is $2048-32=\boxed{\textbf{(B)}\ 2016}$.
