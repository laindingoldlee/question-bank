Set $B=(0,0)$, $A=(0,1)$, $C=(2,0)$. If the square has side $s$ and $D=(d,0)$, then $F=(d+s,s)$ and $G=(d,s)$. The line $AC$ gives $d+3s=2$. The circle condition gives $d^2+(s-1)^2=1$. Hence
\[
(2-3s)^2+s^2-2s=0,\qquad (5s-2)(s-1)=0.
\]
The solution $s=1$ has $d=-1$ and is invalid. Thus $s=\dfrac{2}{5}$, $d=\dfrac{4}{5}$, and the area is $\dfrac{4}{25}$. The answer is $\boxed{29}$.

\end{asy}
