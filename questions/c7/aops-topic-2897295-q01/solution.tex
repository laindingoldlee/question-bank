Induct on the number $2k+1$ of planets. Take the closest pair $A,B$: they observe each other.

If some other astronomer observes $A$ or $B$, then the remaining $2k+1$ planets receive at most $(2k+1)-1=2k$ observations, so one of them is unobserved.

Otherwise, the astronomers on the other $2k-1$ planets all observe planets among themselves, and each one's nearest planet is the same as within that smaller system. For $k=1$ this system is a single planet, observed by no one. For $k\ge2$ it is an odd system of $2k-1$ planets, and the induction hypothesis gives an unobserved planet. $\blacksquare$
