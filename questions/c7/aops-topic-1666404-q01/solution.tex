First find the value of $x=4.\overline{9}=4.999\ldots$. Multiplying by $10$ shifts the digits: $10x=49.999\ldots$, so
\[
10x-x=49.999\ldots-4.999\ldots=45,\qquad x=5 .
\]
(Another way: $0.\overline{9}=\dfrac{9}{9}=1$, or: the distance between $4.\overline{9}$ and $5$ is smaller than $10^{-n}$ for every $n$, so it is $0$.) Hence $4.\overline{9}$ is the integer $5$, and
\[
\left\lfloor4.\overline{9}\right\rfloor=\lfloor5\rfloor=\boxed{5}.
\]
(The tempting answer $4$ comes from treating $4.\overline{9}$ as a number slightly less than $5$, which it is not.)
