\textbf{(a)} Multiplying out, the sum telescopes:
\[(a-b)\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}a^{n-1-k}b^k=\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}a^{n-k}b^k-\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}a^{n-1-k}b^{k+1}=\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}a^{n-k}b^k-\sum_{k=1}^{n}a^{n-k}b^{k}=a^n-b^n.\]

\textbf{(b)} Replace $b$ by $-b$ in (a). Since $n$ is odd, $(-b)^n=-b^n$, so
\[a^n+b^n=a^n-(-b)^n=(a+b)\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}a^{n-1-k}(-b)^k,\]
which is the stated formula (the signs alternate, starting with $+a^{n-1}$ and ending with $+b^{n-1}$ because $n-1$ is even).

\textbf{(c)} With $n=5$, $a=x$, $b=2$:
\[x^5+32=\boxed{(x+2)\left(x^4-2x^3+4x^2-8x+16\right)}.\]
With $n=2021$ (odd), $a=2$, $b=3$, (b) shows $2^{2021}+3^{2021}=(2+3)\cdot N$, where $N$ is an integer (a sum of products of powers of $2$ and $3$). So $5$ divides $2^{2021}+3^{2021}$. $\blacksquare$
