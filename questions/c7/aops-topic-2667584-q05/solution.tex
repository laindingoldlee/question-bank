In a right triangle with hypotenuse $1$ and an acute angle of $15^\circ$, the legs are $\sin15^\circ$ and $\cos15^\circ=\sin75^\circ$. Hence
\[\left(\sin15^\circ+\cos15^\circ\right)^2=\sin^215^\circ+\cos^215^\circ+2\sin15^\circ\cos15^\circ=1+\sin30^\circ=\dfrac32,\]
and
\[\left(\sin15^\circ+\sin75^\circ\right)^6=\left(\dfrac32\right)^3=\boxed{\dfrac{27}{8}}.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(Cos(15),0),C=(Cos(15),Sin(15));
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.8));draw(B+(-0.04,0)--B+(-0.04,0.04)--B+(0,0.04));
label("$1$",(A+C)/2,NW,fontsize(8pt));label("$\cos15^\circ=\sin75^\circ$",(A+B)/2,S,fontsize(8pt));label("$\sin15^\circ$",(B+C)/2,E,fontsize(8pt));
label("$15^\circ$",A,dir(7)*5,fontsize(7pt));label("$75^\circ$",C,dir(-120)*2.5,fontsize(7pt));
\end{asy}
