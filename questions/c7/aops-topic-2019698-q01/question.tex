The student council at Round Junior High School has eight members who meet at a circular table with eight seats. If the four officers must sit together (in any order), how many distinguishable circular seating orders are possible? Two seating orders are distinguishable if one is not a rotation of the other. A student argues that the answer is $3!\cdot4!$ because one person should be fixed in place to remove rotations; explain what is wrong with this argument.
