A circle of radius $1$ with centre $O_1$ rests on top of a circle of radius $2$ with centre $O_2$; the two circles are externally tangent at the point $X$. A point $P$, different from $O_1$, is chosen inside the smaller circle. The smaller circle is now rolled without slipping around the outside of the larger circle, carrying $O_1$ and $P$ with it, until the line $PO_1$ is, for the first time, parallel to the original line $PO_1$ (or coincides with it). At that moment the circles are tangent at the point $Y$. Find $XY^2$.

$\textbf{(A)}\ 4\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ 12\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ 13\qquad\textbf{(D)}\ 15\qquad\textbf{(E)}\ 16$
