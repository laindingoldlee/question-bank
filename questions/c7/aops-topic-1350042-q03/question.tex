How many ways are there to put $20$ distinguishable balls into $15$ indistinguishable baskets? (Baskets may stay empty.) Express the answer using Stirling numbers of the second kind $S(n,k)$, the number of ways to split $n$ distinguishable objects into $k$ non-empty unlabelled groups.
