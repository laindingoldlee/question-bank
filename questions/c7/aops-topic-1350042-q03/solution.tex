Since the baskets look alike, a distribution is determined by how the balls are grouped: it is a partition of the set of $20$ balls into some number $k$ of non-empty groups, with $1\le k\le15$ (the remaining $15-k$ baskets are empty, and empty baskets are indistinguishable). There are $S(20,k)$ partitions into exactly $k$ groups, so the answer is
\[
\boxed{\sum_{k=1}^{15}S(20,k)}=51724135127267 .
\]
(Equivalently, the Bell number $B_{20}$ minus the partitions into $16$ to $20$ groups.)
