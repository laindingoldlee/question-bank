A divisor $2^a7^b$ (with $0\le a\le4$, $0\le b\le9$) is a perfect square when $a$ and $b$ are even, and it is even when $a\ge1$. So $a\in\{2,4\}$ ($2$ choices) and $b\in\{0,2,4,6,8\}$ ($5$ choices), giving $2\cdot5=\boxed{10}$.
