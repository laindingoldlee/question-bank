The mean gives $a+b+c+d=4\cdot34=136$. The median of four ordered numbers is the average of the middle two, so $b+c=2\cdot33=66$. Hence $a+d=136-66=70$. The range gives $d-a=32$. Subtracting, $2a=70-32=38$, so
\[
a=\boxed{19}
\]
(and $d=51$). Such ages exist, for example $19,30,36,51$.
