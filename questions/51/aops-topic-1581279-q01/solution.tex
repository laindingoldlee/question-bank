Going from $4.9$ to $5.9$ multiplies the strength by $10$, and going from $5.9$ to $6.9$ multiplies it by $10$ again. So the $6.9$ earthquake is
\[
10\cdot10=\boxed{100}
\]
times as strong as the $4.9$ earthquake. (In general, a difference of $d$ on the scale is a factor of $10^d$ in strength.)
