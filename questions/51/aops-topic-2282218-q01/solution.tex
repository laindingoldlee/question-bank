Rationalise: $\dfrac{100}{\sqrt n+\sqrt{n-1}}=\dfrac{100(\sqrt n-\sqrt{n-1})}{n-(n-1)}=100(\sqrt n-\sqrt{n-1})$. The first $N$ bonuses add up to a telescoping sum:
\[\sum_{n=1}^{N}100(\sqrt n-\sqrt{n-1})=100\sqrt N .\]
We need $1200+100\sqrt N\ge5695$, that is, $\sqrt N\ge44.95$, or $N\ge44.95^2=2020.5025$. So $N=\boxed{2021}$ bonuses are needed. (After $2020$ bonuses the rating is $1200+100\sqrt{2020}\approx5694.4$; after $2021$ it is about $5695.6$.)
