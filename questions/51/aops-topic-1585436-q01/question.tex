A bamboo tree has been growing for many years, by the same amount every night. Every morning a gardener cuts it so that it becomes shorter than $1$ metre; he always cuts off a piece whose length is a whole number of metres, and he writes the length of the piece in his journal.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Is it possible that over $10$ days the sequence $7,7,7,6,7,7,6,7,7,7$ is written?
\item[(b)] Is it possible that over $10$ days the sequence $7,7,7,6,7,6,7,7,6,7$ is written?
\end{enumerate}
Explain your answers.
