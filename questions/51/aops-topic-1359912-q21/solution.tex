Raising each contribution by $\$0.10$ changes the total from $\$0.95$ short to $\$1.15$ over, an increase of $\$2.10$. So there are $\dfrac{2.10}{0.10}=\boxed{21}$ students. (The decorations cost $21\cdot1.50+0.95=\$32.45$.)
