Count the two-digit numbers with no $1$ and no $9$: the tens digit is one of $2,\dots,8$ ($7$ choices) and the units digit one of $0,2,3,\dots,8$ ($8$ choices), giving $56$. So $90-56=34$ two-digit numbers contain a $1$ or a $9$.

The primes among them are
\[11,13,17,19,29,31,41,59,61,71,79,89,97,\]
which is $13$ primes. The probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{13}{34}}$.
