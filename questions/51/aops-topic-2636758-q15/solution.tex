$G$ lies outside the circle, and its power with respect to the circle is
\[GA\cdot GB=3\cdot(3+12)=45.\]
So $CG\,(CG+5)=45$ and $EG\,(EG+4)=45$. The positive solutions are
\[CG=\dfrac{-5+\sqrt{205}}{2},\qquad EG=-2+\sqrt{49}=5.\]
Hence $AG+CG+EG=3+5+\dfrac{\sqrt{205}-5}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{11+\sqrt{205}}{2}}$. (The chords exist: $G$ is at distance $9$ from the centre, and chords of length $5$ and $4$ through $G$ are at distances $\sqrt{36-6.25}$ and $\sqrt{32}$ from the centre, both less than $9$.)

draw(G--B,linewidth(0.6));draw(G--D,linewidth(0.6));draw(G--F2,linewidth(0.6));dot(G);dot(O);
label("$G$",G,W);label("$A$",A,NW,fontsize(8pt));label("$B$",B,E);label("$O$",O,S,fontsize(8pt));

\end{asy}
