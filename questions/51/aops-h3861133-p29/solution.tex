Write the original difficulty scores as $X\in[0,1],Y\in[0,2],Z\in[0,3]$, independently uniform. The only derangements are the orders $Y<Z<X$ and $Z<X<Y$. Their probabilities, using joint density $\dfrac{1}{6}$, are $\dfrac{1}{6}\int_0^1\dfrac{x^2}{2}\,dx=\dfrac{1}{36}$ and $\dfrac{1}{6}\int_0^1x(2-x)\,dx=\dfrac{1}{9}$. Thus the answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{5}{36}}$.
