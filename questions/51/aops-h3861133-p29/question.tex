Tiger is taking Day $2$ of the $2025$ IMO TST. The test consists of three problems. This time, Tiger decides not to look at the problem numbers. When doing the $n$th problem, he gives the problem a difficulty rating, chosen uniformly at random from $[0,n]$. He then orders the problems in increasing order of difficulty. Find the probability that none of the problems in Tiger's ordering are in the same place they were in for the original ordering.
