Three horses $A,B,C$ run a race. ``Odds of $r$-to-$s$ against'' a horse mean that a $\$1$ bet on it loses $\$1$ if it loses and wins $\$\dfrac rs$ if it wins. The odds against $A$ are $2$-to-$1$ and against $B$ are $3$-to-$1$. What should the odds against $C$ be so that the betting is fair, i.e. there is a combination of bets that neither gains nor loses money whichever horse wins?
