For an integer $N\ge3$, arrange ruby, emerald, and aquamarine marbles in a circle. Initially each color count is between $1$ and $2N$. Allowed moves are:
\begin{itemize}
\item replace two adjacent emeralds by one ruby and one aquamarine in either order;
\item replace adjacent ruby and aquamarine by two emeralds;
\item remove one of two adjacent aquamarines.
\end{itemize}
If a sequence of moves ends with exactly $N$ marbles, what is the greatest possible initial total $M$?
