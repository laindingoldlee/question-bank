Write the color counts as $R,E,A$. The quantity $W=2R+E$ is invariant. Also define the deficiency $D=E+2A$, so the total size $L$ satisfies $2L=W+D$. The first two moves preserve $D$; removal decreases it by $2$, but requires $A\ge2$, and afterward still leaves $A\ge1$, so $D\ge2$. Initially $D\ge3$. Thus at every reachable stage $D\ge2$, and at the final stage $W=2N-D\le2N-2$.

Initially $R\ge1$, so $R+E=W-R\le2N-3$, and $A\le2N$. Hence $M\le4N-3$.

This bound is attainable with initial counts
\[
R=1,\quad E=2N-4,\quad A=2N.
\]
Replace the emeralds in adjacent pairs to obtain $N-1$ rubies and $3N-2$ aquamarines. Adjacent ruby and aquamarine marbles can exchange places in two moves: replace them by two emeralds, then replace those in the reverse order. Thus collect the aquamarines into one block, and repeatedly delete from adjacent pairs until one remains. The final circle has $N-1$ rubies and one aquamarine, or $N$ marbles. All initial counts satisfy their bounds because $N\ge3$. Therefore $\boxed{M=4N-3}$.
