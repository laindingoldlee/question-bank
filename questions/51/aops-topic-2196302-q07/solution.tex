A prime divisible by $2$ has $2$ as a factor, so it must be $2$ itself; similarly a prime divisible by $3$ is $3$. No prime is divisible by $4$ (it would also be divisible by $2$, so it would be $2$, which is not divisible by $4$). The primes are $2$ and $3$, so the answer is $\boxed{2}$.
