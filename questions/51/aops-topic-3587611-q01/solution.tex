Multiply the first fraction by $a^2$ in numerator and denominator; because $a^2b^2=4$, it becomes $\dfrac{a^3}{a^3+4}$. Similarly the other becomes $\dfrac{b^3}{b^3+4}$. Put $u=a^3+b^3$, noting $a^3b^3=8$. Combining the fractions gives $\dfrac{16+4u}{24+4u}=\dfrac{7}{8}$, whence $u=10$. Therefore $a^9+b^9=(a^3+b^3)^3-3a^3b^3(a^3+b^3)=1000-240=\boxed{760}$.
