The solutions are $e^{\dfrac{2\pi ik}{N}}$ for $k=0,1,\dots,N-1$, all on the unit circle, so the polygon is inscribed in a circle of radius $1$. Two adjacent vertices subtend a central angle $\dfrac{2\pi}{N}$, so the side (a chord) has length
\[\boxed{2\sin\dfrac{\pi}{N}}.\]
For example $N=6$ gives $1$, and $N=4$ gives $\sqrt2$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw(unitcircle,gray);draw((-1.3,0)--(1.3,0),gray,Arrow(4));draw((0,-1.3)--(0,1.3),gray,Arrow(4));
pair[] V;for(int k=0;k<6;++k)V[k]=dir(60*k);
for(int k=0;k<6;++k){draw(V[k]--V[(k+1)%6],linewidth(0.8));dot(V[k]);}
draw((0,0)--V[0],dashed);draw((0,0)--V[1],dashed);label("$1$",(V[0]+V[1])/2,NE,fontsize(8pt));label("$1$",(0.5,0),S,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}
