Because $ 11 $ and $ 13 $ are prime, the divisor-count formula forces $ A=p^{10}$ and $ B=q^{12}$ for primes $ p,q $. The least choices are $ A=3^{10}=59049 $ and $ B=2^{12}=4096 $. Their difference is $ 54953=179\cdot307 $. Neither factor has a prime divisor at most its square root (test $ 2,3,5,7,11,13 $ for $ 179 $, and also $ 17 $ for $ 307 $), so the smallest prime factor is $ 179 $. The digit product is $ 1\cdot7\cdot9=\boxed{63}$.
