Let $f(x)=\ln(6-x)$ and $g(x)=\left|x^2-10x+15\right|$. The domain of $f(g(x))$ can be written in interval notation as $(a,b)\cup(c,d)$. What is $b+d$?

$\textbf{(a)}\ 6\qquad\textbf{(b)}\ 11\qquad\textbf{(c)}\ 8\qquad\textbf{(d)}\ 12\qquad\textbf{(e)}\ 10$
