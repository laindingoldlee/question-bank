$f(g(x))=\ln\bigl(6-g(x)\bigr)$ is defined exactly when $g(x)<6$, that is
\[
-6<x^2-10x+15<6 .
\]
The right inequality is $x^2-10x+9<0$, i.e.\ $(x-1)(x-9)<0$, so $1<x<9$. The left inequality is $x^2-10x+21>0$, i.e.\ $(x-3)(x-7)>0$, so $x<3$ or $x>7$. Together: the domain is $(1,3)\cup(7,9)$, and $b+d=3+9=\boxed{\textbf{(d)}\ 12}$.
