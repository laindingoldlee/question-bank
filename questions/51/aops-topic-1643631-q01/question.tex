Let $a\ne0$, $b$, $c$ be real numbers. Prove that the two roots $r_1,r_2$ of $ax^2+bx+c=0$ given by the quadratic formula (complex if $b^2-4ac<0$, equal if $b^2-4ac=0$) always satisfy
\[
r_1+r_2=-\dfrac{b}{a}\qquad\text{and}\qquad r_1r_2=\dfrac{c}{a},
\]
and that $ax^2+bx+c=a(x-r_1)(x-r_2)$ for all $x$, even when the quadratic does not factor over the integers.
