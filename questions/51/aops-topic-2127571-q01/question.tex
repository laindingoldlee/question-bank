Let $m$ and $n$ be relatively prime positive integers, and suppose that $\dfrac{mx}{n}$ is an integer.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] If $x$ may be any real number, must $x$ be an integer multiple of $n$? Justify your answer.
\item[(b)] Prove that if $x$ is an integer, then $x$ is a multiple of $n$.
\item[(c)] Show by an example that the conclusion of (b) can fail if $m$ and $n$ are not relatively prime.
\end{enumerate}
