The numerator is $\sum_{k=1}^{1005}kx^{2k}$, whereas the denominator is $2\sum_{k=1}^{1005}kx^{2k-1}$. The numerator equals $\dfrac{x}{2}$ times the denominator, so the quotient is $\boxed{\dfrac{x}{2}}$. For real nonzero $x$, the denominator is $2x\sum_{k=1}^{1005}k(x^2)^{k-1}\ne0$, since the sum is positive.
