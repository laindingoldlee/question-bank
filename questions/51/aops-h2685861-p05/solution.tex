The total remains $2016$. Subtracting the mean $672$ from each coordinate, the update multiplies every deviation by $-2$. Thus
\[
x_i=672+(-2)^i(x_0-672),
\]
and similarly for $y_i,z_i$.

The distinct starting integers cannot all be at least $672$, since their sum is $2016$. Some coordinate is therefore at most $671$. At step $10$ its value is at most $672-1024<0$, unless a negative value has already occurred. Thus every triple reaches a negative entry by step $10$.

The triple $(671,672,673)$ attains this delay: its deviations are $-1,0,1$, so through step $9$ the least coordinate is $672-512=160>0$. At step $10$ it becomes negative. Therefore $\boxed{N=10}$.
