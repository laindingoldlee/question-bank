The polynomial is nonzero. Comparing leading coefficients gives $2^{\deg p}=8$, so $\deg p=3$. Setting $x=-10$ gives $p(-4)=0$, and $x=4$ gives $p(8)=0$. Then $x=-2$ and $p(-4)=0$ imply $p(4)=0$. Thus
\[
p(t)=c(t+4)(t-4)(t-8).
\]
Since $p(1)=105c=210$, $c=2$. Therefore $\boxed{p(10)=2\cdot14\cdot6\cdot2=336}$. Substituting the factored polynomial also verifies the original identity.
