Consider the series
\[
\left(\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{4}+\cdots\right)+\left(\dfrac{1}{2^2}+\dfrac{1}{3^2}+\dfrac{1}{4^2}+\cdots\right)+\left(\dfrac{1}{2^3}+\dfrac{1}{3^3}+\dfrac{1}{4^3}+\cdots\right)+\cdots .
\]
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that this series diverges.
\item[(b)] Evaluate the series obtained by deleting the first bracket, $\displaystyle\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}\sum_{k=2}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{k^n}$.
\end{enumerate}
