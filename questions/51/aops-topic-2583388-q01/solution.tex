Let $m$ and $l$ be the current ages of Micolas and Licolas. Then
\[m-13=6(l-13),\qquad m+103=2(l+103),\]
so $m=6l-65$ and $m=2l+103$. Hence $4l=168$, so $l=42$ and $m=187$.

Check: $187-13=174=6\cdot29$ and $187+103=290=2\cdot145$.

The sum is $187+42=\boxed{229}$. (Nicolas's age is not needed.)
