A prime number $n$ less than $40$ is chosen uniformly at random. The probability that $n^2-100$ can be written in the form $\dfrac{7mq}{2}$ for some integers $m$ and $q$ is $\dfrac {a}{b}$, where $a$ and $b$ are relatively prime positive integers. Compute $a^2b+ab^2+ab+a+2b$.
