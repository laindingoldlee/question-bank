Set $t = \dfrac{x}{y} > 0$. Subtracting 3 from the left side of the second equation and putting the terms over a common denominator gives $-\dfrac{3t(4t^4+2t^3+15t^2+2t+4)}{(t+2)(2t+1)(t^2+2)(2t^2+1)}<0$. Thus the second equation cannot hold for positive $x,y$, independently of the first. There are \boxed{\text{no solutions}}.
