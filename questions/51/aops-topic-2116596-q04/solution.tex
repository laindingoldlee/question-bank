Expanding,
\[(S-1)(E-1)(O-1)=SEO-(SE+SO+EO)+(S+E+O)-1=SEO-SE-SO-EO+68 .\]
So
\[SEO-SE-SO-EO=(S-1)(E-1)(O-1)-68 .\]
Since $S,E,O\ge1$, the product $(S-1)(E-1)(O-1)$ is at least $0$, and it equals $0$ when one of the numbers is $1$, for example $(S,E,O)=(1,2,66)$. The least value is therefore $\boxed{-68}$.
