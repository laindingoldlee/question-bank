$28\cdot24\cdot60\cdot60=2{,}419{,}200$, so there are $\boxed{2419200}$ seconds in $28$ days.

Six weeks is $42$ days, i.e. $42\cdot24\cdot60\cdot60$ seconds. In prime factors this is $(2\cdot3\cdot7)(2^3\cdot3)(2^2\cdot3\cdot5)^2=2^8\cdot3^4\cdot5^2\cdot7$, and $10!=2^8\cdot3^4\cdot5^2\cdot7$ as well (counting factors of $2,3,5,7$ in $1,\dots,10$). Both equal $3628800$.
