In isosceles triangle $ABD$, the vertex angle $BAD$ is 30 degrees, so $ABD=75^\circ$. Therefore $C=180^\circ-60^\circ-75^\circ=\boxed{45^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=dir(0),D=dir(30),C=(sqrt(3)+1)/2*dir(60);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--D);label("A",A,SW);label("B",B,SE);label("C",C,N);label("D",D,E);
\end{asy}
