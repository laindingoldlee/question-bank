Choose which $4$ days are rainy in $\dbinom74=35$ ways; each such pattern has probability $\left(\dfrac13\right)^4\left(\dfrac23\right)^3=\dfrac{8}{2187}$. The answer is $35\cdot\dfrac{8}{2187}=\boxed{\textbf{(D) }\dfrac{280}{2187}}$.
