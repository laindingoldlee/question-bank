A point $P=(x,y)$ lies on the hyperbola $(x-p)(y-q)=r$, where $r\ne0$, and the sum of its coordinates is $x+y=s$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find all possible values of $x-y$ in terms of $p$, $q$, $r$ and $s$.
\item[(b)] Show that when $p=q$ the positive difference $|x-y|$ between the coordinates of $P$ is determined by $p$, $r$ and $s$, and find it. Show by an example that when $p\ne q$ it is not determined by $p,q,r,s$.
\end{enumerate}
