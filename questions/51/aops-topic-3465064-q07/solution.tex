Let $E_j$ be the expected remaining rounds when Darcy has $j$ cookies; $E_2=0$. First-step equations give $E_1=1+\dfrac{1}{3}E_0$ and $E_0=1+\dfrac{1}{3}E_0+\dfrac{2}{3}E_1$. The latter gives $E_0=\dfrac{3}{2}+E_1$, so $E_1=\dfrac{3}{2}+\dfrac{1}{3}E_1$, yielding $\boxed{E_1=\dfrac{9}{4}}$. Finiteness follows because from either transient state there is probability at least $\dfrac{4}{9}$ of absorption in the next two rounds; the tail is geometrically bounded.
