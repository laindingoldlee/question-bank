Let the original counts be $A,B$. Then $B+4=4(A-4)$ and $A+2=B-2$. The second gives $B=A+4$; substituting gives $A+8=4A-16$, hence $A=8,B=12$. Their counts are $\boxed{8,12}$ and total $\boxed{20}$.
