When the month, day and two-digit year are written in the form m/d/yy (without extra zeros), some dates form palindromes, which read the same forwards and backwards when the slashes are ignored; for example, 8/14/18 gives $81418$. What is the total number of palindrome dates that occur in $2018$ (including the example)?
