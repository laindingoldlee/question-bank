A score is $5c+2b$ with $b,c\ge0$ integers and $b+c\le15$. For even scores, write $c=2j$ with $0\le j\le7$: attainable even values run in steps of two from $10j$ to $30+6j$. The intervals for $j=0,\ldots,6$ together cover every even score from $0$ through $66$; $j=7$ adds $70,72$, leaving only $68,74$ absent among even numbers up to $75$. For odd scores write $c=2j+1$, $0\le j\le7$: the intervals from $10j+5$ to $33+6j$ cover all odds from $5$ to $69$ for $j\le6$, and $j=7$ adds $75$. Thus the absent scores in $0,\ldots,75$ are $1,3,68,71,73,74$, six in all. The answer is $76-6=\boxed{70}$.
