Multiplying numerator and denominator of $f(\dfrac{N}{x})$ by $\dfrac{x^2}{N}$ shows $f(\dfrac{N}{x})=\dfrac{\dfrac{Nx}{2}+x^2}{x^2+Nx+N}$. Hence $f(x)+f(\dfrac{N}{x})=1$. Pair the terms for exponents $k$ and $20-k$, leaving the middle term $f(2^{10})=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Ten pairs plus that term give $\boxed{\dfrac{21}{2}}$.
