The conditions fix the elements $1$ to $6$: $1$ lies in $X$ and $Y$ but not $Z$; $2,3$ lie in $Y$ and $Z$ but not $X$; $4,5,6$ lie in $X$ and $Z$ but not $Y$. (These are consistent with $X\cap Y\cap Z=\varnothing$.)

Each of the other $24$ elements lies in no two of the sets, so it is in none of them, or in exactly one ($X$, $Y$ or $Z$): $4$ choices each. The number of triples is $4^{24}=\boxed{2^{48}}$.
