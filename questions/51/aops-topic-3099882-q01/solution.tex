The sum is even and greater than 4. If it had at most two prime factors with multiplicity, it would be twice a prime, say $p+q=2r$. But then $p<r<q$, contradicting that $p,q$ are consecutive primes. The cases of an even prime or $2^2$ are already excluded by $p+q\ge8$. Therefore it has at least three prime factors.
