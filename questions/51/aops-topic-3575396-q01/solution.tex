Cancel the square of the first factor against its fifth power. The result is $\boxed{\dfrac{(2x^2-3y^2-3)^2}{(3xy-3x-6)^3(-x^3-6xy+4x)^5}}$. The original expression requires $3xy-3x-6\ne0$ and $-x^3-6xy+4x\ne0$; cancellation does not remove these restrictions.
