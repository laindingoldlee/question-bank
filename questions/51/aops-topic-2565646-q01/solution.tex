Note that $6+2\sqrt5=1+2\sqrt5+5=\left(1+\sqrt5\right)^2$, and $1+\sqrt5>0$. Hence
\[\left(6+2\sqrt5\right)^{\dfrac32}=\left(\left(1+\sqrt5\right)^2\right)^{\dfrac32}=\left(1+\sqrt5\right)^3=1+3\sqrt5+3\cdot5+5\sqrt5=\boxed{16+8\sqrt5}.\]
