Put $B=(-4,-4,0),C=(4,-4,0),D=(4,4,0),E=(-4,4,0)$ and $A=(0,0,4\sqrt2)$. Then $M=(-2,-2,2\sqrt2)$ and $N=(2,-2,2\sqrt2)$. The parallel sides $DE,MN$ have lengths $8,4$, and their perpendicular separation in the common plane is $\sqrt{6^2+(2\sqrt2)^2}=2\sqrt{11}$. Thus the trapezoid's area is $\boxed{\dfrac{8+4}{2}\cdot2\sqrt{11}=12\sqrt{11}}$.
