Let each grade have $k$ sick students. The grade sizes are $5k,\dfrac{10k}{3},2k$, totaling $\dfrac{31k}{3}$. The sick fraction is $\dfrac{3k}{\dfrac{31k}{3}}=\dfrac{9}{31}$, so $\boxed{a+b=40}$.
