The only increasing geometric triples in $1,\ldots,9$ are $124,139,248,249,369$. For each possible GT triple, remove intersecting triples from this list. GT can know that ST has the largest number, 9, only if every surviving triple contains 9. GT must also have at least two surviving possibilities whose complementary CT triples disagree about being arithmetic. Testing the $\binom93=84$ GT triples under these two conditions leaves only $\{2,5,7\}$. Its ST possibilities are $139,369$, leaving CT respectively $468$ (arithmetic) and $148$ (not arithmetic). Thus $\boxed{257}$.
