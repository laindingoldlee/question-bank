ST, GT, and CT are $3$ friends that are each given $3$ unique positive integers from $1$ to $9$ inclusive such that none of them share any numbers. They then have the following perfectly logical conversation.

\begin{itemize}

\item ST: My $3$ numbers form a geometric sequence.

\item GT: ST has the larger number, and I don't know if CT's numbers form an arithmetic sequence.

\end{itemize}

If GT's numbers are $a$, $b$, $c$ with $a<b<c$, find $100a+10b+c$.
