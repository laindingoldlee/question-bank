Sugar can be bought in $3$-pound bags or $5$-pound bags. A $3$-pound bag costs $\$2.10$ and a $5$-pound bag costs $\$3.39$. When spending the least amount of money possible to buy exactly $34$ pounds of sugar, how many $5$-pound bags are bought?
