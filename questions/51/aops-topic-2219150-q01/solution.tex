One solution is
\[\boxed{1+2+3+4+5+6+7+8\times9=100}.\]
\textbf{How to find it.} With only plus signs, $1+2+\cdots+9=45$, which is $55$ short of $100$. Changing the last plus sign to a times sign replaces $8+9=17$ by $8\times9=72$, which adds exactly $72-17=55$. Check: $1+2+3+4+5+6+7=28$ and $28+72=100$.
