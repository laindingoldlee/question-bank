Numbers 1,2,3 are too small. Each integer 4 through 10 is representable: $2+2,2+3,3+3,2+5,3+5,2+7,3+7$. An odd sum of primes must use 2, so 11 is impossible because 9 is composite. Numbers 12 through 16 are $5+7,2+11,3+11,2+13,3+13$. Number 17 is impossible because 15 is composite. The five numbers sum to $1+2+3+11+17=\boxed{34}$.
