Let Mary win $w$ games. She lost exactly the $3$ games that Juan won, so her score is $2w-3=5$, which gives $w=4$. The total number of games is $w+3=\boxed{7}$.

The student's first step is right: the $3$ losses cost Mary $3$ points, so her wins must have earned $8$ points. The error is in the next step: each win is worth $2$ points, so $8$ points come from $\dfrac{8}{2}=4$ wins, not $8$ games; and the $3$ games she lost must be added to the total. Check: $4\cdot2-3\cdot1=5$.
