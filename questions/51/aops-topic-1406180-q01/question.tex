Juan and Mary play a two-person game in which the winner gains $2$ points and the loser loses $1$ point (there are no ties). Both start with $0$ points. If Juan won exactly $3$ games and Mary had a final score of $5$ points, how many games did they play?

A student argues: ``Mary lost the $3$ games Juan won, giving her $-3$ points. Since she finished with $5$, she gained $5-(-3)=8$ more, so they played $8$ games.'' Find the error.
