The function $f(x)=x^x=e^{x\ln x}$ is increasing for $x>1$, because $x\ln x$ is a product of positive increasing factors there. Since $3.14159<\pi<3.1416$,
\[3.14159^{3.14159}<\pi^\pi<3.1416^{3.1416}.\]
Using logarithms (or a calculator), $3.14159\ln3.14159\approx3.59625$ and $3.1416\ln3.1416\approx3.59628$, so both bounds round to $36.46$: $3.14159^{3.14159}\approx36.4620$ and $3.1416^{3.1416}\approx36.4627$. Hence $36<\pi^\pi<36.5$, and $\pi^\pi\approx36.462$ rounds to $\boxed{36}$.
