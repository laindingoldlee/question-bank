The two legs of the walk are perpendicular, so the house, the store and the library form a right triangle with legs $1$ and $2$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s=0.2){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
pair H=(0,0), St=(0,1), L=(2,1);
draw(H--St--L,linewidth(0.9)); draw(H--L,red+dashed);
draw(ra(St,H-St,L-St,0.15));
dot(H);dot(St);dot(L);
label("house",H,S); label("store",St,NW); label("library",L,NE);
label("$1$",(0,0.5),W); label("$2$",(1,1),N); label("$\sqrt{5}$",(1,0.5),SE,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

By the Pythagorean theorem the distance is $\sqrt{1^2+2^2}=\boxed{\sqrt5}$ miles.
