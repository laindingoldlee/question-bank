The digits that are perfect squares or cubes are \(0=0^2\), \(1\), \(4\), \(9\) (squares) and \(8\) (a cube). So the allowed digits are \(\{0,1,4,8,9\}\), with nonzero digits summing to \(22\).

\emph{Two-digit numbers:} \(4\cdot4=16\) of them. Each nonzero digit is the tens digit \(4\) times (sum \(4\cdot22\cdot10=880\)); as units digit, \(0\) appears \(4\) times and each nonzero digit \(3\) times (sum \(3\cdot22=66\)). Total \(946\).

\emph{Three-digit numbers:} \(4\cdot4\cdot3=48\) of them. Each nonzero digit is the hundreds digit \(12\) times (sum \(12\cdot22\cdot100=26400\)). In the tens place, a given nonzero digit appears \(3\cdot3=9\) times (hundreds digit from the other three nonzero digits, units from the remaining three), so the tens sum is \(9\cdot22\cdot10=1980\); likewise the units sum is \(9\cdot22=198\). Total \(28578\).

Altogether there are \(64\) numbers with sum \(946+28578=29524\), so the mean is
\[
\dfrac{29524}{64}=\boxed{\dfrac{7381}{16}}.
\]
