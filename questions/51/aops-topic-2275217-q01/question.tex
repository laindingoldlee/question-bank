Consider real numbers $a,b,c$ with
\[a+b+c=abc .\]
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Describe all real solutions $(a,b,c)$.
\item[(b)] Prove or disprove: the only solutions are $(1,2,3)$ and $(-1,-2,-3)$.
\item[(c)] Find all solutions in integers.
\end{enumerate}
