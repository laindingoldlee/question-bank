The original equation requires $x\ge0$. Squaring gives $x+2=x^2$, or $(x-2)(x+1)=0$. The candidate $-1$ violates $x\ge0$ and indeed gives $1=-1$ in the original equation; $2$ checks. Hence $\boxed{x=2}$. Negative candidates are not automatically extraneous in every equation: they must be judged against the original conditions.
