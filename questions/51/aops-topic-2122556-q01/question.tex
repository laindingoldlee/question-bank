An electronics store has $3$ refrigerators and plenty of washing machines and dishwashers. Each customer buys exactly one appliance and is equally likely to choose each of the three types, independently of the other customers (as long as refrigerators are in stock). What is the probability that all three refrigerators are sold within the first $6$ purchases?
