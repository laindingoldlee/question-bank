Two guesses are off by one, so Norb's age is one more or one less than two of the guesses. Two guesses differ from the same number by one only if they differ by $2$ and the age lies between them. Among the guesses, the pairs that differ by $2$ are $(28,30)$, $(30,32)$, $(36,38)$, giving ages $29$, $31$, $37$.

At least half of the ten students guessed too low, so at least $5$ guesses are below the age. For $29$ only $24,28$ are below; for $31$ only $24,28,30$. For $37$ the guesses $24,28,30,32,36$ are below, which is $5$. Finally $37$ is prime. So Norb is $\boxed{37}$, choice $\textbf{(C)}$.
