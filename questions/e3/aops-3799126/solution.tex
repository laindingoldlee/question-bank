There are $3\cdot4\cdot2\cdot4=96$ equally likely outcomes. The numbers of wrong symbols on the four rings are $2,3,1,3$. Choosing the two wrong rings gives $2\cdot3+2\cdot1+2\cdot3+3\cdot1+3\cdot3+1\cdot3=29$ outcomes. All other rings must equal the winning symbols, so there is no extra factor. The probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{29}{96}}$.
