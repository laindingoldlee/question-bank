Unfold reflections by reflecting the box instead of the ray. The straight ray then has position $(t,t,t)$. It reaches a corner precisely when $t$ is simultaneously a multiple of 7,11,13. The least positive such value is $\operatorname{lcm}(7,11,13)=1001$. Its distance is $\boxed{1001\sqrt3}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(4,0),C=(5.2,1.3),D=(1.2,1.3),Ap=(0,3),Bp=(4,3),Cp=(5.2,4.3),Dp=(1.2,4.3);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(Ap--Bp--Cp--Dp--cycle);draw(A--Ap);draw(B--Bp);draw(C--Cp);draw(D--Dp,dashed);draw(A--(2,2),blue,Arrow);label("$7$",(A+B)/2,S);label("$11$",(B+C)/2,SE);label("$13$",(C+Cp)/2,E);label("$O$",A,SW);
\end{asy}
