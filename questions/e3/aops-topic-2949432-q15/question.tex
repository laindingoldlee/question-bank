$ABCA'B'C'$ is a triangular prism of height $3$ whose bases $ABC$ and $A'B'C'$ are equilateral triangles of side $\sqrt6$. Points $P$ and $Q$ inside the prism make $ABCP$ and $A'B'C'Q$ regular tetrahedra. The volume of the intersection of the two tetrahedra is $\dfrac{\sqrt m}n$, with $m$ squarefree. Find $m+n$.
