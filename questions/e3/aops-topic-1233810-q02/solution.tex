For \emph{every} $n$. Indeed
\[(n+1)(n+2)\cdots(2n)=\dfrac{(2n)!}{n!}=\dfrac{(2n)!}{2^nn!}\cdot2^n,\]
and $\dfrac{(2n)!}{2^nn!}=\dfrac{1\cdot2\cdot3\cdots(2n)}{2\cdot4\cdots(2n)}=1\cdot3\cdot5\cdots(2n-1)$. So $A=1\cdot3\cdots(2n-1)$, an (odd) natural number for all $n\ge1$.
