Count all $5^6$ assignments and subtract those that miss some child, by inclusion-exclusion over the set of children left out:
\[5^6-\binom51 4^6+\binom52 3^6-\binom53 2^6+\binom54 1^6=15625-20480+7290-640+5=\boxed{1800}.\]
(Directly: choose the two gifts that go together, $\binom62$, and assign the five "units" to the children, $5!$: $15\cdot120=1800$.)
