$343=7^3$ and $2187=3^7$, so $x=3$ and $y=7$. Then
\[\left(\dfrac{3}{7}\right)^{-4}=\left(\dfrac{7}{3}\right)^4=\dfrac{2401}{81}\approx 29.64,\]
which is $\boxed{29.6}$ to the nearest tenth.
