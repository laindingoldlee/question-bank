\textbf{Arithmetic progression.} The common difference is $\dfrac{B-A}{3}$, so
\[
X=A+\dfrac{B-A}{3}=\dfrac{2A+B}{3},\qquad Y=A+\dfrac{2(B-A)}{3}=\dfrac{A+2B}{3}.
\]

\textbf{Harmonic progression.} The reciprocals $\dfrac{1}{A},\dfrac{1}{U},\dfrac{1}{V},\dfrac{1}{B}$ are in arithmetic progression, so in the same way
\[
\dfrac{1}{U}=\dfrac{\dfrac{2}{A}+\dfrac{1}{B}}{3}=\dfrac{A+2B}{3AB},\qquad \dfrac{1}{V}=\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{A}+\dfrac{2}{B}}{3}=\dfrac{2A+B}{3AB},
\]
so $U=\dfrac{3AB}{A+2B}$ and $V=\dfrac{3AB}{2A+B}$ (all positive since $A,B>0$).

\textbf{Products.}
\[
XV=\dfrac{2A+B}{3}\cdot\dfrac{3AB}{2A+B}=AB,\qquad YU=\dfrac{A+2B}{3}\cdot\dfrac{3AB}{A+2B}=AB .
\]
Hence $XV=YU=AB$. $\blacksquare$
