The rule says $f(x+1)=2f(x)$: each step of $1$ doubles the value. From $f(2)=4$ we get $f(3)=8$, and in general $f(n)=2^n$ for integers $n\ge2$. So
\[
f(10)-f(7)=1024-128=\boxed{896}.
\]
