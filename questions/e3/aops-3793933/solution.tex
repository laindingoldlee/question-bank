Put $u=\ln2,v=\ln3,w=\ln5$, so $a=\dfrac{u+v+w}{u+v}$ and $b=\dfrac{3u+v}{v+w}$. Substitute and multiply numerator and denominator by $(u+v)(v+w)$. The numerator factors as $(2u+v+w)(3u+3v+2w)$, and the denominator as $(2u+v)(3u+3v+2w)$. All factors are positive, so cancellation gives $\dfrac{2u+v+w}{2u+v}=\boxed{\log_{12}60}$.
