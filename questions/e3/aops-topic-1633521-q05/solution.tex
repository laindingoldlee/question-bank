By the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality for the vectors $(1,9,44)$ and $\left(\sqrt a,\sqrt b,\sqrt c\right)$,
\[
\left(\sqrt a+9\sqrt b+44\sqrt c\right)^2\le\left(1^2+9^2+44^2\right)(a+b+c)=2018(a+b+c),
\]
since $1+81+1936=2018$. The given equation says that equality holds, which happens exactly when the vectors are proportional: $\sqrt b=9\sqrt a$ and $\sqrt c=44\sqrt a$, i.e.\ $b=81a$ and $c=1936a$. Therefore
\[
\dfrac{b+c}{a}=81+1936=\boxed{2017}.
\]
