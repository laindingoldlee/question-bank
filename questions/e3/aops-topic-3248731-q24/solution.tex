Taking logarithms twice gives $10^k=\pi^{\pi^\pi}\log_{10}\pi$, then $k=\pi^\pi\log_{10}\pi+\log_{10}(\log_{10}\pi)$. Evaluating this manageable expression gives $k\approx17.8236453394$, hence $\boxed{17.824}$. No direct computation of the enormous power tower is needed.
