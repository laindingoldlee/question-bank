The digit sum shows divisibility by $3$, and $23871=3\cdot7957=3\cdot73\cdot109$, where $73,109$ are prime. The only two-digit divisor in this factorization is $73$ (the others are $1,3,109,219,327,7957,23871$). Thus the factors are $73$ and $327$, with sum $\boxed{400}$.
