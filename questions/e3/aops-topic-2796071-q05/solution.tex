The sum is $1111A+111B+11C+D=2012$. Since $1111\cdot2>2012$, $A=1$ and $111B+11C+D=901$. Since $11C+D\le108$, we need $111B\ge793$, so $B=8$ and $11C+D=13$, giving $C=1$, $D=2$.

$A+B+C+D=1+8+1+2=\boxed{12}$.
