Collect place values: $A$ appears $4$ times as units, $B$ three times as tens, $C$ twice as hundreds and $D$ once as thousands:
\[
1000D+200C+30B+4A=2016 .
\]
Since $200C+30B+4A\le1800+270+36=2106$ and is positive, $D=1$ or $D=2$. If $D=2$, then $200C+30B+4A=16$ forces $C=0$, which is not allowed. So $D=1$ and
\[
200C+30B+4A=1016.
\]
As $30B+4A\le306$, we need $200C\ge710$, so $C\ge4$; and $C\le5$. If $C=5$, then $30B+4A=16$ forces $B=0$, not allowed. So $C=4$ and $30B+4A=216$. Then $30B\equiv216\pmod 4$, i.e.\ $2B\equiv0\pmod4$, so $B$ is even; with $4A\le36$ we need $30B\ge180$, so $B\in\{6,8\}$. $B=8$ gives $4A=-24$, impossible; $B=6$ gives $A=9$.

Hence $\boxed{A=9,\ B=6,\ C=4,\ D=1}$: $9+69+469+1469=2016$.
