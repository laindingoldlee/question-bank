In the sum
\[
A+\overline{BA}+\overline{CBA}+\overline{DCBA}=2016,
\]
$A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ are digits and $\overline{BA}$, $\overline{CBA}$, $\overline{DCBA}$ are numbers written with these digits (their leading digits $B$, $C$, $D$ are not $0$). Find $A$, $B$, $C$ and $D$.
