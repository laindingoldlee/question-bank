For any fixed chosen light, flipping it is a bijection of the $2^8$ equally likely colourings. Thus the final colouring is still uniform, even after averaging over the chosen light. Exactly two final colourings are monochromatic, so $\boxed{\dfrac{2}{2^8}=\dfrac{1}{128}}$, choice A.
