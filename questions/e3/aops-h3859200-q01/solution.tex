Uniformity gives $\Pr(T\le x)=\dfrac{x}{100}$. Thus the expected payoff is
\[
E(x)=(100-x)\dfrac{x}{100}
=25-\dfrac{(x-50)^2}{100}.
\]
It is maximized uniquely at $\boxed{x=50}$, with maximum expected payoff $\boxed{\$25}$. The acceptance probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{x}{100}}$, and at the optimum it is $\dfrac{1}{2}$.
