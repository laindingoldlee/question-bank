A unit circle is centred at the origin. A second circle of radius $1$ must lie inside the square with vertices $(8,8)$, $(8,-8)$, $(-8,-8)$ and $(-8,8)$, and must be tangent to at least one side of the square. What is the largest possible distance between the two circles (the shortest distance from a point of one circle to a point of the other)?
