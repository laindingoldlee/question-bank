The equation is $(x+2)(y+3)=6$, with $x+2\ne0$. Thus $x+2$ ranges over the eight signed divisors $\pm1,\pm2,\pm3,\pm6$. Their sum is zero, so subtracting $2$ eight times gives $\boxed{-16}$.
