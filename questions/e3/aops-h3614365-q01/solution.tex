Exclude $x=0,3$. Subtracting gives $\dfrac{6-5x}{x(3-x)}>0$. Its critical points are $0,\dfrac{6}{5},3$. Testing the four sign intervals gives negative, positive, negative, positive signs, respectively. Since the inequality is strict, the solution is
\[
\boxed{\left(0,\dfrac{6}{5}\right)\cup(3,\infty)}.
\]
