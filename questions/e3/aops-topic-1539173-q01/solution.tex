In a geometric sequence, terms equally far from a given term multiply to the square of that term: if the ratio is $r$, then $x=\dfrac{3}{r}$, $y=3r$, $w=\dfrac{3}{r^2}$, $z=3r^2$, so
\[
xy=3^2=9\qquad\text{and}\qquad wz=3^2=9 .
\]
Hence $wxyz=9\cdot9=\boxed{81}$. (Quick rule: the product of the four terms around the middle term $3$ is $3^4$.)
