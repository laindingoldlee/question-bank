Factoring gives $(x-7)(y-7)=49$. Positive factor pairs give $(x,y)=(8,56),(14,14),(56,8)$. Negative factor pairs give $(6,-42),(0,0),(-42,6)$; $(0,0)$ must be discarded because the original reciprocals are undefined. The requested sum is $56+14+8-42+6=\boxed{42}$.
