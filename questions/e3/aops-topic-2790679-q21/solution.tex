For positive integers, the unique parent is $\lfloor\dfrac{n}{2}\rfloor $, so shortest paths go upward to the lowest common ancestor and then downward. The ancestor chains are $ 1000,500,250,125,62,31,\ldots $ and $ 2018,1009,504,252,126,63,31,\ldots $. The first common ancestor is 31, at distances 5 and 6. Thus the minimum is $\boxed{11}$ hops. The self-loop at 0 cannot shorten a path.
