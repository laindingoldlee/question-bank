Let $O$ be the circumcenter of $\Delta ABC$, where $AB = 5$, $BC = 7$, and $CA = 8$. Then, let $M$ be the intersection of $CO$ and $AB$, and $N \neq A$ be the intersection of $(AMO)$ with $AC$. In addition, let $H$ be the point on $BC$ such that $HO \perp MN$, and $D$ be the point on $\overline{AH}$ such that $BDOC$ is cyclic. The value of $OD^2$ can be expressed as $\dfrac{m}{n}$ for relatively prime positive integers $m$ and $n$. Compute $m+n$.
