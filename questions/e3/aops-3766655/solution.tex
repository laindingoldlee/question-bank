Take $B = (0,0) , C = (7,0) , A = (\dfrac{5}{7} , \dfrac{20 \sqrt{3}}{7})$ from the three side lengths. Equidistance gives $O = (\dfrac{7}{2} , \dfrac{7 \sqrt{3}}{6})$. Intersecting $CO$ with $AB$ gives $M = (\dfrac{7}{13} , \dfrac{28 \sqrt{3}}{13})$. Substitute the line $AC$ into the circle through $A,M,O$ and remove the root $A$ to obtain $N = (\dfrac{105}{26} , \dfrac{35 \sqrt{3}}{26})$. The equation $(H-O)\cdot(N-M)=0$ with $H$ on the x-axis gives $H = (\dfrac{35}{13} , 0)$. The circle through $B,O,C$ has equation $x^2 + y^2 - 7 x + (\dfrac{7 \sqrt{3}}{3}) y = 0$. Substitution of $A+t(H-A)$ gives $t = \dfrac{13}{20}$ or $\dfrac{13}{6}$; only the first lies on segment $AH$. Thus $D=(2,\sqrt3)$, and $O D^2 = (\dfrac{3}{2})^2 + (\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{6})^2 = \dfrac{7}{3}$. Therefore $\boxed{m+n=10}$.
