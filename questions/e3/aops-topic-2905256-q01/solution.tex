Let the sphere have radius $R$ and the hole radius $a$, with $a^2+3^2=R^2$. At height $z$ ($|z|\le3$) the cross-section is an annulus of area $\pi\bigl((R^2-z^2)-a^2\bigr)=\pi(9-z^2)$, which is independent of $R$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

\end{asy}

So $V=\displaystyle\int_{-3}^3\pi(9-z^2)\,dz=\pi(54-18)=\boxed{36\pi}$. (Remarkably, the answer does not depend on the sphere's size.)
