(b) first. Given such a sequence, put
\[
g_0=k-n_1,\quad g_1=n_1-n_2,\quad\dots,\quad g_{i-1}=n_{i-1}-n_i,\quad g_i=n_i .
\]
These are $i+1$ nonnegative integers with sum $k$. Conversely, any $i+1$ nonnegative integers $g_0,\dots,g_i$ with sum $k$ give back the sequence via $n_j=g_j+g_{j+1}+\cdots+g_i$, which satisfies $k\ge n_1\ge\cdots\ge n_i\ge0$. So the count equals the number of nonnegative solutions of $g_0+\cdots+g_i=k$, which by stars and bars is
\[
\boxed{\dbinom{k+i}{i}} .
\]
(a) Here $k=15$ and $i=4$: $\dbinom{19}{4}=\boxed{3876}$.
