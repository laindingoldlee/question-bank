\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] In how many ways can integers $a,b,c,d$ be chosen with $15\ge a\ge b\ge c\ge d\ge0$?
\item[(b)] Generalise: in how many ways can integers $n_1,\dots,n_i$ be chosen with $k\ge n_1\ge n_2\ge\cdots\ge n_i\ge0$?
\end{enumerate}
