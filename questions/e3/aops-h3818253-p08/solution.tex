For $x=-1,0,\ldots,10$, the right side $(x+1)(x^2+1)$ takes the values
\[
0,1,4,15,40,85,156,259,400,585,820,1111.
\]
Only $0,1,4,400$ are squares: each other entry lies strictly between consecutive squares (respectively $9,16$; $36,49$; $81,100$; $144,169$; $256,289$; $576,625$; $784,841$; $1089,1156$).
Hence $\boxed{(-1,0),(0,\pm1),(1,\pm2),(7,\pm20)}$.
