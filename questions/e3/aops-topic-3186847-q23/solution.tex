Put $A=(0,0)$, $B=(w,0)$, $C=(w,1)$, $D=(0,1)$, $P=(tw,t)$, where $0<t<1$. The circle through A,P,B has equation $x^2+y^2-wx+(w^2-t(w^2+1))y=0$, so $X=(0,\alpha)$ with $\alpha=t(w^2+1)-w^2<1$. Since $|\alpha|=5$, $\alpha=-5$. The circle through C,P,D meets the y-axis at $y=1$ and $y=\beta=t(w^2+1)>0$. Since $|\beta-1|=2$, $\beta=3$. Subtracting $\alpha=\beta-w^2$ gives $w^2=8$. The rectangle area is w, so its square is $\boxed 8$.
\begin{asy}
import geometry; size(220);
real w=sqrt(8);pair A=(0,0),B=(w,0),C=(w,1),D=(0,1),P=(w/3,1/3),X=(0,-5),Y=(0,3);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C,dashed);draw(X--Y,dashed);draw(circle((w/2,-2.5),sqrt(8.25)));draw(circle((w/2,2),sqrt(3)));label("$A$",A,W);label("$B$",B,E);label("$C$",C,E);label("$D$",D,W);label("$P$",P,N);label("$X$",X,W);label("$Y$",Y,W);
\end{asy}
