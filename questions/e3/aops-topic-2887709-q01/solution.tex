There are $26-6=20$ choices for each of the first two letters, $26$ for the third and $10$ for each digit: $20\cdot20\cdot26\cdot10^3=\boxed{10400000}$. The logo is the same on every plate of that state, so it contributes a factor of $1$, not $50$.
