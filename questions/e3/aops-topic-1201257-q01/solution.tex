$(2x)^3=8x^3$ and $\sqrt{128}=8\sqrt2$, so $x^3=\sqrt2$, i.e. $x=\sqrt[3]{\sqrt2}$. Comparing with $x=\sqrt[n]{\sqrt2}$ (both equal $2^{\dfrac{1}{2n}}$, and $2^{\dfrac{1}{6}}=2^{\dfrac{1}{2n}}$ forces $n=3$) gives $n=\boxed{3}$. The poster's answer is correct.
