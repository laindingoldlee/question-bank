A positive integer is divided by $5$, and the quotient and remainder are recorded. The same number is divided by $3$, and again the quotient and remainder are recorded. The second pair consists of the same two numbers as the first pair, in the other order (the quotient on division by $3$ equals the remainder on division by $5$, and the remainder on division by $3$ equals the quotient on division by $5$). Find the product of all possible values of the original number.
