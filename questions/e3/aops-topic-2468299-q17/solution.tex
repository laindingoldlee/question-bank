We need $11k=5+15m$ for nonnegative integers $k,m$. Mod $15$: $11k\equiv5$, and since $11\cdot11=121\equiv1$, $k\equiv55\equiv10\pmod{15}$. The smallest is $k=10$, i.e. $110$ minutes after 7:00 ($m=7$). Kody wakes at $\boxed{\text{8:50 a.m.}}$
