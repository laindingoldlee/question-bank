If both coordinates were at most 157, then $\dfrac{34}{n}+\dfrac{123}{m}\ge\dfrac{157}{157}=1$, contradicting the condition after division by $mn$. Thus every beautiful pair has a coordinate at least 158. The pair $(158,158)$ is beautiful because $158>34+123$. Therefore no larger bound can hold, and $\boxed{K=158}$, choice D.
