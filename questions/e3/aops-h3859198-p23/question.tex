Call a pair of positive integers \((m,n)\) **beautiful** if



\[
mn>34m+123n.
\]

Suppose \(K\) is a positive integer such that every beautiful pair has at least one coordinate not smaller than \(K\). Find the largest possible value of \(K\).



\[
\textbf{(A)}\ 89
\qquad
\textbf{(B)}\ 123
\qquad
\textbf{(C)}\ 140
\qquad
\textbf{(D)}\ 158
\qquad
\textbf{(E)}\ 162
\]
