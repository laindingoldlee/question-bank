Eight dots are arranged in $2$ rows of $4$, forming a grid of unit squares:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.2cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
for(int i=0;i<4;++i) for(int j=0;j<2;++j) dot((i,j),linewidth(4));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

How many isosceles triangles have all three vertices among these dots?
