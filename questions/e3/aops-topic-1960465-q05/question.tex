In right triangle $ABC$, $AB=5$, $BC=12$ and $AC=13$. Point $P$ lies on side $AC$ so that ray $BP$ bisects $\angle ABC$. Points $Q$ and $R$ lie on side $BC$ so that $BQ=QR=RC$. Segments $AQ$ and $AR$ intersect segment $BP$ at $X$ and $Y$, respectively. What is the area of quadrilateral $QRYX$? Express your answer as a common fraction.
