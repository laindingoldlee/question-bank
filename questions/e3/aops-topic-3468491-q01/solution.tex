An endpoint $(x,y)$ must have $|x|+|y|\le100$ and $x+y$ even. Conversely every such point is reached by $|x|+|y|$ direct hops and then pairs of back-and-forth hops. For positive Manhattan distance $d$, there are $4d$ lattice points. Therefore the count is $1+\sum_{k=1}^{50}4(2k)=1+8\cdot1275=\boxed{10201}$.
