The vertices and foci lie on the $x$-axis, so the hyperbola has the form
\[\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}-\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1,\]
with vertices $(\pm a,0)$, foci $(\pm c,0)$ and $c^2=a^2+b^2$. Here $a^2=33$ and $c^2=59$, so $b^2=59-33=26$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
real a=sqrt(33), bb=sqrt(26), c=sqrt(59);
pair hp(real t){return (a*cosh(t),bb*sinh(t));}
pair hm(real t){return (-a*cosh(t),bb*sinh(t));}
draw((-12.5,0)--(12.5,0),gray,Arrow(4)); draw((0,-9.5)--(0,9.5),gray,Arrow(4));
draw((-12,-12*bb/a)--(12,12*bb/a),gray+dashed); draw((-12,12*bb/a)--(12,-12*bb/a),gray+dashed);
draw(graph(hp,-1.32,1.32),blue+linewidth(0.9)); draw(graph(hm,-1.32,1.32),blue+linewidth(0.9));
dot((a,0)); dot((-a,0)); dot((c,0),red); dot((-c,0),red);
label("$V_1$",(-a,0),NE); label("$V_2$",(a,0),NW);
label("$F_1$",(-c,0),S,red); label("$F_2$",(c,0),S,red);
label("$x$",(12.5,0),SE); label("$y$",(0,9.5),NE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The figure shows the two branches with vertices $V_1,V_2=\left(\mp\sqrt{33},0\right)$, foci $F_1,F_2=\left(\mp\sqrt{59},0\right)$ and the asymptotes $y=\pm\sqrt{\dfrac{26}{33}}\,x$ (dashed). The equation is
\[\boxed{\dfrac{x^2}{33}-\dfrac{y^2}{26}=1} .\]
