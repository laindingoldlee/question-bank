The tower is read as $2005^{\left(2007^{2009}\right)}$. Since $2005=7\cdot286+3$, we need $3^{2007^{2009}}\bmod7$. The powers of $3$ modulo $7$ are $3,2,6,4,5,1$, repeating with period $6$ (as Fermat's little theorem predicts, $3^6\equiv1$). So we need the exponent modulo $6$.

$2007\equiv3\pmod6$, and $3^k\equiv3\pmod6$ for every $k\ge1$ (it is odd and divisible by $3$). Hence $2007^{2009}\equiv3\pmod6$ and
\[
2005^{2007^{2009}}\equiv3^3=27\equiv\boxed{6}\pmod7 .
\]
