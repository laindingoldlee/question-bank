At the beginning of those three digits, long division has a remainder $r$ with $0<r<n$. Therefore
\[
251\le\dfrac{1000r}{n}<252,\qquad
0.004n\le4r-n<0.008n.
\]
The integer $4r-n$ is positive. If $n\le125$, the upper bound is at most $1$, impossible. At $n=126$, the only candidate is $4r-n=1$, but $127$ is not divisible by $4$. At $n=127$, choose $r=m=32$; then
\[
251<\dfrac{32000}{127}<252.
\]
Also $\gcd(32,127)=1$, so $\dfrac{32}{127}=0.251968\ldots$ works. The answer is $\boxed{127}$.
