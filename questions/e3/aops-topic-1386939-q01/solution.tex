Repeatedly subtract the largest power of $2$ that fits (this gives the binary expansion, which is unique):
\begin{align*}
45&=32+8+4+1, & 52&=32+16+4,\\
63&=32+16+8+4+2+1, & 71&=64+4+2+1,\\
89&=64+16+8+1, & 115&=64+32+16+2+1,\\
296&=256+32+8, & 3012&=2048+512+256+128+64+4.
\end{align*}
For example, $3012-2048=964$, $964-512=452$, $452-256=196$, $196-128=68$, $68-64=4$.
