The three kinds of candy are distributed independently. By stars and bars, $n$ identical items go to $3$ children with at least one each in $\dbinom{n-1}{2}$ ways:
\[
\dbinom62\cdot\dbinom72\cdot\dbinom52=15\cdot21\cdot10=\boxed{3150}.
\]
The student's $\dbinom{11}{3}=165$ treats all the leftover candies as one kind; but a bear and a worm are different, so each kind must be shared out separately.
