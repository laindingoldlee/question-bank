On a game show there are three doors. Behind one is a car, behind another is a goat, and behind the third is a goat with a radioactive sample and a Geiger counter: with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, independently of everything else, an atom has decayed and this goat has turned into a car. The arrangement behind the doors is random. You choose a door at random. The host, who knows which door hides what but not whether the atom has decayed, then opens the door that is \emph{most likely} to hide a goat among the two doors you did not choose. (If the opened door shows a car, the game ends.) The host opens a door and it shows a goat. To maximise your chance of winning a car, should you stay or switch? Find both probabilities.
