Write $12=1+11$ and use the binomial theorem:
\[
12^{70}=(1+11)^{70}=1+\dbinom{70}{1}11+\dbinom{70}{2}11^2+\cdots+11^{70}.
\]
Every term from $\dbinom{70}{2}11^2$ on is divisible by $11^2=121$, so
\[
12^{70}\equiv1+70\cdot11=771\pmod{121}.
\]
Since $771=6\cdot121+45$, the remainder is $\boxed{45}$.
