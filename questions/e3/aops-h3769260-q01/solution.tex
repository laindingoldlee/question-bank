The decrease is $D=\dfrac{1}{x(x+1)}-\dfrac{2}{y(y+2)}+\dfrac{3}{z(z+3)}$. If $z\ge2$, then $D<\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{3}{10}=\dfrac{4}{5}$. With $z=1$, the largest positive $x$ term occurs at $x=2$, and the negative term is largest at $y=9$; these digits are distinct. This yields $D=\dfrac{1}{6}-\dfrac{2}{99}+\dfrac{3}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{355}{396}}>\dfrac{4}{5}$, establishing the global maximum.
