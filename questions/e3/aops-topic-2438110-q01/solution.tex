\textbf{(a)} $16^{\dfrac32}=\left(16^{\dfrac12}\right)^3=4^3=\boxed{64}$.

\textbf{(b)} The real cube root of $-8$ is $-2$, since $(-2)^3=-8$. So $(-8)^{\dfrac23}=\left((-8)^{\dfrac13}\right)^2=(-2)^2=\boxed{4}$.
