If Kelly wore red, Ann did not wear red would also be true, impossible. Thus Kelly is not red. If Ann did not wear red were true, the third claim would have to be false, putting Kathy in blue and forcing Kelly into red, again impossible. Therefore Ann wore red and the first two claims are false. The true claim is $\boxed{\text{Kathy did not wear blue}}$; Kathy wore white and Kelly blue.
