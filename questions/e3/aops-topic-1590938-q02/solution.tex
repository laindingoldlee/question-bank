Since $45=9\cdot5$ and $6!=720=45\cdot16$, every $n!$ with $n\ge6$ is a multiple of $6!$ and hence of $45$. So only the first five terms matter:
\[
1!+2!+3!+4!+5!=1+2+6+24+120=153=3\cdot45+18 .
\]
The remainder is $\boxed{18}$.
