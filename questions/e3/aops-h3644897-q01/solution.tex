Put $MT=u$, $MI=v$, and let their included angle be $\theta$. Then $uv=1$ and $\sin\theta=\dfrac{40}{41}$, so $\cos\theta=\pm\dfrac{9}{41}$. The diagonal has square $u^2+v^2+2uv\cos\theta\ge2-\dfrac{18}{41}=\dfrac{64}{41}$ by AM-GM. Equality is attained when $u=v=1$ and the angle is obtuse. Thus $\boxed{|a-b|=23}$.

\end{asy}
