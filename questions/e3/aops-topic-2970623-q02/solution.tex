The radius is $6$ and the height $25$, so the slant height is $\ell=\sqrt{25^2+6^2}=\sqrt{661}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(-6,0),B=(6,0),V=(0,10);draw(ellipse((0,0),6,1.5));draw(A--V--B);draw((0,0)--V,dashed);draw((0,0)--B,dashed);
label("$h=25$",(0,5),W);label("$r=6$",(3,-1.6),S);label("$\ell$",(3,5),NE);
\end{asy}

$v=\dfrac13\pi\cdot36\cdot25=300\pi$ and $a=\pi r^2+\pi r\ell=36\pi+6\pi\sqrt{661}$. Hence
\[av=300\pi(36\pi+6\pi\sqrt{661})=\boxed{1800\pi^2\left(6+\sqrt{661}\right)}.\]
