The discriminant is $(-3)^2-4\cdot2\cdot5=9-40=-31<0$, so there are no real roots. Equivalently,
\[
2x^2-3x+5=2\left(x-\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^2+\dfrac{31}{8}>0
\]
for every real $x$. The answer is $\boxed{0}$.
