In 2016 the Flying Turtles finished their baseball season with a record of $95$ wins and $67$ losses. The Dolphins finished the season with $84$ wins and $78$ losses. The Flying Turtles and the Dolphins played each other $19$ times during the season (no game ended in a tie). If the Flying Turtles had $F$ wins against teams other than the Dolphins, and the Dolphins had $D$ wins against teams other than the Flying Turtles, what is the value of $F+D$?
