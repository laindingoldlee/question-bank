With no carries, each decimal digit of the sum is the sum of the corresponding digits of $a_1,a_2,a_3$. The digits of $1010101$ are $1,0,1,0,1,0,1$. For a digit $0$, all three digits are $0$ ($1$ way); for a digit $1$, exactly one of the three numbers has a $1$ there ($3$ ways). With four $1$-digits there are $3^4=\boxed{81}$ triples.
