Let the edge lengths be $x,y,z$ with $xy=35$, $yz=15$ and $xz=21$. Multiplying the three equations,
\[
(xyz)^2=35\cdot15\cdot21=11025=105^2,
\]
so the volume is $xyz=\boxed{105}$ cubic centimetres. (Indeed $x=\dfrac{105}{15}=7$, $y=\dfrac{105}{21}=5$, $z=\dfrac{105}{35}=3$.)

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real a=7,bq=3,c=5;pair d=(0.55,0.4);
pair P0=(0,0),P1=(a,0),P2=(a,c),P3=(0,c);
pair Q0=P0+bq*d,Q1=P1+bq*d,Q2=P2+bq*d,Q3=P3+bq*d;
draw(P0--P1--P2--P3--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(P1--Q1--Q2--Q3--P3,linewidth(0.9));draw(P2--Q2,linewidth(0.9));
draw(P0--Q0,dashed);draw(Q0--Q1,dashed);draw(Q0--Q3,dashed);
label("$7$",(P0+P1)/2,S);label("$5$",(P0+P3)/2,W);label("$3$",(P1+Q1)/2,SE);
label("$35$",(P0+P2)/2);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
