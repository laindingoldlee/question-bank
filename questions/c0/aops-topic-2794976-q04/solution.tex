\emph{Violet:} if it told the truth it would have $9$ legs, an odd number, so it would be a liar; contradiction. So violet lies, and both its sentences are false; in particular dark-blue does not have $8$ legs.

\emph{Dark-blue:} its first sentence ``I have $8$ legs'' is false, so dark-blue lies.

\emph{Green:} if green told the truth, dark-blue would have $6$ legs, an even number, making dark-blue truthful; contradiction. So green lies and does not have $8$ legs.

\emph{Striped:} if it lied, the sentence ``None of you three has $8$ legs'' would be false, so one of green, dark-blue, violet would have $8$ legs; but all three are liars with odd numbers of legs. So striped tells the truth.

Therefore the \textbf{striped} polyleg has exactly $8$ legs (and the others have none).
