A polyleg with an even number of legs always tells the truth; one with an odd number of legs always lies (every sentence it says is false). A green polyleg says to a dark-blue one: ``I have $8$ legs. You have only $6$ legs!'' The dark-blue one replies: ``I have $8$ legs. You have only $7$ legs!'' A violet polyleg adds: ``The dark-blue polyleg has $8$ legs. I have $9$ legs!'' A striped polyleg says: ``None of you three has $8$ legs. Only I have $8$ legs!'' Which polyleg has exactly $8$ legs?
