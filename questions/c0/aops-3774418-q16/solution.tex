Rescale the segment to $[0,1]$. The ordered pair $(X,Y)$ is uniform in the unit square. The complement $|X-Y|\ge\dfrac{1}{4}$ consists of two right triangles with legs $\dfrac{3}{4}$, of total area $\dfrac{9}{16}$. Thus the probability is $1-\dfrac{9}{16}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{16}}$.

import graph;

\end{asy}
