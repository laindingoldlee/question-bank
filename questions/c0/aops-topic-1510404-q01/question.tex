A line $\ell$ is tangent to a circle with centre $O$ at the point $B$, and $BD$ is a chord. Let $X$ be a point of $\ell$ on the same side of line $OB$ as $D$, and let $\theta=\angle BOD$ be the measure of the arc $BD$ that lies inside $\angle XBD$, where $0^\circ<\theta<180^\circ$. Prove that
\[
\angle XBD=\dfrac{\theta}{2},
\]
that is, the angle between a tangent and a chord is half of the intercepted arc. (For example, if arc $BD=110^\circ$, then $\angle XBD=55^\circ$.)
