In triangle $ABC$, $AB=425$, $BC=450$ and $AC=510$. An interior point $P$ is drawn, and segments are drawn through $P$ parallel to the sides of the triangle: $DF\parallel AC$ with $D$ on $AB$ and $F$ on $BC$; $EH\parallel BC$ with $E$ on $AB$ and $H$ on $AC$; $GI\parallel AB$ with $G$ on $BC$ and $I$ on $AC$. If the three segments $DF$, $EH$ and $GI$ all have the same length $x$, find $x$.
