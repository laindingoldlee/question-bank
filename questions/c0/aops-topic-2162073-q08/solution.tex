There are $\dbinom{12}{2}=66$ equally likely pairs of socks. The pairs of one colour number
\[\dbinom62+\dbinom42+\dbinom22=15+6+1=22,\]
so the probability is $\dfrac{22}{66}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{3}}$.
