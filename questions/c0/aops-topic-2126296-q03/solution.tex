The sum of the four consecutive positive integers $a,a+1,a+2,a+3$ is $4a+6$, where $a\ge1$. So the numbers that \emph{can} be written in this way are
\[10,\ 14,\ 18,\ \dots,\]
the numbers that are at least $10$ and leave remainder $2$ on division by $4$. Below $2020$ the largest is $2018=4\cdot503+6$, so there are $503$ of them (one for each $a=1,2,\dots,503$).

There are $2019$ positive integers less than $2020$, so
\[2019-503=\boxed{1516}\]
of them cannot be written as the sum of four consecutive positive integers.
