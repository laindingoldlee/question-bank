Factor $2$ out of the numerator: $2+2n=2(1+n)$, so
\[\dfrac{2+2n}{2}=\dfrac{2(n+1)}{2}=n+1,\qquad\text{hence}\qquad n\cdot\dfrac{2+2n}{2}=n(n+1).\]
The sum $2+4+\cdots+2n$ is an arithmetic series of $n$ terms with first term $2$ and last term $2n$, so it equals the number of terms times the average of the first and last terms:
\[2+4+\cdots+2n=n\cdot\dfrac{2+2n}{2}=\boxed{n(n+1)}.\]
