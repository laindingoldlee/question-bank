If $n=p_1^{a_1}\cdots p_r^{a_r}$ has $10$ divisors, then $(a_1+1)\cdots(a_r+1)=10$, so $n=p^9$ or $n=p^4q$ with distinct primes $p,q$. A multiple of $6$ has at least the two primes $2$ and $3$, so $n=p^4q$ with $\{p,q\}=\{2,3\}$:
\[
n=2^4\cdot3=48\qquad\text{or}\qquad n=3^4\cdot2=162 .
\]
The sum is $48+162=\boxed{210}$.
