The coefficients $A,B,D,E$ of the system $Ax+By=C$, $Dx+Ey=F$ are chosen independently and uniformly from $\{1,2,\dots,6\}$ (and $C,F$ arbitrarily). What is the probability that the system has exactly one solution?
