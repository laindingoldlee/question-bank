Both expressions in the maximum are at most $2z+2017$, so their sum is at most $2(2z+2017)$:
\[4x^2+6y+9y^2+4x+2z^2+4038\le4z+4034 .\]
Rearranging and completing squares,
\[\left(4x^2+4x+1\right)+\left(9y^2+6y+1\right)+2\left(z^2-2z+1\right)\le0,\]
that is,
\[(2x+1)^2+(3y+1)^2+2(z-1)^2\le0 .\]
A sum of squares with positive coefficients is nonnegative, and it is $0$ only when every square is $0$. Hence
\[x=-\dfrac{1}{2},\qquad y=-\dfrac{1}{3},\qquad z=1,\]
and then $z-2x+3y=1+1-1=1$, so $\sqrt{z-2x+3y}=\boxed{1}$, a positive integer. $\blacksquare$

(These values really satisfy the hypothesis: both expressions equal $1-2+1+2019=2019=2\cdot1+2017$.)
