The available digits are $1,2,9$ (once each) and $3$ (up to three times).

\textbf{All digits different:} choose an ordered selection of $3$ of the $4$ distinct digits $1,2,3,9$: $4\cdot3\cdot2=24$ numbers.

\textbf{Exactly two equal digits:} the repeated digit must be $3$. Choose the other digit ($1$, $2$ or $9$: $3$ ways) and its position ($3$ ways): $9$ numbers.

\textbf{All three equal:} only $333$: $1$ number.

Total: $24+9+1=\boxed{34}$.
