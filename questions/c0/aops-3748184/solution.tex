Factor $648=2^3\cdot3^4$. Each die can contribute at most one factor of $3$, so all four rolls must be $3$ or $6$. To contribute at least three factors of $2$, at least three must be $6$. There are $\binom43+\binom44=5$ qualifying ordered outcomes among $6^4=1296$. The probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{5}{1296}}$.
