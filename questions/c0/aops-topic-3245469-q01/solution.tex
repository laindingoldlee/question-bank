The identity $k=2(k+1)-(k+2)$ gives $\dfrac{k2^{k+1}}{(k+1)(k+2)}=\dfrac{2^{k+2}}{k+2}-\dfrac{2^{k+1}}{k+1}$. The sum telescopes, leaving $\dfrac{2^{10}}{10}-\dfrac{2^2}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{502}{5}}$.
