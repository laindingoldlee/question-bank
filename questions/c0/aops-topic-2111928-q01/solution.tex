There are $12$ face cards and $40$ other cards, and $\dbinom{52}{4}=270725$ equally likely hands. Count the complement, the hands with fewer than $2$ face cards:
\begin{itemize}
\item no face card: $\dbinom{40}{4}=91390$ hands;
\item exactly one face card: $12\cdot\dbinom{40}{3}=12\cdot9880=118560$ hands.
\end{itemize}
These are $209950$ hands, so $270725-209950=60775$ hands contain at least two face cards. The probability is
\[\dfrac{60775}{270725}=\boxed{\dfrac{11}{49}}\approx0.224 .\]
(Indeed $60775=11\cdot5525$ and $270725=49\cdot5525$.)
