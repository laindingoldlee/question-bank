Since $\angle ACP=\angle AMP=90^\circ$, points $A,C,P,M$ lie on a circle with diameter $AP$. Hence
\[
\angle MCB=\angle MCP=\angle MAP=\dfrac{A}{2}.
\]
Similarly $B,C,Q,N$ are concyclic, and $\angle NCA=\angle NBQ=\dfrac{B}{2}$. The rays $CM,CN$ lie inside the right angle at $C$, so
\[
\angle MCN=90^\circ-\dfrac{A+B}{2}=\boxed{45^\circ}.
\]
