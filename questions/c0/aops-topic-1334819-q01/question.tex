A regular octagon $ABCDEFGH$ has side length $1$. A bee's hive is at vertex $A$. There is a nectar river along side $DE$ and a water river along side $FG$. The bee flies from $A$, visits both rivers (in either order) and returns to $A$. What is the shortest possible length of its trip?
