$2023=7\cdot17^2$ has $(1+1)(2+1)=6$ divisors. An integer with six divisors is $p^5$ or $p^2q$ for distinct primes. The smallest examples are $12=2^2\cdot3$, $18=3^2\cdot2$, and $20=2^2\cdot5$; the least $p^5$ is $32$. Every other $p^2q$ is at least $20$, with equality as above. The third is $\boxed{20}$.
