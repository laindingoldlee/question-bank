There are $(7-1)!=720$ seatings in all.

\textbf{No two ghosts adjacent.} Seat the four other monsters first: $(4-1)!=6$ ways. They leave $4$ gaps around the table, and the three ghosts go into three different gaps in $4\cdot3\cdot2=24$ ways. That is $6\cdot24=144$ seatings.

\textbf{(a)} $720-144=\boxed{576}$.

\textbf{(b)} All three ghosts in a block: treat the block as one person, giving $(5-1)!=24$ seatings of the $5$ units, times $3!=6$ orders inside the block: $144$. Exactly two adjacent is therefore $576-144=\boxed{432}$.
