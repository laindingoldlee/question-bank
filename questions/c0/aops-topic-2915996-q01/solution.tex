Per hour, headphones use $\dfrac1{17}$ of the charge and speakers $\dfrac13$. With $t$ hours of each, $t\left(\dfrac1{17}+\dfrac13\right)=1$, so $t=\dfrac{51}{20}$ and the total is $2t=\dfrac{51}{10}$ hours $=\boxed{306}$ minutes. This equals $\dfrac{2\cdot17\cdot3}{17+3}$, the harmonic mean: it applies because the two modes run for equal \emph{times}, so the drain rates (reciprocals of the lifetimes) are averaged arithmetically.
