The three-digit multiples of $11$ are $11\cdot10=110$ up to $11\cdot90=990$ (since $11\cdot9=99$ and $11\cdot91=1001$). So they are $11k$ for $k=10,11,\dots,90$, and there are $90-10+1=\boxed{81}$ of them.
