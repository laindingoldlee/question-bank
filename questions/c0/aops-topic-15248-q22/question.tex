A slice with a central angle of $80^\circ$ is cut from a circular pizza of radius $9$ inches. A rectangular pizza measuring $12$ inches by $16$ inches is cut into $20$ pieces of equal area. What is the positive difference, in square inches, between the area of the circular slice and the area of one rectangular piece? Give an exact answer and a decimal approximation.
