The slice is a sector with $\dfrac{80}{360}=\dfrac{2}{9}$ of the area of the pizza: $\dfrac{2}{9}\cdot81\pi=18\pi$ square inches. One rectangular piece has area $\dfrac{12\cdot16}{20}=\dfrac{48}{5}=9.6$ square inches.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0); real r=3;
filldraw(O--arc(O,r,50,130)--cycle,rgb(1,0.88,0.7));
draw(circle(O,r)); label("$80^\circ$",O,8*N,fontsize(8pt)); label("$9$",(O+r*dir(50))/2,SE);
pair P=(5,-2);
draw(P--P+(4,0)--P+(4,3)--P+(0,3)--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
for(int i=1;i<5;++i) draw(P+(i*0.8,0)--P+(i*0.8,3),gray);
for(int j=1;j<4;++j) draw(P+(0,j*0.75)--P+(4,j*0.75),gray);
fill(P--P+(0.8,0)--P+(0.8,0.75)--P+(0,0.75)--cycle,rgb(0.85,0.92,1));
label("$16$",P+(2,0),S); label("$12$",P+(4,1.5),E);
\end{asy}

The difference is $\boxed{18\pi-\dfrac{48}{5}}\approx56.55-9.6=46.95$ square inches.
