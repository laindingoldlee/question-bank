Let $S_n=\dfrac12+\dfrac14+\cdots+\dfrac1{2^n}$. Then $2S_n=1+\dfrac12+\cdots+\dfrac1{2^{n-1}}$, and subtracting, $S_n=2S_n-S_n=1-\dfrac1{2^n}$. (Equivalently: each new term covers exactly half of the remaining gap to $1$.) As $n\to\infty$, $\dfrac1{2^n}\to0$, so the infinite sum, the limit of $S_n$, is $\boxed{1}$.
