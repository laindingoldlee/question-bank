Evaluate the tower from the top: $2^2=4$ and $3^4=81$, so the left side is $2^{2^{81}}$. The right side is $4^{4^x}=2^{2\cdot4^x}=2^{2\cdot2^{2x}}=2^{2^{2x+1}}$. Hence $2^{2x+1}=2^{81}$, so $2x+1=81$ and $x=\boxed{40}$.
