Prove that for every positive integer $s\ne2$, the equation
\[\dfrac1{x_1}+\dfrac1{x_2}+\cdots+\dfrac1{x_s}=1\]
has a solution in which every $x_i$ is a triangular number $\dfrac{k(k+1)}{2}$ with $k$ a positive integer.
