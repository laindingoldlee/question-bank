Multiplying by $n+1$ gives $n^2+n+1\equiv0$, hence $n^3\equiv1$. If $n\equiv1$, this requires $p=3$, which works. Otherwise $n$ has multiplicative order 3, so Fermat's theorem implies $3\mid p-1$. The primes below 30 with this property are 7, 13, 19, and they all work, with respective choices $n=2,3,7$. Thus the complete prime list is 3, 7, 13, 19, and its product is $\boxed{5187}$.
