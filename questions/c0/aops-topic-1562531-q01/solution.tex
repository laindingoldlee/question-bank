The centre of a rectangle is halfway between its top and bottom edges, so the top edge is $\dfrac{3.5}{2}=1.75$ feet above the centre. The distance from the top to the ceiling is therefore
\[
4-1.75=\boxed{2.25\text{ feet}}.
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((-1.2,0)--(5.2,0),linewidth(1.2));
for(int i=0;i<13;++i) draw((-1.2+0.5*i,0)--(-1.2+0.5*i+0.3,0.3),gray);
label("ceiling",(5.2,0),E);
pair c=(2,-4);
draw(box(c-(2,1.75),c+(2,1.75)),linewidth(1));
dot(c);label("centre",c,E);
draw((-0.6,0)--(-0.6,-4),blue,Arrows(TeXHead));label("$4$",(-0.6,-2),W,blue);
draw((2,0)--(2,-2.25),red,Arrows(TeXHead));label("$d$",(2,-1.1),E,red);
draw((4.6,-2.25)--(4.6,-5.75),Arrows(TeXHead));label("$3.5$",(4.6,-4),E);
label("$4$",(2,-5.75),S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

(The width $4$ feet is not needed.)
