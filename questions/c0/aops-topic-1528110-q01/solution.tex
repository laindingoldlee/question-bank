Let $n=2017$, so $2018=n+1$. The expression under the root is
\[
\dfrac{n^2+(n+1)^2\left(n^2+1\right)}{(n+1)^2},
\]
and the numerator is
\[
n^2+\left(n^2+2n+1\right)\left(n^2+1\right)=n^4+2n^3+3n^2+2n+1=\left(n^2+n+1\right)^2 .
\]
So the square root equals $\dfrac{n^2+n+1}{n+1}$ (positive), and the whole expression is
\[
\dfrac{n}{n+1}+\dfrac{n^2+n+1}{n+1}=\dfrac{n^2+2n+1}{n+1}=n+1=\boxed{2018}.
\]
