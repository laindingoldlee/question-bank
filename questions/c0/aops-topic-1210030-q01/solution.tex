Let there be $3k$ girls and $4k$ boys. Then
\[\dfrac{3k-4}{4k-6}=\dfrac{4}{5}\ \Longrightarrow\ 15k-20=16k-24\ \Longrightarrow\ k=4.\]
So there are $12$ girls and $16$ boys, $\boxed{28}$ students (check: $8$ girls and $10$ boys remain, ratio $4:5$).
