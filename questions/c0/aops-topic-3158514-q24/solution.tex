Put $ y=\sqrt[4]{5-x^4}\ge0 $, so $ x+y=2 $ and $ x^4+y^4=5 $. Since $|x|,y\le5^{\dfrac{1}{4}}<2 $, both $ x,y $ are positive. If $ p=xy $, then $ p\le1 $ and $ 5=(x+y)^4-4xy(x+y)^2+2x^2y^2=16-16p+2p^2 $. Its roots are $ 4\pm\dfrac{\sqrt{42}}{2}$, of which only $ p=4-\dfrac{\sqrt{42}}{2}$ lies in $(0,1]$. Conversely the two positive roots of $ t^2-2t+p=0 $ have fourth powers summing to 5, so this value is attained. Thus $\boxed{4-\dfrac{\sqrt{42}}{2}}$.
