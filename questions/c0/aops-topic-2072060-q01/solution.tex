Look at how each term is obtained from the previous one:
\[
5\xrightarrow{+1}6\xrightarrow{-2}4\xrightarrow{\times3}12\xrightarrow{\div4}3\xrightarrow{+5}8\xrightarrow{-6}2\xrightarrow{\times7}14\xrightarrow{\div8}1.75\xrightarrow{+9}10.75\xrightarrow{-10}0.75 .
\]
The operations cycle through $+,\ -,\ \times,\ \div$ applied with $1,2,3,4,5,\dots$ in turn. The next step is $\times11$, giving
\[
0.75\times11=8.25,
\]
and then $\div12$ gives $8.25\div12=0.6875$, which matches the last given term. The missing number is $\boxed{8.25}$.
