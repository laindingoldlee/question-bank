There are $4\cdot6\cdot9=216$ unit cubes in all. The cubes wholly inside form a $2\times4\times7$ block with 56 cubes. Thus the outer layer has $216-56=160$ cubes. A single-cube-wide stack is $\boxed{160\text{ cm}}$ tall.

\end{asy}
