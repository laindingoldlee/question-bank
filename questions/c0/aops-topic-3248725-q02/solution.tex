The sum of two distinct digits is at most $17$, so $11C=E+M$ forces $C=1$ and $E+M=11$. Therefore $\overline{EC}+\overline{MC}=10(E+M)+2C=110+2=\boxed{112}$. Valid digits exist, for example $E=2,M=9$.
