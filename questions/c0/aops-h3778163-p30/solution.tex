The difference is
\[
\left(a-\dfrac{b}{2}\right)^2+\left(d-\dfrac{c}{2}\right)^2+\dfrac{3}{4}(b^2+c^2)-pbc.
\]
Since $b^2+c^2\ge2|bc|$, this is nonnegative whenever $|p|\le\dfrac{3}{2}$. Conversely, choose $a=\dfrac{b}{2},d=\dfrac{c}{2}$ and then $(b,c)=(1,1)$ or $(1,-1)$. These force $p\le\dfrac{3}{2}$ and $p\ge-\dfrac{3}{2}$, respectively. Thus $\boxed{-\dfrac{3}{2}\le p\le\dfrac{3}{2}}$.
