Triangles $ABD$ and $ABC$ have the same height from $A$ to line $BC$, so their areas are in the ratio $BD:BC=1:5$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,3),B=(0,0),C=(5,0),D=(1,0);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--D);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,S);
\end{asy}

The area of $ABC$ is $5\cdot8=\boxed{40}$ in$^2$.
