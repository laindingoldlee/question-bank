Each red bird eats $\dfrac{36}{3\cdot4}=3 $ grams per day, and each blue bird eats $\dfrac{60}{5\cdot3}=4 $. The group eats $ 2(3)+4(4)=22 $ grams per day, so the food lasts $ 88\div22=\boxed4 $ days.
