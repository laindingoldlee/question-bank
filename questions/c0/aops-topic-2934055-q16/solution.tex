At least one prime is 17; call it $p$. Then $qr=17+q+r$, or $(q-1)(r-1)=18$. The positive factor pairs are $(1,18),(2,9),(3,6)$. Only the first gives two primes, namely 2 and 19. Thus $\boxed{pqr=2\cdot17\cdot19=646}$.
