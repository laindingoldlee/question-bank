Let $x$ and $y$ be positive integers. Prove or disprove each statement; when a statement is false, give a counterexample and state the correct result.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Two consecutive integers $x$ and $x+1$ are relatively prime.
\item[(b)] $\gcd(x,x+2)=2$.
\item[(c)] $\gcd(x,x+y)=y$.
\end{enumerate}
