As the centres form a square $ABDC$ of side $1$, its diagonal is $AD=\sqrt2$. Since $X$ is on circle $A$, $AX=1$, and since $X$ is on segment $AD$, $XD=\sqrt2-1$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(1,0),D=(1,1),C=(0,1);
draw(arc(A,1,0,90),blue);
draw(A--B--D--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(A--D,red+linewidth(0.8));
pair X=dir(45); dot(X,red); label("$X$",(0.73,0.6),red);
dot(A);dot(B);dot(C);dot(D);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NW);label("$D$",D,NE);
label("$1$",X/2,dir(-45));
label("$\sqrt2-1$",(0.72,0.93),fontsize(8pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Hence
\[
AX\cdot XD=1\cdot\left(\sqrt2-1\right)=\boxed{\sqrt2-1}.
\]
(The other point of circle $A$ on line $AD$, beyond $A$, would give $1\cdot\left(\sqrt2+1\right)$.)
