An object starts at $1$ on the integer line. At each fair coin toss it moves one unit right for heads and one unit left for tails. It stops on first reaching $0$ or a fixed positive integer $N$; if $N=1$, it stops immediately. Let $P(N)$ be the probability of stopping at $N$.
(a) Find $P(3)$. (b) Find $P(N)$ for all positive integers $N$.
