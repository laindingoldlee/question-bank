For base $u$, the altitude is $\sqrt{25-\dfrac{u^2}{4}}$, so area squared is $\dfrac{u^2(100-u^2)}{16}$. At $u=2\sqrt5$ this equals 100. Thus $x^2(100-x^2)=1600$, or $(x^2-20)(x^2-80)=0$. The other positive base is $\boxed{4\sqrt5}$, which is below 10 and hence valid.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
for(int k=0;k<2;++k){real w=(k+1)*2*sqrt(5),h=sqrt(25-w*w/4);pair A=(12*k,0),B=A+(w,0),C=A+(w/2,h);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(C--(A+B)/2,dashed);label("$5$",(A+C)/2,W);label("$5$",(B+C)/2,E);label(k==0?"$2\sqrt5$":"$4\sqrt5$",(A+B)/2,S);}
\end{asy}
