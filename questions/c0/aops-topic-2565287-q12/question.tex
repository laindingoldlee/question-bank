Define a map $F$ on points by
\[F(x,y)=\begin{cases}(x+1,\,y-1)&\text{if }x>0,\\(x-1,\,y+1)&\text{if }x<0.\end{cases}\]
A robot starts at the point $(1,3)$ and repeatedly moves from its current point $P$ to $F(P)$. How many different points does the robot visit? Prove your answer.
