Write $\sigma(m)$ for the sum of the divisors of $m$. The numbers $51,52,53$ are deficient ($\sigma=72,98,54$), so $n=50,51,52$ fail. For $n=53$: $52$ is deficient ($98<104$) and $54$ is abundant ($120>108$). The answer is $\boxed{53}$.
