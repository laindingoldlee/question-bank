The numbers $199,198,197$ have respectively $2,12,2$ divisors: $199,197$ are prime and $198=2\cdot3^2\cdot11$. But $196=2^2\cdot7^2$ has $(2+1)^2=9$ divisors. Therefore the largest qualifying integer is $\boxed{196}$.
