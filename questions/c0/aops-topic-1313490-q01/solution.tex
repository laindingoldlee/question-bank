Factor: $x^5+4x=x(x^4+4)=x\left(x^2+2x+2\right)\left(x^2-2x+2\right)$ (Sophie Germain). For $x\ge2$, the factors $x\ge2$ and $x^2+2x+2\ge10$ are both greater than $1$, so the number is composite. Hence $x=1$ and $p=1+4=5$, which is prime: $\boxed{x=1,\ p=5}$.
