Move everything to one side:
\[p^{27}-21p^{13}+19p^{10}-p^3-p^2+17p=384.\]
Every term on the left is divisible by $p$, so $p$ divides $384=2^7\cdot3$. Hence $p=2$ or $p=3$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $p=2$: the left side of the original equation is $8+4-34+304=282$, and the right side is $2^{27}-21\cdot2^{13}+19\cdot2^{10}-80=134065072$.
\item $p=3$: the left side is $27+9-51+304=289$, and the right side is $3^{13}\left(3^{14}-21\right)+19\cdot3^{10}-80>3^{13}>289$.
\end{itemize}
Neither works, so $\boxed{0}$ primes satisfy the equation.
