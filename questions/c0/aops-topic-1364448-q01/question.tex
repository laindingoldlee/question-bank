A bin contains $5$ red, $6$ blue and $7$ green balls. Balls are drawn one at a time at random without replacement until all the balls of some colour have been drawn. What is the probability that the red balls are the first colour to be used up?

(A student argues: the probability that the last ball is red is $\dfrac{5}{18}$, and so on, and concludes that the answer is $\dfrac{5}{18}$. Is that right?)
