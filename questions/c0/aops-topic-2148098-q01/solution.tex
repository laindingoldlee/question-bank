The divisor is $12_5=7$. Work digit by digit in base $5$, as in ordinary long division.
\begin{itemize}
\item The first two digits give $41_5=21$; $21\div7=3$ remainder $0$, and $3\cdot12_5=41_5$. Quotient digit $3$, remainder $0$.
\item Bring down $2$: $2_5=2<7$. Quotient digit $0$, remainder $2$.
\item Bring down $3$: $23_5=13$; $13\div7=1$ remainder $6=11_5$. Quotient digit $1$.
\end{itemize}
So $4123_5\div12_5$ has quotient $\boxed{301_5}$ and remainder $\boxed{11_5}$.

Check in base $10$: $4123_5=538$, $301_5=76$, and $76\cdot7+6=538$.
