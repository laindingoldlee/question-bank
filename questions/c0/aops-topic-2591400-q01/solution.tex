Interchanging the first and third digits gives $316$, $234$, $375$, $584$, $709$. In descending order:
\[709,\quad584,\quad375,\quad316,\quad234.\]
The middle number is $375$, whose middle digit is $\boxed{\textbf{(D) }7}$.
