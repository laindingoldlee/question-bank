Use $A=(0,0),O=(0,4),B=(4,0),P=(4,1)$. The far radial point is $D=(\dfrac{24}{5},\dfrac{2}{5})$, so its tangent is $4x-3y=18$. Thus $C=(0,-6)$ and $K=(\dfrac{9}{2},0)$. The right triangle $AKC$ has hypotenuse $\dfrac{15}{2}$ and inradius $\dfrac{(6+\dfrac{9}{2}-\dfrac{15}{2})}{2}=\dfrac{3}{2}$, giving $Q=(\dfrac{3}{2},-\dfrac{3}{2})$. Finally $[OPQ]=\dfrac{|\det((4,-3),(\dfrac{3}{2},-\dfrac{11}{2}))|}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{35}{4}}$.
