Let $\omega_1$ be a circle with center $O$ and radius $4$ and $\omega_2$ be a circle with center $P$ and radius $1$ such that $\omega_1$ and $\omega_2$ are externally tangent to each other and both tangent to line $\overleftrightarrow{AB}$, with $\omega_1$ tangent at $A$, and $\omega_2$ tangent at $B$. Point $D$ lies on $\omega_2$ such that $O$, $P$, and $D$ are collinear and $D$ is not on $\omega_1$. Line $\ell$ is tangent to circle $\omega_2$ at $D$. Let $C$ be the point of intersection of line $\overleftrightarrow{OA}$ and line $\ell$, and let $K$ be the point of intersection of line $\overleftrightarrow{AB}$ and line $\ell$. If $Q$ is the center of the inscribed circle of triangle $\vartriangle AKC$, compute the area of $\vartriangle OPQ$.
