Two angles form a linear pair (they are adjacent, and their non-common sides are opposite rays, so together they make a straight angle of $180^\circ$). The measure of one angle is $16^\circ$ greater than the measure of the other. Find the measures of both angles; call the larger one $\angle A$ and the smaller one $\angle B$.
