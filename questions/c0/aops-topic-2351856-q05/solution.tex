Rewrite as $666x^2-25x-666=0$. The discriminant is $25^2+4\cdot666^2=625+1774224=1774849=41\cdot73\cdot593$, which is not a perfect square, so
\[x=\boxed{\dfrac{25\pm\sqrt{1774849}}{1332}}.\]
(Check: the product of the roots is $\dfrac{-666}{666}=-1$, and indeed $\dfrac{(25+\sqrt{1774849})(25-\sqrt{1774849})}{1332^2}=\dfrac{625-1774849}{1774224}=-1$.)
