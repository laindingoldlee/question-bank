One straight cut divides a convex sheet into at most $2$ pieces (a convex region minus a line has at most two parts), so one minute is not enough.

Two minutes suffice: cut along the full line joining the midpoints of one pair of opposite sides, then along the full line joining the midpoints of the other pair. The two cuts cross at the centre and split the rectangle into four smaller rectangles. (Equally, after the first cut he could stack the two halves and cut both at once.)

So the answer is $\boxed{2}$ minutes.
