Let $p$ and $q$ be real numbers. The quadratic equation
\[
\left(1-q+\dfrac{p^2}{2}\right)x^2+p(1+q)x+q(q-1)+\dfrac{p^2}{2}=0
\]
has a repeated real root. Prove that $p^2=4q$.
