Let $T$ minutes be Bob's catch-up time to Charlie. Their relative speed is $\dfrac{2}{T}$ miles per minute. Alice's catch-up time to Bob is $2T$, so their relative speed is $\dfrac{1}{2T}$. Relative speeds add, giving Alice's speed relative to Charlie as $\dfrac{5}{2T}$. Their gap is $3$ miles, so Alice's catch-up time is $\dfrac{3}{\dfrac{5}{2T}}=\dfrac{6T}{5}=10$. Hence $\boxed{T=\dfrac{25}{3}\text{ minutes}}$.
