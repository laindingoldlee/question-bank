All $100$ numbers from $200$ to $299$ contain a $2$. Among $100$--$199$, a $2$ must be the tens or units digit: $10$ numbers have tens digit $2$, $10$ have units digit $2$, and $1$ ($122$) has both, so $10+10-1=19$ numbers. Likewise $19$ among $300$--$399$, and $100$ and $400$ have no $2$. The total is $100+19+19=\boxed{138}$, answer $\textbf{(C)}$.
