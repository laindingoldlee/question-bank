Taste positions 3,6,9. If none were chocolate, every chocolate run would lie in one of the four remaining two-position blocks. Since isolated chocolate beans are forbidden, each such block contains either zero or two chocolate beans. Their total would be even, contradicting five. Thus three tastings suffice. For the lower bound, any two tasted positions split the remaining nine positions into at most three consecutive intervals. If an interval has length at least five, place all five chocolate beans there. Otherwise the largest length is 3 or 4, and another interval has length at least 2 (the total is nine); place a run of three in the largest and a run of two in another. In both cases a valid placement avoids both tasted positions. Hence two tastings cannot guarantee success, even adaptively, and the minimum is $\boxed3$.
