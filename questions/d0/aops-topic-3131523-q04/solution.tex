A failing draw has $g<2r$, so $g\le2r-1$. With $r\le4$ and blue count at most 8, it has at most $r+g+b\le4+7+8=19$ socks. A draw of all 4 red, all 8 blue, and 7 green shows 19 can fail. Hence $\boxed{20}$ guarantee success.
