The base is $625+1296+49+1-1337+42=676=26^2$. Then $676^3-1=(676-1)(676^2+676+1)=675\cdot457653$. Here $675=3^3\cdot5^2$ and $457653=3\cdot7\cdot19\cdot31\cdot37$, so the answer is $\boxed{3^4\cdot5^2\cdot7\cdot19\cdot31\cdot37}$.
