(a) Yes. Number the glasses cyclically modulo $179$. For each $i=0,\ldots,178$, turn glasses $i,i+1,i+2$. Every glass is turned exactly three times, so all finish upright. (b) No. Turning four glasses changes the number upright by an even amount. Its parity stays even from the initial zero, so it cannot become $179$.
