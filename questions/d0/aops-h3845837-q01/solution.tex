Apply Burnside\textquotesingle s lemma to the twelve symmetries. The identity fixes $2^6=64$ colorings. The two rotations by one or five sectors each fix $2$; the two rotations by two or four sectors each fix $4$; the half-turn fixes $8$. The rotation total is $84$. Each of three reflections through opposite sector centers has two fixed sectors and two exchanged pairs, hence fixes $2^4=16$ colorings. Each of three reflections through opposite hexagon vertices has three exchanged pairs, hence fixes $2^3=8$. The total is $84+48+24=156$, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{156}{12}=13}$ orbits.
