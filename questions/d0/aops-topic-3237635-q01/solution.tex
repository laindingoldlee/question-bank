Digit sums preserve residues modulo nine. Since $11\equiv2\pmod9$ and $2^6\equiv1\pmod9$, while $2023\equiv1\pmod6$, we get $11^{2023}\equiv2\pmod9$. The unique positive one-digit representative is $\boxed2$.
