The assumption means 5 is not a quadratic residue modulo 43, so Euler's criterion gives $ 5^{21}\equiv-1 $. The 21 nonzero quadratic residues $ r $ are exactly the roots of $ X^{21}-1 $ in the field modulo 43. Each occurs twice among $ i^2 $ for $ i=1,\ldots,42 $. Therefore $\prod_{i=1}^{42}(i^2-5)=\left(\prod_r(r-5)\right)^2=(5^{21}-1)^2\equiv4 $. The $ i=0 $ factor is $-5 $, so $ M\equiv-20\equiv\boxed{23}\pmod{43}$.
