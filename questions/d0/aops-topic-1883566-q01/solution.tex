A colour combination is determined by how many marbles of each colour are chosen: nonnegative integers $r+b+p=3$ (with at least three of each colour available, every such choice is possible). By stars and bars there are $\dbinom{5}{2}=\boxed{10}$ combinations. Listing them: three of one colour ($3$ ways), two of one colour and one of another ($3\cdot2=6$ ways), one of each colour ($1$ way). This confirms the poster's answer.
