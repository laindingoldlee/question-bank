A positive integer has exactly three divisors precisely when it is the square of a prime. Write $n=p^2,n+72=q^2$, so $(q-p)(q+p)=72$. The case $p=2$ gives $q^2=76$, impossible. Thus both primes are odd. Set $u=\dfrac{q-p}{2}$, $v=\dfrac{q+p}{2}$; then $uv=18$, $u<v$. Pairs $(1,18),(2,9),(3,6)$ give $(p,q)=(17,19),(7,11),(3,9)$. The last fails primality. Hence $\boxed{n=49,289}$.
