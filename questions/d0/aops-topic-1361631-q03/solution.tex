Use the identity
\[
x^3+y^3+z^3-3xyz=(x+y+z)\left(x^2+y^2+z^2-xy-yz-zx\right)
\]
and $x^2+y^2+z^2=(x+y+z)^2-2(xy+yz+zx)=36-22=14$. Then
\[
x^3+y^3+z^3-3xyz=6\,(14-11)=\boxed{\textbf{(c) }18} .
\]
(For example $x,y,z=1,2,3$: $1+8+27-18=18$.)
