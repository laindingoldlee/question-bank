If $2021=a^2-b^2=(a-b)(a+b)$ with $a>b>0$, then $a-b$ and $a+b$ are complementary divisors of $2021$ with $a-b<a+b$. Both are odd, so $a=\dfrac{(a-b)+(a+b)}{2}$ and $b$ are integers for every such pair.

Trying squares just above $2021$: $45^2=2025=2021+4=2021+2^2$, so
\[2021=45^2-2^2=(45-2)(45+2)=43\cdot47.\]
$43$ and $47$ are prime: neither is divisible by $2$, $3$ or $5$, and $7^2=49>47$. So $2021=43\cdot47$, its only divisor pairs are $1\cdot2021$ and $43\cdot47$, and the representations are
\[2021=1011^2-1010^2=45^2-2^2.\]
Finally $44=2^2\cdot11$, $45=3^2\cdot5$ and $46=2\cdot23$ are composite, so no prime lies between $43$ and $47$: they are consecutive primes.

$\boxed{2021=45^2-2^2=1011^2-1010^2=43\cdot47}$
