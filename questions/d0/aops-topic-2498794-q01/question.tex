\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Two triangles are similar, and each side of the second is $k$ times the corresponding side of the first. Prove that the area of the second triangle is $k^2$ times the area of the first.
\item[(b)] Triangles $ABX$ and $BCX$ are similar, with $AB$ and $BC$ corresponding sides, and $\dfrac{[ABX]}{[BCX]}=\dfrac12$. Find $\dfrac{AB}{BC}$.
\end{enumerate}
