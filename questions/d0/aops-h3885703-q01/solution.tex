The first two conditions are equivalent because the length is odd. Let $x$ mark an odd digit and $y$ a prime digit. There are four even nonprime digits, two odd nonprime digits, one even prime digit, and three odd prime digits, giving the polynomial $4+2x+y+3xy$. The odd-$x$ part after five choices is obtained by substituting $x=1,-1$ and taking half the difference:
\[
\dfrac{(6+4y)^5-(2-2y)^5}{2}.
\]
The prime numbers of prime digits are $2,3,5$. Their respective coefficients are $17120,11680,528$. The total is $29328$, so the answer is $\boxed{328}$.
