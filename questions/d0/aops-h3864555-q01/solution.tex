Write the two numbers as $10a+b$ and $10b+a$, where $a,b\in\{1,\ldots,9\}$ and $a\ne b$. Their square sum is
\[
101(a^2+b^2)+40ab.
\]
By symmetry assume $a<b$. Then $a\ge1,b\ge2$, and every coefficient is positive, so this expression is minimized at $(a,b)=(1,2)$. This pair also meets the reversal condition:
\[
12^2=144,\qquad21^2=441.
\]
Thus the requested minimum is $\boxed{\dfrac{(144+441)}{5}=117}$.
