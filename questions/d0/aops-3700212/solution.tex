Separate the sums. The square-sum identity gives $\sum k^2=\dfrac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6}$, and the geometric sum gives $\sum_{k=1}^n2^k=2^{n+1}-2$. Thus the answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6}+2^{n+1}-2}$. The square-sum identity follows by induction: its change from $n-1$ to $n$ is $n^2$, and it equals one at $n=1$.
