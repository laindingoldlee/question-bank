Since $13=2^2+3^2$, $74=7^2+5^2$ and $85=9^2+2^2$, the triangle can be placed on a lattice: $A=(7,0)$, $B=(9,3)$, $C=(0,5)$ give
\[AB=\sqrt{2^2+3^2}=\sqrt{13},\qquad AC=\sqrt{7^2+5^2}=\sqrt{74},\qquad BC=\sqrt{9^2+2^2}=\sqrt{85}.\]
The triangle sits in the $9\times5$ rectangle with corners $(0,0)$ and $(9,5)$, and the rest of the rectangle consists of three right triangles with legs $2,3$; $9,2$; and $7,5$. Hence
\[[ABC]=45-\dfrac{2\cdot3}{2}-\dfrac{9\cdot2}{2}-\dfrac{7\cdot5}{2}=45-3-9-\dfrac{35}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{31}{2}}.\]
(Check with Heron's formula in the form $16K^2=2\left(a^2b^2+b^2c^2+c^2a^2\right)-a^4-b^4-c^4$ with $a^2,b^2,c^2=13,74,85$: $16K^2=2\cdot8357-12870=3844=62^2$, so $K=\dfrac{62}{4}=\dfrac{31}{2}$.)
