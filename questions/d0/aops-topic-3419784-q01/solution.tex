For $N=1434$, $E(L)=\dfrac{\sum_{j=1}^N j^2}{\sum_{j=1}^N j}=\dfrac{2N+1}{3}=\dfrac{2869}{3}$. In one move the expected increase in the coordinate sum is $(\dfrac{2}{3}-\dfrac{1}{3})E(L)=\dfrac{2869}{9}$. Linearity of expectation gives $a+b=2024\cdot\dfrac{2869}{9}=\boxed{\dfrac{5806856}{9}}$.
