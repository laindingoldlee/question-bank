Let the divisor be $d$. Then $d$ divides $19250-11=19239$ and $20302-3=20299$, and $d>11$ (a remainder is smaller than the divisor). The common divisors of $19239$ and $20299$ divide their difference $1060=2^2\cdot5\cdot53$. Since $19239$ is odd and does not end in $0$ or $5$, the common divisors can only be $1$ and $53$; indeed $19239=53\cdot363$ and $20299=53\cdot383$. So $d=\boxed{53}$. Check: $19250=53\cdot363+11$ and $20302=53\cdot383+3$.
