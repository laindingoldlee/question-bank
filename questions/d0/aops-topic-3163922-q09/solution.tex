Pair corresponding odd and even terms to obtain $\sum_{k=1}^{1005}(-1)^{k+1}((2k-1)^2-(2k)^2)=\sum_{k=1}^{1005}(-1)^{k+1}(1-4k)$. The alternating sum of ones is $1$, while $1-2+3-\cdots+1005=503$. Thus the result is $1-4(503)=\boxed{-2011}$.
