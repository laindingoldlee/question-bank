$a\,\&\,b=\dfrac{2a}b$, so $(a\,\&\,b)\,\&\,c=\dfrac{4a}{bc}$. Also $b\,\&\,c=\dfrac{2b}c$ and $a\,\&\,(b\,\&\,c)=\dfrac{2a}{\dfrac{2b}c}=\dfrac{ac}b$. The condition becomes $\dfrac{4a}{bc}-\dfrac{ac}{b}=a$, and dividing by $a\ne0$ and multiplying by $bc$: $4-c^2=bc$. So $b=\dfrac{4-c^2}{c}$ must be a nonzero integer: $c\mid4$ and $c\ne\pm2$. The positive values are $c=1$ ($b=3$) and $c=4$ ($b=-3$), with sum $\boxed{5}$.
