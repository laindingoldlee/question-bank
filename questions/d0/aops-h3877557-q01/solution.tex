The diagonal has length $2$. In coordinates along it, the other two vertices have axial positions $\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{3}{2}$ and perpendicular distances $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$. Each half-rectangle triangle sweeps a double cone of total height $2$ and base radius $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$, hence volume $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. Their intersection is another double cone: the two radial profiles meet at the diagonal midpoint with radius $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3}$, and its total height is $2$. Its volume is $\dfrac{2\pi}{9}$. Inclusion--exclusion gives swept volume $\pi-\dfrac{2\pi}{9}=\dfrac{7\pi}{9}$, so $\boxed{a+b=16}$.
