Encode each row by a three-bit mask with no adjacent 1s: $000,001,010,100,101$. Consecutive row masks are compatible exactly when their bitwise intersection is zero. Start with count one for each first-row mask, and repeatedly replace $f(v)$ by $\sum_{u\cap v=\varnothing}f(u)$. The second-row counts in the displayed order are $(5,3,4,3,2)$ and the third-row counts are $(17,12,13,12,9)$. Their sum is $\boxed{63}$.
