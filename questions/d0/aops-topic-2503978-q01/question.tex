There are $100$ lockers in a room, numbered $1$ to $100$, all closed. One hundred people enter the room one at a time. For $n=1,2,\dots,100$, the $n$th person changes the state of every $n$th locker, that is, of lockers $n,2n,3n,\dots$ (opening it if it is closed and closing it if it is open). Which lockers are open after everyone has passed through?
