Write $N=2^a5^bm$ with $\gcd(m,10)=1$. Then $\dfrac{(a+2)(b+2)}{(a+1)(b+1)}=\dfrac{21}{16}$ and $\dfrac{(a+3)(b+3)}{(a+1)(b+1)}=\dfrac53$. Trying small cases, $(a,b)=(5,7)$ works: $\dfrac{7\cdot9}{6\cdot8}=\dfrac{21}{16}$ and $\dfrac{8\cdot10}{48}=\dfrac53$. By symmetry, $(7,5)$ also works, and no other pairs do (the second equation bounds $a,b$ and a check of the finitely many cases confirms this). The number of trailing zeros is $\min(a,b)=\boxed{5}$.
