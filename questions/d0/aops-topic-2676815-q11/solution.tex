The areas are $\pi r^2$, and $1+4+9+16+25+36+49=140$. The difference is $\pi|2R-140|$, where $R$ is the sum of the squares of the red radii, so it is $0$ if some radii have squares summing to $70$. Indeed $49+16+4+1=70$ (red radii $1,2,4,7$, blue radii $3,5,6$). The minimum difference is $\boxed{0}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real[] rr={1,2,3,4,5,6,7};bool[] red={true,true,false,true,false,false,true};
real xx=0;
for(int i=0;i<7;++i){xx+=rr[i];pair c=(xx,0);path g=circle(c,rr[i]);if(red[i])filldraw(g,lightgray);else draw(g);label("$"+string(rr[i])+"$",c-(0,rr[i]),S,fontsize(8pt));xx+=rr[i]+0.6;}
\end{asy}
