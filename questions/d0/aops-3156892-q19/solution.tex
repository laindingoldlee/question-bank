An inscribed quadrilateral has area at most half the product of its two diagonals, each at most 2; thus at most 2, attained by a square. A circumscribed quadrilateral has area equal to its semiperimeter, since inradius is 1. If successive tangent half-angles are $\theta_i>0$, their sum is $\pi$ and the semiperimeter is $\sum\tan\theta_i\ge4\tan(\dfrac{\pi}{4})=4$ by convexity of tangent (or repeated tangent AM-GM). Equality is the circumscribed square. The minimum gap is $\boxed{4-2=2}$.
