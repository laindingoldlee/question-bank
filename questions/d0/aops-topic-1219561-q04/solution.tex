$0.\overline{abc}=\dfrac{n}{999}$ with $1\le n\le999$, and $999=3^3\cdot37$. In lowest terms this is $\dfrac {m}{d}$ with $d\mid999$, $d>1$ (or $\dfrac{1}{1}$ for $n=999$), $1\le m<d$ and $\gcd(m,d)=1$; conversely every such fraction occurs (take $n=m\cdot\dfrac{999}{d}$). So we count the integers $m$ that are coprime to some divisor $d>m$ of $999$ (plus $m=1$, already counted).
\begin{itemize}
\item $m$ coprime to $999$, $m<999$: $\varphi(999)=999\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot\dfrac{36}{37}=648$ values.
\item $m$ divisible by $3$ (not by $37$): we need $d$ not divisible by $3$, so $d=37$ and $m<37$: the $12$ multiples of $3$ from $3$ to $36$.
\item $m$ divisible by $37$: we need $d\in\{3,9,27\}$, but then $m<27$: impossible.
\end{itemize}
Total $648+12=\boxed{660}$.
