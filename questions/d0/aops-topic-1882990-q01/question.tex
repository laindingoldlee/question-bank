Ella has a cardboard box in the shape of a cube with edges $3$ inches long. She wants to put as many congruent cylinders as possible into the box, all standing upright on one of their circular faces. Each cylinder has height $3$ inches and volume $6.75\pi$ cubic inches. How many cylinders can she put in the box?
