Following the boundary once makes a full turn. Thus the exterior angles sum to $\boxed{360^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
for(int k=0;k<6;++k){pair P=2*dir(60*k),Q=2*dir(60*(k+1));draw(P--Q);draw(Q--(Q+.4*(Q-P)),dashed);}label("Schematic polygon",(0,0));
\end{asy}
