Let arrival times $x,y$ be minutes after 1:00, uniform in $[0,90]^2$. They meet if $y\le x+20$ and $x\le y+10$. The excluded corner triangles have legs $70$ and $80$, so the favorable area is $90^2-\dfrac{1}{2}(70^2+80^2)=2450$. Thus the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{2450}{8100}=\dfrac{49}{162}}$.

\end{asy}
