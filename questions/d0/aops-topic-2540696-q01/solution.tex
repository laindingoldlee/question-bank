Note $3^{12}+4=27^4+4\cdot1^4$. The Sophie Germain identity
\[a^4+4b^4=\left(a^2+2b^2\right)^2-(2ab)^2=\left(a^2+2ab+2b^2\right)\left(a^2-2ab+2b^2\right)\]
with $a=27$, $b=1$ gives
\[3^{12}+4=(729+54+2)(729-54+2)=785\cdot677.\]
Here $785=5\cdot157$, and $157$ and $677$ are prime: neither is divisible by any prime up to $\sqrt{677}<27$ (that is, $2,3,5,7,11,13,17,19,23$). So $3^{12}+4=5\cdot157\cdot677$, and the greatest prime factor is $\boxed{677}$.
