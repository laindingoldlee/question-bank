Since $20=4\cdot5$ and $(ab)^n=a^nb^n$, we get $20^{20}=(4\cdot5)^{20}=\boxed{4^{20}\cdot5^{20}}$. The other expression is wrong: $4^{40}\cdot5^{20}=4^{20}\cdot\left(4^{20}\cdot5^{20}\right)=4^{20}\cdot20^{20}$, which is far larger. (The correct statement that may have been intended is $20^{20}=2^{40}\cdot5^{20}$, since $4^{20}=\left(2^2\right)^{20}=2^{40}$.)
