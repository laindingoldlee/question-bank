The base diagonal has squared length $L^2+W^2$ by the Pythagorean theorem. That diagonal is perpendicular to a vertical edge of length $H$. Applying the theorem again gives space-diagonal squared length $L^2+W^2+H^2$. Taking the positive square root proves the formula.

\end{asy}
