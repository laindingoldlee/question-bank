Let $O$ be the common centre. The segment contains a point $P$ of the smallest circle and a point $Q$ of the largest circle, so its length is at least
\[
PQ\ge OQ-OP=8-2=6
\]
by the triangle inequality.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0);
draw(circle(O,2));draw(circle(O,4));draw(circle(O,8));
pair P=2*dir(30),Q=8*dir(30);
draw(P--Q,blue+linewidth(1.4));draw(O--P,dashed);
dot(O);dot(P);dot(Q);dot(4*dir(30));
label("$O$",O,SW);label("$P$",P,SE);label("$Q$",Q,NE);
label("$2$",dir(30),1.5*dir(120),fontsize(9pt));
label("$4$",4*dir(-90),N,fontsize(9pt));label("$8$",8*dir(-90),N,fontsize(9pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

A radial segment from a point $P$ on the inner circle to the point $Q$ on the outer circle on the same ray has length exactly $6$, and it crosses the middle circle at the point at distance $4$ from $O$. So the minimum length is $\boxed{6}$ feet.
