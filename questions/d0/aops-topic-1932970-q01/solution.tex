Let $u=x-1$ and $t=2x+4$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $u>0$: then $u^t=1$ exactly when $u=1$ or $t=0$. $u=1$ gives $x=2$ (and $1^8=1$). $t=0$ gives $x=-2$, but then $u=-3<0$, so it is not in this case.
\item $u=0$, i.e.\ $x=1$: $0^6=0\ne1$.
\item $u<0$: the exponent $t$ must be an integer, and $u^t=\pm|u|^t$, so we need $|u|^t=1$ with a positive sign. Either $|u|=1$, i.e.\ $u=-1$ and $x=0$, where $(-1)^4=1$ works; or $t=0$, i.e.\ $x=-2$, where $(-3)^0=1$ works.
\end{itemize}
So the solutions are $\boxed{x=-2,\ 0,\ 2}$.
