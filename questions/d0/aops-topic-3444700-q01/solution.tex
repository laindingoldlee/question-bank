The sum telescopes to $\dfrac{1}{2}(\dfrac{1}{3}-\dfrac{1}{11})=\dfrac{4}{33}$. Hence $\left(\dfrac{1}{M}-\dfrac{1}{32}\right)\dfrac{4}{33}=\dfrac{3}{2024}$ and $\dfrac{1}{M}=\dfrac{1}{32}+\dfrac{99}{8096}=\dfrac{352}{8096}=\dfrac{1}{23}$. Thus $\boxed{M=23}$, which makes every divisor nonzero.
