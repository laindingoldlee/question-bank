The cosine rule gives $AB^2=3^2+4^2-2(3)(4)\cos60^\circ=13$, so $\boxed{AB=\sqrt{13}}$. The sine rule gives $\dfrac{\sin A}{3}=\dfrac{\sin60^\circ}{\sqrt{13}}$, hence $\boxed{\sin A=\dfrac{3\sqrt{39}}{26}}$.
