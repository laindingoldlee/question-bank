Marco writes the polynomials
\[f(x)=nx^4+2x^3+3x^2+4x+5\quad\text{and}\quad g(x)=a(x-1)^4+b(x-1)^3+6(x-1)^2+d(x-1)+e,\]
where $n,a,b,d,e$ are real numbers. He notices that $g(i)=f(i)-|i|$ for each integer $i$ with $-5\le i\le-1$. Then $n^2=\dfrac pq$ for relatively prime positive integers $p,q$. Find $p+q$.
