For $-5\le i\le-1$ we have $|i|=-i$, so $g(i)=f(i)+i$. The polynomial $g(x)-f(x)-x$ has degree at most $4$ and $5$ roots, so it is identically zero: $g(x)=f(x)+x$.

Substitute $x=t+1$. The coefficient of $t^2$ in $g$ is $6$. In
\[f(t+1)+t+1=n(t+1)^4+2(t+1)^3+3(t+1)^2+5(t+1)+5\]
it is $6n+2\cdot3+3=6n+9$. So $6n+9=6$, $n=-\dfrac12$, $n^2=\dfrac14$, and $p+q=\boxed{5}$.
