Let the yacht be at $Y$, let $O$ be the point of the shore closest to it ($YO=15$), let the hospital be at $H$ with $OH=60$, and let the boat land at the point $L$ that is $x$ miles east of $O$; let $\theta$ be the angle between the boat's course and due south.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(9cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=0.12;
pair Y=(0,15)*s, O=(0,0), L=(30/sqrt(77),0)*s, H=(60,0)*s;
draw((-4,0)*s--(64,0)*s,gray(0.5)+linewidth(2));
label("shore",(64,0)*s,E,gray(0.4)+fontsize(8pt));
draw(Y--O,dotted);
draw(Y--L,blue+linewidth(1.1),Arrow(6));
draw(L--H,red+linewidth(1.1),Arrow(6));
real th=aTan((30/sqrt(77))/15);
draw(arc(Y,0.8,-90,-90+th),black);
label("$\theta$",Y+1.12*dir(-90+th/2),fontsize(8pt));
label("$15$",(Y+O)/2,W,fontsize(9pt));
label("$x$",(O+L)/2,S,fontsize(8pt));
label("$60-x$",(L+H)/2,S,fontsize(9pt));
label("boat, $20$ mph",(Y+L)/2+(0.1,0.2),E,blue+fontsize(8pt));
label("ambulance, $90$ mph",(L+H)/2,N,red+fontsize(8pt));
dot(Y); dot(O); dot(L); dot(H);
label("$Y$",Y,N); label("$O$",O,SW); label("$L$",L,NE); label("$H$",H,N);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The total time in hours is
\[T=\dfrac{\sqrt{225+x^2}}{20}+\dfrac{|60-x|}{90}\ \ge\ \dfrac{\sqrt{225+x^2}}{20}+\dfrac{60-x}{90}=\dfrac{9\sqrt{225+x^2}-2x+120}{180}.\]
For every real $x$,
\[81\left(225+x^2\right)-\left(15\sqrt{77}+2x\right)^2=77x^2-60\sqrt{77}\,x+900=\left(\sqrt{77}\,x-30\right)^2\ge0,\]
so $9\sqrt{225+x^2}\ge15\sqrt{77}+2x$, with equality exactly when $x=\dfrac{30}{\sqrt{77}}\approx3.42$ (which is between $0$ and $60$). Therefore
\[T\ge\dfrac{15\sqrt{77}+120}{180}\approx1.40\text{ hours},\]
with equality exactly when the boat lands at $x=\dfrac{30}{\sqrt{77}}$. Then
\[YL=\sqrt{225+\dfrac{900}{77}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{18225}{77}}=\dfrac{135}{\sqrt{77}},\qquad \sin\theta=\dfrac{OL}{YL}=\dfrac{30}{135}=\dfrac{2}{9} .\]
So the boat should head $\boxed{\theta=\arcsin\dfrac{2}{9}\approx12.8^\circ\text{ east of due south}}$ (a bearing of about $167.2^\circ$); the trip then takes about $1.40$ hours. Note that $\sin\theta=\dfrac{20}{90}$ is the ratio of the two speeds.
