Alden, a passenger on a yacht moored $15$ miles due north of a straight east-west coastline, has become ill and must be taken ashore in a small motorboat, which will meet an ambulance at some point on the shore. The ambulance will then drive along the coast to the hospital, which is $60$ miles east of the point of the shore closest to the yacht. The motorboat travels at $20$ mph and the ambulance at $90$ mph. In what direction should the motorboat head to minimise the total travel time to the hospital? Give the angle between the boat's course and due south. (Solve the problem without calculus.)
