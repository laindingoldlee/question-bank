Removing water does not change the amount of solids. The paste is $45\%$ solids, so it contains $0.45\cdot220=99$ grams of solids. Tomatoes are $6\%$ solids, so the mass of tomatoes was
\[
\dfrac{99}{0.06}=1650\text{ g}=\boxed{1.65\text{ kg}} .
\]
