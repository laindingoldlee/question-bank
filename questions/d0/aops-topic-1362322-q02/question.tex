Tomatoes contain $94\%$ water. Tomato paste contains $55\%$ water and is made simply by removing some of the water from tomatoes. If a can contains $220$ grams of tomato paste, how many kilograms of tomatoes were used to make it? Give the answer as a decimal to two decimal places.
