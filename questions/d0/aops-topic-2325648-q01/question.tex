Circles are stacked inside the parabola $y=x^2$, all with their centres on the $y$-axis. The first circle is tangent to the parabola (at two points), and its lowest point is $2$ units above the vertex $(0,0)$ of the parabola. Each following circle lies above the previous one, is tangent to the parabola, and is externally tangent to the previous circle. Find the distance from the vertex of the parabola to the centre of the $243$rd circle (counting the first circle as the $1$st). Express it in the form $\dfrac ab+c\sqrt b$.
