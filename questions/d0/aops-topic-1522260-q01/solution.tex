Let $x$ be the number of participants who are both. Then there are $8x$ philosophers and $13x$ mathematicians. By inclusion--exclusion,
\[
8x+13x-x=20x=2020,\qquad x=101 .
\]
So there were $\boxed{1313}$ mathematicians, $\boxed{808}$ philosophers, and $\boxed{101}$ participants were both.
