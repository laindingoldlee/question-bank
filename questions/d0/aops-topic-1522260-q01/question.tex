At a conference there were $2020$ participants, each a philosopher or a mathematician (or both). Exactly one eighth of the philosophers are also mathematicians, and exactly one thirteenth of the mathematicians are also philosophers. How many mathematicians were there, how many philosophers, and how many participants were both?
