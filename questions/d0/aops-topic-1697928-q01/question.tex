Kayla starts with a pile of $15$ stones. She divides the pile into two new piles and finds the product of the numbers of stones in the two new piles. She then repeatedly divides one of the existing piles into two new piles, each time adding the product of the sizes of the two new piles to her running total, until she has $15$ piles of one stone each. What is the greatest possible ending total? Prove that in fact the total is always the same.
