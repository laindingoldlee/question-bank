The terms are the numbers lying between twin primes: $4$ (between $3,5$), $6$ ($5,7$), $12$ ($11,13$), $18$ ($17,19$), $30$ ($29,31$), $42$ ($41,43$), $60$ ($59,61$), $72$ ($71,73$), $102$ ($101,103$), $108$ ($107,109$). The next twin prime pairs are $(137,139)$, $(149,151)$ and $(179,181)$, so the next terms are $\boxed{138,\ 150,\ 180}$.
