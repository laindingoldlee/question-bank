A nondecreasing sequence is determined by how many times each value $1,2,\dots,10$ occurs. If $n_v$ is the number of terms equal to $v$, then $n_1+n_2+\dots+n_{10}=10$ with all $n_v\ge0$, and every such choice gives exactly one sequence.

By stars and bars ($10$ stars for the terms and $9$ bars separating the values), the number of solutions is
\[
\dbinom{10+9}{9}=\dbinom{19}{9}=\boxed{92378}.
\]
