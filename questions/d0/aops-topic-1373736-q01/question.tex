How many sequences $(a_1,a_2,\dots,a_{10})$ of $10$ integers from $1$ to $10$ inclusive (repetition allowed) satisfy $a_1\le a_2\le\dots\le a_{10}$? An example is $(1,2,3,3,3,4,5,7,8,9)$.
