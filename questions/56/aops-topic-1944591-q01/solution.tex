Solve for $y$: $y=2x+4$. This is slope--intercept form $y=mx+b$, so the slope is $m=2$ and the $y$-intercept is $b=4$, i.e. the line crosses the $y$-axis at $(0,4)$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
draw((-3.5,0)--(2,0),Arrows(TeXHead));draw((0,-1)--(0,7),Arrows(TeXHead));
draw((-2.5,-1)--(1.5,7),blue+linewidth(1));
draw((0,4)--(1,4)--(1,6),dashed);
dot((0,4));dot((-2,0));
label("$x$",(2,0),E);label("$y$",(0,7),N);
label("$(0,4)$",(0,4),W);label("$(-2,0)$",(-2,0),NW);
label("$1$",(0.5,4),S);label("$2$",(1,5),E);
label("$y=2x+4$",(1.4,6.6),E,blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Answer: slope $\boxed{2}$, $y$-intercept $\boxed{4}$. (Going $1$ to the right raises the line by $2$.)
