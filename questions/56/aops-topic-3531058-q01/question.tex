Three mutually externally tangent spheres of radius $6$ rest above a plane. A sphere of radius $10$ is externally tangent to all three and does not meet the plane. Another sphere of radius $r$ rests above the plane and is externally tangent to all three radius-$6$ spheres. Find the shortest distance between the radius-$r$ and radius-$10$ spheres.
