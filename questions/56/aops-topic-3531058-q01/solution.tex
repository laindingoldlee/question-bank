The three equal centers form an equilateral triangle of side $12$ at height $6$, with circumradius $4\sqrt3$. A point equidistant from these centers lies on their perpendicular central axis. The small sphere center is at height $r$, so $48+(r-6)^2=(r+6)^2$, giving $r=2$. If the large center has height $h$, then $48+(h-6)^2=16^2$, so $h=6\pm4\sqrt{13}$. The minus choice has distance from the plane less than $10$ and intersects it, so $h=6+4\sqrt{13}$. The two centers are $h-2=4+4\sqrt{13}$ apart. Subtracting radii $10+2$ gives the gap $\boxed{4\sqrt{13}-8}$. The first sketch shows the three base centers; the second is the vertical plane through their central axis and one base center.

usepackage("amsmath");

pair A=(0,0),B=(12,0),C=(6,6*sqrt(3)),O=(6,2*sqrt(3));draw(circle(A,6));draw(circle(B,6));draw(circle(C,6));draw(A--B--C--cycle,dashed);draw(A--O,dashed);dot(O);label("$O$",O,NE);label("$6$",A+(-3,0),S);label("$12$",(A+B)/2,S);label("centers viewed from above",(6,-8),S);
\end{asy}
