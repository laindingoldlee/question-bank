A negative product needs one or three negative factors.
\begin{itemize}
\item Three negatives: $(-5)(-7)(-8)=-280$.
\item One negative and both positives: the most negative is $(-8)\cdot6\cdot9=-432$.
\end{itemize}
So the smallest product is $\boxed{-432}$.
