A $4\times4$ block of calendar dates has the numbers $1$ through $4$ in the first row, $8$ through $11$ in the second, $15$ through $18$ in the third and $22$ through $25$ in the fourth. The order of the numbers in the second and the fourth rows is reversed. Then the numbers on each of the two diagonals are added. What is the positive difference between the two diagonal sums?
