Call the first vertices $P,Q$ and the third $T=(t,0)$. Since $PQ=\sqrt8<4$, neither $TP$ nor $TQ$ can equal $PQ$: their vertical distances alone are at least $4$ and $6$. Therefore $TP=TQ$, giving $(t-1)^2+16=(t-3)^2+36$. Thus $4t=28$, so $t=\boxed7$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair[] pts={(1,4),(3,6),(7,0)}; string[] names={"$P$","$Q$","$T$"}; path boundary=pts[0]; for(int j=1;j<pts.length;++j)boundary=boundary--pts[j];draw(boundary--cycle); for(int i=0;i<pts.length;++i) label(names[i],pts[i],dir(90+360*i/pts.length));
\end{asy}
