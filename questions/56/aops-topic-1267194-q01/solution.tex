We need $1337^{42}\bmod 100$, and $1337\equiv 37\pmod{100}$. Compute powers of $37$ modulo $100$:
\[37^2=1369\equiv 69,\quad 37^4\equiv 69^2=4761\equiv61,\quad 37^8\equiv61^2=3721\equiv21,\]
\[37^{16}\equiv21^2=441\equiv41,\quad 37^{32}\equiv41^2=1681\equiv81.\]
Since $42=32+8+2$,
\[37^{42}\equiv 81\cdot21\cdot69\pmod{100}.\]
Now $81\cdot21=1701\equiv1$, so $37^{42}\equiv 69$. The last two digits are $\boxed{69}$.
