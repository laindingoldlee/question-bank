Put $ k=x+y $. Then $ k^2+18xy=19 $, so $ xy=\dfrac{19-k^2}{18}>0 $ forces $ k\in\{1,2,3,4\}$. Real roots require $ k^2-4xy=\dfrac{11k^2-38}{9}\ge0 $, ruling out 1 and admitting 2,3,4. Their products are $\dfrac{5}{6},\dfrac{5}{9},\dfrac{1}{6}$. Their sum is $\boxed{\dfrac{14}{9}}$.
