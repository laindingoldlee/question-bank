Three identical bags contain $2$ marbles each: bag A contains two blue marbles, bag B one blue and one red marble, and bag C two red marbles. A person chooses a bag at random and removes one marble at random. If the marble removed is blue, what is the probability that the other marble in that bag is also blue?
