(a) In the $100\times100$ square of pairs, failure occupies two right triangles each with legs 94. Their combined area is $94^2$, so success probability is $1-\dfrac{94^2}{10000}=\boxed{\dfrac{291}{2500}}$. (b) A fixed guess $Y$ covers the interval $[Y-6,Y+6]\cap[0,100]$, of length at most 12. Any $Y\in[6,94]$ achieves length 12, giving maximum probability $\boxed{\dfrac{3}{25}}$.
