Quadrilateral $GHIJ$ has $\overline{GH}\parallel\overline{IJ}$ and $GJ=HI$, and the diagonal $\overline{HJ}$ is drawn. A student claims that triangles $GHJ$ and $IJH$ must be congruent by SAS, because $\angle GHJ=\angle IJH$ (alternate angles), $HJ=JH$, and $GJ=IH$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What is wrong with the student's argument?
\item[(b)] Must triangles $GHJ$ and $IJH$ be congruent? Prove your answer.
\end{enumerate}
