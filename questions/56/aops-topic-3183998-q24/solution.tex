Write $s(n)$ for digit sum. Since $n+s(n)=2015$, we have $n<2015$ and at most four digits, so $s(n)\le36$ and $n\ge1979$. Also $s(n)\equiv n\pmod9$, giving $n\equiv4\pmod9$. The only candidates in that interval are $1984,1993,2002,2011$. Direct digit sums show that $1993$ and $2011$ work, while the others do not. Their sum is $\boxed{4004}$.
