Prove or disprove: for any $n+1$ points $(x_1,y_1),(x_2,y_2),\dots,(x_{n+1},y_{n+1})$ with distinct $x_i$, there is a unique polynomial of degree exactly $n$ whose graph passes through all of them. If the statement is false, state and prove a correct version.
