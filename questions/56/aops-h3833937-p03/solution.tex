Area is proportional to the square of radius. Multiplying the radius by $1.2$ multiplies the area by $1.2^2=1.44$, an increase of $\boxed{44\%}$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(220);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
draw(circle((0,0),1));draw(circle((3,0),1.2));draw((0,0)--(1,0));draw((3,0)--(4.2,0));label("$r$",(.5,0),(0,1));label("$1.2r$",(3.6,0),(0,1));
\end{asy}
