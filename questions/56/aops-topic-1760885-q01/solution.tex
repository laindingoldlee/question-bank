Let $\theta=\sin^{-1}\dfrac{2}{3}$, an acute angle with $\sin\theta=\dfrac{2}{3}$. In a right triangle with hypotenuse $3$ and side $2$ opposite $\theta$, the adjacent side is $\sqrt{9-4}=\sqrt5$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(sqrt(5),0),C=(sqrt(5),2);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw((B+(-0.15,0))--(B+(-0.15,0.15))--(B+(0,0.15)),linewidth(0.4));
draw(arc(A,0.5,0,degrees(C-A)),linewidth(0.6));
label("$\theta$",A+0.75*dir(degrees(C-A)/2));
label("$3$",(A+C)/2,NW);label("$2$",(B+C)/2,E);label("$\sqrt{5}$",(A+B)/2,S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

So $\tan\theta=\dfrac{2}{\sqrt5}$ and $\tan^2\theta=\boxed{\dfrac{4}{5}}$. (Equivalently, $\tan^2\theta=\dfrac{\sin^2\theta}{1-\sin^2\theta}=\dfrac{4}{9}\div\dfrac{5}{9}$.)
