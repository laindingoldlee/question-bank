I invite $10$ couples to a party at my house, so together with my wife and me there are $11$ couples. I ask everyone present except myself how many people they shook hands with, and everyone gives a different answer. Nobody shook hands with his or her own partner, and nobody shook hands with the same person twice. How many people did my wife shake hands with?
