From $\dbinom px=\dfrac{p!}{x!\,(p-x)!}$ we get
\[x!\,(p-x)!\dbinom px=p!.\]
The prime $p$ divides the right side. Every factor of $x!$ and of $(p-x)!$ is a positive integer less than $p$ (because $1\le x\le p-1$), so $p$ does not divide $x!\,(p-x)!$. Since $p$ is prime, it must divide $\dbinom px$. $\blacksquare$

(The restriction on $x$ is needed: $\dbinom p0=\dbinom pp=1$.)
