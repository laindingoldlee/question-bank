On Monday at noon, Jack buys a random number of apples, equally likely to be $1,2,3,4$ or $5$. On Friday of the same week at noon he independently does the same again. Each apple goes bad exactly $d$ days after it is bought (at noon), where $d$ is a random integer from $3$ to $9$ inclusive, all equally likely, independently for each apple. At midnight at the end of the following Monday, Jack has not eaten any of the apples. What is the expected number of apples that have gone bad by then?
