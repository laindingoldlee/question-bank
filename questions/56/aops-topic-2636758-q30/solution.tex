Midnight at the end of the next Monday is $7.5$ days after the first purchase and $3.5$ days after the second.
\begin{itemize}
\item A Monday apple has gone bad if $d\le7$: probability $\dfrac57$.
\item A Friday apple has gone bad if $d\le3$: probability $\dfrac17$.
\end{itemize}
Each purchase contains $3$ apples on average, and the spoilage of each apple is independent of the number bought. By linearity of expectation the answer is
\[3\cdot\dfrac57+3\cdot\dfrac17=\boxed{\dfrac{18}{7}}.\]
