Check small $n$ (with $n-1\ge1$): $n=2$: $\tau(1)=1\ne\tau(3)=2$. $n=3$: $\tau(2)=2\ne\tau(4)=3$. $n=4$: $\tau(3)=\tau(5)=2$. $n=5$: $\tau(4)=3\ne\tau(6)=4$. $n=6$: $\tau(5)=\tau(7)=2$. The answer is $4+6=\boxed{10}$.
