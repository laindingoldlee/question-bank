Reduce modulo $19$: $2015=19\cdot106+1$ and $101=19\cdot5+6$, so $6a\equiv1\pmod{19}$. Since $6\cdot16=96=5\cdot19+1$, we get $a\equiv16\pmod{19}$. As $101a<2015$ forces $a\le19$, $a=16$, and then $19b=2015-1616=399$, so $b=21$. Thus $a+b=\boxed{37}$.
