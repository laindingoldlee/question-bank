Suppose he has $k$ coins. One coin of each of $5$, $10$ and $20$ cents is required, and every other coin is worth at most $50$ cents, so the total is at most $5+10+20+50(k-3)=50k-115$ cents. We need $50k-115\ge395$, so $k\ge10.2$, that is $k\ge11$.

Eleven coins are possible: one $5$, two $10$s, one $20$ and seven $50$s give $5+20+20+350=395$ cents. So the smallest number is $\boxed{11}$.
