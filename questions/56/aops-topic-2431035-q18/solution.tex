Write the number as $100a+10b+c$ with $a=c+2$. Its reversal is $100c+10b+a$, and the difference is
\[(100a+10b+c)-(100c+10b+a)=99(a-c)=99\cdot2=198.\]
The units digit of the result is $\boxed{8}$.
