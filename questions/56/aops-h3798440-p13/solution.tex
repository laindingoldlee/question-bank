Let $D(i,j)$ count monotone paths needing i horizontal and j vertical moves, allowing diagonals. The recurrence is $D(i,j)=D(i-1,j)+D(i,j-1)+D(i-1,j-1)$, with boundary values one. It gives $D(2,2)=13$ and $D(4,4)=321$; for example the last two rows of the four-by-four index table are $(1,7,25,63,129)$ and $(1,9,41,129,321)$. Paths through the central cell number $13^2=169$, since their two halves are independent. Thus the avoiding count is $321-169=\boxed{152}$.
