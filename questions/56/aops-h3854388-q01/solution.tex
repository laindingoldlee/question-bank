The roots are $\dfrac{6\pm\sqrt 6}{15}$, both strictly between $0$ and $1$, so the series converge. Their sums are $\dfrac{p}{1-q}$ and $\dfrac{q}{1-p}$. Vieta gives $p+q=\dfrac{12}{15}$ and $pq=\dfrac{2}{15}$. Hence their product is $\dfrac{pq}{1-p-q+pq}=\dfrac{\dfrac{2}{15}}{1-\dfrac{12}{15}+\dfrac{2}{15}}=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{5}}$.
