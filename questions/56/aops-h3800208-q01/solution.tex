After $k$ students, group $g$ has $g\dfrac{k(k+1)}{2}=1265=5\cdot11\cdot23$. Thus the triangular number $\dfrac{k(k+1)}{2}$ must be a divisor of $1265$. Its divisors are $1,5,11,23,55,115,253,1265$; checking whether $8d+1$ is an odd square shows that only $1,55,253$ are triangular, with $k=1,10,22$. The corresponding group numbers are $1265,23,5$, all within the allowed range. Hence $\boxed{g=5,23,1265}$.
