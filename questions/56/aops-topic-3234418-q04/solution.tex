(a) The components are $5\cos30^\circ=\boxed{\dfrac{5\sqrt3}{2}}$ and $5\sin30^\circ=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{2}}$. (b) On a plane inclined at $30^\circ$, the steepest descent has vertical-to-horizontal ratio $\tan30^\circ=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}<1$; all other directions have an even smaller absolute ratio. Equal positive components would require a $45^\circ$ descent, so it is impossible.
