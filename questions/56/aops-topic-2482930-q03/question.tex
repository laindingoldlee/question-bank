A sphere of radius $42$ is painted red. Then $12$ non-overlapping blue circular patches are painted on its surface. Each patch consists of all points of the sphere whose straight-line distance from a fixed point of the sphere (the centre of the patch) is at most $2$. A point of the sphere's surface is chosen at random, uniformly with respect to area. The probability that it is blue is $\dfrac ab$ in lowest terms. Find $a+b$.
