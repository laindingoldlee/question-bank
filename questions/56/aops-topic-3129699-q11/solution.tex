At time $t$, the sums of coordinates are $t$ and $6-t$, so a meeting can occur only at $t=3$. At that time, if $A$ has taken $i$ right steps, $B$ must take $3-i$ left steps. Summing gives $\dfrac{1}{64}\sum_{i=0}^3\binom3i\binom3{3-i}=\dfrac{20}{64}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{16}}$. The original numerator-denominator sum is 21.
