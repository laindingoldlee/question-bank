$10^{40}=2^{40}5^{40}$ has $41^2=1681$ divisors. $20^{30}=2^{60}5^{30}$ has $61\cdot31=1891$. The common divisors are those of $\gcd=2^{40}5^{30}$: $41\cdot31=1271$. By inclusion-exclusion, $1681+1891-1271=\boxed{2301}$.
