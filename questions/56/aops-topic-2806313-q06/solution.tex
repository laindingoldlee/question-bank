Let the side be $s$. Using $AP$ as a base:
\[[ABP]+[APC]+[APD]=\dfrac12\cdot4\cdot(BB_1+CC_1+DD_1)=12.\]
But $[ABP]+[APC]=[ABC]=\dfrac{s^2}2$ (as $P$ is on $BC$) and $[APD]=\dfrac12\cdot AD\cdot AB=\dfrac{s^2}2$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(3.4641,0),C=(3.4641,3.4641),D=(0,3.4641);pair P=(3.4641,1.6);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--P);draw(P--D);
pair u=unit(P-A);pair f(pair Q){return dot(Q,u)*u;}draw(A--f(C),dotted);draw(B--f(B),dashed);draw(C--f(C),dashed);draw(D--f(D),dashed);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$P$",P,E);
\end{asy}

So $s^2=12$ and $[APD]=\boxed{6}$.
