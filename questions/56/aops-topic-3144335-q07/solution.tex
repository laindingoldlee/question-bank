Being coprime to ten means odd and not divisible by five. Of the twenty odd positive integers below forty, four ($ 5,15,25,35 $) are removed. Among the remaining sixteen, $ 7 $ and $ 21 $ are multiples of seven. Thus the answer is $\boxed{14}$.
