Constant second differences $16$ means $a_n$ is a quadratic in $n$ with leading coefficient $\dfrac{16}2=8$. Since $a_{63}=a_{89}=10$, $a_n=8(n-63)(n-89)+10$. Then $a_{51}=8(-12)(-38)+10=\boxed{3658}$.

(This illustrates the general fact: a sequence whose $p$th differences are constant and nonzero is a polynomial in $n$ of degree $p$.)
