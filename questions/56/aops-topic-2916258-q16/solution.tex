With ratio $r$: $a(r^2-1)=56$. $r=2$ needs $3\mid56$ (no), and $r=3$ gives $a=7$. For $r\ge4$, $r^2-1\in\{15,24,35,48,\dots\}$ and none of these below $56$ divides $56$, while $r^2-1>56$ is impossible. So $b=7\cdot3=\boxed{21}$.
