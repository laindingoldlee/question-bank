Place $Z=(0,0)$, $E=(3,0)$, $N=(3,3)$, $G=(0,3)$, so $Y=\left(\dfrac{3}{2},0\right)$, $I=\left(0,\dfrac{3}{2}\right)$, and the pentagon is the square with the corner triangle $ZYI$ cut off by the line $x+y=\dfrac{3}{2}$.

The inscribed circle of the square (centre $\left(\dfrac{3}{2},\dfrac{3}{2}\right)$, radius $\dfrac{3}{2}$) does not fit: its centre is only $\dfrac{3-\dfrac{3}{2}}{\sqrt2}=\dfrac{3\sqrt2}{4}<\dfrac{3}{2}$ from the cut. A largest circle must touch the cut $YI$ and the two far sides $EN$ ($x=3$) and $NG$ ($y=3$); by symmetry its centre is $O=(c,c)$ with radius $r=3-c$, and its distance to the line $x+y=\dfrac{3}{2}$ is
\[
\dfrac{2c-\dfrac{3}{2}}{\sqrt2}=r=3-c\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 2(3-r)-\dfrac{3}{2}=\sqrt2\,r\quad\Longrightarrow\quad r=\dfrac{\dfrac{9}{2}}{2+\sqrt2}=\dfrac{9(2-\sqrt2)}{4}.
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real r=(18-9*sqrt(2))/4,c=3-r;
pair Z=(0,0),Ep=(3,0),Nn=(3,3),G=(0,3),Y=(1.5,0),I=(0,1.5),O=(c,c),T=(0.75,0.75);
fill(Z--Y--I--cycle,mediumgray+opacity(0.4));
draw(G--I--Y--Ep--Nn--cycle,linewidth(0.9));draw(I--Z--Y,dashed);
draw(circle(O,r),blue+linewidth(0.8));dot(O);
draw(O--(3,c),dashed);draw(O--(c,3),dashed);draw(O--T,red+dashed);
label("$Z$",Z,SW);label("$E$",Ep,SE);label("$N$",Nn,NE);label("$G$",G,NW);label("$Y$",Y,S);label("$I$",I,W);label("$O$",O,NW);label("$T$",T,SW);
label("$r$",(O+(3,c))/2,N);label("$r$",(O+(c,3))/2,W);label("$r$",(O+T)/2,SE,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

This circle lies in the pentagon: its centre is $c=3-r\approx1.68$ from the sides $ZE$ and $ZG$, more than $r\approx1.32$, and it touches $YI$ at $T=\left(\dfrac{3}{4},\dfrac{3}{4}\right)$, which lies between $Y$ and $I$. No larger circle fits: a circle inside the pentagon lies in the half-planes $x\le3$, $y\le3$, $x+y\ge\dfrac{3}{2}$, which form a right isosceles triangle with legs $\dfrac{9}{2}$ whose incircle is exactly this circle. The radius is $\boxed{\dfrac{18-9\sqrt2}{4}}$.
