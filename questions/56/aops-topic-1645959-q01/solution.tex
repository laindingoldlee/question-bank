Keep the first term and group the rest in pairs:
\[
1^2+\sum_{k=1}^{1004}\left((2k+1)^2-(2k)^2\right)=1+\sum_{k=1}^{1004}(4k+1),
\]
because $(2k+1)^2-(2k)^2=(2k+1-2k)(2k+1+2k)=4k+1$ by the difference of squares. Hence the sum is
\[
1+4\cdot\dfrac{1004\cdot1005}{2}+1004=1+2018040+1004=\boxed{2019045}.
\]
Shortcut: $(2k+1)^2-(2k)^2=(2k)+(2k+1)$, so the whole expression equals $1+2+3+\cdots+2009=\dfrac{2009\cdot2010}{2}=2019045$.
