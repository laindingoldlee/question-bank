By Fermat's little theorem, the exponents may be reduced modulo $12$. Since $81\equiv9\pmod{12}$, $2^{81}\equiv2^9=512\equiv5\pmod{13}$. Also $3^3=27\equiv1\pmod{13}$, so $3^{81}\equiv1$. The remainder is $\boxed6$.
