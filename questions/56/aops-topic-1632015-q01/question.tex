Ojns is robbing some gold. He brings a $2.5$ kg briefcase whose inside measures $25$ cm by $75$ cm by $50$ cm. He can lift at most $75$ kg in total (briefcase included).
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] If he fills the whole case with gold, can he lift it?
\item[(b)] If not, what is the greatest number of gold bars, each measuring $3$ cm by $12.5$ cm by $5$ cm, that he can put in the case and still lift it?
\end{enumerate}
The density of gold is $19320$ kilograms per cubic metre.
