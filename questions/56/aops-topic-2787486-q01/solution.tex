Each summand equals $2+\dfrac{x-2020}{d}$. There are $1010$ summands, so the equation becomes $2020+(x-2020)\sum_{d=2}^{1011}\dfrac{1}{d}=2020$. The sum of reciprocals is positive; hence $\boxed{x=2020}$, which makes every original summand equal to $2$.
