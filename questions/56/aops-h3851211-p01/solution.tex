Use $75^\circ=45^\circ+30^\circ$. The addition formulas give $\sin 75^\circ=(\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2})(\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2})+(\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2})(\dfrac{1}{2})=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt 6+\sqrt 2}{4}}$ and $\cos 75^\circ=(\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2})(\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2})-(\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2})(\dfrac{1}{2})=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt 6-\sqrt 2}{4}}$.
