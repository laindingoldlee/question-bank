A sequence satisfies $a_n^2-a_{n-1}a_{n+1}=a_n-a_{n-1}$ for all $n\ge1$, with $a_0=1$ and $a_1=4$. Find the smallest positive integer $k$ such that $a_k$ is a multiple of $220$.
