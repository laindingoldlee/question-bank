A knight pair occupies opposite corners of a $2$ by $3$ rectangle of board squares or a $3$ by $2$ rectangle. There are $7\cdot6=42$ placements of each rectangle shape, and two attacking corner pairs in each. Thus there are $2(42)(2)=168$ unordered attacking pairs. Either square may hold the white knight, giving $\boxed{336}$.
