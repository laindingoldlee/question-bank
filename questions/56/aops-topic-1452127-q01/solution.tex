The hypotenuse is the longest side, so the other leg is $b$ and the hypotenuse is $b+1$. By Pythagoras,
\[
(b+1)^2-b^2=9^2\iff 2b+1=81\iff b=40 .
\]
The sides are $9$, $40$, $41$, and the perimeter is $9+40+41=\boxed{90}$ metres.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(2.6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair C=(0,0),A=(9,0),B=(0,40);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw((0,2)--(2,2)--(2,0));
label("$9$",(A+C)/2,S); label("$b$",(B+C)/2,W); label("$b+1$",(A+B)/2,NE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
