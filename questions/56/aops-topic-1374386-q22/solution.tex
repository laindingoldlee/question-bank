Order the integers $a\le b\le c\le d$. The median is $\dfrac{b+c}{2}$ and the mean is $\dfrac{a+b+c+d}{4}$, so
\[
\text{mean}-\text{median}=\dfrac{(a-b)+(d-c)}{4}.
\]
Here $a-b\le0$ and $d-c\le10-1=9$, so the difference is at most $\dfrac{9}{4}$, attained by $1,1,1,10$ (mean $\dfrac{13}{4}$, median $1$). Symmetrically, $\text{median}-\text{mean}=\dfrac{(b-a)+(c-d)}{4}\le\dfrac{9}{4}$, attained by $1,10,10,10$. Either way the greatest possible difference is $\boxed{\dfrac{9}{4}}$.
