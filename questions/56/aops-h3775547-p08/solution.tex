The values at $0,\dfrac{3}{2},\dfrac{5}{2},4$ are respectively $-6,\dfrac{3}{8},-\dfrac{3}{8},6$. Continuity gives a root in each of the three disjoint intervals between these points. A cubic has at most three roots, so it has exactly three distinct real roots. By Vieta, their sum is $6$ and the sum of pairwise products is $11$. Their squared sum is $6^2-2\cdot11=\boxed{14}$.
