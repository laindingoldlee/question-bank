On Mars, each martian independently is a liar with probability \(\dfrac{1}{5}\) (a liar always gives the wrong answer; everyone else answers truthfully). On a given day it rains with probability \(30\%\). Sally asks five martians whether it is raining, and exactly three of them say that it is. What is the probability that it is actually raining?
