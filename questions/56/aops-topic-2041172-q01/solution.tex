If it is raining, a martian says ``raining'' with probability \(\dfrac{4}{5}\); if it is not raining, with probability \(\dfrac{1}{5}\). The answers are independent given the weather, so
\[
P(\text{3 say yes}\mid\text{rain})=\binom53\left(\dfrac{4}{5}\right)^3\left(\dfrac{1}{5}\right)^2,\qquad
P(\text{3 say yes}\mid\text{no rain})=\binom53\left(\dfrac{1}{5}\right)^3\left(\dfrac{4}{5}\right)^2 .
\]
By Bayes' theorem (the common factor \(\binom53\cdot\dfrac{1}{5^5}\) cancels),
\[
P(\text{rain}\mid\text{3 yes})=\dfrac{0.3\cdot 4^3}{0.3\cdot4^3+0.7\cdot 4^2}=\dfrac{19.2}{19.2+11.2}=\dfrac{19.2}{30.4}=\boxed{\dfrac{12}{19}}.
\]
