Multiply by $a-b\ne0$: $a+b=(a^2-b^2)(a-b)=(a+b)(a-b)^2$, so
\[
(a+b)\left((a-b)^2-1\right)=0 .
\]
Hence either $a+b=0$, or $a-b=\pm1$; and conversely each of these makes the equation true (the steps reverse since $a\ne b$).

Now let $s$ be any real number and take $a=\dfrac{s+1}{2}$, $b=\dfrac{s-1}{2}$. Then $a-b=1$ and $a+b=s$, and indeed
\[
\dfrac{a+b}{a-b}=s=(a+b)(a-b)=a^2-b^2 .
\]
So $a+b$ takes every real value; there is $\boxed{\text{no minimum}}$ (for example $a+b=-1000$ with $a=-499.5$, $b=-500.5$).
