The $10$ meridians all pass through both poles and divide the sphere into $10$ ``orange slices'' (lunes), each running from pole to pole.

Each parallel is a circle around the axis, and it crosses every lune once, cutting it across. The $10$ parallels are at $10$ different latitudes, so they cut each lune into $11$ pieces (as $10$ cuts divide a strip into $11$ parts).

Hence the number of regions is $10\cdot11=\boxed{110}$. (If the $10$ ``meridians'' were full great circles through both poles, there would be $20$ lunes and $220$ regions.)
