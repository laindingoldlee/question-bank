\textbf{Mike's square.} The side is $\dfrac{100}{4}=25$ feet, so the area is $625$ square feet.

\textbf{Milo's circle.} The circumference is $2\pi r=100$, so $r=\dfrac{50}{\pi}$ and the area is
\[
\pi r^2=\pi\cdot\dfrac{2500}{\pi^2}=\dfrac{2500}{\pi}\approx795.8\text{ square feet}.
\]

\textbf{Comparison.} $\dfrac{2500}{\pi}>625$ because $\pi<4$, so Milo's circular pen is larger, and
\[
\dfrac{\dfrac{2500}{\pi}}{625}=\dfrac{4}{\pi}\approx1.27 .
\]
So $\boxed{\text{Milo's pen is larger: }\dfrac{2500}{\pi}\text{ ft}^2\text{ versus }625\text{ ft}^2,\ \dfrac{4}{\pi}\text{ times as large}}$.
