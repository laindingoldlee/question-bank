Solving for the inverse gives $f^{-1}(x)=\dfrac{4}{x-3}$ for $x\ne3$. At an intersection, $3+\dfrac{4}{x}=\dfrac{4}{x-3}$. Multiplication by $x(x-3)$ gives $3x^2-9x-12=0$, or $(x-4)(x+1)=0$. Both permitted values give $f(x)=x$. The intersections are $\boxed{(4,4),(-1,-1)}$.
