If $\sqrt5=\dfrac{a}{b}$ in lowest terms, then $a^2=5b^2$, forcing $5\mid a$. Writing $a=5c$ gives $b^2=5c^2$, so $5\mid b$, contradicting lowest terms. Hence $\sqrt5$ is irrational (and is a real number). If the two lengths were integer multiples of a common unit, their ratio would be rational, contradicting this result. Thus they are incommensurable.
