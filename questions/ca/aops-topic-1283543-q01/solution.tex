There are $\dfrac{6!}{2!}=360$ arrangements in fixed positions. A nontrivial rotation cannot fix any of them, since the green ball would have to stay in place. So every arrangement has $6$ distinct rotations, and there are $\dfrac{360}{6}=\boxed{60}$ circular arrangements.
