Diagonal $AC$ halves the parallelogram, so $[ABC]=30$. Let $[ABP]=F$; then $[BPC]=3F$. The three triangles around $P$ partition $ABC$, giving $F+3F+10=30$, hence $\boxed{F=5}$.

\end{asy}
