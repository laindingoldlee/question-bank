Evaluate each term: $(xy)^2=36$, $(x+y)^2=25$, $(x\cdot x)^2\cdot y^2\div x^2=16\cdot9\div4=36$ (multiplication and division from left to right), and $(y-x)^2=1$. The value is
\[36+25-36+1=\boxed{26}.\]
