Since $AB=AC$, the base angles $B,C$ are equal; with $B=60^\circ$, all angles are $60^\circ$. The triangle is equilateral, so the altitude from $C$ bisects $AB$. Thus $AB=2BX=8$, and the area is $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}8^2=\boxed{16\sqrt3}$.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

pair A=(0,0),B=(8,0),C=(4,4*sqrt(3)),X=(4,0);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(C--X,dashed);label("A",A,SW);label("B",B,SE);label("C",C,N);label("X",X,S);label("4",(B+X)/2,S);
\end{asy}
