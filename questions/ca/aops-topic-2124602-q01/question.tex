\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] A car travels at $40$ mph for one hour and then at $50$ mph for one hour. What is its average speed over the two hours?
\item[(b)] A car travels $100$ miles at $40$ mph and then $100$ miles at $50$ mph. What is its average speed for the whole trip?
\item[(c)] A trip consists of two parts travelled at constant speeds $a$ and $b$. The average speed is the total distance divided by the total time. Prove that it equals $\dfrac{a+b}{2}$ when the two parts take equal times, and $\dfrac{2ab}{a+b}$ when the two parts have equal lengths.
\end{enumerate}
