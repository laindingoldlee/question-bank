A hotel has $100$ rooms, numbered $1$ to $100$, and every room has a blue door. First, all $100$ doors are painted red. Then, for $k=2,3,\dots,100$ in turn, the colour of every $k$th door (doors $k,2k,3k,\dots$) is changed: a red door is repainted blue and a blue door is repainted red. (So for $k=2$ every even-numbered door, which is red at that time, is painted blue.)
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Which doors are red at the end?
\item[(b)] Which doors have had their colour changed exactly twice (counting the first painting from blue to red)?
\item[(c)] Which door or doors were painted the greatest number of times?
\end{enumerate}
