The bleach amounts are $0.04\cdot1=0.04$ gallon and $0.10\cdot2=0.2$ gallon, a total of $0.24$ gallon in $3$ gallons. So the mixture is $\dfrac{0.24}{3}=0.08=\boxed{8\%}$ bleach.
