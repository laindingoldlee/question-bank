Completing the square gives $(x-3)^2=4$, so $x=1,5$. The quadratic formula gives $x=\dfrac{6\pm\sqrt{36-20}}{2}=\dfrac{6\pm4}{2}$, again $\boxed{1,5}$. The formula itself is obtained by completing the square in a general quadratic.
