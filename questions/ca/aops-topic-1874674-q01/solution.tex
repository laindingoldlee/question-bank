A divisor of $120$ that is divisible by $4$ has the form $4m$, and $4m\mid120$ exactly when $m\mid30$. Since $30=2\cdot3\cdot5$ has $2\cdot2\cdot2=8$ divisors, the answer is $\boxed{8}$: namely $4,8,12,20,24,40,60,120$.
