\textbf{(a)} $\boxed{6\times6+6\div6+6\div6=38}$, since $36+1+1=38$. (Without brackets, the only solutions are the three orderings of the terms $6\times6$, $6\div6$, $6\div6$.)

\textbf{(b)} No choice of the five operations without brackets gives $-6$, so brackets are needed. For example,
\[\boxed{(5+5\times5)\div(5-5-5)=-6},\]
since $5+25=30$, $5-5-5=-5$ and $30\div(-5)=-6$.
