Write the numbers as $10p+q$ and $10r+s$. The product is
\[
100pr+10(ps+qr)+qs .
\]
If the tens digits are $p$ and $r$, the numbers are less than $10(p+1)$ and $10(r+1)$, so the product is less than $100(p+1)(r+1)$. For any pair of tens digits other than $\{7,5\}$ we have $(p+1)(r+1)\le8\cdot4=32$, so the product is less than $3200$. With tens digits $7$ and $5$ and units digits $2$ and $3$:
\[
72\cdot53=3816,\qquad 73\cdot52=3796 .
\]
The larger product puts the larger units digit with the smaller tens digit: since the two numbers have a fixed sum $125$, the product is larger when they are closer together. The maximum is $\boxed{3816}$.

(General strategy: put the largest digits in the highest places, then keep the two numbers as close to each other as possible. All $24$ arrangements were checked to confirm.)
