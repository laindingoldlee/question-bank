A frog starts at 1 and makes strictly increasing jumps from $n$ either to $n+1$ or to a proper multiple of $n$. A path is counted by its visited integers, so the jump $1\to2$ is counted once. Let $f(n)$ count paths to $n$, with $f(1)=1$, and define $M = \sum_{n\ge1} \dfrac{f (n)}{2^n}$, $N = \sum_{n\ge1} \dfrac{f (n)}{2^n-1}$. Derive an affine relation $N=P(M)$ from the path recurrence, and compute $P(\sqrt{2025})$.
