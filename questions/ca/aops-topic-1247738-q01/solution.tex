Let the common length be $s$. Triangle $ABD$ is equilateral, so $\angle ADB=60^\circ$ and $\angle BDC=120^\circ$. Triangle $BDC$ has $BD=DC=s$ and apex angle $120^\circ$, so $BC=s\sqrt3=6$ and $s=2\sqrt3$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=2*sqrt(3); pair A=(0,0),D=(s,0),C=(2s,0),B=s*dir(60);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(1)); draw(B--D,blue);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$D$",D,S);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$B$",B,N); label("$6$",(B+C)/2,NE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The area of $ABD$ is $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}s^2=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}\cdot12=3\sqrt3\approx5.2$, i.e. $\boxed{5}$ square centimetres.
