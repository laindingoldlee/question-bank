Take $A=(0,0)$, $B=(1,0)$, $C=(1,1)$, $D=(0,1)$, so $M=\left(\dfrac{1}{2},0\right)$ and $N=\left(0,\dfrac{1}{2}\right)$. For a point $P=(x,y)$:

\textbf{Closer to $N$ than to $M$.} $x^2+\left(y-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^2<\left(x-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^2+y^2$ simplifies to $-y<-x$, i.e.\ $y>x$ (the perpendicular bisector of $MN$ is the diagonal $y=x$).

\textbf{Closer to $N$ than to $C$.} $x^2+\left(y-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^2<(x-1)^2+(y-1)^2$ simplifies to $-y+\dfrac{1}{4}<-2x-2y+2$, i.e.\ $2x+y<\dfrac{7}{4}$.

The region is the quadrilateral with vertices $A=(0,0)$, the intersection $\left(\dfrac{7}{12},\dfrac{7}{12}\right)$ of the two lines, the point $\left(\dfrac{3}{8},1\right)$ where $2x+y=\dfrac{7}{4}$ meets the top side, and $D=(0,1)$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(1,0),C=(1,1),D=(0,1),M=(0.5,0),Nn=(0,0.5);
pair X=(7/12,7/12),Y=(3/8,1);
fill(A--X--Y--D--cycle,paleblue);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(A--X,red+dashed);draw(X--Y,deepgreen+dashed);
draw((7/12,7/12)--(1,1),red+dotted);draw(Y--(7/8,0),deepgreen+dotted);
dot(M);dot(Nn);dot(X);dot(Y);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);
label("$M$",M,S);label("$N$",Nn,W);
label("$\left(\dfrac{7}{12},\dfrac{7}{12}\right)$",X,E,fontsize(8pt));label("$\left(\dfrac{3}{8},1\right)$",Y,N,fontsize(8pt));
label("$y=x$",(0.8,0.8),SE,red+fontsize(8pt));label("$2x+y=\dfrac{7}{4}$",(0.74,0.27),W,deepgreen+fontsize(8pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

By the shoelace formula its area is
\[
\dfrac{1}{2}\left|\,0+\left(\dfrac{7}{12}\cdot1-\dfrac{3}{8}\cdot\dfrac{7}{12}\right)+\left(\dfrac{3}{8}\cdot1-0\right)+0\,\right|=\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{35}{96}+\dfrac{36}{96}\right)=\dfrac{71}{192}.
\]
The square has area $1$, so the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{71}{192}}\approx0.37$.
