Let $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ be the vertices of a square, in that order. Let $M$ be the midpoint of $AB$ and $N$ the midpoint of $AD$. A point is chosen uniformly at random inside the square. What is the probability that it is closer to $N$ than to $M$ and also closer to $N$ than to $C$?
