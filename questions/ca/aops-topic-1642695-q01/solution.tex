We look for two numbers with product $3\cdot(-15)=-45$ and sum $4$: they are $9$ and $-5$. Then
\[
3x^2+4x-15=3x^2+9x-5x-15=3x(x+3)-5(x+3)=\boxed{(3x-5)(x+3)}.
\]
Check: $(3x-5)(x+3)=3x^2+9x-5x-15=3x^2+4x-15$.
