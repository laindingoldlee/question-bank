For points $A,B,C$, put $D=|\det(B-A,C-A)|$, twice the triangle area, and let $u,v,w$ be its three squared side lengths. The extended sine rule gives $R^2=\dfrac{uvw}{4D^2}$. This allows an exact finite maximization over the $\binom{121}{3}=287980$ unordered triples: discard $D=0$, and compare two candidate fractions by cross multiplication. The maximum is $\dfrac{143338}{4}$, attained by $(0,0),(2,3),(7,10)$, whose squared side lengths are $13,149,74$ and whose determinant is $-1$. Thus $R=\dfrac{\sqrt{143338}}{2}\approx189.300$, closest to $\boxed{190}$.
