Positive integers $x$ and $z$ satisfy $x+z>10$ and $z<6$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the least possible value of $x$.
\item[(b)] A student argues: ``$z<6$ gives $-z>-6$; adding this to $x+z>10$ gives $x>4$, so the least value of $x$ is $5$.'' Explain what is wrong.
\end{enumerate}
