Solve these four examples of proof principles. (a) For every positive integer $n$, prove $1+\cdots+n=\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$ by induction. (b) Show that among 13 people, two share a birth month. (c) A nonempty finite set $S$ of positive integers contains $a-b$ whenever $a,b\in S$ and $a>b$. Show every element is a multiple of its least element. (d) Starting with 25 tokens, each move removes two. Can all tokens be removed?
