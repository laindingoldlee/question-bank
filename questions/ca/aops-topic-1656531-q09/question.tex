At some moment the minute hand and the hour hand of a clock form a $90^\circ$ angle. Let $Q$ be the number of minutes until they next form a $90^\circ$ angle. If $Q=\dfrac {m}{n}$ with $m$ and $n$ relatively prime positive integers, what is $m+n$?

(a) $60$\quad (b) $61$\quad (c) $371$\quad (d) $1445$\quad (e) $1859$
