The minute hand turns $6^\circ$ per minute and the hour hand $0.5^\circ$ per minute, so the angle of the minute hand relative to the hour hand grows by $5.5^\circ=\dfrac{11}{2}$ degrees per minute. The hands are at right angles when this relative angle is $90^\circ$ or $270^\circ$ (mod $360^\circ$); consecutive such moments are $180^\circ$ of relative turning apart. So
\[
Q=\dfrac{180}{\dfrac{11}{2}}=\dfrac{360}{11}\text{ minutes},
\]
and $m+n=360+11=\boxed{\textbf{(c)}\ 371}$.
