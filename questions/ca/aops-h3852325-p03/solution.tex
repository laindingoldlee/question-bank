The left side is $3\sin\theta+\dfrac{2\cos^2\theta}{\sin\theta}=\sin\theta+\dfrac{2}{\sin\theta}$, using $\cos^2\theta=1-\sin^2\theta$. Put $s=\sin x\ne0$. The equation becomes $s+\dfrac{2}{s}=4s-5$, or
\[
3s^2-5s-2=(3s+1)(s-2)=0.
\]
The value $s=2$ is impossible. Thus $s=-\dfrac{1}{3}$, giving
\[
x=\pi+\arcsin\dfrac{1}{3}\quad\hbox{or}\quad 2\pi-\arcsin\dfrac{1}{3}.
\]
Both have nonzero sine and cosine, so both satisfy the original equation. To three significant figures, $\boxed{x=3.48\text{ or }5.94}$ radians.
