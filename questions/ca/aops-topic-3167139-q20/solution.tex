Pascal's identity reduces the ratio to $\dfrac{\binom{n+1}{4}}{\binom{n+1}{2}}=\dfrac{(n-1)(n-2)}{12}$. Divisibility by $4$ requires $n\equiv1,2\pmod4$, and divisibility by $3$ requires $n\not\equiv0\pmod3$. Together these are $n\equiv1,2,5,10\pmod{12}$. There are forty such integers in $1$ through $120$, of which $1,2$ are outside the stated interval. Thus the answer is $\boxed{38}$.
