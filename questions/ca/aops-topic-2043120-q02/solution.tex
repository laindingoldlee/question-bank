Factor out \(x\): \(f(x)=x(x^3+15x-16)\). Since \(f(1)=0\) and the cubic factor vanishes at \(x=1\) (\(1+15-16=0\)), the factor theorem gives
\[
x^3+15x-16=(x-1)(x^2+x+16).
\]
The quadratic \(x^2+x+16\) has discriminant \(1-64=-63<0\), so it has no real roots. Hence the real roots of \(f\) are \(\boxed{x=0\text{ and }x=1}\).
