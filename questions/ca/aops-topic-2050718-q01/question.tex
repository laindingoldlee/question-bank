Alice, Bob, and Carol sit in a circle and play a card game in which the only cards are \emph{skips} and \emph{reverses}. Play passes around the circle in the current direction. A skip makes the next player in the current direction lose their turn, so play passes to the player after them; a reverse changes the direction of play, so play passes to the player who went just before (the player who played the reverse does not play again). Alice always plays a skip, Bob always plays a reverse, and Carol plays a skip or a reverse with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$ each, independently every time. Everyone has an unlimited supply of cards and the game goes on forever. In the long run, what fraction of all turns are Alice's?
