Place $B=(0,0),C=(14,0),A=(5,12)$. The circumcentre is $(7,\dfrac{33}{8})$ and radius $\dfrac{65}{8}$, so the specified arc midpoint is $D=(7,-4)$. The angle-bisector theorem gives $BE:EC=13:15$, hence $E=(\dfrac{13}{2},0)$. Consequently $DA=\sqrt{2^2+16^2}=2\sqrt{65}$ and $DE=\sqrt{(\dfrac{1}{2})^2+4^2}=\dfrac{\sqrt{65}}{2}$. Their product is $\boxed{65}$.
