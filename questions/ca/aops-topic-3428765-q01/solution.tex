Adding $45$ gives $(x+5)(y+9)=64$. Each nonzero integer divisor $d$ of $64$ determines exactly one pair $x=d-5$, $y=\dfrac{64}{d}-9$. There are seven positive divisors of $64=2^6$ and seven negative ones. None gives $x=0$, since $5$ is not a divisor of $64$, and none gives $y=0$, since $9$ is not a divisor of $64$. Thus all fourteen pairs are allowed, and the answer is $\boxed{14}$.
