$400=2^4\cdot5^2$, so $x=2^{a}5^{c}$ and $y=2^{b}5^{d}$ with $\max(a,b)=4$ and $\max(c,d)=2$. The pairs $(a,b)$ with $0\le a,b\le4$ and maximum $4$ number $5^2-4^2=9$, and the pairs $(c,d)$ with $0\le c,d\le2$ and maximum $2$ number $3^2-2^2=5$. The exponents of $2$ and $5$ can be chosen independently, so there are $9\cdot5=\boxed{45}$ ordered pairs.
