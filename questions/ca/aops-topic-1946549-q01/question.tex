On his way to school, Kobe sees Sebastian driving in the same direction $\dfrac{1}{2}$ mile ahead of him. After Kobe drives past Sebastian, he continues to see Sebastian in his rearview mirror until Sebastian is $\dfrac{1}{2}$ mile behind him. Kobe drives at a constant $32$ mph and Sebastian at a constant $26$ mph. For how many minutes in total does Kobe see Sebastian?
