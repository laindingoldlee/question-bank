Relative to Sebastian, Kobe moves forward at $32-26=6$ miles per hour. Kobe sees Sebastian while the gap changes from Sebastian $\dfrac{1}{2}$ mile ahead to Sebastian $\dfrac{1}{2}$ mile behind, a relative distance of $1$ mile. This takes
\[
\dfrac{1}{6}\text{ hour}=\boxed{10}\text{ minutes}.
\]
