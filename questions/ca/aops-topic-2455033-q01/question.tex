A quadratic polynomial $f(x)$ has $-1$ as one of its roots. Real numbers $a$ and $b$ satisfy $a+b\ne-2$ and $f(a)+f(b)=0$. Find the other root of $f(x)=0$ in terms of $a$ and $b$. What is it when $a=1$ and $b=2$?
