The block with peak $m\ge2$ has length $2(m-1)$. Through that block the total length is $1+m(m-1)$. The first term is $a_1=1$. For $n>1$, choose the unique integer $m\ge2$ with
\[
1+(m-2)(m-1)<n\le1+m(m-1),
\]
and set $t=n-1-(m-2)(m-1)$. Then
\[
a_n=\begin{cases}t+1,&1\le t\le m-1,\\2m-1-t,&m\le t\le2m-2.\end{cases}
\]
For $n=2025$, $m=46$ and $t=44$, so $a_{2025}=\boxed{45}$.
