The requirement is $2400(0.9)^n<800$, or $(0.9)^n<\dfrac{1}{3}$. Direct exact-power comparisons give $3\cdot9^{10}>10^{10}$ and $3\cdot9^{11}<10^{11}$. Since the powers of 0.9 strictly decrease, the first successful step is $\boxed{n=11}$.
