Let
\[
E=2+\sqrt{2+\sqrt{2+\sqrt{2-\sqrt{2-\sqrt{2+\sqrt{2+\sqrt{2+\sqrt{2-\sqrt{2-\cdots}}}}}}}}}\,,
\]
where the signs (starting with the one after the first $2$) repeat with period $+,+,+,-,-$. Express $E$ in the form $a\cos^2\dfrac{\pi}{c}$ with positive integers $a$ and $c$, and compute $a\cdot2\cdot c^2$.
