Five balls are put into three boxes (boxes may stay empty). How many ways are there to do this if
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] the balls are distinguishable and the boxes are distinguishable;
\item[(b)] the balls are identical and the boxes are distinguishable;
\item[(c)] the balls are distinguishable and the boxes are identical;
\item[(d)] the balls are identical and the boxes are identical?
\end{enumerate}
