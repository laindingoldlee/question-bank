Reel 1 pays with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, so (a) is $\boxed{1}$ dollar. Reel 2 adds expected payment $\dfrac{1}{2}\dfrac{3}{10}\cdot8+\dfrac{1}{2}\dfrac{3}{10}\cdot3=1.65$, so (b) is $\boxed{2.65}$. Both earlier reels pay with probability $\dfrac{3}{20}$; reel 3 adds $\dfrac{1}{2}(\dfrac{3}{20}\cdot30+\dfrac{17}{20}\cdot2)=3.10$, so (c) is $\boxed{5.75}$. No payment from the first three has probability $\dfrac{1}{2}\dfrac{7}{10}\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{7}{40}$. Reel 4 adds $\dfrac{7}{40}\dfrac{2}{10}\cdot80=2.80$, so (d) is $\boxed{8.55}$. For current payment $X$, reel 5 has conditional expected payment $\dfrac{5+1.2+1.5+1.8+2+4}{10}X+\dfrac{3+4+1+1+1}{10}=1.55X+1$. Thus (e) is $1.55(8.55)+1=\boxed{14.2525}$ dollars.
