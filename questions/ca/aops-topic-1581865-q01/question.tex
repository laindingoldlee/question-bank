In a town, $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of the adult men are married to $\dfrac{3}{5}$ of the adult women; each married person is married to exactly one adult of the other sex in the town, so the number of married men equals the number of married women. The adult population is over $100$. What is the smallest possible number of adult residents of the town?
