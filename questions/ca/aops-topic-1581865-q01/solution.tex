Let there be $m$ men and $w$ women. The married men and married women are equally many:
\[
\dfrac{2}{3}m=\dfrac{3}{5}w\qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad 10m=9w .
\]
Since $\gcd(9,10)=1$, $m=9k$ and $w=10k$ for a positive integer $k$, and then $\dfrac{2}{3}m=6k$ is a whole number, as needed. The adult population is $19k>100$, so $k\ge6$, and the smallest population is
\[
19\cdot6=\boxed{114}
\]
($54$ men and $60$ women, $36$ married couples).
