Since ray $PS$ lies inside $\angle QPR$, the angle splits as $\angle QPR=\angle QPS+\angle SPR$:
\[
7x-95=47+(3x-38)\iff 7x-95=3x+9\iff 4x=104\iff x=26 .
\]
Then $\angle RPS=3\cdot26-38=40^\circ$ and $\angle QPR=7\cdot26-95=\boxed{87^\circ}=47^\circ+40^\circ$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair P=(0,0), Q=4*dir(0), Sx=4*dir(47), R=4*dir(87);
draw(P--Q,linewidth(0.9)); draw(P--Sx,linewidth(0.9)); draw(P--R,linewidth(0.9));
draw(arc(P,1.2,0,47),blue); label("$47^\circ$",P+1.55*dir(23.5),blue+fontsize(8pt));
draw(arc(P,1.9,47,87),red); label("$40^\circ$",P+2.3*dir(67),red+fontsize(8pt));
dot(P); label("$P$",P,SW); label("$Q$",Q,E); label("$S$",Sx,NE); label("$R$",R,N);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
