Note $\sqrt{84}=2\sqrt{21}$. Squaring (both sides are positive, so this loses nothing),
\[10+2\sqrt{21}=a+b+2\sqrt{ab}.\]
We claim $a+b=10$ and $ab=21$. Otherwise $\sqrt{ab}=\sqrt{21}+k$ with $k=\dfrac{10-a-b}{2}$ a nonzero rational; squaring gives $ab=21+k^2+2k\sqrt{21}$, which would make $\sqrt{21}$ rational, a contradiction.

So $a$ and $b$ are the roots of $t^2-10t+21=(t-3)(t-7)=0$, and with $a<b$ the only pair is $\boxed{(3,7)}$. Check: $\left(\sqrt3+\sqrt7\right)^2=10+2\sqrt{21}=10+\sqrt{84}$.
