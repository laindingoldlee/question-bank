Choose the thousands, tens, and units digits arbitrarily in $10^3$ ways. The leading digit must have one prescribed residue modulo $3$; among $1,\ldots,9$ each residue has three representatives. Therefore there are $\boxed{3\cdot10^3=3000}$ numbers.
