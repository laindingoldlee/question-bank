Each year the number is multiplied by $\dfrac{16}{4}=\dfrac{64}{16}=4$, so in year $n$ Robin has $4\cdot4^{n-1}=4^n$ robins. In the seventh year he has
\[
4^7=16384
\]
robins, that is, $\boxed{16384}$.
