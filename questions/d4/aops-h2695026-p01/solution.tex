Expanding place values gives
\[
110(X+Y)+2Z=1100W+11Z,\qquad110(X+Y-10W)=9Z.
\]
The right side lies between $0$ and $81$, so both sides are zero. Hence $Z=0$ and $X+Y=10W$. Since $2\le X+Y\le18$ and $W\ne0$, $W=1$ and $X+Y=10$. The digits $X,Y$ must be distinct and different from $0,1$, so the possible ordered pairs are $(2,8),(3,7),(4,6),(6,4),(7,3),(8,2)$. The answers are
\[
\boxed{280,\ 370,\ 460,\ 640,\ 730,\ 820}.
\]
