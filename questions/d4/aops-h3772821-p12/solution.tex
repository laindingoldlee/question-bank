Choose any three of the sixteen points, then subtract collinear triples. The four horizontal and four vertical lines contribute $8\binom43=32$. The two full diagonals contribute $2\binom43=8$, and four shorter diagonals of length three contribute $4$. No other slope can contain three grid points, since a primitive step with a coordinate of magnitude at least two would span at least four units in two steps. Thus the answer is $\binom{16}{3}-32-8-4=560-44=\boxed{516}$.
