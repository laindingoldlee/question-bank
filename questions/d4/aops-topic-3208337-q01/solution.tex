(a) The hypothesis says $p\mid(a-b)(a+b)$. Primality forces $p$ to divide at least one factor, giving the alternatives. (b) Modulo $8$, $3^2\equiv1^2\equiv1$, but $3$ is congruent to neither $1$ nor $-1\equiv7$. Thus a composite modulus need not have the property.
