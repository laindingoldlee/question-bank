Let $x_n$ be the $n$th digit in the concatenation $123456789101112\ldots$ of all the positive integers (so $x_1=1$, $x_2=2$, $\dots$, $x_9=9$, $x_{10}=1$, $x_{11}=0$, $\dots$). Which of the following is \emph{not} a possible value of $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n}x_k$ for a positive integer $n$?

(a) $639$\quad (b) $956$\quad (c) $1198$\quad (d) $8931$\quad (e) $14503$
