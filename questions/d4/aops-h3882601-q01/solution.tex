Put $k=\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor$. In a full block $k^2\le n<(k+1)^2$, the multiples of $k$ are $k^2,k^2+k,k^2+2k$, exactly three. Blocks $k=1,\ldots,30$ are complete and give $90$ values. For $k=31$, the interval is truncated to $961\le n\le 1000$, containing $961$ and $992$. The total is $\boxed{92}$.
