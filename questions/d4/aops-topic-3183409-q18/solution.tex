Let the prices be $a,b$ cents. Subtracting the purchases gives $9a-3b=60$, so $b=3a-20$. The first cost is $43a-140$, a multiple of 100. Thus $43a\equiv40\pmod{100}$, and multiplication by 7 gives $a\equiv80\pmod{100}$. Positive prices and the spending bound allow only $a=80$: the next value 180 gives a first cost 7600 cents alone, and the prior value is negative. Thus the first cost is $43\cdot80-140=3300$ cents, or $\boxed{33\text{ dollars}}$.
