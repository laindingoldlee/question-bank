Since $\overline{ab}=10a+b$, the condition is
\[
10a+b=ab+a+b\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad 9a=ab\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad a(b-9)=0 .
\]
The tens digit $a$ is not $0$, so $b=9$, and every $a=1,\dots,9$ works:
\[
\boxed{19,\ 29,\ 39,\ 49,\ 59,\ 69,\ 79,\ 89,\ 99}.
\]
For example $1\cdot9+1+9=19$ and $9\cdot9+9+9=99$.
