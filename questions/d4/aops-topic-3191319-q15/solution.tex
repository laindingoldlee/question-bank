Let paved time be $t$ hours. Total time is $\dfrac{5}{3}$ hours, so $100t+30(\dfrac{5}{3}-t)=100$. Thus $70t=50$ and $t=\dfrac{5}{7}$. The fraction is $\dfrac{\dfrac{5}{7}}{\dfrac{5}{3}}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{7}}$.
