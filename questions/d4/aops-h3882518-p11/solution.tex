Project to the $(x_1,x_2)$-plane; the area scale is constant, so the distribution is uniform on the triangle $x_1,x_2\ge0$, $x_1+x_2\le1$, of area $\dfrac{1}{2}$. The condition on $x_3$ is automatic. The regions violating $x_1\le\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $x_2\le\dfrac{2}{3}$ are triangles with areas $\dfrac{2}{9}$ and $\dfrac{1}{18}$, and their interiors are disjoint. The desired probability is
\[
1-\dfrac{\dfrac{2}{9}+\dfrac{1}{18}}{\dfrac{1}{2}}=\dfrac{4}{9}.
\]
Hence $\boxed{m+n=13}$.
