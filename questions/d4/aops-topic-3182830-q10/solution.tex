We have $f(x)=\log_n27+(x-1)\log_n(2n)$. Thus $7\log_n27+21\log_n(2n)=42$, or $\log_n(216n^3)=6$. Hence $216n^3=n^6$. Since a logarithm base must satisfy $n>0$, $n\ne1$, this gives $n=6$. Therefore $g_2=27\cdot12=\boxed{324}$.
