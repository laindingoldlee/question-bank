Let $g_x$ be a geometric sequence with first term $27$ and successive ratio $2n$ (so $\dfrac{g_{x+1}}{g_x} = 2n$). Then, define a function $f$ as $f(x) = \log_n(g_x)$, where $n$ is the base of the logarithm. It is known that the sum of the first seven terms of $f(x)$ is $42$. Find $g_2$, the second term of the geometric sequence.

Note: The logarithm base $b$ of $x$, denoted $\log_b(x)$ is equal to the value $y$ such that $b^y = x$. In other words, if $\log_b(x) = y$, then $b^y = x$.
