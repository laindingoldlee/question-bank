The rows have $10,11,\dots,29$ seats. In a row of $n$ seats, no two students may be adjacent, so at most $\left\lceil\dfrac {n}{2}\right\rceil$ students fit (pair the seats $1\text{-}2$, $3\text{-}4$, \dots; each pair holds at most one student, and a lone last seat holds at most one), and this is achieved by using seats $1,3,5,\dots$. The rows are independent, so the maximum is
\[
\sum_{n=10}^{29}\left\lceil\dfrac {n}{2}\right\rceil=5+(6+6)+(7+7)+\cdots+(14+14)+15=5+2\cdot90+15=200,
\]
and the answer is $\boxed{\text{(C)}\ 200}$.
