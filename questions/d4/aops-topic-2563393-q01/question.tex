John and Louis run a $5$-mile race, and Louis wins by a few seconds. John claims that he had the higher average speed, because he ran for $2$ miles, walked for $1$ mile and then ran the last $2$ miles. Louis claims that he had the higher average speed because he won the race. Who is correct? Explain.
