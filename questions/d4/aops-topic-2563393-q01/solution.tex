Average speed is total distance divided by total time. Both ran $5$ miles, and Louis took less time ($t_L<t_J$), so
\[\dfrac{5}{t_L}>\dfrac{5}{t_J}.\]
\textbf{Louis} is correct. How fast John went on parts of the course does not matter; only his total time does.
