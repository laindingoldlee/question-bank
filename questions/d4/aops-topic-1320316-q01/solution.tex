With eight scores, the median is the average of the 4th and 5th smallest, so those two add to $176$.

Let the new scores be $x\le y\le z$. If $x>88$, then only $75,81,86$ are at most $88$, so the 4th and 5th smallest scores both exceed $88$ and so does the median. Hence $x\le88$.

$x=88$ is possible: with new scores $88,88,100$, the sorted list is $75,81,86,88,88,92,98,100$, whose median is $88$. The answer is $\boxed{88}$.
