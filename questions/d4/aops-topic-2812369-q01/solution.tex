The distance between $AB$ and $CD$ is $6$, so the radius is $3$. The diagonal $AC$ has length $10$ and passes through $O$, so the part inside the circle is a diameter of length $6$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(8,0),C=(8,6),D=(0,6),O=(4,3);draw(A--B--C--cycle^^A--D--C);draw(circle(O,3));draw(A--C);dot(O);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$O$",O,S);label("$8$",(4,0),S);label("$6$",(8,3),E);
\end{asy}

The fraction is $\dfrac6{10}=\boxed{\dfrac35}$.
