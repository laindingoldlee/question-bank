The four interior lattice points are $(1,1),(1,2),(2,1),(2,2)$. From any one, exactly two of the four directions stay inside and two hit the boundary. Therefore the probability of avoiding the boundary for all three steps is $(\dfrac{1}{2})^3=\dfrac{1}{8}$, and the desired probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{7}{8}}$.
