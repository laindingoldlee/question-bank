For every positive integer $n$, prove that
\[\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\dfrac{\dbinom{n-2+k}{n-1}}{2^k}=2^{n-1}.\]
For example, $n=2$ gives $\dfrac12+\dfrac24+\dfrac38+\dfrac{4}{16}+\cdots=2$ and $n=3$ gives $\dfrac12+\dfrac34+\dfrac68+\dfrac{10}{16}+\cdots=4$.
