A figure consists of a regular octagon together with the eight segments joining its centre $O$ to its vertices. An ant starts at $O$. At each move it walks along one segment of the figure to a neighbouring point, choosing uniformly at random among all segments at its current point: from $O$ it goes to one of the $8$ vertices, and from a vertex it goes to one of the two neighbouring vertices or to $O$. What is the expected number of moves until the ant first returns to $O$?
