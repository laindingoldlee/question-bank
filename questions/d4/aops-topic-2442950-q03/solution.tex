The first move takes the ant from $O$ to a vertex. From then on, at every vertex the ant has $3$ segments to choose from, exactly one of which leads to $O$. So, whatever vertex it is at, each move takes it to $O$ with probability $\dfrac13$, independently of the past moves.

The number $T$ of further moves until it reaches $O$ is therefore geometric with success probability $p=\dfrac13$: $P(T=k)=\left(\dfrac23\right)^{k-1}\dfrac13$, with mean $\dfrac1p=3$. (Indeed, if $e$ is the expected number of moves from a vertex, then $e=1+\dfrac23e$, so $e=3$.)

The expected number of moves until the ant returns to $O$ is $1+3=\boxed{4}$.
