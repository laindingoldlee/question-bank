Jana needs $\dfrac{2}{6}$ hour $=20$ minutes. Zhao needs $\dfrac{2}{10}$ hour $=12$ minutes and starts $5$ minutes later, so he finishes $5+12=17$ minutes after Jana starts. Jana reaches the end $20-17=\boxed{3}$ minutes after Zhao.
