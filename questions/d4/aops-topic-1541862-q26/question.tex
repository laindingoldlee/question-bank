Jana begins jogging along a path and, $5$ minutes later, Zhao begins riding his bicycle along the same path, which has a length of $2$ miles. Zhao rides his bicycle at a speed of $10$ miles per hour, and Jana's jogging speed is $6$ miles per hour. If they both begin at one end of the path and end at the other, how many minutes after Zhao reaches the end of the path will Jana reach the end of the path?
