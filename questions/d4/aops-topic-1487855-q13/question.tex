Sara and Kaleigh are painting a house, each working at her own constant rate. If Sara works $8$ days, Kaleigh needs $4$ more days to complete the job. If Sara works $4$ days, Kaleigh needs $6$ days to finish. How many days would Sara need to paint the entire house by herself?
