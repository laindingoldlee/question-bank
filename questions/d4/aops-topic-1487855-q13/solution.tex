Let Sara paint $s$ and Kaleigh $k$ of the house per day. Then
\[
8s+4k=1,\qquad 4s+6k=1 .
\]
Doubling the second equation gives $8s+12k=2$; subtracting the first gives $8k=1$, so $k=\dfrac{1}{8}$. Then $8s=1-\dfrac{4}{8}=\dfrac{1}{2}$, so $s=\dfrac{1}{16}$. Sara alone needs $\boxed{16}$ days. (Kaleigh alone would need $8$ days.)
