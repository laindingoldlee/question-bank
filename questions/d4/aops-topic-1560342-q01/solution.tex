\textbf{(a)} Replace $x$ by $x-10$ in the formula: $f(x-10)=-2(x-10)+8=-2x+28$. Then
\[
-4f(x-10)=-4(-2x+28)=\boxed{8x-112}.
\]

\textbf{(b)} $f(-8)=-2(-8)+8=24$, so $15-f(-8)=15-24=\boxed{-9}$.
