Write $y=\dfrac{1+x}{2-x}$ and solve for $x$:
\[
y(2-x)=1+x\iff2y-1=x(1+y)\iff x=\dfrac{2y-1}{y+1}\quad(y\ne-1).
\]
The value $y=-1$ is never taken, since $\dfrac{1+x}{2-x}=-1$ would mean $1+x=x-2$. So $f$ is one-to-one from $\{x\ne2\}$ onto $\{y\ne-1\}$, and
\[
\boxed{f^{-1}(x)=\dfrac{2x-1}{x+1}},\qquad x\ne-1 .
\]
Check: $f\left(f^{-1}(x)\right)=\dfrac{1+\dfrac{2x-1}{x+1}}{2-\dfrac{2x-1}{x+1}}=\dfrac{3x}{3}=x$.
