Put $s=x+y+z$. Cauchy-Schwarz gives $s^2\le3(4s)$, so $0\le s\le12$. Also
\[
xy+yz+zx=\dfrac{s^2-4s}{2}=\dfrac{(s-2)^2}{2}-2.
\]
Thus the minimum is at least $-2$ and the maximum at most $48$. The minimum is attained at $(x,y,z)=(2,\sqrt2,-\sqrt2)$, and the maximum at $(4,4,4)$; both satisfy the constraint. Therefore $M=48,m=-2$ and $\boxed{M+10m=28}$.

AM-GM requires nonnegative inputs. Although $(xy)(yz)(zx)=x^2y^2z^2\ge0$, the individual terms $xy,yz,zx$ may be negative, so this product condition does not justify AM-GM.
