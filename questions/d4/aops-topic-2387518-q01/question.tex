A $2005\times2005$ square is made up of $2005^2$ unit squares. The middle unit square of each row is shaded. A rectangle (of any size, with sides along the grid lines) is chosen at random from all the rectangles in the figure. What is the probability that the chosen rectangle contains at least one shaded square?
