The diameter has length
\[
AB=\sqrt{(10-(-2))^2+(2-4)^2}=\sqrt{144+4}=\sqrt{148},
\]
so the radius satisfies $r^2=\left(\dfrac{AB}{2}\right)^2=\dfrac{148}{4}=37$. The centre is the midpoint $(4,3)$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
pair A=(-2,4), B=(10,2), O=(4,3); real r=sqrt(37);
draw((-9,0)--(12,0),gray,Arrow(5)); draw((0,-4)--(0,10),gray,Arrow(5));
label("$x$",(12,0),E,gray); label("$y$",(0,10),N,gray);
draw(circle(O,r),linewidth(0.9)); draw(A--B,blue);
dot(A); dot(B); dot(O);
label("$A(-2,4)$",A,W); label("$B(10,2)$",B,E); label("$(4,3)$",O,N);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The area is $\pi r^2=\boxed{37\pi}$.
