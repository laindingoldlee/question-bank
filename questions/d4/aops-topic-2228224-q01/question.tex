The medial triangle of a triangle is the triangle formed by the midpoints of its sides. Starting from $\triangle ABC$, take the medial triangle four times in a row (the medial triangle of the medial triangle of the medial triangle of the medial triangle), and call the result $\triangle MON$. Find $\dfrac{[MON]}{[ABC]}$, where $[\,\cdot\,]$ denotes area.
