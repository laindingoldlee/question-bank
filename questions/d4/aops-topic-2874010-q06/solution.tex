Let $E_j$ be the expected remaining number with a current trailing run of $j$ losses. Put $p=\dfrac{3}{4}$, $q=\dfrac{1}{4}$. Then
\[E_0=1+qE_0+pE_1,\quad E_1=1+qE_0+pE_2,\quad E_2=1+qE_0.\]
Substitution gives $E_0=(1+p+p^2)(1+qE_0)$, hence $p^3E_0=1+p+p^2$. Therefore
\[E_0=\dfrac{1+\dfrac{3}{4}+\dfrac{9}{16}}{\dfrac{27}{64}}=\boxed{\dfrac{148}{27}}.\]
