The ratio has $4+8+12+20+24=68$ parts, so one part is $\dfrac{816}{68}=12$ dollars. The third child has $12$ parts, that is $12\cdot12=\boxed{\$144}$. Since $144<150$, the third child \textbf{cannot} afford the $\$150$ purchase (he is $\$6$ short).
