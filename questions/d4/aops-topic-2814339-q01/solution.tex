$30^{39}=2^{39}3^{39}5^{39}$, and a divisor $2^a3^b5^c$ has $a,b,c\in\{0,\dots,39\}$ chosen independently, $40$ options each. It is a multiple of $30^{29}$ iff $a,b,c\ge29$, $11$ options each. The probability is $\left(\dfrac{11}{40}\right)^3=\boxed{\dfrac{1331}{64000}}$.
