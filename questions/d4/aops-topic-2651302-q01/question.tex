Positive integers $x$ and $y$ are such that $\dfrac{\left(x^2-x+1\right)\left(y^2-y+1\right)}{xy}$ is an integer. Prove that $x=y=1$. (In particular, $x\mid x^2-x+1$ and $y\mid y^2-y+1$ do hold.)
