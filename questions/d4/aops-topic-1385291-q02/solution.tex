Let the speeds be $v$ and $1.5v$. When they meet, together they have travelled to $B$ and back to the meeting point, that is, twice the distance $AB$: $240$ miles. In $2.4$ hours,
\[
(v+1.5v)\cdot2.4=6v=240\quad\Longrightarrow\quad v=40 .
\]
So the speeds are $\boxed{40\text{ mph and }60\text{ mph}}$. The faster one has gone $60\cdot2.4=144$ miles, that is, $120$ miles to $B$ and $24$ miles back, so the meeting point is $\boxed{24\text{ miles}}$ from $B$. (Check: the slower one has gone $96=120-24$ miles.)
