Two motorcyclists left point $A$ for point $B$ at the same time. One was $1.5$ times as fast as the other. The one who reached $B$ first immediately turned back, and he met the other motorcyclist $2$ hours $24$ minutes after they left $A$. The distance from $A$ to $B$ is $120$ miles. Find the speeds of the motorcyclists and the distance from their meeting point to $B$.
