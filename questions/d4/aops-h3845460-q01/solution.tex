By the definition of the foot, $D$ belongs to line $AC$, so $A,C,D$ are collinear. Noncollinearity of the original three points says $B$ does not belong to that line. For any $X$ on the line, the perpendicular gives
\[
BX^2=BD^2+DX^2\ge BD^2.
\]
Equality occurs exactly when $X=D$. Taking nonnegative square roots proves the minimum distance is $\boxed{BD}$, attained uniquely at the perpendicular foot.

\end{asy}
