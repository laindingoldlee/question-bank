In Wonderland some towns are connected by railroads. The mirror country Rednowland has the same set of towns, but if two towns $A$ and $B$ in Wonderland are connected, then their mirror towns $A$’ and $B$’ in Rednowland are not connected, and if $A$ and $B$ are not connected, then $A’$ and $B’$ are connected. Alice of Wonderland has to make at least two stops to visit Queentown from her hometown. Show that Ecila of Rednowland can reach any town from any other town with at most two stops.
