Model towns as vertices and railroads as edges of a graph $G$. There are towns $u,z$ whose distance in $G$ is at least three edges. In the complementary graph they are adjacent. Every other town $v$ is adjacent in the complement to at least one of $u,z$: otherwise $u,v,z$ would be a two-edge route in $G$. Thus any two towns in the complement can travel via at most the route $v,u,z,w$, with some vertices omitted when possible. This uses at most three edges, hence at most two intermediate stops.
