Since $7\cdot837=5859$, we have $5858\equiv-1$, $5857\equiv-2$ and $5856\equiv-3\pmod 7$. Hence
\[5858\times5857\times5856\equiv(-1)(-2)(-3)=-6\equiv1\pmod7.\]
The remainder is $\boxed{1}$.
