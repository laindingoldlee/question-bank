There are $N=36^{10}$ strings. The two specified pairs remove two inputs and two outputs; the remaining $N-2$ inputs can be matched bijectively to the remaining outputs in $(N-2)!$ ways. Thus there are $\boxed{(36^{10}-2)!}$ compatible codes. A third input can be sent to any one of the $36^{10}-2$ unused outputs, so its output is not uniquely determined by the two examples.
