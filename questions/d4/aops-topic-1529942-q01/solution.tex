\textbf{The maximal configuration.} Let there be $n$ chords. For chords in general position (no two sharing an endpoint, no three through one interior point), the number of regions is $1+n+I$, where $I$ is the number of interior intersection points: drawing the chords one at a time, a chord that crosses $k$ earlier chords is cut into $k+1$ pieces, and each piece splits a region in two. Each pair of chords meets at most once, so $I\le\binom n2$, and the maximum $1+n+\binom n2$ is reached exactly when every two chords cross inside the circle and no three pass through one point. Then
\[
I=\binom{1500}{2}.
\]

\textbf{Regions touching the circle.} The $2n$ endpoints split the circle into $2n$ arcs, and each arc lies on the boundary of exactly one region. A region cannot touch two different arcs: every region is convex (an intersection of the disc with half-planes), so if it touched two arcs it would contain the segment between points of them; but between the two arcs (on either side) lies an endpoint $X$ of some chord, and that chord crosses every other chord, so it separates the two arcs and would have to cut through the region. Hence exactly $2n$ regions touch the circle.

\textbf{Polygons.} The number of polygons is
\[
\left(1+n+\binom n2\right)-2n=\binom n2-n+1 .
\]
For example, with $n=4$ chords there are $6$ intersection points and $\binom42-4+1=3$ polygons:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real[] th={10,55,100,145}; real[] off={0.3,-0.22,0.12,-0.3};
pair[] U,V;
draw(unitcircle,linewidth(0.9));
for(int i=0;i<4;++i){
  pair n=dir(th[i]), d=dir(th[i]+90), c=off[i]*n; real t=sqrt(1-off[i]^2);
  U.push(c-t*d); V.push(c+t*d); draw(c-t*d--c+t*d,blue);
}
for(int i=0;i<4;++i) for(int j=i+1;j<4;++j) dot(extension(U[i],V[i],U[j],V[j]),red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

(the four chords cross in $6$ points, shown in red, and exactly three of the regions do not touch the circle).

\textbf{Difference.}
\[
I-\left(\binom n2-n+1\right)=n-1=\boxed{1499}.
\]
