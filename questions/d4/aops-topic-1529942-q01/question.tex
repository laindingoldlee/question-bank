In a circle, $1500$ chords are drawn so that they divide the interior of the circle into the largest possible number of regions. Some of these regions are bounded only by the chords (they do not touch the circle); call them the polygons formed by the chords. Find the difference between the number of intersection points of the chords and the number of these polygons.
