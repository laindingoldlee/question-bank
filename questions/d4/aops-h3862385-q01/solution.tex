Number positions in each deck from $1$ to $36$. If a particular card is at position $i$ in the upper deck and its mate at position $j$ in the lower deck, the number between is $36+j-i-1=35+j-i$. Summing over all cards, both $i$ and $j$ run through $1,\ldots,36$, regardless of the shuffle. Their sums cancel, leaving $36\cdot 35=\boxed{1260}$.
