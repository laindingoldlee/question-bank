The ball falls $24$ feet, then rises and falls $18$ feet, then $13.5$ feet, and so on. The rebound heights form a geometric series $18+13.5+\cdots$ with ratio $\dfrac{3}{4}$ and sum $\dfrac{18}{1-\dfrac{3}{4}}=72$; each is travelled twice. The total is
\[24+2\cdot72=\boxed{168}\ \text{feet}.\]
The heights tend to $0$, so the ball ``comes to rest'' on the ground at height $0$.
