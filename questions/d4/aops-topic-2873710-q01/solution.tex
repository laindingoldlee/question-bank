$\varphi$ is multiplicative, so it suffices to bound each prime power $p^k$ ($k\ge1$).

For an odd prime $p\ge3$: $p-1\ge\sqrt p$ (since $p^2-3p+1>0$ for $p\ge3$), so $\varphi(p^k)=p^{k-1}(p-1)\ge p^{k-1}\sqrt p\ge\sqrt{p^k}$, as $p^{k-1}\ge\sqrt{p^{k-1}}$.

For $p=2$: $\varphi(2^k)=2^{k-1}=\dfrac{2^k}2\ge\dfrac{\sqrt{2^k}}2$.

Multiplying over the prime powers of $n$ gives $\varphi(n)\ge\dfrac12\sqrt n$ (the factor $\dfrac12$ appears at most once, for the power of $2$). $\blacksquare$
