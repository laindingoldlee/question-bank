In the padded strings $000$ through $999$, each digit occurs $300$ times. Removing leading zeros does not change counts for digits $1$ through $9$, and removes $111$ zeros (one from each string $000$ through $099$, plus another from $000$ through $009$, plus the last zero in $000$). Appending $1000$ adds one $1$ and three zeros. Thus $1$ appears $301$ times, each of $2$ through $9$ appears $300$ times, and $0$ appears $192$ times. The answer is $\boxed1$.
