\textbf{All plates.} Choose the position of the digit ($5$ ways), the digit ($10$ ways) and the four letters in order ($26^4$ ways): $5\cdot10\cdot26^4$ plates, all equally likely.

\textbf{Favourable plates.} The digit must be $8$, in any of the $5$ positions. The four letters, in order, must include A, M and C. Count these words by their fourth letter:
\begin{itemize}
\item a letter other than A, M, C ($23$ choices): the four letters are distinct, arranged in $4!=24$ ways, giving $23\cdot24=552$;
\item a second A, M or C ($3$ choices): a multiset such as $\{A,A,M,C\}$, arranged in $\dfrac{4!}{2!}=12$ ways, giving $36$.
\end{itemize}
So there are $552+36=588$ suitable letter words. (Check by inclusion--exclusion: $26^4-3\cdot25^4+3\cdot24^4-23^4=588$.)

\textbf{Probability.}
\[
\dfrac{5\cdot1\cdot588}{5\cdot10\cdot26^4}=\dfrac{588}{4569760}=\boxed{\dfrac{147}{1142440}}\approx1.29\cdot10^{-4}.
\]
