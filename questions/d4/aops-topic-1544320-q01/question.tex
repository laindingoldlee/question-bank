In AlphaBetaLand, a license plate consists of $4$ letters (each one of the $26$ letters of the alphabet, repetitions allowed) and one digit (from $0$ to $9$), in any order; for example TRA1N, TRAI1 and 1RAIN are plates. What is the probability that a randomly selected AlphaBetaLand license plate contains all of the letters A, M, C and the digit $8$?
