The numbers are the roots of $t^2-2at+(a^2-1)=0$, by Vieta's formulas. This is $(t-a)^2=1$, so $t=a\pm1$. The numbers are $\boxed{a+1\text{ and }a-1}$. Check: $(a+1)+(a-1)=2a$ and $(a+1)(a-1)=a^2-1$.
