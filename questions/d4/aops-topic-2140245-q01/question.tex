A problem states: ``A point $P$ is placed inside an isosceles triangle $ABC$ with $AB=3$ and $BC=7$. The distances from $P$ to the sides $AB$, $BC$ and $AC$ are $4$, $2$ and $3$, respectively. Find the area of triangle $ABC$.''
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that $AC=7$.
\item[(b)] Find the area of triangle $ABC$.
\item[(c)] Show that no point inside triangle $ABC$ has distances $4$, $2$ and $3$ to $AB$, $BC$ and $AC$, so the problem's data are inconsistent.
\end{enumerate}
