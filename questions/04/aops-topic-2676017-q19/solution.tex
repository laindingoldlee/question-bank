$225=9\cdot25$. A multiple of $25$ ends in $00$, $25$, $50$ or $75$, so with digits $0,1$ it ends in $00$. A multiple of $9$ has digit sum divisible by $9$, so there are at least nine $1$s. The smallest such number is $\boxed{11111111100}$.
