The smallest integer for a fixed set of distinct prime divisors is their product. If there are two primes summing to $28$, the possibilities are $5,23$ and $11,17$, giving $115,187$. If there are three, parity forces one to be $2$, and the others are $3,23$ or $7,19$, giving $138,266$. Four or more distinct primes have product at least $2\cdot3\cdot5\cdot7=210>115$. A single prime cannot sum to $28$. Thus the smallest number is $115$ and its first digit is $\boxed1$.
