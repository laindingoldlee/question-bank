The axial triangle has base 6, height 4, equal sides 5, area 12 and semiperimeter 8. Its inradius, hence the sphere radius, is $\dfrac{12}{8}=\dfrac{3}{2}$. The cone volume is $\dfrac{1}{3}\pi3^2\cdot4=12\pi$, and sphere volume is $\dfrac{4}{3}\pi(\dfrac{3}{2})^3=\dfrac{9}{2}\pi$. Their ratio is $\boxed{\dfrac{8}{3}}$.
