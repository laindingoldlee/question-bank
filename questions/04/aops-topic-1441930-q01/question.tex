A student claims that $\sqrt{16+9}=\sqrt{16}+\sqrt{9}=7$, with this argument: ``Taking a square root is raising to the power $\dfrac{1}{2}$, powers are a kind of multiplication, and in the order of operations multiplication comes before addition; so we must take the square roots before adding.''
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the value of $\sqrt{16+9}$ and explain the error in the argument.
\item[(b)] Prove that $\sqrt{a+b}<\sqrt a+\sqrt b$ for all positive real numbers $a$ and $b$.
\end{enumerate}
