Eight friends, including a pair of twins, sit at a square table with two chairs on each side. The twins insist on sitting on the same side of the table or on opposite sides. All eight friends are distinguishable, and seatings that differ only by a rotation of the table are considered the same. How many seating arrangements are possible?
