First count the seatings with the chairs fixed (not identifying rotations). The first twin can take any of the $8$ chairs. The second twin must sit on the same side ($1$ chair) or on the opposite side ($2$ chairs), so has $3$ choices. The other $6$ friends fill the remaining $6$ chairs in $6!=720$ ways. That gives $8\cdot3\cdot720=17280$ seatings.

A rotation by $90^\circ$, $180^\circ$ or $270^\circ$ moves every person to a different chair, so it never maps a seating to itself, and each seating is one of exactly $4$ seatings that differ by rotations. Rotations also preserve the twins' condition. Therefore the number of arrangements is
\[\dfrac{17280}{4}=\boxed{4320}.\]
