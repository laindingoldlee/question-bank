A triangle $A_0B_0C_0$ has angles $59.999^\circ$, $60^\circ$ and $60.001^\circ$. For $n\ge1$, $A_n$ is the foot of the altitude from $A_{n-1}$ to $B_{n-1}C_{n-1}$, and similarly for $B_n$, $C_n$. Find the least $n$ for which triangle $A_nB_nC_n$ is obtuse.
