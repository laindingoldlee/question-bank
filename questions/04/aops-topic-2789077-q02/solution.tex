$(x^2+5y^2)(u^2+5v^2)=(xu-5yv)^2+5(xv+yu)^2$, which one checks by expanding both sides: each equals $x^2u^2+5x^2v^2+5y^2u^2+25y^2v^2$ (the cross terms $\pm10xuyv$ cancel). $\blacksquare$
