Right triangle $ABC$ has $\angle ABC=90^\circ$, $AB=12$, $BC=16$ and $CA=20$. Let $N$ be the foot of the altitude from $B$ to $AC$, and let $L$ be the midpoint of $BC$. In triangle $BNL$, let $T$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $L$ to $BN$. Let $E$ be the midpoint of $NL$, $F$ the midpoint of $LE$, and $M$ the midpoint of $TL$. Compute
\[\dfrac{[MFE]\cdot ME}{[ABLN]},\]
where $[\ \cdot\ ]$ denotes area.
