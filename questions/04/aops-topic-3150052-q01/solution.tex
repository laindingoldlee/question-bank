The third digit is $3$ or $9$, and the fourth is $4$ or $8$. The second then has three choices among $2,4,6,8$, excluding the fourth digit. The first can be any of the six remaining nonzero digits. Thus there are $2\cdot2\cdot3\cdot6=\boxed{72}$ numbers.
