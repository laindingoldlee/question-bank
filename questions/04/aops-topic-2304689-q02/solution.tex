Since $1.125=\dfrac98$, the mother weighs $8m$ and the father $9m$ pounds for some positive integer $m$ (the mother's weight must be a multiple of $8$ for the father's to be a whole number). Their total is $17m$, and $17m=71k$ for a positive integer $k$. As $71$ is prime and does not divide $17$, it divides $m$, so $m\ge71$. For $m=71$: the father weighs $639$, the mother $568$, the total is $1207=17\cdot71$, and $k=17$. The smallest possible weight of the father is $\boxed{639}$ pounds.
