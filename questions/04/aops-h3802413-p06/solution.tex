A line of $2k+1$ selected people has its tallest person forced to the middle. Choose the $k$ people on the left; their increasing order and the remaining people's decreasing order are forced. Thus there are $\binom{2k}{k}$ arrangements. The possible ordered lengths are $(3,9),(5,7),(7,5),(9,3)$. Therefore the total is
\[2\binom{12}{3}\binom21\binom84+2\binom{12}{5}\binom42\binom63=61600+190080=\boxed{251680}.\]
