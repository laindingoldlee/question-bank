From $|a+3|=2$, $a=-1$ or $a=-5$. From $|b+1|=3$, $b=2$ or $b=-4$. The four sums $a+b$ are $1,-5,-3,-9$, so $|a+b|\in\{1,5,3,9\}$. The value $7$ is impossible: the answer is \textbf{(D)}.
