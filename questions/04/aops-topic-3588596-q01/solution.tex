Write the three equal lengths as $s$, so $BC=2s$. The equal legs make the trapezoid isosceles. Its height is $\sqrt{s^2-(\dfrac{s}{2})^2}=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}s$, and the horizontal distance from $A$ to $C$ is $\dfrac{3}{2}s$. Hence $AC^2=\dfrac{9}{4}s^2+\dfrac{3}{4}s^2=3s^2$. Therefore $AB^2+AC^2=s^2+3s^2=BC^2$, so the converse of Pythagoras gives $\boxed{\angle CAB=90^\circ}$.
