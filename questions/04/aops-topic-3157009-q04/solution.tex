For first term $ a\ge1 $ and length $ k\ge2 $, $ k(2a+k-1)=84 $. The bound $ k(k+1)\le84 $ gives $ k\le8 $. Testing $ k=2,\ldots,8 $ for positive integral $ a=\dfrac{\dfrac{84}{k}-k+1}{2}$ yields exactly $(a,k)=(13,3),(9,4),(3,7)$. Hence $\boxed3 $ representations.
