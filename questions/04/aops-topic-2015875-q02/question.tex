(Menelaus' theorem.) A line $\ell$ that passes through no vertex of triangle $ABC$ meets the lines $BC$, $CA$, $AB$ at $D$, $E$, $F$, respectively. Prove that
\[
\dfrac{BD}{DC}\cdot\dfrac{CE}{EA}\cdot\dfrac{AF}{FB}=1 .
\]
