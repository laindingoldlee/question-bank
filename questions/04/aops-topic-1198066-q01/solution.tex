Option 1 costs the decimal digit sum and Option 2 costs the number of $1$s in binary. A number below $1000$ has at most $10$ binary digits and is not $1023$, so its binary digit sum is at most $9$; hence its decimal digit sum must also be at most $9$, which leaves only a handful of candidates in each hundred. Testing them from the top down, none in $504$--$999$ works (for example $900=1110000100_2$ has four $1$s, $800=1100100000_2$ has three, $700=1010111100_2$ has six), and the first match is $503$: its digit sum is $5+0+3=8$ and $503=111110111_2$ has eight $1$s. So the answer is $\boxed{503}$.
