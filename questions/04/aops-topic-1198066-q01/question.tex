A positive integer less than $1000$ can be transmitted in two ways. Option 1: pay $\$d$ to send each decimal digit $d$. Option 2: write the integer in binary and pay $\$d$ to send each binary digit $d$. What is the largest integer less than $1000$ that costs the same either way?
