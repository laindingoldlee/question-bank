The area is $K=84$ (Heron's formula with $s=21$: $K=\sqrt{21\cdot8\cdot7\cdot6}=84$). The circumradius of a triangle with sides $a,b,c$ is $R=\dfrac{abc}{4K}$, so
\[R=\dfrac{13\cdot14\cdot15}{4\cdot84}=\dfrac{2730}{336}=\boxed{\dfrac{65}{8}}.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0), C=(14,0), A=(5,12);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
label("$A$",A,N); label("$B$",B,SW); label("$C$",C,SE);
label("$13$",(A+B)/2,W); label("$15$",(A+C)/2,NE); label("$14$",(4,0),S);
pair Oc=(7,33/8); draw(circle(Oc,65/8),red); draw(Oc--A,red+dashed); dot(Oc); label("$O$",Oc,NE,red); label("$\dfrac{65}{8}$",(Oc+A)/2,W,red);
\end{asy}

(Check with coordinates $B=(0,0)$, $C=(14,0)$, $A=(5,12)$: the point $O=\left(7,\dfrac{33}{8}\right)$ satisfies $OB^2=49+\dfrac{1089}{64}=\dfrac{4225}{64}=OA^2=4+\left(\dfrac{63}{8}\right)^2$, so $R=\dfrac{65}{8}$.)
