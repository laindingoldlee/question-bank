The first two conditions say $x\equiv3\pmod{91}$, so $x=91k+3$. Divisibility by $12$ needs $91k+3\equiv7k+3\equiv0\pmod{12}$, i.e. $7k\equiv9\pmod{12}$. Since $7\cdot7=49\equiv1$, $k\equiv63\equiv3\pmod{12}$. So $x=91(12j+3)+3=1092j+276$:
\[\boxed{x\equiv276\pmod{1092}},\]
and the smallest positive one is $276=12\cdot23=7\cdot39+3=13\cdot21+3$.
