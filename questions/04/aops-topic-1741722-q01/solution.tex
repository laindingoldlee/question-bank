Choose how many pieces of each length are used, $(n_4,n_3,n_2,n_1)$ with $n_4\le1$, $n_3\le2$, $n_2\le3$, $n_1\le4$ and $4n_4+3n_3+2n_2+n_1=10$; then arrange them in $\dfrac{(n_4+n_3+n_2+n_1)!}{n_4!\,n_3!\,n_2!\,n_1!}$ orders.
\[
\begin{array}{cccc|c}
n_4&n_3&n_2&n_1&\text{orders}\\\hline
0&0&3&4&35\\
0&1&2&3&60\\
0&1&3&1&20\\
0&2&0&4&15\\
0&2&1&2&30\\
0&2&2&0&6\\
1&0&1&4&30\\
1&0&2&2&30\\
1&0&3&0&4\\
1&1&0&3&20\\
1&1&1&1&24\\
1&2&0&0&3
\end{array}
\]
(For $n_4=0$: $3n_3+2n_2+n_1=10$ with $n_1\le4$ forces $3n_3+2n_2\ge6$; for $n_4=1$: $3n_3+2n_2+n_1=6$. These are all the cases.) The total is
\[
35+60+20+15+30+6+30+30+4+20+24+3=\boxed{277}.
\]
