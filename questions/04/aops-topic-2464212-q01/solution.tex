Compare consecutive terms. Since $2(n+1)^2-15(n+1)+11=2n^2-11n-2$,
\[a_{n+1}-a_n=2^n\left(2\left(2n^2-11n-2\right)-\left(2n^2-15n+11\right)\right)=2^n\left(2n^2-7n-15\right)=2^n(2n+3)(n-5).\]
For positive $n$ the factors $2^n$ and $2n+3$ are positive, so
\[a_{n+1}<a_n\ (n<5),\qquad a_6=a_5,\qquad a_{n+1}>a_n\ (n>5).\]
Thus $a_1>a_2>\cdots>a_5=a_6<a_7<\cdots$, and the minimum is attained exactly at $\boxed{n=5\text{ and }n=6}$, with value
\[a_5=32\cdot(50-75+11)=32\cdot(-14)=\boxed{-448}=64\cdot(-7)=a_6.\]
