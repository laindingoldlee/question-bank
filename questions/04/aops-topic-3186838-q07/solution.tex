The horizontal distances are $\sqrt{25^2-24^2}=7$ and $\sqrt{40^2-24^2}=32$. They lie on opposite sides, so the separation is $\boxed{39}$ feet.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(-7,0),B=(0,0),C=(32,0),D=(0,24);draw(A--D--C--A);draw(B--D);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,S);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,N);label("$25$",(A+D)/2,W);label("$40$",(C+D)/2,NE);label("$24$",D/2,E);
\end{asy}
