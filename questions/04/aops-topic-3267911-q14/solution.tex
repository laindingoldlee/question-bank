The cases $c=0,1$ cannot produce a positive three-digit integer. If $b=0$, the exponent is-1, also impossible. Thus $c\in\{2,\ldots,9\}$ and the exponent is a positive odd integer at least 3. The three-digit odd powers of one-digit bases are $2^7=128$, $2^9=512$, $3^5=243$, $5^3=125$, $6^3=216$, $7^3=343$, $8^3=512$, $9^3=729$. Enforcing that the units digit equals the base leaves 512 from base 2,243,125,216,729. Checking the exponent $2ab-1$ retains exactly 512,125,216: their required exponents are 9,3,3. The sum is $\boxed{853}$.
