For positive integers $a\ge b$, the Euclidean algorithm divides $a$ by $b$ with remainder, $a=qb+r$ with $0\le r<b$, replaces $(a,b)$ by $(b,r)$, and repeats until the remainder is $0$; the last nonzero remainder is the output. Prove that the algorithm stops and that its output is $\gcd(a,b)$.
