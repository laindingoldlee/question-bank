Jerry brings three balloons to a party and releases them from the same point. Each balloon rises at a constant rate of $5$ feet per second, while the wind carries it north at a constant rate of $240$ yards per minute. The first balloon pops after $80$ seconds, the second after $153$ seconds, and the third pops after rising $15180$ inches. What is the mean of the straight-line distances, in feet, that the three balloons travel from the release point to where they pop?
