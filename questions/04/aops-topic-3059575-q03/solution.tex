The radius is perpendicular to the tangent, so $ CE=\sqrt{5^2-3^2}=4 $. The right triangles $ AEC $ and $ BED $ are similar, with scale factor $\dfrac{8}{3}$. Hence $ DE=\dfrac{32}{3}$. The internal tangent has its contact points on opposite sides of $ E $, giving $ CD=4+\dfrac{32}{3}=\boxed{\dfrac{44}{3}}$.
