The whole row of Pascal's triangle sums to $2^{12}=4096$, and it is symmetric: $\dbinom{12}{k}=\dbinom{12}{12-k}$. The middle entry is $\dbinom{12}{6}=924$, so
\[\binom{12}{0}+\binom{12}{1}+\cdots+\binom{12}{5}=\dfrac{4096-924}{2}=1586 .\]
Removing $\dbinom{12}{0}+\dbinom{12}{1}+\dbinom{12}{2}=1+12+66=79$ gives
\[\binom{12}{3}+\binom{12}{4}+\binom{12}{5}=1586-79=1507 .\]
Finally $\dbinom{12}{7}=\dbinom{12}{5}=\dfrac{12\cdot11\cdot10\cdot9\cdot8}{120}=792$, so the sum is
\[1507+924+792=\boxed{3223}.\]
