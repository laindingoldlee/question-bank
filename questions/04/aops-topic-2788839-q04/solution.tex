Since $2009=7^2\cdot41$, we have $2009k+3\equiv3\pmod7$. Cubes modulo $7$ are $0^3\equiv0$, $(\pm1)^3\equiv\pm1$, $(\pm2)^3\equiv\pm1$, $(\pm3)^3\equiv\mp1$, so they are $0,1,6$ only, never $3$. $\blacksquare$
