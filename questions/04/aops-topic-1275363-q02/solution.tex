$\dfrac{1}{k(k+1)}=\dfrac{1}{k}-\dfrac{1}{k+1}$, so the sum of the reciprocals telescopes to $1-\dfrac{1}{2000}=\dfrac{1999}{2000}$. Then $x\cdot\dfrac{1999}{2000}=1$ and $x=\boxed{\dfrac{2000}{1999}}$.
