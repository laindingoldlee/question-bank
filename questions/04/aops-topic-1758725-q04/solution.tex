The number of students is a common multiple of $44=2^2\cdot11$ and $24=2^3\cdot3$, and the least one is $\operatorname{lcm}(44,24)=2^3\cdot3\cdot11=264$. A row length works exactly when it divides $264$, so the possible row lengths are the divisors of $264$, of which there are $(3+1)(1+1)(1+1)=16$ (including a single row of $264$ and rows of $1$). She can go $\boxed{16}$ days.
