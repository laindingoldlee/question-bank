Since $2010=3\cdot670$, the number is an integer:
\[\dfrac{2010^{2010}}{3}=670\cdot2010^{2009}=67\cdot201^{2009}\cdot10^{2010}.\]
Its last $2010$ digits are zeros, and the $2011$th digit from the right is the units digit of $67\cdot201^{2009}$, which is $7\cdot1=\boxed{7}$.
