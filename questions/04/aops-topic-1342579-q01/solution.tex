Factor: $x^4-51x^2+50=(x^2-1)(x^2-50)$. This is negative exactly when $x^2$ lies strictly between $1$ and $50$, that is, $x^2\in\{4,9,16,25,36,49\}$, i.e.\ $|x|\in\{2,3,4,5,6,7\}$. That gives $\boxed{12}$ integers.
