James chooses an element $x$ uniformly at random from the set
\[S=\{-2021,\,-2020.5,\,-2020,\,\dots,\,2020,\,2020.5,\,2021\}\]
of all multiples of $0.5$ from $-2021$ to $2021$. He wins if $\lceil x\rceil-\lfloor x\rfloor=1$ and $\sqrt{x^2}=x$. What is the probability that he wins?
