Malcolm has $\$638.54$ in his checking account. He must keep a balance of at least $\$500$ to avoid a fee. He wrote a check for $\$159.50$ today. Write and solve an inequality for the amount $x$ he must deposit to avoid a fee, and find the least such amount. A student wrote $638.54-159.50+x\le500$; explain why this is wrong.
