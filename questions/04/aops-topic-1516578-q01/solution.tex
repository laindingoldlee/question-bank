After the check and a deposit of $x$ dollars the balance is $638.54-159.50+x=479.04+x$. To avoid the fee the balance must be \emph{at least} $500$:
\[
479.04+x\ge500\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad x\ge20.96 .
\]
The least deposit is $\boxed{\$20.96}$.

The student's inequality $479.04+x\le500$ says the balance is \emph{at most} $500$, the opposite of the requirement; it describes the deposits that are not enough (or exactly enough), $x\le20.96$. The words ``maintain a $\$500$ balance'' mean ``balance $\ge500$''.
