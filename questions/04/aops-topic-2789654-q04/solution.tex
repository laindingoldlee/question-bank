Alice always subtracts $1$ from an even number, leaving a positive odd number. From an odd number, every divisor is odd, so Bob either writes zero and loses immediately or leaves a positive even number for Alice. Alice can continue this strategy whenever it is her turn. The positive number strictly decreases, so eventually Bob receives $1$, whose only move writes zero. Thus Alice wins.
