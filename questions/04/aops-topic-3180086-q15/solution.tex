Place the octahedron vertices on coordinate axes at distance $h=\dfrac{3}{\sqrt2}$ from the centre. The face plane $x+y+z=h$ shows the inradius is $r=\dfrac{h}{\sqrt3}=\dfrac{\sqrt6}{2}$. The smaller sphere's centre lies on the axis to the selected vertex; write its distance from the origin as $z$. Its radius is $\rho=\dfrac{h-z}{\sqrt3}$, and external tangency gives $z=r+\rho$. Therefore $(\sqrt3+1)\rho=h-r=(\sqrt3-1)r$, so $\rho=(2-\sqrt3)r=\dfrac{\sqrt{24}-\sqrt{18}}{2}$. This positive radius places the sphere inside all eight face half-spaces. Thus $\boxed{a+b+c=44}$.
