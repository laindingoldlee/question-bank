Let $a=AB$ (a side), $b=AC$ (a short diagonal) and $c=AD$ (a long diagonal). The heptagon is cyclic, so $ACDE$ is a cyclic quadrilateral. Its sides are $AC=b$, $CD=a$, $DE=a$, $EA=c$ (by symmetry $AE=AD$), and its diagonals are $AD=c$ and $CE=b$. Ptolemy's theorem gives
\[AC\cdot DE+CD\cdot EA=AD\cdot CE,\quad\text{i.e.}\quad ba+ac=cb.\]

Dividing by $abc$: $\dfrac1c+\dfrac1b=\dfrac1a$, which is the claim. $\blacksquare$
