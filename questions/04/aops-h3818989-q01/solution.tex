The common domain is $x\notin\pi\mathbb Z$. On this domain both denominator factors are nonzero, and
\[
\dfrac{1-\cos x}{\sin x}
=\dfrac{(1-\cos x)(1+\cos x)}{\sin x(1+\cos x)}
=\dfrac{\sin^2x}{\sin x(1+\cos x)}
=\dfrac{\sin x}{1+\cos x}.
\]
The second equality uses $1-\cos^2x=\sin^2x$.

\end{asy}
