Measure the battery in full charges. Recording uses $\dfrac{1}{2.5}=\dfrac{2}{5}$ of a charge per hour and the game uses $1$ charge per hour, so together they use $\dfrac{2}{5}+1=\dfrac{7}{5}$ of a charge per hour. A full battery lasts
\[\dfrac{1}{\dfrac{7}{5}}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{7}\text{ hour}}=\dfrac{300}{7}\text{ minutes}\approx42.9\text{ minutes}.\]
