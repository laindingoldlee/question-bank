There are $\binom{n^2}{n}$ equally likely subsets of physical marbles. To get all colours, independently choose one of the $n$ marbles of each colour, giving $n^n$ favourable subsets. Therefore the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{n^n}{\binom{n^2}{n}}}$.
