We count possible \emph{teams} (sets of three people), not ways to split all $24$ people into teams at once.

There are $\binom{24}{3}=2024$ teams of three in all. A team contains a couple when it consists of one of the $12$ couples together with one of the other $22$ people; a team of three cannot contain two couples, so these $12\cdot22=264$ teams are counted once each. The number of teams with no couple is
\[
2024-264=\boxed{1760}.
\]
(Directly: choose $3$ of the $12$ couples and one person from each, $\binom{12}{3}\cdot2^3=220\cdot8=1760$.)
