For $x=3k$, $3k+1$, $3k+2$ the values $\dfrac{2x}{3}+1$ are $2k+1$, $2k+1+\dfrac{2}{3}$, $2k+2+\dfrac{1}{3}$, so $q$ takes the values $2k+1,\ 2k+1,\ 2k+2$, with sum $6k+4$. For $x=0,\dots,38$ ($k=0,\dots,12$) the total is
\[\sum_{k=0}^{12}(6k+4)=6\cdot78+52=520.\]
Finally $q(39)=27$ and $q(40)=\left\lfloor27\dfrac{2}{3}\right\rfloor=27$. The sum is $520+54=\boxed{574}$.
