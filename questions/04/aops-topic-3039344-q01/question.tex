Initially half the beans in a jar are in its wet lower half and become soaked. On each shake, a bean currently in either half has probability 0.4 of moving to the other half, with fresh random mixing at each shake. Once soaked, a bean remains soaked. What is the least number of shakes for the expected proportion soaked to be at least 99 percent?
