An initially dry bean remains dry only if it stays in the upper half on every shake, with probability $0.6^k$. Thus the expected dry proportion after $k$ shakes is $\dfrac{1}{2}(0.6)^k$. We need $(0.6)^k\le0.02$. Since $0.6^7=0.0279936>0.02$ and $0.6^8=0.01679616<0.02$, the least number is $\boxed8$. This concerns the expectation, not a deterministic guarantee for every random outcome.
