An integer dividing a repunit must be coprime to ten, since a repunit ends in one. Conversely, for $\gcd(n,10)=1$, among the first $n$ repunits either one has remainder zero modulo $n$ or two have equal remainders. Their difference is a repunit times a power of ten, and that power is invertible modulo $n$, proving existence. Thus count integers up to $2026$ divisible by neither $2$ nor $5$: $2026-1013-405+202=\boxed{810}$.
