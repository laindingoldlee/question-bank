Both terms are divisible by $3a$: $648=3\cdot216=3\cdot6^3$ and $1029=3\cdot343=3\cdot7^3$. So
\[
648a+1029a^4=3a\left(216+343a^3\right)=3a\left(6^3+(7a)^3\right).
\]
By the sum of cubes $u^3+v^3=(u+v)(u^2-uv+v^2)$ with $u=6$, $v=7a$:
\[
648a+1029a^4=\boxed{3a(7a+6)(49a^2-42a+36)} .
\]
The quadratic factor does not factor further, since its discriminant $42^2-4\cdot49\cdot36=1764-7056<0$.
