The possible products ending in $6$ are $6,16,26,36$. Product $6$ arises from $(1,6),(2,3),(3,2),(6,1)$; $16$ from $(4,4)$; $26$ is impossible; $36$ from $(6,6)$. Thus the probability is $\dfrac{6}{36}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{6}}$.
