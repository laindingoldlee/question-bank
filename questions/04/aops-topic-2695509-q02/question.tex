Point $A$ is chosen uniformly at random on the circle of radius $1$ centred at $O$, and point $B$ uniformly on the circle of radius $2$ centred at $O$. Let $A'$ and $B'$ be the images of $A$ and $B$ under a $80^\circ$ counterclockwise rotation about $O$. What is the probability that $A$, $B$, $A'$, $B'$ are the vertices of a convex quadrilateral?
