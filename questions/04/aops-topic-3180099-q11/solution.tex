Each exterior angle is 60 degrees. Taking side directions 0, 60,120,180,240,300 degrees and writing $EF=e,FA=f$, vector closure gives $f-e=1$ and $e+f=11$, so $e=5,f=6$. Coordinates are $A=(0,0),B=(7,0),C=(\dfrac{17}{2},\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}),D=(\dfrac{9}{2},\dfrac{11\sqrt3}{2}),E=(-\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{11\sqrt3}{2}),F=(-3,3\sqrt3)$. The shoelace sum gives area $\dfrac{93\sqrt3}{2}$. Thus $\boxed{a+b+c=98}$.
