The two pairs of opposite rectangle vertices subtend diameters from $P$, so $PA^2+PC^2=PB^2+PD^2$. Write $PA=3t,PD=5t,PB=7s,PC=8s$. Then $9t^2+64s^2=49s^2+25t^2$, giving $\dfrac{t}{s}=\dfrac{\sqrt{15}}{4}$. Hence $\boxed{\dfrac{PA}{PC}=\dfrac{3\sqrt{15}}{32}}$.
