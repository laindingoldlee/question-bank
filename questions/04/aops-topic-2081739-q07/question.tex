Each of the \(14\) guests at a party wore at least one of the colours red, blue and green. Exactly \(8\) wore blue, \(5\) wore red and \(7\) wore green. Two guests wore all three colours, and nobody wore exactly the two colours blue and green, or exactly the two colours red and green. The number of guests who wore only green is one more than the number who wore only blue. How many guests wore only red?
