There are $\dbinom{10}{2}=45$ games. A decisive game gives out $3$ points in total and a drawn game $1+1=2$ points. If $d$ games are draws,
\[
3(45-d)+2d=130\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 135-d=130\quad\Longrightarrow\quad d=\boxed{5}.
\]
