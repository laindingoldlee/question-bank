Let $n=p^aq^b>1$, where $p,q$ are distinct primes and $a,b$ are nonnegative integers. Players alternately choose a divisor of $n$ other than $1$, and no chosen divisor may be a multiple of a previously chosen divisor. A player unable to move loses. Prove the first player has a winning strategy.
