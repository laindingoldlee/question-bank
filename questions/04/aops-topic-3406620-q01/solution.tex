The difference-of-cubes identity gives $(x-5)(x^2+5x+25)$. The quadratic roots are $\dfrac{-5\pm5i\sqrt3}{2}$. Therefore the full factorization is $\boxed{(x-5)(x+\dfrac{5}{2}-\dfrac{5i\sqrt3}{2})(x+\dfrac{5}{2}+\dfrac{5i\sqrt3}{2})}$.
