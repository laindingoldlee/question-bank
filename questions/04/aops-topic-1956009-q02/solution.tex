Yes. Look for $(2^{2019}+2^k)^2=2^{4038}+2^{2020+k}+2^{2k}$; we want $2^{2020+k}+2^{2k}$ to be a single power of $2$, which happens when $2020+k=2k$, i.e. $k=2020$, and then the sum is $2^{4041}$. So with $n=4041$,
\[
2^{4041}+4^{2019}=2^{4038}(2^3+1)=9\cdot2^{4038}=\left(3\cdot2^{2019}\right)^2 .
\]
So $\boxed{n=4041}$ works.
