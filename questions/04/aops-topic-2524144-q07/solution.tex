A quadratic has exactly one (double) real root when its discriminant is $0$: $|n-2|^2-4\cdot81=0$, i.e. $|n-2|=18$. So $n-2=\pm18$, giving $\boxed{n=20\text{ or }n=-16}$. (Then the polynomial is $(x+9)^2$, with the root $-9$.)
