The sum is between $2$ and $12$. The odd sums are $3,5,7,9,11$, and all of them except $9$ are prime. So we need the sum $9$, which occurs for $(3,6),(4,5),(5,4),(6,3)$: $4$ of the $36$ outcomes. The probability is $\dfrac4{36}=\boxed{\textbf{(B) }\dfrac19}$.
