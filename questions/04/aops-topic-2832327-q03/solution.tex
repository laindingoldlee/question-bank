Both factors are powers of $p$, say $2x+3y=p^a$ and $3x+2y=p^b$ with $a,b\ge1$; let $m=\min(a,b)$. Then $p^m$ divides $3(3x+2y)-2(2x+3y)=5x$ and $2(3x+2y)-3(2x+3y)=-5y$.

If $p\ne5$ then $p^m\mid x$ and $p^m\mid y$, so $x,y\ge p^m$ and both factors are at least $5p^m$. But one of them equals $p^m$, a contradiction. So $p=5$, and $5^{m-1}$ divides $x$ and $y$. Write $x=5^{m-1}u$, $y=5^{m-1}v$; then the smaller factor gives $2u+3v=5$ or $3u+2v=5$, so $u=v=1$. Then $x=y$ and both factors equal $5x$.

The solutions are $\boxed{p=5,\ x=y=5^k,\ z=2k+2}$ for integers $k\ge0$.
