\textbf{(a)} A solution corresponds to an arrangement of $20$ stars and $2$ bars in a row: the stars before the first bar give $x$, those between the bars give $y$ and those after the second bar give $z$. Every arrangement of the $22$ symbols gives exactly one solution and vice versa, so the count is the number of ways to choose the positions of the $2$ bars:
\[
\dbinom{22}{2}=\boxed{231}.
\]
In general $x+y+z=n$ has $\dbinom{n+2}{2}$ nonnegative solutions (and $x+y=n$ has $\dbinom{n+1}{1}=n+1$, matching the pattern ``$a+b=7$ has $8$ solutions'').

\textbf{(b)} Put $x=x'+1$, $y=y'+1$, $z=z'+1$ with $x',y',z'\ge0$; then $x'+y'+z'=17$, which has $\dbinom{19}{2}=\boxed{171}$ solutions.
