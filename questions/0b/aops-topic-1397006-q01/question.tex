Three people, Phil, Alfonso and Bill, are discussing the order in which they were born (all three have different ages). Exactly one of them is lying and the other two are telling the truth.
\begin{itemize}
\item Phil: ``I am the oldest.''
\item Alfonso: ``Bill is the oldest.''
\item Bill: ``I am neither the oldest nor the youngest.''
\end{itemize}
Who is lying?
