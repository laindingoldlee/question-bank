Phil and Alfonso cannot both be telling the truth, since they name different oldest people. So the liar is one of them, and Bill tells the truth: Bill is the middle one. In particular Bill is not the oldest, so Alfonso's statement is false. Hence \textbf{Alfonso} is lying, and then Phil's statement is true.

Check: Phil is the oldest, Bill is in the middle, Alfonso is the youngest; Phil and Bill tell the truth and only Alfonso lies.
