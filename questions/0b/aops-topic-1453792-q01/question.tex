In Lietopia everyone lies with probability $99\%$ and tells the truth with probability $1\%$. In Lietruthopolis everyone lies with probability $50\%$ and tells the truth with probability $50\%$. At a Lietopia-Lietruthopolis party, two guests are asked whether they like a new movie, and both say ``yes''. Assume that each guest, independently, is equally likely to come from either city and likes the movie with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, and that each answer is a lie or the truth independently of everything else. What is the probability that both guests like the movie?
