Let $g$ be the greatest common divisor.

\textbf{$6$ divides every term.} A prime $p\ge5$ is odd, so $p^2+2021$ is odd plus odd, which is even. Also $3\nmid p$, so $p\equiv\pm1\pmod3$ and $p^2\equiv1\pmod3$; since $2021\equiv2\pmod3$, we get $p^2+2021\equiv0\pmod3$. Hence $6\mid g$.

\textbf{$g$ is at most $6$.} The first two terms are $5^2+2021=2046$ and $7^2+2021=2070$, and $g$ divides
\[\gcd(2046,2070)=\gcd(2046,24)=\gcd(6,24)=6,\]
using $2070-2046=24$ and $2046=85\cdot24+6$.

Therefore $g=\boxed{6}$, answer $\textbf{(D)}$.
