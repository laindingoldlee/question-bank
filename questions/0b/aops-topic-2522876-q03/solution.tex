\textbf{Positive triples.} $144=2^4\cdot3^2$. Writing $a=2^{x_1}3^{y_1}$, $b=2^{x_2}3^{y_2}$, $c=2^{x_3}3^{y_3}$, we need $x_1+x_2+x_3=4$ and $y_1+y_2+y_3=2$ with nonnegative integers. By stars and bars there are $\dbinom{6}{2}=15$ choices for the $x$'s and $\dbinom42=6$ for the $y$'s, so $90$ positive triples.

\textbf{Signs.} The product is positive, so an even number of $a,b,c$ are negative: none, or one of the $3$ pairs, which is $4$ sign patterns.

The total is $90\cdot4=\boxed{360}$.
