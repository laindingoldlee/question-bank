Assign the categories in $\dfrac{6!}{3!2!1!}=60$ ways. Each assignment has probability $(\dfrac{1}{2})^3(\dfrac{1}{3})^2\dfrac{1}{6}=\dfrac{1}{432}$. Thus the answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{5}{36}}$.
