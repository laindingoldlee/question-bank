\textbf{Quadrant IV has no solutions.} A point there has $x>0$ and $y<0$. But then $2x+1>1$, so $y\ge2x+1$ would force $y>1>0$, a contradiction.

\textbf{The other quadrants contain solutions.} For example:
\begin{itemize}
\item $(1,5)$ in quadrant I: $5\ge3$ and $5>-\dfrac{1}{2}$;
\item $(-1,1)$ in quadrant II: $1\ge-1$ and $1>-\dfrac{3}{2}$;
\item $(-4,-2)$ in quadrant III: $-2\ge-7$ and $-2>-3$.
\end{itemize}
The solution set (shaded) lies above both lines; the boundary lines meet at $\left(-\dfrac{4}{3},-\dfrac{5}{3}\right)$.
