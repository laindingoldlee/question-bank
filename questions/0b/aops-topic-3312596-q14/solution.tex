The number 1 must be first. Among 2,3,4,6, the restrictions are 2 before 4 and 6, and 3 before 6. Their five valid orders are $2346,2364,2436,3246,3264$. The numbers 5 and 7 are unrestricted relative to those four. Choose their positions among the remaining six in $\binom62$ ways and their order in 2 ways. Hence there are $5\binom62\cdot2=150$ valid lists, whose last two digits are $\boxed{50}$.
