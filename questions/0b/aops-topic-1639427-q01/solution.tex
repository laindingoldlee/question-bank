Expand both sides. On the left,
\[
(3a+b)(3b+a)=3a^2+10ab+3b^2=2(a+b)^2+\left(a^2+6ab+b^2\right)=2(a+b)^2+(a+b)^2+4ab .
\]
On the right, $\left(\sqrt a+\sqrt b\right)^2=a+b+2\sqrt{ab}$, so
\[
2(a+b)\left(\sqrt a+\sqrt b\right)^2=2(a+b)^2+4(a+b)\sqrt{ab}.
\]
Subtracting, the left side minus the right side is
\[
(a+b)^2-4(a+b)\sqrt{ab}+4ab=\left(a+b-2\sqrt{ab}\right)^2=\left(\sqrt a-\sqrt b\right)^4\ge0 ,
\]
which proves the inequality. Equality holds exactly when $\sqrt a=\sqrt b$, that is, $\boxed{a=b}$.
