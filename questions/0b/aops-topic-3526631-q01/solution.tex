Put $A=9^{200}$. Polynomial division gives $\dfrac{A^{200}}{A-2}=\sum_{j=0}^{199}2^jA^{199-j}+\dfrac{2^{200}}{A-2}$. The last fraction lies strictly between zero and $\dfrac{1}{2}$, since $9^{200}>2^{201}+2$. Thus the rounded integer is the displayed sum. Since $A\equiv1\pmod{100}$, its residue is $\sum_{j=0}^{199}2^j=2^{200}-1$. Powers of two have period twenty modulo $100$ from the relevant positive exponents, and $2^{20}\equiv76$; alternatively $(2^{20})^{10}\equiv76^{10}\equiv76$. Hence the required remainder is $\boxed{75}$.
