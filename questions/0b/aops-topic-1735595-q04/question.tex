A yacht hits an iceberg that breaks two holes in the hull. Water rushes through one hole at a rate that would sink the yacht in $72$ minutes, and through the other at a rate that would sink it in $2$ hours. How many minutes would it take for the yacht to sink with water rushing through both holes?
