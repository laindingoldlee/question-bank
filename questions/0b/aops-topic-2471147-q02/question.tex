Mark's age and Janice's age are both two-digit numbers when written in base $n$ (so neither leading digit is $0$), and Mark's age written in base $n$ is Janice's age written in base $n$ with its two digits reversed. Their ages differ by $24$ (in base $10$). Find all possible values of $n$.
