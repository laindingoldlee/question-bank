Because $(a-b)^2=a^2+b^2-2ab$ and $a-b=ab$, we have $a^2+b^2=(ab)^2+2ab$. Dividing by $ab\ne0$ gives $\dfrac{a}{b}+\dfrac{b}{a}=ab+2$. The expression is therefore $\boxed2$, choice (E).
