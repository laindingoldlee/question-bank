After $t$ hours a dose retains the fraction $\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^{\dfrac {t}{4}}=2^{-\dfrac {t}{2}}$. At 6:00 p.m. the doses taken at 8:00, 12:00 and 4:00 have been in his body for $10$, $6$ and $2$ hours:
\[500\left(2^{-5}+2^{-3}+2^{-1}\right)=500\cdot\dfrac{1+4+16}{32}=\dfrac{10500}{32}=\boxed{328\dfrac{1}{8}}\ \text{mg}.\]
