The effectiveness of a medication decays geometrically, so that one quarter of a dose's effect remains after $4$ hours. Donovan takes $500$ mg every $4$ hours, starting at 8:00 a.m. How much effective medication remains in his body at 6:00 p.m.? Express your answer as a mixed number of milligrams.
