At a dance party a group of boys and girls exchange dances as follows: one boy dances with $5$ girls, a second boy dances with $6$ girls, and so on, the last boy dancing with all the girls. If $b$ is the number of boys and $g$ the number of girls, find $b$ in terms of $g$.
