Digit sums preserve residues modulo 9, so $n^2\equiv n\pmod9$. The only residues satisfying this are 0 and 1. The candidates strictly between 1999 and 2022 are therefore $2007,2008,2016,2017$. Their squares are $4028049,4032064,4064256,4068289$, with digit sums $27,19,27,37$, respectively, unlike the original digit sums $9,10,9,10$. But $1999^2=3996001$ and both digit sums are 28. Thus $\boxed{1999}$.
