The remaining numbers are those ending in $1,3,7$ or $9$. Only units digits matter. In each block of ten, $10k+1,\dots,10k+9$ for $k=0,1,\dots,200$ (that is, up to $2009$), the remaining numbers end in $1,3,7,9$, whose product $189$ ends in $9$. The leftover numbers $2011,2013,2017$ give $1\cdot3\cdot7=21$, ending in $1$. So the last digit is that of $9^{201}\cdot1$. Since $9^2=81$ ends in $1$, $9^{201}=9\cdot\left(9^2\right)^{100}$ ends in $9$. The answer is $\boxed{9}$.
