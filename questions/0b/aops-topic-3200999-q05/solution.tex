For integer roots $r,s$, Vieta gives $r+s=a$ and $rs=2a$, hence $(r-2)(s-2)=4$. Unordered integer factor pairs of $4$ are $(1,4),(2,2),(-1,-4),(-2,-2)$. They give $a=9,8,-1,0$, respectively. Their sum is $\boxed{16}$.
