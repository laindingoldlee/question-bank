A planet moves in a circular orbit of radius $20{,}000$ miles around a star. A UFO is at the midpoint of the segment joining the star and the planet. The UFO travels in a straight line at constant speed and collides with the planet exactly when the planet has completed $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of its orbit (moving at constant speed). The ratio of the UFO's speed to the planet's speed can be written as $\dfrac{a\sqrt{b}}{c\pi}$, where $a$, $b$, $c$ are positive integers, $a$ and $c$ are relatively prime, and $b$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. Find $a+b+c$.
