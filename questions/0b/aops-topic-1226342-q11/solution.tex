Each round reduces $n$ players to $\left\lceil\dfrac {n}{2}\right\rceil$: $2016\to1008\to504\to252\to126\to63\to32\to16\to8\to4\to2\to1$. That is $\boxed{11}$ rounds; fewer is impossible, since after $r$ rounds at least $\dfrac{2016}{2^r}$ players remain and $2^{10}<2016$.
