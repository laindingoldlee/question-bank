In $ ABEF $, side $ EF $ is parallel to $ AB $. Since $ E $ lies on $ CD $, the two parallelograms have the same base and height, hence both have area 19. Inclusion-exclusion gives union area $ 19+19-11=\boxed{27}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(4,0),C=(6,19.0/4),D=(2,19.0/4),PtE=(50.0/19,19.0/4),F=(-26.0/19,19.0/4);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--B--PtE--F--cycle,blue);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,N);label("$E$",PtE,N);label("$F$",F,NW);
\end{asy}
