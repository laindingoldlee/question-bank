Anthony, Boris, and Carson are playing a game of frisbee! Anthony starts with the frisbee. On each turn, the player holding the frisbee throws it to someone else, after which the frisbee is either caught or dropped. If the frisbee is dropped, the nearest player picks it up. Throughout the game, Anthony throws the frisbee $8$ times and catches it $6$ times, Boris throws the frisbee 10 times and catches it $7$ times, and Carson throws the frisbee $7$ times and catches it $8$ times. How many times is the frisbee dropped during the game?
