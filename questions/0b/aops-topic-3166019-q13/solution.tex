Burnside's lemma on the fourteen symmetries gives: the identity fixes $2^7=128$ colorings; each of six nonidentity rotations fixes only the two monochromatic colorings; each of seven reflections fixes $2^4=16$, because one bead is fixed and three pairs are mirrored. The count is $\dfrac{128+6\cdot2+7\cdot16}{14}=\boxed{18}$.
