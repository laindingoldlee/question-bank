We have \(8!=40320=2^7\cdot3^2\cdot5\cdot7\) (by Legendre's formula, the exponent of \(2\) is \(\left\lfloor\dfrac{8}{2}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{8}{4}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{8}{8}\right\rfloor=4+2+1=7\), and that of \(3\) is \(2\)).

\(N^2\mid 8!\) means that for each prime \(p\), twice the exponent of \(p\) in \(N\) is at most its exponent in \(8!\). So the exponents in \(N\) are at most \(\left\lfloor\dfrac{7}{2}\right\rfloor=3\) for \(2\), \(1\) for \(3\), and \(0\) for \(5\) and \(7\). The largest \(N\) is
\[
N=2^3\cdot3=\boxed{24},
\]
and indeed \(24^2=576\) divides \(40320=576\cdot70\).
