A statement ``yesterday was one of my lying days'' can be made either on a truthful day whose yesterday was a lying day, or on a lying day whose yesterday was a truthful day (so that the statement is false).

\textbf{Lion} (lies Monday to Wednesday). Truthful days with a lying yesterday: Thursday (yesterday Wednesday). Lying days with a truthful yesterday: Monday (yesterday Sunday). So the day is Monday or Thursday.

\textbf{Unicorn} (lies Thursday to Saturday). Truthful days with a lying yesterday: Sunday (yesterday Saturday). Lying days with a truthful yesterday: Thursday (yesterday Wednesday). So the day is Sunday or Thursday.

The only day on both lists is $\boxed{\text{Thursday}}$.
