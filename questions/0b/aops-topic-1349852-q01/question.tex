Alice meets a lion and a unicorn. The lion lies on Mondays, Tuesdays and Wednesdays and tells the truth on the other days of the week. The unicorn lies on Thursdays, Fridays and Saturdays and tells the truth on the other days. On the same day they make these statements:

Lion: ``Yesterday was one of my lying days.''

Unicorn: ``Yesterday was one of my lying days.''

From these two statements Alice can work out the day of the week. What day is it?
