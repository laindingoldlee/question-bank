The diameter of the Earth's orbit around the Sun is about $186$ million miles. A star $X$ is observed from the two ends $A$ and $B$ of a diameter of the orbit (the two points of the orbit that are farthest apart), and the lines of sight $AX$ and $BX$ meet at an angle of $\theta=4.269\times10^{-4}$ degrees. Assume that the star is equally far from $A$ and from $B$. One light-year is about $5.879\times10^{12}$ miles. About how many light-years away is the star? Give your answer to three significant figures.
