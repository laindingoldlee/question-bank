A forest is a rectangle of square blocks, $125$ blocks long and $91$ blocks wide. A fire starts in the centre block at time $0$. Every $3$ seconds the fire spreads by one block in all directions, including diagonally, so after $3$ seconds a $3\times3$ square of blocks is burning, after $6$ seconds a $5\times5$ square, and so on. After how many seconds does the fire first reach a block on the border of the forest?
