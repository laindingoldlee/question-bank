A multiple of $4$ is also a multiple of $2$, so we need a multiple of $4$, a multiple of $3$ and a multiple of $5$ among the four numbers. Let $F_4$, $F_3$, $F_5$ be the events that there is \emph{no} multiple of $4$, $3$, $5$ respectively. Since $120$ is divisible by $3$, $4$ and $5$, the proportions are exact: a single number avoids
\begin{itemize}
\item multiples of $4$ with probability $\dfrac{3}{4}$, of $3$ with probability $\dfrac{2}{3}$, of $5$ with probability $\dfrac{4}{5}$;
\item multiples of $4$ and $3$ with probability $\dfrac{3}{4}\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}=\dfrac{1}{2}$, of $4$ and $5$ with probability $\dfrac{3}{5}$, of $3$ and $5$ with probability $\dfrac{8}{15}$;
\item all three with probability $\dfrac{3}{4}\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot\dfrac{4}{5}=\dfrac{2}{5}$.
\end{itemize}
(Divisibility by $3$, $4$, $5$ is independent for a uniform number from $1$ to $120$, by the Chinese remainder theorem.) The four picks are independent, so by inclusion--exclusion the probability of success is
\[
1-\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^4-\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^4-\left(\dfrac{4}{5}\right)^4+\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^4+\left(\dfrac{3}{5}\right)^4+\left(\dfrac{8}{15}\right)^4-\left(\dfrac{2}{5}\right)^4 .
\]
Over the common denominator $2^8\cdot3^4\cdot5^4=12960000$ the numerator is
\[
12960000-4100625-2560000-5308416+810000+1679616+1048576-331776=4197375,
\]
and $\dfrac{4197375}{12960000}=\boxed{\dfrac{3731}{11520}}\approx0.324$.
