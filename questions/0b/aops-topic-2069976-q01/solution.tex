Since $r$ and $s$ are the roots, the polynomial factors as
\[
ax^2+bx+c=a(x-r)(x-s)=ax^2-a(r+s)x+ars .
\]
Comparing coefficients of $x$ and the constant terms,
\[
b=-a(r+s),\qquad c=ars,
\]
so $\boxed{r+s=-\dfrac {b}{a}}$ and $\boxed{rs=\dfrac {c}{a}}$.

The minus sign appears because a root $r$ enters the factorisation as the factor $x-r$, with a minus sign: expanding $(x-r)(x-s)$ produces the term $-(r+s)x$. (For example, $x^2-5x+6=(x-2)(x-3)$ has roots summing to $5=-\dfrac{-5}{1}$.)
