For a nonnegative integer $n$, $n$ trees are to be planted in a row of $n+10$ plots, at most one tree per plot, so that no two trees are in adjacent plots. Each tree can be one of two types. Let $k(n)$ be the number of ways to plant the trees (choosing both the plots and the types). Find the sum of $k(n)$ over all nonnegative integers $n$ for which $k(n)>0$.
