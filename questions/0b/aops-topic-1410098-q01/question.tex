A flat ring (a disc with a smaller concentric disc cut out of its centre) has inner radius $3$ cm and outer radius $9$ cm. It spins about its centre at $20$ revolutions per minute. A point $P$ on the outer edge and a point $Q$ on the inner edge both go round once in the same time. Find the speed of each point in cm per minute, and explain how the two edges can move at different speeds although they turn at the same number of revolutions per minute.
