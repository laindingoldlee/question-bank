Let the two population sizes be $ P,S $. Their overlap satisfies $\dfrac{32}{33}S=\dfrac{8}{11}P $. Hence $\dfrac{P}{S}=\dfrac{32}{33}\cdot\dfrac{11}{8}=\boxed{\dfrac{4}{3}}$, or $\boxed{4:3}$.
