Multiply the numerator by $2-1=1$ and use the difference of squares repeatedly:
\[(2-1)(2+1)=2^2-1,\quad\left(2^2-1\right)\left(2^2+1\right)=2^4-1,\quad\left(2^4-1\right)\left(2^4+1\right)=2^8-1,\]
and so on, until $\left(2^{16}-1\right)\left(2^{16}+1\right)=2^{32}-1$. So the numerator equals $2^{32}-1$, and the fraction is $\boxed{1}$.
