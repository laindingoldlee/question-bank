A ``red orange'' costs $\$9200$. Glerp wants to buy only a part of one red orange: the part must be between $\dfrac1{921}$ and $\dfrac1{433}$ of an orange, and it must cost less than $\$10$. As a percentage of a whole orange, rounded to the nearest thousandth of a percent, what is the largest part he can take? (Calculators allowed.)
