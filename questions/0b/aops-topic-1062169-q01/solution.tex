Write the sizes in nondecreasing order $0\le a\le b\le c\le d$ with sum 10. Put $u=a,v=b-a,w=c-b,z=d-c$. This gives a bijection with nonnegative solutions of $4u+3v+2w+z=10$. For $u=0,1,2$, the remaining total $M=10-4u$ is 10,6,2. For each $v=0,\ldots,\left\lfloor\dfrac{M}{3}\right\rfloor$, there are $\left\lfloor\dfrac{M-3v}{2}\right\rfloor+1$ choices of $w$, and then $z$ is fixed. The totals are $(6+4+3+1)=14$, $(4+2+1)=7$, and 2. Thus the answer is $\boxed{23}$.
