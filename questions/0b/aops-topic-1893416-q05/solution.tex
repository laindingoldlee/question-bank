We prove $n!>2^n$ for every $n\ge4$ by induction. For $n=4$: $24>16$. If $n!>2^n$ for some $n\ge4$, then $(n+1)!=(n+1)\,n!>2\cdot2^n=2^{n+1}$, since $n+1>2$. Hence $\boxed{80!>2^{80}}$.
