There are $35$ correct answers in all. A majority question has at most $5$ correct answers and any other question at most $2$, so $35\le5q+2(10-q)=20+3q$, giving $q\ge5$.

$q=5$ is possible: let questions $1$-$5$ be answered correctly by everyone ($25$ answers), and questions $6$-$10$ by exactly two students each, namely $\{A,B\},\{A,B\},\{A,B\},\{A,C\},\{C,D\}$, where $A,B,C,D,E$ need $4,3,2,1,0$ more. So $q_{\min}=\boxed{5}$.
