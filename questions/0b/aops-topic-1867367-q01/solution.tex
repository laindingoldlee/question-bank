TARGET has $6$ letters, with T appearing twice and A, R, G, E once each. Arranging $6$ distinct letters gives $6!$ orders, but swapping the two T's gives the same word, so each word is counted $2!$ times. The number of arrangements is $\dfrac{6!}{2!}=\dfrac{720}{2}=\boxed{360}$. (The poster's answer $360$ is correct.)
