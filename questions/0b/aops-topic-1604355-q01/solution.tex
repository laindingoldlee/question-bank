Let $O$ be the centre of the top, and let the cake have height $h$. The perimeter of the top is $28$ inches. Starting at a corner, mark seven points on the perimeter that divide it into seven arcs of length $4$ (measured along the boundary), and cut vertically from $O$ to each of the seven points:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(3.5,3.5);
fill((0,0)--(4,0)--O--cycle,paleyellow);
draw((0,0)--(7,0)--(7,7)--(0,7)--cycle,linewidth(1));
pair[] P={(0,0),(4,0),(7,1),(7,5),(5,7),(1,7),(0,4)};
for(int i=0;i<7;++i){draw(O--P[i],dashed);dot(P[i]);}
dot(O);label("$O$",O+0.55*dir(198));
pair[] M={(2.3,1.1),(5.5,1.4),(5.8,3.4),(5.3,5.6),(3.1,5.9),(1.1,5.1),(1.0,2.3)};
for(int i=0;i<7;++i) label(string(i+1),M[i]);
label("$4$",(2,0),S);label("$3$",(5.5,0),S);
label("$1$",(7,0.5),E);label("$4$",(7,3),E);label("$2$",(7,6),E);

label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline

(The numbers outside the square are the lengths of the boundary pieces between consecutive marked points and corners; every piece has $4$ inches of boundary, for example piece 2 has $3+1$ and piece 4 has $2+2$.)

\textbf{Top area (cake and top frosting).} Each piece is a union of one or two triangles with apex $O$ and bases on the sides of the square. Every such triangle has height $\dfrac{7}{2}$ (the distance from $O$ to each side), so its area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\text{base}\cdot\dfrac{7}{2}$. Adding over the triangles of one piece, its top has area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot4\cdot\dfrac{7}{2}=7$ square inches, one seventh of $49$. Since the cake has constant height, every piece also has the same volume $7h$, and the same amount of top frosting.

\textbf{Side frosting.} The side frosting on a piece is a strip of height $h$ over its $4$ inches of boundary, of area $4h$, again the same for all seven pieces.

So the seven pieces are equal in cake, in top frosting and in side frosting. $\blacksquare$
