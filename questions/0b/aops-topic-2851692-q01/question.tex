At a carnival game there are two identical-looking guns: one holds $6$ bullets and the other $2$. You pick one at random and fire one bullet. Then you may either keep your gun or switch to the other one, after which you fire all the remaining bullets of the gun you hold. Every bullet hits a duck. To maximise the expected number of ducks hit, should you switch?
