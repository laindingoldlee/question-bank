\textbf{Keep:} you hit all the bullets of your gun, $6$ or $2$, so the expectation is $\dfrac{6+2}{2}=4$.

\textbf{Switch:} if you picked the $6$-gun you hit $1+2=3$; if you picked the $2$-gun you hit $1+6=7$. The expectation is $\dfrac{3+7}2=5$.

So you should $\boxed{\text{switch}}$ ($5$ ducks expected against $4$). The first shot costs a bullet from the gun you keep but not from the gun you switch to.
