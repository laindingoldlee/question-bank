Treat Alice--Bonnie as a block with two orientations and Donald--empty as another block with two orientations, alongside Clyde. This gives $3!\cdot2\cdot2=24$ arrangements. The forbidden ones have a consecutive triple Alice--Bonnie--Clyde or Clyde--Bonnie--Alice, two choices. With the Donald--empty block, there are two block orders and two internal orientations, giving $2\cdot2\cdot2=8$ forbidden arrangements. Thus $\boxed{24-8=16}$.
