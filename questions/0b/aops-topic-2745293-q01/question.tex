Let $a$ be a positive integer with last digit $D$, and let $b$ be the number obtained by deleting that last digit, so $a=10b+D$. Prove the following divisibility tests.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $7\mid a\iff7\mid b-2D$;\quad $13\mid a\iff13\mid b+4D$;\quad $17\mid a\iff17\mid b-5D$;\quad $19\mid a\iff19\mid b+2D$;\quad $23\mid a\iff23\mid b+7D$.
\item[(b)] If $a$ has digits $D_1D_2\ldots D_n$ (from the left), then $11\mid a\iff11\mid D_1-D_2+D_3-\cdots+(-1)^{n+1}D_n$.
\end{enumerate}
