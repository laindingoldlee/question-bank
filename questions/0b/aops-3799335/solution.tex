No. Put $D_k=\sum_{i=1}^k(x_i-y_i)\ge0$. Summation by parts gives $\sum_{i=1}^n y_i(x_i-y_i)=y_nD_n+\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}(y_i-y_{i+1})D_i\ge0$. Therefore $\sum y_i^2\le\sum x_iy_i\le\sqrt{(\sum x_i^2)(\sum y_i^2)}$ by Cauchy--Schwarz. If $\sum y_i^2=0$ the result is immediate; otherwise divide and square to get $\sum y_i^2\le\sum x_i^2$.
