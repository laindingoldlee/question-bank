King Arthur levies a $20\%$ tax on all purchases. A customer orders a burger that costs $5$ flurfs ($1$ flurf $=100$ flurffles) and has a coupon for $10\%$ off. Guinevere says the cost is higher if the coupon is applied first and the tax is computed on the discounted price. Lancelot says the cost is higher if the tax is added first and the coupon is applied afterwards. Who is correct? Find the final cost in each case.
