\textbf{Why the segments add.} The points lie on the line in the order $A,B,C,D,E$, so $B$ lies between $A$ and $C$; the segment $AC$ is made of the pieces $AB$ and $BC$ placed end to end, and $AC=AB+BC$. Likewise $C$ and $D$ lie between $B$ and $E$, so $BE=BC+CD+DE$.

\textbf{The ratio.} Let $DE=x$. Then $CD=\dfrac {x}{2}$, $BC=\dfrac{CD}{4}=\dfrac {x}{8}$ and $AB=\dfrac{BC}{3}=\dfrac {x}{24}$. Hence
\[
AC=\dfrac{x}{24}+\dfrac{3x}{24}=\dfrac{x}{6},\qquad BE=\dfrac {x}{8}+\dfrac{4x}{8}+\dfrac{8x}{8}=\dfrac{13x}{8},
\]
and
\[
\dfrac{AC}{BE}=\dfrac{x}{6}\cdot\dfrac{8}{13x}=\boxed{\dfrac{4}{39}}.
\]
