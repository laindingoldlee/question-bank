For $a\ne0$ and a positive integer $n$, the quotient rule $\dfrac{a^m}{a^n}=a^{m-n}$ with $m=n$ gives $a^0=\dfrac{a^n}{a^n}=1$. Explain why this argument cannot be used to show that $0^0=1$.
