Alice and Carl run from Bob along perpendicular directions: Alice runs $5$ m and Carl $12$ m. A rope joins Alice and Carl. From Bob, Derek runs at $30^\circ$ and Edith at $60^\circ$ to Bob--Alice until each reaches the rope. How far does each run?
