From left to right: $\dfrac{a^8}{4}\cdot\dfrac{b^{-6}c^{-2}}{8}=\dfrac{a^8b^{-6}c^{-2}}{32}$. The divisor is $2a^2b^{16}c^3$, giving $\dfrac{a^6b^{-22}c^{-5}}{64}$. The last factor is $16a^{11}b^{-8}c^3$, giving $\dfrac{16}{64}a^{17}b^{-30}c^{-2}=\boxed{\dfrac{a^{17}}{4b^{30}c^2}}$.
