The top face $PQR$ defines an affine height function over the base triangle. The average of an affine function over a triangle is the average of its values at the three vertices; this follows by barycentric coordinates, whose three averages are equal by symmetry and sum to one. Thus the mean height is $\dfrac{(1+2+3)}{3}=2$.

The equilateral base has area $\sqrt3\cdot\dfrac{4^2}{4}=4\sqrt3$. Volume is base area times mean height, so
\[
V=4\sqrt3\cdot2=8\sqrt3,\qquad \boxed{m+n=11}.
\]
