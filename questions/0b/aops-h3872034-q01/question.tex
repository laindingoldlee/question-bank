Equilateral triangle $ABC$ has side length $4$. Segments $AP$, $BQ$, and $CR$ are perpendicular to the plane containing $\triangle ABC$, and the points $P$, $Q$, and $R$ lie on the same side of that plane. Suppose

\[
        AP=1,\qquad BQ=2,\qquad CR=3.
    \]

The volume of the solid $ABCPQR$ can be written in the form

\[
        m\sqrt{n},
    \]where $m$ and $n$ are positive integers and $n$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. Find $m+n$.
