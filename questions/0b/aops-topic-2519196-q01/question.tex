Let $A=(a,b)$ and $B=(c,d)$ be two points, and let $n\ge2$ be an integer.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that for $k=0,1,\dots,n$ the points
\[P_k=\left(\dfrac{(n-k)a+kc}{n},\ \dfrac{(n-k)b+kd}{n}\right)\]
lie on segment $AB$ in this order and divide it into $n$ equal parts. In particular, the two points that trisect $AB$ are $\left(\dfrac{2a+c}{3},\dfrac{2b+d}{3}\right)$ and $\left(\dfrac{a+2c}{3},\dfrac{b+2d}{3}\right)$.
\item[(b)] Find the two points that trisect the segment from $(-2,5)$ to $(7,-1)$.
\end{enumerate}
