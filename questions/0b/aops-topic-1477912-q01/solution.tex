The modulus of a product is the product of the moduli, so $|w^6|=|w|^6$. Here
\[
\left|\dfrac{3}{5}+\dfrac{4}{5}i\right|=\sqrt{\dfrac{9}{25}+\dfrac{16}{25}}=1,
\]
so the answer is $1^6=\boxed{1}$. No expansion is needed.
