Adding and subtracting the equations gives $2x=f+s$ and $2y=f-s$, so $\boxed{x=\dfrac{f+s}{2},\ y=\dfrac{f-s}{2}}$. In the stated case, $\boxed{x=4,y=-1}$; their sum is $3$ and difference is $5$.
