Consider the leftmost column. Its two cells must be in the same tromino: if they were in different trominoes, each would need the opposite-row cell in the next column, forcing overlap. After the tromino covering the first column is placed, its one missing cell in column two forces a second tromino, completing a $2\times3$ block. There are exactly two orientations of this block. Repeating across four blocks gives $\boxed{2^4=16}$ tilings.
