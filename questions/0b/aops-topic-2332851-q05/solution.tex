Factor out $98!$:
\[98!+99!+100!=98!\,(1+99+99\cdot100)=98!\cdot10000=98!\cdot2^4\cdot5^4.\]
The number of factors $5$ in $98!$ is $\left\lfloor\dfrac{98}{5}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{98}{25}\right\rfloor=19+3=22$. So the largest $n$ is $22+4=\boxed{\textbf{(D) }26}$.
