Let $ a_0=1,a_1=3,a_2=4 $ and thereafter $ a_{k+1}=a_k+a_{k-1}$. The listed terms are $ a_1 $ through $ a_9 $, and $ a_{10}=199 $. Since $ a_k^2=a_ka_{k+1}-a_{k-1}a_k $, summation telescopes to $ a_9a_{10}-a_0a_1=123\cdot199-3=\boxed{24474}$.
