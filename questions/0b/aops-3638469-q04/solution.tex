Let $m=\lfloor \dfrac{x}{2}\rfloor$. The even choices are $0,2,\ldots,2m$, and their possible averages are all integers $0,1,\ldots,2m$. Cindy' s chance of matching any actual average is $\dfrac{1}{(2m+1)}$, regardless of Alice and Bob' s distribution. This equals $\dfrac{1}{5}$ exactly when $m=2$, so $\boxed{4\le x<6}$. If $x$ is required to be an integer, the possibilities are 4 and 5.
