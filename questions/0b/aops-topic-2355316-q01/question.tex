In a game on a board of $9$ rows with $10$ squares each, a snake occupies the first four squares of row $5$. Five fruits are placed on five different squares among the $86$ squares not occupied by the snake, with all choices equally likely. What is the probability that at least three of the fruits are in row $5$?
