For $x>0$, $x\sqrt{x\sqrt x}=x\cdot\left(x\cdot x^{\dfrac12}\right)^{\dfrac12}=x\cdot x^{\dfrac34}=x^{\dfrac74}$. So $x^{\dfrac74}=2^{63}$ gives $x=2^{63\cdot\dfrac47}=2^{36}$, and $m=\boxed{36}$.
