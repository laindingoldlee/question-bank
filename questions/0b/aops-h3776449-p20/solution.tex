The mean condition gives $a_{j+1}-a_j=a_j-a_{j-1}$, so the numbers form an arithmetic progression. Its common difference is $\dfrac{44-20}{4}=6$. The row is $20,26,32,38,44$, giving $\boxed{a_3=32}$.

\end{asy}
