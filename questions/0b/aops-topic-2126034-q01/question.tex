Three points $A$, $B$ and $C$ are chosen independently and uniformly at random on the unit circle centred at the origin $O$. Three circles are drawn, with $AB$, $BC$ and $CA$ as diameters. What is the probability that $O$ lies inside at least one of these three circles? Express your answer as a common fraction.
