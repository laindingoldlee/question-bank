In acute triangle $ABC$ with $AB<AC$, let $D$ be the altitude foot from $A$, and let $X$ lie on segment $AD$ with $\angle BXC=90^\circ$. Suppose $AB,AC$ are primes, $BD$ is a perfect square, and $BC$ is the seventh power of a prime. Minimise $[BXD]$. At this minimum, write $AX=\sqrt m-\sqrt n$ with positive integers $m,n$ and find $\lfloor \dfrac{m}{n} \rfloor$.
