The quadratic part factors as $(3a+4b)(2a-3b)$. Look for constants with $(3a+4b+m)(2a-3b+n)$ matching the linear terms: the coefficient of $a$ is $3n+2m=2$ and of $b$ is $4n-3m=31$, giving $n=4$, $m=-5$. Since $mn=-20$, the equation becomes
\[
(3a+4b-5)(2a-3b+4)=45-20=25 .
\]
For positive integers, $3a+4b-5\ge2$, so it is $5$ or $25$, with $2a-3b+4$ equal to $5$ or $1$ respectively.
\begin{itemize}
\item $3a+4b=10$, $2a-3b=1$: $a=2$, $b=1$.
\item $3a+4b=30$, $2a-3b=-3$: $17b=69$, not an integer.
\end{itemize}
The only solution is $\boxed{(a,b)=(2,1)}$. Check: $24-2-12+4+31=45$.
