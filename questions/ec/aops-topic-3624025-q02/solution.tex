The first person says red if the number of red hats among the other 99 is odd, and blue if it is even. Each later person can see all 98 other hats among those 99, so the announced parity determines their own color. Thus 99 guesses are guaranteed correct. The first person cannot guarantee correctness: changing only their unseen hat leaves their information unchanged but flips the correct answer. Hence all 100 cannot be guaranteed, and the optimum is $\boxed{99}$.
