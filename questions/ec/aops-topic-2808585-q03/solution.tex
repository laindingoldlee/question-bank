Take the side on the $y$-axis, of length $3$, as base; the height is the distance $6$ from $(6,0)$ to the $y$-axis. Area $=\dfrac12\cdot3\cdot6=\boxed{9}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((-0.5,0)--(7,0),gray);draw((0,-0.5)--(0,9),gray);draw((0,5)--(0,8)--(6,0)--cycle);label("$(0,5)$",(0,5),W);label("$(0,8)$",(0,8),W);label("$(6,0)$",(6,0),S);
\end{asy}
