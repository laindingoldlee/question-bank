Let the integers be $n-1$, $n$, $n+1$. Then
\[
(n-1)^2+n^2+(n+1)^2=3n^2+2=2525,
\]
so $n^2=841$ and $n=29$. The integers are $28,29,30$ ($784+841+900=2525$), and their sum is $3n=\boxed{87}$.
