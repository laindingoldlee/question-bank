Write $n=10q+r$, where $q\ge0$ and $0\le r\le9$. Then $n+f(n)=11q+r$, so exactly the residue class $10$ modulo $11$ is missed. Conversely any nonnegative integer with remainder $0$ through $9$ is produced by the corresponding $q,r$; a positive output gives $n>0$. Among $1,\ldots,999$, the excluded values are $10,21,\ldots,989$, numbering $90$. Thus the answer is $\boxed{999-90=909}$.
