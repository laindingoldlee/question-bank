Jeremy solves a Rubik's cube in $4$ minutes; Michael solves $5$ cubes in $12$ minutes. Each cube must be solved entirely by one person, and each person works on one cube at a time. What is the fewest number of minutes needed for them to solve $10$ cubes together? Compare with the answer if work on a cube could be shared.
