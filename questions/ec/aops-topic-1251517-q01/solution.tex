If Jeremy solves $j$ cubes and Michael $10-j$, the time is $\max\bigl(4j,\ 2.4(10-j)\bigr)$. For $j=3$: $\max(12,16.8)=16.8$; $j=4$: $\max(16,14.4)=16$; $j=5$: $\max(20,12)=20$. The first term increases and the second decreases in $j$, so the minimum is $\boxed{16}$ minutes, at $j=4$.

If cubes could be shared, the combined rate would be $\dfrac14+\dfrac5{12}=\dfrac23$ cube per minute, giving $15$ minutes. Indivisibility of the cubes is what raises the answer to $16$.
