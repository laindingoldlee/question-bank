The $13$th term is the middle of the first and $25$th terms, because $13-1=25-13=12$. So $a_{25}=2a_{13}-a_1=12-2=\boxed{10}$. (The common difference is $\dfrac{6-2}{12}=\dfrac{1}{3}$, and $2+24\cdot\dfrac{1}{3}=10$.)
