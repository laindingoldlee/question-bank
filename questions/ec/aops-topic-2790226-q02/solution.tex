A $31$-day month consists of four complete weeks plus three extra days. The weekday of the first day and the next two weekdays occur five times; all others occur four times. The only run of three consecutive weekdays containing neither Friday nor Monday is Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday. Hence May 1 is $\boxed{\text{Tuesday}}$.
