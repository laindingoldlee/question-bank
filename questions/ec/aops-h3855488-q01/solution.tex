The notation means that $m$ divides $a-b$, equivalently that $a,b$ have the same remainder on division by $m$. Congruences can be added and multiplied while preserving the modulus.

Here $2^3=8\equiv1\pmod7$ and $2026=3\cdot675+1$. Therefore
\[
2^{2026}\equiv(2^3)^{675}\cdot2\equiv2\pmod7.
\]
The remainder is $\boxed2$.
