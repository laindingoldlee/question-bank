Harry has $10$ flower seeds: $6$ daisy seeds, $2$ rose seeds and $2$ lily seeds; seeds of the same kind are indistinguishable. He plants all of them in $3$ pots, two of which are identical while the third is different. A pot may receive any number of seeds, including none. How many different ways are there to plant the seeds?
