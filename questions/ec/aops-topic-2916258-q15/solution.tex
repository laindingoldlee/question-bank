The quarter circle has area $4\pi$ and each semicircle $2\pi$. The semicircles overlap in a lens formed by two circles of radius $2$ whose centres are $2\sqrt2$ apart. Its area is $2\cdot\left(\dfrac{\pi\cdot4}4-\dfrac12\cdot2\cdot2\right)=2\pi-4$ (two circular segments of $90^\circ$).
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0),A=(4,0),B=(0,4);fill(O--arc(O,4,0,90)--cycle,lightgray);filldraw(arc((2,0),2,0,180)--cycle,white);filldraw(arc((0,2),2,-90,90)--cycle,white);draw(arc(O,4,0,90));draw(O--A);draw(O--B);
label("$O$",O,SW);label("$A$",A,S);label("$B$",B,W);
\end{asy}

The required area is $4\pi-(2\pi+2\pi-(2\pi-4))=\boxed{2\pi-4}$.
