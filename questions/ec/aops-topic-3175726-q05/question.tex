An isosceles right triangle is inscribed in a circle, which is inscribed in a second isosceles right triangle. That triangle is inscribed in another circle, which is inscribed in a third isosceles right triangle. If the largest-to-smallest triangle area ratio is $a+b\sqrt c$ with squarefree $c$, find $a+b+c$.
