Write $A=(a^2,2a),B=(b^2,2b)$ with $a,b>0$ and $a\ne b$. The distance conditions force $C=(2a^2,0),D=(2b^2,0)$. A circle through $C,D$ has equation
\[x^2+y^2-2(a^2+b^2)x+vy+4a^2b^2=0.\]
Substituting $A$ and $B$ gives $2v=a^3-4a-2ab^2=b^3-4b-2ba^2$. Subtraction and $a\ne b$ yield
\[a^2+3ab+b^2=4.\]
Set $p=ab$. The triangle's area is $ab|a-b|=p\sqrt{4-5p}$. By AM--GM applied to $\dfrac{5p}{2},\dfrac{5p}{2},4-5p$, whose sum is 4,
\[\dfrac{25}{4}p^2(4-5p)\le\left(\dfrac{4}{3}\right)^3.\]
The area is at most $\boxed{\dfrac{16\sqrt3}{45}}$. Equality requires $p=\dfrac{8}{15}$. Then $(a-b)^2=\dfrac{4}{3}$ and $(a+b)^2=\dfrac{52}{15}$, giving distinct positive $a,b$ and hence an admissible circle. The bound is attained.
