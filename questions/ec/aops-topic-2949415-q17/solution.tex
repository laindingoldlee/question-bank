If $u_k$ is the probability of occupying $A$ after $k$ moves, then $u_{k+1}=\dfrac{1-u_k}{4}$ and $u_0=1$. Subtracting the fixed point $\dfrac{1}{5}$ gives $u_k=\dfrac{1}{5}+\dfrac{4}{5}(-\dfrac{1}{4})^k$. Thus $u_5=\dfrac{51}{256}$ and $\boxed{p+q=307}$.
