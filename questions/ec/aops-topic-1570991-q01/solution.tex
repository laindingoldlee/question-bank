The square has side $QR=2-(-4)=6$, so its area is $36$.

The triangle shares the vertical side $QT$ (length $8-2=6$) with the square, and $TU=6-2=4$ is horizontal, so the triangle has a right angle at $T$ and area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot6\cdot4=12$. It lies to the right of $QT$, outside the square.

\begin{asy}

Since $S$, $T$, $U$ all lie on the line $y=2$, the union is the quadrilateral (trapezoid) $RQUS$ with parallel sides $RQ=6$ and $SU=10$ and height $6$. Its area is
\[
36+12=\dfrac{6+10}{2}\cdot6=48,
\]
answer $\boxed{\text{(B) } 48}$.
