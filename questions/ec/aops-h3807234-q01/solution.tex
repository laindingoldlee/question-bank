The square $38^2=\boxed{1444}$ is an example. A square can end only in $0,1,4,5,6,9$. A square is congruent to $0$ or $1$ modulo $4$. The last-two-digit numbers $11,55,99$ are $3$ modulo $4$, and $66$ is $2$ modulo $4$, so they are impossible. Among nonzero repeated endings, only $44$, hence the repeated digit $\boxed4$, remains.
