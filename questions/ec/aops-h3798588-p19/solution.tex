If there are $f$ fours and $g$ nines, divisibility by nine requires $4f+9g\equiv0\pmod9$, so $f$ is a positive multiple of nine. Thus at least nine fours and one nine are needed. Divisibility by four forces the ending 44, the only divisible-by-four ending among 44,49,94,99. The least ten-digit arrangement is $4444444944$, placing the nine as far right as the ending allows. Its digit sum is $9\cdot4+9=\boxed{45}$.
