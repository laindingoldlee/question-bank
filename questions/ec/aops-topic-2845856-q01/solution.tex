\textbf{Statement 1.} The days $18$ and $19$ occur only once, so if the month were May or June, Bernard might know. Albert is sure Bernard does not know, so the month is July or August: July 14, July 16, August 14, August 15, August 17.

\textbf{Statement 2.} Bernard now knows, so his day is unique in this list. Day $14$ appears twice, so the options are July 16, August 15, August 17.

\textbf{Statement 3.} Albert now knows, so his month has only one remaining option: July (August has two). The birthday is $\boxed{\text{July 16}}$.
