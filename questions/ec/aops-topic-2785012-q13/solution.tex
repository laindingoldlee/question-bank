The perfect powers at most 32 are $1,4,8,9,16,25,27,32$. Among pair sums between 18 and 32 inclusive, the only possibilities are $18=9+9$, $20=4+16$, $24=8+16$, $25=9+16$, $26=1+25$, $28=1+27$, $29=4+25$, $31=4+27$, and $32=16+16$, each unique. (The potential summand 32 would already make a sum at least 33.) But $33=1+32=8+25$, so the next number is $\boxed{33}$.
