$2014$ people sit around a circular table, numbered $1$ to $2014$ clockwise (so person $2014$ sits next to person $1$). Person $1$ holds a beam. At each step, the person holding the beam eliminates the next surviving person clockwise and passes the beam to the next survivor after that. So person $1$ eliminates person $2$ and passes the beam to person $3$; person $3$ eliminates person $4$ and passes it to person $5$; and so on. After person $2013$ eliminates person $2014$, the beam goes to person $1$, who eliminates person $3$ (the next survivor) and passes it to person $5$, and so on, until one person is left. Who is the last survivor?
