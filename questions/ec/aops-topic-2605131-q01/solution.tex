Staying wins exactly when the initial choice was correct, with probability $\dfrac{1}{100}$. If the initial choice was wrong, the host must leave the prize door closed; switching then wins. Thus switching wins with probability $\boxed{\dfrac{99}{100}}$, while staying wins with probability $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{100}}$.
