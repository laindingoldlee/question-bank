Factor: $(a-b)(a+b)=101 $. Both factors are positive integers, and $ 101 $ is prime, so $ a-b=1 $ and $ a+b=101 $. Therefore $\boxed{(a,b)=(51,50)}$. Indeed $ 2601-2500=101 $. If signed square roots are allowed, the four pairs $(\pm51,\pm50)$ give the same squares.
