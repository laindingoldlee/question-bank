Lines $\ell$ and $m$ are parallel, and a transversal $t$ meets $\ell$ at $P$ and $m$ at $Q$. Let $A$ on $\ell$ and $B$ on $m$ lie on the same side of $t$. Using the Alternate Interior Angles Theorem (if two parallel lines are cut by a transversal, alternate interior angles are congruent), prove the Consecutive Interior Angles Theorem: $\angle APQ$ and $\angle BQP$ are supplementary.
