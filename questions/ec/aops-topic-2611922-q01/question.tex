Let $ABC$ be a triangle, and let $D$ and $E$ be points on the segments $BC$ and $CA$ such that $AE=\lambda\cdot AC$ and $BD=\mu\cdot BC$, where $0<\lambda<1$ and $0<\mu<1$. The segments $AD$ and $BE$ meet at $F$. Find the ratio $AF:FD$ in terms of $\lambda$ and $\mu$.
