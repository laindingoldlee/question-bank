Write $d=2^a3^b$. Equality of divisor counts gives
\[
(a+1)(b+1)=(21-a)(21-b),
\]
equivalently $a+b=20$. The square condition requires both exponents even. Thus
\[
\sum_{d\in D}d=\sum_{j=0}^{10}2^{2j}3^{20-2j}
\equiv 3^{20}\sum_{j=0}^{10}8^j\pmod{17},
\]
because $\dfrac{4}{9}\equiv8\pmod{17}$. Now $3^{20}\equiv13$, $8^4\equiv-1$, and $\sum_{j=0}^7 8^j\equiv0$. The remaining terms sum to $1+8+8^2\equiv5$. The answer is $\boxed{13\cdot5\equiv14\pmod{17}}$.
