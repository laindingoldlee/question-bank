Let $a$, $b$, $c$ be real numbers with $a\ne0$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that for every real $x$,
\[
ax^2+bx+c=a\left(x+\dfrac{b}{2a}\right)^2-\dfrac{b^2-4ac}{4a}.
\]
\item[(b)] Deduce the vertex and the axis of symmetry of the parabola $y=ax^2+bx+c$.
\item[(c)] Use this to find the vertex of $y=2x^2-8x+5$.
\end{enumerate}
