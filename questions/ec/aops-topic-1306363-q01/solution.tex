$x^3-2x^2+x+1=x(x-1)^2+1$. For $x>0$, $x(x-1)^2\ge0$, so the argument is at least $1$, and $\ln$ is increasing with $\ln1=0$. Hence $\ln\left(x^3-2x^2+x+1\right)\ge0$, with equality only at $x=1$. $\blacksquare$
