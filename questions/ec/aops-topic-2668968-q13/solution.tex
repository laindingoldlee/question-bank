By the rules of addition and subtraction,
\[(a+c)-(b+c)=a-b+c-c=a-b.\]
Since $a>b$, the number $a-b$ is positive, so $(a+c)-(b+c)$ is positive. By the definition, $a+c>b+c$. $\blacksquare$
