Let there be $c$ cars and $k$ bicycles before the changes. Afterwards there are $0.7c$ cars and $1.2k$ bicycles, and
\[
\dfrac{0.7c}{1.2k}=\dfrac{1}{3}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\dfrac {c}{k}=\dfrac{1.2}{3\cdot0.7}=\dfrac{1.2}{2.1}=\boxed{\dfrac{4}{7}}.
\]
(The tripled permit price and the lower tuition do not enter the calculation.)
