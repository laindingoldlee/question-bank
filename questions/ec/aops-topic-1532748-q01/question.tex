A college has been trying to decrease the number of cars on campus and increase the number of bicycles. The price of a parking permit was tripled, and the number of cars on campus decreased by $30\%$. Student tuition was decreased, and the number of bicycles on campus increased by $20\%$, producing a car to bicycle ratio of $1:3$. What was the ratio of cars to bicycles before the changes occurred? Express your answer as a common fraction.
