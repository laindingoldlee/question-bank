More tails than heads means $3$ or $4$ tails. Of the $2^4=16$ equally likely sequences, $\dbinom43=4$ have exactly $3$ tails and $\dbinom44=1$ has $4$ tails. The probability is $\dfrac{4+1}{16}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{16}}$. (By symmetry this is also $\dfrac{1-P(\text{2 tails})}{2}=\dfrac{1-\dfrac{6}{16}}{2}$.)
