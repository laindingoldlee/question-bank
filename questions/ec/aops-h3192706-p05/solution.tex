In a column with two stacks and required maximum m, their positive heights can be chosen in $m^2-(m-1)^2=2m-1$ ways. Thus the left pair has three possibilities and the right pair five. Independent choice gives $\boxed{15}$ arrangements.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
for(int x=0;x<2;++x)for(int y=0;y<2;++y)draw(box((x,y),(x+1,y+1)));label("Top view",(1,-.4));for(int x=0;x<2;++x)for(int y=0;y<x+2;++y)draw(box((x+4,y),(x+5,y+1)));label("Front maxima: 2, 3",(5,-.4));
\end{asy}
