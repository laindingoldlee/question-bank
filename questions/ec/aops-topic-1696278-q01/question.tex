Let $ABC$ be a triangle with area $S$. Let $D$ be a point on side $AB$, $E$ a point on side $AC$, and $F$ a point on segment $DE$. Find the smallest constant $k$ such that always
\[
\min\{[DFB],[FEC]\}\le k\,S ,
\]
where $[XYZ]$ denotes the area of triangle $XYZ$.
