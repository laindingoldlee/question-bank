A unit segment from the origin is rotated clockwise by $90^\circ$, then extended by one without changing direction. At step $n$ it rotates by $\dfrac{90^\circ}{2^{n-1}}$ in alternating directions and extends by one. Find the area of the union of all regions swept by rotation, expressed as $\dfrac{a\pi}{b}$ in lowest terms, and give $a+b$.
