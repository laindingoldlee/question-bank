If the first entry is $a$, the conditions are $a+j\equiv0\pmod{3j+1}$ for $0\le j\le6$. Multiplying by three, which is invertible modulo these moduli, converts all of them to $3a-1\equiv0\pmod{3j+1}$. Their least common multiple is $L=16\cdot5\cdot7\cdot13\cdot19=138320$. Thus $3a-1=tL$ for a positive integer $t$. Because $L\equiv2\pmod3$, we need $t\equiv1\pmod3$; the least is $t=1$. Hence $a=46107$, whose remainder modulo $100$ is $\boxed7$.
