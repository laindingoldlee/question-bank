Let the inner side be $s$, the bottom gap be $y$, and the top gap be $6-s-y$. The bottom and top trapezoids have total area
\[
5+10=\dfrac{6+s}{2}(6-s)=\dfrac{36-s^2}{2}.
\]
Thus $s^2=6$. The four trapezoids and the inner square partition the outer square, so
\[
[BCGF]=36-6-5-9-10=\boxed6.
\]

\end{asy}
