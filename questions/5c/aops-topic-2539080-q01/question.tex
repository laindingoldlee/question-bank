Let $a$, $b$, $c$ be real numbers with $a<7$, $b<10$ and $c<13$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that if $a$, $b$, $c$ are all positive, then $abc<7\cdot10\cdot13$.
\item[(b)] Determine all ordered triples $(a,b,c)$ with $a<7$, $b<10$, $c<13$ and $abc>7\cdot10\cdot13$, and give an example.
\end{enumerate}
