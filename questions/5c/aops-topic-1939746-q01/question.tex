Find all triples $(a,n,b)$ where $a$ is a nonnegative integer, $n$ is a positive integer and $b$ is a digit, such that
\[
6^a+1=\overline{\underbrace{bb\cdots b}_{n}},
\]
the $n$-digit number all of whose digits are $b$.
