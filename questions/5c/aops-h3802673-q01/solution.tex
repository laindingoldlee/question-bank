For nonzero real $r$, AM-GM applied to $|r|$ and its reciprocal gives $|r+\dfrac{1}{r}|\ge2$. The specified value lies between one and two, so $x$ is nonreal. Let $c=\cos(\dfrac{\pi}{5})$. Since $\cos(\dfrac{3\pi}{5})=-\cos(\dfrac{2\pi}{5})$, the triple- and double-angle identities give $(c+1)(4c^2-2c-1)=0$. As $c>0$, $c=\dfrac{1+\sqrt5}{4}$. The quadratic for $x$ therefore has roots $e^{\dfrac{i\pi}{5}}$ and $e^{-\dfrac{i\pi}{5}}$. In either case the desired value is $2\cos(\dfrac{2012\pi}{5})=2\cos(\dfrac{2\pi}{5})=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt5-1}{2}}$.
