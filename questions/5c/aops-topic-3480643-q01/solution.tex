By symmetry take $1\le k\le\dfrac{n}{2}$. For $k\ge7$, $\binom nk\ge\binom{2k}{k}\ge\binom{14}{7}=3432>2025$. For $k=2,3,4,5,6$, consecutive coefficients straddling $2025$ are respectively $(\binom{64}{2},\binom{65}{2})=(2016,2080)$, $(\binom{24}{3},\binom{25}{3})=(2024,2300)$, $(\binom{16}{4},\binom{17}{4})=(1820,2380)$, $(\binom{14}{5},\binom{15}{5})=(2002,3003)$, and $(\binom{13}{6},\binom{14}{6})=(1716,3003)$. For fixed $k$, coefficients strictly increase with $n$, so none equals $2025$. Only $k=1,n=2025$ works, giving the two symmetric entries $\binom{2025}{1}$ and $\binom{2025}{2024}$. The answer is $\boxed2$.
