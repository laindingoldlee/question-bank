Put $c_i=b_i-i$. Then $c_{a_i}=-c_i$. Around a permutation cycle, an odd length forces every $c_i=0$, while an even length leaves a free parameter. Hence every cycle must be odd. The possible cycle types are $1^6$, $3\,1^3$, $3^2$, and $5\,1$. Their counts are $1$, $\binom63\cdot2=40$, $\dfrac{\binom63}{2}\cdot2^2=40$, and $6\cdot4!=144$. The total is $\boxed{225}$.
