Two four-sided dice are compared: one with faces $1,2,3,4$ and one with faces $1,3,5,7$. Each die is rolled repeatedly, keeping a running total; if the total exceeds $15$ it resets to $0$, and the process stops when the total equals $15$ exactly. Which die needs fewer rolls on average?
