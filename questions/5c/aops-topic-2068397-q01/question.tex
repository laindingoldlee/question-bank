A student tries to find the hypotenuse $c$ of a right triangle with legs $3$ and $4$ using Heron's formula. With $s=\dfrac{7+c}{2}$ the student writes
\[
c=\sqrt{s(s-3)(s-4)(s-c)}=\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{2}(7+c)(c+1)(c-1)(7-c)}
\]
and gets stuck. Identify the two errors in this setup, and then complete the method correctly to find $c$ using Heron's formula.
