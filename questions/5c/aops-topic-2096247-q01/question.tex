\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] A right triangle has acute angles $40^\circ$ and $50^\circ$. Express the ratio of its sides (shorter leg : longer leg : hypotenuse) using trigonometric functions, and give decimal approximations.
\item[(b)] Prove that $\cos3\theta=4\cos^3\theta-3\cos\theta$, and deduce that $c=\cos40^\circ$ satisfies $8c^3-6c+1=0$.
\end{enumerate}
