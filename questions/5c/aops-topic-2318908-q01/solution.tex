Let $t_n$ be the number of ways to climb $n$ steps. The last move covers $1$, $2$ or $3$ steps, so $t_n=t_{n-1}+t_{n-2}+t_{n-3}$ for $n\ge3$, with $t_0=1$, $t_1=1$, $t_2=2$. Then
\[t_3=4,\ t_4=7,\ t_5=13,\ t_6=24,\ t_7=44,\ t_8=81,\ t_9=149,\ t_{10}=81+149+44=\boxed{274}.\]
