\textbf{Find $f$.} Replacing $x$ by $\dfrac{1}{x}$ in the equation gives a second equation:
\[
f(x)+2f\!\left(\dfrac{1}{x}\right)=3x,\qquad f\!\left(\dfrac{1}{x}\right)+2f(x)=\dfrac{3}{x} .
\]
Treat $f(x)$ and $f\!\left(\dfrac{1}{x}\right)$ as unknowns. Twice the second equation minus the first gives
\[
3f(x)=\dfrac{6}{x}-3x,\qquad\text{so}\qquad f(x)=\dfrac{2}{x}-x .
\]
Check: $f(x)+2f\!\left(\dfrac{1}{x}\right)=\dfrac{2}{x}-x+2\left(2x-\dfrac{1}{x}\right)=3x$.

\textbf{Solve $f(x)=f(-x)$.} We need
\[
\dfrac{2}{x}-x=-\dfrac{2}{x}+x\iff\dfrac{4}{x}=2x\iff x^2=2 .
\]
So $x=\boxed{\pm\sqrt2}$.
