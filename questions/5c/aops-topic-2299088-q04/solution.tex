All $2^{32}$ sequences of flips are equally likely, and $\dbinom{32}{16}=601080390$ of them have exactly $16$ heads. So the probability is
\[\dfrac{\dbinom{32}{16}}{2^{32}}=\dfrac{601080390}{4294967296}=\boxed{\dfrac{300540195}{2147483648}}\approx0.140.\]
