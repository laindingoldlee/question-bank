There are \(6^5=7776\) equally likely outcomes \(\overline{d_1d_2d_3d_4d_5}\) with each \(d_i\in\{1,\dots,6\}\).

\textbf{(a)} The number is divisible by \(11\) iff the alternating sum \(d_1-d_2+d_3-d_4+d_5\) is divisible by \(11\). Let \(u=d_1+d_3+d_5\in[3,18]\) and \(v=d_2+d_4\in[2,12]\); we need \(u-v\in\{-11,0,11\}\) (it lies between \(-9\) and \(16\)), i.e. \(u=v\) or \(u=v+11\).

Let \(a_s\) be the number of ways for three dice to sum to \(s\) and \(b_s\) the number for two dice. For two dice, \(b_s=6-|s-7|\). For three dice, \(a_3,\dots,a_{18}=1,3,6,10,15,21,25,27,27,25,21,15,10,6,3,1\). Then
\[
\sum_{s=3}^{12}a_sb_s=1\cdot2+3\cdot3+6\cdot4+10\cdot5+15\cdot6+21\cdot5+25\cdot4+27\cdot3+27\cdot2+25\cdot1=540,
\]
\[
\sum_{v=2}^{7}a_{v+11}b_v=21\cdot1+15\cdot2+10\cdot3+6\cdot4+3\cdot5+1\cdot6=126 .
\]
So there are \(666\) favourable outcomes and the probability is \(\dfrac{666}{7776}=\boxed{\dfrac{37}{432}}\).

\textbf{(b)} The number is divisible by \(9\) iff the digit sum \(d_1+\cdots+d_5\in[5,30]\) is \(9,18\) or \(27\). The numbers of ways for five dice to total \(9\), \(18\), \(27\) are \(70\), \(780\), \(35\). (By stars and bars with each die at most \(6\): total \(9\): \(\binom{8}{4}=70\); total \(27\) corresponds to total \(35-27=8\) under \(d\mapsto7-d\): \(\binom74=35\); total \(18\): \(\binom{17}{4}-5\binom{11}{4}+\binom{5}{2}\binom{5}{4}=2380-1650+50=780\).) So the probability is
\[
\dfrac{70+780+35}{7776}=\dfrac{885}{7776}=\boxed{\dfrac{295}{2592}}.
\]
