After multiplication the equation is $x^3+y^3-kxy+k^2=0$, with $xy\ne k$. If it contains a line, the cubic polynomial vanishes identically on that line. It cannot contain a vertical line because the coefficient of $y^3$ is $1$. Substituting $y=mx+c$, the coefficient of $x^3$ gives $1+m^3=0$, so $m=-1$. Substitution of $y=-x+c$ then gives coefficients $3c+k$ of $x^2$ and $c^3+k^2$ as constant. Therefore $c=-\dfrac{k}{3}$ and $k^2(1-\dfrac{k}{27})=0$. Positivity forces $k=27$. Now\[x^3+y^3-27xy+729=(x+y+9)(x^2+y^2+81-xy-9x-9y).\]The second factor is $\dfrac{1}{2}((x-y)^2+(y-9)^2+(9-x)^2)$, so it vanishes only at $(9,9)$. On the line $x+y=-9$, $xy\le\dfrac{81}{4}<27$; at the isolated point $xy=81$. Thus no point is removed by the original denominator restriction. The distance is $\dfrac{|9+9+9|}{\sqrt2}=\dfrac{27}{\sqrt2}$, yielding $\boxed{a+b=29}$.
