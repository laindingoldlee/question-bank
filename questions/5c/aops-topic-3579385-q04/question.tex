Exactly one positive real $k$ makes the graph of $\dfrac{x^3+y^3}{xy-k}=k$ consist of an entire line and one point not on it. Determine $k$ and then write the distance from that point to the line as $\dfrac{a}{\sqrt b}$ with positive integers $a,b$ and squarefree $b$. Find $a+b$.
