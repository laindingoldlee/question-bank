The condition says that every empty chair is next to an occupied chair. Each seated person accounts for at most $3$ chairs: their own and the two next to it. Every chair is occupied or next to an occupied chair, so $3N\ge60$, and $N\ge20$.

$N=20$ works: seat people in chairs $2,5,8,\ldots,59$ (every third chair). Then each empty chair, numbered $3k$ or $3k+1$, is next to an occupied chair $3k-1$ or $3k+2$ (chair $60$ is next to chair $59$, and chair $1$ is next to chair $2$). So the smallest value is $N=\boxed{20}$.
