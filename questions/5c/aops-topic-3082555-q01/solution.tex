The arc angle is $\dfrac{12\pi}{18}=\dfrac{2\pi}{3}=120^\circ $. Bisecting the isosceles triangle formed by the two radii gives half the chord as $ 18\sin60^\circ=9\sqrt3 $. Thus $\boxed{AC=18\sqrt3}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair O=(0,0),A=18*dir(30),C=18*dir(150),M=(A+C)/2;draw(circle(O,18));draw(O--A--C--cycle^^O--M,dashed);label("$O$",O,S);label("$A$",A,E);label("$C$",C,W);label("$M$",M,N);label("$18$",A/2,SE);
\end{asy}
