$A$, $B$, $C$, $D$, $E$ are the five digits $5$, $6$, $7$, $8$, $9$ in some order. They form the three-digit number $\overline{ABC}$ and the two-digit number $\overline{DE}$. What is the largest possible value of the product $\overline{ABC}\times\overline{DE}$? Find it without checking all the cases.
