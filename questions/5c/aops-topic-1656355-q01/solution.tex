\textbf{Small values.} $x=0$: both sides are $0$. $x=1$: $1\cdot3=3=3\cdot1$. $x=-1$: $-1\cdot\dfrac{3}{2}=-\dfrac{3}{2}=3\cdot\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)$. So $x=-1,0,1$ are solutions.

\textbf{Symmetry.} Replace $x$ by $-x$ and multiply both sides by $2^x$:
\[
-x\left(2^{-x}+1\right)=3\left(2^{-x}-1\right)\iff -x\left(1+2^x\right)=3\left(1-2^x\right)\iff x\left(2^x+1\right)=3\left(2^x-1\right).
\]
So $x$ is a solution exactly when $-x$ is, and it suffices to treat $x\ge2$.

\textbf{No solutions for $x\ge2$.} The equation is equivalent to $(x-3)2^x+x+3=0$. For $x=2$ the left side is $-4+5=1>0$; for $x\ge3$ it is at least $x+3>0$. So there is no solution with $x\ge2$, and by symmetry none with $x\le-2$.

The solutions are $\boxed{x\in\{-1,0,1\}}$.
