At every intersection, $(x^2+y^2)^2=48+2(2^2)=56$, so $x^2+y^2=2\sqrt{14}$. Set $u=x+y$ and $v=x-y$. Then $u^2=2\sqrt{14}+4$ and $v^2=2\sqrt{14}-4$. All four choices of signs occur. In the $uv$-plane these are the corners of a rectangle with area $4\sqrt{(2\sqrt{14}+4)(2\sqrt{14}-4)}=8\sqrt{10}$. The transformation $(x,y)\mapsto(u,v)$ doubles areas, since its determinant has absolute value $2$. Thus the required area is $\boxed{4\sqrt{10}}$.
