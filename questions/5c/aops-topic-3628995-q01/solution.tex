Write the smallest member as $6k$. The sum is $13(6k)+0+1+\cdots+12=78k+78=78(k+1)$, always divisible by $78$. Consecutive choices of $k$ give consecutive multiples of $78$, whose greatest common divisor is $78$. Thus the greatest guaranteed divisor is $\boxed{78}$.
