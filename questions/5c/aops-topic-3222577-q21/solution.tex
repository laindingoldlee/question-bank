Choose one of two alternating gender patterns and arrange the three men in $3!$ ways. For pattern $M_1,W_a,M_2,W_b,M_3,W_c$, the first woman cannot be partner 1 or 2, so must be partner 3. The middle woman cannot be partner 2 or 3, so must be partner 1. The final woman is then partner 2 and is not beside partner 2. Thus each men's order has exactly one permitted women's order. Reflection gives the same count for the other pattern. Total $2\cdot3!=\boxed{12}$.
