Let the counts be $ 17k,15k $. Then $ 7(17k-90)=8(15k-80)$, so $ 119k-630=120k-640 $ and $ k=10 $. The total is $ 32k=\boxed{320}$; the reduced counts $ 80,70 $ indeed have ratio $ 8:7 $.
