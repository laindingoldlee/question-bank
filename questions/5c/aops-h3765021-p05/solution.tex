Each type must contain at least 16 trees; otherwise 85 could be chosen while omitting a type. Consequently any two types together contain at most $100-16-16=68$ trees, so any 69 trees contain at least three types. The distribution $34,34,16,16$ satisfies the original condition and permits 68 trees from just two types. Thus the least universally guaranteed value is $\boxed{69}$.
