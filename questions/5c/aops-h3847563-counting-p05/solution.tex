Y targets Z because Z is certain to kill Y if left alive; Z targets Y because Y is more dangerous than X. If X deliberately misses, Y eliminates Z with probability $\dfrac{2}{3}$, leaving X to shoot first against Y. X\textquotesingle s chance there solves $p=\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot\dfrac{1}{3} p$, so $p=\dfrac{3}{7}$. If Y misses, Z eliminates Y and X has one chance, $\dfrac{1}{3}$, against Z. Thus missing gives $\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot\dfrac{3}{7}+\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{1}{3}=\dfrac{25}{63}$. Shooting Z instead gives $\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{1}{7}+\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot\dfrac{25}{63}=\dfrac{59}{189}$; shooting Y gives $\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot\dfrac{25}{63}=\dfrac{50}{189}$. Both are lower, so $\boxed{\text{deliberately miss; probability }\dfrac{25}{63}}$.
