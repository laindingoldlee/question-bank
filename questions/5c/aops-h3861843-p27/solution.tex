For $N$ uniform independent points on the unit circle, a cyclic angular gap divided by $2\pi$ has density $(N-1)(1-t)^{N-2}$ on $0<t<1$. Summing signed center-triangle areas therefore gives $E_N=\dfrac{N(N-1)}{2}\int_0^1\sin(2\pi t)(1-t)^{N-2}\,dt$. Expanding the sine and integrating each term yields $E_N=\pi\left(1-\dfrac{(2\pi)^2}{(N+1)(N+2)}+\dfrac{(2\pi)^4}{(N+1)(N+2)(N+3)(N+4)}-\cdots\right)$. Alternating-series bounds give $E_{277}<3.14<E_{278}$, with $E_{278}\approx3.14000582118$. Adding another point can only enlarge the convex hull, so $E_N$ is nondecreasing. Hence $\boxed{N=278}$.
