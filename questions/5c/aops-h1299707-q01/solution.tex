Let the side of $ACEG$ be $a$. The four attached isosceles triangles have equal sides and the same base $a$, so they have a common height $h$. Octagon area is $a^2+2ah$. Thus $\dfrac{a^2}{a^2+2ah}=\dfrac{7}{9}$, giving $h=\dfrac{a}{7}$. Square $BDFH$ has diagonal $a+2h=\dfrac{9a}{7}$ and area $\dfrac{81a^2}{98}$. Dividing by octagon area $\dfrac{9}{7}a^2$ gives $\boxed{\dfrac{9}{14}}$.
