The variable $x$ is directly proportional to $y$, and $x$ is also inversely proportional to $z$. It is known that $y=25$ when $z=8$, and $y=40$ when $z=5$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that $yz$ is constant, and find $y$ when $z=20$.
\item[(b)] Explain why the value of $x$ when $z=20$ cannot be determined from this information.
\end{enumerate}
