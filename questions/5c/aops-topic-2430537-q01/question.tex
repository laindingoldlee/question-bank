In a cross-section through its axis, a sharpened pencil is a pentagon $TUPQV$: a rectangle $UPQV$ with an isosceles triangle $TUV$ (the tip) attached along the side $UV$, with apex angle $\angle UTV=a$. Two identical pencils lie side by side with their tips touching along a whole slanted edge: in the cross-section, the second pentagon is the reflection of the first in the line $TU$. Prove that the angle $\angle PUP'$ between the long sides $UP$ and $UP'$ of the two pencils, outside both pentagons, equals $a$, so that the tip of a third identical pencil fits exactly into this angle.
