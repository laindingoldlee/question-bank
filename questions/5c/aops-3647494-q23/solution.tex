Write the slope in lowest terms as $\dfrac{p}{q}$, where $q>0$. Parallel lattice lines have equations $qy-px=m$ with integer $m$. Across the open unit square, the linear expression ranges over an open interval with integer endpoints and length $|p|+q$, so exactly $|p|+q-1$ such lines meet its interior. Thus $|p|+q=1001$. The positive coprime choices are counted by $\varphi(1001)=1001(1-\dfrac{1}{7})(1-\dfrac{1}{11})(1-\dfrac{1}{13})=720$. Either sign of $p$ is allowed, giving $\boxed{1440}$ slopes.

\end{asy}
