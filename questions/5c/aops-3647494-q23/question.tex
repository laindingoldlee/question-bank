Let $k$ be a rational number. In the coordinate plane, Celine draws a line of slope $k$ through every lattice point. If exactly 1000 distinct lines pass through the interior of the square with vertices $(0, 0)$, $(1, 0)$, $(1, 1)$, and $(0, 1)$, how many possible values of $k$ are there? (The interior of a square does not  contain its boundary.)
