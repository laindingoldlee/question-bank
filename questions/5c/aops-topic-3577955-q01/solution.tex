Since $4096=64^2$, we have $x=64^{14+8\sqrt3}$. Hence $a=\dfrac{1}{14+8\sqrt3}=\dfrac{14-8\sqrt3}{196-192}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{2}-2\sqrt3}$.
