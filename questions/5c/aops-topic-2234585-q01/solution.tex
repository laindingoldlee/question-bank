Write each number from $0$ to $99$ with two digits, $00,01,\ldots,99$. Each of the digits $0,\ldots,9$ appears $10$ times as a tens digit and $10$ times as a units digit, so the digit sums of $00,\ldots,99$ add up to
\[2\cdot10\cdot(0+1+\cdots+9)=900 .\]
The number $00$ contributes $0$, so the $99$ numbers from $1$ to $99$ also have digit sums adding up to $900$, and the mean is
\[\dfrac{900}{99}=\boxed{\dfrac{100}{11}}.\]
