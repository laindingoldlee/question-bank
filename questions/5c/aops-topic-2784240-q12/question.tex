Milo forgot his mother's $9$-digit phone number, but he remembers that it contains only the digits $1$ and $2$, and that all of its $1$s are to the left of all of its $2$s (there may be no $1$s or no $2$s). In the worst case, how many numbers must he dial to reach his mother, counting the successful call?
