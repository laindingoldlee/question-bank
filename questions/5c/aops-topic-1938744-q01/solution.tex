$1+625+1296=1922$, so $a^4+b^4=97$. The fourth powers below $97$ are $1,16,81$, and $97=81+16$ is the only way to write $97$ as a sum of two of them. So $\{a,b\}=\{3,2\}$ and $a+b=\boxed{5}$.
