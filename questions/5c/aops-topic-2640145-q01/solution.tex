Let $M=907-x$ and $L=\operatorname{lcm}(N,N+2)$. Divide: $M=qL+r$ with $0\le r<L$. Both $N$ and $N+2$ divide $M$ and $L$, hence they divide $r=M-qL$. So $r$ is a common multiple of $N$ and $N+2$ that is smaller than the least positive common multiple $L$; therefore $r$ is not positive, i.e. $r=0$, and $L\mid M$. $\blacksquare$

(Concretely, $\gcd(N,N+2)$ divides $2$, so $L=N(N+2)$ for odd $N$ and $L=\dfrac{N(N+2)}{2}$ for even $N$.)
