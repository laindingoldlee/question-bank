Bob's age is a two-digit number when written in base $n$: it equals $na+b$, where $a$ and $b$ are base-$n$ digits with $1\le a\le n-1$ and $0\le b\le n-1$. The base $n\ge2$ is a perfect cube, the digits satisfy $a+b=9$, and
\[na+b=ab+a+b,\]
that is, Bob's age equals the sum of its two digits plus their product. How old is Bob (in base $10$)?
