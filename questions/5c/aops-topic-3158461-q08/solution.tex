Put $ t=ab>1 $. Polynomial division gives $\dfrac{t^2+1}{t-1}=t+1+\dfrac{2}{t-1}$. Thus $ t-1 $ is a positive divisor of 2, so $ t=2 $ or 3. The pairs are $\boxed{(1,2),(2,1),(1,3),(3,1)}$.
