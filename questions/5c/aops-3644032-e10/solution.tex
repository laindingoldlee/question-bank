For each three-digit number $n$ from 500 through 999, double it and check that the seven digit positions in $n$ and $2n$ are all distinct. This is exactly the alphametic condition, since the two occurrences of TWO are identical. The largest admissible double is $2\cdot936=\boxed{1872}$, using the distinct digits $9,3,6,1,8,7,2$. Exhaustively checking the 63 candidates above 936 rules out any larger result; the computation audit performs this finite search.
