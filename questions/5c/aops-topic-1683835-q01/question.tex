Let $b$ and $d$ be nonzero numbers. Prove that $\dfrac {a}{b}=\dfrac {c}{d}$ if and only if $ad=bc$. Use this to check that $\dfrac{5}{8}$ and $\dfrac{20}{32}$ are equivalent fractions.
