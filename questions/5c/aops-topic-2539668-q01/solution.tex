Since $547^2=299209$, we have $547^2+2^2=299213=\left(\sqrt{299213}\right)^2$. By the converse of the Pythagorean theorem the triangle has a right angle between the sides $547$ and $2$, so its area is $\dfrac12\cdot547\cdot2=\boxed{547}$.

label("$547$",(B+C)/2,S);label("$2$",(A+C)/2,W);label("$\sqrt{299213}$",(A+B)/2,NE);
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,E);label("$C$",C,SW);

\end{asy}
