Factor the denominator as $(n-2)(n-1)n(n+1)(n+2)$. Then
\[\dfrac{1}{(n-2)(n-1)n(n+1)(n+2)}=\dfrac{1}{4}\left(\dfrac{1}{(n-2)(n-1)n(n+1)}-\dfrac{1}{(n-1)n(n+1)(n+2)}\right).\]
The sum through $n=N$ telescopes to $\dfrac{1}{4}\left(\dfrac{1}{1\cdot2\cdot3\cdot4}-\dfrac{1}{(N-1)N(N+1)(N+2)}\right)$. Taking $N\to\infty$ gives $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{96}}$.
