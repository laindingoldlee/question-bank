We need the real roots of $f$. Since $f(1)=1+6-41-6+40=0$ and $f(-1)=1-6-41+6+40=0$, $f$ has the factor $x^2-1$, and dividing,
\[f(x)=\left(x^2-1\right)\left(x^2+6x-40\right)=(x-1)(x+1)(x+10)(x-4).\]
The roots in increasing order are $-10,-1,1,4$, so the fish is eaten at $x=\boxed{-1},\ \boxed{1},\ \boxed{4}$.
