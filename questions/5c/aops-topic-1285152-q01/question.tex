Using the formula for the sum of a geometric series, prove that for $a\ne b$ and every positive integer $n$,
\[
a^n-b^n=(a-b)\left(a^{n-1}+a^{n-2}b+\cdots+ab^{n-2}+b^{n-1}\right),
\]
and deduce the factorisation $a^3-b^3=(a-b)(a^2+ab+b^2)$ without expanding the product. Then explain why the identity also holds when $a=b$.
