On a certain island every day is either sunny or rainy. Every sunny day is immediately preceded by two consecutive rainy days, and five days after any rainy day it is again a rainy day. It is sunny today. For how many of the coming days (tomorrow, the day after, and so on, consecutively) can the weather be predicted with certainty?
