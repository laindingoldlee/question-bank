A $1\times10$ strip has squares numbered $1$ to $10$, with a coin on square $1$. Steve and Pete take turns, Steve first; a turn moves the coin forward $1$, $2$ or $3$ squares. Whoever places the coin on square $10$ wins. How should Steve play to win whatever Pete does?
