Squares $10$, $6$ and $2$ (those with $10-n$ divisible by $4$) are the ones to land on. Steve first moves the coin to square $2$. Whenever Pete moves it forward $k$ squares ($k=1,2,3$), Steve moves it $4-k$ squares. So after each of Steve's turns the coin is on $2$, then $6$, then $10$, and Steve wins.
