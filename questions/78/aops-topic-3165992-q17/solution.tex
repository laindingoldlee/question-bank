For the larger positive root, both $x+\dfrac{7}{x}$ and $x-\dfrac{7}{x}$ are positive. Squaring gives $(x+\dfrac{7}{x})^2=2032$ and $(x-\dfrac{7}{x})^2=2004$. Adding their positive roots gives $2x=\sqrt{2032}+\sqrt{2004}$, so $x=\sqrt{508}+\sqrt{501}$. Thus $\boxed{a+b=1009}$.
