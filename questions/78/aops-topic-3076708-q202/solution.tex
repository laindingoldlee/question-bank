Take finite sets $A,B$ of sizes $a,b$. The pairs in $A\times B$ can be counted as $a$ groups of $b$, giving $ab$. Swapping coordinates is a bijection from $A\times B$ to $B\times A$, whose size is $ba$. Therefore $ab=ba$, including zero-sized sets.
