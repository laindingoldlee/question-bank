Scale $AC$ to $1$ and set $A=(0,0),C=(1,0),B=(u,v)$ with $v>0$. The right angle gives $v^2=u(1-u)$ and $AB=\sqrt u$. By the angle bisector theorem $BP:PC=\sqrt u:1$, so $P=\dfrac{B+\sqrt u\,C}{1+\sqrt u}$ and its first coordinate is $\sqrt u$. Since $M$ is the midpoint of $AP$, its first coordinate is $\dfrac{\sqrt u}{2}$. But $M$ lies on $BD$, whose first coordinate is $u$. Therefore $2u=\sqrt u$. Since $u>0$, $u=\dfrac{1}{4}$, which is the required ratio.
\begin{asy}
import graph;
usepackage("amsmath");
import geometry;
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0), C=(6,0), B=(1.5,2.598), D=(1.5,0), P=(3,1.732), M=(1.5,.866); draw(A--B--C--cycle); draw(B--D,dashed); draw(A--P); dot(M); label("$A$",A,(-1,-1));label("$B$",B,(0,1));label("$C$",C,(1,-1));label("$D$",D,(0,-1));label("$P$",P,(1,1));label("$M$",M,(-1,0));
\end{asy}
