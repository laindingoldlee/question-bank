January has $31=4\cdot7+3$ days, so the weekdays of January $1$, $2$ and $3$ occur five times and the other four weekdays occur four times. Tuesday and Saturday occur four times, so the three consecutive weekdays of January $1$--$3$ contain neither Tuesday nor Saturday. The only three consecutive weekdays avoiding both are Wednesday, Thursday, Friday. So January $1$ was a $\boxed{\text{Wednesday}}$, answer $\textbf{(C)}$.
