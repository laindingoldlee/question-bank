The key identity, checked by expanding both sides, is
\[
(a-c)(b-d)=(a-b)(c-d)+(a-d)(b-c).
\]
(Indeed both sides equal $ab-ad-bc+cd$.) Dividing by $(a-b)(c-d)$, which is non-zero by the hypothesis,
\[
\dfrac{(a-c)(b-d)}{(a-b)(c-d)}=1+\dfrac{(a-d)(b-c)}{(a-b)(c-d)}=1-\dfrac{(b-c)(d-a)}{(a-b)(c-d)}=1-\dfrac{2013}{2012}=\boxed{-\dfrac{1}{2012}}.
\]
