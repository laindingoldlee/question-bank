One February 14th, Barbara sent a valentine to a boy, who sent his to another girl, who sent hers to another boy, and so on, until the eighth child sent a valentine to Barbara, completing the circle. The four boys are Chuck, Dave, Matt and Roger; the four girls are Barbara, Jean, Pam and Wendy. Their surnames are Baxter, Clayborne, Douglas, Johnson, Manning, Putnam, Rockwell and Walker, and they live on Ash, Elm, Maple and Oak Streets. Use the clues to find each child's full name, street, and the child to whom each sent a valentine.
\begin{enumerate}
\item One boy and one girl live on each street, and no child sent a valentine to someone living on the same street.
\item Matt and the Walker child both live on Oak Street.
\item Roger sent his valentine to a child who lives on Maple Street, who sent hers to the Putnam child.
\item Jean and the Douglas boy live on Ash Street.
\item The Baxter boy sent a valentine to Pam.
\item Chuck and the Manning child both live on Maple Street.
\item Dave sent his valentine to the Clayborne child, who sent hers to a child from Oak Street.
\item Wendy and the Putnam child live on Elm Street.
\item The Johnson child sent a valentine to the Rockwell girl.
\end{enumerate}
