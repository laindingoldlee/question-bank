The powers of $3$ modulo $7$ repeat with period $6$: $1,3,2,6,4,5$, and one full period sums to $21\equiv0$. Since $100=16\cdot6+4$, the sum is congruent to the first four terms of a period:
\[
1+3+2+6=12\equiv\boxed{5}\pmod 7.
\]
(Alternatively the sum is $\dfrac{3^{100}-1}{2}$, and $3^{100}\equiv3^4\equiv4$, so $2S\equiv3$ and $S\equiv5$.)
