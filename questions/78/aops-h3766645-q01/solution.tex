The two divisibilities and coprimality imply
\[
1155=a+b+tab
\]
for an integer $t$. Since $ab>a+b$ for coprime $a,b>1$, a negative $t$ would make the right side negative. Thus $t\ge0$. If $t=0$, the sum is $1155$, attained by $(a,b)=(2,1153)$.

For $t\ge1$, interchange $a,b$ so $a\le b$. Then $(a+1)^2\le(a+1)(b+1)\le1156$, hence $a\le33$. Also $\gcd(a,1155)=1$, since any common divisor would divide $b$. The candidates are
\[
a=2,4,8,13,16,17,19,23,26,29,31,32.
\]
For each, $b$ is a divisor of $1155-a$ satisfying $b\ge a$, $a+b<1155$, and $b\equiv1155\pmod a$. Factoring these twelve small numbers gives:
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrrrrrrr}
a&2&4&8&13&16&17&19&23&26&29&31&32\\ \hline
\text{qualifying }b&-&-&-&-&67&-&-&-&-&-&-&-
\end{array}
\]
Their factorizations are $1153$, $1151$, $31\cdot37$, $2\cdot571$, $17\cdot67$, $2\cdot569$, $16\cdot71$, $4\cdot283$, $1129$, $2\cdot563$, $4\cdot281$, $1123$, respectively; the single factors shown are prime. The pair $(16,67)$ is coprime and gives sum $83$. Thus the two sums are $1155,83$, whose average is $\boxed{619}$.
