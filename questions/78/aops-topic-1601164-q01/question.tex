Alison has the list of integers $1,2,\dots,100$ and a fair six-sided die. She rolls the die up to three times. After each roll she changes the list as follows, applying every rule that fits the number rolled, in this order:
\begin{itemize}
\item if she rolls a $4$, she stops immediately: the list is not changed and she rolls no more;
\item otherwise, if the roll is a perfect square (that is, $1$), she erases every prime number;
\item if the roll is odd, she erases every perfect power (every number $m^k$ with integers $m\ge1$ and $k\ge2$, so $1$ counts);
\item if the roll is prime, she erases every number that is a multiple of $2$, $3$ or $5$;
\item if she rolls a $6$, she writes every perfect square from $1$ to $100$ back on the list (if it is not already there).
\end{itemize}
What is the expected number of integers on the list when she has finished? Round your answer to the nearest whole number.
