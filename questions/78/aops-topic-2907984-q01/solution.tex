With $n$ parts, AM-GM gives product $\le\left(\dfrac{25}n\right)^n$, with equality for equal parts. The function $g(n)=n\ln\dfrac{25}n$ is concave with maximum at $n=\dfrac{25}e\approx9.2$, so compare $n=9$ and $n=10$: $\left(\dfrac{25}9\right)^9\approx9846$ versus $2.5^{10}\approx9537$. The maximum is $\boxed{\left(\dfrac{25}9\right)^9}\approx9846$, with nine parts of $\dfrac{25}9$.
