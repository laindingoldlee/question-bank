Let $a_1,\dots,a_5\ge1$ be the numbers of the five kinds, with $a_1+\cdots+a_5=10$. Put $b_i=a_i-1\ge0$; then $b_1+\cdots+b_5=5$. By stars and bars ($5$ stars and $4$ bars), the number of solutions is
\[
\dbinom{9}{4}=\boxed{126}.
\]
(Equivalently, place $4$ dividers in the $9$ gaps between $10$ items in a row.)
