Prove that for every positive integer $n$,
\[
\underbrace{55\cdots5}_{n}+\bigl(\underbrace{33\cdots3}_{n}\bigr)^2=\underbrace{11\cdots1}_{n}\underbrace{44\cdots4}_{n},
\]
where the right side is the $2n$-digit number formed by $n$ ones followed by $n$ fours.
