Let $R=\underbrace{11\cdots1}_{n}=\dfrac{10^n-1}{9}$, so $10^n=9R+1$. Then the left side is
\[
5R+(3R)^2=9R^2+5R .
\]
The right side is $R\cdot10^n+4R=R(9R+1)+4R=9R^2+5R$. The two sides are equal, which proves the identity. (For example, $n=2$: $55+33^2=55+1089=1144$.)
