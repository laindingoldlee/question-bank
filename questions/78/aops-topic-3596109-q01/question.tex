An $8\times8$ grid has entry $1+ia+jb$ in row $j+1$, column $i+1$, for $0\le i,j\le7$, where $a,b$ are positive integers. The bottom-right entry is less than $75$. How many such grids contain $45$ in column $5$? Choices: (A) $6$; (B) $3$; (C) $7$; (D) $5$; (E) $4$.
