An isosceles right triangle with legs $1$ has hypotenuse $\sqrt2$, so $\sin45^\circ=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}$. Multiplying numerator and denominator by $\sqrt2$ gives $\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$; the two expressions are equal.

\end{asy}
