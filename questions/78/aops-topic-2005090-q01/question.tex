Right triangle $ABC$ with right angle at $A$ has perimeter $30$ and area $35$. Triangle $ABC$ is reflected over the bisector of angle $A$ to obtain triangle $AB'C'$. Prove that the area of the intersection of triangles $ABC$ and $AB'C'$ is greater than $16$.
