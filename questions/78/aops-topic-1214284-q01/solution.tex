Outside the square it consists of four $5\times1$ rectangles and four quarter discs of radius $1$ at the corners: $25+4\cdot5+\pi=45+\pi$ together with the square itself. Inside the square, the points farther than $1$ km from every side form a $3\times3$ square (dashed), which Bob cannot see. So the area is
\[(45+\pi)-9=\boxed{36+\pi}\ \text{km}^2\approx 39.1\ \text{km}^2.\]
