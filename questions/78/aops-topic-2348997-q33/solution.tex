$2020=2^2\cdot5\cdot101$ has $3\cdot2\cdot2=12$ divisors:
\[1,2,4,5,10,20,101,202,404,505,1010,2020.\]
The non-palindromes are $10,20,1010,2020$, so the probability is $\dfrac4{12}=\boxed{\dfrac13}$.
