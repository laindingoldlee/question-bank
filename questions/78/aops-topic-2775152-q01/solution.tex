The $28$ dominoes have $56$ ends. Each value $k\in\{0,\dots,6\}$ appears on the double $k$-$k$ (twice) and on the $6$ dominoes pairing it with another value, so $8$ times. The total is $8(0+1+\dots+6)=8\cdot21=\boxed{168}$.
