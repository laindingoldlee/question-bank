The first $n$ blocks contain $\dfrac{n(n+1)}2$ terms. Since $\dfrac{63\cdot64}2=2016<2023<2080=\dfrac{64\cdot65}2$, term $2023$ is the $2023-2016=7$th term of block $64$, namely $\boxed{7}$.
