Use $A=(0,0)$, $B=(14,0)$, and let $BC$ end at $C=(u,v)$ above the axis. The perpendicular side directions and convex order give $D=(u,v+3)$, $E=(u-12,v+3)$, $F=(0,5)$. The two length-$8$ conditions are $(u-14)^2+v^2=64$ and $(u-12)^2+(v-2)^2=64$. Subtracting gives $u-v=12$. Hence $(v-2)^2+v^2=64$, so positive $v=1+\sqrt{31}$ and $u=13+\sqrt{31}$. The shoelace formula gives area $13v+4u-12=\boxed{53+17\sqrt{31}}$. The displayed coordinates form a convex hexagon, so the solution is feasible.
