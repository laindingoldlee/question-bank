Mark starts with a number, multiplies it by $2$, divides by $3$, multiplies by $4$, divides by $5$, and so on, alternately multiplying by the even numbers and dividing by the odd numbers, ending by multiplying by $100$. The result is $200$. What number did he start with? (Give an exact expression and a decimal approximation.)
