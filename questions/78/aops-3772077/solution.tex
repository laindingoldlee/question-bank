Choose $A=(0,0),B=(a,0),C=(a,a),D=(0,a)$ and $M=(m,0)$ with $0<m<a$. Since $MB\parallel NC$, write $N=(n,a)$. Equal trapezoid legs give $(n-a)^2+a^2=(a-m)^2+a^2$. The exterior condition $n>a$ gives $n=2a-m$. The distance from $N$ to line $CM$ is the magnitude of the determinant of $(N-C),(M-C)$ divided by $CM$, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{a (a-m)}{\sqrt{a^2+(a-m)^2}}}$. It varies with $m$, so the original data specifying only $a$ do not give one numerical multiple of $a$.
\begin{asy}
import graph;
usepackage("amsmath");
import geometry;
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(4,0),C=(4,4),D=(0,4),M=(1,0),N=(7,4);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(M--B--N--C--M);label("$A$",A,(-1,-1));label("$B$",B,(0,-1));label("$C$",C,(0,1));label("$D$",D,(-1,1));label("$M$",M,(0,-1));label("$N$",N,(1,0));
\end{asy}
