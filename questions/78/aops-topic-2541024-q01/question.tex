For a positive integer $n$, let
\[N_n=1\cdot2\cdot4+2\cdot4\cdot8+3\cdot6\cdot12+\cdots+n\cdot2n\cdot4n,\qquad D_n=1\cdot3\cdot9+2\cdot6\cdot18+3\cdot9\cdot27+\cdots+n\cdot3n\cdot9n.\]
Find $\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{N_n}{D_n}}$.
