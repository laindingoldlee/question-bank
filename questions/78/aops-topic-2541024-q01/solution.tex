The $k$th term of $N_n$ is $k\cdot2k\cdot4k=8k^3$ and the $k$th term of $D_n$ is $k\cdot3k\cdot9k=27k^3$. So, with $T=1^3+2^3+\cdots+n^3$,
\[\dfrac{N_n}{D_n}=\dfrac{8T}{27T}=\dfrac{8}{27},\qquad\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{N_n}{D_n}}=\boxed{\dfrac23}\]
for every $n$.
