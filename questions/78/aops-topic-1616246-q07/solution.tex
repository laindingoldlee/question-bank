Put the cube as $[0,1]^3$ and look at the piece at the corner $(0,0,0)$. Each cone has base radius $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and height $\dfrac{1}{2}$, so its slant sides make $45^\circ$ with its axis. For instance the cone on the face $z=0$ consists of the points with
\[
\sqrt{\left(x-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^2+\left(y-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^2}\le\dfrac{1}{2}-z .
\]
Two neighbouring cones touch along a segment (for example the cones on $z=0$ and $y=0$ both contain the segment from $\left(\dfrac{1}{2},0,0\right)$ to the centre), and these contacts cut the free space into the $8$ corner pieces.

The corner piece is bounded by the three faces through $(0,0,0)$ and by the three cones on those faces, and it is symmetric under permuting the coordinates, so the largest sphere is centred on the diagonal, at $(t,t,t)$. Its distance to each of the three faces is $t$. Its distance to the cone on $z=0$ is found in the plane through the cone's axis and $(t,t,t)$, the plane $x=y$ drawn below (the rectangle $PQQ'P'$ is the section of the cube; $P=(0,0,0)$, $Q=(1,1,0)$; the other four cones are not drawn). In that plane the point has distance $\rho=\sqrt2\left(\dfrac{1}{2}-t\right)$ from the axis and height $z=t$, and the slant side of the cone is the line $\rho+z=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Hence the distance from the centre to that cone is
\[
\dfrac{\rho+z-\dfrac{1}{2}}{\sqrt2}=\dfrac{\left(\sqrt2-1\right)\left(\dfrac{1}{2}-t\right)}{\sqrt2}=\left(1-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\right)\left(\dfrac{1}{2}-t\right).
\]
(The foot of this perpendicular lies on the cone's side, between its base circle and the apex.) By symmetry the distances to the other two cones are the same.

The dotted box marks the corner shown enlarged below: the section of the sphere, with centre $S$, touches the bottom face at $U$ and the side of the cone at $T$, with $SU=ST=r$.

As $t$ grows, the distance $t$ to the faces grows and the distance to the cones shrinks, so the largest sphere has these two distances equal, and its radius is $r=t$:
\[
t=\left(1-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\right)\left(\dfrac{1}{2}-t\right)\iff t\left(2-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\right)=\dfrac{1}{2}\left(1-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\right)\iff t=\dfrac{\sqrt2-1}{2\left(2\sqrt2-1\right)}=\dfrac{\left(\sqrt2-1\right)\left(2\sqrt2+1\right)}{14}=\dfrac{3-\sqrt2}{14}.
\]
Since $(3-\sqrt2)^3=27-27\sqrt2+18-2\sqrt2=45-29\sqrt2$, the volume is
\[
\dfrac{4}{3}\pi\cdot\dfrac{45-29\sqrt2}{14^3}=\dfrac{4\left(45-29\sqrt2\right)\pi}{8232}=\boxed{\dfrac{\left(45-29\sqrt2\right)\pi}{2058}}\approx0.00609 .
\]
