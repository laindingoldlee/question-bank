$AH+HC=4$ implies $AH=3,HC=1$. The right triangle $ABH$ gives $BH^2=16-9=7$. The right triangle $BHC$ then gives $BC^2=7+1=8$, so $\boxed{BC=2\sqrt2}$.

\end{asy}
