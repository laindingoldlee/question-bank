At the MP Donut Hole Factory, Niraek, Theo and Akshaj are coating spherical doughnut holes in powdered sugar. Niraek's doughnut holes have radius $6$ mm, Theo's have radius $8$ mm and Akshaj's have radius $10$ mm. All three workers coat the surface of the doughnut holes at the same rate and start at the same time. Assuming that the powdered sugar coating has negligible thickness and is distributed equally on all doughnut holes, how many doughnut holes will Niraek have covered by the first time all three workers finish their current doughnut hole at the same time?
