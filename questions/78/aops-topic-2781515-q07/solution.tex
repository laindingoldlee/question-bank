Count the $4^3=64$ equally likely direction sequences, even extending unused directions after absorption. Exactly 16 keep all three positions strictly inside the square: after the first step the two possible interior positions are $(2,3),(1,2)$, and each has 8 surviving two-step continuations. Thus the stop probability is $1-\dfrac{16}{64}=\dfrac{3}{4}$ and $\boxed7$.

\end{asy}
