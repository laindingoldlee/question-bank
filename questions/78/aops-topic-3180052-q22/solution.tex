Up to scaling and reflection, take $B=(0,0),M=(1,0),A=(2,0),C=(c,d)$ with $d>0$. Then $X=(c-1,d)$. The circle through $A,M$ has equation $x^2+y^2-3x+vy+2=0$. Subtract its equations at $C,X$ to get $2c-4=0$, so $c=2$. Since $AC=AB=2$, $d=2$. Thus $X=(1,2)$ and intersection $Y=(\dfrac{4}{3},\dfrac{4}{3})$. In this normalized picture, $BY=\dfrac{4\sqrt2}{3}$ and $AY=\dfrac{2\sqrt5}{3}$, so $\dfrac{AY^2}{BY^2}=\dfrac{5}{8}$. Scaling to $BY=8$ gives $\boxed{AY^2=40}$.

\end{asy}
