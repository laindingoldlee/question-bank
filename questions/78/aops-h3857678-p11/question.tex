The white cat drinks water at a rate \(5\) times the black cat's rate, and drinks milk at a rate \(3\) times the black cat's rate.



On the first day, a bowl of water and a plate of milk are placed in front of the cats. The white cat drinks the water while the black cat drinks the milk at the same time.



The white cat finishes the water and then drinks the milk together with the black cat. After a total of \(18\) minutes, the milk is finished.



On the second day, the white cat drinks milk and the black cat drinks water. The white cat finishes the milk first and then drinks water together with the black cat.



After a total of \(N\) minutes, the water is finished. Find \(N\).



\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{a)}\ 14 & \text{b)}\ 16 \\
\text{c)}\ 18 & \text{d)}\ 20 \\
\text{e)}\ 22
\end{array}
