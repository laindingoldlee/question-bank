Using $c^2+d^2=1$ and then $ac=-bd$,
\[
a^2=a^2(c^2+d^2)=(ac)^2+a^2d^2=(bd)^2+a^2d^2=d^2(a^2+b^2)=d^2 .
\]
So $a^2=d^2$, and then
\[
b^2=1-a^2=1-d^2=c^2 .
\]
Therefore $a^2+c^2=a^2+b^2=1$ and $b^2+d^2=c^2+d^2=1$.

For the last identity, $abcd=(ac)(bd)=-(bd)^2=-b^2d^2$, so
\[
(ab+cd)^2=a^2b^2+2abcd+c^2d^2=a^2b^2-2b^2d^2+c^2d^2 .
\]
With $c^2=b^2$ and $d^2=a^2$ this is $a^2b^2-2a^2b^2+a^2b^2=0$, so $ab+cd=0$. $\blacksquare$

(Geometrically: $(a,b)$ and $(c,d)$ are perpendicular unit vectors, so the matrix with rows $(a,b)$ and $(c,d)$ is orthogonal, and then its columns $(a,c)$ and $(b,d)$ are perpendicular unit vectors too.)
