Suppose that $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$ are real numbers such that
\[
a^2+b^2=1,\qquad c^2+d^2=1,\qquad ac+bd=0 .
\]
Show that
\[
a^2+c^2=1,\qquad b^2+d^2=1,\qquad ab+cd=0 .
\]
