Write the radicand as $36(c^{10})^2(a^9)^2(y^4)^2(3y)$. Its principal square root is $6c^{10}|a|^9y^4\sqrt{3y}$. Thus the expression is $\boxed{-54c^{10}|a|^9y^4\sqrt{3y}}$. The absolute value on $a^9$ ensures a nonnegative square root; $c^{10}$ and $y^4$ are already nonnegative.
