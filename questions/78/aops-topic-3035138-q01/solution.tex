Expanding $\prod_{k=3}^{10}(1+\dfrac{1}{k})$ selects each element or $1$, so it sums all subset products including the empty product $1$. The product telescopes to $\dfrac{4}{3}\dfrac{5}{4}\cdots\dfrac{11}{10}=\dfrac{11}{3}$. Subtracting the empty contribution gives $\boxed{\dfrac{8}{3}}$.
