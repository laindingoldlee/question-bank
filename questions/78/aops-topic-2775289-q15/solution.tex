Sum three convergent geometric series: $\dfrac{1}{6}\left(\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{1}{6}}+\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{1}{3}}+\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{1}{2}}\right)=\dfrac{1}{5}+\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{3}=\dfrac{47}{60}$. Thus $\boxed{107}$.
