Not counting the identity, the symmetries of a regular hexagon are the rotations by $60^\circ,120^\circ,180^\circ,240^\circ,300^\circ$ and the reflections in its six axes. Kristina picks six of these eleven transformations at random, with replacement, and applies them in succession. The probability that the vertex $P$ is sent to each of the six vertices exactly once during the process is $\dfrac k{11^6}$. Find $k$.
