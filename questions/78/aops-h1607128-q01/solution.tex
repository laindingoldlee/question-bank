(a) Exponentiation does not distribute over addition: raising the original sides to zero gives $(1+1)^0=2^0$, namely $1=1$, not $1^0+1^0=2^0$. (b) Infinity is not a real number on which ordinary cancellation is defined. Even in the extended real numbers, $1+\infty=2+\infty=\infty$ is true, but subtraction of $\infty$ from $\infty$ is undefined, so it cannot imply $1=2$.
