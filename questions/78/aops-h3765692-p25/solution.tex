Color the board like a chessboard. Moving between side-adjacent cells changes color, so all odd labels have one color and all even labels the other. The eight cells of the specified diagonal have the same color. Their eight distinct labels are consequently either all odd, with sum at least $1+3+\cdots+15=64$, or all even, with sum at least $2+4+\cdots+16=72$. In both cases the sum is at least $\boxed{64}$. This establishes a lower bound, without asserting that equality can be attained.
