Let $p_j$ be the pass probability on test $j$. Then $p_1=0.6$ and $p_{j+1}=0.7p_j+0.6(1-p_j)=0.6+0.1p_j$. Thus $p_2=0.66,p_3=0.666$. Linearity of expectation gives $0.6+0.66+0.666=1.926=\dfrac{963}{500}$, so $\boxed{a+b=1463}$.
