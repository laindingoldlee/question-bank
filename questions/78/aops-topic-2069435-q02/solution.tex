There are $20$ coins, so $\dbinom{20}{3}=1140$ equally likely sets of three coins. The sets with no penny are chosen from the $15$ other coins: $\dbinom{15}{3}=455$. Hence
\[
P(\text{at least one penny})=1-\dfrac{455}{1140}=\dfrac{685}{1140}=\boxed{\dfrac{137}{228}}.
\]
