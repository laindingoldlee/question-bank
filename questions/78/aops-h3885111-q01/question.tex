There exists a square $ABCD$ in the $xy$-plane with following properties:

\begin{itemize}

\item The product of the $x$ coordinates of $A$, $B$, $C$, and $D$ is $50$.

\item The product of the $y$ coordinates of $A$, $B$, $C$, and $D$ is $50$.

\item The sum of the squares of the $x$ coordinates of $A$, $B$, $C$, and $D$ is $600$.

\item The sum of the squares of the $y$ coordinates of $A$, $B$, $C$, and $D$ is $700$.

\end{itemize}

Find the area of $ABCD$.
