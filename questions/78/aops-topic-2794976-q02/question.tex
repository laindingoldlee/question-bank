Two skiers travel one behind the other, first on flat ground and then uphill. On the flat both ski at $12$ kilometres per hour; uphill both slow to $8$ kilometres per hour. Once both are on the uphill section, the distance between them is $300$ metres less than it was on the flat. What was the distance between them on the flat?
