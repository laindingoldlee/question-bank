Let the flat distance be $D$ metres. After the first skier starts uphill, the second needs $\dfrac{D}{12000}$ hours to reach the hill, during which the first travels $8000\cdot\dfrac{D}{12000}=\dfrac23D$ metres. So the new gap is $\dfrac23D=D-300$, giving $D=\boxed{900}$ metres.
