Lines $PS$, $QT$ and $RU$ intersect at a common point $O$, with the rays $OP$, $OQ$, $OR$, $OS$, $OT$, $OU$ in this order around $O$ (so $O$ lies between $P$ and $S$, between $Q$ and $T$, and between $R$ and $U$). $P$ is joined to $Q$, $R$ to $S$, and $T$ to $U$, forming triangles $OPQ$, $ORS$ and $OTU$, as shown. What is the value of $\angle P+\angle Q+\angle R+\angle S+\angle T+\angle U$, where each angle is the angle of the corresponding triangle at that vertex?

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0);
pair U=1.9*dir(20),P=2.2*dir(80),Q=2.0*dir(135),R=2.3*dir(200),Sx=1.8*dir(260),T=2.4*dir(315);
draw(P--Sx); draw(Q--T); draw(R--U);
draw(P--Q,linewidth(0.9)); draw(R--Sx,linewidth(0.9)); draw(T--U,linewidth(0.9));
dot(O); label("$O$",O,2*dir(170));
label("$P$",P,N); label("$Q$",Q,NW); label("$R$",R,W); label("$S$",Sx,S); label("$T$",T,SE); label("$U$",U,E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
