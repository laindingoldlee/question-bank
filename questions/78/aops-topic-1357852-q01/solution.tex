Let $A_5,A_6,A_7$ be the sets of integers from $1$ to $1000$ divisible by $5$, $6$, $7$. Since $5,6,7$ are pairwise coprime, a number is divisible by two or three of them exactly when it is divisible by their product.
\[
|A_5|=200,\quad |A_6|=166,\quad |A_7|=142,
\]
\[
|A_5\cap A_6|=\left\lfloor\dfrac{1000}{30}\right\rfloor=33,\quad |A_5\cap A_7|=\left\lfloor\dfrac{1000}{35}\right\rfloor=28,\quad |A_6\cap A_7|=\left\lfloor\dfrac{1000}{42}\right\rfloor=23,
\]
\[
|A_5\cap A_6\cap A_7|=\left\lfloor\dfrac{1000}{210}\right\rfloor=4 .
\]
By inclusion-exclusion the count is $200+166+142-33-28-23+4=\boxed{428}$.
