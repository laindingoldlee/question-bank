Let $n_{ij}$ be the number assigned to round pair $i,j$. The four degree equations show opposite pair counts are equal: $n_{12}=n_{34}=a$, $n_{13}=n_{24}=b$, and $n_{14}=n_{23}=c$, with $a+b+c=3$. For each nonnegative triple, assigning the six named people gives $\dfrac{6!}{a!^2b!^2c!^2}$ schedules. Triples of types $(3,0,0),(2,1,0),(1,1,1)$ contribute respectively $3\cdot20$, $6\cdot180$, and $720$. The total is $\boxed{1860}$.
