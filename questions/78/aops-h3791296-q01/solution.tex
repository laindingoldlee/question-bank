Translate the intersection to the origin. Reflection across the bisector $y=x$ exchanges the two lines. It sends a direction vector $(1,m_1)$ to $(m_1,1)$, whose slope is $\dfrac{1}{m_1}$. Thus $m_2=\dfrac{1}{m_1}$ and $\boxed{m_1m_2=1}$. The nonzero finite-slope hypothesis excludes the horizontal/vertical pair, for which one slope is undefined.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
draw((-2,0)--(2,0),gray);draw((0,-2)--(0,2),gray);draw((-2,-.6)--(2,.6),blue);draw((-.6,-2)--(.6,2),blue);draw((-1.7,-1.7)--(1.7,1.7),dashed);label("$y=x$",(1.6,1.6),(1,0));label("$m$",(1.7,.51),(0,1));label("$\dfrac{1}{m}$",(.51,1.7),(-1,0));
\end{asy}
