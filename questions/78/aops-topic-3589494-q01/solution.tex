Vieta gives $p+q=5$, $pq=1$, and both roots are positive. The quadratic equation gives $p^2+1=5p$ and $q^2+1=5q$. Since $q=\dfrac{1}{p}$, the first radical is $\sqrt{5p^2}=\sqrt5\,p$, and similarly the second is $\sqrt5\,q$. Their sum is $\sqrt5(p+q)=\boxed{5\sqrt5}$.
