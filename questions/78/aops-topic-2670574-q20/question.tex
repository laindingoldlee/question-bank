Ten people are floating at ten points on the (flat) surface of the sea. We have ten circular lifebuoys, each of radius $42$ metres, which can be placed on the surface simultaneously anywhere we like, provided that no two lifebuoys have a point in common. A person is saved if he lies in the interior of some lifebuoy. Prove that all ten people can be saved, wherever they are.
