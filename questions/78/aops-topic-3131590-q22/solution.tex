Work modulo $60$. After the first draw the total is uniform on the five multiples of $12$, so its zero probability is $\dfrac{1}{5}$. Adding an independent uniform multiple of $15$ makes the total uniform on the twenty multiples of three: the two subgroups intersect only at zero, so each sum has one representation. Adding a uniform multiple of $20$ then makes it uniform on all sixty residues, again by unique representation. Every further independent addition preserves that uniformity. The expectation is therefore $\dfrac{1}{5}+\dfrac{1}{20}+28\cdot\dfrac{1}{60}=\boxed{\dfrac{43}{60}}$.
