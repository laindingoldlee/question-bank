Prove that $\pi$ is irrational, that is, $\pi$ cannot be written as a fraction $\dfrac ab$ of positive integers.

(Hint: suppose $\pi=\dfrac ab$, and for a positive integer $n$ consider $f(x)=\dfrac{x^n(a-bx)^n}{n!}$ and $\displaystyle\int_0^\pi f(x)\sin x\,dx$.)
