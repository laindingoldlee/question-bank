Let $s_k=x^k+\dfrac{1}{x^k}$, so $s_1=4$. Then
\[
s_2=s_1^2-2=14,\qquad s_3=s_1^3-3s_1=64-12=52,
\]
and since $s_2s_3=\left(x^2+\dfrac{1}{x^2}\right)\left(x^3+\dfrac{1}{x^3}\right)=s_5+s_1$,
\[
s_5=s_2s_3-s_1=14\cdot52-4=\boxed{724}.
\]
