Put $A=(0,0)$, $B=(1,0)$, $C=(0,1)$, with $M=\left(\dfrac12,0\right)$ and $N=\left(0,\dfrac12\right)$ the midpoints of $AB$ and $AC$. The medians $BN$ and $CM$ meet at the centroid $G=\left(\dfrac13,\dfrac13\right)$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(1,0),C=(0,1),M=(0.5,0),N=(0,0.5),G=(1/3,1/3);
fill(A--M--G--N--cycle,rgb(0.85,0.9,1));draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(B--N);draw(C--M);
draw((0,0)--(0.07,0)--(0.07,0.07)--(0,0.07));
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,N);label("$M$",M,S);label("$N$",N,W);label("$G$",G,NE);
\end{asy}

The quadrilateral $AMGN$ consists of triangles $AMG$ and $ANG$, each with base $\dfrac12$ and height $\dfrac13$:
\[[AMGN]=2\cdot\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac13=\boxed{\dfrac16}.\]
