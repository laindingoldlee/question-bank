Jamal gets home from work by taxi or by bus. He takes the bus $60\%$ of the time and a taxi otherwise. When he takes a taxi he arrives home after 7 p.m. $8\%$ of the time, and when he takes the bus he arrives after 7 p.m. $15\%$ of the time. Given that Jamal arrived home after 7 p.m., what is the probability that he took the bus? Also explain why the answer is not $0.6\cdot0.15=0.09$.
