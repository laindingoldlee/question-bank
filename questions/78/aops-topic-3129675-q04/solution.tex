The requirement is $100\mid N(N-1)$. Consecutive integers are coprime, so all factors of 4 must belong to one of $N,N-1$, and all factors of 25 to one of them. If both belong to $N$, then $N\equiv0\pmod{100}$; if both belong to $N-1$, then $N\equiv1\pmod{100}$. These give no two-digit values. The other two choices give $N\equiv25$ or $76\pmod{100}$, checked from multiples of 25 and their residues modulo 4. Both work: $25^2=625$, $76^2=5776$. Thus $\boxed{25,76}$.
