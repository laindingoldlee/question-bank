The terms are the odd numbers $2k-1$ for $k=1,\dots,100$, so there are $100$ terms. Pairing the first and last, the second and second-to-last, and so on gives $50$ pairs, each with sum $200$:
\[
1+3+\cdots+199=50\cdot200=\boxed{10000}.
\]
This is $100^2$: in general $1+3+\cdots+(2n-1)=n^2$, as the ``proof without words'' shows by building an $n\times n$ square of dots from L-shaped layers of $1,3,5,\dots$ dots.
