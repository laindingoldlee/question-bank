The chain means two inequalities at once.
\begin{itemize}
\item $n^2>16$ holds exactly when $n>4$ or $n<-4$.
\item $130n>n^2$ means $n^2-130n<0$, that is, $n(n-130)<0$, which holds exactly when $0<n<130$.
\end{itemize}
Both hold exactly when $\boxed{4<n<130}$. The integers in this range are $5,6,\dots,129$, and there are $\boxed{125}$ of them.
