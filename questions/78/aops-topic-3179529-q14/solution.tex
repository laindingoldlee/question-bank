The difference between the numbers of seated boys and girls must always be $-1,0,$ or $1$. The first eight arrivals therefore split into four pairs, each containing one boy and one girl in either order. The last arrival is a boy. There are $2^4$ gender patterns, and $5!4!$ ways to assign the people to those positions. Thus the count is $2^4\cdot5!\cdot4!=\boxed{46080}$.
