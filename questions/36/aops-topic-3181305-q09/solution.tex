The vertex distance is 1, which can be an edge, face diagonal, or space diagonal. The corresponding side lengths are 1,$\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}$,$\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}$, and volumes are 1,$\dfrac{\sqrt2}{4}$,$\dfrac{\sqrt3}{9}$. All are realizable by rotating and translating a cube to the given pair. Their product is $\dfrac{\sqrt6}{36}$, so $\boxed{43}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

pair[] p={(0,0),(2,0),(2,2),(0,2),(.8,.7),(2.8,.7),(2.8,2.7),(.8,2.7)};draw(p[0]--p[1]--p[2]--p[3]--cycle);draw(p[4]--p[5]--p[6]--p[7]--cycle);for(int i=0;i<4;++i)draw(p[i]--p[i+4]);draw(p[0]--p[1],red);draw(p[0]--p[2],blue);draw(p[0]--p[6],green);label("edge",(1,0),S);label("face diagonal",(1,1),W);label("space diagonal",(1.4,1.35),E);
\end{asy}
