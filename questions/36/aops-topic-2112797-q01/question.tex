Bob has an infinite pile of cards and a second pile, which is empty at the start. He repeatedly moves cards from the top of the first pile onto the second pile: each time, independently of the earlier moves, he moves exactly $n$ cards with probability $\dfrac{1}{2^n}$, for $n=1,2,3,\dots$. What is the probability that at some moment the second pile contains exactly $52$ cards?
