Mix $x$ litres of the $20\%$ solution with $y$ litres of the $40\%$ solution. The amount of the chemical is $0.2x+0.4y$, and it must be $30\%$ of the total $x+y$:
\[
0.2x+0.4y=0.3(x+y)\ \Longrightarrow\ 0.1y=0.1x\ \Longrightarrow\ x=y .
\]
So the solutions are mixed in the ratio $\boxed{1:1}$. (This is expected: $30\%$ is exactly halfway between $20\%$ and $40\%$.)
