This is the number of $4$-element subsets of a $6$-element set:
\[
\dbinom{6}{4}=\dbinom{6}{2}=\dfrac{6\cdot5}{2}=\boxed{15}.
\]
(Choosing the $4$ books to take is the same as choosing the $2$ books to leave.) The poster's answer is correct.
