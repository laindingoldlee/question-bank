Let $u=\sqrt[3]{2+\sqrt5}$, $v=\sqrt[3]{2-\sqrt5}$ and $t=u+v$. Then
\[
u^3+v^3=4,\qquad uv=\sqrt[3]{(2+\sqrt5)(2-\sqrt5)}=\sqrt[3]{4-5}=-1 .
\]
Using $(u+v)^3=u^3+v^3+3uv(u+v)$,
\[
t^3=4-3t,\qquad\text{so}\qquad t^3+3t-4=(t-1)(t^2+t+4)=0 .
\]
The quadratic $t^2+t+4$ has discriminant $1-16<0$, so it has no real roots, and $t$ is real. Hence $t=\boxed{1}$.

(Indeed $\left(\dfrac{1+\sqrt5}{2}\right)^3=2+\sqrt5$ and $\left(\dfrac{1-\sqrt5}{2}\right)^3=2-\sqrt5$, and the two cube roots add to $1$.)
