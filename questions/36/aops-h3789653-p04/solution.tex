Put $AD=BE=a$ and $AE=BC=b$, so $AB=a+b$. Choose $A=(0,0),B=(0,a+b),C=(b,a+b),D=(a,0),E=(0,b)$. Then $\overrightarrow{DC}=(b-a,a+b)$ and $\overrightarrow{DE}=(-a,b)$. Their dot product and the absolute determinant both equal $a^2+b^2$. Thus the angle has equal positive sine and cosine, giving $\boxed{45^\circ}$.
