Complementary counting gives $10^3-9^3=\boxed{271}$. Alternatively, if the first $7$ is in the first, second, or third position, the counts are $10^2$, $9\cdot10$, and $9^2$. They sum to $100+90+81=271$. The cases are disjoint, demonstrating a direct construction count of the same set.
