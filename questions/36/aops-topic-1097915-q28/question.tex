In right triangle $ABC$ with right angle at $C$, $AC=5$ and $BC=12$. Points $D$ and $E$ lie on $AB$ and $BC$, respectively, so that $CD$ is perpendicular to $AB$ and $E$ is the midpoint of $BC$. Segments $AE$ and $CD$ meet at $F$. What is the ratio $\dfrac{AF}{FE}$? Express your answer as a common fraction.
