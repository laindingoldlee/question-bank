In triangle $ABC$, $AB=20$, $BC=21$ and $AC=26$. Four congruent circles of radius $r$ lie inside the triangle: each of three of them is tangent to two sides of the triangle, and the fourth is externally tangent to each of the other three and tangent to no side. If the area of triangle $ABC$ is $K$, find $rK$.
