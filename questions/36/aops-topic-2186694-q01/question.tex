A student claims that $n^{10}-10$ is divisible by $10$ for every integer $n$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that the claim is false.
\item[(b)] Find all integers $n$ for which $n^{10}-10$ is divisible by $10$.
\end{enumerate}
