From any vertex the squared distances to the other seven are $1,1,2,2,3,3,4$. There are three pairs at equal distance, each defining an isosceles apex. Their base squared lengths are respectively $2,4,2$, so none is equilateral and no triangle has two counted apexes. The answer is $8\cdot3=\boxed{24}$.
