Pairing each nonzero residue modulo $71$ with its inverse leaves only $1$ and $-1$ unpaired, so $70!\equiv-1\pmod{71}$, and $k(71-k)\equiv-k^2$, so the expression is congruent to $\sum_{k=4}^{67}k^{-2}$. Inversion permutes nonzero residues, hence the full sum from $1$ to $70$ equals $\sum_{k=1}^{70}k^2\equiv0$. The omitted terms, $1,2,3,68,69,70$, sum to $2(1+\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{9})=\dfrac{49}{18}$ modulo $71$. Since $18^{-1}\equiv4$, the requested remainder is $-49\cdot4=-196\equiv\boxed{17}\pmod{71}$. All denominators are invertible modulo $71$; each original summand times $70!$ is integral.
