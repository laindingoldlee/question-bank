For a root $u$, $u^3=9u+9$, so the factor becomes $u^2-u+1$. Let $\alpha,\beta$ be the roots of $x^2-x+1$, with $\alpha+\beta=1$, $\alpha\beta=1$, and $\alpha^3=\beta^3=-1$. Then the desired product is $P(\alpha)P(\beta)=(-10-9\alpha)(-10-9\beta)=100+90+81=\boxed{271}$. The signs cancel because there are six factors when reversing $u-\alpha$ to $\alpha-u$.
