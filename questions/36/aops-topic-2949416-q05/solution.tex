Since $x^2-xy+y^2=(y-\dfrac{x}{2})^2+\dfrac{3x^2}{4}$, the bound $k<10$ forces $x\le3$; similarly $y\le3$. Checking the nine pairs gives values below 10 exactly $1,3,4,7,9$. Thus $\boxed5$ integers work.
