A process acts on a nonzero integer $N$: if $N>0$, replace $N$ by $N-R$, and if $N<0$, replace $N$ by $N+R$, where $R$ is the number formed by reversing the digits of $|N|$. Let $N_m$ be the number $21\underbrace{9\cdots9}_{m-2}78=22\left(10^m-1\right)$ ($m\ge2$, e.g. $2178$, $21978$). Prove that $N_m$ returns to itself after four steps.
