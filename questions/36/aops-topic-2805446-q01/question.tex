Is it possible to place the numbers $1,2,\dots,9$ around a circle so that the sum of any three circularly consecutive numbers is divisible by $3$ and is, moreover,
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] greater than $9$?
\item[(b)] greater than $15$?
\end{enumerate}
