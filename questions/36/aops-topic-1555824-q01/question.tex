For a positive integer $n$, the \emph{digital root} $\operatorname{dr}(n)$ is obtained by adding the digits of $n$, then adding the digits of the result, and so on, until a single digit remains. Prove that
\[
\operatorname{dr}(m)\operatorname{dr}(n)\ge\operatorname{dr}(mn)
\]
for all positive integers $m$ and $n$.
