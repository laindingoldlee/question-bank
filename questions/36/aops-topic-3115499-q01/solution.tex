(a) The sixteen squares contribute $64$ sides before shared sides are removed. Each shared side removes two boundary units, so $P=64-2E$. (b) A $1\times16$ strip has $P=34,L=2$, whereas a $4\times4$ square has $P=16,L=0$. A distribution concentrated on either shape meets all the geometric conditions but gives different expectations. In general $\mathbb E[P]=64-2\mathbb E[E]$, and $\mathbb E[L]$ likewise depends on the sampling law.
