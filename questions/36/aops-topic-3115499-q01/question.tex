A connected union of sixteen unit grid squares has perimeter $P$. Let $E$ count shared full sides and let $L$ count squares that share a side with exactly one other square. (a) Express $P$ in terms of $E$. (b) Explain why neither the expected value of $P$ nor that of $L$ is determined without a probability model.
