We want $x^5+\dfrac{1}{x^5}$. Let $t=x+\dfrac{1}{x}=4$. Then $x^2+\dfrac{1}{x^2}=t^2-2=14$ and $x^3+\dfrac{1}{x^3}=t^3-3t=52$, and
\[x^5+\dfrac{1}{x^5}=\left(x^2+\dfrac{1}{x^2}\right)\left(x^3+\dfrac{1}{x^3}\right)-\left(x+\dfrac{1}{x}\right)=14\cdot52-4=\boxed{724}.\]
