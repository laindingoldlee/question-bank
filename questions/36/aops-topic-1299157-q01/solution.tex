Write $i=e^{\dfrac{i\pi}{2}}$. The solutions of $z^4=i$ are $z=e^{i\left(\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{k\pi}{2}\right)}$ for $k=0,1,2,3$:
\[
\boxed{z=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta,\quad\theta=\dfrac{\pi}{8},\ \dfrac{5\pi}{8},\ \dfrac{9\pi}{8},\ \dfrac{13\pi}{8}}.
\]
For example the principal root is $\cos\dfrac{\pi}{8}+i\sin\dfrac{\pi}{8}=\dfrac{\sqrt{2+\sqrt2}}{2}+i\,\dfrac{\sqrt{2-\sqrt2}}{2}$.
