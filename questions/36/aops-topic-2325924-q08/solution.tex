In each case the remainder is $3$ less than the row length, so $n+3$ is divisible by $6$, $5$ and $13$, hence by $\operatorname{lcm}(6,5,13)=390$. So $n\equiv387\pmod{390}$, and the least $n\ge80$ is $\boxed{\textbf{(D) }387}$. (Check: $387=6\cdot64+3=5\cdot77+2=13\cdot29+10$. The choice $393$ fails: it leaves remainder $3$ on division by $5$.)
