The daily rates are $B=\dfrac{1}{15}$, $A=\dfrac{1}{10}-\dfrac{1}{15}=\dfrac{1}{30}$, and $C=\dfrac{1}{5}-\dfrac{1}{30}=\dfrac{1}{6}$. The first two days complete $2(B+C)=\dfrac{7}{15}$; Wednesday adds $A+B+C=\dfrac{4}{15}$. The remaining $\dfrac{4}{15}$ takes $\dfrac{\dfrac{4}{15}}{\dfrac{1}{5}}=\dfrac{4}{3}$ days, so construction finishes on $\boxed{\text{Friday}}$.
