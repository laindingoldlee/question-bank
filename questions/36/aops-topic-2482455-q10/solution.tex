Pad every number from 0 to 9999 with leading zeros to four digits; this neither creates nor destroys an occurrence of 69. Each of the three starting positions for 69 gives 100 strings. Adjacent starting positions cannot both occur; positions 1 and 3 occur together only in 6969. Thus the count is $300-1=\boxed{299}$. Zero is not counted by the property.
