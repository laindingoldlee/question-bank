Tiham is looking for six-digit positive integers $\overline{PQRSTU}$ (the digits need not be distinct, and $P\ne0$) such that the sum of the three-digit number $\overline{PQR}$ and the number $\overline{STU}$ is divisible by $37$. How many such numbers are there? (Here $\overline{STU}$ may have leading zeros, so it ranges from $0$ to $999$.)
