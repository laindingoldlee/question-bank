Substitution and multiplication by $a^2b^2$ give
\[
(b^2+a^2m^2)x^2+2a^2mkx+a^2(k^2-b^2)=0.
\]
Tangency means this equation has a repeated real root. Its discriminant is
\[
4a^4m^2k^2-4a^2(b^2+a^2m^2)(k^2-b^2)
=4a^2b^2(a^2m^2+b^2-k^2).
\]
Since $a,b>0$, its vanishing implies $\boxed{k^2=a^2m^2+b^2}$. Conversely this condition gives one repeated intersection, hence tangency.
