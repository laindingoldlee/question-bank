Let $\alpha$ and $\beta$ be real numbers, and suppose $e^{i\alpha}+e^{i\beta}=a+bi$ with $a,b$ real.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $e^{-i\alpha}+e^{-i\beta}=a-bi$.
\item[(b)] Show that $(a,b)=(9,12)$ is impossible.
\item[(c)] Find $e^{-i\alpha}+e^{-i\beta}$ when $(a,b)=\left(\dfrac14,\dfrac37\right)$.
\end{enumerate}
