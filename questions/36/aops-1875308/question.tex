Let $ABCD$ be a trapezoid ($AD\parallel BC$) with $\angle A=\angle B= 90^o$ and $AD<BC$. Let $E$ be the intersection point of the non parallel sides $AB$ and $CD$,  $Z$ be the symmetric point of $A$ wrt line $BC$ and $M$ be the midpoint of $EZ$. If it is given than line $CM$ is perpendicular on line $DZ$, then prove that line $ZC$ is perpendicular on line $EC$.
