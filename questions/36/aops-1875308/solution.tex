Choose $B=(0,0),A=(0,a),C=(c,0),D=(d,a)$, where $a>0$ and $0<d<c$. Then $E=(0,e)$ with $e = \dfrac{a c}{c-d}$, $Z=(0,-a)$, and $M = (0 , \dfrac{e-a}{2})$. The perpendicularity $CM\perp DZ$ gives $cd-a(e-a)=0$. Since $e - a = \dfrac{a d}{c-d}$, cancellation of $d>0$ yields $c(c-d)=a^2$. Now $(C-Z) \cdot (C-E) = c^2 - a e = c^2 - \dfrac{a^2 c}{c-d} = 0$. Therefore $\boxed{ZC\perp EC}$.
