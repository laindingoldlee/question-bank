Both numbers are positive, and $x\mapsto x^{30}$ is increasing for $x>0$, so we may compare their $30$th powers:
\[\left(10^{\dfrac{1}{10}}\right)^{30}=10^3=1000,\qquad\left(3^{\dfrac13}\right)^{30}=3^{10}=59049.\]
Since $59049>1000$, $\boxed{3^{\dfrac13}}$ is larger.
