The squarefree parts of 1,2,3,4,5 are 1,2,3,1,5. A square product occurs either when all three parts are 1, giving $ 2^3=8 $ ordered outcomes, or when one part is 1 and the other two are equal among 2,3,5. The latter gives $ 3\cdot2\cdot3=18 $ outcomes. No other pattern cancels every prime exponent. Thus the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{26}{125}}$.
