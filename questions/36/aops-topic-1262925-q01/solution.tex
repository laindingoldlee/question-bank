Let $D_n$ be the number of derangements (permutations with no fixed point) of $n$ objects, and let $N=5041$. Then $P=\dfrac{D_N}{N!}$. A permutation with exactly one fixed point is obtained by choosing the fixed card ($N$ ways) and deranging the rest, so $p=\dfrac{N D_{N-1}}{N!}$.

The derangement numbers satisfy $D_n=nD_{n-1}+(-1)^n$ (this follows from $D_n=n!\sum_{k=0}^n\dfrac{(-1)^k}{k!}$: the last term of the sum gives the $(-1)^n$). Therefore
\[P-p=\dfrac{D_N-ND_{N-1}}{N!}=\dfrac{(-1)^N}{N!}=-\dfrac{1}{5041!},\]
since $5041$ is odd. This is a negative number of absolute value far below $10^{-12}$, so to the nearest trillionth $P-p\approx\boxed{0}$ (more precisely, $-\dfrac{1}{5041!}$).
