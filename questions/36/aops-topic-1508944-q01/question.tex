A king tests a prince with a hallway of $9$ rooms. Exactly one room holds the princess; each of the other eight rooms is either empty or holds a tiger. The sign on the princess's room is true, the signs on rooms with tigers are false, and the signs on empty rooms may be true or false. The signs are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(1)] The princess is in an odd-numbered room.
\item[(2)] This room is empty.
\item[(3)] Sign 5 is right or sign 7 is wrong.
\item[(4)] Sign 1 is wrong.
\item[(5)] Either sign 2 or sign 4 is right.
\item[(6)] Sign 3 is wrong.
\item[(7)] The princess is not in room 1.
\item[(8)] This room contains a tiger and room 9 is empty.
\item[(9)] This room contains a tiger and sign 6 is wrong.
\end{enumerate}
The prince said: ``This is unsolvable! Tell me whether room 8 is empty or not.'' The king told him, and the prince was then able to find the princess. Where is the princess?
