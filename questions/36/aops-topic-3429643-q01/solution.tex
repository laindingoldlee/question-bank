By symmetry, compare the three ticket numbers to a fixed winning set of twenty. There are $\binom{80}{3}=82160$ equally likely triples. Three matches occur in $\binom{20}{3}=1140$ ways, and exactly two in $\binom{20}{2}\binom{60}{1}=11400$ ways. Thus the profit probability is $\dfrac{1140}{82160}=\dfrac{57}{4108}$, the break-even probability is $\dfrac{11400}{82160}=\dfrac{285}{2054}$, and the loss probability is $\dfrac{69620}{82160}=\dfrac{3481}{4108}$. The expected net gain is $\dfrac{42\cdot1140+11400}{82160}-1=\boxed{-\dfrac{286}{1027}\text{ dollars}}$. For a fair three-match payout $R$, solve $1140R+11400=82160$, giving $\boxed{R=\dfrac{3538}{57}\text{ dollars}}$, including the returned stake.
