Choose three distinct numbers from $1$ to $80$, and pay one dollar to play. Twenty numbers are drawn uniformly without replacement. A three-match ticket is paid $42$ dollars, a two-match ticket is paid $1$ dollar, and other tickets receive nothing. Find the probabilities of a profit, breaking even, and losing, and the expected net gain. What three-match payout would make the game fair if the other payouts remain unchanged?
