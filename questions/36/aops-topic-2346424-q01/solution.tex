Since the median $AD$ is perpendicular to $BC$, the line $AD$ is the perpendicular bisector of $BC$, so $AB=AC$ and the triangle is symmetric about $AD$.

$G$ is the centroid, and $BD=DC=3$. In the right triangle $BDG$, $GD=\sqrt{5^2-3^2}=4$, so $AD=3GD=12$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(-3,0), C=(3,0), A=(0,12), D=(0,0), G=(0,4), Ep=(A+C)/2, H=(A+B)/2;
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(A--D,blue); draw(B--Ep,red); draw(C--H,heavygreen);
draw(D+(0,0.5)--D+(0.5,0.5)--D+(0.5,0),blue);
dot(A); dot(B); dot(C); dot(D); dot(G); dot(Ep); dot(H);
label("$A$",A,N); label("$B$",B,SW); label("$C$",C,SE); label("$D$",D,S); label("$G$",G,(1.6,-0.6));
label("$E$",Ep,E); label("$H$",H,W);
label("$5$",(B+G)/2+(-0.4,0.3),red+fontsize(8pt)); label("$4$",(0,2),E,blue+fontsize(8pt));
label("$3$",(1.5,0),S,fontsize(8pt)); label("$3$",(-1.5,0),S,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}

The reflection in $AD$ swaps $B$ and $C$, and hence swaps the midpoints $E$ of $AC$ and $H$ of $AB$, so it maps the median $BE$ onto the median $CH$. Since $G$ divides $BE$ in the ratio $2:1$, $BE=\dfrac32BG=\dfrac{15}2$. Therefore
\[CH=BE=\boxed{\dfrac{15}{2}}.\]
(Check with coordinates: $B=(-3,0)$, $C=(3,0)$, $A=(0,12)$, $H=\left(-\dfrac32,6\right)$, and $CH=\sqrt{\left(\dfrac92\right)^2+6^2}=\sqrt{\dfrac{225}4}=\dfrac{15}2$.)
