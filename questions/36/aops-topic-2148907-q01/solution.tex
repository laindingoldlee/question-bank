The harmonic mean of two positive numbers $p$ and $q$ is $\dfrac{2}{\dfrac{1}{p}+\dfrac{1}{q}}$. With $p=\dfrac{1}{x}$ and $q=\dfrac{1}{y}$ we have $\dfrac{1}{p}+\dfrac{1}{q}=x+y$, so the condition says
\[\dfrac{2}{x+y}=\dfrac{1}{10},\qquad x+y=\boxed{20}.\]
So every pair of positive integers with this property, such as $(1,19)$, $(7,13)$ or $(10,10)$, has the same sum $20$; in particular the greatest possible sum is $20$.
