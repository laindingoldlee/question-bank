If there were $n$ students, the two removed scores sum to $63n-72(n-2)=144-9n$. Nonnegativity forces $n\le16$. This bound occurs when the two removed students score zero and the remaining fourteen each score $72$. Therefore the maximum is $\boxed{16}$.
