Let $d=\gcd(a,b)$ and $m=|a-b|$. Since $a\ne b$, $m\ge1$. As $d$ divides both $a$ and $b$, it divides $m$, so $d\le m$, and therefore $d$ is one of the factors $1,2,\dots,m$ of $m!$; hence $d\mid m!$.

So $d$ divides $a$ and $m!$, hence $d\mid\gcd(a,m!)$; likewise $d\mid\gcd(b,m!)$. Therefore $d$ divides
\[\gcd\bigl(\gcd(a,m!),\ \gcd(b,m!)\bigr)=1,\]
so $d=1$, i.e. $a$ and $b$ are relatively prime. $\blacksquare$
