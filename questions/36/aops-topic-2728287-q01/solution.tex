By inclusion--exclusion over the students who get nothing:
\[3^5-\dbinom31 2^5+\dbinom32 1^5=243-96+3=\boxed{150}.\]
(Alternatively: the awards split as $3,1,1$ in $3\cdot\dbinom53\cdot2=60$ ways or as $2,2,1$ in $3\cdot\dbinom51\cdot\dbinom42=90$ ways.)
