A nonnegative integer $n$ is called a \emph{pbj2006 number} if $n$ leaves a remainder less than $16384$ when divided by $32768$; a \emph{tenebrine number} if its remainder mod $4096$ is less than $2048$; a \emph{john number} if its remainder mod $64$ is less than $32$; a \emph{dotted number} if its remainder mod $8$ is less than $4$; and a \emph{cornie number} if the sum of its binary digits is $8$. How many integers from $0$ to $32767$ inclusive are all five kinds of number?
