Write $n$ in binary with bits $b_0$ (units) through $b_{14}$. The remainder of $n$ mod $2^{k+1}$ is less than $2^k$ exactly when $b_k=0$. So the first four conditions say $b_{14}=b_{11}=b_5=b_2=0$, and the last says exactly $8$ of the bits are $1$. The $8$ ones must be placed among the remaining $11$ bit positions: $\dbinom{11}{8}=\boxed{165}$.
