Using $1+2+\cdots+n=\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$ and $1^2+2^2+\cdots+n^2=\dfrac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6}$, find the mean and the (population) standard deviation of the numbers $1,2,\dots,n$. An answer key gives the standard deviation as $\dfrac{\sqrt{3n^2-9}}{6}$; is it correct?
