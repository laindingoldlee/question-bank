Multiplication gives $5x^2=50x$, or $5x(x-10)=0$, whose roots are $0,10$. But $x=0$ does not satisfy the original equation: multiplication by zero erases its distinction between the two sides. In general the new equation is $x(ax-b)=0$, allowing $x=0$ or $x=\dfrac{b}{a}$. The original allows only $\dfrac{b}{a}$; zero is an extraneous additional solution when $b\ne0$. When $b=0$ the two solution sets coincide.
