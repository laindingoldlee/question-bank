Complete the square:
\[
h=-16(t^2-8t)=-16\big((t-4)^2-16\big)=256-16(t-4)^2 .
\]
Since $(t-4)^2\ge0$, the height is at most $256$, with equality at $t=4$ seconds. (Equivalently, the vertex of the parabola is at $t=-\dfrac{128}{2(-16)}=4$.) The maximum height is $256$ feet, answer $\boxed{\textbf{(D)}}$.
