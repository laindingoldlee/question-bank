The height, in feet, of an object $t$ seconds after it is launched vertically upward from the ground with an initial velocity of $128$ feet per second is
\[
h=-16t^2+128t .
\]
What is the maximum height the object reaches?

$\textbf{(A)}\ 4\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ 8\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ 128\qquad\textbf{(D)}\ 256$
