Let $ f $ map the nonnegative integers to themselves and satisfy $ f(f(m))+2nf(m)+f(n)=f(m^2+n)$ for all nonnegative integers $ m,n $. Classify all such functions. If additionally $ f(m)=0 $ implies $ m=0 $, find the remainder of $ f(2022)$ on division by $ 1000 $.
