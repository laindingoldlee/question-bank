Put $ c=f(0)$ and $ a=f(1)$. The case $ m=0 $ gives $ f(c)=0 $. The case $ m=1 $ gives $ f(n+1)-f(n)=2na+f(a)$, and $ n=0 $ shows $ f(a)=a-c $. Summing the recurrence yields $ f(n)=an^2+c(1-n)$. Substituting $ n=c $ gives $ c[(a-1)c+1]=0 $. If $ c>0 $, nonnegative integrality forces $ a=0,c=1 $, but then $ f(2)=-1 $, a contradiction. Thus $ c=0 $ and $ f(n)=an^2 $. Now $ f(a)=a $ gives $ a^3=a $, hence $ a=0 $ or $ 1 $. Direct substitution verifies both the zero function and $ f(n)=n^2 $. The extra condition excludes the zero function, so $ f(2022)=2022^2\equiv22^2=\boxed{484}\pmod{1000}$.
