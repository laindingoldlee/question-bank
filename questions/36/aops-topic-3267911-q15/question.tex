A closed disk partially covers a unit grid square when their intersection has positive area. For each positive integer $k$, let $a_k$ be the greatest nonnegative radius such that no disk of that radius or smaller can partially cover at least $k$ grid squares. Find the sum of all positive integers $n\le12$ for which $a_n\ne a_{n+1}$.
