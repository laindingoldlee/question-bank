Each number under a root is $2$ times a perfect square: $18=2\cdot3^2$, $50=2\cdot5^2$, $98=2\cdot7^2$, $162=2\cdot9^2$, $242=2\cdot11^2$. So
\[A+B+C+D+E=(3+5+7+9+11)\sqrt2=35\sqrt2,\]
and the mean is $\dfrac{35\sqrt2}{5}=\boxed{7\sqrt2}$.
