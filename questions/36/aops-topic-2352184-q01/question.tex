Let $A=\sqrt{18}$, $B=\sqrt{50}$, $C=\sqrt{98}$, $D=\sqrt{162}$ and $E=\sqrt{242}$. What is the arithmetic mean of $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ and $E$? Express your answer in simplest radical form.
