With $(x,y)$ uniform in the unit square, we need $x-y\ge0.2$. This is the right triangle with vertices $(0.2,0)$, $(1,0)$, $(1,0.8)$, of area $\dfrac12\cdot0.8^2=0.32$. The probability is $\boxed{32\%}$.
