After each round he keeps the complementary fraction of what he had: $1-\dfrac13=\dfrac23$, then $1-\dfrac35=\dfrac25$, then $1-\dfrac47=\dfrac37$. So he is left with
\[\dfrac23\cdot\dfrac25\cdot\dfrac37=\dfrac{12}{105}=\boxed{\dfrac{4}{35}}\]
of his original money.
