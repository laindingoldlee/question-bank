Let Carole take $x$ hours. In one hour Nancy does $\dfrac{1}{8}$ of the job and Carole $\dfrac{1}{x}$, so
\[
5\left(\dfrac{1}{8}+\dfrac{1}{x}\right)=1\iff\dfrac{1}{x}=\dfrac{1}{5}-\dfrac{1}{8}=\dfrac{3}{40}\iff x=\boxed{\dfrac{40}{3}}\text{ hours}=13\dfrac{1}{3}\text{ hours}.
\]
