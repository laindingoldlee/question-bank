After $8$ years the balance is $500\cdot1.1^8+100(1.1+1.1^2+\cdots+1.1^8)=500\cdot1.1^8+1100\,(1.1^8-1)$. With $1.1^8=2.14358881$, this is $1071.79+1257.95=\boxed{\$2329.74}$. The deposits total $\$1300$, so the interest is $\$1029.74$.
