A coin lands heads with an unknown probability $p$. Model the unknown $p$ as a number chosen uniformly at random from the interval $[0,1]$; given $p$, the tosses of the coin are independent and each lands heads with probability $p$. The coin is tossed once and lands heads. What is the probability that the next toss also lands heads?
