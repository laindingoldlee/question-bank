The root gaps are $2\sqrt{16-a}$ and $2\sqrt{15-a}$. Write $u=\sqrt{16-a}$ and $v=\sqrt{15-a}$, so $u-v=\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $(u-v)(u+v)=u^2-v^2=1$. Thus $u+v=2$, giving $u=\dfrac{5}{4}$, $v=\dfrac{3}{4}$. Therefore $a=16-\dfrac{25}{16}=\boxed{\dfrac{231}{16}}$, which indeed gives two real roots for each polynomial.
