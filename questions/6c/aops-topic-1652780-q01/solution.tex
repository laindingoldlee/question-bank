For a triangle we need $x>0$ and $2x-1>0$. Then $2x+1$ exceeds $2x-1$, and it exceeds $x$ because $x+1>0$. So $2x+1$ is the hypotenuse, and
\[
x^2+(2x-1)^2=(2x+1)^2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x^2=(2x+1)^2-(2x-1)^2=8x .
\]
Since $x>0$, $\boxed{x=8}$, giving the $8$-$15$-$17$ right triangle.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair C=(0,0),B=(15,0),A=(0,8);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw((0.9,0)--(0.9,0.9)--(0,0.9));
label("$x=8$",(A+C)/2,W);label("$2x-1=15$",(B+C)/2,S);label("$2x+1=17$",(A+B)/2,NE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
