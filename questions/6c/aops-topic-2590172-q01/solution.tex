The quadratic $3x^2+px+84$ opens upward, so it is negative exactly between its two roots. Hence its roots are $4$ and $k$.

Substituting $x=4$: $48+4p+84=0$, so $p=-33$. The product of the roots is $\dfrac{84}{3}=28$, so $4k=28$ and $k=7$. Indeed $3x^2-33x+84=3(x-4)(x-7)$, which is negative exactly for $4<x<7$. So $\boxed{p=-33,\ k=7}$.
