Karle, Kyle and Kattie play Ketchup Wars, which is played in rounds. At the start of a round, every remaining player chooses a cooldown time, a whole number of seconds from $10$ to $19$ inclusive, with no two players choosing the same cooldown. All remaining players squirt ketchup at the start of the round, and then each squirts again every time their cooldown elapses. The round ends the first time after its start that all remaining players squirt at the same moment; then one player is eliminated, and the next round starts at that moment. The game ends when only one player remains. What is the least possible length of the game, in seconds?
