An item has original price $P$ dollars. After an increase of $60\%$ its price would be $8a$ dollars, and after a $70\%$ discount its price would be $3b$ dollars. Express $P$ in terms of $a$, and in terms of $b$, and find the relation between $a$ and $b$.
