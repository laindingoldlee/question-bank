An increase of $60\%$ multiplies the price by $1.6$, so $1.6P=8a$ and $P=\dfrac{8a}{1.6}=\boxed{5a}$.

A discount of $70\%$ leaves $30\%$ of the price, so $0.3P=3b$ and $P=\dfrac{3b}{0.3}=\boxed{10b}$.

Hence $5a=10b$, that is, $\boxed{a=2b}$.
