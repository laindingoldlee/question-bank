At the lowest vertex, let the three positive vertical edge increments be $u\le v\le w$. The eight subset sums of these increments are exactly $0,1,\ldots,7$. The smallest positive sum forces $u=1$, then the smallest missing sum forces $v=2$, and then $w=4$. The three edge directions are orthonormal. If the side length is $L$, their vertical components are $\dfrac{1}{L} , \dfrac{2}{L} , \dfrac{4}{L}$, whose squared sum is 1. Thus $L^2=1+4+16=21$, giving $\boxed{\sqrt{21}}$.
