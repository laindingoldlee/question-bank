Divisible by $4$: the last two digits $\overline{A8}$ form a multiple of $4$, so $A$ is even. Divisible by $3$: the digit sum $26+A$ is a multiple of $3$, so $A\in\{1,4,7\}$. Hence $A=\boxed{4}$.
