The common perimeter is $6.2+8.3+9.5=24$ cm. Hence each square side is $24\div4=6$ cm, and its area is $6^2=\boxed{36\text{ cm}^2}$, the second choice.
\begin{asy}
import geometry;
usepackage("amsmath");
size(260);
pair A=(0,0),B=(9.5,0),C=(59.8/19,sqrt(6.2^2-(59.8/19)^2));draw(A--B--C--cycle);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,N);label("$9.5$",(A+B)/2,S);label("$6.2$",(A+C)/2,NW);label("$8.3$",(B+C)/2,NE);pair D=(13,0),ptE=(19,0),F=(19,6),G=(13,6);draw(D--ptE--F--G--cycle);label("$D$",D,SW);label("$E$",ptE,SE);label("$F$",F,NE);label("$G$",G,NW);label("$6$",(D+ptE)/2,S);
\end{asy}
