Set $O=(0,0),A=(4,0),E=(-e,0),R=(0,r),G=(0,-g)$. Intersecting chords give $4e=rg$. Since $EL:LR=9:4$, $L=(-\dfrac{4e}{13},\dfrac{9r}{13})$. The midpoint of $AG$ is $(2,-\dfrac{g}{2})$; collinearity with $L,O$ gives $g=\dfrac{9r}{e}$. These yield $r=\dfrac{2e}{3},g=6$. Since $ER=13$, $e=3\sqrt{13}$. Therefore $[LOG]=\dfrac{1}{2}g\cdot\dfrac{4e}{13}=\boxed{\dfrac{36\sqrt{13}}{13}}$.
