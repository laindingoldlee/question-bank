As an April Fool's prank, Sean hacks his teacher's digital $12$-hour clock and replaces each digit by a letter, with different digits replaced by different letters. Right now the clock displays $\textbf{M:AT}$, and $14$ minutes later it displays $\textbf{A:TM}$. Find the value of $\textbf{A}\times\textbf{M}$.
