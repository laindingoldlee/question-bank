If the net added amount is $ S $, the discriminant is $ 1-4(1+S)=-3-4S $. Because 2017 is odd, $ S $ is an odd integer and the discriminant is nonnegative exactly when $ S\le-1 $. Reversing every turn pairs a positive-net outcome with a negative-net outcome of equal probability. There is no zero net, so the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$.
