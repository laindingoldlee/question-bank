Rational numbers $a_1,\ldots,a_6,b_1,\ldots,b_7$ satisfy, for every integer $n\ge12$,
\[\sum_{i=1}^6\sum_{j=1}^7a_i b_j\binom ni\binom nj((i+j)n-ij)=26n^2\binom n{12}+5n^2\binom n5.\]
It is given that $a_5b_3$ has a unique value. Write it as $\dfrac{p}{q}$ with coprime positive integers $p,q$, and find $p+q$.
