The segment $P_iP_j$ ($i<j$) contains no red point exactly when both endpoints are among $P_1,\dots,P_5$ or both are among $P_{16},\dots,P_{20}$. That happens for $2\dbinom52=20$ of the $\dbinom{20}2=190$ segments. The probability is $1-\dfrac{20}{190}=\boxed{\dfrac{17}{19}}$.
