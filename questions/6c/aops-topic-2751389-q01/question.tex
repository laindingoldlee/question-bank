There are $20$ points $P_1,P_2,\dots,P_{20}$ on a line, in that order. The $10$ middle points $P_6,\dots,P_{15}$ are red and the rest are blue. Two of the points are chosen at random and the segment joining them is drawn. What is the probability that at least one red point lies on the segment (endpoints included)?
