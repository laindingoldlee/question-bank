Downstream and upstream speeds are $14$ and $6$. For one-way distance $d$, the upstream fraction of time is $\dfrac{\dfrac{d}{6}}{\dfrac{d}{6}+\dfrac{d}{14}}=\dfrac{14}{20}=\boxed{70\%}$.
