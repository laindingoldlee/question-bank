Choose $B=(0,0),E=(4,0)$ and write $A=(u,v),C=(tu,-tv)$ with $t>0$, since $BE$ bisects the angle at $B$. We may take $v>0$. The median endpoint is $D = (\dfrac{t u}{2} , - \dfrac{t v}{2})$. Perpendicularity to $BE$ forces $\dfrac{t u}{2} = u$; nondegeneracy gives $u\ne0$, so $t=2$. Thus $AD=2v=4$, giving $v=2$. The intercept of $AC$ on the horizontal axis is $(\dfrac{4 u}{3} , 0)$, so $u=3$. The side lengths are $\sqrt{13},2\sqrt{13},3\sqrt5$, of which $\boxed{\sqrt{13}}$ is shortest.
