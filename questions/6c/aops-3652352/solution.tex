By symmetry consider the portion with $x,y\le\dfrac{1}{2}$ in the square $[0,1]^2$. The farthest vertex is $(1,1)$, so the active boundary is $(1-x)^2+(1-y)^2=1$. This quarter-region has area $\int_{\dfrac{1}{2}}^{\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}}(\sqrt{1-u^2}-\dfrac{1}{2})\,du=\dfrac{\pi}{12}-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}+\dfrac{1}{4}$. Multiplying by four gives $\boxed{1+\dfrac{\pi}{3}-\sqrt3}$. The integral can also be evaluated as a 30-degree circular sector minus a right triangle and a rectangle.

\end{asy}
