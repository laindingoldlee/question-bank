Modulo $9$, $20\equiv2$ and $2^6\equiv1$, so $20^{19}\equiv2$. The integer is even, and of the two residues modulo $18$ congruent to $2$ modulo $9$, namely $2,11$, only $2$ is even. Thus the remainder is $\boxed2$.
