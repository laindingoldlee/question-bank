A barrel was exactly $\dfrac{2}{3}$ full of wine. The publican took out $1$ litre less than half of its contents. Then he returned and took out $1$ litre less than $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of what remained. He had $15$ litres left. How many litres did the barrel hold?
