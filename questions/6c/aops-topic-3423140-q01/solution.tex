The first ten terms are $2,3,5,8,13,21,35,58,96,160$. Because $a_{n-1}$ is an integer, the fractional part of $\dfrac{5}{3}a_{n-1}$ is at most $\dfrac{2}{3}$. Thus $\dfrac{5}{3}a_{n-1}-\dfrac{2}{3}\le a_n\le\dfrac{5}{3}a_{n-1}$. Both desired bounds hold for $n=1$. Assuming them for $n-1$, the lower estimate becomes $a_n\ge\dfrac{5}{3}\left((\dfrac{5}{3})^{n-2}+1\right)-\dfrac{2}{3}=(\dfrac{5}{3})^{n-1}+1$, while the upper estimate becomes $a_n\le2(\dfrac{5}{3})^{n-1}$. Induction proves both.
