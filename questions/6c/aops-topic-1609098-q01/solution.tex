Two vectors are orthogonal exactly when their dot product is $0$:
\[
(a,2)\cdot(a,-2)=a^2-4=0\qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad a=\pm2 .
\]
Both values work. For $a=2$ the vectors $(2,2)$ and $(2,-2)$ point along the lines $y=x$ and $y=-x$, which are perpendicular; for $a=-2$ we get their mirror images $(-2,2)$ and $(-2,-2)$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((-3.2,0)--(3.2,0),gray,Arrow(TeXHead));draw((0,-3)--(0,3),gray,Arrow(TeXHead));
label("$x$",(3.2,0),E,gray);label("$y$",(0,3),N,gray);
draw((0,0)--(2,2),blue+linewidth(1),Arrow(TeXHead));draw((0,0)--(2,-2),blue+linewidth(1),Arrow(TeXHead));
draw((0,0)--(-2,2),red+linewidth(1),Arrow(TeXHead));draw((0,0)--(-2,-2),red+linewidth(1),Arrow(TeXHead));
pair u=unit((1,1)),v=unit((1,-1));real s=0.4;
draw(s*u--s*(u+v)--s*v,blue);
pair p=unit((-1,1)),q=unit((-1,-1));
draw(s*p--s*(p+q)--s*q,red);
label("$(2,2)$",(2,2),NE,blue);label("$(2,-2)$",(2,-2),SE,blue);
label("$(-2,2)$",(-2,2),NW,red);label("$(-2,-2)$",(-2,-2),SW,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

So $a=\boxed{2\text{ or }-2}$.
