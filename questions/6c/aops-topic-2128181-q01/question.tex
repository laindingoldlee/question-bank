It is the year $2017$. For each $N=1,2,3,\dots,2017$, your friend says: ``The year I was born in is a multiple of $N$.'' Your friend is at most $40$ years old, so was born in one of the years $1976$ to $2017$. What is the maximum possible number of true statements among these $2017$?
