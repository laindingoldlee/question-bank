The product is odd only if both numbers are odd, which has probability $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{1}{4}$. So the product is even with probability $1-\dfrac{1}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{4}}$ (that is, $\dfrac{27}{36}$, as the poster found).
