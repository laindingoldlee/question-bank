For $n=1$ the number is $0$, which is not prime.

For $n\ge2$, by the difference of squares,
\[n^{2020}-1=\left(n^{1010}-1\right)\left(n^{1010}+1\right).\]
Both factors are integers greater than $1$: $n^{1010}-1\ge2^{1010}-1>1$, and $n^{1010}+1>n^{1010}-1$. A product of two integers greater than $1$ is composite, so $n^{2020}-1$ is not prime. $\blacksquare$
