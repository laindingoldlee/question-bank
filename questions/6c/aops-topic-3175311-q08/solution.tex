Factoring gives $u\#v=u^2+uv+v^2$. Vieta gives $a+b+c=-2$ and $ab+bc+ca=3$, hence $a^2+b^2+c^2=4-6=-2$. The requested sum is $2(a^2+b^2+c^2)+(ab+bc+ca)=-4+3=\boxed{-1}$. The roots are distinct: a repeated root would satisfy both the polynomial and $3x^2+4x+3=0$. Eliminating the cubic gives $x^2+3x+6=0$, and then $5x+15=0$, forcing $x=-3$, which does not satisfy the derivative equation.
