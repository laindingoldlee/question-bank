Consider the infinite series $1+1-1+1-1+1-\cdots$ (a first term $1$, followed by alternating terms $+1$ and $-1$).
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Does the series converge?
\item[(b)] Let $s_n$ be the sum of the first $n$ terms. Find $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\dfrac{s_1+s_2+\cdots+s_n}{n}$.
\end{enumerate}
