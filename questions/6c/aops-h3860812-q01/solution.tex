Let the opposite side lengths be $a,b,c$, the area be $K$, and the semiperimeter be $s$. Since $K=\dfrac{ah_a}{2}=\dfrac{bh_b}{2}=\dfrac{ch_c}{2}$,
\[
\dfrac{1}{h_a}+\dfrac{1}{h_b}+\dfrac{1}{h_c}
=\dfrac{a+b+c}{2K}=\dfrac{s}{K}.
\]
The inradius area formula $K=rs$ then gives the required value $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{r}}$.

pair A=(1,3),B=(0,0),C=(5,0);real a=length(B-C),b=length(C-A),c=length(A-B);pair I=(a*A+b*B+c*C)/(a+b+c);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(circle(I,I.y),blue);draw(A--(1,0),dashed);label("$A$",A,(0,1));label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$h_a$",(1,1.7),(-1,0));draw(I--(I.x,0),red);label("$r$",(I+(I.x,0))/2,(1,0));
\end{asy}
