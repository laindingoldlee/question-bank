Write the digits as $ a,b,c $. If $ b,c\le4 $, there are no carries from either lower position, and concatenating $ 2a,2b,2c $ gives the correct double, whether $ 2a $ has one or two digits. If $ b\ge5 $ or $ c\ge5 $, concatenation shifts an earlier positive contribution farther left than ordinary multiplication does, so it exceeds $ 200a+20b+2c $. More explicitly its value is $ 2a\cdot10^{\ell_b+\ell_c}+2b\cdot10^{\ell_c}+2c $, where $\ell_b,\ell_c $ are 1 or 2 according as their doubled digits have one or two digits; if either length is 2, the first term alone exceeds its correct positional contribution, and no term decreases. Thus exactly $ 9\cdot5\cdot5=\boxed{225}$ numbers work.
