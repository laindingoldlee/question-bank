The first $8$ games give $7+4+3+6+8+3+1+5=37$ points. After the ninth game the total $37+x$ with $0\le x\le9$ is a multiple of $9$, so $37+x=45$ and $x=8$. After the tenth game $45+y$ with $0\le y\le9$ is a multiple of $10$, so $y=5$. The product is $8\cdot5=\boxed{\textbf{(B) }40}$.
