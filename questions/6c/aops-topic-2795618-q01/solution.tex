(Necessity, for completeness: a multiple of $20$ ends in two even digits, so it is never alternating. The converse is IMO Shortlist 2004 N6.)

The list is the positive integers not divisible by $20$, starting with $1$. The last of the first $N$ numbers is $700-1=699$, which is not a multiple of $20$. Among $1,\dots,699$ there are $\left\lfloor\dfrac{699}{20}\right\rfloor=34$ multiples of $20$, so $N=699-34=\boxed{665}$.
