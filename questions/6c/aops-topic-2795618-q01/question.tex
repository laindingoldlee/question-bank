A positive integer is alternating if every two consecutive decimal digits have different parity. The positive integers that have an alternating multiple are listed in increasing order. You may use the known theorem that $n$ has an alternating multiple if and only if $20\nmid n$. If the first $N$ numbers of the list have first and last term summing to $700$, find $N$.
