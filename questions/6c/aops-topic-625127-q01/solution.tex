The midpoint of $AB$ is $M=\left(\dfrac{3}{2},3\right)$, and $AB$ has slope $\dfrac{4-2}{2-1}=2$, so the perpendicular bisector has slope $-\dfrac{1}{2}$:
\[
y-3=-\dfrac{1}{2}\left(x-\dfrac{3}{2}\right).
\]
At $x=4$: $c=3-\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{5}{2}=3-\dfrac{5}{4}=\dfrac{7}{4}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
pair A=(1,2),B=(2,4),M=(1.5,3),Q=(4,1.75);
draw((-0.5,0)--(5,0),Arrow(TeXHead)); draw((0,-0.3)--(0,5),Arrow(TeXHead));
label("$x$",(5,0),E); label("$y$",(0,5),N);
draw(A--B,linewidth(0.9));
pair u=unit((2,-1));
draw(M-1.6*u--Q+0.9*u,blue+linewidth(0.9));
draw(M+0.2*unit(B-A)--M+0.2*unit(B-A)+0.2*u--M+0.2*u);
dot(A);dot(B);dot(M);dot(Q,red);
label("$A(1,2)$",A,W);label("$B(2,4)$",B,W);label("$M$",M,2.2*dir(-70));
label("$(4,c)$",Q+(0,0.12),N,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Check: $(4,c)$ must be equally far from $A$ and $B$: $9+(c-2)^2=4+(c-4)^2$ gives $9-4c+4=4-8c+16$, so $4c=7$. The answer is $\boxed{\text{(B) }\dfrac{7}{4}}$.
