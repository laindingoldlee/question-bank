There is a unique angle $\theta$ between $0^\circ$ and $90^\circ$ such that, for nonnegative integers $n$, $\tan(2^n\theta)$ is positive when $n$ is a multiple of $3$ and negative otherwise. The degree measure of $\theta$ is $\dfrac pq$, where $p$ and $q$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $p+q$.
