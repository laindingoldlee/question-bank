Write the right side as $\sin (\dfrac{\pi}{2} - \pi \sin \theta)$. Equality of sines gives either $\cos \theta + \sin \theta = \dfrac{1}{2} + 2 k$ or $\cos \theta - \sin \theta = \dfrac{1}{2} + 2 k$ for integer $k$. Each left side lies in $[-\sqrt2,\sqrt2]$, forcing $k=0$. Apply $\cos \theta \pm \sin \theta = \sqrt{2} \cos (\theta \mp \dfrac{\pi}{4})$ to obtain the result.
