A regular tetrahedron stands on a plane on one of its faces. It is rolled repeatedly, each time tipping over one of the edges of the bottom face (no sliding). After some rolls it comes back to its original position, standing on the same triangle of the plane. Must each vertex of the tetrahedron be back at its original place?
