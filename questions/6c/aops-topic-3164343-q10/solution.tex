The summand equals $\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{1}{(2k-1)(2k+1)}+\dfrac{1}{(2k+3)(2k+5)}\right)$, as bringing to a common denominator verifies. Use $\dfrac{1}{u(u+2)}=\dfrac{1}{2}(\dfrac{1}{u}-\dfrac{1}{u+2})$. The first resulting series is $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and the second $\dfrac{1}{10}$. Thus the total is $\dfrac{1}{2}(\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{10})=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{10}}$.
