A monic cubic $P$ has real coefficients and nonzero roots $a,b,c$, counted over $\mathbb C$. Suppose $\dfrac{1}{a}+\dfrac{1}{b}+\dfrac{1}{c}=4$, $a^2+b^2+c^2=36$, $P(1)=2$, and the coefficient of $x^2$ is negative. Express $|P(0)|=\dfrac{u+v\sqrt w}{z}$ in reduced form, with $w$ squarefree. Find $u+v+w+z$.
