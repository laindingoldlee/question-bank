Write $s=a+b+c$, $t=ab+bc+ca$, $u_0=abc$. Then $s>0$, $t=4u_0$, $s^2-2t=36$, and $1-s+t-u_0=2$. Thus $u_0=\dfrac{s+1}{3}$ and $3s^2-8s-116=0$. Its positive root is $s=\dfrac{4+2\sqrt{91}}{3}$, giving $|P(0)|=u_0=\dfrac{7+2\sqrt{91}}{9}$. Therefore the requested sum is $\boxed{109}$. These coefficients define a monic real cubic with nonzero constant term, so the conditions are attainable.
