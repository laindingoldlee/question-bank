The factorization is $561=3\cdot11\cdot17$. For each prime $p$ in this list, $p-1$ divides $560$. If $p\mid a$, then $a^{561}-a\equiv0\pmod p$ immediately. Otherwise Fermat's little theorem gives
\[
a^{560}\equiv1\pmod p,\qquad a^{561}\equiv a\pmod p.
\]
Thus each of the three pairwise relatively prime factors divides the difference, so their product $\boxed{561}$ does as well.
