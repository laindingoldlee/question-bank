An isosceles trapezoid $ABCD$ has $AB\parallel CD$, $AB=1$, $CD=2$, and $BC=AD=1$. Choose $E$ uniformly on $CD$, and let $F\in CD$ satisfy $EC=FD$. Let $G$ be the intersection of the lines $AE$ and $BF$ whenever they are not parallel. What is the probability that $\angle AGB>30^\circ$? The single parallel case has probability zero.
