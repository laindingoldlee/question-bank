The hexagon has side 2 and area $6\sqrt3$. Each external equilateral triangle has area $\sqrt3$. Each circular cap beyond a hexagon side lies inside its attached triangle: after taking the chord horizontal, the circle cap has tangents making 30 degrees with the chord at its endpoints, whereas the triangle sides make 60 degrees, and the cap is on the inner side of both triangle edges. Thus the circle is contained in the union of the hexagon and six external triangles. The triangles have disjoint interiors and do not meet the hexagon interior. The desired union is exactly this polygonal union, of area $6\sqrt3+6\sqrt3=\boxed{12\sqrt3}$.

\end{asy}
