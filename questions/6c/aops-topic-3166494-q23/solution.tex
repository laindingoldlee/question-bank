Heron's formula gives area $\sqrt{42\cdot16\cdot14\cdot12}=336$, so the inradius is $r=\dfrac{336}{42}=8$. The inward parallels are each at distance $8-d$ from the original incenter. They therefore form the image of the original triangle under homothety centered there with ratio $\dfrac{8-d}{8}$. The original perimeter is $84$, giving new perimeter $\boxed{84\left(1-\dfrac{d}{8}\right)}$.
