The angle at $A$ is 90 degrees, so the other two angles are 25 and 65 degrees. Put $B$ at polar angle 0 and $E$ at 180 degrees; then $A$ has polar angle 50 degrees. The parallel chord $ED$ forces $D$ to have angle 230 degrees, and the horizontal chord $DC$ gives $C$ angle 310 degrees. The arc $BD$ not containing $C$ has measure 230 degrees. Thus the inscribed angle is $\boxed{115^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair B=dir(0),A=dir(50),E0=dir(180),D=dir(230),C=dir(310);draw(circle((0,0),1));draw(E0--B--A--E0--D--C--B);draw(C--D);label("A",A,NE);label("B",B,E);label("C",C,SE);label("D",D,SW);label("E",E0,W);
\end{asy}
