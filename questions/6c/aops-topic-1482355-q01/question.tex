Bob and Alex play a game with a $4$-digit string $\overline{abcd}$ of decimal digits. On each turn the player to move increases or decreases one digit by $1$, keeping every digit between $0$ and $9$, and the same string may never appear more than twice during the game. The game ends as soon as the string becomes $\overline{badc}$ (the first two digits swapped and the last two digits swapped), and the player who made that move wins.

The starting string is $1337$, so the target is $3173$, and Bob moves first. Prove that Bob can never win.
