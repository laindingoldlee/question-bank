Use $A=(0,0)$, $B=\left(\dfrac{12}{5},0\right)$, $C=\left(\dfrac{12}{5},5\right)$, $P=(0,t)$. The perpendicularity condition gives
\[
\dfrac{144}{25}-t(5-t)=0.
\]
Thus $t=\dfrac{9}{5}$ or $\dfrac{16}{5}$. Moreover $BP^2=5t$ and $PC^2=5(5-t)$, so $BP^2+PC^2=25$ and $BP\cdot PC=5\sqrt{t(5-t)}=12$. Consequently $(BP+PC)^2=25+24=49$, giving $\boxed7$ in either configuration.

\end{asy}
