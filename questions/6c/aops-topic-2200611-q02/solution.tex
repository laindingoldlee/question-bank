The first two congruences say $x\equiv-1$ modulo $3$ and $5$, so $x\equiv-1\pmod{15}$: $x\in\{14,29,44,59,74,89,104,119,\dots\}$. Modulo $8$ these are $6,5,\dots$, so the smallest is $x=\boxed{29}$ (indeed $29=3\cdot9+2=5\cdot5+4=8\cdot3+5$). By the Chinese remainder theorem the solutions are exactly $x\equiv29\pmod{120}$.
