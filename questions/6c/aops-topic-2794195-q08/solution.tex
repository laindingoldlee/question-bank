Use legs along the axes, so the incenter is $(1,1)$ and its distance to every side is 1. The leash disk has radius $\sqrt2$. Each side cuts off a cap with central angle 90 degrees, of area $\dfrac{1}{4}\pi(\sqrt2)^2-\dfrac{1}{2}(\sqrt2)^2\sin90^\circ=\dfrac{\pi}{2}-1$. These three caps have disjoint interiors: for two side lines, the closest point outside both halfplanes is their common vertex, because the incenter is on the interior angle bisector; the three vertex distances are $\sqrt2,\sqrt5,\sqrt{10}$. Thus no positive-area overlap occurs within the disk. The accessible area is $2\pi-3(\dfrac{\pi}{2}-1)=\boxed{3+\dfrac{\pi}{2}}$.
