\textbf{Meeting.} Let $m$ and $s$ be MrBeast's and your arrival times, in minutes after 11:00. The visits $[m,m+15]$ and $[s,s+20]$ overlap exactly when $s\le m+15$ and $m\le s+20$. In the $60\times60$ square of equally likely pairs $(m,s)$, the failing pairs form two triangles: $s>m+15$ (legs $45$) and $s<m-20$ (legs $40$). So
\[P(\text{meet})=1-\dfrac{\dfrac{45^2}2+\dfrac{40^2}2}{3600}=1-\dfrac{1812.5}{3600}=\dfrac{143}{288}.\]

\textbf{The game.} Let $N(k)$ be the number of the $36$ dice outcomes with sum at least $k$:
\[N(2)=36,\ N(3)=35,\ N(4)=33,\ N(5)=30,\ N(6)=26,\ N(7)=21,\ N(8)=15,\ N(9)=10,\ N(10)=6,\ N(11)=3,\ N(12)=1.\]
The expected prize for choosing $k$ is $1000k\cdot\dfrac{N(k)}{36}$, and $k\,N(k)$ takes the values $36,72,105,132,150,156,147,120,90,60,33,12$ for $k=1,\dots,12$. The maximum is at $k=6$, worth $1000\cdot\dfrac{156}{36}=\dfrac{13000}3$ dollars.

\textbf{Total.} The meeting and the dice are independent, so the expected winnings are
\[\dfrac{143}{288}\cdot\dfrac{13000}{3}=\boxed{\dfrac{232375}{108}\approx2151.62\text{ dollars}}.\]
