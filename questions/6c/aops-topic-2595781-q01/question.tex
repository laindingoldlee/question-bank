You and MrBeast each arrive at a coffee shop at a time chosen uniformly at random between 11:00 and 12:00, independently. MrBeast stays for $15$ minutes and you stay for $20$ minutes (even if this runs past 12:00). If your visits overlap, you play his game once: you name an integer $k$ from $1$ to $12$, he rolls two fair dice, and if the sum is at least $k$ you win $1000k$ dollars (otherwise nothing). If you choose $k$ to maximise your expected winnings, what is the expected amount of money you win?
