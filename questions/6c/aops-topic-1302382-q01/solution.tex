The houses before $x$ sum to $\dfrac{(x-1)x}{2}$ and those after it sum to $\dfrac{99\cdot100}{2}-\dfrac{x(x+1)}{2}$. So
\[
\dfrac{3x(x-1)}{2}=4950-\dfrac{x(x+1)}{2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad4x^2-2x-9900=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad2x^2-x-4950=0 .
\]
The discriminant is $1+39600=199^2$, so $x=\dfrac{1+199}{4}=\boxed{50}$. Check: $3\cdot1225=3675=4950-1275$.
