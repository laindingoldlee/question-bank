Let
\[\alpha=\sqrt{1-\sqrt{2-\sqrt{3-\sqrt{4-\sqrt5}}}}.\]
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the smallest possible degree of a nonzero polynomial $f(x)$ with rational coefficients such that $f(\alpha)=0$.
\item[(b)] If $f$ has this smallest degree and leading coefficient $1$, what is the sum of its coefficients?
\end{enumerate}
