Write $n=\prod p_i^{e_i}$. Since $\prod(e_i+1)=225$ is odd, every $e_i$ is even. The square-divisor count is $\prod\dfrac{e_i+2}{2}$. Splitting an odd composite factor $uv=e_i+1$ into two factors does not increase this count, because $\dfrac{uv+1}{2}\ge\dfrac{(u+1)(v+1)}{4}$, equivalent to $(u-1)(v-1)\ge0$. Splitting fully, $225=3\cdot3\cdot5\cdot5$, gives the minimum $2\cdot2\cdot3\cdot3=\boxed{36}$, attained by exponents $2,2,4,4$ on distinct primes.
