Let $x=a+b$, $y=b+c$, $z=c+a$. Then $a=\dfrac{x+z-y}{2}>0$ and similarly, so each of $x,y,z$ is less than the sum of the other two, and all are at least $2$. Factorisations of $340=2^2\cdot5\cdot17$ into three factors at least $2$ are $\{2,2,85\}$, $\{2,5,34\}$, $\{2,10,17\}$, $\{4,5,17\}$; in each, the largest factor exceeds the sum of the other two. So $\boxed{\text{no}}$, there are no such positive integers. (Even allowing $0$, none exist: checked directly.)
