Against fixed other submissions totalling $S_0$ from $t$ teams, your $x$ scores $x$ exactly when $x\le\dfrac{S_0+x}{t+1}$, equivalently $tx\le S_0$. If $t>0$, the best response is the largest integer at most both 15 and $\dfrac{S_0}{t}$, provided it is at least one. Without the other submissions, this is not a unique fixed number. Any profile where all teams submit the same $x$ is a pure Nash equilibrium: increasing one's number earns zero, and lowering it cannot improve the score.
