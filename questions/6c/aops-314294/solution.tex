If a pencil costs $c$ cents, then $c$ divides both $143$ and $195$. Their greatest common divisor is $13$, so $c=1$ or $13$. The $c=1$ case would require $195$ sixth graders, exceeding $30$. Therefore $c=13$, and the group sizes are $\dfrac{143}{13} = 11$ and $\dfrac{195}{13} = 15$. The difference is $\boxed4$.
