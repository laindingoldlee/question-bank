Call a real sequence $(a_n)_{n\ge1}$ admissible if $|a_n|<1$ for $n>1$, $a_ma_n=-a_{mn}$ for all positive integers $m,n$, $\sum_{n\ge1}a_n=-\dfrac{1}{12}$, and $a_{67}=\dfrac{1}{7}$. Among admissible sequences for which the sum $S$ of the positive terms is finite, determine the greatest constant $C$ for which $S\ge C$ always holds.
