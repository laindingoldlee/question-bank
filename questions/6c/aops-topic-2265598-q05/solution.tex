An average of $81$ over five tests means a total of $5\cdot81=405$ points. The first three tests give $76+94+87=257$, so the last two must total $405-257=148$. One of them can be at most $100$, so the other is at least $148-100=48$, and $48$ is possible (with $100$ on the other test). The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(A)}\ 48}$.
