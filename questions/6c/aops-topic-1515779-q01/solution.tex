Let Markus take $t$ hours per basket, so Avi takes $t+\dfrac{1}{2}$ hours. In $28$ hours they make $\dfrac{28}{t}$ and $\dfrac{28}{t+\dfrac{1}{2}}$ baskets, so
\[
\dfrac{28}{t}-\dfrac{28}{t+\dfrac{1}{2}}=1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 28\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=t\left(t+\dfrac{1}{2}\right)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 2t^2+t-28=0 .
\]
This factors as $(2t-7)(t+4)=0$, and $t>0$, so $t=\dfrac{7}{2}$. Avi takes $\dfrac{7}{2}+\dfrac{1}{2}=\boxed{4}$ hours per basket. (Check: in $28$ hours Markus makes $8$ baskets and Avi makes $7$.)
