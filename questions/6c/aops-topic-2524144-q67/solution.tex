Alex wins with probability $\dfrac{\binom75}{2^7}=\dfrac{21}{128}=\dfrac{84}{512}$, and Anthony with probability $\dfrac{\binom95}{2^9}=\dfrac{126}{512}$. So $\boxed{\text{Anthony}}$ is more likely to win ($\approx24.6\%$ against $\approx16.4\%$).
