Each pair picks $k+2k=3k$ apples with integer $0\le k\le50$, at most 150. Since $13\cdot150<2025\le14\cdot150$, at least 14 pairs are needed. Thirteen pairs picking 150 each and one picking 75 attain the bound, giving $\boxed{28}$ children. Every pair total is divisible by 3, so 2026, which is not, is impossible.
