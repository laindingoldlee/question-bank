\textbf{Last year.} $2p-104=2(p-52)$ is an even prime, so it equals $2$ and $p=53$ (and $53$, $107$ are indeed prime).

\textbf{Now.} The weights are $q+7$, $3q+53$ and $4q-10$. The father is the heaviest:
\[4q-10>3q+53\iff q>63,\qquad 4q-10>q+7\iff q>\dfrac{17}{3}.\]
So $q>63$, and the smallest prime above $63$ is $\boxed{67}$. (Then the weights are $74$, $254$ and $258$ pounds.)
