Last year my mother weighed $p$ pounds, I weighed $2p+1$ pounds and my father weighed $2p-104$ pounds, and all three weights were prime numbers. Now my mother weighs $q+7$ pounds, where $q$ is a prime, I weigh $3q+p$ pounds, and my father, who is now the heaviest of the three, weighs $4q-10$ pounds. Find the smallest possible value of $q$.
