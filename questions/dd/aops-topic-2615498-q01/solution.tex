Write $n=2m$. The equation is $\dfrac{2m(2m+1)}2=\dfrac{m(m+1)(2m+1)}6$, i.e. $m(2m+1)=\dfrac{m(m+1)(2m+1)}6$. Dividing by $m(2m+1)>0$ gives $m+1=6$, so $m=5$ and $n=\boxed{10}$.
