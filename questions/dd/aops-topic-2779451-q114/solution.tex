Completing squares gives $3(x-2)^2+4(y+1)^2+(z+3)^2=14$. Every point is within distance $\sqrt{14}$ of center $(2,-1,-3)$, since its squared ordinary distance is at most 14. The triangle inequality bounds the distance between two points by $2\sqrt{14}$. The two points $(2,-1,-3\pm\sqrt{14})$ attain it. Answer: $\boxed{2\sqrt{14}}$.
