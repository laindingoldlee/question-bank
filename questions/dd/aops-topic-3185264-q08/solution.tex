One colour cannot work because adjacent sides must differ. Two colours work by alternating around the even cycle of eight sides. Thus the minimum is $\boxed2$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
for(int i=0;i<8;++i)draw(2*dir(45*i)--2*dir(45*(i+1)),i%2==0 ? blue : red);label("Alternating colours",(0,0));
\end{asy}
