Assume it never stops. Each student retains their larger card, and after receiving the next card their maximum cannot decrease. The sum of these 25 maxima is a bounded integer, so eventually every maximum remains fixed forever. From then on every arriving card must be strictly smaller than the fixed maximum at that seat; equality would stop the game and a larger value would increase the maximum. Thus each of the 25 travelling cards circulates through every seat. There are 25 retained cards, an odd number. Since each label has only two copies, at least one label occurs exactly once among retained cards, leaving its other copy travelling. That copy eventually reaches its retained twin and stops the game, a contradiction.
