Opposite vertices $A,D$ are endpoints of a diameter of the circumcircle. Its radius equals the hexagon side, so $AD=2AB$ and $AB=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{2}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair[] P;for(int j=0;j<6;++j)P.push(dir(60*j));for(int j=0;j<6;++j)draw(P[j]--P[(j+1)%6]);draw(P[0]--P[3],blue);label("$A$",P[0],E);label("$B$",P[1],NE);label("$D$",P[3],W);
\end{asy}
