The centre is the centroid, so each median has length $\dfrac{3}{2}\cdot3=\dfrac{9}{2}$, which is the height. The side is $\dfrac{2}{\sqrt3}\cdot\dfrac{9}{2}=3\sqrt3$, and the area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot3\sqrt3\cdot\dfrac{9}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{27\sqrt3}{4}}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw(circle((0,0),3),gray); pair A=3*dir(90),B=3*dir(210),C=3*dir(330); draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); dot((0,0)); draw((0,0)--A,red); label("$3$",(0,1.5),E,red); label("$O$",(0,0),S);
label("$A$",A,N); label("$B$",B,SW); label("$C$",C,SE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
