Apply the law of cosines in triangles $ABM$ and $ACM$ with the supplementary angles at $M$ (equivalently, Stewart's theorem): $AB^2+AC^2=2AM^2+2BM^2$. So
\[AM^2=\dfrac{2b^2+2c^2-a^2}{4}=\dfrac{578+162-256}{4}=121,\]
