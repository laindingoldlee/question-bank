\textbf{(a)} From February $1$, $2002$ to February $1$, $2003$ is $12$ months, and from February $1$ to May $1$, $2003$ is $3$ more months (February, March, April). So it took $12+3=\boxed{15}$ months.

\textbf{(b)} Division is done before addition and subtraction, so
\[m+5\div24-8=15+\dfrac{5}{24}-8=7+\dfrac{5}{24}=\boxed{\dfrac{173}{24}}=7\dfrac{5}{24}.\]
