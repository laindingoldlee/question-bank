Each purchased cookie costs $p$ dollars, and every nonempty purchase receives one extra free cookie. Spending exactly 27.50 dollars buys some total number of cookies. If the paid-cookie price were 3 dollars lower, the same spending would buy twice that total, again including one free cookie. Find $p$.
