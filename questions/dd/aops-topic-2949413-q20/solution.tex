Let $k$ be the originally paid-for count. The new paid-for count is $2(k+1)-1=2k+1$. Therefore $pk=\dfrac{55}{2}$ and $(p-3)(2k+1)=\dfrac{55}{2}$. Eliminating $p$ gives $12k^2-49k-55=(k-5)(12k+11)=0$. Positivity forces $k=5$, so $\boxed{p=5.50\text{ dollars}}$.
