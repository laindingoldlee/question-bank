The first term is $-0.6$ and the common ratio is $\dfrac{3}{-0.6}=-5$ (also $\dfrac{-15}{3}=\dfrac{75}{-15}=-5$). So
\[
\boxed{c(1)=-0.6,\qquad c(n)=c(n-1)\cdot(-5)} .
\]
