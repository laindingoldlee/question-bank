Allow the centre $X$ of an annulus to vary over the disk of radius 19 concentric with the given disk. Each fixed point $P$ contributes to the count exactly when $2\le XP\le3$, a region of area $5\pi$. This entire region lies in the radius-19 disk because $OP\le16$. Integrating the number of covered points over possible centres therefore gives $650\cdot5\pi$. If every annulus covered at most nine points, this integral would be at most $9\cdot361\pi=3249\pi$, smaller than $3250\pi$. Hence some annulus covers at least $\boxed{10}$ points. Boundary choices do not affect the area argument.
