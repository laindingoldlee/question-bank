Divide by $2$: $3x^2+15x+25=0$. The discriminant is $15^2-4\cdot3\cdot25=225-300=-75<0$, so there are no real roots. Since $\sqrt{-75}=5i\sqrt3$, the quadratic formula gives
\[
x=\dfrac{-15\pm5i\sqrt3}{6}=\boxed{-\dfrac{5}{2}\pm\dfrac{5\sqrt3}{6}\,i}.
\]
Check with Vieta's formulas: the two roots have sum $-5=-\dfrac{15}{3}$ and product $\dfrac{25}{4}+\dfrac{75}{36}=\dfrac{25}{3}$, as required.
