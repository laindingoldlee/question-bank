Yes. A complex number is any expression $b+ai$ with $a,b\in\mathbb R$, and $0\cdot i=0$, so $b+0i=b$. Hence every real number $b$ is the complex number $b+0i$: the reals form the subset of complex numbers with imaginary part $0$. The conclusion should be stated as "every \emph{real} number is complex"; this does not mean every complex number is real (for example $i$ is not).
