For example,
\[\boxed{(1\times2+3)\times4\times5}=5\times20=100.\]
(Here the $1$ is used inside the sum, $1\times2+3=5$, instead of as an outside factor.) In fact, a computer search over all ways of inserting the four operations and parentheses shows that every expression equal to $100$ is one of these two, up to regrouping the products, such as $\left((1\times2+3)\times4\right)\times5$.
