Use $y=\dfrac{2x-7}{3}$ or $x=\dfrac{7+3y}{2}$. The pairs are $\boxed{(11,5),(14,7),(17,9),(1.4,-1.4),(4.4,0.6)}$. There are infinitely many real solutions, one for every real input $x$.
