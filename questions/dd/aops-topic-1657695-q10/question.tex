Joe has a paper rectangle with side lengths $x$ and $y$, where $x$ and $y$ are integers greater than $4$. He cuts out a $2\times2$ square from each corner, then folds the sides upward to make an open box. Finally, he closes the top with another rectangle. If the volume of the new shape is $216$ cubic units, how many ordered pairs $(x,y)$ are possible?
