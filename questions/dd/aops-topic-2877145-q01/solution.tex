Squares are $\equiv0$ or $1\pmod3$. If $3\nmid a$ and $3\nmid b$, then $a^2+b^2\equiv2\pmod3$, which is not a square residue. So $3\mid a$ or $3\mid b$, i.e. $3\mid ab$. $\blacksquare$
