In triangle $ABC$, mark points $D_1, \dots D_{9}$ on $BC$, $E_1, \dots E_{9}$ on $AC$, and $F_1, \dots F_{9}$ on $AB$ so that each side is divided into $10$ equal segments. For each $i=1, 2, \dots, 9$, draw the segments $AD_i$, $BE_i$, and $CF_i$. Find the number of nonoverlapping regions into which these segments partition triangle $ABC$.
