The two positive bases multiply to 1, so their $ x $ th powers $ u,v $ satisfy $ uv=1 $ and $ u+v=10 $. Therefore $(u-v)^2=100-4=96 $, giving $\boxed{\pm4\sqrt6}$. Both signs occur because replacing $ x $ by $-x $ interchanges $ u,v $, and $ u=5+2\sqrt6 $ can be obtained from the base $ 7+4\sqrt3>1 $ by a real exponent.
