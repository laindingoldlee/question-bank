Multiply by $9$. The coefficient of $a^2$ on the left is $4+1+4=9$, and likewise for $b^2$ and $c^2$. The coefficient of $ab$ is $8$ from the first square, $-4$ from the second and $-4$ from the third, i.e. $0$; likewise for $bc$ and $ca$. So the left side times $9$ is $9(a^2+b^2+c^2)$. $\blacksquare$
