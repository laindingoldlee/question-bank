Bianca is reading a book of $360$ pages and wants to read at least half of it by Friday. With probability $\dfrac15$ she is too lazy to read any of it; with probability $\dfrac25$ she reads exactly half of it; and with probability $\dfrac25$ she reads $\dfrac{7}{15}$ of it, and in that case, with probability $\dfrac13$, she also reads an extra $\dfrac{1}{15}$ of the book. What is the probability that she reads at least half of the book by Friday?
