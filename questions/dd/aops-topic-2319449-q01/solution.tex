Reading $\dfrac{7}{15}$ of the book is less than half, but $\dfrac{7}{15}+\dfrac{1}{15}=\dfrac{8}{15}$ is more than half. So she reads at least half exactly when she reads half of it, or reads $\dfrac{7}{15}$ and then the extra $\dfrac{1}{15}$. The probability is
\[\dfrac25+\dfrac25\cdot\dfrac13=\dfrac{6}{15}+\dfrac{2}{15}=\boxed{\dfrac{8}{15}}.\]
