Let $w$ feet be the length of each of the two sides perpendicular to the house. The side along the house is $60$ feet, so the fence consists of the opposite side, also $60$ feet, and two sides of length $w$: its length is $60+2w$. We need
\[60+2w\ge160,\qquad 2w\ge100,\qquad w\ge50.\]
So the play area is $\boxed{60\text{ feet (along the house) by }w\text{ feet, with }w\ge50}$. For example, exactly $160$ feet of fencing gives a $60$ by $50$ rectangle.
