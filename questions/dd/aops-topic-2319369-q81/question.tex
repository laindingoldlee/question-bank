You want to fence a rectangular play area for kids in the backyard. To save on fencing, the whole back wall of your house, which is $60$ feet wide, forms one side of the rectangle, and the fence forms the other three sides. You want to use at least $160$ feet of fencing. What are the possible dimensions of the play area?
