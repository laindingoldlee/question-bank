Each of $a,\dots,e$ appears in $4$ of the $10$ pair sums, so $4(a+b+c+d+e)=164$ and $a+b+c+d+e=41$. The smallest sum is $a+b=5$ and the largest is $d+e=29$, so $c=41-34=\boxed{7}$. (Indeed $a,b,c,d,e=2,3,7,11,18$.)
