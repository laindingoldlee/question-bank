Let $H=1+\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{3}+\cdots+\dfrac{1}{10}=\dfrac{7381}{2520}$. The probabilities of Al's die must add up to $1$, so $P(\text{Al rolls }n)=\dfrac{1}{nH}$. (They cannot be exactly $\dfrac{1}{n}$, because $H>1$.)

If Al rolls $n$, Jimmy's number is greater with probability $\dfrac{10-n}{10}$. Hence
\[
P=\sum_{n=1}^{10}\dfrac{1}{nH}\cdot\dfrac{10-n}{10}=\dfrac{1}{10H}\sum_{n=1}^{10}\left(\dfrac{10}{n}-1\right)=\dfrac{10H-10}{10H}=1-\dfrac{1}{H} .
\]
So
\[
P=1-\dfrac{2520}{7381}=\boxed{\dfrac{4861}{7381}}.
\]
