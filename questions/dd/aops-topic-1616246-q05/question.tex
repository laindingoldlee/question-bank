Jimmy throws a dart at a board that shows one of the numbers $1,2,\dots,10$, each with probability $\dfrac{1}{10}$. Al rolls a $10$-sided die with faces $1,2,\dots,10$ that is loaded so that the probability of rolling $n$ is proportional to $\dfrac{1}{n}$. The two results are independent. What is the probability that Jimmy's number is greater than Al's number? Express your answer as a common fraction.
