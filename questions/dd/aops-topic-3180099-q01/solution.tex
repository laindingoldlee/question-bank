The two diagonals and the two lines through opposite side midpoints preserve the square, for $\boxed4$ lines. A symmetry must permute vertices, and these exhaust the possible reflection axes.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw((-1,-1)--(1,-1)--(1,1)--(-1,1)--cycle);draw((-1.3,0)--(1.3,0)^^(0,-1.3)--(0,1.3)^^(-1.2,-1.2)--(1.2,1.2)^^(-1.2,1.2)--(1.2,-1.2),dashed);
\end{asy}
