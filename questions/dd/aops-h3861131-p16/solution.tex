Condition on the three ordered score values. Their original problem labels are uniformly distributed among the six permutations, independently of those values. Each rank therefore has probability $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of matching its submitted position. Conditional expected credit is one third of the sum of the three scores. Each original score has mean $\dfrac{1}{2}$, so the unconditional expectation is $\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot3\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$.
