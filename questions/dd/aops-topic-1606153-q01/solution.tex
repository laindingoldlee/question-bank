Since $3^2=9\equiv1\pmod8$, the powers of $3$ alternate modulo $8$: $3^{\text{odd}}\equiv3$ and $3^{\text{even}}\equiv1$. Among the exponents $1,\dots,33$ there are $17$ odd and $16$ even ones, so
\[
3^1+3^2+\cdots+3^{33}\equiv17\cdot3+16\cdot1=67\equiv\boxed{3}\pmod8 .
\]
(Pairing also works: $3^{2j-1}+3^{2j}=4\cdot3^{2j-1}\equiv4\cdot3=12\equiv4$, so the $16$ pairs give $64\equiv0$, and the remaining term $3^{33}\equiv3$.)
