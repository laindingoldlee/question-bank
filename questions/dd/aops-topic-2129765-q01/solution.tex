Work backwards.
\begin{itemize}
\item At the second store, after paying the extra \$16 she had \$4, so before that she had \$20. This \$20 is what was left after spending one third of her money there, so it is two thirds of what she brought into the second store: she had $20\cdot\dfrac{3}{2}=\$30$.
\item At the first store, after paying the extra \$16 she had \$30, so before that she had \$46. This is half of her original money, so she started with $\boxed{\$92}$.
\end{itemize}
Check: from \$92 she spends $46+16=62$, leaving \$30; then she spends $10+16=26$, leaving \$4.
