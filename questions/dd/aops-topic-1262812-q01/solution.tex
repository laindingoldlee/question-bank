Since $25\equiv7\pmod{18}$, we need $7^{2015}\bmod 18$. Now $7^2=49\equiv13$ and $7^3\equiv 7\cdot13=91\equiv1\pmod{18}$. As $2015=3\cdot671+2$,
\[7^{2015}=\left(7^3\right)^{671}\cdot7^2\equiv 13\pmod{18}.\]
The remainder is $\boxed{13}$.
