In a box, there are $4$ green balls, $4$ blue balls, $2$ red balls, a brown ball, a white ball, and a black ball. These balls are randomly drawn out of the box one at a time (without replacement) until two of the same color have been removed. This process requires that at most $7$ balls be removed. The probability that $7$ balls are drawn can be expressed as $\dfrac{m}{n}$ , where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Compute $m + n$.
