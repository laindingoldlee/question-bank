A unit cube is held above a horizontal table so that exactly one of its vertices touches the table. Its \emph{shadow} is the set of points of the table directly below the cube (its vertical projection), and its \emph{height} is the distance from the table to the highest point of the cube. Prove that the area of the shadow equals the height, whatever the orientation of the cube.
