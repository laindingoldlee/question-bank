Equal sides $AB,BC$ are opposite equal angles at $C,A$. Thus $\angle BAC=\boxed{50^\circ}$. The angle sum then gives $\angle ABC=180^\circ-50^\circ-50^\circ=\boxed{80^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(-1,0),C=(1,0),B=(0,tan(50*pi/180));draw(A--B--C--cycle);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,N);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$50^\circ$",C+(-.3,.1));label("$AB=BC$",(0,-.4));
\end{asy}
