A tennis ball has a volume of $4.5\pi$ cubic inches. A cylindrical can contains three tennis balls; the radius of the can equals the radius of the balls, and the height of the can equals the combined diameters of the three balls. How many square inches are in the surface area of the can (including its top and bottom)? Express your answer as a common fraction in terms of $\pi$.
