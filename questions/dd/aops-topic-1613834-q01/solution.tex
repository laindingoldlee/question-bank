Place the cube as $[0,40]^3$. Each sphere has radius $10$ and touches the three faces through one corner, so its centre is $10$ from each of them: the centres are the eight points with every coordinate equal to $10$ or $30$. By symmetry the central sphere has its centre at the centre of the cube, $O=(20,20,20)$.

The distance from $O$ to the centre $P=(10,10,10)$ of a corner sphere is
\[
OP=\sqrt{10^2+10^2+10^2}=10\sqrt3 .
\]
The central sphere touches the corner sphere when $OP=r+10$, so
\[
r=10\sqrt3-10\approx17.32-10=7.32 .
\]
The figure shows the cross-section through two opposite vertical edges of the cube (a $40\times40\sqrt2$ rectangle); it contains the centres of four of the spheres and of the central sphere.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=sqrt(2);
draw((0,0)--(40*s,0)--(40*s,40)--(0,40)--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
pair[] P={(10*s,10),(30*s,10),(10*s,30),(30*s,30)};
for(int i=0;i<4;++i){draw(circle(P[i],10));dot(P[i]);}
pair O=(20*s,20); real r=10*sqrt(3)-10;
fill(circle(O,r),rgb(1,0.88,0.85));draw(circle(O,r),red+linewidth(0.9));dot(O);
draw(O--P[0],blue+dashed);
label("$O$",O,E);label("$P$",P[0],SW);
label("$10\sqrt3$",(19.8,10.4),blue+fontsize(8pt));
draw(P[0]--P[0]+(0,-10),dashed);label("$10$",P[0]+(0,-5),E);
label("$40$",(0,20),W);label("$40\sqrt2$",(20*s,0),S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Among the choices, $7.32$ is closest to $7.2$ (distance $0.12$, against $0.28$ for $7.6$), so the answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(B)}\ 7.2}$.
