Put $D=(0,0),C=(2t+5,0),A=(t,h),B=(t+5,h)$; equal legs give $t^2+h^2=25$. Since $CG=7$, the unit square has $G=(2t-2,0),H=(2t-3,0),F=(2t-2,1),E=(2t-3,1)$. The condition $BF=5$ becomes $(t-7)^2+(h-1)^2=25$, or $h+7t=25$. Substituting into the leg equation gives $(t-3)(t-4)=0$. Only $t=3$ yields positive $h$, namely four. Thus $D=(0,0),A=(3,4),E=(3,1)$, and $\boxed{[ADE]=\dfrac{9}{2}}$.
