Work modulo $7$. Since $3^3=27\equiv-1$, we have $3^{333}=(3^3)^{111}\equiv(-1)^{111}=-1$. Since $4^3=64\equiv1$, we have $4^{444}=(4^3)^{148}\equiv1$. So
\[
3^{333}+4^{444}\equiv-1+1=0\pmod 7,
\]
and the answer is $\boxed{\text{yes}}$.
