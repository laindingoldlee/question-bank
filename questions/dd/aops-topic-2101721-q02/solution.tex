We need $x\ge0$ for $\sqrt x$ to be defined, and then $\sqrt{x^2}=|x|=x$. The equation becomes $2\sqrt x=x$. Squaring gives $4x=x^2$, so $x(x-4)=0$ and $x=0$ or $x=4$.

Both check: $0+0=0$ and $2+2=4=\sqrt{16}$. So $x=\boxed{0\text{ or }4}$.
