First order the four directors and put the eight associates into the five gaps before, between, and after them. Each gap can contain at most two associates. Starting with two in every gap leaves a deficit of two: either one gap is empty (five choices), or two gaps contain one associate each ($\binom52=10$ choices). There is at most one empty gap, so three consecutive directors cannot occur. Thus there are fifteen type patterns. Filling the director and associate slots gives $N=15\cdot4!\cdot8!$, and $\boxed{\dfrac{N}{8!}=360}$.
