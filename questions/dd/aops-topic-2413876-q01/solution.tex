The faces $ABCD$, $ABEF$, $ADGF$ show that $B$, $D$, $F$ are the neighbours of $A$, that $E$ is the neighbour of $B$ and $F$, and that $G$ is the neighbour of $D$ and $F$; $H$ is opposite $A$. Use coordinates
\[A=(0,0,0),\ B=(6,0,0),\ C=(6,6,0),\ D=(0,6,0),\ F=(0,0,6),\ E=(6,0,6),\ G=(0,6,6),\ H=(6,6,6).\]

\textbf{Two parallel triangles.} Let $s(x,y,z)=x-y-z$. Then $s=0$ at $A$, $C$ and $E$, and $s=-6$ at $D$, $F$ and $H$. So triangle $ACE$ lies in the plane $x-y-z=0$ and triangle $DFH$ in the parallel plane $x-y-z=-6$. Both triangles are equilateral with side $6\sqrt2$ (each side is a face diagonal), so each has area
\[\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}\left(6\sqrt2\right)^2=18\sqrt3.\]
The distance from a point $P$ to the plane $x-y-z=t$ is $\dfrac{|s(P)-t|}{\sqrt3}$.

\textbf{The position of $P$.} The volume of $PACE$ is
\[\dfrac13\cdot18\sqrt3\cdot\dfrac{|s(P)|}{\sqrt3}=6|s(P)|=64,\qquad\text{so}\qquad s(P)=\pm\dfrac{32}{3}.\]
The condition $PB<PG$ decides the sign. Since $B-G=(6,-6,-6)$, $|B|^2=36$ and $|G|^2=72$,
\[PB^2-PG^2=-2P\cdot(B-G)+|B|^2-|G|^2=-12\,s(P)-36,\]
which is negative exactly when $s(P)>-3$. Hence $s(P)=\dfrac{32}{3}$.

\textbf{The answer.} The volume of $FPHD$ is
\[\dfrac13\cdot18\sqrt3\cdot\dfrac{|s(P)+6|}{\sqrt3}=6\left(\dfrac{32}{3}+6\right)=\boxed{100}.\]
(With $s(P)=-\dfrac{32}{3}$, the case excluded by $PB<PG$, it would be $6\cdot\dfrac{14}{3}=28$.)
