The vowels are A, E, A, I and the consonants are M, T, H, M, T, C, S.

First arrange the $7$ consonants, in which M and T each appear twice: $\dfrac{7!}{2!\,2!}=1260$ ways. They create $8$ gaps (before, between and after them), and the vowels must go into $4$ different gaps: $\dbinom84=70$ ways. Finally the vowels A, A, E, I can be ordered in $\dfrac{4!}{2!}=12$ ways. Hence
\[
n=1260\cdot70\cdot12=1058400=2^5\cdot3^3\cdot5^2\cdot7^2,
\]
and $n$ has $(5+1)(3+1)(2+1)(2+1)=216$ positive divisors. The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(D) }216}$.
