If the percentage as a fraction is $t$, then $a(1+t)=b(1-t)$. Thus $t=\dfrac{b-a}{a+b}$ and the agreed price is $a\left(1+\dfrac{b-a}{a+b}\right)=\boxed{\dfrac{2ab}{a+b}}$.
