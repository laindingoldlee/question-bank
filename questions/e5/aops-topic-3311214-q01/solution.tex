Two triangle boundaries have at most six crossings, since each side crosses the other convex triangle at most twice. The first triangle creates two regions and the $k$th adds at most $6(k-1)$, so the maximum is at most $3n(n-1)+2$. To attain it, take equilateral triangles with distinct sufficiently small rotations about one centre; every pair has six transverse crossings. Generic small translations avoid triple crossings while preserving all pair crossings. Each new boundary arc then adds a region. Thus the answer is $\boxed{3\cdot10^{1234567890}(10^{1234567890}+1)+2}$.
