Home to Waffle House takes $6$ blocks, $2$ of them north: $\dbinom62=15$ routes. Waffle House to work takes $4$ blocks east and $5$ north: $\dbinom94=126$ routes. The total is $15\cdot126=\boxed{1890}$.
