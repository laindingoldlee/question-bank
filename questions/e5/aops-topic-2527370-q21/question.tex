Jack walks along the streets of a grid from his home to his work, which is $8$ blocks east and $7$ blocks north, using exactly $15$ blocks (each block east or north). He must stop at Waffle House, which is $4$ blocks east and $2$ blocks north of his home. How many routes can he take?
