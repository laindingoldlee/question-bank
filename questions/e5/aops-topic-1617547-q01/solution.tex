Apply $t(2x)=2t(x)$ repeatedly, starting from $t(3)=1$:
\[
t(6)=2,\quad t(12)=4,\quad t(24)=8,\quad t(48)=16,\quad t(96)=32,\quad t(192)=64 .
\]
In general $t\left(3\cdot2^k\right)=2^k$. Since $t$ is one-to-one, $192$ is the only number that $t$ sends to $64$, so
\[
t^{-1}(64)=\boxed{192}.
\]
(Such functions exist, for example $t(x)=\dfrac {x}{3}$.)
