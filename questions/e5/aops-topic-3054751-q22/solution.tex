The angle subtending diameter $BC$ is $\angle A=90^\circ$. The angle sum gives $\angle C=180^\circ-90^\circ-40^\circ=\boxed{50^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair B=(-1,0),C=(1,0),A=dir(80);draw(arc((0,0),1,0,180));draw(A--B--C--cycle);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);
\end{asy}
