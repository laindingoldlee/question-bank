Scale to $A=(0,0),B=(2,0),C=(0,2)$, so $M=(0,1)$. Line $BM$ has slope $-\dfrac{1}{2}$, hence $AD$ has equation $y=2x$. Intersecting with $BC$, whose equation is $x+y=2$, gives $D=\left(\dfrac{2}{3},\dfrac{4}{3}\right)$.

The angle between the downward vertical $MA$ and vector $MB=(2,-1)$ has tangent $2$. The angle between the upward vertical $MC$ and vector $MD=\left(\dfrac{2}{3},\dfrac{1}{3}\right)$ also has tangent $2$. Both are acute, so they are equal. Thus $q:r=1:1$, and $\boxed{q+r=2}$.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

pair A=(0,0),B=(4,0),C=(0,4),M=(0,2),D=(4/3,8/3);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(B--M,blue);draw(A--D,red);draw(M--D,dashed);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,N);label("$M$",M,W);label("$D$",D,NE);
\end{asy}
