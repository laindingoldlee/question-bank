Waldo is counting down starting from $20$. Every second he says the largest integer greater than $1$ that is less than the number he said last and is relatively prime to every number he has already said. He stops when no such integer exists. What is the last number he says? (Two numbers are relatively prime if their greatest common divisor is $1$.)
