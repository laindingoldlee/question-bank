Simulate, keeping the numbers said so far.
\begin{itemize}
\item Start: $20$.
\item $19$ is coprime to $20$: say $19$. Then $18$ (shares $2$ with $20$) fails; $17$ works.
\item $16$ (factor $2$) and $15$ (shares $5$ with $20$) and $14$ (factor $2$) fail; $13$ works.
\item $12$ fails; $11$ works.
\item $10$ shares $2$ and $5$ with $20$, so it fails; $9=3^2$ is coprime to $20,19,17,13,11$: say $9$.
\item $8$ fails; $7$ works.
\item $6,4,2$ are even, $5$ divides $20$, and $3$ divides $9$, so nothing more can be said.
\end{itemize}
The numbers said are $20,19,17,13,11,9,7$, and the last is $\boxed{7}$. The tempting answer $10$ is impossible since $\gcd(10,20)=10$.
