Through day $n$, value is $5(2^{n+1}-2)$ and payments are $25n(n+1)$. For $n=1,\ldots,7$, the respective net values are $-40,-120,-230,-350,-440,-420,-130$, all negative. At $n=8$ the net is $2550-1800=750>0$. Thus the first profitable cumulative day is $\boxed8$.
