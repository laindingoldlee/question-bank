Let present ages be $F,S$ and the described time be $t$ years ago. Then $F=3(S-t)$ and $F-t=2(S+2)$. Substituting $t=S-\dfrac{F}{3}$ into the second gives $4F=9S+12$. Together with $F+S=55$, this yields $220-4S=9S+12$, so $S=16$ and $F=39$. The described time is $3$ years ago: the son was $13$ and father $36=2(16+2)$. Thus $\boxed{F=39,S=16}$.
