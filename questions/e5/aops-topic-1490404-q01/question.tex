Let $P(x)$ be a polynomial of degree $10$ with leading coefficient $20$ such that
\[
P(1)=17,\ P(2)=27,\ P(3)=37,\ \dots,\ P(10)=107 .
\]
Then $P(0)$ can be written as $a\cdot b!+c$, where $a$, $b$, $c$ are positive integers with $c<b$, and $b$ is as large as possible. Find $a+b+c$.

$\textbf{(A)}\ 18\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ 37\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ 116\qquad\textbf{(D)}\ 227\qquad\textbf{(E)}\ 360$
