The product is a square iff the exponents of $2$, $3$ and $5$ are all even. Record each face by its parity vector: $1,4\mapsto(0,0,0)$, $2\mapsto(1,0,0)$, $3\mapsto(0,1,0)$, $5\mapsto(0,0,1)$, $6\mapsto(1,1,0)$. By character averaging,
\[P=\dfrac18\sum_{s\in\{\pm1\}^3}\left(E\,s^{v}\right)^{10},\]
where $E\,s^{v}=\dfrac{1+1+s_1+s_2+s_3+s_1s_2}{6}$. The eight values are $1$, $\dfrac23$ (for $s=(+,+,-)$), $\dfrac13$ three times, and $0$ three times. Therefore
\[P=\dfrac18\left(1+\left(\dfrac23\right)^{10}+3\left(\dfrac13\right)^{10}\right)=\dfrac{3^{10}+2^{10}+3}{8\cdot3^{10}}=\dfrac{60076}{472392}=\dfrac{15019}{118098}.\]
So $a=\boxed{15019}$.
