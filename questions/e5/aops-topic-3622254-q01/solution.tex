The first quartile is $Q_1=\dfrac{4+5}{2}=4.5$. The third quartile is $Q_3=\dfrac{10+12}{2}=11$. The interquartile range, the spread of the middle half of the data under this convention, is $Q_3-Q_1=\boxed{6.5}$.
