Vertical angles are equal, adjacent angles on a straight line sum to $180^\circ$, and corresponding angles on parallel lines are equal. Thus all four acute angles are $65^\circ$, and all four obtuse angles are $180^\circ-65^\circ=\boxed{115^\circ}$.

\end{asy}
