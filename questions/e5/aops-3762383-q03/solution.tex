Label positions and correct digits by residues modulo 5. Each digit has exactly one aligning rotation, so the five rotation match-counts sum to five. Failure means each count is exactly one. Equivalently, for the permutation $p$, the five residues $p(i)-i$ must all be distinct. Checking the 24 permutations with $p(0)=0$ gives three such permutations (multiplication by 2, 3, or 4 modulo 5); adding a constant to all values gives 15 failing permutations in total. Thus the probability is $1 - \dfrac{15}{120} = \boxed{\dfrac{7}{8}}$. The 24-case check is performed by permuting $p(1),\ldots,p(4)$ and rejecting any repeated difference residue.
