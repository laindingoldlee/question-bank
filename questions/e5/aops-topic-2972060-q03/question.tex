Sixteen teams play a knockout tournament; every match is won by either team with probability $\dfrac12$, independently. Round-of-16 matches: $M_1$: NED--USA, $M_2$: ARG--AUS, $M_3$: JPN--CRO, $M_4$: BRA--KOR, $M_5$: ENG--SEN, $M_6$: FRA--POL, $M_7$: MAR--ESP, $M_8$: POR--SUI. Quarterfinals: winners of $M_1$ v $M_2$, $M_3$ v $M_4$, $M_5$ v $M_6$, $M_7$ v $M_8$. Semifinals: winners of the first two quarterfinals meet, and winners of the last two meet; the semifinal winners play the final. What is the probability that SUI beats POR, then wins its quarterfinal, then loses its semifinal?
