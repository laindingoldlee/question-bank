You face two doors: one leads to a prize and the other to losing. A guard stands in front of each door; one guard always tells the truth and the other always lies, but you do not know which is which. The guards answer only with the words "Ghi" and "Pon"; one of these means yes and the other means no, but you do not know which. You may choose one guard and ask a single yes-or-no question. What question lets you identify the prize door? Prove that it works.
