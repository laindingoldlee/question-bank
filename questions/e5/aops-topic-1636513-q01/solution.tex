Suppose $|x|\ge|y|$ (the other case is symmetric). Then $2x^2\ge2017$, so $x^2\ge1009$ and $|x|\ge32$; also $x^2\le2017$ gives $|x|\le44$. For $|x|=32,33,\dots,44$ the value $2017-x^2$ is
\[
993,\ 928,\ 861,\ 792,\ 721,\ 648,\ 573,\ 496,\ 417,\ 336,\ 253,\ 168,\ 81 .
\]
None of the first twelve is a perfect square, since $31^2<993<32^2$, $30^2<928<31^2$, $29^2<861<30^2$, $28^2<792<29^2$, $26^2<721<27^2$, $25^2<648<26^2$, $23^2<573<24^2$, $22^2<496<23^2$, $20^2<417<21^2$, $18^2<336<19^2$, $15^2<253<16^2$ and $12^2<168<13^2$; so the only square in the list is $81=9^2$, for $|x|=44$. Hence $\{|x|,|y|\}=\{44,9\}$ (indeed $1936+81=2017$), and with all choices of signs and order the solutions are
\[
\boxed{(x,y)=(\pm44,\pm9)\text{ and }(\pm9,\pm44)},
\]
eight solutions in all.
