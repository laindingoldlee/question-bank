For $0\le x\le3$, the expression is at most $81$, while for $x\ge4$ it is at least $256$. Thus a positive solution has $3<x<4$. If $x\ge\dfrac{10}{3}$, then $\lfloor3x\rfloor\ge10$, so the expression is at least $\dfrac{10}{3}\cdot33=110$, impossible. Hence $3<x<\dfrac{10}{3}$ and $\lfloor3x\rfloor=9$. Put $j=\lfloor9x\rfloor\in\{27,28,29\}$. Since $xj=88$, testing $x=\dfrac{88}{j}$ gives only $j=28$, namely $x=\dfrac{22}{7}$. For negative $x$, write $x=-y$ with $y>0$. The expression becomes $y\lceil y\lfloor y\lceil y\rceil\rfloor\rceil$. If $y\le3$, this is at most $81$. If $y>3$, then $\lceil y\rceil\ge4$, $\lfloor y\lceil y\rceil\rfloor\ge12$, and the final ceiling is at least $37$, making the value greater than $111$. Thus there are no negative solutions. The unique solution is $\boxed{\dfrac{22}{7}}$.
