Two couples each carry a total of $58$ kg of luggage. Each person receives the same free allowance of $P$ kg; excess weight is charged at one common positive rate per kg. The couples pay $11$ and $20$ dollars respectively. Individual luggage weights may be any nonnegative real numbers. What is the smallest possible $P$?
