If $P\ge29$, it cannot improve on the construction below. For $P<29$, a couple's total excess is at least $58-2P$ and at most $58-P$, attained by balanced luggage and by one person carrying all luggage respectively. Since the rate is common, $\dfrac{20}{11}\le\dfrac{58-P}{58-2P}$. Multiplying by the positive denominator yields $1160-40P\le638-11P$, so $P\ge18$. This is attainable: set $P=18$ and charge half a dollar per excess kg. Luggage weights $29,29$ give excess $22$ and fee $11$; weights $58,0$ give excess $40$ and fee $20$. Thus the minimum is $\boxed{18\text{ kg}}$.
