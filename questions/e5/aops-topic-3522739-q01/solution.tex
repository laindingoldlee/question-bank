Angle $ADB$ is right because $AB$ is a diameter. Thus $BD=5$ and $AD=5\sqrt3$. Since $DC$ is on line $DB$, triangle $ADC$ is right at $D$. Put $CD=m$ and $AC=k$. Then $k^2=m^2+75$, so $(k-m)(k+m)=75$. The positive odd factor pairs are $(1,75),(3,25),(5,15)$, producing $(k,m)=(38,37),(14,11),(10,5)$. Each has $m>0$ and gives a valid point beyond $D$. The required sum is $38+14+10=\boxed{62}$.

\end{asy}
