A circle has diameter $AB=10$. A ray from $B$ making angle $60^\circ$ with $BA$ meets the circle again at $D$, and $C$ lies beyond $D$ on this ray. If $AC$ and $CD$ are positive integers, find the sum of the possible values of $AC$.
