Linearity gives expectation $\dfrac{1}{10000}\sum_{j=1}^{100}j^2=\dfrac{100\cdot101\cdot201}{6\cdot10000}=\dfrac{6767}{200}$. The numerator is divisible by neither 2 nor 5, so the fraction is reduced. The answer is $\boxed{6767200}$.
