The AMC 8 has $25$ questions, each with $5$ answer choices of which exactly one is correct; a correct answer scores $1$ point and a wrong answer scores $0$. Suppose that $10$ million students take the test and that every student guesses on every question, choosing one of the $5$ choices uniformly at random. What is the expected value of the total of all the students' scores?
