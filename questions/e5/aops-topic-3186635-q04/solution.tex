The two-digit squares are $16,25,36,49,64,81$. Only $16$ and $36$ have exactly one prime neighbour: $17$ and $37$, respectively. Repetition is allowed, so the possible sums are $16+16$, $16+36$, and $36+36$, giving $\boxed{32,52,72}$.
