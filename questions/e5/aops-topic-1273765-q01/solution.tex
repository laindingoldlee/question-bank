Use coordinates $A=000$, $B=001$. By symmetry the other vertices fall into four classes:
\begin{itemize}
\item $X$: $010,100$ (neighbours of $A$ other than $B$);
\item $Y$: $011,101$ (neighbours of $B$ other than $A$);
\item $Z$: $110$;
\item $W$: $111$.
\end{itemize}
Let $p_V$ be the probability, starting from a vertex of class $V$, of eventually entering $B$ from $A$. Entering $B$ from a $Y$ vertex counts as failure. So
\[
p_A=\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{2}{3}p_X,\quad p_X=\dfrac{p_A+p_Y+p_Z}{3},\quad p_Y=\dfrac{p_X+p_W}{3},\quad p_Z=\dfrac{2p_X+p_W}{3},\quad p_W=\dfrac{p_Z+2p_Y}{3} .
\]
Solving this linear system gives $p_X=\dfrac{3}{8}$, $p_Y=\dfrac{5}{24}$, $p_Z=\dfrac{1}{3}$, $p_W=\dfrac{1}{4}$ (each equation can be checked directly), and
\[
p_A=\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot\dfrac{3}{8}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{12}}.
\]
