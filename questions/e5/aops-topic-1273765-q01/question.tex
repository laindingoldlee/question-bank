The vertices of a cube are $A,B,C,D,E,F,G,H$, with $A$ and $B$ adjacent. An animal starts at $A$ and repeatedly moves to a uniformly random adjacent vertex, stopping when it reaches $B$. What is the probability that it reaches $B$ by a move from $A$?
