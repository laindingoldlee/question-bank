Let the fraction of chocolate be $c$. The price per pound of the mixture is a weighted average:
\[
10c+4(1-c)=6\iff6c=2\iff c=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{3}}.
\]
Quick version: $6$ is $2$ above $4$ and $4$ below $10$, so the weights of chocolate and nuts are in the ratio $2:4=1:2$.
