A shop sells chocolates at $\$12$ for $6$ chocolates, and gives $2$ free chocolates for every empty chocolate packet returned. A boy spends $\$42$ on chocolates. Every day he eats $5$ chocolates, and at the end of the day he returns the $5$ empty packets and receives the free chocolates. How many (uneaten) chocolates does he have at the end of the $100$th day?
