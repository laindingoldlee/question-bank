Write everything as a power of $2$: $\sqrt2=2^{\dfrac12}$ and $\sqrt[3]{2^2}=2^{\dfrac23}$. So the left side is
\[2^{\dfrac12+\dfrac23-2}=2^{-\dfrac56},\]
and $n=\boxed{-\dfrac56}$.
