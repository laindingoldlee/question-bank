A five-letter string uses distinct letters from the 26-letter alphabet. In the guess MATHS, A,T,S are yellow and M,H absent. In BOATS, B in position 1 and A in position 3 are green, T,S are yellow, and O absent. Green means correct position; yellow means present elsewhere. How many answer strings are possible?
