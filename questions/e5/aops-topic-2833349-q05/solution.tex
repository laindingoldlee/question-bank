The answer has B first and A third. T must occupy position 2 or 5, and S position 2 or 4. On the remaining positions 2,4,5, the possible placements of T and S are $(2,4),(5,2),(5,4)$, three choices. The fifth letter can be any letter other than B,A,T,S,M,H,O, giving 19 choices. Thus the count is $ 3\cdot19=\boxed{57}$.
