Let $N=p^4+p^3+p^2+p+1=m^2$. Then $4N=4p^4+4p^3+4p^2+4p+4$, and
\[
(2p^2+p)^2=4p^4+4p^3+p^2<4N<4p^4+4p^3+9p^2+4p+4=(2p^2+p+2)^2 .
\]
Since $4N=(2m)^2$ is a square strictly between two squares two apart, $4N=(2p^2+p+1)^2=4p^4+4p^3+5p^2+2p+1$. Subtracting, $p^2-2p-3=0$, so $(p-3)(p+1)=0$ and $p=3$. Indeed $81+27+9+3+1=121=11^2$. The only prime is $\boxed{p=3}$.
