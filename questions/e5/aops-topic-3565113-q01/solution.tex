Write its vertex vectors as $a,b,c,d$. The successive midpoint vectors are $e=\dfrac{a+b}{2}$, $f=\dfrac{b+c}{2}$, $g=\dfrac{c+d}{2}$, $h=\dfrac{d+a}{2}$. Then $f-e=g-h=\dfrac{c-a}{2}$ and $g-f=h-e=\dfrac{d-b}{2}$. Hence opposite sides are equal parallel vectors, proving the result. Degenerate configurations may give a degenerate parallelogram.
\begin{asy}
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(4,0),C=(3,3),D=(-1,2);pair E=(A+B)/2,F=(B+C)/2,G=(C+D)/2,H=(D+A)/2;draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(E--F--G--H--cycle,dashed);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$E$",E,S);label("$F$",F,E);label("$G$",G,N);label("$H$",H,W);
\end{asy}
