The number of zeroes is the exponent of $5$, because there are more factors of $2$: $z(n)=\sum_{j\ge1}\left\lfloor\dfrac{n}{5^j}\right\rfloor$. We have $z(404)=80+16+3=99$ and $z(405)=81+16+3=100$. Since $z$ is nondecreasing, the least value is $\boxed{405}$.
