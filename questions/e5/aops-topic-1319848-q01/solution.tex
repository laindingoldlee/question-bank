Altogether the school is $8+7=15$ miles west and $8$ miles north of home, so the straight-line distance is $\sqrt{15^2+8^2}=\sqrt{289}=17$ miles.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair H=(0,0),P=(-8,0),Q=(-8,8),Sp=(-15,8);
draw(H--P--Q--Sp,blue,Arrow); draw(H--Sp,red+dashed); draw(Sp--(-15,0)--P,gray+dotted);
label("$8$",(H+P)/2,S); label("$8$",(-8,2),W); label("$7$",(Q+Sp)/2,N); label("$17$",(-3.75,2),N,red);
label("home",H,SE); label("school",Sp,NW); label("$15$",(-11.5,0),S,gray);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The bus route is $8+8+7=23$ miles, so the direct trip would be $23-17=\boxed{6}$ miles shorter.
