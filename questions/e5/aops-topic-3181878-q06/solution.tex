If the radius is $r$ and the point is distance $d$ from the centre, the extremes are $r+d=7$ and $r-d=3$. Adding gives $r=5$, so the area is $25\pi$ and $\boxed{k=25}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair O=(0,0),P=(2,0);draw(circle(O,5));draw((-5,0)--(5,0));dot(O);dot(P);label("$O$",O,N);label("$P$",P,N);label("$7$",(-1.5,0),S);label("$3$",(3.5,0),S);
\end{asy}
