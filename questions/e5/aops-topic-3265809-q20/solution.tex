Each of $|a|,|b|$ is at most $\sqrt{10}$. Therefore $|a^3+b^3|\le|a|^3+|b|^3\le\sqrt{10}(a^2+b^2)=10\sqrt{10}<100$. This contradicts the second condition. Hence $\boxed{\text{no such real pair exists}}$, and no value of the fourth-power sum is realized under those assumptions.
