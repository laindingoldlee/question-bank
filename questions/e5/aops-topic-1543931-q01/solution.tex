For a prime $p$, the exponent of $p$ in $275!$ is $\left\lfloor\dfrac{275}{p}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{275}{p^2}\right\rfloor+\cdots$. For $p>16$ we have $p^2>275$, so the exponent is just $\left\lfloor\dfrac{275}{p}\right\rfloor$, and we need
\[
\left\lfloor\dfrac{275}{p}\right\rfloor\ge3\iff 3p\le275\iff p\le91 .
\]
The largest prime not exceeding $91=7\cdot13$ is $89$. Indeed $89,\ 178,\ 267$ are multiples of $89$ up to $275$, so $89^3\mid275!$, while for the next prime $97$ only $97$ and $194$ appear. Hence $p=\boxed{89}$.
