You will play a simple video game in which you have to cross four identical zones: Zone $1$, Zone $2$, Zone $3$, and Zone $4$. If you manage to cross Zone $1$, you then attempt to cross Zone $2$, and so on.



In each zone, the probability that you encounter an alien is $\dfrac{3}{5}$. If you do encounter an alien, then there's a probability of $\dfrac{2}{3}$ that you escape, in which case you return to the start of the zone and try again; otherwise, the alien unalives you. If you don't encounter an alien, you successfully cross the zone.



All the given probabilities apply independently of other attempts to cross a given zone and attempts to cross other zones. Find the probability that you successfully cross all four zones.
