Let $p$ be the probability of eventually crossing a single zone. On the next attempt, success occurs directly with probability $\dfrac{2}{5}$. An alien appears and is escaped with probability $(\dfrac{3}{5})(\dfrac{2}{3})=\dfrac{2}{5}$, restarting the identical situation. Thus $p=\dfrac{2}{5}+(\dfrac{2}{5})p$, giving $p=\dfrac{2}{3}$. The chance of repeating forever is zero since $(\dfrac{2}{5})^k\to 0$. The four zones are independent, so the probability of clearing all four is $(\dfrac{2}{3})^4=\boxed{\dfrac{16}{81}}$.
