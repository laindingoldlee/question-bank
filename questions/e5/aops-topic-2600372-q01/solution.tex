$(-1+i)^2=1-2i+i^2=-2i$, so
\[(-1+i)^{14}=(-2i)^7=-128\,i^7=-128\cdot(-i)=128i\]
and
\[(-1+i)^{15}=128i(-1+i)=-128-128i.\]
The second number is the complex conjugate, so $(-1-i)^{15}=-128+128i$. The sum is $\boxed{-256}$.
