(a) Let $E_0,E_H,E_{HT}$ be expected additional flips from no matching suffix, suffix H, and suffix HT. First-step equations give $E_0=1+\dfrac{1}{3}E_0+\dfrac{2}{3}E_H$, $E_H=1+\dfrac{2}{3}E_H+\dfrac{1}{3}E_{HT}$, and $E_{HT}=1+\dfrac{2}{3}E_H$. These yield $E_H=12$ and $\boxed{E_0 = \dfrac{27}{2}}$. (b) With analogous states $F_0,F_T,F_{TH}$, the equations are $F_0=1+\dfrac{2}{3}F_0+\dfrac{1}{3}F_T$, $F_T=1+\dfrac{1}{3}F_T+\dfrac{2}{3}F_{TH}$, and $F_{TH}=1+\dfrac{2}{3}F_0$. Hence $F_0=3+F_T$, $F_T=\dfrac{3}{2}+F_{TH}$, so $F_0=\dfrac{11}{2}+\dfrac{2}{3}F_0$ and $\boxed{F_0 = \dfrac{33}{2}}$. The differing answers reflect pattern overlap; dividing by a single-block probability is not a general waiting-time rule.
