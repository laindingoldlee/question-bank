Let \(H\) be the foot of the altitude from \(A\) and let \(\theta=\dfrac{1}{2}\angle BAC\). Write \([XYZ]\) for the area of triangle \(XYZ\).

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(1,3.2), B=(0,0), C=(6,0);
real c=abs(A-B), bb=abs(A-C);
pair D=(bb*B+c*C)/(c+bb);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); draw(A--D,blue);
pair H=(A.x,0); draw(A--H,dashed);
draw(H--(H+(0.15,0))--(H+(0.15,0.15))--(H+(0,0.15)));
dot(A);dot(B);dot(C);dot(D);
label("$A$",A,N); label("$B$",B,SW); label("$C$",C,SE); label("$D$",D,S); label("$H$",H,S);
draw(arc(A,0.7,degrees(B-A),degrees(D-A)),blue); draw(arc(A,0.85,degrees(D-A),degrees(C-A)),blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Triangles \(ABD\) and \(ACD\) have the same height \(AH\) over the bases \(BD\) and \(DC\), so
\[
\dfrac{[ABD]}{[ACD]}=\dfrac{BD}{DC}.
\]
Using the formula \(\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot(\text{side})\cdot(\text{side})\cdot\sin(\text{included angle})\) with the equal angles \(\angle BAD=\angle DAC=\theta\),
\[
\dfrac{[ABD]}{[ACD]}=\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{2}\,AB\cdot AD\sin\theta}{\dfrac{1}{2}\,AC\cdot AD\sin\theta}=\dfrac{AB}{AC}.
\]
Hence \(\dfrac{BD}{DC}=\dfrac{AB}{AC}<1\) (the angle bisector theorem), so \(CD>BD\). \(\blacksquare\)
