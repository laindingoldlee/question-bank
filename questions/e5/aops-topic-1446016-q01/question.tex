Eight students $A,B,C,D,E,F,G,H$ sit around a round table in this order, going clockwise. They stand up and sit down again around the same table in some order; two seatings that differ only by a rotation are considered the same, so there are $7!=5040$ possible seatings (including the original one). A student \emph{keeps both neighbours} if, in the new seating, the student's left neighbour and right neighbour are the same people as in the original seating (with left and right as seen by that student). In how many of the $5040$ seatings does at least one student keep both neighbours?
