The equation is $x^2+y^2=458$. Checking squares below $458$: $458-17^2=169=13^2$, and no other pair works (e.g. $458-21^2=17$, $458-19^2=97$, $458-15^2=233$ are not squares). So $x=17$, $y=13$ and $\binom{17}{13}=\binom{17}4=\boxed{2380}$.
