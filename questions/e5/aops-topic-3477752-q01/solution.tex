The line through the two stationary pins is unchanged. Reflection through a point on this line sends the moving pin to the opposite side at exactly the same perpendicular distance from the line. Therefore the absolute triangle area, one-half base times altitude, is invariant. The initial area is $\dfrac{1}{2}(1)(4)=2$, whereas the target has area $\dfrac{1}{2}(2)(3)=3$. Since these differ, the answer is $\boxed{\text{no}}$.
