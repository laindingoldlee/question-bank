A purchase total is $8a+12b+15c$ with $a,b,c\ge 0$ integers. For a total congruent to $1$ modulo $4$, $3c\equiv 1\pmod 4$, so $c\equiv 3\pmod 4$. For total $49$ this forces $c=3$ and leaves $4$, which is not $8a+12b$; hence $49$ is impossible. On the other hand, totals $50,51,52,53,54,55,56,57$ are respectively $30+20$, $15+36$, $40+12$, $45+8$, $30+24$, $15+40$, $7\cdot 8$, and $45+12$, each realizable. Every larger integer is one of these eight totals plus a nonnegative multiple of $8$. Therefore the largest impossible total is $\boxed{49}$.
