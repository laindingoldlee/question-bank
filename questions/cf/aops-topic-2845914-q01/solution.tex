$\dfrac ab<\dfrac{12}{12}=1$ always. Values arbitrarily close to $1$ occur (take $a$ close to $12$ and $b$ close to $12$), but $1$ is never reached because the inequalities are strict. So there is $\boxed{\text{no maximum}}$, and the least upper bound is $\boxed{1}$.
