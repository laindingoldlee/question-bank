The coefficient of $x^3$ is $\binom{20}{3}=\dfrac{20\cdot19\cdot18}{6}=\boxed{1140}$. If $E,O$ are the sums of the even- and odd-power coefficients, substituting $x=1$ gives $E+O=2^{20}$ and substituting $x=-1$ gives $E-O=0$. Thus $E=\boxed{2^{19}=524288}$. These methods avoid writing every term.
