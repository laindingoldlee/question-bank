Let $A=1$, $B=2$, $\ldots$, $Z=26$. The product of a string of letters is the product of the numbers of its letters; for example, the product of the four-letter string MATH is $13\cdot1\cdot20\cdot8=2080$. How many six-letter strings have a product of $32{,}032$?
