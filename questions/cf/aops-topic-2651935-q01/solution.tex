Let $P$ be the product. Since $2-1=1$, we may multiply $P$ by $2-1$ and use $(x-1)(x+1)=x^2-1$ repeatedly:
\[(2-1)(2+1)=2^2-1,\quad\left(2^2-1\right)\left(2^2+1\right)=2^4-1,\quad\dots,\quad\left(2^{2048}-1\right)\left(2^{2048}+1\right)=2^{4096}-1.\]
So $P=2^{4096}-1$ and $P+1=\boxed{2^{4096}}$.
