Let $ABCD$ be a square with side length $a$ and centre $O$ (the intersection of its diagonals). The diagonal $AC$ cuts it into two right isosceles triangles with hypotenuse $a\sqrt2$. Prove that the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ is the same as the circle traced by the vertices of the square when the square is rotated through $360^\circ$ about $O$, and that this circle is the boundary of the region swept out by the square.
