Let $s_n=x^n+x^{-n}$. Then $s_0=2$, $s_1=4$, $s_{-n}=s_n$, and
\[\left(x+\dfrac1x\right)\left(x^n+\dfrac1{x^n}\right)=s_{n+1}+s_{n-1},\qquad\text{so}\qquad s_{n+1}=4s_n-s_{n-1}.\]
This gives
\[s_0,s_1,\dots,s_7=2,\ 4,\ 14,\ 52,\ 194,\ 724,\ 2702,\ 10084.\]
The sequence is strictly increasing for $n\ge0$: if $s_n>s_{n-1}>0$ then $s_{n+1}-s_n=3s_n-s_{n-1}>0$. Since $s_6=2702<9980<10084=s_7$, no $n\ge0$ works, and negative $n$ give the same values. Hence $\boxed{\text{no such integer }n}$ exists. $\blacksquare$
