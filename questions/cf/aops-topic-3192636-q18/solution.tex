The square contributes 100. Each semicircle has radius 5 and area $25\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, so total heart area is $\boxed{100+25\pi}$. The unused area is $\boxed{125-25\pi}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(10,0),C=(10,10),D=(0,10);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(arc((5,10),5,0,180),blue);draw(arc((0,5),5,90,270),blue);label("$10$",(A+B)/2,S);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$C$",C,NE);
\end{asy}
