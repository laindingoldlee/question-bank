\textbf{Independent events.} The penny and the nickel are independent, each heads with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, so the probability is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{4}}$.

\textbf{Counting outcomes.} An outcome records all four coins, so there are $2^4=16$ equally likely outcomes. A successful outcome has the penny and the nickel showing heads, while the dime and the quarter may show anything. The dime has $2$ possibilities and the quarter has $2$, so there are $2\cdot2=4$ successful outcomes:
\[
HHHH,\quad HHHT,\quad HHTH,\quad HHTT
\]
(in the order penny, nickel, dime, quarter). The probability is $\dfrac{4}{16}=\dfrac{1}{4}$.

The "$2$ possibilities for each" refers to the dime and the quarter. The question says nothing about them, and each successful outcome still has to say what they show.
