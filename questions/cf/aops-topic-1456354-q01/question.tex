Four coins are flipped at the same time: a penny, a nickel, a dime and a quarter. What is the probability that the penny and the nickel both come up heads? Solve it in two ways: with independent events, and by counting equally likely outcomes. Explain why the counting method finds $4$ successful outcomes.
