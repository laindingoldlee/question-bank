Without the restriction, the number of ways is the number of nonnegative solutions of $x_1+\cdots+x_8=50$, which by stars and bars is $\dbinom{57}{7}=264{,}385{,}836$. The forbidden ways put all $50$ scientists in one cave: $8$ ways. The answer is
\[
\dbinom{57}{7}-8=\boxed{264{,}385{,}828} .
\]
