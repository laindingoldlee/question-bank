IQ $=kf^2$ with $100=25k$, so IQ $=4f^2$. With fingers $a,b,c\ge1$ and $a+b+c=20$, the total IQ is $4\left(a^2+b^2+c^2\right)$.

\textbf{Largest.} Since $a^2+b^2+c^2=(a+b+c)^2-2(ab+bc+ca)=400-2(ab+bc+ca)$, we want $ab+bc+ca$ small. Moving a finger from a smaller count to a larger (or equal) one increases the sum of squares: $(x+1)^2+(y-1)^2-x^2-y^2=2(x-y)+2>0$ for $x\ge y$. So the maximum is at $(18,1,1)$: $324+1+1=326$, total IQ $4\cdot326=1304$.

\textbf{Smallest.} Moving a finger from a larger count to a smaller one, when the counts differ by at least $2$, decreases the sum of squares, so at the minimum the counts differ by at most $1$: $(7,7,6)$, with $49+49+36=134$ and total IQ $4\cdot134=536$.

The largest possible sum is $\boxed{1304}$ and the smallest is $\boxed{536}$.
