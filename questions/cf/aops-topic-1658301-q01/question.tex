On a distant planet, the IQ of a creature is directly proportional to the square of the number of fingers on its (single) hand, and a creature with $5$ fingers has an IQ of $100$. Three such creatures in a room have $20$ fingers among them, and each has at least one finger. What are the largest and the smallest possible sums of their IQ scores?
