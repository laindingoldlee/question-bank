Give a combinatorial proof that $(2^m)^n=(2^n)^m$ for positive integers $m,n$, by counting one set in two ways. Then generalise: for positive integers $a_1,\dots,a_k$ and any permutation $\sigma$ of $\{1,\dots,k\}$, show that the power tower $\left(\cdots\left(\left(2^{a_{\sigma(1)}}\right)^{a_{\sigma(2)}}\right)\cdots\right)^{a_{\sigma(k)}}$ does not depend on $\sigma$.
