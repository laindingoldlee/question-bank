A kite has two pairs of equal adjacent sides. The two shorter sides total $10$, so the two longer sides total $38-10=28$, and each is $\boxed{14}$ cm (the shorter ones being $5$ cm each). (Sketch not to scale.)

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),C=(17,0); pair B=(4,3),D=(4,-3); draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); draw(B--D,gray); draw(A--C,gray);
label("$5$",(A+B)/2,NW); label("$5$",(A+D)/2,SW); label("$14$",(B+C)/2,NE); label("$14$",(C+D)/2,SE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
