Note that $2018=2\cdot1009$. Then
\[
1009^2-1009=1009\cdot1008=1009\cdot2\cdot504=2018\cdot504,
\]
so $1009^2\equiv1009\pmod{2018}$. Since $0\le1009<2018$, the remainder is $\boxed{1009}$.

(Another view: $1009$ is odd, so $1009^2=1009\cdot(\text{odd number})$ is an odd multiple of $1009$, and every odd multiple of $1009$ leaves remainder $1009$ on division by $2\cdot1009$.)
