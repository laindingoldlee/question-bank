Encode knights by one and knaves by zero. Each statement is true exactly when its two neighbours differ, yielding $x_i=x_{i-1}+x_{i+1}\pmod2$ and hence period three. Any nonzero three-term block has two ones and one zero; the all-zero pattern is excluded by the 48 knights. Thus two-thirds of the people are knights, and the total is $48\cdot\dfrac{3}{2}=\boxed{72}$.
