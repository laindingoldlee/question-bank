In an isosceles triangle this bisector is also an altitude and median. Thus $BD=\sqrt{4^2-(2\sqrt2)^2}=2\sqrt2$, and $BC=4\sqrt2$. Area is $\dfrac{1}{2}(4\sqrt2)(2\sqrt2)=\boxed8$.

usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,2*sqrt(2)),B=(-2*sqrt(2),0),C=(2*sqrt(2),0),D=(0,0);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--D,dashed);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,S);
\end{asy}
