For each of the $7$ deletions, the swaps of two unequal letters give $\dbinom62$ minus the pairs of equal letters, and different operations can give the same string. A careful count of the distinct strings gives $\boxed{100}$ (checked by enumerating all $7\cdot15$ operations).
