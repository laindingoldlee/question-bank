For $k=0$ and $k=19$ the value is $1$, not divisible by $19$.

For $1\le k\le18$, write $\dbinom{19}k\cdot k!\,(19-k)!=19!$. The prime $19$ divides $19!$. But $k!$ and $(19-k)!$ are products of numbers from $1$ to $18$, none divisible by the prime $19$, so $19\nmid k!(19-k)!$. Since $19$ is prime and divides the product $\dbinom{19}k\cdot k!(19-k)!$, it divides $\dbinom{19}{k}$.

So the answer is $\boxed{18}$ (all $k$ from $1$ to $18$), not $4$ as in the post.
