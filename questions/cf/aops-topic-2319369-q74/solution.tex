The scores $90$ and $95$ are $1$ and $2$ standard deviations above the mean. About $95\%$ of scores lie within $2$ standard deviations and $68\%$ within $1$, so by symmetry about $\dfrac{95\%-68\%}{2}=13.5\%$ lie between $1$ and $2$ standard deviations above the mean. That is about $0.135\cdot20000=\boxed{2700}$ students. (With the exact normal distribution, the fraction is about $13.59\%$, about $2718$ students.)
