Factor $2025=3^4\cdot5^2$. Every die must show 1,3,or 5; exactly four show 3, exactly two show 5, and the rest show 1. For $n\ge6$ the probability is $P_n=\dfrac{n!}{4!2!(n-6)!}\cdot6^{-n}$. The ratio is $\dfrac{P_{n+1}}{P_n}=\dfrac{n+1}{6(n-5)}$. At $n=6$ this is $\dfrac{7}{6}>1$, while for every $n\ge7$ it is less than 1 because $n+1<6n-30$. For $n<6$, the probability is zero. Thus the unique maximum occurs at $\boxed{n=7}$.
