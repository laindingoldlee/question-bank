Such an $n$ is not divisible by 59, so Fermat gives $n^{58}\equiv1$. Also $n^{60}=(n^4)^{15}\equiv1$, hence $n^2\equiv1$. Since 59 is prime, $n\equiv\pm1\pmod{59}$. The largest representative below 2023 is $34\cdot59+1=\boxed{2007}$; the next residue of either sign is at least 2064.
