First cancel the common factor $5$, then multiply by the conjugate of the denominator:
\[\dfrac{5+20i}{5-20i}=\dfrac{1+4i}{1-4i}=\dfrac{(1+4i)^2}{(1-4i)(1+4i)}=\dfrac{1+8i-16}{17}=\boxed{-\dfrac{15}{17}+\dfrac{8}{17}i}.\]
