Three real numbers $x$, $y$, $z$ are chosen independently and uniformly at random from the interval $[0,1]$, so that the point $(x,y,z)$ is uniformly distributed in the unit cube. Let $T$ be the solid tetrahedron with vertices $O=(0,0,0)$, $A=(1,0,0)$, $B=(0,1,0)$ and $C=(0,0,1)$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that a point $(x,y,z)$ lies in $T$ if and only if $x\ge0$, $y\ge0$, $z\ge0$ and $x+y+z\le1$. In particular, every point inside $T$ satisfies $x+y+z<1$.
\item[(b)] Hence find the probability that $x+y+z<1$.
\end{enumerate}
