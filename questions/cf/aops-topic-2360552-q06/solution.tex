The even numbers up to $10000$ multiply to $2\cdot4\cdots10000=2^{5000}\cdot5000!$. Dividing $10000!$ by this leaves the odd numbers: $\boxed{\text{(D)}\ \dfrac{10000!}{2^{5000}\cdot5000!}}$.
