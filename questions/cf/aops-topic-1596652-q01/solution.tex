Measure positions from $H$'s starting point.

\textbf{First stage.} $H$ covers $49$ m at $7$ m/s in $7$ s. At $t=7$, $H$ is at $49$ m and $B$ is at $100+5\cdot7=135$ m, so $H$ has not yet caught up and the gap is $86$ m.

\textbf{Second stage.} Now $H$ gains $10-5=5$ metres every second, so he closes the gap of $86$ m in $\dfrac{86}{5}=17.2$ s.

Hence $H$ passes $B$ at
\[
t=7+17.2=\boxed{24.2\text{ s}}
\]
after the start, at $49+10\cdot17.2=221$ m from $H$'s start (check: $B$ is at $100+5\cdot24.2=221$ m too).
