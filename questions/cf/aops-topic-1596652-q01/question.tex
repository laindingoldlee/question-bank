Two runners start at the same moment and run in the same direction along a straight path. Runner $B$ starts $100$ m ahead of runner $H$ and runs at a constant $5$ m/s. Runner $H$ runs at $7$ m/s until he has covered $49$ m, and from then on at $10$ m/s. How many seconds after the start does $H$ pass $B$, and how far from $H$'s starting point?
