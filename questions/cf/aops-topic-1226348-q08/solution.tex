$f$ doubles $x$ modulo $1$, so $f^{17}(x)=2^{17}x-\lfloor2^{17}x\rfloor$. The condition $f^{17}(x)=x$ means $(2^{17}-1)x$ is an integer, i.e. $x=\dfrac {k}{2^{17}-1}$ with $k=0,1,\dots,2^{17}-2$. The sum is
\[\dfrac{1}{2^{17}-1}\cdot\dfrac{(2^{17}-2)(2^{17}-1)}{2}=2^{16}-1=\boxed{65535}.\]
