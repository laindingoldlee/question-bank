Put $BC=L$. The similarity scales are $\sqrt2$ and 2, giving $A C = \dfrac{L}{\sqrt{2}} , A B = \dfrac{L}{2}$. Choose $B=(0,0),C=(L,0)$; the distance equations yield $A = (\dfrac{3 L}{8} , \dfrac{L \sqrt{7}}{8})$. Its area gives $L = \dfrac{4}{7^{\dfrac{1}{4}}}$. The indicated similarities give $D = (- \dfrac{L}{4} , - \dfrac{L \sqrt{7}}{4})$ and $E = (\dfrac{3 L}{2} , - \dfrac{L \sqrt{7}}{2})$. The two perpendicular lines are $y = - \dfrac{5 x}{\sqrt{7}}$ and $y = \dfrac{3 (x-L)}{\sqrt{7}}$, meeting at $F = (\dfrac{3 L}{8} , - \dfrac{15 L}{8\sqrt7})$. Thus $A F = \dfrac{11 L}{4\sqrt7} = \dfrac{11}{7^{\dfrac{3}{4}}}$, so $\boxed{a+b=18}$.
