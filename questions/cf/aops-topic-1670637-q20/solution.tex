The sum of two dice is one of the $11$ numbers $2,3,\dots,12$. If Scott had rolled $12$ or more times, two of the sums would be equal by the pigeonhole principle. Rolling each of the $11$ sums once is possible, so the maximum is $\boxed{11}$.
