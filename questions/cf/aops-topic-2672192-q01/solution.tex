Each pipe fills $\dfrac14$ of the tank. Let the filling take $T$ minutes; $C$ is open for $T-10$ minutes and $D$ for $15$. So $D$ fills the tank alone in $60$ minutes and $C$ in $4(T-10)$ minutes. Then
\[\dfrac{1}{4(T-10)}+\dfrac1{60}=\dfrac1{24}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\dfrac{1}{4(T-10)}=\dfrac1{40},\]
so $T=20$. Pipe $A$ fills $\dfrac14$ of the tank in $20$ minutes, so alone it needs $\boxed{80\text{ minutes}}$.
