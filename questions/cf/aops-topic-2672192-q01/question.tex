Four pipes $A,B,C,D$ fill a tank. Pipe $A$ is open the whole time, and pipe $B$ is open for the first $8$ minutes. Two minutes after $B$ is closed, pipe $C$ is opened and kept open until the tank is full. Pipe $D$ is open for the last $15$ minutes. Each pipe fills an equal share of the tank, and pipes $C$ and $D$ together can fill the tank in $24$ minutes. How long would pipe $A$ alone take to fill the tank?
