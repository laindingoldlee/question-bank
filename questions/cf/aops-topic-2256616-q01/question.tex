Two points are drawn on a circle so that the measure of the major arc they determine exceeds the measure of the minor arc by $100^\circ$. What is the measure of the minor arc, in degrees?
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(A)] $110$
\item[(B)] $120$
\item[(C)] $130$
\item[(D)] $140$
\item[(E)] $150$
\end{enumerate}
