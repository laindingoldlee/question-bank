Let $ABC$ be right at $B$, and let $D$ be the foot on hypotenuse $AC$. Similarity of triangles $ABD$ and $ACB$ gives $AB^2=AC\cdot AD$. Similarly, triangles $CBD$ and $CAB$ give $BC^2=AC\cdot DC$. Adding gives $AB^2+BC^2=AC(AD+DC)=AC^2$, as required.
