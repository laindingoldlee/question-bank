Multiply by $5mn$: $5n+5m+5=mn$, so $mn-5m-5n=5$ and
\[
(m-5)(n-5)=30 .
\]
Since $m,n\ge1$, both factors are at least $-4$. If both were negative, their product would be at most $16<30$; so both are positive divisors of $30$. Each of the $8$ divisors $d$ of $30$ gives $(m,n)=(d+5,\dfrac{30}{d}+5)$:
\[
(6,35),(7,20),(8,15),(10,11),(11,10),(15,8),(20,7),(35,6).
\]
So there are $\boxed{8}$ ordered pairs.
