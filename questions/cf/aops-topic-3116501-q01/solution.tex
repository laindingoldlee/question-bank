Writing $P=(x,y)$ and squaring nonnegative distances gives $x^2+y^2=4((x-9)^2+(y-15)^2)$. Simplification and completing squares yield $\boxed{(x-12)^2+(y-20)^2=136}$. Every point on this circle satisfies the squared equation and thus the original distance relation.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(9,15),C=(12,20),P=C+sqrt(136)*dir(20);draw(circle(C,sqrt(136)));draw(A--P--B,blue);dot(A);dot(B);dot(C);dot(P);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$(12,20)$",C,N);label("$P$",P,E);
\end{asy}
