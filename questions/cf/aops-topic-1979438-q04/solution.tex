These integers are $100+k\cdot10^{15}$ for $k=0,1,\dots,499$ (the smallest is $100$ itself). Their sum is
\[
500\cdot100+10^{15}(0+1+\cdots+499)=50000+10^{15}\cdot\dfrac{499\cdot500}{2}=50000+124750\cdot10^{15}.
\]
So the sum is $\boxed{124750000000000050000}$, i.e.\ $1.2475\cdot10^{20}+50000$.
