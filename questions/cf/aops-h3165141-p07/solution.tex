The linear map $(x,y)\mapsto(u,v)=(3x+5y,5x+2y)$ sends the open unit square bijectively to the interior of the lattice parallelogram with vertices $(0,0),(3,5),(8,7),(5,2)$. Its area is the absolute determinant $|6-25|=19$. Each edge has no lattice points between its endpoints because the coordinate steps $(3,5)$ and $(5,2)$ are coprime; hence there are four boundary lattice points. Pick\textquotesingle s theorem gives $I=19-\dfrac{4}{2}+1=\boxed{18}$ interior lattice points, one for each allowed pair.
