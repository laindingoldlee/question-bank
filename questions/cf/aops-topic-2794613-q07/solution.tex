Opposite semicircles touch only at the square's center, so every positive-area overlap is between adjacent semicircles. The four such lenses have disjoint interiors. Each lens consists of two 90-degree sectors of radius 6 minus two right triangles of legs 6, and hence has area $ 2\cdot\dfrac{\pi6^2}{4}-2\cdot\dfrac{6^2}{2}=18\pi-36 $. The total is $\boxed{72\pi-144}$.
