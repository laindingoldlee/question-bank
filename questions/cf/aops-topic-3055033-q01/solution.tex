Factor the equations as $ x(x+2y)=16 $ and $ 2y(x+2y)=9 $. Adding gives $(x+2y)^2=25 $. Positivity gives $ x+2y=5 $, so $ x=\dfrac{16}{5}$ and $ y=\dfrac{9}{10}$. Hence $\boxed{x+y=\dfrac{41}{10}}$. Factoring their difference as 7 does not force integer factor values, since the variables are real.
