Since $k+2k=3k$, the terms are $\dfrac{2^k}{3k}$, and
\[
f(3)=\dfrac{2^2}{3\cdot2}+\dfrac{2^3}{3\cdot3}=\dfrac{4}{6}+\dfrac{8}{9}=\dfrac{6}{9}+\dfrac{8}{9}=\boxed{\dfrac{14}{9}}.
\]
