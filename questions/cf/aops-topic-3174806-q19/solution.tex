Start with the first-quadrant quarter-disk, area $\dfrac{25\pi}{4}$. Remove its horizontal strip with $0\le y<1$ and add the disk portion $-1\le x<0,y\ge1$. Rotating the added portion 90 degrees clockwise gives exactly the removed strip except for the unit square $[0,1]^2$. Thus the net area change is $-1$, and the desired area is $\dfrac{25\pi}{4}-1$. Hence $\boxed{a+b+c=30}$.
