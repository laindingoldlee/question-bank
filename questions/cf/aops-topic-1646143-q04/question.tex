Find the sum of all whole numbers $N$ for which exactly three of the following four statements are true.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(1)] $N$ is prime.
\item[(2)] $N-1$ is prime.
\item[(3)] $2<\sqrt N<4$.
\item[(4)] $N$ is $2$ less than a perfect square.
\end{enumerate}
