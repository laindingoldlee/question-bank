Alex, Bob, and Carl have nonnegative integer loss counts $A,B,C$, and $B=C-8$. Alex claims $A=2C-2$; Bob claims $B=\dfrac{C+A}{2}+1$; Carl claims $C=20$. Exactly one claim is false. Find every possible triple and identify the liar.
