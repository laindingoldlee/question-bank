If Carl lies, the other two equations give $B=\dfrac{3C}{2}$; combining with $B=C-8$ forces $C=-16$, impossible. Thus $C=20$ and $B=12$. If Alex tells the truth, $A=38$ and Bob's claim is false. If Bob tells the truth, $12=\dfrac{20+A}{2}+1$ gives $A=2$, and Alex lies. Both satisfy exactly one false claim. Thus $\boxed{(A,B,C)=(38,12,20)\text{ with Bob lying},\ (2,12,20)\text{ with Alex lying}}$.
