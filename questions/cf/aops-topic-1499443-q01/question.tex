Four points are chosen at random from a $5\times5$ square grid of $25$ points (all sets of four distinct points equally likely). What is the probability that the four points are the vertices of a rectangle whose sides are parallel to the sides of the grid? Solve it in two ways: by counting unordered sets, and by choosing the points one at a time, and explain why the two methods agree.
