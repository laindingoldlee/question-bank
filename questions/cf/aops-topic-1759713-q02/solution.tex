If there are $a$ touchdowns and $b$ field goals, $5a+3b=25$ with $a,b\ge0$. Then $5\mid3b$, so $5\mid b$: $b=0$, $a=5$ or $b=5$, $a=2$ ($b=10$ is too many). Five touchdowns give $1$ sequence; two touchdowns and five field goals can be ordered in $\dbinom72=21$ ways. The answer is $1+21=\boxed{22}$.
