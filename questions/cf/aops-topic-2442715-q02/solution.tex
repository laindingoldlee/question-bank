The two coins show one head and one tail in $2$ of the $4$ equally likely outcomes (HT and TH), with probability $\dfrac12$. The die shows an even number with probability $\dfrac36=\dfrac12$. The die and the coins are independent, so the probability is
\[\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac12=\boxed{\dfrac14}.\]
