If 3 and 4 are legs, the third side is 5. If 4 is the hypotenuse, the third side is $\sqrt{4^2-3^2}=\sqrt7$. The side 3 cannot be the hypotenuse because 4 is longer. Thus the minimum is $\boxed{\sqrt7}$.
