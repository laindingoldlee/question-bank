Find all positive integers $x$ such that the digits of $x^2$ and $x^3$ together use each of the digits $0,1,\dots,9$ exactly once. (For example, $x=19$ fails: $19^2=361$ and $19^3=6859$ repeat $6$ and miss $0,2,4,7$.)
