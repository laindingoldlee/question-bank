Choose the five union elements in $\binom85$ ways, then the two intersection elements in $\binom52$ ways. Each of the other three belongs to exactly one of $A,B$, giving $2^3$ assignments. Thus $\binom85\binom52\cdot8=\boxed{4480}$.
