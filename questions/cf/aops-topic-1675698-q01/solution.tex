The $3$ puppies eat $4$ bags in $2$ hours, that is $2$ bags per hour, so one puppy eats $\dfrac{2}{3}$ bag per hour, and $6$ puppies eat $6\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}=4$ bags per hour. At that rate $100$ bags take $\dfrac{100}{4}=\boxed{25}$ hours.
