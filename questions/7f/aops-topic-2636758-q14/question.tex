An eccentric $12$-hour clock rings on every hour, $24$ times a day. The days of the week are numbered Monday $=1$, Tuesday $=2$, $\dots$, Sunday $=7$, and at 12 o'clock the hour is taken to be $12$ (midnight, 12:00 a.m., is an a.m.\ hour and noon, 12:00 p.m., is a p.m.\ hour). At an a.m.\ hour the clock rings (day number)$\times$(hour) times, and at a p.m.\ hour it rings (day number)$\times$(hour)$^2$ times. For example, at 3:00 p.m.\ on a Tuesday it rings $2\times3^2=18$ times. How many times does the clock ring in the year $2024$? (1 January 2024 was a Monday.)
