Let $x$ and $y$ be positive integers, and let $\dfrac {a}{b}$ be the fraction $\dfrac {x}{y}$ reduced to lowest terms (so $a,b$ are positive integers with $\gcd(a,b)=1$ and $\dfrac {a}{b}=\dfrac {x}{y}$). Prove that
\[\operatorname{lcm}(x,y)=xb=ay .\]
