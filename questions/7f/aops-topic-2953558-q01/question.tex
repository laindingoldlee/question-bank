Ten players numbered $1$ to $10$ play a team game. Two of them are chosen uniformly at random as captains. Starting with the higher-numbered captain, the captains alternately draft the remaining players, and each always picks the lowest-numbered player still available (lower numbers are better players). Players $1$, $2$ and $3$ are so strong that the team containing two of them always wins. Which player has the highest probability of being on the winning team, which has the lowest, and what are these probabilities?
