Let $N$ be the total and $B$ the number of blue marbles. Removing a red changes blue probability by $\dfrac{B}{N-1}-\dfrac{B}{N}=\dfrac{B}{N(N-1)}=\dfrac{1}{2025}$. Thus $2025=81\cdot25$ divides $N(N-1)$. Since consecutive integers are coprime, $N$ is $0$ or $1$ modulo each of $81,25$. The four residues modulo $2025$ are $0,1,325,1701$. The smallest feasible $N>1$ is therefore $325$, giving $B=\dfrac{325\cdot324}{2025}=52$. This has positive red count and is valid. Since $N(N-1)$ increases with $N$, the minimum is $\boxed{52}$.
