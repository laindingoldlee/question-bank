The two equilateral triangles share side $EF$ and have opposite vertices $A,C$. Their common altitude is half $AC$. If $EF=t$, then $AC=\sqrt3t$. For square side $s$, $AC=\sqrt2s$, giving $t^2=\dfrac{2s^2}{3}$. The triangle area is $\dfrac{\sqrt3t^2}{4}=\dfrac{\sqrt3s^2}{6}$. Thus the required ratio is $\boxed{2\sqrt3}$.
