Five painters can each paint a certain wall alone. The first painter takes $10$ hours, and each painter takes one hour less than the previous one, so the painters take $10$, $9$, $8$, $7$ and $6$ hours. Working together at these constant rates, how long do the five painters take to paint the wall?
