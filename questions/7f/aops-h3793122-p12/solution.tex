Let the square be $A=(0,0),B=(s,0),C=(s,s),D=(0,s)$. Since $OA=OB$, write $O=(\dfrac{s}{2},y)$. Subtracting $OD^2=13$ from $OA^2=25$ gives $2sy-s^2=12$, so $y=\dfrac{s^2+12}{2s}$. Substitution in $OA^2=25$ gives $(s^2)^2-38s^2+72=0$, hence $s^2=2$ or 36. For 36, $y=4$ and $O=(3,4)$ lies inside the square, contradicting the hypothesis. For 2, $y=\dfrac{7}{\sqrt2}>s$, so $O$ is outside. Thus the area is $\boxed2$. 
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(220);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
real s=sqrt(2);pair A=(0,0),B=(s,0),C=(s,s),D=(0,s),O=(s/2,7/sqrt(2));draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--O--B);draw(O--D,dashed);label("$A$",A,(-1,-1));label("$B$",B,(1,-1));label("$C$",C,(1,0));label("$D$",D,(-1,0));label("$O$",O,(0,1));
\end{asy}
