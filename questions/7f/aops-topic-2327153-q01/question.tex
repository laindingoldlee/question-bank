Find a formula, in terms of $n$, for
\[S_n=\dfrac{1}{1\cdot3}+\dfrac{1}{2\cdot4}+\dfrac{1}{3\cdot5}+\cdots+\dfrac{1}{n(n+2)}.\]
A student computed $S_1=\dfrac13$, $S_2=\dfrac{11}{24}$, $S_3=\dfrac{21}{40}$ and $S_4=\dfrac{31}{56}$; use your formula to check these values.
