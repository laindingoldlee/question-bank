Suppose $A$, $R$, $S$ and $T$ are distinct digits from $1$ to $9$, and $\sqrt{\overline{STARS}}=\overline{SAT}$, where $\overline{STARS}$ and $\overline{SAT}$ are the numbers written with these digits. Find $A$, $R$, $S$ and $T$. Is the answer unique?
