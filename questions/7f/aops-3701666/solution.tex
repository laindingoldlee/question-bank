Use $B=(-3,0),C=(3,0),A=(0,4)$, since the altitude is $\sqrt{5^2-3^2}=4$. The area is 12 and semiperimeter 8, so the inradius is $r = \dfrac{12}{8} = \dfrac{3}{2}$. By symmetry $I = (0 , \dfrac{3}{2})$. Hence $I B = \sqrt{9 + \dfrac{9}{4}} = \boxed{\dfrac{3 \sqrt{5}}{2}}$.

\end{asy}
