$x^4-1=-1$ gives $x^4=0$, so $x=0$. $x^4-1=1$ gives $x^4=2$, whose solutions are $x=\sqrt[4]2\,i^k$ for $k=0,1,2,3$. So the solutions are $\boxed{x=0,\ \pm\sqrt[4]2,\ \pm i\sqrt[4]2}$.
