Their centers are the leg midpoints, separated by the midsegment length $\dfrac{AB+CD}{2}$. Their radii are 5 and 9. Internal tangency would give center distance 4, hence $AB+CD=8$, contradicting the strict quadrilateral inequality $AD<AB+BC+CD$ because $18<18$ would be required. Thus tangency is external, the center distance is 14, and $AB+CD=28$. Perimeter is $28+10+18=\boxed{56}$.
