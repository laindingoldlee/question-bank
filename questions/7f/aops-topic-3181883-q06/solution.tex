The two straight edges total $A$, and the arc is $\dfrac{A\pi}{B}$. Thus $(A-30)+\left(\dfrac{A}{B}-3\right)\pi=0$. Irrationality of $\pi$ forces $A=30$ and $\dfrac{A}{B}=3$, hence $B=10$ and $A+B=\boxed{40}$.
