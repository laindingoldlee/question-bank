Let $a,b,c$ be real numbers with $ac\ne0$. The quartic
\[acx^4+b(a+c)x^3+\left(a^2+b^2+c^2\right)x^2+b(a+c)x+ac\]
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(A)] has four complex (non-real) roots
\item[(B)] has either four real roots or four non-real roots
\item[(C)] has two real roots and two non-real roots
\item[(D)] has four real roots
\end{enumerate}
Which statement is always true?
