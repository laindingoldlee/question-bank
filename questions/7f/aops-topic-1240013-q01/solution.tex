The quartic factors as
\[\left(ax^2+bx+c\right)\left(cx^2+bx+a\right)\]
(expand: $x^4$: $ac$; $x^3$: $ab+bc$; $x^2$: $a^2+b^2+c^2$; $x$: $bc+ab$; constant: $ca$). Both quadratic factors have discriminant $b^2-4ac$. So either both have real roots (four real roots) or both have non-real roots (four non-real roots). The answer is $\boxed{\text{(B)}}$.
