Since $\dfrac{2}{n^2-1}=\dfrac{1}{n-1}-\dfrac{1}{n+1}$, the sum through $n=N$ is $1+\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{N}-\dfrac{1}{N+1}$. The last two terms approach zero, so the sum is $\boxed{\dfrac{3}{2}}$.
