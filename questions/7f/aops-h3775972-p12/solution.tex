If all three variables are at most $6$, every pair sum is at most $12$, giving $7^3=343$ triples. Otherwise exactly one variable exceeds $6$. If that variable is $a=7,\ldots,12$, each of $b,c$ may range independently from $0$ to $12-a$; their pair sum is then automatically at most $10$. This contributes $\sum_{j=0}^5(j+1)^2=91$ for each choice of the large coordinate. Total $343+3\cdot91=\boxed{616}$.
