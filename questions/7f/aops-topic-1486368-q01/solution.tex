Put the base on the $x$-axis: $B=(-2,0)$, $C=(2,0)$ and apex $A=(0,h)$ with $h>0$. The midpoints of the legs are $M=\left(1,\dfrac{h}{2}\right)$ on $AC$ and $N=\left(-1,\dfrac{h}{2}\right)$ on $AB$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s=0.2){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
pair B=(-2,0),C=(2,0),A=(0,6);
pair Mb=(A+C)/2, Mc=(A+B)/2, G=(A+B+C)/3;
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(B--Mb,blue); draw(C--Mc,blue);
draw(ra(G,Mb-G,Mc-G,0.3));
dot(A);dot(B);dot(C);dot(Mb);dot(Mc);dot(G);
label("$A$",A,N); label("$B$",B,SW); label("$C$",C,SE);
label("$M$",Mb,E); label("$N$",Mc,W); label("$G$",G+(0.5,0));
label("$4$",(0,0),S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The medians $BM$ and $CN$ have direction vectors
\[
M-B=\left(3,\dfrac{h}{2}\right),\qquad N-C=\left(-3,\dfrac{h}{2}\right),
\]
and they are perpendicular when the dot product vanishes: $-9+\dfrac{h^2}{4}=0$, so $h=6$. The area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot4\cdot6=\boxed{12}$.
