Draw from the box labelled ``one white and one black''. Since that label is wrong, the box holds two balls of the same colour, and the drawn ball shows which.

Say it is white, so this box is ``two whites''. The box labelled ``two blacks'' is not two blacks and not two whites, so it is ``one of each''. The box labelled ``two whites'' is therefore ``two blacks''. The case of a black ball is symmetric.
