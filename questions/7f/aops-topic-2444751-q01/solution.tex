Let $S=3^0+3^1+\cdots+3^{100}$. Multiplying by $3$ shifts every term one place:
\[3S=3^1+3^2+\cdots+3^{101}.\]
Subtracting, all terms except $3^{101}$ and $3^0=1$ cancel:
\[3S-S=3^{101}-1,\qquad\text{so}\qquad S=\boxed{\dfrac{3^{101}-1}{2}}.\]
(Check on a small case: $3^0+3^1+3^2=13=\dfrac{3^3-1}{2}$.)
