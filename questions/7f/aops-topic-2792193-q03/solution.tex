Consider a girl with left neighbour $L$ and right neighbour $R$. If $L$ and $R$ match, she tells the truth about $R$, so she says yes iff $R$ is blue iff $L$ is blue. If they differ, she lies about $R$, so she says yes iff $R$ is red iff $L$ is blue. Either way she says "Yes" exactly when the girl on her left wears blue. Each blue girl is the left neighbour of exactly one girl, so exactly $\boxed{17}$ girls say "Yes", for every arrangement.
