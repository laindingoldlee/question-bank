\begin{enumerate}\item Choose the boys and girls separately: $\binom63\binom52=\boxed{200}$.\item For $k$ of each sex there are $\binom6k\binom5k$ committees. Vandermonde's identity gives $\sum_{k=0}^{5}\binom6k\binom5k=\sum_{k=0}^{5}\binom6k\binom5{5-k}=\binom{11}{5}=462$. Removing the empty committee gives $\boxed{461}$.\item Subtract committees with zero or one girl: $\binom{11}{5}-\binom65-\binom51\binom64=462-6-75=\boxed{381}$.\item Ignoring the forbidden pair, the count is $\binom52\binom62+\binom53\binom61+\binom54=215$. A committee containing both specified people must choose two more from the remaining nine, including at least one of the four remaining girls. There are $\binom92-\binom52=26$ such committees. The required count is $215-26=\boxed{189}$.\end{enumerate}
