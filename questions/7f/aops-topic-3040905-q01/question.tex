A group contains six boys $M_1,\ldots,M_6$ and five girls $G_1,\ldots,G_5$. Count the committees in each independent case.\begin{enumerate}\item Five members, exactly three boys and two girls.\item At least two members, with equal numbers of boys and girls.\item Five members, with at least two girls.\item Four members, with at least two girls, and not containing both $M_1$ and $G_1$.\end{enumerate}
