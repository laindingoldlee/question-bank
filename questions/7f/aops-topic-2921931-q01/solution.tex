It is divisible by $\operatorname{lcm}(1,\dots,17)=2^4\cdot3^2\cdot5\cdot7\cdot11\cdot13\cdot17=12252240$. Divisibility by $10$ gives $B=0$. The number is then $\overline{ABC}\cdot1001000+0=\overline{A0C}\cdot1001000$, and $1001000=2^3\cdot5^3\cdot7\cdot11\cdot13$. So $\overline{A0C}$ must be divisible by $2\cdot9\cdot17=306$. The three-digit multiples of $306$ are $306,612,918$, and only $306$ has middle digit $0$. So $\overline{ABC}=\boxed{306}$.
