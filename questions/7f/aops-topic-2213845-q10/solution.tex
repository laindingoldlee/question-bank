Factor: $m^3-3m^2+2m=(m-2)(m-1)m$, a product of three consecutive positive integers. Since $79$ and $83$ are primes, each divides one of them: some $83y$ and $79x$ (positive integers $x,y$) lie in $\{m-2,m-1,m\}$, so $|83y-79x|\le2$.

Since $83\cdot20-79\cdot21=1660-1659=1$, the solutions of $83y-79x=e$ satisfy $y\equiv20e\pmod{79}$. For $e=1,-1,2,-2$ the smallest positive values of $y$ are $20$, $59$, $40$, $39$, and $e=0$ needs $79\mid y$. So the smallest multiple of $83$ lying within $2$ of a positive multiple of $79$ is $83\cdot20=1660$, next to $1659=79\cdot21$.

As $m$ is at least the multiple $83y$ among $m-2,m-1,m$, we get $m\ge1660$, and $m=1660$ works: $1660\cdot1659\cdot1658$ is divisible by $83$ and by $79$. The answer is $\boxed{1660}$.
