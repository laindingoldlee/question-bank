Unique prime factorisation gives each triple exactly once. The nonnegative series factors into three geometric series: $\left(\sum_{a\ge0}2^{-a}\right)\left(\sum_{b\ge0}3^{-b}\right)\left(\sum_{c\ge0}5^{-c}\right)=2\cdot\dfrac{3}{2}\cdot\dfrac{5}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{15}{4}}$. The factorization follows first for finite boxes of exponents and then by increasing limits.
