A rectangular grid of unit squares is $6$ squares wide and $5$ squares tall. Point $A$ is its top-left corner and point $B$ its bottom-right corner. Billy walks along the grid lines from $A$ to $B$, moving only right or down. In how many distinct ways can he get from $A$ to $B$?

(A) $462$\qquad (B) $86400$\qquad (C) $402$\qquad (D) $482$
