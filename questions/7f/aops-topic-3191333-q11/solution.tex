The arithmetic condition gives $2x=-\sqrt{-x}-x$, hence $3x=-\sqrt{-x}$. Let $u=\sqrt{-x}\ge0$. Then $3u^2=u$, so $u=0$ or $\dfrac{1}{3}$. The zero case violates distinctness. Thus $\boxed{x=-\dfrac{1}{9}}$, producing the distinct sequence $-\dfrac{1}{3},-\dfrac{1}{9},\dfrac{1}{9}$.
