A customer designs a birthday cake by choosing a flavour (marble, vanilla or chocolate), a size (small, medium or large), a frosting (buttercream or whipped cream), and any set of extras from swirls, sprinkles, chocolate sprinkles and candy (possibly none or all of them). He does not want a vanilla cake. Let $W$ be the number of different cakes he can design. How many positive divisors does $W$ have?
