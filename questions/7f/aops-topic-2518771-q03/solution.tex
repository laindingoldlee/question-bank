By the multiplication principle: $2$ flavours (marble or chocolate), $3$ sizes, $2$ frostings, and $2^4=16$ subsets of the $4$ extras. So
\[W=2\cdot3\cdot2\cdot16=192=2^6\cdot3,\]
which has $(6+1)(1+1)=\boxed{14}$ positive divisors.
