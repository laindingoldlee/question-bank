Put $a=a_1$ and $a_2=1-a$, where $0<a<1$. The expression is $F(a)=a\sqrt{\dfrac{1-a}{1+a}}$. Its square is $G(a)=\dfrac{a^2(1-a)}{1+a}$. Differentiation gives $G^{\prime}(a)=\dfrac{2a(1-a-a^2)}{(1+a)^2}$, so the unique maximum occurs at $a=\dfrac{\sqrt5-1}{2}$. Since $a^2=1-a$ and $1 + a = \dfrac{1}{a}$, the maximum of $F$ is $a^{\dfrac{5}{2}}$. Thus the answer is $\boxed{\left (\dfrac{\sqrt5-1}{2}\right)^{\dfrac{5}{2}}}$.
