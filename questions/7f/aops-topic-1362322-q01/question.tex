On a table there are cartons of juice and cookies for the students and invigilators in a room. If every student takes $2$ cartons of juice and $1$ cookie, there will be $121$ cookies left and no juice left. If every student and every invigilator takes $1$ carton of juice and $2$ cookies, there will be $100$ cartons of juice left and no cookies left. How many cookies were on the table at the beginning?
