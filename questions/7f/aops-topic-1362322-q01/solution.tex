Let there be $s$ students and $n$ people in all (students and invigilators), with $J$ cartons and $C$ cookies. The two scenarios give
\[
J=2s,\qquad C=s+121,\qquad J=n+100,\qquad C=2n .
\]
From the first and third, $n=2s-100$; then $s+121=2(2s-100)$, so $3s=321$ and $s=107$. Hence $n=114$ (so $7$ invigilators), $J=214$ and $C=\boxed{228}$ cookies.

Check: $107$ students take $214$ cartons and $107$ cookies, leaving $121$ cookies; $114$ people take $114$ cartons and $228$ cookies, leaving $100$ cartons.
