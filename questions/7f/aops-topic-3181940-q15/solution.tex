Ties have probability zero. Suppose $z$ is the largest side; multiply the resulting volume by 3. For fixed $z$, triangle and obtuse conditions are $x+y>z$ and $x^2+y^2<z^2$, with $x,y>0$. The permitted region is the quarter disk of radius $z$ minus the right triangle under $x+y=z$, of area $z^2(\dfrac{\pi}{4}-\dfrac{1}{2})$. Integrating gives probability $3\int_0^1z^2(\dfrac{\pi}{4}-\dfrac{1}{2})\,dz=\dfrac{\pi-2}{4}$. Thus $\boxed{a+b+c=7}$.
