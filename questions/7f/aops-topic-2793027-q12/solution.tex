Triangle $ATB$ is right, so $AB=\sqrt{4^2+12^2}=4\sqrt{10}$. Power of a point gives $AC\cdot AB=AT^2=16$, so $AC=\dfrac{4}{\sqrt{10}}$. Then $CB=4\sqrt{10}-\dfrac{4}{\sqrt{10}}=\dfrac{36}{\sqrt{10}}$, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{9}}$.
