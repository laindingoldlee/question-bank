Since $24$ American ounces equal $25$ English ounces, one American ounce equals $\dfrac{25}{24}$ English ounces. It is bigger by
\[
\dfrac{25}{24}-1=\dfrac{1}{24}=0.041\overline{6},
\]
that is, by $\boxed{4\dfrac{1}{6}\%}\approx4.17\%$.

(Not $4\%$: $4\%$ would be the amount by which the English ounce is \emph{smaller} than the American one, $1-\dfrac{24}{25}=\dfrac{1}{25}$.)
