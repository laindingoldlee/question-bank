The series converges absolutely by the ratio test. Let its sum be $S$. Subtracting the shifted series $\dfrac{1}{4}S$ gives $\dfrac{3}{4}S=\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{4^i}=\dfrac{1}{3}$. Hence $\boxed{S=\dfrac{4}{9}}$.
