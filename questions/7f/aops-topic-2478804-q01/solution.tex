An odd integer can be written as $2k+1$, and
\[(2k+1)^2=4k^2+4k+1=4k(k+1)+1\equiv1\pmod4.\]
So $A^2\equiv1$ and $B^2\equiv1\pmod4$, and $A^2+B^2\equiv2\pmod4$. Hence $A^2+B^2$ is even but not divisible by $4$. Since the odd primes are odd integers, this proves the claim. $\blacksquare$

(In fact $k(k+1)$ is even, so odd squares are $\equiv1\pmod8$ and $A^2+B^2\equiv2\pmod8$.)
