Take $A=(0,0)$, $B=(20,0)$, $C=(20,20)$, $D=(0,20)$. Then $M=(20,5)$ and $N=(10,20)$. A point on $BN$ is $(20-10t,20t)$, while $AM$ has equation $y=\dfrac{x}{4}$. Thus $20t=\dfrac{(20-10t)}{4}$, giving $t=\dfrac{2}{9}$ and $P=(\dfrac{160}{9},\dfrac{40}{9})$. With base $BM=5$ and horizontal altitude $20-\dfrac{160}{9}=\dfrac{20}{9}$,
\[
[BPM]=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot5\cdot\dfrac{20}{9}=\dfrac{50}{9}.
\]
Therefore $\boxed{m+n=59}$.
