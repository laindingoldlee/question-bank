Work modulo $3$. Since $85=84+1$ and $84=3\cdot28$, we have $85\equiv1$, so $85^9\equiv1^9=1\pmod 3$. Both $21$ and $6$ are multiples of $3$, so $21^9\equiv0$ and $6^9\equiv0$. Hence
\[
85^9-21^9+6^9\equiv1-0+0=1\pmod 3 .
\]
The remainder is $\boxed{1}$, so the number is not divisible by $3$.

Why subtracting the other two terms cannot help: $21^9-6^9$ is a multiple of $3$, and $85^9$ is $1$ more than a multiple of $3$. Adding or subtracting a multiple of $3$ never changes the remainder on division by $3$. (Without congruences: $85^9-1=(85-1)(85^8+\cdots+1)$ is a multiple of $84$, hence of $3$.)
