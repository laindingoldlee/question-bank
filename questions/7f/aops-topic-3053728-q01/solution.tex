Let $ H $ count heads. Then $ E((-1)^H)=P(H\text{ even})-P(H\text{ odd})$. Independence factors this expectation as \[\prod_{k=1}^{10}\left(\dfrac{1}{k+2}-\dfrac{k+1}{k+2}\right)=\prod_{k=1}^{10}\dfrac{k}{k+2}=\dfrac{2}{11\cdot12}=\dfrac{1}{66},\]where the ten negative signs cancel. Together with the sum of the two probabilities being 1, this gives $\boxed{P(H\text{ even})=\dfrac{67}{132}}$.
