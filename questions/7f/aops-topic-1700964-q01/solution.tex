Suppose $x^{2018}\equiv10\pmod{100}$. Then $x^{2018}=100k+10$ for some integer $k$.

The right side is even, so $x^{2018}$ is even and therefore $x$ is even. But then $x^{2018}$ is divisible by $2^{2018}$, in particular by $4$. On the other hand $100k+10\equiv2\pmod4$, which is not divisible by $4$. This contradiction shows that no such $x$ exists. $\blacksquare$

(So the question ``what is $x^2\bmod10$'' has no answer: the condition can never hold. In fact even powers $x^{2m}$ with $m\ge1$ can never be $\equiv2\pmod 4$.)
