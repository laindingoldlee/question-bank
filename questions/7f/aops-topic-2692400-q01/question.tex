Two parallel lines cut a circle of radius $1$ into three regions of equal area. Show that the distance between the lines is $d=2\cos\dfrac\theta2$, where $\theta$ is the unique solution in $(0,\pi)$ of $\theta-\sin\theta=\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$, and find $d$ to three decimal places.
