A positive integer $n$ is \emph{perfect}, \emph{abundant} or \emph{deficient} according as the sum of its proper divisors equals, exceeds or is less than $n$; equivalently, as $\sigma(n)=2n$, $\sigma(n)>2n$ or $\sigma(n)<2n$, where $\sigma(n)$ is the sum of all positive divisors of $n$. Using the formula for $\sigma$ in terms of the prime factorisation, classify $496$, $945$ and $1000$.
