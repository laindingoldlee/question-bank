For $k\ge1$,
\[
1+\dfrac{1}{k^2}+\dfrac{1}{(k+1)^2}
=\left(1+\dfrac{1}{k(k+1)}\right)^2.
\]
Taking the positive root and using $\dfrac{1}{(k(k+1))}=\dfrac{1}{k}-\dfrac{1}{(k+1)}$ gives
\[
A=2019+1-\dfrac{1}{2020}
=2019\cdot\dfrac{2021}{2020}.
\]
