For a fixed product, the sum is small when the numbers are close together; $\sqrt[3]{60}\approx3.9$. The triple $3,4,5$ has product $60$ and sum $12$. Checking all triples $a\le b\le c$ with $abc=60$:
\[
\begin{array}{c}
(1,1,60),(1,2,30),(1,3,20),(1,4,15),(1,5,12),(1,6,10),\\
(2,2,15),(2,3,10),(2,5,6),(3,4,5)
\end{array}
\]
with sums $62,33,24,20,18,17,19,15,13,12$. The smallest is $\boxed{12}$.
