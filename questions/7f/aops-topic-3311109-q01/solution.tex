The first equation factors as $(x-y-5)(x^2+xy+y^2)=0$. The second factor equals $(x+\dfrac{y}{2})^2+\dfrac{3}{4}y^2$, so it vanishes only at $(0,0)$, which fails the other equation. Thus $x=y+5$. Substitution gives $y(y-5)=6$, or $(y-6)(y+1)=0$. The solutions are $\boxed{(11,6),(4,-1)}$, both verified in the original equations.
