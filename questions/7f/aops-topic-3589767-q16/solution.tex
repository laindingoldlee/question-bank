The equation forces $x>0$. Factoring gives $(2^x-3^y)(2^x+3^y)=55$. Both factors are positive odd integers, the second larger. The only pairs are $(1,55)$ and $(5,11)$. The first gives $2^x=28$, impossible; the second gives $2^x=8$ and $3^y=3$, hence $x=3,y=1$. Therefore the answer is $\boxed3$.
