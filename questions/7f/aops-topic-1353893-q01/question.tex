For a positive integer $n$, let $f(n)$ be the exponent of the highest power of $2$ dividing $n!$. (For example $10!=2^8\cdot3^4\cdot5^2\cdot7$, so $f(10)=8$.) Compute $f(1)+f(2)+\cdots+f(1023)$.
