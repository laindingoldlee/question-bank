Let
\[N=9\times99\times999\times\cdots\times\underbrace{99\cdots9}_{999\text{ nines}}.\]
A student finds the remainder of $N$ on division by $1000$ as follows: ``Modulo $1000$ there are $333$ groups of $9\cdot99\cdot999\equiv9\cdot99\cdot(-1)\equiv-891\equiv109$. To account for the $333$ groups we multiply: $109\cdot333\equiv297$. So the remainder is $297$.''
Find the errors in this argument, and find the correct remainder.
