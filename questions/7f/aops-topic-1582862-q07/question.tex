You want to decorate the side of a cylindrical can by colouring a rectangular piece of paper, $19$ cm by $29$ cm, and wrapping it around the can so that it fits exactly (one side of the paper goes once around the can and the other side is the height of the can). Find the two possible diameters of the can, to the nearest $0.01$ cm. Use $3.14159$ for $\pi$.
