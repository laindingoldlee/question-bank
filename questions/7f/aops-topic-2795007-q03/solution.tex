A composite number $n<50$ has a prime factor at most $\sqrt{50}<8$, i.e. one of $2,3,5,7$. Two list members cannot share a prime, so at most four composites appear. Any prime may appear. The primes between $20$ and $50$ are $23,29,31,37,41,43,47$ (seven of them). So the list has at most $7+4=11$ numbers.

This is achieved by the seven primes together with $32=2^5$, $27=3^3$, $25=5^2$, $49=7^2$. The answer is $\boxed{11}$.
