The constant term is $9\ne0$, so no root is $0$. By Vieta's formulas,
\[rs+rt+ru+st+su+tu=\dfrac{1}{3},\qquad rstu=\dfrac{9}{3}=3.\]
Put the six fractions over the common denominator $rstu$. The numerator of $\dfrac{1}{rs}$ becomes $tu$, that of $\dfrac{1}{rt}$ becomes $su$, and so on: each pair is replaced by the complementary pair. So the numerators again run over all six products of two roots, and
\[\dfrac{1}{rs}+\dfrac{1}{rt}+\dfrac{1}{ru}+\dfrac{1}{st}+\dfrac{1}{su}+\dfrac{1}{tu}=\dfrac{tu+su+st+ru+rt+rs}{rstu}=\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{3}}{3}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{9}}.\]
