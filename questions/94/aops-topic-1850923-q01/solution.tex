\textbf{(a)} By the difference of squares, $(n+d)(n-d)=n^2-d^2$, so $(n+d)(n-d)+d^2=n^2$.

\textbf{(b)} For $47$ take $d=3$: $47^2=50\cdot44+9=2200+9=2209$. For $996$ take $d=4$: $996^2=1000\cdot992+16=992000+16=992016$. So $47^2=\boxed{2209}$ and $996^2=\boxed{992016}$.
