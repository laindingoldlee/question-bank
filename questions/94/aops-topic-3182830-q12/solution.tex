For each prime, its exponents $u$ in $a$ and $v$ in $b$ satisfy $2u+3v=e$. For $e=7$ the sole nonnegative solution is $(u,v)=(2,1)$, and for $e=5$ it is $(1,1)$. Consequently $a=2^2\cdot3^2\cdot5=180$ and $b=2\cdot3\cdot5=30$. There is $\boxed1$ representation.
