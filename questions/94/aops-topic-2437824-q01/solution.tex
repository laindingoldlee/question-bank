By the multinomial theorem,
\[(x+y+z)^{2021}=\sum_{a+b+c=2021}\dfrac{2021!}{a!\,b!\,c!}\,x^ay^bz^c,\]
with every coefficient positive, and $(x+y-z)^{2021}$ is the same sum with $z$ replaced by $-z$, i.e. each term multiplied by $(-1)^c$. In the difference, the terms with $c$ even cancel and the terms with $c$ odd double. So the terms are exactly the monomials $x^ay^bz^c$ with $a+b+c=2021$ and $c$ odd.

For a fixed odd $c$, there are $2022-c$ pairs $(a,b)$ of nonnegative integers with $a+b=2021-c$. Summing over $c=1,3,\dots,2021$:
\[2021+2019+\cdots+3+1=1011^2=\boxed{1022121}\]
(the sum of the first $1011$ odd numbers).
