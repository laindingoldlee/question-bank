Each term is $x_1^{e_1}\cdots x_k^{e_k}$ with nonnegative $e_i$ summing to $n$. By stars and bars there are $\dbinom{n+k-1}{k-1}$ of them; for $k=3$ this is $\boxed{\dbinom{n+2}{2}=\dfrac{(n+1)(n+2)}{2}}$.

The coefficient of $x_1^{e_1}\cdots x_k^{e_k}$ is the number of ways to choose, from the $n$ factors, which $e_1$ supply $x_1$, which $e_2$ supply $x_2$, and so on: the multinomial coefficient $\dfrac{n!}{e_1!e_2!\cdots e_k!}$.
