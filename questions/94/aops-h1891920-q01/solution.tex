Let the proportional sides be $a,3a$, so the third is $17$. The triangle inequalities reduce to $4a>17$ and $2a<17$. For integer $a$ this gives $5\le a\le8$. The perimeter $4a+17$ is maximised at $a=8$, yielding $\boxed{49}$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(24,0);real x=(8^2+24^2-17^2)/48.0;pair C=(x,sqrt(64-x*x));draw(A--B--C--cycle);label("$24$",(12,0),(0,-1));label("$8$",(A+C)/2,NW);label("$17$",(B+C)/2,NE);
\end{asy}
