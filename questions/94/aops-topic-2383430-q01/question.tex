Let $x$ be an integer with $1\le x\le100$. For how many values of $x$ are there positive integers $m$ and $n$ such that $m$, $n$ and $\sqrt{x}$ are the side lengths of a non-degenerate right triangle with $m\le\sqrt{x}<n$?
