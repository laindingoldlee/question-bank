For $ F(x)=\sum_{i\ge0}B_ix^i $, the recurrence gives $(1-x-x^2)F(x)=5-4x $. The series converges at $ x=\dfrac{1}{51}$ because the terms grow at most geometrically with ratio 2. Substitution gives $ F(\dfrac{1}{51})=\dfrac{5-\dfrac{4}{51}}{1-\dfrac{1}{51}-\dfrac{1}{2601}}=\boxed{\dfrac{12801}{2549}}$.
