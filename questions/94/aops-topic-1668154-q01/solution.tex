Use the complement: all six dice show different numbers. There are $6^6=46656$ equally likely outcomes (the dice are told apart, say by colour), and all six numbers are different in $6!=720$ of them, since the first die has $6$ choices, the second $5$, and so on. Hence
\[
P(\text{some two equal})=1-\dfrac{720}{46656}=1-\dfrac{5}{324}=\boxed{\dfrac{319}{324}}.
\]
