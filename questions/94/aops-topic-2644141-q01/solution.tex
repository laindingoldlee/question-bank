Each remainder is one less than its divisor, so $x+1$ is divisible by $2,3,4,6,7,8,9$ and $10$, that is, by
\[\operatorname{lcm}(2,3,4,6,7,8,9,10)=2^3\cdot3^2\cdot5\cdot7=2520.\]
Conversely, every $x$ with $2520\mid x+1$ works. The least positive one is $x=\boxed{2519}$.
