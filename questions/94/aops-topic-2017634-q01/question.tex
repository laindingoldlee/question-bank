While walking in the park, Nicole and Valerie came to a large round clearing surrounded by a ring of cottonwood trees and decided to count the trees. Nicole walked around the clearing and counted all the trees. Valerie did the same, but started from a different tree. Nicole's $20$th tree was Valerie's $7$th, while Nicole's $7$th tree was Valerie's $94$th. How many trees were growing around the clearing?
