The total after $x$ days is
\[3+6+\cdots+3x=3(1+2+\cdots+x)=\dfrac{3x(x+1)}{2}.\]
Setting this equal to $2460$ gives $x(x+1)=1640$. Since $40\cdot41=1640$ and $x(x+1)$ increases with $x$ for $x>0$, the answer is $x=\boxed{40}$. (Equivalently, $x^2+x-1640=(x-40)(x+41)=0$.)
