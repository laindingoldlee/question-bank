Ties in coordinates have probability zero. A point is a corner exactly when it is both an $x$-extreme and a $y$-extreme. The two $x$-extreme indices form a fixed pair conditional on the $x$ coordinates; the two $y$-extreme indices are an independent uniform pair among the six. They are disjoint with probability $\dfrac{\binom42}{\binom62}=\dfrac{2}{5}$. Hence the desired probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{3}{5}}$.

\end{asy}
