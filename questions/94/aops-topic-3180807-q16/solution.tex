The shoelace formula gives triangle area $120$. The side lengths are $\sqrt{288},\sqrt{272},\sqrt{272}$, all exceeding $12$, so the radius-$6$ disks centered at the vertices are disjoint. Also every altitude exceeds $\dfrac{240}{\sqrt{288}}>6$, so each disk cuts out exactly its vertex sector. Their central angles sum to $\pi$, and their total area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot6^2\pi=18\pi$. Therefore the available area is $\boxed{120-18\pi}$.
