Bob and Fred race. Bob runs a track of length $L$ at a constant speed $b$. Fred's track is Bob's track with an extra curve added, and this extra curve makes up $\dfrac14$ of the length of Fred's whole track. Fred runs at a constant speed $v$, except on the extra curve, where he runs at half that speed. How much faster than Bob must Fred run (that is, how large must $v$ be compared with $b$) for Fred to win?
