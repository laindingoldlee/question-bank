Because factorials strictly increase for $n\ge2$, it suffices to locate the logarithm of one factorial and its neighbours. Rigorous logarithmic bounds give $2024.49146293<\log(404!)<2024.49146295$. For example, sum certified bounds for $\log k$ over $1\le k\le404$; each may be obtained by range reduction and $\log z = 2 \sum_{j\ge0} \dfrac{(\dfrac{z-1}{z+1})^{2j+1}}{2j+1}$ with its geometric tail bound. This places $404!$ strictly between $e^{2024}$ and $e^{2025}$. Moreover $\log(403!)=\log(404!)-\log404<2024$, while $\log(405!)=\log(404!)+\log405>2025$. Monotonicity excludes all other integers. Therefore $\boxed{n=404}$.
