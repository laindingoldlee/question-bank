For each student, the indicator of having yellow equals the indicator of having no gray plus the indicator of having two different colours minus the indicator of having orange. Check the six possible colour multisets: OO, YY, GG, OY, OG and YG; the identity holds in each. Summing gives the yellow count $48+34-56=\boxed{26}$.
