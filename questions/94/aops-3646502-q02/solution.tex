Each subdivision quarters the area. Two subdivisions make a smallest square one sixteenth of the original, so the largest area is $16\cdot2=\boxed{32}$.

import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
draw(box((0,0),(4,4)));draw((2,0)--(2,4)^^(0,2)--(4,2));draw((1,0)--(1,2)^^(0,1)--(2,1));label("$2$",(.5,.5));
\end{asy}
