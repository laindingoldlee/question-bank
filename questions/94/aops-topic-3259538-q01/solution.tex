If membership is $N$, then $N+2$ is divisible by $8$ and $N+3$ by $9$. Thus $N\equiv6\pmod8$ and $N\equiv6\pmod9$, implying $N\equiv6\pmod{72}$. The only value in the interval is $\boxed{150}$. Indeed $150=8(19)-2=9(17)-3$. Empty positions are subtracted from a complete formation, not added to it.
