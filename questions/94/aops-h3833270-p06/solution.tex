Let AB=s and place $A=(0,0),B=(s,0),C=(s+2,0),D=(\dfrac{s}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt3s}{2})$. The distance equation gives $(\dfrac{s}{2}+2)^2+\dfrac{3s^2}{4}=52$, so $s^2+2s-48=0$. The positive root is six, hence $AC=6+2=\boxed8$.
