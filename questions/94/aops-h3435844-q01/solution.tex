Since $OA\perp AD$, triangle $OAD$ is right isosceles. Choose
\[
O=(0,0),\quad D=(\sqrt2,0),\quad
A=E+\left(0,\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}\right),\quad E=\left(\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2},0\right).
\]
Thus (a) $\boxed{OE=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}}$.

Write line $BC$ as $y=m(x-\sqrt2)$. It cuts the unit circle twice, so its distance from $O$ is less than $1$, giving $m^2<1$. Also $F=\left(\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2},-\dfrac{m}{\sqrt2}\right)$, hence
\[
[OEF]=\dfrac{|m|}{4}.
\]
The circle $ADO$ has diameter $OD$ and equation $x^2+y^2-\sqrt2x=0$. Intersecting with the secant gives
\[
H=\left(\dfrac{\sqrt2m^2}{1+m^2},-\dfrac{\sqrt2m}{1+m^2}\right),\quad
FH=\dfrac{1-m^2}{\sqrt{2(1+m^2)}}.
\]

(b) Put $t=m^2$. The condition gives $6(1-t)^2=1+t$, or $(2t-1)(3t-5)=0$. Only $t=\dfrac{1}{2}$ satisfies $t<1$. Therefore $\boxed{[OEF]=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{8}}$.

(c) A chord of length $\sqrt3$ in a unit circle is at distance $\dfrac{1}{2}$ from its center. Thus $\dfrac{2m^2}{1+m^2}=\dfrac{1}{4}$, giving $m^2=\dfrac{1}{7}$ and $\boxed{[OEF]=\dfrac{\sqrt7}{28}}$.

(d) The angle at $E$ is right. Its internal bisector through $B$ has $B=E+(t,\pm t)$, where $t>0$ and the sign is that of $F_y$. The unit-circle equation gives $t=\dfrac{\sqrt3-1}{2\sqrt2}$. Thus
\[
B_x=\dfrac{\sqrt3+1}{2\sqrt2},\qquad
|B_y|=\dfrac{\sqrt3-1}{2\sqrt2},
\]
and
\[
|m|=\dfrac{|B_y|}{\sqrt2-B_x}
=\dfrac{\sqrt3-1}{3-\sqrt3}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}.
\]
Hence $\boxed{[OEF]=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{12}}$.
