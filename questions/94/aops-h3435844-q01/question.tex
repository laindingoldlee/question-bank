Triangle $ABC$ has circumcenter $O$. Its tangent at $A$ meets line $BC$ at $D$, with $AD=AO=1$. The line through $A$ perpendicular to $OD$ meets $OD$ at $E$ and $BC$ at $F$. The circle through $A,D,O$ meets $BC$ again at $H$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Find $OE$.
\item If $FH=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{12}}$, find $[OEF]$.
\item If $BC=\sqrt3$, find $[OEF]$.
\item If $B$ lies on the internal angle bisector of $\angle DEF$, find $[OEF]$.
\end{enumerate}
The extra assumptions in the last three parts are separate.
