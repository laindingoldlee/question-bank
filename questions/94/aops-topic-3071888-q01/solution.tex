The saving is $402.50-250=152.50$ dollars. Divide by the original price, not the new price: the saving percentage is $\dfrac{152.50}{402.50}\cdot100\%=\boxed{\dfrac{6100}{161}\%}\approx\boxed{37.89\%}$.
