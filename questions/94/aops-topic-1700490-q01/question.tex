For real numbers, use the following convention: $x^y$ is defined when $x>0$ (with $x^y=e^{y\ln x}$), when $x=0$ and $y>0$ (with $0^y=0$), and when $x<0$ and $y$ is an integer. Prove that if $x^y=1$, then at least one of the following holds:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $x=-1$ and $y$ is an even integer;
\item[(b)] $x=1$;
\item[(c)] $y=0$ (and $x\ne0$).
\end{enumerate}
