The complement is that Alice's $6$ correct questions are all among Bob's $8$. Given Bob's set, this happens for $\binom86=28$ of the $\binom{10}6=210$ equally likely choices of Alice's set. So the answer is $1-\dfrac{28}{210}=1-\dfrac2{15}=\boxed{\dfrac{13}{15}}$.
