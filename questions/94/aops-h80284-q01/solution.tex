For $k\ge1$, the powers $9^{k-1}$ and $9^k$ have the same number of digits exactly when $9^k$ has leading digit $9$. Indeed, dividing a number with leading digit $9$ by $9$ preserves its digit count; dividing a number with leading digit at most $8$ by $9$ decreases it by one.

Each multiplication by $9$ increases the digit count by either zero or one. From $9^0=1$ to $9^{4000}$, the total increase is $3817-1=3816$ over $4000$ steps. Thus $4000-3816=184$ steps preserve the count. Since $9^0$ itself does not begin with $9$, the answer is $\boxed{184}$.
