The first fraction is $\dfrac{3}{2}w^5x^{-6}y^5$; the divisor is $\dfrac{16w^{12}y^4}{4}=4w^{12}y^4$. Dividing, $\dfrac{3}{8}w^{-7}x^{-6}y=\boxed{\dfrac{3y}{8w^7x^6}}$.
