When Melanie catches up, both have covered the same distance, $85\cdot7=595$ miles. Joan covers this at $76$ mph in
\[
\dfrac{595}{76}\text{ hours}=\boxed{7\dfrac{63}{76}\text{ hours}}\approx7.83\text{ hours}.
\]
