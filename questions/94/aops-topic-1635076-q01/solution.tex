By definition of the logarithm, $2^x=14$ exactly when
\[
x=\boxed{\log_2 14}=\log_2(2\cdot7)=1+\log_27\approx3.807 ,
\]
so $14=2^{\log_2 14}$. Since $2^3=8<14<16=2^4$ and $2^x$ is increasing, $x$ lies between $\boxed{3\text{ and }4}$.
