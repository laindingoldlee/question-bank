Since $70=2\cdot5\cdot7$ and each digit is at most $9$, no digit can contain two of the primes $2,5,7$ (that would be at least $10$). So three digits are $2$, $5$, $7$ and the fourth is $1$ (no digit is $0$, as the product is not $0$). The largest number with digits $1,2,5,7$ puts them in decreasing order: $\boxed{7521}$.
