If $k=0$, degree comparison forces $P$ to be quadratic. Writing $P(x)=ax^2+bx+c$ gives $4ax^2+2b=18x^2$, so $P(x)=\dfrac{9}{2}x^2+c$ with arbitrary real $c$. Then $P(k)=c$ is undetermined. If $k\ne0$, a linear polynomial cannot satisfy the constant and quadratic coefficients; degree comparison for degree at least two gives $2d-2=d+1$, hence $d=3$. Comparing coefficients for a cubic gives $k=6$ and $P(x)=3x^3-3x$, so $P(k)=630$. Therefore the source has no unique answer without excluding $k=0$; under that added assumption the answer is $\boxed{630}$.
