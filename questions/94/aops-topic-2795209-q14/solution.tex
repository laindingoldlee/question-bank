Each residue class modulo $3$ contains three of the numbers $1,\dots,9$. Choose $c$ first ($9$ ways) and let its residue be $r$. The ordered pairs $(a,b)$ and $(d,e)$ must each have residue sum $\equiv -r$.

The residue pairs summing to $-r$ are $(r,r)$ and the two orders of the other two residues $s,t$ (since $r+r\equiv-r$ and $s+t\equiv-r$). After $c$ is chosen, class $r$ has $2$ numbers left and classes $s,t$ have $3$ each.

\begin{itemize}
\item $(a,b)$ both from class $r$: $2\cdot1=2$ ways. Then $(d,e)$ must be one from $s$ and one from $t$: $3\cdot3\cdot2=18$ ways. Subtotal $36$.
\item $(a,b)$ one from $s$, one from $t$: $3\cdot3\cdot2=18$ ways. Then $(d,e)$ is either both from class $r$ ($2$ ways) or one each from $s,t$ ($2\cdot2\cdot2=8$ ways). Subtotal $18\cdot10=180$.
\end{itemize}
So each $c$ gives $216$ sequences, and the total is $9\cdot216=\boxed{1944}$.
