Squaring gives $x^2+x^{-2}=3$; positivity yields $x+x^{-1}=\sqrt5$. Therefore $x^2-x^{-2}=\sqrt5$, $x^4-x^{-4}=3\sqrt5$, and $x^4+x^{-4}=3^2-2=7$. Finally $x^8-x^{-8}=(x^4-x^{-4})(x^4+x^{-4})=\boxed{21\sqrt5}$.
