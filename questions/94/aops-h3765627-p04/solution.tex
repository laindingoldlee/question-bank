For any 3 by 3 magic square with center $e$, summing the middle row, middle column, and two diagonals counts all nine cells once and the center three extra times. Thus $4S=3S+3e$ and $S=3e=150$. Opposite cells sum to $2e=100$. Let the upper-left cell be $a$. Then middle-left is 44, bottom-left is $106-a$, and upper-right is $a-6$. The top row gives $a+38+(a-6)=150$, so $a=\boxed{59}$.

\end{asy}
