Write $N=32k+30$. We need $32k+30\equiv44\pmod{58}$, i.e. $32k\equiv14\pmod{58}$, i.e. $16k\equiv7\pmod{29}$. Since $16\cdot20=320=11\cdot29+1$, the inverse of $16$ modulo $29$ is $20$, so $k\equiv140\equiv24\pmod{29}$. Hence $N\equiv32\cdot24+30=798\pmod{\operatorname{lcm}(32,58)=928}$.

The numbers below $3000$ are $\boxed{798,\ 1726\ \text{and}\ 2654}$.
