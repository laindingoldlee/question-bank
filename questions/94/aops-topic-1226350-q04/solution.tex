The radius at height $h$ is $10-\dfrac {h}{2}$. The volume of lava up to height $h$ is the cone of radius $10$ (height $20$) minus the cone above height $h$, with radius $10-\dfrac {h}{2}$ and height $20-h$:
\[V(h)=\dfrac{\pi}{3}\left(10^2\cdot20-\left(10-\dfrac {h}{2}\right)^2(20-h)\right)=\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\left(1000-\left(10-\dfrac {h}{2}\right)^3\right).\]
