A volcano is a hollow cone, point up, with everything above height $6$ m removed; the resulting frustum has bottom radius $10$ m, top radius $7$ m and height $6$ m. It fills with lava until it is half full (by volume). Then it erupts, lowering the lava level to a height of $2$ m. What fraction of the lava remained?
