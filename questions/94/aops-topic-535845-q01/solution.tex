In $24$ hours the spider crawls down $\dfrac{24}{2}=12$ inches $=1$ foot.

Let day $0$ be the first day, when the spider is placed at height $3$ feet. Each later morning it is found $1$ foot lower than where it was placed the day before, and then raised $3$ feet, so its placed height rises by $2$ feet per day. After the move on day $k$ it is at $3+2k$ feet, and on the morning of day $k$ it is found at
\[
3+2(k-1)-1=2k\text{ feet}.
\]
Tony can move it up $3$ feet only if $2k+3\le18$, i.e.\ $k\le7.5$. On day $7$ he finds it at $14$ feet and moves it to $17$ feet. On day $8$ he finds it at $16$ feet, and $16+3=19>18$. So he runs out of room
\[
\boxed{8\text{ days}}
\]
after the first day.
