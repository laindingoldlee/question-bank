Tony has a sticky toy spider that slowly crawls down a wall: left untouched, it moves down $1$ inch every $2$ hours. One morning at $9$ o'clock, Tony sticks the spider to the living-room wall $3$ feet above the floor. Every following morning at $9$ o'clock he finds the spider and moves it up $3$ feet from where he finds it. The wall is $18$ feet high. On which day after the first day will Tony first be unable to place the spider $3$ feet higher?
