$2x^2-3x-2=(2x+1)(x-2)$, so for $x\ne2$ the two expressions are equal. But $\dfrac{2x^2-3x-2}{x-2}$ is undefined at $x=2$. So the graph is the line $y=2x+1$ with one point removed: a hole at $\boxed{(2,5)}$. Multiplying numerator and denominator by $x-2$ changes the domain, which is why the two forms are not the same function.
