Choose the thousands and hundreds digits and the units digit arbitrarily: $10^3$ choices. The sum of those digits plus three determines a unique residue for the first digit modulo nine. Exactly one of $1,\ldots,9$ has that residue. Therefore the answer is $\boxed{1000}$.
