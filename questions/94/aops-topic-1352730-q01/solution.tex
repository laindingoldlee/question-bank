Add up the money coming in and going out: you pay $6+8=14$ dollars and receive $7+9=16$ dollars. The profit is $16-14=\boxed{2}$ dollars. (Buying the toy back for $8$ is not a loss of $1$ on top of this: the two trades $6\to7$ and $8\to9$ each earn $1$ dollar.)
