Three congruent circles are mutually externally tangent, and each circle is tangent to a different side of an equilateral triangle with side length $2$ (the circles lie inside the triangle, each touching one side). The common radius of the circles can be written as $a-\sqrt b$, where $a$ and $b$ are positive integers. What is $3a+b$?
