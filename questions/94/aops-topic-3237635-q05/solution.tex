Both radii are $2$ and the centre distance is $3$. Let the circle intersections be $U,V$, and let $H$ be the midpoint of $OP$. Then $OH=\dfrac{3}{2}$ and $UH=\sqrt{4-\dfrac{9}{4}}=\dfrac{\sqrt7}{2}$. Put $\theta=\arccos(\dfrac{3}{4})$ in radians. The overlap comprises two congruent circular segments. Each has sector area $\dfrac{1}{2}(2^2)(2\theta)=4\theta$. The triangle to subtract from one segment has base $UV=\sqrt7$ and height $OH=\dfrac{3}{2}$, so its area is $\dfrac{3\sqrt7}{4}$. Therefore the total is
\[\boxed{8\arccos(\dfrac{3}{4})-\dfrac{3\sqrt7}{2}}.\]
