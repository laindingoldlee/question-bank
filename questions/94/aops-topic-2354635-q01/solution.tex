Collect the terms with $r$: $2r-xr=\dfrac23$, that is, $r(2-x)=\dfrac23$. If $x\ne2$,
\[r=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{3(2-x)}}=\dfrac{2}{6-3x}.\]
If $x=2$, the equation becomes $0\cdot r=\dfrac23$, which has $\boxed{\text{no solution}}$.
