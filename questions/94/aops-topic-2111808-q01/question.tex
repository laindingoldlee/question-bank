Ten marbles $A,B,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,J$ finish a race in places $1$st to $10$th, with no ties. Use the clues to find the complete finishing order.
\begin{enumerate}
\item $F$ finished in an odd-numbered place.
\item $C$ finished ahead of $E$ and $F$, but behind $B$ and $G$.
\item $A$ finished in the top $5$, but not in the top $3$.
\item $J$ did not finish first, fifth, sixth or last.
\item $I$ finished exactly one place ahead of $D$ and exactly one place behind $J$.
\item $I$ finished in one of the last three places.
\item $A$ finished ahead of $J$ and behind $C$.
\item $F$ did not finish in the top $5$, but finished ahead of $J$; $J$ finished ahead of $I$, and $I$ ahead of $D$.
\item $C$ finished exactly three places ahead of $F$.
\item $H$ did not win, but finished ahead of $B$.
\end{enumerate}
