By clue 5, $J,I,D$ finish in three consecutive places, and by clue 6, $I$ is $8$th or $9$th.

\begin{itemize}
\item If $I$ were $8$th, then $J$ would be $7$th. $F$ finishes ahead of $J$ but not in the top $5$ (clue 8), so $F$ would be $6$th, contradicting clue 1.
\item So $I$ is $9$th, $J$ is $8$th and $D$ is $10$th. Now $F$ is $6$th or $7$th, and clue 1 gives $F$ $7$th.
\item Clue 9: $C$ is $4$th. Clue 3 and clue 7 ($A$ behind $C$): $A$ is $5$th.
\item Clue 2: $B$ and $G$ are ahead of $C$, so $B,G$ are in places $1$ to $3$, and so is the remaining marble $H$ or $E$ that fills those places. $E$ is behind $C$, so places $1$ to $3$ are $B,G,H$, and $E$ is $6$th.
\item Clue 10: $H$ is ahead of $B$ and is not first, so $G$ is $1$st, $H$ is $2$nd and $B$ is $3$rd.
\end{itemize}
All clues check (clue 4 holds since $J$ is $8$th). The order is
\[\boxed{G,\ H,\ B,\ C,\ A,\ E,\ F,\ J,\ I,\ D}.\]
