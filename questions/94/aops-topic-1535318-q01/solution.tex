Since $i\cdot(-i)=-i^2=1$, the reciprocal of $i$ is $i^{-1}=-i$. On the other hand $i^3=i^2\cdot i=-i$. Hence $i^{-1}=-i=i^3$.

Another way to see it: $i^4=(i^2)^2=1$, so multiplying by $i^4$ does not change a number, and $i^{-1}=i^{-1}\cdot i^4=i^3$. (So the powers of $i$ repeat with period $4$ also for negative exponents.) $\blacksquare$
