Pair the outer and inner factors. Let $t=2000\cdot2015=4030000$. Then
\[2007\cdot2008=(2000+7)(2015-7)=2000\cdot2015+7(2015-2000)-49=t+56 .\]
So the expression under the root is
\[t(t+56)+784=t^2+56t+28^2=(t+28)^2,\]
and the answer is $t+28=\boxed{4030028}$.
