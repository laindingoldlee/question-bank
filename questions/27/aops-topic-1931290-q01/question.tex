How many ways are there to arrange the letters of the word MACHINES so that no two vowels are next to each other?

A student answered as follows: ``There are $8!$ arrangements in all. To count those with two vowels together, glue two vowels into one block: $7!$ arrangements, times $2$ orders inside the block, times $3$ choices of the two vowels. So the answer is $8!-7!\cdot2\cdot3=10080$.'' Explain the error and give the correct answer.
