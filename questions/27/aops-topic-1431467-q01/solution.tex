Replace $A,B,C$ by the digits $0,1,2$. Alphabetical order of the words is then increasing order of the base-$3$ numbers $000_3,001_3,\dots,222_3$, and the word with value $v$ is Word $v+1$. Since
\[
BAB=101_3=9+0+1=10,
\]
$BAB$ is Word $\boxed{11}$.

(Directly: the $9$ words beginning with $A$ come first, then $BAA$ is Word $10$ and $BAB$ is Word $11$.)
