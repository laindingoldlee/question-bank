Points $A_1,A_2,\dots,A_{2015}$ in the plane satisfy $A_iA_{i+1}=1$ for $1\le i\le2014$ and $A_{j-1}A_j\perp A_jA_{j+1}$ for $2\le j\le2014$. Show that these conditions do not determine the distance $A_1A_{2015}$, and find its least possible value.
