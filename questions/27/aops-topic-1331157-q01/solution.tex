Let the numbers be $a$ and $b$. Since $|a^2-b^2|=|a-b|\,|a+b|$, we get $|a+b|=\dfrac{24}{6}=4$. Then
\[
ab=\dfrac{(a+b)^2-(a-b)^2}{4}=\dfrac{16-36}{4}=\boxed{-5}.
\]
For example $5$ and $-1$: difference $6$, squares $25$ and $1$ differ by $24$.
