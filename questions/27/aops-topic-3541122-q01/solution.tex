For each prime $p_i$, assign nonnegative exponents to the k factors, summing to $e_i$. Stars and bars gives $\binom{e_i+k-1}{k-1}$ choices. The exponent assignments for different primes are independent, and they determine the factors uniquely. Thus the number is $\boxed{\prod_{i=1}^r\binom{e_i+k-1}{k-1}}$.
