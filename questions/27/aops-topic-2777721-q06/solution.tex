Let $ F_n $ count valid strings of length $ n $. Those ending green contribute $ F_{n-1}$, and those ending red have a preceding green and contribute $ F_{n-2}$. Thus $ F_n=F_{n-1}+F_{n-2}$, with $ F_0=1,F_1=2 $. The next values are 3,5,8,13, so $\boxed{F_5=13}$.
