Let \(R\) be the region of all points \((x,y)\) satisfying

\[
(x^2-y^2-12x)^2+(2xy-12y)^2\le 144(x^2+y^2) \qquad \text{and}\qquad x^3-3xy^2\ge 0.
\]The area of \(R\) can be written in the form \(a\pi+b\sqrt{c}\), where \(a,b,c\) are positive integers and \(c\) is not divisible by the square of any prime. Find \(a+b+c\).
