Two regular hexagons are drawn so that each vertex of the inner hexagon lies on a side of the outer hexagon. The outer hexagon has sides of length $4$ inches, and each vertex of the inner hexagon is $1$ inch from the nearest vertex of the outer hexagon. What is the ratio of the area of the inner hexagon to the area of the outer hexagon? Express your answer as a common fraction.
