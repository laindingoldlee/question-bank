Each side of the outer hexagon is split by an inner vertex into pieces of length $1$ and $3$. Going around, the pieces alternate, so at each outer vertex $P$ the two neighbouring inner vertices $X$ and $Y$ satisfy $PX=3$ and $PY=1$ (or the reverse), with the interior angle $\angle XPY=120^\circ$.

By the law of cosines, the side $s$ of the inner hexagon satisfies
\[
s^2=XY^2=3^2+1^2-2\cdot3\cdot1\cos120^\circ=9+1+3=13 .
\]
Regular hexagons are similar, so the ratio of their areas is the square of the ratio of their sides:
\[
\dfrac{s^2}{4^2}=\boxed{\dfrac{13}{16}} .
\]
