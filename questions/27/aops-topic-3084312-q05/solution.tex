Let $R_a=\dfrac{a^2a!}{a^a}$. Its values for $a=1,2,3,4,5$ are $1,2,2,\dfrac{3}{2},\dfrac{24}{25}$. Also $\dfrac{R_{a+1}}{R_a}=\left(\dfrac{a}{a+1}\right)^{a-2}<1$ for $a>2$. Therefore no $a\ge5$ works, and the solutions are $\boxed{2,3,4}$.
