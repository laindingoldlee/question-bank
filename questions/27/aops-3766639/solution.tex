A prime product requires counts $(p,1,1)$ in some order, with $p$ prime. During five rolls the only possibilities are $(2,1,1)$ at time four and $(3,1,1)$ at time five. The first event has $3\cdot\dfrac{4!}{2!}\cdot3=108$ length-five roll sequences. Additional successes must finish at $(3,1,1)$ without already having all faces at time four; the fifth roll must then be one of the singleton faces. Choose the triple face in $3$ ways, the final singleton in $2$ ways, and arrange the first four rolls in $4$ ways: $24$ additional sequences. The probability is $\dfrac{108+24}{3^5}=\boxed{\dfrac{44}{81}}$.
