In a large hostel the rooms are numbered $2,4,6,\dots,1998,2000$, and every room has one occupant. At first all doors are open. Then the occupant of room $2$ changes the state (open to closed, or closed to open) of the doors of all rooms whose numbers are multiples of $2$; next the occupant of room $4$ changes the state of the doors of all rooms whose numbers are multiples of $4$; and so on, each occupant in turn in increasing order of room number. When everyone has finished, what is the highest-numbered room whose door is closed?
