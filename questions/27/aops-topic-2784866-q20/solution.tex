A 30-degree inscribed angle intercepts the short arc spanning two polygon steps. There are 12 pairs of endpoints for such an arc, and 9 possible third vertices outside the short arc, giving 108 incidences of triangles with a 30-degree angle. A triangle with two such angles has cyclic gaps 2,2,8 and occurs in 12 positions; it was counted twice. No triangle has three 30-degree angles. Thus 96 of the $\binom{12}{3}=220 $ triangles qualify, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{24}{55}}$.

\end{asy}
