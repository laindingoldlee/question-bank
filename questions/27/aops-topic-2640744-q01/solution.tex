We have $4^{40}=2^{80}$, whose positive divisors are $2^j$ with $0\le j\le80$.

\textbf{(a)} $2^j$ is a perfect square exactly when $j$ is even (by unique factorisation), giving $j=0,2,\dots,80$: $\boxed{41}$ divisors, the powers $4^i$, $0\le i\le40$.

\textbf{(b)} $2^j$ is a perfect fourth power exactly when $4\mid j$, giving $j=0,4,\dots,80$: $\boxed{21}$ divisors, and $2^{4k}=4^{2k}$ for $k=0,\dots,20$. $\blacksquare$
