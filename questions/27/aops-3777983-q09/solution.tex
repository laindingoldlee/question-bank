View occupied cells as edges of a bipartite graph on rows and columns. Every vertex has degree two, so the graph is a disjoint union of even cycles. In each cycle, there are exactly two alternating edge-colourings with colours red and blue. Thus $2^{f(A)}$ counts decompositions into an ordered pair of disjoint perfect matchings. Choose the red matching in $6!$ ways; the blue one is a derangement relative to it, with $!6=265$ choices by inclusion-exclusion. Therefore the sum is $\boxed{6!\cdot265=190800}$.
