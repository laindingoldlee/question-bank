Write $x=r(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)$. By De Moivre's theorem, $x^8=r^8(\cos8\theta+i\sin8\theta)=1$ forces $r=1$ and $8\theta=360^\circ k$, so $\theta=45^\circ k$ for $k=0,1,\dots,7$. The eight solutions are
\[
\boxed{\;\pm1,\quad\pm i,\quad\pm\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\pm\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\,i\;}
\]
(all four sign choices in the last group). Equivalently, $x^8-1=(x-1)(x+1)(x^2+1)(x^4+1)$ and $x^4+1=0$ gives $x^2=\pm i$.
