The product $\dfrac{a}{b}\cdot\dfrac{b}{a}=\dfrac{ab}{ba}=1$. Thus the two quotients are multiplicative inverses, giving the stated equality. Both $a$ and $b$ must be nonzero so that the quotients and reciprocal are defined.
