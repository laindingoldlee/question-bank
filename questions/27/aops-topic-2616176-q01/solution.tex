The units digit of $3C$ is $C$, so $2C$ is a multiple of $10$ and $C\in\{0,5\}$. Then $\overline{ABC}=\dfrac{\overline{79C}}{3}$, and $790$ is not divisible by $3$, so $C=5$ and $\overline{ABC}=\dfrac{795}{3}=265$. Thus $A=2$, $B=6$, $C=5$, and $3A+2B+C=6+12+5=\boxed{23}$.
