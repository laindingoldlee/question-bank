\textbf{(a)} $N$ has $k$ digits exactly when $10^{k-1}\le N<10^k$, i.e. when $k-1\le\log_{10}N<k$, i.e. when $k-1=\lfloor\log_{10}N\rfloor$.

\textbf{(b)} $\log_{10}3^{87}=87\log_{10}3$. From $0.477115\le\log_{10}3<0.477125$,
\[41.509\le87\log_{10}3<41.510,\]
so $\left\lfloor\log_{10}3^{87}\right\rfloor=41$, and $3^{87}$ has $\boxed{42}$ digits.
