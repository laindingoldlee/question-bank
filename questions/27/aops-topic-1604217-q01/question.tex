Cool calculators for Mathletes cost \$15 each and all other calculators cost \$6 each. Gregg went to the store and bought at least one of each kind. If he had switched the numbers of the two kinds of calculator he bought, he would have spent \$87. What is the sum of all the possible amounts he could have spent?
