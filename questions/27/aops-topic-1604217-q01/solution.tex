Let Gregg buy $c\ge1$ cool calculators and $p\ge1$ others; he spent $15c+6p$. With the numbers switched he would buy $p$ cool and $c$ others, so
\[
15p+6c=87\qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad 5p+2c=29 .
\]
Then $p$ is odd (since $5p=29-2c$ is odd) and $5p\le29-2=27$, so $p\in\{1,3,5\}$:
\[
p=1,\ c=12;\qquad p=3,\ c=7;\qquad p=5,\ c=2 .
\]
The amounts actually spent are $15\cdot12+6\cdot1=186$, $15\cdot7+6\cdot3=123$ and $15\cdot2+6\cdot5=60$ dollars, and their sum is
\[
186+123+60=\boxed{369}\text{ dollars}.
\]
