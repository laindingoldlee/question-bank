SATURDAY has the letters $S,T,U,R,D,Y$ once each and $A$ twice, so $7$ distinct letters.

\emph{No repeated letter.} Arrange $4$ of the $7$ distinct letters: $7\cdot6\cdot5\cdot4=840$.

\emph{Two A's.} Choose the other two letters from the $6$ non-A letters in $\dbinom62=15$ ways and arrange $A,A,\square,\square$ in $\dfrac{4!}{2!}=12$ ways: $180$.

Total: $840+180=\boxed{1020}$.
