A circle $\omega$ with the same centre $I$ as the incircle of triangle $ABC$ meets each side of the triangle at two points, and the six points form a convex hexagon $A_1A_2B_1B_2C_1C_2$, where $A_1,A_2$ lie on $BC$, $B_1,B_2$ on $CA$, and $C_1,C_2$ on $AB$. Prove that if $A_1B_1$ is parallel to the bisector of angle $B$, then $A_2C_2$ is parallel to the bisector of angle $C$.
