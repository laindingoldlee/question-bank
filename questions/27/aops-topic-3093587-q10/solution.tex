The combined rate is $\dfrac{1}{6}+\dfrac{1}{7}=\dfrac{13}{42}$. Schedule (i) takes $\dfrac{84}{13}$ hours. In (ii), after 6 hours Bob has $\dfrac{1}{7}$ wall left; collaboration takes another $\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{7}}{\dfrac{13}{42}}=\dfrac{6}{13}$ hour. Its total is also $\dfrac{84}{13}$ hours. Thus the saving is $\boxed0$. Both schedules keep both workers productive throughout.
