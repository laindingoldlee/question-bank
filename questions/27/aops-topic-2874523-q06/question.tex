In a two-player card game, cards are numbered 1, 2, and 3. Each player reveals the top card. The higher card wins; its owner appends the losing card and then the winning card to their deck. Evelyn begins with deck $(2)$ and Odette with deck $(1,3)$, listed top first. Determine whether the game ends.
