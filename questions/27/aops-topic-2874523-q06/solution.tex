List the decks after each move:\[(2);(1,3)\ \longrightarrow\ (1,2);(3)\ \longrightarrow\ (2);(1,3).\]In the first move $2$ beats $1$, leaving Odette with $3$. In the second $3$ beats $1$, restoring the initial state. Neither deck becomes empty, so these two states repeat forever and the game never ends.
