Let $x\ge0$ and $b>0$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Simplify $\dfrac{\sqrt{63x}}{\sqrt{8b^2}}$ and rationalise the denominator.
\item[(b)] A student multiplies the numerator and the denominator by $\sqrt{8b^2}$ and writes the result as $\dfrac{504xb^2}{8b^2}$. Find the error, and show that the method, carried out correctly, gives the same answer as (a).
\end{enumerate}
