A game-playing system reaches the 8 k stage with certainty. Conditional on reaching the preceding stage, its probabilities of reaching 16 k, 32 k and 65 k are $99\%$, $80\%$ and $13\%$, respectively. What is its chance of reaching 65 k from the start, rounded to the nearest $0.01\%$?
