Each of the three digits can be chosen independently as $2$ or $5$ (neither is $0$, so the hundreds digit causes no problem). By the multiplication principle there are $2\cdot2\cdot2=\boxed{8}$ such integers: $222,225,252,255,522,525,552,555$.
