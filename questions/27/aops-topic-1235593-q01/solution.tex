Count the triangles with no two vertices adjacent. Going around the polygon, the $13$ vertices not chosen split into three gaps, each of at least one vertex: $g_1+g_2+g_3=13$, $g_i\ge1$. Fixing a starting chosen vertex, there are $16$ choices for it and $\dbinom{12}{2}$ gap sequences; each triangle is counted $3$ times (once per vertex as start). So the number is
\[\dfrac{16\cdot\dbinom{12}2}{3}=\dfrac{16\cdot66}{3}=\boxed{352}.\]
