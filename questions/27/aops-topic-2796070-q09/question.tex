Max assigns the ten digits $0$--$9$ to the ten letters $M,A,T,H,W,O,R,K,S,X$, different letters receiving different digits, and no number starting with $0$. He observes
\[\begin{array}{cccccc}&&M&A&T&H\\+&W&O&R&K&S\\\hline &X&2&0&1&2\end{array}\]
where $2,0,1,2$ are the actual digits of the sum. Find $M+A+T+H+W+O+R+K+S$.
