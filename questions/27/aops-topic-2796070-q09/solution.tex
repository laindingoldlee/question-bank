A number is congruent modulo $9$ to its digit sum. The nine letters other than $X$ use all digits except $X$, so their sum is $45-X$. Therefore
\[\overline{MATH}+\overline{WORKS}\equiv 45-X,\qquad \overline{X2012}\equiv X+5\pmod9.\]
So $45-X\equiv X+5$, i.e. $2X\equiv40\equiv4\pmod9$, giving $X\equiv2\pmod9$. As $X$ is a nonzero digit, $X=2$.

Such an assignment exists, e.g. $3045+18967=22012$. The required sum is $45-2=\boxed{43}$.
