Every square has $14$ horizontal or vertical destinations. In either diagonal direction, diagonal lengths are $1,2,\ldots,7,8,7,\ldots,1$. A length-$k$ diagonal contributes $k(k-1)$ ordered pairs of starting and destination squares. Hence the diagonal contribution to the average is $\dfrac{2\left(2\sum_{k=1}^7k(k-1)+8\cdot7\right)}{64}=\dfrac{2(224+56)}{64}=\dfrac{35}{4}$. Adding $14$ gives $\boxed{\dfrac{91}{4}}$.
