Choose $A=(0,0),B=(w,0),C=(w,h),D=(0,h)$, where $w,h>0$, and write $P=tC,Q=sC$. The first perpendicularity gives
\[
(P-D)\cdot C=0,\qquad t=\dfrac{h^2}{w^2+h^2}.
\]
Since $M=\left(w,\dfrac{h}{2}\right)$, the second gives
\[
(D-sC)\cdot(M-sC)
=(s-1)\left((w^2+h^2)s-\dfrac{h^2}{2}\right)=0.
\]
The excluded solution $s=1$ is $Q=C$. Thus $s=\dfrac{h^2}{2(w^2+h^2)}=\dfrac{t}{2}$, exactly the midpoint condition.
