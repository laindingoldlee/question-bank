Let $x_1,x_2,x_3$ be the numbers of students who were late on exactly one, exactly two, and exactly all three days. Counting students and counting late-arrivals,
\[x_1+x_2+x_3=22,\qquad x_1+2x_2+3x_3=15+12+9=36.\]
Subtracting gives $x_2+2x_3=14$, so $x_3\le 7$, with equality only if $x_2=0$ and then $x_1=15$.

The value $x_3=7$ is achievable: let $7$ students be late on all three days, $8$ more only on Monday, $5$ more only on Tuesday, and $2$ more only on Wednesday. Then Monday has $7+8=15$, Tuesday $7+5=12$, Wednesday $7+2=9$ late students, and there are $7+8+5+2=22$ students in all.

Hence the greatest possible number is
\[\boxed{7}.\]
