The product is at most $99^2<10^4$, so it is $99$ or $999$ ($9999=99\cdot101$ needs a three-digit factor). $99$ has no factorisation into two two-digit numbers ($99=9\cdot11=3\cdot33$). And $999=27\cdot37$, the only such pair. The sum is $\boxed{64}$.
