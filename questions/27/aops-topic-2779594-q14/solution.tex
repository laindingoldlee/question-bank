Completing the product gives $(x-16)(y-20)=2336=2^5\cdot73$. Both factors must be positive: if both were negative, their product would be at most $15\cdot19=285$. The positive divisors below $\sqrt{2336}$ are 1,2,4,8,16,32, and the complementary factors are 2336,1168,584,292,146,73. Any pair other than 32 and 73 contains a factor at least 146, making $\max(x,y)>89$. The two orientations of 32 and 73 give $(x,y)=(48,93)$ or $(89,52)$. Thus the minimum is $\boxed{89}$.
