Let $ABC$ be an equilateral triangle, and let $D$, $E$, $F$ be points in the interiors of sides $AB$, $BC$, $CA$ respectively. Prove that the circumcentres of triangles $ADF$, $BED$ and $CFE$ form an equilateral triangle.
