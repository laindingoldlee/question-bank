If the usual travel time is $T$ minutes, doubling the speed saves $\dfrac{T}{2}=7$ minutes, so $T=14$. Let the usual speed be $v$ km/h. The distance equality is $14v=17.5(v-1)$, giving $v=5$. The distance is $5(\dfrac{14}{60})=\dfrac{7}{6}$ km, or $1166.666\ldots$ meters. Rounded to the nearest integer it is $\boxed{1167\text{ m}}$.
