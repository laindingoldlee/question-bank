A number with strictly increasing digits cannot contain $0$ (it could not be the leading digit, and a later $0$ would be smaller than what precedes it). Such a number is determined by its set of digits from $\{1,\dots,9\}$, written in increasing order: $\dbinom91+\dbinom92+\dbinom93=9+36+84=129$ numbers (one-digit numbers have no pair of digits out of order, so they count as increasing). Hence $999-129=\boxed{870}$ numbers are not increasing.
