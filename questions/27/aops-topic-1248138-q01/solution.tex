Put $A=(0,0)$, $B=(s,0)$, $D=(0,s)$, $C=(s,s)$. Then $P=(6,3)$, and $CP=s$:
\[(s-6)^2+(s-3)^2=s^2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad s^2-18s+45=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad s=15\text{ or }s=3.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(15,0),C=(15,15),D=(0,15),P=(6,3);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(1)); draw(arc(C,15,180,270),blue);
draw(C--P,red); dot(P); draw(P--(6,0),dashed); draw(P--(0,3),dashed);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$P$",P,NE);
label("$3$",(6,1.5),E); label("$6$",(3,3),N);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

With $s=3$ the point $(6,3)$ is outside the square, so the side is $\boxed{15}$ inches.
