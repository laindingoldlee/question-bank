One hundred lightbulbs stand in a row, numbered $1$ to $100$ from left to right, all off. For $k=1,2,\dots,100$ in turn, someone flips every $k$th bulb, counting from the left end when $k$ is odd and from the right end when $k$ is even. (So for $k=2$ the bulbs flipped are $99,97,\dots,1$, the 2nd, 4th, ... from the right.) How many bulbs are on at the end?
