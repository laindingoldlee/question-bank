Factor: $6006=2\cdot3\cdot7\cdot11\cdot13$. Since $6006$ itself is not relatively prime to $6006$, we may count among $1,2,\dots,6006$. A number is relatively prime to $6006$ when it is divisible by none of $2,3,7,11,13$, and by inclusion-exclusion (Euler's totient function) the count is
\[6006\left(1-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)\left(1-\dfrac{1}{3}\right)\left(1-\dfrac{1}{7}\right)\left(1-\dfrac{1}{11}\right)\left(1-\dfrac{1}{13}\right)=1\cdot2\cdot6\cdot10\cdot12=\boxed{1440}.\]
