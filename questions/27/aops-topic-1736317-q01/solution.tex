The whole job is $90\cdot20=1800$ man-days. After $5$ days, $90\cdot5=450$ man-days are done and $1350$ remain. To do them in $10$ days requires $\dfrac{1350}{10}=135$ men, that is $135-90=\boxed{45}$ more men.
