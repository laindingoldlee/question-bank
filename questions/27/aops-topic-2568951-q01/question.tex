A problem is posted online. During each second after posting, independently, there is a probability of $\dfrac1{86400}$ that the problem is solved in that second. The poster's madness, in madness units, equals the number of seconds that have passed: if the problem is solved during the $k$th second, the madness at that moment is $k$. What is the expected madness when the problem is solved?
