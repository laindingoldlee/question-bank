For a fixed $y$, the product $xy$ is a linear function of $x$, so on the interval $-9\le x\le3$ its largest and smallest values occur at $x=-9$ or $x=3$; in the same way, for fixed $x$ the extremes occur at $y=-4$ or $y=10$. So the extreme values of $xy$ are among the four corner products:
\[(-9)(-4)=36,\qquad(-9)(10)=-90,\qquad3\cdot(-4)=-12,\qquad3\cdot10=30.\]
The maximum is $36$ and the minimum is $-90$, and the difference is $36-(-90)=\boxed{126}$.
