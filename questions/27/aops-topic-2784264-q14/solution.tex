If the circumradius of $H_1$ is $R$, its inradius is $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}R$. This is the circumradius of $H_2$. Similarity therefore gives $\dfrac{[H_1]}{[H_2]}=\dfrac{R^2}{\dfrac{3R^2}{4}}=\dfrac{4}{3}$, so $\boxed7$.

\end{asy}
