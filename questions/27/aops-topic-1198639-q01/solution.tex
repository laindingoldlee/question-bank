Since $7$ is prime, a nontrivial rotation fixes only the two one-colour spinners. The $2^7=128$ colourings consist of the $2$ constant ones and $126$ others, which fall into orbits of size exactly $7$, giving $\dfrac{126}{7}=18$ classes. The total is $18+2=\boxed{20}$. (By Burnside: $\dfrac{128+6\cdot2}{7}=20$.)
