Every ten-consecutive-integer block contains at least one multiple of $7$ and at most two. There are $\lfloor\dfrac{1000}{7}\rfloor=142$ multiples altogether. If $k$ blocks contain two, the remaining $100-k$ contain one, so $2k+(100-k)=142$. Thus $\boxed{k=42}$.
