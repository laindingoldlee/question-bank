Let there be $N$ pieces. After two days he has eaten $\dfrac{3N}8+50$ and $\dfrac{5N}8-50$ remain, so
\[\dfrac{3N}{8}+50=\dfrac57\left(\dfrac{5N}{8}-50\right).\]
Multiplying by $56$: $21N+2800=25N-2000$, so $N=1200$. The number left is $750-50=\boxed{700}$. (Check: he has eaten $500=\dfrac57\cdot700$.)
