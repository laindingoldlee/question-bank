Staying wins with probability $\dfrac{2}{10}=\dfrac{1}{5}$. Switching uniformly to one of the seven unopened alternatives wins with probability $\dfrac{1}{5}\cdot\dfrac{1}{7}+\dfrac{4}{5}\cdot\dfrac{2}{7}=\dfrac{9}{35}$. Under the stated symmetric host rule the seven alternatives have equal conditional probabilities, so these are the optimal and worst options. Their difference is $\boxed{\dfrac{2}{35}}$.
