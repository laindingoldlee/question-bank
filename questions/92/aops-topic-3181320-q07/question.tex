Two distinct boxes are selected uniformly from ten boxes, and one red ball is placed in each. You choose one box. A host who knows the contents chooses uniformly two empty boxes among the other nine and opens them. You may keep your box or select any unopened alternative. What is the difference between the optimal and worst winning probabilities?
