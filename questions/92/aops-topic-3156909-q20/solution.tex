Write the unique increasing order as $ a,a+d,a+2d $ with $ d\ge1 $. For $ d=1,\ldots,1999 $, the first term has $ 4000-2d $ choices. Hence the count is $\sum_{d=1}^{1999}(4000-2d)=1999\cdot2000=\boxed{3998000}$.
