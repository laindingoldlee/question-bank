Let $C_0$ be a unit circle and $H_0$ the regular hexagon inscribed in $C_0$. For $i\ge0$, let $C_{i+1}$ be the circle inscribed in $H_i$ and $H_{i+1}$ the regular hexagon inscribed in $C_{i+1}$. Find $\operatorname{Area}(C_0)+\operatorname{Area}(C_1)+\operatorname{Area}(C_2)+\cdots$.
