In the central plane the available square is $[-\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{1}{2}]^2$. After dilation the axis-aligned ellipse has coordinate extrema $\pm ta$ and $\pm \dfrac{t}{4}$. It fits exactly when $ta\le\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\dfrac{t}{4}\le\dfrac{1}{2}$. Both are most restrictive at $t=2$, giving $\boxed{0<a\le\dfrac{1}{4}}$. There are infinitely many such values.
