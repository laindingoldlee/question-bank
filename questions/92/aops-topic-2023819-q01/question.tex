Forty teams play a tournament in which every team plays every other team exactly once. No ties occur, and each team has a $50\%$ chance of winning any game it plays. The probability that no two teams win the same number of games is $\dfrac {m}{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $\log_2 n$.
