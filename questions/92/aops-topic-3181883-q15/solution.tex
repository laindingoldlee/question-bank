Any two omitted houses must be at least four positions apart. If there are $k$ omissions at positions $x_1<\cdots<x_k$, replace $x_i$ by $x_i-3(i-1)$. This gives an arbitrary $k$-subset of $\{1,\ldots,20-3k\}$. Thus the answer is $\sum_{k=0}^{5}\binom{20-3k}{k}=1+17+91+165+70+1=\boxed{345}$.
