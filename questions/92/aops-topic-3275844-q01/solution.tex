The second block is $r^5$ times the first, so $r^5=-\dfrac{1}{40}$ and $\boxed{r=-40^{-\dfrac{1}{5}}}$. This has magnitude less than one. From $55=a\dfrac{1-r^5}{1-r}$, the first term is $\boxed{a=\dfrac{2200}{41}(1+40^{-\dfrac{1}{5}})}$. The infinite sum is $\dfrac{a}{1-r}=\boxed{\dfrac{2200}{41}}$.
