Pythagoras gives $BC=\sqrt{9^2+12^2}=15$. Comparing the two area formulas gives $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot9\cdot12=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot15\cdot AH$, hence $\boxed{AH=\dfrac{36}{5}}$. The right triangles $ABH$ and $CBA$ are similar, so $AB^2=BH\cdot BC$, giving $\boxed{BH=\dfrac{27}{5}}$. Subtracting from $15$ gives $\boxed{CH=\dfrac{48}{5}}$.
