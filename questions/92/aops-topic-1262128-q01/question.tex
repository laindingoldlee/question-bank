A chicken at $(16,0)$ runs to $(0,8)$, then to $(-4,0)$, then to $(0,-2)$, then to $(1,0)$, and so on, spiralling towards the origin: each time it touches the next axis (in the order positive $x$, positive $y$, negative $x$, negative $y$, \dots) at a point half as far from the origin as the previous one. It runs in straight lines at $2$ units per minute. How many minutes pass before it reaches the origin?
