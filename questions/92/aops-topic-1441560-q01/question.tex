Triangles $ABC$ and $DEF$ are similar, with $A\leftrightarrow D$, $B\leftrightarrow E$, $C\leftrightarrow F$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Which corresponding parts must be congruent? Give an example showing that corresponding sides need not be congruent (so ``corresponding parts of congruent triangles are congruent'' cannot be used for sides of similar triangles).
\item[(b)] Prove that if, in addition, $AB=DE$, then the triangles are congruent.
\end{enumerate}
