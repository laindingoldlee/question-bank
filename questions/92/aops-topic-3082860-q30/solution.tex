Person 1 gets B with probability $\dfrac{1}{6}$. Conditional on that, person 3 has five equally likely remaining items, of which D is one. Hence the probability is $\dfrac{1}{6}\cdot\dfrac{1}{5}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{30}}$.
