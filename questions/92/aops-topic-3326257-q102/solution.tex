The matchup win probability is $p=\binom32(\dfrac{2}{3})^2\dfrac{1}{3}+(\dfrac{2}{3})^3=\dfrac{20}{27}$, and $q=\dfrac{7}{27}$. Enumerating the four-result sequences ending at 2 gives one with two wins, LLWW, and three with three wins, WLWW, WWLW, WWWL. Their total probability is $p^2q^2+3p^3q=\boxed{\dfrac{187600}{531441}}$.
