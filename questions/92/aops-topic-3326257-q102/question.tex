Bob starts at level 4 of a four-level league. A matchup consists of three independent games, each won with probability $\dfrac{2}{3}$; winning at least two wins the matchup. A matchup win moves him one level toward 1, and a loss one toward 4, with reflection by staying at an endpoint. Find the probability he is at level 2 after four matchups.
