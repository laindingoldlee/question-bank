Consider the increasing arithmetic sequence of all positive integers that leave remainder $2$ when divided by $3$, remainder $1$ when divided by $4$, and remainder $3$ when divided by $5$. Apart from the first term itself, what is the smallest term of the sequence that is divisible by the first term?
