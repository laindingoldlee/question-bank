Numbers $\equiv3\pmod5$ that are $\equiv1\pmod4$ are $13,33,53,\dots$, i.e. $\equiv13\pmod{20}$. Among $13,33,53$ only $53$ is $\equiv2\pmod3$. By the Chinese remainder theorem the sequence is $53,113,173,\dots$, i.e. $53+60k$ for $k\ge0$.

We need $53\mid53+60k$ with $k\ge1$, i.e. $53\mid60k$. Since $53$ is prime and does not divide $60$, this means $53\mid k$, so the smallest is $k=53$, giving $53+60\cdot53=53\cdot61=\boxed{3233}$.
