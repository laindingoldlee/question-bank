Positivity ensures every denominator is nonzero. Starting with two consecutive terms $x,y$, direct calculation gives the next five terms:
\[
\dfrac{y+1}{x},\quad
\dfrac{x+y+1}{xy},\quad
\dfrac{x+1}{y},\quad x,\quad y.
\]
For example, the fifth term follows because
\[
\dfrac{\dfrac{(x+y+1)}{(xy)}+1}{\dfrac{(y+1)}{x}}
=\dfrac{(x+1)(y+1)}{xy}\dfrac{x}{y+1}
=\dfrac{x+1}{y}.
\]
The ordered pair of consecutive terms repeats after five steps. Since the recurrence determines all subsequent terms, $a_{n+5}=a_n$ for all $n$.
The period need not be exactly five: a constant sequence is possible when its value is the positive root of $x^2=x+1$.
