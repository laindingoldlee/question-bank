The radius to the tangency point is perpendicular to the line, whose slope is $-1$. Its line through the centre therefore has slope 1 and equation $y=x$. Intersecting with $x+y=4$ gives $\boxed{(2,2)}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw(circle((3,3),sqrt(2)));draw((0,4)--(4,0));draw((3,3)--(2,2),blue);dot((2,2));label("$(3,3)$",(3,3),N);label("$(2,2)$",(2,2),SW);
\end{asy}
