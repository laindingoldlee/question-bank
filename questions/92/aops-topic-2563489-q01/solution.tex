Adding the first and third equations gives $x+y+z+w=360$, and adding the second and fourth gives the same. So the equations are dependent: only three are independent, and there is a one-parameter family of solutions. With $x=t$:
\[\boxed{(x,y,z,w)=(t,\ 120-t,\ 112+t,\ 128-t),\quad t\in\mathbb R.}\]
(Check: $y+z=232$ and $w+z=240$.) The individual unknowns are not determined, but $x+y+z+w=360$ is.
