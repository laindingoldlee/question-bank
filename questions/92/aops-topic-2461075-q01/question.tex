In 1914 Ramanujan noted the approximation
\[\pi\approx r=\sqrt[4]{9^2+\dfrac{19^2}{22}}.\]
On a calculator, $r$ divided by $\pi$ is displayed as $1$, which led a student to believe that $r=\pi$ exactly.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Write $9^2+\dfrac{19^2}{22}$ as a fraction in lowest terms.
\item[(b)] Given that $3.1415926535<\pi<3.1415926536$, prove that $r<\pi$, so the approximation is not exact.
\item[(c)] Prove that $0<\pi-r<1.3\cdot10^{-9}$, and explain why a calculator that rounds its results to $9$ significant digits displays $\dfrac r\pi$ as $1$.
\end{enumerate}
