There are $15$ people in an office, and two committees have to be formed. The first committee will have $6$ people, with $1$ person named chair and $2$ people named co-secretaries. The second committee will have $9$ people, with $3$ named co-chairs, $1$ secretary and $2$ co-treasurers. No person can be on both committees and no person can have more than one role within a committee (the members without a named role are ordinary members). How many ways are there to form these two committees?
