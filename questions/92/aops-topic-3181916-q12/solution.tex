For the 96 values coprime to 29, Euler's theorem gives remainder 1. The remaining moduli are 29, 58, 87. Their totients are 28, 28, 56, respectively. The corresponding remainders are 0, 29, 58: powers of 29 modulo 58 are always 29, while modulo 87 they alternate 29, 58. Therefore the sum is $96+0+29+58=\boxed{183}$.
