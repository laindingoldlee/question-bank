Write $p_j=\sum_{k=1}^7k^j$, so $p_1=28$, $p_2=140$, and $p_3=784$. Expanding $p_1^3$ groups ordered terms with three distinct indices, two equal indices, and all equal indices. Thus $6\sum_{a<b<c}abc=p_1^3-3p_1p_2+2p_3=11760$. The requested sum is $\boxed{1960}$.
