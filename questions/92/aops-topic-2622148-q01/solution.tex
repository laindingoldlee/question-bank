Put $a=f(2)$ and $b=f\left(-\dfrac12\right)$. Substituting $x=2$ and $x=-\dfrac12$:
\[a+\dfrac12b=3,\qquad b-2a=3.\]
From the second, $b=2a+3$; then $a+a+\dfrac32=3$, so $f(2)=a=\boxed{\dfrac34}$.

(Such a function exists: solving the same pair of equations for general $x$ gives $f(x)=\dfrac{3(x-1)}{2x}$, which satisfies the equation.)
