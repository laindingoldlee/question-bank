Let $f(x)$ be a polynomial with integer coefficients. Prove that $f(x)$ has the same parity for all even integers $x$, and the same parity for all odd integers $x$. More precisely, show that $f(x)$ has the parity of $f(0)$ when $x$ is even and the parity of $f(1)$ when $x$ is odd.
