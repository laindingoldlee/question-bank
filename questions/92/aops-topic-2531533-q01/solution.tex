First multiply the two bases:
\[\left(2x+\dfrac{1}{x^2}\right)\left(x^2+\dfrac{1}{2x}\right)=2x^3+1+1+\dfrac{1}{2x^3}=\dfrac{4x^6+4x^3+1}{2x^3}=\dfrac{\left(2x^3+1\right)^2}{2x^3}.\]
So the expression equals $\dfrac{\left(2x^3+1\right)^6}{8x^9}$, and we need the coefficient of $x^{15}=\left(x^3\right)^5$ in $\left(2x^3+1\right)^6$, divided by $8$:
\[\dfrac{1}{8}\dbinom65\,2^5=\dfrac{6\cdot32}{8}=\boxed{24}.\]
