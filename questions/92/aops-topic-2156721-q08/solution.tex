Since $80=2^4\cdot5$, an integer is relatively prime to $80$ exactly when it is divisible by neither $2$ nor $5$. Among $1,\dots,80$ there are $40$ multiples of $2$, $16$ multiples of $5$ and $8$ multiples of $10$, so $40+16-8=48$ are divisible by $2$ or $5$, and the answer is $80-48=\boxed{32}$. (This is $\varphi(80)=80\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{4}{5}=32$.)
