The square contributes $4^2=16$. The eight semicircles each have radius one, so together they contribute $8\cdot\dfrac{\pi}{2}=4\pi$. These regions have disjoint interiors. With the prescribed $\pi=3$, the total is $16+12=\boxed{28}$.
