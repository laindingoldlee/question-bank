There are 900 three-digit integers. Multiples of 3 number $ 333-33=300 $, multiples of 5 number $ 199-19=180 $, and multiples of 15 number $ 66-6=60 $. Inclusion--exclusion gives $ 900-300-180+60=\boxed{480}$.
