The people eat at least $1+\cdots+13=91$ slices, so $5x\ge91$ and $x\ge19$. This is attainable by assigning the five pizzas the slice-count groups $(13,6),(12,7),(11,8),(10,9),(1,2,3,4,5)$. Their sums are 19, 19, 19, 19, 15. Thus $\boxed{19}$.
