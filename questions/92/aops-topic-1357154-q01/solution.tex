Every square is $0$ or $1$ modulo $4$ (even squares are multiples of $4$, and $(2k+1)^2=4k^2+4k+1$). So $x^2+y^2$ is $0$, $1$ or $2$ modulo $4$. But $2015=4\cdot503+3\equiv3\pmod4$. Hence there are $\boxed{0}$ integer solutions.
