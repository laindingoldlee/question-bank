Let $x_1$, $x_2$, $x_3$ be the numbers of students taking exactly one, two and three subjects. Counting students and subject enrolments,
\[x_1+x_2+x_3=500,\qquad x_1+2x_2+3x_3=380+420+350=1150 .\]
Subtracting, $x_2+2x_3=650$, and then $x_1=500-x_2-x_3=500-(650-2x_3)-x_3=x_3-150$. Since $x_1\ge0$, we get $x_3\ge150$.

The value $150$ is possible. Take $x_3=150$, $x_1=0$, and let $p$, $q$, $r$ students take exactly mathematics and science, mathematics and English, science and English. We need
\[150+p+q=380,\qquad150+p+r=420,\qquad150+q+r=350,\]
which gives $p=150$, $q=80$, $r=120$ (and $p+q+r=350=x_2$). So the least possible number is $\boxed{150}$.
