In a lottery each of $50$ states starts with a jackpot of $100$ million dollars. Each week there is one drawing with one winner in one state. The winner receives that state's current jackpot, that state's jackpot resets to $100$ million dollars, and every other state's jackpot increases by $1$ million dollars. After the $100$th drawing the average of the $50$ jackpots is $144.88$ million dollars. In total, how many dollars have been awarded in the first $100$ drawings?
