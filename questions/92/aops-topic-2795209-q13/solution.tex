Work in millions. Let $J$ be the jackpot won in a drawing. The total of all jackpots changes by $-J+100+49\cdot1=149-J$.

Initially the total is $50\cdot100=5000$; at the end it is $50\cdot144.88=7244$. Summing over the $100$ drawings,
\[7244=5000+100\cdot149-\sum J,\qquad\text{so}\qquad \sum J=19900-7244=12656.\]
The total awarded is $12656$ million, i.e. $\boxed{12{,}656{,}000{,}000}$ dollars.
