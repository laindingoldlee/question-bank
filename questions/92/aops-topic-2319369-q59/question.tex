A tray of blueberry muffins takes $4$ cups of milk and $3$ cups of flour; a tray of pumpkin muffins takes $2$ cups of milk and $3$ cups of flour. A baker has $16$ cups of milk and $15$ cups of flour. The profit is $3$ dollars per tray of blueberry muffins and $2$ dollars per tray of pumpkin muffins. How many trays of each should be made to maximise the profit?
