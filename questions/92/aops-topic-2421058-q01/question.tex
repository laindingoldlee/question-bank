A cube of side length $n$, where $n$ is a positive integer, has its whole surface painted red and is then cut into $n^3$ unit cubes. One unit cube is chosen at random, and it is rolled so that each of its six faces is equally likely to end up on top. Prove that the probability that the top face is red is $\dfrac1n$.
