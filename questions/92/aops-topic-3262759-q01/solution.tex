(a) At most one head occurs in $\binom40+\binom41=5$ of $16$ outcomes, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{11}{16}}$.
(b) Sums $8,9,10,11,12$ have respectively $5,4,3,2,1$ ordered outcomes, so the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{15}{36}=\dfrac{5}{12}}$.
