Let the quantities be $x$, $y$, $y$ kg. Then
\[1.20x+1.44y+1.74y=1.41(x+2y)\ \Longrightarrow\ 0.21x=0.36y\ \Longrightarrow\ 7x=12y.\]
So the ratio is $x:y:y=\boxed{12:7:7}$. (Check: $\dfrac{14.40+10.08+12.18}{26}=\dfrac{36.66}{26}=1.41$.)

Without the extra condition the general condition is $0.21x_1=0.03x_2+0.33x_3$, i.e. $7x_1=x_2+11x_3$, which has infinitely many solutions in ratio.
