If the current suffix is OO, OOP occurs before POP with probability one, since the next P completes OOP. Let $p_P,p_{PO},p_O$ be the desired probabilities with the indicated relevant suffixes. From PO, the next P wins and the next O gives OO, so $p_{PO}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. From P, another P leaves the state unchanged and O gives PO: $p_P=\dfrac{1}{2}p_P+\dfrac{1}{2}p_{PO}$, so $p_P=\dfrac{1}{2}$. From O, O loses eventually and P gives suffix P, so $p_O=\dfrac{1}{2}p_P=\dfrac{1}{4}$. The first letter gives either O or P equally, hence the answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}(\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{2})=\dfrac{3}{8}}$.
