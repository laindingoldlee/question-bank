The run times in minutes are $10,10,20,40,80,160,\dots$, and the total is $5\cdot60+20=320$ minutes. Since $10+10+20+40+80+160=320$, she makes $6$ runs.

The distances are $1.389$ times $1,1,2,4,8,16$, i.e. $1.389\times32$ miles in total:
\[1.389\times32=\boxed{44.448}\text{ miles}.\]
