$i^k+i^{-k}=2\cos\dfrac{k\pi}2$, which is $0$ for odd $k$, $-2$ for $k\equiv2\pmod4$ and $2$ for $k\equiv0\pmod4$. For $k=1,\dots,10$ these sum to $-2+2-2+2-2=-2$. Adding $i^0=1$ gives $\boxed{-1}$.
