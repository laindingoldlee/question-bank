Choose the number that stays fixed in $8$ ways; the other $7$ numbers must then form a derangement (no number in its original place). The number of derangements of $7$ objects, by inclusion-exclusion, is
\[
D_7=7!\left(1-\dfrac{1}{1!}+\dfrac{1}{2!}-\dfrac{1}{3!}+\dfrac{1}{4!}-\dfrac{1}{5!}+\dfrac{1}{6!}-\dfrac{1}{7!}\right)=5040-5040+2520-840+210-42+7-1=1854 .
\]
The answer is $8\cdot1854=\boxed{14832}$.
