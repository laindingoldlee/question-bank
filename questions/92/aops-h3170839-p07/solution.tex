Sophie Germain's factorization gives
\[
(a^2-2ab+2b^2)(a^2+2ab+2b^2)=61\cdot281.
\]
The two factors are positive, and the second is larger. Both $61$ and $281$ are prime (trial division by primes up to their square roots suffices). The smaller factor $(a-b)^2+b^2$ cannot be $1$: that would force $(a,b)=(1,1)$ and product $5$. Hence the factors are $61,281$. Their difference gives $4ab=220$, so $ab=55$; their sum gives $a^2+2b^2=171$.
Testing the four ordered factor pairs of $55$, namely $(1,55),(5,11),(11,5),(55,1)$, leaves only $\boxed{(a,b)=(11,5)}$, which directly verifies the equation.
