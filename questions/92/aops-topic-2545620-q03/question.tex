In the sequence
\[2119133711,\qquad11937771,\qquad133333333377777771111111,\qquad\dots\]
each term is obtained by reading the previous term in pairs of digits, a pair $kd$ meaning ``$k$ copies of the digit $d$''. For example, $2119133711=21\,|\,19\,|\,13\,|\,37\,|\,11$ gives $11\,9\,3\,777\,1=11937771$. Check the second step and describe the next term.
