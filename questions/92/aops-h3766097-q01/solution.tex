In one diagonal direction there are eight odd-length diagonals, each requiring an empty cell. All these diagonals lie on one checkerboard colour. In the other direction there are eight odd-length diagonals on the opposite colour, also requiring eight empty cells. Thus at least sixteen cells are empty, giving at most $48$ tokens. For equality, leave both longest diagonals empty and fill every other cell. Each row and column loses two cells. Each short odd-length diagonal loses one cell, every other short even-length diagonal loses none, and each longest diagonal loses all eight. All line counts are even, so the maximum is $\boxed{48}$.
