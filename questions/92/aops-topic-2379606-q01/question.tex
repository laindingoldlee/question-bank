Five houses in a row, numbered $1$ to $5$ from left to right, are each a different colour. In each lives a person of a different nationality; each person drinks a different drink, smokes a different brand and keeps a different pet. The colours are red, green, white, yellow and blue; the nationalities are Brit, Swede, Dane, Norwegian and German; the drinks are tea, coffee, milk, beer and water; the brands are Pall Mall, Dunhill, Blends, BlueMaster and Prince; the pets are dogs, birds, cats, horses and fish.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The Brit lives in the red house.
\item The Swede keeps dogs.
\item The Dane drinks tea.
\item The green house is immediately to the left of the white house.
\item The owner of the green house drinks coffee.
\item The Pall Mall smoker keeps birds.
\item The owner of the yellow house smokes Dunhill.
\item The person in the centre house drinks milk.
\item The Norwegian lives in the first house.
\item The Blends smoker lives next to the one who keeps cats.
\item The one who keeps horses lives next to the Dunhill smoker.
\item The BlueMaster smoker drinks beer.
\item The German smokes Prince.
\item The Norwegian lives next to the blue house.
\item The Blends smoker has a neighbour who drinks water.
\end{enumerate}
Who keeps the fish? Determine all the attributes.
