The set has $10$ letters, of which $4$ are vowels ($A$, $O$, $U$, $E$) and $6$ are consonants. It has $2^{10}=1024$ subsets, and the subsets with no vowel are exactly the subsets of the $6$ consonants, of which there are $2^6=64$ (including the empty set). By complementary counting,
\[
1024-64=\boxed{960}
\]
subsets contain at least one vowel.
