Triangle $ABC$ has a right angle at $A$, and two of its sides have lengths $2\sqrt3$ and $3$. The point $A$ is rotated about $B$ to a point $A'$ such that $\angle A'BC=90^\circ$. Find the least possible length of $AA'$, over all such triangles and all such rotations.
