Write $z=a+bi$ with $a,b$ real. Then $z^2=a^2-b^2+2abi$, so
\[a^2-b^2=-4,\qquad 2ab=3 .\]
A quick extra equation comes from absolute values: $|z|^2=|z^2|=\left|-4+3i\right|=5$, so $a^2+b^2=5$. Adding and subtracting, $a^2=\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $b^2=\dfrac{9}{2}$. Since $2ab=3>0$, $a$ and $b$ have the same sign. Hence
\[z=\pm\left(\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}+\dfrac{3\sqrt2}{2}i\right)=\boxed{\pm\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}(1+3i)}.\]
Check: $\left(\dfrac{1+3i}{\sqrt2}\right)^2=\dfrac{1+6i-9}{2}=-4+3i$.
