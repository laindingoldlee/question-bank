The units digits of $3^1,3^2,3^3,3^4,\dots$ repeat with period $4$: $3,9,7,1,\dots$. Since $450=4\cdot112+2$, the units digit of $3^{450}$ is the same as that of $3^2$, so $d=9$. Then $2^9=512$, whose units digit is $\boxed{2}$.
