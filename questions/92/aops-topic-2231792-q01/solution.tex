Work modulo $5$. Since $14\equiv-1$,
\[14^{92}\equiv(-1)^{92}=1 .\]
Since $17\equiv2$ and $2^4=16\equiv1$,
\[17^{76}\equiv2^{76}=\left(2^4\right)^{19}\equiv1 .\]
So $14^{92}\cdot17^{76}\equiv1\cdot1=1$, and the remainder is $\boxed{1}$.
