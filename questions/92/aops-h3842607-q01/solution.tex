First remove the factor $7$: $777777777=7(111111111)$. Long division of the nine-digit repunit by $9$ gives
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{111111111}{9}=12345679}.
\]
The final carry explains the ending: $9(12345670)=111111030$, leaving $81$. This requires a final quotient digit $9$, not $8$. Direct multiplication verifies $9(12345679)=111111111$. The observed pattern is an exact consequence of base-ten division.
