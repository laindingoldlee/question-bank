Apollonius\textquotesingle s median formula gives $BM^2=\dfrac{2\cdot25+2\cdot9-36}{4}=8$, so $BM=2\sqrt2$. Intersecting chords at $M$ give $BM\cdot ME=AM\cdot MC=9$. Hence $ME=\dfrac{9\sqrt2}{4}$, and in the usual reduced radical convention $\boxed{p+q+r=15}$.

\end{asy}
