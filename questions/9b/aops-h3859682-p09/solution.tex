Let $F_j(r)$ count button strings of length $j$ ending at residue $r$ modulo $100$. Initialize $F_0(32)=1$, with all other entries zero. For each residue $r$, add $F_j(r)$ to each of $F_{j+1}(2r\bmod100),F_{j+1}(3r\bmod100),F_{j+1}(7r\bmod100)$. This counts each string exactly once by its final button. Equivalently, enumerate nonnegative $a+b+c=9$ with $32\cdot2^a3^b7^c\equiv24\pmod{100}$ and sum $\dfrac{9!}{a!b!c!}$. The admissible triples $(a,b,c)$ are $(0,0,9),(1,2,6),(2,4,3),(3,6,0),(5,0,4),(6,2,1)$, contributing $1,252,1260,84,126,252$, respectively. Their sum gives $\boxed{1975}$.
