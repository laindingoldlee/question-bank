Let $S$ be the sum, so $S=\displaystyle\sum_{k\ge3}(k-1)3^{-k}$. Then
\[S-\dfrac S3=\dfrac{2}{27}+\dfrac{1}{81}+\dfrac{1}{243}+\cdots=\dfrac2{27}+\dfrac{\dfrac1{81}}{1-\dfrac13}=\dfrac2{27}+\dfrac1{54}=\dfrac5{54}.\]
So $\dfrac23S=\dfrac5{54}$ and $S=\boxed{\dfrac5{36}}$.
