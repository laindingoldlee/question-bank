First sum over $0\le k<360$. Expand $\tau(\gcd(k,360))$ as the number of divisors $d$ of $360$ that divide $k$, and interchange the finite sums. For each $d\mid360$ the inner sum is $\sum_{j=0}^{\dfrac{360}{d} - 1} \cos (jd^\circ)$. The sum of equally spaced roots of unity gives zero unless $d=360$, in which case it is $1$. Thus the first $360$ terms sum to $1$. The last term is $\tau(360)=(3+1)(2+1)(1+1)=24$. The total is $\boxed{25}$.
