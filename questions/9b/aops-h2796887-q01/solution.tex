Take the lines to be $y=0,3,6$ and set $A=(0,0),B=(u,6)$. A perpendicular equal-length side vector is either $(-6,u)$ or $(6,-u)$. Thus the height of $C$ is $6+u$ or $6-u$, respectively. Requiring height 3 gives $u=-3$ or $u=3$. In either case the square's area is $AB^2=u^2+36=\boxed{45}$. The two configurations are mirror images.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(3,6),C=(9,3),D=(6,-3);for(int k=0;k<3;++k){draw((-1,3*k)--(10,3*k),gray+dashed);label("$L_"+string(k+1)+"$",(10,3*k),(1,0));}draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);label("$A$",A,(-1,-1));label("$B$",B,(0,1));label("$C$",C,(1,-1));label("$D$",D,(0,-1));
\end{asy}
