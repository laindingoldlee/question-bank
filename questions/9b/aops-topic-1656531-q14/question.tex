For a square matrix $A$, the trace $\operatorname{Tr}(A)$ is the sum of its diagonal entries. Let $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\2&1\end{pmatrix}$. What is the remainder when $\operatorname{Tr}\left(A^{2018}\right)$ is divided by $100$?

(a) $22$\quad (b) $28$\quad (c) $68$\quad (d) $88$\quad (e) $90$
