\textbf{A formula for $A^n$.} By symmetry $A^n=\begin{pmatrix}a_n&b_n\\b_n&a_n\end{pmatrix}$ (true for $n=1$, and the product of two such matrices has the same form). Applying $A$ to the vectors $(1,1)$ and $(1,-1)$:
\[
A\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix}=3\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix},\qquad A\begin{pmatrix}1\\-1\end{pmatrix}=-\begin{pmatrix}1\\-1\end{pmatrix},
\]
so $a_n+b_n=3^n$ and $a_n-b_n=(-1)^n$. Hence $\operatorname{Tr}\left(A^n\right)=2a_n=3^n+(-1)^n$, and $\operatorname{Tr}\left(A^{2018}\right)=3^{2018}+1$.

\textbf{Modulo $100$.} $3^{10}=59049\equiv49$, so $3^{20}\equiv49^2=2401\equiv1\pmod{100}$. As $2018=20\cdot100+18$, $3^{2018}\equiv3^{18}$, and $3^{18}\equiv89$ because $9\cdot89=801\equiv1$ and $3^{18}\cdot9=3^{20}\equiv1$. So the trace is $\equiv89+1=\boxed{\textbf{(e)}\ 90}\pmod{100}$.
