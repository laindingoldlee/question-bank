Place the centroid at the origin. Let the corresponding vertex vectors be $u,v$, so $u\cdot v=0$ and the third vertex is $-u-v$. The two given squared side lengths are $|u+2v|^2=|u|^2+4|v|^2$ and $|2u+v|^2=4|u|^2+|v|^2$. Their sum is five times $|u|^2+|v|^2=|u-v|^2$, the third squared side. Hence the answer is $\boxed{\sqrt{\dfrac{56^2+33^2}{5}}=13\sqrt5}$.
