The tangent-secant theorem gives $BD\cdot BE=BF\cdot BG=AB^2=484$. Hence $BE=\dfrac{484}{9}$ and BG=121. Triangles BDG and BFE have the same included angle at B and satisfy $\dfrac{BD}{BF}=\dfrac{9}{4}=\dfrac{BG}{BE}$. They are similar by side-angle-side, so the ratio of their third sides is $\boxed{\dfrac{DG}{EF}=\dfrac{9}{4}}$.
