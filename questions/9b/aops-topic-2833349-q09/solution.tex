The volume factor is 2.16. Dividing by the height factor 1.5 gives cross-sectional area factor 1.44, so the diameter factor is $\sqrt{1.44}=1.2 $. The new diameter is 6 cm, exceeding 5 by $\boxed1 $ cm.
