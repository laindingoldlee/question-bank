Write the integers in order as $a\le4\le c$, with $4$ in the middle because it is the median. The mean is $12$, so $a+4+c=36$ and $c=32-a$. Since $a\le4$, $c\ge28$, with equality when $a=4$. The numbers $4,4,28$ have median $4$ and mean $\dfrac{36}{3}=12$, so the smallest possible largest integer is $\boxed{28}$.

The numbers $15,4,17$ have mean $12$, but their median is not $4$: in order they are $4,15,17$, and the median is the middle number $15$. The median is found after sorting, not by looking at the middle of the list as written.
