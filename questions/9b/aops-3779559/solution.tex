Complete the square: $(x-5)^2-y^2=21$, so $(x-5-y)(x-5+y)=21$. The ordered factor pairs of $21$ are $(1,21),(3,7),(7,3),(21,1)$ and their negatives. Recover $x=5+\dfrac{u+v}{2}$ and $y=\dfrac{v-u}{2}$. This gives $\boxed{(16,\pm10),(10,\pm2),(0,\pm2),(-6,\pm10)}$. Every factor pair has odd entries, hence all these coordinates are integral and the enumeration is complete.
