Dad saved $6$ minutes of driving, $3$ minutes each way, so he met Constantine $3$ minutes before he would usually have reached the school, i.e. $3$ minutes before the usual end of the lesson. Constantine had been walking $14$ minutes, so the lesson ended $14+3=\boxed{17}$ minutes early.
