A cyclic kite has opposite equal angles at its nonsymmetric vertices; since they are supplementary, both are right angles. Its symmetry diagonal is therefore a diameter. Kite area gives $AC\cdot \dfrac{BD}{2}=48$, so $AC=16$. Since $BD=6$ cannot be a diameter while the other diagonal is 16, $AC$ is the diameter. The circle radius is eight, giving area $\boxed{64\pi}$.
