The left side is $2x$, and $x^2+1>0$, so multiplying by $x^2+1$ gives
\[2x\left(x^2+1\right)=4x\iff 2x\left(x^2-1\right)=0\iff 2x(x-1)(x+1)=0 .\]
So $x=\boxed{-1,\ 0\text{ or }1}$, and all three check.
