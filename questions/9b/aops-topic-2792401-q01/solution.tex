Only the sum of the numbers starting with 6 and 7 can reach 148; their maximum is $69+79=148$, so they must be 69 and 79. If the remaining number is $a$, requiring $a+69\ge120$ gives $a\ge51$. But then $a+79\ge130$, so it cannot be in 120 through 129. Thus $\boxed{\text{no}}$.
