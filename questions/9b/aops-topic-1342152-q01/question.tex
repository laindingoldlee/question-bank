Some positive integers have both of these properties:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] the sum of the squares of their digits is $50$, and
\item[(ii)] each digit is larger than the one to its left.
\end{enumerate}
What is the product of the digits of the largest integer with both properties?
