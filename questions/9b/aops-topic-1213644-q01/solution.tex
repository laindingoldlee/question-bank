Increasing a number by $130\%$ adds $130\%$ of the number to the number itself, so the result is $100\%+130\%=230\%$ of the original:
\[
27\cdot2.3=62.1 .
\]
(Check: $130\%$ of $27$ is $1.3\cdot27=35.1$, and $27+35.1=62.1$.) The answer is $\boxed{62.1}$.
