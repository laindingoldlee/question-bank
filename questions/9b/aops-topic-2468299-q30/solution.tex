$2020=2^2\cdot5\cdot101$ and $2020^2=2^4\cdot5^2\cdot101^2$. The exponents of each prime can be chosen independently.
\begin{itemize}
\item Prime $2$: exponents in $\{2,3,4\}$ with minimum $2$ and maximum $4$: by inclusion--exclusion $3^3-2\cdot2^3+1=12$.
\item Primes $5$ and $101$: exponents in $\{1,2\}$ with minimum $1$ and maximum $2$: $2^3-2=6$ each.
\end{itemize}
The total is $12\cdot6\cdot6=\boxed{432}$.
