If $A$ is the vertex angle, the base angles are 75 degrees. Otherwise $A$ is a base angle, giving the remaining angles 30 and 120 degrees. Thus the maximum is $\boxed{120^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),C=(2,0),B=(1,1/sqrt(3));draw(A--B--C--cycle);label("$A:30^\circ$",A,SW);label("$B:120^\circ$",B,N);label("$C:30^\circ$",C,SE);
\end{asy}
