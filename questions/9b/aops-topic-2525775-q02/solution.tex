Multiply out:
\[(a+b+c)\left(\dfrac1a+\dfrac1b+\dfrac1c\right)=3+\left(\dfrac ab+\dfrac ba\right)+\left(\dfrac bc+\dfrac cb\right)+\left(\dfrac ca+\dfrac ac\right).\]
For positive $x,y$, $\dfrac xy+\dfrac yx-2=\dfrac{(x-y)^2}{xy}\ge0$ (this is AM-GM for $\dfrac xy$ and $\dfrac yx$). So the product is at least $3+2+2+2=9$. Dividing by $a+b+c>0$ gives the inequality, with equality exactly when $a=b=c$. $\blacksquare$
