Both quadrilaterals are trapezoids with the parallel vertical sides on the lines $x=0$ and $x=4$, at distance $4$ apart.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0),A=(0,3),B=(4,4),D=(4,0),C=(4,8.5);
fill(O--A--B--D--cycle,palegray);
draw(O--A--C--D--cycle,linewidth(1));draw(A--B,dashed);
draw((-0.8,0)--(5.5,0),gray,Arrow(TeXHead));draw((0,-0.8)--(0,9.5),gray,Arrow(TeXHead));
dot(O);dot(A);dot(B);dot(C);dot(D);
label("$O(0,0)$",O,SW);label("$A(0,3)$",A,W);label("$B(4,4)$",B,E);label("$D(4,0)$",D,SE);label("$C(4,y)$",C,E);
label("(not to scale)",(2.8,9.6),fontsize(8pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

For $ABDO$ the parallel sides are $OA=3$ and $DB=4$, so
\[
[ABDO]=\dfrac{3+4}{2}\cdot4=14 .
\]
For $ACDO$ the parallel sides are $OA=3$ and $DC=y$, so $[ACDO]=\dfrac{3+y}{2}\cdot4=2(3+y)$. The condition gives
\[
2(3+y)=5\cdot14=70\quad\Longrightarrow\quad y=\boxed{32}.
\]
