Each rounded value is more than $\dfrac{1}{2}$ below the original at worst: under half-up rounding, an exact half rounds upward. A total decrease of $3$ therefore requires more than six entries, so at least seven. Seven suffice: use six percentages of $14.49\%$ and one of $13.06\%$. They total $100\%$, while the rounded values sum to $6\cdot14+13=97$. The answer is $\boxed7$.
