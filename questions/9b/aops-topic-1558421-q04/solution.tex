The last congruence says $N\equiv0\pmod5$, i.e.\ $5\mid N$. The numbers $\equiv1\pmod4$ are $1,5,9,\dots$, and $5$ is already $\equiv2\pmod3$ and divisible by $5$. Since $3$, $4$, $5$ are pairwise coprime, the Chinese remainder theorem says the solutions form one class modulo $60$:
\[
\boxed{N\equiv5\pmod{60}}.
\]
