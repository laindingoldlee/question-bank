Triangle $ABC$ has a right angle at $C$, with $AC=8$ and $BC=6$ (so $AB=10$). Points $Q$ on side $CA$ and $P$ on side $CB$ satisfy $CQ=CP=2$. Segments $AP$ and $BQ$ meet at $R$, and line $CR$ meets side $AB$ at $S$. Lines $AB$ and $QP$ meet at $T$. Find $TS$.
