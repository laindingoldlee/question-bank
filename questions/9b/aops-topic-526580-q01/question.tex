In American football a team can score $2$ points (a safety), $3$ points (a field goal) or $6$ points (a touchdown); after a touchdown it may score $1$ additional point. So each scoring play adds $2$, $3$, $6$ or $7$ points. The final score of a game is $21$ to $17$. Counting each team's scoring only by how many plays of each kind it made (not their order), how many combinations of scoring plays for the two teams are possible?
