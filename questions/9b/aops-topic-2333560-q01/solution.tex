First arrange the $7$ men in a row: $7!$ ways. They create $8$ gaps (one before the first man, one between each pair of neighbours and one after the last man). No two women are adjacent exactly when the women occupy $4$ different gaps. So choose $4$ of the $8$ gaps, $\dbinom84=70$ ways, and place the women in them in order, $4!$ ways. The number of arrangements is
\[7!\cdot\dbinom84\cdot4!=5040\cdot70\cdot24=\boxed{8{,}467{,}200}.\]
The student's answer $11!-6!\cdot4!=39{,}899{,}520$ subtracts far too little: the arrangements in which some two women are adjacent number $11!-8467200=31{,}449{,}600$, not $6!\cdot4!=17280$. (The product $6!\cdot4!$ does not count any natural set of ``bad'' arrangements; even the arrangements with all four women in one block already number $8!\cdot4!=967680$.)
