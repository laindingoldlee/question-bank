A $3$ by $4$ rectangle ($3$ rows, $4$ columns) is divided into $1$ by $1$ squares, as shown. Each square of the top row and of the rightmost column contains a star ($6$ stars in all), and the $3$ squares of the rightmost column are shaded.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
for(int i=0;i<4;++i) for(int j=0;j<3;++j){
  if(i==3) fill((i,j)--(i+1,j)--(i+1,j+1)--(i,j+1)--cycle,gray(0.75));
  draw((i,j)--(i+1,j)--(i+1,j+1)--(i,j+1)--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
  if(i==3 || j==2) label("$\star$",(i+0.5,j+0.5),fontsize(14pt));
}
label("start",(0.5,0.5),fontsize(8pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Henri stands in the bottom-left square, marked ``start''. Teresa flips a fair coin. If it lands heads, Henri moves one square up; if it lands tails, Henri moves one square to the right. This continues until Henri moves into a square with a star, and then the game ends. If this final square is shaded, Teresa wins; otherwise Henri wins. What is the probability that Teresa wins? Express your answer as a common fraction.
