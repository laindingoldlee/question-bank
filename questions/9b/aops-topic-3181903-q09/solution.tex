Digit sums preserve remainders modulo $9$. Also $S(N)\le18189$, hence $S(S(N))\le45$, then $S(S(S(N)))\le12$, and finally $S(S(S(S(N))))\le9$. This last positive integer is congruent to $1$ modulo $9$, so it is $\boxed1$.
