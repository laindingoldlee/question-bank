The Miquel point of the three lines $AB,BC,CA$ with side points $F,D,E$ lies on the circle $AFE$: this follows by adding the directed equal-angle identities from circles $BFD$ and $CED$, giving $\angle FPE\equiv\angle FAE\pmod{180^\circ}$. Thus $A,F,E,P$ are concyclic. The cosine law in $ABC$ gives $\cos A=\dfrac{2}{5}$ and $\sin A=\dfrac{\sqrt{21}}{5}$. Since $AF=12$ and $AE=15$, the cosine law gives $EF^2=12^2+15^2-2\cdot12\cdot15\cdot\dfrac{2}{5}=225$, so $EF=15$. The circle radius is $R=\dfrac{EF}{2\sin A}=\dfrac{75}{2\sqrt{21}}$. Since $PF$ subtends $30^\circ$ at $E$, $PF=2R\sin30^\circ=\boxed{\dfrac{25\sqrt{21}}{14}}$. This is irrational. Consequently the supplied request to write $PF=\dfrac{m}{n}$ with integers is inconsistent with its other data.
