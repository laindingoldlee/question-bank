The set difference consists of the numbers in $(-3,5]$, i.e.\ with $-3<x\le5$, that are not in $(-\infty,2]$, i.e.\ with $x>2$. Both conditions together give $2<x\le5$, so the answer is $\boxed{(2,5]}$. The endpoint $2$ is excluded because it belongs to $(-\infty,2]$, and $5$ is included because it belongs to $(-3,5]$.
