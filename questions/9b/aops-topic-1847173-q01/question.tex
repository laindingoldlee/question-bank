In how many ways can $5$ different letters be mailed if
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $2$ mailboxes are available;
\item[(b)] $4$ mailboxes are available?
\end{enumerate}
(Each letter goes into one mailbox, and a mailbox may stay empty.)
