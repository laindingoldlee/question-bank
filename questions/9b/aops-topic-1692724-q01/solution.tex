Let Susan drive $s$ km; Sam drove $s+25$ km. If Susan had driven $2s$ km, Sam's distance would be $10$ km more than that:
\[
s+25=2s+10\quad\Longrightarrow\quad s=15 .
\]
So Sam drove $15+25=\boxed{40}$ km. (Check: $40-15=25$ and $40-30=10$.)
