Scale temporarily to $a=1$ and choose $A=(0,0,0),B=(1,0,0),C=(1,1,0),B_1=(1,0,1),M=(1,0,\dfrac{1}{2})$. Direction vectors are $u=(1,0,1)$ and $v=(0,-1,\dfrac{1}{2})$, so $u\times v=(1,-\dfrac{1}{2},-1)$ has length $\dfrac{3}{2}$. The distance of the skew lines is $\dfrac{|(C-A)\cdot(u\times v)|}{|u\times v|}=\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{2}}{\dfrac{3}{2}}=\dfrac{1}{3}$. Restoring scale gives $\boxed{\dfrac{a}{3}}$.

\end{asy}
