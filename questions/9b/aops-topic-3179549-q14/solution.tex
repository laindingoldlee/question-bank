For the exponent $a$ of $2$, the conditions are $a$ odd and $a\equiv0\pmod3$, so the least is $a=3$. For the exponent $b$ of $3$, they are $b$ even and $b+1\equiv0\pmod3$, so the least is $b=2$. Other prime exponents can be zero. Thus the least $N$ is $2^3\cdot3^2=\boxed{72}$.
