The number halfway between two numbers is their average:
\[\dfrac12\left(\dfrac18+\dfrac{1}{10}\right)=\dfrac12\left(\dfrac{5}{40}+\dfrac{4}{40}\right)=\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac{9}{40}=\dfrac{9}{80}.\]
(Check: $\dfrac18=\dfrac{10}{80}$ and $\dfrac{1}{10}=\dfrac{8}{80}$, and $\dfrac{9}{80}$ is exactly between them.) The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(E) }\dfrac{9}{80}}$.
