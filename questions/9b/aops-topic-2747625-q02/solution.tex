The interior angles of an $11$-gon add up to $9\cdot180^\circ=1620^\circ$, so each one is
\[\dfrac{1620^\circ}{11}=147.2727\ldots^\circ\approx\boxed{147.273^\circ}.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair[] V;for(int i=0;i<11;++i)V[i]=dir(90+360*i/11);
for(int i=0;i<11;++i)draw(V[i]--V[(i+1)%11],linewidth(0.8));
draw(arc(V[0],0.25,degrees(V[1]-V[0]),degrees(V[10]-V[0])));
label("$\theta$",V[0]+0.32*dir(270),fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}
