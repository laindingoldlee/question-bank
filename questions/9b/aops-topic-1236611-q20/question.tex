There are $5$ ways to write $4$ as an ordered sum of $1$s and $2$s ($1+1+1+1$, $1+1+2$, $1+2+1$, $2+1+1$, $2+2$). How many ways are there to write $8$ as an ordered sum of $1$s and $2$s?
