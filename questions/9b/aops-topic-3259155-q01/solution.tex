The gcd of two positive integers divides each integer, and hence divides their least common multiple. But $4\nmid87$. Therefore $\boxed{\text{no such integers exist}}$. Although $\gcd(a,b)\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=ab$ holds for actual pairs, substituting incompatible proposed values gives only a necessary product, not a valid construction.
