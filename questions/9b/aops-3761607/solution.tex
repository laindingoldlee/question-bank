Let $t=\sqrt[3]{67}$. Since $(t-4)(t^2+4t+16)=3$, the reciprocal is $\dfrac{t^2+4t+16}{3}$. We have $4<t<4.0625$, because $4^3<67$ and $(\dfrac{65}{16})^3 = \dfrac{274625}{4096} > 67$. Thus the reciprocal is greater than $16$ and less than $\dfrac{(\dfrac{65}{16})^2 + 4 (\dfrac{65}{16}) + 16}{3} = \dfrac{12481}{768} < 17$. Its floor is $\boxed{16}$.
