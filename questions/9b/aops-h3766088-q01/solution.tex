A term ending in $5$ and a term ending in a nonzero even digit cannot both occur: if the even term comes first, no later multiple is odd; if the $5$ term comes first, every later even multiple ends in $0$. Hence all ten digits cannot occur, and the length is at most $9$. The chain $\boxed{1,3,9,27,54,108,216,432,2160}$ has distinct units digits and each term is a multiple of its predecessor, hence of every earlier term. Therefore the maximum is $\boxed9$.
