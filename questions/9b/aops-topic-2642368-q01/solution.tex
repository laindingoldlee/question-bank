Divide by $50$ and $25$: $7x+5y=14$ and $7x^2+5y^2=28$. Then $y=\dfrac{14-7x}{5}$, and substituting,
\[7x^2+\dfrac{(14-7x)^2}{5}=28\iff35x^2+196-196x+49x^2=140\iff84x^2-196x+56=0\iff3x^2-7x+2=0.\]
So $x=2$ or $x=\dfrac13$, with $y=0$ or $y=\dfrac15\left(14-\dfrac73\right)=\dfrac73$. The solutions are
\[\boxed{(x,y)=(2,0)\ \text{and}\ \left(\dfrac13,\dfrac73\right)}.\]
