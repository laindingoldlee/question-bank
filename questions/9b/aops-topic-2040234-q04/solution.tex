Give position $i$ from the left weight $2^{i-1}$, and let $V$ be the sum of the weights of tails. If the leftmost tail is at $j$, every earlier coin is a head. Flipping changes $V$ by $-2^{j-1}+(1+2+\cdots+2^{j-2})=-1$. Thus each move decreases the nonnegative integer $V$ exactly by one. After exactly the initial $V$ moves it reaches zero, which means all heads.
