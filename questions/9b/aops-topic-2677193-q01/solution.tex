$20^{19}=2^{38}5^{19}$. Write $a=2^{i}5^{j}$ and $b=2^{k}5^{l}$ with $0\le i,k\le38$ and $0\le j,l\le19$: there are $(39\cdot20)^2=780^2=608400$ pairs. Now $ab\mid20^{19}$ exactly when $i+k\le38$ and $j+l\le19$. The number of pairs $(i,k)$ of nonnegative integers with $i+k\le38$ is $1+2+\cdots+39=780$, and of pairs $(j,l)$ with $j+l\le19$ is $1+2+\cdots+20=210$. So the answer is
\[608400-780\cdot210=608400-163800=\boxed{444600}.\]
