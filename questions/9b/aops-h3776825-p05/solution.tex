The hypotenuse is $5$. Equal tangent lengths from each vertex give, along the two legs, a total of $3+4=5+2r$, where the two tangent lengths from the right-angle vertex each equal $r$. Thus $r=\dfrac{3+4-5}{2}=\boxed1$.
