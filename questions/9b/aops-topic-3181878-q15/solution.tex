Each fixed value is absent with probability $(\dfrac{9}{10})^{10}$. Summing the ten appearance indicators gives expectation $10(1-(\dfrac{9}{10})^{10})=\dfrac{10^{10}-9^{10}}{10^9}=\dfrac{6513215599}{1000000000}$. The numerator is divisible by neither 2 nor 5, so this is reduced. The remainder of the sum is $\boxed{599}$.
