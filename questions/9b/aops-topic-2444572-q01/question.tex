Conner and his $6$ friends ($7$ people in all) sit in a row of $7$ seats at the movies. Three of the friends have salted popcorn, two have buttered popcorn, one has caramel popcorn, and Conner has both salted and buttered popcorn. No two of the three friends with salted popcorn may sit next to each other, the two friends with buttered popcorn must sit next to each other, and Conner and the friend with caramel popcorn may sit anywhere. In how many ways can the $7$ people be seated?
