Glue the two buttered-popcorn friends into a block; the block can be ordered in $2$ ways. Now seat the units ``block'', caramel friend and Conner first, in $3!=6$ orders. They leave $4$ gaps (before the first unit, between units, after the last):
\[\_\ U\ \_\ U\ \_\ U\ \_\]
Two salted friends are next to each other exactly when they are in the same gap, since the block is never split. So the three salted friends must go into $3$ different gaps: $\dbinom43=4$ choices of gaps, and $3!=6$ ways to order the salted friends in them. Every seating satisfying the conditions arises exactly once in this way, so the number of seatings is
\[2\cdot6\cdot4\cdot6=\boxed{288}.\]
