Zachary wants to write down all $n$-digit positive integers whose digits are all $1$, $2$ or $3$ and in which no two $1$'s are next to each other. Let $a_n$ be the number of such integers.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that $a_n=2a_{n-1}+2a_{n-2}$ for $n\ge3$, with $a_1=3$ and $a_2=8$.
\item[(b)] Find a closed formula for $a_n$.
\end{enumerate}
