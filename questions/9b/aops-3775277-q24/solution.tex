If the smallest radius is $r\le3$, the centre distances are $10+r,3+r,13$. The largest is $13$, so $(10+r)^2+(3+r)^2=13^2$. This simplifies to $(r-2)(r+15)=0$. Positivity gives $r=2$, and the circumference is $\boxed{4\pi}$.
