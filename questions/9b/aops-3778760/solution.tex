Encode an order by the column selected at each step. Every word with three A\textquotesingle s, three B\textquotesingle s and two C\textquotesingle s gives exactly one valid target order, because the target within the chosen column is forced. Conversely every valid order yields such a word. Thus the number is the multiset permutation count $\boxed{\dfrac{8!}{3!3!2!}=560}$.
