The expression equals $\dfrac{a^2+b^2+c^2}{abc}$. By Vieta's formulas $a+b+c=6$, $ab+bc+ca=8$ and $abc=-12$, so $a^2+b^2+c^2=36-16=20$ and the value is $\dfrac{20}{-12}=\boxed{-\dfrac{5}{3}}$.
