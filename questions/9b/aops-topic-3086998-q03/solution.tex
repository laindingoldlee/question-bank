The numerator factors as $5x(x+3)(x^2-3x+8)$. The denominator is $5x(x+9)(x+3)(x-3)(x^2-3x+8)$. Since the quadratic has discriminant $9-32=-23$, it never vanishes for real $x$. Cancelling common factors gives $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{(x+9)(x-3)}}$, with original restrictions $x\notin\{-9,-3,0,3\}$.
