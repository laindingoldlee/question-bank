An average that rounds to $82$ is at least $81.5$, so the total is at least $5\cdot81.5=407.5$, i.e.\ at least $408$. The other four tests contribute at most $4\cdot90=360$, so the fourth test score is at least $408-360=48$.

This is possible: scores $90,90,90,48,90$ have total $408$ and average $81.6$, which rounds to $82$, and the lowest score is on the fourth test. The answer is $\boxed{48}$.
