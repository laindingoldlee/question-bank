Assume $x\le y$. Both are even; put $a=v_2(x)$ and $b=v_2(y)$. Their odd parts are coprime, and $\min(ay,bx)=28$. If $bx=28$, then $x$ is an even divisor of $28$, giving $x\in\{2,4,14,28\}$. These are realized by $(x,y)=(2,16384),(4,128),(14,36),(28,30)$, respectively. If $ay=28$, then $y\in\{2,4,14,28\}$ and $a=\dfrac{28}{y}$. The first two possibilities violate $2^a\le x\le y$. For $y=14,a=2,b=1$, the condition $bx\ge28$ violates $x\le14$. Thus $y=28,a=1,b=2$. We need $14\le x\le28$, $v_2(x)=1$, and its odd part coprime to $7$. The candidates are $14,18,22,26$; only $14$ fails the odd-part condition. This adds $18,22,26$, each valid with $y=28$. The complete set is $\{2,4,14,18,22,26,28\}$, whose sum is $\boxed{114}$.
