Parallel cross-section length varies linearly with height. Thus the upper-to-lower height ratio is $(5-3):(6-5)=2:1$. The area ratio is therefore $(3+5)\cdot2:(5+6)\cdot1=16:11$. Hence $\boxed{a+b=27}$.
