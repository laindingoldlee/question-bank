Triangle $DBE$ has angles $60^\circ$ at $B$ and $90^\circ$ at $E$, so $BE=x$ gives $DE=\sqrt3x$. Since $\angle DEF=60^\circ$, $\angle FEC=30^\circ$, and with $\angle C=60^\circ$ triangle $FEC$ has a right angle at $F$; so $EC=\dfrac{EF}{\cos30^\circ}=\dfrac{\sqrt3x}{\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}}=2x$. Thus $BC=3x$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0),C=(3,0),A=(1.5,1.5*sqrt(3)); real t=1;
pair E=(t,0),D=(t,sqrt(3)*t),F=(2.5*t,sqrt(3)*t/2);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(1)); draw(D--E--F--cycle,blue+linewidth(1));
draw((t,0.2)--(t+0.2,0.2)--(t+0.2,0));
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$E$",E,S);label("$F$",F,NE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The area ratio is $\left(\dfrac{DE}{BC}\right)^2=\left(\dfrac{\sqrt3x}{3x}\right)^2=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{3}}$, choice (C).
