Let a knife weigh $k$ ounces and a fork $f$ ounces. Then
\[
2k=3f,\qquad 5f+2k=28 .
\]
The two knives in the second equation can be replaced by three forks, since they weigh the same. So $5f+3f=28$, that is $8f=28$, and
\[
f=\dfrac{28}{8}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{2}=3.5}\ \text{ounces}.
\]
Check: then $2k=3f=10.5$, so $k=5.25$, and $5(3.5)+2(5.25)=17.5+10.5=28$.
