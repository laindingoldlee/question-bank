(a) Reverse a Collatz trajectory. An even-to-half step reverses by doubling. An odd step $x\mapsto3x+1$ reverses by subtracting one and dividing by three, with a positive integer result. Thus reversing the whole trajectory gives a legal calculator path. (b) One path is $1,2,4,8,16,5,10,20,40,13,26,52,17,34,11,22,7$. Each step is one of the permitted operations. The original unrestricted reachability assertion is not proved here; this exercise gives a precise sufficient condition and a concrete construction.
