A calculator starts at $1$ and permits $x\mapsto2x$ and $x\mapsto\dfrac{x-1}{3}$, the latter only when the result is a positive integer. (a) Show that any positive integer whose Collatz trajectory reaches $1$ can be reached by this calculator. (b) Give a path from $1$ to $7$. Here the Collatz map sends even $x$ to $\dfrac{x}{2}$ and odd $x>1$ to $3x+1$.
