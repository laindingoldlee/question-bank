The last digit of $18^n$ is the last digit of $8^n$, and these repeat with period $4$: $8,4,2,6,8,4,2,6,\dots$. Each block of four consecutive powers contributes last digits with sum $8+4+2+6=20$, which ends in $0$. The twenty powers form $5$ such blocks, so the sum ends in $\boxed{0}$.
