By the defining recursion, $g_{64}=3\uparrow^{g_{63}}3$ and $g_{62}=3\uparrow^{g_{61}}3$. Thus the required exact expression is $\boxed{\dfrac{3\uparrow^{g_{63}}3}{3\uparrow^{g_{61}}3}}$. Different numbers of arrows do not obey the ordinary exponent quotient rule, so the arrow counts cannot simply be subtracted.
