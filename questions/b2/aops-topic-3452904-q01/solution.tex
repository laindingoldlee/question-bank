For population $N$, families hosting alone, in pairs, and in triples number $\dfrac{N}{10},\dfrac{N}{20},\dfrac{N}{20}$. The corresponding dinner counts are $\dfrac{N}{10},\dfrac{N}{40},\dfrac{N}{60}$, forcing $N$ to be a multiple of $120$. At $N=120$, there are $12+3+2=17$ dinners, $24$ hosts and $108$ attendees. The other $84$ attendees may be assigned freely, proving attainability. Thus (a) $\boxed{120}$; (b) the mean is $\dfrac{\dfrac{9N}{10}}{\dfrac{17N}{120}}=\boxed{\dfrac{108}{17}}$.
