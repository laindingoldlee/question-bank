In a hypothetical community, $90\%$ of families attend one Thanksgiving dinner each. Exactly $20\%$ of all families host; $10\%$ host with at least one other family, and $5\%$ host with exactly two other families. No dinner has more than three hosts, and each host hosts one dinner. (a) Find the minimum population of families. (b) Find the mean number of families attending a dinner.
