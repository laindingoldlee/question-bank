Each competitor in a building contest builds a structure from $8$ unit cubes. The cubes stand on a flat floor in a grid arrangement, cubes sharing faces with their neighbours; nothing is glued, so every cube that is not on the floor rests on top of a cube directly beneath it. The structure has $h$ levels, every level from the floor up to the top containing at least one cube. The score of a structure is (area of its bottom level) $\times$ (height $h$). The judges noticed that all competitors received different scores. At most how many competitors were there, and what is the largest possible score?
