Red-blue edges total $29\cdot4=116$. Counting them at blue endpoints gives $2B=116$, so $B=58$. The blue-blue edges number $\dfrac{58\cdot2}{2}=58$. Adding gives $\boxed{174}$ edges. A feasible simple graph can be constructed as follows. Put the 29 red vertices on a cycle, replace each edge by two length-two paths with distinct new blue middle vertices, and finally join all 58 blue vertices in a cycle. Each red vertex then has four blue neighbours, and each blue vertex has two red and two blue neighbours.
