Initially orange juice is $\dfrac{6}{5}$ quarts. If $x$ quarts are added, $\dfrac{\dfrac{6}{5}+x}{3+x}=\dfrac{3}{5}$. Thus $6+5x=9+3x$, giving $\boxed{x=\dfrac{3}{2}\text{ quarts}}$.
