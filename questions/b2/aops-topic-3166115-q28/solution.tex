Since $f(x)-1=(x-1)^3$, induction gives $f^{\circ5}(2019)=2018^{3^5}+1=2018^{243}+1$. Modulo $25$, Euler's theorem reduces the exponent modulo $20$, so $18^{243}\equiv18^3\equiv7$. Modulo $4$, the power is $0$. The residue modulo $100$ satisfying these two conditions is $32$, so the final answer is $\boxed{33}$.
