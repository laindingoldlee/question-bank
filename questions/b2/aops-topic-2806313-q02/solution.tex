$AC=AB=BC$ (all radii), so triangle $ABC$ is equilateral and $\angle CAB=60^\circ$; similarly $\angle BAD=60^\circ$. Hence arc $CBD$ subtends $120^\circ$ at $A$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(1,0);draw(circle(A,1));draw(circle(B,1),dashed);pair C=dir(60),D=dir(-60);draw(arc(A,1,-60,60),linewidth(1.5));
draw(A--C);draw(A--D);dot(A);dot(B);label("$A$",A,W);label("$B$",B,E);label("$C$",C,N);label("$D$",D,S);label("$120^\circ$",A,4*E);
\end{asy}

The ratio is $\dfrac{120}{360}=\boxed{\dfrac13}$.
