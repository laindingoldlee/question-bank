Let $a_n$ be the number of ordered sequences of $2$s, $3$s and $4$s with sum $n$. Looking at the last beast in the row,
\[
a_n=a_{n-2}+a_{n-3}+a_{n-4},\qquad a_0=1,\ a_1=0,
\]
with $a_m=0$ for $m<0$. Then
\[
a_2=1,\ a_3=1,\ a_4=2,\ a_5=2,\ a_6=4,\ a_7=5,\ a_8=8,\ a_9=11,\ a_{10}=8+5+4=\boxed{17}.
\]
(Check by multisets: $2+2+2+2+2$ gives $1$ order; $2+2+3+3$ gives $6$; $2+4+4$ gives $3$; $2+2+2+4$ gives $4$; $3+3+4$ gives $3$; total $17$.)
