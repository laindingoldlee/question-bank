Alice and Bob played $25$ games of rock-paper-scissors. Alice played rock $12$ times, scissors $6$ times, and paper $7$ times. Bob played rock $13$ times, scissors $9$ times, and paper $3$ times. If there were no ties, who won the most games?

(Remember, in each game each player picks one of rock, paper, or scissors. Rock beats scissors, scissors beat paper, and paper beats rock. If they choose the same object, the result is a tie.)
