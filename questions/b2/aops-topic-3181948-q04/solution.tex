Let breaststroke time be $t$. The other times are $2t+1$ and $6t-5$. The distance equation is $25t+30(2t+1)+40(6t-5)=1130$, or $325t=1300$. Thus the times are 4, 9, 19 minutes, totalling $\boxed{32\text{ minutes}}$.
