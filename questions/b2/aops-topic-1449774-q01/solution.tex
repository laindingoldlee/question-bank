The remainder is less than the digit sum $s\le18$.

\textbf{Remainders $16$ and $17$ are impossible.} They need $s\ge17$, that is, the number is $89$, $98$ or $99$; but $89=5\cdot17+4$, $98=5\cdot17+13$ and $99=5\cdot18+9$.

\textbf{Remainder $15$ occurs.} It needs $s\ge16$. For $s=16$ the numbers are $79$, $88$, $97$, and $79=4\cdot16+15$.

So the largest remainder is $\boxed{15}$, attained by $79$.
