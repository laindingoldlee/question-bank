$x\equiv1\pmod3$ and $x\equiv2\pmod5$ give $x\equiv7\pmod{15}$. Of $7,22,37,52,67,82,97$, the one $\equiv5\pmod7$ is $82$, so $x\equiv82\pmod{105}$. Of $82,187,292,397,502,607,712,\dots$, the first $\equiv8\pmod{11}$ is $712$ ($712=64\cdot11+8$). So $x\equiv712\pmod{1155}$, and the second smallest positive solution is $712+1155=\boxed{1867}$.
