From exponent 2 onward the last two digits cycle 36, 16, 96, 76, 56 with period 5: multiplication by 6 returns 56 to 36 modulo 100. Since $2022-2$ is divisible by 5, the final two digits are 36, and the tens digit is $\boxed3$.
