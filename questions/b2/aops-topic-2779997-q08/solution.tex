The radius is 5. The distance from a vertex to the center is half the diagonal, $ BO=5\sqrt2 $. Since $ X $ lies between $ B,O $ and $ OX=5 $, $\boxed{BX=5\sqrt2-5}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair B=(10,0),O=(5,5),X=O+5unit(B-O);draw((0,0)--B--(10,10)--(0,10)--cycle);draw(circle(O,5));draw(B--O);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$O$",O,N);label("$X$",X,NE);label("$10$",(5,0),S);
\end{asy}
