Santa visits $7$ places in turn after Christmas. It takes him one day to travel from one place to the next, and he stays at each place for $2\dfrac{3}{7}$ weeks. He can teleport from the North Pole to the first place and from the last place back to the North Pole instantly. If he leaves the North Pole at 7 A.M.\ on January $3$, $2017$, on what date does he get back?
