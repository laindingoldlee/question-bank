Each stay lasts $2\dfrac{3}{7}$ weeks $=17$ days. There are $7$ stays and $6$ one-day trips between consecutive places, so he is away for $7\cdot17+6=125$ days. Counting $125$ days from January $3$: January has $28$ more days (to January $31$), leaving $97$; February $28$ leaves $69$; March $31$ leaves $38$; April $30$ leaves $8$. He returns at 7 A.M.\ on $\boxed{\text{May }8}$, written 5/8.
