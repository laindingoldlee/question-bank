Choose partners for the three men in order: $11\cdot10\cdot9=990$ ways. The remaining $8$ women are split into $4$ unordered pairs in $7\cdot5\cdot3\cdot1=105$ ways (the first woman picks a partner from $7$, the next unpaired woman from $5$, and so on). The total is $990\cdot105=\boxed{103950}$.
