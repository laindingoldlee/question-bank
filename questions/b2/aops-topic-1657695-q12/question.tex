Let $a,b,c$ be the integer side lengths of a scalene triangle, where $a,b,c$ are not three consecutive integers and $c$ is the largest side. Given that $x=a+b-2c$ is a root of the polynomial $f(x)=x^2+k$, where $k$ is a constant, what is the largest possible value of $k$?
