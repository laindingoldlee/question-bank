Let $ABCD$ be a square of side 5, with $A=(0,0),B=(5,0),C=(5,5),D=(0,5)$. Take $E$ on ray $AC$ beyond $C$ with $CE=AC$. Put $a=[ADE]$. The internal bisector of $\angle DEA$ meets the circumcircle of $ADE$ again at $Z$, whose centre is $O_\alpha$. Line $ZO_\alpha$ meets $BC,AD$ at $X,Y$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of $XB$, $P=YM\cap AB$ (extended), and $O_\beta$ the centre of the circle through $A,B,X,Y$. Put $x=[XO_\beta P]$. On nonnegative integers define $f(m,0)=f(0,n)=0$ and $f(m+1,n+1)=f(m,n+1)-f(m+1,n)+f(m,n)$. Set $y=|\lfloor f(\lfloor a\rfloor,\lfloor x\rfloor)\rfloor|$. A bag has $\lfloor a\rfloor$ Hershey bars, $\lfloor x\rfloor$ KitKat bars, and $y$ other bars. Five bars are chosen uniformly without replacement; let $T$ be the expected KitKat count. Evaluate $\sum_{j+k=\lfloor T\rfloor,\ j,k\ge1}\dfrac{j+k}{jk}$, with an empty sum defined as zero.
