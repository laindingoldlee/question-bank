Given sum $s$, B is smaller with probability $\dfrac{s-1}{12}$. Since $E[s]=7$, part(a) is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$. Conditional expected payment is $\dfrac{1+\cdots+(s-1)}{12}=\dfrac{s(s-1)}{24}$. The sum has variance $2\cdot\dfrac{35}{12}=\dfrac{35}{6}$, so $E[s^2]=49+\dfrac{35}{6}=\dfrac{329}{6}$. Thus $E[s(s-1)]=\dfrac{287}{6}$ and expected payment is $\boxed{\dfrac{287}{144}}$.
