All $10$ faces are equally likely to be the face showing. Of these, $7$ are blue: $6$ on the three blue-blue cards and $1$ on the blue-white card. Given that a blue face shows, it is equally likely to be any of these $7$, and the other side is blue for the $6$ faces on blue-blue cards. So the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{6}{7}}$.
