Rationalise the denominator:
\[
\dfrac{1}{3-\sqrt7}=\dfrac{3+\sqrt7}{(3-\sqrt7)(3+\sqrt7)}=\dfrac{3+\sqrt7}{2}.
\]
Since $2<\sqrt7<3$, this number lies between $\dfrac{5}{2}$ and $3$, so $a=2$ and
\[
b=\dfrac{3+\sqrt7}{2}-2=\dfrac{\sqrt7-1}{2}.
\]
Now $a^2+ab+b^2=(a+b)^2-ab$, with
\[
(a+b)^2=\left(\dfrac{3+\sqrt7}{2}\right)^2=\dfrac{16+6\sqrt7}{4}=\dfrac{8+3\sqrt7}{2},\qquad ab=\sqrt7-1 .
\]
Therefore
\[
a^2+ab+b^2=\dfrac{8+3\sqrt7}{2}-\sqrt7+1=\boxed{\dfrac{10+\sqrt7}{2}}.
\]
