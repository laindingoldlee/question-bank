A quadratic sequence has general term $u_n=an^2+bn+c$ with real numbers $a\ne0$, $b$, $c$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find $a$, $b$, $c$ for the sequence $1,3,6,10,15,\dots$ (with $u_1=1$, $u_2=3$, $u_3=6$), and conclude that the coefficients of a sequence of integers need not be integers.
\item[(b)] Prove that $u_n$ is an integer for every positive integer $n$ if and only if $2a$, $a+b$ and $c$ are all integers.
\end{enumerate}
