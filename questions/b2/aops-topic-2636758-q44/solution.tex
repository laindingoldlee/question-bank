Substitute $y=-x-4$:
\[3x(-x-4)+4(-x-4)+11=0\iff3x^2+16x+5=0\iff(3x+1)(x+5)=0.\]
So $x=-5$ or $x=-\dfrac13$, and the points are $\boxed{P=(-5,1)\text{ and }Q=\left(-\dfrac13,-\dfrac{11}{3}\right)}$. (Both satisfy $3x+4\ne0$; the second graph is the hyperbola $y=-\dfrac{11}{3x+4}$.)

\begin{asy}

draw((-7,0)--(3,0),gray,Arrow);draw((0,-7.5)--(0,7.5),gray,Arrow);draw((-4/3,-7.5)--(-4/3,7.5),dashed+gray);
dot((-5,1));dot((-1/3,-11/3));
label("$P$",(-5,1),N,fontsize(8pt));label("$Q$",(-1/3,-11/3),E,fontsize(8pt));

\end{asy}
