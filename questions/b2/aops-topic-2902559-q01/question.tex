Find all seven-digit numbers $\overline{abcdefg}$ with distinct digits such that: $\overline{abcdefg}$ is divisible by $11$; $\overline{abc}$ and $\overline{efg}$ are divisible by $9$; $\overline{abc}$ and $\overline{bcd}$ are divisible by $5$; $\overline{cde}$ is prime; and the number read backwards, $\overline{gfedcba}$, is divisible by $4$. (Three-digit blocks may start with $0$.)
