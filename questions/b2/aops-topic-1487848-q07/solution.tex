The punch contains $d\cdot\dfrac{d}{100}=\dfrac{d^2}{100}$ litres of grape juice. After adding $x$ litres of pure grape juice we need
\[
\dfrac{\dfrac{d^2}{100}+x}{d+x}=\dfrac{3d}{100}.
\]
Multiplying by $100(d+x)$: $d^2+100x=3d^2+3dx$, so $(100-3d)x=2d^2$ and
\[
x=\boxed{\dfrac{2d^2}{100-3d}}\ \text{litres}.
\]
The condition $d<\dfrac{100}{3}$ is needed: otherwise $3d\%$ would be $100\%$ or more, which no mixture can reach. (Check with $d=10$: $10$ litres of $10\%$ punch, add $x=\dfrac{200}{70}=\dfrac{20}{7}$ litres, giving $\dfrac{1+\dfrac{20}{7}}{10+\dfrac{20}{7}}=\dfrac{27}{90}=30\%$.)
