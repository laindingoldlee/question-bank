Choose which of the $5$ shots is missed in $\binom51=5$ ways; each such sequence has probability $\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^4\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}$. So
\[
P=5\cdot\dfrac{81}{256}\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{405}{1024}}\approx0.396 .
\]
