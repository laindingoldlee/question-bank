Let $x$ dollars be invested in bonds, so $2x$ dollars are invested in stocks. The annual income is
\[0.08\cdot2x+0.06x=0.22x=325.60,\]
so $x=\dfrac{325.60}{0.22}=1480$. She has $2x=\boxed{\$2960}$ invested in stocks. (Check: $8\%$ of $2960$ is $236.80$, $6\%$ of $1480$ is $88.80$, total $325.60$.)
