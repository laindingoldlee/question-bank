Write a root as $u+iv$. Then $u^2-v^2=-258$ and $2uv=-12\sqrt{138}$. Also $(u^2+v^2)^2=258^2+144\cdot138=294^2$, giving $u^2+v^2=294$. Thus $u^2=18,v^2=276$ with opposite signs. The roots are $\boxed{\pm(3\sqrt2-2i\sqrt{69})}$. The principal root is $3\sqrt2-2i\sqrt{69}$, with positive real part.
