The center is $O=(5,5)$ and the squared radius is $3^2+2^2=13$, so the equation is $\boxed{(x-5)^2+(y-5)^2=13}$. A nonvertical line through $P$ is $y-10=m(x-10)$. Its distance from $O$ is $\dfrac{|5m-5|}{\sqrt{m^2+1}}$. Tangency requires $25(m-1)^2=13(m^2+1)$, or $6m^2-25m+6=0$. Thus the two tangents are
\[\boxed{y-10=\dfrac{25\pm\sqrt{481}}{12}(x-10)}.\]
The vertical line $x=10$ has distance $5>\sqrt{13}$ from $O$ and is not tangent.
\begin{asy}
size(7cm);
pair A=(2,3),B=(8,7),O=(5,5),P=(10,10); real r=sqrt(13);
draw(circle(O,r)); draw(A--B); dot(A);dot(B);dot(O);dot(P);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,NE);label("$O$",O,W);label("$P$",P,NE);
for(int e=-1;e<=1;e+=2){real m=(25+e*sqrt(481))/12;pair T=O-((m*O.x-O.y+10-10*m)/(m*m+1))*(m,-1);draw(P--T);draw(O--T,dashed);}

\end{asy}
