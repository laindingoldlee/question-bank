Centre the large square at the origin, so the disk centres are $(\pm \dfrac{X}{2},\pm \dfrac{X}{2})$. A side of the central diamond is $x+y=a$. Its distance from $(\dfrac{X}{2},\dfrac{X}{2})$ is $\dfrac{(X-a)}{\sqrt2}=\dfrac{X}{2}$, yielding $a=X(1-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2})$. The small square has diagonals $2a,2a$, hence area $2a^2=\boxed{X^2(3-2\sqrt2)}$.

import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
draw((-1,-1)--(1,-1)--(1,1)--(-1,1)--cycle);for(int i:new int[]{-1,1})for(int j:new int[]{-1,1})draw(circle((i*.5,j*.5),.5));real a=1-1/sqrt(2);filldraw((a,0)--(0,a)--(-a,0)--(0,-a)--cycle,lightblue,blue);label("$2X$",(0,-1),(0,-1));
\end{asy}
