A game with a winner gives out $3$ points in total, and a tie gives out $1+1=2$ points. If $t$ games were ties,
\[
3(96-t)+2t=271\quad\Longrightarrow\quad288-t=271\quad\Longrightarrow\quad t=\boxed{17}.
\]
