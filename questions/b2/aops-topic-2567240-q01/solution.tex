Take the length of the loaf to be $1$. Weni's cuts are at the points $x$ with $0<x<1$ and $10x$ an integer ($9$ cuts), Lia's where $15x$ is an integer ($14$ cuts) and Anggi's where $18x$ is an integer ($17$ cuts).

A point is cut by both Weni and Lia exactly when $10x$ and $15x$ are integers, which happens exactly when $5x$ is an integer (since $5x=15x-10x$, and conversely $10x=2\cdot5x$, $15x=3\cdot5x$). Those are $4$ points. In the same way, Weni and Anggi share the points where $2x$ is an integer ($1$ point), Lia and Anggi share the points where $3x$ is an integer ($2$ points), and all three would share points where $x$ is an integer (none inside the loaf).

By inclusion--exclusion the number of distinct cuts is
\[9+14+17-4-1-2+0=33,\]
so there are $33+1=\boxed{34}$ pieces.
