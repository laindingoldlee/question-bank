Let $ABC$ be the triangle with $AB=13$, $BC=14$ and $AC=15$. Let $H$ be its orthocentre and $H'$ the reflection of $H$ across $BC$. Let $D$ be the point where the bisector of $\angle A$ meets $BC$, and let $E$ be the point where the incircle of triangle $ABC$ touches $AC$. Find the area of the concave pentagon $HECDH'$.
