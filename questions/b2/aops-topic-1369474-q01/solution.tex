Treat $u=x-2y$ as a single quantity: the expression is $-4u^2+8u=-4u(u-2)$. Substituting back,
\[-4(x-2y)^2+8(x-2y)=\boxed{-4(x-2y)(x-2y-2)},\]
which can also be written $4(x-2y)(2-x+2y)$.
