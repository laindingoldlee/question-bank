An odd multiple of an odd number has an odd multiplier. The multiples of $5$ up to $2025$ have multipliers $1$ through $405$, of which $203$ are odd. For $7$, the multipliers go through $289$, giving $145$ odd multiples. Those counted twice are odd multiples of $35$; their multipliers go through $57$, giving $29$. Thus inclusion-exclusion gives $\boxed{203+145-29=319}$.
