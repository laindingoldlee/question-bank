We find the last three digits, that is, $7^{100}$ modulo $1000$. Since $7^4=2401$,
\[7^{100}=2401^{25}\equiv401^{25}=(1+400)^{25}\pmod{1000}.\]
By the binomial theorem,
\[(1+400)^{25}=1+25\cdot400+\dbinom{25}{2}400^2+\cdots.\]
Here $25\cdot400=10000$, and every later term contains $400^2=160000$; all of these are multiples of $1000$. So $7^{100}\equiv1\pmod{1000}$: the last three digits of $7^{100}$ are $001$.

The tens digit is $\boxed{0}$ and the hundreds digit is $\boxed{0}$. (For the tens digit alone it is enough that $7^4=2401\equiv1\pmod{100}$, so $7^{100}=\left(7^4\right)^{25}\equiv1\pmod{100}$.)
