Since $\dfrac{d}{c+8}$ is a positive integer, $d\ge c+8$; with $d\le9$ this gives $c\le1$. Since $\dfrac{c}{b+1}$ is a positive integer, $c\ge b+1\ge1$, so $c=1$, then $b+1\mid1$ gives $b=0$, and $d\ge9$ gives $d=9$ (and $\dfrac{9}{9}=1$). Finally $\dfrac{2}{a}$ must be a positive integer, so $a=1$ or $a=2$. The numbers are $\boxed{1019\ \text{and}\ 2019}$.
