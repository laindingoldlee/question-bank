Two thousand points are given on a circle. Label one of them $1$. From it count $2$ points clockwise and label that point $2$; from there count $3$ points clockwise and label that point $3$; continue until the labels $1,2,\dots,1993$ are all used. Some points get more than one label and some none. What is the smallest integer that labels the same point as $1993$?
