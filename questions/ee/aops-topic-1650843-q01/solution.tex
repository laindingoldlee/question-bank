Adding and subtracting the equations,
\[
y=\dfrac{1}{2}\left(z+\dfrac{z-2}{2}\right)=\dfrac{3z-2}{4},\qquad x=\dfrac{1}{2}\left(z-\dfrac{z-2}{2}\right)=\dfrac{z+2}{4}.
\]
For $x$ to be an integer we need $z\equiv2\pmod4$; write $z=4k+2$ with $k\ge0$. Then $x=k+1$ and $y=3k+1$, both positive integers.

\textbf{Coprimality.} Any common divisor of $x$ and $y$ divides $z=x+y$, and any common divisor of all three divides $x$ and $y$; so $\gcd(x,y,z)=\gcd(x,y)$, and
\[
\gcd(k+1,3k+1)=\gcd(k+1,(3k+1)-3(k+1))=\gcd(k+1,2).
\]
This is $1$ exactly when $k+1$ is odd, that is $k=2m$ is even. Hence the solutions are
\[
\boxed{(x,y,z)=(2m+1,\ 6m+1,\ 8m+2),\quad m=0,1,2,\dots}
\]
for example $(1,1,2)$, $(3,7,10)$, $(5,13,18)$. Check: $x+y=8m+2=z$ and $y-x=4m=\dfrac{(8m+2)-2}{2}$.
