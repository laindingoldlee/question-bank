For a fixed single-digit multiplier $m$, the four other digit coefficients are $10m,m,10,1$. Since $10m>10>m>1$, maximize by matching larger digits to larger coefficients. For $m=2,3,4,5,6$, this gives respectively $64\cdot2+53=181$, $64\cdot3+52=244$, $63\cdot4+52=304$, $63\cdot5+42=357$, and $53\cdot6+42=360$. Hence the maximum is $\boxed{360}$.
