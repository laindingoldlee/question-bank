The base angles are $78^\circ$. Reflect $A$ across $BC$ to $A^{\prime}$. Reflection swaps $D,E$ and fixes $B$, so $\angle BEA=\angle BDA^{\prime}$. The crossing condition puts ray $DB$ between $DA$ and $DA^{\prime}$; hence the requested sum is $\angle ADA^{\prime}$. The arc $AA^{\prime}$ not containing $D$ has central angle $2\cdot78^\circ=156^\circ$. By the inscribed-angle theorem, the sum is $\boxed{78^\circ}$.
