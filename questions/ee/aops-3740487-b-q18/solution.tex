If the area is $K$, the three sides are $\dfrac{2K}{12},\dfrac{2K}{15},\dfrac{2K}{20}$, in ratio $5:4:3$. Thus the triangle is a scaled $3$-$4$-$5$ right triangle. Write its legs as $3t,4t$ and hypotenuse as $5t$. Its smallest altitude is the one to the hypotenuse, namely $\dfrac{(3t)(4t)}{5t}=\dfrac{12t}{5}$. Setting this equal to 12 gives $t=5$, so $K=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot15\cdot20=\boxed{150}$, choice D.
