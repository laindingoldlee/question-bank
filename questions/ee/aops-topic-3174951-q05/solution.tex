If the counts are $x,y,z$, the equation reduces to $10x+5y+2z=200$. Modulo $2$, $y$ must be even; write $y=2k$. Then $5x+5k+z=100$, so $x,k\ge0$ and $x+k\le20$, with $z$ uniquely determined. There are $\sum_{j=0}^{20}(j+1)=\dfrac{21\cdot22}{2}=\boxed{231}$ choices.
