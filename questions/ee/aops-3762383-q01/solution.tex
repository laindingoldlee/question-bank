For a fixed order of denominators $d_1,\ldots,d_6$, define remaining fraction $r_0=1$, share $p_i = \dfrac{r_{i-1}}{d_i}$, and $r_i=r_{i-1}-p_i$. The least valid initial stash is the lcm of the reduced denominators of $p_1,\ldots,p_6,r_6$. Thus all $6!=720$ orders can be checked exactly using fractions. The minimum eighth-share by its position 1 through 6 is $39,9,3,3,6,8$, so the global minimum is 3. It is attained from 78 coins in order $2,3,13,8,21,5$: the successive shares are $39,13,2,3,1,4$, leaving 16 for the last pirate. Hence $\boxed3$.
