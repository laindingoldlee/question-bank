Six pirates take fractions $\dfrac{1}{2} , \dfrac{1}{3} , \dfrac{1}{5} , \dfrac{1}{8} , \dfrac{1}{13} , \dfrac{1}{21}$ of the coins remaining when their turns arrive, in any order; a seventh takes the remainder last. Every share must be an integer and the initial stash positive. What is the least possible share for the pirate taking $\dfrac{1}{8}$?
