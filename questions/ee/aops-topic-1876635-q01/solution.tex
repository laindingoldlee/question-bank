Let the rate be $r$ (as a decimal). After $3$ years the compound interest is $1000\left[(1+r)^3-1\right]$ and the simple interest is $1000\cdot3r$, so
\[
1000\left[(1+r)^3-1-3r\right]=1000\left(3r^2+r^3\right)=61,\qquad\text{i.e.}\qquad f(r)=r^3+3r^2=0.061 .
\]
For $r>0$, $f$ is increasing, so there is exactly one positive solution. Now
\[
f(0.139)=0.002685619+0.057963=0.060648619<0.061,\qquad f(0.1395)=0.002714704875+0.05838075=0.061095454875>0.061,
\]
so $0.139<r<0.1395$, i.e.\ the rate is between $13.9\%$ and $13.95\%$. To the nearest tenth of a percent the rate is $\boxed{13.9\%}$ (more precisely about $13.94\%$).
