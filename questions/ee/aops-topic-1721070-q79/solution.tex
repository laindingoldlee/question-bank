With denominator $12$: $a=\dfrac{24}{12}+\dfrac{9}{12}+\dfrac{10}{12}=\dfrac{43}{12}$, and $b=\dfrac{3}{2}+\dfrac{3}{2}=3$. So $\dfrac {a}{b}=\dfrac{43}{12\cdot3}=\boxed{\dfrac{43}{36}}$.
