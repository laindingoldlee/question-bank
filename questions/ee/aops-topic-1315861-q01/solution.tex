Let $L=\operatorname{lcm}(m,n)$ and divide: $s=qL+r$ with $0\le r<L$. Since $m$ divides both $s$ and $L$, it divides $r=s-qL$; likewise $n\mid r$. So $r$ is a common multiple of $m$ and $n$ smaller than $L$. By the minimality of $L$, $r$ cannot be a positive common multiple, so $r=0$ and $L\mid s$. $\blacksquare$
