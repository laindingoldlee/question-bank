Because $b^2$ is a square, $ab^2$ being a square forces $a$ to be a square; similarly $b$ is a square. Their gcd is therefore a square. Conversely every square at most $2021$ is attainable by taking $a=b$ equal to it. The possible gcds are $1^2,\ldots,44^2$, whose sum is $\dfrac{44\cdot45\cdot89}{6}=\boxed{29370}$.
