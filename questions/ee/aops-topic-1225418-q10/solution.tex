Buildings at positions $i<j$ have $j-i-1$ buildings between them; this is odd exactly when $j-i$ is even. So all chosen positions must have the same parity. There are $8$ odd and $8$ even positions, giving $2\left(2^8-1\right)=\boxed{510}$ nonempty sets.
