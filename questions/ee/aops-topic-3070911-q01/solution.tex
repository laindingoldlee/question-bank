The seven-day multiplier is $\dfrac{14400}{24000}=\dfrac{3}{5}$. Therefore $A(t)=24000\left(\dfrac{3}{5}\right)^{\dfrac{t}{7}}$. May 26 is day 14, so $A(14)=8640$, which rounds to $\boxed{8600}$. June 16 is day 35, so $A(35)=1866.24$, which rounds to $\boxed{1900}$. June 5 is day 24, giving $\boxed{24000\left(\dfrac{3}{5}\right)^{\dfrac{24}{7}}}$.
