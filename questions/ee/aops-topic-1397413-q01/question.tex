In right triangle $ABC$ the right angle is at $C$, and $\overline{CD}$ is the altitude to the hypotenuse $\overline{AB}$. Let $BC=a$, $AC=b$, $AB=c$ and $CD=h$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Express $h$ in terms of $a$ and $b$.
\item[(b)] Prove that $\dfrac{1}{h^2}=\dfrac{1}{a^2}+\dfrac{1}{b^2}$.
\end{enumerate}
