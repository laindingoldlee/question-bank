On day $i$ the playing time is $j=\dfrac{20i-i^2}{10}$ hours, so the survival probability is
\[
1-\dfrac{j}{10}=\dfrac{100-20i+i^2}{100}=\left(\dfrac{10-i}{10}\right)^2 .
\]
Multiplying over $i=1,\dots,5$,
\[
P=\left(\dfrac{9\cdot8\cdot7\cdot6\cdot5}{10^5}\right)^2,\qquad \sqrt P=\dfrac{15120}{100000}=\dfrac{189}{1250}.
\]
Since $189=3^3\cdot7$ and $1250=2\cdot5^4$, the fraction is in lowest terms, and $m+n=1439$. The remainder is $\boxed{\textbf{(g)}\ 439}$.
