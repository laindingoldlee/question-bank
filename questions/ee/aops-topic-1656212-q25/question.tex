In a five-day week, Adam plays a games console for $\dfrac{1}{10}\left(20i-i^2\right)$ hours on day $i$ ($i=1,2,3,4,5$). If the console is played for $j$ hours on a given day, it breaks down that day with probability $\dfrac{j}{10}$, independently of other days. Let $P$ be the probability that the console survives the entire week. Then $\sqrt P$ can be written as $\dfrac {m}{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find the remainder when $m+n$ is divided by $1000$.

(a) $023$\quad (b) $035$\quad (c) $067$\quad (d) $120$\quad (e) $221$\quad (f) $255$\quad (g) $439$\quad (h) $559$\quad (i) $701$\quad (j) $827$
