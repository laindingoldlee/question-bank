At most $5\cdot3=15$ different-color pairs exist, and $x\ge0$, so $y-x\le15$. Draw all red lines parallel and all blue lines parallel in a different direction. Then $x=0$ and every red-blue pair intersects, so the maximum is $\boxed{15}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
for(int x=0;x<5;++x)draw((x,-1)--(x,3),red);for(int y=0;y<3;++y)draw((-1,y)--(5,y),blue);label("red",(4.8,2.8),E);label("blue",(5,0),E);
\end{asy}
