In rectangle $ABCD$, $AB=20$ and $BC=10$. Let $E$ be a point on $\overline{CD}$ such that $\angle CBE=15^\circ$. What is $AE$?

(One solution takes the point $F$ on $\overline{CD}$ with $\angle CBF=30^\circ$, so that $BE$ bisects $\angle CBF$, and states $CF=\dfrac{10\sqrt3}{3}$. A student thinks $CF$ should be $10\sqrt3$. Who is right?)
