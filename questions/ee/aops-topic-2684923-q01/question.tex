Let $r,s,t$ be the roots of $x^3+ax^2+bx+c$, let $P_j=r^j+s^j+t^j$, and let $e_j$ be the $j$th elementary symmetric sum of the roots (the sum of all products of $j$ distinct roots). Newton's identity for $j=4$ reads
\[P_4=e_1P_3-e_2P_2+e_3P_1-4e_4.\]
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What is $e_4$ for a cubic, and why?
\item[(b)] Express $r^4+s^4+t^4$ in terms of $a,b,c$.
\end{enumerate}
