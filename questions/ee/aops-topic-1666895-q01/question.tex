Two teams play a series of games; the first team to win $n$ games wins the series. Each team wins each game with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, independently of the other games.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] For $n=2$, what is the expected number of games played?
\item[(b)] Find the expected number of games played for general $n$, in closed form.
\end{enumerate}
