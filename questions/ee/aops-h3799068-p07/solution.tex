For $n=26$, the concatenation is $262728$, which contains 6272 beginning at its second digit. If $n\le25$, the three numbers are at most 27 and hence have at most two digits. A four-digit substring must cross a boundary. Its middle pair 27 can only arise within the number 27 or across a units-two to tens-seven boundary; the latter is impossible below 28. For 27 to occur as the middle two digits and have following digit two, the next number must be 28, requiring $n\ge26$. Hence $\boxed{26}$ is smallest.
