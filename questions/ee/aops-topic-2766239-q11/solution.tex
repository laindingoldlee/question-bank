Writing step counts $R,L,U,D$, the conditions imply $R-L=2$, $U-D=3$, and $L+D=1$. If $(L,D)=(1,0)$, the counts are $(3,1,3,0)$, giving $\dfrac{7!}{3!3!}=140$. If $(L,D)=(0,1)$, counts are $(2,0,4,1)$, giving $\dfrac{7!}{2!4!}=105$. Total $\boxed{245}$.
