With first term $a$ and difference $d$: $a+6d=48$ and $S_{22}=11(2a+21d)=1551$, so $2a+21d=141$. Substituting $a=48-6d$: $96+9d=141$, $d=5$, $a=18$. Then $t_{12}=a+11d=\boxed{73}$.
