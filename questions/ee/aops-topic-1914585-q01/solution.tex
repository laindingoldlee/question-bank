The sector is $\dfrac{45}{360}=\dfrac{1}{8}$ of the disc, so its area is $\dfrac{1}{8}\cdot\pi\cdot12^2=18\pi$.

For the triangle, the height from $B$ to $OA$ is $12\sin45^\circ=6\sqrt2$, so its area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot12\cdot6\sqrt2=36\sqrt2$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0),A=(12,0),B=12*dir(45);
path seg=arc(O,12,0,45)--cycle;
fill(seg,palegreen);
draw(O--A,linewidth(1)); draw(O--B,linewidth(1)); draw(arc(O,12,0,45),linewidth(1)); draw(A--B,dashed);
draw(arc(O,2.2,0,45),linewidth(0.6)); label("$45^\circ$",2.2*dir(22.5),E);
dot(O);dot(A);dot(B);
label("$O$",O,SW); label("$A$",A,SE); label("$B$",B,N);
label("$12$",(O+A)/2,S); label("$12$",(O+B)/2,NW);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The difference (the shaded segment) is
\[
\boxed{18\pi-36\sqrt2}\ \text{cm}^2\approx5.64\ \text{cm}^2 .
\]
