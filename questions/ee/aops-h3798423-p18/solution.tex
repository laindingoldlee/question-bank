The ratio has nine parts, so each part is $\dfrac{180^\circ}{9}=20^\circ$. The interior angles are $40^\circ,60^\circ,80^\circ$. An exterior angle is supplementary to its interior angle, making the largest $180^\circ-40^\circ=\boxed{140^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(4,0),C=4sin(60*pi/180)/sin(80*pi/180)*dir(40);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--(-1.5,0));label("$40^\circ$",A,(1,1));label("$60^\circ$",B,(-1,1));label("$80^\circ$",C,(0,1));label("$140^\circ$",(-.4,.5));
\end{asy}
