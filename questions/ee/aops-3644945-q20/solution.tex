Write the roots as $q-d,q,q+d$, with $d>0$. Vieta gives $a=3q,b=3q^2-d^2,c=q(q^2-d^2)$. The condition $a+c=2b$ gives $(q-2)d^2=q(q^2-6q+3)$. For $q=2$ this is impossible, and for $q=3,4,5$ the right side is negative. At $q=6$, $d^2 = \dfrac{9}{2}$ works, with $a = 18 , b = \dfrac{207}{2} , c = 189$ strictly increasing. Thus the minimum sum is $\boxed{18}$.
