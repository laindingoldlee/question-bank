Write the number as $n=10q+d$, where $d\in\{1,\dots,9\}$ is the units digit and $q$ runs over the $90$ integers $10,11,\dots,99$. Then $d\mid n$ exactly when $d\mid10q$.
\[
\begin{array}{c|l|c}
d & \text{condition on }q & \text{count}\\\hline
1 & \text{none} & 90\\
2 & \text{none ($10q$ is even)} & 90\\
3 & 3\mid q & 30\\
4 & 2\mid q & 45\\
5 & \text{none} & 90\\
6 & 3\mid q & 30\\
7 & 7\mid q\ (q=14,21,\dots,98) & 13\\
8 & 4\mid q\ (q=12,16,\dots,96) & 22\\
9 & 9\mid q\ (q=18,27,\dots,99) & 10
\end{array}
\]
(For $d=4$: $4\mid10q\iff2\mid5q\iff2\mid q$; for $d=6$: $6\mid10q\iff3\mid q$; for $d=8$: $8\mid10q\iff4\mid q$.) The total is
\[
90+90+30+45+90+30+13+22+10=\boxed{420}.
\]
