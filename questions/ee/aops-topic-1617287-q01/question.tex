Prove that if $m\ne0$, $n\ne0$ and $m\ne n$, then the value of
\[
\dfrac{2}{mn}\div\left(\dfrac{1}{m}-\dfrac{1}{n}\right)^2-\dfrac{m^2+n^2}{(m-n)^2}
\]
does not depend on $m$ and $n$, and find this value.
