Find all functions $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ such that
\[f\left(\lfloor x\rfloor y\right)=f(x)\lfloor f(y)\rfloor\qquad\text{for all real }x,y,\]
where $\lfloor t\rfloor$ denotes the greatest integer not exceeding $t$.
