A standard die is thrown three times. Given that the number on the third throw equals the sum of the numbers on the first two throws, the probability that a $2$ appeared at least once can be written as $\dfrac{m}{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $m+n$.
