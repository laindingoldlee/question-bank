From the third and fourth terms: $7p+6q=13$ and $13p+7q=20$. Solving (the determinant $49-78\ne0$, so the solution is unique) gives $p=q=1$: each term is the sum of the two before it. This fits the rest: $13+20=33$, $20+33=53$, $33+53=86$. The next term is $53+86=\boxed{139}$.
