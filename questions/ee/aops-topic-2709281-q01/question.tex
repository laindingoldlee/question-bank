A bird's home is $100$ miles from its friend's nest. It flies toward the nest, and after covering $70\%$ of the remaining distance it changes personality and turns back toward home. After covering $70\%$ of the way home it changes again and turns toward the nest, and so on. It stops after flying $150$ miles in total. How many personality changes does it make?
