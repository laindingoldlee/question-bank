Leg 1: $70$ miles toward the nest (position $70$), then change $1$. Leg 2: $70\%$ of the $70$ miles home, i.e. $49$ miles (position $21$, total $119$), then change $2$. Leg 3 would be $70\%$ of the remaining $79$ miles, i.e. $55.3$ miles, but the bird stops after $150-119=31$ more miles. So it changes $\boxed{2}$ times.
