Take the center as the complex origin. Let $p,a_j$ be the complex coordinates, all of modulus $1$. The vertices form a rotated set of roots of unity, so $\sum a_j=0$ and $\sum\overline{a_j}=0$. Now
\[
|p-a_j|^2=(p-a_j)(\overline p-\overline{a_j})
=2-p\overline{a_j}-\overline p\,a_j.
\]
Summing gives
\[
\boxed{\sum_{j=1}^nPA_j^2=2n}.
\]
This does not depend on $p$, including when $P$ coincides with a vertex.

\end{asy}
