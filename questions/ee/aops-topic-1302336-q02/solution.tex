$\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{30}k^2=\dfrac{30\cdot31\cdot61}{6}=9455$, so the units digit is $\boxed{5}$. (Alternatively, the units digits of $1^2,\dots,10^2$ add to $45$, and this repeats three times: $135$.)
