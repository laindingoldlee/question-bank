Use $1001=7\cdot11\cdot13$. Since $793793=793\cdot1001$, the $12$-digit block $793793793793=793793\cdot10^6+793793$ is a multiple of $7$. Now
\[
N=8\cdot10^{13}+793793793793\cdot10+8\equiv8\cdot10^{13}+8\pmod7 .
\]
Also $10^3\equiv-1\pmod7$ (as $1001\equiv0$), so $10^{12}=(10^3)^4\equiv1$ and $10^{13}\equiv10\equiv3\pmod7$. Hence
\[
N\equiv8\cdot3+8=32\equiv\boxed{4}\pmod7 .
\]
