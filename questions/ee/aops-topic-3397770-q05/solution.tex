The joint probability of selecting the double-headed coin and observing ten heads is $\dfrac{1}{10}$. For an ordinary coin the corresponding joint probability is $\dfrac{9}{10}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2^{10}}$. Bayes\' theorem gives $\dfrac{1}{1+\dfrac{9}{1024}}=\boxed{\dfrac{1024}{1033}}$.
