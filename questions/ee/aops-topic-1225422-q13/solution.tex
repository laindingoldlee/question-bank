The graph is a rhombus with vertices $(\pm12,0)$ and $(0,\pm6)$. Its diagonals are $24$ and $12$, so its area is $\dfrac{24\cdot12}{2}=\boxed{144}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((-14,0)--(14,0),Arrow); draw((0,-8)--(0,8),Arrow); draw((12,0)--(0,6)--(-12,0)--(0,-6)--cycle,blue+linewidth(1));
label("$12$",(12,0),S); label("$6$",(0,6),E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
