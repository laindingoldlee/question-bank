$m$ must share a prime with $55$ (so $5$ or $11$ divides $m$) and with $66$ (so $2$, $3$ or $11$ divides $m$). The candidates are multiples of $11$ (least $11$) or multiples of $5\cdot2=10$ or $5\cdot3=15$. The least is $m=10$, and $80-m=\boxed{70}$.
