Let $r(x)=\dfrac{p(x)}{q(x)}$, where $p$ and $q$ are polynomials of degrees $m$ and $n$ with leading coefficients $a\ne0$ and $b\ne0$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that if $m<n$, then $r(x)\to0$ as $x\to\pm\infty$, so $y=0$ is a horizontal asymptote.
\item[(b)] Prove that if $m=n$, then $r(x)\to\dfrac ab$ as $x\to\pm\infty$.
\item[(c)] Prove that if $m>n$, then $\left|r(x)\right|\to\infty$ as $x\to\pm\infty$, so there is no horizontal asymptote.
\item[(d)] The function $f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-9}{x-3}$ equals $x+3$ whenever $x\ne3$. Explain why its graph has a hole at $x=3$.
\end{enumerate}
