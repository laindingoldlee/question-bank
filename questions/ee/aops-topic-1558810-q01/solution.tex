Let $x$ ounces of the $3\%$ spice be added. Counting ounces of salt:
\[
0.03x+0.06\cdot175=0.05(x+175)\iff0.03x+10.5=0.05x+8.75\iff1.75=0.02x\iff x=87.5 .
\]
So $\boxed{87.5\text{ ounces}}$ should be added. (Check: $2.625+10.5=13.125$ ounces of salt in $262.5$ ounces, which is $5\%$.)
