Let $p_j(a)$ be the probability of $a$ approvals after $j$ people. Initially $p_1(0)=p_1(1)=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Given $a$ approvals, the next approval probability is $\dfrac{a}{(2a+2)}$. Apply $p_{j+1}(a)=p_j(a)(1-\dfrac{a}{(2a+2)})+\dfrac{p_j(a-1)(a-1)}{(2a)}$, with absent terms zero. After four people, the probabilities of three and four approvals sum to $\dfrac{29}{288}$. Hence $p+q=\boxed{317}$. This recurrence conditions on every possible approval count and includes the fact that zero approvals can never increase.
