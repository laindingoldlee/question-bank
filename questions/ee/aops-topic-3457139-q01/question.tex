In a rally, player N attempts the first shot, then players N and D alternate while play continues. On N's $k$th shot, for $1\le k\le6$, the mutually exclusive probabilities of a clean winner and a miss are $\dfrac{k}{10}$ and $\dfrac{k}{20}$; otherwise play continues. On N's seventh shot, a winner occurs with probability $\dfrac{2}{3}$ and a miss with probability $\dfrac{1}{3}$. D never hits a winner. D chooses forehand or backhand equally: on its first five shots the miss probabilities are $\dfrac{1}{5},\dfrac{2}{5}$; subsequently they are $\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{7}{10}$. Outcomes use these conditional probabilities independently of prior history. Count every attempted shot, including the final shot. Find (a) expected rally length, (b) expected points won by each player in $200$ independent rallies, and (c) expected clean winners by N in those rallies.
