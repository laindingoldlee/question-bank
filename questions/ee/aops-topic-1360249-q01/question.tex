When the sum
\[
\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1\cdot3}{2\cdot4}+\dfrac{1\cdot3\cdot5}{2\cdot4\cdot6}+\cdots+\dfrac{1\cdot3\cdot5\cdots2015}{2\cdot4\cdot6\cdots2016}
\]
is written as a fraction in lowest terms, its denominator is $2^xy$ with $y$ odd. Find $xy$.
