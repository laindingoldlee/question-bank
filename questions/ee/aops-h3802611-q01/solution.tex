The integers cannot all be negative, and a consecutive triple crossing from negative to positive would contain zero. Thus all are positive. The sum $\dfrac{1}{n}+\dfrac{1}{n+1}+\dfrac{1}{n+2}$ strictly decreases for positive integer $n$. At $n=6$ it equals $\dfrac{28+24+21}{168}=\dfrac{73}{168}$, so this is the unique triple. Its sum is $\boxed{21}$.
