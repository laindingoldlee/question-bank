How many of the following $5$ statements are false?
\begin{itemize}
\item Out of any $300$ people, at least $25$ have their birthday in the same month.
\item Out of any $26$ words, at least $5$ start with a vowel.
\item Out of any $1000$ people, at least $3$ have the same birthday (ignoring the year).
\item Among any $5$ points inside a square of side length $2$, there are two points at distance at most $\sqrt2$ from each other.
\item Among any $30$ different dates in one calendar year, there are two that are at most $12$ days apart.
\end{itemize}
