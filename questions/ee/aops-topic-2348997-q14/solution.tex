\begin{itemize}
\item True: $300=12\cdot25$, so by the pigeonhole principle some month has at least $25$ birthdays.
\item False: all $26$ words could start with consonants.
\item True: there are at most $366$ possible birthdays, and $2\cdot366=732<1000$, so some birthday is shared by at least $3$ people.
\item True: cut the square into four $1\times1$ squares; two of the points lie in the same small square, at distance at most its diagonal $\sqrt2$.
\item True: list the dates in order. The $29$ gaps between consecutive dates add up to at most $365$ days, and $29\cdot13=377>365$, so some gap is at most $12$ days.
\end{itemize}
Exactly $\boxed{1}$ statement is false.
