If a typical nondelayed flight has mean $p$ passengers, the delayed and nondelayed passenger totals are proportional to $(\dfrac{2}{3})(2p)$ and $(\dfrac{1}{3})p$. Thus the delayed fraction is $\dfrac{4}{4+1}=\boxed{\dfrac{4}{5}}$.
