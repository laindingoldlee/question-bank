Let $a$, $b$, $c$ be positive integers with $a^2+b^2=c^2$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show by an example that $b$ need not be divisible by $4$.
\item[(b)] Prove that at least one of $a$ and $b$ is divisible by $4$.
\end{enumerate}
