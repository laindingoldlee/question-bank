Six different moons (silver, gold, bronze, adamantine, selenium and a sixth moon) are to be arranged in a row. The silver moon may not be next to the gold moon or the bronze moon. The adamantine moon may not be next to the selenium moon, nor next to any moon that is next to the selenium moon. Also, $6$ million identical asteroids are to be shared among the six moons so that each moon gets at least $1$ million. In how many ways can the moons be arranged and the asteroids shared?
