Let the depth be $d$ feet. The stone takes $t_1$ seconds to fall, where $16t_1^2=d$, so $t_1=\dfrac{\sqrt d}{4}$; the sound takes $t_2=\dfrac{d}{1120}$ seconds to come back up. Put $u=\sqrt d>0$. Then
\[\dfrac {u}{4}+\dfrac{u^2}{1120}=8.9\iff u^2+280u-9968=0 .\]
Since $280^2+4\cdot9968=118272=256\cdot462$,
\[u=\dfrac{-280+16\sqrt{462}}{2}=-140+8\sqrt{462}\approx31.954 .\]
So $d=u^2\approx1021.03$, and the depth is about $\boxed{1021}$ feet. (Check: the fall takes about $7.989$ seconds and the sound about $0.912$ seconds, $8.9$ seconds in all.)
