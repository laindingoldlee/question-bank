A stone is dropped into a well, and the splash is heard $8.9$ seconds after it is dropped. The stone falls $16t^2$ feet in $t$ seconds, and the speed of sound is $1120$ feet per second. How many feet is it from the top of the well to the surface of the water? Express your answer to the nearest whole number.
