If the birth year is $\overline{19ab}$, then $2019-(1900+10a+b)=1+9+a+b$, i.e.\ $119-10a-b=10+a+b$, so $11a+2b=109$. Since $2b\le18$, $11a\ge91$, so $a=9$ and then $b=5$: birth year $1995$. If the birth year is $\overline{200b}$ with $b\le8$, then $19-b=2+b$ gives $2b=17$, impossible. So you were born in $1995$ and your age is $\boxed{24}$. (Check: $1+9+9+5=24$.)
