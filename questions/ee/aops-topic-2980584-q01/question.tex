An equilateral triangle has side length $8$. Joining the midpoints of its sides gives a smaller equilateral triangle of side $4$; repeating, the $n$th triangle ($n=0,1,2,\dots$) has side $\dfrac8{2^n}$. Using the (scaled) area values $A_0=32$, $A_1=8$, $A_2=2,\dots$, which are proportional to the true areas, find a formula for $A_n$ and the total $A_0+A_1+A_2+\cdots$.
