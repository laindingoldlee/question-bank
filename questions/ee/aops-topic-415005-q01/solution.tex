$\dfrac54=1\dfrac14$. Now $\dfrac{10}{8}=\dfrac54$, $\dfrac{3}{12}=\dfrac14$ and $\dfrac{10}{40}=\dfrac14$, but $1\dfrac15=\dfrac65\ne\dfrac54$. The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(D) }1\dfrac15}$.
