The outer bracket is positive, so $3+\left[2+(1+x^2)^2\right]^2=12$, giving $\left[2+(1+x^2)^2\right]^2=9$, so $2+(1+x^2)^2=3$ (it is positive), $(1+x^2)^2=1$ and $1+x^2=1$. Hence $x=\boxed{0}$.
