A regular pentagon of side $s$ has area $\dfrac{5s^2\cot36^\circ}{4}$. Thus $\boxed{s=\sqrt{(\dfrac{8048}{5})\tan36^\circ}}\approx34.20$. The area formula follows by splitting it into five isosceles triangles, each with base $s$ and altitude $\dfrac{s\cot36^\circ}{2}$.

\end{asy}
