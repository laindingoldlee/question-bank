Each child has $24$ equally likely sex-month outcomes. Of $24^2$ ordered pairs, $24^2-23^2=47$ meet the condition. If both are boys there are $12^2$ ordered month pairs, of which $12^2-11^2=23$ have a February birthday. Thus the conditional probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{23}{47}}$.
