Apple always loved eating watermelons. Each normal watermelon has exactly $13$ black seeds and $25$ brown seeds, whereas each strange watermelon has $45$ black seeds and $2$ brown seeds. If Apple bought $14$ normal watermelons and $7$ strange watermelons, then let $a$ be the total number of black seeds and $b$ be the total number of brown seeds. What is $a - b$?
