The coefficients are $c_k=\dbinom{12}{k}5^{12-k}(-7)^k$ for $k=0,\dots,12$; they are positive for even $k$ and negative for odd $k$, so the greatest one has even $k$. Compare consecutive even terms:
\[
\dfrac{c_{k+2}}{c_k}=\dfrac{(12-k)(11-k)}{(k+1)(k+2)}\cdot\dfrac{49}{25}.
\]
This ratio is greater than $1$ for $k=0,2,4,6$ (for $k=6$: $\dfrac{6\cdot5}{7\cdot8}\cdot\dfrac{49}{25}=\dfrac{1470}{1400}>1$) and less than $1$ for $k=8$ ($\dfrac{4\cdot3}{9\cdot10}\cdot\dfrac{49}{25}<1$) and beyond, since it decreases as $k$ grows. So the maximum is at $k=8$:
\[
c_8=\dbinom{12}{8}5^4\,7^8=495\cdot625\cdot5764801=\boxed{1783485309375}.
\]
(The coefficient of largest absolute value is $c_7=-\dbinom{12}{7}5^5\,7^7$, which is negative.)
