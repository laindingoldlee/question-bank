Let $S=\{n,n+1,n+2,\dots,n+9\}$, where $n$ is an integer. Two of the integers in $S$ are each multiplied by $10$, and the sum of the ten resulting numbers is $100$. What is the greatest possible value of the sum of the original elements of $S$?
