The subsets of $\{25,10,5\}$ have the sums $0,5,10,15,25,30,35,40$, all different multiples of $5$. Adding the penny or not turns each into two amounts, $s$ and $s+1$; since all the $s$ are multiples of $5$, the $16$ amounts
\[
0,1,5,6,10,11,15,16,25,26,30,31,35,36,40,41
\]
are all different. Excluding $0$ (no coins), there are $\boxed{15}$ amounts. In other words, every one of the $2^4-1=15$ nonempty sets of coins gives a different total.
