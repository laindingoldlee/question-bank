Brad bicycles from home at an average speed of $9$ miles per hour until he gets a flat tire. He then walks his bike back home by the same route at $3$ miles per hour. The whole round trip takes $6$ hours. What was his average speed for the round trip, in miles per hour? Express your answer as a decimal to the nearest tenth.
