Let the one-way distance be $d$. Then $\dfrac {d}{9}+\dfrac {d}{3}=6$, so $\dfrac{4d}{9}=6$ and $d=13.5$ miles. The total distance is $27$ miles in $6$ hours, so the average speed is $\dfrac{27}{6}=\boxed{4.5}$ miles per hour. (It is the harmonic mean of $9$ and $3$, not their ordinary average $6$.)
