$\sin\theta=\sqrt k$ or $\sin\theta=-\sqrt k$, with $0<\sqrt k<1$. Each equation $\sin\theta=s$ with $0<|s|<1$ has exactly two solutions in $[0,2\pi)$. So there are $\boxed{4}$ solutions.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph; real f(real t){return sin(t)^2;} draw(graph(f,0,2pi),blue); draw((0,0)--(6.6,0),Arrow); draw((0,0)--(0,1.2),Arrow); draw((0,0.4)--(2pi,0.4),red+dashed); label("$k$",(0,0.4),W);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
