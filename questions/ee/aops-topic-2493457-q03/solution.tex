The paint is spread equally over the $6$ faces, so each face has $\dfrac{300}{6}=50$ square feet of green border. Each face has area $10^2=100$ square feet, so the white square on it has area $100-50=\boxed{50}$ square feet (its side is $5\sqrt2\approx7.07$ feet, which fits inside the face).

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=sqrt(50), m=(10-s)/2;
fill(box((0,0),(10,10)),rgb(0.6,0.85,0.6));
fill(box((m,m),(m+s,m+s)),white);
draw(box((0,0),(10,10)),linewidth(0.8));
draw(box((m,m),(m+s,m+s)),linewidth(0.8));
label("$10$",(5,0),S); label("$10$",(10,5),E);
label("$5\sqrt2$",(5,m),N,fontsize(9pt)); label("white",(5,5));
label("green",(5,m/2),fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}
