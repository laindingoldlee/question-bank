We use induction on $n$. For $n=1$ both sides equal $1$.

Suppose the formula holds for $n$. Then
\[
\sum_{k=1}^{n+1}(-1)^{k-1}k^2=(-1)^{n-1}\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}+(-1)^{n}(n+1)^2=(-1)^{n}(n+1)\left((n+1)-\dfrac {n}{2}\right)=(-1)^{n}\dfrac{(n+1)(n+2)}{2},
\]
which is the formula for $n+1$. This completes the induction. $\blacksquare$

(Directly: pairing $(2j-1)^2-(2j)^2=-(4j-1)$ gives, for even $n=2m$, $-\sum_{j=1}^m(4j-1)=-m(2m+1)=-\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$.)
