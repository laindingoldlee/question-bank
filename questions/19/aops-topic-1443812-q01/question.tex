A water container stands on its circular bottom and tapers linearly (it is a frustum of a cone) to a circular top whose radius is one-half the radius of the bottom. A mark halfway up the side says ``200 gallons'', meaning that the container holds $200$ gallons when filled up to the mark. How many gallons, to the nearest tenth, does the full container hold?
