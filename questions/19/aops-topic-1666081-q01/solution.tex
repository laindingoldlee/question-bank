\textbf{At least two new sizes.} An order of $2$ nuggets must be a single pack of $2$, because every pack has at least $2$ nuggets and the old packs are bigger than $2$. An order of $3$ nuggets must be a single pack of $3$, because two or more packs contain at least $4$ nuggets. So the packs of $2$ and $3$ must both be added, and $x\ge2$.

\textbf{Two are enough.} With packs of $2$ and $3$ added, every even $n\ge2$ is $\dfrac {n}{2}$ packs of $2$, and every odd $n\ge3$ is one pack of $3$ and $\dfrac{n-3}{2}$ packs of $2$.

So the smallest value is $x=\boxed{2}$.
