Imagine playing four games even if the match has already ended. Kevin wins the match exactly when at least two of these four games are his wins. Hence his winning probability is $1-\dfrac{\binom40+\binom41}{2^4}=\dfrac{11}{16}$. Thus $a+b=\boxed{27}$.
