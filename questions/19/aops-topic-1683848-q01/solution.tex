A common year has $365$ days and a leap year $366$. In seven consecutive years there are $7\cdot365+L=2555+L$ days, where $L$ is the number of leap years among them. Usually $L$ is $1$ or $2$ (leap years are $4$ years apart), and $L=0$ is possible only across a century year that is not a leap year, such as $1897$--$1903$. In every case $0\le L\le2<7$, and $2555=7\cdot365$, so the number of full weeks is
\[
\left\lfloor\dfrac{2555+L}{7}\right\rfloor=\boxed{365}.
\]
(The day of the week on which the first year starts does not matter.)
