Consider integer sequences with these two properties: (i) the first two terms are given; (ii) every later term is the remainder when the product of the two previous terms is divided by $9$. For how many choices of the first two terms $a_1,a_2\in\{0,1,2,\dots,8\}$ is the $2018$th term equal to $1$? List them.
