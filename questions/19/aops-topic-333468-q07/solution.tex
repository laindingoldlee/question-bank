Split the range.
\begin{itemize}
\item $1$ to $9$: $1+2+\cdots+9=45$.
\item $10$ to $99$: each tens digit $1,\dots,9$ appears $10$ times, contributing $10\cdot45=450$; each units digit $0,\dots,9$ appears $9$ times, contributing $9\cdot45=405$. Total $855$.
\item $100$ to $110$: the hundreds digit $1$ appears $11$ times ($11$); the tens digits are $0$ except in $110$ ($1$); the units digits are $0,1,\dots,9$ and $0$ ($45$). Total $57$.
\end{itemize}
The sum is $45+855+57=\boxed{957}$.
