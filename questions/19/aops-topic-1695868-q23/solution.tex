Look at the tosses in pairs, starting from a tie. After two tosses the game is over if they were HH (Xena wins, probability $\dfrac{3}{4}\cdot\dfrac{3}{4}=\dfrac{9}{16}$) or TT (Yolanda wins, probability $\dfrac{1}{16}$); otherwise (HT or TH, probability $\dfrac{6}{16}=\dfrac{3}{8}$) the score is tied again and the game starts afresh. (The game cannot end after an odd number of tosses, since the lead after an odd number of tosses is odd.) So
\[
P(\text{Xena})=\dfrac{9}{16}+\dfrac{3}{8}\cdot\dfrac{9}{16}+\left(\dfrac{3}{8}\right)^2\dfrac{9}{16}+\cdots=\dfrac{\dfrac{9}{16}}{1-\dfrac{3}{8}}=\boxed{\dfrac{9}{10}}.
\]
This is the geometric series the poster was looking for, with ratio $\dfrac{3}{8}$. Equivalently, the game is decided by the first non-tying pair, which is HH with probability $\dfrac{9}{9+1}$.
