Xena and Yolanda toss an unfair coin that lands heads up $75\%$ of the time. If the coin lands heads up, Xena gets a point; otherwise, Yolanda gets a point. The game ends when one player has a two-point lead over the other, and that player is the winner. What is the probability that Xena wins the game? Express your answer as a common fraction.
