Decompose the union into the disjoint sets $A\setminus B$, $A\cap B$, and $B\setminus A$. In $P(A)+P(B)$, the first and third are counted once but the intersection is counted twice. Subtracting one copy of $P(A\cap B)$ counts each of the three pieces exactly once, giving the stated identity.
