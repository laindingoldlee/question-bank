Let triangle $ABC$ have its right angle at $A$, and let $M$ be the midpoint of $BC$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(5,0),C=(0,3.2),M=(B+C)/2;
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(1)); draw(circle(M,abs(B-C)/2),blue+dashed);
draw(A--M,red); dot(M); draw((0.3,0)--(0.3,0.3)--(0,0.3));
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,N);label("$M$",M,NE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Complete the triangle to the rectangle $ABDC$ (with $D=B+C-A$). The diagonals of a rectangle are equal and bisect each other, so $M$ is also the midpoint of $AD$ and $MA=\dfrac{1}{2}AD=\dfrac{1}{2}BC=MB=MC$. Thus $M$ is equidistant from $A$, $B$, $C$, so it is the circumcentre, and the circumcircle has $BC$ as a diameter. $\blacksquare$
