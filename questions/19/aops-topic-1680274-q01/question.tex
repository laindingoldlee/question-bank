A character starts at $(0,0)$ in the coordinate plane and wants to reach $(1,1)$. Each move goes either north or east by any positive distance. Prove that every route made of finitely many such moves has length exactly $2$. Then explain why the ``staircase'' routes, which get closer and closer to the diagonal segment of length $\sqrt2$, do not show that the minimum length is $\sqrt2$.
