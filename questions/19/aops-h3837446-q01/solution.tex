Write $x=ny+r$, where $n=\left\lfloor\dfrac{x}{y}\right\rfloor$ and $0\le r<y$. The equation becomes
\[
(3n+17)y+3r=20n,\qquad n(20-3y)=17y+3r.
\]
Its positive left original expression forces $n>0$, and $(3n+17)y\le20n$ gives $y<\dfrac{20}{3}$. Hence only $y=1,\ldots,6$ need checking.

For each such $y$, require $n=\dfrac{17y+3r}{20-3y}$ to be an integer with $0\le r<y$. The successful triples $(y,r,n)$ are
\[
(1,0,1),\ (5,0,17),\ (6,0,51),\ (6,2,54),\ (6,4,57).
\]
Thus the pairs $(x,y)$ are $(1,1),(85,5),(306,6),(326,6),(346,6)$, and the count is $\boxed5$.
