The regular star $S(n,k)$ is formed by joining every $k$th of $n$ equally spaced points on a circle ($1<k<\dfrac n2$, $\gcd(n,k)=1$). Show that the sum of its $n$ tip angles is $180^\circ(n-2k)$, and find the sum for the two regular $7$-pointed stars $S(7,2)$ and $S(7,3)$.
