From February $7$, $1812$ to February $7$, $2012$ is $200$ years. Leap days in between: years $1816,1820,\dots,2012$ divisible by $4$ number $50$; remove $1900$ (not a leap year), giving $49$. Total days $=200\cdot365+49$, and $365\equiv1\pmod7$, so the shift is $200+49=249\equiv4\pmod7$. Going back $4$ days from Tuesday gives $\boxed{\text{Friday}}$.
