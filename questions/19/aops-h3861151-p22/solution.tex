Write $a_i=iq_i+r_i$, with $q_i\ge 0$ and $0\le r_i<i$. Then the condition becomes $\sum_{i=1}^{2025}q_i=2025$. Stars and bars gives $\binom{4049}{2024}$ choices of the quotients. Independently the remainders have $1\cdot 2\cdots 2025=2025!$ choices. Thus the answer is $\boxed{2025!\binom{4049}{2024}}$.
