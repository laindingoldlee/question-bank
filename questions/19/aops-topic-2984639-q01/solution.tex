Since $\dfrac56<3\cdot\dfrac1a$, we get $a<\dfrac{18}5$, and $a\ge2$ (as $a=1$ exceeds $\dfrac56$). So $a\in\{2,3\}$.

\emph{$a=2$:} $\dfrac1b+\dfrac1c=\dfrac13$, i.e. $(b-3)(c-3)=9$ with $3<b<c$: $b-3=1$, $c-3=9$, giving $(4,12)$.

\emph{$a=3$:} $\dfrac1b+\dfrac1c=\dfrac12$, i.e. $(b-2)(c-2)=4$ with $3<b<c$: the factor pairs $(1,4),(2,2)$ give $b=3$ or $b=c=4$, neither allowed.

The only representation is $\boxed{\dfrac56=\dfrac12+\dfrac14+\dfrac1{12}}$.
