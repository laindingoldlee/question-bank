The original cube's faces are all covered. Each of the $6$ new cubes shows its other $5$ faces. No two of these lie in a common plane with a common edge: a new cube's side faces are perpendicular to the faces of its neighbouring new cubes. So the solid has $6\cdot5=\boxed{30}$ faces.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import three;currentprojection=orthographic(4,3,2);draw(unitcube,white,black);draw(shift(0,0,1)*unitcube,white,black);draw(shift(1,0,0)*unitcube,white,black);draw(shift(0,1,0)*unitcube,white,black);
\end{asy}
