\textbf{No.} The line divides the plane into two half-planes, and no vertex lies on it. A segment crosses the line iff its endpoints are in opposite half-planes. Travelling once around the closed path, we would switch sides $11$ times and return to the starting vertex. But returning to the starting side requires an even number of switches, a contradiction. $\boxed{\text{Impossible}}$ (for any odd number of segments).
