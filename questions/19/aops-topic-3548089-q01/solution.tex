With zero or five green beads there is one bracelet each. With one or four green beads there is again one each. With two green beads, their circular separation is either 1 or 2, giving two bracelets. Complementing colors gives two bracelets with three green beads. The total is $1+1+2+2+1+1=\boxed 8$.
