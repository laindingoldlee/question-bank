Since $1800=2^3\cdot3^2\cdot5^2$, every positive divisor is $2^a3^b5^c$ with $0\le a\le3$, $0\le b\le2$, $0\le c\le2$, and expanding
\[\left(1+2+4+8\right)\left(1+3+9\right)\left(1+5+25\right)\]
gives each divisor exactly once. So the sum of the divisors is
\[15\cdot13\cdot31=\boxed{6045}.\]
