The first part takes $\dfrac{15}{30}=\dfrac12$ hour. If he drives $d$ more miles, they take $\dfrac{d}{55}$ hour, and we need
\[\dfrac{15+d}{\dfrac12+\dfrac{d}{55}}=50\iff15+d=25+\dfrac{50d}{55}\iff d-\dfrac{10d}{11}=10\iff\dfrac{d}{11}=10.\]
So he must drive $\boxed{110}$ more miles. (Check: $125$ miles in $\dfrac12+2=\dfrac52$ hours is $50$ miles per hour.)
