$n\ge2$ players sit around a circle. Player $i$ initially has $k+n-i$ coins, where $k\ge1$ is an integer. In order, player $1$ gives one coin to player $2$, player $2$ gives two to player $3$, and so on cyclically; the amount to transfer increases by one each turn. Stop before the first transfer a player cannot afford. If two cyclically adjacent players then have positive amounts with one exactly five times the other, find every possible initial total.
