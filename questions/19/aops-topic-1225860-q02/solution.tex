Rewrite: $x^4+2x^2+1=x+6\sqrt x+9$, i.e. $(x^2+1)^2=(\sqrt x+3)^2$. Both bases are positive, so $x^2+1=\sqrt x+3$ and $x^2-\sqrt x=\boxed{2}$. (Such $x$ exists: $x^2-\sqrt x$ goes from $0$ at $x=1$ to $4-\sqrt2>2$ at $x=2$.)
