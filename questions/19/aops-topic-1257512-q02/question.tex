In triangle $ABC$, $AB=13$, $AC=14$ and $BC=15$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of $AC$. Point $P$ lies on segment $BM$ with $AP\perp PC$. The area of triangle $APC$ can be written as $\dfrac{p\sqrt q}{r}$, where $p,q,r$ are positive integers, $p$ and $r$ are relatively prime and $q$ is squarefree. Find $p+q+r$.
