If $P$ has degree $d\ge2$, the composition has degree $d^2$; a linear $P$ would give degree at most one. Hence $d=2$. Write $P(x)=ax^2+bx+c$. Comparing leading coefficients gives $a^3=1$, so $a=1$. The cubic coefficient is $2(b+1)=-6$, giving $b=-4$. The quadratic coefficient is then $9+2c-4=7$, so $c=1$.

Substituting $P(x)=x^2-4x+1$ verifies the complete given identity. Therefore $\boxed{P(25)=625-100+1=526}$.
