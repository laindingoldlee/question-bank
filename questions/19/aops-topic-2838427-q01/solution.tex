Let $B_k$ be the balance after the $k$th payment. Then $B_1=50000-1000$ and $B_{k+1}=1.02B_k-1000$. Unrolling,
\[B_{10}=50000(1.02)^9-1000\sum_{j=0}^{9}(1.02)^j=50000(1.02)^9-1000\cdot\dfrac{1.02^{10}-1}{0.02}\approx\boxed{48804.91}\text{ dollars}.\]
