There are $4\cdot6\cdot8\cdot10\cdot12\cdot20=460800$ equally likely outcomes. Counting the outcomes with sum at least $41$ (by multiplying the generating functions $\prod_s(x+\cdots+x^s)$ and adding the coefficients of $x^{41},\dots,x^{60}$, done by computer) gives $84427$. The probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{84427}{460800}}\approx0.183$.
