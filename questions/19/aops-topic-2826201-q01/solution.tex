Let $o_i$ be the amounts before and $n_i$ after. Friend $A_i$ keeps $\dfrac{o_i}2$ and receives $\dfrac{o_{i+1}}2$ from $A_{i+1}$, so $n_i=\dfrac{o_i+o_{i+1}}2$.

Take the alternating sum around the even cycle:
\[\sum_{i=1}^{100}(-1)^{i+1}n_i=\dfrac12\left(\sum(-1)^{i+1}o_i+\sum(-1)^{i+1}o_{i+1}\right)=\dfrac12\left(S-S\right)=0,\]
where $S=\sum(-1)^{i+1}o_i$ (shifting the index by one flips every sign). Hence
\[n_{100}=(n_1+n_3+\cdots+n_{99})-(n_2+\cdots+n_{98})=50\cdot2020-49\cdot2021=\boxed{1971}.\]
