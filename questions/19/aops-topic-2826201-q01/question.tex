$100$ friends $A_1,A_2,\dots,A_{100}$ sit around a round table, with $A_{i+1}$ to the left of $A_i$ (indices mod $100$). Each has a jar of candies. At the same moment, every friend moves half of the candies in their jar to the jar of the friend on their right. Afterwards, $A_1,A_3,\dots,A_{99}$ each have $2020$ candies and $A_2,A_4,\dots,A_{98}$ each have $2021$ candies. How many candies does $A_{100}$ have now?
