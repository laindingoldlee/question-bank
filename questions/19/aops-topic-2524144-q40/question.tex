Ben drives $24$ miles along one highway and then $10$ miles along a perpendicular highway to a rest stop, at $60$ miles per hour throughout. A straight road leads directly from his starting point to the rest stop. At what constant speed would he have to drive along the straight road to take the same time? Round to the nearest mile per hour.
