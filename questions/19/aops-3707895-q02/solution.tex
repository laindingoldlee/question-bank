Use $A=(0,0)$, $B=(1,0)$, $C=(1,1)$, $D=(0,1)$. Then $B^{\prime}=(\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2},\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2})$ and line $B^{\prime}C^{\prime}$ has equation $x+y=\sqrt2$. Thus $E=(\sqrt2-1,1)$. The shoelace formula gives area $\dfrac{1}{2}(\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}+\sqrt2-1-\dfrac{\sqrt2-1}{\sqrt2})=\boxed{\sqrt2-1}$.
\begin{asy}
import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(1,0),C=(1,1),D=(0,1),P=dir(45),Q=(0,sqrt(2)),R=dir(135),E=(sqrt(2)-1,1);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--P--Q--R--cycle,blue);draw(A--C,dashed);label("$A$",A,(0,-1));label("$B$",B,(1,-1));label("$C$",C,(1,1));label("$D$",D,(-1,0));label("$B^{\prime}$",P,(1,0));label("$C^{\prime}$",Q,(0,1));label("$D^{\prime}$",R,(-1,0));label("$E$",E,(0,1));
\end{asy}
