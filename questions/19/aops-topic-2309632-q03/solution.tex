The $n$th triangular number is $T_n=\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$. Since $7$ is an odd prime, $7\mid T_n$ exactly when $7\mid n(n+1)$, i.e. when $n\equiv0$ or $n\equiv6\pmod7$. Among $n=1,\dots,100$ there are $14$ multiples of $7$ ($7,\dots,98$) and $14$ numbers $\equiv6\pmod7$ ($6,13,\dots,97$). So $28$ of the $100$ triangular numbers are divisible by $7$, a fraction of
\[\dfrac{28}{100}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{25}}.\]
