Triangle $MEC$ has a right angle at $E$ and $\angle MCE=45^\circ$ (the diagonal bisects the right angle at $C$). So it is an isosceles right triangle with hypotenuse $MC=14$, and $EC=\dfrac{14}{\sqrt2}=7\sqrt2$. Hence
\[AE=AC-EC=28\sqrt2-7\sqrt2=21\sqrt2,\]
and $a=\boxed{21}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(28,0),C=(28,28),D=(0,28),M=(14,28),Ep=(21,21);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.8));draw(A--C);draw(M--Ep,dashed);
real k=1.6;pair u=dir(45),v=dir(135);draw(Ep+k*u--Ep+k*u+k*v--Ep+k*v);
dot(M);dot(Ep);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$M$",M,N);label("$E$",Ep,SE);
label("$14$",(M+C)/2,N);label("$28$",(A+B)/2,S);
\end{asy}

(In coordinates with $A=(0,0)$ and $C=(28,28)$, the projection of $M=(14,28)$ onto the line $y=x$ is $E=(21,21)$.)
