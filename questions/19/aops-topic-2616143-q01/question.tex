Square $ABCD$ has side length $28$, and $M$ is the midpoint of $\overline{CD}$. Let $E$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $M$ to the diagonal $\overline{AC}$. If $AE=a\sqrt2$ for an integer $a$, find $a$.
