The two zeros give $ y=a(x-13)(x-3)$. Substituting $(10,1.4)$ gives $ 1.4=-21a $, so $ a=-\dfrac{1}{15}$. Therefore $\boxed{y=-\dfrac{(x-13)(x-3)}{15}}$.

\end{asy}
