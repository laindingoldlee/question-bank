There are $ 300 $ boys and $ 200 $ girls. If $ g $ girls choose 3, then $\dfrac{g}{200}=\dfrac{27}{20}\dfrac{b}{300}$, so $ 10g=9b $. Coprimality forces $ b=10k,g=9k $ for an integer $ k\ge1 $. The constraints $ b\le300,g\le200 $ give $ k\le22 $. Thus $\boxed{b\in\{10,20,\ldots,220\}}$, and all these choices are feasible. The supplied information does not determine a unique count.
