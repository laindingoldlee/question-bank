\(\dbinom54p^4(1-p)=\dbinom53p^3(1-p)^2\), i.e.\ \(5p^4(1-p)=10p^3(1-p)^2\). Dividing by \(5p^3(1-p)\ne 0\) gives \(p=2(1-p)\), so \(p=\dfrac{2}{3}\) and \(m+n=\boxed{5}\).
