Collect the terms in $x$:
\[
a^2x-abx-2x=-2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x\left(a^2-ab-2\right)=-2 .
\]
If $a^2-ab\ne2$, then
\[
\boxed{x=\dfrac{2}{2+ab-a^2}} .
\]
If $a^2-ab=2$, the equation reads $0=-2$, so there is no solution.
