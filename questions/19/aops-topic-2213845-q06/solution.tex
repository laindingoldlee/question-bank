We choose an unordered set of $3$ of the $50$ boys:
\[\dbinom{50}{3}=\dfrac{50\cdot49\cdot48}{6}=\boxed{19\,600}.\]
(If the three captains had different roles, the answer would be $50\cdot49\cdot48=117\,600$.)
