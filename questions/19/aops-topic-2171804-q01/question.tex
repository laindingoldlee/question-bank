Let $a$, $b$, $c$ be three different single-digit positive integers. The maximum possible value of the sum of the two solutions of
\[(x-a)(x-b)+(x-b)(x-c)=0\]
can be written as $\dfrac {p}{q}$, where $p$ and $q$ are relatively prime positive integers. What is $p+q$?
