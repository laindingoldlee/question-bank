The taps repeat in blocks of $8$:
\[
\underbrace{T,\ I,\ M,\ R,\ P,\ R,\ M,\ I}_{\text{taps }1\text{--}8},\ \underbrace{T,\ I,\ M,\ R,\ P,\ R,\ M,\ I}_{\text{taps }9\text{--}16},\ \dots
\]
(thumb $T$, index $I$, middle $M$, ring $R$, pinky $P$). Indeed tap $9$ is the thumb and tap $10$ the index finger, which starts the pattern again. Since $141=8\cdot17+5$, tap $141$ is the same finger as tap $5$: the $\boxed{\text{pinky}}$.
