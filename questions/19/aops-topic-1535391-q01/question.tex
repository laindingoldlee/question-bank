Ray taps his fingers while waiting in a queue. First he puts his thumb down, then his index finger, third his middle finger, fourth his ring finger and fifth his pinky. Then he reverses order, with his ring finger 6th, middle 7th, index 8th and thumb 9th. Then he starts over again with his index finger 10th, and continues in this way. Ray taps his fingers $141$ times. What was the $141$st finger that he tapped?
