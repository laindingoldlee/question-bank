The line passes through the center $(0,0)$ of the circle. Its chord is therefore a diameter, whose midpoint is the center. The answer is $\boxed{(0,0)}$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(220);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair O=(0,0),Y=12*unit((7,1)),Z=-Y;draw(circle(O,12));draw(-1.15*Y--1.15*Y);dot(O);label("$O(0,0)$",O,(0,-1));label("$Y$",Y,(1,1));label("$Z$",Z,(-1,-1));
\end{asy}
