There are $2005$ powers, from $5^0$ through $5^{2004}$. Their digit lengths range through every integer from $1$ to $1401$: multiplication by $5$ increases the digit length by at most one. A fixed digit length contains at most two powers, since a factor $25$ exceeds a decade.

If a decade contains two powers, they are $u,5u$, and necessarily $10^r\le u<2\cdot10^r$; hence precisely its first power starts with $1$. Conversely, a power starting with $1$ has its next power in the same decade, except possibly at the end of our list. The last power starts with $5$, so this exception does not occur. Consequently the desired count is the number of two-power decades, namely $2005-1401=\boxed{604}$.
