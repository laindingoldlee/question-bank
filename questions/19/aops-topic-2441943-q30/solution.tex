Wheel $A$ makes $30$ revolutions per minute, so $30\cdot10=300$ teeth pass each point of contact per minute. Meshing wheels move the same number of teeth past their contact point, so wheel $B$ turns $\dfrac{300}{6}=\boxed{50}$ times per minute. (The middle wheel does not affect the answer.)
