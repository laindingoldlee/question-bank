Since $16260\cdot6150=99999000$,
\[
\dfrac{1}{6150}=\dfrac{16260}{99999000}=\dfrac{1}{1000}\cdot\dfrac{16260}{99999}=0.000\overline{16260}.
\]
(Indeed $\dfrac{16260}{99999}=0.\overline{16260}$, and dividing by $1000$ moves the decimal point three places.) So the digits after the decimal point are $0,0,0$ followed by the block $1,6,2,6,0$ repeated.

The $150$th digit is the $(150-3)$th $=147$th digit of the repeating part. Since $147=5\cdot29+2$, it is the second digit of the block $16260$, namely $\boxed{6}$.
