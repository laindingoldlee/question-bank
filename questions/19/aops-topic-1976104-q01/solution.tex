The great circle has circumference $2\pi r=5280$ ft, so $r=\dfrac{5280}{2\pi}=\dfrac{2640}{\pi}$ ft.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw(circle((0,0),1),linewidth(1));
path eq=scale(1,0.28)*unitcircle;
draw(subpath(eq,0,2),dashed);draw(subpath(eq,2,4),linewidth(1.1));
label("circumference $5280$ ft",(0,-1),S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The surface area is
\[
4\pi r^2=4\pi\cdot\dfrac{2640^2}{\pi^2}=\dfrac{(2\cdot2640)^2}{\pi}=\dfrac{5280^2}{\pi}=\boxed{\dfrac{27878400}{\pi}}\text{ ft}^2\approx8.87\times10^6\text{ ft}^2 .
\]
