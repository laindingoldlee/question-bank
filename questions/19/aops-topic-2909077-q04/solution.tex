$X$ is the midpoint of arc $BC$, so by the incentre--excentre lemma $XI=XB=XC$. The inradius is $ID=7$, so $\sin\dfrac A2=\dfrac7{25}$ and $\cos\dfrac A2=\dfrac{24}{25}$. In isosceles triangle $XBC$, $\angle BXC=180^\circ-A$, so $BC=2XB\cos\dfrac A2$.

Hence $XI=XB=\dfrac{24}{2\cdot\dfrac{24}{25}}=\boxed{\dfrac{25}2}$.
