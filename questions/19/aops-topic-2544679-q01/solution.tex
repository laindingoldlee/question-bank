Multiplying by $5$ and subtracting $5$,
\[x^2-4=\dfrac{2020}{y^2+4}.\]
The left side is an integer and the right side is positive, so $d=y^2+4$ is a divisor of $2020=2^2\cdot5\cdot101$ with $d\ge4$ and $d-4$ a perfect square. The divisors of $2020$ are $1,2,4,5,10,20,101,202,404,505,1010,2020$; subtracting $4$ gives a square only for $d=4,5,20,404$ (giving $0,1,16,400$). Then $x^2=4+\dfrac{2020}{d}$ equals
\[509,\qquad408,\qquad105,\qquad9,\]
respectively, and only $9$ is a square. So $d=404$, $y^2=400$, $x^2=9$, and the solutions are
\[\boxed{(x,y)=(3,20),\ (3,-20),\ (-3,20),\ (-3,-20)}.\]
(Check: $\dfrac{10}{5}=2=1+\dfrac{404}{404}$.)
