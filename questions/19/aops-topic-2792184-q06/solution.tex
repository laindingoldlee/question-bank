Start with any split. While some senator has at least two enemies in his own party, move him to the other party: there he has at most $3-2=1$ enemy. This lowers the number of enemy pairs within the same party by at least $2-1=1$. That number is a non-negative integer, so the process stops, and then every senator has at most one enemy in his party. $\blacksquare$
