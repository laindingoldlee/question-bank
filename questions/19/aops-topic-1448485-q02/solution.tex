Let the roots be $r_1,\dots,r_n$ with $n=2017$, $e_1=\sum r_i$ and $e_2=\sum_{i<j}r_ir_j$. By Vieta, the $x^{2016}$ coefficient is $-e_1$ and the $x^{2015}$ coefficient is $e_2=2017$. Now
\[
e_1^2=\sum r_i^2+2e_2,\qquad\text{and}\qquad \sum r_i^2\ge\dfrac{e_1^2}{n}
\]
by the Cauchy-Schwarz (or QM-AM) inequality. Hence $e_1^2\left(1-\dfrac{1}{n}\right)\ge2e_2$, so
\[
e_1^2\ge\dfrac{2e_2\,n}{n-1}=\dfrac{2\cdot2017\cdot2017}{2016}=\boxed{\dfrac{2017^2}{1008}}=\dfrac{4068289}{1008}\approx4036.001,
\]
with equality when all roots are equal: $r_i=t$ with $\dbinom{2017}{2}t^2=2017$, that is $t^2=\dfrac{1}{1008}$.
