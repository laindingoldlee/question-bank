Let
\[
P=\prod_{k=\sum_{n=1}^{10} n}^{\ \sum_{a=1}^{100} a} k .
\]
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Write $P$ as a quotient of two factorials.
\item[(b)] How many zeros does $P$ end with?
\end{enumerate}
