\textbf{(a)} The limits are $\sum_{n=1}^{10}n=\dfrac{10\cdot11}{2}=55$ and $\sum_{a=1}^{100}a=\dfrac{100\cdot101}{2}=5050$. So $P=55\cdot56\cdots5050$, and
\[
P=\boxed{\dfrac{5050!}{54!}} .
\]

\textbf{(b)} The number of trailing zeros is the smaller of the exponents of $2$ and $5$ in $P$, and the exponent of $5$ is the smaller one (there are more factors $2$ than factors $5$ among $55,\dots,5050$). By Legendre's formula,
\[
v_5(5050!)=\left\lfloor\dfrac{5050}{5}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{5050}{25}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{5050}{125}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{5050}{625}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{5050}{3125}\right\rfloor=1010+202+40+8+1=1261,
\]
\[
v_5(54!)=10+2=12 .
\]
Hence $v_5(P)=1261-12=1249$, and $P$ ends with $\boxed{1249}$ zeros.
