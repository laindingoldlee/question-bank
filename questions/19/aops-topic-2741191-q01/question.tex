Justin has $12$ distinct shirts, $179$ distinct pairs of jeans and $7$ distinct hats. An outfit consists of exactly two shirts, one pair of jeans, and at most two hats (possibly none). The order of the shirts and of the hats does not matter. How many different outfits are possible?
