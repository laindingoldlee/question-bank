Put $x=w+a,y=w+a+b,z=w+a+b+c$, where $a,b,c\ge0$. The equation is $10w+5a+2b+c=100$. For fixed $w,a$, there are $\lfloor\dfrac{100-10w-5a}{2}\rfloor+1$ choices of $b$, each determining $c$. Writing $j=10-w$, the sum over $a=0,\ldots,2j$ is
\[
\sum_{a=0}^{2j}\left(\left\lfloor\dfrac{10j-5a}{2}\right\rfloor+1\right)=5j^2+4j+1.
\]
This follows by separating even and odd $a$. Summing for $j=0,\ldots,10$ gives $5\cdot385+4\cdot55+11=\boxed{2156}$.
