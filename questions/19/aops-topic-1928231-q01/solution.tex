It is not true. Take $a=b=20$, $c=11$, $d=36$ (all greater than $10$). Then
\[
ab=400>396=cd,\qquad\text{but}\qquad a+b=40<47=c+d .
\]
So the answer is $\boxed{\text{no}}$.

The reason: for a fixed sum, the product is largest when the two numbers are equal, since $xy=\left(\dfrac{x+y}{2}\right)^2-\left(\dfrac{x-y}{2}\right)^2$. A balanced pair such as $20,20$ can therefore have a larger product than a lopsided pair such as $11,36$ even though its sum is smaller.
