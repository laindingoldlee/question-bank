Let $u=x+y$ and $v=20x+21y$, so $u^2v=12$ and $uv^2=18$. Multiplying, $(uv)^3=216$, so $uv=6$. Then $u=\dfrac{12}{6}=2$ and $v=\dfrac{18}{6}=3$. Hence
\[21x+20y=41(x+y)-(20x+21y)=82-3=\boxed{79}.\]
(Here $x=39$, $y=-37$.)
