The first pack has $ 3!=6 $ possible orders. For every subsequent pack, its first colour must differ from the preceding pack's last, giving two choices; the remaining colours can appear in either order. Thus each later pack contributes 4 choices, and the total is $ 6\cdot4^3=\boxed{384}$.
