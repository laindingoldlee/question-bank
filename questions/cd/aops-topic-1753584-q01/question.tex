A circle has radius $6$ cm. Two radii $OA$ and $OB$ form a central angle of $120^\circ$, so the minor arc $AB$ measures $120^\circ$. The sector $AOB$ is cut by the chord $AB$ into the triangle $AOB$ and the region between the chord and the minor arc, which is shaded. Find the area of the shaded region.
