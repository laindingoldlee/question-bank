Removing $x$ mL removes $0.2x$ mL of acid. Adding $x$ mL pure acid makes the acid amount $36-0.2x+x=36+0.8x$, while total volume stays $180$. Thus $36+0.8x=54$, giving $\boxed{x=22.5\text{ mL}}$.
