If the opposite-face pairs are $(a,a'),(b,b'),(c,c')$, the eight vertex products sum to $(a+a')(b+b')(c+c')=385=5\cdot7\cdot11$. Each pair sum is at least three, so each must receive one of these prime factors; the pair sums are $5,7,11$. Their total, the sum of all six face numbers, is $\boxed{23}$.
Pairs summing to five are $(1,4),(2,3)$; those summing to seven are $(1,6),(2,5),(3,4)$; those summing to eleven are $(1,10),(2,9),(3,8),(4,7),(5,6)$. Requiring all six entries to be distinct leaves exactly
\[\boxed{\{(1,4),(2,5),(3,8)\}\quad\text{or}\quad\{(2,3),(1,6),(4,7)\}}.\]
Each pairing indeed makes the product of pair sums $385$.
