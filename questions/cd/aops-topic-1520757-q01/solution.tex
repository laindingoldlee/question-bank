\textbf{Step 1.} $16=2\cdot7+2$, so $16\equiv2\pmod7$.

\textbf{Step 2.} Then $16^3\equiv2^3=8\equiv1\pmod7$.

\textbf{Step 3.} The exponent $1889$ is a typo for $1989=3\cdot663$, the largest multiple of $3$ not exceeding $1991$. So
\[
16^{1989}=\left(16^3\right)^{663}\equiv1^{663}=1\pmod7 .
\]
($16^{1889}$ is not $\equiv1$: $1889=3\cdot629+2$, so $16^{1889}\equiv16^2\equiv4$.)

\textbf{Step 4.} Finally $16^{1991}=16^{1989}\cdot16^2\equiv1\cdot2^2=4\pmod7$. The remainder is $\boxed{4}$.
