$\overline{abcba}$ has digit sum $2a+2b+c=7$ with $a\ge1$, so $c$ is odd. $c=1$: $a+b=3$ gives $3$ options; $c=3$: $a+b=2$ gives $2$; $c=5$: $a+b=1$ gives $1$; $c=7$: $a+b=0$ is impossible with $a\ge1$. Total $\boxed{6}$.
