All $\binom{12}{5}=792$ purchases are equally likely. Purchases missing rap, country, or metal number $\binom85=56$, $\binom75=21$, and $\binom95=126$, respectively. A purchase missing both rap and metal must contain all five country CDs, so there is one double-counted purchase. No other purchase can miss two categories, since rap and metal each have fewer than five CDs. Hence the valid count is $792-56-21-126+1=590$, and the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{295}{396}}$.
