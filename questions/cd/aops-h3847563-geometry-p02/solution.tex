Draw a segment $AB$. Draw circles centered at $A$ and $B$, each of radius $AB$, and let $C$ be either intersection. Then $AB=AC=BC$, so triangle $ABC$ is equilateral and $\angle CAB=60^\circ$. The two choices of $C$ give reflected constructions. 
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(220);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(2,0),C=(1,sqrt(3)),D=(1,-sqrt(3));draw(circle(A,2));draw(circle(B,2));draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--D--B,dashed);label("$A$",A,(-1,0));label("$B$",B,(1,0));label("$C$",C,(0,1));label("$C_2$",D,(0,-1));
\end{asy}
