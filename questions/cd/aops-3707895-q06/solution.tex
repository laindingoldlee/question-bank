Let $\angle CAB=\alpha$. Ordered similarity gives $\angle DBC=\alpha$ and $\angle BDC=90^\circ$. Since $AB\perp BC$ and $AE\parallel BD$ points into the opposite-side triangle, $\angle BAE=90^\circ-\alpha$. But $AE$ bisects $\angle BAC$, so $\dfrac{\alpha}{2}=90^\circ-\alpha$. Thus $\boxed{\alpha=60^\circ}$.
