By Vieta's formulas, $p$ and $n$ are the two roots of
\[t^2-47t+210=0,\qquad\text{that is,}\qquad (t-5)(t-42)=0 .\]
So $\{p,n\}=\{5,42\}$. Since $n$ is even (and $5$ is prime), $n=\boxed{42}$.
