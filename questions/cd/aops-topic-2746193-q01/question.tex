Countably many boxes, labelled by the positive integers, each contain a real number. One hundred prisoners enter one at a time. Each prisoner may open as many boxes as he likes (even infinitely many), but must leave at least one box closed and then guess the number in one closed box. The boxes are closed again before the next prisoner enters, and the prisoners cannot communicate once the game starts, though they agree on a strategy beforehand. Find a strategy that guarantees that at least $99$ prisoners guess correctly. (You may use the axiom of choice.)
