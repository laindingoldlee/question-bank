\textbf{One Euclidean step on the exponents.} For integers $a>b>0$,
\[
\left(2^a-1\right)-2^{a-b}\left(2^b-1\right)=2^{a-b}-1,
\]
so $\gcd\left(2^a-1,2^b-1\right)=\gcd\left(2^b-1,2^{a-b}-1\right)$: replacing $a$ by $a-b$ does not change the gcd. Repeating this is exactly the Euclidean algorithm (by subtraction) on the exponents, so
\[
\gcd\left(2^a-1,2^b-1\right)=2^{\gcd(a,b)}-1 .
\]

\textbf{The exponents.} $1998-1989=9$, and $1989=9\cdot221$, so $\gcd(1998,1989)=\gcd(1989,9)=9$.

Hence
\[
\gcd\left(2^{1998}-1,2^{1989}-1\right)=2^9-1=\boxed{511}.
\]
