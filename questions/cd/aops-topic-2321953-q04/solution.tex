Complete the squares: $x^2-8x=(x-4)^2-16$ and $y^2-12y=(y-6)^2-36$. The equation becomes
\[(x-4)^2+(y-6)^2-52=-36,\quad\text{i.e.}\quad(x-4)^2+(y-6)^2=16.\]
So the centre is $\boxed{(4,6)}$, the radius is $\boxed{4}$, and the area is $\boxed{16\pi}$.
