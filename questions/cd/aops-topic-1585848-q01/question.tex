Let $\lceil x\rceil$ be the least integer greater than or equal to $x$, and let $\lfloor x\rfloor$ be the greatest integer less than or equal to $x$. What is the value of
\[
\lfloor\sqrt1\rfloor+\lceil\sqrt2\rceil+\lfloor\sqrt3\rfloor+\lceil\sqrt4\rceil+\cdots+\lfloor\sqrt{2015}\rfloor+\lceil\sqrt{2016}\rceil ?
\]
(Odd $k$ use the floor and even $k$ use the ceiling.)

(A) $60363$\qquad (B) $60364$\qquad (C) $60365$\qquad (D) $60366$\qquad (E) $60367$
