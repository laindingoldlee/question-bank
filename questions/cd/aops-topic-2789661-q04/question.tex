A two-digit number is written on the board, and Juliette correctly answers the eight questions "Is it divisible by $2$?", "$\dots$by $3$?", $\dots$, "$\dots$by $9$?", saying YES exactly four times. Without knowing the number, can you be sure of the answer to (1) one of the questions; (2) two of the questions?
