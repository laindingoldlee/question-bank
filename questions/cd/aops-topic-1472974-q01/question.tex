A student notices that $\dbinom22+\dbinom32+\cdots+\dbinom{b-1}{2}$ "appears to equal" $\dbinom b2$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that the student's formula is false.
\item[(b)] Prove that for every integer $b\ge3$,
\[
\dbinom22+\dbinom32+\cdots+\dbinom{b-1}{2}=\dbinom b3 .
\]
\end{enumerate}
