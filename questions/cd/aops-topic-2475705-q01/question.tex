A light is controlled by $5$ on--off switches. Exactly one of the $2^5=32$ on--off combinations turns the light on, and you do not know which one. The switches start in an unknown position, and the light is on exactly when the switches show the correct combination, so you see at once whether the current combination is correct. Each move flips one switch. What is the smallest number $m$ such that some strategy is guaranteed to turn the light on within $m$ flips, whatever the correct combination and the starting position?
