All terms are positive, so we may sum in any order. The geometric series give $\displaystyle\sum_{c\ge1}\left(\dfrac1{a+1}\right)^c=\dfrac1a$ and $\displaystyle\sum_{d\ge1}\left(\dfrac1{a+b+1}\right)^d=\dfrac1{a+b}$. So the sum is $\displaystyle\sum_{a\ge1}\dfrac1a\sum_{b\ge1}\dfrac1{a+b}$. Already for $a=1$ the inner sum $\dfrac12+\dfrac13+\cdots$ is the harmonic series minus its first term, which diverges. $\blacksquare$
