Samuel's Christmas break starts on Friday, December 16, and he returns to school on January 4, so the break consists of the days from December 16 to January 3, inclusive. Take the number of days in the break, multiply it by $9$, subtract $20$, multiply by $100$, divide by $10$, add $11$, and finally take the square root. What number do you get?
