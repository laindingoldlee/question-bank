December $16$ to December $31$ is $31-16+1=16$ days, and January $1$ to January $3$ is $3$ more days, so the break has $19$ days. Then
\[
19\cdot9=171,\quad 171-20=151,\quad 151\cdot100=15100,\quad \dfrac{15100}{10}=1510,\quad 1510+11=1521,\quad \sqrt{1521}=\boxed{39}.
\]
