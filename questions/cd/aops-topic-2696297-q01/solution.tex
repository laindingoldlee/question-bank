$25^3=15625$ while $24^3+7^3=13824+343=14167$, so $\boxed{\text{the single }25\text{ in cube}}$ is worth more. Mentally: $25^3-24^3=25^2+25\cdot24+24^2>3\cdot24^2>7^3$. (The trap is that $24^2+7^2=25^2$ for squares, not for cubes.)
