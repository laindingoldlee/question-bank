Fold a side 2 square in half along a line joining opposite side midpoints, obtaining a 2-by 1 rectangle. Fold the rectangle along the perpendicular bisector of a diagonal, sending one diagonal endpoint to the other. Find the area covered by the final folded paper, counting overlaps once.
