Use the rectangle $[0,2]\times[0,1]$ and fold $(2,1)$ to $(0,0)$ along $2x+y=\dfrac{5}{2}$. The fixed half has area 1 and vertices $(0,0),(\dfrac{5}{4},0),(\dfrac{3}{4},1),(0,1)$. The reflected half also has area 1 and vertices $(\dfrac{5}{4},0),(\dfrac{4}{5},-\dfrac{3}{5}),(0,0),(\dfrac{3}{4},1)$. Their overlap, for $0\le y\le1$, extends from $x=\dfrac{3y}{4}$ to $x=\dfrac{5}{4}-\dfrac{y}{2}$. Its area is $\int_0^1(\dfrac{5}{4}-\dfrac{5y}{4})\,dy=\dfrac{5}{8}$. The union area is $1+1-\dfrac{5}{8}=\boxed{\dfrac{11}{8}}$.
