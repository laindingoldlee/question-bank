The two angles are supplementary, so they are $120^\circ$ and $60^\circ$. Put $AB=w$. Vectors from the center to $A,B$ are proportional to $(-w,-1)$ and $(w,-1)$. Thus $\cos120^\circ=\dfrac{1-w^2}{1+w^2}=-\dfrac{1}{2}$, yielding $w^2=3$. The area is $w\cdot1=\boxed{\sqrt3}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
real w=sqrt(3);pair A=(0,0),Bp=(w,0),C=(w,1),D=(0,1),Ep=(w/2,.5);draw(A--Bp--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C^^Bp--D);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",Bp,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$E$",Ep,E);
\end{asy}
