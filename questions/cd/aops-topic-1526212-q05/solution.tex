$1000^3=2^9\cdot5^9$, so its factors are $2^i5^j$ with $0\le i,j\le9$: $10\cdot10=100$ factors. A factor is a perfect square exactly when $i$ and $j$ are both even, with $5$ choices each ($0,2,4,6,8$): $25$ factors. The fraction is $\dfrac{25}{100}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{4}}$.
