Let there be $n$ twos in general (here $n=2018$). Each expression is a sum of signed \emph{terms}, the blocks of twos joined by $\times$ and $\div$:
\[
\text{value}=T_1\pm T_2\pm\cdots\pm T_r ,
\]
where $T_1$ is the first block and the sign of every later term is the $+$ or $-$ in front of it.

\textbf{Later terms cancel.} Fix an expression and a term $T_i$ with $i\ge2$. Changing the sign in front of $T_i$ (from $+$ to $-$ or back) gives another expression with exactly the same terms. These two expressions contribute $+T_i$ and $-T_i$, so over all expressions the contributions of all terms other than the first cancel. Hence the total is the sum, over all expressions, of the first term $T_1$.

\textbf{The first term.} Suppose the first block has $k$ twos. Its $k-1$ operations are $\times$ or $\div$, and each $\times2$ multiplies by $2$ while each $\div2$ multiplies by $\dfrac{1}{2}$, so summing over the $2^{k-1}$ choices,
\[
\sum T_1=2\left(2+\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{k-1}=2\left(\dfrac{5}{2}\right)^{k-1}.
\]
If $k<n$, the next operation is $+$ or $-$ ($2$ choices) and the remaining $n-1-k$ operations are free ($4^{n-1-k}$ choices). If $k=n$ the whole expression is one block. So the total is
\[
\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}2\left(\dfrac{5}{2}\right)^{k-1}\cdot2\cdot4^{n-1-k}+2\left(\dfrac{5}{2}\right)^{n-1}
=4^{n-1}\sum_{j=0}^{n-2}\left(\dfrac{5}{8}\right)^{j}+2\left(\dfrac{5}{2}\right)^{n-1}.
\]
The geometric sum is $\dfrac{1-\left(\dfrac{5}{8}\right)^{n-1}}{\dfrac{3}{8}}$, so the total is
\[
\dfrac{8}{3}\cdot4^{n-1}-\dfrac{8}{3}\left(\dfrac{5}{2}\right)^{n-1}+2\left(\dfrac{5}{2}\right)^{n-1}=\dfrac{2}{3}\left(4^{n}-\left(\dfrac{5}{2}\right)^{n-1}\right).
\]
(For example $n=2$: $4+0+4+1=9=\dfrac{2}{3}\left(16-\dfrac{5}{2}\right)$.) For $n=2018$ the sum of all results is
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{2}{3}\left(4^{2018}-\left(\dfrac{5}{2}\right)^{2017}\right)=\dfrac{2^{6053}-5^{2017}}{3\cdot2^{2016}}}.
\]
