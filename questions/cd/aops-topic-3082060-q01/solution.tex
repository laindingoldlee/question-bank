Long division works exactly as in base ten, but each new digit shifts a remainder by a factor of $ 16 $. First $(9A7)_{16}-2(3E9)_{16}=(1D5)_{16}$. Bring down the next digit 1: $(1D51)_{16}-7(3E9)_{16}=(1F2)_{16}$. Bring down B: $(1F2B)_{16}-7(3E9)_{16}=(3CC)_{16}$. The last remainder is less than $(3E9)_{16}$, so \[\boxed{Q=(277)_{16},\qquad R=(3CC)_{16}}.\]As an independent base-ten check, $ 632603=1001\cdot631+972 $.
