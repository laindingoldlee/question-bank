The multiplicities must be $2,1,1$. Choose the repeated value in $6$ ways, its two positions in $\binom42=6$ ways, and the distinct ordered values in the other positions in $5\cdot4$ ways. Thus there are $720$ favorable outcomes among $6^4=1296$, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{5}{9}}$.
