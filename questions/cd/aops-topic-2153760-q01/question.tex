A positive integer has $n$ digits and leftmost digit $5$. The $5$ is moved from the front to the end, keeping the other digits in order, to form a new number.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that the new number can never be $3$ times the original number.
\item[(b)] Find the smallest $n$ for which the new number can be one third of the original number, and find the original number in that case.
\end{enumerate}
