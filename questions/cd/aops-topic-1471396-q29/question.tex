A $36$-inch rope is cut into three pieces. One piece is five inches longer than another, and one piece is twice as long as another. What is the sum of the possible lengths of the longest piece? Express your answer as a decimal to the nearest tenth.

A student lists five cases (pieces as functions of $a$ and $b$):
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
(1)\ a,\ a+5,\ 2a: & 15.5\\
(2)\ a,\ 2a,\ 2a+5: & 17.4\\
(3)\ a,\ a+5,\ 2(a+5): & 20.5\\
(4)\ a,\ b,\ c=2a=b+5: & 20.5\\
(5)\ a,\ b,\ c=2b=a+5: & 16.4
\end{array}
\]
and gets $90.3$. Find the error.
