Since $QRS$ is divisible by $5$ and $0$ is not available, $S=5$. Since $PQR$ is divisible by $4$, the two-digit number $QR$ is divisible by $4$; with distinct digits from $1,2,3,4$ this means $QR\in\{12,24,32\}$, so $R=2$ or $R=4$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $R=2$: $RST$ divisible by $3$ needs $2+5+T\equiv0\pmod3$, so $T\equiv2\pmod 3$, i.e. $T=2$ or $5$, both already used.
\item $R=4$: then $Q=2$, and $4+5+T\equiv0\pmod3$ gives $T=3$.
\end{itemize}
So $Q=2$, $R=4$, $S=5$, $T=3$, and the remaining digit is $P=1$. The number is $12453$, and $P=\boxed{1}$, answer $\textbf{(A)}$.
