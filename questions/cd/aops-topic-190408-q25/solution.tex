The line $y=\dfrac {x}{2}$ passes through the centre and meets the sides $x=a$ and $x=-a$ at $\left(a,\dfrac {a}{2}\right)$ and $\left(-a,-\dfrac {a}{2}\right)$. The upper piece has vertices $\left(a,\dfrac {a}{2}\right)$, $(a,a)$, $(-a,a)$, $\left(-a,-\dfrac {a}{2}\right)$ (the two pieces are congruent by the half-turn about the origin).

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real a=2;
pair P1=(a,a/2),P2=(a,a),P3=(-a,a),P4=(-a,-a/2);
fill(P1--P2--P3--P4--cycle,paleblue);
draw((-a,-a)--(a,-a)--(a,a)--(-a,a)--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(P4--P1,red);
draw((-2.8,0)--(2.8,0),gray,Arrow); draw((0,-2.6)--(0,2.6),gray,Arrow);
dot(P1);dot(P4);
label("$(a,\dfrac{a}{2})$",P1,SE); label("$(-a,-\dfrac{a}{2})$",P4,W);
label("$\dfrac{a}{2}$",(a,0.75a),E); label("$2a$",(-a/2,a),N); label("$\dfrac{3a}{2}$",(-a,0.5a),W);
label(rotate(degrees(atan(0.5)))*Label("$a\sqrt{5}$"),(1.0,0.5)+0.3*dir(116.565),red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Its sides are $a-\dfrac {a}{2}=\dfrac {a}{2}$, $2a$, $a+\dfrac {a}{2}=\dfrac{3a}{2}$, and the cut, of length $\sqrt{(2a)^2+a^2}=a\sqrt5$. The perimeter is
\[
\dfrac {a}{2}+2a+\dfrac{3a}{2}+a\sqrt5=\boxed{4a+a\sqrt5}.
\]
