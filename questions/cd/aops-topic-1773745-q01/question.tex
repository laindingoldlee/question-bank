In $\triangle ABC$, $AB=13$, $BC=12$ and $AC=5$. Consider all points inside or on the triangle for which the sum of the distances to the three sides of $\triangle ABC$ is $6$. They form a segment; the average distance from a point of this segment to side $AC$ (averaged uniformly along the segment) can be written as $\dfrac {m}{n}$ in lowest terms. Find $m+n$.
