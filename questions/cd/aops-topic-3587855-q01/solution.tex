An integer is coprime to $100$ exactly when it is divisible by neither $2$ nor $5$. There are $\phi(100)=100(1-\dfrac{1}{2})(1-\dfrac{1}{5})=40$ such integers. They pair as $k$ and $100-k$, with each pair summing to $100$, and no fixed point because $50$ is not coprime to $100$. Thus the sum is $20\cdot100=\boxed{2000}$.
