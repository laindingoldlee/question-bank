First compose once:
\[
f(g(x))=4(5x+3)-2=20x+10 .
\]
Then $f(g(f(g(x))))=20(20x+10)+10=400x+210$. Setting $400x+210=20$ gives $400x=-190$, so
\[
x=\boxed{-\dfrac{19}{40}} .
\]
The poster's answer is correct.
