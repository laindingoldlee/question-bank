Let $n$ be a positive integer.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(1)] Prove that if $2^n-1$ is a multiple of $3$, then $n$ is even.
\item[(2)] Prove that if $2^n-2$ is a multiple of $3$, then $n$ is odd.
\end{enumerate}
