A square plaza contains a lamp at every lattice point $(i,j)$, where $i,j\in\{0,1,2,3,4\}$ except at $(2,2)$, where there is a fountain.

Three of the $24$ lamps are selected uniformly at random. If the three selected lamps are collinear, the selection is discarded, and three new lamps are selected. This process continues until the selected lamps determine a triangle.

The probability that the fountain lies strictly inside the resulting triangle is $\dfrac{m}{n},$ where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $m+n$.
