A student claims that $x^2+y^2\le3xy$ for all real numbers $x$ and $y$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that the claim is false.
\item[(b)] For positive real numbers $x$ and $y$, find exactly when the inequality holds, in terms of the ratio $t=\dfrac {x}{y}$.
\end{enumerate}
