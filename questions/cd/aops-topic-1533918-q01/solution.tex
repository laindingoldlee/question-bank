Each picks the blue marble with probability $\dfrac{1}{3}$, and the two picks are from different buckets, so they are independent. The probability that both are blue is
\[
\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{1}{3}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{9}}.
\]
