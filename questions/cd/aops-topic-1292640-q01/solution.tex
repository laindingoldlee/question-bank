Let $v_n=an^2$. Its second difference is $a\left((n+2)^2-2(n+1)^2+n^2\right)=2a$. So $w_n=u_n-v_n$ has second differences $0$, i.e. its first differences are constant, say $b$; then $w_n=w_1+(n-1)b=bn+c$ with $c=w_1-b$. Hence $u_n=an^2+bn+c$. $\blacksquare$ (Conversely every such quadratic has constant second difference $2a$, which is why quadratic formulas are used for ``second-level'' sequences.)
