The final digit must be $0$ or $1$, since no square ends in $2$. A square ending in $0$ ends in $00$, which is forbidden. Thus it ends in $1$. Its square root is between $102$ and $149$ and ends in $1$ or $9$. Squaring $109,111,119,121,129,131,139,141,149$ gives $11881,12321,14161,14641,16641,17161,19321,19881,22201$. Only the last has the required digits. Hence $\boxed{22201=149^2}$.
