With positive integer queries, two suffice. First ask for $S=P(1)$, the sum of the positive coefficients. Then ask for $P(S+1)$; its base-$(S+1)$ digits are exactly the coefficients because each is at most $S$. One integer query cannot always suffice: for a fixed positive integer $k$, the distinct positive-coefficient polynomials $x+(k+1)$ and $2x+1$ agree at $k$. Thus the minimum in that model is two. Literally, if arbitrary real queries and exact answers are allowed, one query at a transcendental number suffices, since distinct integer polynomials cannot agree there. The question must restrict queries to obtain the usual answer $\boxed2$.
