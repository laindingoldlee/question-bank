A biker rides $27$ miles in $4$ hours against the wind. With the wind, he rides the same distance in $3.23$ hours. Assuming that the biker's own speed and the wind speed are constant (the wind adds to his speed in one direction and subtracts from it in the other), what is the speed of the wind? Give the exact value and a decimal to the nearest hundredth.
