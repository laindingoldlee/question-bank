Let the biker's speed in still air be $v$ and the wind speed $w$, in miles per hour. Then
\[
v-w=\dfrac{27}{4},\qquad v+w=\dfrac{27}{3.23}=\dfrac{2700}{323}.
\]
Subtracting and halving,
\[
w=\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{2700}{323}-\dfrac{27}{4}\right)=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{10800-8721}{1292}=\boxed{\dfrac{2079}{2584}}\approx\boxed{0.80}\text{ mph}.
\]
