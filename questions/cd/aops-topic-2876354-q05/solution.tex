We have $(x-y)^2=(x+y)^2-4xy=29$, so $x-y=\pm\sqrt{29}$. Also $x^2+xy+y^2=(x+y)^2-xy=44$. Therefore $x^3-y^3=(x-y)(x^2+xy+y^2)=\boxed{\pm44\sqrt{29}}$. Both signs occur by interchanging the roots $\dfrac{7\pm\sqrt{29}}{2}$.
