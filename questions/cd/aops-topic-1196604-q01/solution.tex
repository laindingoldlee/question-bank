Taking logarithms: $(x-2)\log6=(3-x)\log4$, so $x(\log6+\log4)=2\log6+3\log4$ and
\[x=\dfrac{2\log6+3\log4}{\log24}=\dfrac{\log(36\cdot64)}{\log24}=\log_{24}2304.\]
Since $2304=24^2\cdot4$, this is $x=\boxed{2+\log_{24}4}\approx2.436$.

(Check: with $x-2=\log_{24}4=t$, the equation is $6^t=4^{1-t}$, i.e. $24^t=4$.)
