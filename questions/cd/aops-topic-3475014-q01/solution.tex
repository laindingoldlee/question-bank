Let $V_n$ be the optimal value before a spin when $n$ spins remain. With one spin $V_1=0.8+0.45+0.5=1.75$. After observing prize $p$, rejecting is worth $V_{n-1}$, so accept exactly when $p\ge V_{n-1}$, and $V_n=0.8\max(1,V_{n-1})+0.15\max(3,V_{n-1})+0.05\max(10,V_{n-1})$. This gives $V_2=2.35,V_3=2.83,V_4=3.214$. For $n\ge5$ through 8, reject both 1 and 3, giving $V_n=0.95V_{n-1}+0.5$. Hence $V_5=3.5533,V_6=3.875635,V_7=4.18185325$, and $\boxed{V_8=4.4727605875}$ dollars. The comparison at each state proves optimality by backward induction.
