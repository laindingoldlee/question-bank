A wheel yields prizes of 1, 3, and 10 dollars with probabilities $0.8,0.15,0.05$. After a spin, you may accept its prize and stop or reject it and spin again. You may use at most eight independent spins and collect only one prize; you must accept the eighth if you reach it. Find the optimal expected prize.
