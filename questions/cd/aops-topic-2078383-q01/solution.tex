The Grunters win the series exactly when they win the last game played and have won exactly \(2\) of the earlier games. There are three cases, by the length of the series.
\begin{itemize}
\item In \(3\) games: they win games \(1\)--\(3\). The \(2\) earlier wins come from \(2\) earlier games, \(\dbinom20=1\) way: probability \(\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^3\).
\item In \(4\) games: they win game \(4\) and \(2\) of the first \(3\), losing one of them, \(\dbinom31=3\) choices for the loss: probability \(3\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^3\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}\).
\item In \(5\) games: they win game \(5\) and \(2\) of the first \(4\), losing \(2\), \(\dbinom42=6\) choices: probability \(6\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^3\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^2\).
\end{itemize}
These cases are disjoint, so
\[
P=\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^3\left(1+\dfrac{3}{4}+\dfrac{6}{16}\right)=\dfrac{27}{64}\cdot\dfrac{17}{8}=\boxed{\dfrac{459}{512}}.
\]
(This is the textbook expression \(\dbinom20\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^3\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^0+\dbinom31\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^3\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^1+\dbinom42\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^3\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^2\); the binomial coefficient counts where the losses fall among the games before the clinching win.)
