Kylo Ren takes the first place. The remaining four places hold Captain Phasma and three identical Stormtroopers, so an arrangement is determined by Phasma's place: $4$ choices. The answer is $\boxed{4}$ (equivalently $\dfrac{4!}{3!}=4$).
