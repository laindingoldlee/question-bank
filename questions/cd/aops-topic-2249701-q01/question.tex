A rectangular prism has as its vertices the eight points $(x,y,z)$ with $x\in\{0,24\}$, $y\in\{0,12\}$ and $z\in\{0,12\}$, namely $(0,0,0)$, $(0,0,12)$, $(0,12,0)$, $(0,12,12)$, $(24,0,0)$, $(24,0,12)$, $(24,12,0)$ and $(24,12,12)$. A sphere of radius $2$ is chosen at random from all spheres of radius $2$ that lie entirely inside the prism (that is, its centre is chosen uniformly from all possible centres). What is the probability that the point $(21,6,6)$ lies inside the chosen sphere?
