Write $P(x)$ for the polynomial. Its coefficients change sign only once, so by Descartes' rule of signs $P$ has exactly one positive root. For large $x$ the terms $2x^4-37135x^3$ dominate, so the root is a little above $\dfrac{37135}{2}=18567.5$. Trying integers just above this, $x=18575$ works, and dividing gives
\[
P(x)=(x-18575)\left(2x^3+15x^2+34x+24\right).
\]
(Check: the $x^3$ coefficient is $15-2\cdot18575=-37135$, the $x^2$ coefficient is $34-15\cdot18575=-278591$, the $x$ coefficient is $24-34\cdot18575=-631526$, and the constant is $-24\cdot18575=-445800$.) The cubic factor has only positive coefficients, so it has no positive root; in fact $2x^3+15x^2+34x+24=(x+2)(x+4)(2x+3)$. Hence the house number is $\boxed{18575}$.
