Only $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of the water reaches the pool, so the pool fills at $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of the normal rate, and the time is multiplied by $\dfrac{3}{2}$:
\[
15\cdot\dfrac{3}{2}=\boxed{22.5}\text{ minutes}.
\]
