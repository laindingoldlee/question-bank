Normally, the hose in Elena's garden fills her small pool in $15$ minutes. However, a leak in the hose lets $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of the water flowing through the hose spill into the flower bed. How many minutes will it take to fill the pool? Express your answer as a decimal to the nearest tenth.
