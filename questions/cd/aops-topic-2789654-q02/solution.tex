No. Since any digit can appear in the units place of a two-digit prime, all four digits must be among $1,3,7,9$. Distinctness forces exactly these four. Consider the card bearing $9$. If the opposite card displays $1$, one arrangement is $91=7\cdot13$; if it displays $3$, one arrangement is $39=3\cdot13$. Thus the other card could only display $7$ on either side, contradicting distinct digits.
