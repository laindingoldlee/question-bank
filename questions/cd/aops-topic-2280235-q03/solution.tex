By the binomial theorem,
\[11^{1200}=(1+10)^{1200}=1+\dbinom{1200}{1}10+\dbinom{1200}{2}10^2+\dbinom{1200}{3}10^3+\cdots .\]
All terms from $\dbinom{1200}{3}10^3$ on are multiples of $1000$. Also $\dbinom{1200}{1}10=12000$ and $\dbinom{1200}{2}10^2=719400\cdot100$ are multiples of $1000$. Hence $11^{1200}\equiv1\pmod{1000}$, and the last three digits are $\boxed{001}$.
