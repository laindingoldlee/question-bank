Two of seven people in a hat are drawn to win a prize.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] If the first name drawn is not put back, what is the probability that you win?
\item[(b)] If the first name drawn is put back before the second draw, what is the probability that you win at least once?
\end{enumerate}
