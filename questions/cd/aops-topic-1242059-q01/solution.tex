(a) You are not drawn with probability $\dfrac{6}{7}\cdot\dfrac{5}{6}=\dfrac{5}{7}$, so you win with probability $\boxed{\dfrac{2}{7}}$.

(b) You are missed both times with probability $\left(\dfrac{6}{7}\right)^2=\dfrac{36}{49}$, so you win with probability $\boxed{\dfrac{13}{49}}$ (slightly less than $\dfrac{2}{7}=\dfrac{14}{49}$, because you could be drawn twice).
