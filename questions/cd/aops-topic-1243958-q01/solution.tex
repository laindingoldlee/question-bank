Numbers below $1000$: three digits $a+b+c=25$, each at most $9$; with $a'=9-a$ etc., $a'+b'+c'=2$, which has $\dbinom42=6$ solutions. Numbers from $1000$ to $1999$: $1$ followed by $a+b+c=24$: $a'+b'+c'=3$, $\dbinom52=10$ solutions. $2000$ has digit sum $2$. Total $6+10=\boxed{16}$.
