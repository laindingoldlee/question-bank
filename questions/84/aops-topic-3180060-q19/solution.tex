At the five diagonal cells, the tuple index is identical in both grids, so agreement is certain. At each of the 20 other cells, the two entries have distinct tuple indices and agree with probability $\dfrac{1}{5}$. Thus the expectation is $5+20\cdot\dfrac{1}{5}=\boxed9$.
