Let the sides be $a,b,c$ and the area $K$. Each altitude satisfies $K=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot(\text{side})\cdot(\text{altitude to it})$, so
\[
\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot a\cdot6=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot b\cdot6=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot c\cdot6,
\]
and $a=b=c$: the triangle is equilateral. In an equilateral triangle of side $s$ the altitude is $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}s$, so $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}s=6$ and $s=\dfrac{12}{\sqrt3}=4\sqrt3$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
void ra(pair V, pair P1, pair P2, real s){pair u=unit(P1-V), v=unit(P2-V); draw(V+s*u--V+s*(u+v)--V+s*v);}
real s=4*sqrt(3);
pair B=(0,0), C=(s,0), A=(s/2,6);
pair Ma=(B+C)/2, Mb=(A+C)/2, Mc=(A+B)/2;
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(1));
draw(A--Ma,dashed); draw(B--Mb,dashed); draw(C--Mc,dashed);
ra(Ma,C,A,0.35); ra(Mb,C,B,0.35); ra(Mc,B,C,0.35);
label("$A$",A,N); label("$B$",B,SW); label("$C$",C,SE);
label("$6$",A+0.3*(Ma-A),E);
label("$6$",B+0.28*(Mb-B),S);
label("$6$",C+0.28*(Mc-C),S);
label("$4\sqrt{3}$",Ma,S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The perimeter is $3\cdot4\sqrt3=\boxed{12\sqrt3}$.
