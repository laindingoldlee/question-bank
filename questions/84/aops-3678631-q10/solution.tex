If $k$ angles are selected, $k(180-\dfrac{360}{n})=3300$, hence $n=\dfrac{6k}{3k-55}$. Positivity forces $k\ge19$; the condition $k<n$ forces $3k-55<6$, hence $k\le20$. Values $k=19,20$ yield $\boxed{n=57,24}$, respectively, and both satisfy the conditions.
