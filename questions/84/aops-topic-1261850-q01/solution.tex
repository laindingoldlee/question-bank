Tejas paints $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of the fence in $2$ hours: $\dfrac{1}{6}$ per hour. From $2$ to $3$ pm together they paint $\dfrac{4}{5}-\dfrac{1}{3}=\dfrac{7}{15}$, so Jason's rate is $\dfrac{7}{15}-\dfrac{1}{6}=\dfrac{14-5}{30}=\dfrac{3}{10}$ per hour. The remaining $\dfrac{1}{5}$ takes Jason $\dfrac{1}{5}\div\dfrac{3}{10}=\dfrac{2}{3}$ hour $=40$ minutes, so he finishes at $\boxed{3{:}40\text{ pm}}$.
