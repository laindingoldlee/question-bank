$3^8+2^{10}=6561+1024=7585=5\cdot1517=5\cdot37\cdot41$. (Alternatively, $3^8+4\cdot4^4=a^4+4b^4$ with $a=3^2$, $b=4$ factors by Sophie Germain as $(a^2+2b^2+2ab)(a^2+2b^2-2ab)=(81+32+72)(81+32-72)=185\cdot41$.) The greatest prime factor is $\boxed{41}$.
