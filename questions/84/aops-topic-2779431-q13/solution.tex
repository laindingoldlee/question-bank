Write $x=ga,y=gb$, where $\gcd(a,b)=1$. Then $g(1+ab-a-b)=8$, or $g(a-1)(b-1)=8$. Thus $a,b>1$. For $g=1$, unordered $(a-1,b-1)$ are $(1,8),(2,4)$, giving four ordered coprime pairs $(a,b)$. For $g=2$, only $(1,4)$ and its reversal qualify; $(2,2)$ does not. For $g=4$, $(1,2)$ and its reversal qualify. For $g=8$, $(1,1)$ is not coprime after adding 1. The count is $4+2+2=\boxed8$.
