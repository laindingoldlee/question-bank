Let the first speed be $v$. Equal times give $\dfrac mv=\dfrac{\dfrac43m}{v+12}$, i.e. $4v=3(v+12)$, so $v=36$ and each leg takes $\dfrac m{36}$ hours. The total distance is $\dfrac73m$ and the total time is $\dfrac{2m}{36}$, so the average speed is $\dfrac73m\cdot\dfrac{36}{2m}=42$. \textbf{(C)}
