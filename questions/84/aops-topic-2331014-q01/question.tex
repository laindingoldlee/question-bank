An ant is on the top left square of an $m\times n$ grid of squares, where $m$ and $n$ are integers greater than $1$. In each move the ant goes to a square that shares a side with its current square. Prove that the ant can visit every square exactly once and return to its starting square if and only if $mn$ is even.
