The thousands digit is $5$ (the number $5000$ itself is excluded). Let the tens digit be $t$. The ones digit $\dfrac {t}{2}$ must be a whole number, so $t$ is even, and the hundreds digit $3t$ must be at most $9$, so $t\le3$. Hence $t=0$ or $t=2$. The choice $t=0$ gives $5000$, which is not strictly between $5000$ and $6000$. So $t=2$, the hundreds digit is $6$ and the ones digit is $1$: the number is $\boxed{5621}$.
