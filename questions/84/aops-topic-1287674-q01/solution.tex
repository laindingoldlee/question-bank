A die is determined by how many faces carry each integer: nonnegative integers $c_1,\dots,c_{12}$ with $c_1+\cdots+c_{12}=12$. By stars and bars there are
\[
\dbinom{12+12-1}{12}=\dbinom{23}{12}=\boxed{1352078}
\]
distinct dice.
