Let $n$ be the number of members and $r_i$ the number of meetings attended by member $i$. Then $\sum r_i=400$. Two meetings cannot share two members, since that pair would meet twice. Counting pairs of meetings through each member gives
\[
\sum_{i=1}^n\binom{r_i}{2}\le\binom{40}{2}=780.
\]
Thus $\sum r_i^2\le1960$. Cauchy-Schwarz yields
\[
160000=(\sum r_i)^2\le n\sum r_i^2\le1960n.
\]
Consequently $n\ge\dfrac{4000}{49}>81$, so $\boxed{n\ge82}$. This is a necessary lower bound; no claim that equality is attainable is required.
