A pantry has $5$ shelves, one above another, and each shelf holds exactly one of five kinds of cookies: macaroons, oatmeal raisin, chocolate chip, gingerbread and sugar cookies. The macaroons are on the shelf directly below the oatmeal raisin cookies, the chocolate chip cookies are on a shelf above the gingerbread cookies, and the oatmeal raisin cookies are $3$ shelves below the sugar cookies. Which kind of cookie is on the third (middle) shelf?
