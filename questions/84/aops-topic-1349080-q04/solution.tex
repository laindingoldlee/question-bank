Number the shelves $1$ (top) to $5$ (bottom). If the sugar cookies are on shelf $s$, the oatmeal raisin cookies are on shelf $s+3$ and the macaroons on shelf $s+4\le5$, so $s=1$: sugar on $1$, oatmeal raisin on $4$, macaroons on $5$. Shelves $2$ and $3$ hold chocolate chip and gingerbread, with chocolate chip above, so chocolate chip is on shelf $2$ and the \textbf{gingerbread} cookies are on the third shelf. (The middle shelf is the third shelf counted from either end, so the answer does not depend on the numbering.)
