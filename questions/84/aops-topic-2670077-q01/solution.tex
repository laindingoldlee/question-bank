The factors have the form $\dfrac{(2j)\triangle(2j-2)}{(j-1)\triangle(j-2)}$ for $j=50,49,\dots,2$. Using $x^2-y^2=(x-y)(x+y)$,
\[\dfrac{(2j)^2-(2j-2)^2}{(j-1)^2-(j-2)^2}=\dfrac{2(4j-2)}{2j-3}=4\cdot\dfrac{2j-1}{2j-3}.\]
There are $49$ factors, and the fractions telescope:
\[4^{49}\cdot\dfrac31\cdot\dfrac53\cdots\dfrac{99}{97}=2^{98}\cdot99.\]
So $a=98$, $b=99$, and $a+b=\boxed{197}$.
