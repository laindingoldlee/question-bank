Let $a\triangle b=a^2-b^2$. Given that
\[\left(\dfrac{100\triangle98}{49\triangle48}\right)\left(\dfrac{98\triangle96}{48\triangle47}\right)\left(\dfrac{96\triangle94}{47\triangle46}\right)\cdots\left(\dfrac{4\triangle2}{1\triangle0}\right)\]
can be expressed in the form $2^ab$ where $b$ is odd, find $a+b$.
