Undo the logarithms from the outside in. From $\log_2(\cdots)=2$,
\[
\log_3\left(\log_4\left(2^n\right)\right)=2^2=4 .
\]
Then $\log_4\left(2^n\right)=3^4=81$, so $2^n=4^{81}=2^{162}$ and
\[
n=\boxed{162}.
\]
