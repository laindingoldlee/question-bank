The last digit is $0$ or $5$, and all digits lie in $\{0,\dots,5\}$ with $5$ used.

\emph{Last digit $5$.} The first digit is from $\{1,2,3,4\}$ ($4$ ways), then the middle two digits are an ordered choice from the remaining $4$ digits of $\{0,\dots,4\}$: $4\cdot4\cdot3=48$.

\emph{Last digit $0$.} $5$ must be among the first three digits. Those three digits are distinct from $\{1,\dots,5\}$ and include $5$: $5\cdot4\cdot3-4\cdot3\cdot2=60-24=36$.

Total $\boxed{84}$.
