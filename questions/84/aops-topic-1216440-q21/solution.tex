The infinite geometric series has first term $\dfrac{1}{3}$ and ratio $\dfrac{1}{3}$, so it sums to $\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{3}}{1-\dfrac{1}{3}}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. The total is $3+\dfrac{1}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{2}}$.
