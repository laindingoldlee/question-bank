$x=-2$: $16-64+100-76+24=0$. $x=-3$: $81-216+225-114+24=0$. Dividing by $(x+2)(x+3)=x^2+5x+6$ gives $x^2+3x+4$ (discriminant $-7$). So the answer is $\boxed{(x+2)(x+3)(x^2+3x+4)}$.
