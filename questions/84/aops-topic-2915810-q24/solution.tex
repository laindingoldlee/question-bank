Let $F(t)=(t-1)(t-2)(t-3)(t-4)=t^4-10t^3+35t^2-50t+24$. Then $P(x,y)=\dfrac{F(x)-F(y)}{x-y}$ is the polynomial $x^3+x^2y+xy^2+y^3-10(x^2+xy+y^2)+35(x+y)-50$, of total degree 3, and vanishes at all specified pairs. To rule out degree at most 2, fix any $y=j\in\{1,2,3,4\}$. The polynomial in $x$ has the other three values as roots and degree at most 2, so it is identically zero. Each coefficient as a polynomial in $y$ consequently vanishes at four distinct values while having degree at most 2, so every coefficient is zero. This contradicts nonzero $P$. Thus the minimum is $\boxed3$.
