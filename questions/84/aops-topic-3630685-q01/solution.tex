Take $P(x)=\prod_{j=1}^{10}(x-j)$. It vanishes at each of $1,2,\ldots,10$, and these numbers are distinct. A product of ten monic linear factors has degree $\boxed{10}$. No nonzero polynomial of smaller degree could have ten distinct roots.
