The sum is even exactly when the subset contains an even number of odd elements from $\{1,3,5\}$: $0$ or $2$ of them, in $1+3=4$ ways, together with any subset of $\{2,4\}$ ($4$ ways). That gives $16$ subsets with even sum, including the empty set and the singletons $\{2\}$, $\{4\}$. Removing those $3$ leaves $\boxed{13}$.
