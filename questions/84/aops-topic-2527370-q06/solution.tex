$10\cdot9\cdot8\cdot7=5040$, and $7!=5040$. Since factorials increase strictly for $x\ge1$, the only solution is $x=\boxed{7}$. (Indeed $10\cdot9\cdot8\cdot7=\dfrac{10!}{3!}=\dfrac{10!}{6}$, and $10\cdot9\cdot8=720=6!$ gives $10\cdot9\cdot8\cdot7=7\cdot6!=7!$.)
