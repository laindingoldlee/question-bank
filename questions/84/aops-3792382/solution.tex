The only possible digits are $1,2,4,8$, with transitions $1\to2$, $2\to1$ or $4$, $4\to2$ or $8$, and $8\to4$. Let $E_k(d),O_k(d)$ be the probabilities after $k$ digits of ending at $d$ with even or odd digit sum. Start with $O_1(1)=1$. Along an edge $d\to e$, add half the source probability if $d=2,4$, and all of it otherwise, preserving parity for even $e$ and reversing it for $e=1$. Successive exact updates give total even probabilities for $k=1,\ldots,10$ of $0,0,\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{3}{8},\dfrac{3}{8},\dfrac{15}{32},\dfrac{15}{32},\dfrac{59}{128},\dfrac{59}{128}$. Hence the answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{59}{128}}$.
