Two eggs, $T$ and $P$, are dropped from the windows of a tall building: first from floor $1$, then (if they survive) from floor $2$, then from floor $3$, and so on, both at the same time, until each breaks. A drop from floor $n$ leaves egg $T$ unbroken with probability $1-\dfrac{1}{100-n}$, and leaves egg $P$ unbroken with probability $1-\dfrac{1}{99-n}$, independently of everything else. What is the probability that egg $P$ breaks from a lower floor than egg $T$?
