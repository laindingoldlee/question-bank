Divide by $6^x$: $g(x)=\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^x+\left(\dfrac{5}{6}\right)^x=1$. Both terms decrease as $x$ increases, so $g$ is decreasing and the equation has exactly one solution. Now
\[
g(2)=\dfrac{4}{9}+\dfrac{25}{36}=\dfrac{41}{36}>1,\qquad g(3)=\dfrac{8}{27}+\dfrac{125}{216}=\dfrac{189}{216}<1,
\]
so $2<x<3$, and the greatest integer less than $x$ is $\boxed{2}$. (Numerically $x\approx2.49$.)
