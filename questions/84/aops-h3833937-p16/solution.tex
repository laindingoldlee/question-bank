Write the expression as $2^{2020}(225+2^{n-2020})$. Put $k=n-2020\ge0$. Since $2^{2020}$ is a square, we need $s^2=225+2^k$, or $(s-15)(s+15)=2^k$. At $k=0$ there is no square. For $k\ge1$, both factors are powers of two and differ by $30$. Write them $2^u,2^v$, with $u<v$. Then $2^u(2^{v-u}-1)=30$, forcing $u=1$ and $2^{v-u}=16$, so $v=5$. Hence $k=6$ and $\boxed{n=2026}$.
