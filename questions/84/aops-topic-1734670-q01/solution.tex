The sum of the coefficients of a polynomial $p$ is $p(1)$. Here $f(1)=1+2+\dots+25=\dfrac{25\cdot26}{2}=325$, so the required sum is $325^{2018}$. Every power of a number ending in $5$ ends in $5$, so the units digit is $\boxed{5}$.
