\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Let $n\ge1$ and let $k$ be any integer. Prove that among any $n$ consecutive integers exactly one is congruent to $k$ modulo $n$, and deduce that among any $qn$ consecutive integers exactly $q$ are congruent to $k$ modulo $n$.
\item[(b)] How many three-digit positive integers are congruent to $12$ modulo $60$?
\item[(c)] How many three-digit positive integers are congruent to $2$ modulo $7$, and how many are congruent to $0$ modulo $7$? Explain why the estimate $\dfrac{900}{7}$ cannot be exact.
\end{enumerate}
