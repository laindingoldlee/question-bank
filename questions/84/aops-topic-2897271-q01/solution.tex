Let the cars' paces (hours per km) be $p_1,p_2,p_3$. Leaving at equal intervals and reaching $Y$ together means $p_1,p_2,p_3$ form an arithmetic progression, with common difference $\delta$. From $Y$: $120p_1-120p_2=1$, so $\delta=\dfrac1{120}$.

At the meeting, the first car has gone $80$ km past $Y$ and the third $120+40=160$ km, in equal times: $80p_1=160p_3=160(p_1-2\delta)$. So $p_1=4\delta=\dfrac1{30}$, and the speed is $\boxed{30\text{ km per hour}}$.
