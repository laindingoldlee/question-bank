Three cars leave $X$ at equal time intervals and drive the same road to $Z$ at constant speeds. They pass point $Y$, between $X$ and $Z$, simultaneously. The first car reaches $Z$ an hour after the second. The third car, on reaching $Z$, turns straight back and meets the first car $40$ km from $Z$. If $YZ=120$ km, find the speed of the first car.
