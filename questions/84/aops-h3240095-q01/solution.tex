Subtract the given equations:
\[
(z-y)(x+y+z)=125.
\]
Put $d=z-y>0$. Since $x+y+z>d$, the factor pairs of $125$ allow only $(d,x+y+z)=(1,125)$ or $(5,25)$.

If $d=1$, then $z=y+1$ and $x=124-2y$. Substitution into $z^2-xy=22$ gives $3y^2-122y-21=0$. Its discriminant is $15136$, strictly between $123^2$ and $124^2$, so no integer solution occurs.

If $d=5$, then $z=y+5$ and $x=20-2y$. The same equation becomes $3y^2-10y+3=0$, whose roots are $3$ and $\dfrac{1}{3}$. Thus $(x,y,z)=(14,3,8)$; these are distinct positive integers and satisfy both equations. Therefore
\[
\boxed{x^2-yz=196-24=172}.
\]
