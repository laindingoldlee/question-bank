ELEMENT has $7$ letters: three E's and L, M, N, T. The number of distinct arrangements is $\dfrac{7!}{3!}=840$. If the three E's are glued into one block, we arrange $5$ distinct objects: $5!=120$ ways. All distinct arrangements are equally likely, so the probability is $\dfrac{120}{840}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{7}}$.
