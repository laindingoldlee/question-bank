Let $ABC$ be a triangle with $AB=AC$, and let $CH$ be the altitude from $C$ to $AB$. The circle with centre $C$ and radius $CH$ meets $AC$ at $D$. Point $T$ on $BC$ is such that $HT$ bisects $\angle BHC$, $H'$ is the foot of the perpendicular from $H$ to $BC$, and $M$ is the midpoint of $BH$. Prove that $DH'$, $CM$ and $HT$ are concurrent.
