For every $k\ge1$, since $\sqrt{k-1}<\sqrt k$,
\[\dfrac1{\sqrt k}=\dfrac{2}{2\sqrt k}<\dfrac{2}{\sqrt k+\sqrt{k-1}}=2\left(\sqrt k-\sqrt{k-1}\right).\]
Summing over $k=1,\dots,n$, the right side telescopes to $2\sqrt n$. $\blacksquare$
