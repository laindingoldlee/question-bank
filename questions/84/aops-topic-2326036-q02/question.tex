When I go trick-or-treating at a house, I get a Reese's with probability $\dfrac1{11}$. If I do not get one, then with probability $\dfrac19$ I go back and ask for another, and when I ask, I get one with probability $\dfrac15$. Similarly, I get sour gummy worms with probability $\dfrac1{14}$; if I do not, then with probability $60\%$ I go back and ask, and when I ask, I get them with probability $10\%$. I can never end up with both a Reese's and gummy worms. What is the probability that I end up with a Reese's or gummy worms?
