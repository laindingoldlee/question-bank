After giving $15\%$ of $60$, i.e. $9$, I have $51$. My mom gives me $2\cdot\dfrac{51}{3}=34$, so I have $85$. My brother gets $40\%$ of $85$, i.e. $34$, leaving $51$. That is $\boxed{9\text{ fewer}}$ than the $60$ I started with.
