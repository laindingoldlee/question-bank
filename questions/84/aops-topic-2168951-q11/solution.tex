Let $t$ be the number of tails, so there are $10-t$ heads. Since $0$ is a factor of nothing, $t\ge1$, and then $t\mid 10-t$ exactly when $t\mid10$ (because $10-t$ and $10$ differ by a multiple of $t$). So $t\in\{1,2,5,10\}$; the case $t=10$ is allowed because $10$ is a factor of $0$. The probability is
\[\dfrac{\dbinom{10}{1}+\dbinom{10}{2}+\dbinom{10}{5}+\dbinom{10}{10}}{2^{10}}=\dfrac{10+45+252+1}{1024}=\dfrac{308}{1024}=\boxed{\dfrac{77}{256}},\]
answer $\textbf{(B)}$.
