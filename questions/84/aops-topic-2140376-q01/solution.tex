Neither $a$ nor $b$ is $0$, so $|a|\ge1$ and $|b|\ge1$, and $\left(|a|-1\right)\left(|b|-1\right)\ge0$. Expanding, $|a|+|b|\le|a||b|+1=2021$. Hence
\[a-b\le|a|+|b|\le2021 .\]
Equality holds for $a=2020$, $b=-1$ (then $|ab|=2020$ and $a-b=2021$). The greatest value is $\boxed{2021}$.
