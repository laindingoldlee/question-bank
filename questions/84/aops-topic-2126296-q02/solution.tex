The rows have $11,12,\dots,39$ seats (the $29$th row has $11+28=39$). The sum of this arithmetic sequence is the number of terms times the average of the first and last terms:
\[29\cdot\dfrac{11+39}{2}=29\cdot25=\boxed{725}.\]
