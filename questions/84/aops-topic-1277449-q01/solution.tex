Let $c_n$ be the number of connected labelled graphs on $n$ vertices. Every one of the $2^{\binom n2}$ graphs splits uniquely into the component containing vertex $1$ (of size $k$) and an arbitrary graph on the other $n-k$ vertices, so
\[
2^{\binom n2}=\sum_{k=1}^{n}\dbinom{n-1}{k-1}c_k\,2^{\binom{n-k}{2}} .
\]
This gives $c_1=1$, $c_2=1$, $c_3=8-(1\cdot1\cdot2+2\cdot1\cdot1)=4$, $c_4=64-(8+3\cdot2+3\cdot4)=38$, and
\[
c_5=1024-\left(1\cdot1\cdot64+4\cdot1\cdot8+6\cdot4\cdot2+4\cdot38\cdot1\right)=1024-296=\boxed{728}.
\]
