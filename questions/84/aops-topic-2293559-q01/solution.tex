\textbf{(a)} Profit is revenue minus cost:
\[P(n)=84.95n-\left(-0.002n^2+20n+30{,}000\right)=\boxed{0.002n^2+64.95n-30{,}000}.\]

\textbf{(b)} $P(400)=0.002\cdot160{,}000+64.95\cdot400-30{,}000=320+25{,}980-30{,}000=-3700$. The company makes a loss: the profit is $\boxed{-\$3700}$.

\textbf{(c)} $P(5000)=0.002\cdot25{,}000{,}000+64.95\cdot5000-30{,}000=50{,}000+324{,}750-30{,}000=\boxed{\$344{,}750}$.

\textbf{(d)} For $n\ge0$ both $0.002n^2$ and $64.95n$ increase with $n$, so $P$ is increasing, and we need the smallest whole number $n$ with $P(n)\ge0$. Multiplying $P(n)=0$ by $500$ gives
\[n^2+32{,}475n-15{,}000{,}000=0,\qquad n=\dfrac{-32{,}475+\sqrt{32{,}475^2+60{,}000{,}000}}{2}\approx455.5.\]
Indeed $P(455)=414.05+29{,}552.25-30{,}000=-33.70<0$ and $P(456)=415.872+29{,}617.20-30{,}000=33.072>0$. The company breaks even once $\boxed{456}$ phones are sold.
