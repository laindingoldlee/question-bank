The digit-sum conditions are
\[A+B+C=13,\quad B+D+E+F=18,\quad F+G+H=14,\quad H+J=11,\quad I+J=7.\]
Adding them, the ten letters (sum $45$) appear once each, with $B,F,H,J$ appearing twice: $45+B+F+H+J=63$, so $B+F+H+J=18$ and hence $B+F=7$.

By place value,
\[M=5301+1000(B+F+H+I)+100(A+D+G)+10(B+E+H+J)+(C+F+J).\]
From the last two conditions, $H=11-J$ and $I=7-J$, and $H\le9$ forces $J\ge2$. Then $B+F+H+I=7+18-2J=25-2J$.

\emph{If $J\ge3$}: the thousands part is at most $19000$, and bounding the other brackets by the largest available digits,
\[M\le5301+19000+100(9+8+7)+10(9+8+7+6)+(9+8+7)=27025.\]

\emph{If $J=2$}: $H=9$, $I=5$, and $A,B,C,D,E,F,G$ use the digits $0,1,3,4,6,7,8$. Now $F+G=5$ forces $\{F,G\}=\{1,4\}$. If $F=4$ then $B=3$, $G=1$, and $A+C=10$ must come from $\{0,6,7,8\}$, which is impossible. So $F=1$, $G=4$, $B=6$, and from $\{0,3,7,8\}$ we need $A+C=7$, $D+E=11$: $\{A,C\}=\{0,7\}$, $\{D,E\}=\{3,8\}$. Then
\[M=5301+21000+100(A+D+4)+10(E+17)+(C+3)=26874+100A+100D+10E+C,\]
which is largest for $A=7$, $C=0$, $D=8$, $E=3$: $M=26874+700+800+30=28404$. This exceeds $27025$.

The PINs are $5760$, $6831$, $1494$, $9027$, $5292$ (each with digit sum $18$), and the maximum is $\boxed{28404}$.
