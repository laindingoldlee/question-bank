By the binomial theorem,
\[
(1+1)^n=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\dbinom{n}{k},\qquad (1-1)^n=\sum_{k=0}^{n}(-1)^k\dbinom{n}{k}.
\]
Subtracting, the terms with $k$ even cancel and the terms with $k$ odd double:
\[
2^n-0^n=2\left[\dbinom{n}{1}+\dbinom{n}{3}+\dbinom{n}{5}+\cdots\right].
\]
Since $n\ge1$, $0^n=0$, so the sum of the odd-index coefficients is $\dfrac{2^n}{2}=2^{n-1}$. $\blacksquare$

(Adding the two expansions instead shows that the even-index coefficients also add to $2^{n-1}$. For $n=0$ the identity fails, since the left side is the empty sum $0$.)
