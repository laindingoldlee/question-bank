Let $n$ be a positive integer. Use the binomial theorem to prove that
\[
\dbinom{n}{1}+\dbinom{n}{3}+\dbinom{n}{5}+\cdots=2^{n-1},
\]
where the sum runs over all odd $k\le n$.
