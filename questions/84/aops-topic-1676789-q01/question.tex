Let $n$ be a positive integer, and let $s(n)$ be the number of $1$s in the binary representation of $n$. Prove that exactly $2^{s(n)}$ of the binomial coefficients $\dbinom n0,\dbinom n1,\dots,\dbinom nn$ are odd.
