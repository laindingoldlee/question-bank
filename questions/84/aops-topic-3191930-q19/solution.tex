The slant height is $\sqrt{4+5}=3$. Unfold the surface to a sector of radius 3 and angle $4\dfrac{\pi}{3}$. The two rim points are separated by sector angle $2\dfrac{\pi}{3}$. Their straight chord has length $2\cdot3\sin(\dfrac{\pi}{3})=3\sqrt3$ and lies in a suitable unfolded sector. Other windings cannot give a smaller valid angular separation, while a route via the apex has length 6. Thus the minimum is $\boxed{3\sqrt3}$.

\end{asy}
