Reducing modulo $3$ gives $a\equiv 78\equiv0\pmod 3$, so the prime $a$ equals $3$. Then $3b+6c+9d=75$, i.e.
\[
b+2c+3d=25 .
\]
Since $2c$ is even, $b+3d$ is odd, so $b$ and $d$ have opposite parity: exactly one of them is the even prime $2$.

\textbf{Case $b=2$.} Then $2c+3d=23$, so $d$ is odd and $3d\le23-4=19$, giving $d\in\{3,5\}$. For $d=3$, $c=7$ (prime); for $d=5$, $c=4$ (not prime). This gives $(3,2,7,3)$.

\textbf{Case $d=2$.} Then $b+2c=19$ with $b$ an odd prime, so $c\le8$ and $c\in\{2,3,5,7\}$, giving $b=15,13,9,5$. Only $b=13$ ($c=3$) and $b=5$ ($c=7$) are prime. This gives $(3,13,3,2)$ and $(3,5,7,2)$.

The solutions are
\[
\boxed{(a,b,c,d)=(3,2,7,3),\ (3,5,7,2),\ (3,13,3,2)} .
\]
