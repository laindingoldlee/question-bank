Use $X=(0,0),Z=(4,0),Y=(0,3)$. The ray $XN$ has equation $y=x$, and $YZ$ has equation $\dfrac{x}{4}+\dfrac{y}{3}=1$. Therefore $N=(\dfrac{12}{7},\dfrac{12}{7})$, giving $\boxed{XN=\dfrac{12\sqrt2}{7}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair X=(0,0),Y=(0,3),Z=(4,0),Nn=(12/7,12/7);draw(X--Y--Z--cycle);draw(X--Nn,blue);label("X",X,SW);label("Y",Y,N);label("Z",Z,SE);label("N",Nn,NE);label("3",(0,1.5),W);label("4",(2,0),S);
\end{asy}
