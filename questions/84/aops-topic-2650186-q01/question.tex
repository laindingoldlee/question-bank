Jimmy bakes a cake in a pan that holds at most $300$ equal pieces, and shares it. Jen takes $\dfrac1{30}$ of the pieces, John takes $5$ pieces, Jack takes $\dfrac1{20}$ of the pieces, James takes $\dfrac1{45}$ of the pieces and Jackie takes $7$ pieces. Every share is a whole number of pieces. What percentage of the cake is left for Jimmy?
