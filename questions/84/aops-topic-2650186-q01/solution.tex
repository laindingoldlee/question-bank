The number $N$ of pieces is a multiple of $30$, $20$ and $45$, hence of $\operatorname{lcm}(30,20,45)=180$, and $N\le300$; so $N=180$. The friends take $6+5+9+4+7=31$ pieces, leaving $149$. Jimmy gets
\[\dfrac{149}{180}=\boxed{82\dfrac79\%}\approx82.78\%.\]
