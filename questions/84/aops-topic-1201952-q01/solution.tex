Here $x>0$ ($x=0$ gives $1=0$). Divide both sides by $x$:
\[\dfrac{(x-1)^2}{x}=\dfrac{x+1}{\sqrt x},\qquad\text{i.e.}\qquad\left(\sqrt x-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt x}\right)^2=\sqrt x+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt x}.\]
Let $t=\sqrt x+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt x}\ge2$. Since $\left(\sqrt x-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt x}\right)^2=t^2-4$, we get $t^2-t-4=0$, so
\[t=\boxed{\dfrac{1+\sqrt{17}}{2}}\]
(the other root is negative).
