Record the numbers of white flowers in the three gaps between consecutive red flowers: $(g_1,g_2,g_3)$ with sum $3$, up to cyclic rotation. The possibilities are $(3,0,0)$, $(1,1,1)$, $(2,1,0)$ and $(2,0,1)$. Flipping reverses the cyclic order, which turns $(2,1,0)$ into $(2,0,1)$. So there are $\boxed{3}$ garlands.
