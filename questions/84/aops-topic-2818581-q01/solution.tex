$E=\dfrac56\cdot2-\dfrac16\cdot16=\dfrac{10-16}6=\boxed{-\$1}$. You come out ahead in one play with probability $\boxed{\dfrac56}$. The two facts are compatible: the rare loss is large enough to outweigh the frequent small wins, and over many plays the average gain tends to $-\$1$.
