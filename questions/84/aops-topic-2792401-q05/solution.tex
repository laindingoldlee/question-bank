Begin with the one-entry block 14. Suppose an increasing clockwise block $k,k+1,\ldots,14$ has been formed. Move it counterclockwise across preceding entries until its predecessor is $k-1$. Any intervening entry $x$ is at most $k-2$. To move the block past $x$, swap $x$ successively with $k,k+1,\ldots,14$; every difference is at least 2, so all swaps are legal. Upon reaching $k-1$, include it in the block. Repeating down to $k=2$ creates the full ordered circle.
