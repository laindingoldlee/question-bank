A well holds unlimited water. You have a $5$-ounce bucket and a $3$-ounce bucket, with no other markings, and you may fill a bucket from the well, empty a bucket into the well or into a pot, and pour one bucket into the other until one is empty or the other is full. How can you put exactly $1$ ounce of water into the pot? Exactly $4$ ounces? Exactly $7$ ounces?
