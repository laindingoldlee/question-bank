The fifth digit belongs to both groups of five: it is the smallest of the first five digits and also the smallest of the last five. So it is smaller than all other eight digits, i.e.\ it is $1$.

The remaining digits $2,\dots,9$ are split into the four that come before the $1$ and the four that come after it. Once this split is chosen the number is determined: the first four digits must be written in decreasing order and the last four in increasing order, and then both conditions hold. Hence the count is
\[
\dbinom84=\boxed{70}.
\]
