An integer root $m$ cannot be $0$. Substitution gives $km^2+m+1=0$, or $\boxed{k=-\dfrac{m+1}{m^2},\quad m\in\mathbb Z\setminus\{0\}}$. Every such value indeed gives root $m$. If $k$ is integral, the equation implies $m\mid1$, hence $m=1$ or $-1$. These give $\boxed{k=-2\text{ or }0}$. The case $k=0$ is linear and still has the integer root $-1$.
