$2015=5\cdot13\cdot31$. Among $1$ through $27$, the non-coprime numbers are $5,10,13,15,20,25,26$, exactly seven. Thus there are twenty coprime numbers through $27$, and $27$ itself is coprime, so $p=27$. The fraction is $\dfrac{1988}{27}$, already reduced since $1988$ is not divisible by $3$. Hence $a+b=1988+27=\boxed{2015}$.
