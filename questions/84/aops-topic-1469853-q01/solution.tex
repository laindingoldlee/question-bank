Since $18$ large jugs weigh as much as $30$ small jugs, one large jug weighs as much as $\dfrac{30}{18}=\dfrac{5}{3}$ small jugs. With $25$ small jugs loaded, the camel can still carry the weight of $30-25=5$ small jugs, which is the weight of $5\cdot\dfrac{3}{5}=\boxed{3}$ large jugs.
