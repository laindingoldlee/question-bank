Simplify from inside outward: $\sqrt{3-2\sqrt2}=\sqrt2-1$, then $\sqrt{10+4(\sqrt2-1)}=2+\sqrt2$, then $\sqrt{23-6(2+\sqrt2)}=3-\sqrt2$. The full number is therefore $\sqrt{3+2\sqrt2}=1+\sqrt2$. Its rational conjugate is $1-\sqrt2$, so the monic rational quadratic is $(x-1)^2-2=x^2-2x-1$. Its coefficient sum is $\boxed{-2}$. Irrationality of $\sqrt2$ makes this monic quadratic unique.
