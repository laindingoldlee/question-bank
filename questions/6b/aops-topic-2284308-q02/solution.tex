If the side length is $s$ cm, the area is $s^2=49$, and since $s>0$, $s=\boxed{7}$ cm.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((0,0)--(7,0)--(7,7)--(0,7)--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
label("$s$",(3.5,0),S); label("$s$",(7,3.5),E);
label("area $49\ \mathrm{cm}^2$",(3.5,3.5));
\end{asy}
