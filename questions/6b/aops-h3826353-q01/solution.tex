Let $t_1=1000,t_2=x$ and $t_n=t_{n-2}-t_{n-1}$. Repeated use of the recurrence gives
\[
t_{11}=34000-55x,\qquad t_{12}=89x-55000.
\]
Any sequence that remains nonnegative through term $12$ must therefore satisfy
\[
\dfrac{55000}{89}\le x\le\dfrac{34000}{55}.
\]
The endpoints lie strictly between $617,618$ and between $618,619$, respectively. Thus the only integer possibility is $x=618$.

For this value the sequence is
\[
1000,618,382,236,146,90,56,34,22,12,10,2,8,-6.
\]
It has length $14$. Every other positive integer has a negative term by term $12$, so none can match that length. Hence the unique maximizing value is $\boxed{618}$.
