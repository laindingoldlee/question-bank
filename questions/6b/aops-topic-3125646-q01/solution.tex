Raising both sides to the power $\dfrac{8}{3}$ yields $u=2^{\dfrac{8}{3}}=4\sqrt[3]{4}$. Conversely this value has $\dfrac{3}{8}$ power equal to $2$. The phrase a fractional-index root is thus interpretable by reciprocal exponents, once positivity fixes the meaning.
