Label vertices modulo 12, with starting positions 0 and 6. The difference of their four-step displacements is the sum of eight independent equally likely signs, because negating one ant's steps preserves their distribution. To cancel the initial separation modulo 12, that sum must be 6 or $-6$; its magnitude cannot exceed 8. Each case requires seven signs of one kind and one of the other, giving $2\binom81=16$ outcomes among $2^8=256$. Thus $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{16}}$.
