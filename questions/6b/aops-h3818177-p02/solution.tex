Scale the side length to $1$ and put $E=(0,0)$, $A=(1,0)$. The directions of $AB$ and $BC$ are $72^\circ$ and $144^\circ$. Therefore
\[B=(1+\cos72^\circ,\sin72^\circ),\quad C=(1+\cos72^\circ+\cos144^\circ,\sin72^\circ+\sin144^\circ).\]
Since $F=(\dfrac{1}{2},0)$ and $H=\dfrac{1}{2}(F+\dfrac{1}{2}(B+C))$, its coordinates are
\[H=\left(\dfrac{3+2\cos72^\circ+\cos144^\circ}{4},\dfrac{2\sin72^\circ+\sin144^\circ}{4}\right).\]
Both coordinates are positive, and $EF$ lies on the positive horizontal axis, so the tangent of the required angle is their ratio. Using $\cos72^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt5-1}{4}$, $\cos36^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt5+1}{4}$ and the corresponding positive sine values gives
\[\boxed{\dfrac{2\sqrt{10+2\sqrt5}+\sqrt{10-2\sqrt5}}{9+\sqrt5}}.\]
