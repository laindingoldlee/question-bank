Each condition is $x-1\equiv 0\pmod j$. Consequently $x-1$ is a multiple of $\operatorname{lcm}(1,2,\ldots,10)=2^3\cdot 3^2\cdot 5\cdot 7=2520$. The full answer is $\boxed{x=2520k+1\ (k=1,2,\ldots)}$, with least value $\boxed{2521}$.
