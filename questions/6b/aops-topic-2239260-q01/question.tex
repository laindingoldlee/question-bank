A fisher fishes at a lake, making at most one attempt to catch a fish each day.
\begin{itemize}
\item On Mondays and Tuesdays he makes an attempt with probability $80\%$. If he attempts, he succeeds with probability $80\%$ on a Monday and $30\%$ on a Tuesday.
\item On Wednesdays he never fishes.
\item On Thursdays, Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays he makes an attempt with probability $30\%$, and if he attempts, he succeeds with probability $20\%$.
\end{itemize}
All days are independent. What is the probability that during one week, from Monday to Sunday, there are two consecutive days on both of which he catches a fish?
