Use vectors $A=0,B=u=(2,0),D=v=(p,q)$, with $q>0$ and $p^2+q^2=9$. Then $N = \dfrac{2 v}{3}$, $M = u + \dfrac{v}{2}$, and solving $X=tM=u+s(N-u)$ gives $X = \dfrac{4 u}{7} + \dfrac{2 v}{7}$. Write the circle through $N,D,C$ as $|r|^2-L\cdot r+K=0$. Substitution of $N,D$ gives $K=6,L\cdot v=15$; substituting $C=u+v$ gives $L\cdot u=4+4p$. Substituting $X$ now gives $72-80p=0$, so $p = \dfrac{9}{10}$. Thus the parallelogram\textquotesingle s squared area is $4 q^2 = 4 (9 - \dfrac{81}{100}) = \boxed{\dfrac{819}{25}}$. Also $p>0$ implies $BD<AC$, as required.

\end{asy}
