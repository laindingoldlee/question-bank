If the original is $\overline{abc}$, then $a<c$ and $ac\equiv7\pmod{10}$. The digit pairs with $a<c$ are $(1,7),(3,9)$. The second would give a product greater than $300\cdot900$, too large. Thus the numbers are $107+10b$ and $701+10b$. Their product strictly increases for $b=0,\ldots,9$, and $b=2$ gives $127\cdot721=91567$. This proves the unique larger number is $\boxed{721}$.
