Let the level part be $f$ miles and the hill $h$ miles (each way). The total time is
\[\dfrac{f}{8}+\dfrac{h}{5}+\dfrac{h}{20}+\dfrac{f}{8}=\dfrac {f}{4}+\dfrac{4h+h}{20}=\dfrac {f}{4}+\dfrac {h}{4}=7 .\]
So $f+h=28$, and the total distance is $2(f+h)=\boxed{56}$ miles. (Her average speed over a hill climbed at $5$ and descended at $20$ is the harmonic mean $8$ mph, the same as on the level, which is why $f$ and $h$ need not be known separately.)
