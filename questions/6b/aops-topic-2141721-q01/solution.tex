The numbers being squared are $5k-2$ for $k=1,2,\dots,20$ (since $5\cdot20-2=98$). Using $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{20}k=210$ and $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{20}k^2=\dfrac{20\cdot21\cdot41}{6}=2870$,
\[\sum_{k=1}^{20}(5k-2)^2=25\sum k^2-20\sum k+4\cdot20=25\cdot2870-20\cdot210+80=71750-4200+80=\boxed{67630}.\]
