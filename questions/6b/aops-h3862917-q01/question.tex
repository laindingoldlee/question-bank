Two circles $\Omega_1,\Omega_2$ meet at $A,E$. The tangent to $\Omega_1$ at $A$ meets $\Omega_2$ again at $D$. The tangent to $\Omega_2$ at $D$ meets $\Omega_1$ at $B,C$, with $BD>CD$. Given $\dfrac{AE}{CE}=\dfrac{7}{3}$ and $\dfrac{AB}{AC}=\dfrac{5}{4}$, write $\dfrac{BC}{AC}=\dfrac{m}{n}$ in lowest terms and find $m+n$.
