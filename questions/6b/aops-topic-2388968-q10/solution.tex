There are three red cards (queen of hearts, $2$ of diamonds, $4$ of hearts) and three black cards. The red block and the black block can come in $2$ orders, and the cards inside each block in $3!$ orders. The answer is $2\cdot3!\cdot3!=\boxed{72}$.
