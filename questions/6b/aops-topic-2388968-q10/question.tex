Betty has six cards: the king of spades, queen of hearts, ace of clubs, $2$ of diamonds, $3$ of spades and $4$ of hearts. Hearts and diamonds are red; clubs and spades are black. In how many ways can she arrange the cards in a row so that the red cards are all next to each other and the black cards are all next to each other?
