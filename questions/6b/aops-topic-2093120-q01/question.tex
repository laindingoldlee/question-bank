Serena took two real numbers, which may or may not be integers (and may be negative), rounded each of them up to the nearest integer, and multiplied the results; she got $100$. When she instead rounded each of the original numbers down to the nearest integer and multiplied the results, she got $X$. Find the greatest possible value of $X$.
