Let $O$ be the centre, $OA$ the radius, and $PQ$ the chord, crossing $OA$ at its midpoint $M$ at a right angle. Then $OM=6$ and $OP=12$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair ft(pair P,pair U,pair V){return U+dot(P-U,V-U)/dot(V-U,V-U)*(V-U);}
path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s=0.2){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
real R=2; pair O=(0,0), A=(R,0), M=(R/2,0), P=(R/2,R*sqrt(3)/2), Q=(R/2,-R*sqrt(3)/2);
draw(circle(O,R)); draw(O--A,linewidth(0.9)); draw(P--Q,blue+linewidth(0.9)); draw(O--P,red+dashed);
draw(ra(M,P-M,A-M,0.15));
dot(O);dot(A);dot(M);dot(P);dot(Q);
label("$O$",O,W); label("$A$",A,E); label("$M$",M+(0.12,0),SE); label("$P$",P,NE); label("$Q$",Q,SE);
label("$6$",(O+M)/2,S); label("$12$",(O+P)/2,NW,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

In right triangle $OMP$, $PM=\sqrt{12^2-6^2}=\sqrt{108}=6\sqrt3$. The perpendicular from the centre bisects the chord, so
\[
PQ=2\cdot PM=\boxed{12\sqrt3} .
\]
