My friend arrives at a cafe at a uniformly random time between 5:00 and 6:00 and stays $n$ minutes. I arrive at a uniformly random time between 5:15 and 6:15, independently, and stay $15$ minutes. We say we meet if our visits overlap. Over $56$ mornings the expected number of meetings is $21$. Find $n$, assuming $n\le15$.
