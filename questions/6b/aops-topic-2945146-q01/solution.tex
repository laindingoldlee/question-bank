Measure in minutes after 5:00: friend $X\sim U[0,60]$, me $Y\sim U[15,75]$. We meet iff $-n\le X-Y\le15$. With $u=X-Y+15$, the density of $u$ is $\dfrac{60-|u|}{3600}$ on $[-60,60]$. So for $n\le15$
\[P=\dfrac1{3600}\int_{15-n}^{30}(60-u)\,du.\]
We need $P=\dfrac{21}{56}=\dfrac38$, i.e. the integral equals $1350$. At $n=15$ it is $\left[60u-\dfrac{u^2}2\right]_0^{30}=1800-450=1350$, and $P$ is strictly increasing in $n$. So $n=\boxed{15}$.
