The radicand factors as $(2x+17)(x-1)$, so the domain is $x\le-\dfrac{17}{2}$ or $x\ge1$. On the first interval the right side is negative, so the strict inequality always holds. On the second, both sides are nonnegative, so squaring gives $x^2+9x-26>0$. Its relevant root is $\dfrac{-9+\sqrt{185}}{2}>1$. Thus the solution is $\boxed{(-\infty,-\dfrac{17}{2}]\cup(\dfrac{-9+\sqrt{185}}{2},\infty)}$.
