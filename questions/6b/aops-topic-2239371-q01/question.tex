\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] A quadratic $ax^2+bx+c$ ($a\ne0$) has real roots only, and both of them (possibly equal) are negative. Must its coefficients be positive? Prove that $a$, $b$, $c$ all have the same sign.
\item[(b)] Conversely, if $a$, $b$, $c$ are all positive, must the quadratic have real negative roots?
\end{enumerate}
