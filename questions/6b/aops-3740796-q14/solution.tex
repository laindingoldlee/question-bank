Compare the square of the longest side with the sum of the other squares: $14^2=196>25+144=169$. By the law of cosines, the cosine of the angle opposite 14 is $\dfrac{5^2+12^2-14^2}{2\cdot5\cdot12}=-\dfrac{9}{40}<0$. Thus this angle exceeds $90^\circ$ and the triangle is $\boxed{\text{obtuse}}$.
