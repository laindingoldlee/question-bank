Let $R=\sqrt{2018^{4036}+1}$ and $\theta=\arctan(2018^{-2018})$, so $0 < \theta < \dfrac{\pi}{2}$. The 2018 distinct roots are $z_k = R^{\dfrac{1}{2018}} \exp (\dfrac{i (\theta+2\pi k)}{2018})$ for $k=0,\ldots,2017$. The first-quadrant condition is $0<\theta+2\pi k<1009\pi$, yielding $k=0,\ldots,504$, or 505 roots. The second-quadrant condition is $1009\pi<\theta+2\pi k<2018\pi$, giving $k=505,\ldots,1008$, or 504 roots.
