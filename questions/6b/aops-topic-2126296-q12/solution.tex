Let the common difference be $d$ and the number of terms $n\ge2$. Then $20+(n-1)d=2040$, so $(n-1)d=2020$. Hence $d$ is a positive integer dividing $2020$, and conversely each positive divisor $d$ of $2020$ gives exactly one such sequence (with $n=1+\dfrac{2020}{d}$ terms). Since $2020=2^2\cdot5\cdot101$, the number of positive divisors is
\[(2+1)(1+1)(1+1)=\boxed{12}.\]
(If at least three terms were required, $d=2020$ would be excluded, leaving $11$.)
