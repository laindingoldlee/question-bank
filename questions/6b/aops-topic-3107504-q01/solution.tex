Write the target as $ 2n $. Choose the unique $ t\ge0 $ satisfying $\dfrac{t(t+1)}{2}\le n<\dfrac{(t+1)(t+2)}{2}$. Set $ x=n-\dfrac{t(t+1)}{2}$ and $ y=t-x $. The inequalities ensure $ 0\le x\le t $, so both are nonnegative integers. Since $ x+y=t $, the expression becomes $ t^2+t+2x=2n $, as required.
