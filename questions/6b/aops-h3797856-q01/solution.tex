Let $x_i$ be one for a knight and zero for a liar, with cyclic indices. Truthfulness requires $x_i=x_{i-1}+x_{i+1}\pmod2$, because the statement is true exactly when the neighbours differ. Thus $x_{i+1}=x_i+x_{i-1}$ and $x_{i+3}=x_i$. Since $\gcd(3,2026)=1$, all entries are equal. Substituting a constant $x$ gives $x=2x\pmod2$, hence $x=0$. All liars do work, since their neighbours are both liars and every statement is false. Therefore the maximum is $\boxed0$.
