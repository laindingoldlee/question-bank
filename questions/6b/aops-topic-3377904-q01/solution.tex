(a) Stars and bars gives $\binom{26}{6}=\boxed{230230}$. (b) A distribution is a partition of $20$ into at most seven positive parts. Transposing its Ferrers diagram gives a partition into parts at most seven. Let $P(n,k)$ count these, with $P(0,k)=1$, $P(n,0)=0$ for $n>0$, and $P(n,k)=0$ for $n<0$. Classifying by whether a part $k$ occurs gives $P(n,k)=P(n,k-1)+P(n-k,k)$. Applying this recurrence for $k=1,\ldots,7$ gives $P(20,7)=\boxed{364}$. A reproducible table procedure is: start $d_0=1,d_1=\cdots=d_{20}=0$; for each $k=1,\ldots,7$, update $d_n\leftarrow d_n+d_{n-k}$ in increasing order $n=k,\ldots,20$. The final $d_{20}$ is $364$. Multiplying or dividing by $7!$ is invalid because repeated occupancies have different symmetry counts.
