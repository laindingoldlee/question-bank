For positive integers $n$ and $b$ where $b > 1$, define $s_b(n)$ as the sum of digits in the base-$b$ representation of $n$. A positive integer $p$ is said to dominate another positive integer $q$ if for all positive integers $n$, $s_p(n)$ is greater than or equal to $s_q(n)$. Find the number of ordered pairs $(p, q)$ of distinct positive integers between $2$ and $100$ inclusive such that $p$ dominates $q$.
