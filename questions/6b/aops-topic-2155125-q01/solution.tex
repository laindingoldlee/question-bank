An ordered pair of disjoint subsets is the same as a labelling of each of the $7$ numbers $0,1,\dots,6$ with $A$, $B$ or $N$ (in neither set). The condition says that in the string of labels (in the order $0,1,\dots,6$) no two adjacent labels are both $A$ or both $B$. So we count strings of length $7$ over $\{A,B,N\}$ with no $AA$ and no $BB$.

Let $x_k$ be the number of valid strings of length $k$ ending in $N$, and $y_k$ the number ending in $A$ (by symmetry also the number ending in $B$). An $N$ may follow anything, while an $A$ may follow only $N$ or $B$. So
\[x_{k+1}=x_k+2y_k,\qquad y_{k+1}=x_k+y_k,\qquad x_1=y_1=1 .\]
\[\begin{array}{c|ccccccc}
k&1&2&3&4&5&6&7\\\hline
x_k&1&3&7&17&41&99&239\\
y_k&1&2&5&12&29&70&169
\end{array}\]
The number of strings of length $7$ is $x_7+2y_7=239+338=\boxed{577}$.

(The totals $3,7,17,41,99,239,577$ satisfy $t_{k+1}=2t_k+t_{k-1}$.)
