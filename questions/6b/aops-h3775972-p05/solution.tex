Put $g=\gcd(n,30)$, a divisor of $30$. Since $\operatorname{lcm}(n,30)=\dfrac{30n}{g}$, we have $n=\dfrac{576g}{g+30}$. Check $g=1,2,3,5,6,10,15,30$. The integer candidates are $n=36,96,144,192,288$ for $g=2,6,10,15,30$, respectively; only $n=96$ has the corresponding gcd, namely $6$. Thus $\boxed{n=96}$, and its lcm with $30$ is $480$.
