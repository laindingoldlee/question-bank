Factor: $10001=73\cdot137$, and $73=8^2+3^2$, $137=11^2+4^2$. The identity
\[(p^2+q^2)(r^2+s^2)=(pr-qs)^2+(ps+qr)^2\]
gives
\[10001=(8\cdot11-3\cdot4)^2+(8\cdot4+3\cdot11)^2=76^2+65^2=5776+4225.\]
So $10001=\boxed{65^2+76^2}$. (The other sign choice gives $100^2+1^2$, which is excluded.)
