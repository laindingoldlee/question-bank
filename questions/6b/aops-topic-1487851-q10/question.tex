A bottle contains $1000$ grams of a $15\%$ alcohol solution. Then $100$ grams of alcohol solution A and $400$ grams of alcohol solution B are added to the bottle, forming a $14\%$ alcohol solution. The percentage of alcohol in solution A is twice the percentage of alcohol in solution B. What is the percentage of alcohol in solution A?
