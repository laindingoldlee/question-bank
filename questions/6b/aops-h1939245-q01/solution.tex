The hypotenuse is $10$, so its midpoint $O$ is distance $5$ from each vertex. The rotation gives $OC=OC^{\prime}=5$ and $\angle COC^{\prime}=90^\circ$. Hence $CC^{\prime}=\sqrt{5^2+5^2}=5\sqrt2\approx\boxed{7.07}$.
