Let the side be $s$ and the centre $O$. Then $OA=OE=s$ and $\angle AOE=120^\circ$ (two sixths of a turn).

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair[] V; string[] L={"$A$","$B$","$C$","$D$","$E$","$F$"};
for(int k=0;k<6;++k) V[k]=dir(120+60*k);
draw(V[0]--V[1]--V[2]--V[3]--V[4]--V[5]--cycle,linewidth(1));
for(int k=0;k<6;++k) label(L[k],V[k],dir(120+60*k));
draw(V[0]--V[4],red+dashed); draw(V[0]--(0,0)--V[4],gray); dot((0,0)); label("$O$",(0,0),S);
draw(arc((0,0),0.2,0,120),gray);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

In the isosceles triangle $AOE$, the altitude from $O$ splits it into two $30$-$60$-$90$ triangles, so $AE=2\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}s=\sqrt3\,s$. Hence $s=\dfrac{5}{\sqrt3}$, and since the hexagon consists of six equilateral triangles of side $s$,
\[
[ABCDEF]=6\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}s^2=\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}\cdot\dfrac{25}{3}=\boxed{\dfrac{25\sqrt3}{2}}.
\]
