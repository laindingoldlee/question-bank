Write $a=3u,b=3v$ with $\gcd(u,v)=1$. Then the sum is $\dfrac{u+v}{3uv}$. Since $\gcd(u+v,uv)=1$, cancellation is by $g=\gcd(u+v,3)$, either 1 or 3. Thus $u+v=8g$. For sum 8, the unordered coprime pairs are $(1,7),(3,5)$, giving denominators 21,45. For sum 24, they are $(1,23),(5,19),(7,17),(11,13)$, giving denominators 23,95,119,143 after cancellation. The least is 21 and greatest 143; their sum is $\boxed{164}$.
