Given the union condition, each element independently has three equally likely membership states: A only, B only, or both. Thus the probability is $\dfrac{\binom{100}{3}2^{97}}{3^{100}}$. Since $\binom{100}{3}=161700=2^2\cdot3\cdot5^2\cdot7^2\cdot11$, reduction removes one factor of $3$ and gives $m=2^{99}5^27^211$. The number of divisors is $(99+1)(2+1)(2+1)(1+1)=\boxed{1800}$.
