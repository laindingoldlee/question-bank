Sets $A$ and $B$ are independently chosen uniformly among the subsets of $\{1,\ldots,100\}$. Given that their union is the whole set, the probability that their intersection has exactly three elements is $\dfrac{m}{n}$ in lowest terms. How many positive divisors does $m$ have?
