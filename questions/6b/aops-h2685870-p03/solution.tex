Choose $B=(0,0)$, $C=(c,0)$, $A=(u,v)$ with $c>0$ and $v\ne0$. The orthocenter is
\[
H=(u,h),\qquad h=\dfrac{u(c-u)}{v}.
\]
The circumcircle has equation $x^2+y^2-cx+(h-v)y=0$. If $X=(t,0)$, the reflected point is $(2t-u,-h)$. Substituting into the circle equation gives
\[
(2t-u)^2-c(2t-u)+vh
=\bigl((2t-u)-u\bigr)\bigl((2t-u)-(c-u)\bigr)=0.
\]
Thus $t=u$ or $t=\dfrac{c}{2}$. The midpoint of $BC$ always qualifies. The other candidate is the foot of the altitude from $A$, and qualifies exactly when it lies on the closed segment $BC$. If the two candidates coincide, there is only one distinct solution.
