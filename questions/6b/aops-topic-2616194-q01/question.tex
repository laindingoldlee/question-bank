A rectangle with base $1$ and height $2$ is inscribed in an equilateral triangle, with its base on a side of the triangle and its other two vertices on the other two sides. Another rectangle, with height $1$, is inscribed in the same triangle in the same way. Its base can be written as a fully simplified fraction $\dfrac{a+b\sqrt3}{c}$ with $\gcd(a,b,c)=1$. Find $a+b+c$.
