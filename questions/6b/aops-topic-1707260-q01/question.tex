For positive integers $N$ and $r$, let $F(N,r)$ be the number of ways to write $N$ as a sum of $r$ positive integers when the order of the summands does not matter (so $F(5,2)=2$, from $1+4$ and $2+3$), with $F(N,r)=0$ when $N<r$. No simple closed formula for $F(N,r)$ is known for general $r$, but small $r$ can be handled.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $F(N,2)=\left\lfloor\dfrac {N}{2}\right\rfloor$.
\item[(b)] Prove that $F(N,r)=F(N-1,r-1)+F(N-r,r)$ for $2\le r<N$.
\item[(c)] Prove that $F(N,3)$ is the integer nearest to $\dfrac{N^2}{12}$ for every $N\ge1$.
\end{enumerate}
