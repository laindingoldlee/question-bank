Let $s=w+x+y+z$. The equations say
\[s+2z=12,\qquad s+2y=15,\qquad s+2x=18,\qquad s+2w=21 .\]
Adding them gives $6s=66$, so $s=11$. Then $z=\dfrac{1}{2}$, $y=2$, $x=\dfrac{7}{2}$ and $w=5$. The solution is
\[\boxed{(w,x,y,z)=\left(5,\ \dfrac{7}{2},\ 2,\ \dfrac{1}{2}\right)}.\]
(Check: $5+\dfrac{7}{2}+2+\dfrac{3}{2}=12$.)
