In triangle $ABC$, $\angle BAC=90^\circ$ and $BC=2AB$. Points $D$ on $AC$ and $E$ on $AB$ satisfy $\angle ABD=\dfrac{1}{3}\angle ABC$ and $\angle ACE=\dfrac{1}{3}\angle ACB$. Let $BD$ meet $CE$ at $F$. Let $H$ be the reflection of $F$ in line $AC$ (so $HF\perp AC$ and the midpoint of $HF$ lies on $AC$), and let $K$ be the reflection of $F$ in line $BC$. Prove that $H$, $D$, $K$ are collinear and that triangle $DEF$ is isosceles.
