Through $(1,1)$ there are $\binom21\binom93=168$ paths. Through $(4,3)$ there are $\binom73\binom41=140$. Through both there are $\binom21\binom52\binom41=80$. Subtract those twice from the sum to leave exactly one: $168+140-160=\boxed{148}$.

\end{asy}
