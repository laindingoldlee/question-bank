Place the grid center at the origin. Square centers have integer coordinates $(x,y)$ with $-7\le x,y\le7$, and rotate to $(y,-x)$. The squared displacement is $2(x^2+y^2)$. Thus we need $x^2+y^2<\dfrac{9}{2}$, allowing squared norms $0,1,2,4$. These contribute $1,4,4,4$ points, respectively, for a total of $13$. The probability is $\dfrac{13}{225}$, so $\boxed{m+n=238}$.
