$|-2|=2>|1|=1 $, yet $-2<1 $, so absolute value compares distance from zero rather than real-number order. In any ordered field, every nonzero square is positive: either $ x>0 $ or $-x>0 $, and multiplying positives gives $ x^2>0 $. If such an order existed on the complex numbers, $ i^2=-1 $ would be positive. But extension of the real order makes $-1<0 $, a contradiction. Thus expressions such as $ 2i>1 $ have no usual ordered-field meaning; one may compare their moduli instead.
