Let $n$ and $k$ be positive integers. We distribute $n$ identical balls into $k$ distinguishable boxes.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that the number of distributions in which every box gets at least one ball is $\dbinom{n-1}{k-1}$.
\item[(b)] Prove that the number of distributions with no restriction is $\dbinom{n+k-1}{k-1}$.
\item[(c)] Let $m\ge1$ with $n\ge mk$. Prove that the number of distributions in which every box gets at least $m$ balls is $\dbinom{n-1-(m-1)k}{k-1}$.
\end{enumerate}
