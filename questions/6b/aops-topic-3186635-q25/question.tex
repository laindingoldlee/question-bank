On the Cartesian plane, a line $\ell$ intersects a parabola with a vertical axis of symmetry at $(0, 5)$ and $(4, 4)$. The focus $F$ of the parabola lies below $\ell$,  and the distance from $F$ to $\ell$ is $\dfrac{16}{\sqrt{17}}$. Let the vertex of the parabola be $(x, y)$. The sum of all possible values of $y$ can be expressed as $\dfrac{p}{q}$ for relatively prime positive integers $p, q$. Find $p + q$.
