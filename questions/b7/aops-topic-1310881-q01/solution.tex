The speeds are $40$, $24$ and $48$ km/h. The times are $\dfrac{5}{40}=\dfrac{1}{8}$ h, $\dfrac{6}{24}=\dfrac{1}{4}$ h and $\dfrac{6}{48}=\dfrac{1}{8}$ h, a total of $\dfrac{1}{2}$ h for $17$ km. The average speed is $\dfrac{17}{\dfrac{1}{2}}=\boxed{34\text{ km/h}}$.
