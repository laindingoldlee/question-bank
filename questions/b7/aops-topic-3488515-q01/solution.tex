Multiplying by $12$ gives $4(2x+1)=3(x+5)$. Thus $8x+4=3x+15$, so $5x=11$ and $\boxed{x=\dfrac{11}{5}}$. The denominators are fixed nonzero constants, so no solution is lost or introduced.
