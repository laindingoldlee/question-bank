The apple total is $\dfrac{k(k+1)}{2}$, and the pear total is $ 6k $. Equating and dividing by $ k>0 $ gives $ k+1=12 $, so $ k=11 $. The money is $ 6\cdot11=\boxed{66}$ dollars.
