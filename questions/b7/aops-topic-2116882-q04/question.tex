In the sequence
\[1,\ 6,\ 6,\ 20,\ 15,\ 42,\ 28,\ \dots\]
the terms in odd positions follow one simple pattern and the terms in even positions follow another. Identify the two patterns and find the $8$th and $9$th terms.
