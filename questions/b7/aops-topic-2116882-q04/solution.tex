\textbf{Odd positions.} The $1$st, $3$rd, $5$th and $7$th terms are $1,6,15,28$. These are triangular numbers $T_n=\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$ with $n$ equal to the position: $T_1=1$, $T_3=6$, $T_5=15$, $T_7=28$.

\textbf{Even positions.} The $2$nd, $4$th and $6$th terms are $6=2\cdot3$, $20=4\cdot5$ and $42=6\cdot7$, that is, $n(n+1)=2T_n$ where $n$ is the position.

So the $n$th term is $\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$ when $n$ is odd and $n(n+1)$ when $n$ is even. The $8$th term is $8\cdot9=\boxed{72}$, and the $9$th term is $\dfrac{9\cdot10}{2}=\boxed{45}$.
