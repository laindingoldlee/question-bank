Trailing zeros do not affect the digit sum, so consider $N=654000003987=654\cdot10^9+3987$. Then
\[
N^2=654^2\cdot10^{18}+2\cdot654\cdot3987\cdot10^9+3987^2=427716\cdot10^{18}+5214996\cdot10^{9}+15896169 .
\]
The three pieces do not overlap when written out: $15896169<10^9$ occupies the last $9$ places (with a leading $0$), and $5214996<10^9$ occupies the next $9$ places. So
\[
N^2=427716\,005214996\,015896169,
\]
and its digit sum is the sum of the digit sums of the three pieces:
\[
(4+2+7+7+1+6)+(5+2+1+4+9+9+6)+(1+5+8+9+6+1+6+9)=27+36+45=\boxed{108}.
\]
