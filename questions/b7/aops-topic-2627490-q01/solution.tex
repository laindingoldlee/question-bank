\emph{Recursive computation.} Let $b_j=x^{2^j}+x^{-2^j}$. Then $b_0=3$ and, by squaring,
\[b_{j+1}=b_j^2-2,\]
giving $b_1=7$, $b_2=47$, $b_3=2207$, and so on; the answer is $b_7$.

\emph{Closed form.} From $x^2-3x+1=0$, $x=\dfrac{3\pm\sqrt5}{2}$, and the two values are reciprocals of each other. Either way,
\[x^{128}+\dfrac1{x^{128}}=\boxed{\left(\dfrac{3+\sqrt5}{2}\right)^{128}+\left(\dfrac{3-\sqrt5}{2}\right)^{128}}.\]
Since $\dfrac{3+\sqrt5}{2}=\left(\dfrac{1+\sqrt5}{2}\right)^2$, this is the Lucas number $L_{256}\approx3.17\times10^{53}$, an integer.
