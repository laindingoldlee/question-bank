Scale temporarily to a unit cube with $A=(0,0,0)$ and $B=(1,1,1)$. Unfolding two adjacent faces gives a $2$ by $1$ rectangle with shortest path length $\sqrt5$ and crossing at an edge midpoint. Thus $C=(1,\dfrac{1}{2},0)$, $D_1=(\dfrac{1}{2},1,0)$ and $D_2=(0,\dfrac{1}{2},1)$ are all possible crossing points. For every one, the squared distances to $A$ and $B$ are $\dfrac{5}{4}$. The target expression is therefore $2-CD^2$. With $D=D_1$ it is $\dfrac{3}{2}$, whereas with $D=D_2$ it is $0$. Rescaling by $13^2$ gives $\dfrac{507}{2}$ or $0$. Hence the statement does not determine a unique answer unless it specifies which points $C,D$ mean.

\end{asy}
