There are $6!=720$ seatings in all. If $A$ and $B$ are neighbours, glue them into a block: the block and the other $5$ people form $6$ units around the table, arranged in $5!=120$ ways, and the block can be $AB$ or $BA$, giving $240$ seatings. So $A$ is not next to $B$ in $720-240=\boxed{480}$ seatings.
