Equality of the row sums gives $4+8+p=6+11+q$, hence $p-q=5$. Two odd primes have even difference, so one of $p,q$ is $2$. Since $p>q$, $q=2$ and $p=7$, both prime and distinct from the four shown entries. The hidden-number sum is $\boxed9$.
