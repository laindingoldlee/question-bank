The sum has $n-99$ terms with average $\dfrac{100+n}{2}$, so $(n-99)(n+100)=5600$, i.e. $n^2+n-15500=0$. The discriminant is $1+62000=62001=249^2$, so $n=\dfrac{-1+249}{2}=\boxed{124}$. Check: $25$ terms with average $112$ give $2800$.
