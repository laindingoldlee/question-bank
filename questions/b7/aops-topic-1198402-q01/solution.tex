A distribution is determined by the numbers $t_1,\dots,t_5$ of taffy pieces ($0\le t_i\le3$, $\sum t_i=7$); child $i$ then gets $3-t_i$ licorice, and the licorice total is automatically $15-7=8$. By inclusion--exclusion on the condition $t_i\ge4$:
\[\dbinom{11}{4}-\dbinom51\dbinom74=330-175=\boxed{155}.\]
