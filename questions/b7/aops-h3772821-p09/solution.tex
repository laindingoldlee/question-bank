Kairat adds fifteen minutes to his displayed time, so his clock must show $11{:}45$ when he concludes noon. Since it is actually ten minutes fast, the true time is $11{:}35$. Maksat\textquotesingle s slow clock shows $11{:}20$, and he subtracts ten minutes because he thinks it is fast. He therefore believes the time is $\boxed{11{:}10}$.
