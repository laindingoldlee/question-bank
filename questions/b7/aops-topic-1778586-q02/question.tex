A square is inscribed in a circle of radius $1$, and the region inside the circle but outside the square is shaded. Then a circle is inscribed in the square, a square is inscribed in this new circle, and the region between the new circle and the new square is shaded. This process is repeated infinitely many times. What is the total area of the shaded region?
