My mail arrives at a random time between 3 p.m.\ and 5 p.m. I arrive home at a random time between 4 p.m.\ and 5 p.m.\ and check my mail. Both times are uniformly distributed and independent. What is the probability that my mail has been delivered when I check it? Express your answer as a common fraction.
