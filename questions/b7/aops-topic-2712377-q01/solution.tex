Number the days $1,2,3,\dots$. Al rests on days $d\equiv0\pmod4$, and Barb on days $d\equiv8,9,0\pmod{10}$. Both conditions depend only on $d\bmod20$. Of the residues $8,9,10,18,19,0$ mod $20$ (Barb's rest-days), the multiples of $4$ are $8$ and $0$. So there are $2$ common rest-days in every block of $20$ days, and $\dfrac{1000}{20}\cdot2=\boxed{\text{(E) }100}$.
