Take $A=(0,0)$, $B=(14,0)$, $D=(5,0)$. Since $AC=CD=10$, point $C$ lies on the perpendicular bisector of $AD$:
\[
C=\left(\dfrac{5}{2},\dfrac{5\sqrt{15}}{2}\right).
\]
The angle at $A$ has cosine $\dfrac{1}{4}$. The two intersections of the ray $AC$ with the circle centered at $B$ of radius $14$ are $A$ and $E$; the distance equation gives $AE=2AB\cos A=7$. Thus
\[
E=\left(\dfrac{7}{4},\dfrac{7\sqrt{15}}{4}\right).
\]
Intersecting $BE$ and $CD$ gives $F=(\dfrac{7}{2},\dfrac{3\sqrt{15}}{2})$; substitution in both line equations verifies it. The shoelace formula in the required boundary order $A,C,F,B$ gives
\[
[ACFB]=\dfrac{1}{2}\left|
\dfrac{15\sqrt{15}}{4}-\dfrac{35\sqrt{15}}{4}-21\sqrt{15}
\right|=\boxed{13\sqrt{15}}.
\]
