Choose $x$ from $0,1,2,3,4,5$ and put $y=5-x$. The solutions are $\boxed{(0,5),(1,4),(2,3),(3,2),(4,1),(5,0)}$. The displayed fractional pair satisfies the equation but its coordinates are not integers, so it is a real solution rather than an integer solution.
