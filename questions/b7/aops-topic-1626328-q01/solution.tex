The common denominator is $(a-b)(a+b)(x+y)=(a^2-b^2)(x+y)$. The numerator is
\[
a(x+y)(a+b)-(bx-ay)(a-b).
\]
Expanding,
\[
a(a+b)(x+y)=a^2x+a^2y+abx+aby,\qquad (bx-ay)(a-b)=abx-b^2x-a^2y+aby,
\]
and the difference is $a^2x+2a^2y+b^2x$. Hence
\[
\dfrac{a(x+y)}{(a-b)(x+y)}-\dfrac{bx-ay}{(a+b)(x+y)}=\boxed{\dfrac{(a^2+b^2)x+2a^2y}{(a^2-b^2)(x+y)}}.
\]
The numerator does not share a factor with the denominator in general (for example, with $a=2$, $b=1$, $x=y=1$ the value is $\dfrac{13}{6}$), so this is fully simplified. (The first fraction alone simplifies to $\dfrac{a}{a-b}$.)
