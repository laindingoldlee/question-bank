Among $1,2,\ldots,34$, each multiple of $2$ contributes a factor $2$, each multiple of $4$ an extra factor $2$, each multiple of $8$ another, and so on. So the exponent of $2$ in $34!$ is
\[\left\lfloor\dfrac{34}{2}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{34}{4}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{34}{8}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{34}{16}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{34}{32}\right\rfloor=17+8+4+2+1=\boxed{32}.\]
For example, $24=2^3\cdot3$ is counted in the first three terms, once for each of its three factors $2$.
