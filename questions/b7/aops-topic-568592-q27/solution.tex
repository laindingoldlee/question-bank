Let $M$ be the midpoint of the hypotenuse $BC$. In a right triangle the midpoint of the hypotenuse is the circumcentre, so $MA=MB=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$. Triangle $MAB$ is isosceles with base angles $75^\circ$, so $\angle AMB=180^\circ-2\cdot75^\circ=30^\circ$.

Let $H$ be the foot of the altitude from $A$. In the right triangle $AHM$ with $\angle AMH=30^\circ$,
\[
AH=MA\sin30^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}.
\]
So the area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot BC\cdot AH=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\sqrt3\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{8}}$.

(With trigonometry: the legs are $\sqrt3\cos75^\circ$ and $\sqrt3\sin75^\circ$, so the area is $\dfrac{3}{2}\sin75^\circ\cos75^\circ=\dfrac{3}{4}\sin150^\circ=\dfrac{3}{8}$.)
