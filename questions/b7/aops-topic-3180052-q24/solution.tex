Every denominator is nonzero modulo 2017. The identity $i^{-1}(i+1)^{-1}=i^{-1}-(i+1)^{-1}$ telescopes to $1-2016^{-1}$. Since $2016\equiv-1$, its inverse is $-1$, and the answer is $\boxed2$.
