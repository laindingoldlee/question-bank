(a) For any numbers $a,b$,
\[
(a+b)^2+(a-b)^2=\left(a^2+2ab+b^2\right)+\left(a^2-2ab+b^2\right)=2\left(a^2+b^2\right).
\]
For $a=3$, $b=4$ this is $7^2+(-1)^2=7^2+1^2=50$.

(b) For any numbers $a,b,c$,
\[
(a+b+c)^2+(a-b)^2+(b-c)^2+(c-a)^2=3\left(a^2+b^2+c^2\right).
\]
Indeed, $(a+b+c)^2=a^2+b^2+c^2+2(ab+bc+ca)$, and
\[
(a-b)^2+(b-c)^2+(c-a)^2=2\left(a^2+b^2+c^2\right)-2(ab+bc+ca);
\]
adding the two gives $3\left(a^2+b^2+c^2\right)$. For example, $3\left(1^2+2^2+3^2\right)=42=6^2+1^2+1^2+2^2$. If $a,b,c$ are integers, the four squares are squares of integers.
