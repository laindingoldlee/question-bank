\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that twice the sum of two squares is again a sum of two squares; for example $2\left(3^2+4^2\right)=7^2+1^2$.
\item[(b)] (Lewis Carroll) Prove that three times the sum of three squares is a sum of four squares.
\end{enumerate}
