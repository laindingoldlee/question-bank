The points lie on the same side with probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$, in which case the condition always holds. They lie on adjacent sides with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Their distances $u,v$ from the common corner are then independent uniform variables in $[0,1]$; the condition $u^2+v^2\le1$ occupies a quarter-unit-disk of area $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$. Opposite sides occur with probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$ and contribute probability zero, since only exactly aligned points have distance $1$. Therefore the answer is $\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{\pi}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{2+\pi}{8}}$.
