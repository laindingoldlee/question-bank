For general $N$, $L(x,y)=\binom{x+y}{x}$. Applying the hockey-stick identity twice gives $\sum_{x=0}^N\sum_{y=0}^N\binom{x+y}{x}=\sum_{x=0}^N\binom{x+N+1}{x+1}=\binom{2N+2}{N+1}-1$. Thus $k=2021,j=1$ works. Central binomial coefficients increase strictly, and the immediately preceding one is smaller than $S$ (the successive ratio is greater than two), so no smaller $k$ can leave a positive $j$. Hence $\boxed{k+j=2022}$.
