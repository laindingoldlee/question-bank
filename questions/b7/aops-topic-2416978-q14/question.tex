In an equilateral triangle with side length $x$, each angle is trisected, and the trisectors are extended until they meet the opposite sides. The six trisectors bound a small hexagon around the centre of the triangle. Find the area of this hexagon when $x=12$, and find a formula for its area in terms of $x$.
