An odd integer can be written $2k+1$ with $k$ an integer. Then
\[
(2k+1)^2=4k^2+4k+1=4k(k+1)+1 .
\]
Of the two consecutive integers $k$ and $k+1$, one is even, so $k(k+1)$ is even, say $k(k+1)=2m$. Hence
\[
(2k+1)^2=8m+1,
\]
which is one more than a multiple of $8$.
