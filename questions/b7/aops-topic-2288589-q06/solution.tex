Since $9^{100}=10^{100}\cdot0.9^{100}$ and $0<0.9^{100}<0.9$,
\[0<9^{100}<0.9\cdot10^{100}=9\cdot10^{99}.\]
Therefore
\[10^{99}=10^{100}-9\cdot10^{99}<10^{100}-9^{100}<10^{100}.\]
Every integer $N$ with $10^{99}\le N<10^{100}$ has exactly $100$ digits, so $10^{100}-9^{100}$ has $\boxed{100}$ digits.
