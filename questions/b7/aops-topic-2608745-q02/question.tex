The Duke of York had $10000$ men, each with one hat. He marched $\dfrac78$ of them up the hill, and then marched $\dfrac{49}{50}$ of these men down again. On the way down, $20\%$ of the men marching down lost their hats, and nobody found a lost hat again. The Duke then called all his men together for a hat count. How many hats did he count?
