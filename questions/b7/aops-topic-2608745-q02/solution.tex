$\dfrac78\cdot10000=8750$ men went up, and $\dfrac{49}{50}\cdot8750=8575$ came down. Of these, $20\%$, i.e. $\dfrac15\cdot8575=1715$, lost their hats. All other hats remain, so the count is $10000-1715=\boxed{8285}$.
