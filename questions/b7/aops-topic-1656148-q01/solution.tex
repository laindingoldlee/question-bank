Compare the values: (a) $4$; (b) $0.75\cdot5=3.75$; (c) $2\sqrt3=\sqrt{12}<\sqrt{12.25}=3.5$; (d) $\dfrac{24}{5}=4.8$; (e) $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}<\dfrac{3\cdot3.2}{2}=4.8$. All are below $5$, and the largest is $\boxed{\textbf{(d)}\ \dfrac{24}{5}}$ (as $\pi<3.2$, choice (e) is about $4.71$).
