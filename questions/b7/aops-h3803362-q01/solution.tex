Fix $R_1=r$, where $r$ is any integer from 1 to 6. There are three choices of opposite parity for $R_2$, two distinct choices $r,7-r$ for $R_3$, and one choice for $R_4$. The two choices for $R_3$ are distinct because $2r=7$ has no integer solution. Independence gives the conditional probability $\dfrac{3}{6}\cdot\dfrac{2}{6}\cdot\dfrac{1}{6}=\dfrac{1}{36}$ for every $r$. Averaging over the six equally likely first rolls leaves the same answer, $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{36}}$.
