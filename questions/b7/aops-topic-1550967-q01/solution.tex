Let Miki be $m$ years old now, so Jenny is $m+4$. Five years ago: $m+4-5=2(m-5)$, so $m-1=2m-10$ and $m=9$. Miki is $\boxed{9}$ and Jenny is $\boxed{13}$. (Check: five years ago they were $4$ and $8$.)
