If $u_n$ is the count after $n$ minutes, $u_{n+1}=2u_n-1$. Thus $u_{n+1}-1=2(u_n-1)$ and $u_n=2^n+1$. At 60 minutes this is $2^{60}+1$. The least allowed base is 2 and the least positive remainder is 1, so $\boxed{a+b+c=63}$.
