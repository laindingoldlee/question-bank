The polynomial is $(x-1)(x-2)\cdots(x-7)$. Multiply one factor at a time:
\begin{align*}
(x-1)(x-2)&=x^2-3x+2,\\
\times(x-3)&:\ x^3-6x^2+11x-6,\\
\times(x-4)&:\ x^4-10x^3+35x^2-50x+24,\\
\times(x-5)&:\ x^5-15x^4+85x^3-225x^2+274x-120,\\
\times(x-6)&:\ x^6-21x^5+175x^4-735x^3+1624x^2-1764x+720,\\
\times(x-7)&:\ x^7-28x^6+322x^5-1960x^4+6769x^3-13132x^2+13068x-5040.
\end{align*}
So the answer is
\[\boxed{x^7-28x^6+322x^5-1960x^4+6769x^3-13132x^2+13068x-5040}.\]
Checks by Vieta's formulas: the sum of the roots is $28$, their product is $7!=5040$, and the sum of their pairwise products is $\dfrac{28^2-(1^2+\cdots+7^2)}{2}=\dfrac{784-140}2=322$.
