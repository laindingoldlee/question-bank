Since $2021=43\cdot47$, the trailing-zero count is the smaller of the two prime valuations. They are $\left\lfloor\dfrac{2021}{43}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{2021}{43^2}\right\rfloor=47+1=48$ and $\left\lfloor\dfrac{2021}{47}\right\rfloor=43$. Therefore the count is $\boxed{43}$.
