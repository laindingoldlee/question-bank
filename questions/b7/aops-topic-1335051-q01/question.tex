\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove the pigeonhole principle: if more than $kn$ objects are put into $n$ boxes, then some box contains at least $k+1$ objects.
\item[(b)] Show that among any $13$ people, two were born in the same month.
\item[(c)] Show that among any $5$ integers, two have a difference divisible by $4$.
\end{enumerate}
