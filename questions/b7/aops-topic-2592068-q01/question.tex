Prove the converse of the Pythagorean theorem without constructing a second triangle: if a triangle has side lengths $a$, $b$, $c$ with $a^2+b^2=c^2$, then the angle opposite the side $c$ is a right angle.

(Hint: show that $c^2<a^2+b^2$ when that angle is acute and $c^2>a^2+b^2$ when it is obtuse.)
