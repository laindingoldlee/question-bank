Only water evaporates, so the dry matter stays the same. One week ago the dry matter was $10\%$ of $100$ kg, that is, $10$ kg. Now the dry matter is $100\%-80\%=20\%$ of the fruit, so the fruit weighs
\[
\dfrac{10}{0.2}=\boxed{50}\ \text{kg}.
\]
