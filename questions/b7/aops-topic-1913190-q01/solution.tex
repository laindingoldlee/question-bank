Altogether there were $90+80+70+60=300$ correct solutions. Each student solved at most $3$ problems, so the $100$ students produced at most $300$ solutions; since there are exactly $300$, every student solved exactly $3$ problems, i.e.\ missed exactly one. The numbers of students who missed problems $1,2,3,4$ are $10,20,30,40$ (adding to $100$, as they must). A student solved both problems $3$ and $4$ exactly when the problem they missed was $1$ or $2$, so the answer is $10+20=\boxed{30}$.
