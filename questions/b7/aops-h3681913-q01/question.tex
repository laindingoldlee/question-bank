Consider the Hyperbola $\dfrac{x^2}{100}-\dfrac{y^2}{64}=1$ with focii at $S_1$ and $S,$ where $S$ lies on the positive $x$ axis. Let $P$ be a point on the hyperbola, in the first quadrant. Let $\angle SPS_1=\alpha,$ with $\alpha<\dfrac{\pi}{2}.$ The straight line passing through the point $S$ and having the slope as that of the tangent at $P$ to the hyperbola, intersects the straight line $S_1P$ at $P_1.$ Let $\delta$ be the distance of $P$ from the straight line $SP_1,$ and $\beta=S_1P.$ Then find the greatest integer less than or equal to $\dfrac{\beta\delta}{9} \mathrm{sin}   \dfrac{\alpha}{2}.$
