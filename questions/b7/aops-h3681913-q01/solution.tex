Here $a=10$, $b=8$, and the focal distance is $S_1S=2\sqrt{a^2+b^2}=4\sqrt{41}$. Write $r=PS$ and $\beta=PS_1$. On the right branch, $\beta-r=2a=20$.

The tangent to a hyperbola bisects the angle between the rays from $P$ to the two foci. One way to see this is to differentiate the constant distance difference: its normal is the difference of the two unit focal vectors, which is perpendicular to their sum. Since $SP_1$ is parallel to this tangent, the distance from $P$ to $SP_1$ is
\[
\delta=r\sin(\dfrac{\alpha}{2}).
\]
By the cosine rule in the focal triangle,
\[
(4\sqrt{41})^2=(\beta-r)^2+4\beta r\sin^2(\dfrac{\alpha}{2}).
\]
It follows that $\beta r\sin^2(\dfrac{\alpha}{2})=\dfrac{(656-400)}{4}=64$. Therefore the requested expression is always $\dfrac{64}{9}$, and its floor is $\boxed7$.
