Assume the two shots are independent with common success probability $\dfrac{p}{100}$. The probability of making both is one half, so $(\dfrac{p}{100})^2=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Thus $p=\dfrac{100}{\sqrt2}\approx70.71$, rounding to the nearest ten percent as $\boxed{70\%}$. This is a calculation from the hypothetical probability data, not a factual season statistic.
