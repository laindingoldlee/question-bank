Let $P(x)$ and $Q(x)$ be polynomials with real coefficients, and suppose $P(x)$ divides $Q(x)$, that is, $Q(x)=P(x)R(x)$ for some polynomial $R(x)$ with real coefficients. Must $P$ and $Q$ have integer coefficients? Justify your answer.
