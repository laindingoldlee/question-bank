If every initial tile is 2, there are ten merge levels, from 2 to 4 up to 1024 to 2048. At each level the merged output values sum to 2048, so the total is $10\cdot2048=20480$. Replacing two initial 2 tiles and their merge by a spawned 4 reduces the score by 4 without affecting later merges. At most 512 such bottom-level merges can be omitted, and all can be omitted by starting entirely with 4 tiles. This gives a minimum $20480-512\cdot4=18432$. Thus the minimum and maximum are $\boxed{18432\text{ and }20480}$.
