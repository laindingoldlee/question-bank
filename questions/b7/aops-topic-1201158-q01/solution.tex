All ordered triples of distinct cards, with each showing a random side, are equally likely, and the observation only depends on which cards are where, so we count ordered triples (left, middle, right) consistent with what we see: left and right are two different cards numbered $2$, and the middle is a heart different from both.
\begin{itemize}
\item Neither $2$-card is the $2$ of hearts: $3\cdot2=6$ ordered choices, then $4$ hearts for the middle: $24$.
\item One of them is the $2$ of hearts: $2\cdot3=6$ ordered choices, then $3$ hearts for the middle: $18$.
\end{itemize}
The probability is $\dfrac{18}{24+18}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{7}}$.
