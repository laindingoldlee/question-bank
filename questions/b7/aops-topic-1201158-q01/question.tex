A special deck has $16$ cards: for each of the four suits (clubs, diamonds, hearts, spades) there are cards numbered $1,2,3,4$; each card shows its suit on one side and its number on the other. After shuffling, three cards are dealt in a row on the table: the left and right cards show the number $2$, and the middle card shows a heart. What is the probability that one of the two cards showing $2$ has a heart on its other side?
