A lunar elevator has only two buttons: $[+1]$ and $[\times2]$. Pressing a button moves the elevator to another floor according to the chosen operation. For example, if the elevator is initially on floor $9$ and $[+1]$ is pressed, it goes to $10$. If then $[\times2]$ is pressed, it goes to $20$. What is the minimum number of button presses needed to go from floor $1$ to floor $200$?
