The nine-press sequence $1,2,3,6,12,24,25,50,100,200$ works. To prove optimality, give a positive integer the weight (binary digit count)+(binary one count)-2. Doubling increases this weight by one. Adding one increases it by at most one: a carry replaces trailing ones with zeros, while a no-carry addition adds just one one. The starting weight is zero; $200=(11001000)_2$ has eight digits and three ones, hence weight nine. At least nine presses are required, so the minimum is $\boxed9$.
