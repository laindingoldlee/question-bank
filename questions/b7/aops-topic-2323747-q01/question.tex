Each day a man arrives at the train station after work, and his wife, who always arrives exactly on time, meets him there and drives him home. One day he catches an earlier train and arrives at the station an hour early. He immediately starts walking home along the route his wife drives. His wife, who left home at the usual time, meets him on her way to the station, picks him up and drives him the rest of the way home. They arrive home $20$ minutes earlier than usual. How long did the man walk?
