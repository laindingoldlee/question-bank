The wife drives at her usual speed and saves $20$ minutes in total, because she does not drive the stretch from the meeting point to the station and back. So that stretch takes her $10$ minutes one way: she met her husband $10$ minutes before she would usually reach the station, that is, $10$ minutes before his usual arrival time.

He reached the station $60$ minutes before his usual arrival time and walked until $10$ minutes before it, so he walked for $\boxed{50\text{ minutes}}$.
