Urn A contains $4$ white balls and $2$ red balls. Urn B contains $3$ red balls and $3$ black balls. An urn is chosen at random (each with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$) and a ball is drawn from it and \emph{not} returned. Then again an urn is chosen at random and a ball is drawn from it. What is the probability that the first ball drawn was red, given that the second ball drawn was black?
