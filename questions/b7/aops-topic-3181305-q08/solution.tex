Let $u=g(3)$ and $v=g(-\dfrac{1}{3})$. The equations at 3 and $-\dfrac{1}{3}$ are $4u-\dfrac{v}{3}=17$ and $4v+3u=17$. Multiplying the first by 3 and eliminating $v$ yields $51u=221$, so $u=\dfrac{13}{3}$. Hence $\boxed{a+b=16}$.
