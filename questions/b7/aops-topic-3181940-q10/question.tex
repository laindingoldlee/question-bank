Concatenate $2^1,2^2,\ldots,2^{2023}$ in decimal order to form $X$, and let $Y=\prod_{k=1}^{2023}2^k$. If $2^a,2^b$ are the greatest powers of 2 dividing $X,Y$, find $\dfrac{b}{a}$.
