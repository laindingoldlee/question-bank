The concatenation through $2^3$ is 248, with exactly three factors of 2. If the concatenation through $2^{k-1}$ has valuation $k-1$ and $k\ge4$, appending $2^k$ gives $10^dX_{k-1}+2^k$, where $d\ge2$. The first term has valuation at least $k+1$, so the new valuation is exactly $k$. Induction gives $a=2023$. Meanwhile $b=1+\cdots+2023=2023\cdot1012$. Thus $\boxed{\dfrac{b}{a}=1012}$.
