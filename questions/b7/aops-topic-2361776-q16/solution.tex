The semiperimeter is $s=\dfrac{13+14+15}{2}=21$. By Heron's formula the area is
\[K=\sqrt{21\cdot(21-13)(21-14)(21-15)}=\sqrt{21\cdot8\cdot7\cdot6}=\sqrt{7056}=84.\]
Joining the incentre $I$ to the vertices splits the triangle into three triangles with bases $13$, $14$, $15$ and common height $r$, so $K=\dfrac12r(13+14+15)=rs$. Hence
\[r=\dfrac Ks=\dfrac{84}{21}=\boxed{4}.\]
