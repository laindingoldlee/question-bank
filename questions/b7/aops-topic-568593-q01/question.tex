Dr.\ Math's four-digit house number $ABCD$ contains no zeros and can be split into two different two-digit primes ``$AB$'' and ``$CD$'', where the digits $A$, $B$, $C$ and $D$ are not necessarily distinct. If each of the two-digit primes is less than $40$, how many such house numbers are possible? Explain why the answer is not $8\cdot7\cdot2$.
