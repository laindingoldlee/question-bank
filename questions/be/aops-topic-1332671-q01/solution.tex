Expanding, the expression is $(a+b+c)+2(ab+bc+ca)$. By Vieta's formulas, $a+b+c=-\dfrac{4}{5}$ and $ab+bc+ca=-\dfrac{8}{5}$. Hence the value is
\[
-\dfrac{4}{5}+2\left(-\dfrac{8}{5}\right)=-\dfrac{20}{5}=\boxed{-4}.
\]
(The cubic actually has only one real root, near $-1.94$; the other two roots are non-real, which does not affect the computation.)
