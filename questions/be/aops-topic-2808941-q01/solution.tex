The net displacement is 3 miles east and 4 miles north. The Pythagorean theorem gives $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=\boxed{5}$ miles.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(0,5),C=(3,5),D=(3,4);draw(A--B--C--D);draw(A--D,blue);label("start",A,SW);label("finish",D,SE);label("3",(1.5,5),N);label("5",(0,2.5),W);label("1",(3,4.5),E);
\end{asy}
