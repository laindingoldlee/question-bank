In quadrilateral $AFCD$, triangles $ADC$ and $AFC$ are congruent isosceles triangles with $DA=DC$ and $FA=FC$, and $D$ and $F$ lie on opposite sides of $AC$. The line through $A$ perpendicular to $AD$ and the line through $C$ perpendicular to $CD$ meet at $F$. A point $B$ outside the quadrilateral satisfies $AB=8$ and $\angle CAB=75^\circ$, and segment $CB$ crosses side $AF$ at a point $E$ with $AE:EF=4:5$. Find $CB$ exactly, and round it to the nearest integer.
