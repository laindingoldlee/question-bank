I wrote down my friend's telephone number, but my note has only six digits, while the real number has seven: I left out one digit, and I do not know which digit or where. (A telephone number may start with any digit, including $0$.) How many different telephone numbers must I try to be sure of reaching my friend?

$\textbf{(A)}\ 55\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ 60\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ 64\qquad\textbf{(D)}\ 70\qquad\textbf{(E)}\ 80$
