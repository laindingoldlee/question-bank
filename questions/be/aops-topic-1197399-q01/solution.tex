There are $7$ places to insert a digit and $10$ choices of digit, giving $70$ insertions, but some give the same number: inserting a digit $d$ just before or just after an existing digit $d$ gives the same result. Count distinct results by always inserting the new digit in the leftmost possible equivalent place, i.e. never directly after a copy of itself. At the front all $10$ digits are allowed; after each of the $6$ written digits, $9$ digits are allowed (all but that digit). That gives $10+6\cdot9=\boxed{64}$, answer $\textbf{(C)}$.
