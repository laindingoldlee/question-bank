Triangle $ABC$ has $AB=13$, $BC=14$, $CA=15$. Let $H$ be its orthocenter, $M$ the midpoint of $BC$, and $F$ the foot of the altitude from $C$ to $AB$. Let $K$ lie on line $BC$ with $HK\perp HM$, and put $P=HK\cap AB$. The circumcircle of $FPK$ meets line $BC$ again at $Q\ne K$. Find $QK$.
