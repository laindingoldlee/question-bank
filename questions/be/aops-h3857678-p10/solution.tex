Divisibility by five forces $B=4$ or 9. Divisibility by eleven gives $A+B+C-15\equiv0\pmod{11}$. Since $1\le A\le9$ and the other digits lie from zero to nine, the only possible sums are 4,15,26; the value 4 is impossible because $B\ge4$ and $A\ge1$. Both 15 and 26 occur, for example $(A,B,C)=(2,4,9)$ and $(8,9,9)$. Thus there are $\boxed{2}$ values, choice C.
