The three points fail to form a non-degenerate triangle exactly when they are collinear (coincident points count as collinear).

A line meets the boundary of a convex polygon either in at most two points or in a segment lying along one side. Hence three distinct collinear points on the boundary all lie on the same side of the square. Conversely, three points on one side are collinear. (Corners, which lie on two sides, and coincidences occur with probability $0$.)

Each point lies on a given side with probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$, so the probability that all three lie on the same side is $4\cdot\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^3=\dfrac{1}{16}$. Therefore the required probability is
\[1-\dfrac{1}{16}=\boxed{\dfrac{15}{16}}.\]
