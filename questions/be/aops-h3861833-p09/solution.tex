The angle $AOB$ subtends the diameter of the smaller semicircle, so it is $90^\circ$. Hence $AB=\sqrt2$, and the semicircle has radius $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}$ and area $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$. The minor segment of the unit circle beyond $AB$ has area $\dfrac{\pi}{4}-\dfrac{1}{2}$. The two pieces meet only along $AB$, so their total is $\boxed{\dfrac{\pi-1}{2}}$.

\end{asy}
