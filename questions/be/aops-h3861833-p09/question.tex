Let $\Omega$ be a circle with center $O$ and radius $1$. A semicircle $\omega$ with diameter $AB$ is constructed such that $A$ and $B$ lie on circle $O$, $O$ lies on $\omega$, and $O$ does not lie on line $AB$. Find the area of the region contained by the minor arc $AB$ on $\Omega$ and arc $AOB$ on $\omega$.
