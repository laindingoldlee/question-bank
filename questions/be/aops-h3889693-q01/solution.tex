The hypotenuse is $10$, the area is $24$, and the inradius is $r=\dfrac{24}{12}=2$. Points more than $1$ from every side form the inner triangle bounded by three parallel lines a distance $1$ inward from the original sides. The two triangles are homothetic about the incenter, with ratio $\dfrac{(r-1)}{r}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Its area is therefore one fourth of the original area. Boundaries have probability zero, so the desired probability is $1-\dfrac{1}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{4}}$.
