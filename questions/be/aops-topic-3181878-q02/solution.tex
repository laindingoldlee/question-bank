Each row has at most $\lfloor\dfrac{25}{6}\rfloor+1=5$ desks, since $m$ desks require span at least $6(m-1)$. The same holds for columns, giving at most 25. A 5-by 5 grid of spacing 6 spans 24 feet each way and attains the bound. Thus $\boxed{25}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw((0,0)--(25,0)--(25,25)--(0,25)--cycle);for(int i=0;i<5;++i)for(int j=0;j<5;++j)dot((.5+6*i,.5+6*j));label("6-foot grid spacing",(12.5,-2));
\end{asy}
