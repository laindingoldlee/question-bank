After $n$ days he has eaten
\[2+6+18+\cdots+2\cdot3^{n-1}=2\cdot\dfrac{3^n-1}{3-1}=3^n-1\]
pounds. The running totals are $2,8,26,80,242,728,2186$, and $3^n-1>900$ first holds for $n=7$ ($3^6-1=728$, $3^7-1=2186$). Day $1$ is Monday, so day $7$ is $\boxed{\text{Sunday}}$.
