If $n$ has $k$ digits, then $D(n)=n\cdot10^k+n=n(10^k+1)$. So $\dfrac{D(n)}n=10^k+1$, whose digit sum is $2$. The digits of $D(n)$ are two copies of the digits of $n$, so $S(D(n))=2S(n)$. Both sides equal $2$. $\blacksquare$
