Partition 1 through 9 by their odd parts: $\{1,2,4,8\},\{3,6\},\{5\},\{7\},\{9\}$. Within each group every smaller member divides every larger, so at most one per group is allowed, giving at most five. The set $\{5,6,7,8,9\}$ attains five because doubling its least member exceeds nine. Thus the maximum is $\boxed5$.
