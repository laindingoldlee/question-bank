Write $ m=ga,n=gb $ with $\gcd(a,b)=1 $. Then $ g(1+ab)=2017 $. As 2017 is prime and $ 1+ab\ge2 $, we get $ g=1 $ and $ ab=2016=2^5\cdot3^2\cdot7 $. Each of the three complete prime-power blocks must be assigned to exactly one of $ a,b $, giving $\boxed{2^3=8}$ ordered pairs.
