Each scratched square is equally likely to be any of the $15$ squares, so its expected value is
\[\dfrac{5\cdot15+4\cdot0+3\cdot50+2\cdot75+150}{15}=\dfrac{525}{15}=35\text{ dollars}.\]
By linearity of expectation (which holds even though the squares are drawn without replacement), the expected winning is $3\cdot35=\boxed{\$105}$, and the expected net gain is $105-100=\boxed{\$5}$.
