Gary buys a virtual scratch-off ticket for $\$100$. It has $15$ squares, of which $5$ contain $\$15$, $4$ contain $\$0$, $3$ contain $\$50$, $2$ contain $\$75$ and $1$ contains $\$150$. He scratches $3$ different squares, chosen at random, and wins the total of the amounts in them. What is his expected winning? What is his expected net gain?
