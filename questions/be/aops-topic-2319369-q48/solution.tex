Together they wash $5$ cars per hour. If Erick washes $e$ cars per hour, John washes $3e$, so $4e=5$: Erick washes $\dfrac54$ and John $\dfrac{15}4$ cars per hour. Alone, John needs $30\div\dfrac{15}{4}=8$ hours and Erick $30\div\dfrac54=24$ hours: $\boxed{\text{John }8\text{ hours, Erick }24\text{ hours}}$. (Check: $\dfrac{30}{8}+\dfrac{30}{24}=3.75+1.25=5$ cars per hour.)
