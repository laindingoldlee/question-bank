The probability of no acceptance is $(\dfrac{3}{4})^4(\dfrac{2}{3})^4=\dfrac{1}{16}$. For a specified success in the first group, replace its failure factor by success, multiplying by $\dfrac{1}{3}$; in the other group multiply by $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Summing over the eight programs gives $\dfrac{1}{16}(4\cdot\dfrac{1}{3}+4\cdot\dfrac{1}{2})=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{24}}$.
