A year has
\[
365.25\cdot24\cdot60\cdot60=31{,}557{,}600\approx3.15576\times10^{7}\text{ seconds}.
\]
So light travels
\[
186{,}282\cdot31{,}557{,}600=5{,}878{,}612{,}843{,}200\text{ miles}\approx\boxed{5.88\times10^{12}\text{ miles}}.
\]
In scientific notation the multiplication is $(1.86282\times10^5)(3.15576\times10^7)\approx5.8786\times10^{12}$: multiply the leading numbers and add the exponents. (An answer such as $12\times10^5$ is far too small: it would mean light covers only $1.2$ million miles in a year, less than it travels in $7$ seconds.)
