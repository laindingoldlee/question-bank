From $m$ cards one can make $2^m$ sets (each card is in or out). If Harry has $h$ cards, then $2^{h+4}-2^h=15\cdot2^h=960$, so $2^h=64$ and $h=6$. Larry has $10$ cards, which leaves remainder $\boxed{\textbf{(A) }0}$ on division by $5$.
