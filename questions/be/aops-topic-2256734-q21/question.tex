Larry and Harry each have a stack of different cards, and Larry has four more cards than Harry. Larry can make $960$ more sets of cards from his stack than Harry can, where order does not matter (the empty set counts as a set). For example, from cards $A,B,C$ one can make $8$ sets. What is the remainder when the number of Larry's cards is divided by $5$?

$\textbf{(A) }0\qquad\textbf{(B) }1\qquad\textbf{(C) }2\qquad\textbf{(D) }3\qquad\textbf{(E) }4$
