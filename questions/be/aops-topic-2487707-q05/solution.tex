The arc is $\dfrac{68}{360}$ of the circumference $2\pi\cdot2\pi=4\pi^2$, so its length is
\[\dfrac{68}{360}\cdot4\pi^2=\dfrac{17}{90}\cdot4\pi^2=\boxed{\dfrac{34\pi^2}{45}}\approx7.46.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real r=2pi;
draw(circle((0,0),r),gray);
draw(arc((0,0),r,0,68),red+linewidth(1.4));
draw((0,0)--r*dir(0)); draw((0,0)--r*dir(68));
draw(arc((0,0),1.4,0,68)); label("$68^\circ$",2.3*dir(34),fontsize(8pt));
label("$2\pi$",0.5*r*dir(0),S,fontsize(8pt)); dot((0,0));
\end{asy}
