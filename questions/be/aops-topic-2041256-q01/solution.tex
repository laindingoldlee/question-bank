The total number of votes is
\[
21+14+13+17+9+15+7+32=128 .
\]
\textbf{(a)} The favourable voters number \(32+15+9=56\), so the probability is \(\dfrac{56}{128}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{16}}\).

\textbf{(b)} The mean number of votes per option is \(\dfrac{128}{8}=\boxed{16}\).
