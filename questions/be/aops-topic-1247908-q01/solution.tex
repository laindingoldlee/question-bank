Write $9^{2016}=(10-1)^{2016}$ and expand modulo $1000$: only the terms with $10^0$, $10^1$, $10^2$ matter.
\[(10-1)^{2016}\equiv1-2016\cdot10+\dbinom{2016}{2}\cdot100\pmod{1000}.\]
Now $2016\cdot10=20160\equiv160$ and $\dbinom{2016}2=2031120$, so $\dbinom{2016}{2}\cdot100=203112000\equiv0\pmod{1000}$. Hence
\[9^{2016}\equiv1-160+0=-159\equiv\boxed{841}\pmod{1000}.\]
