How many positive integers less than \(84\) are relatively prime to \(30\)?

A student found three different answers: \(23\) by listing, \(25\) by inclusion--exclusion using \(\left\lfloor\dfrac{84}{2}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{84}{3}\right\rfloor+\cdots\) with \(\left\lfloor\dfrac{84}{3}\right\rfloor\) taken as \(26\), and \(22.4\) from \(84\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot\dfrac{4}{5}\). Find the correct answer and explain the errors.
