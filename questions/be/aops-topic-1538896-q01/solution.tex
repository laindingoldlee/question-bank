Since $3$ is prime, the product is a multiple of $3$ exactly when at least one roll is a multiple of $3$ (a $3$ or a $6$). So they survive exactly when all four rolls are among $1,2,4,5$, which has probability $\dfrac{4}{6}=\dfrac{2}{3}$ for each roll, independently. The probability is
\[
\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^4=\boxed{\dfrac{16}{81}}.
\]
