Three days ago there were $p$ cupcakes on the counter. Two days ago exactly $20\%$ of them were eaten. Some more were eaten yesterday, and some today. Today, after the eating, there are $30\%$ fewer cupcakes than there were at the end of yesterday, and half as many as there were three days ago. If a whole number of cupcakes was eaten each day, what is the least possible value of $p$?
