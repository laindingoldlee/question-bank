After two days ago there are $\dfrac{4}{5}p$ cupcakes, so $5\mid p$. Let $y$ be the number left at the end of yesterday. Today there are $\dfrac{7}{10}y=\dfrac {p}{2}$, so $y=\dfrac{5p}{7}$, and today's count $\dfrac {p}{2}$ must be a whole number.

So $p$ is divisible by $5$, by $7$ (for $y$ to be whole) and by $2$, hence by $70$. Also the counts must decrease: $\dfrac{4}{5}p\ge\dfrac{5}{7}p\ge\dfrac{1}{2}p$, which holds. The least value is $p=\boxed{70}$: the counts are $70$, $56$, $50$, $35$, with $14$, $6$ and $15$ cupcakes eaten on the three days.
