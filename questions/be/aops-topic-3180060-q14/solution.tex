Write $x=k+\dfrac{1}{7}$ for an integer $k$. Then the fractional part of $x^2$ is the residue of $14k+1$ modulo 49, divided by 49. As $k$ runs modulo 7 the residues are 1, 15, 29, 43, 8, 22, 36. Their greatest is 43, so the answer is $\boxed{43049}$.
