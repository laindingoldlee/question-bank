Within any 11 consecutive integers, connect numbers differing by 4 or 7. These edges form an 11-cycle, in the residue order $0,4,8,1,5,9,2,6,10,3,7,0$. At most five vertices of an odd 11-cycle can be selected without adjacent vertices. Partition $1,\ldots,1980$ into 180 such blocks, giving at most 900 selected numbers there. The last nine numbers contain a matching of four forbidden pairs, so at most five can be selected: relative to $1980$, use pairs $(1,5),(2,6),(3,7),(4,8)$ and singleton 9. Thus $|S|\le905$. Select all integers congruent to $1,3,4,6,9\pmod{11}$. No two of these residues differ by 4 or 7 modulo 11, and all five occur in the final nine positions. This attains $\boxed{905}$.
