Let $x_k$, $t_k$ and $n_k$ be the numbers of allowed words of length $k$ ending in one of $J,A,Y$, in $T$, and in $N$, respectively. A letter $J,A,Y$ may follow anything; $T$ may follow anything except $N$; $N$ may follow anything except $T$. So
\[x_{k+1}=3(x_k+t_k+n_k),\qquad t_{k+1}=x_k+t_k,\qquad n_{k+1}=x_k+n_k,\]
starting from $x_1=3$, $t_1=n_1=1$. By symmetry $t_k=n_k$:
\[\begin{array}{c|cccccccc}k&1&2&3&4&5&6&7&8\\ \hline x_k&3&15&69&321&1491&6927&32181&149505\\ t_k=n_k&1&4&19&88&409&1900&8827&41008\end{array}\]
The number of words of length $8$ is $149505+2\cdot41008=\boxed{231521}$.
