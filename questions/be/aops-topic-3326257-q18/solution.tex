For every positive integer $m$, the binomial theorem gives $(1+10)^m\equiv1+10m\pmod{100}$, since all higher terms contain 100. Here the exponent $m=11^{11^{11}}$ is 1 modulo 10, so the entire power is $1+10=\boxed{11}\pmod{100}$.
