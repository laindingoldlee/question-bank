In an election between $A$ and $B$, during the counting of the votes neither candidate was ever more than $2$ votes ahead, and the vote ended in a tie, $6$ votes to $6$ votes. Two votes for the same candidate are indistinguishable. In how many orders could the votes have been counted? One possibility is $AABBABBABABA$.
