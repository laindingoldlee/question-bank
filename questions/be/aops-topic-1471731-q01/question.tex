Let $\varphi=\dfrac{1+\sqrt5}{2}$, the golden ratio.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Define $x_1=1$ and $x_{n+1}=\sqrt{1+x_n}$ for $n\ge1$. Prove that the sequence is increasing and that $x_n<\varphi$ for all $n$, and deduce that $\sqrt{1+\sqrt{1+\sqrt{1+\cdots}}}=\lim x_n=\varphi$.
\item[(b)] A student writes $\varphi=1+\sqrt{1+\sqrt{1+\cdots}}$. What is the true value of the expression on the right?
\end{enumerate}
