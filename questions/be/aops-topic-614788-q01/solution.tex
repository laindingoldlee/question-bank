By the law of cosines with $AB=AC=x$:
\[\left(\sqrt3-1\right)^2=2x^2-2x^2\cos30^\circ=x^2\left(2-\sqrt3\right).\]
Since $\left(\sqrt3-1\right)^2=4-2\sqrt3=2\left(2-\sqrt3\right)$, we get $x^2=2$ and $AB=\boxed{\sqrt2}$.
