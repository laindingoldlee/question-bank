In square $ABCD$, points $X$ and $Y$ trisect side $AD$, with $X$ closer to $A$. Segments $BX$ and $BY$ are drawn. A small circle is inscribed in triangle $BXY$. A larger circle of radius $6$ lies inside quadrilateral $BCDY$ and is tangent to $BC$, $CD$ and $BY$. What is the radius of the smaller circle? Express your answer as a decimal to the nearest hundredth.
