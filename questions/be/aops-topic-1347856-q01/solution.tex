Let the side of the square be $s$ and use coordinates $B=(0,0)$, $C=(s,0)$, $D=(s,s)$, $A=(0,s)$, so $X=\left(\dfrac {s}{3},s\right)$ and $Y=\left(\dfrac{2s}{3},s\right)$.

\textbf{The side length.} The large circle is tangent to $BC$ and $CD$, so its centre is $(s-6,6)$. Line $BY$ is $3x-2y=0$, and the centre lies below it, so the tangency condition is
\[
\dfrac{3(s-6)-2\cdot6}{\sqrt{13}}=6\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 3s-30=6\sqrt{13}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad s=10+2\sqrt{13}\approx17.211 .
\]
The circle reaches only up to height $12<s$, so it does lie inside $BCDY$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=10+2*sqrt(13);
pair B=(0,0), C=(s,0), D=(s,s), A=(0,s), X=(s/3,s), Y=(2s/3,s);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(B--X,linewidth(0.9)); draw(B--Y,linewidth(0.9));
pair O=(s-6,6); draw(circle(O,6),blue);
real a=abs(X-Y), bb=abs(B-Y), c=abs(B-X);
pair I=(a*B+bb*X+c*Y)/(a+bb+c); real r=s/(1+sqrt(10)+sqrt(13));
draw(circle(I,r),red);
draw(O--(s-6,0),blue+dashed); label("$6$",(s-6,3),W,blue);
dot(O,blue); dot(I,red); draw(I--I+r*dir(200),red+dashed); label("$r$",I+r/2*dir(200),N,red);
label("$A$",A,NW); label("$B$",B,SW); label("$C$",C,SE); label("$D$",D,NE); label("$X$",X,N); label("$Y$",Y,N);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

\textbf{The small circle.} Triangle $BXY$ has base $XY=\dfrac {s}{3}$ on line $AD$ and height $s$, so its area is $\dfrac{s^2}{6}$. Its sides are
\[
XY=\dfrac {s}{3},\qquad BX=\sqrt{\dfrac{s^2}{9}+s^2}=\dfrac{s\sqrt{10}}{3},\qquad BY=\sqrt{\dfrac{4s^2}{9}+s^2}=\dfrac{s\sqrt{13}}{3}.
\]
The inradius is area divided by the semiperimeter:
\[
r=\dfrac{\dfrac{s^2}{6}}{\dfrac {s}{6}\left(1+\sqrt{10}+\sqrt{13}\right)}=\dfrac{s}{1+\sqrt{10}+\sqrt{13}}=\dfrac{10+2\sqrt{13}}{1+\sqrt{10}+\sqrt{13}}\approx\dfrac{17.211}{7.768}\approx\boxed{2.22}.
\]
