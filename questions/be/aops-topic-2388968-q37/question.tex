Masadca has $10$ different types of soap, with unlimited copies of each. A \emph{product} is either a single soap or a combination of soaps of different types; for example, combining soaps $A$ and $B$ gives soap $AB$ (the order does not matter). Each product can be sold only once. A product made from $k$ soaps sells for $5k$ dollars; for example, $A$ sells for $\$5$, $AB$ for $\$10$ and $ABC$ for $\$15$. How much money does Masadca make by selling every possible product once?
