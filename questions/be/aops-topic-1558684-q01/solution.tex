Let $BC=x$ and $AB=2x$, and let the square have side $s$. Place $B=(0,0)$, $A=(2x,0)$, $C=(0,x)$; then $E=(s,s)$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real h=3; pair B=(0,0),A=(2h,0),C=(0,h); real s=2h/3;
pair D=(s,0),F=(0,s),Ep=(s,s);
fill(B--D--Ep--F--cycle,paleblue);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(1));
draw(B--D--Ep--F--cycle,blue+linewidth(0.9));
real m=0.18; draw((m,0)--(m,m)--(0,m));
label("$A$",A,SE);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,NW);label("$D$",D,S);label("$F$",F,W);label("$E$",Ep,NE);
label("$s$",(s/2,s),N,blue);label("$x$",(-0.9,h/2+0.4),W);label("$2x$",(h,-0.8),S);
draw((-0.9,0)--(-0.9,h),Arrows(TeXHead));draw((0,-0.8)--(2h,-0.8),Arrows(TeXHead));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The hypotenuse $AC$ is the line $\dfrac{X}{2x}+\dfrac{Y}{x}=1$. Since $E$ lies on it,
\[
\dfrac{s}{2x}+\dfrac{s}{x}=1\iff\dfrac{3s}{2x}=1\iff s=\dfrac{2x}{3}.
\]
(Equivalently, triangle $EDA$ is similar to triangle $CBA$, so $DA=2DE=2s$ and $AB=s+2s=3s$.)

The square has area $s^2=\dfrac{4x^2}{9}$ and the triangle has area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot2x\cdot x=x^2$, so the ratio is
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{4}{9}}.
\]
