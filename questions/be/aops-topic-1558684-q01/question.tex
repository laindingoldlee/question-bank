Triangle $ABC$ has a right angle at $B$, and its base $AB$ is twice its height $BC$, that is, $AB=2BC$. A square $DEFB$ is inscribed in the triangle: one vertex is $B$, the vertex $D$ lies on $AB$ between $A$ and $B$, the vertex $F$ lies on $BC$ between $B$ and $C$, and the vertex $E$ lies on the hypotenuse $AC$. Find the ratio of the area of the square $DEFB$ to the area of triangle $ABC$.
