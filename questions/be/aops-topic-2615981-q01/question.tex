Prove that for every integer $n\ge2$ there exist $n$ consecutive positive integers $a_1,a_2,\dots,a_n$ such that
\[\dfrac{1}{n+1}<\dfrac{1}{a_1}+\dfrac{1}{a_2}+\cdots+\dfrac{1}{a_n}<\dfrac1n.\]
