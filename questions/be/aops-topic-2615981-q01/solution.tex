The sum of $n$ reciprocals near $a$ is about $\dfrac na$, which suggests $a\approx n^2$. Take $a_i=n^2+i-1$ for $i=1,\dots,n$.

Each term is at most $\dfrac{1}{n^2}$, with strict inequality for $i\ge2$ (which exists since $n\ge2$). So the sum is less than $n\cdot\dfrac{1}{n^2}=\dfrac1n$.

Each term is at least $\dfrac{1}{n^2+n-1}>\dfrac{1}{n^2+n}$. So the sum is greater than $\dfrac{n}{n^2+n}=\dfrac{1}{n+1}$. $\blacksquare$
