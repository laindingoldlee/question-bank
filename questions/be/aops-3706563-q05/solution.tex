Write $\angle ACB=\gamma$ and $\angle ABC=\gamma+45^\circ$. Then $\angle BAD=135^\circ-2\gamma$. Since $AB=AD$, $\angle ABD=\dfrac{180^\circ-(135^\circ-2\gamma)}{2}=22.5^\circ+\gamma$. As $D$ is on $AC$, $BD$ lies inside $\angle ABC$, and $\angle CBD=(\gamma+45^\circ)-(\gamma+22.5^\circ)=\boxed{22.5^\circ}$.
