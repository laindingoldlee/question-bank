Pair the points from outside in. For any $P$ on the line, $PA_1+PA_5\ge A_1A_5$, with equality exactly when $P$ lies on segment $A_1A_5$; similarly $PA_2+PA_4\ge A_2A_4$, with equality exactly when $P$ is on segment $A_2A_4$; and $PA_3\ge0$, with equality exactly when $P=A_3$. So the sum is at least $A_1A_5+A_2A_4$, and equality holds only when $P=A_3$ (which lies on both segments). The answer is $\boxed{\text{(C) } A_3}$, the median point.
