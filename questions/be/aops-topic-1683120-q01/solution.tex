The shaded area is $\dfrac{2}{5}$ of the rectangle and $\dfrac{5}{8}$ of the triangle. Take the shaded area to be $10k$ (a common multiple of $2$ and $5$). Then the rectangle has area $25k$ and the triangle $16k$. The unshaded parts are $25k-10k=15k$ (rectangle) and $16k-10k=6k$ (triangle), $21k$ in total. So
\[
21k-10k=11k=132\ \Longrightarrow\ k=12,
\]
and the shaded area is $10k=\boxed{120\text{ cm}^2}$.
