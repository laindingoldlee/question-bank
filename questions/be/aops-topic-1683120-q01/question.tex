A triangle overlaps a rectangle; the overlapping region is shaded. The ratio of the shaded area to the area of the rectangle is $2:5$, and the ratio of the shaded area to the area of the triangle is $5:8$. The total area of the unshaded parts of the two figures exceeds the shaded area by $132$ cm$^2$. What is the shaded area?
