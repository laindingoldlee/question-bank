For angle $x$, its supplement is $180-x$ and that angle’s complement is $90-(180-x)=x-90$. Thus $3(x-90)=x-60$, yielding $x=\boxed{105^\circ}$. Its supplement 75 degrees has complement 15 degrees, confirming the relation.

usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw((0,0)--(2,0));draw((0,0)--2*dir(105));draw(arc((0,0),.7,0,105));label("$105^\circ$",(.9,.8));
\end{asy}
