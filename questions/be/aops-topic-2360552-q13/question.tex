Let $P(x)=x^2-3x-9$. A real number $x$ is chosen at random from the interval $5\le x\le15$. The probability that $\left\lfloor\sqrt{P(x)}\right\rfloor=\sqrt{P(\lfloor x\rfloor)}$ equals $\dfrac{\sqrt a+\sqrt b+\sqrt c-d}{e}$, where $a,b,c,d,e$ are positive integers. Find $a+b+c+d+e$.
