Taking real cube roots (the real cube root is unique), the condition is equivalent to
\[
f\left(z^3\right)=\sqrt[3]{\sqrt[3]z}=\sqrt[9]{z}.
\]
Writing $t=z^3$, $z=\sqrt[3]t$, we get
\[
f(t)=\sqrt[9]{\sqrt[3]t}=\boxed{\sqrt[27]{t}} .
\]
Check: $\left(f\left(z^3\right)\right)^3=\left(\sqrt[27]{z^3}\right)^3=\left(\sqrt[9]{z}\right)^3=\sqrt[3]z$.
