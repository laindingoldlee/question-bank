Let $S_n=\displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}i\cdot2^{\,n-1-i}$. We prove $S_n=2^n-n-1$ by induction on $n$.

For $n=1$: $S_1=0=2-1-1$.

Suppose $S_n=2^n-n-1$. Splitting off the last term,
\[S_{n+1}=\sum_{i=0}^{n}i\cdot2^{\,n-i}=2\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}i\cdot2^{\,n-1-i}+n=2S_n+n=2^{n+1}-2n-2+n=2^{n+1}-(n+1)-1 .\]
This completes the induction, so $S_n+n+1=2^n$ for all $n\ge1$. $\blacksquare$

(Check: $n=3$ gives $0\cdot4+1\cdot2+2\cdot1+3+1=8=2^3$.)
