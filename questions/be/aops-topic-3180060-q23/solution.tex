If there are $k$ positive steps, the distance is $|2k-7|$. Pairing symmetric outcomes, the expectation is $\dfrac{2}{128}(7\binom70+5\binom71+3\binom72+\binom73)=\dfrac{280}{128}=\dfrac{35}{16}$. The answer is $\boxed{35016}$.
