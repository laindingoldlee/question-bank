The denominator $D=(x+q)^2+p^2-q^2$ is positive. Cross-multiplying the lower bound gives difference $2q(x-p)^2\ge0$; the upper bound gives difference $2q(x+p)^2\ge0$. Explicitly these are $(p+q)(x^2-2qx+p^2)-(p-q)D$ and $(p+q)D-(p-q)(x^2-2qx+p^2)$. Lower equality holds at $x=p$ and upper equality at $x=-p$.
