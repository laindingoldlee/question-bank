With $2$ socks she might have one white and one black, so $2$ is not enough. With $3$ socks, by the pigeonhole principle two of them have the same colour, since there are only $2$ colours. So she must take $\boxed{3}$ socks. (Probability is not needed: ``assured'' asks for the worst case.)
