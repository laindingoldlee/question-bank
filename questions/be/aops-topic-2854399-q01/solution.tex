$x+6+\dfrac1x=5$, so $x+\dfrac1x=-1$ and $x^2+\dfrac1{x^2}=\left(x+\dfrac1x\right)^2-2=\boxed{-1}$. (Here $x$ is not real, which is why the value is negative.)
