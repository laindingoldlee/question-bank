$P(\text{2nd white})=\dfrac37$ by symmetry (each position is equally likely to hold any marble). $P(\text{both white})=\dfrac37\cdot\dfrac26=\dfrac17$. Hence
\[P(\text{1st white}\mid\text{2nd white})=\dfrac{\dfrac17}{\dfrac37}=\boxed{\dfrac13}.\]

Intuitively: given that one particular white marble is second, the first is one of the remaining $6$ marbles, $2$ of which are white.
