Let $x$, $y$, $z$ be the numbers of darts scoring $3$, $5$, $7$. Then $x+y+z=15$ and $3x+5y+7z=75$. Subtracting three times the first equation from the second gives $2y+4z=30$, so $y=15-2z$, and then $x=15-y-z=z$.

So the solutions are $(x,y,z)=(z,15-2z,z)$ with $0\le z\le7$, which gives $\boxed{8}$ ways.
