Rearranging gives $x^2-(26+27i)x-26+26i=0$. This factors as $(x-i)(x-26-26i)=0$, because the roots have sum $26+27i$ and product $26i+26i^2=-26+26i$. Their moduli are $1$ and $26\sqrt2$, respectively, so the ordered answers are $\boxed{i,\ 26+26i}$.
