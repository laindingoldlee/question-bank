Classify by subset size. Singletons contribute five. Two-element subsets need even sum, giving $\binom32+\binom22=4$. Three-element subsets with sum divisible by three are $123,135,234,345$, four. A four-element subset has sum $15-a$, divisible by four only when the omitted element is three, giving one. The full set also works. Total $5+4+4+1+1=\boxed{15}$.
