Subtracting consecutive four-cell sums shows that entries four places apart in a row or column are equal. Thus the seven row totals have the pattern $r_1,r_2,r_3,r_4,r_1,r_2,r_3$. The first four rows total $7\cdot19=133$, by summing their four-cell vertical blocks. Therefore the full total is $2\cdot133-r_4=235$, giving the central row total $\boxed{r_4=31}$.

\end{asy}
