Besides the trivial example $f(x)=x$, there are functions with $f(f(f(x)))=x$. Show that $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{1-x}$ is such a function: for every real $x\ne0,1$, the expression $f(f(f(x)))$ is defined and equals $x$.
