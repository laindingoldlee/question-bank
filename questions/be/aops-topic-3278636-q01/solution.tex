Neither the axis direction nor the rotation of the square about it is fixed, so there are infinitely many placements. Choose base centre $(2,2,-2)$ and vertices $\boxed{(1,1,-2),(3,1,-2),(3,3,-2),(1,3,-2)}$. Each is $\sqrt2$ from the base centre. Perpendicularity of the altitude gives lateral-edge length $\boxed{\sqrt{3^2+2}=\sqrt{11}}$.
