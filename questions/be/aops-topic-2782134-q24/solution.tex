The center has coordinates $ O=(t,t)$. Equating squared distances to $ B,C $ gives $(t-12)^2+t^2=t^2+(t-16)^2 $, hence $ t=14 $. The squared radius is $(14-12)^2+14^2=200 $. One quarter of the area is $\dfrac{200\pi}{4}=\boxed{50\pi}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(12,0),C=(0,16),O=(14,14);draw(circle(O,sqrt(200)));draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--O,dashed);dot(O);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,S);label("$C$",C,W);label("$O$",O,NE);
\end{asy}
