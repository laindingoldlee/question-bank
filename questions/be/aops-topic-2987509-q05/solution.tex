Two primes differing by an odd number have different parities, so one of them is $2$ and the other is $23$. The number is $2^a\cdot23^b$ with $a,b\ge1$ and three digits. Since $2\cdot23^2=1058$ is too large, $b=1$, and $23\cdot2^a$ is at most $999$ for $a\le5$: $23\cdot32=736$. The largest is $\boxed{736}$.
