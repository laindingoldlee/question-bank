The qualifying residues are $n\equiv1,4\pmod5$. In $1$ through $1795$ there are $359$ blocks of five, each with two qualifying values. Of $1796,1797$, only $1796$ qualifies. Thus the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{719}{1797}}$.
