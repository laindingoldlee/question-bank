Find all four-digit numbers $\overline{abcd}$ (with digits $a,b,c,d$ and $a,b\ne0$) for which there are positive integers $m$ and $n$ such that
\[\overline{abcd}=2^m\cdot3^n\cdot11\qquad\text{and}\qquad\overline{bacd}=2^{m-1}\cdot3^{n-1}\cdot41.\]
