We show $P\ge0$, with equality at $a=b=c=1$ (where $3+1=4$ and $P=3-3=0$). So the minimum is $\boxed{0}$.

\textbf{Case 1: $a,b,c$ are all less than $1$.} Then $ab<a$, $bc<b$, $ca<c$, so $P>0$.

\textbf{Case 2: not all are less than $1$.} They cannot all exceed $1$ (then $a+b+c+abc>4$), so some number is at least $1$ and some other is at most $1$. Relabel so that $(a-1)(b-1)\le0$. Write $s=a+b$ and $t=ab$; then $t\le s-1$. The condition gives $c=\dfrac{4-s}{1+t}$, so $c-1=\dfrac{3-s-t}{1+t}$, and
\[
-P=ab+bc+ca-(a+b+c)=t-c+s(c-1)=\dfrac{t(1+t)-(4-s)+s(3-s-t)}{1+t}=\dfrac{t(t+1-s)-(s-2)^2}{1+t}.
\]
Since $t>0$ and $t+1-s\le0$, the numerator is at most $0$, so $P\ge0$.

In both cases $P\ge0$, and the value $0$ is attained at $a=b=c=1$.
