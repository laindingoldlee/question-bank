Let $a$ be the number of Poke Balls and $b$ the number of Potions. Then $200a+300b=12000$, that is,
\[2a+3b=120,\qquad a,b\ge0 .\]
Since $2a$ and $120$ are even, $b$ is even: $b=2m$, and then $a=60-3m$. We need $a\ge0$, so $m=0,1,\ldots,20$. That gives $\boxed{21}$ combinations, from $(a,b)=(60,0)$ to $(0,40)$.
