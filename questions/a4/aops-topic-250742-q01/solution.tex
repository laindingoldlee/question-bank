Row $37$ has $2\cdot37-1=73$ squares. Since the colours alternate and the row begins and ends with white, the squares in odd positions $1,3,\dots,73$ are white ($37$ of them) and those in even positions $2,4,\dots,72$ are black. So there are $\boxed{\textbf{(C) }36}$ black squares. (In general, row $n$ has $n-1$ black squares.)
