The angle-bisector theorem gives $AB:BC=3:8$. Since these lengths are integers and 3,8 are coprime, write $AB=3k,BC=8k$ with positive integer $k$. The triangle inequalities with $AC=11$ give $11k>11$ and $5k<11$, forcing $k=2$. Thus the sides are $6,16,11$ and the perimeter is $\boxed{33}$.
