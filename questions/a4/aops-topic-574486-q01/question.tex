In the $3\times3$ magic square
\[
\begin{array}{|c|c|c|}\hline a&b&c\\\hline d&e&f\\\hline g&h&i\\\hline\end{array}
\]
all rows, columns and both diagonals have the same sum. Show that $b+d=2i$, $b+f=2g$, $f+h=2a$ and $d+h=2c$.
