Let $S$ be the common sum. Adding the middle row, the middle column and both diagonals counts $e$ four times and every other entry once, so $4S=(\text{total})+3e=3S+3e$, giving $S=3e$.

Write $a=e+p$ and $c=e+q$. Then the diagonals give $i=e-p$ and $g=e-q$; the top row gives $b=3e-a-c=e-p-q$; the left column gives $d=3e-a-g=e-p+q$; the right column gives $f=3e-c-i=e+p-q$; and the bottom row gives $h=3e-g-i=e+p+q$. Now
\[
b+d=2e-2p=2i,\quad b+f=2e-2q=2g,\quad f+h=2e+2p=2a,\quad d+h=2e+2q=2c. \qquad\blacksquare
\]
