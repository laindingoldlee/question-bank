If the common difference is $d>0$, then $(n-1)d=20$ and $d(2a_n-d)=63$. Therefore $d$ divides both 20 and 63, so $d=1$. It follows that $2a_n-1=63$, giving $a_n=32,a_1=12,n=21$. The sum is $\dfrac{21(12+32)}{2}=\boxed{462}$.
