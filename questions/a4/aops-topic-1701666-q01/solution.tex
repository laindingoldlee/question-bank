Work modulo $7$. The squares of $0,1,2,3,4,5,6$ are congruent to
\[
0,\ 1,\ 4,\ 2,\ 2,\ 4,\ 1\pmod 7,
\]
so every square is $\equiv0,1,2$ or $4\pmod7$, and it is $\equiv0$ only when the number itself is divisible by $7$.

If $a^2+b^2\equiv0\pmod7$ and $a^2\equiv r$ with $r\in\{1,2,4\}$, then $b^2\equiv-r\equiv6,5$ or $3\pmod7$, none of which is a square. So $a^2\equiv0$, hence $b^2\equiv0$, and therefore $7\mid a$ and $7\mid b$. $\blacksquare$
