By the triangle inequality for absolute values,
\[
|x+2|+|1-x|\ge|(x+2)+(1-x)|=3,\qquad |y-5|+|1+y|=|5-y|+|y+1|\ge|(5-y)+(y+1)|=6 .
\]
The equation says $\left(|x+2|+|1-x|\right)+\left(|y-5|+|1+y|\right)=9=3+6$, so both inequalities are equalities. Equality holds exactly when the two terms have the same sign, that is, $x+2\ge0$ and $1-x\ge0$, and $5-y\ge0$ and $y+1\ge0$:
\[
-2\le x\le1,\qquad -1\le y\le5 ,
\]
and every such pair is a solution.

For fixed $y$, $xy$ is linear in $x$, so its extremes on this rectangle occur at corners: $(-2)(-1)=2$, $(-2)\cdot5=-10$, $1\cdot(-1)=-1$, $1\cdot5=5$. So the maximum is $5$, the minimum is $-10$, and their sum is $\boxed{-5}$.
