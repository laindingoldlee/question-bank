Let $u=2b$ and $v=\dfrac1b$. Then $uv=2$ and $u^2+v^2=2$, so
\[8b^3+\dfrac1{b^3}=u^3+v^3=(u+v)\left(u^2-uv+v^2\right)=(u+v)(2-2)=\boxed{0}.\]
(No real $b$ works, since $4b^2+\dfrac1{b^2}\ge2\sqrt4=4$ for real $b$; so $b$ is necessarily non-real, e.g. $b=\dfrac{\sqrt6+i\sqrt2}{4}$.)
