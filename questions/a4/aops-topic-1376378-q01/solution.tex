Let $M$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $Z$ to $WX$, and let $d=ZM$. The perpendicular from the centre to a chord bisects the chord (triangles $ZMW$ and $ZMX$ are congruent right triangles, since $ZW=ZX$), so $MX=4$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s=0.2){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
pair Z=(0,0); real r=5;
pair Wp=(-4,-3), X=(4,-3), M=(0,-3);
draw(circle(Z,r),linewidth(0.9)); draw(Wp--X,linewidth(0.9));
draw(Z--M,red+dashed); draw(Z--X,blue);
draw(ra(M,X-M,Z-M,0.4));
dot(Z); dot(M);
label("$Z$",Z,N); label("$W$",Wp,SW); label("$X$",X,SE); label("$M$",M,S);
label("$4$",(M+X)/2,S); label("$5$",(Z+X)/2,NE,blue); label("$d$",(Z+M)/2,W,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

In the right triangle $ZMX$ the hypotenuse is the radius $ZX=5$, so
\[
d^2+4^2=5^2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad d=\sqrt{25-16}=\boxed{3}.
\]
