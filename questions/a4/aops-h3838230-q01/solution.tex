For $x>0$, subtract $\dfrac{2}{3}$ to obtain $f(x)-\dfrac{2}{3}=\dfrac{119x-11}{9x^3+123x+201}$. At $x=67^{67}>100$, this difference is positive and less than $\dfrac{119}{9x^2}<\dfrac{119}{90000}<\dfrac{1}{600}$. Consequently $0.665<\dfrac{2}{3}<f(x)<\dfrac{2}{3}+\dfrac{1}{600}<0.675$. The value therefore rounds to $\boxed{0.67}$.
