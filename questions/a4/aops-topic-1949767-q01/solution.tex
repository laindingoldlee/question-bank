Since $28^2=784<793<841=29^2$, it is enough to test primes up to $23$. It is not divisible by $2,3$ (digit sum $19$), $5$, $7$ ($7\cdot113=791$) or $11$ ($7-9+3=1$), but $13\cdot61=793$. So $793$ is not prime: $\boxed{793=13\cdot61}$, and $61$ is prime.
