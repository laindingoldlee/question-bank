Without the upper bound 6, positive quadruples summing to 10 number $\binom93=84$. If a specified die is at least 7, subtract 6 to get a positive quadruple of total 4, giving exactly one outcome. Two dice cannot both be at least 7. Thus $84-4=\boxed{80}$.
