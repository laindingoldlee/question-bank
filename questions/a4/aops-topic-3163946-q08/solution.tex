Fix a student $A$. Let $U$ consist of $A$ and all friends of $A$, and $V$ all nonfriends of $A$. Any two other members of $U$ must be friends, since their triple with $A$ already has two friendship edges. Any two members of $V$ must be friends, since their triple with $A$ has no other friendship edge. A member of $U\setminus\{A\}$ and a member of $V$ cannot be friends, since that would give two edges in their triple with $A$. Thus the graph is exactly two disjoint cliques. If their sizes are $k,15-k$, the friendship count is $\binom k2+\binom{15-k}{2}$, minimized by sizes $7,8$. This gives $21+28=\boxed{49}$, and that construction satisfies every triple condition.
