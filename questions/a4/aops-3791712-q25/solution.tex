Use $M=(0,0),A=(0,4),H=(0,2),B=(-b,0),C=(b,0)$. Perpendicularity of $CH$ and $AB$ gives $(b,-2)\cdot(b,4)=0$, hence $b^2=8$. Projection of $C$ onto $AB$ gives $D = (- \dfrac{b}{3} , \dfrac{8}{3})$. Therefore $M D^2 = \dfrac{b^2}{9} + \dfrac{64}{9} = 8$, so $\boxed{MD=2\sqrt2}$.
