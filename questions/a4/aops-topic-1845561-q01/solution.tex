Since $2018=2\cdot1009$ and $1009$ is prime, $2018^{2017}=2^{2017}\cdot1009^{2017}$. Every positive factor has the form $2^a\cdot1009^b$ with $0\le a\le2017$ and $0\le b\le2017$, and it is even exactly when $a\ge1$. So there are $2017$ choices for $a$ and $2018$ choices for $b$, giving
\[
2017\cdot2018=\boxed{4070306}
\]
even factors.
