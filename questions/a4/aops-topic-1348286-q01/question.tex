A turtle starts at the origin $(0,0)$ of the coordinate plane. On each of $6$ turns a spinner shows Up, Down, Right or Left, each with probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$, and the turtle moves $1$ unit in that direction. Let $P$ be the probability that the turtle is back at the origin after the $6$ turns. Compute $512P+264$.
