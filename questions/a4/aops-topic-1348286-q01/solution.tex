There are $4^6=4096$ equally likely sequences of moves. The turtle returns to the origin when the numbers of Ups and Downs are equal, say $i$ each, and the numbers of Rights and Lefts are equal, $3-i$ each. Counting arrangements,
\[
\sum_{i=0}^{3}\dfrac{6!}{i!\,i!\,(3-i)!\,(3-i)!}=20+180+180+20=400 .
\]
(Or: this equals $\dbinom63^2$, since rotating the plane by $45^\circ$ turns each move into a pair of independent $\pm1$ steps in two new directions, and each coordinate returns to $0$ in $\dbinom63$ ways.) So $P=\dfrac{400}{4096}=\dfrac{25}{256}$ and
\[
512P+264=50+264=\boxed{314}.
\]
