Since $ 3n $ is divisible by 3, its digit sum is also divisible by 3. The positive digit sum must therefore be a multiple of 33, at least 33. The smallest positive integer with digit sum at least 33 is 6999: three digits cannot suffice, and among four-digit numbers the leading digit must be at least 6, with all remaining digits 9 when it is 6. The number 6999 has digit sum 33 and is divisible by 3, yielding $\boxed{n=2333}$.
