Let there be $b$ boys and $65-b$ girls. Then $3\mid b$ and $4\mid65-b$, i.e. $b\equiv1\pmod4$. The numbers that are $0$ mod $3$ and $1$ mod $4$ are those $\equiv9\pmod{12}$: $9,21,33,45,57$. With $b\ge19$ the smallest is $b=21$, which leaves $65-21=\boxed{44}$ girls ($11$ groups of $4$, and $7$ groups of $3$ boys).
