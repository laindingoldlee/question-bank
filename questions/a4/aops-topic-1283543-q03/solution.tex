Use Burnside's lemma over the $6$ rotations. The identity fixes all $\dbinom63=20$ colourings. Rotations by $1$ or $5$ places fix only one-coloured circles: $0$. Rotations by $2$ or $4$ places split the positions into two cycles of $3$; one cycle red and one white: $2$ each. The rotation by $3$ places has three cycles of $2$, which cannot hold exactly $3$ reds: $0$. The count is
\[
\dfrac{20+0+2+0+2+0}{6}=\boxed{4}:
\]
RRRWWW, RRWRWW, RRWWRW and RWRWRW.
