Triangle $ABD$ has half the square area, namely $50$. Because $P$ is beyond the diagonal intersection toward $C$, quadrilateral $ABPD$ is the union of triangles $ABD$ and $PBD$ with disjoint interiors. Thus $[PBD]=70-50=\boxed{20}$.

\end{asy}
