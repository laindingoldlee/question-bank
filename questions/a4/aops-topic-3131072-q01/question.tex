A player starts with 10 health and is human. Each minute, independently, an event causes 5 damage with probability $0.04$, immediate death with probability $0.017$, permanent transformation into a pig with probability $0.06$, and no change otherwise. Transformation does not affect health or future event probabilities. Find (a) the probability of surviving 20160 minutes; (b) the probability of surviving as a pig.
