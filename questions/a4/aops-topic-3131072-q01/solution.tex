Write $N=20160$. Survival requires no immediate-death event and at most one 5-damage event. The probability of neither kind in one minute is $1-0.017-0.04=0.943$, giving (a) $\boxed{0.943^N+0.04N\,0.943^{N-1}}$. To survive as a human, a path must also avoid every transformation. The probability of a minute with none of these three events is 0.883. Human survival therefore has probability $0.883^N+0.04N\,0.883^{N-1}$. Subtracting gives (b) $\boxed{0.943^N+0.04N\,0.943^{N-1}-0.883^N-0.04N\,0.883^{N-1}}$.
