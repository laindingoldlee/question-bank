Since $324=4\cdot3^4$, the Sophie Germain identity gives
\[x^4+324=\left(x^2+6x+18\right)\left(x^2-6x+18\right)=\left((x+3)^2+9\right)\left((x-3)^2+9\right).\]
Writing $f(m)=m^2+9$, the numerator is $f(13)f(7)\,f(25)f(19)\,f(37)f(31)\,f(49)f(43)\,f(61)f(55)$ and the denominator is $f(7)f(1)\,f(19)f(13)\,f(31)f(25)\,f(43)f(37)\,f(55)f(49)$. Everything cancels except
\[\dfrac{f(61)}{f(1)}=\dfrac{3721+9}{10}=\boxed{373}.\]
