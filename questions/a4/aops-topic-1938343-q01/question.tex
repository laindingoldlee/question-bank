Jotaro holds a ball at $(0,0)$ and DIO stands at $(5,0)$. There are walls along the lines $y=1$ and $y=-1$, and the ball bounces off a wall like a light ray (angle of incidence equals angle of reflection). Jotaro throws the ball so that it travels in straight segments, bounces exactly three times, and reaches DIO. What is the minimum distance the ball can travel?
