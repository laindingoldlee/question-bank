Write $F=k\dfrac{m_1m_2}{d^2}$. The new force is $k\dfrac{(2m_1)(2m_2)}{(3d)^2}=\boxed{\dfrac{4}{9}F}$. Thus $\dfrac{4}{9}$ of the original force remains, a decrease of $\dfrac{5}{9}$.
