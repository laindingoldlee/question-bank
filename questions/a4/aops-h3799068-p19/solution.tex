Write $221=qn+r$, with $q\ge1$ and $0\le r<n$. The condition $q\mid r$ is equivalent to $q\mid221$. Since $221=13\cdot17$, possible quotients are $1,13,17,221$. A fixed quotient occurs when $\dfrac{221}{q+1}<n\le\dfrac{221}{q}$. The respective integer ranges are $111\le n\le221$, $16\le n\le17$, $n=13$, and $n=1$, giving counts $111,2,1,1$. Thus there are $\boxed{115}$ lucky integers.
