\textbf{(a)} From $a_2$ and $a_3$: $p+q=5$ and $5p+q=13$, so $p=2$, $q=3$. These fit the given terms: $2\cdot13+3\cdot5=41$ and $2\cdot41+3\cdot13=121$. The next term is $a_6=2\cdot121+3\cdot41=\boxed{365}$.

\textbf{(b)} By strong induction. For $n=0,1$: $\dfrac{1+1}{2}=1$ and $\dfrac{3-1}{2}=1$. If the formula holds for $n-1$ and $n-2$ (with $n\ge2$), then
\[2a_{n-1}+3a_{n-2}=\dfrac{2\cdot3^{n-1}+3\cdot3^{n-2}}{2}+\dfrac{2(-1)^{n-1}+3(-1)^{n-2}}{2}=\dfrac{3^n}{2}+\dfrac{(-1)^{n}}{2},\]
because $2\cdot3^{n-1}+3^{n-1}=3^n$ and $(-1)^{n-2}\left(-2+3\right)=(-1)^n$. So $a_n=\dfrac{3^n+(-1)^n}{2}$. $\blacksquare$
