Let there be $t$ tourists and $c$ cookies per package. Then $2c\equiv1$ and $3c\equiv13\pmod t$, with $t>13$ (a remainder is less than the divisor). Subtracting, $c\equiv12$, so $2c\equiv24\equiv1$, i.e. $t\mid23$. Hence $t=\boxed{23}$, the only divisor of $23$ exceeding $13$. (For instance $c=12$: $24=23+1$ and $36=23+13$.)
