By the difference of squares,
\[2^{14}-1=(2^7-1)(2^7+1)=127\cdot129=3\cdot43\cdot127.\]
The largest proper divisor is the number divided by its smallest prime factor, $3$:
\[\dfrac{16383}{3}=\boxed{5461}.\]
