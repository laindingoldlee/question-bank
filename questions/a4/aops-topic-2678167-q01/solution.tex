$D$, $E$, $V$ are consecutive vertices, so $DE=EV$ is the side $s$ of the octagon and $DV$ is a short diagonal, which is longer than $s$. The congruence $DEV\cong ROY$ matches $DE=RO$ and $EV=OY$, so $s=64$. (Either way, the two equal sides of the isosceles triangle $DEV$ are the sides of the octagon.) The perimeter is $8\cdot64=\boxed{512}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair[] V;string[] nm={"B","I","S","W","A","D","E","V"};
for(int i=0;i<8;++i)V[i]=dir(112.5-45i);
fill(V[5]--V[6]--V[7]--cycle,lightgray);
for(int i=0;i<8;++i){draw(V[i]--V[(i+1)%8],linewidth(0.8));label("$"+nm[i]+"$",V[i],V[i]);}
draw(V[5]--V[7],dashed);label("$64$",(V[5]+V[6])/2,(V[5]+V[6])/2,fontsize(8pt));label("$64$",(V[6]+V[7])/2,(V[6]+V[7])/2,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}
