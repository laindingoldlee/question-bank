The 27 dollars already includes the 2 dollars kept by the bellhop: $27=25+2$. Adding the 2 dollars again double-counts it. The original 30 dollars is accounted for by $\boxed{30=25+2+3}$: hotel, bellhop, and refunds respectively. No dollar is missing.
