$AB\Gamma\Delta$ is a rectangle, and points $K,\Lambda,M,N$ lie on sides $AB$, $B\Gamma$, $\Gamma\Delta$, $\Delta A$ with
\[\dfrac{AK}{KB}=\dfrac{B\Lambda}{\Lambda\Gamma}=\dfrac{\Gamma M}{M\Delta}=\dfrac{\Delta N}{NA}=2.\]
If $E_1$ is the area of $K\Lambda MN$ and $E_2$ the area of the rectangle, find $\dfrac{E_1}{E_2}$.

$\textbf{(A)}\ \dfrac{5}{9}\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ \dfrac{1}{3}\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ \dfrac{9}{5}\qquad\textbf{(D)}\ \dfrac{3}{5}\qquad\textbf{(E)}\ \text{none of these}$
