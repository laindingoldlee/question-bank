Let $AB=x$ and $B\Gamma=y$. The quadrilateral $K\Lambda MN$ is the rectangle minus four corner right triangles:
\begin{itemize}
\item at $B$: legs $KB=\dfrac {x}{3}$ and $B\Lambda=\dfrac{2y}{3}$, area $\dfrac{xy}{9}$;
\item at $\Gamma$: legs $\Lambda\Gamma=\dfrac {y}{3}$ and $\Gamma M=\dfrac{2x}{3}$, area $\dfrac{xy}{9}$;
\item at $\Delta$ and at $A$, likewise $\dfrac{xy}{9}$ each.
\end{itemize}

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(6,0),C=(6,3),D=(0,3); pair K=(4,0),L=(6,2),M=(2,3),N=(0,1);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); filldraw(K--L--M--N--cycle,lightblue,blue);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$\Gamma$",C,NE);label("$\Delta$",D,NW);label("$K$",K,S);label("$\Lambda$",L,E);label("$M$",M,N);label("$N$",N,W);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

So $E_1=xy-\dfrac{4xy}{9}=\dfrac{5}{9}xy$ and $\dfrac{E_1}{E_2}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{9}}$, answer $\textbf{(A)}$, for every rectangle. (The posted solution wrongly treated $K\Lambda MN$ as a rhombus; it is a parallelogram, and the area-from-diagonals formula does not apply.)
