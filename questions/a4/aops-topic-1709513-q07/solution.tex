The $n$th term is $144-8(n-1)=152-8n$. It is negative when $8n>152$, i.e.\ $n>19$. (The $19$th term is $0$, which is not negative.) So the first negative term is the $\boxed{20\text{th}}$ term, equal to $-8$.
