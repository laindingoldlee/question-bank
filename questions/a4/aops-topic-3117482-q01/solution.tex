The $\binom{21}{2}=210$ unordered pairs have sums among the $197$ integers $3,4,\ldots,199$. Two distinct pairs therefore have the same sum. They cannot share an element, because cancelling it would make the pairs identical. Thus the four elements are distinct and give the desired equality.
