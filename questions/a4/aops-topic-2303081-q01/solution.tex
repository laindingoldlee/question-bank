Substitute $y=x-0.9$ and try the whole numbers $x$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $x=0$: $y=-0.9$ and $x^4+y^5=(-0.9)^5<0$;
\item $x=1$: $y=0.1$ and $x^4+y^5=1+0.00001=1.00001$;
\item $x=2$: $y=1.1$ and, since $1.1^2=1.21$, $1.1^3=1.331$, $1.1^4=1.4641$, $1.1^5=1.61051$, we get $x^4+y^5=16+1.61051=17.61051$;
\item $x\ge3$: $y>0$, so $x^4+y^5>x^4\ge81$.
\end{itemize}
So $x=2$ and $y=1.1$. Ben's price is $7-2=5$ dollars, and Jerry's price is $\dfrac{7}{1.1}=\dfrac{70}{11}$ dollars. The difference is
\[\dfrac{70}{11}-5=\boxed{\dfrac{15}{11}}\text{ dollars}\approx\$1.36.\]
