An ice cream costs $7$ dollars. Ben suggests a coupon that takes $x$ dollars off the price, and Jerry suggests a coupon that divides the price by $y$. The numbers $x$ and $y$ satisfy
\[x^4+y^5=17.61051\qquad\text{and}\qquad x-y=0.9,\]
and $x$ is a whole number. What is the absolute value of the difference between the price Ben suggests and the price Jerry suggests?
