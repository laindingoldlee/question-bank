Let $f$ and $g$ be functions from the real numbers to the real numbers, each with an inverse function.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] A student writes $f\left(g\left(g^{-1}\left(f^{-1}(x)\right)\right)\right)=f\left(f^{-1}(x)\right)$. Is this step valid? Simplify the expression completely.
\item[(b)] Deduce that the composition $f\circ g$ has an inverse, namely $(f\circ g)^{-1}=g^{-1}\circ f^{-1}$.
\end{enumerate}
