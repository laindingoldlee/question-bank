The region consists of the points outside the square at distance at most $2$ from it. Next to each side it is a $10\times2$ rectangle, and at each corner it is a quarter of a disc of radius $2$ centred at the corner (the points whose nearest point of the square is that corner).

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
fill((-2,0)--(12,0)--(12,10)--(-2,10)--cycle,rgb(0.85,1,0.85));
fill((0,-2)--(10,-2)--(10,12)--(0,12)--cycle,rgb(0.85,1,0.85));
for(pair c : new pair[]{(0,0),(10,0),(10,10),(0,10)}) fill(circle(c,2),rgb(0.85,1,0.85));
fill((0,0)--(10,0)--(10,10)--(0,10)--cycle,white);
draw((0,0)--(10,0)--(10,10)--(0,10)--cycle,linewidth(1));
draw((0,-2)--(10,-2)^^(12,0)--(12,10)^^(10,12)--(0,12)^^(-2,10)--(-2,0),darkgreen+linewidth(0.9));
draw(arc((10,0),2,-90,0)^^arc((10,10),2,0,90)^^arc((0,10),2,90,180)^^arc((0,0),2,180,270),darkgreen+linewidth(0.9));
draw((10,10)--(12,10),dashed); draw((10,10)--(10,12),dashed); draw((10,10)--(10,10)+2*dir(45),dashed);
label("house",(5,5)); label("$10$",(5,0),N); label("$2$",(10,11),W); label("$2$",(11,10),S);
\end{asy}

So its area is
\[4\cdot(10\cdot2)+4\cdot\dfrac14\pi\cdot2^2=\boxed{80+4\pi}\approx92.6.\]
