A house in the shape of a square with side length $10$ is surrounded by flowers. A fence runs all around the house at a constant distance $2$ from it: the fence consists of all points at distance exactly $2$ from the square. Weedy the Greedy is trapped between the house and the fence and destroys all the flowers there. What is the area of the region between the house and the fence?
