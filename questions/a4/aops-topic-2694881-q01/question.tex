Let $f(x)=x^3-n_1x^2+\left(n_2-k^2\right)x-\left(n_3-k^4\right)$, where $n_1,n_2,n_3$ form a geometric sequence with common ratio $k$, and suppose the roots of $f$ form an arithmetic sequence with common difference $k$. Show that the roots cannot all be nonzero (so the original problem, which asks for $k$ assuming nonzero roots, has no solution).
