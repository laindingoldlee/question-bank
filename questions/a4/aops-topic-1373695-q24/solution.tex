Let her buy $x$ items at $30$ cents, $y$ at $\$2$ and $z$ at $\$3$. In cents,
\[
x+y+z=30,\qquad 30x+200y+300z=3000 .
\]
Subtracting $30$ times the first equation from the second: $170y+270z=2100$, i.e.\ $17y+27z=210$.

Now $z\ge0$ and $27z\le210$ give $z\le7$, and $210-27z$ must be divisible by $17$. Trying $z=0,1,\dots,7$: $210,183,156,129,102,75,48,21$; only $102=17\cdot6$ works, at $z=4$. So $y=6$, $z=4$ and $x=30-6-4=\boxed{20}$. Check: $20\cdot0.30+6\cdot2+4\cdot3=6+12+12=30$ dollars.
