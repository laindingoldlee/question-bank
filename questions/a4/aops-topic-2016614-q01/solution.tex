The number of trailing zeros of $N$ in base $12$ is the largest $k$ with $12^k\mid N$. Since $12=2^2\cdot3$, we need the exponents of $2$ and $3$ in $100!$. By Legendre's formula,
\[
v_2(100!)=\left\lfloor\dfrac{100}{2}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{100}{4}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{100}{8}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{100}{16}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{100}{32}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{100}{64}\right\rfloor=50+25+12+6+3+1=97,
\]
\[
v_3(100!)=33+11+3+1=48 .
\]
Now $12^k=2^{2k}3^k$ divides $100!$ exactly when $2k\le97$ and $k\le48$, that is $k\le48$. So $100!$ ends in $\boxed{48}$ zeros in base $12$.

In general, to count trailing zeros of $n!$ in base $b=p_1^{e_1}\cdots p_r^{e_r}$, compute $\left\lfloor\dfrac{v_{p_i}(n!)}{e_i}\right\rfloor$ for each prime and take the smallest.
