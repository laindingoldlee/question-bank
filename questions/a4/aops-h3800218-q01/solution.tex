The three equal gaps must sum to nine chairs, so each gap is three. Thus each team occupies one of $\{1,4,7\}$, $\{2,5,8\}$, $\{3,6,9\}$. Assign teams to these groups in $3!$ ways and their members within each group in $(3!)^3$ ways. Since chairs are labelled, there is no division by rotations. The answer is $\boxed{(3!)^4=1296}$.
