Let \(P\) be the point \((3,2)\). Let \(Q\) be the reflection of \(P\) about the \(x\)-axis, let \(R\) be the reflection of \(Q\) about the line \(y=-x\), and let \(S\) be the reflection of \(R\) through the origin. Then \(PQRS\) is a convex quadrilateral. What is the area of \(PQRS\)?

\(\textbf{(A) }14\qquad\textbf{(B) }15\qquad\textbf{(C) }16\qquad\textbf{(D) }17\qquad\textbf{(E) }18\)
