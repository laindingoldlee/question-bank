An ellipse is the set of points $P$ with $PF_1+PF_2=2a$, where $F_1\ne F_2$ are fixed points (the foci) and $2a>F_1F_2$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of $F_1F_2$. Prove that the ellipse is symmetric about $M$: if $P$ is on the ellipse, so is the point $P'$ with $M$ as the midpoint of $PP'$. Explain why this means that the centre of the ellipse is equidistant from the foci.
