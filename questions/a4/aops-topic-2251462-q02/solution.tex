The statements have the form ``if $p$ then $q$'' and ``if $q$ then $r$''. Chaining them (the law of syllogism) gives ``if $p$ then $r$'': if Karl runs $1$ mile, then he runs $5280$ feet. Nothing says that Karl actually ran, so (A) and (B) do not follow. The answer is $\boxed{\text{(C)}}$.
