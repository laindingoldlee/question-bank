The \emph{averaging method} approximates $\sqrt N$ (for $N>0$) by starting from a guess $x>0$ and replacing it by $x'=\dfrac{1}{2}\left(x+\dfrac{N}{x}\right)$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $x'-\sqrt N=\dfrac{(x-\sqrt N)^2}{2x}$, so in particular $x'\ge\sqrt N$.
\item[(b)] For $N=10$ start with $x_0=3$ and compute $x_1$ and $x_2$ as fractions. Show that $0<x_2-\sqrt{10}<10^{-5}$.
\end{enumerate}
