We need $x^2+y^2=5$ with integers $x,y$. Since $x^2,y^2\in\{0,1,4\}$ (squares at most $5$), the only way is $\{x^2,y^2\}=\{1,4\}$. So $(x,y)=(\pm1,\pm2)$ or $(\pm2,\pm1)$, giving $\boxed{8}$ points.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
for(int i=-3;i<=3;++i){draw((i,-3.3)--(i,3.3),gray(0.85));draw((-3.3,i)--(3.3,i),gray(0.85));}
draw((-3.5,0)--(3.6,0),Arrow(4));draw((0,-3.5)--(0,3.6),Arrow(4));
draw(circle((0,0),sqrt(5)),dashed);
pair[] P={(1,2),(2,1),(2,-1),(1,-2),(-1,-2),(-2,-1),(-2,1),(-1,2)};
for(pair p:P)dot(p,black+3.5bp);
draw((0,0)--(1,2));label("$\sqrt5$",(0.5,1),W);
label("$x$",(3.6,0),S);label("$y$",(0,3.6),W);
\end{asy}
