Write $BC=a$. Apollonius's median formula applied to median $BE$ gives
\[
BE^2=\dfrac{2BA^2+2BC^2-AC^2}{4}
=\dfrac{196+2a^2}{4}.
\]
Setting this equal to $196$ gives $a^2=294$, hence $\boxed{BC=7\sqrt6}$.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

\end{asy}
