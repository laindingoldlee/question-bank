Every game hands out exactly $2$ points, so the teams have $80$ points in total. The bottom four teams have $80-(15+13+12+11)=29$ points. If the fifth-place team has $x$ and each sixth-place team has $y$, then $x+3y=29$ with $y<x<11$ (fifth place is strictly behind fourth and strictly ahead of sixth).

From $x=29-3y<11$ we get $y\ge7$; from $y<29-3y$ we get $y\le7$. So $y=7$ and $x=\boxed{8}$.
