In a soccer league with $8$ teams, each team earns $2$ points for a win, $1$ point for a tie and $0$ points for a loss. After a total of $40$ games, each team has played the same number of games, and the top four teams have $15$, $13$, $12$ and $11$ points. There is one team in fifth place and three teams tied for sixth place. How many points does the fifth-place team have?
