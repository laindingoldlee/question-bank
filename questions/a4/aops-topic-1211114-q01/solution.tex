Multiply the fractional part by $5$ repeatedly and record integer parts:
\[\dfrac{3}{8}\cdot5=\dfrac{15}{8}=1+\dfrac{7}{8},\qquad \dfrac{7}{8}\cdot5=\dfrac{35}{8}=4+\dfrac{3}{8},\]
and we are back to $\dfrac{3}{8}$. So $\dfrac{3}{8}=\boxed{0.\overline{14}_5}$. Check: $0.\overline{14}_5=\dfrac{1\cdot5+4}{5^2-1}=\dfrac{9}{24}=\dfrac{3}{8}$.
