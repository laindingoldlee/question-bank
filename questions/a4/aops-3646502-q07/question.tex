Tanish is playing a video game. Unfortunately, two cosmic rays randomly hit his computer and cause two bit flips! If the original number in base $2$ was $110101110$, and the positive difference between the original number and new number (in base $10$) is $56$, what is the new number (in base $10$)?



Note: In base $2$, a bit flip is described by the change of a digit from $0$ to $1$, or $1$ to $0$.
