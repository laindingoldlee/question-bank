By stars and bars, the number of ordered triples of positive integers with sum $2005$ is
\[N=\dbinom{2004}{2}=\dfrac{2004\cdot2003}{2}=1002\cdot2003=2007006.\]
The remainder modulo $1000$ is $\boxed{6}$.
