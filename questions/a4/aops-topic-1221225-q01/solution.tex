Integer roots divide $60$. Testing: $x=1$ gives $1-29-31+31-32+60=0$; $x=-2$ gives $-32-464+248+124+64+60=0$. Dividing,
\[x^5-29x^4-31x^3+31x^2-32x+60=(x-1)(x+2)(x^3-30x^2+x-30)=(x-1)(x+2)(x-30)(x^2+1).\]
So the integer roots are $\boxed{1,\ -2,\ 30}$ (and $x^2+1$ has no real roots).
