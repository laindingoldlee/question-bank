A ``special house'' is a solid made of a cube with a right square pyramid on top of it: the base of the pyramid is the top face of the cube. The cube's edge length $s$ and the pyramid's height $h$ are positive integers, the height of the pyramid is greater than the height of the cube ($h>s$), and the slant height of the pyramid (the distance from its apex to the midpoint of an edge of its base) is less than the length of a diagonal of the base.

For a given $s$, let $N(s)$ be the number of possible values of $h$. What is the smallest $s$ for which $N(s)=100$?
