Gabriel paints $\dfrac13$ of the wall per hour and Jeff $\dfrac1x$. Jeff worked $1+5=6$ hours and Gabriel $1$ hour, so
\[\dfrac13+\dfrac6x=1\iff\dfrac6x=\dfrac23\iff x=\boxed{9}.\]
