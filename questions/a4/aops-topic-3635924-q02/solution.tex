Measure position northward from Austin's start. At 10:30, Austin is $20(1.5)=30$ kilometers north. At 11:15, Jessica is $150-60(1.25)=75$ kilometers north. The train travels $75-30=45$ kilometers in $\dfrac{3}{4}$ hour, so its speed is $\dfrac{45}{\dfrac{3}{4}}=\boxed{60}$ kilometers per hour.
