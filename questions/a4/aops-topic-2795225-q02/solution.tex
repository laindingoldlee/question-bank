The obtuse triangle contains 120. It cannot contain 80. If it contains 55, its remaining angle is 5, leaving 80 and 10 in the other triangle, whose third angle would be 90, not acute. If it contains 10, its third angle is 50 and the acute triangle is 80,55,45. If it contains neither 55 nor 10, all three supplied angles 80,55,10 would be in the other triangle and would not sum to 180. Thus the acute triangle has smallest angle $\boxed{45^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(3,0),C=extension(A,A+dir(120),B,B+dir(130));draw(A--B--C--cycle);label("$120^\circ$",A,SW);label("$50^\circ$",B,SE);label("$10^\circ$",C,N);pair D=(5,0),Ee=(8,0),F=extension(D,D+dir(80),Ee,Ee+dir(125));draw(D--Ee--F--cycle);label("$80^\circ$",D,SW);label("$55^\circ$",Ee,SE);label("$45^\circ$",F,N);
\end{asy}
