Put $u=|x|$, with domain $u\ge2$. The equation becomes $\sqrt{u^2-4}=u-2$. Both sides are nonnegative, so squaring is equivalent: $u^2-4=u^2-4u+4$, giving $u=2$. Both values work, hence $\boxed{x=\pm2}$.
