Divide by $5$: $x^2+4x+5=0$, that is $(x+2)^2=-1$. There is no real root, since a square is never negative. Over the complex numbers, $x+2=\pm i$, so
\[
x=\boxed{-2\pm i}.
\]
(The quadratic formula gives the same: the discriminant is $16-20=-4$ and $x=\dfrac{-4\pm2i}{2}$.) Check: $(-2+i)^2+4(-2+i)+5=(3-4i)+(-8+4i)+5=0$.
