Write $x=\prod p_i^{a_i}$. Then $\tau(x^x)=\prod(a_ix+1)=703=19\cdot37$. There are at most two distinct primes in $x$. With two, $x$ divides both $18$ and $36$, hence divides $18$. Only $6$ and $18$ among these divisors have two distinct primes; $6$ gives $49$ divisors, whereas $18=2\cdot3^2$ gives $19\cdot37=703$. With one prime, $x=p^a$ and $ap^a=702$. Since $a2^a\le702$, $a\le6$, and the divisors of $702$ in that range are $1,2,3,6$. The respective values $702,351,234,117$ are not a prime, square, cube, or sixth power. Thus $\boxed{x=18}$.
