Put $A=(0,0),B=(s,0),C=(0,s),X=(u,v)$. From $AX=6,BX=4$, obtain $u^2+v^2=36$ and $2su=s^2+20$. The dot-product angle condition gives $36-su=24\cos135^\circ=-12\sqrt2$. Thus $su=36+12\sqrt2$ and $s^2=52+24\sqrt2$. Since $v>0$, $(sv)^2=36s^2-(su)^2=288$, so $sv=12\sqrt2$. Therefore $CX^2=s^2-2sv+36=\boxed{88}$. 
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(220);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
real s=sqrt(52+24*sqrt(2));pair A=(0,0),B=(s,0),C=(0,s),X=((36+12*sqrt(2))/s,12*sqrt(2)/s);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--X--B);draw(C--X,dashed);label("$A$",A,(-1,-1));label("$B$",B,(1,-1));label("$C$",C,(-1,1));label("$X$",X,(0,1));
\end{asy}
