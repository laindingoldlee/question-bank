The line passes through $(0,0)$ and $(1,a)$, so it rises $a$ units for each unit to the right. If $\theta$ is its angle with the positive $x$-axis, then $\tan\theta=\dfrac{a}{1}=a$. Hence
\[\theta=\boxed{\arctan a}\]
(positive for $a>0$, negative for $a<0$). The acute angle between the line and the $x$-axis is $\arctan|a|$.

usepackage("amsmath");

defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));

draw((0,0)--(2,1.5),blue+linewidth(1)); draw((1,0)--(1,0.75),dashed);
label("$1$",(0.5,0),S); label("$a$",(1,0.375),E); dot((1,0.75)); label("$(1,a)$",(1,0.75),NW);
draw(arc((0,0),0.4,0,degrees(atan(0.75))),Arrow); label("$\theta$",(0.45,0.15),E);
label("$y=ax$",(2,1.5),E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
