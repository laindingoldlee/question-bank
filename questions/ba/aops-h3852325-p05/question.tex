For $x>-3$, $x\ne4$, an identity is given by
\[
\dfrac{x^4-x^3-10x^2+3x-9}{x^2-x-12}=x^2+P+\dfrac{Q}{x-4}.
\]
Find $P,Q$. Let $C$ be the graph of the expression on the left for $-3<x<3.5$. Find its tangent at $x=2$. Finally find the exact area bounded by $C$, the coordinate axes, and the line $x=2$.
