Multiplication by $(x-4)(x+3)$ and comparison of coefficients gives $P=2,Q=5$; indeed
\[
(x^2+2)(x^2-x-12)+5(x+3)=x^4-x^3-10x^2+3x-9.
\]
Thus $g(x)=x^2+2+\dfrac{5}{x-4}$ and $g'(x)=2x-\dfrac{5}{(x-4)^2}$. At $x=2$, $g(2)=\dfrac{7}{2}$ and $g'(2)=\dfrac{11}{4}$. The tangent is
\[
\boxed{y=\dfrac{11}{4}x-2}.
\]
On $[0,2]$ the curve is positive: for $0\le x\le1$, $2-\dfrac{5}{4-x}\ge\dfrac{1}{3}$; for $1\le x\le2$, $x^2+2-\dfrac{5}{4-x}\ge\dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore its bounded area is
\[
\int_0^2\left(x^2+2+\dfrac{5}{x-4}\right)\,dx
=\left[\dfrac{x^3}{3}+2x+5\ln|x-4|\right]_0^2
=\boxed{\dfrac{20}{3}-5\ln2}.
\]
