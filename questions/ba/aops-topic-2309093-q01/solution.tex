\textbf{Counting proof.} Count the ways to choose, from $m$ people, a committee of $n$ people and, within it, a subcommittee of $r$ people.
\begin{itemize}
\item Choose the committee first ($\dbinom mn$ ways), then the subcommittee from its $n$ members ($\dbinom nr$ ways).
\item Choose the subcommittee first ($\dbinom mr$ ways), then the other $n-r$ committee members from the remaining $m-r$ people ($\dbinom{m-r}{n-r}$ ways).
\end{itemize}
Both count the same choices, so the two products are equal.

\textbf{Algebraic proof.}
\[\dbinom mn\dbinom nr=\dfrac{m!}{n!\,(m-n)!}\cdot\dfrac{n!}{r!\,(n-r)!}=\dfrac{m!}{r!\,(n-r)!\,(m-n)!},\]
\[\dbinom mr\dbinom{m-r}{n-r}=\dfrac{m!}{r!\,(m-r)!}\cdot\dfrac{(m-r)!}{(n-r)!\,(m-n)!}=\dfrac{m!}{r!\,(n-r)!\,(m-n)!}.\]
$\blacksquare$
