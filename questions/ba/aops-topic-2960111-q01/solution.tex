The digits divisible by $3$ are $0,3,6,9$. Count the complement: every digit lies in $\{1,2,4,5,7,8\}$, giving $6^3=216$ numbers (none has a leading zero). There are $900$ three-digit numbers, so the answer is $900-216=\boxed{684}$.
