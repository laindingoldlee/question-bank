Let the original number be $\overline{abc}$ with $a\ge1$; the reversal is $r=100c+10b+a$. We need $r$ even, so $a\in\{2,4,6,8\}$, and $11\mid r$, i.e. $c-b+a\equiv0\pmod{11}$, so $b=a+c$ or $b=a+c-11$.

For fixed $a$: $b=a+c\le9$ gives $10-a$ choices of $c$; $b=a+c-11\ge0$ gives $c\ge11-a$, i.e. $a-1$ choices. That is $9$ per value of $a$, $36$ in all. The probability is $\dfrac{36}{900}=\dfrac1{25}$. \textbf{(D)}
