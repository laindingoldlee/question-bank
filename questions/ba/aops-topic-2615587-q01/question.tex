A rectangular house measures $30$ feet by $40$ feet. A goat outside the house is tied to one corner of the house with a $55$-foot leash. The leash can wrap around the corners of the house but cannot pass through it, and the goat and leash have negligible size. What is the area of the region in which the goat can roam?
