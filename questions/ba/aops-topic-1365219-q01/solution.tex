Factor $2$ out of the terms with $y$ and complete the square inside:
\[
2y^2-6y+18=2\left(y^2-3y\right)+18=2\left(\left(y-\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^2-\dfrac{9}{4}\right)+18 =2\left(y-\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^2-\dfrac{9}{2}+18 .
\]
So
\[
2y^2-6y+18=\boxed{2\left(y-\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^2+\dfrac{27}{2}}.
\]
The student's expression $2\left(y-\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^2=2y^2-6y+\dfrac{9}{2}$ is missing the constant $\dfrac{27}{2}$; completing the square produces a square \emph{plus a constant}. (The student also switched the variable from $y$ to $x$.)
