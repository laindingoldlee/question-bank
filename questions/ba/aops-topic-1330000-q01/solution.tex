When the shark starts, the tuna is $13.5\cdot7=94.5$ ft ahead, and the shark closes the gap at $15-13.5=1.5$ ft/s.

(a) After $30$ s the gap is $94.5-1.5\cdot30=\boxed{49.5\text{ ft}}$.

(b) The gap closes after $\dfrac{94.5}{1.5}=\boxed{63\text{ s}}$.

(c) In that time the shark swims $15\cdot63=\boxed{945\text{ ft}}$ (and the tuna, in $70$ s, also covers $13.5\cdot70=945$ ft).
