A shark swims at $15$ feet per second and a tuna at $13.5$ feet per second, in the same direction along a line. The tuna has a $7$-second head start.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] How far behind the tuna is the shark $30$ seconds after the shark starts?
\item[(b)] How long after it starts does the shark take to catch the tuna?
\item[(c)] How far does the shark travel before catching the tuna?
\end{enumerate}
