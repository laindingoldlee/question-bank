Let $F (x) = e^x \ln x + \dfrac{e^x}{x} - e x$ for $x>0$. Then $F^{\prime}(1)=0$ and $F^{\prime\prime} (x) = e^x (\ln x + \dfrac{3}{x} - \dfrac{3}{x^2} + \dfrac{2}{x^3})$. Since $\ln x \ge 1 - \dfrac{1}{x}$, the parenthesis is at least $\dfrac{x^3+2x^2-3x+2}{x^3} > 0$: the quadratic $2x^2-3x+2$ has negative discriminant and positive leading coefficient. Thus $F$ is strictly convex with its unique minimum $F(1)=0$. Also $F\to\infty$ as $x\to0^+$ and as $x\to\infty$. Therefore $F(x)-a$ has two zeros exactly when $\boxed{a>0}$, one zero when $a=0$, and none when $a<0$.
