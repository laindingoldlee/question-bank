One die has a $2$ on three faces, a $3$ on two faces and a $5$ on the sixth face. A second die has a $1$ on one face, a $4$ on four faces and a $6$ on the sixth face. Both dice are fair (each face equally likely).
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] How many equally likely outcomes (face pairs) are there when both dice are rolled? A student answers $3\cdot3=9$; what is wrong with that?
\item[(b)] Using generating functions, find for each possible total the number of face pairs that give it. Which total is most likely, and what is its probability?
\end{enumerate}
