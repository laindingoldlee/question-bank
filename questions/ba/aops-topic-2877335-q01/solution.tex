Let $s$ and $\ell$ be the numbers of small and large dogs. Then $s+\ell=49$ and $s-\ell=36$. Adding gives $2s=85$, which has no integer solution.

Equivalently, $s+\ell$ and $s-\ell$ differ by $2\ell$, so they have the same parity; but $49$ is odd and $36$ is even. So $\boxed{\text{no such split exists}}$.
