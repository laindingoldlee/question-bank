Subtracting gives $(x-y)(x+y)=7 $. Since $ x,y>0 $, we have $ x+y>|x-y|$; both factors are positive integers. The only possibility is $ x-y=1,x+y=7 $. This yields $ x=4,y=3 $, which satisfy both equations. Hence $\boxed{x+y=7}$.
