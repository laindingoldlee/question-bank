Let $P(1)=p$ and $P(k)=q$ for $k=2,\dots,6$. Then $p+5q=1$. The trickster expects to keep $50$ cents, so the expected payout is $\$2.50$:
\[
p+(2+3+4+5+6)q=p+20q=2.5 .
\]
Subtracting, $15q=1.5$, so $q=0.1$ and $p=0.5$ (indeed $p>q$). The probability of an odd number is
\[
P(1)+P(3)+P(5)=0.5+0.1+0.1=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{10}} .
\]
