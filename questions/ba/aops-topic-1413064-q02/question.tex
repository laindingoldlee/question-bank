A trickster runs a street game: a customer pays $\$3$, rolls a die and receives as many dollars as the number rolled. The trickster has rigged the die so that he expects to make $50$ cents per roll. On his die the numbers $2,3,4,5,6$ are equally likely, and $1$ is more likely than each of them. What is the probability of rolling an odd number on the trickster's die?
