Count the complement: of the $26^3$ strings, exactly $25^3$ avoid A. The answer is $26^3-25^3=\boxed{1951}$. Choosing a marked A-position counts a string with two As twice and AAA three times, so these cases are overcounted. Equivalently, exactly one, two, or three As gives $3\cdot 25^2+3\cdot 25+1=1951$.
