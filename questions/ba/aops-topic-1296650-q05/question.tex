Fourteen identical pieces of candy are shared among six children. The oldest wants at least $4$ pieces, the next oldest wants an even number of pieces, one child wants at most $2$ pieces, the youngest wants at most $1$ piece, and the other two children accept any number (including zero). In how many ways can the candy be distributed?
