When reading a book, Charlie made a list by writing down, at the end of each day, the page number of the last page he had finished. (He always finished every page he started.) His mom thought the list showed the number of pages he read each day. At the end of the $8$th day she added the numbers on his list and thought Charlie had read $432$ pages. If Charlie started reading on page $1$ and read the same number of pages each day of the eight days, how many pages had he actually read by the end of the $8$th day?
