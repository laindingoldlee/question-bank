If Charlie read $p$ pages a day, the list is $p,2p,3p,\dots,8p$, whose sum is
\[
(1+2+\dots+8)p=36p=432,
\]
so $p=12$. By the end of the $8$th day he had read $8p=\boxed{96}$ pages (the last number on his list).
