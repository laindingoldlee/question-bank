Substitution gives $x^4-2x^2=64x^6$, or $x^2(64x^4-x^2+2)=0$. The quadratic $64u^2-u+2$ has negative discriminant $1-512$ and positive leading coefficient, so it is positive for every real $u$. Thus $x=0$, $y=-1$, and the answer is $\boxed1$.
