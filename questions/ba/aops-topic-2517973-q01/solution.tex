Put $a=2^x$ and $b=3^x$. Then $4^x=a^2$, $6^x=ab$ and $9^x=b^2$, so the equation is
\[a^2+5ab+6b^2=0\iff(a+2b)(a+3b)=0.\]
But for real $x$, $a=2^x>0$ and $b=3^x>0$, so $a+2b>0$ and $a+3b>0$, and the product cannot be $0$. (Even more directly: each of $4^x$, $6^x$, $9^x$ is positive, so the left side is positive.)

So the equation has $\boxed{\text{no real solutions}}$.
