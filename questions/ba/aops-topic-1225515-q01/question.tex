$12911$ people stand in a circle, numbered $1$ to $12911$. Person $1$ gets a candy, person $2$ leaves, person $3$ gets a candy, person $4$ leaves, and so on around the circle (people who already have candy are still in the circle and count again in later laps), skipping people who have left, until only one person remains. What is that person's number?
