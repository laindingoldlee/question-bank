This is the Josephus problem with every second person eliminated. If $n=2^m+L$ with $0\le L<2^m$, the survivor is $2L+1$. (After $L$ eliminations, $2^m$ people remain and the next to receive candy is person $2L+1$; with a power of $2$ the person who starts a round survives, since each full lap halves the circle and keeps the starter.)

Here $2^{13}=8192$ and $12911=8192+4719$, so the survivor is $2\cdot4719+1=\boxed{9439}$.
