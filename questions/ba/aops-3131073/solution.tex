The notation $a\equiv b\pmod8$ means $8\mid(a-b)$, equivalently the two integers have the same remainder upon division by $8$. A quadratic residue is a remainder attained by an integer square. Squaring representatives $0,1,\ldots,7$ gives remainders $0,1,4,1,0,1,4,1$. Thus the possible square remainders are $\boxed{0,1,4}$. Under conventions excluding zero from the term quadratic residue, the nonzero residues are $1,4$.
