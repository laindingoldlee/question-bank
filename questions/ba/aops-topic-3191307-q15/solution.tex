If pizza is included, all three can eat it, and the other four items are arbitrary: $2^4=16$ choices. Without pizza, at least one of hamburger and hot dog must be included ($3$ choices), and at least one of apple pie and ice cream ($3$ choices). Olivia can eat the selected meat item. The total is $16+9=\boxed{25}$.
