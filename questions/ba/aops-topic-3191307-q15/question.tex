A menu contains hamburger, hot dog, pizza, apple pie, and ice cream. Sam eats hamburger, hot dog, or pizza; Emma eats pizza, apple pie, or ice cream; Olivia eats everything except apple pie. How many subsets of menu items give each person at least one item they can eat?
