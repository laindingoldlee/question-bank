Let the length be $L$. Comparing distances during the same time gives $\dfrac{v_C}{v_B}=\dfrac{L-35}{L-15}$. Comparing at Boris\textquotesingle s finish gives $\dfrac{v_C}{v_B}=\dfrac{L-22}{L}$. Equate and cross-multiply: $L(L-35)=(L-15)(L-22)$, so $2L=330$. Thus $\boxed{L=165\text{ metres}}$. This exceeds $35$ and gives consistent positive speeds.
