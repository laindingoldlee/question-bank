Consider the $|y|+1$ numbers $x^0,x^1,\dots,x^{|y|}$ modulo $|y|$. There are only $|y|$ residues, so by the pigeonhole principle two of them, $x^m$ and $x^n$ with $m\ne n$, are congruent. Then $y\mid x^m-x^n$. $\blacksquare$
