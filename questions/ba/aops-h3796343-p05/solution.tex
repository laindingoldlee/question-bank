Put $u=ab$ and $t=\dfrac{x}{b}$. The fixed-point equation becomes $ut(1+t)=1$. Also $f=\dfrac{b}{u+1}$, so $t=\dfrac{3}{2(u+1)}$. Substitution gives $3u(2u+5)=4(u+1)^2$, or $(2u-1)(u+4)=0$. Since $u>0$, $u=\dfrac{1}{2}$, and $\boxed{100ab=50}$. Conversely this positive value satisfies the equations, so no extraneous positive value was introduced.
