Al has \(2019\) red jelly beans, Bob has \(2018\) green jelly beans and Carl has \(x\) blue jelly beans. All the beans are put together, and beans are drawn without looking. The minimum number of beans that must be drawn to guarantee at least \(2\) beans of each colour is \(4041\). Compute \(x\).
