Grandfather has three apple trees with $39$ apples in total, and each tree has the same number of apples. The apples grow on exactly eight branches: one tree has two branches and each of the other two trees has three branches. Every branch carries at least one apple, and no two branches carry the same number of apples. Find all possibilities for the numbers of apples on the branches of each tree.
