Write $2\dfrac{1}{6}=\dfrac{13}{6}$ and $0.15=\dfrac{3}{20}$. Then
\[
\dfrac{11}{15}x=\dfrac{13}{6}-\dfrac{3}{20}=\dfrac{130-9}{60}=\dfrac{121}{60},\qquad x=\dfrac{121}{60}\cdot\dfrac{15}{11}=\boxed{\dfrac{11}{4}}.
\]
