The product is $50\cdot51\cdots100=\dfrac{100!}{49!}$. The number of trailing zeros is the number of factors $10=2\cdot5$, and there are far more factors $2$ than $5$, so we count factors of $5$.

Multiples of $5$ from $50$ to $100$: $50,55,\dots,100$, that is, $11$ numbers. Multiples of $25$: $50,75,100$, each giving one extra factor $5$. (No multiple of $125$.) The total is $11+3=14$, so the product ends in $\boxed{14}$ zeros. (Equivalently, $100!$ has $24$ factors $5$ and $49!$ has $10$, and $24-10=14$.)
