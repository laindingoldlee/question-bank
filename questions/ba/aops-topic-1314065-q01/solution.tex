All $2^3\cdot2^5=256$ outcomes are equally likely. Both show $k$ heads in $\dbinom3k\dbinom5k$ ways:
\[
\sum_{k=0}^{3}\dbinom3k\dbinom5k=1\cdot1+3\cdot5+3\cdot10+1\cdot10=56 .
\]
So the probability is $\dfrac{56}{256}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{32}}$; the key is right. (A total of $51$ comes from miscounting a case; for instance using $\dbinom52=10$ with weight $3$ but $\dbinom53$ with weight $\dfrac{1}{2}$.)
