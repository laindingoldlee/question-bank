Jamie draws a path on the coordinate plane. He starts at $(0,0)$ and repeatedly draws a segment of length $1$ either to the right or up, each with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, independently. What is the probability that his path passes through the point $(4,4)$? Express your answer as a common fraction.
