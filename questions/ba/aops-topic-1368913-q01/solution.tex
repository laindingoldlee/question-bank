Measure rates in pools per hour. Taps $A$, $B$, $C$ together fill $\dfrac{1}{8}$ and taps $A$ and $B$ fill $\dfrac{1}{12}$, so tap $C$ alone fills
\[\dfrac{1}{8}-\dfrac{1}{12}=\dfrac{3-2}{24}=\dfrac{1}{24}\]
of the pool per hour. Tap $C$ alone takes $\boxed{24}$ hours. (The time for $A$ alone, $36$ hours, is not needed; it gives tap $B$ the rate $\dfrac{1}{12}-\dfrac{1}{36}=\dfrac{1}{18}$.)
