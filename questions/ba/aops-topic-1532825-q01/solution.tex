Since $64=2^6$ and $512=2^9$, we need $x$ with $(2^6)^x=2^9$, i.e.\ $6x=9$. So
\[
\log_{64}512=\dfrac{9}{6}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{2}}.
\]
(Check: $64^{\dfrac{3}{2}}=\left(\sqrt{64}\right)^3=8^3=512$.)
