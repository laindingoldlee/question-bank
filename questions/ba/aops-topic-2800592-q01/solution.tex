Let the current be $c\ge0$, so your speed in still water is $c+3$. Upstream your speed is $3$, taking $1$ hour. Downstream it is $2c+3\ge3$, taking at most $1$ hour. So the round trip takes at most $2$ hours, not $4.5$. (Formally, $1+\dfrac3{2c+3}=4.5$ gives $c=-\dfrac{15}{14}<0$.) The problem has $\boxed{\text{no valid solution}}$.
