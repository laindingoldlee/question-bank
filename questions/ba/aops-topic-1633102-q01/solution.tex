The last digits of the powers of $4$ alternate: $4^1=4$, $4^2=16$, $4^3=64$, $4^4=256,\dots$, because $4\cdot4=16$ ends in $6$ and $6\cdot4=24$ ends in $4$. Odd powers end in $4$ and even powers end in $6$, so $4^{50}$ ends in $\boxed{6}$.
