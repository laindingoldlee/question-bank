On a strip of length 5 the reversed block must be C,B,A in cells 3,4,5. C, initially at 3, could then never move, but A would have to use jumps $1\to3\to5$, contradicting the occupied cell 3. For six cells the following legal sequence works (dots denote empty cells):
\[ABC\cdot\cdot\cdot\to A\cdot CB\cdot\cdot\to\cdot ACB\cdot\cdot\to\cdot AC\cdot B\cdot\to\cdot\cdot CAB\cdot\to\cdot\cdot C\cdot BA\to\cdot\cdot\cdot CBA.\]
