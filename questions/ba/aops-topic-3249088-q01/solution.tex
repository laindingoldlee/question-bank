Maintain the current factorial $p$ and accumulated total $S$, initially $p=1,S=0$. For $k=1,\ldots,n$, replace $p$ by $kp$ and $S$ by $S+p$. Inductively $p=k!$ after step $k$ and $S=\sum_{j=1}^k j!$, proving correctness. Each iteration uses one multiplication and one addition. For $n=6$, the sum is $1+2+6+24+120+720=\boxed{873}$.
