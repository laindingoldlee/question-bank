In the $20$ minutes from 7:05 to 7:25 they cover $\dfrac{3}{4}-\dfrac{1}{3}=\dfrac{5}{12}$ of the trip, so the whole trip takes $20\cdot\dfrac{12}{5}=48$ minutes. The remaining $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of the trip takes $12$ minutes, so they arrive at $\boxed{\text{7:37 AM}}$.
