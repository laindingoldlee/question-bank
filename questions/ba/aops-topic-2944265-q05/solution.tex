The formal binomial expansion gives $\displaystyle(1-4x)^{-\dfrac{1}{2}}=\sum_{k\ge0}\binom{2k}{k}x^k$: indeed $\binom{-\dfrac{1}{2}}{k}(-4)^k=\dfrac{(2k)!}{(k!)^2}$. Squaring both sides gives $(1-4x)^{-1}=\sum_{n\ge0}4^n x^n$. The coefficient of $x^n$ on the squared series is exactly the stated convolution, proving the identity. Formal power series avoid any convergence issue because each coefficient uses finitely many terms.
