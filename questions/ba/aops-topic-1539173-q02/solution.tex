Divide digit by digit in base $6$, as in long division. The first digit $3$ divided by $2$ gives $1$ with remainder $1$; the remainder is worth $1\cdot6=6$ units in the next place, so the next step divides $6+4=10$ by $2$, giving $5$. Hence
\[
\dfrac{34_6}{2_6}=\boxed{15_6}.
\]
Check: $34_6=3\cdot6+4=22$, $22\div2=11$, and $11=1\cdot6+5=15_6$. (The same short division works for longer numbers: an odd digit leaves a remainder $1$, which adds $6$ to the next digit before halving.)
