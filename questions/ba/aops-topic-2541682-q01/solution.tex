By Vieta's formulas, $a+b+c=2$, $ab+bc+ca=5$ and $abc=6$. Expanding,
\[(a+b+c)(ab+bc+ca)=a^2b+a^2c+b^2a+b^2c+c^2a+c^2b+3abc.\]
Hence the required value is $(a+b+c)(ab+bc+ca)+abc=2\cdot5+6=\boxed{16}$.
