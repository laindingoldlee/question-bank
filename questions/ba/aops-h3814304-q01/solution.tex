The domain is $x\ge0$. Put $t=\sqrt x\ge0$. AM-GM applied to $\phi t^4,\dfrac{\mu}{3},\dfrac{\mu}{3},\dfrac{\mu}{3}$ gives
\[
\phi t^4+\mu\ge4\left(\dfrac{\phi\mu^3}{27}\right)^{\dfrac{1}{4}}t=\psi t.
\]
Equality holds exactly when $\phi t^4=\dfrac{\mu}{3}$; $t=0$ is not a root because $\mu>0$. Hence there is a unique real root,
\[
\boxed{x=\sqrt{\dfrac{\mu}{3\phi}}=\dfrac{16\mu^2}{9\psi^2}}.
\]
