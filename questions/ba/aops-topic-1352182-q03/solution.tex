The list price is $18\cdot6=\$108$. After the $42\%$ discount he pays $58\%$ of it: $108\cdot0.58=\$62.64$. With $8\%$ tax:
\[
62.64\cdot1.08=67.6512,
\]
so he pays $\boxed{\$67.65}$. (Since both steps are multiplications, applying the tax before the discount would give the same total, $108\cdot1.08\cdot0.58$.)
