The two $A$'s occupy a pair of the $7$ positions, and each of the $\dbinom72=21$ pairs is equally likely (the other five letters are different and fill the remaining positions in $5!$ ways in every case). Six of the pairs are adjacent, so the probability that the $A$'s are not next to each other is
\[1-\dfrac{6}{21}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{7}}.\]
