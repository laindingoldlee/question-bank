Let $u=1+y$ and $v=1-y$. Then $u^2+v^2=2+2y^2$, so $1+y^2=\dfrac{u^2+v^2}{2}$ and the expression is
\[u^2-x^2(u^2+v^2)+x^4v^2=u^2(1-x^2)-x^2v^2(1-x^2)=(1-x^2)(u^2-x^2v^2).\]
Hence it equals $(1-x^2)(u-xv)(u+xv)$, that is,
\[\boxed{(1-x)(1+x)(1+y-x+xy)(1+y+x-xy)}.\]
