If the prime exponents of $k$ are $e_i$, then $\prod(e_i+1)=70=2\cdot5\cdot7$. The unordered multiplicative partitions of $70$ are $(70),(2,35),(5,14),(7,10),(2,5,7)$. Cubing changes each divisor factor $d=e_i+1$ to $3e_i+1=3d-2$. The resulting divisor counts are $208,412,520,532,988$, all different and all realizable by choosing distinct primes. Hence $\boxed5$.
