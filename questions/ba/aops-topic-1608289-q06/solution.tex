Let the free allowance be $a$ kg, the rate $r$ dollars per kg, and the two weights $w_1$, $w_2$ (both above $a$, since both passengers pay), with $w_1+w_2=105$. Then
\[
(w_1-a)r=12,\qquad (w_2-a)r=18,\qquad (105-a)r=78 .
\]
Adding the first two equations gives $(105-2a)r=30$. Subtracting this from the third gives $ar=48$. Then the third equation reads $105r-48=78$, so $r=1.2$ dollars per kg and
\[
a=\dfrac{48}{1.2}=\boxed{40\text{ kg}}.
\]
Check: the passengers carry $40+10=50$ kg and $40+15=55$ kg, paying $12$ and $18$ dollars; one passenger with $105$ kg would pay $1.2\cdot65=78$ dollars.
