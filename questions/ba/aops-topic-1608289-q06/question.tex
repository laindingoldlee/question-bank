The combined weight of two airline passengers' checked luggage is $105$ kg. The airline allows each passenger a certain maximum weight free of charge and charges a constant rate per kg for any extra weight. One passenger pays \$12.00 for the extra weight of her luggage, and the other passenger pays \$18.00 for the extra weight of his luggage. If all the luggage had belonged to one of the passengers, the overweight charge would have been \$78.00. How many kg of luggage is one person permitted without charge?
