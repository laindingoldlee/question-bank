A chess player wins each game with probability $70\%$, independently of the other games, and has $\$100$. A bet of $\$s$ on a game wins $\$s$ if the player wins that game and loses $\$s$ otherwise. The player chooses between two plans: (I) ten bets of $\$10$, one on each of ten games, or (II) one bet of $\$100$ on a single game.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Under which plan is the player more likely to end with more than $\$100$?
\item[(b)] Which plan has the larger expected final amount?
\item[(c)] More generally, consider the plan of $n$ bets of $\$\dfrac{100}{n}$ each on $n$ different games. Show that all these plans have the same expected profit, and find the probability of making a profit for $n=2$ and for $n=4$.
\end{enumerate}
