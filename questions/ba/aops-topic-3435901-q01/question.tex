A continuously running twelve-hour analogue watch shows the correct time at Monday noon. At Tuesday 11 a.m. it is $1$ minute $15$ seconds slow, and it continues losing time at that constant rate. After how many elapsed days will its hour, minute, and second hands next all show the correct time? Ignore date and AM/PM displays.
