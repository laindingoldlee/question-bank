The watch loses $75$ seconds in $23$ hours. The three hands first match again when the total lag is exactly $12$ hours, or $43200$ seconds. The required actual time is $\dfrac{43200}{75}\cdot23$ hours. Converting to days gives $\dfrac{43200\cdot23}{75\cdot24}=\boxed{552\text{ days}}$. Any smaller positive lag than twelve hours cannot align the hour hand together with the other hands.
