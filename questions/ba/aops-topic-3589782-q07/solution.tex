Count the smaller numbers lexicographically. A smaller first digit gives $4\cdot5^4$ numbers. With first two digits $51$, a smaller third digit gives $3\cdot5^2$. With prefix $514$, a smaller fourth digit gives $2\cdot5$; with prefix $5143$, a smaller last digit gives $1$. Thus there are $2500+75+10+1=2586$ smaller numbers, and the requested position is $\boxed{2587}$.
