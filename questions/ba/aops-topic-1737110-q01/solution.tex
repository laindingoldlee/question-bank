The cubes below $1729$ are $1,8,27,64,125,216,343,512,729,1000,1331,1728$. Checking which pairs add up to $1729$: $1729-1728=1=1^3$ and $1729-1000=729=9^3$, and no other difference $1729-k^3$ (for $k=10,11,12$ or $k\le9$) is a cube apart from these. So
\[
1729=1^3+12^3=9^3+10^3,
\]
and $A+B+C+D=1+12+9+10=\boxed{32}$.
