Angle $A$ and its complement together make a right angle:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0);
draw(O--(3,0),linewidth(1));draw(O--(0,3),linewidth(1));draw(O--3*dir(18),linewidth(1));
real s=0.3; draw((s,0)--(s,s)--(0,s));
draw(arc(O,1.2,18,90),blue);draw(arc(O,2.2,0,18),red);
label("$A$",1.55*dir(54),blue);label("$90^\circ-A$",2.3*dir(9),E,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

\[
A=4(90^\circ-A)\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad5A=360^\circ\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad A=\boxed{72^\circ}.
\]
(Check: the complement is $18^\circ$, and $4\cdot18^\circ=72^\circ$.)
