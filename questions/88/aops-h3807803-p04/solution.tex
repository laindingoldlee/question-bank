With the usual coordinates, $O=(3,3)$ and $P=(2,0)$. The slope of $OP$ is $3$, so $PQ$ has equation $y=-\dfrac{1}{3}(x-2)$. Its second boundary intersection is $Q=\left(0,\dfrac{2}{3}\right)$. Hence
\[
[APQ]=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot2\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{3}}.
\]
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(6,0),C=(6,6),D=(0,6);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);label("$A$",A,(-1,-1));label("$B$",B,(1,-1));label("$C$",C,(1,1));label("$D$",D,(-1,1));pair O=(3,3),P=(2,0),Q=(0,2./3);draw(O--P,dashed);filldraw(A--P--Q--cycle,lightblue);dot(O);label("$O$",O,(1,0));label("$P$",P,(0,-1));label("$Q$",Q,(-1,0));
\end{asy}
