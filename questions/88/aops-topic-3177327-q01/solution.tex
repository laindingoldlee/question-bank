Multiply numerators and denominators: $\dfrac{2\cdot9}{3\cdot10}=\dfrac{18}{30}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{5}}$. Equivalently cancel the common factors before multiplying. Common denominators are needed for adding fractions, not multiplying them.
