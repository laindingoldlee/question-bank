Write $a=7m$ and $b=7n$ with $\gcd(m,n)=1$. Then $\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=7mn=70$, so $mn=10$ with $m$ and $n$ coprime:
\[
\{m,n\}=\{1,10\}\quad\text{or}\quad\{2,5\}.
\]
So $\{a,b\}=\{7,70\}$ or $\{14,35\}$ (check: $\gcd(14,35)=7$ and $\operatorname{lcm}(14,35)=70$), and
\[
a+b=\boxed{77\ \text{ or }\ 49}.
\]
