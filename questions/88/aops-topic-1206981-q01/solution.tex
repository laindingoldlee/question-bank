A cube of edge $r\sqrt3$ has volume $3\sqrt3\,r^3$, so six of them have volume $18\sqrt3\,r^3$. A cube of edge $r\sqrt[3]2$ has volume $2r^3$. We need $2n\ge18\sqrt3$, i.e. $n\ge9\sqrt3\approx15.59$. The least such $n$ is $\boxed{16}$. (Equality is impossible, since $9\sqrt3$ is irrational.)
