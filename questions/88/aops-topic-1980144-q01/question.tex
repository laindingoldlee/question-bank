Twenty-five chips, each marked with a different integer from $1$ through $25$, are placed in a jar. A student draws a chip, tells everyone the number, and returns the chip to the jar. Nine more students do the same. What is the probability that at least two of the $10$ students draw the same chip? Express your answer as a decimal to the nearest hundredth.
