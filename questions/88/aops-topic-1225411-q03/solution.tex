$(a-b)(a+b)=2016$ with $a-b$ and $a+b$ of the same parity; since $2016$ is even, both are even. To maximise $a+b$ take $a-b=2$, $a+b=1008$ ($a=505$, $b=503$). The maximum sum is $\boxed{1008}$.
