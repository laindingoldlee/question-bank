Place the right-angle vertex at the origin and the legs on the axes. Up to interchanging axes, the centroid is $G=(3,4)$. Since a centroid averages vertex coordinates, the other vertices are $(9,0)$ and $(0,12)$. The hypotenuse is $4x+3y=36$. Its distance from $G$ is
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{|4\cdot3+3\cdot4-36|}{\sqrt{4^2+3^2}}=\dfrac{12}{5}}.
\]

\end{asy}
