Seven congruent circles are arranged so that six of them surround the seventh: each of the six outer circles is tangent to the middle circle and to its two neighbouring outer circles. A large circle is internally tangent to each of the six outer circles. The six small regions enclosed between the middle circle and two neighbouring outer circles are shaded purple, and their total area is $6\sqrt3-3\pi$. What is the area of the large circle?
