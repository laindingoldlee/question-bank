A shrine has $3$ temples and a magic pond. Each time you swim in the pond, the number of flowers you are carrying triples. You swim in the pond before each temple: pond, temple, pond, temple, pond, temple. At each temple you place the same number of flowers, and after the third temple you have no flowers left. What is the least number of flowers you could have started with?
