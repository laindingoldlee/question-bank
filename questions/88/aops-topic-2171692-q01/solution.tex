Let $n$ be the starting number and $x$ the number placed at each temple. The numbers of flowers after each temple are
\[3n-x,\qquad3(3n-x)-x=9n-4x,\qquad 3(9n-4x)-x=27n-13x .\]
So $27n=13x$. Since $\gcd(27,13)=1$, $13$ divides $n$, so $n\ge13$. With $n=13$ and $x=27$:
\[13\to39\to12\to36\to9\to27\to0 .\]
The least possible starting number is $\boxed{13}$.
