For $P=(x,y)$, the condition $PA=2PB$ with $A=(a,0)$, $B=(-a,0)$ is
\[(x-a)^2+y^2=4\left[(x+a)^2+y^2\right]\iff3x^2+10ax+3a^2+3y^2=0\iff\left(x+\dfrac{5a}{3}\right)^2+y^2=\left(\dfrac{4a}{3}\right)^2.\]
So the path is the circle with centre $\left(-\dfrac{5a}3,0\right)$ and radius $\dfrac{4a}3$ (an Apollonius circle). For $a=3$:
\[\boxed{(x+5)^2+y^2=16},\]
the circle with centre $(-5,0)$ and radius $4$.
