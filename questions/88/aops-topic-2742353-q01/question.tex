Find an equation of the path of a point that moves so that its distance from $(3,0)$ is always twice its distance from $(-3,0)$. More generally, find the path when $(3,0)$ and $(-3,0)$ are replaced by $(a,0)$ and $(-a,0)$, with $a>0$.
