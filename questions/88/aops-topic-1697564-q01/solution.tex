There are $6^4=1296$ equally likely outcomes. Choose the two numbers that appear: $\dbinom62=15$ ways. Each of the four dice shows one of the two chosen numbers, $2^4=16$ ways, but we must exclude the $2$ outcomes in which all four dice show the same number. So there are $15\cdot14=210$ favourable outcomes, and the probability is
\[
\dfrac{210}{1296}=\boxed{\dfrac{35}{216}}.
\]
