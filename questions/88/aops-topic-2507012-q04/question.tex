A positive integer $N$ leaves remainder $1$ when divided by $2$, remainder $4$ when divided by $5$, remainder $9$ when divided by $10$, and in general remainder $d-1$ when divided by $d$, for every positive divisor $d$ of $100$. What is the smallest possible value of $N$?
