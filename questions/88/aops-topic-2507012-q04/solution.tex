The condition says $N\equiv-1\pmod d$, i.e. $d\mid N+1$, for every divisor $d$ of $100$ (for $d=1$ the remainder $0$ is automatic). This holds exactly when $100\mid N+1$. The smallest positive such $N$ is $\boxed{99}$.
