Use Burnside's lemma with the $16$ symmetries of the regular octagon.
\begin{itemize}
\item Rotations by $0,1,\dots,7$ sections fix $2^{\gcd(k,8)}$ colourings: $256+2+4+2+16+2+4+2=288$.
\item The $4$ reflections through the midpoints of two opposite sections fix $2$ sections and swap $3$ pairs: $2^5=32$ each. The $4$ reflections through boundaries swap $4$ pairs: $2^4=16$ each. Total $128+64=192$.
\end{itemize}
The number of distinct colourings is $\dfrac{288+192}{16}=\boxed{30}$. (Casework by the number $j$ of sections of the first colour gives $1,1,4,5,8,5,4,1,1$ for $j=0,\dots,8$, again $30$.)
