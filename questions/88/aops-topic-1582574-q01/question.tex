Extend the Fibonacci numbers to negative indices by the same rule $F_{n+1}=F_n+F_{n-1}$, starting from $F_0=0$, $F_1=1$ (so $F_{-1}=1$, $F_{-2}=-1$, $F_{-3}=2,\dots$). For a sequence $(a_n)_{n\ge0}$, its sequence of first-order differences is $(a_{n+1}-a_n)_{n\ge0}$.

Prove that for every $k\ge1$, the sequence of $k$-th order differences of $F_0,F_1,F_2,\dots$ is $F_{-k},F_{1-k},F_{2-k},\dots$, the Fibonacci sequence shifted back by $k$ places. (For example, the first differences of $0,1,1,2,3,5,\dots$ are $1,0,1,1,2,3,\dots$ and the second differences are $-1,1,0,1,1,2,\dots$.)
