The sum is $\dfrac{2023\cdot2024}{2}=2023\cdot1012=2^2\cdot7\cdot11\cdot17^2\cdot23 $. A divisor independently chooses each prime exponent, giving $(2+1)(1+1)^3(2+1)=\boxed{72}$ divisors.
