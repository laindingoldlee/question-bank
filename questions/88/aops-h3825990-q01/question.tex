For each positive integer $n$, compare the principal angles represented by $n$ radians and $n$ degrees, both in $[0,360^\circ)$. Find the limiting proportion for which the radian interpretation is larger. You may use the following equidistribution fact: for irrational $\alpha$ and real $\beta$, the fractional parts of $k\alpha+\beta$ are uniformly distributed in $[0,1)$.
