The point $(a,b,c)$ is uniformly distributed in the cube $(-20,20)^3$, of volume $40^3=64000$, so the probability is the volume of the region described divided by $64000$.

The condition $a^2+b^2+c^2\le1$ describes the ball of radius $1$ centred at the origin, which lies inside the cube and has volume $\dfrac{4}{3}\pi$. The plane $a=b$ and the plane $c=0$ pass through the centre and cut the ball into four pieces according to whether $a\ge b$ or $a<b$ and whether $c>0$ or $c\le0$. The reflections $(a,b,c)\mapsto(b,a,c)$ and $(a,b,c)\mapsto(a,b,-c)$ map the ball to itself and carry these four pieces onto one another (up to boundary points, which have no volume), so each piece has volume $\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot\dfrac{4}{3}\pi=\dfrac{\pi}{3}$. The picture shows the cross-section $c=0$ with the half $a\ge b$ shaded.

Hence the probability is
\[\dfrac{\pi\div3}{64000}=\boxed{\dfrac{\pi}{192000}}\approx1.64\times10^{-5}.\]
