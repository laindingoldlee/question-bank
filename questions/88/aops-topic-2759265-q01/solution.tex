(a) This is a geometric sequence with first term $64$ and ratio $\dfrac12$: $a_n=64\left(\dfrac12\right)^{n-1}=\boxed{2^{7-n}}$.

(b) The number of matches must be a positive integer, so $n=1,2,\dots,7$, giving $64,32,16,8,4,2,1$ (round $7$ is the final).
