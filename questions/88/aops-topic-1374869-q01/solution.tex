The numerical coefficient of a term is the number that multiplies the variable part. Write the expression as
\[
7x+y-z=7\cdot x+1\cdot y+(-1)\cdot z .
\]
So the terms $7x$, $y$ and $-z$ have numerical coefficients $\boxed{7,\ 1\text{ and }-1}$. The letters $y$ and $z$ are variables, not coefficients; a term written without a number has coefficient $1$ (or $-1$ when it has a minus sign).
