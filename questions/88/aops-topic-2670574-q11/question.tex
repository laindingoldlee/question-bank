Ten prisoners stand in single file facing forward, each seeing the hats of all prisoners in front of him. Each hat has one of $100$ colours, and no prisoner can see his own. Starting from the back, each prisoner in turn says aloud a guess for his own colour; everyone hears every guess and whether it was right. Prove that the prisoners can agree on a strategy beforehand which guarantees that at most one of them guesses wrongly.
