Substituting $x=1$ gives $a+5=4$, so $a=-1$. The equation becomes $-x^2+5x-4=0$, i.e. $x^2-5x+4=(x-1)(x-4)=0$. The other solution is $\boxed{4}$. (By Vieta, the product of the roots is $4$.)
