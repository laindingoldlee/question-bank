Two circles $\omega_1$ and $\omega_2$ are defined such that $\omega_1$ is smaller than $\omega_2$. Let $X$ and $Y$ be the intersections of the two circles. Let $P$ be the intersection of their common external tangents and let $W$ be the intersection of $PX$ and $\omega_1$. The common external tangent closer to $X$ touches $\omega_1$ and $\omega_2$ at $A$ and $B$ respectively, so that $PA=75$ and $AB=33$. Given that the quantity $WX$ can be expressed as $\dfrac{m}{n}$ where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers, find $m+n$.
