From February 7, 2018 to February 7, 2081 is $63$ years. Each year shifts the weekday by $365\equiv1\pmod7$ day, plus one more for each February 29 in between. The leap days in this period are those of $2020,2024,\dots,2080$, that is $\dfrac{2080-2020}{4}+1=16$ of them ($2000$ and $2100$ play no role). The total number of days is
\[
63\cdot365+16=23011=7\cdot3287+2,
\]
so the weekday moves $2$ days forward: Wednesday $\to$ Friday. The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(e)}\ \text{Friday}}$.
