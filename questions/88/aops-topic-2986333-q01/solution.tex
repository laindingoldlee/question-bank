The ten sums add up to $(1+2+\cdots+10)+(1+2+\cdots+10)=110$, which ends in $0$.

Suppose the ten sums all ended in different digits. Then their last digits would be $0,1,\dots,9$ in some order, so the total would end in the same digit as $0+1+\cdots+9=45$, namely $5$. This contradicts $110$ ending in $0$. Hence two sums share a last digit. $\blacksquare$
