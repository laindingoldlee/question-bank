Coefficient comparison gives $c=-a$, $b+d=a^2$, $a(d-b)=0$, and $bd=4$. If $a=0$, then $d=-b$ and $bd=-b^2=4$, impossible. Thus $d=b$. Now $b^2=4$ and $2b=a^2$ force $b=d=2$ and $a=\pm2,c=\mp2$. Both work by Sophie Germain’s factorization. The two ordered quadruples are $\boxed{(2,2,-2,2),(-2,2,2,2)}$.
