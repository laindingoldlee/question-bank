For each positive integer $t$, take $z=2t^2+1$, $x=tz$, and $y=-tz$. The cubes of $x,y$ cancel, while $x^2+y^2+z^2=(2t^2+1)z^2=z^3$. Therefore each triple works, and the distinct increasing $z$ values give infinitely many triples.
