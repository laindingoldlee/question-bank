Arnold and Billy are reading the same book. Arnold reads twice as many pages per day as Billy does, but Billy starts two days earlier. They both finish $\dfrac23$ of the book at the same moment, and when Arnold finishes the book, Billy is $10$ pages behind him. How many pages does Billy read each day?
