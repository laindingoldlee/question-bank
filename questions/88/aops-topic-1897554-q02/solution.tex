The equation says $n^2=15$. But $3^2=9<15<16=4^2$, and squares of natural numbers increase with $n$, so $n^2$ jumps from $9$ to $16$ and never equals $15$. Hence there is $\boxed{\text{no such }n}$ (indeed $n=\sqrt{15}$ is not even rational).
