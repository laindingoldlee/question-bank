It suffices to consider the first nine houses. Up to interchanging colours, the only eight-house strings with no monochromatic three-term arithmetic progression are RRBBRRBB, RBRBBRBR and RBBRRBBR. This short list is obtained by extending a string one position at a time and rejecting a colour whenever it completes a progression; checking all step sizes one, two and three verifies the list. For these three strings, a red ninth house creates respectively progressions $(1,5,9),(3,6,9),(1,5,9)$, while a blue ninth creates $(7,8,9),(5,7,9),(3,6,9)$. Every extension fails. Therefore nine, and hence eleven, houses contain the required triple.
