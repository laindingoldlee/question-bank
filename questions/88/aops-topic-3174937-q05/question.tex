Particles occupy a planar annulus with inner radius $53000$ and outer radius $63000$ kilometers. Their density per unit area is proportional to the reciprocal of the radius. Choose two particles independently according to this density. Find the probability that their orbital radii differ by less than $4000$ kilometers.
