The intersections are $-\dfrac{\pi}{2},0,\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. On $[0,\dfrac{\pi}{2}]$, concavity of sine puts it above its chord $\dfrac{2x}{\pi}$; outside $[-\dfrac{\pi}{2},\dfrac{\pi}{2}]$ the line has magnitude greater than one, preventing other intersections. Symmetry makes the two lobe areas equal. Their total is $2 \int_0^{\dfrac{\pi}{2}} (\sin x-\dfrac{2x}{\pi}) \, d x = 2 - \dfrac{\pi}{2} \approx 0.429204$, which lies in $\boxed{(0.42,0.43)}$.
