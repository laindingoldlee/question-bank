We need $7^{2006}\bmod 100$. Since $7^2=49$ and $7^4=2401\equiv1\pmod{100}$, and $2006=4\cdot501+2$,
\[
7^{2006}=(7^4)^{501}\cdot7^2\equiv1\cdot49=49\pmod{100}.
\]
So the last two digits are $49$: the tens digit is $\boxed{4}$ and the units digit is $\boxed{9}$.
