Let the other leg be $b$. By the Pythagorean theorem $5^2+b^2=13^2$, so $b^2=169-25=144$ and $b=12$ cm.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair ft(pair P,pair U,pair V){return U+dot(P-U,V-U)/dot(V-U,V-U)*(V-U);}
path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s=0.2){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
pair A=(0,0),B=(12,0),C=(0,5);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(ra(A,B-A,C-A,0.8));
label("$5$",(A+C)/2,W);label("$13$",(B+C)/2,NE);label("$12$",(A+B)/2,S,blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

In a right triangle the two legs are a base and the corresponding height, so the area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot5\cdot12=\boxed{30}$ square centimetres.
