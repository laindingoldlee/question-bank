Let $u=13(157-77x)$ and $v=10(201-100x)$. The left side is $u^2+v^2$. On the right,
\[77x-157=-(157-77x),\qquad 1000x-2010=-10(201-100x),\]
so the right side is
\[26\cdot10\,(157-77x)(201-100x)=2\cdot13(157-77x)\cdot10(201-100x)=2uv .\]
The equation becomes $u^2+v^2=2uv$, that is, $(u-v)^2=0$, so $u=v$:
\[13(157-77x)=10(201-100x)\iff2041-1001x=2010-1000x\iff x=\boxed{31}.\]
