Captain Greenbeard's treasure chest holds $100$ grams of a mix of gold, silver and bronze coins. Gold, silver and bronze coins weigh $6$ grams, $2$ grams and $5$ grams each, and are worth $30$, $8$ and $15$ droubles each. Greenbeard has at least one coin of each kind. What is the greatest possible total value, in droubles, of the coins in the chest?
