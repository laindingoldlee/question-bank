The odd single-digit primes are $3$, $5$ and $7$, so we count the integers from $1$ to $2019$ divisible by none of $3$, $5$, $7$. The number of multiples of $d$ up to $2019$ is $\left\lfloor\dfrac{2019}{d}\right\rfloor$:
\[\left\lfloor\dfrac{2019}{3}\right\rfloor=673,\quad \left\lfloor\dfrac{2019}{5}\right\rfloor=403,\quad \left\lfloor\dfrac{2019}{7}\right\rfloor=288,\]
\[\left\lfloor\dfrac{2019}{15}\right\rfloor=134,\quad \left\lfloor\dfrac{2019}{21}\right\rfloor=96,\quad \left\lfloor\dfrac{2019}{35}\right\rfloor=57,\quad \left\lfloor\dfrac{2019}{105}\right\rfloor=19 .\]
By inclusion-exclusion, the number divisible by at least one of $3$, $5$, $7$ is
\[673+403+288-134-96-57+19=1096,\]
so the number divisible by none of them is $2019-1096=\boxed{923}$.
