Consider the Pythagorean triples $(x,y,z)$ of positive integers, $x^2+y^2=z^2$ with $x<y<z$, in which the longer leg is one less than the hypotenuse ($z=y+1$). Ordered by the shorter leg $x$, what is the $79^{\text{th}}$ such triple?
