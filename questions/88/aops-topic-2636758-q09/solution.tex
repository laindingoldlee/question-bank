With $z=y+1$, $x^2=z^2-y^2=2y+1$, so $x$ is odd, $y=\dfrac{x^2-1}{2}$ and $z=\dfrac{x^2+1}{2}$. The condition $x<y$ means $x^2-2x-1>0$, i.e. $x\ge3$. Conversely every odd $x\ge3$ gives such a triple. So the triples are indexed by $x=3,5,7,\dots$, and the $79^{\text{th}}$ has $x=2\cdot79+1=159$, $y=\dfrac{159^2-1}{2}=12640$, $z=12641$:
\[\boxed{(159,\ 12640,\ 12641)}.\]
