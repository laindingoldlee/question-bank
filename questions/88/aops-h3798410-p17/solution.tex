Apply Burnside's lemma to the eight symmetries of the square. The identity fixes all $\binom92=36$ pairs. A quarter-turn or three-quarter-turn fixes none; a half-turn fixes the four opposite-cell pairs. Each of the four reflections fixes three pairs of individually fixed cells (choosing two of its three axis cells) and three swapped pairs, for six total. The orbit count is
\[\dfrac{36+0+4+0+4\cdot6}{8}=\boxed8.\]
The diagram displays one representative of each equivalence class.
