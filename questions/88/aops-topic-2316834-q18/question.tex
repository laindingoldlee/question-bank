A fountain shoots a water arc modelled by $y=-0.006x^2+1.2x+10$, where $x$ is the horizontal distance (in feet) from the north shore of a river and $y$ is the height (in feet) above the river. Does the water arc reach a height of $50$ feet? If so, how far from the north shore (to the nearest foot) is it $50$ feet above the water?
