Completing the square, $y=-0.006(x-100)^2+70$, so the highest point is $70$ feet, at $x=100$: \textbf{yes}, the arc reaches $50$ feet.

Setting $y=50$: $0.006(x-100)^2=20$, so $(x-100)^2=\dfrac{20}{0.006}=\dfrac{10000}{3}$ and
\[x=100\pm\dfrac{100}{\sqrt3}\approx100\pm57.74.\]
So the arc is $50$ feet above the water about $\boxed{42\text{ feet}}$ and about $\boxed{158\text{ feet}}$ from the north shore.
