Choose $31$ elements from $41$ ordered elements. If the $21$st selected element is in position $21+r$, choose its $20$ predecessors in $\binom{20+r}{20}$ ways and its $10$ successors in $\binom{20-r}{10}$ ways. The allowable $r$ are $0$ through $10$. This partitions all choices, proving that the answer is $\boxed{\binom{41}{31}}=\boxed{\binom{41}{10}}$. Alternatively the same convolution is the coefficient of $x^{10}$ in $(1-x)^{-21}(1-x)^{-11}=(1-x)^{-32}$.
