There are $\dbinom53=10$ sets of $3$ colours. Those containing pink and orange: $3$ (any third colour); those containing purple and yellow: $3$. No set of $3$ contains both pairs. So $10-3-3=\boxed{4}$ sets are allowed: red with one of pink/orange and one of purple/yellow.
