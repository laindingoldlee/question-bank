Let the outer value be $ k $. Then $\sqrt{n-25}=k^2-n $ is a nonnegative integer $ t $, so $ n=t^2+25 $ and $ k^2=t^2+t+25 $. Hence $(2k-2t-1)(2k+2t+1)=99 $. Both factors are positive odd integers. Their pairs listed with the smaller factor first are $(1,99),(3,33),(9,11)$, yielding $(k,t)=(25,24),(9,7),(5,0)$. Thus $ n=601,74,25 $, all valid. Their sum is $\boxed{700}$.
