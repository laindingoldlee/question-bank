Bob places his pencil at the centre of a rectangular sheet of paper that measures $22$ inches from west to east and $20$ inches from south to north. He draws a $0.5$-inch segment to the north. Then, without lifting his pencil, he draws a $1.5$-inch segment to the east, and so on: each time he finishes a segment, he turns $90^\circ$ clockwise and makes the next segment $1$ inch longer than the previous one. He stops when the pencil reaches the edge of the paper. What is the total length of the line he has drawn on the paper?
