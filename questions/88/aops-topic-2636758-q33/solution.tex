The centroid is the average of the vertices: $A=\left(\dfrac{0+7+11}{3},\dfrac{0+24+6}{3}\right)=(6,10)$. So $AP=\sqrt{36+100}=\sqrt{136}=\boxed{2\sqrt{34}}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair P=(0,0),Q=(7,24),R=(11,6),A=(6,10);
draw(P--Q--R--cycle,linewidth(0.8));draw(P--(Q+R)/2,dashed);dot(A);
label("$P$",P,SW);label("$Q(7,24)$",Q,N,fontsize(8pt));label("$R(11,6)$",R,E,fontsize(8pt));label("$A$",A,SE);
\end{asy}
