The condition makes $f$ a permutation consisting only of fixed points and disjoint transpositions. If it has $k$ transpositions, choose and pair their $2k$ elements in $\dfrac{8!}{2^k k!(8-2k)!}$ ways. Summing for $k=0,1,2,3,4$ gives $1+28+210+420+105=\boxed{764}$.
