The denominators are $2\cdot4,\ 4\cdot6,\ 6\cdot8,\dots,98\cdot100$, i.e. $(2k)(2k+2)=4k(k+1)$ for $k=1,\dots,49$. So the sum is
\[\dfrac{1}{4}\sum_{k=1}^{49}\left(\dfrac{1}{k}-\dfrac{1}{k+1}\right)=\dfrac{1}{4}\left(1-\dfrac{1}{50}\right)=\boxed{\dfrac{49}{200}}.\]
