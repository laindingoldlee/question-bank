$\angle BAD=180^\circ-150^\circ=30^\circ$, so $BE=6\sin30^\circ=3$ and $BF=\dfrac32$. Since $BC\parallel AD$, $BE\perp BC$.

label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,N);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,SE);label("$E$",E,S);label("$F$",F,W);
\end{asy}

In right triangle $FBC$: $CF=\sqrt{\left(\dfrac32\right)^2+6^2}=\sqrt{\dfrac{153}4}=\dfrac{3\sqrt{17}}2$, so $a+b+c=3+17+2=\boxed{22}$.
