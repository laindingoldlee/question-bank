Bob pays for a pack of pencils costing $1.30$ dollars using exactly $14$ coins, all nickels, dimes and quarters. A pack of pens costs $3.50$ dollars, which is exactly the value of twice as many dimes, three times as many nickels and three times as many quarters as Bob used. How many dimes did Bob use?
