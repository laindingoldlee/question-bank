Let Bob use $n$ nickels, $d$ dimes and $q$ quarters. Working in cents:
\[n+d+q=14,\qquad5n+10d+25q=130,\qquad5(3n)+10(2d)+25(3q)=350.\]
Dividing by $5$, the last two are $n+2d+5q=26$ and $3n+4d+15q=70$. Subtracting the first equation from the second gives $d+4q=12$; subtracting $3$ times the first from the third gives $d+12q=28$. Hence $8q=16$, $q=2$, $d=4$ and $n=8$. Check: $40+40+50=130$ cents, and $3\cdot8$ nickels, $2\cdot4$ dimes and $3\cdot2$ quarters make $120+80+150=350$ cents. Bob used $\boxed{4}$ dimes.
