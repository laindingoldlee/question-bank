A score of 29 would be one below the maximum 30. Any change from a correct answer loses either 2 or 3 points, so a loss of 1 is impossible. The other scores occur as $17=3(5)+2$, $21=3(7)$, $13=3(4)+1$, and $23=3(7)+2$, using at most ten questions in each case. Thus $\boxed{29}$, choice C.
