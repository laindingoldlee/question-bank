By Legendre, $v_2(n!)=n-s_2(n)$, where $s_2$ is the binary digit sum. We need $n-s_2(n)\ge n-1$, i.e. $s_2(n)=1$, so $n$ is a power of $2$: $1,2,4,\dots,1024$. The sum is $2^{11}-1=\boxed{2047}$.
