A lattice point is a point $(x,y)$ whose coordinates are both integers. A triangle has vertices $(1,1)$, $(9,1)$ and $(9,n)$, where $n>1$ is an integer. For what value of $n$ are there exactly $560$ lattice points strictly inside the triangle?
