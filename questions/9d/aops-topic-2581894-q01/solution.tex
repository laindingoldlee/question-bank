List the numbers of cans in increasing order, $c_1\le c_2\le\cdots\le c_{100}$. The median is $\dfrac{c_{50}+c_{51}}2$.

\emph{Upper bound.} Since $c_1,\dots,c_{49}\ge1$, we have $c_{50}+c_{51}+\cdots+c_{100}\le252-49=203$. The fifty numbers $c_{51},\dots,c_{100}$ are each at least $c_{51}$, so $50c_{51}\le203-c_{50}\le202$, and $c_{51}\le4$.
\begin{itemize}
\item If $c_{51}=4$, then $c_{51}+\cdots+c_{100}\ge200$, so $c_{50}\le203-200=3$, and the median is at most $\dfrac{3+4}2=3.5$.
\item If $c_{51}\le3$, then $c_{50}\le c_{51}\le3$, and the median is at most $3$.
\end{itemize}

\emph{Construction.} $49$ customers buy $1$ can, one buys $3$, and $50$ buy $4$: the total is $49+3+200=252$, and the median is $\dfrac{3+4}2=3.5$.

The maximum median is $\boxed{3.5}$.
