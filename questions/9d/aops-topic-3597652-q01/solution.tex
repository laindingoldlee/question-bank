Squaring and matching the coefficients of $\sqrt6,\sqrt{10},\sqrt{15}$ gives $ab=52$, $ac=234$, $bc=72$. Hence $a^2=\dfrac{52\cdot234}{72}=169$, so $a=13$, $b=4$, $c=18$. Check the rational part: $2(13)^2+3(4)^2+5(18)^2=338+48+1620=2006$. Positivity ensures the correct square root. Thus $\boxed{abc=936}$.
