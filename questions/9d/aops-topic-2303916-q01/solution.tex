Write $\sqrt3=\dfrac{3}{\sqrt3}$ (since $\sqrt3\cdot\sqrt3=3$). Then
\[\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}+\sqrt3=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}+\dfrac{3}{\sqrt3}=\dfrac{4}{\sqrt3}=\dfrac{4\sqrt3}{3}.\]
The answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{4\sqrt3}{3}}$.
