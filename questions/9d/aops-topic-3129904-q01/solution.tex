Adding exponents gives $P_N=2^{\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}(1-k)}=2^{N-\dfrac{N(N-1)}{2}}$. The exponent tends to $-\infty$, so $\boxed{\lim P_N=0}$. Splitting off the initial factor merely rewrites the product as 2 times its remaining tail; writing the latter as one half of the original gives an identity rather than a new equation. It does not compute the limit or justify convergence.
