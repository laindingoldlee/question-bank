For a random problem, let $F$, $A$ and $W$ be the events that FTWA, Adihaya's Lemma and WLT can be used. Since $W\subseteq A$, ``all three'' is the event $F\cap W$. By independence, $P(F\cap W)=0.5\cdot0.3=0.15$. By linearity of expectation, the expected number of such problems is $40\cdot0.15=\boxed{6}$.

(Without the independence assumption the answer is not determined: $P(F\cap W)$ could be anything from $0$ to $0.3$.)
