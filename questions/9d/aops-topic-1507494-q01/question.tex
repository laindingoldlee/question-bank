Let $x_1,x_2$ be the roots of $3x^2+5x-6=0$. Without solving the equation, find a quadratic equation with integer coefficients whose roots are
\[
y_1=x_1+\dfrac{1}{x_2}\qquad\text{and}\qquad y_2=x_2+\dfrac{1}{x_1}.
\]
