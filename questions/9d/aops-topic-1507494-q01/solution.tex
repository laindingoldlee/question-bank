By Vieta's formulas, $x_1+x_2=-\dfrac{5}{3}$ and $x_1x_2=-2$ (so neither root is $0$). Write $y_1=\dfrac{x_1x_2+1}{x_2}$ and $y_2=\dfrac{x_1x_2+1}{x_1}$. Then
\[
y_1+y_2=(x_1+x_2)+\dfrac{x_1+x_2}{x_1x_2}=-\dfrac{5}{3}+\dfrac{-\dfrac{5}{3}}{-2}=-\dfrac{5}{3}+\dfrac{5}{6}=-\dfrac{5}{6},
\]
\[
y_1y_2=\dfrac{(x_1x_2+1)^2}{x_1x_2}=\dfrac{(-1)^2}{-2}=-\dfrac{1}{2} .
\]
So $y_1,y_2$ are the roots of $y^2+\dfrac{5}{6}y-\dfrac{1}{2}=0$, i.e.\ of
\[
\boxed{6y^2+5y-3=0}.
\]
