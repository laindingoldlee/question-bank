For any $n$,
\[
n(n+1)(n+2)(n+3)+1=(n^2+3n)(n^2+3n+2)+1=(n^2+3n)^2+2(n^2+3n)+1=(n^2+3n+1)^2 .
\]
With $n=2014$, the answer is $n^2+3n+1=n(n+3)+1=2014\cdot2017+1=4062238+1=\boxed{4062239}$.
