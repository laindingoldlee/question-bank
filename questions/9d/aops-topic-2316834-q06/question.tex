A rugby player kicks a ball from $1$ foot above the ground with an initial vertical velocity of $60$ feet per second, so that its height after $t$ seconds is $h=-16t^2+60t+1$ feet. When is the ball at a height of $40$ feet? Give exact values and approximations to the nearest hundredth of a second.
