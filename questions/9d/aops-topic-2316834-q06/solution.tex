We need $-16t^2+60t+1=40$, that is, $16t^2-60t+39=0$. By the quadratic formula,
\[t=\dfrac{60\pm\sqrt{3600-4\cdot16\cdot39}}{32}=\dfrac{60\pm\sqrt{1104}}{32}=\dfrac{60\pm4\sqrt{69}}{32}=\dfrac{15\pm\sqrt{69}}{8}.\]
Since $\sqrt{69}\approx8.307$, the ball is at $40$ feet at
\[t=\boxed{\dfrac{15-\sqrt{69}}{8}\approx0.84\text{ s}}\ \text{(on the way up) and }\ t=\boxed{\dfrac{15+\sqrt{69}}{8}\approx2.91\text{ s}}\ \text{(on the way down)}.\]
