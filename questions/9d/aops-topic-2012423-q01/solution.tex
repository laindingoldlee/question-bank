\textbf{(a)} Each of the $4$ balls independently chooses one of $3$ boxes: $3^4=\boxed{81}$ ways.

\textbf{(b)} Now only the partition of the set of four balls into at most three nonempty groups matters (the groups are the contents of the nonempty boxes). Count by the shape of the group sizes:
\begin{itemize}
\item $4$: one group, $1$ way;
\item $3+1$: choose the single ball, $4$ ways;
\item $2+2$: pair ball $1$ with one of the other three, $3$ ways;
\item $2+1+1$: choose the pair, $\dbinom42=6$ ways.
\end{itemize}
(The shape $1+1+1+1$ would need four boxes.) Total: $1+4+3+6=\boxed{14}$.

\textbf{(c)} We need the number of nonnegative integer solutions of $x_1+x_2+x_3=4$, where $x_i$ is the number of balls in box $i$. By stars and bars this is $\dbinom{4+2}{2}=\boxed{15}$.

\textbf{(d)} The green balls and the red balls are placed independently. The two identical green balls can be placed in $\dbinom{2+2}{2}=6$ ways (solutions of $g_1+g_2+g_3=2$), and likewise the red balls in $6$ ways. Total: $6\cdot6=\boxed{36}$.
