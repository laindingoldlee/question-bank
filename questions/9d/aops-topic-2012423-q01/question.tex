In how many ways can $4$ balls be put into $3$ boxes (boxes may be left empty) if
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] the balls are distinguishable and the boxes are distinguishable;
\item[(b)] the balls are distinguishable but the boxes are not;
\item[(c)] the balls are indistinguishable but the boxes are distinguishable;
\item[(d)] $2$ of the balls are indistinguishably green, the other $2$ are indistinguishably red, and the boxes are distinguishable?
\end{enumerate}
