The digit counts are $x=1+\lfloor2019\log_{10}2\rfloor$ and $y=1+\lfloor2019\log_{10}5\rfloor$. The two numbers inside the floors sum to $2019$ and neither is an integer, since no positive power of $2$ or $5$ is a power of ten. Their fractional parts therefore sum to one, and their floors sum to $2018$. Hence $\boxed{x+y=2020}$.
