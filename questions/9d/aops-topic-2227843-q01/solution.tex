The common side length $\ell$ divides both areas, so the largest possible value is $\gcd(345678,123456)$ (and it is achieved: the other sides are then the two quotients, which are different). By the Euclidean algorithm,
\begin{align*}
345678&=2\cdot123456+98766,\\
123456&=1\cdot98766+24690,\\
98766&=4\cdot24690+6,\\
24690&=4115\cdot6+0 .
\end{align*}
So $\gcd=\boxed{6}$, with rectangles $6\times57613$ and $6\times20576$.
