The common divisors are exactly the divisors of $\gcd(60,24)=12$. Since $120=2^3\cdot3\cdot5$ has $16$ divisors and $12=2^2\cdot3$ has $6$, the probability is $\dfrac{6}{16}=\dfrac{3}{8}$. Thus $\boxed{m+n=11}$.
