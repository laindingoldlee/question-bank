For radius $r>0$ and height $h>0$, $S=2\pi r^2+2\pi rh$, so $V=\pi r^2h=\dfrac{Sr}{2}-\pi r^3$. Its derivative is $\dfrac{S}{2}-3\pi r^2$, positive before and negative after $r=\sqrt{\dfrac{S}{6\pi}}$. At that radius $h=2r$ and $\boxed{V_{\max}=\sqrt{\dfrac{S^3}{54\pi}}}$.
