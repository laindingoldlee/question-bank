Each month every flower adds $3$ new ones, so the number of flowers is multiplied by $4$ each month:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{cccccccc}
Aug & Sep & Oct & Nov & Dec & Jan & Feb & Mar\\\hline
$1$ & $4$ & $16$ & $64$ & $256$ & $1024$ & $4096$ & $16384$
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The first month with more than $10000$ flowers is $\boxed{\text{March}}$ (of the following year).
