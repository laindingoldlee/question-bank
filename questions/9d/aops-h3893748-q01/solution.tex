The domain is $x\ge\dfrac{2}{3}$. For $\dfrac{2}{3}\le x<\dfrac{5}{2}$, the left side is $(7-2x)\dfrac{5-2x}{\sqrt{3x-2}+\sqrt{x+3}}$, a strictly decreasing positive function: both numerator factors decrease and the denominator increases. It equals $5$ at $x=1$, so this interval has exactly that solution. On $[\dfrac{5}{2},\dfrac{7}{2}]$ the left side is nonpositive. For $x>\dfrac{7}{2}$, both factors are positive and strictly increasing. Indeed the radical difference has positive derivative, since $\dfrac{3}{\sqrt{3x-2}}>\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x+3}}$. At $x=6$ the product is $5(4-3)=5$. Thus the full solution set is $\boxed{\{1,6\}}$.
