In the prime factorization of $N$, the exponent of $2$ must be odd and divisible by $3$, so its least value is $3$. The exponent of $3$ must be even and congruent to $1$ modulo $3$, so its least value is $4$. Other prime exponents can be zero. Thus $N=2^3\cdot3^4=\boxed{648}$. Indeed $648=2\cdot18^2=3\cdot6^3$.
