\textbf{No.} Group the terms: $\dfrac13+\dfrac14>\dfrac12$, $\dfrac15+\cdots+\dfrac18>4\cdot\dfrac18=\dfrac12$, and in general the $2^k$ terms from $\dfrac1{2^k+1}$ to $\dfrac1{2^{k+1}}$ sum to more than $\dfrac12$. So the partial sums exceed any bound: the series $\boxed{\text{diverges}}$.
