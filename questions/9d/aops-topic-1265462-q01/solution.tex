Let $x=0.\overline{234}_6$. Multiplying by $6^3$ shifts the point three places: $6^3x=234.\overline{234}_6$, so $(6^3-1)x=234_6$. Now $234_6=2\cdot36+3\cdot6+4=94$ and $6^3-1=215$, so
\[x=\dfrac{94}{215},\]
which is in lowest terms since $94=2\cdot47$ and $215=5\cdot43$. The answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{94}{215}}$.

In general, a base-$n$ repeating block $\overline{d_1d_2\cdots d_k}_n$ right after the point equals $\dfrac{(d_1d_2\cdots d_k)_n}{n^k-1}$.
