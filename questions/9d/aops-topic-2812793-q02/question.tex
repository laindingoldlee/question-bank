It takes Franz $45$ minutes to walk between home and school. One morning she walked halfway to school, remembered her calculator, ran home, spent $5$ minutes finding it, and ran all the way to school. She runs twice as fast as she walks. How many minutes longer than usual did the trip take? Express your answer as a mixed number.
