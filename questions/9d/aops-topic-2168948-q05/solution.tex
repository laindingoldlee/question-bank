Suppose there are $N$ topics, of which a fraction $p$ are spam. The administrators delete $\dfrac{pN}{A}$ spam topics, leaving $pN\left(1-\dfrac{1}{A}\right)$ spam topics among $N-\dfrac{pN}{A}$ topics. So
\[\dfrac{p\left(1-\dfrac{1}{A}\right)}{1-\dfrac {p}{A}}=\dfrac{1}{B}\iff\dfrac{p(A-1)}{A-p}=\dfrac{1}{B}\iff Bp(A-1)=A-p\iff p=\dfrac{A}{B(A-1)+1}.\]
Since $A+(B-1)(A-1)=A+AB-A-B+1=AB-B+1=B(A-1)+1$, this is
\[p=\boxed{\dfrac{A}{A+(B-1)(A-1)}},\]
answer $\textbf{(C)}$. (Check: with $A=2$, $B=3$ we get $p=\dfrac{2}{4}=\dfrac{1}{2}$; of $100$ topics, $50$ are spam, $25$ are deleted, and $25$ of the remaining $75$ are spam, which is $\dfrac{1}{3}$.)
