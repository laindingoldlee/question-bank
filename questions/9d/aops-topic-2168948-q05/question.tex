Whenever a spam topic is created on a forum, the administrators delete it with probability $\dfrac{1}{A}$, where $A>1$. After the deletions, $\dfrac{1}{B}$ of all the topics are spam. Without the administrators, what fraction of all topics would be spam? (Treat the expected proportions as exact.)

$\textbf{(A)}\ \dfrac{A}{B(A-1)}\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ \dfrac{A}{A+B(A-1)}\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ \dfrac{A}{A+(B-1)(A-1)}$

$\textbf{(D)}\ \dfrac{A-1}{A-1+(B-1)(A-1)}\qquad\textbf{(E)}\ \dfrac{A+B}{AB}$
