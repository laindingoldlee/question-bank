If $v$ milliliters are added, they contain $\dfrac{v}{3}$ sugar and $\dfrac{2v}{3}$ tea. The equation is $\dfrac{\dfrac{v}{3}}{100+\dfrac{2v}{3}}=\dfrac{1}{7}$, giving $7v=300+2v$, hence $\boxed{v=60}$ milliliters.
