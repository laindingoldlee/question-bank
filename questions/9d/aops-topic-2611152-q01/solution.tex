Usually the trip takes $\dfrac24=\dfrac12$ hour $=30$ minutes. Today the first part takes $\dfrac{4}{5}\div2=\dfrac25$ hour $=24$ minutes, leaving $6$ minutes $=\dfrac1{10}$ hour for $\dfrac65$ miles. The speed must be $\dfrac65\div\dfrac1{10}=12$ miles per hour, answer $\boxed{\textbf{(E)}\ 12}$.
