Inclusion--exclusion gives $!5=5!\left(1-1+\dfrac{1}{2!}-\dfrac{1}{3!}+\dfrac{1}{4!}-\dfrac{1}{5!}\right)=44$. At any fixed position, its original digit is prohibited. By relabelling the other four positions and digits together, each of the other four digits occurs equally often, namely $\dfrac{44}{4}=11$ times. Thus each place whose original digit is $d$ contributes a digit sum $11(15-d)$. The total is $11(15\cdot11111-12345)=\boxed{1697520}$.
