Vieta gives $m+n=2026$, $mn=2026$, hence $m^2+n^2=2026^2-4052=4100624$. The discriminant is $2026\cdot 2022=4096572$, strictly between $2024^2$ and $2025^2$, so the roots are irrational. Their squares are also irrational because $m^2=2026m-2026$ and similarly for $n$. Two noninteger numbers whose sum is an integer have fractional parts summing to $1$. Consequently the requested sum is $4100624-1=\boxed{4100623}$.
