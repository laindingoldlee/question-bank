Use the identity
\[
a^3+b^3+c^3-3abc=(a+b+c)\left(a^2+b^2+c^2-ab-bc-ca\right).
\]
Since $a^2+b^2+c^2=(a+b+c)^2-2(ab+bc+ca)$, the second factor is $(a+b+c)^2-3(ab+bc+ca)$. With $ab+bc+ca=0$ this is $(a+b+c)^2$, so
\[
a^3+b^3+c^3-3abc=(a+b+c)^3 .
\]
The two expressions are equal, so their H.C.F. is $\boxed{(a+b+c)^3}$.
