Niki is baking cookies for X-Camp's bake sale at a rate of $n$ cookies per minute, where $n$ is a positive integer. After $20$ minutes, the total number of cookies she has is a multiple of $12$. She bakes for $5$ more minutes, then accidentally drops $26$ cookies on the way to the sale. If she arrives to the sale with a prime number of cookies, what is the smallest possible number of cookies she could have?
