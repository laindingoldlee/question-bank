Note that $f(-1)=a-b+c$. So
\[5a-5b+5c=5f(-1)=5\cdot5=\boxed{25}.\]
The other two values are not needed. (They are consistent: solving the three equations gives $a=\dfrac{11}{60}$, $b=\dfrac{3}{20}$, $c=\dfrac{149}{30}$, and indeed $a-b+c=5$.)
