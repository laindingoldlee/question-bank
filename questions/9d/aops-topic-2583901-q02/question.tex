Let $ABC$ be an equilateral triangle. Let $D$ be on $\overline{BC}$ with $BD=3$ and $DC=5$, and let $E$ and $F$ be on $\overline{AC}$ and $\overline{AB}$ respectively such that triangles $BDF$ and $CDE$ are equilateral. Let $N$ be the point on the same side of line $EF$ as $A$ such that triangle $NEF$ is equilateral. Compute $NA^2$.
