The first ten terms sum to $10a+45b=500$, i.e. $2a+9b=100$. The last ten sum to $10a+145b=500+100b$, which is smallest for the smallest valid $b$. Since $9b=100-2a$ is even and less than $100$, $b$ is even; $b=2$ gives $a=41$. The minimum is $500+200=\boxed{700}$.
