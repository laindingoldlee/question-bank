A regular hexagon of side $a$ consists of six equilateral triangles of side $a$. Its circumradius is $a$, each opposite-vertex diagonal is $2a$, and thus $s=6a$, $k=6a$. The condition gives $18a=108$, so $\boxed{a=6}$.
Its area is $6\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}a^2=\boxed{54\sqrt3}$. Each vertex has $OP^2=a^2=36$, so the sum is $\boxed{216}$.

\end{asy}
