Let the base be $b$, equal sides $l$, and altitude $h$. The inradius is $r=\dfrac{bh}{2l+b}$. Both incenter and centroid lie on the symmetry axis, at heights $r$ and $\dfrac{h}{3}$ above the base. The two intersections of that axis with the incircle have heights $0$ and $2r$, so the centroid-on-circle condition gives $\dfrac{h}{3}=2r$. Thus $\dfrac{b}{2l+b}=\dfrac{1}{6}$, yielding $\boxed{\dfrac{AB}{BC}=\dfrac{5}{2}}$.
