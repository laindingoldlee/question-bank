For fixed ordered distinct $A,B$, there are $(|\Delta x|+1)(|\Delta y|+1)-2$ favourable cells. For a single coordinate, the sum of $|i-j|+1$ over all nine ordered pairs $i,j\in\{1,2,3\}$ is $3+8+6=17$. Hence the sum of inclusive rectangle sizes over all ordered cell pairs is $17^2=289$. Remove the nine equal pairs, then subtract the two corners for the 72 unequal pairs: $289-9-144=136$. The total number of ordered distinct triples is $9\cdot8\cdot7=504$. The probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{17}{63}}$.
