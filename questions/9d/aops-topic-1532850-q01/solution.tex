There are $\dfrac{6!}{2!\,2!\,2!}=90$ arrangements in all. Let $A_O$, $A_B$, $A_G$ be the sets of arrangements in which the two oranges of that colour are adjacent. Gluing an adjacent pair into one block:
\[
|A_O|=\dfrac{5!}{2!\,2!}=30,\qquad|A_O\cap A_B|=\dfrac{4!}{2!}=12,\qquad|A_O\cap A_B\cap A_G|=3!=6 ,
\]
and the same for the other colours. By inclusion--exclusion the number of good arrangements is
\[
90-3\cdot30+3\cdot12-6=\boxed{30}.
\]
