Since $3\cdot5=15\equiv1\pmod{14}$, we have $3^{-1}\equiv5$. Then
\[
3^{-2}\equiv5^2=25\equiv11,\qquad 3^{-3}\equiv5\cdot11=55\equiv13\pmod{14}.
\]
So $3^{-1}+3^{-2}+3^{-3}\equiv5+11+13=29\equiv1\pmod{14}$, and the inverse of $1$ is $\boxed{1}$.

(Check without computing the inverses separately: $3^{-1}+3^{-2}+3^{-3}=\dfrac{9+3+1}{27}=13\cdot27^{-1}$, and $13\equiv-1$, $27\equiv-1\pmod{14}$, so the sum is $(-1)(-1)^{-1}=1$.)
