Smileys: $2$ faces; upside-down: $1$ face (the $2$); composites: $4,6,8,9,10$, which is $5$ faces. The probability is $\dfrac2{10}\cdot\dfrac1{10}\cdot\dfrac5{10}=\boxed{\dfrac1{100}}$.
