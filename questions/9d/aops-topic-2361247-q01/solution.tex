Since $91\cdot10989=999999$,
\[\dfrac{90}{91}=\dfrac{90\cdot10989}{999999}=\dfrac{989010}{999999}=0.\overline{989010}.\]
(Check: $\dfrac{90}{91}=1-\dfrac{1}{91}=1-0.\overline{010989}$.) The repeating block is $989010$, and its digit sum is $9+8+9+0+1+0=\boxed{27}$.
