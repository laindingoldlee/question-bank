$3^5=243\equiv19\pmod{224}$, so $3^{10}\equiv361\equiv137$ and $3^{11}\equiv411\equiv411-224=187$. The remainder is $\boxed{187}$. (Indeed $3^{11}=177147=790\cdot224+187$.)
