The factor pairs of $60$ are $(1,60),(2,30),(3,20),(4,15),(5,12),(6,10)$, with sums $61,32,23,19,17,16$ and differences $59,28,17,11,7,4$. The only number that is both a sum and a difference is $17$: $A-B=20-3$ and $C+D=5+12$. So $A=\boxed{20}$ (and $B=3$; the numbers $20,3,5,12$ are distinct).
