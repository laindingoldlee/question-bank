To fail at least two classes, Ian can recover at most two pages from each of those classes. Even if he has every page from the other two, the total is at most
\[
2+2+6+6=16.
\]
This bound is attainable, so $16$ pages do not guarantee success in three classes. Any $17$ recovered pages force at least three pages in at least three classes. The minimum is therefore $\boxed{17}$.
