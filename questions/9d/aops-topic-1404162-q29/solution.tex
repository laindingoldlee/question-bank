Factor repeatedly as a difference of squares:
\[
3^{32}-1=(3^{16}+1)(3^8+1)(3^4+1)(3^2+1)(3+1)(3-1).
\]
So $3^4-1=(3^2+1)(3+1)(3-1)=80$ and $3^4+1=82$ both divide $3^{32}-1$, and both lie between $75$ and $85$. Since the problem says there are exactly two such divisors, they are $80$ and $82$, and the product is $80\cdot82=\boxed{6560}$, choice (B).

(One can confirm there are no others: $3^{32}-1=2^7\cdot5\cdot17\cdot41\cdot193\cdot21523361$ has no divisors $76,\dots,84$ other than $80$ and $82$, since $76=4\cdot19$, $77=7\cdot11$, $78=2\cdot3\cdot13$, $79$, $81=3^4$, $83$, $84=4\cdot3\cdot7$ each involve a prime $3,7,11,13,19,79,83$ that does not divide it.)
