By Legendre's formula, the exponent of $7$ in $m!$ is $\left\lfloor\dfrac {m}{7}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac {m}{49}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac {m}{343}\right\rfloor+\cdots$. Hence
\begin{align*}
2016!:&\quad 288+41+5=334,\\
1013!:&\quad 144+20+2=166,\\
1003!:&\quad 143+20+2=165.
\end{align*}
Since $S=\dfrac{2016!}{1013!\,1003!}$, the exponent of $7$ in $S$ is $334-166-165=3$.

(Equivalently, by Kummer's theorem it is the number of carries when $1013=2645_7$ and $1003=2632_7$ are added in base $7$; there are carries out of the first three places, so again $3$.)

So $n=\boxed{3}$; the guess $2$ in the post is too small.
