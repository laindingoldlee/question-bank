For a positive integer $n$, let $a=\left\lfloor\dfrac n{10}\right\rfloor$ and let $b=n-10a$ be the last digit of $n$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that the statement ``$3n-20\left\lfloor\dfrac n{10}\right\rfloor\equiv0\pmod7$ for all positive integers $n$'' is false.
\item[(b)] Prove that $3n-20\left\lfloor\dfrac n{10}\right\rfloor\equiv0\pmod7$ holds exactly when $7\mid a+b$.
\end{enumerate}
