The identity $k(k+1)(k+2)=6\binom{k+2}3$ telescopes by Pascal\textquotesingle s identity to $S=6\binom{70}4=5501370$. This gives the candidate $6(70+4)=444$. To prove minimality by finite arithmetic, a better candidate must have $n+r<444$, since $a\ge1$. By symmetry choose $r\le\dfrac{n}{2}$. For each $1\le n<444$, generate the binomial row recursively, retaining only coefficients at most $S$ dividing $S$. Set $a=\dfrac{S}{\binom nr}$ and compare $a(n+r)$. The minimum is attained at $(a,n,r)=(6,70,4)$, giving $\boxed{444}$. This search includes $r=0$, whose value is at least $S$.
