The left side is a perfect square in $x^2$:
\[x^4-2x^2+1=\left(x^2-1\right)^2=(x-1)^2(x+1)^2 .\]
So the equation holds exactly when $x^2=1$, i.e. $x=\boxed{\pm1}$ (each a double root).
