Let $a\ne b$. The reflection of the point $P(a,b)$ in the line $y=x$ is $P'(b,a)$. Prove that the segment $PP'$ is perpendicular to the line $y=x$ and is bisected by it, and that $PP'$ is a diagonal of a square whose other diagonal lies on the line $y=x$. Find the other two vertices of this square.
