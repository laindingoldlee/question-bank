Two partners sell $n$ cows at $n$ currency units each. They spend all proceeds on the greatest possible number of adult sheep at 10 units each and one lamb costing a positive integer less than 10. When dividing the animals, both partners receive at least one adult sheep; one receives one more adult sheep than the other, who receives the lamb. The first then pays the second an integer amount to equalize value. Find that payment and the least possible numbers of cows and adult sheep.
