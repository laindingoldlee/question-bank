The adult-sheep count $s$ is odd, and $n^2=10s+\ell$ with $1\le\ell\le9$. Therefore $n^2\equiv10+\ell\pmod{20}$. Square residues modulo 20 are $0,1,4,5,9,16$; only 16 is between 11 and 19, so $\ell=6$. The difference in animal values is $10-6=4$, requiring payment $\boxed2$ to equalize. Both partners receive adults, so $s\ge3$. The least square with remainder 16 modulo 20 and at least 36 is $6^2=36$, giving $\boxed{n=6,\ s=3}$.
