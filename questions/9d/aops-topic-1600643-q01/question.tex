A sequence is defined by $a_1=0$, $a_2=4$ and $a_n=4(a_{n-1}-a_{n-2})$ for $n>2$. What is the largest value of $n$ such that $n<100$ and $a_n$ is a power of $2$? Prove that $a_n$ is a power of $2$ exactly when $n-1$ is a power of $2$.
