Find the error in the following ``proof'' that $\sqrt2=1$.

Let $x$ be a real number. On the one hand $\sqrt{x^2}=x^1$. On the other hand $\sqrt{x^2}=x^{2^{\dfrac{1}{2}}}=x^{\sqrt2}$. Since $x^1=x^{\sqrt2}$, we get $\sqrt2=1$.
