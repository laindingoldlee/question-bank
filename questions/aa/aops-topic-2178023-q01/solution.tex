Since $2\equiv-1\pmod3$, we have $2^n\equiv(-1)^n\pmod3$.

\textbf{(a)} $2020$ is even, so $2^{2020}\equiv1\pmod3$, and the least positive $x$ is $\boxed{1}$.

\textbf{(b)} The remainder is $\boxed{1\text{ if }n\text{ is even and }2\text{ if }n\text{ is odd}}$ (because $(-1)^n\equiv-1\equiv2$ for odd $n$).
