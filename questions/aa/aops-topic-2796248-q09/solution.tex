Let the common sum be $S$, and name the blanks: top right $a$; row 2 blank $b$; row 3 blanks $c,d$; row 4 blanks $e,f$ (left and right).

Column 1: $26+11+41+e=S$, so $e=S-78$. Row 4: $e+35+38+f=S$ gives $f=5$. Column 3: $8+29+d+38=S$ gives $d=S-75$. Row 2: $b=S-54$. The main diagonal $26+b+d+f=S$ gives $26+(S-54)+(S-75)+5=S$, so $S=98$.

Then row 1 gives $a=98-51=\boxed{47}$. (The rest: $b=44$, $d=23$, $e=20$, column 2 gives $c=2$; column 4 $47+14+32+5=98$ and the other diagonal $47+29+2+20=98$ check.)
