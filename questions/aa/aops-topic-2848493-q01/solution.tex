Clearing denominators, $97y-97x=xy$, i.e. $xy+97x-97y=0$, which factors as $(97-x)(y+97)=97^2$. Since $y+97>97$ and $97$ is prime, $y+97=97^2$ and $97-x=1$. So $(x,y)=\boxed{(96,9312)}$.
