At every lunch, three friends share their cheesy crackers but agree not to eat them until they all have the same number. At the first lunch, Axel gives Ben and Chloe as many crackers as they each already have. At the second lunch, Ben gives Axel and Chloe as many crackers as they each already have. At the third lunch, Chloe gives Axel and Ben as many crackers as they each already have, after which they each have $8$ crackers, so they eat them. How many crackers did Axel start with?
