The relative speed is 90 miles per hour. In 8 seconds the second train moves $\dfrac{90\cdot8}{3600}=\dfrac{1}{5}$ mile relative to the passenger. Its length is $\dfrac{5280}{5}=\boxed{1056\text{ feet}}$.
