For distance $D$, $2+\dfrac{D}{500}=\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{D}{300}$. Thus $\dfrac{3}{2}=D(\dfrac{1}{300}-\dfrac{1}{500})=\dfrac{D}{750}$, giving $D=\boxed{1125}$ miles.
