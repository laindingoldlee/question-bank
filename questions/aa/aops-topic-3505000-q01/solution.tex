The constant ratio is $\dfrac{y}{x}=\dfrac{9}{5}$. Thus $y=14\cdot\dfrac{9}{5}=\boxed{\dfrac{126}{5}}$. Equivalently, cross-multiplying $\dfrac{14}{y}=\dfrac{5}{9}$ gives $5y=126$.
