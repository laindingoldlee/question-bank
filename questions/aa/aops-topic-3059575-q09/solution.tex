Put $ O=(0,0),P=(6,0)$. An upper unit normal $ n $ to a common tangent satisfies $(P-O)\cdot n=2-4=-2 $, so $ n=(-\dfrac{1}{3},\dfrac{2\sqrt2}{3})$. Thus $ A=(-\dfrac{2}{3},\dfrac{4\sqrt2}{3})$, $ D=(\dfrac{14}{3},\dfrac{8\sqrt2}{3})$, and $ B,C $ are their reflections across the horizontal axis. The shoelace cross-product sum for $ A,O,B,C,P,D $ is $ 48\sqrt2 $, giving area $\boxed{24\sqrt2}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair O=(0,0),P=(6,0),A=(-2.0/3,4*sqrt(2)/3),B=(-2.0/3,-4*sqrt(2)/3),C=(14.0/3,-8*sqrt(2)/3),D=(14.0/3,8*sqrt(2)/3);draw(circle(O,2)^^circle(P,4));filldraw(A--O--B--C--P--D--cycle,lightgray+opacity(.5));draw(A--D^^B--C,blue);label("$O$",O,E);label("$P$",P,E);label("$A$",A,NW);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,S);label("$D$",D,N);
\end{asy}
