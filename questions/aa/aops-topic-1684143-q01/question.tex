Amy marks two points $A$ and $B$ that are $4$ inches apart. She draws a circle that has segment $AB$ as a diameter. She then draws a larger circle, which overlaps the first circle, through $A$ and $B$, such that the arc from $A$ to $B$ along its circumference is a quarter-circle. What is the total area covered by the two circles? Express your answer in terms of $\pi$.
