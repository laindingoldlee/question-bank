During football season, $25$ teams are ranked by three reporters, Alice, Bob and Cecil. Each reporter ranks all $25$ teams, using each of the places $1$ through $25$ exactly once. A team earns $25$ points for each first-place ranking, $24$ points for each second-place ranking, and so on, down to $1$ point for each $25$th-place ranking. The Hedgehogs earned $27$ points in total from the three reporters. In how many ways could the three reporters have assigned their places to the Hedgehogs? (For example, Alice $14$th, Bob $17$th and Cecil $20$th is one such way.)
