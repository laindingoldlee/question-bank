A place $k$ is worth $26-k$ points, so the places of the Hedgehogs correspond exactly to the triple $(x,y,z)$ of points they received from Alice, Bob and Cecil, with $1\le x,y,z\le25$ and
\[x+y+z=27.\]
The reporters are different people, so the triples are ordered: for example, (Alice $1$ point, Bob $2$, Cecil $24$) and (Alice $2$, Bob $1$, Cecil $24$) are different ways. (The example has points $12+9+6=27$.)

The upper bound is automatic: if $x+y+z=27$ with $y,z\ge1$, then $x\le25$. By stars and bars, the number of ordered triples of positive integers with sum $27$ is
\[\dbinom{26}{2}=\boxed{325}.\]
(Put $27$ stars in a row and choose $2$ of the $26$ gaps between them for the bars.)
