Since $a\le b\le c$, we have $a^3\le1000$, so $a\le10$ and $a$ must divide $1000$. For each $a=1,2,4,5,8,10$, count divisors $b$ of $\dfrac{1000}{a}$ with $a\le b\le\sqrt{\dfrac{1000}{a}}$. The respective lists are $(1,2,4,5,8,10,20,25)$; $(2,4,5,10,20)$; $(5,10)$; $(5,8,10)$; the empty list; and $(10)$. Then $c$ is determined. The count is $8+5+2+3+0+1=\boxed{19}$.
