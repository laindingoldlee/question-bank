An integer $x$ would give $1+3x=12$, which is not integral. For nonintegers, the quotient differs from one by $\dfrac{1}{\lfloor x\rfloor}$. Negative $x$ cannot give 12; $0<x<1$ is undefined; and direct bounds force $3<x<4$. In that interval the quotient is $\dfrac{4}{3}$, so $x=\dfrac{(12-\dfrac{4}{3})}{3}=\dfrac{32}{9}$. This lies in the required interval, and $a+b=\boxed{41}$.
