In the equation \[\dfrac{\lceil x \rceil }{ \lfloor x \rfloor} + 3x = 12,\]the value of $x$ can be expressed as $\dfrac{a}{b}$ , where $a$ and $b$ are relatively prime. Find $a + b$.



Note: Here, $\lfloor x \rfloor$ denotes the greatest integer less than or equal to $x$, while $\lceil x \rceil$ denotes the lowest integer greater than or equal to $x$. For example, $\lfloor 4.2  \rfloor = 4$ and $\lceil 4.2\rceil  = 5$.
