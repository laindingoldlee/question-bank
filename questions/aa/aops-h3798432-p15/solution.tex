Let e be the number of alternate-day students working yesterday. Then $8-e$ work today and e tomorrow. Let u and v be the once-every-three-days students working yesterday and today. The totals give $e+u=5$ and $(8-e)+v=3$. Thus $e\le5$ from u nonnegative and $e\ge5$ from v nonnegative. Therefore $e=5,u=v=0$, and all three third-day students work tomorrow. Tomorrow's total is $7+5+3=\boxed{15}$.
