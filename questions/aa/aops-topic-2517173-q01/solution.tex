The denominators are nonzero ($4b+9$ and $2c+1$ are odd, and $a\ne-11$ for the fractions to be defined). Cross-multiplying the first equality:
\[a(4b+9)=4b(a+11)\iff9a=44b.\]
Cross-multiplying the second:
\[4b(2c+1)=2c(4b+9)\iff4b=18c\iff2b=9c.\]
So $c$ is even; write $c=2k$. Then $b=9k$ and $a=\dfrac{44b}{9}=44k$, and
\[a^2+b^2+c^2=\left(44^2+9^2+2^2\right)k^2=(1936+81+4)k^2=2021k^2=2021,\]
so $k=\pm1$. Both work: for $k=1$ all three fractions equal $\dfrac{44}{55}=\dfrac{36}{45}=\dfrac45$; for $k=-1$ they equal $\dfrac{-44}{-33}=\dfrac{-36}{-27}=\dfrac{-4}{-3}=\dfrac43$. The solutions are
\[\boxed{(a,b,c)=(44,9,2)\ \text{or}\ (-44,-9,-2)}.\]
