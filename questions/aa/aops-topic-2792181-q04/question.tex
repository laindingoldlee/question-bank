Nine dwarves live in a $3\times3$ arrangement of rooms. On Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday mornings each dwarf rubs noses with the dwarves in rooms sharing a wall with his; between mornings the dwarves switch rooms arbitrarily. Show that some two dwarves never rub noses.
