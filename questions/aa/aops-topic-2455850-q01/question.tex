A large $5\times5\times5$ cube is formed from $125$ small $1\times1\times1$ cubes. Three central columns pass through the small cube at the very centre of the large cube: one from top to bottom, one from front to back and one from left to right. All the small cubes in these three columns are removed. What is the surface area of the resulting solid?
