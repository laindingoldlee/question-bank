Testing $x=1,2,3$ gives respectively $-24,-84,0$ for the left side. For $x\ge 4$, $(x+4)^3\le(2x)^3=8x^3<x^5$, since $x^2>8$. Thus the left side is positive. The unique positive integer solution is $\boxed 3$.
