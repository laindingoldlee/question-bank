The radius satisfies $\pi R^2=162\pi$, so $R^2=162$ and $R=9\sqrt2$.

\textbf{The largest inscribed triangle is equilateral.} Among the triangles inscribed in the circle there is one of largest area (the area depends continuously on the positions of the three vertices on the circle). In such a triangle $ABC$, keep $B$ and $C$ fixed: the area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot BC\cdot(\text{distance from }A\text{ to }BC)$ is largest when $A$ is the point of the circle farthest from line $BC$, which is where the perpendicular bisector of $BC$ meets the circle on $A$'s side. So $AB=AC$. By the same argument at the other vertices, $BA=BC$, so the largest triangle is equilateral.

\textbf{Its area.} An equilateral triangle inscribed in a circle of radius $R$ is made of three triangles with two sides $R$ and angle $120^\circ$ at the centre, so its area is
\[
3\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}R^2\sin120^\circ=\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{4}R^2=\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{4}\cdot162=\dfrac{243\sqrt3}{2}\approx210.44 .
\]

Rounded to the nearest whole number, the area is $\boxed{210}$ square units.
