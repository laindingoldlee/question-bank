This is a geometric series with first term $1$, ratio $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $11$ terms (since $1024=2^{10}$). Its sum is
\[
\dfrac{1-\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{11}}{1-\dfrac{1}{2}}=2-\dfrac{1}{1024}=\boxed{\dfrac{2047}{1024}} .
\]
