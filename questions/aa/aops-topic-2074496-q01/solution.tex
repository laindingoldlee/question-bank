Since \(\overline{AB0AB}=1000\cdot\overline{AB}+\overline{AB}=1001\cdot\overline{AB}\), the quotient is always \(1001\). Thus \(\overline{A00B}=1001\), so \(A=1\) and \(B=1\), and \(A+B=\boxed{2}\).
