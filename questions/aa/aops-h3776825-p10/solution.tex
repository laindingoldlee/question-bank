Divide the rectangle into six $1\times\dfrac{1}{2}$ rectangles. Assign boundary points consistently to one cell. By the pigeonhole principle some cell contains two points. Their distance is at most that cell\textquotesingle s diagonal, $\sqrt{1^2+(\dfrac{1}{2})^2}=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt5}{2}}$.
