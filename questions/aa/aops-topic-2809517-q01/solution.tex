Work from the inside: $-\dfrac{268}{179}+1=-\dfrac{89}{179}$; its reciprocal plus $1$ is $-\dfrac{179}{89}+1=-\dfrac{90}{89}$; the reciprocal plus $1$ is $-\dfrac{89}{90}+1=\dfrac1{90}$; the reciprocal plus $1$ is $90+1=\boxed{91}$.
