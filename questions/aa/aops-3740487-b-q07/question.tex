Say that a mouse named Rocky has a block of cheese that is in the shape of a triangular prism, with both triangles being equilateral and the side length of the equilateral triangles being $40\sqrt[4]{3}$ units. The height of Rocky's cheese is $40$ units. Unfortunately, Rocky's cheese has $30$ nonoverlapping holes in the shape of hemispheres with radius $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt[3]{\pi}}$ units. Assume each entire hemispherical hole is removed from the solid. What is the volume of Rocky's cheese?

$\textbf{(A)}~47,980\qquad\textbf{(B)}~50,000\qquad\textbf{(C)}~\dfrac{40,000\pi}{3}\qquad\textbf{(D)}~\dfrac{30,000\pi}{7}\qquad\textbf{(E)}~\text{None of the above}\qquad$
