Let the horse be worth $h$ dollars. Seven months' pay is $\dfrac{7}{12}$ of the yearly pay:
\[
\dfrac{7}{12}(19200+h)=10450+h\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 11200+\dfrac{7h}{12}=10450+h\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \dfrac{5h}{12}=750 ,
\]
so $h=\boxed{1800}$. (Check: $\dfrac{7}{12}\cdot21000=12250=10450+1800$.)
