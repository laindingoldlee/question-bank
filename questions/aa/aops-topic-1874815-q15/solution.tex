The formula is $\boxed{\left(\dfrac {a}{b}\right)^n=\dfrac{a^n}{b^n}}$.

Proof by induction on $n$. For $n=1$ both sides equal $\dfrac {a}{b}$. If $\left(\dfrac {a}{b}\right)^n=\dfrac{a^n}{b^n}$, then, multiplying fractions numerator by numerator and denominator by denominator,
\[
\left(\dfrac {a}{b}\right)^{n+1}=\left(\dfrac {a}{b}\right)^n\cdot\dfrac {a}{b}=\dfrac{a^n}{b^n}\cdot\dfrac {a}{b}=\dfrac{a^{n}\cdot a}{b^{n}\cdot b}=\dfrac{a^{n+1}}{b^{n+1}} .
\]
So the formula holds for all positive integers $n$. (It also holds for $n=0$, as $1=\dfrac{1}{1}$, and, when $a\ne0$, for negative integers $n$, since $\left(\dfrac {a}{b}\right)^{-m}=\left(\dfrac {b}{a}\right)^{m}=\dfrac{b^m}{a^m}=\dfrac{a^{-m}}{b^{-m}}$.)
