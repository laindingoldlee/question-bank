A parallelogram is determined by its pair of horizontal lines and its pair of vertical lines, and different choices give different parallelograms. So the number of parallelograms is
\[
\binom n2\binom92=36\binom n2 .
\]
Setting $36\binom n2=360$ gives $\binom n2=\dfrac{n(n-1)}{2}=10$, so $n(n-1)=20$ and $n=\boxed{5}$.

(Counting only the smallest cells, as in the poster's pattern, gives $(n-1)\cdot8$; the question counts all parallelograms, which is why combinations are needed.)
