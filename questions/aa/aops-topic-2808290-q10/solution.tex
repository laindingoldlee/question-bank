Power of a point gives $AB\cdot AC=AP^2$, so $AC=20$ and $BC=15$. Let $A=(0,0)$ and the circle centre be $(\dfrac{25}{2},0)$. Since $AP\perp OP$, $P\cdot(P-O)=0$. As $|P|^2=100$, its $x$ coordinate is 8, and $y^2=100-64=36$. Therefore $PB=\sqrt{(8-5)^2+6^2}=3\sqrt5$, giving $\boxed{35}$.
