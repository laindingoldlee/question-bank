Points $A,B,C$ are collinear in that order, with $BC$ a diameter of a circle. The tangent from $A$ touches the circle at $P$. If $AP=10$, $AB=5$, and $PB=a\sqrt b$ with $b$ squarefree, find $10a+b$.
