An empty field gets one new house each noon, beginning Monday. On day $n=1,\ldots,5$, a storm independently occurs that afternoon with probability $\dfrac{n}{8}$ and destroys all houses. Find the probability that the number of houses on Friday evening is not 1, in lowest terms $\dfrac{p}{q}$, and give $p+q$.
