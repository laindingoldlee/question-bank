Exactly one house survives Friday evening precisely when Thursday has a storm and Friday does not. Its probability is $\dfrac{4}{8}\cdot\dfrac{3}{8}=\dfrac{3}{16}$. Therefore the requested probability is $\dfrac{13}{16}$ and $\boxed{p+q=29}$.
