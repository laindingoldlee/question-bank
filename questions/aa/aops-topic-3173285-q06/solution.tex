The equation is $2^{n+1}=n^{2x}$. Unique prime factorization forces $n$ to be a power of $2$. The case $n=1$ fails directly. For $n=2^k$ with $k\ge1$, comparing exponents of $2$ gives $n+1=2kx$. The left side is odd and the right side is even, a contradiction. Thus there are $\boxed{\text{no solutions}}$.
