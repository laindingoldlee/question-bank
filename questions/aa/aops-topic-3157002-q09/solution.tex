Inductively $ F_k=\lceil\dfrac{k}{2}\rceil $. Indeed if $ F_{k-1}=\lceil\dfrac{k-1}{2}\rceil $, the difference from $ k $ is positive and equals $\lceil\dfrac{k}{2}\rceil $, by checking even and odd $ k $. Thus $\boxed{1009}$.
