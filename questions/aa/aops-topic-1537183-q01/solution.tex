We need integer solutions of $x^2+y^2=81$.

If $x=0$ or $y=0$, we get the four points $(\pm9,0)$ and $(0,\pm9)$.

If both $x$ and $y$ are nonzero, then $x^2$ is one of $1,4,9,16,25,36,49,64$, and $81-x^2$ is respectively $80,77,72,65,56,45,32,17$, none of which is a perfect square. (Alternatively: $81=3^4$, and a multiple of $3$ that is a sum of two squares must have both squares divisible by $3$, since squares are $0$ or $1$ modulo $3$; this reduces to $x^2+y^2=9$ and then to $x^2+y^2=1$, which has only the solutions on the axes.)

So there are exactly $\boxed{4}$ such points.
