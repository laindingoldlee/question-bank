Since $1999=2000-1$ and $5\mid2000$, we have $1999\equiv-1\pmod5$. Hence
\[1999^{2000}\equiv(-1)^{2000}=1\pmod5,\]
because $2000$ is even. The remainder is $\boxed{1}$ (not $4$: that would be the remainder of an odd power such as $1999^{1999}$).
