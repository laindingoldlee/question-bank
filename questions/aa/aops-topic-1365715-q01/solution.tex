The length $L$ satisfies $\dfrac{3}{8}L=240$, so $L=\dfrac{8\cdot240}{3}=640$ m.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0), B=(8,0), C=(8,3), D=(0,3);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
label("$640$ m",(A+B)/2,S); label("$240$ m",(B+C)/2,E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The area is $640\cdot240=153\,600\text{ m}^2$. Since $1\text{ km}=1000$ m, $1\text{ km}^2=1\,000\,000\text{ m}^2$, so the area is $0.1536\text{ km}^2\approx\boxed{0.154\text{ km}^2}$.
