The condition is $3n+45\equiv16\pmod{1060}$, i.e. $3n\equiv-29\equiv1031\pmod{1060}$. Write $3n=1031+1060t$ with an integer $t$. Since $1031\equiv2$ and $1060\equiv1\pmod3$, divisibility by $3$ needs $2+t\equiv0\pmod 3$, i.e. $t\equiv1\pmod3$. The smallest positive $n$ comes from $t=1$ (for $t\le-2$, $n$ is negative):
\[3n=2091,\qquad n=697.\]
Then
\[18n+17=6\cdot2091+17=12563=6\cdot1920+1043,\]
so the remainder is $\boxed{1043}$.
