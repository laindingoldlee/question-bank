$\dfrac m3+\dfrac{m^2}2+\dfrac{m^3}6=\dfrac{m^3+3m^2+2m}6=\dfrac{m(m+1)(m+2)}6$. Among three consecutive integers one is divisible by $3$ and at least one by $2$, so $6$ divides the product. $\blacksquare$
