The solutions of $x(2x-7)=3$ may be written in the form $\dfrac{m+\sqrt n}{p}$ and $\dfrac{m-\sqrt n}{p}$, where $m$, $n$, $p$ are positive integers with greatest common divisor $1$. Find $m+n+p$.
