Expand: $2x^2-7x-3=0$. By the quadratic formula,
\[
x=\dfrac{7\pm\sqrt{49+24}}{4}=\dfrac{7\pm\sqrt{73}}{4}.
\]
So $m=7$, $n=73$, $p=4$ (and $\gcd(7,73,4)=1$), giving $m+n+p=\boxed{84}$.
