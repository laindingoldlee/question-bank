One-digit numbers have difference 0, and 100 reverses to 1, giving 99. For a two-digit number $10a+b$, the difference is $9(a-b)=45$, so $a-b=5$. Maximizing $a\le9$ gives $a=9,b=4$. Thus $\boxed{94}$, and $94-49=45$.
