$N\equiv-5\pmod{11}$, $N\equiv-5\pmod{100}$ and $N\equiv-2\pmod{101}$. So $N=1100k-5$ with $1100k-5\equiv-2\pmod{101}$. Since $1100=10\cdot101+90\equiv90\equiv-11$, we need $-11k\equiv3$, i.e. $11k\equiv-3\equiv98$. As $11\cdot55=605=6\cdot101-1\equiv-1$, the inverse of $11$ is $-55\equiv46$, so $k\equiv98\cdot46=4508\equiv64\pmod{101}$. Thus $N=1100\cdot64-5=70395$.

Each bag holds at most $101$ candies, so at least $\left\lceil\dfrac{70395}{101}\right\rceil=697$ bags are needed. And $697$ suffice: $695\cdot101+2\cdot100=70195+200=70395$. Answer $\boxed{697}$.
