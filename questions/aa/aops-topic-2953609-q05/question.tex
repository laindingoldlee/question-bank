A positive integer $N$ is the smallest that leaves remainder $6$ when divided by $11$, remainder $95$ when divided by $100$, and remainder $99$ when divided by $101$. Bags hold exactly $11$, $100$ or $101$ candies. If $N$ candies are packed into full bags, what is the least number of bags needed?
