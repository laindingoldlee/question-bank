Use $C=(0,0),A=(\sqrt2,0),B=(0,\sqrt2)$. The external right-angle vertices are $D=(-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2})$, $E=(\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2},-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2})$, and $F=(\sqrt2,\sqrt2)$. Thus $DE=2$ and line $DE$ is $x+y=0$. The distance from $F$ to that line is 2. Therefore $[DEF]=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot2\cdot2=\boxed2$.

\end{asy}
