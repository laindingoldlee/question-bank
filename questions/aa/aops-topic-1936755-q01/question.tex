Right triangle $ABC$ has hypotenuse $AC$, $\angle CAB=30^\circ$ and $BC=\sqrt2$. Right triangle $ACD$ has hypotenuse $AD$ and $\angle DAC=45^\circ$. The interiors of triangles $ABC$ and $ACD$ do not overlap. Find the length of the perpendicular from $D$ to line $AB$.
