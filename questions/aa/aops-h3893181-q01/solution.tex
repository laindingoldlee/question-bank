No root is zero. Put $w=\dfrac{13r-1}{r}=13-\dfrac{1}{r}$. Then $w^{10}=-1$, so the ten values of $w$ are all the tenth roots of $-1$, each of modulus $1$. Thus $|\dfrac{1}{r}|^2=|13-w|^2=170-26\operatorname{Re}(w)$. The ten roots $w$ sum to zero, so the sum over all ten roots $r$ is $1700$. Each conjugate pair contributes twice the corresponding term in the question. The answer is therefore $\boxed{850}$.
