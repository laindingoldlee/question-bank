The displacement is $(99,999)=9(11,111)$, with $\gcd(11,111)=1$. Every lattice step along this direction is an integer multiple of $(11,111)$: the line relation $111(x-1)=11(y-1)$ forces $11\mid x-1$. The interior points are therefore
\[
(1+11j,1+111j),\qquad j=1,\ldots,8.
\]
There are $\boxed8$, choice $\boxed{\text{D}}$.
