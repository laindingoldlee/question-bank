Write $43_8=4\cdot8+3$. Then
\[
(4\cdot8+3)^2=16\cdot8^2+24\cdot8+9 .
\]
Now carry in base $8$, from the right: $9=1\cdot8+1$ gives the units digit $1$ and a carry of $1$; then $24+1=25=3\cdot8+1$ gives the eights digit $1$ and a carry of $3$; then $16+3=19=2\cdot8+3$ gives the digit $3$ and a carry of $2$. Hence
\[
\left(43_8\right)^2=\boxed{2311_8}.
\]
Check in base $10$: $43_8=35$, $35^2=1225=2\cdot512+3\cdot64+1\cdot8+1$.
