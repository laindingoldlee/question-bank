Serena gets a grade of $75\%$ on her chapter test. She does not remember how many problems there were, but she remembers that there were at most $18$ problems, that every problem was worth the same (integer) number of points, and that she received no points for an incorrect or skipped problem. Find the sum of all the possible numbers of problems the test could have had.
