If there were $n$ problems and Serena solved $k$ of them, each worth $w$ points, her grade is $\dfrac{kw}{nw}=\dfrac kn$. So $\dfrac kn=\dfrac34$, i.e. $4k=3n$. Since $\gcd(3,4)=1$, $4\mid n$; conversely every multiple of $4$ works with $k=\dfrac{3n}{4}$. With $n\le18$ the possibilities are $4,8,12,16$, with sum $\boxed{40}$.
