\textbf{Finding $a$.} $20^4=160000<187665<194481=21^4$, so $20<\sqrt[4]{187665}<21$. Also $20.5^2=420.25$ and $20.5^4=420.25^2=176610.0625<187665$, so $\sqrt[4]{187665}>20.5$ and $a=21$.

\textbf{Finding $b$.} $11^5=161051<187665<248832=12^5$, and $11.5^5=11.5\cdot(132.25)^2=11.5\cdot17490.0625=201135.71875>187665$, so $11<\sqrt[5]{187665}<11.5$ and $b=11$.

Hence $11a-4b=231-44=\boxed{187}$.
