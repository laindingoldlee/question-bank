For radius $r$, both centres lie in the inner square of side $8-2r$. Their separation is at most its diagonal $(8-2r)\sqrt2$, but nonoverlap requires separation at least $2r$. Thus $r\le\dfrac{8}{2+\sqrt2}=8-4\sqrt2$. Placing the centres at opposite vertices of the inner square attains equality, so the maximum is $\boxed{8-4\sqrt2}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
real r=8-4*sqrt(2);draw((0,0)--(8,0)--(8,8)--(0,8)--cycle);draw(circle((r,r),r),blue);draw(circle((8-r,8-r),r),red);draw((r,r)--(8-r,8-r),dashed);
\end{asy}
