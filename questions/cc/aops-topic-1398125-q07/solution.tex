Express everything in terms of $w$:
\[
z=\dfrac{5}{8}w,\qquad y=\dfrac{2}{3}z=\dfrac{5}{12}w,\qquad x=\dfrac{3}{4}y=\dfrac{5}{16}w .
\]
Then
\[
\dfrac{x+y+z}{w}=\dfrac{5}{16}+\dfrac{5}{12}+\dfrac{5}{8}=\dfrac{15+20+30}{48}=\boxed{\dfrac{65}{48}} .
\]
(For instance $w=48$, $z=30$, $y=20$, $x=15$.)
