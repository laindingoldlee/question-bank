The positive integers $1,2,3,\dots$ are written down in order and each is coloured with one of $k$ colours. The rule is that there must be no three \emph{distinct} numbers $a<b<c$ of the same colour with $a+b=c$. Prove that for every $k$ there is a number $N$ such that it is impossible to colour all of $1,2,\dots,N$ by the rule; that is, no matter how many colours are allowed, one is always forced to stop.
