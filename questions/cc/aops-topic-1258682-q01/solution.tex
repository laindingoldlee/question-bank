From the second equation $b=1-\dfrac{1}{c}=\dfrac{c-1}{c}$, so $\dfrac{1}{b}=\dfrac{c}{c-1}$. Then
\[a=1-\dfrac{c}{c-1}=-\dfrac{1}{c-1},\qquad\dfrac{1}{a}=1-c,\]
and $c+\dfrac{1}{a}=\boxed{1}$. (All quantities are assumed defined: $b,c\ne0$, $c\ne1$.)
