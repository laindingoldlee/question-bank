By Fermat's little theorem $a^6\equiv1\pmod7$ for $7\nmid a$. Reducing the exponents mod $6$:
\[2^{20}\equiv2^2=4,\quad3^{30}\equiv1,\quad4^{40}\equiv4^4=256\equiv4,\quad5^{50}\equiv5^2=25\equiv4,\quad6^{60}\equiv1.\]
The sum is $\equiv4+1+4+4+1=14\equiv\boxed{0}\pmod7$.
