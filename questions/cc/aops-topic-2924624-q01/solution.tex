Since $b>0$ and $x>y$, $\dfrac xb>\dfrac yb$. Since $0<b<a$, $\dfrac1b>\dfrac1a$, and multiplying by $y>0$ gives $\dfrac yb>\dfrac ya$. So $\dfrac xb>\dfrac ya$. $\blacksquare$

Without positivity: $a=2$, $b=1$, $x=-1$, $y=-2$ gives $\dfrac xb=-1$ and $\dfrac ya=-1$, so the strict inequality fails. The step $\dfrac yb>\dfrac ya$ uses $y>0$.
