The conditions say $r\equiv2\pmod3$, $r\equiv1\pmod2$ and $r\equiv3\pmod4$. The last implies the second. Also $r\equiv3\pmod4$ and $r\equiv2\pmod3$ both say $r\equiv-1$: $r+1$ is divisible by $4$ and by $3$, hence by $12$. So $r=12m-1$ for an integer $m$ (no Chinese remainder theorem needed beyond this observation).

The sum is
\[
\dfrac{12r+4(r-2)+6(r-1)+3(r-3)}{12}=\dfrac{25r-23}{12}=\dfrac{25(12m-1)-23}{12}=25m-4 .
\]
This is positive exactly for $m\ge1$, and smallest for $m=1$ ($r=11$): $\boxed{21}$. Check: $11+3+5+2=21$.
