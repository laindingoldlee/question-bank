Dividing by 5 gives $ x+2y<4 $. Since $ x\ge1 $, $ y\ge2 $ is impossible. With $ y=1 $, we need $ 1\le x<2 $, so $(1,1)$ is the only pair and the answer is $\boxed1 $.
