If the first number is $n$, the sum of the six largest possible remaining numbers is $(n+2)+\cdots+(n+7)=6n+27$. Thus $2026\le6n+27$, giving $n\ge334$. For $n=334$, erase $334$ and $340$ from $334,\ldots,341$; the remaining sum is $8\cdot334+28-334-340=2026$. Therefore the minimum is $\boxed{334}$.
