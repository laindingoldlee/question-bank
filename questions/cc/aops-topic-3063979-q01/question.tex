A walker starts at a chair between two houses and heads toward the first house. After reaching each house, the walker turns around and heads toward the other. At every subsequent encounter with the chair, the walker independently crashes with probability $\dfrac{1}{3}$ and stops. What is the probability of first crashing on the encounter after leaving the second house for the second time?
