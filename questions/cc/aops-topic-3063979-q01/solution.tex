The successive chair encounters occur after leaving the first house, the second house, the first house again, and the second house again. Thus the specified crash is the fourth encounter. The walker must survive three encounters and crash on the fourth, giving $\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^3\dfrac{1}{3}=\boxed{\dfrac{8}{81}}$.
