Two figures are \emph{similar} if one can be obtained from the other by a rigid motion (a combination of translations, rotations and reflections) followed by a dilation. A parabola is the set of points that are equidistant from a point $F$ (the focus) and a line $\ell$ (the directrix) not through $F$. Prove that any two parabolas are similar.
