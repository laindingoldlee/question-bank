Since $A$ is an integer, both square roots are integers: otherwise $2\sqrt{BC}=A^2-B-C$ first forces the square-free parts to agree, and their positive sum remains irrational. Write $B=u^2,C=v^2$, so $A=u+v$. The equation $v^2=u^2+u+v$ implies $(v-u)(v+u)=v+u$, hence $v=u+1$. Thus $A=2u+1$. If $u$ has $d\ge2$ digits, then $s(2u+1)+s((u+1)^2)\le9(d+1+2d+1)=27d+18<10^{2d-2}\le u^2$. Hence $1\le u\le9$. Testing these nine values in $u^2=s(2u+1)+s((u+1)^2)$ gives only $u=4$. Therefore $\boxed{A=9,B=16,C=25}$.
