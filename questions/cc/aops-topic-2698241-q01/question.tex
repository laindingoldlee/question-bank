Let $A$ be the integer obtained by writing the decimal numbers $2^{10},2^{20},\dots,2^{10n}$ one after another (for $n=2$, $A=10241048576$). Find the smallest integer $n\ge3$ with $A\equiv2^{10n}\pmod{2^{170}}$.
