Let $N$ be the integer obtained by writing $2000,1999,1998,\dots,2,1$ one after another (so $N=20001999199819971996\cdots654321$), and let $X$, $Y$, $Z$ be the integers obtained by appending the digits $42$, $21$, $00$ to $N$. Find $X^2-2Y^2+Z^2$.
