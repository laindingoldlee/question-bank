Scale to $A=(0,0)$, $B=(1,0)$, $E=(1,1)$, $F=(1,2)$, $C=(1,3)$, $D=(0,3)$. Set $\alpha=\arctan2$ and $\beta=\arctan3$. The four angles are $45^\circ$, $\alpha-45^\circ$, $\beta-45^\circ$, and $45^\circ$, whose sum is $\alpha+\beta$. The tangent addition formula gives $\tan(\alpha+\beta)=\dfrac{2+3}{1-6}=-1$. As $90^\circ<\alpha+\beta<180^\circ$, their sum is $\boxed{135^\circ}$.

size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(1,0),E=(1,1),F=(1,2),C=(1,3),D=(0,3);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--E);draw(A--F);draw(A--C);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$E$",E,E);label("$F$",F,E);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);
\end{asy}
