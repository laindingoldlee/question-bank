Equal areas imply $s^2=\pi r^2$, so $rs=\dfrac{s^2}{\sqrt\pi}$. This is a positive integer and is minimized at 1. Therefore $s^2=\sqrt\pi$ and $\boxed{s=\pi^{\dfrac{1}{4}}}$.
