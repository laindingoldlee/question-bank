Use $A=(0,0),B=(s,0),C=(0,s)$, $K=(7,0)$, and $N=(0,3)$. Rotating vector $KN=(-7,3)$ clockwise through $90^\circ$ gives $KL=(3,7)$, so $L=(10,7)$. The other orientation would lie outside the triangle. Since $L$ lies on $BC$, whose equation is $x+y=s$, we get $s=10+7=\boxed{17}$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(17,0),C=(0,17),K=(7,0),L=(10,7),M=(3,10),N=(0,3);draw(A--B--C--cycle);filldraw(K--L--M--N--cycle,lightblue);label("A",A,SW);label("B",B,SE);label("C",C,NW);label("K",K,S);label("L",L,NE);label("M",M,NW);label("N",N,W);label("7",(3.5,0),S);label("3",(0,1.5),W);
\end{asy}
