What is the sum of all odd positive integers \(n\) such that the base-\(8\) expansion of \(\dfrac{1}{n}\) is purely repeating with minimal period exactly \(4\)? Give the answer in base \(10\).

A student argued: ``every such \(\dfrac{1}{n}\) equals \(\dfrac{x}{8^4-1}\), so \(n\) must divide \(8^4-1=4095\); add the divisors of \(4095\) other than \(4095\) itself.'' This gives \(4641\). Explain the error and find the correct sum.
