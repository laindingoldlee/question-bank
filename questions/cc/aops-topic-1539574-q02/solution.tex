Let the parallel sides be $a$ and $b$ and the height $h=16$. The median is parallel to the bases and its length is their average, $m=\dfrac{a+b}{2}$ (figure not to scale):

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real k=0.25;
pair A=(0,0),B=(5,0),C=(4,16*k),D=(1,16*k);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
pair M1=(A+D)/2,M2=(B+C)/2;
draw(M1--M2,blue+linewidth(0.9));
draw((2.5,0)--(2.5,16*k),dashed);
real s=0.15;draw((2.5,s)--(2.5+s,s)--(2.5+s,0));
label("$a$",(A+B)/2+(-0.6,0),S);label("$b$",(C+D)/2,N);
label("$h=16$",(2.5,16*k*0.78),E);label("$m$",(M1+M2)/2+(-1.2,0),N,blue);
dot(M1,blue);dot(M2,blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The area of a trapezoid is $\dfrac{a+b}{2}\cdot h=m\cdot h$. Hence
\[
m=\dfrac{64}{16}=\boxed{4}.
\]
