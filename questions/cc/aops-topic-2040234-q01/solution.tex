The product condition makes the expression $a^2+b^2+c^2$. AM--GM yields $a^2+b^2+c^2\ge3\sqrt[3]{a^2b^2c^2}=3$. Equality requires $a=b=c=1$, which is allowed. The minimum is $\boxed3$.
