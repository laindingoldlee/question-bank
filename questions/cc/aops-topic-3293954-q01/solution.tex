Put $AB=AC=s$, $AD=x>0$, $AE=y>0$, and $\alpha=\angle BAC$. The cosine rule and $BD=CE$ imply $s^2+x^2-2sx\cos\alpha=s^2+y^2-2sy\cos\alpha$. Thus $(x-y)(x+y-2s\cos\alpha)=0$. Since $\cos\alpha\le0$, the second factor is positive, so $x=y$. Hence $\dfrac{AD}{AC}=\dfrac{AE}{AB}$, and the converse of the triangle proportionality theorem gives $DE\parallel BC$.
