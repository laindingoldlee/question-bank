For $n=\overline{abcd}$ the new number is $\overline{bcda}$, and
\[\overline{abcd}+\overline{bcda}=1001a+1100b+110c+11d=11(91a+100b+10c+d),\]
a multiple of $11$. Among the reports, only $9867=11\cdot897$ is divisible by $11$ ($8612$, $4322$, $13859$ leave remainders $10,10,10$).
