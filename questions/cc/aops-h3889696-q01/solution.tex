Write the number as $100a+10b+c$, with $1\le a\le9$ and $0\le b,c\le9$. Its digit sum is $13$ or $26$. Put $a+b+c=13t$, where $t=1$ or $2$. Then
\[
100a+10b+c=99a+9b+13t\equiv8a+9b\pmod{13},
\]
so $b\equiv2a\pmod{13}$. For $a=1,\ldots,9$, the admissible pairs $(a,b)$ with $b\le9$ are
\[
(1,2),(2,4),(3,6),(4,8),(7,1),(8,3),(9,5).
\]
Testing $c=13t-a-b$ yields exactly $247,364,481,715,832$. Thus there are $\boxed5$ numbers.
