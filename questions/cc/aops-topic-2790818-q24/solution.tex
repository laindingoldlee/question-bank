Let $y=x(1-x)\in\left[0,\dfrac14\right]$. Then $y\ge t$ exactly when $x$ lies between the roots of $x^2-x+t=0$, which happens with probability $\sqrt{1-4t}$. The density of $y$, $\dfrac2{\sqrt{1-4t}}$, increases with $t$, so the best window of width $\dfrac1{25}$ is the top one, $\left[\dfrac14-\dfrac1{25},\dfrac14\right]$ (guess $\dfrac{23}{100}$). The winning probability is $\sqrt{1-4\cdot\dfrac{21}{100}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{16}{100}}=\boxed{\dfrac25}$.
