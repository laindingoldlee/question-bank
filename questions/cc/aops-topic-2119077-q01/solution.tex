\textbf{Second equation.} Rearranging, $yz-z=y^2-1$, that is, $z(y-1)=(y-1)(y+1)$. Since $y>1729$, $y-1\ne0$, so $z=y+1$.

\textbf{First equation.} By the difference of cubes, $y^3-x^3=(y-x)\left(x^2+xy+y^2\right)$, so the equation says
\[\left(x^2+xy+y^2\right)(y-x-1)=0 .\]
Since $x,y>0$, $x^2+xy+y^2>0$, so $y=x+1$.

Hence the solutions are exactly $(x,x+1,x+2)$. All three coordinates lie strictly between $1729$ and $1999$ exactly when $x\ge1730$ and $x+2\le1998$, that is, $1730\le x\le1996$. That gives
\[1996-1730+1=\boxed{267}\]
triples.
