A quotient $\dfrac{a}{b}$ is the unique number $q$ satisfying $bq=a$, when such a unique number exists. For $a=b=0$, every number satisfies $0q=0$, so there is no unique quotient. The identity $\dfrac{a}{a}=1$ requires $a\ne0$ and does not apply. Thus $\dfrac{0}{0}$ is undefined in ordinary arithmetic.
