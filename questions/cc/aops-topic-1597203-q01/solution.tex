By the quadratic formula with $a=1$, $b=-1$, $c=-1$,
\[
x=\dfrac{1\pm\sqrt{(-1)^2-4\cdot1\cdot(-1)}}{2}=\dfrac{1\pm\sqrt5}{2}.
\]
So the solutions are
\[
\boxed{x=\dfrac{1+\sqrt5}{2}\quad\text{or}\quad x=\dfrac{1-\sqrt5}{2}}.
\]
(The positive root $\dfrac{1+\sqrt5}{2}\approx1.618$ is the golden ratio; the equation says $x^2=x+1$, i.e.\ $x=1+\dfrac{1}{x}$ for either root.)
