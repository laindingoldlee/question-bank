There is a linear polynomial $f(x)$ with $f(2)=6$ and $f(8)=18$. Let $q(x)=\left(f(x)\right)^{10}$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find all solutions of $q(x)=0$, and the sum of the distinct solutions.
\item[(b)] Find the sum of the roots of the polynomial $q(x)$, counted with multiplicity.
\end{enumerate}
