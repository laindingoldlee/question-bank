The gaps between neighbouring bars of a jail window are $6$, $7$, $5.5$, $8$, $8.5$ and $8$ centimetres wide. A person can squeeze through a gap if his thickness is at most $0.5$ centimetre more than the width of the gap. Glerp can squeeze through exactly three of the six gaps. What is the largest possible thickness of Glerp?
