Assume independent rolls of a six-sided die labelled 1 through 6. The prime-roll probability is $p=\dfrac{(2^2+3^2+5^2)}{(1^2+\cdots+6^2)}=\dfrac{38}{91}$. If Jane fails and May fails, play restarts. Thus Jane' s win probability $w$ obeys $w=p+(1-p)^2w$, giving $w=\dfrac{1}{(2-p)}=\dfrac{91}{144}$. Therefore $\boxed{m+n=235}$.
