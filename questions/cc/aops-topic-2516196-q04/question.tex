The lines $y=3x-2$, $y=5x+6$ and $y=2x$ are drawn in the coordinate plane, and their pairwise intersection points are $A$, $B$ and $C$. A second plane meets the coordinate plane along the line $x=5$ and makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with it. Through each of $A$, $B$, $C$, a line perpendicular to the coordinate plane is drawn, meeting the second plane at $A'$, $B'$, $C'$. The area of triangle $A'B'C'$ can be written as $\dfrac{x\sqrt y}{z}$, where $x,y,z$ are positive integers, $\gcd(x,z)=1$ and $y$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. Find $xyz$.
