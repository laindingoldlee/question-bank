Either one child gets three prizes, or two children get two each.
\begin{itemize}
\item One triple: choose it in $\dbinom73=35$ ways; then $5$ bundles go to $5$ children in $120$ ways: $4200$.
\item Two pairs: choose the two unordered pairs of prizes in $\dfrac{1}{2}\dbinom72\dbinom52=105$ ways; then $5$ bundles in $120$ ways: $12600$.
\end{itemize}
Total: $4200+12600=\boxed{16800}$. (By inclusion--exclusion: $\sum_{j=0}^{5}(-1)^j\dbinom5j(5-j)^7=16800$.)
