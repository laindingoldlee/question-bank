The first score is 3. The second exceeds it when $\dfrac{40}{n^2}>3 $, or $ n^2<\dfrac{40}{3}$. Since $ 3^2<\dfrac{40}{3}<4^2 $, the worst qualifying place is $\boxed3 $.
