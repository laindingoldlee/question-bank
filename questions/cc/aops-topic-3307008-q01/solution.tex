Every even $n$ works because the exponent is even: $1012$ choices. For odd $n$, the base $3n$ must itself be a square, by prime factorisation. Equivalently $n=3k^2$ for odd $k$. The bound gives $k\le25$, yielding $13$ possibilities. Total $\boxed{1025}$.
