Thales' s theorem gives $CA^2+CB^2=AB^2=4r^2$. By Cauchy--Schwarz, $(CA+CB)^2\le2(CA^2+CB^2)=8r^2$, with equality exactly when $CA=CB$. Thus the maximum is $\boxed{2\sqrt2r}$, attained at either midpoint of the two semicircular arcs from $A$ to $B$.

\end{asy}
