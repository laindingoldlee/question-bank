For $x=0,1,2,3$, the expression is negative. At $x=4$ it is $700$, lying strictly between $26^2=676$ and $27^2=729$. At $x=5$ it is $14\cdot6\cdot21=1764=42^2$. Thus the least value is $\boxed5$.
