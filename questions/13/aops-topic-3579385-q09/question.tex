Fill each cell of a $4\times4$ grid with A, B, C, or D so that in every row and column the numbers of A and B agree, and the numbers of C and D agree. If there are $N$ fillings, find $N\bmod7$. Choices: (A) $0$; (B) $1$; (C) $3$; (D) $4$; (E) $6$.
