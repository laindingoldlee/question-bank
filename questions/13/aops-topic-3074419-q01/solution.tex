Put $r=\dfrac{AX}{BX}>0$. Dividing the condition by $BX^2$ gives $r^2=r+1$. The positive root is $\boxed{\dfrac{1+\sqrt5}{2}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
real r=(1+sqrt(5))/2;pair A=(0,0),X=(r,0),B=(r+1,0);draw(A--B);dot(A);dot(X);dot(B);label("$A$",A,S);label("$X$",X,S);label("$B$",B,S);label("$r$",(r/2,0),N);label("$1$",((2r+1)/2,0),N);
\end{asy}
