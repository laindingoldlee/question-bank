The five factors are distinct nonzero integers. Their sorted absolute values are at least $1,1,2,2,3$, whose product already equals $12$. Thus the factors are $1,-1,2,-2$ and either $3$ or $-3$. Their product is positive only with $3$. Their sum is $3$, so $45-(a+b+c+d+e)=3$, giving $\boxed{42}$.
