Putting $x=y=0$ gives $f(0)=f(0)+f(0)$, so $f(0)=0$.

The sum of the coefficients of a polynomial is its value at $1$, so $f(1)=3$. Putting $x=y=1$ gives $f(2)=f(1)+f(1)=6$. Hence
\[f(2)+f(0)=\boxed{6}.\]
(In fact $f(x)=3x$: by induction $f(n)=nf(1)=3n$ for every positive integer $n$, so the polynomial $f(x)-3x$ has infinitely many roots and must be the zero polynomial.)
