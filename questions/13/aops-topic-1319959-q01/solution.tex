Every dart score leaves remainder $2$ when divided by $3$, so the total of six darts leaves remainder $12\equiv0$: the total is a multiple of $3$. It also lies between $6\cdot2=12$ and $6\cdot8=48$. Among the choices only $36$ and $51$ are multiples of $3$, and $51>48$. So only $\boxed{36}$ is possible, for example $2+2+8+8+8+8=36$.
