Let Will be $s$ and me $11s+1$ in January 2000. Nine years later,
\[11s+1+9=3(s+9)+7\iff11s+10=3s+34\iff8s=24\iff s=3.\]
Will was $\boxed{3}$ years old (and I was $34$; in 2009 we were $43$ and $12$, and $43=3\cdot12+7$).
