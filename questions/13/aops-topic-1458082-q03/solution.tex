Choose the $5$ states that have a finalist, in $\dbinom{50}{5}=2118760$ ways, and then one of the two participants from each chosen state, in $2^5=32$ ways. The number of sets is
\[
\dbinom{50}{5}\cdot2^5=2118760\cdot32=\boxed{67800320}.
\]
The product $100\cdot98\cdot96\cdot94\cdot92$ counts \emph{ordered} lists of finalists (first, second, $\dots$, fifth). Each set of five finalists appears in $5!=120$ different orders, so the product is $120$ times too large:
\[
\dfrac{100\cdot98\cdot96\cdot94\cdot92}{120}=67800320 .
\]
