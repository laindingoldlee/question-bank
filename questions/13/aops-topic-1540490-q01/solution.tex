Let the fulcrum be $d$ feet from Shandra's seat, so it is $14-d$ feet from her sister's seat. Balance requires
\[
96d=72(14-d)\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad 168d=1008\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad d=\boxed{6}\text{ feet}.
\]
(Check: $96\cdot6=576=72\cdot8$.)
