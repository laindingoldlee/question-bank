A seesaw is in balance when the weight on one side of the fulcrum times its distance from the fulcrum equals the weight on the other side times its distance from the fulcrum. Shandra weighs $96$ pounds and her little sister weighs $72$ pounds. The seesaw has seats $14$ feet apart, and the position of the fulcrum can be adjusted. If each girl sits in her seat, how far from Shandra's seat should the fulcrum be for perfect balance?
