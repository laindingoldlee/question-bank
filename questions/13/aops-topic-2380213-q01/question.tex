Three friends agree to meet in front of a stadium. Each of them arrives at a time chosen uniformly at random between 3 p.m. and 4 p.m., independently of the others, and each waits at most $30$ minutes for the others (and leaves at 4 p.m. at the latest). What is the probability that all three friends are together at some moment?
