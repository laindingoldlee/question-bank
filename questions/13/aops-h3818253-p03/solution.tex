Neither exponent can be zero. Modulo $3$, $x$ is odd; modulo $8$, this forces $y$ odd. Odd exponents modulo $6$ give $5^x\pmod7$ in the ordered list $5,6,3$ and $3^y\pmod7$ in $3,6,5$. The difference is $2$ only when $x\equiv y\equiv1\pmod6$. If $y\ge2$, reduction modulo $9$ would give $5^x\equiv2$, hence $x\equiv5\pmod6$, a contradiction. Thus $y=1$, and $\boxed{(x,y)=(1,1)}$.
