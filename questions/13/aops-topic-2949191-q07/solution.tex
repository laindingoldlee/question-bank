Write the downward-rounded values as $10a,10b$, with positive integers $ab=12$. The upward values are at most $10(a+1),10(b+1)$, so $N\le100(13+a+b)$. Among factor pairs of 12, the greatest sum is 13 from 1 and 12. Taking both original numbers strictly above their lower multiples attains $N=100\cdot26=\boxed{2600}$.
