$12=2^2\cdot3$ and $18=2\cdot3^2$. The GCF takes the smaller powers: $2\cdot3=6$; the LCM takes the larger: $2^2\cdot3^2=36$. (Check: $6\cdot36=216=12\cdot18$.) The sum is $6+36=\boxed{42}$.
