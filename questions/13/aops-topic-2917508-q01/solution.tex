This is the subtractive Euclidean algorithm: $(810,750)\to(60,750)\to(60,690)\to\cdots\to(60,30)\to(30,30)$. The gcd is preserved at each step and the process stops when the cakes are equal, i.e. at $\gcd(750,810)=30$. Each cat gets $\boxed{30\text{ g}}$.
