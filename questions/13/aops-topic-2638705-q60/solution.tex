Let $r=\{x\}$ and $n=x-r$. For integer $x$, floor and ceiling are $n$, so their product is $n^2=\boxed{x^2+r^2-2xr}$. For noninteger $x$, their product is $n(n+1)=(x-r)(x-r+1)=\boxed{x^2+x+r^2-(2x+1)r}$.
