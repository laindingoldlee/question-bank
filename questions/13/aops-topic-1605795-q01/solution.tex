Let Joel be $j$ years old and let $d$ be the common difference, so Evan is $j+d$ and Alex is $j-d$. Then
\[
(j+d)+j+(j-d)=3j=39,
\]
and Joel is $j=\boxed{13}$ years old. (The ages form an arithmetic sequence, and the middle term is the average.)
