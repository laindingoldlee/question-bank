Condition on Jack's roll.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Jack rolls a double} (probability $\dfrac{6}{36}$): Jill must roll the same double, one outcome of $36$: probability $\dfrac{1}{36}$.
\item \textbf{Jack rolls two different numbers} (probability $\dfrac{30}{36}$): Jill must roll these two numbers in either order, $2$ outcomes of $36$: probability $\dfrac{2}{36}$.
\end{itemize}
Hence
\[
P=\dfrac{6}{36}\cdot\dfrac{1}{36}+\dfrac{30}{36}\cdot\dfrac{2}{36}=\dfrac{6+60}{1296}=\dfrac{66}{1296}=\boxed{\dfrac{11}{216}}.
\]
