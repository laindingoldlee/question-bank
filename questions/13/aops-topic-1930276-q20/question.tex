For a prime $p$, let $X_p$ be the $9p$-digit number
\[
\underbrace{11\cdots1}_{p}\underbrace{22\cdots2}_{p}\underbrace{33\cdots3}_{p}\cdots\underbrace{99\cdots9}_{p},
\]
in which each nonzero digit is written exactly $p$ times. Prove that $X_p-123456789$ is divisible by $p$.
