A composite $n$ has a divisor no larger than $\sqrt n$: otherwise both factors of a nontrivial factorization would exceed $\sqrt n$. Therefore trial division up to the integer square root is complete.
\begin{verbatim}
from math import isqrt
p=[n for n in range(2,1000)
   if all(n%d for d in range(2,isqrt(n)+1))]
assert len(p)==168 and p[-1]==997
\end{verbatim}
The answers are $\boxed{168}$ primes and largest $\boxed{997}$. This avoids modifying a list while iterating over it.
