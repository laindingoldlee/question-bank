There are $4!=24$ such numbers. In each of the four places, each digit appears in $\dfrac{24}{4}=6$ of them. So each place contributes $6(1+2+3+4)=60$ times its place value, and the sum is
\[
60\cdot(1000+100+10+1)=60\cdot1111=\boxed{66660}.
\]
