Bill, Bob, and Bo each independently pick a real number uniformly at random between \(0\) and \(6\), inclusive. The probability that the sum of the squares of their three numbers is at most \(20\) can be written as \(\dfrac{a\sqrt b\,\pi}{c}\), where \(a,b,c\) are positive integers, \(\gcd(a,c)=1\), and \(b\) is not divisible by the square of any prime. Find \(a+b+c\).
