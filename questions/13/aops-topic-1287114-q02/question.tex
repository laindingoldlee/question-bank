Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. Which of the following is always true?
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] All angles formed are obtuse.
\item[(b)] All exterior angles formed are acute.
\item[(c)] The number of exterior angles formed equals the number of interior angles formed.
\item[(d)] The sum of all the angles formed is $360^\circ$.
\end{enumerate}
