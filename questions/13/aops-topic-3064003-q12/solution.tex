The possible target points are $(\pm1,\pm1)$. To reach $(1,1)$ in four steps, the directions must be two east, one west, one north, or one east, two north, one south. Each multiset has $\dfrac{4!}{2!}=12$ orders. By symmetry there are $4\cdot24=96$ successful walks among $4^4=256$, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{3}{8}}$.
