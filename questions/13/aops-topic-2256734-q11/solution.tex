Let the side be $4$, with $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $C=(4,4)$, $D=(0,4)$. Then $P=(3,0)$, $Q=(4,3)$, $R=(3,4)$ and $S=(0,3)$. So $PR$ is the vertical line $x=3$, $SQ$ is the horizontal line $y=3$, and $X=(3,3)$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0), B=(4,0), C=(4,4), D=(0,4), P=(3,0), Q=(4,3), R=(3,4), T=(0,3), X=(3,3);
fill(P--X--T--cycle,rgb(0.85,0.92,1));
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); draw(P--R); draw(T--Q); draw(P--T,blue+linewidth(0.9));
dot(P); dot(Q); dot(R); dot(T); dot(X);
label("$A$",A,SW); label("$B$",B,SE); label("$C$",C,NE); label("$D$",D,NW);
label("$P$",P,S); label("$Q$",Q,E); label("$R$",R,N); label("$S$",T,W); label("$X$",X,NE);
label("$3$",(1.5,0),S,fontsize(8pt)); label("$1$",(3.5,0),S,fontsize(8pt)); label("$3$",(0,1.5),W,fontsize(8pt)); label("$1$",(0,3.5),W,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}

Triangle $PXS$ has a right angle at $X$ with legs $PX=3$ and $XS=3$, so its area is $\dfrac92$, and the ratio is $\dfrac{9}{2\cdot16}=\dfrac{9}{32}$. Hence $m+n=\boxed{\textbf{(E) }41}$.
