Points $P$, $Q$, $R$, $S$ lie on sides $\overline{AB}$, $\overline{BC}$, $\overline{CD}$, $\overline{DA}$, respectively, of square $ABCD$, with $PA=3\cdot PB$, $QB=3\cdot QC$, $RD=3\cdot RC$ and $SA=3\cdot SD$. Segments $\overline{PR}$, $\overline{SQ}$ and $\overline{PS}$ are drawn, and $\overline{PR}$ meets $\overline{SQ}$ at $X$. The ratio of the area of triangle $PXS$ to the area of square $ABCD$ is $\dfrac mn$ for relatively prime positive integers $m$ and $n$. What is $m+n$?

$\textbf{(A) }11\qquad\textbf{(B) }25\qquad\textbf{(C) }31\qquad\textbf{(D) }34\qquad\textbf{(E) }41$
