Let $I$ be a point inside triangle $ABC$. Points $M$, $N$, $P$ lie on the sides $AC$, $AB$, $BC$ respectively, and
\[
MN\parallel BI,\qquad MP\parallel AI,\qquad PN\parallel CI .
\]
Prove that
\[
\dfrac{AM}{AC}+\dfrac{BN}{BA}+\dfrac{CP}{CB}=1 .
\]
