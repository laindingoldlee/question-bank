All $\dbinom83=56$ position sets for the three nickels are equally likely. The complement event is that all three nickels are among the first four draws, i.e.\ the nickel positions form a $3$-element subset of $\{1,2,3,4\}$. There are $\dbinom43=4$ such subsets:
\[NNNQ\ldots,\quad NNQN\ldots,\quad NQNN\ldots,\quad QNNN\ldots\]
The student forgot $NNNQQQQQ$ (all three nickels in the first three draws). So the probability is
\[1-\dfrac{4}{56}=1-\dfrac{1}{14}=\boxed{\dfrac{13}{14}} .\]
