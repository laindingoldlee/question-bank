Every time a box is filled with $8$ boxes, the number of empty boxes goes up by $7$ (one stops being empty, eight new empty ones appear), and the total goes up by $8$. Starting from $11$ empty boxes, $102=11+7k$ gives $k=13$ fillings. The total is $11+8\cdot13=\boxed{115}$.
