The possible multiples are $3k$ with $1\le k\le66$. Such a representation is equivalent to $k$ being the sum of two distinct positive integers. Every $k\ge3$ works as $1+(k-1)$, while $1,2$ do not. Thus there are $66-2=\boxed{64}$.
