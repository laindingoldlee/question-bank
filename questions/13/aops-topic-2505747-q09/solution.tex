The number $N$ satisfies $N\equiv2\pmod9$ and $N\equiv5\pmod{11}$. The numbers $\equiv5\pmod{11}$ are $5,16,27,38,\dots$, with remainders $5,7,0,2$ on division by $9$. So the smallest is $N=\boxed{38}$ (by the Chinese remainder theorem, the solutions are $N\equiv38\pmod{99}$).
