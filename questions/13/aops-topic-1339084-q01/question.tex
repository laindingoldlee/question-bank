Jo, Po and Mo each pick a positive number. Jo says, ``My number is Po's number times Mo's number, divided by $2$.'' Po says, ``Jo's number times Mo's number, divided by $4$, is my number.'' Mo says, ``Jo's number times Po's number, divided by $8$, is equal to my number.'' If Jo's number is $J$, Po's is $P$ and Mo's is $M$, find the ordered triple $(J,P,M)$.
