The statements say
\[
J=\dfrac{PM}{2},\qquad P=\dfrac{JM}{4},\qquad M=\dfrac{JP}{8}.
\]
Multiplying the three equations gives $JPM=\dfrac{(JPM)^2}{64}$, and since $JPM>0$, $JPM=64$.

Now multiply the first equation by $J$: $J^2=\dfrac{JPM}{2}=32$, so $J=4\sqrt2$. Similarly $P^2=\dfrac{JPM}{4}=16$, so $P=4$, and $M^2=\dfrac{JPM}{8}=8$, so $M=2\sqrt2$ (all positive).

Check: $\dfrac{PM}{2}=\dfrac{4\cdot2\sqrt2}{2}=4\sqrt2=J$, $\dfrac{JM}{4}=\dfrac{4\sqrt2\cdot2\sqrt2}{4}=4=P$, $\dfrac{JP}{8}=\dfrac{16\sqrt2}{8}=2\sqrt2=M$. So
\[
(J,P,M)=\boxed{\left(4\sqrt2,\ 4,\ 2\sqrt2\right)}.
\]
