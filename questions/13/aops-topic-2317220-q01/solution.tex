First,
\[ab+bc+ca=\dfrac{(a+b+c)^2-(a^2+b^2+c^2)}{2}=\dfrac{9-6}{2}=\dfrac32.\]
Next use the identity
\[a^3+b^3+c^3-3abc=(a+b+c)\left(a^2+b^2+c^2-ab-bc-ca\right).\]
It gives $10-3abc=3\left(6-\dfrac32\right)=\dfrac{27}{2}$, so $3abc=10-\dfrac{27}{2}=-\dfrac72$ and
\[abc=\boxed{-\dfrac76}.\]
(Such real numbers exist: $a$, $b$, $c$ are the roots of $t^3-3t^2+\dfrac32t+\dfrac76=0$, which has three real roots, approximately $-0.405$, $1.564$ and $1.841$.)
