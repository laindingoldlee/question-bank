The seated people form maximal blocks of consecutive occupied chairs. A person raises a hand exactly when their block has at least $2$ people. Suppose there are $k\ge1$ blocks of size at least $2$, containing the $50$ hand-raisers, and $m$ isolated people (blocks of size $1$). Different blocks are separated by at least one empty chair, so
\[
150\ \ge\ 50+m+(k+m-1)\ \ge\ 50+2m ,
\]
which gives $m\le50$. So at most $50+50=100$ people are present.

$100$ is possible: seat $50$ people in chairs $1$ to $50$ and one person in each of the chairs $52,54,\dots,150$ ($50$ chairs). The people in chairs $1$ to $50$ raise their hands, and nobody else has a neighbour. The answer is $\boxed{100}$.
