The lines determine six distinct rays from $ O $, dividing a full turn into six sectors. Thus the disk has $\boxed6 $ regions, choice A. They cannot produce a seventh central triangle because all three lines are concurrent.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw(circle((0,0),1));for(int k=0;k<3;++k)draw(-1.2dir(60*k)--1.2dir(60*k));dot("$O$",(0,0),SW);for(int k=0;k<6;++k)label(string(k+1),.65dir(30+60*k));
\end{asy}
