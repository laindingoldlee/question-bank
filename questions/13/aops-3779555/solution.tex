Let the inradius be $r$. Equal tangent lengths give legs $20+r$ and $24+r$, and hypotenuse $44$. Pythagoras gives $(20+r)^2+(24+r)^2=44^2$, or $r^2+44r=480$. The area is $\dfrac{(20+r)(24+r)}{2}=\dfrac{480+44r+r^2}{2}=\boxed{480}$.
