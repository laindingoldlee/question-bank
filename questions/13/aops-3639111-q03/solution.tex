Work backward, counting the proposer's own affirmative vote. With D and E, D keeps everything because a tied vote passes. With C,D,E, C buys E's vote for one dollar, because E otherwise gets zero. With B,C,D,E, B buys D's vote for one dollar. Thus if A is rejected, C and E each receive zero. A can buy both for one dollar each, securing three of five votes and keeping $694201434-2=\boxed{694201432}$ dollars. The allocation to A,B,C,D,E is $(694201432,0,1,0,1)$. Two outside votes are necessary and each costs at least one, so this is optimal.
