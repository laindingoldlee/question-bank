A double root means the discriminant is zero:
\[
\left(\dfrac{1}{k}\right)^2-4\cdot\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}=0\iff\dfrac{1}{k^2}=\dfrac{1}{3}\iff k^2=3 .
\]
So $k=\boxed{\pm\sqrt3}$.
