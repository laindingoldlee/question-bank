The last three base-$4$ digits are the base-$4$ digits of the remainder modulo $4^3=64$. Now $10\equiv10$, $3195=49\cdot64+59\equiv59\equiv-5$ and $4949=77\cdot64+21\equiv21\pmod{64}$. So
\[
10\cdot3195\cdot4949\equiv10\cdot(-5)\cdot21=-1050\equiv-1050+17\cdot64=38\pmod{64}.
\]
Since $38=2\cdot16+1\cdot4+2$, the last three digits are $\boxed{212}$.
