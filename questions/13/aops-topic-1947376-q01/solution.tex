All $6^6=46656$ sequences of rolls are equally likely. The favourable sequences are the orderings of $1,2,\dots,6$, of which there are $6!=720$. So the probability is
\[
\dfrac{720}{46656}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{324}} .
\]
