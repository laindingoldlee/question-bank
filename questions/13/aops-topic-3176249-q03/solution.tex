Each midsegment is parallel to the third side and half its length, hence has length 1. The perimeter is $\boxed3$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(1,sqrt(3)),B=(0,0),C=(2,0),D=(A+B)/2,Ee=(B+C)/2,F=(C+A)/2;draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(D--Ee--F--cycle,blue);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$E$",Ee,S);label("$F$",F,NE);
\end{asy}
