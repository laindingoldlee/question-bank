Since $2000=166\cdot12+8$, we have $2000^{2000}\equiv 8^{2000}\pmod{12}$. Now $8^2=64\equiv4$ and $8\cdot 4=32\equiv 8$, $4\cdot 4=16\equiv 4\pmod{12}$, so the powers of $8$ alternate $8,4,8,4,\dots$, with even powers $\equiv 4$. Hence the remainder is $\boxed{4}$.

(Check: $2000^{2000}$ is divisible by $4$, and $2000\equiv 2\pmod 3$ gives $2^{2000}=4^{1000}\equiv1\pmod 3$; the number in $\{0,\dots,11\}$ that is $0\bmod4$ and $1\bmod 3$ is $4$.)
