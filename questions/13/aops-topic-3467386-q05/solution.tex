Let each elf make $k$ presents. Since $9k$ ends in 5, $k$ ends in 5. For minimality, test $k=5,15,25,35,45,55$; their elevenfold totals are $55,165,275,385,495,605$, with digit sums $10,12,14,16,18,11$. The first valid value is $k=55$, and $9k=495$ indeed ends in 5. Thus the minimum removed is $2k=\boxed{110}$.
