The choices are independent, each odd with probability $\dfrac12$, so the probability is $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac12=\boxed{\dfrac14}$. (Equivalently, $30^2$ of the $60^2$ equally likely ordered pairs are both odd.)

The multisets are not equally likely: an unordered pair of different numbers $\{a,b\}$ arises from two ordered outcomes, $(a,b)$ and $(b,a)$, while $\{a,a\}$ arises from only one. Dividing counts of multisets therefore does not give a probability. (The student's ratio is $\dfrac{465}{1830}=\dfrac{31}{122}\ne\dfrac14$.)
