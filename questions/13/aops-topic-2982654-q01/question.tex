Two numbers are chosen independently and uniformly at random from $\{1,2,\dots,60\}$ (repetition allowed). What is the probability that both are odd? A student argues: ``there are $\binom{31}{2}$ multisets of two odd numbers and $\binom{61}{2}$ multisets of two numbers, so the probability is $\dfrac{\binom{31}{2}}{\binom{61}{2}}$.'' Find the correct probability and explain the error.
