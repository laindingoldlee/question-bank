No. Suppose $2^m$ contains each digit exactly $k\ge1$ times. Then its digit sum is $k(0+1+\cdots+9)=45k$, which is divisible by $9$, so $2^m$ is divisible by $9$ and in particular by $3$. But the only prime factor of $2^m$ is $2$: a contradiction. $\blacksquare$
