Factor $2018=2\cdot1009$, and $1009$ is prime (it is not divisible by any prime up to $31$, and $32^2=1024>1009$). So $2018^n\mid2018!$ exactly when both $2^n$ and $1009^n$ divide $2018!$.

The exponent of the prime $1009$ in $2018!$ is
\[
\left\lfloor\dfrac{2018}{1009}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{2018}{1009^2}\right\rfloor=2+0=2,
\]
coming from the factors $1009$ and $2018$. The exponent of $2$ is far larger (already $\left\lfloor\dfrac{2018}{2}\right\rfloor=1009$ factors are even). So the limiting prime is $1009$ and the answer is $n=\boxed{2}$.
