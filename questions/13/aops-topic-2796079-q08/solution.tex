$(x-y)(x+y)=40$ with $0<x-y<x+y$ of the same parity, so both are even: $(2,20)$ or $(4,10)$. These give $(x,y)=(11,9)$ and $(7,3)$. The largest $x-y$ is $\boxed{4}$.
