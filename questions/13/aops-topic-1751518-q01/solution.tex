Since $M\ge x$ and $M\ge y$, we get $2M\ge x+y\ge9$, so $M\ge\dfrac{9}{2}$, and as $M$ is an integer, $M\ge5$. The pair $(x,y)=(4,5)$ has $x+y=9$ and $M=5$, so no larger bound holds. The answer is $\boxed{5}$.
