For each integer $k$ from $1$ to $25$, the point $\left(k,\,k+\left\lfloor\sqrt k\right\rfloor\right)$ is plotted. Two of these $25$ points are chosen at random and joined by a segment of slope $s$. The probability that $1<s<2$ is $\dfrac mn$ in lowest terms. Find $m+n$.
