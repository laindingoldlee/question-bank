Write $f(x)=\sum_k c_kx^k$. Then $f(x)-f(a)=\sum_k c_k\left(x^k-a^k\right)$, and each term factors:
\[
x^k-a^k=(x-a)\left(x^{k-1}+x^{k-2}a+\dots+a^{k-1}\right).
\]
So $f(x)-f(a)=(x-a)g(x)$ with $g$ a polynomial whose coefficients are integer combinations of powers of $a$. For integers $x$ and $a$, $g(x)$ is an integer, so $x-a\mid f(x)-f(a)$. $\blacksquare$
