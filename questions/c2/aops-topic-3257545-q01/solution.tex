For $n=1,\ldots,998$, the concatenation lies from $12$ through $998999$, within the interval. At $n=999$ it is $9991000$, already too large. Concatenation is strictly increasing in $n$: between digit-length changes this is immediate, and a length increase makes a larger leading part followed by at least as many digits. Thus there are no later valid values and no duplicates. The count is $\boxed{998}$.
