The midpoint of both diameters $BC$ and $MN$ is the circle's centre, so in vector notation $M+N=B+C$. Since $2M=A+B$, we get $N-M=C-A$. Thus $MN$ is parallel and equal to $AC$. The line through $M$ parallel to $AC$ is halfway between $B$ and the line $AC$, because $M$ is the midpoint of $AB$. Hence the perpendicular distance from $C$ to $MN$ is half the distance from $B$ to $AC$. Comparing areas using the equal bases $MN,AC$ proves $[MNC]=\dfrac{1}{2}[ABC]$.
