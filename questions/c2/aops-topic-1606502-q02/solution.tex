Let $R=\underbrace{11\ldots1}_{22}$. Then
\[
9R=\underbrace{99\ldots9}_{22}=10^{22}-1 .
\]
Since $23$ is prime and does not divide $10$, Fermat's little theorem gives $10^{22}\equiv1\pmod{23}$, so $23\mid 10^{22}-1=9R$. As $\gcd(9,23)=1$, it follows that $23\mid R$. $\blacksquare$

(Check: $R=23\cdot48\,309\,178\,743\,961\,352\,657$.)
