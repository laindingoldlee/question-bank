In a game, each reap is worth $5$ minutes, except that with probability $10\%$ it is doubled and worth $10$ minutes. Independently, each reap gives a ``free reap'' with probability $5\%$: the player immediately reaps again, and the new reap follows the same rules. A player reaps once and keeps reaping as long as she gets free reaps. What is the expected total time she gains?
