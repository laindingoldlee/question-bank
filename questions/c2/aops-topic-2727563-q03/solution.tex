Let $h(t)=pt^2+qt$ with $h(1)=3$ and $h(2)=4$. Then $p+q=3$ and $4p+2q=4$, so $p=-1$, $q=4$ and $h(t)=4t-t^2$. Setting $4t-t^2=-221$ gives $t^2-4t-221=0$, i.e. $(t-17)(t+13)=0$, so $t=17$. The rock has moved $\boxed{17}$ ft east.
