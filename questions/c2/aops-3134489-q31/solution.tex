Subtract one: $a_{n+1}-1=(a_n-1)^2$, so $a_n=2^{2^{n-1}}+1$. Repeated difference-of-squares factoring gives $\prod_{n=1}^{2012}a_n=2^{2^{2012}}-1$. Since $2^{2012}\equiv16\pmod{20}$, the leading power is 36 modulo 100 (use modulo 25 with period 20, and modulo 4). Hence the requested remainder is $\boxed{35}$.
