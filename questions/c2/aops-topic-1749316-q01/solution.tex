Multiplying by $40$ gives $5x+8y=31$. Since $x\ge1$, $8y\le26$, so $y\in\{1,2,3\}$: $y=1$ gives $5x=23$, $y=3$ gives $5x=7$ (no integer $x$), and $y=2$ gives $x=3$. The only solution is $(x,y)=(3,2)$, so $x+y=\boxed{5}$.
