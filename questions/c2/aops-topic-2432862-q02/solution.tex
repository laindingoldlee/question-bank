A purchase is determined by how many of each fruit are bought: $0$ or $1$ strawberry ($2$ choices), likewise $2$ choices each for the lemon and the peach, $0$ to $3$ apples ($4$ choices), $0$ to $4$ oranges ($5$ choices) and $0$ to $5$ berries ($6$ choices). That gives $2\cdot2\cdot2\cdot4\cdot5\cdot6=960$ selections, one of which is the empty one. So there are $960-1=\boxed{959}$ ways.
