LemonBerry goes to a store to buy fruit. The grocer has only $1$ strawberry, $1$ lemon, $1$ peach, $3$ apples, $4$ oranges and $5$ berries left in stock, and fruits of the same type are indistinguishable. In how many ways can LemonBerry buy at least $1$ fruit?
