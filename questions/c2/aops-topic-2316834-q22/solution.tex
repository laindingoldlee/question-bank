\textbf{(a)} Set $-0.08x^2+1.6x+10=15$: $0.08x^2-1.6x+5=0$, or $x^2-20x+62.5=0$. So
\[x=10\pm\sqrt{100-62.5}=10\pm\sqrt{37.5}\approx10\pm6.12,\]
that is, $x\approx3.9$ or $x\approx16.1$. About $15$ tons were caught in about $\boxed{1999}$ and in about $\boxed{2011}$.

\textbf{(b)} No. The model is a downward parabola: $y=0$ when $x^2-20x-125=0$, that is, $x=25$, so it predicts that the catch falls to $0$ in 2020 and becomes negative afterwards, which is impossible. (It is also only fitted to the data from 1995 to 2014.)
