The amount $y$ (in tons) of trout caught in a lake from 1995 to 2014 can be modelled by $y=-0.08x^2+1.6x+10$, where $x$ is the number of years since 1995.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] In which years were about $15$ tons of trout caught?
\item[(b)] Can this model be used to predict the catch in future years? Explain.
\end{enumerate}
