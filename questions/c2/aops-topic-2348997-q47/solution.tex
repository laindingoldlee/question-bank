Let the hexagon have side $s$. It consists of six equilateral triangles of side $s$, and the inscribed circle has radius equal to their height $r=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}s$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair[] P;for(int i=0;i<6;++i)P[i]=dir(60*i);
draw(P[0]--P[1]--P[2]--P[3]--P[4]--P[5]--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
real r=sqrt(3)/2;draw(circle((0,0),r));
draw((0,0)--P[0]);draw((0,0)--(P[0]+P[1])/2,dashed);dot((0,0));
label("$s$",(P[0]+P[1])/2,NE);label("$r$",(P[0]+P[1])/4,NW,fontsize(8pt));label("$s$",P[0]/2,S,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}

\[R=\dfrac{\pi\cdot\dfrac34s^2}{6\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}s^2}=\dfrac{\pi}{2\sqrt3},\qquad R^2=\dfrac{\pi^2}{12}.\]
So $a=1$, $b=12$, and $a+b=\boxed{13}$.
