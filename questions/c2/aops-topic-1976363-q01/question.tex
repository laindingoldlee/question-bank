Prove that the number of ordered $k$-tuples $(x_1,\dots,x_k)$ of nonnegative integers with $x_1+x_2+\cdots+x_k=n$ is $\dbinom{n+k-1}{k-1}$. Use this to find the number of ordered quadruples of nonnegative integers $(a,b,c,d)$ with $a+b+c+d=10$.
