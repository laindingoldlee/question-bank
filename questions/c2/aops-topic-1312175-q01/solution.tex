$P$ is divisible by $15$ and $28$ exactly when it is a multiple of $\operatorname{lcm}(15,28)=420=2^2\cdot3\cdot5\cdot7$. Every such $P$ is divisible by $Q$ exactly when $Q\mid420$. The divisors of $420$ between $30$ and $48$ are $35$ and $42$, so the sum is $\boxed{77}$.
