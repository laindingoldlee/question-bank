From $A$ to $G$ there are $6$ equal gaps covering $20-2=18$, so each gap is $3$. Then $AC=2\cdot3=6$ and $DG=3\cdot3=9$, so $AC+DG=\boxed{15}$. (The points are at $2,5,8,11,14,17,20$.)
