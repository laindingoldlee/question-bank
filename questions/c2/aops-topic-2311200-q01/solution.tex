Look at remainders modulo $5$. If $p\ne5$, then $p\equiv1,2,3$ or $4\pmod5$, and $p^4\equiv1,16,81,256\equiv1\pmod5$ in each case. Then
\[p^4+34\equiv1+34=35\equiv0\pmod5,\]
so $p^4+34$ is a multiple of $5$ greater than $5$, and not prime.

Hence $p=5$, and then $p^4+34=625+34=659$. This is prime: it is not divisible by $2,3,5,7,11,13,17,19$ or $23$ (the remainders are $1,2,4,1,10,9,13,13,15$), and $29^2=841>659$. So $p=\boxed{5}$.
