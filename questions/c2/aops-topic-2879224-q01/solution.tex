Let $x=0.\overline{d_1\dots d_n}$. Then $10^nx=D.\overline{d_1\dots d_n}=D+x$, so $(10^n-1)x=D$ and $x=\dfrac D{10^n-1}$. $\blacksquare$ (Equivalently, $x=D\left(10^{-n}+10^{-2n}+\cdots\right)$, a geometric series.)
