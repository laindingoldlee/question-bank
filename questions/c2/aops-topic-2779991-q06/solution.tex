The example 7841 works because $ 784=28^2 $ and $ 841=29^2 $. Any larger candidate must begin with a three-digit square at least 784. Above 784 these squares are 841,900,961;900 contains zero, while no square from 410 through 419 or 610 through 619 exists. With prefix 784, the suffix must be the only square from 840 through 849, namely 841. Hence $\boxed{7841}$ is maximal.
