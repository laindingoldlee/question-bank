A square has an odd number of divisors, whose median is its odd square root, so cannot qualify. For a nonsquare, the two central divisors multiply to the number and must be odd positive integers summing to twelve. The pairs are $(1,11),(3,9),(5,7)$, giving candidate numbers $11,27,35$. The first is prime, whereas 27 has divisors $1,3,9,27$ and median six. It is the smallest valid candidate. Their sum is $\boxed{40}$.
