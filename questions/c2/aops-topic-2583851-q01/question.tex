Three forum users are viewing a thread. The first has $x$ days of school left, the second has $y$ days left, and the third has $t$ fewer than $x+y$ days left. Altogether $a$ users are viewing the thread, including these three, where $a\ge4$. For the other $a-3$ viewers, the average number of days of school left is $b$. Find the total number of days of school left for all $a$ viewers, in terms of $x$, $y$, $t$, $a$ and $b$.
