Factor $2520=2^3\cdot3^2\cdot5\cdot7$. Coprimality means each entire prime power must belong to exactly one of $m,n$. Each of the four distinct prime powers has two choices, independently. Thus there are $\boxed{2^4=16}$ ordered pairs, including $(1,2520)$ and its reversal.
