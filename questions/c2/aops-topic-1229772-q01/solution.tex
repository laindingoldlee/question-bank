The exponent of $3$ in $n!$ is $\left\lfloor\dfrac {n}{3}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac {n}{9}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac {n}{27}\right\rfloor+\cdots$. For $n=32$: $10+3+1=14$; for $n=33$: $11+3+1=15$. So $n=\boxed{33}$.
