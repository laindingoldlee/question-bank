There are $100$ students in the union. If $x$ is the math-history intersection, inclusion-exclusion gives
\[
100=48+40+48-24-10-x+6=108-x.
\]
Hence $\boxed{x=8}$. The exclusive regions are nonnegative: math only $22$, English only $12$, history only $36$, math-English only $18$, math-history only $2$, English-history only $4$, and all three $6$, confirming consistency.
