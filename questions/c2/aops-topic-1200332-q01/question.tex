A student argues: ``There are $N$ people and $2$ die every second, so I die in the next second with probability $\dfrac{2}{N}$. Next, the probability that I die in the second after that is $\dfrac{N-2}{N}\cdot\dfrac{2}{N+2}$, which is smaller, and the following second it is smaller still. So my chance of dying keeps falling, and I become more and more immortal.'' Find the error.
