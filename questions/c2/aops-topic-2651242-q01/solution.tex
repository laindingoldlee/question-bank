We have $x^4=81+\dfrac{361}{22}=\dfrac{2143}{22}=97.40909090909\ldots$, so
\[\pi^4-x^4=97.4090910340024\ldots-97.4090909090909\ldots=1.24911\ldots\times10^{-7}>0,\]
and therefore $x<\pi$. Factor
\[\pi-x=\dfrac{\pi^4-x^4}{(\pi+x)\left(\pi^2+x^2\right)}.\]
Since $3.1415^4=97.3976\ldots<x^4$, we have $3.1415<x<\pi<3.1416$, so the denominator lies between $4\cdot3.1415^3=124.0141\ldots$ and $4\cdot3.1416^3=124.0259\ldots$. The numerator lies between $1.249114\times10^{-7}$ and $1.249116\times10^{-7}$. Hence
\[1.00713\times10^{-9}<\pi-x<1.00725\times10^{-9},\]
and $\pi-x=\boxed{1.007\times10^{-9}}$ to four significant figures. (This is Ramanujan's approximation $\pi\approx\sqrt[4]{\dfrac{2143}{22}}$, correct to $8$ decimal places.)
