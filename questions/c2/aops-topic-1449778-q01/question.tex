An operation $*$ on the nonnegative integers (not commutative) satisfies, for all nonnegative integers $a,b$:
\[
0*b=b+1,\qquad (a+1)*0=a*1,\qquad (a+1)*(b+1)=a*\big((a+1)*b\big).
\]
Find $1*4$.
