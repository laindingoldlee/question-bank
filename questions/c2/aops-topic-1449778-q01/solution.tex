First, $1*0=0*1=2$. For $b\ge0$,
\[
1*(b+1)=0*(1*b)=1*b+1 .
\]
So $1*b$ increases by $1$ each time $b$ increases by $1$, and $1*b=b+2$. In particular
\[
1*4=\boxed{6}.
\]
(The rules define the Ackermann function; for example $2*b=2b+3$ and $3*b=2^{b+3}-3$.)
