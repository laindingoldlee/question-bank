There are $\binom62=15$ placements before identifying rotations. The nontrivial rotation preserving the rectangular grid is a half-turn. A two-red-cell placement is fixed precisely when the red cells form one of the three opposite pairs. Thus there are $3$ singleton orbits and $\dfrac{15-3}{2}=6$ two-element orbits, for a total of $\boxed9$.
