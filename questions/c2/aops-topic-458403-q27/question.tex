The numbers $1$ through $9$ are placed, one in each circle, in a triangular arrangement of nine circles with four circles on each side (the three corner circles each belong to two sides). The sum $s$ of the four numbers on each side of the triangle is the same. The sum of the numbers in the three corner circles is $12$. What is $s$?
