Write $f(x)=(x-1)(x-2)(x-3)(x-6)+L(x)$, where $L$ is the unique polynomial of degree at most $3$ with $L(1)=1$, $L(2)=5$, $L(3)=19$, $L(6)=665$. By Lagrange interpolation,
\[L(x)=\dfrac{136x^3-741x^2+1331x-711}{15},\]
so $L(4)=\dfrac{487}{5}$ and $L(5)=\dfrac{1473}{5}$, with sum $392$. The product term gives $(3)(2)(1)(-2)+(4)(3)(2)(-1)=-12-24=-36$. Hence
\[f(4)+f(5)=-36+392=\boxed{356}.\]
