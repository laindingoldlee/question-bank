Let $x$ be the number of dogs with all three traits. Each pairwise number counts the dogs with exactly those two traits plus the $x$ dogs with all three, so the number with exactly two traits is
\[
(21-x)+(18-x)+(19-x)=58-3x=22,
\]
giving $x=12$. The dogs that are brown and spotted but not friendly number $21-12=\boxed{\textbf{(C) }9}$.

(Check by inclusion--exclusion: $39+50+39-21-18-19+12=82$, so every dog has at least one trait, consistent with the total.)
