A total of $82$ dogs are at an animal rescue centre. The numbers of dogs with each trait (counting dogs that may also have other traits) are: brown fur $39$, spotted $50$, friendly $39$, brown fur and spotted $21$, spotted and friendly $18$, brown fur and friendly $19$. Exactly $22$ dogs have exactly two of these traits. How many dogs have brown fur and are spotted, but are not friendly?

$\textbf{(A) }6\qquad\textbf{(B) }7\qquad\textbf{(C) }9\qquad\textbf{(D) }11\qquad\textbf{(E) }12$
