Let $u=x^{\dfrac{1}{3}}$ and $v=y^{\dfrac{1}{3}}$. The numerator is $\dfrac{1}{x}-\dfrac{1}{y}=\dfrac{y-x}{xy}=\dfrac{v^3-u^3}{xy}=-\dfrac{(u-v)(u^2+uv+v^2)}{xy}$. Dividing by $u-v$ (nonzero since $x\ne y$):
\[\dfrac{x^{-1}-y^{-1}}{x^{\dfrac{1}{3}}-y^{\dfrac{1}{3}}}=\boxed{-\dfrac{x^{\dfrac{2}{3}}+x^{\dfrac{1}{3}}y^{\dfrac{1}{3}}+y^{\dfrac{2}{3}}}{xy}}.\]
