Let $BE=x$ and $EC=y$, so $BC=x+y$, and let $s$ be the side of the equilateral triangle. The right triangles $ABE$ and $DCE$ give $s^2=49+x^2$ and $s^2=25+y^2$. Dropping the perpendicular from $D$ to $AB$ (length $x+y$, cutting off $7-5=2$ from $AB$) gives $s^2=AD^2=(x+y)^2+4$. Hence
\[
y^2-x^2=24,\qquad (x+y)^2+4=49+x^2\iff y^2+2xy=45 .
\]
Subtracting, $x^2+2xy=21$, so $y=\dfrac{21-x^2}{2x}$. Substituting into $y^2-x^2=24$:
\[
\left(21-x^2\right)^2-4x^4=96x^2\iff x^4+46x^2-147=0\iff\left(x^2-3\right)\left(x^2+49\right)=0 .
\]
So $x=\sqrt3$, $y=\dfrac{18}{2\sqrt3}=3\sqrt3$ and $BC=4\sqrt3$. (Check: $s^2=49+3=25+27=48+4=52$.)

draw((0,0.45)--(0.45,0.45)--(0.45,0));draw((w-0.45,0)--(w-0.45,0.45)--(w,0.45));
label("$A$",A,NW);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,NE);label("$E$",E,S);
label("$7$",(A+B)/2,W);label("$5$",(C+D)/2,E);
label("$\sqrt3$",(B+E)/2,S);label("$3\sqrt3$",(E+C)/2,S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The area of the trapezium is
\[
\dfrac{AB+CD}{2}\cdot BC=\dfrac{7+5}{2}\cdot4\sqrt3=\boxed{24\sqrt3}.
\]
