An integer is even if it equals $ 2k $ for some integer $ k $. Since $ 0=2\cdot0 $, zero is even. If it were odd, $ 0=2k+1 $ would force $ k=-\dfrac{1}{2}$, not an integer. Thus $\boxed{\text{zero is even and not odd}}$.
