Apply the AM-GM inequality to the $n+2$ positive numbers consisting of $n+1$ copies of $x^{n+2}$ and one copy of $3^{n+2}$:
\[
\dfrac{(n+1)x^{n+2}+3^{n+2}}{n+2}\ \ge\ \sqrt[n+2]{\left(x^{n+2}\right)^{n+1}\cdot3^{n+2}}=x^{n+1}\cdot3 .
\]
Hence
\[
(n+1)x^{n+2}-3(n+2)x^{n+1}\ \ge\ -3^{n+2},
\]
with equality only when $x^{n+2}=3^{n+2}$, i.e.\ $x=3$. Therefore
\[
(n+1)x^{n+2}-3(n+2)x^{n+1}+a^{n+2}\ \ge\ a^{n+2}-3^{n+2}>0,
\]
since $a>3>0$. $\blacksquare$

(The proof uses only $x>0$; the hypothesis $x>1$ is not needed. The bound is sharp: at $x=3$ the expression equals $a^{n+2}-3^{n+2}$, which can be made arbitrarily small by taking $a$ close to $3$.)
