A date written in the form mm/dd/yy is called a \emph{perfect day} if its day number dd is a prime number. For instance, February 23, 2018 (02/23/18) and April 7, 2018 (04/07/18) are perfect days. How many perfect days are there from January 1, 2018 to December 31, 2020, inclusive?
