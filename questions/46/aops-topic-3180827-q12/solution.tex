Of the 49 integers, the exceptions are 1 and all prime powers. There are 15 primes below 50; the additional prime powers are 4, 8, 16, 32, 9, 27, 25, 49, eight numbers. Thus the answer is $49-1-15-8=\boxed{25}$.
