An exact integer-numerator and integer-denominator expression is $S=\dfrac{\sum_{k=2}^{100}\dfrac{100!}{k}}{100!}$. For bounds, $H_{10}=\dfrac{7381}{2520}>2.9$, while the decreasing function $\dfrac{1}{x}$ gives $\sum_{k=11}^{100}\dfrac{1}{k}>\int_{11}^{101}\dfrac{dx}{x}=\log\dfrac{101}{11}>2.2$. Therefore $S=H_{10}-1+\sum_{k=11}^{100}\dfrac{1}{k}>4.1>4$. Also $S<\int_1^{100}\dfrac{dx}{x}=\log 100<5$. Thus $\boxed{4<S<5}$.
