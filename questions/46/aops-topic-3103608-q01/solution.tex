Each cut increases the count by $6$. After $m$ individual cuts there are $7+6m$ pieces, always congruent to $1$ modulo $6$. Cutting several pieces at one stage is equivalent to performing their cuts successively. Since $605\equiv5\pmod6$, the target is $\boxed{\text{impossible}}$.
