Each piece is $24$ feet long. The triangle has side $8$, and the circle has circumference $24$, so its radius is $r=\dfrac{24}{2\pi}=\dfrac{12}{\pi}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real r=12/pi;
pair A=(0,0),B=(8,0),C=(4,4*sqrt(3));
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
label("$8$",(A+B)/2,S); label("$8$",(B+C)/2,NE); label("$8$",(A+C)/2,NW);
pair O=(14,r);
draw(circle(O,r),linewidth(0.9)+blue); dot(O,blue);
draw(O--O+r*dir(0),blue+dashed);
label("$r$",O+(r/2,0.45),blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

\[
\text{triangle: }\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}\cdot8^2=16\sqrt3\approx27.71,\qquad \text{circle: }\pi r^2=\dfrac{144}{\pi}\approx45.84 .
\]
The combined area is about $73.55$, which is $\boxed{74}$ square feet to the nearest whole number.
