Number the days from 1 November, so the birthdays are on days $1,12,18,21$ and $31$ (1 December is day $31$, since November has $30$ days). We want the day $d$ minimising
\[
S(d)=|d-1|+|d-12|+|d-18|+|d-21|+|d-31| .
\]
Pair the outer birthdays: $|d-1|+|d-31|\ge30$ with equality for $1\le d\le31$, and $|d-12|+|d-21|\ge9$ with equality for $12\le d\le21$. The middle term $|d-18|$ is $0$ only for $d=18$. So $S(d)\ge39$, with equality exactly when $d=18$, the \emph{median} of the five days.

Check: $S(18)=17+6+0+3+13=39$, while $S(17)=16+5+1+4+14=40$. The party should be on $\boxed{\textbf{(D)}\ \text{18 November}}$.

The average of the dates ($16.6$, close to 17 Nov) minimises the sum of the \emph{squares} of the distances, not the sum of the distances; that is why it gives the wrong answer here.
