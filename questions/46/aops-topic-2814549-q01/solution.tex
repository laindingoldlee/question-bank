Count $k$-person committees from $n$ people, one of whom is Albert. Committees containing Albert: choose the other $k-1$ from $n-1$, $\binom{n-1}{k-1}$ ways. Committees without Albert: $\binom{n-1}k$. Together they give all $\binom nk$ committees. $\blacksquare$
