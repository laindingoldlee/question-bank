There are $9$ matches, and the $k$-th match decides who gets prize $11-k$ (its loser), the winner of the last match getting the first prize. The players in each match are determined by the earlier results.

Each sequence of $9$ results gives a prize distribution, and different sequences give different distributions: at the first match where two sequences differ, the same two players meet but the loser differs, so prize $11-k$ goes to different people. Conversely every outcome of the play-offs arises this way. Hence there are $2^9=\boxed{512}$ ways.
