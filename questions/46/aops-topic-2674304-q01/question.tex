In a sudoku tournament the winner is decided by play-offs among the top $10$ ranked participants. Participants ranked $10$ and $9$ play first; the loser receives the $10^{\text{th}}$ prize and the winner plays participant $8$. The loser of that match receives the $9^{\text{th}}$ prize and the winner plays participant $7$, and so on, until the final match against participant $1$, whose winner receives the $1^{\text{st}}$ prize. In how many ways can the $10$ participants receive the prizes?
