For a point $X$, let $d(X)$ be its distance to the nearest point of the edge (the nearest side of the rectangle).

\textbf{A stick of length $\ell$ can touch $X$ exactly when $\ell\ge d(X)$.} The stick is a segment from a point $Y$ on the edge to $X$, so it needs $\ell=XY\ge d(X)$. Conversely, if $\ell\ge d(X)$: as $Y$ moves around the edge, the distance $XY$ varies continuously from $d(X)$ up to the distance to the farthest corner, which is at least half the diagonal and so more than $\dfrac{a}{2}$; hence some edge point $Y$ has $XY=\ell$ whenever $d(X)\le\ell\le\dfrac{a}{2}$. The segment $XY$ stays inside the pool because the rectangle is convex.

\textbf{The largest value of $d(X)$ is $\dfrac{a}{2}$.} If $X$ is at distances $x$ and $a-x$ from the two sides of length $b$, then $d(X)\le\min(x,a-x)\le\dfrac{a}{2}$. At the centre $O$, the distances to the sides are $\dfrac{a}{2}$ and $\dfrac{b}{2}\ge\dfrac{a}{2}$, so $d(O)=\dfrac{a}{2}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s=0.2){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}

draw(O--(O.x,0),gray+dashed);draw(O--(0,O.y),gray+dashed);draw(O--(w,O.y),gray+dashed);
draw(O--(O.x,h),red+linewidth(1.2));
dot(O);label("$O$",O,2*dir(-45));
label("$\dfrac{a}{2}$",(O.x,0.75*h),E,red);
label("$b$",(w/4,0),S);label("$a$",(0,h/4),W);

\end{asy}

So a stick of length $\dfrac{a}{2}$ reaches every point, and no shorter stick reaches the centre. The answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{a}{2}}$, half the shorter side; that is, $\dfrac{1}{2}\min(a,b)$.
