A rectangular pool has side lengths $a\le b$. A point $X$ on the water surface must be touched with the tip of a straight stick that lies flat on the surface, while the other end of the stick touches the edge of the pool (the stick may not reach over the edge). What is the shortest length of stick that can touch every point $X$ of the pool in this way? Express the answer in terms of $a$ and $b$.
