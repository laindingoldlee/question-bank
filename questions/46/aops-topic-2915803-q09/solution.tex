$\boxed{17}=1^3+4^2=2^3+3^2$. Below 17, cubes available are 1 and 8. Adding positive squares gives respectively 2,5,10 and 9,12 below 17, with no overlap. Cubes 27 and larger cannot contribute. This proves minimality.
