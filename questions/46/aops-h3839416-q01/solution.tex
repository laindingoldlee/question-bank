On $\sqrt k\le x<\sqrt{k+1}$, where $k=1,\ldots,99$, we have $f(x)=kx$. The endpoint jumps do not affect area. Therefore
\[
\int_1^{10}x\lfloor x^2\rfloor\,dx
=\sum_{k=1}^{99}\int_{\sqrt k}^{\sqrt{k+1}}kx\,dx
=\sum_{k=1}^{99}\dfrac{k}{2}
=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{99\cdot100}{2}
=\boxed{2475}.
\]
