The product is odd exactly when every chosen number is odd. Row $k$ contains $\left\lceil\dfrac {k}{2}\right\rceil$ odd numbers out of $k$, and the choices are independent, so
\[
P=\dfrac{1}{1}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot\dfrac{2}{4}\cdot\dfrac{3}{5}=\dfrac{12}{120}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{10}}.
\]
No casework is needed.
