Start both timers and the 15-minute interval together. At minute 7, turn the 7-minute timer over. At minute 11, the 11-minute timer empties; turn the 7-minute timer over again. It has run for 4 minutes since its last turn, so after inversion exactly 4 minutes of sand are above the neck. It empties at minute 15, ending the required interval.
