Pair each sequence of $11$ flips with the sequence obtained by swapping heads and tails. This is a bijection between equally likely outcomes. A sequence has more than $5$ heads exactly when it has at most $5$ tails, i.e. exactly when its partner has at most $5$ heads. Since $11$ is odd, every sequence has either more than $5$ heads or more than $5$ tails, but not both. So exactly half of the $2^{11}$ sequences have more than $5$ heads, and the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac12}$.
