The circle is $(x-4)^2+(y-3)^2=5$, so $y\ge3-\sqrt5>0$. Put $m=\dfrac{x}{y}$. Substitution gives
\[
(m^2+1)y^2-(8m+6)y+20=0.
\]
Real intersection requires discriminant $-16m^2+96m-44\ge0$, equivalently $\dfrac{1}{2}\le m\le\dfrac{11}{2}$. At the upper endpoint, $(x,y)=(\dfrac{22}{5},\dfrac{4}{5})$ lies on the circle. The maximum is therefore $\boxed{\dfrac{11}{2}}$.

\end{asy}
