\textbf{No.} $f$ is not one-to-one: $f(0)=-3$ and $f\!\left(-\dfrac23\right)=3\cdot\left(-\dfrac8{27}\right)+2\cdot\dfrac49-3=-\dfrac89+\dfrac89-3=-3$. (Indeed $f'(x)=9x^2+4x$ changes sign.) An inverse exists only after restricting the domain, e.g. to $x\ge0$.
