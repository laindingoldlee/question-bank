Amy performs three tricks per minute. Each trick is attempted even after a drop; its drop probability is respectively $\dfrac{1}{5},\dfrac{2}{5},\dfrac{4}{5}$. All attempts are independent. She stops at the end of the first minute by which she has dropped the pencil at least five times. If her expected duration is $\dfrac{a}{b}$ in lowest terms, find $a+b$.
