The common value is a positive common multiple of $4,5,6,7$, hence a multiple of $\operatorname{lcm}(4,5,6,7)=420$. Then $b=\dfrac{420m}{5}=84m$, which is smallest for $m=1$: $b=\boxed{84}$.
