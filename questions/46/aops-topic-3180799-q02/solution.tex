The ratios of length, width, and thickness are $\dfrac{1}{4},3,2$. Multiplying gives $\dfrac{3}{2}$, so the ratio is $\boxed{3:2}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(3,0),C=(3,1),D=(0,1),v=(.5,.4);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(D--(D+v)--(C+v)--(B+v)--B);draw(C--(C+v));label("length",(1.5,0),S);label("width",B+v/2,SE);label("thickness",(0,.5),W);
\end{asy}
