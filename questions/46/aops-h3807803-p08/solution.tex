The circle has center $O=(6,3)$ and radius $3$. Write $E=(e,6)$, where $0\le e\le6$, so $AE$ has equation $6x-ey=0$. Tangency requires
\[
\dfrac{|36-3e|}{\sqrt{36+e^2}}=3.
\]
Since $e\le6$, this simplifies to $(12-e)^2=36+e^2$, giving $e=\dfrac{9}{2}$. Hence $\boxed{DE=\dfrac{9}{2}}$.
