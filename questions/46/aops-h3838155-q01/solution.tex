Since $6^2+8^2=10^2$, the converse of Pythagoras gives a right angle between the sides $6$ and $8$. The area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot 6\cdot 8=\boxed{24}$. Heron\textquotesingle s formula with $s=\dfrac{6+8+10}{2}=12$ gives $\sqrt{12(12-6)(12-8)(12-10)}=\sqrt{576}=24$.
