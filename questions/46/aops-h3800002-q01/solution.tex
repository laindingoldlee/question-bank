Each stage retains three quarters of the previous area, so the area after $n$ stages is $\boxed{(\dfrac{3}{4})^n}$ and tends to zero. The given dimension equation gives $2^d=3$, hence $\boxed{d=\dfrac{\log3}{\log2}}$. This is approximately $1.585$, rather than $\dfrac{4}{3}$; it describes the specified scaling law, not an ordinary area.
