We look for two numbers with product $6\cdot(-35)=-210$ and sum $11$: they are $21$ and $-10$. So
\[
6x^2+11x-35=6x^2+21x-10x-35=3x(2x+7)-5(2x+7)=(3x-5)(2x+7),
\]
and the solutions are $x=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{3}}$ and $x=\boxed{-\dfrac{7}{2}}$. (The quadratic formula gives the same: the discriminant is $121+840=961=31^2$, and $x=\dfrac{-11\pm31}{12}$.)
