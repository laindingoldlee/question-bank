A regular hexagon splits into six equilateral triangles whose side is its circumradius $s$. Each has the same area $10$ as the given triangle. Thus the hexagon area is $\boxed{60}$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair[] p;for(int i=0;i<6;++i)p.push(dir(60*i));for(int i=0;i<6;++i){draw(p[i]--p[(i+1)%6]);draw((0,0)--p[i]);}label("$s$",(.5,0),S);
\end{asy}
