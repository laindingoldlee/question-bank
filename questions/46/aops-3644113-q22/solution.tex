Unroll the cylinder. The two helices have horizontal displacements $+h$ and $-h$ at height h because their slope is one. They coincide around the pole when $2h$ is a multiple of the circumference $2\pi$, so $h=k\pi$. For $0\le h\le50$, $k=0,1,\ldots,15$, giving $\boxed{16}$ intersections including A.
