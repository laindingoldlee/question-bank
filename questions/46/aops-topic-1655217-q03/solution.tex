The total birth weight was $7\cdot15=105$ ounces. The total change is
\[
3\cdot10+2\cdot15-2\cdot3=30+30-6=54
\]
ounces, so the new total is $159$ ounces and the new mean is
\[
\dfrac{159}{7}=\boxed{22\dfrac{5}{7}\ \text{ounces}}\approx22.7\text{ ounces}.
\]
(Only the total matters, not the individual weights.)
