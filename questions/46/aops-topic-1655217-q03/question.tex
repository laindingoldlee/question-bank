Seven puppies had a mean birth weight of $15$ ounces. After two weeks, $3$ of the puppies had each gained $10$ ounces, $2$ had each gained $15$ ounces, and $2$ sick puppies had each lost $3$ ounces, compared with their birth weights. What was the mean weight of the puppies then?
