In triangle $ABC$ with $AB\ne AC$, let $I$ be the incenter and $I_a$ the $A$-excenter. Let $K$ be the intersection of line $BC$ with the external angle bisector at $A$. Let $E$ be the midpoint of the arc from $B$ to $C$ that contains $A$ on the circumcircle. Prove that $K$ is the orthocenter of triangle $II_aE$.
