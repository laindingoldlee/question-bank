Subtracting their equations gives $(x-y)(x+y-16)=0$. If $x+y=16$, substituting yields $x^2-16x+73=0$, whose discriminant is $-36$, so there is no real solution in that case. Hence $x=y$, and then $x^2-22x+121=(x-11)^2=0$. The sole real intersection is $(11,11)$. Implicit differentiation gives slopes $\dfrac{2x-19}{3}$ and $\dfrac{3}{2y-19}$, both $1$ there, verifying tangency. Thus $\boxed{ab=121}$.

\end{asy}
