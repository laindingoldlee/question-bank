Split into two geometric series: $\displaystyle\sum_{n\ge0}\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^n+\sum_{n\ge0}\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^n=\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{1}{3}}+\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{1}{2}}=\dfrac{3}{2}+2=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{2}}$.
