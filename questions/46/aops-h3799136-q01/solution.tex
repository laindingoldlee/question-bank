(a) The points $D,E,F$ are collinear by construction, so $\boxed{[DEF]=0}$.

(b) Put $AB=L$ and $DE=DF=h$; the equality follows because the diameter is perpendicular to and bisects chord $EF$. Then $AD=\dfrac{L}{3}$ and
\[
60=[ABE]=\dfrac{1}{2}Lh.
\]
Using $EF=2h$ as the base of triangle $AEF$ gives
\[
[AEF]=\dfrac{1}{2}(2h)\dfrac{L}{3}=\dfrac{Lh}{3}=\boxed{40}.
\]
