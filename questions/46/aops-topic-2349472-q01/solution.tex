If the common difference is $d$, then $6=1+2d$, so $d=\dfrac52$. Hence $x=1+d$ and $y=6+d$, and
\[x+y=7+2d=7+5=\boxed{12}.\]
(Indeed $x=\dfrac72$ and $y=\dfrac{17}2$.)
