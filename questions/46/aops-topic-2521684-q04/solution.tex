For nonzero $a,b$, $a\star b=\dfrac{b-a}{ab}=\dfrac1a-\dfrac1b$. So the sum telescopes:
\[\left(1-\dfrac12\right)+\left(\dfrac12-\dfrac13\right)+\cdots+\left(\dfrac1{99}-\dfrac1{100}\right)=1-\dfrac1{100}=\boxed{\dfrac{99}{100}}.\]
