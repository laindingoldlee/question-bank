The $k$th term is
\[\dfrac{k}{k(k+1)(k+2)}=\dfrac{1}{(k+1)(k+2)}=\dfrac{1}{k+1}-\dfrac{1}{k+2}.\]
So the sum telescopes:
\[\left(\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{3}\right)+\left(\dfrac{1}{3}-\dfrac{1}{4}\right)+\cdots+\left(\dfrac{1}{n+1}-\dfrac{1}{n+2}\right)=\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{n+2}=\boxed{\dfrac{n}{2(n+2)}}.\]
(Check: $n=1$ gives $\dfrac{1}{6}$.)
