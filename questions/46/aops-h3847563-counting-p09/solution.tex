Row $n$ has entries $\binom nk$. Symmetry is $\binom nk=\binom n{n-k}$, by complementing subsets. Pascal\textquotesingle s recurrence is $\binom nk=\binom{n-1}{k-1}+\binom{n-1}k$, by splitting subsets according to membership of a specified element. Finally the row sum is $2^n$, counting all subsets of an $n$-element set.
