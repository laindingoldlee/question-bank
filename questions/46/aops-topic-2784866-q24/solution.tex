AM-GM gives $ 8x+\dfrac{2}{x}\ge2\sqrt{16}=8 $, with equality when $ 8x=\dfrac{2}{x}$, or $ x=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Thus the expression's minimum is $ 18+n $, giving $\boxed{n=2000}$.
