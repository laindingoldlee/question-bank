Multiply both sides by $\dfrac{9}{5}$ and add $32$:
\[
\dfrac{9}{5}c=f-32\ \Rightarrow\ \boxed{f=\dfrac{9}{5}c+32}.
\]
(This converts a Celsius temperature $c$ to Fahrenheit.)
