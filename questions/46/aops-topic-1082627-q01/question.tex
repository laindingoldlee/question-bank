Forty cards each bear one of the numbers $1,2,\dots,10$, with each number on four cards. Four cards are drawn at random without replacement. Let $p$ be the probability that all four bear the same number, and $q$ the probability that three bear a number $a$ and the other a different number $b$. What is $\dfrac {q}{p}$?
