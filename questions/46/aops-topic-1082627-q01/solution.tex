With $N=\dbinom{40}4$ equally likely draws: four of a kind can be chosen in $10$ ways, so $p=\dfrac{10}{N}$. For $q$: choose $a$ ($10$ ways), three of its four cards ($4$ ways), $b\ne a$ ($9$ ways) and one of its four cards ($4$ ways): $q=\dfrac{1440}{N}$. So $\dfrac {q}{p}=\boxed{144}$. (The attempted answer $9$ forgot the factors $4\cdot4$ for which cards are chosen.)
