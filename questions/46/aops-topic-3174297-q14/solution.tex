The divisibility condition is equivalent to divisibility by $\operatorname{lcm}(2,3,4,6,8,9)=72$. Write the number as $72k$; the bounds give $98\le k\le111$. Its last digit is $4$ exactly when $2k\equiv4\pmod{10}$, or $k\equiv2\pmod5$. Hence $k=102,107$, giving $7344,7704$. Their sum is $\boxed{15048}$.
