For positive numbers, the smaller the number, the larger its reciprocal. The smallest choice is $\dfrac13$ (since $\dfrac13<\dfrac25<1<5<1986$), whose reciprocal is $3$. The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(A)}\ \dfrac13}$.
