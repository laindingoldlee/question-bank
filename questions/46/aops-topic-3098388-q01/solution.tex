For $x>0$, integration of $\dfrac{1}{t}$ over $[x,x+1]$ gives $\dfrac{1}{x+1}<\ln(x+1)-\ln x<\dfrac{1}{x}$. Thus $(H_{n+1}-\ln(n+1))-(H_n-\ln n)=\dfrac{1}{n+1}-\ln(1+\dfrac{1}{n})<0$. On the other hand, $\ln n=\int_1^n\dfrac{dt}{t}\le H_{n-1}<H_n$, so the sequence is positive. A decreasing sequence bounded below converges. Its limit is the Euler--Mascheroni constant, distinct from the base $e$ of natural logarithms.
