Since the circumcentre $O$ is on $AB$, $AB$ is a diameter and $\angle C=90^\circ$. Then $BC=\sqrt{29^2-20^2}=21$ and the inradius is $r=\dfrac{20+21-29}{2}=6$.

Put $C=(0,0)$, $A=(20,0)$, $B=(0,21)$. Then $O$ is the midpoint of $AB$, $(10,10.5)$, and the incentre is $I=(r,r)=(6,6)$. So
\[OI=\sqrt{4^2+4.5^2}=\sqrt{36.25}=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt{145}}{2}\approx6.02}.\]
(This agrees with Euler's formula $OI^2=R^2-2Rr=14.5^2-2\cdot14.5\cdot6$.)

label("$A$",A,SE);label("$B$",B,NW);label("$C$",C,SW);label("$O$",O,NE);label("$I$",I,SW);

\end{asy}
