Among $1$ through $1199$, the counts divisible by $2,3,7$ are $599,399,171$. The pairwise intersection counts for $6,14,21$ are $199,85,57$, and the triple intersection count for $42$ is $28$. Inclusion--exclusion therefore leaves $1199-(599+399+171)+(199+85+57)-28=\boxed{343}$.
