For nonnegative $x$, the second-iterate map is $G(x)=12\dfrac{1+x}{13+x}$, with derivative $\dfrac{144}{(13+x)^2}\le\dfrac{144}{169}<1$. Since $G(3)=3$, the mean-value theorem gives $|x_{n+2}-3|\le(\dfrac{144}{169})|x_n-3|$. Both parity subsequences converge to $\boxed3$, justifying the infinite continued fraction.
