For base $ b $, $(2014)_b=2b^3+b+4 $. The cubes from 5 through 9 sum to $ 125+216+343+512+729=1925 $, while the bases sum to 35. Hence the total is $ 2\cdot1925+35+5\cdot4=\boxed{3905}$.
