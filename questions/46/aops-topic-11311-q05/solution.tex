The percent reading $X$ or $Y$ is $35+29-10=54$. Among those, the ones who also read $Z$ are the readers of ($X$ and $Z$) or ($Y$ and $Z$): $8+13-4=17$ percent. So the percent reading $X$ or $Y$ but not $Z$ is
\[
54-17=\boxed{37\%}.
\]
(Region by region: only $X$: $35-10-8+4=21$; only $Y$: $29-10-13+4=10$; $X$ and $Y$ but not $Z$: $10-4=6$; total $21+10+6=37$.)
