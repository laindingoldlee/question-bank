Let the common center be $O$ and the circumradii be $R,r$. For any point $F$ on chord $AB$,
$AF\cdot FB=R^2-OF^2$; this follows either from the power of $F$ or from the chord equation. Since $OD=OE=OF=r$, put $K=R^2-r^2>0$. Then
\[
4FB=18DC=3EA=K.
\]
Ceva's theorem gives
\[
1=\dfrac{BD}{DC}\dfrac{CE}{EA}\dfrac{AF}{FB}
=\dfrac{18^2\,3^2\,4^2}{K^3}.
\]
Hence $K^3=216^2=36^3$, so $K=36$. The sides are
\[
AB=4+9=13,\quad BC=18+2=20,\quad CA=3+12=15.
\]
Their perimeter is $\boxed{48}$.
