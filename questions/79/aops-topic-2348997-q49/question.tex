For a positive integer $n$, let $s(n)$ be the sum of its digits. Call $n$ \emph{interesting} if at least one of the numbers $s(n)$, $s(s(n))$, $s(s(s(n)))$, \dots{} is divisible by $7$. For example, $988$ is interesting since $s(988)=25$ and $s(25)=7$, and $257$ is interesting since $s(257)=14$. How many interesting numbers are there from $1$ to $100$, inclusive?
