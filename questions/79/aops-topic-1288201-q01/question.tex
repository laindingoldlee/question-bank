Let $N$ be the number of functions $f:\{1,2,\dots,101\}\to\{1,2,\dots,101\}$ such that $f^{101}(1)=2$, where $f^{101}$ is $f$ applied $101$ times. Find the remainder when $N$ is divided by $103$.
