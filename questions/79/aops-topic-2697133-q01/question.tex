For a positive integer $n$, we want a sequence of length $2n$ in which each of $1,2,\dots,n$ appears twice, the second appearance of $r$ being exactly $r$ places after the first (for example $4,2,3,2,4,3,1,1$ for $n=4$). Show that such a sequence can exist only if $n\equiv0$ or $1\pmod4$, and give examples for $n=1,4,5,8,9$.
