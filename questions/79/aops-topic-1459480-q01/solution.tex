In a $30^\circ$-$60^\circ$-$90^\circ$ triangle the sides are in the ratio $1:\sqrt3:2$ (shorter leg : longer leg : hypotenuse). If the shorter leg is $x$, the longer leg is $x\sqrt3$, so
\[
x\sqrt3=15,\qquad x=\dfrac{15}{\sqrt3}=\dfrac{15\sqrt3}{3}=\boxed{5\sqrt3}\approx8.66 .
\]
The number $x$ does not have to be a whole number: here we solve $x\sqrt3=15$ for $x$ rather than substitute $15$ for $x$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair ft(pair P,pair U,pair V){return U+dot(P-U,V-U)/dot(V-U,V-U)*(V-U);}
path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s=0.2){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
pair A=(0,0),B=(5,0),C=(0,5/sqrt(3));
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(ra(A,B-A,C-A,0.3));
draw(arc(B,0.9,150,180),red); label("$30^\circ$",B+1.75*dir(171),red);
draw(arc(C,0.7,270,330),red); label("$60^\circ$",C+1.05*dir(296),red);
label("$15$",(A+B)/2,S); label("$x$",(A+C)/2,W); label("$2x$",(B+C)/2,NE);
label("$A$",A,SW); label("$B$",B,SE); label("$C$",C,N);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
