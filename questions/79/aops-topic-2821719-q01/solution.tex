$31=4\cdot7+3$, so the weekdays of days $1,2,3$ occur $5$ times and the other four weekdays occur $4$ times. Mondays and Wednesdays are equally many exactly when the three weekdays of days $1$ to $3$ contain both Monday and Wednesday or neither.

Both: only the start Monday (Mon, Tue, Wed). Neither: starts Thursday (Thu, Fri, Sat) and Friday (Fri, Sat, Sun). Every other start contains exactly one of them. So $\boxed{3}$ days are possible: Monday, Thursday and Friday.
