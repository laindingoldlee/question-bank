If the annual growth factor is $q$, then $q^6=2$. The required growth factor is $\dfrac{9600}{300}=32=2^5=q^{30}$. Because $q>1$, this occurs uniquely after $\boxed{30}$ years. The initial deposit does not affect the doubling time.
