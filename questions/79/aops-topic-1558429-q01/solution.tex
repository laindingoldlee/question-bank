They approach each other at $12+6=18$ mph. They are $20$ miles apart for the first time when the gap has shrunk by $200-20=180$ miles, which takes
\[
\dfrac{180}{18}=\boxed{10\text{ hours}}.
\]
(If they keep riding after passing each other, they are $20$ miles apart again when they have covered $220$ miles together, after $\dfrac{220}{18}=12\dfrac{2}{9}$ hours.)
