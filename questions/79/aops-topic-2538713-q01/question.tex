A building has $26$ floors and there are $8$ moppers. The manager chooses one floor to be cleaned and assigns moppers to it: floor $2$ is very dirty and needs $2$ moppers, while every other floor needs $1$ mopper. In how many ways can the manager choose a floor together with the moppers it needs?
