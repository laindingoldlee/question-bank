If the chosen floor is not floor $2$, there are $25$ choices of floor and $8$ choices of mopper: $25\cdot8=200$ ways. If floor $2$ is chosen, two of the $8$ moppers are needed, in $\binom82=28$ ways. In total there are $200+28=\boxed{228}$ ways.
