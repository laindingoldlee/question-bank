Using $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{99}k=4950$ and $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{99}k^2=\dfrac{99\cdot100\cdot199}{6}=328350$,
\[
S=\sum_{k=1}^{99}(a+k)^2=99a^2+2a\cdot4950+328350=3\left(33a^2+3300a+109450\right).
\]
The bracket is $\equiv109450\equiv1\pmod3$ (its digit sum is $19$), so $3$ divides $S$ but $9$ does not.

Suppose $S=b^c$ with $c\ge2$. Since $3\mid b^c$ and $3$ is prime, $3\mid b$, and then $3^c\mid b^c$, so $9\mid S$, a contradiction. Hence no such $b,c$ exist. $\blacksquare$
