Lev reaches $100$ after $\dfrac{100}{5}=20$ minutes. At that moment Alex starts $100$ units behind Lev and gains $7-5=2$ units per minute, so he needs $\dfrac{100}{2}=50$ more minutes. Alex catches Lev $20+50=\boxed{70}$ minutes after the start. (Check: Lev has run $5\cdot70=350$ units and Alex $7\cdot50=350$ units.)
