A regular hexagon consists of six equilateral triangles, so its area is $\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}(\sqrt{2\sqrt3})^2=9$. The equal-area square has side $3$ and perimeter $\boxed{12}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
path h=dir(0);for(int k=1;k<6;++k)h=h--dir(60*k);draw(h--cycle);draw((2,-.75)--(3.5,-.75)--(3.5,.75)--(2,.75)--cycle);label("hexagon",(0,-1.3));label("equal-area square",(2.75,-1.3));
\end{asy}
