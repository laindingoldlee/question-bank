Use $A=(0,0),B=(4,0),C=(4,4),D=(0,4)$, so $M=(2,0),N=(4,2),P=(2,4)$. Intersecting the triangle boundary lines gives the overlap quadrilateral $(1,2),(\dfrac{8}{5},\dfrac{4}{5}),(4,2),(\dfrac{8}{5},\dfrac{16}{5})$. Its diagonals are horizontal and vertical, with lengths 3 and $\dfrac{12}{5}$, so area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot3\cdot\dfrac{12}{5}=\dfrac{18}{5}$. Hence $\boxed{a+b=23}$.
