The exponent of $2$ in $n!$ is $v_2(n!)=\sum_{j\ge1}\left\lfloor\dfrac{n}{2^j}\right\rfloor$. At $n=100$ it is $50+25+12+6+3+1=97$, and at $101$ it is unchanged. At $99$ it is $95$, while at $102$ it is $98$. The exponent is nondecreasing as $n$ increases, so the only possibilities are $\boxed{100,101}$.
