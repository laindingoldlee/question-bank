$\dfrac{36}{n+1}$ is an integer exactly when $n+1$ is a positive divisor of $36$ with $n+1\ge2$. Since $36=2^2\cdot3^2$ has $(2+1)(2+1)=9$ positive divisors, and the divisor $1$ would give $n=0$, there are $9-1=\boxed{8}$ values: $n=1,2,3,5,8,11,17,35$.
