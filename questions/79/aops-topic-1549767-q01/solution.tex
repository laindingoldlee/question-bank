The fractions are $\dfrac{k}{2015-k}$ for $k=1,\dots,1007$. Since $\gcd(k,2015-k)=\gcd(k,2015)$, a fraction is in lowest terms exactly when $k$ is coprime to $2015=5\cdot13\cdot31$.

Among $1,\dots,2014$ there are $\varphi(2015)=4\cdot12\cdot30=1440$ numbers coprime to $2015$, and they come in pairs $k$, $2015-k$ (both coprime or neither), one in $1,\dots,1007$ and one in $1008,\dots,2014$. So the answer is $\dfrac{1440}{2}=\boxed{720}$.
