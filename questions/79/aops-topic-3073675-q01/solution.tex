(a) Choose any two different numbers and put the larger first: $\binom{10}{2}=\boxed{45}$ choices. (b) The outputs for $(9,3)$ are $12,6,27,3,729$; for $(10,2)$ they are $12,8,20,5,100$. (c) Their expected outputs are respectively $\dfrac{12+6+27+3+729}{5}=\dfrac{777}{5}$ and $\dfrac{12+8+20+5+100}{5}=29$. Thus $(9,3)$ has the larger expectation. This comparison does not assert an optimal strategy for an unspecified multi-round game.
