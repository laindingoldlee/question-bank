Two distinct integers $a,b$ are selected from $1$ through $10$, with $a>b$. (a) How many ordered choices are possible? (b) Evaluate $a+b,a-b,ab,\dfrac{a}{b},a^b$ for $(a,b)=(9,3)$ and $(10,2)$. (c) If one of these five operations is selected uniformly, which of these two pairs has the larger expected output?
