You stand $100$ meters from a concrete wall, and your friend Billy stands $200$ meters farther from the wall, on the same side and on the same line perpendicular to the wall. You give a loud yell. Billy hears both the original sound and its echo from the wall. Taking the speed of sound to be $300$ meters per second, how many seconds pass between Billy hearing the original yell and hearing the echo? Give your answer as a decimal rounded to the nearest hundredth.
