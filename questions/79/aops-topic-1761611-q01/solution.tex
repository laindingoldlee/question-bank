The direct sound travels $200$ m from you to Billy. The echo travels $100$ m from you to the wall and then $300$ m from the wall back to Billy, $400$ m in all. The extra distance is $200$ m, so the time difference is $\dfrac{200}{300}=\dfrac{2}{3}\approx\boxed{0.67}$ seconds.
