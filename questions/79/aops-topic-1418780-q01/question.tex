In how many ways can the sum $0+1+2+\dots+n$ (with $n+1$ terms, in this order) be fully parenthesised, so that every pair of parentheses encloses exactly two summands, each a single number or a parenthesised group? For example, $0+1+2$ can be written as $(0+(1+2))$ or $((0+1)+2)$, two ways, and $0+1+2+3$ in $5$ ways. Find a formula in terms of $n$.
