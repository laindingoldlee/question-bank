Heron gives the original area $\sqrt{21\cdot8\cdot7\cdot6}=84$. The triangle whose sides are the three medians has three quarters of the original area; this follows by arranging the three centroid vectors head-to-tail and using the centroid ratio two to one. Hence the new area is $3\cdot\dfrac{84}{4}=\boxed{63}$.
