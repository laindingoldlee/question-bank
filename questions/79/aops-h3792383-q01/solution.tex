Put $u=\dfrac{3a+b}{2a+3b}$. The numerator identities
\[
9b-a=-3(3a+b)+4(2a+3b),\quad
4a-b=2(3a+b)-(2a+3b)
\]
transform the expression into $2u+\dfrac{4}{u}-4$. AM-GM gives $2u+\dfrac{4}{u}\ge4\sqrt2$, with equality at $u=\sqrt2$. This value is feasible: take $\dfrac{a}{b}=9+7\sqrt2$, which gives the required $u$.
Thus the minimum is $4\sqrt2-4$, and $\boxed{100x+10y+z=424}$.
