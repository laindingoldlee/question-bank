Equivalent fractions come from multiplying the numerator and denominator by the same number. Since $28=7\cdot4$, the first blank is $5\cdot4=20$. Since $35=5\cdot7$, the second blank is $7\cdot7=49$. So
\[
\dfrac{5}{7}=\dfrac{\boxed{20}}{28}=\dfrac{35}{\boxed{49}} .
\]
Check by cross-multiplying: $5\cdot28=140=7\cdot20$ and $5\cdot49=245=7\cdot35$.
