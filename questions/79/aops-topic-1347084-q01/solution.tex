Write the numbers as $a<b<c<d<e<12$. The mean is $6$, so the sum is $36$; the median is $\dfrac{c+d}{2}=6$, so $c+d=12$. Then
\[
a+b+e=36-12-12=12 .
\]
The sum of the three largest numbers is $d+e+12$.

Since $a\ge1$ and $b\ge2$, we get $e=12-a-b\le9$. Also $d<e\le9$, so $d\le8$. Hence $d+e+12\le8+9+12=29$.

This is achieved by $\{1,2,4,8,9,12\}$: the sum is $36$, the median is $\dfrac{4+8}{2}=6$, and the three largest numbers sum to $\boxed{29}$.
