Bob rolls $4$ fair six-sided dice, each with faces numbered $1$, $2$, $3$, $4$, $5$ and $7$. He records the sum of the numbers on the top faces of every pair of dice, and writes these $6$ sums on the $6$ faces of a new, blank die. When this new die is rolled, what is the probability that it shows an even number? Express your answer as a common fraction.
