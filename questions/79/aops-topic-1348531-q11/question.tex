A pool is filled by two hoses. Hose $A$ alone fills it in $60$ minutes and hose $B$ alone in $90$ minutes. At 12:00 P.M.\ hose $A$ is turned on, and at 12:20 P.M.\ hose $B$ is also turned on. At 12:29 P.M.\ a hole forms in the pool that alone would drain a full pool in $72$ minutes. At what time is the pool full?
