By 12:20 hose $A$ has filled $\dfrac{20}{60}=\dfrac{1}{3}$ of the pool. From 12:20 to 12:29 both hoses fill $9\left(\dfrac{1}{60}+\dfrac{1}{90}\right)=9\cdot\dfrac{5}{180}=\dfrac{1}{4}$, so $\dfrac{7}{12}$ is full. After that the net rate is
\[
\dfrac{1}{60}+\dfrac{1}{90}-\dfrac{1}{72}=\dfrac{6+4-5}{360}=\dfrac{1}{72}
\]
of the pool per minute, and the remaining $\dfrac{5}{12}$ takes $\dfrac{5}{12}\cdot72=30$ minutes. The pool is full at $\boxed{\text{12:59 P.M.}}$
