In triangle $ABC$, the median from $A$ meets $BC$ at $M$, the median from $B$ meets $CA$ at $N$, and the median from $C$ meets $AB$ at $O$. The three medians meet at the point $P$. Writing $[XYZ]$ for the area of triangle $XYZ$, prove that
\[[APN]=[NPC]=[CPM]=[MPB]=[BPO]=[OPA]=\dfrac16[ABC].\]
