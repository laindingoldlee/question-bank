The area is $84$ (Heron: $\sqrt{21\cdot8\cdot7\cdot6}$), so $R=\dfrac{abc}{4K}=\dfrac{13\cdot14\cdot15}{336}=\dfrac{65}8$ and $AP=2R=\dfrac{65}4$ is a diameter.

dot(O);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,W);label("$C$",C,E);label("$P$",P,S);label("$O$",O,NE);
\end{asy}

Hence $\angle ACP=90^\circ$ and
\[CP=\sqrt{AP^2-AC^2}=\sqrt{\dfrac{4225}{16}-225}=\dfrac{\sqrt{625}}4=\boxed{\dfrac{25}4}.\]
