The people with at least one of the two types make up $100\%-46\%=54\%$. By inclusion--exclusion,
\[
|A\cup B|=|A|+|B|-|A\cap B|\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 54\%=43\%+15\%-|A\cap B| ,
\]
so $|A\cap B|=58\%-54\%=\boxed{4\%}$.
