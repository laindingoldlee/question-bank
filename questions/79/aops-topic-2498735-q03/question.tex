What is the maximum possible area of a triangle with vertices $(a,0)$, $(b,0)$ and $(c,d)$, where $a\ne b$,
\[1+a-2a^2=1+b-2b^2=0,\qquad 1+c-2c^2=d\qquad\text{and}\qquad d>0?\]
Express your answer as a common fraction.
