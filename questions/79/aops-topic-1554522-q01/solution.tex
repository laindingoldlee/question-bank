The bucket holds $\dfrac{450}{12}=37.5$ dozen, so the cost per dozen is
\[
\dfrac{16.20}{37.5}=0.432\text{ dollars}.
\]
The increase is $3.50-0.432=3.068$ dollars per dozen, which as a percent of the cost is
\[
\dfrac{3.068}{0.432}\cdot100\%=\dfrac{767}{108}\cdot100\%=710.185\ldots\%\approx\boxed{710.2\%}.
\]
(The selling price is about $8.1$ times the cost price. A percent increase of about $710\%$ is correct; written to the nearest tenth it is $710.2\%$, which may be why ``$710\%$'' was marked wrong.)
