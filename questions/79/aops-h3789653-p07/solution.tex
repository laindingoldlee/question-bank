Join the centre to all vertices. The octagon consists of eight equal-area central triangles. Hexagon $EDCBXO$ contains the three triangles $OBC,OCD,ODE$ and half of $OAB$, since $X$ is the midpoint of $AB$. Its area is $\dfrac{7}{2}$ central-triangle units, so the requested ratio is $\dfrac{8}{\dfrac{7}{2}}=\boxed{\dfrac{16}{7}}$.
