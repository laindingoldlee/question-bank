Let each third have length $d$ miles. The times are $\dfrac {d}{3}$, $\dfrac {d}{1}$ and $\dfrac {d}{6}$ hours, a total of
\[
d\left(\dfrac{1}{3}+1+\dfrac{1}{6}\right)=\dfrac{3d}{2}\text{ hours}.
\]
Average speed is total distance over total time:
\[
\dfrac{3d}{\dfrac{3d}{2}}=\boxed{2}\text{ mph}.
\]
The length $d$ cancels, so the marathon's length is not needed. (This is the harmonic mean of $3$, $1$ and $6$, not their ordinary mean $\dfrac{10}{3}$.)
