The three identical little pigs are distributed among $7$ houses in $\dbinom{3+6}{6}=\dbinom{9}{3}=84$ ways (stars and bars). The mother and father can each go to any of the $7$ houses: $7^2=49$ ways. The total is $84\cdot49=\boxed{4116}$.
