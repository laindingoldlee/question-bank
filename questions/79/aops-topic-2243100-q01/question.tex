Sayori plays hangman and always picks her word from the following list of $88$ words:

Adventure, Alone, Amazing, Awesome, Beauty, Bed, Bliss, Broken, Calm, Charm, Cheer, Childhood, Clumsy, Color, Comfort, Cry, Dance, Dark, Daydream, Dazzle, Death, Defeat, Depression, Embrace, Empty, Excitement, Extraordinary, Family, Fear, Feather, Fireflies, Fireworks, Flower, Flying, Forgive, Friends, Fun, Grief, Happiness, Heart, Holiday, Hope, Hopeless, Hurt, Joy, Laugh, Lazy, Loud, Love, Lucky, Marriage, Memories, Misery, Misfortune, Music, Nature, Ocean, Pain, Party, Peaceful, Prayer, Passion, Play, Precious, Promise, Rainbow, Raincloud, Romance, Rose, Sadness, Scars, Shame, Silly, Sing, Smile, Sparkle, Special, Sunny, Sunset, Sweet, Tears, Together, Tragedy, Treasure, Unrequited, Vacation, Warm, Wonderful.

She plays $3$ games. What is the probability that, among her three words, one is ``Laugh'', one contains exactly two letters A (upper or lower case), and one begins with S, in some order, if
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] in each game she picks a word uniformly at random from the list, independently of the other games (so a word may be picked again);
\item[(b)] she picks three different words, all sets of three different words being equally likely?
\end{enumerate}
