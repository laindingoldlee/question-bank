Before the final day's divisions there must be 1009 cells. The previous day ends with an even number, at least 1009, hence at least 1010. Repeating this backwards gives minimal preceding day-end counts $1010,506,254,128,64,32,16,8,4,2,1$. Consequently the last eleven days require at least $1009+505+253+127+64+32+16+8+4+2+1=2021$ divisions. A shorter history cannot reach 2018, since eleven doublings are necessary; a longer history adds divisions before these necessary last stages. The forward sequence $1,2,4,8,16,32,64,128,254,506,1010,2018$ is attainable by fusing one pair on each of the last four days. Thus the minimum is $\boxed{2021}$.
