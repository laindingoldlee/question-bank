If $\sigma(n)=186$, then $n<186$, since both $1$ and $n$ are divisors. A complete bounded calculation gives $n=80$ and $122$. One can reproduce the check without listing every divisor: for each prime power $p^e$ dividing $n$, its divisor sum $1+p+\cdots+p^e$ must divide $186=2\cdot3\cdot31$. Among prime powers below $186$, the only such sums are $3$ for $2$, $31$ for $2^4$, $6$ for $5$, and $62$ for $61$. Coprime combinations whose sums multiply to $186$ are $2\cdot61=122$ and $2^4\cdot5=80$. Their digit sums are $5$ and $8$, so the requested total is $\boxed{13}$.
