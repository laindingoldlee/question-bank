A pond is divided into a $4\times4$ grid of $16$ squares, and a frog starts on a corner square. Each jump takes the frog horizontally or vertically to another square in the same row or column, and it must jump over at least one square (it may not land on a neighbouring square). The frog stops when it can no longer jump without landing on a square it has already visited. What is the maximum number of jumps it can make?
