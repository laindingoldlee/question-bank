Place $B=(0,0)$, $A=(6,0)$, $C=(0,8)$. The triangle is right, with inradius $\dfrac{(6+8-10)}{2}=2$, so its incenter is $I=(2,2)$. Reflection across $AB$ and $BC$ gives $A'=(2,-2)$ and $B'=(-2,2)$.

The hypotenuse has equation $4x+3y=24$. Reflecting $I$ across it gives
\[
C'=I-\dfrac{2(4\cdot2+3\cdot2-24)}{4^2+3^2}(4,3)
=\left(\dfrac{26}{5},\dfrac{22}{5}\right).
\]
Using vectors $B'-A'=(-4,4)$ and $C'-A'=(\dfrac{16}{5},\dfrac{32}{5})$,
\[
[A'B'C']=\dfrac{1}{2}\left|-\dfrac{128}{5}-\dfrac{64}{5}\right|=\dfrac{96}{5}.
\]
Thus $\boxed{|m-n|=91}$.
