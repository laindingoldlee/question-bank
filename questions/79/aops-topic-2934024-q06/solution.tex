The first five attacks total 15 damage and can kill at most two monsters. From turn 6 onward each attack kills at most one. Thus 12 turns kill at most $2+7=9$, so at least 13 are needed. Attacks 1 and 5 kill one monster,2 and 4 kill another, and 3 can damage any third monster. Turns 6 through 13 then kill the remaining eight. Hence $\boxed{13}$.
