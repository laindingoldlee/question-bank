Chicken nuggets are sold in packs of $4$, $6$, $9$ and $20$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What is the greatest number of nuggets that cannot be bought exactly?
\item[(b)] What is the answer if the pack of $20$ is no longer sold?
\end{enumerate}
