A game show has eight contestants: six ``chumps'', among them Marcus and Richard, and two ``nerds''. At every moment one chump is the \emph{chooser}; Marcus is the first chooser. In each round the chooser picks one of the other remaining contestants uniformly at random and duels with them; the loser leaves the game.
\begin{itemize}
\item Against a chump, each of the two wins with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. The winner (whoever it is) becomes, or stays, the chooser.
\item Against a nerd, the chooser wins with probability $\dfrac{1}{3}$. The nerd leaves the game after the duel whatever its result. If the chooser wins, the chooser stays the chooser; if the chooser loses, the chooser leaves and a chump chosen uniformly at random from the remaining chumps becomes the chooser.
\end{itemize}
The game ends as soon as only one chump remains; that chump is the survivor. What is the probability that Richard is the survivor? Give the exact value and round it to the nearest thousandth.
