Let the highway route be $d$ miles. Then the back-road route is $d-3$ miles. Measuring time in hours, with $5$ minutes $=\dfrac{1}{12}$ hour,
\[\dfrac{d-3}{36}=\dfrac{d}{45}+\dfrac{1}{12}.\]
Multiplying by $180$: $5(d-3)=4d+15$, so $5d-15=4d+15$ and $d=\boxed{30}$.

Check: the highway takes $\dfrac{30}{45}$ hour $=40$ minutes, and the back roads take $\dfrac{27}{36}$ hour $=45$ minutes, which is $5$ minutes longer.
