The shoelace formula says that if a polygon has vertices $(x_1,y_1),(x_2,y_2),\dots,(x_n,y_n)$ listed in order around its boundary, then its area is
\[
\dfrac{1}{2}\left|\,(x_1y_2-x_2y_1)+(x_2y_3-x_3y_2)+\cdots+(x_ny_1-x_1y_n)\,\right| .
\]
Use it to find the area of quadrilateral $ABCD$ with $A(1,1)$, $B(6,2)$, $C(5,6)$, $D(2,5)$, and check the answer by another method.
