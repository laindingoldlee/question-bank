Listing the vertices in order $A,B,C,D$ and back to $A$:
\[
\begin{array}{rcl}
x_Ay_B-x_By_A&=&1\cdot2-6\cdot1=-4\\
x_By_C-x_Cy_B&=&6\cdot6-5\cdot2=26\\
x_Cy_D-x_Dy_C&=&5\cdot5-2\cdot6=13\\
x_Dy_A-x_Ay_D&=&2\cdot1-1\cdot5=-3
\end{array}
\]
The sum is $32$, so the area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot32=\boxed{16}$. (The name comes from writing the coordinates in two columns and multiplying along crossing diagonals, like the laces of a shoe.)

\textbf{Check.} The quadrilateral lies in the dashed $5\times5$ square $[1,6]\times[1,6]$ of area $25$. The part of the square outside $ABCD$ consists of the triangle below $AB$ (legs $5$ and $1$, area $2.5$), the triangle to the right of $BC$ (legs $1$ and $4$, area $2$), and the region to the upper left with vertices $(1,1),(2,5),(5,6),(1,6)$. Split that region by the line $x=2$ into the trapezoid $(1,1),(2,5),(2,6),(1,6)$ with parallel sides $5$ and $1$ and width $1$ (area $3$) and the triangle $(2,5),(5,6),(2,6)$ (legs $3$ and $1$, area $1.5$). The outside area is $2.5+2+3+1.5=9$, so the quadrilateral has area $25-9=16$, as before.
