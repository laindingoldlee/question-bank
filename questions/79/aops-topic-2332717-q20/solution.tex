We count arrangements of $A,A,B,B,C,C$ with no two equal letters adjacent. There are $\dfrac{6!}{2!2!2!}=90$ arrangements. Gluing a pair, e.g. $AA$, gives $\dfrac{5!}{2!2!}=30$; gluing two pairs, $\dfrac{4!}{2!}=12$; gluing all three, $3!=6$. By inclusion-exclusion, $90-3\cdot30+3\cdot12-6=30$. \textbf{(D)}
