A rook starts at $a1$ and a stationary black pawn stands at $h7$. At each step choose uniformly among all legal rook destinations on an otherwise empty board; the rook may capture the pawn but may not pass through it. Capture ends the experiment successfully. Otherwise make exactly three rook moves. Count success also if the final rook occupies $h6$ or attacks $h6$ without the pawn blocking that attack. Find the success probability.
