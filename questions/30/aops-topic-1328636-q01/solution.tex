This is a geometric series with ratio $1+i$, so the left side is $\dfrac{(1+i)^{k+1}-1}{(1+i)-1}=\dfrac{(1+i)^{k+1}-1}{i}$. Setting it equal to $1025i$ gives $(1+i)^{k+1}-1=1025i^2=-1025$, so $(1+i)^{k+1}=-1024$.

Now $(1+i)^2=2i$ and $(1+i)^4=-4$, so $(1+i)^{20}=(-4)^5=-1024$. Since $|1+i|^{k+1}=2^{\dfrac{k+1}{2}}$ must equal $1024=2^{10}$, the exponent $k+1=20$ is forced. Hence $k=\boxed{19}$.
