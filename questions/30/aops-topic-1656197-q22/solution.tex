The last two digits of $7^n$ are $07,49,43,01$ for $n=1,2,3,4$, and since $7^4\equiv1\pmod{100}$ they repeat with period $4$. So the tens digits are $0,4,4,0,0,4,4,0,\dots$, summing to $8$ per period. As $2018=4\cdot504+2$,
\[
\sum_{k=1}^{2018}a_k=504\cdot8+(0+4)=4036,
\]
and the remainder is $\boxed{\textbf{(c)}\ 036}$.
