Write Cart A's profits in increasing order as $a\le m\le h$ and Cart B's as $b\le m\le h$: with three values the median is the middle one, and the medians and maxima agree. The means differ by $27$, so the sums differ by $3\cdot27=81$:
\[
(a+m+h)-(b+m+h)=a-b=81 .
\]
The lowest profits differ by $\boxed{\$81}$.
