Each equilateral triangle has altitude $6\sqrt3$. Measured from the bottom of the square, the two apex heights are $6\sqrt3$ and $12-6\sqrt3$. Both apices lie on the vertical midline, so their distance is $6\sqrt3-(12-6\sqrt3)=\boxed{12(\sqrt3-1)}$.
