An object moves forward with velocity $v(t)=1+t^2$ metres per second for $0\le t\le3$. Approximate its velocity graph by straight segments between equally spaced sample times.
\begin{enumerate}
\item For sample times $0,1,2,3$, find the acceleration on each straight segment and the estimated distance.
\item Using $n$ equal time intervals, prove that the trapezoidal distance estimate is $12+\dfrac{9}{2n^2}$ metres. Find its limit as $n\to\infty$.
\end{enumerate}
