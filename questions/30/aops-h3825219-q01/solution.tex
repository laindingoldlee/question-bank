\begin{enumerate}
\item The sampled velocities are $1,2,5,10$. Their consecutive slopes are $\boxed{1,3,5}$ metres per second squared. The three trapezoid areas total
\[
\dfrac{1+2}{2}+\dfrac{2+5}{2}+\dfrac{5+10}{2}
=\boxed{\dfrac{25}{2}\text{ metres}}.
\]
\item Let $h=\dfrac{3}{n}$. The constant part of the velocity contributes exactly $3$. The quadratic part contributes
\[
h^3\left(\sum_{j=1}^{n-1}j^2+\dfrac{n^2}{2}\right)
=\dfrac{27}{n^3}\left(\dfrac{(n-1)n(2n-1)}{6}+\dfrac{n^2}{2}\right)
=9+\dfrac{9}{2n^2}.
\]
Adding the constant part gives the stated estimate. Its limit is $\boxed{12\text{ metres}}$, the area under the positive velocity graph. The approximation exceeds this limit by exactly $\dfrac{9}{2n^2}$.
\end{enumerate}
