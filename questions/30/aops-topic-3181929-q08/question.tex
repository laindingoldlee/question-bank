Four disjoint overlap regions are formed by pairs of disks of radius 6. In each pair, the common chord subtends a 60-degree minor arc on each circle. No point belongs to three disks. The total overlap area is $a\pi-b\sqrt c$, with positive integer coefficients and squarefree $c$. Find $a+b+c$.
