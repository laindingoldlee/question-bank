Write $n=10^8k+18421997$ with $k\ge0$ an integer. Modulo $71$:
\[10^2=100\equiv29,\qquad10^4\equiv29^2=841\equiv-11,\qquad10^8\equiv121\equiv50,\]
and $18421997=71\cdot259464+53\equiv53$. So we need $50k+53\equiv0$, i.e. $50k\equiv18\pmod{71}$. Since $50\cdot27=1350=19\cdot71+1$, the inverse of $50$ is $27$, and
\[k\equiv18\cdot27=486=6\cdot71+60\equiv60\pmod{71}.\]
The smallest choice is $k=60$, giving $n=\boxed{6018421997}=71\cdot84766507$.
