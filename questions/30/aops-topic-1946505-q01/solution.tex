The sum of the squares of four consecutive primes increases as the primes increase, so at most one block of four consecutive primes works, and we can scan:
\[
\begin{aligned}
2^2+3^2+5^2+7^2&=87,\\
11^2+13^2+17^2+19^2&=940,\\
13^2+17^2+19^2+23^2&=1348,\\
17^2+19^2+23^2+29^2&=289+361+529+841=2020,\\
19^2+23^2+29^2+31^2&=2692>2020 .
\end{aligned}
\]
So the primes are $17,19,23,29$, and $a+b+c+d=\boxed{88}$.
