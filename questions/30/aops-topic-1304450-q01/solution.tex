$(10a+5)^2=100a^2+100a+25=100\,a(a+1)+25$. Since $25<100$, the last two digits are $25$ and the digits in front form $a(a+1)$.

For $85$: $a=8$, $8\cdot9=72$, so $85^2=\boxed{7225}$. For $115$: $a=11$, $11\cdot12=132$, so $115^2=\boxed{13225}$.
