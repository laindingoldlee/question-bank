Put $A=(0,0),C=(14,0)$ and $B=(u,v)$ above the axis. From $u^2+v^2=36$ and $(u-14)^2+v^2=100$, obtain $u=\dfrac{33}{7}$ and $v=\dfrac{15\sqrt3}{7}$. The external equilateral vertex is $D=(7,-7\sqrt3)$. Hence $BD^2=(\dfrac{16}{7})^2+(\dfrac{64\sqrt3}{7})^2=256$, giving $\boxed{BD=16}$.
