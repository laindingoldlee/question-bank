Such a rectangle is $a$ squares by $2a$ squares, with $2a\le 8$, so $a\in\{1,2,3,4\}$, and it can be placed with its long side horizontal or vertical.

With the long side horizontal, the rectangle covers $2a$ consecutive columns, which can be chosen in $8-2a+1=9-2a$ ways, and $a$ consecutive rows, which can be chosen in $9-a$ ways. So there are $(9-a)(9-2a)$ placements:
\[
a=1:\ 8\cdot7=56,\qquad a=2:\ 7\cdot5=35,\qquad a=3:\ 6\cdot3=18,\qquad a=4:\ 5\cdot1=5 ,
\]
in total $56+35+18+5=114$. By symmetry there are another $114$ with the long side vertical, and no rectangle is counted twice (a $2:1$ rectangle is not a square). The answer is
\[
2\cdot114=\boxed{228}.
\]
