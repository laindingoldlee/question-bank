A student asks when an inequality sign must be flipped after squaring both sides or taking square roots.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that if $0\le a<b$, then $a^2<b^2$, and that if $a<b\le0$, then $a^2>b^2$.
\item[(b)] Give examples with $a<0<b$ where $a^2<b^2$, where $a^2=b^2$, and where $a^2>b^2$.
\item[(c)] Prove that if $0\le a<b$, then $\sqrt a<\sqrt b$.
\item[(d)] Use these facts to solve the inequality $\sqrt{x+2}>x$ for real $x$.
\end{enumerate}
