Put $u=x-5$, $v=y-3$. Squaring gives $uv=16$ with $u,v\ge0$, hence both positive. The ordered factor pairs are $(1,16),(2,8),(4,4),(8,2),(16,1)$. Since $xy=(u+5)(v+3)=31+3u+5v$, their products are $114,77,63,65,84$. The largest is $\boxed{114}$, attained at $(x,y)=(6,19)$.
