Multiply by the nonzero denominator: $7-kx+\varepsilon Z=k-kx+\varepsilon kY$. The $-kx$ terms cancel. If $1+\varepsilon Y\ne0$, then $\boxed{k=\dfrac{7+\varepsilon Z}{1+\varepsilon Y}}$. If $1+\varepsilon Y=0$, the equation has every real $k$ as a solution when $7+\varepsilon Z=0$, and no solution otherwise. The original denominator restriction still applies.
