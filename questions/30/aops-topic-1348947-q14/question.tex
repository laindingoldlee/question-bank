Call a positive integer $a$ \emph{masterful} if there is a positive integer $b$ such that $\dfrac{a^2+b^2}{ab+1}$ is an integer. Find the largest three-digit integer $a$ for which there is a positive integer $b$ such that $\dfrac{a^2+b^2}{ab+1}$ is an integer and neither $a$ nor $b$ is a perfect cube.
