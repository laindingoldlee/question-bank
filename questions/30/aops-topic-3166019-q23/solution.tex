Start with the all-positive series and subtract twice its even-indexed terms. Their sum is $\sum_{k\ge1}\dfrac{1}{(2k)^2}=\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot\dfrac{\pi^2}{6}$. Thus the alternating sum is $\dfrac{\pi^2}{6}-\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{\pi^2}{6}=\boxed{\dfrac{\pi^2}{12}}$.
