Over the common denominator $420$,
\[\dfrac{1}{20}-\dfrac{1}{21}=\dfrac{21-20}{420}=\dfrac{1}{420},\qquad\dfrac{20}{21}-\dfrac{21}{20}=\dfrac{400-441}{420}=-\dfrac{41}{420}.\]
So the expression is $\dfrac{1}{420}+\dfrac{41}{420}=\dfrac{42}{420}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{10}}$, answer $\textbf{(C)}$.
