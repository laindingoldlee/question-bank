Let $S=\{a+4b:a,b\ge0,\ a+b\le25\}$. For fixed $b$, this contains the integer interval from $4b$ to $25+3b$. The normal totals are $3S$ and the evil totals $2S$. Construct these finite sets by the 351 possible pairs $(a,b)$ and count $2S\setminus3S$; this gives $\boxed{65}$. The interval description, or the explicit loops $b=0,\ldots,25$ and $a=0,\ldots,25-b$, tests every permitted purchase exactly and removes repeated totals.
