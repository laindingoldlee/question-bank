Write the line as $y=mx+k$. Then $18=4m+k$, so $k=18-4m$, and
\[
a=m+k=18-3m,\qquad b=2m+k=18-2m .
\]
Hence
\[
1.5b-a=27-3m-(18-3m)=\boxed{9},
\]
whatever the slope $m$ is.
