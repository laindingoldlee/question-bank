\textbf{Bounding $a^b$.} If $b\le0$, then $a\ne0$ and $\left|a^b\right|\le1$. If $b=1$, $a^b=a\le3$. If $b=2$, $a^b=a^2\le9$. If $b=3$, then $a\ne3$, so $a^3\le8$. Hence $a^b\le9$.

\textbf{Bounding $-cd$.} The largest values of $-cd$ for two different numbers from the list are $9$ (only for $\{c,d\}=\{3,-3\}$), then $6$ (for $\{2,-3\}$ or $\{-2,3\}$); every other pair gives $-cd\le4$.
\begin{itemize}
\item If $\{c,d\}=\{3,-3\}$, then $a,b\in\{-2,-1,0,1,2\}$ are different, and $a^b\le4$ (the largest is $(-2)^2$). The total is at most $4+9=13$.
\item Otherwise $-cd\le6$ and the total is at most $9+6=15$.
\end{itemize}
The value $15$ is attained with $a=-3$, $b=2$, $c=-2$, $d=3$: $(-3)^2-(-2)(3)=9+6=15$. The answer is $\boxed{15}$.
