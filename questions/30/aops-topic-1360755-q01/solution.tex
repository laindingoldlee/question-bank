Two numbers $a<b$ with $a+b=8$: $\{1,7\},\{2,6\},\{3,5\}$ (not $\{4,4\}$, which is not two distinct numbers): $3$ sets.

Three numbers $a<b<c$ with $a+b+c=8$: if $a=1$, then $b+c=7$ with $1<b<c$, giving $\{1,2,5\}$ and $\{1,3,4\}$; if $a\ge2$, then $a+b+c\ge2+3+4=9>8$. So $2$ sets.

The total is $3+2=\boxed{5}$.
