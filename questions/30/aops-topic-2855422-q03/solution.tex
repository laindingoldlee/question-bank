Let $L=9^{10}\log_{10}8$. Writing $L=k+f$ with $k$ an integer and $0\le f<1$, we have $8^{9^{10}}=10^k\cdot10^f$, so the leading digit is $\lfloor10^f\rfloor$. Here $9^{10}=3486784401$ and, to enough precision, $L\approx3148880079+0.34280$, so $10^f\approx2.202$. The leading digit is $\boxed{2}$. (About $15$ significant digits of $\log_{10}8$ are needed, since $L$ has $10$ digits before the point.)
