Each factor is $\dfrac{n^2-1}{n^2}=\dfrac{n-1}{n}\cdot\dfrac{n+1}{n}$. Both products telescope:
\[\prod_{n=9}^{2021}\dfrac{n-1}{n}=\dfrac{8}{2021},\qquad\prod_{n=9}^{2021}\dfrac{n+1}{n}=\dfrac{2022}{9}.\]
So the product is $\dfrac{8\cdot2022}{2021\cdot9}=\dfrac{8\cdot674}{2021\cdot3}=\boxed{\dfrac{5392}{6063}}$.
