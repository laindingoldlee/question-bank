$2020=2^2\cdot5\cdot101$. The sets of four distinct factors with product $2020$ are $\{1,2,5,202\}$, $\{1,2,10,101\}$ and $\{1,4,5,101\}$. Put the two smallest on top: $\dfrac3{207}=\dfrac1{69}$, $\dfrac3{111}=\dfrac1{37}$, $\dfrac5{106}$. The smallest is $\boxed{\textbf{(d) }\dfrac1{69}}$.
