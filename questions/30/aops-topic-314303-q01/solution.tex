Pad numbers below $1000$ to three digits. Each position has $9$ choices, excluding $1$, giving $9^3=729$ strings. Remove $000$, which is not positive. The endpoint $1000$ contains $1$ and is not eligible. Thus the count is $\boxed{728}$.
