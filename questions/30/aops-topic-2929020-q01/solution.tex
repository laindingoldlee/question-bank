The midline has length $\dfrac{a+b}2$ and each part has height $\dfrac h2$. So
\[S_1=\dfrac h4\left(a+\dfrac{a+b}2\right)=\dfrac h8(3a+b),\qquad S_2=\dfrac h8(a+3b).\]
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(6,0),C=(4.5,3),D=(1.5,3);pair M=(A+D)/2,N=(B+C)/2;draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(M--N,dashed);
label("$b$",(3,0),S);label("$a$",(3,3),N);label("$\dfrac{a+b}2$",N,E);label("$S_1$",(3,2.3));label("$S_2$",(3,0.7));
\end{asy}

Then $S_1(a+3b)=S_2(3a+b)$ gives $\boxed{\dfrac ab=\dfrac{3S_2-S_1}{3S_1-S_2}}$. (Since $S_1<3S_2$ and $S_2<3S_1$ always, both numerator and denominator are positive.)
