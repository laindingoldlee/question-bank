Since $77760=7776\cdot10=6^5\cdot10=2^6\cdot3^5\cdot5$, the odd factors of $77760$ are exactly the factors of its odd part $3^5\cdot5=1215$. There are $(5+1)(1+1)=12$ of them, and they pair up as $d$ and $\dfrac{1215}{d}$ (no factor is paired with itself, since $1215$ is not a perfect square). Each of the $6$ pairs has product $1215$, so the product of all odd factors is
\[\boxed{1215^6}=3^{30}\cdot5^6 .\]
