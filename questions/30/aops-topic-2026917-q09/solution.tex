The radius is $6$ yards.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0);
fill(arc(O,6,0,180)--cycle,paleyellow);
draw(arc(O,6,0,180)--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(O--6*dir(60),gray); dot(O);
draw((-6,-1.4)--(6,-1.4),Arrows(4)); label("$12$",(0,-1.4),S);
label("$r=6$",3*dir(60),NW);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

A semicircle has half the area of the full circle:
\[\dfrac{1}{2}\pi\cdot6^2=\boxed{18\pi}\ \text{square yards}.\]
