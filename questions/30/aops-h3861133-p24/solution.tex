Let $p_i$ be the probability that the player about to flip makes the final head, when the current streak has $i$ heads, for $i=0,1,2$. A tail resets the streak and switches players; a head advances it and switches players unless it ends the game. Thus $p_0=1-\dfrac{p_0+p_1}{2}$, $p_1=1-\dfrac{p_0+p_2}{2}$, and $p_2=1-\dfrac{p_0}{2}$. Substitution yields $p_1=\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{p_0}{4}$ and $3p_0+p_1=2$, hence $\boxed{p_0=\dfrac{6}{11}}$.
