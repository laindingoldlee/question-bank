With $n=1$: $f(m+1)=f(m)+12m-3$. So for $n\ge1$,
\[f(n)=\sum_{m=1}^{n-1}(12m-3)=6n(n-1)-3(n-1)=3(n-1)(2n-1).\]
(One checks that $f(n)=3(n-1)(2n-1)$ satisfies the equation for all integers $m,n$.) Hence $f(19)=3\cdot18\cdot37=\boxed{1998}$.
