Let $a,b,c$ be real numbers with $a+b$, $b+c$ and $c+a$ nonzero. Show that $\dfrac{2a}{a+b}+\dfrac{2b}{b+c}+\dfrac{2c}{c+a}=3$ does \emph{not} hold in general, and prove that it holds exactly when at least two of $a,b,c$ are equal.
