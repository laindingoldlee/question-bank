In a simplified model of Carnatic music there are seven notes S, R, G, M, P, D, N. The notes S and P have one form each; R, G, D and N have $3$ forms each; M has $2$. A \emph{raaga} consists of an ascent S R G M P D N S and a descent S N D P M G R S, where each of the notes R, G, M, D, N in the ascent and in the descent is independently assigned one of its forms (the ascent and descent may use different forms). How many raagas are there?
