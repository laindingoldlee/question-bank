There are $3\cdot3=9$ equally likely pairs of choices, and the tie pairs are (rock, rock), (paper, paper), (scissors, scissors). The probability is $\dfrac{3}{9}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{3}}$. (Equivalently: whatever the first player chooses, the second matches it with probability $\dfrac{1}{3}$.)
