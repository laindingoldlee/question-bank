Four girls, Patricia, Dorothy, Mandy and Sarah, stand in a line with $10$ boys, the girls appearing in that order from left to right. Let $p$ be the number of boys between Patricia and Dorothy, $d$ the number between Dorothy and Mandy, and $m$ the number between Mandy and Sarah. The number of arrangements of the line in which the product $pdm$ is even can be written as $10!\times a$. Find $a$. (For example, $PBDBBMBBBBBBBS$ is an acceptable arrangement. If two girls stand next to each other, the number of boys between them is $0$.)

$\textbf{(A) }931\qquad\textbf{(B) }939\qquad\textbf{(C) }953\qquad\textbf{(D) }971\qquad\textbf{(E) }981$
