Five mice numbered $1$ to $5$ play a game with four rounds. In round $n$, if mouse $n$ is still alive, it eats mouse $n+1$ with probability $\dfrac12$. The probability that mouse $5$ is uneaten at the end is $\dfrac ab$ with $a,b$ coprime positive integers. Find $b-a$.
