A cyclic quadrilateral $PIEE'$ is inscribed in a fixed circle, with $PE'=PI<IE=EE'$. Lines $PE'$ and $IE$ meet at $X$. When the area of triangle $IEE'$ is as large as possible, $\left(\dfrac{IP}{IX}\right)^2=\dfrac pq$ in lowest terms. Find $p+q$.
