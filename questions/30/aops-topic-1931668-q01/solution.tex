The condition is $0\le C\le35$, that is,
\[
0\le\dfrac{5}{9}(F-32)\le35 .
\]
Multiplying by $\dfrac{9}{5}$ (a positive number) gives $0\le F-32\le63$, so
\[
\boxed{32\le F\le95}.
\]
The minimum operating temperature is $32^\circ$F and the maximum is $95^\circ$F.
