Let $a_n=1+\dfrac{n(n-1)}{2}$. Then $a_1,\ldots,a_{10}=1,2,4,7,11,16,22,29,37,46$. Each displayed term without its sign equals $\dfrac{a_n+a_{n+1}}{a_na_{n+1}}=\dfrac{1}{a_n}+\dfrac{1}{a_{n+1}}$. Alternating these sums cancels every intermediate reciprocal, leaving $\dfrac{1}{a_1}+\dfrac{1}{a_{10}}=1+\dfrac{1}{46}=\boxed{\dfrac{47}{46}}$.
