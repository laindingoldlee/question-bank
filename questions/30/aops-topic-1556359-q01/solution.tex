Each element of $S$ is left out of exactly one of the five sums, so adding the five sums counts every element $4$ times:
\[
4\cdot(\text{sum of }S)=169+153+182+193+127=824,\qquad\text{sum of }S=206 .
\]
Each element is $206$ minus the sum of the other four, so the elements are $206-169=37$, $53$, $24$, $13$ and $79$ (distinct positive integers, as required). The largest is the one omitted from the smallest sum: $206-127=\boxed{79}$.
