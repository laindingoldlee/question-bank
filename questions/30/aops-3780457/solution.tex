Let $D$ be the midpoint of the arc $BC$ not containing $A$. Equal arcs give $DB=DC$, and $AD$ bisects angle $A$, so $A,I,D$ are collinear. In triangle $BDI$, $\angle D B I = \angle D B C + \angle C B I = \dfrac{A}{2} + \dfrac{B}{2} = 90^\circ - \dfrac{C}{2}$, while $\angle B I D = 180^\circ - \angle B I A = 90^\circ - \dfrac{C}{2}$. Thus $\angle DBI=\angle BID$ and $DI=DB$. Therefore $\boxed{DI=DB=DC}$.

\end{asy}
