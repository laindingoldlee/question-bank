Matt will arrange four identical, dotless dominoes ($1\times2$ rectangles) on a $5$ by $4$ grid of unit squares ($5$ columns, $4$ rows) so that a path is formed from the upper left-hand corner $A$ to the lower right-hand corner $B$. In a path, consecutive dominoes must touch at their sides and not just their corners. No domino may be placed diagonally; each domino covers exactly two of the unit squares of the grid. One arrangement is shown. How many distinct arrangements are possible, including the one shown?

\begin{asy}

for(int i=0;i<=W;++i) draw((i,0)--(i,H),gray);
for(int j=0;j<=H;++j) draw((0,j)--(W,j),gray);

 filldraw(box(lo+(0.08,0.08),hi-(0.08,0.08)),mediumgray,black+linewidth(0.8));}
dom((0,3),(1,3));dom((2,3),(2,2));dom((2,1),(3,1));dom((3,0),(4,0));
label("$A$",(0,H),NW);label("$B$",(W,0),SE);
