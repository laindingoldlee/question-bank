Pair the terms: by the triangle inequality,
\[
|x-2|+|x+1|\ge|(x+1)-(x-2)|=3,\qquad |x-1|+|x|\ge|x-(x-1)|=1,
\]
with equality in the first exactly when $-1\le x\le2$ and in the second exactly when $0\le x\le1$. So $f(x)\ge4$ for all $x$, with equality for every $x$ in $[0,1]$; for example $f(0)=2+1+0+1=4$. The minimum value is $\boxed{4}$.

(Geometrically, $f(x)$ is the total distance from $x$ to the points $2,1,0,-1$ on the number line, which is smallest between the two middle points $0$ and $1$.)
