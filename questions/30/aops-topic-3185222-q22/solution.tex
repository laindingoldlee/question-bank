Each root satisfies $r_i^{24}=6r_i-5$. By Vieta's formula, the sum of the roots is zero because the coefficient of $x^{23}$ is zero. Summing the root equations gives $\sum r_i^{24}=6\sum r_i-24\cdot5=\boxed{-120}$.
