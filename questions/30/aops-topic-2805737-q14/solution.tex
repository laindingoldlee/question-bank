If the largest is $a$, the sum is $a(1+\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{9}+\dfrac{1}{27})=360^\circ$. Thus $a=360\cdot\dfrac{27}{40}=\boxed{243^\circ}$, consistent with a concave vertex.
