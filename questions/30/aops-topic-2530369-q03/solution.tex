The height of the cone is $h=\sqrt{9^2-5^2}=\sqrt{56}=2\sqrt{14}$, so its volume is
\[\dfrac13\pi\cdot5^2\cdot2\sqrt{14}=\dfrac{50\sqrt{14}}{3}\pi.\]
(The cone does fit inside the sphere, e.g. with its base centred at the centre of the sphere: every point of the cone is then within $\max\left(5,2\sqrt{14}\right)<15$ of the centre.) The sphere has volume $\dfrac43\pi\cdot15^3=4500\pi$. The required volume is
\[4500\pi-\dfrac{50\sqrt{14}}{3}\pi=\boxed{\dfrac{\left(13500-50\sqrt{14}\right)\pi}{3}}\approx13941.\]
