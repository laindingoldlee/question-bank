The syrup contains $0.25\cdot10=2.5$ lb of sugar. After adding $s$ lb of sugar there are $2.5+s$ lb of sugar in $10+s$ lb of syrup, so
\[
\dfrac{2.5+s}{10+s}=0.4\iff2.5+s=4+0.4s\iff0.6s=1.5\iff s=2.5 .
\]
She needs to add $\boxed{2.5\text{ lb}}$ of sugar. Check: $\dfrac{5}{12.5}=0.4$.
