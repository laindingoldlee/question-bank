Triangle $ABC$ is isosceles with $AB=BC=7$ and $CA=10$. A point $D$ outside triangle $ABC$ satisfies $BD=1$ and $\angle ADC=90^\circ$, and the segment $AD$ neither meets the segment $BC$ nor passes through $B$. What is the smallest possible area of triangle $ADC$? (If no such point $D$ exists, prove it.)
