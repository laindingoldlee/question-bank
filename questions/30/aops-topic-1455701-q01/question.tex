\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] A convex quadrilateral has an inscribed circle (a circle tangent to all four sides) of radius $r$, and its semiperimeter is $s$. Prove that its area is $A=rs$.
\item[(b)] Does $A=rs$ make sense for every quadrilateral? Show that a $1\times2$ rectangle has no inscribed circle.
\end{enumerate}
