The ratio $CW:WF=2:1$ means that $W$ is two-thirds of the way from $C$ to $F$. Thus $W=C+\dfrac{2}{3}(F-C)=(3,-3)+\dfrac{2}{3}(-6,6)=\boxed{(-1,1)}$.

\end{asy}
