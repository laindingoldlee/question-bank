The recurrence counts words with $a,b,c,d$ copies of four letters, so $f=\dfrac{(a+b+c+d)!}{a!b!c!d!}$. The exponent of $2$ in this integer is $s_2(a)+s_2(b)+s_2(c)+s_2(d)-s_2(a+b+c+d)$, from the factorial valuation formula. This is zero exactly when binary addition involves no carries. In each of the four bit positions, a $1$ may occur in none of the four numbers or in exactly one of them: five choices. Independent bit choices give $\boxed{5^4=625}$ quadruples.
