Is there a relation that the slopes of the three sides of every triangle must satisfy? Prove that
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] two sides of a triangle never have the same slope, and
\item[(b)] for any three distinct real numbers $m_1$, $m_2$, $m_3$ there is a triangle whose sides have slopes $m_1$, $m_2$, $m_3$.
\end{enumerate}
