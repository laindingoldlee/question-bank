If $\deg f=m\ge2$, the two sides have degrees $m^2$ and $m$, impossible. Every constant polynomial $f=c$, $-4\le c\le9$, works; its values sum to $35$. For a nonconstant linear polynomial $f=ax+b$, comparison of coefficients gives $a(a-1)=a$ and $ab=-a$, hence $a=2,b=-1$. This contributes $1399$. The required sum is $\boxed{1434}$.
