An airline flies between two countries $A$ and $B$, each with four cities, and operates one two-way flight for each of the $16$ pairs of a city in $A$ and a city in $B$ (and no other flights). It must close $9$ of the $16$ flights, but it wants it still to be possible to travel between any two of the $8$ cities, possibly using several flights. How many choices of $9$ flights to close satisfy this?
