By the distance formula,
\[OQ^2=(-5)^2+\left(5+3\sqrt3\right)^2=25+25+30\sqrt3+27=77+30\sqrt3,\]
\[OR^2=5^2+\left(5-3\sqrt3\right)^2=25+25-30\sqrt3+27=77-30\sqrt3.\]
The sum is $\boxed{154}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real t=3*sqrt(3);
pair O=(0,0), Q=(-5,5+t), R=(5,5-t);
draw((-6.5,0)--(7.5,0),Arrow(6)); draw((0,-1.5)--(0,12),Arrow(6));
label("$x$",(7.5,0),E); label("$y$",(0,12),N);
draw(O--Q,blue+linewidth(0.9)); draw(O--R,blue+linewidth(0.9));
draw(Q--(-5,0),dashed);
draw((5,-0.1)--(5,0.1));
dot(O); dot(Q); dot(R);
label("$O$",O,SW); label("$Q\left(-5,\,5+3\sqrt3\right)$",Q,N); label("$R\left(5,\,5-3\sqrt3\right)$",R,dir(-70));
label("$-5$",(-5,0),S,fontsize(8pt)); label("$5$",(5,0.1),N,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}
