Suppose both of these statements are true:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(1)] Lucas sometimes eats gingerbread cookies.
\item[(2)] Erica never eats sugar cookies.
\end{enumerate}
Which of the following statements must also be true?
\begin{enumerate}
\item[I.] Lucas never eats sugar cookies.
\item[II.] Erica sometimes eats gingerbread cookies.
\item[III.] Lucas and Erica never eat sugar cookies together.
\end{enumerate}
