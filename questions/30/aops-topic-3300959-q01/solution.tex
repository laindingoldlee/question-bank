Let $u_j$ be the win probability when the first gambler has $j$. Then $u_0=0,u_8=1$, and $u_j=\dfrac{2}{3}u_{j+1}+\dfrac{1}{3}u_{j-1}$. Rearrangement gives $u_{j+1}-u_j=\dfrac{1}{2}(u_j-u_{j-1})$. The consecutive differences form a geometric progression, so
\[u_j=\dfrac{1-(\dfrac{1}{2})^j}{1-(\dfrac{1}{2})^8}.\]
At $j=6$ this gives $\boxed{\dfrac{84}{85}}$. The finite absorbing walk reaches a boundary with probability one: in every block of eight games, eight wins or eight losses have a fixed positive probability of absorption. Thus the boundary-value calculation describes the eventual winner.
