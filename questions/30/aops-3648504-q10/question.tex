Let $\vartriangle ABC$ be a triangle with $AB = 13$, $BC = 14$, and $AC = 15$. The $B$-excenter $I_B$ is the center of the circle which lies outside  $\vartriangle ABC$ and is tangent to segment $AC$ as well as lines $AB$ and $BC$. The $C$-excenter $I_C$ is defined similarly. Find the distance between the two points at which line $I_BI_C$ intersects the circumcircle of $\vartriangle ABC$.
