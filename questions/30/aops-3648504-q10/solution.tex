Use $B=(0,0),C=(14,0),A=(5,12)$. Signed incenter coordinates give $I_B=\dfrac{(14A-15B+13C)}{12}=(21,14)$ and $I_C=\dfrac{(14A+15B-13C)}{16}=(-7,\dfrac{21}{2})$. Their line is $8y-x=91$. The circumcenter is $O=(7,\dfrac{33}{8})$, with radius $R=\dfrac{65}{8}$. Its distance to this line is $\dfrac{|-7+33-91|}{\sqrt{65}}=\sqrt{65}$. A chord at distance $d$ from the centre has length $2\sqrt{R^2-d^2}$, here $\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt{65}}{4}}$.

\end{asy}
