Suppose $\sqrt{11}=\dfrac {p}{q}$ with positive integers $p,q$ and the fraction in lowest terms. Then $p^2=11q^2$, so the prime $11$ divides $p^2$ and hence divides $p$ (a prime dividing a product divides one of the factors). Write $p=11k$. Then $121k^2=11q^2$, so $q^2=11k^2$, and in the same way $11$ divides $q$. Now $11$ divides both $p$ and $q$, contradicting that $\dfrac {p}{q}$ is in lowest terms. Hence $\sqrt{11}$ is irrational. $\blacksquare$
