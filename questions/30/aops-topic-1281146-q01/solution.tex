The first three factors give $0.9\cdot0.99\cdot0.999=0.890109$. For the rest, using $(1-x_1)(1-x_2)\cdots\ge1-(x_1+x_2+\cdots)$ for $0\le x_i\le1$,
\[
1\ge\prod_{k=4}^{2014}\left(1-\dfrac{1}{10^k}\right)\ge1-\sum_{k=4}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{10^k}=1-\dfrac{1}{9000}.
\]
So the product $P$ satisfies $0.890109\left(1-\dfrac{1}{9000}\right)<P\le0.890109$, i.e. $0.89000<P<0.89011$. To the nearest thousandth, $P\approx\boxed{0.890}$.
