Symmetry gives $AD=AE$. In triangle $ABE$, the angles at $A,B,E$ are $60^\circ,15^\circ,105^\circ$. The sine rule yields $\dfrac{AE}{AB}=\dfrac{\sin15^\circ}{\sin105^\circ}=2-\sqrt3$. Thus $AE=AD=4-2\sqrt3$. Since their included angle is $60^\circ$, triangle $ADE$ is equilateral, giving $\boxed{DE=4-2\sqrt3}$.
