Infinitely many solutions require proportional equations. The coefficient determinant is $56-m(m+1)$, which vanishes only at $m=7$ or $-8$. At $m=7$, the second equation is exactly $-2$ times the first, including its constant, so all points on one line solve the system. At $m=-8$, the left sides have ratio $\dfrac{7}{4}$, but the constants are $-7$ and 29 and lack that ratio, so there is no solution. Thus $\boxed{m=7}$.
