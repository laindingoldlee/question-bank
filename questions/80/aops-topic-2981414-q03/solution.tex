Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral are supplementary. So $\angle C=180^\circ-64^\circ=\boxed{116^\circ}$ and $\angle D=180^\circ-104^\circ=\boxed{76^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw(circle((0,0),1));pair A=dir(200),B=dir(110),C=dir(20),D=dir(-70);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);
label("$A$",A,W);label("$B$",B,N);label("$C$",C,E);label("$D$",D,S);
\end{asy}
