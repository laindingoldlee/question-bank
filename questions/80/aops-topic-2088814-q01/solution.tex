Triangles $OAB$ and $OCD$ are similar ($AB\parallel CD$), so $\dfrac{DO}{OB}=\dfrac {n}{m}$ and $\dfrac{DO}{DB}=\dfrac{n}{m+n}$. Since $EO\parallel AB$, triangles $DEO$ and $DAB$ are similar, so
\[EO=AB\cdot\dfrac{DO}{DB}=\dfrac{mn}{m+n}.\]
In the same way (using triangles $COF$ and $CAB$) $OF=\dfrac{mn}{m+n}$, so
\[EF=\dfrac{2mn}{m+n},\]
the harmonic mean of the bases. We need distinct positive integers $m,n$ with $m+n$ dividing $2mn$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $m+n=7$: $7$ is prime and does not divide $2mn$ (as $m,n<7$).
\item $m+n\le6$: the pairs $\{1,2\},\{1,3\},\{1,4\},\{2,3\},\{1,5\},\{2,4\}$ give $2mn=4,6,8,12,10,16$, not divisible by $3,4,5,5,6,6$ respectively.
\item $m+n=8$: $\{2,6\}$ gives $EF=\dfrac{24}{8}=3$.
\end{itemize}
So the least possible value of $m+n$ is $\boxed{8}$ (bases $2$ and $6$, with $EF=3$).
