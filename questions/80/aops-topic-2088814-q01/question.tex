In trapezoid $ABCD$, $AB\parallel DC$. Points $E$ and $F$ lie on $AD$ and $BC$, respectively, so that segment $EF$ passes through the intersection of the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ and is parallel to $AB$ and $CD$. Suppose $AB=m$ and $CD=n$, where $m$ and $n$ are distinct positive integers, and $EF$ is also an integer. What is the least possible value of $m+n$?
