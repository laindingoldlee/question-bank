$0.\overline{23}_k=(2k+3)\left(\dfrac1{k^2}+\dfrac1{k^4}+\cdots\right)$, a geometric series with ratio $\dfrac1{k^2}$, so it equals $\dfrac{2k+3}{k^2}\cdot\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac1{k^2}}=\dfrac{2k+3}{k^2-1}$.

Setting $\dfrac{2k+3}{k^2-1}=\dfrac7{51}$: $7k^2-102k-160=0$, so $(k-16)(7k+10)=0$ and $k=\boxed{16}$.
