The right side exceeds one, so $x>1$. Put $t=x^3>1$ and cube both sides to get $t^t=36^3=6^6$. The function $t^t$ is strictly increasing for $t>1$: increasing either its base or its positive exponent strictly increases its value. Hence $t=6$, giving $\boxed{x=\sqrt[3]6}$. Direct substitution yields $(\sqrt[3]6)^6=36$.
