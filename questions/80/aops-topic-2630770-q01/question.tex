A smaller circle has radius $r$, circumference $c$ and diameter $d$; a larger circle has radius $R$, circumference $C$ and diameter $D$. The larger circle is the image of the smaller one under a dilation with scale factor $k>0$. Fill in the blank, and justify your answer: the ratios $\dfrac Cc$, $\dfrac Rr$ and $\dfrac Dd$ are all equal to \underline{\hspace{1.5cm}}.
