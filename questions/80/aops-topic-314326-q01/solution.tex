Eight rows begin and end with white, each containing $8$ white squares. The other seven rows each contain $7$. Thus the white count is $8\cdot8+7\cdot7=\boxed{113}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
for(int i=0;i<15;++i)for(int j=0;j<15;++j){path p=(i,j)--(i+1,j)--(i+1,j+1)--(i,j+1)--cycle;filldraw(p,((i+j)%2==0)?white:black,gray);}label("$15\times15$",(7.5,-1));
\end{asy}
