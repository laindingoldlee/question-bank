Every two-digit palindrome is $11d$ for $1\le d\le9$, so the sum is a multiple of $11$. The least three-digit multiple is $110$, which is not a palindrome and satisfies $110=11+22+77$ with distinct terms. Thus $N=110$ and its digit sum is $\boxed2$.
