Rita is selecting a sandwich at the deli. The deli has four types of meat, three types of cheese and two types of bread. A deluxe sandwich consists of exactly one meat type, two different types of cheese and one bread type. How many different deluxe sandwich combinations are possible? A student counts the cheese choices as $3\cdot2=6$ (three choices for the first cheese, then two for the second) and gets $4\cdot6\cdot2=48$. Explain the error.
