By Vieta, $\alpha+\beta+\gamma=-p$, $\alpha\beta+\beta\gamma+\gamma\alpha=q$, $\alpha\beta\gamma=-r$. For the new roots: sum $=q$; sum of pairwise products $=\alpha\beta\gamma(\alpha+\beta+\gamma)=pr$; product $=(\alpha\beta\gamma)^2=r^2$. So the equation is $x^3-qx^2+prx-r^2=0$: $\boxed{\text{(d)}}$.
