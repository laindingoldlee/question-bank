Divisibility by 2,3,5 is equivalent to being a multiple of 30. Among them, those divisible by 4 are the multiples of 60. Thus the count is $\lfloor\dfrac{999}{30}\rfloor-\lfloor\dfrac{999}{60}\rfloor=33-16=\boxed{17}$.
