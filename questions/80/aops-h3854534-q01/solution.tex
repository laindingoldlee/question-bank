The target generating polynomial is $x^2(1+x+\cdots+x^{11})^2$. Each replacement polynomial has lowest exponent at least one, so each has lowest exponent exactly one. After removing those factors of $x$, the cyclotomic factorization is $\Phi_2^2\Phi_3^2\Phi_4^2\Phi_6^2\Phi_{12}^2$. Evaluation at one forces all $\Phi_3^2$ into the nine-face polynomial and all $\Phi_2^2\Phi_4^2$ into the sixteen-face polynomial. The other factors evaluate to one. Thus the degree of the nine-face polynomial after removing $x$ is at most $4+4+8=16$. Every cyclotomic factor here is reciprocal, so that die\textquotesingle s mean is at most nine. The two means sum to thirteen, making the total label sum $9u+16(13-u)=208-7u\ge145$. Attain equality with generating polynomials $x(1+x^4+x^8)^2$ and $x(1+x+x^2+x^3)^2$. Their coefficient sums are nine and sixteen and their product is the target. Hence $\boxed{145}$.
