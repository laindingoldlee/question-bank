Twelve years contain four three-year periods, and each period multiplies the population by $1.15$. So the population becomes
\[
1{,}234{,}137\cdot1.15^4=1{,}234{,}137\cdot1.74900625=2{,}158{,}513.33\ldots,
\]
which is about $\boxed{2{,}158{,}513}$ people.
