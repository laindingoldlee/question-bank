Long division gives $\dfrac{1}{7}=0.\overline{142857}$, with a repeating block of $6$ digits. Since $100=6\cdot16+4$, the $100^{\text{th}}$ digit is the $4^{\text{th}}$ digit of the block $142857$, which is $\boxed{8}$.
