Of the $36$ equally likely ordered outcomes, the six $(1,6),(2,5),(3,4),(4,3),(5,2),(6,1)$ have sum $7$. Thus the probability is $\dfrac{6}{36}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{6}}$.
