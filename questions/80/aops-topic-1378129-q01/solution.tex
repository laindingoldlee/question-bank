Use the Euclidean algorithm to find the greatest common divisor:
\[
\begin{aligned}
67891&=5\cdot13245+1666,\\
13245&=7\cdot1666+1583,\\
1666&=1\cdot1583+83,\\
1583&=19\cdot83+6,\\
83&=13\cdot6+5,\\
6&=1\cdot5+1 .
\end{aligned}
\]
So $\gcd(13245,67891)=1$, and the least common multiple is the product:
\[
\operatorname{lcm}(13245,67891)=13245\cdot67891=\boxed{899216295}.
\]
