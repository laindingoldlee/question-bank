Put $c=\cos40^\circ$ and $s=\sin40^\circ$. Since $\cot10^\circ=\tan80^\circ$, the expression is
\[
s\left[10-(2c-1)\left(\dfrac{2c}{2c^2-1}-\dfrac{4}{c}\right)\right].
\]
The triple-angle formula gives $8c^3-6c+1=0$. Direct common-denominator simplification shows
\[
10-(2c-1)\left(\dfrac{2c}{2c^2-1}-\dfrac{4}{c}\right)-6(4c^2-1)
=-\dfrac{2(3c^2-2)(8c^3-6c+1)}{c(2c^2-1)}=0.
\]
Thus the answer is $6s(4c^2-1)=6\sin120^\circ=\boxed{3\sqrt3}$.
