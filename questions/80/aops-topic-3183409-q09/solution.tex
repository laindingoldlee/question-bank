The semicircle radius is 2, so the square side is 4. In coordinates $A=(0,0),B=(4,0),C=(4,4),D=(0,4)$, we have $O=(0,2)$ and $BO=\sqrt{4^2+2^2}=2\sqrt5$. Thus $CP=BP-BC=\boxed{2\sqrt5-4}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(4,0),C=(4,4),D=(0,4),O=(0,2),P=(4,sqrt(20));draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(arc(O,2,90,270));draw(B--O^^B--P);draw(arc(B,sqrt(20),70,170),dashed);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,E);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$O$",O,W);label("$P$",P,N);
\end{asy}
