Write $N=5^a7^bM$ with $\gcd(M,35)=1$, and let $T=\tau(M^2)$, an odd positive integer. The equations are $(2a+2)(2b+1)T=18$ and $(2a+1)(2b+2)T=20$. Since $T$ divides both 18 and 20 and is odd, $T=1$, so $M=1$. Now $(a+1)(2b+1)=9$ and $(2a+1)(b+1)=10$. Testing the three odd factors of nine gives only $a=2,b=1$. Therefore $\boxed{N=175}$.
