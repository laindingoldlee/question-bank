Fred and Jane play a coin-flipping game with a fair coin. On each flip, Fred gets a point if it lands heads and Jane gets a point otherwise. The first person to score $10$ points wins. The score is now $8$ to $7$ in Fred's favour. What is the probability that Fred wins? Express your answer as a common fraction.
