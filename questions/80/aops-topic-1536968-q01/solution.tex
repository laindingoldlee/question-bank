Fred needs $2$ more points and Jane needs $3$. Imagine that exactly $4$ more flips are made, even if the game is already decided. Fred wins the real game exactly when at least $2$ of these $4$ flips are heads: if so, he reaches $10$ before Jane can collect $3$ tails (there are at most $2$ tails), and otherwise Jane gets at least $3$ tails before Fred gets $2$ heads. Hence
\[
P(\text{Fred wins})=1-\dfrac{\dbinom40+\dbinom41}{2^4}=1-\dfrac{5}{16}=\boxed{\dfrac{11}{16}}.
\]
