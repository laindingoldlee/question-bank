If the square's side is $s$, each rectangle has sides $s$ and $\dfrac{s}{3}$. Its perimeter is $2(s+\dfrac{s}{3})=\dfrac{8s}{3}=24$, so $s=9$. The area is $\boxed{81}$, choice \textbf{D}.
