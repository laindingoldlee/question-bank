If $n$ has six different prime factors $p_1<p_2<\cdots<p_6$, then $p_i$ is at least the $i$-th prime, so
\[
n\ge p_1p_2\cdots p_6\ge2\cdot3\cdot5\cdot7\cdot11\cdot13=30030 .
\]
And $30030$ itself has the six prime factors $2,3,5,7,11,13$. So the answer is $\boxed{30030}$.
