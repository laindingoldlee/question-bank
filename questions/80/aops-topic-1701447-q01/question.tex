Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle with circumcircle $S$ and circumcentre $O$. Let line $AO$ meet $BC$ at $E$, and let the altitude from $A$ meet $S$ again at $D$. Prove that the lines $DE$ and $BO$ meet at a point of $S$ if and only if
\[
\sin B\cos^2B=\sin C\cos^2C\cos A,
\]
and show that this happens, for example, when $\angle A=\angle B=67.5^\circ$ and $\angle C=45^\circ$.
