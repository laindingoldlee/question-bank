Since $f(x)-f(-x)=2bx$, we get $f(4)-f(-4)=8b=9$, so $b=\dfrac{9}{8}$. Then
\[f(1)=f(-1)+2b=1+\dfrac{9}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{13}{4}}.\]
(Solving fully: $a=\dfrac{43}{120}$, $b=\dfrac{9}{8}$, $c=\dfrac{53}{30}$, and $a+b+c=\dfrac{43+135+212}{120}=\dfrac{390}{120}=\dfrac{13}{4}$.)
