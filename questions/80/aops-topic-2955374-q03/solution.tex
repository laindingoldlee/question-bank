There are $\dbinom{10}2=45$ pairs. Count pairs $a<b$ with $a+b\le10$: $a=0$: $9$; $a=1$: $8$; $a=2$: $6$ ($b=3..8$); $a=3$: $4$; $a=4$: $2$; $a\ge5$: none. Total $29$, so the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{29}{45}}$.
