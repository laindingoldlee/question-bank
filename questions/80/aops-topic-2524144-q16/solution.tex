For every $x$ in the domain of $f^{-1}$, $f\left(f^{-1}(x)\right)=x$, since $f$ undoes $f^{-1}$. So $f\left(f^{-1}(7)\right)=\boxed{7}$. (Directly: $f^{-1}(7)=45$ and $f(x)=\dfrac{x+4}7$, so $f(45)=7$.)
