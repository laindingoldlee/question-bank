A game ending with exactly $10$ points is a sequence of positive integers with sum $10$; every such sequence is a valid game, because all its partial sums before the last are less than $10$ (so the game does not stop early) and every part is at most $10$. So we count the compositions of $10$. Write $10$ as ten $1$'s in a row; a composition corresponds to choosing which of the $9$ gaps between them are cuts. Hence there are $2^9=\boxed{512}$ ways.
