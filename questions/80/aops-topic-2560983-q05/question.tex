In a game, on each turn Ivy earns $1,2,3,\dots,9$ or $10$ points, and the game ends as soon as her total is at least $10$. Different orders of the turns count as different ways (for example, $4$ then $6$ is different from $6$ then $4$). In how many ways can she finish the game with exactly $10$ points?
