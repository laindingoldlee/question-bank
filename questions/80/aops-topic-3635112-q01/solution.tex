Since $-17=-4\cdot 5+3$, the first residue is $\boxed 3$. Powers of $7$ modulo $10$ cycle through $7,9,3,1$ with period $4$. Since $2025\equiv 1\pmod 4$, $7^{2025}\equiv 7\pmod{10}$, so its units digit is $\boxed 7$.
