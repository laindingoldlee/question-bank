The ice and water account for $\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{3}=\dfrac{5}{6}$ of the pole, so the $8$ inches above the ice are the remaining $\dfrac{1}{6}$. The pole is $6\cdot8=\boxed{48\text{ inches}}$ long. (Check: $24$ in the ice, $16$ in the water, $8$ above: $24+16+8=48$.)
