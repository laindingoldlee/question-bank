Every sibling of Jim must share a characteristic with Jim (brown eyes or blond hair). Benjamin and Tevyn have blue eyes and black hair, so they share nothing with Jim and are in the other family. The remaining candidates are Nadeen (brown, black), Austin (blue, blond) and Sue (blue, blond).

Jim's two siblings must also share a characteristic with each other. Nadeen shares nothing with Austin, and nothing with Sue. So Jim's siblings are Austin and Sue, and the other family is Benjamin, Nadeen and Tevyn, in which every two children share black hair, as required.

The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(E)}\ \text{Austin and Sue}}$. (Under the stricter reading that one characteristic is common to all three siblings, the answer is the same: Jim, Austin and Sue all have blond hair, and Benjamin, Nadeen and Tevyn all have black hair.)
