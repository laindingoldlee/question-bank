Draw diagonals from one vertex to every nonadjacent vertex. Convexity keeps these diagonals inside the polygon, dividing it into $n-2$ triangles with disjoint interiors. Their angles partition the polygon angles, so the sum is $(n-2)180^\circ$. For $n=5,6$, this gives $\boxed{540^\circ,720^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair[] P; for(int i=0;i<6;++i)P.push(dir(90+60*i));for(int i=0;i<6;++i){draw(P[i]--P[(i+1)%6]);label("$"+string(i+1)+"$",P[i],P[i]);}for(int i=2;i<=4;++i)draw(P[0]--P[i],blue);
\end{asy}
