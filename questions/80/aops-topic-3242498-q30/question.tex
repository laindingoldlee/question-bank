Define $f(X)=\dfrac{X}{2}$ for even positive integers $X$, and $f(X)=3X+1$ for odd positive integers $X$. Find all positive integers $N$ such that $N\nmid X$ implies $N\nmid f(X)$ for every positive integer $X$.
