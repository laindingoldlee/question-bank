Take $AB$ on $y=0$ and $CD$ on $y=6$. Since $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot6\cdot|y_E|=9$, $y_E=3$ or $-3$. The respective distances to $CD$ are $|3-6|=3$ and $|-3-6|=9$. Their sum is $\boxed{12}$.
