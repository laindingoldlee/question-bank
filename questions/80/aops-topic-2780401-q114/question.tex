The cyclic entries of a square start as $(1,2,3,4)$. Each round, Leptina swaps two adjacent entries; then Zandar chooses adjacent $ a\ge b $ and replaces $ a $ by $ a-b $. Leptina delays and Zandar hastens the first time their sum is 2. What is the least number of Zandar's moves that guarantees success?
