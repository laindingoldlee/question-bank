For the first word, if A is written $a$ times and S is written $s$ times, the number of arrangements of the letters is $\dfrac{(1+a+s)!}{a!\,s!}$. Different pairs $(a,s)$ give different collections of letters, so the counts add:
\[\begin{array}{c|ccc} & s=1 & s=2 & s=3\\ \hline a=1 & 6 & 12 & 20\\ a=2 & 12 & 30 & 60\\ a=3 & 20 & 60 & 140\end{array}\qquad\text{total }360.\]
The second word has the same structure (one I, $v$ copies of V and $y$ copies of Y), so it also has $360$ possibilities. The two words are independent, so the number of phrases is $360^2=\boxed{129600}$.
