Hanna writes the phrase "YAS IVY". In the word YAS she writes the letter Y once, the letter A either $1$, $2$ or $3$ times and the letter S either $1$, $2$ or $3$ times. In the word IVY she writes the letter I once, the letter V either $1$, $2$ or $3$ times and the letter Y either $1$, $2$ or $3$ times. She then rearranges the letters within each word (letters cannot move from one word to the other). How many different final phrases are possible?
