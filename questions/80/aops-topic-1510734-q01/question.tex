Pierre throws darts that land randomly on a dartboard. The dartboard is a circle of radius $2$ units with a concentric inner circle of radius $1$ unit, and both circles are divided into six congruent sectors by three diameters. Four of the six inner sectors are numbered. Assuming each dart lands at a uniformly random point of the board, what is the probability that a dart lands in one of the four inner numbered sectors? Express your answer as a decimal to the nearest hundredth.
