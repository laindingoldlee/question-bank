$10+10^2+\cdots+10^{2016}$ is $2016$ ones followed by a $0$. Its last five digits are $11110$, and $11110-2016=9094$, i.e. $09094$, with no borrow past these five digits. So the result is $2012$ ones followed by $09094$, and its digit sum is $2012+9+9+4=\boxed{2034}$.
