Zeus threw, on average, $12$ lightning bolts per day in the month of March. During the first week of April, he averaged $15$ lightning bolts per day. How many lightning bolts does Zeus need to throw per day, on average, for the rest of April to keep a $12$-bolt-per-day average over March and April together? Express your answer to the nearest integer.
