March and April have $31+30=61$ days, so he needs $61\cdot12=732$ bolts in total. March gave $31\cdot12=372$, and the first week of April $7\cdot15=105$, so the remaining $30-7=23$ days need
\[
732-372-105=255\text{ bolts},\qquad\dfrac{255}{23}\approx11.09 ,
\]
i.e.\ about $\boxed{11}$ bolts per day. (Equivalently: April alone must average $12$, i.e.\ $360$ bolts, and $360-105=255$.)
