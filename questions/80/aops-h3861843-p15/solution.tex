Rearrange as $(a-b)^2-(ab)^2=9$. With $d=a-b>0$, $(d-ab)(d+ab)=9$. Both factors are positive since their sum is positive. The ordered factor pairs are $(1,9),(3,3),(9,1)$, giving $(d,ab)=(5,4),(3,0),(5,-4)$. The first has no integer solution because $b^2+5b-4=0$ has nonsquare discriminant $41$. The second gives $(a,b)=(3,0),(0,-3)$. The third gives $b^2+5b+4=0$, hence $(a,b)=(4,-1),(1,-4)$. These four pairs all satisfy the original equation.
