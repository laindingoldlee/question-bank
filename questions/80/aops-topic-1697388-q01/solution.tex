Let Jack's time be $t$ hours. Jill covers the same distance at $1.3$ times the speed, so her time is $\dfrac{t}{1.3}=\dfrac{10t}{13}$. The difference is $20$ minutes $=\dfrac{1}{3}$ hour:
\[
t-\dfrac{10t}{13}=\dfrac{3t}{13}=\dfrac{1}{3}\ \Longrightarrow\ t=\boxed{\dfrac{13}{9}}\text{ hours}
\]
(that is, $1$ hour $26\dfrac{2}{3}$ minutes). Check: Jill drove $\dfrac{10}{9}$ hours, and $\dfrac{13}{9}-\dfrac{10}{9}=\dfrac{1}{3}$.
