The sum is $\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{1}{10}+\dfrac{1}{11}+\dots+\dfrac{1}{19}\right)$. Adding the ten fractions over the common denominator $\operatorname{lcm}(10,\dots,19)$ and halving gives
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{33464927}{93117024}}\approx0.3594 .
\]
(The pairing $\dfrac{1}{10+k}+\dfrac{1}{19-k}=\dfrac{29}{(10+k)(19-k)}$ for $k=0,\dots,4$ shortens the work: the sum is $\dfrac{29}{2}\left(\dfrac{1}{190}+\dfrac{1}{198}+\dfrac{1}{204}+\dfrac{1}{208}+\dfrac{1}{210}\right)$.)
