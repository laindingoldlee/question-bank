In the right triangle $AHC$ the angle at $A$ is $45^\circ$, so it is isosceles: $AH=CH$. Since $CH=\dfrac{1}{2}AB$, also $AH=\dfrac{1}{2}AB$, so $H$ is the midpoint of $AB$ and $HB=\dfrac{1}{2}AB=CH$.

Then the right triangle $BHC$ is also isosceles ($HB=HC$), so $\angle HBC=45^\circ$. Hence $\angle ABC=\boxed{45^\circ}$ (and $\angle ACB=90^\circ$). The figure marks $AH=HB=HC$.
