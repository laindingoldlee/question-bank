Divide by $c^d>0$ and put $u=\dfrac {a}{c}$, $v=\dfrac {b}{c}$, so $0<u,v<1$ and $u+v=1$. The equation becomes
\[
u^d+v^d=1 .
\]
Since $0<u<1$ and $0<v<1$, both $u^d$ and $v^d$ are strictly decreasing in $d$, so the left side is strictly decreasing and equals $1$ for at most one $d$. As $u+v=1$, $d=1$ works. Hence the only solution is $\boxed{d=1}$.
