If the number has $k$ twos, it has $7-2k$ ones and $7-k$ digits, and it is determined by choosing which $k$ of the $7-k$ positions hold the twos. So the count is
\[
\sum_{k=0}^{3}\dbinom{7-k}{k}=\dbinom70+\dbinom61+\dbinom52+\dbinom43=1+6+10+4=\boxed{21}.
\]
(These are the compositions of $7$ into parts $1$ and $2$, counted by the Fibonacci number $F_8=21$.)
