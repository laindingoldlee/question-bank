A sculpture is made of a $1$-inch cube placed on top of a $3$-inch cube, which is placed on top of a $5$-inch cube; each cube sits in the middle of the top face of the cube below it. The entire surface of the sculpture is painted, including the bottom face of the $5$-inch cube. How many square inches of surface are painted?
