Use $A=(0,0)$, $B=(1,0)$, $C=(1,1)$, $D=(0,1)$, so $M=(\dfrac{1}{2},1)$. The slope of $AM$ is $2$, hence that of $MN$ is $-\dfrac{1}{2}$. At $x=1$, $N=(1,\dfrac{3}{4})$. The line $NP$ has slope $2$, so setting $y=0$ gives $P=(\dfrac{5}{8},0)$. Therefore $\dfrac{AP}{PB}=\dfrac{\dfrac{5}{8}}{\dfrac{3}{8}}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{3}}$.

\end{asy}
