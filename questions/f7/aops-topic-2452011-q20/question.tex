Ash is choosing $15$ new members of the student council from $100$ distinguishable students. At most $6$ of the new members may be ``special'' members and at most $9$ may be ``normal'' members, and each new member is one or the other. In how many ways can he choose the $15$ new members (deciding also who is special and who is normal)?
