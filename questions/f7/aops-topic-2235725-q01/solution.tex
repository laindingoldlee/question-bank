Every term of the expansion has degree $4$, so a term containing $a^2b^2$ contains no $c$. Setting $c=0$ keeps exactly the terms without $c$:
\[(-a)(-b)(a-b)(a+b)=ab\left(a^2-b^2\right)=a^3b-ab^3 .\]
There is no $a^2b^2$ term here, so the coefficient is $\boxed{0}$.
