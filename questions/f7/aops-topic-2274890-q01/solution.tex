Let $x=0.121212\ldots_3$. Multiplying by $3^2=9$ shifts the base-three point two places:
\[9x=12.121212\ldots_3=12_3+x=5+x.\]
So $8x=5$ and $x=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{8}}$.

Check with a geometric series: each block $12_3$ contributes $\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{2}{9}=\dfrac{5}{9}$ times a power of $\dfrac{1}{9}$, so
\[x=\dfrac{5}{9}\left(1+\dfrac{1}{9}+\dfrac{1}{81}+\cdots\right)=\dfrac{5}{9}\cdot\dfrac{9}{8}=\dfrac{5}{8}.\]
