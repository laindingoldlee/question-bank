\textbf{(a)} By the difference of squares, $b^2-a^2=(b-a)(b+a)$. If $a=b-1$, then $b-a=1$, so $b^2-a^2=a+b$. $\blacksquare$

\textbf{(b)} With $b=1002$ and $a=1001=b-1$, part (a) gives $1002^2-1001^2=1001+1002=\boxed{2003}$.

\textbf{(c)} Here the difference is $2$: $1003^2-1001^2=(1003-1001)(1003+1001)=2\cdot2004=\boxed{4008}$.
