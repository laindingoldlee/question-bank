A circle is tangent to segment $AB$ at $A$ and to segment $BC$ at $C$. If $AB=BC=13$ and $AC=10$, what is the radius of the circle?

$\textbf{(A)}\ $$\dfrac{60}{13}$ \qquad $\textbf{(B)}\ $$5$ \qquad $\textbf{(C)}\ $$\dfrac{26}5$ \qquad $\textbf{(D)}\ $$\dfrac{65}{12}$ \qquad $\textbf{(E)}\ $$\dfrac{156}{25}$
