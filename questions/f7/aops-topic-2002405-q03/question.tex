Call a $4$-dimensional rectangular box a \emph{quarbe}; its side lengths are written $(w,x,y,z)$ with all entries positive. For example, the volume of a $(3,4,5,6)$ quarbe is $3\cdot4\cdot5\cdot6=360$. Place a $(3,4,5,6)$ quarbe as $[0,3]\times[0,4]\times[0,5]\times[0,6]$. How many quarbes of all sizes, with integer side lengths and with all vertices at integer points, lie inside it (with edges parallel to its edges)?
