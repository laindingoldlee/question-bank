A sub-quarbe with edges parallel to the coordinate axes and integer vertices is determined by choosing, independently in each coordinate, two distinct integer endpoints. In the first coordinate the endpoints are chosen from $\{0,1,2,3\}$ in $\binom42=6$ ways; similarly $\binom52=10$, $\binom62=15$ and $\binom72=21$ ways in the other coordinates. The total is
\[
6\cdot10\cdot15\cdot21=\boxed{18900}.
\]
