Let $P(x)=13(x-a)(x-b)(x-c)$. We have $P(1)=9$ and $P(-1)=-27$. Thus $P(1)P(-1)=-169(1-a^2)(1-b^2)(1-c^2)$. The requested reciprocal is $-\dfrac{169}{9(-27)}=\boxed{\dfrac{169}{243}}$.
