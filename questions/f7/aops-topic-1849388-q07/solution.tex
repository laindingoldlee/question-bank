Let the coloured cells be in positions $p_1<p_2<p_3<p_4$ with $p_{i+1}-p_i\ge2$. Put $q_i=p_i-(i-1)$. Then $1\le q_1<q_2<q_3<q_4\le10-3=7$, and conversely every $4$-element subset of $\{1,\dots,7\}$ gives a valid colouring. This is a one-to-one correspondence, so the number of colourings is $\dbinom74=\boxed{35}$.

(Equivalently: place the $6$ uncoloured cells in a row; the $4$ coloured cells go into $4$ of the $7$ gaps between and around them.)
