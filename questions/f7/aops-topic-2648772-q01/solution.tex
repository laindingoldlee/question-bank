We claim $\displaystyle\sum_{k}\binom nk\binom k3=\binom n32^{n-3}$. The left side counts pairs $(S,T)$ with $T\subseteq S\subseteq\{1,\dots,n\}$ and $|T|=3$: choose $S$ of size $k$, then $T$ inside it. Alternatively choose $T$ first ($\binom n3$ ways) and then $S\setminus T$ as any subset of the other $n-3$ elements ($2^{n-3}$ ways).

With $n=2012$,
\[P=\dfrac{\dfrac{2012\cdot2011\cdot2010}{6}\cdot2^{2009}}{2011\cdot2^{2011}}=\dfrac{2012\cdot2010}{24}=\dfrac{4044120}{24}=\boxed{168505}.\]
