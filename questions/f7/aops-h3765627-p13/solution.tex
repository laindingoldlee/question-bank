If there were at least 102 people, select 102 of them and sum the numbers of friendships over all their 100-person subsets. There are $\binom{102}{100}=5151$ subsets, so this sum would be odd. But each friendship is counted $\binom{100}{98}=4950$ times, making the sum even, a contradiction. For 101 people, arrange friendships as the edges of a cycle of length 101. Every 100-person subset deletes one vertex and its two incident edges, leaving 99 friendships, odd. Hence the maximum is $\boxed{101}$.
