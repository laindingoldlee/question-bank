\textbf{(a)} Let $t=\sqrt x>0$. The equation becomes $t^2-4=\sqrt{2018+t}$; the right side is positive, so $t>2$. Let $g(t)=t^2-4-\sqrt{2018+t}$. For $2\le s<t$,
\[
\left(t^2-4\right)-\left(s^2-4\right)=(t-s)(t+s)\ge4(t-s),\qquad\sqrt{2018+t}-\sqrt{2018+s}=\dfrac{t-s}{\sqrt{2018+t}+\sqrt{2018+s}}<t-s ,
\]
so $g(t)>g(s)$: $g$ is strictly increasing and has at most one zero. Now $t=7$ works: $49-4=45=\sqrt{2025}=\sqrt{2018+7}$. So $t=7$ and $\boxed{x=49}$.

\textbf{(b)} The system says $x^2+y^2+z^2=2018$ and $20x+23y+33z=2018$. Note that $20^2+23^2+33^2=400+529+1089=2018$. Hence
\[
(x-20)^2+(y-23)^2+(z-33)^2=\left(x^2+y^2+z^2\right)-2(20x+23y+33z)+2018=2018-4036+2018=0 .
\]
A sum of squares of real numbers is $0$ only if each is $0$, so $\boxed{(x,y,z)=(20,23,33)}$, which indeed satisfies both equations.
