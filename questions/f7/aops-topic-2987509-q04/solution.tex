After $n$ full days and nights he is $n$ miles up. At the start of day $28$ he is $27$ miles up, and that day he climbs $3$ miles to reach $30$. (He never reaches it earlier: at the end of day $k\le27$ he is at most $k+2\le29$ miles up.) Answer: day $\boxed{28}$.
