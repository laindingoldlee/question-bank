Two planes $M$ and $N$ are each perpendicular to a third plane $P$, and $M$ and $N$ intersect in a line $m$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] A student thinks that two planes perpendicular to the same plane must be parallel, so that $M$ and $N$ cannot intersect. Give an example showing that this is false.
\item[(b)] Prove that the line $m$ is perpendicular to $P$.
\end{enumerate}
