For $x,y\ge0$, $\sqrt x\sqrt y$ is nonnegative and its square equals $xy$, so it is the principal square root of $xy$. For $x=y=-1$, principal roots give $\sqrt x\sqrt y=i^2=-1$ while $\sqrt{xy}=\sqrt1=1$. Thus the unrestricted complex identity is false.
