There are $4!=24$ seatings. Angie has four choices; Carlos is then forced into the opposite seat. Bridget and Diego can fill the remaining seats in $2!$ ways, so there are $4\cdot2=8$ favourable arrangements. The probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{3}}$. Counting four misses the two possible orders of the other people.
