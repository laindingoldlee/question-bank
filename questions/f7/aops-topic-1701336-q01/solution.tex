A perfect square with three different prime factors $p<q<r$ has each of them to an even power, so it is a multiple of $(pqr)^2$. The smallest choice is $p,q,r=2,3,5$ with exponents $2$, giving $(2\cdot3\cdot5)^2=\boxed{900}$. Any other such square is at least $(2\cdot3\cdot5)^2$, since replacing a prime by a larger one or raising an exponent only increases it.
