Two dice are rolled repeatedly, and a dot is placed at $(a-1,b-1)$, a point of the $6\times6$ grid $\{0,\dots,5\}^2$ (repeated points do not count as new moves). What is the maximum number of distinct points that can be placed with no four of them forming a (non-degenerate) parallelogram?
