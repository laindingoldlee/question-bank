Skip is training for a marathon and has $7$ weeks to prepare. He runs $x$ miles in week $1$, and in each later week he runs $1.1$ times as far as in the week before. What is the least value of $x$ for which he runs at least $50$ miles in week $7$? Give your answer to two decimal places.
