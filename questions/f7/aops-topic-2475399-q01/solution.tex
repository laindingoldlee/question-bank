The hour hand turns $\dfrac{360^\circ}{10}=36^\circ$ per hour. In $69\dfrac23=\dfrac{209}3$ hours it turns $36\cdot\dfrac{209}3=2508^\circ$, and $2508-6\cdot360=348$, so it points $348^\circ$ clockwise from the top.

The minute hand makes a full turn every hour, so after $69$ hours and $40$ minutes it is at $\dfrac{40}{60}\cdot360^\circ=240^\circ$.

The hands differ by $348^\circ-240^\circ=108^\circ\le180^\circ$, so the angle between them is $\boxed{108^\circ}$.
