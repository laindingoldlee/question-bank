A $10$-hour analogue clock has $10$ hour marks, each $6$ minutes apart for the minute hand; the hour hand makes one full turn in $10$ hours, the minute hand one full turn every $60$ minutes, and each hour has $60$ minutes. The clock starts with both hands pointing straight up and runs for $69$ hours and $40$ minutes. What is the angle between the hour hand and the minute hand then?
