$\left(a^3b^5\right)^4=a^{12}b^{20}$. A positive factor is $a^ib^j$ with $0\le i\le12$ and $0\le j\le20$, and different pairs $(i,j)$ give different factors (unique factorisation). So there are $13\cdot21=\boxed{273}$ factors.
