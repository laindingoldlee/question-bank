Let $T>36$ be the minutes from Olivia's start until the second meeting. Their speeds in laps per minute are $\dfrac{1}{T}$ and $\dfrac{1}{T-20}$. At the first meeting their total distance is one lap, so $\dfrac{36}{T}+\dfrac{16}{T-20}=1$. This gives $T^2-72T+720=(T-60)(T-12)=0$. Only $T=60$ is allowed. Thus Olivia takes $\boxed{60\text{ minutes}}$, Liam $\boxed{40\text{ minutes}}$, and they complete those laps at 1:24 p.m.
