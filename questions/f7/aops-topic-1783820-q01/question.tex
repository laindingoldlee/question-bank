Harry chooses two distinct integers $a,b$ and Voldemort chooses two distinct integers $c,d$, all from $\{1,2,\dots,9\}$, so that the four numbers $a,b,c,d$ are all different; every such choice is equally likely. If some two of the four numbers have a sum that is a perfect square, Voldemort spares Harry. Find the probability that Voldemort spares Harry.
