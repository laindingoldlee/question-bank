Work in the frame of the water, where the raft is at rest. The boat moves away from the raft for $1$ hour, is at rest for $\dfrac12$ hour, then returns at the same speed, which takes $1$ hour. So they meet $2.5$ hours after noon. In that time the raft drifted $7.5$ miles with the current, so the current is $\dfrac{7.5}{2.5}=\boxed{3}$ mph.
