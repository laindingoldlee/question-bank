At noon a motorboat going upstream passes a raft drifting with the current. The boat continues upstream for $1$ hour, then turns off its motor and drifts for $\dfrac12$ hour, then motors downstream until it meets the raft, $7.5$ miles from the point where they first met. The boat's speed relative to the water is constant when the motor is on. Find the speed of the current.
