Let the square have side $x$ cm. The cut is parallel to the shorter sides, so the plank's width is $x$ and its length is $x+120$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((0,0)--(150,0)--(150,30)--(0,30)--cycle,linewidth(1));
draw((30,0)--(30,30),dashed+linewidth(0.9));
label("square",(15,15));label("rectangle",(90,15));
label("$x$",(15,0),S);label("$120$",(90,0),S);label("$x$",(0,15),W);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Hence
\[
x(x+120)=4500\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x^2+120x-4500=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad(x-30)(x+150)=0 ,
\]
and since $x>0$, the square has side $\boxed{30\text{ cm}}$. (Check: $30\cdot150=4500$, and the rectangle is $120$ by $30$.)
