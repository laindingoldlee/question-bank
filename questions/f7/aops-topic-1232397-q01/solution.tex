With widths $a,b\ge1$: $13a+19b=376$. Modulo $13$: $19b\equiv6b\equiv376\equiv12$, so $b\equiv2\pmod{13}$, i.e. $b=2$ or $b=15$ ($b\le19$ since $19b<376$). Then $a=26$ or $a=7$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $a=26$, $b=2$: areas $338$ and $38$, difference $300$.
\item $a=7$, $b=15$: areas $91$ and $285$, difference $194$.
\end{itemize}
The difference is $\boxed{194\text{ or }300}$ cm$^2$.
