A byte has $8$ bits. An encoding assigns each object a different binary string. (a) How many whole bytes are needed for four ordered uppercase English letters together with a label from $1$ through $10$? (b) Do three bytes suffice for two ordered uppercase letters, one of six colours, one of twelve months, and one of $95$ printable characters?
