Adding gives $2xy=0$, hence $x=0$ or $y=0$. Subtracting gives $2x+2y=1$. Combining these yields exactly $\boxed{(x,y)=(0,\dfrac{1}{2}),(\dfrac{1}{2},0)}$. Both satisfy the original equations; a product may indeed be zero.
