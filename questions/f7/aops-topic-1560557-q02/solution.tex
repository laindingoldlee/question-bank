The $n$-th term is $\dfrac{2n+1}{n^2(n+1)^2}$. Since $(n+1)^2-n^2=2n+1$,
\[
\dfrac{2n+1}{n^2(n+1)^2}=\dfrac{(n+1)^2-n^2}{n^2(n+1)^2}=\dfrac{1}{n^2}-\dfrac{1}{(n+1)^2}.
\]
The partial sums telescope:
\[
\sum_{n=1}^{N}\left(\dfrac{1}{n^2}-\dfrac{1}{(n+1)^2}\right)=1-\dfrac{1}{(N+1)^2}\longrightarrow1 .
\]
So the sum is $\boxed{1}$.
