We have $10^{10}=2^{10}5^{10}$, $12^{12}=2^{24}3^{12}$ and $15^{15}=3^{15}5^{15}$, with $11\cdot11=121$, $25\cdot13=325$ and $16\cdot16=256$ divisors. The common divisors of pairs are the divisors of $2^{10}$, $5^{10}$ and $3^{12}$ ($11$, $11$ and $13$ of them), and only $1$ divides all three. By inclusion--exclusion the count is
\[121+325+256-11-11-13+1=\boxed{668}.\]
