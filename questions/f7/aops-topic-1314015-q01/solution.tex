From the first equation $x=y+2$. Substituting, $y(y+2)=12$, i.e. $y^2+2y-12=0$, so $y=-1\pm\sqrt{13}$ and $x=y+2=1\pm\sqrt{13}$. The solutions are
\[
\boxed{(x,y)=\left(1+\sqrt{13},\,-1+\sqrt{13}\right)\ \text{or}\ \left(1-\sqrt{13},\,-1-\sqrt{13}\right)}.
\]
