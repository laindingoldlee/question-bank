The sum of Angie's age and John's age equals Michael's age. Angie's age is a prime number, but John's and Michael's ages are not prime. The sum of John's and Michael's ages is prime, and Angie's age minus John's age is prime. One more than half of John's age is a prime number. Angie is the only teenager of the three, and she is older than John. How old is John?
