Square $ABCD$ has side length $1$. Point $X$ lies on $BC$ and point $Y$ lies on $CD$ so that $AX=AY$, and the area of triangle $AXY$ is $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of the area of $ABCD$. What is the length of $XY$? Express your answer in simplest radical form.
