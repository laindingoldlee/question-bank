Anton types the $7$ characters $A,o,P,S,R,0,X$ of his passcode in a random order (all $7!$ orders equally likely). Caps Lock starts off. Whenever he must type a capital letter while Caps Lock is off, he turns it on, and he turns it off only when he must type a lowercase letter. The digit $0$ can be typed either way. What is the probability that he turns Caps Lock on at most once?
