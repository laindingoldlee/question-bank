Dividing by $2$ and factoring yields $(16a-63)(16b-63)=3969=3^4\cdot7^2$. Since $\dfrac{1}{a}<\dfrac{16}{63}$, $a\ge4$, and similarly $b\ge4$, so both factors are positive. The positive divisors of $3969$ congruent to $1$ modulo $16$ are $1,49,81,3969$; their complementary divisors also have that residue. They give $(a,b)=(4,252),(7,9),(9,7),(252,4)$. Hence the number is $\boxed4$.
