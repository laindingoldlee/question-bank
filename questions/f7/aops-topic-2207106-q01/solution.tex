If the numbers are $x\ge y$, then
\[(x-y)^2=(x+y)^2-4xy=2824^2-4\cdot1625295=7974976-6501180=1473796.\]
Since $1200^2=1440000$ and the last digit $6$ means the root ends in $4$ or $6$, try $1214^2=1473796$. So $x-y=\boxed{1214}$, and then $x=\dfrac{2824+1214}{2}=2019$, $y=805$ (check: $2019\cdot805=1625295$).
