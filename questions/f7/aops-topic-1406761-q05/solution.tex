Choose the $3$ rows used, in $\binom{5}{3}=10$ ways, and the $3$ columns used, in $\binom{5}{3}=10$ ways. The three points must use each chosen row once and each chosen column once, so they correspond to a matching of the rows with the columns: $3!=6$ ways. The total is $10\cdot10\cdot6=\boxed{600}$.
