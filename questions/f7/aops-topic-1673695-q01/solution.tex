Let $t=\sqrt{ab}>0$, so $ab=t^2$.

\textbf{Numerator.} $\sqrt{\dfrac{t^2}{2}}+\sqrt8=\dfrac{t}{\sqrt2}+2\sqrt2=\dfrac{t+4}{\sqrt2}$.

\textbf{Denominator.} The expression under the root is a perfect square:
\[
\dfrac{t^2+16}{8}+t=\dfrac{t^2+8t+16}{8}=\dfrac{(t+4)^2}{8},
\]
so the denominator is $\dfrac{t+4}{\sqrt8}=\dfrac{t+4}{2\sqrt2}$ (using $t+4>0$).

\textbf{Quotient.}
\[
\dfrac{\dfrac{t+4}{\sqrt2}}{\dfrac{t+4}{2\sqrt2}}=\boxed{2},
\]
for all positive $a$ and $b$.
