A point on the edge travels one circumference, $\pi d$, per revolution.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O1=(0,0),O2=(4.2,0);
draw(circle(O1,1.75),linewidth(0.9));draw(circle(O1,0.2));
draw(circle(O2,1.0208),linewidth(0.9));draw(circle(O2,0.2));
draw(O1--O1+1.75*dir(30));label("$6$ in",O1+0.9*dir(30),dir(120));
draw(O2--O2+1.0208*dir(30));label("$3.5$ in",O2+0.5*dir(30),dir(120),fontsize(8pt));
label("$33$ rpm",O1+(0,-1.75),S);label("$45$ rpm",O2+(0,-1.02),S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

\textbf{$33$ rpm record:} $d=12$ in $=1$ ft, so its edge moves $33\pi\cdot1$ ft per minute, i.e.\ $\dfrac{33\pi}{60}\approx1.7279$ ft per second.

\textbf{$45$ rpm record:} $d=7$ in $=\dfrac{7}{12}$ ft, so its edge moves $45\pi\cdot\dfrac{7}{12}=\dfrac{105\pi}{4}$ ft per minute, i.e.\ $\dfrac{105\pi}{240}\approx1.3744$ ft per second.

The difference is
\[
\dfrac{33\pi}{60}-\dfrac{105\pi}{240}=\dfrac{132\pi-105\pi}{240}=\dfrac{27\pi}{240}=\dfrac{9\pi}{80}\approx\boxed{0.35\text{ ft per second}},
\]
the edge of the larger, slower record being faster.
