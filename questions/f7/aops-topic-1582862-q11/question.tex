A $45$ rpm record has a diameter of $7$ inches and spins at $45$ revolutions per minute. A $33$ rpm record has a diameter of $12$ inches and spins at $33$ revolutions per minute. Find the difference between the speeds of a point on the edge of the $33$ rpm record and of a point on the edge of the $45$ rpm record, in feet per second, to the nearest hundredth. Use $3.14159$ for $\pi$.
