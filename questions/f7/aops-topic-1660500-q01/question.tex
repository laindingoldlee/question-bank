Two players play tic-tac-toe on a $3\times3$ board: the first player, X, makes the first move by playing in the centre square, and the players then alternate, the game stopping as soon as someone completes a line of three or the board is full. A \emph{game} is the whole sequence of moves, so two games are different if the moves are made in different squares or in a different order (in particular, games that are rotations or reflections of each other are counted as different). In how many games does the second player, O, win?
