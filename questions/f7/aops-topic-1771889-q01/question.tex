Bridge is played by four players with an ordinary deck of $52$ cards. Each player begins with a hand of $13$ cards. In how many ways can a bridge game start, that is, in how many ways can the $52$ cards be dealt into hands of $13$ cards for the four (distinguishable) players? (Ignore partnerships; the order of the cards within a hand does not matter.)
