The probability of a true blue car and a blue report is $\dfrac{15}{100}\cdot\dfrac{80}{100}=\dfrac{12}{100}$. The probability of a green car with a blue report is $\dfrac{85}{100}\cdot\dfrac{20}{100}=\dfrac{17}{100}$. Conditioning on a blue report gives $\dfrac{12}{12+17}=\boxed{\dfrac{12}{29}}$.
