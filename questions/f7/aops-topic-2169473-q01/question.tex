Arrange the nine numbers $1, 7, 13, 31, 37, 43, 61, 67, 73$ in a $3\times3$ grid so that the three rows, the three columns and the two diagonals all have the same sum. (Apart from $1$, all of these numbers are prime; this ``prime magic square'' is due to the puzzle composer Henry Ernest Dudeney.)
