The ten letters are two A's, two I's and the six single letters D, E, H, M, N, O. An eight-letter arrangement leaves out exactly two of the ten letters. Sort by which multiset of $8$ letters is used.
\begin{itemize}
\item Both A's and both I's are used (two of the six single letters left out): $\binom{6}{2}=15$ multisets, each with $\dfrac{8!}{2!\,2!}=10080$ arrangements: $151200$.
\item One of the pairs is used in full and the other appears once (one letter of that pair and one single letter left out): $2\cdot6=12$ multisets, each with $\dfrac{8!}{2!}=20160$ arrangements: $241920$.
\item One pair is left out entirely (both A's or both I's): $2$ multisets, each with $\dfrac{8!}{2!}=20160$ arrangements: $40320$.
\item One A and one I are left out: $1$ multiset of eight different letters, $8!=40320$ arrangements.
\end{itemize}
The total is
\[
151200+241920+40320+40320=\boxed{473760}.
\]
