The centre lies on the perpendicular bisector of the chord from $(0,3)$ to $(1,2)$: it passes through $\left(\dfrac12,\dfrac52\right)$ with slope $1$, so $y=x+2$. Distance $5$ from $(0,3)$: $x^2+(x-1)^2=25$, i.e. $x^2-x-12=0$, so $x=4$ or $x=-3$. The centres are $(4,6)$ and $(-3,-1)$, giving $xy=\boxed{24\text{ or }3}$.
