The angle sum and $A+B=C$ give $2C=180^\circ$, so $C=90^\circ$. The given sides $BC=3$ and $AC=6$ are the perpendicular legs. The area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot3\cdot6=\boxed9$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair C=(0,0),A=(6,0),B=(0,3);draw(A--B--C--cycle);label("A",A,E);label("B",B,N);label("C",C,SW);label("6",(A+C)/2,S);label("3",(B+C)/2,W);
\end{asy}
