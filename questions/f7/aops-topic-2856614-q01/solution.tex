$(x-y)(x+y)=48$ with both factors of the same parity (even) and $x-y<x+y$: $2\cdot24$, $4\cdot12$, $6\cdot8$. These give $(x,y)=(13,11),(8,4),(7,1)$, so $x-y\in\boxed{\{2,4,6\}}$.
