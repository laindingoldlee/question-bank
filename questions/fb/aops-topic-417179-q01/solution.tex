The probability of no tails is $\dfrac{1}{2^n}$ and of exactly one tail is $\dfrac{n}{2^n}$, so we need
\[
\dfrac{n+1}{2^n}=\dfrac{3}{16}\iff 16(n+1)=3\cdot2^n .
\]
The right side must be divisible by $16$, so $n\ge4$; $n=4$ gives $80\ne48$ and $n=5$ gives $96=96$. For $n\ge5$ the left side grows by $16$ at each step while the right side at least doubles, so there is no other solution. Hence $n=\boxed{5}$.
