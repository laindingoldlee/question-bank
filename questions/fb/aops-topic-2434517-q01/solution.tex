The distance from the $y$-axis to $A$ is $|-30|=30$, and the distance from the $x$-axis to $B$ is $|2a|=2a$ (as $a>0$).

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
draw((-50,0)--(230,0),Arrow(6)); draw((0,-48)--(0,105),Arrow(6));
label("$x$",(230,0),E); label("$y$",(0,105),N);
pair A=(-30,-45), B=(200,80);
dot(A); dot(B);
draw(A--(0,-45),red+linewidth(0.9)); label("$30$",(-15,-45),S,red);
draw(B--(200,0),blue+linewidth(0.9)); label("$2a$",(200,40),E,blue);
label("$A(-30,-45)$",A,W); label("$B(5a,2a)$",B,N);
\end{asy}

So
\[\dfrac23\cdot30=\dfrac14\cdot2a\iff20=\dfrac{a}{2}\iff a=\boxed{40}.\]
(The figure shows $B$ for $a=40$.)
