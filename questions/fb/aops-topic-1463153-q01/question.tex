A \emph{pentomino} is a shape made of $5$ unit squares joined edge to edge; two pentominoes are considered the same if one can be turned into the other by a rotation or a reflection. There are $1,1,2,5$ polyominoes made of $1,2,3,4$ squares, and the Catalan numbers begin $C_0,C_1,C_2,C_3,C_4,\dots=1,1,2,5,14,\dots$
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that there are exactly $12$ pentominoes.
\item[(b)] Is the number of polyominoes with $n$ squares always the Catalan number $C_{n-1}$?
\end{enumerate}
