Rithwik's $13$ friends try to share his candy equally and find $6$ pieces left over. Then $3$ friends leave, and the remaining $10$ friends find $7$ pieces left over. Then $3$ more leave, and the remaining $7$ friends can share the candy equally with none left over. What is the second smallest possible number of pieces of candy?
