There are $13\cdot13=169$ equally likely ordered pairs. The pairs with sum $19$ are $(6,13),(7,12),\dots,(13,6)$: the first number can be any of $6,7,\dots,13$ (then the second, $19-$first, is between $6$ and $13$), so there are $8$ pairs. The probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{8}{169}}$.
