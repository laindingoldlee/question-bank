Let the monkey be $m$ years old now and its mother $M$ years old.

\textbf{The ages.} Read the long sentence from the end. Each clause names an age, and the next clause names an age equal to a multiple of it:
\begin{itemize}
\item ``one-fourth as old as it is now'': the monkey's age $\dfrac {m}{4}$;
\item the mother was three times that: $\dfrac{3m}{4}$;
\item the monkey was as old as that: $\dfrac{3m}{4}$;
\item the mother was one-third of that: $\dfrac {m}{4}$;
\item the monkey was twice that: $\dfrac {m}{2}$;
\item the mother will be four times that: $2m$;
\item the monkey will be as old as that: $2m$;
\item the mother was one-half of that: $m$;
\item the monkey will be three times that: $3m$;
\item the mother is now one-half of that: $M=\dfrac{3m}{2}$.
\end{itemize}
With $m+M=30$ this gives $\dfrac{5m}{2}=30$, so $m=12$ and $M=18$.

\textbf{The weights} (in ounces). The rope is $12$ feet long and weighs $12\cdot\dfrac{1}{3}=4$ pounds $=64$ ounces. The monkey weighs $18$ ounces, and so does the weight. If the banana weighs $w$ ounces, then
\[
\dfrac{18}{2}+w=\dfrac{64+18}{4}=20.5,\qquad w=11.5 .
\]
At $2$ ounces per inch, the banana is $\dfrac{11.5}{2}=\boxed{5.75\text{ inches}}$ long.
