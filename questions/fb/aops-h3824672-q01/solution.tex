Reflection in an angle bisector exchanges the two lines. Let $h=7x-4y+1$. Reflection in $h=0$ maps $(x,y)$ to
\[
(x',y')=\left(x-\dfrac{14h}{65},\ y+\dfrac{8h}{65}\right),
\]
because the normal is $(7,-4)$ and its squared length is $65$. The reflected point lies on the known line exactly when
\[
3x'+4y'-11=3x+4y-11-\dfrac{10h}{65}=0.
\]
Multiplying and simplifying gives $\boxed{5x+12y=29}$. Reflection is an involution, so this equation is precisely the reflected line.

\end{asy}
