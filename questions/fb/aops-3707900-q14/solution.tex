Let current ages be $a,b,c,d,e$ and elapsed time $t$. The conditions give $b+t=3a$, $2t=b+d+e$, $t=2d$, and $e+t=2b+1$. Substituting the last two into the second gives $5d=3b+1$, and then $9a=11d-1$. Thus $a\equiv6\pmod{11}$. The only positive odd $a<30$ is $17$, so $d=14$, $b=23$, $t=28$, $e=19$. Any positive $c$ is consistent. The required sum is $\boxed{40}$.
