Ana and her four sisters Berta, Ceci, Diana and Elena have positive integer ages. Ana is an odd number of years old below $30$. At a future time when Berta is three times Ana's current age: (a) Ana and Ceci's ages then sum to the five current ages; (b) Diana is three times her current age; (c) Elena is one more than twice Berta's current age. Find Ana plus Berta's current ages.
