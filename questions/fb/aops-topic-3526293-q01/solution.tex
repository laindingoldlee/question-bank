Each numerator summand splits as $\dfrac{1}{2023-2k}-\dfrac{1}{2023-k}$. Put $H_n=\sum_{j=1}^n\dfrac{1}{j}$. The odd reciprocals through $2021$ sum to $H_{2022}-\dfrac{1}{2}H_{1011}$; the subtracted reciprocals from $1012$ through $2022$ sum to $H_{2022}-H_{1011}$. Thus the numerator is $\dfrac{1}{2}H_{1011}$, while the denominator is $2022H_{1011}$. Cancelling the positive harmonic sum gives $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{4044}}$.
