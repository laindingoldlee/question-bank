The equation is $(x^2+y^2-1)^2+z^2=0$, so $z=0$ and $x^2+y^2=1$. Cauchy-Schwarz gives $x+y\le\sqrt2$, with equality only at $x=y=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}$. Then $xy+xz+yz=\dfrac{1}{2}$, giving $\boxed{a+b=3}$.
