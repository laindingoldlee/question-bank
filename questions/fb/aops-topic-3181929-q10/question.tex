Among real points satisfying $x^4+2x^2y^2+y^4-2x^2-2y^2+z^2+1=0$, maximize $x+y+z$. At a maximizing point write $xy+xz+yz=\dfrac{a}{b}$ in lowest terms. Find $a+b$.
