The multiples of $4$ give
\[
4+8+\cdots+2016=4(1+2+\cdots+504)=4\cdot\dfrac{504\cdot505}{2}=509040,
\]
so $S=509041$. Since $509040=3\cdot169680$, we get $S\equiv1\pmod3$.

(Without the exact value: $4k\equiv k\pmod3$, so $S\equiv1+(1+2+\cdots+504)=1+252\cdot505\pmod 3$, and $252$ is a multiple of $3$.)

Hence $S^4\equiv1^4=1\pmod3$, and the remainder is $\boxed{1}$.
