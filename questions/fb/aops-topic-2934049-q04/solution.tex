A cube is congruent to 0, 1, or $-1$ modulo 9. Therefore a sum of two cubes cannot be 3, 4, 5, or 6 modulo 9, excluding the four candidates after 2. But $7=2^3+(-1)^3$, so the answer is $\boxed7$.
