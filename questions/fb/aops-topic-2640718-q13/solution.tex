Without the restriction there are $\dbinom52=10$ routes (choose which $2$ of the $5$ blocks go north). Routes through the dangerous intersection: $\dbinom21=2$ ways to reach it and $\dbinom31=3$ ways to continue, so $6$. The answer is $10-6=\boxed{4}$.
