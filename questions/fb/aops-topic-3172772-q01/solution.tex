The equation is $x^{x^x}=16$. For $0<x\le1$, the left side is at most $1$, so $x>1$. On $x>1$, both $x$ and $x^x$ increase strictly; consequently $x^{x^x}$ increases strictly. The value $x=2$ gives $2^{2^2}=16$. Therefore the unique solution is $\boxed2$.
