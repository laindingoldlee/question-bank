Highway A has an exit at every whole-number mile mark ($1,2,3,\dots$). Highway B has its $k$th exit at the $\dfrac{T_k}{10}$ mile mark, where $T_k=\dfrac{k(k+1)}{2}$ is the $k$th triangular number. The two highways start at the same point. Within the first $201.8$ miles, at how many mile marks do both highways have an exit?
