A common exit is a mark $\dfrac{T_k}{10}$ that is a whole number and at most $201.8$, i.e.\ $T_k\le2018$ and $10\mid T_k$. Since $T_{63}=2016$ and $T_{64}=2080$, we need $1\le k\le63$ with $10\mid\dfrac{k(k+1)}{2}$, i.e.\ $20\mid k(k+1)$.

As $k$ and $k+1$ are coprime, $4$ must divide one of them and $5$ must divide one of them. Modulo $20$ this gives $k\equiv0,4,15,19\pmod{20}$ (namely: $4\mid k,5\mid k$; $4\mid k,5\mid k+1$; $4\mid k+1,5\mid k$; $4\mid k+1,5\mid k+1$). In $1\le k\le63$ these are
\[
4,15,19,20,\ 24,35,39,40,\ 44,55,59,60,
\]
so there are $\boxed{12}$ common exits.
