Divide both sides and the modulus by 2: $15a\equiv1\pmod{28}$. Since $15^2\equiv1\pmod{28}$, multiplication by 15 yields $a\equiv15\pmod{28}$. Thus $\boxed{a=15+28k\ (k\in\mathbb Z)}$.
