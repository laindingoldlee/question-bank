The common digits lie in $0,\ldots,5$, with $A\ne0$. Decimal divisibility gives $D=0$ or 5. Modulo 5, $6\equiv1$, so the base-six number is divisible by 5 exactly when $A+B+C+D\equiv0$. For each of the $6^2$ ordered choices of $B,C$, exactly one $A\in\{1,2,3,4,5\}$ has the required residue. Either choice of $D$ works, giving $\boxed{36\cdot2=72}$.
