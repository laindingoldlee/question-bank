Let Alex attempt $A$ and Pat $P$. Then $A+P=105$, $A$ is a positive multiple of $3$, and $P$ a positive multiple of $5$. Since $105$ is a multiple of $5$, $A$ is also divisible by $5$, hence by $15$. The total made is $\dfrac{A}{3}+\dfrac{3(105-A)}{5}=63-\dfrac{4A}{15}$, maximized at the smallest allowed $A=15$. Then $P=90$ and the total is $\boxed{59}$.
