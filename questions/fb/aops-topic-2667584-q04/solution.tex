$P(\text{green and drawer 1})=\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac35=\dfrac3{10}$ and $P(\text{green and drawer 2})=\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac2{10}=\dfrac1{10}$. By Bayes' theorem,
\[P(\text{drawer 1}\mid\text{green})=\dfrac{\dfrac3{10}}{\dfrac3{10}+\dfrac1{10}}=\boxed{\dfrac34}.\]
