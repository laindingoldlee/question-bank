Drawer 1 contains $3$ green socks and $2$ blue socks, and drawer 2 contains $2$ green socks and $8$ blue socks. A drawer is chosen at random (each with probability $\dfrac12$), and then a sock is chosen at random from that drawer. Given that the sock is green, what is the probability that it came from drawer 1?
