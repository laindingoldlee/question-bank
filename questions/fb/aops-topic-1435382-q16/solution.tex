First find the multisets of three digits with product $24=2^3\cdot3$ (no digit can be $0$):
\[
\{1,3,8\},\quad \{1,4,6\},\quad \{2,3,4\},\quad \{2,2,6\}.
\]
(Systematically: if the digits include $1$, the other two have product $24$: $3\cdot8$ or $4\cdot6$. Otherwise all digits are at least $2$: $2\cdot2\cdot6$ or $2\cdot3\cdot4$.) The first three sets have distinct digits and give $3!=6$ numbers each; $\{2,2,6\}$ gives $3$ numbers ($226$, $262$, $622$). The total is $6+6+6+3=\boxed{\textbf{(D)}\ 21}$.
