The number $17$ cannot be $4a+7b$ for nonnegative integers $a,b$: modulo $4$, one would need $3b\equiv1$, hence $b\equiv3\pmod4$, forcing $7b\ge21$. On the other hand, $18=4+7+7$, $19=4+4+4+7$, $20=5\cdot4$, and $21=3\cdot7$. Every integer at least $18$ is one of these four numbers plus a nonnegative multiple of $4$. Therefore the answer is $\boxed{17}$.
