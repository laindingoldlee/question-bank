The integers from $1$ to $100$ are randomly separated into $2$ groups of $50$ integers each. What is the probability that $37$ and $89$ are in the same group? A student argues that the answer is $\dfrac{1}{2}$, since the two numbers are either in the same group or in different groups. Find the correct probability and explain the error.
