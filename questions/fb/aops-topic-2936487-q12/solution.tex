Orient $AB$ vertically, with $O=(0,0)$, $A=(h,-1),B=(h,1)$ and $h=\cot22.5^\circ=1+\sqrt2$. The circle center is $(u,0)$ and its radius is $u$, because it passes through $O$. Substituting $A$ gives $(h-u)^2+1=u^2$, hence $u=\dfrac{h^2+1}{2h}=\sqrt2$. The distance from its center to side $AB$ is 1. The two adjacent side lines have normals at 45 degrees to this one, so their distances from the circle center are $h-\dfrac{u}{\sqrt2}=\sqrt2$, exactly the radius. All more distant octagon side lines have still greater distances. Thus only the cap beyond $AB$ lies outside. It has central angle 90 degrees, area $\dfrac{1}{4}\pi(\sqrt2)^2-\dfrac{1}{2}(\sqrt2)^2\sin90^\circ=\boxed{\dfrac{\pi}{2}-1}$.

usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
real R=1/sin(pi/8);pair[] V;for(int k=0;k<8;++k)V.push(R*dir(-22.5+45*k));for(int k=0;k<8;++k)draw(V[k]--V[(k+1)%8]);draw(circle((sqrt(2),0),sqrt(2)),blue);draw((0,0)--V[0]);draw((0,0)--V[1]);label("$O$",(0,0),W);label("$A$",V[0],SE);label("$B$",V[1],NE);
\end{asy}
