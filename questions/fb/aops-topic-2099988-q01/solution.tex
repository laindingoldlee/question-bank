Since $p^2=2q^2+1$ is odd, $p$ is odd, so $p-1$ and $p+1$ are consecutive even numbers; one of them is divisible by $4$, and so $(p-1)(p+1)$ is divisible by $8$. Hence
\[2q^2=p^2-1=(p-1)(p+1)\]
is divisible by $8$, so $q^2$ is divisible by $4$ and $q$ is even. The only even prime is $q=2$, and then $p^2=9$, $p=3$.

So the only solution is $\boxed{p=3,\ q=2}$ (indeed $9-8=1$).
