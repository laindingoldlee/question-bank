An island has $100$ inhabitants, each of whom is either a truth-teller, who always tells the truth, or a liar, who always lies. A visitor asks some of them: ``How many truth-tellers and how many liars live on this island?'' Thirty-one of them answer ``$64$ truth-tellers and $36$ liars'', and twenty-nine answer ``$29$ truth-tellers and $31$ liars''. At least one of the people who answered is a truth-teller. How many truth-tellers and how many liars live on the island?
