The second answer claims $29+31=60$ inhabitants, but there are $100$, so it is false: all $29$ people who gave it are liars.

So the truth-teller among those who answered gave the first answer, and that answer is true: there are $\boxed{64\text{ truth-tellers and }36\text{ liars}}$.

This is consistent: the $31$ people who gave the true answer are truth-tellers, the $29$ others who answered are liars, and among the $40$ people not asked there are $64-31=33$ truth-tellers and $36-29=7$ liars.

\emph{Remark.} The condition that someone who answered tells the truth is needed. Without it, all $60$ people who answered could be liars, and then any number of truth-tellers from $0$ to $40$ would be consistent with the answers.
