The four outcomes HH, HT, TH, TT are equally likely, each with probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$. Harry wins only with HH and Ron only with TT, while Hermione wins with HT or TH. So
\[P(\text{Harry})=\dfrac{1}{4}=25\%,\qquad P(\text{Ron})=\dfrac{1}{4}=25\%,\qquad P(\text{Hermione})=\dfrac{2}{4}=\dfrac{1}{2}=50\%,\]
and \textbf{Hermione} has the highest chance.
