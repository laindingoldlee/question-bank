An arrangement of the six letters C, C, C, A, G, N in a row is fixed. Each day, Farley chooses an arrangement of the six letters G, G, G, A, C, N in a row, uniformly at random among all the different arrangements and independently of the other days. The two arrangements \emph{match} at a position if they have the same letter there. On the first day on which the arrangements match in at least $4$ positions, Farley solves all the problems of a contest and stops. What is the expected number of days, including that day?
