Let $u=(x+1)^2$, so $x(x+2)=u-1$. Then $\dfrac{1}{u-1}-\dfrac{1}{u}=\dfrac{1}{u(u-1)}=\dfrac{1}{12}$, so $u^2-u-12=0$ and $u=4$ ($u=-3<0$ is impossible). Thus $x+1=\pm2$: $x=\boxed{1\text{ or }-3}$; both make all denominators nonzero.
