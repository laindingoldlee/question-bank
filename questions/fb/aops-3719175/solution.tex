Polynomial division gives $f(x)=x^2-6x+1+\dfrac{1}{x^2+1}=(x-3)^2-8+\dfrac{1}{x^2+1}>-8$. Thus every integer value is at least $-7$. Since $f(2)=-\dfrac{34}{5}>-7$ and $f(3)=-\dfrac{79}{10}<-7$, continuity gives a real $x$ between $2$ and $3$ for which $f(x)=-7$. The least integer attained is therefore $\boxed{-7}$; this is different from asking for the minimum real value.
