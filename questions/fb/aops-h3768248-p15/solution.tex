A statement that the next person is truthful means the speaker and next person have the same type; a statement that the next person is a liar means they have opposite types. Flipping every person\textquotesingle s type preserves all these same/opposite relations and hence every reported statement. Therefore any consistent liar fraction $p$ has a complementary consistent fraction $1-p$. If the fraction is uniquely determined, $p=1-p$, so it is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$.
