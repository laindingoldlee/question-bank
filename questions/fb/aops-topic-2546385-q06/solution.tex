He gains $10$ counted posts per day and needs $1000-104=896$ more. After $89$ more days he has $994$, and after $90$ more days $1004$. So he reaches $1000$ on the $90$th day after April $30$. May has $31$ days and June $30$, so day $61$ is June $30$ and day $90$ is $\boxed{\text{July }29}$.
