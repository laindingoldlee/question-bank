The hundreds digit has 8 choices. Divisibility by 4 depends only on the last two digits. For units digit 0 or 8, the tens digit must be even and not 4, giving 4 choices each. For units digit 2 or 6, the tens digit must be odd, giving 5 choices each. No other units digit works. Thus there are $ 8(4+4+5+5)=\boxed{144}$ numbers.
