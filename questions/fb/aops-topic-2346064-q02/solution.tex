Together they earned $15+20+25+40=100$ dollars, so each friend should end up with $\dfrac{100}{4}=25$ dollars. The friend who earned $\$40$ keeps $\$25$ and gives away $40-25=15$ dollars. The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(C) }\$15}$.
