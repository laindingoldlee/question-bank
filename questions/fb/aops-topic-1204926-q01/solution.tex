The midpoints form a regular hexagon $ABCDEF$ whose side is $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$ times the original side, so its area is $\dfrac{3}{4}X$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair[] V; for(int k=0;k<6;++k) V.push(dir(60*k));
pair[] M; for(int k=0;k<6;++k) M.push((V[k]+V[(k+1)%6])/2);
draw(V[0]--V[1]--V[2]--V[3]--V[4]--V[5]--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
filldraw(M[0]--M[1]--M[2]--M[3]--M[5]--cycle,lightblue,blue); draw(M[3]--M[4]--M[5],dashed);
string[] n={"A","B","C","D","E","F"}; for(int k=0;k<6;++k){ dot(M[k]); label("$"+n[k]+"$",M[k],1.2*unit(M[k])); }
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Pentagon $ABCDF$ is this hexagon minus triangle $DEF$. In a regular hexagon of side $t$ (area $\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}t^2$), the triangle on three consecutive vertices has area $\dfrac{1}{2}t^2\sin120^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}t^2$, which is $\dfrac{1}{6}$ of the hexagon. So
\[Y=\dfrac{5}{6}\cdot\dfrac{3}{4}X=\dfrac{5}{8}X,\qquad \dfrac {Y}{X}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{8}}.\]
