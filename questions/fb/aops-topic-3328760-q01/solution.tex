Since $2^{10}=1024$, $\boxed{\log_2 1024=10}$. Since $2^9=512<1000<1024=2^{10}$, monotonicity gives $\boxed{9<\log_2 1000<10}$. In general, for $a>0,a\ne1,x>0$, $\log_a x=\dfrac{\log x}{\log a}$, which follows by taking logarithms of $a^t=x$.
