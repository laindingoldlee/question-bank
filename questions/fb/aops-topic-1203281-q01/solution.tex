The sum is odd, so the number $k$ of odd integers is odd. With $k$ odd numbers and $1006-k$ even ones, the sum is at most the sum of the largest $k$ odd numbers up to $2011$ and the largest $1006-k$ even numbers up to $2012$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $k=1$: at most $2011+(4+6+\cdots+2012)=2011+1013040=1015051<1019057$. Impossible.
\item $k=3$: at most $(2011+2009+2007)+(8+10+\cdots+2012)=6027+1013030=1019057$, exactly the required sum.
\end{itemize}
So the least possible number is $\boxed{3}$ (attained only by the odd numbers $2007,2009,2011$ and the even numbers $8,10,\dots,2012$).
