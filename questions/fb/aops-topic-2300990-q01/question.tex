Let $p$ and $q$ be positive integers with $\gcd(p,q)=1$. Prove that $\left(x^{pq}-1\right)(x-1)$ is divisible by $\left(x^p-1\right)\left(x^q-1\right)$, that is, the quotient is a polynomial with integer coefficients.
