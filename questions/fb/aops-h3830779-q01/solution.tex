The allowed adjacent pairs are $19,38,57,76,95,25,75$. Digits $3$ and $8$ cannot start a sufficiently long valid string, and neither can $6$, which has no successor. A long valid string therefore starts with $1,2,5,7$ or $9$, then follows the alternating cycle $5,7,5,7,\ldots$, possibly replacing the final $5$ after a $7$ by $6$.
For length $2024$, starting with $5$ gives one string, starting with $7$ gives two, starting with $2$ or $9$ gives two each, and starting with $1$ (whose forced prefix is $195$) gives one. The answer is $\boxed{1+2+2+2+1=8}$. No other branching is possible because leaving the cycle for $6$ must happen at the final digit.
