Damir calls a natural number $N$ beautiful if it simultaneously satisfies the following three properties:

$\bullet$ The decimal representation of $N$ contains $2024$ digits;

$\bullet$ The decimal representation of $N$ contains no zeros;

$\bullet$ Every two-digit number formed by two consecutive digits of $N$ is divisible by $19$ or by $25$.

Determine the number of beautiful numbers. Be sure to justify your answer.
