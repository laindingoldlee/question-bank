Consider the equation $x^x=512$ for positive real numbers $x$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that the equation has exactly one positive real solution, and that this solution lies between $4$ and $5$.
\item[(b)] Prove that this solution is irrational.
\end{enumerate}
