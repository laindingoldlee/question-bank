Let his income be $x$ dollars. After living expenses $0.6x$ remains, after the car payment $0.5\cdot0.6x=0.3x$, and after food $0.75\cdot0.3x=0.225x$. So
\[
0.225x=300,\qquad x=\dfrac{300}{0.225}=\dfrac{4000}{3}=\boxed{1333\dfrac{1}{3}\text{ dollars}}\approx\$1333.33 .
\]
