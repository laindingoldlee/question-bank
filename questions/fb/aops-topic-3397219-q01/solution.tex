The expression requires $c\ne0$, while $k$ may be any real number. The factors simplify to $-\dfrac{c^{27}}{27}$ and $4489c^{-18}k^{18}$. Their product is $\boxed{-\dfrac{4489c^9k^{18}}{27}}$, retaining $c\ne0$.
