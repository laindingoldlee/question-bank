Find positive integers $A\le10$, $B\le11$, $C\le12$ satisfying: (i) $A$ is the sum of a square's side with perimeter $B$ and another square's side with area $C$; (ii) $B=(C-1)(A+1)-(C+1)(A-1)$; (iii) $A\ne B$ and $C$ is the digit sum of $9|A-B|$.
