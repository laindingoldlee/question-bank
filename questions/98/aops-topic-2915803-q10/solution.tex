Since $1\le|A-B|\le10$, the possible multiples $9|A-B|$ are 9,18,...,90, all with digit sum 9. Hence $C=9$. Part(ii) simplifies to $B=2(C-A)=18-2A$. Part(i) gives $A=\dfrac{B}{4}+3$. Substitution yields $4A=18-2A+12$, so $A=5,B=8$. These satisfy all bounds and the digit-sum condition. Thus $\boxed{(A,B,C)=(5,8,9)}$ uniquely.
