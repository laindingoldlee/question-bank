A spelling quiz has $100$ different words, quizzed in a uniformly random order; among them are ``art'', ``of'', ``problem'' and ``solving''. What is the probability that these four words appear in the order ``art'', ``of'', ``problem'', ``solving'' (not necessarily consecutively)?
