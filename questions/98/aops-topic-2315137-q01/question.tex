A big $5\times5\times5$ cube is assembled by gluing together $125$ unit cubes. The big cube is cut by the plane through two diagonally opposite vertical edges of it; this plane makes $45^\circ$ angles with the two side faces that meet at each of these edges. How many of the unit cubes are cut, that is, have the plane passing through their interior?

$\textbf{(A) }30\qquad\textbf{(B) }25\qquad\textbf{(C) }20\qquad\textbf{(D) }15\qquad\textbf{(E) }10$
