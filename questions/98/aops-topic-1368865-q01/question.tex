Sam and Aam each eat jackfruits at a constant rate. Once, Sam ate for $3$ hours and Aam ate for $5$ hours, and together they ate $50$ jackfruits. Another time, Sam ate for $26$ hours and Aam for $10$ hours, and together they ate $200$ jackfruits. What is the least time in which they can eat $252$ jackfruits?
