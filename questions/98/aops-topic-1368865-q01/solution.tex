Let Sam eat $s$ and Aam eat $a$ jackfruits per hour. Then
\[3s+5a=50,\qquad 26s+10a=200 .\]
Doubling the first equation gives $6s+10a=100$; subtracting from the second gives $20s=100$, so $s=5$ and then $a=\dfrac{50-15}{5}=7$.

The time is least when both eat at the same time, at a combined rate of $5+7=12$ jackfruits per hour. Then $252$ jackfruits take $\dfrac{252}{12}=\boxed{21}$ hours.
