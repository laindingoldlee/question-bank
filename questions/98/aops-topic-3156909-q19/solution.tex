Pair each divisor $ d $ with $\dfrac{120}{d}$. Then the reciprocal sum is $\dfrac{\sigma(120)}{120}$. Since $ 120=2^3\cdot3\cdot5 $, $\sigma(120)=(1+2+4+8)(1+3)(1+5)=360 $. Thus $\boxed3 $.
