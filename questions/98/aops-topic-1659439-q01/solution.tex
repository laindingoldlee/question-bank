The number of trailing zeros of $2018!$ is the exponent of $5$ in it (there are far more factors $2$ than $5$):
\[
\left\lfloor\dfrac{2018}{5}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{2018}{25}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{2018}{125}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{2018}{625}\right\rfloor=403+80+16+3=502 .
\]
So $2018!$ ends in $502$ zeros, and its last $100$ digits are \boxed{\text{all }0}.
