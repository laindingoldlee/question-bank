A walker starts at $(0,0)$ on the lattice square $0\le x,y\le4$ and moves one unit up or right each day. When both moves stay in the square, each is equally likely; on a boundary the remaining move is forced. The walker stops on reaching $(4,4)$. After every move except the last, rain occurs independently with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, but never wets a walker still on $y=0$. The walker complains when not wetted. Find the expected number of complaints.
