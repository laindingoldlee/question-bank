There are seven eligible nights. Let $K$ be the number spent on the bottom row. The first $k$ moves are right with probability $2^{-k}$ for $1\le k\le4$; after four right moves an up move is forced. Thus $E[K]=\sum_{k=1}^4 2^{-k}=\dfrac{15}{16}$. Each bottom-row night certainly produces a complaint, and every other eligible night does so with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Hence $E[\text{complaints}]=\dfrac{7}{2}+\dfrac{1}{2}E[K]=\boxed{\dfrac{127}{32}}$.
