Each up-down pair of years multiplies the value by $1.3\cdot0.7=0.91$, so the six years multiply it by $0.91^3$. The $3$-year stock multiplies its value by $\left(1-\dfrac{x}{100}\right)^3$. Hence $1-\dfrac{x}{100}=0.91$ and $x=\boxed{9}$.
