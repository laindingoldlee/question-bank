A $6$-year stock goes up $30\%$ in the first year, down $30\%$ in the second, up $30\%$ in the third, down $30\%$ in the fourth, up $30\%$ in the fifth and down $30\%$ in the sixth. Over the six years its value changes by the same factor as a $3$-year stock that loses $x\%$ in each of its three years. Compute $x$.
