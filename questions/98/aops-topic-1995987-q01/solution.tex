The first fraction is $3^{-2}a^{-4}b^{-3}$. The second is $3^{7-2}a^{7+2+2}b^{-2}=3^5a^{11}b^{-2}$. Dividing,
\[
3^{-2-5}a^{-4-11}b^{-3+2}=3^{-7}a^{-15}b^{-1}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2187\,a^{15}b}}.
\]
