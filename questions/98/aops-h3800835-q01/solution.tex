Compute $9N=10N-N$ by column subtraction. Borrowing in the units column, but nowhere else because the digits increase strictly, gives the five digits
\[a,\quad b-a,\quad c-b,\quad d-c-1,\quad10-d.\]
They are valid digits, including a possible zero in the fourth position. Their sum telescopes to $a+(b-a)+(c-b)+(d-c-1)+(10-d)=\boxed9$.
