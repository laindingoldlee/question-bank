Infinitely many beasts stand in a line. Grogg stands at the front holding $n$ pieces of candy ($n\ge1$), and nobody else has any. Grogg gives one piece each to the next $n$ beasts in line and then leaves the line. The other beasts repeat this process: the beast at the front, holding $k$ pieces of candy, gives one piece each to the next $k$ beasts in line and then leaves the line. For which values of $n$ does some beast other than Grogg, at some moment, hold all $n$ pieces of candy?
