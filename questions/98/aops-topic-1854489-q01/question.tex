$A$ and $B$ are nonempty finite sets of integers with no common elements, $|A|=m$ and $|B|=n$. For every $k$ in $A\cup B$, at least one of the following holds: $k+17\in A$, or $k-31\in B$. Find the ratio $m:n$.
