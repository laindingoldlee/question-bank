Kelvin the Frog has an infinite row of lilypads, each with a coin on it showing heads. Every minute he flips the coin on the lilypad he is standing on (a fair flip). If it lands heads he hops one pad to the right, and if tails one pad to the left; the coin he flipped stays as it landed. He stops, and goes to sleep, as soon as he lands on a lilypad whose coin shows tails. What is the expected number of minutes before Kelvin goes to sleep?
