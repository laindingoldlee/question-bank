Jack and Colin play the following game. Jack picks a positive real number $a$. Then Colin must choose a positive integer $n$ such that $na$, rounded to the nearest tenth, is an integer; once Colin chooses $n$, Jack gets $n$ points. What is the greatest number of points that Jack can guarantee himself? (Numbers exactly halfway between two tenths are rounded up.)
