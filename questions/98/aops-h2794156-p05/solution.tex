Measure time $t$ in minutes from Bonnie's start. At integer or half-integer times, one cyclist is at a vertex and the other inside an edge. Otherwise their completed-edge counts are $k=\lfloor t\rfloor$ and $\ell=\left\lfloor t-\dfrac{1}{2}\right\rfloor$, differing by zero or one.

Turning at every vertex switches between vertical and horizontal edges. If the counts differ by one, the current edges have different orientations and can meet only at vertices, which neither cyclist occupies.

If $k=\ell$, their current edges start at vertices of the same checkerboard color. They cannot traverse the same edge in opposite directions because its endpoints have different colors. If they traverse the same edge in the same direction, their fractional progress differs by $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Thus they $\boxed{\text{never meet}}$.
