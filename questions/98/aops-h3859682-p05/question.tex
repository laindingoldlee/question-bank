Two circles $\omega_1$ and $\omega_2$ with radius $4$ are centered at $A$ and $B$, respectively, so that $B$ lies on $\omega_1$ and $A$ lies on $\omega_2$. Let points $C$ and $D$ be the intersections of $\omega_1$ and $\omega_2$. Point $P$ is on $\omega_1$ such that $\angle APC=45^{\circ}$, and $Q$ is the intersection of $PC$ and $\omega_2$ not at $C$. If lines $PA$ and $QB$ intersect at $E$, the value of $DE^2$ can be expressed as $\dfrac{m}{n}$ where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $m+n$.
