In her $5$-minute head start Jana covers $6\cdot\dfrac{5}{60}=\dfrac{1}{2}$ mile. Zhao gains on her at $10-6=4$ miles per hour, so he closes the gap in
\[
\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{2}}{4}=\dfrac{1}{8}\text{ hour}=\boxed{7\dfrac{1}{2}}\text{ minutes}.
\]
At that moment Zhao has ridden $10\cdot\dfrac{1}{8}=1.25$ miles, less than $2$ miles, so the catch-up happens on the path.
