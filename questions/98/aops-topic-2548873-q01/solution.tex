A student avoids platform A with probability $0.38$ and platform B with probability $0.55$. By independence, the probability of using neither is $0.38\cdot0.55=0.209$, i.e. $\boxed{20.9\%}$, answer \textbf{C}.

(Equivalently, by inclusion--exclusion, $P(A\cup B)=0.62+0.45-0.62\cdot0.45=0.791$, and $1-0.791=0.209$.)
