After a rigid motion or reflection, use $D=(0,0)$, $B=(-u,0)$, $C=(u,0)$, and $O=(0,6)$. Equilateral $ODH$ gives $H=(3\sqrt3,3)$, up to horizontal reflection. The altitude from $A$ is vertical, so $A=(3\sqrt3,v)$. In coordinates centred at $O$, the orthocentre vector is the sum of the three vertex vectors; thus, in these coordinates, $H=A+B+C-2O$, giving $v=15$. Equal circumradii yield $u^2+36=OA^2=27+81=108$, so $u=6\sqrt2$. The area is $\dfrac{1}{2}(2u)(15)=90\sqrt2$, and $\boxed{a+b=92}$. Reflections give the same area.
