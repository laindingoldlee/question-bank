Substitute $x=1+t$ to obtain $y=-6+t^3+3t$. The expression $t^3+3t$ is odd, so the graph is invariant under $(1+t,-6+v)\mapsto(1-t,-6-v)$. The center is $\boxed{(1,-6)}$. Uniqueness follows because eliminating the quadratic term after translation forces $3a-3=0$, so $a=1$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair[] P;for(int k=-20;k<=20;++k){real t=k/10.0;P.push((t,t*t*t/5+3*t/5));}path q=P[0];for(int k=1;k<P.length;++k)q=q--P[k];draw(q,blue);dot((0,0));label("center $(1,-6)$",(0,0),E);label("translated schematic",(0,-3.5));
\end{asy}
