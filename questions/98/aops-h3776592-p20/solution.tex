The three-digit cases are $111,\ldots,999$, giving nine. For four digits with repeated nonzero digit $d$, the exceptional digit can be zero in three nonleading positions, or one of eight other nonzero digits in any of four positions. This gives $9(3+8\cdot4)=315$. Repeated zero gives $1000,\ldots,9000$, nine more. The endpoint $10000$ has four zeroes, so it fails. The total is $\boxed{9+315+9=333}$.
