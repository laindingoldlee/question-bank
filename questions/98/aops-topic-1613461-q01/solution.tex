$b(x)$ is defined exactly when its input to $a$, namely $\dfrac {x}{2}$, lies in $[-8,8]$:
\[
-8\le\dfrac {x}{2}\le8\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad-16\le x\le16 .
\]
So the domain of $b$ is $\boxed{[-16,16]}$. (The graph of $b$ is the graph of $a$ stretched horizontally by a factor of $2$.)
