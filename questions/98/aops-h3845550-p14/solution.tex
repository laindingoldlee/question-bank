The factor is independent of $x$ and lies strictly between zero and one. The product through $N$ equals $\left(\dfrac{n^2}{(n+1)^2}\right)^N$, which tends to $\boxed 0$. If the intended factor was $\dfrac{x^2}{(x+1)^2}$, that different product also tends to zero, since its partial product is $\dfrac{1}{(N+1)^2}$.
