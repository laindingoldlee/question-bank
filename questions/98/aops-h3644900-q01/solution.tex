The value depends on $n$ modulo $7$ and, for nonzero residues, on the exponent modulo $6$. Fermat\textquotesingle s little theorem therefore makes $42$ a period. If $T$ is any period, $f(7)=0$ forces $7\mid T$, because $n^n$ is divisible by $7$ exactly when $n$ is. Now take $n=3$: the bases $3+T$ and $3$ agree modulo $7$, so $3^T\equiv1\pmod7$. The multiplicative order of $3$ modulo $7$ is $6$, as $3,3^2,3^3$ are $3,2,6$ and $3^6\equiv1$. Hence $6\mid T$. Every period is divisible by $42$, and the least is $\boxed{42}$.
