The last digits are: $5^5\to5$, $6^6\to6$, $7^7\to3$ (the cycle $7,9,3,1$ and $7\equiv3\pmod4$), $8^8\to6$ (the cycle $8,4,2,6$ and $8\equiv0\pmod4$), $9^9\to9$ (odd power). The sum ends in the last digit of $5+6+3+6+9=29$, i.e. $\boxed{9}$.
