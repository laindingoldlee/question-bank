Model a frisbee rim as a circle of circumference 1. A hand permanently holds an arc of length 0.1. On each throw, a dog independently bites a uniformly positioned arc of length 0.1, leaving saliva there permanently. Count the throw on which saliva first touches the hand arc. Find the expected count; endpoint contacts have probability zero.
