Six cubes with edges $1,2,3,4,5,6$ are stacked into a tower, largest at the bottom and each cube resting on the one below it. Each cube is placed so that one of its corners lines up with a corner of the cube below (so two of its side faces are flush with two side faces of the cube below). What is the total surface area of the tower, including the bottom?
