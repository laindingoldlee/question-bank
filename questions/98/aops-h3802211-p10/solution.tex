Since $PAE$ is equilateral, the rhombus has side $AE$, so $EQ=ED$. The inward equilateral triangle $AEQ$ has angle $AEQ=60^\circ$. Thus $\angle DEQ=108^\circ-60^\circ=48^\circ$, and isosceles triangle $DEQ$ has base angles $66^\circ$. In the regular pentagon, $\angle EDA=36^\circ$, so $\angle ADQ=66^\circ-36^\circ=30^\circ$. Also $\angle DAB=72^\circ$. The ray $DQ$ meets $AB$ at the interior point $R$, as these ray directions show. Triangle $ADR$ therefore gives $\boxed{\angle ARQ=180^\circ-72^\circ-30^\circ=78^\circ}$.
