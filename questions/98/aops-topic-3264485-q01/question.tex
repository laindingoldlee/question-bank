In a $30$-day month, one designated day has two independent withdrawals and every other day has one. A day is selected uniformly. Each withdrawal is a percentage $y$ chosen uniformly from $3.48,3.49,\ldots,14.34$, and both percentages are measured against the same original stock. Find the probability that the chosen day has two withdrawals whose total exceeds $28\%$.
