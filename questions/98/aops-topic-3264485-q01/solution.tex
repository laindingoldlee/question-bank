There are $1434-348+1=1087$ possible hundredth-percent values. On the two-withdrawal day write the values as $1434-u$ and $1434-v$, with $0\le u,v\le1086$. Their sum exceeds $2800$ exactly when $u+v\le67$. This gives $1+2+\cdots+68=\dfrac{68\cdot69}{2}=2346$ ordered pairs. Multiply by the probability $\dfrac{1}{30}$ of choosing that day:
\[\boxed{\dfrac{2346}{30\cdot1087^2}=\dfrac{391}{5907845}}.\]
