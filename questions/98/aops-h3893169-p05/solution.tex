The first two equations give $ab+bc+ca=-\dfrac{1}{2}$. The reciprocal equation therefore gives $abc=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Thus $a,b,c$ are the roots, in some order, of
\[t^3-\dfrac{1}{2}t-\dfrac{1}{2}=(t-1)\left(t^2+t+\dfrac{1}{2}\right).\]
The roots are $1,\dfrac{-1+i}{2},\dfrac{-1-i}{2}$. All six permutations satisfy the equations. These are all the solutions, and none has all entries real.
