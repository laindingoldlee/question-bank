A goat keeps a pet human on a leash. One end of the leash is tied to the human and the other end to one vertex of the goat's barn, which is a regular hexagon of side length $120$. The leash has length $600$, it cannot pass through the barn, and the human must stay outside the barn. What is the area of the region the human can reach?
