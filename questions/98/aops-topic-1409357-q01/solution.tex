A divisor of $A$ is $2^a3^b5^c7^d11^e$ with $0\le a\le3$, $0\le b\le5$, $0\le c\le7$, $0\le d\le11$, $0\le e\le2$, so $A$ has
\[
4\cdot6\cdot8\cdot12\cdot3=6912
\]
divisors. A divisor is divisible by $30=2\cdot3\cdot5$ exactly when $a,b,c\ge1$; there are $3\cdot5\cdot7\cdot12\cdot3=3780$ of these. By complementary counting the answer is
\[
6912-3780=\boxed{3132}.
\]
