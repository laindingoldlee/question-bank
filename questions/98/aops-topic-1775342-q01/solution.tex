Suppose the first messenger leaves when the king is $d$ km from the castle. He reaches the castle $\dfrac{d}{10}$ hours later.

In the next hour the king and the second messenger walk $5$ km further, so the second messenger leaves $1$ hour later from $d+5$ km away and needs $\dfrac{d+5}{10}=\dfrac {d}{10}+\dfrac{1}{2}$ hours. Measured from the first departure, he arrives after $1+\dfrac{d}{10}+\dfrac{1}{2}$ hours.

The difference is $1\dfrac{1}{2}$ hours $=\boxed{90}$ minutes.
