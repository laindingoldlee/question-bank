A king and his messengers are walking at $5$ km/h from the king's castle to his summer palace. At a certain moment the king sends one messenger back to the castle; one hour later he sends another messenger back. The messengers walk at $10$ km/h. How many minutes pass between the arrivals of the two messengers at the castle?
