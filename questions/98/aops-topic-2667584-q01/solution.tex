$4000=2^5\cdot5^3$. Write $a=2^{x_1}5^{y_1}$, $b=2^{x_2}5^{y_2}$, $c=2^{x_3}5^{y_3}$. Then $x_1+x_2+x_3=5$ and $y_1+y_2+y_3=3$ with nonnegative integers. By stars and bars there are $\dbinom72=21$ and $\dbinom52=10$ solutions, so there are $21\cdot10=\boxed{210}$ ordered triples.
