Multiply both sides by \(bd(b+d)\neq 0\):
\[
(ad-bc)(b+d)=bd(a-c)\iff abd+ad^2-b^2c-bcd=abd-bcd\iff ad^2=b^2c .
\]
All steps are reversible, so the identity holds exactly when
\[
\boxed{ad^2=b^2c},\quad\text{equivalently}\quad \dfrac{a}{b^2}=\dfrac{c}{d^2}.
\]
Check on the example: \(9\cdot 10^2=900=6^2\cdot 25\), and indeed \(\dfrac{9}{6}-\dfrac{25}{10}=-1=\dfrac{-16}{16}\).
