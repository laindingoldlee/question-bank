For which fractions \(\dfrac {a}{b}\) and \(\dfrac {c}{d}\) (with \(b\), \(d\), \(b+d\) nonzero) is it true that
\[
\dfrac {a}{b}-\dfrac {c}{d}=\dfrac{a-c}{b+d}\,?
\]
For example, \(\dfrac{9}{6}-\dfrac{25}{10}=\dfrac{9-25}{6+10}\).
