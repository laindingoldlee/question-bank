The numbers add up to $3\cdot16=48$. The greatest number $c$ is one less than a cube: $c\in\{7,26,63,\ldots\}$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $c\ge63$ is impossible since $c<48$.
\item $c=7$ is impossible: three numbers at most $7$ add up to at most $21<48$.
\end{itemize}
So $c=26$; the least is $\dfrac{26}{2}-5=8$, and the middle number is $48-26-8=14$. Indeed $8<14<26$. The numbers are $\boxed{8,\ 14,\ 26}$.
