Consider functions $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ with $f(3x)=3f(x)$ for all real $x$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that $f(x)=cx$ satisfies the condition for every constant $c$, so there are solutions other than $f(x)=0$.
\item[(b)] Show that $f(x)=|x|$ is also a solution, so not every solution has the form $f(x)=cx$.
\item[(c)] Prove that every solution has $f(0)=0$.
\end{enumerate}
