Find the error in the following ``proof''.

\emph{Claim.} There are no rational numbers between $0$ and $1$ (exclusive).

\emph{Proof.} Suppose there were rational numbers between $0$ and $1$, and let $a$ be the smallest of them. Since $x^2-x=x(x-1)<0$ for $0<x<1$, we have $a^2<a$. Also $a^2>0$. So $0<a^2<a<1$. But $a^2$ is rational (a product of rational numbers is rational), so $a^2$ is a rational number between $0$ and $1$ smaller than $a$, contradicting the choice of $a$. Hence there are no rational numbers between $0$ and $1$.
