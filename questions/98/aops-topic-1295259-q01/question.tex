An $8\times7$ rectangle (width $8$, height $7$) has a $3$-$4$-$5$ right triangle drawn in each corner, with its right angle at the corner of the rectangle and its legs along the sides of the rectangle. The two lower triangles have their legs of length $4$ along the bottom edge of the rectangle, and the two upper triangles have their legs of length $3$ along the top edge. A circle is tangent to the hypotenuse of each of the four triangles. The diameter of the circle is $\dfrac {m}{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $m+n$.
