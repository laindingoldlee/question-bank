By the double-angle formula,
\[
\tan2a=\dfrac{2\tan a}{1-\tan^2a}=\dfrac{2\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}}{1-\dfrac{1}{4}}=\dfrac{1}{\dfrac{3}{4}}=\dfrac{4}{3} .
\]
For complementary angles, $\tan(90^\circ-\theta)=\dfrac{1}{\tan\theta}$ (in a right triangle the legs swap roles), so
\[
\tan(90^\circ-2a)=\dfrac{1}{\tan2a}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{4}}.
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair R=(0,0),P=(3,0),Q=(0,4);
draw(P--Q--R--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw((0.3,0)--(0.3,0.3)--(0,0.3));
draw(arc(P,0.6,degrees(Q-P),180));label("$2a$",P+0.95*dir((degrees(Q-P)+180)/2),fontsize(8pt));
draw(arc(Q,0.7,270,degrees(P-Q)));pair mq=Q+0.7*dir((270+degrees(P-Q))/2);draw(mq--Q+(1.1,-0.25),gray);label("$90^\circ-2a$",Q+(1.1,-0.25),E,fontsize(8pt));
label("$P$",P,SE);label("$Q$",Q,N);label("$R$",R,SW);
label("$3$",(P+R)/2,S);label("$4$",(Q+R)/2,W);label("$5$",(P+Q)/2,NE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

In the $3$-$4$-$5$ right triangle above, the angle at $P$ has tangent $\dfrac{4}{3}$, so it is $2a$; the angle at $Q$ is $90^\circ-2a$, with tangent $\dfrac{3}{4}$. (This uses $\tan a=\dfrac{1}{2}$ with $a$ acute; then $2a<90^\circ$.)
