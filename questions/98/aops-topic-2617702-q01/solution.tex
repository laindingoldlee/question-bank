Let there be $n$ nickels. Then there are $2n$ quarters and $n+8$ dimes, and in cents
\[5n+10(n+8)+25\cdot2n=665\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad65n=585\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad n=9.\]
So there are $9+8=\boxed{17}$ dimes. (Check: $45+170+450=665$ cents.)
