For a positive integer $x$, the function $t\mapsto t^x$ is strictly increasing on positive numbers: if $a<b$ then $a^x<b^x$, and if $a>b$ then $a^x>b^x$. So $a^x=b^x$ forces $a=b$. $\blacksquare$ (For $x=0$ the claim fails, which is why $x\ne0$ is needed; for even $x$ and negative numbers it also fails: $(-2)^2=2^2$.)
