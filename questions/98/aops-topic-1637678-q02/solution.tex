Each trailing zero comes from a factor $10=2\cdot5$, and there are more factors $2$ than factors $5$ in $200!$, so we count the factors of $5$. Among $1,\dots,200$ there are $\left\lfloor\dfrac{200}{5}\right\rfloor=40$ multiples of $5$, $\left\lfloor\dfrac{200}{25}\right\rfloor=8$ multiples of $25$ (each giving a second $5$) and $\left\lfloor\dfrac{200}{125}\right\rfloor=1$ multiple of $125$ (a third $5$). So $200!$ contains $5^{40+8+1}=5^{49}$ exactly, and it ends in $\boxed{49}$ zeros.
