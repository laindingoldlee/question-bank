Each square root needs a nonnegative argument:
\[
2-0.5t\ge0\iff t\le4,\qquad -1.1t+4\ge0\iff t\le\dfrac{4}{1.1}=\dfrac{40}{11},\qquad t-3\ge0\iff t\ge3 .
\]
Since $\dfrac{40}{11}\approx3.64<4$, all three hold exactly when $3\le t\le\dfrac{40}{11}$. The domain is $\boxed{\left[3,\dfrac{40}{11}\right]}$.
