Put $u=\sqrt{x-3}\ge0$. Then $u^2+u-42=(u-6)(u+7)=0$, so only $u=6$ is admissible. Therefore $\boxed{x=39}$. Direct substitution gives $39-3+\sqrt{39-3}=36+6=42$, verifying the solution.
