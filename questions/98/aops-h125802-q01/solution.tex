Heron\textquotesingle s formula with semiperimeter $21$ gives original area $\sqrt{21\cdot8\cdot7\cdot6}=84$. The overlap ratio is the same for every triangle, since an invertible affine map preserves area ratios and commutes with a half-turn about the centroid. For the reference triangle $(0,0),(3,0),(0,3)$, the rotated triangle has vertices $(2,2),(-1,2),(2,-1)$. Their overlap removes three corner right triangles of legs $1$ from the original triangle of legs $3$, so its area is $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of the original. Therefore the union has area $2\cdot84-\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot84=\boxed{112}$.
