The reciprocals of three positive integers add up to $1$. One of the three integers is a multiple of $2$, and a different one of them is a multiple of $3$. The multiple of $2$ is divided by $2$, the multiple of $3$ is divided by $3$, and the two quotients are added. What is the largest possible value of this sum?
