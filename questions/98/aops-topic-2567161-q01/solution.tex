First find all solutions of $\dfrac1a+\dfrac1b+\dfrac1c=1$ with $a\le b\le c$. Since each reciprocal is less than $1$, $a\ge2$; since $\dfrac3a\ge1$, $a\le3$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $a=3$: then $\dfrac1b+\dfrac1c=\dfrac23$ with $3\le b\le c$, which forces $b=c=3$.
\item $a=2$: then $\dfrac1b+\dfrac1c=\dfrac12$, i.e. $bc=2b+2c$, i.e. $(b-2)(c-2)=4$ with $1\le b-2\le c-2$. So $(b,c)=(3,6)$ or $(4,4)$.
\end{itemize}
The solutions are $\{3,3,3\}$, $\{2,4,4\}$ and $\{2,3,6\}$. The first has no even number and the second has no multiple of $3$, so the numbers are $2,3,6$.

The multiple of $2$ is $2$ or $6$ and the (different) multiple of $3$ is $3$ or $6$:
\[(2,3):\ 1+1=2,\qquad (2,6):\ 1+2=3,\qquad (6,3):\ 3+1=4.\]
The largest possible sum is $\boxed{4}$.
