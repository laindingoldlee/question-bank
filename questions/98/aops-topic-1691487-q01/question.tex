Define $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ by
\[
f(x)=\begin{cases}1,&x\text{ rational},\\0,&x\text{ irrational}.\end{cases}
\]
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that the graph of $f$ contains no segment of the line $y=0$ and no segment of the line $y=1$, even though every interval of the $x$-axis contains points of the graph on both lines.
\item[(b)] Prove that $f$ is not continuous at any real number.
\end{enumerate}
