Lev and Alex buy a hat and leave a tip. Lev wants to tip $20\%$ of the bill, while Alex wants to round the bill up to the next whole dollar (on a bill of $\$8.36$ he would tip $64$ cents; on a whole-dollar bill he would tip nothing). Costs are whole numbers of cents. What is the largest possible cost of the hat, in cents, for which Alex wants to tip more than Lev?
