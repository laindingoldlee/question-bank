Place $O_1=(0,0)$, $O_2=(6,0)$, $P_1=(2,0)$, $P_2=(4,0)$. The center of $\omega$ is $(3,t)$ and its radius squared is $1+t^2$. Its equation is $x^2+y^2-6x-2ty+8=0$. Intersecting it with $x^2+y^2=4$ gives $3x+ty=6$, so the other intersection is $Q_1=(\dfrac{2(9-t^2)}{t^2+9},\dfrac{12t}{t^2+9})$. By reflection, the vector $O_2Q_2$ is $(-u,v)$ when $O_1Q_1=(u,v)$. Parallelism forces $2uv=0$. If $t=0$, the first two circles are tangent, contrary to distinct intersections. Hence $u=0$, so $t^2=9$. The desired area is $\pi(1+t^2)=\boxed{10\pi}$.
