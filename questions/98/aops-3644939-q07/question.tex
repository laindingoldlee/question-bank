Helena writes down the number $0$ on a chalkboard. Then, every minute afterwards, she counts how many digits in total are on the board and writes down that number. She repeats this until she has written $50$ separate numbers on the board (including the first number, 0). For example, if $0$, $3$, $12$, and $147$ were written on the board, there would be four numbers and seven digits. What is the last number Helena writes?
