A ladder of length $1$ slides down: its top stays against a vertical wall and its foot stays on the horizontal floor, from the vertical position to the horizontal position.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that the midpoint of the ladder traces a quarter of the circle with radius $\dfrac{1}{2}$ centred at the corner where the wall meets the floor.
\item[(b)] A student objects: ``If the foot is at distance $x$ from the wall, the top is at height $1-x$. At the moment the midpoint is at $\left(\dfrac{\sqrt2}{4},\dfrac{\sqrt2}{4}\right)$, the foot and the top are both at $\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$, and $\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}+\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\ne1$, so the midpoint cannot trace a quarter circle.'' Find the error.
\end{enumerate}
