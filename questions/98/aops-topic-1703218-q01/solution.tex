Since $87=6\cdot14+3$ and $14=2\cdot5+4$, one expression is
\[
\boxed{6\times(2\times5+4)+3=87}.
\]
Check: $2\times5+4=14$, $6\times14=84$ and $84+3=87$, and each of $2,3,4,5,6$ is used once.

There are other answers, for example $6\times\left(3\times4+\dfrac{5}{2}\right)=6\times14.5=87$.
