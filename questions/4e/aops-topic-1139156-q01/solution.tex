Let her income be $I>28000$. Then
\[\dfrac{p}{100}\cdot28000+\dfrac{p+2}{100}(I-28000)=\dfrac{p+0.25}{100}I.\]
The terms with $p$ cancel ($p\cdot28000+p(I-28000)=pI$), leaving $2(I-28000)=0.25I$, so $1.75I=56000$ and $I=\boxed{\$32{,}000}$.
