Write $m-34=x^2$ and $m+7=y^2$ with integers $x,y\ge0$. Subtracting,
\[
y^2-x^2=41\quad\Longrightarrow\quad(y-x)(y+x)=41 .
\]
Since $y+x\ge0$ and the product is positive, both factors are positive, and $y-x\le y+x$. As $41$ is prime, $y-x=1$ and $y+x=41$, so $y=21$, $x=20$. Then $m=x^2+34=400+34=\boxed{434}$, the only solution. Check: $434-34=400=20^2$ and $434+7=441=21^2$.
