In an alternate universe every day is $2\%$ longer than the day before, but people still use our $12$-month calendar. Jon gets a haircut every $67485$ minutes and Billy gets a haircut every $30425$ minutes. Both get a haircut at the same moment on January $2$, when the day is $1520$ minutes long. On what date do they next get a haircut at the same moment? (Assume that no February $29$ occurs in the period.)
