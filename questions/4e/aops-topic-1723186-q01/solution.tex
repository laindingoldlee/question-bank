Take the team with the smallest label: its opponent can be chosen in $7$ ways. Among the remaining $6$ teams, the smallest-labelled one has $5$ possible opponents, then $3$, then $1$. So there are $7\cdot5\cdot3\cdot1=105$ pairings. (Equivalently, $\dfrac{8!}{2^4\cdot4!}=\dfrac{40320}{384}=105$: order all teams, pair them off consecutively, and divide by the orders within each pair and the order of the pairs.) The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(B) }105}$.
