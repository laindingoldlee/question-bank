A parkour course has $4$ jumps. On each attempt at a jump, the player succeeds with probability $90\%$, independently. If he fails, he respawns at the block he jumped from (instantly) and tries that jump again. A failed attempt takes $2$ seconds and a successful jump takes $1$ second. What is the expected time to complete the course?
