The characteristic equation $ r^2-2r-3=(r-3)(r+1)=0 $ suggests $ a_n=A3^{n-1}+B(-1)^{n-1}$. The initial conditions give $ A+B=1 $ and $ 3A-B=2 $, so $ A=\dfrac{3}{4},B=\dfrac{1}{4}$. Thus $\boxed{a_n=\dfrac{3^n+(-1)^{n-1}}{4}}$. Direct substitution verifies the recurrence and initial values, which uniquely determine the sequence by induction.
