Since $c\ne0$, the product of the roots $mn=\dfrac {c}{a}$ is nonzero, so $m\ne0$ and $n\ne0$. For $x\ne0$,
\[
cx^2+bx+a=x^2\left(a\left(\dfrac{1}{x}\right)^2+b\left(\dfrac{1}{x}\right)+c\right),
\]
so $x$ is a root of $cx^2+bx+a$ exactly when $\dfrac{1}{x}$ is a root of $ax^2+bx+c$, i.e. $\dfrac{1}{x}\in\{m,n\}$. Also $x=0$ is not a root because $a\ne0$. Hence the roots are $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{m}\text{ and }\dfrac{1}{n}}$.

(With Vieta: $\dfrac{1}{m}+\dfrac{1}{n}=\dfrac{m+n}{mn}=\dfrac{-\dfrac {b}{a}}{\dfrac {c}{a}}=-\dfrac {b}{c}$ and $\dfrac{1}{m}\cdot\dfrac{1}{n}=\dfrac {a}{c}$, which are the sum and product of the roots of $cx^2+bx+a$.)
