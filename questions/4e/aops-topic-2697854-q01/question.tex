A set of distinct positive integers, each less than $7$ and with at least two elements, is chosen. Abigail is told its sum and Benjamin its product. Abigail says: ``I don't know whether you know my number.'' Benjamin replies: ``I know your number, and now I know that you know my number too.'' What is the set?
