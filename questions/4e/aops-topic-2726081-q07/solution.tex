Let the side be $s$, the diagonals $d_1,d_2$ and the radius $r$. The area of the rhombus is $\dfrac{d_1d_2}{2}$. Since the circle touches all four sides, splitting the rhombus into four triangles from the centre gives area $\dfrac12r\cdot4s=2rs$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(-1.6,0),B=(0,-1.25),C=(1.6,0),D=(0,1.25);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.8));draw(A--C,dashed);draw(B--D,dashed);
real r=1.6*1.25/abs(A-D);draw(circle((0,0),r),gray);
pair T=r*dir(degrees(D-A)+90);draw((0,0)--T,linewidth(0.6));label("$r$",T/2,NE,fontsize(7pt));
\end{asy}

Given $4s=d_1d_2$, the area is $\dfrac{4s}{2}=2s$, so $2rs=2s$ and $r=\boxed{1}$.
