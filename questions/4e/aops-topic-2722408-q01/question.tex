Let $T_n=\displaystyle\sum_{m=1}^{n}m$, $A_n=\displaystyle\sum_{m=1}^{n}T_m$ and $B_n=\displaystyle\sum_{m=1}^{n}A_m$. For how many positive integers $n\le2021$ is $B_n=kT_n$ for some positive integer $k$?
