Subtract consecutive recurrences: $a_{n+5}-a_{n+4}=a_{n+4}-a_n$, so $a_{n+5}=2a_{n+4}-a_n\equiv a_n\pmod2$. Thus parities repeat with period five. The first five terms are $1,1,2,3,7$, with only the third even. Among $2011=5\cdot402+1$ terms, there are $402$ full blocks and one extra odd term. Hence $\boxed{402}$ terms are even.
