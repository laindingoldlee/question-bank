A triangle in the coordinate plane has vertices $(-4,0)$, $(6,0)$ and $(0,5)$. The line $y=\dfrac{5}{4}x+c$, where $c$ is a positive number, divides the triangle into a trapezoid and a smaller triangle whose areas, respectively, are in the ratio $5:4$. What is the value of $c$? Express your answer as a common fraction.
