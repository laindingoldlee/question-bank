Three odd primes would have odd sum, so one prime equals 2. Two copies of 2 would force the third to be 62, not prime. Set $a=2$. Then $b+c=64$ and $bc=1071-2\cdot64=943=23\cdot41$. Thus $b,c$ are 23 and 41, giving $abc=\boxed{1886}$.
