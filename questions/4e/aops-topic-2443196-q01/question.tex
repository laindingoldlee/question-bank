Let $ABC$ be a triangle. Triangles $DEF$ and $PQR$ have $D$ and $P$ on side $AB$, $E$ and $Q$ on side $BC$, and $F$ and $R$ on side $CA$. The six points $D,E,F,P,Q,R$ are distinct and none of them is a vertex of $ABC$. What is the least possible number of points that lie on both the boundary of triangle $DEF$ and the boundary of triangle $PQR$?
