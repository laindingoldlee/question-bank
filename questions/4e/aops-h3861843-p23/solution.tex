Measure angles in turns, and let $h\in[0,1)$ be the hour-hand angle. The minute-hand angle is $12h$ modulo one. Swapping the hands yields another valid time precisely when $144h\equiv h\pmod1$, or $143h\in\mathbb Z$. There are $143$ candidates in the half-open twelve-hour interval. At $11$ of them the hands overlap, since also $11h\in\mathbb Z$; these swaps do not give a different time. The other $143-11=\boxed{132}$ times are ambiguous.
