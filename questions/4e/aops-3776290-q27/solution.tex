Subtracting equations gives $x^2+xy=36$, so $x\ne0$ and $y=\dfrac{36}{x}-x$. Substitution into $y^2-2xy=-15$ and multiplication by $x^2$ yield $x^4-43x^2+432=0$, or $(x^2-16)(x^2-27)=0$. Thus $x=\pm4,\pm3\sqrt3$, each giving exactly one real $y$ and satisfying both equations. There are $\boxed4$ solutions.
