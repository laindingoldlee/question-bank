Within one division there are $\dbinom62=15$ pairs of teams, each playing twice, so $30$ games; the two divisions give $60$ games. Between the divisions, each of the $6$ teams in one division plays each of the $6$ in the other once: $36$ games. Total:
\[
60+36=96,
\]
choice $\boxed{\textbf{(B)}\ 96}$.

(Check by counting team-games: each team plays $2\cdot5+6=16$ games, and $\dfrac{12\cdot16}{2}=96$ since each game involves two teams.)
