The first inequality says $ x(y-z)<0 $. Since $ y-z>0 $, necessarily $ x<0 $. The product $ xyz $ can have any sign. With $ x=-1 $, choose $(y,z)=(2,1)$ for a negative product, $(1,-1)$ for a positive product, or $(1,0)$ for zero. Each choice satisfies the two given inequalities.
