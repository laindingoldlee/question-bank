Let $x$ dollars be invested at $14\%$, so $25000-x$ dollars are invested at $16\%$. The yearly interest is
\[
0.14x+0.16(25000-x)=4000-0.02x .
\]
We need $4000-0.02x\ge3600$, that is $0.02x\le400$, so $x\le20000$. The most that can be invested at $14\%$ is $\boxed{\$20{,}000}$. (Check: $0.14\cdot20000+0.16\cdot5000=2800+800=3600$.)
