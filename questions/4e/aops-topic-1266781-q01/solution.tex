Since $6^2+8^2=10^2$, angle $B$ is a right angle.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0),A=(0,6),C=(8,0),D=(A+C)/2,F=(4,0);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(1)); draw(B--D,blue); draw(D--F,red+dashed);
draw((0,0.4)--(0.4,0.4)--(0.4,0)); draw((4,0.4)--(4.4,0.4)--(4.4,0));
dot(D); label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,NE);label("$F$",F,S);
label("$6$",(0,3),W); label("$8$",(2,0),S); label("$3$",(4,1.5),E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

In a right triangle the midpoint of the hypotenuse is equidistant from all three vertices, so the midpoint $D$ of $AC$ satisfies $AD=DB=5$; and it is the only point of $AC$ with $AD=DB$ (the points with $DA=DB$ lie on the perpendicular bisector of $AB$, which meets line $AC$ only once).

The foot $F$ of the perpendicular from $D$ to $BC$ makes $DF\parallel AB$ with $D$ the midpoint of $AC$, so $DF$ is a midline of triangle $ABC$: $DF=\dfrac{1}{2}AB=\boxed{3}$.
