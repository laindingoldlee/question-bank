A rectangular sheet of paper is 8.5 inches wide and 11 inches tall. Fold it along the perpendicular bisector of a diagonal so that the diagonal's opposite vertices coincide. Find the perimeter of the union of the folded sheet, to the nearest tenth of an inch.
