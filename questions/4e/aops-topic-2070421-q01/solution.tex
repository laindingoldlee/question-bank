The characteristic equation is $r^2=2r+15$, i.e.\ $(r-5)(r+3)=0$, with roots $5$ and $-3$. Every sequence $A\cdot5^n+B\cdot(-3)^n$ satisfies the recurrence, and since a solution is determined by $a_1,a_2$, it suffices to match these:
\[
5A-3B=1,\qquad 25A+9B=7 .
\]
Multiplying the first by $3$ and adding gives $40A=10$, so $A=\dfrac{1}{4}$, and then $3B=5A-1=\dfrac{1}{4}$, so $B=\dfrac{1}{12}$. Hence
\[
\boxed{a_n=\dfrac{5^n}{4}+\dfrac{(-3)^n}{12}=\dfrac{3\cdot5^n+(-3)^n}{12}}.
\]
Check: $a_1=\dfrac{15-3}{12}=1$, $a_2=\dfrac{75+9}{12}=7$, and $a_3=\dfrac{375-27}{12}=29=2\cdot7+15\cdot1$.
