For a prime factorisation, $\sigma$ is the product over the prime powers $p^e$ of $1+p+\cdots+p^e$.

\textbf{(a)} $1635=3\cdot5\cdot109$ and $1634=2\cdot19\cdot43$, with all factors prime. So
\[\sigma(1635)=4\cdot6\cdot110=2640,\qquad\sigma(1634)=3\cdot20\cdot44=2640.\]

\textbf{(b)} $2640=2^4\cdot3\cdot5\cdot11$, so
\[\sigma(2640)=(1+2+4+8+16)\cdot4\cdot6\cdot12=31\cdot288=8928.\]
Then $1635+2640+8928=13203$. Solving $\dfrac{k(k+1)}{2}=13203$ gives $k^2+k-26406=0$, with discriminant $1+4\cdot26406=105625=325^2$, so $k=\dfrac{-1+325}{2}=162$. Indeed
\[13203=\dfrac{162\cdot163}{2}=\boxed{T_{162}}.\]
