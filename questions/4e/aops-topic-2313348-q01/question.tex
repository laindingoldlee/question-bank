Let $\sigma(n)$ denote the sum of the positive divisors of $n$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that $\sigma(1635)=\sigma(1634)$.
\item[(b)] Show that $1635+\sigma(1635)+\sigma(\sigma(1635))$ is a triangular number, that is, a number of the form $\dfrac{k(k+1)}{2}$.
\end{enumerate}
