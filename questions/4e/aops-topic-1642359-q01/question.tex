Let $N$ be the number of non-congruent triangles with all of the following properties:
\begin{itemize}
\item all three side lengths are integers at most $15$;
\item the area is an integer;
\item at least one angle measures $30^\circ$, $45^\circ$, $60^\circ$, $120^\circ$, $135^\circ$ or $150^\circ$.
\end{itemize}
Compute $N$.
