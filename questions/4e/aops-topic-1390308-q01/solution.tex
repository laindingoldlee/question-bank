Let the year be $\overline{pqtu}$. We need $t+1=5u$ with $0\le t\le9$, so $(t,u)=(4,1)$ or $(t,u)=(9,2)$. The year is before $2016$, so $p=1$ or $p=2$ (and $p=2$ forces the year to begin $20$, so $q\le1$).
\begin{itemize}
\item $(t,u)=(4,1)$: then $p\ne1$ (the digits are different), so $p=2$ and $q=17-2-4-1=10$, impossible.
\item $(t,u)=(9,2)$: then $p\ne2$, so $p=1$ and $q=17-1-9-2=5$. The digits $1,5,9,2$ are all different.
\end{itemize}
The year is $\boxed{1592}$. (As the poster notes, the real year is $1492$; the puzzle's data do not fit it, since $1+4+9+2=16$.)
