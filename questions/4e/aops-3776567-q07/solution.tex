Measure distance in the son\textquotesingle s step lengths. During six father steps, the father travels $6\cdot\dfrac{8}{5}=\dfrac{48}{5}$ while the son travels $7$. The gain is $\dfrac{13}{5}$. Closing $52$ units requires $52\div\dfrac{13}{5}=20$ such intervals, or $20\cdot6=\boxed{120}$ father steps.
