Let $r_2=1$. A regular $n$-gon with inradius $r_{n-1}$ has area $A_n=n r_{n-1}^2\tan(\dfrac{\pi}{n})$, and its circumradius is $r_n=\dfrac{r_{n-1}}{\cos(\dfrac{\pi}{n})}$. Therefore the exact requested result is $\boxed{\sum_{n=3}^8(-1)^n n\tan(\dfrac{\pi}{n})\prod_{j=3}^{n-1}\sec^2(\dfrac{\pi}{j})}$. The empty product is one. Evaluating the six terms gives approximately $\boxed{35.6738428760}$. The formula follows directly by splitting each polygon into $n$ triangles of height its inradius.
