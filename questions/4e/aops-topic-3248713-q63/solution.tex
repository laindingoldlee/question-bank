If the integer is $n$, integrality of $\dfrac{3}{5}n$ requires $5\mid n$. The first candidates are $5,10,15$. Their proper-divisor sums are $1,8,9$, respectively, whereas three-fifths of them is $3,6,9$. Therefore the smallest is $\boxed{15}$.
