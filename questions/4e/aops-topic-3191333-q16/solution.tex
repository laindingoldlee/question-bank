The crease has midpoint $(\dfrac{1}{4},\dfrac{1}{2})$ and slope $\dfrac{1}{2}$, so reflection sends a horizontal direction vector $(1,0)$ to $(\dfrac{3}{5},\dfrac{4}{5})$. Thus the reflected edge lies on the line through $P$ of slope $\dfrac{4}{3}$. At $x=1$ it has $y=\dfrac{4}{3}(1-\dfrac{1}{2})=\dfrac{2}{3}$, giving $\boxed{BQ=\dfrac{2}{3}}$. The intersection is on the reflected edge because its parameter is $\dfrac{5}{6}\in[0,1]$.
