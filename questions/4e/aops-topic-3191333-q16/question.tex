A unit square has $A=(0,0),B=(1,0),C=(1,1),D=(0,1)$ and $P=(\dfrac{1}{2},0)$. Fold across the perpendicular bisector of $DP$, sending $D$ to $P$. The image of edge $DC$ meets line $BC$ at $Q$. Find $BQ$.
