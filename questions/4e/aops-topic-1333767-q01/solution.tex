One solution is
\[
\boxed{99\div9+9=20},
\]
since $99\div9=11$ and $11+9=20$.

Joining two nines is necessary: a computer search over all ways of combining four separate nines with $+,-,\times,\div$ and any bracketing shows that $20$ cannot be obtained (for example $9+9+9\div9=19$ is the closest natural attempt).
