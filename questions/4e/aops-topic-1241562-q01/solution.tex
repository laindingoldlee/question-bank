Note $x^3+9x^2+27x+28=(x+3)^3+1$, a sum of cubes:
\[(x+3)^3+1=(x+4)\left((x+3)^2-(x+3)+1\right)=(x+4)\left(x^2+5x+7\right).\]
So the quotient is $\boxed{x^2+5x+7}$ with remainder $0$.
