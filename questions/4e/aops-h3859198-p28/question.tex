A regular \(101\)-gon has a real number assigned to each vertex. The smallest assigned number is \(1\), and the largest is \(M\). Each edge is labeled with the sum of the numbers at its endpoints. If the difference between the largest and smallest edge labels is \(1\), what is the largest possible value of \(M\)?



\[
\textbf{(A)}\ 3
\qquad
\textbf{(B)}\ 34
\qquad
\textbf{(C)}\ 51
\qquad
\textbf{(D)}\ 67
\qquad
\textbf{(E)}\ 101
\]
