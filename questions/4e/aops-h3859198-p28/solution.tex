Along any path with an even number $2k$ of edges, the difference of the endpoint values is an alternating sum of edge labels. Pair successive terms; each pair differs by at most one, so the endpoint difference is at most $k$. Between any two vertices of an odd 101-cycle, one of the two paths has even length at most 100. Therefore $M-1\le50$. Equality is attained by the cyclic sequence $1,50,2,49,3,48,\ldots,50,1,51$: its edge sums are 51 or 52. Thus $\boxed{M=51}$, choice C.
