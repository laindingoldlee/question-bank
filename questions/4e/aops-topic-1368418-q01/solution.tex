$AN$ and $CM$ are medians of triangle $ABC$, so $P$ is the centroid. Place $B=(0,0)$, $A=(0,6)$, $C=(8,0)$; then $P=\left(\dfrac{0+0+8}{3},\dfrac{6+0+0}{3}\right)=\left(\dfrac{8}{3},2\right)$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0),A=(0,6),C=(8,0),M=(0,3),N=(4,0),P=(8/3,2);
fill(A--P--C--cycle,lightblue);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); draw(A--N,red); draw(C--M,red);
draw((0,0.5)--(0.5,0.5)--(0.5,0));
dot(M); dot(N); dot(P);
label("$A$",A,N); label("$B$",B,SW); label("$C$",C,SE); label("$M$",M,W); label("$N$",N,S); label("$P$",P,SW);
label("$3$",(0,4.5),W); label("$3$",(0,1.5),W); label("$4$",(2,0),S); label("$4$",(6,0),S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The centroid is $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of the way from side $AC$ to the opposite vertex $B$, so the height of triangle $APC$ on base $AC$ is $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of the height of $ABC$. Hence
\[[APC]=\dfrac{1}{3}[ABC]=\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot6\cdot8=\boxed{8}.\]
(Shoelace check: $\dfrac{1}{2}\left|0(2-0)+\dfrac{8}{3}(0-6)+8(6-2)\right|=\dfrac{1}{2}\left|-16+32\right|=8$.)
