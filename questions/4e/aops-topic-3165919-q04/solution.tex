Put $D=(0,0)$ and $C=(6,0)$. Since $AD=AC=5$, the vertex $A$ lies above the midpoint of $CD$ at $(3,4)$ after reflection if needed. Convex cyclic order and $AB\parallel CD$, $AB=3$ place $B=(6,4)$. Hence $\boxed{BC=4}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(3,4),Bp=(6,4),C=(6,0),D=(0,0);draw(A--Bp--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C,dashed);label("$A$",A,NW);label("$B$",Bp,NE);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,SW);
\end{asy}
