Place the radius 1 and radius 3 centres at $(0,0),(4,0)$. The radius 12 centre has distances 13 and 15 from these, giving coordinates $(-5,12)$ (or its reflection). The tangency points, dividing centre segments in the radius ratios, are $X=(1,0)$, $Y=(-\dfrac{5}{13},\dfrac{12}{13})$, $Z=(\dfrac{11}{5},\dfrac{12}{5})$. The point $K=(1,\dfrac{3}{2})$ has squared distance $\dfrac{9}{4}$ from each: from $Y$ the difference vector is $(-\dfrac{18}{13},-\dfrac{15}{26})$, and from $Z$ it is $(\dfrac{6}{5},\dfrac{9}{10})$. Thus the circumradius is $\boxed{\dfrac{3}{2}}$.

\end{asy}
