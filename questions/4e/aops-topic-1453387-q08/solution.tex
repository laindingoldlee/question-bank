Divide by $2$ and complete the squares:
\[
x^2+2x+y^2-4y=3\iff (x+1)^2+(y-2)^2=8 .
\]
So the graph is the circle with centre $C=(-1,2)$ and radius $2\sqrt2$. The distance from $(5,7)$ to the centre is $\sqrt{6^2+5^2}=\sqrt{61}>2\sqrt2$, so the point is outside the circle. For any point $X$ on the circle, $PX\ge PC-CX=\sqrt{61}-2\sqrt2$ by the triangle inequality, with equality at the point $Q$ where segment $PC$ crosses the circle. The distance is
\[
\boxed{\sqrt{61}-2\sqrt2}\approx4.98 .
\]
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
pair C=(-1,2); real rr=2*sqrt(2); pair P=(5,7);
pair Qn=C+rr*unit(P-C);
xaxis(-5,7,Arrow); yaxis(-2,8.5,Arrow);
draw(circle(C,rr),blue+linewidth(0.9));
draw(C--Qn,gray+dashed); draw(Qn--P,red+linewidth(1));
dot(C); dot(P); dot(Qn,red);
label("$(-1,2)$",C,SW); label("$(5,7)$",P,E); label("$Q$",Qn+(0.1,0),E,red);
label("$2\sqrt{2}$",C+0.4*(Qn-C),NW);
label("$x$",(7,0),S); label("$y$",(0,8.5),W);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
