For $a,b,c>0$ the expression is $\dfrac{54}{\dfrac3c+\dfrac4a+\dfrac{18}b}$. By Cauchy--Schwarz,
\[\left(\dfrac4a+\dfrac{18}b+\dfrac3c\right)\left(\dfrac32a+\dfrac13b+2c\right)\ge\left(\sqrt6+\sqrt6+\sqrt6\right)^2=54,\]
so $\dfrac4a+\dfrac{18}b+\dfrac3c\ge9$ and the expression is at most $6$. Equality needs each product $\dfrac4a\cdot\dfrac32a$, $\dfrac{18}b\cdot\dfrac b3$, $\dfrac3c\cdot2c$ to be proportional to its bracket term, which happens at $a=\dfrac43$, $b=6$, $c=1$ (check: $2+2+2=6$). The maximum is $\boxed{6}$.
