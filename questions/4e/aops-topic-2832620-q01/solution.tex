$x=\sqrt[n]n$ is a root of the monic polynomial $t^n-n$. By the rational root theorem, a rational root of a monic integer polynomial is an integer. So if $x$ were rational, $x=k$ would be an integer with $k^n=n$. But $k\ge2$ (as $n>1$) gives $k^n\ge2^n>n$, a contradiction. $\blacksquare$
