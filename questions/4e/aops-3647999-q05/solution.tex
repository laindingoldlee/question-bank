Let $g=\gcd(x,y)$ and $\ell=\operatorname{lcm}(x,y)$. Since $g$ divides both $x+y=33$ and $g+\ell=87$, $g=1$ or 3. Also $xy=g\ell=g(87-g)$. For $g=1$, the quadratic with root sum 33 and product 86 has discriminant 745, not a square. For $g=3$, the product is 252 and the roots are 12,21, which indeed have gcd 3. Therefore $\boxed{xy=252}$.
