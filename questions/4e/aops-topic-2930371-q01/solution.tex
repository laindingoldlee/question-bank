$k!+(k+1)!+(k+2)!=k!\bigl(1+(k+1)+(k+1)(k+2)\bigr)=k!(k+2)^2$. So the general term is
\[\dfrac{k+2}{k!(k+2)^2}=\dfrac1{k!(k+2)}=\dfrac{k+1}{(k+2)!}=\dfrac1{(k+1)!}-\dfrac1{(k+2)!}.\]
Summing over $k=1,\dots,2015$ telescopes to $\dfrac1{2!}-\dfrac1{2017!}$. So $n=2017$ and its digit sum is $\boxed{10}$.
