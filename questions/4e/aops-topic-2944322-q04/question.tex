There are unique integers $a_2,\dots,a_7$ with $0\le a_i<i$ such that $\dfrac57=\dfrac{a_2}{2!}+\dfrac{a_3}{3!}+\cdots+\dfrac{a_7}{7!}$. Find $a_2+\cdots+a_7$.
