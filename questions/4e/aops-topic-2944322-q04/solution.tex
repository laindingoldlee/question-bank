Extract digits greedily. $\dfrac57\cdot2=\dfrac{10}7$, so $a_2=1$, remainder $\dfrac57-\dfrac12=\dfrac3{14}$. Then $\dfrac3{14}\cdot6=\dfrac97$, so $a_3=1$, remainder $\dfrac1{21}$. Then $\dfrac{24}{21}$ gives $a_4=1$, remainder $\dfrac1{168}$. Then $\dfrac{120}{168}<1$ gives $a_5=0$. Then $\dfrac{720}{168}$ gives $a_6=4$, remainder $\dfrac1{168}-\dfrac4{720}=\dfrac1{2520}$. Finally $\dfrac{5040}{2520}=2$ gives $a_7=2$, remainder $0$. The sum is $1+1+1+0+4+2=\boxed{9}$.
