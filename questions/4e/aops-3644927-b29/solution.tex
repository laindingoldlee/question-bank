The actual simulated total depends on the random sample, so estimate its expectation. For an isotropic three-dimensional walk, conditioning on its radial distance $R$ gives $\mathbb{E}|X|=\dfrac{\mathbb{E}R}{2}$, where $X$ is one coordinate. Each step contributes an independent uniform variable on $[-1,1]$ to $X$. Integrating the ten-fold uniform convolution gives $\mathbb{E}R=\dfrac{4}{2^{10}11!}\sum_{k=0}^4(-1)^k\binom{10}k(10-2k)^{11}=\dfrac{292223}{99792}\approx2.9283209$. Multiply this exact expression by 1000 for the expected total, approximately $\boxed{2928.32}$ units. A particular simulation need not equal its expectation.
