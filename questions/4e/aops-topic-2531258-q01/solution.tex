A polygon with $x$ sides has $\dfrac{x(x-3)}{2}$ diagonals, so $x^2-3x-270=0$, i.e. $(x-18)(x+15)=0$ and $x=18$. The repair probability is $\dfrac{18}{32}=\dfrac{9}{16}$.

The two events are disjoint. Multiplying along the chain,
\[P(\text{fails and gets fixed})=\dfrac25\cdot\dfrac57\cdot\dfrac{9}{10}\cdot\dfrac49\cdot\dfrac{9}{16}=\dfrac{9}{140}.\]
So the probability is
\[\dfrac35+\dfrac{9}{140}=\dfrac{84}{140}+\dfrac{9}{140}=\boxed{\dfrac{93}{140}}.\]
