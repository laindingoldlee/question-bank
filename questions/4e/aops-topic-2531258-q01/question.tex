My game disc works with probability $\dfrac35$. If it does not work, I remember to tell my mom with probability $\dfrac57$; if I tell her, she takes it to the game shop with probability $\dfrac{9}{10}$; if she takes it, the shop answers its phone with probability $\dfrac49$; and if the shop answers, it can fix the disc with probability $\dfrac{x}{32}$, where $x$ is the number of sides of a polygon with $135$ diagonals. What is the probability that either the disc works from the start, or it does not work and the shop eventually fixes it?
