The spending amounts describe disjoint transactions, so they correctly sum to $20+15+9+6=50$. The balances are snapshots, not disjoint portions of the original money: money retained after several purchases is counted repeatedly. In fact $30+15+6+0=15+2\cdot9+3\cdot6=51$. There is no reason for that sum to equal the initial amount; no extra dollar exists.
