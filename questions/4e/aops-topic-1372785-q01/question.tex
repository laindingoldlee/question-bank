A box contains $15$ slips of paper bearing the numbers $1$ to $15$, one number on each slip. Three slips are drawn at random, one at a time, without replacement. What is the probability that the three numbers drawn are consecutive integers drawn in increasing order? Express your answer as a common fraction.
