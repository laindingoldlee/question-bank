There are $15\cdot14\cdot13$ equally likely ordered draws. The favourable ones are $(k,k+1,k+2)$ for $k=1,2,\dots,13$, which is $13$ draws. So the probability is
\[
\dfrac{13}{15\cdot14\cdot13}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{210}}.
\]
