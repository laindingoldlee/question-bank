Roll a fair four-sided die to select a coin worth $ 1,5,10,25 $ cents respectively, then toss it fairly. The payoff is its value for heads and zero for tails. Repeat independently twice. Find the expected total payoff, rounded to the nearest cent. Choices: (A) $ 1 $, (B) $ 2 $, (C) $ 3 $, (D) $ 6 $, (E) $ 10 $.
