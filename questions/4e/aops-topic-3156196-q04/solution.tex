One play has expectation $\dfrac{1}{2}\dfrac{1+5+10+25}{4}=\dfrac{41}{8}$ cents. By linearity, two plays have expectation $\dfrac{41}{4}=10.25 $ cents, rounding to $\boxed{10}$ cents, choice E.
