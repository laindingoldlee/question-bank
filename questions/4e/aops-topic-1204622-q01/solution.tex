There are $\dfrac{5!}{2!}=60$ arrangements. Those that neither start nor end with E have both E's among the middle three positions: choose their positions in $\dbinom32=3$ ways and arrange S, W, T in the rest in $3!=6$ ways, $18$ arrangements. So the probability is $1-\dfrac{18}{60}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{10}}$.
