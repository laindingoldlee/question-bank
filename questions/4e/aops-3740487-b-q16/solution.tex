Scale $x$ to 1. The right triangle $PCD$ has legs 1 and $\dfrac{4}{3}$, hypotenuse $\dfrac{5}{3}$, and inradius $r_E=\dfrac{1+\dfrac{4}{3}-\dfrac{5}{3}}{2}=\dfrac{1}{3}$. Triangle $DCQ$ has legs 2 and $\dfrac{4}{3}$, hypotenuse $\dfrac{2\sqrt{13}}{3}$, and inradius $r_F=\dfrac{5-\sqrt{13}}{3}$. (For a right triangle, inradius equals half the sum of the legs minus the hypotenuse.) Thus $\dfrac{r_E}{r_F}=\dfrac{1}{5-\sqrt{13}}=\boxed{\dfrac{5+\sqrt{13}}{12}}$, choice D.

\end{asy}
