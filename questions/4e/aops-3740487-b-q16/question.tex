A line is a common tangent at the external contact point $D$ of circles with centres $P$ and $Q$ such that $P$ is on one side of the line and $Q$ is one the other, and they are touching. Let the radius of circle $P$ be $x$, and let the radius of circle $Q$ be $2x$. Let $C$ be a point on line $\overline{AB}$ such that the contact point $D$, is $\dfrac{4}{3}x$ units away from point $C$. Let $E$ and $F$ be inscribed circles in triangles $\triangle PCD$ and $\triangle DCQ$, respectively. Find the ratio of the radius of $E$ to the radius of $F$.

$\textbf{(A)}~\dfrac{4+3\sqrt{2}}{2}\qquad\textbf{(B)}~2\sqrt{5}\qquad\textbf{(C)}~\dfrac{2+4\sqrt{3}}{5}\qquad\textbf{(D)}~\dfrac{5+\sqrt{13}}{12}\qquad\textbf{(E)}~4\sqrt{5}\qquad$
