There are two bins, $X$ and $Y$. Initially bin $X$ holds $3$ balls and bin $Y$ is empty. At each move, one of the three balls is chosen uniformly at random and moved to the other bin. What is the expected number of moves until, for the first time, all three balls are in bin $Y$?
