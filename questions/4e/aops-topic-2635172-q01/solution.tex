Let $E_k$ be the expected number of further moves when $k$ balls are in $Y$; $E_3=0$. A move transfers a ball from $Y$ to $X$ with probability $\dfrac k3$ and from $X$ to $Y$ otherwise:
\[E_0=1+E_1,\qquad E_1=1+\dfrac13E_0+\dfrac23E_2,\qquad E_2=1+\dfrac23E_1.\]
Substituting the first into the second: $E_1=1+\dfrac13+\dfrac13E_1+\dfrac23E_2$, so $E_1=2+E_2$. Then $E_2=1+\dfrac23\left(2+E_2\right)$ gives $E_2=7$, $E_1=9$, and
\[E_0=\boxed{10}.\]
