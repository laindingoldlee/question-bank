The right-hand side is never negative, so a solution must satisfy $m-\dfrac{1}{2}\ge0$, that is $m\ge\dfrac{1}{2}$. For such $m$ we have $3m+1>0$, so $\left|3m+1\right|=3m+1$ and the equation becomes
\[
m-\dfrac{1}{2}=3m+1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad m=-\dfrac{3}{4} ,
\]
which contradicts $m\ge\dfrac{1}{2}$. (Directly: $3m+1-\left(m-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)=2m+\dfrac{3}{2}>0$ for $m\ge\dfrac{1}{2}$.) Hence the equation has $\boxed{\text{no real solution}}$.
