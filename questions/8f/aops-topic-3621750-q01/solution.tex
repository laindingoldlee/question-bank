For the lower bound, $\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{6}=\dfrac{1}{2}$, and $\dfrac{1}{5}+\dfrac{1}{7}+\dfrac{1}{8}>\dfrac{1}{5}+\dfrac{1}{8}+\dfrac{1}{8}=\dfrac{9}{20}$. Hence $S>2+\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{9}{20}=3.7>3.5$. For the upper bound, $\dfrac{1}{3}<\dfrac{1}{2}$, and each of $\dfrac{1}{5},\dfrac{1}{6},\dfrac{1}{7},\dfrac{1}{8}$ is less than $\dfrac{1}{4}$. Thus $S<2+\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{4}+1=4.25<4.5$. The nearest integer is $\boxed 4$.
