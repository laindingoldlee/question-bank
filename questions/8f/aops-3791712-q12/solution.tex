The tops have equal heights and project to the centroids of their base triangles. These centroids are each one third of the triangle altitude, $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{6}$, from the shared edge on opposite sides. Their separation, and hence the top-vertex distance, is $\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}}$.

\end{asy}
