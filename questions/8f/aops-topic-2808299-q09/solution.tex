Area ratios are preserved by affine transformations, so use $B=(0,0),A=(0,2),C=(2,0)$. Then $M=(1,0)$, $P=(\dfrac{1}{2},1)$, $N=(0,1)$. The lines are $y=1-x$ and $y=2x$, giving $G=(\dfrac{1}{3},\dfrac{2}{3})$. Hence $[NGB]=\dfrac{1}{6}$ whereas $[ABC]=2$, a ratio of $\dfrac{1}{12}$. The required area is $96\cdot\dfrac{1}{12}=\boxed8$.

\end{asy}
