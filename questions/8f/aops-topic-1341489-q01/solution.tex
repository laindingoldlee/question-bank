Since $\angle F=\angle G=90^\circ$, the side $FG$ is perpendicular to both $GK$ and $FL$, so the parallel sides (bases) are $GK$ and $FL$, and $\angle L=180^\circ-120^\circ=60^\circ$.

In triangle $FKL$ we have $KF=KL=a$, so it is isosceles with $\angle KFL=\angle KLF=60^\circ$; hence it is equilateral and $FL=a$. Also $\angle GKF=\angle KFL=60^\circ$ (alternate angles), so in the right triangle $FGK$ the side $GK=FK\cos60^\circ=\dfrac {a}{2}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s=0.2){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
real a=4, h=a*sqrt(3)/2;
pair G=(0,0), K=(a/2,0), F=(0,h), L=(a,h);
pair M1=(F+G)/2, M2=(K+L)/2;
draw(F--G--K--L--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(F--K,blue); draw(M1--M2,red+dashed);
draw(ra(G,K-G,F-G,0.3)); draw(ra(F,G-F,L-F,0.3));
label("$F$",F,NW); label("$G$",G,SW); label("$K$",K,SE); label("$L$",L,NE);
label("$a$",F+0.25*(K-F),NE,blue); label("$a$",K+0.3*(L-K),E);
label("$\dfrac{a}{2}$",(G+K)/2,S); label("$a$",(F+L)/2,N);
draw(arc(K,0.45,60,180)); label("$120^\circ$",K+0.8*dir(90),fontsize(8pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The midsegment is the average of the bases:
\[
\dfrac{GK+FL}{2}=\dfrac{\dfrac {a}{2}+a}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{3a}{4}}.
\]
