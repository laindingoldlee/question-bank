Two dice are fair four-sided dice. A third has probabilities $\dfrac{1}{6},\dfrac{1}{6},\dfrac{1}{6},\dfrac{1}{2}$ for 1,2,3,4. Choose one die uniformly and test it with three independent rolls. If those rolls agree, discard it and choose one of the other two uniformly, with no further test. Otherwise keep it. Roll the final die once independently. Find the probability of 4 as $\dfrac{m}{n}$ in lowest terms, and give $m+n$.
