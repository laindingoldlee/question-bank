A fair die is rejected with probability $4(\dfrac{1}{4})^3=\dfrac{1}{16}$; the biased die with probability $3(\dfrac{1}{6})^3+(\dfrac{1}{2})^3=\dfrac{5}{36}$. The chance the final die is biased is $\dfrac{1}{3}(1-\dfrac{5}{36})+\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot\dfrac{1}{16}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{133}{432}$. Hence the winning chance is $\dfrac{133}{432}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}+(1-\dfrac{133}{432})\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{565}{1728}$. Thus $\boxed{m+n=2293}$.
