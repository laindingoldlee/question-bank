A train from $A$ to $B$ had an accident after $1$ hour. It stopped for $30$ minutes and then continued at $\dfrac45$ of its usual rate, arriving $2$ hours late. Had the accident happened $80$ miles farther on, the train would have been only $1$ hour late. What is the usual rate of the train?
