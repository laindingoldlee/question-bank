Let the usual rate be $r$ and the distance $D$. Then
\[1+\dfrac12+\dfrac{D-r}{\dfrac45r}=\dfrac Dr+2,\qquad1+\dfrac{80}{r}+\dfrac12+\dfrac{D-r-80}{\dfrac45r}=\dfrac Dr+1.\]
Subtracting the second equation from the first: $-\dfrac{80}r+\dfrac{80}{\dfrac45r}=1$, i.e. $\dfrac{100-80}{r}=1$, so $r=\boxed{20}$ miles per hour. (Then $D=140$ miles.)
