The digit counts are nonincreasing as the base increases, so they must strictly decrease. If the base-$5$ count is one, then $n\le4$, which cannot supply four distinct counts. If it is two, then $n<25$, whereas a base-$3$ count of at least four would require $n\ge27$. If the base-$5$ count is three, the base-$3$ count must be at least five, requiring $n\ge81$. Larger base-$5$ counts require even larger $n$. At $n=81$, the counts in bases $2,3,4,5$ are $7,5,4,3$, respectively. Hence the minimum is $\boxed{81}$.
