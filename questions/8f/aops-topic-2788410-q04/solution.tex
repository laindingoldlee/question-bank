Let $X=AC\cap BE$ and $Y=AD\cap BE$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,3),B=(2.9,0.9),C=(1.6,-2.4),D=(-1.9,-2.3),E=(-2.8,1.0);draw(A--C--E--B--D--cycle);
pair X=extension(A,C,B,E),Y=extension(A,D,B,E);dot(X);dot(Y);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,E);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,SW);label("$E$",E,W);label("$X$",X,NE);label("$Y$",Y,NW);
\end{asy}

In triangle $XCE$ (with $X$ on $AC$ and on $BE$), the angle $\angle AXY$ is the exterior angle at $X$, so $\angle AXY=\angle C+\angle E$ (the tip angles at $C$ and $E$). Similarly, in triangle $YBD$, $\angle AYX=\angle B+\angle D$. In triangle $AXY$:
\[\angle A+\angle AXY+\angle AYX=\angle A+\angle B+\angle C+\angle D+\angle E=180^\circ.\ \blacksquare\]
