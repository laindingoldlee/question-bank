The losing dates are those with day $d=m+19$, where $m$ is the month number. All are valid, ending with December 31. Any move from such a date breaks this equality. Conversely, if $d<m+19$, increase the day to $m+19$; if $d>m+19$, increase the month to $d-19$, a later month at most December, whose day $d$ is valid. Thus every nonterminal position off this list has a move onto it, proving the losing-position description by backwards induction. From January 1, choose $\boxed{\text{January 20}}$ and thereafter return to this list.
