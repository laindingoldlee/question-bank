The ten even numbers form such a set, since every sum of two distinct positive even numbers is an even integer greater than $2$. For the upper bound partition the set into the pair $\{1,2\}$ and the nine pairs
\[
\{3,20\},\{4,19\},\ldots,\{11,12\}.
\]
The first pair has prime sum $3$, and each other pair has prime sum $23$. An admissible set contains at most one element from each pair, hence at most ten. The answer is $\boxed{10}$.
