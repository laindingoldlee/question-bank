Let $N=(n+2)!$. The numbers $N+2,N+3,\dots,N+(n+2)$ are $n+1$ consecutive integers, and $N+k$ is divisible by $k$ (since $k\le n+2$ divides $N$) and larger than $k$, for $2\le k\le n+2$. So all are composite. $\blacksquare$
