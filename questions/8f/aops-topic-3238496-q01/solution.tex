Count multiples of successive powers of 2: $\lfloor\dfrac{75}{2}\rfloor+\lfloor\dfrac{75}{4}\rfloor+\lfloor\dfrac{75}{8}\rfloor+\lfloor\dfrac{75}{16}\rfloor+\lfloor\dfrac{75}{32}\rfloor+\lfloor\dfrac{75}{64}\rfloor=37+18+9+4+2+1=\boxed{71}$. Each factor of 2 is counted once.
