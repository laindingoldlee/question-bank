An integer is a difference of two squares exactly when it is odd or divisible by 4. Necessity follows from the square residues 0 and 1 modulo 4. For sufficiency, an odd integer $2k+1=(k+1)^2-k^2$, while $4k=(k+1)^2-(k-1)^2$ for $k\ge1$.

Thus the only forbidden products are 2 modulo 4. Such a product requires one of the 50 odd integers and one of the 25 integers congruent to 2 modulo 4. There are $50\cdot25=1250$ forbidden unordered pairs out of $\binom{100}{2}=4950$. The desired probability is
\[1-\dfrac{1250}{4950}=\boxed{\dfrac{74}{99}}.\]
