Work upwards from values above $5$:
\[
f(6)=4,\qquad f(5)=f(f(8))=f(6)=4,\qquad f(4)=f(f(7))=f(5)=4,
\]
and then
\[
f(1)=f(f(4))=f(4)=\boxed{4}.
\]
(Similarly $f(3)=f(f(6))=f(4)=4$ and $f(2)=f(f(5))=f(4)=4$: in fact $f(x)=4$ for every integer $x\le5$, as one checks by downward induction in steps of $3$.)
