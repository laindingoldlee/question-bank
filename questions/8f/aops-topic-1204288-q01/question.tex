Ten distinct cities are arranged on a circle, and every pair of cities is joined by a straight road. A tourist wants to visit every city exactly once, travelling along roads from city to city. He may start at any city, but his route may not cross itself. How many different routes are possible? (A route is the ordered list of cities in the order visited.)
