A man has to take a fox, a goose and a sack of corn across a stream. His boat can carry only himself and one of the three at a time. He cannot leave the fox alone with the goose, or the goose alone with the corn (when he is with them, nothing happens). Show how he can get everything across, and prove that he needs at least $7$ crossings.
