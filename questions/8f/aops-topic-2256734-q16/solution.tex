$\dfrac{1}{2020}=\dfrac{5}{10100}=\dfrac{1}{100}\cdot\dfrac{5}{101}$, and $\dfrac{5}{101}=5\cdot0.\overline{0099}=0.\overline{0495}$ (since $5\cdot99=495$). Dividing by $100$ shifts the digits two places:
\[\dfrac{1}{2020}=0.00\,0495\,0495\,0495\ldots\]
So for $k\ge3$ the $k$th digit of $\dfrac1{2020}$ is the $(k-2)$th digit of $0.\overline{0495}$. For $k=2020$ this is the $2018$th digit; since $2018=4\cdot504+2$, it is the second digit of the block $0495$, namely $\boxed{\textbf{(C) }4}$.
