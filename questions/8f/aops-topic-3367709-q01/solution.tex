The positions of the four A-books can have the patterns $A\_A\_A\_A\_$, $A\_A\_A\_\_A$, $A\_A\_\_A\_A$, $A\_\_A\_A\_A$, or $\_A\_A\_A\_A$. In the first and last patterns all empty positions are separated, so any of four positions can hold C, giving $8$ fillings. Each of the other three patterns has one pair of adjacent empty positions; these must hold B and C in either order, while the remaining empty positions hold B. This gives $3\cdot2=6$. The total is $\boxed{14}$.
