Three children $A$, $B$, $C$ each start with $500$ candies and trade in rounds. In every round, each child gives one candy to one of the other two: $A$ gives it to $B$ with probability $\dfrac23$ and to $C$ with probability $\dfrac13$, while $B$ and $C$ each choose one of the other two children with probability $\dfrac12$ each; all choices are independent. Find the expected number of candies $A$ has after $917$ rounds. (Assume a child can always give; in fact nobody can run out during the first $500$ rounds, and a negative count is allowed afterwards.)
