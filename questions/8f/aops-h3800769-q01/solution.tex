Using bases along $DC$, $[ADC]=6+10=16$ and $DE:EC=6:10$, so $\dfrac{DE}{DC}=\dfrac{3}{8}$. Similarly $[BDC]=17+7=24$ and $\dfrac{DF}{DC}=\dfrac{17}{24}$. Thus $\dfrac{EF}{DC}=\dfrac{17}{24}-\dfrac{3}{8}=\dfrac{1}{3}$. Triangles $BEF,BDC$ share the altitude from $B$, yielding $[BEF]=\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot24=\boxed8$.
