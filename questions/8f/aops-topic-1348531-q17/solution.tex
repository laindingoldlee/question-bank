Jamie's speed is $50$ meters per minute, so she arrives after $240$ minutes, at 4:00 P.M. Bob's speed is $60$ meters per minute, so he arrives after $200$ minutes, at 4:50 P.M. By 4:50 Jamie has eaten $2\cdot50=100$ scoops. After that they eat $5$ scoops per minute together, and the remaining $50$ scoops take $10$ minutes. They reach $150$ scoops at $\boxed{\text{5:00 P.M.}}$
