Jamie the bird flies along a straight $1000$-meter track in $20$ minutes, and Bob the cheetah runs along a straight $1500$-meter track in $25$ minutes; both always move at these constant speeds. At 12:00 P.M.\ Jamie sets off along a $12000$-meter path to an ice-cream shop, and at 1:30 P.M.\ Bob sets off along a $12000$-meter path to the same shop. On arrival, Jamie eats $2$ scoops per minute and Bob eats $3$ scoops per minute, without stopping. At what time will they have eaten $150$ scoops in total?
