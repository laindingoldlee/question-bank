Let Nora be $n$ years old now; then Mary is $n+4$. The condition says $n-3=\dfrac{n+4}{2}$, so $2n-6=n+4$ and $n=10$. Mary is $\boxed{14}$ years old. (Check: three years ago Nora was $7$, half of $14$.)
