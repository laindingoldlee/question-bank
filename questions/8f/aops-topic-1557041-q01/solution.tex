Factor: $702=2\cdot3^3\cdot13$. A perfect square has every prime to an even power, so $K$ must contain at least $2^1\cdot3^1\cdot13^1$. The smallest choice is $K=2\cdot3\cdot13=\boxed{78}$, giving $702\cdot78=54756=234^2$.
