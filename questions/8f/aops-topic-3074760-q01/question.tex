For $2\le n\le999$, define $w(n)$ to be the sum of the prime factors of $n$, counted with multiplicity, plus $e\sqrt[e]{n}$ for every integer $e>1$ for which $n$ is an exact $e$th power, plus $\left\lceil\dfrac{n}{3}\right\rceil$ if $n$ is triangular. Set $w(0)=0,w(1)=1$. What is the maximum sum of $w(n)$ over fifteen distinct numbers chosen from $0,\ldots,999$?
