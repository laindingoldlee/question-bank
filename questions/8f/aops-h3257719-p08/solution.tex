Center the cube at the origin, with $|x|,|y|,|z|\le\dfrac{1}{2}$. The eight cuts retain $\pm x\pm y\pm z\le\dfrac{1}{2}$, equivalent to $|x|+|y|+|z|\le\dfrac{1}{2}$. This is an octahedron with vertices at the face centers. Each of its eight coordinate-octant pieces is a right tetrahedron with perpendicular edges $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and volume $\dfrac{1}{6}(\dfrac{1}{2})^3=\dfrac{1}{48}$. Therefore the total is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{6}}$.

\end{asy}
