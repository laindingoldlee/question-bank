The condition is $\lceil\log_{10}(8d+1)\rceil\le3$, equivalent to $\log_{10}(8d+1)\le3$. Thus $8d+1\le1000$, or $d\le\dfrac{999}{8}=124.875$. Since $d$ is integral, the largest is $\boxed{124}$.
