Since $AB^2+BC^2=25+144=169=CA^2$, the triangle has a right angle at $B$, with hypotenuse $CA=13$. The side opposite $C$ is $AB=5$, so
\[\sin C=\dfrac{AB}{CA}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{13}}.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0),C=(12,0),A=(0,5);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.8));draw((0,0.7)--(0.7,0.7)--(0.7,0));
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);
label("$5$",(A+B)/2,W);label("$12$",(B+C)/2,S);label("$13$",(A+C)/2,NE);
\end{asy}
