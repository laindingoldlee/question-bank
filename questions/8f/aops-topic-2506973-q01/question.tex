Triangle $ABC$ has $AB=4\sqrt5$, $BC=2\sqrt5$ and $AC=10$. The internal bisector of $\angle ABC$ meets $AC$ at $D$. Side $AC$ is extended beyond $C$ to a point $E$ such that $CE=BD$. The area of triangle $BCE$ can be written as $\dfrac{a\sqrt b}{c}$, where $a$, $b$, $c$ are positive integers, $a$ and $c$ are relatively prime, and $b$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. Find $a+b+c$.
