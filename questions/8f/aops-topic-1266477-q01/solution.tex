We have $\sqrt[4]{16}=2$ and $\sqrt[6]{36}=\sqrt[6]{6^2}=\sqrt[3]{6}$. Hence
\[\sqrt[3]{9}\cdot2\cdot\sqrt[3]{6}=2\sqrt[3]{54}=2\sqrt[3]{27\cdot2}=6\sqrt[3]{2}.\]
So $a=6$, $b=3$, $c=2$ and $a+b+c=\boxed{11}$.
