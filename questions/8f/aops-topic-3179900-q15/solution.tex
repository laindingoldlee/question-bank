At 1:00 the hour hand leads by 30 degrees. The minute hand gains $6-\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{11}{2}$ degrees per minute. The second coincidence occurs after gaining $30+360=390$ degrees, so the time is $\dfrac{780}{11}$ minutes and $\boxed{a+b=791}$.
