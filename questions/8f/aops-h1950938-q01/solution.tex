The four tails create five gaps of heads, of sizes $x_1,\ldots,x_5\ge0$ with sum $12$. All $\binom{16}{4}=1820$ choices of tail positions are equally likely. A valid gap size is at most $4$. By inclusion--exclusion the valid count is $\binom{16}{4}-5\binom{11}{4}+\binom52\binom64=1820-1650+150=320$. Three gaps cannot each contain at least five heads, so there are no further terms. The probability is $\dfrac{320}{1820}=\boxed{\dfrac{16}{91}}$.
