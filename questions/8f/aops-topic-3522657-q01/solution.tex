If $T$ is the total number of tails, its probability-generating polynomial is\[E(t^T)=\dfrac{(8+t)^{180}(1+2t)^{300}(1+t)^{1545}}{9^{180}3^{300}2^{1545}}.\]Independence justifies multiplying the one-coin polynomials. Expanding by the binomial theorem and summing the required coefficients gives the exact probability\[\boxed{\dfrac{\displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{180}\sum_{j=0}^{300}\binom{180}{i}8^{180-i}\binom{300}{j}2^j\sum_{k=\max(0,1300-i-j)}^{1545}\binom{1545}{k}}{9^{180}3^{300}2^{1545}}}.\]An inner sum whose lower endpoint exceeds its upper endpoint is zero. This finite rational expression specifies the probability exactly without requiring an enormous decimal expansion.
