Circles centered at $O,P$ have radii $2,3$ and touch externally at $A$. An equilateral triangle $ABC$ is inscribed in the first circle. Extend $AB$ past $B$ to $D$ with $BD=\sqrt3$. A tangent from $D$ touches the second circle at $F$. Find $DF^2$.
