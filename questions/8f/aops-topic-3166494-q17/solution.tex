Use $A=(0,0)$, $O=(-2,0)$, $P=(3,0)$, and $B=(-3,\sqrt3)$. Since $AB=2\sqrt3$, the extension gives $D=(-\dfrac{9}{2},\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2})$. Thus $DP^2=(\dfrac{15}{2})^2+(\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2})^2=63$. The tangent-radius right triangle gives $DF^2=DP^2-3^2=\boxed{54}$.

\end{asy}
