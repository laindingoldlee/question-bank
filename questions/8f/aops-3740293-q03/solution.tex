The answer is $\boxed{287}=17\cdot19-17-19$. First, if $17a+19b=287$, reducing modulo 17 gives $2b\equiv15$, hence $b\equiv16\pmod{17}$. Then $19b\ge304>287$, impossible. Next let $N\ge288$. As 19 is invertible modulo 17, choose $b\in\{0,\ldots,16\}$ so that $N-19b$ is divisible by 17. We have $N-19b\ge288-304=-16$. An integer multiple of 17 at least $-16$ is nonnegative, so $a=\dfrac{N-19b}{17}\ge0$. Thus every larger integer is representable.
