Three switches control exactly one bulb in another room. The bulb is initially off and cool, becomes detectably warm after ten minutes on, and remains warm briefly after being switched off. You may operate the switches freely, then enter the bulb room once and inspect its light and warmth. How can you identify the controlling switch?
