Turn switch 1 on for ten minutes, then turn it off and turn switch 2 on. Immediately inspect the bulb. If lit, switch 2 controls it. If unlit but warm, switch 1 controls it. If unlit and cool, switch 3 controls it. These three mutually exclusive observations identify all possibilities.
