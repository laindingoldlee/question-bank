Let
\[A=\left\{x\in\mathbb N:\ x+3=5^a\text{ for some }a\in\mathbb N\right\},\qquad B=\left\{x\in\mathbb N:\ 5x+1=13^b\text{ for some }b\in\mathbb N\right\}.\]
Find $A\cap B$.
