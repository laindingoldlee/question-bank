Because AE=2s=30, s=15. The unit-side pairs in this equilateral chain are AB,AC,BC,BD,CD,CE,DE, seven pairs. The other three pairs AD,BE,AE have lengths $s\sqrt3,s\sqrt3,2s$, respectively. Thus seven of the $\binom52=10$ pairs qualify, and the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{7}{10}}$.
