For $n=2023$, the difference is $\dfrac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6}-n^2=\dfrac{n(n-1)(2n-1)}{6}$. Factor $2023=7\cdot17^2$, $2022=2\cdot3\cdot337$, and $4045=5\cdot809$. Cancelling $6$ leaves $N=7\cdot17^2\cdot337\cdot5\cdot809$. Both $337$ and $809$ are prime, as trial division by primes up to their square roots verifies. The largest prime factor is $\boxed{809}$.
