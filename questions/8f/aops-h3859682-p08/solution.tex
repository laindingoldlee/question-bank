Set $B=(0,0),C=(14,0),A=(\dfrac{17}{4},\dfrac{3\sqrt{55}}{4}),D=(10,0)$. Then $AD=8$, so the ray condition allows two points: $E=D+\varepsilon\dfrac{5}{8}(D-A)$, with $\varepsilon=1$ beyond $D$ or $-1$ between $A,D$. The circumcenters are $O=(7,-\dfrac{7}{\sqrt{55}})$, $P=(5,\dfrac{13}{3\sqrt{55}})$, and $Q=(12,\dfrac{46-80\varepsilon}{3\sqrt{55}})$, found by equal-distance equations. Therefore $OD^2+PQ^2=\dfrac{36640-5280\varepsilon}{495}$. For $\varepsilon=1$ its square root is $\dfrac{56\sqrt{22}}{33}$, giving 111. For $\varepsilon=-1$ it is $\dfrac{8\sqrt{1441}}{33}$, giving 1482. Thus the stated ray condition permits $\boxed{111\text{ or }1482}$.
