Let the album have $p$ pages and let Mark have $N$ stamps. The conditions are
\[N>20p,\qquad N\le23(p-1),\qquad N+21p=500.\]
Substituting $N=500-21p$: the first gives $41p<500$, so $p\le12$; the second gives $500-21p\le23p-23$, i.e. $44p\ge523$, so $p\ge12$ (as $\dfrac{523}{44}>11$). Hence $p=12$ and $N=500-252=248$. Check: $248>240=20\cdot12$, and $248\le253=23\cdot11$. The album has $\boxed{12}$ pages.
