Mark is putting his stamps into an album. If he puts $20$ stamps on each page, the album does not have enough pages for all his stamps. If he puts $23$ stamps on each page, at least one page is left completely empty. If someone gave Mark an identical album already holding $21$ stamps on every page, Mark would have $500$ stamps in total. How many pages does the album have?
