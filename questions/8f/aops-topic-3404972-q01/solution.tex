Complete a square and subtract: $x^4+x^2+1=(x^2+1)^2-x^2=\boxed{(x^2+x+1)(x^2-x+1)}$. Both quadratic discriminants are $1-4=-3$, so neither has a real root, yet their product is reducible. Real roots detect linear factors; reducibility may instead involve quadratic factors.
