Let $O$ be the centre. The inscribed angle $\angle BAC=40^\circ$ subtends arc $BC$ (the arc not containing $A$), so the central angle is $\angle BOC=80^\circ$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s=0.2){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
pair O=(0,0);
pair A=dir(100),B=dir(230),C=dir(310);
pair D=dir(270)/Cos(40);
draw(circle(O,1));
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(B--D--C,blue+linewidth(0.9));
draw(O--B,gray+dashed); draw(O--C,gray+dashed);
draw(ra(B,O-B,D-B,0.1),gray); draw(ra(C,O-C,D-C,0.1),gray);
draw(arc(A,0.45,degrees(B-A),degrees(C-A)),red); label("$40^\circ$",A+(0.06,-0.6),red+fontsize(8pt));
dot(A);dot(B);dot(C);dot(D);dot(O);
label("$A$",A,N); label("$B$",B,W); label("$C$",C,E); label("$D$",D,S); label("$O$",O,N);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

A tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of tangency, so $\angle OBD=\angle OCD=90^\circ$. The angles of quadrilateral $OBDC$ add up to $360^\circ$:
\[
\angle BDC=360^\circ-90^\circ-90^\circ-80^\circ=\boxed{100^\circ}.
\]
