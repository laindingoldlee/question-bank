There are $4\cdot3=12$ equally likely ordered pairs. The numbers are $12,13,17,21,23,27,31,32,37,71,72,73$, and the primes among them are $13,17,23,31,37,71,73$: seven of them. The probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{7}{12}}$.
