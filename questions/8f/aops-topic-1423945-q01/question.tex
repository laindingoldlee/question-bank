Last season a middle school football team scored $36$ times and earned $212$ points. They scored only field goals worth $3$ points and touchdowns worth $7$ points (with all extra points). How many field goals did they score? A student assumes all $36$ scores were touchdowns, gets $7\cdot36=252$, and answers $252-212=40$. Find the error and the correct answer.
