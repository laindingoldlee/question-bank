Factor both cubics. By grouping,
\[x^3+2x^2-3x-6=x^2(x+2)-3(x+2)=(x+2)(x^2-3),\]
with real roots $-2,\sqrt3,-\sqrt3$. For the second, $x=1$ is a root ($6-3-2-1=0$):
\[6x^3-3x^2-2x-1=(x-1)(6x^2+3x+1),\]
and $6x^2+3x+1$ has discriminant $9-24<0$, so the only real root is $1$. The real numbers satisfying either equation are $-2,\sqrt3,-\sqrt3,1$ (all different), with product
\[(-2)\cdot\sqrt3\cdot\left(-\sqrt3\right)\cdot1=\boxed{6}.\]
(Caution: multiplying the products of roots from Vieta's formulas, $6\cdot\dfrac{1}{6}=1$, would give the product over all complex roots, which is not what is asked.)
