Multiply term by term:
\[
(-4\sqrt{2x})(-2\sqrt2)=8\sqrt{4x}=16\sqrt x,\qquad (-4\sqrt{2x})(-4)=16\sqrt{2x},
\]
\[
2(-2\sqrt2)=-4\sqrt2,\qquad 2(-4)=-8 .
\]
Hence the product is
\[
\boxed{16\sqrt x+16\sqrt{2x}-4\sqrt2-8}.
\]
