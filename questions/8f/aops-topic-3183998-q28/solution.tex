$333\equiv3\pmod{33}$. Powers of $3$ modulo $33$ have period five after the first term, since $3^1,3^2,3^3,3^4,3^5,3^6$ have remainders $3,9,27,15,12,3$. Since $333\equiv3\pmod5$, the remainder is $\boxed{27}$.
