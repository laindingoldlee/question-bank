Write the dividend as $x^3+0x^2+2x-3$. Subtract $x^2(x-1)$ to leave $x^2+2x-3$; subtract $x(x-1)$ to leave $3x-3$; subtract $3(x-1)$ to leave zero. Thus the quotient is $\boxed{x^2+x+3}$ and the remainder is $\boxed0$.
