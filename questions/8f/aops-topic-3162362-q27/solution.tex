The arc $ BC $ not containing $ E $ has measure 34 degrees. The arcs intercepted by angles $ CDE $ and $ EAB $ together comprise the entire circle plus that arc $ BC $: the first is $ CBAE $, the second $ EDCB $. Thus the angle sum is $\dfrac{360+34}{2}=\boxed{197^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=dir(170),B=dir(220),C=dir(254),D=dir(315),Ep=dir(40);draw(circle((0,0),1));draw(A--B--C--D--Ep--cycle);draw(Ep--B^^Ep--C,dashed);label("$A$",A,W);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,S);label("$D$",D,SE);label("$E$",Ep,NE);
\end{asy}
