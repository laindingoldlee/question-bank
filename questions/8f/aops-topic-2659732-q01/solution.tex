We show that $\varphi(n)$ is even for every $n\ge3$; an odd prime $p$ is then never a value.

If $n$ has an odd prime factor $q$, write $n=q^em$ with $q\nmid m$. Then $\varphi(n)=\varphi\left(q^e\right)\varphi(m)=q^{e-1}(q-1)\varphi(m)$, which is divisible by the even number $q-1$.

Otherwise $n=2^k$, and $n\ge3$ forces $k\ge2$, so $\varphi(n)=2^{k-1}$ is even.

Hence $\varphi(n)$ is even for $n\ge3$ and cannot equal an odd prime. $\blacksquare$
