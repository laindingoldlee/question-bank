Since $1000000=2^6 5^6$, each divisor is $2^a 5^b$ with $0\le a,b\le 6$. If both exponents are positive, the divisor is a multiple of 10 and ends in zero. The remaining candidates are $1,2,4,8,16,32,64$ and $5,25,125,625,3125,15625$. None contains zero, so the answer is $\boxed{13}$.
