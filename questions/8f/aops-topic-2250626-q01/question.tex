For numbers $a$ and $b$, a \emph{move that adds $(a,b)$} takes a particle from a point $(c,d)$ to the point $(c+a,d+b)$. A particle moves by these rules: if the move that brought it to its current position added $(p,q)$, then its next move adds either $(p-1,q)$ or $(p,q-1)$, and neither coordinate of an added vector may ever be negative. Particle $A$ starts at $(0,0)$ and its first move takes it to $(3,4)$. It keeps moving by the rules until no further move is possible, and at that moment it is at a point $(r,r)$. What is $r$?
