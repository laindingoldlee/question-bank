Work in cents with $n$ nickels, $d$ dimes, $q$ quarters and $h$ half-dollars, all at most $4$: $5n+10d+25q+50h=100$. Since $5n+10d\le60$, we organise by $h$ and $q$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $h=2$: nothing else, $1$ way.
\item $h=1$: $q=2$ gives $1$ way; $q=1$ needs $5n+10d=25$, i.e. $(n,d)=(1,2),(3,1)$; $q=0$ needs $5n+10d=50$, i.e. $(n,d)=(2,4),(4,3)$. Total $5$.
\item $h=0$: $q=4$ gives $1$ way; $q=3$ gives $(1,2),(3,1)$; $q=2$ gives $(2,4),(4,3)$; $q\le1$ would need $5n+10d\ge75>60$. Total $5$.
\end{itemize}
Altogether $1+5+5=\boxed{11}$ ways.
