Write the number as $100a+10b+c$, with $a,c\ne0$. Modulo $7$, the two divisibility conditions are $2a+3b+c\equiv0$ and $a+3b+2c\equiv0$, hence $a\equiv c\pmod7$. Since the digits are distinct, the unordered endpoint pair must be $\{1,8\}$ or $\{2,9\}$. For $(a,c)=(1,8)$ or its reversal, the first congruence gives $b=6$; for endpoints $2,9$, it gives $b=5$. The numbers are $168,861,259,952$, with sum $\boxed{2240}$.
