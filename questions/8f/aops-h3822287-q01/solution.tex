Bringing everything to the left and factoring gives
\[
x^4+x^3-2x^2-3ax-a^2=(x^2+2x+a)(x^2-x-a).
\]
Thus the roots are
\[
\boxed{-1\pm\sqrt{1-a},\qquad \dfrac{1\pm\sqrt{1+4a}}{2}}.
\]
Both quadratic discriminants are positive because $a<1$ and $a>\dfrac{3}{4}$. If the quadratics shared a root, subtracting their equations would give $3x+2a=0$. Substitution into the first would then give $\dfrac{a(4a-3)}{9}=0$, impossible in the given interval. Hence all four real roots are distinct.
