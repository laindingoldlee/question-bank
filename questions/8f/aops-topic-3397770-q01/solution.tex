The total $29$ cannot occur. It is odd, so it would require one $15$-point play, leaving $14$; neither $14$, $14-6$, nor $14-12$ is a nonnegative multiple of $10$. On the other hand, $30=15+15$, $31=15+10+6$, $32=10+10+6+6$, $33=15+6+6+6$, $34=10+6+6+6+6$, and $35=15+10+10$. Every integer at least $30$ is one of these six totals plus a nonnegative multiple of $6$. Thus the greatest impossible total is $\boxed{29}$.
