\textbf{(a)} $34\times46=34\times40+34\times6=1360+204=1564$.

\textbf{(b)} The factors are $40-6$ and $40+6$, so
\[34\times46=(40-6)(40+6)=40^2-6^2=1600-36=1564 .\]
Either way, $34\times46=\boxed{1564}$. Method (b) works quickly whenever the two factors are equally far from a round number.
