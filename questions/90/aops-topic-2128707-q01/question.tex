Let $x=28$ and $g=5$.
\begin{itemize}
\item The largest altitude of a triangle with side lengths $(x-1)-4$, $x-4$ and $(x+1)-4$ can be written as $\dfrac {a}{b}\sqrt c$, where $a,b,c$ are positive integers, $\gcd(a,b)=1$ and $c$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. Let $y=a+b+c$.
\item The largest altitude of a triangle with side lengths $(x-1)+4$, $x+4$ and $(x+1)+4$ can be written as $\dfrac {d}{e}\sqrt f$ in the same way. Let $z=d+e+f$.
\end{itemize}
Find $h$ if $(g+1)!-(y+z)=g!+h$.
