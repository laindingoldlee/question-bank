For a field trip, if buses with $45$ seats are rented, $15$ students have no seat. If buses with $60$ seats are rented, one bus fewer is needed and all the seats are exactly filled. A $45$-seat bus costs $\$250$ and a $60$-seat bus costs $\$300$. If every student must be seated, which type of bus is cheaper to rent, how many such buses are needed, and how much money is saved by the better choice?
