The areas are $1,k^2,k^4,\ldots$. Since $0<k<1$, their geometric series converges and
\[
\dfrac{1}{1-k^2}=2.
\]
Thus $k^2=\dfrac{1}{2}$ and the positive value is $\boxed{k=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}}$. In particular, the second square has side $k<1$, as required.

The perimeters are $4,4k,4k^2,\ldots$, whose sum is
\[
\dfrac{4}{1-k}=\dfrac{4}{1-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}}
=\boxed{8+4\sqrt2}.
\]
