Suppose the minimum value $m$ is negative. It occurs at an interior index $j$, since both endpoints are nonnegative. The inequality centered at $j$ gives $a_{j-1}+a_{j+1}\le2m$. Both neighbours are at least $m$, so both equal $m$. Applying the same argument repeatedly propagates the minimum to an endpoint, contradicting its nonnegativity. Therefore no negative minimum exists.
