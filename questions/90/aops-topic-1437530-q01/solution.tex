For $r\ne3,-5$, cross-multiply and multiply out the binomials:
\[
(r+9)(r+5)=(r-2)(r-3)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad r^2+14r+45=r^2-5r+6 .
\]
The $r^2$ terms cancel: $19r=-39$, so $r=\boxed{-\dfrac{39}{19}}$, which is allowed.
