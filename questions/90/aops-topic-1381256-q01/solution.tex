Write the number as $10a+7$, where $a$ is the two-digit number formed by its first two digits. Moving the $7$ to the front gives $700+a$. So
\[700+a=10a+7+324\iff9a=369\iff a=41.\]
The number is $\boxed{417}$. (Check: $741-417=324$.)
