Each exponent in the product is a sum of distinct powers of two, so binary representation selects its contributing terms uniquely. Since
\[2025=2047-22=(1+2+4+\cdots+1024)-(2+4+16),\]
choose constant terms precisely in the factors of degrees 2, 4, and 16. They are $3,5,9$, so $N=3\cdot5\cdot9=135$. The remainder is $\boxed{35}$.
