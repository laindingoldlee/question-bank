Train A and train B leave the same station on side-by-side tracks in the same direction. Train A leaves at 12:28 p.m. and train B leaves $42$ minutes later. Train A travels at a constant $45$ miles per hour and train B at a constant $55$ miles per hour. At what time does train B catch up with train A, and how far from the station are they then?
