You and a bus each arrive at a bus stop at a random time between 12:00 p.m.\ and 1:00 p.m., independently and uniformly. When the bus arrives it waits $5$ minutes and then leaves. When you arrive, you wait up to $20$ minutes and then leave if the bus has not come. (The waiting times may run past 1:00.) What is the probability that you catch the bus?
