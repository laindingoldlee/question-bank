Since $DA=DB$, $D$ lies on the perpendicular bisector of $AB$, so $DC$ is the height of the triangle on base $AB=2r$. Then $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot2r\cdot DC=\pi r^2$, so $DC=\boxed{\pi r}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(2.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw(unitcircle); pair A=(-1,0),B=(1,0),D=(0,pi);
draw(A--B--D--cycle,blue); draw((0,0)--D,dashed); dot((0,0)); label("$C$",(0,0),S); label("$A$",A,W); label("$B$",B,E); label("$D$",D,N);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
