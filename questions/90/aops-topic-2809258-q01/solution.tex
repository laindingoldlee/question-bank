Factor: $x^2y+xy^2+x+y=(x+y)(xy+1)=10(x+y)=100$, so $x+y=10$. Then $x^2+y^2=(x+y)^2-2xy=100-18=\boxed{82}$ (indeed $\{x,y\}=\{1,9\}$).

The attempted solution squared $x+y=10$ as $x^2+xy+y^2=100$; the correct expansion is $(x+y)^2=x^2+2xy+y^2$.
