We know $6\equiv36\pmod{10}$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Is it valid to divide by $3$ and conclude $2\equiv12\pmod{10}$? Why?
\item[(b)] Is it valid to divide $2\equiv12\pmod{10}$ by $2$ and conclude $1\equiv6\pmod{10}$?
\end{enumerate}
