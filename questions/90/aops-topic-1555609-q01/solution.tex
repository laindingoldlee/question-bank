From floor $1$ to floor $4$ he climbs $3$ flights of stairs, not $4$, so each flight takes $\dfrac{60}{3}=20$ seconds. From floor $1$ to floor $16$ he climbs $15$ flights, which take $15\cdot20=300$ seconds $=5$ minutes. The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(C)}\ 5}$. (The tempting answer $4$ comes from dividing $16$ by $4$, which counts floors instead of flights.)
