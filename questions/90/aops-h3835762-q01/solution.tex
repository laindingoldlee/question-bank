The constraint gives $x>0$, $y=\dfrac{36}{x}-x$, and $x\le6$. Therefore the objective equals $25x+\dfrac{1296}{x}$. Subtract 366 and factor:
\[25x+\dfrac{1296}{x}-366=\dfrac{(x-6)(25x-216)}{x}.\]
For $0<x\le6$, both numerator factors are nonpositive, so the difference is nonnegative. Equality holds at $x=6,y=0$. The minimum is $\boxed{366}$.
