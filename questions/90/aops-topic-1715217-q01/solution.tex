Square twice:
\[
x^2+\dfrac{1}{x^2}=\left(x+\dfrac{1}{x}\right)^2-2=441-2=439,
\]
\[
x^4+\dfrac{1}{x^4}=\left(x^2+\dfrac{1}{x^2}\right)^2-2=439^2-2=192721-2=\boxed{192719}.
\]
