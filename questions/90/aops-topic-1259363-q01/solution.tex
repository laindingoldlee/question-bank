Multiply by $2$ and add $1$ (Simon's favourite factoring trick):
\[4xy+2x+2y+1=105\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad(2x+1)(2y+1)=105.\]
Every divisor of $105=3\cdot5\cdot7$ is odd, so each factorisation $105=d\cdot\dfrac{105}{d}$ with $d$ a positive or negative divisor gives integers $x=\dfrac{d-1}{2}$, $y=\dfrac{\dfrac{105}{d}-1}{2}$. There are $8$ positive and $8$ negative divisors, so $\boxed{16}$ ordered pairs.
