The expression is symmetric, so order the numbers as $p\le q\le r$ (a rearrangement of $x,y,z$). Then
\[
|x-y|+|y-z|+|z-x|=(q-p)+(r-q)+(r-p)=2(r-p).
\]
So $r-p=10$. The largest of the three differences is the one between the largest and the smallest number, $r-p$, since the others are $q-p\le r-p$ and $r-q\le r-p$. Hence the largest difference is always exactly $\boxed{10}$.
