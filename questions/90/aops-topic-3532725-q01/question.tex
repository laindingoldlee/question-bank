Let $n\ge2$, and let $a,b,c,d$ be integers with $\gcd(c,n)=\gcd(d,n)=1$. Prove $ac^{-1}+bd^{-1}\equiv(ad+bc)(cd)^{-1}\pmod n$, where inverses are modulo $n$.
