The product $cd$ is invertible, and $(cd)^{-1}\equiv c^{-1}d^{-1}$. Consequently $(ad+bc)c^{-1}d^{-1}\equiv a(dd^{-1})c^{-1}+b(cc^{-1})d^{-1}\equiv ac^{-1}+bd^{-1}\pmod n$, as required.
