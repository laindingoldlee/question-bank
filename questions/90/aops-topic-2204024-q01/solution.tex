A quick factorisation: $2016=32\cdot63=2^5\cdot3^2\cdot7$ (or note $2016$ is divisible by $9$, since its digit sum is $9$, and $2016=9\cdot224=9\cdot32\cdot7$). The distinct primes are $2$, $3$, $7$, with sum $\boxed{\textbf{(B) }12}$.
