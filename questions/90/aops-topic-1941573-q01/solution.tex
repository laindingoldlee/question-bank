Dividing both sides by $\dfrac{1}{3}$ means multiplying \emph{every} term by $3$. The student multiplied $\dfrac{2}{3}b$ and $61$ by $3$ but left the term $b$ unchanged. (Also, the check fails: $\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot61+61\ne61$.)

Correctly, multiplying by $3$ gives $2b+3b=183$, so $5b=183$ and $b=\boxed{\dfrac{183}{5}}=36.6$. Check: $\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot36.6+36.6=24.4+36.6=61$.
