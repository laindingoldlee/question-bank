We show that the sum of the squares of the three numbers never changes. For the two new numbers,
\[
\left(\dfrac{3a+4b}{5}\right)^2+\left(\dfrac{4a-3b}{5}\right)^2=\dfrac{9a^2+24ab+16b^2+16a^2-24ab+9b^2}{25}=a^2+b^2 ,
\]
and the third number is unchanged. So the sum of the squares is an invariant, and it always equals its starting value
\[
2^2+3^2+6^2=49 .
\]
If a number $x$ is on the board, then $x^2$ is at most the sum of the three squares, so $x^2\le49$ and $x\le7$. Hence no number greater than $7$ can ever appear. $\blacksquare$
