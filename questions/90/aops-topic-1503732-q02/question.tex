The numbers $2$, $3$, $6$ are written on a blackboard. A move consists of choosing two of the numbers on the board, say $a$ and $b$, and replacing them by
\[
\dfrac{3a}{5}+\dfrac{4b}{5}\qquad\text{and}\qquad\dfrac{4a}{5}-\dfrac{3b}{5}.
\]
Prove that no number greater than $7$ can ever appear on the board.
