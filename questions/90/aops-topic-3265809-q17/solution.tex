Measure time $t$ in hours from a triple coincidence at midnight. Hour and minute hands agree when $\dfrac{11t}{12}$ is an integer, so $t=\dfrac{12k}{11}$. Second and minute hands agree when $59t$ is an integer. Substitution gives $\dfrac{708k}{11}$ integral; since $708$ and $11$ are coprime, $11\mid k$. Thus triple coincidences occur exactly at multiples of twelve hours, giving $\boxed2$ in the specified half-open day.

\end{asy}
