Order the scores $a\le b\le c\le d\le e$; the median is $c$. Since $d,e\le100$, $a+b+c\ge204-200=4$, and since $a,b\le c$, $3c\ge4$, so $c\ge2$. The scores $0,2,2,100,100$ (sum $204$) have median $2$. The answer is $\boxed{2}$.
