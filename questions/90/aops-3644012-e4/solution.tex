Each diagonal divides the quadrilateral into two of the four triangles. Thus the sum of all four triangle areas is twice the quadrilateral area, which is $\dfrac{24}{2}=\boxed{12}$.
\begin{asy}
import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(5,0),C=(4,3),D=(1,4);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C,blue);draw(B--D,red);label("$A$",A,(-1,-1));label("$B$",B,(1,-1));label("$C$",C,(1,1));label("$D$",D,(-1,1));
\end{asy}
