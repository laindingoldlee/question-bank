Let $V$ be the sum of the squares of the four pile sizes. A move changes it by $(a-1)^2+(b+1)^2-a^2-b^2=2-2(a-b)$. Thus earnings telescope to $2026+\dfrac{1600-V_{\rm final}}{2}$. The smallest possible $V$ is $4\cdot20^2=1600$, by Cauchy--Schwarz; the largest is $80^2=6400$, since all cross terms in $(\sum a_i)^2$ are nonnegative. Both endpoints are achievable: use $1013$ out-and-back moves to finish at the initial state; or consolidate all cards in $60$ moves and add $983$ out-and-back pairs. Hence maximum earnings are $\boxed{2026}$ and minimum earnings are $\boxed{-374}$.
