Suppose $1\%$ of the planets in the universe contain water. Astronomers develop a test that is $95\%$ accurate: it says ``water'' for $95\%$ of the planets that contain water, and ``no water'' for $95\%$ of the planets that do not. What is the probability that a planet identified by the test as containing water really does contain water? Express your answer as a common fraction.
