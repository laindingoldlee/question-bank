Write the number as $\overline{abcd}$. Then $a+d=14-9=5$ and $a-d=1$, so $a=3$ and $d=2$. Divisibility by $11$ requires $(a+c)-(b+d)=c-b+1$ to be a multiple of $11$. With $b+c=9$, the difference $c-b$ is odd and between $-9$ and $9$, so $c-b=-1$; then $c=4$, $b=5$. The integer is $\boxed{3542}$, and indeed $3542=11\cdot322$.
