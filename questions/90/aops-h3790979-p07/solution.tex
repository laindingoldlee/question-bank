Let $C=(0,0)$, $A=(u,0)$, $B=(v\cos\gamma,v\sin\gamma)$, where $\gamma=\angle ACB$. The feet are $B_1=(v\cos\gamma,0)$ and $A_1=(u\cos^2\gamma,u\cos\gamma\sin\gamma)$. Subtracting coordinates gives $A_1B_1^2=\cos^2\gamma(u^2+v^2-2uv\cos\gamma)=AB^2\cos^2\gamma$. Hence $13^2\cos^2\gamma=25$, and since $\sin\gamma>0$, $13\sin\gamma=\sqrt{169-25}=\boxed{12}$. The calculation covers both acute and obtuse angles.
