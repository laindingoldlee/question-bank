Five swimmers, Omar, Mario, Miguel, Edgar and Beto, occupy lanes $1$ through $5$, one per lane. Beto is not next to Mario or Edgar. Omar is at an end. Miguel is in an interior lane and neither adjacent swimmer is Mario. Edgar is not in lanes $2,3,5$. Determine the lane assignment.
