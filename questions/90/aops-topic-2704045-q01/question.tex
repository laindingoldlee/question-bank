Given that $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\dfrac{\sin\dfrac{k\pi}{6}}{3^k}=\dfrac{a+b\sqrt c}{d}$ with positive integers $a,b,c,d$, where $\gcd(a,b,d)=1$ and $c$ is squarefree, find $a+b+c+d-1$.
