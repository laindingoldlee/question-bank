Dividing, $a=\dfrac yz+\dfrac zy$, $b=\dfrac zx+\dfrac xz$ and $c=\dfrac xy+\dfrac yx$. Put $u=\dfrac yz$, $v=\dfrac zx$, $w=\dfrac xy$; then $uvw=1$ and
\[a=u+\dfrac1u,\qquad b=v+\dfrac1v,\qquad c=w+\dfrac1w.\]
Expand the product:
\[abc=uvw+\dfrac{uv}{w}+\dfrac{uw}{v}+\dfrac{vw}{u}+\dfrac{u}{vw}+\dfrac{v}{uw}+\dfrac{w}{uv}+\dfrac{1}{uvw}.\]
Since $uvw=1$ we have $vw=\dfrac1u$, $uw=\dfrac1v$, $uv=\dfrac1w$, so $\dfrac{uv}{w}=\dfrac1{w^2}$, $\dfrac{u}{vw}=u^2$, and so on:
\[abc=2+u^2+\dfrac1{u^2}+v^2+\dfrac1{v^2}+w^2+\dfrac1{w^2}.\]
On the other hand $a^2=u^2+2+\dfrac1{u^2}$, and similarly for $b^2$ and $c^2$, so
\[a^2+b^2+c^2=6+u^2+\dfrac1{u^2}+v^2+\dfrac1{v^2}+w^2+\dfrac1{w^2}=abc+4.\]
Hence $a^2+b^2+c^2-abc=\boxed{4}$ for every such $x,y,z$.
