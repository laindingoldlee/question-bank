The small cubes have total surface area $27\cdot6\cdot9=1458$ square inches. The painted area is the original surface, $6\cdot81=486$. So the unpainted area is $1458-486=\boxed{972}$ square inches.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(2.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair o=(0.5,0.35); for(int i=0;i<=3;++i){draw((i,0)--(i,3)); draw((0,i)--(3,i)); draw((3,i)--(3,i)+3*o); draw((i,3)--(i,3)+3*o);} draw((0,3)+3*o--(3,3)+3*o--(3,0)+3*o); for(int i=0;i<=3;++i){draw((0,3)+i*o--(3,3)+i*o); draw((3,0)+i*o--(3,3)+i*o);}
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
