A five-digit number starting $10$ has the form $\overline{10abc}$ with $c$ even. Its odd digits are $1$ and whichever of $a,b$ are odd, so we need $a$ and $b$ to be two different odd digits other than $1$. The smallest choice is $a=3$, $b=5$, and then $c=0$ is the smallest even digit. (Numbers $\overline{100bc}$ have too few odd digits.) The answer is $\boxed{10350}$.
