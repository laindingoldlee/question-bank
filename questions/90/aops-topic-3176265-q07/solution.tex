If $AD=h$, the centers are $\sqrt{4+h^2}$ apart. Tangency gives this distance as $4$, so $h=2\sqrt3$. Rectangle area is $4\sqrt3$. Each quarter disk has area $\pi$, and their interiors are disjoint. The remaining area is $\boxed{4\sqrt3-2\pi}$.

usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
real h=2*sqrt(3);draw((0,0)--(2,0)--(2,h)--(0,h)--cycle);draw(arc((0,0),2,0,90),blue);draw(arc((2,h),2,180,270),blue);label("$A$",(0,0),SW);label("$B$",(2,0),SE);label("$C$",(2,h),NE);label("$D$",(0,h),NW);
\end{asy}
