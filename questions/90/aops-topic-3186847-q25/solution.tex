Let $p_j=a^j+b^j+c^j+d^j$. Newton's identities for $g=x^4-x^3+x^2-x+1$ give $p_1=1$, $p_2=-1$, $p_3=1$, $p_4=-1$. Also $(x+1)g(x)=x^5+1$, so every root has fifth power -1 and $p_5=-4$. Thus the requested sum is $p_5+2p_4-2p_3+4p_2+5p_1+24=-4-2-2-4+5+24=\boxed{17}$.
