Bill claims that for every point in quadrant III of the coordinate plane, reflecting the point across the line $y=-x$ gives the same point as rotating it by $180^\circ$ about the origin. Is Bill's claim
\[\textbf{(A) }\text{always true}\qquad\textbf{(B) }\text{sometimes true}\qquad\textbf{(C) }\text{never true}?\]
Prove your answer.
