Let $p=P(K=C)$. Normalization gives $p(1+2+\cdots+C)=1$, so $p=\dfrac{2}{C(C+1)}$. Thus $E[K]=\dfrac{2}{C(C+1)}\sum_{k=1}^{C}k(C+1-k)$. Applying the formulas for sums of integers and squares simplifies this to $\boxed{\dfrac{C+2}{3}}$.
