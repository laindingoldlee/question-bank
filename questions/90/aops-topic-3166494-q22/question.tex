For a positive integer $C$, a random daily cup count $K$ lies in $\{1,\ldots,C\}$. For each $X=1,\ldots,C$, the probability of $K=C+1-X$ is $X$ times the probability of $K=C$. Find $E[K]$ in terms of $C$.
