Three fair dice are rolled, and then every number is replaced by its $8$-complement $8-k$, except that $1$'s and $4$'s are kept (so $2\to6$, $3\to5$, $5\to3$, $6\to2$, $1\to1$, $4\to4$). What is the probability that the sum of the three new numbers is $15$?
