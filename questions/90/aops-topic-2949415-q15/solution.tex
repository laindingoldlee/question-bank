Since the endpoint residues are one and two modulo three, $n=\dfrac{2^{2021}-2}{3}-\dfrac{2^{2020}-1}{3}=\dfrac{2^{2020}-1}{3}=1+4+\cdots+4^{1009}$. Its binary digits therefore have ones exactly at positions $0,2,\ldots,2018$, giving $\boxed{1010}$.
