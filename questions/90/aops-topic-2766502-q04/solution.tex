Mix $a$ units of the first with $b$ units of the second. The paper fraction is $\dfrac{0.6a+0.2b}{a+b}=0.4$ exactly when $0.2a=0.2b$, i.e. $a=b$. So $\boxed{\text{yes, in equal amounts (ratio }1:1)}$.
