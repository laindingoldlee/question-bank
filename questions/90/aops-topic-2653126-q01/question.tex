A $10\times21$ rectangular bar of chocolate is divided by grid lines into $210$ unit squares. A \emph{break} takes one piece and splits it into two pieces along a single grid line (pieces may not be stacked). Prove that, however the breaks are made, exactly $209$ breaks are needed to split the bar into its $210$ unit squares.
