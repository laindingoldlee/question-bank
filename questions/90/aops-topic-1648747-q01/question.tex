Let $x$ and $y$ be real numbers with $x=y$. Find every error in the following ``proof'' that $1=0$:
\[
x=y\ \Rightarrow\ x^2=xy\ \Rightarrow\ x^2-y^2=xy-y^2\ \Rightarrow\ (x-y)(x+y)=y(x-y)\ \Rightarrow\ x+y=y\ \Rightarrow\ x=0\ \Rightarrow\ \dfrac {x}{x}=\dfrac{0}{x}\ \Rightarrow\ 1=0 .
\]
