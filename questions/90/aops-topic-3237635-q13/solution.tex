Vieta's formulas give $a+b=-10,ab=-1000,c+d=-100,cd=-1000$. Grouping the expanded terms, the expression is
\[(a^2+b^2)(c+d)-(a+b)(c^2+d^2)-4abcd.\]
Now $a^2+b^2=100+2000=2100$ and $c^2+d^2=10000+2000=12000$. Substitution yields $2100(-100)-(-10)(12000)-4(1000000)=\boxed{-4090000}$.
