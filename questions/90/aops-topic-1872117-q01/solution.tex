First, $23232=2^6\cdot363=2^6\cdot3\cdot11^2$.

Every positive divisor of $2^6\cdot3^1\cdot11^2$ has the form $2^x\cdot3^y\cdot11^z$ with $0\le x\le6$, $0\le y\le1$, $0\le z\le2$, because its prime factors must come from those of $23232$, each used at most as often as in $23232$. Conversely every such product is a divisor, and different exponent triples give different numbers (unique factorisation). So divisors correspond exactly to choices of exponents: $x$ has $6+1=7$ choices (the $+1$ is the choice $x=0$, leaving out the prime $2$), $y$ has $1+1=2$, and $z$ has $2+1=3$. By the multiplication principle the number of divisors is
\[
(6+1)(1+1)(2+1)=\boxed{42}.
\]
