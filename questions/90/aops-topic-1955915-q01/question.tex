The largest circle is inscribed in a square. A medium circle lies in a corner of the square, tangent to the two sides of the square at that corner and to the largest circle. A smallest circle lies in the same corner, tangent to the same two sides and to the medium circle. What is the ratio of the area of the smallest circle to the area of the largest circle?
