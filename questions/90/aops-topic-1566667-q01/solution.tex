Let $m=15\sqrt3$, so $m^2=675$ and $3m^2=2025$. Then
\[
\bigl(2025m-m^3\bigr)-\bigl(2025n-n^3\bigr)=n^3-3m^2n+2m^3=(n-m)^2(n+2m),
\]
as expanding the right side shows: $(n^2-2mn+m^2)(n+2m)=n^3-3m^2n+2m^3$. For $n\ge0$ the right side is $\ge0$, with equality only at $n=m$. So the greatest value occurs at
\[
n=15\sqrt3=\sqrt{675}\approx25.98,
\]
which rounds to $\boxed{26}$. (In calculus terms, the derivative $2025-3n^2$ vanishes at $n^2=675$.)

About AM-GM on $n(45-n)(45+n)$: the three factors have no constant sum, so making them ``as close together as possible'' does not apply directly; one would need weights, and the identity above avoids this.
