There are $5$ holes in a row in a field, and a rabbit lives in one of them. Every night the rabbit moves to a neighbouring hole (one to the left or one to the right; from an end hole it must move inwards). Every day you may check exactly one hole. What is the minimum number of days needed to be sure of finding the rabbit?
