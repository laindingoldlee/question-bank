Normalize $AB=BD=1$. Equal altitudes to $BC$ imply $\dfrac{DC}{BC}=\dfrac{1}{4}$, so $BC=\dfrac{4}{3}$. Use $B=(0,0),D=(1,0),A=(\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}),C=(\dfrac{4}{3},0)$. Then $AC^2=(\dfrac{5}{6})^2+\dfrac{3}{4}=\dfrac{13}{9}$. Thus $\boxed{m+n=22}$.
