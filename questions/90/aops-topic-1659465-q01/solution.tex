Since $100^k=2^{2k}5^{2k}$, we need the exponents of $2$ and $5$ in $2018!$. By Legendre's formula,
\[
v_5(2018!)=\left\lfloor\dfrac{2018}{5}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{2018}{25}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{2018}{125}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{2018}{625}\right\rfloor=403+80+16+3=502,
\]
\[
v_2(2018!)=1009+504+252+126+63+31+15+7+3+1=2011 .
\]
We need $2k\le2011$ and $2k\le502$, so the largest $k$ is $\left\lfloor\dfrac{502}{2}\right\rfloor=\boxed{251}$.
