The intersecting-diagonals angle theorem says that the angle between $AC$ and $BD$ is half the sum of one pair of opposite intercepted arcs. Since this angle is 90 degrees, the corresponding arc sum is 180 degrees. In particular $\boxed{\widehat{AB}+\widehat{CD}=180^\circ}$. Here the indicated arcs between consecutive vertices are minor: the remaining arc $BCD$ measures 100 degrees, so arcs $BC,CD<100$; perpendicularity then gives $AB=180-CD<180$.

usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=dir(0),B=dir(120),C=dir(160),D=dir(220);draw(circle((0,0),1));draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C);draw(B--D);label("A",A,E);label("B",B,N);label("C",C,W);label("D",D,SW);
\end{asy}
