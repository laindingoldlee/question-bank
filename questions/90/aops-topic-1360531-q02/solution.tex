Let the side of the rhombus be $s$, so $KE=ED=s$ and $NE=6-s$. In a rhombus opposite sides are parallel, so $ED\parallel KF$, that is, $ED\parallel KM$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=30/11;
pair K=(0,0), M=(5,0), Nn=(1.2,sqrt(36-1.44));
pair F=(s,0), Ee=s*Nn/6, D=F+Ee;
draw(K--M--Nn--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
filldraw(K--F--D--Ee--cycle,paleblue,blue);
draw(K--D,red+dashed);
dot(F);dot(D);dot(Ee);
label("$K$",K,SW); label("$M$",M,SE); label("$N$",Nn,N);
label("$F$",F,S); label("$D$",D,NE); label("$E$",Ee,W);
draw((0,-0.6)--(5,-0.6),Arrows(4)); draw((0,-0.25)--(0,-0.85),gray); draw((5,-0.25)--(5,-0.85),gray); label("$5$",(2.5,-0.6),S);
pair un=unit(Nn-M), nv=(un.y,-un.x); draw((M+0.85*nv)--(Nn+0.85*nv),Arrows(4)); draw((M+0.35*nv)--(M+1.1*nv),gray); draw((Nn+0.35*nv)--(Nn+1.1*nv),gray); label("$7$",(M+Nn)/2+0.85*nv,nv);
label("$s$",(K+F)/2,S,blue); label("$s$",(F+D)/2,E,blue); label("$s$",(D+Ee)/2+(0,0.22),blue); label("$s$",(Ee+K)/2,W,blue);
label("$6-s$",(Ee+Nn)/2,W,fontsize(8pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Therefore triangle $NED$ is similar to triangle $NKM$, and
\[
\dfrac{ED}{KM}=\dfrac{NE}{NK}\iff\dfrac{s}{5}=\dfrac{6-s}{6}\iff 6s=30-5s\iff s=\dfrac{30}{11}.
\]
So $\boxed{NE=\dfrac{36}{11}}$ and $\boxed{EK=\dfrac{30}{11}}$. (Equivalently, the diagonal $KD$ bisects angle $K$, and the angle bisector theorem gives $\dfrac{MD}{DN}=\dfrac{KM}{KN}=\dfrac{5}{6}$.)
