The four dice have total face sum $4\cdot21=84$. The middle cube has three hidden faces, including an opposite pair, so their sum is at least $7+1=8$. Each other cube has one hidden face, at least 1. These minima can be realised independently by orienting each die. Thus the exposed sum is $84-8-3=\boxed{73}$.

\end{asy}
