Euclid's algorithm gives remainders $ 824-2(324)=176 $, $ 324-176=148 $, $ 176-148=28 $, $ 148-5(28)=8 $, $ 28-3(8)=4 $, and $ 8=2(4)$. Thus the gcd is $\boxed4 $. Comparing exponents in the prime factorizations proves $\gcd(a,b)\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=ab $, so the lcm is $\dfrac{324\cdot824}{4}=81\cdot824=\boxed{66744}$. Dividing before multiplying keeps the intermediate arithmetic smaller.
