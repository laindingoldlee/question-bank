Let the base be $b$ and the height $h$, and let the rectangle have height $y$ ($0<y<h$). Its top side lies on a line parallel to the base at height $y$, where the triangle's width is $b\left(1-\dfrac {y}{h}\right)$ by similar triangles; the rectangle's width equals this. So its area is
\[R=by\left(1-\dfrac {y}{h}\right)=\dfrac {b}{h}\,y(h-y)\le\dfrac {b}{h}\cdot\dfrac{h^2}{4}=\dfrac{bh}{4}=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{bh}{2},\]
by AM-GM on $y$ and $h-y$, with equality at $y=\dfrac {h}{2}$. $\blacksquare$
