No. Take $O=(0,0)$, $A=(-2,0)$, $C=(1,0)$, $B=(0,2)$ and $D=(0,-1)$. These form a convex quadrilateral whose diagonals meet at $O$. Then $OD=OC=1$ but $OB=2$. Equal segments on different diagonals do not force either diagonal to be bisected.
\begin{asy}
import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
pair A=(-2,0),B=(0,2),C=(1,0),D=(0,-1),O=(0,0);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C^^B--D,dashed);label("$A$",A,(-1,0));label("$B$",B,(0,1));label("$C$",C,(1,0));label("$D$",D,(0,-1));label("$O$",O,(-1,-1));label("$1$",(0,-.5),(1,0));label("$2$",(0,1),(1,0));
\end{asy}
