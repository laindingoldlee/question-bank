The inequality $|n|<|n-3|$ says $n$ is closer to $0$ than to $3$, i.e. $n<\dfrac{3}{2}$. The inequality $|n-3|<9$ gives $-6<n<12$. So $-6<n<\dfrac{3}{2}$, i.e. $n\in\{-5,-4,\dots,1\}$, with sum $\boxed{-14}$.
