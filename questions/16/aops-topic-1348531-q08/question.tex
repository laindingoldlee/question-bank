Sam has a pile of $240$ snowballs and throws them at a steady rate of $15$ snowballs per minute. Meanwhile it is snowing, and his pile grows at a steady rate of $15$ snowballs every $3$ minutes. After $t$ minutes he has used up all his snowballs. Compute
\[
\dfrac{t^2+5t+(t-10)}{2t},
\]
and express your answer as a common fraction.
