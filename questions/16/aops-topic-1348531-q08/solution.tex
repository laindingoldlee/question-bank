The pile shrinks at a net rate of $15-\dfrac{15}{3}=10$ snowballs per minute, so it is empty after $t=\dfrac{240}{10}=24$ minutes. Then
\[
\dfrac{t^2+5t+(t-10)}{2t}=\dfrac{576+120+14}{48}=\dfrac{710}{48}=\boxed{\dfrac{355}{24}}.
\]
