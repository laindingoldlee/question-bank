The tens digit belongs to $\{2,3,5,7\}$, and the units digit to $\{3,5,7\}$. The twelve candidates are $23,25,27,33,35,37,53,55,57,73,75,77$. Dividing each by its digit sum leaves only $27$, since $\dfrac{27}{2+7}=3$ and none of the other eleven quotients is integral. The required sum is $\boxed{27}$.
