Point $C=(5,1,2)$ in space. For a point $X\ne C$, define its \emph{weight} as $\dfrac1{XC}$. Points $P,Q\ne C$ are distinct, and $|\text{weight}(P)-\text{weight}(Q)|=PQ$. Prove that at least one of $P,Q$ lies on or inside the unit sphere centred at $C$.
