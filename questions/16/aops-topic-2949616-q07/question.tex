Let
\[f(x)=\begin{cases}\sqrt{x^2}-2,&x<0,\\5,&x=0,\\x^2-3,&0<x<20,\\ \dfrac x{11},&x\ge20.\end{cases}\]
Find $f^{2022}(1)$, the result of applying $f$ to $1$ a total of $2022$ times.
