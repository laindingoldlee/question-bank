Every day except possibly the final day removes at least two socks. Thus if there are $d$ days, $20\ge2(d-1)+1$, giving $d\le10$. Ten days occur if the socks come out as ten matching pairs. One day cannot empty the box: after the first sock, at most ten socks of the other colour can appear before a matching sock must appear, so that day removes at most 12 socks. Two days are possible: on day 1 draw one white sock, eight black socks, then one white sock; on day 2 draw one black sock, eight white socks, then one black sock. Each day stops at its first colour match and all socks are used. Thus $a=2$, $b=10$, and $\boxed{ab=20}$.
