John has a box of $20$ socks. $10$ of them are white and $10$ are black. Every day, John takes a sock out of the box, without looking. He then proceeds to take more socks out of the box (without looking) until he finds a sock that has the same color as the first sock. He does not put any sock which he has taken out back. The number of days that John takes to empty the box lies between $a$ and $b$, inclusive. If $a$ and $b$ are as close as possible, compute the product $ab$. If the box becomes empty before a match is found, that day ends.
