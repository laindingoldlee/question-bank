The Fibonacci numbers are defined by $F_0=0$, $F_1=1$ and $F_{k+1}=F_k+F_{k-1}$ for $k\ge1$. Is it true that
\[F_{n+m+2}=F_{n+1}F_{m+1}+F_{n+1}F_m+F_nF_{m+1}\]
for all integers $n,m\ge0$? Prove your answer.
