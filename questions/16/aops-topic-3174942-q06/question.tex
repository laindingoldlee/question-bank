A tank leaks into an initially empty upright frustum at a constant rate of $10$ liters per minute. The frustum has height $1$ meter, bottom radius $0.6$ meter, and top radius $0.8$ meter. Independently, a cup is sold from the original tank every $20$ seconds, with the first sale at $20$ seconds. How many cups have been sold when the collected liquid reaches height $0.7$ meter?
