We need $7^{1086}\bmod1000$. Note $7^4=2401\equiv401\pmod{1000}$, and $401^k\equiv1+400k\pmod{1000}$ (the other terms of $(1+400)^k$ are multiples of $400^2$, which is divisible by $1000$). Since $1086=4\cdot271+2$,
\[7^{1086}=\left(7^4\right)^{271}\cdot7^2\equiv(1+400\cdot271)\cdot49\equiv(1+108400)\cdot49\equiv401\cdot49=19649\equiv649\pmod{1000}.\]
The last three digits are $\boxed{649}$.
