There are $64$ equally likely ordered pairs. In $8$ of them the two numbers are equal. The remaining $56$ split evenly by symmetry into $28$ with the second number larger and $28$ with the first larger. So the favourable pairs number $8+28=36$, and the probability is
\[
\dfrac{36}{64}=\boxed{\dfrac{9}{16}}.
\]
