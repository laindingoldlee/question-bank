Each pair $(a_i,b_i)$ has one member at most 50 and one at least 51. Indeed, if both were at most 50, the $i$ numbers $a_1,\ldots,a_i$ and the $51-i$ numbers $b_i,\ldots,b_{50}$ would be 51 distinct numbers at most 50, impossible. If both exceeded 50, the $51-i$ numbers $a_i,\ldots,a_{50}$ and the $i$ numbers $b_1,\ldots,b_i$ would similarly be 51 distinct numbers above 50. Therefore each absolute difference subtracts a lower-half number from an upper-half number. Every number is used once, giving $\sum_{51}^{100}k-\sum_1^{50}k=50\cdot50=\boxed{2500}$.
