In $2x+3y=504$, $3y$ must be even, so $y$ is even: $y=0,2,\dots,168$ (since $3y\le504$), each giving one $x$. That is $\boxed{85}$ solutions.

In $2x+5y=504$, $y$ must be even with $5y\le504$: $y=0,2,\dots,100$, giving $\boxed{51}$ solutions.
