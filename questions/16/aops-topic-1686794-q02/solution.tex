There are $\dbinom{12}{2}=66$ pairs of squares. Pairs sharing a side: in each of the $3$ rows of $4$ squares there are $3$ horizontally adjacent pairs, giving $9$; in each of the $4$ columns of $3$ squares there are $2$ vertically adjacent pairs, giving $8$. So $17$ pairs share a side, and the answer is $66-17=\boxed{49}$.
