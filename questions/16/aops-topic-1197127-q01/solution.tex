A distribution is a list of $5$ nonnegative counts with sum $10$. Represent it as a row of $10$ stars and $4$ bars, the bars separating the boxes. The arrangements are counted by choosing the positions of the $4$ bars among $14$ places:
\[\dbinom{14}{4}=\boxed{1001}.\]
