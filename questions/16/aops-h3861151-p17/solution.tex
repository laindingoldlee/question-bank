At $x=27$, $3^{27}=27^9$, so equality does not suffice. The function $\dfrac{\log x}{x}$ is strictly decreasing for $x\ge3$, because its derivative is $\dfrac{1-\log x}{x^2}<0$. Thus $3^x>x^9$ holds for $x>27$ and fails for $3\le x\le27$. It also fails at $x=2$ by direct comparison. Hence the smallest integer is $\boxed{28}$.
