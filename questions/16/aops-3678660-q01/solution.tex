Subtract one candy per student. The remaining eight satisfy $x_1+\cdots+x_4=8$ with $0\le x_i\le5$. Without upper bounds there are $\binom{11}3=165$ solutions. For each specified $x_i\ge6$, subtract six to obtain $\binom53=10$ solutions. Two such violations cannot occur with sum eight. Hence the answer is $165-4\cdot10=\boxed{125}$.
