$47$ is prime, and $4=2^2$ is a square, so by Fermat's little theorem
\[
4^{23}=2^{46}\equiv1\pmod{47}.
\]
Since $1105=23\cdot48+1$, we get $4^{1105}=\left(4^{23}\right)^{48}\cdot4\equiv4\pmod{47}$. The remainder is $\boxed{4}$.
