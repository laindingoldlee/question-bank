Point $C$ lies outside the circle. Its power is $AC^2-5^2=81-25=56$. The secant theorem gives $CB\cdot CD=56$, hence $\boxed{BC=8}$. In particular $D$ lies between $C$ and $B$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair C=(0,0),B=(8,0),D=(7,0),A=(7.5,sqrt(81-7.5^2));draw(circle(A,5));draw(A--B--C--cycle);dot(D);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,SW);label("$D$",D,S);
\end{asy}
