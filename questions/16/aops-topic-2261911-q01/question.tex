Square $ABCD$ has side length $14$. Square $OPXS'$ is constructed so that $O$ and $P$ are the midpoints of $AB$ and $BC$, respectively, and $X$ and $S'$ lie outside $ABCD$. Then square $UTIM$ is constructed so that $T$ and $U$ are the midpoints of $PX$ and $XS'$, respectively, and $I$ and $M$ lie outside $OPXS'$. ``Optimus Prime'' is the heptagon $OPTIMUS'$. Find its perimeter.
