All $\dbinom53=10$ sets of three cards are equally likely. The largest value is $4$ exactly when the set contains $4$, does not contain $5$, and its other two cards come from $\{1,2,3\}$: $\dbinom32=3$ sets. The probability is $\boxed{\textbf{(C) }\dfrac{3}{10}}$.
