The digit conditions require $a\ge6,b\ge5$. Expanding gives $3a+5=4b+2$, so $3(a+1)=4b$. Thus $a=4k-1,b=3k$ for an integer $k$, and the digit bounds force $k\ge2$. Taking $k=2$ gives $a=7,b=6$, so $\boxed{13}$.
