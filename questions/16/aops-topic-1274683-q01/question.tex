Let $ABC$ be an equilateral triangle with altitude $AH$. Point $D$ lies on ray $HC$ with $HD=HA$. Point $E$ lies on the extension of $AB$ beyond $B$ such that $\angle BDE=15^\circ$. Prove that triangle $HED$ is isosceles.
