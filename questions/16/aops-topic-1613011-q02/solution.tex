The largest product of $N$ with a one-digit positive integer is $9N$, and $9N\le9\cdot999=8991$ never exceeds four digits. So $N$ has the property exactly when $9N\ge1000$, i.e. $N\ge\dfrac{1000}{9}=111.1\ldots$, i.e. $N\ge112$. The number of such three-digit integers is
\[
999-112+1=\boxed{888}.
\]
(Under the stricter reading ``for every one-digit integer $k\ge2$, $kN$ has four digits'', the condition is $2N\ge1000$, giving $500$ numbers; $k=1$ never works.)
