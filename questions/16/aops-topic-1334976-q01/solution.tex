The square has side $\sqrt{25}=5$. An equilateral triangle with side $s$ has area $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}s^2$, so $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}s^2=9\sqrt3$ gives $s^2=36$ and $s=6$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((0,0)--(5,0)--(5,5)--(0,5)--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
label("$5$",(2.5,0),S); label("area $25$",(2.5,2.5),fontsize(9pt));
pair P=(7,0),Q=(13,0),T=(10,3*sqrt(3));
draw(P--Q--T--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
label("$6$",(P+Q)/2,S); label("area $9\sqrt{3}$",(10,1.6),fontsize(9pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The difference of the side lengths is $6-5=\boxed{1}$.
