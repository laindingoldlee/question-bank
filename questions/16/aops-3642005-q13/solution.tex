For small-circle radius r and centre distance d from O, tangency to the two rays gives $r=d\sin30^\circ=\dfrac{d}{2}$. Internal tangency gives d+r=12, hence r=4. The inscribed square has diagonal eight and area $\boxed{32}$.
\begin{asy}
import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
pair O=(0,0),M=12*dir(30),N=12*dir(-30),S=(8,0);draw(circle(O,12));draw(O--M^^O--N);draw(circle(S,4),blue);real a=2*sqrt(2);draw(S+(-a,-a)--S+(a,-a)--S+(a,a)--S+(-a,a)--cycle);label("$O$",O,(-1,0));label("$M$",M,(1,1));label("$N$",N,(1,-1));
\end{asy}
