Let $s=a+b$ and $p=ab$. Then $p=\dfrac{s^2-5}{2}$ and $7=s^3-3ps$, giving $s^3-15s+14=0$. This factors as $(s-1)(s^2+s-14)$, whose roots are $1$ and $\dfrac{-1\pm\sqrt{57}}{2}$, all real. Conversely each such $s$, together with $p=\dfrac{s^2-5}{2}$, gives complex roots of $t^2-st+p=0$ satisfying both original equations. The largest possible sum is $\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt{57}-1}{2}}$.
