Bob's and Charlie's ages $b$ and $c$ satisfy $10\le b,c\le99$ and $b=2c$, and the sum of the digits of $c$ is twice the sum of the digits of $b$. Let $x$ be the positive number with $x^2=10^2+b^2+c^2$, and let $y$ be the sum of the squares of the digits of $x$. Find $x-y$.
