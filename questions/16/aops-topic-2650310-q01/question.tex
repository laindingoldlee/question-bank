Dr. Evil's island base lies on a great circle of the Earth. He wants to fly once around the world along this great circle, leaving the island and returning to it from the other side, riding in the same plane the whole time. All his planes fly at the same constant speed with the same fuel consumption, and a full tank lets a plane fly exactly halfway around the world. Planes can transfer any amount of fuel to one another in mid-air when they are at the same place, and can refuel without limit at the island; refuelling, transfers and turning take no time. Every plane must return safely to the island. What is the minimum number of planes he needs? Prove your answer.
