Immediately after creation at minute $n\ge6$, batches born at minutes $1$ through $n-6$ have lost three apples each. The total is $5n-3(n-6)=2n+18$. It first reaches $50$ at $n=16$; earlier creation events have smaller totals, and vanishing events only decrease the count. Therefore the first time is $\boxed{12{:}16\text{ p.m.}}$.
