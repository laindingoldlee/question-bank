$ABCD$ is a unit square, and $E,F,G,H$ are points on the sides $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, $DA$ respectively, with $BE=CF=DG=AH$. The segments $AF$, $BG$, $CH$ and $DE$ enclose a small square of area $\dfrac{1}{6519}$. Find $BE$. More generally, find $BE$ when the small square has area $\dfrac1n$ for an integer $n>1$.
