Four lines in general position (no parallels or triple intersection) produce $1+1+2+3+4=11$ regions. There are 8 unbounded regions, two associated with each line direction, leaving 3 bounded regions. Degeneracies cannot increase the number of bounded regions: perturbing parallel or concurrent lines slightly preserves all existing bounded open regions and can only add some. Hence the maximum is $\boxed3$.

usepackage("amsmath");

\end{asy}
