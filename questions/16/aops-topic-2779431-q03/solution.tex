The tropical proportion must be $\dfrac{0.40}{0.75}=\dfrac{8}{15}$. Cherry is $\dfrac{1}{10}$. The remaining pure orange is $1-\dfrac{8}{15}-\dfrac{1}{10}=\dfrac{11}{30}$. Multiplying all proportions by 30 gives $\boxed{11:16:3}$. Its orange content is $\dfrac{11}{30}+\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot\dfrac{8}{15}=\dfrac{1}{2}$, as required.
