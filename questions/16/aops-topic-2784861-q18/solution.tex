The recurrence gives $ a_2=1-a_1 $, $ a_3=1+a_1 $, and in general $ a_{2j+1}=j+a_1 $. Thus $ a_{15}=7+a_1 $. The closing equation gives $ 7+2a_1=15 $, so $ a_1=4 $ and $\boxed{a_{15}=11}$.
