A rhombus has diagonals of lengths $12$ and $16$ units. A congruent copy of it is rotated $90^\circ$ and placed so that the shorter diagonal of the rotated copy lies along the line of the longer diagonal of the original rhombus, and the two diagonals overlap in a segment of length $4$ units. What is the area of the region common to the two rhombi? Express your answer as a decimal to the nearest hundredth.
