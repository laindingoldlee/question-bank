The total product is 1. The complement of the upper-left $2\times2$ block is the union of the last row and last column, whose product is the reciprocal of the bottom-right entry. Therefore the block product equals that corner entry, so the corner is 2. The same argument makes all four corners 2. Row and column products then force all four edge-middle entries to be $\dfrac{1}{4}$, and the center to be $\boxed{16}$. The resulting array has rows $(2,\dfrac{1}{4},2)$, $(\dfrac{1}{4},16,\dfrac{1}{4})$, $(2,\dfrac{1}{4},2)$; direct multiplication verifies every condition.
