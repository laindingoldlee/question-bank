One prime must be 5. Call the other two $p,q$. Dividing by 5 gives $pq=p+q+5$, hence $(p-1)(q-1)=6$. The positive factor pairs are $(1,6),(2,3)$ and reversals. Only the first yields two primes,2 and 7; the second gives 3 and 4. Thus the primes are $\boxed{2,5,7}$.
