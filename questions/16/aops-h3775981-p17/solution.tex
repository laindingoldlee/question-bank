If the two repdigits have equal length, their absolute difference is another repdigit or zero. Otherwise let the longer one have digit $a$ and length $n$, and the shorter have digit $b$ and length $m<n$. If $a\ge b$, subtraction uses only digits $a$ and $a-b$. If $a<b$, borrowing produces at most four types: unchanged leading $a$s, one digit $a-1$, repeated digits $a-b+9$, and the final digit $a-b+10$. Thus at most four distinct digits occur. The example $2222-55=2167$ attains four, so the maximum is $\boxed4$.
