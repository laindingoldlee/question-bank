The units digit is even, so there must be exactly two even digits. If the hundreds digit is even, there are 4 choices for it, 5 odd tens choices, and 5 even units choices, giving 100. If the hundreds digit is odd, there are 5 choices for each of it, the even tens, and even units, giving 125. Total $\boxed{225}$.
