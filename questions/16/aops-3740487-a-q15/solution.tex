Let arrival times $u,v$ be minutes after 6:00, uniform and independent on $[0,60]$. They meet exactly when $|u-v|\le15$. The complement consists of two right triangles of leg 45 inside the $60\times60$ square. Thus the probability is $1-\dfrac{2\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot45^2}{60^2}=1-\dfrac{9}{16}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{16}}$, choice D.

\end{asy}
