$55^{12}=5^{12}\cdot11^{12}=(5^3)^4(11^2)^6=125^4\cdot121^6$. Modulo $123$, $125\equiv2$ and $121\equiv-2$, so
\[55^{12}\equiv2^4\cdot(-2)^6=2^{10}=1024=8\cdot123+40\equiv\boxed{40}.\]
