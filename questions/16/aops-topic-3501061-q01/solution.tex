The digit sum gives residue $43\equiv7\pmod9$. A cube has residue only $0,1,$ or $8$ modulo $9$, so (iii) is false. Therefore both (i) and (ii) hold. The number is $k^2$ with $317\le k\le707$. Check this finite range by computing $k^2$ and adding its decimal digits; the only digit sum of $43$ occurs at $k=707$, with $707^2=499849$. It has six digits, is below $500000$, and has digit sum $4+9+9+8+4+9=43$. Therefore $\boxed{499849}$ is the unique answer.
