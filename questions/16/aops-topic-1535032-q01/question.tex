In trapezium $ABCD$ the sides $AB$ and $DC$ are parallel. Let $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of the legs $AD$ and $BC$; the segment $MN$ is called the median (or midline) of the trapezium. Prove that $MN$ is parallel to the bases, and that $MN=\dfrac{AB+DC}{2}$.
