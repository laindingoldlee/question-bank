Replace $y$ by $-y$ in the identity
\[
z^w-y^w=(z-y)\left(z^{w-1}+z^{w-2}y+\cdots+zy^{w-2}+y^{w-1}\right),
\]
which holds for every positive integer $w$ (expand: the sum telescopes). Since $w$ is odd, $(-y)^w=-y^w$, so
\[
\boxed{z^w+y^w=(z+y)\left(z^{w-1}-z^{w-2}y+z^{w-3}y^2-\cdots-zy^{w-2}+y^{w-1}\right)}
\]
with alternating signs ($\sum_{k=0}^{w-1}(-1)^kz^{w-1-k}y^k$). For even $w$ this fails; for instance $z^2+y^2$ has no real linear factor.
