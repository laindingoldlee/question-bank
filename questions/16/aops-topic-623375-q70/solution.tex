Since $176^2=30976$ and $177^2=31329$, the root lies between $176$ and $177$, and much closer to $176$: $31000-30976=24$, while $31329-31000=329$. More precisely $\sqrt{31000}\approx176+\dfrac{24}{352}\approx176.07$. The nearest integer is $\boxed{176}$.
