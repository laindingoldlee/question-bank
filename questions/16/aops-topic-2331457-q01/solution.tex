The candidate needs $270-214=56$ more votes, and the five states have $3+20+16+15+6=60$ votes in all. So the candidate may lose states worth at most $4$ votes: losing $Q$, $R$, $S$ or $T$ (each at least $6$ votes) is fatal, while losing $P$ ($3$ votes) is allowed. Thus the candidate wins exactly when $Q$, $R$, $S$ and $T$ are all won, whatever happens in $P$. By independence the probability is
\[0.8\cdot0.75\cdot0.5\cdot0.25=\dfrac45\cdot\dfrac34\cdot\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac14=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{40}}=7.5\%.\]
