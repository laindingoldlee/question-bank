A candidate has $214$ electoral votes and needs at least $270$ to win. Five states remain, and the candidate wins each of them independently with the following probabilities: state $P$ ($3$ votes) with probability $90\%$, state $Q$ ($20$ votes) with $80\%$, state $R$ ($16$ votes) with $75\%$, state $S$ ($15$ votes) with $50\%$, and state $T$ ($6$ votes) with $25\%$. What is the probability that the candidate wins the election?
