Let the side lengths be $a<b<c$, so $AB=a$, $AC=b$ and $AD=c$. The smallest square lies inside the middle one (they share the corner $A$ and two directions), so the uncovered part of the middle square (shaded) has area
\[
b^2-a^2=(b-a)(b+a)=33 .
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real a=4,bb=7,c=10;
fill((a,0)--(bb,0)--(bb,bb)--(0,bb)--(0,a)--(a,a)--cycle,rgb(0.75,0.85,1));
draw((0,0)--(c,0)--(c,c)--(0,c)--cycle,linewidth(1));
draw((bb,0)--(bb,bb)--(0,bb),linewidth(1));
draw((a,0)--(a,a)--(0,a),linewidth(1));
label("$A$",(0,0),SW);label("$B$",(a,0),S);label("$C$",(bb,0),S);label("$D$",(c,0),SE);
label("$b^2-a^2=33$",(bb/2+0.9,(a+bb)/2));
label("$a$",(a/2,a),N);label("$b$",(bb/2,bb),N);label("$c$",(c/2,c),N);
draw((a,-1.1)--(bb,-1.1),Arrows(TeXHead));draw((bb,-1.1)--(c,-1.1),Arrows(TeXHead));
label("$b-a$",((a+bb)/2,-1.1),S);label("$c-b$",((bb+c)/2,-1.1),S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Here $b-a$ and $b+a$ are positive integers with $b-a<b+a$ and product $33$, so
\[
(b-a,\ b+a)=(1,33)\quad\text{or}\quad(3,11),
\]
giving $(a,b)=(16,17)$ or $(a,b)=(4,7)$. The condition $BC=CD$ says $b-a=c-b$, so $c=2b-a$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $a=16$, $b=17$, $c=18$ (check: $17^2-16^2=33$);
\item $a=4$, $b=7$, $c=10$ (check: $7^2-4^2=33$; this case is drawn in the sketch).
\end{itemize}
So the side lengths are $\boxed{16,\ 17,\ 18\text{ cm}}$ or $\boxed{4,\ 7,\ 10\text{ cm}}$.
