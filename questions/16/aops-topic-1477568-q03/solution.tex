Let $q$ be the number of quarters. Then she has $q+4$ pennies and $q+5$ nickels. Counting in cents,
\[
25q+(q+4)+5(q+5)=308\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad 31q+29=308\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad q=9 .
\]
So she has $9$ quarters, $13$ pennies and $\boxed{14}$ nickels (check: $225+13+70=308$).

The total number of coins is not needed: the two relations express every count in terms of one unknown, and the total value gives one equation for it.
