There are $\binom{20}{2}=190$ unordered card pairs. The complementary value pairs are $(4,6),(5,5),(5,6),(6,6)$, with respective counts $16,6,16,6$. Thus $44$ pairs fail, and the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{190-44}{190}=\dfrac{73}{95}}$.
