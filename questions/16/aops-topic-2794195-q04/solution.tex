Let $A=(0,0),B=(1,0),P=(x,y)$. The inequality becomes $2(x-\dfrac{1}{2})^2+2y^2<\dfrac{1}{2}$, or $(x-\dfrac{1}{2})^2+y^2<\dfrac{1}{4}$. Within the square this is an upper semicircle of radius $\dfrac{1}{2}$, with area $\boxed{\dfrac{\pi}{8}}$. The square area is 1.
