Let the total be $S$. There must be at least six entries: if there were five or fewer, the first two and last three would cover all entries, yet their sums add to only $0.85S$. If there were seven or more, at least two middle entries would remain. Each would be at least as large as the corresponding first entries, so their sum would be at least $0.25S$, contradicting the middle total $0.15S$. Thus there are $\boxed6$ entries. The strictly increasing list $11,14,15,18,20,22$ shows attainability.
