For a fixed distance, time is inversely proportional to speed. Her speed was $1.25=\dfrac{5}{4}$ times the old speed, so her time was $\dfrac{4}{5}$ of the old time:
\[
\dfrac{4}{5}\cdot48=\boxed{38.4\text{ seconds}}.
\]
(Taking $25\%$ off the \emph{time} would give $36$ seconds, which corresponds to a speed $\dfrac{4}{3}$ times the old one, i.e.\ about $33\%$ faster, not $25\%$.)
