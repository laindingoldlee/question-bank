Reaching the midpoint takes $\dfrac{600}{40}=15 $ minutes. The return takes 40 minutes and covers 600 steps upward, giving net upward speed 15. If the escalator speed is $ v $, then $ v-40=15 $, so $\boxed{v=55\text{ steps per minute}}$.
