Let the terms be $a_1,a_2,\dots$ with common difference $d$. The sum of three consecutive terms is three times the middle one, so $3a_2=189$ and $3a_5=369$, giving $a_2=63$ and $a_5=123$. Then $3d=60$, $d=20$, and $a_{10}=a_5+5d=123+100=\boxed{223}$.
