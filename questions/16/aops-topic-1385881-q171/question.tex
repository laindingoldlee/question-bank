Becky writes a number on the blackboard and applies the following rule: she picks any number on the board greater than $1$, erases it, and replaces it with the list of all its proper divisors (the positive divisors other than the number itself). The experiment ends when there are only $1$'s on the board. If Becky starts with the number $72$, how many $1$'s will be on the board when she is finished? Find a quicker way than writing out every step.
