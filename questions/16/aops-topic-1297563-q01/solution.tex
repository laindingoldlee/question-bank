\textbf{Size.} $4444^{4444}<10^{4\cdot4444}$, so it has at most $17776$ digits and $a\le9\cdot17776=159984$. A number not exceeding $159984$ has digit sum at most $45$ (for six-digit numbers starting with $1$ the sum is at most $1+4+9\cdot4=41$; five-digit numbers give at most $45$), so $b\le45$. Among numbers up to $45$ the largest digit sum is $3+9=12$ (at $39$), so the answer is at most $12$.

\textbf{Remainder mod 9.} A number is congruent to its digit sum mod $9$. Since $4444\equiv7$ and $7^3\equiv1\pmod9$, and $4444=3\cdot1481+1$, we get $4444^{4444}\equiv7\pmod9$. So the answer $\equiv7\pmod9$ and is at most $12$; it is $\boxed{7}$.
