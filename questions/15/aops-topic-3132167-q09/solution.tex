Each occupies a 60-degree angle at that vertex. Disjoint interiors imply their angles total at most 360 degrees, so at most 6 fit. Six triangles from the center to consecutive vertices of a regular hexagon attain the bound. Answer $\boxed6$.

\end{asy}
