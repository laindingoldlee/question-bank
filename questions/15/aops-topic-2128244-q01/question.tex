Four cars $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ start at the four corners of a square region with side length $60$ miles, in this order around the square. At the same moment, all four cars start driving straight towards the centre of the square at $30$ miles per hour. A dragonfly starts at car $A$ at the same moment and flies at $60$ miles per hour to car $B$, then to car $C$, then to car $D$, then back to $A$, and so on, until the four cars meet at the centre. What total distance does the dragonfly fly?
