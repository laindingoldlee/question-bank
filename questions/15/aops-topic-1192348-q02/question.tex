Three cards are drawn at random from a standard $52$-card deck (Ace $=1$, number cards at face value, Jack $=11$, Queen $=12$, King $=13$). The lowest of the three is removed (one of them if there is a tie), and then a fourth card is drawn from the remaining $49$ cards of the deck. What is the expected sum of the three cards now held?
