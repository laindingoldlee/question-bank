Factor $a^4-b^4=(a^2+b^2)(a^2-b^2)$. Then $74(a^2-b^2)=1776$, so $a^2-b^2=24$. Together with $a^2+b^2=74$ this gives $a^2=49$ and $b^2=25$, so $a=7$ and $b=5$ (both positive). Hence $ab^2=7\cdot25=\boxed{175}$, answer $\textbf{(D)}$.
