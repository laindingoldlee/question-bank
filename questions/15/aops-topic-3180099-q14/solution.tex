By Vieta, the root-square sum is $(-3)^2-2a=9-2a$, while the coefficient sum is $a+12$. Put $d=9-2a$, an odd nonzero integer. Since $2(a+12)=33-d$, the divisibility condition is equivalent to $d\mid33$. Hence $d=\pm1,\pm3,\pm11,\pm33$. Opposite divisor pairs give values $a(d)+a(-d)=9$, and there are four pairs. Therefore the sum is $\boxed{36}$.
