Since $20^2+21^2=29^2$, angle $B$ is right. Therefore $BD$ bisects it, and the angle bisector theorem yields $\dfrac{AD}{DC}=\dfrac{20}{21}$. Thus $AD=\dfrac{20}{41}\cdot29=\dfrac{580}{41}$, giving $a+b=\boxed{621}$.
