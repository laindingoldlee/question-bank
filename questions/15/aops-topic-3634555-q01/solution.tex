Choose a partition into groups $A,B$ that the first person values at least $1$ each. The second person values all cupcakes at least $2$, because their own assumed partition has two groups each worth at least $1$. Therefore they value at least one of $A,B$ at least $1$; otherwise their total would be less than $2$. Give that group to the second person and the other to the first. Both meet their thresholds.
