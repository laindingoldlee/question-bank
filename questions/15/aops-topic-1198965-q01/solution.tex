The tail after ``$4+$'' is again $x$, so $x=\dfrac{2}{2+\dfrac{5}{3+\dfrac{1}{4+x}}}$. Simplify from the inside:
\[3+\dfrac{1}{4+x}=\dfrac{3x+13}{x+4},\qquad 2+\dfrac{5(x+4)}{3x+13}=\dfrac{11x+46}{3x+13},\qquad x=\dfrac{2(3x+13)}{11x+46}.\]
So $11x^2+46x=6x+26$, i.e. $11x^2+40x-26=0$, and since $x>0$,
\[x=\dfrac{-40+\sqrt{1600+1144}}{22}=\dfrac{-20+7\sqrt{14}}{11}\approx0.563.\]
Answer: $\boxed{\dfrac{7\sqrt{14}-20}{11}}$.
