From the third equation, $z=4-x-y$. Substituting into the first two:
\[3x+4y-(4-x-y)=5\iff 4x+5y=9,\qquad 2x+y-4(4-x-y)=-5\iff 6x+5y=11 .\]
Subtracting, $2x=2$, so $x=1$, then $y=1$ and $z=2$. The solution is $\boxed{(x,y,z)=(1,1,2)}$. (Check: $3+4-2=5$, $2+1-8=-5$, $1+1+2=4$.)
