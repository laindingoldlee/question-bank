Place $C=(0,0)$, $A=(t,0)$ with $t>0$, and $B=(12,12)$. The condition $AB=13$ gives $(t-12)^2+144=169$, so $t=7$ or $17$. The area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot t\cdot12=6t$, giving $42$ and $102$. The difference is $\boxed{60}$.

\end{asy}
