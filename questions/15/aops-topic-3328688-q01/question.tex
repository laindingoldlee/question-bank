Let $p$ be an odd prime and define $f(n)=\sqrt n$ if $n$ is a perfect square, and $f(n)=n+p$ otherwise. Suppose an orbit returns to its starting value after finitely many steps, never visits a multiple of $p$, and has $k$ square-root steps. Let $r_1,\ldots,r_k$ be the positive integers immediately after those square-root steps in cyclic order. Prove $r_1^{2^k-1}\equiv1\pmod p$, and deduce the necessary congruences for $k=1,2$.
