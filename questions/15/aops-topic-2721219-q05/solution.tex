Since $1024=4\cdot4^4$, the Sophie Germain identity $a^4+4b^4=\left(a^2+2b^2+2ab\right)\left(a^2+2b^2-2ab\right)$ with $a=51$, $b=4$ gives
\[51^4+1024=(2601+32+408)(2601+32-408)=3041\cdot2225.\]
Here $2225=5^2\cdot89$, and $3041$ is prime (it has no prime factor up to $\sqrt{3041}<56$). So $51^4+1024=5^2\cdot89\cdot3041$ has $\boxed{3}$ distinct prime factors.
