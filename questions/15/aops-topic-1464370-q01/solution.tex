Split each term: $k(k+n)=k^2+nk$. Using $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^mk^2=\dfrac{m(m+1)(2m+1)}{6}$ and $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^mk=\dfrac{m(m+1)}{2}$,
\[
\sum_{k=1}^mk(k+n)=\dfrac{m(m+1)(2m+1)}{6}+n\cdot\dfrac{m(m+1)}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{m(m+1)(2m+1+3n)}{6}} .
\]
For $n=1$ this is $\dfrac{m(m+1)(2m+4)}{6}=\dfrac{m(m+1)(m+2)}{3}$, the formula found in the post.
