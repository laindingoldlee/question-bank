Becky writes some numbers on a blackboard and repeatedly applies this rule: she picks any number on the board that is greater than $1$, erases it, and replaces it with the list of all its proper divisors (positive divisors other than the number itself). For example, a $6$ is replaced by $1,2,3$. The experiment ends when only $1$s remain. If Becky starts with just the number $108$, how many $1$s are on the board at the end?
