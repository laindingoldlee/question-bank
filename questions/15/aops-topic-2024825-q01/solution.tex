Each number on the board is processed independently of the others, so let $f(n)$ be the number of $1$s eventually produced by a single $n$. Then $f(1)=1$ and, for $n>1$,
\[
f(n)=\sum_{d\mid n,\ d<n}f(d),
\]
regardless of the order in which Becky chooses numbers.

Now $108=2^2\cdot3^3$, and all divisors have the form $2^a3^b$. Building up:
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
 & b=0 & b=1 & b=2 & b=3\\\hline
a=0 & f(1)=1 & f(3)=1 & f(9)=2 & f(27)=4\\
a=1 & f(2)=1 & f(6)=3 & f(18)=8 & f(54)=20\\
a=2 & f(4)=2 & f(12)=8 & f(36)=26 & f(108)=?
\end{array}
\]
For instance $f(18)=f(1)+f(2)+f(3)+f(6)+f(9)=1+1+1+3+2=8$ and $f(36)=f(1)+f(2)+f(3)+f(4)+f(6)+f(9)+f(12)+f(18)=26$. Finally, summing $f$ over all entries of the table except $108$ itself,
\[
f(108)=1+1+2+4+1+3+8+20+2+8+26=\boxed{76}.
\]
