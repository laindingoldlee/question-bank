$240=2^4\cdot3\cdot5$, $600=2^3\cdot3\cdot5^2$, $750=2\cdot3\cdot5^3$. Pairwise gcds: $\gcd(240,600)=120$ ($16$ divisors), $\gcd(240,750)=30$ ($8$), $\gcd(600,750)=150$ ($12$), and $\gcd$ of all three is $30$ ($8$). Divisors of exactly two: $(16-8)+(8-8)+(12-8)=\boxed{12}$.
