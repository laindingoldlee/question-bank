A student argues: ``Perpendicular lines have slopes whose product is $-1$. A vertical line (undefined slope) is perpendicular to a horizontal line (slope $0$). So undefined times $0$ is $-1$; but anything times $0$ is $0$, so $0=-1$.'' Find the error.
