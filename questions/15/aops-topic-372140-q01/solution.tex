$180=2^2\cdot3^2\cdot5$. A divisor $2^a3^b5^c$ is even when $a\ge1$, so $a\in\{1,2\}$, $b\in\{0,1,2\}$, $c\in\{0,1\}$, giving $2\cdot3\cdot2=\boxed{12}$ even divisors.
