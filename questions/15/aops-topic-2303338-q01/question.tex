A forum thread has $410$ posts. Then person A posts one sentence split over $7$ posts, person B adds $2$ spam posts, and the original poster adds $3$ posts telling them to stop. Finally a moderator deletes $\dfrac{\sqrt[3]{19683}-1}{2}$ posts. How many posts $n$ remain in the thread?
