Let $\overline{d_kd_{k-1}\ldots d_1d_0}$ be a positive integer divisible by $11$ (leading digit $d_k\ne0$). Prove that the number $\overline{d_0d_1\ldots d_{k-1}d_k}$ obtained by reading its digits backwards (leading zeros, if any, are dropped) is also divisible by $11$.
