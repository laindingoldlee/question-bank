Let $N=\sum_{i=0}^{k}d_i10^i$ and let $R=\sum_{i=0}^{k}d_i10^{k-i}$ be the reversed number (dropping leading zeros does not change its value). Since $10\equiv-1\pmod{11}$,
\[R\equiv\sum_{i=0}^{k}d_i(-1)^{k-i}=(-1)^k\sum_{i=0}^{k}d_i(-1)^{i}\equiv(-1)^kN\pmod{11}.\]
So if $11\mid N$, then $11\mid R$. $\blacksquare$ (For example, $11\mid 2\,893=11\cdot263$ and $3\,982=11\cdot362$.)
