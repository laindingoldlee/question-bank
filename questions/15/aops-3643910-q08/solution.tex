Assume blocks are aligned with the box. Colour unit cells by the parity of x+y, independent of height. Every $2\times2\times1$ block has two cells of each colour in any axis-aligned orientation. The $7\times7\times4$ box has a colour excess of four, so at least four cells must remain empty. Thus at most $\dfrac{(196-4)}{4}=48$ blocks fit. To attain this, remove a corner column of height four. Tile the remaining $7\times7$ floor by 24 dominoes: a $6\times7$ rectangle plus a $1\times6$ strip. Above each domino place two blocks of dimensions $1\times2\times2$. This gives $\boxed{48}$.

\end{asy}
