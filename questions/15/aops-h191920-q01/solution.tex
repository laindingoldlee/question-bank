The operations $x\mapsto3x$ and $x\mapsto3x+1$ append a ternary digit $0$ or $1$. Starting from zero, they generate exactly the nonnegative integers whose ternary digits are all $0$ or $1$.

Every choice of seven such digits gives a number at most
\[
1111111_3=1+3+\cdots+3^6=\dfrac{3^7-1}{2}=1093<2008.
\]
Any allowed number requiring eight digits is at least $3^7=2187>2008$. Leading zeros therefore give a one-to-one encoding by seven binary choices, including zero. The count is $\boxed{2^7=128}$.
