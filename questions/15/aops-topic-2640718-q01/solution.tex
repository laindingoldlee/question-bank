Let $O$ be the centre, the midpoint of $\overline{AB}$. The centroid is $G=\dfrac{A+B+C}{3}$, and $A+B=2O$, so
\[G=O+\dfrac13(C-O).\]
So $G$ is the image of $C$ under the dilation with centre $O$ and ratio $\dfrac13$, and it traces the circle with centre $O$ and radius $\dfrac{12}{3}=4$, missing the two points that correspond to $C=A$ and $C=B$ (the open dots). The bounded area is $16\pi\approx50.27$, so the answer is $\boxed{50}$.
