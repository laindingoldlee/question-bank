The area of a rectangle is $2x^2+x-3$ square units.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Explain why the area alone does not determine the perimeter.
\item[(b)] Assuming that the side lengths are polynomials in $x$ with integer coefficients and positive leading coefficients (the intended reading of such textbook questions), find the side lengths, the perimeter, and the values of $x$ for which the rectangle exists.
\end{enumerate}
