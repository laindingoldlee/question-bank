Glue the twins into one block $T$ (the twins can be ordered inside it in $2$ ways). Now arrange $6$ units in a row: $T$, the other boy $B$, and the $4$ girls. There are $6!=720$ arrangements, and in $2\cdot5!=240$ of them $B$ is next to $T$ (glue $B$ and $T$ in either order). So $720-240=480$ arrangements keep $B$ away from both twins (if $B$ is not next to the block, he is next to neither twin). With the $2$ orders of the twins, the answer is
\[
480\cdot2=\boxed{960}.
\]
