Equality occurs with two A's or two N's; there is only one B. The probability is $\dfrac{3}{6}\dfrac{2}{5}+\dfrac{2}{6}\dfrac{1}{5}=\dfrac{6}{30}+\dfrac{2}{30}=\boxed{\dfrac{4}{15}}$. A uniform shuffle of labelled copies induces a uniform distribution on distinct letter arrangements, so this calculation also applies to uniformly selected distinct arrangements.
