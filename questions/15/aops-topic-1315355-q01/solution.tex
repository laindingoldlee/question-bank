The truncations $3,\ 3+\dfrac{1}{3},\dots$ converge (as for every continued fraction with positive integer terms), and the limit $x>3$ satisfies $x=3+\dfrac{1}{x}$, i.e. $x^2-3x-1=0$. Taking the positive root, $x=\boxed{\dfrac{3+\sqrt{13}}{2}}\approx3.303$.
