The weights of goats on a farm are normally distributed with mean $60$ kg and standard deviation $10$ kg. A truck can carry at most $650$ kg. If $10$ goats are selected at random (independently), what is the probability that their total weight exceeds the truck's limit? Give the answer to three decimal places.
