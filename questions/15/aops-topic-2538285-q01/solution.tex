A sum of independent normal random variables is normal; its mean is the sum of the means and its variance is the sum of the variances. So the total weight $T$ is normal with
\[\text{mean }10\cdot60=600\text{ kg},\qquad\text{variance }10\cdot10^2=1000,\qquad\text{standard deviation }\sqrt{1000}\approx31.62\text{ kg}.\]
Standardising,
\[P(T>650)=P\left(Z>\dfrac{650-600}{\sqrt{1000}}\right)=P(Z>1.581)\approx1-0.9431=\boxed{0.057}.\]
(Note that the standard deviation of the total is $10\sqrt{10}$, not $10\cdot10=100$.)
