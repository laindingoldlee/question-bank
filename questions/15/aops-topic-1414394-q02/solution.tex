Since $ab=1$, we have $\dfrac{1}{a}=b$ and $\dfrac{1}{b}=a$, so
\[
\dfrac{b-1}{a}+\dfrac{a-1}{b}=b(b-1)+a(a-1)=a^2+b^2-(a+b)=s^2-2-s,
\]
where $s=a+b$. The condition becomes $s^2-s-2=40$, i.e.\ $(s-7)(s+6)=0$. Both values occur for real $a,b$: they are the roots of $t^2-st+1=0$, whose discriminant $s^2-4$ is $45$ or $32$, positive in both cases. So the minimum of $a+b$ is $\boxed{-6}$ (with $a,b=-3\pm2\sqrt2$).
