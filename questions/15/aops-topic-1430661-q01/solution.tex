Use Burnside's lemma: the number of colourings up to rotation is the average, over the $24$ rotations of the cube, of the number of colourings fixed by each rotation. A rotation fixes a colouring exactly when each cycle of faces under that rotation is one colour, so it fixes $n^{c}$ colourings, where $c$ is its number of face cycles.
\[
\begin{array}{l|c|c}
\text{rotation} & \text{how many} & \text{face cycles}\\ \hline
\text{identity} & 1 & 6\\
90^\circ\text{ or }270^\circ\text{ about a face axis} & 6 & 3\\
180^\circ\text{ about a face axis} & 3 & 4\\
120^\circ\text{ or }240^\circ\text{ about a vertex axis} & 8 & 2\\
180^\circ\text{ about an edge axis} & 6 & 3
\end{array}
\]
Hence the number of colourings is
\[
\dfrac{n^6+3n^4+12n^3+8n^2}{24}.
\]
For $n=3$: $\dfrac{729+243+324+72}{24}=\dfrac{1368}{24}=\boxed{57}$.
