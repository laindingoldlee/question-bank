The two events are disjoint. There are $ 6 $ all-equal outcomes and $ 6\cdot5\cdot4\cdot3\cdot2=720 $ all-distinct outcomes among $ 6^5=7776 $ equally likely sequences. The percentage is $ 100\cdot\dfrac{726}{7776}\approx9.33642 $, hence $\boxed{9.3\%}$.
