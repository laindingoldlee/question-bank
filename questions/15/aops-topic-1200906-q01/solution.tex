$\sqrt{x^4+x^2}=\sqrt{x^2\left(x^2+1\right)}=\sqrt{x^2}\cdot\sqrt{x^2+1}=\boxed{|x|\sqrt{x^2+1}}$. The absolute value is needed: for $x=-1$ the left side is $\sqrt2$, not $-\sqrt2$.
