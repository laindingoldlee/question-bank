Set $t=\dfrac{p}{q}$ and $N=n^2+1$. Rearranging gives $2Nt^2-(4N-2)t+N=0$, whose roots are
\[
t=\dfrac{2N-1\pm\sqrt{2n^4-1}}{2N}.
\]
A rational square root of an integer must be an integer. Thus $2n^4-1$ must be a square. For $n=2,\ldots,12$ these values are
\[
31,161,511,1249,2591,4801,8191,13121,19999,29281,41471,
\]
strictly between consecutive squares with lower square roots
$5,12,22,35,50,69,90,114,141,171,203$, respectively. At $n=13$, $2n^4-1=57121=239^2$ and one root is $t=\dfrac{5}{17}$. Therefore $(p,q)=(5,17)$ works, and the least $n$ is $\boxed{13}$.
