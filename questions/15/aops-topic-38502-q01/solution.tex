The first part takes $\dfrac{120}{40}=3$ hours and the second part $\dfrac{120}{60}=2$ hours. Average speed is total distance divided by total time:
\[\dfrac{240}{3+2}=\boxed{48}\ \text{miles per hour}.\]
(Not $50$: the car spends more time at the slower speed. For equal distances the average speed is the harmonic mean $\dfrac{2\cdot40\cdot60}{40+60}=48$.)
