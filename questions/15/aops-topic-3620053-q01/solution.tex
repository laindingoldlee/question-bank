Write $0\le a<b<c$ and $a+b+c=abc$. If $a=0$, the positive sum cannot be zero. If $a=1$, rearrangement gives $(b-1)(c-1)=2$, so $(b,c)=(2,3)$. If $a\ge2$, then $b\ge3,c\ge4$, and $abc\ge6c>3c\ge a+b+c$, impossible. Thus the unique triple is $\boxed{\{1,2,3\}}$.
