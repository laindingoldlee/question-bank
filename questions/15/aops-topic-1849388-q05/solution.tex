Let the other sides be $x$ and $3x$ with $x$ a positive integer. The triangle inequality requires
\[
3x<15+x\iff x<7.5,\qquad 15<x+3x\iff x>3.75,
\]
(and $x+15>3x$ is the first condition). So $4\le x\le7$, and the perimeter $15+4x$ is largest for $x=7$: sides $7$, $15$, $21$, perimeter $\boxed{43}$. This triangle exists since $7+15=22>21$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0),C=(21,0);
real x=(49-225+441)/42;pair A=(x,sqrt(49-x^2));
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(1));
label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$A$",A,N);
label("$21$",(B+C)/2,S);label("$7$",(A+B)/2,NW);label("$15$",(A+C)/2,NE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
