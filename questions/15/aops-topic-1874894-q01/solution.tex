Since $\dfrac{1}{k(k+1)}=\dfrac{1}{k}-\dfrac{1}{k+1}$, the sum telescopes:
\[
\left(1-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)+\left(\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{3}\right)+\dots+\left(\dfrac{1}{6}-\dfrac{1}{7}\right)=1-\dfrac{1}{7}=\boxed{\dfrac{6}{7}}.
\]
