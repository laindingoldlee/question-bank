A yellow cab charges $50$ cents for the first half-mile and $15$ cents for each quarter-mile after that. A blue cab charges $95$ cents for the first half-mile and $25$ cents for each half-mile after that. For what distance (a whole number of half-miles) do the two cabs charge the same?
