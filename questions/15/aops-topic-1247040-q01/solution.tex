For a distance of $d$ miles ($d\ge\dfrac{1}{2}$, a multiple of $\dfrac{1}{2}$), the yellow cab charges $50+60\left(d-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)$ cents and the blue cab $95+50\left(d-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)$ cents. Setting them equal: $10\left(d-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)=45$, so $d=\boxed{5}$ miles (both charge $\$3.20$).
