June has $30$ days, so from $6{:}00$ a.m.\ June $1$ to $6{:}00$ a.m.\ July $1$ is $30\cdot24=720$ hours, that is $60$ periods of $12$ hours. In that time the clock advances $60\cdot19=1140$ hours. A $12$-hour clock face repeats every $12$ hours, and $1140=12\cdot95$, so the hands return exactly to where they started. The clock shows $\boxed{6{:}00}$.
