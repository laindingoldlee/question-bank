From $2\pi r=4s$ we get $\dfrac rs=\dfrac2\pi\approx0.637$, which is $\boxed{0.6}$ to the nearest tenth.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=pi/2;
draw(circle((0,0),1),linewidth(0.8));draw((0,0)--(1,0));dot((0,0));label("$r$",(0.5,0),N);
draw(shift(1.6,-s/2)*scale(s)*unitsquare,linewidth(0.8));label("$s$",(1.6+s/2,-s/2),S);
\end{asy}
