Circles $C_1$ and $C_2$ both have radius $r$, are tangent to the same line $L$, and lie on the same side of $L$; the distance between their centres is $x$. A third circle $C_3$, of radius $s$, is externally tangent to both $C_1$ and $C_2$ and rests on top of them (its centre is farther from $L$ than the centres of $C_1$ and $C_2$). Find the distance $h$ from the centre of $C_3$ to $L$ in terms of $x$, $r$ and $s$. (Assume $x\le2(r+s)$, so that such a circle exists.)
