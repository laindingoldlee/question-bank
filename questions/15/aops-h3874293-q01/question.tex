Pyro and Pyro's $66$ friends each eat at least one of three foods: kimchi, rice, and chicken. The same number of people eat each of the three foods. For any two of these foods, the number of people who eat both is exactly one-third of the number of people who eat the first food. (For example, if $30$ people eat kimchi, then exactly $10$ people eat both kimchi and rice, vice versa) If exactly $3$ people eat all three foods, how many people eat kimchi?
