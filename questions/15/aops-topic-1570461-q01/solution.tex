By the AM-GM inequality (equivalently, $(a-b)^2\ge0$),
\[
(a+b)^2\ge4ab .
\]
Combining this with the hypothesis $ab>a+b$ gives
\[
(a+b)^2\ge4ab>4(a+b).
\]
Since $a+b>0$, we may divide by $a+b$ and obtain $a+b>4$. $\blacksquare$

(The bound is sharp: for $a=b=2+\varepsilon$ with small $\varepsilon>0$ we have $ab>a+b$ and $a+b$ just above $4$; for $a=b=2$ we get $ab=a+b=4$.)
