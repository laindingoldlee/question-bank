There are $x\ge2$ girls standing in a circle. The first girl throws a ball clockwise, skipping the next $n$ girls ($n\ge0$), to the girl after them. Each girl who catches the ball throws it in the same way, always skipping $n$ girls. Find, in terms of $x$ and $n$, the number $y$ of throws after which the ball first returns to the first girl.
