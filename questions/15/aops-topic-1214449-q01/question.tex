Two circles are internally tangent at point $A$. Segment $AC$ is a diameter of the larger circle and segment $AB$, lying along $AC$, is a diameter of the smaller circle. If $AC:AB=4$, the region inside the larger circle but outside the smaller circle is shaded. What fraction of the area of the larger circle is shaded?

$\textbf{(A)}\ \dfrac{4}{5}\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ \dfrac{7}{8}\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ \dfrac{3}{4}\qquad\textbf{(D)}\ \dfrac{5}{6}\qquad\textbf{(E)}\ \dfrac{15}{16}$
