A yacht travels between ports $X$ and $Y$, which are $240$ miles apart along a river. Against the current the yacht travels at $18$ mph, and with the current at $26$ mph. A motorboat moves at $20$ mph in still water. How long does the motorboat take to make a round trip between $X$ and $Y$?
