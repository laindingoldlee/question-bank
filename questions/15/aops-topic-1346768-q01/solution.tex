Let the yacht's still-water speed be $v$ and the current $c$. Then $v+c=26$ and $v-c=18$, so $v=22$ and $c=4$ mph.

The motorboat (still-water speed $20$ mph) therefore travels at $20+4=24$ mph with the current and $20-4=16$ mph against it. The round trip takes
\[
\dfrac{240}{24}+\dfrac{240}{16}=10+15=\boxed{25\text{ hours}}.
\]
(Note that this is more than $\dfrac{480}{20}=24$ hours: the current slows the boat for longer than it speeds it up.)
