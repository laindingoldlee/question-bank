Let $x$, $y$, $a$, $b$ be real numbers. Prove that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] $|x|\le b$ if and only if $-b\le x\le b$;
\item[(ii)] $\big||a|-|b|\big|\le|a-b|$;
\item[(iii)] $x^2+xy+y^2\ge0$;
\item[(iv)] if $x>0$ and $y>0$, then $x^2-xy+y^2>0$.
\end{enumerate}
