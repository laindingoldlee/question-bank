(i) If $x\ge0$ then $|x|=x$, and $|x|\le b$ means $x\le b$; together with $x\ge0\ge-x$ this is the same as $-b\le x\le b$ (if $x\le b$ then $b\ge0$, so $-b\le0\le x$; conversely $-b\le x\le b$ gives $x\le b$). If $x<0$ then $|x|=-x$, and $-x\le b$ means $x\ge-b$; with $x<0$ this is the same as $-b\le x\le b$ (here $-b\le x<0$ forces $b>0>x$). In both cases the two statements are equivalent. (If $b<0$, both are false.)

(ii) By the triangle inequality, $|a|=|(a-b)+b|\le|a-b|+|b|$, so $|a|-|b|\le|a-b|$. Swapping $a$ and $b$, $|b|-|a|\le|b-a|=|a-b|$. So $-|a-b|\le|a|-|b|\le|a-b|$, which by (i) means $\big||a|-|b|\big|\le|a-b|$.

(iii) Complete the square:
\[
x^2+xy+y^2=\left(x+\dfrac {y}{2}\right)^2+\dfrac{3y^2}{4}\ge0 ,
\]
as a sum of two squares (with equality only for $x=y=0$).

(iv) Similarly
\[
x^2-xy+y^2=\left(x-\dfrac {y}{2}\right)^2+\dfrac{3y^2}{4}\ge\dfrac{3y^2}{4}>0 ,
\]
since $y>0$. (In fact $x^2-xy+y^2>0$ whenever $x$ and $y$ are not both zero.) $\blacksquare$
