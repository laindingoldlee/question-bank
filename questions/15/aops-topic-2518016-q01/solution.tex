Work from the innermost parentheses outwards:
\[2-2022=-2020,\qquad 20-(-2020)=2040,\qquad 202-2040=-1838,\]
so the expression is
\[2022-(-1838)-22=3860-22=3838.\]
The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(D)}~3838}$.
