The expected number of posts per day is
\[0.2\cdot20+\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot15+0.3\cdot10+0.1\cdot5=4+5+3+0.5=12.5.\]
From June $1$ to December $31$ there are $30+31+31+30+31+30+31=214$ days, so by linearity of expectation the expected total is $5629+214\cdot12.5=5629+2675=\boxed{8304}$.
