A forum user makes $20$ posts on $20\%$ of days, $15$ posts on $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of days, $10$ posts on $30\%$ of days, $5$ posts on $10\%$ of days, and no posts on the remaining days, independently from day to day. He has $5629$ posts at the end of May $31$, $2016$. What is the expected number of posts he will have at the end of December $31$, $2016$? Round to the nearest integer.
