Write everything as a power of $2$:
\[
32^2\cdot16^3\cdot8^4\cdot4^5=2^{10}\cdot2^{12}\cdot2^{12}\cdot2^{10}=2^{44},
\]
so the square root is $2^{22}$. Since $16^5=2^{20}$, the expression equals
\[
\dfrac{2^{22}}{2^{20}}=2^2=\boxed{4}.
\]
