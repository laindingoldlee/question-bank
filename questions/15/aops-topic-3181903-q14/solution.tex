Divisibility by $14$ requires an even final digit and divisibility by $7$. The final digit is even with probability $\dfrac{4}{7}$. For any fixed choices of all digits except the penultimate one, the seven choices for that digit give all residues modulo $7$, since its coefficient is $10$, invertible modulo $7$. Therefore the conditional probability of divisibility by $7$ is always $\dfrac{1}{7}$, even after requiring an even last digit. The answer is $m+n=4+49=\boxed{53}$.
