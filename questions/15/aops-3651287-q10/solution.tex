Expand $(a+\sqrt b)^3=a^3+3ab+(3a^2+b)\sqrt b$. Write $b=3c^2$ with positive integer $c$, by comparing square-free radical parts. Then $a(a^2+9c^2)=305=5\cdot61$, so $a\in\{1,5,61,305\}$. Since $a^3\le305$, only 1 or 5 is possible. At $a=1$, $c^2=\dfrac{304}{9}$ is not an integer. At $a=5$, $c^2=4$, giving $b=12$; the radical coefficient is $2(75+12)=174$. Hence $\boxed{100a+b=512}$.
