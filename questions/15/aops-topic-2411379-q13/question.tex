The geometric mean of two positive numbers $a$ and $b$ is $\sqrt{ab}$. The third term of an arithmetic sequence of positive numbers, in which the common difference is not $0$, is the geometric mean of the first and eleventh terms. What is the ratio of the second term to the first term of the sequence? Express your answer as a common fraction.
