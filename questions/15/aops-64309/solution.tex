The circumference is $3+4+5=12$, giving radius $R = \dfrac{6}{\pi}$. The three central angles are $\dfrac{\pi}{2} , \dfrac{2 \pi}{3} , \dfrac{5 \pi}{6}$, all less than $\pi$, so the centre lies inside the triangle. Sum the three centre-based triangle areas: $K = (\dfrac{R^2}{2}) (\sin (\dfrac{\pi}{2}) + \sin (\dfrac{2 \pi}{3}) + \sin (\dfrac{5 \pi}{6})) = \boxed{\dfrac{9 (3+\sqrt3)}{\pi^2}}$.
