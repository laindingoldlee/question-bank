Carol and David see disjoint sets of trees; together they see every tree. It is common knowledge that the total is either $18$ or $20$, that counts are nonnegative integers, and that both reason perfectly. Carol sees $12$ trees and David sees $8$, but neither knows the other's count. Each day Carol first announces the total if she can deduce it with certainty, otherwise passes publicly; David then does the same. On which turn is the total first determined?
