Let $F_1=F_2=1$ and $F_{k+2}=F_{k+1}+F_k$ be the Fibonacci numbers, and let $n$ be a positive integer. Prove that the sequence of remainders of $F_1,F_2,F_3,\dots$ on division by $n$ is periodic from the start: there is a positive integer $p$ with $F_{k+p}\equiv F_k\pmod n$ for every $k\ge1$.
