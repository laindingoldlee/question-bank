If the number is $x$, then $\sqrt{10(x+3)+19}-1=x$, i.e. $\sqrt{10x+49}=x+1$. This needs $x\ge-1$, and squaring gives $10x+49=x^2+2x+1$, i.e. $x^2-8x-48=(x-12)(x+4)=0$. The root $x=-4$ is extraneous ($\sqrt9-1=2\ne-4$), and $x=12$ works ($\sqrt{169}-1=12$). Her number was $\boxed{12}$.
