A number starting with nine is possible, so maximize that leading digit. If the second digit is $b$, the last two sum to $13-b$. The divisor condition says $b\mid13-b$, hence $b\mid13$. As a nonzero digit, this forces $b=1$. The remaining sum is twelve, maximized lexicographically by digits nine and three. Thus the largest number is $\boxed{9193}$.
