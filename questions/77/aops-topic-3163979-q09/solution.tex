Put $a=|m|,b=|n|$. If $ab$ is even, exactly half the squares are black, so $ab=64$. Its seven ordered positive factor pairs each allow all four sign choices, giving 28. If $ab$ is odd, both sides are odd and the black count is either $\dfrac{ab+1}{2}$ or $\dfrac{ab-1}{2}$. Exactly two of the four quadrants have each case. Thus $ab=63$ or 65 contributes $2(\tau(63)+\tau(65))=2(6+4)=20$. Total $\boxed{48}$. Reversing all colors merely exchanges the two quadrant classes and leaves the count unchanged.
