Unit lattice squares are checkerboard-colored, with $[0,1]\times[0,1]$ black. For nonzero integers $m,n$, let the axis-aligned rectangle have opposite vertices $(0,0)$ and $(m,n)$. How many ordered pairs $(m,n)$ make it contain exactly 32 black squares?
