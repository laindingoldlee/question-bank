We need $x=\dfrac{1}{x}+2$ with $x\ne0$, i.e. $x^2-2x-1=0$. Its discriminant is $8>0$, so it has two real roots, $1\pm\sqrt2$, and neither is $0$. By Vieta's formulas their sum is $\boxed{2}$.
