A circle with centre $O$ has radius $6$ cm. Chords $AB$ and $CD$ are perpendicular, have equal length, and meet at $E$ inside the circle, with $E\ne O$. Two squares each have a vertex at $E$ and two sides lying along the chords. They lie in opposite angles at $E$: the larger square lies in the angle facing $O$, and each square has its vertex opposite $E$ on the circle. If the larger square has three times the area of the smaller one, find $AB$ to the nearest hundredth of a centimetre.
