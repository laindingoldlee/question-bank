Translate the shorter base along the longer to form a triangle whose sides are $15,20,26-1=25$ and whose height equals the trapezoid height. This is a right triangle, of area $150$. Taking its side $25$ as base gives height $12$. Thus the trapezoid area is $\dfrac{1+26}{2}\cdot12=\boxed{162}$.
