Each $2\times2$ block must contain at least two coloured cells: otherwise three uncoloured cells form an L. Partition the board into sixteen disjoint $2\times2$ blocks to obtain a lower bound of $32$. Colouring the usual $32$ black chessboard cells attains it, because every three-cell L contains both chessboard colours. Thus the minimum is $\boxed{32}$.

\end{asy}
