Factor: $(a-b)(a+b)=2016$. The numbers $a-b$ and $a+b$ have the same parity (they differ by $2b$), and their product is even, so both are even. Write $a-b=2u$ and $a+b=2v$ with integers $u,v$; then
\[
uv=\dfrac{2016}{4}=504,\qquad a=u+v,\quad b=v-u .
\]
Conversely, every pair of integers $(u,v)$ with $uv=504$ gives integers $a,b$ with $a^2-b^2=4uv=2016$, and different $(u,v)$ give different $(a,b)$.

Since $504=2^3\cdot3^2\cdot7$ has $(3+1)(2+1)(1+1)=24$ positive divisors, there are $24$ ordered pairs $(u,v)$ of positive integers and $24$ of negative integers with $uv=504$. Hence there are $\boxed{48}$ ordered pairs $(a,b)$.
