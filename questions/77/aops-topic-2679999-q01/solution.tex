The palindrome is $\overline{47xy74}$, so it is between $470000$ and $479999$, ends in $4$, and is $\equiv74\pmod{100}$, hence $\equiv2\pmod4$.
\begin{itemize}
\item Five or more consecutive integers include a multiple of $5$, so the product ends in $0$ or $5$: impossible.
\item Four consecutive integers include two consecutive even numbers, one a multiple of $4$, so the product is divisible by $8$: impossible.
\item Two consecutive integers: $n(n+1)$ ends in $0$, $2$ or $6$ (check the ten last digits of $n$), never $4$: impossible.
\item Three consecutive integers: $(n-1)n(n+1)=n^3-n$. For $n\le77$ this is less than $77^3=456533<470000$; for $n\ge79$ it is at least $79^3-79=492960>479999$. So $n=78$, and $77\cdot78\cdot79=474474$, which is indeed a palindrome.
\end{itemize}
The integers are $77,78,79$, with sum $\boxed{234}$.
