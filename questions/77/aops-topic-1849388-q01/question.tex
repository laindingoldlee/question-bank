Alex, Bob and Ciara are friends of different ages. Exactly one of the following statements is true:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(I)] Bob is the oldest.
\item[(II)] Alex is not the oldest.
\item[(III)] Ciara is not the youngest.
\end{enumerate}
List the friends from oldest to youngest.
