Put $u=ab>1$. The first equation becomes $u+\dfrac{1}{u}=2.5$, giving $u=2$ rather than its reciprocal. Similarly $bc=4$ and $ca=5$. Therefore $a^2=\dfrac{2\cdot5}{4}=\dfrac{5}{2}$, $b^2=\dfrac{2\cdot4}{5}=\dfrac{8}{5}$, and $c^2=\dfrac{4\cdot5}{2}=10$. Their positive square roots exceed one and satisfy the data. The answer is $10(\dfrac{5}{2}+\dfrac{8}{5}+10)=\boxed{141}$.
