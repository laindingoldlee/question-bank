From $(0,1)$: $a+b=1$. From $(1,0)$: $\dfrac ae+b=0$. Subtracting, $a\left(1-\dfrac1e\right)=1$, so
\[a=\boxed{\dfrac{e}{e-1}},\qquad b=1-a=\boxed{-\dfrac{1}{e-1}}.\]
