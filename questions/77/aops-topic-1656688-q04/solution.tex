Since $A\cap B\subseteq A\subseteq A\cup B$,
\[
P(A\cap B)\le P(A\cup B)=\dfrac{1}{4} .
\]
Equality holds when $A=B$ is an event of probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$ (then $A\cap B=A\cup B=A$). The maximum is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{4}}$.
