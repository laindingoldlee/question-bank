Let $k=s(n)$, so $n=59k$ with positive integer $k$. For $k=1,\ldots,9$, the numbers $59k$ are $59,118,177,236,295,354,413,472,531$, whose digit sums are $14,10,15,11,16,12,8,13,9$. The first match $s(59k)=k$ is $k=9$, giving $\boxed{n=531}$. Every smaller candidate would correspond to $k<9$, all of which were checked.
