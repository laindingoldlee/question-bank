Place $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $C=(4,4)$, $D=(0,4)$, with the semicircle below $AB$. Its centre is $(2,0)$, its radius is $2$, and $X=(2,-2)$.

The centre $E$ is equidistant from $C$ and $D$, so it lies on the line $x=2$; write $E=(2,k)$. A circle through $X$ that is tangent to the semicircle at $X$ has its centre on the line joining $X$ to the semicircle's centre, which is again $x=2$, so this is consistent. The radius is $r=EX=k+2$, and $ED=r$ gives
\[
2^2+(4-k)^2=(k+2)^2\ \Longrightarrow\ 4+16-8k=4k+4\ \Longrightarrow\ k=\dfrac{4}{3} .
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(4,0),C=(4,4),D=(0,4),X=(2,-2),O=(2,4/3);
real R=10/3;
draw(circle(O,R),blue+linewidth(0.8));
draw(arc((2,0),2,180,360),linewidth(0.8));
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(O--D,dashed);draw(O--X,dashed);
dot(O);dot(X);
label("$A$",A,dir(160));label("$B$",B,dir(20));label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);
label("$X$",X,S);label("$E$",O,dir(-20));
label("$r$",(O+D)/2+0.45*(0.8,0.6));label("$4$",(A+D)/2,E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

So $E=\left(2,\dfrac{4}{3}\right)$ and $r=\dfrac{4}{3}+2=\boxed{\dfrac{10}{3}}$. The semicircle (radius $2$) lies inside the circle and touches it at $X$, since the distance between the centres, $\dfrac{4}{3}$, equals $\dfrac{10}{3}-2$.
