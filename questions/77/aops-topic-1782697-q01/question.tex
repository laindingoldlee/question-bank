In an acute triangle $ABC$, a circle of radius $66$ has its diameter on side $BC$ and touches sides $AB$ and $AC$. Similarly, a circle of radius $55$ has its diameter on side $CA$ and touches $AB$ and $BC$, and a circle of radius $60$ has its diameter on side $AB$ and touches $BC$ and $CA$. What is the radius of the incircle of triangle $ABC$?
