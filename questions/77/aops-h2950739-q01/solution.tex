Put $\alpha=\angle DAE=\angle EAB$. Parallelism gives $\angle AEB=\alpha$, so $AB=BE=\ell$ and $\angle ABE=180^\circ-2\alpha$. Consequently $AE=2\ell\cos\alpha$.

Equal tangent lengths from $B$ give
\[
BM=BP=\ell-\dfrac{AE}{2}=\ell(1-\cos\alpha).
\]
In isosceles triangle $BMP$, the included angle at $B$ is $180^\circ-2\alpha$, so
\[
MP=2\ell(1-\cos\alpha)\cos\alpha.
\]
The hypothesis $MP=\dfrac{\ell}{2}$ becomes $4c(1-c)=1$, where $c=\cos\alpha$. Thus $(2c-1)^2=0$, giving $c=\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\boxed{\alpha=60^\circ}$.
