Three mutually externally tangent circles have radii $2,3,3$. One further circle lies in their central gap and is externally tangent to all three. Another encloses all three and is internally tangent to them. If the sum of these two radii is $\dfrac{m}{n}$ in lowest terms, find $m+n$.
