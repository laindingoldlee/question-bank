Choose $A=(0,0)$ and $B=(2,0)$ with the hexagon above $AB$. Equal distances to $A,B$ imply $P=(1,y)$. The right-angle condition gives $(A-P)\cdot(B-P)=-1+y^2=0$, so $y=\pm1$. Interiority selects $P=(1,1)$. In the regular hexagon, $D=(2,2\sqrt3)$. Thus $DP^2=(2-1)^2+(2\sqrt3-1)^2=\boxed{14-4\sqrt3\text{ cm}^2}$.

\end{asy}
