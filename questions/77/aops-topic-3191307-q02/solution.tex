Let the counts be $q,d,n,p$. Exact change for $1,2,3,4$ requires $p\ge4$; the total value gives $p\equiv2\pmod5$, so $p\ge7$. To make $24$ cents without quarters requires at least $24$ cents in smaller coins, hence $q\le3$. If $N=q+d+n+p$, then $10N=117-15q+5n+9p\ge117-45+63=135$, so $N\ge14$. Three quarters, three dimes, one nickel, and seven pennies total $117$ cents using $14$ coins. The pennies cover $0$--$7$, the nickel extends this to $12$, successive dimes to $42$, and quarters to $117$, with no gaps. Thus the minimum is $\boxed{14}$.
