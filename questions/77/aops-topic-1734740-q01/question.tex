Two distinct points $P_1$ and $P_2$ lie in the square with vertices $(1,1)$, $(1,-1)$, $(-1,-1)$, $(-1,1)$ (boundary included). Let $a$ be the length of $\overline{P_1P_2}$ and $b$ the sum of the $y$-coordinates of the two points. Two new distinct points $P_3$ and $P_4$ are plotted so that the difference of their $y$-coordinates is $a$ and the difference of their $x$-coordinates is $b$. What is the greatest integer less than or equal to the maximum possible length of $\overline{P_3P_4}$?
