The paper covering the northern hemisphere of a globe of radius $R$ is cut off along the equator. Can it be laid flat on a table without stretching? Make this precise and prove the answer as follows. Let $N$ be the north pole and $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ points on the equator, in this order, each a quarter of the equator from the next. Distances on the hemisphere are measured along the surface (the length of the shortest path on the hemisphere). Prove that there is no way to place the hemisphere in a plane so that the straight-line distance between the images of any two points equals their distance on the surface.
