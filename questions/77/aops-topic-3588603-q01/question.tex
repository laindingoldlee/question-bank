In parallelogram $ABCD$, $E$ lies on $AB$ and $DE$ bisects $\angle ADB$. Let the lines $DE,BC$ meet at $F$, and the lines $CE,AD$ meet at $G$. Prove $BC^2=BF\cdot AG$.
