Let Charlie pay \(c\in\{0,5,\dots,55\}\) and put \(s=60-c\in\{5,10,\dots,60\}\). We count pairs \((a,b)\) with \(a+b=s\), \(0\le a\le 17\), \(0\le b\le 30\); that is, \(a\) ranges over \(\max(0,s-30)\le a\le\min(17,s)\).
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccccccccccc}
s&60&55&50&45&40&35&30&25&20&15&10&5\\\hline
\#(a,b)&0&0&0&3&8&13&18&18&18&16&11&6
\end{array}
\]
The total is \(3+8+13+18+18+18+16+11+6=\boxed{111}\).
