Alice, Bob and Charlie together pay \(60\) dollars for a puzzle cube. Each pays a whole number of dollars (possibly \(0\)). Alice can pay at most \(17\) dollars and Bob at most \(30\) dollars. Charlie can pay up to \(55\) dollars, but only in multiples of \(5\) dollars. In how many ways can they pay?
