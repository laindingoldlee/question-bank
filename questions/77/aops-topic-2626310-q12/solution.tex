Dividing by $\omega$ (a rotation and scaling, which preserves equilateral triangles), the points $1$, $\omega$ and $\lambda$ form an equilateral triangle. Its side $\overline{1\lambda}$ lies on the real axis, so its third vertex is
\[\omega=\dfrac{1+\lambda}{2}\pm\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}(\lambda-1)i.\]

\begin{asy}

dot(U);dot(L);dot(W);dot(Z);

label("$|\omega|$",(W+Z)/2,NW);
\end{asy}

Then $|\omega|^2=\dfrac{(\lambda+1)^2+3(\lambda-1)^2}{4}=\lambda^2-\lambda+1=2016^2$, and since $\lambda>1$,
\[\lambda=\dfrac{1+\sqrt{4\cdot2016^2-3}}{2}=\dfrac{1+\sqrt{16257021}}{2}.\]
Here $16257021=3\cdot11\cdot23\cdot21419$ is squarefree, so $a=1$, $b=16257021$, $c=2$ and $a+b+c=16257024=4032^2$. The remainder mod $1000$ is $\boxed{24}$.
