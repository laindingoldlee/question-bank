For some complex number $\omega$ with $|\omega|=2016$, there is a real number $\lambda>1$ such that $\omega$, $\omega^2$ and $\lambda\omega$ are the vertices of an equilateral triangle in the complex plane. Then $\lambda$ can be written in the form $\dfrac{a+\sqrt b}{c}$, where $a$, $b$ and $c$ are positive integers and $b$ is squarefree. Find the remainder when $a+b+c$ is divided by $1000$.
