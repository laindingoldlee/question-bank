Expand the product:
\[
(a+b+c)\left(\dfrac{1}{a}+\dfrac{1}{b}+\dfrac{1}{c}\right)=3+\left(\dfrac {a}{b}+\dfrac {b}{a}\right)+\left(\dfrac {b}{c}+\dfrac {c}{b}\right)+\left(\dfrac {c}{a}+\dfrac {a}{c}\right).
\]
For positive $x,y$,
\[
\dfrac {x}{y}+\dfrac {y}{x}-2=\dfrac{x^2+y^2-2xy}{xy}=\dfrac{(x-y)^2}{xy}\ge0 ,
\]
so each bracket is at least $2$. Hence the product is at least $3+2+2+2=9\ge6$. $\blacksquare$

Equality in the bound $9$ holds exactly when all three brackets equal $2$, that is, when $a=b=c$. (The bound $6$ is never attained.)
