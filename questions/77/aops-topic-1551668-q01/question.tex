Prove that for all positive real numbers $a$, $b$, $c$,
\[
(a+b+c)\left(\dfrac{1}{a}+\dfrac{1}{b}+\dfrac{1}{c}\right)\ge6 .
\]
In fact, prove the stronger bound $9$, and say when equality holds in it.
