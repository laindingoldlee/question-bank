Each of the $8$ positions independently holds one of $2$ symbols, so there are $2^8=\boxed{256}$ codes.

Sticks and stones counts \emph{how many} of each symbol are used, that is, the solutions of $a+b=8$ in nonnegative integers ($9$ of them). That ignores the \emph{order} of the symbols: for example, ``three $<$ and five $>$'' is one solution of $a+b=8$ but corresponds to $\dbinom83=56$ different codes. Indeed $\sum_{a=0}^{8}\dbinom8a=2^8$.
