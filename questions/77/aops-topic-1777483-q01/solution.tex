Let the longer side be $l$ and the shorter side $w$. Half the perimeter is $l+w$; subtracting four-fifths of the shorter side leaves $l+\dfrac {w}{5}$. By the Pythagorean theorem,
\[
l^2+w^2=\left(l+\dfrac {w}{5}\right)^2=l^2+\dfrac{2lw}{5}+\dfrac{w^2}{25},
\]
so $\dfrac{24}{25}w^2=\dfrac{2}{5}lw$ and $\dfrac {w}{l}=\dfrac{10}{24}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{12}}$. (Check: a $5$ by $12$ rectangle has diagonal $13$, and $\dfrac{34}{2}-\dfrac{4}{5}\cdot5=17-4=13$.)

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(12,0),C=(12,5),D=(0,5);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(A--C,blue+linewidth(0.8));
real s=0.6; draw(B+(-s,0)--B+(-s,s)--B+(0,s));
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);
label("$l$",(A+B)/2,S);label("$w$",(B+C)/2,E);label("$l+\dfrac{w}{5}$",(A+C)/2+(0.6,0),dir(-60),blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The figure shows the rectangle drawn with the answer's proportions, $l=12$ and $w=5$.
