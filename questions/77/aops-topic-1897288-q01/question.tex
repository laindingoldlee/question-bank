The $16$ points $(i,j)$ with $i,j\in\{0,1,2,3\}$ are the vertices of a $3\times3$ grid of unit squares. Prove that among any $6$ of these points there are three that lie on one line or are the vertices of a right triangle. Show also that $6$ cannot be replaced by $5$.
