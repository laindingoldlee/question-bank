The terms are $\binom{20}{k}a^{20-k}b^k$ for $k=0,1,\ldots,20$, so there are $\boxed{21}$ terms. The requested term has $k=8$, giving coefficient $\binom{20}{8}=\dfrac{20!}{8!12!}=\boxed{125970}$.
