Write $p$ for Len's win probability. He wins immediately with probability $0.6$; if both miss, probability $0.4\cdot0.6=0.24$, the situation restarts. Thus $p=0.6+0.24p$, so $p=\dfrac{15}{19}$. By linearity of expectation, the expected wins are $38p=\boxed{30}$.
