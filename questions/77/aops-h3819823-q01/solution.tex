The six triangles have equal sides and equal $60^\circ$ central angles, so they are congruent and have equal areas. Two of the six are yellow, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{2}{6}=\dfrac{1}{3}}$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

\end{asy}
