\begin{itemize}
\item Sphere: $\dfrac43\pi\cdot1^3=\dfrac{4\pi}{3}\approx4.189$.
\item Regular tetrahedron with edge $1$: the base is an equilateral triangle of area $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}$. The apex lies above the centre of the base, which is at distance $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}$ from each base vertex, so the height is $\sqrt{1-\dfrac13}=\sqrt{\dfrac23}$. The volume is $\dfrac13\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}\cdot\sqrt{\dfrac23}=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{12}\approx0.118$.
\item Cone: $\dfrac13\pi\cdot2^2\cdot1=\dfrac{4\pi}{3}\approx4.189$, exactly the same as the sphere.
\item Cube: $1^3=1$.
\end{itemize}
The largest volume is $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$ cubic metres (the sphere or the cone), and $73\%$ of it is
\[0.73\cdot\dfrac{4\pi}{3}\approx0.73\cdot4.18879\approx3.0578.\]
The largest amount is about $\boxed{3.06}$ cubic metres.
