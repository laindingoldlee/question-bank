A shop sells hollow glass ice cream makers in four shapes: a sphere of radius $1$ m; a regular tetrahedron with all edges $1$ m; a cone of height $1$ m whose base has radius $2$ m; and a cube with edges $1$ m. An ice cream maker can be filled with ice cream only up to $73\%$ of its volume. A store buys one ice cream maker. What is the largest amount of ice cream it can hold, in cubic metres, rounded to the nearest hundredth?
