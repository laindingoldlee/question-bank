Take $A=(-1,0),B=(1,0),D=(-1,2),C=(1,2),E=(0,2+\sqrt3)$. Put $h=2+\sqrt3$. Then $AE^2=BE^2=1+h^2=4h$, and $[ABE]=h$. The circumradius formula gives $R=\dfrac{AB\cdot AE\cdot BE}{4[ABE]}=\dfrac{2(4h)}{4h}=\boxed2$.
