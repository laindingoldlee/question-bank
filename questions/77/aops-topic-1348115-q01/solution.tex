At the $k$-th house he spends $12k$ minutes. Over the $20568$ houses the total is
\[
12(1+2+\cdots+20568)=12\cdot\dfrac{20568\cdot20569}{2}=6\cdot20568\cdot20569\text{ minutes}.
\]
In hours this is
\[
\dfrac{6\cdot20568\cdot20569}{60}=\dfrac{20568\cdot20569}{10}=\dfrac{423063192}{10}=\boxed{42306319.2}\text{ hours}.
\]
(That is about $4800$ years, so Alissa should not wait up.)
