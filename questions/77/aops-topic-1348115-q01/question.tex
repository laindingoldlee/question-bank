At the first house, getting the presents down the chimney takes him $12$ minutes, and at each later house it takes him $12$ minutes longer than at the previous house. Ignoring travel time between houses, how many hours have gone by when Santa reaches Alissa's house (that is, when he has finished the $20568$ houses)?
