Put $u=3x$, $v=5y$. Then $u+v=7$ and $u,v\ge 0$, so $9x^2+25y^2=u^2+v^2=(u+v)^2-2uv\le 49$. Equality occurs when $u=0$ or $v=0$. The maximum is $\boxed{49}$.
