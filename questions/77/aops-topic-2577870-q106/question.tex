Let $u=(0,1)$, $v=(-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2},-\dfrac{1}{2})$, and $w=(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2},-\dfrac{1}{2})$. Draw the three rhombi with vertices $(0,u,u+v,v)$, $(0,v,v+w,w)$, and $(0,w,w+u,u)$. Find the total area of their union.
