Record the region hit by each of the five darts, in the order they are thrown. There are $3^5=243$ equally likely sequences (the darts may be identical to look at, but they are thrown separately, so the sequences are the equally likely outcomes).

The favourable sequences contain the letters $L,L,C,R,R$ in some order. The number of such arrangements is
\[
\dfrac{5!}{2!\,1!\,2!}=30 .
\]
So the probability is
\[
\dfrac{30}{243}=\boxed{\dfrac{10}{81}}.
\]
