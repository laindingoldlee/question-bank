Five darts are thrown, one after another, at a dartboard made of three rectangular regions of equal area (left, centre and right). Each dart hits exactly one of the regions, and each region is equally likely to be hit, independently of the other darts. What is the probability that exactly $2$ darts hit the left rectangle, exactly $1$ dart hits the centre rectangle and exactly $2$ darts hit the right rectangle? Express your answer as a common fraction.
