(a) Can a five-digit integer $\overline{abcdc}$ have remainders $a,b,c,d,c$ on division by $2,3,4,5,6$, respectively? Prove your answer.
(b) In a corrected version, a five-digit integer $\overline{abcde}$ has remainders $a,b,c,d,e$ on division by $2,3,4,5,6$, respectively. Find the integer and its remainder modulo $100$. Different letters need not denote different digits.
