Let the tens digit be $t$ and the units digit $u$. Then $\dfrac{u}{t}=\dfrac{1}{2}$, so $t=2u$, and $t=u+2$. Hence $2u=u+2$, so $u=2$ and $t=4$. The number is $\boxed{42}$. Check: $\dfrac{2}{4}=\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $4=2+2$.
