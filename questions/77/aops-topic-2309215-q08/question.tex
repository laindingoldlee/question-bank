A regular pentagon is inscribed in a circle of radius $2$, and all of its diagonals are drawn. The diagonals form a five-pointed star, which is a (non-convex) decagon whose vertices are the five vertices of the pentagon and the five points where the diagonals cross. Find the area of this decagon in simplest radical form.
