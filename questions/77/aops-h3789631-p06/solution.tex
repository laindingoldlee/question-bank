Let $R$ cherries remain after dinner. The reserved $45\%$ is $\dfrac{9}{20}R$, so $R$ is a multiple of 20. The number found is $\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{11}{20}R=\dfrac{11R}{60}$, an integer, so $R$ is a multiple of 60. Since $0<R<111$, necessarily $R=60$ and the grandmother finds $\boxed{11}$ cherries.
