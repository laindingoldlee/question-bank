Remove triangles $ BEF,DGH $ from the square. Their perpendicular leg lengths are 3 and 5 in each case, so each has area $\dfrac{15}{2}$. The hexagon area is $ 49-15=\boxed{34}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(7,0),C=(7,7),D=(0,7),PtE=(4,0),F=(7,5),G=(5,7),H=(0,4);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--PtE--F--C--G--H--cycle,blue);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$E$",PtE,S);label("$F$",F,E);label("$G$",G,N);label("$H$",H,W);
\end{asy}
