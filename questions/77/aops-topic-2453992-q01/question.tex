$ABC$ is an isosceles right triangle with the right angle at $C$. Let $E$ and $F$ be the midpoints of $CB$ and $CA$, and let $D$ be the point such that $CEDF$ is a square. Prove that $D$ lies on $AB$; in fact, $D$ is the midpoint of $AB$.
