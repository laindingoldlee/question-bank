Rectangle $ABCD$ is inscribed in a circle. Points $E$, $F$, $G$, and $H$ are the midpoints of minor arcs $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, and $DA$. Given that $AB=6$ and $BC=8$, what is the area of the overlap between $ABCD$ and $EFGH$?

Note: In this problem, the overlap is defined as the intersection of $ABCD$ and $EFGH$, not the union.
