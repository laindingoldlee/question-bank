Place the rectangle at $(\pm3,\pm4)$, on the circle of radius $5$. The arc midpoints are $(0,\pm5),(\pm5,0)$, so their quadrilateral is $|x|+|y|\le5$. At each corner of the rectangle, the excluded piece is a right triangle with legs $2$, of area $2$. Thus the overlap is $6\cdot8-4\cdot2=\boxed{40}$.
