A sealed envelope contains a card with a single digit on it. Three of the following statements are true, and the other is false.
\begin{itemize}
\item[I.] The digit is $1$.
\item[II.] The digit is not $2$.
\item[III.] The digit is $3$.
\item[IV.] The digit is not $4$.
\end{itemize}
Which of the four statements must necessarily be true, and what are the possible digits?
