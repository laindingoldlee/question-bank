View the deal and reveal order as $26$ labeled pairs of positions. Among the $52$ positions, the set of $26$ positions occupied by ones is uniformly chosen.

If there are $x$ pairs $11$, conservation of the numbers of zeros and ones forces exactly $x$ pairs $00$. Thus $A$ scores $2x$ and $B$ scores $26-2x$. A strict win for $A$ means $x\ge7$; a tie is impossible.

For fixed $x$, choose the $2x$ matching pairs, select which $x$ of them are $11$, and orient the remaining mismatched pairs in $2^{26-2x}$ ways. Hence
\[
\boxed{\Pr(A\text{ wins})=
\dfrac{\displaystyle\sum_{x=7}^{13}\binom{26}{2x}\binom{2x}{x}2^{26-2x}}{\binom{52}{26}}
=\dfrac{11409831835}{24805848987}\approx0.459965}.
\]
