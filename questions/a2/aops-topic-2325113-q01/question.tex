Twelve objects are to be placed on $3$ different shelves, in a row on each shelf, so that every shelf has at least one object. Five objects are red, three are blue, three are yellow and one is green; objects of the same colour are identical. In how many ways can this be done? (An arrangement is determined by the left-to-right sequence of colours on each shelf.)
