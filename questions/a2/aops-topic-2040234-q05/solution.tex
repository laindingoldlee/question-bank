Put $g(x)=f(x)-x$. Then $g(2x)=g(x)$, hence $g(x)=g(\dfrac{x}{2^n})$ for every $n\ge0$. The arguments tend to zero, so continuity implies $g(x)=g(0)$. Therefore $\boxed{f(x)=x+C}$ for arbitrary real $C$. Substitution verifies every such function.
