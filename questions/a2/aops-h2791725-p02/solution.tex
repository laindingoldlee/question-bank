A big volcano erupting this year erupted once in the preceding five years; any other big volcano erupted twice. A small volcano erupting this year erupted twice in those five years, and any other small volcano three times. If $b,s$ are the numbers erupting this year, the past total is $2\cdot8-b+3\cdot6-s=34-(b+s)=30$. Thus $\boxed{b+s=4}$.
