The number is determined by $a,c,e$, with $a\ge1$, $c\ge0$, $e\ge0$, and the conditions that $b=a+c\le 9$ and $d=c+e\le9$ are digits.

Fix $c$. Then $a$ ranges over $1,\dots,9-c$ ($9-c$ choices) and $e$ over $0,\dots,9-c$ ($10-c$ choices); $c=9$ is impossible since $a\ge1$. The total is
\[\sum_{c=0}^{8}(9-c)(10-c)=\sum_{k=1}^{9}k(k+1)=\sum_{k=1}^{9}k^2+\sum_{k=1}^9k=285+45=\boxed{330}.\]
