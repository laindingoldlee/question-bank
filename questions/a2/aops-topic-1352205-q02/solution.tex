The first pick is blue with probability $\dfrac{1}{18}$. Then $17$ wallpapers remain, $8$ of them brown, so the second pick is brown with probability $\dfrac{8}{17}$. Hence
\[
P=\dfrac{1}{18}\cdot\dfrac{8}{17}=\dfrac{8}{306}=\boxed{\dfrac{4}{153}}\approx0.0261,
\]
which is about $\boxed{3\%}$.
