Samchooo has $18$ wallpapers in $5$ colours: $1$ red, $1$ blue, $3$ green, $5$ white and $8$ brown. He picks one at random, sets it aside, and then picks another at random from the rest. What is the probability that the first is the blue one and the second is brown? Give the exact value and the value rounded to the nearest whole percent.
