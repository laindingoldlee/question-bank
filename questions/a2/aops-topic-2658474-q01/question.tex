Let $n\ge2$ be an integer and let $k$ be one of the base-$n$ digits $0,1,\dots,n-1$. Let $S$ be the set of positive integers whose base-$n$ representation contains no digit equal to $k$. Prove that the sum of the reciprocals of the elements of $S$ converges, and that it is at most $n(n-1)$.
