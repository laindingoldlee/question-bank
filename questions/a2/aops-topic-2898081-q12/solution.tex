Place $D=(0,0)$, $C=(\sqrt3,0)$, $A=(0,1)$. The bisector is $y=x$, and $AC$ is $y=1-\dfrac x{\sqrt3}$. They meet at $x=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{\sqrt3+1}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair D=(0,0),C=(sqrt(3),0),B=(sqrt(3),1),A=(0,1);pair E=extension(A,C,D,D+(1,1));draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C);draw(D--E);fill(A--D--E--cycle,lightgray);
label("$A$",A,NW);label("$B$",B,NE);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,SW);label("$E$",E,N);
\end{asy}

$[ADE]=\dfrac12\cdot AD\cdot x=\dfrac x2$ and $[ABCD]=\sqrt3$, so the ratio is $\dfrac1{2(\sqrt3+1)}=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt3-1}4}$.
