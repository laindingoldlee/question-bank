At each root, $p^3+5=5p+4$. For $P(x)=x^3-5x+1$, logarithmic differentiation gives $\dfrac{\sum1}{(t-p)}=\dfrac{P\prime(t)}{P(t)}$. Thus the desired sum is $-\dfrac{(\dfrac{1}{5})P\prime(-\dfrac{4}{5})}{P(-\dfrac{4}{5})}$. Here $P\prime(-\dfrac{4}{5})=-\dfrac{77}{25}$ and $P(-\dfrac{4}{5})=\dfrac{561}{125}$, giving $\dfrac{77}{561}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{51}}$.
