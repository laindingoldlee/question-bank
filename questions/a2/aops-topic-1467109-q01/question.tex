A word on the planet Silliasaurus uses at most one each of the letters $S$, $I$ and $Y$, at most two of the letter $L$, and no other letters. Each word has from $1$ to $5$ letters, and the Silliasaurus language uses all possible such words. How many words are in the Silliasaurus vocabulary?
