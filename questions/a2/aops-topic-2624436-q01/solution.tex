Substitute $y=1-x$:
\[(x+2)^2+(5-x)^2=25\iff2x^2-6x+4=0\iff x^2-3x+2=(x-1)(x-2)=0.\]
So $x=1,y=0$ or $x=2,y=-1$. The intersection points are $\boxed{(1,0)\text{ and }(2,-1)}$. (Check: $3^2+4^2=4^2+3^2=25$.)
