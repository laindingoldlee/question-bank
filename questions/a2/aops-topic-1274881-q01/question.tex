Three brothers agree to buy seed wheat. Adam buys $45$ sacks and Ben buys $75$ sacks, while Charlie stays home. At home they share the $120$ sacks equally, and Charlie pays $\$1400$ for his share, which is divided between Adam and Ben in fair proportion to what they paid for the sacks Charlie received. How much does each of Adam and Ben receive?
