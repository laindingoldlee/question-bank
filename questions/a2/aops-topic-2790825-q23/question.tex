Points $A$ and $B$ satisfy $AB=1$. A variable line $\ell$ passes through $A$, and $\ell'$ is the line through $B$ perpendicular to $\ell$. Points $X$ on $\ell$ and $Y$ on $\ell'$ satisfy $AX=BY=1$. Find the length of the locus of the midpoint of $XY$.
