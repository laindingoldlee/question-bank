Let $Q(t)=(t-1)(t-9)(t-25)(t-49)$ and define
\[R(t)=Q(t)-x^2\dfrac{Q(t)}{t-1}-y^2\dfrac{Q(t)}{t-9}-z^2\dfrac{Q(t)}{t-25}-w^2\dfrac{Q(t)}{t-49}.\]
Every quotient here is a polynomial. The hypotheses make $4,16,36,64$ roots of the monic quartic $R$, so $R(t)=(t-4)(t-16)(t-36)(t-64)$. Compare the coefficients of $t^3$:
\[-(1+9+25+49)-(x^2+y^2+z^2+w^2)=-(4+16+36+64).\]
The requested sum is $120-84=\boxed{36}$.
