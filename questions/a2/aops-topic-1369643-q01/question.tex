A triangle has side lengths $a\le b\le c$. Prove that the triangle is acute if $a^2+b^2>c^2$, right if $a^2+b^2=c^2$, and obtuse if $a^2+b^2<c^2$. Then classify the triangles with side lengths
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $6,7,9$;
\item[(b)] $5,12,13$;
\item[(c)] $4,5,7$.
\end{enumerate}
