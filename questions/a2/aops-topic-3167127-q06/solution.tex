Let $t=\log_2x$, with $x>0$. Then $\log_4\sqrt x=\dfrac{t}{4}$, so $\dfrac{t^2}{4}=36$ and $t=\pm12$. The resulting values are $4096$ and $\dfrac{1}{4096}$; only $4096$ is an integer. Its digit sum is $4+0+9+6=\boxed{19}$.
