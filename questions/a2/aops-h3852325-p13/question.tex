For $y>0$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Differentiate $(1+2\ln y)^{-2}$ where it is defined.
\item Find the constant and nonconstant solutions of
\[
\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{\sin2x}{3}\,y(1+2\ln y)^3,\qquad -\dfrac{\pi}{2}<x<\dfrac{\pi}{2}.
\]
State the intervals on which your formulas are defined.
\item Find the solution with $y=1$ at $x=\dfrac{\pi}{6}$, and express it as $y=e^{A\sec x-\dfrac{1}{2}}$.
\end{enumerate}
