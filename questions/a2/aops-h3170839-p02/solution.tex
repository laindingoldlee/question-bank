There are $11$ letters, with four S's and two I's. The total number of distinct arrangements is $\dfrac{11!}{4!2!}$. Treating the S-block as one object leaves eight objects with two identical I's, giving $\dfrac{8!}{2!}$ favorable arrangements. The probability is
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{8!\,4!}{11!}=\dfrac{4}{165}}.
\]
