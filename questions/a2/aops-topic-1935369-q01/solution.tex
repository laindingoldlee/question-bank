Complete the square: $(a^4+a^2+2)^2=a^8+2a^6+5a^4+4a^2+4$, so
\[
a^8+a^6+a^4+4=(a^4+a^2+2)^2-\left(a^6+4a^4+4a^2\right)=(a^4+a^2+2)^2-(a^3+2a)^2 .
\]
By the difference of squares,
\[
a^8+a^6+a^4+4=\left(a^4-a^3+a^2-2a+2\right)\left(a^4+a^3+a^2+2a+2\right).
\]
For $a\ge2$ the first factor is $a^3(a-1)+a(a-2)+2\ge8+0+2=10$, and the second factor is even larger, so the number is a product of two factors greater than $1$ and is not prime.

For $a=1$ the number is $1+1+1+4=7$, which is prime. So the only answer is $a=\boxed{1}$.
