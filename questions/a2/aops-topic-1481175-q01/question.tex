A $4$-foot by $2$-foot rectangular flag consists of a $2$-foot by $2$-foot black square with a $1$-foot by $2$-foot white rectangle on each of two opposite sides of the square. A white symbol covers one-third of the area of the black square. What fraction of the total area of the front of the flag is white? Express your answer as a common fraction.
