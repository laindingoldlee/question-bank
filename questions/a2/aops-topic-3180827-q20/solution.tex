The term indexed by $n=2,\ldots,99$ is $(2n+1)(n^2+(n+1)^2)=((n+1)^2-n^2)((n+1)^2+n^2)=(n+1)^4-n^4$. The sum telescopes to $100^4-2^4=\boxed{99999984}$.
