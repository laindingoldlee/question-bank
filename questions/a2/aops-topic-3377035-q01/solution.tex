The first two inequalities give $y+1\le x\le5-y$. Such an $x$ exists exactly when $y+1\le5-y$, or $y\le2$. Together with $y\ge0$, the complete solution is $\boxed{0\le y\le2,\quad y+1\le x\le5-y}$. The lower bound on $x$ automatically implies $x\ge0$.
