Each term is $0.02$ times the previous one: $0.14\cdot0.02=0.0028$, $0.0028\cdot0.02=0.000056$, and so on. This is an infinite geometric series with first term $0.14$ and ratio $0.02$, and $|0.02|<1$, so its sum is
\[
\dfrac{0.14}{1-0.02}=\dfrac{0.14}{0.98}=\dfrac{14}{98}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{7}}.
\]
