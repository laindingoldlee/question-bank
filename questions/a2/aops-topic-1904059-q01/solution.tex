Adding the equations gives $3x=3.5$, so $x=\dfrac{7}{6}$, and then $y=x-\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{2}{3}$. Check: $2\cdot\dfrac{7}{6}+\dfrac{2}{3}=\dfrac{7}{3}+\dfrac{2}{3}=3$. The solution is $\boxed{x=\dfrac{7}{6},\ y=\dfrac{2}{3}}$.
