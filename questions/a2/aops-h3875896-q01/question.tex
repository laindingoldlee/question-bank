A waterwheel of radius $6$ feet makes one counterclockwise revolution every $10$ seconds. A rim point starts directly above the center at $t=0$. Let $h(t)$ be its signed height above the horizontal center line.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Give the coordinates of the five successive quarter-cycle points $F,G,J,K,P$ starting with $F$ at $t=0$.
\item Write $h(t)=a\sin(b(t+c))+d$.
\item On the open interval from $J$ to $K$, state the sign of $h$, whether it is increasing or decreasing, its concavity, and whether its rate of change is increasing or decreasing.
\end{enumerate}
