The radius gives amplitude $6$, the midline is $0$, and the angular speed is $2\dfrac{\pi}{10}=\dfrac{\pi}{5}$. Since the point starts at the maximum,
\[
h(t)=6\cos(\pi \dfrac{t}{5})=6\sin\left(\dfrac{\pi}{5}(t+\dfrac{5}{2})\right).
\]
Thus $(a,b,c,d)=(6,\dfrac{\pi}{5},\dfrac{5}{2},0)$ is one valid choice. The five points are
\[
F=(0,6),\quad G=(\dfrac{5}{2},0),\quad J=(5,-6),\quad K=(\dfrac{15}{2},0),\quad P=(10,6).
\]
On $5<t<\dfrac{15}{2}$, the height is negative and increasing. Also $h''(t)=-(\dfrac{\pi}{5})^2h(t)>0$, so the graph is concave upward and the rate of change $h'(t)$ is increasing.
