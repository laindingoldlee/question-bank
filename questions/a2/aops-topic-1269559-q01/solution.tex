The binomial series is
\[(1+x)^{\dfrac{1}{3}}=1+\dfrac{1}{3}x+\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{3}\left(-\dfrac{2}{3}\right)}{2!}x^2+\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{3}\left(-\dfrac{2}{3}\right)\left(-\dfrac{5}{3}\right)}{3!}x^3+\cdots=1+\dfrac{x}{3}-\dfrac{x^2}{9}+\dfrac{5x^3}{81}-\cdots.\]
At $x=2$ the first four terms are $1+\dfrac{2}{3}-\dfrac{4}{9}+\dfrac{40}{81}$, whose sum is
\[\dfrac{81+54-36+40}{81}=\boxed{\dfrac{139}{81}}.\]
(Since $|x|=2>1$ the full series diverges, so these partial sums do not approach $\sqrt[3]{3}\approx1.442$; the question only asks for the four terms.)
