Let $d_k$ be the number of digits of $5^k$. Multiplication by $5$ either preserves the digit count or increases it by one. It preserves it exactly when $5^k$ begins with $1$: a $d$-digit integer $N$ satisfies $5N<10^d$ precisely when $N<2\cdot10^{d-1}$. Therefore the number of verdant terms for $k=0,\ldots,6729$ is the number of zero increments among the $6730$ differences $d_{k+1}-d_k$. Their sum is $4705-1=4704$, so the count is $6730-4704=\boxed{2026}$.
