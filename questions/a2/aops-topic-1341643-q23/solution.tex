The rotation takes the centre $O$ to the centre $O'$, with $PO'=PO=2$ and $\angle OPO'=90^\circ$, so $OO'=2\sqrt2$. Both circles have radius $4$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0), P=(2,0), Op=(2,-2);
pair H=(O+Op)/2; pair u=unit(Op-O); pair v=(u.y,-u.x);
real hh=sqrt(16-2);
pair M=H+hh*v, Nn=H-hh*v;
draw(circle(O,4),linewidth(0.9)); draw(circle(Op,4),blue+linewidth(0.9));
draw(M--Nn,red); draw(O--Op,dashed); draw(O--P--Op,gray+dashed);
draw(O--M,gray);
dot(O);dot(P);dot(Op);dot(M);dot(Nn);dot(H);
label("$O$",O,W); label("$P$",P,E); label("$O'$",Op,SE,blue); label("$M$",M,dir(200)); label("$N$",Nn,NE);
label("$4$",(O+M)/2,NW,gray);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The common chord $MN$ is perpendicular to $OO'$ and bisected by it at the midpoint $H$ of $OO'$, where $OH=\sqrt2$. In the right triangle $OHM$,
\[
HM=\sqrt{OM^2-OH^2}=\sqrt{16-2}=\sqrt{14},
\]
so $MN=2HM=\boxed{2\sqrt{14}}$.
