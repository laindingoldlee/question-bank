Circle $O$ has radius $4$ units, and point $P$ lies inside the circle, $2$ units from the centre $O$. Circle $O'$ is the image of circle $O$ under a rotation of $90^\circ$ counterclockwise about $P$. Circle $O'$ intersects circle $O$ at points $M$ and $N$. What is the length of segment $MN$? Express your answer in simplest radical form.
