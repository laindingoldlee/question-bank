Five poles, each a cylinder of diameter $8$ inches and at least $8$ inches tall, stand upright in an empty cylindrical pot of height $8$ inches. One pole stands at the centre of the pot; each of the other four touches the central pole and the side of the pot, and they are spaced evenly around it. How many cubic inches of water can be poured into the pot before it overflows? Express your answer in terms of $\pi$.
