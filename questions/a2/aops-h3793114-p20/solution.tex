Let $BC=b$. Then $AB=b+2$ and $AC=\dfrac{5b}{12}$. The perimeter equation gives $\dfrac{29b}{12}+2=60$, so $b=24$. The sides are $10,24,26$, satisfying $10^2+24^2=26^2$. Hence the area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot10\cdot24=\boxed{120\text{ cm}^2}$.
