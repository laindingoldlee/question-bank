The volume of one barrel of oil is $150$ liters. A chemist has one such barrel. The first chemist poured out $\dfrac{1}{2}$ of the barrel, the second $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of the remaining amount, the third $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of what remained after that, and the fourteenth $\dfrac{1}{15}$ of what remained. How many liters of oil remain in the barrel?
