A path consists of $4$ steps right and $4$ steps up in some order, so there are $\dbinom{8}{4}=70$ paths in all.

A path through $(3,3)$ goes from $(1,1)$ to $(3,3)$ in $\dbinom{4}{2}=6$ ways and then from $(3,3)$ to $(5,5)$ in $6$ ways, so $36$ paths pass through $(3,3)$. Hence $70-36=\boxed{34}$ paths avoid it.
