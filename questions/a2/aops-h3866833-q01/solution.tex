Let $F(N)=\sum_{j=1}^N f(j)$. Odd terms contribute the sum of the first $\lceil \dfrac{N}{2}\rceil$ odd numbers, while $f(2j)=f(j)$. Hence
\[
F(N)=\left\lceil\dfrac{N}{2}\right\rceil^2+F(\lfloor \dfrac{N}{2}\rfloor).
\]
Applying this at $4048,2023,2024$,
\[
\begin{aligned}
F(4048)-F(2023)
&=2024^2+F(2024)-1012^2-F(1011)\\
&=2024^2+F(1012)-F(1011)\\
&=2024^2+f(1012).
\end{aligned}
\]
Since $1012=4\cdot253$, $f(1012)=253$. The answer is $\boxed{4096829}$.
