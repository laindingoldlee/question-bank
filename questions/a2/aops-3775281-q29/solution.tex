Composition gives $f(f(x))=\dfrac{k^2x}{(2k+6)x+9}$ wherever defined. Equality with $x$ on the domain forces $2k+6=0$ and $k^2=9$, hence $k=-3$. For that value, $f$ never takes the excluded value $-\dfrac{3}{2}$, and the composition simplifies to $x$ for every $x\ne-\dfrac{3}{2}$. Thus $\boxed{k=-3}$.
