With $t$ tens and $f$ fifties: $t+f=160$ and $10t+50f=1760$. Then $1600+40f=1760$, so $f=4$ and $t=156$: $\boxed{156\text{ ten-dollar bills and }4\text{ fifty-dollar bills}}$.
