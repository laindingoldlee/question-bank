The Lorenz differential equations are $x\prime=\sigma(y-x)$, $y\prime=x(\rho-z)-y$, $z\prime=xy-\beta z$. Give a forward Euler algorithm for $N$ steps of length $h>0$, using supplied parameters and initial values. Compute one step from $(1,1,1)$ with $(\sigma,\rho,\beta)=(10,28,\dfrac{8}{3})$ and $h=\dfrac{1}{100}$.
