Subtract corner triangles from square area 25: $x=25-\dfrac{1}{2}(3)(4)-\dfrac{1}{2}(2)(5)-\dfrac{1}{2}(1)(5)=\dfrac{23}{2}$. Thus $\boxed{2x=23}$.
