If the $100$ pints contain $r$ pints of red, then $\dfrac{r+10}{120}=\dfrac15$, so $r=14$. Perplexing purple is $86\%$ blue, and $150$ pints contain $0.86\cdot150=129$ pints of blue. Answer $\boxed{\textbf{(A)}\ 129}$.
