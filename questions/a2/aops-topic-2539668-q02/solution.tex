If the angle between the sides $18$ and $7$ is $\theta$, the area is
\[\dfrac12\cdot18\cdot7\cdot\sin\theta=63\sin\theta\le63,\]
with equality exactly when $\theta=90^\circ$, i.e. when $x=\sqrt{18^2+7^2}=\sqrt{373}$. The maximum area is $\boxed{63}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair P=(0,0),Q=(18,0),R=(0,7),R2=7*dir(125);
draw(P--Q--R--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
draw(P--R2--Q,dashed);
draw((0.8,0)--(0.8,0.8)--(0,0.8));
label("$18$",(P+Q)/2,S);label("$7$",(P+R)/2,W);label("$\sqrt{373}$",(Q+R)/2,NE);
draw(arc(P,1.6,0,125));label("$\theta$",1.6*dir(62),NE);
label("$7$",(P+R2)/2,SW);
\end{asy}

(The dashed triangle shows a non-right angle $\theta$; its height on the side $18$ is $7\sin\theta<7$.)
