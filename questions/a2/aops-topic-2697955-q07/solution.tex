Two lines cross, forming angles $\angle1$, $\angle2$, $\angle3$ in order around the crossing point, so that $\angle1$ and $\angle3$ are vertical angles. Angles $\angle1$ and $\angle2$ together form a straight angle, and so do $\angle2$ and $\angle3$:
\[\angle1+\angle2=180^\circ=\angle2+\angle3.\]
Hence $\angle1=\angle3$. $\blacksquare$
