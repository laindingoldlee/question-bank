Let her buy $n$ oranges of each kind. The cost is $\dfrac n2+\dfrac n3=\dfrac{5n}6$ rupees for $2n$ oranges, i.e. $\dfrac5{12}$ rupee per orange. For a $20\%$ profit she must sell each orange for $1.2\cdot\dfrac5{12}=\dfrac12$ rupee, that is, at $\boxed{2\text{ oranges for a rupee}}$.
