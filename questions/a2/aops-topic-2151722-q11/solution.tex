The radii are $3$, $5$, $7$ and $9$ cm, so the board is a disc of radius $9$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
fill(circle((0,0),9),lightgray); fill(circle((0,0),7),white); fill(circle((0,0),5),lightgray); fill(circle((0,0),3),paleblue);
draw(circle((0,0),9),linewidth(0.8)); draw(circle((0,0),7),linewidth(0.8)); draw(circle((0,0),5),linewidth(0.8));
draw(circle((0,0),3),blue+linewidth(1));
draw((0,0)--(3,0),blue); label("$3$",(1.5,0),N,blue);
draw((0,0)--(0,-9),red); label("$9$",(0,-8),E,red);
draw((0,0)--dir(135)*5,gray); label("$5$",dir(135)*4,SW,fontsize(8pt));
draw((0,0)--dir(45)*7,gray); label("$7$",dir(45)*6,SE,fontsize(8pt));
dot((0,0));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

For a uniformly random point, the probability is the ratio of areas:
\[\dfrac{\pi\cdot3^2}{\pi\cdot9^2}=\dfrac{9}{81}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{9}}.\]
