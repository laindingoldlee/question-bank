A jar contains $20$ red, $15$ green and $10$ yellow balls. Three balls are drawn at random without replacement, and none of them is red. A fourth ball is then drawn at random from the $42$ balls that are left. What is the probability that the fourth ball is green? Explain why the shortcut $\dfrac{15}{25}\cdot\dfrac{22}{42}$ gives the right answer.
