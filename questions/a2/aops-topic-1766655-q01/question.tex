The great rhombicuboctahedron is a convex polyhedron with $26$ faces: $12$ square faces, $6$ regular octagonal faces and $8$ regular hexagonal faces. At each vertex, one square, one hexagon and one octagon meet. How many of the segments joining two vertices of the polyhedron lie in its interior (apart from their endpoints)?
