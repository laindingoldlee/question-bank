Adding the known total $13$ gives $\boxed{\dfrac{44}{3},28,\dfrac{77}{5}\text{ minutes}}$. Each case is realizable: choose the width of the first column and the height of the top row to realize their given cell, then determine the remaining width and height from the two adjacent cells.
