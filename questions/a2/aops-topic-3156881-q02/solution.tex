The triangle inequality bounds the distance by $ 5+7+5=17 $. Taking opposite outermost points on the line of centres attains it, so $\boxed{17}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(7,0),P=(-5,0),Q=(12,0);draw(circle(A,5));draw(circle(B,5));draw(P--Q,blue);dot("$A$",A,S);dot("$B$",B,S);label("$P$",P,W);label("$Q$",Q,E);
\end{asy}
