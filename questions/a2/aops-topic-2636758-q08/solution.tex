By Vieta, $r+s+t=-13$, $rs+st+tr=-21$ and $rst=-6$. The sum equals
\[(r+s+t)\left(\dfrac1r+\dfrac1s+\dfrac1t\right)-3=\dfrac{(r+s+t)(rs+st+tr)}{rst}-3=\dfrac{(-13)(-21)}{-6}-3=-\dfrac{91}{2}-3=\boxed{-\dfrac{97}{2}}.\]
