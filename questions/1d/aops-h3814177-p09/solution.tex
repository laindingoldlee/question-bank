The two Tuesdays are different exactly when the month starts on Tuesday: then they are the 1st and the 8th. Otherwise the first Tuesday follows the first Monday. Both months must therefore start on Tuesday, so the first month has a multiple of seven days. The only Gregorian month length with that property is $28$, making it February in a common year. The next month is March, and the specified Tuesday is $\boxed{\text{March 8}}$.
