Suppose $g(x)=11x^2-1$ and $f$ is a function with $\dfrac{g(x)}{f(x)}=10x$ for all $x\ne0$ where $f(x)\ne0$. For what negative value of $x$ does $f(x)=1$? Express your answer as a common fraction.
