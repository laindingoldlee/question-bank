Write $u^2=M+1213$ and $v^2=M+3773$ with $v>u\ge0$. Then $(v-u)(v+u)=2560$. The factors have the same parity, and their product is even, so both are even. If $h=v-u$, then $u=\dfrac{\dfrac{2560}{h}-h}{2}$ decreases as positive $h$ increases. Its maximum therefore occurs at the smallest allowed $h=2$, with $v+u=1280$. This gives $u=639$ and $M=639^2-1213=407108$. The units digit is $\boxed8$.
