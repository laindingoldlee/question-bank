Glue $C,D$ into a block (2 internal orders): $5$ units around a circle give $4!\cdot2=48$ seatings. In those where $A,B$ are also adjacent, glue them too: $4$ units give $3!\cdot2\cdot2=24$. So $48-24=\boxed{24}$.
