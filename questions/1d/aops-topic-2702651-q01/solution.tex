The salaries form a geometric sequence with ratio $1.1$, so in year $8$ he earns $10000\cdot1.1^7=\boxed{\text{Rs.\ }19487.17}$. (After $8$ full years, i.e. in year $9$, it would be $10000\cdot1.1^8\approx21435.89$.)
