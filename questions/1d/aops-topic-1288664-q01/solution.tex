Expanding $(a+d)(b+e)(c+f)$ gives the six given terms together with $ace$ and $bdf$:
\[
(a+d)(b+e)(c+f)=(abc+bcd+cde+def+efa+fab)+ace+bdf .
\]
Since $ace,bdf\ge0$, by AM--GM the given expression is at most
\[
(a+d)(b+e)(c+f)\le\left(\dfrac{(a+d)+(b+e)+(c+f)}{3}\right)^3=2^3=8 .
\]
Equality holds for $a=b=c=2$, $d=e=f=0$: then $abc=8$ and all other terms are $0$. The maximum is $\boxed{8}$.
