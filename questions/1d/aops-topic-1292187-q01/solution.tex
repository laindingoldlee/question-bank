No. Let the progression be $a,a+d,a+2d,\dots$ with $a\ge2$ and $d\ge1$. The term with index $a$ is
\[
a+ad=a(1+d),
\]
a product of two integers greater than $1$, so it is composite. (A constant sequence $p,p,p,\dots$ trivially consists of primes; the question is only interesting for $d\ge1$. Finite progressions of primes can be long, e.g. $5,11,17,23,29$.)
