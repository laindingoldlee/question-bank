A point $(x,y)$ is on the circle exactly when its distance from $(2,7)$ is $4$, i.e. $\sqrt{(x-2)^2+(y-7)^2}=4$. Squaring:
\[\boxed{(x-2)^2+(y-7)^2=16}.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((-3,0)--(7,0),Arrow); draw((0,-1)--(0,12),Arrow); draw(circle((2,7),4),blue); dot((2,7)); draw((2,7)--(6,7)); label("$4$",(4,7),N); label("$(2,7)$",(2,7),SW);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
