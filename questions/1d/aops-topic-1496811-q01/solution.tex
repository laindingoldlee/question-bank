\textbf{One digit.} Every $n$ from $1$ to $9$ equals the product of its digits.

\textbf{Two digits.} For $n=10a+b$ with $a\ge1$ we have $n=10a+b\ge10a>ab$, because $b\le9<10$. So no two-digit number works.

\textbf{Three digits below $200$.} Here $n=100+10b+c\ge100$, while the product of the digits is $1\cdot b\cdot c\le81$. So none works.

The answer is $\boxed{1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9}$.
