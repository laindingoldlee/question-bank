\textbf{False.} The statement says: whatever $a\ne0$, $b$, $c$ are, \emph{some} $x$ makes $ax^2+bx+c\ge0$. A counterexample is $a=-1$, $b=0$, $c=-1$: then $-x^2-1\le-1<0$ for every $x$. (The statement does hold when $a>0$, and when $a<0$ exactly if $b^2-4ac\ge0$.)
