The equations say $a^2-b^2=29^2-28^2=57$ and $a^2-c^2=29^2-27^2=112$. From $(a-b)(a+b)=57$: $(1,57)$ or $(3,19)$, giving $a=29$ or $a=11$. With $a<20$, $a=11$, $b=8$. Then $c^2=121-112=9$, $c=3$. So $a+b+c=\boxed{22}$.
