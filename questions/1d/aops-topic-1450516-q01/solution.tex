Since $t^2-t-56=(t-8)(t+7)$, for $t\ne8$ the left side is $t+7$. The equation becomes $(t+7)(t+5)=3$ with $t\ne-5$, so
\[
t^2+12t+32=0\iff(t+4)(t+8)=0 .
\]
Both $t=-4$ and $t=-8$ are allowed (neither is $8$ or $-5$). The greatest value is $\boxed{-4}$.
