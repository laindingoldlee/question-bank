There are $\dbinom42\cdot2=12$ games. At most one team can win all its games (two such teams would have to beat each other), and at most one can lose all of them. So the event is a disjoint union, over ordered pairs $(T,U)$ of different teams, of the events ``$T$ goes $6$-$0$ and $U$ goes $0$-$6$''.

For a fixed pair, $T$ must win its $6$ games (including both against $U$), and $U$ must also lose its $4$ games against the other two teams. These are $10$ different games, so the probability is $2^{-10}$. With $4\cdot3=12$ ordered pairs, the answer is
\[
\dfrac{12}{1024}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{256}}.
\]
