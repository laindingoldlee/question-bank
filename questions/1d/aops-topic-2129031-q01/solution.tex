Put $x=a+1$, $y=b-2$, $z=c+3$; this is a one-to-one correspondence between integer triples, and the condition becomes $x^2+y^2+z^2\le2$. The square of an integer is $0$, $1$ or at least $4$, so each of $x,y,z$ is $-1$, $0$ or $1$, and at most two of them are nonzero.
\begin{itemize}
\item All three zero: $1$ triple.
\item Exactly one nonzero: $3$ choices of the coordinate and $2$ signs, $6$ triples.
\item Exactly two nonzero: $3$ choices of the pair and $2\cdot2$ signs, $12$ triples.
\end{itemize}
The total is $1+6+12=\boxed{19}$.
