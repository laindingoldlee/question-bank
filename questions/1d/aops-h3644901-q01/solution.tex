The first and third differ by $2$, giving $n^2-2an-a+2=0$, or $(n-a)^2=(a+2)(a-1)$. Since $(a+1)n^2$ is an integer, $n^2$ is rational; the equation then makes $n$ rational. A rational square root of an integer is an integer. For $a\ge3$, however, $a^2<a^2+a-2<(a+1)^2$, impossible. If $a=1$, $n=1$ and $m^2=3$. If $a=2$, $n=0$ or $4$, giving $m^2=1$ or $49$. Thus the largest is $\boxed{49}$, attained by consecutive integers $48,49,50$.
