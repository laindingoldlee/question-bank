Adding the equations gives $2x=5$, so $x=\dfrac{5}{2}$; then $y=2-x=-\dfrac{1}{2}$. Check: $\dfrac{5}{2}-\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)=3$. The solution is $\boxed{x=\dfrac{5}{2},\ y=-\dfrac{1}{2}}$.
