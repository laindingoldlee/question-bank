After buying and drinking $b$ bottles, each exchange-and-drink reduces the empty count by $3$. Thus the maximum total drinks are $b+\lfloor\dfrac{b-1}{3}\rfloor=\lfloor\dfrac{4b-1}{3}\rfloor$. This bound is achievable by repeatedly exchanging while at least four empties remain. To reach $357$ drinks needs $4m-1\ge1071$, so $m=268$. For $119$ drinks, $4n-1\ge357$, so $n=90$. Hence $\boxed{m-2n=88}$.
