Four empty bottles can be exchanged for one full bottle. Three people each want to drink $119$ bottles. Let $m$ be the minimum initial purchase if they pool all empties, and $n$ the minimum initial purchase per person if they exchange independently. Bottles cannot be borrowed. Find $m-2n$.
