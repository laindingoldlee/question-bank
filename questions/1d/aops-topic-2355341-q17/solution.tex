Write everything as powers of $3$: $27^{104}=3^{312}$, $9^6=3^{12}$, $27^{102}=3^{306}$, $9^3=3^6$. Then
\[\dfrac{3^{312}+3^{12}}{3^{306}+3^{6}}=\dfrac{3^{12}\left(3^{300}+1\right)}{3^{6}\left(3^{300}+1\right)}=3^6,\]
and $\sqrt{3^6}=3^3=27$: $\boxed{\textbf{(B)}\ 27}$.
