Write $m=12a$, $n=12b$ with $\gcd(a,b)=1$. Then $\operatorname{lcm}=12ab=480$, so $ab=40$, and $a,b>\dfrac{50}{12}$ means $a,b\ge5$. The coprime factorisations of $40$ are $1\cdot40$ and $5\cdot8$; only $\{5,8\}$ works. So $\{m,n\}=\{60,96\}$ and $m+n=\boxed{156}$.
