Let $S_{16}=\sum_{k=1}^{16}\dfrac{1}{\sqrt k}$; direct evaluation bounds it by $6.66399<S_{16}<6.66400$. Since $x^{-\dfrac{1}{2}}$ decreases, $\int_{17}^{2023}\dfrac{dx}{\sqrt x}<\sum_{k=17}^{2022}\dfrac{1}{\sqrt k}<\int_{16}^{2022}\dfrac{dx}{\sqrt x}$. Therefore the full sum lies between $6.66399+2(\sqrt{2023}-\sqrt{17})$ and $6.66400+2(\sqrt{2022}-4)$. These are strictly above $88$ and below $89$, respectively (approximately $88.37$ and $88.60$). The floor is consequently $\boxed{88}$.
