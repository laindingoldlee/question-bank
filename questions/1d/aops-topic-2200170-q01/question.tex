The integers $1,2,\dots,100$ are written on a blackboard, each exactly once. In one operation, two numbers $a$ and $b$ on the board are erased and replaced by the greatest common divisor of $a^2+b^2+2$ and $a^2b^2+3$. After $99$ operations only one number is left. Prove that this number is not a perfect square.
