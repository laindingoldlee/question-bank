A rectangle has its base on the $x$-axis and its two upper vertices on the semicircle $y=\sqrt{9-x^2}$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Write the area $A$ of the rectangle as a function of $x$, where $(x,y)$ with $x>0$ is the upper right vertex.
\item[(b)] Find the largest possible area.
\end{enumerate}
