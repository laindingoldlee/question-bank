Point $A$ ranges over the circle centered at the origin with radius $5$. Its distance from $C=(3,0)$ ranges continuously from $5-3=2$ to $5+3=8$. Rotating $A$ about $C$ therefore produces every point whose distance from $C$ lies between $2$ and $8$. The region is an annulus of area $\pi(8^2-2^2)=\boxed{60\pi}$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(220);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair O=(0,0),C=(3,0);draw(circle(O,5),dashed);draw(circle(C,2));draw(circle(C,8));dot(O);dot(C);label("$O$",O,(-1,0));label("$C(3,0)$",C,(1,0));label("$2$",C+(0,2),(0,1));label("$8$",C+(0,8),(0,1));
\end{asy}
