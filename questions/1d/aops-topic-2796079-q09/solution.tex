Let the distance be $d$, the boat's speed $v$ and the current $c$. Then $v+c=\dfrac d8$ and $v-c=\dfrac d{24}$, so $v=\dfrac12\left(\dfrac d8+\dfrac d{24}\right)=\dfrac d{12}$. The trip takes $\boxed{12}$ hours.
