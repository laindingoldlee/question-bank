Write $x=k+t$, with integer $k\ge0$ and $0\le t<1$. The lower boundary is $k+2t$. For $k=0,\ldots,98$, the vertical-strip area is $\int_0^1(100-k-2t)\,dt=99-k$, summing to $\dfrac{99\cdot100}{2}=4950$. In the last strip $k=99$, only $0\le t\le\dfrac{1}{2}$ contributes, forming a triangle of base $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and height 1, area $\dfrac{1}{4}$. There is no further positive-area region. Thus the area is $\boxed{\dfrac{19801}{4}}$.
