Let Amy start with $a$ and Daisy with $d$. After the first gift Amy has $0.8a$ and Daisy has $d+0.2a$. After the second gift Daisy has $0.8(d+0.2a)=0.8d+0.16a$ and Amy has $0.8a+0.2(d+0.2a)=0.84a+0.2d$. Equating,
\[
0.84a+0.2d=0.8d+0.16a\iff0.68a=0.6d\iff\dfrac {a}{d}=\dfrac{0.6}{0.68}=\dfrac{15}{17}.
\]
The ratio is $\boxed{15:17}$. (Check with $a=15$, $d=17$: $12$ and $20$, then $16$ and $16$.)
