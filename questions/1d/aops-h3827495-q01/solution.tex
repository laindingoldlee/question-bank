Taking squared moduli gives
\[
1=|e^{i\alpha}+e^{i\beta}|^2=2+2\cos(\alpha-\beta),
\]
so
\[
\boxed{\alpha-\beta\equiv\pm\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\pmod{2\pi}}.
\]
For the more precise relation, divide by $e^{i\gamma}$. Two unit complex numbers now sum to $1$. Writing the first as $u$, the condition $|1-u|=|u|=1$ gives $\operatorname{Re}u=\dfrac{1}{2}$, hence $u=\dfrac{1}{2}\pm\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}i$. Therefore
\[
\boxed{\{e^{i\alpha},e^{i\beta}\}
=\{e^{i(\gamma+\dfrac{\pi}{3})},e^{i(\gamma-\dfrac{\pi}{3})}\}}.
\]
The triangle with vertices $0,e^{i\alpha},e^{i\gamma}$ is equilateral.
