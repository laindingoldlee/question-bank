Factor $2024=2^3\cdot11\cdot23$. For a prime with exponent $e$ in the lcm, the two exponents may range from 0 to $e$, with maximum exactly $e$. There are $(e+1)^2-e^2=2e+1$ such ordered choices. Independently applying this to the three primes gives $7\cdot3\cdot3=\boxed{63}$.
