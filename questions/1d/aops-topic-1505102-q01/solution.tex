Let the numbers be $x$ and $y$. Then $x+y=14$ and $x^2+y^2=108$. Since $(x+y)^2=x^2+y^2+2xy$,
\[
2xy=14^2-108=196-108=88,\qquad xy=\boxed{44}.
\]
Such real numbers exist: they are the roots of $t^2-14t+44=0$, namely $7\pm\sqrt5$.
