Put $U=(0,0)$ and $V=(0,12)$. The two boundary circles have equations $x^2+y^2=48$ and $x^2+(y-12)^2=192$. Their common chord is $y=0$, with endpoints $(\pm4\sqrt3,0)$. The intersection consists of the upper semicircle of the small disk, area $24\pi$, and the lower segment of the large disk. Its chord subtends $60^\circ$ at $V$ because its half-length is $4\sqrt3$ and its radius is $8\sqrt3$. Thus the segment area is $\dfrac{60}{360}\pi\cdot192-\dfrac{1}{2}(8\sqrt3)^2\sin60^\circ=32\pi-48\sqrt3$. The desired area is $\boxed{56\pi-48\sqrt3}$, giving $a+b+c=\boxed{11}$.

\end{asy}
