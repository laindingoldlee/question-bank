In a dice game, five fair dice are rolled. A player has four dice showing the same number and a fifth die showing $2$. He keeps the $2$ and rerolls the other four dice. What is the probability that the five dice then form a large straight, that is, five consecutive values ($1$-$2$-$3$-$4$-$5$ or $2$-$3$-$4$-$5$-$6$)?
