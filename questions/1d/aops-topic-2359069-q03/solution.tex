Count the complement. If the $k$ objects form one block, treat the block as a single object: $(n-k+1)!$ orders, times $k!$ orders inside the block. Subtracting from the $n!$ total arrangements gives the formula. $\blacksquare$ (Note that this counts arrangements where \emph{not all} $k$ are together; it does not require that no two of them are adjacent.)
