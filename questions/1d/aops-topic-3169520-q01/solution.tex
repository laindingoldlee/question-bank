Choose the four pairs represented in $\binom54=5$ ways. From each chosen pair choose its left or right glove, giving $2^4$ independent choices. Thus there are $5\cdot2^4=\boxed{80}$ sets. The exponent is four because four individual within-pair choices are made, not eight.
