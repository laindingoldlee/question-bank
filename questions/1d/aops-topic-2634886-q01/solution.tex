The first $2n$ positive integers are the first $n$ odd ones together with the even ones $2,4,\dots,2n$. The $k$th powers of the even ones sum to $2^k\left(1^k+\cdots+n^k\right)=2^kf_k(n)$. Hence
\[g_k(n)=\boxed{f_k(2n)-2^kf_k(n)}.\]
For example, with $k=1$: $f_1(2n)-2f_1(n)=n(2n+1)-n(n+1)=n^2$, the familiar sum of the first $n$ odd numbers.
