For a positive integer $k$, let $f_k(n)=1^k+2^k+\cdots+n^k$ be the sum of the $k$th powers of the first $n$ positive integers. Find a closed form, in terms of $f_k$, for
\[g_k(n)=1^k+3^k+5^k+\cdots+(2n-1)^k,\]
the sum of the $k$th powers of the first $n$ odd positive integers.
