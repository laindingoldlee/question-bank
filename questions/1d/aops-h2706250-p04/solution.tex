The cancellation divides by $x-1=0$, which is invalid. Before cancellation the equality is merely $0=0$ and provides no information about $x+1$.

If one nevertheless accepts $2=1$, adding $n-1$ to both sides gives $n+1=n$ for every positive integer $n$. Starting with $1=1$ and applying this repeatedly yields $n=1$ for every positive integer. The absurd conclusion traces back to division by zero.
