There are $2^{10}=1024$ subsets of the $10$ items. Remove the empty set and the $10$ one-item subsets:
\[
1024-1-10=\boxed{1013}.
\]
(Equivalently, $\dbinom{10}{2}+\dbinom{10}{3}+\cdots+\dbinom{10}{10}=1013$.)
