Let there be $N$ elves. Every elf brought at least one of the two, so by inclusion--exclusion the number who brought both is
\[
\dfrac{3}{4}N+\dfrac{2}{5}N-N=\dfrac{3}{20}N .
\]
Since $\dfrac{3}{4}N$ and $\dfrac{2}{5}N$ are whole numbers, $N$ is a multiple of $20$. The smallest case $N=20$ gives $15$ with candy canes, $8$ with cookies and $\boxed{3}$ with both.
