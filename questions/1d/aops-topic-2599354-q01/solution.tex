$x^2-200x+10000=(x-100)^2=0$, so $x=100$: diesel also gives $100$ miles per $5$ gallons. The $200$ gallons of oil are $40$ lots of $5$ gallons, giving $40\cdot100=4000$ miles, and the diesel gives another $4000$ miles. In total the car drives $\boxed{8000}$ miles.
