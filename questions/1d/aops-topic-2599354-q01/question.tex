A Lamborghini uses $5$ gallons of oil for every $100$ miles. With $5$ gallons of diesel it travels $x$ miles, where $x^2-200x+10000=0$. An oil store owner has $200$ gallons of oil and $200$ gallons of diesel. How many miles can the Lamborghini drive in total if it uses all of the oil and all of the diesel?
