There are integers $a_1,a_2,\dots,a_{240}$ such that
\[x(x+1)(x+2)(x+3)\cdots(x+239)=\sum_{k=1}^{240}a_kx^k.\]
Find the number of integers $k$ with $1\le k\le240$ such that $a_k$ is a multiple of $3$.
