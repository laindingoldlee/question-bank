On Monday, a single worker painted a fence alone for two hours. Then two more painters came and, together, the three finished the job $1.5$ hours later. On Tuesday, a single worker began painting an identical fence at 8:00 a.m. Later, two more workers showed up and, together, the three workers finished the fence at 10:54 a.m. All workers paint at the same constant rate. For how many minutes did the first worker paint alone on Tuesday?
