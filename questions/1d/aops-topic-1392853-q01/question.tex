Note that $3^2+3\cdot5+5^2=7^2$. Show that for all integers $m>n>0$,
\[
(a,b,c)=\left(m^2-n^2,\ 2mn+n^2,\ m^2+mn+n^2\right)
\]
satisfies $a^2+ab+b^2=c^2$, and that every solution of $a^2+ab+b^2=c^2$ in positive integers is a rational multiple of one of these triples. (Hint: find all rational points on the curve $X^2+XY+Y^2=1$ by drawing lines through the point $(-1,0)$.)
