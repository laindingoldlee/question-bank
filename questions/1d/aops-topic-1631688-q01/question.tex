Two circles are tangent at a point $T$. Two lines through $T$ (neither of them the common tangent at $T$) meet the first circle again at $A_1$ and $B_1$ and the second circle again at $A_2$ and $B_2$, where $A_1$, $A_2$ lie on one line and $B_1$, $B_2$ on the other. Prove that the chords $A_1B_1$ and $A_2B_2$ are parallel.
