Since $k^2-1=(k-1)(k+1)$,
\[\dfrac1{k^2-1}=\dfrac12\left(\dfrac1{k-1}-\dfrac1{k+1}\right).\]
The sum telescopes: the terms $\dfrac1{k-1}$ for $k=2,\dots,20$ are $\dfrac11,\dots,\dfrac1{19}$ and the terms $\dfrac1{k+1}$ are $\dfrac13,\dots,\dfrac1{21}$, so
\[\sum_{k=2}^{20}\dfrac1{k^2-1}=\dfrac12\left(1+\dfrac12-\dfrac1{20}-\dfrac1{21}\right)=\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac{630-21-20}{420}=\boxed{\dfrac{589}{840}}.\]
