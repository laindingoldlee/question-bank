Write $n=2^a m$ with $m$ odd. The odd factors of $n$ are the factors of $m$; call their number $d(m)$. Every factor of $n$ is $2^j t$ with $0\le j\le a$ and $t\mid m$, and it is even when $j\ge1$, so $n$ has $a\,d(m)$ even factors.

So $n$ is spicy exactly when $a\,d(m)=2d(m)$, that is $a=2$: the spicy numbers are $n=4m$ with $m$ odd. We need $4m\le420$, so $m$ is an odd number from $1$ to $105$; there are $\boxed{53}$ of them.
