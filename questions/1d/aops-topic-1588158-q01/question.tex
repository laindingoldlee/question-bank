A power tower is evaluated from the top down: $a^{b^c}$ means $a^{\left(b^c\right)}$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Write $3^{3^{3^3}}$ in the form $3^N$, giving $N$ as a power of $3$.
\item[(b)] Write $\left(\left(3^3\right)^3\right)^3$ in the form $3^M$.
\item[(c)] A student evaluates $3^{3^{3^3}}$ as $3^{3^9}$. What is the mistake?
\end{enumerate}
