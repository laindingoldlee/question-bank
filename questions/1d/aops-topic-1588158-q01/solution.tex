\textbf{(a)} Work from the top: $3^3=27$, so $3^{3^{3^3}}=3^{\left(3^{27}\right)}$, that is,
\[
N=3^{27}=7\,625\,597\,484\,987 .
\]

\textbf{(b)} Repeated powers multiply exponents, $\left(a^m\right)^n=a^{mn}$, so $\left(\left(3^3\right)^3\right)^3=3^{3\cdot3\cdot3}$, that is, $M=27$. (This is the bottom-up reading $27^{3^3}\to19683^3$ from the post; it equals $3^{27}\approx7.6\cdot10^{12}$, whereas the tower in (a) has more than $3.6\cdot10^{12}$ digits.)

\textbf{(c)} The top of the tower is $3^3=27$, not $9$: the student replaced $3^3$ by $3\cdot3$. Correctly, $3^{3^{3^3}}=3^{3^{27}}$. The tower $3^{3^{\left(3^3\right)}}$ in the second question of the post is the same number, since the top-down convention already puts the parentheses there.

So $\boxed{N=3^{27},\ M=27}$.
