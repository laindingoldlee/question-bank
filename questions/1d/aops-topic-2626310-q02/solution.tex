Let $A_i$ be the arrangements in which couple $i$ sits together. Treating a couple as a block (in $2$ orders): $|A_i|=2\cdot5!=240$, $|A_i\cap A_j|=2^2\cdot4!=96$, $|A_1\cap A_2\cap A_3|=2^3\cdot3!=48$. By inclusion-exclusion the number with no couple together is
\[6!-3\cdot240+3\cdot96-48=720-720+288-48=\boxed{240}.\]
