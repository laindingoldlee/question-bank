Let $t=x+\dfrac{1}{x}\ge2$. Then $t^3-3t=18$, or $(t-3)(t^2+3t+6)=0$, giving $t=3$. Expanding or using the recurrence for $x^n+x^{-n}$ gives the fifth sum $t^5-5t^3+5t=243-135+15=\boxed{123}$.
