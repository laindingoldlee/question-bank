Take the army's speed as $1$ and the runner's as $v$. The trip forward takes $\dfrac{200}{v-1}$ and the trip back $\dfrac{200}{v+1}$; during the total time the army moves $200$, so the total time is $200$:
\[\dfrac{1}{v-1}+\dfrac{1}{v+1}=1\ \Longrightarrow\ 2v=v^2-1\ \Longrightarrow\ v=1+\sqrt2.\]
The runner runs $200v=\boxed{200+200\sqrt2}\approx482.8$ yards.
