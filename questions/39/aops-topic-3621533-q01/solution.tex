The name lengths for 1 through 10 are $(3,3,5,4,4,3,5,5,4,3)$. For each $a<b$, compute L and test one of $2a=b+L$, $2b=a+L$, or $2L=a+b$. The suitable pairs are $\boxed{(1,4),(2,10),(4,10),(5,6),(5,7),(6,7)}$. To verify completeness without an oracle, the stated three equalities applied to all 45 pairs form an exhaustive finite test. With an oracle and exactly six promised successes, test pairs in lexicographic order until either six successes have appeared or thirty-nine failures have appeared; in the latter case all untested pairs are successes. This uses at most 44 queries and is a guaranteed procedure, without asserting it is optimal.
