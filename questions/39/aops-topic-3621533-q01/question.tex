For two distinct integers a,b from 1 through 10, let L be the total number of letters in their English names (one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten). Call the unordered pair suitable if a,b,L form an arithmetic progression in some order, with nonzero common difference. Find every suitable pair. Then give a guaranteed finite procedure for finding all suitable pairs if only a yes/no oracle is available and exactly six pairs are promised to be suitable; an optimal procedure is not required.
