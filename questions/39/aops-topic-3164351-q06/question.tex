Tile a fixed 5-by-3 rectangle along its unit grid lines using five rectangles of sizes 1-by-1,1-by-2,1-by-3,1-by-4,1-by-5, each exactly once. Pieces may be rotated. Count distinct placements; reflected placements count separately.
