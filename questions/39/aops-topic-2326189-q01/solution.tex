If $n^2$ ended in $51$, then $n^2=100k+51$ for some integer $k$, and since $100$ is a multiple of $4$, $n^2\equiv51\equiv3\pmod4$. But squares leave remainder $0$ or $1$ on division by $4$: $(2j)^2=4j^2$ and $(2j+1)^2=4(j^2+j)+1$. So no square ends in $51$. $\blacksquare$

(The same argument rules out the endings $03,07,11,15,\dots,99$, i.e. every two-digit ending that is $3$ modulo $4$.)
