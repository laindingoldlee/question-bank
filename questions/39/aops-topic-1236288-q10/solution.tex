We need the derangements of $4$ objects: $D_4=4!\left(1-1+\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{6}+\dfrac{1}{24}\right)=9$, out of $24$ arrangements. The probability is $\dfrac{9}{24}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{8}}$.
