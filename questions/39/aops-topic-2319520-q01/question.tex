A group of $100$ people are labelled $1$ to $100$. For each $n$, person $n$ gives a hug to every person $k$ with $k<n$ and $\gcd(k,n)=1$. (So person $1$ hugs nobody, and person $100$ hugs everyone below $100$ who is relatively prime to $100$.) Which person gives the most hugs?
