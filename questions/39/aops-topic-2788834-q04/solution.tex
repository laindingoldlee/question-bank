We construct a $k$-digit integer $N_k$, all of whose digits lie in $\{1,2,3,4,5\}$, divisible by $5^k$. Begin with $N_1=5$. Given $N_k$, choose $d\in\{1,2,3,4,5\}$ such that $\dfrac{N_k}{5^k}+d2^k\equiv0\pmod5$. Such a digit exists because multiplication by $2^k$ permutes residues modulo 5. Then $N_{k+1}=d10^k+N_k$ has the required digits and divisibility. Induction reaches $k=1000$.
