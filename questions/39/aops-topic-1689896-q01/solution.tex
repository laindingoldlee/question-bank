Removing the brackets, the minus sign in front of the second bracket changes both signs inside it:
\[
(b^2-a^2)-(a^2-b^2)=b^2-a^2-a^2+b^2=\boxed{2b^2-2a^2}=2(b^2-a^2).
\]
The book's answer $2b^2$ is wrong in general: for $a=1$, $b=2$ the expression is $(4-1)-(1-4)=6$, while $2b^2=8$. The two agree only when $a=0$.
