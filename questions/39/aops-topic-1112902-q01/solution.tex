Alphabetical order of four-letter codes is the same as counting in base $26$ with digits $A=0,B=1,\dots,Z=25$. Book AAAA is number $0$, and the book with number $n$ is in spot $100.00+0.01n$.

The letters of MATH are $M=12$, $A=0$, $T=19$, $H=7$, so its number is
\[
n=12\cdot26^3+0\cdot26^2+19\cdot26+7=210912+0+494+7=211413 .
\]
Its spot is $100+0.01\cdot211413=100+2114.13=\boxed{2214.13}$.
