A librarian arranges her books by a code made of four capital letters. Book AAAA is placed in spot $100.00$, book AAAB in spot $100.01$, book AAAC in spot $100.02$, and so on: the books are in alphabetical order of their codes, and each next book is placed $0.01$ further along. In what spot is book MATH placed?
