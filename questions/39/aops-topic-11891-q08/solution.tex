Let the tetrahedron be $ABCD$ with base $ABC$, and let $G$ be the centre of the base, the foot of the height $h=DG$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s=0.2){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
pair A=(0.0000,0.0000),B=(5.0000,0.0000),C=(4.6651,1.5155),D=(3.2217,4.5877),G=(3.2217,0.5052);
draw(A--B--C,linewidth(0.9)); draw(A--D--B,linewidth(0.9)); draw(C--D,linewidth(0.9)); draw(A--C,dashed);
draw(D--G,red+dashed); draw(A--G,blue+dashed);
dot(G,red);
label("$A$",A,SW); label("$B$",B,SE); label("$C$",C,E); label("$D$",D,N); label("$G$",G,E);
label("$5$",(A+B)/2,S); label("$h$",(D+G)/2,E,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The base is equilateral with side $5$, so its area is $\dfrac{25\sqrt3}{4}$, and $AG$ is $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of its altitude $\dfrac{5\sqrt3}{2}$, so $AG=\dfrac{5\sqrt3}{3}=\dfrac{5}{\sqrt3}$. In the right triangle $AGD$,
\[
h^2=AD^2-AG^2=25-\dfrac{25}{3}=\dfrac{50}{3},\qquad h=\dfrac{5\sqrt6}{3}.
\]
Hence
\[
V=\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{25\sqrt3}{4}\cdot\dfrac{5\sqrt6}{3}=\dfrac{125\sqrt{18}}{36}=\boxed{\dfrac{125\sqrt2}{12}}\text{ cubic units}.
\]
