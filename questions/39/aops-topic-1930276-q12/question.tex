Two non-parallel lines $a$ and $b$ are drawn on a sheet of paper, but they meet at a point $Z$ off the sheet. Using straightedge and compass on the sheet only, construct an angle twice as large as the acute angle $\theta$ between $a$ and $b$, and prove that the construction works.
