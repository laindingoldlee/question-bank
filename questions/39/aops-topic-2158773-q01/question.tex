Let $m$ and $n$ be positive integers and let $a$ and $b$ be integers.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that the system
\[x\equiv a\pmod m,\qquad x\equiv b\pmod n\]
has an integer solution if and only if $\gcd(m,n)$ divides $a-b$.
\item[(b)] Does the system $x\equiv1\pmod4$, $x\equiv2\pmod6$ have a solution?
\end{enumerate}
