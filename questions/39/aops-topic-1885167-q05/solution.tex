The numbers with remainder $5$ on division by $7$ are $5,12,19,\dots$, and the first of these with remainder $3$ on division by $4$ is $19$. By the Chinese remainder theorem the solutions are exactly $n\equiv19\pmod{28}$: $19,47,75,103,\dots$ The largest below $100$ is $\boxed{75}$ (check: $75=4\cdot18+3=7\cdot10+5$).
