Choose the pixel that differs ($n^2$ ways) and the colours of the other $n^2-1$ pixels ($2^{n^2-1}$ ways); the two images in the pair have that pixel black and white respectively. Each pair arises exactly once, so the count is $\boxed{n^2\cdot2^{n^2-1}}$. (For $n=4$ this is $16\cdot2^{15}=524288$.)
