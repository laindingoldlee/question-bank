Consider the $m+1$ numbers $a^0,a^1,\dots,a^m$ modulo $m$. There are only $m$ residues, so by the pigeonhole principle two of them agree: $a^b\equiv a^c\pmod m$ for some $0\le b<c\le m$. $\blacksquare$
