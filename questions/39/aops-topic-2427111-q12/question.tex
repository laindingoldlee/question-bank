The dots $(a,b)$ with integer coordinates $0\le a\le99$ and $0\le b\le99$ form a $100\times100$ grid, with $(0,0)$ at the bottom left. A point $P$ starts at $(0,0)$ and may jump to any dot of the grid of the form $(x-y,\,x+y)$, where $x$ and $y$ are positive integers. How many dots of the grid can $P$ jump to?
