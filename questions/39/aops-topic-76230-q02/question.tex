Erica flips a fair coin. Nate then flips the coin and wins if his flip matches Erica's. If it does not match, Noah flips the coin and wins if his flip matches Nate's. If Noah does not win, Erica flips and wins if her flip matches Noah's, and the game continues in this way (Nate, Noah, Erica, Nate, \dots) until someone wins. What is the probability that Nate wins? Express your answer as a common fraction.
