After Erica's first flip, every flip matches the previous flip with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, independently of everything before, and the first flip that matches wins for the person who made it. The flips are made by Nate, Noah, Erica, Nate, Noah, Erica, \dots, so Nate makes flips number $1,4,7,\dots$ of this sequence. Hence
\[
P(\text{Nate})=\dfrac{1}{2}+\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^4+\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^7+\cdots=\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{2}}{1-\dfrac{1}{8}}=\boxed{\dfrac{4}{7}}.
\]
(Similarly Noah wins with probability $\dfrac{2}{7}$ and Erica with $\dfrac{1}{7}$. The answer $\dfrac{1}{5}$ suggested in the post is wrong.)
