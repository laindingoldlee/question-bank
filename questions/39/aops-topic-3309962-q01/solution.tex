For exactly $k$ silver squares, choose their $k$ rows and $k$ columns and match the chosen rows to columns: $\binom4k^2k!$ choices. Hence the total is
\[\sum_{k=0}^4\binom4k^2k!=1+16+72+96+24=\boxed{209}.\]
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath"); size(220);
for(int k=0;k<=4;++k){draw((k,0)--(k,4));draw((0,k)--(4,k));} for(int k=0;k<3;++k)filldraw((k+0.15,k+0.15)--(k+0.85,k+0.15)--(k+0.85,k+0.85)--(k+0.15,k+0.85)--cycle,gray); label("Example with three silver squares",(2,-0.5));
\end{asy}
