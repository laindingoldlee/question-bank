For $a\ne0$, multiplication by $a$ is invertible. The equation $aq=a$ has the unique solution $q=1$, while $aq=0$ has the unique solution $q=0$. In contrast, $0q=0$ holds for every $q$, so it does not specify a unique quotient. Thus $\dfrac{0}{0}$ is undefined.
