Each complementary pair gives $\tan\theta+\tan(90^\circ-\theta)=\dfrac{2}{\sin2\theta}$. The product is therefore $\dfrac{16}{\sin10^\circ\sin30^\circ\sin50^\circ\sin70^\circ}$. The identity $\sin3x=4\sin x\sin(60^\circ+x)\sin(60^\circ-x)$ at $x=10^\circ$ gives $\sin10^\circ\sin50^\circ\sin70^\circ=\dfrac{1}{8}$. Including $\sin30^\circ=\dfrac{1}{2}$ makes the denominator $\dfrac{1}{16}$, and the answer $\boxed{256}$.
