Find the value of
\[
\sqrt{7+\sqrt{7^2+\sqrt{7^4+\sqrt{7^8+\cdots}}}}\ ,
\]
where the exponents of $7$ are $1,2,4,8,\dots$ and the value is the limit of the finite nested radicals obtained by stopping after the term $7^{2^n}$.
