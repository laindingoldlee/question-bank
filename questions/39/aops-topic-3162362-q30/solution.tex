Repeated difference-of-squares factorisation gives
\[2^{36}-1=(2^9-1)(2^9+1)(2^{18}+1)=(7\cdot73)(3^3\cdot19)(5\cdot13\cdot37\cdot109).\]
Every displayed factor is prime, verifiable by trial division up to its square root. The distinct-prime sum is $ 3+5+7+13+19+37+73+109=\boxed{266}$.
