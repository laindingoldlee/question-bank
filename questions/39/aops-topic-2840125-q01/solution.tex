Let $a_n$ be the number of $n$-step walks from $A$ back to $A$, and $c_n$ the number ending at a given other vertex (by symmetry $B$ and $C$ give the same count). Then $a_n+2c_n=2^n$, and $a_{n+1}=2c_n$. So $c_{n+1}=\dfrac{2^{n+1}-a_{n+1}}{2}=2^n-c_n$. With $c_0=0$ this gives $c_n=\dfrac{2^n-(-1)^n}3$. Hence $c_{10}=\dfrac{1024-1}3=\boxed{341}$.
