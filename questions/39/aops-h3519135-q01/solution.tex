The two possible equilateral apices on base $AB$ are
\[\left(\dfrac{a\pm\sqrt3b}{2},\dfrac{b\pm\sqrt3a}{2}\right).\]
The minus signs cannot yield two positive coordinates: they would require $a>\sqrt3b$ and $b>\sqrt3a$ simultaneously. Hence $a+\sqrt3b=40$ and $\sqrt3a+b=50$. Solving gives $a=25\sqrt3-20$, $b=20\sqrt3-25$, both positive. The side length squared is $AB^2=a^2+b^2=4100-2000\sqrt3$. Thus the area is
\[\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}(4100-2000\sqrt3)=1025\sqrt3-1500}.\]

\end{asy}
