Once the display is irrational it can never later become an integer: looking backwards, an integer result could only have come from an integer via either operation. Thus taking a square root at 2,3,or 5 permanently prevents hitting 6. Let $p_j$ be the chance from integer $j\le6$. Then $p_6=1$, $p_0=p_1=p_2$, $p_2=\dfrac{p_3}{2}$, $p_3=\dfrac{p_4}{2}$, $p_4=\dfrac{p_5+p_2}{2}$, and $p_5=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore $p_2=\dfrac{p_4}{4}=\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{p_2}{2}}{4}$, giving $\boxed{p_0=\dfrac{1}{14}}$. The self-loops at 0,1 have probability tending to zero of continuing forever, so the equations account for all outcomes.
