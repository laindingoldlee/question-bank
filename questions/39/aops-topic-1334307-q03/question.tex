Last year the ticket price for an opening ceremony was $100$ dollars, and $50{,}000$ people attended. The organisers know that for every dollar the price is raised, $1{,}000$ fewer people attend (and for every dollar it is lowered, $1{,}000$ more attend). Assuming there are no costs, what whole-dollar ticket price gives the largest total ticket income?
