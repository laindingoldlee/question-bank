At a price of $p$ dollars the attendance is $50000-1000(p-100)=1000(150-p)$, so the income is
\[
I(p)=1000\,p(150-p)=1000\bigl(5625-(p-75)^2\bigr).
\]
This is largest when $p=75$, so the best price is $\boxed{75\text{ dollars}}$, giving $75\cdot75000=5{,}625{,}000$ dollars. Note that the best move is to lower the price, not raise it.
