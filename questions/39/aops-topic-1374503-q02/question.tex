Each of the $6$ congruent sectors of a circular spinner is coloured with one of $4$ colours (colours may repeat). Colourings that differ by a rotation of the spinner are considered the same. How many different colourings are there? Solve it without Burnside's lemma, by sorting colourings by their rotational symmetry.
