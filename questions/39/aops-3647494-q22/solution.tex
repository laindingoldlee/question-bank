Choose $B=(0,0)$, $C=(16,0)$ and $A=(\dfrac{7}{2},\dfrac{5\sqrt{11}}{2})$, as follows from $AB=9$ and $AC=15$. The two intersections on $BC$ are $D=(4,0)$ and $E=(9,0)$, so the circle centre has first coordinate $\dfrac{13}{2}$. Power of $B$ is 36, hence its tangent length along $BA$ is six. Perpendicularity to $BA$ at that tangent point then gives centre $U=(\dfrac{13}{2},\dfrac{25}{(2\sqrt{11})})$ and radius $\dfrac{15}{\sqrt{11}}$. The line $AC$ is $\sqrt{11}x+5y-16\sqrt{11}=0$, whose distance from $U$ is $\dfrac{7}{\sqrt{11}}$. Therefore the intercepted chord has length $2\sqrt{\dfrac{225}{11}-\dfrac{49}{11}}=\boxed8$.
