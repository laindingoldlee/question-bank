Write two perpendicular equal edge vectors of the resulting square as $(a,b)$ and $(-b,a)$. The original edge vectors are $(a,\dfrac{b}{2}),(-b,\dfrac{a}{2})$. Horizontal stretching instead gives $(2a,\dfrac{b}{2}),(-2b,\dfrac{a}{2})$. Equality of their squared lengths yields $4a^2+\dfrac{b^2}{4}=4b^2+\dfrac{a^2}{4}$, so $a^2=b^2>0$. The square side is $\sqrt2|a|$ and the rhombus side is $\dfrac{\sqrt{17}}{2}|a|$. The ratio is $\boxed{\dfrac{2\sqrt{34}}{17}}$.
