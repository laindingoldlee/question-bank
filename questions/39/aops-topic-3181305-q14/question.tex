A student waves on day 1. Each later day he waves with probability $\dfrac{3}{4}$ if he waved the preceding day, or $\dfrac{1}{4}$ if he did not. Write the day 8 waving probability as $\dfrac{p}{q}$ in lowest terms and find $p+q$.
