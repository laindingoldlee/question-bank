Let $u_n$ be the waving probability. Then $u_{n+1}=\dfrac{3}{4}u_n+\dfrac{1}{4}(1-u_n)=\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{2}u_n$, so $u_{n+1}-\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{1}{2}(u_n-\dfrac{1}{2})$. With $u_1=1$, we get $u_8=\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{256}=\dfrac{129}{256}$. Hence $\boxed{385}$.
