Let $\angle A=x$. Then $\angle B=\dfrac {x}{2}$, $\angle C=\dfrac {x}{3}$ and $\angle D=\dfrac {x}{4}$. The angles of a quadrilateral add up to $360^\circ$:
\[
x\left(1+\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{4}\right)=\dfrac{25x}{12}=360^\circ,
\]
so $x=\dfrac{12\cdot360^\circ}{25}=\boxed{172.8^\circ}$. The other angles are $\angle B=86.4^\circ$, $\angle C=57.6^\circ$ and $\angle D=43.2^\circ$. Such a quadrilateral exists; one is drawn below (the angle at $A$ is almost straight).

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair p(real t){return (Cos(t),Sin(t));}
real a=1.5, b=3, c=4.579468053231521;
pair A=(0,0), B=A+a*p(0), C=B+b*p(93.6), D=C+c*p(216);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
label("$A$",A,S); label("$B$",B,SE); label("$C$",C,N); label("$D$",D,W);
label("$172.8^\circ$",A+(0,0.3),fontsize(8pt)); label("$86.4^\circ$",B+0.8*dir(137),fontsize(8pt));
label("$57.6^\circ$",C+0.9*dir(245),fontsize(8pt)); label("$43.2^\circ$",D+1.5*dir(14),fontsize(8pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
