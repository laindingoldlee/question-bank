A right triangle $OAB$ has its right angle at $O$, and $\overline{OH}$ is the altitude from $O$ to $\overline{AB}$. Given that $AB\cdot AH\cdot HB=21$, the maximum possible value of $OA\cdot OB\cdot OH$ is $M$ and the minimum is $m$. Find $M+m$.
