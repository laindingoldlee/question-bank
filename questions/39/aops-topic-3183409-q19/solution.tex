Let unit vectors $u,v$ point along $AB,AD$, with $A=0$. Then $B=13u,D=37v,C=13u+37v$. The bisector at $A$ has direction $u+v$, so its intersection with line $CD$ is $F=37u+37v$. The bisector at $B$ has direction $v-u$, so the parallel through $A$ meets that line at $E=-37u+37v$. Hence $EF=74|u|=\boxed{74}$.

\end{asy}
