The available multiset has two F, two K, and five other distinct letters. Omitting one F or one K gives $\dfrac{8!}{2!}$ strings in each case. Omitting one of the five singletons gives $\dfrac{8!}{2!2!}$ strings for each omitted letter. These cases have different resulting multisets, so the total is $2\cdot20160+5\cdot10080=\boxed{90720}$.
