On day $k$ Peter puts up $48+2(k-1)$ signs and $3$ are taken down, so the number of signs grows by $45+2(k-1)$ on day $k$. After $16$ days the number of signs is
\[
\sum_{k=1}^{16}\bigl(45+2(k-1)\bigr)=16\cdot45+2(0+1+\cdots+15)=720+240=\boxed{960}.
\]
