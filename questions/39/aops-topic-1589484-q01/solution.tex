The gradient is the drop in elevation per unit of horizontal distance, i.e.\ the absolute value of the slope of the stream's profile:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((0,0)--(5.6,0),gray,Arrow(TeXHead));draw((0,0)--(0,1900/400+0.3),gray,Arrow(TeXHead));
label("distance (mi)",(5.6,0),E,gray);label("elevation (ft)",(0,1900/400+0.3),N,gray);
pair P=(0,4),Q=(5,1);
draw(P--Q,blue+linewidth(1));dot(P);dot(Q);
for(int i=1;i<=5;++i){draw((i,0)--(i,-0.1));label(string(i),(i,-0.1),S);}
label("$1600$",(0,4),W);label("$400$",(0,1),W);draw((0,1)--(-0.1,1));
draw(Q--(5,4)--P,red+dashed);
label("$1200$ ft",(5,2.5),E,red);label("$5$ mi",(2.5,4),N,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

\[
\dfrac{1600-400}{5}=\dfrac{1200\text{ ft}}{5\text{ mi}}=\boxed{240\text{ feet per mile}}.
\]
