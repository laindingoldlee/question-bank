He paid $70\%$ of $\$10$, which is $\$7$. He sold it for $150\%$ of $\$7$:
\[1.5\cdot7=\boxed{\$10.50}.\]
(Note that $-30\%$ followed by $+50\%$ is not $+20\%$ of the original price, because the $50\%$ is taken of the smaller amount: $0.7\cdot1.5=1.05$, a $5\%$ increase.)
