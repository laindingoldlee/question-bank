The denominator is $\sum_{k=1}^6\dfrac{1}{(2k-1)(2k+1)}=\dfrac{1}{2}\sum_{k=1}^6\left(\dfrac{1}{2k-1}-\dfrac{1}{2k+1}\right)=\dfrac{1}{2}(1-\dfrac{1}{13})=\dfrac{6}{13}$. Thus the expression is $24\cdot\dfrac{13}{6}=\boxed{52}$.
