Two entries cannot have the same residue modulo $125$, nor residues that are negatives of one another. The residues split into $\{0\}$ and $62$ opposite pairs $\{r,125-r\}$. At most one entry may come from each group, giving at most $63$. The integers $0,1,\ldots,62$ attain this: their nonzero pairwise differences and their pairwise sums are between $1$ and $123$. Thus the maximum is $\boxed{63}$.
