For $x\ne-4$,
\[
g(x)+2=\dfrac{2x+2x+8}{x+4}=\dfrac{4(x+2)}{x+4},
\]
so, when also $g(x)\ne-2$, that is $x\ne-2$,
\[
f(g(x))=\dfrac{4g(x)}{g(x)+2}=\dfrac{\dfrac{8x}{x+4}}{\dfrac{4(x+2)}{x+4}}=\boxed{\dfrac{2x}{x+2}} .
\]
The composition is defined for all real $x$ except $x=-4$ (where $g$ is undefined) and $x=-2$ (where $g(x)=-2$ and $f$ is undefined).
