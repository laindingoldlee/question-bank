Working backwards, $f(f(x))$ is $6$ or $10$. Since $f(x)\ge0$, the next possible values are $f(x)=2,14,18$. Thus $x=6,10,-6,22,-10,26$. Their sum is $p=48$, and the minimum is $q=-10$. The product is $\boxed{-480}$.
