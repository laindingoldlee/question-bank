The ellipses are $\dfrac{x^2}{16}+\dfrac{y^2}{9}=1$ and $\dfrac{x^2}{9}+\dfrac{y^2}{16}=1$. Subtracting, $\left(\dfrac1{16}-\dfrac19\right)(x^2-y^2)=0$, so $x^2=y^2$. Then $x^2\left(\dfrac1{16}+\dfrac19\right)=1$, so $x^2=\dfrac{144}{25}$. The intersection points are $\left(\pm\dfrac{12}5,\pm\dfrac{12}5\right)$ (all four sign choices).

These are the vertices of an axis-parallel square of side $\dfrac{24}{5}$ (consecutive vertices differ in exactly one coordinate, by $\dfrac{24}5$). Its area is $\left(\dfrac{24}{5}\right)^2=\boxed{\dfrac{576}{25}}$.
