Rewrite the equation as $100x^2-17x-2i=0$. The quadratic formula holds for complex coefficients, so
\[x=\dfrac{17\pm w}{200},\]
where $w$ is a square root of the discriminant $\Delta=17^2-4\cdot100\cdot(-2i)=289+800i$.

\textbf{The square root.} Write $w=u+vi$ with $u,v$ real. Then $w^2=u^2-v^2+2uvi=289+800i$ gives $u^2-v^2=289$ and $uv=400$. Also $|w|^2=|\Delta|$, so
\[u^2+v^2=\sqrt{289^2+800^2}=\sqrt{723521}.\]
Hence $u^2=\dfrac{\sqrt{723521}+289}{2}$ and $v^2=\dfrac{\sqrt{723521}-289}{2}$. Since $uv=400>0$, $u$ and $v$ have the same sign, and we may take
\[u=\sqrt{\dfrac{\sqrt{723521}+289}{2}}\approx23.8705,\qquad v=\sqrt{\dfrac{\sqrt{723521}-289}{2}}\approx16.7571.\]

\textbf{The roots.}
\[\boxed{x_1=\dfrac{17+u}{200}+\dfrac{v}{200}\,i\approx0.2044+0.0838i,\qquad x_2=\dfrac{17-u}{200}-\dfrac{v}{200}\,i\approx-0.0344-0.0838i}\]
with $u,v$ as above. (Check: $x_1+x_2=\dfrac{17}{100}$ and $x_1x_2=\dfrac{17^2-w^2}{200^2}=\dfrac{289-289-800i}{40000}=-\dfrac{i}{50}$, as Vieta's formulas require for $100x^2-17x-2i$.)
