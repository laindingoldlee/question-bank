Let the common opposite-face sum be $S$. The hidden labels are $S-6,S-10,S-15$. If $S$ were even, the first two would be distinct even primes, which is impossible since the only even prime is $2$. Thus $S$ is odd, and $S-15$ is an even prime, forcing $S-15=2$. Hence $S=17$ and the hidden labels are $11,7,2$. All are prime, so the product is $\boxed{154}$.
