Factor: $y(2x+y-1)=46$. So $y$ is a divisor of $46=2\cdot23$, i.e. $y\in\{\pm1,\pm2,\pm23,\pm46\}$, and then
\[
2x=\dfrac{46}{y}-y+1 .
\]
The right side must be even, i.e. $\dfrac{46}{y}-y$ must be odd. Since $46=y\cdot\dfrac{46}{y}$ has exactly one factor $2$, one of $y$ and $\dfrac{46}{y}$ is even and the other odd, so this always holds. The eight divisors give
\[
\boxed{(x,y)=(23,1),\ (11,2),\ (-10,23),\ (-22,46),\ (-22,-1),\ (-10,-2),\ (11,-23),\ (23,-46)}.
\]
For example $y=2$: $2x=23-2+1=22$, $x=11$, and $2\cdot11\cdot2+4-2=46$. (The solutions in positive integers are $(23,1)$ and $(11,2)$.)
