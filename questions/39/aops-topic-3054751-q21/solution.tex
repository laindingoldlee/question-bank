The altitude is $2\sqrt3$, and the inradius is one third of it, $\dfrac{2\sqrt3}{3}$. The circle area is $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$ and the triangle area is $4\sqrt3$. Their ratio is $\boxed{\dfrac{\pi\sqrt3}{9}}$.
