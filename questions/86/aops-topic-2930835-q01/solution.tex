Every cube is $\equiv0,1$ or $-1\pmod9$: if $3\mid n$ then $27\mid n^3$, and $(3m\pm1)^3=27m^3\pm27m^2+9m\pm1\equiv\pm1$. So a sum of three cubes is $\equiv0,\pm1,\pm2,\pm3\pmod9$ and never $\equiv\pm4$. Since $4\equiv4$, there are no solutions. $\blacksquare$
