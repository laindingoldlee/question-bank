Write $i=e^{i(\dfrac{\pi}{2}+2\pi k)}$. Taking fourth roots gives $\boxed{z=\cos(\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{k\pi}{2})+i\sin(\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac{k\pi}{2}),\quad k=0,1,2,3}$. These four distinct roots have fourth power $i$, and a degree 4 polynomial has no others.
