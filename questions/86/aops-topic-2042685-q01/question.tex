A ball moves inside a rectangular box of dimensions \(10\) by \(50\), bouncing off the walls elastically (angle of incidence equals angle of reflection). The ball starts from the midpoint \(M\) of side \(AB\), which is one of the sides of length \(50\), and moves along a line of slope \(k\) (measured with \(AB\) horizontal). The ball reaches a corner of the box for the first time right after exactly three bounces off the walls. Which of the following numbers could be \(k\)?

\(\textbf{(A) }0.2\qquad\textbf{(B) }0.5\qquad\textbf{(C) }0.75\qquad\textbf{(D) }1.6\qquad\textbf{(E) }1.25\)
