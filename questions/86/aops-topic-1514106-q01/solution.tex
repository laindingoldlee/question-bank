\textbf{(a)} Using the difference of squares twice,
\[
x^{8k}-16=\left(x^{4k}-4\right)\left(x^{4k}+4\right)=\left(x^{2k}-2\right)\left(x^{2k}+2\right)\left(x^{4k}+4\right).
\]
So the calculator's answer is correct (it is not a glitch); it is just not factored as far as possible.

\textbf{(b)} Complete the square (the Sophie Germain identity):
\[
x^{4k}+4=\left(x^{4k}+4x^{2k}+4\right)-4x^{2k}=\left(x^{2k}+2\right)^2-\left(2x^k\right)^2=\left(x^{2k}-2x^k+2\right)\left(x^{2k}+2x^k+2\right).
\]
Therefore
\[
\boxed{x^{8k}-16=\left(x^{2k}-2\right)\left(x^{2k}+2\right)\left(x^{2k}-2x^k+2\right)\left(x^{2k}+2x^k+2\right)}.
\]
For $k=1$: $x^8-16=(x^2-2)(x^2+2)(x^2-2x+2)(x^2+2x+2)$.
