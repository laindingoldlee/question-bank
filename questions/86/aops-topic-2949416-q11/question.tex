Nine chickens stand on a 3-by 3 lattice grid with unit spacing. Independently, each chooses one of its nearest grid neighbours uniformly and moves halfway toward it. If the expected number of pairs that meet is $\dfrac{p}{q}$ in lowest terms, find $p+q$.
