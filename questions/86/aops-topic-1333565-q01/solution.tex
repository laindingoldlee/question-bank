The number of trailing zeros of $m!$ is the exponent of $5$ in $m!$ (there are more factors of $2$), which by Legendre's formula is $\sum_{j\ge1}\left\lfloor\dfrac{m}{5^j}\right\rfloor$. For $m=5^8$:
\[
\dfrac{5^8}{5}+\dfrac{5^8}{5^2}+\dots+\dfrac{5^8}{5^8}=5^7+5^6+\dots+5+1=\dfrac{5^8-1}{4}=\boxed{97656}.
\]
The student's sum $5^7+\dots+5=97655$ is one too small: it misses the term $\left\lfloor\dfrac{5^8}{5^8}\right\rfloor=1$, which counts the extra factor of $5$ in $5^8$ itself.
