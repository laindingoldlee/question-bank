Two numbers are chosen independently and uniformly at random, with replacement, from $\{1,2,\dots,x\}$.

(a) Show that the expected value of the larger one is $\dfrac{(x+1)(4x-1)}{6x}$.

(b) More generally, if $k$ numbers are chosen independently and uniformly from the interval $(0,1)$, show that the expected maximum is $\dfrac k{k+1}$.
