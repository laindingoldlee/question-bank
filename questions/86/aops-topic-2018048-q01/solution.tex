The money meter advances at $\$9.00$ per $3$ seconds, that is $\$3.00$ per second. The volume advances at $15$ gallons per $105$ seconds, so one gallon takes
\[
\dfrac{105}{15}=7\text{ seconds}.
\]
Since both rates are constant, one gallon costs $7\cdot \$3.00=\boxed{\$21.00}$.
