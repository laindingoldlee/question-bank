Count subsets of size $r+1$ from $\{1,\ldots,n+1\}$ by their largest element. If it is $k+1$, the other $r$ elements can be chosen in $\binom kr$ ways from the first $k$. Summing gives the identity. Alternatively Pascal's identity gives $\binom kr=\binom{k+1}{r+1}-\binom k{r+1}$. Summing telescopes to $\binom{n+1}{r+1}-\binom r{r+1}=\binom{n+1}{r+1}$. These arguments avoid the source's off-by-one count of variables in stars and bars.
