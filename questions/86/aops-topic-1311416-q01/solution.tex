They start $0.8$ mile apart. During minute $k$ they close at $(k+2)$ mph, i.e. $\dfrac{k+2}{60}$ mile. After $m$ full minutes they have closed
\[
\dfrac{1}{60}\sum_{k=1}^{m}(k+2)=\dfrac{1}{60}\left(\dfrac{m(m+1)}{2}+2m\right).
\]
We need $0.8=\dfrac{48}{60}$ mile. After $7$ minutes they have closed $\dfrac{28+14}{60}=\dfrac{42}{60}$, and after $8$ minutes $\dfrac{52}{60}$, so they collide during the $8$th minute. The remaining $\dfrac{6}{60}$ mile is closed at $10$ mph, taking $\dfrac{1}{100}$ h $=36$ s. The collision happens after $7$ min $36$ s $=\boxed{456.0\text{ s}}$.
