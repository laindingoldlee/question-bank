A train $1760$ yards ($1$ mile) long is divided into $10$ equal cars, numbered $1$ (front) to $10$ (back). A toy train TT1 starts at the front of the train and a toy train TT2 starts at the boundary between cars $8$ and $9$; they move toward each other along the train. TT1 moves at $1$ mph during the first minute, $2$ mph during the second minute, and so on (its speed rises by $1$ mph at the start of each minute). TT2 moves at a constant $2$ mph. How long after they start do they collide, to the nearest tenth of a second?
