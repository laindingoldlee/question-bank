The sum is $\log_{13}(n!)$ for $n=513513$. The rigorous Stirling bounds give $L+\dfrac{1}{12n+1}<\ln(n!)<L+\dfrac{1}{12n}$, where $L=(n+\dfrac{1}{2})\ln n-n+\dfrac{1}{2}\ln(2\pi)$. Dividing both bounds by $\ln13$ places the result between $2432286.8747396$ and $2432286.8747398$ (these rounded outer bounds comfortably contain the numerical evaluations). Hence its floor is $\boxed{2432286}$. The small remainder bounds certify that no integer boundary is near the numerical value.
