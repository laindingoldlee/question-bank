By symmetry use the eighth of $[-1,1]^2$ given by $0\le y\le x\le1$. The distances are $1-x$ and $\dfrac{x-y}{\sqrt2}$. The required inequality is $y<(1+\sqrt2)x-\sqrt2$. Its region in this eighth is a triangle with vertices $(2-\sqrt2,0),(1,0),(1,1)$, of area $\dfrac{\sqrt2-1}{2}$. Dividing by the eighth-region area $\dfrac{1}{2}$ gives $\boxed{\sqrt2-1}$. Boundaries have probability zero.
