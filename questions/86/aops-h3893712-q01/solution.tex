Among the other labels, 6 is coprime only to 1 and 5. It must occupy a corner, since middle cells have three neighbors. Choose the corner in four ways and place 1 and 5 in its neighbors in two ways. The remaining cells form a three-cell path. The numbers 2 and 4 cannot be adjacent, so must occupy the endpoints in either order, leaving 3 in the middle. All resulting adjacencies are coprime. The count is $4\cdot2\cdot2=\boxed{16}$.
