Let $z=a+bi$. Then $z^2=a^2-b^2+2abi$, so
\[
a^2-b^2=-16,\qquad 2ab=30 .
\]
Also $|z|^2=|z^2|$, so $a^2+b^2=\sqrt{16^2+30^2}=\sqrt{1156}=34$. Adding and subtracting: $a^2=9$, $b^2=25$. Since $ab=15>0$, $a$ and $b$ have the same sign, so
\[
z=\boxed{\pm(3+5i)} .
\]
Check: $(3+5i)^2=9-25+30i=-16+30i$.
