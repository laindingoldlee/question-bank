Writing $10$ as an ordered sum of $k$ positive integers means choosing $k-1$ of the $9$ gaps between ten $1$s to cut, so there are $\dbinom{9}{k-1}$ ways. In total there are $2^9=512$ ordered sums. Those with $1$, $2$ or $3$ terms number $\dbinom90+\dbinom91+\dbinom92=1+9+36=46$. So the answer is $512-46=\boxed{466}$.
