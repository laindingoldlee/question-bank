The argument of the outer $P$ is a positive integer $m$ (it is at least $S(S(n))\ge1$). The product of the digits of any number is either $0$ or a product of digits $1,\dots,9$. So its only possible prime factors are $2$, $3$, $5$, $7$. But $1984=2^6\cdot31$ has the prime factor $31$. Hence $P(m)=1984$ is impossible for every $m$, and the number of solutions is $\boxed{0}$.
