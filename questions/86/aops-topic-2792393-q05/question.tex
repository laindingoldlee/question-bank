There are $100$ pancakes, carrying $1,2,\dots,100$ blueberries, stacked in some order. Repeatedly, count the blueberries $N$ on the top pancake and flip the top $N$ pancakes upside down as a block. Prove that the pancake with one blueberry eventually reaches the top.
