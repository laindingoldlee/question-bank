For $k\ge1$, $a_{k+1}-1=\dfrac{a_k^2-1}{2}=\dfrac{(a_k-1)(a_k+1)}{2}$, so
\[\dfrac1{a_{k+1}-1}=\dfrac{2}{(a_k-1)(a_k+1)}=\dfrac1{a_k-1}-\dfrac1{a_k+1},\qquad\text{i.e.}\qquad\dfrac1{a_k+1}=\dfrac1{a_k-1}-\dfrac1{a_{k+1}-1}.\]
(All $a_k\ge3$ for $k\ge1$, so nothing is zero.) Summing for $k=1,\dots,n-1$ telescopes:
\[\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}\dfrac1{a_k+1}=\dfrac1{a_1-1}-\dfrac1{a_n-1}=\dfrac12-\dfrac1{a_n-1}.\]
Adding the $k=0$ term $\dfrac1{a_0+1}=\dfrac12$ and $\dfrac1{a_n-1}$ gives $S_n=1$ for every $n\ge1$. So $S_{2021}=\boxed{1}$.
