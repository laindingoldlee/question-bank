Take $A=(0,0),B=(1,0),C=(1,\sqrt3)$. The right angle and $AC=2$ force these coordinates up to reflection. Convexity places the equilateral vertex $D$ on the opposite side of $AC$ from $B$, giving $D=(-1,\sqrt3)$. Thus $BD^2=2^2+(\sqrt3)^2=7$, so $\boxed{BD=\sqrt7}$, choice C. 
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(220);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(1,0),C=(1,sqrt(3)),D=(-1,sqrt(3));draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C);draw(B--D,dashed);label("$A$",A,(-1,-1));label("$B$",B,(1,-1));label("$C$",C,(1,1));label("$D$",D,(-1,1));
\end{asy}
