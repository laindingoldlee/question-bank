Put $b_n=2a_n+3$. The recurrence becomes $b_{n+1}=b_n^2-b_n+1$, with $b_1=15$. Thus $b_{n+1}-1=b_n(b_n-1)$ and $\dfrac{1}{b_n}=\dfrac{1}{b_n-1}-\dfrac{1}{b_{n+1}-1}$. Since $b_n\ge15$ and $b_{n+1}>b_n$, the sequence tends to infinity (a finite limit would have to satisfy $L=L^2-L+1$, hence $L=1$). Summing and taking limits gives $\boxed{S=\dfrac{1}{14}}$.
