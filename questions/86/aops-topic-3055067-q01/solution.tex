The quadratic $ q(n)=\dfrac{5n^2-13n+10}{2}$ gives the first three values and $ q(4)=19 $. For a desired fourth value $ a $, use \[p(n)=q(n)+\dfrac{a-19}{6}(n-1)(n-2)(n-3).\]The added term vanishes at 1,2,3 and equals $ a-19 $ at 4. Thus the prefix alone determines no unique next term.
