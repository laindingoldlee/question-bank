$(3x+2y)^2(3x-2y)^2=\left((3x+2y)(3x-2y)\right)^2=(9x^2-4y^2)^2$, so the expression is $(9x^2-4y^2)^6$. By the binomial theorem it is $\sum_{k=0}^{6}\dbinom6k(9x^2)^{6-k}(-4y^2)^k$, whose $7$ terms have different powers $x^{12-2k}y^{2k}$ and nonzero coefficients. There are $\boxed{7}$ terms.
