With $6$ turkeys on $5$ plates and at least one on each plate, exactly one plate gets two turkeys and the others get one each.

\textbf{(a)} Only the numbers matter, so we just choose the plate with two turkeys: $\boxed{5}$ ways.

\textbf{(b)} Choose the two turkeys that share a plate, $\dbinom62=15$ ways; then the $5$ groups (one pair and four single turkeys) go onto the $5$ different plates in $5!=120$ ways. In total $15\cdot120=\boxed{1800}$ ways.
