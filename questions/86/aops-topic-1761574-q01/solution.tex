By the hockey-stick identity, $\displaystyle\sum_{i=3}^{n}\dbinom i3=\dbinom{n+1}{4}$. We have $\dbinom{16}{4}=1820<2019$ and $\dbinom{17}{4}=2380\ge2019$, so $n+1=17$ and $n=\boxed{16}$.
