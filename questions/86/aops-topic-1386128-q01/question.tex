A student states: ``Let $A$ be a finite set of real numbers such that for all distinct $x,y,z\in A$ we have $x^2+yz\in A$. Then there is a positive integer $M$ such that $x\sqrt M$ is rational for every $x\in A$.''

Show that this statement is false as written, and say which hypothesis must be added.
