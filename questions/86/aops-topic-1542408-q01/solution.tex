Dividing $2216$ by $a$ leaves remainder $29$ exactly when $2216=qa+29$ for some integer $q\ge0$ and $29<a$ (a remainder is always less than the divisor). So $a$ must divide
\[
2216-29=2187=3^7
\]
and satisfy $a>29$.

The divisors of $3^7$ are $1,3,9,27,81,243,729,2187$, and those greater than $29$ are $81,243,729,2187$. For example, $2216=27\cdot81+29$ and $2216=1\cdot2187+29$.

So there are $\boxed{4}$ such integers $a$.
