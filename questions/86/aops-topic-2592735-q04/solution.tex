Factor the denominator as a difference of squares:
\[n^4+n^2+1=\left(n^2+1\right)^2-n^2=\left(n^2-n+1\right)\left(n^2+n+1\right).\]
Since $\left(n^2+n+1\right)-\left(n^2-n+1\right)=2n$,
\[\dfrac{n}{n^4+n^2+1}=\dfrac12\left(\dfrac{1}{n^2-n+1}-\dfrac{1}{n^2+n+1}\right).\]
Also $(n+1)^2-(n+1)+1=n^2+n+1$, so the sum telescopes:
\[\sum_{n=1}^{10}\dfrac{n}{n^4+n^2+1}=\dfrac12\left(\dfrac{1}{1}-\dfrac{1}{10^2+10+1}\right)=\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac{110}{111}=\boxed{\dfrac{55}{111}}.\]
