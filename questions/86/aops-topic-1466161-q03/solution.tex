Use the Sophie Germain identity $x^4+4y^4=(x^2+2y^2+2xy)(x^2+2y^2-2xy)$, which follows from
\[
x^4+4y^4=(x^2+2y^2)^2-(2xy)^2 .
\]
Here $3^8=9^4$ and $2^{10}=4\cdot2^8=4\cdot4^4$, so with $x=9$, $y=4$:
\[
2^{10}+3^8=(81+32+72)(81+32-72)=185\cdot41=5\cdot37\cdot41 .
\]
(Check: $1024+6561=7585=5\cdot37\cdot41$.) The largest prime factor is $m=41$, so $n=\dfrac{7585}{41}=\boxed{185}$. This agrees with the condition $m+n=226$ given in the original problem.
