Joining the center $O$ to the vertices gives six equilateral triangles, so each circumradius is $6$. Opposite vertices $A,D$ lie on a diameter, hence $AD=2\cdot6=\boxed{12\text{ cm}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair[] P;string[] L={"A","B","C","D","E","F"};for(int i=0;i<6;++i){P.push(6dir(60*i));label("$"+L[i]+"$",P[i],P[i]);}draw(P[0]--P[1]--P[2]--P[3]--P[4]--P[5]--cycle);draw(P[0]--P[3],blue);dot("$O$",(0,0),S);label("$6$",(P[0]+P[1])/2,NE);
\end{asy}
