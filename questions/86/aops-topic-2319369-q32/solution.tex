For $n\ge12$ CDs, store A costs $104+2n$ and store B costs $10(n-12)$. Equal when $104+2n=10n-120$, so $8n=224$ and $n=\boxed{28}$ (both cost $160$ dollars). (For $n<12$, store B costs $0$ while A costs more than $104$.)
