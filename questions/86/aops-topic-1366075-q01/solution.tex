Factor the quadratic form: $2x^2-3xy-2y^2=(2x+y)(x-2y)$. So with $u=2x+y$ and $v=x-2y$ we need $uv=7$, and conversely
\[
x=\dfrac{2u+v}{5},\qquad y=\dfrac{u-2v}{5}
\]
must be integers. The four factorisations give:
\begin{itemize}
\item $(u,v)=(1,7)$: $x=\dfrac{9}{5}$, not an integer;
\item $(u,v)=(7,1)$: $x=3$, $y=1$;
\item $(u,v)=(-1,-7)$: $x=-\dfrac{9}{5}$, not an integer;
\item $(u,v)=(-7,-1)$: $x=-3$, $y=-1$.
\end{itemize}
The solutions are $\boxed{(x,y)=(3,1)\text{ and }(-3,-1)}$. Check: $18-9-2=7$.
