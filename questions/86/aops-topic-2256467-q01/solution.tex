Split the sum:
\[\sum_{n=0}^{50}(2n-1)=2\sum_{n=0}^{50}n-\sum_{n=0}^{50}1=2\cdot\dfrac{50\cdot51}{2}-51=2550-51=\boxed{2499}.\]
(The terms are $-1,1,3,\dots,99$: the term for $n=0$ is $-1$, and $1+3+\cdots+99=50^2=2500$.)
