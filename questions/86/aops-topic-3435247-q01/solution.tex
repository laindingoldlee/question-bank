Write $P(x)=ax^2+bx+c$. The difference of the supplied values is $P(2)-P(1)=3 a+b=3$. The requested difference is $(49-16)a+(7+4)b=33 a+11 b=11(3 a+b)=\boxed{33}$. It is independent of the individual coefficients.
