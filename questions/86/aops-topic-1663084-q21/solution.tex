The area of an annulus is the area of the outer disc minus the area of the inner disc. Annulus A has area $\pi\left(6^2-2^2\right)=32\pi$. If the inner radius of B is $r$, its area is $\pi\left(81-r^2\right)$. Setting these equal,
\[
81-r^2=32,\qquad r^2=49,\qquad r=\boxed{7\text{ cm}}.
\]
