Pad $0$ through $1999$ with leading zeros. The thousands column contributes $1000$. Each of the last three columns takes every digit equally often in each block of $1000$, so contributes $2\cdot100(0+\cdots+9)=9000$. This subtotal is $28000$. For $2000$ through $2020$, the thousands digits contribute $42$, and the last two digits $00$ through $20$ contribute $45+55+2=102$. Thus the grand total is $28000+42+102=\boxed{28144}$.
