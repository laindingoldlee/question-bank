Some objects are distributed among $n$ boxes $B_1,\dots,B_n$. All of them are then taken out and redistributed among $n+1$ new boxes $C_1,\dots,C_{n+1}$, with no new box empty. Prove that there are at least two objects each of which is in a new box containing fewer objects than the old box that contained it.
