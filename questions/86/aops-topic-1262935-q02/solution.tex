Let the large square have side $3$, so each side is cut into pieces $1$ and $2$. Each side of the small square is the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs $1$ and $2$, so its square has area $1^2+2^2=5$. The ratio is $\boxed{\dfrac{5}{9}}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((0,0)--(3,0)--(3,3)--(0,3)--cycle,linewidth(1));
draw((1,0)--(3,1)--(2,3)--(0,2)--cycle,blue+linewidth(1));
label("$1$",(0.5,0),S); label("$2$",(2,0),S); label("$1$",(3,0.5),E); label("$2$",(3,2),E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
