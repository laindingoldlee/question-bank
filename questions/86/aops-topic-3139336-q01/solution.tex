For $ n\ge2 $, every factor 2 through $ n $ is at least 2, so $ n!\ge2^{n-1}$, which grows without bound. Thus the sequence diverges to positive infinity. The recurrence is defined for finite integer arguments; infinity is not an integer that can be substituted into it. Expressions such as infinity minus 1 or infinity times an unspecified infinity factorial therefore do not establish an equality of real numbers. The valid conclusion is the unbounded limit of the finite factorial sequence.
