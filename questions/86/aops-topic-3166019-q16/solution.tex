The sequence increases because the square-root map is increasing and $t_1>t_0$. It remains at most two, since $t_n\le2$ implies $t_{n+1}\le\sqrt4=2$. Thus it converges to a nonnegative $L$ satisfying $L^2=2+L$, whose roots are $2,-1$. The valid limit is $\boxed2$.
