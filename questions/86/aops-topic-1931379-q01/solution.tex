We have $S=5(1+2+\cdots+10000)=5\cdot\dfrac{10000\cdot10001}{2}=25000\cdot10001=\boxed{250025000}$.

Since $25000=2^3\cdot5^5$ and $10001=73\cdot137$ (both prime), the prime factorisation is
\[
S=\boxed{2^3\cdot5^5\cdot73\cdot137}.
\]
The digits of $S$ include $0$, so the product of its digits is $\boxed{0}$.
