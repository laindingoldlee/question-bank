Use the Euclidean algorithm:
\[
\begin{aligned}
15476&=1\cdot12354+3122,\\
12354&=3\cdot3122+2988,\\
3122&=1\cdot2988+134,\\
2988&=22\cdot134+40,\\
134&=3\cdot40+14,\\
40&=2\cdot14+12,\\
14&=1\cdot12+2,\\
12&=6\cdot2 .
\end{aligned}
\]
The last nonzero remainder is $\boxed{2}$. (Check: $12354=2\cdot3\cdot29\cdot71$ and $15476=2^2\cdot53\cdot73$ share only the factor $2$.)
