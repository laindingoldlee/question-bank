Squaring gives $ab=784-c!>0$, so $1\le c\le6$ because $7!>784$. For each $c$, the number of ordered factor pairs is the divisor count of $784-c!$. The six values and their factorizations are $783=3^3\cdot29$, $782=2\cdot17\cdot23$, $778=2\cdot389$, $760=2^3\cdot5\cdot19$, $664=2^3\cdot83$, $64=2^6$. Their divisor counts are 8,8,4,16,8,7. Thus the total is $\boxed{51}$.
