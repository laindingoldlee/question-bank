At ordinary minute increments the decrease is at most 8, when the units digit changes 9 to 0. At an hour change, the minutes 59 contribute 14 and then become 00, so the decrease is 14 plus the decrease in the hour digit sum. Among hour transitions 1 to 2 through 12 to 1, the greatest hour-sum decrease is 8, at 9 to 10. Thus the maximum is $ 14+8=\boxed{22}$, attained at 9:59 to 10:00.
