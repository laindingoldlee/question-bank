This is $S=\sum_{k\equiv2\pmod3}\binom{2021}k$. Let $\omega=e^{\dfrac{2\pi i}3}$. The roots-of-unity filter gives
\[S=\dfrac13\left(2^{2021}+\omega^{-2}(1+\omega)^{2021}+\omega^{-4}(1+\omega^2)^{2021}\right).\]
Since $1+\omega=-\omega^2$ and $1+\omega^2=-\omega$: $(1+\omega)^{2021}=-\omega^{4042}=-\omega$ and $(1+\omega^2)^{2021}=-\omega^{2021}=-\omega^2$. So the bracket is $2^{2021}-\omega^{-1}-\omega^{-2}=2^{2021}-\omega^2-\omega=2^{2021}+1$, and
\[S=\boxed{\dfrac{2^{2021}+1}{3}}.\]
