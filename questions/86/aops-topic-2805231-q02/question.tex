A box contains $1000$ candies, and unlimited chocolates and muffins are available outside. One may replace: two candies by a chocolate; two muffins by a chocolate; two chocolates by a candy and a muffin; a candy and a chocolate by a muffin; a muffin and a chocolate by a candy. Can exactly one object remain in the box?
