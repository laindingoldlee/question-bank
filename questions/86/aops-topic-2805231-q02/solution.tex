With $c,m,b$ the numbers of candies, muffins and chocolates, the quantity $I=c+3m+2b\pmod4$ is invariant: the five moves change it by $-2+2$, $-6+2$, $1+3-4$, $-1-2+3$, $1-3-2$, all $\equiv0\pmod4$. Initially $I=1000\equiv0$. A single candy, muffin or chocolate gives $I\equiv1$, $3$ or $2$. So $\boxed{\text{no}}$. $\blacksquare$
