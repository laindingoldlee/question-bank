$\dfrac{q^3p}{2p^3}=\dfrac{q^3}{2p^2}$, so its $(-2)$nd power is $\dfrac{4p^4}{q^6}$. Also $\dfrac{6p^5q^{-1}}{4p^2q^3}=\dfrac{3}{2}p^3q^{-4}$, whose square is $\dfrac{9}{4}p^6q^{-8}$. The product is $9p^{10}q^{-14}=\boxed{\dfrac{9p^{10}}{q^{14}}}$.
