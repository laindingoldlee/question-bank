Scale $AB=AC=1$. Bisecting the isosceles triangle shows $BC=2\sin10^\circ$, so $AD=2\sin10^\circ$. Consider a triangle with angles at $A,C,D$ respectively $20^\circ,10^\circ,150^\circ$ and side $AC=1$. By the sine rule its side $AD$ is $\dfrac{\sin10^\circ}{\sin150^\circ}=2\sin10^\circ$. This triangle has the same two sides $AC,AD$ and included angle $20^\circ$ as our triangle $ACD$, so SAS congruence gives $\boxed{\angle DCA=10^\circ}$.

\end{asy}
