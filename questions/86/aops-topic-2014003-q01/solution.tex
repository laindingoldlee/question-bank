Work modulo $4$, using $F_{n+2}\equiv F_{n+1}+F_n$:
\[
F_1,\dots,F_{12}\equiv 1,1,2,3,1,0,1,1,2,3,1,0 \pmod 4 .
\]
(Directly, $F_{12}=144=4\cdot36$.) The remainder is $\boxed{0}$.
