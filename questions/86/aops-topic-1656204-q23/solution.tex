The point $(A,B,C)$ is uniform in the cube $[0,2]^3$ of volume $8$. The complementary event $A+B+C\le1$ is the corner tetrahedron with vertices $(0,0,0)$, $(1,0,0)$, $(0,1,0)$, $(0,0,1)$ (shaded), of volume $\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot1=\dfrac{1}{6}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair X=(1,0),Y=(0.45,0.35),Z=(0,1);
pair p(real a,real b,real c){return a*X+b*Y+c*Z;}
pair O=p(0,0,0);
draw(p(2,0,0)--p(2,2,0));draw(p(2,2,0)--p(0,2,0),dashed);draw(p(0,2,0)--O,dashed);draw(O--p(2,0,0));
draw(p(0,0,2)--p(2,0,2)--p(2,2,2)--p(0,2,2)--cycle);
draw(O--p(0,0,2));draw(p(2,0,0)--p(2,0,2));draw(p(2,2,0)--p(2,2,2));draw(p(0,2,0)--p(0,2,2),dashed);
filldraw(p(1,0,0)--p(0,1,0)--p(0,0,1)--cycle,palegray,blue);
draw(O--p(0,1,0),blue+dashed);
draw(p(2,0,0)--p(2.5,0,0),gray,Arrow(TeXHead));label("$A$",p(2.5,0,0),E,gray);
draw(p(0,0,2)--p(0,0,2.5),gray,Arrow(TeXHead));label("$C$",p(0,0,2.5),N,gray);
label("$B$",p(0,2,0),NW,gray);
label("$1$",p(1,0,0),S);label("$2$",p(2,0,0),S);label("$O$",O,SW);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

So
\[
P(A+B+C>1)=1-\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{6}}{8}=1-\dfrac{1}{48}=\boxed{\textbf{(f)}\ \dfrac{47}{48}} .
\]
