Treat MC as one block; its letters cannot occur elsewhere. Of the other $24$ letters, $5$ are vowels and $19$ consonants. Choosing $k$ other letters gives $\binom{24}{k}-\binom{19}{k}$ allowable sets, where a binomial coefficient is zero outside its range. Arrange these letters and the block in $(k+1)!$ ways. The block is unique because letters cannot repeat. Therefore the exact count is $\boxed{\sum_{k=1}^{24}(\binom{24}{k}-\binom{19}{k})(k+1)!}$.
