The adjacency graph of the 12 squares is connected, so it has at least 11 shared edges. Each shared edge removes 2 from the initial perimeter 48, giving at most $48-22=26$. A 1-by 12 strip, divided into three 1-by 4 tetrominoes, has exactly 11 shared edges and perimeter 26. Thus $\boxed{26}$.

for(int i=0;i<12;++i)filldraw((i,0)--(i+1,0)--(i+1,1)--(i,1)--cycle,i<4?lightgray:(i<8?white:lightgray));label("three straight tetrominoes",(6,-.6));
\end{asy}
