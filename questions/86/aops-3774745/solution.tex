For a monic polynomial $P(z)=z^n+c_1z^{n-1}+\cdots+c_n=\prod_{j=1}^n(z-r_j)$, let $p_k=\sum r_j^k$. Expanding at infinity gives $\dfrac{P^{\prime} (z)}{P (z)} = \sum_{k\ge0} p_k z^{-k-1}$, with $p_0=n$. Multiply by $P(z)$ and compare coefficients of $z^{n-k-1}$. For $1\le k\le n$, this yields $\boxed{p_k+c_1p_{k-1}+\cdots+c_{k-1}p_1+kc_k=0}$. For $k>n$, the derivative contributes zero and one obtains $\boxed{p_k+c_1p_{k-1}+\cdots+c_np_{k-n}=0}$. At $k=n$, the second form also works because $c_np_0=nc_n$. These are compatible formulas for different index ranges.
