With $j$ jumps their damage is $32-2^{5-j}$; when $j>5$ this is nonintegral and cannot combine with integer blast damage to give 150. In $n$ blasts at most $\lfloor\dfrac{n}{2}\rfloor$ fireblasts can receive the 3-point freezing bonus: each needs a distinct preceding iceblast. The maximum blast damage is $11\lfloor\dfrac{n}{2}\rfloor+5(n\bmod2)$. For at most 24 attacks and $j=0,1,2,3,4,5$, the corresponding total maxima are $132,142,145,143,140,135$, all below 150. Twenty-five attacks attain 150: two jumps contribute 24, eleven ice-fire pairs contribute 121, and one ordinary fireblast contributes 5. Thus $\boxed{25}$.
