Subtracting the second equation from the first,
\[a^4=2^{2015}-2^{2016}+5^{2016}-5^{2015}+2015=-2^{2015}+4\cdot5^{2015}+2015.\]
Reduce modulo $5$: $2^4\equiv1$ and $2015\equiv3\pmod4$, so $2^{2015}\equiv2^3\equiv3$, and the right side is $\equiv-3\equiv2\pmod5$. But a fourth power is $\equiv0$ or $1\pmod5$ (Fermat). Contradiction, so no solutions exist. $\blacksquare$
