Pair the outer factors and the inner factors:
\[
50\cdot53=2650,\qquad 51\cdot52=2652 .
\]
These differ by $2$; with $m=2651$, their average, the product is $(m-1)(m+1)=m^2-1$. So
\[
1+50\cdot51\cdot52\cdot53=m^2,\qquad\sqrt{1+50\cdot51\cdot52\cdot53}=\boxed{2651}.
\]
In general, $n(n+3)=n^2+3n$ and $(n+1)(n+2)=n^2+3n+2$ differ by $2$, so
\[
n(n+1)(n+2)(n+3)+1=(n^2+3n+1)^2 .
\]
This is why a substitution works best when it makes the two pairs symmetric, for example $y=n^2+3n+1$ (here $y=2651$), or $x=51.5$, the middle of the four factors: $(x-1.5)(x+1.5)(x-0.5)(x+0.5)+1=\left(x^2-\dfrac{5}{4}\right)^2$.
