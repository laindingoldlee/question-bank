The circumferences of the two wheels of a carriage are $\dfrac{7}{2}$ metres and $\dfrac{11}{3}$ metres. A chalk mark is put on the point of contact of each wheel with the ground before the carriage starts moving straight forward. How far must the carriage go before both chalk marks next touch the ground at the same time? Explain why the answer is the least common multiple of the two numerators.
