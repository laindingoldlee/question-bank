Let $F_n$ be the Fibonacci numbers with $F_1=F_2=1$, and $L_n$ the Lucas numbers ($L_1=1$, $L_2=3$, $L_{n+1}=L_n+L_{n-1}$). Prove that $F_{m-k}+F_{m+k}=F_mL_k$ for even $k$ and $m>k$, and hence express $\dfrac{F_{2021}+F_{4069}}{F_{3045}}$ as a Lucas number.
