The diagonal of the $a$ by $b$ base has length $d$ with $d^2=a^2+b^2$. That base diagonal and the vertical edge $c$ are perpendicular, so the space diagonal $D$ satisfies $D^2=d^2+c^2=a^2+b^2+c^2$. In orthogonal coordinates, project a point onto the first $n-1$ coordinate directions. Its projection-distance squared is $x_1^2+\cdots+x_{n-1}^2$ by induction; the remaining displacement $x_n$ is perpendicular. Another application of Pythagoras gives distance $\boxed{\sqrt{x_1^2+\cdots+x_n^2}}$.
