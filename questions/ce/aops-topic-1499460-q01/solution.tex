We have $h(x)=3x^2+5x-2$. Its discriminant is $25+24=49>0$, so there are two real roots, and by Vieta's formulas their sum is $-\dfrac{5}{3}$. Indeed $3x^2+5x-2=(3x-1)(x+2)$, with roots $\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $-2$, and $\dfrac{1}{3}-2=\boxed{-\dfrac{5}{3}}$.
