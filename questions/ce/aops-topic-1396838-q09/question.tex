A rectangular piece of paper measures $40$ inches on its long side. It is folded in half along a line parallel to its short sides (so the long sides are halved). The ratio of the long side of the original sheet to its short side is the same as the ratio of the long side of the folded sheet to its short side. What is the length of the short side of the original sheet? Express your answer in simplest radical form.
