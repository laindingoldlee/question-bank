Let the short side be $x$, with $x<40$. The folded sheet measures $x$ by $20$. Its long side must be $x$: if it were $20$, the condition $\dfrac{40}{x}=\dfrac{20}{x}$ would be impossible. So $x\ge20$ and
\[
\dfrac{40}{x}=\dfrac{x}{20}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x^2=800\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x=\boxed{20\sqrt2}.
\]
Indeed $20\sqrt2\approx28.3$ lies between $20$ and $40$. (This is the shape of A4 paper: halving it gives a similar rectangle.)

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real x=20*sqrt(2);
draw((0,0)--(40,0)--(40,x)--(0,x)--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw((20,0)--(20,x+2),dashed);
label("$40$",(20,0),S); label("$x$",(40,x/2),E); label("$20$",(10,x),N); label("$20$",(30,x),N);
label("fold",(20,x+2),N,fontsize(8pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
