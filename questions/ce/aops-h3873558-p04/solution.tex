Let the smaller radius be $r$ and $AB=d$. Containment implies $d+r\le16$. The perpendicular distance of $C$ from line $AB$ is at most $r$, so $[ABC]\le\dfrac{dr}{2}\le\dfrac{r(16-r)}{2}=32-\dfrac{(r-8)^2}{2}\le32$. Equality occurs for $r=d=8$, with $BC$ perpendicular to $AB$ and length $8$. Thus the maximum is $\boxed{32}$.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(8,0),C=(8,8);draw(circle(A,16));draw(circle(B,8),blue);filldraw(A--B--C--cycle,lightblue);label("A",A,SW);label("B",B,S);label("C",C,NE);label("8",(A+B)/2,S);label("8",(B+C)/2,E);
\end{asy}
