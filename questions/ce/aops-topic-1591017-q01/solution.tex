\textbf{Real numbers.} The square of a real number is never negative, so there is no real solution.

\textbf{Complex numbers.} The numbers whose square is $-4$ are $2i$ and $-2i$, since $(\pm2i)^2=4i^2=-4$, and a quadratic equation has at most two roots. So $c-4=\pm2i$ and
\[
c=\boxed{4+2i,\ 4-2i}.
\]
Check: $(4+2i-4)^2=(2i)^2=-4$, and similarly for $4-2i$.
