The $2\times2$ block is determined by its upper-left square, which can be any of the $3\times3=9$ squares in the first three rows and columns. It leaves $16-4=12$ free squares, and the five identical outposts choose $5$ of them in $\dbinom{12}{5}=792$ ways. The total is $9\cdot792=\boxed{7128}$.
