There are at most 28 total memberships, so at least three per party allows at most nine parties. To attain nine, arrange nine party labels in a cycle and assign one person to each neighboring pair, using nine people. Assign four more people to four disjoint pairs covering eight labels. Each of those eight parties now has three members; give the last party one exclusive member. This uses fourteen people, each in at most two parties, and creates nine distinct three-member parties. Hence $\boxed9$.
