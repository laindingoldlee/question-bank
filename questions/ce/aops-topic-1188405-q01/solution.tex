\textbf{Burnside's lemma.} Place the beads at the $6$ positions of a circle. The rotation group has $6$ elements; count the colourings with $3$ white and $3$ black beads fixed by each rotation.
\begin{itemize}
\item The identity fixes all $\dbinom63=20$ colourings.
\item Rotations by $1$ or $5$ positions (one cycle of length $6$) fix only one-colour necklaces: $0$.
\item Rotations by $2$ or $4$ positions (two cycles of length $3$) fix colourings in which each cycle has one colour; one cycle white and one black: $2$ each.
\item The rotation by $3$ positions (three cycles of length $2$) fixes colourings with an even number of white beads: $0$.
\end{itemize}
So the number of necklaces is
\[
\dfrac{20+0+2+0+2+0}{6}=\boxed{4}.
\]

\textbf{Check by listing.} Reading around the necklace, they are $WWWBBB$, $WBWBWB$, $WWBWBB$ and $WWBBWB$, with $6$, $2$, $6$ and $6$ rotations respectively ($6+2+6+6=20$). The last two are mirror images of each other, so they would merge if reflections were also counted as the same (giving $3$).
