Choose the paired face values in $\binom62$ ways, the singleton value in $4$ ways, and their positions in $\dfrac{5!}{2!2!}=30$ ways. Out of $6^5$ equally likely rolls, this gives $\boxed{\dfrac{\binom62\cdot4\cdot30}{6^5}=\dfrac{25}{108}}$.
