The points equidistant from $(0,0)$ and $(3,3)$ form the perpendicular bisector of the segment joining them: it passes through the midpoint $\left(\dfrac32,\dfrac32\right)$ with slope $-1$, i.e. the line $x+y=3$. (Algebraically, $x^2+y^2<(x-3)^2+(y-3)^2\iff x+y<3$.) Inside the square, the points with $x+y\ge3$ form the triangle with vertices $(2,1)$, $(1,2)$, $(2,2)$, of area $\dfrac12$. So the favourable area is $4-\dfrac12=\dfrac72$, and the probability is $\dfrac{7}{2}\cdot\dfrac14=\boxed{\dfrac78}$.
