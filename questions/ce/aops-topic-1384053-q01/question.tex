A positive integer $m$ is \emph{abundant} if the sum of its positive divisors exceeds $2m$, and \emph{deficient} if that sum is less than $2m$. Let $n$ be an integer with $n\ge50$. Find the smallest such $n$ for which $n-1$ is abundant and $n+1$ is deficient.
