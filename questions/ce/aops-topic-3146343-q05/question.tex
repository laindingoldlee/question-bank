Two soccer teams alternate penalty shots, each independently scoring with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Each initially has five shots; stop early if one cannot even tie using its remaining shots. If tied after five each, play pairs of one shot per team until a pair breaks the tie. Find the expected total goals as a reduced fraction $\dfrac{A}{B}$, and compute $1000A+B$.
