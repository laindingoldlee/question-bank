Represent the schedule by a simple bipartite graph in which each vertex has degree two. If the total scheduled player count is $N$, the number of matches is $N$ by the degree sum. Removing the two absent vertices cancels four matches if they were not opponents, or three if they were opponents. Thus $0.2N=4$ or $3$, giving $N=20$ or $15$. The two groups have equal size because their degree sums agree, so $N$ is even; $15$ is impossible. A cycle of length twenty with one absent vertex in each group that are not adjacent realizes the other case. Hence the number actually participating is $\boxed{18}$.
