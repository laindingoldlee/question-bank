The square residues for a block of eight consecutive integers are a permutation of $0,1,4,1,0,1,4,1$, summing to $12\equiv4\pmod8$. The first $48$ terms form six blocks, whose total is $6\cdot4\equiv0$. The last two terms have residues $2022^2\equiv4$ and $2023^2\equiv1$. Therefore the remainder is $\boxed5$.
