During a school break, students take apples from a basket: the first student gets $1$ apple, the next gets $2$, then $4$, then $8$, and so on, each student getting twice as many as the one before. After the last student takes his share, exactly $1$ apple is left in the basket. The last student's apples weigh $5$ kg. Assuming all apples have the same weight, how many kilograms of apples were in the basket at the start?
