Suppose there are $n$ students. The last student gets $2^{n-1}$ apples, and the basket held
\[
1+2+4+\dots+2^{n-1}+1=(2^n-1)+1=2^n=2\cdot2^{n-1}
\]
apples. So the basket held exactly twice as many apples as the last student received. Since the apples weigh the same, the basket held $2\cdot5=\boxed{10}$ kg of apples.
