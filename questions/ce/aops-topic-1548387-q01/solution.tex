The favourable region is the disc of radius $1$ about the centre $O$; it lies inside the octagon, since the octagon's apothem $\dfrac{9}{2}(1+\sqrt2)\approx10.9$ exceeds $1$:

\textbf{Area of the octagon.} A regular octagon of side $s$ is a square of side $s+2\cdot\dfrac{s}{\sqrt2}=s(1+\sqrt2)$ with four corner right isosceles triangles of legs $\dfrac{s}{\sqrt2}$ cut off, so its area is
\[
s^2(1+\sqrt2)^2-4\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{s^2}{2}=s^2\left(3+2\sqrt2-1\right)=2(1+\sqrt2)s^2 .
\]
With $s=9$ this is $162(1+\sqrt2)$.

\textbf{Probability.}
\[
\dfrac{\pi\cdot1^2}{162(1+\sqrt2)}=\dfrac{\pi(\sqrt2-1)}{162(\sqrt2+1)(\sqrt2-1)}=\boxed{\dfrac{(\sqrt2-1)\pi}{162}}\approx0.0080 .
\]
