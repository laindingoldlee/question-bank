The $n$th term is $102+4(n-1)$. If $300$ were a term, then $4(n-1)=198$, i.e.\ $n-1=49.5$, which is not an integer. (Equivalently, every term is $\equiv2\pmod4$, while $300\equiv0\pmod4$.) So $\boxed{300\text{ is not a term}}$; the nearest terms are $298$ and $302$.
