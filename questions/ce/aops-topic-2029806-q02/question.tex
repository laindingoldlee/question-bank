A flask in the shape of a right circular cone contains some water. When the flask stands on its circular base (vertex up), the water surface is \(10\) units below the vertex. When the flask is turned upside down (vertex down), the water surface is \(3\) units below the base. What is the height of the flask? Express your answer in simplest radical form.
