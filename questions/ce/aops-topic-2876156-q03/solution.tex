The player plays exactly $k$ games when the first $k-1$ games fail and game $k$ succeeds, with probability $\left(\dfrac{15}{16}\right)^{k-1}\dfrac1{16}$. So
\[P(9)=\dfrac{15^8}{16^9},\qquad P(10)=\dfrac{15^9}{16^{10}},\]
and $\dfrac{P(9)}{P(10)}=\boxed{\dfrac{16}{15}}$.
