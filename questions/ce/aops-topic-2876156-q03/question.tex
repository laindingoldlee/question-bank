In each game a player independently has probability $\dfrac1{16}$ of being chosen as the murderer. The player keeps playing until chosen as the murderer, plays that game, and then quits. What is the probability that the player plays exactly $9$ games? Exactly $10$ games? What is the ratio of the first probability to the second?
