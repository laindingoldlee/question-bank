Measure distance in units with a dog step $=9$ units and a rabbit step $=5$ units (so $5$ dog steps $=45=9$ rabbit steps). In the time of $2$ dog steps the dog covers $18$ units and the rabbit $3\cdot5=15$ units, so the dog gains $3$ units per $2$ of its steps. The initial gap is $10\cdot9=90$ units, which takes $\dfrac{90}{3}=30$ such periods, i.e. $\boxed{60}$ dog steps.
