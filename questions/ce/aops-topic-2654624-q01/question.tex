A dog spots a rabbit $10$ of the dog's steps ahead of it, and at that moment both start running in the same direction. The distance the dog covers in $5$ steps equals the distance the rabbit covers in $9$ steps, and in the time the dog takes $2$ steps, the rabbit takes $3$ steps. How many steps does the dog need to run to catch the rabbit?
