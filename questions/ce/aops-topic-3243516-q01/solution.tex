It fails at $(1,1,1)$, where $3>1$. For all three at least two, divide by the positive product: $\dfrac{x+y+z}{xyz}=\dfrac{1}{yz}+\dfrac{1}{xz}+\dfrac{1}{xy}\le\dfrac{3}{4}<1$. Therefore the inequality holds strictly in the stated restricted domain.
