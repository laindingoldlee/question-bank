Since $23^2=529=9\cdot59-2$, $23$ is a solution. Any solution satisfies $59\mid(x-23)(x+23)$. As $59$ is prime, $x\equiv23$ or $-23\equiv36\pmod{59}$. The least positive solution is therefore $\boxed{23}$.
