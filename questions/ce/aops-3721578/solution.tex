Count days $t\ge0$ after Monday, November 17. We require $t\equiv0\pmod7$ and $t\equiv2\pmod{11}$. Write $t=7k$; since $7^{-1}\equiv8\pmod{11}$, $k\equiv5\pmod{11}$. Thus the first two shared visits are $t=35$ and $t=112$. The first is December 22, 2025, and adding $77$ days gives $\boxed{\text{Monday, March 9, 2026}}$. Both visits are Mondays because $t$ is a multiple of seven.
