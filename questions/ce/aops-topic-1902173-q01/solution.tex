Let the legs be $CA=5$ and $CB=10$, with the right angle at $C$, and let $CH=h$ be the altitude to the hypotenuse $AB$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair C=(0,0), A=(0,5), B=(10,0);
pair H=A+dot(C-A,unit(B-A))*unit(B-A);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(C--H,blue+dashed);
draw((0.4,0)--(0.4,0.4)--(0,0.4));
pair u=unit(A-H), v=unit(C-H);
draw((H+0.4*u)--(H+0.4*u+0.4*v)--(H+0.4*v),blue);
label("$C$",C,SW);label("$A$",A,NW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$H$",H,NE);
label("$5$",(0,2.5),W);label("$10$",(5,0),S);label("$5\sqrt5$",(6.2,1.9),NE);
label("$h$",(C+H)/2,SE,blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

By the Pythagorean theorem, $AB=\sqrt{5^2+10^2}=\sqrt{125}=5\sqrt5$. Computing the area in two ways,
\[
\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot5\cdot10=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot5\sqrt5\cdot h\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad h=\dfrac{50}{5\sqrt5}=\dfrac{10}{\sqrt5}=\boxed{2\sqrt5}\text{ cm}.
\]
