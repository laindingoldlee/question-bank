We need $|x-20|=2|x-35|$. Either $x-20=2(x-35)$, giving $x=50$, or $x-20=-2(x-35)$, giving $3x=90$, $x=30$. Check: $30$ is $10$ from $20$ and $5$ from $35$; $50$ is $30$ from $20$ and $15$ from $35$. The sum is $\boxed{80}$.
