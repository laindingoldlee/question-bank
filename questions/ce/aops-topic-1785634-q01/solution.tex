Dividing by $\dfrac{1}{3}$ is multiplying by $3$, so $\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{2}}{\dfrac{1}{3}}=\dfrac{3}{2}$, and $\dfrac{3}{2}-\dfrac{2}{3}=\dfrac{9-4}{6}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{6}}$.
