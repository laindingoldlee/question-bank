Mickey Mouse is walking on the Cartesian Plane. He is at the point $(5,0)$ and is trying to get to his biology classroom at $(0,0)$. In each part of his journey, he chooses a lattice point and walks to the lattice point in a straight line. Once he arrives at that lattice point, he chooses another lattice point and repeats the process. Given that Mickey Mouse must be walking closer to his biology classroom at all times, find the maximum distance he travels on his journey.
