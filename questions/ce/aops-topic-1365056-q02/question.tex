Prove or disprove: for all real numbers $a$ and $b$, the graph of $|a|xy=|b|$ intersects the graph of $y=\pm\sqrt x$ (that is, the parabola $x=y^2$). If the statement is false, find exactly which pairs $(a,b)$ make it true.
