POONAM has two O letters, so $X=\dfrac{6!}{2!}=360$. ALLEN has two L letters, giving $Y=\dfrac{5!}{2!}=60$. NITHIN has two N and two I letters, so $Z=\dfrac{6!}{2!2!}=180$. Thus $\dfrac{X+Z}{Y}=\boxed9$.
