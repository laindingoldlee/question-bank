Maylin glues together seven unit cubes: a centre cube, and one more cube glued face to face onto each of the six faces of the centre cube. A plane $\mathcal{P}$ passes through the midpoints of six of the edges of the centre cube, and it passes through all seven cubes. Let $\mathcal{R}$ be the area of the cross-section that $\mathcal{P}$ makes with Maylin's solid. Find $\mathcal{R}^2$.

$\textbf{(A) }\dfrac{3}{16}\qquad\textbf{(B) }\dfrac{27}{64}\qquad\textbf{(C) }\dfrac{27}{16}\qquad\textbf{(D) }\dfrac{27}{8}\qquad\textbf{(E) }\dfrac{27}{4}$
