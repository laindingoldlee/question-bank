Place the $8$ red tiles in a row; they create $9$ gaps (including both ends). No two blue tiles may be adjacent, so each gap gets at most one blue tile, and conversely any choice of $6$ different gaps gives a valid row. The number of rows is
\[\dbinom96=\boxed{84}.\]
