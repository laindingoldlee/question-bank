With H heads, the score is $2 H-(5-H)=3 H-5$. For H 0 through 5, the scores are $-5,-2,1,4,7,10$. The squares occur only for H 2 or 3. These have probability $\dfrac{\binom 52+\binom 53}{32}=\dfrac{20}{32}$. The complementary probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{3}{8}}$.
