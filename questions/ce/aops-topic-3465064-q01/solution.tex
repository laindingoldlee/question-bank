The perpendicular from the centre to the chord bisects the chord and has length 3. A half-chord is a leg of a right triangle with hypotenuse 5, so its length is $\sqrt{25-9}=4$. The chord length is $\boxed8$.
\begin{asy}
import geometry; usepackage("amsmath"); size(240);
pair O=(0,0),M=(0,3),A=(-4,3),B=(4,3);draw(circle(O,5));draw(circle(O,3),blue);draw(A--B);draw(O--M^^O--B,dashed);label("$O$",O,S);label("$M$",M,N);label("$A$",A,NW);label("$B$",B,NE);
\end{asy}
