$1998=2\cdot3^3\cdot37$. Each age divides $1998$, whose divisors are
\[1,2,3,6,9,18,27,37,54,74,111,222,333,666,999,1998.\]
The father's and mother's ages are two of these divisors differing by $10$, and the only such pair is $37$ and $27$. Then the children's ages have product $\dfrac{1998}{37\cdot27}=2$, so they are $2$, $1$ and $1$.

The father is $\boxed{37}$, the mother $27$, and the children are $2$, $1$ and $1$ years old.
