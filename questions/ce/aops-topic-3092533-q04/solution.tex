The number is divisible by 3. Modulo 11, $30\equiv8$ and $8^{10}\equiv1$. The exponent $T_{29}$ is divisible by 10, so $T_{30}\equiv1\pmod{11}$. Among the residues $1,12,23$ congruent to 1 modulo 11, only 12 is divisible by 3. The answer is $\boxed{12}$.
