Let the first term be $a_1=x$ and the ratio $r$, with $|r|<1$. The squares form a geometric series with first term $x^2$ and ratio $r^2$, and the cubes one with first term $x^3$ and ratio $r^3$. So
\[\dfrac{x}{1-r}=3,\qquad \dfrac{x^2}{1-r^2}=3.\]
Dividing the second equation by the first, $\dfrac{x}{1+r}=1$, so $x=1+r$. Then $\dfrac{1+r}{1-r}=3$ gives $1+r=3-3r$, so $r=\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $x=\dfrac{3}{2}$. The sum of the cubes is
\[\dfrac{x^3}{1-r^3}=\dfrac{\dfrac{27}{8}}{1-\dfrac{1}{8}}=\dfrac{27}{7}.\]
So $a=27$, $b=7$ and $a+b=\boxed{34}$.
