$a_1+a_2+a_3+\cdots$ is an infinite geometric series whose sum is $3$. Replacing each term of the series by its square gives a series whose sum is also $3$. Replacing each term by its cube gives a series whose sum is $\dfrac {a}{b}$, where $a$ and $b$ are coprime positive integers. Find $a+b$.
