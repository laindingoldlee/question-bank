Rectangle $ABCD$ is inscribed in a semicircle with diameter $\overline{FE}$: the side $DA$ lies on $\overline{FE}$ (with $F$, $D$, $A$, $E$ in this order) and $B$, $C$ lie on the arc. Let $DA=16$ and $FD=AE=9$. What is the area of the region inside the semicircle but outside the rectangle?
