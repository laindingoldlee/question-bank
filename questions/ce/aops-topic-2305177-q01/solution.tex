An increase of $125\%$ means the new height is $225\%=\dfrac94$ of the old height. In inches,
\[12B+A=\dfrac94(12A+B)\iff48B+4A=108A+9B\iff39B=104A\iff3B=8A.\]
So $B$ is a multiple of $8$ less than $12$, i.e. $B=8$ and $A=3$. (Check: $3$ ft $8$ in $=44$ in, and $\dfrac94\cdot44=99$ in $=8$ ft $3$ in.) Hence $A+B=\boxed{11}$.
