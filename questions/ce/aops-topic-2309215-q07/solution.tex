Each sum is an infinite geometric series with first term $a$ and ratio $a$, where $0<a<1$, so its sum is $\dfrac{a}{1-a}$:
\[\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{2^n}=\dfrac{\dfrac12}{1-\dfrac12}=1,\qquad \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{3^n}=\dfrac{\dfrac13}{1-\dfrac13}=\dfrac12.\]
The total is $1+\dfrac12=\boxed{\dfrac32}$.
