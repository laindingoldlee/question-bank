Per minute, Monika mows $\dfrac1{45}$ of the yard and Marcelo $\dfrac1{105}$. Together they mow
\[\dfrac1{45}+\dfrac1{105}=\dfrac{7}{315}+\dfrac{3}{315}=\dfrac{10}{315}=\dfrac{2}{63}\]
of the yard per minute, so they need $\dfrac{63}{2}=\boxed{31.5}$ minutes.
