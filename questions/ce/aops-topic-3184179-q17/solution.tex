The equal distances place $A,B,C$ on a circle centred at $M$, with $BC$ a diameter of length 4. Thus $\angle A=90^\circ$. The 30-60-90 triangle has legs $AB=2$, $AC=2\sqrt3$, and area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot2\cdot2\sqrt3=\boxed{2\sqrt3}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair B=(0,0),C=(4,0),M=(2,0),A=(1,sqrt(3));draw(circle(M,2));draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--M,dashed);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,W);label("$C$",C,E);label("$M$",M,S);
\end{asy}
