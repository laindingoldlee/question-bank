Let the distances in kilometres be $x,y$. Then $\dfrac{x}{6}+\dfrac{y}{15}=\dfrac{37}{120}$ and $\dfrac{x}{18}+\dfrac{y}{27}=\dfrac{61}{360}$. Thus $20x+8y=37$ and $20x+\dfrac{40}{3}y=61$. Subtraction gives $y=\dfrac{9}{2}$ and then $x=\dfrac{1}{20}$. The requested distance is $\boxed{50\text{ metres}}$.
