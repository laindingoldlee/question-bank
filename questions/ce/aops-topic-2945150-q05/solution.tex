The left side is nonnegative, and the second radical requires $|x|\ge1$, so $x\ge1$. Also $2\sqrt{x^2-1}\le x$, giving $4x^2-4\le x^2$, or $x^2\le\dfrac{4}{3}$. The first radical requires $k\le x^2$, hence $3k\le4$. Equality is attained at $k=\dfrac{4}{3}$, $x=\dfrac{2}{\sqrt3}$, when the first radical is 0 and the second term equals $x$. Thus $\boxed{\max3k=4}$.
