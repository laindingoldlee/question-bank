The total divisor count is $1+4+346=351=3^3\cdot13$. The four prime divisors mean $N$ has four different prime factors, so its four exponent-plus-one factors are all at least $2$ and multiply to $351$. They must therefore be $3,3,3,13$, yielding exponents $2,2,2,12$. A square divisor chooses an even exponent at each prime, giving $2\cdot2\cdot2\cdot7=\boxed{56}$ square divisors.
