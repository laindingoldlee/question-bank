A pilot flying due east is forced to make a detour from her original route to avoid turbulent weather. She turns $30^\circ$ north of east. After travelling some distance, she turns and rejoins her original route, at a point $1000$ km from where she began the detour. The turn back put her on a heading making a $45^\circ$ angle with the original route. How much farther did the pilot travel because of her detour? Express your answer to the nearest whole kilometre.
