Multiply numerator and denominator by $24+10\sqrt 2$. The denominator becomes $576-200=376$. The numerator expands to $52+100\sqrt 2+20\sqrt 3+24\sqrt 6$. Dividing all coefficients by 4 gives $\boxed{\dfrac{13+25\sqrt 2+5\sqrt 3+6\sqrt 6}{94}}$.
