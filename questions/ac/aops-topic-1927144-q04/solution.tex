The bug returns exactly when it makes $3$ moves right and $3$ left. Of the $2^6=64$ equally likely move sequences, $\binom63=20$ have this property, so the probability is
\[
\dfrac{20}{64}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{16}} .
\]
