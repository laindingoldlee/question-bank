The original expression requires $x\ne0$ and $n\ne0$. The factors are $16n^{18}$ and $-\dfrac{1}{8}n^{-18}x^9$. Multiplying cancels the powers of $n$ and gives $\boxed{-2x^9}$, with both original nonzero restrictions retained.
