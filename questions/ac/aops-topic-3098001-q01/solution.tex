A number from 1 through $p^a$ is not coprime to $p^a$ exactly when it is divisible by $p$. There are $p^{a-1}$ such multiples. Subtracting them from the $p^a$ possibilities gives $\boxed{\varphi(p^a)=p^a-p^{a-1}}$.
