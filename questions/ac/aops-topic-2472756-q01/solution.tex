A vocal range is determined by its lowest note and its highest note, which is the same or higher. There are $\dbinom{12}{2}=66$ ranges with two different end notes and $12$ ranges of a single note, so $78$ ranges in all.

A range contains every note from C to G exactly when its lowest note is at or below C and its highest note is at or above G. Since C is the lowest of all the notes, the lowest note must be C, and the highest note can be any of G, G$\sharp$, A, A$\sharp$, B: $5$ ranges. The probability is
\[\boxed{\dfrac{5}{78}}.\]
