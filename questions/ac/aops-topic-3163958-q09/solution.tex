Write $t=x-1006\ge1006$. Since $1006^2=1012036$, $f_{2012}(x)-1006=\sqrt{t^2-1010024}$. Thus the composition is $\sqrt{t^2+a-2022060}+1006$. Equality to $t+1006$ for all such $t$ forces $\boxed{a=2022060}$. Conversely this choice makes the outer radicand $t^2$, with $t>0$, and the inner radicand is nonnegative throughout the domain, so the identity holds.
