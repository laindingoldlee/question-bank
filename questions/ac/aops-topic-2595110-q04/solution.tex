If $b=2^m5^k$, let $t=\max(m,k)$. Then $\dfrac ab=\dfrac{a\cdot2^{t-m}5^{t-k}}{10^t}$, which is a terminating decimal.

Conversely, if $\dfrac ab=\dfrac N{10^t}$ for integers $N$ and $t\ge0$, then $a\cdot10^t=Nb$, so $b\mid a\cdot10^t$. As $\gcd(a,b)=1$, $b\mid10^t=2^t5^t$, so $b$ has no prime factors other than $2$ and $5$. $\blacksquare$

The hypothesis ``in lowest terms'' is needed: $\dfrac36=0.5$ terminates although $6$ has the prime factor $3$.
