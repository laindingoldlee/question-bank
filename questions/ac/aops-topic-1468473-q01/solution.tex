The total of the five scores was $5\cdot90=450$. Raising one score by $5$ makes the total $455$, so the new mean is $\dfrac{455}{5}=\boxed{91}$. (Raising one of $n$ scores by $5$ raises the mean by $\dfrac{5}{n}$.)
