Billy rows a boat along a straight river at a constant speed of $\cos(2\pi)$ km per hour relative to the water. A wind adds $\sin t$ km per hour to his speed in his direction of travel, $t$ hours after he starts (a negative value slows him down). He rows for $10\pi$ hours. What is the difference between the distance he travels and the distance he would travel without the wind?
