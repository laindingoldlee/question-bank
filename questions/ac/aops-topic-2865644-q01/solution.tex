They close the gap at $5+10=15$ miles per hour, so they meet after $\dfrac{30}{15}=\boxed{2}$ hours. By then A has walked $5\cdot2=10$ miles, so they meet $\boxed{10}$ miles from A's starting point ($20$ miles from B's).
