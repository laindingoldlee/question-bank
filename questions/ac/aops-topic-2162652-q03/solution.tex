Take natural logarithms:
\[x-1=(x-2)(\ln8-1)=(x-2)\ln8-x+2 .\]
So $2x-3=(x-2)\ln 8$, i.e. $x(\ln8-2)=2\ln8-3$, and since $\ln8=3\ln2$,
\[x=\boxed{\dfrac{6\ln2-3}{3\ln2-2}}\approx14.59 .\]
(Here $\ln8\approx2.0794$, so the denominator $\ln8-2\approx0.0794$ is positive but small, which is why the solution is fairly large.)
