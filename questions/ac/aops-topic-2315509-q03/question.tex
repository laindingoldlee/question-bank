A student's SAT score is decided by two magical dice. For the English section he rolls a fair $6$-sided die with the numbers $0$ through $5$ on it; if it shows $n$, he misses $n$ questions and scores $800-10n$. For the Math section he rolls a fair $6$-sided die with the numbers $0,0,0,0,0,1$ on it; if it shows $n$, he scores $800-10n$. His overall score is the sum of the two section scores. What is his expected overall score?
