The probability of exactly two wins is $\binom32\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^2\dfrac{2}{3}=\dfrac{6}{27}$. The probability of three wins is $\dfrac{1}{27}$. Adding gives $\boxed{\dfrac{7}{27}}$.
