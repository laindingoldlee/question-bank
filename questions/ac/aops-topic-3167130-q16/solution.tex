Eliminate $y$ to obtain $x+y=20+x-x^3$, with $0<x<\sqrt[3]{20}$. Its derivative is $1-3x^2$, positive before $x=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}$ and negative after it. The unique maximum is therefore $20+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}-\dfrac{1}{3\sqrt3}=\boxed{20+\dfrac{2\sqrt3}{9}}$.
