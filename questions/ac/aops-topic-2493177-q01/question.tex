Ten labelled positions are colored red(R), green(G), or blue(B), with no prescribed color counts. Forbidden consecutive triples are RRR, GGG, BBB, BRB, GBG, RGR. In each of the following layouts, count the colorings.\begin{enumerate}\item A circle labelled 0 through 9 in order: triples wrap around, and positions i and i+5 must have different colors for $0\le i<5$.\item Two rows of five labelled positions: consecutive triples lie within a row, and the two positions in every column differ.\item Five rows of two labelled positions: consecutive triples lie within a row (so none exist), and the two positions of each row differ.\end{enumerate}
