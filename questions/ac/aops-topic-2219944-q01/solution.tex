Multiplying both terms of a ratio by the same number does not change it. Multiplying by $1000$ removes the decimals:
\[2.050:1=2050:1000.\]
Since $\gcd(2050,1000)=50$, dividing by $50$ gives $\boxed{41:20}$. (As a single number the ratio is $\dfrac{41}{20}=2.05$: the first quantity is $2.05$ times the second.)
