Put $q=\sqrt x>0$. The geometric factorization is
\[
q^n-q^{-n}=(q-q^{-1})\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}q^{n-1-2j}.
\]
The $n$ positive terms in the sum have product $1$, since their exponents add to zero. AM-GM therefore gives their sum at least $n$. Squaring the identity yields
\[
(q^n-q^{-n})^2\ge n^2(q-q^{-1})^2,
\]
which is the desired inequality. Equality holds for all $x$ when $n=1$, and for $x=1$ when $n>1$.
