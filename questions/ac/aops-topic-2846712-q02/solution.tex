One-digit numbers qualify vacuously. For two digits $\overline{ab}$ we need $b=\dfrac{3a}2$, so $a$ is even: $a=2,4,6$ gives $23$, $46$, $69$ ($a=8$ gives $12$, not a digit). The greatest is $\boxed{69}$.
