Replacing $x$ by $1-x$ gives $2f(1-x)+f(x)=(1-x)^2$. Twice the original equation minus this equation gives $3f(x)=2x^2-(1-x)^2=x^2+2x-1$. Hence $\boxed{f(x)=\dfrac{x^2+2x-1}{3}}$. Substituting this formula in the original identity verifies it for every real $x$.
