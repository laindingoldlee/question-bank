After $8$ steps every path is on the line $x+y=0$, at one of the points $(-4,4),(-3,3),\ldots,(4,-4)$. The points $(-1,1),(0,0),(1,-1)$ are strictly inside the square, so a valid path passes through one of $(-4,4),(-3,3),(-2,2)$ or their mirror images $(4,-4),(3,-3),(2,-2)$.

Consider a path through $(-k,k)$ with $k\in\{2,3,4\}$. Before that point it has $x\le-k\le-2$, and after it it has $y\ge k\ge2$, so it automatically stays outside or on the boundary of the square. The first half is any arrangement of $4-k$ right-steps and $4+k$ up-steps, and the second half any arrangement of $4+k$ right-steps and $4-k$ up-steps, giving $\binom{8}{4-k}^2$ paths:
\[
k=4:\ 1^2=1,\qquad k=3:\ 8^2=64,\qquad k=2:\ 28^2=784 .
\]
By symmetry in the line $y=x$, the paths through $(k,-k)$ contribute the same. The total is
\[
2(1+64+784)=\boxed{1698}.
\]
