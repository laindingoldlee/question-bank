On an island every inhabitant is either a knight, who always tells the truth, or a knave, who always lies. You stand at a road and want to know whether it leads to the City of Knights or to the City of Knaves. An islander, of whom you do not know whether he is a knight or a knave, will answer one yes-or-no question. What single question can you ask so that his answer tells you where the road leads?
