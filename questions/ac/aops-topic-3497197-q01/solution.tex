There are $\binom42=6$ possible pairs, of which $2\cdot2=4$ have different colors. Thus a trial succeeds with probability $\dfrac{2}{3}$ and fails with probability $\dfrac{1}{3}$. Replacement resets the urn. Two failures followed by success have probability $(\dfrac{1}{3})^2\dfrac{2}{3}=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{27}}$, choice (b).
