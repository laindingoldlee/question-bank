Let the bases be $4k,5k$ and the height be $h$. The triangles formed by the diagonals and the two parallel bases are similar, so the diagonal intersection is at height $\dfrac{4}{9}h$ from the shorter base. A parallel cross-section has length varying linearly with height, hence the cut has length
\[
4k+\dfrac{4}{9}(5k-4k)=\dfrac{40k}{9}.
\]
The two areas are therefore
\[
\dfrac{1}{2}\left(4k+\dfrac{40k}{9}\right)\dfrac{4h}{9}
=\dfrac{152kh}{81},
\]
\[
\dfrac{1}{2}\left(5k+\dfrac{40k}{9}\right)\dfrac{5h}{9}
=\dfrac{425kh}{162}.
\]
Their requested ratio is $\boxed{304:425}$.
