Completing squares gives $(x-7)^2+(y-3)^2=64 $. By Cauchy--Schwarz, $ 3(x-7)+4(y-3)\le\sqrt{3^2+4^2}\cdot8=40 $. Consequently $ 3x+4y\le21+12+40=73 $. Equality occurs when $(x-7,y-3)=\dfrac{8}{5}(3,4)$, namely at $(\dfrac{59}{5},\dfrac{47}{5})$, so the maximum is $\boxed{73}$.
