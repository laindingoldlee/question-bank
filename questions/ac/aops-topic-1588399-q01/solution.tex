Since $\sqrt x=x^{\dfrac{1}{2}}$ and $\left(x^m\right)^n=x^{mn}$,
\[
\left(\sqrt x\right)^3=\left(x^{\dfrac{1}{2}}\right)^3=\boxed{x^{\dfrac{3}{2}}}\qquad\left(=x\sqrt x\right).
\]
The key's $\dfrac{3}{x^2}$ is wrong: for $x=4$ we get $\left(\sqrt4\right)^3=2^3=8$, while $\dfrac{3}{4^2}=\dfrac{3}{16}$. (Also $4^{\dfrac{3}{2}}=\left(\sqrt4\right)^3=8$, as it should be.)
