By Vieta's formulas, $a$, $b$, $c$ are the roots of
\[
t^3-2t^2-7t+14=0 .
\]
Group the terms: $t^2(t-2)-7(t-2)=(t-2)(t^2-7)$. The roots are $2$, $\sqrt7$ and $-\sqrt7$, and since $\sqrt7\approx2.65>2$, the largest is $\boxed{\sqrt7}$.
