The sum of the coefficients of a polynomial $P$ is $P(1)$. Here
\[P(1)=(1-2)(1-y)(1-z)=-(1-y-z+yz)=-1+y+z-yz.\]
Setting this equal to $y+z$ gives $-1-yz=0$, so $yz=\boxed{-1}$.
