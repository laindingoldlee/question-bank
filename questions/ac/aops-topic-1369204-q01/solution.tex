Let $w_n$ be the number of ways to climb $n$ steps. The last jump is either one step (from step $n-1$) or two steps (from step $n-2$), so
\[w_n=w_{n-1}+w_{n-2},\qquad w_1=1,\ w_2=2 .\]
Then $w_3=3$, $w_4=5$, $w_5=\boxed{8}$. These are the Fibonacci numbers.

Check by listing according to the number of two-step jumps: none ($11111$, $1$ way); one ($2111$ in any order, $4$ ways); two ($221$ in any order, $3$ ways). Total $1+4+3=8$.
