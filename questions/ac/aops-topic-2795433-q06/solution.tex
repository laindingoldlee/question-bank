If ``I am $C$'s dad'' were true, $C$ would be the child, so $R$ would be the mom. But then both of $C$'s statements would be true. So it is false, and ``I am $R$'s daughter'' is true: $A$ is the child, a daughter.

Then ``$R$ and $A$ are my parents'' is false, so ``$R$ and $A$ have different genders'' is true. Hence $R$ is male, the dad, and $C$ is the mom. Answer: $\boxed{R\text{ is the dad; }A\text{ is the child, a daughter}}$.
