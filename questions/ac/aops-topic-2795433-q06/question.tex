A family of dwarfs has a dad, a mom and a child, named $A$, $R$, $C$ in some order. $C$ said: ``$R$ and $A$ have different genders'' and ``$R$ and $A$ are my parents.'' $A$ said: ``I am $C$'s dad'' and ``I am $R$'s daughter.'' Each dwarf told exactly one truth and one lie. Who is the dad, who is the child, and is the child a son or a daughter?
