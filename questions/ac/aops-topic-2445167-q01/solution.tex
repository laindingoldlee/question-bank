Move everything to the left side and complete squares one variable at a time:
\begin{align*}
0&=x^2-4xy+5y^2-6yz+10z^2-2z+1\\
&=(x-2y)^2+y^2-6yz+10z^2-2z+1\\
&=(x-2y)^2+(y-3z)^2+z^2-2z+1\\
&=(x-2y)^2+(y-3z)^2+(z-1)^2.
\end{align*}
A square of a real number is never negative (the ``trivial inequality''), so a sum of three squares is $0$ only when each square is $0$:
\[z-1=0,\qquad y-3z=0,\qquad x-2y=0.\]
Hence $z=1$, $y=3$, $x=6$, and the only solution is $\boxed{(x,y,z)=(6,3,1)}$ (it is even the only real solution). Check: $36+45+10=91$ and $72+18+2-1=91$.
