Since $g(n)\le2024$, $n\le20249$. Numbers with at most four digits cannot work: both truncated numbers are at most $999$, so their sum is at most $1998$. Thus write a five-digit number as $n=10000a+10m+b$, where $a=1$ or $2$, $0\le m\le999$, and $0\le b\le9$. The equation becomes $1000a+11m+b=2024$.
For $a=1$, $11m+b=1024$ forces $m=93,b=1$, giving $10931$. For $a=2$, $11m+b=24$ forces $m=2,b=2$, giving $20022$. Their sum is $\boxed{30953}$.
