A tennis tournament has an even number $n\ge2$ of players, among them the best friends Luke and Liam. In the first round every player plays exactly one match, and the pairing of the players into matches is chosen uniformly at random among all possible pairings. What is the probability that Luke and Liam play each other in the first round?
