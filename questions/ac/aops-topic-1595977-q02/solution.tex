Let $N=20172017=2017\cdot10001=2017\cdot73\cdot137$, a product of three distinct primes. The equation is
\[
(c-b)(c+b)=N^2 .
\]
Put $d=c-b$ and $e=c+b$. Then $de=N^2$ and $0<d<e$ (since $b>0$). Conversely, any factorisation $N^2=de$ with $d<e$ gives a solution $c=\dfrac{e+d}{2}$, $b=\dfrac{e-d}{2}$: these are positive integers because $N^2$ is odd, so $d$ and $e$ are both odd.

So the solutions correspond to the divisors $d$ of $N^2$ with $d<N$. Since $N^2=2017^2\cdot73^2\cdot137^2$ has $3\cdot3\cdot3=27$ divisors, and they pair up as $d\leftrightarrow\dfrac{N^2}{d}$ except for $d=N$, the number of divisors below $N$ is $\dfrac{27-1}{2}=13$.

There are $\boxed{13}$ pairs $(b,c)$.
