Equality for every x requires equal x-coefficients and constants: $a=5 b$ and $2 b=3 a$. Substituting gives $2 b=15 b$, so $b=0$ and $a=0$. These values plainly work. Thus $\boxed{(a,b)=(0,0)}$.
