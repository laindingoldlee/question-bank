$B=\dfrac{51\cdot52\cdots100}{2^{50}}=\dfrac{100!}{50!\cdot2^{50}}$. Since $2^{50}\cdot50!=2\cdot4\cdot6\cdots100$ is the product of the even numbers up to $100$,
\[
B=\dfrac{1\cdot2\cdot3\cdots100}{2\cdot4\cdots100}=1\cdot3\cdot5\cdots99=A .
\]
So $\boxed{A=B}$.
