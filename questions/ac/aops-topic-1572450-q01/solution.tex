Measure rates in driveways per minute. Together they do $\dfrac{1}{30}$, and Gertrude does $\dfrac{1}{40}$, so Bertha does
\[
\dfrac{1}{30}-\dfrac{1}{40}=\dfrac{4-3}{120}=\dfrac{1}{120}
\]
of the driveway per minute. Alone she needs $\boxed{120\text{ minutes}}$ (two hours).
