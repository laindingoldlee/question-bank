Taking $x=y=0$ forces $a\ge1$. For fixed $t=xy$, the minimum of $x^2+y^2$ is $2|t|$, attained with $x=y$ if $t\ge0$ and $x=-y$ if $t\le0$. We therefore require $at^2+2|t|-3t+a-1\ge0$ for all real $t$.
For $t\le0$, the expression $at^2-5t+a-1$ is nonnegative when $a\ge1$. For $t\ge0$ it is
\[
a\left(t-\dfrac{1}{2a}\right)^2+a-1-\dfrac{1}{4a}.
\]
Its minimizing argument is allowed, so necessity and sufficiency are $4a^2-4a-1\ge0$ and $a\ge1$. Hence $\boxed{a\ge\dfrac{1+\sqrt2}{2}}$.
