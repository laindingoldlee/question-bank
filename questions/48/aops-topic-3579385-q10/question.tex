A $4\times4$ square grid is partitioned by selecting some of its three full internal horizontal grid lines and some of its three full internal vertical grid lines, and cutting along every selected line from boundary to boundary. How many partitions into at least two pieces result? Explain why any interior point where at least three pieces meet is a vertex of four pieces.
