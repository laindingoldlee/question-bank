Adding $11\cdot17=187$ to both sides gives
\[(x+17)(y+11)=4726+187=4913=17^3.\]
So $x+17$ is one of the $8$ integer divisors $\pm1,\pm17,\pm289,\pm4913$ of $17^3$, and then $y+11$ is determined. There are $\boxed{8}$ ordered pairs.
