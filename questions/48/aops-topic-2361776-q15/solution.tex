The ellipse has area $\pi\cdot8\cdot10=80\pi$. The circle has $2\pi r=18\pi$, so $r=9$ and its area is $81\pi$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph; pair O=(0,0);
draw(ellipse(O,10,8),blue+linewidth(0.9)); draw(circle(O,9),red);
draw(O--(10,0),blue); draw(O--(0,8),blue); dot(O);
label("$10$",(5,0),S,blue); label("$8$",(0,4),W,blue); label("$r=9$",9*dir(45),NE,red);
\end{asy}

The difference is $81\pi-80\pi=\boxed{\pi}$.
