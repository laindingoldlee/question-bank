Each side meets the circle at most twice, giving at most ten intersections. A concentric circle with radius strictly between the pentagon's apothem and circumradius crosses every side twice. Thus $\boxed{10}$ is attainable.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw(circle((0,0),.9));path h=dir(90);for(int j=1;j<5;++j)h=h--dir(90+72*j);draw(h--cycle,blue);label("ten crossings",(0,-1.3));
\end{asy}
