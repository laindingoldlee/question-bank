Let the nickel give $j$ heads. For $j=0,1,2$, its probabilities are $\dfrac{1}{4},\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{1}{4}$, and the probabilities that the penny exceeds $j$ are $\dfrac{7}{8},\dfrac{4}{8},\dfrac{1}{8}$. Thus the desired probability is $\dfrac{1}{4}\dfrac{7}{8}+\dfrac{1}{2}\dfrac{4}{8}+\dfrac{1}{4}\dfrac{1}{8}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$.
