Every $f_n(1)=1$. Differentiating $f_n(x)=\exp(f_{n-1}(x)\ln x)$ gives
\[f_n'(x)=f_n(x)\left(f_{n-1}'(x)\ln x+\dfrac{f_{n-1}(x)}{x}\right).\]
At $x=1$ the logarithmic term vanishes, leaving $f_n'(1)=1$. Therefore the answer is $\boxed1$, choice B.
