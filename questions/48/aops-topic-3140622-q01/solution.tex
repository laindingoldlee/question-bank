Undo one deletion stage: the $k$ th surviving position after removing every $m$ th item came from position $k+\left\lfloor\dfrac{k-1}{m-1}\right\rfloor$. Apply this for $m=5,4,3$, in reverse order. Positions 2019,2020,2021 become 2523,2524,2526, then 3363,3365,3367, then 5044,5047,5050. Their original digits are 4,2,5, so the sum is $\boxed{11}$.
