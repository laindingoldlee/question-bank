Van Eck's sequence starts with $0$. After that, each term looks at the previous term $t$: if $t$ has appeared earlier, the new term is the number of steps back to its most recent earlier occurrence; if not, the new term is $0$. Check that the sequence begins
\[0,0,1,0,2,0,2,2,1,6,0,5,0,2,6,5,4,0\]
and find the next term.
