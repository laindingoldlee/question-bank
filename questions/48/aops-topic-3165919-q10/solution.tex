Once the first residue modulo three is chosen, all subsequent residues are forced to alternate with its negative. The residue patterns are $00000$, $12121$, and $21212$. There are four digits of residue zero and three of each other residue. Thus the count is $4^5+2\cdot3^5=1024+486=\boxed{1510}$.
