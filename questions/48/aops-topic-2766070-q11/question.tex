Eight people sit around a round table. Each person's enemies are the person directly across the table and the two people beside them; everyone else is a friend. They now reseat themselves around the table so that no two enemies sit next to each other. Seatings that differ by a rotation are considered the same (mirror images are different). In how many ways can they do this?
