There are $\dbinom{20}{2}=190$ equally likely pairs of seats. In one circle of $10$ seats numbered $0$ to $9$ around the circle, the pairs with exactly one seat between them are $\{i,i+2\}$ (indices mod $10$) for $i=0,\dots,9$; these are $10$ different pairs. The two circles give $20$ such pairs, so the probability is
\[\dfrac{20}{190}=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{19}}.\]
(The answer is the same if ``one chair apart'' means adjacent seats, since each circle also has $10$ adjacent pairs.)
