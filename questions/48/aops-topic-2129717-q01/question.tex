Find the error in the following ``proof'' that there are infinitely many pairs of twin primes (primes that differ by $2$).

\emph{Let $p_1=2,p_2=3,p_3=5,\dots$ be the primes in increasing order, and let $P_n=p_1p_2\cdots p_n$. For every $n$, the numbers $P_n-1$ and $P_n+1$ are both prime, and they differ by $2$. Hence there are infinitely many pairs of twin primes.}
