Testing residues $n=0,\ldots,9$ in $n^3+9n^2+7n$ modulo $10$ gives $0,7,8,9,6,5,2,3,4,1$, respectively. Thus $n=10t+7$. Expanding gives\[n^3+2009n^2+27n=1000t^3+203000t^2+283000t+98973.\]All terms except the constant are multiples of $1000$, so the last three digits are $\boxed{973}$.
