Collect the terms with $y$ into a square: $(x+y-5)^2=x^2+y^2+2xy-10x-10y+25$, so the equation becomes
\[
(x+y-5)^2+x^2-4x+4=0,\qquad\text{i.e.}\qquad(x+y-5)^2+(x-2)^2=0 .
\]
Both squares must vanish: $x=2$ and $y=3$. The only solution is $\boxed{(x,y)=(2,3)}$.
