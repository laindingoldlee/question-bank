For side lengths $s,t$, the perimeter ratio is $4s:4t=s:t=a:b$. Squaring gives area ratio $s^2:t^2=\boxed{a^2:b^2}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw((0,0)--(2,0)--(2,2)--(0,2)--cycle);draw((4,0)--(7,0)--(7,3)--(4,3)--cycle);label("$s$",(1,0),S);label("$t$",(5.5,0),S);label("$s^2$",(1,1));label("$t^2$",(5.5,1.5));
\end{asy}
