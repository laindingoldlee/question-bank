The list has $\dfrac{350}{50}=7$ numbers. Besides $100$ and the largest number $L$, there are $5$ other distinct positive integers, whose sum is at least $1+2+3+4+5=15$. So
\[
L\le350-100-15=235 .
\]
The list $1,2,3,4,5,100,235$ attains this, so the answer is $\boxed{235}$.
