In trapezoid $ABCD$, $AB\parallel CD$, $AB<CD$, and $O=AD\cap BC$ lies beyond the smaller base. Given $BO=5,BC=11$, the perpendicular feet $H,I$ of $A,B$ lie on segment $CD$, and $\dfrac{AH}{AD}=\dfrac{2}{3}$, $\dfrac{BI}{BC}=\dfrac{5}{6}$, express $AB+CD=\dfrac{a\sqrt b+c\sqrt d}{e}$ in reduced form with positive coefficients, squarefree $b<d$, and find $a+b+c+d-e$.
