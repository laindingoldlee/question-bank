The height is $h=BI=\dfrac{5}{6}\cdot11=\dfrac{55}{6}$ and $AD=\dfrac{3}{2}h=\dfrac{55}{4}$. The horizontal projections of the two legs are $DH=\sqrt{AD^2-h^2}=\dfrac{55\sqrt5}{12}$ and $IC=\sqrt{11^2-h^2}=\dfrac{11\sqrt{11}}{6}$. Since both feet lie on the longer base, $CD-AB=DH+IC$. Similar triangles $OAB,OCD$ give $\dfrac{AB}{CD}=\dfrac{5}{16}$, so $AB+CD=\dfrac{21}{11}(CD-AB)=\dfrac{35\sqrt5+14\sqrt{11}}{4}$. The requested value is $35+5+14+11-4=\boxed{61}$.
