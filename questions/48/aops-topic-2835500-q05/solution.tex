Every integer is $3m$, $3m+1$ or $3m+2$. Their squares are $9m^2$, $9m^2+6m+1$ and $9m^2+12m+4=3(3m^2+4m+1)+1$. So every square leaves remainder $0$ or $1$ on division by $3$, never $2$. $\blacksquare$
