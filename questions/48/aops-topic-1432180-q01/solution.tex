ALASKA has three A's and one each of L, S, K. Sort by the number of A's used.
\begin{itemize}
\item No A, or one A, with all three letters different: the letters are chosen from the four distinct letters A, L, S, K, giving $4\cdot3\cdot2=24$ strings.
\item Exactly two A's: choose the third letter ($3$ ways) and its position ($3$ ways): $9$ strings.
\item Three A's: AAA, $1$ string.
\end{itemize}
The total is $24+9+1=\boxed{34}$.
