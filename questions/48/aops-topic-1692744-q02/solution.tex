In the time Ross runs $500$ yards, Atacus runs $475$ yards, so Atacus's speed is $\dfrac{475}{500}=\dfrac{19}{20}$ of Ross's. In the second race Ross runs $520$ yards. In that time Atacus runs $\dfrac{19}{20}\cdot520=494$ yards, so he is still $6$ yards from the finish. So $\boxed{\text{Ross wins by }6\text{ yards}}$. (By time: Ross needs $520$ units of his time, Atacus $500\cdot\dfrac{20}{19}=526\dfrac{6}{19}$, so Ross also finishes first.)
