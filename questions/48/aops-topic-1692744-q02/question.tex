Ross and Atacus, starting together, ran a $500$-yard race, each at a constant speed. When Ross crossed the finish line, Atacus was exactly $25$ yards behind him. They run the race again, this time with Ross starting $20$ yards behind the original starting line, and each running at the same constant speed as before. Who wins, and by how much?
