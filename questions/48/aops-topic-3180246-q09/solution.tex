Since $rst=9$, $t\ne0$. Substitute $r+s=8-2t$ and $rs=\dfrac{9}{t}$ into the second relation to get $\dfrac{18}{t}+t(8-2t)=12$. This simplifies to $(t-3)(t^2-t+3)=0$. The quadratic has negative discriminant, so real $t=3$. Then $r+s=2,rs=3$, and $P(x)=(x-3)(x^2-2x+3)$. Thus $P(2)=-3$ and $\boxed{|P(2)|=3}$.
