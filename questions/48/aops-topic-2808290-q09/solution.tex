By the angle-bisector theorem, $BM:MC=13:15$, so $MC=\dfrac{15}{28}BC$. The strict triangle inequalities give $2<BC<28$, hence $\dfrac{15}{14}<MC<15$. Every value in this interval is attainable by a nondegenerate triangle with sides 13,15,$BC$. The integer possibilities are 2 through 14, whose sum is $\dfrac{(2+14)13}{2}=\boxed{104}$.
