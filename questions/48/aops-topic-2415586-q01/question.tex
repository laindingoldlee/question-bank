Triangle $ABC$ has $AB=13$, $BC=15$ and $CA=14$. Let $BM$ be the altitude from $B$, with $M$ on $AC$. The circle centred at $C$ that is tangent to line $BM$ meets side $BC$ at $N$. Let $Z$ be the midpoint of the minor arc $MN$ of this circle. Find the area of quadrilateral $MZNC$.
