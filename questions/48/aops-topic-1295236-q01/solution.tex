The digit sum must be at least $27$, so the number has at least $3$ digits; the only $3$-digit number with digit sum $27$ is $999=27\cdot37$, a multiple of $27$. Four-digit numbers with digit sum $27$ in increasing order are $1899,1989,1998,2799,\dots$, and $1899=27\cdot70+9$ is not a multiple of $27$. So the answer is $\boxed{1899}$.
