Alex, Benny and Calvin took part in a $15$ km race. Alex won, finishing $4$ km ahead of Benny and $7$ km ahead of Calvin. Each runner kept a constant speed throughout the race. How far apart were Benny and Calvin when Benny finished the race? A student answers $3$ km. Find the correct distance and explain the error.
