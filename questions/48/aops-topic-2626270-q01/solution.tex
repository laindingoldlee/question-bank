The sums are nested: each upper limit depends on the variable of the sum to its left, so the expression is evaluated from the inside out (from right to left). For fixed $n_1$, the innermost sum counts the values $0\le n_0\le n_1$; the next counts pairs with $0\le n_0\le n_1\le n_2$; and so on. Altogether the expression counts the $61$-tuples of integers with
\[0\le n_0\le n_1\le\cdots\le n_{59}\le n_{60}\le2.\]
Such a non-decreasing tuple is determined by how many of its entries equal $0$, $1$ and $2$, i.e. by nonnegative integers $u+v+w=61$. By stars and bars there are
\[\binom{61+2}{2}=\binom{63}{2}=\boxed{1953}\]
such tuples.
