A square is $0$ or $1$ modulo $4$, so a sum of two squares is $0$, $1$ or $2$ modulo $4$, never $3$. Any $4$ consecutive integers include one that is $3\pmod4$, so a block has length at most $3$. The block $72=6^2+6^2$, $73=8^2+3^2$, $74=7^2+5^2$ has length $3$. The answer is $\boxed{3}$.
