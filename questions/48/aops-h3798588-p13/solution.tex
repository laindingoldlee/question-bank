Write the three column widths as $a,b,c$ and row heights as $u,v,w$. The corner perimeters sum to $4(a+c+u+w)$ and the central perimeter is $2(b+v)$. The four side-centre rectangles have total perimeter $2(a+c+u+w)+4(b+v)$, which is half the corner sum plus twice the centre perimeter. Thus the answer is $\dfrac{10+14+14+18}{2}+2\cdot14=\boxed{56}$.
