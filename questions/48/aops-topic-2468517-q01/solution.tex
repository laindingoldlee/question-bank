Going from the $8^\text{th}$ term to the $80^\text{th}$ term adds the common difference $d$ exactly $72$ times, so
\[72d=-582-(-6)=-576,\qquad d=-8.\]
The third term is the first term plus $2d$, so the absolute difference is $|2d|=\boxed{16}$.
