Find all ordered quadruples $(w,x,y,z)$ of real numbers that satisfy the system
\[
\begin{aligned}
wxy+wx+xy+yw+w+x+y&=11,\\
xyz+xy+yz+zx+x+y+z&=35,\\
yzw+yz+zw+wy+y+z+w&=47,\\
zwx+zw+wx+xz+z+w+x&=143.
\end{aligned}
\]
