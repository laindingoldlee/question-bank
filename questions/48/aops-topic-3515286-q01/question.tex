In the least-absolute-remainder Euclidean algorithm, begin with integers $a\ge b>0$, and at every division choose the remainder of smallest absolute value, replacing it by its absolute value. Count the final division with zero remainder. If $b$ has $d$ decimal digits and the algorithm uses $r$ divisions, prove $r<\dfrac{8}{3}d+\dfrac{4}{3}$.
