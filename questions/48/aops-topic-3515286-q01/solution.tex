Let $u_0=b,u_1,\ldots,u_{r-1}$ be the positive remainder chain, followed by zero. Each $u_{i+1}\le\dfrac{1}{2}u_i$. For adjacent positive terms, the preceding dividend is $qu_i\pm u_{i+1}$. In the plus case $q\ge2$. In the minus case, $q=2$ would contradict the bound $u_i\le\dfrac{1}{2}u_{i-1}$, so $q\ge3$, and $3u_i-u_{i+1}\ge2u_i+u_{i+1}$. Thus $u_{i-1}\ge2u_i+u_{i+1}$. Let $P_1=1,P_2=2,P_{j+1}=2P_j+P_{j-1}$. Backward induction gives $b\ge P_r$. For $r=1$ the claimed bound is immediate. For $r\ge2$, set $\lambda=1+\sqrt2$. The recurrence solution is $P_r=\dfrac{\lambda^r-(-\lambda^{-1})^r}{2\sqrt2}\ge2\lambda^{r-2}$; the inequality follows by bounding $\lambda^{-2r}\le\lambda^{-4}$ in this formula. Since $\lambda^8=577+408\sqrt2>1000$ and $2^4>10$, we obtain $b\ge P_r>10^{\dfrac{3r-4}{8}}$. But $b<10^d$, so $d>\dfrac{3r-4}{8}$, equivalent to the required bound.
