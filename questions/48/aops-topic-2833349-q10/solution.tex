With probability 1, all four endpoints are distinct. Among the three ways to pair their cyclically ordered positions, one pairing alternates endpoints and makes the chords cross; the other two pairings do not cross. By symmetry these pairings are equally likely. Noncrossing chords yield three pieces, so the probability is $\dfrac{2}{3}$. Therefore $\boxed{100p+q=203}$.
