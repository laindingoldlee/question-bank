The condition is $n^2\equiv1\pmod{100}$. It requires $n$ odd and $n^2\equiv1\pmod{25}$. Since $\gcd(n-1,n+1)$ divides $2$, the latter condition implies $n\equiv1$ or $-1\pmod{25}$. The odd residues modulo $100$ are $1,49,51,99$. The two-digit values are $49,51,99$, with sum $\boxed{199}$.
