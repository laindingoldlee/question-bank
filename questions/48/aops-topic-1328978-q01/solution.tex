A time of $m$ minutes and $s$ seconds ($0\le s\le59$) is $60m+s$ seconds, and its display $m's$ (with $s$ written as two digits) read as a number is $100m+s$. These are equal only if $100m=60m$, i.e. $m=0$. So for every time of one minute or more the reading is too large (by $40m$), and the answer is \textbf{no}.
