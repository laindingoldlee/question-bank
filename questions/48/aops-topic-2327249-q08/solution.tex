Pair the first and last terms, the second and second-to-last, and so on: writing the sum forwards and backwards and adding gives $101$ columns each summing to $102$, so twice the sum is $101\cdot102$. Hence
\[1+2+\cdots+101=\dfrac{101\cdot102}{2}=\boxed{5151}.\]
