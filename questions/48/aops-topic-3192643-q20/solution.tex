The boundary cannot obstruct any of the first four steps. A return path has either two right and two left steps (6 orders), two up and two down (6), or one of each (24). Thus probability is $\dfrac{36}{4^4}=\dfrac{9}{64}$, giving $\boxed{m+n=73}$.
