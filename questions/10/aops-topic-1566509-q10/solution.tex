The $3$rd, $20$th and $37$th terms are equally spaced in the sequence ($17$ steps apart), so they form an arithmetic sequence themselves, and the $20$th term is the average of the other two:
\[
a_{37}=2a_{20}-a_3=2\cdot55-20=\boxed{90}.
\]
(Directly: $17d=55-20=35$, so $a_{37}=a_{20}+17d=55+35=90$.)
