Jason has three coupons. Coupon A decreases the price of an item by $20\%$. Coupon B subtracts $50$ dollars from the price. Coupon C decreases the price by $10\%$ and then subtracts $20$ dollars. Jason uses all three coupons, one after another in some order, on a $1000$-dollar computer. What is the least amount of money he can pay for the computer, in dollars?
