Let $f(r)=r\left(\sqrt{2660}-r\right)$ for real $r\ge0$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the maximum value of $f$ and where it is attained.
\item[(b)] For which $r\ge0$ is $f(r)<0$?
\item[(c)] How many prime numbers are values of $f$? What is the smallest $r\ge0$ for which $f(r)$ is prime?
\end{enumerate}
