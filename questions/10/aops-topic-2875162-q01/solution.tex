Note $\sqrt{2660}=2\sqrt{665}$, so $f(r)=665-\left(r-\sqrt{665}\right)^2$.

(a) The maximum is $\boxed{665}$, attained at $r=\sqrt{665}$ (half of $\sqrt{2660}$).

(b) $f(r)<0$ iff $r>\boxed{\sqrt{2660}=2\sqrt{665}}$.

(c) As $r$ goes from $0$ to $\sqrt{665}$, $f$ increases continuously from $0$ to $665$, and then decreases without bound. So the values of $f$ are all real numbers at most $665$, and the prime values are the primes up to $665$. There are $\boxed{121}$ of them.

For a prime $p\le665$, the smallest $r$ with $f(r)=p$ is the smaller root of $r^2-2\sqrt{665}\,r+p=0$, namely $r=\sqrt{665}-\sqrt{665-p}$. This increases with $p$, so it is smallest for $p=2$: $r=\boxed{\sqrt{665}-\sqrt{663}}\approx0.0388$.
