Right triangle $ABC$ gives $AC^2=25+324=349$. Put $AD=x>0$. The cosine rule in $ADC$ gives $349=x^2+9-6x\cos120^\circ=x^2+3x+9$. Thus $(x-17)(x+20)=0$, so $x=17$ and $\boxed{AD^2=289}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(5,0),C=(5,18);real L=sqrt(349),proj=(289+349-9)/(2*L),hh=sqrt(289-proj*proj);pair u=C/L,D=proj*u+hh*rotate(90)*u;draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C,dashed);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);
\end{asy}
