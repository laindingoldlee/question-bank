A professor awards the ceiling $\lceil r\rceil$ of a nonnegative raw score whenever there exists a finite sequence of upward roundings to powers of ten that ends at $\lceil r\rceil$. A single rounding to an integer is permitted. A marked score of at least 90 earns an A. Find the largest raw score not earning an A as $\dfrac{m}{n}$ in lowest terms, and give $m+n$.
