Under the stated upward-rounding rule, choose the one-term sequence with exponent $a_1=0$. This rounds every raw score directly to its ceiling, so every $r>89$ receives at least 90, while $r=89$ receives 89. Thus the largest non-A raw score is $R = 89 = \dfrac{89}{1}$, giving $\boxed{m+n=90}$. Iterated rounding cannot change this conclusion because the permitted direct rounding already qualifies.
