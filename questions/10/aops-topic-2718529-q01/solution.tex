A radius perpendicular to a chord bisects it, so the half-chord ($9$), the distance to the centre ($3$) and the radius form a right triangle:
\[r=\sqrt{9^2+3^2}=\sqrt{90}=\boxed{3\sqrt{10}}\text{ in}.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real R=sqrt(90);pair O=(0,0),M=(0,3),X=(-9,3),Y=(9,3);
draw(circle(O,R),linewidth(0.8));draw(X--Y,linewidth(0.8));draw(O--(0,R));draw(O--Y,dashed);
draw(M+(0.4,0)--M+(0.4,0.4)--M+(0,0.4));dot(O);
label("$O$",O,S);label("$3$",(0,1.5),W,fontsize(8pt));label("$9$",(4.5,3),N,fontsize(8pt));label("$r$",Y/2,SE,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}
