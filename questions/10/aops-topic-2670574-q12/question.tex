Countably many prisoners $P_1,P_2,P_3,\ldots$ stand in a line, with $P_1$ at the back, all facing forward, so that $P_k$ sees the hats of exactly $P_{k+1},P_{k+2},\ldots$. On each hat a real number is written, and no prisoner can see his own. Each prisoner must guess the number on his own hat, but the prisoners are deaf: they get no information at all from the guesses or fates of the others. They may agree on a strategy beforehand. Prove that there is a strategy which guarantees that only finitely many prisoners guess wrongly. (You may use the axiom of choice.)
