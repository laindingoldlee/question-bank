A game has six suspect cards, six weapon cards and nine room cards. One uniformly chosen card of each category is hidden. The remaining eighteen cards are dealt uniformly into three labelled hands of six. Independent games are played with one blind guess in each game. What is the least number of games needed for at least a $ 75\%$ chance of a correct guess in some game?
