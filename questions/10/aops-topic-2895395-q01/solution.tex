An $m\times n$ grid has $m(n+1)$ segments one way and $n(m+1)$ the other, so $2mn+m+n=1000$. Multiply by $2$ and add $1$: $(2m+1)(2n+1)=2001=3\cdot23\cdot29$. The factorisations into two factors greater than $1$ are $3\cdot667$, $23\cdot87$ and $29\cdot69$. They give $\boxed{1\times333,\ 11\times43,\ 14\times34}$ (in either order).
