For $n$ attendees, the ordinary count is $\binom n2$ and the extra handshakes number $n-1$. Thus $\dfrac{(n-1)(n+2)}{2}=2015$, or $n^2+n-4032=0$. This factors as $(n-63)(n+64)=0$, so the positive solution is $\boxed{63}$.
