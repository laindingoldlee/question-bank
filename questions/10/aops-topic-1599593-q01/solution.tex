``$x$ is $3$ larger than its reciprocal'' means $x=\dfrac{1}{x}+3$, i.e. $x-\dfrac{1}{x}=3$. (Such $x$ exist: $x^2-3x-1=0$ has real roots $\dfrac{3\pm\sqrt{13}}{2}$.) Therefore
\[
\left(x-\dfrac{1}{x}\right)^4=3^4=\boxed{81}.
\]
