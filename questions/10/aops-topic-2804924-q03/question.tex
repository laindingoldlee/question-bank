In the long multiplication below, distinct letters are distinct digits ($a,b,d,f\ne0$):
\[\begin{array}{r}abc\\ \times\ de\\ \hline fagc\\ bbh\phantom{0}\\ \hline ffegc\end{array}\]
That is, $abc\cdot e=\overline{fagc}$, $abc\cdot d=\overline{bbh}$ and $abc\cdot\overline{de}=\overline{ffegc}$. Find all solutions.
