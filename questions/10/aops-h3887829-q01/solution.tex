Let the common ratio be $r$. If $r\ne 0$, then $27r^{14}=99r^{10}$ gives $r^4=\dfrac{11}{3}$. Therefore $a_9=27r^8=27(\dfrac{11}{3})^2=\boxed{363}$. The standard contest convention here requires a nonzero common ratio. If zero is allowed, $r=0$ also satisfies the displayed equality and gives $a_9=0$; that convention must be excluded for a unique answer.
