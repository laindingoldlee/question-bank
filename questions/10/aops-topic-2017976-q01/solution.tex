Number the gates $1,2,\dots,12$. The ordered pair (old gate, new gate) is equally likely to be any of the $12\cdot 11=132$ ordered pairs of different gates. Dave walks at most $400$ feet exactly when the two gate numbers differ by at most $4$.

For a difference $d$ with $1\le d\le 11$ there are $12-d$ unordered pairs, hence $2(12-d)$ ordered pairs. The favourable ordered pairs number
\[
2\left(11+10+9+8\right)=76 .
\]
So the probability is
\[
\dfrac{76}{132}=\dfrac{19}{33},
\]
and $m+n=19+33=\boxed{52}$.
