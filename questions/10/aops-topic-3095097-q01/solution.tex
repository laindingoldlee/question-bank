Since $2\equiv-1\pmod3$, the expression is congruent to $(-1)^n-1$. This is 0 for even $n$ and $-2\equiv1$ for odd $n$. The remainders are $\boxed{0\text{ if }n\text{ is even; }1\text{ if }n\text{ is odd}}$.
