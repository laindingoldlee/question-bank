Let their common center be $O$. The radius $OD=13$ is perpendicular to chord $BC$, so $D$ is its midpoint and $BD^2=18^2-13^2=155$. Choose $D=(0,13)$ and $B=(\sqrt{155},13)$, with $O=(0,0)$. The opposite endpoint of the diameter is $A=(-\sqrt{155},-13)$. Therefore
\[
AD^2=155+26^2=831,\qquad \boxed{AD=\sqrt{831}}.
\]
