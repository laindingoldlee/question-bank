Adjacent people must have different genders, so the genders alternate. With $4$ girls and $3$ boys in $7$ seats, the only alternating pattern is $G\,B\,G\,B\,G\,B\,G$ (starting with a boy would need $4$ boys). The girls can be arranged in their seats in $4!$ ways and the boys in $3!$ ways, giving $4!\cdot3!=24\cdot6=\boxed{144}$ seatings.
