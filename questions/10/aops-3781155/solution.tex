Multiplying the recurrence by $a_{n+1}$ gives $a_{n+1}a_{n+2}=a_na_{n+1}+2$. Starting with $a_1a_2=2$, induction yields $a_na_{n+1}=2n$. Hence $\dfrac{a_{2k}}{a_{2k-2}} = \dfrac{2k-1}{2k-2}$, so $a_{2k}=2\dfrac{(2k-1)!!}{(2k-2)!!}=\dfrac{(2k-1)!}{2^{2k-3}((k-1)!)^2}$. For $k=1006$, the requested value is $\dfrac{2011!}{2011(1005!)^2}=\boxed{\binom{2010}{1005}}$.
