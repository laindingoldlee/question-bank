Each turn succeeds with probability $\dfrac14$. Cathleen wins on her first turn, or both miss and the game restarts: $p=\dfrac14+\left(\dfrac34\right)^2p$, so $\dfrac7{16}p=\dfrac14$ and $p=\boxed{\dfrac47}$.
