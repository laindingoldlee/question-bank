There are $16!$ unrestricted orders. Independently swap the positions of the sock and shoe in any of the eight designated pairs. This partitions orders into classes of $2^8$ orders, with exactly one satisfying all eight precedence requirements. Thus the count is $\boxed{\dfrac{16!}{2^8}}$.
