Put $O=(0,0,0),N=(10,0,0),K=(10,10,0)$ and $R=(u,0,v)$. Then $u^2+v^2=36$ and $(u-10)^2+v^2=64$. Consequently $RK^2=(u-10)^2+10^2+v^2=164$, so $RK=2\sqrt{41}$ and $\boxed{43}$.
