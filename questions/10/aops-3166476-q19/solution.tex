Triangles $ABF$ and $CEF$ are similar, so $AB:CE=\sqrt{175:28}=5:2$. The same similarity gives $AF:FC=5:2$, so $[BCF]=\dfrac{2}{5}[ABF]=70$. As $F$ lies five sevenths of the way from $A$ to $C$, $[ABF]=\dfrac{5}{14}[ABCD]$, yielding rectangle area 490. Remove triangles $ABF,BCF,CEF$ to get $[ADEF]=490-175-70-28=\boxed{217}$.
