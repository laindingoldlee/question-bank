The angle-bisector theorem gives $AD:DC=4:5$, so $D=\dfrac{5A+4C}{9}$ in vector notation. The incenter is the side-length weighted average $I=\dfrac{5A+6B+4C}{15}=\dfrac{9D+6B}{15}$, as follows by comparing its three equal-altitude subtriangle areas. Hence $BI:ID=9:6$, and $\boxed{\dfrac{BI}{DI}=\dfrac{3}{2}}$.
