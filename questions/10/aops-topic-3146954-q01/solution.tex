Let the digits be $a,b$, with $1\le a\le7$ and $0\le b\le7$. In decimal values, $8a+b+5(a+b)=8b+a$, so $b=6a$. The bounds force $a=1,b=6$. Thus $\boxed{16_8}$. Indeed $14+5(7)=49=61_8$.
