\textbf{(a)} Let the incircle, with radius $r$, touch $AB$ at $F$ and $AC$ at $E$. Tangent segments from a point to a circle are equal, so if the tangent lengths from $A$, $B$, $C$ are $x$, $y$, $z$, then $x+y=c$, $y+z=a$, $z+x=b$; adding, $x+y+z=s$, so $AF=AE=x=s-a$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0), C=(14,0), A=(5,12);
real a=14, b0=15, c=13, s=(a+b0+c)/2;
pair I=(a*A+b0*B+c*C)/(a+b0+c);
pair Fp=A+(s-a)/c*(B-A), Ep=A+(s-a)/b0*(C-A);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(circle(I,4),blue);
draw(A--I,red); draw(I--Fp,blue); draw(I--Ep,blue+dashed);
pair u1=unit(A-Fp)*0.6, u2=unit(I-Fp)*0.6; draw(Fp+u1--Fp+u1+u2--Fp+u2,blue);
dot(I); dot(Fp); dot(Ep);
label("$A$",A,N); label("$B$",B,SW); label("$C$",C,SE); label("$I$",I,SE); label("$F$",Fp,W); label("$E$",Ep,NE);
label("$s-a$",(A+Fp)/2,W,fontsize(8pt)); label("$r$",(I+Fp)/2,S,blue+fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}

The radius $IF$ is perpendicular to $AB$, so by Pythagoras in the right triangle $AFI$,
\[AI^2=(s-a)^2+r^2.\]
The area $K$ of the triangle is $rs$ (split the triangle into the three triangles $IBC$, $ICA$, $IAB$ of height $r$), and by Heron's formula $K^2=s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)$. Hence $r^2=\dfrac{K^2}{s^2}=\dfrac{(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}{s}$ and
\[AI^2=(s-a)^2+\dfrac{(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}{s}=\dfrac{s-a}{s}\Big[s(s-a)+(s-b)(s-c)\Big].\]
The bracket is $s^2-as+s^2-(b+c)s+bc=2s^2-(a+b+c)s+bc=bc$, since $a+b+c=2s$. Therefore
\[AI^2=\dfrac{bc(s-a)}{s}.\ \blacksquare\]

\textbf{(b)} Here $s=21$ and $s-a=7$, so $AI^2=\dfrac{15\cdot13\cdot7}{21}=65$ and $AI=\boxed{\sqrt{65}}$. (Directly: $K=\sqrt{21\cdot7\cdot6\cdot8}=84$, $r=\dfrac{84}{21}=4$ and $AI=\sqrt{7^2+4^2}=\sqrt{65}$.)
