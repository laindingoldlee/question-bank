Let $I$ be the incentre of triangle $ABC$, let $a=BC$, $b=CA$, $c=AB$ and let $s=\dfrac{a+b+c}{2}$ be the semiperimeter.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $AI^2=\dfrac{bc(s-a)}{s}$, so that the distance from a vertex to the incentre is determined by the side lengths alone.
\item[(b)] Find $AI$ when $a=14$, $b=15$ and $c=13$.
\end{enumerate}
