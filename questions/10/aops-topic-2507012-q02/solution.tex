The sum is
\[\sum_{k=1}^{99}k(100-k)=100\sum_{k=1}^{99}k-\sum_{k=1}^{99}k^2=100\cdot\dfrac{99\cdot100}{2}-\dfrac{99\cdot100\cdot199}{6}=495000-328350=\boxed{166650}.\]
(This equals $\dbinom{101}{3}$: choosing $3$ numbers from $0,1,\dots,100$ by their middle number $k$ gives $k(100-k)$ choices.)
