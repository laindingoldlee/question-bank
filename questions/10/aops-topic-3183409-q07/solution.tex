Stapling requires at least $\dfrac{105}{3}=35$ minutes. The combined filling rate is $\dfrac{5}{3}+\dfrac{7}{5}=\dfrac{46}{15}>3$, so filling can keep up with the stapler; operations may be performed in either order. Thus the bottleneck bound is attained and the time is $\boxed{35\text{ minutes}}$.
