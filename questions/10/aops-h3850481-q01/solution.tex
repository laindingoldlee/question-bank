Let $e_1=x+y+z$, $e_2=xy+yz+zx$, and $e_3=xyz$. Then $e_1=3$ and
\[
e_2=\dfrac{e_1^2-(x^2+y^2+z^2)}{2}=2.
\]
Newton's identity $p_3=e_1p_2-e_2p_1+3e_3$ gives $7=15-6+3e_3$, so $e_3=-\dfrac{2}{3}$.
The next identity is $p_4=e_1p_3-e_2p_2+e_3p_1$, yielding
\[
p_4=3\cdot7-2\cdot5-\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot3=\boxed9.
\]
