By place value,
\[\text{RABBIT}+\text{RIBBIT}=200000R+10000(A+I)+2200B+20I+2T.\]
The letters R, A, B, I, T are five different digits.

With $R\ge2$ the sum is at least $400000$, while $R=1$ keeps it below $200000+10000\cdot17+2200\cdot9+20\cdot9+2\cdot9<400000$. So $R=1$.

Next, $A$ and $I$ are different digits other than $1$, so $A+I\ge0+2=2$. If $A+I\ge3$, the sum is at least $230000$. If $A+I=2$, then $\{A,I\}=\{0,2\}$, and $B,T\ge3$ since the digits $0,1,2$ are used. The term $2200B$ is smallest for $B=3$; then $20I$ is smallest for $I=0$, $A=2$; and then $T\ge4$, so $2T\ge8$. The minimum in this case is
\[220000+6600+0+8=226608<230000.\]
It is attained by $123304+103304=\boxed{226608}$.
