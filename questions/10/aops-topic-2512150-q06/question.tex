An equilateral triangle and a square have equal areas. Let the side length of the triangle be $t$ and the side length of the square be $s$. Let $n$ be the smallest positive integer such that $\left(\dfrac{s}{t}\right)^n$ is a rational number, and write $\left(\dfrac{s}{t}\right)^n=\dfrac pq$ with $p$ and $q$ relatively prime positive integers. Find $n+p+q$.
