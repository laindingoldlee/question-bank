\textbf{(a)} The mixture is worth Rs $15$ per kg exactly when $8x_1+10x_2+16x_3+18x_4=15\left(x_1+x_2+x_3+x_4\right)$, i.e. $-7x_1-5x_2+x_3+3x_4=0$, which is $7x_1+5x_2=x_3+3x_4$. This one equation in four unknowns has many solutions: for example $3:1:5:7$ ($21+5=5+21$) and $1:3:7:5$ ($7+15=7+15$) both work.

\textbf{(b)} With $x_1=x_2=p$ and $x_3=x_4=q$ the condition reads $12p=4q$, so $q=3p$ and the proportion is $\boxed{1:1:3:3}$. (Check: $\dfrac{8+10+48+54}{8}=15$.)
