Scale the board to length $1$; the pieces $(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)$ are then uniformly distributed on the set $x_i\ge0$, $\sum x_i=1$, and we want all $x_i\le\dfrac{8}{30}$. For uniform pieces, the probability that a given set of $k$ pieces all exceed $t$ is $(1-kt)^3$ when $kt<1$. By inclusion--exclusion with $t=\dfrac{8}{30}$:
\[P=1-4\left(\dfrac{22}{30}\right)^3+6\left(\dfrac{14}{30}\right)^3-4\left(\dfrac{6}{30}\right)^3=\dfrac{3375-5324+2058-108}{3375}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{3375}}.\]
