The digit $8$ must be smaller than each base, so $b\ge9$ and $c\ge9$. The equation says $8b+5=5c+8$, i.e.\ $8b-3=5c$. So $8b\equiv3\pmod5$, i.e.\ $3b\equiv3$, i.e.\ $b\equiv1\pmod5$. The smallest $b\ge9$ with $b\equiv1\pmod5$ is $b=11$, and then $5c=85$, $c=17\ge9$. Check: $85_{11}=93=58_{17}$. The answer is $\boxed{11}$.
