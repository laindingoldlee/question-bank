For two relatively prime positive integers $a$ and $b$, the largest integer that cannot be written as $ax+by$ with nonnegative integers $x,y$ is $ab-a-b$ (the Chicken McNugget theorem). For three or more numbers there is no comparably simple general formula, but particular cases can still be solved. Find the largest integer that cannot be written as $6x+10y+15z$ with nonnegative integers $x$, $y$, $z$.
