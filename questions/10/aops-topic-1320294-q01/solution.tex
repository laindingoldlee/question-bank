Only the primes $2$ and $5$ need appear in $x$ (any other prime would have to appear to an exponent divisible by both $3$ and $2$, which only makes $x$ larger). Write $x=2^a5^b$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $4x=2^{a+2}5^b$ is a cube: $a+2\equiv0$ and $b\equiv0\pmod3$.
\item $5x=2^a5^{b+1}$ is a square: $a$ and $b+1$ are even.
\end{itemize}
The smallest $a$ that is even with $a\equiv1\pmod3$ is $a=4$; the smallest $b$ that is odd with $b\equiv0\pmod3$ is $b=3$. So $x=2^4\cdot5^3=\boxed{2000}$. Check: $4x=8000=20^3$ and $5x=10000=100^2$.
