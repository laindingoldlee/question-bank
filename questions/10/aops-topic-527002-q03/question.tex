There are $169$ lamps, each with an on/off switch. A remote control changes exactly $19$ switches at once (each time it is used you may choose which $19$ switches are changed).
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Some lamps are on at the start. Show that, whatever the starting position, you can turn all the lamps off with the remote control.
\item[(b)] If all lamps are on at the start, what is the least number of uses needed to turn all lamps off?
\end{enumerate}
