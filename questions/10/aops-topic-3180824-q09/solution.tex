Set $A=(0,0),B=(1,0),D=(0,1)$ and $E=(t,t)$, where $t=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}$. The vectors from $E$ to $B,D$ have dot product $-2t(1-t)$ and equal squared length $1-2t+2t^2$. Thus the cosine of the angle is $\dfrac{1-\sqrt2}{2-\sqrt2}=-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$, giving $\boxed{135^\circ}$.

\end{asy}
