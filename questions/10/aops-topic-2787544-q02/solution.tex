Let sunrise be $t$ hours before noon. Before meeting, Alice covered in $t$ hours what Bob covers after the meeting in $9$ hours; Bob covered in $t$ hours what Alice covers in $4$ hours. So $\dfrac{v_A}{v_B}=\dfrac9t=\dfrac t4$, giving $t^2=36$, $t=6$. Sunrise was at $\boxed{6\text{ a.m.}}$
