Driving toward each other, they close the gap at $60+30=90$ miles per hour. In $10$ minutes, that is $\dfrac{1}{6}$ of an hour, they close
\[90\cdot\dfrac{1}{6}=\boxed{15\text{ miles}},\]
which was their starting distance. (Jack drives $10$ miles and Joe $5$ miles.)
