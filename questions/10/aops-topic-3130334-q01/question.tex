A player starts with 10 health. Every 10 seconds, independently, an attack occurs with probability $\dfrac{1}{36}$. An attack removes either 5 or 10 health with equal probabilities. There is no healing or other damage. Find the probability of surviving a fortnight, meaning health remains strictly positive.
