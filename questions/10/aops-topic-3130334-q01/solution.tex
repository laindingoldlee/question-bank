A fortnight has $14\cdot24\cdot60\cdot6=120960$ intervals; write this number as $N$. In each interval the probabilities of no damage,5 damage,10 damage are $\dfrac{35}{36},\dfrac{1}{72},\dfrac{1}{72}$. Survival requires no 10-damage attack and at most one 5-damage attack. These disjoint cases give $\boxed{(\dfrac{35}{36})^{120960}+\dfrac{120960}{72}(\dfrac{35}{36})^{120959}}$.
