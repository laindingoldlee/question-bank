Add $21$ and factor: $(x-7)(y-3)=98$. Both factors have the same sign. They cannot both be negative: $1\le x\le6,1\le y\le2$ would make their product at most $6\cdot2=12$. Thus use the six positive divisors $1,2,7,14,49,98$ as $x-7$. The solutions are $\boxed{(8,101),(9,52),(14,17),(21,10),(56,5),(105,4)}$, six in total.
