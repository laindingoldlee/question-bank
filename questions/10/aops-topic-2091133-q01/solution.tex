Number the starting positions in the hand $1$ (top) to $10$ (bottom) and follow the positions, not the cards.
\begin{itemize}
\item First pass: the cards in positions $1,3,5,7,9$ are dealt, and $2,4,6,8,10$ are moved to the bottom in that order. The hand is now $2,4,6,8,10$.
\item Deal $2$, move $4$ (hand $6,8,10,4$); deal $6$, move $8$ (hand $10,4,8$); deal $10$, move $4$ (hand $8,4$); deal $8$, move $4$; deal $4$.
\end{itemize}
So the cards are dealt from the positions
\[1,3,5,7,9,2,6,10,8,4\]
in that order. To deal ace, $2$, \dots, $10$, the ace must be in position $1$, the $2$ in position $3$, the $3$ in position $5$, and so on, the $9$ in position $8$ and the $10$ in position $4$. Reading positions $1$ to $10$, the starting order from top to bottom is
\[\boxed{\text{A},\ 6,\ 2,\ 10,\ 3,\ 7,\ 4,\ 9,\ 5,\ 8}.\]
(Check by dealing: A, then $6$ goes under; $2$, then $10$ under; $3$, $7$ under; $4$, $9$ under; $5$, $8$ under; the hand is now $6,10,7,9,8$, and the process deals $6,7,8$ and then $9,10$.)
