Take the ace (counted as $1$) through $10$ of one suit and hold them face down in a pile. Repeat the following until all the cards are on the table: deal the top card face up onto the table (on top of the cards already dealt), then move the next top card to the bottom of the pile in your hand. In what order, from top to bottom, must the cards be in your hand at the start so that they are dealt in the order ace, $2$, $3$, \dots, $10$ (so that the $10$ ends up on top)?
