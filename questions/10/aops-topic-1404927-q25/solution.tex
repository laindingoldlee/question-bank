With $m$ problems, a paper is described (for this purpose) by the pair $(p,q)$ of numbers of ``$+$'' and ``$-$'' marks, where $p,q\ge0$ and $p+q\le m$. The number of such pairs is
\[
\sum_{s=0}^{m}(s+1)=\dfrac{(m+1)(m+2)}{2}.
\]
The $55$ papers need $55$ different pairs, so $\dfrac{(m+1)(m+2)}{2}\ge55$. For $m=8$ this is $45<55$, and for $m=9$ it is exactly $55$; so $m=9$ is the least, and it works (give the $55$ children the $55$ different pairs). The answer is $\boxed{\text{(B) } 9}$.
