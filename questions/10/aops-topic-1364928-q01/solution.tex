The region $|x|+|y|\le4$ is the square (diamond) with vertices $(\pm4,0)$ and $(0,\pm4)$. The condition $y\le0$ keeps its lower half, the triangle with vertices $(-4,0)$, $(4,0)$ and $(0,-4)$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
fill((-4,0)--(4,0)--(0,-4)--cycle,lightblue);
draw((-4,0)--(0,4)--(4,0)--(0,-4)--cycle,gray+dashed);
draw((-4,0)--(4,0)--(0,-4)--cycle,blue+linewidth(0.9));
draw((-5.2,0)--(5.2,0),Arrow(TeXHead)); draw((0,-5.2)--(0,5.2),Arrow(TeXHead));
label("$x$",(5.2,0),E); label("$y$",(0,5.2),N);
label("$-4$",(-4,0),NW); label("$4$",(4,0),NE); label("$-4$",(0,-4),SW); label("$4$",(0,4),NE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

That triangle has base $8$ and height $4$, so its area is $\dfrac{8\cdot4}{2}=\boxed{16}$ square units.
