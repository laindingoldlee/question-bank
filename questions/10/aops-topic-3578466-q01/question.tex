Mrs. Lee promises that every student scoring at least $90$ receives an A. Which of the following outcomes are consistent with her promise, and how many are possible?
\begin{enumerate}\item Lauren scores $80$ and receives an A.\item Lauren scores $90$ and receives an A.\item Lauren scores $80$ and does not receive an A.\item Lauren scores $90$ and does not receive an A.\end{enumerate}
