A \(5\times8\) board is filled with real numbers, one number in each cell. In every row, all eight numbers are distinct. Moreover, for each row, exactly three numbers appearing in that row do not appear in any other row. What is the maximum possible number of distinct numbers on the board?



\[
\textbf{(A)}\ 20
\qquad
\textbf{(B)}\ 23
\qquad
\textbf{(C)}\ 25
\qquad
\textbf{(D)}\ 27
\qquad
\textbf{(E)}\ 30
\]
