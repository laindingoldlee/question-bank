A product of two nonzero integers is negative exactly when one is negative and the other positive. The set has $3$ negative and $2$ positive numbers, so there are $3\cdot2=6$ such pairs out of $\dbinom52=10$ pairs. The probability is $\dfrac{6}{10}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{5}}$.
