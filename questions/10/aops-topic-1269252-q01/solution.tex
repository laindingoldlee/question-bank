We have $\dfrac{97}{36}=2+\dfrac{25}{36}\approx 2.694$ and $\dfrac{96}{35}=2+\dfrac{26}{35}\approx2.743$, so we need $\dfrac{p}{q}=2+\dfrac{r}{q}$ with $0.694<\dfrac {r}{q}<0.743$.
Checking denominators in turn: $q=1,2,3,4,5,6$ give the nearest candidates $\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{2}{3},\dfrac{3}{4},\dfrac{3}{5},\dfrac{4}{5},\dfrac{4}{6},\dfrac{5}{6}$, none of which lies in the interval ($\dfrac{2}{3}\approx0.667$ is too small and $\dfrac{3}{4}=0.75$ too large). For $q=7$, $\dfrac{5}{7}\approx0.714$ works. Hence the answer is
\[2+\dfrac{5}{7}=\boxed{\dfrac{19}{7}}.\]
