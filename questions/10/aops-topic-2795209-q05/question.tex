A $12$-hour digital clock shows hours (one or two digits, $1$ to $12$), minutes (two digits) and seconds (two digits). A \emph{binary time} is a displayed time using only the digits $0$ and $1$, such as $1\!:\!11\!:\!11$ or $10\!:\!01\!:\!10$. How many binary times occur from 12:00:00 AM up to, but not including, 12:00:00 PM?
