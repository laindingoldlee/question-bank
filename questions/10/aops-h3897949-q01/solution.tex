For tangent circles of radii $r,R$, the distance between their tangency points on the axis is $2\sqrt{rR}$. If a circle of radius $t$ is inserted between them, $\sqrt{rR}=\sqrt{rt}+\sqrt{Rt}$, so $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt t}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt r}+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt R}$. Assign each circle this reciprocal-square-root weight. The endpoint-weight sum is $S=\dfrac{1}{60}+\dfrac{1}{61}=\dfrac{121}{3660}$. If $T_k$ is the total after stage $k$, the new weights sum to $2T_{k-1}-S$, since internal weights occur in two gaps and endpoints in one. Thus $T_k=3T_{k-1}-S$, $T_0=S$, giving $T_k=\dfrac{3^k+1}{2}S$. At $k=5$, the answer is $122S=\boxed{\dfrac{7381}{1830}}$.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
real r=1,R=3721/3600.0,t=1/(1/sqrt(r)+1/sqrt(R))^2;pair A=(0,r),B=(2*sqrt(r*R),R),C=(2*sqrt(r*t),t);draw((-1.3,0)--(3.5,0));draw(circle(A,r));draw(circle(B,R));draw(circle(C,t),blue);draw(A--B,dashed);draw(A--C--B,dashed);label("$r$",A);label("$R$",B);label("$t$",C);label("$x$-axis",(3.3,0),S);
\end{asy}
