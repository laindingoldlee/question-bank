The pizza toppings available are pesto, pepperoni, peppers and pineapple (a pizza may have any subset, including none), and the desserts are pumpkin pie, pretzels and popsicles. Three animals each choose a pizza and a dessert uniformly at random, independently. What is the probability that at least two choose the same pizza or at least two choose the same dessert (or both)?
