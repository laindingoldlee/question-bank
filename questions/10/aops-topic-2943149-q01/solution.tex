There are $16$ pizzas and $3$ desserts. Use the complement: all three pizzas different and all three desserts different, with probability
\[\dfrac{16\cdot15\cdot14}{16^3}\cdot\dfrac{3\cdot2\cdot1}{3^3}=\dfrac{105}{128}\cdot\dfrac29=\dfrac{35}{192}.\]
The answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{157}{192}}$.
