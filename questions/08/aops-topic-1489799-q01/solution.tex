Let Donkey Kong be $d$ meters from the finish. Then the gap is $3d$, and $3d+d=140$, so $d=35$. In the same time Bowser Jr.\ covers $140$ meters and Donkey Kong $35$ meters, so the ratio of their speeds is
\[
140:35=\boxed{4:1}.
\]
