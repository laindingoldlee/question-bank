Towers of exponents are evaluated from the top down: $a^{b^c}$ means $a^{\left(b^c\right)}$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that $\left(\left(x^a\right)^b\right)^c=x^{abc}$ for every real $x>0$ and all positive integers $a,b,c$.
\item[(b)] A student claims that a tower behaves in the same way: $x^{a^{b^c}}=x^{abc}$. Give a counterexample.
\item[(c)] Find all triples of positive integers $(a,b,c)$ for which $x^{a^{b^c}}=x^{abc}$ holds for every $x>0$.
\end{enumerate}
