Triangle $ACD$ has $\angle CAD=55^\circ$, so the arc $CD$ not containing $A$ has measure 110 degrees. If $B$ were on that arc, the arc $BD$ not containing $C$ would be strictly shorter than 110 degrees, forcing $\angle BCD<55^\circ$, a contradiction. Thus $B$ lies on the other arc, and $\angle CBD$ intercepts the 110-degree arc. Therefore $\boxed{55^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=3*dir(190),B=3*dir(240),C=3*dir(110),D=3*dir(0);draw(circle((0,0),3));draw(A--C--D--A);draw(B--C);draw(B--D);label("$A$",A,W);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,N);label("$D$",D,E);
\end{asy}
