Multiplication by $a_n$ and collection gives $(a_n+1)a_{n+1}=3(a_n+1)(a_n+2)$. Starting at $6$, the resulting positive recurrence is $a_{n+1}=3a_n+6$, so cancellation is always valid. Hence $a_n+3=3^n(a_0+3)=3^{n+2}$. Since $3^{11}=177147<202403<531441=3^{12}$, the first index is $m=10$ and $p=531441-3=531438$. Thus $\boxed{m+p=531448}$, choice D.
