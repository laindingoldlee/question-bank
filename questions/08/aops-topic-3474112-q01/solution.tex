Substitute $z=x^{\dfrac{1}{2}}y^{\dfrac{-1}{2}}$ to obtain $\dfrac{\sqrt z}{\sqrt x}=x^{\dfrac{-1}{4}}y^{\dfrac{-1}{4}}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{\sqrt[4]{xy}}}$. Positivity makes every denominator and radical valid.
