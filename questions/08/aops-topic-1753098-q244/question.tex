When $64$ unit cubes are glued together to form a $4\times4\times4$ cube, there are $76$ distinct lines that pass through the centres of exactly four unit cubes. If $1000$ unit cubes are glued together to form a $10\times10\times10$ cube, how many distinct lines pass through the centres of ten unit cubes?
