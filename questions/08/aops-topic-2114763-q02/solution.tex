The first equation says $y=\dfrac{3x}{2}$. Substituting into the second,
\[x-\dfrac{3x}{2}=2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad -\dfrac{x}{2}=2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x=-4,\]
and then $y=\dfrac{3\cdot(-4)}{2}=-6$. The solution is $\boxed{(x,y)=(-4,-6)}$.

(Check: $-2+(-4)=-6$ and $-4-(-6)=2$.)
