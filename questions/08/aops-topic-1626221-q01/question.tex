A student argues: ``The angles of a $45^\circ$-$45^\circ$-$90^\circ$ triangle are in the ratio $45:45:90=1:1:2$, so its sides should be in the ratio $1:1:2$, not $1:1:\sqrt2$.''
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the hypotenuse of an isosceles right triangle whose legs have length $1$.
\item[(b)] Show that no triangle has sides in the ratio $1:1:2$, and explain the error in the argument.
\end{enumerate}
