There are six available primes and seven composites. For fixed face positions, choose which $k$ of the three opposite pairs receive primes, then assign distinct labels: $\binom3k(6)_{2k}(7)_{6-2k}$, where $(n)_j=n(n-1)\cdots(n-j+1)$. Every rotational orbit has 24 members because all labels are distinct. Thus the answer is $\dfrac{1}{24}\sum_{k=0}^3\binom3k(6)_{2k}(7)_{6-2k}=\boxed{5280}$. The sum terms are 5040,75600,45360,720, totaling 126720.
