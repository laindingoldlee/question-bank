There are $2^4=16$ equally likely outcomes. Two heads and two tails occur when we choose which $2$ of the $4$ coins show heads, in $\dbinom42=6$ ways. The probability is
\[
\dfrac{6}{16}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{8}}.
\]
