Fido's leash is tied to a stake at the centre of his yard, which is in the shape of a regular hexagon. His leash is exactly long enough to reach the midpoint of each side of the yard. If the fraction of the area of the yard that he is able to reach is expressed in simplest radical form as $\dfrac{\sqrt a}{b}\pi$, what is the value of the product $ab$?
