The fraction \(\dfrac{22}{7}\) is a well-known approximation to \(\pi\). Find the fraction \(\dfrac {p}{q}\) (with \(q\) a positive integer) with the smallest denominator such that
\[
\left|\pi-\dfrac {p}{q}\right|<\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot10^{-6}.
\]
