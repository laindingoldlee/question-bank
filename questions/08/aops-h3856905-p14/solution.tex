Scale the square to $A=(0,0),B=(1,0),C=(1,1),D=(0,1)$. Then $M=(\dfrac{1}{2},0)$, $N=(1,\dfrac{1}{2})$, and the inward equilateral vertex is $X=(\dfrac{3-\sqrt3}{4},\dfrac{1+\sqrt3}{4})$. Taking the dot product of $M-X$ and $C-X$ gives $\cos\angle MXC=\dfrac{1-\sqrt3}{2\sqrt{8-2\sqrt3}}$. Hence the exact answer is $\boxed{\arccos\!\left(\dfrac{1-\sqrt3}{2\sqrt{8-2\sqrt3}}\right)}$. The other equilateral vertex lies outside the square.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

\end{asy}
