Let $A=(-1,0,0),B=(1,0,0),C=(0,-1,h),D=(0,1,h)$. These coordinates realize the required edges when $2+h^2=100$, so $h=7\sqrt2$. Conversely, equal distances from $C,D$ to $A,B$ place them in the perpendicular bisector plane; their common distance from the midpoint of $AB$ and their separation 2 imply their midpoint lies on a line perpendicular to $CD$, yielding exactly this configuration up to rigid motion. Its volume is $\dfrac{1}{6}|\det(B-A,C-A,D-A)|=\dfrac{2h}{3}=\boxed{\dfrac{14\sqrt2}{3}}$.
