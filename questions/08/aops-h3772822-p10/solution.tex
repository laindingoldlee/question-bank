Let the third cyclist\textquotesingle s speed be $v>12$. At his start, the second is $10$ km ahead and the first $24$ km ahead. Catch-up times are $\dfrac{10}{v-10}$ and $\dfrac{24}{v-12}$. Their difference is $2$, giving $\dfrac{24}{v-12}-\dfrac{10}{v-10}=2$. Simplifying yields $(v-20)(v-9)=0$. The required $v>12$ is $\boxed{20\text{ km per hour}}$.
