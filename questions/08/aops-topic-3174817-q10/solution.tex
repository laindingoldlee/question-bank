The first values are $a_1=0,a_2=1,a_3=3$, so indices $1,2$ qualify. Suppose $a_s=s$ with $s\ge3$. Then $a_{s+1}=2s$, and the following low states are $a_{s+2+2t}=s-1-t$ for $0\le t\le s-2$; the intervening states are greater than their indices. The next equality is $a_{3s-1}=3s-1$. These formulas follow directly by applying the two recurrence branches successively. Thus the equality indices are $s_j=\dfrac{5\cdot3^j+1}{2}$ for $j\ge0$, and a complete interval between consecutive equality indices contains $s_j-1$ qualifying indices. Put $T=3^{2019}$, so the upper cutoff is $3T$. The last equality below it is $s_{2019}=\dfrac{5T+1}{2}$, and the next exceeds $3T$. The earlier complete intervals contribute $\sum_{j=0}^{2018}(s_j-1)=\dfrac{5(T-1)}{4}-\dfrac{2019}{2}$. Since $T\equiv3\pmod4$, the final partial interval contributes $\left\lfloor\dfrac{3T-s_{2019}}{2}\right\rfloor=\dfrac{T-3}{4}$. Including the initial two indices gives $K=\dfrac{3T-2019}{2}$, and therefore $\boxed{2K+2=3^{2020}-2017}$.
