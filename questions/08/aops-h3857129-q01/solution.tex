Units give $2S=S$, so $S=0$ with no carry. Let the tens carry be $c$. The hundreds equation is $O+A+c=A+10d$, hence $O+c=10d$. Since $O\ne0$, this forces $O=9,c=d=1$. Thus $P=D+R+1\le8$, as digit nine is already used. To maximize the result we seek $P=8$, then $A\le7$. With $A=7$, the remaining tens digits cannot be $0,7,8,9$, so $G,T\le6$. They are distinct and must sum to at least ten; their maximum sum is eleven. Thus $L\le1$. The bound $8710$ is attained by $3960+4750=8710$, with all eight letter digits distinct. Hence $\boxed{8710}$.
