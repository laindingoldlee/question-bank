The LCM is $2^2\cdot3^2\cdot5^3\cdot11$. Since $a,b$ are coprime, each prime power belongs wholly to one. The factor $3^2$ must belong to $b$, while $2^2$ and $5^3$ must belong to $a$. The factor $11$ can go to either. Thus $(a,b)$ is $(500,99)$ or $(5500,9)$; the smaller sum is $\boxed{599}$.
