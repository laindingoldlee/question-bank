If the side lengths are $u,v,w$, then $u+v+w=\dfrac{80}{4}=20$, $uv+vw+wu=\dfrac{248}{2}=124$, and $uvw=240$. Therefore the polynomial is $x^3-20x^2+124x-240$. The data are consistent: its roots are $4,6,10$.
Factor $20=2^2\cdot5$, $124=2^2\cdot31$ and $240=2^4\cdot3\cdot5$. The least common multiple is $2^4\cdot3\cdot5\cdot31=7440$, and the greatest common divisor including $1$ is $1$. The answer is $\boxed{7439}$.
