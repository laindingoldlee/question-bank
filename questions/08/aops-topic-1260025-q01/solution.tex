Let $t$ be the number with all three pets. The fish owners split as only fish ($12$), fish and cat only ($2$), fish and dog only ($3$) and all three ($t$), so $12+2+3+t=20$ and $t=3$.

Then the dog owners consist of $19$ (dog and cat only), $3$ (dog and fish only), $3$ (all three) and $50-25=25$ with only a dog; the cat owners consist of $19+2+3=24$ and $40-24=16$ with only a cat. The number with at least one pet is
\[25+16+12+19+2+3+3=80,\]
so $\boxed{20}$ students had none of the pets.
