If $r=\sqrt2+\sqrt3$ were rational, squaring would give $\sqrt6=\dfrac{r^2-5}{2}$ rational. But if $\sqrt6=\dfrac{a}{b}$ in lowest terms, then $a^2=6b^2$ forces $a$ even. Writing $a=2c$ gives $2c^2=3b^2$, forcing $b$ even as well, a contradiction. Thus $\sqrt6$, and consequently $\sqrt2+\sqrt3$, is irrational.
