Let $f(x)=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x+1}+3}$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the domain and range of $f$.
\item[(b)] A student multiplies the numerator and denominator by $\sqrt{x+1}-3$ to get $f(x)=\dfrac{\sqrt{x+1}-3}{x-8}$ and concludes that $8$ is not in the domain. But $f(8)=\dfrac{1}{6}$. Explain the error.
\end{enumerate}
