A student claims: ``For any positive integers $a$ and $b$ with $a>b$, $a^2-b^2=a+b$,'' and gives the examples $3^2-2^2=3+2$ and $21^2-20^2=21+20$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Give a counterexample to the claim.
\item[(b)] Find exactly which pairs of positive integers $a>b$ satisfy $a^2-b^2=a+b$.
\end{enumerate}
