Let $P$ be any point inside a square $ABCD$ of side $s$. Let $G_1,G_2,G_3,G_4$ be the centroids of triangles $APB$, $BPC$, $CPD$ and $DPA$. Prove that the area of quadrilateral $G_1G_2G_3G_4$ does not depend on $P$, and find it.
