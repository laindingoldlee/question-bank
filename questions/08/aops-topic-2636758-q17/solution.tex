By the rational root theorem try small divisors: $x=3$ gives $243-27-144-48-24=0$ and $x=-2$ gives $48+8-64+32-24=0$. Dividing by $(x-3)(x+2)=x^2-x-6$,
\[3x^4-x^3-16x^2-16x-24=\left(x^2-x-6\right)\left(3x^2+2x+4\right).\]
The quadratic factor has roots $\dfrac{-2\pm\sqrt{4-48}}{6}=\dfrac{-1\pm i\sqrt{11}}{3}$. The roots are
\[\boxed{3,\ -2,\ \dfrac{-1+i\sqrt{11}}{3},\ \dfrac{-1-i\sqrt{11}}{3}}.\]
