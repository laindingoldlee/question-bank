Represent a solution by $12$ stars and $2$ bars in a row: the stars before the first bar count $x$, those between the bars $y$, and those after the second bar $z$. Each arrangement of $12$ stars and $2$ bars gives exactly one solution and conversely, so the number of solutions is
\[
\dbinom{14}{2}=\boxed{91}.
\]
(Check by summing over $x$: for each $x$ from $0$ to $12$ there are $13-x$ choices of $y$, and $13+12+\cdots+1=91$.)
