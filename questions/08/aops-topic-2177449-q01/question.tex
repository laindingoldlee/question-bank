In base $31$ the digits are $0,1,\ldots,9$ followed by the letters $a,b,c,\ldots,u$, which stand for $10,11,12,\ldots,30$. Find the sum of the digits of the base-$31$ numeral $42gd76jm5ad_{31}$, and write this sum in hexadecimal (base $16$).
