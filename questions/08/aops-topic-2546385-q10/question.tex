An ordering of the cards $1,2,\dots,10$ in a row is called \emph{non-plummeting} if every card is followed (if at all) by a card whose number is at least $1$ less than its own; that is, if a card shows $x$, the next card shows at least $x-1$. For example $3,2$ and $3,4$ are allowed, but $3,1$ is not. Jacqueline arranges the $10$ cards in a non-plummeting order chosen uniformly at random from all such orders. What is the probability that the cards are in increasing or decreasing order?
