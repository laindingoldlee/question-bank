The exterior cube volume is $234+109=343$, so its side is 7. The cavity dimensions are $7-2t,7-2t,7-t$, giving $(7-2t)^2(7-t)=234$. At $t=\dfrac{1}{2}$, these are 6,6,6.5 and the volume is 234. On $0<t<3.5$, all three positive factors decrease strictly with $t$, so this solution is unique. Thus $\boxed{t=\dfrac{1}{2}\text{ cm}}$.
