Four lights in a row are numbered $1$ to $4$ from left to right, and all are initially on. Every second the lights change as follows.
\begin{itemize}
\item If $0$ or $1$ lights are on, the rightmost light that is off is turned on.
\item If $3$ or $4$ lights are on, the leftmost light that is on is turned off.
\item If exactly $2$ lights are on: if the number of lights on has just increased, the other two lights are turned on; if it has just decreased, the two lights that are on are turned off.
\end{itemize}
After the $2018$th second, which lights are on?

(a) $1,2$\quad (b) $3,4$\quad (c) $1,3$\quad (d) $2,4$\quad (e) All lights are off.
