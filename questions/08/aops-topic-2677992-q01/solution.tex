Selling at a $40\%$ loss means the selling price is $0.6$ of the cost, so $32$ toffees cost $\dfrac1{0.6}=\dfrac53$ rupees. For a $20\%$ gain he must sell at $1.2$ times the cost, so Rs $1$ must buy toffees costing $\dfrac{1}{1.2}=\dfrac56$ rupees. That is $\dfrac{5}{6}\div\dfrac{5}{3}\cdot32=\boxed{16}$ toffees.
