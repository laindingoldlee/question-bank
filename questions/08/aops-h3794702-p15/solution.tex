An equilateral triangular grid with $n$ unit edges per side has $n^2$ small triangles. Thus $n^2=1024$, giving $n=32$. In each of the three edge directions, there are $1+2+\cdots+n=\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$ toothpicks. The total is $3\cdot\dfrac{32\cdot33}{2}=\boxed{1584}$.
