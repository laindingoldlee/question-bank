The domain is $kz\ge0$, because $k^7z^7=(kz)^7$. Extract even powers:\[\sqrt{8k^7z^7}=\sqrt{4k^6z^6\cdot2kz}=2|k|^3|z|^3\sqrt{2kz}.\]Thus the result is $\boxed{-6k^5|k|^3|z|^3\sqrt{2kz}}$. One must not replace $k^5|k|^3$ by $|k|^8$ for negative $k$: the former retains the sign of $k^5$. The formula also gives zero when $kz=0$.
