By symmetry Eric and Raymond have equal chances. Bob wins outright when both others roll at most 3, probability $\dfrac{9}{36}$. If exactly one rolls 4 and the other is at most 3, probability $\dfrac{6}{36}$, he wins the two-player tie with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. If both roll 4, probability $\dfrac{1}{36}$, he wins with probability $\dfrac{1}{3}$. Thus Bob's chance is $\dfrac{9}{36}+\dfrac{3}{36}+\dfrac{1}{108}=\dfrac{37}{108}$. Eric's chance is half the remainder, $\dfrac{71}{216}$, giving $\boxed{p+q=287}$.
