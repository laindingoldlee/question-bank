The comparison of numerical values is $ 2\pi r>\pi r^2 $. Since $\pi r>0 $, division gives $ 2>r $. Thus $\boxed{0<r<2}$ inches. This compares numerical measurements in the stated units, rather than equating quantities of different dimensions.
