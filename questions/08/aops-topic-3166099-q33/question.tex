Do there exist functions $f,g,h:\mathbb Q\to\mathbb Q$ satisfying $f(x+g(y))=g(h(f(x)))+y$ for all rational $x,y$, together with $f(6)=2$, $g(\dfrac{1}{2})=2$, and $h(\dfrac{7}{2})=2$? If so, find all possible $f(2019)$.
