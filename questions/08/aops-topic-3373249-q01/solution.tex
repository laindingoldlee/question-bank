Any real $x$ can be used. For example, $x=0,2$ give $(0,-2),(2,4)$. Since the equation describes a line, any two distinct points calculated this way determine its whole graph. Convenient values are a choice, not a restriction.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw((-1,0)--(3,0),Arrow);draw((0,-3)--(0,6),Arrow);draw((-.3,-2.9)--(2.5,5.5),blue);dot("$(0,-2)$",(0,-2),W);dot("$(2,4)$",(2,4),E);label("$x$",(3,0),S);label("$y$",(0,6),W);
\end{asy}
