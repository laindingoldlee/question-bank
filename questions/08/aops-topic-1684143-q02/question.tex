Points $A$, $P$, $Q$ and $B$ lie on a line in that order. Points $E$ and $C$ lie on the same side of the line with $AE=EQ=10$, $BC=CP=10$ and $AQ=BP=12$. Segments $EQ$ and $CP$ meet at $D$. If the perimeter of the concave pentagon $ABCDE$ is $52$ units, what is its area? Express your answer as a common fraction.
