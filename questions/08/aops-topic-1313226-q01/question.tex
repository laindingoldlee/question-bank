The rectangle with vertices $(0,0)$, $(2,0)$, $(2,3)$, $(0,3)$ is rolled along the $x$-axis by rotating it $90^\circ$ clockwise about $(2,0)$, then about $(5,0)$, then about $(7,0)$, then about $(10,0)$ (each time about its bottom right corner). Find the area of the region between the curve traced by the point that starts at $(1,1)$ and the $x$-axis.
