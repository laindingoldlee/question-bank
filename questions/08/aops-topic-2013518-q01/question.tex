Let $M=\{6,12,18,24,30,\dots\}$ be the positive multiples of $6$ and $N=\{35,70,105,140,175,\dots\}$ the positive multiples of $35$. An integer $k$ is ``picked at random'' from $M\cup N$ in the following sense: for a large number $X$, $k$ is chosen uniformly from the elements of $M\cup N$ that are at most $X$, and we take the limit of the probability as $X\to\infty$. What is the probability that $21$ divides $k$?
