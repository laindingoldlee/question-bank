Since $5\mid a+b+c+d$, $f(a+b+c+d)=0$, and the expression is $f(abcd)-3$. A remainder mod $5$ is at most $4$, so the expression is at most $1$. It equals $1$ for $(a,b,c,d)=(1,2,3,4)$: the sum is $10\equiv0$ and $abcd=24\equiv4\pmod5$. So the greatest value is $\boxed{1}$.
