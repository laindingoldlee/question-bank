A jar contains some marbles. $\dfrac{3}{5}$ of the marbles are red and $\dfrac{2}{5}$ are green. Then $14$ red marbles are removed and replaced by the same number of green marbles. The proportion of red marbles is now $\dfrac{1}{2}$. How many red marbles were in the jar originally?
