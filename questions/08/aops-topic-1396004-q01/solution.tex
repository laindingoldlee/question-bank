Let the jar hold $5t$ marbles, $3t$ red and $2t$ green. The total does not change, and afterwards there are $3t-14$ red marbles, so
\[
\dfrac{3t-14}{5t}=\dfrac{1}{2}\ \Longrightarrow\ 6t-28=5t\ \Longrightarrow\ t=28 .
\]
There were originally $3\cdot28=\boxed{84}$ red marbles (out of $140$); afterwards $70$ of the $140$ are red.
