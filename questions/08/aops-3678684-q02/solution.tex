Since $x\le y\le z$, $\dfrac{5}{6}\le\dfrac{3}{x}$, so $x\le3$; $x=1$ is impossible. For $x=2$, $\dfrac{1}{y}+\dfrac{1}{z}=\dfrac{1}{3}$ gives $(y-3)(z-3)=9$, hence $(y,z)=(4,12),(6,6)$. For $x=3$, $\dfrac{1}{y}+\dfrac{1}{z}=\dfrac{1}{2}$ gives $(y-2)(z-2)=4$, hence $(y,z)=(3,6),(4,4)$. The complete triples are $\boxed{(2,4,12),(2,6,6),(3,3,6),(3,4,4)}$.
