Let $p=\dfrac{2}{3}$, $q=\dfrac{1}{3}$. By the binomial theorem, $(q+p)^n=\sum_k\dbinom nk p^kq^{n-k}$ and $(q-p)^n=\sum_k\dbinom nk(-p)^kq^{n-k}$. Adding, the odd-$k$ terms cancel, so
\[
P(\text{even})=\dfrac{(q+p)^n+(q-p)^n}{2}=\dfrac{1+\left(-\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^{2015}}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{3^{2015}-1}{2\cdot3^{2015}}}.
\]
