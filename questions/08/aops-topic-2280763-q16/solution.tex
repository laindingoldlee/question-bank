Since $BD=BC+CD=26$ and $\angle A=90^\circ$, the Pythagorean theorem gives $AB=\sqrt{26^2-24^2}=10$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0), B=(10,0), D=(0,24), C=(5,12), F=(4.8,0);
pair E=extension(D,F,A,C);
draw(A--B--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); draw(A--C,linewidth(0.9));
draw(D--F,blue);
draw((0,1)--(1,1)--(1,0));
dot(C); dot(E); dot(F);
label("$A$",A,SW); label("$B$",B,SE); label("$D$",D,N); label("$C$",C,NE); label("$E$",E,W); label("$F$",F,S);
label("$24$",(A+D)/2,W); label("$13$",(B+C)/2,NE); label("$13$",(C+D)/2,NE);
\end{asy}

In triangle $ABD$, $DF$ bisects angle $D$, so by the angle bisector theorem
\[\dfrac{AF}{FB}=\dfrac{DA}{DB}=\dfrac{24}{26}=\dfrac{12}{13}.\]
Hence $AF=\dfrac{12}{25}\cdot AB=\dfrac{12}{25}\cdot10=\boxed{\dfrac{24}{5}}$.

(The point $E$ and the median $AC$ are not needed.)
