The two triangles have a common altitude to line $BC$, so their areas are proportional to their bases. Since $PC=\dfrac{4}{5}BC$, $\boxed{[ACP]:[ABC]=4:5}$. 
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
import geometry;
size(260);
pair A=(1,3),B=(0,0),C=(5,0),P=(1,0);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--P);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$P$",P,S);label("$1$",(.5,0),S);label("$4$",(3,0),S);
\end{asy}
