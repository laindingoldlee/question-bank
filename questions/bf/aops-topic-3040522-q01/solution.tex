Let $t$ be the number of hours from sunrise to noon, and let Pat and Dan have speeds $u$ and $v$. The distance Pat covers after meeting equals the distance Dan covered before meeting, so $5u=tv$. Similarly, $8v=tu$. Multiplying and cancelling the positive product $uv$ gives $t^2=40$, hence $t=2\sqrt{10}$. Sunrise was therefore $\boxed{2\sqrt{10}\text{ hours before noon}}$, approximately $5{:}40{:}31$ a.m.
