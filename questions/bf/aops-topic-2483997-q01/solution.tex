Let $u$ be Dave's speed relative to water and $v$ the current speed, with $u>v>0$. The first six-mile leg takes $t=\dfrac{6}{u-v}$ hours. In the water's frame the hat is stationary after it is lost. Dave rows away from it for $t$ more hours and needs exactly $t$ hours to return to it. The total journey therefore takes $3t=8$, so the hat drifts for $2t=\dfrac{16}{3}$ hours over $6$ miles. Thus $v=6\div\dfrac{16}{3}=\boxed{\dfrac{9}{8}\text{ mph}}$.
