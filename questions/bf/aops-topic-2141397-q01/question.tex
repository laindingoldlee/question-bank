The letters
\[\texttt{atharratuatharratuatharratu}\ldots\]
are written in a row, the block of nine letters \texttt{atharratu} repeating forever. Two people, $X$ and $Y$, both start on the first letter \texttt{a}, and each of them takes $2020^5-1$ steps by the same rule: from a vowel (\texttt{a} or \texttt{u}) a person moves $2$ letters forward, and from a consonant $1$ letter forward. On which letter do they end?
