Number the positions $0,1,2,\dots$; the letter at position $j$ depends only on $j$ modulo $9$:
\[\texttt{a}(0)\ \texttt{t}(1)\ \texttt{h}(2)\ \texttt{a}(3)\ \texttt{r}(4)\ \texttt{r}(5)\ \texttt{a}(6)\ \texttt{t}(7)\ \texttt{u}(8).\]
Follow the walk, reducing positions modulo $9$:
\[0\,(\texttt{a})\to2\,(\texttt{h})\to3\,(\texttt{a})\to5\,(\texttt{r})\to6\,(\texttt{a})\to8\,(\texttt{u})\to10\equiv1\,(\texttt{t})\to2\,(\texttt{h})\to\cdots\]
After the first step the walker is at position $2$, and after the seventh step at position $2$ again. So from the first step on, the letters repeat with period $6$: after $s\ge1$ steps the letter is
\[\texttt{h},\ \texttt{a},\ \texttt{r},\ \texttt{a},\ \texttt{u},\ \texttt{t}\qquad\text{for } s\equiv1,2,3,4,5,0\pmod6 .\]
Now $2020\equiv4\pmod6$ and $4^2=16\equiv4\pmod6$, so every power of $2020$ is $\equiv4\pmod6$, and
\[s=2020^5-1\equiv3\pmod6 .\]
Both $X$ and $Y$ follow the same rule, so both end on the letter $\boxed{\texttt{r}}$ (the second \texttt{r} of a block).
