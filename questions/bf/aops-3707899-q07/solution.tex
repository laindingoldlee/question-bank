The first block is $10,12,14,16,18$, or $20$. For the first five blocks, take $c=1,\ldots,9$ and $d=0,2,4,6,8$. For each fixed block with digit sum $s$, count prime values of $s+c+d$. The counts, obtained by testing $c=1,\ldots,9$ in the five columns $d=0,2,4,6,8$, are respectively $15,14,13,13,13$. For block $20$, only $cd=10,12,14,16,18$ are allowed; their digit sums including the two are $3,5,7,9,11$, giving four. Total $15+14+13+13+13+4=\boxed{72}$.
