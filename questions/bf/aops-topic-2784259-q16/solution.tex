Condition on Corey's value $ j $. Albert and Beatrice must both draw below $ j $, with probability $\dfrac{(j-1)(j-2)}{49\cdot48}$, and Dora must draw at most $ j $, with probability $\dfrac{j}{50}$. Thus the desired probability is $\dfrac{\sum_{j=1}^{50}j(j-1)(j-2)}{50^2\cdot49\cdot48}$. Using $ j(j-1)(j-2)=6\binom j3 $ and the hockey-stick identity, the numerator is $ 6\binom{51}{4}$, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{51}{200}}$.
