The photo is $12$ inches on a side, and the frame adds $4$ inches on each side (all lengths in inches):

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
fill(box((0,0),(20,20)),lightgray);fill(box((4,4),(16,16)),white);
draw(box((0,0),(20,20)),linewidth(0.9));draw(box((4,4),(16,16)),linewidth(0.9));
label("photo",(10,10));
draw((4,2.2)--(16,2.2),Arrows(5));label("$12$",(10,2.2),S);
draw((0,-1.5)--(20,-1.5),Arrows(5));label("$20$",(10,-1.5),S);
draw((16,13)--(20,13),Arrows(5));label("$4$",(18,13),N);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

So the outside of the frame is $12+2\cdot4=20$ inches on a side. The enlargement factor is
\[
y=\dfrac{20}{12}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{3}}.
\]
