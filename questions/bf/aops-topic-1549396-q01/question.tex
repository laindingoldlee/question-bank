In a photographer's studio, a frame $4$ inches wide is placed around a square photo $1$ foot on a side. The photo is then enlarged by multiplying each of its dimensions by $y$. The enlargement has the same dimensions as the outside of the frame. Find $y$, expressed as a common fraction.
