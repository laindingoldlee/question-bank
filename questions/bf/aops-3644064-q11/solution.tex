Integral comparison gives $\log(n+1)<H_n\le1+\log n$, where $\log$ is natural. Now $1+879\log10<2025$, while $880\log10>2025$ (for example $2.30258<\log10<2.30259$ proves both). Hence $H_{10^{879}}<2025$ and $H_{10^{880}-1}>2025$. Therefore $10^{879}<N<10^{880}$ and $N$ has $\boxed{880}$ digits. No Euler constant or extremely large exact harmonic sum is needed.
