Stephen lives in a strange world where $1+1=2$, $2+2=3$, $3+3=4$, and so on: adding a number to itself gives the next number. So every number from $2$ on is made from two copies of the number before it, and these are made in turn from smaller numbers, all the way down to $1$s. For example, $3$ needs four $1$s: two $1$s make each of two $2$s, which are added to make $3$.

How many $1$s are needed to make $20$? Find a formula for the number of $1$s needed to make the number $n$.
