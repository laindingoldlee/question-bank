Let $a_n$ be the number of $1$s needed to make $n$. Then $a_1=1$, and since $n$ is made from two copies of $n-1$,
\[
a_n=2a_{n-1}\qquad(n\ge2).
\]
So the numbers of $1$s double at each step: $a_1=1$, $a_2=2$, $a_3=4$, $a_4=8$, and in general $a_n=2^{n-1}$ (by induction: if $a_{n-1}=2^{n-2}$ then $a_n=2\cdot2^{n-2}=2^{n-1}$).

Hence $20$ needs $a_{20}=2^{19}=\boxed{524288}$ ones, and the formula is $a_n=2^{n-1}$.
