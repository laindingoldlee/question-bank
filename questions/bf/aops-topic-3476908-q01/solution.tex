First give Bobette two apples and each other friend one. Eight apples remain. If Joe receives $j$ additional apples, then $j=0,1,2$. For fixed $j$, the other four friends receive $8-j$ apples in $\binom{11-j}{3}$ ways. Thus the answer is $\binom{11}{3}+\binom{10}{3}+\binom{9}{3}=165+120+84=\boxed{369}$.
