After $n$ years the balance is $1000\cdot1.03^n$ dollars. Year by year:
\[1000\to1030\to1060.90\to1092.727\to1125.50881\ldots\]
After $4$ years the balance is $\$1125.51$ (to the nearest cent), and the balance increases every year, so no other $n$ works. The answer is $\boxed{4}$ years.
