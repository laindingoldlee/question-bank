When a scientist dives in salt water to a depth of $9$ feet below the surface, the pressure due to the atmosphere and surrounding water is $18.7$ pounds per square inch. As the scientist descends, the pressure increases linearly. At a depth of $14$ feet, the pressure is $20.9$ pounds per square inch. Find a linear model giving the pressure $p$, in pounds per square inch, at a depth of $d$ feet below the surface.
