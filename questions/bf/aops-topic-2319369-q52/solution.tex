By the triangle inequality the third side $x$ satisfies $x<2+6=8$ and $6<2+x$, i.e. $x>4$ (the third inequality $2<6+x$ is automatic). So $\boxed{4<x<8}$, and every such $x$ works.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0), B=(6,0), C=(1.2,1.5);
draw(A--B,linewidth(0.9)); draw(A--C,linewidth(0.9)); draw(C--B,blue+linewidth(0.9));
label("$6$",(A+B)/2,S); label("$2$",(A+C)/2,NW); label("$x$",(B+C)/2,NE,blue);
\end{asy}
