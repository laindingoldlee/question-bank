Vieta gives $r_1+r_2+r_3=5$ and $r_1r_2+r_2r_3+r_3r_1=-8$. Put $r_3=t$. The two linear relations give $r_2=-5-3t$ and $r_1=10+2t$. Substitution into the pair-product sum yields $-50-35t-7t^2=-8$, so $(t+2)(t+3)=0$. For $t=-2$, the roots are 6, 1,$-2$, giving $a=-r_1r_2r_3=12$. For $t=-3$, they are 4, 4,$-3$, giving $a=48$. Both triples satisfy the relation, so the sum is $\boxed{60}$.
