The two legs are each $7$. Its area is $\dfrac{49}{2}$ and semiperimeter is $7+\dfrac{7\sqrt2}{2}$. Thus $r=\dfrac{K}{s}=\dfrac{7}{2+\sqrt2}=\boxed{7-\dfrac{7\sqrt2}{2}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
real r=7-7sqrt(2)/2;pair A=(0,0),B=(7,0),C=(0,7),I=(r,r);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(circle(I,r),blue);draw(I--(r,0),dashed);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,N);label("$7$",(3.5,0),S);label("$7$",(0,3.5),W);label("$7\sqrt2$",(3.5,3.5),NE);label("$r$",(r,r/2),E);
\end{asy}
