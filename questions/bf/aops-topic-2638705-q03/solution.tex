The condition is $5p+4\equiv8\pmod{11}$, so $5p\equiv4$. Since $5\cdot9\equiv1$, $p\equiv36\equiv3\pmod{11}$. The two-digit possibilities are $\boxed{14,25,36,47,58,69,80,91}$.
