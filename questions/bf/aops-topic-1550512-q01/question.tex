An integer $N$ is formed by writing the integers from $1$ to $50$ in order:
\[
N=123456789101112\ldots484950 .
\]
Some of the digits of $N$ are discarded, and the remaining digits, in their original order, form a new integer whose digit sum is $200$. Let $M$ be the largest integer that can be formed in this way. What are the first ten digits of $M$?
