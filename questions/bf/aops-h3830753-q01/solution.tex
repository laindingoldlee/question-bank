The square has side $10$. Write the additional width and height as $x,y>0$. The two adjacent areas are $10x$ and $10y$, so $x=7y$. The outside perimeter gives $2(20+x+y)=120$, hence $x+y=40$. Therefore $x=35$ and $y=5$.
The lower-right piece is $35$ by $5$. If it is cut into $m>1$ equal horizontal strips, each has height $\dfrac{5}{m}$, a positive integer. Thus $m$ must be the divisor $5$. Each strip is $35$ by $1$, with perimeter $72$, and the total is $\boxed{5\cdot72=360}$.
