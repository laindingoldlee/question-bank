A rectangle of perimeter $120$ is divided by one horizontal and one vertical line into a $2\times2$ array of rectangles. The upper-left piece is a square of area $100$. The upper-right piece has seven times the area of the lower-left piece. The lower-right piece is cut horizontally into more than one congruent rectangle, each with integer side lengths. Find the sum of the perimeters of these smaller rectangles.
