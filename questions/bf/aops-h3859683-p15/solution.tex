Translate the horizontal coordinate so the equation is $y^2=c|x|$, where $c=\dfrac{2024}{2025}$. Write square vertices relative to its center $(u,v)$ as $(u+a,v+b),(u-b,v+a),(u-a,v-b),(u+b,v-a)$, choosing $a,b>0$; a zero coordinate would force two distinct vertices onto the sole point of the curve with $y=0$, impossible. Subtract the equations for opposite vertices. They give $4vb=c(|u+a|-|u-a|)$ and $4va=-c(|u+b|-|u-b|)$. For nonzero $u$ the right sides have opposite signs, impossible; hence $u=v=0$. Now $b^2=ca,a^2=cb$, so $a=b=c$. The square side is $2c=\dfrac{4048}{2025}$, giving $\boxed{6073}$.

\end{asy}
