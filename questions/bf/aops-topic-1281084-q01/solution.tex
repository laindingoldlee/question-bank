Squaring gives $x=4x^2-24x+36$, i.e. $4x^2-25x+36=0$, so
\[
x=\dfrac{25\pm\sqrt{625-576}}{8}=\dfrac{25\pm7}{8},\qquad x=4\text{ or }x=\dfrac{9}{4} .
\]
Check: $x=4$ gives $\sqrt4=2=2\cdot4-6$, a solution. $x=\dfrac{9}{4}$ gives $\sqrt{\dfrac{9}{4}}=\dfrac{3}{2}$ but $2\cdot\dfrac{9}{4}-6=-\dfrac{3}{2}$, so it is extraneous (it solves $-\sqrt x=2x-6$). The only solution is $\boxed{x=4}$.
