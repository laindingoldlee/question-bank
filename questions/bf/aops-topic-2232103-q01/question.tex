Let $p$ be a prime and $a$ an integer not divisible by $p$. The \emph{order} of $a$ modulo $p$ is the smallest positive integer $d$ with $a^d\equiv1\pmod p$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that for every positive integer $m$, $a^m\equiv1\pmod p$ if and only if $d\mid m$.
\item[(b)] Deduce that $d$ divides $\varphi(p)=p-1$, and find the order of $2$ modulo $13$ and modulo $7$.
\end{enumerate}
