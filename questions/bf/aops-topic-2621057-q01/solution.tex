$f$ is defined for all real $x$. $g$ needs $x-4\ge0$, and $\dfrac{f}{g}$ also needs $g(x)\ne0$, i.e. $x\ne4$. So the domain is $x>4$, that is, $\boxed{(4,\infty)}$.
