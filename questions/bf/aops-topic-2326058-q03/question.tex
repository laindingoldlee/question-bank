In a battle between zombies and skeletons, every skeleton was shot by at least one zombie, but no zombie shot every skeleton. Prove that there are two zombies $a$ and $b$ and two skeletons $x$ and $y$ such that $a$ shot $x$ and $b$ shot $y$, but $a$ did not shoot $y$ and $b$ did not shoot $x$.
