The circumradius equals the side 2. The inradius is the altitude of an equilateral triangle of side 2, namely $\sqrt3$. The area difference is $\pi(4-3)=\pi$, so $\boxed{a=1}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair[] p;path q;for(int i=0;i<6;++i){p.push(2*dir(60*i));q=q--p[i];}draw(q--cycle);draw(circle((0,0),2)^^circle((0,0),sqrt(3)));draw((0,0)--p[0]^^(0,0)--(p[0]+p[1])/2);label("$2$",(1,0),S);label("$\sqrt3$",(.65,.5),NW);
\end{asy}
