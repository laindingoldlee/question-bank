Directly proportional to $y$ (with $z,w$ fixed) means $x=c_1y$, where $c_1$ may depend on $z$ and $w$; similarly for $z$ and, inversely, for $w$. A single formula consistent with all three is $x=\dfrac{kyz}{w}$: holding any two variables fixed, it shows the required proportionality in the third. (Conversely, changing $y$, $z$, $w$ one at a time from one triple to another multiplies $x$ by the ratios $\dfrac{y'}{y}$, $\dfrac{z'}{z}$, $\dfrac{w}{w'}$, which gives the same formula.)

From $4=\dfrac{k\cdot8\cdot5}{6}$ we get $k=\dfrac{3}{5}$. Then $x=\dfrac{3}{5}\cdot\dfrac{10\cdot9}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{27}{2}}$.
