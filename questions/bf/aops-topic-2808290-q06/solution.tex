The hypotenuse is $2\sqrt{13}$. The inradius of a right triangle is half the sum of its legs minus its hypotenuse: $r=\dfrac{4+6-2\sqrt{13}}{2}=5-\sqrt{13}$. Hence $\boxed{a+b=18}$.
