Let the original totals be $ A,M $. The new total is $\dfrac{5}{4}A+\dfrac{3}{4}M=\dfrac{9}{10}(A+M)$. Hence $\dfrac{7}{20}A=\dfrac{3}{20}M $, so the ratio is $\boxed{3:7}$.
