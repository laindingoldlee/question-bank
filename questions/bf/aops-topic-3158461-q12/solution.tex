Order the vertex labels $ a_1<\cdots<a_n $, where $ n=2016 $. The increasing chain
\[a_1<a_2<a_1+a_2<a_1+a_3<\cdots<a_1+a_n<a_2+a_n<\cdots<a_{n-1}+a_n\]
contains $ 2n-1 $ distinct values, all present. Label the vertices with $ 1,2,\ldots,n $: the pair sums range from 3 to $ 2n-1 $, and together with the vertices the labels are exactly $ 1,\ldots,2n-1 $. Thus the minimum is $\boxed{4031}$. The polygon merely specifies all pairs.
