Let the rectangle be $ABCD$ with $AB=16$, $BC=12$, and fold triangle $ACD$ over the diagonal $AC$, so that $D$ goes to $D'$. The folded edge $CD'$ crosses side $AB$ at a point $P$, and the overlap is triangle $APC$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),Bv=(16,0),C=(16,12),D=(0,12);
pair Dp=(11.52,-3.36),P=(12.5,0);
fill(A--P--C--cycle,gray(0.8));
draw(A--Bv--C--D--cycle,linewidth(1));
draw(A--C,linewidth(1));
draw(A--Dp--C,dashed);
dot(P);
label("$A$",A,W);label("$B$",Bv,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$D'$",Dp,S);label("$P$",P,NE);
label("$16$",(8,12),N);label("$12$",(0,6),W);label("$12.5$",(6.25,0),S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

By the symmetry of the fold, $\angle PCA=\angle DCA$, and $\angle DCA=\angle CAB$ since $DC\parallel AB$. So $\angle PCA=\angle PAC$ and triangle $APC$ is isosceles with $PA=PC$. Let $PA=PC=t$; then $PB=16-t$ and the right triangle $PBC$ gives
\[
(16-t)^2+12^2=t^2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 400=32t\quad\Longrightarrow\quad t=12.5 .
\]
The overlap triangle $APC$ has base $AP=12.5$ on $AB$ and height $BC=12$, so its area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot12.5\cdot12=\boxed{75}$.
