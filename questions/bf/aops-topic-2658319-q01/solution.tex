Altogether Sally ends up $3+7=10$ miles north and $5$ miles east of $A$. These two displacements are perpendicular, so by the Pythagorean theorem her distance from $A$ is
\[\sqrt{10^2+5^2}=\sqrt{125}=\boxed{5\sqrt5\text{ miles}}\approx11.2\text{ miles}.\]
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),P1=(0,3),P2=(5,3),B=(5,10);
draw((0,3)--(0,10)--B,dotted);
draw(A--P1--P2--B,linewidth(0.9),Arrow(6));
draw(A--B,dashed);
dot(A);dot(B);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("end",B,NE);
label("$3$",(0,1.5),W);label("$5$",(2.5,3),S);label("$7$",(5,6.5),E);
label("$10$",(0,6.5),W);label("$5$",(2.5,10),N);
label("$5\sqrt5$",(2.5,5),NW);
\end{asy}
