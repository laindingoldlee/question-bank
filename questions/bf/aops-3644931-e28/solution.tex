For a quick estimate replace the discrete distribution by independent uniform coefficients on $[-1,1]$. A negative discriminant requires $ac>0$, with probability one half. Conditional on that, $u=|ac|$ has density $-\log u$ on $(0,1)$, and $|b|<2\sqrt u$ has probability $\min(2\sqrt u,1)$. Thus the no-real-root probability is $\dfrac{1}{2}[\int_0^{\dfrac{1}{4}}2\sqrt u(-\log u)\,du+\int_{\dfrac{1}{4}}^1(-\log u)\,du]=\dfrac{31}{72}-\dfrac{\log2}{12}$. The desired estimate is $\boxed{\dfrac{41}{72}+\dfrac{\log2}{12}\approx0.6272}$. The finite integer model differs slightly; zero-leading-coefficient cases must also be specified if an exact probability is desired.
