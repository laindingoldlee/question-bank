Directions of side lines differ by multiples of $\dfrac{360^\circ}{n}$, modulo $180^\circ$. Thus $42n=360k+180n\ell$ for some integers $k,\ell$, implying $60\mid n$ because $42n$ must be divisible by $180$ and, after $n=30t$, the equation forces $t$ even. For $n=60$, sides seven steps apart differ by $7\cdot6^\circ=42^\circ$. Hence $\boxed{60}$ is attainable and minimal.
