Put $u=t^2\ge0$. The equation becomes $u^3-4u+3=0$. Since $u=1$ is a root, $u-1$ is a factor:
\[
u^3-4u+3=(u-1)(u^2+u-3).
\]
The quadratic factor has roots $u=\dfrac{-1\pm\sqrt{13}}{2}$. The root $\dfrac{-1-\sqrt{13}}{2}$ is negative, so it gives no real $t$. The other two values of $u$ are positive:
\begin{itemize}
\item $u=1$ gives $t=\pm1$;
\item $u=\dfrac{\sqrt{13}-1}{2}$ gives $t=\pm\sqrt{\dfrac{\sqrt{13}-1}{2}}$.
\end{itemize}
So the real solutions are
\[
\boxed{t=\pm1\quad\text{and}\quad t=\pm\sqrt{\dfrac{\sqrt{13}-1}{2}}}\qquad\left(\sqrt{\dfrac{\sqrt{13}-1}{2}}\approx1.2522\right).
\]
