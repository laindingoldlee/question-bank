There are $\binom{16}{3}=560$ equally likely occupied-square sets. For a nonattacking placement choose three rows and three columns, in $\binom43^2$ ways, then biject the chosen rows with the chosen columns in $3!$ ways. Thus there are $4^2\cdot6=96$ favorable placements, and the probability is $\dfrac{96}{560}=\boxed{\dfrac{6}{35}}$.
