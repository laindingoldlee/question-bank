The three-digit numbers $100,\dots,999$ are $900$ numbers, contributing $900\cdot3=2700$ digits. The four-digit numbers $1000,\dots,2824$ are $2824-1000+1=1825$ numbers, contributing $1825\cdot4=7300$ digits. In total $n$ has $2700+7300=\boxed{10000}$ digits.
