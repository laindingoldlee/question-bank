Larry travels on the lattice points of a $10\times10$ grid from $(0,0)$ to $(10,10)$, each step going one unit right or one unit up. He must never travel more vertically than horizontally, that is, every point $(x,y)$ on his path satisfies $y\le x$. For $1\le n\le9$, in universe $n$ there is a black hole at $(n,n)$ that he must avoid. Let $k_n$ be the number of paths Larry can take in universe $n$. Find $k_1+k_2+\cdots+k_9$.
