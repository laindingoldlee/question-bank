A probability satisfies $0<\dfrac mn\le1$ (with $m\ge1$). Since $m+n=2021$ is odd, $m\ne n$, so $m<n$, i.e. $1\le m\le1010$. Also $\gcd(m,n)=\gcd(m,2021)=1$ with $2021=43\cdot47$. The $\varphi(2021)=42\cdot46=1932$ residues coprime to $2021$ pair up as $m\leftrightarrow2021-m$, exactly one of each pair being at most $1010$. So there are $\dfrac{1932}2=\boxed{966}$ values.
