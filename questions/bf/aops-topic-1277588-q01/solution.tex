A common point $(a,b)$ would satisfy $b=f(a)$ and $b=f(a)+3$, giving $0=3$. So no such point exists. (Geometrically, the second graph is the first shifted up by $3$, and over each $x$ in the domain the two graphs have exactly one point each, at heights differing by $3$.) $\blacksquare$
