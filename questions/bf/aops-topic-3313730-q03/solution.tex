The thousands digit is 6,7 or 8. For 6 or 8, the units digit has four even choices after excluding the thousands digit; the other positions have 8 and 7 choices. These contribute $2\cdot4\cdot8\cdot7=448$. For thousands digit 7, all five even units digits work, contributing $5\cdot8\cdot7=280$. The total is $\boxed{728}$. The excluded endpoints have repeated zero digits and would not affect the count.
