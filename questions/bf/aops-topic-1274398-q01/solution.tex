Factorise: $1890=2\cdot3^3\cdot5\cdot7$, $1930=2\cdot5\cdot193$, $1970=2\cdot5\cdot197$. So
\[
N=2^3\cdot3^3\cdot5^3\cdot7\cdot193\cdot197,
\]
which has $4\cdot4\cdot4\cdot2\cdot2\cdot2=512$ divisors. Every divisor of $45=3^2\cdot5$ divides $N$, and $45$ has $6$ divisors. The answer is $512-6=\boxed{506}$.
