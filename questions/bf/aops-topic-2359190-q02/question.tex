On average, out of $1000$ coins, one is biased and the other $999$ are fair. A fair coin comes up heads $50\%$ of the time, and a biased coin $80\%$ of the time. You pick a coin at random, toss it $20$ times, and get $20$ heads in a row. Which is closest to the probability that you have the biased coin?
\[\textbf{(A) }1\%\qquad\textbf{(B) }14\%\qquad\textbf{(C) }33\%\qquad\textbf{(D) }67\%\qquad\textbf{(E) }90\%\]
