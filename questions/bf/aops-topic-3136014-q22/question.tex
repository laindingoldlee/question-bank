Two perfectly reflecting walls are rays forming a 45-degree wedge. A point ball starts strictly inside the wedge and travels along a straight line between reflections, obeying equal incidence and reflection angles. It never hits the common vertex. What is the greatest number of wall collisions before it escapes?
