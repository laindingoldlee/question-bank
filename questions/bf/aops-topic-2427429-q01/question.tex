For positive integers $a,b,n$ define an operation $a\mathbin{\fbox{$n$}}b$ by
\[a\mathbin{\fbox{$1$}}b=a^b,\qquad a\mathbin{\fbox{$n$}}b=a\mathbin{\fbox{$n-1$}}\Bigl(a\mathbin{\fbox{$n-1$}}\bigl(\cdots\bigl(a\mathbin{\fbox{$n-1$}}a\bigr)\cdots\bigr)\Bigr)\quad(n>1),\]
where there are $b$ copies of $a$ on the right and the expression is evaluated from right to left. (For example, $a\mathbin{\fbox{$2$}}3=a^{\left(a^a\right)}$.) Find the last two digits of $2\mathbin{\fbox{$2021$}}3$.
