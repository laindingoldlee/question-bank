\textbf{Solution 1 is correct}; the answer is $\dfrac{2}{3}$. Ties have probability $0$, and by symmetry each of the $6$ orderings has probability $\dfrac{1}{6}$; $x$ is least in $2$ of them, so $P(x\text{ least})=\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $P(x\text{ not least})=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{3}}$.

\textbf{The error in Solution 2.} It treats ``$y>x$'' and ``$z>x$'' as independent events, each of probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Each does have probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, but they are not independent: both depend on the same $x$. If $y>x$, then $x$ is probably small, which makes $z>x$ more likely. Conditioning on $x$ correctly,
\[
P(y>x\text{ and }z>x)=\int_0^1(1-x)^2\,dx=\dfrac{1}{3}\ne\dfrac{1}{4} ,
\]
which agrees with Solution 1. $\blacksquare$
