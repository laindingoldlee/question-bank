Rotate and scale so $R=(0,0)$ and $I=(1,0)$. The chord $CE$ is horizontal; its two endpoints are symmetric about the vertical diameter. The inscribed angle $ICE$ makes the minor arc $IE$ have measure $50^\circ$. Up to reflection in the horizontal diameter, $E$ is at polar angle $50^\circ$, and its horizontal partner $C$ at $130^\circ$. The arc $CE$ not containing $I$ therefore has measure $80^\circ$. Its inscribed angle is $\boxed{40^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair R=(0,0),I=dir(0),Ep=dir(50),C=dir(130);draw(unitcircle);draw(I--C--Ep--cycle);draw(R--I,dashed);label("$R$",R,SW);label("$I$",I,E);label("$C$",C,NW);label("$E$",Ep,NE);label("$25^\circ$",C+(.3,-.08));
\end{asy}
