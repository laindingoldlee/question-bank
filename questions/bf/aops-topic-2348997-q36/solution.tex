Each factor equals $\dfrac{2k^2}{2(k+1)^2}=\left(\dfrac{k}{k+1}\right)^2$. The order does not matter, and the product telescopes:
\[\prod_{k=101}^{1009}\left(\dfrac{k}{k+1}\right)^2=\left(\dfrac{101}{1010}\right)^2=\left(\dfrac1{10}\right)^2.\]
The square root is $\boxed{\dfrac1{10}}$.
