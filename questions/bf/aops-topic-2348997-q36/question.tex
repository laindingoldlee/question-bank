Compute
\[\sqrt{\left(\dfrac{101\cdot202}{102\cdot204}\right)\left(\dfrac{1009\cdot2018}{1010\cdot2020}\right)\left(\dfrac{102\cdot204}{103\cdot206}\right)\left(\dfrac{1008\cdot2016}{1009\cdot2018}\right)\cdots\left(\dfrac{554\cdot1108}{555\cdot1110}\right)\left(\dfrac{556\cdot1112}{557\cdot1114}\right)\left(\dfrac{555\cdot1110}{556\cdot1112}\right)},\]
where the factors are $\dfrac{k\cdot2k}{(k+1)\cdot2(k+1)}$ for each $k=101,102,\dots,1009$ exactly once, taken alternately from the low end and the high end. Express your answer as a common fraction.
