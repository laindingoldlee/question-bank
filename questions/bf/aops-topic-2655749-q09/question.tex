A student was supposed to multiply the number $66$ by the repeating decimal $1.\overline{ab}=1.ababab\ldots$, where $a$ and $b$ are digits. He did not notice the notation and just multiplied $66$ by the terminating decimal $1.ab$. Later he found that his answer was $0.5$ less than the correct answer. What is the two-digit number $\underline{a}\,\underline{b}$?
