Let $n$ be the two-digit number $\underline{a}\,\underline{b}$. Then $1.\overline{ab}=1+\dfrac{n}{99}$ and $1.ab=1+\dfrac{n}{100}$, so
\[66\left(\dfrac n{99}-\dfrac n{100}\right)=\dfrac{66n}{9900}=\dfrac{n}{150}=\dfrac12.\]
Hence $n=\boxed{75}$. (Check: $66\cdot1.\overline{75}=66\cdot\dfrac{174}{99}=116$ and $66\cdot1.75=115.5$.)
