Vieta gives $p+q+r=-9$, $pq+qr+rp=21$, $pqr=8$. (a) The numerator after combining over $pqr$ is $p^2+q^2+r^2=81-42=39$, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{39}{8}}$. (b) Use $p^3+q^3+r^3-3pqr=(p+q+r)((p+q+r)^2-3(pq+qr+rp))$. This is $-9(81-63)=\boxed{-162}$.
