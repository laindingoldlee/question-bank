Let $t$ be the probability of tails. Then $1-t=4t^2$, so $4t^2+t-1=0$ and, taking the positive root, $t=\dfrac{\sqrt{17}-1}8$. Thus $a+b+c=17+1+8=26$, answer $\boxed{\textbf{(C)}\ 26}$.
