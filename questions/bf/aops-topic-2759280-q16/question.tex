Richard's unfair coin comes up heads with four times the probability that it comes up tails twice in a row. He flips the coin once, and Vanessa bets that it will show tails. The probability that she wins can be written as $\dfrac{\sqrt a-b}c$, where $a,b,c$ are positive integers and $a$ is not divisible by the square of a prime. What is $a+b+c$?

$\textbf{(A)}\ 20\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ 23\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ 26\qquad\textbf{(D)}\ 29\qquad\textbf{(E)}\ 32$
