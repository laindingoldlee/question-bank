Semicircles are drawn inward on all five sides of a regular pentagon with side length $2$, each with a side as its diameter; no part of any semicircle goes outside the pentagon. The total area of the regions where two semicircles overlap can be written as $\dfrac{p\pi-\sqrt{q+\sqrt{r}}}{s}$, where $p,q,r,s$ are positive integers. Find the remainder when the smallest possible value of $p+q+r+s$ is divided by $420$.
