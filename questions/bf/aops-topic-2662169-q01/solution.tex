Modulo $7$: $5^1\equiv5$, $5^2\equiv4$, $5^3\equiv6\equiv-1$, $5^4\equiv2$, $5^5\equiv3$, $5^6\equiv1$. So the powers of $5$ repeat with period $6$, and $5^n\equiv-1$ exactly when $n\equiv3\pmod6$. The answer is $\boxed{n=6k+3,\ k=0,1,2,\dots}$.
