We count partitions of $10$ in which each part $k$ is used at most $k$ times (so at most one $1$, at most two $2$s, at most three $3$s, and so on). Listing them by largest part:
\begin{itemize}
\item largest part at least $6$: $10$, $9+1$, $8+2$, $7+3$, $7+2+1$, $6+4$, $6+3+1$, $6+2+2$ ($8$ partitions);
\item largest part $5$: $5+5$, $5+4+1$, $5+3+2$, $5+2+2+1$ ($4$);
\item largest part $4$: $4+4+2$, $4+3+3$, $4+3+2+1$ ($3$; for instance $4+4+1+1$ and $4+2+2+2$ are not allowed);
\item largest part $3$: $3+3+3+1$, $3+3+2+2$ ($2$);
\item largest part at most $2$: impossible, since $2+2+1=5<10$.
\end{itemize}
The total is $8+4+3+2=\boxed{17}$.
