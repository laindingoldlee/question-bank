A stamp collector has $1$ stamp worth $1$ cent, $2$ stamps worth $2$ cents each, $3$ stamps worth $3$ cents each, and so on. In how many different ways can he choose stamps with a total value of $10$ cents? (Stamps of the same value are interchangeable, and order does not matter.)
