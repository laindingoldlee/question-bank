Let there be $r$ roses and $b$ bees. The first condition gives $b=r+1$. In the second, $r-1$ roses get two bees each and one rose gets none, so $b=2(r-1)$. Hence $r+1=2r-2$, so $r=3$ and $b=4$: there are $\boxed{4\text{ bees and }3\text{ roses}}$.
