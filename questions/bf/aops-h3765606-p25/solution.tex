The three integers cannot all be positive, since positive integers of sum 3 have product at most 1. Thus they are $-a,-b,c$ for positive $a,b,c$, with $abc=32$ and $c=a+b+3$. Every factor is a power of 2. The value $c$ cannot be 1, so it is even, forcing $a,b$ to have opposite parity. One is therefore 1; write $a=1$, so $c=b+4$. Two powers of 2 differing by 4 must be $4,8$: factoring $2^u(2^{v-u}-1)=4$ proves this. Thus the integers are $-1,-4,8$, and the smallest is $\boxed{-4}$.
