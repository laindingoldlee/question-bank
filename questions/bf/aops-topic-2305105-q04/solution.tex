From one birthday to the next, the day of the week moves forward by $1$ day ($365=52\cdot7+1$), or by $2$ days when a February $29$ falls in between. Between his birthday in $2000$ and his birthday in $2058$ there are $58$ years. The February $29$ dates in between are those of $2004,2008,\dots,2056$; there are $14$ of them, since February $29$, $2000$ came before his birth. The total shift is $58+14=72\equiv2\pmod7$ days, so his birthday in $2058$ is $2$ days after Tuesday: $\boxed{\text{Thursday}}$.

(If he had been born in January or February of $2000$, before February $29$, the shift would be $73\equiv3$ and the answer would be Friday.)
