Let \(ABCD\) be a unit square. Let \(C_1\) be the quarter circle centered at \(A\) with radius \(1\), and \(C_2\) the quarter circle centered at \(B\) with radius \(1\), both lying inside the square. Find the radius of the circle that is internally tangent to \(C_1\), externally tangent to \(C_2\), and tangent to side \(AD\).



\[
\textbf{(A)}\ \dfrac{1}{8}
\qquad
\textbf{(B)}\ \dfrac{1}{5\sqrt2}
\qquad
\textbf{(C)}\ \dfrac{1}{7}
\qquad
\textbf{(D)}\ \dfrac{1}{4\sqrt3}
\qquad
\textbf{(E)}\ \dfrac{1}{6}
\]
