The median is the average of the $2020$th and $2021$st smallest numbers. For $k\le2020$, the number of entries at most $k$ is $k+\lfloor\sqrt k\rfloor$: the numbers $1,\dots,k$ from the first list and the squares $1^2,\dots,\lfloor\sqrt k\rfloor^2$.

Since $44^2=1936$ and $45^2=2025$, for $1936\le k\le2020$ this count is $k+44$, and no new square appears between $1937$ and $2020$. The count is $2019$ at $k=1975$, $2020$ at $k=1976$ and $2021$ at $k=1977$. So the $2020$th and $2021$st smallest entries are $1976$ and $1977$, and the median is
\[\dfrac{1976+1977}{2}=\boxed{1976.5}.\]
