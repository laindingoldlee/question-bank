We check the numbers below $30$ that are not prime.

\textbf{$1$, $4$, $6$.} $1$ is not prime and is not a sum of two primes (each prime is at least $2$). The only sums of two distinct primes up to $7$ are $2+3=5$, $2+5=7$ and $3+5=8$ (already too big), so $4$ and $6$ are not such sums ($4=2+2$ and $6=3+3$ repeat a prime).

\textbf{The other even composites.} $8=3+5$, $10=3+7$, $12=5+7$, $14=3+11$, $16=3+13$, $18=5+13$, $20=3+17$, $22=3+19$, $24=5+19$, $26=3+23$, $28=5+23$.

\textbf{Odd composites.} A sum of two odd primes is even, so an odd number is a sum of two distinct primes only as $2+(n-2)$ with $n-2$ prime:
\[
9=2+7,\quad 15=2+13,\quad 21=2+19,\quad 25=2+23,
\]
but $27-2=25$ is not prime, so $27$ is not such a sum.

The numbers are $\boxed{1,\ 4,\ 6,\ 27}$.
