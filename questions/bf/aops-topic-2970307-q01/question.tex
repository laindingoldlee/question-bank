Triangle $ABC$ has $a=BC=3$, $b=CA=4$, $c=AB=5$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of the segment joining the midpoints of $AC$ and $BC$, and let $N$ be the midpoint of $MC$. Let $P$ be the midpoint of the segment joining the midpoints of $AM$ and $AN$. Find $PC$.
