Take the student with the smallest number: $7$ choices for a partner. Among the remaining $6$, the smallest has $5$ choices, then $3$, then $1$. So there are $7\cdot5\cdot3\cdot1=\boxed{105}$ ways. (Equivalently $\dfrac{8!}{2^4\cdot4!}=105$: order the students, pair them consecutively, and divide by the orders within pairs and of the pairs.)
