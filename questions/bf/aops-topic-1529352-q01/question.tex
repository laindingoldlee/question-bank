For a whole number $a\ge0$ define
\[
f(a)=\sqrt{a+\sqrt{a+\sqrt{a+\cdots}}}
\]
as the limit of the sequence $x_1=\sqrt a$, $x_{k+1}=\sqrt{a+x_k}$. Find the sum of the five smallest whole numbers $a$ for which $f(a)$ is a positive integer.
