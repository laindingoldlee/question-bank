(a) A fair unit bet has expected profit $p\dfrac{r}{s}-(1-p)=0$, hence $p=\dfrac{s}{r+s}$. The first two probabilities are $\dfrac{1}{3},\dfrac{1}{4}$, leaving $\dfrac{5}{12}$ for C. Its fair odds against are $\boxed{7\text{ to }5}$. (b) Bet $20,15,12$ dollars on A,B,C respectively. The cost is $47$ dollars. The total payout is $3(20)=4(15)=5(12)=60$ dollars whichever horse wins, giving guaranteed profit $\boxed{13\text{ dollars}}$. The implied probabilities summing to less than one indicate inconsistent offered odds, not a possibility that no horse wins.
