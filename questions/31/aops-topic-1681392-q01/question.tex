A straight line that does not pass through the common centre of two concentric circles cuts the smaller circle at $A_1,A_2$ and the larger circle at $B_1,B_2$. The tangent to the smaller circle at $A_i$ and the tangent to the larger circle at $B_j$ meet at a point $P_{ij}$ ($i,j\in\{1,2\}$). Prove that the four points $P_{11},P_{12},P_{21},P_{22}$ lie on a circle with the same centre.
