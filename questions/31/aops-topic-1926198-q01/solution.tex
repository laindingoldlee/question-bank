\textbf{(a)} True: adding $23$ to both sides of $x-23=y-23$ gives $\boxed{x=y}$.

\textbf{(b)} False, for every such $x$ and $y$: by (a), $y>x+100$ would mean $x>x+100$, that is $0>100$, which is impossible. (For example $x=y=0$ satisfies the hypothesis but not $0>100$.) So $y>x+100$ is \boxed{\text{never true}}.
