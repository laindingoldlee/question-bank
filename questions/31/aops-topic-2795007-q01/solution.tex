If $p$ hours have passed, then $p+\dfrac43p=24$, so $\dfrac73p=24$ and $p=\dfrac{72}{7}$. The remaining time is $\dfrac43\cdot\dfrac{72}7=\boxed{\dfrac{96}{7}}$ hours, i.e. $13\dfrac57$ hours.
