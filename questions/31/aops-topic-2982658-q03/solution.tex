The total stays $5$. From $(r,b)=(1,4)$: red is drawn with probability $\dfrac15$, giving $(0,5)$ (all blue); blue is drawn with probability $\dfrac45$, giving $(3,2)$. From $(3,2)$: red, probability $\dfrac35$, gives $(1,4)$; blue, probability $\dfrac25$, gives $(5,0)$ (all red).

Let $p$ and $p'$ be the probabilities of ending all red from $(1,4)$ and $(3,2)$. Then $p=\dfrac45p'$ and $p'=\dfrac35p+\dfrac25$. So $p=\dfrac{12}{25}p+\dfrac8{25}$, giving $p=\boxed{\dfrac8{13}}$.
