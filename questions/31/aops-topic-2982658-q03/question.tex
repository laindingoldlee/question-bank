An urn holds $1$ red and $4$ blue marbles. Each minute Elysia draws a marble uniformly at random (and returns it). If its colour has at least $2$ marbles in the urn, she replaces $2$ marbles of that colour by $2$ of the other colour; if its colour has only $1$ marble, that marble is replaced by the other colour. She stops when all marbles have the same colour. What is the probability that they are all red at the end?
