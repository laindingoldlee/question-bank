The product is a multiple of $14$ iff some number is even and some number is $7$. By inclusion-exclusion on the complements (no even number: $5$ choices each; no $7$: $8$ choices; neither: $4$ choices):
\[1-\left(\dfrac89\right)^4-\left(\dfrac59\right)^4+\left(\dfrac49\right)^4=\dfrac{6561-4096-625+256}{6561}=\boxed{\dfrac{2096}{6561}}.\]
