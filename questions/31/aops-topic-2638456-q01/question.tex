Let acute triangle $ABC$ have $AB>AC$, and let $D$ be a point on segment $AB$ such that there is a point $E$ outside the triangle for which $ADEC$ is a rhombus. Suppose that triangle $ECA$ has area $2$, triangle $EBA$ has area $3$, and $\angle EBA=90^\circ$. Find $BC$.
