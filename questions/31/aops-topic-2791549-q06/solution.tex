Each problem was missed by exactly $3$ students, giving $\binom32=3$ pairs of students who both missed it. That is at most $8\cdot3=24$ pairs that share a missed problem, but there are $\binom82=28$ pairs of students. So some pair shares no missed problem, i.e. between them they solved all eight. $\blacksquare$
