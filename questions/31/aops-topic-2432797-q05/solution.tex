\[P(\text{pollen}\mid\text{sneeze})=\dfrac{0.2\cdot0.9}{0.2\cdot0.9+0.8\cdot0.001}=\dfrac{0.18}{0.1808}=\dfrac{1800}{1808}=\boxed{\dfrac{225}{226}}\approx0.996.\]
