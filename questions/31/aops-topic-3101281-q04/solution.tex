The tangent addition formula $\tan(A+B)=\dfrac{\tan A+\tan B}{1-\tan A\tan B}$ gives successive tangents $\dfrac{7}{11}$, $\dfrac{53}{59}$, and $\dfrac{139}{131}$. The sum of the angles is positive and smaller than $\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{6}+\dfrac{1}{12}=\dfrac{5}{6}<\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, since $\arctan t<t$ for $t>0$. Thus no branch adjustment is needed and $\boxed{m+n=270}$.
