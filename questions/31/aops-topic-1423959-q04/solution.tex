$B$ works on the odd-numbered days. In any $14$ consecutive days starting on a Sunday, $A$ builds $14$ sections, $B$ works $7$ days and builds $14$, and $C$ works $4$ weekend days and builds $16$: $44$ sections in all. After $9$ such fortnights ($126$ days), $396$ sections are built. Day $127$ is a Sunday and an odd day. Continuing day by day:
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccccccc}
\text{day} & 127 & 128 & 129 & 130 & 131 & 132 & 133 & 134\\
\text{weekday} & \text{Sun} & \text{Mon} & \text{Tue} & \text{Wed} & \text{Thu} & \text{Fri} & \text{Sat} & \text{Sun}\\
\text{built} & 7 & 1 & 3 & 1 & 3 & 1 & 7 & 5\\
\text{total} & 403 & 404 & 407 & 408 & 411 & 412 & 419 & 424
\end{array}
\]
The total first reaches $420$ on day $\boxed{134}$. (If $B$ instead started on the second day, the totals for days $127$ to $134$ would be $401,404,405,408,409,412,417,424$, and the answer would still be $134$.)
