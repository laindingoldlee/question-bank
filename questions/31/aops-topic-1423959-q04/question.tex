Three people $A$, $B$, $C$ are building a wall. Each day that they work, $A$ builds $1$ section, $B$ builds $2$ sections and $C$ builds $4$ sections. $A$ works every day, $B$ works on the first day and on every second day after that, and $C$ works only on Saturdays and Sundays. If they start on a Sunday, on which day (counting the first day as day $1$) is the $420$th section completed?
