If $n^2+20n+12=m^2$, then $(n+10)^2-m^2=88$, so $(n+10-m)(n+10+m)=88$. Both factors are positive and have the same parity. The only positive even factor pairs, in increasing order, are $(2,44)$ and $(4,22)$. Their sums give $n+10=23$ or 13, hence $n=13$ or 3. Both work and are below 1000. Thus there are $\boxed2$ integers, choice B. The source key E is incorrect.
