Think of all the attacks as one sequence of independent trials, each a success (kill) with probability $\dfrac45$ and a failure with probability $\dfrac15$; the first ghost acts until the first failure and the second until the second failure. At least $4$ people survive exactly when there are at most $6$ kills before the second failure, i.e. when the second failure is among the first $8$ attempts, i.e. when the first $8$ attempts contain at least $2$ failures. (Since $6<10$, running out of people does not matter.) So the probability is
\[1-\left(\dfrac45\right)^8-8\cdot\dfrac15\left(\dfrac45\right)^7=1-\dfrac{65536}{390625}-\dfrac{131072}{390625}=\boxed{\dfrac{194017}{390625}}\approx0.497.\]
