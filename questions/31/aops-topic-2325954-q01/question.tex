There are $10$ people and $2$ ghosts in a room. The ghosts attack one after the other. A ghost attacks people one at a time: each attack kills a person with probability $80\%$, independently, and when an attack fails, the ghost is destroyed (and the next ghost begins). What is the probability that at least $4$ people remain alive after both ghosts are destroyed?
