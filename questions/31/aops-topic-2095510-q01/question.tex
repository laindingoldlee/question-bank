Penny flips three fair coins into a box with two compartments. Each coin lands heads or tails with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, and lands in either compartment with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, independently of everything else. What is the probability that at least one of the compartments contains at least two coins that landed heads? Express your answer as a common fraction.
