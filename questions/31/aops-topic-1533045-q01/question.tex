In the equation
\[
\dfrac{\overline{724a}+n}{\overline{5b43}-n}=\dfrac{67}{23},
\]
$a$ and $b$ are (unknown) decimal digits, so that $\overline{724a}$ and $\overline{5b43}$ are four-digit numbers, and $n$ is an integer. Find $n$.
