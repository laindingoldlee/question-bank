Let the three cube roots be $u,v,w$. Their cubes satisfy $u^3+v^3=w^3$, while the equation says $u+v=w$. Cubing the latter gives $w^3=u^3+v^3+3uvw$, so $uvw=0$. Thus $x=\dfrac{1}{4},-\dfrac{1}{4},$ or $0$. Each satisfies the original equation directly, giving $\boxed{\{-\dfrac{1}{4},0,\dfrac{1}{4}\}}$.
