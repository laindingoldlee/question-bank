We prove the stronger statement $2^n\ge n+1$. For $n=0$, $1\ge1$. If $2^k\ge k+1$, then
\[2^{k+1}=2\cdot2^k\ge2k+2\ge k+2.\]
Hence $2^n\ge n+1>n$ for all $n\ge0$. $\blacksquare$ The flawed argument proves $2^{k+1}>k$, which is weaker than the needed $2^{k+1}>k+1$.
