If $c=0$, the other four entries are arbitrary, giving $3^4=81$. If $c\ne0$, it has two choices. When $b=d=0$, $a,e$ are arbitrary: $9$ choices. When $b=0,d\ne0$, we must have $e=0$, giving $3\cdot2=6$ choices. When $b\ne0,d=0$, we must have $a=0$, again giving $6$. The case $b,d\ne0$ is forbidden. The total is $81+2(9+6+6)=\boxed{123}$.
