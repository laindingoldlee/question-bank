By the binomial theorem, $(u+v)^5=\sum_{k=0}^{5}\binom5k u^{5-k}v^k$; the coefficients $1,5,10,10,5,1$ are row $5$ of Pascal's triangle.

\textbf{(a)} With $u=x$, $v=y$, the term with $y^3$ is $\binom53x^2y^3$, so the coefficient is $\binom53=\boxed{10}$.

\textbf{(b)} With $u=2x$, $v=y$, the term with $y^3$ is $\binom53(2x)^2y^3=10\cdot4\,x^2y^3$, so the coefficient is $\boxed{40}$.
