By the difference of squares, $u^2-v^2=(u+v)(u-v)$ with $u=20+12$ and $v=20-12$:
\[
(20+12)^2-(20-12)^2=(40)(24)=\boxed{960}.
\]
(Directly: $32^2-8^2=1024-64=960$. In general $(a+b)^2-(a-b)^2=4ab=4\cdot20\cdot12$.)
