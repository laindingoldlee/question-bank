Write $b=pa$ with $p\in\{2,3,5\}$. For fixed $p$, $a$ runs over all divisors of $\dfrac{3600}{p}$ and $b-a=(p-1)a$. Thus the answer is $\sigma(1800)+2\sigma(1200)+4\sigma(720)$. Since $3600=2^4 3^2 5^2$, the three divisor sums are $(15)(13)(31)=6045$, $(31)(4)(31)=3844$, and $(31)(13)(6)=2418$. Therefore the total is $6045+7688+9672=\boxed{23405}$.
