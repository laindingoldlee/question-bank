Let the medians be $AD=x$ and $BE=y$, meeting at the centroid $G$. The centroid divides each median in the ratio $2:1$, so $AG=\dfrac{2x}{3}$ and $BG=\dfrac{2y}{3}$, and triangle $AGB$ is right-angled at $G$:
\[[AGB]=\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac{2x}{3}\cdot\dfrac{2y}{3}=\dfrac{2xy}{9}.\]
The three medians split the triangle into six pieces of equal area, so $[AGB]$ is one third of $[ABC]$. Hence $[ABC]=3\cdot\dfrac{2xy}{9}=\dfrac{2xy}{3}$. $\blacksquare$
