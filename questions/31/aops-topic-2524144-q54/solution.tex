In round $n$ the bot gains $n$ with probability $\dfrac14$, loses $n-1$ with probability $\dfrac14$, and gains nothing with probability $\dfrac12$. Its expected gain in round $n$ is
\[\dfrac14\,n-\dfrac14(n-1)=\dfrac14.\]
By linearity of expectation, over $10$ rounds the expected total is $10\cdot\dfrac14=\boxed{\dfrac52\text{ dollars}}$.
