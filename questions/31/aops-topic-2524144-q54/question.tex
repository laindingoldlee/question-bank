A game lasts $10$ rounds. In round $n$ a player chooses either to keep (nothing happens) or to switch; switching wins $n$ dollars or loses $n-1$ dollars, each with probability $\dfrac12$. A bot chooses keep or switch at random, each with probability $\dfrac12$, independently in every round. What is the expected total amount the bot wins?
