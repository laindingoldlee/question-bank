For an odd positive integer $m$, perform one Collatz step $3m+1$ and then divide by 2 repeatedly until the result is odd.\begin{enumerate}\item Show that if $m\equiv1\pmod4$, at least two divisions occur.\item Show that if $m\equiv3\pmod4$, exactly one occurs.\item In the second case, determine precisely when the resulting odd integer is $1\pmod4$.\end{enumerate}
