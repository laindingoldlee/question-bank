Factor $2023=7\cdot17^2$. The factorial contains $\lfloor\dfrac{2023}{7}\rfloor+\lfloor\dfrac{2023}{49}\rfloor+\lfloor\dfrac{2023}{343}\rfloor=289+41+5=335$ factors 7, and $\lfloor\dfrac{2023}{17}\rfloor+\lfloor\dfrac{2023}{289}\rfloor=119+7=126$ factors 17. Thus the largest power $2023^k$ dividing the factorial has $k=\min(335,\lfloor\dfrac{126}{2}\rfloor)=\boxed{63}$, which is exactly the trailing-zero count.
