All terms are nonnegative, so regroup by $n=i+j+k$. There are $\binom{n+2}{2}$ triples with this sum. Thus
\[\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\dfrac{\binom{n+2}{2}}{(n+1)2^{n+1}}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\dfrac{n+2}{2^{n+2}}.\]
Using $\sum_{n\ge0}2^{-n}=2$ and $\sum_{n\ge0}n2^{-n}=2$, the value is $\dfrac{1}{4}(2+4)=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{2}}$. The second series follows by differentiating the geometric series for $|x|<1$ and substituting $x=\dfrac{1}{2}$.
