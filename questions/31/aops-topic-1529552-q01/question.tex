In hexagon $ABCDEF$, shown below, adjacent sides are perpendicular, and the hexagon is L-shaped (its interior angle at $E$ is $270^\circ$). If $AB=8$, $BC=6$, $CD=3$ and $DE=4$, what fraction of the segment $AC$ lies inside the hexagon? Express your answer as a common fraction.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,8),B=(0,0),C=(6,0),D=(6,3),E=(2,3),F=(2,8);
draw(A--B--C--D--E--F--cycle,linewidth(1));
label("$A$",A,NW);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,NE);label("$E$",E,NE);label("$F$",F,NE);
label("$8$",(A+B)/2,W);label("$6$",(B+C)/2,S);label("$3$",(C+D)/2,E);label("$4$",(D+E)/2,N);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
