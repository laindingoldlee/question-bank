Let the carries from the units, tens, hundreds, and thousands columns be $u,v,w,z$, each $0$ or $1$. The columns give
\[
2S=T+10u,\quad2S+u=R+10v,\quad
2O+v=O+10w,
\]
\[
2R+w=P+10z,\qquad2C+z=S.
\]
If $S\le4$, then $u=v=0$ and $R=T=2S$, impossible. Hence $S\ge5$, $u=v=1$, and the hundreds equation forces $O=9,w=1$. Thus $S\ne9$ and
\[
R=2S-9,\qquad T=2S-10.
\]
Only $S=5,6,7,8$ remain:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c|c|l}
S&R&T&P&z&\text{leading-column result}\\ \hline
5&1&0&3&0&2C=5\text{ impossible}\\
6&3&2&7&0&C=3=R\text{ forbidden}\\
7&5&4&1&1&C=3\text{ valid}\\
8&7&6&5&1&2C=7\text{ impossible}
\end{array}
\]
Therefore the unique solution is
\[
\boxed{35977+35977=71954}.
\]
