A customer buys four dozen pieces of fruit, apples and oranges, for $\$5.68$. Every apple costs the same whole number of cents, every orange costs the same whole number of cents, and an apple costs ten cents more than an orange. More apples than oranges are bought. How many pieces of each kind of fruit does the customer buy?
