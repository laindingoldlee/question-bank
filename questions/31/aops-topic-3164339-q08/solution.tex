Let $u=\log a_1<0$ and $v=\log r$, where $r$ is the common ratio. The condition gives $20(u+19v)=18(u+17v)$, so $u=-37v$ and $v>0$. The logarithm of the first-$n$ product is $nu+\dfrac{n(n-1)}{2}v=\dfrac{n(n-75)}{2}v$. It is positive exactly when $n>75$, hence $\boxed{76}$.
