The number lies between $1\,234\,000$ and $1\,234\,999$. Since $1110^2=1\,232\,100$ is too small and $1112^2=1\,236\,544$ is too large, the square root, an integer between $1110$ and $1112$, must be $1111$. Indeed $1111^2=1\,234\,321$ has the required form. The square root is $\boxed{1111}$.
