We need $23a+7=21b+6$, that is $21b-23a=1$. Working modulo $21$: $23a\equiv2a\equiv-1\equiv20\pmod{21}$, so $a\equiv10\pmod{21}$. With $a=10$: $x=23\cdot10+7=237$ and $b=\dfrac{237-6}{21}=11$. Check: $237=23\cdot10+7=21\cdot11+6$.

So the claim is false. In general $a=10+21t$ and $b=11+23t$, giving
\[
x=237+483t\qquad(t\ge0),
\]
where $483=21\cdot23$. The smallest such $x$ is $\boxed{237}$ (whether or not $0$ counts as a natural number, since $a=0$ would need $x=7$, and $7\not\equiv6\pmod{21}$).
