Among $1,2,\dots,N$ there are $\left\lfloor\sqrt N\right\rfloor$ perfect squares, so $N-\left\lfloor\sqrt N\right\rfloor$ non-squares. Since $40^2=1600<1651<1681=41^2$, the number $1651$ is not a square and
\[1651-\left\lfloor\sqrt{1651}\right\rfloor=1651-40=1611.\]
So $1651$ is the $1611$th non-square, and the answer is $\boxed{1651}$.
