Drying removes only liquid, so the mass of dry matter stays the same. The dried plums contain $75\%$ dry matter:
\[
0.75\cdot315=236.25\text{ kg}.
\]
In the fresh plums the dry matter is $21\%$ of the mass, so the mass of fresh plums is
\[
\dfrac{236.25}{0.21}=\boxed{1125}\text{ kg}.
\]
