\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] For how many two-element subsets $\{a,b\}$ of $\{1,2,3,\dots,36\}$ is the product $ab$ a perfect square?
\item[(b)] A student argues: ``Since $ab=ba$, whenever $\{a,b\}$ works so does $\{b,a\}$. So the subsets come in pairs, and the answer must be even.'' What is wrong with this argument?
\end{enumerate}
