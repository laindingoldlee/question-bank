Write a ticket as $1000X+Y$ with $X,Y\in\{0,\dots,999\}$. If it is lucky, so is $1000Y+X$, and the two sum to $1001(X+Y)$. Pairing each lucky ticket with this partner (a ticket with $X=Y$ is its own partner and equals $1001X$), the total is a sum of multiples of $1001=7\cdot11\cdot13$, hence divisible by $13$. $\blacksquare$
