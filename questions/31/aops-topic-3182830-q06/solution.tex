Only the $8$-, $12$-, and $20$-sided dice can show $7$. Thus the probability is $\dfrac{1}{5}\left(\dfrac{1}{8}+\dfrac{1}{12}+\dfrac{1}{20}\right)=\dfrac{31}{600}$, giving $\boxed{631}$.
