The centre must be at least one half from all four sides. The only feasible location is the square's centre. This singleton has area zero, so its probability under the uniform area distribution is $\boxed0$. Nevertheless it is a possible point in the sample space. A probability-zero event need not be empty in a continuous model; this differs from rolling a seven on a die whose sample space has only one through six.
