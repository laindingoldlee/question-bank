Use $ 10001!+2,10001!+3,\ldots,10001!+10001 $. There are $ 10000 $ terms. For each $ k $ from $ 2 $ to $ 10001 $, the term $ 10001!+k $ is divisible by $ k $ and larger than $ k $, so it is composite.
