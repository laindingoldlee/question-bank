Put $D=(0,0),A=(-a,0),B=(a,0)$. Since $BC=CD$, $C=(\dfrac{a}{2},h)$ with $h>0$. Since $AE:EC=1:3$, $E=(-\dfrac{5a}{8},\dfrac{h}{4})$. Let $\delta=\angle ADE$. Then $\tan\delta=\dfrac{2h}{(5a)}$ and $\tan\angle ABC=\dfrac{2h}{a}$. Using $\angle ABC=2\delta$ in the double-angle formula yields $h^2=\dfrac{15a^2}{4}$. Now $AC^2=\dfrac{9a^2}{4}+h^2=6a^2=16^2$, while $BC^2=\dfrac{a^2}{4}+h^2=4a^2$. Hence $\boxed{BC=\dfrac{16\sqrt6}{3}}$.

\end{asy}
