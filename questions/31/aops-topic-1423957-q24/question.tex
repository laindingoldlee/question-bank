A sequence of natural numbers is constructed by listing the first $4$, then skipping one, listing the next $5$, skipping $2$, listing $6$, skipping $3$, and, on the $n$th iteration, listing $n+3$ and skipping $n$. The sequence begins $1,2,3,4,6,7,8,9,10,13$. What is the $500{,}000$th number in the sequence? Explain also why the last number reached at the end of the $n$th iteration (after its skipped numbers) is $(n+2)^2-4$.

$\textbf{(A)}\ 996{,}506\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ 996{,}507\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ 996{,}508\qquad\textbf{(D)}\ 996{,}509\qquad\textbf{(E)}\ 996{,}510$
