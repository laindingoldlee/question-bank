Direct calculation shows that $n=3,4,5,6,7$ satisfy the inequality, while $8!=40320<46656=6^6$. Let $R_n=\dfrac{n!}{(n-2)^{n-2}}$. Then $\dfrac{R_{n+1}}{R_n}=\dfrac{n+1}{n-1}\left(\dfrac{n-2}{n-1}\right)^{n-2}$. By the binomial theorem, $(1+\dfrac{1}{n-2})^{n-2}\ge2$, so this ratio is at most $\dfrac{n+1}{2(n-1)}<1$ for $n>3$. Thus after 8 it stays below 1, and the full solution set is $\boxed{\{3,4,5,6,7\}}$.
