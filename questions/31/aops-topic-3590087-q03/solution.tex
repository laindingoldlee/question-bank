Choose the boy's room in $5$ ways. The seven girls occupy the other four rooms, so one room has one girl and three have two. Choose the singleton girl in $7$ ways and her room in $4$ ways. The remaining six girls go into three labelled pairs in $\dfrac{6!}{(2!)^3}=90$ ways. Thus the answer is $5\cdot7\cdot4\cdot90=\boxed{12600}$.
