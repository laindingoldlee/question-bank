Every child holds a boy's hand or a girl's hand (or both). Since $22+30=52=40+12$, exactly $12$ children hold both a boy's and a girl's hand, $22-12=10$ hold only boys' hands (both neighbours boys), and $30-12=18$ hold only girls' hands.

Now count, over all children, the number of neighbours who are girls. The $18$ children with two girl neighbours contribute $2$ each and the $12$ mixed children contribute $1$ each: $36+12=48$. On the other hand, each girl is a neighbour of exactly two children, so this count is $2G$. Hence $2G=48$ and $G=\boxed{24}$.

(Check: boys are counted $2\cdot10+12=32=2\cdot 16$ times, and $24+16=40$.)
