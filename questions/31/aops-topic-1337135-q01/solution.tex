Write $\overline{nnn}=111n$ and $\overline{mmmooo}=111000m+111o=111(1000m+o)$. Then
\[
\dfrac{111n(111n+1)}{2}=111(1000m+o)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad n(111n+1)=2(1000m+o).
\]
Try $n=1,\dots,9$; the right side lies between $2000$ and $2\cdot9009$, and we need $\dfrac{n(111n+1)}{2}=1000m+o$ with $o\le9$:
\[
\dfrac{n(111n+1)}{2}=56,\ 223,\ 501,\ 890,\ 1390,\ 2001,\ 2723,\ 3556,\ 4500\quad(n=1,\dots,9).
\]
Only $n=6$ gives the form $1000m+o$: $2001=1000\cdot2+1$, so $m=2$, $o=1$. Indeed
\[
T_{666}=\dfrac{666\cdot667}{2}=222111 .
\]
The answer is $\overline{nnn}=\boxed{666}$.
