Let $S=\sum_{n\ge1}F_nx^n$ with $x=\dfrac1{10}$ (the series converges since $F_n<2^n$). Using $F_{n}=F_{n-1}+F_{n-2}$ for $n\ge3$,
\[S-xS-x^2S=F_1x+(F_2-F_1)x^2=x,\]
so $S=\dfrac{x}{1-x-x^2}=\dfrac{\dfrac1{10}}{1-\dfrac1{10}-\dfrac1{100}}=\boxed{\dfrac{10}{89}}$.
