The equation is $x^3+13x-34=0$. Trying divisors of $34$, $x=2$ works ($8+26-34=0$). Dividing, $x^3+13x-34=(x-2)(x^2+2x+17)$, and $x^2+2x+17=(x+1)^2+16$ has roots $-1\pm4i$. So the solutions are $\boxed{x=2,\ x=-1\pm4i}$; the only real one is $2$.
