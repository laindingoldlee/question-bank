There are $60000-25000=35000$ in-person votes, of which B got $\dfrac25\cdot35000=14000$. B needs at least $30000$ votes in total, so at least $16000$ absentee votes, which is
\[\dfrac{16000}{25000}=\boxed{64\%}\]
of the absentee votes.
