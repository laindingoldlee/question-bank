In a state, $60{,}000$ votes are cast for two candidates, A and B, and $25{,}000$ of them are absentee votes. Of the in-person votes, $\dfrac35$ went to A and $\dfrac25$ went to B. At least what percent of the absentee votes must go to B for B to get at least $50\%$ of all the votes?
