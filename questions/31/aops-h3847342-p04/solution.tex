Write the number as $10a+b$, where $1\le a\le9$ and $0\le b\le9$. The square difference is $99(a^2-b^2)$, so $(a-b)(a+b)=16$. Both factors have the same parity. The positive same-parity factor pairs are $(2,8)$ and $(4,4)$, giving $(a,b)=(5,3)$ or $(4,0)$. Thus the numbers are $53$ and $40$, whose sum is $\boxed{93}$.
