Bob and Billy each have a collection of items. Each of them sorts his collection into as many equal piles as there are letters in his name: Bob gets $1$ item left over and Billy gets $2$ left over. Bert, who has no collection of his own, combines the two collections and sorts them into $4$ equal piles (the number of letters in ``Bert''), and gets $3$ left over. The two collections together have fewer than $1000$ items. Which totals are possible?
