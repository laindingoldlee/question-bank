Let $h,p$ be the numbers sold. Then $21h+17p=360$, with $0\le p\le16$. Modulo 21, $17p\equiv3$, and since $17\cdot5\equiv1$, $p\equiv15\pmod{21}$. Thus $p=15$, yielding $h=5$ within stock. The answer is $\boxed{15}$.
