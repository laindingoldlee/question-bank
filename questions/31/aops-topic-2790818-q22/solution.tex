Reducing mod $2$, $3$ and $5$: $15z$ is even, so $z$ is even; $10y$ is a multiple of $3$, so $3\mid y$; $6x$ is a multiple of $5$, so $5\mid x$. Writing $x=5a$, $y=3b$, $z=2c$ gives $30(a+b+c)=300$, i.e. $a+b+c=10$, which has $\dbinom{12}2=\boxed{66}$ nonnegative solutions.
