Choose the colour of $A$ in $4$ ways and the different colour of $C$ in $3$ ways. Each of $B,D$ is adjacent to both $A,C$ and may independently use either of the other two colours. There is no restriction between $B,D$. The total is $\boxed{4\cdot3\cdot2\cdot2=48}$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(2,0),C=(2,2),D=(0,2);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C);dot(A,red+6bp);dot(C,blue+6bp);dot(B,heavygreen+6bp);dot(D,heavygreen+6bp);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);
\end{asy}
