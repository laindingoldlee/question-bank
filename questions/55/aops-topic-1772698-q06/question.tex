One thousand people stand in a row. Exactly one of them, equally likely to be any of the $1000$, is volcano-proof. Luke is chosen at random. A guard who knows who is volcano-proof then throws $998$ of the other $999$ people into a volcano, choosing them so that the volcano-proof person is never thrown in (if Luke himself is the volcano-proof person, the guard spares one of the other $999$ chosen at random). None of the $998$ survives. What is the probability that the one remaining person other than Luke is volcano-proof?
