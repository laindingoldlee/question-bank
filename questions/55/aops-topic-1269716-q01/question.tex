Let $a=4^{6^{8^{\cdot^{\cdot^{\cdot^{2016}}}}}}$ be the power tower of the even numbers $4,6,8,\dots,2016$, and let $b=4^{6^{8^{\cdot^{\cdot^{\cdot^{2016^{2018}}}}}}}$ be the tower of $4,6,8,\dots,2016,2018$. Find
\[\gcd\left(2^{a}+1,\ 2^{b}+3\right).\]
