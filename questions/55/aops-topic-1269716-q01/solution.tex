Both $a$ and $b$ are powers of $4$: $a=4^m$ and $b=4^n$, where $m$ and $n$ are the towers starting at $6$. Replacing the top exponent $2016$ by $2016^{2018}$ makes every level larger, so $n>m$. Hence $b=a\cdot 4^{n-m}$, so $a\mid b$ and $\dfrac{b}{a}$ is even.

Let $A=2^a+1$. Then $2^a\equiv -1\pmod A$, so
\[2^b=\left(2^a\right)^{\dfrac{b}{a}}\equiv(-1)^{\dfrac{b}{a}}=1\pmod A.\]
Therefore $2^b+3\equiv 4\pmod A$, and
\[\gcd(A,\,2^b+3)=\gcd(A,4)=\boxed{1},\]
because $A$ is odd.
