Completing the square gives $(p-13)^2\equiv168\equiv1\pmod{167}$. Since $167$ is prime, $p-13\equiv\pm1$, so $p\equiv12$ or $14\pmod{167}$. The candidates below $179$ are just $12,14$, both composite. The next is $179$, which is not divisible by $2,3,5,7,11,$ or $13$, the primes at most its square root. Thus $\boxed{p=179}$.
