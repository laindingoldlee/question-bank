Five judges vote on whether to promote a contestant: judges $1$, $3$ and $4$ vote for, and judges $2$ and $5$ vote against. Each judge's vote carries a weight of $0$ or $1$, chosen independently with equal probability. The contestant is promoted if the total weight of the votes for him is at least the total weight of the votes against him. What is the probability that he is promoted?
