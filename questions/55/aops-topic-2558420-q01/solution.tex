Let $F$ be the total weight for ($0$ to $3$) and $G$ the total weight against ($0$ to $2$). Then
\[P(G=0)=\dfrac14,\ P(G=1)=\dfrac12,\ P(G=2)=\dfrac14,\qquad P(F\ge1)=\dfrac78,\ P(F\ge2)=\dfrac{3+1}{8}=\dfrac12.\]
By independence,
\[P(F\ge G)=\dfrac14\cdot1+\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac78+\dfrac14\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac{4+7+2}{16}=\boxed{\dfrac{13}{16}}.\]
