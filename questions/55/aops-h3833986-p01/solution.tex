Since $320=10\cdot2^5$, $\log_{10}(320^{320})=320+1600\log_{10}2$. The bounds $0.30102999<\log_{10}2<0.30103000$ give $801.647984<\log_{10}(320^{320})<801.648$. Thus the digit count is $\lfloor\log_{10}(320^{320})\rfloor+1=\boxed{802}$. Substituting $0.3$ instead gives $800$, whose rounding error is too large to identify the relevant integer interval.
