Factor $2018=2\cdot1009$, where $1009$ is prime (it is not divisible by any prime up to $31$, and $32^2>1009$). Let $r$ be the remainder.

\textbf{Modulo $2$:} $1000^{2018}$ is even, so $r\equiv0\pmod2$.

\textbf{Modulo $1009$:} $1000\equiv-9$. By Fermat's little theorem $(-9)^{1008}\equiv1$, and $2018=2\cdot1008+2$, so
\[
1000^{2018}\equiv(-9)^{2018}=\left((-9)^{1008}\right)^2(-9)^2\equiv81\pmod{1009}.
\]

\textbf{Combine (Chinese remainder theorem).} The numbers below $2018$ that are $\equiv81\pmod{1009}$ are $81$ and $1090$; the even one is $1090$. So $r=\boxed{1090}$.
