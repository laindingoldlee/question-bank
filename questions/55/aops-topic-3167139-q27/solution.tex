Against smaller numbers, $a$ wins exactly against its proper positive divisors, of which there are $\tau(a)-1$. Against larger numbers, it wins against the $20-a$ choices except the $\lfloor\dfrac{20}{a}\rfloor-1$ multiples of $a$. Thus the number of winning opponents is $w(a)=\tau(a)+20-a-\lfloor\dfrac{20}{a}\rfloor$. For $a=1,2,\ldots,20$, these counts are $0,10,13,14,13,15,13,14,12,12,10,13,8,9,8,8,4,7,2,5$. The unique maximum is $15$ at $\boxed{a=6}$, giving winning probability $\boxed{\dfrac{3}{4}}$.
