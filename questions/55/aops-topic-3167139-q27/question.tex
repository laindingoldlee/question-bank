Choose an integer $a$ from $1$ through $20$. An opponent independently chooses uniformly from the same set. Equal choices tie. For unequal choices, the larger wins if divisible by the smaller; otherwise the smaller wins. Which fixed choice maximizes your probability of winning, and what is that probability?
