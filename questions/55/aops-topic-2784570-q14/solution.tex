Only the 100-sided die can supply a factor of $7$. Four of its outcomes are multiples of $21$ and automatically win. Ten other outcomes are multiples of $7$ but not $3$. In these cases the chance of a factor of $3$ from the other dice is $1-\dfrac{3}{4}\cdot\dfrac{4}{6}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Thus the win probability is $\dfrac{4+\dfrac{10}{2}}{100}=\dfrac{9}{100}$. Zero expected gain means $\dfrac{9}{100}W-\dfrac{91}{100}\cdot100=0$, so $\boxed{W=\dfrac{9100}{9}}$ dollars.
