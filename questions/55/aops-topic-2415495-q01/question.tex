Seven students, including Alex, have perfect scores on a contest. Each of the other six, independently, has a $20\%$ chance of having their score cancelled; Alex's score is certainly valid. In the next competition, every competitor without a valid perfect score finishes below every student with a valid perfect score, and Alex is equally likely to finish in each of the positions $1,2,\dots,k$, where $k$ is the number of students with valid perfect scores (including Alex). Alex qualifies if he finishes in the top $4$. To the nearest percent, what is the probability that Alex qualifies?
