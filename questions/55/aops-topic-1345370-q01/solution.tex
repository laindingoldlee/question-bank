Yes to all three. Take $k=0$: $0=3\cdot0$ and $0=4\cdot0$, so $0$ is a multiple of $3$ and of $4$; in the same way $0=d\cdot0$ for every integer $d$, so $0$ is a multiple of every integer. In particular $0$ is a common multiple of $3$ and $4$. (This is why the least common multiple is defined as the least \emph{positive} common multiple, and why counting problems must say whether $0$ is included.)
