The altitude satisfies $h^2=pq$ and $h=\dfrac{p+q}{4}$. With $t=\dfrac{p}{q}$, this gives $16t=(t+1)^2$, or $t^2-14t+1=0$. The root below 1 is $\boxed{7-4\sqrt3}$.

\end{asy}
