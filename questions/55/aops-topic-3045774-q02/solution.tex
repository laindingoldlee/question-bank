The discriminant is $ 4(49-4z)$. Thus $ 49-4z=q^2 $ for a nonnegative integer $ q $. Since $ z>0 $, $ q<7 $; reduction modulo 4 requires $ q $ odd. The possibilities $ q=1,3,5 $ give $\boxed{z=12,10,6}$. Conversely the roots $\dfrac{7\pm q}{4}$ are rational for all three values.
