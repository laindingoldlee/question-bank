Every integer cube is congruent to 0,1 or $-1$ modulo 9. Therefore a difference of two cubes is congruent to 0, $\pm1$ or $\pm2$ modulo 9, never 6. The equation has $\boxed{\text{no solutions}}$, even without the positivity or coprimality conditions.
