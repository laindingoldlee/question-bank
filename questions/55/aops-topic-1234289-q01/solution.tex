Rotate coordinates so the given direction is vertical, and let $f(t)$ be the area of the part of the region to the left of the line $x=t$. Since the region is bounded, $f(t)=0$ for $t$ small enough and $f(t)=A$ (the whole area) for $t$ large enough. The function $f$ is continuous: moving the line by $h$ changes $f$ by at most $h\cdot H$, where $H$ is the height of a box containing the region. By the intermediate value theorem there is $t$ with $f(t)=\dfrac {A}{2}$, and the line $x=t$ bisects the area. $\blacksquare$
