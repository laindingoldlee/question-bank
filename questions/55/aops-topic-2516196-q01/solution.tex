The unordered triples of positive integers with product $72$, with their sums, are:
\[
\begin{array}{llll}
(1,1,72):74 & (1,2,36):39 & (1,3,24):28 & (1,4,18):23\\
(1,6,12):19 & (1,8,9):18 & (2,2,18):22 & (2,3,12):17\\
(2,4,9):15 & (2,6,6):14 & (3,3,8):14 & (3,4,6):13
\end{array}
\]
B knows the sum but cannot determine the ages, so the sum is shared by two triples. The only repeated sum is $14$, from $(2,6,6)$ and $(3,3,8)$. So the sum of the ages is $\boxed{14}$.

A's last statement settles which: in $(2,6,6)$ the two equal ages are $6$, not younger than A, while in $(3,3,8)$ two cousins aged $3<6$ have the same age. So the cousins are $\boxed{3,\ 3\text{ and }8}$ years old.
