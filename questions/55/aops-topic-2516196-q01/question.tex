Two people meet at a movie theatre.

A: ``I am $6$. I have $3$ cousins whose ages are whole numbers. The product of their ages is $72$, and the sum of their ages is the sum of our ticket numbers.''

B: ``I know the sum of our ticket numbers, but I still do not know their ages.''

A: ``Two of my cousins who are younger than me have the same age.''

B: ``Now I know their ages.''

Find the sum of the ages of the $3$ cousins, and their ages.
