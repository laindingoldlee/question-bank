By Vieta's formulas the roots satisfy $r+s=\dfrac{15}3=5$ and $rs=\dfrac{81}3=27$. So
\[r^2+s^2=(r+s)^2-2rs=25-54=\boxed{-29}.\]
(The answer is negative because the roots are non-real: the discriminant $225-972$ is negative. The roots are $\dfrac{5\pm i\sqrt{83}}{2}$.)
