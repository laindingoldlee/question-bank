Put $t=5^x$, which is positive. The equation becomes
\[
t(t-1)=10\qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad t^2-t-10=0,\qquad t=\dfrac{1\pm\sqrt{41}}{2}.
\]
Since $t>0$, only $t=\dfrac{1+\sqrt{41}}{2}$ is possible (the other root is negative). Hence
\[
5^x=\dfrac{1+\sqrt{41}}{2},\qquad x=\boxed{\log_5\dfrac{1+\sqrt{41}}{2}}\approx0.957 .
\]
