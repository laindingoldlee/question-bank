Choose the three odd digits from $\{1,3,5,7,9\}$ in $\dbinom53=10$ ways and the three even digits from $\{0,2,4,6,8\}$ in $\dbinom53=10$ ways, and arrange the six chosen digits in $6!=720$ ways: $10\cdot10\cdot720=72000$ arrangements. From these remove the ones starting with $0$: the even digits must include $0$ ($\dbinom42=6$ ways to choose the other two), the odd digits can be chosen in $10$ ways, and the remaining five digits are arranged after the leading $0$ in $5!=120$ ways: $6\cdot10\cdot120=7200$. The answer is $72000-7200=\boxed{64800}$.
