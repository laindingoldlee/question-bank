In base $b$, the numeral $10101$ means
\[
N=b^4+b^2+1 .
\]
Factor it by completing the square:
\[
b^4+b^2+1=\left(b^4+2b^2+1\right)-b^2=\left(b^2+1\right)^2-b^2=\left(b^2+b+1\right)\left(b^2-b+1\right).
\]
For $b\ge2$, both factors exceed $1$: $b^2-b+1=b(b-1)+1\ge3$ and $b^2+b+1\ge7$. So $N$ is a product of two integers greater than $1$, hence \boxed{N\text{ is composite for every base }b\ge2}. (For example, in base $10$: $10101=111\cdot91=3\cdot7\cdot13\cdot37$; in base $2$: $10101_2=21=7\cdot3$.)
