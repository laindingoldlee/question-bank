Place $R=(0,0)$ and $K=(15,0)$. Solving for the vertex gives $D=(\dfrac{33}{5},\dfrac{56}{5})$. The altitude from $D$ is vertical. Since $DK$ has slope $-\dfrac{4}{3}$, the altitude from $R$ has slope $\dfrac{3}{4}$. Hence $E=(\dfrac{33}{5},\dfrac{99}{20})$. The three distances are $DE=\dfrac{25}{4}$, $RE=\dfrac{33}{4}$, and $KE=\dfrac{39}{4}$, by the distance formula. Their sum is $\dfrac{97}{4}$, whose floor is $\boxed{24}$.

\end{asy}
