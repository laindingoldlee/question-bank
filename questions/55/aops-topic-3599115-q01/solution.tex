The roots of $w^m=1$ are $e^{\dfrac{2\pi i k}{m}}$ for $k=0,\ldots,m-1$. Thus (a) gives $1,-\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}i,-\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}i$; (b) gives $1,i,-1,-i$; (c) gives $e^{\dfrac{2\pi i k}{5}}$ for $k=0,1,2,3,4$. For the monic polynomial $w^m-1$, the product of roots is $(-1)^{m+1}$ by Vieta. Therefore (d) is $1+(-1)+1=\boxed1$.
