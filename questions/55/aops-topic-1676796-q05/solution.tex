There are $900$ three-digit numbers. Those with three different digits number $9\cdot9\cdot8=648$ (hundreds digit $1$--$9$, tens digit any of the other $9$ digits, units digit any of the remaining $8$). So $900-648=\boxed{252}$ have at least two equal digits.
