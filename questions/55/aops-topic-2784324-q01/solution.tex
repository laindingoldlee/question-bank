Write $n=17k+11$. Then $100\le17k+11\le300$ gives $\dfrac{89}{17}\le k\le\dfrac{289}{17}=17$, so $6\le k\le17$. The $12$ integers are
\[\boxed{113,\ 130,\ 147,\ 164,\ 181,\ 198,\ 215,\ 232,\ 249,\ 266,\ 283,\ 300}.\]
