Vieta gives $a+b+c=1$, $ab+bc+ca=-5$, $abc=-2$. The identity $(a+b)(b+c)(c+a)=(a+b+c)(ab+bc+ca)-abc$ gives $-5+2=-3$. Cubing yields $\boxed{-27}$.
