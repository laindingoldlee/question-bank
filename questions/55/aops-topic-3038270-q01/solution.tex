No day has two rainy half-days, so the 13 rainy days account for exactly 13 rainy half-days. There are $ 11+12=23 $ sunny half-days, making 36 half-days in total. Thus the vacation lasted $\boxed{18}$ days. Indeed there are $ 18-11=7 $ rainy mornings and $ 18-12=6 $ rainy afternoons, totalling 13.
