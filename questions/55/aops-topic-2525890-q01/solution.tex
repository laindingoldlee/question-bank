Measure the position of each hand in degrees clockwise from the $12$.
\begin{itemize}
\item The minute hand turns $6^\circ$ per minute, so at $20$ minutes past the hour it is at $20\cdot6^\circ=120^\circ$.
\item The hour hand turns $30^\circ$ per hour, i.e. $0.5^\circ$ per minute, so at $6{:}20$ it is at $6\cdot30^\circ+20\cdot0.5^\circ=190^\circ$.
\end{itemize}
The hands differ by $190^\circ-120^\circ=70^\circ\le180^\circ$, so the angle between them is $\boxed{70^\circ}$, and the book's $50^\circ$ is wrong.

The same value comes from following the gap: at $6{:}00$ the hands are $180^\circ$ apart, and until $6{:}20$ the minute hand is still behind the hour hand (clockwise) and catches up on it at $6-0.5=5.5^\circ$ per minute, so after $20$ minutes the gap is $180^\circ-20\cdot5.5^\circ=70^\circ$.
