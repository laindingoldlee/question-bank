All $\binom{10}{2}=45$ pairs are equally likely, and $\binom52=10$ of them consist of two green cows. The probability is $\dfrac{10}{45}=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{9}}$. (Equivalently $\dfrac{5}{10}\cdot\dfrac{4}{9}$.)
