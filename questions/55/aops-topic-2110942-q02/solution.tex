The square has side $\sqrt{98}=7\sqrt2$, so the string has length $28\sqrt2$.

Let the first rectangle have sides $2k$ and $3k$ and the second $3j$ and $8j$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real j=2*sqrt(2)/3, k=2*j;
draw((0,0)--(3k,0)--(3k,2k)--(0,2k)--cycle,blue+linewidth(1));
fill((0,0)--(3k,0)--(3k,2k)--(0,2k)--cycle,paleblue+opacity(0.4));
label("$3k$",(1.5k,0),S); label("$2k$",(0,k),W);
real o=3k+2.2;
draw((o,0)--(o+8j,0)--(o+8j,3j)--(o,3j)--cycle,red+linewidth(1));
fill((o,0)--(o+8j,0)--(o+8j,3j)--(o,3j)--cycle,palered+opacity(0.4));
label("$8j$",(o+4j,0),S); label("$3j$",(o,1.5j),W);
label("$6k^2$",(1.5k,k)); label("$24j^2$",(o+4j,1.5j));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Their perimeters are $10k$ and $22j$, and their areas are $6k^2$ and $24j^2$. Equal areas give $6k^2=24j^2$, so $k=2j$. The two pieces make up the whole string:
\[10k+22j=20j+22j=42j=28\sqrt2,\qquad j=\dfrac{2\sqrt2}{3}.\]
Each area is $24j^2=24\cdot\dfrac{8}{9}=\dfrac{64}{3}$, so $m+n=64+3=\boxed{67}$.
