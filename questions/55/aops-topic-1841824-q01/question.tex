A Norman window is a rectangle with a semicircle on top: the diameter of the semicircle is the top side of the rectangle. The frame runs along the whole outline of the window (the bottom, the two vertical sides and the semicircular arc), and only $12$ ft of frame material is available. What should the dimensions of the rectangular part be to let in as much light as possible, that is, to make the area of the window as large as possible?
