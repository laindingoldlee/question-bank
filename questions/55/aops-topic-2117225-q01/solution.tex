The first coefficients $1,5,10,10$ are those of $(x+1)^5=x^5+5x^4+10x^3+10x^2+5x+1$. Hence
\[f(x)=(x+1)^5-7x .\]
Therefore
\[f(-2021)=(-2020)^5+7\cdot2021,\qquad f(2019)=2020^5-7\cdot2019 .\]
Since $(-2020)^5=-2020^5$, the fifth powers cancel and
\[f(-2021)+f(2019)=7(2021-2019)=\boxed{14}.\]
