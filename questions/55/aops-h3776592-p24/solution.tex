The sine rule in $PAB$ and $PAC$, together with the tangent-chord theorem, gives
\[
\dfrac{PB}{PC}=\dfrac{AB\sin C}{AC\sin B}=\dfrac{AB^2}{AC^2}=\dfrac{1}{4}.
\]
Here the angles at $P$ have equal sines, and the final equality uses the sine rule in $ABC$. Since $P$ is exterior to the circle, it is outside segment $BC$; the ratio places it beyond $B$. Thus $PC=PB+12$, giving $PB=4,PC=16$. Power of a point now gives $\boxed{AP=\sqrt{PB\cdot PC}=8}$.

\end{asy}
