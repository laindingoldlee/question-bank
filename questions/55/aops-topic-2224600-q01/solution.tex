Clearing denominators, $21(a+b)=4ab$, i.e. $16ab-84a-84b=0$, and adding $441$ to both sides:
\[(4a-21)(4b-21)=441=3^2\cdot7^2 .\]
Both factors exceed $-21$. If both were negative, each would have absolute value at most $17$ and their product would be at most $289<441$; so both are positive divisors of $441$. Also $4a-21\equiv-21\equiv3\pmod4$. The divisors of $441$ are $1,3,7,9,21,49,63,147,441$, and those $\equiv3\pmod4$ are $3,7,63,147$. The factor pairs are $(3,147)$, $(7,63)$, $(63,7)$, $(147,3)$, giving
\[(a,b)=\boxed{(6,42),\ (7,21),\ (21,7),\ (42,6)}.\]
(Check: $\dfrac{1}{6}+\dfrac{1}{42}=\dfrac{8}{42}=\dfrac{4}{21}$ and $\dfrac{1}{7}+\dfrac{1}{21}=\dfrac{4}{21}$.)
