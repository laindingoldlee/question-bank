The crossing takes $20\div5=4$ seconds. Its resultant speed is $\sqrt{5^2+12^2}=13$ meters per second, so it travels $13\cdot4=\boxed{52}$ meters. Equivalently, its displacements are $20$ across and $48$ downstream.
