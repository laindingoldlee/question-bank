Both numbers have the form $\overline{abc}\cdot1001$, and $1001=7\cdot11\cdot13$. Since $876=2^2\cdot3\cdot73$ and $678=2\cdot3\cdot113$,
\[876876=2^2\cdot3\cdot7\cdot11\cdot13\cdot73,\qquad 678678=2\cdot3\cdot7\cdot11\cdot13\cdot113.\]
So $m=3\cdot2^5=96$ and $n=2^6=64$, and $m-n=\boxed{32}$.
