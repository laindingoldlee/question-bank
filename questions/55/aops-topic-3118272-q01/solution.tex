Write the random digit in three-bit binary form, allowing leading zeros; interpret $1$ as heads and $0$ as tails. The eight equally likely binary strings correspond exactly to the eight coin outcomes. The head-count probabilities are $\dfrac{1}{8},\dfrac{3}{8},\dfrac{3}{8},\dfrac{1}{8}$, because the counts are $\binom30,\binom31,\binom32,\binom33$. Multiplying by $40$ gives expected counts $\boxed{5,15,15,5}$. The expected bar chart has these heights at $0,1,2,3$ heads. Actual simulated counts vary; they are not required to equal expectations.
