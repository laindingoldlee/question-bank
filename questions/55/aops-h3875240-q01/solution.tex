Let $\zeta=e^{2\pi \dfrac{i}{13}}$, with $A_j$ represented by $\zeta^j$. Factoring $\dfrac{(z^{13}-1)}{(z-1)}$ and setting $z=1$ gives
\[
\prod_{j=1}^{12}(1-\zeta^j)=13.
\]
Taking absolute values gives the product of all twelve chord lengths from $A_0$. The lengths for $j$ and $13-j$ agree, so this product is exactly $P^2$. Therefore $\boxed{P^2=13}$.
