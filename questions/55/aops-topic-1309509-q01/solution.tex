Let $u=\sqrt{x+\sqrt{x+11}}$ and $v=\sqrt{x-\sqrt{x+11}}$. Then $u+v=4$ and $u^2-v^2=2\sqrt{x+11}$, so $u-v=\dfrac{\sqrt{x+11}}{2}$. Hence $u=2+\dfrac{\sqrt{x+11}}{4}$, $v=2-\dfrac{\sqrt{x+11}}{4}$, and
\[
u^2+v^2=8+\dfrac{x+11}{8}=2x\quad\Longrightarrow\quad64+x+11=16x\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x=5 .
\]
Check: $\sqrt{16}=4$, so the left side is $\sqrt9+\sqrt1=4$. The solution is $\boxed{x=5}$.
