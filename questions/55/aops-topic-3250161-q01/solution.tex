For $f=0$, the numerator must vanish, so $\boxed{a=e}$. For $f=1$, we need $a-e=s$. Together with $a+e\le s$, this gives $2e\le0$, hence $e=0,a=s$: every serve is an ace. For $f=-1$, similarly $e-a=s$ forces $a=0,e=s$: every serve is an error. Thus the extreme efficiencies occur precisely in those two all-one-type cases.
