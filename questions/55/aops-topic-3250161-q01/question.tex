A player has $a$ aces and $e$ service errors among $s>0$ serves, with $a,e$ nonnegative integers and $a+e\le s$. Ace efficiency is $f=\dfrac{a-e}{s}$. Characterize when $f=0$, $f=1$, or $f=-1$.
