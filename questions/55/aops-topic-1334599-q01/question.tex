Let $S=\{1,2,3,\dots,2016\}$. A subset $A$ of $S$ is chosen uniformly at random. Then a subset $B$ of $S\setminus A$ is chosen uniformly at random (so $A\cap B=\varnothing$). Next, subsets $C\subseteq A$ and $D\subseteq B$ are chosen uniformly at random, and $E=C\cup D$.

Two solutions are proposed for the expected number of elements of $E$:
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Solution 1.} $A$ has $1008$ elements on average, so $B$ has $504$, $C$ has $504$ and $D$ has $252$, giving $504+252=756$.
\item \emph{Solution 2.} Each element is in exactly one of the five states: only in $S$; in $A$ but not $C$; in $B$ but not $D$; in $C$ (so in $A$ and $E$); in $D$ (so in $B$ and $E$). Two of the five states put the element in $E$, so the answer is $\dfrac{2}{5}\cdot2016=806.4$.
\end{itemize}
Which solution is correct? Find the error in the other.
