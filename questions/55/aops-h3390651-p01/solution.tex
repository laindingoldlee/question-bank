Repeated difference-of-squares factorization gives
\[
3^{1024}-1=(3^2-1)\prod_{j=1}^{9}(3^{2^j}+1).
\]
The first factor is $8$. For every $j\ge1$, $3^{2^j}\equiv1\pmod8$, so each of the nine remaining factors is congruent to $2\pmod8$ and contains exactly one factor of $2$. Hence the exact power is $2^{3+9}$ and $\boxed{n=12}$.
