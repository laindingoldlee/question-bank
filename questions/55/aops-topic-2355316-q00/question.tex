A board is a rectangle of $10$ columns and $9$ rows of unit squares. Three apples are placed on three different squares, with all choices of three squares equally likely. Two apples are \emph{next to each other} if their squares share a side. What is the probability that exactly two of the apples are next to each other, that is, exactly one pair of apples is next to each other?
