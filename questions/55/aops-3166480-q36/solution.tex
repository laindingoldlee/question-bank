If $k$ chords are drawn, choose their $2k$ endpoints in $\binom{30}{2k}$ ways. Noncrossing perfect matchings of those endpoints are counted by the Catalan number $C_k=\dfrac{1}{k+1}\binom{2k}{k}$. One derivation pairs the first endpoint with an odd-numbered position, splitting the circle into two independent matchings and giving $C_k=\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}C_jC_{k-1-j}$, $C_0=1$. Thus the answer is $\boxed{\sum_{k=0}^{15}\binom{30}{2k}\dfrac{1}{k+1}\binom{2k}{k}=1697385471211}$.
