Rationalise each term with the conjugate of its denominator.
\[
\dfrac{6}{2\sqrt3-\sqrt6}=\dfrac{6\left(2\sqrt3+\sqrt6\right)}{12-6}=2\sqrt3+\sqrt6,\qquad
\dfrac{\sqrt6}{\sqrt3+\sqrt2}=\dfrac{\sqrt6\left(\sqrt3-\sqrt2\right)}{3-2}=3\sqrt2-2\sqrt3,
\]
\[
\dfrac{4\sqrt3}{\sqrt6-\sqrt2}=\dfrac{4\sqrt3\left(\sqrt6+\sqrt2\right)}{6-2}=3\sqrt2+\sqrt6 .
\]
So the expression equals $\left(2\sqrt3+\sqrt6\right)+\left(3\sqrt2-2\sqrt3\right)-\left(3\sqrt2+\sqrt6\right)=\boxed{0}$.
