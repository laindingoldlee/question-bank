Let $N=10^{888}$ and $R=\dfrac{N-1}{9}=\underbrace{11\ldots1}_{888}$. Then $A=3R$ and $B=6R$, so $AB=18R^2=2R\cdot9R=2R(N-1)=2RN-2R$. In digits:
\[
AB=\underbrace{22\ldots2}_{888}\underbrace{00\ldots0}_{888}-\underbrace{22\ldots2}_{888}=\boxed{\underbrace{22\ldots2}_{887}1\underbrace{77\ldots7}_{887}8}.
\]
The subtraction: the last $888$ digits are $10^{888}-\underbrace{22\ldots2}_{888}=\underbrace{77\ldots7}_{887}8$, with a borrow that turns the last $2$ of the first block into $1$. The pattern matches small cases: $3\cdot6=18$, $33\cdot66=2178$, $333\cdot666=221778$. The product has $1776$ digits.
