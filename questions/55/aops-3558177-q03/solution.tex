Let $v_t(j)$ count safe length-$t$ strings ending in $j$. Initially $v_1(j)=1$, and $v_{t+1}(j)=\sum_{i:|i-j|\ne2}v_t(i)$. Applying this recurrence seven times counts all safe strings without overlap. Their total is $312768$, out of $6^8=1679616$. Thus the requested probability is $1-\dfrac{312768}{1679616}=\dfrac{791}{972}\approx\boxed{0.814}$.
