A figure consists of five equal squares in the form of a cross: a central square with one square attached to each of its four sides.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
path cr=(0,-1)--(1,-1)--(1,0)--(2,0)--(2,1)--(1,1)--(1,2)--(0,2)--(0,1)--(-1,1)--(-1,0)--(0,0)--cycle;
fill(cr,rgb(0.88,0.92,1));
draw((0,0)--(1,0)--(1,1)--(0,1)--cycle,gray);
draw(cr,linewidth(1));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Show how to divide the cross by two straight cuts into four pieces that can be fitted together to form a square.
