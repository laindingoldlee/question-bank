Let $u=13x+4y$ and $v=29x+9y$. Every common divisor of $x,y$ divides both $u,v$. Conversely,
\[
x=9u-4v,\qquad y=-29u+13v,
\]
so every common divisor of $u,v$ divides both $x,y$. Their sets of common divisors agree, proving the equality of their nonnegative greatest common divisors. This also covers $(x,y)=(0,0)$ under the convention $\gcd(0,0)=0$.
