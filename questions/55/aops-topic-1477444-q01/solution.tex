From $6x-6=2x+14$ we get $x=5$, so the square has side $24$. Every triangle of the net is equilateral with side $24$, so its area is $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}\cdot24^2=144\sqrt3$ (base $24$, height $12\sqrt3$). The surface area of the pyramid is the area of the net:
\[
24^2+4\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}\cdot24^2=24^2\left(1+\sqrt3\right).
\]
So $a=24$, $b=1$, $c=3$ and $a+b+c=\boxed{28}$.

\begin{asy}

real s=3; real h=s*sqrt(3)/2;
pair A=(0,0),B=(s,0),C=(s,s),D=(0,s);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(A--(s/2,-h)--B,linewidth(0.9)); draw(B--(s+h,s/2)--C,linewidth(0.9)); draw(C--(s/2,s+h)--D,linewidth(0.9)); draw(D--(-h,s/2)--A,linewidth(0.9));
label("$24$",(s/2,0),N); label(rotate(90)*"$24$",(0,s/2),E); label("$24$",(s/2,-h)+(B-(s/2,-h))/2,SE); label("$24$",(s/2,-h)+(A-(s/2,-h))/2,SW);

\end{asy}
