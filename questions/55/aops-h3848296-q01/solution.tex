If two variables are equal, the displayed equalities force the third equal as well. Thus under the hypothesis all three are distinct. Subtraction gives
\[
a-b=\dfrac{b-c}{bc},\quad
b-c=\dfrac{c-a}{ca},\quad
c-a=\dfrac{a-b}{ab}.
\]
Multiplying and canceling the nonzero product of differences yields $\boxed{(abc)^2=1}$. For example $(a,b,c)=(2,-1,\dfrac{1}{2})$ makes each common expression equal to $1$ and has $(abc)^2=1$.
