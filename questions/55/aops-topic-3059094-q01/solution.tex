Amanda wins a turn with probability $\dfrac{2}{3}$, and a complete pair of turns has no winner with probability $\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}=\dfrac{2}{9}$. Amanda has at most five turns. Thus her win probability is \[\dfrac{2}{3}\sum_{j=0}^4\left(\dfrac{2}{9}\right)^j=\dfrac{6}{7}\left(1-\dfrac{32}{59049}\right)=\boxed{\dfrac{16862}{19683}}.\]
