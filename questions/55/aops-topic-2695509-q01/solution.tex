The pairs $\{1,19\},\{2,18\},\dots,\{9,11\}$ each allow $3$ choices (neither, or one of the two), and $10$ and $20$ are free (since $10+10$ uses a single element). So $N=3^9\cdot2^2=78732$, and the remainder is $\boxed{2}$.
