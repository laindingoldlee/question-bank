The opponent's seventh attack would defeat $H$, so $H$ wins exactly when its first seven attacks remove at least all opponent health. Measured in tenths, the total potential damage in seven attacks is $7+X$, where $X$ is the number of stronger attacks. Thus victory is equivalent to $X\ge3$, with $X$ binomial with parameters $7,\dfrac{1}{2}$. Pre-generating all seven attacks is valid even if the game ends earlier. The probability is $1-\dfrac{\binom70+\binom71+\binom72}{2^7}=1-\dfrac{29}{128}=\boxed{\dfrac{99}{128}}$.
