Let $p(x)=-7x^4+2x^3-8$. Then
\[
p'(x)=-28x^3+6x^2=2x^2(3-14x).
\]
So $p'(x)\ge0$ for $x\le\dfrac{3}{14}$ and $p'(x)\le0$ for $x\ge\dfrac{3}{14}$: $p$ increases up to $x=\dfrac{3}{14}$ and decreases after it (the critical point $x=0$ is not an extremum, since $p'$ does not change sign there). Since $p(x)\to-\infty$ as $|x|\to\infty$, the maximum is
\[
p\!\left(\dfrac{3}{14}\right)=x^3(2-7x)-8\Big|_{x=\dfrac{3}{14}}=\dfrac{27}{2744}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}-8=\dfrac{27}{5488}-8=\boxed{-\dfrac{43877}{5488}} .
\]
(Here $2-7\cdot\dfrac{3}{14}=2-\dfrac{3}{2}=\dfrac{1}{2}$.) Numerically the maximum is about $-7.9951$.
