``If a number is a multiple of $12$, then it is a multiple of $4$.'' The hypothesis is ``$n$ is a multiple of $12$''; the conclusion is ``$n$ is a multiple of $4$.'' The statement is true ($12=4\cdot3$). The converse is false: $8$ is a multiple of $4$ but not of $12$.
