For chord endpoints $A,B$ and centre $O$, the triangle inequality gives $AB\le AO+OB=2r$. Equality holds exactly when $A,O,B$ are collinear with $O$ between the endpoints, that is, when $AB$ is a diameter.
\begin{asy}
import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
pair O=(0,0),A=dir(150),B=dir(20);draw(unitcircle);draw(A--B);draw(A--O--B,dashed);draw((-1,0)--(1,0),blue);label("$O$",O,(0,-1));label("$A$",A,(-1,1));label("$B$",B,(1,1));label("$r$",A/2,(0,-1));label("$r$",B/2,(0,-1));
\end{asy}
