The number of goals is binomial with $n=6$, $p=\dfrac{1}{4}$:
\[P(\ge4)=\dbinom64\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^4\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^2+\dbinom65\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^5\dfrac{3}{4}+\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^6=\dfrac{15\cdot9+6\cdot3+1}{4^6}=\dfrac{154}{4096}=\boxed{\dfrac{77}{2048}}.\]
