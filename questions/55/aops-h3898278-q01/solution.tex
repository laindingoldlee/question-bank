Factor as $(p^2-1)(p^2+2)$. An odd square is $1$ modulo $8$, so the first factor is divisible by $8$. Since $p$ is not divisible by $3$, $p^2\equiv1\pmod3$, making both factors divisible by $3$. Hence every value is divisible by $72$. For $p=5,7$, the values are $648=72\cdot9$ and $2448=72\cdot34$, whose gcd is $72$. Therefore no larger common divisor is possible, and the answer is $\boxed{72}$.
