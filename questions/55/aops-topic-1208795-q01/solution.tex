Let the numbers be $a,b$, with $s=a+b$ and $p=ab$. Then $\dfrac{1}{a}+\dfrac{1}{b}=\dfrac {s}{p}=2$, so $s=2p$, and $a^2+b^2=s^2-2p=20$. Hence $4p^2-2p-20=0$, i.e. $2p^2-p-10=0=(2p-5)(p+2)$. Since the numbers are positive, $p=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{2}}$ (and then $s=5$, and $t^2-5t+\dfrac{5}{2}=0$ has two positive real roots).
