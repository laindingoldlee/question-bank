$|x-4|\le3$ means $1\le x\le7$; $|x-4|\ge1$ means $x\le3$ or $x\ge5$. Together: $\boxed{1\le x\le3\ \text{or}\ 5\le x\le7}$. (Geometrically: points whose distance from $4$ is between $1$ and $3$.)
