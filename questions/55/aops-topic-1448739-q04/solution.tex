No root is $0$, since the constant term is $4$. By Vieta's formulas, the sum of the products of the roots taken six at a time is $(-1)^6\cdot\dfrac{5}{6}$, and the product of all seven roots is $(-1)^7\cdot\dfrac{4}{6}$. Hence
\[
\sum\dfrac{1}{p}=\dfrac{\text{(sum of products of six roots)}}{pqrstuv}=\dfrac{\dfrac{5}{6}}{-\dfrac{4}{6}}=\boxed{-\dfrac{5}{4}}.
\]
(Equivalently, the reciprocals are the roots of the reversed polynomial $4x^7+5x^6+8x^5+\dots+6$, whose root sum is $-\dfrac{5}{4}$.)
