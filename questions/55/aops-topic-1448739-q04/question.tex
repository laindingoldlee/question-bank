Let $p,q,r,s,t,u,v$ be the seven (complex) roots of
\[
6x^7+4x^6+3x^5+x^4+2x^3+8x^2+5x+4 .
\]
Find $\dfrac{1}{p}+\dfrac{1}{q}+\dfrac{1}{r}+\dfrac{1}{s}+\dfrac{1}{t}+\dfrac{1}{u}+\dfrac{1}{v}$.
