Jack and Max are on an $8\times8$ grid of dots (coordinates $0$ to $7$). Jack starts at $(0,0)$ and each second moves one unit up or one unit right, each with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$; Max starts at $(7,7)$ and each second moves one unit down or one unit left, each with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. What is the probability that they meet at a dot?
