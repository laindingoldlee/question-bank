Write $c_n = \dfrac{2\cdot4\cdots2n}{1\cdot3\cdots(2n+1)}$, with $c_0=1$. Then $(2n+1)c_n=2nc_{n-1}$, and the convergent power series satisfies $(1-x^2)f^{\prime}(x)-xf(x)=1$, $f(0)=0$. Multiplying by the integrating factor $\sqrt{1-x^2}$ gives $(\sqrt{1-x^2}f(x))^{\prime} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}$. Integrating yields $f (x) = \dfrac{\arcsin x}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}$. Therefore $\boxed{f (\dfrac{1}{2}) = \dfrac{\pi}{3\sqrt3}}$, so $a+b=\boxed6$.
