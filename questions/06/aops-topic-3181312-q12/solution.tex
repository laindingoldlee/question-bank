For any position, the place value is coprime to 7. Thus two eligible replacements differ by 7. A tens or units digit is unique among 0 through 9 exactly when it lies in $\{3,4,5,6\}$. A hundreds digit is unique among 1 through 9 exactly when it lies in $\{3,4,5,6,7\}$. Checking the resulting $5\cdot4\cdot4=80$ candidates for divisibility by 7 gives 336,343,364,434,455,546,553,644,665,735,756,763. Hence there are $\boxed{12}$.
