Let $p$ be the probability that the player whose turn is starting wins. That player wins the current run with probability $\dfrac{1}{8}$; otherwise a tail occurs first and gives the opponent a fresh turn. Thus $p=\dfrac{1}{8}+\dfrac{7}{8}(1-p)$, yielding $\boxed{p=\dfrac{8}{15}}$. This is Elmo's winning probability, hence Cookie Monster's losing probability.
