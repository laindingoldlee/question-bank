For $k=1,\ldots,20$, surviving the first $k-1$ years has probability $\dfrac{19}{20}\cdot\dfrac{18}{19}\cdots\dfrac{21-k}{22-k}=\dfrac{21-k}{20}$, with an empty product for $k=1$. The probability of erupting in year $k$ is then $\dfrac{21-k}{20}\cdot\dfrac{1}{21-k}=\dfrac{1}{20}$. Thus the waiting time is uniform on $1,\ldots,20$, and its expectation is $\boxed{\dfrac{21}{2}}$ years.
