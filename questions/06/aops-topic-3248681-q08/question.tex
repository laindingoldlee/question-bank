A person takes $65$ seconds to traverse a $30$-metre staircase. At the same walking speed relative to an escalator, the same distance takes $25$ seconds. How long does it take when standing still on the escalator? Write the reduced fraction $\dfrac{u}{v}$ and find $u+v$.
