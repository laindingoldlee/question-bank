A solution determines one residue triple, and the triple uniquely determines $x\pmod{30}$ because $30$ is the lcm of the three moduli. Thus exactly $30$ distinct residue triples are attainable, one for each $x=0,\ldots,29$. There are $6\cdot10\cdot15=900$ equally likely triples, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{30}}$.
