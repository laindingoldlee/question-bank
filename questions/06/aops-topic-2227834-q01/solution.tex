Jonas paints $\dfrac{64}{16}=4$ square inches per minute and Ricky $\dfrac{100}{20}=5$ square inches per minute, so together they paint $9$ square inches per minute. The wall has area $144$ square inches, so they need $\dfrac{144}{9}=\boxed{16}$ minutes.
