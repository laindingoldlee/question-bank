The red and blue marbles make up $1-\dfrac{1}{3}=\dfrac{2}{3}$ of the bag, and there are $78+24=102$ of them. So the bag contains $\dfrac{3}{2}\cdot102=153$ marbles, and
\[P(\text{red})=\dfrac{78}{153}=\boxed{\dfrac{26}{51}}.\]
