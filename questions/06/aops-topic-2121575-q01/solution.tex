Since $\left(\sqrt2-1\right)\left(\sqrt2+1\right)=2-1=1$, we have $\sqrt2-1=\left(\sqrt2+1\right)^{-1}$. Therefore
\[\left(\sqrt2-1\right)^{1-\sqrt3}=\left(\sqrt2+1\right)^{-(1-\sqrt3)}=\left(\sqrt2+1\right)^{\sqrt3-1},\]
and the whole expression is
\[\left(\sqrt2+1\right)^{(\sqrt3-1)-(1+\sqrt3)}=\left(\sqrt2+1\right)^{-2}=\left(\sqrt2-1\right)^2=\boxed{3-2\sqrt2}.\]
The irrational exponents disappear because both powers have the same base $\sqrt2+1$ once $\sqrt2-1$ is rewritten as its reciprocal.
