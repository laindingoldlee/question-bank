The four original centres form a regular tetrahedron of side 2. Put its vertices at $\dfrac{(\pm1,\pm1,\pm1)}{\sqrt2}$, choosing the four sign patterns with positive sign product. Their mutual distances are 2 and their distance from the origin is $\sqrt{\dfrac{3}{2}}$. A point equidistant from all four vertices is uniquely their circumcentre, so the small sphere has radius $\sqrt{\dfrac{3}{2}}-1$. Its diameter is $\sqrt6-2$, giving $\boxed{a+b=8}$.
