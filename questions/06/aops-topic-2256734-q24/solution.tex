Let the solutions be $r\ne s$, positive integers. By Vieta's formulas $b=r+s$ and $c=rs$, so
\[r+s+rs=80\iff(r+1)(s+1)=81.\]
Both factors are at least $2$, so $\{r+1,s+1\}$ is $\{3,27\}$ or $\{9,9\}$; the latter gives $r=s$. So $\{r,s\}=\{2,26\}$, $b=28$, $c=52$ and $bc=1456$, which leaves remainder $\boxed{\textbf{(B) }1}$ on division by $5$.
