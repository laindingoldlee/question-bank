From October $27$, $2020$ to October $27$, $2021$ there are $365$ days. Numbering the following days $d=1,\dots,365$, he publishes on day $d$ when $14\mid d$, $21\mid d$ or $35\mid d$. By inclusion--exclusion (using $\operatorname{lcm}(14,21)=42$, $\operatorname{lcm}(14,35)=70$, $\operatorname{lcm}(21,35)=105$, $\operatorname{lcm}(14,21,35)=210$):
\[\left\lfloor\dfrac{365}{14}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{365}{21}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{365}{35}\right\rfloor-\left\lfloor\dfrac{365}{42}\right\rfloor-\left\lfloor\dfrac{365}{70}\right\rfloor-\left\lfloor\dfrac{365}{105}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{365}{210}\right\rfloor\]
\[=26+17+10-8-5-3+1=\boxed{38}.\]
