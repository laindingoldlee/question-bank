Use the formula $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{n}k^4=\dfrac{n(n+1)(2n+1)\left(3n^2+3n-1\right)}{30}$ (which can be proved by induction or by summing $(k+1)^5-k^5$). With $n=100$:
\[\dfrac{100\cdot101\cdot201\cdot30299}{30}=\boxed{2050333330}.\]
