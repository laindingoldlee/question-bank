A $3$-digit palindrome is $\overline{aba}$ with $1\le a\le9$ and $0\le b\le9$, and its digit sum is $2a+b=9$. Then $b=9-2a\ge0$ forces $a\le4$, and every $a=1,2,3,4$ works: $171$, $252$, $333$, $414$. The answer is $\boxed{4}$.
