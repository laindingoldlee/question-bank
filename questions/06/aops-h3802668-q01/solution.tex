(a) The probability of receiving none is $(1-\dfrac{1}{k})^n$, so the answer is $\boxed{1-(1-\dfrac{1}{k})^n}$. (b) Stars and bars gives $\binom{n+k-1}{k-1}$ allocations in total and $\binom{n+k-2}{k-2}$ giving this student none. Subtracting their ratio from one gives $\boxed{\dfrac{n}{n+k-1}}$. The two answers differ because independent handouts do not make all allocation vectors equally likely.
