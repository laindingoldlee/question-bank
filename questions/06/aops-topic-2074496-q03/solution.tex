Because \((\sqrt2-1)(\sqrt2+1)=1\), we have \(\sqrt2-1=(\sqrt2+1)^{-1}\). Hence the expression equals
\[
(\sqrt2+1)^{-(1-\sqrt x)-(1+\sqrt x)}=(\sqrt2+1)^{-2}=(\sqrt2-1)^2=\boxed{3-2\sqrt2}.
\]
