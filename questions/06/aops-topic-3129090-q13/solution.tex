Remaining area is $(36-9\pi)r^2$. Dividing by $\pi r^2$ gives $\dfrac{36}{\pi}-9$, which lies strictly between 2 and 3 since $3<\pi<\dfrac{36}{11}$. Thus $\boxed2$ whole additional disks can be made.

\end{asy}
