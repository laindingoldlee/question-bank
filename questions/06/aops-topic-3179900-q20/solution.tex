Let $BC=x$. The stated correspondences give $AB=2\sqrt3x$ and $CD=\dfrac{x}{2\sqrt3}$. The area equation is $\dfrac{1}{2}(AB+CD)x=\dfrac{13\sqrt3}{12}x^2=49\sqrt3$, so $x^2=\dfrac{588}{13}$. The side $AD$ joins the tops of perpendicular segments $AB,CD$, hence $AD^2=x^2+(AB-CD)^2=x^2(1+\dfrac{121}{12})=\dfrac{6517}{13}$. Therefore $\boxed{a+b=6530}$.
