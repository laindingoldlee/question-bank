Convex quadrilateral $ABCD$ has $\angle ABC=\angle BCD=90^\circ$ and area $49\sqrt3$. An interior point $E$ satisfies the ordered similarities $\triangle ABC\sim\triangle BCD\sim\triangle BEC$ and $\dfrac{BE}{CE}=2\sqrt3$. Express $AD^2=\dfrac{a}{b}$ in lowest terms and find $a+b$.
