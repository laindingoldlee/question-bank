Write the integer as $10k+5$. Then $(10k+5)^2=100k^2+100k+25=100k(k+1)+25$. Thus multiply $k$ by $k+1$ and append the two digits $25$. For $95$, $k=9$ and $9\cdot10=90$, so $\boxed{95^2=9025}$.
