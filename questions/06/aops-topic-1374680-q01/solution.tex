The first gear makes one turn in $60\div\dfrac{100}{3}=\dfrac{9}{5}$ seconds, and the second in $\dfrac{60}{45}=\dfrac{4}{3}$ seconds. The first mark points north at times $\dfrac{9}{5}k$, and the second at times $\dfrac{4}{3}m$ ($k,m$ nonnegative integers). We need the least positive $t$ with
\[
t=\dfrac{9k}{5}=\dfrac{4m}{3}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad27k=20m .
\]
Since $\gcd(27,20)=1$, $k$ must be a multiple of $20$; the least choice $k=20$ gives $t=\dfrac{9\cdot20}{5}=\boxed{36}$ seconds. (In $36$ seconds the gears make $20$ and $27$ turns.)
