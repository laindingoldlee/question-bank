Count the complement. Write the numbers $0$ to $599$ with three digits (leading zeros allowed). Those with no digit $5$ have hundreds digit in $\{0,1,2,3,4\}$ ($5$ choices) and tens and units digits from the nine digits other than $5$: $5\cdot9\cdot9=405$ numbers, including $000$. So $404$ of the numbers $1$ to $599$ have no $5$, and so does $600$: $405$ numbers from $1$ to $600$ have no $5$. The answer is $600-405=\boxed{195}$.
