Let $T$ be the team's total. Alexa's $\dfrac{T}{4}$ and Brittany's $\dfrac{2T}{7}$ are whole numbers, so $T$ is a multiple of $28$; write $T=28k$. The other seven players scored
\[x=T-\dfrac{T}{4}-\dfrac{2T}{7}-15=\dfrac{13T}{28}-15=13k-15\]
points, and $0\le x\le7\cdot2=14$. So $15\le13k\le29$, which forces $k=2$ ($k=1$ gives $x<0$ and $k=3$ gives $x=24>14$). Hence $T=56$ and
\[x=13\cdot2-15=\boxed{11}.\]
(Check: $14+16+15+11=56$.)
