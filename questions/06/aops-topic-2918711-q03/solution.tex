Each vertex lies on three mutually adjacent faces, one from each opposite pair $\{1,6\},\{2,5\},\{3,4\}$, so its number is a multiple of the lcm of those three face numbers. Taking each vertex equal to that lcm gives
\[6,4,15,20\ (\text{with }1),\quad6,12,30,60\ (\text{with }6),\]
with sum $153$. Check the gcds: face $1$: $\gcd(6,4,15,20)=1$; face $6$: $\gcd(6,12,30,60)=6$; face $2$: $\gcd(6,4,6,12)=2$; face $5$: $\gcd(15,20,30,60)=5$; face $3$: $\gcd(6,15,6,30)=3$; face $4$: $\gcd(4,20,12,60)=4$.
