(a) $c=b+1$ gives $a^2=(b+1)^2-b^2=2b+1$, so $a$ is odd and $b=\dfrac{a^2-1}2$; $b\ge1$ needs $a\ge3$. Conversely, any odd $a\ge3$ with this $b$ and $c=b+1$ satisfies $a^2+b^2=c^2$.

(b) $c=b+2$ gives $a^2=4b+4$, so $a$ is even and $b=\dfrac{a^2-4}4$; $b\ge1$ needs $a\ge4$. Conversely these values work. $\blacksquare$
