Prove: (a) the Pythagorean triples $a^2+b^2=c^2$ (positive integers) with $c=b+1$ are exactly those with $a$ odd, $a\ge3$, $b=\dfrac{a^2-1}2$; (b) those with $c=b+2$ are exactly those with $a$ even, $a\ge4$, $b=\dfrac{a^2-4}4$.
