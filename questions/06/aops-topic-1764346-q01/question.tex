Let $x$ and $y$ be distinct positive integers such that $x\mid y$, $x+1\mid y+1$ and $x+2\mid y+2$. Given that one of $x$, $x+1$, $x+2$ is divisible by $7$, what is the sum of the digits of the smallest possible value of $y$?
