Normally she wins with probability $\dfrac45\cdot\dfrac35+\dfrac15\cdot\dfrac25=\dfrac{14}{25}$, so she wins and stays connected with probability $\dfrac{14}{25}\cdot\dfrac34=\dfrac{21}{50}$. After the hack this becomes $\dfrac35\cdot\dfrac34=\dfrac9{20}$. The change is
\[\dfrac9{20}-\dfrac{21}{50}=\dfrac{45-42}{100}=\dfrac3{100},\]
an \emph{increase} of $\boxed{\dfrac3{100}}$ (from $42\%$ to $45\%$).
