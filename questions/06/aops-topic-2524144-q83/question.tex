In a video game, Julie is the impostor $\dfrac15$ of the time. When she is not the impostor she wins with probability $\dfrac35$, and when she is the impostor she wins with probability $\dfrac25$. Whenever she wins, there is a $\dfrac14$ chance that she then disconnects. If Julie hacks the game so that she is never the impostor, by how much does the probability that she wins and does not disconnect change?
