If every card has the same pair of values, alternate the two values around the even cycle. Otherwise two neighbouring cards have different two-element value sets. Relabel the circle so these are cards 1 and 100, and choose for card 1 a value absent from card 100. For cards 2 through 100 in order, choose any of their two values different from the preceding displayed value; this is always possible. The final value also differs from card 1 because card 1's chosen value is absent from card 100. Thus every neighbouring pair differs.
