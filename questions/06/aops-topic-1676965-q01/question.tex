A change machine is broken. If you put in a penny, it gives back one coin at random: a penny, a nickel, a dime or a quarter, each with probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$, independently of earlier uses. You have one penny and you keep reinserting every penny the machine gives you, stopping when the machine gives you a nickel, dime or quarter. What is the expected value, in cents, of the coin you end up with? What is your expected profit?
