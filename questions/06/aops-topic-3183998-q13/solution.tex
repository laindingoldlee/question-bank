The even triple is one of $(8,6,4),(6,4,2),(4,2,0)$; the odd quadruple is $(9,7,5,3)$ or $(7,5,3,1)$. Their complementary three digits have sums respectively $3,11$ for the first even triple, $9,17$ for the second, and $15,23$ for the third. Only even triple $(6,4,2)$ with odd quadruple $(9,7,5,3)$ leaves digit sum $9$. The remaining digits are $8,1,0$, so $\boxed{A=8}$.
