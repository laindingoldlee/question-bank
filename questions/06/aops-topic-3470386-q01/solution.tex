The probability of two reds is $\dfrac{1}{2}\dfrac{3}{7}\dfrac{2}{6}+\dfrac{1}{2}\dfrac{4}{7}\dfrac{3}{6}=\dfrac{3}{14}$. The probability the first is red is $\dfrac{1}{2}\dfrac{3}{7}+\dfrac{1}{2}\dfrac{4}{7}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Dividing gives $\boxed{\dfrac{3}{7}}$. Equivalently the first red updates the box probabilities to $\dfrac{3}{7}$ and $\dfrac{4}{7}$.
