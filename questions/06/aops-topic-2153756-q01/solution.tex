The condition says that $13$ divides $x^2-9=(x-3)(x+3)$. Since $13$ is prime, it divides $x-3$ or $x+3$, so
\[x\equiv3\pmod{13}\qquad\text{or}\qquad x\equiv-3\equiv10\pmod{13}.\]
The numbers greater than $31$ in these classes start $36,\ 42,\ 49,\dots$ (the class of $3$ gives $42,55,\dots$ and the class of $10$ gives $36,49,\dots$). The least is $\boxed{36}$. Check: $36^2=1296=13\cdot99+9$.
