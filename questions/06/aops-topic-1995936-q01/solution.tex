Let the ages be $a$ and $b$. Then $a+b=40$ and $a+10=2(b+10)$, i.e.\ $a=2b+10$. Substituting, $3b+10=40$, so $b=10$ and $a=30$. A is $\boxed{30}$ and B is $\boxed{10}$ years old. (Check: in $10$ years they are $40$ and $20$.)
