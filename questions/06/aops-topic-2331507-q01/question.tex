Two players $A$ and $B$ play a game with a running total that starts at $0$. Starting with $A$, they take turns adding $1$, $3$ or $4$ to the total and announcing the new total; the total may never exceed a fixed positive integer $n$. The player who announces $n$ wins. (For example, for $n=12$ the game could go $4,7,10,11,12$, and then $A$ wins.) For which $n$ does the second player $B$ have a winning strategy?
