The angle at $R$ is right. Let the legs be $u,v$. The inradius formula $r=\dfrac{u+v-c}{2}$ gives $u+v=18$, while $u^2+v^2=14^2=196$. Thus $2uv=18^2-196=128$, and the area is $\dfrac{uv}{2}=\boxed{32}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath"); size(220);
real u=9+sqrt(17),v=9-sqrt(17); pair R=(0,0),A=(u,0),Sp=(0,v),P=(A+Sp)/2,I=(2,2);draw(circle(P,7),gray);draw(A--R--Sp--cycle);draw(circle(I,2),blue);draw((0.5,0)--(0.5,0.5)--(0,0.5));dot(P);label("$P$",P,NE);label("$A$",A,SE);label("$R$",R,SW);label("$S$",Sp,NW);label("$I$",I,NE);draw(I--(2,0),dashed);label("$2$",(2,1),E);label("$14$",(A+Sp)/2,SW);
\end{asy}
