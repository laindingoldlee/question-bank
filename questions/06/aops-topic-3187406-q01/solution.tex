Let $T_n$ count subsets of $n$ drawers with no adjacent choices, including the empty subset. Whether the last drawer is closed or open gives $T_n=T_{n-1}+T_{n-2}$, with $T_0=1,T_1=2$. Thus $T_2=3,T_3=5,T_4=8,T_5=13$. Remove the empty choice to obtain $\boxed{12}$.
