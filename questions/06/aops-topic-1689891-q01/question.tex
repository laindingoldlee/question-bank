\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that the diagonals of a rectangle are equal in length and bisect each other.
\item[(b)] Deduce that in a right triangle the median to the hypotenuse is half the hypotenuse.
\item[(c)] A student argues: ``If the median to the hypotenuse is half the hypotenuse, then in a rectangle the two diagonals have the same length, and a quadrilateral with equal diagonals is a square.'' Find the error.
\end{enumerate}
