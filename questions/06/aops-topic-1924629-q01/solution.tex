After day $n$ he has met $1+2+\cdots+n=\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$ people. This is a multiple of $32$ exactly when $64\mid n(n+1)$. Of the consecutive integers $n$ and $n+1$ one is odd, so $64$ must divide the other one: $64\mid n$ or $64\mid n+1$. The smallest such $n$ is $63$ (then $\dfrac{63\cdot64}{2}=2016=32\cdot63$). So it happens first on day $\boxed{63}$.
