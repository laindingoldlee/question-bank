The process stops at six consecutive vegetable outcomes. Let $E_j$ be the expected further arrivals after a current run of $j$ vegetables. Then $E_6=0$ and $E_j=1+\dfrac{(E_{j+1}+E_0)}{2}$ for $j<6$. Back-substitution gives $E_0=2^{7}-2=126$. Each arrival independently receives vegetables with probability one half, so the expected number actually tricked with vegetables, including the final run, is $\dfrac{126}{2}=\boxed{63}$. If all arrivals are being counted regardless of outcome, that count is 126.
