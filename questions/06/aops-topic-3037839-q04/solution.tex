The denominators are positive, so cross-multiplication is valid: $ x(13-y)=y(15-x)$. The $ xy $ terms cancel, leaving $ 13x=15y $. Hence all solutions are $\boxed{(x,y)=(15t,13t),\quad0<t<1}$. Every such pair satisfies both the bounds and original proportion. There are infinitely many pairs, so the proportion alone does not determine the two lengths.
