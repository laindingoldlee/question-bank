The assignment is a permutation with no cycles of length 1 or 2. The allowed cycle-type partitions of 8 are $ 8 $, $ 5+3 $, and $ 4+4 $. Their counts are respectively $\dfrac{8!}{8}$, $\dfrac{8!}{5\cdot3}$, and $\dfrac{8!}{4^2\cdot2!}$. Adding gives $ 5040+2688+1260=\boxed{8988}$.
