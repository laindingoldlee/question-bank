An automatic machine sells paperclips. At a price of $c$ cents each, it sells $\left\lfloor\dfrac{100}{c}\right\rfloor$ paperclips per minute. It starts at a price of $1$ cent, and after every minute it increases the price by $1$ cent. What is the machine's total revenue, in cents?
