The domain requires $x>0$. Let the common logarithm be $t$. The equations are $3^t=2^x+1$ and $2^t=3^x-1$. Adding yields $3^t+2^t=3^x+2^x$. Since $u\mapsto3^u+2^u$ is strictly increasing, $t=x$. Thus $3^x-2^x=1$. On $x>0$ its derivative is positive, because $3^x\ln3>2^x\ln2$. The value $x=1$ satisfies it, so the unique answer is $\boxed1$.
