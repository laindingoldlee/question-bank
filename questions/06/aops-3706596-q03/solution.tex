There are $900$ possible quotients, $100\le q\le999$, and $100$ remainders. Among $r=0,\ldots,99$, residue zero modulo eleven occurs ten times and every other residue nine times. Thus each $q$ contributes nine choices, plus one extra if $11\mid q$. There are $\lfloor\dfrac{999}{11}\rfloor-\lfloor\dfrac{99}{11}\rfloor=81$ such quotients. The answer is $900\cdot9+81=\boxed{8181}$.
