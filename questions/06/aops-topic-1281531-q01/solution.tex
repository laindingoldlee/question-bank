Let $S=\sum_{n=1}^{100}\dfrac {n}{3^n}$. Then
\[
S-\dfrac {S}{3}=\sum_{n=1}^{100}\dfrac{1}{3^n}-\dfrac{100}{3^{101}}=\dfrac{1}{2}\left(1-\dfrac{1}{3^{100}}\right)-\dfrac{100}{3^{101}} .
\]
So
\[
S=\dfrac{3}{2}\left(\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{2\cdot3^{100}}-\dfrac{100}{3^{101}}\right)=\dfrac{3}{4}-\dfrac{3}{4\cdot3^{100}}-\dfrac{50}{3^{100}}=\dfrac{3}{4}-\dfrac{203}{4\cdot3^{100}} ,
\]
i.e.
\[
S=\boxed{\dfrac{3^{101}-203}{4\cdot3^{100}}}.
\]
