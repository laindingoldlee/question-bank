The dimes are worth $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of $\$1.80$, i.e.\ $60$ cents ($6$ dimes), and the nickels are worth $120$ cents ($24$ nickels). So Chad has $6+24=\boxed{30}$ coins. (The poster's $9$ dimes and $18$ nickels have twice as many nickels as dimes, but equal values, $90$ cents each: the condition is about the values, not the numbers of coins.)
