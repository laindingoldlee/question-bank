For $a\ge0$ all roots are of nonnegative numbers, and roots can be written as powers. Then
\[
\sqrt[3]{\sqrt[6]{a^9}}=\left(a^{\dfrac{9}{6}}\right)^{\dfrac{1}{3}}=a^{\dfrac{1}{2}},
\qquad
\sqrt[6]{\sqrt[3]{a^9}}=\left(a^{3}\right)^{\dfrac{1}{6}}=a^{\dfrac{1}{2}}.
\]
So the expression is
\[
\left(a^{\dfrac{1}{2}}\right)^4\left(a^{\dfrac{1}{2}}\right)^4=a^2\cdot a^2=\boxed{a^4}.
\]
