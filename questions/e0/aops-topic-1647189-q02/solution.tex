Since $1^0=1$, the $k$-th term is $\dfrac{k^2}{2^k+3^k-1}$:
\[
k=1:\ \dfrac{1}{2+3-1}=\dfrac{1}{4},\qquad k=2:\ \dfrac{4}{4+9-1}=\dfrac{1}{3},\qquad k=3:\ \dfrac{9}{8+27-1}=\dfrac{9}{34},\qquad k=4:\ \dfrac{16}{16+81-1}=\dfrac{1}{6} .
\]
Hence
\[
f(4)=\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{6}+\dfrac{9}{34}=\dfrac{3}{4}+\dfrac{9}{34}=\dfrac{51}{68}+\dfrac{18}{68}=\boxed{\dfrac{69}{68}}.
\]
