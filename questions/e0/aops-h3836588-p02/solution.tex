The denominator must be nonzero. Division gives
\[
\dfrac{x^2+7}{x+3}=x-3+\dfrac{16}{x+3}.
\]
Therefore $x+3$ must be one of the signed divisors $\pm1,\pm2,\pm4,\pm8,\pm16$. Subtracting $3$ gives
\[
\boxed{x\in\{-19,-11,-7,-5,-4,-2,-1,1,5,13\}}.
\]
Every listed value has a nonzero denominator and satisfies the condition.
