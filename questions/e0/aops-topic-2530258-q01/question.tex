A $100$-inch long trough has triangular ends with side lengths $6$ inches, $8$ inches and $10$ inches, with the $10$-inch side facing upwards. The trough is initially empty. On a rainy day, rain is captured by the entire $10$-inch by $100$-inch top surface and collects at the bottom of the trough. Rain falls at a rate of $0.06$ inches per hour for $10$ hours. What is the height of the water in the trough after the rain stops?
