Let $A_k$ be the set of multiples of $k$ in $\{1,\dots,300\}$, so $|A_k|=\left\lfloor\dfrac{300}{k}\right\rfloor$. By inclusion--exclusion,
\[
|A_3\cup A_4\cup A_5|=100+75+60-25-20-15+5=180 .
\]
The selected numbers not divisible by $5$ are the multiples of $3$ or $4$ that are not multiples of $5$:
\[
|A_3\cup A_4|-|A_{15}\cup A_{20}|=(100+75-25)-(20+15-5)=150-30=120 .
\]
So the probability is $\dfrac{120}{180}=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{3}}$.
