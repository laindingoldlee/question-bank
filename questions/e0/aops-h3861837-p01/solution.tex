The angles $CLA,ALE$ are $108^\circ,120^\circ$. If the polygon interiors lie on the same side of $LA$, their difference gives $12^\circ$. If on opposite sides, the smaller angle is $360^\circ-108^\circ-120^\circ=132^\circ$. Their sum is $\boxed{144^\circ}$.

\end{asy}
