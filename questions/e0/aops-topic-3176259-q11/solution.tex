Use $A=(-1,0)$, $B=(1,0)$, $C=(0,\sqrt3)$, so the circle is $x^2+y^2=1$. Tangency gives $D\cdot(C-D)=0$, hence $y_D=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}$ and $x_D=-\sqrt{\dfrac{2}{3}}$ for the point closer to $A$. The determinant area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\left|\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}-\sqrt3(1-\sqrt{\dfrac{2}{3}})\right|=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(-1,0),Bp=(1,0),C=(0,sqrt(3)),D=(-sqrt(2/3),1/sqrt(3));draw(circle((0,0),1));draw(A--Bp--C--cycle);draw(C--D--A,blue);label("$A$",A,W);label("$B$",Bp,E);label("$C$",C,N);label("$D$",D,NW);
\end{asy}
