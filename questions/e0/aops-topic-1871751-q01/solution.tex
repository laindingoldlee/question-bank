Both conditions say that $x$ is one less than a multiple: $x\equiv-1\pmod4$ and $x\equiv-1\pmod6$. So $x+1$ is a common multiple of $4$ and $6$, that is, a multiple of $\operatorname{lcm}(4,6)=12$. Conversely, every $x$ with $12\mid x+1$ satisfies both congruences. Hence the solutions are $x\equiv11\pmod{12}$, and the smallest positive one is $\boxed{11}$. (Check: $11=2\cdot4+3=6+5$.)
