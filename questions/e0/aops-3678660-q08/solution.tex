Polynomial division gives $x^2-1+\dfrac{21}{2x-7}$. For integrality, the odd integer $2x-7\ge-5$ must divide 21, hence it is $-3,-1,1,3,7,21$. The corresponding $x$ are $2,3,4,5,7,14$; the first two give negative values and the rest positive. The requested product is $4\cdot5\cdot7\cdot14=\boxed{1960}$.
