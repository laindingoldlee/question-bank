Distributivity gives $ab+a(-b)=a(b+(-b))=a\cdot0=0$. Thus $a(-b)$ is the additive inverse of $ab$, proving the claim. The identity $a\cdot0=0$ follows by cancelling one copy of $a\cdot0$ from $a(0+0)=a\cdot0+a\cdot0$.
