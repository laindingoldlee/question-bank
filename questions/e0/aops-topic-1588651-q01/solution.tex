Look for squares of the form $\left(\sqrt u\pm\sqrt v\right)^2=u+v\pm2\sqrt{uv}$ with $u+v=10$ and $2\sqrt{uv}=4\sqrt6$, i.e.\ $uv=24$: take $u=6$, $v=4$. Then
\[
10+4\sqrt6=\left(\sqrt6+2\right)^2,\qquad10-4\sqrt6=\left(\sqrt6-2\right)^2 .
\]
Both $\sqrt6+2$ and $\sqrt6-2$ are positive, so they are the square roots, and
\[
\sqrt{10+4\sqrt6}-\sqrt{10-4\sqrt6}=\left(\sqrt6+2\right)-\left(\sqrt6-2\right)=\boxed{4}.
\]
(Alternatively, the value $t$ is positive and $t^2=20-2\sqrt{100-96}=16$.)
