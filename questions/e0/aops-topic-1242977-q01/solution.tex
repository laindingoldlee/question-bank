Let $S$ be the number of visible stairs and let the escalator move $k$ stairs while the man takes one step. Going with it, he takes $60$ steps and the escalator contributes $60k$: $S=60+60k$. Going against it: $S=90-90k$. So $150k=30$, $k=\dfrac{1}{5}$, and $S=\boxed{72}$.
