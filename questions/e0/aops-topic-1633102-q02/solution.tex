$2^{11}=2048$. For $6^{14}$: $6^2=36$, $6^4=1296$, $6^7=6^4\cdot6^2\cdot6=1296\cdot216=279936$, and $6^{14}=279936^2=78364164096$. Hence
\[
2^{11}+6^{14}=2048+78364164096=\boxed{78364166144}.
\]
(As a check, $2^{11}+6^{14}=2^{11}\left(1+2^3\cdot3^{14}\right)=2048\cdot38263753$.)
