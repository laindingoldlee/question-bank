Let $A$'s cost be $x$. Then $B$ paid $1.2x$ and sold for $0.85\cdot1.2x=1.02x$. Selling at $1.02x$, $A$ would have gained $0.02x$, i.e. a \textbf{gain} of $\boxed{2\%}$.
