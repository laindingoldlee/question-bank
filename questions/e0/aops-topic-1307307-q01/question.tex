A coin of diameter $1$ inch is tossed onto an $8\times8$ board of $1$-inch square tiles, its centre landing uniformly at random on the board (the lines between tiles have no width). What is the probability that the coin lies entirely within one tile? Explain why the answer is $0$ even though such positions exist.
