Let $r$ be the radius. Since $OB\perp AB$ and $OC\perp AC$, triangles $ABO$ and $ACO$ are right-angled at $B$ and $C$. By the Pythagorean theorem,
\[AB^2=AO^2-OB^2=AO^2-r^2=AO^2-OC^2=AC^2.\]
Hence $AB=AC$, and triangle $ABC$ is isosceles. $\blacksquare$ (Equivalently, the two right triangles are congruent by RHS.)

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0);real d=1.9;
pair A=(-d,0.0);
pair B=(-1/d,sqrt(d^2-1)/d),C=(-1/d,-sqrt(d^2-1)/d);
draw(circle(O,1));
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
draw(O--B,dashed);draw(O--C,dashed);draw(O--A,dotted);
pair u=unit(O-B),v=unit(A-B);draw((B+0.12*u)--(B+0.12*u+0.12*v)--(B+0.12*v));
pair u2=unit(O-C),v2=unit(A-C);draw((C+0.12*u2)--(C+0.12*u2+0.12*v2)--(C+0.12*v2));
dot(O);dot(A);dot(B);dot(C);
label("$O$",O,E);label("$A$",A,W);label("$B$",B,N);label("$C$",C,S);label("$r$",(O+B)/2,NE,fontsize(9pt));label("$r$",(O+C)/2,SE,fontsize(9pt));
\end{asy}
