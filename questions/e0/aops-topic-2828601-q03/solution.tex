The angle opposite the leg $5$ has sine $\dfrac5{10}=\dfrac12$, so it is $30^\circ$; the other acute angle is $60^\circ$ and the other leg is $5\sqrt3$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair C=(0,0),A=(sqrt(75),0),B=(0,5);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw((0,0.5)--(0.5,0.5)--(0.5,0));
label("$5$",(0,2.5),W);label("$10$",(A+B)/2,NE);label("$30^\circ$",A,10*dir(165));label("$5\sqrt3$",(A.x/2,0),S);
\end{asy}

The smallest angle is $\boxed{30^\circ}$.
