The average $\dfrac{a+b}{2}$ is an integer exactly when $a+b$ is even, that is, when $a$ and $b$ have the same parity.

The argument is correct. Counting ordered pairs, the first number can be any of the $500$, and the second must be one of the other $249$ numbers of the same parity (there are $250$ odd and $250$ even numbers). Each unordered pair is obtained twice, once in each order, so the count is
\[\dfrac{500\cdot249}{2}=\boxed{62250}.\]
Equivalently, choose two odd numbers or two even numbers: $\dbinom{250}{2}+\dbinom{250}{2}=2\cdot31125=62250$.
