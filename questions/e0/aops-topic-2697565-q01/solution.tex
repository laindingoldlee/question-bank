$\angle CDB=\angle CAB=45^\circ$ (inscribed angles on arc $CB$), and $\angle BCD=90^\circ$ because $BD$ is a diameter. So triangle $BCD$ is an isosceles right triangle with $BC=CD=2$, $BD=2\sqrt2$, and radius $\sqrt2$. Then $\alpha=2\pi$, $100\alpha\approx628.3$, and the answer is $\boxed{628}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=dir(180),D=dir(0),C=dir(-90),A=dir(120);
draw(unitcircle,gray);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.8));draw(B--D,dashed);draw(A--C,dashed);
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,W);label("$C$",C,S);label("$D$",D,E);label("$2$",(C+D)/2,SE,fontsize(8pt));label("$45^\circ$",A+(0.05,-0.28),fontsize(6pt));
\end{asy}
