A $k$-face is obtained by choosing which $k$ coordinates may vary over $[0,1]$, then fixing every other coordinate at $0$ or $1$. There are
\[
\boxed{\binom nk2^{n-k}}
\]
such faces. Thus the requested counts are
\[
\boxed{2^{2026}},\quad
\boxed{2026\cdot2^{2025}},\quad
\boxed{\binom{2026}{2}2^{2024}},\quad
\boxed{\binom{2026}{3}2^{2023}}.
\]
The same coordinate-choice principle covers $k=0$ and $k=n$.

\end{asy}
