The number has $1$, $2$, $3$ or $4$ digits, and its last digit is $2$, $4$ or $6$.

\textbf{One digit:} $2,4,6$: $3$ numbers.

\textbf{Two digits:} $3$ choices for the last digit, then $6$ for the first: $18$.

\textbf{Three digits:} $3\cdot6\cdot5=90$.

\textbf{Four digits} (all are less than $6000$ exactly when the first digit is at most $5$). If the last digit is $6$, the first digit is any of $1,2,3,4,5$ ($5$ ways); if the last digit is $2$ or $4$, the first digit is one of the other four digits from $1$ to $5$ ($4$ ways each). The two middle digits are then chosen from the remaining $5$ digits in $5\cdot4=20$ ways:
\[
(5+4+4)\cdot20=260 .
\]

Total: $3+18+90+260=\boxed{371}$. (If only four-digit numbers are meant, the answer is $260$.)
