Let the octagon have horizontal sides on top and bottom. The cutting lines are horizontal through the left and right vertical sides' endpoints.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=4, d=s/sqrt(2); pair P0=(-s/2-d,-s/2),P1=(-s/2,-s/2-d),P2=(s/2,-s/2-d),P3=(s/2+d,-s/2),P4=(s/2+d,s/2),P5=(s/2,s/2+d),P6=(-s/2,s/2+d),P7=(-s/2-d,s/2);
fill(P7--P0--P3--P4--cycle,palegray); fill(P4--P5--P6--P7--cycle,palegray);
draw(P0--P1--P2--P3--P4--P5--P6--P7--cycle,linewidth(1)); draw(P0--P3); draw(P7--P4);
label("$4$",(P1+P2)/2,S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Each slanted side of length $4$ has horizontal and vertical projections $\dfrac{4}{\sqrt2}=2\sqrt2$. So the octagon is $4+4\sqrt2$ wide.
\begin{itemize}
\item Rectangle: $(4+4\sqrt2)\cdot4=16+16\sqrt2$.
\item Trapezoid: bases $4$ and $4+4\sqrt2$, height $2\sqrt2$: $\dfrac{8+4\sqrt2}{2}\cdot2\sqrt2=8+8\sqrt2$.
\end{itemize}
Shaded area $=16+16\sqrt2+8+8\sqrt2=\boxed{24+24\sqrt2}\approx57.9$. (Check: the whole octagon is $2(1+\sqrt2)\cdot4^2=32+32\sqrt2$.)
