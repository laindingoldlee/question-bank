Let $t$ be minutes after 12:00, with $0<t<720$. The hour and minute angles clockwise from 12 are $\dfrac{t}{2}$ and $6t$, modulo $360^\circ$. Equal angular distance from 6 means either equal angles or reflection about the 12--6 axis. Overlap is excluded, so the angle sum must be $0$ modulo $360^\circ$: $\dfrac{13t}{2}=360k$. Thus $\boxed{t=\dfrac{720k}{13}\text{ minutes},\quad k=1,\ldots,12}$. None overlap: the angular difference is then $\dfrac{3960k}{13}$ degrees, a multiple of $360$ only if $13\mid k$.
