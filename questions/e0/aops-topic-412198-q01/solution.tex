The factor pairs of $24$ are $1\cdot24$, $2\cdot12$, $3\cdot8$ and $4\cdot6$, with sums $25$, $14$, $11$ and $10$. Only $3$ and $8$ have sum $11$, so the larger number is $\boxed{8}$, answer $\textbf{(D)}$.
