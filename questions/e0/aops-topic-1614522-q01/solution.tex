A committee is a subset of the $6$ students with at least $3$ members. Counting by size,
\[
\dbinom63+\dbinom64+\dbinom65+\dbinom66=20+15+6+1=\boxed{42}.
\]
(Alternatively: there are $2^6=64$ subsets in all, and $\dbinom60+\dbinom61+\dbinom62=1+6+15=22$ of them have fewer than $3$ members, leaving $64-22=42$.)
