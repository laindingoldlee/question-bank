The equation requires $a>b$, so put $d=a-b\ge1$. Expansion gives
\[
(3d-1)b^2+d(3d-1)b+d^3=25.
\]
Every term is positive, so $d^3<25$, leaving $d=1,2$. For $d=1$, $2b^2+2b+1=25$, hence $b=3,a=4$. For $d=2$, the left side is $5b^2+10b+8$, which is $23$ at $b=1$ and at least $48$ at $b\ge2$; no solution results. Thus $(4,3)$ is the only pair and the requested maximum is $\boxed{4^2+3^3=43}$.
