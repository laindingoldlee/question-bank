Put $A=(0,0)$, $C=(24,0)$, $X=(7,0)$, $Y=(15,0)$, $O=(12,h)$, and $B=(u,v)$ with $v>0$. Since the triangle is acute, $h>0$. The perpendicularities give $5(u-24)+hv=0$ and $3u-hv=0$. Consequently $u=15$ and $hv=45$. Because $B$ lies on the circle through $A,C$, $(u-12)^2+(v-h)^2=144+h^2$. Substituting gives $9+v^2-90=144$, so $v=15$ and $h=3$. The squared radius is $144+9=153$, and the area is $153\pi$. Thus $\boxed{k=153}$, choice (C).
\begin{asy}
import geometry;
size(260);
pair A=(0,0),C=(24,0),B=(15,15),O=(12,3),X=(7,0),Y=(15,0);draw(circle(O,sqrt(153)));draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(O--X^^O--Y,dashed);dot(O);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$B$",B,N);label("$O$",O,NW);label("$X$",X,S);label("$Y$",Y,S);
\end{asy}
