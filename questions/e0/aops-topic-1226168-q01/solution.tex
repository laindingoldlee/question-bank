Exchanging a quarter or a nickel does not change the total, while exchanging a penny adds $125-1=124$ cents. So the total is always $1+124k$ cents. Among $362,512,629,744,906$ (each amount minus one cent), only $744=124\cdot6$ is a multiple of $124$. The answer is $\boxed{\text{(D)}}$ \$7.45.
