Reduce the coefficient to $\dfrac{64}{25}$. (a) Its positive integer outputs are 1,2,\ldots, so the least positive real input producing one is $\boxed{\dfrac{25}{64}}$. (b) For integral $ n $, coprimality of 64 and 25 forces 25 to divide $ n $, so the least is $\boxed{25}$.
