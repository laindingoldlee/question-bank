Let $r,s=\dfrac{x\pm\sqrt{x^2-4y}}2$ be the roots of $t^2-xt+y$. Then $r^n+s^n$ satisfies the same recurrence, with $r+s=x$ and $r^2+s^2=x^2-2y$. Hence
\[a_n=\boxed{\left(\dfrac{x+\sqrt{x^2-4y}}2\right)^n+\left(\dfrac{x-\sqrt{x^2-4y}}2\right)^n}.\]
