For $a\ne0$, $\dfrac{a}{a}=1$ and the quotient exponent law gives $\dfrac{a^1}{a^1}=a^{1-1}=a^0$. At $a=0$ this uses division by zero and is invalid. A value for $0^0$ therefore needs a separately stated convention; the argument proves no value for it.
