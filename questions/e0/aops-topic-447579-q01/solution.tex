If the last digit is $5$, the first has four choices $1,2,3,4$, and the middle positions have $4\cdot3$ choices from the remaining digits $0$ through $4$: $48$ numbers. If the last digit is $0$, the other digits are distinct from $1$ through $5$ and must include $5$. Choose the other two in $\binom{4}{2}$ ways and arrange them in $3!$ ways, giving $36$. The total is $48+36=\boxed{84}$, choice D.
