The $9$ seats of a row are labelled $1$ to $9$ from left to right. Nine students, $3$ boys and $6$ girls, are given the numbers $1$ to $9$ at random (each assignment equally likely) to indicate which seat to take. Given that the total of the three numbers given to the boys is $12$, what is the probability that no two boys sit next to each other? Express your answer as a common fraction.
