\textbf{Equal angles from the circle.} The inscribed angles $\angle CBD$ and $\angle CAD$ stand on the same arc $CD$, so $\angle CBD=30^\circ$. Also $\angle DCA$ and $\angle DBA$ stand on arc $DA$, so $\angle DCA=\angle DBA=\angle DAB$ (the last because $DA=DB$).

\textbf{An auxiliary point.} Let $M$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $D$ to $AC$, and let $E$ be the reflection of $C$ in $M$, so that $DE=DC$ and $\angle DEC=\angle DCE$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),C=(106.8,0),D=(75,25*sqrt(3));
pair cc(pair P,pair Q,pair R){pair a=Q-P,c=R-P; real d=2*(a.x*c.y-a.y*c.x);
  return P+((c.y*dot(a,a)-a.y*dot(c,c))/d,(a.x*dot(c,c)-c.x*dot(a,a))/d);}
pair O=cc(A,C,D); real R=abs(A-O);
pair u=unit(D-O); pair B=2*(O+dot(A-O,u)*u)-A;
pair M=(75,0),Ep=(43.2,0);
draw(circle(O,R),gray);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));draw(A--C,linewidth(0.9));draw(B--D,linewidth(0.9));
draw(D--M,dashed);draw(D--Ep,blue);
draw(M+(-3,0)--M+(-3,3)--M+(0,3));
draw(arc(A,16,0,30));label("$30^\circ$",A+22*dir(15));
dot(Ep,blue);dot(M);
label("$A$",A,W);label("$B$",B,S);label("$C$",C,E);label("$D$",D,N);
label("$M$",M,SW);label("$E$",Ep,NW,blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Triangle $AMD$ is a $30^\circ$-$60^\circ$-$90^\circ$ triangle with hypotenuse $AD=50\sqrt3$, so
\[
DM=\dfrac{AD}{2}=25\sqrt3,\qquad AM=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}AD=75 .
\]
Hence $MC=AC-AM=31.8$, $ME=31.8$, and $E$ lies on segment $AM$ with $AE=75-31.8=43.2$.

\textbf{$AE=BC$.} Compare triangles $DAE$ and $DBC$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $DA=DB$;
\item $\angle DAE=\angle DAC=30^\circ=\angle DBC$;
\item $\angle DEA=180^\circ-\angle DEC=180^\circ-\angle DCA=180^\circ-\angle DAB=\angle DCB$, using the cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$ for the last step ($\angle DAB+\angle DCB=180^\circ$).
\end{itemize}
So the triangles are congruent (AAS), and $BC=AE=\boxed{43.2}$ cm.
