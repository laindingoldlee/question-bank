All $\dbinom{28}{5}=98280$ sets of positions for the red balls in the drawing order are equally likely. Count the arrangements with \emph{no} three consecutive reds. Line up the $23$ green balls; they create $24$ gaps (including both ends), and the reds go into the gaps with at most $2$ in each gap. If $j$ gaps receive two reds and $5-2j$ gaps receive one red, the number of ways is $\dbinom{24}{j}\dbinom{24-j}{5-2j}$:
\[
j=0:\ \dbinom{24}{5}=42504,\qquad j=1:\ 24\dbinom{23}{3}=42504,\qquad j=2:\ \dbinom{24}{2}\cdot22=6072 .
\]
So $91080$ arrangements avoid three reds in a row, and $98280-91080=7200$ contain such a run. The probability is
\[
\dfrac{7200}{98280}=\boxed{\dfrac{20}{273}}.
\]
