Any even number repeatedly halves until an odd number $m$ is reached. If $m=1$, the orbit cycles $1\to2\to1$ and succeeds. If $m\ge3$ is odd, then $m^2+1\equiv2\pmod8$, so the next odd term is exactly $\dfrac{m^2+1}{2}>m$. The odd terms therefore strictly increase and never reach 1. Thus precisely powers of two succeed: $1,2,4,8,16,32,64$. The answer is $\boxed7$.
