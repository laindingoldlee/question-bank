Scale so that $AD=\sqrt3$. Since $\angle B=45^\circ$ and $\angle C=60^\circ$, $BD=\sqrt3$ and $DC=1$, so $AB=\sqrt6$, $AC=2$, $BC=\sqrt3+1$ and
\[
[ABC]=\dfrac{1}{2}(\sqrt3+1)\sqrt3=\dfrac{3+\sqrt3}{2}.
\]
\emph{Triangle $EAB$.} The similarity $E\leftrightarrow A$, $A\leftrightarrow B$, $B\leftrightarrow D$ gives $\angle EAB=\angle ABD=45^\circ$ and $\angle EBA=\angle ADB=90^\circ$, so $EAB$ is an isosceles right triangle with $EB=AB=\sqrt6$ and $AE=\sqrt{12}=2\sqrt3$.

\emph{Triangle $FCA$.} The similarity $F\leftrightarrow C$, $C\leftrightarrow A$, $A\leftrightarrow D$ gives $\angle FCA=\angle CAD=30^\circ$ and $\angle FAC=90^\circ$, so $AF=AC\tan30^\circ=\dfrac{2}{\sqrt3}$.

draw(Ee--B,blue); draw(F--C,red);
draw(ra(D,C-D,A-D)); draw(ra(B,A-B,Ee-B)); draw(ra(A,C-A,F-A));

\end{asy}

The angle $\angle EAF$, measured through the triangle, is $\angle EAB+\angle BAC+\angle CAF=45^\circ+75^\circ+90^\circ=210^\circ$, so the angle of triangle $FAE$ at $A$ is $150^\circ$, with sine $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Thus
\[
[FAE]=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot2\sqrt3\cdot\dfrac{2}{\sqrt3}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=1,\qquad \dfrac{[FAE]}{[ABC]}=\dfrac{2}{3+\sqrt3}=\dfrac{3-\sqrt3}{3}.
\]
The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(B)}\ \dfrac{3-\sqrt3}{3}}$.
