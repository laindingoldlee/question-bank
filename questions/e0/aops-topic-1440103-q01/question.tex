In triangle $ABC$ let $D$ be the foot of the altitude from $A$ to side $BC$. Points $E$ and $F$ are constructed outside triangle $ABC$ (on the opposite side of line $AB$ from $C$, and on the opposite side of line $AC$ from $B$, respectively) such that $\angle EBA=\angle FAC=90^\circ$, $\triangle EAB\sim\triangle ABD$, and $\triangle FCA\sim\triangle CAD$. If $\angle C=60^\circ$ and $\angle B=45^\circ$, what is $\dfrac{[FAE]}{[ABC]}$?

$\textbf{(A)}\ 2-\sqrt3\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ \dfrac{3-\sqrt3}{3}\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ \dfrac{1}{2}\qquad\textbf{(D)}\ \dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\qquad\textbf{(E)}\ \dfrac{2}{3}$
