First the sum:
\[
(x^2-3xy-y^2)+(2x^2+5xy-4y^2)=3x^2+2xy-5y^2 .
\]
Then subtract it, changing the sign of every term:
\[
(6x^2-7xy+8y^2)-(3x^2+2xy-5y^2)=\boxed{3x^2-9xy+13y^2}.
\]
