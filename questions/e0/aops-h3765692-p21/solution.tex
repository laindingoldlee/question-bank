Let $\alpha=\log_2 5$, so $2<\alpha<3$ and $\lfloor867\alpha\rfloor=2013$. The inequalities are $n\alpha<m<m+2<(n+1)\alpha$. For fixed $n\ge0$, there are $\lfloor(n+1)\alpha\rfloor-\lfloor n\alpha\rfloor-2$ such integers $m$ (zero or one); no positive power of 5 is a power of 2, so no positive endpoint is integral. All relevant $n$ are $0,\ldots,866$: for $n=867$ the lower endpoint already exceeds 2013, and for $n<0$ the upper bound is at most 0. All counted $m$ are between 1 and 2012. Summing telescopes to $2013-2\cdot867=\boxed{279}$.
