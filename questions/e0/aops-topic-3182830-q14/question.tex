Let $P(x)=20x^4+24x^3+10x^2+21x+7$ have roots $a,b,c,d$. Let $Q$ be the degree-four polynomial with roots $\dfrac{1}{a-2},\dfrac{1}{b-2},\dfrac{1}{c-2},\dfrac{1}{d-2}$, integer coefficients of greatest common divisor $1$, and positive leading coefficient. Find $Q(2)$.
