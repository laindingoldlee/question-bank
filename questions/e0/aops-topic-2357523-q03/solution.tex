By Vieta's formulas, the roots of $x^2+5x+6$ have sum $-5$, and the roots of $x^3-x^2+2x-2$ have product $-\dfrac{-2}{1}=2$. (Directly: $x^2+5x+6=(x+2)(x+3)$ has roots $-2,-3$, and $x^3-x^2+2x-2=(x-1)(x^2+2)$ has roots $1$, $i\sqrt2$, $-i\sqrt2$, with product $1\cdot2=2$.) The answer is $-5+2=\boxed{-3}$.
