A planet is a perfect sphere with centre $O$, south pole $S$ and radius $R=10^{33}$ units. An ant starts at a point $A$, crawls $d=10^{14}$ units due south, then $d$ units due east, then $d$ units due north, and finds itself back at $A$. (Starting points from which the first leg ends exactly at the south pole are not allowed, since ``east'' is undefined there.) Over all possible starting points $A$, find the smallest possible value of $\left\lfloor10^{26}\cdot\angle SOA\right\rfloor$, where $\angle SOA$ is measured in radians.
