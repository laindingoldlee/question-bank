The minute hand moves $6^\circ$ per minute, so at $16$ minutes past it is at $16\cdot6=96^\circ$ from $12$. The hour hand moves $30^\circ$ per hour and $\dfrac{1}{2}^\circ$ per minute, so at $4{:}16$ it is at $4\cdot30+16\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=128^\circ$. The angle between them is $128-96=\boxed{32}$ degrees.
