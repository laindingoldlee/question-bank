By AM-GM,
\[
\dfrac{a}{3b}+\dfrac{b}{9c}+\dfrac{c}{27a}\ge3\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{a}{3b}\cdot\dfrac{b}{9c}\cdot\dfrac{c}{27a}}=3\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{1}{729}}=3\cdot\dfrac{1}{9}=\dfrac{1}{3} .
\]
Equality holds when all three terms equal $\dfrac{1}{9}$: $\dfrac {a}{3b}=\dfrac{1}{9}$ gives $b=3a$, $\dfrac{b}{9c}=\dfrac{1}{9}$ gives $c=b$, and then $\dfrac{c}{27a}=\dfrac{3a}{27a}=\dfrac{1}{9}$ as well; for example $(a,b,c)=(1,3,3)$. The minimum is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{3}}$.
