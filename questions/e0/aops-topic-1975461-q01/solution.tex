Write the numbers as $gx$ and $gy$ with $g$ the GCD and $\gcd(x,y)=1$. The LCM is $gxy$, so $xy=7$; with $x\le y$ this means $x=1$, $y=7$. Then $g(x+y)=8g=392$, so $g=\boxed{49}$ (the numbers are $49$ and $343$).
