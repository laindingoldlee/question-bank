Let the roots be $r,r+1$. Then $P(x)=k(x-r)(x-r-1)$ with $k\ne0$. The given nonzero ratio allows cancellation, yielding $\dfrac{2017-r}{2015-r}=\dfrac{2016}{2017}$. Cross-multiplication gives $r=2017^2-2016\cdot2015=6049$. The larger root is $\boxed{6050}$.
