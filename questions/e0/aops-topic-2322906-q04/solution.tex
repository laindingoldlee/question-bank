Such integers are $341k+5$. We need $1000<341k+5<2000$, i.e. $995<341k<1995$, so $k=3,4,5$ ($341\cdot2=682$ and $341\cdot6=2046$ are too small and too large). The integers are $1028$, $1369$ and $1710$: $\boxed{3}$ of them.
