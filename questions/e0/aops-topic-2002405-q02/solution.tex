Every move increases one coordinate, so the sum of the coordinates increases by $1$ each move; hence the bug can never revisit a point, and the condition is automatic. A route is a sequence of $2+3+4=9$ moves containing $2$ $x$-moves, $3$ $y$-moves and $4$ $z$-moves, and every such sequence works. The number of routes is
\[
\dfrac{9!}{2!\,3!\,4!}=\boxed{1260}.
\]
