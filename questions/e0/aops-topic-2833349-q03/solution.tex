The sum is at most 1998, so its thousands digit is $ W=1 $. Let $ u,v $ be the units and tens carries. The tens column gives $ I+u=10v $. If $ v=1 $, then $ I=9,u=1 $, but the hundreds column would require $ 1+M+1=19 $, impossible. Hence $ v=u=0,I=0 $. The hundreds column gives $ 1+M=10 $, so $ M=9 $, and the units column gives $ N+1=9 $, so $ N=8 $. Thus $ 108+981=\boxed{1089}$.
