Allison and Edward cycle at constant speeds around an oval track of length $360$ metres. Riding in the same direction, Allison overtakes Edward every $3$ minutes. If one of them rides in the opposite direction instead, they meet every $20$ seconds. What is Allison's speed, in metres per second?
