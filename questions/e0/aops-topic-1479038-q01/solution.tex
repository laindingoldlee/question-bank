Let Allison's and Edward's speeds be $a$ and $e$ metres per second, with $a>e$ since Allison overtakes Edward.

In the same direction, Allison gains one full lap ($360$ m) on Edward between consecutive overtakings, so $(a-e)\cdot180=360$ and $a-e=2$.

In opposite directions, they cover one lap together between consecutive meetings, so $(a+e)\cdot20=360$ and $a+e=18$.

Adding, $2a=20$, so Allison's speed is $\boxed{10}$ metres per second (and Edward's is $8$).
