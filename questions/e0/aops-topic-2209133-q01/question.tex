A teacher teaches a class $3$ days a week: Monday, Wednesday and Friday. The days of the week are taken in a cycle, so Sunday and the following Monday are also consecutive.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that whichever day he adds as a fourth teaching day, he will teach on two consecutive days.
\item[(b)] Show more generally that any $4$ of the $7$ days of the week include two consecutive days.
\end{enumerate}
