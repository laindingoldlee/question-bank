For a positive integer $n$, let $f(n)$ be the units digit of the sum of the units digits of the perfect cubes $1^3,2^3,\dots,n^3$. Which of the following could be the value of $f(1)+f(2)+\cdots+f(k)$ for some positive integer $k$?

(a) $70$\quad (b) $158$\quad (c) $449$\quad (d) $1615$\quad (e) $7904$
