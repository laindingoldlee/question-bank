There were $100$ students in a math contest consisting of $4$ questions. $90$ of them got problem $1$ correct, $85$ got problem $2$ correct, $60$ got problem $3$ correct and $40$ got problem $4$ correct. Anyone who got $3$ or more problems correct advanced to the next round. What is the least possible number, and what is the greatest possible number, of students who advanced?
