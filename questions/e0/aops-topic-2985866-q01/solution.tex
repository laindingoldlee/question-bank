Each roll shows $10-(\text{bottom})$, so the visible total is $30-(\text{sum of the three bottoms})$. We need the bottoms to sum to $9$.

Ordered triples from $\{1,2,3,4\}$ with sum $9$: by the symmetry $t\mapsto5-t$ they correspond to triples with sum $6$: $(1,1,4)$ in $3$ orders, $(1,2,3)$ in $6$, $(2,2,2)$ in $1$: $10$ triples. The probability is $\dfrac{10}{64}=\boxed{\dfrac5{32}}$.
