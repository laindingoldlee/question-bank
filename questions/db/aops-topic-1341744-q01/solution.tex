Write $x^2-y^2=(x-y)(x+y)=4\cdot3^{2050}$. Since $x-y$ and $x+y$ have the same parity and their product is even, both are even: $x-y=2d$, $x+y=2e$ with $de=3^{2050}$ and $0<d<e$ (because $y>0$). Conversely each such factorisation gives positive integers $x=e+d$, $y=e-d$.

The divisors of $3^{2050}$ are $d=3^k$, $k=0,\dots,2050$, with $e=3^{2050-k}$; the condition $d<e$ means $k<1025$, so $k=0,1,\dots,1024$. There are $\boxed{1025}$ pairs.
