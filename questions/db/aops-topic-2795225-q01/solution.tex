The dog gains at 2 kilometres per hour, so a 250-metre lead takes $\dfrac{0.25}{2}=\dfrac{1}{8}$ hour, or 7.5 minutes. After Ben reverses, the distance to close is 250 metres and the combined speed is 18 kilometres per hour. This takes $\dfrac{0.25}{18}=\dfrac{1}{72}$ hour, or $\dfrac{5}{6}$ minute. Total: $\dfrac{15}{2}+\dfrac{5}{6}=\boxed{\dfrac{25}{3}\text{ minutes}}$, which is 8 minutes 20 seconds.
