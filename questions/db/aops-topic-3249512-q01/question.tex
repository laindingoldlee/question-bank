A project requires $90$ completed daily tasks. Work begins on January 2, 2024, with one task completed that day. The worker completes one task on each day except every third scheduled day, which is skipped. A skipped day makes no progress but does not undo earlier work. The project may be used starting the day after its final task. Find that date in month, day, year format.
