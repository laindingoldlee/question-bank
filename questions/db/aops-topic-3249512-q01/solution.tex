Each complete three-day block contains two completed tasks. After $44$ such blocks, $132$ days have elapsed and $88$ tasks are done. The next two days complete tasks $89$ and $90$, so the final task is on scheduled day $134$. Starting with January 2 as day one, this is $133$ days later, May 14, 2024. The following day is therefore $\boxed{\text{May 15, 2024}}$.
For the calendar check, May 14 is day $31+29+31+30+14=135$ of the leap year; January 2 is day two, so their difference is $133$ days.
