We need two numbers with product $28\cdot63=1764$ and sum $-85$: $-36$ and $-49$. So $28e^2-49e-36e+63=7e(4e-7)-9(4e-7)=(7e-9)(4e-7)$, and $e=\boxed{\dfrac97\text{ or }\dfrac74}$.
