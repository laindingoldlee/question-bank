Every choice of four digits from $0,1,\ldots,7$ has exactly one decreasing arrangement, and it lies in the stated interval (the least is $3210$). Any decreasing four-digit number beginning with $8$ is at least $8210$, too large, and one beginning with $9$ is also too large. Thus there are $\binom84=\boxed{70}$.
