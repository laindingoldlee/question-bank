The region has area $60\cdot20+20\cdot20=1600$. Relative to $D$, its two left 20-by 20 squares are entirely within radius $20\sqrt2$, contributing 800. In the lower-right rectangle the disk contributes $\int_0^{20}20\,dx+\int_{20}^{20\sqrt2}\sqrt{800-x^2}\,dx=200+100\pi$. Thus the total disk intersection is $1000+100\pi$, and the desired probability is $\dfrac{600-100\pi}{1600}=\dfrac{6-\pi}{16}$. Hence $\boxed{a+b+c=23}$.
