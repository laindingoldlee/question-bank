The Lucas sequence $\ell_n$ is defined on the positive integers by $\ell_1 = 2$, $\ell_2 = 1$, and $\ell_n = \ell_{n-1} + \ell_{n-2}$ for $n \ge 3$. The first few terms of the Lucas sequence are $\{2, 1, 3, 4, 7, 11, ...\}$. Now, consider the following infinite series, where the $n$th term is the $n$th Lucas number divided by $4^n$. \[\dfrac{2}{4}+\dfrac{1}{16}+\dfrac{3}{64}+\dfrac{4}{256}+... +\dfrac{\ell_n}{4^n} + ...\]This sum approaches a value denoted $\dfrac{a}{b}$ , where $a$ and $b$ are relatively prime. Find $a + b$.
