In triangle $ABC$ the angle bisectors $AA_1$ and $BB_1$ are drawn ($A_1$ on $BC$, $B_1$ on $CA$). Prove that for every point $M$ of segment $A_1B_1$, the distance from $M$ to $AB$ equals the sum of the distances from $M$ to $AC$ and to $BC$.
