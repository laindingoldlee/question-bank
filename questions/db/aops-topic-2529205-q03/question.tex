Let $p$ be a prime such that $q=2p-1$ is also prime, and let $k$ be an integer with $q\nmid k$ and $k\not\equiv1\pmod q$. Writing $k^{-j}$ for the inverse of $k^j$ modulo $q$, find the residue modulo $q$ of
\[S=k^{-1}+k^{-2}+k^{-3}+\cdots+k^{-(p-1)}.\]
Express the answer in terms of $k$, distinguishing whether or not $k$ is a quadratic residue modulo $q$.
