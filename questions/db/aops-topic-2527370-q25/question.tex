A $21\times28$ foot kitchen floor is covered with $588$ one-foot-square tiles. Jed walks in a straight line from one corner of the floor to the opposite corner. On how many tiles does he step, i.e. how many tiles does his path pass through the interior of? (Tiles that the path only touches at a corner do not count.)
