We have $2074\bmod50=24$ and $(50\cdot2024)\bmod7=1$. Thus $50\diamond2024=24$. Next $(24\cdot50)\bmod7=3$, and $24-50-3=-29$. The result is $\boxed{-\dfrac{29}{3}}$. Products of remainders must themselves be reduced modulo the modulus; in these computations the products were already in the correct range.
