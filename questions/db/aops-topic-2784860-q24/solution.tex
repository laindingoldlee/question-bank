Measure paper in hundredths of a roll. Five trips use 95 units from A. On the sixth, A's last 5 units are used and 14 are taken from B, after which A is replaced. Thus each six-trip block uses 14 units from B. After seven blocks, or 42 trips, A is full and B has $ 100-7\cdot14=2 $ units. Five more trips leave 5 in A and 2 in B, only 7 total, too little for the next trip's 19. No earlier block fails because B has at least 14 when its withdrawal is needed. Hence $\boxed{47}$ complete trips precede the first failure.
