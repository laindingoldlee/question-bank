The $k$th factor ($k=0,1,2,\dots$) is $\displaystyle\sum_{j\ge k}2^{-j}=2^{1-k}$, so the factors are $2,1,\dfrac12,\dfrac14,\dots$. The product of the first $m$ factors is $2^{\sum_{k=0}^{m-1}(1-k)}=2^{m-\dfrac{m(m-1)}{2}}$, which tends to $0$. So the infinite product is $\boxed{0}$.
