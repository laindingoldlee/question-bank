Carlos has $12$ different CDs he would like to buy: $4$ are rap music, $5$ are country music and $3$ are heavy metal music. He can afford only $5$ of them, and he selects $5$ of them at random. What is the probability that his purchase includes at least one CD from each of the three categories? Also explain what is wrong with counting the favourable choices as $(4\cdot5\cdot3)\cdot\binom92$.
