Without Keisha, the probability $\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{7}{12}$ is shared between Libbey and Christine in the ratio $\dfrac{1}{3}:\dfrac{1}{4}=4:3$. Hence Libbey wins with probability
\[\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{3}}{\dfrac{7}{12}}=\boxed{\dfrac{4}{7}}.\]
