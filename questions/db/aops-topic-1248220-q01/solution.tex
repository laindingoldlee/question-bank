Factor: $\dfrac{n\left(1+n+n^2\right)}{n^4\left(1+n+n^2\right)}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{n^3}}$. Cancelling is valid because $1+n+n^2=\left(n+\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^2+\dfrac{3}{4}>0$ for every real $n$.
