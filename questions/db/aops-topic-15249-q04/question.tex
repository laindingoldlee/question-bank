A runner covered $40$ miles in exactly $6$ hours. His speed was $12$ miles per hour for the first $5$ miles, $7$ miles per hour for the next $10$ miles and $6$ miles per hour for the next $15$ miles. Running at a constant speed over the final $10$ miles, what was his speed on that stretch? Express your answer as a common fraction.
