If the legs are $ a,b $ and hypotenuse $ c $, the area relation gives $ ab=c $. Also $ a+b=9-c $, so $(9-c)^2=a^2+b^2+2ab=c^2+2c $. Hence $ 81=20c $, and the area is $\dfrac{c}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{81}{40}}$. The resulting legs are positive and real since their sum is $\dfrac{99}{20}$ and product $\dfrac{81}{20}$, with positive discriminant.
