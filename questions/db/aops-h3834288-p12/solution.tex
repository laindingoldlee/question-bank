Use indicator variables for divisibility by $1,2,3,4,5,6$. Because 1200 is divisible by 60, the needed residue classes are exactly uniform. Their probabilities for the product are respectively $1,\dfrac{3}{4},\dfrac{5}{9},\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{9}{25},\dfrac{5}{12}$. For four, failure means both factors odd or exactly one factor has precisely one factor of two, giving failure probability $\dfrac{1}{4}+2\dfrac{1}{4}\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. For six, the divisibility events at two and three are independent by residue uniformity. Summing gives $\dfrac{806}{225}$, so $\boxed{a+b=1031}$.
