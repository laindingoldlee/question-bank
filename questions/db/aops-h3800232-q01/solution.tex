Translate the centres to coordinates in $\{0,1,2\}^3$. Any three collinear grid points here form a length-three line with step components $0,1,$ or $-1$. Classify by how many coordinates change. If one changes, there are $3\cdot3^2=27$ axis-parallel lines. If two change, choose their coordinate plane in three ways, the fixed coordinate in three ways, and one of two diagonal directions, giving $18$. If all three change, there are four body diagonals. Hence there are $\boxed{27+18+4=49}$ choices.
