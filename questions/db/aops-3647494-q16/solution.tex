For a tangential polygon, area equals inradius times semiperimeter. Hence $[ADE]=\dfrac{11}{2}$ and $[DBCE]=3\cdot\dfrac{31}{2}=\dfrac{93}{2}$, giving $[ABC]=52$. The quadrilateral incircle is also tangent to the three sides of $ABC$, so its radius three is the triangle inradius. Adding the two given perimeters double-counts $DE$, so the triangle semiperimeter is $21-DE$. Thus $52=3(21-DE)$ and $\boxed{DE=\dfrac{11}{3}}$.
