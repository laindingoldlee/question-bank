A relay race has three successive legs of $200$, $100$, and $50$ feet. The three runners on one team run at $50$, $25$, and $20$ feet per minute, respectively. Each runner on a second team has half the speed of the corresponding runner on the first team. How many minutes does the second team need to complete the relay? Assume constant speeds and instantaneous handoffs.
