Group as $2(1000x^6-1)+x(100x^4+10x^2+1)$. Since $1000x^6-1=(10x^2-1)(100x^4+10x^2+1)$, the polynomial equals
\[(100x^4+10x^2+1)(20x^2+x-2).\]
The quartic factor is always positive, so the real roots are $x=\dfrac{-1\pm\sqrt{161}}{40}$. Hence $m+n+r=-1+161+40=\boxed{200}$.
