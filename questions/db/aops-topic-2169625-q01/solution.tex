The other three numbers have sum $30-1-8=21$ and product $\dfrac{2520}{8}=315=3^2\cdot5\cdot7$. None of them is $0$ (the product is not $0$), and none is $1$ or $8$ (the numbers are different). Each must divide $315$, so they come from $\{3,5,7,9\}$. The products of three of these are
\[3\cdot5\cdot7=105,\qquad3\cdot5\cdot9=135,\qquad3\cdot7\cdot9=189,\qquad5\cdot7\cdot9=315,\]
so the numbers are $\boxed{5,\ 7,\ 9}$, and indeed $5+7+9=21$.
