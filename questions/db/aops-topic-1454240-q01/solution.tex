The $8$ litres of syrup contain $0.48\cdot8=3.84$ litres' worth of sugar (the concentration is measured in the same way for both solutions). Adding water does not change the amount of sugar, but the total volume becomes $8+2=10$ litres. So the new concentration is
\[
\dfrac{3.84}{10}=0.384=\boxed{38.4\%}.
\]
It is not $48\%$: the mixture is $10$ litres, but only $8$ of them came from the $48\%$ syrup.
