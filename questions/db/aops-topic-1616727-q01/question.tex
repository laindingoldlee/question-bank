Anil left his house for school $S$ minutes later than usual, but he travelled at $\dfrac{4}{3}$ of his usual speed and reached school $S$ minutes earlier than usual. The next day he left his house $S$ minutes earlier than his usual time and travelled at $\dfrac{8}{11}$ of his usual speed. He reached school
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $S$ minutes late
\item[(b)] $2S$ minutes late
\item[(c)] exactly on time
\item[(d)] cannot be determined
\end{enumerate}
