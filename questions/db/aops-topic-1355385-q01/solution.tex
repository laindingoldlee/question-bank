Every such number has digit sum $3+4+5+6=18$, a multiple of $3$, so all of them are divisible by $3$. A number is divisible by $6$ when it is also even, i.e.\ when its last digit is $4$ or $6$. For each of these $2$ choices the other three digits can be arranged in $3!=6$ ways, so $\boxed{12}$ numbers are divisible by $6$.
