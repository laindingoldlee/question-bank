Use position vectors. The midpoint equations give $2D=A+E$, $2E=B+F$, $2F=C+D$, hence $D=\dfrac{4A+2B+C}{7}$, $E=\dfrac{A+4B+2C}{7}$, $F=\dfrac{2A+B+4C}{7}$. Area ratios are affine-invariant, so compute with $A=(0,0),B=(1,0),C=(0,1)$. The smaller vertices are $(\dfrac{2}{7},\dfrac{1}{7}),(\dfrac{4}{7},\dfrac{2}{7}),(\dfrac{1}{7},\dfrac{4}{7})$. The determinant of its edge vectors is $\dfrac{2}{7}\cdot\dfrac{3}{7}-\dfrac{1}{7}(-\dfrac{1}{7})=\dfrac{1}{7}$, compared with determinant 1 for the original triangle. Thus the area ratio is $\dfrac{1}{7}$ and the requested area is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{7}}$.

\end{asy}
