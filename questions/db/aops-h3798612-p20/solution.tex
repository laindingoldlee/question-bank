Since $2^x=3^y+7>8$ for positive $y$, $x\ge4$. Modulo three, $2^x\equiv1$, so $x$ is even. Modulo eight, $3^y\equiv1$, so $y$ is even. Write $x=2m,y=2k$. Then
\[(2^m-3^k)(2^m+3^k)=7.\]
Both factors are positive integers, forcing them to be 1 and 7. Thus $2^m=4$ and $3^k=3$, giving $(x,y)=(4,2)$. The requested sum is $\boxed6$.
