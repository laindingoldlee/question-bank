Ash has $20$ identical bows and $100$ boxes, numbered $1$ to $100$. He puts all the bows into the boxes at random, in such a way that every possible distribution (that is, every list of how many bows are in each box) is equally likely. What is the probability that exactly $10$ boxes contain at least one bow?
