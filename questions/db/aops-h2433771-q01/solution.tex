There are $\binom{12}{4}=495$ possible green-position sets. In a valid arrangement the clockwise red gaps between successive greens are four positive integers summing to $8$, giving $\binom73=35$ gap lists. Choose a distinguished green starting position in $12$ ways, then divide by $4$ because every arrangement has four choices of distinguished green. Thus there are $\dfrac{12}{4}\binom73=105$ valid sets. The probability is $\dfrac{105}{495}=\dfrac{7}{33}$, and $\boxed{p+q=40}$.
