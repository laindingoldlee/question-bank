Here $10!=3628800=2^8\cdot3^4\cdot5^2\cdot7$, and $\sqrt{10!}\approx1904.9$. For $a\le\sqrt{10!}$, the function $a+\dfrac{10!}{a}$ decreases as $a$ increases, so we want the largest divisor $a$ of $10!$ that is at most $1904$.

$1890=2\cdot3^3\cdot5\cdot7$ divides $10!$, with $\dfrac{10!}{1890}=1920$. No number from $1891$ to $1904$ divides $10!$, since each has a prime factor larger than $7$ or too many factors of $7$: $1891=31\cdot61$, $1892=4\cdot11\cdot43$, $1893=3\cdot631$, $1894=2\cdot947$, $1895=5\cdot379$, $1896=8\cdot3\cdot79$, $1897=7\cdot271$, $1898=2\cdot13\cdot73$, $1899=9\cdot211$, $1900=4\cdot25\cdot19$, $1901$ is prime, $1902=6\cdot317$, $1903=11\cdot173$, $1904=16\cdot7\cdot17$.

So the least sum is $1890+1920=\boxed{3810}$.
