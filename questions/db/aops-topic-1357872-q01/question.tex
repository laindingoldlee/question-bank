How many of the following ten real numbers are rational?
\[
-2,\quad 0,\quad \pi,\quad -0.1111\ldots,\quad 3.7256,\quad \sqrt3,\quad \sqrt9,\quad 0.112123123412345\ldots,\quad -\dfrac{3}{124},\quad \sqrt{2\pi}
\]
(Here $-0.1111\ldots$ repeats the digit $1$ forever and $0.112123123412345\ldots$ continues the pattern $1,12,123,1234,\dots$.)
