In a village, every coffin must be carried up a dangerous mountain by one person. On each trip, independently of everything else, the carrier either delivers the coffin and returns safely (probability $\dfrac12$) or falls with it (probability $\dfrac12$). A carrier who falls dies, so his own coffin must now be carried up as well, and the coffin he dropped must still be carried up. Initially one person has died, so one coffin is waiting. Let $T$ be the total number of trips.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that the process ends with probability $1$.
\item[(b)] Show that the expected value of $T$ is infinite.
\end{enumerate}
