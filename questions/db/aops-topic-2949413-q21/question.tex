A circle is inscribed in rhombus $ABCD$, with $\angle ABC<90^\circ$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of $AB$, and let $E$ be the tangency point on $AB$. The other tangent from $M$ meets $BC$ at $F$. If $AE=3$ and $BE=12$, find $[MFB]$.
