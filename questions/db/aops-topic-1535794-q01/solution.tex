The product is odd exactly when both factors are odd. By independence,
\[
P(AB\text{ odd})=\left(1-\dfrac{2}{3}\right)\left(1-\dfrac{3}{5}\right)=\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{2}{5}=\dfrac{2}{15},
\]
so
\[
P(AB\text{ even})=1-\dfrac{2}{15}=\boxed{\dfrac{13}{15}}.
\]
