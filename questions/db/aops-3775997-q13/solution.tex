Write the multiplication as $N(10t+u)$, where $N$ is two-digit and $t\ge1$. Both partial products $Nt,Nu$ are two-digit, and $Nt$ ends in $7$. A four-digit total requires $10Nt+Nu\ge1000$, so $Nt\ge91$. Thus $Nt=97$. Primality of $97$ forces $N=97,t=1$. Since $Nu$ is two-digit, $u=1$. The result is $97\cdot11=\boxed{1067}$.
