The price of mowing a lawn is a function of the number of hours worked: $p=f(h)$ dollars for $h$ hours.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What does the inverse function $f^{-1}$ represent?
\item[(b)] Under what condition on $f$ does the inverse function exist?
\item[(c)] For example, suppose $f(h)=20h+15$ for $h\ge0$. Find a formula for $f^{-1}(p)$, compute $f^{-1}(95)$, and interpret the result.
\end{enumerate}
