With the frame, the outer rectangle measures $(36+2\cdot2)\times(24+2\cdot2)=40\times28$ inches. The frame is the outer rectangle minus the painting:
\[
40\cdot28-36\cdot24=1120-864=256\text{ square inches}.
\]

The cost is $256\cdot\$1.50=\boxed{\$384}$.
