$\overline{aba}=101a+10b$. Summing over $a=1,\dots,9$ and $b=0,\dots,9$:
\[101\cdot45\cdot10+10\cdot45\cdot9=45450+4050=\boxed{49500}.\]
(The posted $540$ forgot that each $a$ appears with $10$ values of $b$, and mis-weighted the digits.)
