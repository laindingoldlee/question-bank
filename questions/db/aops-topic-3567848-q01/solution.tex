Put $p=\dfrac{7}{10}$ and $q=\dfrac{3}{10}$. From a tied score of at least $3$ each, the win probability $u$ satisfies $u=p^2+2pqu$, so $u=\dfrac{p^2}{p^2+q^2}=\dfrac{49}{58}$. A wins before this tie by scores $4:0$, $4:1$, or $4:2$, with probabilities $p^4$, $4p^4q$, and $10p^4q^2$. The probability of reaching $3:3$ is $20p^3q^3$. Therefore the answer is $\boxed{p^4(1+4q+10q^2)+20p^3q^3\dfrac{49}{58}}$, approximately $0.900789$, or $90.0789\%$.
