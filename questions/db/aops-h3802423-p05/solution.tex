A divisor with digit sum $1$ is $10^k$ for some $k\ge1$, so the number is divisible by $10$. A divisor with digit sum $9$ is divisible by $9$. A square with both properties is therefore a multiple of $900=30^2$, hence is $900t^2$ for a positive integer $t$.
The first four candidates are $900,3600,8100,14400$. Neither $900$ nor $8100$ has a divisor of digit sum $8$: a divisor divisible by $3$ has digit sum divisible by $3$; the remaining divisors in either case are $1,2,4,5,10,20,25,50,100$, none with digit sum $8$.
Both $3600$ and $14400$ are exotic. Divisors witnessing sums $1$ through $9$ are respectively $10,2,3,4,5,6,25,8,9$. Hence the second square is $\boxed{14400}$.
