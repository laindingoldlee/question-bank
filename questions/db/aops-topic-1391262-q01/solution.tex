Choose the books one after the other. The probability that both are from the first series is $\dfrac{a}{k}\cdot\dfrac{a-1}{k-1}$, and that both are from the second series is $\dfrac{b}{k}\cdot\dfrac{b-1}{k-1}$. These events are disjoint, so the probability is
\[
\dfrac{a(a-1)+b(b-1)}{k(k-1)}=\dfrac{a^2+b^2-(a+b)}{k^2-k}=\dfrac{a^2+b^2-k}{k^2-k},
\]
using $a+b=k$. (Counting unordered pairs gives the same: $\dfrac{\binom a2+\binom b2}{\binom k2}$.)
