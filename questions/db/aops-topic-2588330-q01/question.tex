Strings of the letters $M$, $I$, $U$ are transformed by the following rules, where $x$ stands for any string of letters.
\begin{enumerate}
\item A string $Mx$ may be replaced by $Mxx$ (for example $MIU\to MIUIU$).
\item Any three consecutive letters $III$ may be replaced by $U$.
\item Any two consecutive letters $UU$ may be removed.
\item If a string ends in $I$, a $U$ may be added at the end.
\end{enumerate}
Starting from the string $MI$, is it possible to reach the string $MU$? Prove your answer.
