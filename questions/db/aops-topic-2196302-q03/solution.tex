Let the numbers be $n+1$ and $n$. Then $(n+1)^2-n^2=2n+1=77$, so $n=38$ and the larger number is $39$. Its units digit is $\boxed{9}$. (Check: $39^2-38^2=(39-38)(39+38)=77$.)
