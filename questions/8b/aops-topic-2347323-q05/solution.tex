Counting the letters (without regard to case), the word has $50$ letters:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{c|cccccccccc}
letter & a & r & d & y & m & u & h & j & g & p\\ \hline
count & $15$ & $5$ & $3$ & $3$ & $3$ & $3$ & $2$ & $2$ & $2$ & $2$
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
and the ten letters i, s, n, k, t, e, b, v, o, l appear once each; indeed $15+5+4\cdot3+4\cdot2+10=50$.

The number of arrangements of $50$ letters in which some letters repeat is $50!$ divided by the factorials of the repetition counts (arrangements that only swap equal letters are the same):
\[\boxed{\dfrac{50!}{15!\cdot5!\cdot(3!)^4\cdot(2!)^4}}=9346930879803650106857771955886109491200000000\approx9.35\times10^{45}.\]
