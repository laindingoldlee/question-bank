On the number line, $-3.4$ lies between $-4$ and $-3$. Its distance to $-3$ is $0.4$ and its distance to $-4$ is $0.6$, so the nearest whole number is $\boxed{-3}$. (A common mistake is to ``round down'' to $-4$; rounding to the nearest integer looks at distance, and $-3$ is closer.)
