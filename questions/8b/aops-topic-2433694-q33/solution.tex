The pizza cost $8+2=10$ dollars. A plain slice costs $\dfrac88=1$ dollar, and an anchovy slice costs $1+\dfrac24=1.5$ dollars. Doug ate the other $3$ plain slices and paid $3$ dollars; Dave ate $4$ anchovy slices and $1$ plain slice and paid $6+1=7$ dollars. Dave paid $7-3=4$ dollars more: the answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(D)}\ 4}$.
