A playlist has six distinct songs. After listening to its first three songs, a listener independently shuffles all six uniformly and again listens from the start. This repeats until every song has been heard. Count only shuffles occurring before that moment. Find the expected count as a reduced fraction $\dfrac{a}{b}$, then find $a+b$.
