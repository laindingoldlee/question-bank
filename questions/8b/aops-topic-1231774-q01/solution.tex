Pair each divisor $d$ with $\dfrac {N}{d}$. Then the product $P$ satisfies
\[P^2=\prod_{d\mid N}d\cdot\prod_{d\mid N}\dfrac {N}{d}=N^n,\]
so $P=\boxed{N^{\dfrac {n}{2}}}$ (for a perfect square, the divisor $\sqrt N$ is paired with itself, and the formula still holds).
