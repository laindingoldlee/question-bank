Each term of the expansion is a product of three of the letters (one from each factor). By the multinomial theorem:
\begin{itemize}
\item cubes $a^3,b^3,c^3,d^3$ have coefficient $1$;
\item terms $x^2y$ with $x\ne y$ ($12$ of them) have coefficient $3$;
\item terms $xyz$ with three different letters ($4$ of them) have coefficient $6$.
\end{itemize}
So
\begin{multline*}
(a+b+c+d)^3=a^3+b^3+c^3+d^3+3\left(a^2b+a^2c+a^2d+b^2a+b^2c+b^2d+c^2a+c^2b+c^2d+d^2a+d^2b+d^2c\right)\\+6(abc+abd+acd+bcd).
\end{multline*}
Check: setting $a=b=c=d=1$ gives $4+3\cdot12+6\cdot4=64=4^3$.
