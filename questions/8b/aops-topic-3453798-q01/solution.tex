Fix the number $k$ of summands, where $2\le k\le24$. Write $24=qk+r$ with $0\le r<k$. The only possible multiset has $k-r$ copies of $q$ and $r$ copies of $q+1$. Indeed two adjacent values are necessary unless all are equal, and their average forces these values and multiplicities. Conversely that multiset always works, since $q\ge1$. Thus there is exactly one representation for each $k=2,3,\ldots,24$, for $\boxed{23}$ representations. The formula describes every one without ordering duplicates.
