For each real number $x\ge0$, define

\[
A(x)=
\left\lfloor x\right\rfloor
+\left\lfloor x+\dfrac{1}{6}\right\rfloor
+\left\lfloor x+\dfrac{2}{6}\right\rfloor
+\left\lfloor x+\dfrac{3}{6}\right\rfloor
+\left\lfloor x+\dfrac{4}{6}\right\rfloor
+\left\lfloor x+\dfrac{5}{6}\right\rfloor.
\]

Let $S$ be the set of all positive integers $n$ for which there exists a real number $x\ge0$ satisfying

\[
n=
A(x)
+\left\lfloor\sqrt{A(x)}\right\rfloor
+\left\lceil\sqrt[3]{A(x)}\right\rceil.
\]

Here, $\lfloor y\rfloor$ denotes the greatest integer less than or equal to $y$, and $\lceil y\rceil$ denotes the least integer greater than or equal to $y$.

How many positive integers $n\le2026$ do not belong to $S$?
