Boris plays a game in which he rolls two standard four-sided dice (faces $1,2,3,4$) independently and at random; at the end of the game he receives a number of dollars equal to the product of the two numbers showing. After the initial roll of both dice he may pay $2$ dollars to reroll one die of his choice, and he may pay to reroll as many times as he wishes. If Boris plays to maximise his expected net gain (winnings minus money paid for rerolls), what is his expected net gain from playing once?
