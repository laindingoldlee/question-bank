Triangle $PMQ$ is isosceles, so $\angle PQM=\angle PMQ$, which is acute (two equal base angles of a triangle are less than $90^\circ$).

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair P=(1.6,2.8),M=(0,0),Q=(3.2,0),Np=(5.2,0); draw(P--M--Np--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); draw(P--Q,blue);
label("$P$",P,N); label("$M$",M,SW); label("$Q$",Q,S); label("$N$",Np,SE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Hence $\angle PQN=180^\circ-\angle PQM$ is obtuse. In triangle $PQN$ the obtuse angle at $Q$ is the largest angle, so the opposite side $PN$ is the longest side: $PN>PQ=PM$. $\blacksquare$
