$2^{100}=\left(2^{10}\right)^{10}=1024^{10}>1000^{10}=10^{30}$. For the upper bound, $2^{100}=1.024^{10}\cdot10^{30}$, and
\[1.024^2=1.048576<1.05,\qquad1.05^5=1.2762815625<10,\]
so $1.024^{10}<1.05^5<10$ and $2^{100}<10^{31}$. Hence $2^{100}$ has $\boxed{31}$ digits.
