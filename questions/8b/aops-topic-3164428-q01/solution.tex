Examples are: $d=0$: $(0!+0!+0!+0!)!=24$; $d=1$: $(1+1+1+1)!=24$; $d=2$: $(2+2)!\cdot\dfrac{2}{2}=24$; $d=3$: $3\cdot3\cdot3-3=24$; $d=4$: $4\cdot4+4+4=24$; $d=5$: $5\cdot5-\dfrac{5}{5}=24$; $d=6$: $\dfrac{6!}{6\cdot6-6}=\dfrac{720}{30}=24$. Each expression uses exactly four copies of its designated digit.
