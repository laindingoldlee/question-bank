\textbf{$31$ cannot be bought.} Using no pack of $23$, we need $31=5a+12b$; but $31$, $31-12=19$ and $31-24=7$ are not multiples of $5$. Using a pack of $23$ leaves $8$, which is not a combination of $5$ and $12$; two packs of $23$ are too many. So $31$ is impossible.

\textbf{Every number from $32$ on can be bought.} The five consecutive numbers $32$ to $36$ can:
\[
32=12+4\cdot5,\quad 33=23+2\cdot5,\quad 34=2\cdot12+2\cdot5,\quad 35=7\cdot5,\quad 36=3\cdot12 .
\]
Any larger number is one of these plus a multiple of $5$, so it can be bought by adding packs of $5$.

The greatest unattainable amount is $\boxed{31}$.
