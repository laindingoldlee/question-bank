Write the repdigit as $111d$, $1\le d\le9$. Then $999d\le n\le999d+8$. The other floor is at least $2024$, so $n\ge8268$, forcing $d=9$ and $8991\le n\le8999$. The second floor then ranges from $2204$ to $2206$, of which only $2204$ uses the required digits. Its interval is $8988\le n\le8991$, so $n=8991$ and the answer is $\boxed{91}$.
