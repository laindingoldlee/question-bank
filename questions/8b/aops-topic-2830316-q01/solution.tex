Suppose $\sqrt3=\dfrac pq$ in lowest terms. Then $p^2=3q^2$, so $3\mid p^2$ and hence $3\mid p$. Writing $p=3r$ gives $q^2=3r^2$, so $3\mid q$, a contradiction. So $\sqrt3$ is irrational.

A terminating or eventually repeating decimal represents a rational number, so the decimal expansion of $\sqrt3$ neither terminates nor repeats. $\blacksquare$
