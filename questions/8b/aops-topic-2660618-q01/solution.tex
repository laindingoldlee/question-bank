Let $d=\gcd(m,n)$, $m=dx$, $n=dy$ with $\gcd(x,y)=1$. Then $\operatorname{lcm}(m,n)=dxy$, so $xy=120$, and $d(x+y)=667=23\cdot29$.

The coprime pairs with $xy=120=2^3\cdot3\cdot5$ are, up to order, $(1,120)$, $(3,40)$, $(5,24)$, $(8,15)$, with sums $121$, $43$, $29$, $23$. Only $29$ and $23$ divide $667$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $x+y=29$: $d=23$ and $\{m,n\}=\{115,552\}$;
\item $x+y=23$: $d=29$ and $\{m,n\}=\{232,435\}$.
\end{itemize}
So $(m,n)=\boxed{(115,552),\ (552,115),\ (232,435),\ (435,232)}$.
