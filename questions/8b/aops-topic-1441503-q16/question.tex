To play a game of darts, Michael throws three darts at a dart board made of three concentric circles with radii $1$, $2$ and $3$ units. A dart in the inner disc scores $10$ points, a dart in the middle ring scores $5$ points, and a dart in the outer ring scores $1$ point. His score is the sum of the points for the three darts. If each dart hits the board at a point chosen uniformly at random, independently, what is the probability that his score is divisible by $3$? Express your answer as a common fraction.
