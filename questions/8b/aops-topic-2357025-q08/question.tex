Mr.\ O's birthday cake had the phrase ``We need zero'' stamped ten times around its side, $100$ letters in all (spaces are not letters). Then every letter except the letters n and o was wiped off, so that the cake now says ``no'' ten times. Mr.\ O eats two of the letters on the cake, chosen at random with all pairs equally likely. By how much does the probability that he eats one o and one n on the old cake differ from that probability on the new cake?
