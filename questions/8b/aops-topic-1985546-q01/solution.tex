Complete the square:
\[
h(t)=-16\left(t^2-\dfrac{9}{4}t\right)+9=-16\left(t-\dfrac{9}{8}\right)^2+16\cdot\dfrac{81}{64}+9=-16\left(t-\dfrac{9}{8}\right)^2+\dfrac{117}{4}.
\]
The square term is \(0\) at \(t=\dfrac{9}{8}\) and negative otherwise, so the maximum height is
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{117}{4}\text{ feet}}=29.25\text{ feet}.
\]
