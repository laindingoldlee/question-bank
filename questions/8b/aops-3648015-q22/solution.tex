Let $E_j$ be the expected remaining shuffles when card 1 is at position $j$, and $E_6=0$. From the top, $E_1=1+\dfrac{1}{5}\sum_{j=2}^6E_j$. For $2\le j\le5$, removing the top card moves card 1 to $j-1$. Inserting before it in one of the $j-2$ permitted positions restores position $j$; the other $7-j$ choices leave it at $j-1$. Thus $E_j=1+\dfrac{j-2}{5}E_j+\dfrac{7-j}{5}E_{j-1}$, or $E_j-E_{j-1}=\dfrac{5}{7-j}$. Substitute these differences into the top equation to obtain $E_1=\dfrac{223}{12}$. For checking, $(E_1,\ldots,E_5)=(\dfrac{223}{12},\dfrac{235}{12},\dfrac{125}{6},\dfrac{45}{2},25)$. Therefore $\boxed{m+n=235}$.
