Six cards numbered $1$ through $6$ are stacked so that the cards are in an ascending order from top to bottom ($1$ is at top, $6$ is at bottom). Nate shuffles the deck using a method called the top shuffle. A top shuffle is an operation where the card at the top of the stack is moved to a randomly chosen position that is not at the top. The relative order of all other cards remain unchanged. Once the card numbered $1$ goes to the bottom, Nate considers the deck well-shuffled and stops shuffling. The expected number of times Nate performs the top shuffle can be expressed as $\dfrac{m}{n}$ where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $m + n$.
