You go out on exactly $8$ days iff your second sickness happens on day $8$: exactly one sickness on days $1$--$7$, and sickness on day $8$. (If you are never sick twice you go out all $12$ days.)
\[P=\binom71\cdot\dfrac14\left(\dfrac34\right)^6\cdot\dfrac14=\dfrac{7\cdot3^6}{4^8}=\boxed{\dfrac{5103}{65536}}.\]
