For $12$ cold days you must go outside for five minutes at 3 a.m. Each time you go out, independently, there is a probability $\dfrac14$ that you get sick that day (you always recover by the end of that day). You stop being forced to go out once the twelve days are over or once you have been sick twice. What is the probability that you are forced to go out on exactly $8$ days? Give a common fraction.
