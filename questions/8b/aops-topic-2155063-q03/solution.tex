In one hour Anna does $1$ job, Peter $\dfrac{1}{3}$ and Maria $\dfrac{1}{6}$ of a job, so together they do $1+\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{6}=\dfrac{3}{2}$ jobs per hour. One job takes $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of an hour, which is $\boxed{40\text{ minutes}}$.
