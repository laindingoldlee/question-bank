At each 15-second completion time $t=15j$, Katrina has unfolded $j$ cranes while Christie has finished $\lfloor\dfrac{j}{8}\rfloor$ new ones. The stock is $21+\lfloor\dfrac{j}{8}\rfloor-j$. For $j\le22$ it is positive: the values decrease between production instants and at $j=22$ equal 1. At $j=23$ it is $21+2-23=0$. Thus the first empty stock occurs at $15\cdot23=\boxed{345\text{ seconds}}$. There is no simultaneous production at that instant.
