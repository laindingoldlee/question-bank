Cube the given equation:
\[\left(x+\dfrac{1}{x}\right)^3=x^3+3x+\dfrac{3}{x}+\dfrac{1}{x^3}=x^3+\dfrac{1}{x^3}+3\left(x+\dfrac{1}{x}\right).\]
So
\[x^3+\dfrac{1}{x^3}=4^3-3\cdot4=64-12=\boxed{52}.\]
(Here $x=2\pm\sqrt3$, both positive, and either gives $52$.)
