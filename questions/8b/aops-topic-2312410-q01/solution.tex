Let $a=\sqrt[4]{23}$ and $b=\sqrt[4]{29}$, so $a^2=\sqrt{23}$, $b^2=\sqrt{29}$ and $b^4-a^4=29-23=6$. Using the difference of squares twice,
\[\dfrac{1}{a+b}=\dfrac{b-a}{b^2-a^2}=\dfrac{(b-a)(b^2+a^2)}{b^4-a^4}.\]
Hence
\[\dfrac{1}{\sqrt[4]{23}+\sqrt[4]{29}}=\boxed{\dfrac{\left(\sqrt[4]{29}-\sqrt[4]{23}\right)\left(\sqrt{29}+\sqrt{23}\right)}{6}}.\]
