Coefficient comparison gives $a+c=1$, $bd=-5$, $ac+b+d=-2$, and $ad+bc=17$. Since $bd=-5$, the possible sums $b+d$ are $4$ or $-4$. The latter would give $ac=2$ with $a+c=1$, impossible for integers since the corresponding quadratic has discriminant $-7$. Hence $b+d=4$, and the requested sum is $\boxed{1+4=5}$.
Existence is verified by
\[(x^2+3x-1)(x^2-2x+5)=x^4+x^3-2x^2+17x-5.\]
