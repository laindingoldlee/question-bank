Group the nine factors as four pairs and one extra factor: $(-1)^9=\bigl((-1)(-1)\bigr)^4\cdot(-1)=1^4\cdot(-1)=\boxed{-1}$. In general an odd power of $-1$ is $-1$ and an even power is $1$.
