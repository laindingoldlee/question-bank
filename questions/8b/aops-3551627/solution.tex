Put $s=a+b+c$. By Cauchy--Schwarz, $\left(\dfrac{1}{s-a}+\dfrac{1}{s-b}+\dfrac{1}{s-c}\right)((s-a)+(s-b)+(s-c))\ge9$. The second factor is $2s$, so multiplying the reciprocal sum by $s$ gives at least $\dfrac{9}{2}$. Since $\sum\dfrac{a}{s-a}=s\sum\dfrac{1}{s-a}-3$, the desired sum is at least $\dfrac{3}{2}$. Equality holds at $a=b=c$.
