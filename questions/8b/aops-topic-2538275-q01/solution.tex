Let the side be $s$, so $P=4s$. The diagonal $AC$ is the hypotenuse of the right triangle $ABC$ with legs $s$ and $s$, so by the Pythagorean theorem $d^2=s^2+s^2=2s^2$ and $d=s\sqrt2$. Hence $s=\dfrac d{\sqrt2}$ and
\[P=4s=\dfrac{4d}{\sqrt2}=2\sqrt2\,d.\ \blacksquare\]
