The odd positive integers are written in rows of four, in a snake pattern:
\[
\begin{array}{cccc}
1&3&5&7\\
15&13&11&9\\
17&19&21&23\\
31&29&27&25\\
\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\vdots
\end{array}
\]
The odd rows are written from left to right and the even rows from right to left. The number $2011$ is in row $n$ and column $m$. What is $n+m$?
