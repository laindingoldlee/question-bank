Put $t=x-y>0$. Then $(x+y)^2=t^2+4xy=t^2+8836$, so the expression is $t+\dfrac{8836}{t}\ge2\sqrt{8836}=188$ by AM--GM. Equality is attained at $t=94$, for example by $x=47(\sqrt2+1)$ and $y=47(\sqrt2-1)$. Thus the minimum is $\boxed{188}$.
