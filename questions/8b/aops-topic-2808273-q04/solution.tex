The horizontal projections of the centres lie within radii 8 and 7 of the cylinder axis, so their horizontal separation is at most 15. Their spatial separation is at least 17; hence their vertical separation is at least $\sqrt{17^2-15^2}=8$. The cylinder height is therefore at least $8+8+9=25$. This is attained with horizontal centre separation 15, vertical separation 8, the radius 8 sphere touching the bottom and the radius 9 sphere touching the top. Thus $\boxed{25}$.
