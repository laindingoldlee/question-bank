We need $A(t)=2A_0$:
\[A_0e^{rt}=2A_0\iff e^{rt}=2\iff rt=\ln2\iff t=\boxed{\dfrac{\ln2}{r}}\text{ years}.\]
The doubling time does not depend on $A_0$. For $r=0.05$ (a rate of $5\%$), $t=\dfrac{\ln2}{0.05}\approx\dfrac{0.693}{0.05}\approx\boxed{13.9}$ years. (This is the source of the ``rule of $70$'': the doubling time is about $70$ divided by the percentage rate.)
