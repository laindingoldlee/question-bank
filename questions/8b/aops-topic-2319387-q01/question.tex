An amount of money $A_0$ grows with continuous compounding, so that after $t$ years it is $A(t)=A_0e^{rt}$, where $r>0$ is a fixed rate and $e=2.71828\ldots$. How long does it take for the money to double? Give an exact formula, and evaluate it for $r=0.05$.
