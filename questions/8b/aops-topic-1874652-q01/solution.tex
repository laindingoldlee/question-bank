The smallest multiple of $17$ above $23$ is $34=17\cdot2$, and the largest below $227$ is $221=17\cdot13$ (since $17\cdot14=238$). So the multiples are $17k$ for $k=2,3,\dots,13$: there are $\boxed{12}$ of them.
