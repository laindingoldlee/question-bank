Every such sum is at least $1+2+3=6$. Conversely, any $n\ge6$ is $(n-5)+2\cdot1+3\cdot1$ with $n-5\ge1$. So exactly $1,2,3,4,5$ are not representable, and the largest is $\boxed{5}$. (The coefficient $1$ on $a$ makes this much easier than the two-variable Chicken McNugget theorem.)
