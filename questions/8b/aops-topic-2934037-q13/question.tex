Find the mutually dependent answers $A,B,C$ to these three conditions. (i) A quadratic has real roots summing to $2^{10}+4$. Translating its graph left by $B$ and down by $C$ leaves two real roots whose sum is $A$. (ii) Inside a $45^\circ$ angle with vertex $O$, point $P$ has distance $OP=C$ and makes angle $A$ degrees with one boundary ray. Its reflections in the two boundary lines are $X,Y$, and $B=XY^2$. (iii) Two points have distance $B$. The number of points at distance $A$ from the second and nonnegative integer distance from the first is $C$.
