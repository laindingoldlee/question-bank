The number $\dfrac{1}{i+j-1}$ is written in the square in row $i$ and column $j$ of an $n\times n$ table ($1\le i,j\le n$). Then $n$ squares are chosen so that no two of them are in the same row or in the same column. Prove that the sum of the numbers in the chosen squares is at least $1$.
