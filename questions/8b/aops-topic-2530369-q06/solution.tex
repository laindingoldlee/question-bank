Complete the square:
\[5x^2+7x+14=5\left(x+\dfrac{7}{10}\right)^2+14-\dfrac{49}{20}=5\left(x+\dfrac{7}{10}\right)^2+\dfrac{231}{20}>0\]
for every real $x$. (Equivalently, the discriminant $49-280<0$ and the leading coefficient is positive.) So the inequality has \boxed{\text{no real solutions}}.
