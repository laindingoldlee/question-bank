The two favourable counts are $\binom{N-6}{6}$ and $6\binom{N-6}{5}$. For $N=11$ the first is 0 and the second positive, so $N\ge12$. Dividing their equality by $\binom{N-6}{5}$ gives $\dfrac{N-11}{6}=6$. Hence $\boxed{N=47}$.
