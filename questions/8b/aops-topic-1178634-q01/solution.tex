$\dfrac{1}{x}+\dfrac{1}{y}=\dfrac{x+y}{xy}=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{3}}$. (Here $x$ and $y$ are non-real complex numbers, since $t^2-2t+3$ has negative discriminant, but the identity still holds.)
