How many different $10$-character passwords are possible if the first $4$ characters are digits from $0$ to $9$, the next $3$ characters are lowercase letters from a to z, the last $3$ characters are capital letters from A to Z, and no character appears twice in a password? (A lowercase letter and the corresponding capital letter are different characters.)
