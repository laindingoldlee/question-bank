From $7b-2a=0$ we get $a=\dfrac{7b}{2}$ (not $\dfrac{2b}{7}$). Substituting, $12\cdot\dfrac{7b}{2}+3b=42b+3b=45b=1$, so $b=\dfrac{1}{45}$ and $a=\dfrac{7}{90}$. The average is
\[
\dfrac{a+b}{2}=\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{7}{90}+\dfrac{2}{90}\right)=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{20}}.
\]
