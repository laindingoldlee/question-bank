Divide by $9$: $5a=6b$. Since $5\mid6b$ and $\gcd(5,6)=1$, $5\mid b$; write $b=5t$, then $a=6t$ with $t$ a positive integer. So $a+b=11t\ge11$, with equality for $a=6$, $b=5$ (check: $45\cdot6=270=54\cdot5$). The answer is $\boxed{11}$.
