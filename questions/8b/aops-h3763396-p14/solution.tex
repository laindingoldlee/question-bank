Let the son\textquotesingle s current age be $s$, so the daughter is $s+2$. The parents contribute an age increase of $8$ over four years. A child of current age $r$ contributes $\min(r,4)$ to the increase, counting no age before birth. Therefore $8+\min(s,4)+\min(s+2,4)=73-58=15$. If $s\ge4$, the left side is $16$; if $s\le2$, it is at most $14$. Thus $\boxed{s=3}$. The ages $34,31,5,3$ verify all conditions.
