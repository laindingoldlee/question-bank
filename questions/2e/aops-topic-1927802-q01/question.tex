The complex numbers $z$ and $w$ satisfy $z^{13}=w$, $w^{11}=z$, and the imaginary part of $z$ is $\sin\dfrac{m\pi}{n}$ for relatively prime positive integers $m$ and $n$ with $m<n$. Find $n$. In particular, explain why $z$ is a $142$nd root of unity, so that its imaginary part has the form $\sin\dfrac{2k\pi}{142}$.
