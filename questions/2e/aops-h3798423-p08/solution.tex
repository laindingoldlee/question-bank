If $k$ players each score five, their combined score is $5k$. Their games against each other contribute exactly $\binom k2$ points, so $\dfrac{k(k-1)}{2}\le5k$ and $k\le11$. Eleven players attain this by drawing every game among themselves and losing all games against the other nineteen: each gets ten half-points. Hence the maximum is $\boxed{11}$.
