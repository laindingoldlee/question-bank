The top row can be any ordered pair of different colours: $6$ ways. Given a row $(x,y)$, the next row $(x',y')$ needs $x'\ne x$, $y'\ne y$, $x'\ne y'$. With third colour $z$: $x'\in\{y,z\}$ and $y'\in\{x,z\}$, excluding $x'=y'=z$, leaving $(y,x),(y,z),(z,x)$: $3$ ways. Hence the total is $6\cdot3^5=\boxed{1458}$.
