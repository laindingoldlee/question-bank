A parking lot has $12$ spots arranged in $6$ rows of $2$ (a $6\times2$ grid). Each spot gets a red, white or blue car. The two cars in the same row must have different colours, and cars in vertically adjacent spots must have different colours (diagonal neighbours may match). How many colourings are possible?
