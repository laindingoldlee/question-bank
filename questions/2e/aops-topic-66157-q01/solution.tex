Note that $n^2-n=n(n-1)$ is always divisible by $k=1$, so the condition fails exactly when $n^2-n$ is divisible by \emph{every} $k$ from $1$ to $n$. Check small $n$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $n=1$: $n^2-n=0$, divisible by $1$ (all).
\item $n=2$: $2$, divisible by $1$ and $2$ (all).
\item $n=3$: $6$, divisible by $1,2,3$ (all).
\item $n=4$: $12$, divisible by $1,2,3,4$ (all).
\item $n=5$: $20$, divisible by $1,2,4,5$ but not by $3$.
\end{itemize}
So the least such $n$ is $\boxed{5}$.
