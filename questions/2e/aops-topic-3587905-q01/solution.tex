Count by length. There are $9$ one-digit numbers. Every two-digit number is balanced, giving $90$. For three digits, either all are equal ($9$) or all distinct ($9\cdot9\cdot8=648$), giving $657$. For four digits: all equal gives $9$; all distinct gives $9\cdot9\cdot8\cdot7=4536$; two distinct nonzero digits used twice gives $\binom92\binom42=216$; zero and one nonzero digit each twice gives $9\cdot3=27$, since the first digit cannot be zero. The four-digit total is $4788$. Hence the overall count is $9+90+657+4788=\boxed{5544}$.
