Simplify: $\dfrac{1000}{968}=\dfrac{125}{121}=1+\dfrac{4}{121}$. Long division of $4$ by $121$ (bringing down zeros: $40$, $400=3\cdot121+37$, $370=3\cdot121+7$, $70$, $700=5\cdot121+95$, $950=7\cdot121+103$, $1030=8\cdot121+62$, $620=5\cdot121+15$, $150=1\cdot121+29$, \dots) gives
\[\dfrac{4}{121}=0.033057851\ldots\]
Hence $\dfrac{1000}{968}=1.033057851\ldots$, and the first ten digits are $\boxed{1033057851}$.
