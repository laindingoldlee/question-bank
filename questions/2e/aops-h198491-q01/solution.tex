Factor $n^4+4=(n^2-2n+2)(n^2+2n+2)$. Then
\[
\dfrac{n}{n^4+4}
=\dfrac{1}{4}\left(\dfrac{1}{(n-1)^2+1}-\dfrac{1}{(n+1)^2+1}\right).
\]
The partial sum through $N$ is
\[
\dfrac{1}{4}\left(1+\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{N^2+1}-\dfrac{1}{(N+1)^2+1}\right).
\]
The final two terms tend to zero, proving convergence and giving $\boxed{\dfrac{3}{8}}$.
