Consider the equation $\sqrt[3]{x+20}+\sqrt[3]{x-20}=\sqrt[3]{30}$ (real cube roots).
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that it has exactly one real solution $x$, and find the integer $n$ with $n<x<n+1$.
\item[(b)] Show that this solution is the only real root of $4x^3+225x^2+2700x-175500=0$.
\end{enumerate}
