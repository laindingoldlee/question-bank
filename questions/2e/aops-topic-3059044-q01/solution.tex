The second equation gives $x=\dfrac{y^2}{2}+64$. Substitution in the first, followed by multiplication by 4, yields $y^4-4y^3+256y^2+16348=0$. But the left side is $(y^2-2y)^2+252y^2+16348>0$ for every real $y$. Thus there are no real solutions, so no real ratio $x:y$ is determined.
