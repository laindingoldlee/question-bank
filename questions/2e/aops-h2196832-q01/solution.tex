The rays have perpendicular unit directions $u=(\dfrac{3}{5},\dfrac{4}{5})$ and $v=(-\dfrac{4}{5},\dfrac{3}{5})$. The integer legs must be five and twelve. For endpoints $5u,12v$, the vertical intercept is $\dfrac{5\cdot12}{\dfrac{3\cdot5}{5}+\dfrac{4\cdot12}{5}}=\dfrac{100}{21}$; reversing the legs gives $\dfrac{5\cdot12}{\dfrac{3\cdot12}{5}+\dfrac{4\cdot5}{5}}=\dfrac{75}{14}$. Their difference is $\boxed{\dfrac{25}{42}}$. These intercepts can be obtained by writing the line in the orthonormal coordinates along $u,v$: $\dfrac{s}{a}+\dfrac{t}{b}=1$, with $s=\dfrac{4}{5}y,t=\dfrac{3}{5}y$ on the vertical axis. 
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(220);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair O=(0,0),u=(3/5,4/5),v=(-4/5,3/5);draw(12*v--O--12*u);draw(5*u--12*v);draw(12*u--5*v,dashed);draw((0,0)--(0,11),gray);label("$O$",O,(0,-1));label("$5u$",5*u,(1,0));label("$12u$",12*u,(1,0));label("$5v$",5*v,(-1,0));label("$12v$",12*v,(-1,0));
\end{asy}
