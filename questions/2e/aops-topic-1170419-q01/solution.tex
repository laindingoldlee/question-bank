Add the first equation to twice the second to obtain $4a+b=19$, so $b=19-4a$. The second equation then gives $2c=25-5a$, or $c=\dfrac{25-5a}{2}$. Every real $a$ yields a solution, since substitution verifies both equations. Examples are $\boxed{(5,-1,0)}$ and $\boxed{(3,7,5)}$. Thus all solutions are $(a,19-4a,\dfrac{25-5a}{2})$ with $a\in\mathbb R$, an infinite family.
