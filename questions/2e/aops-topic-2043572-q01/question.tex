There are \(50\) envelopes, each containing one piece of paper with either \(1\) or \(-1\) written on it. John cannot see the numbers, but he may pick any \(3\) envelopes and ask an oracle, who tells him the product of the three numbers in them. What is the least number of questions John needs in order to be sure of the product of all \(50\) numbers?
