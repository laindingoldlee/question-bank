It must be divisible by $8$ and by $3$. Divisibility by $8$ depends on the last three digits; among the $8$ strings of $4$s and $5$s only $544=8\cdot68$ works (the number must be even, so it ends in $4$, and $444,454,554$ are not multiples of $8$). The digit sum must be a multiple of $3$; $544$ itself has digit sum $13$, and a single extra digit $d$ in front needs $13+d\equiv0\pmod3$, i.e. $d=5$. So the answer is $\boxed{5544}=24\cdot231$.
