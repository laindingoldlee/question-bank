The original vertices in question are $(3,3)$ and $(1,1)$. Under the dilation, the centre becomes $(-2,-2)+3(4,4)=(10,10)$ and the top-right vertex becomes $(13,13)$. Rotating $315^\circ$ clockwise is the same as rotating $45^\circ$ counterclockwise. The vector $(3,3)$ from the new centre therefore becomes $(0,3\sqrt2)$, so the final vertex is $(10,10+3\sqrt2)$. Its distance from $(1,1)$ is
\[\sqrt{9^2+(9+3\sqrt2)^2}=\boxed{\sqrt{180+54\sqrt2}}.\]
