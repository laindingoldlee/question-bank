A closed loop of string of length $L$ is laid flat in the plane.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the area enclosed when the string forms a circle.
\item[(b)] Show that when the string forms a regular $n$-gon ($n\ge3$), the enclosed area is $\dfrac{L^2}{4n\tan\dfrac{180^\circ}{n}}$.
\item[(c)] Prove that the circle encloses more area than every regular polygon with the same perimeter $L$.
\end{enumerate}
