We need $x\ne0$, $x\ne2017$ and $x\ne2019$. Put $u=\dfrac{1}{x}$ and cross-multiply:
\[
\left(\dfrac{1}{2016}-u\right)\left(\dfrac{1}{2019}-u\right)=\left(\dfrac{1}{2018}-u\right)\left(\dfrac{1}{2017}-u\right).
\]
Let $p=2016\cdot2019$ and $q=2017\cdot2018$. Since $\dfrac{1}{2016}+\dfrac{1}{2019}=\dfrac{4035}{p}$ and $\dfrac{1}{2017}+\dfrac{1}{2018}=\dfrac{4035}{q}$, expanding gives
\[
u^2-\dfrac{4035u}{p}+\dfrac{1}{p}=u^2-\dfrac{4035u}{q}+\dfrac{1}{q}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
(1-4035u)\left(\dfrac{1}{p}-\dfrac{1}{q}\right)=0 .
\]
As $p=q-2\ne q$, we get $u=\dfrac{1}{4035}$, so $x=\boxed{4035}$, which is allowed.
