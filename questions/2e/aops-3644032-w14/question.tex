Olivia has a triangle $ABC$, and Ivy is trying to guess its area. Olivia tells Ivy that angle $A$ is $30^{\circ}$ and that side $AB$ equals $10$, but Ivy cannot determine the area of ABC with that information alone. Olivia then tells Ivy the value of side $BC$, and Ivy is able to uniquely determine the triangle’s area. What is the sum of all possible positive integers that CANNOT have been the value of $BC$?
