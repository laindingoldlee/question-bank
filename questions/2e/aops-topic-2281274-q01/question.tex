There are $n$ people of distinct heights standing in a line in a uniformly random order. Bob stands in front of the line, looking along it. He can see a person if everybody in front of that person is shorter than that person. (For example, if they stand from shortest at the front to tallest at the back, Bob sees all $n$ people.) Find the expected number of people Bob can see.
