An angle bisector meets the opposite circumcircle arc at its midpoint, so $PB=PC$. Therefore the altitude PY also bisects BC, giving $BY=15$. Pythagoras gives $PB=\sqrt{15^2+8^2}=17$. The perimeter is $30+17+17=\boxed{64}$.
