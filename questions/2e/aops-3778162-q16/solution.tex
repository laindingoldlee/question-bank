Complete the square: $x^2-(y-\dfrac{1}{2})^2=0$. Factoring gives $(x-y+\dfrac{1}{2})(x+y-\dfrac{1}{2})=0$. The graph is the union of the two intersecting lines $\boxed{y=x+\dfrac{1}{2}\text{ and }y=-x+\dfrac{1}{2}}$.
\begin{asy}
import graph;
usepackage("amsmath");
import geometry;
size(7cm);
draw((-2,-1.5)--(2,2.5));draw((-2,2.5)--(2,-1.5));dot((0,.5));label("$(0 , \dfrac{1}{2})$",(0,.5),(1,0));label("$y = x + \dfrac{1}{2}$",(2,2.5),(0,1));label("$y = - x + \dfrac{1}{2}$",(-2,2.5),(0,1));
\end{asy}
