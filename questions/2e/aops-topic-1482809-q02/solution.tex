Suppose, for contradiction, that every box has at most $2$ pieces of mail. Then the $50$ boxes hold at most $50\cdot2=100$ pieces, fewer than the $101$ delivered. This is impossible, so some box contains at least $3$ pieces. $\blacksquare$

(This is the pigeonhole principle: $101$ objects in $50$ boxes put at least $\left\lceil\dfrac{101}{50}\right\rceil=3$ objects in some box.)
