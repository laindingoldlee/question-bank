Take $B=(0,0)$, $D=(d,0)$, $E=(u,0)$, $A=(u,12)$. Since the diagonals bisect one another, $C=(d-u,-12)$. The right angle at $A$ gives $u(d-u)=144$. The distance $CE$ gives $(d-2u)^2+144=193$, hence $(d-2u)^2=49$. Therefore $d^2=(d-2u)^2+4u(d-u)=49+576=625$, so $BD=25$. The diagonal divides the rectangle into two triangles of height $12$, giving area $25\cdot12=\boxed{300}$. The two possible values $u=9,16$ correspond to reflection and give the same area.

\end{asy}
