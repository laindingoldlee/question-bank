The concatenation is $1000M+N$, so $N(3M-1)=1000M$. Since $\gcd(M,3M-1)=1$, $d=3M-1$ divides 1000. Its bounds are $29\le d\le296$ and $d\equiv2\pmod3$. Among the divisors of $1000=2^3 5^3$, only $50,125,200$ qualify. These yield $(M,N)=(17,340),(42,336),(67,335)$, all with the required digit lengths. Their concatenations sum to $17340+42336+67335=\boxed{127011}$.
