Fill the eight noncentral cells of a $3\times3$ grid using eight distinct numbers from $1,\ldots,9$, omitting one number $E$. Require the top and bottom row sums and the left and right column sums all to be equal; impose no condition on the incomplete middle row or column. Find one arrangement, and determine exactly which exclusions $E$ are impossible.
