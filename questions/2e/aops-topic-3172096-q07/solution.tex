The distinct possible products are $1,2,3,4,5,6,8,9,10,12,15,16,18,20,24,25,30,36$, eighteen in total. Before a third occurrence, each can occur at most twice, allowing at most $36$ rolls. These can be realized by obtaining each possible product twice. The next roll forces a third occurrence. Thus the maximum is $\boxed{37}$.
