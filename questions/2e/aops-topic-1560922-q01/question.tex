In a school fair game, the probabilities that a player wins first, second and third prize are $0.000000123$, $0.000005667$ and $0.020349976$ respectively, and the probability that a player wins no prize is $0.026$. The only other possible outcome is a consolation prize. $100$ people play the game, independently of each other.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What is the probability that a player wins a consolation prize?
\item[(b)] What is the expected number of players in each of the five categories (first, second, third, consolation, no prize)?
\item[(c)] For each category, what is the most likely number of players in it?
\end{enumerate}
