The colours repeat every $5$ days, so on day $d$ he wears the same colour as on day $r$, where $r$ is the remainder of $d$ on division by $5$ (with $r=5$ instead of $0$). Since $30=6\cdot5$, day $30$ has the same colour as day $5$, which is $\boxed{\text{white}}$.
