Mr.~Sanchez has shirts in the colours yellow, red, blue, green and white. Each day he wears a shirt, repeating the colours in this order (yellow, red, blue, green, white, yellow, red, \ldots). If he wears a yellow shirt on the first day of the month, what colour does he wear on the $30$th day of the month?
