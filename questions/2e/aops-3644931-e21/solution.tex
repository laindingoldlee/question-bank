Use $A=(0,0),B=(w,0)$ and top side at height 18. The circle intersections with $BC$ are at heights zero and ten, so its centre height is five. Tangency to the top gives radius 13. Its horizontal distance to $B$ is $\dfrac{w}{2}$, so $(\dfrac{w}{2})^2+5^2=13^2$. Therefore $w=\boxed{24}$.
