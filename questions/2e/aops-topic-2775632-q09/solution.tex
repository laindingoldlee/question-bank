If $x=2$, then $z=6y-212=2(3y-106)$ is even, so primality would force $z=2=x$, forbidden. Thus $x$ is odd. If $y$ were odd, $z$ would be even and hence 2, giving $x(x+3y)=218=2\cdot109$, impossible for positive odd $x$ and $x+3y>x$. Hence $y=2$ and $z=x^2+6x-216=(x-12)(x+18)$. Positivity forces $x>12$, and primality forces $x-12=1$. Thus $(x,y,z)=(13,2,31)$, and $\boxed{806}$.
