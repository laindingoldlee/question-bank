A spectator draws triangle $ABC$, which a magician sees. The spectator then secretly chooses a point $P$ and reflects it through the points $A$, $B$, $C$ to get $P_a$, $P_b$, $P_c$. The magician claims to know the area of triangle $P_aP_bP_c$. Is this possible?
