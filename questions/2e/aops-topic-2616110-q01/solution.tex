Take $11$ quarters (\$2.75) and $3$ pennies: $14$ coins. Fewer coins is impossible: $3$ pennies are needed (the amount is $\equiv3\pmod5$), and \$2.75 needs at least $11$ coins, since every coin is worth at most $25$ cents. So the answer is $\boxed{14}$.
