The average diameter of a car tyre is about $26.7$ inches. The tyre's warranty is good for $70{,}000$ miles. About how many revolutions will the tyre make before the warranty runs out? Give the answer to the nearest $100$ revolutions. Use $3.14159$ for $\pi$.
