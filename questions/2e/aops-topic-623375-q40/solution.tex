Use $\pi^2\approx9.8696$: then $\pi^4\approx97.409$ and
\[
\pi^5\approx97.409\times3.14159\approx306.02 .
\]
To the nearest integer, $\pi^5\approx\boxed{306}$.

(For a quick mental estimate within $5\%$: $\pi^2\approx9.87\approx10$ gives $\pi^4\approx97$, and $97\times3.14\approx305$. The crude $3^5=243$ is $21\%$ too small.)
