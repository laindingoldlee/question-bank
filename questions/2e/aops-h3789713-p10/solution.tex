For fixed integer $z$, solve to get $x=\dfrac{11+13z}{1+z^2}$ and $y=\dfrac{13-11z}{1+z^2}$. Then $x^2+y^2=\dfrac{290}{1+z^2}$, so $1+z^2$ divides $290$. Its divisors give the candidate values $z=0,\pm1,\pm2,\pm3,\pm12,\pm17$. Checking the two formulas, exactly $z=-12,-2,-1,0,1,3$ give integer $x,y$, respectively $(-1,1),(-3,7),(-1,12),(11,13),(12,1),(5,-2)$. Thus there are $\boxed6$ solutions.
