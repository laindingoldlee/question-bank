\[3000=2^3\cdot3\cdot5^3,\quad 2520=2^3\cdot3^2\cdot5\cdot7,\quad 1600=2^6\cdot5^2,\quad 1250=2\cdot5^4.\]
The smallest exponents are $2^1$, $3^0$, $5^1$, $7^0$, so the gcd is $\boxed{10}$.
