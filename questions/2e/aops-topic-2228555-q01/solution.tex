The integers from $20$ to $39$ with at least one even digit are all of $20,\ldots,29$ (tens digit $2$) and $30,32,34,36,38$: $15$ numbers. Those with both digits even are $20,22,24,26,28$: $5$ numbers. So the probability is $\dfrac{5}{15}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{3}}$.
