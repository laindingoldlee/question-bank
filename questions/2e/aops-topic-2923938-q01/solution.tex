Multiply by $3-2=1$ and use the difference of squares repeatedly: $(3-2)(3+2)=3^2-2^2$, then $(3^2-2^2)(3^2+2^2)=3^4-2^4$, and so on. The product is $\boxed{3^{128}-2^{128}}$.
