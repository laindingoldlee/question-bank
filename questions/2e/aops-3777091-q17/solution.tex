Put $A=(0,0),B=(4,0),D=(0,3),C=(4,3)$. Since $BD$ has slope $-\dfrac{3}{4}$, the line is $y=\dfrac{4}{3}x+3$. Its intersections are $P=(-\dfrac{9}{4},0)$ and $Q=(4,\dfrac{25}{3})$. Therefore $PQ=\sqrt{(\dfrac{25}{4})^2+(\dfrac{25}{3})^2}=\boxed{\dfrac{125}{12}}$.
