For $512\le n\le999$, $n$ has $10$ binary digits, so we need $n$ to have exactly $10$ divisors. Then $n=p^9$ or $n=p^4q$ with $p\ne q$ primes. The only ninth power in range is $2^9=512$. With $n=p^4q\le999$: $p=2$ gives $16q$ with $q\le62$, largest $q=61$, $n=976$; $p=3$ gives $81q$ with $q\le12$, largest $891$; $p\ge5$ gives $625q\ge1250$. So the largest is $\boxed{976}=2^4\cdot61=1111010000_2$, with $10$ divisors and $10$ binary digits.
