Set $O=(0,0),X=(14,0),N=(0,34),K=(14,34)$, $R=(r,34)$ and $B=(14,b)$. Equality of lengths gives $b=\sqrt{r^2+960}$, for $0\le r\le14$. Thus
\[
RB^2=2r^2-28r+2312-68\sqrt{r^2+960}.
\]
Its derivative is $4r-28-\dfrac{68r}{\sqrt{r^2+960}}\le2r-28\le0$, because $\sqrt{r^2+960}\le34$. Therefore the maximum occurs at $r=0$, and equals $\sqrt{2312-544\sqrt{15}}\approx\boxed{14.32}$.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

\end{asy}
