In equiangular octagon $CAROLINE$, $CA=RO=LI=NE=\sqrt2$ and $AR=OL=IN=EC=1$. The self-intersecting octagon $CORNELIA$ encloses six non-overlapping triangular regions. Let $K$ be the area enclosed by $CORNELIA$, that is, the total area of the six triangular regions. Then $K=\dfrac ab$, where $a$ and $b$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $a+b$.
