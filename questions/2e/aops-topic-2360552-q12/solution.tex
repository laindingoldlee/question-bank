The octagon is a $3\times3$ square with four unit right-isosceles corners cut off: $C(0,1)$, $A(1,0)$, $R(2,0)$, $O(3,1)$, $L(3,2)$, $I(2,3)$, $N(1,3)$, $E(0,2)$. The path $CORNELIA$ consists of the horizontal segments $CO$ ($y=1$) and $EL$ ($y=2$), the crossing segments $RN$ ($x=2-\dfrac y3$) and $IA$ ($x=1+\dfrac y3$), and the four corner diagonals.

$RN$ and $IA$ meet $y=1$ at $x=\dfrac53$ and $\dfrac43$, meet $y=2$ at $\dfrac43$ and $\dfrac53$, and cross each other at $\left(\dfrac32,\dfrac32\right)$. The six triangles are:
\begin{itemize}
\item four corner triangles, such as $A$, $C$, $\left(\dfrac43,1\right)$, each with base $\dfrac43$ and height $1$, so area $\dfrac23$;
\item two small middle triangles, with base $\dfrac13$ and height $\dfrac12$, so area $\dfrac1{12}$.
\end{itemize}
So $K=4\cdot\dfrac23+2\cdot\dfrac1{12}=\dfrac{17}{6}$, and $a+b=\boxed{23}$.
