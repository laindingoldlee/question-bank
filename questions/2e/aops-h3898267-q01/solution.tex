We have $a^2+b^2\ge2027$. If $a+b\le46$, the largest possible squared sum is $45^2+1=2026$, so $a+b\ge47$. Thus the total is at least $49$. Total $49$ forces $c=d=1$, giving $a^2+b^2=2027\equiv3\pmod4$, impossible. For total $50$, either $c=d=1$ again, or $c+d=3$ and $a+b=47$. In the latter case $cd^2$ is $2$ or $4$, so $a^2+b^2$ is even, whereas $a+b$ is odd; this is impossible by parity. Finally $(a,b,c,d)=(45,2,3,1)$ satisfies $2025+4-3=2026$ and has total $51$. Hence the minimum is $\boxed{51}$.
