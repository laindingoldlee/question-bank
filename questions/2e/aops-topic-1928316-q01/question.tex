Three boxes are labelled ``APPLES'', ``ORANGES'' and ``APPLES AND ORANGES''. One box contains only apples, one contains only oranges, and one contains both apples and oranges, but every box is labelled incorrectly. You may choose one box and take out a single fruit without looking inside. Show how to choose the box so that, after seeing the fruit, you can always label all three boxes correctly, and explain why choosing either of the other boxes might not be enough.
