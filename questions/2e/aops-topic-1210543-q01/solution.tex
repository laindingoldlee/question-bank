Take $M=8=2^2+2^2$ and $N=5=1^2+2^2$. Then $M+N=13=2^2+3^2$ is Pythagorean, while $M-N=3$ is not: the only squares below $3$ are $1$, and $1+1=2\ne3$.

So $\boxed{M=8,\ N=5}$ works (many other pairs do too).
