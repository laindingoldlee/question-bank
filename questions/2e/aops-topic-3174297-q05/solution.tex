Subtract $1$ from each die value. We need five nonnegative integers summing to $2$; the upper bound of $5$ is automatic. Stars and bars gives $\binom64=15$ outcomes out of $6^5=7776$. Hence the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{5}{2592}}$.
