Let  $\omega$ be a circle with center $O$ and radius $40$. Then, let $P$ be a point such that $OP = 20$, $Q$ be the intersection of ray $\overrightarrow{OP}$ and  $\omega$, and $R$ be a point on $\omega$ such that $PR = 25$. The line  $\overleftrightarrow{PR}$ intersects  $\omega$ again at point $X$. The area of $\vartriangle XPQ$ can be expressed as $m\sqrt{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are integers such that $n$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. Find $m + n$.
