Model starts as whole days, so the weather cycle has 70 possible phases. A seven-day vacation is entirely sunny for exactly four starting phases inside the ten-day sunny block. Thus 17 vacations can cover at most 68 phases, and at least 18 are needed. Eighteen starts spaced four phase-days apart cover all phases; place them in sufficiently distant cycles if necessary to keep the actual vacations disjoint. Therefore $\boxed{18}$ suffice.
