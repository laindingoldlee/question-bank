Circle $A$ has centre $(0,0)$ and radius $4$. Circle $B$ has centre $(40,40)$ and radius $6$. Both radii start increasing at the same moment, and the radius of circle $B$ increases at twice the rate of the radius of circle $A$. When the circles are externally tangent at the point $P(x,y)$, which lies between the centres, what is the value of $x$? Express your answer in simplest radical form.
