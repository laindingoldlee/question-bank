Two civilizations initially differ by one technology level, with civilization $A$ ahead. During each century, each independently advances by one level with probability $20\%$. After any failure its next-century success probability doubles, capped at $100\%$; after a success it resets to $20\%$. A journey takes four centuries. Find the probability that $A$ is still strictly ahead at arrival, to two significant figures.
