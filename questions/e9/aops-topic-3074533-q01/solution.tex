Label the samples $0$ through $999$ and write each label as a ten-bit binary word, adding leading zeroes. Sensor $j$ receives a small portion of exactly those samples whose $j$th bit is $1$. After one day, the ten positive or negative results reproduce the binary label of the contaminated sample. All-negative means label $0$. Ten sensors suffice because $2^{10}=1024\ge1000$; nine would give at most $512$ result patterns and could not distinguish all samples.
