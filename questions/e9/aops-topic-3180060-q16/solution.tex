Use $A=(0,0),B=(1,0),C=(1,1),D=(0,1)$. The inscribed square has vertices $E=(\dfrac{1}{3},0),F=(1,\dfrac{1}{3}),G=(\dfrac{2}{3},1),H=(0,\dfrac{2}{3})$ and centre $(\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{1}{2})$. Its side is $\dfrac{\sqrt5}{3}$, so its inradius is $\dfrac{\sqrt5}{6}$. The outer sides are distance $\dfrac{1}{2}$ from the common centre. Thus the minimum gap is $\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{\sqrt5}{6}=\dfrac{3-\sqrt5}{6}$, giving $\boxed{30506}$.
