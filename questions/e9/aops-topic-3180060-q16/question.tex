A square $EFGH$ has one vertex on each successive side of unit square $ABCD$, with $E\in AB$ and $EB=2AE$. Its incircle is $\omega$. The least distance from $\omega$ to the boundary of $ABCD$ is $\dfrac{P-\sqrt Q}{R}$ in reduced form with squarefree $Q$. Find $10000P+100Q+R$.
