The underside of a bridge is $68$ metres above the water. As a boat approaches, the height of the top of its mast above the water, $t$ seconds after a certain moment, is modelled by
\[
h(t)=-16t^2+63t+4\ \text{metres}.
\]
Does the top of the mast ever reach the height of the bridge? Answer without a graphing calculator.
