Complete the square:
\[
h(t)=-16\left(t^2-\dfrac{63}{16}t\right)+4=-16\left(t-\dfrac{63}{32}\right)^2+\dfrac{3969}{64}+4=-16\left(t-\dfrac{63}{32}\right)^2+\dfrac{4225}{64}.
\]
So the greatest value of $h$ is $\dfrac{4225}{64}\approx66.02$ m, reached at $t=\dfrac{63}{32}$. Equivalently, the equation $-16t^2+63t+4=68$, that is $16t^2-63t+64=0$, has discriminant $63^2-4\cdot16\cdot64=3969-4096=-127<0$, so it has no real solution.

The mast never reaches $68$ m, so $\boxed{\text{yes, the boat can pass under the bridge}}$ (with about $2$ m to spare at its highest).
