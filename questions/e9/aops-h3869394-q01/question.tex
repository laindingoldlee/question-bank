In \(\triangle ABC\), the side lengths are \(AB=5\), \(AC=8\), and \(BC=7\). Let \(\omega\) be the incircle, and let \(\omega\) touch \(BC\) at \(D\). A line \(\ell\) is tangent to \(\omega\) and intersects the rays \(\overrightarrow{AB}\) and \(\overrightarrow{AC}\) at points \(M\) and \(N\), respectively, with \(\omega\) lying inside \(\triangle AMN\). Let \(\Gamma\) be the circumcircle of \(\triangle AMN\), and suppose line \(AD\) meets \(\Gamma\) again at \(P\). As \(\ell\) varies, the minimum possible value of \(AP^2\) is \(\dfrac{r}{s}\), where \(r\) and \(s\) are relatively prime positive integers. Find \(r+s\).
