(a) The real odd roots are $-|x|^{\dfrac{1}{2n+1}}$, tending to $\boxed{-1}$. (b) The principal complex roots are $\exp\left(\dfrac{\ln|x|+i\pi}{n}\right)$, tending to $\boxed1$. A negative number has no real even root, so an unrestricted sequence of real $n$ th roots does not exist. The principal complex convention chooses a different branch from the negative real odd root.
