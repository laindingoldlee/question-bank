\textbf{(a)} Following the steps,
\[
(2d+5)\cdot50+1763-Y=100d+250+1763-Y=100d+(2013-Y).
\]

\textbf{(b)} Someone born in year $Y$ turns $2013-Y$ during $2013$; before the birthday their age is $2012-Y$, one less. If $10\le 2013-Y\le99$, then $2013-Y$ has exactly two digits, and adding $100d$ puts the digit $d$ in front of them. For example, with $d=9$ and $Y=2000$ the result is $900+13=913$: the digit $9$ and then $13$, the age reached in $2013$ (the poster was $12$ and would turn $13$ later that year, as the trick predicts).

\textbf{(c)} In year $N$ the result should be $100d+(N-Y)$. Since the steps give $100d+250+c-Y$ when $c$ is added instead of $1763$, we need $250+c=N$, that is,
\[
c=\boxed{N-250}.
\]
(For $2013$ this gives $1763$; for $2026$ it would be $1776$.)
