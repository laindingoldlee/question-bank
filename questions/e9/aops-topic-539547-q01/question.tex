Here is a number trick, performed in the year $2013$. Take the last digit $d$ of your phone number ($1\le d\le9$). Multiply it by $2$, add $5$, multiply by $50$, add $1763$, and subtract the year $Y$ in which you were born.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that the result is $100d+(2013-Y)$.
\item[(b)] Explain why, for anyone between $10$ and $99$ years old, the result is $d$ followed by the two digits of the age the person reaches in $2013$ (their age, or their age plus one if their birthday in $2013$ has not happened yet).
\item[(c)] What number should replace $1763$ to make the trick work in the year $N$?
\end{enumerate}
