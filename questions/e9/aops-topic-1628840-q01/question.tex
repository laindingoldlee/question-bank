Consider the sum
\[
\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{6}+\dfrac{1}{10}+\dfrac{1}{15}+\dfrac{1}{21}+\cdots+\dfrac{1}{91}+\dfrac{1}{105},
\]
whose denominators are the triangular numbers $3,6,10,\dots,105$. Prove that the sum of the first $n$ terms is $\dfrac{n}{n+2}$, and evaluate the whole sum.
