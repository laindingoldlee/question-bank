Reduce the first two coefficients to $\dfrac{8}{7}$ and $\dfrac{5}{7}$. Their sum minus $\dfrac{1}{2}$ is $\dfrac{13}{7}-\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{26-7}{14}=\dfrac{19}{14}$. The expression is $\boxed{\dfrac{19 x}{14}}$.
