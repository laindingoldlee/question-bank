Assume that stretching the plane by a factor $k>0$ in one direction (the map $(x,y)\mapsto(x,ky)$) multiplies every area by $k$. Prove that the region enclosed by the ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1$ has area $\pi ab$.
