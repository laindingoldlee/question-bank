A Pythagorean triple $(a,b,c)$ consists of positive integers with $a^2+b^2=c^2$; it is primitive if $\gcd(a,b,c)=1$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that the hypotenuse $c$ of every primitive Pythagorean triple is a sum of two squares of positive integers.
\item[(b)] Is the same true for every Pythagorean triple?
\end{enumerate}
