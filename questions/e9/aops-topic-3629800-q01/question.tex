Let $d_1,d_2,\ldots$ be the digits $3,1,4,1,5,9,2,6,5,3,5,\ldots$ of $\pi$. Start with $u_0=8$ and define $r_i$ to be the remainder when $u_{i-1}$ is divided by $d_i$, and $u_i=u_{i-1}+r_i$. Find $r_{11}$.
