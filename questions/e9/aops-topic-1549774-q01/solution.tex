Measure grass in ``cow-days'' (the amount one cow eats in a day). Let the field start with $G$ units and grow $r$ units per day. Then
\[
G+6r=27\cdot6=162,\qquad G+9r=23\cdot9=207 .
\]
Subtracting, $3r=45$, so $r=15$ and $G=72$. With $21$ cows the grass runs out after $t$ days, where
\[
72+15t=21t\iff6t=72\iff t=\boxed{12}.
\]
(Check: $72+15\cdot12=252=21\cdot12$.)
