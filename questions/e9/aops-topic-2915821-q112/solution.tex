Put $ u=a+b=-(c+d)$. Then $-u^2(ab+cd)=28 $, so $ u^2 $ is 1 or 4. If $ u^2=1 $, both discriminants $ u^2-4ab $ and $ u^2-4cd $ are positive odd squares whose sum is 114. No two odd squares sum to 114: the possible positive odd squares at most 113 are 1, 9, 25, 49, 81, none having a complementary square. If $ u^2=4 $, the discriminants are nonnegative even squares summing to 36; only 0 and 36 work. Thus the two products $ ab,cd $ are 1 and $-8 $, in either order. Their product is $\boxed{-8}$, attained by $(a,b,c,d)=(1,1,2,-4)$.
