Let $s=x+y>0$ and $p=xy>0$. Since $x^3+y^3=s^3-3ps$, the equation becomes
\[2s^3-3ps+30p=2000,\quad\text{i.e.}\quad 2\left(s^3-1000\right)-3p(s-10)=0.\]
Factoring $s^3-1000=(s-10)(s^2+10s+100)$,
\[(s-10)\left(2s^2+20s+200-3p\right)=0.\]
Since $s^2=(x+y)^2\ge4xy=4p$, we get $2s^2-3p\ge8p-3p>0$, and $20s+200>0$. So the second factor is positive, and therefore $s=x+y=10$. $\blacksquare$
