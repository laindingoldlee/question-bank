The longest segment inside a rectangular box is a space diagonal, of length
\[\sqrt{3^2+4^2+5^2}=\sqrt{50}\approx7.07.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real w=4,h=5,dx=1.3,dy=0.9;
pair A=(0,0),B=(w,0),C=(w,h),D=(0,h),v=(dx,dy);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(B--B+v--C+v--C);draw(D--D+v--C+v);
draw(A--A+v,dashed);draw(A+v--B+v,dashed);draw(A+v--D+v,dashed);
draw(A--C+v,linewidth(1));
label("$4$",(A+B)/2,S);label("$5$",(B+C)/2+(0.1,0),E);label("$3$",B+v/2,SE);
\end{asy}

So a stick of length $7$ fits (along the diagonal) but one of length $8$ does not. The answer is $\boxed{7}$ inches.
