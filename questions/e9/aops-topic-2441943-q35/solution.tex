The rectangle measures $18$ cm by $6$ cm, with area $108\text{ cm}^2$. Each equilateral triangle has side $6$, height $3\sqrt3$ and area $\dfrac12\cdot6\cdot3\sqrt3=9\sqrt3$. The total area is
\[108+6\cdot9\sqrt3=108+54\sqrt3\approx\boxed{201.53}\text{ cm}^2.\]
(Count: the rectangle uses $8$ toothpicks and each triangle $2$ more, $8+12=20$.)

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=sqrt(3)/2;
fill((0,0)--(3,0)--(3,1)--(0,1)--cycle,gray(0.9));
draw((0,0)--(3,0)--(3,1)--(0,1)--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
for(int k=0;k<3;++k){draw((k,0)--(k+0.5,-s)--(k+1,0),linewidth(0.8));draw((k,1)--(k+0.5,1+s)--(k+1,1),linewidth(0.8));}
label("$18$",(1.5,0.5));label("$6$",(3,0.5),E);label("$6$",(0.25,1+s/2),NW);
\end{asy}
