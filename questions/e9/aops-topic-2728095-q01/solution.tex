Such a number is determined by the multiset of its digits: arrange them in non-increasing order. A multiset of $8$ digits from $\{0,1,\dots,9\}$ can be chosen in $\dbinom{8+9}{9}=\dbinom{17}{8}=24310$ ways (stars and bars). The first digit is the largest digit, so it is $0$ only for the multiset of eight $0$'s. The answer is $24310-1=\boxed{24309}$. In general there are $\dbinom{n+9}{9}-1$ such $n$-digit numbers.
