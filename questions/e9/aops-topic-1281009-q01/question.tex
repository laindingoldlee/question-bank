A relief organisation bought $15$ blankets, $5$ cots and $10$ lanterns for $\$1100$ one week; $20$ blankets, $10$ cots and $15$ lanterns for $\$1800$ the next; and $10$ blankets, $15$ cots and $5$ lanterns for $\$1525$ the third week, at the same prices. Find the price of a blanket, a cot and a lantern.
