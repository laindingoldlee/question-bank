Dividing by $5$: $3b+c+2l=220$, $4b+2c+3l=360$, $2b+3c+l=305$. From the first, $c=220-3b-2l$. Substituting:
\[
4b+440-6b-4l+3l=360\implies2b+l=80,\qquad2b+660-9b-6l+l=305\implies7b+5l=355 .
\]
From $l=80-2b$: $7b+400-10b=355$, so $b=15$, $l=50$, $c=220-45-100=75$. A blanket costs $\boxed{\$15}$, a cot $\boxed{\$75}$ and a lantern $\boxed{\$50}$.
