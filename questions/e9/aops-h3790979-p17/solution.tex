Power of a point gives $AM\cdot BM=15^2-13^2=56$. Also $AM+BM=18$, so the two lengths are the roots of $t^2-18t+56=0$, namely $14,4$. Since $AM>BM$, $\boxed{AM=14}$.

\end{asy}
