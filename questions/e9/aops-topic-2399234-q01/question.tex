Bob draws a triangle with an obtuse angle in which two of the sides have lengths $10$ and $15$. He then changes the obtuse angle into a right angle, keeping the lengths of the two sides that form this angle, and the resulting right triangle has area $75$. Can the area of Bob's original triangle be determined? If so, find it; if not, find all possible values.
