A circle with centre $O$ has radius $5$. Let $P$ be a point outside the circle with $PO=13$. A line through $P$ is tangent to the circle at $N$. The line $PO$, extended beyond $O$, meets the circle at $Q$ (so $O$ is between $P$ and $Q$). Let $R$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $Q$ to the line $PN$ (extended). Find $NR+RQ$.

$\textbf{(A)}\ \dfrac{150}{13}\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ \dfrac{153}{13}\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ 12\qquad\textbf{(D)}\ \dfrac{159}{13}\qquad\textbf{(E)}\ \dfrac{162}{13}$
