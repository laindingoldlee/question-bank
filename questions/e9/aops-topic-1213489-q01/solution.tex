$7000$ has repeated digits, so the thousands digit is $4$, $5$ or $6$.
\begin{itemize}
\item Thousands digit $4$ or $6$ (even): the units digit is one of the other $4$ even digits, and the middle two digits can be filled in $8\cdot7=56$ ways: $2\cdot4\cdot56=448$.
\item Thousands digit $5$: the units digit is any of the $5$ even digits, and the middle digits in $8\cdot7=56$ ways: $5\cdot56=280$.
\end{itemize}
Total: $448+280=\boxed{728}$.
