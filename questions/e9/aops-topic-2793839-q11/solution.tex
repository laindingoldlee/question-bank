The common perpendicular from the center bisects both chords. The inner half-chord is $1$, so the squared distance from the center to the line is $2^2-1^2=3$. The outer half-chord is $3$, so its squared radius is $3+3^2=12$. The outer area is $\boxed{12\pi}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair O=(0,0);real h=sqrt(3);draw(circle(O,2));draw(circle(O,sqrt(12)));draw((-3,h)--(3,h));draw(O--(0,h),dashed);label("$O$",O,S);label("$A$",(-3,h),NW);label("$B$",(-1,h),NW);label("$C$",(1,h),NE);label("$D$",(3,h),NE);
\end{asy}
