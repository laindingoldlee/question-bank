The distance between Arlington and Pittsburgh is $477$ miles. At the same moment, one driver leaves each city and drives towards the other city along the same straight road at $50$ miles per hour. Also at that moment, a bird leaves Pittsburgh and flies towards the driver coming from Arlington. Whenever the bird reaches one of the drivers, it immediately turns around and flies towards the other driver. Because of the wind, the bird flies at $70$ miles per hour when flying in the direction from Pittsburgh towards Arlington, and at $60$ miles per hour when flying in the direction from Arlington towards Pittsburgh. To the nearest mile, how far does the bird fly before the two drivers meet?
