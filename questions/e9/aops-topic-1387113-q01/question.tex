Let $n\ge3$. A stick of length $1$ is broken at $n-1$ points chosen independently and uniformly at random, giving $n$ pieces. Find, in terms of $n$, the probability that every three of the pieces can be the sides of a (non-degenerate) triangle. (You may use the fact that if $E_1,\dots,E_n$ are independent exponential random variables with mean $1$ and $T=E_1+\dots+E_n$, then $\left(\dfrac{E_1}{T},\dots,\dfrac{E_n}{T}\right)$ has the same distribution as the $n$ piece lengths.)
