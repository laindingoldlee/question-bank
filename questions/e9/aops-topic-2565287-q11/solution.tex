Multiply the numerator and the denominator by the conjugate $-i-7$ of the denominator:
\[(i-7)(-i-7)=-i^2-7i+7i+49=50.\]
So
\[\dfrac{\sqrt5}{i-7}=\dfrac{\sqrt5(-7-i)}{50}=\boxed{-\dfrac{7\sqrt5}{50}-\dfrac{\sqrt5}{50}\,i}.\]
