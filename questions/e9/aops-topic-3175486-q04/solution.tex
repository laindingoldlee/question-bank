Being even and divisible by $5$ forces units digit $0$, hence hundreds digit $0$. The number is therefore of form $t0t0$, and the range leaves $2020$ or $3030$. The latter is divisible by $3$, while the former is not. Thus the number is $\boxed{2020}$.
