In a year when Ana is $t$ on January 1, Bob and Carla are $t-2,t-9$. Ana is oldest, so $t^2=(t-2)^2+(t-9)^2$, giving $(t-17)(t-5)=0$. The value five would make Carla' s age negative. Thus $t=17$, with ages $17,15,8$, in $\boxed{2017}$.
