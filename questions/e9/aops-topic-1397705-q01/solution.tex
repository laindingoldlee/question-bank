The five integers have sum $5\cdot35=175$. Let the least one be $a$; then the greatest is $a+22$, and the other three are distinct odd integers strictly between them, so they are at most $a+20$, $a+18$ and $a+16$. Hence
\[
175\le a+(a+16)+(a+18)+(a+20)+(a+22)=5a+76,
\]
so $a\ge\dfrac{99}{5}=19.8$. Since $a$ is odd, $a\ge21$.

$a=21$ is possible: with least $21$ and greatest $43$ the middle three must have sum $175-21-43=111$, and $35+37+39=111$. The list $21,35,37,39,43$ consists of distinct odd integers with mean $35$ and range $22$. The answer is $\boxed{21}$.
