There are $45$ even two-digit numbers ($10,12,\dots,98$). Among them:
\begin{itemize}
\item multiples of $3$, i.e.\ multiples of $6$: $12,18,\dots,96$, which is $16-1=15$ numbers;
\item multiples of $7$, i.e.\ multiples of $14$: $14,28,\dots,98$, which is $7$ numbers;
\item multiples of both, i.e.\ multiples of $42$: $42,84$, which is $2$ numbers.
\end{itemize}
By inclusion--exclusion the number to remove is $15+7-2=20$, so the answer is $45-20=\boxed{25}$.
