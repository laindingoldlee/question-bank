The terms form an arithmetic sequence with first term $2$ and common difference $3$. The number of terms is $\dfrac{92-2}{3}+1=31$. The sum is the number of terms times the average of the first and last terms:
\[
31\cdot\dfrac{2+92}{2}=31\cdot47=\boxed{1457}.
\]
