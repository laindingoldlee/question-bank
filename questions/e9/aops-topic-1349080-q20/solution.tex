\textbf{Vertices.} $-5x+47=\dfrac{3}{2}x-18$ gives $x=10$, so $(10,-3)$. $-5x+47=\dfrac{-7x+409}{17}$ gives $-85x+799=-7x+409$, $x=5$, so $(5,22)$. $\dfrac{3}{2}x-18=\dfrac{-7x+409}{17}$ gives $51x-612=-14x+818$, $x=22$, so $(22,15)$.

\textbf{Centre.} The centre $(h,k)$ is equidistant from the vertices. From $(10,-3)$ and $(5,22)$: $(h-10)^2+(k+3)^2=(h-5)^2+(k-22)^2$, which simplifies to $-10h+50k=400$, i.e.\ $h=5k-40$. From $(10,-3)$ and $(22,15)$: $(h-10)^2+(k+3)^2=(h-22)^2+(k-15)^2$, which simplifies to $24h+36k=600$, i.e.\ $2h+3k=50$. Substituting, $10k-80+3k=50$, so $k=10$ and $h=10$, so the centre is $O=(10,10)$. The radius is the distance from $(10,10)$ to $(10,-3)$, which is $13$ (check: $(5,22)$ and $(22,15)$ are also at distance $\sqrt{25+144}=13$).
