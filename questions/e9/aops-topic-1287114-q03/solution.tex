$45=6\cdot7+3$. Any $42$ consecutive days contain exactly $6$ Mondays, and the remaining $3$ days contain at most one more Monday; choosing them to avoid Monday (for example, the period starts on a Tuesday, so the extra days are Tuesday to Thursday) gives exactly $6$. The minimum is $\boxed{\textbf{(b) }6}$.
