The grid contains the $16$ points whose $x$- and $y$-coordinates both lie in $\{0,1,2,3\}$. A square with all four of its vertices among these $16$ points has area $A$. What is the sum of all possible values of $A$?

(A student argued: "the grid allows squares of sizes $1\times1$, $2\times2$, $3\times3$ and $4\times4$, so the answer is $1+4+9+16=30$." Explain what is wrong.)
