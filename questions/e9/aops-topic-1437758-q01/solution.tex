A point $(x,y)$ lies above the line exactly when $y>2x+7$. Compare:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
(x,y) & 2x+7 & \text{above?}\\ \hline
(3,10) & 13 & \text{no}\\
(6,20) & 19 & \text{yes}\\
(12,35) & 31 & \text{yes}\\
(18,40) & 43 & \text{no}\\
(20,50) & 47 & \text{yes}
\end{array}
\]
The sum of the $x$-coordinates of the points above the line is $6+12+20=\boxed{38}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real k=0.4; pair P(real x,real y){return (x,k*y);}
draw(P(0,0)--P(23,0),Arrow(TeXHead)); draw(P(0,0)--P(0,58),Arrow(TeXHead));
label("$x$",P(23,0),E); label("$y$",P(0,58),N);
for(int t=10;t<=50;t+=10){draw(P(0,t)--P(-0.4,t)); label("$"+string(t)+"$",P(-0.4,t),W,fontsize(8pt));}
for(int t=5;t<=20;t+=5){draw(P(t,0)--P(t,-1)); label("$"+string(t)+"$",P(t,-1),S,fontsize(8pt));}
draw(P(0,7)--P(22,51),blue+linewidth(0.9)); label("$y=2x+7$",P(22,51),E,blue);
pair[] Q={P(3,10),P(6,20),P(12,35),P(18,40),P(20,50)};
bool[] up={false,true,true,false,true};
string[] L={"$(3,10)$","$(6,20)$","$(12,35)$","$(18,40)$","$(20,50)$"};
for(int i=0;i<5;++i){dot(Q[i],up[i]?red:black); label(L[i],Q[i],up[i]?2*NW:SE,up[i]?red:black);}
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The red points are above the line.
