Measure distances in laps. Bina runs $\dfrac{12}{35}$ and Andreas $\dfrac{17}{35}$ laps per minute. Running in opposite directions, the distance between them along the track (measured in Bina's direction) grows at $\dfrac{12}{35}+\dfrac{17}{35}=\dfrac{29}{35}$ laps per minute, and they meet exactly when this distance is a whole number of laps, i.e.\ at the times
\[
t=\dfrac{35k}{29}\text{ minutes},\qquad k=1,2,\dots
\]
During the run ($0<t\le35$) this gives $k=1,\dots,29$, and $k=29$ is the finish. So they pass each other $\boxed{28}$ times in between.
