Bina and Andreas start at the same point on a circular track and begin running in opposite directions at constant speeds. After $35$ minutes they are both back at the starting point for the first time together; Bina has run $12$ laps and Andreas has run $17$ laps. How many times did they pass each other during their run, not counting the times they were together at the start and at the finish?
