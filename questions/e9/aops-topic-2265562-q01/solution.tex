There are $4^4=256$ equally likely sequences of rolls. A nondecreasing sequence is determined by how many times each of the values $1,2,3,4$ occurs (then the order is forced). So we count solutions of
\[n_1+n_2+n_3+n_4=4\]
in nonnegative integers. By stars and bars there are $\dbinom{4+3}{3}=\dbinom73=35$ of them. The probability is
\[\boxed{\dfrac{35}{256}}.\]
