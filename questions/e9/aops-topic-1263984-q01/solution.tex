Let $R$ be the circumradius of the octagon. Each side subtends $45^\circ$ at the centre, so $5=2R\sin22.5^\circ$ and
\[R^2=\dfrac{25}{4\sin^2 22.5^\circ}=\dfrac{25}{2(1-\cos45^\circ)}=\dfrac{25}{2-\sqrt2}=\dfrac{25(2+\sqrt2)}{2}.\]

label("$5$",(V[1]+V[2])/2,N);

\emph{Upper bound.} Any rectangle inside the octagon lies inside its circumcircle. A rectangle with diagonal at most $2R$ has area at most $\dfrac{1}{2}(2R)^2=2R^2$ (its area is $\dfrac{1}{2}d^2\sin\theta$, where $d$ is the diagonal and $\theta$ the angle between the diagonals).

\emph{Attained.} Joining every other vertex of the octagon gives a square inscribed in the circumcircle, with area $2R^2$.

So the largest rectangle is this square, with area
\[2R^2=\boxed{25\left(2+\sqrt2\right)}=50+25\sqrt2\approx85.4\]
and side $R\sqrt2=5\sqrt{2+\sqrt2}\approx9.24$.
