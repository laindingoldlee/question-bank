Put $AB=L$, $BC=H$, larger side $s=\dfrac{LH}{L+H}$, and smaller side $t=\dfrac{HL^2}{(L+H)^2}$. These formulas follow by substituting the upper-right corners into $y=H(1-\dfrac{x}{L})$. Let $r=\dfrac{L}{L+H}$, so $t=rs$ and $\dfrac{s^2}{LH}=r(1-r)$. The shaded gap has area $\dfrac{1}{2}t(s-t)$, and triangle $ABC$ has area $\dfrac{1}{2}LH$. Their ratio is $\dfrac{t(s-t)}{LH}=r^2(1-r)^2=\left(\dfrac{s^2}{LH}\right)^2=\dfrac{9}{289}$. Positivity gives $\dfrac{s^2}{LH}=\dfrac{3}{17}$, so the desired ratio is $\boxed{\dfrac{6}{17}}$. Both possible aspect ratios satisfy the same calculation.
