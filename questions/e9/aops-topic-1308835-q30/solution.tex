Here $\cos x\ne0$ (otherwise $\sin x=0$ too), so $\tan x=\dfrac{1}{2015}$. Then
\[
\sin2x=\dfrac{2\tan x}{1+\tan^2x}=\dfrac{\dfrac{2}{2015}}{1+\dfrac{1}{2015^2}}=\dfrac{2\cdot2015}{2015^2+1}=\boxed{\dfrac{4030}{4060226}}=\dfrac{2015}{2030113}.
\]
