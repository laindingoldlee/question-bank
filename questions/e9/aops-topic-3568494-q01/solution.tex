The second equation gives $y=-\dfrac{3}{25}x$. The first gives $z=-4-\dfrac{62}{25}x$. Substituting in the third yields $\dfrac{414}{25}x+16=4$, so $x=-\dfrac{50}{69}$, $y=\dfrac{6}{69}$, and $z=-\dfrac{152}{69}$. Multiplication by $-69$ gives $\boxed{(50,-6,152)}$.
