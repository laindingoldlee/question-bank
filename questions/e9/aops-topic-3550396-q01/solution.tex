For n 1 the range is 0, impossible. For n 2 a unique mode requires equal entries, again range 0. For n 3 a unique mode must repeat at least twice; since it is x and the mean is x, the third entry is also x, giving range 0. For n 4 the list $(1,2,2,3)$ has mean 2, median 2, unique mode 2, and range 2. Hence the least n is $\boxed 4$.
