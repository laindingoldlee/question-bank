We have $f(x)=(x-1)^2(x-2)$, so the equation requires $f(x)=1$ or $f(x)=2$. For $x\le2$, $f(x)\le0$. For $x>2$, both positive factors strictly increase, so $f$ strictly increases from 0 to infinity. Thus each equation has exactly one real solution. The root of $f(x)=1$ lies between $\dfrac{12}{5}$ and 3, since $f(\dfrac{12}{5})=\dfrac{98}{125}<1$ and $f(3)=4$. The root of $f(x)=2$ lies between $\dfrac{13}{5}$ and 3, since $f(\dfrac{13}{5})=\dfrac{192}{125}<2$. Their sum therefore lies strictly between 5 and 6, giving $\boxed{\lfloor n\rfloor=5}$.
