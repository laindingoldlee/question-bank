Join the center to all vertices, producing $n$ congruent triangles. Bisecting one gives a right triangle with opposite side $\dfrac{x}{2}$ and angle $\dfrac{\pi}{n}$ at the center. Therefore the apothem is
\[
h=\dfrac{x}{2\tan(\dfrac{\pi}{n})}.
\]
Each central triangle has area $\dfrac{xh}{2}$, so the polygon has area
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{nx^2}{4\tan(\dfrac{\pi}{n})}}.
\]
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
int n=7;pair O=(0,0),A=dir(0),B=dir(360.0/n),H=(A+B)/2;for(int j=0;j<n;++j){draw(dir(360.0*j/n)--dir(360.0*(j+1)/n));draw(O--dir(360.0*j/n),gray);}draw(O--H,red);label("$x$",(A+B)/2,NE);label("$h$",H/2,NW);label("$O$",O,SW);
\end{asy}
