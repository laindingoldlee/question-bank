$3^{2^n}\equiv-1\pmod p$, so $3^{2^{n+1}}\equiv1$. The order $d$ of $3$ modulo $p$ divides $2^{n+1}$ but not $2^n$ (otherwise $3^{2^n}\equiv1\ne-1$, as $p$ is odd). Hence $d=2^{n+1}$. By Fermat's little theorem $d\mid p-1$, so $2^{n+1}\mid p-1$. $\blacksquare$
