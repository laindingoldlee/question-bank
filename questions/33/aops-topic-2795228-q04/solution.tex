For any odd $p$, $p^2-1=(p-1)(p+1)$ is divisible by 8. Since $p$ is not divisible by 3, its square is 1 modulo 3. The same holds for $q$. Thus $p^2-q^2$ is divisible by both 8 and 3 and therefore by 24.
