Jela and Benn play a game in the complex plane. All numbers chosen in the game must have absolute value at most $187$. The players take turns, Jela first. On each turn the player chooses a complex number whose distance to every number chosen so far (by either player) is greater than $1$. The first player who cannot choose a number loses. Which player has a winning strategy?
