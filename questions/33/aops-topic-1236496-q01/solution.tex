Write the numbers as $m-3,m-1,m+1,m+3$ ($m$ even). Their product is $\left(m^2-9\right)\left(m^2-1\right)=t^2-10t+9$ with $t=m^2$. So $t^2-10t+9=99900009$, i.e. $t^2-10t-99900000=0$, giving $t=\dfrac{10+\sqrt{100+399600000}}{2}=\dfrac{10+19990}{2}=10000$. So $m=100$ and the numbers are $97,99,101,103$. The sum of their squares is
\[(m-3)^2+(m-1)^2+(m+1)^2+(m+3)^2=4m^2+20=\boxed{40020}.\]
