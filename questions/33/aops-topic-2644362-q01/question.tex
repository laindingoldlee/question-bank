An Argentine ant colony begins day $1$ with a single queen and no other ants. During each day, every queen present at the start of that day lays $60$ eggs, which hatch immediately. At the end of each day the queens are recounted: there is the original queen plus one new queen for every complete $120$ ants, i.e. $1+\left\lfloor\dfrac{T}{120}\right\rfloor$ queens, where $T$ is the total number of ants. (So after day $1$ there are $61$ ants and $1$ queen, after day $2$ there are $121$ ants and $2$ queens, and after day $3$ there are $241$ ants and $3$ queens.) How many ants are there at the end of day $10$?
