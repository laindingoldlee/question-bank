Tom starts with $N$ coins; Jerry has none. Jerry moves first. On each turn a player transfers a positive number of coins from the other player to themselves, and no transfer size may ever be repeated. The game ends when the next player cannot move. Over all legal plays, what is the greatest final number Jerry can have for (a)$N=13$; (b)$N=50$?
