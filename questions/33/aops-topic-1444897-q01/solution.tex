A five-digit palindrome has the form $\overline{abcba}$. After $15951$, the next palindrome with first two digits $15$ would need middle digit greater than $9$, so the next one starts with $16$: it is $\overline{16c61}$ with the smallest $c$, namely $16061$. The bus travelled $16061-15951=110$ miles in $2$ hours, at
\[
\dfrac{110}{2}=\boxed{55}\ \text{miles per hour}.
\]
