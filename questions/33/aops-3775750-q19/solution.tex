A table of only knaves is impossible: their assertion would be true. Every occupied table therefore contains a knight, and each knight\textquotesingle s assertion requires at least two knights there. With all ten tables occupied, at least twenty people are knights, so at most eighty are knaves. Two knights and eight knaves at every table attain this bound; each knight speaks truth and each knave lies. The maximum is $\boxed{80}$.
