Let $m\ge n\ge0$ be integers. A lattice path from $(0,0)$ to $(m,n)$ uses unit steps right $(1,0)$ and up $(0,1)$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that the number of such paths that never go above the line $y=x$ (touching it is allowed) is
\[\dfrac{m-n+1}{m+1}\dbinom{m+n}{n}.\]
\item[(b)] Deduce that the number of such paths from $(0,0)$ to $(n,n)$ is the Catalan number $C_n=\dfrac{1}{n+1}\dbinom{2n}{n}$.
\item[(c)] Compute the numbers for the endpoints $(5,3)$ and $(5,5)$.
\end{enumerate}
