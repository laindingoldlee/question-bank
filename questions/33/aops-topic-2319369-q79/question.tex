You have a job at a restaurant paying $8$ dollars per hour and a job at a store paying $10$ dollars per hour. You want to earn at least $200$ dollars per week, working at most $25$ hours per week in total.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Give three different ways to split your hours.
\item[(b)] How many possibilities are there if both numbers of hours are whole numbers?
\end{enumerate}
