A car radiator has a capacity of $17$ quarts of fluid. It is full and contains a $41\%$ solution of coolant. How much of the solution must be drained and replaced by $100\%$ coolant to bring the mixture to a $50\%$ solution? Give the exact amount and its value to the nearest tenth of a quart.
