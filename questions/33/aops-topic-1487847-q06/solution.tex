Let $x$ quarts be drained and replaced by pure coolant. The coolant afterwards is
\[
0.41(17-x)+x=6.97+0.59x ,
\]
and it must equal $0.5\cdot17=8.5$ quarts. So $0.59x=1.53$ and
\[
x=\dfrac{153}{59}\approx\boxed{2.6}\ \text{quarts}.
\]
