Let $\sigma(n)$ denote the sum of the positive divisors of $n$. We need $n\ge\sigma(n)$. For $n=1$, $\sigma(1)=1$, so equality holds. For $n>1$, both $1$ and $n$ are divisors, so $\sigma(n)\ge n+1>n$. So only $n=1$ works, and the answer is $\boxed{1}$.

(``Not less than'' means $n\ge\sigma(n)$. Perfect and abundant numbers satisfy the opposite type of inequality, comparing $\sigma(n)$ with $2n$, so they are not relevant here.)
