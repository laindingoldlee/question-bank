The angle-bisector theorem makes the legs $9t,13t$. Their area is $\dfrac{1}{2}(9t)(13t)=585$, so $t^2=10$. The hypotenuse is $t\sqrt{250}=50$, and its median from the right angle is half of it. Thus $\boxed{25}$.
