Multiplying by $b$ and $a$ gives $ab+1=3b=5a$, so $\dfrac{a}{b}=\dfrac{3}{5}$. Consequently $\dfrac{a+b}{a-b}=\dfrac{3+5}{3-5}=\boxed{-4}$. Since $b\ne0$ and $a=\dfrac{3b}{5}$, the final denominator is nonzero.
