We need $x\ne0$ for $\dfrac{12}{7x}$, and $x^x$ must be defined. Subtracting $x^x$ from both sides and multiplying by $7x$,
\[-7x+12=\dfrac{12}{7x}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad -49x^2+84x=12\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad 49x^2-84x+12=0 .\]
Hence
\[x=\dfrac{84\pm\sqrt{84^2-4\cdot49\cdot12}}{98}=\dfrac{84\pm\sqrt{4704}}{98}=\dfrac{84\pm28\sqrt6}{98}=\boxed{\dfrac{6\pm2\sqrt6}{7}}.\]
Both roots are positive (since $2\sqrt6\approx4.90<6$), so $x^x$ is defined for them, and both are solutions: $x\approx1.557$ and $x\approx0.157$.
