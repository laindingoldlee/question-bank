Two lines with slopes $m$ and $n$, where $m>n>0$, intersect at the origin, and the line $y=x$ bisects the angle between them.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $mn=1$.
\item[(b)] If $m+n=2\sqrt{65}$, find $m-n$.
\end{enumerate}
