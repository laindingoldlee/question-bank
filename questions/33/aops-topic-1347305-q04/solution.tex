\[
\boxed{z^w-y^w=(z-y)\left(z^{w-1}+z^{w-2}y+\cdots+zy^{w-2}+y^{w-1}\right)=(z-y)\sum_{k=0}^{w-1}z^{w-1-k}y^k}.
\]
Proof: multiplying the sum by $z$ gives $z^w+z^{w-1}y+\cdots+zy^{w-1}$, and multiplying it by $y$ gives $z^{w-1}y+\cdots+zy^{w-1}+y^w$; subtracting, everything cancels except $z^w-y^w$.
