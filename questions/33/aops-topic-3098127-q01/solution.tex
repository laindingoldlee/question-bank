Let $T$ be the permutation induced by $S$, and let $x$ be the starting state. Finiteness and the pigeonhole principle give $T^i(x)=T^j(x)$ for some $0\le i<j$. Apply the inverse permutation $T^{-i}$ to obtain $x=T^{j-i}(x)$. Thus a positive number of repetitions returns to the starting state. Reversibility is what rules out an initial nonperiodic tail.
