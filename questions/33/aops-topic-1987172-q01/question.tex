Stacey has some pennies, nickels and dimes (at least one coin of each kind) divided between her left and right pockets. She has the same number of coins in each pocket, and the coins in her two pockets have the same total value. Stacey does not have the same number of pennies in each pocket. What is the minimum total value of the coins in both pockets combined?
