After $n$ days she has earned $1+2+\cdots+2^{n-1}=2^n-1$ cents. We need $2^n-1>100000$. Since $2^{16}=65536$ and $2^{17}=131072$, the answer is $\boxed{17}$ days. (Using $2^{n-1}$, the pay on day $n$ alone, instead of the total, or mistaking $65535$ cents for more than $\$1000$, gives the wrong answer $16$.)
