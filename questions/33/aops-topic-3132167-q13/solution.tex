Each round gives a win with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, loss with $\dfrac{1}{4}$, and replay with $\dfrac{1}{4}$. If $p$ is eventual win probability, $p=\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{4}p$, so $\boxed{p=\dfrac{2}{3}}$. Endless ties have probability 0.
