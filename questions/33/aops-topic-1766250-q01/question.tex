Shi Ting's mother gives her $8$ chocolate bars on each day she practises the piano for $30$ minutes or more, and $5$ chocolate bars on each day she practises for less than $30$ minutes (she ``falls short of the $30$-minute mark''). She practises on $18$ days.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that she cannot have earned a total of $53$ chocolate bars.
\item[(b)] Which totals are possible?
\item[(c)] If she earned $117$ chocolate bars, on how many days did she fall short of the $30$-minute mark?
\end{enumerate}
