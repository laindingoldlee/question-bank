The divisor lattice of $2024=2^3\cdot11\cdot23$ has 16 elements. The number of ordered pairs d,e with d dividing e, including equality, is $(1+2+3+4)(1+2)^2=90$. Remove the 16 equal pairs; each remaining comparable unordered pair occurs once in this oriented count. Thus the probability is $\dfrac{74}{\binom{16}2}=\boxed{\dfrac{37}{60}}$.
