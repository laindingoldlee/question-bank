Let the cube have edge $s$, so $s^3=624$, and let the vertex be $P$, with $X$, $Y$, $Z$ the midpoints of the three edges at $P$. The segments $PX$, $PY$, $PZ$ have length $\dfrac {s}{2}$ and are mutually perpendicular.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair d=(0.45,0.32);
pair P0=(0,0),P1=(1,0),P2=(1,1),P3=(0,1);
draw(P0--P1--P2--P3--cycle); draw(P1--P1+d--P2+d--P2); draw(P3--P3+d--P2+d);
draw(P0--P0+d,dashed); draw(P0+d--P1+d,dashed); draw(P0+d--P3+d,dashed);
pair X=(0.5,0), Y=(0,0.5), Z=0.5*d;
fill(P0--X--Y--cycle,lightred); 
draw(X--Y--Z--cycle,red); draw(P0--X,red+linewidth(1)); draw(P0--Y,red+linewidth(1)); draw(P0--Z,red+dashed);
dot(P0); dot(X); dot(Y); dot(Z);
label("$P$",P0,SW); label("$X$",X,S); label("$Y$",Y,W); label("$Z$",Z,1.3*dir(169));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Take the right triangle $PXY$ as the base: its area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac {s}{2}\right)^2=\dfrac{s^2}{8}$, and the height from $Z$ is $PZ=\dfrac {s}{2}$. So the volume is
\[\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{s^2}{8}\cdot\dfrac{s}{2}=\dfrac{s^3}{48}=\dfrac{624}{48}=\boxed{13}\text{ cubic centimetres}.\]
