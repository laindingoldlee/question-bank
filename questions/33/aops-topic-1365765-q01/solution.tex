Each term splits as a difference:
\[
\dfrac{1}{k^2-1}=\dfrac{1}{(k-1)(k+1)}=\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{1}{k-1}-\dfrac{1}{k+1}\right).
\]
Summing for $k=2,\dots,6$, the terms telescope: every $\dfrac{1}{j}$ with $3\le j\le5$ appears once with each sign, leaving
\[
\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{1}{1}+\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{6}-\dfrac{1}{7}\right)=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{42+21-7-6}{42}=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{50}{42}=\boxed{\dfrac{25}{42}}.
\]
This is fast enough for a countdown round: the answer is $\dfrac{1}{2}\left(1+\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{6}-\dfrac{1}{7}\right)$ at a glance.
