Complex conjugation preserves a polynomial equation with real coefficients, so $\boxed{2-3i}$ is also a root. The quadratic is $(x-(2+3i))(x-(2-3i))=(x-2)^2+9=\boxed{x^2-4x+13}$. The number $-2+3i$ is not the conjugate and is not forced.
