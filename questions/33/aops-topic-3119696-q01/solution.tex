Let $n_j$ count occurrences of face $j$. Then $\sum n_j=10$ and $\sum j^2n_j=40$. Subtracting gives $3n_2+8n_3+15n_4+24n_5+35n_6=30$. Thus $n_6=0,n_5\le1,n_4\le2,n_3\le3$. Checking these small ranges gives the count vectors and multinomial counts $(8,0,0,2,0,0):45$, $(7,2,0,0,1,0):360$, $(5,2,3,0,0,0):2520$, $(4,5,0,1,0,0):1260$, and $(0,10,0,0,0,0):1$. They total $\boxed{4186}$. The most frequent multiset is five ones, two twos, and three threes, with $2520$ orders.
