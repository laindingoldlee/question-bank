With $r$, $s$, $p$ pounds: $r+s+p=9$, $r=2s$ and $2r+s+1.5p=14.5$. Substituting $r=2s$ gives $3s+p=9$ and $5s+1.5p=14.5$. Then $p=9-3s$, so $5s+13.5-4.5s=14.5$ and $s=2$. Hence $\boxed{4\text{ lb raisins},\ 2\text{ lb sunflower seeds},\ 3\text{ lb peanuts}}$. (The poster's three equations were correct.)
