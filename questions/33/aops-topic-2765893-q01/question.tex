A store makes $9$ pounds of trail mix from raisins at $\$2$ per pound, sunflower seeds at $\$1$ per pound and chocolate-covered peanuts at $\$1.50$ per pound. The mix has twice as many pounds of raisins as of sunflower seeds, and the total cost is $\$14.50$. How many pounds of each ingredient are used?
