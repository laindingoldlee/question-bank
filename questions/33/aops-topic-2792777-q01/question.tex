Bryan is shown $10$ boxes, exactly one of which contains a gold coin. He takes one box at random without opening it. Carl, who knows where the coin is, opens $5$ of the other boxes, all empty. Bryan returns his box and picks one of the remaining $4$ closed boxes at random. What is the probability that he gets the coin?
