The terms are $5\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^k$, so this is a geometric series with first term $5\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}=\dfrac{10}{3}$ and ratio $\dfrac{2}{3}$; since $\left|\dfrac{2}{3}\right|<1$ it converges, and
\[
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}5\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^k=\dfrac{\dfrac{10}{3}}{1-\dfrac{2}{3}}=\dfrac{\dfrac{10}{3}}{\dfrac{1}{3}}=\boxed{10}.
\]
