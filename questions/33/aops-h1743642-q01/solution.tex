For $0<r<1$, let $T=\sum_{k\ge1}kr^k$ and $S=\sum_{k\ge1}k^2r^k$. Shifting the convergent series gives
\[(1-r)T=\sum_{k\ge1}r^k=\dfrac{r}{1-r},\]
so $T=\dfrac{r}{(1-r)^2}$. Similarly,
\[(1-r)S=\sum_{k\ge1}(2k-1)r^k=2T-\dfrac{r}{1-r}=\dfrac{r(1+r)}{(1-r)^2}.\]
Thus $S=\dfrac{r(1+r)}{(1-r)^3}$. With $r=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}$, this becomes $\sqrt2(\sqrt2+1)^4=\boxed{24+17\sqrt2}$.
