The row must begin and end in red. Red $5$ can be adjacent only to blue $5$, so it is an endpoint; red $4$ similarly must be the other endpoint beside blue $4$. Red $3$ must be between blue $3$ and blue $6$, while red $2$ must lie between blue $4$ and blue $6$. These force the row, up to reversal, to be $5_R,5_B,1_R,3_B,3_R,6_B,2_R,4_B,4_R$. The blue cards in positions $4$ and $6$ sum to $3+6=\boxed9$.
