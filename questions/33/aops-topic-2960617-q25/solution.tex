Let $p_n$ be the probability of being at the starting leaf after $n$ hops. It can return only when currently elsewhere, giving $p_{n+1}=\dfrac{1-p_n}{3}$. Starting with $p_0=1$, we obtain $p_1=0,p_2=\dfrac{1}{3},p_3=\dfrac{2}{9}$, and $p_4=\dfrac{1-\dfrac{2}{9}}{3}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{27}}$.
