There are $6\cdot6=36$ equally likely ordered outcomes. The sum $9$ arises from $(3,6),(4,5),(5,4),(6,3)$, which is $4$ outcomes. So the probability is $\dfrac{4}{36}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{9}}$.
