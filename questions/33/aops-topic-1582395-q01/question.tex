In triangle $ABC$ with incentre $I$, the incircle touches $AB$, $BC$ and $CA$ at $X$, $Y$ and $Z$ respectively. Prove or disprove:
\[
[IXAZ]=[IXBY]=[IYCZ]=\dfrac{[ABC]}{3}
\]
for every triangle $ABC$. (Here $[\cdot]$ denotes area.)
