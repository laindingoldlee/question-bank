Arturo needs to move six paintings by different artists between floors in a museum:

$\bullet$ Paul's painting: Floor $4$ to Floor $9$

$\bullet$ Yayoi's painting: Floor $2$ to Floor $7$

$\bullet$ Frida's painting: Floor $4$ to Floor $1$

$\bullet$ Rene's painting: Floor $2$ to Floor $4$

$\bullet$ Leonardo's painting: Floor $7$ to Floor $4$

$\bullet$ Georgia's painting: Floor $8$ to Floor $2$

The paintings can be moved in any order, but the elevator can only hold one painting at a time. Arturo starts and ends on Floor $1$.What is the smallest number of floors he can travel and deliver all the paintings to their desired destinations? (For example, if he takes the elevator from Floor $1$ to Floor $7$,then from Floor$7$ to Floor $3$, he has traveled $(7-1) +(7-3) = 10$ floors.)
