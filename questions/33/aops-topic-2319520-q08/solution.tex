From a date in one year to the same date in the next year there are $365$ days, or $366$ days if the interval contains a February 29. Since $365=7\cdot52+1$ and $366=7\cdot52+2$, the day of the week moves forward by $1$, or by $2$ when the interval contains February 29.

Because the date is after the end of February, the interval from the date in year $Y$ to the same date in year $Y+1$ contains February 29 exactly when $Y+1$ is a leap year. So, starting from Tuesday in 2020:
\begin{itemize}
\item 2021: $+1$, Wednesday; 2022: $+1$, Thursday; 2023: $+1$, Friday;
\item 2024 (leap year): $+2$, Sunday; 2025: $+1$, Monday; 2026: $+1$, Tuesday.
\end{itemize}
So the anniversary next falls on a Tuesday in $\boxed{2026}$.
