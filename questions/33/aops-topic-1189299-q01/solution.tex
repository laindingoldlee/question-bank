Let $y=\sqrt{6+\sqrt{6+\cdots}}$. Then $y=\sqrt{6+y}$, so $y^2-y-6=(y-3)(y+2)=0$ and $y=3$ (the sequence of truncations increases to $3$). The requested value is $\sqrt{2+y}=\boxed{\sqrt5}$.
