Since $\log_2\dfrac{n}{2^k}=m-k$, the sum is $S=\sum_{k=0}^m(m-k)2^k$. Then
\[S=\sum_{j=0}^{m}j\,2^{m-j}=2^m\sum_{j=0}^m\dfrac{j}{2^j}=2^m\left(2-\dfrac{m+2}{2^m}\right)=2^{m+1}-m-2,\]
using $\sum_{j=0}^m\dfrac{j}{2^j}=2-\dfrac{m+2}{2^m}$. In terms of $n$:
\[S=\boxed{2n-\log_2n-2}.\]
(Check $n=4$: $1\cdot2+2\cdot1+4\cdot0=4=8-2-2$.)
