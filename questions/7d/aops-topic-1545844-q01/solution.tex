Since $24=26-2$ and $26=2\times13$, it remains to make $2$ from the unused numbers $8$ and $4$, and $8\div4=2$. So
\[
\boxed{2\times13-8\div4=24}.
\]
(Other answers exist, for example $13\times8\div4-2=26-2=24$.)
