Call a position losing if the player to move loses with best play. $(0,0)$ is losing. Working upward, a position is losing iff no move reaches a losing position; this gives the losing positions $(0,0)$, $(1,2)$, $(3,5)$, $(4,7)$, $(6,10),\dots$ (and their reverses): each new pair uses the smallest number not yet used, with difference one more than the previous pair. From each of these, every move changes one pile or both by the same amount, so it cannot reach another pair in the list (all smaller numbers and all differences are already used).

$(7,9)$ is not losing, and Tom can move to the losing position $(7,4)$ by taking $5$ rocks from the second pile. So $\boxed{\text{Tom}}$ wins, and he keeps moving to positions on the list.
