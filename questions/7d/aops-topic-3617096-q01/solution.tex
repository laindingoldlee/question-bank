Mary's first statement excludes $1,10$ for her. Given this, Jerry would know her number if his number were $1,2,9,$ or $10$, so his reply excludes those four values. Mary now knows precisely when just one of her two neighbouring integers survives. This occurs for Mary's values $2,3,8,9$, whose surviving neighbours are respectively $3,4,7,8$. For $4,5,6,7$ both neighbours remain possible. Thus the requested sum is $2+3+8+9=\boxed{22}$.
