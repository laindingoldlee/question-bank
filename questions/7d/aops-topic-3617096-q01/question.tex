Larry chooses two consecutive integers from $1$ through $10$, tells Mary one and Jerry the other, and makes this setup common knowledge. Mary says, ``I do not know your number. Jerry replies, ``I do not know your number either. Mary then says, ``Now I know your number. Assuming correct logical reasoning, find the sum of all possible numbers Mary has.
