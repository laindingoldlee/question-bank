If at least two letters occur and total length is $N$, the multinomial count is at least $N$, so $N\le12$. Exhaustively checking the three letter counts within this bound gives only permutations of counts $(11,1,0)$ or $(2,1,1)$. There are 6 assignments of the first count pattern and 3 of the second, each giving 12 words. Total $(6+3)12=\boxed{108}$.\begin{verbatim}
from math import factorial
patterns=[]
for a in range(13):
 for b in range(13-a):
  for c in range(13-a-b):
   n=a+b+c
   if n and factorial(n)//(factorial(a)*factorial(b)*factorial(c))==12:
    patterns.append((a,b,c))
assert len(patterns)==9
\end{verbatim}
