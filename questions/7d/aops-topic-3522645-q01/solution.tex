All $\binom{6942}{4269}$ subsets are equally likely, and $\binom{6939}{4269}$ use only diamonds. Equivalently, all three non-diamonds must lie among the $2673$ unselected cards. Therefore the probability is\[\boxed{\dfrac{\binom{6939}{4269}}{\binom{6942}{4269}}=\dfrac{2673\cdot2672\cdot2671}{6942\cdot6941\cdot6940}}.\]
