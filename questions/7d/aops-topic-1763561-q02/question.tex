I want to eat $100$ bananas, and my brother Walt is willing to help. I eat $3$ bananas per minute and Walt eats $5$ bananas per minute. I start alone and eat for $15$ minutes; then Walt joins me and we eat together until all $100$ bananas are gone. How many bananas does Walt eat?
