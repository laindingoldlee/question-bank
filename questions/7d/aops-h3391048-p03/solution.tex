In fact $v_3(2^{3^n}+1)=n+1$ for every $n\ge0$. The base case is $2+1=3$. If $u=2^{3^n}$, then $u\equiv-1\pmod3$ and
\[
u^3+1=(u+1)(u^2-u+1).
\]
Writing $u=3t-1$ gives $u^2-u+1=3(3t^2-3t+1)$, whose 3-adic valuation is exactly $1$. Each step therefore adds exactly one factor of $3$. At $n=100$ the valuation is $101$, proving the claim.
