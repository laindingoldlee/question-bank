Primality of $n+1$ forces $n\ge1$. Above 10 and at most 15, the only $n$ with $n+1$ prime is 12, but $12^2+3=147$ is composite. At $n=10$, $n+1=11$ and $n^2+3=103$ are prime. Thus $\boxed{10}$.
