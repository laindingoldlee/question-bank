Let the prism dimensions be $a,b,h$. The cone radius satisfies $2r\le\min(a,b)$, and its height is at most $h$. Thus its volume is at most $\dfrac{\pi}{3}\cdot\dfrac{ab}{4}\,h=\dfrac{250\pi}{3}$, since $\min(a,b)^2\le ab$. Equality is attained with a square prism base, the circle inscribed in it, and the cone apex on the top face. Therefore the maximum is $\boxed{\dfrac{250\pi}{3}}$.
