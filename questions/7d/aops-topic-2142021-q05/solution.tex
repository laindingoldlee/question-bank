Each sector has angle $120^\circ$ and radius $6$. When it is rolled into a cone, the radius $6$ becomes the slant height, and the arc of the sector becomes the circumference of the base.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0);
filldraw(O--arc(O,6,90,210)--cycle,paleblue,linewidth(0.8));
draw(circle(O,6),linewidth(0.9));
draw(O--6*dir(90)); draw(O--6*dir(210)); draw(O--6*dir(330));
draw(arc(O,0.9,90,210)); label("$6$",3*dir(90),E); label("$120^\circ$",1.7*dir(150),fontsize(8pt));
pair T=(13,4*sqrt(2)), M=(13,0);
draw(shift(M)*xscale(2)*yscale(0.6)*arc((0,0),1,180,360),linewidth(0.9));
draw(shift(M)*xscale(2)*yscale(0.6)*arc((0,0),1,0,180),dashed);
draw((11,0)--T--(15,0),linewidth(0.9));
draw(T--M,red+dashed); draw(M--(15,0),blue);
draw(M+(0.3,0)--M+(0.3,0.3)--M+(0,0.3));
label("$6$",(T+(15,0))/2,NE); label("$2$",(M+(15,0))/2,S,blue); label("$h$",(T+M)/2,W,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The arc length is $\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot2\pi\cdot6=4\pi$, so the base radius $r$ satisfies $2\pi r=4\pi$, $r=2$. The height, the base radius and the slant height form a right triangle, so
\[h=\sqrt{6^2-2^2}=\sqrt{32}=\boxed{4\sqrt2}\text{ inches}.\]
