The leading terms in the sum cannot cancel, so $P$ has degree $3$. Write $P(x)=ax^3+bx^2+cx+d$. Expanding gives
\[
P(x+1)+P(x-1)
=2ax^3+2bx^2+(6a+2c)x+2b+2d.
\]
Coefficient comparison yields $a=\dfrac{1}{2},b=0,c=-\dfrac{3}{2},d=0$. Hence $P(x)=\dfrac{(x^3-3x)}{2}$, and
\[
\boxed{P(12)=\dfrac{1728-36}{2}=846}.
\]
