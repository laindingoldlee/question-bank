Let $\phi$ be Euler's totient function: $\phi(d)$ is the number of integers $j$ with $1\le j\le d$ and $\gcd(j,d)=1$. Prove that for every positive integer $n$,
\[\sum_{d\mid n}\phi(d)=n,\]
where the sum runs over the positive divisors $d$ of $n$.
