The right angle is at $C$, and $AB=10$. If the legs have lengths $u,v$, then $u^2+v^2=100$ and area $\dfrac{uv}{2}\le\dfrac{u^2+v^2}{4}=25$. Equality occurs when $u=v=5\sqrt2$, for example at $C=(7,7)$. Hence the maximum is $\boxed{25}$.
