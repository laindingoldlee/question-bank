The ratio $\dfrac{c_{k+1}}{c_k}=\dfrac{2025-k}{k+1}$ locates the two equal maxima at $k=1012,1013$. Compute the central coefficient exactly using $b_0=1$ and $b_{j+1}=\dfrac{b_j(2025-j)}{j+1}$ for $j=0,\ldots,1011$. Integer comparison gives $10^{607}\le b_{1012}<10^{608}$, so it has $\boxed{608}$ digits.
