If $m$ math teachers attend, $m+1$ science teachers attend, for $m=0,1,2,3$:
\[\dbinom30\dbinom51+\dbinom31\dbinom52+\dbinom32\dbinom53+\dbinom33\dbinom54=5+30+30+5=\boxed{70}.\]
(By Vandermonde's identity the sum is $\sum_m\dbinom3m\dbinom5{4-m}=\dbinom84=70$.) The poser's $65$ omits the group with no math teachers and one science teacher, which also satisfies the rule.
