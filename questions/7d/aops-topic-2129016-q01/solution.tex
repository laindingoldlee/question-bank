If the price changes by $k$ dollars ($k>0$ is an increase, $k<0$ a decrease), the price is $20+k$ dollars and $80-2k$ meals are sold, so the weekly revenue is
\[R(k)=(20+k)(80-2k)=1600+40k-2k^2=1800-2(k-10)^2 .\]
\textbf{(a)} Since $(k-10)^2\ge0$, the revenue is at most $\boxed{\$1800}$, attained at $k=10$.

\textbf{(b)} The price is then $20+10=\boxed{\$30}$ per meal, with $60$ meals sold.
