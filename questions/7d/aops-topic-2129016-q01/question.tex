Jae prices the family meals at his restaurant at \$20 each and currently sells $80$ meals a week. For every dollar he raises the price, he sells $2$ fewer meals a week, and for every dollar he lowers the price, he sells $2$ more.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What is the maximum possible weekly revenue?
\item[(b)] What price per meal gives this revenue?
\end{enumerate}
