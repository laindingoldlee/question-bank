The running totals are the sums of the first $1$, $2$, $3$, $4$, $5$ chips. A total of $10$ occurs exactly when the first $k$ chips form a set with sum $10$ for some $k$. The subsets of $\{2,3,4,5,6\}$ with sum $10$ are $\{4,6\}$ and $\{2,3,5\}$ (a single chip is at most $6$, and four chips sum to at least $14$).

All $5!$ orders are equally likely.
\begin{itemize}
\item The first two chips are $\{4,6\}$: probability $\dfrac{2\cdot1}{5\cdot4}=\dfrac{1}{10}$.
\item The first three chips are $\{2,3,5\}$: probability $\dfrac{3!\cdot2!}{5!}=\dfrac{12}{120}=\dfrac{1}{10}$.
\end{itemize}
These events are disjoint (in the first, the first two chips sum to $10$; in the second they sum to less than $10$). The probability is $\dfrac{1}{10}+\dfrac{1}{10}=\dfrac{1}{5}=\boxed{0.2}$.
