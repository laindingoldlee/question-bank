A bag contains five chips numbered $2$ through $6$. Danya draws chips from the bag one at a time, without replacement, and sets them aside. After each draw, she totals the numbers on all the chips she has drawn so far. What is the probability that at some point her total equals $10$? Express your answer as a decimal to the nearest tenth.
