Let the center be $(u,v)$. Equality of squared distances to $(0,0)$ and $(2,6)$ gives $4u+12v=40$, or $u+3v=10$. Equality with $(9,1)$ gives $18u+2v=82$, or $9u+v=41$. Solving yields
\[
(u,v)=\left(\dfrac{113}{26},\dfrac{49}{26}\right).
\]
Both fractions are reduced, so $\boxed{a+b+c=113+49+26=188}$.

\end{asy}
