Split $f$ into its even and odd parts: $f(x)=E(x)+O(x)$ with $E(x)=ax^4+bx^2+(a+b)^3$ and $O(x)=x^3+x$. Then $E(-x)=E(x)$ and $O(-x)=-O(x)$, so
\[f(6)-f(-6)=2O(6)=2\left(216+6\right)=444 .\]
Hence
\[f(-6)=f(6)-444=1-444=\boxed{-443}.\]
