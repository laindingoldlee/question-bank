Measure grass in cow-days (the amount one cow eats in one day). Let $G$ be the initial amount of grass and $g$ the amount that grows per day. The field is eaten bare exactly when consumption equals the initial grass plus growth:
\[G+4g=36\cdot4=144,\qquad G+9g=21\cdot9=189.\]
Subtracting, $5g=45$, so $g=9$ and $G=108$. With $15$ cows lasting $d$ days, $108+9d=15d$, so $6d=108$ and $d=\boxed{18}$ days.
