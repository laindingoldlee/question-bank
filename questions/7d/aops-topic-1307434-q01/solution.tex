Sophie Germain's identity gives $n^4+\dfrac{1}{4}=\left(n^2+\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^2-n^2=\left(n^2-n+\dfrac{1}{2}\right)\left(n^2+n+\dfrac{1}{2}\right)$. Let $f(n)=n^2-n+\dfrac{1}{2}$; then $n^2+n+\dfrac{1}{2}=f(n+1)$, so $n^4+\dfrac{1}{4}=f(n)f(n+1)$. Hence
\[
A=\dfrac{f(1)f(2)\cdot f(3)f(4)\cdots f(11)f(12)}{f(2)f(3)\cdot f(4)f(5)\cdots f(12)f(13)}=\dfrac{f(1)}{f(13)}=\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{2}}{156+\dfrac{1}{2}}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{313}}.
\]
