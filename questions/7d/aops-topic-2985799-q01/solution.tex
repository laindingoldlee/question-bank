$ab=20(a+b)$ is equivalent to $ab-20a-20b+400=400$, i.e. $(a-20)(b-20)=400$ (Simon's favourite factoring trick).

If both factors were negative, then $a-20\ge-19$ and $b-20\ge-19$ would give a product at most $19^2=361<400$. So both factors are positive divisors of $400=2^4\cdot5^2$, which has $5\cdot3=15$ divisors. Each divisor $d$ gives the pair $(20+d,\,20+\dfrac{400}d)$. There are $\boxed{15}$ ordered pairs.
