An integer $m$ ends up erased exactly when it can be reached from $1$ by steps of $+7$ and $+11$, that is, when $m=1+7a+11b$ for some nonnegative integers $a,b$ (all intermediate numbers lie between $1$ and $m$, so they are on the board).

So $m$ remains exactly when $m-1$ is \emph{not} of the form $7a+11b$. By the Chicken McNugget theorem, since $\gcd(7,11)=1$, the largest integer not of this form is $7\cdot11-7-11=59$, and every integer greater than $59$ is of this form. Hence $1+59=60$ remains and every integer from $61$ to $2010$ is erased. The largest remaining integer is $\boxed{60}$.

(Check that $59$ is not representable: $59-11b$ for $b=0,1,2,3,4,5$ gives $59,48,37,26,15,4$, none of which is a multiple of $7$.)
