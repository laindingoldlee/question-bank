Max has eight identical cups. Each cup contains a different combination of nickels, dimes and quarters (a combination may leave out a type of coin), and the coins in each cup total $45$ cents. Max randomly selects a cup. What is the probability that the cup he selects contains at least three dimes? Express your answer as a common fraction.
