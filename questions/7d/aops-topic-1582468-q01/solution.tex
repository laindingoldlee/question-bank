Now Nick is $x-n$ years old. In $3n$ years:
\begin{itemize}
\item Andy will be $x+3n$;
\item Nick will be $x-n+3n=x+2n$;
\item Sam will be twice Nick's age then, $2(x+2n)=2x+4n$.
\end{itemize}
The total of Andy's and Sam's ages then is
\[
(x+3n)+(2x+4n)=\boxed{3x+7n}.
\]
(The answer $3x+4n$ comes from a different reading: Sam's age \emph{now} is $2(x-n)$, so in $3n$ years Sam is $2x+n$ and the total is $3x+4n$. The wording "Sam's age is twice Nick's age $3n$ years later" refers to both ages $3n$ years later, which gives the book's answer.)
