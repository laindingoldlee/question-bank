In rectangle $ABCD$, let $AB=20$ and $BC=18$. Point $P$ is on $\overline{AB}$ such that $\dfrac{AP}{BP}=\dfrac{2}{3}$, and point $Q$ is on $\overline{BC}$ such that $\dfrac{BQ}{CQ}=\dfrac{4}{5}$. Segments $\overline{DP}$ and $\overline{AQ}$ intersect at $R$, and line $CR$ intersects $\overline{AB}$ at $S$. Compute $AS$.
