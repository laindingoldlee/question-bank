Fix Kezia's position. April is equally likely to be in any of the other $k-1$ places, and exactly $2$ of them are next to Kezia (for $k\ge3$). So the probability is $\dfrac{2}{k-1}=\dfrac{1}{7}$, which gives $k-1=14$ and $k=\boxed{15}$.
