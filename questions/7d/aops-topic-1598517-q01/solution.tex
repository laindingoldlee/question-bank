Let the roots be $r_1,\dots,r_{2018}$. Since the leading coefficient is $2019$,
\[
P(x)=2019\prod_{i=1}^{2018}(x-r_i).
\]
Put $x=-1$:
\[
P(-1)=2019\prod_{i=1}^{2018}(-1-r_i)=2019\,(-1)^{2018}\prod_{i=1}^{2018}(1+r_i)=2019\prod_{i=1}^{2018}(r_i+1).
\]
On the other hand,
\[
P(-1)=1-2+3-4+\dots-2018+2019=\underbrace{(1-2)+(3-4)+\dots+(2017-2018)}_{1009\text{ pairs}}+2019=-1009+2019=1010 .
\]
So the product of the elements of $S_2$ is
\[
\prod_{i=1}^{2018}(r_i+1)=\boxed{\dfrac{1010}{2019}}.
\]
