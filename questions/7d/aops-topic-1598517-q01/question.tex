Let
\[
P(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{2018}(k+1)x^k=2019x^{2018}+2018x^{2017}+\dots+3x^2+2x+1 .
\]
Let $S$ be the multiset of all (complex) roots of $P$, counted with multiplicity. A new multiset $S_2$ is formed by increasing each element of $S$ by $1$. Find the product of all the elements of $S_2$.
