\textbf{(a)} Measure angles from $ON$, counterclockwise for $OM$. After $t$ seconds, $OM$ is at $3t$ degrees and $OP$ at $-6t$ degrees. They coincide when $3t-(-6t)=9t$ is a multiple of $360$. The smallest positive solution is $t=\dfrac{360}{9}=\boxed{40}$ seconds.

\textbf{(b)} $30$ minutes is $1800$ seconds. In that time $OP$ turns $6\cdot1800=10800^\circ$, i.e.\ $30$ revolutions, and $OM$ turns $3\cdot1800=5400^\circ$, i.e.\ $15$ revolutions. So $OP$ completes $30-15=\boxed{15}$ more revolutions.
