For Patricia to win in the first exchange, she must catch and Patrick must miss, with probability $(\dfrac{1}{2})(\dfrac{1}{3})=\dfrac{1}{6}$. Both catch and restart the situation with probability $(\dfrac{1}{2})(\dfrac{2}{3})=\dfrac{1}{3}$. Thus her win probability is $\dfrac{(\dfrac{1}{6})}{(1-\dfrac{1}{3})}=\dfrac{1}{4}$, giving $p+q=\boxed5$.
