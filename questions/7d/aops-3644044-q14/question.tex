Patrick and Patricia are playing a game of catch. The first person to fail to catch the ball loses and Patrick starts throwing first. If the probability that Patrick catches the ball is $\dfrac{2}{3}$ and the probability that Patricia catches it is $\dfrac{1}{2}$ , the probability that Patricia wins can be expressed as $\dfrac{p}{q}$ , where $p$ and $q$ are relatively prime. Find $p + q$.
