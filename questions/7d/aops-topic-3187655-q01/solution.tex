Each condition says $N+3$ is divisible by the relevant size. Thus $N+3$ is a multiple of $\operatorname{lcm}(7,6,5)=210$. The only possibility in the allowed interval is $N+3=420$, giving $\boxed{N=417}$. Since $417=3\cdot139$ with $139$ prime, the possible group sizes are $\boxed{1,3,139,417}$, yielding $417,139,3,1$ groups respectively.
