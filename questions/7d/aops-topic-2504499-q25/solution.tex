Let $a$ be Player $1$'s winning probability when he holds the die, and $b$ when someone else does. Then
\[a=\dfrac16+\dfrac16a+\dfrac46b,\qquad b=\dfrac16a+\dfrac46b.\]
The second gives $b=\dfrac a2$; substituting, $a=\dfrac16+\dfrac a6+\dfrac a3$, so $a=\boxed{\dfrac13}$.
