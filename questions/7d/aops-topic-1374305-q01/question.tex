A bucket of coal weighing $800$ lb hangs at the bottom of a $500$ ft mine shaft from a cable that weighs $2$ lb per foot. The cable is wound up at the top of the shaft, so the hanging part of the cable always reaches exactly from the top of the shaft to the bucket. Find the work required
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] to lift the bucket from the bottom to the midpoint of the shaft;
\item[(b)] to lift the bucket from the midpoint to the top of the shaft;
\item[(c)] to lift the bucket all the way up the shaft.
\end{enumerate}
