Take $B=(0,0)$ and $C=(8,0)$, with the pentagon oriented counterclockwise. The $60^\circ$ angle and equal adjacent sides give $A=(4,4\sqrt3)$. At $C,D$ the exterior turns are $30^\circ$, so
\[
D=C+4\sqrt3(\cos30^\circ,\sin30^\circ)=(14,2\sqrt3),
\]
\[
E=D+6(\cos60^\circ,\sin60^\circ)=(17,5\sqrt3).
\]
The shoelace determinant for $A,B,C,D,E$ has forward sum $(16+70+68)\sqrt3=154\sqrt3$ and backward sum $(34+20)\sqrt3=54\sqrt3$. Thus
\[
\boxed{[ABCDE]=\dfrac{1}{2}(154-54)\sqrt3=50\sqrt3}.
\]

\end{asy}
