For a complex number $z=x+yi$ with real $x,y$, its \emph{conjugate} is $\bar z=x-yi$. Let $z=3+4i$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find $\bar z$ and $z\bar z$.
\item[(b)] Show that $z\bar z=x^2+y^2$ for every complex number $z=x+yi$.
\item[(c)] Use the conjugate to write $\dfrac{1}{z}$ in the form $p+qi$ with real $p,q$.
\end{enumerate}
