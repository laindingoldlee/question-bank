Let the side of the square be $s$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(8cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=sqrt(1334);
pair A=(0,0),B=(23,0),C=(23+s,0),D=(81+s,0),F=(23,s),Ep=(23+s,s);
pair G=extension(A,F,D,Ep);
fill(B--C--Ep--F--cycle,paleyellow);
draw(A--G--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(B--F--Ep--C,linewidth(0.9));
real q=3.2;
draw((B+(0,q))--(B+(q,q))--(B+(q,0)));draw((C+(0,q))--(C+(-q,q))--(C+(-q,0)));
pair u=unit(A-G),v=unit(D-G);draw(G+q*u--G+q*(u+v)--G+q*v);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,S);label("$C$",C,S);label("$D$",D,SE);label("$G$",G,N);label("$F$",F,NW);label("$E$",Ep,NE);
label("$23$",(A+B)/2,S);label("$58$",(C+D)/2,S);label("$s$",(B+C)/2,S);label("$s$",(B+F)/2,E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The segments $BF$ and $CE$ are perpendicular to $AD$. In right triangle $ABF$ the angle at $A$ is $\angle GAD$, and in right triangle $ECD$ the angle at $D$ is $\angle GDA$. Since $\angle G=90^\circ$, these two angles add up to $90^\circ$, so $\angle AFB=90^\circ-\angle A=\angle D$. Hence triangles $ABF$ and $ECD$ are similar (right angles at $B$ and $C$, and $\angle AFB=\angle EDC$), with
\[
\dfrac{AB}{BF}=\dfrac{EC}{CD}\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad \dfrac{23}{s}=\dfrac{s}{58}.
\]
Therefore the area of the square is
\[
s^2=23\cdot58=\boxed{1334}.
\]
