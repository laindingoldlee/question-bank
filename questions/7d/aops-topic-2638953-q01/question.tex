In a word ladder, one letter is changed at each step and every step gives an English word; for example,
\[\text{SHIP}\to\text{SLIP}\to\text{SLOP}\to\text{SLOT}\to\text{SOOT}\to\text{LOOT}\to\text{LOOK}\to\text{LOCK}\to\text{DOCK}.\]
Assume that every word in a ladder contains at least one vowel (A, E, I, O or U). Prove that every ladder from SHIP to DOCK contains a word with exactly two vowels.
