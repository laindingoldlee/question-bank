Draw a conclusion using all premises about kittens: (1) No kitten that loves fish is unteachable. (2) No kitten without a tail will play with a gorilla. (3) Kittens with whiskers always love fish. (4) No teachable kitten has green eyes. (5) No kittens have tails unless they have whiskers.
