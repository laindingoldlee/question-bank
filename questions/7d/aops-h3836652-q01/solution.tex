Let $AB=w$, $BC=h$, and let the perpendicular distances from $P$ to $AB$ and $CD$ be $u$ and $v$. Since these are opposite sides, $u+v=h$. Hence
\[
[PAB]+[PCD]=\dfrac{wu}{2}+\dfrac{wv}{2}=\dfrac{wh}{2}.
\]
The same argument with bases $BC$ and $DA$ gives $[PBC]+[PDA]=\dfrac{wh}{2}$. Therefore
\[
8+20=12+[PDA],\qquad \boxed{[PDA]=16}.
\]
The rectangle's area is twice either opposite sum: $\boxed{2(8+20)=56}$.
