For every eligible fraction,
\[
\left|\dfrac{a}{b}-\dfrac{3}{4}\right|=\dfrac{|4a-3b|}{4b}\ge\dfrac{1}{4b}\ge\dfrac{1}{60},
\]
since the numerator is a positive integer and $b\le15$. Equality occurs for $a=11,b=15$, because $4\cdot11-3\cdot15=-1$ and the fraction is reduced. Therefore the closest fraction is $\dfrac{11}{15}$, and $\boxed{a+b=26}$.
