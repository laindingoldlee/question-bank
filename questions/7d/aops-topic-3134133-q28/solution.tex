Treat $AO$ or $OA$ as a block. With the two identical $F$'s, there are $2\cdot\dfrac{8!}{2!}=40320$ arrangements. Those also having adjacent $F$'s treat $FF$ as another block, giving $2\cdot7!=10080$. The difference is $\boxed{30240}$.
