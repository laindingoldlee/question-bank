$x\in A\setminus(B\cup C)$ iff $x\in A$ and $x\notin B\cup C$, iff $x\in A$, $x\notin B$ and $x\notin C$. That holds iff $x\in A\setminus B$ and $x\in A\setminus C$, i.e. $x\in(A\setminus B)\cap(A\setminus C)$. Both sets have the same elements, so they are equal. $\blacksquare$
