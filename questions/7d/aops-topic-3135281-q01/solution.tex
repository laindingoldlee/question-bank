Since $ 1050=2\cdot3\cdot5^2\cdot7 $, $\varphi(1050)=240 $. Euler's theorem applies because 11 is coprime to 1050, giving $ 11^{240}\equiv1 $. Thus the desired residue is the inverse of 11. Since $ 11\cdot191=2101=2\cdot1050+1 $, the answer is $\boxed{191}$.
