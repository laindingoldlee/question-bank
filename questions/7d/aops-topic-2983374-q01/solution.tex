Let $d=a-b$. Then $\dfrac{a^3-b^3}{(a-b)^3}=\dfrac{a^2+ab+b^2}{d^2}=\dfrac{d^2+3ab}{d^2}$, so $1+\dfrac{3ab}{d^2}=\dfrac{73}3$ and $9ab=70d^2$.

Any prime dividing $d$ and $a$ divides $b=a-d$, so $\gcd(d,a)=\gcd(d,b)=1$ and $\gcd(d^2,ab)=1$. From $d^2\mid9ab$ we get $d^2\mid9$, so $d\in\{1,3\}$. $d=1$ gives $9ab=70$, impossible. $d=3$ gives $ab=70$ with $a-b=3$: $a=10$, $b=7$ (coprime). So $a-b=\boxed{3}$.
