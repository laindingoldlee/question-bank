If the digits are $a,b$, then $ab(a+b)=330$. Their sum is a divisor of $330$ between $2$ and $18$, so it is $2,3,5,6,10,11$ or $15$. The discriminant $(a+b)^2-4ab=s^2-\dfrac{1320}{s}$ is negative for $s\le10$, equals $1$ for $s=11$, and equals $137$ for $s=15$, which is not a square. Thus $a+b=11$, $ab=30$, so the digits are $5,6$. The two numbers are $\boxed{56,65}$.
