Write the halved number as $m=10a+b$, where $1\le a\le4$ and $0\le b\le9$ (since the original is two-digit). The condition is $m^2=4(10b+a)$, so $m$ is even. Also $m^2\le4\cdot94<400$, hence $m<20$ and $a=1$. Thus $(10+b)^2=40b+4$, or $b^2-20b+96=(b-8)(b-12)=0$. Only $b=8$ is a digit. Hence $m=18$ and the original is $\boxed{36}$; indeed $\sqrt{81}=9=\dfrac{36}{4}$.
