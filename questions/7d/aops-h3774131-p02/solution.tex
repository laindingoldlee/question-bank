Since $2381^2-2380^2=4761=69^2$, the triangle is right. Its inradius is half the sum of the legs minus the hypotenuse: $r=\dfrac{69+2380-2381}{2}=\boxed{34}$. This follows from equal tangent segments from the vertices.
