Suppose $\dfrac {p}{q}=\dfrac {r}{s}$, where $q,r,s$ are non-zero real numbers. A student claims that $\dfrac{p^2+q^2}{r^2+s^2}=\dfrac{1}{4}$. Show that the value of $\dfrac{p^2+q^2}{r^2+s^2}$ is not determined by the given condition by expressing it in terms of $q$ and $s$, and decide when the student's answer is correct.
