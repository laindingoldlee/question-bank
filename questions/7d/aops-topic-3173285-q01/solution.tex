By the binomial theorem, terms containing $10^3$ vanish modulo $1000$, so $11^{2002}\equiv1+2002\cdot10+\binom{2002}{2}100\pmod{1000}$. Since $\binom{2002}{2}=2003001$, the residue is $1+20+100=\boxed{121}$.
