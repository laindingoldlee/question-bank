$\boxed{\text{Yes}}$. An equilateral triangle of side $0.9$ cm has area $\dfrac{\sqrt3}4\cdot0.81\approx0.35$ cm$^2$. The isosceles triangle with sides $101$, $101$ and $202-10^{-8}$ (cm) has height $\sqrt{101^2-\left(101-\dfrac{10^{-8}}2\right)^2}\approx\sqrt{1.01\cdot10^{-6}}\approx0.001$, so its area is about $101\cdot0.001\approx0.1$ cm$^2$, smaller (sketch not to scale):
