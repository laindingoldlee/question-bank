We have $P(1)=P(2)=2y$, $P(3)=P(4)=4y$ and $P(5)=P(6)=8y$. The six probabilities add up to $1$:
\[2(2y+4y+8y)=28y=1,\qquad y=\dfrac1{28}.\]
So $P(6)=8y=\boxed{\dfrac27}$.
