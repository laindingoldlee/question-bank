List the integer right triangles with hypotenuse $c<30$, legs $a<b$. The primitive ones (sides with no common factor) have $c\in\{5,13,17,25,29\}$:
\[
(3,4,5),\ (5,12,13),\ (8,15,17),\ (7,24,25),\ (20,21,29).
\]
Every other one is a multiple $k(a,b,c)$ of a primitive one with $kc<30$:
\[
(6,8,10),\ (9,12,15),\ (12,16,20),\ (15,20,25),\ (10,24,26).
\]
($k=6$ for $(3,4,5)$ gives $c=30$, which is not allowed, and $2\cdot13=26$ is the only multiple of $13$ below $30$.) So there are $10$ triangles. The hypotenuse is odd for $5,13,17,25,29$ and also for $15$ and $25$ (the triangles $(9,12,15)$, $(15,20,25)$), that is, for $7$ of them. The probability is
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{7}{10}} .
\]
Counting each triangle twice, as $(a,b)$ and $(b,a)$, gives $\dfrac{14}{20}$, the same answer.
