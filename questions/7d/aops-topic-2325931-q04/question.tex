Kevin's candy bowl holds $80$ crunchy chocolates, $20$ caramel chocolates and $40$ peanut chocolates. He wants to add equal numbers of caramel and peanut chocolates so that $20\%$ of the chocolates are caramel. How many of each would he have to add? Is this possible?
