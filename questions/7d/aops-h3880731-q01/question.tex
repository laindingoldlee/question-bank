Let $ABCD$ be a nonrectangular isosceles trapezoid, with $AB\parallel CD$, and let $P$ lie strictly inside side $AD$. Put $Q=CP\cap BD$. Lines through $P$ parallel to $BC$ and through $Q$ parallel to $AC$ meet line $AB$ at $M,N$, respectively. Prove that the circumcircles of $APM$ and $BQN$ are tangent.
