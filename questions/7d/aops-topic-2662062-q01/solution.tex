Let $f(x)=px^2+qx+r$ with $p\ne0$. Comparing the coefficients of $x^2$ in $f(x)-kf(x-1)=x^2+kx$ gives $p-kp=1$, i.e. $p(1-k)=1$. With integers and $k\ge1$ this forces $1-k=-1$, so $k=2$ and $p=-1$.

Now $f(x)=-x^2+qx+r$ and
\[f(x)-2f(x-1)=-x^2+qx+r+2(x-1)^2-2q(x-1)-2r=x^2+(-q-4)x+(2+2q-r).\]
This must equal $x^2+2x$, so $-q-4=2$ and $2+2q-r=0$, giving $q=-6$ and $r=-10$. Thus $f(x)=-x^2-6x-10$, $f(1)=-17$, and $|f(1)|=\boxed{17}$.
