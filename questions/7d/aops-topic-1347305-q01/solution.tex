Since $z=y$ makes the expression zero, $z-y$ is a factor, and dividing (or using the geometric-series identity) gives
\[
z^5-y^5=\boxed{(z-y)\left(z^4+z^3y+z^2y^2+zy^3+y^4\right)}.
\]
Check by expanding: the middle terms cancel in pairs, leaving $z^5-y^5$.
