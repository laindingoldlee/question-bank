All the angles of the two polygons together sum to $360^\circ+180^\circ=540^\circ$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(3,0),C=(3.6,1.8),D=(0.8,2.4);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);
draw(arc(A,0.5,0,degrees(D-A)));label("$\theta$",A,3*dir(35));
pair P=(5,0),Q=(8,0),R=(6.2,2.6);draw(P--Q--R--cycle);
draw(arc(P,0.5,0,degrees(R-P)));label("$\theta$",P,3*dir(30));
label("quadrilateral: $360^\circ$",(1.8,-0.2),S);label("triangle: $180^\circ$",(6.5,-0.2),S);
\end{asy}

So $2\theta+410^\circ=540^\circ$, and $\theta=\boxed{65^\circ}$.
