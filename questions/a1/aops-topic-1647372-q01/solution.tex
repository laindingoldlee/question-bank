Since $5^{29}=\dfrac{10^{29}}{2^{29}}$ and $2^{29}=536870912$, the number $5^{29}$ has $29-8=21$ digits ($2^{29}$ lies between $10^8$ and $10^9$). Multiplying out (for example as $5^{29}=5^{10}\cdot5^{10}\cdot5^{9}$ with $5^{10}=9765625$ and $5^9=1953125$) gives
\[
5^{29}=186264514923095703125 .
\]
Reading from the left, the digits are $1,8,6,2,6,4,5,1,4,9,2,3,\dots$, so the $12$th digit is $\boxed{3}$. (The first digits can also be seen from $\dfrac{1}{2^{29}}=1.86264514923\ldots\times10^{-9}$.)
