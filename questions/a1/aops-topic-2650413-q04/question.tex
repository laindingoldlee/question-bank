The sequence $x_1,x_2,\dots,x_{100}$ of positive integers satisfies $x_k\mid x_{k+1}$ for $1\le k\le99$, and $x_{100}=2^{33}3^{33}5^{33}$. Exactly half of the terms are divisible by $2$, and exactly one tenth of the terms are divisible by $10$. What is the minimum possible number of indices $k$ ($1\le k\le99$) with $x_k=x_{k+1}$?
