The boundary consists of six 6-by 6 planar patches, twelve quarter-cylinder patches of radius 3 and length 6, and eight sphere octants of radius 3. Their areas are $6\cdot36=216$, $12\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}(2\pi\cdot3\cdot6)=108\pi$, and $8\cdot\dfrac{1}{8}(4\pi\cdot3^2)=36\pi$. Thus the total is $216+144\pi$ and $\boxed{m+n=360}$.
