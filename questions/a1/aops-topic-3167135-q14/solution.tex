Put $t=(x-2)^2\ge0$. The inequality becomes $t^2+t-4\le16$, or $(t+5)(t-4)\le0$. Combining with $t\ge0$ gives $0\le t\le4$, hence $(x-2)^2\le4$ and $0\le x\le4$. The endpoint sum is $\boxed4$.
