If either number is an integer, both are integers and the rounded sum stays $5$. Otherwise write their fractional parts as $u,v\in(0,1)$. Since their sum is an integer, $u+v=1$ and the integer parts sum to $4$. To round to a total of $6$, both must round up, so $u,v\ge\dfrac{1}{2}$. Consequently $u=v=\dfrac{1}{2}$. The pairs are $\boxed{(0.5,4.5),(1.5,3.5),(2.5,2.5),(3.5,1.5),(4.5,0.5)}$.
