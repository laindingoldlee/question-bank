$ 2023=7\cdot17^2 $ has three prime factors counted with multiplicity. Therefore at most three of the five integers have absolute value greater than $ 1 $; distinctness forces the other two to be $ 1,-1 $. The other three absolute values must be $ 7,17,17 $. The two occurrences of $ 17 $ must have opposite signs, and positivity of the product forces the $ 7 $ to be positive. Thus the only set is $\{-17,-1,1,7,17\}$, whose sum is $\boxed7 $.
