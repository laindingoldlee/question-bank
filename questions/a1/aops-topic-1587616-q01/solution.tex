For a positive integer $a$ we have $a!=a\cdot(a-1)!$ (this also holds for $a=1$, since $0!=1$). Hence
\[
\dfrac{a!}{(a-1)!}=\dfrac{a\cdot(a-1)!}{(a-1)!}=\boxed{a}.
\]
