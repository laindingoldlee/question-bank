A student is asked to ``simplify'' $P(x,y)=x^3+y^3+x^2+y^2$ and looks for a factorisation. Prove that $P$ cannot be written as a product of two non-constant polynomials in $x$ and $y$ with complex coefficients. (So the expression is already in simplest form; the best one can do is regroup it, for example as $(x+y)\left(x^2-xy+y^2\right)+x^2+y^2$.)
