Let $t=100A+10B+C$, so the number is $352000052+1000t$. Its last two digits ensure divisibility by $4$. Modulo $11$, $352000052\equiv8$ and $1000\equiv-1$, hence divisibility by $11$ means $t\equiv8\pmod{11}$. In $0\le t\le999$, the values are $8,19,\ldots,998$, numbering $\dfrac{998-8}{11}+1=\boxed{91}$. Each has a unique three-digit representation with leading zeros allowed.
