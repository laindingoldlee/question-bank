\textbf{False.} For every $x>0$,
\[x+\dfrac1x-2=\dfrac{(x-1)^2}{x}\ge0,\]
so $x+\dfrac1x\ge2$, with equality only at $x=1$. The negation of the statement is proved, so no such $x$ exists. $\blacksquare$
