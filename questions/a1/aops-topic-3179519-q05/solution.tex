In the remaining times $12:44$ through $12:59$, the four digits cannot be four distinct consecutive digits. In hour $13$, the other two digits must be $0,2$ or $2,4$ to complete a consecutive set. The earliest legal minute is $02$, giving $\boxed{13:02}$.
