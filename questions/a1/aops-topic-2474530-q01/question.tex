Let $\omega$ be a circle and $P$ a point not on $\omega$. Two lines through $P$ meet $\omega$ at $A,B$ and at $C,D$ respectively.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $PA\cdot PB=PC\cdot PD$ when $P$ is inside $\omega$.
\item[(b)] Prove that $PA\cdot PB=PC\cdot PD$ when $P$ is outside $\omega$.
\item[(c)] If $P$ is outside $\omega$ and $PT$ is tangent to $\omega$ at $T$, prove that $PT^2=PA\cdot PB$.
\end{enumerate}
(This common value is the \emph{power} of $P$ with respect to $\omega$.)
