Since $\gcd(m,n)=1$, the number of divisors is multiplicative: $mn$ has $12\cdot10=120$ positive divisors.

For any positive integer $N$ with $t$ divisors, the divisors pair up as $d\leftrightarrow\dfrac {N}{d}$, each pair having product $N$. Hence the product of all the divisors, squared, is $N^t$ (multiply the product of all $d$ by the product of all $\dfrac {N}{d}$, which is the same product), so the product is $N^{\dfrac {t}{2}}$. (This also covers a perfect square, where $\sqrt N$ is paired with itself.)

With $N=mn$ and $t=120$, the product of the divisors of $mn$ is $\boxed{(mn)^{60}}$.
