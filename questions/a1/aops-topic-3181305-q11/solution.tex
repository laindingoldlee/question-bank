The overlap of each pair has area $\dfrac{8\pi}{3}-2\sqrt3$, from two 120-degree sectors minus their triangles. The triple intersection is a Reuleaux triangle: its area is three 60-degree sectors minus twice the centre triangle area, namely $3\cdot\dfrac{2\pi}{3}-2\sqrt3=2\pi-2\sqrt3$. Summing the three pair overlaps counts each triple-covered point three times, so subtract twice the triple intersection. The desired area is $8\pi-6\sqrt3-2(2\pi-2\sqrt3)=4\pi-2\sqrt3$. Therefore $m^2+n^2+q^2=16+4+9=\boxed{29}$.
