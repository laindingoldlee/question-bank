Quadrilateral $ABCD$ has an inscribed circle, and its diagonals meet at $P$. Let $R_1,R_2,R_3,R_4$ be the circumradii of triangles $APB$, $BPC$, $CPD$, $DPA$. If $R_1=31$, $R_2=24$ and $R_3=12$, find $R_4$.
