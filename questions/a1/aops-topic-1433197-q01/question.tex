Let $p$, $q$, $r$, $s$ be integers with $s\neq0$ such that
\[
r^2+prs+qs^2=0 .
\]
Prove that $s$ divides $r$. (Equivalently: every rational root $\dfrac{r}{s}$ of $x^2+px+q=0$ with integer $p$, $q$ is an integer.)
