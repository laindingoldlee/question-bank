Since $7^3=343=38\cdot9+1$, we have $7^3\equiv1\pmod9$, so the powers of $7$ repeat with period $3$ modulo $9$: $7^1\equiv7$, $7^2\equiv4$, $7^3\equiv1$.

The exponents $1,3,5,\dots,2017$ are $2k+1$ for $k=0,1,\dots,1008$, which is $1009$ terms. Three consecutive terms $7^{2k+1}+7^{2k+3}+7^{2k+5}$ have exponents covering all residues modulo $3$, so each such group is $\equiv7+1+4=12\equiv3\pmod9$.

Since $1009=3\cdot336+1$, there are $336$ full groups plus the final term $7^{2017}$. As $2017=3\cdot672+1$, $7^{2017}\equiv7$. Hence the sum is
\[
\equiv336\cdot3+7=1015=112\cdot9+7\pmod9,
\]
and the remainder is $\boxed{7}$.
