(a) Find the least integer $n\ge2$ such that $\binom n2$ ends in 1434, or prove none exists. (b) For a fixed positive integer $C$, give a finite procedure describing all $n\ge C$ for which $\binom nC\equiv1434\pmod{10000}$, and decide when there are infinitely many.
