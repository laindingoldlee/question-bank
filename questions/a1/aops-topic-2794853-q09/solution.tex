Place $A=(0,0),B=(5,0)$. Since $ABC$ is right at $C$, $C=(\dfrac{16}{5},\dfrac{12}{5})$ up to reflection. Now $AE=10$, so $E=\dfrac{10}{4}C=(8,6)$, and $D=(8,0)$. Thus $BE=3\sqrt5$ and $CD=\sqrt{(\dfrac{24}{5})^2+(\dfrac{12}{5})^2}=\dfrac{12\sqrt5}{5}$. The ratio is $\boxed{\dfrac{5}{4}}$.
