For 2017 the only allowable month divisor is 1, giving January 17. For 2018 the months 1,2,3,6,9 yield days 18,9,6,3,2. For 2019 only month 1 works. For 2020 the months 1,2,4,5,10 yield days 20,10,5,4,2. Every listed day is valid for its month, and all other divisor pairs have month greater than 12. The total is $1+5+1+5=\boxed{12}$.
