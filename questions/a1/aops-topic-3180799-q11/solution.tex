Scale the hexagon side to 1 and place $NG$ horizontally. The distance between the parallel sides $NG$ and $UM$ is $\sqrt3$. The square rises 1 unit from $NG$, so the rectangle has width 1 and height $\sqrt3-1$. The hexagon area is six equilateral-triangle areas, or $\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}$. Hence the fraction is $\dfrac{\sqrt3-1}{\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}}=\boxed{\dfrac{2(3-\sqrt3)}{9}}$.
