Let $O$ be the centre. The radii $OE$ and $OF$ are perpendicular to the tangent lines at $E$ and $F$. The right triangles $OEG$ and $OFG$ have the common hypotenuse $OG$ and legs $OE=OF=6$, so they are congruent, and $GO$ bisects $\angle EGF$: $\angle OGF=30^\circ$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair Gp=(0,0); real r=6;
pair O=(r/Sin(30))*dir(30);
pair Ep=Gp+(r/Tan(30))*dir(0), F=Gp+(r/Tan(30))*dir(60);
draw(Gp--Gp+15*dir(0),linewidth(0.8)); draw(Gp--Gp+15*dir(60),linewidth(0.8));
draw(circle(O,r),blue);
draw(O--Ep,dashed); draw(O--F,dashed); draw(Gp--O,dashed);
real t=0.7; draw(Ep+(-t,0)--Ep+(-t,t)--Ep+(0,t));
draw(F+t*dir(240)--F+t*dir(240)+t*dir(-30)--F+t*dir(-30));
draw(arc(Gp,2.2,0,30),red); label("$30^\circ$",Gp+3.2*dir(15),red+fontsize(8pt));
label("$6$",(O+F)/2,NE,fontsize(9pt));
dot(O); label("$O$",O,E); label("$G$",Gp,W); label("$E$",Ep,S); label("$F$",F,NW);
label("$B$",Gp+15*dir(0),S); label("$D$",Gp+15*dir(60),NW);
\end{asy}

In the right triangle $OFG$, $\tan30^\circ=\dfrac{OF}{FG}$, so
\[FG=\dfrac{6}{\tan30^\circ}=6\sqrt3,\]
i.e. $FG=\boxed{6\sqrt3}$. (Equivalently, $OFG$ is a $30$-$60$-$90$ triangle with short leg $OF=6$.)
