There are three standard six-sided dice, one red, one white and one blue, placed on a table. In how many ways can the sum of the $15$ visible faces (all faces except the three bottom faces) equal $56$? Two ways are different only if the bottom face of some die is different; rotating a die without changing its bottom face does not count as a new way.
