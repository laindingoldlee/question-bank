Each die has faces summing to $1+2+\cdots+6=21$, so all $18$ faces sum to $63$. The visible faces sum to $56$ exactly when the three bottom faces sum to $63-56=7$. So we count ordered triples (red, white, blue bottoms) of numbers from $1$ to $6$ with sum $7$. With $x_i\ge1$ and $x_1+x_2+x_3=7$, stars and bars gives $\dbinom{6}{2}=15$ triples, and none has an entry above $5$, so all are allowed. The answer is $\boxed{15}$.
