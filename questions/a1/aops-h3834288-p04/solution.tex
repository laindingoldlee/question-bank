By symmetry, the probability of more heads than tails is half of one minus the tie probability. A tie has $k$ heads, $k$ tails, and $6-2k$ disappearances, for $k=0,1,2,3$. Its outcome count is $1+30+90+20=141$ out of $3^6=729$. Therefore the desired probability is $\dfrac{1-\dfrac{141}{729}}{2}=\dfrac{98}{243}$ and $\boxed{a+b=341}$.
