Each student of a college is enrolled in exactly one of $8$ departments, numbered $1$ to $8$. The numbers of students enrolled in three cyclically consecutive departments are:
\begin{itemize}
\item departments $1,2,3$: $93$; departments $2,3,4$: $96$; departments $3,4,5$: $96$; departments $4,5,6$: $99$;
\item departments $5,6,7$: $100$; departments $6,7,8$: $99$; departments $7,8,1$: $97$; departments $8,1,2$: $94$.
\end{itemize}
How many students are enrolled in each department?
