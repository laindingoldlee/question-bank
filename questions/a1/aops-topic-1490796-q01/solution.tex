Let $\rho$ be the radius of circle $P$. Both centres lie on the bisector of angle $A$, which is $60^\circ$. For a circle tangent to both sides of this angle, the radius to a point of tangency, the bisector and the side form a $30^\circ$-$60^\circ$-$90^\circ$ triangle, so the distance from $A$ to the centre is twice the radius:
\[
AO=2\cdot20=40,\qquad AP=2\rho .
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair ft(pair P,pair U,pair V){return U+dot(P-U,V-U)/dot(V-U,V-U)*(V-U);}
path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s=0.2){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
real r=20; real side=2*sqrt(3)*r;
pair B=(-side/2,0),C=(side/2,0),A=(0,3r),O=(0,r);
real rho=r/3; pair P=(0,3r-2*rho);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(circle(O,r),blue); draw(circle(P,rho),red);
pair T=ft(P,A,B), U=ft(O,A,B);
draw(A--(0,0),gray+dashed);
draw(P--T,red); draw(O--U,blue);
draw(ra(T,A-T,P-T,2.5)); draw(ra(U,A-U,O-U,2.5));
dot(A);dot(O,blue);dot(P,red);
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);
label("$O$",O,E,blue);label("$P$",P,E,red);
label("$20$",(O+U)/2,dir(240),blue); label("$\rho$",(P+T)/2,dir(240),red+fontsize(8pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Circle $P$ lies between $A$ and circle $O$ and touches it externally, so
\[
AO=AP+PO=2\rho+(\rho+20).
\]
Hence $40=3\rho+20$ and $\rho=\boxed{\dfrac{20}{3}}$.
