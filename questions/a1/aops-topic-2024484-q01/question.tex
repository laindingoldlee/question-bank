There are $48$ convex polygons: one triangle, one quadrilateral, one pentagon, and so on, up to one $50$-gon, so that each number of sides from $3$ to $50$ occurs once. No two sides of different polygons lie on a common line, and no point lies on sides of three or more of the polygons. What is the maximum possible number of points at which a side of one polygon meets a side of another polygon?
