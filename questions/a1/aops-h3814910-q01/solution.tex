Both sides are positive. Their squared difference is
\[
(a+d)(b+c)-(\sqrt{ab}+\sqrt{cd})^2
=ac+bd-2\sqrt{abcd}=(\sqrt{ac}-\sqrt{bd})^2\ge0.
\]
This proves the inequality, with equality exactly when $\boxed{ac=bd}$.
Alternatively, it is Cauchy-Schwarz applied to $(\sqrt a,\sqrt d)$ and $(\sqrt b,\sqrt c)$; equality means those vectors are proportional.
