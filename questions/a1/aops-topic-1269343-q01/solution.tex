The minute hand moves $6^\circ$ per minute and the hour hand $0.5^\circ$ per minute, so the minute hand gains $5.5^\circ$ per minute on the hour hand. At $3{:}00$ the minute hand is $90^\circ$ behind the hour hand. It must gain $90^\circ$ to catch up and then another $180^\circ$ to be directly opposite, a total of $270^\circ$:
\[t=\dfrac{270}{5.5}=\dfrac{540}{11}=\boxed{49\dfrac{1}{11}}\ \text{minutes}\]
(that is, at about $3{:}49$).
