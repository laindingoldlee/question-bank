Let the sides be $3a$ and $3b$ with positive integers $a\le b$. Then $9ab=216$, so $ab=24$, giving
\[
(a,b)\in\{(1,24),(2,12),(3,8),(4,6)\}.
\]
The rectangles are $3\times72$, $6\times36$, $9\times24$ and $12\times18$, with perimeters $150$, $84$, $66$ and $60$, all different. (Different factor pairs of the same product always have different sums, so the perimeters are automatically distinct.) The maximum is $\boxed{4}$.
