Multiplying by $xy$ gives $2y+2x=xy$, that is, $xy-2x-2y+4=4$, or
\[(x-2)(y-2)=4 .\]
So $y-2$ is an integer divisor of $4$, and $y-2\le4$, so $y\le6$. For $y=6$ we get $x-2=1$, $x=3$, and indeed $\dfrac{2}{3}+\dfrac{2}{6}=1$. The largest possible value is $y=\boxed{6}$.

(The integer condition on $x$ matters: if $x$ could be any real number, then $x=\dfrac{2y}{y-2}$ works for every $y>2$, and $y$ would have no maximum.)
