A rook moves on a board one square at a time, up, down, left or right.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that a rook cannot start in a corner of an ordinary $8\times8$ chessboard, visit every square exactly once, and finish in the opposite corner. (So the number of such paths is $0$.)
\item[(b)] For which boards with $m$ rows and $n$ columns ($m,n\ge2$) is such a path, from one corner through every square exactly once to the opposite corner, possible?
\end{enumerate}
