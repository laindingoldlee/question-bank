Fix the first point by rotational symmetry. The smaller central angle $\theta$ is uniform on $[0,\pi]$. The chord is distance $2\cos(\dfrac{\theta}{2})$ from the center, so intersection occurs when $\theta\ge 2\dfrac{\pi}{3}$. The favorable angular interval has length $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, giving probability $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{3}}$. Tangencies and coincident endpoints have probability zero.
