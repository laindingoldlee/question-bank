A repeated root requires discriminant $b^2-4c=0$. Since $b>0$, $\sqrt c=\dfrac{b}{2}$. Therefore $\dfrac{b+2}{\sqrt c+1}=\dfrac{b+2}{\dfrac{b+2}{2}}=\boxed2$.
