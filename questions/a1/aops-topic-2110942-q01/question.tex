A magic $3\times5$ board can toggle each of its $15$ cells between black and white. A \emph{pattern} is an assignment of black or white to each of the $15$ cells, so there are $2^{15}$ patterns. On Day $1$ the board shows the all-black pattern. At the beginning of every later day it switches to a new pattern. The board dies if two of the patterns it has shown (on any two days) differ in fewer than $3$ cells, and it also dies if it ever shows the all-white pattern. What is the maximum number of days the board can stay alive?
