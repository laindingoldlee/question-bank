Place the right-angle vertex at $A=(0,0)$ and the others at $B=(b,0)$ and $C=(0,a)$. Their midpoint is $M=(\dfrac{b}{2},\dfrac{a}{2})$, so $AM=\sqrt{\dfrac{a^2+b^2}{4}}=\dfrac{BC}{2}$ by Pythagoras.
\begin{asy}
import geometry; usepackage("amsmath"); size(240);
pair A=(0,0),B=(4,0),C=(0,3),M=(B+C)/2;draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--M,dashed);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,N);label("$M$",M,NE);
\end{asy}
