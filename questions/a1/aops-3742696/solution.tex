Let the opposite side lengths be $a,b,c$ and the positive area be $K$. Since $ah_A=bh_B=ch_C=2K$, the desired inequality becomes $\dfrac{a+b}{2K} > \dfrac{c}{2K}$. This is precisely the strict triangle inequality $a+b>c$.

\end{asy}
