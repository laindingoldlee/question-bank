The diagonal $AC$ is the hypotenuse of right triangle $ABC$, with the right angle at $B$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(8cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(0,7),C=(24,7),D=(24,0);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(1));draw(A--C,blue+linewidth(1));
draw(B+(0.9,0)--B+(0.9,-0.9)--B+(0,-0.9));
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,NW);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,SE);
label("$7$",(A+B)/2,W);label("$24$",(B+C)/2,N);label("$25$",(A+C)/2,SE,blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

By the Pythagorean theorem $BC=\sqrt{25^2-7^2}=\sqrt{625-49}=\sqrt{576}=24$. The area is $AB\cdot BC=7\cdot24=\boxed{168}$.
