Positive real numbers $a,b,c$ are such that twice any one of them is less than $a+b+c$, and
\[(a+b+c)(-a+b+c)(a-b+c)(a+b-c)=4a^2b^2.\]
Suppose also that $a+b=10$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the smallest possible value of $c$.
\item[(b)] Show that $c<10$, but that $c$ can be as close to $10$ as we like (so $c$ has no largest value).
\end{enumerate}
