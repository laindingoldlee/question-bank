$\dfrac{5^n-2^n}{10^n}=\left(\dfrac12\right)^n-\left(\dfrac15\right)^n$, so the sum is $\dfrac{\dfrac12}{1-\dfrac12}-\dfrac{\dfrac15}{1-\dfrac15}=1-\dfrac14=\boxed{\dfrac34}$.
