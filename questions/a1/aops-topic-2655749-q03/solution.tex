Since $x^2y^2=4$,
\[x+y+\dfrac{x^3}{y^2}+\dfrac{y^3}{x^2}=4+\dfrac{x^5+y^5}{x^2y^2}=4+\dfrac{x^5+y^5}{4}.\]
Let $s_n=x^n+y^n$. As $x,y$ are the roots of $t^2=4t+2$, we have $s_n=4s_{n-1}+2s_{n-2}$, with $s_0=2$, $s_1=4$:
\[s_2=20,\quad s_3=88,\quad s_4=392,\quad s_5=4\cdot392+2\cdot88=1744.\]
So the value is $4+\dfrac{1744}{4}=4+436=\boxed{440}$.
