The first eight tests total $8\cdot80=640$ points, and the last two total at most $200$. So the ten tests total at most $840$, and $N\le\dfrac{840}{10}=84$, with equality when Pat scores $100$ on both remaining tests. The maximum is $\boxed{84}$.
