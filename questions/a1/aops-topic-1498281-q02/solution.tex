The remainders of $2^1,2^2,2^3,2^4$ on division by $5$ are $2,4,3,1$, and since $2^4\equiv1\pmod5$ they repeat with period $4$. As $31=4\cdot7+3$,
\[
2^{31}=\left(2^4\right)^7\cdot2^3\equiv8\equiv\boxed{3}\pmod5 .
\]
