\textbf{(a)} $(2w)^2=2w\cdot2w=2\cdot2\cdot w\cdot w=\boxed{4w^2}$ (exponent rule $(ab)^2=a^2b^2$).

\textbf{(b)} $(2+w)^2=(2+w)(2+w)=4+2w+2w+w^2=\boxed{w^2+4w+4}$, by FOIL (First, Outer, Inner, Last).

\textbf{(c)} FOIL is a way to multiply two \emph{binomials}, that is, two sums of two terms, such as $(2+w)(2+w)$. The expression $2w$ is a single term (a product, not a sum), so there is nothing to FOIL: $(2w)^2$ is just $2w$ times $2w$. If one insists on writing $2w=2w+0$, FOIL gives $4w^2+0+0+0=4w^2$, the same answer. Correct algebra can never give two different values for the same expression; a different answer comes from treating $2w$ as if it were $2+w$.
