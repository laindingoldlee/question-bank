The power of a point $P$ with respect to a circle with centre $O$ and radius $r$ is $PO^2-r^2$. Here it is $6^2-4^2=\boxed{20}$.

For a point outside the circle this equals the square of the tangent length: if $PT$ is tangent at $T$, then $\angle OTP=90^\circ$ and $PT^2=PO^2-OT^2=20$. It also equals $PA\cdot PB$ for any line through $P$ meeting the circle at $A$ and $B$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s=0.2){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
pair O=(0,0), P=(6,0);
real t=sqrt(20); pair T=O+4*dir(aCos(4/6));
draw(circle(O,4),linewidth(0.9));
draw(O--P,dashed); draw(O--T,blue); draw(P--T,red);
draw(ra(T,O-T,P-T,0.45));
dot(O);dot(P);dot(T);
label("$O$",O,SW); label("$P$",P,E); label("$T$",T,N);
label("$4$",(O+T)/2,NW,blue); label("$6$",O+(2.2,0),S); label("$\sqrt{20}$",(P+T)/2,NE,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
