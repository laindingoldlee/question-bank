Locker $m$ is touched by person $n$ exactly when $2n\mid m$. Odd lockers are never touched. For $m=2j$ ($1\le j\le500$), $2n\mid 2j$ means $n\mid j$, so locker $2j$ is toggled once for every divisor $n$ of $j$ (all such $n$ are at most $500$). It ends open exactly when $j$ has an odd number of divisors, i.e.\ when $j$ is a perfect square. The squares from $1$ to $500$ are $1^2,\dots,22^2$ (since $22^2=484$ and $23^2=529$), so $\boxed{22}$ lockers are open (numbers $2,8,18,\dots,968$).
