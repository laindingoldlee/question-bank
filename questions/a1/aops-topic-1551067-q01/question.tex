When the width of a rectangular box is increased by $2$ cm and its length is increased by $1$ cm (the height staying the same), the resulting solid is a cube, whose volume exceeds the original volume by $96\text{ cm}^3$. Find the sum of the length, width and height of the original box, in centimetres.
