Let the cube have edge $s$. Then the original box has height $h=s$, length $l=s-1$ and width $w=s-2$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair k=(0.45,0.32);
pair p(real x,real y,real z){return (x,z)+y*k;}
void bx(pair o,real a,real b,real c,pen q){
 pair P(real x,real y,real z){return o+p(x,y,z);}
 draw(P(0,0,0)--P(a,0,0)--P(a,0,c)--P(0,0,c)--cycle,q);
 draw(P(a,0,0)--P(a,b,0)--P(a,b,c)--P(a,0,c),q);
 draw(P(0,0,c)--P(0,b,c)--P(a,b,c),q);
 draw(P(0,0,0)--P(0,b,0)--P(a,b,0),dashed+gray(0.5));draw(P(0,b,0)--P(0,b,c),dashed+gray(0.5));}
bx((0,0),5,4,6,linewidth(0.9));
label("$l$",(2.5,0),S);label("$w$",p(5,2,0),SE);label("$h$",(0,3),W);
pair o=(11,0);
bx(o,6,6,6,linewidth(0.9));
label("$l+1$",o+(3,0),S);label("$w+2$",o+p(6,3,0),SE);label("$h$",o+(0,3),W);
label("original box",(2.5+0.9,-2.6));label("cube",o+(3+1.35,-2.6));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The volume condition is
\[
s^3-s(s-1)(s-2)=96 .
\]
Since $(s-1)(s-2)=s^2-3s+2$, the left side is $s(3s-2)$, so
\[
3s^2-2s-96=0\iff(s-6)(3s+16)=0 ,
\]
and $s=6$ (as $s>0$). The original box is $5\times4\times6$ (check: $216-120=96$), and the sum of its dimensions is $5+4+6=\boxed{15}$.
