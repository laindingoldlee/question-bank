Treat the twins as one block (only one internal order, since they look identical). Then we arrange $4$ units: the twin block, Angelica, Eliza and Peggy, in $4!=24$ ways. In $2\cdot3!=12$ of them Angelica and Eliza are next to each other, so $24-12=12$ arrangements satisfy the first two conditions.

Peggy's condition is automatic. Suppose Peggy were at least $3$ places away from both Angelica and Eliza in the row of $5$ places. If Peggy is at an end, both sisters must be in the two places at the other end, which are next to each other, which is not allowed. If Peggy is in place $2$, $3$ or $4$, there are fewer than two places at distance at least $3$ from her. So every one of the $12$ arrangements works: the answer is $\boxed{12}$.
