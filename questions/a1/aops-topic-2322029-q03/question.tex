Five friends (Mary, Melisa, Angelica, Eliza and Peggy) stand in a row for a photo. Mary and Melisa are identical twins, so swapping them gives the same photo, and they must stand next to each other. Angelica will not stand next to Eliza. Peggy's sisters are Angelica and Eliza, and Peggy wants to stand next to at least one of them or with exactly one person between her and at least one of them. How many different photos are possible?
