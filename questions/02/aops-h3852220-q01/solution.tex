If $\theta=\angle ABC$, then $[ABC]=\dfrac{1}{2}(20)(26)\sin\theta=260\sin\theta$, where $0<\theta<\pi$. The possible real areas fill $(0,260]$.
Hence every positive integer from $1$ to $260$ is attainable: choose $\theta=\arcsin(\dfrac{K}{260})$ for area $K$. The largest and smallest integer areas are $260$ and $1$, whose difference is $\boxed{259}$.

\end{asy}
