Let $\omega$ be the circle inscribed in rhombus $ABCD$, which has side length $4$ and $\angle DAB=60^\circ$. There are two lines parallel to $BD$ and tangent to $\omega$. They meet sides $AB$, $BC$, $CD$ and $DA$ at $P$, $Q$, $R$ and $S$ respectively. What is the area of quadrilateral $PQRS$?
