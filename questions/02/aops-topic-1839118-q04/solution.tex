Pair the outer and inner factors: $x(x+9)=x^2+9x$ and $(x+3)(x+6)=x^2+9x+18$. With $t=x^2+9x$,
\[t(t+18)+56=t^2+18t+56=(t+4)(t+14).\]
So the expression equals $(x^2+9x+4)(x^2+9x+14)$, and $x^2+9x+14=(x+2)(x+7)$, while $x^2+9x+4$ has discriminant $65$, not a square. Therefore
\[x(x+3)(x+6)(x+9)+56=\boxed{(x+2)(x+7)(x^2+9x+4)}.\]
