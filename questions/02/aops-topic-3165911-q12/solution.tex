On a point Euhan scores with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{5}{8}$. A wrong call has probability $\dfrac{1}{8}$, so conditional on an Euhan point, its chance of being a wrong call is $\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{8}}{\dfrac{5}{8}}=\dfrac{1}{5}$. Conditional on any complete sequence of point winners, these labels still have that conditional probability, because all points are independent and the stopping rule depends only on winners. In any sequence where Euhan wins, he has exactly twenty-five scoring points. By linearity their expected number of wrong-call labels is $25\cdot\dfrac{1}{5}=\boxed5$. Averaging over all winning sequences leaves the same value.
