In a tennis game, each independent point is equally likely truly in or out. Euhan always calls out balls correctly; for an in ball he calls it correctly with probability $\dfrac{3}{4}$ and incorrectly out with probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$, independently. An out call gives Euhan a point and an in call gives Minjune a point. First to twenty-five points wins. Conditional on Euhan winning, find his expected number of wrong calls.
