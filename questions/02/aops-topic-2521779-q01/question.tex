For $n\ge2$ let
\[S_n=\dfrac13+\dfrac18+\dfrac1{15}+\cdots+\dfrac{1}{n^2-1}=\sum_{k=2}^{n}\dfrac{1}{k^2-1}.\]
Find a closed form for $S_n$, and find the value of the infinite sum $\displaystyle\sum_{k=2}^{\infty}\dfrac1{k^2-1}$.
