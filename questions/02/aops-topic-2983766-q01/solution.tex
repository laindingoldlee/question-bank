Let $f(k)=\sum_{i=1}^7(k+i)^2x_i$. Expanding, $f(k)=k^2\sum x_i+2k\sum ix_i+\sum i^2x_i$ is a quadratic in $k$. We know $f(0)=1$, $f(1)=12$, $f(2)=123$ and want $f(3)$.

For a quadratic the second differences are constant: first differences $11$ and $111$, second difference $100$, so the next first difference is $211$ and $f(3)=123+211=\boxed{334}$.
