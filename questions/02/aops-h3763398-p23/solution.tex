Let $n$ have prime exponents $e_p$. For $\dfrac{n}{2}$ to be a square, $e_2$ must be odd and all other exponents even. For $\dfrac{n}{3}$ to be a cube, $e_3\equiv1\pmod3$ and all other exponents are multiples of 3. The least possible values are therefore $e_2=3,e_3=4$, and zero for other primes. Thus $n=2^3\cdot3^4=\boxed{648}$, with $\dfrac{n}{2}=18^2$ and $\dfrac{n}{3}=6^3$.
