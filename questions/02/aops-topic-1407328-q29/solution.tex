\textbf{$11$ cannot be written.} It is odd, and $4a+6b$ is even, so $c$ must be odd; $c\ge1$ gives $11-9=2=4a+6b$, which is impossible.

\textbf{Every integer $n\ge12$ can be written.} We have
\[
12=4\cdot3,\qquad 13=4+9,\qquad 14=4\cdot2+6,\qquad 15=6+9,
\]
and adding $4$ (increasing $a$) to a representation of $n$ gives one of $n+4$. So all of $12,13,14,15,16,\dots$ are representable.

Hence the answer is $\boxed{11}$.
