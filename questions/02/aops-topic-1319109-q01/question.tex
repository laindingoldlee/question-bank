Three Pile Nim is a two-person game. Pattie and Malik take turns. There are three piles of chips; on each turn a player takes one or more chips from any one pile, and the player who takes the last chip wins. At one point there are $1$, $2$ and $6$ chips in the three piles, and it is Malik's turn. With best play, Malik has exactly one winning move. What is it?
