For an integer $x>1$, let $\operatorname{lpf}(x)$ and $\operatorname{gpf}(x)$ be its least and greatest prime factors. Among integers $2\le x\le100$, let $m$ count those whose two factors sum to 10, and $n$ those whose two factors sum to 5. Find $n-m$.
