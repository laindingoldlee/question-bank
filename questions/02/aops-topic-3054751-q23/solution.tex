A sum of 10 requires least and greatest factors $(3,7)$ or $(5,5)$. In the first case both 3 and 7 divide $x$; below 101, the possibilities are 21 and 63, since adding a factor 5 or another 7 exceeds 100. In the second case the possibilities are 5 and 25. Thus $m=4$. A sum of 5 requires $(2,3)$, so $x=2^a3^b$ with $a,b\ge1$. For $b=1,2,3$ there are respectively 5,3,1 possible exponents $a$, and higher $b$ are impossible. Thus $n=9$ and $\boxed{n-m=5}$.
