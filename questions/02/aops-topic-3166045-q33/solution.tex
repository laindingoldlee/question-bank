The difference $f(n)-f(n-1)$ is $-\dfrac{1}{2}$ times the preceding difference. Starting with difference one, summing the geometric progression gives $f(n)=\dfrac{2}{3}\left(1-(-\dfrac{1}{2})^{n-1}\right)$. The exponential term tends to zero, so the limit is $\boxed{\dfrac{2}{3}}$.
