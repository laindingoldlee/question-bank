A circle with centre $O$ has radius $5$. From a point $P$ with $PO=9$, the two tangents to the circle touch it at $Q$ and $R$. A third tangent touches the circle at a point $U$ of the minor arc $QR$ and meets the segments $PQ$ and $PR$ at $S$ and $T$, respectively. Find the perimeter of triangle $PST$.
