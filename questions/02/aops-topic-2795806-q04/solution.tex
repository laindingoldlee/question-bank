For a point $X$ not on any line, let $c(X)$ be the number of lines having $X$ on one fixed chosen side (choose a ``positive'' side for each line). All points of one region lie on the same side of every line, so $c$ is constant on each region. Colour a region brown if $c$ is even and green if $c$ is odd.

Two regions sharing a boundary segment lie on the same sides of every line except the line containing that segment, where they are on opposite sides. So their values of $c$ differ by exactly $1$, and they get different colours. $\blacksquare$

(Alternatively, induct on the number of lines: when a new line is added, swap the colours of all regions on one side of it.)
