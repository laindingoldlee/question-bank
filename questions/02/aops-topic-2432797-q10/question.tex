Bessie pours one cup of dandelion milk into one large container and one cup of daisy milk into another large container. She pours $\dfrac23$ of the daisy milk into the first container and stirs thoroughly. Then she pours $\dfrac23$ of the mixture in the first container into the second container and stirs thoroughly. Finally, she pours $\dfrac23$ of the mixture in the second container into the first container and stirs thoroughly. What is the final ratio of dandelion milk to daisy milk in the first container?
