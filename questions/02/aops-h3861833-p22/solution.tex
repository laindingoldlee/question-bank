The numerator sum is $n(2n+1)$ and divisor is $n\dfrac{n+1}{2}$. Their quotient is $4-\dfrac{2}{n+1}$, between $3$ and $4$ for $n\ge 2$. The remainder is therefore $n(2n+1)-3n\dfrac{n+1}{2}=n\dfrac{n-1}{2}$. Summing for $n=2,\ldots,14$ gives $\sum\binom n2=\binom{15}{3}=\boxed{455}$, by counting three-element sets by their largest element.
