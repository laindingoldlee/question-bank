A shelf holds $35$ boxes at the beginning of Day $1$. On Day $k$, first half of the boxes are thrown away (if the number is odd, the number thrown away is rounded down), and then $60-k$ new boxes are added. At the end of which day are there, for the first time, fewer than $4$ boxes on the shelf?
