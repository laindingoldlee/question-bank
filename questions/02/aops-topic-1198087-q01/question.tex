Define $x\star y=xy+x+y$ for digits $x,y$ with $1\le x\le9$ and $0\le y\le9$, and let $\overline{xy}$ be the two-digit number with tens digit $x$ and units digit $y$. Find the sum of all values of $x\star y$ for which $x\star y=\overline{xy}$.
