Write $T,S,M,B,C$ for the truth values that each named suspect is guilty. Requiring exactly one truth in Terry\textquotesingle s pair gives $S=C$. Steve\textquotesingle s pair gives $M\ne C$. Matt\textquotesingle s pair gives $C=T$. Ben\textquotesingle s pair repeats $M\ne S$. Carl\textquotesingle s pair gives $B=T$. Thus $T=S=B=C$ and $M$ is their opposite. There are exactly two guilt assignments: Matt alone, or all four of Terry, Steve, Ben, Carl. Therefore the statements alone do not uniquely identify the criminals. With the additional assumption of exactly one criminal, the answer is $\boxed{\text{Matt}}$.
