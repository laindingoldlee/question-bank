After the specified fall its value is $\dfrac{I}{3}$, regardless of the earlier peak. The last two losses leave $\dfrac{I}{3}\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot\dfrac{1}{5}=\dfrac{I}{60}=50$. Hence $\boxed{I=3000\text{ dollars}}$.
