Put $A=(0,0)$, $B=(6,0)$, $C=(6,6)$, $D=(0,6)$, so $P=(0,1)$ and $Q=(6,1)$. The overlap is bounded by the lines
\[AC:\ y=x,\qquad BD:\ y=6-x,\qquad PC:\ y=\dfrac56x+1,\qquad DQ:\ y=6-\dfrac56x.\]
Its vertices are $AC\cap BD=(3,3)$, $AC\cap DQ=\left(\dfrac{36}{11},\dfrac{36}{11}\right)$, $PC\cap DQ=\left(3,\dfrac72\right)$ and $PC\cap BD=\left(\dfrac{30}{11},\dfrac{36}{11}\right)$. Its diagonals are the vertical segment from $(3,3)$ to $\left(3,\dfrac72\right)$, of length $\dfrac12$, and the horizontal segment of length $\dfrac6{11}$; they are perpendicular and cross inside. So the area is
\[\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac6{11}=\boxed{\dfrac3{22}}.\]
