The integer $1$ and all $25$ primes at most $100$ qualify. An even squarefree composite cannot qualify, because every odd prime is $1$ modulo $2$. An odd integer with three distinct prime factors is at least $3\cdot5\cdot7=105$. Thus it remains to count products of two odd primes. Those satisfying the condition are $15,33,51,69,87$ (factor $3$), $35,65,85,95$ (factor $5$), and $77,91$ (factor $7$). Larger smaller-prime factors give products exceeding $100$. There are $11$ such products, for a total of $1+25+11=\boxed{37}$.
