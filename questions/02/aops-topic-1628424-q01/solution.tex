As $x\to\infty$, $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^x=\dfrac{1}{2^x}$ is positive and tends to $0$, so $y$ approaches $-\dfrac{3}{2}$ from above but never reaches it. (As $x\to-\infty$, $y$ grows without bound.) The asymptote is the line $\boxed{y=-\dfrac{3}{2}}$. The graph is the graph of $y=\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^x$, whose asymptote is $y=0$, moved down by $\dfrac{3}{2}$.
