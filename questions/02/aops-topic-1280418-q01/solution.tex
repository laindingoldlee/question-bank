Going from one January birthday to the next adds $365$ days ($\equiv1$ weekday) or $366$ days ($\equiv2$) if a February $29$ lies in between. From January $1988$ to January $1993$ the February $29$s are in $1988$ and $1992$, so the shift is $2+1+1+1+2=7\equiv0$ days. Her birthday is again on a $\boxed{\text{Wednesday}}$.
