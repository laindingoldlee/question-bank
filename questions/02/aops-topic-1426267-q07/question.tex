Triangle $ABC$ is isosceles with $AC=BC$. Point $P$ lies on side $BC$ so that segment $AP$ bisects $\angle BAC$. Point $D$ lies outside triangle $ABC$ on ray $BC$ (beyond $C$). Point $Q$ is on side $AB$ so that triangle $DQB$ is similar to triangle $APB$ (with $D$, $Q$, $B$ corresponding to $A$, $P$, $B$). Point $R$ lies on side $AB$ so that segment $DR$ bisects $\angle QDB$. If the angle bisectors $AP$ and $DR$ intersect at a right angle, what is the degree measure of $\angle ABC$? Express your answer as a common fraction.
