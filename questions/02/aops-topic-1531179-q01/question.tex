How many multiples of $3$ are between $62$ and $215$? A student argued: ``Dividing the list $62,\dots,215$ by $3$ gives $20\dfrac{2}{3},\dots,71\dfrac{2}{3}$; subtracting $19\dfrac{2}{3}$ gives $1,\dots,52$, so there are $52$.'' Find the error and the correct answer.
