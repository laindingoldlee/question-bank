\textbf{The error.} The multiples of $3$ in the list become the \emph{whole numbers} in the list from $20\dfrac{2}{3}$ to $71\dfrac{2}{3}$, namely $21,22,\dots,71$. Subtracting $19\dfrac{2}{3}$ moves the endpoints to $1$ and $52$, but it moves the whole numbers $21,\dots,71$ to the non-integers $1\dfrac{1}{3},\dots,51\dfrac{1}{3}$; the shift has to be by a whole number to keep counting whole numbers. Counting the integers from $1$ to $52$ counts the wrong set.

\textbf{Correct count.} The multiples of $3$ are $3\cdot21=63$ up to $3\cdot71=213$, so there are $71-21+1=\boxed{51}$ of them (subtract $20$ from $21,\dots,71$ to get $1,\dots,51$).
