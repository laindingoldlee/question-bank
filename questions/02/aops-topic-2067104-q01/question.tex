Real or complex numbers \(a,b,c\) satisfy
\[
a+b+c=3,\qquad a^2+b^2+c^2=5,\qquad a^3+b^3+c^3=7.
\]
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] A student derived \(ab+bc+ca=2\) and \(3a+bc-a^2=2\) (both correct), then substituted \(bc=(3-a-c)(3-a-b)\) into \(3a-(3-a-c)(3-a-b)-a^2=2\), concluded \(a\in\{1,2\}\), and reached a contradiction. Find the error.
\item[(b)] Find \(a^4+b^4+c^4\).
\end{enumerate}
