Stefan takes out a four-year loan of $\$12{,}000$ at an interest rate of $8.5\%$ compounded annually. At the end of each year the year's interest is added to the amount he owes, and he immediately makes a payment; the following year he is charged interest on the amount he still owes after that payment. The four payments are all equal, and after the fourth payment he owes nothing. What is the amount of each payment, to the nearest cent?
