First, $(-1)\cdot0=0$, so distributivity gives
$0=(-1)(1+(-1))=-1+(-1)(-1)$; adding $1$ proves $(-1)(-1)=1$.
For a complex number $z=x+iy$, multiplying by $-1$ gives $-x-iy$, the half-turn $(x,y)\mapsto(-x,-y)$. Multiplying by $i$ gives $-y+ix$, the quarter-turn $(x,y)\mapsto(-y,x)$. Thus $i^2=-1$ and $\boxed{i^4=1}$. A quarter-turn represents multiplication by $i$, while the negative real unit represents a half-turn.
