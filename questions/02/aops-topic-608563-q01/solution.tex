The palindromes after $15951$ are $16061$, $16161$, $16261$, $\dots$ (once the first two digits are $16$, the palindromes are $\overline{16c61}$ for $c=0,1,2,\dots$, and none lie between $15951$ and $16061$). The third one is $16261$. The car travelled $16261-15951=310$ miles in $5$ hours, at
\[
\dfrac{310}{5}=\boxed{62}\ \text{miles per hour}.
\]
