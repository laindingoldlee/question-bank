Treat token colours as states and each chosen reward as a directed transition, possibly terminating play. If all repeated-state cycles in Bob's path had zero total cash, deleting those cycles would leave a path with no repeated colour and at most 32 rewards, totaling at most 320 dollars. Since Bob earns 500, some reachable cycle has positive cash reward. Rob follows the same choices to reach that cycle, then repeats it as many times as necessary. Rewards and successor tokens are fixed, so each repetition earns the same positive amount. Eventually his total reaches at least 1000 dollars.
