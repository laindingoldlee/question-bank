A machine accepts tokens of 32 colours. For each colour there are two fixed choices, each awarding cash between 0 and 10 dollars and possibly one new token. Bob starts with white and follows a finite sequence earning 500 dollars. Rob also has white and knows Bob's sequence. Prove Rob can earn at least 1000 dollars.
