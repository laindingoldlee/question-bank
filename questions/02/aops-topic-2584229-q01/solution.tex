The digit $5$ appears, so $b\ge6$. Now
\[441_b=4b^2+4b+1=(2b+1)^2,\]
so $n=2b+1$ and $(n-2)^2=(2b-1)^2$. Hence
\[3b^2+5b+1=4b^2-4b+1\iff b^2-9b=0\iff b=\boxed{9}.\]
Check: $441_9=324+36+1=361=19^2$ and $351_9=243+45+1=289=17^2$.

(About the guess-and-check approach: the digit $5$ only gives $b\ge6$, so $b=6$ must not be excluded in advance. In general $(n-2)^2-351_b=(2b-1)^2-\left(3b^2+5b+1\right)=b(b-9)$, which is positive for $b>9$ and negative for $6\le b<9$. At $b=10$ it equals $361-351>0$, so $b=10$ is too large: that is the real reason the answer is below $10$.)
