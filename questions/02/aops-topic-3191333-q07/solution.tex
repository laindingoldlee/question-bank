If its edge is $s$, successive applications of Pythagoras give body diagonal $s\sqrt3$. Thus $s=3$ and volume $\boxed{27}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(3,0),C=(3,3),D=(0,3),v=(1,1);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw((A+v)--(B+v)--(C+v)--(D+v)--cycle);draw(A--(A+v)^^B--(B+v)^^C--(C+v)^^D--(D+v));draw(A--(C+v),blue);label("$3\sqrt3$",(2,2),NW);
\end{asy}
