Use the complement: fewer than $4$ heads means $0$, $1$, $2$ or $3$ heads, which happens in
\[
\dbinom90+\dbinom91+\dbinom92+\dbinom93=1+9+36+84=130
\]
of the $2^9=512$ equally likely outcomes. So the probability is $1-\dfrac{130}{512}=\dfrac{382}{512}=\boxed{\dfrac{191}{256}}$.
