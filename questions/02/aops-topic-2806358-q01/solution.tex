$\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{100}k^2=\dfrac{100\cdot101\cdot201}6=338350$, and $338350=7\cdot48335+5$. The remainder is $\boxed{5}$.

(Alternatively, any $7$ consecutive squares sum to $0+1+4+2+2+4+1=14\equiv0\pmod7$; $98$ terms contribute $0$ and $99^2+100^2\equiv1+4=5$.)
