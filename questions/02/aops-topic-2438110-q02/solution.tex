\textbf{(a)} $\sqrt{-9}=i\sqrt9=\boxed{3i}$; indeed $(3i)^2=9i^2=-9$.

\textbf{(b)} $\sqrt{-8}=i\sqrt8=\boxed{2i\sqrt2}$, since $\sqrt8=\sqrt{4\cdot2}=2\sqrt2$; indeed $\left(2\sqrt2\,i\right)^2=8i^2=-8$.
