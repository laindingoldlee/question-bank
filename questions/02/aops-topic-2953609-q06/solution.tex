Suppose we are at different vertices $u\ne v$. I move to $a\ne u$ and the spider moves to $b\ne v$, giving $9$ equally likely pairs. We meet iff $a=b$, which requires $a\notin\{u,v\}$: $2$ choices, each allowing $b=a$. So each minute we meet with probability $\dfrac29$, independently of the past. The waiting time is geometric with mean $\boxed{\dfrac92}$ minutes.
