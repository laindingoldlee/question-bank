I stand at one vertex of a regular tetrahedron and a spider stands at another. Every minute we both move simultaneously, each to one of the other three vertices chosen uniformly at random and independently. We meet when we arrive at the same vertex (passing on an edge does not count). What is the expected number of minutes until we meet?
