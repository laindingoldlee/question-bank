The quadrilateral is cyclic, so power of $P$ gives $PB\cdot PA=PC\cdot PD$, hence $PD=72$. Put $P=(0,0),B=(3,0),A=(24,0)$. Since $AD\perp PA$, $D$ has first coordinate $24$. Its second coordinate squared is $72^2-24^2=4608$. Thus $BD^2=(24-3)^2+4608=\boxed{5049}$.
