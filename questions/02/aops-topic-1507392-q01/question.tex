For each positive integer $n$ define
\[
f(n)=\left\lfloor\dfrac{n!\bmod(n+1)}{n}\right\rfloor(n-1)+2,
\]
where $n!\bmod(n+1)$ is the remainder of $n!$ on division by $n+1$. Prove that $f(n)=n+1$ if $n+1$ is prime, and $f(n)=2$ otherwise. Deduce that every value of $f$ is prime and that every prime is a value of $f$.
