A mouse is three times as smart as a bird, but a bird is twice as strong as a mouse. A bird is twice as smart as a cat, but only one fourth as strong as a cat. A dog is one and a half times as strong as a cat and twice as smart as a cat. A human is four times as strong as a dog but only two and a half times as smart as a dog. How many times as strong as a mouse is a human, and how many times as smart?
