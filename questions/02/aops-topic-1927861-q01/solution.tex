Measure everything relative to the cat.

\textbf{Smartness.} Let the cat's smartness be $1$. Then the bird has $2$, the mouse $3\cdot2=6$, the dog $2$, and the human $2.5\cdot2=5$. So the human is $\dfrac{5}{6}$ as smart as the mouse.

\textbf{Strength.} Let the cat's strength be $4$. Then the bird has $\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot4=1$, the mouse $\dfrac{1}{2}$ (the bird is twice as strong), the dog $1.5\cdot4=6$, and the human $4\cdot6=24$. So the human is $\dfrac{24}{\dfrac{1}{2}}=48$ times as strong as the mouse.

Answer: the human is $\boxed{48}$ times as strong as the mouse and $\boxed{\dfrac{5}{6}}$ times as smart (that is, less smart than the mouse).
