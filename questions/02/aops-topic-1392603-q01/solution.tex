Follow the balance:
\[
x\ \to\ x+100\ \to\ 2x+200\ \to\ 2x+150\ \to\ 4x+300\ \to\ 3x+300\ \to\ \dfrac{2}{3}(3x+300)=2x+200 .
\]
So $2x+200=500$ and $x=\boxed{150}$. (Check: $150,250,500,450,900,750,500$.)
