Emma opened an account with $x$ dollars. She added $\$100$ to it. She doubled the money in her account. She then took out $\$50$. She doubled the money in the account again. She then took out an amount equal to her initial deposit. Finally she took out $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of her money. At the end, $\$500$ was in her account. What is $x$?
