Write $r=\sqrt3$. With $A=(0,0),B=(6,0),C=(6,6),D=(0,6)$, the slopes of $DE,DF$ are $-r,-\dfrac{1}{r}$. Therefore
\[
E=(2r,0),\quad F=(6,6-2r),\quad
G=(3r-3,3r-3),\quad H=(9-3r,9-3r).
\]
These are obtained by intersecting the respective lines with the sides and with $y=x$. Applying the shoelace formula to the ordered vertices $B,E,G,H,F$, the signed doubled area is
\[
2r(3r-3)+(9-3r)(6-2r)-6(9-3r)-6(6-2r)=-12r.
\]
Hence the area is $\boxed{6\sqrt3}$.
