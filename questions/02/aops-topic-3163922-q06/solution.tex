The distance between adjacent side midpoints is $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt 2}$, so tangency gives semicircle radius $r=\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt 2}$. Their interiors do not overlap. Their total area is $4\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\pi r^2=\dfrac{\pi}{4}$. Thus the outside area is $1-\dfrac{\pi}{4}$, and $a+b=1+\dfrac{1}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{4}}$.
\begin{asy}
import geometry; size(220);
real r=1/(2*sqrt(2));draw((0,0)--(1,0)--(1,1)--(0,1)--cycle);draw(arc((0.5,0),r,0,180));draw(arc((1,0.5),r,90,270));draw(arc((0.5,1),r,180,360));draw(arc((0,0.5),r,-90,90));label("$A$",(0,0),SW);label("$B$",(1,0),SE);label("$C$",(1,1),NE);label("$D$",(0,1),NW);label("$R$",(0.5,0.5));
\end{asy}
