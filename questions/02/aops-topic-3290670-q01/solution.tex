Status must alternate, giving $2$ of $16$ patterns. For numbers fix the first in three ways. If the third equals it, the second and fourth each have two choices; otherwise the third has two choices and both neighbours are forced to the remaining number. This gives $3(4+2)=18$ of $81$ assignments. Independence gives $\dfrac{2}{16}\dfrac{18}{81}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{36}}$.
