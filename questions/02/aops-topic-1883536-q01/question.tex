A palindrome is a word that reads the same backwards as forwards, such as ``eye'' or ``qwertyytrewq''. How many letters are in the shortest palindrome that contains the letters b, o, g, t, r and o (that is, at least one b, g, t, r and at least two o's), in any order and not necessarily adjacent?
