In a palindrome every letter occurs an even number of times, except possibly the single middle letter of an odd-length palindrome. So at most one letter occurs an odd number of times.

Each of b, g, t, r occurs at least once, and at most one of them can occur an odd number of times, so at least three of them occur at least twice. Together with at least two o's, the palindrome has at least $3\cdot2+1+2=9$ letters.

Nine letters suffice: ``botgrgtob'' reads the same in both directions and contains b, o, g, t, r, o. So the answer is $\boxed{9}$.
