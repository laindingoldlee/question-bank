Let the first two days together be $T$ tons. The third day is $\dfrac{5}{9}T$, so
\[T+\dfrac{5}{9}T=\dfrac{14}{9}T=98,\qquad T=63 .\]
The first day is $3$ tons less than the second, so the first day is $\dfrac{63-3}{2}=30$ and the second is $33$. The third day is $\dfrac{5}{9}\cdot63=35$. So the store sold $\boxed{30,\ 33\text{ and }35}$ tons on the three days (total $98$).
