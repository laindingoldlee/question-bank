For $c\ne4,5$, cross-multiplying gives $c(c-4)=4(c-5)$, that is
\[
c^2-8c+20=0 .
\]
Its discriminant is $64-80=-16<0$, so there are \textbf{no real solutions}. Completing the square, $(c-4)^2=-4$, so the complex solutions are $\boxed{c=4\pm2i}$ (neither is $4$ or $5$, so both satisfy the original equation).
