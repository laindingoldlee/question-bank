Luna has an unlimited supply of pieces labelled $1$, $2$, $4$ and $5$. She puts down pieces one at a time until the labels add up to exactly $8$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] How many different collections of pieces can she end up with (the order does not matter)?
\item[(b)] How many different sequences of pieces can she put down (the order matters)?
\end{enumerate}
