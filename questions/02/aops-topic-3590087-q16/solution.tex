Arrange all five people in one sequence in $5!$ ways, then divide that sequence into four ordered queues by choosing their nonnegative lengths summing to $5$. Stars and bars gives $\binom83=56$ length choices. Every queue arrangement corresponds uniquely to this sequence and length choice. Thus the answer is $5!\binom83=\boxed{6720}$.
