For a palindromic date $d_1d_2m_1m_2y_1y_2y_3y_4$, the year must be $m_2m_1d_2d_1$. To be a positive four-digit year below 2000, its first digit is 1, so the month ends in 1. The possible months are 01 and 11; month 11 yields years 11xx, later than month 01's 10xx. Maximizing the final two year digits means maximizing the reversal of a valid November day. The largest is 92, corresponding to day 29. Thus the latest date is 29 November 1192, whose digit sum is $2+9+1+1+1+1+9+2=\boxed{26}$.
