There is a unique polynomial \(P(x)\) of the form
\[
P(x)=7x^7+c_1x^6+c_2x^5+\cdots+c_6x+c_7
\]
such that \(P(1)=1,\ P(2)=2,\ \dots,\ P(7)=7\). Determine \(P(0)\).
