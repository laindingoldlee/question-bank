Let \(Q(x)=P(x)-x\). Then \(Q\) has degree \(7\) with leading coefficient \(7\), and \(Q(k)=0\) for \(k=1,2,\dots,7\). A degree-\(7\) polynomial with these seven roots is
\[
Q(x)=7(x-1)(x-2)\cdots(x-7).
\]
(This also shows the polynomial \(P\) exists and is unique.) Therefore
\[
P(0)=Q(0)+0=7\cdot(-1)^7\cdot 7!=-7\cdot5040=\boxed{-35280}.
\]
