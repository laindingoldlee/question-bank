A circle tangent to both rays from $A$ has its centre on the bisector of angle $A$, which is the altitude $AM$ to $BC$ ($M$ the midpoint of $BC$). The two circles are the incircle (radius $r$, centre $I$) and the excircle opposite $A$ (radius $r_a$, centre $I_a$), both touching $BC$ at $M$.

For a circle of radius $\rho$ tangent to both rays, the distance from $A$ to its centre is $\dfrac{\rho}{\sin30^\circ}=2\rho$.
\begin{itemize}
\item Incircle: $AM=AI+IM=2r+r=3r$.
\item Excircle: $AM=AI_a-I_aM=2r_a-r_a=r_a$.
\end{itemize}
So $r_a=3r$, and the ratio is $\boxed{3}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,sqrt(3)),B=(-1,0),C=(1,0);
real r=sqrt(3)/3,ra=sqrt(3);pair I=(0,r),Ia=(0,-ra);
draw(A--A+2.6*(B-A),gray);draw(A--A+2.6*(C-A),gray);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
draw(circle(I,r),blue);draw(circle(Ia,ra),red);
dot(I);dot(Ia);dot((0,0));draw(A--Ia,dashed);
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,W);label("$C$",C,E);label("$M$",(0,0),SW,fontsize(7pt));
label("$I$",I,W,fontsize(8pt));label("$I_a$",Ia,W,fontsize(8pt));
label("$r$",(0,r/2),E,blue+fontsize(8pt));label("$r_a$",(0,-ra/2),E,red+fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}
