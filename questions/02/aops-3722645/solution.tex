The captains are the three tallest exactly when those three athletes receive different colours. The first colour is arbitrary. Of the remaining eight bands, six have a different colour, and after two different colours have been chosen, three of the remaining seven bands have the third colour. Thus the probability is $\dfrac{6}{8}\cdot\dfrac{3}{7}=\boxed{\dfrac{9}{28}}$, choice C. The six shorter athletes cannot displace any of these captains.
