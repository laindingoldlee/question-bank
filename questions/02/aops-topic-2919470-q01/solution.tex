Substitute $x=t-2$: $t^3+5t-15=0$. The function $t^3+5t$ is increasing, so there is exactly one real root. By Cardano, with $t=u+v$, $uv=-\dfrac53$ and $u^3+v^3=15$, $u^3,v^3$ are the roots of $z^2-15z-\dfrac{125}{27}=0$, i.e. $\dfrac{15}2\pm\dfrac{5\sqrt{789}}{18}$. Hence
\[x=-2+\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{15}2+\dfrac{5\sqrt{789}}{18}}+\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{15}2-\dfrac{5\sqrt{789}}{18}}\approx\boxed{-0.1886}.\]
