In triangle $ABC$, $AB=14$, $BC=15$ and $AC=13$. Let $X$ be the circumcentre of the triangle. The supplement of $\angle BXC$ is one of the angles of a right triangle with integer side lengths. Find the smallest possible perimeter of this right triangle.
