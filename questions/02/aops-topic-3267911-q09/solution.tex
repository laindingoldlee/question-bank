Let $E_0$ be the remaining expectation when the latest roll is not 1, including the start, and $E_1$ when it is 1. Then $E_0=1+\dfrac{1}{3}E_1+\dfrac{2}{3}E_0$, so $E_0=3+E_1$. Also $E_1=1+\dfrac{1}{3}E_1+\dfrac{1}{3}E_0$, since a 2 finishes and a 0 resets. Thus $2E_1=3+E_0$, giving $E_1=6$ and $\boxed{E_0=9}$. Expectations are finite because each disjoint pair of rolls has probability $\dfrac{1}{9}$ of being 1,2.
