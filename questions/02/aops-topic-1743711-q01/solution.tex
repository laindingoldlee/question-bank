The sum of an odd number of consecutive integers is the number of terms times the middle term, so the middle (ninth) term is $\dfrac{2006}{17}=118$. The integers are $110,\dots,126$, and the second largest is $118+7=\boxed{\textbf{(G)}\ 125}$.
