The hexagon inradius is $4\cos30^\circ=2\sqrt3$, so its incircle area is $12\pi$ and $x=24\pi$. An equilateral triangle's inradius is one third of its altitude, here $2$, so its incircle area is $4\pi$ and $y=12\pi$. Therefore $\dfrac{x^2}{y^2}=(\dfrac{24\pi}{12\pi})^2=\boxed4$. 
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

pair O=(0,0);path h;for(int i=0;i<6;++i){if(i==0)h=4*dir(60*i);else h=h--4*dir(60*i);}draw(h--cycle);draw(circle(O,2*sqrt(3)));draw(O--3.4641016*dir(30),dashed);label("$2\sqrt3$",1.7*dir(30),NW);pair A=(10,6),B=(10-2*sqrt(3),0),C=(10+2*sqrt(3),0),I=(10,2);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(circle(I,2));draw(A--(10,0),dashed);label("$6$",(10,4),E);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);
\end{asy}
