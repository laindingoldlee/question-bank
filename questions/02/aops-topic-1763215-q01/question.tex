In an isosceles trapezoid $ABCD$ with $AB\parallel DC$ and $AB<DC$, an altitude is dropped from $A$ to the point $E$ on the longer base $DC$. The legs $AD$ and $BC$ are $2$ cm long and $DE=1$ cm, and segment $BE$ is drawn. Show that the length of the shorter base $AB$ is not determined by this information. (A student argues that $AB$ must be $2$ cm, because ``$BE$ is an altitude, so triangle $EAB$ has a right angle at $A$, and the figure splits into three equilateral triangles''. Find the error.)
