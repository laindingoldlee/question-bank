The first player to finish an apple chooses the larger remaining apple. The other player finishes their first apple before this second apple can be finished, since the sum of the two lightest apples is $500>450$. For each first choice by Arman, take the worst outcome over Kuanysh\textquotesingle s three responses. The guaranteed totals for first choices $200,300,400,450$ are respectively $600,700,600,650$. For example, after Arman chooses $300$, Kuanysh can choose $200,400,450$, giving Arman totals $700,750,700$. Thus Arman chooses $300$ and guarantees $\boxed{700\text{ g}}$.
