Georgia lives $3$ miles from the bank of a straight river. Her friend Caleigh lives $2$ miles from the river on the same side, $12$ miles further along the river. Georgia jogs from her house to the river and then to Caleigh's house at $5$ mph. What is the least time this can take? Express your answer as a mixed number of hours.
