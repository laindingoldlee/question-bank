Treat the history books as one block. Then there are $6$ units (the block and $5$ math books), arranged in $6!$ ways, and the history books can be ordered inside the block in $7!$ ways. The answer is $6!\cdot7!=720\cdot5040=\boxed{3{,}628{,}800}$.
