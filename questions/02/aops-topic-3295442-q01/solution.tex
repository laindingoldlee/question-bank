Factoring gives $(x-1)(x-5)=0$, so $x=1,5$. Completing the square gives $(x-3)^2=4$, so $x=3\pm2$, the same roots. Factoring rewrites a product equal to zero, whereas completing the square isolates a squared linear expression.
