Order the numbers $a_1\le\cdots\le a_{2008}$. The first 100 have positive sum, so $a_{100}>0$; otherwise all 100 would be nonpositive. Every remaining number is at least $a_{100}$ and is positive. Adding them to the positive sum of the first 100 gives a positive total.
