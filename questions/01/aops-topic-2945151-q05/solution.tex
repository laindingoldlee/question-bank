Let $u_0,u_1,u_2$ be the expected value of $(-1)^T$ for the remaining waiting time $T$, when the current suffix matching a prefix of 245 is empty,2,or 24. The process terminates almost surely: disjoint blocks of three have independent success probability $\dfrac{1}{216}$. Every new roll changes the sign, and a completed pattern has remaining value 1. First-step conditioning gives $6u_0=-(5u_0+u_1)$, $6u_1=-(4u_0+u_1+u_2)$, $6u_2=-(4u_0+u_1+1)$. Thus $u_1=-11u_0$, $u_2=73u_0$, and $431u_0=-1$. Since $u_0=P(T\text{ even})-P(T\text{ odd})$, the required probability is $\dfrac{1-u_0}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{216}{431}}$.
