Five friends each have a mark. A spell permutes the marks so that nobody keeps the mark they had just before the spell; each cast applies a uniformly random such permutation (a derangement) to the current arrangement, independently. Starting from the correct arrangement, the spell is cast repeatedly. What is the expected total number of casts until everyone has their own mark again?
