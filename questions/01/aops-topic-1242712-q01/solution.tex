\textbf{Yes.} With $s=a+b=c+d$ and $p=ab=cd$, both $\{a,b\}$ and $\{c,d\}$ are the roots of $t^2-st+p=0$ (with multiplicity), so $\{a,b\}=\{c,d\}$ as multisets. The ordering conditions then force $a=c$ (the larger root) and $b=d$ (the smaller). (For real numbers; the conditions $a\ge b$, $c\ge d$ presuppose real values.)
