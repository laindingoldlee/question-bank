\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What is the sum of all integer values of $n$ for which $\dbinom{20}{n}+\dbinom{20}{10}=\dbinom{21}{11}$?
\item[(b)] A student says: ``Since $\dbinom{20}{n}=\dbinom{20}{20-n}$, the solutions always come in pairs $n$ and $20-n$, each pair adds up to $20$, so the answer is $20$ whatever the equation is.'' What is wrong with this argument?
\end{enumerate}
