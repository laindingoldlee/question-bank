Since $123=3\cdot41$ and $328=2^3\cdot41$, we have $\gcd(123,328)=41$ and $\operatorname{lcm}(123,328)=2^3\cdot3\cdot41=984$. The product is
\[41\cdot984=\boxed{40344}.\]
This equals $123\cdot328$, as it must: for any positive integers $m,n$, $\gcd(m,n)\cdot\operatorname{lcm}(m,n)=mn$, because for each prime the gcd takes the smaller and the lcm the larger of the two exponents, and the two together use both exponents.
