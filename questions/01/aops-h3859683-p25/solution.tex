Write $d=\dfrac{|N-e|}{N}$. A positive score requires $\lfloor150(1-d)\rfloor\ge121$, equivalent to $150(1-d)\ge121$. Thus $d\le\dfrac{29}{150}$, or $\boxed{\dfrac{121N}{150}\le e\le\dfrac{179N}{150}}$. Integer estimates are the integers in this interval. The exact estimate $e=N$ gives the maximum score 30.
