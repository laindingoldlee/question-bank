All $\dbinom{15}{11}=\dbinom{15}{4}=1365$ choices of $11$ kittens are equally likely. Choosing $4$ of the $6$ boys and $7$ of the $9$ girls can be done in $\dbinom64\dbinom97=15\cdot36=540$ ways. So
\[
P=\dfrac{540}{1365}=\boxed{\dfrac{36}{91}\approx0.396}.
\]
