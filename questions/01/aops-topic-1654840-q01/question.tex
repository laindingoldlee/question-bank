A TV studio has brought in $6$ boy kittens and $9$ girl kittens for a cat food commercial. The director chooses $11$ of the $15$ kittens at random. What is the probability that exactly $4$ boy kittens and $7$ girl kittens are chosen? Give the exact value and the value rounded to three decimal places.
