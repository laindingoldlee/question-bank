A game show has $n+1$ curtains, where $n\ge2$. A car is behind one of them, chosen uniformly at random, and a goat is behind each of the other $n$. The contestant first chooses a curtain, without opening it. Then there are $n-1$ rounds. In each round:
\begin{itemize}
\item the host opens a curtain with a goat behind it, chosen uniformly at random among the curtains that are still closed, hide a goat, and are not the contestant's current curtain;
\item then the contestant either stays with the current curtain or switches to another closed curtain.
\end{itemize}
After the last round the contestant's curtain is opened, and the contestant wins if it hides the car. A strategy tells the contestant, depending on what has happened so far, whether and where to switch in each round. Find the greatest possible probability of winning, and a strategy that achieves it.
