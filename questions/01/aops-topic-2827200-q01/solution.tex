The equation says $\dfrac{a!}{(a-b)!}=a(a-1)\cdots(a-b+1)=a+b$.

If $b=1$, it reads $a=a+1$: impossible. If $b\ge2$ (so $a\ge3$), the product is at least $a(a-1)\ge2a>a+b$. So there are $\boxed{\text{no solutions}}$. (With $a<b$, as originally written, $(a-b)!$ is undefined.)
