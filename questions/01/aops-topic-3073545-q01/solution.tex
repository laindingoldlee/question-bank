There are $\binom82=28$ pairs, and their positive differences lie in $1,\ldots,14$. If each difference occurred at most twice, all fourteen would have to occur exactly twice. But difference $14$ can arise only from the single pair $\{1,15\}$, so it occurs at most once. This contradiction proves that some difference occurs at least three times.
