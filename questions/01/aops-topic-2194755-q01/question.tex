Anna, Bob and Carson stand at the vertices $A$, $B$ and $C$ of an equilateral triangle. At a signal, all three start walking at the same moment, in straight lines and at the same constant speed: Anna walks to $B$, Bob walks to $C$, and Carson walks to $A$. They agree that the distance between any two of them must always be at least $6$ feet. What is the smallest possible side length of triangle $ABC$, in feet?
