Let the integers be \(a<b<c<d<e\), so \(x=e\). Then \(a+b=320-283=37\), \(e=119-a\), and \(c+d=283-e=164+a\).

\emph{Constraints on \(a\).} Since \(a<b=37-a\), we get \(a\le18\); also \(a\ge1\).

\emph{Every \(a\) from \(1\) to \(18\) works.} Take \(d=118-a\) and \(c=46+2a\). Then \(c+d=164+a\), and
\[
b=37-a<46+2a=c<118-a=d<119-a=e,
\]
because \(3a>-9\) and \(3a<72\). So all conditions hold.

Hence \(x=e=119-a\) ranges over \(101,\dots,118\), and the required difference is \(118-101=\boxed{17}\).
