Yaozu walks his dog along the paths of a park. From his starting point he walks $20$ meters south, $40$ meters east, $20$ meters south again and $20$ meters west, and then returns to his starting point in a straight line. The park is the region enclosed by his path. What is the area of the park, in square meters?
