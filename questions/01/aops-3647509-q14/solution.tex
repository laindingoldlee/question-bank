For every prime $p$, the cube condition requires $n\,v_p(n)+25v_p(20)\equiv0\pmod3$. A multiple of three cannot work, because the condition at five would read $25\equiv0$. If $n\equiv1\pmod3$, the valuations of two and five in $n$ are respectively two and one modulo three, and other valuations are zero; hence $n=20t^3$, with $t\equiv1\pmod3$. If $n\equiv2\pmod3$, they are one and two modulo three, giving $n=50t^3$, with $t\equiv2\pmod3$. The smallest values are $20,400,1280$, whose sum is $\boxed{1700}$.
