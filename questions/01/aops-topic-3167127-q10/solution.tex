All terms are nonnegative, so interchange summation order: $\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}j\sum_{i=j}^{\infty}2^{-i}=\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}j\,2^{1-j}$. Using $\sum_{j\ge1}jt^j=\dfrac{t}{(1-t)^2}$ for $|t|<1$, at $t=\dfrac{1}{2}$ this equals $2\cdot2=\boxed4$. The displayed generating identity follows by differentiating the convergent geometric series.
