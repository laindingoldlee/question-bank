One year ago an animal shelter had $20$ dogs whose average age was $9$ years. No dogs arrived or left during the year. Today a group of puppies, each exactly one year old, arrives, and now the average age of all the dogs is $4$ years. How many puppies arrived?

$\textbf{(A)}\ 25\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ 40\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ 60\qquad\textbf{(D)}\ 120\qquad\textbf{(E)}\ 160$
