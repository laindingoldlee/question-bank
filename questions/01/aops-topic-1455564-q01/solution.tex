There are $17-2=15$ groups of three adjacent digits. Their sums, from the left, are
\[
\begin{array}{l}
5+8+4=17,\ 8+4+3=15,\ 4+3+9=16,\ 3+9+5=17,\ 9+5+3=17,\\
5+3+8=16,\ 3+8+1=12,\ 8+1+2=11,\ 1+2+7=10,\ 2+7+8=17,\\
7+8+1=16,\ 8+1+6=15,\ 1+6+7=14,\ 6+7+4=17,\ 7+4+9=20.
\end{array}
\]
Five of them equal $17$, so $15-5=\boxed{10}$ groups do not.
