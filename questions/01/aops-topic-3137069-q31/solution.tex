Let $ u_n $ count paths ending at 0 and $ v_n $ count paths ending at either fixed boundary height. Symmetry gives $ u_{n+1}=u_n+2v_n $ and $ v_{n+1}=u_n+v_n $, starting $(u_0,v_0)=(1,0)$. Successive pairs are $(1,1),(3,2),(7,5),(17,12),(41,29),(99,70),(239,169),(577,408),(1393,985),(3363,2378)$. Thus the answer is $\boxed{3363}$.
