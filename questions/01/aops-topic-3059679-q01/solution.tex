The initial base area is $ 2\cdot4=8\text{ m}^2 $, and the depth is $\dfrac{1}{4}$ metre, so water volume is $ 2\text{ m}^3 $. The smallest face has area $ 1\cdot2=2\text{ m}^2 $. Thus the new depth is $ 2\div2=\boxed{1\text{ metre}}$.
