Condition on the specified order of first appearances. The first draw is red and costs one draw. From then until the first nonred, the waiting time is geometric with success probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, hence mean two; conditioning this nonred to be green does not change the waiting time, since its color and waiting duration are independent. Once green has appeared, only blue remains; its waiting time has mean $\dfrac{1}{\dfrac{2}{10}}=5$. The total conditional expectation is $1+2+5=\boxed8$.
