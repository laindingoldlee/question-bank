Label the six positions temporarily. There are $\binom63=20$ colour strings. For rotations, the identity fixes all 20. Rotations through two or four positions each have two cycles of length 3; exactly one cycle must be black, giving 2 fixed strings each. Rotations through one or five positions require a single colour, and rotation through three positions would require an even number of black positions, so none of these fixes a string with three black positions. Burnside\textquotesingle s lemma gives $\dfrac{20+2+2}{6}=\boxed4$. Representatives are BBBWWW, BBWBWW, BBWWBW, and BWBWBW in cyclic order.
\begin{asy}
import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);

\end{asy}
