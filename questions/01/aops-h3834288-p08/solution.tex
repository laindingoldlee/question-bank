Take $A=(0,0),B=(24,0),C=(55,0)$. Then $D=(12,12\sqrt3)$ and $E=(\dfrac{79}{2},\dfrac{31\sqrt3}{2})$. Rotating the vector $E-D$ by $\pm60^\circ$ gives the two equilateral third vertices $(31,0)$ and $(\dfrac{41}{2},\dfrac{55\sqrt3}{2})$. The first is closer to the line, so $AF+BF+CF=31+7+24=\boxed{62}$.
