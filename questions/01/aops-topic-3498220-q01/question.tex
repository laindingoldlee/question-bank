Start with a circle of radius $10$ centered at the origin. For each integer radius $r=9,8,\ldots,1$, draw every circle of radius $r$ internally tangent at a point of the horizontal axis to any circle of radius $r+1$ already drawn. Count each geometrically identical circle only once. How many circles, including the original, are drawn?
