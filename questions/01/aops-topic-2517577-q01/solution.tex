The continued fraction repeats after two levels, so
\[a=1+\dfrac{1}{2+\dfrac1a}=1+\dfrac{a}{2a+1}.\]
(The value of this periodic continued fraction is the limit of its convergents, which exists and is greater than $1$.) Then $(a-1)(2a+1)=a$, i.e. $2a^2-2a-1=0$, and since $a>1$,
\[a=\dfrac{2+\sqrt{12}}{4}=\dfrac{1+\sqrt3}{2},\qquad 2a=1+\sqrt3.\]
Now
\[4a^2=(2a)^2=4+2\sqrt3\implies4a^2-4=2\sqrt3,\]
\[8a^3=(2a)^3=1+3\sqrt3+9+3\sqrt3=10+6\sqrt3\implies8a^3-10=6\sqrt3.\]
So the expression equals
\[\left(6\sqrt3\right)^4+\left(2\sqrt3\right)^2=1296\cdot9+12=11664+12=11676,\]
and the remainder is $\boxed{676}$.
