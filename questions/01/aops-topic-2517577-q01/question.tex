Let
\[a=1+\dfrac{1}{2+\dfrac{1}{1+\dfrac{1}{2+\dfrac{1}{1+\cdots}}}}\]
be the value of the infinite continued fraction in which $2$ and $1$ alternate. Find the remainder when $\left(8a^3-10\right)^4+\left(4a^2-4\right)^2$ is divided by $1000$.
