Move everything to one side and factor:
\[
a^{2015}+b^{2015}-a^{2014}b-ab^{2014}=a^{2014}(a-b)-b^{2014}(a-b)=(a-b)\left(a^{2014}-b^{2014}\right).
\]
For positive $a,b$, the map $t\mapsto t^{2014}$ is increasing, so $a-b$ and $a^{2014}-b^{2014}$ have the same sign (or are both $0$). Their product is therefore $\ge0$, which proves the inequality, with equality exactly when $a=b$. $\blacksquare$
