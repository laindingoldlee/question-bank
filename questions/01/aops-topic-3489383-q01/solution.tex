Let $p_s$ be the probability of a step $s$, and $P(z)=\sum_{s=2}^{12}p_sz^s=(\dfrac{z+z^2+\cdots+z^6}{6})^2$. Since all steps are positive, $u_0=1$ and $u_n=\sum_s p_su_{n-s}$ with negative subscripts zero. The generating function is $U(z)=\dfrac{1}{1-P(z)}$. For $|z|<1$, $|P(z)|<1$. On $|z|=1$, equality $P(z)=1$ requires all $z^s=1$ for positive $p_s$, in particular $z^2=z^3=1$, hence $z=1$. This root is simple, since $P'(1)=E(S)=7$. Thus $U(z)=\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{7}}{1-z}+R(z)$, where the rational function $R$ has all poles strictly outside the unit disk. Its coefficients tend to zero: partial fractions express them as finite sums of polynomials in $n$ times $\alpha^{-n}$ with $|\alpha|>1$ (a polynomial part affects finitely many terms). Consequently $\boxed{\lim_{n\to\infty}u_n=\dfrac{1}{7}}$.
