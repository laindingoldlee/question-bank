The reflection across $2x-y+3=0$ sends the x-coordinate to $x-\dfrac{4(2x-y+3)}{5}$. The original x-coordinate sum is 0, and the three values of $2x-y+3$ are $-2,-3,-7$. Hence the reflected sum is $0-\dfrac{4}{5}(-12)=\dfrac{48}{5}$, giving $\boxed{53}$.
