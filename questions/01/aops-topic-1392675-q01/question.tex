Segments $AB$ and $CD$ intersect at a point $X$ inside both of them. A student claims that always
\[
AX\cdot CX=BX\cdot DX .
\]
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show by an example that the claim is false in general.
\item[(b)] Prove that $AX\cdot CX=BX\cdot DX$ holds if and only if $AD\parallel BC$.
\end{enumerate}
