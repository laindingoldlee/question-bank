You have $150$ Jolly Ranchers.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Each day you give away $2\%$ of the Jolly Ranchers you have at that moment (pieces may be cut, so fractional amounts are allowed). Will you ever run out?
\item[(b)] Each day you instead give away $2\%$ of the original $150$. On which day do you run out?
\end{enumerate}
