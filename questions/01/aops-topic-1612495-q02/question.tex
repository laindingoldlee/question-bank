On a tropical island the inhabitants hold an annual coconut-rolling competition. A coconut's distance in a round is the distance it rolls in $10$ seconds. The official coconut is a perfect sphere of radius $7$ centimetres. It is released rolling (without slipping) in a straight line at $10$ revolutions per second, and it slows down in steps: its speed is constant during each second and drops by $10\pi$ centimetres per second at the end of each second. Find the exact distance the coconut travels in a round.
