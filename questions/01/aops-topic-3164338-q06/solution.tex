For digit sums $s=0,1,\ldots,10$, the hour frequencies are $(1,2,3,3,3,3,2,2,2,2,1)$. The corresponding minute frequencies are $(1,2,3,4,5,6,6,6,6,6,5)$. Pairing equal sums gives $1+4+9+12+15+18+12+12+12+12+5=\boxed{112}$. Leading zeroes do not alter digit sums.
