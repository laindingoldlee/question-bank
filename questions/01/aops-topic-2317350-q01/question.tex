In triangle $ABC$, $D$ is the foot of the perpendicular from $B$ to line $AC$. Points $E$ and $F$ lie on sides $AB$ and $BC$ so that $CE$ bisects $\angle BCA$ and $AF$ bisects $\angle CAB$. The lines $BD$, $CE$ and $AF$ pass through one point. The median from $A$ to side $BC$ meets $BD$ at $H$. Find $\dfrac{BH}{HD}$.
