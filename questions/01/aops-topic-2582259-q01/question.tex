Which of the following situations involves a permutation, that is, an arrangement in which the order matters?
\begin{itemize}
\item[\textbf{(A)}] Arranging the letters of a given word subject to a condition.
\item[\textbf{(B)}] Selecting one song to be played from a list.
\item[\textbf{(C)}] Choosing students to represent the class.
\item[\textbf{(D)}] Choosing $3$ cards from a deck of $52$ cards.
\end{itemize}
