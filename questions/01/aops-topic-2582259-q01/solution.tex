In (A) the letters are put in order, and different orders give different arrangements, so this is a permutation.

In (C) and (D) a group is chosen and the order of choosing does not matter, so these are combinations. In (B) only one item is chosen; there is no arrangement, and the count is just the number of songs.

The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(A)}}$.
