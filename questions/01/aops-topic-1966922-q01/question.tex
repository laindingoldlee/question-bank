Frank's painting is on a large triangular canvas with side lengths $4$ meters, $5$ meters and $7$ meters. He wants a frame whose inner border runs exactly along the sides of the canvas and such that every point of its outer border is at distance exactly $2$ cm from the closest point of the canvas. In square centimeters, what is the area of the frame, to the nearest whole number?
