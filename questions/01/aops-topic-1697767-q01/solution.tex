$f(x^2)=0$ exactly when $x^2$ is a root of $f$, i.e.\ $x^2\in\{-7,-5,5\}$. For real $x$, $x^2\ge0$, so $x^2=-7$ and $x^2=-5$ are impossible, and $x^2=5$ gives $x=\pm\sqrt5$. The real roots of $f(x^2)$ are $\boxed{\sqrt5\text{ and }-\sqrt5}$.
