If the current age is $n$, then $n+1$ is one of 1,8,27,64, the cubes at most 100. Subtracting 2 gives possible squares $-1,6,25,62$. Only 25 is a square, so $n+1=27$ and $\boxed{n=26}$. Indeed 25 is a square and 27 a cube.
