Lines $\ell$ and $m$ are parallel, and a transversal $t$ meets $\ell$ at $P$ and $m$ at $Q$. Let $X$ be a point of $t$ such that $P$ lies between $X$ and $Q$, and let $A$ on $\ell$ and $B$ on $m$ lie on the same side of $t$. You may use two facts: (1) \emph{Playfair's axiom}: through a point not on a line, there is at most one line parallel to that line; (2) if a transversal makes congruent corresponding angles with two lines, then the lines are parallel. Prove the Corresponding Angles Theorem: $\angle XPA\cong\angle PQB$.
