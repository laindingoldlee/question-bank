On the same side of $t$ as $B$, draw the ray $QB'$ with $\angle PQB'=\angle XPA$ (call this angle $\alpha$), and let $m'$ be the line $QB'$. (The dashed line is drawn as if $m'$ were different from $m$, which is what we rule out.)

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair Q=(0,0), P=(2/Tan(60),2);
pair dt=dir(60);
draw((-2.6,2)--(4.6,2),linewidth(0.9)); draw((-2.6,0)--(4.6,0),linewidth(0.9));
draw(Q-1.3*dt--P+1.3*dt,linewidth(0.9));
draw((2.4,2)--(2.6,2),Arrow(5)); draw((2.4,0)--(2.6,0),Arrow(5));
label("$\ell$",(4.6,2),E); label("$m$",(4.6,0),E); label("$t$",P+1.3*dt,N);
dot(P); dot(Q);
dot(P); label("$P$",P,NW);
dot(Q); label("$Q$",Q,SE);
dot(P+0.9*dt); label("$X$",P+0.9*dt,W);
dot((4,2)); label("$A$",(4,2),N);
dot((3,0)); label("$B$",(3,0),S);
draw(arc(P,0.45,0,60),red); label("$\alpha$",P+0.77*dir(30.0),red+fontsize(8pt));
draw(arc(Q,0.45,8,60),blue); label("$\alpha$",Q+0.77*dir(34.0),blue+fontsize(8pt));
draw(Q-2.2*dir(8)--Q+4.4*dir(8),dashed+blue); label("$m'$",Q+4.4*dir(8),E,blue); dot(Q+3*dir(8),blue); label("$B'$",Q+3*dir(8),N,blue);
\end{asy}

The transversal $t$ makes congruent corresponding angles $\angle XPA$ and $\angle PQB'$ with $\ell$ and $m'$, so by fact (2), $m'\parallel\ell$. Now $m$ and $m'$ both pass through $Q$, which is not on $\ell$, and both are parallel to $\ell$. By Playfair's axiom (1), $m'=m$. Since $B$ and $B'$ lie on the same side of $t$, the rays $QB'$ and $QB$ are then the same ray, so
\[\angle PQB=\angle PQB'=\alpha=\angle XPA.\ \blacksquare\]
