\textbf{Exactly those not $\equiv2\pmod4$.} Odd $n=2k+1=(k+1)^2-k^2$. For $n=4k$, $n=(k+1)^2-(k-1)^2$. Conversely, $a^2-b^2=(a-b)(a+b)$ with both factors of the same parity, so the product is odd or divisible by $4$, never $\equiv2\pmod4$. $\blacksquare$
