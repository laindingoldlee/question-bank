Lines $\ell$ and $m$ are parallel, and a transversal $t$ meets $\ell$ at $P$ and $m$ at $Q$. Let $A$ on $\ell$ and $B$ on $m$ lie on opposite sides of $t$. Using the Corresponding Angles Theorem (if two parallel lines are cut by a transversal, corresponding angles are congruent) and the Vertical Angles Theorem, prove the Alternate Interior Angles Theorem: $\angle APQ\cong\angle BQP$.
