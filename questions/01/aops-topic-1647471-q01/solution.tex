The first dream is one of the $3$ bad kinds with probability $\dfrac{3}{19}$. Given this, $18$ kinds remain, $10$ of them nightmares, so the second dream is a nightmare with probability $\dfrac{10}{18}$. Hence
\[
P=\dfrac{3}{19}\cdot\dfrac{10}{18}=\dfrac{30}{342}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{57}}.
\]
(Equivalently: there are $19\cdot18=342$ equally likely ordered pairs of different kinds, and $3\cdot10=30$ of them are (bad, nightmare).)
