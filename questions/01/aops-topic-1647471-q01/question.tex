A person has exactly two dreams in a night. Each dream is one of $19$ equally likely kinds: $3$ kinds of bad dream, $6$ kinds of good dream and $10$ kinds of nightmare. The two dreams of a night are chosen at random without replacement (so they are of different kinds), in order. What is the probability that the first dream is a bad dream and the second is a nightmare?
