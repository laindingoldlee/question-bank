Let Sara be paid $p$ dollars per cake, so Linda is paid $p-11$. Equal hourly earnings means
\[
6(p-11)=4p\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 2p=66\quad\Longrightarrow\quad p=\boxed{33}.
\]
(Check: Linda earns $6\cdot22=132$ and Sara $4\cdot33=132$ dollars per hour.)
