By the rational root theorem, any integer root divides $30$. Trying $x=2$: $8-40+62-30=0$, so $x-2$ is a factor. Dividing,
\[x^3-10x^2+31x-30=(x-2)\left(x^2-8x+15\right)=(x-2)(x-3)(x-5).\]
The roots are $\boxed{2,\ 3,\ 5}$. (Check with Vieta's formulas: $2+3+5=10$, $2\cdot3+3\cdot5+5\cdot2=31$, $2\cdot3\cdot5=30$.)
