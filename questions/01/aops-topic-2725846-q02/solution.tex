The candidates are $2^a\cdot3^2\cdot5^b\cdot7^c$ with $a\in\{2,3,4\}$, $b,c\in\{0,1\}$. The three-digit ones are
\[144=2^4\cdot3^2,\quad180=2^2\cdot3^2\cdot5,\quad252=2^2\cdot3^2\cdot7,\quad360=2^3\cdot3^2\cdot5,\quad504=2^3\cdot3^2\cdot7,\quad720=2^4\cdot3^2\cdot5\]
($36$ and $72$ are too small, the others too large). Their divisor sums are $403$, $546$, $728$, $1170$, $1560$, $2418$, each more than twice the number. So all six are very special: $\boxed{144,180,252,360,504,720}$.
