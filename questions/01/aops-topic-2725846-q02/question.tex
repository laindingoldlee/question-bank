A three-digit number is \emph{very special} if its prime factorisation contains $2$ exactly two, three or four times, $3$ exactly twice, $5$ at most once, $7$ at most once, and no other primes, and the number is abundant (the sum of its divisors exceeds twice the number). Find all very special numbers.
