Let the legs be $x$ and $y$. Then $x^2+y^2=36$ and $\dfrac12xy=9$, so $xy=18$ and
\[(x+y)^2=x^2+y^2+2xy=36+36=72,\qquad x+y=6\sqrt2.\]
(In fact $(x-y)^2=36-36=0$, so $x=y=3\sqrt2$: the triangle is isosceles.)

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=3*sqrt(2);
pair A=(0,0), B=(s,0), C=(0,s);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); draw((0.3,0)--(0.3,0.3)--(0,0.3));
label("$3\sqrt2$",(A+B)/2,S); label("$3\sqrt2$",(A+C)/2,W); label("$6$",(B+C)/2,NE);
\end{asy}
