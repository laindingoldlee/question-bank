All exponents lie in $\{0,1,\ldots,10\}$, because $ 2^{11}>2017 $. Conversely the largest sum using two distinct exponents in that set is $ 2^{10}+2^9=1536<2017 $. Unique binary expansion gives $\binom{11}{2}=\boxed{55}$.
