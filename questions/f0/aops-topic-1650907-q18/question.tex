Together two farmers rented a pasture for $\$140$. The first farmer placed his four oxen in the pasture, and the second farmer placed his $30$ sheep in the pasture. How much should the first farmer pay if one ox eats as much as ten sheep, and the rent is based on the portion of the pasture eaten by each farmer's animals?
