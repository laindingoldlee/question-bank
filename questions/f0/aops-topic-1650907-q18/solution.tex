Measure grass in ``sheep units''. The four oxen eat as much as $40$ sheep, so together the animals eat $40+30=70$ units, of which the first farmer's animals eat $40$. He should pay
\[
\dfrac{40}{70}\cdot140=\boxed{\$80}.
\]
