The triple $(x,y,z)$ is uniformly distributed in the cube $[0,2]^3$, of volume $8$. The triples with $x+y+z<2$ form the tetrahedron with vertices $(0,0,0)$, $(2,0,0)$, $(0,2,0)$, $(0,0,2)$, a pyramid with a right-triangular base of area $\dfrac12\cdot2\cdot2=2$ and height $2$, so its volume is $\dfrac13\cdot2\cdot2=\dfrac43$. (The tetrahedron lies inside the cube, because $x+y+z<2$ with nonnegative coordinates forces each coordinate to be less than $2$.)

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair pr(real x,real y,real z){return (x+0.45*y,z+0.35*y);}
real s=2;
pair O=pr(0,0,0),X=pr(s,0,0),Y=pr(0,s,0),Z=pr(0,0,s);
fill(X--Y--Z--cycle,rgb(0.8,0.88,1));
draw(pr(0,s,0)--pr(s,s,0),dashed); draw(pr(0,s,0)--pr(0,s,s),dashed); draw(O--Y,dashed);
draw(O--X--pr(s,0,s)--Z--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
draw(X--pr(s,s,0)--pr(s,s,s)--pr(s,0,s)); draw(Z--pr(0,s,s)--pr(s,s,s));
draw(X--Y,blue+dashed); draw(Y--Z,blue+dashed); draw(X--Z,blue);
dot(O); dot(X); dot(Y); dot(Z);
label("$O$",O,SW,fontsize(8pt)); label("$(2,0,0)$",X,S,fontsize(8pt)); label("$(0,2,0)$",Y,2*W,fontsize(7pt)); label("$(0,0,2)$",Z,W,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}

The probability is
\[\dfrac{\dfrac43}{8}=\boxed{\dfrac16}.\]
