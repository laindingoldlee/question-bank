Let Sandy's speed be $v$ mph. When they meet, Sandy has driven $6+5=11$ hours and Sara $5$ hours, over the same distance:
\[
11v=5(v+81)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 6v=405\quad\Longrightarrow\quad v=\boxed{67.5\text{ mph}}.
\]
