The regular octagon $ABCDEFGH$ has its centre at $J$. Each of the vertices and the centre are to be associated with one of the digits $1$ through $9$, with each digit used once, in such a way that the sums of the numbers on the lines $AJE$, $BJF$, $CJG$ and $DJH$ are all equal. In how many ways can this be done?

$\textbf{(A) }384\qquad\textbf{(B) }576\qquad\textbf{(C) }1152\qquad\textbf{(D) }1680\qquad\textbf{(E) }3024$

In particular, explain why the digit at $J$ must be $1$, $5$ or $9$.
