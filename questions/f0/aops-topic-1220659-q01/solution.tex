Each interior angle of a regular hexagon is $120^\circ$, so in triangle $EFA$ we have $FE=FA=x$ and $\angle EFA=120^\circ$. By the law of cosines,
\[EA^2=x^2+x^2-2x^2\cos120^\circ=3x^2,\]
so $EA=x\sqrt3$. (Equivalently, the altitude from $F$ splits $EFA$ into two $30^\circ$-$60^\circ$-$90^\circ$ triangles with long leg $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}x$.)

label("$A$",V[0],E);label("$B$",V[1],NE);label("$C$",V[2],NW);label("$D$",V[3],W);label("$E$",V[4],SW);label("$F$",V[5],SE);label("$120^\circ$",(0.3,-0.73),fontsize(7pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
