Even $x$ gives zero, since its 99th power contains at least four factors of two. Odd $x$ has $x^4\equiv1\pmod{16}$, so $x^{99}\equiv x^3$. Cubing permutes the eight odd residues: cubing twice is the identity because $x^9\equiv x$. Therefore the possible remainders are $\boxed{0,1,3,5,7,9,11,13,15}$.
