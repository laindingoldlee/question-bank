There are $\dbinom63=20$ equally likely choices. The only equilateral triangles are those formed by alternate vertices, and there are $2$ of them. The probability is $\dfrac2{20}=\boxed{\dfrac1{10}}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(2.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair[] V;for(int i=0;i<6;++i)V[i]=dir(60*i);
draw(V[0]--V[1]--V[2]--V[3]--V[4]--V[5]--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
draw(V[0]--V[2]--V[4]--cycle,dashed);draw(V[1]--V[3]--V[5]--cycle,dotted);
for(int i=0;i<6;++i)dot(V[i]);
\end{asy}
