Annual compounding gives $(1+\dfrac{x}{100})^3=\dfrac{1331}{1000}=(\dfrac{11}{10})^3$, so $x=10$. Simple interest gives $1000(1+\dfrac{3y}{100})=1331$, hence $y=\dfrac{331}{30}$. Therefore $x+y=\dfrac{631}{30}$, already reduced, and $\boxed{a+b=661}$.
