Let $F(r,h)$ be the probability of success with $r$ untouched pairs and $h\le2$ unmatched held tiles; the bag has $2r+h$ tiles. A draw matches a held tile with probability $\dfrac{h}{(2r+h)}$, or opens a new pair with probability $2\dfrac{r}{(2r+h)}$; the latter loses immediately when $h=2$. Thus
\[
F(r,0)=F(r-1,1),\qquad F(r,2)=\dfrac{1}{r+1}F(r,1).
\]
For $r\ge1$,
\[
F(r,1)=\dfrac{F(r,0)+2rF(r-1,2)}{2r+1}
=\dfrac{3}{2r+1}F(r,0).
\]
Starting with $F(1,0)=1$ gives
\[
F(6,0)=\prod_{r=1}^{5}\dfrac{3}{2r+1}=\dfrac{9}{385}.
\]
Therefore $\boxed{p+q=394}$.
