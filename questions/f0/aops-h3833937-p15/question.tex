There are $2026$ coins on a table, and each coin is labeled with an integer. The sum of the values of these coins is $22286$. A mysterious machine does the following operation:

(a) Pick any two coins. Call the numbers on these coins $a$ and $b$.

(b) Replace the two coins with new coins valued at $\dfrac{a+69b}{2}$ and $\dfrac{a-67b}{2}$.

After the machine performs this operation $2048$ times, what is the sum of the numbers of the $2026$ coins?
