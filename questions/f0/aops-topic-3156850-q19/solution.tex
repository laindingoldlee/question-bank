In laps per minute, $ v_Z-v_Y=(v_Z-v_X)+(v_X-v_Y)=\dfrac{1}{8}+\dfrac{1}{12}=\dfrac{5}{24}$. One relative lap therefore takes $\dfrac{24}{5}$ minutes, or $\boxed{288}$ seconds.
