After $n$ hours he has swum
\[4999.999\left(1+\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{4}+\cdots+\dfrac{1}{2^{n-1}}\right)=4999.999\left(2-\dfrac{1}{2^{n-1}}\right)<2\cdot4999.999=9999.998\]
kilometres. So his total is always less than $9999.998$ km (it approaches $9999.998$ km as $n$ grows), and \textbf{no}, he never reaches $10000$ km.
