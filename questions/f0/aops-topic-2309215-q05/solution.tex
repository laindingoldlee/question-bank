The nonnegative integers with remainder $2$ on division by $3$ are $2,5,8,11,\dots$, that is, $3k+2$ for $k=0,1,2,\dots$. The total is smallest when the $14$ different numbers are the $14$ smallest of these, $2,5,\dots,41$ ($k=0,\dots,13$). Their sum is
\[\sum_{k=0}^{13}(3k+2)=3\cdot\dfrac{13\cdot14}{2}+14\cdot2=273+28=\boxed{301}.\]
