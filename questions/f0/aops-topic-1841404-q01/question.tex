Find the pair $(a,b)$ of real numbers with $a+b=13$ and $\sqrt{25-a^2}=\sqrt{144-b^2}$. A student writes:
\[
25-a^2=144-b^2,\quad a^2-b^2=-119,\quad(a+b)(a-b)=-119,\quad13(a-b)=-119,\quad a-b=-\dfrac{119}{13},
\]
``so $(a,b)=\left(-\dfrac{40}{13},\dfrac{79}{13}\right)$.'' Find the error and give a complete solution.
