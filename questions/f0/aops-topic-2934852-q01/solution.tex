A score is $6c+1.5b$ with $c+b\le25$. Two pairs give the same score iff $6c+1.5b=6c'+1.5b'$, i.e. $b'-b=4(c-c')$. So $(c,b)$ and $(c-1,b+4)$ tie whenever $c\ge1$ and $c+b+3\le25$, i.e. $c+b\le22$, and $(c+1,b-4)$ ties when $b\ge4$. Hence $(c,b)$ is unique iff ($c=0$ or $c+b\ge23$) and $b\le3$. This gives $(0,0),(0,1),(0,2),(0,3)$ and the pairs with $b\le3$, $23\le c+b\le25$, of which there are $4+4+4=12$. Total $\boxed{16}$.
