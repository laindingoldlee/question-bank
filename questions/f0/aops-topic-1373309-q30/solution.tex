The point satisfies $(x-5)^2+(y-15)^2=130$ and $x=2y$. Substituting,
\[
(2y-5)^2+(y-15)^2=130\iff5y^2-50y+250=130\iff y^2-10y+24=0\iff(y-4)(y-6)=0 .
\]
So $Q=(8,4)$ or $Q=(12,6)$, the two points where the line $x=2y$ meets the circle:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;

label("$x$",(20,0),E);label("$y$",(0,28),N);
pair C=(5,15); real r=sqrt(130);
draw(circle(C,r),blue+linewidth(0.9));
draw((-2,-1)--(19,9.5),red);
pair P1=(8,4),P2=(12,6);
dot(C);dot(P1,red);dot(P2,red);
label("$(5,15)$",C,N);label("$(8,4)$",(9.6,1.7));label("$(12,6)$",P2,SE);
label("$x=2y$",(19,9.5),N,red);

\end{asy}
