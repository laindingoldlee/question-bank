Fix a prime and let its exponents in $a,b,c$ be $\alpha\le\beta\le\gamma$. The exponent in the numerator is the maximum of the three pairwise minima, namely $\beta$. The exponent in the denominator is the minimum of the pairwise maxima, also $\beta$. This holds for every prime, so the quotient is always exactly $1$.

All $99^3$ triples qualify. Since $99^3=3^6\cdot11^3$, the answer is $\boxed{3\cdot6+11\cdot3=51}$.
