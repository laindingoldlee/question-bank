Let $ABC$ be a triangle and let $t$ be the tangent to its circumcircle at $A$. Points $D$ on side $AB$ and $E$ on side $AC$ are chosen so that $DE$ is parallel to $t$. If $AD=6$, $AE=5$ and $CE=7$, find $BD$.
