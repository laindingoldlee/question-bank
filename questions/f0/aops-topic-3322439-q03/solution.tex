The center of an equilateral triangle divides each median in the ratio $2:1$, with the longer part toward the vertex. Hence its circumradius is twice its inradius: $R=2r$. The circle area ratio is $\dfrac{r^2}{R^2}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{4}}$.
\begin{asy}
import geometry;
usepackage("amsmath");
size(270);
pair A=2dir(90),B=2dir(210),C=2dir(330);draw(circle((0,0),2));draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(circle((0,0),1),blue);draw(A--(0,-1),dashed);label("$R$",(0,1),E);label("$r$",(0,-.5),E);
\end{asy}
