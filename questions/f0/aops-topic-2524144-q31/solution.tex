There are $\left\lfloor\dfrac{100}3\right\rfloor=33$ multiples of $3$, $\left\lfloor\dfrac{100}7\right\rfloor=14$ multiples of $7$, and $\left\lfloor\dfrac{100}{21}\right\rfloor=4$ multiples of both (that is, of $21$). Numbers divisible by exactly one of $3$ and $7$ number $(33-4)+(14-4)=\boxed{39}$.
