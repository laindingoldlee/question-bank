A permutation with $C$ cycles needs exactly $400-C$ swaps: each length-$k$ cycle can be fixed with $k-1$ swaps, while a swap increases the cycle count by at most 1. The expected number of cycles of length $k$ is $\binom{400}{k}\dfrac{(k-1)!(400-k)!}{400!}=\dfrac{1}{k}$, so $x=400-H_{400}$. We have $H_{400}>0$ and $H_{400}<H_{512}\le1+9=10$, grouping denominators into intervals $(2^{j-1},2^j]$, each contributing at most 1. Thus $390<x<400$, so $19.5<\dfrac{x}{20}<20$. Therefore $\boxed{19}$.
