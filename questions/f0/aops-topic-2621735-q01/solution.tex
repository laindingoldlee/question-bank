The number is $s^3$, where $s$ is its digit sum, and $100\le s^3\le999$ means $5\le s\le9$. The cubes are $125$, $216$, $343$, $512$ and $729$, with digit sums $8$, $9$, $10$, $8$, $18$. Only $512$ has digit sum equal to its cube root ($5+1+2=8$). Its last digit $2$ is indeed $\sqrt[3]8$. The number is $\boxed{512}$.
