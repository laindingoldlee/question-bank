In math team, every pair of members are either bros or opps. If the previous person is a bro, then a math team member will make a statement of the same truth value, and if the previous person is an opp, then a math team member will make a statement of the opposite truth value. The five captains of math team have the following conversation.

\begin{itemize}

\item William: I'm Muztaba's opp.

\item Ella: Among me, Muztaba, and Jacob there are an even number of pairs of opps.

\item Jacob: Peter and William are opps.

\item Muztaba: Jacob has no opps.

\item Ella: Jacob has three opps.

\item Peter: I'm Jacob's opp.

\item William: Peter has no opps.

\item Peter: Jacob and Ella are not opps.

\item Muztaba: I have more opps than Jacob.

\end{itemize}

Let $W$, $E$, $J$, $M$, and $P$ be the number of oppos of William, Ella, Jacob, Muztaba, and Peter, repsectively. Find $10000W+1000E+100J+10M+P.$
