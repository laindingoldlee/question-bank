Add one stick to the first group and two to the last. The result is $\boxed{\mathrm{III}+\mathrm{I}=\mathrm{IIII}}$, representing $3+1=4$. Exactly three sticks have been added. These are tally counts, so the group of four is written with four sticks.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
int[] n={3,1,4};real[] x={0,2,4};for(int j=0;j<3;++j)for(int i=0;i<n[j];++i)draw((x[j]+.25i,0)--(x[j]+.25i,1),((j==0&&i==2)||(j==2&&i>=2))?red+linewidth(2):black+linewidth(2));label("$+$",(1.3,.5));label("$=$",(3.3,.5));label("Red sticks are the three additions",(2.3,-.5));
\end{asy}
