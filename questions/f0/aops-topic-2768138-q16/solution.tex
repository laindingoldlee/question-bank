Choose the three students who get wrong tests: $\dbinom{20}3=1140$ ways. Their three tests must be permuted with no one getting their own test: $2$ derangements of $3$ objects. The total is $1140\cdot2=2280$. Answer $\boxed{\textbf{(E)}\ 2280}$.
