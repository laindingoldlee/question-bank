The integer readings are squares between $10^2=100$ and $35^2=1225$, filtered so the hour is 1 through 12 and the last two digits are below 60. Compare each valid reading with the reading three hours ahead. The only pairs in a twelve-hour cycle are $1{:}00\to4{:}00$ (100 and 400), and $11{:}56\to2{:}56$ (1156 and 256). Each occurs twice in 24 hours, giving $\boxed{4}$ minutes. Equivalently factor square differences 300 for nonwrapping times and 900 for wrapping times, discarding invalid minute fields.
