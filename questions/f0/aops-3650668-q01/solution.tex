A corner touches three other squares, an edge-middle touches five, and the centre touches eight. Since all other squares are mines, exactly the four edge-middle positions show five. The probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{4}{9}}$.
\begin{asy}
import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
for(int r=0;r<3;++r)for(int c=0;c<3;++c){path p=(c,r)--(c+1,r)--(c+1,r+1)--(c,r+1)--cycle;if((r==1)!=(c==1))fill(p,lightblue);draw(p);}label("four eligible positions",(1.5,-.5));
\end{asy}
