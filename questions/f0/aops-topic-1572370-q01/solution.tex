Let $AC=a$, so $BC=3a$ and $CD=DE=EB=a$. Let $G$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $F$ to $BC$.

\textbf{Where $G$ is.} Since $FG\parallel AC$ (both are perpendicular to $BC$) and $F$ is the midpoint of $AB$, the line $FG$ is a midline of triangle $ABC$: $G$ is the midpoint of $BC$ and $FG=\dfrac{1}{2}AC=\dfrac {a}{2}$. So $CG=\dfrac{3a}{2}$, which means $G$ is also the midpoint of $DE$, with $DG=GE=\dfrac {a}{2}$.

\textbf{Two congruent isosceles right triangles.} In right triangles $FGD$ and $FGE$, the legs are $FG=\dfrac {a}{2}$ and $DG=GE=\dfrac {a}{2}$. So both are isosceles right triangles, congruent to each other. Hence
\[
FD=FE\qquad\text{and}\qquad\angle DFG=\angle GFE=45^\circ,
\]
so $\angle DFE=90^\circ$. Therefore triangle $DFE$ is isosceles and right-angled at $F$. $\blacksquare$

(In coordinates with $C=(0,0)$, $A=(0,1)$, $B=(3,0)$: $F=\left(\dfrac{3}{2},\dfrac{1}{2}\right)$, $D=(1,0)$, $E=(2,0)$, and $\overrightarrow{FD}=\left(-\dfrac{1}{2},-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)$, $\overrightarrow{FE}=\left(\dfrac{1}{2},-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)$ have equal lengths and dot product $0$.)
