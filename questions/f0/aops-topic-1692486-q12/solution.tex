By inclusion--exclusion, the number of students in at least one competition is
\[
14+13+17-5-6-7+4=\boxed{30}.
\]
(Region by region: $4$ in all three; $1$, $2$, $3$ in exactly band and chorus, band and orchestra, chorus and orchestra; $14-4-1-2=7$ band only, $13-4-1-3=5$ chorus only, $17-4-2-3=8$ orchestra only; $4+1+2+3+7+5+8=30$.)
