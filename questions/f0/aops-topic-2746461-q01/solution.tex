Let $O$ be the centre, $E$ the point on the equator and $S$ the South Pole. The radii $OE$ and $OS$ are perpendicular (the equator is $90^\circ$ of latitude from the pole), so triangle $EOS$ is a right isosceles triangle with legs $4000$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0),E=(1,0),S=(0,-1);
draw(unitcircle);draw((-1,0)--(1,0),dashed);draw(O--S,dashed);draw(E--S,linewidth(1));
draw((0,-0.1)--(0.1,-0.1)--(0.1,0));
dot(O);dot(E);dot(S);
label("$O$",O,NW);label("$E$",E,E);label("$S$",S,S);label("$4000$",(0.5,0),N,fontsize(8pt));label("$4000$",(0,-0.5),W,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}

Hence $ES=4000\sqrt2$ miles: $\boxed{4000\sqrt2}$.
