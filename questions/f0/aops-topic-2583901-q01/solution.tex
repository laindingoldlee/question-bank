The expected loss is $P(\text{even})-P(\text{odd})$. With $p=\dfrac35$ and $q=\dfrac25$,
\[P(\text{even})-P(\text{odd})=\sum_{k=0}^{10}(-1)^k\dbinom{10}{k}p^kq^{10-k}=(q-p)^{10}=\left(-\dfrac15\right)^{10}=\dfrac{1}{5^{10}}\]
by the binomial theorem. Since $\dfrac{1}{5^{10}}=\dfrac{2^{10}}{10^{10}}=1024\cdot10^{-10}$ and $1024$ is not a multiple of $10$, we get $a=\boxed{1024}$ (and $b=-10$).
