A gambler plays a game in which $10$ coins are flipped; he wins a dollar if the number of heads is odd and loses a dollar if it is even. With fair coins his expected loss per round is exactly $0$. However, the house has rigged the coins: each comes up heads with probability $\dfrac35$ and tails with probability $\dfrac25$, independently. The gambler's new expected loss per round, in dollars, can be written as $a\cdot10^b$, where $a$ and $b$ are integers and $a$ is not a multiple of $10$. Find $a$.
