Using $\cos(x-\dfrac{\pi}{3})=\dfrac{1}{2}\cos x+\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\sin x$, the expression is
\[
6\bigl(\cos6x-\cos(x-\dfrac{\pi}{3})\bigr)\le12.
\]
Equality holds at $x=4\dfrac{\pi}{3}$, where $\cos6x=1$ and $\cos(x-\dfrac{\pi}{3})=-1$. Thus $L=12$, and $\boxed{(L-7)^4=625}$.
