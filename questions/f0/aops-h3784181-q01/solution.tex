The sample size is $100\cdot40=4000$. The percentage is $\dfrac{1}{4000}\cdot100\%=\boxed{0.025\%}$. At the same proportion, the population count would be $80000\cdot\dfrac{1}{4000}=\boxed{20}$. This is a conditional arithmetic calculation; the sample alone does not establish that the population proportion is the same.
