The reciprocal of $-2$ is $-\dfrac{1}{2}$, and $-2<-\dfrac{1}{2}$. The values $-1$ and $1$ equal their reciprocals, $0$ has no reciprocal, and $2>\dfrac{1}{2}$. Thus $\boxed{\text{(A)}\ -2}$.
