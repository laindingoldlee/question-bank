The first statement has the form ``if $p$ then $q$'' with $p$ = ``you want to buy the school lunch today'' and $q$ = ``you will need \$$2.50$''. The second statement tells us only that you have \$$2.50$, which is not the hypothesis $p$.
\begin{itemize}
\item (A) would need the converse ``if $q$ then $p$'': people bring \$$2.50$ for many reasons. It does not follow.
\item (B) does not follow either: nothing says that wanting lunch or having the money means you will buy it.
\item (C) talks about every day, but the statements are only about today.
\end{itemize}
So the answer is $\boxed{\text{(D)}}$.
