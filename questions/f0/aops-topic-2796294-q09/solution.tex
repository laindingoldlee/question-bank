Add triangle $ABX$ to each: $[APX]+[ABX]=[ABP]$ and $[BQX]+[ABX]=[ABQ]$. Hence the difference is $[ABP]-[ABQ]$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair C=(0,0),A=(0,15),B=(20,0),P=(0,8),Q=(13,0);pair X=extension(A,Q,B,P);
fill(A--P--X--cycle,gray(0.85));fill(B--Q--X--cycle,gray(0.6));
draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--Q);draw(B--P);
draw(C+(0.8,0)--C+(0.8,0.8)--C+(0,0.8));
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,SW);label("$P$",P,W);label("$Q$",Q,S);label("$X$",X,N);
label("$7$",(0,11.5),W);label("$7$",(16.5,0),S);
\end{asy}

Triangle $ABP$ has base $AP=7$ on line $AC$ and height $BC=20$: area $70$. Triangle $ABQ$ has base $BQ=7$ on line $BC$ and height $AC=15$: area $\dfrac{105}{2}$. The difference is $70-\dfrac{105}2=\boxed{\dfrac{35}{2}}$.
