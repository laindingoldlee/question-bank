In space, line $l_1$ is $\{(x,y,z): x=z=4\}$ and line $l_2$ is $\{(x,y,z): x=z=9\}$. Let $P_1$ be a point on $l_1$, $P_2$ a point on the plane $z=0$, and $P_3$ a point on $l_2$. Find the minimum possible value of $P_1P_2+P_2P_3$.
