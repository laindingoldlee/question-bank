Positive integers $a_0,a_1,\ldots,a_{200}$ are written in a row, with $a_0=a_{200}=19999$. Each of the other numbers is less than the average of its two neighbours, and all the differences
\[\dfrac{a_{i-1}+a_{i+1}}{2}-a_i\qquad(1\le i\le199)\]
are equal to the same integer. Find $a_0+a_1+\cdots+a_{200}$.
