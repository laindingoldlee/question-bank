Put $A=(0,0),B=(12,0),D=(0,12),C=(12,12)$. Then $F=(-8,12)$ and $E=(e,12)$ with $e>0$. Powers of $E$ in the two circles give $EA\cdot EP=ED\cdot EF=e(e+8)$ and $EA\cdot EP=EB^2=(e-12)^2+144$. Therefore $e(e+8)=(e-12)^2+144$, so $32e=288$ and $e=9$. Thus $EA=15$ and $EP=\dfrac{EB^2}{EA}=\dfrac{153}{15}=\dfrac{51}{5}$. Since $P$ lies between $A,E$, $AP=15-\dfrac{51}{5}=\dfrac{24}{5}$. The requested sum is $\boxed{29}$.
