A muffin tray holds $9$ muffins: $3$ banana nut, $3$ blueberry and $3$ chocolate chip. People passing by take the muffins one at a time, each time choosing uniformly at random among the muffins that are left, until all are eaten. What is the probability that at least one muffin of each flavour has been taken before all the muffins of any single flavour have been taken?
