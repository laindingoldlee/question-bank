There are $n\ge1$ lines in a plane. Find, with proof, the maximum possible number of
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] regions (bounded and unbounded) into which the lines divide the plane;
\item[(ii)] bounded regions into which the lines divide the plane.
\end{enumerate}
