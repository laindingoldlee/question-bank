Let $ax^2+bx+c=a(x-r)(x-s)$, so $b=-a(r+s)$ and $c=ars$. Then
\[
x^2+bx+ac=x^2-a(r+s)x+a^2rs=(x-ar)(x-as),
\]
so $u=ar$ and $v=as$, and $a\left(x-\dfrac {u}{a}\right)\left(x-\dfrac {v}{a}\right)=a(x-r)(x-s)=ax^2+bx+c$. $\blacksquare$

Example: $x^2-5x+4=(x-4)(x-1)$, so $2x^2-5x+2=2(x-2)\left(x-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)=\boxed{(x-2)(2x-1)}$.
