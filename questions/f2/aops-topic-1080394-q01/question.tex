A trick for factoring $ax^2+bx+c$ (``slip, slide and divide''): factor $x^2+bx+ac$ as $(x-u)(x-v)$; then $ax^2+bx+c=a\left(x-\dfrac {u}{a}\right)\left(x-\dfrac {v}{a}\right)$. Prove that the trick works, and use it to factor $2x^2-5x+2$.
