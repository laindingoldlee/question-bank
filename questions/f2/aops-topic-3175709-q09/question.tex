Right triangle $ABC$ has right angle $B$ and $AB<BC$. Rectangle $ADEC$ has opposite sides $AC,DE$, with $B$ on $DE$. Let $M=DC\cap AB$ and $N=AE\cap BC$. Given $CN=6$ and $BN=4$, write $MN^2=\dfrac{m}{n}$ in lowest terms and find $m+n$.
