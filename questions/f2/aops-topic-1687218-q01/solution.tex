Each side is $\dfrac{40}{4}=10$ cm. The diagonals of a rhombus bisect each other at right angles, so if the diagonals are $6k$ and $8k$, the half-diagonals $3k$ and $4k$ are the legs of a right triangle with hypotenuse a side:
\[
(3k)^2+(4k)^2=10^2\ \Longrightarrow\ 25k^2=100\ \Longrightarrow\ k=2 .
\]
So the diagonals are $\boxed{12\text{ cm and }16\text{ cm}}$.

draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(A--C,dashed);draw(B--D,dashed);
real q=0.6; draw((q,0)--(q,q)--(0,q),red);
label("$A$",A,W);label("$B$",B,N);label("$C$",C,E);label("$D$",D,S);label("$O$",O,dir(225));
label("$4k$",(A+O)/2,S);label("$3k$",(O+B)/2,W);label("$10$",(A+B)/2,NW);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
