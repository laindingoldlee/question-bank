Two segments $P_1Q_1$ and $P_2Q_2$ (with $P$'s on the first side, $Q$'s on the second) cross inside the angle exactly when the order of $P_1,P_2$ along the first side is opposite to the order of $Q_1,Q_2$ along the second side. So every choice of two of the five points and two of the six gives exactly one crossing (pair them ``crosswise''). The number of intersection points is
\[\dbinom52\dbinom62=10\cdot15=\boxed{150}.\]
