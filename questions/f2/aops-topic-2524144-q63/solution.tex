The five-digit base-$7$ numbers are the integers from $7^4=2401$ to $7^5-1=16806$. The number of multiples of $3$ among them is
\[\left\lfloor\dfrac{16806}3\right\rfloor-\left\lfloor\dfrac{2400}3\right\rfloor=5602-800=\boxed{4802}.\]
