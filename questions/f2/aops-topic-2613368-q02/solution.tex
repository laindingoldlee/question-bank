Write the numbers as $\dfrac ar$, $a$, $ar$ with $a>0$ and $r>0$ (all terms are positive), $r\ne1$ (they are distinct). Their product is $a^3=2197=13^3$, so $a=13$. Since $r\ne1$, the terms are strictly increasing or strictly decreasing, so the median is the middle term $a$: $\boxed{\textbf{(C) }13}$.
