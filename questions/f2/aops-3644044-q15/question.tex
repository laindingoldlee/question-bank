There is a unique set of digits $\{a, b, c\}$ between $0$ and $9$ such that \[0.a\overline{bc} + 0.\overline{cb} = \dfrac{39}{55.}\]Find $100a+10b+c$, the number formed when $a$, $b$, and $c$ are linked together into one three-digit value.



Note: The notation $0.\overline{xyz}$ denotes the repeating decimal $0.xyzxyzxyz...$, where $x$, $y$, and $z$ are digits.
