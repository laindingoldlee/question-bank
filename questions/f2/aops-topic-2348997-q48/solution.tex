$2021^{15}=43^{15}\cdot47^{15}$, and its divisors are $43^i47^j$ with $0\le i,j\le15$.
\begin{itemize}
\item Cubes: $i,j\in\{0,3,6,9,12,15\}$, giving $36$.
\item Fourth powers: $i,j\in\{0,4,8,12\}$, giving $16$.
\item Both (twelfth powers): $i,j\in\{0,12\}$, giving $4$.
\end{itemize}
By inclusion-exclusion the answer is $36+16-4=\boxed{48}$.
