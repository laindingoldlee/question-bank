``$x\%$ of $x$'' means $\dfrac{x}{100}\cdot x$. So $x=\dfrac{x^2}{100}$, i.e. $x^2-100x=x(x-100)=0$, giving $\boxed{x=0\text{ or }x=100}$. (Indeed $100\%$ of $100$ is $100$.)
