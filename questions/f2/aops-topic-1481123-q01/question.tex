Describe and sketch the graph of $y=ax^{b}$ for $x>0$ in each case:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $a>0$ and $b<0$;
\item[(b)] $a<0$ and $b<0$;
\item[(c)] $a>0$ and $b>0$;
\item[(d)] $a<0$ and $b>0$.
\end{enumerate}
For each case say whether the function is increasing or decreasing, which quadrant the graph lies in, and what happens as $x\to0^{+}$ and as $x\to\infty$.
