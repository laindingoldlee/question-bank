Take the cross-section through the cone axis and the generator of contact. It is an isosceles triangle of height 12, base 10, and slant sides 13. The plane's normal lies in this cross-section. The farthest point is the opposite base-rim point, whose distance from the contact side is the altitude to a side 13. By the area formula this is $\dfrac{10\cdot12}{13}=\boxed{\dfrac{120}{13}}$.
