There are nine qualifying three-digit numbers, 111 through 999. Among 1000 through 1999, a repeated digit other than one must fill the last three positions; this gives nine numbers. For digit one, choose exactly two of the last three places as ones and one of nine other digits for the remaining place, giving 27, plus 1111. Thus this interval contributes $9+27+1=37$. Finally 2000 and 2022 qualify in 2000 through 2025, and no others do. The total is $9+37+2=\boxed{48}$.
