Let $T$ be the pedestrian\textquotesingle s total time. At the cyclist\textquotesingle s arrival, the pedestrian has walked $80\%$ of the route, so the cyclist\textquotesingle s total riding time is $0.8T-40$. At the midpoint meeting, elapsed time is both $\dfrac{T}{2}$ and $40+\dfrac{0.8T-40}{2}$. Hence $0.5T=0.4T+20$, giving $\boxed{T=200\text{ minutes}}$.
