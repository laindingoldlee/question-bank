Since $x+y+1>1$, its square is greater than $x+y+1$, which in turn is greater than both $x$ and $y$. Thus $\boxed{\text{E}}$ always works.

The others fail: for A take $(x,y)=\left(2,\dfrac{1}{10}\right)$, giving $\dfrac{6}{5}<2$; for B and D take $x=y=\dfrac{1}{10}$, giving $\dfrac{1}{25}$ and $\dfrac{1}{50}$; for C take $x=\dfrac{1}{10},y=\dfrac{1}{1000}$, giving $\dfrac{11}{1000}<x$.
