Let $AB=BC=d$ and let the speeds be $a,b,c$ (per hour). Bogdan gains $d$ on Cosmin in $\dfrac12$ hour: $b-c=2d$. Alex gains $d$ on Bogdan in $\dfrac34$ hour: $a-b=\dfrac43d$. So $a-c=\dfrac{10}3d$, and Alex gains the $2d$ separating him from Cosmin in $2d\cdot\dfrac{3}{10d}=\dfrac35$ hour, i.e. $36$ minutes. Alex overtakes Cosmin at $\boxed{10\!:\!36}$.
