Santa distributes $20$ identical candy canes to five children: Aidan, Bill, Cecilia, Donald and Emma. Donald and Emma each receive at most $1$ candy cane (possibly $0$), and Aidan, Bill and Cecilia each receive at least $3$ candy canes. All $20$ candy canes are given out. In how many ways can Santa distribute them?
