A land has $2016$ cities, with a nonstop flight between every pair of cities. The price of a flight is the same in both directions, and different pairs of cities have different prices. Prove that one can plan a route of $2015$ consecutive flights in which each flight is cheaper than the previous one (visiting a city more than once is allowed).
