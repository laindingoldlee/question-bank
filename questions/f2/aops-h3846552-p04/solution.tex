Heron gives area $\sqrt{21\cdot8\cdot7\cdot6}=84$, so the altitude to $BC$ is twelve and the inradius is four. The other parallel tangent lies eight above $BC$, leaving altitude four in triangle $ADE$. Similarity gives $\dfrac{DE}{14}=\dfrac{4}{12}$, hence $\boxed{DE=\dfrac{14}{3}}$.

\end{asy}
