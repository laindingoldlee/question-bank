A plate is determined by how many Kavuns ($0$, $1$ or $2$: $3$ choices) and how many Laddus ($0$ to $3$: $4$ choices) it holds. That gives $3\cdot4=12$ plates, one of which is empty. So there are $12-1=\boxed{11}$ nonempty plates.
