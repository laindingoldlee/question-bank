$222\equiv2\pmod{22}$, so we need $2^{222}\bmod22$. Modulo $2$ it is $0$. Modulo $11$: $2^{10}\equiv1$ by Fermat's little theorem, and $222\equiv2\pmod{10}$, so $2^{222}\equiv2^2=4$. The number that is even and $\equiv4\pmod{11}$, between $0$ and $21$, is $4$. The remainder is $\boxed{4}$.
