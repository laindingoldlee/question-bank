A piano is worth \$1{,}000{,}000. A violin is worth $\dfrac45$ of the piano, and a second violin is worth $\dfrac34$ of the first violin. A customer buys the second violin during a holiday sale of $30\%$ off all string instruments, and then a $20\%$ tax is added to the sale price. How much does the customer pay?
