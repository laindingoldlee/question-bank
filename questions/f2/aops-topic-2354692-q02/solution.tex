The first violin is worth $\dfrac45\cdot1000000=800000$ dollars and the second $\dfrac34\cdot800000=600000$ dollars. After $30\%$ off, the price is $0.7\cdot600000=420000$, and with $20\%$ tax the customer pays $1.2\cdot420000=\boxed{\$504{,}000}$. (Since $0.7\cdot1.2=1.2\cdot0.7$, the result is the same if the tax is computed first.)
