The five angles sum to $540^\circ$, so their mean is $108^\circ$. In increasing order they are $108-2d,108-d,108,108+d,108+2d$, where the common difference $d$ is a nonnegative integer. Convexity gives $108+2d<180$, so $d\le35$. At $d=35$ the angles are $38,73,108,143,178$ degrees, all strictly between $0$ and $180$ and summing to $540$; such angles form a convex pentagon. Hence the maximum is $\boxed{178^\circ}$.
