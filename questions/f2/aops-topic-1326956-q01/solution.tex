Treat points as vectors. The midpoint of $BC$ is $M=\dfrac{B+C}{2}$. The centroid $G$ lies on the median $AM$ with $AG:GM=2:1$, so
\[
G=A+\dfrac{2}{3}(M-A)=\dfrac{1}{3}A+\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot\dfrac{B+C}{2}=\dfrac{A+B+C}{3}.
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,3),B=(-2,0),C=(4,0); pair M=(B+C)/2, G=(A+B+C)/3;
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); draw(A--M,red+dashed); dot(M); dot(G,blue);
label("$A(x_1,y_1)$",A,N); label("$B(x_2,y_2)$",B,SW); label("$C(x_3,y_3)$",C,SE); label("$M$",M,S); label("$G$",G,E,blue);
label("$2$",(A+G)/2,W,fontsize(8pt)); label("$1$",(G+M)/2,W,fontsize(8pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Taking coordinates gives $\left(\dfrac{x_1+x_2+x_3}{3},\dfrac{y_1+y_2+y_3}{3}\right)$. (The same point is obtained from every median, by symmetry of the formula, which also shows the three medians are concurrent.) $\blacksquare$
