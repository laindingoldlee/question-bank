Let $a$ of the eleven alternate-day students eat on the first day; the other $11-a$ eat on the second day, and $a$ again on the third. Among the seventeen every-third-day students, let $u,v,w$ eat on these three days. Then $a+u=17$, $11-a+v=4$, and $u+v+w=17$, giving $v=a-7,w=7$. Nonnegativity forces $7\le a\le11$. The third day consumes $9+a+7=16+a$, between $23$ and $27$. The three-day total is therefore between $62$ and $66$, never a multiple of $12$. Thus the claimed whole-box delivery is impossible.
