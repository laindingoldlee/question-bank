LABELLER has three L's, two E's, and A, B, R. First arrange the five non-L letters E, E, A, B, R ($\dfrac{5!}{2}=60$ ways), then insert the three L's into distinct gaps among the $6$ gaps (ends included), so no two L's touch.
\begin{itemize}
\item E's not adjacent: $60-4!=36$ arrangements; choose any $3$ of the $6$ gaps: $36\cdot\dbinom63=720$.
\item E's adjacent: $4!=24$ arrangements; one L must go between the two E's, and the other two into distinct gaps among the remaining $5$: $24\cdot\dbinom52=240$.
\end{itemize}
Total: $720+240=\boxed{960}$.
