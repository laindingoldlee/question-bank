Parallel sunlight gives similar right triangles. If the tree height is $h$, its shadow is $h-14$, so $\dfrac{h}{h-14}=\dfrac{15}{9}$. Thus $9h=15h-210$ and $\boxed{h=35\text{ m}}$. Its shadow is $21$ meters, consistent with the ratio.

\end{asy}
