An integer with fifteen divisors has form $p^{14}$ or $p^4q^2$ for distinct primes. The product of three die results is at most $216$ and has only prime factors $2,3,5$. The only eligible number is $2^4 3^2=144$; all other such prime-power patterns exceed $216$. Among die faces, achieving two factors of $3$ and four factors of $2$ forces results $6,6,4$. Their sum is $\boxed{16}$.
