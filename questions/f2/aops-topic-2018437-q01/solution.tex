In a unimodal permutation the peak $a_k$ is the largest number, $8$. Each of $1,\ldots,7$ lies either to the left of $8$ or to the right, and once this choice is made the order is forced: the left part increasing, the right part decreasing. So unimodal-type permutations (allowing $k=1$ or $k=n$) correspond to subsets of $\{1,\ldots,7\}$ placed on the left: $2^7=128$ of them.

The condition $1<k<n$ excludes the empty left part ($k=1$) and the empty right part ($k=n$). The answer is $128-2=\boxed{126}$.
