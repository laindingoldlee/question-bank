A sequence is \emph{unimodal} if $a_1<a_2<\cdots<a_{k-1}<a_k>a_{k+1}>\cdots>a_{n-1}>a_n$ for some value of $k$ such that $1<k<n$. How many permutations of the numbers $1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8$ form a unimodal sequence?
