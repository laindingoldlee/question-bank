Modulo $60$, the numbers $\equiv10\pmod{12}$ are $10,22,34,46,58$, and only $46\equiv1\pmod5$. So the integer is $\equiv46\pmod{60}$, hence $\equiv\boxed{16}\pmod{30}$.
