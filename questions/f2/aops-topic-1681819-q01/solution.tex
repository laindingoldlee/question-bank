Let $h=AD$. Since $\tan A>0$, angle $A$ is acute.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(-3,0),D=(0,0),C=(17,0),A=(0,11);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));draw(A--D,dashed);
real r=0.8;draw(D+(r,0)--D+(r,r)--D+(0,r),linewidth(0.6));
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,S);
label("$3$",(B+D)/2,S);label("$17$",(D+C)/2,S);label("$h$",(A+D)/2,E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

If $D$ lies between $B$ and $C$, then $\angle CAB=\angle BAD+\angle DAC$ with $\tan\angle BAD=\dfrac{3}{h}$ and $\tan\angle DAC=\dfrac{17}{h}$, so
\[
\dfrac{22}{7}=\dfrac{\dfrac{3}{h}+\dfrac{17}{h}}{1-\dfrac{51}{h^2}}=\dfrac{20h}{h^2-51}
\ \Longrightarrow\ 22h^2-140h-1122=0\ \Longrightarrow\ 11h^2-70h-561=0 .
\]
This factors as $(h-11)(11h+51)=0$, so $h=11$. (If $D$ were outside segment $BC$, we would have $BC=14$ and $\tan A=\dfrac{\dfrac{17}{h}-\dfrac{3}{h}}{1+\dfrac{51}{h^2}}=\dfrac{14h}{h^2+51}$; then $22h^2-98h+1122=0$, which has no real roots.) The area is
\[
\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot BC\cdot AD=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot20\cdot11=\boxed{110}.
\]
