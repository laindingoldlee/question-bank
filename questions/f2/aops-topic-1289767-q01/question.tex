Celia, Desi and Everett each wear a hat showing a different whole number from $1$ to $9$. No one can see their own number, but each sees the other two. Everett says, "The sum of the numbers I see is $6$." Celia says, "The product of the numbers I see is $10$." What is the sum of all the numbers that Everett could possibly have on his hat?
