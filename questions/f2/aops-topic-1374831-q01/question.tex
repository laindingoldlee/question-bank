Gary plays a carnival game with a box of $20$ red and $10$ green marbles. He draws a marble five times, without replacement, and wins if at least four of the five marbles are green. After his first three draws he takes a phone call. If he has drawn at least two green marbles by then, the carnival man replaces $5$ of the green marbles remaining in the box with red marbles. Then Gary makes his last two draws. What is the probability that Gary wins? Express your answer as a common fraction.
