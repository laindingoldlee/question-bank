Gary needs at least two green marbles among the first three draws, so the replacement always happens when he can still win.

\textbf{Exactly two green in the first three draws.} Probability $\dfrac{\dbinom{10}{2}\dbinom{20}{1}}{\dbinom{30}{3}}=\dfrac{900}{4060}$. The box then has $8$ green and $19$ red; after the replacement it has $3$ green and $24$ red. Gary needs both remaining draws green: $\dfrac{3}{27}\cdot\dfrac{2}{26}=\dfrac{1}{117}$.

\textbf{Three green in the first three draws.} Probability $\dfrac{\dbinom{10}{3}}{\dbinom{30}{3}}=\dfrac{120}{4060}$. The box has $7$ green and $20$ red; after the replacement $2$ green and $25$ red. Gary needs at least one green in two draws: $1-\dfrac{25}{27}\cdot\dfrac{24}{26}=1-\dfrac{100}{117}=\dfrac{17}{117}$.

Adding,
\[
P=\dfrac{900}{4060}\cdot\dfrac{1}{117}+\dfrac{120}{4060}\cdot\dfrac{17}{117}=\dfrac{900+2040}{4060\cdot117}=\dfrac{2940}{475020}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{1131}}.
\]
