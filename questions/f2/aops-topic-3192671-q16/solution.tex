A year with neither success has probability $\dfrac{19}{20}\dfrac{21}{22}=\dfrac{399}{440}$. A year with J alone succeeding has probability $\dfrac{21}{440}$. Summing over any number of initial no-success years gives $\dfrac{\dfrac{21}{440}}{1-\dfrac{399}{440}}=\boxed{\dfrac{21}{41}}$. Simultaneous first successes do not count.
