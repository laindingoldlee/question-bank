A $36^\circ$ arc is $\dfrac{36}{360}=\dfrac1{10}$ of the circle, so the circumference is $50$ and $r=\dfrac{50}{2\pi}=\dfrac{25}\pi$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0);real R=2;draw(circle(O,R));draw(O--R*dir(0));draw(O--R*dir(36));draw(arc(O,R,0,36),linewidth(2));draw(arc(O,0.5,0,36));
label("$36^\circ$",1.05*dir(18),fontsize(8pt));label("$5$",R*dir(18),E);dot(O);label("$O$",O,W);
\end{asy}

The area is $\pi r^2=\dfrac{625}\pi$ square inches, which is $\approx198.9$; with $\pi=3$ it is $\dfrac{625}3\approx\boxed{208.3}$.
