Write $n=2^a5^bm$ with $\gcd(m,10)=1$. The expansion of $\dfrac{1}{n}$ terminates iff $m=1$, and the number of non-repeating digits after the point is $\max(a,b)$. So we need $m>1$ and $\max(a,b)=4$. The smallest such $n$ is $2^4\cdot3=\boxed{48}$: indeed $\dfrac{1}{48}=0.0208\overline{3}$.
