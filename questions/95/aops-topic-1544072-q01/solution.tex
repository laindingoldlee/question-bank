\textbf{All arrangements.} SARAH has $5$ letters with A twice, so there are $\dfrac{5!}{2!}=60$ distinct arrangements.

\textbf{The A's together.} Treat AA as one block; with S, R, H that is $4$ distinct objects, arranged in $4!=24$ ways.

\textbf{Answer.} $60-24=\boxed{36}$.

(Directly: arrange S, R, H in $3!=6$ ways; the two identical A's go into $2$ of the $4$ gaps around them, $\dbinom42=6$ ways; $6\cdot6=36$.)
