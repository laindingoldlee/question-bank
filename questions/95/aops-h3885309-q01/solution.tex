The $400$ points are partitioned by their lines, so $\sum_LC_L=400$. Each line has a unique primitive direction $(a,b)$ with $\gcd(a,b)=1$ and $1\le a,b\le20$. There is one direction with $a=b$, namely $(1,1)$. For each $b=2,\ldots,20$, there are $\phi(b)$ primitive directions with $a<b$, and the same number with $a>b$. Thus
\[
K=1+2\sum_{b=2}^{20}\phi(b)=2\sum_{b=1}^{20}\phi(b)-1=255,
\]
using
\[
\phi(1),\ldots,\phi(20)=1,1,2,2,4,2,6,4,6,4,10,4,12,6,8,8,16,6,18,8,
\]
whose sum is $128$. Hence the answer is $400-255=\boxed{145}$. More generally the sum for a bound $N$ is $N^2-2\sum_{b=1}^{N}\phi(b)+1$.
