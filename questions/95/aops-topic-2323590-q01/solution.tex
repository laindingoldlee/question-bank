First combine the like terms: the equation is $13x^2+5=0$, i.e. $13x^2+0\cdot x+5=0$. By Vieta's formulas the sum of the roots is $-\dfrac{0}{13}=\boxed{0}$ and the product is $\boxed{\dfrac{5}{13}}$. (The roots are $\pm i\sqrt{\dfrac{5}{13}}$, which are not real.)
