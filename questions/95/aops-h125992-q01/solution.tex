By symmetry, more wins and more losses have equal probabilities. The number of the $3^6=729$ outcome strings with equal wins and losses is
\[
\sum_{k=0}^3\dfrac{6!}{k!k!(6-2k)!}=1+30+90+20=141.
\]
Hence the desired probability is $\dfrac{(729-141)}{(2\cdot729)}=\dfrac{98}{243}$, giving $\boxed{m+n=341}$.
