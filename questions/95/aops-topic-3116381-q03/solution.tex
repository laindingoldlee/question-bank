There are $\binom{10}{2}=45$ pairs of vertices. Ten pairs form sides, leaving $45-10=\boxed{35}$ diagonals.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair[] V;for(int i=0;i<10;++i)V.push(dir(90+36*i));for(int i=0;i<10;++i){draw(V[i]--V[(i+1)%10]);label(string(i+1),1.13V[i]);}for(int i=2;i<9;++i)draw(V[0]--V[i],blue);
\end{asy}
