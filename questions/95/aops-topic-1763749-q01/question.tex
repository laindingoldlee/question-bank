Bill is sent to a donut shop to buy exactly six donuts. The shop has four kinds of donuts, and Bill must get at least one of each kind. How many combinations will satisfy the order? Also explain why the count $4\cdot3\cdot2\cdot1\cdot4\cdot4$ (``one of each kind in some order, then any two'') is wrong.
