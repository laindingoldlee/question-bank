Write $D=(1-t)B+tC$ and $E=(1-u)C+uA$, where $0\le t,u\le1$. The triangle inequality for vectors first gives
\[
DE\le(1-t)BE+t\,CE.
\]
Also $BE\le(1-u)BC+u\,BA\le BC$, and $CE=u\,CA\le BC$. Substitution proves $\boxed{DE\le BC}$, including endpoints.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(1,3),B=(0,0),C=(5,0),D=(3.3,0),E=.4C+.6A;draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(D--E,blue);draw(B--E,dashed);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,S);label("$E$",E,NE);
\end{asy}
