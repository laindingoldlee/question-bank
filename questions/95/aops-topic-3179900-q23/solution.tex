The identity $(ac-bd)^2+(ad+bc)^2=(a^2+b^2)(c^2+d^2)$ gives $c^2+d^2=\dfrac{33^2+56^2}{5}=845$. This is attainable, for example by taking $a=\sqrt5,b=0,c=\dfrac{33}{\sqrt5},d=\dfrac{56}{\sqrt5}$. Thus the only value, and requested sum, is $\boxed{845}$.
