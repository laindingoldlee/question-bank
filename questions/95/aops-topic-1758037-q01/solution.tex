We have $(x-y)^2=(x+y)^2-4xy=100-36=64$, so $x-y=\pm8$. Then
\[
x^2-y^2=(x+y)(x-y)=10\cdot(\pm8)=\boxed{\pm80}.
\]
Indeed $(x,y)=(9,1)$ gives $80$ and $(x,y)=(1,9)$ gives $-80$.
