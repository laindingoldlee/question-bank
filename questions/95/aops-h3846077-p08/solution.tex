Since $2016=2^5\,3^2\,7$, it has $(5+1)(2+1)(1+1)=36$ divisors, giving $36^2=1296$ ordered pairs. For a prime exponent $e$, exponent pairs $(i,j)$ with $i+j\le e$ number $\dfrac{(e+1)(e+2)}{2}$. Thus the pairs whose product remains a divisor number $21\cdot6\cdot3=378$. The complementary count is $\boxed{1296-378=918}$.
