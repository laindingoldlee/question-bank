In triangle $ABC$, $AB=BC=5$ and $AC=6$. Drop the altitude $BP$ to $AC$; then drop the perpendicular from $P$ to $BC$, meeting it at $Q$; then drop the perpendicular from $Q$ to $AC$, then from that point to $BC$, then to $AC$, and so on indefinitely. What is the total length of all the segments drawn, including $BP$?
