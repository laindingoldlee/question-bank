Let the larger radius be $ R>10 $ and the smaller $ r $. Triangle $ AA_1B $ is right at $ A_1 $ by the diameter theorem. Thus the sine of the angle between the tangent $ AA_1 $ and $ AB $ is $\dfrac{5}{2R}$. The perpendicular distance of $ P $ from that line is therefore $ r=(R+10)\dfrac{5}{2R}$. Similarly the tangent from $ B $ gives $ r=(R-10)\dfrac{15}{2R}$. Equating yields $ 5(R+10)=15(R-10)$, hence $ R=20 $. Substitution gives $\boxed{r=\dfrac{15}{4}}$.
