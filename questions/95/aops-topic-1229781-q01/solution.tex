The exponent of $3$ in $20!$ is $6+2=8$. $20!=3^8\cdot m$ with $m=\dfrac{20!}{3^8}$, so the remainder is $3^8\cdot(m\bmod9)$. To find $m\bmod9$, remove the factors of $3$ from $1,\dots,20$; the remaining product modulo $9$ is
\[1\cdot2\cdot1\cdot4\cdot5\cdot2\cdot7\cdot8\cdot1\cdot10\cdot11\cdot4\cdot13\cdot14\cdot5\cdot16\cdot17\cdot2\cdot19\cdot20,\]
where $3,6,9,12,15,18$ became $1,2,1,4,5,2$. Modulo $9$ this is $7$, so the remainder is $3^8\cdot7=\boxed{45927}$.
