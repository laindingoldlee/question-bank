\textbf{From $0$ to $999$.} Write each of these numbers with three digits ($000$ to $999$). In each of the three positions every digit $0$--$9$ occurs $100$ times, so the total of all digit sums is
\[
3\cdot100\cdot(0+1+\cdots+9)=300\cdot45=13500 .
\]
\textbf{From $1000$ to $1999$.} The last three digits run through $000$--$999$ again ($13500$), and the leading $1$ contributes $1000$: total $14500$.

\textbf{From $2000$ to $2008$.} The digit sums are $2,3,\dots,10$, with total $9\cdot2+(0+1+\cdots+8)=18+36=54$.

Adding (the number $0$ contributes nothing),
\[
13500+14500+54=\boxed{28054}.
\]
