The daily pays in dollars are
\[I=75+2p,\qquad M=100+0.05p,\qquad S=150+0.03p.\]
\begin{itemize}
\item $I>S\iff1.97p>75\iff p>38.07$, i.e. $p\ge39$.
\item $I>M\iff1.95p>25\iff p>12.8$, i.e. $p\ge13$.
\item $M>S\iff0.02p>50\iff p>2500$.
\end{itemize}
For $p\le38$, $S$ beats $I$, and also beats $M$ since $p\le2500$. For $p\ge39$, $I$ beats both $S$ and $M$. Middle-Earth would need $p\le12$ (to beat $I$) and $p>2500$ (to beat $S$), which is impossible. (At $p=38$: $I=151$ and $S=151.14$; at $p=39$: $I=153$ and $S=151.17$.)

So $\boxed{\text{Surface-Earth for }p\le38,\ \text{Inner-Earth for }p\ge39,\ \text{Middle-Earth never}}$.
