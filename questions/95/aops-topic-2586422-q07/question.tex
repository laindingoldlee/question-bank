Three jobs are available. Inner-Earth pays $\$75$ a day plus $\$2$ per problem solved; Middle-Earth pays $\$100$ a day plus $5$ cents per problem; Surface-Earth pays $\$150$ a day plus $3$ cents per problem. If you solve $p$ problems a day (a nonnegative integer), for which values of $p$ is each job the best paid?
