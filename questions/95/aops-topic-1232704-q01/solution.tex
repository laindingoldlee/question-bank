The divisors of $2015=5\cdot13\cdot31$ are $1,5,13,31,65,155,403,2015$. If $X<31$, then the remainders for $31,65,155,403,2015$ are each $X$, the remainder for $1$ is $0$, and those for $5$ and $13$ are $X\bmod5$ and $X\bmod13$. The total is $5X+(X\bmod5)+(X\bmod13)=121$. Since the last two are at most $4+12$, $5X\ge105$, so $X\ge21$. Try $X=21$: $105+1+8=114$. $X=22$: $110+2+9=121$. So $X=\boxed{22}$; no smaller $X$ works, since $X\le20$ gives a total at most $100+4+12=116$ and $X=21$ gives $114$.
