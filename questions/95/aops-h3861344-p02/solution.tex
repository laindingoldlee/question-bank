Let the carries from right to left be $r_1,r_2,r_3,r_4$. The leading digit $a$ is at most $2$ and is even, because it is the units digit of $4e$. Thus $a=2$. The leading-column equation $4a+r_4=e$ forces $e=8$ or $9$; the units-column equation then forces $e=8$, $r_1=3$, $r_4=0$.

Now $4b+r_3=d$, so $b\le2$. The tens-column equation $4d+3=b+10r_2$ makes $b$ odd, hence $b=1$. It follows that $4d\equiv8\pmod{10}$, so $d=2$ or $7$; distinctness excludes $2$. Thus $d=7,r_2=3$, and $4b+r_3=d$ gives $r_3=3$. The middle column says $4c+3=c+30$, so $c=9$.

The unique number is $\boxed{21978}$, and indeed $4\cdot21978=87912$.
