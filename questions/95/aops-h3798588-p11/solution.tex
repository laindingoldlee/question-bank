Let the blue count be $b$, and let green and red totals be $G,R$. Since the eleven counts sum to 220, $G+R=220-b$ and $G-R=30$, giving $G=125-\dfrac{b}{2}$, $R=95-\dfrac{b}{2}$. Five boxes hold at most $21+22+23+24+25=115$, so $b\ge20$. They hold at least $15+16+17+18+19=85$, so $b\le20$. Thus $\boxed{b=20}$, attained by those two groups.
