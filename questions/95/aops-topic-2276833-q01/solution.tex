A domino corresponds to an unordered pair of numbers from $0$ to $12$, possibly equal. There are $\dbinom{13}{2}=78$ dominoes with two different numbers and $13$ ``doubles'', for $91$ dominoes in all. The probability of a double is
\[\dfrac{13}{91}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{7}}.\]
