Let the numbers be $a_1<a_2<\cdots<a_8$; their sum is $8\cdot18=144$ and the median is $\dfrac{a_4+a_5}{2}$. Since they are distinct integers,
\[
a_1+a_2+a_3\ge1+2+3=6,\qquad a_6+a_7+a_8\ge(a_5+1)+(a_5+2)+(a_5+3)=3a_5+6 .
\]
So $144\ge12+a_4+4a_5$, i.e.\ $a_4+4a_5\le132$. If $a_5\le26$, then $a_4+a_5\le2a_5-1\le51$; if $a_5\ge27$, then $a_4+a_5\le132-3a_5\le51$. Either way $a_4+a_5\le51$, and the median is at most $25.5$. This is attained by $1,2,3,24,27,28,29,30$ (sum $144$, median $\dfrac{24+27}{2}=25.5$). The smallest integer $\ge25.5$ is $\boxed{\textbf{(a)}\ 26}$.
