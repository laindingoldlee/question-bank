The condition is $100z+10y+x+xyz=100x+10y+z$, i.e.
\[xyz=99(x-z).\]
The left side is at least $0$, so $x\ge z$.

If $x>z$, the right side is a positive multiple of $11$, so $11\mid xyz$. As $11$ is prime and every digit is less than $11$, one of $x,y,z$ would be $0$; but then $xyz=0$, contradicting $x>z$.

So $x=z$, and then $x\cdot y\cdot x=0$ with $x\ge1$ forces $y=0$. Conversely every $\overline{x0x}$ works. The numbers are
\[\boxed{101,\ 202,\ 303,\ 404,\ 505,\ 606,\ 707,\ 808,\ 909}.\]
