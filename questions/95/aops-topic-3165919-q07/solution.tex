There must be zero or two even digits. With zero, all three digits are odd, giving $5^3=125$. With two and an odd leading digit there are $5\cdot5^2=125$. With an even leading digit, there are four choices for it, two choices of which remaining position is even, and five choices for each remaining digit, giving $4\cdot2\cdot5^2=200$. The total is $\boxed{450}$.
