Record the outcome as an ordered list of $5$ results; there are $6^5=7776$ equally likely lists. For exactly $k$ sixes, choose which $k$ of the $5$ rolls are sixes in $\dbinom5k$ ways; each of the other $5-k$ rolls must be one of the $5$ non-sixes, giving $5^{5-k}$ choices. It does not matter that one die is rolled five times rather than five dice once: each \emph{roll} is a position in the list, and each non-six roll independently has $5$ possible values. So
\[
P=\dfrac{\dbinom50 5^5+\dbinom51 5^4+\dbinom52 5^3}{6^5}=\dfrac{3125+3125+1250}{7776}=\dfrac{7500}{7776}=\boxed{\dfrac{625}{648}} .
\]
(Equivalently, $1-P=\dfrac{\dbinom53 5^2+\dbinom54 5+1}{6^5}=\dfrac{276}{7776}$.)
