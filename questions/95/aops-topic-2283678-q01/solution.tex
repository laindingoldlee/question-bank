First,
\[a^2+b^2+c^2-(ab+bc+ca)=\dfrac{1}{2}\left[(a-b)^2+(b-c)^2+(c-a)^2\right]\ge0,\]
with equality exactly when $a=b=c$. Hence
\[(a+b+c)^2=a^2+b^2+c^2+2(ab+bc+ca)\ge3(ab+bc+ca)=9,\]
and since $a+b+c>0$, we get $a+b+c\ge3$.

Equality requires $a=b=c$, and then $3a^2=3$ gives $a=1$. Conversely $a=b=c=1$ gives $ab+bc+ca=3$ and $a+b+c=3$. So the minimum value $3$ is attained exactly at $\boxed{a=b=c=1}$.
