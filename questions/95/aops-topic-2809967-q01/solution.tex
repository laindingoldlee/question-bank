Let $m=n(n+1)$, which is even, say $m=2j$. Then $n^2+n+2=m+2$ and the product is $m(m+2)=2j(2j+2)=4j(j+1)$, divisible by $4$ (in fact by $8$, since $j(j+1)$ is even). $\blacksquare$
