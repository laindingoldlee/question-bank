\textbf{A plan with $5$ crossings.} (1) Both children cross. (2) One child returns. (3) The father crosses alone. (4) The other child returns. (5) Both children cross. Every trip carries at most $200$ pounds, and at the end everyone is across.

\textbf{At least $5$ crossings.} Since everyone starts on one bank and ends on the other, the crossings alternate forward, back, forward, \dots, and end with a forward crossing; with $f$ forward crossings there are $f-1$ return crossings. A forward crossing carries at most $200$ pounds and a return crossing carries at least $100$ pounds (someone must row back). The net weight moved across is therefore at most
\[
200f-100(f-1)=100f+100 .
\]
This must be the total weight $400$ pounds, so $f\ge3$, and the number of crossings is $2f-1\ge\boxed{5}$.
