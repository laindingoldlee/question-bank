$665=5\cdot7\cdot19$, so $7\mid a^2+b^2$. Squares modulo $7$ are $0,1,2,4$, and the only pair summing to $0\pmod7$ is $0+0$. So $7\mid a$ and $7\mid b$, whence $49\mid a^2+b^2=665$. But $665=49\cdot13+28$, a contradiction. $\blacksquare$
