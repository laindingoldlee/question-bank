The original quadratic has no real roots, since its discriminant is $-3$. Its complex roots are $\dfrac{-1\pm i\sqrt3}{2}$, both satisfying $x^3=1$ but neither equal to 1. The implication $x^3=1\Rightarrow x=1$ is valid for real $x$ but not complex $x$. Restricting to reals instead makes the initial assumption impossible. Either interpretation invalidates the argument.
