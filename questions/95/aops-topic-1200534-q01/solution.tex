Join the centre to the three vertices. This splits the triangle into three congruent triangles, each with two sides $r$ and included angle $120^\circ$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw(circle((0,0),1),gray); pair A=dir(90),B=dir(210),C=dir(330); draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw((0,0)--A,blue); draw((0,0)--B,blue); draw((0,0)--C,blue); dot((0,0)); label("$r$",(0,0.5),E); label("$120^\circ$",(0,0),S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Each has area $\dfrac{1}{2}r^2\sin120^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}r^2$, so the triangle has area $3\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}r^2=\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{4}r^2$.
