Find the cubic polynomial $p(x)$ whose roots are $\dfrac{i}{\sqrt2}$, $-\dfrac{i}{\sqrt2}$ and $2$, and which satisfies $p(1)=1$. Show also that no \emph{monic} cubic has these roots and satisfies $p(1)=1$.
