Let Julia have $3k$ peas and Vlada $2k$. After the gift, Julia has $3k-15$ and Vlada has $2k+15$, so
\[
3k-15=(2k+15)+10\quad\Longrightarrow\quad k=40 .
\]
Julia started with $3\cdot40=\boxed{120}$ peas (Vlada had $80$; afterwards $105$ and $95$).
