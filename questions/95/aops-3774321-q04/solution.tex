Call the four products $A,B,C,D$ in the listed order. First $\dfrac{B}{A} = \dfrac{51}{120} < 1$. Also $C>D$, by pairing $3,5,\ldots,97$ with $2,4,\ldots,96$. Finally $\dfrac{C}{A} = \prod_{k=1}^{49} \dfrac{2k-1}{k+1}$. Its first four factors multiply to $\dfrac{7}{8}$, and the fifth raises this to $\dfrac{21}{16}>1$; every subsequent factor exceeds one. Thus $C>A>B$ and $C>D$. The largest is $\boxed{1\cdot3\cdot5\cdots97}$.
