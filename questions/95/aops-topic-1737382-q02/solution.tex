At an intersection point $x^2+8x+7=6x+18$, that is $x^2+2x-11=0$. The discriminant is $2^2+4\cdot11=48>0$, so there are two real solutions $x=-1\pm2\sqrt3$, each giving one point. The answer is $\boxed{2}$.
