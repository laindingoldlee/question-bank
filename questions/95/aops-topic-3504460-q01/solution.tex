The four tails define five head-runs, of lengths $h_1,\ldots,h_5\ge0$ summing to $12$. Each sequence corresponds to exactly one such tuple. There are $\binom{16}{4}=1820$ tuples. To impose $h_i\le4$, inclusion--exclusion gives $\binom{16}{4}-5\binom{11}{4}+\binom52\binom64=1820-1650+150=320$; three violations would require at least $15$ heads and are impossible. All conditional sequences are equally likely, so the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{16}{91}}$.
