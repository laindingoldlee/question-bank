For each story there are seven allowed subsets of girls who like it: the empty set, three singletons, and three pairs. Each of the three pair subsets must occur at least once among the four stories. Inclusion--exclusion gives $n=7^4-3\cdot6^4+3\cdot5^4-4^4=132$. The requested digit-square sum is $1^2+3^2+2^2=\boxed{14}$.
