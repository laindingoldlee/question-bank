Do not follow Joe's back-and-forth path (he turns around infinitely often as Bob approaches the far end). Joe runs without stopping at $5$ miles per hour for exactly as long as Bob runs. Bob needs $\dfrac{100}{1}=100$ hours to reach the far end, so Joe runs
\[
5\cdot100=\boxed{500}
\]
miles.
