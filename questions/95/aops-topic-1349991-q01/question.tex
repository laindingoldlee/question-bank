Bob and Joe start together at one end of a $100$-mile track and both run toward the other end, Bob at $1$ mile per hour and Joe at $5$ miles per hour. Whenever Joe meets Bob or reaches an end of the track, he immediately turns around and keeps running at the same speed. They stop when Bob reaches the far end. How many miles does Joe run?
