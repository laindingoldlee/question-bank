The formula is $\boxed{a_n=2^{n-1}}$. It holds at $n=1,2$, and substituting the preceding two powers gives $3\cdot2^{n-2}-2\cdot2^{n-3}=2^{n-1}$, proving it by induction. The first three terms are 1,2,4, whose differences are 1 and 2, so it is not arithmetic; it is geometric with ratio 2.
