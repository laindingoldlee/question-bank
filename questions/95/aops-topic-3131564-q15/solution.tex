Put $s=x+y+z$ and $p=xy+yz+zx$. Substituting $x+y=s-z$ and $xy=p-z(s-z)$ shows each of $x,y,z$ is a root of $T^3-(3s+2)T^2+(3s^2+s)T-s^3+s^2-3p$. If all three are distinct, Vieta gives $s=3s+2$, hence $s=-1$, then $p=2$ and $xyz=4$. The cubic is $T^3+T^2+2T-4=(T-1)(T^2+2T+4)$, and its three roots verify the equations. If all are equal to $a$, then $a^2=8a^3$, giving nonzero sum $\dfrac{3}{8}$. If exactly two equal $a$, their equation forces $a=0$ or $\dfrac{1}{8}$. For $a=0$, the third entry is 1, giving sum 1. For $a=\dfrac{1}{8}$, put the third entry $\dfrac{u}{8}$; the remaining equation factors as $(u-1)(u^2-4u+7)=0$. Excluding the all-equal case gives $u=2\pm i\sqrt3$ and sums $\dfrac{4\pm i\sqrt3}{8}$. These exhaust and attain the distinct nonzero sums. Their product is $(-1)(\dfrac{3}{8})(1)(\dfrac{19}{64})=\boxed{-\dfrac{57}{512}}$.
