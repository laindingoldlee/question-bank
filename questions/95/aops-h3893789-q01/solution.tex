Work modulo $2029$ and put $v=a-b$, $w=b-c$. Subtracting consecutive given relations gives $776v+3279w\equiv0$ and $-3279v-2503w\equiv0$. The determinant of this system is $3279^2-776\cdot2503=8809513\equiv1624$. It is invertible modulo $2029$, since $2029-1624=405$, $1624-4\cdot405=4$, and $405-101\cdot4=1$. Hence $v\equiv w\equiv0$, so $a\equiv b\equiv c$. Therefore $a^3+b^3+c^3-3abc\equiv3a^3-3a^3=0$, as required.
