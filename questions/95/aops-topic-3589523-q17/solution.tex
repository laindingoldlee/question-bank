Pad every number with leading zeros to three positions. We count nonnegative triples $(a,b,c)$ with $a+b+c=9$. No digit can exceed $9$, so there is no extra restriction. Stars and bars gives $\binom{11}{2}=\boxed{55}$. The all-zero triple is absent automatically.
