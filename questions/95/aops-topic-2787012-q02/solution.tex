If $c=1$, the sum is greater than1 and at most $1+\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{3}<2$, impossible. If $c\ge3$, the sum is at most $\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{5}<1$, also impossible. Thus $c=2$ and the sum must be 1. Now $\dfrac{1}{a}+\dfrac{1}{b}=\dfrac{1}{2}$, equivalently $(a-2)(b-2)=4$. Since $a>b>2$, the only factor pair is 4 and 1. Hence $\boxed{(a,b,c)=(6,3,2)}$.
