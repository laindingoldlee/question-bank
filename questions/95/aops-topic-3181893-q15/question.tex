For $n\ge2$, let $\phi(n)$ count positive integers below $n$ coprime to $n$. Let $S(k)$ be the set of composite divisors of $k$. Evaluate $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{50}\left(k-\sum_{d\in S(k)}\phi(d)\right)$.
