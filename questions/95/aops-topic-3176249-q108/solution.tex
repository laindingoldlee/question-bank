Let the rhombus side be $s$. Since $EF\parallel DG$ and $A\in EF$, its altitude between these parallel sides is the distance from $A$ to $DG$, namely $s\sin30^\circ=\dfrac{s}{2}$. On the other hand, triangle $ADG$ has base $AD=9$ and altitude 8 because $G$ lies on line $BC$. Its area is 36. Taking base $DG=s$ instead, the same area is $\dfrac{1}{2}s\cdot\dfrac{s}{2}=\dfrac{s^2}{4}$. Thus $s^2=144$ and $\boxed{DE=12}$.
