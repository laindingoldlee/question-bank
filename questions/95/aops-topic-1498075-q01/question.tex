A student wants to rewrite $\dfrac{b^2}{a^2+b^2}$ as $\dfrac{b^2}{a^2}-1$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that these two expressions are not equal for all nonzero real $a$, $b$.
\item[(b)] For positive $a$ and $b$, find all values of $\dfrac {a}{b}$ for which the two expressions are equal.
\item[(c)] Give a correct way to rewrite $\dfrac{b^2}{a^2+b^2}$ in terms of $\dfrac {a}{b}$.
\end{enumerate}
