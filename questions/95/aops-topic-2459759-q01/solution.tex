Work with differences from a convenient base value, $940385990$. The differences are
\[-9,\quad-2,\quad1,\quad4,\quad13,\]
with sum $7$ and mean $\dfrac75=1.4$. Since the mean of the numbers is the base value plus the mean of the differences, the mean is $940385990+1.4=\boxed{940385991.4}$.
