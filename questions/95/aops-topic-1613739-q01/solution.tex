The first $21$ terms are symmetric about the $11$th term, so their sum is $21a_{11}=273$ and $a_{11}=13$. From $a_5=7$ to $a_{11}=13$ is $6$ steps, so the common difference is $d=1$. Then
\[
a_{49}=a_{11}+38d=13+38=\boxed{51}.
\]
