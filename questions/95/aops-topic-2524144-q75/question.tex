For a positive integer $n$, plot the point $(p,e)$ for each prime $p$ dividing $n$, where $e$ is the exponent of $p$ in the prime factorisation of $n$. Find the least positive integer $n$ for which at least four of these points lie on the line $y=2x$. Give the answer in factored form.
