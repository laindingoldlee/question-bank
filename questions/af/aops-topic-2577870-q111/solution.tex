The ordinary sum has expectation 7. A double $d,d$ adds an extra $2d$, with probability $\dfrac{1}{36}$ for each $d=1,\ldots,6$. Thus the expectation is $7+\dfrac{2(1+2+3+4+5+6)}{36}=7+\dfrac{7}{6}=\boxed{\dfrac{49}{6}}$.
