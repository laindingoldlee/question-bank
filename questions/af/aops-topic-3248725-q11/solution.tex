Since $N^2$ has at most four digits, $s(N^2)\le36$, so $s(N)\le6$. For digit sums $1,2,3,4,5,6$, respectively, checking the possible two-digit numbers gives these valid lists: $\{10\}$, $\{11,20\}$, $\{12,21,30\}$, $\{13,22,31\}$, $\varnothing$, $\varnothing$. For completeness, the candidates at sums $4,5,6$ are $13,22,31,40$; $14,23,32,41,50$; and $15,24,33,42,51,60$; squaring and summing digits rejects precisely the unlisted entries. The valid integers sum to $10+11+20+12+21+30+13+22+31=\boxed{170}$.
