Let $p(x)=x^3+2x^2+2=(x-a)(x-b)(x-c)$. Then
\[f(1)=(1-a^2)(1-b^2)(1-c^2)=\bigl[(1-a)(1-b)(1-c)\bigr]\bigl[(1+a)(1+b)(1+c)\bigr].\]
The first bracket is $p(1)=5$. The second is $(-1)^3(-1-a)(-1-b)(-1-c)=-p(-1)=-3$. So $f(1)=\boxed{-15}$.
