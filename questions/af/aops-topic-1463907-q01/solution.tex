Write $Q=\dfrac{kVA}{d}$. From the first capacitor,
\[
300=\dfrac{k\cdot25\cdot16}{\dfrac{1}{3}}=1200k,\qquad k=\dfrac{1}{4}.
\]
For the second capacitor,
\[
Q=\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot\dfrac{28\cdot12}{\dfrac{1}{7}}=\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot28\cdot12\cdot7=588,
\]
answer \textbf{(D)}.
