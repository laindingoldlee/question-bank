The charge stored on a capacitor varies jointly as the voltage and the area of the plates, and inversely as the distance between the plates. A capacitor with plate area $16$, plate distance $\dfrac{1}{3}$ and voltage $25$ stores $300$ units of charge. How much charge does a capacitor with plate area $12$, plate distance $\dfrac{1}{7}$ and voltage $28$ store?

(A) $108$\qquad (B) $\dfrac{7500}{49}$\qquad (C) $\dfrac{1875}{4}$\qquad (D) $588$\qquad (E) none of these
