The teacher whispers a positive integer $A$ to Anna, $B$ to Brett, and $C$ to Chris. The students do not know one another's numbers, but they all know that $A+B+C=14$. Then:
\begin{itemize}
\item Anna says: ``I know that Brett and Chris have different numbers.''
\item Brett says: ``I already knew (before Anna spoke) that all three of our numbers were different.''
\item Chris says: ``Now I know all three of our numbers.''
\end{itemize}
Everyone is truthful and reasons perfectly. What is the product $ABC$?
