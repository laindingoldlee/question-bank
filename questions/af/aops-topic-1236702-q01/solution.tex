Casework on the number of students:
\[\dfrac{\dbinom53\dbinom42+\dbinom54\dbinom41+\dbinom55}{\dbinom95}=\dfrac{60+20+1}{126}=\boxed{\dfrac{9}{14}}.\]
The shortcut overcounts: a group with $4$ students is produced $\dbinom43=4$ times (once for each choice of which $3$ of its students were ``given'' first) and a group with $5$ students $\dbinom53=10$ times. Indeed $60+4\cdot20+10\cdot1=150=\dbinom53\dbinom62$.
