A committee has $5$ students and $4$ teachers. A group of $5$ members is chosen at random. What is the probability that it has at least $3$ students? Also explain why the count $\dbinom53\dbinom62$ (choose $3$ students, then any $2$ of the remaining $6$ people) is wrong.
