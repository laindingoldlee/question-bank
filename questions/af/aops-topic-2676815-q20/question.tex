In a competition with $2021$ participants, gold, silver and bronze medals are awarded so that
(i) the number of silver medals is at least twice the number of gold medals,
(ii) the number of bronze medals is at least twice the number of silver medals,
(iii) the total number of medals is at most $40\%$ of the number of participants.
The director first makes the number of gold medals as large as possible. Under that condition, what is the largest possible number of bronze medals?
