Let the numbers be $g,s,b$. The total is at most $\left\lfloor0.4\cdot2021\right\rfloor=808$. Since $s\ge2g$ and $b\ge2s\ge4g$, we get $7g\le808$, so $g\le115$, and $g=115$ is possible. With $g=115$: $s\ge230$ and $s+b\le693$, so $b\le693-s\le463$. The choice $s=230$, $b=463\ge460$ works. The answer is $\boxed{463}$.
