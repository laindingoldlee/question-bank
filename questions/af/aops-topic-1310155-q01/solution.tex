$4\dfrac{1}{3}$ hours is $260$ minutes, i.e. $\dfrac{260}{15}=\dfrac{52}{3}$ periods of $15$ minutes. At a steady rate the rise is $\dfrac{52}{3}\cdot\dfrac{1}{6}=\boxed{\dfrac{26}{9}\text{ inches}}\approx2.9$ inches. (The starting level, $1$ inch below sea level, does not affect the rise.)
