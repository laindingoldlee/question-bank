Subtracting equalities gives $(a-c)(b+1)=(b-a)(c+1)=(c-b)(a+1)=0$. If all three numbers are equal, their sum magnitude is $\sqrt{3(B+2)}$. Otherwise at least two must equal $-1$: for example if $a\ne c$, then $b=-1$, and another factored equality forces $a=-1$ or $c=-1$. The remaining coordinate is $\pm\sqrt B$, yielding magnitudes $\sqrt B+2$ and $\sqrt B-2$ since $B>4$. All three values are attained. They are distinct because $3B+6-(\sqrt B+2)^2=2(\sqrt B-1)^2>0$. Their sum is $\boxed{\sqrt{3B+6}+2\sqrt B}$.
