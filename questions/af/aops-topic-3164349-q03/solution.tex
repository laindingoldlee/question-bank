For every prime $p$, the exponent $3n\,v_p(n)$ must be divisible by $4$. If $4\mid n$, this always holds, giving $499$ integers. If $n\equiv2\pmod4$, its exponent of $2$ in $n^{3n}$ is $3n\equiv2\pmod4$, so it fails. If $n$ is odd, $3n$ is invertible modulo $4$, so every exponent in $n$ must be a multiple of $4$. The odd fourth powers below $2000$ are $1,81,625$. Therefore the total is $\boxed{502}$.
