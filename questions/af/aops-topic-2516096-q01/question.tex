Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle with altitudes $AD$, $BE$ and $CF$ (so $D$, $E$, $F$ lie on $BC$, $CA$, $AB$). Prove that
\[\triangle AEF\sim\triangle ABC,\qquad\triangle BDF\sim\triangle BAC,\qquad\triangle CDE\sim\triangle CAB.\]
