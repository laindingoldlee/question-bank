In a tournament, $32$ teams are drawn into $8$ groups of $4$, and in each group every final ranking of the four teams is equally likely. The team that finishes third in a group goes to a second competition. That competition is a knockout tournament of $32$ teams (5 rounds), in which each match is won by either team with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, independently. Atletico Madrid plays in the group stage of the first tournament. What is the probability that Atletico Madrid wins the second competition?
