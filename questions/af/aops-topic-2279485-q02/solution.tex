Atletico must finish exactly third in its group, which has probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$. It must then win all $5$ rounds of the second competition, with probability $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^5=\dfrac{1}{32}$. So the probability is
\[\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot\dfrac{1}{32}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{128}}.\]
