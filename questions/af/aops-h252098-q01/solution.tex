Set $t=|a+b|\ge0$, so $c=19-t$. If $t\le19$, then $ab=78+t$, but
\[
(a-b)^2=t^2-4t-312<0\qquad(0\le t\le19),
\]
impossible.

Thus $t\ge20$ and $ab=116-t$. The transformation $(a,b)\mapsto(-a,-b)$ preserves both equations and exchanges positive and negative sums. First take $a+b=t>0$. Then
\[
(a+1)(b+1)=ab+a+b+1=117.
\]
The two factors must be positive, since if both were negative then $a+b<0$. The six ordered positive factor pairs of $117=3^2\cdot13$ are
\[
(1,117),(3,39),(9,13),(13,9),(39,3),(117,1).
\]
They give sums $t=116,40,20,20,40,116$, all in the permitted range. Negating $a,b$ gives six additional triples, with the same uniquely determined $c$. The answer is $\boxed{12}$, choice $\boxed{\text{E}}$.
