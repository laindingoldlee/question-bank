A ball is thrown straight up. It rises $70$ metres during the first second. From then until $5$ seconds after the throw it keeps rising at a constant speed of $30$ metres per second, and after that it falls at a constant speed of $45$ metres per second. How high is the ball $7$ seconds after the throw?
