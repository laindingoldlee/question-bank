After $1$ second the ball is $70$ m high. From $t=1$ to $t=5$ it rises for $4$ seconds at $30$ metres per second, gaining $120$ m, so at $t=5$ it is $70+120=190$ m high. From $t=5$ to $t=7$ it falls for $2$ seconds at $45$ metres per second, losing $90$ m. At $t=7$ its height is
\[
190-90=\boxed{100\text{ metres}}.
\]
