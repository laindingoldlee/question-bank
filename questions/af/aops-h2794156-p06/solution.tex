Subtract the lightest weight from every weight. The resulting integers are nonnegative, one is zero, and the hypothesis persists because each group contains 16 cows. Let their total be $S$. Removing cow $i$ shows $S-w_i$ is even. Thus all $w_i$ have the parity of $S$; since one is zero, all are even. Divide by 2 and repeat: the same hypotheses still hold.

Any positive adjusted weight would therefore be divisible by every power of 2, impossible. All adjusted weights are zero, so the original weights are equal.
