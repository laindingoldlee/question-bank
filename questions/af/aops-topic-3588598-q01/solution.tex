If the hexagon has side $s$, it consists of six equilateral triangles of side $s$. The equal-perimeter triangle has side $2s$, so its area is four times that of one small equilateral triangle. The area ratio is therefore $4:6=2:3$, and the required area is $120\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}=\boxed{80\text{ cm}^2}$.

\end{asy}
