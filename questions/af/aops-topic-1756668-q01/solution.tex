Let the bag hold $N$ jellybeans. Mike has $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac {N}{3}=\dfrac {N}{6}$ left and Zac has $\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot\dfrac {N}{2}=\dfrac {N}{3}$ left, together $\dfrac {N}{2}=45$, so $N=90$.
