A monkey types a random sequence of $9$ distinct letters, all such sequences being equally likely. What is the probability that the letters in the odd-numbered positions ($1$st, $3$rd, $5$th, $7$th, $9$th) are in alphabetical order and the letters in the even-numbered positions ($2$nd, $4$th, $6$th, $8$th) are also in alphabetical order? (For example, awbxcydze works: abcde and wxyz are both in alphabetical order.)
