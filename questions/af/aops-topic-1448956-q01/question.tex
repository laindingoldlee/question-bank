A positive integer $N$ is a common multiple of $2$, $3$, $4$ and $5$, and it is simultaneously a perfect square, a perfect cube, a perfect fourth power and a perfect fifth power. If $N$ is written as $N=x^y$ with positive integers $x$ and $y$, find the least possible value of $x+y$ (over all such $N$ and all such ways of writing them).
