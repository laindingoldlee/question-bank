Number the days $1$ (November 1, 2020) to $365$ (October 31, 2021); there are $365$ days because February 2021 has $28$ days. He calls on day $k$ exactly when $7\mid k$ or $3\mid k$. By inclusion-exclusion the number of such days is
\[\left\lfloor\dfrac{365}{7}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{365}{3}\right\rfloor-\left\lfloor\dfrac{365}{21}\right\rfloor=52+121-17=\boxed{156}.\]
