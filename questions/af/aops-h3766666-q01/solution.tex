The parity of the number of nonred balls is unchanged. Removing two nonred balls either removes two of one colour or one of each, and replaces them with red. Removing one red and one nonred replaces them with one nonred. Removing two red changes no nonred count. Initially the nonred count is $2023+2025=4048$, even. A single green or blue ball would give odd count, so the last ball must be $\boxed{\text{red}}$.
