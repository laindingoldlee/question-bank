All three areas equal $2^2=4$. Let the hexagon have side $s$ and the triangle side $t$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(8cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=sqrt(8/(3*sqrt(3))),t=sqrt(16/sqrt(3));
draw(box((0,0),(2,2)),linewidth(0.9));label("$2$",(1,0),S);
pair c=(3.9,1);pair[] H;for(int k=0;k<6;++k)H.push(c+s*dir(60*k));
draw(H[0]--H[1]--H[2]--H[3]--H[4]--H[5]--cycle,linewidth(0.9));label("$s$",(H[4]+H[5])/2,S);
pair T0=(5.8,0),T1=(5.8+t,0),T2=(5.8+t/2,t*sqrt(3)/2);
draw(T0--T1--T2--cycle,linewidth(0.9));label("$t$",(T0+T1)/2,S);
label("$4$",(1,1));label("$4$",c);label("$4$",(T0+T1+T2)/3);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

A regular hexagon is made of six equilateral triangles of side $s$, so its area is $6\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}s^2$; the triangle has area $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}t^2$. Setting these equal,
\[
6s^2=t^2,\qquad \dfrac {s}{t}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt6}=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt6}{6}} .
\]
(The common area $4$ and the square are not even needed for the ratio.)
