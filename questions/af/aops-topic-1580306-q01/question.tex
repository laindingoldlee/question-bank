Convex pentagon $ABCDE$ satisfies $AB=AE=16$, $BC=5$, $CD=14$ and $DE=9$. If
\[
\angle BAC+\angle ACB+\angle EAD+\angle ADE=180^\circ,
\]
what is the maximum possible area of $ABCDE$?
