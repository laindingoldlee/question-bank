The binary expansion is $2336=2^{11}+2^8+2^5$. If any two of the three exponents were equal, combining them would leave a sum of at most two powers of two, which cannot have three nonzero binary digits. Hence the exponents are distinct and uniquely $x=5,y=8,z=11$. The sides are $6,8,10$, and $6^2+8^2=10^2$. Thus the triangle is $\boxed{\text{right angled and scalene}}$.
