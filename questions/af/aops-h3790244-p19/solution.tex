The intersecting segments give $\angle BGF=\angle CGE=180^\circ-64^\circ=116^\circ$. The two convex quadrilaterals $ABGF$ and $GCDE$ each have angle sum $360^\circ$. Adding their angle sums counts the six requested angles plus the two $116^\circ$ angles at $G$. Hence the requested sum is $720^\circ-232^\circ=\boxed{488^\circ}$.

\end{asy}
