Write the coordinates as $a,a+d,a+2d$, where $a,d$ are positive integers. The determinant formula gives area $\dfrac{d}{2}\,3^a(3^d-1)^2$. Thus $d3^a(3^d-1)^2=3456$. If $d\ge3$, the left side is at least $3\cdot3\cdot26^2=6084$, impossible. For $d=1$, the equation would give $3^a=864$, not a power of 3. For $d=2$, it gives $128\cdot3^a=3456$, hence $a=3$. The rightmost coordinate is $a+2d=\boxed7$.
