Now $9$ scores are below $80$, five are $80$, and $7$ are at least $85$; the median is $80$. For a median of $85$, the $10$th and $11$th scores must average $85$, and since no score lies strictly between $80$ and $85$ (all scores are multiples of $5$), both must be $85$. So at least $11$ scores must be at least $85$, and at most $9$ below. Only the $80$s can rise to $85$ with $5$ extra points, so at least $11-7=4$ students need extra points. Raising four of the $80$s to $85$ works: the sorted list then has its $10$th and $11$th scores both equal to $85$. Answer $\boxed{\textbf{(C)}\ 4}$.
