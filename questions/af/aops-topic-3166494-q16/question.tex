A walker starts at the origin with initial angular coordinate0 degrees. Away from the origin, its polar coordinates have integer radius $r\ge1$ and angle $\theta$ modulo $360$ degrees. A move increases either $r$ or $\theta$ by one. Revisiting a physical point is forbidden, so an angular move at the origin is forbidden. Count all finite valid paths ending at radius $1$ through $2020$, with any integer angle.
