Measure angles clockwise from the 12 o'clock position, and let the time be $t$ minutes after 2:00. The minute hand moves $6^\circ$ per minute and the hour hand $\dfrac{1}{2}^\circ$ per minute, starting from $60^\circ$ at 2:00:
\[
\text{minute hand: }6t,\qquad\text{hour hand: }60+\dfrac {t}{2} .
\]
At 2:30 ($t=30$) the minute hand is at $180^\circ$ and the hour hand at $75^\circ$, so the minute hand is $105^\circ$ ahead. It gains $\dfrac{11}{2}^\circ$ per minute. The hands are next opposite when the minute hand is $180^\circ$ ahead:
\[
6t-\left(60+\dfrac {t}{2}\right)=180\iff\dfrac{11}{2}t=240\iff t=\dfrac{480}{11}=43\dfrac{7}{11}.
\]
This is after $t=30$, and no earlier opposition occurs after 2:30, since the gap grows steadily from $105^\circ$. So the hands are opposite at $\boxed{43\dfrac{7}{11}\text{ minutes past }2}$, about 2:43:38.
