The argument transformation cycles $-\dfrac{1}{2}\mapsto2\mapsto\dfrac{3}{4}\mapsto\dfrac{1}{3}\mapsto-\dfrac{1}{2}$. Denote the four function values by $A,B,C,D$ in that order. The equations give $A=-4B+3$, $B=C+3$, $C=\dfrac{8}{3}D+3$, and $D=6A+3$. Thus $C=16A+11$, $B=16A+14$, and $A=-64A-53$. Therefore $65A=-53$ and $\boxed{F(-\dfrac{1}{2})=-\dfrac{53}{65}}$.
