Three farmers are at the market, each starting with no money. Farmer John sells $3$ carrots for $1$ dollar, Farmer Jihn sells $4$ carrots for $2$ dollars, and Farmer Jahn sells $5$ carrots for $3$ dollars.
\begin{itemize}
\item In the first hour, John sells $18$ carrots, Jihn sells $16$ carrots and Jahn sells $5$ carrots. Then Jahn takes $5$ dollars from each of the other two.
\item In the second hour, John sells $21$ carrots, Jihn sells $4$ carrots and Jahn sells $10$ carrots. Then Jihn takes $4$ dollars from each of the other two.
\item In the third hour, John sells $3$ carrots, Jihn sells $8$ carrots and Jahn sells $15$ carrots. Then John takes $3$ dollars from each of the other two.
\end{itemize}
These three hours are repeated exactly the same way two more times. Then, in one more hour, each farmer sells $18$ carrots. While they walk home, a burglar takes $5$ dollars from each farmer. How many dollars does each farmer have at the end, and how many does the burglar have?
