The cube has edge $2\cdot15=30$ and volume $27000$.

Take the cross-section through the axis of the cone, which passes through the centre $O$ of the sphere. The apex is on the sphere and the base is $24$ below it, so the base plane is at distance $24-15=9$ from $O$. The base circle lies on the sphere, so its radius is $\sqrt{15^2-9^2}=12$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw(box((-15,-15),(15,15)),linewidth(0.8));
draw(circle((0,0),15),blue);
pair T=(0,15),L=(-12,-9),R=(12,-9);
fill(T--L--R--cycle,rgb(1,0.9,0.85)); draw(T--L--R--cycle,red+linewidth(0.9));
dot((0,0)); draw((0,0)--R,dashed); label("$15$",(6,-4.5),NE,fontsize(8pt));
draw((0,0)--(0,-9),dashed); label("$9$",(0,-4.5),W,fontsize(8pt));
label("$12$",(6,-9),S,fontsize(8pt));
draw((-17,15)--(-17,-9),Arrows(4)); label("$24$",(-17,3),W,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}

The cone has volume $\dfrac13\pi\cdot12^2\cdot24=1152\pi$, so the required volume is $27000-1152\pi$. Then $m=27000$, $n=1152$ and $m+n=\boxed{28152}$.
