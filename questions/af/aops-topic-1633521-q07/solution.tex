Both fractions must be defined and nonzero, so $x,y\ne0$; put $u=\dfrac{1}{x}$, $v=\dfrac{1}{y}$. Then $\dfrac{x+y}{xy}=u+v$ and $\dfrac{x^2+y^2}{x^2y^2}=u^2+v^2$, so the system is
\[
u+v=30,\qquad u^2+v^2=2018 .
\]
Hence $2uv=900-2018=-1118$, $uv=-559$, and $u$, $v$ are the roots of $t^2-30t-559=0$, i.e.\ $t=15\pm\sqrt{225+559}=15\pm28$: $43$ and $-13$. They are distinct, so
\[
\boxed{(x,y)=\left(\dfrac{1}{43},-\dfrac{1}{13}\right)\text{ or }\left(-\dfrac{1}{13},\dfrac{1}{43}\right)}.
\]
(Check: $x+y=\dfrac{-30}{559}\ne0$ and $\dfrac{xy}{x+y}=\dfrac{-\dfrac{1}{559}}{-\dfrac{30}{559}}=\dfrac{1}{30}$.)
