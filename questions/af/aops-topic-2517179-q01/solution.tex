Let $d$ be a common divisor. Since
\[2018!+1=2018\cdot2017!+1=2018\left(2017!+1\right)-2017,\]
$d$ divides $2017$. Every divisor of $2017$ is at most $2017$, so it divides $2017!$; hence $d$ divides $\left(2017!+1\right)-2017!=1$. Therefore
\[\gcd\left(2017!+1,\ 2018!+1\right)=\boxed{1}.\]
