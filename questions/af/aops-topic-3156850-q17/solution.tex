Since the progression is increasing, $ a_0\ne0 $. Dividing by $ a_0 $ gives $ 2(r^3+r^2-r-1)=r^4-1 $, or $(r^2-1)(r^2-2r-1)=0 $. The positive roots are 1 and $ 1+\sqrt2 $. Ratio 1 would make the progression constant, so $\boxed{r=1+\sqrt2}$.
