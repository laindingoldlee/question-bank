A person has two six-sided dice. One is fair; the other comes up $1$ with probability $\dfrac34$ and each of the other five faces with probability $\dfrac{1}{20}$. The person picks one of the two dice at random, each with probability $\dfrac12$, and rolls it four times. Given that the first three rolls are all ones, the probability that the fourth roll is also a one is $\dfrac pq$, where $p$ and $q$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $p+q$.
