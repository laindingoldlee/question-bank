The altitude from $A$ to $BC$ has length $\sqrt{20^2-12^2}=16$, so the triangle area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot24\cdot16=192$. Its circumradius is
\[R=\dfrac{AB\cdot AC\cdot BC}{4[ABC]}=\dfrac{20\cdot20\cdot24}{4\cdot192}=\dfrac{25}{2}.\]
The cyclic order is $A,D,B,C$. By Ptolemy's theorem,
\[AB\cdot CD=AD\cdot BC+BD\cdot AC=15\cdot24+7\cdot20=500.\]
Hence $CD=25=2R$. A chord attains length $2R$ only when it passes through the center, so $CD$ is a diameter.
