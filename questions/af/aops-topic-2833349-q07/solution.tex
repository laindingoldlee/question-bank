Since $ 2022=2\cdot3\cdot337 $, it has 8 divisors. Exponent patterns giving 8 divisors are 7;3 and 1; or 1,1,1. Their smallest representatives are $ 2^7=128 $, $ 2^3\cdot3=24 $, and $ 2\cdot3\cdot5=30 $. The least is $\boxed{24}$.
