When a pufferfish is threatened by a danger of level $D$ on the Puffer Scale, it swells so that the ratio of its swollen diameter to its original diameter is directly proportional to $2^D$. A pufferfish of diameter $12$ inches swells to a diameter of $128$ inches at danger level $5$. To what diameter will a pufferfish of diameter $9$ inches swell at danger level $9$?
