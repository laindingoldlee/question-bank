By Heron's formula with $s=81$: $K^2=81(81-a)(81-b)(81-c)$. The three factors are positive with sum $3\cdot81-162=81$, so by AM-GM their product is at most $27^3$, with equality when $a=b=c=54$. Hence the maximum area is $\sqrt{81\cdot27^3}=9\cdot27\sqrt{27}=729\sqrt3$, attained by the equilateral triangle of side $54$: $\boxed{729\sqrt3}$.
