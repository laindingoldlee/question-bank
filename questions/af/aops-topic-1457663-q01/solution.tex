This is a finite geometric series with first term $a=\dfrac{1}{2}$, ratio $r=\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $n=10$ terms. Its sum is
\[
a\cdot\dfrac{1-r^{n}}{1-r}=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{1-\dfrac{1}{1024}}{\dfrac{1}{2}}=\boxed{\dfrac{1023}{1024}} .
\]
(Equivalently, $1-\dfrac{1}{2^{10}}$: each term is the gap left by the previous partial sum.)

The formula $\dfrac{a}{1-r}$ is the sum of the \emph{infinite} series $a+ar+ar^2+\cdots$ (for $|r|<1$). It comes from $a\cdot\dfrac{1-r^n}{1-r}$ by letting $n\to\infty$, so that $r^n\to0$. For ten terms $r^{10}=\dfrac{1}{1024}$ is not $0$, so $\dfrac{a}{1-r}=1$ overestimates the sum by exactly $\dfrac{1}{1024}$.
