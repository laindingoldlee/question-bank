Multiplying $\dfrac{1}{x}+\dfrac{1}{y}+\dfrac{1}{z}=0$ by $xyz\ne0$ gives $xy+yz+zx=0$. Hence
\[
x^2+y^2+z^2=(x+y+z)^2-2(xy+yz+zx)=1-0=\boxed{1},
\]
choice (B). Such numbers exist, for example $x=y=\dfrac{2}{3}$, $z=-\dfrac{1}{3}$: $\dfrac{3}{2}+\dfrac{3}{2}-3=0$ and $\dfrac{4}{9}+\dfrac{4}{9}+\dfrac{1}{9}=1$.
