Replace every die result $d$ by $11-d$. This is a probability-preserving bijection that sends $S$ to $121-S$. Therefore sums at most $60$ and sums at least $61$ have equal probability:
\[
\Pr(S>60)=\dfrac{1}{2}.
\]
The event $S=61$ has positive probability (ten dice showing $6$ and one showing $1$ is one possible outcome). Thus $\Pr(S>61)<\dfrac{1}{2}$. For $k\le60$, monotonicity gives probability at least $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Hence $\boxed{k=61}$.
