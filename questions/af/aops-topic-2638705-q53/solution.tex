(a) For $x=\log_2 7$, the left side is $49+21=70$, not $40$. The correct value is $\boxed{x=\log_2 5}$, since $25+15=40$. (b) Change of base gives $\dfrac{\log5}{\log2}\approx\boxed{2.322}$. (c) Rewrite the equation as $(2^x-5)(2^x+8)=0$. Since $2^x+8>0$, only $2^x=5$ is possible. Strict increase of $2^x$ gives a unique real solution.
