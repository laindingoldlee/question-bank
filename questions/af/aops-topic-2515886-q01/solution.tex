Modulo $4$, $4x-17y=1$ gives $-y\equiv1$, so $y\equiv3\pmod4$. Write $y=4t+3$ with $t\ge0$. Then
\[4x=1+17(4t+3)=68t+52,\qquad x=17t+13.\]
Conversely every $t\ge0$ gives a solution in positive integers. The condition $x\le1000$ becomes $17t\le987$, i.e. $t\le58$. So $t=0,1,\dots,58$ and there are $\boxed{59}$ pairs (from $(13,3)$ to $(999,235)$), with $59$ different values of $y$.
