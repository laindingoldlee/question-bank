Dividing by $x$: $x-\dfrac1x=3$, which is (b): $\boxed{3}$. Also the other root is $x'=-\dfrac1x$, with $x+x'=3$, $xx'=-1$.

(a) $x^2+\dfrac1{x^2}=\left(x-\dfrac1x\right)^2+2=\boxed{11}$.

(c) $x^5-\dfrac1{x^5}=x^5+x'^5$. With $v_k=x^k+x'^k$ we have $v_0=2$, $v_1=3$ and $v_k=3v_{k-1}+v_{k-2}$: $v_2=11$, $v_3=36$, $v_4=119$, $v_5=\boxed{393}$.

(d) $x^7+\dfrac1{x^7}=x^7-x'^7$, which depends on which root $x$ is. With $u_k=x^k-x'^k$: $u_0=0$, $u_1=x-x'=\pm\sqrt{13}$ and $u_k=3u_{k-1}+u_{k-2}$, giving $u_7=1189\,u_1$. So $x^7+\dfrac1{x^7}=\boxed{\pm1189\sqrt{13}}$: $+$ for $x=\dfrac{3+\sqrt{13}}2$ and $-$ for $x=\dfrac{3-\sqrt{13}}2$.
