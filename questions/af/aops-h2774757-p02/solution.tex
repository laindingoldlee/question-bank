Let $A$ be the area of one end face and $L$ the lateral area of one prism. The joined surface area is $2A+nL$. The condition $2A+11L=3(2A+L)$ gives $L=\dfrac{A}{2}$. Then $2A+nL=9(2A+L)$ yields $\boxed{n=41}$.
