Count nights after April $5$. Ghost nightmares occur on nights $19k$ and cat nightmares on nights $1+13m$ ($k,m\ge0$). We need $19k\equiv1\pmod{13}$, i.e. $6k\equiv1\pmod{13}$. Since $6\cdot11=66=5\cdot13+1$, the smallest solution is $k=11$, i.e. night $209$ (and $209=1+13\cdot16$). The next coincidence is $19\cdot13=247$ nights later, after the end of the year.

Counting $209$ days from April $5$: to May $5$ is $30$, to June $5$ is $61$, to July $5$ is $91$, to August $5$ is $122$, to September $5$ is $153$, to October $5$ is $183$, and $26$ more days reach October $31$. So yes: both nightmares occur on the night of $\boxed{\text{October }31}$ (Halloween).
