Barry has a nightmare about ghosts every $19$ days and a nightmare about black cats every $13$ days. He had the ghost nightmare on the night of April $5$, and the cat nightmare the very next night, April $6$. Do the two nightmares ever occur on the same night before the end of the year? If so, on what date?
