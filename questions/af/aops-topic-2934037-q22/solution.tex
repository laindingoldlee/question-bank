Blocks of length 2 imply adjacent terms have the same parity. Thus all terms have the same parity, and consecutive distinct terms differ by at least 2. From the last term being at least 1, the terms are at least 19, 17,..., 1 respectively, whose sum is 100. This sequence works: the average of any contiguous block is the average of its odd first and last terms, hence an integer. Therefore the minimum is $\boxed{100}$.
