For side length $s$, $OM=\dfrac{s}{2}$ and $OA=\dfrac{s}{\sqrt2}$. Hence $s(\sqrt2-1)=2$, so $s=2(\sqrt2+1)$. The area is $s^2=\boxed{12+8\sqrt2}$.
