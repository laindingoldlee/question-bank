A Martian makes a rope that fits snugly around the equator of the Earth. The Martian King wants a little slack: the rope should go around the equator with a gap of exactly one inch between the Earth and the rope all the way round. The Martian says she only needs to add a short piece of rope. The King laughs, saying she will need miles of extra rope, because the diameter of the Earth is almost $8{,}000$ miles. Who is right? Exactly how much rope must be added? (Treat the equator as a circle.)
