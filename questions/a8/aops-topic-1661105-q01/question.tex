The digits $a$ and $b$ are nonzero and coprime, and $\overline{ab}=10a+b$ and $\overline{ba}=10b+a$ are two-digit numbers. The greatest common divisor of $\overline{ab}$ and $\overline{ba}$ equals $a+b$. How many ordered pairs $(a,b)$ are possible?
