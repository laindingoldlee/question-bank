Since $a^2=a-2$, we can lower every power of $a$:
\[
a^3=a\cdot a^2=a^2-2a=(a-2)-2a=-a-2,
\]
\[
a^4=a\cdot a^3=-a^2-2a=-(a-2)-2a=2-3a .
\]
So $a^4=2-3a$. $\blacksquare$

(The roots are $a=\dfrac{1\pm i\sqrt7}{2}$, which are not real; the argument uses only $a^2=a-2$ and so works for both of them.)
