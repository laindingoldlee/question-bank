One hundred teams each submit an integer from $1$ to $1000$. The other ninety-nine teams all submit $400$. If you submit $E$, let $A$ be one quarter of the mean of all one hundred submissions. Your score is $\lfloor\max(0,30-|A-E|,2-|E-1000|)\rfloor$. Find your greatest score and all submissions achieving it.
