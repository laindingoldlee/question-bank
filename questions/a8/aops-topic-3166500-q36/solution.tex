Here $A=\dfrac{39600+E}{400}=99+\dfrac{E}{400}$, so $|A-E|=|99-\dfrac{399E}{400}|$. This is never zero for integer $E$, because its zero is $\dfrac{39600}{399}$, not an integer. Thus the main score is at most $29$ after flooring, and the fallback term is at most two. For a main score of $29$, we need $|99-\dfrac{399E}{400}|\le1$, or $\dfrac{39200}{399}\le E\le\dfrac{40000}{399}$. The only integers in this interval are $99,100$, and both yield a score of $29$. Thus the optimum is $\boxed{29}$ points, attained exactly by $\boxed{E=99\text{ or }100}$.
