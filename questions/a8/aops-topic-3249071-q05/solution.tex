Pythagoras gives $BC=\sqrt{58^2-40^2}=42$. Since cosine is adjacent over hypotenuse, $\sec C=\dfrac{AC}{BC}=\boxed{\dfrac{29}{21}}$. Secant is a ratio, not an angle measured in degrees.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair B=(0,0),A=(0,40),C=(42,0);draw(A--B--C--cycle);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,E);label("$40$",(A+B)/2,W);label("$42$",(B+C)/2,S);label("$58$",(A+C)/2,NE);
\end{asy}
