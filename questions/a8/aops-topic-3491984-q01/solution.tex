For fixed first value $x$, the allowed region $y,z\ge0$, $y+z<1-x$ is a right triangle of area $\dfrac{(1-x)^2}{2}$ in the unit square. Therefore the probability is $\int_0^1\dfrac{(1-x)^2}{2}\,dx=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{6}}$. Boundary equalities have probability zero.
