By inclusion--exclusion, $73+52-23=102$ students like at least one of the subjects, so $140-102=38$ like neither. The difference of the probabilities is
\[
\dfrac{102}{140}-\dfrac{38}{140}=\dfrac{64}{140}=\boxed{\dfrac{16}{35}} .
\]
