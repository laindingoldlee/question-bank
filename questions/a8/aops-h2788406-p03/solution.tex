The exponent is
\[
v_2(3998!)-v_2(1999!)
=\sum_{j\ge1}\left(\left\lfloor\dfrac{3998}{2^j}\right\rfloor-\left\lfloor\dfrac{1999}{2^j}\right\rfloor\right).
\]
Since $3998=2\cdot1999$, the first sum equals $1999+\sum_{j\ge1}\lfloor\dfrac{1999}{2^j}\rfloor$. The remaining terms cancel, leaving $\boxed{1999}$. Thus the highest power itself is $2^{1999}$.
