Define $T_0(t)=1,T_1(t)=t$ and $T_{k+1}(t)=2tT_k(t)-T_{k-1}(t)$. The cosine addition identity proves $T_k(\cos x)=\cos kx$, so $P=T_{10}$ because two polynomials agreeing on an interval are identical. The same recurrence shows $T_k\left(\dfrac{u+u^{-1}}{2}\right)=\dfrac{u^k+u^{-k}}{2}$. Take $u=2$, obtaining $P (\dfrac{5}{4}) = \dfrac{2^{10}+2^{-10}}{2} = \boxed{\dfrac{1048577}{2048}}$.
