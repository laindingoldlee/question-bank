A tangle is a sequence of digits constructed by picking a number $N\ge  0$ and writing the integers from $0$ to $N$ in some order, with no spaces. For example, $010123459876$ is a tangle with $N = 10$. A palindromic sequence reads the same forward or backward, such as $878$ or $6226$. The shortest palindromic tangle is $0$. How long is the second-shortest palindromic tangle?
