A palindrome has at most one digit with odd multiplicity. Counting the digits in $0,1,\ldots,N$ shows at least two odd multiplicities for every $1\le N\le18$, so none is possible. At $N=19$, all multiplicities are even and the length is $10+10\cdot2=30$. A construction, with commas marking whole-number blocks, is $0,12,1,13,4,15,6,17,8,19,9,18,7,16,5,14,3,11,2,10$. Removing commas gives a palindrome and uses every integer from zero to nineteen once. Thus $\boxed{30}$ is the second-shortest length.
