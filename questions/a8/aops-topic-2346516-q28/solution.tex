Elly is second, and Bobby finished next to her, so Bobby is first or third. Bobby finished after Alice, so he is not first; hence Bobby is third. Alice finished before Bobby, so she is first or second, and second is taken by Elly. So $\boxed{\text{Alice}}$ finished first. (Cassy and Danny are fourth and fifth in some order; the clues do not decide which.)
