Represent the pair as a point of the square $[-20,10]^2$, of area $900$. The product is positive when both numbers are negative (a $20\times20$ square) or both positive (a $10\times10$ square).
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
fill((-20,-20)--(0,-20)--(0,0)--(-20,0)--cycle,gray(0.8));fill((0,0)--(10,0)--(10,10)--(0,10)--cycle,gray(0.8));
draw((-20,-20)--(10,-20)--(10,10)--(-20,10)--cycle);draw((0,-20)--(0,10));draw((-20,0)--(10,0));
label("$-20$",(-20,-20),S);label("$10$",(10,-20),S);label("$-20$",(-20,-20),W);label("$10$",(-20,10),W);label("$x$",(10,-20),E);label("$y$",(-20,10),N);
\end{asy}

The probability is $\dfrac{400+100}{900}=\boxed{\dfrac59}$.

Counting integers treats the choice as discrete: $31^2$ outcomes including the value $0$, whose products are $0$. The numbers are chosen from a continuum, so probabilities come from lengths and areas, not from counts of integers (a single value such as $0$ has probability $0$).
