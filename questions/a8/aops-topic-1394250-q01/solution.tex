Expanding, $(bx+c)^2=b^2x^2+2bcx+c^2$. Comparing coefficients,
\[
b^2=a,\qquad 2bc=1,\qquad c^2=1 .
\]
So $c=\pm1$, $b=\dfrac{1}{2c}=\pm\dfrac{1}{2}$ (same sign as $c$), and $a=b^2=\dfrac{1}{4}$. There are two triples:
\[
\boxed{(a,b,c)=\left(\dfrac{1}{4},\dfrac{1}{2},1\right)\ \text{ or }\ \left(\dfrac{1}{4},-\dfrac{1}{2},-1\right).}
\]
In both cases $abc=\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{1}{8}$, since $bc=\dfrac{1}{2}$. The sum $a+b+c$ is $\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{3}{2}=\dfrac{7}{4}$ for the first triple and $\dfrac{1}{4}-\dfrac{3}{2}=-\dfrac{5}{4}$ for the second.
