Find the error in the following ``proof'' that $2=1$, and explain what $\dfrac{0}{0}$ means.

Let $x=y$. Then $x^2=xy$, so $x^2-y^2=xy-y^2$, i.e. $(x+y)(x-y)=y(x-y)$. Cancelling $x-y$ gives $x+y=y$; substituting $x=y$ gives $2y=y$, and dividing by $y$ gives $2=1$.
