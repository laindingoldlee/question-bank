Let $S\left(2^n\right)=k$ and $S\left(5^n\right)=m$, so $10^{k-1}<2^n<10^k$ and $10^{m-1}<5^n<10^m$ (strict, since neither is a power of $10$ for $n\ge1$). Multiplying, $10^{k+m-2}<10^n<10^{k+m}$, so $n=k+m-1$, i.e. $k+m=n+1$. Then $m-k\equiv m+k=n+1\pmod2$, which is even exactly when $n$ is \textbf{odd}.
