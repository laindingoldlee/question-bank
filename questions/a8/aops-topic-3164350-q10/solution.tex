An outfit that is better than every outfit to its left can never be removed, since any remaining left neighbour is worse; the same holds for a right-to-left record. At termination there is no interior local minimum. A sequence of distinct qualities without a local minimum increases to its maximum and then decreases, so every remaining outfit must be one of those original left or right records: an earlier larger left record or a later larger right record would otherwise contradict that shape. Thus the final set is independent of removal order and consists exactly of the union of the two record sets. The $k$th position is a left record with probability $\dfrac{1}{k}$, and similarly from the right. The maximum is the only outfit in both sets. Expected size is $2H_8-1=2\cdot\dfrac{761}{280}-1=\dfrac{621}{140}$. Therefore $\boxed{a+b=761}$.
