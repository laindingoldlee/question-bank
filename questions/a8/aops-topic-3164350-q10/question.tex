Eight outfits of distinct quality are initially in uniformly random order. Repeatedly remove an interior outfit that is worse than both its immediate neighbours, choosing uniformly among available removals, until none remain. If the expected final count is $\dfrac{a}{b}$ in lowest terms, find $a+b$.
