At least $69\%$ of 6 means at least 5 loyal pirates. Hence the probability is $\binom65\left(\dfrac{16}{17}\right)^5\dfrac{1}{17}+\left(\dfrac{16}{17}\right)^6=\dfrac{22\cdot16^5}{17^6}\approx0.9557$. Rounded to the nearest percent, this is $\boxed{96\%}$.
