In the first half hour the airplane flies $350$ miles, so the distance is $650$ miles when the interceptor leaves. They approach each other at $700+800=1500$ mph, so they meet after $\dfrac{650}{1500}=\dfrac{13}{30}$ hour, that is, $\boxed{26\text{ minutes}}$.
