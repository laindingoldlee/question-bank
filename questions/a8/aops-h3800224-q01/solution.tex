For each red number $r=\overline{ab}$ between $10$ and $99$, there are $\lfloor\dfrac{99}{r}\rfloor$ positive multiples between $10$ and $99$. Grouping by this quotient gives
\[\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrrrr}\text{quotient}&1&2&3&4&5&6&7&8&9\\\text{number of }r&50&16&9&5&3&2&2&1&2\end{array}\]
The total is $50+32+27+20+15+12+14+8+18=\boxed{196}$. Each pair of two-digit numbers gives exactly one four-digit integer.
