Two dogs, each running at $10$ feet per second, run toward each other from $500$ feet apart. As they run, a flea flies from the nose of one dog to the nose of the other at $25$ feet per second, turns around immediately, and keeps flying back and forth between the dogs' noses in this way until it is squashed when the dogs collide. How far does the flea fly?
