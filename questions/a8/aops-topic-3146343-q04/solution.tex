The first $n$ days provide $\dfrac{n}{2}(256+(256-7(n-1)))=\dfrac{n(519-7n)}{2}$ worker-days. At $n=24$ this is 4212 exactly; all daily counts through 24 are positive. The first 23 days provide less, since day 24 contributes 95. Thus completion is at the end of $\boxed{\text{day24}}$.
