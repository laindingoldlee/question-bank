In a penalty shootout between France and Spain, the teams shoot alternately, France first, and each team has $5$ shots. The shootout stops as soon as one team has scored so many more goals than the other that the other team can no longer catch up with its remaining shots. Every shot is independent of the others: the shooter misses the goal with probability $5\%$, and a shot on target is saved with probability $2\%$ by the Spanish goalkeeper and with probability $25\%$ by the French goalkeeper; otherwise it is a goal. What is the probability that Spain wins the shootout with all $10$ shots taken? Give the answer to four decimal places.
