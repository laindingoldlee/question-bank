The angle-bisector theorem gives $\dfrac{AB}{AC}=\dfrac{BD}{DC}=\dfrac{1}{2}$, hence $AC=420$. In right triangle $AEC$, $AE=\sqrt{525^2-420^2}=\boxed{315}$.
