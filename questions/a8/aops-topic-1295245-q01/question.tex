In an abandoned chemistry lab, Gerome finds a two-pan balance and three $1$-gram weights, three $5$-gram weights and three $50$-gram weights. He places a pile of chemicals on one pan and any of the weights on either pan. How many different positive weights of chemicals can he measure?
