We have $T(n)=\lfloor\dfrac{n}{5}\rfloor+\lfloor\dfrac{n}{25}\rfloor+\lfloor\dfrac{n}{125}\rfloor+\cdots$. It jumps by at least 3 only when $125\mid n$. Two successive integers can both be skipped only in such a jump. Before the values exceed 100, these occur at 125,250,375, where $T(n)$ jumps respectively $28\to31$, $59\to62$, and $90\to93$. Thus the pairs of missing consecutive values start at $m=29,60,91$, giving $\boxed3$. All other jumps in this range are at most 2, so skip at most one integer.
