In the right isosceles triangle $ABC$ with the right angle at $C$, points $D$ and $E$ lie on the legs $AC$ and $BC$ respectively, with $CD=CE$. Points $L$ and $K$ on the hypotenuse $AB$ are such that $DL\perp AE$ and $CK\perp AE$. Prove that $LK=KB$.
