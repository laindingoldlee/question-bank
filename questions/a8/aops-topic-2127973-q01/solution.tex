By the formula for a geometric series,
\[1+2+2^2+\cdots+2^{10000}=2^{10001}-1 .\]
Since $2^3=8\equiv1\pmod7$ and $10001=3\cdot3333+2$,
\[2^{10001}=\left(2^3\right)^{3333}\cdot2^2\equiv1\cdot4=4\pmod 7,\]
so the sum is congruent to $4-1=3$. The remainder is $\boxed{3}$.

(Another way: the terms come in blocks $2^{3k}+2^{3k+1}+2^{3k+2}=7\cdot2^{3k}$, each divisible by $7$. The $10001$ terms form $3333$ such blocks and two extra terms $2^{9999}+2^{10000}\equiv1+2=3\pmod7$.)
