A tesseract with side length $n$ is the set of points $(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)$ with $0\le x_i\le n$ for each $i$, where the distance between two points is $\sqrt{(x_1-y_1)^2+(x_2-y_2)^2+(x_3-y_3)^2+(x_4-y_4)^2}$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What is its four-dimensional volume (hypervolume)?
\item[(b)] What is the length of its longest diagonal?
\end{enumerate}
