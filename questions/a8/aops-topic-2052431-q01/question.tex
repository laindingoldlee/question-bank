A function $f$ is defined on the complex numbers by $f(z)=(a+bi)z$, where $a$ and $b$ are positive real numbers. This function has the property that for each complex number $z$, the point $f(z)$ is equidistant from $z$ and from the origin. Given that $|a+bi|=8$, find $b^2$.
