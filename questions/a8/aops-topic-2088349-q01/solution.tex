The discriminant is $4^2-4\cdot6\cdot3=16-72=-56<0$, so there are no real solutions. By the quadratic formula,
\[x=\dfrac{-4\pm\sqrt{-56}}{12}=\dfrac{-4\pm2i\sqrt{14}}{12}=\boxed{\dfrac{-2\pm i\sqrt{14}}{6}}.\]
