All four numbers are the same: each is $3.444\ldots$ (the bar only says where the repetition of $4$ starts to be marked). And
\[3.\overline4=3+\dfrac{4}{9}=\dfrac{31}{9}.\]
So the expression is $\dfrac{31}{9}+\dfrac{31}{9}+\dfrac{31}{9}-\dfrac{31}{9}=2\cdot\dfrac{31}{9}=\boxed{\dfrac{62}{9}}$.
