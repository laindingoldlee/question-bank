Since $\left(3+2\sqrt2\right)\left(3-2\sqrt2\right)=9-8=1$, we have $\dfrac{1}{x}=3-2\sqrt2$ and $x+\dfrac{1}{x}=6$. Square twice:
\[
x^2+\dfrac{1}{x^2}=\left(x+\dfrac{1}{x}\right)^2-2=34,\qquad x^4+\dfrac{1}{x^4}=\left(x^2+\dfrac{1}{x^2}\right)^2-2=34^2-2=\boxed{1154}.
\]
