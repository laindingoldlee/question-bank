For a positive integer $n$, consider any $n$ consecutive positive integers.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that for $n\ge2$ the greatest common divisor of $n$ consecutive positive integers is always $1$. (For $n=1$ it is the number itself, so it is not determined by $n$.)
\item[(b)] Find, in terms of $n$, the largest integer $d$ that divides the product of every $n$ consecutive positive integers.
\end{enumerate}
