Find the positive real number $x$ such that
\[
\sqrt{x+\sqrt{x+\sqrt{x+\cdots}}}=5,
\]
where the left side means the limit of the sequence $a_1=\sqrt x$, $a_{n+1}=\sqrt{x+a_n}$. Check that for this $x$ the sequence really converges to $5$.
