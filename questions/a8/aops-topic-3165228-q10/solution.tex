Four distinct intersections require $0<k<1$. Their abscissas are $-a,-b,b,a$, where $a=(1+k)^{\dfrac{1}{4}}$ and $b=(1-k)^{\dfrac{1}{4}}$. The condition $2b=2(a-b)$ gives $a=2b$, hence $1+k=16(1-k)$. Solving gives $\boxed{k=\dfrac{15}{17}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

import graph;real f(real x){return abs(x^4-1);}real k=15/17,a=(1+k)^.25,b=(1-k)^.25;draw(graph(f,-1.25,1.25));draw((-1.3,k)--(1.3,k),blue);pair[]v={(-a,k),(-b,k),(b,k),(a,k)};string[]names={"A","B","C","D"};for(int j=0;j<4;++j)label("$"+names[j]+"$",v[j],N);
\end{asy}
