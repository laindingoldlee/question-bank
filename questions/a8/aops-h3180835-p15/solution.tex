Let the rectangle sides be $a\ge b$. The four $45^\circ$ angle bisectors bound a square with diagonals of length $a-b$, so its area is $\dfrac{(a-b)^2}{2}=18$. Hence $(a-b)^2=36$. Since $ab=216$, $(a+b)^2=(a-b)^2+4ab=36+864=900$, giving $a+b=30$. The perimeter is $\boxed{60}$.
