For the midpoint $M$ of $BC$, the median formula gives $AM^2=\dfrac{2AB^2+2AC^2-BC^2}{4}=\dfrac{72+128-49}{4}=\dfrac{151}{4}$. Thus $\boxed{AM=\dfrac{\sqrt{151}}{2}}$.
