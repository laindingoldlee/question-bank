Ask: ``Is either your number $1$, or your number $3$ and the unrevealed coin landed heads? If $n=1$, the first clause is true, so he answers yes regardless of the coin. If $n=2$, both clauses are false, so he answers no. If $n=3$, the proposition is equivalent to the unknown coin outcome, so he answers I do not know. The three answers distinguish the three integers.
