Jerry decides whom to sell his share of a company to by flipping a fair coin: heads means AoPS, tails means his partner Ben. Whenever the coin shows AoPS, he reflips it with probability $63\%$; whenever it shows Ben, he reflips it with probability $42\%$. He keeps going until he decides not to reflip, and then sells to the side shown by the last flip. What is the probability that he sells to AoPS?
