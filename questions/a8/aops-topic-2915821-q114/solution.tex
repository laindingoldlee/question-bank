The positive product and negative sum force both roots to be negative. Write them as $-k $ and $-(202200-k)$ with $ 1\le k\le101100 $, so each unordered root pair occurs once. The product condition is $ 2022\mid k(202200-k)$, equivalent to $ 2022\mid k^2 $. Since $ 2022=2\cdot3\cdot337 $ is squarefree, this is equivalent to $ 2022\mid k $. There are $\dfrac{101100}{2022}=50 $ such values. The product $ k(202200-k)$ strictly increases on this half-interval, so they give distinct $ m $. Thus the answer is $\boxed{50}$.
