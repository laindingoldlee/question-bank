Triangle $ PCD $ is equilateral, with all sides 4. The parallel condition makes triangles $ PAB $ and $ PCD $ similar, so $ PAB $ is also equilateral. Hence $ PB=AB=2\pi\cdot4=\boxed{8\pi}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair P=(0,0),C=(4,0),D=4dir(60),A=(8*pi,0),B=8*pi*dir(60);draw(circle(P,4));draw(P--A--B--cycle);draw(C--D,blue);label("$P$",P,SW);label("$C$",C,S);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$A$",A,SE);label("$B$",B,N);
\end{asy}
