Use $B=(0,0),C=(6,0),A=(0,3),D=(6,12)$. Line $AD$ meets $BC$ extended at $T=(-2,0)$. The angle condition is equivalent, by supplementary inscribed angles, to $B,D,T,X$ being cyclic on the arc lying inside the trapezoid. Their circle has equation $x^2+y^2+2x-16y=0$, centre $O=(-1,8)$ and radius $\sqrt{65}$. The point $C$ is outside it, at distance $OC=\sqrt{113}$. Thus $CX\ge OC-\sqrt{65}$ by the triangle inequality. The closest point $X = O + \sqrt{\dfrac{65}{113}} (C-O)$ lies strictly inside the trapezoid and on the required arc, so the bound is attained. The minimum is $\boxed{\sqrt{113}-\sqrt{65}}$.
