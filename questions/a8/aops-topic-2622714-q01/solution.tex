The number of computers is proportional to (robots)$\times$(hours). Six robots working $36$ hours give $216$ robot-hours for $18$ computers, i.e. one computer per $12$ robot-hours. Two days are $48$ hours, so $24$ robots give $24\cdot48=1152$ robot-hours, which make $\dfrac{1152}{12}=\boxed{96}$ computers.

(Step by step: four times as many robots make $72$ computers in $36$ hours, and $\dfrac{48}{36}=\dfrac43$ as much time gives $72\cdot\dfrac43=96$.)
