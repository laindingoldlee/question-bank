The layer lengths form the arithmetic sequence $10,30,50,\dots,10+20(n-1)=20n-10$. Their sum is
\[\dfrac{n\left(10+(20n-10)\right)}{2}=\dfrac{n\cdot20n}{2}=10n^2 .\]
So the ratio of the total length to $n$ is $\dfrac{10n^2}{n}=\boxed{10n}$, that is, $10n:1$. (In other words, the average layer length is $10n$ metres.)
