Let $c$ be Charlene's age and $s$ the sum of Betty's and Ashley's ages. Then $c-s=16$ and $c^2-s^2=1632$. Factoring,
\[
c^2-s^2=(c-s)(c+s)=16(c+s)=1632,\qquad\text{so}\qquad c+s=102 .
\]
The sum of the three ages is $c+s=\boxed{102}$. (Indeed $c=59$ and $s=43$.)
