Two students take a $25$-question test on which each correct answer scores $1$ point and every other answer scores $0$, so each score is an integer from $0$ to $25$. The first student scores strictly higher than the second. The sum of their scores leaves remainder $0$ when divided by $9$, and the product of their scores leaves remainder $4$ when divided by $7$. How many ordered pairs of scores (first, second) are possible?
