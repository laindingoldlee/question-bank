Put $AB=7t$, $BC=6t$, $CD=2t$. The shared hypotenuse satisfies $AC^2=85t^2$, so $AD^2=85t^2-4t^2=81t^2$ and $AD=9t$. The diagonal divides the convex quadrilateral into two right triangles, hence $120=\dfrac{1}{2}(7t)(6t)+\dfrac{1}{2}(9t)(2t)=30t^2$. Thus $t=2$ and the perimeter is $(7+6+2+9)t=\boxed{48\text{ cm}}$.
