The smallest factor is $1$ and the next is the smallest prime factor $p$ of $n$, so $1+p=6$ and $p=5$. The two largest factors are $n$ and $\dfrac {n}{p}=\dfrac {n}{5}$, so $n+\dfrac {n}{5}=\dfrac{6n}{5}=1122$, giving $n=935$. Check: $935=5\cdot11\cdot17$ is odd and not divisible by $3$, so its smallest prime factor is indeed $5$. The answer is $\boxed{935}$.
