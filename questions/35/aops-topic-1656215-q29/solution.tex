Every multiple of $4$ is a multiple of $2$, so the case $a_n=4$ never happens, and a multiple of $2$ that is not a multiple of $4$ is $\equiv2\pmod4$. Count $n\le60$ in each case.
\begin{itemize}
\item $a_n=1$: $n$ coprime to $30$. There are $60\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot\dfrac{4}{5}=16$ such $n$, contributing $16$.
\item $a_n=2$: $n\equiv2\pmod4$ and not divisible by $3$ or $5$. The $15$ numbers $2,6,\dots,58$ include $5$ multiples of $3$ ($6,18,30,42,54$) and $3$ multiples of $5$ ($10,30,50$), with $30$ in both, leaving $15-5-3+1=8$: contribution $16$.
\item $a_n=3$: odd multiples of $3$ not divisible by $5$: $3,9,21,27,33,39,51,57$, that is $8$ numbers: contribution $24$.
\item $a_n=5$: odd multiples of $5$ not divisible by $3$: $5,25,35,55$: contribution $20$.
\end{itemize}
The sum is $16+16+24+20=\boxed{76}$.
