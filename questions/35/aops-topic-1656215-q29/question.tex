For a positive integer $n$ define $a_n$ as follows: $a_n=1$ if $n$ is not a multiple of any of $2,3,4,5$; $a_n=2$ if $n$ is a multiple of $2$ but not of $3$, $4$ or $5$; $a_n=3$ if $n$ is a multiple of $3$ but not of $2$, $4$ or $5$; $a_n=4$ if $n$ is a multiple of $4$ but not of $2$, $3$ or $5$; $a_n=5$ if $n$ is a multiple of $5$ but not of $2$, $3$ or $4$; and $a_n=0$ otherwise. Compute $\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{60}a_i$.
