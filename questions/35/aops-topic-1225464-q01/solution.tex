Let $a=\sqrt[3]{\sqrt{52}+5}$, $b=\sqrt[3]{\sqrt{52}-5}$. Then $a^3-b^3=10$ and $ab=\sqrt[3]{52-25}=3$. With $x=a-b$:
\[x^3=a^3-b^3-3ab(a-b)=10-9x,\]
so $x^3+9x-10=0$, i.e. $(x-1)(x^2+x+10)=0$. The quadratic has no real roots, so $x=\boxed{1}$.
