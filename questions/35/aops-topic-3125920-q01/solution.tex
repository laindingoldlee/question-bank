For every prime $p$, the integers from $1$ to $p^2$ not coprime to $p^2$ are precisely the $p$ multiples of $p$. Hence $\phi(p^2)=p^2-p$. There are infinitely many primes: a finite list $p_1,\ldots,p_t$ would omit any prime divisor of $p_1\cdots p_t+1$. Thus there are infinitely many solutions $k=p$.
