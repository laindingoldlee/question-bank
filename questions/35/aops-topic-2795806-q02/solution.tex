The second partial product is $\overline{cde}\times a=\overline{cde}$, so $a=1$. The first partial product is $\overline{cde}\times b=\overline{feg}$, and the total is $\overline{cde}\times\overline{1b}=\overline{bbcg}$.

Since $\overline{cde}\cdot10+\overline{feg}=\overline{bbcg}$ and $\overline{feg}<1000$, the thousands digit $b$ equals $c$ or $c+1$; the digits are distinct, so $b=c+1$. As $\overline{cde}\cdot b$ has three digits, $100c\cdot(c+1)<1000$, so $c(c+1)<10$ and $c\in\{1,2\}$; $c=1$ would repeat $a$, so $c=2$ and $b=3$.

Now $\overline{2de}\cdot13=\overline{332g}$ lies between $3320$ and $3329$, so $\overline{2de}=256$ (as $255\cdot13=3315$ and $257\cdot13=3341$). Then $d=5$, $e=6$, and $256\cdot3=768$ gives $f=7$, $g=8$; the total $3328$ matches $\overline{bbcg}$.

The unique solution is $\boxed{a=1,b=3,c=2,d=5,e=6,f=7,g=8}$, i.e. $256\times13=3328$.
