Each of three original faces loses a right triangle of area $\dfrac{1}{2}$. The new face $BCD$ is equilateral with side $\sqrt2$, so its area is $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$. The surface area becomes $6-\dfrac{3}{2}+\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{9+\sqrt3}{2}}$.

\end{asy}
