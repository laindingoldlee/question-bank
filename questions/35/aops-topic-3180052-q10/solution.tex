To touch five decades, even a birth just before the end of the first decade requires passing all 30 years of the three intervening decades before reaching the fifth. Thus completed age is at least 30. Someone born on December 31, 1999 has touched five decades by January 1, 2030 and is 30. Therefore $\boxed{30}$.
