Multiply $\sqrt{x+15}+\sqrt x=15$ by $\sqrt{x+15}-\sqrt x$. The left side becomes $(x+15)-x=15$, so
\[\sqrt{x+15}-\sqrt x=\dfrac{15}{15}=1 .\]
Adding this to the original equation gives $2\sqrt{x+15}=16$, so $x+15=64$ and $x=\boxed{49}$.

Check: $\sqrt{64}+\sqrt{49}=8+7=15$.
