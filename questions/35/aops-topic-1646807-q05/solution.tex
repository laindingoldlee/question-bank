Each pound is $0.45359237$ kg, so
\[
200\text{ kg}=\dfrac{200}{0.45359237}\text{ pounds}\approx\boxed{440.92}\text{ pounds}.
\]
(The rough rule $1$ kg $\approx2.2$ pounds gives $440$.)
