Let $a,b,c$ be their respective real times to count to one minute. Then $b=2c-10$, $c=2a-35$, and $a=2b-15$. Substituting gives $a=4c-35$ and $c=8c-105$, so $c=15$, $b=20$ and $a=25$. Ryan's two-minute time is $2b=\boxed{40\text{ seconds}}$.
