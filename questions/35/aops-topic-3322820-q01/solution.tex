Once an average is appended to a list, the new average stays unchanged. Thus all terms from the third onward equal the average of the first two. Since the twelfth is $28$, the third and fifth are $28$, and $\dfrac{10+a_2}{2}=28$ gives $a_2=46$. The requested sum is $46+28=\boxed{74}$.
