Let the angles between the common sides $a,b$ be $\theta,\phi$. Equal areas give $\sin\theta=\sin\phi$. Since $c\ne d$, the angles differ, so $\phi=180^\circ-\theta$. The cosine rule then gives $c^2+d^2=2(a^2+b^2)$. Hence $a^2+b^2=\boxed{1012}$.
