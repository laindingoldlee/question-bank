If one variable is zero, the equation forces all three zero. Otherwise sort them as $1\le x\le y\le z$. If $x\ge3$, then $xyz\ge9z>3z\ge x+y+z$, impossible. If $x=2$ and $y\ge2$, $2yz-y-z-2$ is already positive at $(y,z)=(2,2)$ and increases, impossible. Thus $x=1$, and $(y-1)(z-1)=2$, giving $(y,z)=(2,3)$. Hence the complete answer is $\boxed{(0,0,0)\text{ and the six permutations of }(1,2,3)}$.
