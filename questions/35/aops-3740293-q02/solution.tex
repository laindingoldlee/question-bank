From 24 December of year $y-1$ to 24 December of year $y$, the weekday advances by $1+L(y)$ days modulo 7, where $L(y)=1$ when $y$ is divisible by 4 but not by 100, or is divisible by 400; otherwise $L(y)=0$. Number weekdays Monday 0 through Sunday 6, and start $w=2$ for Wednesday in 2025. For each $y=2026,\ldots,3000$, update $w\leftarrow(w+1+L(y))\bmod7$ and count the year if $w<5$. The counts on the successive intervals $2026$--$2099$, $2100$--$2199$, $2200$--$2299$, $2300$--$2399$, $2400$--$2499$, $2500$--$2599$, $2600$--$2699$, $2700$--$2799$, $2800$--$2899$, $2900$--$2999$, and $3000$ are respectively $54,71,71,72,72,71,71,72,72,71,1$. Their sum is $\boxed{698}$. The recurrence accounts for every year, including non-leap century years, so the enumeration is exact.
