Two teams play until one has won $4$ games. The Grunters win each game with probability $\dfrac{3}{4}$, independently. A student computes the probability that the Grunters win the championship in exactly $7$ games as $\dbinom63\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^3\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^3=\dfrac{135}{1024}$, claiming the book's answer is wrong. Find the error and the correct probability.
