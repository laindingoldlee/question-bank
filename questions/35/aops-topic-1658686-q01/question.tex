Two players take turns counting upwards from $1$. On each turn a player says the next one, two or three numbers (for example, if the last number said was $14$, the next player may say $15$; or $15,16$; or $15,16,17$). The player who says the number $N$ loses.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] For $N=21$, which player can force a win, and how?
\item[(b)] For $N=31$, which player can force a win, and how?
\item[(c)] For which $N$ can the first player force a win?
\end{enumerate}
