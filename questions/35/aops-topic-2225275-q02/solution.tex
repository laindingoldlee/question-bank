Write $5^{30}=\dfrac{10^{30}}{2^{30}}$. Since $2^{10}=1024$, we have $2^{30}=1024^3=1073741824$, so
\[10^9<2^{30}<10^{10}.\]
Dividing $10^{30}$ by these bounds gives
\[10^{20}<5^{30}<10^{21}.\]
A number between $10^{20}$ and $10^{21}$ has $\boxed{21}$ digits. (Indeed $5^{30}=931322574615478515625$.)
