A product $ab$ is a perfect square exactly when $a$ and $b$ have the same squarefree part. The faces split into classes by squarefree part: $\{1,4\}$, $\{2\}$, $\{3\}$, $\{5\}$, $\{6\}$. The four dice must land in four different classes.

If the four classes include $\{1,4\}$ ($4$ choices of the other three classes), there are $4!\cdot2$ ordered outcomes for each choice; otherwise ($1$ choice) there are $4!$. Total $4\cdot48+24=216$, so the probability is $\dfrac{216}{6^4}=\boxed{\dfrac16}$.
