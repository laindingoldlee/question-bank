The first term ends in 3. For every later term, its exponent is a power of 3 with an odd exponent, and therefore congruent to 3 modulo 4. Powers of 3 modulo 10 have period 4, so each later tower ends in 7. The sum ends as $3+2021\cdot7=14150$, giving $\boxed0$.
