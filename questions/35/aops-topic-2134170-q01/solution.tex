The polynomial $(x+3)^4$ has degree $4$, so it contributes nothing to $x^5$. By the binomial theorem, the $x^5$ term of $(2x+6)^9$ is $\dbinom95(2x)^5\,6^4$, so the coefficient is
\[\binom95\cdot2^5\cdot6^4=126\cdot32\cdot1296=\boxed{5225472}.\]
