A $0$ at the end cannot work (deleting it divides by $10$). Two-digit numbers end in $0$, so try three digits $\overline{a0b}$: $100a+b=9(10a+b)$ gives $10a=8b$, i.e. $5a=4b$, so $a=4$, $b=5$. The answer is $\boxed{405}=9\cdot45$.
