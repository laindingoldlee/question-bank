Rambo is starting a new workout program. On the first day he plans to do $24$ sit-ups, and on each following day he plans to do $3$ more sit-ups than on the day before. Which recursive formula gives the number of sit-ups he does on any later day? (Here Now is the number for the current day and Next is the number for the following day.)
\begin{itemize}
\item[\textbf{(A)}] $\text{Next}=24\times\text{Now}+3$, starting at $24$
\item[\textbf{(B)}] $\text{Next}=\text{Now}+24$, starting at $24$
\item[\textbf{(C)}] $\text{Next}=\text{Now}+3$, starting at $24$
\item[\textbf{(D)}] $\text{Next}=3\times\text{Now}+24$, starting at $24$
\end{itemize}
