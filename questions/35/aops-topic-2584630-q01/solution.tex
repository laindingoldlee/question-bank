Each day's number is the previous day's number plus $3$, and the first value is $24$. So Next $=$ Now $+\,3$, starting at $24$, which gives $24,27,30,33,\dots$

The other formulas grow far too fast: (A) gives $24,579,\dots$, (B) gives $24,48,\dots$ and (D) gives $24,96,\dots$

The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(C)}}$.
