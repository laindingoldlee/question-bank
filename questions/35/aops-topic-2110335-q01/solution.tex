Suppose there are $k$ boxes.
\begin{itemize}
\item The apples are shared equally, so $k$ divides $60$.
\item The numbers of pears are $k$ different positive integers with sum $60$, so $60\ge1+2+\cdots+k=\dfrac{k(k+1)}{2}$. Since $\dfrac{11\cdot12}{2}=66>60$, we get $k\le10$.
\end{itemize}
And $k=10$ works: $6$ apples in each box, and pears $1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9,15$ (sum $60$). The maximum is $10$, answer $\boxed{\textbf{(D)}}$.
