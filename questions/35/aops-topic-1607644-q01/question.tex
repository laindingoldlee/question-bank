The Fibonacci numbers are defined by $F_0=0$, $F_1=1$ and $F_n=F_{n-1}+F_{n-2}$ for $n\ge2$. A googol is $10^{100}$ and a googolplex is $10^{\text{googol}}$. What is the last digit of $F_{\text{googolplex}}$?
