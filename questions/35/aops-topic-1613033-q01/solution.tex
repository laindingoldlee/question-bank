Since the five numbers are nonnegative with sum $14$, each is automatically at most $14$, so the upper bounds are no restriction.

\textbf{All solutions.} By stars and bars, the number of nonnegative solutions of $a+b+c+d+e=14$ is $\dbinom{14+4}{4}=\dbinom{18}4=3060$.

\textbf{Solutions with $a=7$.} Then $b+c+d+e=7$, with $\dbinom{7+3}{3}=\dbinom{10}3=120$ solutions.

So the answer is $3060-120=\boxed{2940}$.
