Let $a=30000=3\cdot10^4$. Then $a^3=27\cdot10^{12}$, so the dividend is $a^3-1$ and the divisor is $a-1$. By the difference of cubes,
\[\dfrac{a^3-1}{a-1}=a^2+a+1=900000000+30000+1=\boxed{900030001}.\]
