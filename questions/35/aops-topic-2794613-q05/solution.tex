Factor $ 50=2\cdot5^2 $, $ 175=5^2\cdot7 $, and $ 1925=5^2\cdot7\cdot11 $. The gcd condition makes $ n $ odd with exactly one factor 5. The lcm condition forces exactly one factor 11 and allows a factor 7 or none, with no other primes. Therefore $ n=55 $ or 385, summing to $\boxed{440}$.
