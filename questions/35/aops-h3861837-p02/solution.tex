A number has exactly three divisors precisely when it is a prime square. Archer\textquotesingle s two-digit possibilities are 25 and 49. Giant can know his number is smaller only if it is between 10 and 24. Archer can then know it is more than twice Giant\textquotesingle s only if his own number is 49. Giant\textquotesingle s divisibility restricts his number to 18. The difference is $\boxed{31}$.
