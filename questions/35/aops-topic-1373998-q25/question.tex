The number $5^{867}$ is between $2^{2013}$ and $2^{2014}$. How many pairs of integers $(m,n)$ are there such that $1\le m\le2012$ and
\[
5^n<2^m<2^{m+2}<5^{n+1}?
\]
$\textbf{(A) }278\qquad\textbf{(B) }279\qquad\textbf{(C) }280\qquad\textbf{(D) }281\qquad\textbf{(E) }282$

In particular, explain why between any two consecutive powers of $5$ there are either $2$ or $3$ powers of $2$.
