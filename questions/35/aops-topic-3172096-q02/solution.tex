As fractions of the original total, the remaining red and blue counts are $\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{1}{16}$ and $\dfrac{3}{4}\cdot\dfrac{3}{4}=\dfrac{9}{16}$. Thus red beans make up $\dfrac{1}{1+9}=\dfrac{1}{10}=\boxed{10\%}$ of the remainder.
