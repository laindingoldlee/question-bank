Let $AB=x$. Convexity and the right angles at $A,B$ place $E,C$ at height 3 above $AB$, making $ABCE$ a rectangle. Triangle $CDE$ is right isosceles with hypotenuse $CE=x$, so its area is $\dfrac{x^2}{4}$. Hence $3x+\dfrac{x^2}{4}=72$, or $(x-12)(x+24)=0$. Positivity gives $\boxed{12}$.
