The principal argument starts at $\pi$ and is halved at each step. Induction gives $z_n=\cos(\pi 2^{-n})+i\sin(\pi 2^{-n})$. Thus $z_n\to1$. Taking a limit of a sequence of different operations is not the same as asserting $\sqrt{-1}=-1$. Moreover, the principal square root of $1$ is $1$, not $-1$. The algebraic equation $w^2=1$ has two roots, but the principal square-root function selects just one.
