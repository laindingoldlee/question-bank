Scale to $A=(0,0)$, $B=(3,0)$, $C=(0,3)$. Then $D=(1,2)$. Projection of $B$ onto the line spanned by $(1,2)$ gives $E=(\dfrac{3}{5},\dfrac{6}{5})$. Vectors $ED=(\dfrac{2}{5},\dfrac{4}{5})$ and $EC=(-\dfrac{3}{5},\dfrac{9}{5})$ have dot product $\dfrac{6}{5}$ and product of lengths $\dfrac{6\sqrt2}{5}$. Thus the cosine is $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}$ and $\boxed{\angle CED=45^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
import geometry;
size(240);
pair A=(0,0),B=(3,0),C=(0,3),D=(1,2),E=(.6,1.2);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--D^^B--E^^C--E,dashed);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,N);label("$D$",D,NE);label("$E$",E,W);
\end{asy}
