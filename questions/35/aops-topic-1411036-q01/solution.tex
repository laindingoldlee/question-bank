We have $450=2\cdot3^2\cdot5^2$. A divisor $d$ of $450$ is a multiple of $3$ exactly when $d=3e$ with $e$ a divisor of $\dfrac{450}{3}=150=2\cdot3\cdot5^2$. So dividing by $3$ turns our divisors into \emph{all} the divisors of $150$, a set we know how to handle.

$150$ has $(1+1)(1+1)(2+1)=12$ divisors, and they pair up as $e\cdot\dfrac{150}{e}=150$, so their product is $150^{6}$ (six pairs). Therefore
\[
\prod d=\prod(3e)=3^{12}\cdot150^6=2^6\cdot3^{18}\cdot5^{12}=\boxed{6053445140625000000}.
\]
