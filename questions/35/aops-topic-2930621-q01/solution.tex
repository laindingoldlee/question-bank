$n-10(a-2b)=10a+b-10a+20b=21b$, which is a multiple of $7$. So $n\equiv10(a-2b)\pmod7$. Since $\gcd(10,7)=1$, $7\mid n$ iff $7\mid a-2b$. $\blacksquare$
