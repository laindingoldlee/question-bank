Two strips of width $n$ meters are cut off an $8$ meter by $10$ meter rectangle, one strip along a side of length $10$ and one along a side of length $8$, so that what remains is a smaller rectangle. How wide, in meters, must the strips be so that the area of the remaining rectangle is one-tenth of the area of the original rectangle?
