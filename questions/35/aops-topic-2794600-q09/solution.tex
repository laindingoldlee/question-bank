The centers form an equilateral triangle of side 2. The common region consists of that triangle and three circular segments. Each segment is a 60-degree sector of radius 2 minus the equilateral triangle, so the total is $ 3\cdot\dfrac{60}{360}\pi\cdot2^2-2\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}\cdot2^2=2\pi-2\sqrt3 $. Thus $(m,n,q)=(2,2,3)$ and $\boxed{m+n+q=7}$.
