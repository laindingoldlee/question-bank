The two bisected angles are adjacent along ray $CD$, so
\[\angle GCH=\angle ECD+\angle DCF=\dfrac{15^\circ}{2}+\dfrac{165^\circ}{2}=\dfrac{180^\circ}{2}=90^\circ .\]
(The bisectors of two supplementary adjacent angles are always perpendicular.)

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair C=(0,0), A=(-5.5,0), B=(5.5,0);
pair Dd=5*dir(165), G=4*dir(172.5), H=4.2*dir(82.5);
draw(A--B,linewidth(0.9));
draw(C--Dd); draw(C--G,blue); draw(C--4.9*dir(82.5),blue);
draw(G--H,red+linewidth(0.9));
draw(0.35*dir(82.5)--0.35*dir(82.5)+0.35*dir(172.5)--0.35*dir(172.5));
dot(C); dot(G); dot(H);
label("$A$",A,S); label("$B$",B,S); label("$C$",C,S); label("$D$",Dd,NW);
label("$G$",G,W); label("$H$",H,E);
label("$20$",(C+G)/2,S,fontsize(8pt)); label("$21$",(C+H)/2,E,fontsize(8pt)); label("$29$",(G+H)/2,NW,red+fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}

By the Pythagorean theorem in the right triangle $GCH$,
\[GH=\sqrt{20^2+21^2}=\sqrt{841}=\boxed{29}.\]
