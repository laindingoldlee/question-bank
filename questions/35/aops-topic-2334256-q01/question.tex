Point $C$ lies on line $AB$, between $A$ and $B$. A ray $CD$ is drawn so that $\angle ACD=15^\circ$; so line $AB$ and ray $CD$ form the angles $\angle ACD=15^\circ$ and $\angle DCB=165^\circ$. Rays $CE$ and $CF$ bisect $\angle ACD$ and $\angle DCB$ respectively. Point $G$ on ray $CE$ has $CG=20$, and point $H$ on ray $CF$ has $CH=21$. What is the length of $GH$?
