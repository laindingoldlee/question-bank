At $N=1$, both win surely. For $2\le N\le 7$, the losing probabilities are $\dfrac{(N-2)(N-1)}{72}$ for two dice and $\dfrac{N-1}{12}$ for one die. Equality would force $N=8$, outside this range. For $7\le N\le 12$, winning probabilities are $\dfrac{(13-N)(14-N)}{72}$ and $\dfrac{13-N}{12}$. Since $13-N>0$, equality forces $14-N=6$, giving $N=8$. Hence the full answer is $\boxed{N=1,8}$.
