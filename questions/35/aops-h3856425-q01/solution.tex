The equation is $10N+2=3(2\cdot10^{11}+N)$, giving $N=85714285714$. Factoring yields $N=2\cdot683\cdot953\cdot65843$, with all four factors prime (trial division up to each square root verifies this). The third-largest is $683$. Its only different prime digit permutation is $863$, since the other permutations end in an even digit. Subtracting six gives $\boxed{857}$, also prime. A difference of six is not the usual twin-prime difference of two.
