The probability of losing all of the first $N$ games is $\prod_{n=1}^{N}\left(1-\dfrac{1}{(n+1)^2}\right)=\prod_{n=1}^{N}\dfrac{n(n+2)}{(n+1)^2}=\dfrac{N+2}{2(N+1)}$. The events of losing the first $N$ games decrease to the event of never winning. Their probabilities therefore tend to $\dfrac{1}{2}$. The probability of at least one win is $1-\dfrac{1}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$.
