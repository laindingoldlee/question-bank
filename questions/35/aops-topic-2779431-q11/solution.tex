For occupied indices $x_1<\cdots<x_5$ with gaps at least 2, set $y_i=x_i-(i-1)$. Then $1\le y_1<\cdots<y_5\le8$, giving $\binom85=56$ seat sets. Assign students in $5!=120$ orders. Total $\boxed{6720}$.
