There are $9\cdot8\cdot7=504$ such numbers. By symmetry each digit $d\in\{1,\dots,9\}$ appears in the hundreds place in $8\cdot7=56$ of them, and likewise $56$ times in the tens place and $56$ times in the units place. So the sum is
\[
56\cdot(1+2+\cdots+9)\cdot(100+10+1)=56\cdot45\cdot111=\boxed{279720}.
\]
