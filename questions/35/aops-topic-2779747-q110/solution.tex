Factor the right side as $(x^2-y^2)^2(x^2+y^2)(x^4+x^2y^2+y^4)(x^4-x^2y^2+y^4)$. The squared difference factors into the two forms with $ c=2,-2 $. The middle quadratic has $ c=0 $. Since $(x^2+cxy+y^2)(x^2-cxy+y^2)=x^4+(2-c^2)x^2y^2+y^4 $, the remaining quartics correspond to $ c=\pm1 $ and $ c=\pm\sqrt3 $. Thus $\boxed{S=\{-2,-\sqrt3,-1,0,1,\sqrt3,2\}}$. Multiplying these factors verifies the identity.
