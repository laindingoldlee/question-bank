Prove or disprove each statement.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] A town has $100$ people and three groups, Loaps, Ikas and Reetas, with $99$ members each; everyone belongs to at least one group. Then at least one person is both a Loap and an Ika.
\item[(b)] A town has $100$ people and $26$ groups (Anas, Bnas, \dots, Znas) with $99$ members each; everyone belongs to at least one group. Then at least one person is both an Ana and an Ena.
\item[(c)] In the town of (b), at least two people are both Anas and Enas.
\end{enumerate}
