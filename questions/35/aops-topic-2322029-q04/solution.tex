Peggy can only impress her sisters along one branch: Burr does not intercept ($\dfrac16$), the sisters look at her ($\dfrac25$), they listen rather than brushing her off ($\dfrac45$), and she impresses them ($\dfrac7{20}$):
\[\dfrac16\cdot\dfrac25\cdot\dfrac45\cdot\dfrac{7}{20}=\dfrac{56}{3000}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{375}}\approx1.87\%.\]
