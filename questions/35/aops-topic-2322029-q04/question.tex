Angelica, Peggy and Eliza are walking through the streets of New York.
\begin{itemize}
\item With probability $\dfrac56$, Aaron Burr intercepts them (and then Peggy is ignored).
\item If he does not intercept them, Peggy gets frustrated at being left out. Then with probability $60\%$ Angelica and Eliza ignore her and keep walking. Otherwise (probability $40\%$) they look at her; then with probability $20\%$ they just say ``You don't understand'' and keep going, and otherwise they actually listen to her. If they listen, Peggy impresses them with probability $35\%$.
\end{itemize}
What is the probability that Peggy impresses her sisters?
