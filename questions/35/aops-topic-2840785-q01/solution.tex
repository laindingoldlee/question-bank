Line up the players ($16!$ ways) and pair positions $1$--$2$ into the first colour, $3$--$4$ into the second, and so on. Each arrangement arises $2^8$ times (swapping within teams). So there are $\dfrac{16!}{2^8}=\boxed{81729648000}$ arrangements.
