Six balls are placed into three boxes; boxes may be left empty. Count the number of ways in each of the four cases:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] indistinguishable balls, distinguishable boxes;
\item[(b)] distinguishable balls, distinguishable boxes;
\item[(c)] distinguishable balls, indistinguishable boxes;
\item[(d)] indistinguishable balls, indistinguishable boxes.
\end{enumerate}
For each case, also state the general method for $n$ balls and $k$ boxes.
