In triangle $AOB$,
\[\angle AOB=180^\circ-\dfrac A2-\dfrac B2=180^\circ-\dfrac{180^\circ-C}2=90^\circ+\dfrac C2>90^\circ.\ \blacksquare\]
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(5,0),C=(1.8,3);real la=length(C-B),lb=length(C-A),lc=length(B-A);pair O=(la*A+lb*B+lc*C)/(la+lb+lc);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--O--B,dashed);dot(O);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,N);label("$O$",O,N);
\end{asy}
