Condition on the four unlabeled positions. The three possible pairings into two chords are equally likely, and exactly one pairing joins opposite vertices of the resulting convex quadrilateral. Only this pairing crosses. Thus the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{3}}$.

usepackage("amsmath");

pair A=dir(20),C=dir(100),B=dir(190),D=dir(290);draw(unitcircle);draw(A--B);draw(C--D);label("$A$",A,E);label("$B$",B,W);label("$C$",C,N);label("$D$",D,S);
\end{asy}
