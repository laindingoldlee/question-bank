There are $\binom62=15$ equally likely pairs. The product is a multiple of $10$ when the pair contains $10$ ($5$ pairs), or contains $5$ and an even number other than $10$, i.e.\ $\{5,2\}$ or $\{5,6\}$ ($2$ pairs). So the probability is $\dfrac{7}{15}$: $\boxed{\dfrac{7}{15}}$.
