Let $A$ be the set of all $8$-digit strings in base $3$, that is, strings of $8$ digits each equal to $0$, $1$ or $2$ (leading zeros allowed, such as $00120010$). Prove that among any $4$ elements of $A$, some two coincide in at least $2$ places (they have the same digit in at least two of the eight positions).
