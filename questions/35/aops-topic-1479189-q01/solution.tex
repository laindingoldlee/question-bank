The congruence says that $18$ divides $943n-617n=326n$. Since $326=18\cdot18+2$, we have $326n\equiv2n\pmod{18}$, so the condition is $18\mid2n$, that is, $9\mid n$.

So the solutions are exactly the multiples of $9$: $n\equiv0\pmod 9$, and the smallest positive one is $\boxed{9}$.

(Note that one may not simply ``cancel'' $n$: the congruence holds for some $n$ even though $617\not\equiv943\pmod{18}$.)
