Do there exist positive integers $a\ne b$ such that all four of the quadratic mean $\sqrt{\dfrac{a^2+b^2}{2}}$, the arithmetic mean $\dfrac{a+b}{2}$, the geometric mean $\sqrt{ab}$ and the harmonic mean $\dfrac{2ab}{a+b}$ are integers? Prove your answer. You may use the following theorem of Fermat, but you should also prove it:

\emph{Lemma.} There are no positive integers $x,y,z$ with $x^4-y^4=z^2$.
