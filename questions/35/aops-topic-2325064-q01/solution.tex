At the right end, $f\left(\dfrac{10}3\right)=\dfrac{1000}{27}+\dfrac{100}{9}-30+1=\dfrac{1000+300-783}{27}=\dfrac{517}{27}$. We show that this is the maximum. Since $x=\dfrac{10}3$ is a root of $\dfrac{517}{27}-f(x)$, dividing by $10-3x$ gives
\[\dfrac{517}{27}-f(x)=\dfrac{(10-3x)\left(9x^2+39x+49\right)}{27}.\]
(Expand to check: $(10-3x)(9x^2+39x+49)=-27x^3-27x^2+243x+490$, and $517-27f(x)=517-27x^3-27x^2+243x-27$.) The quadratic $9x^2+39x+49$ has discriminant $39^2-4\cdot9\cdot49=1521-1764<0$, so it is always positive, and $10-3x\ge0$ on the interval. Hence $f(x)\le\dfrac{517}{27}$ for all $x$ in the interval, with equality at $x=\dfrac{10}3$.

The maximum height is $\boxed{\dfrac{517}{27}}\approx19.15$. (The local maximum of $f$, at $x=\dfrac{-1-2\sqrt7}{3}\approx-2.10$ where $f'(x)=3x^2+2x-9=0$, has the smaller value $\dfrac{110+112\sqrt7}{27}\approx15.05$.)
