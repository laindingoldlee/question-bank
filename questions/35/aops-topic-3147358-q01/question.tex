In triangle $ABC$, let $I$ and $O$ be the incenter and circumcenter. Ray $AO$ internally bisects $\angle IAC$. Given $AB+AC=21$ and $BC=7$, write $AI=\dfrac{m}{n}$ in lowest terms and find $m+n$.
