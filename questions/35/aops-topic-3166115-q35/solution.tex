For every fixed $E$, the number $X=N-1$ has the same binomial distribution with parameters $29,\dfrac{1}{30}$. Thus expected score is proportional to $E$, uniquely maximized by $\boxed{E=30}$. To evaluate it, use $\dfrac{1}{X+1}=\int_0^1t^X\,dt$, giving $E[\dfrac{1}{X+1}]=\int_0^1(\dfrac{29+t}{30})^{29}\,dt=1-(\dfrac{29}{30})^{30}$. Hence the optimal expectation is $\boxed{15\left(1-(\dfrac{29}{30})^{30}\right)}$.
