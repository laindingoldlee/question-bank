You choose an integer $E$ from $1$ to $30$. Twenty-nine other teams independently choose uniformly from the same set. Your score is $\dfrac{E}{2N}$, where $N$ is the total number of teams, including yours, choosing $E$. Find the choice maximizing expected score and that expected score.
