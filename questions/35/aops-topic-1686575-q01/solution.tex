George drank $8-3=5$ pounds of juice, which is $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of the juice. So the full amount of juice weighs $5\div\dfrac{2}{3}=\dfrac{15}{2}=7.5$ pounds, and the empty bottle weighs $8-7.5=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$ pound. Check: the bottle plus the remaining $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of the juice weighs $0.5+2.5=3$ pounds.
