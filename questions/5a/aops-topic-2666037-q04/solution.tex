$3^{3n+3}=27^{n+1}=(1+26)^{n+1}$. By the binomial theorem, every term from the third on contains $26^2=676=4\cdot169$, so
\[27^{n+1}\equiv1+26(n+1)=26n+27\pmod{169}.\]
Hence $169\mid3^{3n+3}-26n-27$. $\blacksquare$
