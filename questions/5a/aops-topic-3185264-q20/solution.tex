Every solution must satisfy the squared equation $(2x-6)(x+6)^2=(x-11)(3x-7)^2$. Moving terms and factoring gives $(x-19)(7x^2-26x+17)=0$. The quadratic roots are $\dfrac{13\pm5\sqrt2}{7}$, both irrational. Thus the only possible integer is $19$, which satisfies the original equation because both sides equal $2$. Therefore $\boxed{19}$.
