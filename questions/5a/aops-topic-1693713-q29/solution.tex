Let the numbers be $x>y>0$. Then
\[
x^2-y^2=2(x-y)^2\ \Longrightarrow\ (x-y)(x+y)=2(x-y)^2 .
\]
Since $x\ne y$, divide by $x-y$: $x+y=2(x-y)$, so $x=3y$. The ratio of the smaller to the larger is $\dfrac {y}{x}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{3}}$.
