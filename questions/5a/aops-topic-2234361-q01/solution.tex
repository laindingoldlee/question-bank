Let the numbers be $x$ and $y$. Then
\[(x+1)(y-1)-xy=y-x-1=2020,\qquad\text{so}\qquad y-x=2021.\]
Now
\[xy-(x-1)(y+1)=xy-\left(xy+x-y-1\right)=y-x+1=2022.\]
The product decreases by $\boxed{2022}$.
