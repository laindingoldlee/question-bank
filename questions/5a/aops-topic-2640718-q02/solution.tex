Let the side be $s$ and $AP=BQ=x$. Then $AB=8-2x=s$, so $x=\dfrac{8-s}{2}$. The configuration is symmetric: right triangles $BQC$ and $APH$ have hypotenuse $s$ and leg $x$, so $QC=PH$; then $RD=6-s-QC=6-s-PH=SG$, so right triangles $DRE$ and $GSF$ (hypotenuse $s$) give $RE=SF$, and $EF=8-2\,RE=s$ gives $RE=x$. Hence right triangles $BQC$ and $DRE$ have equal hypotenuses and equal legs $x$, so $QC=RD$, and $CD=6-2\,QC=s$ gives $QC=RD=\dfrac{6-s}{2}$. Side $\overline{BC}$ is the hypotenuse of right triangle $BQC$:
\[\left(\dfrac{8-s}2\right)^2+\left(\dfrac{6-s}2\right)^2=s^2\iff(8-s)^2+(6-s)^2=4s^2\iff s^2+14s-50=0.\]
The positive root is $s=-7+\sqrt{99}=-7+3\sqrt{11}\approx2.95$, giving $x\approx2.53<4$. So $k+m+n=-7+3+11=\boxed{7}$.
