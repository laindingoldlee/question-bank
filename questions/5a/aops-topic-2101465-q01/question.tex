Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle, and let $AX$ and $AY$ be rays such that the angles $XAB$ and $YAC$ have no interior point in common with triangle $ABC$ and $\angle XAB=\angle YAC<90^\circ$. Let $B_0$ and $C_0$ be the feet of the perpendiculars from $B$ to $AX$ and from $C$ to $AY$, respectively. If $M$ is the midpoint of $BC$, prove that $MB_0=MC_0$.
