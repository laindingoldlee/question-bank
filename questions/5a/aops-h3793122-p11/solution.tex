For $x\ge0$, the expression is $x(x-1)^2(x-5)$. Every integer from zero through five qualifies; at six it is 150, and all factors are nonnegative and increasing thereafter. For $x=-t<0$, it is $t(t^2-1)(t-5)$. Integers $1\le t\le5$ qualify, while at six it is 210 and increases thereafter. Thus the integer solutions are $-5,-4,\ldots,5$, whose sum is $\boxed0$.
