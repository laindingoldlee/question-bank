Let the legs be $a,b$ and the hypotenuse $c$. The inradius formula gives $a+b-c=2$, so $c=a+b-2$. Substituting into $c^2=a^2+b^2$ yields $(a-2)(b-2)=2$. Both factors are positive because $c>\max(a,b)$ implies $2=a+b-c<\min(a,b)$. AM--GM gives $a+b\ge4+2\sqrt2$. The same equation gives $\dfrac{ab}{2}=a+b-1$, so the area is at least $3+2\sqrt2$. Equality occurs at $a=b=2+\sqrt2$, yielding $\boxed{3+2\sqrt2}$.
