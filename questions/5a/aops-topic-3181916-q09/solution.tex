For $|r|<1$, the geometric sum and its derivative give $\sum_{k\ge1}r^k=\dfrac{r}{1-r}$ and $\sum_{k\ge1}kr^k=\dfrac{r}{(1-r)^2}$. Hence the odd-numerator contribution is $\dfrac{22}{100}-\dfrac{1}{10}=\dfrac{3}{25}$ and the even-numerator contribution is $\dfrac{26}{144}=\dfrac{13}{72}$. Their sum is $\dfrac{541}{1800}$, already reduced, so $\boxed{a=541}$.
