Each turn has score $ 3+4r $ with $ r\in\{0,1,2\}$. The total is $ 18+4s $ where $ 0\le s\le12 $. Every integer $ s $ in this interval is a sum of six numbers from $\{0,1,2\}$: use as many twos as possible and at most one one. Thus $\boxed{13}$ scores occur.
