Andy and Bethany have a rectangular array of numbers with $40$ rows and $75$ columns. Andy adds the numbers in each row; the average of his $40$ sums is $A$. Bethany adds the numbers in each column; the average of her $75$ sums is $B$. Assuming that the numbers in the array do not add up to $0$, what is the value of $\dfrac {A}{B}$?
