Let $T$ be the sum of all $3000$ numbers in the array. Andy's $40$ row sums add up to $T$, and so do Bethany's $75$ column sums. Hence
\[A=\dfrac{T}{40},\qquad B=\dfrac{T}{75},\qquad \dfrac {A}{B}=\dfrac{75}{40}=\boxed{\dfrac{15}{8}}.\]
