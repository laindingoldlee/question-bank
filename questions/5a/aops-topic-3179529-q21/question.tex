A circular lake of radius $2$ km has a concentric circular island of radius $1$ km. Two racers travel between opposite ends of a diameter. Chad swims at $2$ km per hour and runs on the island at $5$ km per hour; Jordan swims only. Both choose fastest routes and tie. Find Jordan's swimming speed.
