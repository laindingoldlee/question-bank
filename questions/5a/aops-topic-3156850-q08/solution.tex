The string is ZW YXV, namely $ Z,W,Y,X,V $. Reversing gives $ V,X,Y,W,Z $, and swapping $ W $ left twice gives $ V,W,X,Y,Z $, so three moves suffice. Two adjacent swaps cannot remove the original eight inversions. One reversal together with one adjacent swap cannot suffice, since the reversed string has two inversions; this remains true if the swap precedes reversal, because reversal converts an adjacent swap to another adjacent swap. Two reversals restore the original. Thus the minimum is $\boxed3 $.
