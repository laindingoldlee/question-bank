For $ n\ge1 $, $ x_n=1-10^{-n}$ terminates in decimal. Its reduced denominator is $ 10^n $, so it cannot terminate in base three, whose terminating denominators have only the prime factor $ 3 $. A rational nonterminating expansion is eventually periodic. The sequence $ x_n $ approaches $ 1 $ from below. The number $ 1 $ itself terminates in both bases and is not eligible. Thus the supremum is $\boxed1 $, but there is no greatest eligible number.
