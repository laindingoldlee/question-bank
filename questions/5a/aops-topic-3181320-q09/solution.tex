Let $E$ be the expected number starting a fresh experiment. Its first attempt contributes one. With probability $\dfrac{3}{8}$ an accident causes one extra attempt, which succeeds with probability $\dfrac{3}{4}$ and then restarts the process. An immediate success, probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, also restarts it. Therefore $E=1+\dfrac{3}{8}(1+\dfrac{3}{4}E)+\dfrac{1}{2}E$. Solving gives $\dfrac{7}{32}E=\dfrac{11}{8}$, so $\boxed{E=\dfrac{44}{7}}$.
