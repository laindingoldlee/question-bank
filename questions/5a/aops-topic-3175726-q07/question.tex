Choose a side of a square uniformly. Independently choose $X,Y$ uniformly on that side, and choose $Z$ uniformly on the whole perimeter. If the probability that triangle $XYZ$ contains the center is $\dfrac{a}{b}$ in lowest terms, find $a+b$. Degenerate triangles count as failures.
