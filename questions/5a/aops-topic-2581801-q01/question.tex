Triangle $ABC$ has acute angles at $B$ and $C$. Let $a=BC$, and let $h=AH$ be the altitude from $A$, with foot $H$ on side $BC$. Construct the square $AHGF$ on the side of line $AH$ facing $B$, so that $G$ lies on line $BC$ and $AF\parallel BC$. Let line $CF$ meet side $AB$ at $E$. Let $D$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $E$ to $BC$, let the line through $E$ parallel to $BC$ meet side $AC$ at $X$, and let $Y$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $X$ to $BC$.

Prove that $EXYD$ is a square inscribed in triangle $ABC$ (with $D$ and $Y$ on side $BC$), and show that its side length is $\dfrac{ah}{a+h}$.
