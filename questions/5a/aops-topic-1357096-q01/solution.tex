Let the common ratio be $r$ (nonzero, since $4\ne0$). Then $w=\dfrac{4}{r^2}$, $x=\dfrac{4}{r}$, $y=4r$, $z=4r^2$, so
\[
wxyz=\dfrac{4}{r^2}\cdot\dfrac{4}{r}\cdot4r\cdot4r^2=4^4=\boxed{256}.
\]
Equivalently, terms symmetric about the middle term have product equal to the square of the middle term: $wz=xy=16$.
