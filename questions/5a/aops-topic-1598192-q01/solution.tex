Let the dredge lift $x$ tons per minute. Then
\[
\dfrac{10000}{x}-\dfrac{10000}{x+50}=10 .
\]
Multiplying by $x(x+50)$: $10000(x+50)-10000x=10x(x+50)$, i.e.\ $500000=10x^2+500x$, so
\[
x^2+50x-50000=0\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad (x-200)(x+250)=0 .
\]
Since $x>0$, the dredge lifts $\boxed{200}$ tons per minute. Check: $10000\div200=50$ minutes and $10000\div250=40$ minutes.
