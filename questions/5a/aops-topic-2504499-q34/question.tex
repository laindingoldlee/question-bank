Right triangle $ABC$ has $AB=AC=6$ cm. Circular arcs centred at $A$, $B$ and $C$ are drawn inside the triangle so that the arc centred at $A$ is tangent to $BC$ and the arcs centred at $B$ and $C$ are tangent to the arc centred at $A$. Find the area inside the triangle but outside the three arcs, to the nearest hundredth.
