The arc at $A$ has radius equal to the distance from $A$ to $BC$, namely $3\sqrt2$, and is a quarter circle. The arcs at $B$ and $C$ have radius $6-3\sqrt2$ and angle $45^\circ$ each.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(6,0),C=(0,6);real r=3*sqrt(2),s=6-3*sqrt(2);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(arc(A,r,0,90));draw(arc(B,s,135,180));draw(arc(C,s,270,315));label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NW);label("$3\sqrt2$",r*dir(30),NE);
\end{asy}

The area is
\[18-\dfrac14\pi(3\sqrt2)^2-2\cdot\dfrac18\pi(6-3\sqrt2)^2=18-\dfrac92\pi-\dfrac\pi4(54-36\sqrt2)\approx\boxed{1.44}\text{ cm}^2.\]
