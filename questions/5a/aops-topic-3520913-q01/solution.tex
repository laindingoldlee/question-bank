The number of positive integer triples summing to $42$ is $\binom{41}{2}=820$. Each entry is at most $40$, so the upper limit of $69$ excludes none. There are $69^3$ equally likely rolls. The probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{820}{328509}}$.
