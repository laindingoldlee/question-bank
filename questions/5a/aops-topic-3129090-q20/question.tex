A climber selects any finite sequence of 2-step and 3-step jumps whose lengths sum to 10. Each attempted jump of length $d$ succeeds independently with probability $\dfrac{1}{d}$; failure leaves the climber in place, and that jump is retried until success. Find the expected number of attempts to complete the sequence.
