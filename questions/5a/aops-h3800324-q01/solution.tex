The larger triangle has height $6\sqrt3$ and its centroid is $2\sqrt3$ above its base. In centred coordinates, its top vertex has height $4\sqrt3$, while the top horizontal side of the downward smaller triangle has height $\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}$. That line cuts off a small equilateral corner triangle of height $\dfrac{5\sqrt3}{2}$, hence side length $5$. By threefold symmetry, all three larger corners are cut off in the same way.
Each original side retains length $12-5-5=2$, and each of the three new cut edges has length $5$. Thus the intersection perimeter is $\boxed{3\cdot2+3\cdot5=21}$.
