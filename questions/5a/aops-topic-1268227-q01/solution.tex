Such a number is $\overline{abcde}$ with $a\in\{2,3,4,5,6\}$ ($5$ choices), $b$ any digit ($10$), $c=5$, $d$ any digit ($10$) and $e$ odd ($5$). The count is $5\cdot10\cdot1\cdot10\cdot5=\boxed{2500}$.
