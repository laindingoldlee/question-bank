Define a map $f$ on the points $(x,y)$ of the plane with $x\ne5$ by
\[f(x,y)=\begin{cases}(x+2,\,y)&\text{if }x>5,\\(x-4,\,y+2)&\text{if }x<5.\end{cases}\]
A robot starts at a point $(x_0,y_0)$ with $x_0\ne5$ and repeatedly moves from its current point $P$ to $f(P)$. Prove that, whatever the starting point, the robot visits infinitely many different points.
