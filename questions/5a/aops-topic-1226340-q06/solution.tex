By inclusion--exclusion: $3^4-3\cdot2^4+3\cdot1^4=81-48+3=\boxed{36}$. (Or: choose the pair mapped to the same value, $\dbinom42=6$ ways, then match the three classes to the three values, $3!=6$ ways.)
