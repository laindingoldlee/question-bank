Put $ k=f(1)$. The relation gives $ f(0)=2k $, $ f(2)=4k $, $ f(-2)=8k $, and $ f(6)=16k $. The expression is therefore $\boxed{24k^2\ (k\in\mathbb Z)}$. Every integer $ k $ is attainable: assign $ f(1)=k $ and arbitrary integer values to the other odd inputs, then for an even input $ n $ define $ f(n)=2f(1-\dfrac{n}{2})$. Repeated replacement by $ 1-\dfrac{n}{2}$ eventually reaches an odd integer. Indeed its distance from $\dfrac{2}{3}$ is halved at each step, whereas an integer always has distance at least $\dfrac{1}{3}$ from $\dfrac{2}{3}$; an endless even chain is impossible. This defines an integer-valued function satisfying exactly the required relation.
