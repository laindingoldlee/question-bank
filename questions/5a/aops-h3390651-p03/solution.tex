Every section is traveled uphill once and downhill once. Thus each mile of road contributes $\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{12}=\dfrac{1}{3}$ hour to the round-trip time. The total time is $\dfrac{50}{60}+\dfrac{3}{2}=\dfrac{7}{3}$ hours, so the one-way road distance is
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{\dfrac{7}{3}}{\dfrac{1}{3}}=7\text{ miles}}.
\]
