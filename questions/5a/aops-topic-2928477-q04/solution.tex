The equations are $a=T(b+c)$ and its two cyclic companions. Adding gives $a+b+c=2T(a+b+c)$. If the sum is nonzero, then $T=\dfrac{1}{2}$, realized by $a=b=c=1$. If the sum is zero, each denominator is the negative of its numerator; nonzero denominators force all three numerators nonzero, so $T=-1$. This is realized by $(a,b,c)=(1,1,-2)$. Thus $\boxed{T\in\{\dfrac{1}{2},-1\}}$.
