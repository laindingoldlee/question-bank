Write $i=e^{\dfrac{i\pi}{2}}$. Its three cube roots are $e^{i(\dfrac{\pi}{6}+\dfrac{2\pi k}{3})}$, $k=0,1,2$. Explicitly these are $\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt3+i}{2},\dfrac{-\sqrt3+i}{2},-i}$. Cubing each gives $i$, and a cubic has no further roots.
