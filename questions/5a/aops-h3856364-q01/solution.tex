Divide by the nonzero integer $n$, obtaining $x=\dfrac{a}{(nb)}$, then reduce numerator and denominator by their gcd. The answers, in order, are
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{1}{6},\ \dfrac{1}{30},\ \dfrac{2}{507},\ \dfrac{3}{16},\
\dfrac{7}{90},\ \dfrac{5}{36},\ \dfrac{1}{126},\ \dfrac{3}{64},\

\]
The solving operation is division of both sides by $n$; the final simplification is reduction to lowest terms.
