If the disc radius is $R$, Bob's distance to its centre is $R-1$. His perpendicular walk of length $3$ forms a right triangle with hypotenuse $R$, so $(R-1)^2+9=R^2$. Thus $2R=10$ and $\boxed{R=5\text{ metres}}$.
