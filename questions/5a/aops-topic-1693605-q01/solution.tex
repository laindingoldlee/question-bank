The fraction of copper in the alloy is $\dfrac{3.5}{4.5}=\dfrac{7}{9}$. So $18.9$ g of alloy contains $\dfrac{7}{9}\cdot18.9=\boxed{14.7\text{ g}}$ of copper. (Alternatively, $18.9=4.2\cdot4.5$, so the copper is $4.2\cdot3.5=14.7$ g.)
