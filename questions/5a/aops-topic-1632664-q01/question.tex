Consider $n^3-5n^2+8n-4=(n-1)(n-2)^2$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What is the sum of the roots counted with multiplicity, and what is the sum of the distinct roots?
\item[(b)] When a competition problem asks for ``the sum of the roots'' of a polynomial, which of these is normally meant?
\end{enumerate}
