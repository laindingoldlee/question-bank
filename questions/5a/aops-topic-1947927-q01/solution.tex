Count days after November $6$, $2019$. November has $30$ days, so November $30$ is $24$ days later and December $31$ is $24+31=55$ days later. Hence January $1$, $2020$ is day $56$ after the first Math Day.

Math Days fall on the days that are multiples of $10$ after November $6$. The first multiple of $10$ that is at least $56$ is $60$, which is $4$ days after January $1$. So the first Math Day of $2020$ is $\boxed{\text{January }5,\ 2020}$. (The Math Days are Nov $6,16,26$, Dec $6,16,26$, Jan $5$.)
