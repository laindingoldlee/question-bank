Let iron have density $f$ and copper density $c$, in g per cm$^3$. Then
\[
4.5f+8c=101.5,\qquad 3f-2c=6.8 .
\]
Multiply the second equation by $4$: $12f-8c=27.2$. Adding this to the first gives $16.5f=128.7$, so $f=7.8$. Then $2c=3\cdot7.8-6.8=16.6$, so $c=8.3$.

Iron: $\boxed{7.8}$ grams per cm$^3$; copper: $\boxed{8.3}$ grams per cm$^3$. (Check: $4.5\cdot7.8+8\cdot8.3=35.1+66.4=101.5$.)
