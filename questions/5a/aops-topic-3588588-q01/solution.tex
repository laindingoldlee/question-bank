If $n\le50$, every $100$ consecutive integers contain at least two multiples of $n$. If $51\le n\le66$, both $2n$ and $3n$ lie between $101$ and $200$. For $n=67$, only $134$ lies in the interval, since $67<101$ and $201>200$. Hence the minimum is $\boxed{67}$.
