With $x=5^2=25$, the number is $x^4+x^2+1=\left(x^2+1\right)^2-x^2=\left(x^2+x+1\right)\left(x^2-x+1\right)$:
\[5^8+5^4+1=(625+25+1)(625-25+1)=651\cdot601.\]
Here $651=3\cdot7\cdot31$, and $601$ is prime (it is not divisible by $2,3,5,7,11,13,17,19,23$, and $25^2>601$). So
\[5^8+5^4+1=\boxed{3\cdot7\cdot31\cdot601}.\]
