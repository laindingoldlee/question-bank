Alexi rolled four standard dice and lined them up to create a $4$-digit number. He removed two dice from the line and rolled them again. Alexi then returned each re-rolled die to its original position in the line, thereby creating a new $4$-digit number. What is the probability that the new $4$-digit number is greater than the original $4$-digit number? Express your answer as a common fraction.
