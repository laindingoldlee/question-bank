Let $u=\dfrac1x$ and $v=\dfrac1y$. Then $u+v=4$ and $2u+3v=7$. Subtracting twice the first equation from the second gives $v=-1$, so $u=5$. Hence $x=\dfrac15$ and $y=-1$, and
\[\dfrac{7-4y}{x}=\dfrac{7+4}{\dfrac15}=\boxed{55}.\]
