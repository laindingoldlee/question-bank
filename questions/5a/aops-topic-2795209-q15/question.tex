A regular octahedron has six vertices: a top vertex, a bottom vertex, and four middle vertices forming a square, each adjacent to its two neighbours in the square and to the top and bottom. How many walks along exactly seven edges start at the top vertex and end at the bottom vertex? (Edges and vertices may be repeated.)
