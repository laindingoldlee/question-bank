If $n$ is a multiple of $3^k$ with $k\ge1$, then $n$ is a multiple of $3$; conversely every multiple of $3$ is a multiple of $3^1$. So the funky numbers are exactly the multiples of $3$. From $1$ to $2018$ there are $\left\lfloor\dfrac{2018}{3}\right\rfloor=\boxed{672}$ of them.

(With $k=0$ allowed, every positive integer would be funky, since $3^0=1$ divides everything; that is why the definition requires $k\ge1$.)
