The angle-addition formula gives $\sqrt3\sin x+\cos x=2\sin(x+\dfrac{\pi}{6})$. This is at most 2, attained when $x=\dfrac{\pi}{3}$. Thus the maximum is $\boxed2$.

\end{asy}
