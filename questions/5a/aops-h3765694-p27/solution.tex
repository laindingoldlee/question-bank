A divisor with digit sum 9 forces $9\mid N$. A divisor with digit sum 7 is coprime to 9; call it $d$. If $N<288$, then $9d\le N<288$, so $d\le31$, and its only possibilities are $7,16,25$. Thus $N$ must be one of $63,126,189,252,144,225$. The first four and 225 have no divisor of digit sum 8 (listing their divisors verifies this); 144 has no divisor of digit sum 5. None works. For $N=288$, divisors $1,2,3,4,32,6,16,8,9$ have digit sums $1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9$. Therefore the least value is $\boxed{288}$.
