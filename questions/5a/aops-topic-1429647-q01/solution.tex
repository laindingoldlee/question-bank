Write both bounds with denominator $27$: $\dfrac{2}{3}=\dfrac{18}{27}$ and $\dfrac{8}{9}=\dfrac{24}{27}$. So $18<k<24$, that is $k=19,20,21,22,23$, and their sum is
\[
19+20+21+22+23=5\cdot21=\boxed{105}.
\]
