Let $N$ be the number of ordered $n$-tuples $(a_1,a_2,\dots,a_n)$, over all $n\ge1$, of integers with $a_i\in\{1,2,\dots,1001\}$ for all $i$ and
\[\sum_{j=1}^{n}a_j=2004 .\]
Find the remainder when $N$ is divided by $1000$.

$\textbf{(A)}\ 001 \qquad\textbf{(B)}\ 489 \qquad\textbf{(C)}\ 753 \qquad\textbf{(D)}\ 969 \qquad\textbf{(E)}\ 993$
