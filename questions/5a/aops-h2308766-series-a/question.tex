Four players Alan, Bob, Cam, Dan occupy cyclic positions $0,1,2,3$. Add their selected bits and reduce modulo four to select the loser. (a) If all bits are independent and fair, who is least likely to lose? (b) As Bob, choose a bit to minimize your loss chance when the others are fair. (c) Do the same if Cam\textquotesingle s bit is known to be one. (d) With Alan fixed at one, characterize pure-strategy Nash equilibria for the other three players, each minimizing their own loss indicator.
