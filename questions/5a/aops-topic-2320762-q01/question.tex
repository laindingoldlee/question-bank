Five nebulas are arranged in a row: a purple one, a blue one, a green one and two identical pink ones. The purple and blue nebulas may not be next to each other. Also, $8$ billion stars are shared among the nebulas so that each nebula gets a whole number of billions of stars, at least $1$ billion. In how many ways can the nebulas be arranged and the stars shared?
