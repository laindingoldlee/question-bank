Subtract $2l$ from both sides and divide by $2$:
\[w=\dfrac{P-2l}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{P}{2}-l}.\]
(Both forms are the same; an answer such as $-1+\dfrac{1}{2}P$ is not, since it drops the variable $l$.)
