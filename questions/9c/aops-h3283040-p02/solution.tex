Suppose the complete finite list is $p_1,\ldots,p_k$. Form $N=4p_1\cdots p_k-1>1$, which is $3$ modulo $4$ and divisible by none of the listed primes. Every prime factor of $N$ is odd. If they were all $1$ modulo $4$, their product would be $1$ modulo $4$, impossible. Thus $N$ has a prime factor congruent to $3$ modulo $4$ outside the list, a contradiction.
