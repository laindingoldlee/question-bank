The sequence $3,\ 8,\ 23,\ 68,\ 203,\ \dots$ follows a rule of the form $a_{n+1}=p\,a_n+q$ for constants $p$ and $q$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find $p$ and $q$, and the next term of the sequence.
\item[(b)] Find a formula for the $n$th term $a_n$.
\end{enumerate}
