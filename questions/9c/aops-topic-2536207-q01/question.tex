The set of positive integers is the union of two disjoint subsets $\{f(1),f(2),f(3),\dots\}$ and $\{g(1),g(2),g(3),\dots\}$, where
\[f(1)<f(2)<f(3)<\cdots,\qquad g(1)<g(2)<g(3)<\cdots,\qquad\text{and}\qquad g(n)=f(f(n))+1\ \text{for all } n\ge1.\]
Determine $f(240)$.
