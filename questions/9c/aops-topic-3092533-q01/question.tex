Thirty-two cards initially occupy positions 1 through 32 in order. At stage $j=0,1,2,3,4$, split the row into consecutive blocks of length $2^{j+1}$. Independently for each block and stage, swap its two halves with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. What is the probability card 1 finishes in position 27?
