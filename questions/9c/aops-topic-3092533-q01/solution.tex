Number positions from 0 through 31 in five-bit binary. At stage $j$, the swap affecting card 1 toggles precisely bit $j$ of its position. Each bit is therefore independently 0 or 1 with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, regardless of the other block swaps. Every final position has probability $(\dfrac{1}{2})^5$, so the requested probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{32}}$.
