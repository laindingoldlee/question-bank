The volume of a sphere of radius $r$ is $\dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(2.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real r=1;
draw(circle((0,0),r),linewidth(0.9));
draw(shift(0,0)*scale(r,0.3*r)*arc((0,0),1,180,360)); draw(shift(0,0)*scale(r,0.3*r)*arc((0,0),1,0,180),dashed);
draw((0,0)--r*dir(40),red); dot((0,0));
label("$r$",0.5*r*dir(40),NW,red); label("$V=1$",(0,-r),S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Setting $\dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3=1$ gives $r^3=\dfrac{3}{4\pi}$, so
\[
r=\boxed{\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{3}{4\pi}}}\approx0.620 .
\]
