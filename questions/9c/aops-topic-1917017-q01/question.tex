A symmetrical cross with four equal arms (a square with four congruent rectangles attached to its four sides, each rectangle as wide as the square) has area $2016\ \text{cm}^2$, and all of its sides have integer lengths in centimetres. What is the smallest possible perimeter of the cross, in centimetres?
