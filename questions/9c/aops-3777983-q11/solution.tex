Let the small radius be $r$, so the large radius is $2r$. Put $T=(0,0),A=(-9,0),B=(16,0)$ and the small centre at $(0,r)$. The large centre is $(\dfrac{7}{2} , h)$ because it lies over the chord midpoint. Tangency gives $\dfrac{49}{4} + (h-r)^2 = r^2$, or $h^2 + \dfrac{49}{4} = 2 h r$. The radius to $A$ gives $4 r^2 = h^2 + \dfrac{625}{4}$. Subtracting yields $4r^2-2hr=144$, so $h = 2 r - \dfrac{72}{r}$. Substitution gives $r^2 = \dfrac{20736}{527}$. Hence the large radius is $\boxed{\dfrac{288}{\sqrt{527}}}$.

\end{asy}
