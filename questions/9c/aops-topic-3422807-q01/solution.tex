Parallelism gives $a=\sqrt3$. The distance between lines $\sqrt3x-y+b=0$ and $\sqrt3x-y-1=0$ is $\dfrac{|b+1|}{\sqrt{3+1}}=\dfrac{|b+1|}{2}$. Setting it equal to $3$ gives $b=5$ or $-7$. The smaller product is $\boxed{-7\sqrt3}$.
\begin{asy}
import geometry;
usepackage("amsmath");
size(260);
real m=sqrt(3); draw((-2,-2*m-1)--(2,2*m-1));draw((-2,-2*m+5)--(2,2*m+5));draw((-2,-2*m-7)--(2,2*m-7));label("$y=\sqrt3x-1$",(2,2*m-1),E);label("$b=5$",(2,2*m+5),E);label("$b=-7$",(2,2*m-7),E);
\end{asy}
