Each intersection uses at least one pair of lines, and each pair has at most one intersection, giving at most $\binom92=36$. The bound is attained when no two lines are parallel and no three meet at a point. For example take $y=ix+i^2$ for $i=0,1,\ldots,8$. Lines $i,j$ meet at $(-i-j,-ij)$. A third index $k$ could lie there only if $k^2-k(i+j)+ij=(k-i)(k-j)=0$, so $k=i$ or $k=j$. Hence all $\boxed{36}$ pairwise intersections are distinct.
