Jack picks an integer from $1$ to $10$ inclusive, and Jill independently picks an integer from $1$ to $10$ inclusive (possibly the same as Jack's), each uniformly at random. What is the probability that the product of their two numbers has an even number of positive divisors and more than two positive divisors?
