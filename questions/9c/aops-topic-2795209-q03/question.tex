Consider all fractions whose numerator and denominator are positive integers written using four distinct digits in total, each used exactly once (for example $\dfrac{35}{78}$ or $\dfrac{4}{123}$). Numbers may not begin with $0$. Which such fraction is closest to $1$ but not equal to $1$?
