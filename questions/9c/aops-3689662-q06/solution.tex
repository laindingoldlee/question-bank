Eliminating time gives $\boxed{r=\dfrac{v}{\omega}\theta}$. Increasing the angle by $2\pi$ increases the radius by the constant $\boxed{\dfrac{2\pi v}{\omega}}$. This constant radial spacing characterizes the Archimedean spiral in this model.
\begin{asy}
import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
pair f(real t){return t*dir(degrees(t));}draw(graph(f,0,4pi));draw((0,0)--(4pi,0),dashed);label("$O$",(0,0),(-1,-1));label("$2\pi$",(2pi,0),(0,-1));label("$4\pi$",(4pi,0),(1,0));
\end{asy}
