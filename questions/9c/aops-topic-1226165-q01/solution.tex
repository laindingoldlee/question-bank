Let the plane fly south for $t$ hours and then fly straight to where the carrier will be at time $5$. With the start at the origin, $x$ east and $y$ north, the turning point is $(0,-400t)$ and the carrier at time $5$ is at $160(\sin60^\circ,\cos60^\circ)=(80\sqrt3,80)$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0),P=(0,-955.4),C=(80*sqrt(3),80);
draw(O--P,blue,Arrow(6)); draw(P--C,blue+dashed,Arrow(6)); draw(O--C,red,Arrow(6));
dot(O);label("start",O,W);label("turn",P,S);label("carrier at $t=5$",C,NE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The return leg must take the remaining $5-t$ hours:
\[(80\sqrt3)^2+(80+400t)^2=400^2(5-t)^2.\]
Expanding, $19200+6400+64000t=4000000-1600000t$, so $t=\dfrac{3974400}{1664000}=\dfrac{621}{260}$ hours. The distance south is $400t=\boxed{\dfrac{12420}{13}\text{ km}}\approx955.4$ km.
