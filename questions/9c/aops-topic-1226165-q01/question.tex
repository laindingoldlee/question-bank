An airplane leaves an aircraft carrier and flies due south at $400$ km/h. The carrier moves $60^\circ$ east of north at $32$ km/h. The plane has fuel for $5$ hours of flying, and flies at $400$ km/h on the way back too. What is the greatest distance south the plane can fly and still return to the carrier? (Assume a flat Earth.)
