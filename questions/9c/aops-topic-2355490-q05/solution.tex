The object stops after $\dfrac{20}{0.075}=\dfrac{800}{3}=266\dfrac23$ seconds. The meteor hits it $\dfrac{10}{3}$ seconds later, at time $\dfrac{800}{3}+\dfrac{10}{3}=270$ seconds. From then on its speed is $200+\dfrac{t-270}{2}$ at time $t$, so at $t=424$ the speed is
\[200+\dfrac{154}{2}=\boxed{277}\text{ units per second}.\]
