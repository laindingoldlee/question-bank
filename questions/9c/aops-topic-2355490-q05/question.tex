An object is moving at $20$ units per second, and its speed decreases steadily by $0.075$ units per second every second until it comes to rest. Exactly $\dfrac{10}{3}$ seconds after it comes to rest, it is hit by a meteor and immediately moves at $200$ units per second; after that its speed increases steadily by $1$ unit per second every $2$ seconds. How fast is it moving $424$ seconds after the start?
