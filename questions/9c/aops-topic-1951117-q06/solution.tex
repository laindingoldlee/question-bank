Carol finished ahead of Arthur, so Carol was not last. Bella was not last either. Hence the last runner was $\boxed{\text{Arthur}}$. (For instance, Bella--Carol--Arthur or Carol--Bella--Arthur; both fit, and in both Arthur is last.)
