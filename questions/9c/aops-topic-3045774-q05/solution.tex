The logarithms require $ x>3 $. Combining them and exponentiating yields $(x+1)(x+5)(x-3)=64(x+13)$. Rearranging factors as $(x-11)(x^2+14x+77)=0 $. The quadratic has discriminant $ 196-308=-112<0 $, so its roots are not real. The remaining root $ 11 $ is in the domain; hence $\boxed{x=11}$.
