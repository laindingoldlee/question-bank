If the first three digits form the number $m$ (with $100\le m\le999$), then
\[Z=1000m+m=1001m=7\cdot11\cdot13\cdot m.\]
So $1001$ divides every such $Z$. No larger integer divides all of them: $Z=1001\cdot100$ and $Z=1001\cdot101$ both occur, and $\gcd(100,101)=1$, so the greatest common divisor of all such $Z$ is $\boxed{1001}$. The primes that must divide $Z$ are $\boxed{7,\ 11\text{ and }13}$.
