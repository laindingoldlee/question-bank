The general term is $\dfrac{2^{k+1}}{k+1}\binom{10}k$, and $\dfrac1{k+1}\binom{10}k=\dfrac1{11}\binom{11}{k+1}$. So the sum is
\[\dfrac1{11}\sum_{j=1}^{11}\binom{11}{j}2^j=\dfrac{3^{11}-1}{11}=\boxed{\dfrac{177146}{11}}.\]
