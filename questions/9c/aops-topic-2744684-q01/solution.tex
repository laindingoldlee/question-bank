Any common divisor of $x$ and $x-5$ divides $x-(x-5)=5$, and any common divisor of $x$ and $5$ divides $x-5$. So $\gcd(x,x-5)=\gcd(x,5)$, which is $\boxed{5\text{ if }5\mid x,\ \text{and }1\text{ otherwise}}$.
