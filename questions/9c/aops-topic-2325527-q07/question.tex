I have $10$ different pairs of jeans, $12$ different shirts, $6$ different hoodies, $9$ different pairs of socks, $3$ different pairs of shoes and $20$ different scrunchies. An outfit consists of exactly one item of each of these six kinds. For how many days can I wear a different outfit each day?
