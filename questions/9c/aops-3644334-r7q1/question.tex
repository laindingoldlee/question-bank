Let $\overline{AB}$ be a diameter of the circle $\omega$ with radius $r$. Let point $C \ne A,B$ be on segment $\overline{AB}$ such that $AC = s$. The tangent line $\ell$ to $\omega$ at $A$ contains a variable point $P$, and $Q$ is defined as the point on $\ell$ such that $\overline{PC} \perp \overline{BQ}$. Lastly, the extension of line $\overleftrightarrow{AB}$ intersects the circumcircle of $\vartriangle BPQ$ at $R$. Let $F(r, s)$ be a function of r and s that denotes the length of $\overline{AP}$ for the minimum area of quadrilateral $BPRQ$. For how many ordered pairs of integers $(r, s)$ with $1 \le r \le  20$ will $F(r, s)$ also be an integer?
