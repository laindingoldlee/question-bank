Use $A=(0,0),B=(2r,0),C=(s,0),P=(0,p),Q=(0,q)$. Perpendicularity gives $pq=-2rs$. The circle through B,P,Q has constant term pq, so its other horizontal intercept is $R=(-s,0)$. Thus diagonal BR has fixed length $2r+s$, and quadrilateral area is $\dfrac{(2r+s)|p-q|}{2}$. Since p,q have opposite signs and fixed product, the minimum occurs at $p=-q=\sqrt{2rs}$. Hence $F(r,s)=\sqrt{2rs}$. Test $1\le r\le20$, $1\le s<2r$, retaining perfect squares $2rs$. The nonzero counts by r are: one each for $r=2,4,6,10,12,14,20$; three each for $r=8,16$; two for $r=9$; and five for $r=18$. The total is $\boxed{20}$. The computation audit lists all pairs.
