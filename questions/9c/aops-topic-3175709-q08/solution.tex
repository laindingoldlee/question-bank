The order of $2$ modulo $25$ is $20$: Euler gives a divisor of $20$, while $2^{10}\equiv-1$ and $2^4\equiv16$ rule out all proper divisors. The order of $3$ modulo $29$ is $28$: $3^{14}\equiv-1$ and $3^4\equiv23$ rule out its proper divisors. Thus $a$ is a multiple of $\operatorname{lcm}(20,28)=140$. There are $\boxed7$ such positive multiples at most $1000$.
