For each $k\ge1$,
\[
k\cdot k!=(k+1)\cdot k!-k!=(k+1)!-k! .
\]
Hence the sum telescopes:
\[
\sum_{k=1}^{n}k\cdot k!=\sum_{k=1}^{n}\bigl((k+1)!-k!\bigr)=(n+1)!-1!=(n+1)!-1 .\qquad\blacksquare
\]
