Whatever the top card is, exactly one of the remaining $51$ cards has the same rank and the same colour (for example, the only match for the $7$ of hearts is the $7$ of diamonds). All $51$ cards are equally likely to be second, so the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{51}}$.
