After three steps a token is back in its initial basket with probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$, and at either other basket with probability $\dfrac{3}{8}$. These values follow by enumerating its eight equally likely three-step choices. For any specified destination basket, independence gives probability $(\dfrac{1}{4})(\dfrac{3}{8})^2=\dfrac{9}{256}$. The three possible shared baskets are disjoint events, so the total is $\boxed{\dfrac{27}{256}}$.
