A circle $\omega$ with radius $6$ and a circle $\gamma$ with radius $2$ are internally tangent. A chord of $\omega$ is tangent to $\gamma$ and perpendicular to the line through the centres of $\omega$ and $\gamma$. Two congruent circles, one on each side of the line of centres, are each internally tangent to $\omega$, externally tangent to $\gamma$ and tangent to the chord. What is the radius of either of the two congruent circles?
