Let $R=\underbrace{11\cdots1}_{2005}=\dfrac{10^{2005}-1}{9}$, so $10^{2005}=9R+1$. The right-hand side is
\[N=R\cdot10^{2005}+2R=R\left(10^{2005}+2\right)=R(9R+3)=3R\,(3R+1).\]
So the equation is $x^2+x-3R(3R+1)=0$, i.e.
\[(x-3R)(x+3R+1)=0.\]
Hence
\[\boxed{x=3R=\underbrace{33\cdots3}_{2005}\quad\text{or}\quad x=-(3R+1)=-\underbrace{33\cdots3}_{2004}4}.\]
