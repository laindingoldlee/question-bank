An experiment in which an event $A$ has probability $p$ is repeated $n$ times independently. Let $X$ be the number of times $A$ occurs, so that the experimental probability of $A$ is $\dfrac Xn$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that the expected value of $\dfrac Xn$ equals $p$.
\item[(b)] Is $\dfrac Xn$ always equal to $p$?
\end{enumerate}
