Taking turns, each of them removes $50$ balls. If Paul took $p$ black balls, then he took $50-p$ red ones and
\[
(50-p)+4p=110\Rightarrow 3p=60\Rightarrow p=20 .
\]
If Helen took $h$ black balls, then $2(50-h)+3h=121$, so $h=21$. The jar held $20+21=\boxed{41}$ black balls.
