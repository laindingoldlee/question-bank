A big jar contains $100$ balls, some red and the rest black. Paul and Helen take turns removing a ball until the jar is empty. Each time Paul takes out a red ball he gets $\$1$, and each time he takes out a black ball he gets $\$4$. Helen gets $\$2$ for a red ball and $\$3$ for a black ball. When all the balls are gone, Paul has $\$110$ and Helen has $\$121$. How many black balls were in the jar originally?
