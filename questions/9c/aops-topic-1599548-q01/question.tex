A line $\ell_1$ and a point $P$ are given in the plane. The line $\ell_1$ is rotated about $P$ through an acute angle $\alpha$ to give the line $\ell_2$. Show that $\ell_1$ and $\ell_2$ intersect, and that the acute angle between them is $\alpha$.
