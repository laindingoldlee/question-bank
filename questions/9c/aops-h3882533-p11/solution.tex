Label a face by $(j,\varepsilon)$, where $j\in\mathbb Z_{12}$ identifies a base edge and $\varepsilon\in\{0,1\}$ identifies the upper or lower pyramid. A move either changes $j$ by $1$ or $-1$, or flips $\varepsilon$. The opposite face to $(0,0)$ is $(6,1)$.

If there are $h$ horizontal moves, the number of flips is $13-h$, which must be odd. Also $h$ must be even and at least $6$. Thus $h=6,8,10,12$. The net horizontal displacement must be $6$ or $-6$, since $h\le12$. For each $h$, choose its move positions and then the right/left directions. The total is
\[
\sum_{h\in\{6,8,10,12\}}\binom{13}{h}\,2\binom{h}{\dfrac{(h+6)}{2}}
=3432+20592+25740+5720=\boxed{55484}.
\]
