A number is a multiple of $4$ exactly when its last two digits form a multiple of $4$. Using only $0,1,2$, the possible endings are $00$, $12$ and $20$ ($3$ choices). The first digit is $1$ or $2$ ($2$ choices) and the six middle digits are free ($3^6=729$ choices). The count is $2\cdot729\cdot3=\boxed{4374}$.
