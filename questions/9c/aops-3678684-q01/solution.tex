Measure value in nickels. If $n,d,q$ are coin counts, $n+2d+5q=25$, with $n\le20$. A selection of 23 or more coins would have excess value $d+4q\le2$, and hence would require at least 21 nickels, impossible. A selection of 19 nickels and three dimes uses 22 coins and pays exactly 125 cents. Thus the maximum is $\boxed{22}$.
