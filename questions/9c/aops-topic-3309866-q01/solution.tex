For $k\ge2$, $A_k=\sum_{j=0}^{\lfloor\dfrac{k-1}{2}\rfloor}k^{k-1-2j}$. Repeated multiplication modulo $100$ gives
\[\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrrrr}k&1&2&3&4&5&6&7&8&9\\\hline A_k\bmod100&1&2&10&68&51&98&0&40&5\end{array}\]
The residues sum to $275$, so the last two digits are $\boxed{75}$.
