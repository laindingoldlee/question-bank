Call a positive integer beautiful if its decimal digits are nondecreasing from left to right.
\begin{enumerate}\item Count the beautiful integers with at most $20$ digits.
\item Prove that no beautiful integer is congruent to $10$ modulo $11$.
\item Does there exist a rational-coefficient polynomial taking integer values at every beautiful integer, but a noninteger value at some positive nonbeautiful integer? Prove your answer.\end{enumerate}
