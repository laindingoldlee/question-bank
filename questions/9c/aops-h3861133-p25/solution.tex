Put $A=(0,0),B=(1,0),C=(\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2})$, and write $D=(d,0),E=(\dfrac{e}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt3e}{2})$. The equality $PD=PE$ makes $P$ the midpoint of $DE$. The circumcircle is $x^2+y^2-x-\dfrac{y}{\sqrt3}=0$. Substituting the midpoint gives $d^2+de+e^2=2(d+e)$. The radius through that midpoint is perpendicular to $DE$, so its center is equidistant from $D,E$, giving $(d-e)(d+e-1)=0$. If $d=e$, the nonzero solution is $d=e=\dfrac{4}{3}$, placing $P$ off the minor arc $AB$. Thus $d+e=1$ and $de=-1$. The minor-arc condition selects $e=\dfrac{1-\sqrt5}{2}<0$. Therefore $[ADE]=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}|de|=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}}$.
