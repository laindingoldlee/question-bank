By the angle-bisector theorem, $BD:DC=12:15=4:5$, so $BD=8$ and $BC=18$. The semiperimeter is $\dfrac{45}{2}$. Heron gives area $\sqrt{\dfrac{45}{2}\dfrac{21}{2}\dfrac{15}{2}\dfrac{9}{2}}=\dfrac{135\sqrt7}{4}$. Hence $\boxed{m+n+p=146}$.
