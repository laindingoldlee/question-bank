The $k$th odd number is $2k-1=k^2-(k-1)^2$. Summing telescopes to $\boxed{1+3+\cdots+(2n-1)=n^2}$. Geometrically, enlarging a $(k-1)$-square to a $k$-square adds an L-shaped layer of $k+(k-1)=2k-1$ unit squares.
\begin{asy}
import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
for(int k=1;k<=5;++k){fill((k-1,0)--(k,0)--(k,k)--(0,k)--(0,k-1)--(k-1,k-1)--cycle,gray(.95-.1*k));}for(int i=0;i<=5;++i){draw((i,0)--(i,5));draw((0,i)--(5,i));}label("$1+3+5+7+9=25$",(2.5,-.5));
\end{asy}
