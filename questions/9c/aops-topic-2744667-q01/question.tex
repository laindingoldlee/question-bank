A scout troop offers $28$ merit badges, $14$ silver and $14$ gold. Tom has collected $25\%$ of them, exactly one of which is gold. Richard has collected $50\%$ of them, eleven of which are gold. Harry has collected exactly the badges Tom has, together with Richard's gold badges. Tom and Richard earn no more badges, and Harry does not know which silver badges Richard has. How many more badges must Harry earn to be certain that he has every silver badge that Richard has?
