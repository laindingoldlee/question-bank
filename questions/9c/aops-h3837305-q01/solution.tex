\begin{enumerate}
\item Neither bound follows from the stated conditions. Take $n=1,b=1,a_1=1,a_2=2$. The ordering and gap bound hold, but the product is $2$, while both proposed bounds equal $1$. A bound on consecutive gaps does not force symmetric deviations about $b$.
\item By AM-GM applied to each paired set,
\[
a_i a_{2n+1-i}\le\left(\dfrac{a_i+a_{2n+1-i}}{2}\right)^2\le b^2.
\]
Multiplying these $n$ inequalities gives the desired bound. Equality requires equality in every pair, so each pair has equal entries summing to $2b$. Thus equality holds exactly when $\boxed{a_1=\cdots=a_{2n}=b}$.
\end{enumerate}
