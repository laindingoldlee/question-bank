Positive real numbers satisfy $a_1\le\cdots\le a_n\le b\le a_{n+1}\le\cdots\le a_{2n}$ and $a_{i+1}-a_i\le1$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Is $\prod_{i=1}^{2n}a_i\le b^{2n}$ necessarily true? Is the alternative bound $b^{2b}$ necessarily valid?
\item Prove the following corrected result: if $a_i+a_{2n+1-i}\le2b$ for $1\le i\le n$, then $\prod_{i=1}^{2n}a_i\le b^{2n}$. Determine the equality case.
\end{enumerate}
