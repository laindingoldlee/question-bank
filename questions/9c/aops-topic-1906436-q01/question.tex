Martian music uses $9$ different pitches. Every measure is $5$ beats long, and each note lasts $4$, $2$, $1$ or $\dfrac{1}{2}$ beat. Only one note is played at a time, there are no rests, and no note may run over the end of a measure (for example, two $4$-beat notes in a row are forbidden). A piece is determined by its sequence of notes, each note having a pitch and a length. How many different two-measure pieces are there?
