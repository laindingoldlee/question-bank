Let the individual times be $x,y>0$. Then $\dfrac{1}{x}+\dfrac{1}{y}=\dfrac{1}{4}$ and $\dfrac{x}{2}+\dfrac{y}{2}=9$. Hence $x+y=18$ and $xy=72$. They are the roots of $t^2-18t+72=(t-6)(t-12)$, so the times are $\boxed{6\text{ and }12\text{ hours}}$.
