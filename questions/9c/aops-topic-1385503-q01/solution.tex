Since $2\cdot\overline{ML}<200$, the three-digit sum begins with $D=1$. Then
\[
2(10M+L)=100+10M+S\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 10M+2L=100+S .
\]
As $2L\le18$ and $S\ge0$, we need $10M\ge82$, so $M=9$, and then $2L=10+S$, so $L\ge5$ and $S=2L-10$. The candidates are $L=5,6,7,8,9$ with $S=0,2,4,6,8$; $L=9$ is ruled out because $M=9$. Each remaining one uses distinct digits ($D=1$, $M=9$):
\[
95+95=190,\qquad 96+96=192,\qquad 97+97=194,\qquad 98+98=196 .
\]
So there are $\boxed{4}$ solutions, the four sums above.
