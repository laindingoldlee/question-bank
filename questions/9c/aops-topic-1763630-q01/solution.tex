Fix $n\ge1$. Whatever the first $n-1$ coins show, exactly one of the two outcomes of the last coin makes the total number of heads even. So for each $n\in\{1,\dots,6\}$ the probability of an even number of heads is exactly $\dfrac{1}{2}$, and therefore the overall probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$.
