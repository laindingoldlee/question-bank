Every strip length is at most $2016$, so the total covered area is at most
\[
1+2+\cdots+2016=\dfrac{2016\cdot2017}{2}.
\]
Hence $2016n$ is at most this quantity and $n\le1008$.

To attain $1008$, use $1008$ columns of height $2016$. For each $k=1,\ldots,1007$, split column $k$ into vertical strips of lengths $k$ and $2016-k$. Leave the last column as one strip of length $2016$. Every length from $1$ to $2016$ except $1008$ occurs exactly once. This tiles the board with distinct lengths, proving $\boxed{n=1008}$.
