The spacings are $\dfrac{20}{35}$ and $\dfrac{20}{50}$. Rungs $i,j$ coincide when $\dfrac{i}{35}=\dfrac{j}{50}$, or $10i=7j$. The least positive solution is $i=7,j=10$, at height $\boxed4$ feet. Equal height minimizes their vertical separation to zero.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw((0,0)--(0,20)^^(3,0)--(3,20));for(int i=1;i<=34;++i)draw((-.2,20*i/35.0)--(.2,20*i/35.0));for(int j=1;j<=49;++j)draw((2.8,20*j/50.0)--(3.2,20*j/50.0));draw((0,4)--(3,4),blue);label("rung 7",(0,4),W);label("rung 10",(3,4),E);
\end{asy}
