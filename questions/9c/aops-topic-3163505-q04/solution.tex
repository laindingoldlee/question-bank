The possible exponent patterns in a prime factorization are $(11),(5,1),(3,2),(2,1,1)$, since the factors one larger than the exponents multiply to $12$. For a fixed pattern the minimum assigns larger exponents to smaller primes. The respective minima are $2^{11}=2048$, $2^5\cdot3=96$, $2^3\cdot3^2=72$, and $2^2\cdot3\cdot5=60$. Hence the answer is $\boxed{60}$.
