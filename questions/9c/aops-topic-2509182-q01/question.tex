Triangle $ABC$ has $\angle B=90^\circ$ and $AB=BC$. Two concentric circles $C_1$ and $C_2$ are centred at $B$; $C_1$ has radius $r$ and $C_2$ has radius $2r$, and the part of the disc bounded by $C_2$ that lies inside the angle $ABC$ is entirely inside the triangle (so $C_2$ does not cross the hypotenuse $AC$). The ratio of the area inside triangle $ABC$ but outside $C_2$ to the area inside triangle $ABC$ but outside $C_1$ is $2:3$. If $AB=n\sqrt\pi$, what is the value of $\dfrac nr$?
