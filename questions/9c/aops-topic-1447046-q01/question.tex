Let $x=0.999\ldots$, the decimal with infinitely many nines.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] A student argues: ``$0.999\ldots$ and $1$ are different numbers, because one starts with $0$ and the other starts with $1$.'' Explain what is wrong with this argument.
\item[(b)] Prove that $x=1$.
\end{enumerate}
