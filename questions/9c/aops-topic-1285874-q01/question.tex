A gardener plants three maple trees, four oaks and five birch trees in a row, in random order, each arrangement being equally likely. Let $\dfrac {m}{n}$ in lowest terms be the probability that no two birch trees are next to one another. Find $m+n$. Also explain why, in the gap method, the arrangement of the $7$ non-birch trees does not need to be counted separately.
