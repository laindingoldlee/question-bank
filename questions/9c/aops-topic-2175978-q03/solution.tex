At an intersection point, $8x+7=x^2+8x-9$, so $x^2=16$ and $x=\pm4$. Then $y=8(4)+7=39$ or $y=8(-4)+7=-25$. The graphs meet at
\[\boxed{(4,39)\text{ and }(-4,-25)}.\]
