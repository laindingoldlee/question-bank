A walker crosses a field $40$ meters wide at a constant speed of $1$ meter per second. Split the crossing into stages: stage $1$ is the first half of the field, stage $2$ is half of what remains, stage $3$ is half of what then remains, and so on forever.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] How long does stage $k$ take, and how long do all the stages take together?
\item[(b)] A student argues that the walker can never reach the far side, because infinitely many stages must be completed. Explain the error.
\item[(c)] After how many complete stages is the walker, for the first time, less than $1$ centimeter from the far side?
\end{enumerate}
