Substitute $y=\dfrac{(1-x)}{2}$, so $0<x<1$. Direct factorisation gives
\[
\left(\dfrac{1}{x}+\dfrac{2}{y}\right)(1+48xy^2)-25
=\dfrac{(2x-1)^2(3x-1)^2}{x(1-x)}\ge0.
\]
All denominators and $1+48xy^2$ are positive, so division proves the desired inequality. Equality holds exactly at $x=\dfrac{1}{2}$ or $x=\dfrac{1}{3}$, corresponding to
