Let $n=10a+b$, where $b$ is the units digit of $n$ and $a$ is the number formed by the other digits.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $19$ divides $n$ if and only if $19$ divides $a+2b$.
\item[(b)] Use this test to decide whether $36497$ is divisible by $19$.
\end{enumerate}
