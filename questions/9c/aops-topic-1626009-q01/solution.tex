\textbf{(a)} We have $2n=20a+2b=19a+(a+2b)$. So $2n$ and $a+2b$ differ by a multiple of $19$, and $19\mid2n\iff19\mid a+2b$. Since $\gcd(2,19)=1$, $19\mid 2n\iff19\mid n$. Hence $19\mid n\iff19\mid a+2b$. $\blacksquare$

\textbf{(b)} Apply the test repeatedly (each step keeps divisibility by $19$ the same):
\[
36497\to3649+2\cdot7=3663\to366+2\cdot3=372\to37+2\cdot2=41\to4+2\cdot1=6 .
\]
Since $19\nmid6$ (equivalently $19\nmid41$), $36497$ is $\boxed{\text{not divisible by }19}$. Indeed $36497=19\cdot1920+17$.
