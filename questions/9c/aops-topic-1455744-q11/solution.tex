Let $a_1=100$ and $a_{k+1}=2a_k+1$. Adding $1$ to both sides,
\[
a_{k+1}+1=2(a_k+1),
\]
so the numbers $a_k+1$ double at each step: $a_k+1=101\cdot2^{k-1}$. For the eleventh bee
\[
a_{11}=101\cdot2^{10}-1=101\cdot1024-1=\boxed{103423}.
\]
(Applying $x\mapsto2x+1$ ten times to $100$ gives $2^{10}\cdot100+(2^{10}-1)$, the same number; it is not $(2x+1)^{10}$ or $201^{10}$, since the rule is applied to the previous result, not multiplied.)
