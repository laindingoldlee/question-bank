Let $b$ be the number of purchases and $s$ the number of sales. Since emeralds cannot be sold before they exist, $s\le b$. The remaining gold is $2025-16b+4s\ge0$, so $12b\le2025$ and $b\le168$. Thus the credits total $b+s\le336$. Repeating a purchase followed by a sale 168 times is feasible: each cycle costs 12 gold, leaving 9 after the last sale. Therefore the maximum is $\boxed{336}$.
