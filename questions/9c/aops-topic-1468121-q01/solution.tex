Let the ratio be $r$ with $|r|<1$ and let a term be $t\ne0$. The terms after it sum to $\dfrac{tr}{1-r}$, so
\[
t=4\cdot\dfrac{tr}{1-r}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad1-r=4r\quad\Longrightarrow\quad r=\dfrac{1}{5} .
\]
The sum is $\dfrac{2}{1-\dfrac{1}{5}}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{2}}$. (Check: the terms after $2$ sum to $\dfrac{1}{2}$, and $2=4\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}$.)

The poster's equation $2=4(2r+2r^2+\cdots)$ was right; the bracket is itself a geometric series, $2r+2r^2+\cdots=\dfrac{2r}{1-r}$.
