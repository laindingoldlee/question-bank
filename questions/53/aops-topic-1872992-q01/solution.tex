By definition $n!=1\cdot2\cdots(n-1)\cdot n=(n-1)!\cdot n$ (for $n=1$ this reads $1!=0!\cdot1$ with $0!=1$). Since $(n-1)!\ne0$,
\[
\dfrac{(n-1)!}{n!}=\dfrac{(n-1)!}{(n-1)!\cdot n}=\dfrac{1}{n} .
\]
