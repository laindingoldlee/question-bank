Each square root halves the exponent: $\sqrt{40}=40^{\dfrac{1}{2}}$, $\sqrt{\sqrt{40}}=40^{\dfrac{1}{4}}$, and in general $k$ nested square roots give $40^{\dfrac{1}{2^k}}$. With $k=14$ and $2^{14}=16384$, the number is
\[\boxed{40^{\dfrac{1}{16384}}}.\]
(It is only slightly larger than $1$, about $1.000225$.)
