For each prime $p$, let $\alpha$ and $\beta$ be the exponents of $p$ in $a$ and $b$. The exponent of $p$ in $\gcd(a^2,b^2)$ is $\min(2\alpha,2\beta)=2\min(\alpha,\beta)$, which is the exponent of $p$ in $\gcd(a,b)^2$. Since every prime appears to the same power on both sides, they are equal. $\blacksquare$
