From $h(1)=88$ and $h(2)=84$: $a+b=28$ and $4a+2b=24$, i.e. $2a+b=12$. Subtracting, $a=-16$ and $b=44$. The initial speed is the coefficient $b$ (the rate of change of $h$ at $t=0$), so it was $\boxed{44\text{ feet per second}}$. (The value $a=-16$ matches the gravitational acceleration of $32$ feet per second per second.)
