Use the nonnegative integer potential $V=\sum n_i^2$, where $n_i$ are apartment populations. If a group of $k\ge16$ leaves its apartment and moves to $k$ distinct other apartments with previous occupancies $b_1,\ldots,b_k$, the change is $-k^2+\sum((b_i+1)^2-b_i^2)=-k^2+k+2\sum b_i$. Since at most $119-k$ people live outside the vacated apartment, this is at most $238-k^2-k\le-34$. Thus every move strictly decreases a nonnegative integer. Only finitely many moves are possible; if an overpopulated apartment remained, another move would be possible. Therefore eventually every apartment has at most 15 residents.
