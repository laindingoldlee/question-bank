Classify eligible cards as even only (5 cards), divisible by 3 only (3 cards), or both (2 cards: 6 and 12). Let their selected counts be $(a,b,c)$. A valid partition exists exactly when $a+b+c=4$, $a,b\le2$, and $c\le2$. The possible triples and counts are $(2,2,0):30$, $(1,2,1):30$, $(2,1,1):60$, $(0,2,2):3$, $(1,1,2):15$, $(2,0,2):10$, using $\binom{5}{a}\binom{3}{b}\binom{2}{c}$. These disjoint cases total $148$. Out of $\binom{15}{4}=1365$ selections, the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{148}{1365}}$.
