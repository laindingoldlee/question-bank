\textbf{Counting.} Add up, over all $30$ people, the number of hands each person shakes:
\[15\cdot5+15\cdot7=75+105=180.\]
Every handshake involves two people, so it is counted exactly twice in this sum. Hence there are $\dfrac{180}{2}=\boxed{90}$ handshakes.

\textbf{Such a party exists.} Number the boys $B_0,\dots,B_{14}$ and the girls $G_0,\dots,G_{14}$, with indices taken modulo $15$. Let girl $G_i$ shake hands with the five boys $B_i,B_{i+1},\dots,B_{i+4}$ and with the two girls $G_{i-1}$ and $G_{i+1}$. Then each girl shakes $5+2=7$ hands, and each boy $B_j$ shakes hands exactly with the five girls $G_j,G_{j-1},\dots,G_{j-4}$. (There are $75$ boy-girl handshakes and $15$ girl-girl handshakes, $90$ in all.)
