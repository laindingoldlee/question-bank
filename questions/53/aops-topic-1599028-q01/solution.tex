Let the school have $g$ girls and $1200-g$ boys. Then
\[
\dfrac{2}{3}g+\dfrac{1}{2}(1200-g)=730\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\dfrac{1}{6}g=130\quad\Longrightarrow\quad g=780 .
\]
So $\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot780=\boxed{520}$ girls attend (and $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot420=210$ boys, for $730$ in all).
