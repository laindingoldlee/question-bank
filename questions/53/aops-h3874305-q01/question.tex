At a family dinner, eight siblings, Ava, Ben, Cara, Diego, Ella, Finn, Grace, and Henry, are seated around a circular table.

Exactly one person must sit between Ava and Ben. Cara and Diego may not sit next to each other, and Ella and Finn may not sit next to each other.

Two seatings are considered the same if one can be obtained from the other by rotating the table, but reflections are considered different. How many valid seatings are possible?
