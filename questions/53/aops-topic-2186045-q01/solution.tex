The population grows on average by $3-\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{11}{4}$ pigeons per hour. It must grow by $1694-1100=594$, which takes
\[594\div\dfrac{11}{4}=594\cdot\dfrac{4}{11}=216\text{ hours}=\dfrac{216}{24}=\boxed{9}\text{ days}.\]
