A right circular cone has base radius $6$ and height $9$. A plane parallel to the base, at distance $3$ above the base, cuts the cone.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What kind of figure is the cross-section, and what is its area?
\item[(b)] The plane cuts the cone into two solids. Name them and find their volumes.
\item[(c)] What other cross-section can a plane parallel to the base produce?
\end{enumerate}
