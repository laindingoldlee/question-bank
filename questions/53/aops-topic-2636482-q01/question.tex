Find all pairs of primes $(p,q)$ for which there is a positive integer $m$ with
\[\dfrac{pq}{p+q}=\dfrac{m^2+6}{m+1}.\]
In the case $p\ne q$, explain why one may equate numerators and denominators after dividing $m^2+6$ and $m+1$ by their greatest common divisor, and why that divisor is $1$ or $7$.
