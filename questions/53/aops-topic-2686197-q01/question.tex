The point $(4,5)$ lies on the graph of $y=f(x)$, and $g(x)=2f(x-6)-3$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Which point must lie on the graph of $y=g(x)$?
\item[(b)] Show that $(x,y)$ is on the graph of $f$ if and only if $(x+6,\,2y-3)$ is on the graph of $g$, and describe the graph of $g$ as a sequence of transformations of the graph of $f$.
\item[(c)] Would ``shift down $3$, then stretch vertically by a factor of $2$'' give the same graph?
\end{enumerate}
