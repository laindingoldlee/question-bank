Maria has unmarked containers holding $3$, $9$ and $20$ cups, and an unlimited water supply. She may fill a container, empty it, or pour from one container into another until the first is empty or the second is full. How many of the whole numbers of cups from $1$ to $20$ inclusive can she measure (that is, have in one of the containers)?
