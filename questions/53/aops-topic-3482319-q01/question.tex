A rabbit and hedgehog start at the same point of a $550$-meter circular track, in opposite directions, at constant speeds $10$ and $1$ meters per second. At their first meeting the hedgehog turns immediately and follows the rabbit's direction. The rabbit finishes after one lap; the hedgehog finishes at its first return to the start. How many seconds later does the hedgehog finish?
