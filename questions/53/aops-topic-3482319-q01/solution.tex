They first meet after $\dfrac{550}{10+1}=50$ seconds. The hedgehog is then $50$ meters from the start along its reverse route, and after turning needs $50$ more seconds to return. It finishes at $100$ seconds; the rabbit finishes at $\dfrac{550}{10}=55$ seconds. The difference is $\boxed{45\text{ seconds}}$.
