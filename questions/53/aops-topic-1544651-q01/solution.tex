In base $10$, $101_2=5$ and $110_2=6$, so the product is $30=16+8+4+2=\boxed{11110_2}$.

Directly in binary: $101_2\times110_2=101_2\times100_2+101_2\times10_2=10100_2+1010_2=11110_2$.
