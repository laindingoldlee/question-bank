Any one of the three known points can be opposite the fourth. Using the common diagonal midpoint, the fourth point is the sum of the other two known points minus the opposite known point. The possibilities are $(4,8),(-8,4),(8,-4)$, with coordinate sums $12,-4,4$. Thus the least is $\boxed{-4}$.

\end{asy}
