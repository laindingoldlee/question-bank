The 100-degree angle cannot be one of the equal base angles, since two such angles would exceed 180 degrees. It is the apex angle, so the other two each measure $\dfrac{180-100}{2}=\boxed{40^\circ}$. 
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(-1,0),B=(1,0),C=(0,tan(40*pi/180));draw(A--B--C--cycle);label("$40^\circ$",A+(.35,.12));label("$40^\circ$",B+(-.35,.12));label("$100^\circ$",C+(0,-.2));
\end{asy}
