The two ground speeds are $\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{4}}{\dfrac{15}{60}}=1$ and $\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{4}}{\dfrac{8}{60}}=\dfrac{15}{8}$ miles per minute. Adding the equations $v-w=1$ and $v+w=\dfrac{15}{8}$ gives $\boxed{v=\dfrac{23}{16}}$ miles per minute.
