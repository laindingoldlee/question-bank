An outer circle has radius $k$, and an inner circle of radius $\dfrac{k}{2}$ is internally tangent to it. A line meets them at $A,B,C,D$ in that order with $AB=BC=CD$. Four circles are internally tangent to the outer circle, externally tangent to the inner circle, and tangent to the line. If the larger such circles have diameter $d$, write $\dfrac{d}{k}=\dfrac{m}{n}$ in lowest terms and find $m+n$.
