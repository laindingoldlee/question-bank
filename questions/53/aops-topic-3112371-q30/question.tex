A contestant may choose one shot. Its prize and success probability are: free throw, $1000$ dollars and $60\%$; three-point shot, $2500$ dollars and $35\%$; half-court shot, $10000$ dollars and $10\%$; full-court shot, $50000$ dollars and $1\%$. A miss pays zero. Which maximizes expected prize?
