The base $ABC$ is right-angled at $B$, with area $24$ and circumradius $5$. Since $D$ is equidistant from all three vertices, its perpendicular projection is the circumcentre $O$, the midpoint of $AC$. Thus its height is $\sqrt{20^2-5^2}=5\sqrt{15}$. The volume is $\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot24\cdot5\sqrt{15}=40\sqrt{15}$, so $a+b=\boxed{55}$.
