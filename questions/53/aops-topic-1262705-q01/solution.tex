Suppose such integers exist. Since $5\nmid 48=\operatorname{lcm}(b,c)$, neither $b$ nor $c$ is divisible by $5$. Since $5\mid 20=\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)$, the factor $5$ must come from $a$, so $5\mid a$. But then $5\mid\operatorname{lcm}(c,a)=36$, which is false. Hence no such integers exist (so the question ``find $a+b+c$'' has no answer). $\blacksquare$
