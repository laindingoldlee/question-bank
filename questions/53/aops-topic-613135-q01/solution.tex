For each pair compute $\gcd+\operatorname{lcm}$:
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccccccccc}
\text{pair}&4,9&4,12&4,16&4,18&9,12&9,16&9,18&12,16&12,18&16,18\\\hline
\gcd&1&4&4&2&3&1&9&4&6&2\\
\operatorname{lcm}&36&12&16&36&36&144&18&48&36&144\\
\text{sum}&37&16&20&38&39&145&27&52&42&146
\end{array}
\]
The least sum is $\boxed{16}$, from the pair $4,12$.

(A shortcut: since $\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)\ge\max(a,b)$ with equality when one number divides the other, the best candidates are pairs where one number divides the other: $(4,12)$ gives $16$, $(4,16)$ gives $20$, $(9,18)$ gives $27$. Every other pair has $\operatorname{lcm}\ge36$.)
