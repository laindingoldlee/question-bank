(a) No: $\sqrt2$ and $-\sqrt2$ are irrational but sum to 0. (b) If $\sqrt{13}=\dfrac{a}{b}$ in lowest terms with positive integers $a,b$, then $a^2=13b^2$. Primality of 13 implies $13\mid a$; write $a=13c$. Then $b^2=13c^2$, so $13\mid b$, contradicting coprimality. Thus $\sqrt{13}$ is irrational.
