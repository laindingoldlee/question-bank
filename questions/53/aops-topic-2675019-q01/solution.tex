He runs $5\cdot10=50$ minutes and walks $4\cdot5=20$ minutes. With walking speed $w$ metres per minute,
\[50\cdot2w+20w=120w=24000,\]
so $w=200$ and the running speed is $\boxed{400}$ metres per minute.
