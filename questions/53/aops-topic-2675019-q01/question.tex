Budi takes part in a $24$ km race. He runs for $10$ minutes, then walks for $5$ minutes, runs for $10$ minutes, walks for $5$ minutes, and so on. He reaches the finish line exactly as he completes his fifth $10$-minute run. If his running speed is twice his walking speed, what is his running speed in metres per minute?
