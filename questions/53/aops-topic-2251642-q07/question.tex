A stadium staircase with $150$ steps is numbered from $1$ to $150$. Jan starts on step $130$ and steps down to step $127$, then to step $124$, and continues downward to step $13$, stepping only on every third step. Jen starts on step $3$ and steps up to step $7$, then to step $11$, and continues upward to step $139$, stepping only on every fourth step. How many steps were stepped on by both Jan and Jen (not necessarily at the same time)?
