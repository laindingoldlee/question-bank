Jan steps on $13,16,\ldots,130$: the numbers from $13$ to $130$ that leave remainder $1$ on division by $3$. Jen steps on $3,7,\ldots,139$: the numbers from $3$ to $139$ that leave remainder $3$ on division by $4$.

A common step lies between $13$ and $130$ and satisfies both conditions. Checking $1,4,7,10$ (remainder $1$ mod $3$) for remainder $3$ mod $4$, we find $7$; so the common numbers are those $\equiv7\pmod{12}$: $19,31,43,\ldots,127$. There are $\dfrac{127-19}{12}+1=\boxed{10}$ of them.
