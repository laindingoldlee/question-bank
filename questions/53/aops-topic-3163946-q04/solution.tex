Let runner speed be $v$ and tunnel length $L$. Before the train enters, each runner travels $1200$ meters, so the forward runner has traveled $2400$ meters from the entrance. The remaining train journey takes $\dfrac{L}{9v}$, during which that runner covers $\dfrac{L}{9}$. Therefore $L-2400=\dfrac{L}{9}$, giving $\boxed{L=2700}$ meters.
