The monic cubic $f(x)-a$ has roots 1,7,10, hence equals $(x-1)(x-7)(x-10)$. At $x=2$ it equals $1\cdot(-5)\cdot(-8)=40$, so $b-a=40$ and $\boxed{|a-b|=40}$. The other supplied values are consistent, since the same product is 40 at 5 and 11.
