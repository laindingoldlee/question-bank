Arrange the five distinct directors around the table in $4!=24$ ways. The associates must occupy the five intervening gaps. In twelve director arrangements the two E-directors are neighbors in the director cycle; their incident gaps cover three gaps, leaving two for the E-associate. In the other twelve arrangements their incident gaps cover four, leaving one. After placing the E-associate, the others can be permuted in $4!$ ways. Thus $(12\cdot2+12\cdot1)4!=\boxed{864}$.
