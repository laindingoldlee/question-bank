The triple $(1,2,1)$ works because $1^3+2^3=9=9(1^3)$. Multiplying all coordinates by any positive integer $t$ preserves the homogeneous cubic equation, so $\boxed{(x,y,z)=(t,2t,t)}$ supplies infinitely many positive solutions.
