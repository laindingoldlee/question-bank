It starts snowing in Houston on Wednesday. On Thursday the storm cloud moves to Denver or to Memphis, each with probability $\dfrac12$. On Friday, from Denver it moves to Seattle or Minneapolis, and from Memphis to Chicago or New York City, each with probability $\dfrac12$. What is the probability that it snows in New York City on Friday?
