Divisibility by $18$ means divisibility by $2$ and by $9$. So $b$ is even, $b\in\{2,4,6,8\}$, and the digit sum $1+2+8+7+a+4+5+b=27+a+b$ is a multiple of $9$, so $a+b\in\{9,18\}$. With $b$ even, $a+b=18$ would need $a=b=9$, which is impossible. So $a+b=9$:
\[
(a,b)=(7,2),\ (5,4),\ (3,6),\ (1,8),
\]
with $|a-b|=5,1,3,7$. The largest is $\boxed{7}$ (from $12871458$).
