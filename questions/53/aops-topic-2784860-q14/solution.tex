The head and tail probabilities are $\dfrac{2}{3}$ and $\dfrac{1}{3}$. Exactly two heads occur with probability $\binom32(\dfrac{2}{3})^2\dfrac{1}{3}=\dfrac{4}{9}$. Thus $\boxed{m+n=13}$.
