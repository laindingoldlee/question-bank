The condition is $ 100\mid x(x-1)$. Consecutive integers are coprime, so modulo 4, $ x $ is 0 or 1, and modulo 25 it is 0 or 1. Combining these choices gives residues 0, 1, 25, 76 modulo 100. In the positive interval they are 100, 1, 25, 76, with sum $\boxed{202}$.
