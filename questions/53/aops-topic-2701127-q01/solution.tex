For $0\le k<n$,
\[\dfrac{\binom n{k+1}}{\binom nk}=\dfrac{n-k}{k+1}.\]
This ratio is at least $1$ exactly when $n-k\ge k+1$, i.e. $k\le\dfrac{n-1}2$. So the coefficients increase up to $k=\left\lfloor\dfrac n2\right\rfloor$ (weakly at the last step when $n$ is odd) and decrease afterwards. Hence the maximum is attained at $k=\left\lfloor\dfrac n2\right\rfloor$ (and also at $\left\lceil\dfrac n2\right\rceil$). $\blacksquare$
