A number with exactly $8$ divisors has the form $p^7$, $p^3q$ or $pqr$ ($p,q,r$ distinct primes). The smallest values are $2^7=128$, $2^3\cdot3=24$ and $2\cdot3\cdot5=30$, so the smallest such number is $24$. Hence $x=29$ and the goal was $\boxed{30}$.
