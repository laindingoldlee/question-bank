Multiply the right side $R=\sum_{k=0}^{16}(-1)^kx^k$ by $x+1$:
\[xR+R=\sum_{k=0}^{16}(-1)^kx^{k+1}+\sum_{k=0}^{16}(-1)^kx^k.\]
Each $x^j$ with $1\le j\le16$ appears once with sign $(-1)^{j-1}$ and once with $(-1)^j$, and cancels. What remains is $x^{17}$ (sign $(-1)^{16}=+1$) and $1$. So $(x+1)R=x^{17}+1$. $\blacksquare$ (This is the geometric series with ratio $-x$: $\dfrac{1-(-x)^{17}}{1+x}$.)
