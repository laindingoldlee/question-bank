Each pen costs at least $\dfrac{7}{12}$ dollars, so at most $\lfloor\dfrac{41\cdot12}{7}\rfloor=70$ pens can be bought. Ten six-packs and two five-packs cost $35+6=41$ dollars and contain $60+10=70$ pens. Thus $\boxed{70}$.
