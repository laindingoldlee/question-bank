A ball (sphere) of radius $R$ rests on a horizontal floor and touches a vertical wall. Find, in terms of $R$, the radius of the largest sphere that can roll along the corner between the ball, the wall and the floor (that is, pass through the gap between them while touching the wall and the floor).
