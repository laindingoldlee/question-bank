The semiperimeter of $ ABC $ is 18, so Heron's formula gives area $\sqrt{18\cdot9\cdot7\cdot2}=18\sqrt7 $. Sharing altitude from $ C $, triangle $ CBE $ has $\dfrac{4}{9}$ of that area. Sharing altitude from $ E $ to $ BC $, triangle $ CEF $ has $\dfrac{7}{11}$ of $ CBE $'s area. Thus $[CEF]=18\sqrt7\cdot\dfrac{4}{9}\cdot\dfrac{7}{11}=\boxed{\dfrac{56\sqrt7}{11}}$.
