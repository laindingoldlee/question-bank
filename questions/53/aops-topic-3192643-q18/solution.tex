For circumradius 1, the rectangle has adjacent chord lengths $2\sin75^\circ$ and $2\sin15^\circ$. Its area is $4\sin75^\circ\sin15^\circ=1$. The dodecagon area is $12\cdot(\dfrac{1}{2})\sin30^\circ=3$. Thus the ratio is $\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $\boxed{m+n=4}$.

\end{asy}
