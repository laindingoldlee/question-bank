A heads run of length $k$ contributes $k^2=k+2\binom k2$. Thus $M$ counts every heads position once, plus twice every pair of positions belonging to the same heads run. Two positions separated by $d$ steps belong to the same run exactly when their $d+1$ intervening and endpoint coins are all heads, of probability $2^{-(d+1)}$. With $n$ coins, linearity gives $\mathbb E[M]=\dfrac{n}{2}+\sum_{d=1}^{n-1}(n-d)2^{-d}$. The finite geometric sums simplify this to $\dfrac{3n}{2}-2+2^{1-n}$. At $n=2023$ the answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{6065}{2}+2^{-2022}}$.
