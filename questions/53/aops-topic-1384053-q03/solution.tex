Write $\sigma(m)$ for the sum of the divisors of $m$. We need two abundant numbers differing by $2$, both at least $49$. The abundant numbers from $49$ on start $54,56,60,\dots$ ($49,50,51,52,53,55$ are deficient), and $\sigma(54)=120>108$, $\sigma(56)=120>112$. So $n-1=54$, $n+1=56$ and $n=\boxed{55}$.
