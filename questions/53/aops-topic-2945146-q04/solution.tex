\emph{Move 1:} add $k=2n$ to all $n$ terms. Now every $a_i\ge2n$.

\emph{Moves $2,\dots,n+1$:} for $i=1,2,\dots,n$ in turn, apply (b) to the first $i$ terms with $m_i=a_i-(i-1)$, where $a_i$ is the current (unchanged) value. Since $a_i\ge2n$, $m_i\ge n+1>i-1$. So $a_i=m_i+(i-1)$ with $0\le i-1<m_i$, and it becomes $i-1$. The earlier terms already equal $0,1,\dots,i-2$, which are less than $m_i$, so they are unchanged, and later terms are untouched.

After these $n+1$ moves the sequence is $0,1,\dots,n-1$, which is strictly increasing. $\blacksquare$
