A sequence of $n$ nonnegative integers $a_1,\dots,a_n$ may be changed by two kinds of moves: (a) choose $j$ and a nonnegative integer $k$ and add $k$ to each of $a_1,\dots,a_j$; (b) choose $j$ and a positive integer $m$ and replace each of $a_1,\dots,a_j$ by its remainder modulo $m$. Prove that the sequence can always be made strictly increasing in at most $n+1$ moves.
