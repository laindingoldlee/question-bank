Two truthful people cannot be adjacent. Also three liars cannot be consecutive, since the middle one would make a true claim. Conversely these two restrictions make every claim consistent. Thus between consecutive truthful people there are either one or two liars. If $t$ is the number truthful, then $t\le125-t\le2t$, giving $42\le t\le62$. Both extremes occur: for $t=42$, use 41 gaps of two liars and one gap of one; for $t=62$, use one gap of two and 61 gaps of one. The requested maximum and minimum are $\boxed{(83,63)}$.
