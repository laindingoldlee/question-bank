Let $ABC$ be an isosceles triangle with $AB=AC$, let $\Gamma$ be its circumcircle, and let $O$ be the centre of $\Gamma$. The line $CO$ meets $\Gamma$ again at $D$. The line through $D$ parallel to $AC$ meets the segment $AB$ at $E$. Suppose that $AE:EB=2:1$. Prove that triangle $ABC$ is equilateral.
