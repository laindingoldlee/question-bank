Scale to $A=(0,0),B=(1,0),C=(u,v)$ and put $r=u^2+v^2$, $s=(u-1)^2+v^2$. Acuteness gives $0<u<1$, $v>0$, and $r-u>0$. Orthogonal projection and the two parallel-line conditions give
\[
A_1=B+\dfrac{1-u}{s}(C-B),\quad B_1=\dfrac{u}{r} C,\quad
K=\left(\dfrac{u}{r},0\right),\quad M=\left(\dfrac{r-u}{s},0\right).
\]
For the vectors $\mathbf p=A-A_1,\mathbf q=K-A_1$ and $\mathbf p'=B-B_1,\mathbf q'=M-B_1$, direct expansion gives
\[
\mathbf p\cdot\mathbf q=\mathbf p'\cdot\mathbf q'
=\dfrac{v^2(r-u)}{rs}>0,
\]
\[
|\det(\mathbf p,\mathbf q)|=|\det(\mathbf p',\mathbf q')|
=\dfrac{uv(1-u)}{rs}.
\]
Both angles are acute and their tangents, the absolute determinant divided by the dot product, are equal. Hence the angles themselves are equal.
