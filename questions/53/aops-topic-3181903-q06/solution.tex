The radius is $45$. Joining the centre to all vertices forms six equilateral triangles of side $45$. Their total area is $6\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}\cdot45^2=\dfrac{6075\sqrt3}{2}$. Therefore $a+b+c=6075+3+2=\boxed{6080}$.
