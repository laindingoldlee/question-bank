Let there be $m$ men and $w$ women. Then
\[
m+w=37,\qquad \dfrac{m}{3}+\dfrac{2w}{5}=14 .
\]
Multiplying the second equation by $15$ gives $5m+6w=210$. Substituting $m=37-w$:
\[
5(37-w)+6w=210\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 185+w=210\quad\Longrightarrow\quad w=25,\ m=12 .
\]
So there are $\dfrac{12}{3}=4$ Australian men and $\dfrac{2\cdot25}{5}=10$ Australian women:
\[
\boxed{4\text{ Australian men and }10\text{ Australian women}}.
\]
Check: $4+10=14$, and the Americans are $8$ men and $15$ women, $23$ people in all.
