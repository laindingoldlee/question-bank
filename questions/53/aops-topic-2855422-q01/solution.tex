Let $L=9^{10}\log_{10}7$. Writing $L=k+f$ with $k$ an integer and $0\le f<1$, we have $7^{9^{10}}=10^k\cdot10^f$, so the leading digit is $\lfloor10^f\rfloor$. Here $9^{10}=3486784401$ and, to enough precision, $L\approx2946674663+0.23738$, so $10^f\approx1.727$. The leading digit is $\boxed{1}$. (About $15$ significant digits of $\log_{10}7$ are needed, since $L$ has $10$ digits before the point.)
