Put $r = \dfrac{9}{4}$ and $u = \tan (\dfrac{x}{2}) , v = \tan (\dfrac{y}{2}) , w = \tan (\dfrac{z}{2})$, with degree angles. The positive sine equations and angle sum force each angle into $(0,180^\circ)$. The first equation becomes $u \in \{ \dfrac{r}{a} , \dfrac{a}{r} \}$, and similarly for $v,w$. Since the half-angles sum to $180^\circ$, $u+v+w=uvw$, so $w = \dfrac{u+v}{uv-1} > 0$. Now enumerate $a=1,\ldots,29$, set $b=30-a$, and choose the two possible values of each of $u,v$. Whenever $uv>1$, test $c = \dfrac{r}{w}$ and $c=rw$ for positive integrality. Exact fraction arithmetic gives only $(a,b,c)=(3,27,2)$ and $(27,3,2)$. Conversely their associated positive half-angles have sum $180^\circ$ and satisfy all equations. Thus $abc(a+b+c)=3\cdot27\cdot2\cdot32=5184$, whose last three digits are $\boxed{184}$.
