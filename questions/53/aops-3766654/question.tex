Let $a$, $b$, $c$, be positive integers, and $x$, $y$, and $z$ be positive real numbers such that the following equations hold:

\begin{align*}
\left(a^2+\dfrac{81}{16}\right)\sin(x^\circ)&=\dfrac{9}{2}a \\
\left(b^2+\dfrac{81}{16}\right)\sin(y^\circ)&=\dfrac{9}{2}b \\
\left(c^2+\dfrac{81}{16}\right)\sin(z^\circ)&=\dfrac{9}{2}c \\
a+b &= 30 \\
x+y+z&=360.
\end{align*}Find the last 3 digits of $abc(a+b+c)$.
