Use $G$ as origin, so $A+B+C=0$. Then $X=\dfrac{B+C}{3}=-\dfrac{A}{3}$, with analogous expressions for $Y,Z$. Hence $[ABC]=9[XYZ]=63$. Also $3A=D+B+C=D-A$, so $D=4A$, and similarly $E=4B,F=4C$. Therefore $[DEF]=16[ABC]=\boxed{1008}$.
