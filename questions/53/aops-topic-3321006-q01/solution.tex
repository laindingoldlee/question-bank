Let there be $N$ members and $R$ readers. Let $d,m\in\{0,1\}$ indicate whether Deng and Mao read the book. Then $R-d=\dfrac{N-1}{10}$ and $R-m=\dfrac{N-1}{12}$. Subtracting gives $m-d=\dfrac{N-1}{60}>0$, so $m=1,d=0,N=61$. The first equation gives $R=6$. Thus the fraction is $\boxed{\dfrac{6}{61}}$.
