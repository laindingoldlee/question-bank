In the quarterfinals of a single elimination countdown competition, the $8$ competitors are all of equal skill. When any $2$ of them compete, there is exactly a $50\%$ chance of either one winning. If the initial bracket is randomized, the probability that two of the competitors, Daniel and Anish, face off in one of the rounds can be expressed as $\dfrac{p}{q}$ for relatively prime positive integers $p$, $q$. Find $p + q$.
