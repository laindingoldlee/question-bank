Condition on where Anish lies relative to Daniel. They share a first-round match with probability $\dfrac{1}{7}$, and then certainly meet. They meet in the semifinal with contribution $\dfrac{2}{7}\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}$, or in the final with contribution $\dfrac{4}{7}\cdot\dfrac{1}{16}$. The sum is $\dfrac{1}{4}$, so $p+q=\boxed5$.
