Fill in a Venn diagram from the centre outwards.
\begin{itemize}
\item All three: $3$.
\item Soccer and tennis only: $5-3=2$. Soccer and basketball only: $4-3=1$.
\item Basketball: $19=2+1+3+x$, where $x$ is the number playing tennis and basketball only, so $x=13$.
\item Tennis: $18=t+2+13+3$, where $t$ is the number playing tennis only, so $t=0$.
\end{itemize}
So $\boxed{0}$ students play tennis only. (Also, $14-2-1-3=8$ play soccer only.)
