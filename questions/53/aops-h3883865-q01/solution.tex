The complement must have its chosen elements at least three apart. If it has $k$ elements $a_1<\cdots<a_k$, the compressed positions $a_j-2(j-1)$ form a $k$-subset of $\{1,\ldots,10-2(k-1)\}$. Therefore the counts for $k=0,1,2,3,4$ are $1,10,\binom 82=28,\binom 63=20,\binom 44=1$. No larger $k$ fits. Their sum is $\boxed{60}$.
