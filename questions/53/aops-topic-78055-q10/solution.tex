Clearing denominators, $\dfrac{1}{a}+\dfrac{1}{b}=\dfrac{2}{15}$ becomes $15(a+b)=2ab$, that is $4ab-30a-30b=0$, or
\[
(2a-15)(2b-15)=225 .
\]
Both factors are odd integers greater than $-15$. If both were negative, each would have absolute value less than $15$, and their product would be less than $15\cdot15=225$; so both are positive. With $a\ge b$ we need $2a-15\ge 2b-15$, so we pair the divisors of $225=3^2\cdot5^2$ as
\[
225\cdot1,\quad 75\cdot3,\quad 45\cdot5,\quad 25\cdot9,\quad 15\cdot15,
\]
giving $(a,b)=(120,8),\ (45,9),\ (30,10),\ (20,12),\ (15,15)$. There are $\boxed{5}$ ways.
