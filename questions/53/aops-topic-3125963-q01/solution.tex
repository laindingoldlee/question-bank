(a) Set $d=m-4$. Then $d$ is a signed divisor of $8$, and $(m,n)=(4+d,4+\dfrac{8}{d})$ for $d\in\{\pm1,\pm2,\pm4,\pm8\}$. Thus the ordered pairs are $(5,12),(6,8),(8,6),(12,5),(3,-4),(2,0),(0,2),(-4,3)$. (b) Expanding the equation gives $mn-4m-4n+8=0$, so $m^2+n^2=(m+n-4)^2$. This proves the claim for every solution.
