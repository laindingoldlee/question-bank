On the AMC 10 there are $25$ questions; each correct answer scores $6$ points, each wrong answer $0$ points and each unanswered question $1.5$ points. A student answers exactly $x$ questions ($1\le x\le25$), of which $c$ are correct ($0\le c\le x$), and leaves the rest blank. For how many pairs $(x,c)$ is the score an integer multiple of $x$? (Zero counts as a multiple.)
