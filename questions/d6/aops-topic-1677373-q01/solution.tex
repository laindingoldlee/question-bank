List the triples of positive whole numbers with product $36$ (in non-decreasing order) and their sums:
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccccccc}
\text{ages}&1,1,36&1,2,18&1,3,12&1,4,9&1,6,6&2,2,9&2,3,6&3,3,4\\\hline
\text{sum}&38&21&16&14&13&13&11&10
\end{array}
\]
The mother says that even the sum would not determine the ages. Every sum except $13$ belongs to only one triple, so the sum must be $13$, and the ages are $1,6,6$ or $2,2,9$. Finally, ``my oldest daughter'' says there is a single oldest daughter, which rules out $1,6,6$ (two daughters of age $6$ would share the title). The daughters are $\boxed{2,\ 2\text{ and }9}$ years old.
