A census taker asks a woman how many children she has and how old they are.

``I have three daughters, their ages are whole numbers, and the product of their ages is $36$,'' says the mother.

``That's not enough information,'' says the census taker.

``I could tell you the sum of their ages, but you would still be stumped.''

``I wish you would tell me something more.''

``Okay: my oldest daughter, Annie, likes dogs.''

What are the ages of the three daughters?
