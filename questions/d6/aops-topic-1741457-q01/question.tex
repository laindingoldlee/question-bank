In a card game, Noah draws cards at random, without replacement, from a deck of $21$ cards: $20$ cards numbered $1$ through $20$ and a joker. Noah keeps all of the cards he draws before he draws the joker. What is the probability that the cards Noah keeps include exactly $4$ prime-numbered cards? Express your answer as a common fraction.
