The constant term is nonzero, so no root is zero. The expression rearranges to $\sum_{n=1}^{2031}b_n-\sum_{n=1}^{2031}\dfrac{1}{b_n}$. Vieta gives the first sum 0 from the absent $x^{2030}$ coefficient. The reciprocal sum is negative the linear coefficient divided by the constant coefficient, namely $-\dfrac{2022}{2028}$. Thus the answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{337}{338}}$.
