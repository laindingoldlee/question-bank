Factor: $(x-y)(x+y)=303=3\cdot101$, where $101$ is prime. Since $x,y>0$, $x+y$ is positive, so $x-y$ is positive too, and $x-y<x+y$. The factorisations of $303$ into a smaller and a larger positive factor are $1\cdot303$ and $3\cdot101$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $x-y=1$, $x+y=303$: $x=152$, $y=151$;
\item $x-y=3$, $x+y=101$: $x=52$, $y=49$.
\end{itemize}
So the solutions are $\boxed{(x,y)=(52,49)\text{ and }(152,151)}$. (Check: $152^2-151^2=152+151=303$.)
