Consider the data set
\[
3,\ 5,\ 6,\ 6,\ 8,\ 10,\ 11,\ 15 .
\]
Find
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] the mean, the median, the mode and the range;
\item[(b)] the five-number summary (minimum, first quartile $Q_1$, median, third quartile $Q_3$, maximum), where $Q_1$ and $Q_3$ are the medians of the lower half and the upper half of the data;
\item[(c)] the interquartile range $\text{IQR}=Q_3-Q_1$;
\item[(d)] the mean absolute deviation (MAD), the mean of the distances of the data values from the mean.
\end{enumerate}
Describe the box plot of the data.
