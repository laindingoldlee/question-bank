If the outer pair mismatches, there are $9$ leading-digit choices, $9$ unequal last-digit choices, $10$ choices for the matching inner pair, and $10$ middle digits: $8100$. If the inner pair mismatches, there are $9$ matching outer-pair choices, $10\cdot9$ ordered unequal inner digits, and $10$ middle digits: another $8100$. Thus $\boxed{16200}$.
