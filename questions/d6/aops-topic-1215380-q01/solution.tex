In the $3$-$4$-$5$ triangle the numbers $3$ and $4$ are the \emph{legs} and $5$ is the hypotenuse. Here $400$ is the hypotenuse, so the triangle is not a scaled $3$-$4$-$5$ triangle. Pat's answer is also impossible on its face: the hypotenuse is the longest side, so a leg cannot be $500>400$.

By the Pythagorean theorem the other leg is
\[
\sqrt{400^2-300^2}=\sqrt{70000}=\boxed{100\sqrt7\ \text{cm}}\approx264.6\text{ cm}.
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s=0.2){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
pair C=(0,0),A=(0,3),B=(2.6458,0);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); draw(ra(C,A-C,B-C,0.2));
label("$300$",(A+C)/2,W); label("$400$",(A+B)/2,NE); label("$100\sqrt7$",(B+C)/2,S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
