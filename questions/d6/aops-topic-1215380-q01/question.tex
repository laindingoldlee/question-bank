Pat knows that one leg of a right triangle is $300$ cm and the hypotenuse is $400$ cm. Pat notes that $300=3\cdot100$ and $400=4\cdot100$ and uses the $3$-$4$-$5$ Pythagorean triple to conclude that the other leg is $5\cdot100=500$ cm. Explain why Pat's method does not work, and find the correct length of the other leg.
