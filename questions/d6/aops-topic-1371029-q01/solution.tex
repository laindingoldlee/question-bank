Each interior angle of a hexagon with equal angles is $\dfrac{720^\circ}{6}=120^\circ$. Extend the sides $AB$, $CD$, $EF$ until they meet pairwise at $X$ (lines $AB$ and $CD$), $Y$ (lines $CD$ and $EF$) and $Z$ (lines $EF$ and $AB$).

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0), B=A+4*dir(0), C=B+1*dir(60), D=C+3*dir(120), Ep=D+2*dir(180), F=Ep+3*dir(240);
pair X=extension(A,B,C,D), Y=extension(C,D,Ep,F), Z=extension(Ep,F,A,B);
fill(B--X--C--cycle,palegray); fill(D--Y--Ep--cycle,palegray); fill(F--Z--A--cycle,palegray);
draw(B--X--C,dashed); draw(D--Y--Ep,dashed); draw(F--Z--A,dashed);
draw(A--B--C--D--Ep--F--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
dot(X);dot(Y);dot(Z);
label("$A$",A,S); label("$B$",B,S); label("$C$",C,E); label("$D$",D,NE); label("$E$",Ep,NW); label("$F$",F,W);
label("$X$",X,SE); label("$Y$",Y,N); label("$Z$",Z,SW);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

In triangle $BXC$, the angles at $B$ and $C$ are exterior angles of the hexagon, $180^\circ-120^\circ=60^\circ$, so triangle $BXC$ is equilateral and $BX=XC=BC$. In the same way triangles $DYE$ and $FZA$ are equilateral, with $DY=YE=DE$ and $FZ=ZA=FA$. Then triangle $XYZ$ has all angles $60^\circ$, so it is equilateral, and its sides are
\[
ZX=ZA+AB+BX=AF+AB+BC,\qquad XY=XC+CD+DY=BC+CD+DE,\qquad YZ=YE+EF+FZ=DE+EF+AF .
\]
From $ZX=XY$: $AF+AB=CD+DE$, i.e.\ $AB-DE=CD-AF$.

From $XY=YZ$: $BC+CD=EF+AF$, i.e.\ $EF-BC=CD-AF$.

Hence $AB-DE=EF-BC=CD-AF$. $\blacksquare$
