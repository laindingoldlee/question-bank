Take $B=(0,0),C=(10,0),A=(5,5\sqrt3),P=(20,0)$. Then $PA=\sqrt{15^2+(5\sqrt3)^2}=10\sqrt3$ and $PC=10$. The ratio is $\boxed{\sqrt3}$.

\end{asy}
