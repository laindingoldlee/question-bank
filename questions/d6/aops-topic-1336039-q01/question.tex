Each of the valves $A$, $B$ and $C$, when open, releases water into a tank at its own constant rate. With all three valves open, the tank fills in $1$ hour; with only valves $A$ and $C$ open it takes $1.5$ hours; and with only valves $B$ and $C$ open it takes $2$ hours. How long will it take to fill the tank with only valves $A$ and $B$ open?
