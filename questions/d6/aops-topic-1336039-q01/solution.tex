Let $a$, $b$, $c$ be the rates in tanks per hour. Then
\[
a+b+c=1,\qquad a+c=\dfrac{2}{3},\qquad b+c=\dfrac{1}{2} .
\]
Subtracting, $b=1-\dfrac{2}{3}=\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $a=1-\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{1}{2}$, so $c=\dfrac{1}{6}$. Then $a+b=\dfrac{5}{6}$, and valves $A$ and $B$ fill the tank in $\dfrac{6}{5}$ hours $=\boxed{72\text{ minutes}}$ ($1.2$ hours).
