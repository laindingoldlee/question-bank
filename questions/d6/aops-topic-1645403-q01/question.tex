\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Factor $x^4+y^4$ as a product of two quadratic polynomials with real coefficients.
\item[(b)] Show that $x^4+y^4$ cannot be written as a product of two non-constant polynomials with integer coefficients.
\end{enumerate}
