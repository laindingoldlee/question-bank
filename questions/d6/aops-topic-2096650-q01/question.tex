When a polynomial $P(x)$ is divided by $(x-r)^2$, the remainder has the form $ax+b$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] By the factor theorem, $P(x)-P(r)=(x-r)S(x)$ for some polynomial $S(x)$. Show that the remainder when $P(x)$ is divided by $(x-r)^2$ is
\[S(r)\,(x-r)+P(r).\]
\item[(b)] Find the remainder when $x^{100}$ is divided by $(x-1)^2$.
\end{enumerate}
