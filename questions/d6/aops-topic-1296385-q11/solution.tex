The digit sum is even when the number of odd digits is $0$ or $2$.
\begin{itemize}
\item No odd digits: three distinct even digits, not leading with $0$: $5\cdot4\cdot3-4\cdot3=60-12=48$.
\item Two odd digits and one even: choose the two odd digits in order of placement and the even digit. Count arrangements of $\{o_1,o_2,e\}$: if $e\ne0$: $\dbinom52\cdot4\cdot3!=240$; if $e=0$: $\dbinom52\cdot(3!-2!)=10\cdot4=40$. Total $280$.
\end{itemize}
The answer is $48+280=\boxed{328}$.
