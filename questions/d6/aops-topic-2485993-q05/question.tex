A frog starts at the origin of the coordinate plane. Every minute it jumps $1$ unit up, down, left or right, each with probability $\dfrac14$, independently of its earlier jumps. What is the probability that after $60$ minutes the frog is back at the origin? Express the answer using binomial coefficients.
