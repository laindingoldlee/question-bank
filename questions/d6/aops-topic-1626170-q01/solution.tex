Factor: $5096=2^3\cdot637=2^3\cdot7^2\cdot13$ and $117=3^2\cdot13$. The least common multiple takes the highest power of each prime:
\[
\operatorname{lcm}[5096,117]=2^3\cdot3^2\cdot7^2\cdot13=\boxed{45864}.
\]
(Equivalently, $\gcd(5096,117)=13$ and $\dfrac{5096\cdot117}{13}=5096\cdot9=45864$.)
