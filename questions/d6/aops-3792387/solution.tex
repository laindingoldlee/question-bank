For any coordinate, the recurrence is $a_n=\dfrac{a_{n-1}+a_{n-2}+a_{n-3}}{3}$. The weighted sum $a_n+2a_{n+1}+3a_{n+2}$ is invariant: subtract two consecutive such sums and use the recurrence. Taking limits yields $L=\dfrac{A_0+2A_1+3A_2}{6}$ in vector form. Choose $A_0=(0,0),A_1=(24,0),A_2=(12,12\sqrt3)$. Then $L=(14,6\sqrt3)$, so $\boxed{A_0L^2=196+108=304}$.
