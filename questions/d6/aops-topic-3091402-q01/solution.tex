Each person misses your fixed birthday with probability $\dfrac{364}{365}$. Independence gives probability $\left(\dfrac{364}{365}\right)^{50}$ that everyone misses it. Taking the complement gives $\boxed{1-\left(\dfrac{364}{365}\right)^{50}}\approx0.1282 $.
