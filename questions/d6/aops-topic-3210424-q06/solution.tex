The locus is a circle centred at $M$ of radius 12. Since $AM=12\sqrt3>12$, the shortest distance from $A$ to the circle is $AM-12=12\sqrt3-12=\sqrt{432}-12$. Thus $\boxed{a+b=444}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,12*sqrt(3)),B=(-12,0),C=(12,0),M=(0,0),P=(0,12);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(circle(M,12),blue);draw(A--M,dashed);dot(P);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,W);label("$C$",C,E);label("$M$",M,S);label("$P$",P,E);
\end{asy}
