Let $X=AC\cap BD$. The intersecting-chord angle theorem gives $90^\circ=\dfrac{1}{2}(\widehat{AB}+\widehat{CD})$, using the arcs intercepted by that angle and its vertical angle. The corresponding half-central angles are complementary. Thus $AB^2+CD^2=4(\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta)=4$. Since the lengths are $x,3x$, $10x^2=4$ and $\boxed{x=\dfrac{\sqrt{10}}{5}}$.
\begin{asy}
import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
real t=degrees(asin(1/sqrt(10)));pair A=dir(-t),B=dir(t),C=dir(90+t),D=dir(270-t);draw(unitcircle);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C^^B--D,blue);label("$A$",A,(1,-1));label("$B$",B,(1,1));label("$C$",C,(-1,1));label("$D$",D,(-1,-1));label("$x$",(A+B)/2,(1,0));label("$3x$",(C+D)/2,(-1,0));
\end{asy}
