Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle with $AB<AC$. A circle through $B$ and $C$ that does not pass through $A$ meets the sides $AB$ and $AC$ again at $E$ and $F$ respectively ($E\ne B$, $F\ne C$). Lines $BF$ and $CE$ meet at $D$. Let $P$ be the midpoint of $BC$, and let $K$ be the reflection of $D$ in $P$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that triangles $KBC$ and $DFE$ are similar, and that $\dfrac{AE}{AC}=\dfrac{DE}{CK}$.
\item[(b)] Let $M$ and $N$ be the feet of the perpendiculars from $D$ to $AB$ and $AC$. Prove that $MN\perp AK$ and that $MA^2+NK^2=NA^2+MK^2$.
\item[(c)] Let $I$ and $J$ be the midpoints of $AD$ and $MN$. Prove that $I$, $J$, $P$ are collinear.
\item[(d)] Line $IJ$ meets the circumcircle of triangle $IMN$ again at $T$. Prove that $AD$ is tangent to the circumcircle of triangle $DTJ$.
\end{enumerate}
