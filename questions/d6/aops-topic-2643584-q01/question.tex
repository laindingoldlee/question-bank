\[\dfrac{\left(a^{10}-b^{10}\right)\left(b^{10}-c^{10}\right)\left(c^{10}-a^{10}\right)}{\left(a^9+b^9\right)\left(b^9+c^9\right)\left(c^9+a^9\right)}\ge\dfrac{125\left((a-b)^3+(b-c)^3+(c-a)^3\right)}{3}.\]
