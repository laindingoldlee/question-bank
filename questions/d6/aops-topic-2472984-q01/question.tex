A student writes $0.\overline{0}1$ for ``the decimal with infinitely many zeros after the point, followed by a $1$'', and asks whether it equals $0$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Explain why $0.\overline{0}1$ does not describe a decimal expansion.
\item[(b)] Prove that if a real number $x$ satisfies $0\le x<10^{-n}$ for every positive integer $n$, then $x=0$.
\end{enumerate}
