A list contains $20$ numbers. For each positive integer $n$ from $1$ to $20$, the $n$th number in the list is either $\dfrac{1}{2}n$ or $2n$. If the mean of the numbers in the list is exactly $12$, and the product of the numbers is $P$, what is the greatest possible value of $\dfrac{P}{20!}$?
