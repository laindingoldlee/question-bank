Let $A=(x_1,y_1)$ and $B=(x_2,y_2)$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the coordinates of the point $P$ on the segment $AB$ with $AP=\dfrac13AB$. More generally, find the point $P$ on $AB$ with $AP=\dfrac1nAB$ for a positive integer $n$.
\item[(b)] A student guesses that the point in (a) is $G=\left(\dfrac{x_1+x_2}{3},\dfrac{y_1+y_2}{3}\right)$, by analogy with the midpoint formula. Show that this guess is wrong in general, and find exactly when it gives the right point.
\end{enumerate}
