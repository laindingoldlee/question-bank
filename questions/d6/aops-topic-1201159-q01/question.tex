A group of $2k$ people is to be chosen from $n$ married couples ($2k\le n$). In how many ways can this be done if
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] the group contains no married couple;
\item[(b)] the group contains at least one married couple;
\item[(c)] the group contains at least two married couples?
\end{enumerate}
