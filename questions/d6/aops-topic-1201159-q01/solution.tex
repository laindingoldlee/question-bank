(a) Choose the $2k$ couples that are represented, then one person from each: $\dbinom{n}{2k}2^{2k}$.

(b) Subtract (a) from all choices: $\dbinom{2n}{2k}-\dbinom n{2k}2^{2k}$.

(c) Groups with exactly one couple: choose that couple ($n$ ways), then $2k-2$ other couples each contributing one person: $n\dbinom{n-1}{2k-2}2^{2k-2}$. So the number with at least two couples is
\[\dbinom{2n}{2k}-\dbinom n{2k}2^{2k}-n\dbinom{n-1}{2k-2}2^{2k-2}.\]
$\boxed{\text{(a) }\dbinom n{2k}2^{2k};\ \text{(b) }\dbinom{2n}{2k}-\dbinom n{2k}2^{2k};\ \text{(c) }\dbinom{2n}{2k}-\dbinom n{2k}2^{2k}-n\dbinom{n-1}{2k-2}2^{2k-2}}$
