Collect terms on each side. The left side is $24x+23$. On the right, the $x$-terms are $x+x+7x+x+11x+3x=24x$ and the constants are $32+3+9+7+3=54$. So the equation is
\[
24x+23=24x+54\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad 23=54,
\]
which is false for every $x$. The equation has $\boxed{\text{no solutions}}$.
