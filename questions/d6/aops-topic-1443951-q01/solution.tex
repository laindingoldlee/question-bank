The $k$-th term is $10^k-1$, so
\[
N=\sum_{k=1}^{999}\left(10^k-1\right)=(10+100+\cdots+10^{999})-999=\underbrace{11\ldots1}_{999\text{ ones}}0-999 .
\]
The number $\underbrace{11\ldots1}_{999}0$ ends in the four digits $1110$. Subtracting $999$ only affects these four digits, since $1110-999=111$, i.e.\ the last four digits become $0111$. Hence
\[
N=\underbrace{11\ldots1}_{996\text{ ones}}0111 ,
\]
and the digit $1$ appears $996+3=\boxed{999}$ times.
