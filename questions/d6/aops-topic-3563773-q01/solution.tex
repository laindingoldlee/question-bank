Modulo $2$, $n^5\equiv n$. Modulo $3$, the residues $0,1,-1$ likewise satisfy $n^5\equiv n$. Modulo $5$, this holds for $n\equiv0$ and by Fermat's little theorem for nonzero residues. Thus $2,3,5$ all divide $n^5-n$. They are pairwise coprime, so their product $\boxed{30}$ divides it.
