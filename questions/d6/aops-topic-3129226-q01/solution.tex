Consider just the first three nightly totals. They increase strictly, and the difference between the first and third is at most 20. Assign each total one divisor from 22 or 25 that divides it. Two of the three totals have the same assigned divisor. Their positive difference must then be at least 22, contradicting the bound of 20. Thus already the first three reports cannot all be correct.
