Write $a=2a'$, and so on, with $a',b',c',d',e'$ nonnegative integers. The equation becomes $a'+b'+c'+d'+e'=55$. By stars and bars the number of solutions is
\[\dbinom{55+4}{4}=\dbinom{59}{4}=\boxed{455126}.\]
