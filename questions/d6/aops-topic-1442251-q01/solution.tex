Any two chords that cross inside the circle have four distinct endpoints, and conversely any four of the points, taken in order around the circle as $P_1P_2P_3P_4$, give exactly one crossing pair of chords, $P_1P_3$ and $P_2P_4$ (the other two pairings do not cross). So the number of crossing pairs is $\dbinom{2016}{4}$. Different crossing pairs give different points exactly when no three chords pass through one interior point, which can be arranged by placing the points generically. So the maximum is
\[
\dbinom{2016}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{2016!}{4!\,2012!}}.
\]
