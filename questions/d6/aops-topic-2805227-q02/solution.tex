Rewrite as $xy-x-y+1=4$, i.e. $(x-1)(y-1)=4$. Both factors are non-negative integers (as $x,y\ge1$) with product $4$: $(1,4),(2,2),(4,1)$. So $(x,y)=\boxed{(2,5),(3,3),(5,2)}$.
