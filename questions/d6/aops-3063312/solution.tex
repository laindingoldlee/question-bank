Set $a=\ln x,b=\ln y,c=\ln z$, all positive. Multiplication gives $ab+c=3b=4a$, so $b = \dfrac{4 a}{3}$ and $c = 4 a - \dfrac{4 a^2}{3}$, with $0<a<3$. The objective is $a c = 4 a^2 - \dfrac{4 a^3}{3}$. Its derivative is $4a(2-a)$, positive up to $a=2$ and negative afterward. Therefore the maximum is $\boxed{\dfrac{16}{3}}$ at $(a,b,c) = (2 , \dfrac{8}{3} , \dfrac{8}{3})$, corresponding to positive admissible $x,y,z$.
