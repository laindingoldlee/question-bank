The largest total uses as many octagons as possible: $3$ octagons and $1$ triangle, worth $36+4=40$ points. The smallest uses $4$ triangles, worth $16$ points. The difference is $40-16=24$: $\boxed{\textbf{(D)}\ 24}$.
