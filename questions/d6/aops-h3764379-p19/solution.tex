Positive prime differences imply $p>q>r$. If all three were odd, both $p-q$ and $q-r$ would be 2, making $p-r=4$, impossible. Thus $r=2$ and $p-q=2$. The three numbers $q-2,q,q+2$ must be prime; one is divisible by 3, forcing $q=5$ (the possibility $q=3$ gives $q-2=1$). Hence $(p,q,r)=(7,5,2)$ and $p+2q+3r=\boxed{23}$.
