Measure distance in hundredths of a light-year. Write the increasing positive integers as $a_1<\cdots<a_7$, with sum $35$. Set $b_i=a_i-i$. Then $0\le b_1\le\cdots\le b_7$ and their sum is $35-(1+\cdots+7)=7$. Thus plans correspond to partitions of $7$ into at most seven parts, with zeros padding the list. This is every partition of $7$. The standard recurrence $P(n,k)=P(n,k-1)+P(n-k,k)$ for partitions using parts at most $k$, starting $P(0,k)=1$, gives $p(0),\ldots,p(7)=1,1,2,3,5,7,11,15$. Therefore there are $\boxed{15}$ plans.
