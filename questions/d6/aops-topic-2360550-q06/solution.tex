Since $i^2=-1$, the equation is $z^2+iz-20=0$. By the quadratic formula,
\[z=\dfrac{-i\pm\sqrt{i^2+80}}{2}=\dfrac{-i\pm\sqrt{79}}{2}.\]
So $z=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt{79}-i}{2}\ \text{ or }\ \dfrac{-\sqrt{79}-i}{2}}$. (There is no real solution: for real $z$ the imaginary part $z$ of the left side must vanish, and $z=0$ gives $-1\ne19$.)
