Take $A=(0,0),B=(12,0),C=(0,16)$. Since $BC=20$,
\[
D=B+\dfrac{1}{5}(C-B)=\left(\dfrac{48}{5},\dfrac{16}{5}\right),
\]
\[
E=C+\dfrac{2}{5}(B-C)=\left(\dfrac{24}{5},\dfrac{48}{5}\right).
\]
The slopes of $AD,AE$ are $\dfrac{1}{3},2$. Their acute angle difference has tangent
\[
\dfrac{2-\dfrac{1}{3}}{1+2\cdot\dfrac{1}{3}}=1.
\]
Hence $\boxed{\angle DAE=45^\circ}$.

\end{asy}
