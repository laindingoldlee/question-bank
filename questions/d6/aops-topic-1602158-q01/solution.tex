\textbf{Factor.} $30030=2\cdot3\cdot5\cdot7\cdot11\cdot13$. Using $77=7\cdot11$, $91=7\cdot13$, $143=11\cdot13$,
\[
A=7^{m+4034}\,11^{2018}\,13^{2016}+7^{p+4035}\,11^{2017}\,13^{2017}=11^{2017}\,13^{2016}\,B,\qquad B=11\cdot7^{m+4034}+13\cdot7^{p+4035}.
\]
\textbf{The primes $7$, $11$, $13$} always divide $A$ (each appears in $11^{2017}13^{2016}$ or in both terms of $B$).

\textbf{The primes $2$ and $3$} always divide $B$: both terms of $B$ are odd, so $B$ is even; and $7\equiv1\pmod3$ gives $B\equiv11+13=24\equiv0\pmod3$.

\textbf{The prime $5$.} Since $5\nmid11^{2017}13^{2016}$, we need $5\mid B$. Modulo $5$, $7\equiv2$, $11\equiv1$, $13\equiv3$, and $2^4\equiv1$; also $4034\equiv2$ and $4035\equiv3\pmod4$. Hence
\[
B\equiv2^{m+2}+3\cdot2^{p+3}\pmod5 .
\]
Now $3\cdot2^{p+3}=24\cdot2^p\equiv-2^p\equiv-2^{p+4}\pmod5$, so $B\equiv2^{m+2}-2^{p+4}\pmod5$. The powers $2^0,2^1,2^2,2^3$ are distinct modulo $5$ and repeat with period $4$, so $5\mid B$ exactly when $m+2\equiv p+4\pmod4$, i.e.\ $m\equiv p+2\pmod4$.

Therefore $30030\mid A$ if and only if $m\equiv p+2\pmod 4$. In particular every pair $(m,p)=(k+2,k)$ with $k\ge0$ works, so there are infinitely many such pairs. $\blacksquare$
