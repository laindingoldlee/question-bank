Let
\[
A=7^m\cdot77^{2018}\cdot91^{2016}+7^{4035+p}\cdot143^{2017},
\]
where $m$ and $p$ are nonnegative integers. Prove that $A$ is divisible by $30030$ if and only if $m\equiv p+2\pmod4$, and conclude that there are infinitely many pairs $(m,p)$ for which $30030\mid A$.
