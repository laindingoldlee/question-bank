First reduce the fraction: $\dfrac{3600}{243}=\dfrac{400}{27}$ (divide by $9$). Since $27=3^3$ is a perfect cube,
\[\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{400}{27}}=\dfrac{\sqrt[3]{400}}{3},\]
and $400=8\cdot50=2^3\cdot50$ gives $\sqrt[3]{400}=2\sqrt[3]{50}$. So
\[\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{3600}{243}}=\boxed{\dfrac{2\sqrt[3]{50}}{3}}\approx2.456 .\]
($50=2\cdot5^2$ has no cube factor, so this is fully simplified.)
