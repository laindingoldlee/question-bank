In the cryptarithm
\[
\begin{array}{cccccc}
 & & S & E & N & D\\
+ & & M & O & R & E\\\hline
 & M & O & N & E & Y
\end{array}
\]
different letters stand for different digits, the same letter always stands for the same digit, and the leading digits $S$ and $M$ are not $0$. Find the value of each letter.
