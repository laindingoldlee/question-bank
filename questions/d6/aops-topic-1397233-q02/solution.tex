There are $\binom{100}{3}=161700$ equally likely sets of three books, and $\binom{50}{3}=19600$ sets of each genre. The probability is
\[
\dfrac{2\cdot19600}{161700}=\dfrac{392}{1617}=\boxed{\dfrac{8}{33}} .
\]
(Equivalently: $\dfrac{49}{99}\cdot\dfrac{48}{98}=\dfrac{8}{33}$.)
