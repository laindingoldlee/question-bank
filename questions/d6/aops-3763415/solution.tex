Set $A=(0,8),B=(-6,0),C=(6,0)$, using the isosceles altitude. Write $P=(x,y)$ inside the triangle. The right angle $APC$ gives $x^2+y^2-6x-8y=0$. Taking tangents of $\angle ABP=\angle PCB$ gives $\dfrac{8(x+6)-6y}{6(x+6)+8y} = \dfrac{y}{6-x}$; all denominators are positive in the interior. Simplifying gives $x^2+y^2+9y-36=0$, hence $6x+17y=36$. Intersecting with the circle equation gives $P=C$ (excluded) or $P = (- \dfrac{24}{13} , \dfrac{36}{13})$. Line $BP$ meets $AC$ at $D = (2 , \dfrac{16}{3})$, so $\boxed{B D = \dfrac{8 \sqrt{13}}{3}}$.
