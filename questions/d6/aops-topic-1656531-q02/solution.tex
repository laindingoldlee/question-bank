Put A at $0$ and B at $1$ on a number line. Then C is at $0.6$ and D at $0.4\cdot0.6=0.24$. The fraction of the way from D to B at which C lies is
\[
\dfrac{0.6-0.24}{1-0.24}=\dfrac{0.36}{0.76}=\boxed{\textbf{(d)}\ \dfrac{9}{19}} .
\]
