Work backwards from the end, undoing each step.
\begin{itemize}
\item After taking out a third she had $\$500$, which is two thirds of the previous amount: before that she had $500\cdot\dfrac{3}{2}=750$.
\item Before the second doubling: $750\div2=375$.
\item Before taking out $\$100$: $375+100=475$.
\item Before the first doubling: $475\div2=237.5$.
\item Before adding $\$200$: $237.5-200=37.5$.
\end{itemize}
So $x=\boxed{37.50}$ dollars. Check: $37.5+200=237.5$, then $475$, $375$, $750$, and $750-250=500$.
