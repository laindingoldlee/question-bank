Both variables are nonzero. Put $u=y+\dfrac{1}{y}$. The equations give $x=\dfrac{u}{2}$ and $7x=u^2-2$, hence $2u^2-7u-4=0$. Its roots are 4 and $-\dfrac{1}{2}$. For real nonzero $y$, $|y+\dfrac{1}{y}|\ge2$, so only $u=4$ remains. Thus $x=2$ and $y^2-4y+1=0$, giving $\boxed{(2,2+\sqrt3),(2,2-\sqrt3)}$.
