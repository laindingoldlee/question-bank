Give each student one notebook first; then distribute the remaining $3$ notebooks freely among $4$ students. By stars and bars this is $\dbinom{3+3}{3}=20$. (Equivalently, choose $3$ of the $6$ gaps between $7$ notebooks in a row: $\dbinom63=20$.) The answer is $\boxed{20}$.
