Rhonda has $10$ quarts of salt and candy wrappers that hold $5$, $2$ or $1$ fluid ounces of salt. She fills wrappers completely until all the salt is used. In how many ways can she do this, if two ways are the same when they use the same numbers of wrappers of each size? ($1$ quart $=32$ fluid ounces.)
