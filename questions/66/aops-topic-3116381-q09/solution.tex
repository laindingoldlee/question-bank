Multiply by $2$ and complete the product to obtain $(2x+7)(2y+5)=147$. Each signed divisor $d$ of $147$ determines exactly one pair $x=\dfrac{d-7}{2},y=\dfrac{\dfrac{147}{d}-5}{2}$; these are integers because every divisor is odd. Since $147=3\cdot7^2$, there are $2(2)(3)=12$ signed divisors, so $\boxed{12}$ pairs. The sums of $d$ and $\dfrac{147}{d}$ over signed divisors are both zero by pairing signs. Thus $\sum(x+y)=-6\cdot12=\boxed{-72}$.
