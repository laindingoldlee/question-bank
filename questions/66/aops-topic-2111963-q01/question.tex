John is learning to ride a bike, and he gets a piece of candy if he rides $500$ ft without stopping. Two things can make him stop.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Fear.} In the first $250$ ft he stops because he gets scared with probability $\dfrac{1}{3}$; in the second $250$ ft this probability is $\dfrac{3}{2}$ times as large.
\item \textbf{Obstacles.} In the first $100$ ft he stops because of an obstacle with probability $\dfrac{1}{8}$; in each later $100$-ft stretch this probability is $\dfrac{2}{5}$ of what it was in the stretch before.
\end{itemize}
All these events are independent. What is the probability that John gets a piece of candy?
