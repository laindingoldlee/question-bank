Equal sides give $\angle B=\angle C=75^\circ$, so $\angle A=30^\circ$. Therefore the area is $\dfrac{1}{2}(4)(4)\sin30^\circ=\boxed4$.

import geometry; usepackage("amsmath"); size(240);
pair A=(0,3.8637),B=(-1.0353,0),C=(1.0353,0);draw(A--B--C--cycle);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);
\end{asy}
