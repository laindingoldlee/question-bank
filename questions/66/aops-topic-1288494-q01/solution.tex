Let the integers be $n,n+1,n+2$. Multiplying $\dfrac{1}{n}+\dfrac{1}{n+1}+\dfrac{1}{n+2}=\dfrac{47}{n(n+1)(n+2)}$ by the product gives
\[
(n+1)(n+2)+n(n+2)+n(n+1)=47\iff3n^2+6n+2=47\iff n^2+2n-15=0,
\]
so $n=3$ (the root $-5$ is not positive). The sum is $\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{5}=\boxed{\dfrac{47}{60}}$.
