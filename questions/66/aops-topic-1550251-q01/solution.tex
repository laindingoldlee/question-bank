Let $S=\displaystyle\sum_{n\ge1}\dfrac{2n}{5^n}$; the series converges, since its terms are eventually smaller than those of a convergent geometric series. Then
\[
\dfrac {S}{5}=\dfrac{2}{25}+\dfrac{4}{125}+\dfrac{6}{625}+\cdots ,
\]
and subtracting term by term,
\[
S-\dfrac {S}{5}=\dfrac{2}{5}+\dfrac{2}{25}+\dfrac{2}{125}+\cdots=\dfrac{\dfrac{2}{5}}{1-\dfrac{1}{5}}=\dfrac{1}{2} .
\]
So $\dfrac{4}{5}S=\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $S=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{8}}$.
