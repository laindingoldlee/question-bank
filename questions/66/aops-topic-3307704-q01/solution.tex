For three boundary vertices, let $a,b,c$ be their positive cyclic gaps. Each triangle angle is $22.5^\circ$ times the opposite gap, so all gaps must be less than $4$. Since they sum to $8$, they must be $2,3,3$. The unique gap of length $2$ starts at any of $8$ vertices, giving $8$ triangles.
For a triangle using the centre, its central angle must be acute, hence $45^\circ$. Its other vertices must be adjacent, giving another $8$ triangles, each with base angles $67.5^\circ$. Total $\boxed{16}$.

\end{asy}
