For an even term $m>2$, the next term $\dfrac{m}{2}$ is smaller. For an odd term $m>1$, two steps give $\dfrac{m+1}{2}<m$. Thus from every integer greater than 2 a smaller positive integer is reached in at most two steps. Infinite strict descent in positive integers is impossible, so eventually 1 or 2 is reached. These map to each other, giving the asserted cycle.
