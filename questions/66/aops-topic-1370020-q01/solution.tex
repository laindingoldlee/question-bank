Write everything with primes: $9^{12}=3^{24}$ and $12^9=2^{18}3^9$. So
\[
9^{12}-12^9=3^9\left(3^{15}-2^{18}\right)=3^9\left((3^5)^3-(2^6)^3\right)=3^9\,(3^5-2^6)\left(3^{10}+3^5\cdot2^6+2^{12}\right)
\]
by the difference of cubes. Here $3^5-2^6=243-64=179$ and $3^{10}+3^5\cdot2^6+2^{12}=59049+15552+4096=78697$.

The number $179$ is prime: it is not divisible by $2,3,5,7,11,13$, and $17^2>179$. The other factor $78697$ is itself prime (trial division by the primes up to $\sqrt{78697}\approx280.5$ finds no factor). So
\[
9^{12}-12^9=3^9\cdot179\cdot78697,
\]
and the only three-digit prime factor is $\boxed{179}$.
