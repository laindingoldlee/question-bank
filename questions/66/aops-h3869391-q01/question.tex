Let the sequence of rationals \(x_1,x_2,\ldots\) be defined by

\[
x_1=\dfrac{17}{4}, \qquad x_{k+1}=\dfrac{x_k^2+2}{2x_k-1}
\]for all \(k\ge 1\). Then \(x_{2026}\) can be expressed as \(\dfrac{m}{n}\) for relatively prime positive integers \(m,n\). Find the remainder when \(m+n\) is divided by \(1000\).
