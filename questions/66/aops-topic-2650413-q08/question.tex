Triangle $ABC$ has $AB=3$, $AC=4$ and $BC=5$. It is rotated $90^\circ$ clockwise about a point in its plane to form triangle $A'B'C'$. The sides of the two triangles meet as follows: $\overline{A'C'}$ meets $\overline{AC}$ at $D$ and $\overline{BC}$ at $E$, and $\overline{B'C'}$ meets $\overline{BC}$ at $F$ and $\overline{AC}$ at $G$. Given that $[DEC]=[EFC']$ and $[DEFG]=\dfrac1{27}[ABC]$, the distance from $B'$ to line $AB$ is $\dfrac mn$ in lowest terms. Find $m+n$. (Here $[\,\cdot\,]$ denotes area.)
