Let the equilateral side be $s$. The inscribed angle $BPC$ is $120^\circ$, so $s^2=5^2+4^2-2\cdot5\cdot4\cos120^\circ=61$. Ptolemy gives $AP=BP+CP=9$. The sine form of the cevian ratio gives $BD:DC=\sin\angle BAP:\sin\angle PAC=BP:PC=5:4$. Thus $BD\cdot DC=\dfrac{20s^2}{81}=\dfrac{1220}{81}$. Stewart's theorem for the equal sides gives $AD^2=s^2-BD\cdot DC=\dfrac{3721}{81}$, so $AD=\dfrac{61}{9}$. Therefore $\boxed{DP=9-\dfrac{61}{9}=\dfrac{20}{9}}$.
