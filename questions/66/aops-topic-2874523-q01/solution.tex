Mark each edge with an up or down sign according to whether height rises or falls as the circle is traversed. A local maximum is an up-to-down transition and a local minimum a down-to-up transition. In a cyclic sign sequence these transitions occur equally often, so there are three local minima as well. Every other person is in between. The number is $19-3-3=\boxed{13}$.
