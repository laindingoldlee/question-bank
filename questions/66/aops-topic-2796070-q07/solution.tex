Let the number be $n=s^2$. Then $n-\dfrac n4-2s=15$, so $\dfrac34s^2-2s-15=0$, i.e. $3s^2-8s-60=0$, $(3s+10)(s-6)=0$. Since $s>0$, $s=6$ and $n=\boxed{36}$. Check: $36-9-12=15$.
