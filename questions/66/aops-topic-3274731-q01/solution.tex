The roots are $i\sqrt6$ and $i\sqrt5$, so their product is $i^2\sqrt{30}=\boxed{-\sqrt{30}}$. The product rule for principal real square roots requires nonnegative real radicands; it cannot be used here to replace the product by $\sqrt{30}$.
