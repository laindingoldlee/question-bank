For a positive integer $n$, let $f(n)$ be the least positive integer that does not divide $n$, and write $f^k$ for $f$ applied $k$ times. The \emph{length} of $n$ is the least positive integer $k$ with $f^k(n)=2$. Prove that every positive integer has a length and that no positive integer has length $4$ or more. What is the least positive integer of length $3$?
