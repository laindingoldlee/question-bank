The number $9009=91\cdot99$ is a palindrome. Any larger candidate would require both factors at least 92, because $91\cdot99=9009$. It would be a four-digit palindrome, hence divisible by 11 by the alternating-digit test. Since 11 is prime, one factor must be divisible by 11; the only such factor from 92 to 99 is 99. The products $99k$ for $k=92,\ldots,99$ are $9108,9207,9306,9405,9504,9603,9702,9801$, none palindromic. Thus the maximum is $\boxed{9009}$.
