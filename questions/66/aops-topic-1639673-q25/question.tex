Residents were surveyed to decide which flowers to plant in a new public garden. A total of $N$ people took part in the survey. Exactly $\dfrac{9}{14}$ of those surveyed said that the colour of the flower was important, and exactly $\dfrac{7}{12}$ of those surveyed said that the smell of the flower was important. In total, $753$ people said that both the colour and the smell were important. How many possible values are there for $N$?

$\textbf{(A)}\ 22\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ 23\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ 21\qquad\textbf{(D)}\ 24\qquad\textbf{(E)}\ 25$
