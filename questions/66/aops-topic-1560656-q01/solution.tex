Work modulo $100$: $2017\equiv17$. Since $\gcd(17,100)=1$ and $\varphi(100)=40$, Euler's theorem gives $17^{40}\equiv1\pmod{100}$. As $2017=50\cdot40+17$,
\[
2017^{2017}\equiv17^{17}\pmod{100}.
\]
Repeated squaring modulo $100$: $17^2=289\equiv89$, $17^4\equiv89^2=7921\equiv21$, $17^8\equiv21^2=441\equiv41$, $17^{16}\equiv41^2=1681\equiv81$. Then
\[
17^{17}=17^{16}\cdot17\equiv81\cdot17=1377\equiv77\pmod{100}.
\]
The last two digits are $\boxed{77}$.
