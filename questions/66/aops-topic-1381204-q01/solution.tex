Let $x$ grams of the $85\%$ mixture be used, so $600-x$ grams of the $55\%$ mixture are used. Counting flax:
\[
0.85x+0.55(600-x)=0.75\cdot600\iff 0.30x+330=450\iff x=400 .
\]
He must buy $\boxed{400}$ grams of the $85\%$ mixture (and $200$ grams of the $55\%$ mixture).
