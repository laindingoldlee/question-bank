John has the equation $3x+5=16+2x$. He picks one of five operations at random, each with probability $\dfrac15$, and applies it to both sides: add $x$, subtract $x$, multiply by $x$, divide by $x$, or square. What is the probability that the resulting equation implies the original one, that is, that it has exactly the same real solutions as the original equation?
