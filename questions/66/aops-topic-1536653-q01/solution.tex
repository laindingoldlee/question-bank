Let $x=AP$ and $y=AQ$, so $PD=1-x$ and $QB=1-y$. The perimeter condition $x+y+PQ=2$ gives
\[
PQ=(1-x)+(1-y)=PD+QB .
\]
Rotate triangle $CDP$ by $90^\circ$ about $C$ so that $D$ goes to $B$. Since $\angle CDP=\angle CBA=90^\circ$, the image $P'$ of $P$ lies on line $AB$ beyond $B$, with $BP'=DP$ and $CP'=CP$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real q=0.600000,p=0.571429;
pair A=(0,0),B=(1,0),C=(1,1),D=(0,1),P=(0,p),Q=(q,0),Pp=(1+(1-p),0);
fill(A--Q--P--cycle,paleyellow);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(P--Q,linewidth(0.9));draw(C--P,blue);draw(C--Q,blue);
draw(B--Pp,dashed);draw(C--Pp,dashed+red);
dot(P);dot(Q);dot(Pp);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,S);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$P$",P,W);label("$Q$",Q,S);label("$P'$",Pp,S);
draw(arc(C,0.22,degrees(Q-C),degrees(P-C)),blue);label("$45^\circ$",C+0.26*dir((degrees(Q-C)+degrees(P-C))/2),dir((degrees(Q-C)+degrees(P-C))/2),blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Then $QP'=QB+BP'=QB+DP=PQ$. So triangles $CPQ$ and $CP'Q$ have $CP=CP'$, $PQ=P'Q$ and the common side $CQ$; they are congruent (SSS), and $\angle PCQ=\angle P'CQ$. The rotation turns $CP$ into $CP'$ through $90^\circ$, so $\angle PCP'=90^\circ$, and it is split by $CQ$ into the two equal angles. Hence
\[
\angle PCQ=\boxed{45^\circ}.
\]
