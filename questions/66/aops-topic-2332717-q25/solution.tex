Each die independently becomes even on the initial roll or on some reroll; let $T$ be the number of rerolls it needs. Each roll is even with probability $\dfrac12$, so $P(T\le k)=1-\left(\dfrac12\right)^{k+1}$.

She stops after exactly $4$ rounds iff $\max(T_1,T_2)=4$:
\[P=\left(1-\dfrac1{32}\right)^2-\left(1-\dfrac1{16}\right)^2=\dfrac{961-900}{1024}=\dfrac{61}{1024}.\ \textbf{(A)}\]
