One-digit numbers never qualify. Two-digit numbers qualify exactly when both digits agree, giving 9. Three-digit strings qualify only with multiplicities 2,1: there are $10\cdot 9\cdot 3=270$ strings allowing a leading zero. By symmetry one-tenth have leading zero, leaving 243. Four-digit strings qualify with multiplicities 2,1,1 or 2,2. Their respective counts, allowing a leading zero, are $10\binom 92\dfrac{4!}{2!}=4320$ and $\binom{10}2\dfrac{4!}{2!2!}=270$. Again retain nine-tenths, giving 4131. Total $9+243+4131=\boxed{4383}$.
