The smallest angle is $\angle B$, so $E$ is the foot of the bisector from $B$ on $AC$. By the angle bisector theorem, $\dfrac{AE}{EC}=\dfrac{AB}{BC}$. Hence
\[\dfrac{AE\cdot AC}{AB\cdot EC}=\dfrac{AB}{BC}\cdot\dfrac{AC}{AB}=\dfrac{AC}{BC}.\]
By the law of sines, $\dfrac{AC}{BC}=\dfrac{\sin B}{\sin A}$, so the value is
\[\boxed{\dfrac{\sin34^\circ}{\sin79^\circ}}\approx0.570.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0),C=(5,0);
pair A=intersectionpoint(B--B+8*dir(34),C--C+8*dir(180-67));
pair Ep=intersectionpoint(B--B+8*dir(17),A--C);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.8));draw(B--Ep,dashed);
draw(arc(B,0.9,0,17));draw(arc(B,1.0,17,34));
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$E$",Ep,NE);
label("$79^\circ$",A+0.85*unit(unit(B-A)+unit(C-A)),fontsize(8pt));label("$67^\circ$",C+0.85*unit(unit(A-C)+unit(B-C)),fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}
