Let $X=x^{100}$ and $Y=y^{100}$; both are $\ge0$, and the equation reads $\left(16X^2+1\right)\left(Y^2+1\right)=16XY$. By AM-GM, $16X^2+1\ge2\cdot4X=8X$ and $Y^2+1\ge2Y$, so
\[\left(16X^2+1\right)\left(Y^2+1\right)\ge8X\cdot2Y=16XY,\]
since all quantities are nonnegative. Both factors on the left are positive, so equality holds only if $16X^2=1$ and $Y^2=1$, i.e. $x^{100}=\dfrac14$ and $y^{100}=1$. These give $x=\pm2^{-\dfrac1{50}}$ and $y=\pm1$, and every such pair works. There are $\boxed{4}$ solutions.
