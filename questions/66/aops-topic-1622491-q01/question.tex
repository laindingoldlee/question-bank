In the fraction $\dfrac {a}{b}$, the numbers $a$ and $b$ are positive integers. Amanda multiplies the numerator of the fraction by $340$, and then adds a positive integer $k$ to the denominator, so that the new fraction $\dfrac{340a}{b+k}$ equals $\dfrac{2a}{3b}$. The number $k$ depends on the fraction $\dfrac {a}{b}$. Over all possible fractions, which possible value of $k$ is closest to $2017$?
