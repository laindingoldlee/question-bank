The condition is
\[
\dfrac{340a}{b+k}=\dfrac{2a}{3b}\iff 3b\cdot340a=2a(b+k)\iff 510b=b+k\iff k=509b,
\]
where we divided by $2a\ne0$. So the possible values of $k$ are exactly the positive multiples of $509$ (each is obtained with $b=\dfrac{k}{509}$ and any $a$).

The multiples of $509$ near $2017$ are $3\cdot509=1527$ and $4\cdot509=2036$. Since $2036-2017=19$ and $2017-1527=490$, the value closest to $2017$ is
\[
k=\boxed{2036}\qquad(\text{with }b=4).
\]
