The slope of the line is
\[\dfrac{22-10}{15-6}=\dfrac{12}{9}=\dfrac{4}{3} .\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
xaxis(-2,17,Arrow(TeXHead)); yaxis(-1,25,Arrow(TeXHead));
draw((-1,2-4/3)--(16.5,24),blue+linewidth(0.9));
dot((6,10)); dot((15,22)); dot((0,2),red);
label("$(6,10)$",(6,10),SE); label("$(15,22)$",(15,22),SE); label("$(0,b)$",(0,2),W,red);
draw((6,10)--(15,10)--(15,22),dashed);
label("$9$",(10.5,10),N); label("$12$",(15,16),E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Going from $(6,10)$ to $x=0$ means moving $6$ units to the left, so $y$ decreases by $\dfrac{4}{3}\cdot6=8$. Hence $b=10-8=\boxed{2}$. (Check: the line $y=\dfrac{4}{3}x+2$ passes through $(15,22)$, since $\dfrac{4}{3}\cdot15+2=22$.)
