Let $S,E,C,T,I,O$ and $N$ be positive integers whose product equals their sum:
\[S\cdot E\cdot C\cdot T\cdot I\cdot O\cdot N=S+E+C+T+I+O+N.\]
Compute the maximum possible value of $N$.
