Pad each number to three digits. The positive square sums possible are $ 1,4,9,16,25 $. Stars and bars gives respectively $\binom32=3 $, $\binom62=15 $, and $\binom{11}{2}=55 $ for the first three. For sum $ 16 $, subtract cases with a digit at least $ 10 $: $\binom{18}{2}-3\binom82=69 $; two such digits cannot occur. Complementing each digit $ d $ by $ 9-d $ sends sum $ 25 $ to sum $ 2 $, giving $\binom42=6 $. The total is $ 3+15+55+69+6=\boxed{148}$.
