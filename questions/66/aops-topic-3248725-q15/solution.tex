Put $S=x+y+z$ and $Q=x^2+y^2+z^2$. Subtracting equations gives $S(z-x)=11$, $S(x-y)=13$, and $S(z-y)=24$. Summing equations gives $2Q+xy+yz+zx=110$, hence $S^2=220-3Q$. Also $(x-y)^2+(y-z)^2+(z-x)^2=3Q-S^2=\dfrac{13^2+24^2+11^2}{S^2}=\dfrac{866}{S^2}$. With $t=S^2$, this becomes $t(220-2t)=866$, or $t^2-110t+433=0$. Thus $t=55\pm36\sqrt2$. But $x=y+\dfrac{13}{S}$ and $z=y+\dfrac{24}{S}$ imply $3Sy=t-37>0$, ruling out the smaller root. Therefore $Q=\dfrac{220-t}{3}=\boxed{55-12\sqrt2}$.
