For sets $X$ and $Y$, let $X-Y$ be the set of elements of $X$ not in $Y$, and let $X\,\Delta\,Y=(X-Y)\cup(Y-X)$ be their symmetric difference. Suppose that $A$, $B$, $C$ are sets with
\[(A-B)\,\Delta\,(B-C)=A\,\Delta\,B .\]
Prove that $A\cap B=B\cap C$.
