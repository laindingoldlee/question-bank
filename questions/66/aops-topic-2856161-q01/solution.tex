$0=2\cdot0$ with $0$ an integer, so $0$ is $\boxed{\text{even}}$. It is not odd: $0=2k+1$ would give $k=-\dfrac12$, not an integer. (In general no integer is both even and odd, since $2k=2j+1$ would give $2(k-j)=1$.)
