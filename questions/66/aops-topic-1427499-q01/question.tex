A sphere is inscribed in a right circular cone with base radius $6$ and height $10$. The radius of the sphere can be written in simplest form as $\dfrac{a\sqrt{b}-c}{d}$, where $a,b,c,d$ are positive integers and $b$ is square-free. Find the sum of the numerator and the denominator of $\dfrac{ab}{cd}$ when it is written in lowest terms.
