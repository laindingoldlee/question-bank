Put $A=(0,0),B=(12,0),D=(0,4),C=(12,4)$, and $E=(e,0)$. Then $F=(4,4)$ and $G=(8,4)$, while $DB$ has equation $y=4-\dfrac{x}{3}$. Parameterizing $X=E+t(F-E)$ and $Y=E+u(G-E)$ gives $t=\dfrac{12-e}{16-e}$ and $u=\dfrac{12-e}{20-e}$. The determinant area is $[EXY]=8tu$. Let $b=EB=12-e\ge0$. The given area becomes $\dfrac{8b^2}{(b+4)(b+8)}=\dfrac{8}{15}$, so $(7b+8)(b-2)=0$. Hence $\boxed{EB=2}$.
