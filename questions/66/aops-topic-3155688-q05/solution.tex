The route length is $ 2(285+15)=600 $ miles. The second flight takes $\dfrac{600}{315}=\dfrac{40}{21}$ hours. From its departure to the meeting there are four hours, so its earliness is $ 4-\dfrac{40}{21}=\dfrac{44}{21}$ hours, or $\boxed{\dfrac{880}{7}\text{ minutes}}$.
