For $n=1$: $1=2^1-1$. If $1+2+\cdots+2^{n-1}=2^n-1$, then adding $2^n$ gives $2^n-1+2^n=2^{n+1}-1$, which is the statement for $n+1$. By induction it holds for all $n\ge1$. $\blacksquare$
