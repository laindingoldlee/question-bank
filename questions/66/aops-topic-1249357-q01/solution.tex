Modulo $3$, $a_{n+1}\equiv a_n$, so every term is $\equiv a_1\equiv2\pmod3$; no term is divisible by $3$. Now
\[\gcd(a_{n+1},a_n)=\gcd(a_n+3a_{n-1},a_n)=\gcd(3a_{n-1},a_n)=\gcd(a_{n-1},a_n),\]
the last step because $3\nmid a_n$. Repeating, $\gcd(a_{2012},a_{2011})=\gcd(a_2,a_1)=\gcd(8,5)=\boxed{1}$.
