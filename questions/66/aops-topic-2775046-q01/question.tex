Let $c_0,c_1,c_2,\dots$ be real numbers, and let $f_n(x)=c_nx^n$ for $n=0,1,2,\dots$. All the graphs $y=f_n(x)$ pass through a common point. If $c_0=2$ and $c_1=1$, find $c_0+c_1+c_2+\cdots$.
