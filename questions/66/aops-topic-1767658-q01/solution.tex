Let $x$ students read both subjects. Then $x$ is a quarter of the mathematics readers and a third of the history readers, so $4x$ read mathematics and $3x$ read history. By inclusion-exclusion the class has $4x+3x-x=6x$ students. The fraction reading history is $\dfrac{3x}{6x}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$.
