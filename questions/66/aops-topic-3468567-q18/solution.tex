For $k=0$, the divisor is 1 and divisibility holds. For every positive $k$, $5^k\equiv1\pmod4$, so $5^k+1\equiv2\pmod4$: it is divisible by 2 but not by 4. Thus precisely $k=1$ among positive integers works, proving the assertion.
