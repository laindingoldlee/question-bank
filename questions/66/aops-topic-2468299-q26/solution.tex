Let $a\le b\le c$ with $c=2(a+b)+1$. The mean is $\dfrac{3a+3b+1}{3}=a+b+\dfrac13$, which rounds to $a+b$. The median is $b$, so $a+b=b$ and $a=0$. The product is therefore $\boxed{0}$ (for instance $0,1,3$).
