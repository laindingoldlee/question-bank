The expression is the limit of $a_1=2$, $a_{k+1}=\dfrac{2}{1+a_k}$ (that is, $2,\dfrac23,\dfrac65,\dfrac{10}{11},\dots$). If the limit is $L$, then $L=\dfrac{2}{1+L}$, so $L^2+L-2=(L+2)(L-1)=0$; all terms are positive, so $L=\boxed{1}$.

The limit exists: all $a_k$ lie in $\left[\dfrac23,2\right]$ for $k\ge2$, and
\[\left|a_{k+1}-1\right|=\dfrac{\left|1-a_k\right|}{1+a_k}\le\dfrac35\left|a_k-1\right|,\]
so $a_k\to1$.
