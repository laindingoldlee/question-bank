Completing the square (Sophie Germain's identity),
\[b^4+4a^4=\left(b^2+2a^2\right)^2-(2ab)^2=\left(b^2+2ab+2a^2\right)\left(b^2-2ab+2a^2\right).\]
The smaller factor is $b^2-2ab+2a^2=(b-a)^2+a^2\ge a^2\ge4$, and the larger is bigger still, so $b^4+4a^4$ is a product of two integers greater than $1$. $\blacksquare$
