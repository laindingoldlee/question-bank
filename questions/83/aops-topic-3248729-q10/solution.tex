Two heads have probability $\dfrac{1}{16}$, two tails $\dfrac{9}{16}$, and a repeat $\dfrac{6}{16}$. If $p$ is Lily’s loss probability, $p=\dfrac{1}{16}+\dfrac{6}{16}p$, giving $\boxed{p=\dfrac{1}{10}}$. Equivalently, among terminating outcomes, one tenth are two heads.
