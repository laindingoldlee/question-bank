Let $b=x^2-5x+5$ and $e=x^2-8x+15$. For a real-defined power to equal 1, either $e=0$ with $b\ne0$, or $b=1$, or $b=-1$ with an even integer exponent. The case $e=0$ gives $x=3,5$, both valid. The case $b=1$ gives $x=1,4$, both valid. Finally $b=-1$ gives $x=2,3$; their exponents are 3 and 0, so only 3 works and is already counted. A zero base cannot work. The solution set is $\{1,3,4,5\}$, with sum $\boxed{13}$.
