Alice needs $\dfrac{20}{6}$ hours $=200$ minutes and Betty needs $\dfrac{20}{4}$ hours $=300$ minutes. Measure the start times $a$ (Alice) and $b$ (Betty) in minutes after 9:00; the point $(a,b)$ is uniform in the square $[0,120]\times[0,120]$, of area $14400$.

Betty finishes first when $b+300<a+200$, that is $a-b>100$. In the square this is the triangle with vertices $(100,0)$, $(120,0)$, $(120,20)$, of area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot20\cdot20=200$. Hence the probability is
\[
\dfrac{200}{14400}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{72}}.
\]
