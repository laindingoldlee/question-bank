Alice and Betty run a $20$-mile race. Alice runs at a constant $6$ miles per hour and Betty at a constant $4$ miles per hour. Each of them starts at a time chosen uniformly at random between 9:00 and 11:00, independently of the other. What is the probability that Betty finishes the race before Alice? Express your answer as a common fraction.
