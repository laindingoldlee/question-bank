The apples and the oranges are distributed independently.

By stars and bars, the number of ways to give $20$ identical apples to $5$ children is the number of nonnegative integer solutions of $a_1+\dots+a_5=20$, namely $\dbinom{20+4}{4}=\dbinom{24}{4}=10626$. Likewise the oranges can be given out in $\dbinom{15+4}{4}=\dbinom{19}{4}=3876$ ways.

By the multiplication principle the total is
\[
\dbinom{24}{4}\dbinom{19}{4}=10626\cdot3876=\boxed{41186376}.
\]
