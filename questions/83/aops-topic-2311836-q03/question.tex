You have $8$ presents to give to $7$ children, and each child must receive at least one present. All $8$ presents are given away.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] In how many ways can this be done if the presents are all different?
\item[(b)] In how many ways can this be done if the presents are identical?
\end{enumerate}
