Since $8=7+1$, exactly one child receives $2$ presents and every other child receives $1$.

\textbf{(a)} Choose the child who gets two presents ($7$ ways) and the two presents for that child ($\dbinom82=28$ ways), then hand out the remaining $6$ presents to the other $6$ children, one each ($6!=720$ ways). The number of ways is
\[7\cdot28\cdot720=\boxed{141120}.\]

\textbf{(b)} Only the choice of the child who gets two presents matters: $\boxed{7}$ ways.
