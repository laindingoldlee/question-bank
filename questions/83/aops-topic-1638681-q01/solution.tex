For $x>0$ all four square roots are defined and
\[
\sqrt{8x}\cdot\sqrt{10x}\cdot\sqrt{3x}\cdot\sqrt{15x}=\sqrt{8\cdot10\cdot3\cdot15\,x^4}=\sqrt{3600x^4}=60x^2 .
\]
So $60x^2=15$, $x^2=\dfrac{1}{4}$, and since $x>0$, $\boxed{x=\dfrac{1}{2}}$ is the only value.
