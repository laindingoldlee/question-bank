Printer $B$ alone does $\dfrac{1}{24}-\dfrac{1}{60}=\dfrac{5-2}{120}=\dfrac{1}{40}$ of the job per minute, so it would take $40$ minutes. If the job has $N$ pages, the rates are $\dfrac {N}{60}$ and $\dfrac {N}{40}$ pages per minute, and
\[\dfrac {N}{40}-\dfrac {N}{60}=5\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\dfrac{N}{120}=5\quad\Longrightarrow\quad N=\boxed{600}.\]
