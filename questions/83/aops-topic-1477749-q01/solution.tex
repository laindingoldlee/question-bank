Let the sides be $x$, $3x$ and $15$, with $x$ a positive integer. The triangle inequality requires
\[
3x<x+15\ \ (\text{so }x<7.5),\qquad 15<x+3x\ \ (\text{so }x>3.75),\qquad x<3x+15\ (\text{always}).
\]
So $4\le x\le7$. The perimeter $4x+15$ is largest for $x=7$: the sides $7$, $21$, $15$ satisfy $7+15=22>21$, and the maximum perimeter is $4\cdot7+15=\boxed{43}$ centimetres.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair P=(0,0),Q=(21,0); real xx=617/42; pair R=(xx,sqrt(225-xx^2));
draw(P--Q--R--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
dot(P);dot(Q);dot(R);
label("$21=3\cdot7$",(P+Q)/2,S); label("$15$",(P+R)/2,NW); label("$7$",(Q+R)/2,NE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
