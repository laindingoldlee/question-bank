For each value of $x$, the largest $y^z$ with $y+z=7-x$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $x=1$, $y+z=6$: $y^z\in\{1^5,2^4,3^3,4^2,5^1\}=\{1,16,27,16,5\}$, maximum $27$, giving $1+27=28$;
\item $x=2$, $y+z=5$: $y^z\in\{1,8,9,4\}$, giving at most $2+9=11$;
\item $x=3$, $y+z=4$: $y^z\in\{1,4,3\}$, giving at most $7$;
\item $x=4$: $y^z\le2$, giving at most $6$; $x=5$: $y=z=1$, giving $6$.
\end{itemize}
The greatest value is $\boxed{28}$, at $x=1$, $y=z=3$.
