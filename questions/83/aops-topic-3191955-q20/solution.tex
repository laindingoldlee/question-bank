The period is the multiplicative order of $10$ modulo $31$. We have $10^3\equiv8$ and $10^5\equiv25$, while $10^{15}\equiv25^3=15625\equiv1\pmod{31}$. The proper positive divisors of $15$ are $1,3,5$, and none gives residue $1$. Thus the order, and the decimal period, is $\boxed{15}$.
