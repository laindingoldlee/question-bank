Set $u=6x,v=5y$. The equations become $u=v^2-1$, $v=u^2-1$. Subtraction gives $(u-v)(u+v+1)=0$. Their four intersections are the two points on $u=v$ with $u^2-u-1=0$, and $(u,v)=(0,-1),(-1,0)$. These opposite pairs form the diagonals, meeting at $u=v=-\dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore $(x,y)=(-\dfrac{1}{12},-\dfrac{1}{10})$ and $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{(xy)}=120}$.

\end{asy}
