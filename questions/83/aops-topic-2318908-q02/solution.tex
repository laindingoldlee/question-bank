A way of climbing is a sequence of positive moves adding up to $10$. Think of the $10$ steps as $10$ units in a row, with $9$ gaps between consecutive units. A way of climbing corresponds exactly to choosing which of the $9$ gaps are ends of moves: for example, moves $3,1,6$ correspond to cutting after units $3$ and $4$. Each gap is cut or not, independently, so there are
\[2^9=\boxed{512}\]
ways.
