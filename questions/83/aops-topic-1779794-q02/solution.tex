Let $p=\langle x\rangle$. The equation is $p^2+2p+1-2p-8-9=0$, i.e.\ $p^2=16$, so $p=4$ (a count is nonnegative). The primes up to $x$ are $2,3,5,7$ exactly when $7\le x<11$, i.e.\ $x\in\{7,8,9,10\}$. The sum is $7+8+9+10=\boxed{34}$.
