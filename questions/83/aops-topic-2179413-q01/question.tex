Let $\varphi(n)$ denote the number of integers from $1$ to $n$ that are relatively prime to $n$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Explain why $\varphi\left(p^\alpha\right)=p^\alpha-p^{\alpha-1}=p^\alpha\left(1-\dfrac{1}{p}\right)$ for every prime $p$ and every positive integer $\alpha$.
\item[(b)] A student argues: ``$\varphi$ is multiplicative, so $\varphi\left(p^\alpha\right)=\varphi(p)^\alpha=(p-1)^\alpha$.'' He then computes $\varphi(9000)=\varphi\left(2^3\cdot3^2\cdot5^3\right)=1^3\cdot2^2\cdot4^3=256$. Find the error, and compute $\varphi(9000)$ correctly.
\end{enumerate}
