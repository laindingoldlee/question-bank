Since $x^z-y^z=x+y>0$, we have $x>y\ge1$, so $x\ge2$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $z=1$: $x-y=x+y$ gives $y=0$, which is not allowed.
\item $z=2$: $(x-y)(x+y)=x+y$, and dividing by $x+y>0$ gives $x-y=1$.
\item $z\ge3$: then
\[x^z-y^z=(x-y)\left(x^{z-1}+x^{z-2}y+\cdots+y^{z-1}\right)\ge x^{z-1}+x^{z-2}y+\cdots+y^{z-1}.\]
Here $x^{z-1}\ge x^2>x$ (as $x\ge2$), the last term $y^{z-1}\ge y$, and the other terms are positive. So $x^z-y^z>x+y$, a contradiction.
\end{itemize}
Hence $z=2$ and $x=y+1$. $\blacksquare$
