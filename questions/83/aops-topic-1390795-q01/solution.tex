Put $a=\sqrt x$ and $b=\sqrt y$ (both positive). The equation is $\dfrac{3}{b}-\dfrac{1}{a}=\dfrac{2}{a+b}$. One equation is enough because only the ratio is asked for and every term has the same degree. Multiply by $ab(a+b)$:
\[
3a(a+b)-b(a+b)=2ab\iff 3a^2+3ab-ab-b^2-2ab=0\iff 3a^2=b^2 .
\]
So $y=b^2=3a^2=3x$ and $\dfrac{x}{y}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{3}}$.
