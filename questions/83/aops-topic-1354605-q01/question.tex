Twelve points are equally spaced on a circle. Red and Blue each choose, simultaneously and uniformly at random, two of the points (independently of each other) and draw the chord between them. Red wins if the two chords have a point in common (including endpoints, and including the case of the same chord); otherwise Blue wins.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What is the probability that Red wins?
\item[(b)] Suppose the rule is changed so that Red wins only if the chords cross at a point that is an endpoint of neither chord. What is Red's probability now?
\end{enumerate}
