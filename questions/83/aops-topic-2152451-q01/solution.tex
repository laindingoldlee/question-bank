Let $s=\sqrt{2\sqrt5-2}$. Since $2\sqrt5-2<4$ (because $\sqrt5<3$), we have $0<s<2$, so both outer square roots are defined, and $M>0$. Squaring,
\[M^2=(2+s)+(2-s)-2\sqrt{(2+s)(2-s)}=4-2\sqrt{4-s^2}=4-2\sqrt{6-2\sqrt5}.\]
Now $6-2\sqrt5=5-2\sqrt5+1=\left(\sqrt5-1\right)^2$, and $\sqrt5-1>0$, so $\sqrt{6-2\sqrt5}=\sqrt5-1$. Hence
\[M^2=4-2\left(\sqrt5-1\right)=6-2\sqrt5=\left(\sqrt5-1\right)^2,\]
and since $M>0$,
\[M=\boxed{\sqrt5-1}\approx1.236 .\]
