At time $t\ge10$ seconds the first player has covered $5+10(t-2)$ metres and the chaser $20(t-10)$. Setting them equal: $20t-200=10t-15$, so $t=\boxed{18.5}$ seconds after the start ($8.5$ seconds after the chaser sets off).
