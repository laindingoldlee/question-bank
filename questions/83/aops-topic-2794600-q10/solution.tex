The arithmetic condition gives $ 6k^2=8-8k+k^4+2k^3 $, or $(k^2-4)(k^2+2k-2)=0 $. Hence the candidates are $ 2,-2,-1+\sqrt3,-1-\sqrt3 $. The geometric ratio is $\dfrac{1}{k}$, and convergence requires $|k|>1 $. This excludes only $-1+\sqrt3 $. The remaining values sum to $\boxed{-1-\sqrt3}$.
