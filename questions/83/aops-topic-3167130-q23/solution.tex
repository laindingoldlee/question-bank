Adding equations gives $a^2+b^2+c^2=184$. Substitution yields $a^2=\dfrac{268}{3}$, $b^2=\dfrac{172}{3}$, and $c^2=\dfrac{112}{3}$. The squared-side area identity $16K^2=2(a^2b^2+b^2c^2+c^2a^2)-a^4-b^4-c^4$ now gives $K^2=\dfrac{1600}{3}$. Hence $\boxed{K=\dfrac{40\sqrt3}{3}}$. Alternatively, the given equations say the medians have lengths $5,7,8$; their triangle has area $10\sqrt3$ by Heron, and the original triangle has four-thirds that area.

\end{asy}
