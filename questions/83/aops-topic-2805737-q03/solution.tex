The whole volume is $\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot25\cdot10=\dfrac{250}{3}$. The removed top pyramid has height 4, hence linear scale $\dfrac{2}{5}$ and volume fraction $\dfrac{8}{125}$. The desired volume is $\dfrac{250}{3}(1-\dfrac{8}{125})=\boxed{78}$.
