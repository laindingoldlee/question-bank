Let the morning price be $p$ and the afternoon price $q$, with $p>q>0$, and let $d=p-q$. If a farmer with $n$ chickens sold $m$ of them in the morning, he received $mp+(n-m)q=nq+md$. So, with $a,b,c$ the morning sales of $A,B,C$,
\[
10q+ad=16q+bd=26q+cd=35 .
\]
Subtracting, $(a-b)d=6q$ and $(b-c)d=10q$. Both right sides are positive, so $a>b>c$, and $\dfrac{a-b}{b-c}=\dfrac{6}{10}=\dfrac{3}{5}$. Hence $a-b=3k$ and $b-c=5k$ for a positive integer $k$, and $a-c=8k$. But $a\le9$ and $c\ge1$, so $a-c\le8$; thus $k=1$, $a=9$, $c=1$, $b=6$.

Then $3d=6q$, so $d=2q$, and $10q+9\cdot2q=28q=35$ gives $q=\dfrac{5}{4}$ and $p=q+d=\dfrac{15}{4}$. The morning price was $\boxed{\$3.75}$ and the afternoon price $\boxed{\$1.25}$.

Check: $A$: $9(3.75)+1(1.25)=35$; $B$: $6(3.75)+10(1.25)=35$; $C$: $1(3.75)+25(1.25)=35$.
