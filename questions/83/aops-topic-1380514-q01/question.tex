Three farmers go to the market to sell chickens, and they agree to charge the same price per chicken. Farmer $A$ has $10$ chickens, farmer $B$ has $16$ and farmer $C$ has $26$. In the morning each farmer sells at least one chicken but not all of them. At lunch they all lower the price to a new common price, and in the afternoon they sell all their remaining chickens. Each farmer ends up with $35$ dollars. What were the morning and afternoon prices of a chicken?
