One portion costs $19.25$ dollars, or $1925$ cents. All pennies allow zero quarters. Since each quarter is $25$ cents, at most $\dfrac{1925}{25}=77$ quarters are possible, and exactly $77$ pays the bill. The difference is $\boxed{77}$.
