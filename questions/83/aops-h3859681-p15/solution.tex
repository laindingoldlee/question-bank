Power of $A$ with respect to $\omega_2$ gives $AX\cdot AB=AY\cdot AC=800$, so $AB=32$. Put $A=(0,0),B=(32,0),M=(16,0),N=(8,0)$, and let the tangent circle center be $O=(16,r)$. The midpoint of $BN$ is $K=(20,0)$. Since $OK$ is the perpendicular bisector of chord $DE$, line $AD$ has slope $\dfrac{4}{r}$. The other tangent from $B$ has contact point $D=(\dfrac{48r^2+4096}{r^2+256},\dfrac{512r}{r^2+256})$, obtained by reflecting $M$ across line $BO$. Thus its slope from $A$ is $\dfrac{32r}{3r^2+256}=\dfrac{4}{r}$, giving $r^2=\dfrac{256}{5}$. Consequently $D=(\dfrac{64}{3},\dfrac{16\sqrt5}{3})$ and $AD^2=\dfrac{1792}{3}$. Finally $AE\cdot AD=AM^2=256$, so $AE^2=\dfrac{768}{7}$ and $\boxed{775}$. Reflection across $AB$ yields the same value.
