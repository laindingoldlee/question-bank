In $\triangle ABC$, $AB=9$ and $AC=6$. $D$ is the midpoint of $BC$, and $F$ is the point on $AB$ such that $CF$ bisects $\angle C$. The segments $AD$ and $CF$ meet at $P$, and line $BP$ meets $AC$ at $E$. As $BC$ varies over all values for which the triangle exists, $AE$ takes values in an interval. The sum of the greatest lower bound and the least upper bound of the possible values of $AE$ can be written as $\dfrac {m}{n}$ in lowest terms. Find $m+n$.
