Four people have cubes with distinct integer side lengths greater than $1$. Each paints all faces and divides the cube into unit cubes. Cerena reports the number with zero painted faces, Faith the number with exactly one, Edna the number with exactly two, and Veronica the number with exactly three. Whose original side lengths can be uniquely recovered from their reports?
