Emma buys five apples and five peaches; one $5$-dollar bill is not enough, but two are, with change. Jonah buys $2$ apples and $12$ peaches; one $10$-dollar bill is not enough, but two are, with change. Every fruit costs a whole number of cents. Helen buys $25$ peaches. What is the least amount, in cents, that Helen can expect to pay?
