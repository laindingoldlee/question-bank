Let an apple cost $a$ cents and a peach $p$ cents. Then $500<5a+5p<1000$, i.e. $100<a+p<200$, and $1000<2a+12p<2000$. To minimise $25p$ we minimise $p$. Since $a\le199-p$,
\[
1000<2a+12p\le2(199-p)+12p=398+10p,
\]
so $p\ge61$. With $p=61$ and $a=138$: $a+p=199$, $2a+12p=276+732=1008$, which satisfies all conditions. The least amount is $25\cdot61=\boxed{1525}$ cents.
