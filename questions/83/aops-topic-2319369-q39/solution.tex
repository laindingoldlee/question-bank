With $x$ liters of lemonade and $4-x$ of the strawberry drink: $0.06x+0.15(4-x)=0.10\cdot4$, so $0.6-0.09x=0.4$ and $x=\dfrac{20}9$. Mix $\boxed{\dfrac{20}{9}\approx2.22\text{ liters of lemonade and }\dfrac{16}{9}\approx1.78\text{ liters of strawberry drink}}$.
