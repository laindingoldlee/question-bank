For a median to a side, the shared median and the equal half-sides cancel when subtracting the two perimeters. The difference is therefore the absolute difference of the other two original sides. If the sides are $a\le b\le c$, the conditions say $b-a=10$ and $c-b=10$. The median to the middle side produces difference $c-a=\boxed{20}$.
