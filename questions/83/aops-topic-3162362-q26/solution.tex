Separate even and odd indices. Their common recurrence has characteristic roots 1 and $-\dfrac{1}{2}$. Matching initial terms yields
\[x_{2k}=\dfrac{2}{51}\left(1-(-\dfrac{1}{2})^k\right),\qquad x_{2k+1}=\dfrac{1}{12}-\dfrac{1}{30}(-\dfrac{1}{2})^k.\]
Let $ K=\dfrac{2017!}{2}$, which is even and at least 8. Then
\[x_{2K}-x_{2K-1}=-\dfrac{3}{68}-\dfrac{9}{85}2^{-K}.\]
The positive magnitude lies strictly between $\dfrac{1}{23}$ and $\dfrac{1}{22}$: the lower bound follows from $\dfrac{3}{68}>\dfrac{1}{23}$, and the upper from $ 2^{-K}\le\dfrac{1}{256}$. Its reciprocal with negative sign therefore lies strictly between $-23 $ and $-22 $. The floor is $\boxed{-23}$.
