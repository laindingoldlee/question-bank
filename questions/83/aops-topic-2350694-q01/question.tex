Point $P$ lies inside an equilateral triangle $ABC$ with side length $1$. The lines $AP$, $BP$, $CP$ meet the sides $BC$, $CA$, $AB$ at $D$, $E$, $F$, respectively. Prove that
\[PD+PE+PF<1.\]
