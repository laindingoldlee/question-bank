The first square is in position $ABCD$, where a position lists the vertices starting with the lower left vertex and continuing clockwise. After the square is rotated $90^\circ$ clockwise about its centre, the second square is in position $DABC$. Next, square $DABC$ is reflected over its vertical line of symmetry, so the third square is in position $CBAD$. If the pattern of alternately rotating $90^\circ$ clockwise and reflecting over the vertical line of symmetry continues, in what position will the $2007$th square be? Write the answer starting with the lower left vertex and continuing clockwise.
