Adding the three equations gives
\[x^2y^2-6xy+x^2+y^2+z^2-2z+5=0,\]
which can be rewritten as
\[(xy-2)^2+(x-y)^2+(z-1)^2=0.\]
(Indeed $(xy-2)^2+(x-y)^2+(z-1)^2=x^2y^2-4xy+4+x^2-2xy+y^2+z^2-2z+1$.) Hence $xy=2$, $x=y$ and $z=1$, so $x=y=\pm\sqrt2$. Both triples satisfy all three equations: $4-8+1=-3$, $2+2-1=3$ and $1-4-2=-5$. The solutions are
\[\boxed{\left(\sqrt2,\sqrt2,1\right)\text{ and }\left(-\sqrt2,-\sqrt2,1\right)}.\]
