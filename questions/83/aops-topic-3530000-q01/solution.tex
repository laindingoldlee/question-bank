Symmetry gives $\Pr(S_1>S_2)=\dfrac{1}{2}(1-\Pr(S_1=S_2))$. Replace every player-2 rank $r$ by $14-r$, which preserves its uniform distribution. A tie is then equivalent to twenty independent ranks summing to $140$. Subtracting one from each rank gives twenty integers in $\{0,\ldots,12\}$ summing to $120$. By inclusion--exclusion, their number is $N=\sum_{j=0}^{9}(-1)^j\binom{20}{j}\binom{139-13j}{19}$. There are $13^{20}$ equally likely rank sequences. Hence the exact probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}\left(1-\dfrac{N}{13^{20}}\right)}=\boxed{\dfrac{713665779353388923907}{1461920290375446110677}}$.
