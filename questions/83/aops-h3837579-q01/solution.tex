For any original root $r$, $r^3=r^2+1$ and $r^4=r^2+r+1$. Also Vieta gives $\alpha+\beta+\gamma=1$ and $\alpha\beta+\beta\gamma+\gamma\alpha=0$, hence $\alpha^2+\beta^2+\gamma^2=1$. Therefore the three new roots simplify to $\gamma+3,\alpha+3,\beta+3$.

Their monic polynomial is obtained by shifting the original one:
\[
(x-3)^3-(x-3)^2-1=x^3-10x^2+33x-37.
\]
Thus $a=10,b=33,c=-37$, giving $\boxed{a+b+c=6}$.
