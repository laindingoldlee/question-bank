Replacing $x$ by $\dfrac{1}{x}$ gives $3f\!\left(\dfrac{1}{x}\right)-\dfrac{5}{x} f(x)=\dfrac{1}{x}-7$, so
\[f\!\left(\dfrac{1}{x}\right)=\dfrac{1}{3}\left(\dfrac{1}{x}-7+\dfrac{5}{x}f(x)\right).\]
Substituting into the original equation:
\[3f(x)-\dfrac{5x}{3}\left(\dfrac{1}{x}-7+\dfrac{5}{x} f(x)\right)=x-7\ \Longrightarrow\ 9f(x)-5+35x-25f(x)=3x-21,\]
so $-16f(x)=-32x-16$ and $f(x)=2x+1$. (Check: $3(2x+1)-5x\left(\dfrac{2}{x}+1\right)=6x+3-10-5x=x-7$.) Hence $f(2010)=\boxed{4021}$.
