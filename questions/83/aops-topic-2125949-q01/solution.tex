Since $120=2^3\cdot3\cdot5$, every positive divisor of $120$ is $2^a3^b5^c$ with $0\le a\le3$, $0\le b\le1$, $0\le c\le1$. Expanding the product
\[(1+2+4+8)(1+3)(1+5)\]
gives each such divisor exactly once, so the sum of all positive divisors of $120$ is $15\cdot4\cdot6=360$. Leaving out $120$ itself, the sum of the proper divisors is
\[360-120=\boxed{240}.\]
(The same method works for any $n=p_1^{e_1}\cdots p_k^{e_k}$: the sum of all divisors is $\prod\left(1+p_i+\cdots+p_i^{e_i}\right)$, and the proper divisors add up to that minus $n$.)
