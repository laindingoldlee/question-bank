First count the colour patterns: words with three letters each of $R$, $G$, $B$ and no two equal adjacent letters.

Write down the six letters $R,R,R,G,G,G$ in some order and then insert the three $B$'s into the $7$ gaps (including the two ends), at most one $B$ per gap. If the $RG$-word has $j$ adjacent equal pairs, each of those $j$ gaps must receive a $B$, and the other $3-j$ $B$'s go into distinct gaps among the remaining $7-j$: there are $\dbinom{7-j}{3-j}$ ways (and none if $j>3$).

An $RG$-word with $k$ runs has $j=6-k$. Counting the $\dbinom63=20$ words by their number of runs: $k=6$: $2$ words; $k=5$: $4$; $k=4$: $8$; $k=3$: $4$; $k=2$: $2$. Hence the number of colour patterns is
\[
2\dbinom73+4\dbinom62+8\dbinom51+4\dbinom40=70+60+40+4=174 .
\]
For each pattern, the three red people can be put in the red seats in $3!$ ways, and likewise for green and blue. The total is
\[
174\cdot(3!)^3=174\cdot216=\boxed{37584}.
\]
