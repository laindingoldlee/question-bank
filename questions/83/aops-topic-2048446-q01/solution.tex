For every $t>0$,
\[t^3-2t^2+t=t(t-1)^2\ge0,\qquad\text{so}\qquad t^3-t^2\ge t^2-t .\]
Applying this to $t=a$ and $t=b$ and adding,
\[\left(a^3+b^3\right)-\left(a^2+b^2\right)\ge\left(a^2+b^2\right)-(a+b)>0,\]
where the last step is the hypothesis. Hence $a^3+b^3>a^2+b^2$. $\blacksquare$
