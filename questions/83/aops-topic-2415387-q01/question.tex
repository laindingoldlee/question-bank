Mrs Ong has three children whose ages are positive whole numbers. The product of their ages is $96$, and the sum of their ages equals the number of trees in the park. Mrs Tan counts the trees, thinks for a while, and says that she still cannot work out the ages. Mrs Ong then says that she must go and pick up her children from the school across the park. From this Mrs Tan learns that all three children are at school, and she knows that the school takes no child younger than $2$. Now Mrs Tan knows all the ages. How many trees are in the park, and how old is the youngest child?
