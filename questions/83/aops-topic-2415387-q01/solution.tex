List the triples of positive integers $a\le b\le c$ with $abc=96=2^5\cdot3$ and their sums:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{c|c||c|c||c|c}
ages & sum & ages & sum & ages & sum\\
\hline
$1,1,96$ & $98$ & $1,6,16$ & $23$ & $2,4,12$ & $18$\\
$1,2,48$ & $51$ & $1,8,12$ & $21$ & $2,6,8$ & $16$\\
$1,3,32$ & $36$ & $2,2,24$ & $28$ & $3,4,8$ & $15$\\
$1,4,24$ & $29$ & $2,3,16$ & $21$ & $4,4,6$ & $14$
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Mrs Tan knows the sum but cannot find the ages, so the sum must belong to more than one triple. The only repeated sum is $21$, from $(1,8,12)$ and $(2,3,16)$. So there are $\boxed{21}$ trees.

Mrs Ong's remark tells Mrs Tan that all three children attend the school, which takes no child younger than $2$. This rules out $(1,8,12)$, so the ages are $2$, $3$ and $16$, and the youngest child is $\boxed{2}$ years old.
