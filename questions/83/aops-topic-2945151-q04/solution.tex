Use $A=(0,0),C=(30,0),B=(9,0),D=(15,h)$ with $h\ne0$. Put $u=AD$ and $v=BD$. Then $u^2-v^2=15^2-6^2=189$, so $(u-v)(u+v)=189$. Its positive ordered factor pairs are $(1,189),(3,63),(7,27),(9,21)$, yielding $(u,v)=(95,94),(33,30),(17,10),(15,6)$. The last has $h=0$ and is excluded; the others have $u>15$ and therefore produce genuine triangles. Their perimeters are 220,96,64, whose sum is $\boxed{380}$.
