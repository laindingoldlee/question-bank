Female biology students constitute one quarter of the school. Dividing by the female proportion gives $\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{4}}{\dfrac{3}{5}}=\dfrac{5}{12}$, or $41\dfrac{2}{3}$ percent. Rounded to the nearest percent, $\boxed{42\%}$.
