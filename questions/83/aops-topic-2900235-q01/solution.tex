The condition is exactly $2520=2^3\cdot3^2\cdot5\cdot7\mid q$, and $q=2520$ is possible. The numbers among $11,\dots,20$ dividing $2520$ are $12,14,15,18,20$. The others ($11,13,16,17,19$) do not divide $q=2520$. So the answer is $\boxed{5}$.
