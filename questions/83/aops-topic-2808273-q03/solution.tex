Without wrapping, the goat reaches three quarters of a disk of radius 6. The rope can also wrap around either adjacent corner, leaving length 2 and adding one quarter-disk there. These added regions do not overlap, since their centres are $4\sqrt2>4$ apart. The opposite corner cannot be reached because the boundary route has length 8. Hence the area is $\dfrac{3}{4}\pi6^2+2\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}\pi2^2=\boxed{29\pi}$.
