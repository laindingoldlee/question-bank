Rewrite the equation as \(2x^2-5xy+2y^2+x+y=0\), that is,
\[
(2x-y)(x-2y)+x+y=0.
\]
Put \(u=2x-y\) and \(v=x-2y\). Then \(u-v=x+y\), so \(uv+u-v=0\), or
\[
(u-1)(v+1)=-1.
\]
With integers, either \(u-1=1,\ v+1=-1\) or \(u-1=-1,\ v+1=1\).
\begin{itemize}
\item \(u=2,\ v=-2\): \(2x-y=2\) and \(x-2y=-2\) give \(x=2,\ y=2\).
\item \(u=0,\ v=0\): \(2x=y\) and \(x=2y\) give \(x=y=0\).
\end{itemize}
Both pairs satisfy the equation (\(16+4=20\) and \(0=0\)). The solutions are
\[
\boxed{(x,y)=(0,0)\text{ and }(2,2)}.
\]
