Normalize elapsed time to 1 and let riding fractions be $u_i$. Then $u_1=2(1-u_2)$, $u_2=3(1-u_3)$, $u_3=4(1-u_1)$. Solving gives $(u_1,u_2,u_3)=(\dfrac{4}{5},\dfrac{3}{5},\dfrac{4}{5})$. Equal distances give first and third speeds both 24. The second rides for three quarters as long, so its speed is $24\cdot\dfrac{4}{3}=\boxed{32}$ kilometres per hour.
