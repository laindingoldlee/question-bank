Three motorcyclists cover equal total distances, starting and finishing simultaneously. The first rides for twice as long as the second rests; the second rides for three times as long as the third rests; the third rides for four times as long as the first rests. Riding speeds are constant. The first and third speeds sum to 48 kilometres per hour. Find the second speed.
