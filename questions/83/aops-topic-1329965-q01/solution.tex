When the son starts, the father is $48$ km ahead. The son gains $73-48=25$ km each hour, so he catches up after $\dfrac{48}{25}=\boxed{1.92\text{ hours}}$ (1 hour $55.2$ minutes). Check: the son drives $73\cdot1.92=140.16$ km and the father $48\cdot2.92=140.16$ km.
