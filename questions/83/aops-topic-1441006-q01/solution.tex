Complete the square:
\[
0=a^2-ab+b^2=\left(a-\dfrac {b}{2}\right)^2+\dfrac{3}{4}b^2 .
\]
Both terms are non-negative, so both are $0$: $b=0$, and then $a=\dfrac {b}{2}=0$. $\blacksquare$

(Alternatively, $a^2+b^2\ge2|ab|\ge|ab|\ge ab$, and equality throughout forces $ab=0$ and then $a=b=0$.)
