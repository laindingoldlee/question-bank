The bills are identical and the children are different, so a distribution is a solution of $x_1+\cdots+x_5=10$ in nonnegative integers. By stars and bars ($10$ stars, $4$ bars) there are $\dbinom{14}{4}=\boxed{1001}$ ways. (If every child must get at least one dollar, give each one dollar first and distribute the other $5$: $\dbinom94=126$ ways.)
