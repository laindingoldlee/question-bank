There are $36$ equally likely ordered outcomes. The sum is at most $4$ for $(1,1)$, $(1,2)$, $(2,1)$, $(1,3)$, $(3,1)$, $(2,2)$, that is $6$ outcomes. So
\[
P(\text{sum}>4)=1-\dfrac{6}{36}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{6}}.
\]
