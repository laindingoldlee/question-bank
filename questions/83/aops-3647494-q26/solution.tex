If the positive masses in kilograms are $m_1,\ldots,m_n$, the target value is $r=\log_{10}(\prod_i m_i)=\sum_i\log_{10}m_i$. The unrounded score for estimate $E$ is $10\cdot3^{-|r-E|}$, maximised at $E=r$, where it equals ten. A numerical target cannot be calculated without the masses. Under rounding half up, a score of at least one requires $10\cdot3^{-|r-E|}\ge\dfrac{1}{2}$, or $|r-E|\le\log_3 20$. A different tie rule affects only the two boundary estimates.
