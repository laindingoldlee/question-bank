Let the square side be $s$, the common offset be $t$, and put $A=(0,0)$, $B=(s,0)$, $C=(s,s)$, $D=(0,s)$, $X=(u,v)$. Splitting each region into two triangles gives $[XEAH]=\dfrac{tv+(s-t)u}{2}$ and $[XGCF]=\dfrac{t(s-v)+(s-t)(s-u)}{2}$. Their sum is $\dfrac{s^2}{2}$. Similarly the other two opposite regions sum to $\dfrac{s^2}{2}$. Consequently $81+125=49+[XHDG]$, giving $\boxed{157}$. This works for every permitted interior $X$ and offset.
