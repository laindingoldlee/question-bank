Let $D$ be the left side minus the right side. Since $x-1<\lfloor x\rfloor\le x$, we have $D>\dfrac{2N}{105}-2$. Therefore $N\ge105$ cannot work. For $1\le N\le104$, exact evaluation of the four floors gives zeros at $1,2,5,7,8,10,11,14,15,16,17,20,21,22,23,25,26,28,29,30,31,32,35,50,56,65$. This finite check can be reproduced by incrementing each floor at multiples of its denominator. The greatest is $\boxed{65}$.
