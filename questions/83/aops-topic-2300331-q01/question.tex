A pizza starts with $49$ slices. Each use of a strange oven changes the number $s$ of slices: if $s$ is even, the oven adds $5$ slices; if $s$ is odd, it removes one slice and then halves the number of remaining slices. Right after the fourth use of the oven, one slice is dropped and thrown away; after that the oven is used again and again. RetroTurtle and his $4$ friends want exactly $2$ slices each, that is, exactly $10$ slices in total. Does the pizza ever have exactly $10$ slices? If so, after how many uses of the oven?
