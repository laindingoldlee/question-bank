Let $ABCD$ be a parallelogram and $P$ a point inside it. Prove that
\[
[PAB]=[PBC]=[PCD]=[PDA]\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad P\text{ is the intersection point of the diagonals }AC\text{ and }BD,
\]
where $[\;]$ denotes area.
