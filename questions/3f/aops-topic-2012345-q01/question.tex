Semicircle $PQR$ has diameter $PR=5\sqrt{13}$, and arc $PQ$ measures $90^\circ$. Points $A$ and $B$ lie on arc $PQ$ (strictly between $P$ and $Q$) so that each of the triangles $PAR$ and $PBR$ has exactly two sides of integer length, and $PB>PA>1$. Let $PB$ and $AR$ intersect at $S$. Find the perimeter of pentagon $PASBR$. Express your answer in simplest radical form.
