If $r_n$ is the rain probability, then $r_{n+1}=0.3r_n+0.1(1-r_n)=0.1+0.2r_n$. The fixed point is $r=\dfrac{1}{8}$, and $r_{n+1}-\dfrac{1}{8}=0.2(r_n-\dfrac{1}{8})$ proves convergence from any starting state. Thus $k=12.5$ and $\boxed{10k=125}$.
