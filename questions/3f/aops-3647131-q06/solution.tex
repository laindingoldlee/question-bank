Classify the equilateral triangles according to whether their horizontal side is at the bottom or top. Two triangles of the same orientation have at most two boundary intersections: their intersection is a triangle with those same three side directions. Oppositely oriented triangles have at most six. If $k$ point up and $6-k$ point down, the total is at most $2[\binom k2+\binom{6-k}2]+6k(6-k)=30+4k(6-k)\le66$. The bound is attained by three slightly translated upward triangles and three slightly translated downward triangles near a six-pointed-star configuration. Make the translations generic to avoid triple intersections. Hence the maximum is $\boxed{66}$.

\end{asy}
