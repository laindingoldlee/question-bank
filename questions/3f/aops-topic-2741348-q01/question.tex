Define the \emph{marshinization} of a function to be the average of its maximum value and the negative of its minimum value, i.e. $\dfrac{\max f-\min f}{2}$. Find the marshinization of $f(x)=\sin^2x+\cos^2(1-x)$ (angles in radians).
