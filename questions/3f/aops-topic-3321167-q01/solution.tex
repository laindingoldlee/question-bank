Since $(3x+\dfrac{17}{3})^2=9x^2+34x+\dfrac{289}{9}$, add and subtract $\boxed{\dfrac{289}{9}}$. The equation becomes $(3x+\dfrac{17}{3})^2-\dfrac{289}{9}-\sqrt2=0$. If one first divides the entire equation by nine, the corresponding added constant in the normalized quadratic is instead $\dfrac{289}{81}$.
