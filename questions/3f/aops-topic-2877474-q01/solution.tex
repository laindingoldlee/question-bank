Four points, one of them the origin, are coplanar exactly when the three position vectors are linearly dependent, i.e. when
\[\det\begin{pmatrix}n&1&4\\5&n&2\\8&6&n\end{pmatrix}=n(n^2-12)-(5n-16)+4(30-8n)=n^3-49n+136=0.\]
Hence $n(n+7)(n-7)=n^3-49n=\boxed{-136}$.

(The cubic $n^3-49n+136$ has exactly one real root, so the value is forced rather than a minimum over several choices.)
