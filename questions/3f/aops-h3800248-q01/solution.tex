Each merge preserves existing coins and adds $p-2$, so the final total is independent of which piles were chosen:
\[n+\sum_{p=2}^{n}(p-2)=n+\dfrac{(n-1)(n-2)}{2}=\dfrac{n(n-1)}{2}+1.\]
For $n\le63$ this is at most $1954$, and for $n\ge64$ it is at least $2017$. Hence $2016$ is impossible. The value $2017$ is attained exactly at $\boxed{n=64}$, since $\dfrac{64\cdot63}{2}+1=2017$ and the formula increases strictly for $n\ge1$.
