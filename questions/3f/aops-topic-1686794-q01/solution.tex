Count by the pattern of digits.
\begin{itemize}
\item Three different digits $1,2,3$: $3!=6$ numbers.
\item A repeated digit: the pair is $22$ or $33$ ($2$ ways), the third digit is one of the other two values ($2$ ways), and the odd digit can be in any of $3$ positions: $2\cdot2\cdot3=12$ numbers.
\end{itemize}
No digit appears three times. The total is $6+12=\boxed{18}$.
