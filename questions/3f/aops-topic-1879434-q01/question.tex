Triangles $ABC$ and $DEF$ are isosceles with $AB=AC=DE=DF=5$ and $BC=EF=6$. A congruence statement $\triangle ABC\cong\triangle XYZ$ means that the correspondence $A\leftrightarrow X$, $B\leftrightarrow Y$, $C\leftrightarrow Z$ matches every side and every angle of $\triangle ABC$ with an equal side or angle of the other triangle. Which of the six statements
\[
\triangle ABC\cong\triangle DEF,\ \triangle DFE,\ \triangle EDF,\ \triangle EFD,\ \triangle FDE,\ \triangle FED
\]
are correct?
