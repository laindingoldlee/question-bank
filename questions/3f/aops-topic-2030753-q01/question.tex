Let $ABC$ be a right triangle with $\angle BCA=90^\circ$ and $CA<CB$. Let $D$ be the point on side $BC$ with $DA=DB$, and let $E$ be the point on side $AB$, different from $A$, with $CE=CA$. Prove that $AD\perp CE$.
