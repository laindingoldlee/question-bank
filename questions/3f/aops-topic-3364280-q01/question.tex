For four-digit positive integers $n$, compare the first three and last three decimal digits of $n^2$. Count the values of $n$ for which (a) these three-digit strings are identical; (b) the sets of digits appearing in the two strings are equal, ignoring order and multiplicity. A complete finite algorithm is allowed.
