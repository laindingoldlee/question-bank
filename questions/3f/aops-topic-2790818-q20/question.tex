A semicircle of radius $1$ has its diameter on a line $\ell$ and is tangent to a line $m$. Let $r$ be the radius of the largest circle that is tangent to $\ell$, to $m$ and to the semicircle and lies on the same side of $m$ as the semicircle. As the point of tangency of $m$ with the semicircle varies, $r$ takes all values in an interval $[a,b]$. Find $b-a$.
