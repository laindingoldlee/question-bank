A diagonal joins vertices separated by $j$ sides along the shorter boundary route, where $2\le j\le1013$. Its length is $2R\sin\dfrac{j\pi}{2026}$. Sine is strictly increasing on this interval, so there are exactly $1012$ possible lengths. Choosing one of each avoids equality; choosing $1013$ diagonals forces an equal pair by the pigeonhole principle. Thus the answer is $\boxed{1013}$.
