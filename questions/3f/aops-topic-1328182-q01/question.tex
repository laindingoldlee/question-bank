An equilateral triangle with side length $6$ has a square of side length $6$ attached outward to each of its sides, as shown.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(6,0),C=(3,3*sqrt(3));
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
pair[] P={A,B,C}; for(int i=0;i<3;++i){pair U=P[i],V=P[(i+1)%3]; pair n=rotate(-90)*(V-U); draw(U--U+n--V+n--V,linewidth(0.9));}
pair X=A+rotate(-90)*(A-C), Y=B+rotate(-90)*(C-B);
dot(X);dot(Y); label("$A$",X,W); label("$B$",Y,E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The distance between the two farthest vertices $A$ and $B$ of this figure can be written as $m+\sqrt n$, where $m$ and $n$ are positive integers. Find $m+n$.
