A rectangle has perimeter $50$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that if the side lengths may be any positive real numbers, the area of the rectangle has no smallest possible value.
\item[(b)] What is the smallest possible area if the side lengths are positive integers?
\end{enumerate}
