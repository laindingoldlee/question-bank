For an integer $k\ge1$, let $T_k$ be the power tower $2019^{2019^{\cdot^{\cdot^{2019}}}}$ containing $k$ copies of $2019$ (so $T_1=2019$ and $T_{k+1}=2019^{T_k}$). Find the remainder when $T_k$ is divided by $2020$, and show that it does not depend on $k$.
