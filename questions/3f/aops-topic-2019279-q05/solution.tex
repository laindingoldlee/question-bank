Modulo $2020$ we have $2019\equiv-1$. Every $T_k$ is odd, being a power of the odd number $2019$. For $k=1$, $T_1=2019$. For $k\ge2$, $T_k=2019^{T_{k-1}}$ with an odd exponent $T_{k-1}$, so
\[
T_k\equiv(-1)^{T_{k-1}}=-1\equiv2019\pmod{2020}.
\]
So for every $k$ the remainder is $\boxed{2019}$.
