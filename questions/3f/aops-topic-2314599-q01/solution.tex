In one hour Leif rakes $\dfrac{1}{2.5}=\dfrac25$ of the yard and Autumn rakes $\dfrac14$ of it. Together they rake
\[\dfrac25+\dfrac14=\dfrac{13}{20}\]
of the yard per hour, so the whole yard takes $\dfrac{20}{13}$ hours, which is
\[\dfrac{20}{13}\cdot60=\dfrac{1200}{13}\approx92.3\text{ minutes},\]
or $\boxed{92}$ minutes to the nearest minute.
