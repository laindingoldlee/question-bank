A distribution is just a list of basket contents in decreasing order, i.e. a partition of $20$ into at most $15$ positive parts. There are $p(20)=627$ partitions of $20$ in all. By conjugation (transposing the Young diagram), partitions with more than $15$ parts correspond to partitions with largest part at least $16$; a partition of $20$ with largest part $m\ge16$ is $m$ followed by a partition of $20-m$, giving
\[
p(4)+p(3)+p(2)+p(1)+p(0)=5+3+2+1+1=12 .
\]
So the answer is $627-12=\boxed{615}$.
