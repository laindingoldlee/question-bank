Comparing consecutive definitions gives $a_n=2+\dfrac{2024}{a_{n-1}}$. Put $P_0=P_1=1$ and $P_n=a_nP_{n-1}$. Then $P_n=2P_{n-1}+2024P_{n-2}$, whose solution is $P_n=\dfrac{46^n+(-44)^n}{2}$. With $r=\dfrac{22}{23}$ and odd $n$, $a_n=46-\dfrac{90r^{n-1}}{1+r^{n-1}}<46$. At $n=2025$, $r^{2024}<2^{-88}<\dfrac{1}{89}$, because $(\dfrac{23}{22})^{23}>1+\dfrac{23}{22}>2$. This makes the subtracted term less than one. Hence $45<a_{2025}<46$ and $\boxed{\lfloor a_{2025}\rfloor=45}$.
