Adding and subtracting the equations:
\[(u-x)(v+y)=105,\qquad(u+x)(v-y)=-27.\]
Since $u-x\le8$ and $v+y\le18$, the only factorisation of $105$ that fits is $7\cdot15$. So $u=x+7$, which forces $x\in\{1,2\}$ and $u+x\in\{9,11\}$; also $v+y=15$. Since $11\nmid27$, $u+x=9$ and $v-y=-3$. Hence $x=1$, $u=8$, $v=6$, $y=9$, and $u+v+x+y=\boxed{24}$.
