Let the integer common difference be $d$. The endpoint ranges give $231\le14d\le249$, so $d=17$. Then $a_2=a_1+17\le20$ forces $a_1\le3$, while $a_{15}=a_1+238\ge241$ forces $a_1\ge3$. Thus $a_1=3$, and $a_{14}=3+13\cdot17=224$. Its digit sum is $\boxed8$.
