Since $16=2^4$, we have $16^4=\left(2^4\right)^4=2^{16}$. The $16$th root of $2^{16}$ is the positive number whose $16$th power is $2^{16}$, so
\[
\sqrt[16]{16^4}=\sqrt[16]{2^{16}}=\boxed{2}.
\]
(Equivalently, $\sqrt[16]{16^4}=16^{\dfrac{4}{16}}=16^{\dfrac{1}{4}}=2$.)
