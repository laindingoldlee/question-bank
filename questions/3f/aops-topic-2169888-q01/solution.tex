Let $a=\sqrt[4]2$ and $b=\sqrt[4]3$. Using the difference of squares twice,
\[(a+b)(a-b)(a^2+b^2)=(a^2-b^2)(a^2+b^2)=a^4-b^4=2-3=-1 .\]
So multiply the numerator and the denominator by $(a-b)(a^2+b^2)=\left(\sqrt[4]2-\sqrt[4]3\right)\left(\sqrt2+\sqrt3\right)$:
\[\dfrac{7}{\sqrt[4]2+\sqrt[4]3}=\dfrac{7\left(\sqrt[4]2-\sqrt[4]3\right)\left(\sqrt2+\sqrt3\right)}{-1}=\boxed{7\left(\sqrt[4]3-\sqrt[4]2\right)\left(\sqrt2+\sqrt3\right)} .\]
Expanding, with $\sqrt[4]3\cdot\sqrt2=\sqrt[4]{12}$, $\sqrt[4]3\cdot\sqrt3=\sqrt[4]{27}$, $\sqrt[4]2\cdot\sqrt2=\sqrt[4]8$ and $\sqrt[4]2\cdot\sqrt3=\sqrt[4]{18}$, this is also
\[7\left(\sqrt[4]{27}+\sqrt[4]{12}-\sqrt[4]{18}-\sqrt[4]{8}\right)\approx 2.794 .\]
