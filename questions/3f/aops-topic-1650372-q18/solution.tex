The product telescopes:
\[
\dfrac {a}{d}=\dfrac {a}{b}\cdot\dfrac {b}{c}\cdot\dfrac {c}{d}=\dfrac{3}{5}\cdot\dfrac{15}{6}\cdot6=\dfrac{3}{5}\cdot15=\boxed{9}.
\]
