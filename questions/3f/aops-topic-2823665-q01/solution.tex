Cross-multiplying the first equation: $(2a+b)(3c+d)=(3a+b)(2c+d)$, i.e. $6ac+2ad+3bc+bd=6ac+3ad+2bc+bd$, so $bc=ad$. Hence $\dfrac ac=\dfrac bd=r$, i.e. $a=rc$ and $b=rd$.

Then $\dfrac{ka+b}{kc+d}=\dfrac{r(kc+d)}{kc+d}=r$ for every $k$. The second condition gives $r=4$, so $\dfrac{5a+b}{5c+d}=\boxed{4}$.
