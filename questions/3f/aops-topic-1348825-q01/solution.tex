Move everything to one side: $xy-2x-3y=0$. Adding $6$ to both sides makes the left side factor:
\[
xy-2x-3y+6=6\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad (x-3)(y-2)=6 .
\]
Since $x\ge1$ and $y\ge1$, we have $x-3\ge-2$ and $y-2\ge-1$. If both factors were negative, their product $6$ would need $|x-3|\cdot|y-2|=6$ with $|x-3|\le2$ and $|y-2|\le1$, which is impossible. So both factors are positive divisors of $6$:
\[
(x-3,\ y-2)\in\{(1,6),(2,3),(3,2),(6,1)\}.
\]
This gives the $4$ solutions
\[
\boxed{(x,y)=(4,8),\ (5,5),\ (6,4),\ (9,3)} .
\]
Each checks: for example $4\cdot8=32=8+24$.
