Let the ages be $a\le b$ with $ab=1188$. In a year the product is $(a+1)(b+1)=ab+a+b+1=1189+a+b$. The factor pairs of $1188$ and the resulting products are
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccccccccccc}
(a,b)&(1,1188)&(2,594)&(3,396)&(4,297)&(6,198)&(9,132)\\ \hline
(a+1)(b+1)&2378&1785&1588&1490&1393&1330
\end{array}
\]
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccccc}
(a,b)&(11,108)&(12,99)&(18,66)&(22,54)&(27,44)&(33,36)\\ \hline
(a+1)(b+1)&1308&1300&1273&1265&1260&1258
\end{array}
\]
Here $1785=5\cdot357$ is divisible by $5$; $1260=2^2\cdot3^2\cdot5\cdot7$ is divisible by $6$ and $18$; $1258=2\cdot17\cdot37$ is divisible by $37$. But none of the twelve products is divisible by $22$: a multiple of $22$ is even, and the even products are $2378=2\cdot29\cdot41$, $1588=4\cdot397$, $1490=2\cdot5\cdot149$, $1330=2\cdot5\cdot7\cdot19$, $1308=4\cdot3\cdot109$, $1300=4\cdot25\cdot13$, $1260$ and $1258$, none of which is divisible by $11$.

So the answer is $\boxed{\text{(D) } 22}$.
