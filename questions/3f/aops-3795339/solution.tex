Put $R(t)=Q(1+t)$. Since $e^x$ covers all positive reals, $R(t)+R(-t)$ is constant as a polynomial identity. Consequently $R$ has no positive even-degree terms, so $R\prime$ is even of degree $98$ and has zero coefficient of $t^{97}$. Its root sum is zero by Vieta. The roots of $Q\prime(x)=R\prime(x-1)$ are obtained by adding $1$ to each of these $98$ roots. The required sum is $\boxed{98}$.
