Of the grade $5$ students in a city, $9\%$ like riding a scooter and $14\%$ like skateboarding; of the grade $6$ students, $11\%$ like scooters and $7\%$ like skateboarding. No student likes both, and over both grades the numbers liking scooters and skateboarding are equal. There are $59400$ grade $5$ and $6$ students in total. How many grade $6$ students are there?
