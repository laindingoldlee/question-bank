With $p$ grade $5$ and $q$ grade $6$ students: $0.09p+0.11q=0.14p+0.07q$, so $0.04q=0.05p$ and $q=\dfrac54p$. Then $\dfrac94p=59400$ gives $p=26400$ and $q=\boxed{33000}$.
