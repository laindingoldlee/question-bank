The prime factorization is $11!=2^8 3^4 5^2 7\cdot11$. Independently for each prime with maximum exponent $e$, the ordered exponents of $(a,b)$ have $(e+1)^2$ possibilities, of which $\dfrac{(e+1)(e+2)}{2}$ satisfy the exponent in $a$ at least that in $b$. The required probability is therefore $\dfrac{10}{18}\dfrac{6}{10}\dfrac{4}{6}\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^2=\dfrac{1}{8}$. Hence $\boxed{10m+n=18}$.
