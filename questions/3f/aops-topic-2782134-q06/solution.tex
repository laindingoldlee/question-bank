Their angular coordinates $ u,v $ are independent uniform numbers in $[0,\pi]$. The condition is $|u-v|>\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. In the square of side $\pi $, it occupies two right triangles with legs $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, of total area $\dfrac{\pi^2}{4}$. Dividing by $\pi^2 $ gives $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{4}}$.
