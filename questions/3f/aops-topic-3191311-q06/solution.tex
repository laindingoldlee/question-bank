The complementary subset must have no adjacent vertices. If a fixed vertex is excluded from this independent subset, the remaining five vertices form a path with $13$ independent subsets. If it is included, its two neighbors are excluded, leaving a path of three vertices with $5$ independent subsets. For a path of length $n$, counts satisfy $I_n=I_{n-1}+I_{n-2}$ with $I_0=1,I_1=2$, giving $I_3=5,I_5=13$. Total $\boxed{18}$.
