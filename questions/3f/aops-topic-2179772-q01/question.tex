Throughout, $a$ and $c$ are positive real numbers different from $1$, and $b$ and $d$ are positive real numbers. Recall that $\log_a y=t$ means $a^t=y$. Prove the following properties of logarithms.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $\log_a b^n=n\log_a b$ for every real number $n$.
\item[(b)] $\log_a b+\log_a c=\log_a(bc)$.
\item[(c)] $\log_a b-\log_a c=\log_a\dfrac{b}{c}$.
\item[(d)] $\dfrac{\log_a b}{\log_a c}=\log_c b$.
\item[(e)] $\left(\log_a b\right)\left(\log_c d\right)=\left(\log_a d\right)\left(\log_c b\right)$.
\item[(f)] $\log_{a^n}b^n=\log_a b$ for every real number $n\ne0$.
\end{enumerate}
