The whole trip is $34215+5785=40000$ km, so we want the largest number not exceeding $40000$ whose digits are five consecutive digits in some order.

Such a number below $40000$ has first digit at most $3$. To be as large as possible its first digit should be $3$, and the five consecutive digits must then include $3$: they are $\{0,\dots,4\}$, $\{1,\dots,5\}$, $\{2,\dots,6\}$ or $\{3,\dots,7\}$. Putting $3$ first and the other digits in decreasing order gives $34210$, $35421$, $36542$, $37654$; the largest is $37654$. He then has
\[
40000-37654=\boxed{2346}\text{ km}
\]
left to travel.
