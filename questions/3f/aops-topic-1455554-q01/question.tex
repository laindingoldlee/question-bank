On his trip around the world, Phileas Fogg has travelled $34215$ kilometres, a number whose five digits are five consecutive digits ($1,2,3,4,5$ in some order). At this point he has $5785$ km left to travel to finish his trip. When, later on this trip, he has travelled the greatest number of kilometres that is written with five consecutive digits (in some order), how many kilometres will he have left to travel?
