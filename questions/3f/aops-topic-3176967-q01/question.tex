Six independent fair coins are placed in order. Inspect them until either three heads or three tails have appeared, remove those three matching coins, and consider the other three. Explain why the probability that the remaining coins are all opposite to the removed ones is not simply $\dfrac{1}{8}$. Find the actual probability.
