The speed of a car increases uniformly from $30$ km/h to $75$ km/h in $15$ seconds. It then decreases uniformly for $50$ seconds, until the car stops.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the acceleration of the car during the first $15$ seconds in metres per second squared, and interpret it.
\item[(b)] What is the speed of the car, in km/h, $30$ seconds after the start?
\end{enumerate}
