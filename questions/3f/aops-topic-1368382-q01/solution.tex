CCARAVEN has $8$ letters: $C$ twice, $A$ twice, and $R$, $V$, $E$, $N$ once each. Arranging $8$ letters in which two pairs are identical gives
\[\dfrac{8!}{2!\,2!}=\dfrac{40320}{4}=\boxed{10080}\]
arrangements (counting the original order as one of them).
