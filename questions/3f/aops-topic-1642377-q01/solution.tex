The number of trailing zeros of $n!$ is the exponent of $5$ in $n!$ (there are more factors $2$ than $5$):
\[
Z(n)=\left\lfloor\dfrac {n}{5}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac {n}{25}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac {n}{125}\right\rfloor+\cdots .
\]
$Z$ is non-decreasing and only changes at multiples of $5$. Now $Z(100)=20+4=24$ and $Z(105)=21+4=25$, while $Z(110)=22+4=26$. So $Z(n)=25$ exactly for $n=105,106,107,108,109$, which is $\boxed{5}$ values.
