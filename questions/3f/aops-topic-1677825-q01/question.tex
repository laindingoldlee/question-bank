An ``EV spread'' is a $6$-tuple of integers $(a_1,a_2,\dots,a_6)$ with $0\le a_i\le252$ for each $i$ and $a_1+a_2+\cdots+a_6\le510$. How many EV spreads are there? (Leave the answer in terms of binomial coefficients, then evaluate it.)
