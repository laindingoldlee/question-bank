A person climbs a staircase from the floor (step $0$) to the top (step $20$), going up $1$ or $2$ steps at a time. Step $13$ is booby-trapped: landing on it is safe only if the person has already landed on step $7$, which deactivates the trap. In how many ways can the person climb the staircase safely?
