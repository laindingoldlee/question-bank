For $21\le n\le99$, the digit total is $2n-9$, giving mean strictly between 1 and 2. For $100\le n\le108$, it is $9+180+3(n-99)=3n-108$. This is below $2n$ until $n=108$, where the mean is 2. Hence $\boxed{108}$.
