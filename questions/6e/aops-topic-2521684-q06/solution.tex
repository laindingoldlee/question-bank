The sum is $\dfrac{10^6\left(10^6+1\right)}{2}=500000\cdot1000001$. Modulo $12$: $500000=12\cdot41666+8\equiv8$ and $1000001=12\cdot83333+5\equiv5$, so the sum is $\equiv8\cdot5=40\equiv\boxed{4}\pmod{12}$.
