Add the three equations:
\[
(6x+5y)+(x-2z)+(2y+9z)=7x+7y+7z=28+19+37=84 ,
\]
so $x+y+z=\dfrac{84}{7}=\boxed{\textbf{(c)}\ 12}$. (Solving the system completely gives $x=53$, $y=-58$, $z=17$, and indeed $53-58+17=12$.)
