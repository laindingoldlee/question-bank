On an island live $13$ red, $15$ blue and $17$ green chameleons. Whenever two chameleons of different colours meet, both change to the third colour: a red and a green one both become blue, a red and a blue one both become green, and a blue and a green one both become red. Meetings may happen any number of times, in any order. Is it possible that at some moment all the chameleons have the same colour? Prove your answer.
