Let the first term be $a$ and the common difference $d$. Then $a+2d=20$, and the sum of the first eight terms is $\dfrac{8}{2}\left(2a+7d\right)=244$, so $2a+7d=61$. Subtracting twice the first equation, $3d=21$, so $d=7$ and $a=20-14=\boxed{6}$. (The terms are $6,13,20,27,34,41,48,55$, with sum $244$.)
