Compute: $u_2=\dfrac{2}{4}=\dfrac{1}{2}$, $u_3=\dfrac{3+2}{4-\dfrac{13}{2}}=-2$, $u_4=\dfrac{-12+2}{4+26}=-\dfrac{1}{3}$, $u_5=\dfrac{-2+2}{4+\dfrac{13}{3}}=0=u_1$. Since each term determines the next, the sequence repeats with period $4$:
\[
u_n=\begin{cases}0,&n\equiv1\pmod4\\[2pt]\dfrac{1}{2},&n\equiv2\pmod4\\[2pt]-2,&n\equiv3\pmod4\\[2pt]-\dfrac{1}{3},&n\equiv0\pmod4.\end{cases}
\]
(The map $u\mapsto\dfrac{6u+2}{-13u+4}$ corresponds to the matrix $\begin{pmatrix}6&2\\-13&4\end{pmatrix}$ with eigenvalues $5\pm5i$; its fourth power is $-2500I$, so applying the map four times is the identity.)
