Arrangements of $4$ people in a circle up to rotation number $(4-1)!=6$. Here a seating and its mirror image give every person the same two neighbours, so they are the same; this pairs up the $6$ arrangements. The answer is $\dfrac{6}{2}=\boxed{3}$ (they are determined by who sits opposite person $A$).
