Taking reciprocals, $\dfrac{1}{a\#b}=\dfrac{a+b}{ab}=\dfrac{1}{a}+\dfrac{1}{b}$. So $\#$ adds reciprocals; in particular it is associative, and the grouping does not matter. With ten copies of $2010$,
\[
\dfrac{1}{\text{value}}=10\cdot\dfrac{1}{2010}=\dfrac{1}{201},
\]
so the value is $\boxed{201}$.
