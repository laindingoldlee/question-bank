Let the numbers be $n$ and $n+18$. Then $n(n+18)=403$, i.e.
\[
n^2+18n-403=0\iff(n-13)(n+31)=0 .
\]
As $n$ is a natural number, $n=13$, and the numbers are $\boxed{13\text{ and }31}$ (indeed $13\cdot31=403$).
