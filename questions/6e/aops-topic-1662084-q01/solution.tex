Convert to single units: $43\text{ km }200\text{ m}=43.2$ km and $2\text{ L }400\text{ ml}=2.4$ L. At a constant rate of consumption the distance per litre is
\[
\dfrac{43.2}{2.4}=\dfrac{432}{24}=\boxed{18\text{ km}}.
\]
