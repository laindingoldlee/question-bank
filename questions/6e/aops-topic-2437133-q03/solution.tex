By the quadratic formula with $a=1$, $b=5$, $c=-7$, the discriminant is $b^2-4ac=25+28=53$, and
\[x=\boxed{\dfrac{-5\pm\sqrt{53}}{2}}\]
(approximately $1.14$ and $-6.14$). Check: the roots add up to $-5$ and multiply to $\dfrac{25-53}{4}=-7$, as Vieta's formulas require.
