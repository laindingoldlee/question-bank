BLUEMOUNTAINS has $13$ letters, of which the vowels are U, E, O, U, A, I: six vowels with U repeated. In a random arrangement, the set of positions occupied by vowels and the order in which the vowels appear in those positions are independent, and every distinguishable order of the vowels A, E, I, O, U, U is equally likely. There are $\dfrac{6!}{2!}=360$ such orders, and exactly one of them, A, E, I, O, U, U, is alphabetical. The probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{360}}$.
