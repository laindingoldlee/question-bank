Measure time in hours after 9:00 a.m.: Billy arrives at time $x$ and Johnny at time $y$, and the point $(x,y)$ is uniformly distributed in the square $0\le x\le2$, $0\le y\le2$ of area $4$. They arrive within half an hour of each other exactly when $|x-y|\le\dfrac{1}{2}$, the shaded band:

label("$0$",(0,0),SW);label("$\dfrac{1}{2}$",(0.5,0),S);label("$2$",(2,0),S);

The unshaded part consists of two right isosceles triangles with legs $2-\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{3}{2}$, of total area $2\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\left(\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^2=\dfrac{9}{4}$. So the band has area $4-\dfrac{9}{4}=\dfrac{7}{4}$, and the probability is
\[
\dfrac{\dfrac{7}{4}}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{16}}.
\]
