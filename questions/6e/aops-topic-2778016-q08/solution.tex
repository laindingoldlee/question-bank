Let per-animal catches be $ d,c $. Then $ 3d+4c=\dfrac{23}{20}(4d+3c)$, giving $ 11c=32d $. Write $ d=11t,c=32t $. The team totals are 161 t,140 t,119 t, so $ 420t=120 $. Thus C catches $ 119\cdot\dfrac{120}{420}=\boxed{34}$ mice.
