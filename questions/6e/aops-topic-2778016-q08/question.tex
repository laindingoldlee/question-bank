Team A has 3 dogs and 4 cats, B has 4 dogs and 3 cats, and C has 5 dogs and 2 cats. Each dog catches at the same constant rate and each cat at another constant rate. Working for the same time, the teams catch 120 mice in total, and A catches 15 percent more than B. How many does C catch?
