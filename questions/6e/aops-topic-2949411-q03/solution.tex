If the vertex labels are $n,n+1,n+2$, their sum is $3n+3$. The edge labels sum to $6n+6$, equal to the centre label. Thus the total is $15n+15=120$, giving $\boxed{n=7}$. All edge and centre labels exceed 7.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(4,0),C=(2,3);draw(A--B--C--cycle);label("$n$",A,SW);label("$n+1$",B,SE);label("$n+2$",C,N);label("$2n+1$",(A+B)/2,S);label("$2n+2$",(A+C)/2,W);label("$2n+3$",(B+C)/2,E);label("$6n+6$",(2,1));
\end{asy}
