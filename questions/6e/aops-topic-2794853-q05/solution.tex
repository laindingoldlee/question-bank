Let $h$ be the distance from $P$ to line $AD$, so its distance to $BC$ is $h-4$. Similarity gives $\dfrac{EF}{AD}=\dfrac{h-4}{h}=\dfrac{3}{4}$, whence $h=16$. The area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot4\cdot16=\boxed{32}$.
