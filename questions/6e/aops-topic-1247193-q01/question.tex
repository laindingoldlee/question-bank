A student states the identity $\dbinom NK=\dbinom N{K-1}+\dbinom{N-1}{K-1}$. Show that it is false in general, and prove the correct Pascal's rule $\dbinom NK=\dbinom{N-1}K+\dbinom{N-1}{K-1}$ for $1\le K\le N-1$.
