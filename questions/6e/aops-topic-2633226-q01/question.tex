For how many integers $n$ with $1\le n\le1000$ is $\sqrt n$ already in simplest radical form, that is, $n$ has no perfect-square factor greater than $1$? (For example $282$, $67$ and $91$ count, but $96$, $512$ and $961$ do not.)
