The pairs are $\{n,n+7\}$ with $1\le n\le93$. We need $5\mid n$ or $5\mid n+7$, i.e. $n\equiv0$ or $3\pmod5$ (these cannot both hold). There are $18$ values $n\equiv0$ ($5,\dots,90$) and $19$ values $n\equiv3$ ($3,\dots,93$). Total $\boxed{37}$.
