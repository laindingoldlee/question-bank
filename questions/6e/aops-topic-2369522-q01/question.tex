Two circles with the same centre have radii $r$ and $\sqrt2\,r$. Two points are chosen on the circle of radius $r$ and one point on the circle of radius $\sqrt2\,r$, each uniformly at random and independently. Find the probability that the triangle formed by the three points is obtuse. (An exact expression as a definite integral, together with its value to three decimal places, is enough.)
