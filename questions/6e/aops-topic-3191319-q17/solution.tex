The fraction reduces to $\dfrac{2^{32}5^{16}}{2^{80}}=\dfrac{5^{16}}{2^{48}}$. A reduced fraction with denominator $2^{48}$ first becomes an integer after multiplication by $10^{48}$; smaller powers retain a factor of $2$ in the denominator. Hence there are exactly $\boxed{48}$ decimal places.
