First choose the $k$ vertices of the cycle: $\dbinom nk$ ways. Next count the cycles through $k$ given labelled vertices. Listing the vertices around the cycle gives $k!$ sequences, and each cycle arises from exactly $2k$ of them ($k$ starting points and $2$ directions; for $k\ge3$ the two directions give the same edge set but different sequences). So there are $\dfrac{k!}{2k}=\dfrac{(k-1)!}{2}$ cycles. The total is
\[\boxed{\dbinom nk\cdot\dfrac{(k-1)!}{2}=\dfrac{n!}{2k\,(n-k)!}}.\]
For example, $n=k=4$ gives $3$ graphs.
