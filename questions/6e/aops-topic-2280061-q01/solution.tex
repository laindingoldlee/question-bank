Let the time now be $m$ minutes after 10:00, with $0<m<60$, and measure angles in degrees clockwise from 12. The minute hand moves $6^\circ$ per minute and the hour hand $0.5^\circ$ per minute, starting from $300^\circ$ at 10:00.
\begin{itemize}
\item Six minutes from now, the minute hand is at $6(m+6)$ degrees.
\item Three minutes ago, the hour hand was at $300+0.5(m-3)$ degrees.
\end{itemize}
Being opposite means the angles differ by $180^\circ$ (modulo $360^\circ$):
\[6(m+6)\equiv300+0.5(m-3)+180\pmod{360}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad5.5m\equiv442.5\equiv82.5\pmod{360}.\]
So $m=15+\dfrac{360k}{5.5}$ for an integer $k$, and the only value with $0<m<60$ is $m=15$. The time is $\boxed{10{:}15}$.

Check: at 10:21 the minute hand is at $126^\circ$; at 10:12 the hour hand was at $306^\circ=126^\circ+180^\circ$.
