Since $(x-4y)^2\ge0$, $x^2+16y^2\ge8xy$. Thus the expression is at least $24xy+4z^2=\dfrac{1536}{z}+4z^2$. Apply AM–GM to $\dfrac{768}{z},\dfrac{768}{z},4z^2$: the sum is at least $3\sqrt[3]{768^2\cdot4}=192\sqrt[3]9$. Equality holds when $x=4y$ and $z^3=192$. For example, $z=4\sqrt[3]3$, $y=\dfrac{2}{\sqrt[6]3}$ and $x=\dfrac{8}{\sqrt[6]3}$ have product 64 and attain equality. Therefore the minimum is $\boxed{192\sqrt[3]9}$.
