Corner: $2$. Squares next to a corner on the edge: $3$. Other edge squares, and the diagonal neighbour of a corner: $4$. Second ring apart from its corners: $6$. Central $4\times4$: $8$. Checking the rings covers every square, so $n\in\{2,3,4,6,8\}$: $\boxed{5}$ values ($5$ and $7$ never occur).
