The centers are $29$ apart. Their perpendicular offsets from the common tangent differ by $21$, forming a right triangle with the tangent segment. Therefore $AB=\sqrt{29^2-21^2}=\boxed{20}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair O=(0,0),P=(29,0),u=(21/29,20/29),A=25*u,Bp=P+4*u;draw(circle(O,25));draw(circle(P,4));draw(A--Bp,blue);draw(O--A,dashed);draw(P--Bp,dashed);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",Bp,N);label("$25$",O);label("$4$",P);
\end{asy}
