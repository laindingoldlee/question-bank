Let $ABCD$ be a convex quadrilateral, $P=AD\cap BC$ and $Q=AB\cap CD$, where $A$ lies between $P$ and $D$, $B$ between $A$ and $Q$, $C$ between $D$ and $Q$, and $B$ between $P$ and $C$. Prove that the circumcircles of $PAB$, $PCD$, $QAD$ and $QBC$ pass through a common point.
