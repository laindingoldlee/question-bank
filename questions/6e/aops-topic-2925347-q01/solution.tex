Use directed angles modulo $180^\circ$. Let $M\ne P$ be the second intersection of circles $PAB$ and $PCD$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(3,0),C=(4,3),D=(-1,4);pair P=extension(A,D,B,C),Q=extension(A,B,C,D);
pair cc(pair a,pair b,pair c){real d=2*(a.x*(b.y-c.y)+b.x*(c.y-a.y)+c.x*(a.y-b.y));real ux=((a.x^2+a.y^2)*(b.y-c.y)+(b.x^2+b.y^2)*(c.y-a.y)+(c.x^2+c.y^2)*(a.y-b.y))/d;real uy=((a.x^2+a.y^2)*(c.x-b.x)+(b.x^2+b.y^2)*(a.x-c.x)+(c.x^2+c.y^2)*(b.x-a.x))/d;return (ux,uy);}
draw(P--D);draw(P--C);draw(A--Q);draw(D--Q);
pair O1=cc(P,A,B),O2=cc(Q,B,C);draw(circle(O1,abs(O1-P)),gray);draw(circle(O2,abs(O2-Q)),gray);
label("$A$",A,NW);label("$B$",B,NE);label("$C$",C,N);label("$D$",D,N);label("$P$",P,S);label("$Q$",Q,E);
\end{asy}

Then
\[\angle(AM,AQ)=\angle(AM,AB)=\angle(PM,PB)=\angle(PM,PC)=\angle(DM,DC)=\angle(DM,DQ),\]
using in turn $Q\in AB$, circle $PABM$, $C\in PB$, circle $PCDM$ and $Q\in DC$. Hence $A,D,M,Q$ are concyclic. Similarly
\[\angle(BM,BQ)=\angle(BM,BA)=\angle(PM,PA)=\angle(PM,PD)=\angle(CM,CD)=\angle(CM,CQ),\]
so $B,C,M,Q$ are concyclic. All four circles pass through $M$ (the Miquel point). $\blacksquare$
