The number is $22n$: even and divisible by $11$. Divisibility by $11$ means the alternating digit sum is $0$, i.e. the digits in odd places and in even places form the pairs $\{4,7\}$ and $\{5,6\}$. Being even, it ends in $4$ or $6$. The largest is $7546$ (pattern $7,5,4,6$), so $n=\dfrac{7546}{22}=\boxed{343}$.
