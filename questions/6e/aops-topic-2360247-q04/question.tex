Two circles of radius $3$ are externally tangent at a point $T$. A third circle has centre $T$ and is internally tangent to both of the smaller circles, which lie inside it. A rhombus has two opposite vertices at the centres $O_1$, $O_2$ of the two smaller circles, and its other two vertices on the third circle. Find the area of the rhombus.
