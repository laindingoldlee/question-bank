Tram $A$ reaches speed $10$ at $t=5$, after moving $25$ metres. Thus for $t\ge5$, $s_A=10t-25$. Tram $B$ reaches speed $12$ at $t=24$, after moving $24$ metres; for $t\ge24$, $s_B=12t-264$.

Their positive speeds agree while $B$ accelerates, when $3(t-20)=10$, so $t=\dfrac{70}{3}$. At this instant
\[
s_A=\dfrac{625}{3},\qquad
s_B=\dfrac{1}{2}(3)\left(\dfrac{10}{3}\right)^2=\dfrac{50}{3}.
\]
The lead is $\boxed{\dfrac{575}{3}\text{ metres}}$.
At $t=24$, $A$ still leads, and afterward $B$ is faster. The catch-up equation is $10t-25=12t-264$, yielding $t=\dfrac{239}{2}$. Their distance is
\[
\boxed{10\left(\dfrac{239}{2}\right)-25=1170\text{ metres}}.
\]
