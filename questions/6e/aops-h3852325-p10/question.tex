Two trams start at the same depot on parallel tracks. Tram $A$ starts at $t=0$, accelerates at $2$ metres per second squared for $5$ seconds, then maintains its speed. Tram $B$ starts from rest at $t=20$, accelerates at $3$ metres per second squared for $4$ seconds, then maintains its speed. They travel in the same direction and are modeled as particles.
\begin{enumerate}\item Sketch both speed-time graphs until $B$ catches $A$.
\item When they have the same positive speed, find the distance by which $A$ leads.
\item Find their distance from the depot when $B$ catches $A$.\end{enumerate}
