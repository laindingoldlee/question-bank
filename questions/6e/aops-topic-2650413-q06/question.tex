For sets $P$ and $Q$, $P\,\Delta\,Q$ denotes the set of elements in exactly one of $P$ and $Q$; for example $\{1,2,3,4\}\,\Delta\,\{2,3,5\}=\{1,4,5\}$. Let $X=\{1,2,3,4,5\}$ and $Y=\{1,2,\dots,10\}$. How many pairs $(A,B)$ with $A\subseteq X$ and $B\subseteq Y$ (either may be empty) satisfy $\left|A\,\Delta\,B\right|=3$?
