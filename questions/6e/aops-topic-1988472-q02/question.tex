On each side of a triangle $ABC$ of area $10\text{ cm}^2$, a square is drawn outside the triangle. At each vertex of the triangle, the two squares meeting there each have one more vertex adjacent to that vertex of the triangle; these two points and the vertex form a triangle. (So three new triangles are formed, one at each vertex.) These three triangles are then rotated, each about its vertex of $ABC$, until one of its sides lies along a side of triangle $ABC$, and the squares are removed. What is the total area of the figure formed by triangle $ABC$ and the three rotated triangles?
