Let $E$ be the expectation from a fresh start. The waiting time for the first head has expectation $\dfrac{3}{2}$. After that head, let $D$ be the waiting time to the next head. It is geometric with the same mean. If $D\le4$, the game stops after those $D$ additional flips. If the next four flips are tails, the old head can no longer contribute and the process restarts after four additional flips. Hence
\[E=\dfrac{3}{2}+\mathbb E[D\mathbf1_{D\le4}]+\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^4(4+E).\]
The geometric distribution is memoryless, so $\mathbb E[D\mid D>4]=4+\dfrac{3}{2}=\dfrac{11}{2}$. Thus the truncated expectation equals $\dfrac{3}{2}-\dfrac{1}{81}\cdot\dfrac{11}{2}$. Substitution gives
\[E=3+\dfrac{E-\dfrac{3}{2}}{81},\qquad80E=\dfrac{483}{2},\qquad\boxed{E=\dfrac{483}{160}}.\]
