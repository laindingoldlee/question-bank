Let the one-way distance be $d>0$ and the box count be $2k$. If the two-box speed is $v$, equality of total times gives
\[
2k\left(\dfrac{d}{2.4}+\dfrac{d}{2.8}\right)
=k\left(\dfrac{d}{v}+\dfrac{d}{2.8}\right).
\]
Cancel $kd$ to obtain
\[
\dfrac{1}{v}=\dfrac{2}{2.4}+\dfrac{1}{2.8}
=\dfrac{5}{6}+\dfrac{5}{14}=\dfrac{25}{21}.
\]
Thus $\boxed{v=\dfrac{21}{25}=0.84\text{ miles per hour}}$.
