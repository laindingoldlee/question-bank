Substituting, $2n-1=4m-3$. Since $m\mid4m-3$ and $m\mid4m$, $m$ divides the difference $4m-(4m-3)=3$. So $m\in\{1,3\}$, giving $n=1$ or $n=5$. Both work: $1\mid1$ and $3\mid9$. The pairs are $\boxed{(m,n)=(1,1)\text{ and }(3,5)}$.
