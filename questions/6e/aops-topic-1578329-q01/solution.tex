Let the integers be $a<b<c<d$, with $a+b+c+d=16$. The median is $\dfrac{b+c}{2}$.

\textbf{Range of $b+c$.} The smallest values are $a=1$, $b=2$, $c=3$, giving $b+c=5$ (with $d=10$). For the largest: $a\ge1$ and $d\ge c+1$, so $b+2c\le14$; also $b\le c-1$. Hence $b+c\le14-c$ and $b+c\le2c-1$, so $b+c\le9$ (if $c\ge5$ the first bound gives at most $9$; if $c\le4$ the second gives at most $7$).

\textbf{Every value from $5$ to $9$ occurs:}
\[
\{1,2,3,10\},\ \{1,2,4,9\},\ \{1,3,4,8\},\ \{1,3,5,7\},\ \{1,4,5,6\}
\]
give $b+c=5,6,7,8,9$. So the possible medians are $2.5,3,3.5,4,4.5$, with sum
\[
2.5+3+3.5+4+4.5=\boxed{17.5}.
\]
