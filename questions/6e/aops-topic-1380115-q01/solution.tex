Let $a_n$ be the number of ways for a $1\times n$ strip. Look at the block covering the last cell: a $1\times1$ block leaves a $1\times(n-1)$ strip, and a $1\times2$ block leaves a $1\times(n-2)$ strip. So
\[
a_n=a_{n-1}+a_{n-2},\qquad a_1=1,\ a_2=2 .
\]
Hence $a_3=3$, $a_4=5$, $a_5=8$, $a_6=13$, $a_7=21$, $a_8=\boxed{34}$.
