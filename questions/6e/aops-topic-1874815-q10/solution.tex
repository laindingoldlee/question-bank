Find the binary digits by repeated doubling: a binary digit after the point is $1$ exactly when doubling the current fractional part reaches $1$ or more.
\[
\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot2=\dfrac{2}{3}\ (\text{digit }0),\qquad \dfrac{2}{3}\cdot2=\dfrac{4}{3}=1+\dfrac{1}{3}\ (\text{digit }1),
\]
and we are back at $\dfrac{1}{3}$, so the digits $01$ repeat: $\dfrac{1}{3}=\boxed{0.\overline{01}_2}=0.010101\ldots_2$.

Check: $0.010101\ldots_2=\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{4^2}+\dfrac{1}{4^3}+\cdots=\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{4}}{1-\dfrac{1}{4}}=\dfrac{1}{3}$.
