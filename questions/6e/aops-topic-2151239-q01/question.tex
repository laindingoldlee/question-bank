A positive integer $N$ has $n$ digits and its leftmost digit is $3$. Moving this $3$ from the left end to the right end produces a new number $M$ (for example, $N=3125$ gives $M=1253$).
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that $M=3N$ is impossible.
\item[(b)] Suppose instead that $M=\dfrac {N}{3}$. Find the least possible value of $n$.
\end{enumerate}
