There are $2000$ cells in a jail, numbered $1$ through $2000$, each containing one inmate. One night, while all the inmates are asleep, a series of $2000$ computer glitches occurs. All cells start locked. Glitch $1$ turns the locks of all the cells, unlocking them. Glitch $2$ turns the locks of every even-numbered cell ($2,4,6,\dots$), relocking those cells. Glitch $3$ turns the locks of every third cell ($3,6,9,\dots$), locking those that were unlocked and unlocking those that were locked. This goes on: for each $n=1,2,\dots,2000$, glitch $n$ turns the locks of the cells whose numbers are multiples of $n$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] For a cell numbered $m$, which of the $2000$ glitches affect that cell?
\item[(b)] How many of the prisoners will awaken to unlocked cells?
\end{enumerate}
