The probability is $\dfrac{a(a-1)+b(b-1)}{(a+b)(a+b-1)}$. Setting it equal to $\dfrac{1}{2}$ simplifies to $(b-a)^2=a+b$. With $d=b-a\ge0$, we obtain $a=\dfrac{d(d-1)}{2}$ and $b=\dfrac{d(d+1)}{2}$. The restrictions give $d\ge4$ and $d\le63$, because $63\cdot64=4032\le4052<64\cdot65$. Each such $d$ determines exactly one pair. Hence the answer is $63-4+1=\boxed{60}$.
