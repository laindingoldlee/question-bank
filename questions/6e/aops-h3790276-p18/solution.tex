There are $26+23+21=70$ lost items in total. If $t$ students lost all three, the total losses are at most $3t+2(30-t)=60+t$. Hence $t\ge10$. Equality is feasible: ten students lose all three; among the other twenty, four keep only their pen, seven keep only their ruler, and nine keep only their pencil. This gives exactly the stated losses. Thus the minimum is $\boxed{10}$.
