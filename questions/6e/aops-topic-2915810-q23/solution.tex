Every American must pair with a non-American. Of 1008 non-Americans, exactly two pair with each other; after choosing that unlike-nationality pair, the remaining 1006 can be bijected with the Americans in $1006!$ ways. If other nationality sizes are $n_i$, the number of eligible pairs is $\dfrac{1008^2-\sum n_i^2}{2}$. The square sum is minimized when counts differ by at most 1, because moving one person from a count at least 2 larger to a smaller one decreases the sum. Since $1008=63(16)$, all counts are 16. This gives $\dfrac{1008\cdot992}{2}=499968$ eligible pairs. Hence the maximum is $\boxed{499968\cdot1006!}$. Also $499968=496(1007)+496$, so 1007 does not divide the coefficient.
