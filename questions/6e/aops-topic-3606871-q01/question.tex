A dish is filled to one of $1.5,2,2.5,3$ inches, chosen uniformly. At those heights the spill probabilities are respectively $0.60,0.65,0.70,0.75$. Conditional on spilling, choose an integer $K\ge1$ with $\Pr(K=k)=2^{-k}$ and lose the lesser of $0.5K$ inches and all the water. Find the probability that exactly $2$ inches remain.
