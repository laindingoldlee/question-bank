Only three cases work: start at $2$ inches and do not spill, start at $2.5$ and spill $0.5$, or start at $3$ and spill $1$. The probability is $\dfrac{1}{4}\left(0.35+0.70\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}+0.75\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}\right)=\boxed{\dfrac{71}{320}}$. Starting at $1.5$ cannot work.
