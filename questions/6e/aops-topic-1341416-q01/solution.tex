Two hours is $2\cdot60\cdot60=7200$ seconds. The train covers $1$ mile in each $75$-second interval, and $7200\div75=96$, so it travels $\boxed{96\text{ miles}}$. (Equivalently, its speed is $\dfrac{3600}{75}=48$ miles per hour, and $48\cdot2=96$.)
