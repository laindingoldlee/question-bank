Let $H=1+\dfrac12+\cdots+\dfrac1{2017}$. Then
\[2017\cdot2018\,(Y-X)=2018H-2017\left(H+\dfrac1{2018}\right)=H-\dfrac{2017}{2018}>0,\]
since $H\ge1$. So $\boxed{Y}$ is larger. (The average of $1,\dfrac12,\dots,\dfrac1n$ decreases as $n$ grows, because each new term is smaller than all previous ones.)
