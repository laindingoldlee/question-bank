Since $AB=BC=32\sqrt2$, the hypotenuse is $AC=32\sqrt2\cdot\sqrt2=64$ (a $45$-$45$-$90$ triangle), so the semicircle has radius $32$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0), A=(0,1), C=(1,0), O=(0.5,0.5);
real r=sqrt(2)/2;
fill(arc(O,r,-45,135)--cycle,paleblue);
draw(arc(O,r,-45,135),blue+linewidth(1));
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(1));
draw(B+(0.07,0)--B+(0.07,0.07)--B+(0,0.07));
dot(O,blue);

label("$32\sqrt2$",(A+B)/2,W,fontsize(8pt)); label("$32\sqrt2$",(B+C)/2,S,fontsize(8pt));
label("$64$",(A+C)/2+(-0.08,-0.08),fontsize(8pt));
draw(O--O+r*dir(45),blue+dashed); label("$32$",O+0.5*r*dir(45),NW,blue+fontsize(8pt));
label("$A$",A,W); label("$B$",B,SW); label("$C$",C,S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Its area is
\[\dfrac{1}{2}\pi\cdot32^2=\boxed{512\pi}.\]
