Subtracting the first equation from the third: $2ab+b^2=36$, i.e. $b(2a+b)=36$. The second equation is $h(2a+b)=80$. Dividing, $\dfrac {h}{b}=\dfrac{80}{36}=\dfrac{20}{9}$, so $h=\dfrac{20b}{9}$.

Write $b=9t$, $h=20t$. Then $9t(2a+9t)=36$ gives $2a=\dfrac{4}{t}-9t$, so $a=\dfrac{2}{t}-\dfrac{9t}{2}$. Substituting into $a^2+h^2=64$:
\[
\dfrac{4}{t^2}-18+\dfrac{81t^2}{4}+400t^2=64\iff\dfrac{4}{t^2}+\dfrac{1681t^2}{4}=82\iff\left(\dfrac{2}{t}-\dfrac{41t}{2}\right)^2=0,
\]
so $t^2=\dfrac{4}{41}$ and $t=\pm\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{41}}$. Then $a=\dfrac{2}{t}-\dfrac{9t}{2}=\dfrac{41t}{2}-\dfrac{9t}{2}=16t$. The solutions are
\[
\boxed{(a,b,h)=\pm\left(\dfrac{32}{\sqrt{41}},\dfrac{18}{\sqrt{41}},\dfrac{40}{\sqrt{41}}\right)=\pm\left(\dfrac{32\sqrt{41}}{41},\dfrac{18\sqrt{41}}{41},\dfrac{40\sqrt{41}}{41}\right)}.
\]
Check: $a^2+h^2=\dfrac{1024+1600}{41}=64$, $(2a+b)h=\dfrac{82\cdot40}{41}=80$, $(a+b)^2+h^2=\dfrac{2500+1600}{41}=100$.
