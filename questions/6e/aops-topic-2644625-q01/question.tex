$ABCD$ is a rhombus. A rectangle $A_1B_1C_1D_1$ is inscribed in $ABCD$ (one vertex on each side) so that its diagonals are parallel to sides of $ABCD$. Another rectangle $A_2B_2C_2D_2$ is inscribed in $ABCD$ so that its sides are parallel to the corresponding sides of $A_1B_1C_1D_1$ and its diagonals are perpendicular to sides of $ABCD$. If $A_1B_1=2$ and $A_2B_2=1$, find the area of $ABCD$.
