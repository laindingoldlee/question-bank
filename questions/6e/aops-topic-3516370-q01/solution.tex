Connect two digits when their difference is at least $2$. Their neighbour sets are $N(1)=\{3,4,5\}$, $N(2)=\{4,5\}$, $N(3)=\{1,5\}$, $N(4)=\{1,2\}$, and $N(5)=\{1,2,3\}$. Count paths of four distinct vertices by their first digit. Starting with $1$, the codes are $1352,1425,1524$, giving three. Starting with $2$, they are $2413,2415,2513,2514,2531$, giving five. Starting with $3$, they are $3142,3152,3514,3524$, giving four. Replacing each digit $d$ by $6-d$ gives equal counts for starts $5,4$ as for starts $1,2$. The total is $3+5+4+5+3=\boxed{20}$.
