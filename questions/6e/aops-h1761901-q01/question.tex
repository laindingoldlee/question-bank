Let $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1$ with $a>b>0$, and put $c=\sqrt{a^2-b^2}$. Let $F=(c,0)$ be a focus and $T$ a point on the ellipse lying on neither coordinate axis. Prove that the line through $F$ perpendicular to the tangent at $T$ meets line $OT$ on the directrix $x=\dfrac{a^2}{c}$, where $O$ is the center.
