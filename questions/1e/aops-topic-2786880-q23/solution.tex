Newton's sums give $ p_1=0 $ and $ p_{18}=18 $, since all intermediate coefficients vanish and the coefficient of $ x $ is $-1 $. For each root $ a $, $ a^{19}=a+2 $, so $ a^{37}=a^{18}(a+2)=a+2+2a^{18}$. Summing yields $ p_{37}=0+38+2\cdot18=\boxed{74}$.
