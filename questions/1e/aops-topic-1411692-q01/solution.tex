Rewrite the equation as $xy-5x-5y+25=25$, that is,
\[
(x-5)(y-5)=25 .
\]
So $x-5$ and $y-5$ are an ordered pair of integer divisors of $25$ with product $25$:
\[
(1,25),\ (5,5),\ (25,1),\ (-1,-25),\ (-5,-5),\ (-25,-1),
\]
giving $(x,y)=(6,30),(10,10),(30,6),(4,-20),(0,0),(-20,4)$. There are $\boxed{6}$ ordered pairs.
