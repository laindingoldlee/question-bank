Let $S=1+x+x^2+\dots+x^{n-1}$. Then $xS=x+x^2+\dots+x^n$, and subtracting,
\[
xS-S=x^n-1,
\]
because every other term cancels. Since $x\ne1$ we may divide by $x-1$ to get $S=\dfrac{x^n-1}{x-1}$. $\blacksquare$
