Let $b_n=a_n+a_{n-1}$ for $n\ge1$. The recurrence gives $b_n=b_{n-2}$, so every odd-indexed $b_n$ equals $b_1=31$. Group the desired sum into $1006$ pairs $(a_0+a_1),(a_2+a_3),\ldots,(a_{2010}+a_{2011})$. Each is $31$, giving $1006\cdot31=\boxed{31186}$.
