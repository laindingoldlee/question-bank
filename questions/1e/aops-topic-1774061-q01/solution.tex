The probability is the volume of the region of the unit cube where $x+y\le1$, $y+z\le1$, $z+x\le1$. Fix $x$; then $y,z\in[0,1-x]$ and $y+z\le1$.
\begin{itemize}
\item If $x\ge\dfrac{1}{2}$, then $y+z\le2(1-x)\le1$ automatically, and the cross-section has area $(1-x)^2$.
\item If $x<\dfrac{1}{2}$, the square $[0,1-x]^2$ loses the corner triangle where $y+z>1$, which has legs $2(1-x)-1=1-2x$; the area is $(1-x)^2-\dfrac{(1-2x)^2}{2}$.
\end{itemize}
Integrating,
\[
\int_0^1(1-x)^2\,dx-\int_0^{\dfrac{1}{2}}\dfrac{(1-2x)^2}{2}\,dx=\dfrac{1}{3}-\dfrac{1}{12}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{4}}.
\]
