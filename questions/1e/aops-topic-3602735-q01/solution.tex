The single-colour counts total $50+33+20=103$. The pairwise intersection counts total $\lfloor\dfrac{100}{6}\rfloor+\lfloor\dfrac{100}{10}\rfloor+\lfloor\dfrac{100}{15}\rfloor=16+10+6=32$, and the triple intersection has $3$ elements. Subtracting twice the pairwise total removes numbers counted twice, but leaves each triple counted $3-6=-3$ times, so add $3$ times the triple count. The result is $103-2(32)+3(3)=\boxed{48}$.
