It is true. The sum is $\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{k}r^{nj+m}$ (the exponents go down by $n$ from $nk+m$ to $m$). Multiplying,
\[\left(r^n-1\right)\sum_{j=0}^{k}r^{nj+m}=\sum_{j=0}^{k}\left(r^{n(j+1)+m}-r^{nj+m}\right),\]
which telescopes: the term $r^{n(j+1)+m}$ cancels with $-r^{n(j+1)+m}$ from the next value of $j$, leaving $r^{n(k+1)+m}-r^m$. $\blacksquare$

(The identity only makes sense when $k$ is a nonnegative integer, since $k+1$ is the number of terms; and $r>0$ is needed for real powers $r^{m}$ to be defined.)
