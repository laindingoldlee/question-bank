Prove or disprove: for every real number $r>0$, all real numbers $n,m$ and every nonnegative integer $k$,
\[\left(r^{nk+m}+r^{nk+m-n}+r^{nk+m-2n}+\cdots+r^m\right)\left(r^n-1\right)=r^{n(k+1)+m}-r^m.\]
