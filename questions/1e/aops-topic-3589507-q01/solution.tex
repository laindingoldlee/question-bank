The fraction liking at least one is $1-\dfrac{1}{6}=\dfrac{5}{6}$. Inclusion-exclusion gives the fraction liking both as $\dfrac{3}{4}+\dfrac{3}{5}-\dfrac{5}{6}=\dfrac{45+36-50}{60}=\boxed{\dfrac{31}{60}}$.
