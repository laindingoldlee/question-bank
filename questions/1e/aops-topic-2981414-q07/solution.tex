The line of sight is the hypotenuse of a right triangle whose vertical leg is the depth $d$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair S=(0,0),U=500*dir(-40);draw((-60,0)--(450,0));draw(S--U);draw(U--(U.x,0),dashed);dot(S);dot(U);
label("ship",S,NW);label("sub",U,S);label("$500$",U/2,SW);draw(arc(S,70,-40,0));label("$40^\circ$",S+70*dir(-20),E,fontsize(8pt));label("$d$",(U.x,U.y/2),E);
\end{asy}

$d=500\sin40^\circ\approx500\cdot0.6428\approx\boxed{321}$ m.
