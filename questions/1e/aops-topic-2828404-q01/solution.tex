Each topping is either on or off, so there are $2^9=512$ topping choices and $3\cdot512=\boxed{1536}$ pizzas.

Equivalently, $3\sum_{k=0}^{9}\binom9k=3\cdot2^9$. The student's error was counting ordered selections (such as $9\cdot8$ for two toppings) instead of combinations $\binom92=36$, and the case totals were then added inconsistently.
