A store owner bought $2500$ pens at $0.60$ dollars each. He can sell them in packs of five for $6$ dollars or in packs of nine for $11$ dollars, and he uses the option with the higher price per pen. How many packs must he sell for his profit (the money from sales minus the $1500$ dollars he paid for all $2500$ pens) to be at least $550$ dollars?
