Work backwards. After the third crossing Fox has $0$ coins, so just before paying he had $40$, and before the doubling $20$. Before the second crossing: $\dfrac{20+40}{2}=30$. Before the first crossing: $\dfrac{30+40}{2}=35$. (Check: $35\to70\to30\to60\to20\to40\to0$.) The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(C)}\ 35}$.
