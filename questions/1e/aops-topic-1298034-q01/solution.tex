By the self-similarity, $a=\sqrt{6+b}$ and $b=\sqrt{6-a}$, so $a^2=6+b$ and $b^2=6-a$. Subtracting, $a^2-b^2=a+b$, and since $a+b>0$, $a-b=1$. Then $a^2=6+a-1$, so $a^2-a-5=0$, $a=\dfrac{1+\sqrt{21}}{2}$ and $b=\dfrac{-1+\sqrt{21}}{2}$. Hence
\[
ab=\dfrac{21-1}{4}=\boxed{5}.
\]
