An integer divides both $a$ and $a-b$ exactly when it divides both $a$ and $b$, because $b=a-(a-b)$. Thus $\gcd(a,a-b)=\gcd(a,b)=\boxed{1}$. The gcd is taken positive even if $a-b$ is nonpositive.
