A reduced fraction terminates in base $12$ exactly when its denominator divides some power $12^k$. Since $720=2^4\cdot3^2\cdot5$, its denominator has a factor $5$, whereas $12^k$ has only prime factors $2,3$. Thus the expansion does $\boxed{\text{not terminate}}$.
