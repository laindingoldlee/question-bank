Amy, Barbara, Chris, David and Emily sit in a circle, in that order, saying the prime numbers in increasing order: Amy says $2$, Barbara says $3$, Chris says $5$, and so on around the circle. At some point, the person whose turn it is to speak next observes: ``This is the first time that the sum of the last four numbers said is a perfect square!'' Who made this observation?
