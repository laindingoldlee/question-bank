Let $F(S)$ count valid completions after the set $S$ has been used. The next unused value $a$ is legal exactly when $a<2+\sum_{s\in S}s$. Thus $F(S)=\sum F(S\cup\{a\})$ over such values, with $F(\{1,\ldots,9\})=1$. This recurrence counts each permutation once by its next element. Evaluating its 512 subset states gives $F(\varnothing)=\boxed{1080}$. The computation audit supplies the exact memoised recursion.
