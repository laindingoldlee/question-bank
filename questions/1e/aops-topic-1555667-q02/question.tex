A city has $n$ east-west streets and $n$ north-south streets, evenly spaced, forming a square grid with $n^2$ intersections and no traffic lights. One car is placed at one end of every street (so there are $2n$ cars, each at one of the two ends of its street, one unit outside the grid). All the cars start at the same moment and drive along their streets at the same constant speed. An accident happens if two cars are at the same intersection at the same time. In how many ways can the cars be placed so that no accident occurs? (For example, when $n=2$, one valid placement has a car at the north end of the westmost street, one at the south end of the eastmost street, one at the west end of the southmost street and one at the east end of the northmost street.)
