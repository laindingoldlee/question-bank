Four separate unit squares have total perimeter 16. Every shared edge removes 2 from the union perimeter. The $O$ piece has four shared edges and perimeter 8; every other tetromino has three shared edges and perimeter 10. Thus the sum is $8+6(10)=\boxed{68}$.
