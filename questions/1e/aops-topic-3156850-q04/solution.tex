If Carol's shadow has length $ s $, the pole's shadow has length $ s+10 $. Similar right triangles give $\dfrac{s+10}{s}=\dfrac{21}{5}$. Hence $ 16s=50 $ and $\boxed{s=\dfrac{25}{8}}$ feet.
