First, $x^2+3x+19=\left(x+\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^2+\dfrac{67}{4}>0$ for every $x$, so minimising its square is the same as minimising $x^2+3x+19$ itself. The quadratic is smallest at the integers nearest to $-\dfrac{3}{2}$, namely $x=-1$ and $x=-2$, which both give $1-3+19=4-6+19=17$. (Moving further from $-\dfrac{3}{2}$ only increases $\left(x+\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^2$.) Hence the least value is $17^2=\boxed{289}$.
