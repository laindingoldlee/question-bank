The boundary is a diamond with vertices $(8,-2),(2,4),(-4,-2),(2,-8)$. Its perpendicular diagonals both have length $12$. Splitting into four right triangles gives area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot12\cdot12=\boxed{72}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(8,-2),B=(2,4),C=(-4,-2),D=(2,-8); draw(A--B--C--D--cycle); draw(A--C,dashed); draw(B--D,dashed); label("$(8,-2)$",A,E); label("$(2,4)$",B,N); label("$(-4,-2)$",C,W); label("$(2,-8)$",D,S);
\end{asy}
