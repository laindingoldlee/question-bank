Write $N=10^nq+r$, where $0\le r<10^n$ is the last-$n$-digit integer. Since $10^n=2^n5^n$, we have $N\equiv r\pmod{2^n}$. Thus $N$ and $r$ are divisible by $2^n$ under exactly the same conditions.
