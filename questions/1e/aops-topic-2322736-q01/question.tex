The expression $|3|-5|7|$ is ambiguous, because a bar can open or close an absolute value. Evaluate each of the following readings:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $|3|-5\cdot|7|$;
\item[(b)] $|3|\cdot(-5)\cdot|7|$;
\item[(c)] $\bigl|3\cdot|-5|\cdot7\bigr|$.
\end{enumerate}
