\textbf{(a)} $3-5\cdot7=3-35=\boxed{-32}$.

\textbf{(b)} $3\cdot(-5)\cdot7=\boxed{-105}$.

\textbf{(c)} $|3\cdot5\cdot7|=\boxed{105}$.

The usual reading is (a), with the minus sign as subtraction and $5|7|$ meaning $5\cdot|7|$; writing the brackets or multiplication signs avoids the ambiguity.
