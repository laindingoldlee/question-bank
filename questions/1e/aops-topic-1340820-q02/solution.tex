This is a geometric series with first term $3^2$, ratio $3$ and $14$ terms. Using $S=\dfrac{(\text{last term})\cdot r-(\text{first term})}{r-1}$,
\[
3^2+3^3+\dots+3^{15}=\dfrac{3^{16}-3^2}{3-1}=\dfrac{43046721-9}{2}=\boxed{21523356}.
\]
