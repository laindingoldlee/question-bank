Triangle $ABC$ has $AB=13$, $BC=14$ and $AC=15$. Point $P$ is on $AC$ so that $BP$ bisects $\angle B$. The circle passing through $A$, $B$ and $P$ meets line $BC$ at $B$ and at another point $Q$. Find the perimeter of quadrilateral $ABQP$. Express your answer as a mixed number.
