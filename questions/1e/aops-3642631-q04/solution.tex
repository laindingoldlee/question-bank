If Mark is to guarantee six of the ten points, Lily must be unable to block any subset worth at least five. In particular the castles worth 1 and 4 must together receive at least 11 soldiers, and those worth 2 and 3 must together receive at least 11. Thus Mark needs at least 22. The allocation $5,5,6,6$ to castles worth 1,2,3,4 works: every subset worth at least five has total allocation at least 11. Lily’s ten soldiers cannot match Mark on such a subset, so he loses at most four points and gains at least six. The minimum is $\boxed{22}$.
