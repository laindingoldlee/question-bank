Write initial counts $3k,4k$. Then $3(3k+6)=4(4k-3)$, giving $9k+18=16k-12$, hence $k=\dfrac{30}{7}$. The formal initial total is $7k=30$, but the individual counts $\dfrac{90}{7}$ and $\dfrac{120}{7}$ are not integers. Thus $\boxed{\text{no actual choir satisfies these conditions}}$. The apparent answer $30$ must not be accepted without the integrality check.
