Write $m=gu,n=gv$ with $\gcd(u,v)=1$. Then $g(uv-1)=2023=7\cdot17^2$. For each divisor $d=uv-1$ of 2023, $g$ is fixed and $uv=d+1$. A coprime ordered factorization of an integer with $k$ distinct prime factors has $2^k$ choices, assigning each whole prime power to one factor. Here $d=1,7,17,119,289,2023$, so $d+1=2,8,18,120,290,2024$, giving counts 2,2,4,8,8,8. Their sum is $\boxed{32}$.
