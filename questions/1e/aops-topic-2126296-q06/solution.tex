The number of apples is a multiple of $2$, $3$ and $5$, hence of $\operatorname{lcm}(2,3,5)=30$, and it is less than $100$: so it is $30$, $60$ or $90$. It is not a multiple of $4$, which rules out $60$. The largest possibility is $\boxed{90}$.
