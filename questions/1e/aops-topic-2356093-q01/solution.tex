We need the number of solutions of $a+b+c=6$ in positive integers, where $a,b,c$ are the numbers of pieces the three friends get. Line up the $6$ pieces; there are $5$ gaps between them, and choosing $2$ of the gaps as dividers splits the pieces into three nonempty groups, in order. So there are $\dbinom52=\boxed{10}$ ways.
