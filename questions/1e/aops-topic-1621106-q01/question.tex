A square of area $108\text{ cm}^2$ and a circle have the same centre. Each corner of the square sticks out of the circle: along each side, the part of the side from a corner to the circle has length $\left(3\sqrt3-3\right)$ cm, as shown. The four shaded regions are the parts of the square outside the circle. What is the combined area of the shaded regions, in square centimetres?

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real h=3*sqrt(3);pair O=(0,0);
for(int k=0;k<4;++k){ fill(rotate(90*k)*((h,3)--(h,h)--(3,h)--arc(O,6,60,30)--cycle),lightgray); }
draw((-h,-h)--(h,-h)--(h,h)--(-h,h)--cycle,linewidth(0.9));draw(circle(O,6),linewidth(0.9));
label("$3\sqrt3-3$",((3+h)/2+0.5,h),N,fontsize(9pt));label("$3\sqrt3-3$",(h,(3+h)/2),E,fontsize(9pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
