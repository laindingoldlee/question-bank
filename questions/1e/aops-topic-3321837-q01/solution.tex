Each multiset of three digits from $0,1,\ldots,9$, other than $\{0,0,0\}$, has exactly one smallest valid three-digit arrangement: place its least nonzero digit first and arrange the others increasingly. Conversely each tiny number is this unique representative of its digit multiset. The number of multisets is $\binom{10+3-1}{3}=220$, so removing the all-zero case gives $\boxed{219}$.
