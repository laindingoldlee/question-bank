The unordered triples with product 36 and their sums are $(1,1,36):38$, $(1,2,18):21$, $(1,3,12):16$, $(1,4,9):14$, $(1,6,6):13$, $(2,2,9):13$, $(2,3,6):11$, $(3,3,4):10$. The date restriction discards 38 but does not affect the only repeated sum 13. Initial uncertainty therefore leaves $(1,6,6)$ and $(2,2,9)$. Only the latter has a unique oldest child, so the ages are $\boxed{2,2,9}$.
