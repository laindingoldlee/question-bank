Bag A contains $3$ white and $2$ red balls, and bag B contains $6$ white and $3$ red balls. One of the two bags is chosen at random (each with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$), and then two balls are drawn from that bag at random without replacement. What is the probability that the two balls have the same colour? Express your answer as a common fraction.

(A student computed $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{2}{5}+\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{3}{5}+\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}=1$. What went wrong?)
