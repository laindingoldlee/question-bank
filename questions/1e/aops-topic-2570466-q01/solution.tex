No old-design opening times are supplied, so there is no comparison baseline. Randomly assign comparable opening trials to the old and new designs, use the same opening instructions and timing procedure, and compare the mean opening times. A negative value of $\overline t_{\rm new}-\overline t_{\rm old}$ indicates faster opening in the observed new-design sample. Random assignment reduces systematic differences between the trial groups; sampling variability must still be considered before making a population claim.
