It is the geometric sum $1+10^6+10^{12}+\cdots+10^{6(m-1)}$, i.e. the number $\boxed{1\underbrace{000001\,000001\cdots000001}_{m-1\text{ blocks}}}$: a $1$ followed by $m-1$ blocks of ``$000001$''.
