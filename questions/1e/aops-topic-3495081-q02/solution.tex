Let the other vertices be $P,Q$. Since $OP=OQ$, and $x^2+\dfrac{x^4}{9}$ is strictly increasing in $|x|\ge0$, their abscissas have equal magnitude. They are distinct, so $P=(a,\dfrac{a^2}{3})$, $Q=(-a,\dfrac{a^2}{3})$ with $a>0$. Equality of $OP^2=a^2+\dfrac{a^4}{9}$ and $PQ^2=4a^2$ gives $a^2=27$. The base is $6\sqrt3$, the height is $9$, and the area is $\boxed{27\sqrt3}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
import graph;real f(real x){return x*x/3;}pair O=(0,0),P=(3sqrt(3),9),Q=(-3sqrt(3),9);draw(graph(f,-6,6),gray);draw(O--P--Q--cycle);draw(O--(0,9),dashed);label("$O$",O,S);label("$P$",P,E);label("$Q$",Q,W);label("$9$",(0,4.5),E);
\end{asy}
