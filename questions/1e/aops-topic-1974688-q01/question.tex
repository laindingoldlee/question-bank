Four prisoners $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ are buried up to their necks, all facing the same direction, and cannot move or turn their heads. A wall stands between $A$ and $B$: $A$ is alone on one side of it and sees nothing but the wall, and $B$, $C$, $D$ are lined up on the other side, so that $B$ sees only the wall, $C$ sees $B$, and $D$ sees $B$ and $C$. They know that there are exactly two white hats and two black hats among them, but nobody can see his own hat. They may not communicate. All four are freed if one of them correctly calls out the colour of his own hat within $10$ minutes (and a wrong call sends them all back). After a while, one prisoner calls out his colour and is right. Who was it, and how could he be sure? (Each prisoner reasons perfectly and knows the others do.)
