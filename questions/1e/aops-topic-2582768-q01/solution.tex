There are $\dbinom94=126$ equally likely sets of $4$ balls. Count the sets with exactly $2$, $3$ or $4$ white balls:
\[\dbinom42\dbinom52+\dbinom43\dbinom51+\dbinom44=60+20+1=81.\]
The probability is $\dfrac{81}{126}=\boxed{\dfrac{9}{14}}$.

(Check with the complement: $0$ or $1$ white ball gives $\dbinom54+\dbinom41\dbinom53=5+40=45$ sets, and $126-45=81$.)
