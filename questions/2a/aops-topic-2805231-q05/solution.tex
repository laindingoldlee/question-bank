Group the lines into classes of parallel lines with sizes $a_1,\dots,a_k$, $\sum a_i=9$. Two lines meet iff they are in different classes, and no three are concurrent, so the number of points is $\binom92-\sum\binom{a_i}2=36-\sum\binom{a_i}2$. We need $\sum\binom{a_i}2=19$.

If the largest class has $7$ or more lines, the sum is at least $21$. With largest class $6$ ($15$), the other $3$ lines give $0$, $1$ or $3$: totals $15,16,18$. With largest class $5$ ($10$), the other $4$ give at most $6$: at most $16$. With largest class at most $4$: at most $6+6+0=12$. So $19$ is impossible, and the answer is $\boxed{\text{no}}$.
