$n(n+5)=84$ gives $n^2+5n-84=(n+12)(n-7)=0$, so $n=7$ and the rectangles are $7\times10$, $7\times12$ and $7\times5$.

Two of them can share a full side only along a side of length $7$, giving a $7\times15$, $7\times17$ or $7\times22$ block; the third rectangle (sides $7$ and $12$, $10$ or $5$) cannot match the long side $15$, $17$ or $22$. So the three lie side by side, forming a $7\times27$ rectangle with perimeter $2(7+27)=\boxed{68\text{ inches}}$.
