Let the parallel bases have lengths $a,b$ and the height be $h$. Similarity gives distances from $E$ to the bases in ratio $a:b$, hence $p=\dfrac{a^2h}{2(a+b)}$ and $q=\dfrac{b^2h}{2(a+b)}$. Since $r=\dfrac{(a+b)h}{2}$, we obtain $r=(\sqrt p+\sqrt q)^2$. Thus $(r-p-q)^2=4pq$; expanding and rearranging gives $p^2+q^2+r^2=2(pq+qr+rp)$.
