Squaring a repunit adds shifted copies: $\underbrace{11\ldots1}_n{}^2=\sum_{i,j}10^{i+j}$, where the coefficient of $10^k$ counts the pairs $(i,j)$ with $0\le i,j\le n-1$ and $i+j=k$. For $n=8$ these counts are $1,2,\dots,8,7,\dots,1$, all single digits, so there are no carries:
\[
11111111^2=\boxed{123456787654321}.
\]
