Put $x=9z$ in the definition: $\dfrac x3=3z$, so
\[f(3z)=(9z)^2+9z+1=81z^2+9z+1.\]
The equation $f(3z)=7$ becomes $81z^2+9z-6=0$. Its discriminant $81+4\cdot81\cdot6$ is positive, so it has two real roots, and by Vieta's formulas their sum is $-\dfrac{9}{81}=-\dfrac19$. The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(B) }-\dfrac19}$.
