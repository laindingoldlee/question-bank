The $n$th number is $5n-4$, and $5n-4=12346$ gives $n=2470$. Page $p$ holds the numbers in positions $50(p-1)+1$ to $50p$. Since $2470=49\cdot50+20$, position $2470$ is the $20$th number on page $\boxed{50}$.
