In a fictional scoring system, each $3$-unit training block adds $6$ points to exam A, each $7$-unit block adds $10$ to exam B, and each $10$-unit block adds $1$ to exam C. Scores start at zero and have no caps. Blocks cannot be split. What minimum effort achieves at least $200$ combined points on A and B and at least $21$ on C?
