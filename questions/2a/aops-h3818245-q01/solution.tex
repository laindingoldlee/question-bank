Fix the first chosen edge. Of the five choices for the second edge, one is the same edge and restores all $5$ balls; two share one endpoint and produce a 3-cycle, leaving $2$ fixed balls; and two are disjoint, leaving $1$ fixed ball. Hence the expectation is
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{5+2\cdot2+2\cdot1}{5}=\dfrac{11}{5}}.
\]
