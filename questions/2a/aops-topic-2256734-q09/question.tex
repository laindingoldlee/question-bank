Bill and Rob count cars. Bill counts blue cars, Rob counts red cars, and they both count purple cars (each car has one colour). Of the $196$ cars that pass, $121$ are not blue, red or purple. If Bill counts $36$ cars and Rob counts $49$ cars, how many of the cars were purple?

$\textbf{(A) }10\qquad\textbf{(B) }13\qquad\textbf{(C) }15\qquad\textbf{(D) }16\qquad\textbf{(E) }18$
