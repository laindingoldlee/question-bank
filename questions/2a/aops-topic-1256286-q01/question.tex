A squaring trick for a number $x$: let $A=100(x-25)$ (that is, $x-25$ with two zeros appended) and $B=(50-x)^2$. The claim is that $x^2=A+B$; for example $43^2=1800+49=1849$. Prove that this works for every number $x$.
