Take X as the vector origin and let u be a unit vector along the line. The other intersections are at signed scalar positions $2O\cdot u$ and $2P\cdot u$, obtained by expanding the circle equations. Thus $AB=2|(O-P)\cdot u|\le2|O-P|$, with equality when the line is parallel to OP. Since the radii to X are perpendicular, $OP=\sqrt{18^2+14^2}=2\sqrt{130}$. The maximum is therefore $\boxed{4\sqrt{130}}$.
