The only increasing positive triples summing to 8 are $(1,2,5)$ and $(1,3,4)$. The first would force the remaining triple to be at least $(6,7,8)$, sum 21, impossible. Thus the first triple is $(1,3,4)$. The remaining distinct integers exceed 4 and sum 19. The smallest must be 5, since otherwise their sum is at least 21. The other two sum 14 and are distinct and above 5, forcing 6,8. The product is $1\cdot3\cdot4\cdot5\cdot6\cdot8=\boxed{2880}$.
