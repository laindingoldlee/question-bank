The average interior angle is $180^\circ-\dfrac{360^\circ}{n}$. For $n\ge10$ it is at least 144 degrees, making the condition impossible. For each $3\le n\le9$, the regular polygon has every angle below 144 degrees. Thus $\boxed{10}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
for(int k=0;k<10;++k)draw(2*dir(36*k)--2*dir(36*(k+1)));label("regular decagon: each angle 144 degrees",(0,-2.7));
\end{asy}
