Cauchy-Schwarz gives $(\sqrt x+\sqrt y)^2\le(2x+y)(\dfrac{1}{2}+1)=9 $. Positivity allows square roots, proving the bound. Equality requires $(\sqrt{2x},\sqrt y)$ proportional to $(\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2},1)$, so $ y=4x $. Together with $ 2x+y=6 $, this yields $\boxed{(x,y)=(1,4)}$.
