For $v\le2$, the total is at most $\dfrac{2+1}{6}=\dfrac{1}{2}$ mile. For $2<v\le3$, the three positive-speed intervals cover $\dfrac{v+(v-1)+(v-2)}{6}=\dfrac{v-1}{2}$ miles, at most one, with equality only at $v=3$. Thus the minimum is $\boxed3$ miles per hour.
