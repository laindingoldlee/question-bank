There is one one-digit number, $ 2 $, and one two-digit number, $ 22 $. Among three-digit numbers, either all digits are $ 2 $ or exactly two are. The exceptional digit has $ 8 $ choices in the hundreds position and $ 9 $ in either other position, giving $ 1+8+9+9=27 $. A four-digit number must have at least three twos. Those at most $ 2022 $ are exactly $ 1222,2022 $. Hence the rank is $ 1+1+27+2=\boxed{31}$.
