$H=\dfrac{1}{\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{1}{a}+\dfrac{1}{b}\right)}=\dfrac{2ab}{a+b}$. For $40$ and $60$: $H=\dfrac{2\cdot40\cdot60}{100}=\boxed{48}$. (The formula in the post has $\times$ in place of $+$.)
