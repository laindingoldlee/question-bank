Modulo five, each variable in $0,\ldots,11$ has two complete sets of residues plus two extra residues 0 and 1. For the weighted variable $ka$, the extra residues are 0 and $k$. Any product term using at least one complete residue set distributes its total uniformly over five residues. The all-extra term has 16 choices, and four sum to zero modulo five: subsets of $\{1,2,3,4\}$ with sums 0,5,10, namely the empty set, $\{1,4\},\{2,3\}$, and the full set. Therefore the count is $\dfrac{12^4-16}{5}+4=\boxed{4148}$.
