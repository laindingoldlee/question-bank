\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] The three altitudes of a triangle are in the ratio $21:28:60$, and its perimeter is $420$. What is the length of its shortest side?
\item[(b)] Show that no triangle has altitudes of lengths exactly $21$, $28$ and $60$ and perimeter $420$, by finding the perimeter of the triangle whose altitudes have lengths $21$, $28$ and $60$.
\end{enumerate}
