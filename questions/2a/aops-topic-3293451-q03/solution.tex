The all-positive sum is $55$. Negating a subset of $\{2,\ldots,10\}$ lowers the sum by twice its sum, so that subset must sum to $13$. The two-element possibilities are $\{3,10\},\{4,9\},\{5,8\},\{6,7\}$; the three-element possibilities are $\{2,3,8\},\{2,4,7\},\{2,5,6\},\{3,4,6\}$. No single element is $13$, and four elements sum to at least $2+3+4+5=14$. Thus $\boxed8$ choices work.
