The two numbers $b$ and $\dfrac1b$ have sum $b+\dfrac1b$ and product $1$, so
\[f(x)=(x-b)\left(x-\dfrac1b\right).\]
Hence $f(x)=0$ exactly for $\boxed{x=b\text{ or }x=\dfrac1b}$ (a double root when $b=\pm1$).
