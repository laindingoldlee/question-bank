The roots of $cx^2+bx+a$ are reciprocals of the original roots. Thus the original roots include $\dfrac{1}{2}$; write the other as $t\ne0$. The root sets are $\{\dfrac{1}{2},t\}$ and $\{2,\dfrac{1}{t}\}$. A common root requires either $t=2$ or $t=\dfrac{1}{t}$, the latter giving $t=\pm1$. Conversely all three values work. The possible square sums are $\dfrac{1}{4}+4=\boxed{\dfrac{17}{4}}$ and $\dfrac{1}{4}+1=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{4}}$.
