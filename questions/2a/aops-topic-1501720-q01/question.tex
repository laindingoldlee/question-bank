Let $ABC$ be a triangle, and let $P$, $Q$, $V$ be the midpoints of $AB$, $CA$, $BC$, respectively. Let $M$ be a point outside the triangle. The line through $M$ parallel to $BC$ meets line $AB$ at $E$, and the line through $M$ parallel to $AB$ meets line $BC$ at $F$. Suppose that lines $PF$ and $EV$ meet at a point $N$. Prove that $N$, $Q$ and $M$ are collinear.
