Classify by remainder $r\in\{1,\dots,12\}$ modulo $13$. Two numbers sum to a multiple of $13$ iff their remainders are $r$ and $13-r$ (two numbers with the same nonzero remainder $r$ sum to $2r\not\equiv0$). So from each of the six pairs $\{1,12\},\{2,11\},\{3,10\},\{4,9\},\{5,8\},\{6,7\}$ Larry may use numbers from only one class, and he may take all numbers of that class.

Since $100=7\cdot13+9$, remainders $1$ to $9$ occur $8$ times and $10,11,12$ occur $7$ times. Each pair contains a class of size $8$, so the maximum is $6\cdot8=\boxed{48}$ (e.g. all numbers with remainder $1,2,\dots,6$).
