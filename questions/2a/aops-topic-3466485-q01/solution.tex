A multiplier must supply exactly the primes with odd exponents in the number's prime factorisation. Thus $1001=7\cdot11\cdot13$ needs 1001; $250=2\cdot5^3$ needs 10; $320=2^6\cdot5$ needs 5; $252=2^2\cdot3^2\cdot7$ needs 7; and $7000=2^3\cdot5^3\cdot7$ needs 70. These give the minimal multipliers $\boxed{1001,10,5,7,70}$. A common multiplier is impossible: its exponent of 7 would need to be odd to pair with 1001, but even to pair with 250.
