Expand and regroup the terms by whether they contain $c$:
\[ax^3-a^2x^2-cx^2+abx+acx-bc=ax\left(x^2-ax+b\right)-c\left(x^2-ax+b\right).\]
Therefore
\[ax^{3}-(a^{2}+c)x^{2}+a(b+c)x-bc=\boxed{(ax-c)(x^2-ax+b)}.\]
