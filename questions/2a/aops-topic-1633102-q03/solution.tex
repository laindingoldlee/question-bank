Write both with exponents that are multiples of $9$:
\[
9^{18}=3^{36}=\left(3^4\right)^9=81^9,\qquad8^{21}=2^{63}=\left(2^7\right)^9=128^9 .
\]
Since $128>81$, $\boxed{8^{21}}$ is greater.
