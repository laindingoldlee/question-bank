Call a positive integer \emph{nice} if its prime factorisation contains only $2$s, $3$s and/or $7$s (for example $1$, $8$, $42$ and $2016$ are nice). The sum of the reciprocals of all nice numbers that are not perfect squares can be written as $\dfrac {a}{b}$ in lowest terms. Find $ab$.
