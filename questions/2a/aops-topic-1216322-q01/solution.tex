Every nice number is $2^x3^y7^z$ with $x,y,z\ge0$, so the sum of the reciprocals of all nice numbers factors as a product of geometric series:
\[\left(\sum_{x\ge0}\dfrac{1}{2^x}\right)\left(\sum_{y\ge0}\dfrac{1}{3^y}\right)\left(\sum_{z\ge0}\dfrac{1}{7^z}\right)=2\cdot\dfrac{3}{2}\cdot\dfrac{7}{6}=\dfrac{7}{2}.\]
The square ones have all exponents even, and the same argument gives
\[\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{1}{4}}\cdot\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{1}{9}}\cdot\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{1}{49}}=\dfrac{4}{3}\cdot\dfrac{9}{8}\cdot\dfrac{49}{48}=\dfrac{49}{32}.\]
So the requested sum is $\dfrac{7}{2}-\dfrac{49}{32}=\dfrac{63}{32}$, and $ab=63\cdot32=\boxed{2016}$.
