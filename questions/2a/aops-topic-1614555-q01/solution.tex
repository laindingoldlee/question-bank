The equation $x^2+y^2=25$ says that the distance from $(x,y)$ to the origin is $\sqrt{25}=5$, so it describes the circle with centre $O=(0,0)$ and radius $5$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((-6.5,0)--(6.5,0),gray,Arrow(TeXHead));draw((0,-6.5)--(0,6.5),gray,Arrow(TeXHead));
label("$x$",(6.5,0),E,gray);label("$y$",(0,6.5),N,gray);
fill(circle((0,0),5),rgb(0.88,0.92,1));draw(circle((0,0),5),linewidth(0.9));
draw((0,0)--5*dir(35),blue);label("$5$",2.5*dir(35),NW,blue);
dot((0,0));label("$O$",(0,0),SW);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Its area is $\pi\cdot5^2=\boxed{25\pi}$ square units.
