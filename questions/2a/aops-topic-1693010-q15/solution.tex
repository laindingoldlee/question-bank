Taking $\log_3$ of both sides, $3^a>2^{102}$ is equivalent to $a>102\log_32=102\cdot0.631=64.362$. The smallest such integer is $\boxed{65}$. (With the more precise $\log_32=0.6309\ldots$, $102\log_32=64.35\ldots$, so the answer is the same.)
