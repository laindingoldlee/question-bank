Two weighted coins are given at random to two people, one coin each. For one coin, the probabilities of heads and tails are in the ratio $1:5$; for the other, $4:5$. A player picks one of the two people at random and flips that person's coin. If it shows heads, the player wins and pays nothing; if it shows tails, the player loses \$360 to the two people. What is the expected amount of money the two people make from one game?
