The first coin shows heads with probability $\dfrac16$ and the second with probability $\dfrac49$. The player flips each coin with probability $\dfrac12$, so the probability of heads is
\[\dfrac12\left(\dfrac16+\dfrac49\right)=\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac{11}{18}=\dfrac{11}{36},\]
and the probability of tails is $\dfrac{25}{36}$. The expected amount the two people make is $\dfrac{25}{36}\cdot360=\boxed{\$250}$.
