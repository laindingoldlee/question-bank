Square $ABCD$ has side length $12$, with $A$ at the top left, $B$ at the top right, $C$ at the bottom right and $D$ at the bottom left. The square is folded along a straight crease so that corner $A$ lands on the midpoint $M$ of the bottom side $CD$. The crease runs from a point $P$ on side $AD$ to a point $Q$ on side $BC$, and the part $ABQP$ of the square that is folded over is a trapezoid. What is the area of this trapezoid?
