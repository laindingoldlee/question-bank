The Euclidean algorithm gives $\gcd(F_{n+1},F_n)=\gcd(F_n,F_{n+1}-F_n)=\gcd(F_n,F_{n-1})$. Repeating ends at $\gcd(F_2,F_1)=\gcd(1,1)=1$. The initial pair is covered directly.
