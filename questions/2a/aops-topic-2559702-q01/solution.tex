\emph{Upper bound.} Split $1,\dots,160$ into the $80$ pairs $\{8k+j,\ 8k+j+4\}$ for $k=0,1,\dots,19$ and $j=1,2,3,4$. The two numbers in each pair differ by $4$, so at most one of them can be chosen: at most $80$ numbers.

\emph{Construction.} Choose all numbers of the form $8k+j$ with $j\in\{1,2,3,4\}$; there are $80$ of them. If two chosen numbers differed by $4$, the larger would have remainder $5$, $6$, $7$ or $0$ when divided by $8$, which is not chosen.

The answer is $\boxed{80}$.
