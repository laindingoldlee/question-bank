Mercedes bought some vanilla cupcakes and some chocolate cupcakes. If she eats one of the vanilla cupcakes, then $\dfrac{1}{7}$ of the remaining cupcakes will be vanilla. If she instead eats two of the chocolate cupcakes, then $\dfrac{1}{5}$ of the remaining cupcakes will be vanilla. How many cupcakes did Mercedes buy?
