Because 2020 is a leap year, the period contains $366$ days; number them $d=1,2,\dots,366$. The three calls take place on the days with $7\mid d$, $6\mid d$ and $4\mid d$ respectively. Let $N(k)=\left\lfloor\dfrac{366}{k}\right\rfloor$ be the number of multiples of $k$ among the days.

Two given calls take place on the same day exactly when $d$ is a multiple of the lcm of their periods: $\operatorname{lcm}(7,6)=42$, $\operatorname{lcm}(7,4)=28$, $\operatorname{lcm}(6,4)=12$, and all three happen when $d$ is a multiple of $\operatorname{lcm}(7,6,4)=84$.

In $N(42)+N(28)+N(12)$, a day with exactly two calls is counted once, and a day with all three calls is counted three times. Subtracting $2N(84)$ corrects this, so the number of days is
\[N(42)+N(28)+N(12)-2N(84)=8+13+30-2\cdot4=\boxed{43}.\]
