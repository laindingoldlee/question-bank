Ella calls Mickey every $7$ days, Jenna calls Mickey every $6$ days, and Ella calls Jenna every $4$ days. On August 7, 2019, all three of these calls took place. On how many of the days from August 8, 2019 to August 7, 2020 (inclusive) do at least two of the three calls take place?
