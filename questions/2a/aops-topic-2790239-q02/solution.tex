At any moment there are $\dfrac{120}{10}=12$ trains going one way, equally spaced, and $\dfrac{120}{15}=8$ going the other way. During one circuit ($2$ hours) a train and an oncoming train together cover two full loops relative to each other, so it meets each oncoming train twice. A train in the $10$-minute direction meets $2\cdot8=\boxed{16}$ oncoming trains, and a train in the $15$-minute direction meets $2\cdot12=\boxed{24}$.
