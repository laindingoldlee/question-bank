With the mother's share $m$: the boys get $2m$ each and the girl $3m$, so $m+2m+2m+3m=8m=1$. The mother gets $\boxed{\dfrac18}$, each boy $\boxed{\dfrac14}$, and the girl $\boxed{\dfrac38}$.
