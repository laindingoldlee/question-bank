The denominator is $\dfrac{k(k+1)(k+2)}{6}$, so each term is $3[\dfrac{1}{k(k+1)}-\dfrac{1}{(k+1)(k+2)}]$. Telescoping gives $S=3[\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{2025\cdot2026}]$. Hence $\dfrac{1}{b}-\dfrac{1}{a}=\dfrac{1}{M}$, where $M=2025\cdot2026$. Therefore $a>b$ and $ab=M(a-b)\ge M$. By AM--GM, $a+b\ge2\sqrt M>4050$, so the integer sum is at least $4051$. The choice $a=2026,b=2025$ attains it. Thus $\boxed{4051}$.
