All $-100\le x\le0$ work, since the left side is nonnegative and the right nonpositive. Positive values 1 and 2 also work. At $x=3$ the cubic exceeds the square, and the difference $x^3-(x+1)^2$ strictly increases for integers $x\ge3$, since its successive difference is $3x^2+x-2>0$. Thus the count is $101+2=\boxed{103}$.
