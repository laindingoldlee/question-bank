The missing $z^8$ coefficient gives $\sum c_k=0$ by Vieta. Expand:
\[
S(z)=9|z|^2+\sum_{k=1}^9|c_k|^2
-2\operatorname{Re}\left(z\sum_{k=1}^9\overline{c_k}\right).
\]
The final term is zero. Hence on the stated circle,
\[
\boxed{S(z)=90000+\sum_{k=1}^9|c_k|^2},
\]
a quantity independent of $z$. The individual roots need not be found.
