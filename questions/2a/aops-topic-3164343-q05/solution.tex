Let the counts of 1, 2, 3, 4 be $a,b,c,d$. The probability condition gives $5a+4b+3c+2d=18$, and $a+b+c+d=6$. Subtracting twice the latter gives $3a+2b+c=6$. If $a=0$, $b=0,1,2,3$ each works; if $a=1$, $b=0,1$ work; if $a=2$, only $b=0$ works. The determined $c,d$ are nonnegative in all seven cases. Thus $\boxed7$.
