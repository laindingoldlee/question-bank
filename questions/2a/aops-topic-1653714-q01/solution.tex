The even integers from $1$ to $100$ are $2,4,\dots,100$, with sum $2(1+2+\dots+50)=2\cdot1275=2550$.

The even multiples of $3$ are the multiples of $6$: $6,12,\dots,96$, with sum $6(1+2+\dots+16)=6\cdot136=816$.

The required sum is $2550-816=\boxed{1734}$.
