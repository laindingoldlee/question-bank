A circle with centre at the origin has a diameter of length $8$. The line $14x+3y=7$ meets the circle at $C$ and $D$. The line $y=x$, translated $1$ unit to the right, meets the circle at $E$ and $F$. Find the area of the convex quadrilateral whose vertices are $C$, $D$, $E$, $F$.
