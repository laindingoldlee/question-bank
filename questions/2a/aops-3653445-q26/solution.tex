Count side triples $1\le a\le b\le c\le2024$. The triangle inequality requires $a\ge c-b+1$; obtuseness requires $a^2+b^2<c^2$, or $a\le\lfloor\sqrt{c^2-b^2-1}\rfloor$. Thus for each $b<c$ the number is $\max(0,\min(b,\lfloor\sqrt{c^2-b^2-1}\rfloor)-c+b)$. Sum this expression over $1\le b<c\le2024$ to obtain $\boxed{394363287}$. Every integer-sided triangle is counted exactly once by sorting its sides; strictness is handled by the minus one under the square root.
