Represent each day by its 31-bit vector of participating jugglers. Removing coordinate j causes a collision exactly when two day vectors differ only in coordinate j. If every j were bad, choose one such pair for each of the 31 coordinates. These are 31 distinct edges on 31 day vertices, so they contain a cycle. Traversing a cycle flips each coordinate an even number of times to return to the starting vector. But the selected edges have distinct coordinate labels, making that impossible. Thus at least one juggler can be removed without identifying any two days, and the schedule stays joyful.
