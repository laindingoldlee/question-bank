$\dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3=27\pi$ gives $r^3=\dfrac{81}{4}$. The largest cube has its space diagonal equal to the diameter: $s\sqrt3=2r$, so
\[
V=s^3=\dfrac{8r^3}{3\sqrt3}=\dfrac{8\cdot\dfrac{81}{4}}{3\sqrt3}=\dfrac{54}{\sqrt3}=\boxed{18\sqrt3}\text{ cubic inches}.
\]
