$2^{2n}-1=4^n-1=(4-1)\left(4^{n-1}+4^{n-2}+\cdots+4+1\right)=3\left(4^{n-1}+\cdots+1\right)$, which is a multiple of $3$. (Equivalently, $4\equiv1\pmod3$, so $4^n\equiv1\pmod3$.) $\blacksquare$
