He needs at least $2$ heads. Of the $16$ equally likely outcomes, $\dbinom42+\dbinom43+\dbinom44=6+4+1=11$ have at least $2$ heads. The probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{11}{16}}$, answer $\textbf{(E)}$.
