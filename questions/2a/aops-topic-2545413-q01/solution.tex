Let $u=\sqrt{71-x}$ and $v=\sqrt{3x-17}$; then $3u^2+v^2=196$. By the Cauchy--Schwarz inequality,
\[(u+v)^2=\left(\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}\cdot\sqrt3u+1\cdot v\right)^2\le\left(\dfrac13+1\right)\left(3u^2+v^2\right)=\dfrac{784}{3},\]
so $u+v\le\dfrac{28}{\sqrt3}\approx16.17$, with equality when $\sqrt3u:v=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}:1$, i.e. $v=3u$, which gives $u^2=\dfrac{196}{12}$ and an admissible $x$. Since the maximum $16.17$ lies between $16$ and $17$, the largest value of the floor is $\boxed{16}$.
