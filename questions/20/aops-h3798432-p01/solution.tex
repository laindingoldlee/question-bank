The diagonal summing to seven must use $1,2,4$. Let the centre be c. The other diagonal has two distinct other digits at most nine and eight, so $21-c\le17$, giving $c\ge4$. Hence c is four. Adding the two diagonal sums counts the four corners once and the centre twice, so the corner sum is $7+21-8=20$. The five non-corner cells therefore sum to $45-20=\boxed{25}$. The displayed grid verifies feasibility.
