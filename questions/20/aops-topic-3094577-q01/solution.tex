Telescoping gives $x^n-y^n=(x-y)(x^{n-1}+x^{n-2}y+\cdots+y^{n-1})$. If $n=ab$ with $a,b>1$, then $2^n-1=(2^a-1)(1+2^a+\cdots+2^{a(b-1)})$. Both factors exceed 1, proving compositeness.
