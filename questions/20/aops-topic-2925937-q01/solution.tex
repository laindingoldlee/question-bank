At height fraction $t\in(0,1)$ the vines are at angles $5t$ and $-3t$ turns. They cross when the difference $8t$ is a whole number of turns, i.e. $t=\dfrac k8$ with $k=1,\dots,7$. So they cross $\boxed{7}$ times.
