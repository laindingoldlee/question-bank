Write the angles as $B-d$, $B$, $B+d$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(4,0),C=(1.2,2.6); draw(A--B--C--cycle); label("$A$",A,SW); label("$B$",B,SE); label("$C$",C,N);
label("$60^\circ-d$",(0.95,0.3),fontsize(7)); label("$60^\circ$",(3.25,0.2),fontsize(7)); label("$60^\circ+d$",(1.45,1.95),fontsize(7));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Their sum is $3B=180^\circ$, so $\angle B=\boxed{60^\circ}$.
