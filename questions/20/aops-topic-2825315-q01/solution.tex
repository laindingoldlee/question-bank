(a) This counts surjections from $6$ cubes onto $3$ shelves. By inclusion-exclusion, $3^6-3\cdot2^6+3\cdot1^6=729-192+3=\boxed{540}$.

(b) Arrange the $6$ cubes in a row ($6!$ ways) and cut the row into $3$ nonempty consecutive groups by choosing $2$ of the $5$ gaps: $720\cdot\binom52=\boxed{7200}$.

(Plain stars and bars fails because the cubes are distinct.)
