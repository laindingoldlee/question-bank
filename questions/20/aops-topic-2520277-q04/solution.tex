Fix $a_3=c$ and $a_2=b$ with $b<c$. Then $a_1$ must satisfy $c-b<a_1<b$, which allows $2b-c-1$ values when this is positive. Summing over $b$ for each $c$ from $3$ to $9$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $c=3$: $b=2$ gives $0$; total $0$.
\item $c=4$: $b=3$ gives $1$; total $1$.
\item $c=5$: $b=4$ gives $2$; total $2$.
\item $c=6$: $b=4,5$ give $1+3=4$.
\item $c=7$: $b=5,6$ give $2+4=6$.
\item $c=8$: $b=5,6,7$ give $1+3+5=9$.
\item $c=9$: $b=6,7,8$ give $2+4+6=12$.
\end{itemize}
The total is $0+1+2+4+6+9+12=\boxed{34}$. (These are the triangles with three different integer side lengths less than $10$.)
