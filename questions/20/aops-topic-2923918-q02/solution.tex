Let $\alpha=\arcsin\dfrac45$, so $\cos\alpha=\dfrac35$, and $\beta=\arctan\dfrac5{12}$, so $\sin\beta=\dfrac5{13}$ and $\cos\beta=\dfrac{12}{13}$. Then
\[\cos(\alpha+\beta)=\dfrac35\cdot\dfrac{12}{13}-\dfrac45\cdot\dfrac5{13}=\boxed{\dfrac{16}{65}}.\]
