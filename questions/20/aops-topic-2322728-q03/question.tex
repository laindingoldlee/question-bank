A $3\times3$ magic square uses each of the numbers $1,2,\dots,9$ once, and every row, every column and both diagonals have the same sum. You choose one of all such magic squares at random, all equally likely. Your teacher accepts it only if the top middle square is neither $9$ nor $3$. What is the probability that your square is accepted?
