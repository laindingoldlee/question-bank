An integer $x_0$ (positive, negative or zero) is chosen. Then $2$ is added and the sum is divided by $2$, and this procedure is repeated forever: $x_{n+1}=\dfrac{x_n+2}{2}$. Show that, whatever $x_0$ is, the numbers $x_n$ approach a single value, and find it.
