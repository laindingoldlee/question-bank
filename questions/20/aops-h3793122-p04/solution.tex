The three corner triangles are similar to the original triangle. Their altitude ratios sum to one, because the parallels through the interior point partition the corresponding altitude fractions. If the original area is $K$, then $\sqrt{\dfrac{2}{K}}+\sqrt{\dfrac{8}{K}}+\sqrt{\dfrac{18}{K}}=1$. Hence $\sqrt K=\sqrt2+\sqrt8+\sqrt{18}=6\sqrt2$, and $\boxed{K=72}$.
