The units digits of powers of $2$, $3$ and $7$ repeat with period $4$: $2,4,8,6$; $3,9,7,1$; $7,9,3,1$. Since $222\equiv2$, $333\equiv1$ and $777\equiv1\pmod4$, the units digits are $4$, $3$ and $7$; and $5^{555}$ ends in $5$. The sum ends in the units digit of $4+3+5+7=19$, i.e. $\boxed{9}$.
