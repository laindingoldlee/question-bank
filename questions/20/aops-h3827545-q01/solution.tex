Let $S,T,D$ be the start, turning point, and destination. In triangle $STD$, the angle at $S$ is $\theta$. The interior angle at $T$ is $180^\circ-\alpha$, so the angle at $D$ is $\alpha-\theta$. The sine rule gives
\[
\dfrac{ST}{\sin(\alpha-\theta)}
=\dfrac{SD}{\sin(180^\circ-\alpha)}
=\dfrac{1200}{\sin\alpha}.
\]
Thus the first leg has length
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{1200\sin(\alpha-\theta)}{\sin\alpha}\text{ miles}}.
\]

\end{asy}
