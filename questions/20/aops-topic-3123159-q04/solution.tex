The difference is $99(a-c)$. As $99\equiv1\pmod7$, we require $7\mid a-c$. Among $a,c\in\{1,\ldots,9\}$, there are nine equal pairs and four unequal pairs $(1,8),(8,1),(2,9),(9,2)$. The middle digit has ten choices, so the answer is $13\cdot10=\boxed{130}$.
