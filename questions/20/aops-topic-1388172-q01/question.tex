Twenty-five people, Mr.\ A, Mr.\ B, \dots, Mr.\ Y, have $2,3,\dots,26$ animals respectively (Mr.\ A has $2$, Mr.\ B has $3$, and so on up to Mr.\ Y with $26$). Some of each person's animals are cats: a person with $n$ animals has $c$ cats, where $1\le c<n$. Find the sum of all the possible values of the total number of cats owned by the $25$ people.
