Each person has at least $1$ cat, so the total is at least $25$. A person with $n$ animals has at most $n-1$ cats, so the total is at most
\[
\sum_{n=2}^{26}(n-1)=1+2+\dots+25=325 .
\]
Every integer from $25$ to $325$ occurs: starting from one cat each, we can raise one person's count by $1$ at a time (until each has the maximum), and each step raises the total by $1$. So the possible totals are exactly $25,26,\dots,325$, and their sum is
\[
\dfrac{(25+325)\cdot301}{2}=\boxed{52675}.
\]
