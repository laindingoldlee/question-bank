The subexpression $\dfrac{0}{0}$ is undefined: every real q satisfies $0q=0$, so there is no unique quotient. An arithmetic expression containing that undefined subtraction has no real value, regardless of the other finite terms. Thus the expression is $\boxed{\text{undefined}}$.
