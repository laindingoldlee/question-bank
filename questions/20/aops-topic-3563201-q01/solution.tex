Triangle $POQ$ is isosceles and its angle at $O$ is $78^\circ$, so $\angle PQO=\dfrac{180-78}{2}=\boxed{51^\circ}$. The arc from $P$ through $Q$ to $R$ has measure $201^\circ$. The other arc, and hence the smaller central angle, is $360-201=\boxed{159^\circ}$. 
\begin{asy}
size(7cm);
pair O=(0,0),P=dir(0),Q=dir(78),R=dir(201);draw(circle(O,1));draw(P--O--Q--P);draw(O--R);label("$O$",O,SE);label("$P$",P,E);label("$Q$",Q,N);label("$R$",R,SW);label("$78^\circ$",1.2dir(39));label("$123^\circ$",1.3dir(139.5));
\end{asy}
