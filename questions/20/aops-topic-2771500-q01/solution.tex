After $ j $ moves the entry is $\binom{j+2}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{(j+2)(j+1)}{2}}$. It is finite for every nonnegative integer $ j $, though it grows without bound as $ j $ increases. Infinity is not an entry or a row reached at a finite step.
