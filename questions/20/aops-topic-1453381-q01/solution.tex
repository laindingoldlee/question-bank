Adding the increases,
\[
f(n)=1+3(1+2+\dots+(n-1))=1+3\cdot\dfrac{(n-1)n}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{3n^2-3n+2}{2}} .
\]
Check: $f(1)=1$, $f(2)=4$, $f(3)=10$, $f(4)=19$, and $f(n+1)-f(n)=\dfrac{3(2n)}{2}=3n$.
