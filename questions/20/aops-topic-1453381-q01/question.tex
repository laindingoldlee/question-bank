A function $f$ on the positive integers satisfies $f(1)=1$, $f(2)=4$, $f(3)=10$, $f(4)=19$, and in general the increase from $f(n)$ to $f(n+1)$ is $3n$ (so the increases are $3,6,9,12,\dots$). Find a formula for $f(n)$.
