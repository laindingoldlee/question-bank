There are $3^3=27$ equally likely ordered outcomes. The largest possible sum is $9$, so use the complement: a sum of $9$ comes only from $(3,3,3)$, and a sum of $8$ comes from $3,3,2$ in some order ($3$ ways). So $4$ outcomes have sum at least $8$, and
\[
P(\text{sum}<8)=1-\dfrac{4}{27}=\boxed{\dfrac{23}{27}}.
\]
