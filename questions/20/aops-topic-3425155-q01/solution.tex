Put $F=ab-a-b$. If $F=ax+by$, then $ab=a(x+1)+b(y+1)$. Reducing modulo $a$ and using coprimality gives $a\mid y+1$, so $y+1\ge a$. The right side is then at least $a+ab>ab$, a contradiction. Now take any $n>F$. The residues of $0,b,2b,\ldots,(a-1)b$ modulo $a$ are all distinct, so choose $y\in\{0,\ldots,a-1\}$ with $by\equiv n\pmod a$. Let $x=\dfrac{n-by}{a}$, an integer. Since $n-by>ab-a-b-b(a-1)=-a$, we have $x>-1$, hence $x\ge0$. This represents every $n>F$, proving the result.
