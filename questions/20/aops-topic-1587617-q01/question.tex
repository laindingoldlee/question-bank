Aunt Polly bakes muffins at a constant rate of $4$ muffins per minute. Tom comes into the kitchen when there are $12$ muffins on the table and begins to eat them at a constant rate of $6$ muffins per minute, while Aunt Polly keeps baking. When he leaves the kitchen, there are $4$ muffins on the table. How long did Tom stay in the kitchen?
