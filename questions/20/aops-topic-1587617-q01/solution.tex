While Tom is in the kitchen the number of muffins changes by $4-6=-2$ per minute. It falls from $12$ to $4$, a decrease of $8$, which takes
\[
\dfrac{8}{2}=\boxed{4\text{ minutes}}.
\]
(Check: in $4$ minutes Aunt Polly bakes $16$ muffins and Tom eats $24$, and $12+16-24=4$.)
