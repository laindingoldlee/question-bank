$100$ cats kill $100$ mice in $100$ days, so in $100$ days each cat kills $1$ mouse; the rate is $\dfrac1{100}$ mouse per cat per day. Then $4$ cats kill $4\cdot\dfrac1{100}=\dfrac{1}{25}$ mouse per day, and $4$ mice take
\[\dfrac{4}{\dfrac1{25}}=\boxed{100}\text{ days}.\]
(The tempting answer $4$ days is wrong: fewer cats work more slowly in total, and fewer mice mean less work; the two changes cancel.)
