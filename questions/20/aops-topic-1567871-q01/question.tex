\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that if $a\equiv b\pmod m$ and $p\equiv q\pmod m$, then $a+p\equiv b+q\pmod m$.
\item[(b)] A student tests this with $7\equiv2\pmod5$ and $8\equiv3\pmod5$. He computes $7+2=9\equiv4$ and $8+3=11\equiv1\pmod5$, gets different answers, and concludes that the property fails. Find the error and check the property correctly with these numbers.
\end{enumerate}
