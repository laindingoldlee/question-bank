An ant starts at the bottom of a unit circle tangent to the ground. Each turn it travels clockwise a random arc length uniformly in $[\dfrac{2\pi}{3},\dfrac{4\pi}{3}]$, then the whole circle rotates counterclockwise through $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$. Its two random choices are independent. Apart from its starting instant, touching the bottom point at any time crushes the ant. What is the probability it survives two turns?
