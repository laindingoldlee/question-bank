A deck has six suspects, six weapons and nine rooms. Hide one uniformly chosen card of each category. Then add one duplicate of a fixed suspect, one duplicate of a fixed weapon and one duplicate of a fixed room. Deal the resulting twenty-one physical cards uniformly into three labelled hands of seven. What is the probability that at least one of two specified players receives two cards with the same identity?
