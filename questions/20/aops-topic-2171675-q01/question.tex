In triangle $ABC$, points $E$ and $M$ lie on $AB$, points $G$ and $F$ lie on $BC$, and points $H$ and $N$ lie on $AC$, such that $MN\parallel BC$, $EF\parallel AC$ and $GH\parallel AB$, and the segments $EF$, $GH$, $MN$ all pass through a common point $O$ inside the triangle. Given that $[MOE]=4$, $[NOH]=9$ and $[GOF]=49$, find $[ABC]$. (Here $[XYZ]$ denotes the area of triangle $XYZ$.)
