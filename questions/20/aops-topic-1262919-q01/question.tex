Prove the inclusion--exclusion formula for three finite sets:
\[|A\cup B\cup C|=|A|+|B|+|C|-|A\cap B|-|B\cap C|-|C\cap A|+|A\cap B\cap C|,\]
by checking how many times each element of $A\cup B\cup C$ is counted on the right-hand side.
