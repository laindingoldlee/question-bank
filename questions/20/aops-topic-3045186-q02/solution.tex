(a) On the unit circle cosine is the horizontal coordinate and sine the vertical coordinate. Thus sine is positive in quadrants I,II; cosine in I,IV; and their ratio tangent in I,III. (b) The $30$--$60$--$90$ and isosceles right triangles give $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2},1}$, respectively. (c) Multiply degrees by $\dfrac{\pi}{180}$, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{\pi}{4},\dfrac{\pi}{2},\pi,2\pi}$.
