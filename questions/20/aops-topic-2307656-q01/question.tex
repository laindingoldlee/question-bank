A student is told that $0.\overline{9}\ne0.\overline{99}$, because $0.\overline{9}$ has ``an infinite number of $9$s'' while $0.\overline{99}$ has ``an even infinite number of $9$s''. Show that $0.\overline{9}=0.\overline{99}$ and find their common value.
