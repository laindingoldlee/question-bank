The equation gives $(\sqrt{x}-\sqrt{y})^2=8$ and $\sqrt{xy}=\dfrac{x+y-8}{2}$. A rational square root of an integer is an integer, so $xy$ is a square. Write $x=da^2,y=db^2$ for a common squarefree positive integer $d$ and integers $a>b>0$. Then $d(a-b)^2=8$, forcing $d=2$ and $a-b=2$. Since $x=2(b+2)^2<100$, we have $b=1,2,3,4,5$. The resulting pairs $(x,y)$ are $(18,2),(32,8),(50,18),(72,32),(98,50)$. Their distinct sums are $20,40,68,104,148$, totalling $\boxed{380}$.
