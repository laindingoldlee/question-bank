Put $AB=AC=s$ and $EF=t$. Since the small triangles are equilateral, $DE=AD$ and $DF=DC$, so $s+t=19$. The pentagon has perimeter $AB+BC+CF+FE+EA=3s+t=43$. Subtraction gives $2s=24$, hence $\boxed{AB=12,\ EF=7}$.

\end{asy}
