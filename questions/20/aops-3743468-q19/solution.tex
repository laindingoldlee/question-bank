Divide all costs by two. For denominations 10 and 13, the smallest attainable values in the ten residue classes modulo 10 are $13j$ for $0\le j\le9$, since 13 is coprime to 10. In each residue, all larger numbers differing by a multiple of 10 are attainable. The largest unattainable value is $13\cdot9-10=107$. Doubling gives $\boxed{214}$ dollars.
