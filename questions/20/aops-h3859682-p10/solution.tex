Let the upper-left four entries be $a,b,d,e$. All other entries are forced: $c=18-a-b$, $f=18-d-e$, $g=18-a-d$, $h=18-b-e$, $i=a+b+d+e-18$. Thus count quadruples of integers $0\le a,b,d,e\le18$ for which these five expressions are nonnegative. Equivalently, loop $a=0,\ldots,18$, $b,d=0,\ldots,18-a$, and $e=\max(0,18-a-b-d),\ldots,\min(18-b,18-d)$. Summing the lengths of these intervals gives $N=18145=5\cdot19\cdot191$, so the greatest prime divisor is $\boxed{191}$.
