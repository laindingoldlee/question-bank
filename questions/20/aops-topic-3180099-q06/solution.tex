Divisors pair as $d,\dfrac{n}{d}$ except for an unpaired square root when $n$ is a square. Thus exclude exactly 1, 4, 9. The remaining sum is $\dfrac{15\cdot16}{2}-14=106$, and half is $\boxed{53}$.
