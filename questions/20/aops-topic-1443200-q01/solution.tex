The sum is $1+2+\cdots+n=\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$, so the mean is
\[
\dfrac{1}{n}\cdot\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{n+1}{2}} .
\]
The formula holds for every $n$; when $n$ is odd, $\dfrac{n+1}{2}$ is an integer, namely the middle number of the list (pair $1$ with $n$, $2$ with $n-1$, and so on: each pair averages $\dfrac{n+1}{2}$).
