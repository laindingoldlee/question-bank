After $n$ full days and nights the snail is $n$ feet up. During day $n+1$ it climbs to $n+5$ feet, and it reaches the top on that day exactly when $n+5\ge75$, i.e. $n\ge70$. It does not reach the top earlier: before day $71$ its highest point is $69+5=74$ feet (at the end of day $70$).

So it reaches the top during day $\boxed{71}$, after $70$ nights of sliding. (The tempting answer $75$ ignores that the snail does not slide back once it is at the top.)
