Triangle $ABC$ has $\angle ABC=90^\circ$, with $AB=a$, $BC=c$, and $AC=b$. The equation $ax^2+bx+c=0$ has exactly one solution. Prove that
\[\dfrac{a+c}{b}=\sqrt{\dfrac{3}{2}}.\]
