Modulo $7$, $12\equiv5$ and $11\equiv4$. Since $5^6\equiv1$ and $2011\equiv1\pmod6$, we have $12^{2011}\equiv5$. Also $4^3\equiv1$ and $2012\equiv2\pmod3$, so $11^{2012}\equiv4^2\equiv2\pmod7$. The sum is congruent to $5+2=7$, so the remainder is $\boxed0$.
