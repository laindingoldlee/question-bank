Divisibility by nine gives $32+a+b\equiv0\pmod9$, so $a+b=4$ or $13$. The alternating digit sum for eleven is $2+b-a$, so $b-a=-2$ or $9$. Combining these digit constraints leaves only $a=3,b=1$. The resulting number $1399167=7\cdot199881$ also satisfies the remaining divisibility condition. Thus $\boxed{(a,b)=(3,1)}$.
