If $A=\prod p_i^{e_i}$, then $\tau(A^2)=\prod(2e_i+1)=2019=3\cdot673$, with $673$ prime. With at least two primes this forces exactly two factors $3$ and $673$: $e_1=1$, $e_2=336$. So $\tau(A)=2\cdot337=\boxed{674}$.
