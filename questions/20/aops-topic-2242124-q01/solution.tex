With $c$ correct and $u$ unanswered questions ($c+u\le25$), the score is $6c+1.5u=1.5(4c+u)$. Let $k=4c+u$. For a fixed $c$, $u$ runs from $0$ to $25-c$, so $k$ takes every value from $4c$ to $3c+25$.

For $c\le22$ these intervals overlap or touch the next one ($4(c+1)\le3c+26$), so together they cover every $k$ from $0$ to $3\cdot22+25=91$. Then $c=23$ gives $92$ to $94$, $c=24$ gives $96$ and $97$, and $c=25$ gives $100$. So $k$ takes every value from $0$ to $100$ except $95$, $98$ and $99$ ($98$ possible scores).

The sum of all possible scores is
\[1.5\left(\dfrac{100\cdot101}{2}-95-98-99\right)=1.5\,(5050-292)=1.5\cdot4758=\boxed{7137}.\]
