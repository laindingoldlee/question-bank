Rearranging, $n\left(n^4-2n^3+n^2-4n+5\right)=2$, so the prime $n$ divides $2$, and $n=2$. Check: $32-32+8-16+10-2=0$. So $n=\boxed{2}$.

(In fact $n^5-2n^4+n^3-4n^2+5n-2=(n-2)\left(n^4+n^2-2n+1\right)=(n-2)\left(n^4+(n-1)^2\right)$, and the second factor is positive for every real $n$, so $2$ is the only real root.)
