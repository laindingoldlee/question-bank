Circles $A$ and $B$ share a centre $O$ and have radii $3\sqrt3$ and $3$, respectively. A point $T$ is chosen on circle $A$, and a line $\ell$ through $T$ is tangent to circle $B$ at $M$. Point $N$ is chosen on circle $B$ such that $TM=NM$ and $\angle TMN\le90^\circ$. If the area of quadrilateral $TMON$ is $\dfrac{a(b+\sqrt c)}{d}$, where $a,b,c,d$ are positive integers, $a$ and $d$ are relatively prime and $c$ is squarefree, find $(ac-b)(a+2b)$.
