Let $D$ approach $A$ from the interior. The distances to $AB,AC$ tend to zero and the distance to $BC$ tends to the altitude from $A$. Thus that altitude is $6\sqrt3$. The same argument at $B,C$ shows all three altitudes are equal. Since $[ABC]=\dfrac{1}{2}a h_a=\dfrac{1}{2}b h_b=\dfrac{1}{2}c h_c$, the side lengths are equal too. The triangle is equilateral with height $6\sqrt3$, so its side is $12$ and its area is $\boxed{36\sqrt3}$.

import graph;
usepackage("amsmath");
import geometry;
size(7cm);
pair A=(3,3sqrt(3)),B=(0,0),C=(6,0),D=(2.8,1.5);pair E=(2.8,0),F=B+dot(D-B,A-B)/dot(A-B,A-B)*(A-B),G=C+dot(D-C,A-C)/dot(A-C,A-C)*(A-C);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(D--E,dashed);draw(D--F,dashed);draw(D--G,dashed);label("$A$",A,(0,1));label("$B$",B,(-1,-1));label("$C$",C,(1,-1));label("$D$",D,(1,-1));
\end{asy}
