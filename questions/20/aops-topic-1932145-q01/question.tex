Let $ABCD$ be a square. Point $P$ lies on side $BC$ with $BC=4\,BP$, and point $Q$ lies on side $CD$ with $DC=4\,CQ$. Lines $AQ$ and $DP$ meet at $R$. Prove that $AQ\perp DP$, and find the ratio of the area of triangle $DQR$ to the area of square $ABCD$. (In particular $[DQR]<[ABCD]$.)
