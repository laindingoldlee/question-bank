From the second equation, $x=-5y+2z-25$. Substituting into the first:
\[3(-5y+2z-25)-2y-5z=392\iff-17y+z=467\iff z=467+17y.\]
Substituting into the third:
\[10(-5y+2z-25)+3y-24z=-13\iff-47y-4z=237.\]
With $z=467+17y$: $-47y-1868-68y=237$, so $-115y=2105$ and $y=-\dfrac{421}{23}$. Then
\[z=467-\dfrac{7157}{23}=\dfrac{3584}{23},\qquad x=\dfrac{2105}{23}+\dfrac{7168}{23}-\dfrac{575}{23}=\dfrac{8698}{23}.\]
The solution is
\[\boxed{x=\dfrac{8698}{23},\quad y=-\dfrac{421}{23},\quad z=\dfrac{3584}{23}}.\]
(Check, first equation: $\dfrac{26094+842-17920}{23}=\dfrac{9016}{23}=392$.)
