Reduce modulo $18$: $19x\equiv x$ and $2019=18\cdot112+3$, so $x\equiv3\pmod{18}$. Then $x$ is a multiple of $3$ ($x=18k+3=3(6k+1)$), and since $x$ is prime, $x=3$. Then $18y=2019-57=1962$, so $y=109$, which is prime. The only solution is $\boxed{(x,y)=(3,109)}$.
