A play has two male roles, two female roles and two roles that can be either gender. Only a man can be assigned to a male role, and only a woman can be assigned to a female role. If five men and six women audition, in how many ways can the six roles be assigned? Explain why the answer is not $\dbinom52\dbinom62\dbinom72$.
