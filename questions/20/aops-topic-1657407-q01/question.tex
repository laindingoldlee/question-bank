A bag contains $4$ red marbles and $5$ blue marbles. John repeats the following step $8$ times: he draws two marbles at random from the bag, chooses one of the two at random (each with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$) and puts it back, and throws the other one away. After the $8$ steps one marble is left in the bag. What is the probability that it is red?
