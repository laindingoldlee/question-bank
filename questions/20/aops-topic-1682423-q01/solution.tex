Drop the altitude $BH$ from $B$ to $AC$. Triangle $ABH$ is a $30$-$60$-$90$ triangle with hypotenuse $AB=4$, so $AH=2$ and $BH=2\sqrt3$. Since $AH=2<10$, the foot $H$ lies on side $AC$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),C=(10,0),B=4*dir(60),H=(B.x,0);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(B--H,dashed);
real r=0.35;draw(H+(r,0)--H+(r,r)--H+(0,r),linewidth(0.6));
draw(arc(A,1.0,0,60),linewidth(0.6));label("$60^\circ$",A+1.55*dir(30));
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,N);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$H$",H,S);
label("$4$",(A+B)/2,NW);draw((0,-1.1)--(10,-1.1),linewidth(0.5),Arrows(TeXHead));label("$10$",(5,-1.1),S);
label("$2\sqrt{3}$",(B+H)/2,E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The area is
\[
\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot AC\cdot BH=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot10\cdot2\sqrt3=\boxed{10\sqrt3\text{ cm}^2}.
\]
(Equivalently, $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot AB\cdot AC\sin60^\circ=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot4\cdot10\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$.)
