Adding real and imaginary parts separately, $Z+W=(Z_1+W_1)+(Z_2+W_2)i$, where $Z_1+W_1$ and $Z_2+W_2$ are real. The conjugate of a complex number $p+qi$ (with $p,q$ real) is $p-qi$, so
\[
\overline{Z+W}=(Z_1+W_1)-(Z_2+W_2)i .
\]
Also $\overline Z+\overline W=(Z_1-Z_2i)+(W_1-W_2i)=(Z_1+W_1)-(Z_2+W_2)i$, the same number. $\blacksquare$
