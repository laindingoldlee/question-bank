In RSA encryption with modulus $n=3233=61\cdot53$, we have $\varphi(n)=60\cdot52=3120$, and the public key is $e=17$. The private key is the integer $d$ with $1\le d<3120$ and $de\equiv1\pmod{3120}$; equivalently $d=\dfrac{k\cdot3120+1}{17}$ for a suitable integer $k$. Find $d$ and $k$ without guess and check.
