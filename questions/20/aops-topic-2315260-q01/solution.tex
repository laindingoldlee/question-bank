Today a half-pound package costs $3$ dollars, so today a full pound costs $2\cdot3=6$ dollars. Today's price is $50\%$ off, that is, half of the regular price, so the regular price of a pound is $2\cdot6=12$ dollars: $\boxed{\textbf{(D) }12}$.

The answer $6$ undoes only one of the two halvings: $6$ dollars is the regular price of a \emph{half-pound} package (or today's price of a full pound). The sign gives the sale price of half a pound, so both the ``half price'' and the ``half pound'' must be doubled.
