The second equation gives $y(10f+e)=x^2y$, so $x^2=10f+e$. Thus $x\ge4$ and its two-digit square reverses to $xy$. For $x=4,5,6,7,8,9$, reversing their squares gives $61,52,63,94,46,18$. Only the last is a multiple of its corresponding $x$ with one-digit quotient: $18=9\cdot2$. Therefore $\boxed{x-y=7}$.
