Let $D$ be the convex solid obtained by attaching an outward square pyramid of height 1 to each face of the cube $[-1,1]^3$. (a) Find its edge length and volume. (b) A scaled copy of $D$ has edge length $n$. Suppose it is tiled without overlaps by congruent parallelepipeds generated by the unit vectors $\dfrac{(1,1,1)}{\sqrt3}$, $\dfrac{(1,-1,-1)}{\sqrt3}$ and $\dfrac{(-1,1,-1)}{\sqrt3}$. How many tiles must it contain?
