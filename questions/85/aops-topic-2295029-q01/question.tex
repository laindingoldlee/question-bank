In triangle $ABC$, the line $XY$ through $C$ is parallel to $AB$, with $C$ between $X$ and $Y$. The lines $AX$ and $BY$ are the bisectors of the angles at $A$ and $B$; $AX$ meets $BC$ at $D$, and $BY$ meets $AC$ at $E$. Prove that if $YE=XD$, then $AC=BC$.
