A divisor ending in $0$ forces $10\mid N$. $N=270=2\cdot3^3\cdot5$ works: its divisors $1,2,3,5,6,9,10,15,18,27,30,45,54,90,135,270$ end in $1,2,3,5,6,9,0,5,8,7,0,5,4,0,5,0$, which cover every digit. Checking the multiples of $10$ below $270$ shows each misses some digit (typically $7$, which needs a divisor such as $7$, $17$ or $27$), so the least is $\boxed{270}$ (confirmed by exhaustive search).
