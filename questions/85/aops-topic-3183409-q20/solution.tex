The degree-at-most-four polynomial $P(x)-1$ has four distinct roots, so it equals $k(x^2-25)(x^2-4)$. Its constant term is $-1$, giving $100k=-1$. Hence $P(10)=1-\dfrac{(100-25)(100-4)}{100}=1-72=\boxed{-71}$.
