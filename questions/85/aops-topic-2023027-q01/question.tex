Bob, Son and Joe each choose an integer from $1$ to $10$, independently (the same number may be chosen more than once). It turns out that any two of the three numbers differ by at most $2$. In how many ways can this happen? (The choices are ordered: Bob's, Son's and Joe's numbers are recorded separately.)
