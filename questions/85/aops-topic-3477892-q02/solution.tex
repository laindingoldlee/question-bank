Every factorial from $5!$ onward ends in zero. Thus the expression is congruent to $1-2+6-24=-19\equiv1\pmod{10}$. The entire sum is positive: pair $3!-2!$, $5!-4!$, and so on. Its units digit is therefore $\boxed1$, choice A; the source hint excluding A was incorrect.
