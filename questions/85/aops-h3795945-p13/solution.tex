Let $k$ be the original number of chests and let $r$ be the number of nights on which the new chest receives a diamond. There are thirty nights between the two morning counts, so $30k+r=2022-1200=822$, with $1\le r\le30$. Thus $k=27,r=12$. The twelve nights are August $19$ through August $30$, so the chest was brought before the night of $\boxed{\text{August }19}$.
