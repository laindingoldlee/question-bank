A five-digit flippy number has the form $\overline{ababa}$ with $a\ne b$ and $a\ne0$. Divisibility by $5$ forces the last digit $a$ to be $0$ or $5$, and $a\ne0$, so $a=5$. Divisibility by $3$ requires the digit sum $3a+2b=15+2b$ to be a multiple of $3$, i.e. $b$ is a multiple of $3$: $b\in\{0,3,6,9\}$ (all different from $5$). So there are $4$ such numbers: $50505$, $53535$, $56565$, $59595$. The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(B) }4}$.
