The individual capture probabilities are $\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{1}{5},\dfrac{1}{10}$. Independent phases make the no-capture probability $(1-\dfrac{1}{2})(1-\dfrac{1}{5})(1-\dfrac{1}{10})=\dfrac{9}{25}$. Its complement is $\boxed{\dfrac{16}{25}}$.
