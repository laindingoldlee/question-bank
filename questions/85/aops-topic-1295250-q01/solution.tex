Expanding, each of $x^2,y^2,z^2$ appears with coefficient $1+4+9=14$, and each product $xy$, $yz$, $zx$ appears with coefficient $2(1\cdot3+2\cdot1+3\cdot2)=22$. So the expression is
\[
14(x^2+y^2+z^2)+22(xy+yz+zx).
\]
Since $xy+yz+zx=\dfrac{10^2-50}{2}=25$, the expression always equals $14\cdot50+22\cdot25=\boxed{1250}$; this constant is also its maximum.
