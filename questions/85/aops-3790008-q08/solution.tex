Let $AD=t$. The cosine rule gives $16=49+t^2-7\sqrt3t$, so $t^2-7\sqrt3t+33=0$. Its positive roots are $\boxed{\dfrac{7\sqrt3\pm\sqrt{15}}{2}}$. Both are realised by the two intersections of the specified ray with the circle centred at $B$ of radius 4.
\begin{asy}
import graph;
usepackage("amsmath");
import geometry;
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=7dir(30),D1=((7sqrt(3)-sqrt(15))/2,0),D2=((7sqrt(3)+sqrt(15))/2,0);draw(A--B--D1--A--D2--B);draw(circle(B,4));label("$A$",A,(-1,0));label("$B$",B,(0,1));label("$D_1$",D1,(0,-1));label("$D_2$",D2,(0,-1));
\end{asy}
