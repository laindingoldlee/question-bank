For a specified minimum $m$, any of the three variables can be the minimum, and the others must lie in $[m,\min(1,m+0.1)]$. Ties have probability zero. Integrate to get $3\int_0^{0.9}(0.1)^2\,dm+3\int_{0.9}^1(1-m)^2\,dm=0.027+0.001=\boxed{0.028=\dfrac{7}{250}}$. More generally the range-at-most-$t$ probability is $3t^2-2t^3$.
