Measure time $t$ in minutes after $6{:}00$. The minute hand turns $6^\circ$ per minute and the hour hand $0.5^\circ$ per minute, so the angle from the hour hand to the minute hand (measured clockwise) increases by $5.5^\circ$ per minute. At $6{:}00$ it is $180^\circ$, so at time $t$ it is
\[
\theta(t)=180+5.5t\quad(\text{degrees, taken modulo }360).
\]
The hands form a $150^\circ$ angle exactly when $\theta(t)\equiv150$ or $\theta(t)\equiv210\pmod{360}$. Over $0\le t\le480$, $\theta$ increases continuously from $180$ to $180+2640=2820$.
\begin{itemize}
\item Values $\equiv210$: $210,570,\dots,2730$, which is $\dfrac{2730-210}{360}+1=8$ times.
\item Values $\equiv150$: $510,870,\dots,2670$, which is $\dfrac{2670-510}{360}+1=7$ times.
\end{itemize}
Neither endpoint gives $150^\circ$ (the angles are $180^\circ$ at $6{:}00$ and $60^\circ$ at $14{:}00$). So the answer is $8+7=\boxed{15}$.
