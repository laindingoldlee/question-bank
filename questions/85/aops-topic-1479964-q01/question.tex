For a positive integer $m$, we write $a\equiv b\pmod m$ when $m$ divides $a-b$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Explain why $10\equiv-1\pmod{11}$, and why $5\equiv10\pmod5$ and $10\equiv0\pmod5$ are both true.
\item[(b)] Prove that if $a\equiv b\pmod m$ and $c\equiv d\pmod m$, then $a+c\equiv b+d\pmod m$ and $ac\equiv bd\pmod m$.
\item[(c)] Use (a) and (b) to find the remainder when $10^{2017}$ is divided by $11$.
\end{enumerate}
