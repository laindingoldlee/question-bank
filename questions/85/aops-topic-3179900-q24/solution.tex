Let $E_j$ be expected further rolls when the longest suffix matching a prefix of 2021 has length $j=0,1,2,3$. First-step analysis gives $E_0=1+\dfrac{3E_0+E_1}{4}$, $E_1=1+\dfrac{2E_0+E_1+E_2}{4}$, $E_2=1+\dfrac{3E_0+E_3}{4}$, and $E_3=1+\dfrac{E_0+E_1+E_2}{4}$. In the last equation, digit 1 completes the pattern, digit 0 leaves suffix 20, digit 2 leaves suffix 2, and digit 3 resets. The first three equations imply $E_1=E_0-4,E_2=E_0-16,E_3=E_0-68$. Substituting into the fourth yields $E_0-68=\dfrac{3}{4}E_0-4$, hence $\boxed{E_0=256}$.
