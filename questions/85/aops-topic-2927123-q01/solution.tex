$\left(3^{-1}\right)^{11}=3^{-11}=\dfrac1{177147}$ (not $177147$), and $3^{(-1)^{11}}=3^{-1}=\dfrac13$. Towers of exponents are read from the top down, so $3^{-1^{11}}$ means $3^{\left(-1^{11}\right)}=3^{-1}=\boxed{\dfrac13}$.
