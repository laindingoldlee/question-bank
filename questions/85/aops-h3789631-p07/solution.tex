Write $AO=a,BO=b,CO=c,DO=d$. Perpendicular diagonals give $[AOB]=\dfrac{ab}{2}$, $[BOC]=\dfrac{bc}{2}$, $[COD]=\dfrac{cd}{2}$, and $[DOA]=\dfrac{da}{2}$. Therefore $[AOB][COD]=[BOC][DOA]$, giving $[DOA]=\dfrac{6\cdot4}{5}=\boxed{\dfrac{24}{5}}$.
