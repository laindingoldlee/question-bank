$\dfrac1{36}=\dfrac6{216}$, so $n$ is a sum obtained in exactly $6$ ways with three dice. The counts for $3,4,5,\dots$ are $1,3,6,\dots$, and by the symmetry $s\leftrightarrow21-s$, $n=5$ or $n=16$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $n=5$: two dice sum to $6$ in $5$ ways, so the probability is $\dfrac5{36}$.
\item $n=16$: two dice cannot sum to $17$, so the probability is $0$.
\end{itemize}
The possible values are $\boxed{\dfrac5{36}\text{ or }0}$.
