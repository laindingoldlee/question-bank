Multiply repeatedly by $3$: $0.3\to0$, $0.9\to0$, $2.7\to2$ (keep $0.7$), $2.1\to2$ (keep $0.1$), and the pattern repeats: $0.1=0.\overline{0022}_3$. The fifth digit is $0$, so to four places it is $\boxed{0.0022_3}$.
