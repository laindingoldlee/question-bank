In a simplified blackjack game, all cards are dealt face up from a standard $52$-card deck. Numbered cards count their number, Jack, Queen and King count $10$, and an Ace counts $1$ or $11$ at the holder's choice; a hand worth more than $21$ loses. Your opponent holds a King and a $10$ (total $20$) and stops. You hold a Queen and a $9$ (total $19$) and may draw cards one at a time from the remaining $48$; you win as soon as your total is $20$ or $21$, and you lose if it exceeds $21$. What is the probability that you win?
