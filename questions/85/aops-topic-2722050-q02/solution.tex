Your next card decides the game. An Ace (counted as $1$) gives $20$ and a $2$ gives $21$; any other card is worth at least $3$ and takes you past $21$. None of the four Aces or four $2$'s has been dealt, so the probability is $\dfrac{8}{48}=\boxed{\dfrac16}$.
