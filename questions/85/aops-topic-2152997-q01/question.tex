One hundred people are surveyed, with the following results.
\begin{itemize}
\item Every respondent who owns a car also owns a home.
\item $54$ respondents are men (the rest are women).
\item $30$ of the men do not own a car.
\item $30$ of the women own a home.
\item $5$ of the women own a home but not a car.
\item $15$ of the respondents own a home but not a car.
\end{itemize}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Draw a diagram of the situation and give the number of people in each region.
\item[(b)] How many men own a home but not a car?
\item[(c)] How many women do not own a home?
\end{enumerate}
