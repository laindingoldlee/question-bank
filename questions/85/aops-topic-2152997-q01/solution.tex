Since every car owner owns a home, each person is in exactly one of three groups: car and home, home only, or neither. Split each group by men and women.

\textbf{Women} ($100-54=46$). $30$ own a home, of whom $5$ have no car, so $25$ own a car and a home. The other $46-30=16$ own no home.

\textbf{Men} ($54$). $15$ people own a home but not a car, and $5$ of them are women, so $15-5=10$ are men. $30$ men have no car; $10$ of these own a home, so $20$ own neither. The remaining $54-30=24$ men own a car (and hence a home).

\textbf{(a)} In the diagram the left half is the men and the right half the women; the large oval is home owners and the small oval inside it is car owners.
