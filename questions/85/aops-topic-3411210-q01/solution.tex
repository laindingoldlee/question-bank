For each two-digit prime above $73$, its square exceeds $300$, so its exponent is $\lfloor\dfrac{300}{p}\rfloor-\lfloor\dfrac{200}{p}\rfloor-\lfloor\dfrac{100}{p}\rfloor$. The primes $97,89,83,79$ each give $3-2-1=0$. For $p=73$, the exponent is $4-2-1=1$. Thus the greatest two-digit prime divisor is $\boxed{73}$.
