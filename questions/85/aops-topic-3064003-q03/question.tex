Triangle $ABC$ has perimeter 4. Points $X$ and $Y$ lie on rays $AB$ and $AC$, respectively, with $AX=AY=1$. Suppose lines $XY$ and $BC$ meet at a point $M$ in the interior of segment $BC$. Prove that at least one of triangles $ABM$ and $ACM$ has perimeter 2. Assume the intersection is unique.
