The solution contains $0.02\cdot1000=20$ grams of salt. After adding $s$ grams of salt there are $20+s$ grams of salt in $1000+s$ grams of solution, so
\[
\dfrac{20+s}{1000+s}=\dfrac{4}{100}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 2000+100s=4000+4s\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 96s=2000,
\]
and $s=\boxed{\dfrac{125}{6}\approx20.83}$ grams. (Not $20$ grams: adding salt also increases the total mass.)
