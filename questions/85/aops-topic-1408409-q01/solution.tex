From $(x+y)^2=x^2+y^2+2xy$ we get $9=29+2xy$, so $xy=-10$; and $x+y=3$ or $x+y=-3$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $x+y=3$, $xy=-10$: $x,y$ are the roots of $t^2-3t-10=(t-5)(t+2)$, so with $x>y$: $(x,y)=(5,-2)$.
\item $x+y=-3$, $xy=-10$: the roots of $t^2+3t-10=(t+5)(t-2)$, so $(x,y)=(2,-5)$.
\end{itemize}
The smallest possible value of $x$ is $\boxed{2}$.
