There are $4$ aces and $48$ other cards. An even number of aces means $0$, $2$ or $4$ aces:
\[P=\dfrac{\dbinom44\dbinom{48}0+\dbinom42\dbinom{48}2+\dbinom40\dbinom{48}4}{\dbinom{52}4}=\dfrac{1+6\cdot1128+194580}{270725}=\boxed{\dfrac{201349}{270725}}\approx0.744.\]
