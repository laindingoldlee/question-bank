The two blue marbles divide the row into three ordered gaps. Each gap must contain only red marbles, only green marbles, or nothing. If all reds occupy one gap and all greens another, there are $3\cdot2=6$ choices. Otherwise one colour splits across two gaps, and the other occupies the remaining gap; there are 2 choices of the split colour and 3 choices of the other gap, giving 6. Both colours cannot split, as that would require four gaps. Hence $\boxed{12}$.
