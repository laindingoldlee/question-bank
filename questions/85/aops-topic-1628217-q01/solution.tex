Use the difference of squares repeatedly:
\[
y^8-1=(y^4-1)(y^4+1)=(y^2-1)(y^2+1)(y^4+1)=\boxed{(y-1)(y+1)(y^2+1)(y^4+1)}.
\]
The factors $y^2+1$ and $y^4+1$ cannot be factored further with integer (or rational) coefficients. (With real coefficients, $y^4+1=(y^2+\sqrt2\,y+1)(y^2-\sqrt2\,y+1)$.)
