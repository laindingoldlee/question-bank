Gary and Bob play a game in rounds. The winner of round $k$ receives $2^k$ points (so the first round is worth $2$, the second $4$, and so on), and the game ends as soon as a player has at least $250$ points. Each round is won by either player with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, independently. What is the probability that, when the game ends, the positive difference between the two players' totals is a multiple of $3$?
