Here $a=2i$, $b=-3i$, $c=-5i$, so
\[b^2-4ac=(-3i)^2-4(2i)(-5i)=-9+40i^2=-49,\qquad\sqrt{-49}=7i.\]
Hence
\[x=\dfrac{3i\pm7i}{4i}=\dfrac{3\pm7}{4},\]
i.e. $x=\boxed{\dfrac52\text{ or }-1}$. (Dividing the equation by $i$ first gives $2x^2-3x-5=(2x-5)(x+1)=0$, the same roots.)
