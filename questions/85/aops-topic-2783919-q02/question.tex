A team of entomologists collected the following organisms: $3252$ insects, $1542$ arachnids, $317$ worms, $17$ millipedes and $16$ centipedes. Insects always have six legs and worms always have zero legs. Arachnids usually have eight legs, but occasionally an arachnid has six legs. A millipede has between $34$ and $750$ legs and a centipede has between $30$ and $354$ legs. What is the median number of legs of the organisms collected?
