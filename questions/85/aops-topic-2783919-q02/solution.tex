There are $3252+1542+317+17+16=5144$ organisms, so the median is the average of the $2572$nd and $2573$rd values in increasing order. The $317$ worms come first, with $0$ legs. Every other organism has at least $6$ legs, and the insects alone supply $3252$ organisms with exactly $6$ legs. Any six-legged arachnids only add more $6$s, and all remaining organisms have at least $8$ legs. So positions $318$ through at least $317+3252=3569$ all have value $6$, and both middle values are $6$. The median is $\boxed{6}$.
