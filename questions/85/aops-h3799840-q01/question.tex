Circle $\Gamma$ has centre $O$. Another circle passes through $O$ and meets $\Gamma$ at $A,B$. A point $C$ on the second circle is distinct from $O,A,B$. Lines $AC,BC$ meet $\Gamma$ again at $D,E$, respectively. Assume these intersections are distinct from $A,B$, respectively. Prove that $AE$ and $DB$ are parallel.
