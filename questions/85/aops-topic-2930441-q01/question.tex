A $5$-pound box of nails costs $\$39$ and a $1$-pound box costs $\$11$; assume price varies linearly with weight. (a) Find the price $P$ in terms of weight $w$ (pounds). (b) Predict the price of a $2.5$-pound box. (c) Actual prices for $2$- and $3$-pound boxes are $\$21$ and $\$27$. Are these above or below the model? (d) What weight corresponds to a price of $\$33.75$? (e) What are the units of the slope?
