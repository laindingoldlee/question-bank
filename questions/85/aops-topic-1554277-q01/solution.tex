We need four digits with product $5!=120=2^3\cdot3\cdot5$. No digit is $0$, and one digit is $5$ (the only digit divisible by $5$).

\textbf{Largest.} The leading digit should be as large as possible. It cannot be $9$ ($9\nmid120$); it can be $8$, leaving product $15=5\cdot3\cdot1$ for the other three digits, arranged in decreasing order: $8531$.

\textbf{Smallest.} The leading digit can be $1$, leaving product $120$ for three digits. The second digit cannot be $1$ ($120$ is not a product of two digits) or $2$ ($60$ is not a product of two digits, since $60=6\cdot10$), but it can be $3$, leaving $40=5\cdot8$: $1358$.

The sum is $8531+1358=\boxed{9889}$.
