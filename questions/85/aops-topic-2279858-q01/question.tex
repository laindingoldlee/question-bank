A right circular cone is inscribed in a sphere: its apex and the circle of its base lie on the sphere. The plane of the base lies halfway between the centre of the sphere and the point of the sphere farthest from the apex. The volume of the cone is $36\pi\left(1+\sqrt{10}\right)$. What is the volume of the sphere?
