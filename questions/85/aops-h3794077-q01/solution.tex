The identity $9\cdot12345679=111111111$ gives $m=45$ and product $555555555$. To show minimality, a product with at most seven digits is too small, since $12345679$ already has eight digits. The only eight-digit candidate is $55555555$, which lies strictly between $4\cdot12345679=49382716$ and $5\cdot12345679=61728395$. The smallest nine-digit candidate is the one obtained at $m=45$. Hence $\boxed{45}$.
