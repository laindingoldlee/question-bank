Polynomial division gives
\[2a^5-5a^4+2a^3-8a^2=(a^2-3a+1)(2a^3+a^2+3a)-3a .\]
(Expand: $(a^2-3a+1)(2a^3+a^2+3a)=2a^5-5a^4+2a^3-8a^2+3a$.) Since $a^2-3a+1=0$, the numerator equals $-3a$. Also $a^2+1=3a$, and $a\ne0$. So the fraction is $\dfrac{-3a}{3a}=\boxed{-1}$, for both roots.
