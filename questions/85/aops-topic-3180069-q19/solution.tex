The law of cosines gives $BD^2=49+25-70\cos135^\circ=74+35\sqrt2$. Triangle $ABD$ has area $\dfrac{35\sqrt2}{4}$. In isosceles triangle $BCD$, the altitude to $BD$ has length $\dfrac{BD}{2}\cot22.5^\circ$, so its area is $\dfrac{BD^2}{4}(1+\sqrt2)$. Adding the two triangle areas gives $\dfrac{35\sqrt2+(74+35\sqrt2)(1+\sqrt2)}{4}=\boxed{36+36\sqrt2}$.
