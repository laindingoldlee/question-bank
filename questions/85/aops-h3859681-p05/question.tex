Anthony and Gentry are both driving from the same origin to the same destination. Anthony takes a $300$-mile route, while Gentry takes a $400$-miles route. Their driving speeds (in miles per hour) are chosen independently at random from the interval $[30,60]$. Given the probability that Anthony arrives at the destination before Gentry can be written as $\dfrac{p}{q}$, where $p$ and $q$ are relatively prime integers, find $p+q$.
