Let the speeds be $v,w$. Anthony arrives first when $\dfrac{300}{v}<\dfrac{400}{w}$, or $w<\dfrac{4}{3}v$. The speed sample space is a square of side 30. The unfavorable part is a right triangle of legs 15 and 20, area 150. Hence the probability is $1-\dfrac{150}{900}=\dfrac{5}{6}$, giving $\boxed{p+q=11}$.
