If roots are $r,s$, then $a=-(r+s)$ and $b=rs$, so $r^2+s^2=97$. Assuming $r\le s$ gives $1\le r\le6$. Checking $97-r^2$ for these six values yields a square only at $r=4$, namely 81. Thus the roots are 4 and 9, $a=-13,b=36$, and $\boxed{a+b=23}$.
