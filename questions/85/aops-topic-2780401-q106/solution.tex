Factor $ 81000000=2^6\cdot3^4\cdot5^6 $. The exponent in $ x $ must be even and the exponent in $ y $ a multiple of 3, with their maximum equal to the prescribed exponent. For exponent 6, the choices are $ x $-exponent 6 with $ y $-exponent 0, 3, 6, or $ y $-exponent 6 with $ x $-exponent 0, 2, 4: six choices. For exponent 4, only $ x $-exponent 4 works, with $ y $-exponent 0 or 3: two choices. Thus the answer is $ 6\cdot2\cdot6=\boxed{72}$.
