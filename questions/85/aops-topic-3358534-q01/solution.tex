The common difference is $\dfrac{2}{3}$. Multiplying the list by $3$ gives $11,13,15,\ldots,79,81$. The number of terms is $\dfrac{81-11}{2}+1=\boxed{36}$. The $+1$ counts the first term as well as the gaps.
