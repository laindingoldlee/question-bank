A cylinder has radius $\dfrac{2}{\pi}$ feet. A snake starts at a point on the circumference of the bottom base and slithers along the curved surface of the cylinder at $10$ feet per second, winding around the cylinder exactly $36$ times, until it reaches the point of the top circumference directly above its starting point. A second snake starts at a point on the top circumference and slithers along the surface at $17$ feet per second, winding around exactly $90$ times, until it reaches the point of the bottom circumference directly below its starting point. Each snake takes the shortest path of its kind, and the two snakes start at the same time and arrive at the same time. What is the height of the cylinder, in feet?
