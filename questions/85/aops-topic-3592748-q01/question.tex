Assign the twelve numbers in $\{2,3,4,5,6,8,9,10,11,12,13,14\}$ bijectively to $a,b,c,d,e,f,g,h,i,j,k,l$ so that all nine expressions $1+d+c+a$, $d+l$, $a+b$, $c+b+f$, $c+e+h$, $g+f+h$, $f+j+7$, $h+i+7$, $j+i+k$ are equal. Find one assignment.
