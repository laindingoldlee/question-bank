One assignment is\[(a,b,c,d,e,f,g,h,i,j,k,l)=(9,11,4,6,13,5,12,3,10,8,2,14).\]It uses each required number exactly once. Substituting into the nine expressions gives, in order, $1+6+4+9$, $6+14$, $9+11$, $4+11+5$, $4+13+3$, $12+5+3$, $5+8+7$, $3+10+7$, and $8+10+2$. Each sum is $20$, verifying the construction. A way to search is to choose the common sum $S$ and $a,d,i$, then force $b=S-a$, $l=S-d$, $c=S-1-d-a$, $f=a-c$, $j=S-7-f$, $h=S-7-i$, $k=7+f-i$, $e=7+i-c$, $g=7+i-f$ and test the twelve distinct allowed values.
