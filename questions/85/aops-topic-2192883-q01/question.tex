Alex the geologist is in the desert, $10$ km from a long, straight road and $45$ km from base camp. The base camp is also $10$ km from the road, on the same side of the road as Alex. On the road his jeep can travel at $50$ km per hour, but in the desert sand it can manage only $30$ km per hour, and he may drive across the desert in any direction. Alex wants to return to base camp as quickly as possible. How long will the trip take? (Solve the problem without calculus.)
