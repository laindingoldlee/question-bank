\textbf{Look for a repeated root.} If $p(x)=x^3-547x^2+5040x+22420800$ had a double root $r$ and a third root $s$, then $p(x)=(x-r)^2(x-s)$, and comparing coefficients,
\[2r+s=547,\qquad r^2+2rs=5040,\qquad r^2s=-22420800.\]
Substituting $s=547-2r$ into the second equation gives $3r^2-1094r+5040=0$, i.e. $(r-360)(3r-14)=0$. The value $r=360$ gives $s=-173$, and then $r^2s=129600\cdot(-173)=-22420800$, as required. So
\[p(x)=(x-360)^2(x+173),\]
which can also be checked by expanding.

\textbf{Answer.} The values of $x$ satisfying the equation are $360$ and $-173$, and their sum is
\[360+(-173)=\boxed{187}.\]
Vieta's formula gives $547$ for the sum of the roots \emph{counted with multiplicity}, which counts the double root $360$ twice; the sum of the distinct values of $x$ is $547-360=187$.
