A car is purchased at the end of 2019, denoted by $t=0$. Its value, in thousands of dollars, is modelled by $V(t)=ab^t$, with $a>0$ and $0<b<1$. Given $V(1)=27.2$ and $V(6)=14.8$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Write two equations determining $a,b$.
\item Find $a,b$ to five decimal places.
\item Find the average rate of change from $t=1$ to $t=6$.
\item Use that rate to estimate the value at $t=3$.
\item Explain why the secant-line estimate exceeds $V(t)$ for $1<t<6$.
\item The owner donates the car when its value reaches $2$ thousand dollars, at which instant the owner's retained value becomes zero. Give the domain on which the exponential model describes the retained value before donation.
\end{enumerate}
