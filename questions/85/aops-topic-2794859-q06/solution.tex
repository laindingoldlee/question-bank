Use $B=(0,0),A=(0,3),C=(4,0)$. The distances are $x$ and $y$, so the desired region has $x>y$. The bisector $x=y$ meets $AC$ at $D=(\dfrac{12}{7},\dfrac{12}{7})$. The region is triangle $BCD$, of area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot4\cdot\dfrac{12}{7}=\dfrac{24}{7}$. Dividing by total area 6 gives $\boxed{\dfrac{4}{7}}$.

\end{asy}
