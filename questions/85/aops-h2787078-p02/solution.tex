If one pile has one match, remove the entire other pile; the opponent must take the last match and lose. If both piles exceed one, reduce the larger to equal the smaller. After each opponent move, restore equality while both piles exceed one. If the opponent first leaves one pile empty, reduce the other pile to one; if the opponent first leaves one pile with one match, remove the other pile entirely. In either case the opponent is left the final match. This is a winning strategy.
