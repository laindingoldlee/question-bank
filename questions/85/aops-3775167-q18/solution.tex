Put $K=(0,0),A=(-u,0),C=(u,0),B=(v,h)$, where $u,h>0$. Then $uh=20\sqrt3$, $v^2+h^2=25$, and $(v+u)^2+h^2=64$. Thus $v=\dfrac{39-u^2}{2u}$ and $h^2=\dfrac{1200}{u^2}$. Substitution yields $u^4-178u^2+6321=0$, or $(u^2-49)(u^2-129)=0$. The condition $8 > \dfrac{A C}{2} = u$ selects $u=7$. Therefore $\boxed{AC=14}$.
