\[
123,\ 456,\ 789;\qquad147,\ 258,\ 369;
\]

159,\ 267,\ 348;\qquad168,\ 249,\ 357.
\]
Identify the array positions with pairs of residues modulo $3$. The four families are the three lines of each direction: horizontal, vertical, and the two slopes $1,-1$. Two distinct positions determine exactly one of these lines, proving that every pair occurs exactly once.
