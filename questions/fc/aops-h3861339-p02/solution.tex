The product of all eight divisors is $n^4$, because each divisor pairs with its complementary divisor to give $n$. If $d$ is omitted, then $\dfrac{n^4}{d}=314928=2^4 3^9$.

Every prime divisor of $n$ must appear in this product: if its exponent in $n$ is $e$, its exponent in $\dfrac{n^4}{d}$ is between $3e$ and $4e$. Hence $n=2^a3^b$, with
\[
3a\le4\le4a,\qquad3b\le9\le4b.
\]
These force $a=1,b=3$, so $n=54$. It does have $(1+1)(3+1)=8$ divisors. The omitted divisor is $\dfrac{54^4}{314928}=27$. Thus $\boxed{n=54,\ d=27}$.
