For a composite positive integer $n$, let $D(n)$ be the largest divisor of $n$ that is smaller than $n$. Call a pair $(n,n+1)$ of composite numbers \emph{square} if $D(n)+D(n+1)$ is a perfect square; for example $(35,36)$ is square, since $D(35)+D(36)=7+18=25$. Prove that there are infinitely many square pairs.
