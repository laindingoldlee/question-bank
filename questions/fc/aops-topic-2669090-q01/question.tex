A simple polygon is a closed polygon whose sides do not cross each other. Prove that every simple polygon with $n\ge3$ sides can be cut into triangles by non-crossing diagonals (segments joining two vertices and lying inside the polygon), and that every such cutting uses exactly $n-2$ triangles.
