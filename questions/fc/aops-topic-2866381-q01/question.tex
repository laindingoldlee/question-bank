Let $a_1,a_2,a_3,\dots$ be irrational numbers such that $a_i+a_{i+1}$ is rational for every $i$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $a_1-a_3$ is rational and $a_1+a_3$ is irrational.
\item[(b)] More generally, prove that for $i<j$: if $j-i$ is even then $a_i-a_j$ is rational and $a_i+a_j$ is irrational, while if $j-i$ is odd then $a_i+a_j$ is rational.
\end{enumerate}
