$12$ minutes is $\dfrac{1}{5}$ hour, so the second car gains $\dfrac{2}{5}\div\dfrac{1}{5}=2$ miles per hour on the first. Its speed is $60+2=\boxed{62}$ mph. (The poster's $61.8$ came from treating the gain as a $2$-minute head start.)
