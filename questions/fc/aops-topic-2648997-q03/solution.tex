$[ABD]=\dfrac12\cdot AB\cdot d=\dfrac52d$, where $d$ is the distance from $D$ to line $AB$. So $[ABD]\le5$ exactly when $d\le2$. Since $BC\perp AB$, the distance from line $AB$ grows from $0$ to $6$ (at $C$) across the triangle. The points with $d>2$ form a triangle similar to $ABC$ at vertex $C$, with ratio $\dfrac{6-2}{6}=\dfrac23$, hence with $\dfrac49$ of the area. The probability is $1-\dfrac49=\boxed{\dfrac59}$.
