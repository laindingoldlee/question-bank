Pooh visits Rabbit (a knight) and five other guests at a round table; each is a knight (always truthful) or a liar. Asked how many of their two neighbours are knights, everyone gives the same answer, and Pooh still cannot tell how many knights there are. Then a guest says "It's my birthday" and his neighbour says "Yes, it's his birthday!". How many knights are at the table?
