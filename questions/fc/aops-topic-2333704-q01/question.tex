Points $D$, $E$, $F$ lie on the sides $BC$, $CA$, $AB$ of triangle $ABC$ with
\[\dfrac{DB}{DC}=\dfrac{EC}{EA}=\dfrac{FA}{FB}=r>0.\]
Write $[XYZ]$ for the area of triangle $XYZ$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $\dfrac{[DEF]}{[ABC]}=\dfrac{r^2-r+1}{(r+1)^2}$.
\item[(b)] Some statements of this result put absolute value bars around $\dfrac{r^2-r+1}{(r+1)^2}$. Show that they make no difference: the expression is positive for every real $r\ne-1$.
\end{enumerate}
