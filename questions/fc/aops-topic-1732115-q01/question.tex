Bob comes home from school at a random time between 3:00 and 4:00 pm, and his mom comes home from work at a random time between 3:15 and 4:15 pm (both uniformly distributed, independently). If no one else is in the house when Bob gets home, he starts a game that lasts a random time between $5$ and $15$ minutes (uniformly distributed, independent of the arrival times). If Bob's mom comes home while Bob is playing, there is a $50\%$ chance that she makes him stop, in which case he will rage. What is the probability that Bob will rage?
