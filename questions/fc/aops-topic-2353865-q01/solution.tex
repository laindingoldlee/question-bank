``Exactly two identical digits'' means that one digit occurs exactly twice and the other two digits are different from it and from each other. The number is $\overline{1bcd}$.

\textbf{Case 1: the repeated digit is $1$.} Choose the position of the second $1$ among $b,c,d$ ($3$ ways); the other two positions get different digits other than $1$ ($9\cdot8=72$ ways). Total $3\cdot72=216$.

\textbf{Case 2: the repeated digit is some $x\ne1$.} Then $x$ fills two of the positions $b,c,d$: choose $x$ ($9$ ways, including $0$) and the two positions ($3$ ways); the remaining position gets a digit different from $1$ and $x$ ($8$ ways). Total $9\cdot3\cdot8=216$.

Altogether there are $216+216=\boxed{432}$ such numbers.
