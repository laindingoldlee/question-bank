The sum is 50, and at least two entries equal 10. If exactly two do, the other two smaller entries must have sum at least 3: sum 2 would require both 1, tying the frequency of 10. Hence the largest is at most $50-20-3=27$. If at least three entries equal 10, the largest is at most 19. The list $1,2,10,10,27$ has the required statistics and unique mode 10. Thus $\boxed{27}$.
