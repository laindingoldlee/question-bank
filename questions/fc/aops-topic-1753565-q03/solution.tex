For odd $a$, $a^4-1=(a-1)(a+1)(a^2+1)$. Here $a-1$ and $a+1$ are consecutive even numbers, so one of them is divisible by $4$ and their product by $8$; and $a^2+1$ is even. Hence $16\mid a^4-1$, i.e.\ $a^4\equiv1\pmod{16}$. Likewise $b^4\equiv1\pmod{16}$, so $a^4+b^4-2\equiv0\pmod{16}$. $\blacksquare$
