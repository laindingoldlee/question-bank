Let the terms be $a_1,a_2,\dots$ and $S_n=a_1+\cdots+a_n=n^3$. Then $a_1=S_1=1$ and, for $n\ge2$,
\[
a_n=S_n-S_{n-1}=n^3-(n-1)^3=3n^2-3n+1 .
\]
So the sequence is completely determined (it is $1,7,19,37,\dots$; it is neither arithmetic nor geometric, but no such assumption is needed). In particular
\[
a_{10}=1000-729=\boxed{271}.
\]
