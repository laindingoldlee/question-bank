Add $3$ to both sides: $a_n+3=2(a_{n-1}+3)$. So $a_n+3$ is a geometric sequence with first term $a_1+3=6$ and ratio $2$:
\[
a_n+3=6\cdot2^{n-1}=3\cdot2^n,\qquad a_n=\boxed{3\cdot2^n-3}.
\]
Check: $a_1=3$, $a_2=9$, $a_3=21$, and $2a_{n-1}+3=3\cdot2^n-6+3=a_n$.
