Let $ABC$ be a triangle in which the angles at $B$ and $C$ are acute, and let $O$ be its circumcentre. A point $D$ on segment $BC$ satisfies $\angle BAD=\angle CAO$. Prove that $\angle ADB=90^\circ$.
