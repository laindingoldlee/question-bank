By the definition of the logarithm (with base $x>0$, $x\ne1$),
\[
x^{-\dfrac{3}{2}}=\dfrac{1}{8}\iff x^{\dfrac{3}{2}}=8\iff x=8^{\dfrac{2}{3}}=\left(8^{\dfrac{1}{3}}\right)^2=4 .
\]
Check: $4^{-\dfrac{3}{2}}=\dfrac{1}{\left(\sqrt4\right)^3}=\dfrac{1}{8}$. The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(B) }4}$. (Choice (A) is impossible because a logarithm base must be positive.)
