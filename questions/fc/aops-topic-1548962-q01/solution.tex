\textbf{Labelled teams.} Line the eight people up in one of $8!$ orders, and put the first two in Team 1, the next two in Team 2, and so on. Every assignment to labelled teams arises, and exactly $2^4$ orders give the same assignment (within each team the two people can be listed in $2$ orders). So there are $\dfrac{8!}{2^4}=2520$ splittings into labelled teams.

\textbf{Unlabelled teams.} Each splitting into four (unlabelled) teams corresponds to $4!=24$ labelled ones (the ways to number the teams). So the number of splittings is
\[
\dfrac{2520}{24}=\boxed{105}.
\]
Check: the first person (in any fixed order) has $7$ choices of partner, the first unpaired person then has $5$, then $3$, then $1$: $7\cdot5\cdot3\cdot1=105$.
