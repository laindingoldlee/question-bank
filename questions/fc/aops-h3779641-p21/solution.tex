Expanding place values reduces the condition to $a+c=b+d$. For all four-digit strings allowing initial zero, the counts of ordered pairs with sums zero through eighteen are $1,2,\ldots,10,9,\ldots,1$. Therefore equal-sum pairs number $\sum_{i=1}^{10}i^2+\sum_{i=1}^{9}i^2=385+285=670$. Exclude a=0: choosing c from zero through nine requires b+d=c and gives $1+2+\cdots+10=55$ strings. The answer is $670-55=\boxed{615}$.
