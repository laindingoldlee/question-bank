Let $m$ be the edge count and $d_v$ the degrees. In a triangle-free graph, every edge $uv$ has $d_u+d_v\le100$. Summing yields $\sum d_v^2\le100m$, while Cauchy gives $\sum d_v^2\ge\dfrac{(2m)^2}{100}$, hence $m\le2500$. The number of unordered disjoint edge pairs is $\binom m2-\sum_v\binom{d_v}{2}$. A four-cycle contributes two such pairs, and any disjoint edge pair belongs to at most one four-cycle, since two completions would create a triangle. Thus the cycle count is at most $\dfrac{m^2+m-\sum d_v^2}{4}\le\dfrac{\dfrac{24}{25}m^2+m}{4}\le1500625$. A triangle-free four-person set has at most one four-cycle, so this also bounds the requested sets. The complete bipartite graph with parts of size $50,50$ attains $\binom{50}{2}^2=1500625=35^4$. Therefore the maximum is $\boxed{1500625}$.
