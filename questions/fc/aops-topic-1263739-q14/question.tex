Compute the number of ordered pairs $(a,b)$ of positive integers, other than $(20,16)$ and $(16,20)$, such that $\gcd(a,b)=\gcd(20,16)$ and $\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=\operatorname{lcm}(20,16)$.
