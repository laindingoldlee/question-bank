We have $\gcd(20,16)=4$ and $\operatorname{lcm}(20,16)=80$. Write $a=4a'$, $b=4b'$ with $\gcd(a',b')=1$; then $\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=4a'b'=80$, so $a'b'=20$ with $a',b'$ coprime. Since $20=2^2\cdot5$, each prime power goes entirely to one of them: $(a',b')\in\{(1,20),(20,1),(4,5),(5,4)\}$, i.e.
\[(a,b)\in\{(4,80),(80,4),(16,20),(20,16)\}.\]
Excluding $(20,16)$ and $(16,20)$ leaves $\boxed{2}$ pairs.
