Two ants start $100$ paces apart and move towards each other. Each day, both first advance simultaneously at constant speeds, the first by $\dfrac13$ pace and the second by $\dfrac15$ pace, and then both retreat, the first by $\dfrac14$ and the second by $\dfrac16$ pace. On which day do they meet?
