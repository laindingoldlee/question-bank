Each full day the gap shrinks by $\dfrac13+\dfrac15-\dfrac14-\dfrac16=\dfrac7{60}$ pace. But the ants meet during an advance, as soon as the gap at the start of a day is at most $\dfrac13+\dfrac15=\dfrac8{15}$.

After $k$ full days the gap is $100-\dfrac{7k}{60}$. We need $100-\dfrac{7k}{60}\le\dfrac8{15}$, i.e. $k\ge\dfrac{5968}{7}\approx852.6$, so $k=853$. At the start of day $854$ the gap is $100-\dfrac{5971}{60}=\dfrac{29}{60}\le\dfrac8{15}$, and the ants meet $\dfrac{29}{32}$ of the way through that day's advance.

So they meet on day $\boxed{854}$. (The popular answer $\dfrac{6000}7\approx857.1$ ignores that they meet during an advance.)
