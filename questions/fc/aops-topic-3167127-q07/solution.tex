We have $x+y=20+x-x^2=\dfrac{81}{4}-(x-\dfrac{1}{2})^2$. The upper bound $\dfrac{81}{4}$ occurs at $x=\dfrac{1}{2}$, $y=\dfrac{79}{4}$, both positive. Thus the maximum is $\boxed{\dfrac{81}{4}}$.
