The volume of a tetrahedron with opposite edge vectors $u,v$ and displacement vector $w$ between their lines is $| w \cdot \dfrac{(u\times v) |}{6}$. The normal component of $w$ is the distance $d$ between the lines, so this is $\dfrac{| u | | v | d \sin \theta}{6}$. Substitution gives $1 \cdot \sqrt{3} \cdot 2 \cdot \dfrac{\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}}{6} = \boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$. The formula follows by taking one vertex as the origin in the scalar-triple-product formula for tetrahedron volume.

\end{asy}
