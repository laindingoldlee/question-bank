(a) Interchanging $x,y$ leaves every term unchanged, so $x\star y=y\star x$. (b) Put $u=x\spadesuit y,v=y\spadesuit x$. Then $x*y=u\star v$ and $y*x=v\star u$, equal by part (a). Thus $\boxed{\text{both operations are commutative}}$, regardless of whether $\spadesuit$ is.
