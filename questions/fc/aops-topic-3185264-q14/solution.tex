Let $E_n$ be the expected number after $n$ minutes. The independent multiplier has mean $6$ and the added number has mean $2$, so $E_{n+1}=6E_n+2$. From $E_0=0$, we obtain $E_1=2$, $E_2=14$, and $\boxed{E_3=86}$.
