Since $\gcd(5,768)=1$, the inverse of 5 exists. Also $768=2^8\cdot3$, so $\varphi(768)=768\left(1-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)\left(1-\dfrac{1}{3}\right)=256$. Euler's theorem gives $5^{256}\equiv1\pmod{768}$. Taking inverses yields $\boxed{5^{-256}\equiv1\pmod{768}}$.
