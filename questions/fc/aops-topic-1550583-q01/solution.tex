Dave covered $59\cdot3.1=182.9$ km. Bob had been driving for $3.1+0.4=3.5$ hours and, since they went in opposite directions, covered the rest of the separation:
\[
266.9-182.9=84\text{ km}.
\]
So Bob's speed is $\dfrac{84}{3.5}=\boxed{24\text{ km per hour}}$.

(The general approach: draw a line with Dory's house in the middle, write each distance as rate times time, and add them because the directions are opposite.)
