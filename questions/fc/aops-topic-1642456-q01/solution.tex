Let $A=n$; then the $k$-th letter has value $n+k-1$. So $H=n+7$, $K=n+10$, $L=n+11$, $N=n+13$, and
\[
4n+41=2005\quad\Longrightarrow\quad n=491 .
\]
The $26$ values $491,492,\dots,516$ are an arithmetic sequence, so their average is the average of the first and last:
\[
\dfrac{491+516}{2}=\boxed{503.5}.
\]
