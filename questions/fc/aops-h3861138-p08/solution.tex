Let the two blocks have values $u,v\in\{0,\ldots,99\}$. Divisibility by $99$ gives $u+v\in\{0,99,198\}$. For positive blocks with sum $99$, the smaller divides the larger exactly when it is a divisor of $99$ less than $\dfrac{99}{2}$, namely $1,3,9,11,33$. Each unordered pair contributes two numbers with total $101(u+v)=9999$. The pair $0,99$ is also allowed since $99$ divides zero, and contributes another $9999$. Finally $(99,99)$ gives $9999$. The zero-zero case is excluded because it has no nonzero divisor. Hence the total is $7\cdot9999=\boxed{69993}$.
