A four-digit base-ten string $\overline{abcd}$, allowing leading zeroes, represents a multiple of $99$. Let $u=\overline{ab}$ and $v=\overline{cd}$ be the values of its two two-digit blocks. At least one nonzero block divides the other block; a zero dividend is allowed but a zero divisor is not. Find the sum of all possible values of $\overline{abcd}$.
