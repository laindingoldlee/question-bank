Multiplication of complex numbers in polar form adds arguments. Multiplying $\cos a+i\sin a$ and $\cos b+i\sin b$ and comparing real and imaginary parts with $\cos(a+b)+i\sin(a+b)$ gives
\[
\cos(a+b)=\cos a\cos b-\sin a\sin b,\qquad
\sin(a+b)=\sin a\cos b+\cos a\sin b.
\]
Setting $b=a$ yields
\[
\sin2a=2\sin a\cos a,\qquad
\cos2a=\cos^2a-\sin^2a=2\cos^2a-1=1-2\sin^2a.
\]
The last two forms use $\sin^2a+\cos^2a=1$.
