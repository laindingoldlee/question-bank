The symbol $\circ$ (a small empty circle) between two functions denotes \emph{composition}: $(F\circ G)(x)=F\bigl(G(x)\bigr)$. Let $F(x)=2x+1$ and $G(x)=x^2-3$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Compute $(F\circ G)(2)$ and $(G\circ F)(2)$.
\item[(b)] Find formulas for $(F\circ G)(x)$ and $(G\circ F)(x)$.
\item[(c)] Show that there is no real number $x$ with $(F\circ G)(x)=(G\circ F)(x)$.
\end{enumerate}
