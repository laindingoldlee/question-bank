A monic cubic polynomial $P(x)$ (leading coefficient $1$) has three integer roots, counted with multiplicity, each strictly between $-10$ and $10$. Two of the roots are $-1$ and $2$. What is the difference between the greatest and the least possible values of the sum of the coefficients of $P$?
