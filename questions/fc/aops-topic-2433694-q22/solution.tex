The number of coins is $1+2+\cdots+N=\dfrac{N(N+1)}{2}=2016$, so $N(N+1)=4032=63\cdot64$, and $N=63$ (the product $N(N+1)$ increases with $N$). The sum of the digits of $63$ is $9$: the answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(D)}\ 9}$.
