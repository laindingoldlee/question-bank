Put $m=n-1$, so consecutive corners are $m$ positions apart. At any time after all four corners are occupied, their four visible numbers, in increasing order, are $jm+1,(j+1)m+1,(j+2)m+1,(j+3)m+1$ for some integer $j\ge0$. Their sum gives $(4j+6)m+4=2012$, or $(2j+3)m=1004=4\cdot251$.
The odd factor $2j+3$ is at least three, so it must be $251$. Hence $j=124,m=4$, and the corner numbers are $\boxed{497,501,505,509}$. They occur on a $5\times5$ board, with these values clockwise from the top-left corner, so the solution is attainable and unique.
