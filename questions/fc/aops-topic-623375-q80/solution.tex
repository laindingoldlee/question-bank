Since $7\dfrac{2}{7}=\dfrac{51}{7}$,
\[
123\div\dfrac{51}{7}=\dfrac{123\cdot7}{51}=\dfrac{41\cdot7}{17}=\dfrac{287}{17}=16\dfrac{15}{17}\approx16.88 .
\]
The nearest integer is $\boxed{17}$.
