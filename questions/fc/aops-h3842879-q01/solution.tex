Four arrows suffice: $40+26+17+17=100$. One or two arrows cannot reach $100$, since each scores at most $40$. With three arrows, the largest must be $40$, because $3\cdot29<100$. The other two must sum to $60$, but checking complements of $16,17,23,26,29,40$ gives $44,43,37,34,31,20$, none allowed. Therefore the fewest is $\boxed4$.
