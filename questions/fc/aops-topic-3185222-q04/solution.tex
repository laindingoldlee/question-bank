Slope is change in vertical coordinate divided by change in horizontal coordinate: $\dfrac{3-(-1)}{1-(-1)}=\dfrac{4}{2}=\boxed{2}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw((-2,0)--(2,0),Arrow); draw((0,-2)--(0,5),Arrow); draw((-1.5,-2)--(2,5),blue); dot("$(1,3)$",(1,3),E); dot("$(-1,-1)$",(-1,-1),W); label("$x$",(2,0),S); label("$y$",(0,5),W);
\end{asy}
