A closed bottle in the shape of a right circular cylinder with radius $4$ cm and height $20$ cm is half full of water. The bottle is tilted so that its axis makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the vertical. What is the area of the elliptical water surface inside the bottle? Answer in terms of $\pi$.
