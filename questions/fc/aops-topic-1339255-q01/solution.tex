We need $(a-b)(a+b)=2016$ with $0<a-b<a+b$. The two factors $a-b$ and $a+b$ have the same parity (their sum $2a$ is even). Their product $2016$ is even, so they cannot both be odd; hence both are even. Write $a-b=2u$ and $a+b=2v$ with $u<v$ positive integers. Then $uv=504$, and conversely every factorisation $504=uv$ with $u<v$ gives $a=u+v$, $b=v-u>0$.

Since $504=2^3\cdot3^2\cdot7$ has $(3+1)(2+1)(1+1)=24$ divisors and is not a perfect square, its divisors pair up into $12$ factorisations $uv$ with $u<v$. So there are $\boxed{12}$ ways. (For example $u=1,v=504$ gives $505^2-503^2$, and $u=21,v=24$ gives $45^2-3^2$.)
