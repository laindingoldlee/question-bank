The point $a+bi$ is $(a,b)$ in the plane, so by Pythagoras $|a+bi|=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}$. Hence $|5i|=\boxed{5}$, $|-3+4i|=\sqrt{9+16}=\boxed{5}$ and $|1-i|=\boxed{\sqrt2}$.
