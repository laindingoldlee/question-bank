For every real $t$, $\sqrt{t^2}=|t|$. Since $2\sqrt2=\sqrt8<\sqrt{25}=5$, both $5-2\sqrt2$ and $5+2\sqrt2$ are positive, so the sum is
\[\left(5-2\sqrt2\right)+\left(5+2\sqrt2\right)=\boxed{10}.\]
