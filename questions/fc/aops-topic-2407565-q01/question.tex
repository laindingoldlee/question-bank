On a $25$-question test, each correct answer scores $6$ points and each wrong answer scores $0$ points, and Sam answers every question. Sam answers each of the first $10$ questions correctly for certain, each of the next $10$ correctly with probability $0.8$, and each of the last $5$ correctly with probability $0.5$, all independently. The cutoff score for a prize is equally likely to be $138$ or $144$, independently of Sam's answers. To the nearest thousandth, what is the probability that Sam's score is at least the cutoff?
