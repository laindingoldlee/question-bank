The difference between the two exclusive areas equals the difference between the full areas, because the overlap cancels. The equilateral triangle has circumradius $AO=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}$, so its area is $\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{4} AO^2=\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{8}$. Therefore $x=1-\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{8}=\dfrac{1}{8}(8-\sqrt{27})$, giving $\boxed{35}$.
