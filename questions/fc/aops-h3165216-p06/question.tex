Let $ABCD$ be a unit square with center $O$, and $\vartriangle OEF$ be an equilateral triangle with center $A$. Suppose that $M$ is the area of the region inside the square but outside the triangle and $N$ is the area of the region inside the triangle but outside the square, and let $x = |M -N|$ be the positive difference between $M$ and $N$. If $$x =\dfrac{1}{8}(p -\sqrt{q})$$for positive integers $p$ and $q$, find $p + q$.
