For residues 0,1,2,3,4 modulo 5, the least representable amounts are respectively $0,46,52,23,29$. To verify minimality, for amounts below 52 enumerate the possibilities with no 5 s: $0,23,46,29$; the first residue 2 appears at $23+29=52$. Adding 5 then represents every larger number with each corresponding residue. Thus the greatest missing value is the maximum of these five minima minus 5, namely $\boxed{47}$.
