A cube divisible by $1000=10^3$ is $(10k)^3$. The next cube is $(10k+1)^3$, so
\[(10k+1)^3-(10k)^3=300k^2+30k+1=4921\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 10k^2+k=164\quad\Longrightarrow\quad k=4.\]
The mileage is $40^3=\boxed{64000}$.
