Label the grid
\[\begin{array}{ccc}a&b&c\\ d&e&f\\ g&h&i\end{array}\]
with $e=0$. Add the four lines through the centre: the middle row, the middle column and the two diagonals:
\[(d+e+f)+(b+e+h)+(a+e+i)+(c+e+g)=4S.\]
The left side contains each of the eight outer squares once and the centre four times, so it equals (sum of all nine numbers)$\,+\,3e$. The sum of all nine numbers is the sum of the three rows, $3S$. Hence
\[4S=3S+3e,\qquad\text{so}\qquad S=3e=0.\ \blacksquare\]
(In general, the common sum is always three times the centre entry.)
