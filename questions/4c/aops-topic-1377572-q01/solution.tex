Let $x=a^2$ and $y=b^2$ with $a,b>0$, so $\sqrt{xy}=ab$. The equations become
\[
a^4+ab^3=a(a^3+b^3)=336,\qquad b^4+a^3b=b(a^3+b^3)=112 .
\]
Dividing, $\dfrac {a}{b}=3$, so $a=3b$. Then $b(27b^3+b^3)=28b^4=112$, so $b^4=4$, $y=b^2=2$ and $x=a^2=9b^2=18$. Hence $x+y=\boxed{20}$.

Check: $18^2+2\sqrt{36}=324+12=336$ and $2^2+18\sqrt{36}=4+108=112$.
