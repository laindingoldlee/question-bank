Form the complement graph: two members are adjacent when they have not played each other. Suppose it has no isolated vertex. Take a spanning tree in each connected component and bipartition each tree by even and odd distance from a root. Choose the smaller part in every component. The resulting set $S$ has at most $\left\lfloor\dfrac{2023}{2}\right\rfloor=1011$ vertices, and every vertex outside $S$ has a tree neighbour in $S$ (each tree has at least two vertices). Enlarge $S$ to size 1011 if needed. Every vertex still outside it has a complement-graph neighbour in $S$, so no such member has played every member of $S$. This contradicts the hypothesis. Hence the complement has an isolated vertex, corresponding to a member who played all the others.
