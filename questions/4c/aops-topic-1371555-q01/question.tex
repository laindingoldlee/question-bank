The average of $14$ consecutive even numbers is $119$. What is the smallest of the $14$ numbers?

A student writes the numbers as $x,x+2,\dots$, but lists $x,x+2,\dots,x+14,x+18,\dots,x+28$ and gets a non-integer answer. Find the correct answer and the student's mistake.
