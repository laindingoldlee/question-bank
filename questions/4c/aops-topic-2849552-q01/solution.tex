Factor: $\dfrac{2^{2008}(4+1)}{2^{2003}(4-1)}=2^5\cdot\dfrac53=\boxed{\dfrac{160}3}$.

Identity: $\dfrac{2^{x-y}+1}{2^{x-y}}\cdot2^x=(2^{x-y}+1)2^y=2^x+2^y$. (More generally $a^y+a^z=\dfrac{a^{y-z}+1}{a^{y-z}}\cdot a^y$ for $a\ne0$; the post's version with $a(y-z)$ in the denominator is a typo.)
