The odd part of $(2^m)!$ equals $F(2^m)$ times the odd part of $(2^{m-1})!$: remove a factor $2$ from each even factor, and the remaining even-factor product becomes $(2^{m-1})!$. Iterating yields odd part $\prod_{j=2}^{m}F(2^j)$, since $F(2)=1$. In the entire product $N$, the factor $F(2^j)$ occurs for $m=j,j+1,\ldots,1000$, hence $\boxed{b_j=1001-j}$. Every exponent is nonzero. The product of the distinct arguments is $2^{2+3+\cdots+1000}=2^{500499}$, whose divisor count is $\boxed{500500}$.
