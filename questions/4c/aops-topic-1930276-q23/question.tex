Here $\lfloor t\rfloor$ is the greatest integer not exceeding $t$ and $\{t\}=t-\lfloor t\rfloor$. Solve the system
\[
x+\lfloor y\rfloor+\{z\}=1.1,\qquad \lfloor x\rfloor+\{y\}+z=2.2,\qquad \{x\}+y+\lfloor z\rfloor=3.3 .
\]
