Successive divisions give $1031301=3^2\cdot114589=3^2\cdot19\cdot6031=3^2\cdot19\cdot37\cdot163$. Each factor is prime; for $163$, testing the primes $2,3,5,7,11$ suffices because $\sqrt{163}<13$. Hence the largest prime factor is $\boxed{163}$.
