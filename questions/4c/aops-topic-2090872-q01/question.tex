In triangle $ABC$, $\angle BAC=24^\circ$ and $\angle ACB=30^\circ$. Point $D$ lies on side $AC$ with $AB=CD$. Prove that
\[AB\cdot BC=BD\cdot AC .\]
(A proof without trigonometry is possible.)
