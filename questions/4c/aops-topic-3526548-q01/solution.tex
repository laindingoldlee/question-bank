Write each number uniquely as $dm^2$ with $d$ squarefree. Two numbers have square product precisely when their squarefree parts agree. The squarefree part $d=1$ occurs six times, $d=2$ four times, and $d=3$ three times. Parts $5,6,7$ occur twice each. Every other squarefree part at most $36$ exceeds $9$, so occurs only once. Therefore the count is $\binom62+\binom42+\binom32+3\binom22=15+6+3+3=\boxed{27}$.
