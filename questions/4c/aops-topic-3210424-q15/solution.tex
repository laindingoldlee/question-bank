Unfold the reflections. The displacement is $(60\cos\theta,60\sin\theta)$. Folding each coordinate back to the interval $[-1,1]$ maps even integers to 0. The four axis directions therefore end at the centre. In each of the other eight directions one coordinate has magnitude 30 and folds to 0, while the other has magnitude $30\sqrt3$, between 51 and 52, and folds to magnitude $52-30\sqrt3$. The expectation is $\dfrac{8}{12}(52-30\sqrt3)=\boxed{\dfrac{104-60\sqrt3}{3}\text{ metres}}$.
