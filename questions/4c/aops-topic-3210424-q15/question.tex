A particle starts at the centre of a square of side 2 metres. Uniformly choose one of the 12 directions making angles $0,30,60,\ldots,330$ degrees with the positive horizontal axis. It travels at 1 metre per second and reflects specularly from the sides. Find its expected distance from the starting point after 60 seconds, in simplest radical form.
