Measure angles in degrees clockwise from 12. At $m$ minutes after 8:00 the minute hand is at $6m$ and the hour hand at $240+\dfrac {m}{2}$ (it moves $\dfrac{1}{2}$ degree per minute). They coincide when
\[
6m=240+\dfrac {m}{2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \dfrac{11m}{2}=240\quad\Longrightarrow\quad m=\dfrac{480}{11}=43.\overline{63}.
\]
So the hands overlap at about $\boxed{8{:}43.6}$ PM, i.e. $43.6$ minutes after 8 PM.
