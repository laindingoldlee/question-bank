Write $4p+1=(2m+1)^2$ with $m\ge1$. Then $4p=4m^2+4m$, so
\[p=m(m+1).\]
If $m\ge2$, then $m$ and $m+1$ are both at least $2$ and $p$ would be composite. So $m=1$ and $p=2$; indeed $4\cdot2+1=9=3^2$. Hence $p+1=\boxed{3}$.
