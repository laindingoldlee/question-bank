Since every permutation of the three vertices is a rotation or a reflection of the triangle, a coloring is determined by the \emph{multiset} of colors used on the three vertices. Count multisets of size $3$ from $4$ colors:
\begin{itemize}
\item all three the same: $4$;
\item two the same and one different: $4\cdot3=12$;
\item all different: $\dbinom43=4$.
\end{itemize}
The total is $4+12+4=\boxed{20}$ (equivalently $\dbinom{4+3-1}{3}=20$ by stars and bars).
