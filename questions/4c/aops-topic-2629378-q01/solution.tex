The digit sum is $24$, so $3$ divides it, and a quick check shows $7$ does too:
\[67208001=3\cdot22402667=3\cdot7\cdot3200381=3\cdot7\cdot23\cdot139147.\]
Now $139147$ is close to $373^2=139129$, which suggests Fermat's method: $374^2-139147=139876-139147=729=27^2$, so
\[139147=374^2-27^2=(374-27)(374+27)=347\cdot401.\]
Both factors are prime: $347$ has no prime factor up to $17$ (and $19^2>347$), and $401$ has none up to $19$ (and $20^2<401<21^2$). Hence
\[67208001=\boxed{3\cdot7\cdot23\cdot347\cdot401}.\]
