Let $D(i,j)$ and $C(i,j)$ be Devin's win probabilities from scores $(i,j)$ with Devin or Cowen respectively to flip. Boundaries are both $1$ if $i=3$ and both $0$ if $j=3$. Set $p=\dfrac{2}{3},q=\dfrac{1}{3}$. Then $D=pD(i+1,j)+qC$ and $C=pC(i,j+1)+qD$. Solving the pair gives $D=\dfrac{pD(i+1,j)+pqC(i,j+1)}{1-q^2}$ and the analogous swapped formula for $C$. Evaluating from the boundary inward gives $D(2,2)=\dfrac{3}{4}$, $D(1,1)=\dfrac{21}{32}$, and finally $\boxed{D(0,0)=\dfrac{315}{512}}$.
