Every multiple of $10$ is a multiple of $5$, so we just count the integers divisible by $5$ or $7$: there are $60$ multiples of $5$, $\left\lfloor\dfrac{300}{7}\right\rfloor=42$ multiples of $7$ and $\left\lfloor\dfrac{300}{35}\right\rfloor=8$ multiples of $35$. The answer is $60+42-8=\boxed{94}$.
