Write the terms as $\dfrac ar$, $a$, $ar$. Their product is $a^3=13824=24^3$, so $a=24$. Then $\dfrac{24}r+24+24r=104$, so $24r^2-80r+24=0$, i.e. $3r^2-10r+3=0$, with roots $r=3$ and $r=\dfrac13$. Either way the terms are $\boxed{8,\ 24,\ 72}$ (in one order or the other).
