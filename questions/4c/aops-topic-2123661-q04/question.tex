Betty has a full gallon of milk. She takes away $1$ fluid ounce, and an hour later she takes away $3$ pints. How much milk has Betty taken in total, and how much is left in the jug? (Use US units: $1$ gallon $=8$ pints and $1$ pint $=16$ fluid ounces.)
