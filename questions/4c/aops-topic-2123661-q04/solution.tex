One gallon is $8\cdot16=128$ fluid ounces, and $3$ pints are $3\cdot16=48$ fluid ounces. So Betty has taken
\[1+48=\boxed{49\text{ fluid ounces}},\]
and the jug still holds $128-49=\boxed{79\text{ fluid ounces}}$ (that is, $4$ pints and $15$ fluid ounces).
