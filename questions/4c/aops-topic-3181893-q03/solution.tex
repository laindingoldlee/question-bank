In a five-digit palindrome, outer positions occur in pairs, so the sole prime digit must be the middle digit. Keeping the initial 16, the next prime middle digit after 2 is 3, yielding 16361. The distance is 100 miles, so the average speed is $\boxed{50\text{ miles per hour}}$.
