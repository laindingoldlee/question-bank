Adding $1$ to both sides of the recursion gives
\[
a_{n+1}+1=3a_n+3=3(a_n+1).
\]
So $b_n=a_n+1$ satisfies $b_1=3$ and $b_{n+1}=3b_n$, a geometric sequence: $b_n=3^n$. Hence
\[
\boxed{a_n=3^n-1}.
\]
Check: $3-1=2$, $9-1=8$, $27-1=26$, $81-1=80$, $243-1=242$.
