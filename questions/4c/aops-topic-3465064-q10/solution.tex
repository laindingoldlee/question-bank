Let the common value be the integer $k$. Then $x=\dfrac{5}{2}k$, and the floor condition becomes $k\le\dfrac{5}{4}k<k+1$. The two inequalities give $0\le k<4$, so $k=0,1,2,3$. The largest solution is $\boxed{x=\dfrac{15}{2}}$.
