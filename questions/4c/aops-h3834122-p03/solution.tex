Modulo $49$, the binomial theorem gives $8^x=(1+7)^x\equiv 1+7x$ and $9^x=(2+7)^x\equiv 2^x+7x2^{x-1}$. The expression is therefore congruent to $7(x2^{x-1}-1)$. Divisibility by $49$ is equivalent to $x2^{x-1}\equiv 1\pmod 7$. Powers of $2$ modulo $7$ have period $3$. If $x\equiv 1\pmod 3$, then $x\equiv 1\pmod 7$, giving $x\equiv 1\pmod{21}$. If $x\equiv 2\pmod 3$, then $2x\equiv 1\pmod 7$, giving $x\equiv 11\pmod{21}$. If $x\equiv 0\pmod 3$, then $4x\equiv 1\pmod 7$, giving $x\equiv 9\pmod{21}$. Thus the complete answer is $\boxed{x\equiv 1,9,11\pmod{21},\ x>0}$.
