The discriminant is $\cot^2A\cos^2A+4>0$, so there are two real roots. By Vieta's formulas their sum is the negative of the coefficient of $x$:
\[\boxed{-\cot A\cos A=-\dfrac{\cos^2A}{\sin A}}.\]
