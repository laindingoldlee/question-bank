The cumulative distance after $ n $ full days is $ 15\dfrac{1.15^n-1}{0.15}=100(1.15^n-1)$. Completion requires $ 1.15^n\ge17 $. Now $ 1.15^{20}\approx16.3665<17 $ and $ 1.15^{21}\approx18.8215>17 $, so completion occurs during $\boxed{\text{day }21}$. The final day need not use its full planned distance.
