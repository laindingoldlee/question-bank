By definition $x$ is the exponent with $(\sqrt6)^x=216$. Since $\sqrt6=6^{\dfrac{1}{2}}$ and $216=6^3$,
\[
6^{\dfrac {x}{2}}=6^3\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad\dfrac {x}{2}=3\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad x=\boxed{6}.
\]
Check: $(\sqrt6)^6=6^3=216$.
