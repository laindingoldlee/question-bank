What is the value of
\[
\sqrt{1+2\sqrt{1+2\sqrt{1+2\sqrt{1+\cdots}}}}\ ?
\]
(The radicals continue forever; the value is the limit of the sequence $x_1=1$, $x_{n+1}=\sqrt{1+2x_n}$.) Express your answer in simplest radical form.
