Multiplying by $s-p$ and setting $s=p$ gives $A=\dfrac{1}{f}'(p)$; similarly $B=\dfrac{1}{f}'(q)$ and $C=\dfrac{1}{f}'(r)$. By Vieta,
\[
p+q+r=22,\qquad pq+pr+qr=80,
\]
so $p^2+q^2+r^2=22^2-160=324$. Since $f'(x)=3x^2-44x+80$,
\[
\dfrac{1}{A}+\dfrac{1}{B}+\dfrac{1}{C}
=3(324)-44(22)+3(80)=\boxed{244}.
\]
