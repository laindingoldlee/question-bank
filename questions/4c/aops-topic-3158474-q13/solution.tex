Every rectangle has sides at least $1$, so its perimeter is at least $4$. Cutting into unit squares attains average $4$. Each side of each rectangle is at most $2016$, so every perimeter is at most $8064$, and leaving the board uncut attains that average. Thus $\boxed{(m,M)=(4,8064)}$.
