A $2016\times2016$ board is partitioned along its grid lines into $k\ge1$ rectangles with positive integer side lengths. Let $p$ be the sum of their perimeters. Find the minimum and maximum possible values of $\dfrac{p}{k}$, allowing $k$ to vary.
