Multiply the numerator and the denominator by the conjugate $3+2i$:
\[\dfrac{8-i}{3-2i}=\dfrac{(8-i)(3+2i)}{(3-2i)(3+2i)}=\dfrac{24+16i-3i-2i^2}{9+4}=\dfrac{26+13i}{13}=2+i.\]
So $a=\boxed{2}$.
