The numbers $1,2,\dots,99$ are written around a circle in some order. Two numbers that are not neighbours divide the rest of the circle into two arcs. Call such a pair \emph{good} if both numbers are greater than every number on at least one of the two arcs. Prove that the number of good pairs does not depend on the order, and find it.
