The triangle is right, with area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot30\cdot40=600 $ and semiperimeter 60. Its largest inscribed disk is its incircle, whose radius is $ r=\dfrac{\Delta}{s}=\dfrac{600}{60}=\boxed{10}$.

\end{asy}
