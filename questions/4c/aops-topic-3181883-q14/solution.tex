Take $B=(0,0)$ and $A=(7,0)$. The distance equations give $C=(\dfrac{16}{7},\dfrac{24\sqrt5}{7})$. The center is $O=(0,k)$, and $OC^2=OB^2$ gives $64-2k\dfrac{24\sqrt5}{7}=0$, so $k=\dfrac{28}{3\sqrt5}$. Consequently $AO^2=49+\dfrac{784}{45}=\dfrac{49\cdot61}{45}$, and $AO=\dfrac{7\sqrt{61}}{3\sqrt5}$. The answer is $\boxed{76}$.
