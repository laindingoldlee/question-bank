Steve wants to fill a $4\times4$ magic square with the numbers $1,2,\dots,16$, each once, so that every row, column and both diagonals sum to $34$. He starts with $2$ and $3$ in the first two cells of the top row and $4$ in the first cell of the second row. Can he complete the square?
