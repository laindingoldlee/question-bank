Let the motorcycle be worth $M$ dollars. A full year's pay is $9000+M$, so $5$ months' pay is $\dfrac{5}{12}(9000+M)$. Hence
\[
\dfrac{5}{12}(9000+M)=250+M\iff 45000+5M=3000+12M\iff 7M=42000,
\]
so $M=\boxed{\$6000}$. (Check: $\dfrac{5}{12}\cdot15000=6250=250+6000$.)
