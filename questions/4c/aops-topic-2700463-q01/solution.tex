The sides are $6\cdot13$, $6\cdot14$, $6\cdot15$. Drop the altitude to the side $84$; it splits the side into $x$ and $84-x$ with $78^2-x^2=90^2-(84-x)^2$, giving $x=30$ and altitude $\sqrt{78^2-30^2}=72$. (These are the $5$-$12$-$13$ and $9$-$12$-$15$ right triangles scaled by $6$.) The area is $\dfrac12\cdot84\cdot72=\boxed{3024}$.

label("$78$",(A+B)/2,NW,fontsize(8pt));label("$90$",(A+C)/2,NE,fontsize(8pt));label("$30$",(B+H)/2,S,fontsize(8pt));label("$54$",(H+C)/2,S,fontsize(8pt));label("$72$",(A+H)/2,E,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}
