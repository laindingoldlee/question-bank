Square the given equation:
\[
\left(x+\dfrac{1}{x}\right)^2=x^2+2\cdot x\cdot\dfrac{1}{x}+\dfrac{1}{x^2}=x^2+2+\dfrac{1}{x^2}=169 .
\]
Hence $x^2+\dfrac{1}{x^2}=169-2=\boxed{167}$.
