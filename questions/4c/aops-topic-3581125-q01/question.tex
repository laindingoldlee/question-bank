In a probability game, S moves before P each turn. S chooses uniformly among its available moves: two attacks (each immediately defeats P), Protect (10 uses), and Wait (5 uses). Attacks remain available. The $(j+1)$st use of Protect succeeds with probability $3^{-j}$; failure has the same effect as Wait. P then gets one shot, which independently hits with probability $0.7$ and defeats S unless blocked. P has ten shots; if S survives all ten, S wins. (a) Find S's probability of surviving turn 1. (b) Find S's probability of winning the game; a recursively specified exact rational answer with its evaluated fraction is acceptable.
