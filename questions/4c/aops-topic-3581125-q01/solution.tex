(a) S survives by either attack (probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$), by successful first Protect ($\dfrac{1}{4}$), or by Wait followed by a miss ($\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot\dfrac{3}{10}$). The probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{33}{40}}$.
(b) Let $W(j,k)$ be the conditional win probability after $j$ Protect uses and $k$ Wait uses, when both players remain. Then $j+k$ shots have elapsed. Set $W(j,k)=1$ for $j+k=10$. Otherwise let $m=2+\mathbf1_{j<10}+\mathbf1_{k<5}$. Conditioning on the next move gives
\[W(j,k)=\dfrac{2}{m}+\dfrac{\mathbf1_{j<10}}{m}\left(3^{-j}+(1-3^{-j})\dfrac{3}{10}\right)W(j+1,k)+\dfrac{\mathbf1_{k<5}}{m}\dfrac{3}{10}W(j,k+1).\]
Unavailable-move terms are omitted. Evaluating backward by decreasing $j+k$ in exact fractions gives
\[\boxed{W(0,0)=\dfrac{429610283696607971137623141562961}{605043242301610741482086400000000}\approx0.710048892}.\]
Every nonterminal branch increases $j+k$, so this finite recurrence determines the answer uniquely.
