Let the intercepted arcs $ AD,BC $ have degree measures $ u,v $. The external-secant angle gives $ u-v=68 $, while the intersecting-chord angle gives $ u+v=132 $. Hence $ u=100 $. With radius 45, the requested arc length is $ 45\cdot\dfrac{100\pi}{180}=25\pi $, so $\boxed{k=25}$.
