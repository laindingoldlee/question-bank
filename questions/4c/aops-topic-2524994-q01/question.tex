In arithmetic modulo $7$, the expression $\dfrac52\pmod 7$ means $5\cdot2^{-1}\pmod7$, where $2^{-1}$ is the inverse of $2$ modulo $7$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find $2^{-1}\pmod7$ and compute $5\cdot2^{-1}\pmod7$.
\item[(b)] Check that the result $r$ is the only residue modulo $7$ with $2r\equiv5\pmod7$.
\end{enumerate}
