Put $t=\sqrt[3]x$. Then $t^7+t^4=t$, or $t(t^6+t^3-1)=0$. Hence either $t=0$, giving $x=0$, or $x^2+x-1=0$. All transformations are reversible over the reals, so $\boxed{x\in\{0,\dfrac{-1+\sqrt5}{2},\dfrac{-1-\sqrt5}{2}\}}$.
