Regular tetrahedron $ABCD$ has edge length $6$. Let $r$ be the radius of the sphere inscribed in the tetrahedron, and $O$ its centre.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] In terms of $r$, what is the volume of tetrahedron $OABC$?
\item[(b)] In terms of $r$, what are the volumes of tetrahedra $OABD$, $OACD$ and $OBCD$?
\item[(c)] Find $r$.
\end{enumerate}
