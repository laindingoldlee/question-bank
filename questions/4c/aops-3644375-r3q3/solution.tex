The equilateral altitude is three times its inradius, so $h=15$ and the new longest altitude is 20. This altitude must be to the unequal base: if it were to an equal leg $l$, area $10l=5(l+\dfrac{b}{2})$ would force base $b=2l$, a degenerate triangle. Let the half-base be $a$. Then $20a=5(a+\sqrt{a^2+400})$, giving $3a=\sqrt{a^2+400}$ and $a=5\sqrt2$. The area is $100\sqrt2$, so $m+n=\boxed{102}$.
