Fix one child $X$. Only three children can poke $X$: the left neighbour, $X$ itself and the right neighbour. Each of them avoids $X$ with probability $\dfrac{2}{3}$ (the left neighbour pokes $X$ only by choosing ``right'', $X$ only by choosing ``self'', the right neighbour only by choosing ``left''), and their choices are independent. So
\[
P(X\text{ is not poked})=\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^3=\dfrac{8}{27}.
\]
Let $I_k$ be $1$ if child $k$ is not poked and $0$ otherwise. By linearity of expectation (which holds even though the $I_k$ are dependent), the expected number of unpoked children is
\[
\sum_{k=1}^{2018}E[I_k]=2018\cdot\dfrac{8}{27}=\boxed{\dfrac{16144}{27}}\approx597.9 .
\]
