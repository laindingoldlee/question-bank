Let $N=2025$. The number of lit bulbs is a birth-death chain with upward probability $\dfrac{(N-k)}{N}$ at state $k$. Solving its mean hitting-time recurrence gives $E=\dfrac{\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}[\sum_{j=0}^k\binom Nj]}{\binom{N-1}k}$. Pair complementary terms to obtain $E=\dfrac{2^{N-1}\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}1}{\binom{N-1}k}$. The reciprocal sum is at least two and less than three, since each interior binomial coefficient is at least $N-1$. Hence $2^{2025}\le E<(\dfrac{3}{2})2^{2025}$. Taking base-ten logs bounds it between 609.585 and 609.762, so the required floor is $\boxed{609}$.
