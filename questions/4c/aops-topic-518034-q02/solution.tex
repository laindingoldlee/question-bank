We need $b\mid 2012-2=2010$ and $b>2$ (so that $2$ is a digit). The divisors of $2010=2\cdot3\cdot5\cdot67$ are $1,2,3,5,6,10,15,30,67,134,201,335,402,670,1005,2010$; removing $1$ and $2$ leaves $\boxed{14}$ bases.
