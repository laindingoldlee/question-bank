Ebenezer Scrooge will take a whole number of dollars, less than $1000$, to give away outside the church. He wants to give all of it away with every homeless person receiving the same whole number of dollars, but he does not know how many people will come. To make this possible for as many different numbers of people as possible, how many dollars should he take?
