Write the digits as $a,a+d,a+2d$, with $1\le a\le9$ and all digits between 0 and 9. Their sum is $3(a+d)$, so every such number is divisible by 3. It fails divisibility by 6 exactly when its units digit, hence $a$, is odd. The complete set is obtained by $a\in\{1,3,5,7,9\}$ and $\lceil-\dfrac{a}{2}\rceil\le d\le\lfloor\dfrac{9-a}{2}\rfloor$. Explicitly it is $\boxed{111,123,135,147,159,321,333,345,357,369,531,543,555,567,579,741,753,765,777,789,951,963,975,987,999}$.
