There are $\binom{10}{4}=210$ paths without restrictions. Those through $(2,3)$ number $\binom52\binom51=50$, and those through $(5,4)$ number $\binom94=126$. Paths through both must visit them in that order and number $\binom52\binom41=40$. Inclusion-exclusion gives $\boxed{210-50-126+40=74}$.
