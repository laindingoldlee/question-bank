Two ratios form a proportion when they are equal after both are written in lowest terms (each ratio compares quantities of the same kind in the same unit).

\textbf{(i)} $1$ m $=100$ cm, so $25\text{ cm}:1\text{ m}=25:100=1:4$, and $40:160=1:4$. The ratios are equal, so $\boxed{\text{yes}}$, they form a proportion.

\textbf{(ii)} $39:65=3:5$ (divide by $13$) and $6:10=3:5$ (divide by $2$). The ratios are equal, so $\boxed{\text{yes}}$, they form a proportion.
