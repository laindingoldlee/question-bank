The numbers between $15$ and $30$ that leave remainder $6$ on division by $7$ are $20$ and $27$. Of these, $20=2\cdot8+4$ leaves remainder $4$ on division by $8$, while $27=3\cdot8+3$ leaves remainder $3$. So $x=\boxed{27}$.
