Carmela wrote in her notebook the names and ages of people in a community. Assume the ages are all integers which are at least 1 and at most 60.



Farrell, Kyle, and Albert looked at the notebook and made reports. Each person’s report shows the number of people whose ages belong to the indicated intervals.

\begin{itemize}

\item Farrell's report: $[1,3],[4,6],\dots,[58,60]$

\item Kyle's report: $[1,4],[5,8],\dots,[57,60]$

\item Albert’s report: $[1,5],[6,10],\dots,[56,60]$

\end{itemize}



Andres does not have access to Carmela’s notebook but he has copies of the three reports. An age is said to be universally detectable if, for every possible community population, Andres can figure it out, based on these reports, how many people have that age. Determine all universally detectable ages.
