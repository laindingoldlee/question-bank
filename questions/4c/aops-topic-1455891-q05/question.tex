This is a relay: in each part, $T$ denotes the answer to the previous part.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Three people take turns rolling a fair die: the first person, then the second, then the third, and then the first again, and so on. The first person to roll a six wins, and the game stops. What is the probability that the third person wins?
\item What is $T\cdot91^2$?
\item How many factors of $5$ are there in the prime factorisation of $T$ (that is, what is the exponent of $5$)?
\item How many distinct prime factors does $T$ have?
\item What is the $T$th smallest positive integer that leaves a remainder of $7$ when divided by $11$ and also leaves a remainder of $7$ when divided by $13$?
\end{enumerate}
