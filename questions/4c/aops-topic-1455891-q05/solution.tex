\begin{enumerate}
\item In each round of three rolls, the third person wins in that round with probability $\left(\dfrac{5}{6}\right)^2\dfrac{1}{6}=\dfrac{25}{216}$, and the round has no six with probability $\left(\dfrac{5}{6}\right)^3=\dfrac{125}{216}$. Summing the geometric series over the rounds,
\[
T=\dfrac{\dfrac{25}{216}}{1-\dfrac{125}{216}}=\dfrac{25}{91}.
\]
\item $\dfrac{25}{91}\cdot91^2=25\cdot91=2275$.
\item $2275=5^2\cdot7\cdot13$, so the exponent of $5$ is $2$.
\item $T=2$ has one prime factor, so the answer is $1$.
\item A number leaves remainder $7$ when divided by $11$ and by $13$ exactly when it is $7$ more than a multiple of $\operatorname{lcm}(11,13)=143$. The positive ones are $7,150,293,\dots$; the first is $\boxed{7}$.
\end{enumerate}
Answers: $\dfrac{25}{91},\ 2275,\ 2,\ 1,\ 7$.
