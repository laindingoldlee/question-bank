Simplify the denominator first: $\sqrt{20}=2\sqrt5$, $\sqrt{40}=2\sqrt{10}$ and $\sqrt{80}=4\sqrt5$, so it equals
\[
\sqrt{10}+2\sqrt5+2\sqrt{10}-\sqrt5-4\sqrt5=3\sqrt{10}-3\sqrt5=3\sqrt5\left(\sqrt2-1\right).
\]
Hence
\[
\dfrac{15}{3\sqrt5\left(\sqrt2-1\right)}=\dfrac{\sqrt5}{\sqrt2-1}=\dfrac{\sqrt5\left(\sqrt2+1\right)}{2-1}=\boxed{\sqrt{10}+\sqrt5}.
\]
