Let the chosen integers be $a_1<a_2<\dots<a_6$ and let $d$ be the smallest difference between two of them. The smallest difference is attained by two neighbours in this order, so each of the five gaps $a_{i+1}-a_i$ is at least $d$. Hence
\[
99\ge a_6-a_1=(a_2-a_1)+(a_3-a_2)+\dots+(a_6-a_5)\ge5d,
\]
so $d\le\dfrac{99}{5}$, that is, $d\le19$.

The value $19$ is achieved by $1,20,39,58,77,96$, where every two neighbours differ by $19$. So the largest possible smallest difference is $\boxed{19}$.
