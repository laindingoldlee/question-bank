The positive powers of $2$ that are digits are $2$, $4$ and $8$. Check the multiples of $64$ in order:
\[
64,\ 128,\ 192,\ 256,\ 320,\ 384,\ 448 .
\]
Each of the first six contains a digit not in $\{2,4,8\}$ (namely $6$, $1$, $1$, $5$, $3$, $3$), while $448=7\cdot64$ uses only $4$ and $8$. The answer is $\boxed{448}$.
