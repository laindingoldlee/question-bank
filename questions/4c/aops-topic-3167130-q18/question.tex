A frog starts on pad $1$ and wants to reach pad $7$. On each jump it chooses uniformly among moves forward by $1$ or $2$ that do not exceed $7$. Landing on pad $2$ or $5$ immediately returns it to pad $1$; that attempted jump still counts. Find the expected total number of jumps to reach $7$.
