Let $E_j$ be the expected remaining jumps from safe pad $j$. We have $E_7=0$, $E_6=1$, $E_4=1+\dfrac{E_1+E_6}{2}=\dfrac{3}{2}+\dfrac{E_1}{2}$, and $E_3=1+\dfrac{E_4+E_1}{2}=\dfrac{7}{4}+\dfrac{3E_1}{4}$. At pad $1$, $E_1=1+\dfrac{E_1+E_3}{2}$, so $E_1=2+E_3=\dfrac{15}{4}+\dfrac{3E_1}{4}$. Thus $\boxed{E_1=15}$. The expectation is finite because the safe sequence $1,3,4,6,7$ has fixed positive probability on every restart.
