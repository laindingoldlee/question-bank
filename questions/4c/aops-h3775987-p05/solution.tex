The two distances make the vector from $P$ to opposite corner $C$ have perpendicular components $3,4$, so $PC=5$. Since $AP=10$, the point divides diagonal $AC$ in ratio $2:1$. Thus the full side lengths are three times the two remaining coordinate distances, namely $9$ and $12$. The rectangle area is $\boxed{108}$.
