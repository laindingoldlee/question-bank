\begin{enumerate}
\item Of the $8$ equally likely outcomes, $7$ are not all heads, and one of these is all tails. The probability is $T=\dfrac{1}{7}$.
\item $T^{-1}=7$ and $7!=5040$.
\item $(2\cdot5040)^2=10080^2=101606400$.
\item $\pi\rho^2=101606400$ gives $\rho=\sqrt{\dfrac{101606400}{\pi}}=\dfrac{10080}{\sqrt{\pi}}=\dfrac{10080\sqrt{\pi}}{\pi}$.
\item $\log_xT=1$ means $x^1=T$ (with $x>0$, $x\ne1$), so $x=T=\boxed{\dfrac{10080}{\sqrt{\pi}}}\approx5687$.
\end{enumerate}
Answers: $\dfrac{1}{7},\ 5040,\ 101606400,\ \dfrac{10080}{\sqrt{\pi}},\ \dfrac{10080}{\sqrt{\pi}}$.
