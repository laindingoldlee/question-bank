This is a relay: in each part, $T$ denotes the answer to the previous part.
\begin{enumerate}
\item A fair coin is flipped three times. Given that the three flips are not all heads, what is the probability that they are all tails?
\item What is $\left(T^{-1}\right)!$?
\item Multiply $T$ by $2$ and square the result. What do you get?
\item What is the radius of a circle with area $T$?
\item Find $x$ if $\log_x T=1$.
\end{enumerate}
