A power of 2 is odd exactly when its exponent is 0. The sum is odd precisely when exactly one exponent is 0, since three zeroes cannot sum to 18. Choose that exponent in 3 ways, then split 18 into two positive parts in 17 ways, giving 51 favourable triples. Stars and bars gives $\binom{20}{2}=190 $ total triples. Thus the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{51}{190}}$.
