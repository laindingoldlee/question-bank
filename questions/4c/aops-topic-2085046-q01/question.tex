Solve \(2x^2+4x-1=0\). Completing the square gives \(x=-1\pm\sqrt{\dfrac{3}{2}}\), while the quadratic formula gives \(x=\dfrac{-2\pm\sqrt6}{2}\). Show that these answers agree.
