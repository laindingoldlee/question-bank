If $F$ is the foot of the perpendicular from $C$ to $\ell$, then $\angle BFC=90^\circ$ (or $F=B$ when $\ell\perp BC$), so $F$ lies on the circle with diameter $BC$. Conversely every point of that circle is such a foot. Since $BC^2=2^2+14^2=200$, the circle has radius $\sqrt{50}$ and $\mathcal A=50\pi$. Then $4\mathcal A=200\pi\approx628.3$, and the answer is $\boxed{628}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(20,14),C=(18,0),Mc=(B+C)/2;real rr=abs(B-C)/2;
draw(circle(Mc,rr),linewidth(0.8));dot(B);dot(C);
pair d=dir(200);pair Fp=B+dot(C-B,d)*d;draw(B-1.2*rr*d--B+3.2*rr*d,gray);draw(C--Fp,dashed);dot(Fp,red);
label("$B$",B,NE);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$\ell$",B+3.2*rr*d,S);
\end{asy}
