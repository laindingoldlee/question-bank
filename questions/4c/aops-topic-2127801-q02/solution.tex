Count the numbers \emph{without} a digit $7$ and subtract.

\textbf{From $1$ to $1999$.} Write the numbers $0,1,\dots,1999$ with four digits, using leading zeros. A number with no $7$ has thousands digit $0$ or $1$ ($2$ choices) and each of the other three digits chosen from the $9$ digits other than $7$. That gives $2\cdot9^3=1458$ strings, including $0000$, so $1457$ of the numbers $1,\dots,1999$ have no $7$.

\textbf{From $2000$ to $2020$.} Of these $21$ numbers only $2007$ and $2017$ contain a $7$, so $19$ have none.

So $1457+19=1476$ of the first $2020$ positive integers have no $7$, and
\[2020-1476=\boxed{544}\]
contain at least one $7$.
