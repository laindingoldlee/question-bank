A robber has just robbed the bank at $0$ on the number line. Two guards stand at $3$ and $5$. Because of the rain only one guard is on duty at any time: they take turns, switching every minute, and which of them is on duty during the first minute is decided by a fair coin. At the end of each minute the robber is at an integer; he is caught if, at the end of some minute, he is at the position of the guard on duty during that minute, and he has got past the guards once he reaches $6$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Every minute the robber moves up one space. What is the probability that he gets past the guards?
\item[(b)] Instead, every minute the robber flips a fair coin: on heads he moves up one space, and on tails he stays where he is. What is the probability that he gets past the guards now?
\end{enumerate}
