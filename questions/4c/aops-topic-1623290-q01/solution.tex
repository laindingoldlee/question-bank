Let $t$ hours be the time from leaving until 4 pm and $d$ km the distance. Then $d=38(t+1)=57(t-1)$, so $38t+38=57t-57$, $19t=95$ and $t=5$. The distance is $d=38\cdot6=228$ km, and the required speed is
\[
\dfrac{228}{5}=\boxed{45.6\text{ km per hour}}.
\]
