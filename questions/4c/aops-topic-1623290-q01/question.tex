Scott is leaving his office to attend a meeting that begins at 4 pm. If he drives at $38$ km per hour, he will arrive one hour late. If he drives at $57$ km per hour, he will arrive one hour early. At what average speed must he drive to arrive at exactly 4 pm? Express your answer as a decimal to the nearest tenth.
