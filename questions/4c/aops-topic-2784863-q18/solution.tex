Exclude $ x=-2 $. Multiplying by the positive denominator yields $(x+1)^2\ge2(x+2)^2 $, or $(x+3)^2\le2 $. The integral candidates are $-4,-3,-2 $; excluding the undefined value gives $\boxed{x=-4,-3}$.
