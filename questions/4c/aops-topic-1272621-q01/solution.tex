By Burnside's lemma, a rotation by $k$ sections fixes $2^{\gcd(k,8)}$ colourings. Averaging over $k=0,\dots,7$:
\[
\dfrac{2^8+2^1+2^2+2^1+2^4+2^1+2^2+2^1}{8}=\dfrac{256+2+4+2+16+2+4+2}{8}=\dfrac{288}{8}=\boxed{36}.
\]
