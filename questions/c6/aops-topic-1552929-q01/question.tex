A Greek entered a marketplace selling apples, oranges and bananas. The price of an apple was $1$ dollar more than the difference between the price of $3$ bananas and the price of $2$ oranges ($3$ bananas cost more than $2$ oranges). Each orange cost $2.75$ dollars. The Greek bought $1$ apple, $2$ oranges and $4$ bananas and paid $22$ dollars. What was the price of the apple?
