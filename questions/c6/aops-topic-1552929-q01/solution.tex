Let a banana cost $b$ dollars. Two oranges cost $5.50$, so an apple costs $1+(3b-5.5)=3b-4.5$. The purchase gives
\[
(3b-4.5)+5.5+4b=22\quad\Longrightarrow\quad7b+1=22\quad\Longrightarrow\quad b=3 .
\]
So a banana costs $3$ dollars, $3$ bananas ($9$ dollars) cost more than $2$ oranges, as stated, and the apple cost $3\cdot3-4.5=\boxed{4.50\text{ dollars}}$. Check: $4.50+5.50+12=22$.
