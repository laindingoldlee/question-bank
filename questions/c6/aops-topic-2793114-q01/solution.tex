$A$ cannot tell the truth, since the claim would then contradict itself; hence $A$ is a liar and at least one other person tells the truth. If $B$ were a liar, its statement would be false. Since $A,B$ would both be liars, $C$ would have to be truthful, making exactly one truthful and contradicting $B$ being a liar. Thus $B$ tells the truth and is the only truth-teller. Therefore $A,C$ are liars and $B$ is truthful.
