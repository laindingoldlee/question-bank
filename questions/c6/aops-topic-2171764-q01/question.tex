Mehta has $30$ sticks, one of each length $1,2,3,\ldots,30$ feet. He takes the sticks of lengths $3$, $7$ and $15$ feet and wants to choose one of the remaining sticks so that the four sticks form a quadrilateral with positive area. How many of the remaining $27$ sticks can he choose?
