Each congruence says $x\equiv-1$: $x+1$ is divisible by $2,3,4,5,6$ and $7$, i.e. by
\[\operatorname{lcm}(2,3,4,5,6,7)=420 .\]
So the solutions are $x\equiv-1\pmod{420}$, that is $\boxed{x=420k+419}$ for integers $k$; the smallest positive solution is $419$.
