The number of trailing zeros is the exponent of $10$ in $2006!$. There are many more factors $2$ than factors $5$, so it equals the exponent of $5$, which by Legendre's formula is
\[
\left\lfloor\dfrac{2006}{5}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{2006}{25}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{2006}{125}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{2006}{625}\right\rfloor=401+80+16+3=500 .
\]
(The multiples of $5$ each give one factor $5$, the multiples of $25$ one more, and so on; $5^5=3125>2006$.) So $Z(2006)=\boxed{500}$.
