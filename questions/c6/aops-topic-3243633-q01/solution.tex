The smaller annulus has area $3\pi$, while the sector has area $\dfrac{\theta}{360}\cdot5\pi$. Their sum is half of $9\pi$, so $3+\dfrac{5\theta}{360}=\dfrac{9}{2}$. Therefore $\boxed{\theta=108^\circ}$.

\end{asy}
