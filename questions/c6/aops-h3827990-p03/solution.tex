For a prescribed value $t$, the equation $A=t$ is
\[
x^2-2tx+8t-15=0.
\]
Its discriminant is $4(t-3)(t-5)$, so real roots exist exactly when $t\le3$ or $t\ge5$. No such root equals $4$, because substituting $x=4$ into this quadratic gives $1$, independently of $t$. Therefore the exact range is
\[
\boxed{(-\infty,3]\cup[5,\infty)},
\]
choice $\boxed{\text{C}}$.
