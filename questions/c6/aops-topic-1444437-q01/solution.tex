Put the points on a number line with $A=0$. Each step $A\to B\to C\to D\to E\to F\to A$ moves left or right by the given length, and after the six steps we are back at $A$. So we need signs with
\[
\pm2\pm3\pm5\pm7\pm8\pm9=0 .
\]
The six lengths add up to $34$, so the lengths taken with a minus sign must add up to $17$. The subsets of $\{2,3,5,7,8,9\}$ with sum $17$ are
\[
\{8,9\},\ \{2,7,8\},\ \{3,5,9\},\ \{2,3,5,7\},
\]
where $\{2,3,5,7\}$ is the complement of $\{8,9\}$ and $\{2,7,8\}$ is the complement of $\{3,5,9\}$; complementary choices give mirror-image arrangements with the same distances. So up to reflection there are two cases.
\begin{itemize}
\item Minus signs on $8$ and $9$ ($E\to F$ and $F\to A$ go left): $A=0$, $B=2$, $C=5$, $D=10$, $E=17$, $F=9$. All six points are different, and the farthest points are $A$ and $E$, at distance $17$.
\item Minus signs on $3$, $5$ and $9$ ($B\to C$, $C\to D$ and $F\to A$ go left): $A=0$, $B=2$, $C=-1$, $D=-6$, $E=1$, $F=9$. All six points are different, and the farthest points are $D$ and $F$, at distance $15$.
\end{itemize}
So the distance between the farthest points is $\boxed{17\text{ cm or }15\text{ cm}}$; the data do not determine which.
