Six different points $A,B,C,D,E,F$ lie on a straight line, not necessarily in this order. We know that
\[
AB=2,\quad BC=3,\quad CD=5,\quad DE=7,\quad EF=8,\quad FA=9
\]
(in cm). Find all possible values of the distance between the two points that are farthest apart.
