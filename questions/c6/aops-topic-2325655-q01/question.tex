Let $N$ be the $162$-digit number consisting of $71$ ones, then one $0$, then $18$ nines, then $69$ eights, and finally the digits $9,7,6$:
\[N=\underbrace{11\ldots1}_{71}0\underbrace{99\ldots9}_{18}\underbrace{88\ldots8}_{69}976.\]
Find the remainder when $N$ is divided by $2331$, without a computer.
