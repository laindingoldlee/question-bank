A week has $7$ days. If Foysal is $F$ years old and his mother $M$ years old, then his age in days is $365F$ (approximately; the length of the year cancels) and her age in weeks is $\dfrac{365M}{7}$. Setting these equal gives $M=7F$. Then $F+7F=104$, so $F=13$, $M=91$, and Foysal is $91-13=\boxed{78}$ years younger.
