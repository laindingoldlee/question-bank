The integers $1,2,3,\dots,1000$ are written in order around a circle. Starting at $1$, someone marks $1$, then $13$, $25$, $37$, \dots, that is, every $12$th number, going around the circle again and again. The process stops when a number that is already marked would be marked again. How many of the numbers are never marked?
