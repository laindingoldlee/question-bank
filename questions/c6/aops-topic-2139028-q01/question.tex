Points $A$, $C$, $B$ lie on a line in this order, and semicircles with diameters $AB$ and $AC$ are drawn on the same side of the line. A chord $MN$ of the larger semicircle is parallel to $AB$ and tangent to the smaller semicircle, and $MN=8$. Find the area of the shaded region, which lies inside the larger semicircle and outside the smaller one.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0), C=(6,0), B=(10,0);
fill(arc((5,0),5,0,180)--cycle,paleblue); fill(arc((3,0),3,0,180)--cycle,white);
draw(arc((5,0),5,0,180),linewidth(0.9)); draw(arc((3,0),3,0,180),linewidth(0.9));
draw(A--B,linewidth(0.9));
pair M=(1,3), Nn=(9,3);
draw(M--Nn,red+linewidth(0.9)); dot(M); dot(Nn);
label("$A$",A,SW); label("$C$",C,S); label("$B$",B,SE); label("$M$",M,NW); label("$N$",Nn,NE);
label("$8$",(7,3),N,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
