Let $R$ and $r$ be the radii of the larger and smaller semicircles, $O$ the centre of the larger one, and $T$ the point where $MN$ touches the smaller semicircle.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0), C=(6,0), B=(10,0), O=(5,0), Q=(3,0);
fill(arc(O,5,0,180)--cycle,paleblue); fill(arc(Q,3,0,180)--cycle,white);
draw(arc(O,5,0,180),linewidth(0.9)); draw(arc(Q,3,0,180),linewidth(0.9));
draw(A--B,linewidth(0.9));
pair M=(1,3), Nn=(9,3), K=(5,3), T=(3,3);
draw(M--Nn,red+linewidth(0.9));
draw(O--Nn,blue); draw(O--K,blue+dashed); draw(Q--T,deepgreen+dashed);
draw(K+(0.3,0)--K+(0.3,-0.3)--K+(0,-0.3));
dot(M); dot(Nn); dot(O); dot(Q); dot(K); dot(T);
label("$A$",A,SW); label("$C$",C,S); label("$B$",B,SE); label("$M$",M,NW); label("$N$",Nn,NE);
label("$O$",O,S); label("$K$",K,N); label("$T$",T,N);
label("$4$",(7,3),N,red); label("$R$",(7,1.5),SE,blue); label("$r$",(5,1.5),W,blue); label("$r$",(3,1.5),W,deepgreen);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

A line parallel to $AB$ that touches the smaller semicircle touches it at its highest point, so $MN$ is at height $r$ above $AB$. Let $K$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $O$ to $MN$. The perpendicular from the centre to a chord bisects the chord, so $KN=4$, and $OK=r$. By the Pythagorean theorem in triangle $OKN$,
\[R^2=r^2+4^2,\qquad R^2-r^2=16 .\]
The shaded area is
\[\dfrac{1}{2}\pi R^2-\dfrac{1}{2}\pi r^2=\dfrac{\pi}{2}\left(R^2-r^2\right)=\boxed{8\pi}.\]
The answer does not depend on where $C$ is; only the length of the chord matters.
