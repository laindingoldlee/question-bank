Let $s_k=\sum_i a_i^k$. Newton's identities give $s_1=-2$ and, for k=2,...,5, $s_k+c_1s_{k-1}+\cdots+c_{k-1}s_1+kc_k=0$, where $c_j=j+1$. Substitution successively gives $s_2=s_3=s_4=s_5=-2$. For larger k the recurrence is $s_k+2s_{k-1}+3s_{k-2}+4s_{k-3}+5s_{k-4}+6s_{k-5}=0$. Thus $s_6=-(-4-6-8-10-12)=40$, and $s_7=-(80-6-8-10-12)=\boxed{-44}$.
