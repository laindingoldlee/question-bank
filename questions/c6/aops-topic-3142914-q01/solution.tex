Factor $(a-b^2)(a+b^2)=2009=7^2\cdot41$. The positive factor pairs are $(1,2009),(7,287),(41,49)$. Half their differences are $1004,140,4$; only 4 is a square. Hence $b=2,a=45$, and $\boxed{a+b=47}$.
