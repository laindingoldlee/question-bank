Each attached triangle contributes its two exposed sides to the boundary. There are 12 exposed sides of length 3, so the perimeter is $\boxed{36}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair[] p;path q;for(int i=0;i<12;++i){real r=i%2==0?sqrt(3):1;p.push(r*dir(30*i));q=q--p[i];}draw(q--cycle);for(int i=1;i<12;i+=2){draw((0,0)--p[i],dashed);draw(p[i]--p[(i+2)%12],dashed);}label("12 exposed equal sides",(0,-2.3));
\end{asy}
