If 49 dogs were chosen, each chosen dog's at least two enemies would have to lie among only one unchosen dog, impossible. Thus at most 48 can be chosen. Attain this with 48 dogs each enemies with the same two remaining dogs, and no enemy relations among the 48. Every dog has at least two enemies, so the maximum is $\boxed{48}$.
