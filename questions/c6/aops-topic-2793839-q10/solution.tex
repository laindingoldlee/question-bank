The base in the $xy$-plane is a right triangle of area $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Its perpendicular height is $1$, so the volume is $\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot1=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{6}}$.
