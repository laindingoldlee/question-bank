Put $C=(0,0),A=(a,0),B=(0,b)$. The median intersection is $G=(\dfrac{a}{3},\dfrac{b}{3})$. Thus $9AG^2=4a^2+b^2=576$ and $9BG^2=a^2+4b^2=324$. Adding gives $a^2+b^2=180$, so $CG^2=\dfrac{(a^2+b^2)}{9}=20$. Hence $\boxed{CG=2\sqrt5}$.
