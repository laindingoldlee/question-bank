\textbf{(a)} Write $S,C,V$ for the flavour prices and $c,s,r$ for the decoration prices. Then
\[S+c=(S+r)-(C+r)+(C+c)=8-10+7=\boxed{\$5}.\]
(The information that chocolate flavour and chocolate sprinkles cost the same is not needed for this; it gives $C=c=3.50$, $r=6.50$, $S=1.50$, $s=2.50$, $V=2.50$, consistent with all six prices.)

\textbf{(b)} $3\cdot3=\boxed{9}$ orders.
