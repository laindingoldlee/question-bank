For $x = \dfrac{\pi}{4} + \dfrac{k \pi}{2}$, $| \sin x | = | \cos x | = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ and $|\tan x|=|\cot x|=1$. The first term is $\dfrac{1}{1+1} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. The second is $\dfrac{(\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt2}) \cdot 8}{2\sqrt2+2\sqrt2} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. The third is $\dfrac{\dfrac{2 \sqrt{2}}{2\sqrt2}}{1+1} = \dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore the sum is $\boxed{\dfrac{3}{2}}$ at every such angle.
