By Legendre's formula, the prime factorisation of $30!$ is
\[
30!=2^{26}\cdot3^{14}\cdot5^{7}\cdot7^{4}\cdot11^{2}\cdot13^{2}\cdot17\cdot19\cdot23\cdot29
\]
(for example the exponent of $2$ is $15+7+3+1=26$ and that of $5$ is $6+1=7$). Removing $10^7=2^7\cdot5^7$,
\[
K=\dfrac{30!}{10^7}=2^{19}\cdot3^{14}\cdot7^4\cdot11^2\cdot13^2\cdot17\cdot19\cdot23\cdot29,
\]
and the required digit is the units digit of $K$. Clearly $K$ is even. Modulo $5$, using $2^4\equiv3^4\equiv1$:
\[
2^{19}\equiv2^3\equiv3,\quad 3^{14}\equiv3^2\equiv4,\quad 7^4\equiv1,\quad 11^2\equiv1,\quad 13^2\equiv4,
\]
\[
17\equiv2,\quad 19\equiv4,\quad 23\equiv3,\quad 29\equiv4.
\]
Multiplying, $K\equiv3\cdot4\cdot1\cdot1\cdot4\cdot2\cdot4\cdot3\cdot4\equiv3\pmod5$. The even digit congruent to $3$ modulo $5$ is $8$, so the digit is
\[\boxed{8}.\]
