A traveller comes to a magic bridge. Each time he walks across it, his money triples, but after each crossing he must pay $\$30$ toll. He crosses $10$ times, paying the toll each time. On his eleventh crossing his money triples to exactly $\$30$; he pays the toll and is left with nothing. How much money did he start with? Give the answer as a fraction.
