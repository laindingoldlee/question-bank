Let $x=\dfrac15$. The series converge, since $\dfrac{(n+1)^2}{5^{n+1}}\div\dfrac{n^2}{5^n}\to\dfrac15<1$.

First, $T=\displaystyle\sum_{n\ge1}nx^n$ satisfies
\[T-xT=\sum_{n\ge1}\bigl(n-(n-1)\bigr)x^n=\sum_{n\ge1}x^n=\dfrac{x}{1-x}=\dfrac14,\]
so $\dfrac45T=\dfrac14$ and $T=\dfrac{5}{16}$.

Next, $S=\displaystyle\sum_{n\ge1}n^2x^n$ satisfies
\[S-xS=\sum_{n\ge1}\bigl(n^2-(n-1)^2\bigr)x^n=\sum_{n\ge1}(2n-1)x^n=2T-\dfrac14=\dfrac58-\dfrac28=\dfrac38.\]
So $\dfrac45S=\dfrac38$ and
\[\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\dfrac{n^2}{5^n}=S=\boxed{\dfrac{15}{32}}.\]
