Let $s_n=A_nA_{n+1}$. Then $A_nA_{n+2}=s_n+s_{n+1}$, and the condition becomes $2(s_n+s_{n+1})=s_{n+1}+s_{n+2}$, i.e. $s_{n+2}=s_{n+1}+2s_n$. From $s_1=s_2=1$:
\[s_3,\dots,s_{10}=3,5,11,21,43,85,171,341.\]
So $A_1A_{11}=s_1+\cdots+s_{10}=\boxed{682}$.
