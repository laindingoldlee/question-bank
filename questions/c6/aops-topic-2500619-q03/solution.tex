Since $9^3=729$, we have $1458=2\cdot729=2000_9$ and $2021=1458+6\cdot81+8\cdot9+5=2685_9$. So the numbers in the range are exactly those written $\overline{2abc}_9$ with $\overline{2abc}_9\le2685_9$, and the digit sum is $2+a+b+c$.
\begin{itemize}
\item If $a\le5$: the sum is at most $2+5+8+8=23$.
\item If $a=6$: we need $\overline{bc}_9\le85_9$. If $b=8$ then $c\le5$ and the sum is at most $2+6+8+5=21$; if $b\le7$ the sum is at most $2+6+7+8=23$.
\end{itemize}
So the digit sum is at most $23$, and $23$ is attained, for example by $2588_9=1943$ and $2678_9=2015$. The answer is $\boxed{23}$.
