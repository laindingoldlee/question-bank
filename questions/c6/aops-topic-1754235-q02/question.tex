There were $2019$ inhabitants on an island, each a knight, who always tells the truth, or a knave, who always lies. Every day one inhabitant declared, ``When I depart the island, there will be an equal number of knights and knaves left,'' and then immediately left the island. After $2019$ days there were no inhabitants left on the island. How many knaves were there on the island initially?
