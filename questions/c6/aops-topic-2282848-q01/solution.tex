Waiting $k$ years and then running gives a total of
\[T(k)=k+\dfrac{2000}{2^k}.\]
Going from $k$ to $k+1$ adds $1$ year of waiting and saves $\dfrac{2000}{2^{k+1}}$ years of running, so waiting one more year helps exactly when $\dfrac{2000}{2^{k+1}}>1$, that is, $2^{k+1}<2000$, i.e. $k\le9$. So $T$ decreases up to $k=10$ and increases after that, and the minimum is
\[T(10)=10+\dfrac{2000}{1024}\approx11.95 .\]
(For comparison, $T(9)\approx12.91$ and $T(11)\approx11.98$.) To the nearest integer, the least time is $\boxed{12}$ years.
