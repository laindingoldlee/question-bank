Squaring the first equation and comparing with the second gives $xy+yz+zx=0$. The identity
\[
x^3+y^3+z^3=(x+y+z)^3-3(x+y+z)(xy+yz+zx)+3xyz
\]
then gives $xyz=0$. Suppose $z=0$. The first two equations imply $x+y=1$ and $xy=0$, so $(x,y)=(1,0)$ or $(0,1)$. The same argument applies whichever variable vanishes. Therefore the solutions are $\boxed{(1,0,0),(0,1,0),(0,0,1)}$.
