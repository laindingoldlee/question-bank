Abby, Ben, Cal, Dave, Erica, Finn, Gabe, Harry, Ida and Jade run a race and finish in places $1$ (fastest) to $10$, with no ties. Write each runner's place as the first letter of their name. Find the finishing order from these clues.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Abby finished ahead of Gabe, who finished ahead of Harry ($A<G<H$).
\item $B$, $E$, $F$ and $J$ are the four powers of $2$ among the places, in some order.
\item $D^2=C+J$.
\item $H=I+F$.
\item $I=A+E$.
\item $B-G=J$.
\end{enumerate}
