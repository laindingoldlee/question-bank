For a prime $p$ write $v_p(n)$ for the exponent of $p$ in $n$. The hypothesis says that for every prime $p$,
\[2v_p(b)\le v_p(2)+2v_p(a).\]
If $p$ is odd, $v_p(2)=0$, so $v_p(b)\le v_p(a)$. If $p=2$, then $2v_2(b)\le 1+2v_2(a)$; the left side is even, so in fact $2v_2(b)\le 2v_2(a)$, that is $v_2(b)\le v_2(a)$.

So every prime appears in $b$ to at most the power it has in $a$, which means $b\mid a$.
