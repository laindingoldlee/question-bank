Write every number with three digits, allowing leading zeros. Divisibility of the number by 3 means its digit sum is divisible by 3; it is also divisible by 7. The sum is therefore a multiple of 21. It lies between 0 and 27, and zero corresponds only to the excluded number 0, so the digit sum must be 21.

If the digits are $a,b,c$, put $u=9-a,v=9-b,w=9-c$. Then $u+v+w=6$ with nonnegative entries. The upper bounds of 9 are automatic. Stars and bars gives $\binom82=\boxed{28}$ such numbers.
