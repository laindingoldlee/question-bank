There are $22$ matches in total. Let G lose $L$ times, so she plays $L+1$ matches. She first plays in match one or two. Her sole win occurs by match four and is followed by another appearance, so her last appearance is a loss. Between successive appearances there is one intervening match after a loss and none after her one win. The interval from her first through last appearance therefore has length $(L+1)+(L-1)=2L$. At most one match precedes it and at most one follows it. Hence $22=2L+u+v$ with $u,v\in\{0,1\}$, so $L=10$ or $11$.
