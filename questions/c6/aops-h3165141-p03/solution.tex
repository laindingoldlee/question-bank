The total number of grid rectangles is $\binom62\binom72=15\cdot21=315$. A rectangle contains both red cells exactly when its top boundary has three choices, bottom boundary three, left boundary three, and right boundary three. Thus $3^4=81$ rectangles contain both. With only two red cells, all others contain at most one. The answer is $315-81=\boxed{234}$.
