The total is 55, so Hilda's sum is 27. Write her increasing cards $h_1<\cdots<h_5$ and put $y_i=h_i-(i-1)$. The gap condition makes the $y_i$ five distinct numbers from 1 through 6. Their sum is $27-10=17$, so the omitted number is $21-17=4$. Thus $h=(1,3,5,8,10)$. Marianne has $(2,4,6,7,9)$, whose only consecutive pair is 6 and 7, with sum $\boxed{13}$.
