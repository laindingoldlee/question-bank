Let $w(n)$ be the number of ways to reach $n$. The last hop into $n$ is $1$, $2$ or $3$ units long, so
\[
w(n)=w(n-1)+w(n-2)+w(n-3),\qquad w(0)=1,\ w(1)=1,\ w(2)=2 .
\]
Then
\[
w(3)=4,\quad w(4)=7,\quad w(5)=13,\quad w(6)=24,\quad w(7)=44,\quad w(8)=\boxed{81}.
\]
