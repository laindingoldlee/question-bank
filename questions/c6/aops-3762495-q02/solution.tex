There are $\binom63=20$ first paths and 63 second paths, the latter from the recurrence $D(i,j)=D(i-1,j)+D(i,j-1)+D(i-1,j-1)$ with boundary values 1. For exact area counting, represent each path over the three vertical unit strips by its height at the left and right endpoints; vertical segments have zero width. For strip height differences $v,w$, the area is $\dfrac{|v|+|w|}{2}$ if $vw\ge0$, and $\dfrac{v^2+w^2}{2|v-w|}$ otherwise (two triangles when the paths cross). Enumerating the 20 by 63 pairs with this exact rational test gives 80 pairs of area less than 1. Hence the probability is $\dfrac{80}{1260} = \boxed{\dfrac{4}{63}}$.
