A $2020\times2020$ grid consists of unit square cells. A line is required to be parallel to a side of the grid. A cell is shaved if the line cuts it into two pieces of positive areas $a<b$ with $a<\dfrac{b}{2020}$. Let $S$ be the set of shaved cells. How many distinct sets $S$ have exactly $2020$ cells? Give the answer modulo $1000$.
