A product of two digits from $1$ to $9$ is at most $81$, so it contains a $0$ only if it is $10,20,30,40,50,60,70$ or $80$, i.e. a multiple of $10$, which needs a factor $5$ and an even factor. The possibilities are $2\cdot5=10$, $4\cdot5=20$, $6\cdot5=30$, $8\cdot5=40$ ($50,60,70,80$ need a factor larger than $9$). Each result has one zero.

In the triangular Chinese table each pair appears once, giving $\boxed{4}$ zeros. In the full $9\times9$ table each pair appears twice ($2\times5$ and $5\times2$, and so on), giving $8$ zeros.
