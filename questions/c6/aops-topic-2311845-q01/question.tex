\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Is there a number $N$ such that, in any room with $N$ people, there are certainly two people whose birthdays are in January? Explain.
\item[(b)] What is the minimum number of people in a room for which it is certain that two of them have their birthdays in the same month?
\end{enumerate}
