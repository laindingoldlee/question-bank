\textbf{(a)} $\boxed{\text{No}}$. However many people there are, it is possible that none of them was born in January (for example, all were born in March), so no number of people guarantees even one January birthday.

\textbf{(b)} There are $12$ months. With $12$ people it is possible that all have birthdays in different months, so $12$ is not enough. With $13$ people, by the pigeonhole principle ($13$ people, $12$ months), two of them have birthdays in the same month. The minimum is $\boxed{13}$.
