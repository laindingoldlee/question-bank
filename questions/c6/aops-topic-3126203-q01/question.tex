For a circle of centre $O$ and radius $r$, define the power of a point $P$ as $PO^2-r^2$. Prove that if a secant through an exterior point $P$ meets the circle at $A,B$, then $PA\cdot PB=PO^2-r^2$, and a tangent $PT$ has $PT^2$ equal to the same quantity. Use this to find a tangent length when $PA=4$, $PB=9$.
