The domain excludes $1,2$. Combining gives $\dfrac{-x}{(x-1)(x-2)}\ge0$. Its signs on the intervals separated by $0,1,2$ are positive, negative, positive, negative respectively. The zero $x=0$ is allowed; poles are not. Thus the solution is $\boxed{(-\infty,0]\cup(1,2)}$.
