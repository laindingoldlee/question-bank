$\dfrac{3xy^2}{9x^3}=\dfrac{y^2}{3x^2}$, whose fourth power is $\dfrac{y^8}{81x^8}$. Also $\dfrac{6x^5}{-3y^2}=-\dfrac{2x^5}{y^2}$, whose square is $\dfrac{4x^{10}}{y^4}$. The product is $\boxed{\dfrac{4x^2y^4}{81}}$.
