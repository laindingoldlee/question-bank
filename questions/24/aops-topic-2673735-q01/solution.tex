The $k$-th block (``$123$'' and $k$ zeros) has $k+3$ digits, so the first $m$ blocks have $3m+\dfrac{m(m+1)}2$ digits: $184$ for $m=16$ and $204$ for $m=17$. Hence digits $185$ to $204$ form block $17$: digits $185,186,187$ are $1,2,3$ and digits $188$ to $204$ are zeros. The $200^{\text{th}}$ digit is $\boxed{0}$.
