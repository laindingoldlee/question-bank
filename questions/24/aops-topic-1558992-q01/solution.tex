Evaluate from the top: $3^3=27$, then $3^{27}=7\,625\,597\,484\,987$, so
\[
3\star4=3^{3^{27}}=\boxed{3^{7625597484987}}.
\]
The number of digits of a positive integer $N$ is $\lfloor\log_{10}N\rfloor+1$. Here
\[
\log_{10}N=7625597484987\cdot\log_{10}3\approx7625597484987\cdot0.47712125472=3\,638\,334\,640\,024.0997\ldots,
\]
so $3\star4$ has $\boxed{3\,638\,334\,640\,025}$ digits (about $3.6$ trillion).

(The tower must be read from the top down. Evaluated from the bottom up, $\left(\left(3^3\right)^3\right)^3=3^{27}$ would be far smaller; this is why towers are conventionally evaluated from the top.)
