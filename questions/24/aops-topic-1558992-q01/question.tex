Counting is repeated succession, addition is repeated counting ($3+4$), multiplication is repeated addition ($3\cdot4=3+3+3+3$), and exponentiation is repeated multiplication ($3^4=3\cdot3\cdot3\cdot3$). Let $3\star4$ denote repeated exponentiation: a tower of four $3$'s, evaluated from the top down,
\[
3\star4=3^{3^{3^{3}}}.
\]
Write $3\star4$ as a power of $3$ with an explicit integer exponent, and find how many digits it has.
