Write $\dfrac{1}{a}+\dfrac{1}{b}=\dfrac{1}{6}$ with $a<b$. Rearranging gives $(a-6)(b-6)=36$, and positivity of the original terms forces $a,b>6$. The positive unequal factor pairs of 36 are $(1,36),(2,18),(3,12),(4,9)$; the equal pair $(6,6)$ is excluded. Each gives a unique solution, so there are $\boxed4$ ways.
