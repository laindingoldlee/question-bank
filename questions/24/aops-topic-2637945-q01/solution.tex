$2021$ is just below the square $2025=45^2$:
\[2021=45^2-2^2=(45-2)(45+2)=43\cdot47.\]
Both factors are prime (neither is divisible by $2$, $3$ or $5$, and $7^2>47$). So $2021=\boxed{43\cdot47}$.
