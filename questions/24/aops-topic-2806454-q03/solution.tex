With $x=\dfrac1k$: $\sum nx^n=\dfrac x{(1-x)^2}$ and $\sum x^n=\dfrac x{1-x}$, so
\[\dfrac{5x}{(1-x)^2}-\dfrac x{1-x}=\dfrac{x(4+x)}{(1-x)^2}=\dfrac{13}4.\]
Then $4x^2+16x=13-26x+13x^2$, i.e. $9x^2-42x+13=0$, so $x=\dfrac13$ or $x=\dfrac{13}3$. Since $0<x<1$, $x=\dfrac13$ and $k=\boxed{3}$.
