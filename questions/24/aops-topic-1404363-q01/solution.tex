Give each grid point $(x,y)$ the parity of $x+y$. Every move changes $x$ or $y$ by $1$, so it changes the parity of $x+y$. After three moves the parity has changed an odd number of times, so the final point has a different parity from the start and cannot be the starting point. The probability is $\boxed{0}$.

(After two moves the probability of being back is $\dfrac{4}{16}=\dfrac{1}{4}$, and after four moves it is $\dfrac{36}{256}=\dfrac{9}{64}$.)
