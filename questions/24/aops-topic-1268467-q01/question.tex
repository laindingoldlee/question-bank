Three identical dominoes ($1\times 2$ tiles) are placed on a $3\times 4$ board of unit squares, each covering two squares, so that together they form a continuous pathway (consecutive squares sharing a side) from the upper-left square to the lower-right square. No two dominoes may touch only at a corner. In how many ways can this be done?
