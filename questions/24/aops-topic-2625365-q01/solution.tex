The terms are $a_1,\ a_1+d,\ a_1+2d,\dots$: each step adds $d$, so the $k$th term is $a_1+(k-1)d$ (after $k-1$ steps). If the sequence has $n$ terms, its last term is $L=a_1+(n-1)d$. Solving, $n-1=\dfrac{L-a_1}{d}$, so $n=\dfrac{L-a_1}{d}+1$. (The ``$+1$'' is the usual fencepost count: $\dfrac{L-a_1}{d}$ counts the gaps between consecutive terms, and there is one more term than gaps.) $\blacksquare$
