By Vieta's formulas, $a+b=-1$ and $ab=-2$, so
\[
\dfrac{ab}{a+b}=\dfrac{-2}{-1}=\boxed{2}.
\]
(Directly: $x^2+x-2=(x+2)(x-1)$, so the solutions are $1$ and $-2$, and $\dfrac{1\cdot(-2)}{1+(-2)}=2$.)
