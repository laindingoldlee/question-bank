Since $n\ge2$, $\ln n>0$. Suppose $\ln n=\dfrac pq$ with positive integers $p,q$. Then $e^{p/q}=n$, so $e^p=n^q$, and $e$ is a root of the integer polynomial $x^p-n^q$. This contradicts the transcendence of $e$. Hence $\ln n$ is irrational. $\blacksquare$
