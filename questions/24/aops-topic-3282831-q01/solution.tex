The series converges, for example by the ratio test. If its sum is $S$, then subtracting $S$ from $3S$ termwise gives $2S=1+\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{9}+\cdots=\dfrac{3}{2}$. Hence $\boxed{S=\dfrac{3}{4}}$.
