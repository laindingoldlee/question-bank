A student claims that $1+1=\sin^2\alpha+\cos^2\alpha+\sin^2\alpha+\cos^2\alpha$ for every real number $\alpha$. Prove that $\sin^2\alpha+\cos^2\alpha=1$ for every real $\alpha$, and conclude that the claim is correct.
