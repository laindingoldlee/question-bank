Put $M=2^{1004}$. The next square after $M^2$ is $M^2+2M+1$, so $n<1005$ is impossible and $n=1005$ works. It remains to consider $n=1006,1007$, written as $n=1005+j$ with $j=1,2$. The proposed number is $M^2+2^{j+1}M+1$, which is less than $(M+2^j)^2$ because $1<2^{2j}$. It is greater than $(M+2^j-1)^2$, since the difference is $2M+1-(2^j-1)^2>0$. It therefore lies strictly between consecutive squares. Thus the only answer is $\boxed{n=1005}$.
