Let $p$ be a prime.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that for every $a\in\{1,2,\dots,p-1\}$ there is exactly one $b\in\{1,2,\dots,p-1\}$ with $ab\equiv1\pmod p$ (the inverse of $a$).
\item[(b)] Prove that $b=a$ only for $a=1$ and $a=p-1$. (This is the pairing used in the proof of Wilson's theorem.)
\end{enumerate}
