If the side is $s$ and the radius $r$, then $s^2=\pi r^2$, so $\dfrac sr=\sqrt\pi$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=sqrt(pi);draw((0,0)--(s,0)--(s,s)--(0,s)--cycle);draw(circle((s+1.6,s/2),1));draw((s+1.6,s/2)--(s+2.6,s/2));
label("$s$",(s/2,0),S);label("$r$",(s+2.1,s/2),N);
\end{asy}

Answer $\boxed{\textbf{(B)}\ \sqrt\pi}$.
