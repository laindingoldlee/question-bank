The supplementary opposite angles imply that $ABCD$ is cyclic. Ptolemy's theorem gives $AC\cdot BD=AB\cdot CD+AD\cdot BC=AB(CD+BC)=AB\cdot AC$, so $BD=AB=AD$. Thus $ABD$ is equilateral, and the rotation through the smaller angle has magnitude $60^\circ$ (the same mapping results from its equivalent full-turn representation). It gives $AC'=AC$ and $\angle CAC'=60^\circ$, so $ACC'$ is equilateral. Therefore $\boxed{\angle ACC'=60^\circ}$.
