A positive integer $n$ is called \emph{abundant} if the sum of its proper divisors (the divisors other than $n$ itself) is greater than $n$. Some books instead define $n$ to be abundant if the sum of \emph{all} its positive divisors is greater than $2n$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that the two definitions describe exactly the same numbers.
\item[(b)] Find the smallest abundant number.
\end{enumerate}
