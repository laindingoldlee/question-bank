A regular pentagon lies inside a regular hexagon, with no parallel sides between them, and $P$ is inside the pentagon. A boundary point $Q$ of the pentagon is projective if some boundary point $R$ of the hexagon is collinear with $P,Q$ and satisfies $QR=2019\,PQ$. No order of the three points is prescribed. What is the maximum number of projective points?
