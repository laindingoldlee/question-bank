Write the angles as $6t,7t,8t$. Their sum is $21t=180^\circ$, so the middle angle is $7t=\dfrac{7}{21}180^\circ=\boxed{60^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
import geometry; usepackage("amsmath"); size(240);
pair A=(0,0),B=(4,0),C=4*sin(pi/3)/sin(8*pi/21)*dir(360.0/7);draw(A--B--C--cycle);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);
\end{asy}
