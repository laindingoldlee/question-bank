Let the board have side $2$, so its area is $4$. The inscribed circle has radius $1$ and area $\pi$. The smaller square has diagonal equal to the diameter $2$, so its side is $\sqrt2$ and its area is $2$. The shaded region below has area $\pi-2$, so the probability is
\[\boxed{\dfrac{\pi-2}{4}}\approx0.285.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
fill(circle((0,0),1),mediumgray+opacity(0.5));
fill((1,0)--(0,1)--(-1,0)--(0,-1)--cycle,white);
draw((-1,-1)--(1,-1)--(1,1)--(-1,1)--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
draw(circle((0,0),1));
draw((1,0)--(0,1)--(-1,0)--(0,-1)--cycle);
dot((0,0));draw((0,0)--(1,0),dashed);label("$1$",(0.5,0),S);
label("$2$",(0,-1),S);
\end{asy}
