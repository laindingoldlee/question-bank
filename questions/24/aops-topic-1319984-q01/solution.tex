Eliminate $y$ using the first equation, $y=8-2w-3x-4z$:
\[
\begin{aligned}
5w+8x+4(8-2w-3x-4z)+8z=28&\ \Longrightarrow\ -3w-4x-8z=-4,\\
6w+4x+5(8-2w-3x-4z)+6z=23&\ \Longrightarrow\ -4w-11x-14z=-17,\\
10w+2x-8(8-2w-3x-4z)-4z=6&\ \Longrightarrow\ 26w+26x+28z=70 .
\end{aligned}
\]
From the first of these, $3w=4-4x-8z$. Substituting into the other two (after multiplying by $3$): $-4(4-4x-8z)-33x-42z=-51$, i.e. $-17x-10z=-35$; and $26(4-4x-8z)+78x+84z=210$, i.e. $-26x-124z=106$, i.e. $13x+62z=-53$. Solving $17x+10z=35$ and $13x+62z=-53$: multiply to eliminate $x$: $13(17x+10z)-17(13x+62z)=455+901$, so $(130-1054)z=1356$ and $z=-\dfrac{1356}{924}=-\dfrac{113}{77}$. Then $x=\dfrac{35-10z}{17}=\dfrac{225}{77}$, $w=\dfrac{4-4x-8z}{3}=\dfrac{104}{77}$, and $y=8-2w-3x-4z=\dfrac{185}{77}$.
\[
\boxed{(w,x,y,z)=\left(\dfrac{104}{77},\ \dfrac{225}{77},\ \dfrac{185}{77},\ -\dfrac{113}{77}\right)}
\]
Substituting back confirms all four equations.
