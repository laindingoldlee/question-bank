A solid ball of radius $\dfrac{1}{100}$ is placed at random inside a solid ball of radius $100$ centred at the origin $O$: its centre is chosen uniformly at random from all points for which the small ball lies entirely inside the big one.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What is the probability that the small ball contains the point $Q=(100,0,0)$?
\item[(b)] What is the probability that the small ball contains the point $P=(50,0,0)$?
\end{enumerate}
