Put $A=(0,0),B=(5,0),C=(x,y)$ with $y>0$. The side lengths give $x^2+y^2=900$ and $(x-5)^2+y^2=841$, hence $x = \dfrac{42}{5} , y = \dfrac{144}{5}$. Since $BH\perp AB$, write $H=(5,h)$. The condition $CH\perp AC$ gives $C\cdot(H-C)=0$, or $42 + \dfrac{144 h}{5} = 900$, so $h = \dfrac{715}{24}$. Taking vertical $BH$ as base, $[BCH] = (\dfrac{1}{2}) (\dfrac{715}{24}) (\dfrac{42}{5} - 5) = \boxed{\dfrac{2431}{48}}$.

\end{asy}
