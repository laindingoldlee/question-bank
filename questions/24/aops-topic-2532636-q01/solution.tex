Group the terms as
\[(1-2)+\sum_{k=1}^{667}\bigl(3k+(3k+1)-(3k+2)\bigr),\]
the last group being $2001+2002-2003$. Each group equals $3k-1$, so the value is
\[-1+3\cdot\dfrac{667\cdot668}{2}-667=-1+668334-667=\boxed{667666}.\]
(Check: the sum of $1,\dots,2003$ is $2007006$, and the subtracted numbers $2,5,\dots,2003$ have sum $669670$; $2007006-2\cdot669670=667666$.)
