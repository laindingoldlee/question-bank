A square with side $s$ has area $s^2$, so $s^2=4096$ and $s=\sqrt{4096}=\boxed{64}$ (since $64^2=4096$).

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((0,0)--(64,0)--(64,64)--(0,64)--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
label("area $4096$",(32,32)); label("$s$",(32,0),S); label("$s$",(64,32),E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
