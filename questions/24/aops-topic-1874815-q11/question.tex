The product $2\cdot3\cdot4\cdot5\cdot6$ is to be computed by multiplying two numbers at a time, and parentheses show the order, for example $((2\cdot3)\cdot4)\cdot(5\cdot6)$. Without changing the order of the factors, in how many ways can the parentheses be placed? Find a systematic way to list them all.
