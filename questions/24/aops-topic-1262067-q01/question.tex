Euler's totient function $\varphi(n)$ counts the positive integers $k\le n$ with $\gcd(k,n)=1$. Explain why, if $p_1,\dots,p_r$ are the distinct primes dividing $n$,
\[\varphi(n)=n\left(1-\dfrac{1}{p_1}\right)\left(1-\dfrac{1}{p_2}\right)\cdots\left(1-\dfrac{1}{p_r}\right),\]
and use it to compute $\varphi(360)$.
