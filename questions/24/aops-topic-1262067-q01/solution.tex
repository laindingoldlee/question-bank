A number $k\le n$ is coprime to $n$ exactly when no $p_i$ divides $k$. Among $1,\dots,n$, exactly $\dfrac{n}{d}$ numbers are multiples of a divisor $d$ of $n$. By inclusion--exclusion, the count of numbers divisible by none of the $p_i$ is
\[n-\sum_i\dfrac{n}{p_i}+\sum_{i<j}\dfrac{n}{p_ip_j}-\cdots=n\prod_{i=1}^r\left(1-\dfrac{1}{p_i}\right),\]
since expanding the product gives exactly these terms.

For $360=2^3\cdot3^2\cdot5$:
\[\varphi(360)=360\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot\dfrac{4}{5}=\boxed{96}.\]
