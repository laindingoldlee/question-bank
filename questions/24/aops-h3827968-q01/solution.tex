Write $f(x)=ax^2+bx$. Expanding the left side gives
\[
a\left(x^2+\dfrac{1}{x^2}\right)+b(1-a)\left(x+\dfrac{1}{x}\right)-a^2-b^2.
\]
Since the identity holds for every nonzero $x$, comparing coefficients after multiplication by $x^2$ gives $a=1$ and $1+b^2=2025^2+1$. Thus $b=\pm2025$. The condition $f(1)=1+b<0$ selects $b=-2025$, so
\[
f(2024)=2024(2024-2025)=\boxed{-2024}.
\]
