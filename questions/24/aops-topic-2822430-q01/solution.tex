For example $459=1^2+1^2+4^2+21^2=1+1+16+441$. Other valid answers include $1^2+3^2+7^2+20^2$ and $1^2+5^2+12^2+17^2$.

A quick search: $21^2=441$ leaves $18=1+1+16$. So $\boxed{459=1^2+1^2+4^2+21^2}$.
