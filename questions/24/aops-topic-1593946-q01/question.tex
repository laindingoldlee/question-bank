Ania has $9$ crayons in a box, and at least one of them is blue. Among every $4$ crayons at least two are of the same colour, and among every $5$ crayons at most three are of the same colour. How many blue crayons are in the box?

$\textbf{(A) }2\qquad\textbf{(B) }3\qquad\textbf{(C) }4\qquad\textbf{(D) }1\qquad\textbf{(E) }5$
