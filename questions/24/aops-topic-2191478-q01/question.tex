There are $28$ planes in the sky. Each plane carries a prime number of passengers between $100$ and $110$. Then a prime number of the planes (between $1$ and $28$) crash, and everyone on board them is lost; everyone on the other planes survives. The total number of survivors turns out to be a prime number. What is the smallest possible number of survivors?
