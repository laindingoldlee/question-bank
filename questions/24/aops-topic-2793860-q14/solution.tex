Put ray $AB$ at direction 0 degrees and $AC$ at 60 degrees. Reflection puts $AB^{\prime}$ at 120 degrees and $AC^{\prime}$ at $-60$ degrees. These are opposite rays, so the three points are collinear. Their area is $\boxed0$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(15,0),C=20*dir(60),Bp=15*dir(120),Cp=20*dir(-60);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(Bp--Cp,blue);label("A",A,W);label("B",B,E);label("C",C,N);label("B prime",Bp,NW);label("C prime",Cp,SE);
\end{asy}
