The denominators are $3k(k+1)$ for $k=1,\dots,22$ ($3\cdot22\cdot23=1518$). Since $\dfrac1{k(k+1)}=\dfrac1k-\dfrac1{k+1}$, the sum is $\dfrac13\left(1-\dfrac1{23}\right)=\boxed{\dfrac{22}{69}}$.
