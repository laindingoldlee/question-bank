If Tsuni takes $t$ hours, Kali takes $2t$, and together they do $\dfrac1t+\dfrac1{2t}=\dfrac3{2t}$ of the job per hour. So $\dfrac3{2t}=\dfrac1{2.25}$, $t=3.375$, and Kali takes $2t=\boxed{6.75}$ hours.
