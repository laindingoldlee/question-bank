Subtract the second equation from the first: $5x+y=n$, so $y=n-5x$. Substituting into the second and third equations:
\[x+2(n-5x)+11z=2n\ \Rightarrow\ 11z=9x,\qquad x+3(n-5x)+2z=n\ \Rightarrow\ 2z=14x-2n.\]
So $z=\dfrac{9x}{11}$ and $\dfrac{18x}{11}=14x-2n$, giving $136x=22n$, i.e. $x=\dfrac{11n}{68}$. Then $y=n-\dfrac{55n}{68}=\dfrac{13n}{68}$ and $z=\dfrac{9n}{68}$:
\[\boxed{(x,y,z)=\left(\dfrac{11n}{68},\dfrac{13n}{68},\dfrac{9n}{68}\right)}.\]
