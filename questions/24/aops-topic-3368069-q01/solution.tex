The leftmost of their positions can be $1,2,3,4,5$. For each, Alex and Betsy can occupy the two endpoint positions in $2$ orders. The other five people fill the remaining positions in $5!$ orders. The total is $5\cdot2\cdot5!=\boxed{1200}$.
