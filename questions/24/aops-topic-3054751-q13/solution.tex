Since $2023=7\cdot17^2$, write $n=7^{17}17^{34}$ and $m=7^a17^b$. Divisibility by $n$ requires $2a\ge17,2b\ge34$, while $m\mid n$ requires $a\le17,b\le34$. Thus $9\le a\le17$ and $17\le b\le34$, giving $\boxed{9\cdot18=162}$ choices.
