Five different rockets $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$, $E$ are to be launched from two launch pads labelled $1$ and $2$. Each pad holds only one rocket at a time. The rockets can launch from either pad, in any order, one or two at a time (two rockets may launch simultaneously, one from each pad). A launch pattern lists the launch times in order, giving the rocket(s) and pad(s) at each time; one example is $(C1,\ A1D2,\ E1B2)$, where the commas separate three launch times. How many launch patterns are possible?
