\textbf{Lemma.} If $x,y$ are positive integers with $x+y=s$, then $x^3+y^3\le(s-1)^3+1$, with equality exactly when $x=1$ or $y=1$.

\emph{Proof.} We have $x^3+y^3=(x+y)^3-3xy(x+y)=s^3-3sxy$. Since $(x-1)(y-1)\ge0$, we get $xy\ge x+y-1=s-1$, with equality exactly when $x=1$ or $y=1$. Hence
\[x^3+y^3\le s^3-3s(s-1)=(s-1)^3+1 .\]

Applying the lemma twice,
\[a^3+b^3+c^3\le(a+b-1)^3+1+c^3\le(a+b+c-2)^3+1+1=18^3+2=5834 ,\]
where the second step uses the lemma for the positive integers $a+b-1$ and $c$, whose sum is $19$. Equality holds for $(a,b,c)=(18,1,1)$, since $18^3+1+1=5834$. So the maximum is $\boxed{5834}$.
