Let the vertex half-angle be $\theta $. The distances of the two centres from the vertex are $\dfrac{2}{\sin\theta}$ and $\dfrac{1}{\sin\theta}$. Their difference equals the sum of the radii 3, so $\sin\theta=\dfrac{1}{3}$. The altitude is $\dfrac{2}{\sin\theta}+2=8 $. Since $\tan\theta=\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt2}$, the half-base is $ 8\tan\theta=2\sqrt2 $. The area is $\dfrac{1}{2}(4\sqrt2)(8)=\boxed{16\sqrt2}$.
