We show that $3\nmid n^2+n+2$. Modulo $3$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $n\equiv0$: $n^2+n+2\equiv2$;
\item $n\equiv1$: $n^2+n+2\equiv1+1+2\equiv1$;
\item $n\equiv2$: $n^2+n+2\equiv4+2+2\equiv2$.
\end{itemize}
So $\gcd(n^2+n+2,3)=1$ and
\[
\operatorname{lcm}\left(n^2+n+2,3\right)=3\left(n^2+n+2\right)=3n^2+3n+6 .
\]
This exceeds $n^2+n+1$ by $2n^2+2n+5>0$, so $\boxed{\operatorname{lcm}\left(n^2+n+2,3\right)>n^2+n+1}$ for every natural number $n$ (including $n=0$, where $6>1$).
