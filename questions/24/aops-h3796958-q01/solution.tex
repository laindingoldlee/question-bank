The diagonal would enter a new cell at each interior vertical or horizontal grid-line crossing. At an interior lattice point both crossings coincide and must be counted only once. There are $499+799$ crossings before correcting, and $\gcd(500,800)-1=99$ coincidences. Including the starting cell gives $1+499+799-99=\boxed{1200}$. Equivalently, the diagonal consists of 100 primitive $5\times8$ blocks, crossing twelve cells in each.
