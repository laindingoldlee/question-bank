Imagine every pair of stones joined by a string. Splitting a pile of $a+b$ into $a$ and $b$ cuts exactly $ab$ strings (those between the two new piles), and each string is cut exactly once. So the sum of the products is always $\dbinom{25}{2}=\boxed{300}$, whatever the splits.
