The second player wins by returning the rook to the main diagonal after every first-player move. If the rook is at $(k,k)$ and the first player increases one coordinate to $j>k$, increase the other to $j$. This is legal and maintains the invariant. Eventually the second player reaches $(8,8)$. The first player cannot reach that corner in one move from a different diagonal square.
