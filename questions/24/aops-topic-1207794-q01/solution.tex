Suppose $V$ gallons were lost in each condition. That took $\dfrac {V}{6}$ hours in the shade and $\dfrac {V}{19}$ hours in the sun. The average rate is total water over total time:
\[\dfrac{2V}{\dfrac {V}{6}+\dfrac {V}{19}}=\dfrac{2}{\dfrac{25}{114}}=\dfrac{228}{25}=\boxed{9.12}\ \text{gallons per hour},\]
the harmonic mean of $6$ and $19$ (not their ordinary average $12.5$).
