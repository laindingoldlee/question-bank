Write the odd integers as $2m+1$ and $2n+1$. Then
\[(2m+1)^2-(2n+1)^2=4m(m+1)-4n(n+1).\]
Each of $m(m+1)$ and $n(n+1)$ is a product of two consecutive integers, hence even, so each of $4m(m+1)$ and $4n(n+1)$ is a multiple of $8$, and so is their difference. $\blacksquare$
