Subtract $(5,4,3,2,1)$ to obtain a nonincreasing tuple of five nonnegative integers with sum $6$. Thus count partitions of $6$ into at most five parts. They are $6$, $5+1$, $4+2$, $4+1+1$, $3+3$, $3+2+1$, $3+1+1+1$, $2+2+2$, $2+2+1+1$, and $2+1+1+1+1$. The only remaining partition, six ones, has too many parts. Hence there are $\boxed{10}$ tuples.
