Two candles of the same height are lit at the same time. The first is consumed in $4$ hours and the second in $3$ hours. Assuming that each candle burns at a constant rate, how many hours after being lit was the first candle twice the height of the second?

$\textbf{(a) }\dfrac{3}{4}\qquad\textbf{(b) }1\dfrac{1}{2}\qquad\textbf{(c) }2\qquad\textbf{(d) }2\dfrac{2}{5}$
