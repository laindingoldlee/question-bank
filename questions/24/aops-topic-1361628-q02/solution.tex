Take the initial height as $1$. After $t$ hours the heights are $1-\dfrac{t}{4}$ and $1-\dfrac{t}{3}$. We need
\[
1-\dfrac{t}{4}=2\left(1-\dfrac{t}{3}\right)\iff \dfrac{2t}{3}-\dfrac{t}{4}=1\iff\dfrac{5t}{12}=1\iff t=\dfrac{12}{5}.
\]
So the answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(d) }2\dfrac{2}{5}}$ hours. Check: the heights are then $\dfrac{2}{5}$ and $\dfrac{1}{5}$.
