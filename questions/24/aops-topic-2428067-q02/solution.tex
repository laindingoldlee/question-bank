With this definition,
\[50!=1+2+\cdots+50=\dfrac{50\cdot51}{2}=1275,\]
which ends in the digit $5$. So it has $\boxed{0}$ trailing zeros. (With the usual factorial, $50!$ has $\left\lfloor\dfrac{50}{5}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{50}{25}\right\rfloor=12$ trailing zeros.)
