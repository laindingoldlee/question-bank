With probability one there are no coincident or antipodal points. The triangle fails to contain the centre exactly when the three points fit in an open semicircle. Each such configuration has one unique first point followed clockwise within a semicircle by the other two. For each choice of this first point, the probability is $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^2$. The three disjoint possibilities give failure probability $\dfrac{3}{4}$. Thus the answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{4}}$.
