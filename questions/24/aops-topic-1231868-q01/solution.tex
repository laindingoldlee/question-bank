Since $C=2\pi r$, we have $\pi r=\dfrac {C}{2}$, so $A=\pi r^2=\boxed{\dfrac{rC}{2}}$. Geometrically: cut the disc into thin sectors and rearrange them into a near-rectangle of height $r$ and width half the circumference.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(2cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw(unitcircle,linewidth(1)); draw((0,0)--(1,0)); label("$r$",(0.5,0),S); dot((0,0));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
