In right triangle $ABC$, $\angle A=90^\circ$ and $\angle C=30^\circ$. Let $\omega$ be the circle that passes through $A$ and is tangent to $BC$ at its midpoint. Suppose $\omega$ meets line $AC$ again at $N$ and meets the circumcircle of $ABC$ again at $M$. Prove that $MN\perp BC$.
