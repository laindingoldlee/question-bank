A student's combination is either two fruits of one type ($5$ ways) or two different types ($\binom52=10$ ways): $15$ combinations in all, and with $2000$ of each type every combination is always available. With $15$ students it is possible that all combinations are different. With $16$ students, by the pigeonhole principle two of them took the same combination. The answer is $\boxed{16}$.
