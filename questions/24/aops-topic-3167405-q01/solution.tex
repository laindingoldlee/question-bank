Validity gives $A\in\{1,2\}$, $0\le B\le2$, $0\le C\le3$, $0\le D\le4$, $0\le E\le5$. Since each base is $1$ modulo its required divisor, the conditions are that the successive digit sums are $0$ modulo $2,3,4,5$. If $A=1$, the first condition forces $B=1$, then successively $C=D=E=1$. If $A=2$, either $B=0$, giving $C=D=E=1$, or $B=2$, giving $C=D=E=2$. Thus the digit tuples are $(1,1,1,1,1),(2,0,1,1,1),(2,2,2,2,2)$, with distinct products $1,0,32$. Their sum is $\boxed{33}$.
