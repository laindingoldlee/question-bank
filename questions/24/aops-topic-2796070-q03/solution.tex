For each $z$ count the solutions of $2x+3y=n$ with $n=27-5z$: we need $y\equiv n\pmod 2$ and $3y\le n$.
\[\begin{array}{c|cccccc}z&0&1&2&3&4&5\\\hline n&27&22&17&12&7&2\\ y&1,3,5,7,9&0,2,4,6&1,3,5&0,2,4&1&0\\ \#&5&4&3&3&1&1\end{array}\]
The total is $5+4+3+3+1+1=\boxed{17}$.
