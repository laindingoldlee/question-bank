The expectations are $\dfrac{1+7500}{2}=3750.5$ and $1000\cdot\dfrac{1+6}{2}=3500$, so the single die has the greater expectation. Let the many-dice sum be $S$, always between 1000 and 6000. Conditional on $S$, the single die is smaller with probability $\dfrac{S-1}{7500}$. Taking expectation yields $\boxed{\dfrac{3500-1}{7500}=\dfrac{3499}{7500}}$.
