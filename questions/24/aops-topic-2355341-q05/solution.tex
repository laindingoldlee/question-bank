After the spill, $60\%$ of the water remains, which is $0.6\cdot50\%=30\%$ of a cup's capacity. Each new cup gets $2\%$ of a capacity, so there are $\dfrac{30}{2}=15$ such cups, and the original cup is now empty. The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(B)}\ 15}$.
