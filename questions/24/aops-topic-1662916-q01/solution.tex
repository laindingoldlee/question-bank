There are three groups and three men, and each group needs a man, so each group has exactly one man. Then each group of two has one woman, and the group of three has two women.
\begin{itemize}
\item Choose the man in the group of three: $3$ ways.
\item Choose the two women in the group of three: $\dbinom42=6$ ways.
\item Pair the remaining two men with the remaining two women: $2$ ways. (These pairs form the two groups of two, which are not distinguished.)
\end{itemize}
The total is $3\cdot6\cdot2=\boxed{36}$.
