Factor $x^3-1=(x-1)(x^2+x+1)$. The linear factor gives $x=1$, and the quadratic formula gives $x=\dfrac{-1\pm i\sqrt 3}{2}$. Thus the three solutions are $\boxed{1,\dfrac{-1+i\sqrt 3}{2},\dfrac{-1-i\sqrt 3}{2}}$. Only $1$ is real.
