Tennyson is building a circular corral for his pet dinosaur. He pounds $20$ posts, equally spaced, around the circumference. Each post contributes $6$ inches to the circumference, and the fencing between adjacent posts contributes $8$ feet to the circumference. To the nearest whole number, what is the area of the corral in square feet?
