Ignore draws first. The winner of each decisive game is a fair choice. After two decisive games, someone has two wins with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$; otherwise a third decisive game is needed. Thus the expected decisive-game count is $2+\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{5}{2}$. The mean waiting time for a decisive result is $\dfrac{1}{\dfrac{2}{3}}=\dfrac{3}{2}$ games. The waiting times are independent of which player wins, so expected total games are $\dfrac{5}{2}\cdot\dfrac{3}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{15}{4}}$.
