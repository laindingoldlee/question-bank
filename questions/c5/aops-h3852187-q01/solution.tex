Set $R=\sqrt{(a_{n-1}-1)^2+8}>1$. The fraction is $\dfrac{R^2-1}{R-1}=R+1$, so
\[
(a_n-1)^2=(a_{n-1}-1)^2+8.
\]
The initial square equals $1$, and $a_n>1$ for $n\ge1$, giving $a_n=1+\sqrt{8n+1}$. This is integral exactly when $8n+1=(2k+1)^2$, or $n=\dfrac{k(k+1)}{2}$. The positive triangular numbers at most $100$ correspond to $k=1,\ldots,13$, since the next two triangular numbers are $91$ and $105$. The answer is $\boxed{13}$.
