In both statements the covered region is the overlap of the square and the triangle. So both describe the \emph{same} quantity: the largest possible area $m$ of the overlap of the square and the triangle. Hence, with $T$ the area of the triangle,
\[
\dfrac{2}{3}T=m=\dfrac{3}{4}\cdot4^2=12,
\]
and $T=\dfrac{3}{2}\cdot12=\boxed{18}$.
