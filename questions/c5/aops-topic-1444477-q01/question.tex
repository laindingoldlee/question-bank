A square of side $4$ cm and a triangle lie in a plane and may be moved around. Placing the square on the triangle, at most two-thirds of the triangle's area can be covered. Placing the triangle on the square, at most three-quarters of the square's area can be covered. What is the area of the triangle, in square centimetres?
