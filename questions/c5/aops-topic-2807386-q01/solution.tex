Let $T_n=\dfrac{n(n+1)}2$. Induction on $n$: for $n=1$ both sides are $1$. If it holds for $n-1$, then
\[T_n^2-T_{n-1}^2=(T_n-T_{n-1})(T_n+T_{n-1})=n\cdot\left(\dfrac{n(n+1)}2+\dfrac{(n-1)n}2\right)=n\cdot n^2=n^3,\]
so adding $n^3$ to $1^3+\cdots+(n-1)^3=T_{n-1}^2$ gives $T_n^2$. $\blacksquare$
