Hiram's algebra notes are printed on sheets of paper: the first sheet carries pages $1$ and $2$, the second sheet carries pages $3$ and $4$, and so on. There are at least $100$ pages. While Hiram is at lunch, his roommate tears out several whole sheets (not necessarily consecutive ones), but not the first sheet. The page numbers on the torn-out sheets add up to $101$. How many different sets of sheets could the roommate have torn out?
