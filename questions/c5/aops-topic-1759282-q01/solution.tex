Factors of $n$ pair up as $d$ and $\dfrac {n}{d}$; the only unpaired factor occurs when $d=\dfrac {n}{d}$, i.e.\ $d=\sqrt n$. So $n$ has an odd number of factors exactly when $n$ is a perfect square. Since $44^2=1936\le2000<2025=45^2$, the squares $1^2,\dots,44^2$ qualify: $\boxed{44}$ numbers.
