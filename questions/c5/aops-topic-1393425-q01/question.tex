In right triangle $ABC$ with the right angle at $C$, $AC=24$ units and $BC=7$ units. Point $D$ lies on $\overline{AB}$ so that $\overline{CD}\perp\overline{AB}$. The bisector of the smallest angle of $\triangle ABC$ intersects $\overline{CD}$ at point $E$. What is the length of $\overline{ED}$? Express your answer as a common fraction.
