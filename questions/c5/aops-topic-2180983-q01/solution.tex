Let there be $n$ boys and $n$ girls at the start. Now there are $n+15$ boys among $2n+18$ students, so
\[\dfrac{n+15}{2n+18}=\dfrac{3}{5}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad5n+75=6n+54\quad\Longrightarrow\quad n=21 .\]
The class now has $2\cdot21+18=\boxed{60}$ students ($36$ boys and $24$ girls; $\dfrac{36}{60}=\dfrac{3}{5}$).
