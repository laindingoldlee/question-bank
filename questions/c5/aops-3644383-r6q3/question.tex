Derek, Derrick, Eric, Erik, and Frederick are playing a game. Derek and Derrick have a $\dfrac{1}{6}$ chance of winning the game on their turns. Eric and Erik have a $\dfrac{1}{3}$ chance of winning the game on their turns. Frederick has a $\dfrac{19}{100}$ chance of winning the game on his turn. Turns are determined alphabetically by first name, with Derek going first. The probabilities that the winner of the game has a name starting with ”D”, ”E”, and ”F” are $p_d$, $p_e$, and $p_f$ , respectively. The ratio $p_d : p_e : p_f$ can be expressed as $d : e : f$, where $d$, $e$, and $f$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $d + e - f$.
