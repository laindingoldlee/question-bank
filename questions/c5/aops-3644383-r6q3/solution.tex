Within one complete cycle, a D-name wins with probability $1-(\dfrac{5}{6})^2=\dfrac{11}{36}$, an E-name with probability $(\dfrac{5}{6})^2[1-(\dfrac{2}{3})^2]=\dfrac{125}{324}$, and Frederick with probability $(\dfrac{5}{6})^2(\dfrac{2}{3})^2(\dfrac{19}{100})=\dfrac{19}{324}$. Repeated cycles multiply all three by the same normalisation. Thus $d:e:f=99:125:19$, and $d+e-f=\boxed{205}$.
