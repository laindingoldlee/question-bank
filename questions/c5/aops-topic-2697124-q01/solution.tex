The $k$th cut can cross each of the earlier $k-1$ cuts at most once, so it is divided into at most $k$ segments, each of which splits one piece into two. Hence $k$ cuts give at most $1+(1+2+\cdots+k)=1+\dfrac{k(k+1)}{2}$ pieces, achieved by cuts in general position (no two parallel, no three concurrent, all crossings inside the pancake). For $k=6$: $\boxed{22}$ pieces and $\boxed{44}$ mL of syrup.
