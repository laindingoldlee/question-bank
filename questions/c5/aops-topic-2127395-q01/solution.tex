By Heron's formula with semiperimeter $s=\dfrac{15+16+17}{2}=24$, the area is
\[K=\sqrt{24\cdot9\cdot8\cdot7}=\sqrt{12096}=\sqrt{576\cdot21}=24\sqrt{21}.\]
The altitude to a side of length $\ell$ is $\dfrac{2K}{\ell}$, so the shortest altitude is the one to the longest side, $17$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0), C=(17,0), A=(258/34,sqrt(15^2-(258/34)^2)), D=(A.x,0);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(A--D,red+dashed+linewidth(0.8));
draw(D+(0.6,0)--D+(0.6,0.6)--D+(0,0.6),red);
dot(A); dot(B); dot(C); dot(D,red);
label("$A$",A,N); label("$B$",B,SW); label("$C$",C,SE); label("$D$",D,S,red);

label("$h$",(A+D)/2,E,red);
label("$15$",(A+B)/2,NW); label("$16$",(A+C)/2,NE); draw((0,-2.3)--(17,-2.3),gray+linewidth(0.6),Arrows(4)); label("$17$",(8.5,-2.3),S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

\[h=\dfrac{2\cdot24\sqrt{21}}{17}=\dfrac{48}{17}\sqrt{21}.\]
Here $\gcd(48,17)=1$ and $21=3\cdot7$ is squarefree, so $a+b+c=48+17+21=\boxed{86}$.
