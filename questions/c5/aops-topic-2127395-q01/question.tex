The shortest altitude of a triangle with side lengths $15$, $16$ and $17$ can be written as $\dfrac {a}{b}\sqrt c$, where $a$ and $b$ are relatively prime positive integers and $c$ is a positive integer not divisible by the square of any prime. Find $a+b+c$.
