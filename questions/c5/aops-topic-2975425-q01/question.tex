Isosceles triangle $ABC$ has $\angle CAB=30^\circ$, $\angle ABC=120^\circ$, $\angle BCA=30^\circ$. The perpendicular from $B$ meets $AC$ at $D$. Points $E$ and $F$ on $BD$ are such that $AE$ and $AF$ trisect $\angle CAB$, with $\angle FAC=10^\circ$ and $\angle EAC=20^\circ$. Ray $AE$ meets $BC$ at $G$. Find $\angle AGF$.
