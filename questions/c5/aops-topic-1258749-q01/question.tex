Let $\ell$ be the line $y=\dfrac{4}{9}x-8$. Find, in standard form $Ax+By=C$ with integers $A>0$, $B$, $C$ having no common factor greater than $1$, the equation of the line whose $x$-intercept equals the $y$-intercept of $\ell$ and whose $y$-intercept equals the $x$-intercept of $\ell$. (Are $72=-9x+4y$ and $-72=9x-4y$ different answers?)
