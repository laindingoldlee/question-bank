Find a formula for the number of ways to write a positive integer $a$ as an ordered sum of $b$ positive integers, each at most $c$ (that is, the number of solutions of $x_1+\cdots+x_b=a$ in integers $1\le x_i\le c$). Use it to count the ways for $a=10$, $b=3$, $c=4$.
