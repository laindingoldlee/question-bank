Put $u=a+1,v=2b+1,w=3c+1$. Adding the two given equations produces
\[\dfrac{u}{v}+\dfrac{v}{w}+\dfrac{w}{u}=3.\]
The three terms are positive and their product is 1. AM--GM therefore forces equality of all three, so $u=v=w$. The reciprocal equation gives $\dfrac{3}{u}=2$, hence $u=v=w=\dfrac{3}{2}$. Thus $a=\dfrac{1}{2},b=\dfrac{1}{4},c=\dfrac{1}{6}$, and the requested sum is $2+4+6=\boxed{12}$.
