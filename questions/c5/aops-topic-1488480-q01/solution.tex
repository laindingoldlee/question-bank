For example,
\[
\boxed{\left(8-\dfrac{8}{4}\right)\cdot4}=(8-2)\cdot4=6\cdot4=24 .
\]
Another one is $8-4\cdot(4-8)=8-4\cdot(-4)=8+16=24$. (With addition allowed the task is immediate: $8+8+4+4=24$.)
