By the binomial theorem,
\[101^{101}=(1+100)^{101}=1+101\cdot100+\dbinom{101}{2}100^2+\cdots\equiv1+10100\equiv101\pmod{1000},\]
since every later term is a multiple of $100^2=10000$. The last three digits are $\boxed{101}$.
