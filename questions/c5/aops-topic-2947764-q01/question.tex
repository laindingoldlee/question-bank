Two positive integers $A\le B$ are chosen from $1$ to $10$. Sam is told $A+B$ and Pat is told $AB$; both know this setup. They take turns saying ``I do not know $A$ and $B$,'' Sam first, until one of them knows. Both are perfect logicians and truthful. Let $k$ be the number of ``I do not know'' statements made before someone announces ``I know.'' Find the largest possible finite value of $k$.
