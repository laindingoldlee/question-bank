Note that $641=625+16=5^4+2^4$, so $5^4\equiv-2^4\pmod{641}$. Hence
\[5^{36}=\left(5^4\right)^9\equiv\left(-2^4\right)^9=-2^{36}\pmod{641},\]
and $2^{36}+5^{36}\equiv2^{36}-2^{36}=0\pmod{641}$. $\blacksquare$
