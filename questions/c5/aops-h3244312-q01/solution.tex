Choose the two suits held collectively by Reid and Po-Ru in $\binom42=6$ ways. Choose Reid's $13$ cards from those $26$ in $\binom{26}{13}$ ways; Po-Ru receives the rest. Independently distribute the other $26$ cards between the other two named players in $\binom{26}{13}$ ways. Every deal is counted once because Reid and Po-Ru's union determines its two suits. Thus
\[
N=6\binom{26}{13}^2=6(10400600)^2\equiv\boxed0\pmod{1000}.
\]
