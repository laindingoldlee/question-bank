Iron costs $\dfrac{4}{3}$ dollars per cubic centimetre and tin costs $\dfrac{3}{4}$ dollars per cubic centimetre. The volume of tin costing $\dfrac{4}{3}$ dollars is
\[\dfrac{\ \dfrac{4}{3}\ }{\dfrac{3}{4}}=\dfrac{4}{3}\cdot\dfrac{4}{3}=\boxed{\dfrac{16}{9}}\text{ cubic centimetres},\]
answer $\textbf{(C)}$.
