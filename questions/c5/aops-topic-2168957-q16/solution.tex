Write $k=10^{2021}$. If $S_n$ has side $s$, each side of $S_n$ is cut into pieces $\dfrac{s}{k+1}$ and $\dfrac{ks}{k+1}$, and a side of $S_{n+1}$ is the hypotenuse of a right triangle with these legs (the corner triangles of $S_n$).

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair[] V={(0,0),(4,0),(4,4),(0,4)};
real t=1/4;
pair[] W; for(int i=0;i<4;++i) W.push(V[i]+t*(V[(i+1)%4]-V[i]));
pair[] X; for(int i=0;i<4;++i) X.push(W[i]+t*(W[(i+1)%4]-W[i]));
draw(V[0]--V[1]--V[2]--V[3]--cycle,linewidth(1));
draw(W[0]--W[1]--W[2]--W[3]--cycle,blue+linewidth(0.9));
draw(X[0]--X[1]--X[2]--X[3]--cycle,deepgreen+linewidth(0.8));
label("$S_1$",(0.35,3.6)); label("$S_2$",(1.4,2.9),blue); label("$S_3$",(2,2),deepgreen);
label("$1$",(V[0]+W[0])/2,S); label("$k$",(W[0]+V[1])/2,S);
label("$k+1$",(4,2),E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

(The sketch uses the ratio $1:3$ instead of $1:k$.) By the Pythagorean theorem,
\[A_{n+1}=\dfrac{s^2+k^2s^2}{(k+1)^2}=rA_n,\qquad r=\dfrac{k^2+1}{(k+1)^2}<1 .\]
With $A_1=(k+1)^2$ the geometric series gives
\[S=\dfrac{A_1}{1-r}=\dfrac{(k+1)^2(k+1)^2}{(k+1)^2-(k^2+1)}=\dfrac{(k+1)^4}{2k}=\dfrac{k^4+4k^3+6k^2+4k+1}{2k}=\dfrac{k^3}{2}+2k^2+3k+2+\dfrac{1}{2k}.\]
Since $k$ is even, $\dfrac{k^3}{2}+2k^2+3k+2$ is an integer, and $0<\dfrac{1}{2k}<1$, so the greatest integer less than $S$ is
\[\dfrac{k^3}{2}+2k^2+3k+2=5\cdot10^{6062}+2\cdot10^{4042}+3\cdot10^{2021}+2 .\]
The four terms occupy different decimal places, so the digits are $5$, $2$, $3$, $2$ and zeros, with digit sum $\boxed{12}$, answer $\textbf{(D)}$.
