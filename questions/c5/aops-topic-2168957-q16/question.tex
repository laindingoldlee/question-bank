A square $S_1$ with side length $10^{2021}+1$ is drawn. For each positive integer $n$, the square $S_{n+1}$ is drawn with its vertices at the points that divide the sides of $S_n$ in the ratio $1:10^{2021}$ (all sides cut in the same order going around $S_n$). Let $A_n$ be the area of $S_n$ and $S=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}A_n$. What is the sum of the digits of the greatest integer less than $S$?

$\textbf{(A) } 3 \qquad \textbf{(B) } 6 \qquad \textbf{(C) } 9 \qquad \textbf{(D) } 12 \qquad \textbf{(E) } 15$
