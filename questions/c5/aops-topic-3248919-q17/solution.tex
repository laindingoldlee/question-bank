For an equilateral face of side $s$, removing equal corner triangles of side $t$ leaves alternating hexagon sides $t$ and $s-2t$. Regularity requires $t=s-2t$, so $t=\dfrac{s}{3}$. Each removed vertex pyramid is a scale-$\dfrac{1}{3}$ copy of one half of the original octahedron: its square base is parallel to the equatorial square. Its volume is therefore $\dfrac{1}{27}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{1}{54}$. Six vertices remove $\dfrac{1}{9}$, leaving $\boxed{\dfrac{8}{9}}$.
