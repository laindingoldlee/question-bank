In triangle $ABC$, $\angle BAC=78^\circ$. Points $E$ and $F$ inside the triangle satisfy $\angle ABE=\angle EBF=\angle FBC$ and $\angle ACF=\angle FCE=\angle ECB$. Let $D$ be the intersection of lines $EC$ and $BF$. Draw the configuration and find $\angle BDC$.
