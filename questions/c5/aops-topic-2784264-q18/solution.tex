The integer $108=2^2\cdot3^3$ has digit sum $9=3\cdot3$. Among the smaller three-digit numbers, $100,101,102,103,104,105,106,107$ either are prime or have largest prime factor respectively $5,17,13,7,53$, whereas their digit sums are at most 8 and cannot be three times those factors. Thus $\boxed{108}$.
