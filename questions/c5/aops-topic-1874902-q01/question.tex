Which of the following statements are true? (More than one may be true.)
\begin{enumerate}
\item[I.] The square of an integer is a whole number.
\item[II.] If $a\ne b$ and $b\ne c$, then $a\ne c$.
\item[III.] Every integer has a rational multiplicative inverse.
\item[IV.] The square root of a positive integer is a real number.
\end{enumerate}
