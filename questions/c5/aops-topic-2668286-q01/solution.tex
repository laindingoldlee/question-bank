First, $x\cdot0=0$ for all $x$: $x\cdot0=x(0+0)=x\cdot0+x\cdot0$, and subtracting $x\cdot0$ gives $0=x\cdot0$.

Then $0=(-1)\cdot0=(-1)\bigl(1+(-1)\bigr)=(-1)\cdot1+(-1)(-1)=-1+(-1)(-1)$. Adding $1$ to both sides gives $(-1)(-1)=1$. $\blacksquare$
