Write the equation as $-9x^3+4x^2-x-3=0$. For a cubic $ax^3+bx^2+cx+d=0$ the product of the three roots (with multiplicity) is $-\dfrac da$ by Vieta's formulas, so the product is
\[-\dfrac{-3}{-9}=\boxed{-\dfrac13}.\]
(The equation has one real solution, about $-0.53$, and two non-real complex conjugate solutions; the product is over all three.)
