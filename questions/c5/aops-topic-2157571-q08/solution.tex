From $(0,0)$ to $(2,3)$ Tim makes $2$ right and $3$ up steps: $\dbinom{5}{2}=10$ paths. From $(2,3)$ to $(4,4)$ he makes $2$ right and $1$ up step: $\dbinom31=3$ paths. Any first part can be combined with any second part, so there are $10\cdot3=\boxed{30}$ paths.
