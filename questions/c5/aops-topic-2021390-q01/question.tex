Let $a,b,c$ be nonnegative real numbers with $a+b+c=3$. Prove that
\[
\dfrac{a}{\sqrt{4-b}}+\dfrac{b}{\sqrt{4-c}}+\dfrac{c}{\sqrt{4-a}}\le\sqrt3 ,
\]
and determine when equality holds.
