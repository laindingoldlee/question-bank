Of all employees, $35\%$ are men and $65\%$ are women. The fraction attending is the weighted average
\[0.35\cdot0.20+0.65\cdot0.40=0.07+0.26=0.33,\]
so $\boxed{33\%}$ of the employees went to the picnic.
