A number is congruent modulo $9$ to its digit sum, so the big number is congruent to the sum of the digit sums of $1,\dots,2016$, which is congruent to $1+2+\cdots+2016=\dfrac{2016\cdot2017}{2}=1008\cdot2017$. Since $9\mid1008$, the remainder is $\boxed{0}$.
