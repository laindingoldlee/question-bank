Six cities $A,B,C,D,E,F$ form a cycle in that order. A seventh city $P$ is joined to every city on the cycle, and there are no other roads. A tourist starts at $P$ and each day chooses uniformly among the adjacent cities. The tour ends at the first return to $P$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Find the probability that the tour ends on day $2$.
\item Find the probability that it ends on day $3$.
\item Find the probability that it ends on day $n$ for every positive integer $n$.
\end{enumerate}
