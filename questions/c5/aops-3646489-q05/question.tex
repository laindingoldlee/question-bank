Aiden is running on a soccer field and he suddenly passes out! Luckily, he is in the middle of the field in the center circle such that anyone can run over him. He lies perpendicular to the middle line such that his toes touch the middle line and his head touches the circle. Given that the circle has a diameter of $60$ feet, Aiden is $6$ feet tall, and the distance between his toes to the center of the field can be written as $\sqrt{b}$ feet, find $b$.



Note: $\overline{AH}$ represents his head and toes, respectively, point $O$ represents the center of the circle, and the diagram is not to scale.
