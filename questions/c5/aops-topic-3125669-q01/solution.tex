Pair $(n+j)^2$ from the first sum with $(n-j)^2$ from the second, for $j=0,1,\ldots,n$. Each difference is $4nj$. Therefore the whole difference is $4n(0+1+\cdots+n)=4n\cdot\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}=2n^3+2n^2$.
