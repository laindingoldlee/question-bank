Each side of $ACE$ subtends a $120^\circ$ central angle in the circumcircle of radius $6$, so its length is $6\sqrt3$. Thus $ACE$ is equilateral with area $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}(6\sqrt3)^2=\boxed{27\sqrt3}$.
