\textbf{Method 1 (formulas).} The $k$th term is $k(40-k)$, so the sum is
\[
\sum_{k=1}^{39}k(40-k)=40\sum_{k=1}^{39}k-\sum_{k=1}^{39}k^2=40\cdot780-\dfrac{39\cdot40\cdot79}{6}=31200-20540=10660 .
\]

\textbf{Method 2 (counting).} Count the $3$-element subsets $\{a<b<c\}$ of $\{1,2,\dots,41\}$ by their middle element $b$: there are $b-1$ choices for $a$ and $41-b$ for $c$. With $k=b-1$ running from $1$ to $39$ this gives $\sum k(40-k)$, which is therefore
\[
\dbinom{41}{3}=\dfrac{41\cdot40\cdot39}{6}=\boxed{10660}.
\]
