Square the given equation:
\[
\left(x+\dfrac{1}{x}\right)^2=x^2+2\cdot x\cdot\dfrac{1}{x}+\dfrac{1}{x^2}=x^2+2+\dfrac{1}{x^2}=9 .
\]
So $x^2+\dfrac{1}{x^2}=9-2=\boxed{7}$. (The same idea gives higher powers, e.g. $x^3+\dfrac{1}{x^3}=\left(x+\dfrac{1}{x}\right)^3-3\left(x+\dfrac{1}{x}\right)=18$.)
