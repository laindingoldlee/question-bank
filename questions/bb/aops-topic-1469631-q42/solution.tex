Both sides are positive, so squaring gives an equivalent equation:
\[
a+\dfrac {b}{c}=\dfrac{a^2b}{c}.
\]
Multiply by $c$: $ac+b=a^2b$, so
\[
c=\boxed{\dfrac{b\left(a^2-1\right)}{a}} .
\]
For $c$ to be positive we need $a^2>1$, that is $a>1$; for every $a>1$ and $b>0$ this $c$ works (then $\dfrac {b}{c}=\dfrac{a}{a^2-1}$ and both sides squared equal $\dfrac{a^3}{a^2-1}$).

(Isolating $c$ is easiest after multiplying through by $c$; the equation is linear in $c$.)
