Let $a$, $b$, $c$ be positive real numbers such that
\[
\sqrt{a+\dfrac {b}{c}}=a\sqrt{\dfrac {b}{c}}.
\]
Express $c$ in terms of $a$ and $b$, and find the condition on $a$ for a solution to exist.
