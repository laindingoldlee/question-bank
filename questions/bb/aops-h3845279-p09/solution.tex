Two digits have product at most $81$, so at least three digits are needed. A three-digit number beginning with $1$ would require the other two digits to have product $120$, impossible. Beginning with $2$ would require product $60$, which no two digits have. Beginning with $3$ requires product $40$, attained by digits $5$ and $8$. Ordering these minimally gives $\boxed{358}$. Its product is $3\cdot5\cdot8=120$, and every number with more digits is larger.
