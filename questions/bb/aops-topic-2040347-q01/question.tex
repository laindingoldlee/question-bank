A rectangular grid of points has \(5\) rows and \(n\) columns (\(n\ge5\)), with neighbouring points one unit apart. There are a total of \(100\) squares whose sides are parallel to the grid lines and whose vertices are points of the grid. What is the value of \(n\)?
