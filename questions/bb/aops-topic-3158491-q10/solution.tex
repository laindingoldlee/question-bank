$ 20^{17}=2^{34}5^{17}$. Cube divisors have both exponents divisible by 3, giving 12 choices for the exponent of 2 and 6 for that of 5, or 72. Those also squares have exponents divisible by 6, giving 6 and 3 choices, or 18. Therefore $\boxed{72-18=54}$.
