Divide each equation by the positive product on its left. With $a=\dfrac{1}{x},b=\dfrac{1}{y},c=\dfrac{1}{z}$, the system is $2a+b=1$, $3b+c=1$, $a+6c=1$. Substituting $b=1-2a$ and $c=6a-2$ into the last yields $37a=13$. Hence $a=\dfrac{13}{37},b=\dfrac{11}{37},c=\dfrac{4}{37}$, giving $\boxed{(x,y,z)=(\dfrac{37}{13},\dfrac{37}{11},\dfrac{37}{4})}$.
