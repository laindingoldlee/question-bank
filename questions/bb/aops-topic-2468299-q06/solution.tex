The three long diagonals cut the hexagon into $6$ congruent equilateral triangles, and trapezoid $BCDE$ consists of $3$ of them (the diagonal $BE$ splits the hexagon into two congruent halves). So its area is $\dfrac12\cdot5=\boxed{2.5}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(2.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair[] P;string[] nm={"A","B","C","D","E","F"};for(int i=0;i<6;++i)P[i]=dir(180-60i);
fill(P[1]--P[2]--P[3]--P[4]--cycle,lightgray);
for(int i=0;i<6;++i){draw(P[i]--P[(i+1)%6],linewidth(0.8));label("$"+nm[i]+"$",P[i],P[i]);}
draw(P[0]--P[3],dashed);draw(P[1]--P[4],dashed);draw(P[2]--P[5],dashed);
\end{asy}
