Let $B$ be born in year $y$, so $A$ was born in $y-11$. Then $1770-(y-11)=3+7(1748-y)$, i.e. $1781-y=12239-7y$, so $y=1743$. In $1750$, $B$ was $7$ and $A$ was $18$, a sum of $\boxed{25}$.
