The primes below 10 are $2,3,5,7$. If B had an odd prime, the remaining two digits could include 2 or could both be odd, giving different sum parities. Thus B has 2. C is now equally likely to be the median exactly when C has 5: A may have 3 or 7. Finally, A knows C is not the median only when A has 3. Thus the three-digit number is $\boxed{325}$.
