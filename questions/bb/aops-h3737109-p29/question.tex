Anna, Benny, Charles are each secretly given a number by Daniel. After Daniel announces to everyone that "the three of you each received a different prime number under $10$, and you received each number with equal probability", the children correctly make the following deductions in order,

- Benny: "I know the parity of the sum of our numbers"

- Charles: "There is a $50\%$ chance that my number is the median"

- Anna: "Charles' number is not the median"

Let $A,B,C$ be Anna, Benny, and Charles' number. What is $100A+10B+C$?
