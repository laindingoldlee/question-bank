For $\dfrac{n}{3}$ to be a three-digit integer, $n$ must be a multiple of $3$ with $300\le n\le2997$. For $3n$ to be three-digit, $34\le n\le333$. Combining gives $n=300,303,\ldots,333$, a total of $\dfrac{333-300}{3}+1=\boxed{12}$, choice (A).
