In a survey, $30$ people reported that they enjoy some combination of walking, hiking and jogging. The numbers who enjoy only walking, only hiking and only jogging are equal. The numbers who enjoy exactly walking and hiking, exactly walking and jogging, and exactly hiking and jogging are also equal. Half as many people enjoy exactly two of the activities as enjoy exactly one. If three people enjoy all three activities, how many people enjoy jogging?
