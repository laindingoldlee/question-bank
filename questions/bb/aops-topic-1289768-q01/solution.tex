Let $a$ be the number in each "only one" region and $b$ the number in each "exactly two" region. Then
\[
3a+3b+3=30,\qquad 3b=\dfrac{3a}{2}.
\]
So $a=2b$ and $9b=27$, giving $b=3$ and $a=6$. Joggers are those in the "only jogging" region, the two "exactly two" regions that include jogging, and the centre:
\[
a+2b+3=6+6+3=\boxed{15}.
\]
