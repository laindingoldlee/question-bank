Order the weights $a\le b\le c\le d\le e$. The smallest two sums are $a+b=12$ and $a+c=14$, and the largest sum is $d+e=25$. Every weight appears in four pair sums, so the total weight is $\dfrac{182}{4}=\dfrac{91}{2}$. Hence $a+b+c=\dfrac{41}{2}$, giving $a=12+14-\dfrac{41}{2}=\dfrac{11}{2}$ and $c=\dfrac{17}{2}$. The next-largest sum is $c+e=23$, so the complete weights are $\dfrac{11}{2},\dfrac{13}{2},\dfrac{17}{2},\dfrac{21}{2},\dfrac{29}{2}$, which reproduce every listed sum. The middle weight is $w=c$, hence $\boxed{2w=17}$.
