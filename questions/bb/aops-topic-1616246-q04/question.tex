A \emph{song} is a sequence of $8$ notes, each of which is one of the $26$ letters a to z (letters may repeat). The vowels are a, e, i, o and u. Yvonne calls a song \emph{acceptable} if its first note or its third note (or both) is a vowel. Her friend calls a song \emph{acceptable} if its first two notes are consecutive letters of the alphabet in increasing order (such as c followed by d), or are both vowels. What is the positive difference between the number of songs acceptable to Yvonne and the number acceptable to her friend? Express your answer in the form $m\cdot26^6$.
