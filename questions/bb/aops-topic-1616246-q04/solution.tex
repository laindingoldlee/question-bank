There are $26^8$ songs in all.

\textbf{Yvonne.} A song fails her test when neither the first nor the third note is a vowel: $21\cdot26\cdot21\cdot26^5=441\cdot26^6$ songs. So she accepts
\[
26^8-441\cdot26^6=(676-441)\cdot26^6=235\cdot26^6
\]
songs.

\textbf{Her friend.} Only the first two notes matter; the other six can be anything, giving the factor $26^6$. The ordered pairs of consecutive letters in increasing order are (a,b), (b,c), \dots, (y,z): $25$ pairs. The pairs of two vowels number $5\cdot5=25$. No two vowels are consecutive letters, so these sets of pairs do not overlap, and the friend accepts $(25+25)\cdot26^6=50\cdot26^6$ songs.

The difference is $235\cdot26^6-50\cdot26^6=\boxed{185\cdot26^6}$ ($=57\,149\,418\,560$).
