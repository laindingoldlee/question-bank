A right triangle $ABC$ has legs $CA=3$ and $CB=4$ and hypotenuse $AB=5$. The blue circle has diameter $CA$, the green circle has diameter $CB$, and the pink circle has diameter $AB$. An orange circle lies inside the pink circle and outside the blue and green circles, and is tangent to all three of them. Find the radius of the orange circle.
