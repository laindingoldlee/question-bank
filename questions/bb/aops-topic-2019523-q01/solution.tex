Write $N=\dfrac{25!}{15!}=16\cdot17\cdots25$. Since $20\cdot25=500$,
\[
\dfrac{N}{1000}=\dfrac{16\cdot17\cdot18\cdot19\cdot21\cdot22\cdot23\cdot24}{2}=8\cdot17\cdot18\cdot19\cdot21\cdot22\cdot23\cdot24 .
\]
\textbf{Finding $B$.} $B$ is the units digit of $\dfrac{N}{1000}$. Multiplying units digits: $8\cdot7\to6$, $6\cdot8\to8$, $8\cdot9\to2$, $2\cdot1\to2$, $2\cdot2\to4$, $4\cdot3\to2$, $2\cdot4\to8$. So $B=8$.

\textbf{Finding $A$ without more multiplication.} $N$ is divisible by $9$ (it contains the factors $18$ and $21$), so its digit sum
\[
1+1+8+6+1+6+7+6+2+A+B=38+A+B
\]
is a multiple of $9$. Thus $A+B\in\{7,16\}$; with $B=8$ this forces $A+8=16$, so $A=8$. (Indeed $N=11861676288000$.)

Hence $A\cdot B=\boxed{64}$.
