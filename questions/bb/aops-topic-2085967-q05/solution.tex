\(\lfloor\log k\rfloor\) is one less than the number of digits of \(k\). It is \(0\) for \(1\le k\le 9\), \(1\) for the \(90\) two-digit numbers, \(2\) for the \(900\) three-digit numbers and \(3\) for \(k=1000\). The sum is
\[
0+90+1800+3=\boxed{1893}.
\]
