List the sets $\{a,b,c\}$ with $a<b<c$ and $a+b+c=14$ by their smallest element:
\begin{itemize}
\item $a=1$: $\{1,4,9\},\{1,5,8\},\{1,6,7\}$;
\item $a=2$: $\{2,3,9\},\{2,4,8\},\{2,5,7\}$;
\item $a=3$: $\{3,4,7\},\{3,5,6\}$;
\item $a\ge4$ is impossible, since then $a+b+c\ge4+5+6=15$.
\end{itemize}
There are $8$ sets, and $3$ of them ($\{1,4,9\},\{2,4,8\},\{3,4,7\}$) contain a $4$. The fraction is $\boxed{\dfrac{3}{8}}$.
