The line \(y=2x\) passes through the origin with direction vector \(\mathbf d=(1,2)\). The projection is the point \(F=t\mathbf d\) with \(\overrightarrow{FP}\perp\mathbf d\):
\[
\bigl((7,1)-t(1,2)\bigr)\cdot(1,2)=0\iff 9-5t=0\iff t=\dfrac{9}{5} .
\]
So \(F=\left(\dfrac{9}{5},\dfrac{18}{5}\right)\).

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
pair P=(7,1), F=(9/5,18/5);
draw((-0.5,0)--(8,0),gray,Arrow(TeXHead)); draw((0,-0.5)--(0,5.5),gray,Arrow(TeXHead));
draw((-0.25,-0.5)--(2.75,5.5),blue+linewidth(0.9));
draw(P--F,red);
pair u=unit((1,2)), w=unit(P-F);
draw((F+0.3*u)--(F+0.3*u+0.3*w)--(F+0.3*w));
dot(P); dot(F); dot((0,0));
label("$P(7,1)$",P,E); label("$F\left(\dfrac{9}{5},\dfrac{18}{5}\right)$",F,W);
label("$y=2x$",(2.75,5.5),E,blue); label("$O$",(0,0),SE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The distance is
\[
PF=\sqrt{\left(7-\dfrac{9}{5}\right)^2+\left(1-\dfrac{18}{5}\right)^2}=\sqrt{\dfrac{676}{25}+\dfrac{169}{25}}=\dfrac{13\sqrt5}{5},
\]
agreeing with the point-to-line distance formula \(\dfrac{|2\cdot 7-1|}{\sqrt{2^2+1^2}}=\dfrac{13}{\sqrt5}\).
\[
\boxed{F=\left(\dfrac{9}{5},\dfrac{18}{5}\right),\quad PF=\dfrac{13\sqrt5}{5}}
\]
Projections are useful whenever a perpendicular distance or a foot of a perpendicular is needed, as in this computation.
