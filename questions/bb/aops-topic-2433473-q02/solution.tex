The circumference is $2\pi\cdot1=2\pi$, so the fraction is
\[\dfrac{\dfrac\pi3}{2\pi}=\boxed{\dfrac16}.\]
(The arc subtends a central angle of $\dfrac16\cdot360^\circ=60^\circ$.)

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0); real R=1.4;
draw(circle(O,R),gray); draw(arc(O,R,30,90),blue+linewidth(1.4));
pair A=R*dir(30),B=R*dir(90);
draw(O--A); draw(O--B); dot(O); dot(A); dot(B);
label("$A$",A,NE); label("$B$",B,N); label("$O$",O,S);
draw(arc(O,0.35,30,90)); label("$60^\circ$",0.45*dir(60),dir(60),fontsize(8pt)); label("$1$",A/2,SE);
\end{asy}
