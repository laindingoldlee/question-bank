Let $a>0$, let $m$ be an integer and $n$ a positive integer. Define $a^{\dfrac{1}{n}}$ to be the positive real number whose $n$-th power is $a$, and define $a^{\dfrac{m}{n}}=\left(a^{\dfrac{1}{n}}\right)^m$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $a^{\dfrac{m}{n}}=\sqrt[n]{a^m}$, where $\sqrt[n]{a^m}$ denotes the positive $n$-th root of $a^m$.
\item[(b)] Explain why these definitions are the only ones compatible with the rule $\left(a^r\right)^s=a^{rs}$, and why the condition $a>0$ is needed.
\end{enumerate}
