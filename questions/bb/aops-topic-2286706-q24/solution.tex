Since the mode is unique and appears $23$ times, every other value appears at most $22$ times. The other $k-23$ entries therefore need at least $\left\lceil\dfrac{k-23}{22}\right\rceil$ distinct values, and this many suffice (use $22$ copies of each new value, with fewer of the last one). Hence
\[D(k)=1+\left\lceil\dfrac{k-23}{22}\right\rceil .\]
$D(k)=92$ means $\left\lceil\dfrac{k-23}{22}\right\rceil=91$, that is, $90\cdot22<k-23\le91\cdot22$, or $2003<k\le2025$. The smallest such $k$ is $\boxed{2004}$, answer $\textbf{(A)}$. (With $k=2004$: the mode $23$ times and $91$ other values, $90$ of them $22$ times and one of them once, since $23+90\cdot22+1=2004$.)
