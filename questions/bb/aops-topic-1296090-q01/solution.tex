For a fixed perimeter the circle encloses the most area (the isoperimetric inequality). With circumference $23$, the radius is $\dfrac{23}{2\pi}$ and the area is $\pi\left(\dfrac{23}{2\pi}\right)^2=\dfrac{529}{4\pi}\approx42.1$, so the answer is $\boxed{42}$ square feet. (A rectangle could give at most $\left(\dfrac{23}{4}\right)^2\approx33$.)

\end{asy}
