Tina's $10$ equally likely pairs have sums $3,4,5,5,6,6,7,7,8,9$. For a sum $s$, Sergio wins with probability $\dfrac{10-s}{10}$. Averaging,
\[\dfrac{1}{10}\cdot\dfrac{7+6+5+5+4+4+3+3+2+1}{10}=\dfrac{40}{100}=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{5}}.\]
