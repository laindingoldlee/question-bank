There are $366$ possible birthdays (counting February $29$). With $4\cdot366=1464$ people, it is possible that each birthday is shared by exactly $4$ people. With $1465$ people, by the pigeonhole principle some birthday has at least $\left\lceil\dfrac{1465}{366}\right\rceil=5$ people. The answer is $\boxed{1465}$.
