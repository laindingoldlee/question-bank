Since the digit $2$ appears in $2020_{(x)}$, the base satisfies $x\ge3$; and $x$ is a decimal digit, so $3\le x\le9$. Now
\[2020_{(x)}=2x^3+0\cdot x^2+2x+0=2x^3+2x,\qquad 2\cdot\overline{yx}=2(10y+x)=20y+2x.\]
The equation becomes $2x^3=20y$, i.e. $x^3=10y$. Then $2\mid x^3$ and $5\mid x^3$, and since $2$ and $5$ are prime, $2\mid x$ and $5\mid x$, so $10\mid x$. But $3\le x\le9$, a contradiction. Hence no such $x,y$ exist. $\blacksquare$
