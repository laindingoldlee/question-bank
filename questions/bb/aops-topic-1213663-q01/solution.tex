We need $x\ge0$; put $s=\sqrt x\ge0$, so $x=s^2$. The equation becomes $5s^2-2sy-4s+y^2+1=0$, which can be written as a sum of squares:
\[(s-y)^2+(2s-1)^2=s^2-2sy+y^2+4s^2-4s+1=0.\]
Both squares must vanish: $s=\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $y=s=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Hence $\boxed{x=\dfrac{1}{4},\ y=\dfrac{1}{2}}$, and this pair does satisfy the equation.
