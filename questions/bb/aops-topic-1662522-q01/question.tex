Chicken nuggets are sold in packs of $9$ and $20$. The owner wants to add one new pack size of $x$ nuggets, where $x>1$ is an integer, so that every positive whole number of nuggets can be bought exactly. Prove that no such $x$ exists. Show also that no single new size $x>1$ even makes every whole number of at least $2$ nuggets possible.
