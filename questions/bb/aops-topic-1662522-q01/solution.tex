\textbf{Every positive amount.} An order of $1$ nugget cannot be filled: every pack, old or new, has more than $1$ nugget. So no $x>1$ works.

\textbf{Every amount of at least $2$.} An order of $2$ nuggets can only be one pack of $2$ (the packs of $9$ and $20$ are too big), so we would need $x=2$. But then an order of $3$ nuggets cannot be filled: it would need a single pack of $3$ (two packs give at least $4$), and there is none. So again no single $x>1$ works. $\blacksquare$
