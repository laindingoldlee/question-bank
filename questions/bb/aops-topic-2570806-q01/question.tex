For an angle $\theta$, the point $P$ on the unit circle at angle $\theta$ (measured anticlockwise from the positive $x$-axis) is $(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)$. Prove that for every angle $\theta$,
\[\sin(-\theta)=-\sin\theta\qquad\text{and}\qquad\cos(-\theta)=\cos\theta.\]
