Let $L$ be the number of knaves. If $L<10$, the $10-L$ knights each say $L$, while every knave can say at most 10. Thus the sum is at most $L(10-L)+10L=100-(10-L)^2\le99$. If $L=10$, all statements must be false, so every claimed count is at most 9 and the sum is at most 90. The bound 99 is attained by nine knaves each saying 10 and one knight saying 9. The maximum is $\boxed{99}$.
