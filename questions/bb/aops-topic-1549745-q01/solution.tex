For each $j$ with $2\le j\le2014$, the triangle $A_1A_jA_{j+1}$ has a right angle at $A_j$ and a leg $A_jA_{j+1}=1$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair[] P; P.push((0,0)); P.push((1,0));
for(int k=1;k<8;++k){ pair d=unit(P[k]-P[0]); P.push(P[k]+rotate(90)*d); }
for(int k=2;k<9;++k) draw(P[0]--P[k],dashed+gray(0.4));
for(int k=0;k<8;++k) draw(P[k]--P[k+1],linewidth(1));
for(int k=1;k<8;++k){ pair d=unit(P[k]-P[0]), e=unit(P[k+1]-P[k]); real s=0.12;
  draw(P[k]-s*d--P[k]-s*d+s*e--P[k]+s*e,blue); }
for(int k=0;k<9;++k) dot(P[k]);
label("$A_1$",P[0],dir(-110));
for(int k=1;k<9;++k) label("$A_{"+string(k+1)+"}$",P[k],unit(P[k]-P[0]));
label("$1$",(P[0]+P[1])/2,S);
label("$\sqrt2$",P[0]+0.58*(P[2]-P[0])+(0.1,-0.1));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

By the Pythagorean theorem,
\[
A_1A_{j+1}^2=A_1A_j^2+A_jA_{j+1}^2=A_1A_j^2+1 .
\]
Starting from $A_1A_2^2=1$, each step adds $1$, so $A_1A_j^2=j-1$ for every $j$ (this is the ``spiral of Theodorus''; the figure shows the first nine points). Therefore
\[
A_1A_{2015}=\boxed{\sqrt{2014}}.
\]
