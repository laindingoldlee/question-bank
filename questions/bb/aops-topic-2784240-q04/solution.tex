The route $23\to24\to12\to6\to3\to0$ takes $5$ turns.

Four turns are not enough. A final move to $0$ must come from $3$ (subtract $3$), from $-1$ (add $1$) or from $0$ itself. The numbers reachable in exactly three turns are: after one turn $24,20$; after two $25,21,12,17,10$; after three $26,22,18,13,9,6,14,11,7,5$. None is $3$ or $-1$, so $0$ cannot be reached in four turns (nor in fewer, by the same lists). The answer is $\boxed{5}$.

(Halving an odd number would give a non-integer, and then adding $1$, subtracting $3$ or halving never produces an integer again, so allowing it changes nothing.)
