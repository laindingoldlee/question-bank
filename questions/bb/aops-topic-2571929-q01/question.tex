Let $ABC$ be a scalene triangle that is not right-angled, with circumcentre $O$. Lines $a$, $b$, $c$ pass through $O$ and are parallel to $BC$, $CA$, $AB$ respectively. Let $A_1$ and $A_2$ be the feet of the perpendiculars from $A$ to $b$ and $c$; let $B_1$ and $B_2$ be the feet of the perpendiculars from $B$ to $a$ and $c$; and let $C_1$ and $C_2$ be the feet of the perpendiculars from $C$ to $a$ and $b$. The circle through $A$, $A_1$, $A_2$ meets $a$ again at a point $X\ne O$; the circle through $B$, $B_1$, $B_2$ meets $b$ again at $Y\ne O$; and the circle through $C$, $C_1$, $C_2$ meets $c$ again at $Z\ne O$. Prove that triangle $XYZ$ is similar to triangle $ABC$.
