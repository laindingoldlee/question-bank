For each root, multiply its polynomial equation by $r_i^{j-m}$ and sum. For $j>m$ this gives $\boxed{P_j+c_1P_{j-1}+\cdots+c_mP_{j-m}=0}$. Thus Newton's sums continue as a homogeneous recurrence beyond the degree. In the example the initial Newton sums are $P_1=2,P_2=2,P_3=11$. The recurrence $P_j=2P_{j-1}-P_{j-2}+3P_{j-3}$ gives $P_4=26$ and $\boxed{P_5=47}$.
