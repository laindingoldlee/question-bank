The series has first term $26$ and common difference $-2$, so the sum of the first $n$ terms is
\[S_n=\dfrac n2\left(2\cdot26-2(n-1)\right)=n(27-n).\]
Setting $n(27-n)=182$ gives $n^2-27n+182=0$. To factor, look for two numbers with product $182=2\cdot7\cdot13$ and sum $27$: these are $13$ and $14$. So $(n-13)(n-14)=0$, and $n=13$ or $n=14$.

Both work: the $14$th term is $26-2\cdot13=0$, so adding it does not change the sum. The answer is $\boxed{13\text{ or }14}$ terms.
