Let $a=9+4\sqrt5$ and $b=9-4\sqrt5$, so $a+b=18$ and $ab=1$. Then $s_n=a^n+b^n$ is an integer with $s_n=18s_{n-1}-s_{n-2}$, $s_0=2$, $s_1=18$. This gives $s_2=322$, $s_3=5778$, $s_4=103682$ and $s_5=1860498$.

Since $0<b<1$, $0<b^5<1$, so $a^5=s_5-b^5$ lies strictly between $s_5-1$ and $s_5$. The answer is $\boxed{1860497}$.
