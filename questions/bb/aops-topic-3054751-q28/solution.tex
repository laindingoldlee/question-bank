Scale $RS$ to 1 and set $R=(-\dfrac{1}{2},0),S=(\dfrac{1}{2},0)$. Then $P=(0,\sin60^\circ)$, $T=(\dfrac{1}{2}+\cos72^\circ,\sin72^\circ)$, and $V=(-\dfrac{1}{2}-\cos72^\circ,\sin72^\circ)$. Thus $TV$ is horizontal, and the acute angle $\theta=PTV$ has $\tan\theta=\dfrac{\sin72^\circ-\sin60^\circ}{\cos60^\circ+\cos72^\circ}$. Sum-to-product identities reduce this to $\dfrac{2\cos66^\circ\sin6^\circ}{2\cos66^\circ\cos6^\circ}=\tan6^\circ$. Both angles lie between 0 and 90 degrees, where tangent is injective, so $\boxed{\theta=6^\circ}$.

\end{asy}
