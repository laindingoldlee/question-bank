Lines $m$ and $n$ are parallel. Four distinct points $A,B,C,D$ are chosen on line $n$ and five distinct points $E,F,G,H,I$ on line $m$, and the $20$ segments joining each of the four points on $n$ to each of the five points on $m$ are drawn. The points are chosen to maximise the number of intersection points of these segments in the region strictly between the two lines. What is this maximum number?
