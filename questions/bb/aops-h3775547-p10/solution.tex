For the finite partial sum $S_N$, subtraction gives $S_N-\dfrac{S_N}{2}=\sum_{k=1}^{N}\dfrac{1}{2^k}-\dfrac{N}{2^{N+1}}=1-\dfrac{1}{2^N}-\dfrac{N}{2^{N+1}}$. Therefore $S_N=2-\dfrac{N+2}{2^N}$, which tends to $\boxed2$. The conclusion is correct. The stated argument is invalid because multiplying each term of the geometric series by $2$ gives numerators all equal to $2$, not numerators $k$.
