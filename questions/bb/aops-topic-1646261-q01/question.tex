Every side of an equilateral triangle is divided into $n$ equal segments, and through each dividing point lines parallel to the sides are drawn. This divides the triangle into $n^2$ congruent mini-triangles. A \emph{chain} is a sequence of distinct mini-triangles in which each mini-triangle after the first shares a side with the previous one. What is the largest possible number of mini-triangles in a chain?
