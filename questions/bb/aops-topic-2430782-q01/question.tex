\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that the difference of any two odd squares (such as $1,9,25,49,\dots$) is a multiple of $8$.
\item[(b)] A student argues: ``two odd numbers differ by an even number, so squaring gives a multiple of $4$''. Explain what is wrong with this reasoning and how it can be repaired to give $8$.
\end{enumerate}
