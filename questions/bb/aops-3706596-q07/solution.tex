Burnside's lemma averages fixed sets under the four rotations. The identity fixes $\binom{36}{2}=630$. A half-turn fixes precisely the eighteen pairs of opposite cells. Quarter-turns have only four-cell orbits and cannot fix a two-cell set. Thus the count is $\dfrac{630+18}{4}=\boxed{162}$.
