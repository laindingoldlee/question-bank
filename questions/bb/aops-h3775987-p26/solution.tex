For each $n=0,\ldots,127$, count earlier integers with larger decimal digit sum. Maintain counts $C_j$ of earlier numbers with digit sum $j$, initially all zero, and an accumulator $T=0$. At step $n$, compute its digit sum $s$, add $\sum_{j>s}C_j$ to $T$, then increase $C_s$ by one. This counts every pair once, at its larger member. Exact evaluation of these 128 steps gives $\boxed{2861}$. The restriction $m\le n$ adds no diagonal pairs because the two digit sums are equal when $m=n$.
