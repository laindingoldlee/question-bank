With $0^0=1$, every factor $n^0$ equals $1$, including the one for $n=0$. So the sum is the geometric series
\[\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^n=1+\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{4}+\cdots=\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{1}{2}}=\boxed{2}.\]
