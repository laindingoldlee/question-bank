\textbf{(a)} All $\dbinom{N}{n}$ sets of $n$ marbles are equally likely. The sets consisting of marbles of colour $i$ only number $\dbinom{a_i}{n}$ (this is $0$ when $a_i<n$), and these events for different colours are disjoint. So
\[
P=\dfrac{\dbinom{a_1}{n}+\dbinom{a_2}{n}+\cdots+\dbinom{a_k}{n}}{\dbinom{N}{n}} .
\]

\textbf{(b)} Here $N=18$ and
\[
P=\dfrac{\dbinom52+\dbinom62+\dbinom72}{\dbinom{18}{2}}=\dfrac{10+15+21}{153}=\boxed{\dfrac{46}{153}}.
\]
