A box contains $a_1$ marbles of colour $1$, $a_2$ marbles of colour $2$, \dots, $a_k$ marbles of colour $k$, with $N=a_1+\cdots+a_k$ marbles in all. You draw $n$ marbles at random without replacement.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find a formula for the probability that all $n$ marbles drawn have the same colour.
\item[(b)] Evaluate it when there are $5$, $6$ and $7$ marbles of three colours and $n=2$.
\end{enumerate}
