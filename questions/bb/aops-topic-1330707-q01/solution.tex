By the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality in Engel form,
\[
\dfrac{1^2}{a}+\dfrac{2^2}{b}+\dfrac{3^2}{c}\ge\dfrac{(1+2+3)^2}{a+b+c}=36 .
\]
Equality holds when $\dfrac{1}{a}=\dfrac{2}{b}=\dfrac{3}{c}$, i.e. $(a,b,c)=\left(\dfrac{1}{6},\dfrac{1}{3},\dfrac{1}{2}\right)$. $\blacksquare$
