Xavier writes a sequence of positive integers on a blackboard, beginning with $1$. Each term thereafter equals the smallest positive integer that cannot be expressed as the product of one or more distinct terms already on the blackboard. The first several terms of Xavier's sequence are $1$, $2$, $3$, $4$, $5$, $7$ and $9$. He stops writing when he first writes a number greater than $1000$. At this point, how many composite numbers will Xavier have written?
