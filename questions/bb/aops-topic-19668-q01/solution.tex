The first \(n\) letters of the alphabet occupy \(1+2+\cdots+n=\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}\) positions. Since \(\dfrac{18\cdot 19}{2}=171<180\le 190=\dfrac{19\cdot 20}{2}\), position \(180\) is in the block of the \(19\)th letter, which is \(\boxed{S}\).
