The total distance is $60+30=90$ km.

\textbf{(a)} Average speed is total distance divided by total time, and the total time runs from departure to arrival, \emph{including} stops: from 9:30 to 11:30 is $2$ hours. So the average speed is
\[
\dfrac{90}{2}=\boxed{45},
\]
that is, $45$ kilometres per hour.

\textbf{(b)} The moving time excludes the $45$-minute break: $2-0.75=1.25$ hours ($45$ minutes from $A$ to $B$ and $30$ minutes from $B$ to $C$). The average moving speed is
\[
\dfrac{90}{1.25}=\boxed{72},
\]
that is, $72$ kilometres per hour.
