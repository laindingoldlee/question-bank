A bus leaves Town $A$ at 9:30 a.m. It reaches Town $B$, $60$ km away, at 10:15 a.m., stops there for a $45$-minute break, and then travels a further $30$ km to Town $C$, arriving at 11:30 a.m.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the average speed of the bus for the whole journey from $A$ to $C$.
\item[(b)] Find its average speed while it was moving.
\end{enumerate}
