Two girls, Annie and Wendy, and two boys, Carlos and Mike, grew up in the same neighbourhood. Their last names, in some order, are Mosby, Parker, Richardson and Williams. As children, one wanted to be a dancer, one a clown, one a teacher and one a firefighter. Their actual careers are chef, dentist, surgeon and computer programmer (one each). Use the clues to match each person (first and last name) with their childhood ambition and their career.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] The four are Mike, Ms.\ Mosby, the child dancer and the computer programmer.
\item[(b)] Either the dentist or the computer programmer has the same first and last initials.
\item[(c)] Either Wendy is now a chef or Mike's last name is Richardson.
\item[(d)] Williams never wanted to be a teacher.
\item[(e)] The child clown did not become a computer programmer.
\item[(f)] The dentist never wanted to be a teacher or a dancer.
\item[(g)] Annie is the surgeon.
\end{enumerate}
