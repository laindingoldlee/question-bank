Company A earns $45$ dollars for $3$ hours of tutoring and $105$ dollars for $7$ hours. Company B earns $34$ dollars for $2$ hours and $102$ dollars for $6$ hours. Both earnings are proportional to the hours. If students sign up for the same number of hours at both companies, which company earns more?
