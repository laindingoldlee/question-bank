Each pair overlap has area $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$, from two 120-degree circular segments. The triple overlap has area $\dfrac{\pi-\sqrt3}{2}$, from three 60-degree sectors minus twice the centre triangle. Inclusion-exclusion gives union area $3\pi-3(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2})+\dfrac{\pi-\sqrt3}{2}=\dfrac{3\pi}{2}+\sqrt3$. Thus $\boxed{a+b+c=8}$.
