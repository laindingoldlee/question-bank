Thirty coins lie on a table, $17$ of them showing heads. You must separate the coins into two piles, not necessarily of the same size, each of which shows the same number of heads. Unfortunately you are blindfolded and cannot feel the difference between the two sides of a coin, but you may move coins and turn them over. How can you do it? Prove that your method works.
