A building has $100$ floors. There is an unknown threshold: an egg dropped from floor $k$ breaks if $k\ge f$ and survives if $k<f$, where $f$ is one of $1,2,\dots,100$, or the egg survives a drop from every floor. You have two identical eggs. A broken egg cannot be used again; an egg that survives can be dropped again. What is the least number of drops that always suffices to determine $f$ (or that no floor breaks the egg)? Describe a strategy achieving it.
