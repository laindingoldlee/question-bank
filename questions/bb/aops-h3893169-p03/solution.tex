Write $e_r$ for the elementary symmetric sum of degree $r$ in the roots. The coefficients give $e_1=-1,e_6=-1,e_8=3$. The roots are nonzero. Taking complements of pairs in the product of all eight roots gives
\[\sum_{j<k}\dfrac{1}{x_jx_k}=\dfrac{e_6}{e_8}=-\dfrac{1}{3}.\]
The double sum factors as $\left(\sum_i x_i\right)\left(\sum_{j<k}\dfrac{1}{x_jx_k}\right)$, and equals $(-1)(-\dfrac{1}{3})=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{3}}$.
