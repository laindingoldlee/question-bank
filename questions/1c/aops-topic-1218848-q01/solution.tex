He falls in only if he reaches $-6$ within $8$ steps. Reaching $-6$ needs at least $6$ more tails than heads at some point.
\begin{itemize}
\item At step $6$: all $6$ tails: $1$ sequence of the first $6$ tosses, times $2^2$ for the remaining tosses (he stops, but count all completions of the $8$ tosses for probability): $4$ of the $256$ equally likely toss sequences.
\item At step $8$ (first time): positions after $8$ steps $=-6$ means $7$ tails, $1$ head, without having been at $-6$ at step $6$: the head is among the first $6$ tosses: $6$ sequences.
\end{itemize}
($-6$ can only be reached at even steps.) So $4+6=10$ of $256$ sequences end in the hole, and the probability of finishing is $1-\dfrac{10}{256}=\boxed{\dfrac{123}{128}}$.
