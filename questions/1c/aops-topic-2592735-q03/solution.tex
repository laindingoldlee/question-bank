The terms are exactly the positive integers that leave remainder $1$ when divided by $5$. A square $k^2$ (with $k>0$) leaves remainder $1$ mod $5$ exactly when $k\equiv1$ or $k\equiv4\pmod5$, since the squares of $0,1,2,3,4$ are $0,1,4,4,1$ mod $5$.

The positive $k$ with $k\equiv\pm1\pmod5$ are $1,4,6,9,11,14,16,19,21,24,26,29,\dots$, so the $12$th square in the sequence is
\[29^2=\boxed{841}=5\cdot169-4=a_{169}.\]
