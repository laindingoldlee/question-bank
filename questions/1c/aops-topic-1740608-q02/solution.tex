Every score is $25$ times one of $0,1,\dots,5$, so a total is $25s$ with $s$ a sum of three numbers from $\{0,\dots,5\}$. Such sums take every value from $0$ to $15$ (for instance $s=3q+r$ with $0\le r<3$ is achieved by $r$ darts of $q+1$ and $3-r$ darts of $q$, and $s=15$ by three $5$'s). So there are $\boxed{16}$ possible totals, $0,25,\dots,375$.
