Two lines meet at right angles at $O$. A semicircle of radius $1$ has its diameter on the first line and is tangent to the second line, and a smaller semicircle has its diameter on the second line and is tangent to the first line; both semicircles lie in the same one of the four right angles formed by the lines. The smaller semicircle meets the larger semicircular arc at a point other than $O$, dividing the larger arc into two arcs whose lengths are in the ratio $1:5$. What is the radius of the smaller semicircle? Express your answer in simplest radical form.
