Write $x=m+t$ with integer $m\ge0$ and $0\le t<1$. The equation $t=\dfrac{m+t}{5}$ gives $t=\dfrac{m}{4}$. Positivity excludes $m=0$; the bound $t<1$ allows exactly $m=1,2,3$. Thus $x=\dfrac{5}{4},\dfrac{5}{2},\dfrac{15}{4}$, whose sum is $\boxed{\dfrac{15}{2}}$.
