Explain in what sense ``division is repeated subtraction''. Precisely: let $a\ge0$ and $b>0$ be whole numbers with $a=qb$. Starting from $a$, subtract $b$ repeatedly. Prove that the result first reaches $0$ after exactly $q=a\div b$ subtractions. Then explain the error in the following reasoning: ``$4\div2$ is repeated subtraction: $4-2=2$, $2-2=0$, so $4\div2=0$.''
