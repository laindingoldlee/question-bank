Jade has a bag with $10$ blue, $11$ black, $12$ red and $15$ white balls. He takes balls out without looking.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] How many balls must he take out to be sure of having at least two balls of the same colour?
\item[(b)] How many balls must he take out to be sure of having all the balls of at least one colour?
\end{enumerate}
