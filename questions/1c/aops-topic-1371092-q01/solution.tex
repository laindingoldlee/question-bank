Let the distance be $d$ km. The difference of the times is $15$ minutes $=\dfrac{1}{4}$ hour:
\[
\dfrac{d}{12}-\dfrac{d}{18}=\dfrac{1}{4}\iff\dfrac{3d-2d}{36}=\dfrac{1}{4}\iff d=\boxed{9\text{ km}}.
\]
Check: $\dfrac{9}{12}$ h $=45$ min and $\dfrac{9}{18}$ h $=30$ min differ by $15$ minutes.
