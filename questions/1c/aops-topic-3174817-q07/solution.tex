Divide numerator and denominator by $x^2$. The denominator becomes $x^{-2}+3+x^2=(x+x^{-1})^2+1=(\sqrt3+\dfrac{1}{5})^2+1=\dfrac{101+10\sqrt3}{25}$. Thus the expression is $\dfrac{25}{101+10\sqrt3}$, and the requested sum is $25+101+10+3=\boxed{139}$.
