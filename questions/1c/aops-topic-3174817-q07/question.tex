A nonzero complex number $x$ satisfies $x+\dfrac{1}{x}=\sqrt3+\dfrac{1}{5}$. Express $\dfrac{x^2}{1+3x^2+x^4}$ as $\dfrac{a}{b+c\sqrt d}$ with positive integers, common coefficient gcd $1$, and squarefree $d$. Find $a+b+c+d$.
