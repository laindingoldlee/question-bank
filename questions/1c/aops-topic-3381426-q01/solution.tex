After Bob has run for $t$ seconds, his distance is $5t$, and Alice has run $3(t+20)$. The condition is $5t=\dfrac{3}{2}\cdot3(t+20)$, so $5t=\dfrac{9}{2}t+90$ and $\boxed{t=180}$ seconds. Their distances are $900$ and $600$ meters.
