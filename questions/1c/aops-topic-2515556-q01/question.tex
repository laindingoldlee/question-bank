Ruka's heart rate is initially $50$ beats per minute. She starts talking to Kazuya. During each second of talking, if her heart rate is $n$ beats per minute, then with probability $\dfrac{n(n+1)}{100}\%$ (that is, $\dfrac{n(n+1)}{10000}$) it increases by $1$ beat per minute, independently of the other seconds; otherwise it stays the same. What is the expected number of seconds until her heart rate reaches $90$ beats per minute?
