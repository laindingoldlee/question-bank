For $k$ bags the least possible total is $1+2+\cdots+k=\dfrac{k(k+1)}{2}$. Since $\dfrac{19\cdot20}{2}=190$ but $\dfrac{20\cdot21}{2}=210$, at most $19$ bags are possible, attained with occupancies $1,\ldots,19$. Thus $\boxed{19}$.
