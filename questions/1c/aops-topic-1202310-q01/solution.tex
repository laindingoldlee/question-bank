Between two honks the car moves $5v$ metres closer, so the second sound has $5v$ metres less to travel and arrives $\dfrac{5v}{330}$ seconds earlier than it otherwise would. Hence
\[5-\dfrac{5v}{330}=4.6\quad\Longrightarrow\quad v=\dfrac{0.4\cdot330}{5}=\boxed{26.4\ \text{m/s}}.\]
