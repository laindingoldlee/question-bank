Write $a=gu,b=gv$, where $g\ge13$, $\gcd(u,v)=1$, and $u,v\ge2$ because neither divides the other. Then the sum is $g(u+v)$ and the lcm is $guv$.

The pair $(u,v)=(3,4)$ with $g=14$ gives sum $98$ and lcm $168$. Any candidate with $u+v\ge8$ has sum at least $13\cdot8=104$, so cannot improve it. Up to interchanging $u,v$, the coprime possibilities with sum at most $7$ are $(2,3),(2,5),(3,4)$. Their least admissible $g$ and sums are respectively
\[
(27,135),\quad(16,112),\quad(14,98).
\]
Therefore the minimizing pair is $(42,56)$ up to order, and the requested lcm is $\boxed{168}$.
