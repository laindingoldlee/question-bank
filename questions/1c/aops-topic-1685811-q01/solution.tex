Since $9\cdot\overline{ABCD}<10000$, we have $\overline{ABCD}<1112$, so $A=1$. Then $\overline{DCBA}$ ends in $1$, so $9D$ ends in $1$, which forces $D=9$. Now
\[
9(1000+100B+10C+9)=9000+100C+10B+1
\]
simplifies to $9081+900B+90C=9001+100C+10B$, i.e.\ $890B+80=10C$, or $C=89B+8$. Since $C\le9$, $B=0$ and $C=8$. So $\overline{ABCD}=1089$ (indeed $9\cdot1089=9801$), and
\[
A\cdot B\cdot C\cdot D=1\cdot0\cdot8\cdot9=\boxed{0}.
\]
