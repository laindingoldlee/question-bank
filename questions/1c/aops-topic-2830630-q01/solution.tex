\textbf{$N\ge73$.} Rooks filling $6$ complete rows ($72$ rooks) contain at most $6$ pairwise non-attacking rooks, one per row.

\textbf{$N=73$ suffices.} Suppose some placement has no $7$ non-attacking rooks. View rows and columns as the two sides of a bipartite graph, each rook being an edge. By K\"onig's theorem, the maximum number of non-attacking rooks (a maximum matching) equals the minimum number of lines (rows and columns) covering all rooks. So all rooks lie in $a$ rows and $b$ columns with $a+b\le6$, covering at most $12a+12b-ab\le72$ squares. Hence $73$ rooks always contain $7$ non-attacking ones, and $N=\boxed{73}$.
