Let $f(x)=a_nx^n+a_{n-1}x^{n-1}+\cdots+a_1x+a_0$ be a polynomial with $n$ even, $n\ge6$, such that $f(a)+f(b)=f(a+b)$ for all $a$ and $b$ with $|a|<10$ and $|b|<10$, and $a_n+a_{n-1}+\cdots+a_1+a_0=324$. Find the average of the elements of the set $S=\{a_{n-1},a_{n-3},\dots,a_5,a_3,a_1\}$.
