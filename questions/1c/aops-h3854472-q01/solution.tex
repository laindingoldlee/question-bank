Place the right-angle vertex at $(0,0)$ and the other vertices at $(4,0)$ and $(0,3)$. The inradius is $\dfrac{(3+4-5)}{2}=1$, so $I=(1,1)$.
The hypotenuse midpoint is $(2,\dfrac{3}{2})$, making the median line $3x-4y=0$. The point-to-line distance is
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{|3(1)-4(1)|}{\sqrt{3^2+(-4)^2}}=\dfrac{1}{5}}.
\]
