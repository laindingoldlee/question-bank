The letters are all different, with vowels E, U, O, I, A.

(a) Put Q fourth; choose the vowel for the last place ($5$ ways); arrange the other $5$ letters in the remaining $5$ places ($5!=120$ ways): $5\cdot120=\boxed{600}$.

(b) There are $7!=5040$ arrangements, so the probability is $\dfrac{600}{5040}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{42}}$.
