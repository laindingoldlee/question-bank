The series converges only for $|x|<1$, and then it is a geometric series with sum $\dfrac{x}{1-x}$. So $\dfrac{x}{1-x}=2019$, i.e.\ $x=2019-2019x$ and $x=\boxed{\dfrac{2019}{2020}}$, which indeed satisfies $|x|<1$.
