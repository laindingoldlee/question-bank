A game has six suspect cards, six weapon cards and nine room cards. One uniformly chosen card of each category is hidden. The remaining eighteen cards are dealt uniformly into three labelled hands of six. A player guesses independently and uniformly among identities not in their own hand in each category. What is the unconditional chance that the entire guess is correct?
