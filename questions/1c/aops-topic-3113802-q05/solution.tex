Given hand counts $(a,b,c)$, the success probability is $\dfrac{1}{(6-a)(6-b)(9-c)}$. Weighting by the hand distribution and using $\dfrac{\binom5a}{6-a}=\dfrac{\binom6a}{6}$ and $\dfrac{\binom8c}{9-c}=\dfrac{\binom9c}{9}$ gives \[\dfrac{1}{324\binom{18}{6}}\sum_{a+b+c=6\atop a,b\le5}\binom6a\binom6b\binom9c.\]The unrestricted sum is $\binom{21}{6}$ by counting six selections from groups of sizes $ 6,6,9 $. It includes two forbidden terms, $(6,0,0)$ and $(0,6,0)$, each $ 1 $. Hence the answer is $\dfrac{\binom{21}{6}-2}{324\binom{18}{6}}=\boxed{\dfrac{2087}{231336}}$.
