Because $16^2+30^2=256+900=1156=34^2$, the converse of Pythagoras shows the triangle is right. Its area is $\dfrac{1}{2}(16)(30)=\boxed{240}$.
\begin{asy}
import geometry; usepackage("amsmath"); size(240);
pair A=(0,16),B=(0,0),C=(30,0);draw(A--B--C--cycle);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);
\end{asy}
