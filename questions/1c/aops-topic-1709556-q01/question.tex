In a primary school there are $94$ students in the $7$th grade. Some of them take part in extracurricular activities: Spanish, German and sports. $40$ students study Spanish, $27$ study German and $60$ do sports. Of the students doing sports, $24$ also study Spanish. $10$ students study both Spanish and German, and $12$ students study German and do sports. Only $4$ students take part in all three activities. How many students take part in exactly one activity, and how many take part in none?
