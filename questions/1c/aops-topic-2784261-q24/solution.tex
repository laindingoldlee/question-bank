The first crossing must carry one of each type: carrying just one or two of one type leaves unequal positive populations on the starting bank. After this crossing the banks contain $(2,2)$ and $(1,1)$. A return by one person creates unequal positive populations; returning both restores the initial state. Thus no progress beyond the first crossing is possible, and the task is $\boxed{\text{impossible}}$.
