Some turkeys must measure exactly $9$ minutes on a stopwatch. The stopwatch can be started once and stopped once, and paused and restarted at most once in between. They also have six timers that ring $7$, $8$, $10$, $10$, $11$ and $15$ minutes after they are started; a timer cannot be paused once it is started. Starting or stopping a timer or the stopwatch takes no time, and several of them can be started at the same moment (each turkey holds one device). How can they make the stopwatch show exactly $9$ minutes?
