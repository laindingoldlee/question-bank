The edge lengths are positive integers whose product is $2^{100}$, so each is a power of $2$: $2^a$, $2^b$, $2^c$ with $a+b+c=100$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair o=(0,0); real a=4.2,bb=2.2,c=2.8; pair dz=(1.3,0.9);
draw(o--(a,0)--(a,c)--(0,c)--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
draw((a,0)--(a,0)+dz--(a,c)+dz--(0,c)+dz--(0,c),linewidth(0.8));
draw((a,c)--(a,c)+dz,linewidth(0.8));
draw(o--dz,dashed); draw(dz--(a,0)+dz,dashed); draw(dz--(0,c)+dz,dashed);
label("$2^{a}$",(a/2,0),S); label("$2^{c}$",(0,c/2),W); label("$2^{b}$",(a,0)+dz/2,SE);
\end{asy}

The condition $2^a\ge512=2^9$ means $a\ge9$, and similarly $b,c\ge9$. Put $a'=a-9$, $b'=b-9$, $c'=c-9$; then $a',b',c'$ are nonnegative integers with
\[a'+b'+c'=100-27=73.\]
By stars and bars, the number of ordered solutions is $\dbinom{73+2}{2}=\dbinom{75}{2}=\boxed{2775}$.
