Set the three equal expressions equal to a parameter $t$. Then the points of the line are
\[
x=1+2t,\qquad y=-1-t,\qquad z=2t ,
\]
so the line passes through $(1,-1,0)$ with direction vector $(2,-1,2)$. Substituting into the equation of the plane,
\[
3(1+2t)+2(-1-t)-2t=5\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad 1+2t=5\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad t=2 .
\]
Hence the intersection point is
\[
\boxed{(5,-3,4)} .
\]
Check: $\dfrac{5-1}{2}=2$, $-(-3+1)=2$, $\dfrac{4}{2}=2$, and $3\cdot5+2\cdot(-3)-4=5$.

pair P=(4.8,1.5),dv=(0.45,1);
draw(P--P+4*dv,blue+linewidth(1));
draw(P-1.5*dv--P,blue+dashed);
draw(P-1.5*dv-(0,0)--P-3.2*dv,blue+linewidth(1));
dot(P,red);label("$P$",P,SE,red);
label("$3x+2y-z=5$",(9.6,0.3),E);
label("line",P+4*dv,N,blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
