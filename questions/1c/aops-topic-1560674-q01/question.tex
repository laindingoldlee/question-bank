Coco buys a very large rectangular chocolate bar. Each day she cuts the largest possible square off the bar and eats it, and when the remaining part of the bar is a square, she eats all of it. The table shows the area, in square centimetres, of the square Coco eats on each day.
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccccc}
\text{Day} & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 5 & 6\\\hline
\text{Area eaten (cm}^2) & 225 & 225 & 81 & 36 & 9 & 9
\end{array}
\]
If Coco finishes the chocolate bar on Day $6$, what was the length of the longer side of the bar when she bought it?
