\textbf{(a)} $91=30\cdot3+1$, so $91\bmod3=\boxed{1}$. The statement $91\equiv1\pmod3$ means that $91$ and $1$ leave the same remainder when divided by $3$, or equivalently that $3$ divides $91-1=90$. (Quick check: the digit sum of $91$ is $10$, which leaves remainder $1$.)

\textbf{(b)} $91=13\cdot7$, so $91\bmod7=\boxed{0}$, and $91$ is \textbf{not} prime.
