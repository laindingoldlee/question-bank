January has $31=4\cdot7+3$ days. Every day of the week occurs $4$ times in the first $28$ days, and the last $3$ days, January $29$, $30$, $31$, fall on the same weekdays as January $1$, $2$, $3$: Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday. So exactly these three weekdays occur $5$ times in January, and there are $\boxed{3}$ possibilities.
