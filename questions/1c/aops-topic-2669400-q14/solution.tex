For $k\ge2$, $\dfrac1{k^2}<\dfrac1{k(k-1)}=\dfrac1{k-1}-\dfrac1k$. Hence
\[\sum_{k=1}^{n}\dfrac1{k^2}<1+\sum_{k=2}^{n}\left(\dfrac1{k-1}-\dfrac1k\right)=1+1-\dfrac1n<2\]
(for $n=1$ the sum is $1<2$). $\blacksquare$
