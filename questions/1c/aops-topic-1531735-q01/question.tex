Anthony wants to make a poster reading ``2017''. He has three different colours of paint, red, blue and yellow, and paints each of the four digits $2$, $0$, $1$, $7$ with one colour. How many ways can he paint the poster so that the even digits have different colours from each other and the odd digits have the same colour as each other? (A colour may be used for both an even and an odd digit.)
