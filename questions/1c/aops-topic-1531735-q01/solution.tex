The even digits $2$ and $0$ get two different colours: $3\cdot2=6$ ways. The odd digits $1$ and $7$ get one common colour, chosen freely: $3$ ways. The two choices are independent, so there are
\[
6\cdot3=\boxed{18}
\]
ways. (The count $6$ comes from also requiring the three groups ``$2$'', ``$0$'' and ``$1,7$'' to have three different colours, which the problem does not ask.)
