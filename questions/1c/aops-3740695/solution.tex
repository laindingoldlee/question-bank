Factor $1260=2^2\cdot3^2\cdot5\cdot7$. A reduced fraction terminates in base 10 exactly when its denominator has no prime factors other than 2 and 5. Thus $n$ must cancel $3^2\cdot7=63$, so $63\mid n$. For $n=63$, the fraction becomes $\dfrac{1}{20}=0.05$. Therefore the smallest value is $\boxed{63}$.
