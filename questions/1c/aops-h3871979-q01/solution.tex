Classify $n$ by its remainder modulo $5$. Since $7x\equiv 2x\pmod 5$, the smallest nonnegative $x$ in residues $n=0,1,2,3,4$ is respectively $0,3,1,4,2$. The least representable totals in these residues are $0,21,7,28,14$. Every larger number in the same residue is representable by adding copies of $5$. Therefore the positive nonrepresentable numbers are $\boxed{1,2,3,4,6,8,9,11,13,16,18,23}$.
