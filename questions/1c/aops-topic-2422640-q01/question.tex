Let $a$ and $d$ be real numbers, and let $S_n$ be the sum of the first $n$ terms of the arithmetic series $a+(a+d)+(a+2d)+\cdots$. Prove that $S_n$ tends to a finite limit as $n\to\infty$ if and only if $a=d=0$. (So $0+0+0+\cdots$ is the only convergent infinite arithmetic series.)
