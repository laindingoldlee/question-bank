Quarterly interest is $2.5$ percent, and $5$ years is $20$ quarters. After $5$ years the balance is $10000(1.025)^{20}\approx16386.16$ dollars. You pay half, $8193.08$ dollars, leaving $8193.08$, which grows for another $20$ quarters to $8193.08\cdot(1.025)^{20}\approx13425.32$ dollars. The total payment is
\[
8193.08+13425.32=\boxed{21618.40\text{ dollars}}.
\]
