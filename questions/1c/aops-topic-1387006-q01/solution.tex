\textbf{By Pascal's rule twice.} Using $\dbinom{m+1}{k+1}=\dbinom mk+\dbinom m{k+1}$,
\[
\dbinom{n+2}{r+2}=\dbinom{n+1}{r+1}+\dbinom{n+1}{r+2}
=\left[\dbinom nr+\dbinom n{r+1}\right]+\left[\dbinom n{r+1}+\dbinom n{r+2}\right],
\]
which is the right-hand side.

\textbf{By counting.} Choose $r+2$ people from $n$ ordinary people and two special people $X$, $Y$. Either both $X$ and $Y$ are chosen ($\dbinom nr$ ways to choose the rest), exactly one of them is chosen ($2\dbinom n{r+1}$ ways) or neither is ($\dbinom n{r+2}$ ways). Adding gives $\dbinom{n+2}{r+2}$. $\blacksquare$
