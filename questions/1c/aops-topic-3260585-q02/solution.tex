(a) Every positive product of distinct positive magnitudes also occurs negatively by reversing one sign. The only unpaired negative products are $-k^2$ for which $k^2$ cannot be factored into two distinct integers from $1$ through $15$. These $k$ are
\[1,5,7,8,9,10,11,12,13,14,15.\]
For the excluded $k=2,3,4,6$, alternative factors are $1\cdot4,1\cdot9,2\cdot8,3\cdot12$. Checking the factor pairs of each listed square shows none has distinct factors both at most $15$. Thus the distinct-product sum is
\[-(1+25+49+64+81+100+121+144+169+196+225)=\boxed{-1175}.\]
(b) If the set elements are $t_i$, then $\sum_i t_i=0$ and
\[\sum_{i<j}t_it_j=\dfrac{(\sum_i t_i)^2-\sum_i t_i^2}{2}=-\sum_{k=1}^{15}k^2=\boxed{-1240}.\]
The different answers reflect the two different counting conventions.
