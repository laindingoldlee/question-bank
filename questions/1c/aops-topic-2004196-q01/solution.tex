Dan's total is $4+4=8$ points. John throws a single dart, which scores at most $4$ points, so John's total is at most $4<8$. A tie is impossible, whatever John's accuracy, so the probability is $\boxed{0}$. (The $75\%$ and the geometry of the board are irrelevant: John loses with certainty.)
