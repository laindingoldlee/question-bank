A dartboard is a square of side length $2$ with a circular target inscribed in it. A dart scores $4$ points if it hits the circular target, $2$ points if it hits the board outside the circle, and $0$ points if it misses the board. Dan and John compete: Dan throws two darts and John throws only one, and the player with the larger total score wins. John hits the circular target with probability $75\%$. Given that Dan scores $4$ points with each of his two darts, what is the probability that the two players tie?
