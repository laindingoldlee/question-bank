Let $Q(x)=P(x)-x$. It is a nonnegative quartic with roots at $1$ and $3$. A real root of odd multiplicity would cause a sign change, so each has even multiplicity. Since the total degree is four, $Q(x)=c(x-1)^2(x-3)^2$. At $x=2$, $Q(2)=2$, hence $c=2$. Therefore $P(4)=4+2\cdot9\cdot1=\boxed{22}$.
