A group of $100$ people are labelled $1$ to $100$. Every person whose label $n$ is a composite number gives a hug to every person $k$ with $k<n$ and $\gcd(k,n)=1$; people with other labels give no hugs. Which people give the most hugs, and how many hugs does each of them give?
