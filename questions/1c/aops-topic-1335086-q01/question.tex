For a positive integer $m$, write $a\equiv b\pmod m$ (``$a$ is congruent to $b$ mod $m$'') when $m$ divides $a-b$, that is, when $a$ and $b$ leave the same remainder on division by $m$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that if $a\equiv b$ and $c\equiv d\pmod m$, then $a+c\equiv b+d$ and $ac\equiv bd\pmod m$.
\item[(b)] Use (a) to find the remainder when $3^{100}$ is divided by $7$.
\end{enumerate}
