Real coefficients force the conjugates $3-i$ and $1+2i$ to be roots too. So
\[f(x)=a\bigl(x^2-6x+10\bigr)\bigl(x^2-2x+5\bigr)=\boxed{a\left(x^4-8x^3+27x^2-50x+50\right)},\ a\ne0.\]
