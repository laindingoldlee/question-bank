Square repeatedly modulo $100$: $17^2=289\equiv89$, $17^4\equiv89^2=7921\equiv21$, $17^8\equiv21^2=441\equiv41$, $17^{16}\equiv41^2=1681\equiv81$. Then $17^{17}\equiv81\cdot17=1377\equiv77$. The last two digits are $\boxed{77}$.
