A right cone has a base with a circumference of $16\pi$ inches and a height of $30$ inches. The height of this cone is reduced while the circumference of the base stays the same. The volume of the shorter cone is $192\pi$ cubic inches. What is the ratio of the shorter height to the original height? Express your answer as a common fraction.
