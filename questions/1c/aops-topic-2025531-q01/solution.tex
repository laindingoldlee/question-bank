(a) The three flavour probabilities add to $1$, so
\[P(\text{chocolate})=\boxed{1-x-y}.\]
(This requires $x+y\le1$.)

(b) The cone and the flavour are chosen independently, so
\[P(\text{chocolate and blue})=P(\text{chocolate})\cdot P(\text{blue})=\boxed{x(1-x-y)}.\]
