Max chooses one of two ice cream cones, a blue cone or a red cone; he chooses the blue cone with probability $x$. Independently of the cone, he chooses one of three flavours: marshmallow with probability $x$, vanilla with probability $y$, and otherwise chocolate.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What is the probability that he picks chocolate?
\item[(b)] What is the probability that he picks chocolate and the blue cone?
\end{enumerate}
