With just one prime factor the possibilities are 4, 8, 16, 32, 64;9, 27, 81;25;49. With at least two prime factors the only prime pair possible is 2 and 3, since $2^2\cdot5^2=100$. The allowed mixed powers below 100 are 36 and 72. Three distinct primes already require $2^23^25^2>100$. Including 1 gives $10+2+1=\boxed{13}$.
