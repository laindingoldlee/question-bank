Take $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, $E=(4,3)$. Put $\theta=\angle BAD$, so $\tan\theta=\dfrac{3}{4}$ and $\angle BAC=3\theta$. Write $D=(4t,3t)$; since $D$ is the midpoint of $BC$, $C=(8t-4,6t)$.

The triple-angle identity gives $\cot3\theta=-\dfrac{44}{117}$. Hence
\[
\dfrac{8t-4}{6t}=-\dfrac{44}{117},
\]
so $t=\dfrac{39}{100}$ and $C=(-\dfrac{22}{25},\dfrac{117}{50})$. Therefore
\[
BC^2=\left(\dfrac{122}{25}\right)^2+\left(\dfrac{117}{50}\right)^2
=\dfrac{2929}{100}=29.29.
\]
The nearest integer is $\boxed{29}$.
