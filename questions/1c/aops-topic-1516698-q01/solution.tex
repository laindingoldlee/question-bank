The factor $8$ needs a partner equal to $3$, and the other three numbers give $3$ as $4+5-6$. So
\[
\boxed{(4+5-6)\times8=24}.
\]
(Other expressions also work, for example $(5-(6-4))\times8$ or $8\times(4-(6-5))$; all of them use the same idea of making $3$ from $4$, $5$, $6$.)
