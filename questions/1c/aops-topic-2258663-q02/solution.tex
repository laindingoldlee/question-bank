The period of $24$ months is $24\cdot4=96$ weeks, so the simple interest is
\[2450\cdot0.03\cdot96=2450\cdot2.88=7056\ \text{dollars},\]
and the total amount paid is
\[2450+7056=\boxed{9506}\ \text{dollars}.\]
(Each instalment is $\dfrac{9506}{24}\approx396.08$ dollars.)
