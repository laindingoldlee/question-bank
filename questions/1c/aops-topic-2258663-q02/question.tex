John bought a refrigerator whose cash price is \$$2450$ and paid for it in $24$ equal monthly instalments. The store charged simple interest at $3\%$ per week on the full price of \$$2450$ for the whole period of $24$ months; take one month to be $4$ weeks. What was the total amount John paid for the refrigerator?
