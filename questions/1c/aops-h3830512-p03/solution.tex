Let $E$ be the expectation when no recent head can still contribute, and $E_j$ when a single usable head is followed by $j$ tails, $0\le j\le3$. A head from any $E_j$ ends the experiment; four intervening tails make the old head unusable on the next flip. Thus $E=1+\dfrac{1}{3}E+\dfrac{2}{3}E_0$, and $E_0=1+\dfrac{1}{3}E_1$, $E_1=1+\dfrac{1}{3}E_2$, $E_2=1+\dfrac{1}{3}E_3$, $E_3=1+\dfrac{1}{3}E$. Therefore $E_0=\dfrac{40}{27}+\dfrac{E}{81}$ and $E=\dfrac{3}{2}+E_0$. Solving gives $\boxed{\dfrac{483}{160}}$.
