The direction of a chord is perpendicular to the radius through the midpoint of its minor arc. The minor arc from $5$ to $9$ has midpoint at mark $7$, while the minor arc from $3$ to $8$ has midpoint halfway between marks $5,6$. Those two radii differ by $1.5\cdot30^\circ=45^\circ$. Rotating both directions by $90^\circ$ preserves their angle, so the acute angle between the chord lines is $\boxed{45^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
import geometry;
usepackage("amsmath");
size(270);
pair P5=dir(90-150),P9=dir(90-270),P3=dir(0),P8=dir(90-240);draw(circle((0,0),1));draw(P5--P9,blue);draw(P3--P8,red);for(int k=1;k<=12;++k)label(string(k),1.12dir(90-30*k));
\end{asy}
