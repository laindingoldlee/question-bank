Let $J$ be Jerry's number of cards. It equals $1$ plus a binomial count from nine fair independent deals, so $\mathbb E[J]=1+\dfrac{9}{2}=\dfrac{11}{2}$. Conditional on the cards, his win probability is $\dfrac{J}{10}$. Averaging gives $\dfrac{11}{20}$, hence $p+q=\boxed{31}$.
