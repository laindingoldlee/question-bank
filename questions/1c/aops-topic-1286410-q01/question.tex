Let $a,b,c$ be monomials of the form $kx^e$ with $k$ a positive integer and $e$ a nonnegative integer, where the lcm of two monomials is the monomial whose coefficient is the lcm of the coefficients and whose exponent is the larger exponent. If
\[
\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=4x^2,\qquad\operatorname{lcm}(b,c)=12x^3,\qquad\operatorname{lcm}(a,c)=6x^3,
\]
find all possible values of $c$.
