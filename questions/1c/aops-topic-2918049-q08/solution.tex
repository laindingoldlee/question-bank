Let $\angle AOB=x$ and $\angle BOC=y$, with $x+y=76^\circ$ and $x<y$. Then $\angle MON=\dfrac{x+y}2=38^\circ$ and $\angle MOP=19^\circ$. Since $\angle MOB=\dfrac x2<19^\circ$, $P$ is past $B$, and $\angle BOP=19^\circ-\dfrac x2=5^\circ$. So $x=28^\circ$ and $\angle BOC=\boxed{48^\circ}$.
