Bernie and Jim start together and run around a $400$-foot circular track in the same direction. Bernie runs $\dfrac{5}{2}$ times as fast as Jim, and the difference of their speeds is $5$ miles per hour. How many times will Bernie pass Jim in the first $3$ hours that they run? (Do not count the start.)
