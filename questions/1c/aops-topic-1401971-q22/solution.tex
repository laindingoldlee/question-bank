If Jim's speed is $j$ mph, then $\dfrac{5}{2}j-j=\dfrac{3}{2}j=5$, so $j=\dfrac{10}{3}$ mph and Bernie runs $\dfrac{25}{3}$ mph. Only the difference matters: relative to Jim, Bernie gains $5$ miles per hour, that is $5\cdot5280=26400$ feet per hour. They meet each time Bernie gains another full lap of $400$ feet. In $3$ hours he gains $3\cdot26400=79200$ feet, which is exactly $\dfrac{79200}{400}=198$ laps. So they meet $\boxed{198}$ times (the last meeting is exactly at the end of the third hour).
