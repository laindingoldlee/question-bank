In triangle $ABC$, point $X$ lies on $AB$ and point $Y$ lies on $BC$ such that segments $AY$ and $CX$ intersect at point $Z$ in the interior of $ABC$. If the area of triangle $AZC$ is $9$, the area of triangle $YZC$ is $8$, and the area of triangle $AZX$ is $3$, what is the area of quadrilateral $BXZY$? Express your answer as a common fraction.
