In a lottery there are $4$ bins, and each bin contains the $26$ letters A to Z. A player draws one letter from each bin, each letter equally likely and the bins independent. The player wins if the four letters drawn can be arranged to spell JAKE (so JAKE and KAJE both win). What is the probability of winning?
