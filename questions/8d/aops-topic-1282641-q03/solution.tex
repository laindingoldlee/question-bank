The total distance is $350$ miles, so at an average of $35$ mph the trip takes $10$ hours:
\[
\dfrac{150}{v}+\dfrac{200}{v+25}=10 .
\]
Multiplying by $v(v+25)$: $150(v+25)+200v=10v(v+25)$, so $350v+3750=10v^2+250v$, that is
\[
v^2-10v-375=0\iff(v-25)(v+15)=0 .
\]
Since $v>0$, $v=\boxed{25}$. Check: $\dfrac{150}{25}+\dfrac{200}{50}=6+4=10$ hours.
