At each cut-off corner the hexagon has interior angles $120^\circ$, so the cut makes $60^\circ$ with the adjacent triangle sides. Each removed triangle is therefore equilateral, of side $2,3,5$, respectively. Subtracting their areas from the side-$11$ triangle gives $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}(121-4-9-25)=\boxed{\dfrac{83\sqrt3}{4}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

pair A=(0,0),B=(11,0),C=11*dir(60),P=(2,0),R=(8,0),Ii=B+3*dir(120),M=C+5*dir(-60),Ee=C-5*dir(60),Ss=2*dir(60);draw(A--B--C--cycle);filldraw(P--R--Ii--M--Ee--Ss--cycle,lightgray);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,N);label("$P$",P,S);label("$R$",R,S);label("$I$",Ii,NE);label("$M$",M,NE);label("$E$",Ee,NW);label("$S$",Ss,NW);
\end{asy}
