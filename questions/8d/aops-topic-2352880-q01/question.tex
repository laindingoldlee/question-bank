Let $m\ge2$ and $t\ge2$ be integers. Tara writes $k\ge m$ numbers on a chalkboard so that the list has a unique mode, written exactly $m$ times. Let $K$ be the least value of $k$ for which every such list of $k$ numbers must contain at least $t$ distinct numbers. Then $K=a(mt)+b(m)+c(t)+d$, where $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$ are constants (the same for all such $m$ and $t$). Find $a^4+b^4+c^4+d^4$.
