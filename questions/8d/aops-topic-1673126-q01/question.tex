Two circles, each of radius $r$, intersect so that the centre of each circle lies on the other circle.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the area and the perimeter of the region common to both circles.
\item[(b)] Find the area and the perimeter of the region covered by the two circles together.
\end{enumerate}
