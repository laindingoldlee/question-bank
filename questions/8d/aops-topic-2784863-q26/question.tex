On a sphere of radius 2 miles, a point $ P $ lies on a southern parallel of latitude $\phi $. Traveling north along a meridian for $\pi $ miles reaches a parallel on which traveling east $\pi $ miles completes exactly one full circuit. Starting again at $ P $, travel $\pi $ miles south along the meridian, continuing across the south pole if needed. Find the radius of the parallel through the final point.
