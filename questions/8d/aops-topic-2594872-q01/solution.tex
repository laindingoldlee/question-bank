$\dfrac37+\dfrac26=\dfrac37+\dfrac13=\dfrac{9+7}{21}=\dfrac{16}{21}$. Long division of $16$ by $21$ gives the remainders $16,13,4,19,1,10,16,\dots$, which repeat after $6$ steps, with digits $7,6,1,9,0,4$. So
\[\dfrac{16}{21}=0.\overline{761904},\]
and the repetend is $\boxed{761904}$. (Check: $\dfrac{761904}{999999}=\dfrac{16}{21}$, since $21\cdot761904=16\cdot999999$.)
