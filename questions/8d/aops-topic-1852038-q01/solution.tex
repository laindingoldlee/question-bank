Let the apex be $T$ and the centre of the base be $O$. The slant height of a face with base edge $a$ is $TK$, where $K$ is the midpoint of that edge; $TOK$ is a right triangle with legs $TO=h$ and $OK=\dfrac {b}{2}$. Similarly the slant height $TL$ to the midpoint $L$ of an edge $b$ is the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs $h$ and $OL=\dfrac {a}{2}$. (In the figure, $TK=13$ is blue and $TL=15$ is red.)

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair v=(0.5,0.3);
pair P1=(0,0),P2=(18,0),P3=P2+10*v,P4=10*v;
pair Cn=(P1+P3)/2,T=Cn+(0,12);
pair K=(P1+P2)/2,L=(P2+P3)/2;
draw(P1--P2--P3,linewidth(0.9));draw(P3--P4--P1,dashed);
draw(T--P1,linewidth(0.9));draw(T--P2,linewidth(0.9));draw(T--P3,linewidth(0.9));draw(T--P4,dashed);
draw(T--Cn,dashed);draw(Cn--K,dotted);draw(Cn--L,dotted);
draw(T--K,blue+linewidth(0.9));draw(T--L,red+linewidth(0.9));
dot(Cn);
label("$T$",T,N);label("$O$",Cn,dir(-30));label("$K$",K,S);label("$L$",L,dir(-20));
label("$18$",(P1+K)/2,S);label("$10$",(P2+L)/2,SE);
label("$12$",(11.6,6),E);
label("$13$",K+(5/12)*(T-K),W,blue);
label("$15$",L+(5.5/12)*(T-L),E,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The data are consistent:
\[
k^2=h^2+\left(\dfrac {b}{2}\right)^2=144+25=169=13^2,\qquad l^2=h^2+\left(\dfrac {a}{2}\right)^2=144+81=225=15^2 .
\]
The volume is
\[
V=\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot(\text{base area})\cdot h=\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot18\cdot10\cdot12=\boxed{720\text{ cm}^3}.
\]
