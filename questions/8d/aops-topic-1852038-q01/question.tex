A right rectangular pyramid (its apex lies directly above the centre of its rectangular base) has base edges $a=18$ cm and $b=10$ cm. The slant height of the lateral faces whose base edge is $a$ is $k=13$ cm, the slant height of the lateral faces whose base edge is $b$ is $l=15$ cm, and the height of the pyramid is $h=12$ cm. Check that these data are consistent, and find the volume of the pyramid.
