Transform coordinates to $u=x+y,v=x-y$. Each of the four original moves corresponds to one of the four equally likely sign pairs $(\Delta u,\Delta v)=(\pm1,\pm1)$. Thus both transformed walks independently take ten fair sign steps. They return to zero exactly when each has five positive steps. The number of original paths returning is $\binom{10}{5}^2$, out of $4^{10}=2^{20}$. Hence $n=252^2$ and $\boxed{\sqrt n=252}$.
