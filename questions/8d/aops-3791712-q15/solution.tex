Each semicircle has radius $\dfrac{1}{\pi}$ and endpoint displacement $\dfrac{2}{\pi}$. With $n$ steps, displacement is at most $\dfrac{2 n}{\pi}$, so $n\ge50\pi$, giving $n\ge158$. Conversely 158 equal displacement vectors can achieve any resultant from zero to $\dfrac{316}{\pi}$, by continuously varying a symmetric arrangement of their directions. In particular they can achieve 100. The least distance is $\boxed{158}$ metres.
