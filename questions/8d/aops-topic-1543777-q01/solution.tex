INTERMITTENT has $12$ letters: T four times, I, N and E twice each, and R and M once each.

\textbf{(a)}
\[
\dfrac{12!}{4!\,2!\,2!\,2!}=\dfrac{479001600}{192}=\boxed{2494800}.
\]

\textbf{(b)} Fix a T first and an N last. The middle $10$ letters are T three times, I and E twice each, and N, R, M once each:
\[
\dfrac{10!}{3!\,2!\,2!}=\dfrac{3628800}{24}=\boxed{151200}.
\]

\textbf{(c)} Glue the four T's into one block TTTT. Then there are $9$ objects: the block, I, I, N, N, E, E, R, M:
\[
\dfrac{9!}{2!\,2!\,2!}=\dfrac{362880}{8}=\boxed{45360}.
\]
