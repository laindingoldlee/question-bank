There are $2\cdot6=12$ equally likely outcomes. The sum is $5$ for coin $1$ and die $4$, or coin $2$ and die $3$: $2$ outcomes. The probability is $\dfrac{2}{12}=\dfrac16\approx\boxed{0.17}$.
