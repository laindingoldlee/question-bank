Each airplane flies straight with probability $\dfrac{3}{4}\cdot\dfrac{1}{6}=\dfrac{1}{8}$. Add the indicator expectations to obtain $80\cdot\dfrac{1}{8}=\boxed{10}$. Independence among different airplanes is not required.
