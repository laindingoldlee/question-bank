Since $x^3y^3=(xy)^3$ and $\left(\sqrt5+2\right)\left(\sqrt5-2\right)=5-4=1$ (difference of squares),
\[
\left(\sqrt5+2\right)^3\left(\sqrt5-2\right)^3=\left[\left(\sqrt5+2\right)\left(\sqrt5-2\right)\right]^3=1^3=\boxed{1}.
\]
