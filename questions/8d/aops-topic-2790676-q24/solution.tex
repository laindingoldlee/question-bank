The successive differences satisfy $ a_{n+1}-a_n=-\dfrac{1}{2}(a_n-a_{n-1})$, starting with difference 1. Therefore $ a_n=3+\sum_{j=0}^{n-2}(-\dfrac{1}{2})^j $, which converges to $ 3+\dfrac{1}{1+\dfrac{1}{2}}=\boxed{\dfrac{11}{3}}$.
