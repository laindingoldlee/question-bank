A car takes $30$ minutes to reach a hospital by road. A supersonic jet needs $2$ minutes of flying time to reach the same hospital, but it must first spend $10$ minutes refuelling and then $5$ minutes finding a landing spot. Which arrives first, and by how many minutes?
