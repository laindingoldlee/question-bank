Because $F(u)=(u+7)(u-5)$, one needs $G(x)=-7$ or 5. The first gives $(x+3)(x+7)=0$, and the second $(x+1)(x+9)=0$. Thus the distinct roots are $\boxed{-9,-7,-3,-1}$.
