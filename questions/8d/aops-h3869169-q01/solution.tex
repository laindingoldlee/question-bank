For each prime factor, distribute its exponent $e$ among $a,b,c$. The gcd is one exactly when at least one of the three assigned exponents is zero. The number of such nonnegative triples is
\[
\binom{e+2}{2}-\binom{e-1}{2}=3e
\]
for $e\ge3$; for $e=2$ it is $\binom42=6$, also $3e$. Thus the exponents $6,4,2$ contribute $18,12,6$ choices independently. Therefore $N=18\cdot12\cdot6=1296$, whose remainder modulo $1000$ is $\boxed{296}$.
