On a $25$-question test, each question is answered correctly ($6$ points), answered incorrectly ($0$ points) or left blank ($1.5$ points).
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] How many triples (number correct, number incorrect, number blank) give a positive integer total score?
\item[(b)] How many of the $3^{25}$ answer sheets (each question marked correct, incorrect or blank) give a positive integer total score?
\end{enumerate}
