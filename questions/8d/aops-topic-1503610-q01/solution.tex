Since $x^2=3x-1$, we can reduce the powers of $x$:
\[
x^3=x\cdot x^2=3x^2-x=3(3x-1)-x=8x-3 .
\]
Hence
\[
2x^3-3x^2-11x+8=2(8x-3)-3(3x-1)-11x+8=16x-6-9x+3-11x+8=5-4x .
\]
The value is not the same for both roots. The roots are $x=\dfrac{3\pm\sqrt5}{2}$, so
\[
5-4x=5-2\left(3\pm\sqrt5\right)=-1\mp2\sqrt5 .
\]
The expression equals $\boxed{-1-2\sqrt5}$ when $x=\dfrac{3+\sqrt5}{2}$, and $\boxed{-1+2\sqrt5}$ when $x=\dfrac{3-\sqrt5}{2}$.
