The square lies in one of the two congruent right triangles separated by the diagonal. Their hypotenuse is $c=\sqrt{20^2+24^2}=4\sqrt{61}$, and the altitude to it is $h=\dfrac{20\cdot24}{c}$. At distance $s$ from that hypotenuse, the available parallel width is $c(1-\dfrac{s}{h})$ by similarity. A square of side $s$ fits only if $s\le c(1-\dfrac{s}{h})$, and equality is attained when its opposite side's endpoints reach the legs. Thus
\[s=\dfrac{ch}{c+h}=\dfrac{120\sqrt{61}}{91},\qquad \boxed{s^2=\dfrac{878400}{8281}}.\]
