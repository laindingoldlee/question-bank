Since $ 2^6\equiv-1\pmod{13}$ and $ 2023\equiv7\pmod{12}$, the first power is congruent to $-2 $. Since $ 3^3\equiv1 $ and $ 2023\equiv1\pmod3 $, the second is congruent to $ 3 $. Their sum is $\boxed1 $ modulo thirteen.
