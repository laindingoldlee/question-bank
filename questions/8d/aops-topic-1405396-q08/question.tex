The display on a calculator is broken so that, when the calculator shows a number in decimal form, the tenths and hundredths digits of the number are never visible. Shawna enters a positive integer $N$ in the calculator and presses a button that gives the reciprocal of the integer in decimal form. The nine digits following the missing digits in the display are all threes. What is the sum of all of the possible values of $N$?
