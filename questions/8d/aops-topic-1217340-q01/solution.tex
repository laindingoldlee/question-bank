Let the legs be positive integers $a\le b$ with $a^2+b^2=65$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((0,0)--(7,0)--(0,4)--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); draw((0,0.4)--(0.4,0.4)--(0.4,0));
label("$a$",(3.5,0),S); label("$b$",(0,2),W); label("$\sqrt{65}$",(3.5,2),NE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Checking $a=1,\dots,5$ (since $2a^2\le65$): $65-1=64=8^2$, $65-4=61$, $65-9=56$, $65-16=49=7^2$, $65-25=40$. So the legs are $1,8$ or $4,7$, and the sum of the legs is $\boxed{9\text{ or }11}$.

(Without the integer condition the sum is not determined: it can be any value in $\left(\sqrt{65},\sqrt{130}\,\right]$.)
