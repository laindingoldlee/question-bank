Triangle $BCD$ has angles $30^\circ$ at $B$ and $25^\circ$ at $D$, so $\angle BCD=125^\circ$. At $A$, the given adjacent angles sum to $\angle BAD=50^\circ+60^\circ=110^\circ$. Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral must sum to $180^\circ$, but here they sum to $235^\circ$. Thus $\boxed{\angle BCD=125^\circ}$ and the quadrilateral is not cyclic.
