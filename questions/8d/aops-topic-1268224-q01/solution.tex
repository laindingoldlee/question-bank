The recurrence $n!=n(n-1)!$ at $n=1$ requires $1!=1\cdot 0!$, hence $0!=1$. Combinatorially, there is exactly one ordering of an empty collection: the empty ordering. Thus both viewpoints give $\boxed{0!=1}$.
