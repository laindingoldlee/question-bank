The stripe loses $\dfrac{1}{2}$ of its length in $90$ days, so it loses $\dfrac{1}{180}$ of its length per day. Two weeks and a day is $15$ days, in which it loses
\[
15\cdot\dfrac{1}{180}=\dfrac{1}{12}
\]
of its length. The fraction that remains is
\[
1-\dfrac{1}{12}=\boxed{\dfrac{11}{12}}.
\]
