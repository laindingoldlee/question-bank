The Orz win in exactly $6$ games when they win game $6$ and exactly $3$ of the first $5$ games:
\[P(\text{Orz in }6)=\dbinom53\left(\dfrac34\right)^3\left(\dfrac14\right)^2\cdot\dfrac34=10\cdot\dfrac{3^4}{4^6}.\]
In the same way,
\[P(\text{Ivy in }6)=\dbinom53\left(\dfrac14\right)^3\left(\dfrac34\right)^2\cdot\dfrac14=10\cdot\dfrac{3^2}{4^6}.\]
Given that the series lasted $6$ games,
\[P(\text{Orz won}\mid 6\text{ games})=\dfrac{10\cdot3^4}{10\cdot3^4+10\cdot3^2}=\dfrac{81}{90}=\boxed{\dfrac{9}{10}}.\]
