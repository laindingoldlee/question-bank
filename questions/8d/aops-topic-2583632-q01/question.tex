Two teams, the Orz and the Ivy, play a World Series: a sequence of games that ends as soon as one team has won $4$ games. The Orz win each game with probability $\dfrac34$, independently of the other games. You learn only that the series lasted exactly $6$ games. What is the probability that the Orz won the series?
