Let $\triangle ABC$ be an isosceles triangle  with

\[
AB = BC = \dfrac{25}{3}, \qquad AC = 10.
\]where the vertices $A,B,C$ are in clockwise order.

Let $D$ be the midpoint of $AC$, and let $O$ be the circumcenter of $\triangle ABC$. Let $\Gamma$ be the circle centered at $O$ passing through $D$.



Let $P$ be a point on $\Gamma$ such that $BP$ is tangent to $\Gamma$. Let $P'$ be the image of $P$ under the rotation about $B$ by $\angle CBA$ in the clockwise direction.



If

\[
\dfrac{[ABC]}{[APP']}=\dfrac{m}{n},
\]where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers, find $m+n$.
