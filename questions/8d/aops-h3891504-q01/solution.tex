Take $A=(0,0),B=(b,0),C=(0,c)$ with $b,c>0$. Since $M=(\dfrac{b}{2},\dfrac{c}{2})$, the angle $AM$ makes with $AB$ is $\theta=\arctan(\dfrac{c}{b})$. The hypotenuse has slope $-\dfrac{c}{b}$, so its perpendicular $AH$ has slope $\dfrac{b}{c}$ and makes angle $\phi=\arctan(\dfrac{b}{c})=90^\circ-\theta$ with $AB$. Their average is $45^\circ$, exactly the direction of the right-angle bisector. Thus it bisects the angle between the two rays. If $b=c$, both angles are $45^\circ$, so the altitude, median, and angle bisector coincide.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(4,0),C=(0,3),M=(2,1.5),H=(1.44,1.92);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--M,blue);draw(A--H,red);draw(A--(12/7,12/7),dashed);label("$A$",A,(-1,-1));label("$B$",B,(1,-1));label("$C$",C,(-1,1));label("$M$",M,(1,1));label("$H$",H,(-1,1));label("$45^\circ$",(.8,.2));
\end{asy}
