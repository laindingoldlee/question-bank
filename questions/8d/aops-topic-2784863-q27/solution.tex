Let $ t=2^x+2^{-x}>0 $. Then $ t^2=7+2=9 $, so $ t=3 $. Cubing gives $ 8^x+8^{-x}=t^3-3t=27-9=18 $, already positive. The answer is $\boxed{18}$.
