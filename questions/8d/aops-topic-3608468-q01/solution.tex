The partial sums alternate $1,0,1,0,\ldots$ and have no limit. Thus the series does not converge in the ordinary sense. The equation $x=1-x$ assumes a sum exists and allows a manipulation that is unjustified here. The averages of the first $N$ partial sums tend to $\dfrac{1}{2}$, which is a different averaging convention, not ordinary convergence.
