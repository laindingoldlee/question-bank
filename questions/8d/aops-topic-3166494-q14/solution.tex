The angle-bisector theorem gives $BD:DC=3:4$, so $DC=\dfrac{12}{7}$. Power of point $C$ to the circle gives $CA\cdot CE=CB\cdot CD$, or $4CE=3\cdot\dfrac{12}{7}$. Hence $CE=\dfrac{9}{7}$ and $AE=4-\dfrac{9}{7}=\boxed{\dfrac{19}{7}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),C=(4,0),Bp=(2,sqrt(5)),D=(4*Bp+3*C)/7,Ep=(19/7,0);draw(A--Bp--C--cycle);draw(A--D,blue);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",Bp,N);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,NE);label("$E$",Ep,S);
\end{asy}
