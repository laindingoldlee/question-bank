The allowed digits $0,1,3,4,6,7,8,9$ correspond in order to the base-eight digits $0,1,2,3,4,5,6,7$. This correspondence preserves order, including the lack of leading zeroes. The label $4970$ corresponds to $(3750)_8=3(512)+7(64)+5(8)=2024$. Hence its position is $\boxed{2024}$, and the difference is $\boxed{4970-2024=2946}$.
