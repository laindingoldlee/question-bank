By AM-GM, $a^2+b^2\ge2ab$, $b^2+c^2\ge2bc$ and $c^2+a^2\ge2ca$. Adding gives $2(a^2+b^2+c^2)\ge2(ab+bc+ca)$. Add $a^2+b^2+c^2$ to both sides:
\[3(a^2+b^2+c^2)\ge a^2+b^2+c^2+2(ab+bc+ca)=(a+b+c)^2.\]
$\blacksquare$ Equality holds iff $a=b=c$.
