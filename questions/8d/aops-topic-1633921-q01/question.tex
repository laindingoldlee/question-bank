In non-right triangle $ABC$, $AB=18$ and $AC=x$. Point $D$ lies on segment $BC$ with $BD=12$ and $DC=15$. Let $AD=y$. Find $\dfrac{x}{y}$.

$\textbf{(A)}\ \dfrac{7}{6}\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ \dfrac{6}{5}\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ \dfrac{5}{4}\qquad\textbf{(D)}\ \dfrac{4}{3}\qquad\textbf{(E)}\ \dfrac{3}{2}$
