A king has $12$ knights seated in order $1,2,\dots,12$ around his round table, and a dragon to fight. Going around the table, he skips knight $1$ and sends knight $2$, who is taken by the dragon. He then keeps going around the table among the knights who remain, alternately skipping one knight and sending the next, and every knight he sends is taken. Which knight is the last one he sends? Which knight is sent last if there are $32$ knights? If there are $1030$ knights?
