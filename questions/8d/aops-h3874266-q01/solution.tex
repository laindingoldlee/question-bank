Put $S=x+y+z$ and $Q=x^2+y^2+z^2$. Adding the equations gives $Q-(xy+yz+zx)=61$. Subtracting pairs gives
\[
S(x-y)=-100,\quad S(y-z)=-80,\quad S(x-z)=-180.
\]
In particular $S\ne0$. The identity
\[
(x-y)^2+(y-z)^2+(x-z)^2=2\bigl(Q-xy-yz-zx\bigr)=122
\]
therefore gives $\dfrac{48800}{S^2}=122$, so $S^2=400$. Finally,
\[
3Q=S^2+2\bigl(Q-xy-yz-zx\bigr)=400+122,
\]
and $\boxed{Q=174}$. The triples $(2,7,11)$ and $(-2,-7,-11)$ verify attainability.
