Let $r$ be the inradius and $R$ the circumradius. Splitting into eight congruent center triangles and bisecting one gives
\[
r=2\cot22.5^\circ=2(1+\sqrt2).
\]
Thus $\boxed{r^2=12+8\sqrt2}$. The right triangle with legs $r,2$ and hypotenuse $R$ gives $\boxed{R^2=16+8\sqrt2}$.
The octagon's area is half its perimeter times its inradius:
\[
\boxed{K=\dfrac{1}{2}(32)\,2(1+\sqrt2)=32(1+\sqrt2)}.
\]
Finally the two concentric circles bound an annulus of area
\[
\boxed{\pi(R^2-r^2)=4\pi}.
\]
