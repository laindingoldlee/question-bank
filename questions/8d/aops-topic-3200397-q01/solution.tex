Choose the hundreds digit in five ways, then the tens digit from the four unused digits, and then the units digit from the remaining three. Every valid number has exactly this sequence of choices, and every sequence constructs a valid number. Thus there are $5\cdot4\cdot3=\boxed{60}$.
