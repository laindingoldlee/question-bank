Ted flips a fair coin that shows either ``multiple'' or ``divisor''. He also rolls a fair ten-sided die whose faces are $2012,2013,\dots,2021$; let $d$ be the number rolled. If the coin shows ``multiple'', he picks uniformly at random one of the positive multiples of $d$ that are less than $10d$. If it shows ``divisor'', he picks uniformly at random one of the positive divisors of $d$. What is the probability that Ted's number is $2020$ or $2$?
