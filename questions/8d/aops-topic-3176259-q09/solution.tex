The source is $24$ cm above the lower opening and $72$ cm above the plane. The limiting rays through the opening project a disk with radius $7\cdot\dfrac{72}{24}=21$ cm by similarity. All rays to this disk pass through the opening; outside rays strike the opaque side. The area is $\boxed{441\pi}$ square centimeters.
