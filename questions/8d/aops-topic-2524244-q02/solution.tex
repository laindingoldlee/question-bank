The number of trailing zeros is the exponent of $5$ in $200!$, since there are more factors of $2$ than of $5$. By Legendre's formula this is
\[\left\lfloor\dfrac{200}{5}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{200}{25}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{200}{125}\right\rfloor=40+8+1=\boxed{49}.\]
