The infinite nested radical
\[
E=2+\sqrt{2+\sqrt{2+\sqrt{2-\sqrt{2-\sqrt{2+\sqrt{2+\sqrt{2+\sqrt{2-\sqrt{2-\cdots}}}}}}}}}
\]
has the sign pattern $+,+,+,-,-$ repeating, starting with the sign between the first $2$ and the first radical. It is known that the radical converges. If $E=a\cos^b\left(\dfrac{\pi} {c}\right)$ with positive integers $a,b,c$ and $a$ as small as possible, find $a^2bc$.
