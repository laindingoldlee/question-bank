Lightning gains on Chick at $91-84=7$ laps per hour, so the third pass is at $t=\dfrac{3}{7}$ hour. Lightning and Mater approach at $91+7=98$ laps per hour, so they meet every $\dfrac{1}{98}$ hour; by $t=\dfrac{3}{7}$ that is $98\cdot\dfrac{3}{7}=42$ meetings, the last exactly at $t=\dfrac{3}{7}$. Answer: $\boxed{42}$.
