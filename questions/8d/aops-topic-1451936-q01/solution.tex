Since $129=2^7+1$,
\[
129^2=(2^7+1)^2=2^{14}+2\cdot2^7+1=2^{14}+2^8+2^0 .
\]
So in binary $129^2=100000100000001_2$, a $15$-digit number with ones in the places $14$, $8$, $0$. Thus $B=3$, $A=15-3=12$, and $A-B=\boxed{9}$.
