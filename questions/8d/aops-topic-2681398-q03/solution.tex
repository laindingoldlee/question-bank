\textbf{False.} For $f(x)=\dfrac{x}{x-1}$, the numerator vanishes at $x=0$, yet $f(0)=0$ is defined; and $x=1$, a zero of the denominator, is not in the domain. The correct statement: the domain of a rational function $\dfrac{p(x)}{q(x)}$ is the set of all real numbers except the zeros of the \emph{denominator} $q$. Answer: $\boxed{\text{False}}$.
