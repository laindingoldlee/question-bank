Triangle $ABC$ has side lengths 5, 6, and 7, in some order. Let $D$ be a point in the same plane of $\triangle ABC$ such that $BCD$ is a right triangle. The smallest possible length of $AD$ can be written in the form $\dfrac{\sqrt m-n}{p}$, where $m$, $n$, and $p$ are positive integers, and $m$ is not divisible by any square greater than 1. Find $m+n+p$.
