Squaring, $2+\sqrt{2+x}=16$, so $\sqrt{2+x}=14$, and squaring again, $2+x=196$. Thus $x=194$; checking, $\sqrt{2+\sqrt{196}}=\sqrt{16}=4$. So
\[\boxed{x=194}.\]
