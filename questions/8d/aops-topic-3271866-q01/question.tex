In a hypothetical accounting model, revenue is $R=150000$. Bonus $B$ is $10\%$ of revenue after subtracting both taxes. State charge $S$ is $6\%$ of $R-B$, and federal charge $F$ is $30\%$ of $R-B-S$. Solve for $B,S,F$, giving amounts to the nearest cent.
