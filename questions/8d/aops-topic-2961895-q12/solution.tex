All digits are $0$ or $1$ and $A=1$. Evenness gives $F=0$. The base-ten number is divisible by $3$ iff its number of $1$s is a multiple of $3$; with $F=0$ that number is $3$. The base-two number is divisible by $3$ iff (bits in even positions) $-$ (bits in odd positions) $\equiv0\pmod3$, since $2\equiv-1$. The bits $A,C,E$ carry weights $2^5,2^3,2^1$ (odd powers) and $B,D,F$ even powers. With three $1$s among $A,B,C,D,E$ and $A=1$: if all three are in odd positions ($A,C,E$) the difference is $-3\equiv0$; two odd and one even give $-1$; one odd and two even give $1$. So the $1$s are exactly $A,C,E$. So the number is $101010$, and $101010_{\text{six}}=6^5+6^3+6=7998$. \textbf{(B)}
