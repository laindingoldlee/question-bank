Let $U_k$ count unrestricted $k$-step walks with nonzero steps from $\{-1,0,1\}^3$ ending at $(1,1,1)$. In one coordinate, the numbers of $k$-step sequences summing to $1$, allowing zero steps, are $1,2,6,16$ for $k=1,2,3,4$. Inclusion-exclusion to remove steps whose entire vector is zero gives
\[
U_2=2^3-2\cdot1^3=6,
\]
\[
U_4=16^3-4\cdot6^3+6\cdot2^3-4\cdot1^3=3276.
\]

For $i=1,2,3$, let $E_i$ mean that steps $i$ and $i+1$ are reversals. Choosing that cancelling step pair in $26$ ways leaves a two-step walk, so $|E_i|=26U_2=156$. An intersection $E_1\cap E_2$ or $E_2\cap E_3$ has the pattern $v,-v,v,w$ or its reverse and is counted by $U_2=6$. The intersection $E_1\cap E_3$ forces total displacement zero, so it and the triple intersection contribute nothing.

The admissible count is therefore $3276-3\cdot156+2\cdot6=2820$. Each nonbacktracking walk has probability $\dfrac{1}{(26\cdot25^3)}$, giving
\[
\dfrac{2820}{26\cdot25^3}=\dfrac{282}{40625}.
\]
The requested numerator is $\boxed{282}$.
