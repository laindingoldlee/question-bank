An infinite three-dimensional graph has vertices at all lattice points, and any two vertices are to be connected by a straight path provided that the Euclidean distance between them is exactly $1$, $\sqrt{2}$, or $\sqrt{3}$. A bug starts at $(0,0,0)$. Each move, it travels uniformly at random to one of the $26$ adjacent vertices connected by a path, except that after the first move it refuses to return along the path it just traversed the move immediately before. The probability that the bug is at $(1,1,1)$ after exactly 4 moves can be written as $\dfrac{m}{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $m$.
