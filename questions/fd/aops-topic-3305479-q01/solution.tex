Let $u$ be half its base and $v$ its altitude. Then $uv=16$, $u^2+v^2=49$, so $u+v=9$ and $|u-v|=\sqrt{17}$. The two bases are $9\pm\sqrt{17}$ and the perimeters are $23\pm\sqrt{17}$. Both yield valid triangles; their product is $\boxed{529-17=512}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath"); size(220);
real a=(9+sqrt(17))/2,b=(9-sqrt(17))/2;pair A=(-a,0),B=(a,0),C=(0,b);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw((0,0)--C,dashed);label("$7$",(A+C)/2,NW);label("$7$",(B+C)/2,NE);label("$2u$",(0,0),S);label("$v$",(0,b/2),E);label("Obtuse apex",(0,-1.2));
\end{asy}
