Condition on six distinct points on the circle and fix the starting point. A noncrossing Hamiltonian path must at each step choose one of the two neighbours of the current endpoint among remaining vertices: a diagonal to any other vertex would strand unvisited points on opposite sides, forcing a later crossing. Conversely either neighbour choice preserves noncrossing. There are $2^{6-2}=16$ possible orders, since the last step is forced. Only the two cyclic boundary orders also close without crossings. Thus the conditional probability is $\dfrac{2}{16}=\dfrac{1}{8}$ and $\boxed{a=8}$.
