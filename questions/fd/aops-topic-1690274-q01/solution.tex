A product of real numbers is zero exactly when at least one factor is zero (zero-product property). So $x+5=0$, $x+1=0$ or $x-8=0$, giving
\[
x=\boxed{-5,\ -1,\ 8}.
\]
Each value does make the product zero, for example $(8+5)(8+1)(8-8)=0$.
