Use the complement. Alice rolls a double with probability $\dfrac{1}{6}$, in which case Bob avoids her value with probability $(\dfrac{5}{6})^2$. Otherwise, with probability $\dfrac{5}{6}$, he must avoid two values, with probability $(\dfrac{4}{6})^2$. The probability of no common value is $\dfrac{1}{6}\dfrac{25}{36}+\dfrac{5}{6}\dfrac{16}{36}=\dfrac{35}{72}$. Thus the desired probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{37}{72}}$.
