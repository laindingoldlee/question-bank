Put $L=x+y+z$, $B=(0,0)$, $C=(L,0)$, and $A=(\dfrac{L}{2},\dfrac{L}{2})$. Then $M=(x,0)$ and $N=(x+y,0)$. The dot product of $\overrightarrow{AM}$ and $\overrightarrow{AN}$ is
\[
\dfrac{x^2+z^2+y(x+z)}{2}=\dfrac{yL}{2}.
\]
Their two-dimensional cross product has absolute value $\dfrac{yL}{2}$ as well. The dot product is positive, so the angle is acute; the ratio of cross product to dot product is its tangent. Thus $\tan\angle MAN=1$ and $\boxed{\angle MAN=45^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
real L=2+sqrt(2);pair B=(0,0),C=(L,0),A=(L/2,L/2),M=(1,0),N=(1+sqrt(2),0);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(M--A--N,blue);label("$A$",A,(0,1));label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$M$",M,(0,-1));label("$N$",N,(0,-1));
\end{asy}
