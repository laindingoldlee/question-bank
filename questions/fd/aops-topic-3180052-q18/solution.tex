Each vertex joins $n-3$ non-neighbouring vertices, counting each diagonal twice, so $D(n)=\dfrac{n(n-3)}{2}$. Using $\sum_{n=1}^{26}n=351$ and $\sum_{n=1}^{26}n^2=6201$, and removing the artificial $n=1,2$ terms of $-1$ each, the sum is $\dfrac{6201-3\cdot351}{2}+2=\boxed{2576}$.

\end{asy}
