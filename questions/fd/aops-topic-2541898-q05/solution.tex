Triangle $ABD$ is half the square, with area $\dfrac12\cdot16\cdot16=128$. So triangle $AEB$ has area $\dfrac38\cdot128=48$. It has a right angle at $A$, so its area is $\dfrac12\cdot AB\cdot AE=8\,AE$, giving $AE=6$. Hence $ED=16-6=\boxed{10}$ inches.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(16,0),C=(16,16),D=(0,16),Ep=(0,6);
fill(A--B--Ep--cycle,lightgray);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.8));draw(B--D,dashed);draw(B--Ep);
dot(Ep);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$E$",Ep,W);
label("$6$",(A+Ep)/2,W);label("$10$",(Ep+D)/2,W);label("$16$",(A+B)/2,S);
\end{asy}
