Distinct points $A,B,C,D$ lie in a plane, and no three of them are collinear. Points $B$ and $D$ lie on opposite sides of line $AC$, and points $B$ and $C$ lie on opposite sides of line $AD$. Given that $\angle ABC=\angle ACD$ and $\angle ABD=\angle ADC$, prove that line $AB$ passes through the midpoint of segment $CD$.
