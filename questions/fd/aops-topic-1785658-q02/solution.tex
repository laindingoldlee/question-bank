The solutions of $|x-c|=d$ (with $d>0$) are $c-d$ and $c+d$, the two points at distance $d$ from $c$. So $c$ is their midpoint, $c=\dfrac{-5+33}{2}=14$, and $d=33-14=19$. Hence $c+d=\boxed{33}$ (indeed $c+d$ is the larger solution).
