Robin stands at the lower left corner and Jack at the upper right corner of a $5\times5$ grid of unit squares. They start at the same time, and every second each of them moves one unit along a grid line: Robin moves up or right, and Jack moves down or left, each choosing between his two directions with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, independently. What is the probability that the two boys meet at a point of the grid?
