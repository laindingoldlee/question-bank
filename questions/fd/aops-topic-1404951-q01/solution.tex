The divisor is $x-(b-1)$, so by the remainder theorem the remainder is the value of the polynomial at $x=b-1$:
\[
(b-1)^3+b(b-1)+b=b^3-3b^2+3b-1+b^2-b+b=\boxed{b^3-2b^2+3b-1}.
\]
