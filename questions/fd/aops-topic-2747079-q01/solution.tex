Let the mother, baby and dog weigh $M$, $B$ and $D$ pounds. Then $M+B+D=170$ and $M=100+B+D$, so $2(B+D)=70$ and $B+D=35$. ``$60\%$ less than the baby'' means $D=0.4B$, so $1.4B=35$ and $B=\boxed{25}$ pounds (the dog weighs $10$ and the mother $135$).
