Let $f(0)=1$, and for $n\ge0$ let
\[
f(n+1)=\begin{cases}2f(n),&f(n)<100,\\ f(n)-50,&f(n)\ge100.\end{cases}
\]
What is the value of $f(2018)$?

(a) $64$\quad (b) $96$\quad (c) $104$\quad (d) $164$\quad (e) $196$
