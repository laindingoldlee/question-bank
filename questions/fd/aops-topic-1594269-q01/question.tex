Pizzas at Mario's come in $3$ different sizes, and you may choose any set of the $9$ available toppings (each at most once, including no toppings). How many different pizzas can be ordered? A student argues: "there are $3$ sizes and $10$ possible numbers of toppings ($0$ to $9$), so $3\cdot10=30$ pizzas." Explain the error.
