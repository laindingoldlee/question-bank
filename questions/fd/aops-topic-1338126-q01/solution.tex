Two cartons give $48$ one-foot sections, so the perimeter is $48$ feet and the sides $\ell$ and $w$ (whole numbers of feet) satisfy $\ell+w=24$. The area is
\[
\ell w=\ell(24-\ell)=144-(\ell-12)^2\le144,
\]
with equality for $\ell=w=12$. So the largest garden is a $12\times12$ square with area $\boxed{144}$ square feet. The value $48$ is the perimeter (the total length of fencing), not the area, so the student's answer is wrong.
