The three-digit squares are $10^2,\dots,31^2$. A square never ends in $2,3,7,8$, and $\overline{cab}$ ends in $b$; also $c\ne0$ is the last digit of $\overline{abc}$ and the first digit of $\overline{cab}$. List the squares $\overline{abc}$ with $c\ne0$ and form $\overline{cab}$:
\[
\begin{array}{llllll}
121\to112, & 144\to414, & 169\to916, & 196\to619, & 225\to522, & 256\to625,\\
289\to928, & 324\to432, & 361\to136, & 441\to144, & 484\to448, & 529\to952,\\
576\to657, & 625\to562, & 676\to667, & 729\to972, & 784\to478, & 841\to184,\\
961\to196. & & & & &
\end{array}
\]
Among the right-hand numbers the squares are $625=25^2$, $144=12^2$ and $196=14^2$. So the answers are
\[
\boxed{256,\ 441,\ 961}
\]
($256=16^2\to625=25^2$, $441=21^2\to144=12^2$, $961=31^2\to196=14^2$).
