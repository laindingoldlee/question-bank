The inequality is equivalent to $5\sqrt2+3<5\sqrt3+\sqrt{11}$. Since $2<3$, we have $\sqrt2<\sqrt3$ and so $5\sqrt2<5\sqrt3$. Since $9<11$, we have $3=\sqrt9<\sqrt{11}$. Adding these two inequalities gives $5\sqrt2+3<5\sqrt3+\sqrt{11}$, which proves the claim.
