Five balls are placed into three boxes. For each of the four cases below, find the number of ways (i) if boxes may be empty and (ii) if no box may be empty. Give the general formula for $k$ balls and $n$ boxes in each case.
\begin{enumerate}
\item distinguishable balls, distinguishable boxes;
\item distinguishable balls, indistinguishable boxes;
\item indistinguishable balls, distinguishable boxes;
\item indistinguishable balls, indistinguishable boxes.
\end{enumerate}
