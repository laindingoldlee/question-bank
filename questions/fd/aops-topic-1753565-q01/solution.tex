Modulo $3$, $7\equiv1$ and $2\equiv-1$, so $a(2a^2+7)\equiv a(1-a^2)=-(a-1)a(a+1)\pmod3$. The product of three consecutive integers $a-1,a,a+1$ is divisible by $3$. Hence $3\mid a(2a^2+7)$. $\blacksquare$
