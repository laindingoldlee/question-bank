Each nondecreasing string corresponds uniquely to a multiset of five digits, counted by $\binom{14}{5}=2002$. Among $10^5$ equally likely strings, the probability is $\dfrac{2002}{100000}=\dfrac{1001}{50000}$. Thus $a+b=51001$ and the remainder is $\boxed1$.
