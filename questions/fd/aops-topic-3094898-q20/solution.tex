Put $u=\log_ab$ and $v=\log_ac$, both nonzero. Change of base gives $u^2=5+20v$ and $uv=6+3u$. Thus $v=3+\dfrac{6}{u}$ and $u^3-65u-120=0$. This cubic changes sign in each of $(-7,-6),(-2,-1),(8,9)$, so it has exactly three real roots. None is 0 or $-2$; hence each gives a nonzero $v$ and is realizable by choosing $a=2,b=2^u,c=2^v$. By Vieta, the pairwise root-product sum is $-65$ and their product is 120. The reciprocal sum is therefore $-\dfrac{65}{120}=-\dfrac{13}{24}$, giving $\boxed{m+n=37}$.
