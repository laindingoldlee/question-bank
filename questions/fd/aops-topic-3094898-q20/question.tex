Positive reals $a,b,c$, none equal to 1, satisfy $\log_ab=5\log_b(ac^4)=3\log_c(a^2b)$. Find the sum of reciprocals of all possible values of $\log_ab$, and express it as $-\dfrac{m}{n}$ in lowest terms. Find $m+n$.
