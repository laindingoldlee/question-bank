Let $BC=s$ and $\theta=\angle CBD=\angle CDE$. Triangle $CBD$ has angles $\theta,60^\circ,120^\circ-\theta$, and triangle $CDE$ has angles $120^\circ,\theta,60^\circ-\theta$. The sine rule and $BD=3DE$ give $\sin(60^\circ-\theta)=3\sin\theta$, so $\tan\theta=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{7}$. Therefore $\dfrac{CD}{BC}=\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\sin(120^\circ-\theta)}=\dfrac{1}{4}$ and $\dfrac{CE}{CD}=\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\sin(60^\circ-\theta)}=\dfrac{1}{3}$. Since $\sin120^\circ=\sin60^\circ$, area comparison yields $[CDE]=\dfrac{1}{48}[ABC]=\dfrac{5}{48}$. Thus $\boxed{53}$.
