In an equilateral triangle $\triangle ABC$ with area $5$, let $D$ be a point on segment $AC$ and $E$ be the point on ray $\overrightarrow{BC}$ not on the segment $BC$ such that $\angle CBD=\angle CDE$ and $BD=3ED$. The area of $\triangle CDE$ can be expressed as $\dfrac{a}{b}$ where $a$ and $b$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $a+b$.
