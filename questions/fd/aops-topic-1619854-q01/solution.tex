All $\dbinom{22}{3}$ choices of three students are equally likely. Those containing George are obtained by choosing his $2$ companions from the other $21$: $\dbinom{21}{2}$ ways. So
\[
P=\dfrac{\dbinom{21}{2}}{\dbinom{22}{3}}=\dfrac{210}{1540}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{22}}.
\]
(More simply, by symmetry every student is equally likely to be chosen, and $3$ of the $22$ places are A's.)
