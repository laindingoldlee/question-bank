Suppose $p$ cans are purchased and $e$ exchanges occur. Then $p+e$ drinks are available. Each exchange reduces the total number of can objects by $2$, so after drinking and exchanging there are $p-2e\ge1$ empties. Thus $e\le\left\lfloor\dfrac{p-1}{2}\right\rfloor$. For $p=19$ this bound is $9$, giving $28$ drinks, and it is attainable by repeated exchanges. For $p\le18$, there are at most $18+8=26$ drinks. Hence the minimum is $\boxed{19}$.
