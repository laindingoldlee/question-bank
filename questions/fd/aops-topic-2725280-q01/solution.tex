Since $AB$ and $EF$ are diameters, they pass through $O$, and $\angle AOE$ and $\angle BOF$ are vertical angles, hence equal. Equal central angles subtend equal arcs, so arcs $AE$ and $BF$ are congruent. (The condition $AB\perp CD$ is not needed.) $\blacksquare$

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0),A=dir(180),B=dir(0),C=dir(90),D=dir(270),Ep=dir(140),F=dir(320);
draw(unitcircle,linewidth(0.8));draw(A--B);draw(C--D);draw(Ep--F,dashed);
draw(arc(O,1,140,180),linewidth(2));draw(arc(O,1,320,360),linewidth(2));
dot(O);label("$O$",O,SE,fontsize(8pt));
label("$A$",A,W);label("$B$",B,E);label("$C$",C,N);label("$D$",D,S);label("$E$",Ep,NW);label("$F$",F,SE);
\end{asy}
