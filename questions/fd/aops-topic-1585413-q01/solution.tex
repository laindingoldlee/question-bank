Each remainder is one less than the divisor, so $N+1$ is divisible by $6$, $7$ and $8$, i.e.\ by $\operatorname{lcm}(6,7,8)=168$. The smallest positive possibility is $N+1=168$, so
\[
N=\boxed{167}.
\]
(Check: $167=6\cdot27+5=7\cdot23+6=8\cdot20+7$.)
