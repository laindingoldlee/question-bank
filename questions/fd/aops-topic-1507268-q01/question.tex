In trapezoid $ABCD$, $AB\parallel CD$, and the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ meet at $O$. Prove that triangles $AOB$ and $COD$ are similar, and deduce that
\[
\dfrac{AO}{OC}=\dfrac{BO}{OD}=\dfrac{AB}{CD}.
\]
