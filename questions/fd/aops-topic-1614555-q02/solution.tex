Let each leg be $x$. By the Pythagorean theorem the hypotenuse is $\sqrt{x^2+x^2}=x\sqrt2$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real x=15*(2-sqrt(2));
pair C=(0,0),A=(0,x),B=(x,0);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw((0,0.9)--(0.9,0.9)--(0.9,0));
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,E);label("$C$",C,SW);
label("$x$",(A+C)/2,W);label("$x$",(B+C)/2,S);label("$x\sqrt2$",(A+B)/2,NE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The perimeter gives $2x+x\sqrt2=30$, so
\[
x=\dfrac{30}{2+\sqrt2}=\dfrac{30\left(2-\sqrt2\right)}{\left(2+\sqrt2\right)\left(2-\sqrt2\right)}=\dfrac{30\left(2-\sqrt2\right)}{2}=15\left(2-\sqrt2\right).
\]
Hence $A=\boxed{15}$.
