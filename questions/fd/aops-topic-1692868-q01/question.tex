A deck consists of $n$ different cards, numbered $1,2,\dots,n$ from top to bottom. A \emph{move} consists of taking out a block of consecutive cards from the deck and putting the block back somewhere else in the deck, without flipping it or changing the order of the cards in it. The goal is to reverse the order of the deck (so that it reads $n,n-1,\dots,1$ from top to bottom). What is the minimum number of moves required if
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $n=5$; \quad (b) $n=9$; \quad (c) $n=13$?
\end{enumerate}
