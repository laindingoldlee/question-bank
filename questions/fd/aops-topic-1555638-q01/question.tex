A post claims: ``If $a$ and $b$ are integers and $x^4+ax^3+2011x^2+bx+1=0$ has a real solution, then $a+b>126$.''
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that the claim is false.
\item[(b)] Prove the correct bound: if $a$ and $b$ are real numbers and the equation has a real solution, then $a^2+b^2\ge8044$.
\end{enumerate}
