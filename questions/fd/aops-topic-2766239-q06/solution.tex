The radius is 8 and the arc spans two of eight equal arcs, one quarter of the circumference. Its length is $\dfrac{1}{4}(16\pi)=4\pi$, giving $\boxed{n=4}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
for(int k=0;k<8;++k){pair P=2*dir(45*k);draw(P--2*dir(45*(k+1)));}draw(circle((0,0),2));draw(arc((0,0),2,0,90),blue+2);label("$A$",(2,0),E);label("$B$",2*dir(45),NE);label("$C$",(0,2),N);
\end{asy}
