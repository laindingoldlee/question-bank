Let $[ABC]=S$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0), C=(6,0), A=(2,4.5);
pair E=A+(B-A)/3, D=A+(C-A)/3, F=B+2*(C-B)/3;
filldraw(C--D--E--F--cycle,lightblue+opacity(0.4),blue);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
dot(A); dot(B); dot(C); dot(D); dot(E); dot(F);
label("$A$",A,N); label("$B$",B,SW); label("$C$",C,SE); label("$D$",D,NE); label("$E$",E,W); label("$F$",F,S);
label("$1$",(A+E)/2,W); label("$2$",(E+B)/2,W);
\end{asy}

Since $DE\parallel BC$, triangle $ADE$ is similar to triangle $ABC$ with ratio $\dfrac{AE}{AB}=\dfrac13$, so $[ADE]=\dfrac19S$.

Since $EF\parallel AC$, triangle $EBF$ is similar to triangle $ABC$ with ratio $\dfrac{EB}{AB}=\dfrac23$, so $[EBF]=\dfrac49S$.

The quadrilateral $CDEF$ (a parallelogram) is what remains:
\[[CDEF]=S-\dfrac19S-\dfrac49S=\dfrac49S.\]
The ratio is $\boxed{\textbf{(A) }\dfrac49}$.
