A keystone arch is composed of congruent isosceles trapezoids fitted together along their non-parallel sides, forming a half-ring; the bottom sides of the two end trapezoids are horizontal. In an arch made with \(9\) trapezoids, let \(x\) be the measure in degrees of the larger interior angle of each trapezoid. What is \(x\)?
