The centre $O=(h,k)$ of the circumcircle is equidistant from the three vertices.
\begin{itemize}
\item Equal distances to $(0,0)$ and $(6,6)$: $h^2+k^2=(h-6)^2+(k-6)^2$, which simplifies to $12h+12k=72$, i.e. $h+k=6$.
\item Equal distances to $(0,0)$ and $(9,-3)$: $h^2+k^2=(h-9)^2+(k+3)^2$, which simplifies to $18h-6k=90$, i.e. $3h-k=15$.
\end{itemize}
(These are the perpendicular bisectors of two sides, dashed below; the first one passes through $(9,-3)$, because that vertex is at distance $\sqrt{90}$ from both $(0,0)$ and $(6,6)$.) Adding, $4h=21$, so $h=\dfrac{21}{4}$ and $k=\dfrac34$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0), B=(6,6), C=(9,-3), O=(21/4,3/4);
real R=15*sqrt(2)/4;
draw((-2,0)--(11,0),gray,Arrow(5)); draw((0,-5)--(0,9),gray,Arrow(5));
draw(circle(O,R),blue);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
pair M1=(A+B)/2, M2=(A+C)/2;
draw(C--M1+0.35*(M1-C),dashed+red);
draw(M2-0.4*(1,3)--M2+1.3*(1,3),dashed+red);
draw(O--B,blue); dot(O,blue);
label("$(0,0)$",A,NW); label("$(6,6)$",B,N); label("$(9,-3)$",C,SE);
label("$O$",O,E,blue); label("$R$",(O+B)/2,SE,blue);
\end{asy}

The radius is
\[R=\sqrt{\left(\dfrac{21}{4}\right)^2+\left(\dfrac34\right)^2}=\sqrt{\dfrac{450}{16}}=\boxed{\dfrac{15\sqrt2}{4}}\approx5.30.\]
(Check with $R=\dfrac{abc}{4K}$: the sides are $6\sqrt2$, $\sqrt{90}$, $\sqrt{90}$, the area is $K=\dfrac12\left|6\cdot(-3)-6\cdot9\right|=36$, and $\dfrac{6\sqrt2\cdot90}{144}=\dfrac{15\sqrt2}{4}$.)
