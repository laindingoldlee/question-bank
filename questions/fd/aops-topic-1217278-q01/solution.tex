Without the limit, the number of solutions of $r+g+b=14$ in non-negative integers is $\dbinom{16}{2}=120$. A cup with at least $11$ marbles leaves $3$ marbles for all three cups (after removing $11$), which can be done in $\dbinom{5}{2}=10$ ways; there are $3$ choices of the over-full cup, and two cups cannot both have $11$. So the answer is
\[120-3\cdot10=\boxed{90}.\]
