Let $S=\sum_{n\ge1}\dfrac{1}{n^3}$ (a convergent series). The even terms are $\sum_{m\ge1}\dfrac{1}{(2m)^3}=\dfrac{1}{8}S$, so the odd terms sum to $S-\dfrac{1}{8}S=\dfrac{7}{8}S$. The ratio is $\boxed{\dfrac{7}{8}}$.
