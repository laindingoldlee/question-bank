Express as a common fraction the sum of the reciprocals of the cubes of all odd positive integers divided by the sum of the reciprocals of the cubes of all positive integers, that is,
\[\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{1^3}+\dfrac{1}{3^3}+\dfrac{1}{5^3}+\cdots}{\dfrac{1}{1^3}+\dfrac{1}{2^3}+\dfrac{1}{3^3}+\cdots}.\]
