$100$ ants are placed at different points of a stick, each facing one of the two ends. Every ant walks at the same constant speed, and an ant needs exactly $2$ minutes to walk the whole length of the stick. When two ants meet, both immediately turn around and continue at the same speed. An ant that reaches an end of the stick falls off. Prove that, whatever the starting positions and directions, all ants have fallen off after at most $2$ minutes, and show that $2$ minutes can be needed.
