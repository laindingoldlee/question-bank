Write $p=a_2$, the least prime divisor of $n$. The third divisor is either $p^2$ or a different prime $q$. The latter is impossible, since $p\mid(q^3-p^3)$ would imply $p\mid q$. Thus $n=p^6-p^3=p^3(p^3-1)$. If $p$ were odd then $2$ would be a smaller prime divisor of $n$. Hence $p=2$ and $\boxed{n=56}$. Its first three divisors are indeed $1,2,4$.
