Set $ B=(0,0),A=(a,0),C=(0,c)$ with $ a,c>0 $, so $ P=(17,0)$ and $ M=(\dfrac{a}{2},\dfrac{c}{2})$. From $ CP=20 $, $ c^2=400-289=111 $. The circle through $ B,P,C $ has equation $ x^2+y^2-17x-cy=0 $. Substituting $ M $ gives $ a^2-c^2=34a $, or $ a^2-34a-111=(a-37)(a+3)=0 $. Thus $ a=37 $, consistent with $ P $ on the side, and $\boxed{AC=\sqrt{37^2+111}=2\sqrt{370}}$.

\end{asy}
