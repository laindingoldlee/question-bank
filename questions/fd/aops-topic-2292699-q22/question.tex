A bag contains red and yellow balls. There are ten of each colour, and the balls of each colour are numbered $1$ through $10$. If Arthur draws two balls at random, one after the other without replacement, what is the probability that he draws the yellow ball numbered $3$ followed by a red ball? Express your answer as a common fraction.
