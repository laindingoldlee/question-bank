The equation is $25x^2+40x+45=0$, so $a+b=-\dfrac{40}{25}=-\dfrac85$ and $ab=\dfrac{45}{25}=\dfrac95$. Hence
\[a^2+b^2=(a+b)^2-2ab=\dfrac{64}{25}-\dfrac{18}{5}=\dfrac{64-90}{25}=\boxed{-\dfrac{26}{25}}.\]
(A negative value is possible because the roots are not real: they are complex conjugates.)
