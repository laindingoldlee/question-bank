On the $x$-axis $y=0$, so $3x=9$ and $x=3$. The line meets the $x$-axis at $\boxed{(3,0)}$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
draw((-1.5,0)--(5.5,0),Arrow(TeXHead)); draw((0,-3.4)--(0,2.2),Arrow(TeXHead));
label("$x$",(5.5,0),E); label("$y$",(0,2.2),N);
real f(real x){return (3x-9)/4;}
draw(graph(f,-1,5.4),blue+linewidth(1));
dot((3,0),red); dot((0,-9/4));
label("$(3,0)$",(3,0),SE,red); label("$\left(0,-\dfrac{9}{4}\right)$",(0.3,-2.85),E,fontsize(9pt));
label("$3x-4y=9$",(5.4,f(5.4)),SE,blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

(It meets the $y$-axis at $\left(0,-\dfrac{9}{4}\right)$, and its slope is $\dfrac{3}{4}$.)
