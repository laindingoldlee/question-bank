Let $k$ be the semicircle with centre $O$ and radius $r$ on the diameter $AB$. The circle $k_1$ with centre $O_1$ and radius $r_1$ touches the diameter $AB$ at its midpoint $O$ and touches the semicircle $k$. The circles $k_2$ (centre $O_2$, radius $r_2$) and $k_3$ (centre $O_3$, radius $r_3$) lie on opposite sides of $k_1$ and each touches the semicircle $k$, the diameter $AB$ and the circle $k_1$. Prove that $O$, $O_2$, $O_1$, $O_3$ are the vertices of a rhombus.
