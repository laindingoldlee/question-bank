One minute contains $12$ intervals of $5$ seconds. A balloon survives all of them with probability $\left(\dfrac12\right)^{12}=\dfrac1{4096}$. By linearity of expectation, the expected number of survivors is $\dfrac{3072}{4096}=\boxed{\dfrac34}$.
