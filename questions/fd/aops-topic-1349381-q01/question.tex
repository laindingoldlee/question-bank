Two different numbers are selected at random from the set $\{-2,-1,0,3,4,5\}$ and multiplied together. What is the probability that the product is $0$? Explain why the two counting methods below both give the same answer:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] counting unordered pairs of numbers;
\item[(ii)] counting ordered pairs (first number, second number).
\end{enumerate}
