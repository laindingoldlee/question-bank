Each trailing zero comes from a factor $10=2\cdot5$, and $2020!$ has many more factors $2$ than factors $5$, so the number of trailing zeros is the exponent of $5$ in $2020!$. Every multiple of $5$ up to $2020$ contributes one factor $5$, every multiple of $25$ one more, every multiple of $125$ one more, and every multiple of $625$ one more:
\[\left\lfloor\dfrac{2020}{5}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{2020}{25}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{2020}{125}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{2020}{625}\right\rfloor=404+80+16+3=503.\]
($3125>2020$, so there are no further terms.) So $2020!$ ends in $\boxed{503}$ zeros.
