Modulo $13$, the possible cubes are $0,1,5,8,12$, and the possible fourth powers are $0,1,3,9$. These lists follow by raising the residues $0,1,\ldots,12$ to the relevant powers and reducing. If a fourth power is respectively $0,1,3,9$, a required cube would be $7,6,4,11$ modulo $13$. None is in the cube list. Thus the equation is impossible modulo $13$, and hence over the integers.
