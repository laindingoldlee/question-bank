Write the number as $ 10a+b $. Then $ 7(10a+b)=4(10b+a)$ gives $ 66a=33b $, or $ b=2a $. Since $ 1\le a\le9 $ and $ 0\le b\le9 $, we have $ a=1,2,3,4 $. The numbers are 12,24,36,48, giving $\boxed4 $.
