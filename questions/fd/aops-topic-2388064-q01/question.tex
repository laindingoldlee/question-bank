A pile contains some number of points. Eleven players, numbered $1,2,3,\dots$, take turns in the cyclic order $1,2,3,\dots$, starting again with player $1$ after the last player. The turns are numbered $1,2,3,\dots$ in the order they are played, and on turn number $t$ the player whose turn it is takes $t$ points from the pile. As soon as the pile has fewer points than the number of the current turn, the game ends. When the game ends, the winner (the player with the most points) has $525$ points, and $25$ points are left in the pile. How many points were in the pile at the start?
