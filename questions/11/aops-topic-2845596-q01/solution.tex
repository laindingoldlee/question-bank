(a) There are $11$ tiles with A repeated $4$ times and N twice, so $\dfrac{11!}{4!\,2!}=\boxed{831600}$ distinct arrangements.

(b) Treat the tiles as distinguishable: all $11!$ orders are equally likely, and $4!\,2!$ of them spell GRAMBANANAS. So the probability is $\dfrac{4!\,2!}{11!}=\boxed{\dfrac1{831600}}$ (equivalently, one of the $831600$ equally likely distinct words).

(c) The argument mixes counts: $4!\,2!$ counts orders of \emph{distinguishable} tiles, while $\dfrac{11!}{4!\,2!}$ counts \emph{distinct words}. With TOO, the three words TOO, OTO, OOT are each produced by $2$ of the $3!=6$ tile orders, so $P(\text{TOO})=\dfrac26=\dfrac13$, not $\dfrac23$.
