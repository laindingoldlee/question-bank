In the multiplication
\[
\begin{array}{r}
A\,B\,C\,D\\
\times\quad\ \ D\\
\hline
D\,C\,B\,A
\end{array}
\]
$A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ are different digits and $A\ne0$. Find the product.
