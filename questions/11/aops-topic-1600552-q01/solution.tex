In the first sequence, $y$ is $4$ steps after $x$, so its common difference is $d_1=\dfrac{y-x}{4}$, and $a_2-a_1=d_1$.

In the second sequence, $y$ is $3$ steps after $x$, so its common difference is $d_2=\dfrac{y-x}{3}$, and $b_4-b_3=2d_2$ ($b_4$ is two steps after $b_3$).

Hence
\[
\dfrac{b_4-b_3}{a_2-a_1}=\dfrac{2\cdot\dfrac{y-x}{3}}{\dfrac{y-x}{4}}=\boxed{\dfrac{8}{3}},
\]
which is defined because $x\ne y$.
