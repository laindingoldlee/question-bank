All $\dbinom{11}{2}=55$ pairs of balls are equally likely, and $\dbinom{5}{2}=10$ of them are both white. So the probability is $\dfrac{10}{55}=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{11}}$. (Drawing one at a time: $\dfrac{5}{11}\cdot\dfrac{4}{10}=\dfrac{2}{11}$.) The poster's answer $\dfrac{2}{11}$ is correct.
