The $k$-th term is $10^k-1$, so
\[
A=(10+10^2+\cdots+10^{2016})-2016=\underbrace{11\ldots1}_{2016}0-2016 .
\]
Subtracting $2016$ only affects the last five digits: $11110-2016=9094$, so
\[
A=\underbrace{11\ldots1}_{2012}09094 .
\]
Hence $A$ contains $\boxed{2012}$ digits $1$.
