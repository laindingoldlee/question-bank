Corresponding sides are in the ratio of the hypotenuses, $10:18=5:9$, so triangle $XYZ$ has legs $\dfrac{9}{5}\cdot6=10.8$ and $\dfrac{9}{5}\cdot8=14.4$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(8,0),C=(0,6);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw((A+(0.45,0))--(A+(0.45,0.45))--(A+(0,0.45)));
label("$6$",(A+C)/2,W); label("$8$",(A+B)/2,S); label("$10$",(B+C)/2,NE);
pair o=(11,0); pair X=o,Y=o+(14.4,0),Z=o+(0,10.8);
draw(X--Y--Z--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw((X+(0.45,0))--(X+(0.45,0.45))--(X+(0,0.45)));
label("$18$",(Y+Z)/2,NE); label("$10.8$",(X+Z)/2,W); label("$14.4$",(X+Y)/2,S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Areas scale with the square of the length ratio:
\[
\dfrac{[ABC]}{[XYZ]}=\left(\dfrac{10}{18}\right)^2=\boxed{\dfrac{25}{81}} .
\]
Check: $[ABC]=24$ and $[XYZ]=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot10.8\cdot14.4=77.76=24\cdot\dfrac{81}{25}$.
