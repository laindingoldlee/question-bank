For a real number $t$, let $[t]$ denote the greatest integer not exceeding $t$, so that $[t]\le t<[t]+1$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that the inequality $[xy]\ge[x][y]$ can fail for real numbers $x$ and $y$.
\item[(b)] Prove that $[xy]\ge[x][y]$ for all real numbers $x\ge0$ and $y\ge0$.
\end{enumerate}
