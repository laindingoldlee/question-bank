\textbf{(a)} Take $x=y=-\dfrac12$. Then $[xy]=\left[\dfrac14\right]=0$, but $[x][y]=(-1)(-1)=1>0$.

\textbf{(b)} For $x,y\ge0$ we have $0\le[x]\le x$ and $0\le[y]\le y$. Multiplying these inequalities between nonnegative numbers,
\[[x][y]\le xy.\]
So $[x][y]$ is an integer not exceeding $xy$. Since $[xy]$ is the greatest such integer, $[x][y]\le[xy]$. $\blacksquare$
