In the final race, Steve's car developed engine trouble. For $0<t<10.3$, the polynomial
\[V(t)=t^4-19t^3+108t^2-180t\]
gives the speed of the car, in mph, above (if positive) or below (if negative) his average speed of $200$ mph, $t$ seconds after the trouble began.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Use the remainder theorem to find his speed at $t=1$, $t=5$ and $t=8$.
\item[(b)] How many times did his speed equal $200$ mph during this period, and at which times?
\end{enumerate}
