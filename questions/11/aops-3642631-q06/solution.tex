Write the increasing chosen numbers as $a_1<\cdots<a_{20}$. Since $1000\equiv-1\pmod{91}$, the concatenation is congruent to $S=(a_2-a_1)+(a_4-a_3)+\cdots+(a_{20}-a_{19})$. Each of the ten differences is at least one. Also $S\le a_{20}-a_1-9\le99-9=90$, since the nine gaps between pairs are positive. Thus $10\le S\le90$, so S is not a multiple of 91. Neither is the concatenated number.
