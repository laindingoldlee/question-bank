$630000=63\cdot10^4=2^4\cdot3^2\cdot5^4\cdot7$. A divisor $2^a3^b5^c7^d$ is divisible by $20=2^2\cdot5$ iff $a\ge2$ and $c\ge1$. The choices are $a\in\{2,3,4\}$, $b\in\{0,1,2\}$, $c\in\{1,2,3,4\}$, $d\in\{0,1\}$, giving $3\cdot3\cdot4\cdot2=\boxed{72}$.
