Let $A$ be the focus of the parabola $y = \dfrac{x^2}{24} + 6$, and let $B$ be a point on its directrix. The circle with diameter $AB$ intersects the parabola at $P$, where $B$ and $P$ lie on the same side of the $y$-axis. Line $AP$ is extended to hit the $x$-axis at $C$. Suppose the area of triangle $ABC$ is $64\sqrt{3}$.



Then, the area of the circle can be expressed as $\dfrac{m}{n}\pi$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $m + n$.
