For each integer $j=1,\ldots,2018$, ant $j$ enters the left end of a one-inch segment at time $j$ seconds and walks right at speed $\dfrac{1}{j}$ inches per second, leaving upon reaching the right end. Ants do not interact. How many are on the segment at time $2019$?
