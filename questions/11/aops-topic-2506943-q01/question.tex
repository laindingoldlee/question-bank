In triangle $ABC$, let $D$ be the midpoint of $BC$, and let $E$ be a point on segment $AD$ such that $\angle BEC+\angle BAC=180^\circ$. If $BC=\sqrt7$, then $DE\cdot DA=\dfrac mn$ for relatively prime positive integers $m$ and $n$. Find $m+n$.
