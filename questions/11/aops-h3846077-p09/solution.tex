Let $AB=AC=s$. The apex angle is $40^\circ$, so $BC=2s\sin20^\circ$. In triangle $ACP$, the sine rule gives
\[
\sin\angle APC=\dfrac{AC\sin40^\circ}{AP}
=\dfrac{\sin40^\circ}{2\sin20^\circ}=\cos20^\circ.
\]
Therefore $\angle APC$ is $70^\circ$ or $110^\circ$, making $\angle CAP$ equal to $70^\circ$ or $30^\circ$, respectively. Since $P$ is inside the $40^\circ$ apex angle, only $30^\circ$ is possible. Thus $\boxed{\angle PAB=40^\circ-30^\circ=10^\circ}$.

\end{asy}
