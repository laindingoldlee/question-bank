There are $2018$ possible numbers of hairs ($1,2,\dots,2018$). With $2018$ people it is possible that all the numbers are different (one person with each number), so $2018$ people are not enough. With $2019$ people, by the pigeonhole principle two of them must have the same number of hairs. The minimum is $\boxed{2019}$.
