Let the numbers be $x$ and $y$. Then $y=12-x$ and $x(12-x)=24$, i.e.
\[
x^2-12x+24=0 .
\]
By the quadratic formula,
\[
x=\dfrac{12\pm\sqrt{144-96}}{2}=\dfrac{12\pm4\sqrt3}{2}=6\pm2\sqrt3 .
\]
If $x=6+2\sqrt3$ then $y=6-2\sqrt3$, and vice versa. So the numbers are $\boxed{6+2\sqrt3\text{ and }6-2\sqrt3}$.

Check: their sum is $12$ and their product is $36-12=24$. (Equivalently, two numbers with sum $s$ and product $p$ are the roots of $t^2-st+p=0$; this is Vieta's formulas.)
