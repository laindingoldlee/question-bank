In the right isosceles triangle $x\ge0$, $y\ge0$, $x+y\le a$, where $a>0$, inscribe the rectangle with vertices $(0,0),(w,0),(w,h),(0,h)$ and $w+h=a$. In the triangular region with vertices $(0,h),(w,h),(0,a)$, inscribe a circle. Find $w,h$ and the circle radius that maximize the sum of the rectangle and circle areas.
