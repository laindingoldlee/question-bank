The remaining triangle is right isosceles with legs $w$, so its inradius is
\[
r=\dfrac{w+w-w\sqrt2}{2}=\dfrac{2-\sqrt2}{2}w.
\]
The total area is
\[
S=w(a-w)+\pi r^2
=aw-\left(1-\dfrac{\pi(3-2\sqrt2)}{2}\right)w^2.
\]
Put $K=2-\pi(3-2\sqrt2)$. Since $1<K<2$, completing the square gives
\[
S=\dfrac{a^2}{2K}-\dfrac{K}{2}\left(w-\dfrac{a}{K}\right)^2.
\]
The maximizer lies in $0<w<a$ and is
\[
\boxed{w=\dfrac{a}{K},\quad h=\dfrac{a(K-1)}{K},\quad
r=\dfrac{a(2-\sqrt2)}{2K}},
\quad K=2-\pi(3-2\sqrt2).
\]
The maximum area is $\dfrac{a^2}{2K}$.
