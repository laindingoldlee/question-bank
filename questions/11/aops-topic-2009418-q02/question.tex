A cubical room has edge length $10$ feet, and $A$ and $B$ are two corners of the room that are farthest apart. A caterpillar crawls from $A$ to $B$ along the walls. In feet, what is the length of the shortest path from $A$ to $B$ that the caterpillar may have taken? Express your answer in simplest radical form.
