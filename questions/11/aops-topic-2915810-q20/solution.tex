Write $x=ga,y=gb$ with $\gcd(a,b)=1$. Cancelling $g$ gives $1+ab=3(a+b)$, or $(a-3)(b-3)=8$. Both factors cannot be negative: positivity of $a,b$ would restrict their magnitudes to at most 2, whose product is at most 4. The positive factor pairs are $(1,8),(2,4)$ and reversals, giving $(a,b)=(4,11),(5,7)$ and reversals, all coprime. The least sum is 12, attained with $g=1,a=5,b=7$. Answer $\boxed{12}$.
