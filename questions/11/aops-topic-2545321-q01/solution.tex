Dividing by $a$: $2p+3q+r=4$, $3p+7q+r=9$, $4p+5q+r=8$. Subtracting the first equation from the other two gives $p+4q=5$ and $2p+2q=4$, so $q=1$, $p=1$ and $r=-1$. Thus $a@b=a(a+b-1)$, which also fits the last two values: $5\cdot12=60$ and $6\cdot12=72$. Hence $7@8=7\cdot14=\boxed{98}$.
