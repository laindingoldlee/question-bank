The total label sum is $32\cdot\dfrac{33}{2}=528$, so each player must receive sum 176. Nineteen cards have sum at least $1+\cdots+19=190$, so a player can receive at most 18. This bound is attained by the three disjoint groups $\{1,2,\ldots,17,23\}$, $\{26,28,29,30,31,32\}$ and $\{18,19,20,21,22,24,25,27\}$, each summing to 176. Thus the maximum is $\boxed{18}$.
