The smaller root $b$ of $t^2-2018t+2018$ lies between $1$ and $1.001$: the polynomial has value $1$ at $1$ and value $-1.015999$ at $1.001$, and is strictly decreasing on this interval. Therefore $2016.999<a=2018-b<2017$. Multiplying by $1000$ gives $2016999<1000a<2017000$, so the answer is $\boxed{2016999}$.
