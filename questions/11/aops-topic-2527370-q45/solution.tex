He drives $3\cdot40=120$ miles and uses $\dfrac{120}{20}=6$ gallons. His profit is $1.75\cdot120=210$ dollars, so the gas cost $240-210=30$ dollars, i.e. $\dfrac{30}{6}=\boxed{\$5}$ per gallon.
