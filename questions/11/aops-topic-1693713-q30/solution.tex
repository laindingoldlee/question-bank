Let the side be $s$. The hexagon is made of six equilateral triangles of side $s$, each with height $x$ (the distance from the centre to the midpoint of a side), so $x=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}s$ and
\[
\text{Area}=6\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot s\cdot x=3sx=3\cdot\dfrac{2x}{\sqrt3}\cdot x=2\sqrt3\,x^2 .
\]
Setting $2\sqrt3\,x^2=x^3$ gives $x=2\sqrt3$, so $s=\dfrac{2x}{\sqrt3}=\boxed{4}$ units.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=4,a=2*sqrt(3);
pair O=(0,0);pair[] V;for(int k=0;k<6;++k) V[k]=s*dir(60*k-30);
draw(V[0]--V[1]--V[2]--V[3]--V[4]--V[5]--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
pair Mid=(V[0]+V[1])/2;
draw(O--V[0],gray);draw(O--V[1],gray);draw(O--Mid,blue+linewidth(0.8));
real q=0.3;pair u=unit(O-Mid),v=unit(V[1]-Mid);draw(Mid+q*u--Mid+q*(u+v)--Mid+q*v,blue);
dot(O);label("$O$",O,W);label("$x$",(O+Mid)/2,S,blue);label("$s$",(V[0]+V[1])/2+(0.1,0.9),E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
