Put $z=[DPE]>0$, $w=[BPC]$. The areas of the four triangles at the intersection of $BE,CD$ satisfy $zw=8\cdot3=24$, by writing each as half the product of its two ray lengths and the sine of their angle.

Set $T=[ABC]$, $d=\dfrac{AD}{AB}$, $e=\dfrac{AE}{AC}$. Area decomposition and common altitudes give
\[
dT=8+z,\qquad eT=13+z,\qquad deT=5.
\]
Thus $T=\dfrac{(z+8)(z+13)}{5}$. The five regions also give $T=16+z+\dfrac{24}{z}$. Equating and factoring yields
\[
(z-2)(z^2+18z+60)=0.
\]
The quadratic factor is positive for $z>0$, so $z=2,w=12$, and $\boxed{T=30}$.
