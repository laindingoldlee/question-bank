Measure angles clockwise from 12, in degrees. At $m$ minutes after 9:00 the minute hand is at $6m$ and the hour hand at $270+\dfrac{m}{2}$. The hands point in opposite directions when the hour hand is $180^\circ$ ahead of the minute hand (at 9:00 it is $270^\circ$ ahead, and the gap shrinks by $5.5^\circ$ per minute):
\[
270+\dfrac{m}{2}-6m=180\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \dfrac{11m}{2}=90\quad\Longrightarrow\quad m=\dfrac{180}{11}=16\dfrac{4}{11}.
\]
So the time is $\boxed{9{:}16\dfrac{4}{11}\ \text{AM}}$, about 9:16:22.

(If ``on a straight line'' also allows the hands to overlap, the other time is when the gap is $0$: $m=\dfrac{540}{11}=49\dfrac{1}{11}$, that is $9{:}49\dfrac{1}{11}$ AM.)
