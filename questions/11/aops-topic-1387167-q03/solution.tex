$2n-1$ must be an odd divisor of $20$, positive or negative: $2n-1\in\{1,-1,5,-5\}$, so $n\in\{1,0,3,-2\}$. The sum is $1+0+3-2=\boxed{2}$.

The poster's answer $4$ comes from using only the positive divisors ($n=1$ and $n=3$); negative divisors also make the fraction an integer.
