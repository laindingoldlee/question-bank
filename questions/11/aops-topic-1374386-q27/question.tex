Working in pairs, Alana and Bob can complete a job in $2$ hours, Bob and Cody can do the job in $3$ hours, and Alana and Cody can do the same job in $4$ hours. How many hours will it take Alana, Bob and Cody working together to complete the job? Express your answer as a common fraction.
