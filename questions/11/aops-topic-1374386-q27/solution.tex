Let $a$, $b$, $c$ be the fractions of the job done per hour by Alana, Bob and Cody. Then
\[
a+b=\dfrac{1}{2},\qquad b+c=\dfrac{1}{3},\qquad a+c=\dfrac{1}{4} .
\]
Adding, $2(a+b+c)=\dfrac{6+4+3}{12}=\dfrac{13}{12}$, so together they do $a+b+c=\dfrac{13}{24}$ of the job per hour and need $\boxed{\dfrac{24}{13}}$ hours. (Each rate is positive: $a=\dfrac{13}{24}-\dfrac{1}{3}=\dfrac{5}{24}$, $b=\dfrac{7}{24}$, $c=\dfrac{1}{24}$.)
