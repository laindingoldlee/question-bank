A student wants to show that $1^{\pi}=1$ and argues by induction: ``$1^1=1$; and if $1^n=1$, then $1^{n+1}=1^n\cdot1=1\cdot1=1$. Hence all powers of $1$ are $1$.''
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Exactly which statement does this argument prove?
\item[(b)] Why does it not prove that $1^{\pi}=1$? Give a correct justification that $1^{\pi}=1$.
\end{enumerate}
