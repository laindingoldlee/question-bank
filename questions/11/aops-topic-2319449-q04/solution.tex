Let $a_n$ be the number of ways to climb $n$ stairs. The last stride covers $1$, $2$ or $4$ stairs, so
\[a_n=a_{n-1}+a_{n-2}+a_{n-4},\]
with $a_0=1$ and $a_n=0$ for $n<0$. Then
\[a_1=1,\quad a_2=1+1=2,\quad a_3=2+1=3,\quad a_4=3+2+1=6,\quad a_5=6+3+1=10,\quad a_6=10+6+2=18.\]
There are $\boxed{18}$ ways.
