First, $P\left(\overline A\cap B\right)=P\left(\overline A\mid B\right)P(B)=0.1\cdot0.2=0.02$, so $P(A\cap B)=P(B)-0.02=0.18$.

(a) $P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B)=0.7+0.2-0.18=0.72$, so
\[
P\left(\overline A\cap\overline B\right)=1-P(A\cup B)=\boxed{0.28}.
\]

(b) $P\left(A\cap\overline B\right)=P(A)-P(A\cap B)=0.7-0.18=0.52$ and $P\left(\overline B\right)=0.8$, so
\[
P\left(A\mid\overline B\right)=\dfrac{0.52}{0.8}=\boxed{0.65}.
\]
