A \emph{Venn diagram} for four sets is a drawing of four closed curves such that, for each of the $2^4=16$ ways of choosing which curves a point is inside and which it is outside, some part of the plane has exactly that pattern. Can a Venn diagram for four sets be drawn with four circles? Prove your answer.
