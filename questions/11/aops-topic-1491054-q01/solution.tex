Since $512=8^3$, the numbers $0,1,\dots,511$ are exactly the numbers with at most three base-$8$ digits; padded with leading zeros, they are the $8^3=512$ strings $d_2d_1d_0$ of base-$8$ digits. The strings with no digit $7$ use only the digits $0$ to $6$: there are $7^3=343$ of them. So
\[
512-343=\boxed{169}
\]
of the numbers include a $7$. (If the natural numbers start at $1$, the numbers are $1,\dots,512$; since neither $0$ nor $512=1000_8$ contains a $7$, the answer is the same.)
