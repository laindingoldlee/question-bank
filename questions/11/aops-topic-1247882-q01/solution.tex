The radius of the sphere is $25$. The circle's radius $r$ satisfies $r^2+14^2=25^2$, so $r^2=625-196=429$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw(circle((0,0),25)); draw((-25,0)--(25,0),gray);
draw((-sqrt(429),14)--(sqrt(429),14),blue+linewidth(1)); draw((0,0)--(0,14),dashed); draw((0,0)--(sqrt(429),14),red);
label("$14$",(0,7),W); label("$25$",(sqrt(429)/2,7),SE); label("$r$",(sqrt(429)/2,14),N);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The area is $\pi r^2=\boxed{429\pi}\approx1347.7$ square centimetres.
