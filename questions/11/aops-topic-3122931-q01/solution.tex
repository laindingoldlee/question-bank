Let $p_n$ be the probability of heads on toss $n$. Conditioning on the preceding toss gives $p_{n+1}=\dfrac{1}{2}p_n+(1-p_n)=1-\dfrac{1}{2}p_n$. Starting at $p_1=\dfrac{1}{2}$ gives $p_2=\dfrac{3}{4},p_3=\dfrac{5}{8},p_4=\dfrac{11}{16}$, and $p_5=\boxed{\dfrac{21}{32}}$. In casework, the valid two-tail sequences ending in heads are THTHH, THHTH, and HTHTH; omitting THHTH loses probability $\dfrac{1}{8}$.
