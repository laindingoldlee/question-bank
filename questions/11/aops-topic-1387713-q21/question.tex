Manny has $2$ red socks, $2$ white socks and $2$ blue socks. He chooses two socks at random to wear today, two of the remaining four socks at random to wear tomorrow, and wears the last two socks the day after. What is the probability that on each of the three days Manny wears two socks of different colours? Express your answer as a common fraction.

(A student counts ``cases'': $9$ ways in which the first pair is a matching pair and $12$ ways in which it is not, $21$ cases in all, of which $9$ are good, and gets $\dfrac{9}{21}=\dfrac{3}{7}$. What is wrong with this?)
