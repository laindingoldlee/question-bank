The arc $CD$ not containing $A$ measures $100^\circ$. Angle $ABC$ intercepts arc $AEDC$ and angle $AED$ intercepts arc $ABCD$. These two arcs together contain the whole circle once and arc $CD$ one extra time. Hence the angle sum is $\dfrac{360^\circ+100^\circ}{2}=\boxed{230^\circ}$.

\end{asy}
