Take logarithms and accumulate $L_x=\sum_{j=1}^x\log(1+\dfrac{j}{10^6})$. The target is $L_x>6\log10$. Since the summands are positive, the first crossing is the unique threshold. Direct summation gives the least integer $\boxed{5261}$; the included computation checks both 5260 and 5261. As a quick estimate, $L_x\approx \dfrac{x(x+1)}{(2\cdot10^6)}$ predicts about 5256, close to the exact threshold.
