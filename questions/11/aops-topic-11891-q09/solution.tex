Any $3$ vertices of a cube form a triangle, because no three vertices of a cube lie on a line (every line meets the cube's vertex set in at most $2$ points). So the number of triangles is
\[
\dbinom{8}{3}=\boxed{56}.
\]
For a unit cube they come in three shapes:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair d=(0.45,0.32);
pair[] V(pair o){return new pair[]{o,o+(1,0),o+(1,0)+d,o+d,o+(0,1),o+(1,1),o+(1,1)+d,o+(0,1)+d};}
void cub(pair o){pair[] v=V(o);
  draw(v[0]--v[1]--v[5]--v[4]--cycle); draw(v[1]--v[2]--v[6]--v[5]); draw(v[4]--v[7]--v[6]);
  draw(v[0]--v[3],dashed); draw(v[3]--v[2],dashed); draw(v[3]--v[7],dashed);}
pair o1=(0,0),o2=(2.2,0),o3=(4.4,0);
cub(o1);cub(o2);cub(o3);
pair[] a=V(o1),b=V(o2),c=V(o3);
filldraw(a[0]--a[1]--a[5]--cycle,lightred+opacity(0.5),red);
filldraw(b[0]--b[1]--b[6]--cycle,lightblue+opacity(0.5),blue);
filldraw(c[1]--c[4]--c[6]--cycle,palegreen+opacity(0.5),heavygreen);
label("$1,1,\sqrt2$",(o1+(0.6,-0.1)),S,red); label("$1,\sqrt2,\sqrt3$",(o2+(0.6,-0.1)),S,blue); label("$\sqrt2,\sqrt2,\sqrt2$",(o3+(0.6,-0.1)),S,heavygreen);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

$24$ right isosceles triangles with sides $1,1,\sqrt2$ (four in each face), $24$ right triangles with sides $1,\sqrt2,\sqrt3$, and $8$ equilateral triangles with side $\sqrt2$; indeed $24+24+8=56$.
