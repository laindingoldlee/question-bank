Order the weights $w_1\le w_2\le\cdots\le w_8$. Then
\[
w_1+\cdots+w_6=6\cdot40=240,\qquad w_3+\cdots+w_8=6\cdot55=330 .
\]
Subtracting, $(w_7+w_8)-(w_1+w_2)=90$.

\textbf{Lower bound.} Since $w_8\ge w_7$ and $w_1\le w_2$,
\[
2(w_8-w_1)\ge(w_7+w_8)-(w_1+w_2)=90,
\]
so the range $w_8-w_1$ is at least $45$.

\textbf{It is attained.} Take $w_1=w_2=30$, $w_3=w_4=w_5=w_6=45$, $w_7=w_8=75$. Then the lightest six sum to $60+180=240$, the heaviest six to $180+150=330$, and the range is $75-30=45$. So the least possible range is $\boxed{45}$ pounds.
