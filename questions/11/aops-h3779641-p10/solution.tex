Current tenth-graders have one chance, eleventh-graders two, and twelfth-graders three. Their probabilities of at least one trophy are $1-0.9=0.1$, $1-0.9^2=0.19$, and $1-0.9^3=0.271$. Equal grade sizes give mean probability $\dfrac{0.1+0.19+0.271}{3}=0.187$. Hence the expected percentage is $\boxed{18.7\%}$.
