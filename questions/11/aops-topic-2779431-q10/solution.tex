The vertical separation of the centers is $R-1$, and their distance is $R+1$. Therefore $\dfrac{R-1}{R+1}=\sin60^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$. Solving and rationalizing gives $R=\dfrac{2+\sqrt3}{2-\sqrt3}=\boxed{7+4\sqrt3}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
real R=7+4*sqrt(3);pair A=(0,1),B=((R+1)/2,R);draw(circle(A,1));draw(circle(B,R));draw((-2,0)--(24,0));draw(A--B,blue);draw(A--(B.x,1),dashed);label("$1$",A,W);label("$R$",B,E);label("$60^\circ$",(1,1.7));
\end{asy}
