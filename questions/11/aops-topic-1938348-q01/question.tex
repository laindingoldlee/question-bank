Bob has a deck of $50$ cards labelled $1,2,\dots,50$. He draws $3$ cards at random. Then, as long as his hand contains a card numbered less than $20$, he replaces such a card by a card drawn at random from the cards numbered $20$ or more that are still in the deck. What is the probability that at least two cards of his final hand have the same tens digit?
