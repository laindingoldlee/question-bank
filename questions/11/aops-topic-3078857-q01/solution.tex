The integer is $2^{26}-5=(2^{26}-1)-4$. The number $2^{26}-1$ has twenty-six binary ones. Subtracting $4=2^2$ changes only the third digit from the right from $1$ to $0$. Thus the result is $\boxed{\underbrace{11\cdots1}_{23\text{ ones}}011_2}$, equivalently $\boxed{(11111111111111111111111011)_2}$.
