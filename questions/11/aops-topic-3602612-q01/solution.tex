Put $D=b^2-4ac$ and denote the displayed numbers by $u,v$. The product of their denominators is $b^2-D=4ac\ne0$, so both are defined. We have $u+v=\dfrac{2c(-2b)}{4ac}=-\dfrac{b}{a}$ and $uv=\dfrac{4c^2}{4ac}=\dfrac{c}{a}$. Therefore $a(x-u)(x-v)=ax^2+bx+c$, proving the claim, including the double-root case.
