Let $a,b,c$ be real constants with $ac\ne0$ and $b^2-4ac\ge0$. Prove, using the sum and product of the roots, that the roots of $ax^2+bx+c=0$, counted with multiplicity, are $\dfrac{2c}{-b+\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}$ and $\dfrac{2c}{-b-\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}$.
