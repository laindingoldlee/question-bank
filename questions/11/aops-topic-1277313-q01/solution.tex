No. Suppose $k$ were the smallest integer less than $100$. Then $k-1$ is also an integer, and $k-1<k<100$, so $k-1$ is a smaller integer less than $100$, contradicting the choice of $k$. So the set of integers less than $100$ has no smallest element, and the question "what is the smallest integer value of $n$ with $n<100$" has $\boxed{\text{no answer}}$. (Among \emph{positive} integers the smallest would be $1$; the largest integer below $100$ is $99$.)
