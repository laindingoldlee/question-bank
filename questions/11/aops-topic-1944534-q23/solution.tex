By the binomial theorem,
\[
11^{41}=(1+10)^{41}=1+\binom{41}{1}10+\binom{41}{2}10^2+\binom{41}{3}10^3+\cdots .
\]
Every term from $\binom{41}{3}10^3$ on is divisible by $1000$. Also $\binom{41}{2}10^2=820\cdot100=82000\equiv0\pmod{1000}$. Hence
\[
11^{41}\equiv1+410=411\pmod{1000},
\]
and the remainder is $\boxed{\textbf{(C) }411}$.
