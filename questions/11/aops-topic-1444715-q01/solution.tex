If $N=a^2=b^5$, then in the prime factorisation of $N$ every exponent is divisible by $2$ and by $5$, hence by $10$. So $N$ is a perfect tenth power, and conversely every tenth power $c^{10}=(c^5)^2=(c^2)^5$ works. The smallest tenth power greater than $1$ is
\[
2^{10}=\boxed{1024}=32^2=4^5 .
\]
