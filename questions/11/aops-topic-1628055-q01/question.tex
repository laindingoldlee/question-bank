A company has $52$ employees working in three shifts: $20$ employees work from 9:00 AM to 12:00 noon, $18$ employees from 11:00 AM to 2:00 PM, and $14$ employees from 12:00 noon to 2:00 PM. (At 12:00 noon the first group leaves as the third group arrives.) The company has only $8$ computers, which are shared by the employees who are present.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] During which period of the day are the most employees present, and how many are present then?
\item[(b)] Show that from 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM there are always more employees present than computers, and explain in what sense the computers are in greatest demand during the period found in (a).
\end{enumerate}
