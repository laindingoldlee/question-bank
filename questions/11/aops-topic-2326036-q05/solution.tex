Number the days $1$ to $365$ (February 2021 has $28$ days). Every day on which the first friend is called (a multiple of $14$) is also a multiple of $7$, so the first friend adds no new days. I call on day $k$ exactly when $7\mid k$ or $3\mid k$, and by inclusion-exclusion that happens on
\[\left\lfloor\dfrac{365}{7}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{365}{3}\right\rfloor-\left\lfloor\dfrac{365}{21}\right\rfloor=52+121-17=\boxed{156}\]
days.
