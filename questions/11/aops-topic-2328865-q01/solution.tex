By linearity of expectation, the expected number of correct answers is the sum over the $15$ problems of the probability of getting each one right. So
\[j=15\cdot0.75=11.25,\qquad k=15\cdot0.9=13.5,\]
and
\[j-k=11.25-13.5=\boxed{-2.25}=-\dfrac94.\]
