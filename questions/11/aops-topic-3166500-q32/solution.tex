Use $T=(0,0)$, $B=(20,0)$, and $A=(0,15)$. Then $AB=25$ and power of $A$ gives $AC=\dfrac{15^2}{25}=9$, so $C=(\dfrac{36}{5},\dfrac{48}{5})$. Since $BT$ is a diameter, $CT\perp AB$, hence the circumcircle of $ACT$ has diameter $AT$, center $(0,\dfrac{15}{2})$, and radius $\dfrac{15}{2}$. The tangent at $A$ is $y=15$; intersecting it with the tangent at $C$ gives $P=(\dfrac{45}{8},15)$. Consequently $PB=\sqrt{(20-\dfrac{45}{8})^2+15^2}=\dfrac{5\sqrt{1105}}{8}>20$. The point $S$ ranges over the radius-twenty circle centered at $B$, so the triangle inequality and its equality cases give $\boxed{PS_{\min}=\dfrac{5\sqrt{1105}-160}{8}}$ and $\boxed{PS_{\max}=\dfrac{5\sqrt{1105}+160}{8}}$.

\end{asy}
