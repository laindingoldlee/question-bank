Let $0<r<1$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that for every positive integer $m$,
\[(2m+1)r^m(1-r)<1-r^{2m+1}.\]
\item[(b)] Deduce that $nr^n$ tends to $0$ as $n$ tends to infinity.
\end{enumerate}
