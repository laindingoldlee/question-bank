A rectangle in the strip is determined by its left and right edges, which are two different lines among the $11$ vertical grid lines. So there are
\[
\dbinom{11}{2}=\boxed{55}
\]
rectangles. (By sizes: $10$ of length $1$, $9$ of length $2$, \dots, $1$ of length $10$, and $10+9+\dots+1=55$.)
