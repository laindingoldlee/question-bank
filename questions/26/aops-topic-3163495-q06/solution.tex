Each leading digit contributes six consecutive entries. The eighteenth is the last beginning with $3$, namely $3421$. The nineteenth and twentieth are the first two beginning with $4$, namely $4123,4132$. Thus the required sum is $3421+4132=\boxed{7553}$.
