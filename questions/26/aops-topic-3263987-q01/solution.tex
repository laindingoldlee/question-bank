Take natural logarithms: $(3x-1)\ln4=(x+2)\ln5$. Thus $x(3\ln4-\ln5)=\ln4+2\ln5$. The coefficient is $\ln(\dfrac{64}{5})>0$, giving the unique solution $\boxed{x=\dfrac{\ln100}{\ln(\dfrac{64}{5})}}$.
