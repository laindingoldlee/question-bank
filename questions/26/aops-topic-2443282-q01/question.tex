A right circular cone has base radius $r$ and height $h$. Prove, without using calculus, that its volume is $\dfrac13\pi r^2h$.

You may use: the volume of a cylinder of radius $R$ and height $H$ is $\pi R^2H$; a solid contained in another solid has at most the same volume; and $1^2+2^2+\cdots+n^2=\dfrac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6}$.
