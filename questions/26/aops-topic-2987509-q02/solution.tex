When Billy has walked $3$ miles the ostrich has walked $1.5$. They then approach each other with combined speed $1.5$ times Billy's speed across a gap of $1.5$ miles, so they meet after Billy walks $1$ more mile and the ostrich $0.5$ mile: the meeting point is $2$ miles up. The ostrich then walks $2$ miles down (the speed does not matter). Total: $2+2=\boxed{4}$ miles.
