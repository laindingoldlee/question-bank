Billy and his ostrich start together at the bottom of a $3$-mile hill and walk up it; the ostrich walks at half Billy's speed. When Billy reaches the top he turns around and walks down at the same speed. When he meets the ostrich, both walk down to the bottom (at $300$ times their normal speeds). How many miles does the ostrich walk in total?
