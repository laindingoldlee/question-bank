Set $N=2k+1$ and $d=z-w$. Expanding the squared modulus gives
\[
|Nw+nd|^2=N^2|w|^2+2Nn\operatorname{Re}(d\overline w)+n^2|d|^2.
\]
Pairing indices $2j$ and $2j+1$ yields
\[
\sum_{n=0}^{2k+1}(-1)^n=0,\quad
\sum(-1)^nn=-(k+1),\quad
\sum(-1)^nn^2=-(k+1)(2k+1).
\]
Thus the required sum is
\[
-(k+1)(2k+1)\bigl(2\operatorname{Re}((z-w)\overline w)+|z-w|^2\bigr)
=\boxed{(k+1)(2k+1)(|w|^2-|z|^2)}.
\]
