Let the divider have length $y$ and the other dimension be $x$. Then $xy=6000000$ and fencing is $2x+3y$. AM--GM gives $2x+3y\ge2\sqrt{6xy}=12000$, with equality when $2x=3y$. Solving yields $\boxed{x=3000,\ y=2000}$ feet.
