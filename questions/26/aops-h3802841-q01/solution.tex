The numbers are the roots of $x^4-x^3+x^2-2x+3$. Let $p_k=a^k+b^k+c^k+d^k$. Newton's identities give
\[
p_1=1,\quad p_2=1-2=-1,
\]
\[
p_3=p_2-p_1+3\cdot2=4,\quad
p_4=p_3-p_2+2p_1-4\cdot3=-5.
\]
For $k>4$, multiplying the root equation by the appropriate power and summing gives
\[
p_k=p_{k-1}-p_{k-2}+2p_{k-3}-3p_{k-4}.
\]
Thus $p_5=-5-4+2(-1)-3=-14$ and
\[
p_6=-14-(-5)+2\cdot4-3(-1)=\boxed2.
\]
