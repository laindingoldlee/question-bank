Let $Z=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{k^3}$, which converges (for instance, $\dfrac{1}{k^3}\le\dfrac{1}{k(k-1)}=\dfrac{1}{k-1}-\dfrac{1}{k}$ for $k\ge2$, and these terms telescope). All terms are positive, so we may split the series into its even and odd terms:
\[
Z=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{(2k)^3}+\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{(2k+1)^3}=\dfrac{1}{8}Z+\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{(2k+1)^3}.
\]
Hence the sum over odd numbers is $\dfrac{7}{8}Z$, and the ratio is $\boxed{\dfrac{7}{8}}$. (No closed form for $Z$ is needed.)
