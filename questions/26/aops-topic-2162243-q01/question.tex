Each of $101$ girls buys some greeting cards. One of the girls, Suzie, buys $1000$ fewer cards than the average number bought by the $101$ girls. How many more cards should Suzie buy so that she has $1000$ more cards than the new average of the $101$ girls? (The other girls buy no more cards.)
