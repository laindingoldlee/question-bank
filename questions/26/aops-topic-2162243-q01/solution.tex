Let $A$ be the original average, so the girls have $101A$ cards in total and Suzie has $A-1000$. If Suzie buys $x$ more cards, the total becomes $101A+x$ and the new average is $A+\dfrac{x}{101}$. We need
\[A-1000+x=A+\dfrac{x}{101}+1000\iff x-\dfrac{x}{101}=2000\iff\dfrac{100x}{101}=2000\iff x=\boxed{2020}.\]
Note that Suzie must gain $2000$ on the average, but every card she buys also raises the average by $\dfrac{1}{101}$ of a card, so she needs a little more than $2000$.
