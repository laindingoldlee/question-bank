The centres of the unit spheres form an equilateral triangle of side $2$ at height $1$. The larger centre lies on the vertical line through its circumcenter, whose horizontal distance to each small centre is $\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{3}}$. Tangency makes the centre distance $3$, so its height above the small-centre plane is $\sqrt{9 - \dfrac{4}{3}} = \dfrac{\sqrt{69}}{3}$. Adding the bottom height $1$ and larger radius $2$ gives $\boxed{3 + \dfrac{\sqrt{69}}{3}}$.
