Tony puts together five bags of jelly beans, with the same number of jelly beans in each bag. He then pours all the jelly beans out and arranges them in rows: one jelly bean in the top row, three in the next row, five in the row after that, and so on, each row having two more jelly beans than the row above. He finishes a row with no jelly beans left over, and there are more than $10$ but fewer than $20$ rows. (For example, with exactly $25$ jelly beans he would finish the fifth row with none left over.) How many jelly beans are in each bag?
