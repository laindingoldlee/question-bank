The first $n$ rows contain $1+3+5+\cdots+(2n-1)=n^2$ jelly beans (the sum of the first $n$ odd numbers). So the total is $n^2$ with $11\le n\le19$.

The total was split equally into five bags, so $5\mid n^2$, and since $5$ is prime, $5\mid n$. The only multiple of $5$ from $11$ to $19$ is $n=15$, so there are $15^2=225$ jelly beans and each bag holds
\[
\dfrac{225}{5}=\boxed{45}
\]
jelly beans.
