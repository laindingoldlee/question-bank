A school has $10$ classes with the same number of students in each class. One day the weather was bad and many students were absent: $5$ classes were half full, $3$ classes were $\dfrac34$ full and $2$ classes were $\dfrac18$ empty. A total of $70$ students were absent. How many students are in the school when no students are absent?
