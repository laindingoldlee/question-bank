Find all positive integers $c$ for which there are positive integers $x_1,y_1,x_2,y_2$ with
\[x_1^2+y_1^2=c\qquad\text{and}\qquad x_2^2+y_2^2=c^2.\]
You may use the two-square theorem: a positive integer is a sum of two squares of integers (zero allowed) if and only if every prime $q\equiv3\pmod4$ divides it to an even power.
