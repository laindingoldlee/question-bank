A pizza shop makes $40$ pizzas per day. One quarter of them have $20$ pepperoni slices and $10$ olives each; the other three quarters have $15$ pepperoni slices, $5$ salami slices and $10$ olives each. Pepperoni costs $1$ dollar per slice, olives cost $3$ dollars per $2$ olives, and salami costs $7$ dollars per $5$ slices. How much do the toppings for one day's pizzas cost?
