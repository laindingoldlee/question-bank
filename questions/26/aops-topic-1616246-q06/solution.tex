There are $10$ pizzas of the first kind and $30$ of the second.
\begin{itemize}
\item Pepperoni: $10\cdot20+30\cdot15=650$ slices, costing $650$ dollars.
\item Olives: $40\cdot10=400$ olives, costing $400\cdot\dfrac{3}{2}=600$ dollars.
\item Salami: $30\cdot5=150$ slices, costing $150\cdot\dfrac{7}{5}=210$ dollars.
\end{itemize}
The total is $650+600+210=\boxed{1460\text{ dollars}}$.
