Count by the number $w$ of white faces. $w=0$: $1$. $w=1$: $1$. $w=2$: the two white faces are adjacent or opposite: $2$. $w=3$: the three white faces either meet at a corner or form a ``band'' (two opposite faces and one between): $2$. By symmetry $w=4,5,6$ give $2,1,1$. Total $1+1+2+2+2+1+1=\boxed{10}$.
