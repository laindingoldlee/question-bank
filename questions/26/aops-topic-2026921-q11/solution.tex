We have $10\star20=10\cdot11\cdot12\cdots30$ and $13\star16=13\cdot14\cdots29$. All factors $13$ through $29$ cancel, leaving
\[
\dfrac{10\star20}{13\star16}=10\cdot11\cdot12\cdot30=\boxed{39600}.
\]
