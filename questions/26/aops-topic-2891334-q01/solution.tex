$9^{2-n}=3^{4-2n}=9\cdot3^{2-2n}$, so the denominator is $8\cdot3^{2-2n}$. The fraction is $\dfrac{72}{8\cdot3^{2-2n}}=9\cdot3^{2n-2}=3^{2n}$, and its $n$th root is $\boxed{9}$.
