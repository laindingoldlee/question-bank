The hypotenuse has length $\sqrt{6^2+10^2}=\sqrt{136}=2\sqrt{34}$. In a right triangle the circumcenter is the midpoint of the hypotenuse, so the radius is $\sqrt{34}$. The circle area is $\boxed{34\pi\text{ square centimeters}}$.
\begin{asy}
import geometry; size(220);
pair A=(0,0),B=(10,0),C=(0,6),O=(5,3);draw(circle(O,sqrt(34)));draw(A--B--C--cycle);dot(O);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NW);label("$O$",O,NE);label("$10$",(5,0),S);label("$6$",(0,3),W);
\end{asy}
