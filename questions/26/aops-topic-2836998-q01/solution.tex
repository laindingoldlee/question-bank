There are $3^{10}=59049$ placements. Different placements give different rationals, because a positive rational number in lowest terms has a unique factorisation $\prod p^{e_p}$ with integer exponents, and here each exponent is $1$, $-1$ or $0$ according to the placement. So there are $\boxed{59049}$ distinct rationals.
