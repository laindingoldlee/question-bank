Given that $\dfrac{1}{1^2}+\dfrac{1}{2^2}+\dfrac{1}{3^2}+\cdots=\dfrac{\pi^2}{6}$, the sum
\[
\dfrac{1}{1^2\cdot2^2}+\dfrac{1}{2^2\cdot3^2}+\cdots+\dfrac{1}{n^2(n+1)^2}+\cdots
\]
can be written as $\dfrac{a\pi^2+b\pi+c}{d}$ for integers $a,b,c,d$ with $d>0$ and $\gcd(a,b,c,d)=1$. Find $a+b+c+d$.
