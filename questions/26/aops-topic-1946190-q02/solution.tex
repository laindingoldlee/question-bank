By the triangle inequality for real numbers, $|u|+|v|\ge|u-v|$, so for every real $x$
\[
|3x-4|+|3x+2|\ge\bigl|(3x+2)-(3x-4)\bigr|=6 .
\]
Hence the left side is never less than $6$, and the inequality has $\boxed{\text{no real solutions}}$. (Equality $|3x-4|+|3x+2|=6$ holds exactly when $3x-4\le0\le3x+2$, i.e. $-\dfrac{2}{3}\le x\le\dfrac{4}{3}$; outside that interval the sum is larger than $6$.)
