Triangles $ABC$ and $A'B'C'$ are similar, with $A'B'=k\cdot AB$, $B'C'=k\cdot BC$, $C'A'=k\cdot CA$ for some $k>0$. Prove that the circumradius of $A'B'C'$ is $k$ times the circumradius of $ABC$, and that the inradius of $A'B'C'$ is $k$ times the inradius of $ABC$. (For example, if the sides are in the ratio $5:1$, so are the circumradii and the inradii.)
