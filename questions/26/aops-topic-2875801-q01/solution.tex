Write the palindrome as $N=\displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{2k-1}d_in^i$ with $d_i=d_{2k-1-i}$. Since $n\equiv-1\pmod{n+1}$,
\[N\equiv\sum_{i=0}^{2k-1}(-1)^id_i\pmod{n+1}.\]
Pair the index $i$ with $2k-1-i$. Their sum $2k-1$ is odd, so one index is even and the other odd. The two terms are $d_i$ and $-d_i$, which cancel, so $N\equiv0\pmod{n+1}$. $\blacksquare$
