Newton's forward-difference formula (``Newton's little formula'') says that if the first term is $a_1$, the first difference is $d_1$ and the (constant) second difference is $d_2$, then
\[
a_n=a_1+\binom{n-1}{1}d_1+\binom{n-1}{2}d_2 .
\]
(Reason: $a_n=a_1+\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}\left(d_1+(k-1)d_2\right)$, and $\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}(k-1)=\binom{n-1}{2}$.) Here
\[
a_n=40+28(n-1)+6\cdot\dfrac{(n-1)(n-2)}{2}=40+28(n-1)+3(n-1)(n-2)=\boxed{3n^2+19n+18}.
\]
Check: $n=1,2,3$ give $40,68,102$. Then $a_{10}=300+190+18=\boxed{508}$.
