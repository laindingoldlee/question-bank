Work from left to right, using $(xy)^k=x^ky^k$, $\left(x^p\right)^q=x^{pq}$, $x^px^q=x^{p+q}$ and $x^p\div x^q=x^{p-q}$.

\textbf{(a)} $\left(6mn^{-3}\right)^{-2}=6^{-2}m^{-2}n^{6}$, so
\[
12m^{-5}n^{3}\div6^{-2}m^{-2}n^{6}=12\cdot36\,m^{-5+2}n^{3-6}=432m^{-3}n^{-3}.
\]
Then $\left(m^3n^2\right)^4=m^{12}n^{8}$ and
\[
432m^{-3}n^{-3}\times m^{12}n^{8}=\boxed{432m^{9}n^{5}} .
\]
All exponents are positive here, so this is already a product of powers with no fraction; nothing has to be rewritten.

\textbf{(b)} $\left(d^5\right)^2=d^{10}$ and $\left(d^{-3}e\right)^4=d^{-12}e^{4}$, so the product is $d^{-2}e^{4}$. Dividing by $d^{-4}e^{5}$,
\[
d^{-2-(-4)}e^{4-5}=\boxed{d^{2}e^{-1}} .
\]
Written as a fraction this would be $\dfrac{d^2}{e}$; ``negative index form'' means writing $\dfrac{1}{e}$ as $e^{-1}$ instead.
