The tangent at $P$ is $2x-y=1$, so the normal has direction $(2,-1)$. Substitute $(x,y)=(2+2t,3-t)$ into the hyperbola:
\[
\dfrac{t(11t+30)}{3}=0.
\]
The second point corresponds to $t=-\dfrac{30}{11}$, giving $Q=\left(-\dfrac{38}{11},\dfrac{63}{11}\right)$.
Its tangent is $-38x-21y=11$. Solving it with $2x-y=1$ gives $R=\left(\dfrac{1}{8},-\dfrac{3}{4}\right)$.

Now $Q-P=\left(-\dfrac{60}{11},\dfrac{30}{11}\right)$ and $R-P=\left(-\dfrac{15}{8},-\dfrac{15}{4}\right)$. The determinant formula gives
\[
[PQR]=\dfrac{1}{2}\left|\dfrac{900}{44}+\dfrac{450}{88}\right|
=\boxed{\dfrac{1125}{88}}.
\]
