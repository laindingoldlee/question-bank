Let $x$ be an even integer that is a sum of two squares of integers, $x=a^2+b^2$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $\dfrac {x}{2}$ is also a sum of two squares of integers.
\item[(b)] Show that "squares of positive integers" cannot be required in both places: give an even number that is a sum of two squares of positive integers while its half is not. Prove that if $a$ and $b$ are positive and $a\ne b$, then $\dfrac {x}{2}$ is a sum of two squares of positive integers.
\end{enumerate}
