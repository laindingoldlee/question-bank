The equation factors as $(x-1)(y-1)=50$. Then $x\ne1$, and for each rational $t=x-1\ne0$ we get $y-1=\dfrac{50}{t}$. So the solutions are exactly
\[\boxed{(x,y)=\left(1+t,\ 1+\dfrac{50}{t}\right),\quad t\in\mathbb{Q},\ t\ne0},\]
and there are infinitely many.
