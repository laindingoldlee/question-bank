Let her buy $x$ stamps at $19$ cents and $y$ at $41$ cents: $19x+41y=1000$ with $x,y\ge0$. Modulo $19$: $41\equiv3$ and $1000=19\cdot52+12\equiv12$, so $3y\equiv12$, i.e.\ $y\equiv4\pmod{19}$. Since $41y\le1000$ gives $y\le24$, $y=4$ or $y=23$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $y=4$: $19x=836$, $x=44$, total $48$;
\item $y=23$: $19x=1000-943=57$, $x=3$, total $26$.
\end{itemize}
The smallest total is $\boxed{26}$.
