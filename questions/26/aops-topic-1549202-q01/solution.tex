Let the original speed be $v$ miles per hour. Then
\[
\dfrac{120}{v}-\dfrac{120}{v+8}=\dfrac{1}{2}\iff\dfrac{120\cdot8}{v(v+8)}=\dfrac{1}{2}\iff v(v+8)=1920 .
\]
So $v^2+8v-1920=0$, i.e.\ $(v-40)(v+48)=0$, and since $v>0$, $v=\boxed{40}$. (Check: $3$ hours at $40$ mph, $2.5$ hours at $48$ mph.)
