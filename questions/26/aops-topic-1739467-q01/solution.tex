The mean $46\dfrac{20}{23}=\dfrac{1078}{23}$ has denominator $23$, and the sum of the remaining numbers, $(n-1)\cdot\dfrac{1078}{23}$, is an integer. Since $\gcd(1078,23)=1$, $23$ divides $n-1$.

Erasing one number changes the mean only a little. If the largest number $n$ is erased the mean is $\dfrac{1+2+\dots+(n-1)}{n-1}=\dfrac {n}{2}$, and if $1$ is erased it is $\dfrac{n+2}{2}$. So
\[
\dfrac {n}{2}\le\dfrac{1078}{23}\le\dfrac{n+2}{2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 91<n<94,
\]
so $n=92$ or $93$, and $n-1$ divisible by $23$ forces $n=93$. Then the remaining sum is $92\cdot\dfrac{1078}{23}=4\cdot1078=4312$, while $1+2+\dots+93=\dfrac{93\cdot94}{2}=4371$. Hence
\[
K=4371-4312=\boxed{59}.
\]
