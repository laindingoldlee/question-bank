Let $ABC$ be a triangle with $AB = 4$, $BC = 5$, and $CA = 6$. A triangle $XY Z$ is said to be friendly if it intersects triangle $ABC$ and it is a translation of triangle $ABC$. Let $S$ be the set of points in the plane that are inside some friendly triangle. Compute the ratio of the area of $S$ to the area of triangle $ABC$.
