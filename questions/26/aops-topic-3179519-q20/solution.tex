Write $K$ for the original filled triangle. A translate $K+v$ meets $K$ exactly when $v\in K-K$, so their union is $K+(K-K)=2K-K$. Area ratios are preserved by invertible affine maps, so take $K$ with vertices $(0,0),(1,0),(0,1)$. The sums of a vertex of $2K$ and a vertex of $-K$ have convex hull with vertices $(-1,0),(0,-1),(2,-1),(2,0),(0,2),(-1,2)$. This hexagon is the square $[-1,2]^2$ with corner triangles of areas $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $2$ removed, hence has area $\dfrac{13}{2}$. Since $K$ has area $\dfrac{1}{2}$, the required ratio is $\boxed{13}$. Boundary inclusion does not affect area.
