A path from $(0,0)$ to $(m,n)$ consists of $m$ right steps and $n$ up steps in some order, so there are $\dbinom{m+n}{m}$ of them.

All paths to $(9,6)$: $\dbinom{15}{6}=5005$.

Through $P$: $\dbinom{6}{3}\dbinom{9}{3}=20\cdot 84=1680$ (from $(0,0)$ to $(3,3)$, then from $(3,3)$ to $(9,6)$, which is $6$ right and $3$ up).

Through $Q$: $\dbinom{10}{4}\dbinom{5}{2}=210\cdot 10=2100$ (to $(6,4)$, then $3$ right and $2$ up).

Through both: since $P$ comes before $Q$ on such a path, $\dbinom63\dbinom41\dbinom52=20\cdot4\cdot10=800$ (from $(3,3)$ to $(6,4)$ is $3$ right and $1$ up).

By inclusion--exclusion, the number of paths avoiding both points is
\[5005-1680-2100+800=\boxed{2025}.\]
