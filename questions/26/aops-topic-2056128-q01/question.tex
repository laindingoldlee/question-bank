A person starts at the origin in the $xy$-plane and moves in unit steps, each step either in the positive $x$-direction or in the positive $y$-direction. In how many ways can the person reach $A=(9,6)$ while avoiding both of the points $P=(3,3)$ and $Q=(6,4)$?
