In $2021$ (not a leap year), on the $a$th day of a month with $b$ days, Bob goes to the store with probability $\dfrac ab$, and if he goes, he buys a watermelon with probability $\dfrac{a}{b+1}$. The expected number of days in $2021$ on which he buys a watermelon is $\dfrac xy$, where $x$ and $y$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find the remainder when $x+y$ is divided by $1000$.
