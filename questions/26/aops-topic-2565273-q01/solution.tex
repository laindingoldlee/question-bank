On the $a$th day of a month with $b$ days, the probability of buying a watermelon is $\dfrac ab\cdot\dfrac{a}{b+1}$. By linearity of expectation, the expected number of such days in that month is
\[\sum_{a=1}^{b}\dfrac{a^2}{b(b+1)}=\dfrac{1}{b(b+1)}\cdot\dfrac{b(b+1)(2b+1)}{6}=\dfrac{2b+1}{6}.\]
Summing over the $12$ months, whose lengths add up to $365$,
\[\sum\dfrac{2b+1}{6}=\dfrac{2\cdot365+12}{6}=\dfrac{742}{6}=\dfrac{371}{3}.\]
Since $371=7\cdot53$ is coprime to $3$, $x+y=374$, and the remainder is $\boxed{374}$.
