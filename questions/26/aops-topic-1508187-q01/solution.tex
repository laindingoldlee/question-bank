Factor numerators and denominators:
\[
\dfrac{3a+3b}{15a+300b}=\dfrac{3(a+b)}{15(a+20b)}=\dfrac{a+b}{5(a+20b)},\qquad
\dfrac{4a+2b}{8a+6b}=\dfrac{2(2a+b)}{2(4a+3b)}=\dfrac{2a+b}{4a+3b}.
\]
The denominators $5(a+20b)$ and $4a+3b$ have no common factor, so the least common denominator is
\[
\boxed{5(a+20b)(4a+3b)} .
\]
(If the fractions are not reduced first, the least common multiple of the original denominators $15(a+20b)$ and $2(4a+3b)$ is $30(a+20b)(4a+3b)$.)
