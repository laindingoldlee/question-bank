Juventus finishes first or second in its group with probability $\dfrac{2}{4}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. It must then win $4$ knockout matches, with probability $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^4=\dfrac{1}{16}$. So the probability is
\[\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{1}{16}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{32}}.\]
This agrees with symmetry: all $32$ teams are treated alike, and exactly one of them wins.
