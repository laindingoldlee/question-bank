In a tournament, $32$ teams are drawn into $8$ groups of $4$. In each group, every final ranking of the four teams is equally likely (ties are broken by a fair random draw), and the top two teams of each group advance to a knockout stage of $16$ teams. In the knockout stage each match is won by either team with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, independently, and the loser is eliminated; there are $4$ rounds. What is the probability that a given team (say, Juventus) wins the tournament?
