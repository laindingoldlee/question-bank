The longest side is the hypotenuse, and the other two sides are the equal legs:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.8cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
real h=2^(1/3), l=h/sqrt(2);
pair C=(0,0),A=(l,0),B=(0,l);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));draw(ra(C,A-C,B-C,0.08));
draw((l/2,-0.035)--(l/2,0.035));draw((-0.035,l/2)--(0.035,l/2));
label("$\sqrt[3]{2}$",(A+B)/2,NE);label("$2^a$",(l/2,-0.13));label("$2^a$",(-0.15,l/2));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

In an isosceles right triangle the hypotenuse is $\sqrt2$ times a leg, so a leg is
\[
\dfrac{\sqrt[3]2}{\sqrt2}=\dfrac{2^{\dfrac{1}{3}}}{2^{\dfrac{1}{2}}}=2^{\dfrac{1}{3}-\dfrac{1}{2}}=2^{-\dfrac{1}{6}}.
\]
Hence $a=\boxed{-\dfrac{1}{6}}$.
