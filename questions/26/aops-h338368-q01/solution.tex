Brian must miss his first shot, which has probability $\dfrac{2}{5}$. If Alex then makes his first shot, the remaining shots are unrestricted. If Alex misses that shot, Brian must miss his second shot; Alex's last shot is again unrestricted. Thus the probability is
\[
\dfrac{2}{5}\left(\dfrac{4}{5}+\dfrac{1}{5}\cdot\dfrac{2}{5}\right)=\boxed{\dfrac{44}{125}}.
\]
