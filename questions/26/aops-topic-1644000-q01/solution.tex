From one March 16 to the next there are $365$ days, or $366$ if the year in between contains a February 29. Since $365=52\cdot7+1$ and $366=52\cdot7+2$, the weekday moves forward by $1$ in an ordinary step and by $2$ when a February 29 is crossed.

The February 29s after March 16, 2019 are in 2020 and 2024. So the shifts are:
\[
2019\to2020:\ 2,\quad 2020\to2021:\ 1,\quad 2021\to2022:\ 1,\quad 2022\to2023:\ 1,\quad 2023\to2024:\ 2 .
\]
The running totals are $2,3,4,5,7$, and the first multiple of $7$ is reached in $\boxed{2024}$. (March 16, 2019 was a Saturday, and March 16, 2024 was also a Saturday.)
