Substitution and AM-GM give $10c=7a+4b+\dfrac{(a+c)^2}{a}+\dfrac{(a+c)^2}{b}\ge7a+4(a+c)+\dfrac{(a+c)^2}{a}=12a+6c+\dfrac{c^2}{a}$. Hence $0\ge12a-4c+\dfrac{c^2}{a}=\dfrac{(c-2a)^2}{a}+8a>0$, a contradiction. Therefore there are no such positive numbers.
