Measure each tire\textquotesingle s wear as a fraction of its lifetime. The four tires together accumulate $2\cdot\dfrac{1}{16000}+2\cdot\dfrac{1}{24000}=\dfrac{1}{4800}$ wear units per kilometre. Their total available wear is 4, so the distance is at most $4\cdot4800=19200$ km. Rotate front and rear tires after 9600 km. Each then covers 9600 km in each position, consuming $\dfrac{9600}{16000}+\dfrac{9600}{24000}=1$ lifetime. Thus $\boxed{19200\text{ km}}$ is attainable.
