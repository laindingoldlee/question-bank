There are $4^4=256$ equally likely sequences of steps. To return, the numbers of up and down steps must be equal, say $k$ each, and the numbers of left and right steps equal, $2-k$ each, for $k=0,1,2$. The number of such sequences is
\[
\dfrac{4!}{0!\,0!\,2!\,2!}+\dfrac{4!}{1!\,1!\,1!\,1!}+\dfrac{4!}{2!\,2!\,0!\,0!}=6+24+6=36 .
\]
The probability is $\dfrac{36}{256}=\boxed{\dfrac{9}{64}}$.
