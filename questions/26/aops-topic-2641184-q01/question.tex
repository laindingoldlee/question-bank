Let $ABC$ be a triangle with $\angle BAC=90^\circ$. Let $\omega$ be the circle with centre $B$ through $A$, and let line $BC$ meet $\omega$ at $X$ and $Y$, where $X$ lies between $B$ and $C$. By showing that triangles $CAX$ and $CYA$ are similar, prove that $CA^2=CX\cdot CY$, and deduce the Pythagorean theorem $BC^2=AB^2+AC^2$.
