The sum $(a-b)+(b-a)=a+(-b)+b+(-a)=0$ by associativity, commutativity, and additive inverses. Thus $a-b$ is the additive inverse of $b-a$, proving $a-b=-(b-a)$. Also $-0=0$, since $0+0=0$, so $a-0=a+0=a$.
