First give $2$ rocks to the leader and $1$ to the sub-leader. The remaining $5$ rocks can be given to the $7$ tribesmen in any way. By stars and bars ($5$ stars and $6$ bars) this can be done in
\[\dbinom{5+6}{6}=\dbinom{11}{6}=\boxed{462}\]
ways.
