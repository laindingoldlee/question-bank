Yes, wherever $\tan\theta$ is defined: factor out $\tan^2\theta$ to get $\tan^2\theta\left(\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta\right)=\tan^2\theta\cdot1$. (This is just the identity $\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1$ multiplied by $\tan^2\theta$; it says nothing more about $\tan\theta$.)
