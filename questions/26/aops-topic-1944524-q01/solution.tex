Let there be $g$ guests. Since nobody drank both, the guests who drank something make up $\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{3}=\dfrac{5}{6}$ of the guests, so the $15$ who drank neither are $\dfrac{1}{6}$ of them: $\dfrac {g}{6}=15$, and $g=\boxed{90}$. (Check: $45+30+15=90$.)
