Factor everything:
\[
\dfrac{(r-1)(r-4)}{(r-1)(r-7)}=\dfrac{(r-5)(r+3)}{(r-5)(r+4)} .
\]
The equation only makes sense for $r\ne1,7,5,-4$, and for those $r$ it simplifies to
\[
\dfrac{r-4}{r-7}=\dfrac{r+3}{r+4}.
\]
Cross-multiplying, $(r-4)(r+4)=(r+3)(r-7)$, so $r^2-16=r^2-4r-21$, which gives $4r=-5$ and $r=\boxed{-\dfrac{5}{4}}$. This value is allowed (it is not $1,7,5$ or $-4$).
