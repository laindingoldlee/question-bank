A move sends an even number $m$ to $\dfrac{3m}{2}$ and an odd number $m$ to $\dfrac {m}{2}+\dfrac{5}{2}=\dfrac{m+5}{2}$; both are integers, so every move produces an integer. The results after each move are:
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
n & \text{1st} & \text{2nd} & \text{3rd}\\\hline
1 & 3 & 4 & 6\\
2 & 3 & 4 & 6\\
3 & 4 & 6 & 9\\
4 & 6 & 9 & 7\\
5 & 5 & 5 & 5\\
6 & 9 & 7 & 6\\
7 & 6 & 9 & 7\\
8 & 12 & 18 & 27\\
9 & 7 & 6 & 9\\
10 & 15 & 10 & 15
\end{array}
\]
The greatest result, $27$, occurs only for
\[\boxed{n=8}.\]
