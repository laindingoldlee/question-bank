Karl picks an integer $n$ and performs ``moves'' on it. In each move, Karl divides the current number by $2$; if the result is an integer he multiplies it by $3$, and otherwise he adds $2.5$ to it. For which integer $n$ from $1$ to $10$, inclusive, is the result after $3$ moves the greatest?
