The hypotenuse is $\sqrt{2.4^2+7^2}=7.4$. Thus the circumradius is 3.7 and the inradius is $\dfrac{2.4+7-7.4}{2}=1$. Their difference is $\boxed{2.7}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

pair A=(0,0),B=(7,0),C=(0,2.4);draw(circle((3.5,1.2),3.7));draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(circle((1,1),1),blue);label("7",(3.5,0),S);label("2.4",(0,1.2),W);
\end{asy}
