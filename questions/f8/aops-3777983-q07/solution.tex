Product-to-sum gives $5 \cos (\dfrac{\pi}{6}) - (\dfrac{1}{2}) \sum_{j=0}^9 \cos (\dfrac{\pi}{4} + \dfrac{j \pi}{6})$. The cosine progression sums to $\dfrac{\sin (\dfrac{5 \pi}{6})}{\sin (\dfrac{\pi}{12})} \cos \pi = - \dfrac{1}{2 \sin (\dfrac{\pi}{12})}$. Consequently the answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{5 \sqrt{3}}{2} + \dfrac{\sqrt6+\sqrt2}{4}}$.
