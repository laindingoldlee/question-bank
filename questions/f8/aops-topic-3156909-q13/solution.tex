Ignore the green marble in the random ordering. The four non-green marbles remain in uniformly random order, and one goes to each person in order; green merely adds an extra draw without consuming a person's non-green allocation. Therefore white has equal chance to belong to any of four people, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{4}}$.
