The equation is $|3z+1|=6$, or $|z+\dfrac{1}{3}|=2$. Thus the solution set is the circle centered at $-\dfrac{1}{3}$ with radius $2$ in the complex plane: $\boxed{z=-\dfrac{1}{3}+2(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta),\ 0\le\theta<2\pi}$. Its two real points are only a small subset of the solutions.

usepackage("amsmath");

\end{asy}
