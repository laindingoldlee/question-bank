Direct proportion means $\text{shadow}=k\cdot\text{height}$ for a constant $k$. From the lamppost, $k=\dfrac{2.0}{5.0}=0.4$. For the woman,
\[
\text{shadow}=0.4\times1.6=\boxed{0.64\text{ m}}.
\]
