Each consecutive group joined only by multiplication or division evaluates to $1$. If there are $p$ plus signs and $m$ minus signs, the whole expression is $1+p-m$, so we need $p-m=1$. With six slots, the possibilities are $(p,m)=(1,0),(2,1),(3,2)$. For each, choose the plus/minus positions and fill each remaining slot independently with multiplication or division. The count is $\dfrac{6!}{1!0!5!}2^5+\dfrac{6!}{2!1!3!}2^3+\dfrac{6!}{3!2!1!}2=192+480+120=\boxed{792}$.
