Jack starts $3+7=10$ seconds after the bucket. In those $10$ seconds the bucket gets a head start equal to the distance covered in $10$ seconds at $20$ miles per hour. Jack gains on the bucket at $30-20=10$ miles per hour, half the bucket's speed, so closing the gap takes twice as long as the bucket took to open it:
\[t=\dfrac{20\cdot10}{30-20}=\boxed{20}\text{ seconds}.\]
(Check: after $20$ seconds Jack has run for $20$ seconds at $30$ mph and the bucket for $30$ seconds at $20$ mph, the same distance.) Jill's speed does not matter.
