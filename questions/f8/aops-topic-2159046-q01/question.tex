Jack and Jill are at the top of a hill. Jill drops a bucket, which rolls down the hill at $20$ miles per hour. Three seconds later, Jill slides down the hill at $23$ miles per hour. Seven seconds after Jill starts, Jack runs down the hill at $30$ miles per hour. All three start from the same point, move along the same path at constant speeds, and the hill is long enough. How many seconds after Jack starts does he catch up with the bucket?
