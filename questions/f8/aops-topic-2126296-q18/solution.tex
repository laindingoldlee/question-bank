Modulo $3$ we have $2\equiv-1$ and $7\equiv1$, so
\[N\equiv(-1)^p+1^p=-1+1=0\pmod3,\]
because $p$ is odd. The remainder is $\boxed{0}$.

(In fact $a+b$ divides $a^p+b^p$ for every odd $p$, so $2^p+7^p$ is even divisible by $2+7=9$.)
