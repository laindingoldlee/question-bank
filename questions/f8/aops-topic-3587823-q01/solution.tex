$10!=2^8\cdot3^4\cdot5^2\cdot7$. There are $(8+1)(4+1)(2+1)(1+1)=270$ divisors. An odd divisor has exponent zero on $2$, giving $1\cdot5\cdot3\cdot2=30$ choices. The probability is $\dfrac{30}{270}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{9}}$.
