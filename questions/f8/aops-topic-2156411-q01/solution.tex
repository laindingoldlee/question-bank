Factor:
\[x^4-1=(x-1)(x+1)\left(x^2+1\right).\]
Since $x$ is odd, $x-1$ and $x+1$ are consecutive even numbers, so one of them is divisible by $4$ and the other by $2$; hence $8\mid(x-1)(x+1)$. Also $x^2$ is odd, so $x^2+1$ is even. Therefore $8\cdot2=16$ divides $x^4-1$. $\blacksquare$

(For example, $3^4-1=80=16\cdot5$ and $5^4-1=624=16\cdot39$.)
