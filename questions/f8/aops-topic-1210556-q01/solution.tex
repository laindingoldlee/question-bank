Powers of $2$ modulo $7$ cycle: $2^1\equiv2$, $2^2\equiv4$, $2^3\equiv1$, then repeat with period $3$. So $2^n\equiv1\pmod7$ exactly when $3\mid n$. The answer: $\boxed{n\text{ a multiple of }3}$.
