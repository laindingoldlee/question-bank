Put $Z=(0,h),I=(4,h),S=(-a,0)$. Equality $ZX=ZS$ and $X\ne S$ imply $X=(a,0)$ and hence $P=(3a,0)$. The angle-bisector condition at $Z$, by the tangent half-angle formula, gives $h^2=3a^2$. Thus $SZ$ makes $60^\circ$ with the base, and $SI$ makes $30^\circ$. Consequently $\dfrac{h}{4+a}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}$; with $h=\sqrt3a$, this gives $a=2,h=2\sqrt3$. Therefore $\dfrac{1}{4}[ZIP]=\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot4\cdot2\sqrt3=\boxed{\sqrt3}$.

\end{asy}
