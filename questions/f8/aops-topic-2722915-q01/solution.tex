The perimeter consists of the arc, of length $\pi$, and the diameter, of length $2$. On the arc, $OP=1$. On the diameter, $P$ is uniform on a segment of length $2$ centred at $O$, so $OP$ is uniform on $[0,1]$ with mean $\dfrac12$. Weighting by length,
\[E[OP]=\dfrac{\pi\cdot1+2\cdot\dfrac12}{\pi+2}=\boxed{\dfrac{\pi+1}{\pi+2}}\approx0.806.\]
