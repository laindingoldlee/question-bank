Let $ABCD$ be a square, and let $k$ be the circle with centre $B$ passing through $A$ and $C$. Let $T$ be a point of the arc $AC$ of $k$ lying inside the square, different from $A$ and $C$. The tangent to $k$ at $T$ meets the lines $CD$ and $DA$ at $E$ and $F$. Let $G$ and $H$ be the intersections of $BE$ and $BF$ with the line $AC$. Prove that the lines $BT$, $EH$ and $FG$ pass through one point.
