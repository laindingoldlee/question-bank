Maria paints a flat picture of a house: a $6\times4$ rectangle with a triangular roof of base $6$ and height $2$ on top of it. She paints the whole rectangle and half of the roof (the other half will be painted another colour). Each square unit needs one ounce of paint, and a tube holds $3$ ounces. How many tubes of paint does she need?
