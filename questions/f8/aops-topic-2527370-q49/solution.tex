The rectangle has area $6\cdot4=24$ and the roof has area $\dfrac12\cdot6\cdot2=6$, of which half, $3$, is painted. She needs $24+3=27$ ounces, i.e. $\dfrac{27}{3}=\boxed{9}$ tubes.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
fill((0,0)--(6,0)--(6,4)--(0,4)--cycle,mediumgray);
fill((0,4)--(3,4)--(3,6)--cycle,mediumgray);
draw((0,0)--(6,0)--(6,4)--(0,4)--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
draw((0,4)--(3,6)--(6,4),linewidth(0.8));draw((3,4)--(3,6),dashed);
label("$6$",(3,0),S);label("$4$",(0,2),W);label("$2$",(3,5),E,fontsize(9pt));
\end{asy}
