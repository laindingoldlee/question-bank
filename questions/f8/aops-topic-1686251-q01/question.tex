In triangle $ABC$ the angles $A$ and $B$ are complementary. $D$ is the foot of the altitude from $C$ to $AB$, and $AD=x$, $BD=y$. Equilateral triangles are drawn on $AD$ and on $BD$, outside triangle $ABC$. The area of the equilateral triangle with side $x$ equals the area of triangle $ADC$. Find the number $z$ such that $y=\dfrac{xz}{2}$.
