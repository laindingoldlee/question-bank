A shop starts with 50 mountain bikes and 30 road bikes. In week 1 it sells one-fifth of the mountain bikes and one-third of the road bikes. It then receives 15 mountain bikes and 10 road bikes. In week 2 it sells two-thirds of the newly arrived mountain bikes and half of all road bikes then in stock. How many bikes remain?
