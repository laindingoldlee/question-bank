After week 1, mountain and road counts are $50-10=40$ and $30-10=20$. After delivery they are 55 and 30. Week 2 sells $\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot 15=10$ mountain bikes and 15 road bikes. The remaining total is $(55-10)+(30-15)=\boxed{60}$.
