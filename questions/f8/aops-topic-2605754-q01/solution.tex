By Euler's formula $e^{i\theta}=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta$,
\[2021^{i}=e^{i\ln2021}=\cos\left(\ln2021\right)+i\sin\left(\ln2021\right).\]
Since $\ln2021\approx7.6113$ (radians),
\[\boxed{2021^{i}=\cos(\ln2021)+i\sin(\ln2021)\approx0.240+0.971i}.\]
It lies on the unit circle: $\left|2021^{i}\right|=1$.

(Remark: the complex logarithm of $2021$ is $\ln2021+2\pi ki$ for any integer $k$, so the other values of $2021^{i}$ are $e^{-2\pi k}\left(\cos(\ln2021)+i\sin(\ln2021)\right)$; the principal value is $k=0$.)
