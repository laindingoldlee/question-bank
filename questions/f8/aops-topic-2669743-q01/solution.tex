Repeatedly divide by $16$ and record the remainders, which are the digits from right to left:
\[256=16\cdot16+0,\qquad16=16\cdot1+0,\qquad1=16\cdot0+1.\]
So $c=0$, $b=0$, $a=1$, and $256=1\cdot16^2+0\cdot16+0=\boxed{100_{16}}$.
