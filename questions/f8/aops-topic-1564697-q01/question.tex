Show that $\cot x$ can be written without a fraction in terms of the tangent: prove that
\[
\cot x=\tan\left(90^\circ-x\right)
\]
for every angle $x$ for which $\cot x$ is defined (that is, $x$ not a multiple of $180^\circ$), first for $0^\circ<x<90^\circ$ using a right triangle, and then in general.
