A student argues: ``$1-2=-1$, so $(1-2)^2=(-1)^2=1$ and $\sqrt{(1-2)^2}=1$. Since squaring and then taking the square root gives back the original number, $\sqrt{(1-2)^2}=1-2$, so $1-2=1$, i.e.\ $-1=1$. In the same way, for any positive $x$, $-x=x$.'' Find the error, and prove that no positive number equals its negation.
