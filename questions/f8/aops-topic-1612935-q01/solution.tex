Split $\dfrac{400}{x}$ into two equal parts and apply the AM-GM inequality to the three positive numbers $2\pi x^2$, $\dfrac{200}{x}$, $\dfrac{200}{x}$:
\[
2\pi x^2+\dfrac{200}{x}+\dfrac{200}{x}\ \ge\ 3\sqrt[3]{2\pi x^2\cdot\dfrac{200}{x}\cdot\dfrac{200}{x}}=3\sqrt[3]{80000\pi}=3\cdot20\sqrt[3]{10\pi}=60\sqrt[3]{10\pi}.
\]
The product under the cube root does not depend on $x$, which is why this split works. Equality holds when the three numbers are equal, $2\pi x^2=\dfrac{200}{x}$, i.e. $x^3=\dfrac{100}{\pi}$, which has a positive solution. Hence the minimum value is
\[
\boxed{60\sqrt[3]{10\pi}}\approx 189.3 .
\]
(This is the least surface area $2\pi r^2+2\pi rh$ of a closed cylinder of volume $\pi r^2h=200$.)
