$721$ is not divisible by $2$, $3$ (digit sum $10$) or $5$, but $721=7\cdot103$. The number $103$ is prime: it is not divisible by $2$, $3$, $5$ or $7$, and $11^2=121>103$. So
\[721=\boxed{7\cdot103}.\]
