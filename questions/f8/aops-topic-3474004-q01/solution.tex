Treat Carl and Denise as a block, with two internal orders. There are $2\cdot6!$ circular arrangements of the resulting seven objects. Subtract those where Andrew and Benjamin also form a block: $2\cdot2\cdot5!$. The answer is $1440-480=\boxed{960}$.
