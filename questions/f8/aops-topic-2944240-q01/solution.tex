(a) \textbf{Yes.} $88\equiv1\pmod3$ and $88\equiv1\pmod{29}$ ($88=3\cdot29+1$), so both divide $88^{88}-1$. Modulo $23$, $22\mid88$, so by Fermat $88^{88}=(88^{22})^4\equiv1$. The three primes are distinct, so their product divides.

(b) \textbf{No.} $98\equiv1\pmod{97}$ is fine, but modulo $23$, $98\equiv6$ and $98\equiv10\pmod{22}$, so $98^{98}\equiv6^{10}$. Now $6^2\equiv13$, $6^4\equiv8$, $6^8\equiv18$ and $6^{10}\equiv18\cdot13=234\equiv4\not\equiv1\pmod{23}$. So $23\nmid98^{98}-1$.
