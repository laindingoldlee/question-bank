Put $u=x-2,v=y-2$. The equations become $|u^2-v^2|=4$ and $|u^2+v^2-8|=4$. The latter gives $u^2+v^2=4$ or 12. For 4, the squared-coordinate pairs are $(4,0),(0,4)$, producing four signed solutions. For 12, they are $(8,4),(4,8)$, producing eight signed solutions. In each group, sign symmetry makes the sums of $u$ and $v$ zero. There are 12 solutions, each contributing the constant 4 to $x+y=u+v+4$ after summation. The total is $\boxed{48}$.
