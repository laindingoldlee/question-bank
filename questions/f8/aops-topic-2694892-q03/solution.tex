The wire is a long cylinder of radius $r=0.1125$ cm and length $600\text{ m}=60000$ cm (not to scale below).

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real L=6,r=0.35;
draw(ellipse((0,0),0.12,r));draw((0,r)--(L,r));draw((0,-r)--(L,-r));
draw(shift((L,0))*scale(0.12,r)*arc((0,0),1,-90,90));draw(shift((L,0))*scale(0.12,r)*arc((0,0),1,90,270),dashed);
draw((-0.45,-r)--(-0.45,r),Arrows(4));label("$0.225$ cm",(-0.45,0),W,fontsize(8pt));
draw((0,-r-0.3)--(L,-r-0.3),Arrows(4));label("$600$ m",(L/2,-r-0.3),S,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}

Its volume is
\[V=\pi r^2\ell=\pi(0.1125)^2(60000)=759.375\pi\approx2385.6\text{ cm}^3,\]
so its mass is
\[8.92\times759.375\pi=6773.625\pi\approx21280\text{ g}\approx\boxed{21.3\text{ kg}}.\]
