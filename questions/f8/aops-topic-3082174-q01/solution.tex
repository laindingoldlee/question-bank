The arcs $ AP $ and $ PC $ have equal angular measure. Their chords therefore have equal length, since a chord subtending angle $\theta $ has length $ 2R\sin(\dfrac{\theta}{2})$. Thus $ PA=PC $.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=dir(20),C=dir(140),P=dir(80);draw(unitcircle);draw(A--P--C);label("$A$",A,E);label("$C$",C,NW);label("$P$",P,N);label("$O$",(0,0));
\end{asy}
