Since $12^2=144$ and $21^2=441$, reversing the digits of $12$ reverses the digits of its square; the same happens for $13$ ($13^2=169$, $31^2=961$). Find all two-digit numbers $n$ whose units digit is not $0$ such that the square of the reversal of $n$ is the reversal of $n^2$, and explain why these numbers work.
