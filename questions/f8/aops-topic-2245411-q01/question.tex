In how many ways can a $2\times5$ rectangle be covered, without overlaps, by $1\times2$ dominoes and $1\times1$ squares? (Dominoes may be placed horizontally or vertically; the rectangle's cells are fixed, so tilings that differ by a rotation or reflection of the whole rectangle count as different.)
