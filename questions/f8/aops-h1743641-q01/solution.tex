Let $t=f(\dfrac{12}{13})$. By symmetry $f(\dfrac{1}{13})=2018-t$. Scaling gives $f(\dfrac{4}{13})=\dfrac{t}{2}$, and symmetry then gives $f(\dfrac{9}{13})=2018-\dfrac{t}{2}$. Two more scaling steps yield
\[f\left(\dfrac{1}{13}\right)=\dfrac{1}{4} f\left(\dfrac{9}{13}\right)=\dfrac{2018}{4}-\dfrac{t}{8}.\]
Thus $2018-t=\dfrac{2018}{4}-\dfrac{t}{8}$, or $7t=6(2018)$. The answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{12108}{7}}$.

Nondecreasing is the consistent interpretation of monotonicity here: the equations force $f(\dfrac{1}{3})=f(\dfrac{2}{3})=1009$, so strict increase would be impossible.
