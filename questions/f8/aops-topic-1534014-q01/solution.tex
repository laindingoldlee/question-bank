Choosing six different integers from $1$ to $7$ is the same as choosing the one integer $k$ that is left out, and the $7$ choices of $k$ are equally likely. The six numbers used sum to
\[
(1+2+\cdots+7)-k=28-k ,
\]
which equals $24$ exactly when $k=4$. So the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{7}}$.
