A student claims that
\[\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n-\sum_{n=3}^{\infty}n-1=2.\]
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] For each integer $N\ge3$, compute $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{N}n-\sum_{n=3}^{N}n-1$.
\item[(b)] For each integer $N\ge3$, compute $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{2N}n-\sum_{n=3}^{N}n-1$, and describe what happens as $N\to\infty$.
\item[(c)] Explain why the left side of the claim has no value.
\end{enumerate}
