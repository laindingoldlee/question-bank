Choose uniformly among reduced fractions $\dfrac{m}{n}$ with positive integers $m,n$, $m+n=187$, and $n\ne1$. If the probability of selecting $\dfrac{180}{7}$ is $\dfrac{p}{q}$ in lowest terms, find $p+q$.
