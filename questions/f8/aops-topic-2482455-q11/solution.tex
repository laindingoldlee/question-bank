Since $\gcd(m,187-m)=\gcd(m,187)$, there are $\phi(187)=\phi(11\cdot17)=10\cdot16=160$ coprime choices with $1\le m\le186$. Excluding $n=1$ removes one, leaving 159. The specified fraction is reduced, so its probability is $\dfrac{1}{159}$ and $\boxed{p+q=160}$.
