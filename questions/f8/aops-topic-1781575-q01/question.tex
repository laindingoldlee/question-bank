There are $140$ houses in Protik's village, numbered $1,2,3,\dots,140$. On the first day Protik took chocolates at the houses whose numbers are divisible by $4$. The next day he ate noodles at the houses whose numbers are divisible by $10$. Then he went to all the houses he had not yet visited and had ice cream. At how many houses did he have ice cream?
