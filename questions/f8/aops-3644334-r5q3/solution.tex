Treat the five radicals as lengths of successive vectors with horizontal increments $a-42,b-a,c-b,d-c,154-d$ and vertical increments $1,2,3,4,5$. Their sum vector is $(112,15)$. The triangle inequality gives total length at least $\sqrt{112^2+15^2}=113$. Equality holds when all vectors point in the same direction, with horizontal increments $\dfrac{112}{15}$ times their vertical increments; these determine real a,b,c,d. Thus the minimum is $\boxed{113}$.
