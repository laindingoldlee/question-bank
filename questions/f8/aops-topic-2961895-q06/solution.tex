Let $a_n$ be the number of length-$n$ strings with no two consecutive heads: $a_1=2$, $a_2=3$, $a_n=a_{n-1}+a_{n-2}$ (the string ends in T, or in TH), so $a_3=5$, $a_4=8$. The probability is $1-\dfrac8{16}=\dfrac12$. \textbf{(C)}
