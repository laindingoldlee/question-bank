Triangles $ADE$ and $ABC$ are similar. Their area ratio is the square of their side ratio, so $\dfrac{DE}{BC}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt5}$. Consequently $DE=\dfrac{15}{\sqrt5}=\boxed{3\sqrt5}$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair B=(0,0),C=(15,0),A=(6.6,sqrt(169-6.6^2)),D=A+(B-A)/sqrt(5),E=A+(C-A)/sqrt(5);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(D--E,blue);label("A",A,N);label("B",B,SW);label("C",C,SE);label("D",D,W);label("E",E,(1,0));label("15",(B+C)/2,S);
\end{asy}
