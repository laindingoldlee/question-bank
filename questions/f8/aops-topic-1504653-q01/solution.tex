Number the days of 2003 from $1$ (January 1st) to $365$ (2003 is not a leap year). John rides on days $1,6,11,\dots$, that is, on the days $d$ with $d\equiv1\pmod5$. Jane rides on the days with $d\equiv1\pmod6$.

Both ride exactly when $d-1$ is a multiple of both $5$ and $6$, that is, a multiple of $\operatorname{lcm}(5,6)=30$. So they both ride on days
\[
1,\ 31,\ 61,\ \dots,\ 361 ,
\]
and there are $\dfrac{361-1}{30}+1=\boxed{13}$ such days.
