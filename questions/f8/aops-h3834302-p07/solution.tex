A cell with $d$ neighbors is smallest among itself and those neighbors with probability $\dfrac{1}{d+1}$, by symmetry. There are four corners with two neighbors, $2(8-2)+2(10-2)=28$ noncorner boundary cells with three, and $(8-2)(10-2)=48$ interior cells with four. Linearity of expectation gives $\dfrac{4}{3}+\dfrac{28}{4}+\dfrac{48}{5}=\dfrac{269}{15}$, so $\boxed{a+b=284}$.
