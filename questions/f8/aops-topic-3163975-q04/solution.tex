The total size is $18432$. All sizes are multiples of $256$, so divide by $256$, obtaining $3,4,9,10,16,30$, of total $72$. A partition into two emails of capacity below $37$ scaled units would have to split them exactly into $36$ and $36$. A subset summing to $36$ cannot contain $30$, since the remaining $6$ cannot be formed. Without $30$, it must contain $16$ because the other four sum to $26$, but no subset of $3,4,9,10$ sums to $20$: with $10$, the remaining $10$ is unavailable, and without $10$ the total is $16$. Thus equal halves are impossible. A split $30+4+3=37$ versus $16+10+9=35$ is possible. The smallest capacity allowing two emails is therefore $37\cdot256=9472$, so the requested largest integer is $\boxed{9471}$.
