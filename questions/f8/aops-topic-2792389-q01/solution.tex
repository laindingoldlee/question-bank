Equal gender counts are impossible: both statements would be false, requiring all nine others to be of the opposite gender, giving counts 1 and 9. If men are more numerous, the first five listeners share Pat's gender and the other four do not. Pat's gender therefore numbers 6 and must be male. If women are more numerous, only the last four listeners share Pat's gender, giving 5 of each gender, a contradiction. Hence $\boxed{\text{Pat is male}}$, with 6 men and 4 women.
