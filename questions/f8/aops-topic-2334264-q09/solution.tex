Count by Rai's roll $r$: Byan needs at least $2r$. For $r=1$ there are $5$ good rolls ($2$ to $6$), for $r=2$ there are $3$ ($4,5,6$), for $r=3$ there is $1$ ($6$), and for $r\ge4$ there are none. So $9$ of the $36$ outcomes are good, and the probability is $\dfrac9{36}=\boxed{\textbf{(B) }\dfrac14}$.
