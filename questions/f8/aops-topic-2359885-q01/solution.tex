Sort the numbers by remainder modulo $4$:
\[r=0:\ \{4,8,12\}\ (3),\quad r=1:\ \{1,5,9,13\}\ (4),\quad r=2:\ \{2,6,10\}\ (3),\quad r=3:\ \{3,7,11\}\ (3).\]
The multisets of three remainders with sum divisible by $4$ are $\{0,0,0\}$, $\{0,1,3\}$, $\{0,2,2\}$, $\{1,1,2\}$, $\{2,3,3\}$, giving
\[\dbinom33+3\cdot4\cdot3+3\dbinom32+\dbinom42\cdot3+3\dbinom32=1+36+9+18+9=73\]
choices. There are $\dbinom{13}{3}=286$ choices in all, so the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{73}{286}}$.
