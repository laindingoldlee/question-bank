The mean is $\dfrac{16+x}{5}$. The median depends on where $x$ falls among $1,1,4,10$:
\begin{itemize}
\item if $x\le1$, the sorted list is $x,1,1,4,10$ and the median is $1$: $\dfrac{16+x}{5}=1$ gives $x=-11$, which is indeed $\le1$;
\item if $1\le x\le4$, the median is $x$: $16+x=5x$ gives $x=4$;
\item if $x\ge4$, the median is $4$: $16+x=20$ gives $x=4$.
\end{itemize}
So $x=-11$ or $x=4$, and the smallest possible value is $\boxed{-11}$. (Check: $-11,1,1,4,10$ has sum $5$, mean $1$, median $1$.)
