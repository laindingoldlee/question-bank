Each octal digit corresponds to three binary digits. The first octal digit is uniformly one of 1 through 7. Binary length 30 occurs precisely for leading octal digits 4, 5, 6, 7, whose binary length is 3. Thus the probability is $\dfrac{4}{7}$ and $\boxed{a+b=11}$.
