There are $\dbinom73=35$ triples. A triple fails exactly when its points are collinear. The collinear triples are the three sides with their points ($A,Z,B$; $B,X,C$; $C,Y,A$) and the three cevians ($A,P,X$; $B,P,Y$; $C,P,Z$). No other triple is collinear. So $35-6=\boxed{29}$ triangles.
