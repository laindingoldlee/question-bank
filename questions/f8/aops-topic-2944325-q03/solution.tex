The equal base angles make the trapezoid isosceles, with legs $\ell=QR=SP$ inclined at $45^\circ$. So $SR=PQ+2\ell\cos45^\circ=1+\ell\sqrt2$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real L=2+sqrt(2);pair P=(0,0),Q=(1,0),R=Q+L*dir(-45),S=P+L*dir(-135);draw(P--Q--R--S--cycle);
real h=L/sqrt(2);draw(circle((0.5,-h/2),h/2),dashed);label("$P$",P,NW);label("$Q$",Q,NE);label("$R$",R,SE);label("$S$",S,SW);label("$1$",(0.5,0),N);
\end{asy}

A tangential quadrilateral has equal sums of opposite sides: $PQ+SR=2\ell$, i.e. $2+\ell\sqrt2=2\ell$. So $\ell=\dfrac2{2-\sqrt2}=\boxed{2+\sqrt2}$.
