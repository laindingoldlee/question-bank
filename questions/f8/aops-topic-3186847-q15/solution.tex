A monomial has form $x^ay^bz^c$ with $0\le a,b,c\le 20$ and $a+b+c\ge 20$. Conversely every such triple can be realized by 20 nonempty subsets of $\{x,y,z\}$: put a copies of x, b of y, c of z in 20 slots, never repeating a variable within a slot, and fill empty slots by moving a variable from a slot of size at least two; this is possible while empty slots remain because total copies are at least 20. All coefficients are positive. Thus count $21^3$ triples and subtract those with $a+b+c\le 19$, of which stars and bars gives $\binom{22}{3}=1540$. The answer is $9261-1540=\boxed{7721}$.
