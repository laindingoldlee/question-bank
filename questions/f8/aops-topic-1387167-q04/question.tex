Sarah has four decks of $15$ cards each: one red, one blue, one yellow and one green, and each deck's cards are numbered $1$ through $15$. She combines all four decks into one large deck. Selecting at random and without replacement, what is the probability that Sarah first chooses a red card with a prime number and then chooses a card with a non-prime number on her second selection? Express your answer as a common fraction.
