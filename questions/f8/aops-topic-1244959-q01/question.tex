Let $a,b,c,d,e,f$ be nonzero real numbers with $a,c,e$ distinct and $b,d,f$ distinct. Suppose the lines $y=ax+b$, $y=cx+d$, $y=ex+f$ pass through a common point. Prove or disprove: the lines $y=bx+a$, $y=dx+c$, $y=fx+e$ also pass through a common point.
