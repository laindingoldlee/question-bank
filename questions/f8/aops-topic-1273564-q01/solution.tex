By the order of operations only the second term is divided by $5$: $5^2+\dfrac{5^2}{5}=25+5=\boxed{30}$. The error is rewriting $5^2$ as $\dfrac{5^2}{5}$; that changes the first term from $25$ to $5$. (Only if the whole sum were divided, $\dfrac{5^2+5^2}{5}=10$.)
