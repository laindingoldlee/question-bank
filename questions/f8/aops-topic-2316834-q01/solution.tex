\textbf{(a)} $\dfrac{\sqrt4}{\sqrt{6x}}=\dfrac{2}{\sqrt{6x}}=\dfrac{2\sqrt{6x}}{6x}=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt{6x}}{3x}}$.

\textbf{(b)} $\dfrac{\sqrt{4x}}{\sqrt6}=\dfrac{2\sqrt x\cdot\sqrt6}{6}=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt{6x}}{3}}$.

\textbf{(c)} $\dfrac{\sqrt{6x}}{\sqrt4}=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt{6x}}{2}}$.
