The sequence $n^n$ is strictly increasing, so a solution would require $b\ge a+1$. For $a=1$, $b^b\ge2^2=4>2$. For $a\ge2$, $(a+1)^{a+1}>(a+1)a^a\ge3a^a>a^a+1$. Thus even the next term exceeds $a^a+1$, ruling out every $b$.
