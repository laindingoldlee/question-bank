The smallest possible sum is $1+2+3=6$ and the largest is $5+6+7=18$. Every integer in between occurs: starting from $\{1,2,3\}$, increase one element by $1$ at a time while keeping the three numbers different,
\[
\{1,2,3\},\{1,2,4\},\{1,2,5\},\{1,2,6\},\{1,2,7\},\{1,3,7\},\{1,4,7\},\{1,5,7\},\{1,6,7\},\{2,6,7\},\{3,6,7\},\{4,6,7\},\{5,6,7\},
\]
with sums $6,7,\dots,18$. So there are $18-6+1=\boxed{13}$ different sums.
