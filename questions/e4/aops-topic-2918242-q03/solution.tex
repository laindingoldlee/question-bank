$\angle EID=\angle BIC$ (vertical angles), and $\angle BIC=180^\circ-\dfrac B2-\dfrac C2=90^\circ+\dfrac A2$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0),C=(5,0),A=(1.5,3.5);pair I=(length(C-B)*A+length(C-A)*B+length(B-A)*C)/(length(C-B)+length(C-A)+length(B-A));
pair D=extension(B,I,A,C),E=extension(C,I,A,B);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(B--D);draw(C--E);dot(I);
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,NE);label("$E$",E,W);label("$I$",I,S);
\end{asy}

So $90^\circ+\dfrac A2=110^\circ$ and $\angle A=\boxed{40^\circ}$.
