Yes. For any positive integer $n$, choose $k$ so large that $2^k(\log_2(n+1)-\log_2 n)>1$. There is then an integer $m$ in the half-open interval $[2^k\log_2 n,2^k\log_2(n+1))$. Multiplying $m$ copies of 2 gives $2^m$; applying square root $k$ times gives $2^{\dfrac{m}{2^k}}\in[n,n+1)$. Its floor is $n$. The case $n=1$ is also simply $\lfloor\sqrt2\rfloor$. No concatenation or additional digit is needed.
