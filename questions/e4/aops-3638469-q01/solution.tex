First order the five non-aloe plants. According to whether the sunflower has zero, one or two rose neighbours, the 120 orders divide into $36,72,12$. To check, both-rose-neighbour orders number $3\cdot2!\cdot2!=12$; zero-rose-neighbour orders number 24 with sunflower at an end and 12 with it interior. Insert the three aloes into distinct gaps among the six gaps. Every gap separating a sunflower from an adjacent rose must be filled. Thus choices of gaps are respectively $\binom63,\binom52,\binom41$. Order the three distinct aloes in $3!$ ways. The result is $6(36\cdot20+72\cdot10+12\cdot4)=\boxed{8928}$.
