There are real numbers $a,b,c,d$ with $a\ne0$ and $c>0$ such that for all real $(x,y)$ satisfying $6y^2=2x^3+3x^2+x$, the numbers
\[
x_1=ax+b,\qquad y_1=cy+d
\]
satisfy $y_1^2=x_1^3-36x_1$. Find $a+b+c+d$.
