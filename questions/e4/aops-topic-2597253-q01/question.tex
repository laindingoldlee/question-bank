Is $i$ positive or negative? Show that neither choice makes sense: prove that there is no set $P$ of complex numbers (``the positive numbers'') such that
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] for every complex number $z$, exactly one of $z=0$, $z\in P$, $-z\in P$ holds; and
\item[(ii)] if $z,w\in P$, then $z+w\in P$ and $zw\in P$.
\end{enumerate}
