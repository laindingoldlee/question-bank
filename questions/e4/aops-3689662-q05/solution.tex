The first hypotenuse has squared length $2$. At each step Pythagoras adds $1$ to the previous square. Induction gives the $n$th hypotenuse $\boxed{\sqrt{n+1}}$. The triangles form the spiral of Theodorus when their unit legs are consistently added outward.
\begin{asy}
import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
pair O=(0,0),P=(1,0);draw(O--P);label("$O$",O,(-1,-1));for(int k=1;k<=7;++k){pair Q=P+rotate(90)*P/abs(P);draw(P--Q--O);label("$1$",(P+Q)/2,unit(P+Q));P=Q;}label("$\sqrt8$",P/2,(-1,0));
\end{asy}
