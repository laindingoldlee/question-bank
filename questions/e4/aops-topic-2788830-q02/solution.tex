Giving everyone one toy accounts for 30 toys, leaving 10 extra. The five distinct positive donation amounts are at least 1,2,3,4,5. Since each grade has a student, their required extras already total at least $0+1+2+3+4=10$. Equality forces those exact amounts and exactly one student in each grade donating 2,3,4,5. The remaining $\boxed{26}$ students donate one each.
