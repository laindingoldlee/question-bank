The lateral surface of a right circular cone with base radius $48$ feet and height $14$ feet is cut along a straight line from the apex to the edge of the base and unrolled flat. The result is a circular disc with a sector missing. What is the central angle of the missing sector, in degrees? Express your answer as a decimal to the nearest tenth.
