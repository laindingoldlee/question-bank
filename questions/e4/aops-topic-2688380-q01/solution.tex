\textbf{(a) No.} There are $5!=120$ ways to place the beads in $5$ labelled positions around the circle, but each necklace is counted $5$ times for rotations and $2$ times for turning over, and no arrangement of $5$ distinct beads is fixed by a nontrivial rotation or reflection. So there are $\dfrac{120}{10}=\boxed{12}$ necklaces.

\textbf{(b) Usually.} If $0$ is not among the digits, every ordering is a five-digit number: $\boxed{120}$. If $0$ is one of them, the orderings starting with $0$ (there are $4!=24$) are not five-digit numbers, leaving $120-24=\boxed{96}$.

\textbf{(c) Yes.} A finishing order is an ordering of the $5$ cars: $\boxed{120}$.
