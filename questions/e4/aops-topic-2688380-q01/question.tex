For each situation below, decide whether the answer is $5!=120$; if not, give the correct number.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] The number of different necklaces made from $5$ beads of $5$ different colours (a necklace may be rotated and turned over).
\item[(b)] The number of five-digit numbers that use each of $5$ given different digits exactly once (treat separately the cases where $0$ is or is not one of the given digits).
\item[(c)] The number of possible finishing orders in a race between $5$ cars of different colours (no ties).
\end{enumerate}
