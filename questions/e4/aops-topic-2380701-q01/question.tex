In the song ``The Twelve Days of Christmas'', on the $k$th day the singer receives $k$ gifts of a new kind together with all the gifts of the earlier days again: $1$ gift on the first day, $1+2$ gifts on the second day, $1+2+3$ on the third, and so on up to $1+2+\cdots+12$ on the twelfth day. How many gifts are received in total over the twelve days?
