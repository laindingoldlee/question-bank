Note $2009=7^2\cdot41$. From $\sqrt b=\sqrt{2009}-\sqrt a$, squaring gives $b=2009+a-2\sqrt{2009a}$. So $\sqrt{2009a}=7\sqrt{41a}$ is rational, hence $41a$ is a perfect square, and $a=41m^2$. Similarly $b=41n^2$. Then $m\sqrt{41}+n\sqrt{41}=7\sqrt{41}$, so $m+n=7$ with $m,n\ge0$. The solutions are
\[\boxed{(a,b)=\bigl(41m^2,\ 41(7-m)^2\bigr),\ m=0,1,\dots,7},\]
eight pairs in all, e.g. $(0,2009),(41,1476),(164,1025),(369,656),\dots$
