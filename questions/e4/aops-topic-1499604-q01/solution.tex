With $n$ feet there are $2n$ actions: putting on the sock of foot $i$ and the shoe of foot $i$, for $i=1,\dots,n$. Without restrictions they can be done in $(2n)!$ orders.

For each foot, the two orders of its sock and shoe are matched in pairs by swapping those two actions, and exactly one order in each pair is allowed. The $n$ feet are independent, so exactly a fraction $\dfrac{1}{2^n}$ of all orders is allowed:
\[
\dfrac{(2n)!}{2^n}.
\]
\begin{itemize}
\item $2$ feet: $\dfrac{4!}{4}=\boxed{6}$.
\item $3$ feet: $\dfrac{6!}{8}=\boxed{90}$.
\item $4$ feet: $\dfrac{8!}{16}=\boxed{2520}$.
\end{itemize}
(For two feet with socks $s_1,s_2$ and shoes $h_1,h_2$, the six orders are $s_1s_2h_1h_2$, $s_1s_2h_2h_1$, $s_2s_1h_1h_2$, $s_2s_1h_2h_1$, $s_1h_1s_2h_2$, $s_2h_2s_1h_1$.)
