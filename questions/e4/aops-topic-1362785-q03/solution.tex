Write every factor as a power of ten:
\[
\dfrac{(6\times10^{-2})(3\times10^{-3})}{(2\times10^{3})\cdot3\cdot10^{2}}=\dfrac{18\times10^{-5}}{6\times10^{5}}=3\times10^{-10}.
\]
The result is $\boxed{3\times10^{-10}}$.
