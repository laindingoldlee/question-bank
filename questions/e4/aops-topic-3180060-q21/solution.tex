Write $n=2^a m$ with $m$ odd. The ratio of divisor sums is $\dfrac{2^{a+2}-1}{2^{a+1}-1}=2+\dfrac{1}{2^{a+1}-1}$. This is at least $\dfrac{11}{5}$ exactly when $2^{a+1}-1\le5$, namely $a=0$ or 1. Thus precisely the integers not divisible by 4 qualify, giving $100-25=\boxed{75}$.
