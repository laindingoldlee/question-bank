Let Dora and Maurice now be $D$ and $M$ years old, and let the earlier time be $t$ years ago. Then
\[D-t=\dfrac{M}{4}\qquad\text{and}\qquad D=\dfrac{M-t}{3}.\]
From the first equation $t=D-\dfrac{M}{4}$. Substituting into the second,
\[3D=M-D+\dfrac{M}{4}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 4D=\dfrac{5M}{4}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \dfrac{D}{M}=\dfrac{5}{16}.\]
So the ratio is $\boxed{5:16}$. The smallest whole-number example is $D=5$, $M=16$ with $t=1$: one year ago Dora was $4=\dfrac{16}{4}$, Maurice was $15$, and indeed $5=\dfrac{15}{3}$.
