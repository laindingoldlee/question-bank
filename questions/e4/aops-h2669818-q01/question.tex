Place $1,2,\ldots,9$ exactly once at the nine positions in an X-shaped array, denoted from top to bottom by rows $(a,b)$, $(c,d)$, $(e)$, $(f,g)$, $(h,i)$. Require
\[a+b=h+i=f+g=11,\quad c+d=4,\quad a+h=13,\quad c+f=5,\quad d+g=10,\quad b+i=9,\]
and $a+c+e+g+i=32$, $b+d+e+f+h=21$. Find the placement.
