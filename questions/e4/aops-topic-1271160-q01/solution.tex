For each integer $n\ge2$ take sides $n^2-1$, $2n$ and $n^2+1$. They are positive integers and
\[
(n^2-1)^2+(2n)^2=n^4+2n^2+1=(n^2+1)^2,
\]
so each triple forms a right triangle. The ratio $\dfrac{2n}{n^2+1}$ of a leg to the hypotenuse is strictly decreasing for $n\ge2$, so no two of these triangles are similar. Hence there are infinitely many. $\blacksquare$ (If similar triangles may count separately, the triangles $(3k,4k,5k)$ already give infinitely many.)
