Let $u$ be the units carry. Then $2c=a+10u$. Let $v$ be the tens carry, so $b+u=d+10v$, and hundreds give $a+v=b$. Because $a\ne b$, $v=1$, hence $b=a+1$. Since $b+u\ge10$, necessarily $b=9,u=1$, giving $a=8,d=0$. The units equation then gives $c=9=b$, contradicting distinctness. Thus $\boxed{\text{no}}$.
