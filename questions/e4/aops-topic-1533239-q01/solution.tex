Let the side be $s$. The altitude $AH$ bisects the base, so triangle $ABH$ is a $30$-$60$-$90$ triangle with hypotenuse $s$, short leg $\dfrac {s}{2}$ and long leg $12$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=8*sqrt(3);
pair B=(0,0),C=(s,0),A=(s/2,12),H=(s/2,0);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(1));
draw(A--H,dashed);
real z=0.8; draw(H+(z,0)--H+(z,z)--H+(0,z));
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$H$",H,S);
label("$12$",(A+H)/2,E);
label("$s$",(A+B)/2,NW);label("$\dfrac{s}{2}$",(B+H)/2,S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Hence $12=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}s$, so $s=\dfrac{24}{\sqrt3}=8\sqrt3$, and the area is
\[
\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot s\cdot12=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot8\sqrt3\cdot12=\boxed{48\sqrt3}.
\]
