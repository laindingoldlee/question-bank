Every positive integer has a unique form $du^2$ with $d$ squarefree. The product is a square exactly when both numbers have the same $d$. A class has $\left\lfloor\sqrt{\dfrac{50}{d}}\right\rfloor$ members. Classes with at least two members correspond to squarefree $d\le12$: $1,2,3,5,6,7,10,11$. Their sizes are $7,5,4,3,2,2,2,2$. Thus the count is $\binom72+\binom52+\binom42+\binom32+4\binom22=\boxed{44}$.
