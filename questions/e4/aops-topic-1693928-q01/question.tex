A circle $X$ touches a circle $Y$ internally at $P$. The centre $O$ of circle $Y$ lies outside circle $X$. A diameter $MN$ of circle $Y$ is tangent to circle $X$, and $PM>PN$. The segment $PM$ meets circle $X$ again at $Z$, and $MZ=2PZ$. Find $\angle PMN$.
