Let the common adjacent product be $c$. If $c\ne0$, every entry is nonzero. Moving along a path alternates between a value $t$ and $\dfrac{c}{t}$, so the connected bipartite grid has at most two distinct values.

If $c=0$, no two adjacent cells can both be nonzero. Partition the board into $512$ disjoint adjacent pairs; at most one cell in each pair is nonzero. Thus there are at most $512$ distinct nonzero values, plus zero, for a total at most $513$.

This is attained by writing $512$ distinct nonzero numbers on the black squares of a checkerboard colouring and zero on all white squares. Every adjacent product is then zero. The maximum is $\boxed{513}$.
