A soldier moves along a straight road at a constant speed $V_1$ and fires two shots $335$ seconds apart. A terrorist is running ahead of him along the same road, in the same direction, at a constant speed, and hears the two shots $330$ seconds apart. The speed of sound is $1188$ km per hour, and both men move slower than sound. Who is faster, and by how much? Give the difference of the speeds in km per hour in terms of $V_1$ (in km per hour).
