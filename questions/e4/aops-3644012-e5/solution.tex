Take the midplane parallel to the $2\times3$ faces. The equatorial rhombus has diagonals two and three, so area three. The octahedron consists of two pyramids over this rhombus whose heights sum to four. Its volume is $(\dfrac{1}{3})\cdot3\cdot4=\boxed4$.

import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);

\end{asy}
