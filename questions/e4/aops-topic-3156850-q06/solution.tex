Equating squared distances gives $(x-2)^2+(y+2)^2=(x+3)^2+(y-3)^2 $, which simplifies to $ y=x+1 $. Its intercepts are $-1 $ and 1, whose sum is $\boxed0 $.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw((-4,0)--(4,0),Arrow);draw((0,-3)--(0,5),Arrow);draw((-3,-2)--(3,4),blue);pair A=(2,-2),B=(-3,3);dot(A);dot(B);label("$(2,-2)$",A,SE);label("$(-3,3)$",B,NW);label("$y=x+1$",(2,3),NW);
\end{asy}
