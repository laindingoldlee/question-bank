In the \(xy\)-plane, \(O=(0,0)\), \(A=(0,1)\), \(B=(-1,0)\), \(C=(0,-1)\) and \(D=(1,0)\). For a positive real number \(t\), let \(T_1\) be triangle \(OAB\) translated \(t\) units to the right, and let \(T_2\) be triangle \(OCD\) translated \(2t\) units up. The common region of \(T_1\) and \(T_2\) is a hexagon exactly when \(\dfrac{1}{3}<t<a\). Let \(M\) be the maximum area of this hexagon. Find \(a+M\).
