For two dice, the absolute difference $d\in\{1,\ldots,5\}$ occurs in $2(6-d)$ of the $36$ ordered outcomes. Hence $E|a-b|=\dfrac{2}{36}\sum_{d=1}^5d(6-d)=\dfrac{35}{18}$. By linearity of expectation, the three identical pair contributions sum to $3\cdot\dfrac{35}{18}=\boxed{\dfrac{35}{6}}$.
