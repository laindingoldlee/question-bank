\textbf{The error.} The sequence of rows is \emph{not} arithmetic with difference $5$: consecutive rows differ by $2$ or $3$. Only every \emph{second} row increases by $5$. The student mixed the two: the terms $15,20,\dots,85$ are the odd-numbered rows $1,3,\dots,29$ (that is $15$ rows), but then all the even-numbered rows except the last were left out (and $87$ is the $30$th row's size, but the rows $2,4,\dots,28$ are missing).

\textbf{Correct count.} The rows are $15,17,20,22,25,27,\dots$
\begin{itemize}
\item Odd rows $1,3,\dots,29$: $15,20,\dots,85$, an arithmetic sequence of $15$ terms with sum $\dfrac{15(15+85)}{2}=750$.
\item Even rows $2,4,\dots,30$: $17,22,\dots,87$, also $15$ terms, with sum $\dfrac{15(17+87)}{2}=780$.
\end{itemize}
The auditorium has $750+780=\boxed{1530}$ seats.
