The total length is $\sqrt{8^2+(-6)^2}=10$. Each part therefore has length $\boxed 2$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(220);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(-2,3),C=(6,-3);draw(A--C);for(int i=0;i<=5;++i)dot(A+i*(C-A)/5);label("$A(-2,3)$",A,(0,1));label("$C(6,-3)$",C,(0,-1));
\end{asy}
