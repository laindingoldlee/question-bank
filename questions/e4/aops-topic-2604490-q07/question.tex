A line $\ell$ and two points $A$ and $B$ not on $\ell$ are given in a plane. Find the point $P$ on $\ell$ for which $AP+PB$ is as small as possible. Treat separately the cases where $A$ and $B$ are on opposite sides of $\ell$ and on the same side of $\ell$.
