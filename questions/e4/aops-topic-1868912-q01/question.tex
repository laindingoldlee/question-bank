Four collinear points $A$, $K$, $O$ and $Y$ satisfy the following conditions: $K$ is between $O$ and $A$; $AO+AY=OY$; and $A$ is to the right of $O$. In what order do the four points lie on the line, from left to right? Justify your answer.
