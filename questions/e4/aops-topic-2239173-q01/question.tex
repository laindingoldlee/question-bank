A positive integer $n$ satisfies $n\equiv4\pmod9$, $n\equiv1\pmod5$ and $n\equiv5\pmod8$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $n\equiv1\pmod3$.
\item[(b)] Prove that $n\equiv1\pmod{60}$.
\end{enumerate}
