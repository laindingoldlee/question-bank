Assume $a<b$, so $a<45$. Since $2025=3^4\cdot5^2$, its largest divisor below 45 is 27: the possibilities of form $3^i5^j$ with $j=0,1,2$ have largest values below 45 equal to 27,15,25. For $0<u<v<45$, the difference $(u+\dfrac{2025}{u})-(v+\dfrac{2025}{v})=(v-u)(\dfrac{2025}{uv}-1)$ is positive. Thus $a+\dfrac{2025}{a}$ decreases as $a$ increases below $45$. Hence the minimum occurs at $(a,b)=(27,75)$, and is $\boxed{102}$.
