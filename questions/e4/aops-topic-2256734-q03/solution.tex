The smallest palindrome greater than $2020$ is $2112$ (a four-digit palindrome $\overline{2bb2}$ exceeds $2020$ only for $b\ge1$). So he drove at least $2112-2020=92$ miles in $4$ hours, an average of at least $\boxed{\textbf{(C) }23}$ miles per hour.
