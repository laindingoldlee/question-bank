The addition identity gives $\tan\theta+\tan2\theta=\tan3\theta(1-\tan\theta\tan2\theta)$. Thus either $\tan3\theta=0$ or $\tan\theta\tan2\theta=2$. The first yields $\theta = \dfrac{k \pi}{3}$, all of which lie in the domain. In the second, setting $t=\tan\theta$ gives $\dfrac{2 t^2}{1-t^2} = 2$, so $t = \pm \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$, also in the domain. Therefore $\boxed{\theta = \dfrac{k \pi}{3} \text{ or } \theta = k \pi \pm \arctan (\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}) , \  k \in \mathbb{Z}}$.
