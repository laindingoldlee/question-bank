Use the complement: the sum is a one-digit number that is not composite. Sums are at least $2$, so the sum is one of $2,3,5,7$. For a sum $s\le19$ there are $s-1$ outcomes (the first die shows $1,\dots,s-1$), so the complement has
\[
1+2+4+6=13
\]
outcomes out of $20\cdot18=360$. The probability is $1-\dfrac{13}{360}=\boxed{\textbf{(c)}\ \dfrac{347}{360}}$.
