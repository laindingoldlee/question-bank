The composite digits are $4,6,8,9$; the digits $0$ and $1$ are neither prime nor composite, so the allowed digits are $0,1,2,3,5,7$.

A number below $200$ with allowed digits and at least three digits is $1\overline{tu}$ with $t,u\in\{0,1,2,3,5,7\}$. The largest candidates have $t=7$: $177$, $175$, $173$, \dots
\begin{itemize}
\item $177=3\cdot59$ and $175=5^2\cdot7$ are composite;
\item $173$ is prime: it is not divisible by $2,3,5,7,11$ or $13$, and $13^2=169<173<17^2$, so no other prime factor needs checking.
\end{itemize}
So the answer is $\boxed{173}$.
