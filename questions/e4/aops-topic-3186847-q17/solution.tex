The constraint gives $y^2=8x-2x^2\ge 0$, so $0\le x\le 4$. The target becomes $12x-x^2$. It is increasing on this interval because its derivative is $12-2x>0$, and reaches $48-16=\boxed{32}$ at $(x,y)=(4,0)$.
