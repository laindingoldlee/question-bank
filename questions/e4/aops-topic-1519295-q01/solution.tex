A number is a multiple of $2$, $3$ and $5$ exactly when it is a multiple of $\operatorname{lcm}(2,3,5)=30$. There are $\left\lfloor\dfrac{1000}{30}\right\rfloor=33$ such numbers from $1$ to $1000$.

Among these, the multiples of $8$ are the multiples of $\operatorname{lcm}(30,8)=120$; there are $\left\lfloor\dfrac{1000}{120}\right\rfloor=8$ of them.

So the answer is $33-8=\boxed{25}$.
