A car travels at 80 miles per hour except for stops from elapsed hour 4 to 4.5 and from hour 10 to 10.5. Its trip ends upon reaching 1000 miles. A display rounds elapsed time down to the nearest 20 seconds and distance down to the nearest tenth of a mile. Starting at elapsed hour 1, an observer checks every 7 minutes, omitting checks during stops, and divides displayed distance by displayed elapsed time. Find the minimum and maximum displayed average speeds.
