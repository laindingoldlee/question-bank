Every check occurs at $t_j=1+\dfrac{7j}{60}$ hours, an exact multiple of $20$ seconds, so the time display is exact. For $t<4$ the true average is $80$, and downward distance rounding cannot increase it; at $t=1$ it is exactly $80$, so the maximum is $\boxed{80}$. Between stops the true average $80(1-\dfrac{s}{t})$, with cumulative stop time $s$, increases. Displayed averages with a fixed stop total also increase here: the distance gained per check is $\dfrac{28}{3}$, so rounding errors change by at most $\dfrac{1}{10}$, smaller than the increase contribution $\dfrac{80s\Delta t}{t}$ near either minimum. The first check after the first stop is $j=30$, $t=4.5$, displayed distance $320$, average $\dfrac{640}{9}$. After the second stop the first check has $j=82$, $t=\dfrac{317}{30}$, displayed distance $765.3$, average $\dfrac{22959}{317}>72$. Hence the minimum is $\boxed{\dfrac{640}{9}}$ miles per hour.
