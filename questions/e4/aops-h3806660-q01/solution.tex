Let the legs be $a,b$ and hypotenuse $c$. The area gives $ab=300$, and the perimeter gives $a+b=60-c$. By Pythagoras,
\[(60-c)^2=(a+b)^2=c^2+2ab=c^2+600.\]
Thus $3600-120c=600$, so $\boxed{c=25}$. The legs are $15,20$, which verify the stated data.
