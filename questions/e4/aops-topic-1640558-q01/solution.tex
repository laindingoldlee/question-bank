Look at the quantity
\[
Q=(\text{sum of the numbers on the board})-(\text{number of numbers on the board}).
\]
One operation lowers the sum by $1$ (since $a+b$ is replaced by $a+b-1$) and lowers the count by $1$, so $Q$ does not change. Initially
\[
Q=(1+2+\cdots+40)-40=820-40=780 .
\]
At the end there is one number $x$, so $Q=x-1=780$, and the last number is $\boxed{781}$, whatever choices are made. (Equivalently, subtracting $1$ from every number turns the operation into ordinary addition: $(a-1)+(b-1)=(a+b-1)-1$.)
