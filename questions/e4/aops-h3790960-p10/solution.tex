Let there be $b$ boys and $2b$ girls. The total games are $G=\dfrac{3b(3b-1)}{2}$, and the girls win $\dfrac{7}{16}G$. They necessarily win all $\binom{2b}{2}=b(2b-1)$ games among themselves, so $\dfrac{7}{16}\cdot\dfrac{3b(3b-1)}{2}\ge b(2b-1)$, which simplifies to $b\le11$. Since the two win totals have ratio $7:9$, $16\mid G$, so $32\mid b(3b-1)$. The two factors are coprime and exactly one is even; that even factor must be divisible by $32$. With $1\le b\le11$, this forces $3b-1=32$, giving $b=11$. The total is $\boxed{33}$. Feasibility follows by having every boy beat every girl: girls win $231$ games and boys $297$, in ratio $7:9$.
