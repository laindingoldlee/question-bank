By the extended sine rule, the two equal sides have length $2\sin75^\circ$. Their included angle is 30 degrees, so area is $\dfrac{1}{2}(2\sin75^\circ)^2\sin30^\circ=\sin^275^\circ=\dfrac{2+\sqrt3}{4}$. Hence $\boxed{a+b+c=9}$.
