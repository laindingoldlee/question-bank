Let $n$ and $m$ be positive integers and let $a\ne b$ be decimal digits with $a\ne0$. Consider the number
\[N=\overline{\underbrace{aa\cdots a}_{n\text{ digits}}\underbrace{bb\cdots b}_{m\text{ digits}}}.\]
Find the smallest such number $N$ for which the digit sum of $N$ and the digit sum of $N+1$ are consecutive perfect squares (that is, squares of two consecutive integers, in either order).
