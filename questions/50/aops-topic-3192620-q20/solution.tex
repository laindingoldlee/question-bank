The reciprocal sum equals $\dfrac{\sigma(360)}{360}$. Since $360=2^3 3^2 5$, its divisor sum is $(1+2+4+8)(1+3+9)(1+5)=15\cdot13\cdot6=1170$. The sum is $\dfrac{13}{4}$, so $\boxed{m+n=17}$.
