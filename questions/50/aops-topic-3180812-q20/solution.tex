Scale each amount by two to obtain three independent uniform variables on $[0,1]$. Failure means their sum is less than one, a tetrahedron of volume $\dfrac{1}{6}$ inside the unit cube. Since the joint density is one, failure probability is $\dfrac{1}{6}$ and success probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{5}{6}}$.
