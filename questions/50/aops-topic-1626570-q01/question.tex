A man has two children. You learn that at least one of them is a girl who was born on a Friday the $13$th. What is the probability that both children are girls?

Assume that, independently for each child, the child is a boy or a girl with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$ each, the child was born in a non-leap year, the birthday is equally likely to be any of the $365$ days of that year, and the year is equally likely to begin on each of the seven days of the week. Express your answer as a common fraction.
