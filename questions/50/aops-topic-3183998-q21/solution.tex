Let $Q=ab+bc+ca$ and $P=abc$. The reciprocal condition gives $\dfrac{Q}{P}=\dfrac{1}{5}$, so $P=5Q$. Thus $a^3+b^3+c^3=(a+b+c)^3-3(a+b+c)Q+3P=125-15Q+15Q=125$. The conditions are attainable, for example with $(a,b,c)=(5,1,-1)$. Consequently the greatest value is $\boxed{125}$.
