Since $D$ is on the perpendicular bisector of $BC$, we have $DB=DC=7$, and triangle $DBC$ is isosceles, so $\angle DBC=\angle DCB$. Call this angle $\theta$. Since $BD$ bisects angle $B$, also $\angle ABD=\theta$.

Triangles $ABD$ and $ACB$ share the angle at $A$, and $\angle ABD=\theta=\angle ACB$. So they are similar, with
\[
\dfrac{AB}{AC}=\dfrac{AD}{AB}\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad AB^2=AD\cdot AC=9\cdot16=144,\qquad AB=12 .
\]
So triangle $ABD$ has sides $AB=12$, $BD=7$, $AD=9$. Its semiperimeter is $s=14$, and by Heron's formula
\[
[ABD]=\sqrt{14(14-12)(14-7)(14-9)}=\sqrt{14\cdot2\cdot7\cdot5}=\sqrt{980}=\boxed{14\sqrt5}.
\]
(Check with the angle bisector theorem: $\dfrac{AB}{BC}=\dfrac{AD}{DC}=\dfrac{9}{7}$ gives $BC=\dfrac{28}{3}$, and the triangle with sides $12$, $\dfrac{28}{3}$, $16$ indeed has the perpendicular bisector of $BC$ through $D$.)

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0),C=(28/3,0),A=(-4/3,sqrt(144-16/9)); pair D=A+9/16*(C-A); pair M=(14/3,0);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); draw(B--D,linewidth(0.9));
draw(D--M,blue+dashed);
draw(M+(0.35,0)--M+(0.35,0.35)--M+(0,0.35),blue);
real a1=degrees(D-B), a2=degrees(A-B);
draw(arc(B,1.2,0,a1),red); draw(arc(B,1.4,a1,a2),red); draw(arc(C,1.3,180-a1,180),red);
label("$\theta$",B+1.85*dir(a1/2),red); label("$\theta$",B+2.0*dir((a1+a2)/2),red); label("$\theta$",C+1.9*dir(180-a1/2),red);
pair t1=(B+D)/2, t2=(D+C)/2; pair n1=0.3*unit(rotate(90)*(D-B)), n2=0.3*unit(rotate(90)*(C-D));
draw(t1-n1--t1+n1); draw(t2-n2--t2+n2);
draw((B+M)/2+(0,-0.3)--(B+M)/2+(0,0.3)); draw((M+C)/2+(0,-0.3)--(M+C)/2+(0,0.3));
dot(A);dot(B);dot(C);dot(D);dot(M,blue);
label("$A$",A,N); label("$B$",B,SW); label("$C$",C,SE); label("$D$",D,NE); label("$M$",M,S,blue);
label("$9$",(A+D)/2,NE); label("$7$",(D+C)/2+0.35*unit(rotate(90)*(C-D)),NE); label("$12$",(A+B)/2,W); label("$7$",(B+D)/2+(-0.45,0.25),NW);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
