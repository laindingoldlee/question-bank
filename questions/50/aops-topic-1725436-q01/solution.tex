Each route from $A$ to $D$ through $C$ consists of a path from $A$ to $C$ ($9$ choices) followed by a path from $C$ to $D$ ($5$ choices), and every pair of choices gives a different route. By the multiplication principle there are $9\cdot5=\boxed{45}$ ways.
