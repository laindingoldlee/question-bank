The numbers of workers in teams $X$ and $Y$ are in the ratio $3:4$, and the working efficiencies (work done per day) of a worker in $X$ and a worker in $Y$ are in the ratio $5:4$. The two teams each do a project with the same workload, and $Y$ finishes $9$ days earlier than $X$. Later two new teams are formed: $M$ consists of $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of the workers of $X$ and $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of the workers of $Y$, and $N$ consists of the rest of the workers of $X$ and $Y$. Teams $M$ and $N$ each do a second project, of equal workloads, and $N$ finishes $6$ days earlier than $M$. What is the ratio of the workload of the first project to that of the second project?
