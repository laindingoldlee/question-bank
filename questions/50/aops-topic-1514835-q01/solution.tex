Let $X$ have $3k$ workers and $Y$ have $4k$ workers, and let a worker of $X$ do $5$ units of work per day and a worker of $Y$ do $4$ units. Then the daily rates are
\[
X:\ 15k,\qquad Y:\ 16k .
\]
\textbf{First project}, workload $W_1$:
\[
\dfrac{W_1}{15k}-\dfrac{W_1}{16k}=9\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad\dfrac{W_1}{240k}=9\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad W_1=2160k .
\]
\textbf{New teams.} $M$ has $2k$ workers of $X$ and $\dfrac{4k}{3}$ of $Y$, so its rate is $10k+\dfrac{16k}{3}=\dfrac{46k}{3}$. $N$ has $k$ workers of $X$ and $\dfrac{8k}{3}$ of $Y$, so its rate is $5k+\dfrac{32k}{3}=\dfrac{47k}{3}$.

\textbf{Second project}, workload $W_2$:
\[
\dfrac{3W_2}{46k}-\dfrac{3W_2}{47k}=6\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad\dfrac{3W_2}{2162k}=6\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad W_2=4324k .
\]
Hence
\[
W_1:W_2=2160:4324=\boxed{540:1081}.
\]
(The answer does not depend on $k$; for whole numbers of workers $k$ should be a multiple of $3$.)
