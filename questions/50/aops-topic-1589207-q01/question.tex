A student claims that for every monic polynomial
\[
P(x)=x^n+a_{n-1}x^{n-1}+\cdots+a_1x+a_0\qquad(n\ge3),
\]
the sum of the cubes of its roots (with multiplicity, complex roots allowed) equals $a_{n-1}^3-a_1a_2$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that the claim is false.
\item[(b)] Prove that the sum of the cubes of the roots is $-a_{n-1}^3+3a_{n-1}a_{n-2}-3a_{n-3}$.
\end{enumerate}
