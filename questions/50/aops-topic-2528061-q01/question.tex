Prove the fundamental theorem of algebra: every polynomial
\[p(z)=a_nz^n+a_{n-1}z^{n-1}+\cdots+a_1z+a_0\]
with complex coefficients, $n\ge1$ and $a_n\ne0$, has a complex root. You may use that a continuous real-valued function on a closed disc $\{z:|z|\le R\}$ attains a minimum value.
