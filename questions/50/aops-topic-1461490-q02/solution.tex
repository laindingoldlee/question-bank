Rearrange as $1-3(4a)+3(4a)^2-(4a)^3$, since $3\cdot4a=12a$ and $3\cdot16a^2=48a^2$. This is the expansion of $(u-v)^3=u^3-3u^2v+3uv^2-v^3$ with $u=1$, $v=4a$. So
\[
1-64a^3-12a+48a^2=\boxed{(1-4a)^3}.
\]
