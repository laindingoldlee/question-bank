Five shaded circles lie inside a circle of radius $7$ units. Four small congruent shaded circles are each tangent to the outer circle and to the large shaded circle, and each lies outside the large shaded circle. The radius of each small shaded circle is $\dfrac{1}{5}$ of the radius of the large shaded circle. What fraction of the area of the outer circle is shaded? Express your answer as a common fraction.
