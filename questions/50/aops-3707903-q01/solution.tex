The only increasing distinct positive counts summing to seven are $(1,2,4)$. Assign these to the three friends in $3!$ ways. For any such assignment, concatenate the three ordered cones in a fixed friend order; this bijects with arrangements of seven scoops having multiplicities $2,2,2,1$, of which there are $\dfrac{7!}{2!2!2!}=630$. Thus the total is $6\cdot630=\boxed{3780}$.
