The sum of distances to $1,\ldots,n$ is minimised at a median. Pairing the outermost terms proves the minimum is $\lfloor \dfrac{n^2}{4}\rfloor$: for $n=2m$ it is $m^2$, and for $n=2m+1$ it is $m(m+1)$. Now $\lfloor\dfrac{89^2}{4}\rfloor=1980<2025$, while $\dfrac{90^2}{4}=2025$. The least $n$ is $\boxed{90}$.
