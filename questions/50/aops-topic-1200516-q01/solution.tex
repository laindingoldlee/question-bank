Together they cover $\dfrac{1}{10}+\dfrac{1}{20}=\dfrac{3}{20}$ of a lap per second, and they meet each time they have together covered a whole lap, i.e. every $\dfrac{20}{3}$ seconds. In $600$ seconds that happens $600\div\dfrac{20}{3}=\boxed{90}$ times (the last meeting is exactly at $600$ s).
