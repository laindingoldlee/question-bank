In triangle $ABC$, $\angle C=90^\circ$. Points $P$ and $Q$ are chosen outside the triangle so that $ABPQ$ is a square. Let $CQ$ and $CP$ meet $AB$ at $Y$ and $X$ respectively. Show that
\[
XY^2=AY\cdot XB .
\]
