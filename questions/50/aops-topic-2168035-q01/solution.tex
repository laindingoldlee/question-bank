Let $S_N=\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{N}\dfrac{n}{2^n}$. Then
\[S_N-\dfrac{1}{2}S_N=\sum_{n=1}^{N}\dfrac{n}{2^n}-\sum_{n=1}^{N}\dfrac{n}{2^{n+1}}=\sum_{n=1}^{N}\dfrac{n}{2^n}-\sum_{n=2}^{N+1}\dfrac{n-1}{2^{n}}=\left(\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{4}+\cdots+\dfrac{1}{2^N}\right)-\dfrac{N}{2^{N+1}},\]
because for $2\le n\le N$ the terms combine to $\dfrac{n-(n-1)}{2^n}=\dfrac{1}{2^n}$. The geometric sum is $1-\dfrac{1}{2^N}$, so
\[\dfrac{1}{2}S_N=1-\dfrac{1}{2^N}-\dfrac{N}{2^{N+1}},\qquad S_N=2-\dfrac{N+2}{2^N}.\]
As $N\to\infty$, $\dfrac{N+2}{2^N}\to0$, so the series converges and its sum is $\boxed{2}$.
