The interior and exterior angles at a vertex are supplementary, so their bisectors are perpendicular. A line making an acute angle of $36^\circ$ with the first bisector therefore makes an acute angle of $90^\circ-36^\circ=\boxed{54^\circ}$ with the second.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair C=(0,1);draw((-3,0)--(3,0));draw(C-2*dir(36)--C+1.5*dir(36),blue);draw(C-2*dir(126)--C+1.5*dir(126),red);dot(C);label("C",C,E);label("line AB",(2.5,0),S);label("interior bisector",C+1.5*dir(36),NE);label("exterior bisector",C+1.5*dir(126),NW);
\end{asy}
