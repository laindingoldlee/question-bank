Any solution is odd. The even integers $x-1,x+1$ differ by 2, so one is divisible by exactly one power of 2 and the other must be divisible by $2^{10}$. Hence $x\equiv1$ or $-1\pmod{1024}$. In $0\le x\le2047$ this gives $1,1023,1025,2047$, and direct substitution verifies all four modulo 2048. Their sum is $\boxed{4096}$.
