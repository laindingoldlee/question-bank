The two common chord endpoints and the centres form two equilateral triangles. Each half of the lens is a 120-degree sector minus a triangle of area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot100\sin120^\circ=25\sqrt3$. Hence the lens area is $2(\dfrac{100\pi}{3}-25\sqrt3)=\dfrac{200\pi}{3}-50\sqrt3\approx\boxed{122.8}$.
