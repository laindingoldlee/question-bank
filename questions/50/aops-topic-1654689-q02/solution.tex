The positive odd multiples of $3$ are $3\cdot1,3\cdot3,3\cdot5,\dots$; the $7$th is $3\cdot13=39$.

The positive even multiples of $3$ are the multiples of $6$: $6,12,18,\dots$; the $7$th is $6\cdot7=42$.

The sum is $39+42=\boxed{81}$.
