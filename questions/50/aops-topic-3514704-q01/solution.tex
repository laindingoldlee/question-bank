The southward ground speed is $\dfrac{2000}{5}=400$ mph; northward it is $\dfrac{2000}{8}=250$ mph. If airspeed is $v$ and wind speed $w$, then $v+w=400$ and $v-w=250$. Subtracting gives $2w=150$, so $\boxed{w=75\text{ mph}}$.
