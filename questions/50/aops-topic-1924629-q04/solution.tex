Since $48=2^4\cdot3$, a number is relatively prime to $48$ exactly when it is odd and not a multiple of $3$. Among $1,\dots,48$ there are $24$ odd numbers, and $8$ of them ($3,9,15,\dots,45$) are multiples of $3$. That leaves $24-8=\boxed{16}$. (Equivalently, $\varphi(48)=48\left(1-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)\left(1-\dfrac{1}{3}\right)=16$.)
