Every solution lies in $[-99,99]$, and the equation is odd, so nonzero roots come in opposite pairs. For positive $x$, solutions can occur only on the positive sine humps $(2j,2j+1)$, $j=0,\ldots,49$. On each such interval, $g(x)=99\sin(\pi x)-x$ is strictly concave.
On $(0,1)$, $g(0)=0$, its initial derivative is positive, and $g(1)<0$, so there is exactly one positive root. On each of the other $49$ humps both endpoint values are negative, while at the midpoint $g(2j+\dfrac{1}{2})=99-(2j+\dfrac{1}{2})>0$. Strict concavity and the strictly decreasing derivative give exactly two roots in each. Thus there are $99$ positive roots, $99$ negative roots, and zero, for a total of $\boxed{199}$.
