If $bc-ak=1$, any common divisor of $a,b$ divides 1, proving necessity. Conversely, Bezout's identity gives integers $c_0,k_0$ with $bc_0+ak_0=1$ when the gcd is 1. Replace $c_0$ by its residue $c$ modulo $a$; it is not 0, so $1\le c<a$. The weaker conditions do not suffice: $a=6,b=9$ have opposite parity and neither divides the other, but their gcd is 3 and no inverse exists.
