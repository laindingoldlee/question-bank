The positive integers that can be written as a sum of \emph{distinct} powers of $3$ (using $3^0=1$, $3^1$, $3^2$, \dots, each at most once) are listed in increasing order: $1,3,4,9,10,12,13,27,\dots$. What is the $500$th number in the list?
