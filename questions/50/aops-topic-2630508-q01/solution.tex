All $\dbinom{400}4$ selections are equally likely, and the favourable ones choose $1$ of the $3$ toxic kernels and $3$ of the other $397$:
\[\dfrac{\dbinom31\dbinom{397}3}{\dbinom{400}4}=\dfrac{3\cdot4\cdot396\cdot395}{400\cdot399\cdot398}=\boxed{\dfrac{7821}{264670}}\approx0.0296.\]
