A food manufacturer is analysing a sample of $400$ corn kernels for a toxin. In this sample, $3$ kernels have dangerously high levels of the toxin. If $4$ kernels are selected at random from the sample, what is the probability that exactly one of them has a dangerously high level of the toxin?
