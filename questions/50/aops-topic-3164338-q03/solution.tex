Write $x=10a+b,y=10b+a$, with $1\le a\le9$, $0\le b\le9$, and $a>b$. Since $x-y=9(a-b)$ is divisible by 5, $a-b=5$. Also $x+y=11(a+b)$ is divisible by 7. The possible sums with $a=b+5$ are 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, so $a+b=7$. Thus $a=6,b=1$, giving $\boxed{x=61}$.
