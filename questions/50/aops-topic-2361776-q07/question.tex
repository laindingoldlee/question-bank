From a point $P$ outside a circle with centre $O$ and radius $10$, the tangent segment $PT$ to the circle has length $6$. The line $PO$ cuts the disc into two halves of equal area; it meets the circle at $X$ and $Y$, with $X$ between $P$ and $O$. Find the length $PY$ of this area-bisecting segment from $P$ to the far side of the circle.
