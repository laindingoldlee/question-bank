The nested radical is the limit $L$ of $a_1=\sqrt n$, $a_{k+1}=\sqrt{n+a_k}$. This sequence is increasing (by induction, $a_{k+1}>a_k$ because $a_2>a_1$ and $t\mapsto\sqrt{n+t}$ is increasing) and bounded above by the positive root of $t^2=n+t$, so it converges, and its limit satisfies
\[
L=\sqrt{n+L}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad L^2-L=n,\qquad L=\dfrac{1+\sqrt{1+4n}}{2}.
\]
So $L$ is an integer $m$ exactly when $n=m(m-1)$. Since $n\ge1$ we need $m\ge2$, and $m(m-1)\le40$ gives $m\le6$. Hence
\[
n\in\{\boxed{2,\ 6,\ 12,\ 20,\ 30}\},
\]
with values $L=2,3,4,5,6$ respectively.
