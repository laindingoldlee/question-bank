The trees not in bloom are $20$ percent of all trees $T$, so $\dfrac{20}{100}T=2600$. To undo multiplication by $\dfrac{20}{100}$, multiply both sides by its reciprocal $\dfrac{100}{20}$ (dividing by a fraction is multiplying by its reciprocal): $T=2600\cdot5=13000$. The trees in bloom number $13000-2600=\boxed{10400}$ (or $80$ percent of $13000$).
