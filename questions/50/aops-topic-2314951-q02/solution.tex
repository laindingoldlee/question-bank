Let $s$ be the digit sum, so $1\le s\le27$, and the remainder $r$ satisfies $r\le s-1\le26$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $r=26$ needs $s=27$, so the number is $999=27\cdot37$, with remainder $0$.
\item $r=25$ needs $s\ge26$. For $s=26$ the numbers are $998$, $989$, $899$, with $998=26\cdot38+10$, $989=26\cdot38+1$, $899=26\cdot34+15$; and $s=27$ gives remainder $0$. None gives $25$.
\item $r=24$ is possible: $799$ has $s=25$ and $799=25\cdot31+24$.
\end{itemize}
The largest possible remainder is $\boxed{24}$.
