A man runs once around a track that is $320$ miles long. His basic speed is $x$ miles per hour, where $x>0$. Every $5$ miles he trips over a stone and runs at $x-3$ miles per hour for the next $52$ feet, after which he returns to his previous speed. At the $160$th mile he stops for lunch for $x$ minutes. Then, every $128$ minutes, he slows down by $1$ mile per hour because of tiredness. In the last $20$ minutes he can see the end and runs $1$ mile per hour faster than he otherwise would. The whole trip, lunch included, takes $197$ minutes.

Show that no value of $x$ is consistent with these conditions.
