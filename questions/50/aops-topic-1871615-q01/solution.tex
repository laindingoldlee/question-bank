We do not need the details of the stumbles and the tiredness; we only use that none of them makes him faster. The only increase in speed is the extra $1$ mile per hour in the last $20$ minutes, and every other change lowers his speed. So at every moment he runs at most $x+1$ miles per hour, and covering $320$ miles takes at least
\[
\dfrac{320}{x+1}\text{ hours}=\dfrac{19200}{x+1}\text{ minutes}.
\]
Adding the lunch break of $x$ minutes, the total time $T$ in minutes satisfies
\[
T\ge x+\dfrac{19200}{x+1}=(x+1)+\dfrac{19200}{x+1}-1\ge2\sqrt{19200}-1=160\sqrt3-1>276,
\]
by AM-GM applied to $x+1$ and $\dfrac{19200}{x+1}$ (and $160\sqrt3>277$ since $160^2\cdot3=76800>277^2=76729$). So the trip takes more than $276$ minutes for every $x>0$, and it can never take $197$ minutes. Hence $\boxed{\text{no such } x \text{ exists}}$.
