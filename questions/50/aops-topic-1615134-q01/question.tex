Let $r_1,r_2,r_3,r_4$ be the roots (counted with multiplicity, possibly complex) of the quartic $ax^4+bx^3+cx^2+dx+e=0$, where $a\ne0$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Express $r_1+r_2+r_3+r_4$ and $r_1r_2r_3r_4$ in terms of $a,b,c,d,e$, and also the sums $\sum r_ir_j$ (over the $6$ pairs) and $\sum r_ir_jr_k$ (over the $4$ triples).
\item Use these to find the sum and the product of the roots of $2x^4-6x^3+5x^2+x-8=0$.
\end{enumerate}
