A $200\times200\times200$ Rubik's cube has $6\cdot200^2=240000$ stickers: $200^2=40000$ stickers of each of $6$ colours. Suppose the stickers may be peeled off and stuck back on in any arrangement, one sticker on each of the $240000$ sticker positions, with the cube held fixed in space. How many different colour patterns are possible? Give the answer as an expression in factorials.
