A colour pattern is an arrangement of $240000$ positions into $6$ colour classes of $40000$ positions each. Stickers of the same colour are indistinguishable, so this is a multinomial coefficient: choose the $40000$ positions of the first colour, then of the second, and so on:
\[\dbinom{240000}{40000}\dbinom{200000}{40000}\cdots\dbinom{40000}{40000}=\boxed{\dfrac{240000!}{\left(40000!\right)^6}}.\]
(This count includes the solved pattern.)
