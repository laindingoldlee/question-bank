In rectangle $ABCD$, $AB=11$, $BC=8$. Points $E,F$ on $BC$ have $BE=3$, $EF=2$, $FC=3$. The area inside the rectangle at distance less than 6 from segment $EF$ is $a\pi+\sqrt b+c$ for integers $a,b,c$ with $b>0$. Find $10000a+100b+c$.
