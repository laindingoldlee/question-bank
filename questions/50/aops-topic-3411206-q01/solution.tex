The last two digits are $52$, so divisibility by $4$ is automatic. The alternating digit sum is $b-a-c-3$, which lies from $-21$ to $6$. For divisibility by $11$ it must be $0$ or $-11$. If $a+c=b-3$, then $b=3,\ldots,9$ and the pair counts are $1,2,\ldots,7$, summing to $28$. If $a+c=b+8$, then $b=0,\ldots,9$ and the pair counts are $9,10,9,8,7,6,5,4,3,2$, summing to $63$. Total $\boxed{91}$.
