Each new distinct cut increases the piece count by at least 1, so at least 5 pieces result. Four parallel cuts attain 5. Thus $\boxed5$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw(circle((0,0),2));for(real x:new real[]{-1.2,-.4,.4,1.2})draw((x,-sqrt(4-x*x))--(x,sqrt(4-x*x)),blue);
label("four parallel cuts",(0,-2),S);
\end{asy}
