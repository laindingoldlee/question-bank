Multiplying by $z^3-1$ gives $z^9-1=0$. Conversely the roots of $z^6+z^3+1$ are exactly the ninth roots of unity that are not third roots of unity. Their arguments in $[0^\circ,360^\circ)$ are
\[
40^\circ,80^\circ,160^\circ,200^\circ,280^\circ,320^\circ.
\]
The sole argument in the requested interval is $\boxed{160^\circ}$.
