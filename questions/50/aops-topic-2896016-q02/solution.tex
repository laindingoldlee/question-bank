Let $g(x)=x\ln x$, so $g'(x)=\ln x+1$, which is $0$ at $x=\dfrac1e$. It is negative before and positive after, so this is the minimum. The minimum of $x^x=e^{g(x)}$ is $\boxed{\exp\left(-\dfrac1e\right)}\approx0.692$.
