$x=2^{2y}$, so $2^{2y(1+y)}=2^3$ and $2y^2+2y-3=0$, giving $y=\dfrac{-1\pm\sqrt7}2$. Then $2^z=2x=2^{2y+1}$, so $z=2y+1=\pm\sqrt7$ and $z^2=\boxed{7}$.

The attempt solved $2y^2+2y-3=0$ incorrectly as $y=-1\pm\sqrt7$ (it dropped the division by $2$ in the quadratic formula).
