The difference stays $a_n-b_n=1$. The sum $s_n=a_n+b_n$ obeys $s_{n+1}=3s_n+2$, so $s_n+1=4\cdot3^n$. Thus $a_n=\dfrac{s_n+1}{2}=2\cdot3^n$. Since $3^{20}\equiv1\pmod{100}$ and $3\cdot67\equiv1\pmod{100}$, $3^{2019}\equiv3^{19}\equiv67$. The requested remainder is $\boxed{34}$.
