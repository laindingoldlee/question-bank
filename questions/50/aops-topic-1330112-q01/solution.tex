Every integer that is odd or a multiple of $4$ is a difference of two squares: $2k+1=(k+1)^2-k^2$ and $4k=(k+1)^2-(k-1)^2$.

If $n\not\equiv2\pmod4$, write $n=x^2-y^2$ and take $z=0$. If $n\equiv2\pmod4$, then $n-1$ is odd, so $n-1=x^2-y^2$ and $n=x^2-y^2+1^2$. Hence every positive integer (indeed every integer) has the required form. $\blacksquare$
