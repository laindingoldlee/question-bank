My car shows its fuel efficiency as a bar graph: at the end of each minute a new bar is added showing the miles per gallon (mpg) for that minute. In the first three minutes of a trip I get $42$, $48$ and $40$ mpg. What fuel efficiency must I achieve in the fourth minute so that the average of the four bars is $50$ mpg?
