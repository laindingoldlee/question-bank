Let $E$ be the expected number. The first flip is a tail with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$ (no heads), or a head with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, after which the situation repeats. So $E=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot0+\dfrac{1}{2}(1+E)$, giving $E=\boxed{1}$.
