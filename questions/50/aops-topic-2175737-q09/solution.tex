A professor is between two students exactly when he does not sit at either end and no other professor sits next to him. So the professors' chairs are $3$ of the $7$ inner chairs $2,3,\ldots,8$, no two of them adjacent.

Choosing $3$ pairwise non-adjacent chairs among $7$ in a row is the same as choosing $3$ of $5$: if the chosen positions are $p_1<p_2<p_3$, then $p_1<p_2-1<p_3-2$ are $3$ distinct positions among $5$, and every such choice comes from exactly one admissible triple. So there are $\dbinom53=10$ sets of chairs, and the three professors can be assigned to a set in $3!=6$ ways. The answer is $10\cdot6=\boxed{60}$.
