Group the two kinds of factors; each group telescopes:
\[\dfrac13\cdot\dfrac35\cdot\dfrac57\cdots\dfrac{111}{113}=\dfrac{1}{113},\qquad\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac23\cdot\dfrac34\cdots\dfrac{55}{56}=\dfrac1{56},\]
since in each product every numerator after the first cancels the previous denominator. The product is
\[\dfrac1{113}\cdot\dfrac1{56}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{6328}}.\]
