Evaluate the product
\[\dfrac13\cdot\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac35\cdot\dfrac23\cdot\dfrac57\cdot\dfrac34\cdots\dfrac{111}{113},\]
in which the factors in odd positions are $\dfrac13,\dfrac35,\dfrac57,\dots,\dfrac{111}{113}$ (that is, $\dfrac{2k-1}{2k+1}$ for $k=1,\dots,56$) and the factors in even positions are $\dfrac12,\dfrac23,\dfrac34,\dots,\dfrac{55}{56}$ (that is, $\dfrac{k}{k+1}$ for $k=1,\dots,55$).
