Put $t=7b+5c$. Then $c=\dfrac{(t-7b)}{5}$ and $a=118-b-c$. Substituting into $a(7b+5c)+3bc=0$ gives
\[
21b^2-5tb+t^2-590t=0.
\]
For real $b$, the discriminant must be nonnegative:
\[
25t^2-84(t^2-590t)=59t(840-t)\ge0.
\]
Thus $0\le t\le840$. At $t=840$, the repeated root gives $b=100$, then $c=28$ and $a=-10$. These values satisfy both original conditions. The maximum is $\boxed{840}$.
