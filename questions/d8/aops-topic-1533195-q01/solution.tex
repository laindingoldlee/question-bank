The top card is a spade with probability $\dfrac{13}{52}$; given that, the second is a spade with probability $\dfrac{12}{51}$, and then the third with probability $\dfrac{11}{50}$. So
\[
P=\dfrac{13}{52}\cdot\dfrac{12}{51}\cdot\dfrac{11}{50}=\dfrac{1716}{132600}=\boxed{\dfrac{11}{850}}.
\]
(Equivalently, $\dfrac{\binom{13}{3}}{\binom{52}{3}}=\dfrac{286}{22100}$.)
