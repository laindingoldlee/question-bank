A standard deck of $52$ cards has $13$ ranks and $4$ suits ($\spadesuit$, $\heartsuit$, $\diamondsuit$, $\clubsuit$), with exactly one card of each rank and suit. The deck is randomly arranged. What is the probability that the top three cards are all $\spadesuit$s?
