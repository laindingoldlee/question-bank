Let $m$ and $n$ be relatively prime positive integers such that $m-n$ is odd.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $m+3n$ and $5m+7n$ are relatively prime.
\item[(b)] Prove that $(m+3n)(5m+7n)$ is not a perfect square.
\end{enumerate}
