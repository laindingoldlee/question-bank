The claim holds for $n=1,2$. If it holds for $n$ and $n+1$, then $F_{n+2}\ge n+(n+1)=2n+1\ge n+2$ for $n\ge1$. Induction proves the claim. Merely saying that Fibonacci numbers grow faster does not establish the needed comparison for every index.
