Set $A=(0,0)$, $B=(s,0)$, $D=(0,s)$, $C=(s,s)$, so $P=(4,2)$ and $s>4$. The distance equation is $(s-4)^2+(s-2)^2=36$, or $s^2-6s-8=0$. Its roots are $3\pm\sqrt{17}$, and only the positive feasible root gives $\boxed{s=3+\sqrt{17}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
real s=3+sqrt(17);pair A=(0,0),Bp=(s,0),C=(s,s),D=(0,s),P=(4,2);draw(A--Bp--C--D--cycle);draw(P--C,blue);draw(P--(4,0),dashed);draw(P--(0,2),dashed);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",Bp,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$P$",P,W);label("$6$",(P+C)/2,E);
\end{asy}
