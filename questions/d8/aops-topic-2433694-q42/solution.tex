The sum of two odd numbers is even, and $10001$ is odd, so one of the primes must be even, i.e. equal to $2$. Then the other is $10001-2=9999$, which is divisible by $3$ (its digit sum is $36$) and so is not prime. Hence there are no ways: the answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(A)}\ 0}$.
