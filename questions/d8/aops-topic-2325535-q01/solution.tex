Long division gives
\[\dfrac{3}{26}=0.1153846153846\ldots=0.1\overline{153846}:\]
one digit $1$, followed by the block $153846$ repeated forever. (Indeed $\dfrac3{26}=\dfrac1{10}+\dfrac{2}{130}=\dfrac1{10}+\dfrac{1}{10}\cdot\dfrac{2}{13}$ and $\dfrac2{13}=0.\overline{153846}$.)

The block has digit sum $1+5+3+8+4+6=27$. After the first digit there remain $87916=6\cdot14652+4$ digits: $14652$ complete blocks and then the first four digits $1,5,3,8$ of a block. The sum is
\[1+14652\cdot27+(1+5+3+8)=1+395604+17=\boxed{395622}.\]
