Any common divisor of $F_n$ and $F_{n+1}$ divides $F_{n+1}-F_n=F_{n-1}$, and conversely, so $\gcd(F_n,F_{n+1})=\gcd(F_{n-1},F_n)$ for $n\ge2$. Repeating this down to $\gcd(F_1,F_2)=\gcd(1,1)=1$ shows that $\gcd(F_n,F_{n+1})=1$ for all $n$. The greatest possible value is $\boxed{1}$.
