If $x$ is an integer, $\lceil x\rceil=\lfloor x\rfloor=x$ and the difference is $0$. Otherwise $\lfloor x\rfloor=n<x<n+1=\lceil x\rceil$ and the difference is $1$. The range is $\boxed{\{0,1\}}$.
