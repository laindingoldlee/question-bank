Rewrite: $x^6+\left(x^3-y\right)^2=320$, i.e. $\left(x^3\right)^2+\left(x^3-y\right)^2=320$. The only ways to write $320$ as a sum of two squares are $8^2+16^2$ and $16^2+8^2$ (up to signs). Since $x^3$ is a cube, $x^3=\pm8$ ($\pm16$ is not a cube), so $x=\pm2$ and $x^3-y=\pm16$:
\[(x,y)\in\{(2,-8),\ (2,24),\ (-2,8),\ (-2,-24)\}.\]
