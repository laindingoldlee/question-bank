Complete the square: $h(t)=-16\left(t-\dfrac{5}{8}\right)^2+\dfrac{25}{4}+1$. The maximum is $\boxed{7.25\text{ feet}}$, reached at $t=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{8}\text{ s}}=0.625$ s.
