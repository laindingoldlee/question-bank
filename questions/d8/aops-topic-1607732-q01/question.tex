For a positive integer $n$, let $\Psi(n)$ be the single digit obtained by repeatedly replacing $n$ by the sum of its digits until one digit remains (for example $\Psi(94764)=\Psi(30)=3$). Find
\[
\Psi\bigl(\Psi(1)+\Psi(2)+\Psi(3)+\cdots+\Psi(999)+\Psi(1000)\bigr).
\]
