$20^{18}=2^{36}\cdot5^{18}$, so $a=2^x5^u$ and $b=2^y5^v$ with nonnegative exponents satisfying
\[2x+3y=36,\qquad 2u+3v=18.\]
In the first, $y$ must be even with $0\le y\le12$: $7$ choices, each giving one $x$. In the second, $v$ is even with $0\le v\le6$: $4$ choices. Hence there are $7\cdot4=\boxed{28}$ pairs.
