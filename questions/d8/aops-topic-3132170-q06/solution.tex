Put $u=x^2,v=y^2$. Completing the square gives $uv(u-v)=u\left(\dfrac{u^2}{4}-(v-\dfrac{u}{2})^2\right)\le\dfrac{u^3}{4}\le\dfrac{1}{4}$. Equality requires $u=1$ and $v=\dfrac{u}{2}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore the unique maximizing pair is $\boxed{(1,\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2})}$ and the maximum is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{4}}$.
