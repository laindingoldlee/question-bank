Robert has three candy dispensers, each releasing one candy at a time. One contains only cherry candies, one only lemon candies, and one a mixture of both. He cannot see inside, and he knows that all three labels ("cherry", "lemon", "mixed") are on the wrong dispensers. What is the minimum number of candies he must dispense to relabel all three dispensers correctly?
