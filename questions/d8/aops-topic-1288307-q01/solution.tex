Dispense one candy from the dispenser labelled "mixed". Its label is wrong, so it is a pure dispenser, and the candy shows which flavour it holds; say cherry. The dispenser labelled "lemon" is not lemon, and cherry is taken, so it is the mixed one. The remaining one is lemon. So one candy suffices.

Zero candies is not enough: the labels are a derangement of the true contents, and there are two derangements of three objects, which cannot be told apart without information. The minimum is $\boxed{1}$.
