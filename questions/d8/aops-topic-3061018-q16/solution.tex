Each integer from 1 through 9 is itself plus 0. Each integer from 10 through 18 is 9 plus a one-digit number. Also $19=11+8$ and $20=11+9$. For a sum of 21, if both palindromes have one digit their sum is at most 18. The only two-digit palindrome at most 21 is 11, which would require the other summand to be 10, not a palindrome. Thus the answer is $\boxed{21}$.
