Write $b=a+u,c=a+u+v$ with $u,v\ge0$. The equation becomes $15a+10u+6v=1200$. Modulo two, $a=2t$ is even; dividing by two gives $15t+5u+3v=600$. Modulo five, $v=5w$. The result is $3t+u+3w=120$. For each $j=t+w$ from $0$ through $40$, $u=120-3j$ is determined and there are $j+1$ choices of $(t,w)$. Hence the count is $1+2+\cdots+41=\boxed{861}$.
