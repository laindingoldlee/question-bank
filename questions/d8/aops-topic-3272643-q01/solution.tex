The intersection size is at most $\min(200000,300000)=200000$ and at least $\max(0,200000+300000-600000)=0$. Thus the sharp probability interval is $\boxed{[0,\dfrac{1}{3}]}$. Disjoint trait groups attain zero, and placing all of group $A$ inside group $B$ attains one third. The marginals alone do not determine a single probability.
