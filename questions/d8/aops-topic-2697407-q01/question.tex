Let $\varphi=\dfrac{1+\sqrt5}{2}$. Prove that the sequences $\lfloor n\varphi\rfloor$ and $\lfloor n\varphi^2\rfloor$ ($n=1,2,3,\dots$) together contain every positive integer exactly once.
