There are 8 letters, with two z's and two g's. Total arrangements are $\dfrac{8!}{2!2!}=10080$. Treating adjacent z's as one block gives $\dfrac{7!}{2!}=2520$ forbidden arrangements. The difference is $\boxed{7560}$.
