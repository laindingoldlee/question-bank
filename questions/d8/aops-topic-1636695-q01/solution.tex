Each year the population is multiplied by $1.011$. From $2017$ to $2021$ there are $4$ years, so the population will be
\[
10{,}112{,}669\times1.011^4\approx10{,}112{,}669\times1.044731\approx\boxed{10{,}565{,}022}.
\]
