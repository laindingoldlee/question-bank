In the first two hours the privateer gains $11-8=3$ miles per hour, so at 1:45 p.m. the gap is $10-2\cdot3=4$ miles.

After that the privateer covers $17$ miles while the merchantman covers $15$. The merchantman still makes $8$ mph, so the privateer now makes $8\cdot\dfrac{17}{15}=\dfrac{136}{15}$ mph and gains $\dfrac{136}{15}-8=\dfrac{16}{15}$ mph. The remaining $4$ miles take
\[
\dfrac{4}{\dfrac{16}{15}}=\dfrac{15}{4}\text{ hours}=3\text{ h }45\text{ min}.
\]
The privateer overtakes the merchantman at 1:45 p.m. $+$ 3 h 45 min $=$ 5:30 p.m., answer $\boxed{\textbf{(E)}}$.
