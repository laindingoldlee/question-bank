Let $m$ be the greatest number of rectangles in a tiling of a fixed $3\times3$ square such that all rectangle side lengths are positive integers and no two rectangles are congruent, even after rotation. Let $n$ be the number of tilings with exactly $m$ rectangles, counting rotated or reflected tilings separately. Find $mn$.
