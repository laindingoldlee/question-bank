We count solutions of $x_1+x_2+x_3+x_4=14$ with $1\le x_i\le10$. Without the upper bound, stars and bars gives $\dbinom{13}{3}=286$ solutions in positive integers. A solution with some $x_i\ge11$ has the others equal to $1$ (as $11+3=14$), so there are exactly $4$ such solutions ($11,1,1,1$ in some order). The answer is $286-4=\boxed{282}$.
