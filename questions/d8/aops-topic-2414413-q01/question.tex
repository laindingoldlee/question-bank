Consider the infinite nested radical
\[\sqrt{\dfrac{2}{5\sqrt{\dfrac{2}{5\sqrt{\dfrac{2}{5\sqrt{\cdots}}}}}}}\]
defined as the limit of $x_1=\sqrt{\dfrac25}$, $x_{n+1}=\sqrt{\dfrac{2}{5x_n}}$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that the sequence $(x_n)$ converges, and find its limit.
\item[(b)] A student argued that the expression is undefined: from the equation for its value $x$ he obtained $5\left(\sqrt{x}\right)^2=-x$, took square roots to get $5\sqrt{x}=i\sqrt{x}$, divided by $\sqrt{x}$ to get $5=i$, a contradiction; and on a graphing calculator the truncated expressions gave slightly different values each time. Explain what is wrong with this argument.
\end{enumerate}
