Substituting $y=25-7x$: $x^2+(25-7x)^2=25$ gives $50x^2-350x+600=0$, so $x=3$ or $4$, and the chord meets the circle at $(3,4)$ and $(4,-3)$. These radii are perpendicular ($3\cdot4+4\cdot(-3)=0$).

\end{asy}

The required region is a circular segment with central angle $90^\circ$:
\[\dfrac14\cdot25\pi-\dfrac12\cdot5\cdot5=\boxed{\dfrac{25\pi}4-\dfrac{25}2}.\]
