A goodie bag contains $3$ cookies, $10$ marshmallows, $16$ gingerbread men and $8$ candy canes. A child takes the items out of the bag one at a time, at random, until the bag is empty. What is the probability that the last item taken out is a marshmallow? Express your answer as a common fraction.
