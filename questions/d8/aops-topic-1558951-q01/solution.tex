Factor: $x^2-16=(x+4)(x-4)$. A product of two numbers is $\ge0$ exactly when both factors are $\ge0$ or both are $\le0$.
\begin{itemize}
\item Both $\ge0$: $x+4\ge0$ and $x-4\ge0$, i.e.\ $x\ge4$.
\item Both $\le0$: $x+4\le0$ and $x-4\le0$, i.e.\ $x\le-4$.
\end{itemize}
(Equivalently, the factors change sign at $-4$ and $4$: for $x<-4$ both are negative, so the product is positive; for $-4<x<4$ they have opposite signs, so the product is negative; for $x>4$ both are positive.) Hence
\[
\boxed{x\le-4\ \text{or}\ x\ge4}.
\]
