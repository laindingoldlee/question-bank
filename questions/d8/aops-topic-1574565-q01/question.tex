A group of juniors and seniors took a test. Exactly $\dfrac{3}{5}$ of the juniors and exactly $\dfrac{6}{7}$ of the seniors passed. The number of juniors who passed was $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of the number of seniors who passed. What fraction of the entire group passed the test? Express your answer as a common fraction.
