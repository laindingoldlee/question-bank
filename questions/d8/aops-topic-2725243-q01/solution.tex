A works in cycles of $4$ minutes ($3$ bottles), and B in cycles of $6.5$ minutes ($5$ bottles). Together they average $\dfrac34+\dfrac{5}{6.5}=\dfrac{39+40}{52}=\dfrac{79}{52}$ bottles per minute, so roughly $2015\cdot\dfrac{52}{79}\approx1326$ minutes are needed.

At $t=1326$: $1326=331\cdot4+2$, so A has made $331\cdot3+2=995$ bottles, and $1326=204\cdot6.5$, so B has made $204\cdot5=1020$. Together that is $2015$.

Just before, at $t=1325.5$: A has made $994$ (its 995th bottle finishes at $1326$), and B has made $1020$ (its last bottle finished at $1324.5$, followed by a rest), only $2014$. So the answer is $\boxed{1326}$ minutes.
