Let the roots be $r\le s\le t$. By Vieta's formulas $r+s+t=10$, $rst=30$ and $P=rs+st+tr$. The ways to write $30$ as a product of three positive integers are $1\cdot1\cdot30$, $1\cdot2\cdot15$, $1\cdot3\cdot10$, $1\cdot5\cdot6$, $2\cdot3\cdot5$, with sums $32,18,14,12,10$. Only $2,3,5$ has sum $10$. So
\[
P=2\cdot3+3\cdot5+5\cdot2=\boxed{31},
\]
and indeed $(x-2)(x-3)(x-5)=x^3-10x^2+31x-30$.
