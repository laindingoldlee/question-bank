Assign masses $m_B=1,m_C=3$ to realise $BD:DC=3:1$. The ratio $AE:EC=2:5$ then requires $m_A=\dfrac{15}{2}$. Thus $m_E=m_A+m_C=\dfrac{21}{2}$, and the balance on $BE$ gives $\boxed{BP:PE=\dfrac{21}{2}}$. Equivalently, barycentric coordinates of $P$ are proportional to $(15,2,6)$.
\begin{asy}
import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
pair A=(0,4),B=(-2,0),C=(4,0),D=(2.5,0),E=A+2/7*(C-A),P=(15A+2B+6C)/23;draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--D^^B--E);label("$A$",A,(0,1));label("$B$",B,(-1,-1));label("$C$",C,(1,-1));label("$D$",D,(0,-1));label("$E$",E,(1,1));label("$P$",P,(-1,0));label("$3:1$",(1,-.4));label("$2:5$",(3,2.5));
\end{asy}
