Write $x=2a+1$, $z=2c+1$ and $y=8t$ with $a,c\ge0$ and $t\ge1$. The equation becomes
\[2a+2c+8t=2012\iff a+c=1006-4t .\]
For each $t$ with $1006-4t\ge0$, i.e. $1\le t\le251$, there are $1007-4t$ pairs $(a,c)$. So the number of solutions is
\[\sum_{t=1}^{251}(1007-4t)=251\cdot1007-4\cdot\dfrac{251\cdot252}{2}=252\,757-126\,504=\boxed{126\,253}.\]
