The opposite of $1\dfrac{1}{5}$ is $-\dfrac{6}{5}$. The sum of the other two numbers is
\[
-\dfrac{35}{4}-\dfrac{17}{6}=-\dfrac{105}{12}-\dfrac{34}{12}=-\dfrac{139}{12}.
\]
Adding the opposite, with common denominator $60$:
\[
-\dfrac{139}{12}-\dfrac{6}{5}=-\dfrac{695}{60}-\dfrac{72}{60}=-\dfrac{767}{60}=\boxed{-12\dfrac{47}{60}}.
\]
So $-12\dfrac{7}{10}=-12\dfrac{42}{60}$ is not right; it is $\dfrac{5}{60}=\dfrac{1}{12}$ too large. (As a check in decimals: $-8.75-2.8\overline{3}-1.2=-12.78\overline{3}$.)
