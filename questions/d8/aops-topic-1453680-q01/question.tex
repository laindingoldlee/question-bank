Each of $9$ friends chooses her favourite positive integer.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] The average of the $9$ chosen numbers is $91$. What is the smallest possible median of the $9$ numbers?
\item[(b)] Suppose instead that the median of the $9$ chosen numbers is $91$. Is there a limit to how large the average can be? If so, what is the largest possible average?
\end{enumerate}
