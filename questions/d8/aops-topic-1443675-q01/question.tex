Bus station $A$ is $125$ km from bus station $B$. Bob, Sam and Wob start together from station $A$ towards $B$. Bob drives a bus at $25$ km per hour with Wob on board, while Sam walks at $5$ km per hour. At station $C$, Wob gets off and walks on to $B$ at $5$ km per hour, while Bob immediately drives back towards $A$, meets Sam at station $D$, picks him up, and drives towards $B$ again. When Bob and Sam arrive at $B$, Wob is still $4$ km from $B$. How long, in hours, does Bob take from leaving $A$ until arriving at $B$? (Assume all speeds are constant and no time is lost when turning or picking up.)
