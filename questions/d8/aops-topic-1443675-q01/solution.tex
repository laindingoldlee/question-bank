Let $AC=c$ km.
\begin{itemize}
\item Bob reaches $C$ after $\dfrac{c}{25}$ hours; Sam has walked $\dfrac{c}{5}$ km by then.
\item The gap $c-\dfrac{c}{5}=\dfrac{4c}{5}$ closes at $25+5=30$ km per hour, in $\dfrac{4c}{150}=\dfrac{2c}{75}$ hours. They meet at $D$ with $AD=5\left(\dfrac{c}{25}+\dfrac{2c}{75}\right)=\dfrac{c}{3}$.
\item From $D$ to $B$ takes $\dfrac{125-\dfrac{c}{3}}{25}=5-\dfrac{c}{75}$ hours.
\end{itemize}
So Bob's total time is
\[
T=\dfrac{3c}{75}+\dfrac{2c}{75}+5-\dfrac{c}{75}=5+\dfrac{4c}{75}.
\]
Wob leaves $C$ at time $\dfrac{c}{25}$ and walks for $T-\dfrac{c}{25}$ hours, ending $4$ km short of $B$:
\[
c+5\left(T-\dfrac{c}{25}\right)=121\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\dfrac{4c}{5}+25+\dfrac{4c}{15}=121\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\dfrac{16c}{15}=96\quad\Longrightarrow\quad c=90 .
\]
Then $T=5+\dfrac{360}{75}=\boxed{9.8}$ hours, that is, $9$ hours $48$ minutes. (Check: $AD=30$; Bob drives $90+60+95=245$ km in $9.8$ hours; Wob walks $5\cdot(9.8-3.6)=31$ km from $C$ and is at $121$ km.)
