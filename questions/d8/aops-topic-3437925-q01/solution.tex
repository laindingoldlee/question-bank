Choose one remaining word as an anchor and test every triple of other remaining words with it. Exactly one triple forms its true group, so the first group requires at most $\binom{15}{3}=455$ guesses. Repeat with an anchor among the twelve remaining words, needing at most $\binom{11}{3}=165$. With eight words, at most $\binom73=35$ guesses find the next group. Submit the final four words in one guess. The total is $455+165+35+1=\boxed{656}$, a valid guarantee even without using extra feedback.
