Sixteen distinct words are secretly partitioned into four groups of four. A guess selects four remaining words, and a correct guess removes that entire group. Incorrect guesses may include an extra clue, which you may ignore. Prove that a strategy can guarantee finding and submitting all groups in at most $656$ guesses; no optimality claim is required.
