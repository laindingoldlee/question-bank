A change of $\Delta C$ degrees Celsius is a change of $\dfrac{9}{5}\Delta C$ degrees Fahrenheit (the $+32$ cancels in the difference). The drop is $12-(-8)=20$ Celsius degrees, so it is $\dfrac{9}{5}\cdot20=\boxed{36}$ Fahrenheit degrees. Check: $12^\circ\mathrm{C}=53.6^\circ\mathrm{F}$ and $-8^\circ\mathrm{C}=17.6^\circ\mathrm{F}$, a difference of $36$.
