Let $ ABCD $ be a nondegenerate parallelogram with $ AC=BC $. Choose $ P $ on ray $ AB $ beyond $ B $. The circumcircle of $ ACD $ meets $ PD $ again at $ Q $, and the circumcircle of $ APQ $ meets $ PC $ again at $ R $. Prove that lines $ CD,AQ,BR $ are concurrent.
