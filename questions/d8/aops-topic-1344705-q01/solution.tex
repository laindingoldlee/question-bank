Let $d$ be the intended number of days. The book has $40d$ pages, and reading $25$ pages a day took $d+6$ days:
\[
40d=25(d+6)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad15d=150\quad\Longrightarrow\quad d=\boxed{10}.
\]
(The book has $400$ pages: $10$ days at $40$ pages or $16$ days at $25$ pages.)
