Let $F_1=F_2=1$ and $F_{n+2}=F_{n+1}+F_n$. Form a sequence by writing $F_1$ once, $F_2$ once, $F_3$ twice, and in general $F_n$ exactly $F_n$ times. What is its $2014$th term?
