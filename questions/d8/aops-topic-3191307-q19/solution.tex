The number of terms through the $n$th block is $F_1+\cdots+F_n=F_{n+2}-1$, proved by induction from the Fibonacci recurrence. Since $F_{17}-1=1596<2014\le2583=F_{18}-1$, the term lies in block $16$ and equals $F_{16}=\boxed{987}$.
