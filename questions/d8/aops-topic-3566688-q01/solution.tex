The first equation gives $x=\dfrac{3y-22}{5}$. Substitution in the second gives $18y-132+20y=-170$, hence $38y=-38$ and $y=-1$. Then $x=\dfrac{-3-22}{5}=-5$. Thus $\boxed{(x,y)=(-5,-1)}$, which satisfies both equations.
