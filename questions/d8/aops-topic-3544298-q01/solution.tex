Both numbers are roots of $t^2-84t+923=0$. Its discriminant is $84^2-4(923)=3364=58^2$, so the roots are $\dfrac{84\pm58}{2}=71,13$. Thus $\boxed{(x,y)=(71,13)\text{ or }(13,71)}$.
