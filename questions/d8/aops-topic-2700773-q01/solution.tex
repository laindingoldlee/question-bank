Put $A=(0,0)$, $B=(s,0)$, $C=(s,s)$, $D=(0,s)$, $M=\left(\dfrac s2,0\right)$. Line $AC$ is $y=x$ and line $DM$ is $y=s-2x$, so $E=\left(\dfrac s3,\dfrac s3\right)$. By the shoelace formula on $M,B,C,E$:
\[[BCEM]=\dfrac12\left|s^2-\dfrac{s^2}{6}\right|=\dfrac{5s^2}{12}.\]
So $\dfrac{5s^2}{12}=400$ and the square has area $s^2=\boxed{960}$.

fill(M--B--C--Ep--cycle,lightgray);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.8));draw(A--C);draw(D--M);dot(Ep);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$M$",M,S);label("$E$",Ep,W);
\end{asy}
