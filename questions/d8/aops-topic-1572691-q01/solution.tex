We need the least positive $n$ with $23n\equiv999\equiv-1\pmod{1000}$.

\textbf{Invert $23$ modulo $1000$.} Since $23\cdot87=2001=2\cdot1000+1$, we have $23\cdot87\equiv1\pmod{1000}$. Multiplying $23n\equiv-1$ by $87$:
\[
n\equiv-87\equiv913\pmod{1000}.
\]
The least positive solution is $n=\boxed{913}$, and indeed $23\cdot913=20999$. Every other solution is $913+1000j$, which gives a larger multiple.
