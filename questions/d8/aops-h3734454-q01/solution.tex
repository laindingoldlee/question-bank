Take $A=(0,0),B=(12,0),C=(12,12),D=(0,12)$. Then $E=(4,0)$ and $F=(6,6)$. The lines $AC,DE$ have equations $y=x$ and $y=12-3x$, so $G=(3,3)$. Therefore
\[
[DFG]=\dfrac{1}{2}\left|\det\begin{pmatrix}6&-6\\3&-9\end{pmatrix}\right|
=\dfrac{1}{2}|-54+18|=\boxed{18}.
\]

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

\end{asy}
