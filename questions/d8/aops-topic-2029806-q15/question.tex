Sixteen students stand in a circle. They toss a ball clockwise so that with every toss the ball skips the \(x\) students next to the thrower, where \(x\) is an integer with \(0\le x\le14\). For which values of \(x\) will every student eventually receive the ball? Find the sum of all such values of \(x\).
