Per day, $A$ does $\dfrac{1}{20}$ of the work, and $B$ and $C$ together do $\dfrac{1}{30}+\dfrac{1}{60}=\dfrac{1}{20}$. In each block of $3$ days $A$ works three times and $B,C$ once:
\[3\cdot\dfrac{1}{20}+\dfrac{1}{20}=\dfrac{4}{20}=\dfrac15.\]
After $4$ blocks ($12$ days) $\dfrac45$ is done, and after days $13$ and $14$ (only $A$) $\dfrac45+\dfrac{2}{20}=\dfrac{9}{10}$ is done. Day $15$ is a day on which all three work, adding $\dfrac{1}{20}+\dfrac{1}{20}=\dfrac{1}{10}$. So the work is completed exactly at the end of day $15$: $\boxed{15\text{ days}}$.
