Since $40=2^3\cdot5$, a number is relatively prime to $40$ exactly when it is odd and not a multiple of $5$. Among $1,\dots,40$ there are $20$ odd numbers, and $4$ of them ($5,15,25,35$) are multiples of $5$. So the answer is $20-4=\boxed{16}$. (This is Euler's totient: $\varphi(40)=40\left(1-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)\left(1-\dfrac{1}{5}\right)=16$.)
