\textbf{$6$ always divides it.} Among three consecutive integers one is a multiple of $3$ and at least one is even, so $2\cdot3=6$ divides $n(n+1)(n+2)$.

\textbf{Nothing larger does.} A common divisor of all the products divides each of them, in particular
\[
2\cdot3\cdot4=24,\qquad 3\cdot4\cdot5=60,\qquad 5\cdot6\cdot7=210,
\]
and $\gcd(24,60,210)=6$.

So the greatest such integer is $\boxed{6}$. (The smallest common factor greater than $1$ is $2$; the question as posted, ``lowest common factor'', is presumably meant as the greatest common factor, since the lowest is always $1$.)
