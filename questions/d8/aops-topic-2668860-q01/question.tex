Let $A$ and $B$ be distinct points of a plane, and let $\mathcal H$ be one of the two open half-planes determined by line $AB$. A set is \emph{convex} if it contains the whole segment joining any two of its points. Prove that $\mathcal H$, $\mathcal H\cup\overleftrightarrow{AB}$ and $\mathcal H\cup\overline{AB}$ are all convex.
