The number is $10^{4036}+2\cdot10^{2018}+1=(10^{2018}+1)^2$. Its positive square root is $10^{2018}+1$, whose decimal form is a $1$, then $2017$ zeros, then a $1$. Thus the answer is $\boxed{2017}$.
