In the final of a countdown round, Farley has $3$ points and Bohan has $2$ points, and the first person to reach $4$ points wins. On each problem, independently, there is a $40\%$ chance that Farley answers first and scores a point, a $30\%$ chance that Bohan answers first and scores a point, and a $30\%$ chance that neither answers and nobody scores. There is an unlimited supply of problems. What is the probability that Farley wins?
