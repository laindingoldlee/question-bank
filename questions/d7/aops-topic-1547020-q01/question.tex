A polygon in the coordinate plane does not cross itself, and all of its vertices are lattice points (points with integer coordinates). Let $A$ be its area, let $B$ be the number of lattice points on its boundary (vertices and all other lattice points on its sides), and let $I$ be the number of lattice points strictly inside it. Prove that
\[
A=I+\dfrac{B}{2}-1 .
\]
