Let $c$ be the hypotenuse. By the Pythagorean theorem $c^2=840^2+41^2$, so
\[(c-840)(c+840)=c^2-840^2=41^2=1681.\]
Try $c-840=1$ and $c+840=1681$: both give $c=841$. Indeed
\[841^2-840^2=(841-840)(841+840)=1681=41^2,\]
so $c=841$ satisfies $c^2=840^2+41^2$. Since the hypotenuse is the unique positive number with this property, $c=\boxed{841}$.
