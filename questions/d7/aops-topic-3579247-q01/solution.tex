The allowed digits $0,1,3,4,6,8,9$ correspond in order to base-seven digits $0,1,2,3,4,5,6$. This is an order-preserving bijection, including zero. The final entry $3141$ corresponds to $2131_7=2\cdot343+49+3\cdot7+1=757$. Excluding zero, there are $\boxed{757}$ positive entries.
