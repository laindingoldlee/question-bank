There are $6!=720$ ways to paint the labelled faces. The cube has $24$ rotations, and since all six colours are different, no rotation other than the identity fixes a colouring; so each distinguishable colouring corresponds to exactly $24$ paintings. The answer is $\dfrac{720}{24}=\boxed{30}$.

(Directly: rotate so that colour $1$ is on the bottom; choose the top colour ($5$ ways); the remaining $4$ colours go around the sides in a circle, $3!=6$ ways up to rotation. $5\cdot6=30$.)
