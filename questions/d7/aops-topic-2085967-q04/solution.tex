From the first two equations, \(ab+bc+ca=\dfrac{(a+b+c)^2-(a^2+b^2+c^2)}{2}=\dfrac{9-5}{2}=2\). By Vieta's formulas, \(a,b,c\) are exactly the three roots (with multiplicity) of
\[
x^3-3x^2+2x-6=(x-3)(x^2+2),
\]
namely \(3\), \(i\sqrt2\) and \(-i\sqrt2\). Conversely, any ordering of these three numbers satisfies all three equations. The roots are distinct, so there are \(3!=\boxed{6}\) ordered triples. (None of them is real, so there are no real solutions.)
