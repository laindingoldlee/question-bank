\[\dfrac{10000}{60}=166\dfrac{2}{3}\ \text{minutes}=2\ \text{hours}\ 46\ \text{minutes}\ 40\ \text{seconds}.\]
The answer is $\boxed{2\text{ h }46\text{ min }40\text{ s}}$.
