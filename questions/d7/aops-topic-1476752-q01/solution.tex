Divide the cubic by $x^2-3x-1$:
\[
2x^3-3x^2-11x+8=\left(x^2-3x-1\right)(2x+3)+11 .
\]
(Check: $\left(x^2-3x-1\right)(2x+3)=2x^3+3x^2-6x^2-9x-2x-3=2x^3-3x^2-11x-3$.) Since $x^2-3x-1=0$, the expression equals $\boxed{11}$, for either root $x$.

Equivalently, reduce step by step with $x^2=3x+1$: $2x^3=2x\cdot x^2=6x^2+2x$, so the expression is $3x^2-9x+8=3\left(3x+1\right)-9x+8=11$.
