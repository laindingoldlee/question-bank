Set $B=(0,0),C=(14,0)$. The side lengths give $A=(5,12)$. The perpendicular bisector of $BC$ gives $O=(7,y)$, and $OA^2=OB^2$ gives $4+(12-y)^2=49+y^2$, so $y=\dfrac{33}{8}$. Parametrize $AO$ as $(5,12)+t(2,-\dfrac{63}{8})$. At $y=0$, $t=\dfrac{32}{21}$ and $D=(\dfrac{169}{21},0)$. Therefore $\dfrac{BD}{DC}=\dfrac{169}{294-169}=\boxed{\dfrac{169}{125}}$.
