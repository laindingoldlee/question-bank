The divisor factors as $(x^2-x+1)(x^2+x+1)$. Write its remainder as $R(x)=ax^3+bx^2+cx+d$. Modulo $x^2-x+1$, use $x^2=x-1$, $x^3=-1$ to get
\[
(b+c)x+d-a-b=3x-2.
\]
Modulo $x^2+x+1$, use $x^2=-x-1$, $x^3=1$ to get
\[
(c-b)x+a-b+d=x+2.
\]
Solving gives $b=1,c=2,a=2,d=1$. Thus $R(x)=2x^3+x^2+2x+1$, and its coefficient sum is $\boxed{R(1)=6}$.
