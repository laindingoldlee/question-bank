Polynomial division gives $n^2+\dfrac{7n-15}{2n+1}$. Since $2(7n-15)=7(2n+1)-37$ and $2n+1$ is odd, this is integral exactly when $2n+1$ divides $37$. The largest signed divisor is $37$, yielding $\boxed{n=18}$.
