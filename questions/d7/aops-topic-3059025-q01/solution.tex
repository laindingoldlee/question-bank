The squares $8.055^2=64.883025$ and $8.065^2=65.044225$ show $8.055<\sqrt{65}<8.065$. Thus it lies in the interval rounding to $\boxed{8.06}$. The comparisons are valid because squaring is strictly increasing on nonnegative numbers.
