\textbf{(a)} $g(2)=7$, so $f(g(2))=f(7)=50$; and $f(2)=5$, so $g(f(2))=g(5)=13$. They are \textbf{not} the same: $\boxed{50\ne13}$.

\textbf{(b)}
\[f(g(x))=(2x+3)^2+1=\boxed{4x^2+12x+10},\qquad g(f(x))=2\left(x^2+1\right)+3=\boxed{2x^2+5}.\]
(Check with $x=2$: $16+24+10=50$ and $8+5=13$.)
