A spherical scoop of vanilla ice cream with radius $2$ inches is dropped onto the surface of a dish of hot chocolate sauce. As it melts, the ice cream spreads out uniformly, forming a cylindrical region $8$ inches in radius. Assuming the density of the ice cream stays constant, how many inches deep is the melted ice cream? Express your answer as a common fraction.
