There are $16$ integers between $1$ and $10000$ inclusive whose last four digits in base $2$ are the same as their last four digits in base $5$ (as digit strings, padded with leading zeros). Let $x$ be the sum of these $16$ numbers. What is the remainder when $x$ is divided by $10000$?
