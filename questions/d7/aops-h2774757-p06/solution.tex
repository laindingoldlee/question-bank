A right inscribed triangle has one diameter as its hypotenuse. There are $1009$ diameters and $2016$ choices for the third vertex. No triangle is counted twice. Therefore the probability is $\dfrac{1009\cdot2016}{\binom{2018}3}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{2017}}$.
