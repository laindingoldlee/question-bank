The fraction is $\dfrac {a}{b}$ with $a<b$, and each of the $\dbinom62=15$ pairs is equally likely. It cannot be reduced exactly when $\gcd(a,b)=1$. The reducible ones are those with a common factor:
\[
\dfrac{2}{4},\quad \dfrac{2}{6},\quad \dfrac{3}{6},\quad \dfrac{4}{6},
\]
so $15-4=11$ fractions are in lowest terms. The probability is
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{11}{15}}.
\]
