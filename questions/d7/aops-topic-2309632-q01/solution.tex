This is a geometric series with first term $1$, ratio $-2$ and $11$ terms, so its sum is
\[\dfrac{1-(-2)^{11}}{1-(-2)}=\dfrac{1+2048}{3}=\boxed{683}.\]
(Alternatively, pair the terms after the first: $(-2+4)+(-8+16)+\cdots+(-512+1024)=2+8+32+128+512=682$, plus $1$.)
