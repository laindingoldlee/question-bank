A down escalator has $50$ steps and moves downwards at a constant rate of $100$ steps per minute. On a stationary escalator, Maria climbs at $152$ steps per minute and Dee at $13$ steps per minute. At the same moment both step onto the bottom of the moving escalator and start walking up it at these rates. Each of them leaves the escalator on reaching either end of it. Who leaves the escalator first, and at which end? How many seconds later does the other one leave it?
