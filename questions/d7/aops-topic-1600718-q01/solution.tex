\textbf{No up moves.} The $y$-coordinate never decreases, and each up move raises it by $1$. To be back at height $b$, the token must never move up. So all $n$ moves are left or right.

\textbf{Equal numbers of lefts and rights.} The $x$-coordinate returns to $a$ exactly when the numbers of left and right moves are equal. So $n$ must be even, $n=2m$, with exactly $m$ lefts and $m$ rights in some order.

Each particular sequence of $n$ moves has probability $\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^n$, and there are $\binom{2m}{m}$ sequences of $m$ lefts and $m$ rights. Hence
\[
P(\text{back after }n\text{ moves})=\begin{cases}\dfrac{\binom{2m}{m}}{3^{2m}}, & n=2m\text{ even},\\[2mm] 0, & n\text{ odd}.\end{cases}
\]
For $n=6$: $\dfrac{\binom63}{3^6}=\boxed{\dfrac{20}{729}}$.
