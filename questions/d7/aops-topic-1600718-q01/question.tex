A token starts at the point $(a,b)$ of the coordinate plane. At each move it goes one unit up, one unit left or one unit right, each with probability $\dfrac{1}{3}$, independently of earlier moves (it never moves down). What is the probability that the token is back at $(a,b)$ after $6$ moves? More generally, find this probability after $n$ moves.
