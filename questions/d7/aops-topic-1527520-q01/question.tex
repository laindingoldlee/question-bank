Point $P$ lies outside a circle. A line through $P$ meets the circle at $B$ and $C$, with $B$ between $P$ and $C$, and another line through $P$ meets the circle at $D$ and $E$, with $D$ between $P$ and $E$. The chords have lengths $BC=5$ and $DE=9$, and the tangent segment from $P$ to the circle has length $PA=6$, where $A$ is the point of tangency. Find $BP\cdot PD$.
