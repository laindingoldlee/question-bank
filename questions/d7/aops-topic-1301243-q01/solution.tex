$x^3-27=(x-3)(x^2+3x+9)=0$. The quadratic gives $x=\dfrac{-3\pm\sqrt{9-36}}{2}=\dfrac{-3\pm3i\sqrt3}{2}$. The roots are
\[
\boxed{3,\ \dfrac{-3+3i\sqrt3}{2},\ \dfrac{-3-3i\sqrt3}{2}}.
\]
