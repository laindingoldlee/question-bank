For a positive integer $n$, the $n$th root $\sqrt[n]{a}$ of a number $a$ is a number $y$ with $y^n=a$. Explain why ``$\sqrt[0]{0}$'' is undefined: show that no number $y$ satisfies $y^0=0$.
