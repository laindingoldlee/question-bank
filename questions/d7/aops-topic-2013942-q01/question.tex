Let $ABC$ be a triangle with a right angle at $A$, and let $D$ be the foot of the altitude from $A$ to $BC$. Prove that
\[
AD^2=BD\cdot CD .
\]
(This is often called the geometric mean theorem, or the right-triangle altitude theorem.)
