Let $S(n,m)$ be the number of ways to divide $n$ distinguishable objects into $m$ indistinguishable boxes so that no box is empty (the Stirling numbers of the second kind). Find a recursive formula for $S(n,m)$, with suitable initial values, and prove it. Use it to compute $S(5,3)$.
