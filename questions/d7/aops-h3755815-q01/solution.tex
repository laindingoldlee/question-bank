The semiperimeter is $105$, so Heron's formula gives
\[
[ABC]=\sqrt{105\cdot40\cdot35\cdot30}=2100.
\]
The altitude from $A$ to $BC$ is therefore $\dfrac{2(2100)}{70}=60$.
Write $AD=t\,AB=65t$. The distance from $D$ to $BC$ is $60(1-t)$. By the focus-directrix definition, $65t=60(1-t)$, giving $t=\dfrac{12}{25}$.
Similarly, if $AE=s\,AC=75s$, then $75s=60(1-s)$, so $s=\dfrac{4}{9}$.
The included angle at $A$ is common to the two triangles, hence
\[
[ADE]=ts[ABC]=\dfrac{12}{25}\dfrac{4}{9}\cdot2100=\boxed{448}.
\]
