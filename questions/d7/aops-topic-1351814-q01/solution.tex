$180=2^2\cdot3^2\cdot5$. In a perfect cube every prime exponent is a multiple of $3$, so $N$ must supply at least one more $2$, one more $3$ and two more $5$'s. The least choice is $N=2\cdot3\cdot5^2=\boxed{150}$, and then $180\cdot150=27000=30^3$.
