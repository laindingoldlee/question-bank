Let $x=a^{2^n}$, $y=2^{2^n}$, $A=x+y$ and $B=a^{2^m}+2^{2^m}=x^{2^{m-n}}+y^{2^{m-n}}$. Modulo $A$ we have $x\equiv-y$, so $x^2\equiv y^2$ and hence $x^{2^{m-n}}\equiv y^{2^{m-n}}$ (as $m-n\ge1$). Therefore
\[
B\equiv2y^{2^{m-n}}=2^{1+2^m}\pmod A .
\]
So $B-2^{1+2^m}$ is a multiple of $A$, and any common divisor $d$ of $A$ and $B$ divides $B-\left(B-2^{1+2^m}\right)=2^{1+2^m}$; thus $d$ is a power of $2$. But $A=a^{2^n}+2^{2^n}$ is odd (odd plus even). Hence $d=1$, and the greatest common divisor is $\boxed{1}$.
