Put the $12$-gon in a circle of radius $1$. A segment joining two vertices that are $d$ steps apart ($d=1,\dots,6$) subtends a central angle of $30d^\circ$, so its length is $\ell_d=2\sin(15d^\circ)$:
\[\ell_1=\dfrac{\sqrt6-\sqrt2}{2}\approx0.518,\quad\ell_2=1,\quad\ell_3=\sqrt2,\quad\ell_4=\sqrt3,\quad\ell_5=\dfrac{\sqrt6+\sqrt2}{2}\approx1.932,\quad\ell_6=2.\]

usepackage("amsmath");

defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));

draw(V[0]--V[1]--V[2]--V[3]--V[4]--V[5]--V[6]--V[7]--V[8]--V[9]--V[10]--V[11]--cycle,gray);
pen[] pc={red,orange,heavygreen,blue,purple,black};
for(int d=1;d<=6;++d){ draw(V[0]--V[d],pc[d-1]+linewidth(0.9)); label("$\ell_"+string(d)+"$",V[0]+0.8*(V[d]-V[0]),rotate(90)*unit(V[d]-V[0]),pc[d-1]+fontsize(8pt)); }
for(int k=0;k<12;++k) dot(V[k]);

There are $12$ segments of each length $\ell_1,\dots,\ell_5$ and $6$ diameters of length $\ell_6$, in total $\dbinom{12}{2}=66$, so there are $\dbinom{66}{3}=45760$ equally likely choices.

\textbf{Bad choices.} Three lengths $\ell_a\le\ell_b\le\ell_c$ fail to form a triangle with positive area exactly when $\ell_a+\ell_b\le\ell_c$. Note the two equalities
\[\ell_1+\ell_3=\dfrac{\sqrt6-\sqrt2}{2}+\sqrt2=\dfrac{\sqrt6+\sqrt2}{2}=\ell_5,\qquad\ell_2+\ell_2=2=\ell_6.\]
Using the values above, the failing multisets of steps are:
\begin{itemize}
\item $\{1,1,c\}$ with $c=3,4,5,6$ (as $2\ell_1\approx1.035$ exceeds $\ell_2$ but not $\ell_3$): $\dbinom{12}{2}\cdot12\cdot3+\dbinom{12}{2}\cdot6=2376+396=2772$ choices;
\item $\{1,2,c\}$ with $c=4,5,6$ (as $\ell_1+\ell_2\approx1.518$ lies between $\ell_3$ and $\ell_4$): $12\cdot12\cdot(12+12+6)=4320$;
\item $\{1,3,c\}$ with $c=5,6$ (equality for $c=5$): $12\cdot12\cdot(12+6)=2592$;
\item $\{2,2,6\}$ (equality): $\dbinom{12}{2}\cdot6=396$.
\end{itemize}
All other multisets work: $\ell_1+\ell_4\approx2.25$, $\ell_2+\ell_3\approx2.41$ and every larger pair sum exceed $\ell_6=2$, the largest length. So there are $2772+4320+2592+396=10080$ bad choices, and the probability is
\[1-\dfrac{10080}{45760}=\dfrac{35680}{45760}=\boxed{\textbf{(E) }\dfrac{223}{286}}.\]
