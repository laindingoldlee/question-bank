If any digit is 0,3,6,9, the digit product is divisible by 3. Otherwise the six distinct digits must be exactly 1,2,4,5,7,8. Their sum is 27, so $\overline{HAGRID}$ is divisible by 3. In either case the whole product is divisible by 3.
