Let the numbers be $n,n+1,\dots,n+10$, with sum $11n+55$, and let $n+k$ ($0\le k\le10$) be erased. Then
\[11n+55-(n+k)=2018\iff10n=1963+k.\]
So $1963+k$ is a multiple of $10$, which for $0\le k\le10$ forces $k=7$ and $n=197$. The erased number is $197+7=\boxed{204}$. (Check: $197+\cdots+207=2222$ and $2222-204=2018$.)
