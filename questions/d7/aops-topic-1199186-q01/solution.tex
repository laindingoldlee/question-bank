Two numbers have a square product exactly when they have the same squarefree part. Group $1,\dots,16$ by squarefree part:
\[\{1,4,9,16\},\ \{2,8\},\ \{3,12\},\ \{5\},\{6\},\{7\},\{10\},\{11\},\{13\},\{14\},\{15\}.\]
That is $11$ groups. A collection with no square product has at most one number from each group, so at most $11$ numbers, and $11$ is achieved by taking one from each. So she can draw at most $\boxed{11}$ slips (the $12$th always creates a square product).
