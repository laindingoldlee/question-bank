Drini invented the following game:

a) Take a $4$-digit number $n$.

b) Next, consider the smallest and largest numbers that can be obtained by permuting these digits. Call them $m$ and $M$ respectively.

c) Calculate the difference $M - m$.

d) Repeat the entire process with $n$ = $M - m$.

Show that this game eventually stops: that is, it reaches a number that does not change when applying the rules of the game.
