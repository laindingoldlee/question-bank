Keep four digits at every step, padding with leading zeros. If the descending digits are a,b,c,d, the subtraction is $999(a-d)+90(b-c)$. Set u=a-d and v=b-c, so $0\le v\le u\le9$. Therefore after one step there are only 55 possible states $999u+90v$. Applying the digit-sorting map to these 55 states gives two states already fixed, three reaching a fixed point in one further step, and respectively 12,10,10,10,8 reaching one in two through six further steps. The fixed points are 0000 and 6174. The included exact program checks this finite list and all 10000 four-digit strings, detecting any cycle before claiming termination. Thus every start reaches a fixed point in at most seven steps.
