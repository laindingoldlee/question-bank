The eighth root floor must be at least $2$; if it were $1$, then $n<256$, so the seventh and sixth root floors could not both exceed it and each other (the sixth root floor would need to be at least $3$, requiring $n\ge729$). More directly, if the eighth root floor is $1$, distinctness requires the seventh and sixth floors at least $2,3$, contradicting $n<256$. Thus the eighth floor is at least $2$, forcing the seventh and sixth at least $3,4$. Hence $n\ge4^6=4096$. At $4096$, the eight values are $4096,64,16,8,5,4,3,2$, all distinct. The digit sum is $4+0+9+6=\boxed{19}$.
