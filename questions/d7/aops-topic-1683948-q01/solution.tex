Any three of the nine points form a triangle unless they are collinear. There are $\dbinom93=84$ ways to choose three points. The collinear triples lie on the $3$ rows, the $3$ columns and the $2$ main diagonals, one triple on each, $8$ in all (every other line through two grid points contains only those two points). So there are
\[
84-8=\boxed{76}
\]
triangles.
