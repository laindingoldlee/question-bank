Tamika's three equally likely sums are $8+9=17$, $8+10=18$, $9+10=19$. Carlos' three equally likely products are $3\cdot5=15$, $3\cdot6=18$, $5\cdot6=30$. The two choices are independent, so the $3\cdot3=9$ pairs are equally likely. Tamika's result is larger in exactly these pairs:
\[
(17,15),\quad(18,15),\quad(19,15),\quad(19,18).
\]
(No sum beats $30$, and $18$ only ties $18$.) The probability is $\boxed{\text{(A)}\ \dfrac{4}{9}}$.
