Sean writes the infinite geometric series
\[
\dfrac{1}{8}+\dfrac{1}{8^2}+\dfrac{1}{8^3}+\cdots ,
\]
and Tom writes the finite geometric series
\[
i^{-100}+i^{-99}+i^{-98}+\cdots+i^{99}+i^{100},
\]
where $i^2=-1$. What is the sum of Sean's series and Tom's series?
