The units digit in base $6$ is the remainder modulo $6$. Now
\[
12\equiv0,\qquad 13\equiv1,\qquad 17\equiv-1\pmod6 ,
\]
so
\[
12^{12}+13^{13}+17^{17}\equiv0+1+(-1)^{17}=0\pmod6 .
\]
The units digit is $\boxed{0}$.
