Choose $B=(0,0)$, $C=(9,0)$, and take the quadrilateral above $BC$. The given lengths and angles give $A=(6,2\sqrt3)$ and $D=(9,\sqrt3)$. Direction vectors for the diagonals are
\[
u=C-A=(3,-2\sqrt3),\qquad v=D-B=(9,\sqrt3).
\]
Their dot product is $21$ and the absolute two-dimensional cross product is $21\sqrt3$. Since the dot product is positive, the angle between these directions is acute, and its tangent is $\sqrt3$. Hence the acute diagonal angle is $\boxed{60^\circ}$.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair B=(0,0),C=(9,0),A=(6,2sqrt(3)),D=(9,sqrt(3));draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C,blue);draw(B--D,red);label("$A$",A,(0,1));label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,(1,0));
\end{asy}
