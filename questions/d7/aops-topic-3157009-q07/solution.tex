For roots $ r,s $, Vieta gives $ r+s=-a $ and $ rs=a $, hence $(r+1)(s+1)=1 $. Integer factor pairs of 1 are $(1,1)$ and $(-1,-1)$, giving roots $(0,0)$ or $(-2,-2)$. Thus $\boxed{a=0\text{ or }4}$; both work.
