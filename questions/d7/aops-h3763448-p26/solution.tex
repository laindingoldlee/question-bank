Use column carries in $2\overline{DRAMA}=\overline{TEATR}$. If the carry from tens to hundreds is $c_2$ and the next carry $c_3$, the hundreds column gives $A+c_2=10c_3$. Distinctness excludes $A=0$ because the units column would give $R=0$ too. Thus $A=9,c_2=c_3=1$, and the units column gives $R=8$ and carry 1. The thousands column gives $E=7$ and carry 1. The tens column gives $T=2M-9$, while the leading column gives $T=2D+1$. The only available possibilities for $M$ are 5 and 6 (7,8,9 are already used); $M=5$ would force $D=0$, so $M=6,T=3,D=1$. Indeed $18969+18969=37938$.
The available digits for $I,N,S$ are $0,2,4,5$, with $I,S\ne0$. For each ordered choice of $I,S$, choose the largest unused $N$, since the product increases with $N$. The six possible maxima are
