Complete the square: $x^2+bx+c=\left(x+\dfrac{b}{2}\right)^2+c-\dfrac{b^2}{4}$. The square is nonnegative and is zero exactly at $x=-\dfrac{b}{2}$. Thus the minimum is $\boxed{c-\dfrac{b^2}{4}}$.
