Let $M=(t,t^3)$ with $0\le t\le1$. By the shoelace formula, the triangle with vertices $(0,0)$, $(1,1)$, $(t,t^3)$ has area
\[
\dfrac{1}{2}\left|1\cdot t^3-1\cdot t\right|=\dfrac{t-t^3}{2}=\dfrac{t\left(1-t^2\right)}{2},
\]
since $t^3\le t$ on $[0,1]$.

\textbf{Maximising $t(1-t^2)$.} By AM-GM applied to the three nonnegative numbers $2t^2$, $1-t^2$, $1-t^2$, whose sum is $2$,
\[
2t^2\left(1-t^2\right)^2\le\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^3=\dfrac{8}{27},
\qquad\text{so}\qquad t^2\left(1-t^2\right)^2\le\dfrac{4}{27},\quad t\left(1-t^2\right)\le\dfrac{2}{3\sqrt3},
\]
with equality when $2t^2=1-t^2$, i.e.\ $t=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}$. (With calculus: the derivative $1-3t^2$ vanishes at $t=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}$.)

So the area is greatest for
\[
\boxed{M=\left(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3},\dfrac{\sqrt3}{9}\right)},\qquad\text{with area } \dfrac{1}{3\sqrt3}=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt3}{9}} .
\]
Geometrically, $M$ is the point where the tangent to the curve (slope $3t^2=1$, dashed) is parallel to the side from $(0,0)$ to $(1,1)$, so $M$ is as far as possible from that side.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
real f(real x){return x^3;}
real t=1/sqrt(3);pair O=(0,0),B=(1,1),M=(t,t^3);
fill(O--B--M--cycle,palegray);
draw((0,0)--(1.25,0),gray,Arrow(TeXHead));draw((0,0)--(0,1.25),gray,Arrow(TeXHead));
label("$x$",(1.25,0),E,gray);label("$y$",(0,1.25),N,gray);
draw(graph(f,0,1),linewidth(0.9));
draw(O--B--M--cycle);
draw(M-0.3*(1,1)--M+0.55*(1,1),blue+dashed);
dot(O);dot(B);dot(M);
label("$O$",O,SW);label("$(1,1)$",B,E);label("$M$",M,SE);
label("$y=x^3$",(0.95,0.857),E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
