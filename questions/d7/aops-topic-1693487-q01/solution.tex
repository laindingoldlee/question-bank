Rearrange: $y^3-x^3=19(y-x)$, i.e.\ $(y-x)(y^2+xy+x^2)=19(y-x)$. Since $x<y$, divide by $y-x$:
\[
x^2+xy+y^2=19 .
\]
With $1\le x<y$: $y\ge2$, and $y^2<19$ gives $y\le4$. Trying $y=2$: $x^2+2x+4=19$ has no positive integer root; $y=3$: $x^2+3x+9=19$, $x^2+3x-10=0$, $x=2$; $y=4$: $x^2+4x+16=19$, $x^2+4x-3=0$, no integer root. So $(x,y)=\boxed{(2,3)}$. (Check: $8+57=65=27+38$.)
