Let $S$ be the sum. Then $\dfrac {S}{5}=\dfrac{1}{5^2}+\dfrac{2}{5^3}+\cdots$, and subtracting,
\[S-\dfrac {S}{5}=\dfrac{1}{5}+\dfrac{1}{5^2}+\dfrac{1}{5^3}+\cdots=\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{5}}{1-\dfrac{1}{5}}=\dfrac{1}{4}.\]
So $\dfrac{4S}{5}=\dfrac{1}{4}$ and $S=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{16}}$.
