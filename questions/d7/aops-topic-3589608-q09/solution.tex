If the inradius is $r$, the altitude is $3r$ and the side is $2\sqrt3r$. Thus the perimeter is $6\sqrt3r$. Equating this with $\pi r^2$ and dividing by $r>0$ gives $\boxed{r=\dfrac{6\sqrt3}{\pi}}$.
\begin{asy}
import geometry;
size(240);
pair A=(0,sqrt(3)),B=(-1,0),C=(1,0),O=(0,sqrt(3)/3);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(circle(O,sqrt(3)/3));draw(O--(0,0),dashed);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$r$",(0,.3),E);
\end{asy}
