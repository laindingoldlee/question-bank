Call a perfect square $x$ an \emph{MLG number} if there is a positive integer $y$ such that $x-y$ and $x+y$ are both prime. For example, $4$ is an MLG number, since $4-1=3$ and $4+1=5$ are prime. Find the smallest MLG number greater than $4$.
