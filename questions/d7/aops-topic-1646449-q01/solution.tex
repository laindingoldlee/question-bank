The next perfect square after $4$ is $9$. Taking $y=2$ gives $9-2=7$ and $9+2=11$, both prime. So $9$ is an MLG number, and since there is no perfect square strictly between $4$ and $9$, the smallest MLG number greater than $4$ is $\boxed{9}$.
