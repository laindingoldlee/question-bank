\[\begin{array}{ccc}&A&B\\+&C&A\\\hline&D&A\end{array}\qquad\text{and}\qquad\begin{array}{ccc}&A&B\\-&C&A\\\hline&&A\end{array}\]
what digit does $D$ represent?
