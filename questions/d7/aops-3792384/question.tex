Let $A_1A_2\dots A_{11}$ be a regular hendecagon on the plane. A diagonal $A_iA_j$ is called icey if $i + j$ is odd. A pair of diagonals is called cold if they don't intersect (here we consider $A_1A_2$ and $A_1A_3$ to be intersecting).



Jose picks a pair of distinct diagonals at random. The probability the pair is a cold pair of icey diagonals is $\dfrac{p}{q}$, where $p$ and $q$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $p+q$.



Note: A hendecagon is a polygon with 11 sides.
