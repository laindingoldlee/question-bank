Represent the successive displacements by complex numbers. They form a geometric series with first term $8$ and ratio $3\dfrac{i}{4}$, whose modulus is less than $1$. The limiting point is
\[
z=\dfrac{8}{1-3\dfrac{i}{4}}=\dfrac{128+96i}{25}.
\]
Its distance from the origin is $|z|=\dfrac{8}{|1-\dfrac{3i}{4}|}=\dfrac{8}{(\dfrac{5}{4})}=\boxed{\dfrac{32}{5}}$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair P=(0,0),v=(8,0);path g=P;for(int j=0;j<18;++j){P+=v;g=g--P;v=.75*(rotate(90)*v);}draw(g,blue);pair M=(128.0/25,96.0/25);dot(M,red);draw((0,0)--M,dashed);label("$O$",(0,0),SW);draw(M--(6.5,4.8),gray);label("$M$",(6.5,4.8),NE);
\end{asy}
