Sums of $8$s and $12$s are $4(2a+3b)$: exactly the multiples of $4$ other than $4$ itself.

\emph{$49$ is impossible:} using $k$ packages of $15$, $49-15k\in\{49,34,19,4\}$, and none is a multiple of $4$ other than $4$.

\emph{$50$ to $57$ are possible:} $50=30+12+8$, $51=15+36$, $52=40+12$, $53=45+8$, $54=30+24$, $55=15+40$, $56=56$, $57=45+12$. Adding packages of $8$ then reaches every larger number.

The answer is $\boxed{49}$.
