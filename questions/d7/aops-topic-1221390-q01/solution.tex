The leading digits should be the largest, $5$ and $4$, and the product is larger with a three-digit and a two-digit factor than with a four-digit and a one-digit one (e.g. $5321\cdot4=21284$). For $\overline{5xy}\cdot\overline{4z}$ and $\overline{4xy}\cdot\overline{5z}$ with $\{x,y,z\}=\{1,2,3\}$, the largest candidates are
\[431\cdot52=22412,\quad 521\cdot43=22403,\quad 531\cdot42=22302,\quad 432\cdot51=22032.\]
The maximum is $\boxed{22412}=431\cdot52$ (confirmed by checking all $5!\cdot4$ splits).
