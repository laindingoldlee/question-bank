Let $A_3$, $A_4$, $A_5$ be the sets of multiples of $3$, $4$, $5$ in $\{1,\dots,1000\}$. Then
\[|A_3|=333,\quad|A_4|=250,\quad|A_5|=200,\quad |A_3\cap A_4|=\left\lfloor\dfrac{1000}{12}\right\rfloor=83,\quad|A_3\cap A_5|=\left\lfloor\dfrac{1000}{15}\right\rfloor=66,\]
\[|A_4\cap A_5|=\left\lfloor\dfrac{1000}{20}\right\rfloor=50,\qquad|A_3\cap A_4\cap A_5|=\left\lfloor\dfrac{1000}{60}\right\rfloor=16 .\]
By inclusion-exclusion the answer is $333+250+200-83-66-50+16=\boxed{600}$.
