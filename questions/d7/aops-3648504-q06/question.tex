Professor Gupta is teaching the prime numbers at the Eratosthenes School of Mathematics. He writes all the numbers from $500$ to $1000$ on the board. Then, Professor Gupta asks each prime number, in ascending order, to come up to the board and erase all the numbers it divides. Two goes first and erases all the even numbers. Three goes second and erases all the remaining numbers divisible by three, and so on. Who is the first prime that cannot erase any numbers?
