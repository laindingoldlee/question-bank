A number survives until prime $p$ precisely when its least prime divisor is $p$. Every prime through 31 erases a number: $2,3,5,7,11,13,17,19$ can use respectively $512,729,625,539,517,533,527,589$, and $23,29,31$ use their squares $529,841,961$. For 37, any multiple in the interval is $37k$ with $k<37$, so it already has a smaller prime factor; 37 itself is below 500. Thus the first prime unable to erase anything is $\boxed{37}$.
