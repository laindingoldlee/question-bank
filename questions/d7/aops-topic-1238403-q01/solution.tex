Modulo $2016$, the chosen numbers cover each even residue $0,2,\dots,2014$ exactly twice. A pair is happy when its residues $r,s$ satisfy $r+s\equiv4\pmod{2016}$. The residue $r$ pairs with $4-r$; this equals $r$ for $r=2$ and $r=1010$. The other $1006$ even residues form $503$ pairs $\{r,4-r\}$, each giving $2\cdot2=4$ happy pairs of numbers; the residues $2$ and $1010$ give $1$ pair each ($\{2,2018\}$ and $\{1010,3026\}$). That is $2012+2=2014$ happy pairs out of $\dbinom{2016}{2}=2031120$:
\[\dfrac{2014}{2031120}=\boxed{\dfrac{1007}{1015560}}.\]
