Parallelogram $ABCD$ has height $x$, and diagonal $AC$ has length $13$. The altitude from $A$ meets side $DC$ at $O$, and $y=OC$. The base $DC$ of the parallelogram has length $x+y$. Given that $x$ and $y$ are positive integers with $x<y$, find the area of the parallelogram.
