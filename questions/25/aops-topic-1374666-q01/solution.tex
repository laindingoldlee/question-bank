The word has the $8$ letters M, O, O, N, D, U, S, T: seven different letters, with O appearing twice.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Three different letters:} choose and order $3$ of the $7$ different letters: $7\cdot6\cdot5=210$.
\item \textbf{Two O's and one other letter:} choose the other letter ($6$ ways) and its position among the three places ($3$ ways): $18$.
\end{itemize}
Three O's are impossible. The total is $210+18=\boxed{228}$.
