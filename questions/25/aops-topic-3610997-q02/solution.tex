For coordinates $A=(0,0)$, $B=(w,0)$, $C=(w,h)$, $D=(0,h)$ and $E=(x,y)$, expanding squared distances shows $AE^2+CE^2=BE^2+DE^2$. Hence $DE^2=49+64-25=88$ and $\boxed{DE=2\sqrt{22}}$.
\begin{asy}
import geometry; size(220);
pair A=(0,0),B=(5,0),C=(5,4),D=(0,4),P=(3,1.5);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--P--C,dashed);draw(B--P--D,dashed);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$E$",P,N);
\end{asy}
