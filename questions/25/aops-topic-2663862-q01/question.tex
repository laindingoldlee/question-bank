Let $A$, $B$, $C$ be three distinct points in $n$-dimensional space ($n\ge2$) such that $\angle ABC=\angle ACB$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show by an example that $AB=AC$ need not hold.
\item[(b)] Prove that $AB=AC$ whenever $A$, $B$, $C$ are not collinear.
\end{enumerate}
