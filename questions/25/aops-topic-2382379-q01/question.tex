Maya has $12$ identical chips and feeds all of them to $3$ identical ducks. Since the ducks are identical, only the numbers of chips matter, not which duck gets which number; for example, giving $3$, $4$, $5$ chips is the same as giving $4$, $3$, $5$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] In how many ways can she do this if a duck may get no chips?
\item[(b)] In how many ways if every duck gets at least one chip?
\end{enumerate}
