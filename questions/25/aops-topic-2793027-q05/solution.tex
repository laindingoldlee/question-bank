The altitude is $3\sqrt3$ and the circumcenter lies two-thirds of the way from a vertex to the opposite side. Hence $R=2\sqrt3$ and the circle area is $\boxed{12\pi}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=2sqrt(3)*dir(90),B=2sqrt(3)*dir(210),C=2sqrt(3)*dir(330);draw(circle((0,0),2sqrt(3)));draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw((0,0)--A,dashed);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$O$",(0,0),W);
\end{asy}
