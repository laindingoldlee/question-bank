Write the numbers as $m-3,m-2,\dots,m+3$. The third, fourth and fifth add up to $3m$, and all seven add up to $7m$. So we need
\[
3m=s^2\qquad\text{and}\qquad7m=t^3
\]
for positive integers $s,t$.

\textbf{Exponents of $3$ and $7$.} Write $m=3^x7^yk$ with $k$ coprime to $21$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $3m=3^{x+1}7^yk$ is a square: $x+1$ is even and $y$ is even.
\item $7m=3^x7^{y+1}k$ is a cube: $x$ is a multiple of $3$ and $y+1$ is a multiple of $3$.
\end{itemize}
So $x$ is odd and a multiple of $3$, hence $x\ge3$; and $y$ is even with $y\equiv2\pmod3$, hence $y\ge2$. The smallest choice is $x=3$, $y=2$, $k=1$:
\[
m=3^3\cdot7^2=27\cdot49=\boxed{1323}.
\]
Check: $3m=3969=63^2$ and $7m=9261=21^3$. (The numbers are $1320,1321,\dots,1326$.)
