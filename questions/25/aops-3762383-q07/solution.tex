There must be seven increments of 7 and three of 6. The gcd test for a step $d$ is $\gcd(a_i,d)=1$. Enumerate the $\binom{10}3=120$ positions of the three six-steps and discard a sequence whenever a tested step has a common divisor. Exactly six valid sequences start with 6 and six start with 7. Equivalently, a prefix recurrence extends a partial sum by 6 or 7 only when the gcd test passes, tracking the number of each step; it gives $\boxed{12}$.
