If $T$ is the inner tangency point and $O$ the common centre, right triangle $OTP$ has $OT=1,OP=2$, hence $\angle OPT=30^\circ$. The outer tangent is perpendicular to $OP$, so its acute angle with $PT$ is 60 degrees. The obtuse angle is $\boxed{120^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair O=(0,0),P=(2,0),T=(.5,sqrt(3)/2);draw(circle(O,1));draw(circle(O,2));draw((2,-2.3)--(2,2.3));draw(P--(P+1.5*(T-P)));draw(O--P^^O--T,dashed);label("$O$",O,S);label("$P$",P,SE);label("$T$",T,N);
\end{asy}
