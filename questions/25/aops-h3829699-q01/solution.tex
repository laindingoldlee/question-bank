Reflexivity forces all $2026$ pairs $(a,a)$. Every remaining pair belongs to a two-element block $\{(a,b),(b,a)\}$ with $a\ne b$, and symmetry requires selecting the whole block or neither member. There are $\binom{2026}{2}=2051325$ available blocks.

The number to select is
\[
\dfrac{1013^2+1013-2-2026}{2}=512577.
\]
Every such block selection gives one relation, so the answer is
\[
\boxed{\binom{2051325}{512577}}.
\]
