In square $ABCD$ with $AB=1$ (vertices in order), point $M$ is the midpoint of $AB$, point $T$ is on $BC$ such that $TB=2TC$, and point $H$ is on $CD$ such that $HC=3HD$. What is the area of the convex quadrilateral with vertices $A$, $M$, $T$, $H$?

(a) $\dfrac{1}{2}$\quad (b) $\dfrac{8}{15}$\quad (c) $\dfrac{7}{12}$\quad (d) $\dfrac{3}{5}$\quad (e) $\dfrac{2}{3}$
