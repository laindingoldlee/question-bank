Let the current speed be $v$, with $0<v<12$. The boat's travel time is $\dfrac{27}{12-v}$ hours. The raft travels 17 km in that time plus $\dfrac{8}{3}$ hours, so $v(\dfrac{8}{3}+\dfrac{27}{12-v})=17$. Clearing denominators gives $2v^2-57v+153=0$, with roots $3$ and $\dfrac{51}{2}$. Only $3$ is less than 12, so $\boxed{v=3\text{ kilometres per hour}}$. Indeed the boat takes 3 hours and the raft travels $3(3+\dfrac{8}{3})=17$ km.
