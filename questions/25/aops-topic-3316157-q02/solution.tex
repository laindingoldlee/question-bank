Let $BC=k$. The strict triangle inequalities give $3\le k\le21$. The angle-bisector theorem gives $BD=\dfrac{10}{22}k=\dfrac{5k}{11}$. Its integrality forces $11\mid k$, so $k=11$ and $\boxed{BD=5}$.
\begin{asy}
import geometry;
usepackage("amsmath");

pair B=(0,0),C=(11,0),A=(7/2.0,sqrt(100-49/4.0)),D=(5,0);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--D);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,S);label("$10$",(A+B)/2,W);label("$12$",(A+C)/2,E);
\end{asy}
