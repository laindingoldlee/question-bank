The exponent of $2$ in $21!$ is $\left\lfloor\dfrac{21}{2}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{21}{4}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{21}{8}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{21}{16}\right\rfloor=10+5+2+1=18$, so $21!=2^{18}m$ with $m$ odd. Every divisor is $2^jd$ with $0\le j\le18$ and $d\mid m$, so there are $19\,\tau(m)$ divisors, of which the $\tau(m)$ with $j=0$ are odd. The probability is $\boxed{\dfrac1{19}}$.
