In a pinball game the first shot is worth $1$ million points, and every additional shot scores $500{,}000$ more points than the one before, so the second shot is worth $1.5$ million points, the third $2$ million, and so on. Grogg makes just enough shots to score a total of at least $100$ million points, then walks away. How many points was Grogg's final shot worth?
