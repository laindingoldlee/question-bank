The conditions modulo $3$ and $7$ say $x\equiv2\pmod{21}$, so $x\in\{2,23,44,\ldots\}$; the first of these that is $\equiv3\pmod5$ is $23$. By the Chinese remainder theorem the solutions are exactly $x\equiv23\pmod{105}$. We need $23+105k\le2020$, i.e. $k\le\dfrac{1997}{105}\approx19.02$, so $k=0,1,\dots,19$: $\boxed{\textbf{(E) }20}$ integers.
