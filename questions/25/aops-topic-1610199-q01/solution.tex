There are $2^5=32$ equally likely sequences. Those with exactly $2$ heads are determined by choosing which $2$ of the $5$ flips are heads: $\binom52=10$. The probability is $\dfrac{10}{32}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{16}}$.
