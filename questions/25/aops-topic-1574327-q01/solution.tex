For positive $a$ and $b$, compare $\dfrac {a}{b}$ with $\dfrac{a+1}{b+1}$ by cross-multiplying (both denominators are positive):
\[
\dfrac{a+1}{b+1}>\dfrac {a}{b}\iff b(a+1)>a(b+1)\iff b>a .
\]
Here $a=12345<b=54321$, so adding $1$ to the numerator and the denominator increases the fraction:
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{12346}{54322}>\dfrac{12345}{54321}}.
\]
Directly: $12346\cdot54321-12345\cdot54322=54321-12345=41976>0$. (Intuitively, a fraction less than $1$ moves towards $1$ when the same positive number is added to its numerator and denominator.)
