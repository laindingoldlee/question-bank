Number the days $1,2,3,\dots$ starting with the first day of work.
\begin{itemize}
\item Adam rests on days $4,8,12,\dots$: the multiples of $4$.
\item Ben rests on days $8,9,10,18,19,20,\dots$: the days $\equiv8,9,0\pmod{10}$.
\end{itemize}
A common rest day is even, so it is not $\equiv9\pmod{10}$. Multiples of $4$ that are $\equiv0\pmod{10}$ are the multiples of $20$; multiples of $4$ that are $\equiv8\pmod{10}$ are the days $\equiv8\pmod{20}$. In days $1$ to $100$: $20,40,60,80,100$ and $8,28,48,68,88$, so they both rest on $\boxed{10}$ days.
