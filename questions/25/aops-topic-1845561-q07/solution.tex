The side of the square is $8$. Place $A=(0,0)$, $B=(8,0)$, $C=(8,8)$, $D=(0,8)$; then $O=(4,4)$ and $M=(4,0)$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(8,0),C=(8,8),D=(0,8),O=(4,4),M=(4,0);
fill(M--O--D--A--cycle,rgb(0.8,0.88,1));
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(1));draw(A--C,gray(0.5));draw(B--D,gray(0.5));
draw(M--O,blue+linewidth(1));draw(O--D,blue+linewidth(1));
dot(O);dot(M);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$O$",O,E);label("$M$",M,S);
label("$4$",(M+O)/2,E,blue);label("$8$",(A+D)/2,W);label("$4$",(A+M)/2,S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

In $MODA$ the sides $MO$ (from $(4,0)$ to $(4,4)$) and $AD$ (from $(0,0)$ to $(0,8)$) are both vertical, so they are the parallel sides, with lengths $4$ and $8$, and the distance between them is $AM=4$. The area is
\[
\dfrac{4+8}{2}\cdot4=\boxed{24}
\]
square units. (Equivalently: triangle $AOD$ is a quarter of the square, $16$, and triangle $AMO$ is an eighth, $8$.)
