A convex cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$ has consecutive side lengths $50,78,104,120$. A square has one side on a side of the quadrilateral, lies inside the circumcircle and outside the quadrilateral, and has its other two vertices on the circle. The largest such square has area $a(b-\sqrt c)$, with $a,b,c$ positive integers and $c$ squarefree. Find $a+b+c$.
