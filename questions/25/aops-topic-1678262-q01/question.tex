A teacher wrote a large positive integer $N$ on the board. Thirty students then spoke in turn: for $k=1,2,\dots,30$, the $k$-th student said ``$N$ is divisible by $k+1$.'' The teacher said that exactly two of the students were wrong, and that these two spoke consecutively. Which two students were wrong?
