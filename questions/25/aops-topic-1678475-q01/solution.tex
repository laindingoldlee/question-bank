A number is divisible by each of $16=2^4$, $18=2\cdot3^2$, $24=2^3\cdot3$ and $30=2\cdot3\cdot5$ exactly when it is divisible by their least common multiple, which takes the highest power of each prime:
\[
\operatorname{lcm}(16,18,24,30)=2^4\cdot3^2\cdot5=720 .
\]
The least five-digit number is $10000$, and $10000=13\cdot720+640$, so $13\cdot720=9360$ has only four digits and the next multiple is $14\cdot720=\boxed{10080}$.
