One hat contains balls numbered $1$ through $5$, and another hat contains balls numbered $16$ through $25$. I first choose a hat, and then draw $2$ balls from that hat, one after the other, without replacement. How many different ordered selections of $2$ balls are possible? (``Ball $1$ then ball $2$'' is different from ``ball $2$ then ball $1$''.)
