The two hats contain different balls, so the selections from them are different and we add (addition principle).
\begin{itemize}
\item First hat ($5$ balls): $5\cdot4=20$ ordered selections.
\item Second hat ($10$ balls, numbered $16$ to $25$): $10\cdot9=90$ ordered selections.
\end{itemize}
The total is $20+90=\boxed{110}$.
