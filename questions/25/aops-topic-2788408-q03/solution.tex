Write $n=2^a3^b5^c$ (no other primes are needed). The conditions on the exponents are:
\begin{itemize}
\item $a-1$ even, $a$ divisible by $3$ and $5$: smallest $a=15$;
\item $b$ even, $b-1$ divisible by $3$, $b$ divisible by $5$: smallest $b=10$;
\item $c$ even, $c$ divisible by $3$, $c-1$ divisible by $5$: smallest $c=6$.
\end{itemize}
So $n=\boxed{2^{15}\cdot3^{10}\cdot5^6}$.
