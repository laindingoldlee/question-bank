Order them temporarily as $ a\le b\le c $. Then $ a\ne1 $ and $ 1\le\dfrac{3}{a}$ gives $ a\le3 $. If $ a=3 $, all denominators are at least three, so equality forces $ a=b=c=3 $. If $ a=2 $, then $(b-2)(c-2)=4 $, whose ordered positive factor pairs give $(b,c)=(3,6)$ or $(4,4)$. Thus the solutions are $\boxed{(2,3,6),(2,4,4),(3,3,3)\text{ and all their permutations}}$.
