By Heron's formula ($s=21$) the area is $K=\sqrt{21\cdot8\cdot7\cdot6}=84$. So the inradius is $r=\dfrac Ks=\dfrac{84}{21}=4$, and the circumradius is $R=\dfrac{abc}{4K}=\dfrac{2730}{336}=\dfrac{65}{8}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0), C=(14,0), A=(5,12);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
label("$A$",A,N); label("$B$",B,SW); label("$C$",C,SE);
label("$13$",(A+B)/2,W); label("$15$",(A+C)/2,NE); label("$14$",(4,0),S);
pair I=(14*A+15*B+13*C)/42; draw(circle(I,4),blue); draw(I--(I.x,0),blue); dot(I); label("$I$",I,NW,blue); label("$4$",(I.x,2),E,blue);
pair Oc=(7,33/8); draw(circle(Oc,65/8),red); draw(Oc--A,red+dashed); dot(Oc); label("$O$",Oc,NE,red); label("$\dfrac{65}{8}$",(Oc+A)/2,W,red);
\end{asy}

The difference of the circumferences is
\[2\pi R-2\pi r=2\pi\left(\dfrac{65}{8}-4\right)=2\pi\cdot\dfrac{33}{8}=\boxed{\dfrac{33\pi}{4}}\approx25.9.\]
