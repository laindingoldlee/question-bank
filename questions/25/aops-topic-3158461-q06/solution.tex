Write $ P(x)=(x-r)(x-s)$. The coefficient sum is $ P(1)=(r-1)(s-1)=2016 $, so both roots exceed one. Since $ 2016=2^5\cdot3^2\cdot7 $ has 36 divisors and is not a square, it has 18 unordered positive factor pairs. Each pair uniquely gives an unordered root pair and polynomial, so $\boxed{18}$.
