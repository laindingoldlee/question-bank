Imagine you are a contestant on a game show. The host, Monty Hall, shows you three closed doors: Behind one door is a brand-new luxury car (the prize you want).Behind the other two doors are goats (dud prizes).The game happens in three steps: You choose one door (for example, Door \#1). You do not open it yet. Monty opens one of the remaining doors (e.g., Door \#3) and reveals a goat. Monty knows exactly what is behind every door, and he always chooses to open a door that hides a goat. Monty then turns to you and asks a famous question: "Do you want to switch your choice to Door \#2?"Question: Is it to your advantage to switch your choice, or does it not matter? What is the probability of winning if you switch versus if you stay?
