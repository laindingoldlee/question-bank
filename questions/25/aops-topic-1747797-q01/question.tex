Consider $P=(x-y+1)(y-z+2)(z-x+3)$ for real numbers $x,y,z$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that $P$ has no maximum value over all real $x,y,z$.
\item[(b)] Find the maximum value of $P$ when all three factors are positive.
\end{enumerate}
