Testing the primes through $100$ gives
\[
\boxed{11,13,17,19,23,29,37,43,47,53,59,67,73,79,89}.
\]
For completeness, the other primes through $100$ are $2,3,5,7,31,41,61,71,83,97$; subtracting $6$ gives either a number below $2$ or a composite.

The proposed universal failure criterion is false. The number $127$ is prime (none of $2,3,5,7,11$ divides it), but $127-6=121=11^2$, divisible by neither $5$ nor $7$.
