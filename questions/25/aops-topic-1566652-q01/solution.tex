The inscribed angle $\angle ABC=45^\circ$ subtends arc $AC$, so the central angle $\angle ANC$ is twice as large: $\angle ANC=90^\circ$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real R=4*sqrt(2);pair O=(0,0);
pair A=R*dir(225),C=R*dir(315),B=R*dir(110);
draw(circle(O,R),linewidth(0.9));
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(1));
draw(O--A,blue+dashed);draw(O--C,blue+dashed);
real q=0.6;pair u=unit(A-O),v=unit(C-O);draw(O+q*u--O+q*(u+v)--O+q*v,blue);
draw(arc(B,2.2,degrees(A-B),degrees(C-B)),red);label("$45^\circ$",B+3.15*dir((degrees(A-B)+degrees(C-B))/2),red);
dot(O);label("$N$",O,N);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,N);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$8$",(A+C)/2,S);
label("$4\sqrt{2}$",(O+A)/2,SE,blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Triangle $ANC$ is therefore an isosceles right triangle with legs $NA=NC=R$ (the radius) and hypotenuse $AC=8$. Hence $R\sqrt2=8$, $R=4\sqrt2$, and the area of the circle is
\[
\pi R^2=\boxed{32\pi}\text{ square units}.
\]
(The law of sines gives the same: $2R=\dfrac{AC}{\sin45^\circ}=8\sqrt2$.)
