The nine prescribed positions can be filled in $5!4!$ ways, and the other songs in $11!$. Thus the probability is $\dfrac{5!4!11!}{20!}=\dfrac{1}{21162960}$. Prime factorization gives $21162960=2^4 3^2 5\cdot7\cdot13\cdot17\cdot19$. The exponent sum is $4+2+1+1+1+1+1=\boxed{11}$.
