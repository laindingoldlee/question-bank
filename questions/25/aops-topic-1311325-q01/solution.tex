$x=\dfrac{c-b}{a}$. The six equally likely assignments $(a,b,c)$ give
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccccc}
(a,b,c)&(-5,-3,4)&(-5,4,-3)&(-3,-5,4)&(-3,4,-5)&(4,-5,-3)&(4,-3,-5)\\\hline
x&-\dfrac{7}{5}&\dfrac{7}{5}&-3&3&\dfrac{1}{2}&-\dfrac{1}{2}
\end{array}
\]
Three are negative, so the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$.
