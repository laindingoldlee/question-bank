A group of size $s$ contributes $\binom s3$. If sizes satisfy $a\ge b+2$, moving one point from the larger group to the smaller changes the count by $-\binom{a-1}2+\binom b2<0$. Thus a minimum has sizes differing by at most one. Since $2025=500\cdot4+5\cdot5$, the minimum is $500\binom43+5\binom53=2000+50=\boxed{2050}$.
