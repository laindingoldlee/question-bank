An odd square is $1$ modulo $8$. Since $p\ne3$, its square is also $1$ modulo $3$, hence $p^2\equiv1\pmod{24}$. Since $p\ne5$, its square is $1$ or $4$ modulo $5$. The residues modulo $120$ that are $1$ modulo $24$ are $1,25,49,73,97$; among them only $1,49$ are $1$ or $4$ modulo $5$. Both occur: $11^2\equiv1$ and $7^2\equiv49\pmod{120}$. Thus $\boxed{1,49}$ are precisely the possibilities.
