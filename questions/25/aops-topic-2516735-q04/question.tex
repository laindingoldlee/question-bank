A time of day is chosen uniformly at random from the $1440$ minutes $00{:}00,00{:}01,\dots,23{:}59$ shown on a $24$-hour digital clock, which displays the time as $AB{:}CD$. What is the probability that the two-digit numbers $AB$ and $CD$ are equal?
