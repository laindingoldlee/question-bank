There are $\left\lfloor\dfrac{500}{3}\right\rfloor=166$ multiples of $3$, $\left\lfloor\dfrac{500}{7}\right\rfloor=71$ multiples of $7$, and $\left\lfloor\dfrac{500}{21}\right\rfloor=23$ multiples of both, i.e.\ of $21$. The numbers that are multiples of exactly one of $3,7$ are counted by removing the common multiples from each set:
\[(166-23)+(71-23)=143+48=\boxed{191}.\]
