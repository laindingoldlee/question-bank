The powers of $i$ repeat with period $4$: $i^1=i$, $i^2=-1$, $i^3=-i$, $i^4=1$, and then $i^{n+4}=i^n\cdot i^4=i^n$. So $i^n$ depends only on the remainder of $n$ on division by $4$:
\[i^7=i^{4+3}=\boxed{-i},\qquad i^{13}=i^{12+1}=\boxed{i},\qquad i^{100}=\left(i^4\right)^{25}=\boxed{1}.\]
