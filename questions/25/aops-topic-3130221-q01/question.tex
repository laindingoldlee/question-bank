Two cats consume 35 cans of food per week. At the end of each week their owner independently buys 50 cans with probability $\dfrac{3}{4}$ and buys none otherwise. Initially there are 100 cans. If a week begins with fewer than 35 cans, food runs out during that week. Let $T$ be the time until food runs out, with $T=\infty$ if it never does. Is $\mathbb E[T]$ finite?
