A maximum means the leading coefficient is $-\dfrac12$. With roots $2$ and $10$,
\[y=-\dfrac12(x-2)(x-10)=\boxed{-\dfrac12x^2+6x-10}.\]
The vertex lies midway between the roots, at $x=6$, where $y=-\dfrac12\cdot4\cdot(-4)=8$. So the vertex is $\boxed{(6,8)}$ and the vertex form is $y=-\dfrac12(x-6)^2+8$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
real f(real x){return -0.5*(x-6)^2+8;}
draw((-0.5,0)--(12.5,0),gray,Arrow(4));draw((0,-2.5)--(0,9.5),gray,Arrow(4));
draw(graph(f,0.8,11.2),linewidth(0.8));
dot((2,0));dot((10,0));dot((6,8));
label("$(2,0)$",(2,0),SW,fontsize(7pt));label("$(10,0)$",(10,0),SE,fontsize(7pt));label("$(6,8)$",(6,8),N,fontsize(7pt));
\end{asy}
