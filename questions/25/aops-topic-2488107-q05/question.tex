A company consists of three sub-companies, each with $n$ employees. In each sub-company the CEO chooses a boss and two assistants for the boss: three different people, all from that sub-company, where the two assistants have the same role. His secretary says that there are $\dfrac{n^2+3}{2}$ ways to choose all these leaders. For how many positive integers $n$ can the secretary be right?
