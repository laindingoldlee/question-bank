Triangle $ABC$ has $\angle C=75^\circ$. In triangle $CDE$, the angle at $C$ is $105^\circ$. The sine rule and given ratio give $\dfrac{\sin105^\circ}{\sin\angle CED}=\dfrac{\sin75^\circ}{\sin65^\circ}$, hence $\sin\angle CED=\sin65^\circ$. Since that angle is less than $75^\circ$, it equals $65^\circ$. The remaining angle is $\boxed{10^\circ}$, choice A.
