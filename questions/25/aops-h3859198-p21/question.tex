In triangle \(ABC\),



\[
\angle BAC=65^\circ,\qquad \angle ABC=40^\circ.
\]

Point \(D\) lies on the ray \(BC\) beyond \(C\), and point \(E\) lies on segment \(AC\), such that



\[
\dfrac{|AB|}{|BC|}=\dfrac{|ED|}{|CD|}.
\]

Find \(\angle EDC\).



\[
\textbf{(A)}\ 10^\circ
\qquad
\textbf{(B)}\ 15^\circ
\qquad
\textbf{(C)}\ 20^\circ
\qquad
\textbf{(D)}\ 25^\circ
\qquad
\textbf{(E)}\ 35^\circ
\]
