Player $i$ writes the sum $s_i$ of the three numbers they see. If the four secret numbers have total $T$, then $s_i=T-x_i$. Adding the slips gives $s_1+s_2+s_3+s_4=3T$, because every secret number occurs on three slips. Everyone can therefore compute $T=\dfrac{s_1+s_2+s_3+s_4}{3}$ and then their own number $\boxed{x_i=T-s_i}$. The order in which the other numbers were shown is irrelevant.
