Since $96=3\cdot32$ with coprime factors, both divisibilities are required. The digit sum is $33+A$, so $A\in\{0,3,6,9\}$. Also $4964A64=4964064+100A\equiv4A\pmod{32}$, so $A$ must be $0$ or $8$. Their intersection is only $A=0$, giving sum $\boxed0$.
