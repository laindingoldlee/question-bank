By symmetry assume $a\le b\le c$. Put $p=b-a\ge0$ and $q=c-b\ge0$, so $p+q=c-a\le2$. Cauchy-Schwarz gives
\[
\sqrt p+\sqrt q\le\sqrt{2(p+q)}.
\]
Hence the requested sum is at most
\[
\sqrt{2(p+q)}+\sqrt{p+q}\le(\sqrt2+1)\sqrt2=2+\sqrt2.
\]
Equality requires $p=q$ and $p+q=2$, so $(a,b,c)=(-1,0,1)$ in some order. These values attain the bound.
