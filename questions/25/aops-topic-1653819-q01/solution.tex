Without the restriction there are $20\cdot19\cdot18=6840$ ways.

The forbidden assignments have both Ali and Brenda as officers: choose Ali's office ($3$ ways), Brenda's office ($2$ ways) and the third officer from the other $18$ members, giving $3\cdot2\cdot18=108$ ways.

By complementary counting the answer is $6840-108=\boxed{6732}$.
