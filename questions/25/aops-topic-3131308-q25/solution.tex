At least $20$ points requires $\lfloor150(1-\dfrac{|300-e|}{300})\rfloor\ge140$, equivalent to $150(1-\dfrac{|300-e|}{300})\ge140$. This simplifies to $|300-e|\le20$. Thus $e$ ranges from $280$ through $320$, giving $\boxed{41}$ estimates.
