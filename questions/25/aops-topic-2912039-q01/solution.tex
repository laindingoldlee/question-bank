The equation is $x^3-7x^2+8x-572=0$. Rational root candidates divide $572=4\cdot11\cdot13$, and $x=11$ works: $1331-847+88-572=0$. Then $x^3-7x^2+8x-572=(x-11)(x^2+4x+52)$, and the quadratic has discriminant $16-208<0$. So $\boxed{x=11}$.
