There are $15$ multiples of $2$, $10$ of $3$, and $5$ of $6$. Inclusion--exclusion gives $15+10-5=\boxed{20}$. Alternatively, in each block of six consecutive integers, precisely two are divisible by neither $2$ nor $3$. Five blocks contain $10$ such integers, leaving $30-10=20$.
