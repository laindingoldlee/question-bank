For an odd $n$, its three largest distinct divisors are at most $n,\dfrac{n}{3},\dfrac{n}{5}$: a proper divisor has odd quotient at least 3, and two different proper divisors have distinct odd quotients. Thus $10327\le\dfrac{23n}{15}$, giving $n\ge\dfrac{15\cdot10327}{23}=6735$. This is attained by $n=6735$, whose divisors $6735,2245,1347$ sum to 10327. Hence the minimum is $\boxed{6735}$.
