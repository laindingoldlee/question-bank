Let the family have $B$ boys and $G$ girls. Donald is a boy, so he has $B-1$ brothers and $G$ sisters:
\[
G=B-1 .
\]
Each sister has $G-1$ sisters and $B$ brothers:
\[
G-1=\dfrac {B}{2} .
\]
Substituting, $B-2=\dfrac {B}{2}$, so $B=4$ and $G=3$. There are $\boxed{4\text{ boys and }3\text{ girls}}$: Donald has $3$ brothers and $3$ sisters, and each sister has $2$ sisters and $4$ brothers.
