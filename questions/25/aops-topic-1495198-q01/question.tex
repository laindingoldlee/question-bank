Ten points are given in the plane. Among them there are exactly two sets of four collinear points, and no other three of the points are collinear.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] How many segments have both endpoints among these points?
\item[(b)] How many lines pass through at least two of these points?
\end{enumerate}
