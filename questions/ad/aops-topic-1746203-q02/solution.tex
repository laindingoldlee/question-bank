Modulo $7$, $a\equiv0,\pm1,\pm2,\pm3$, and $(-a)^3=-a^3$. Since $0^3=0$, $1^3=1$, $2^3=8\equiv1$ and $3^3=27\equiv6\equiv-1\pmod7$, every cube is $\equiv0$ or $\pm1$, i.e.\ $a^3\equiv0,1$ or $6\pmod7$. $\blacksquare$
