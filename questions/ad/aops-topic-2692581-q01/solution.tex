A dilation with centre $O$ and scale factor $3$ moves every point $P$ along the ray $OP$ to the point $P'$ that is $3$ times as far from $O$; the shape stays the same, but every length is multiplied by $3$. With centre at the origin this simply multiplies both coordinates by $3$: $(x,y)\mapsto(3x,3y)$. Hence
\[\boxed{G'(-3,-6),\quad H'(-3,9),\quad J'(12,9),\quad K'(12,-6)}.\]
All side lengths triple: $GHJK$ is in fact a square of side $5$, and its image is a square of side $15$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
draw((-5,0)--(14,0),gray,Arrow(4));draw((0,-7.5)--(0,11),gray,Arrow(4));
pair G=(-1,-2),H=(-1,3),J=(4,3),K=(4,-2);
draw(G--H--J--K--cycle,linewidth(0.9));draw(3*G--3*H--3*J--3*K--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(G--3*G,dashed);draw(H--3*H,dashed);draw(J--3*J,dashed);draw(K--3*K,dashed);
dot(G);dot(H);dot(J);dot(K);dot(3*G);dot(3*H);dot(3*J);dot(3*K);
label("$G$",G,NE,fontsize(8pt));label("$H$",H,SE,fontsize(8pt));label("$J$",J,SW,fontsize(8pt));label("$K$",K,NW,fontsize(8pt));
label("$G'(-3,-6)$",3*G,SW,fontsize(8pt));label("$H'(-3,9)$",3*H,NW,fontsize(8pt));label("$J'(12,9)$",3*J,NE,fontsize(8pt));label("$K'(12,-6)$",3*K,SE,fontsize(8pt));label("$O$",(0,0),NE,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}
