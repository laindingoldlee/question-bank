Without restriction there are $\dfrac{4!}{2!}=12$ arrangements (divide by $2!$ because the green boxes are identical). With the greens glued together we arrange $3$ distinct units: $3!=6$. So $12-6=\boxed{6}$ arrangements have the greens apart.
