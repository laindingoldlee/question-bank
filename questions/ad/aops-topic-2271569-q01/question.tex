The number $4$ can be written as a sum of one or more positive integers, ignoring order, in $5$ ways:
\[4,\quad3+1,\quad2+2,\quad2+1+1,\quad1+1+1+1.\]
We say that $4$ has $5$ \emph{partitions}.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] How many partitions does $7$ have?
\item[(b)] How many ways are there to write $7$ as an \emph{ordered} sum of positive integers (so $3+4$ and $4+3$ count separately)? Explain why stars and bars counts ordered sums and so cannot be used directly for (a).
\end{enumerate}
