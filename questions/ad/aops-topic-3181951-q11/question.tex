Two sets of keys are placed independently and uniformly into twelve labelled mailboxes, so both sets may be in the same box. A gust of wind, independent of the key locations, opens boxes $11$ and $12$, and both are empty. Joe now opens a fixed one of boxes $2$ through $10$. If the probability that it contains at least one set of keys is $\dfrac{a}{b}$ in lowest terms, find $a+b$.
