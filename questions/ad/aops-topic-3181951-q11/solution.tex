Conditioning on boxes $11$ and $12$ being empty leaves the two independent key locations uniform over the other ten boxes. Any selected one of boxes $2$ through $10$ is empty with probability $(\dfrac{9}{10})^2=\dfrac{81}{100}$. Thus the chance of at least one key is $\dfrac{19}{100}$ and $a+b=\boxed{119}$.
