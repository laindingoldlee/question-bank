Put $s=\sin a$. Using $\cos2a=1-2s^2$ and $\sin3a=3s-4s^3$, the numerator becomes $4s^3+2s^2-2s=2s(2s^2+s-1)$. Cancel the denominator to obtain $\boxed{2\sin a}$. The original denominator factors as $(2s-1)(s+1)$, so the exclusions are $\sin a \ne \dfrac{1}{2} , - 1$; cancellation does not remove them.
