The triangle can be realized by $A=(0,0)$, $B=(1,1)$, $C=(3,-7)$: the squared distances are $2,58,68$. Its area is half the absolute determinant, $\dfrac{1}{2}|1(-7)-1(3)|=\boxed5$. Since three side lengths determine a triangle up to congruence, this is the required area.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair[] pts={(0,0),(1,1),(3,-7)};string[] names={"$A$","$B$","$C$"};path boundary=pts[0];for(int j=1;j<pts.length;++j)boundary=boundary--pts[j];draw(boundary--cycle);for(int j=0;j<pts.length;++j)label(names[j],pts[j],dir(90+360*j/pts.length));
\end{asy}
