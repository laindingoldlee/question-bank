The numbers of apples received are at most $2$ and add up to $7$, so the multiset of amounts is one of
\[\{2,2,2,1,0,0\},\qquad\{2,2,1,1,1,0\},\qquad\{2,1,1,1,1,1\}.\]
For each type, first decide who gets how many, then which apples each person gets (a multinomial coefficient).
\begin{itemize}
\item $\{2,2,2,1,0,0\}$: $\dfrac{6!}{3!\,1!\,2!}=60$ ways to assign the amounts, and $\dfrac{7!}{2!\,2!\,2!\,1!}=630$ ways to hand out the apples: $37800$.
\item $\{2,2,1,1,1,0\}$: $\dfrac{6!}{2!\,3!\,1!}=60$ and $\dfrac{7!}{2!\,2!}=1260$: $75600$.
\item $\{2,1,1,1,1,1\}$: $6$ and $\dfrac{7!}{2!}=2520$: $15120$.
\end{itemize}
The total is $37800+75600+15120=\boxed{128520}$.
