The difference is $\dfrac{|18p-47q|}{18q}$. For determinant $18p-47q=1$, one has $q\equiv13\pmod{18}$; the largest admissible denominator is 373, giving $p=974$. For determinant $-1$, $q\equiv5\pmod{18}$; the largest admissible denominator is 365, giving $p=953$ (the next choice gives $p=1000$). Among $q\le383$, a determinant magnitude at least 2 gives difference at least $\dfrac{2}{18\cdot383}>\dfrac{1}{18\cdot373}$. For $q\ge384$, $\dfrac{p}{q}\le\dfrac{999}{384}$, whose distance below $\dfrac{47}{18}$ is already greater than this candidate difference. Hence the unique best fraction is $\dfrac{974}{373}$, reduced since its determinant is 1, and $\boxed{974}$ is the numerator.
