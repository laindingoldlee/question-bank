PRISMS students all love ice cream! For a party, Elisa, the MC of PROM$^2$, wants to get $3$ gallons of ice cream for all the volunteers. The ice creams come in three sizes: pints, quarts, and gallons. If there are four quarts in a gallon and two pints in a quart, how many ways can she choose the sizes of the ice cream to get $3$ gallons of ice cream?
