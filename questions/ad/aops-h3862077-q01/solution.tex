Because $P$ lies on the goal segment and $MP\perp GL$, segment $MP$ is the shortest segment from $M$ to the goal. In right triangle $MPL$, $ML=10$ is the hypotenuse and $MP$ is adjacent to the $48^\circ$ angle. Therefore
\[
\cos48^\circ=\dfrac{MP}{10},\qquad \boxed{MP=10\cos48^\circ\text{ metres}}.
\]
