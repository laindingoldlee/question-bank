The modulus is $2+1+2+24=29$, and $2024=29(69)+23$, so $r=23$. Also $2024^2\equiv24^2=576\equiv76\pmod{100}$. The remaining terms contribute $22+0+22+44=88$. Thus the residue is $23+88+76=187\equiv\boxed{87}\pmod{100}$, choice D.
