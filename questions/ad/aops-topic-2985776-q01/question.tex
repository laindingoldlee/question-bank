Let $ABC$ be an equilateral triangle. Points $X$ and $Y$ lie on segments $AB$ and $AC$. Let $P$ and $Q$ be the feet of the perpendiculars from $X$ and $Y$ to $BC$. Prove that $AX+AY=2PQ$.
