If the rhombus side is $a$, its height is $a\sin45^\circ=5$, so $a=5\sqrt2$ and its area is $25\sqrt2$. A regular octagon of side $s$ has area $2(1+\sqrt2)s^2$: it is a square of side $(1+\sqrt2)s$ with four corner right triangles of legs $\dfrac{s}{\sqrt2}$ removed. Hence $2(1+\sqrt2)s^2=\dfrac{25\sqrt2}{2}$, giving $s^2=\dfrac{25}{4}(2-\sqrt2)$ and $\boxed{s=\dfrac{5}{2}\sqrt{2-\sqrt2}}$. Such placement exists: use an octagon of circumradius $\dfrac{5}{2}$ and the rhombus bounded by $x=\pm\dfrac{5}{2}$ and $x+y=\pm\dfrac{5\sqrt2}{2}$. Four octagon vertices touch its sides; all eight vertices lie inside or on its boundary.
