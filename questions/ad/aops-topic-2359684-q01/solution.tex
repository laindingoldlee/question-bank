Cross-multiplying (with $3t+1\ne0$), $5t=4(3t+1)=12t+4$, so $-7t=4$ and $t=\boxed{-\dfrac47}$. Check: $3t+1=-\dfrac{12}{7}+1=-\dfrac57$, and $\dfrac{-\dfrac47}{-\dfrac57}=\dfrac45$.
