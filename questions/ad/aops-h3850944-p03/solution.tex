Use coordinates $A=(0,0),B=(s,0),C=(s,s),D=(0,s)$. Then $I=(\dfrac{2s}{3},0)$, $J=(\dfrac{2s}{3},s)$, and $K=(\dfrac{2s}{3},\dfrac{s}{2})$. Therefore $100^2=DK^2=\dfrac{4s^2}{9}+\dfrac{s^2}{4}=\dfrac{25s^2}{36}$, giving $s^2=14400$. The right-triangle area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\dfrac{2s}{3}\dfrac{s}{2}=\dfrac{s^2}{6}=\boxed{2400}$.
