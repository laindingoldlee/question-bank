Consider any straight column of three dice. If its first outward face is $a$, its opposite face is $7-a$. The matching face of the next die is $7-a$, so that die's opposite face is $a$. The third die then has final outward face $7-a$. Thus the two outside ends of each column sum to $7$. There are nine columns in each of three directions; their ends account for each outside face exactly once. The total is $\boxed{27\cdot7=189}$.
