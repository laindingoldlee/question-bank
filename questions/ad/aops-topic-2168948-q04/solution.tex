The line $x+2y=12$ meets the $x$-axis at $(12,0)$, and $y=mx$ meets the $x$-axis at the origin. For a bounded region we need $m\ne0$ and $m\ne-\dfrac{1}{2}$ (otherwise two of the lines are parallel or equal). Then $y=mx$ meets $x+2y=12$ where $x(1+2m)=12$, at
\[\left(\dfrac{12}{1+2m},\ \dfrac{12m}{1+2m}\right),\]
and the region is a triangle with base $12$ on the $x$-axis and height $\left|\dfrac{12m}{1+2m}\right|$. Its area is $12$ when
\[\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot12\cdot\left|\dfrac{12m}{1+2m}\right|=12\iff|6m|=|1+2m|\iff36m^2=(1+2m)^2\iff32m^2-4m-1=0,\]
that is, $(8m+1)(4m-1)=0$. So $m=\dfrac{1}{4}$ (the triangle with third vertex $(8,2)$) or $m=-\dfrac{1}{8}$ (third vertex $(16,-2)$).

The smallest value is $m=\boxed{-\dfrac{1}{8}}$, answer $\textbf{(B)}$.
