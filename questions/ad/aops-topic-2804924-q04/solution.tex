\textbf{Yes.} Take a $1\times4$ rectangle (perimeter $10$) and cut it into $n$ strips of size $1\times\dfrac4n$ by cuts parallel to the short side. The perimeters total $n\left(2+\dfrac8n\right)=2n+8$. Choosing $n=246$ gives $500$ m. $\boxed{\text{Yes}}$.
