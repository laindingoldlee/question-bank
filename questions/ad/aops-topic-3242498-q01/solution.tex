Your initial choice is correct with probability $\dfrac{1}{120}$. Switching is correct precisely when that choice was wrong, with probability $\dfrac{119}{120}$. The coin requirement has independent probability $\binom64 2^{-6}=\dfrac{15}{64}$. This same factor applies to either strategy, so switching is optimal. The final chance is $\dfrac{119}{120}\cdot\dfrac{15}{64}=\boxed{\dfrac{119}{512}}$.
