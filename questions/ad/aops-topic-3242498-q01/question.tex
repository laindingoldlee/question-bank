A prize is uniformly hidden behind one of $120$ doors. After you select a door, a host who knows the prize location opens $118$ other empty doors, leaving your door and one other closed. You may stay or switch. If your resulting door contains the prize, you must additionally get exactly four heads in six independent fair coin tosses to keep it; otherwise you lose. If your resulting door is empty, you lose regardless of the coins. What strategy maximizes your winning probability, and what is that probability?
