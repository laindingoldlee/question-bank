Factor $P(x)=x^n-1=\prod_{\zeta^n=1}(x-\zeta)$. Differentiating this product and dividing by $P(x)$ gives
\[
\sum_{\zeta^n=1}\dfrac{1}{x-\zeta}=\dfrac{P'(x)}{P(x)}
=\dfrac{nx^{n-1}}{x^n-1}.
\]
At $x=2$ this is $\dfrac{n2^{n-1}}{2^n-1}$. Remove the term for $\zeta=1$, which equals $1$, to obtain
\[
\dfrac{n2^{n-1}}{2^n-1}-1
=\boxed{\dfrac{(n-2)2^{n-1}+1}{2^n-1}}.
\]
