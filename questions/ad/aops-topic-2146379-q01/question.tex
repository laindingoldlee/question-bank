Jack and Will are pirates who each start with $2015!$ pieces of gold. Jack gives Will one half of his gold. Will then gives Jack one third of his gold. Jack then gives Will one fourth of his gold, and so on, back and forth, until finally Will gives Jack $\dfrac{1}{2015}$ of his gold. How much more gold does Will have than Jack at the end?
