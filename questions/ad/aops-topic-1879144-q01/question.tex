For a real number $r$ and a positive integer $n$, define
\[
\dbinom rn=\dfrac{r(r-1)(r-2)\cdots(r-n+1)}{n!}.
\]
Find the value of $\dfrac{\dbinom{-\dfrac{1}{2}}{100}}{\dbinom{\dfrac{1}{2}}{100}}$.
