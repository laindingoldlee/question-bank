The $100!$ cancels, leaving a ratio of two products of $100$ factors each.

The numerator's factors are $-\dfrac{1}{2}-k$ for $k=0,1,\dots,99$:
\[
\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)\left(-\dfrac{3}{2}\right)\left(-\dfrac{5}{2}\right)\cdots\left(-\dfrac{199}{2}\right).
\]
The denominator's factors are $\dfrac{1}{2}-k$ for $k=0,1,\dots,99$:
\[
\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)\left(-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)\left(-\dfrac{3}{2}\right)\cdots\left(-\dfrac{197}{2}\right).
\]
The $99$ factors $-\dfrac{1}{2},-\dfrac{3}{2},\dots,-\dfrac{197}{2}$ appear in both and cancel. What remains is
\[
\dfrac{-\dfrac{199}{2}}{\dfrac{1}{2}}=\boxed{-199}.
\]
(In general the same cancellation gives $\dfrac{\dbinom{-\dfrac{1}{2}}{n}}{\dbinom{\dfrac{1}{2}}{n}}=-(2n-1)$.)
