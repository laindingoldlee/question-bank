$A$, $B$ and $C$ run a $200$ m race, each at a constant speed. When $A$ finishes, $B$ is $40$ m behind $A$ and $C$ is $10$ m behind $B$. When $B$ finishes, $C$ still needs another $2$ seconds to finish. How many seconds does $B$ still have to run when $A$ finishes?
