Let the sides be $x\le4<z$, where $z$ is the unique longest side. The triangle inequality only needs $x+4>z$, so $5\le z\le x+3$. This forces $x\ge2$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $x=2$: $z=5$;
\item $x=3$: $z=5,6$;
\item $x=4$: $z=5,6,7$.
\end{itemize}
The triangles are $(2,4,5)$, $(3,4,5)$, $(3,4,6)$, $(4,4,5)$, $(4,4,6)$, $(4,4,7)$, so there are $\boxed{6}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
label("$z$",(A+B)/2,S);label("$4$",(A+C)/2,NW);label("$x$",(B+C)/2,NE);
\end{asy}
