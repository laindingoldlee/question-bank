Use strong induction. The number 1 equals $2^0$. For a positive integer $N>1$, choose the largest power $2^k\le N$. If $N=2^k$, we are done. Otherwise $0<N-2^k<2^k$. By induction the remainder is a sum of distinct powers of 2; each is less than $2^k$, so adding $2^k$ preserves distinctness. This proves existence for every $N$.
