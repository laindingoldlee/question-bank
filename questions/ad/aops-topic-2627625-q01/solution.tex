Write the elements as $a_1<a_2<\cdots<a_6$, with $a_1+a_2=10$ and $a_5+a_6=44$. We must make $a_3+a_4$ as large as possible. Since $a_5<a_6$ and $a_5+a_6=44$, $a_5\le21$, so $a_4\le20$ and $a_3\le19$. All bounds are attained by $S=\{1,9,19,20,21,23\}$. The greatest average is
\[\dfrac{10+19+20+44}{6}=\dfrac{93}{6}=\boxed{\dfrac{31}{2}}.\]
