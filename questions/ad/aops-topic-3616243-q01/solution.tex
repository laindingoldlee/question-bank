$2016=2^5\cdot3^2\cdot7$. An even divisor has a positive exponent of $2$ ($5$ choices), an exponent of $3$ from $0$ through $2$ ($3$ choices), and exponent of $7$ equal to $0$ or $1$. Thus there are $5\cdot3\cdot2=\boxed{30}$.
