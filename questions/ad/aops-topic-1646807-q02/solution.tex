A gallon is $4$ quarts, a quart is $2$ pints and a pint is $2$ cups, so $1$ gallon $=4\cdot2\cdot2=16$ cups. Hence
\[
218\text{ cups}=\dfrac{218}{16}\text{ gallons}=\dfrac{109}{8}=\boxed{13.625}\text{ gallons}.
\]
