Since $ 500=2^2\cdot5^3 $, the two exponents for each prime have maximum $ e $. The number of ordered exponent pairs is $(e+1)^2-e^2=2e+1 $. Multiplying gives $(2\cdot2+1)(2\cdot3+1)=\boxed{35}$.
