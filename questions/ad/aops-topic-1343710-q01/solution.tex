Five years ago Sam was $x$ and Nina was $2x$, so now Sam is $x+5$ and Nina is $2x+5$. In $t$ years Nina will be $1.5$ times as old as Sam when
\[
2x+5+t=\dfrac{3}{2}\,(x+5+t)\iff 4x+10+2t=3x+15+3t\iff t=x-5 .
\]
So this happens in $\boxed{x-5}$ years. (For example, if $x=10$: Sam is $15$ and Nina $25$ now; in $5$ years they are $20$ and $30$, and $30=1.5\cdot20$.) The condition $x>5$ makes the answer positive; if $x<5$ the time was in the past.
