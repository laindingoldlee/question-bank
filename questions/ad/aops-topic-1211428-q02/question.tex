In triangle $ABC$, $BC:AB:AC=3:4:5$ and the area is $96$. The line through $C$ perpendicular to $AC$ meets line $AB$ at $D$. A point $E$ is chosen so that the side lengths of triangle $DCE$ are consecutive integers and its area is a positive integer. Let $m$ be the area of triangle $ADC$ and $n$ the area of triangle $DCE$. Find $m+n$.
