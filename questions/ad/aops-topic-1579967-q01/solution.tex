Put the cube at $\{0,1\}^3$, so the vertices are the points with coordinates $0$ or $1$. Every plane through at least three vertices is determined by a triple of vertices, and there are $\dbinom83=56$ triples. Sort the triples by their side lengths, which are $1$ (edge), $\sqrt2$ (face diagonal) or $\sqrt3$ (space diagonal).
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Sides $1,1,\sqrt2$:} three vertices of one face. The plane is a face plane such as $x=0$. It contains exactly the $4$ vertices of that face, so it contains $\dbinom43=4$ triples. There are $6$ such planes, accounting for $24$ triples.
\item \textbf{Sides $1,\sqrt2,\sqrt3$:} the plane contains an edge and the opposite parallel edge, for example the plane $x=y$ through the edges $(0,0,z)$ and $(1,1,z)$. It meets the cube in a ``diagonal rectangle'' with exactly $4$ vertices, so again $4$ triples per plane. The $12$ edges form $6$ opposite pairs, giving the $6$ planes $x=y$, $y=z$, $z=x$, $x+y=1$, $y+z=1$, $z+x=1$ and $24$ triples.
\item \textbf{Sides $\sqrt2,\sqrt2,\sqrt2$:} the three neighbours of one vertex, for example $(1,0,0),(0,1,0),(0,0,1)$ in the plane $x+y+z=1$. The other vertices have $x+y+z\in\{0,2,3\}$, so this plane contains exactly $3$ vertices. There is one such plane for each of the $8$ vertices, accounting for the remaining $56-24-24=8$ triples.
\end{itemize}
No two of these planes coincide, and every triple lies in exactly one of them.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(11cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair p(real x,real y,real z){return (x+0.42*z,y+0.32*z);}
void cube(pair o){
  pair v(real x,real y,real z){return o+p(x,y,z);}
  draw(v(0,0,1)--v(1,0,1),dashed);draw(v(0,0,1)--v(0,1,1),dashed);draw(v(0,0,1)--v(0,0,0),dashed);
  draw(v(0,0,0)--v(1,0,0)--v(1,1,0)--v(0,1,0)--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
  draw(v(1,0,0)--v(1,0,1)--v(1,1,1)--v(1,1,0),linewidth(0.8));
  draw(v(0,1,0)--v(0,1,1)--v(1,1,1),linewidth(0.8));
}
pair o1=(0,0),o2=(2.1,0),o3=(4.2,0);
fill(o1+p(0,0,0)--o1+p(1,0,0)--o1+p(1,1,0)--o1+p(0,1,0)--cycle,paleblue);
fill(o2+p(0,0,0)--o2+p(1,0,0)--o2+p(1,1,1)--o2+p(0,1,1)--cycle,palegreen);
fill(o3+p(0,1,0)--o3+p(1,0,0)--o3+p(1,1,1)--cycle,pink);
cube(o1);cube(o2);cube(o3);
draw(o2+p(0,0,0)--o2+p(0,1,1),deepgreen);draw(o2+p(1,0,0)--o2+p(1,1,1),deepgreen);
draw(o3+p(0,1,0)--o3+p(1,0,0)--o3+p(1,1,1)--cycle,red);
label("face: $6$",o1+(0.7,-0.35));label("rectangle: $6$",o2+(0.7,-0.35));label("triangle: $8$",o3+(0.7,-0.35));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Hence the number of planes is
\[
6+6+8=\boxed{20}.
