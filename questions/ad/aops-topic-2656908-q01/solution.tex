Since $y-1>0$ and $x-1>0$, AM-GM gives
\[\dfrac{x^2}{y-1}+4(y-1)\ge2\sqrt{4x^2}=4x,\qquad \dfrac{y^2}{x-1}+4(x-1)\ge4y.\]
Adding the two inequalities,
\[\dfrac{x^2}{y-1}+\dfrac{y^2}{x-1}\ge4x+4y-4(y-1)-4(x-1)=8.\]
Equality needs $x=2(y-1)$ and $y=2(x-1)$, i.e. $x=y=2$, where the left side is $4+4=8$. $\blacksquare$
