Let the interior centre-quadrilateral angles be $\theta_1,\theta_2,\theta_3,\theta_4 $, whose sum is $ 360^\circ $. In the isosceles triangles $ AO_2B $ and $ BO_3C $, the base angles at $ B $ are $ 90^\circ-\dfrac{\theta_2}{2}$ and $ 90^\circ-\dfrac{\theta_3}{2}$. Since $ BO_2 $ and $ BO_3 $ are opposite rays, subtracting these from a straight angle gives $\angle ABC=\dfrac{\theta_2+\theta_3}{2}$. Likewise $\angle CDA=\dfrac{\theta_4+\theta_1}{2}$. Their sum is $ 180^\circ $, so the opposite-angle criterion proves the convex quadrilateral cyclic.
