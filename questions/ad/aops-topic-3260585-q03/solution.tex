Use coordinates $u=x+2y$, $v=2x+y$. The region becomes the square $-10\le u,v\le10$, of area $400$. The linear map has determinant $1-4=-3$, so it multiplies areas by $3$. Hence the original area is $\boxed{\dfrac{400}{3}}$.
Equivalently, the vertices are $(\dfrac{10}{3},\dfrac{10}{3}),(-10,10),(-\dfrac{10}{3},-\dfrac{10}{3}),(10,-10)$, and the shoelace formula gives the same area.

\end{asy}
