Triangle $MNO$ is isosceles with $MN=NO=25$ cm. A segment drawn from the midpoint of $MO$ perpendicular to $MN$ meets $MN$ at $P$, with $NP:PM=4:1$. What is the length of the altitude from $N$ to $MO$? Express your answer in simplest radical form.
