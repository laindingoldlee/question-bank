Write the numbers $0$ through $99$ as two-digit strings, allowing leading zeros. In each position, every digit occurs ten times. The even digits sum to $0+2+4+6+8=20$, so the total contribution is $2\cdot10\cdot20=400$. Adding or removing $0$ changes nothing, and $E(100)=0$. Thus the required sum is $\boxed{400}$.
