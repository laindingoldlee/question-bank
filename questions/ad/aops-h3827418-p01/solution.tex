For $P=(x,2^x)$, the expression is the sum of the distances from $P$ to $O=(0,0)$ and $Q=(-5,1)$. By the triangle inequality it is at least $OQ=\sqrt{26}$, with equality precisely when $P$ lies on segment $OQ$.

The segment has equation $y=-\dfrac{x}{5}$ with $-5\le x\le0$. The function $2^x+\dfrac{x}{5}$ is strictly increasing and is negative at $x=-2$ but positive at $x=-1$. It therefore has a unique zero in $(-2,-1)$, giving the unique equality point on the segment. This point attains the global minimum, so $\boxed{\lfloor x_0\rfloor=-2}$.
