A sequence has $a_1=4$ and $a_n=\dfrac{\left(a_{n-1}\right)^2}{2}$ for $n>1$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find a formula for $a_n$ and prove it.
\item[(b)] Find the smallest $n$ for which $a_n>10000$.
\end{enumerate}
