\textbf{(a)} The first terms are $4=2^2$, $8=2^3$, $32=2^5$, $512=2^9$: the exponents $2,3,5,9$ are $2^{n-1}+1$. We prove $a_n=2^{2^{n-1}+1}$ by induction. For $n=1$, $2^{1+1}=4$. If $a_{n-1}=2^{2^{n-2}+1}$, then
\[a_n=\dfrac{\left(2^{2^{n-2}+1}\right)^2}{2}=\dfrac{2^{2^{n-1}+2}}{2}=2^{2^{n-1}+1}.\]
So $\boxed{a_n=2^{2^{n-1}+1}}$ for all $n\ge1$. $\blacksquare$

\textbf{(b)} The sequence is increasing, $a_4=2^9=512\le10000$ and $a_5=2^{17}=131072>10000$. So $n=\boxed{5}$.
