There are no such integers. If $a+b=c$, then
\[c^2=(a+b)^2=a^2+2ab+b^2 ,\]
and comparing with $a^2+b^2=c^2$ gives $2ab=0$. So $a=0$ or $b=0$, contradicting the assumption that $a$, $b$, $c$ are all nonzero. (The solutions with a zero are exactly $(a,0,a)$ and $(0,b,b)$.) So the statement is $\boxed{\text{false}}$: no such triple exists, even allowing negative integers.
