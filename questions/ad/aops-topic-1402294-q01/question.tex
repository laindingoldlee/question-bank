A billiard table is an $M\times N$ rectangle, where $M$ (the width) and $N$ (the height) are positive integers. There are pockets only at the four corners. A ball, treated as a point, leaves the top-left pocket moving at $45^\circ$ to the sides. There is no friction, and at each side the ball bounces off with the angle of reflection equal to the angle of incidence.

How many times does the ball hit a side before it falls into a pocket, and into which pocket does it fall? Give the answer in terms of $a=\dfrac{M}{g}$ and $b=\dfrac{N}{g}$, where $g=\gcd(M,N)$.
