Since $a$ is the smallest, $\dfrac{3}{a}>1$, so $a<3$; and $a=1$ is impossible, so $a=2$. Then $\dfrac{1}{b}+\dfrac{1}{c}=\dfrac{1}{2}$ with $2<b<c$; $\dfrac{2}{b}>\dfrac{1}{2}$ gives $b<4$, so $b=3$ and $\dfrac{1}{c}=\dfrac{1}{6}$. Hence $c=\boxed{6}$.
