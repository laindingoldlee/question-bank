Note $a\,@\,b=(a+2b)^2$. So $100\,@\,(-50)=0^2=0$ and $3\,@\,4=11^2=121$, and then $0\,@\,121=242^2$. Since $242=9\cdot26+8$, we get $242^2\equiv8^2=64\equiv\boxed{12}\pmod{26}$.
