The infinite decimal $0.999\ldots$ (with infinitely many 9s) means the number that the decimals $0.9,\ 0.99,\ 0.999,\ \dots$ approach.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $0.999\ldots=1$.
\item[(b)] Deduce that $99.999\ldots=100$.
\item[(c)] Show that every number with a terminating decimal expansion other than $0$ also has an expansion ending in infinitely many 9s, and write $3.27$ in that form.
\end{enumerate}
