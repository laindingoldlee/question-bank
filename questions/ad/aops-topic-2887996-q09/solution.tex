A number has exactly $12=12\cdot1=6\cdot2=4\cdot3=3\cdot2\cdot2$ divisors iff its prime factorisation has shape $p^{11}$, $p^5q$, $p^3q^2$ or $p^2qr$. Counting the four-digit numbers of each shape: $p^{11}$ gives $1$ ($2048$); $p^5q$ gives $66$; $p^3q^2$ gives $18$; $p^2qr$ gives $858$. The total is $943$, so the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{943}{9000}}$.
