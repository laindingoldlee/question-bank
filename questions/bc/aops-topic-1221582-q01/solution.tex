Each side times its altitude is twice the area $K$, so the sides are $\dfrac{2K}{3}$, $K$, $K$: the triangle is isosceles with legs $K$ and base $\dfrac{2K}{3}$, and the altitude $3$ falls on the base.

That altitude bisects the base, so $3^2+\left(\dfrac {K}{3}\right)^2=K^2$, i.e. $\dfrac{8K^2}{9}=9$ and $K=\dfrac{9}{2\sqrt2}=\dfrac{9\sqrt2}{4}$. So the area is $\boxed{\dfrac{9\sqrt2}{4}}$ and the perimeter is $K+K+\dfrac{2K}{3}=\dfrac{8K}{3}=\boxed{6\sqrt2}$.
