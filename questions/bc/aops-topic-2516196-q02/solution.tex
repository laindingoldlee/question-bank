For even $k$ the relation reads $x_{k-1}+x_k=k$. Write $m=2n$ and group the sum in consecutive pairs:
\[x_1+\cdots+x_{2n}=\sum_{j=1}^{n}\left(x_{2j-1}+x_{2j}\right)=\sum_{j=1}^{n}2j=n(n+1).\]
(The relations for odd $k$ are not needed.) So $n(n+1)=2550=50\cdot51$, and since $n(n+1)$ is increasing for $n>0$, $n=50$, i.e. $m=\boxed{100}$.
