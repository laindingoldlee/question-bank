The area is $xy=x^3$, while the perimeter is $2x+2x\sqrt{1+x^2}$. Dividing by $x>0$ gives $y=2+2\sqrt{1+y}$, so $y\ge2$. Squaring yields $y^2-8y=0$. Only $\boxed{y=8}$ satisfies the original equation.

\end{asy}
