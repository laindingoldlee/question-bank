A regular hexagon is inscribed in a circle of radius $r$. Four of the six vertices of the hexagon are chosen at random (all choices of four vertices equally likely) and joined to form a convex quadrilateral. What is the probability that the perimeter of the quadrilateral is at most $5r$? Express your answer as a common fraction.
