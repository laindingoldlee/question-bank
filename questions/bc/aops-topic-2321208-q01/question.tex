Dream has $5$ ender pearls and $10$ health points. Every time he throws a pearl, he loses $1$, $2$ or $3$ health points, each with probability $\dfrac13$, independently. He dies when his health reaches $0$ or less. What is the probability that he dies before throwing his fifth pearl, that is, as a result of one of his first four throws?
