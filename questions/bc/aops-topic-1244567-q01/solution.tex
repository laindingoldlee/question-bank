The number bought must be a common multiple of $20$ and $24$, so at least $\operatorname{lcm}(20,24)=120$: $6$ packages of forks and $5$ of knives. The cost is $6\cdot0.39+5\cdot0.45=2.34+2.25=\boxed{\$4.59}$.
