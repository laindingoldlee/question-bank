The divisions are $252=198+54$, $198=3\cdot54+36$, $54=36+18$, and $36=2\cdot18$. Thus the gcd is $18$. Back substitution gives $18=54-36=4\cdot54-198=\boxed{4\cdot252-5\cdot198}$. If $a=qb+r$, an integer divides both $a,b$ exactly when it divides both $b,r$, because $r=a-qb$ and $a=qb+r$. Hence each step preserves the common divisors and the gcd.
