Substitute $y=x+3$: $x(x+3)=18$, so $x^2+3x-18=0$, i.e. $(x+6)(x-3)=0$. Thus $x=3$, $y=6$ or $x=-6$, $y=-3$. Both check: $3\cdot6=18$ and $(-6)(-3)=18$. The solutions are $\boxed{(x,y)=(3,6)\text{ or }(-6,-3)}$.
