Let $E_i$ require the block $i,i+1,i+2,i+3$ for $i=1,2,3,4$. Each event has $2^3=8$ subsets, total $32$. Pairwise intersections have counts $4,4,4$ for adjacent starts, $2,2$ for starts two apart, and $1$ for starts three apart, total $17$. Triple intersections have counts $2,2,1,1$, total $6$, and the fourfold intersection has count $1$. Inclusion--exclusion gives $\boxed{32-17+6-1=20}$.
