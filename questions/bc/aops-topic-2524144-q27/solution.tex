The graph is a $\wedge$ shape with peak $(2,5)$; it meets the $y$-axis at $(0,3)$ and the $x$-axis (for $x>0$) at $(7,0)$. The region is the quadrilateral with vertices $(0,0)$, $(0,3)$, $(2,5)$, $(7,0)$. Split it by the line $x=2$:
\begin{itemize}
\item a trapezoid with parallel sides $3$ and $5$ and width $2$: area $\dfrac{3+5}2\cdot2=8$;
\item a right triangle with legs $5$ and $5$: area $\dfrac{25}2$.
\end{itemize}
The total area is $8+\dfrac{25}2=\boxed{\dfrac{41}2}$.

dot((0,3));dot((2,5));dot((7,0));
