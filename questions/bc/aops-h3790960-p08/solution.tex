The five tips of a pentagram inside a convex pentagon sum to $180^\circ$. One proof follows the star path: it turns through $720^\circ$ in total, and the five turns are $180^\circ$ minus the five tip angles, so their sum is $900^\circ-720^\circ=180^\circ$. Here those tips are $x,2x,3x,1.5x,1.5x$, so $9x=180^\circ$ and $\boxed{x=20^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=dir(0),B=dir(60),C=dir(140),D=dir(260),E=dir(320);draw(A--B--C--D--E--cycle,gray);draw(A--C--E--B--D--A,blue);label("A",A,(1,0));label("B",B,N);label("C",C,NW);label("D",D,S);label("E",E,SE);
\end{asy}
