A marker is made of two right circular frustums $A$ and $B$ joined at their larger bases. $A$ has height $2$ in and base radii $0.2$ in and $0.18$ in; $B$ has height $4$ in and base radii $0.2$ in and $0.18$ in. Find the volume of the marker.
