The original angle bisector gives $EB=EC$. The other two angle bisectors give $XB=XE=10$ and $YC=YE=10$. Put $D=(0,0)$ and $E=(10u,10v)$ with $v>0$ and $u^2+v^2=1$, taking $BC$ horizontal. Since $DE=EX=EY=10$, the second intersections of the two bisector rays with the radius-ten circle centred at $E$ are $X=(10u-10,10v)$ and $Y=(10u+10,10v)$. The circle through $D,E,X$ has equation $x^2+y^2+(10-20u)x+by=0$. Its other intersection with the horizontal axis is $B=(20u-10,0)$. Similarly $C=(20u+10,0)$. Now $EB=EC$ forces the projection of $E$ to be the midpoint of $BC$, so $10u=20u$ and $u=0$. Therefore $v=1$, $B=(-10,0)$, $C=(10,0)$ and $E=(0,10)$. The other intersection of line $DE$ with the circumcircle of $BCE$ is $A=(0,-10)$. Thus $[AXY]=20\cdot\dfrac{20}{2}=\boxed{200}$.
