Let $\vartriangle ABC$ be a triangle of which the angle bisector of $A$ intersects $\overline{BC}$ at $D$ and the circumcircle of $\vartriangle ABC$ at $E$. Let the angle bisector of $\angle BDE$ intersect the circumcircle of $BDE$ at $X$ and let the angle bisector of $\angle CDE$ intersect the circumcircle of $CDE$ at $Y$. Given $BX = DE = CY = 10$, find the area of triangle $\vartriangle AXY$ .
