You have twelve coins, exactly one of which is fake. All genuine coins have the same weight; the fake coin has a different weight, but you do not know whether it is heavier or lighter. You have a two-pan balance that only shows which side is heavier, or that the two sides balance. You may weigh only the coins themselves (no other weights, no marking or cutting). What is the smallest number of weighings that always suffices to identify the fake coin? Prove your answer.
