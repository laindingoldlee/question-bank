In the fraction $\dfrac{16}{64}$ we can ``simplify'' by deleting the digit $6$ from both the numerator and the denominator, obtaining the correct equality $\dfrac{16}{64}=\dfrac14$. Find all fractions less than $1$ whose numerator and denominator are two-digit positive integers for which deleting a digit that appears in both gives a correct equality. Exclude the trivial cases in which the deleted digit is a final $0$ of both numbers (such as $\dfrac{30}{50}=\dfrac35$).
