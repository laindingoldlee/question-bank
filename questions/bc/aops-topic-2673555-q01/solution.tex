Let the numbers be $\dfrac ab$ and $\dfrac cd$ with integers $a,b,c,d$ and $b,d\ne0$. Then
\[\dfrac ab\pm\dfrac cd=\dfrac{ad\pm bc}{bd},\]
where $ad\pm bc$ and $bd$ are integers and $bd\ne0$. So the sum and difference are rational. $\blacksquare$
