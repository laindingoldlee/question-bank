Replacing each roll $a$ by $9-a$ is a bijection of the outcomes that turns sum $18$ into sum $27-18=9$. Ordered triples of positive integers with sum $9$ number $\dbinom82=28$ (stars and bars), and each part is at most $7$, so all are valid rolls. The probability is $\dfrac{28}{8^3}=\boxed{\dfrac7{128}}$.
