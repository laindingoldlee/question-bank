By Cauchy--Schwarz in Engel form, the left side is at least $\dfrac{(a+b+c)^2}{a(b+c)+b(a+c)+c(a+b)}=\dfrac{(a+b+c)^2}{2(ab+bc+ca)}$. Since $ a^2+b^2+c^2\ge ab+bc+ca $, the numerator is at least $ 3(ab+bc+ca)$. Hence the bound $\dfrac{3}{2}$ follows, with equality exactly when $ a=b=c $.
