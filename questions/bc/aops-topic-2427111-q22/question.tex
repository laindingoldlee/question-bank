Circle $\omega_1$ has centre $A$ and radius $4$. Circle $\omega_2$ has centre $B$ and radius $4$, where $B$ lies on $\omega_1$. The circles meet at $C$ and $D$. Line $AB$ meets $\omega_1$ again at $E$ (other than $B$) and meets $\omega_2$ again at $F$ (other than $A$). Find the exact area of pentagon $CEADF$, and round it to the nearest hundredth.
