\textbf{(a)} We need $40000\cdot1.03^{2t}=50000$, i.e.\ $1.03^{2t}=1.25$. Taking logarithms,
\[
2t=\dfrac{\log1.25}{\log1.03}\approx\dfrac{0.096910}{0.012837}\approx7.549,\qquad t\approx\boxed{3.77\text{ years}}.
\]

\textbf{(b)} After $7$ half-years the balance is $40000\cdot1.03^7\approx\$49194.95$, and after $8$ half-years it is $40000\cdot1.03^8\approx\$50670.80$. So the balance first reaches $\$50000$ after $8$ half-years, i.e.\ $\boxed{4\text{ years}}$.

The key's $0.15$ years is wrong: after $0.15$ years the formula gives only $40000\cdot1.03^{0.3}\approx\$40356$. (The figure $3.77$ found by the student is correct.)
