The distance is $57060-56200=860$ miles. Full tanks at both endpoints mean that consumption equals fuel added after departure: $12+20=32$ gallons. Thus $\dfrac{860}{32}=26.875$, rounding to $\boxed{26.9}$ miles per gallon. The initial fill amount is irrelevant.
