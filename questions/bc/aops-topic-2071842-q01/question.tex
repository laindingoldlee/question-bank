The number \(2^{\sqrt2}\) is defined as the limit of \(2^{r}\) as rational numbers \(r\) approach \(\sqrt2\), and its exact value cannot be written with finitely many rational powers. Using \(\dfrac{7}{5}<\sqrt2<\dfrac{10}{7}\), prove that
\[
2.6<2^{\sqrt2}<2.7 .
\]
