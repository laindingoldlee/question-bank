Let $N$ be the number of marbles. Then
\[N\equiv1\pmod3,\qquad N\equiv2\pmod7,\qquad N\equiv0\pmod5 .\]
The multiples of $5$ that are $1$ more than a multiple of $3$ are $10,25,40,55,70,85,100,\dots$ (they go up by $15$). Their remainders on division by $7$ are $3,4,5,6,0,1,2$, so the first one with remainder $2$ is $100$. By the Chinese remainder theorem the solutions are exactly the numbers $\equiv100\pmod{105}$:
\[100,\ 205,\ 310,\ 415,\ \dots\]
The largest below $400$ is $\boxed{310}$. Check: $310=3\cdot103+1=7\cdot44+2=5\cdot62$.
