$A$ sits next to $B$ in $2\cdot5!=240$ seatings. Among these, $B$ is also next to $C$ exactly when $B$ sits between $A$ and $C$, i.e.\ the block $ABC$ or $CBA$ appears; such a block and the other $4$ people form $5$ units, arranged in $4!$ ways, giving $2\cdot4!=48$ seatings. Hence the answer is $240-48=\boxed{192}$.
