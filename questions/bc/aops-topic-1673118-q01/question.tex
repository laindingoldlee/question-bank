A Martian writes numbers in base ten but with its own digit symbols. The following statements, written in Martian notation, are all true:
\[
2+3=4,\quad 26+54=69,\quad 49-26=34,\quad 5\cdot6=31,\quad 29\cdot45=723,\quad \dfrac{615}{9}=74,\quad 23^5=31847,\quad \sqrt{75}=9 .
\]
Assuming each Martian symbol stands for one fixed digit, evaluate (in ordinary notation) the Martian expression $\left(\left(\dfrac{39}{5}\right)^3-31\right)\sqrt{257+34}$.
