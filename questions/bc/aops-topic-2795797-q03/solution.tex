If there were $10$ or more colours, choosing one ball of each of $10$ colours would give $10$ balls of different colours, contrary to the hypothesis. So there are at most $9$ colours.

If every colour occurred on at most $11$ balls, there would be at most $9\cdot11=99<100$ balls. Hence some colour occurs at least $12$ times. $\blacksquare$
