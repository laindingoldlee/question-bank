Isosceles triangle $ABC$ has $AC=BC$ and $\angle ACB=20^\circ$. Its circumcircle has centre $O$ and radius two. Let $M$ be the midpoint of $BC$, and let ray $OM$ meet the circumcircle at $D$. The circle with diameter $OD$ meets $AD$ again at $X\ne D$. Find $[OXD]$.
