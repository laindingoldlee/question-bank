At angular displacement $\theta$ from the downward radius, the height above the bottom is $30-30\cos\theta$. Setting this equal to $15$ gives $\cos\theta=\dfrac{1}{2}$, so the first such angle is $60^\circ$. This is one sixth of a revolution, taking $\boxed{10\text{ seconds}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair O=(0,30),B=(0,0),P=O+30dir(-30);draw(circle(O,30));draw(B--O--P);draw((0,15)--P,dashed);label("$O$",O,W);label("bottom",B,S);label("$P$",P,E);label("$15$",(P.x,7.5));label("$30$",(0,15),W);label("$60^\circ$",O+8dir(-60));
\end{asy}
