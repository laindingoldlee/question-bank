Three odd primes would sum to an odd number, so $p=2$. Thus $q+r=48$ and $r^2-q^2=48(r-q)$. Maximize by minimizing $q$: $q=3$ gives nonprime $r=45$, while $q=5$ gives prime $r=43$. Every larger $q$ decreases the difference. The maximum is $48(43-5)=\boxed{1824}$.
