This is a difference of cubes, $x^3-\left(y^3\right)^3$. Using $a^3-b^3=(a-b)\left(a^2+ab+b^2\right)$ with $a=x$, $b=y^3$:
\[x^3-y^9=\boxed{\left(x-y^3\right)\left(x^2+xy^3+y^6\right)}.\]
(The second factor does not factor further: as a quadratic in $x$ its discriminant $y^6-4y^6=-3y^6$ is negative for $y\ne0$.)
