Regular hexagon $ABCDEF$ has side length $4$. A circle with centre $C$ passes through $B$ and $D$. Point $G$ lies on this circle, outside the hexagon, such that $CG$ is parallel to $AB$. Segment $AG$ meets side $BC$ at $N$. Find the exact value of $\tan\angle BNA$, and the measure of $\angle BNA$ to the nearest tenth of a degree.
