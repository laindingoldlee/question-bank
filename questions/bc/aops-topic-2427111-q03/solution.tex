Place the centre of the hexagon at the origin with
\[A=(4,0),\quad B=\left(2,2\sqrt3\right),\quad C=\left(-2,2\sqrt3\right),\quad D=(-4,0).\]
The circle has radius $CB=4$. The two points of the circle on the line through $C$ parallel to $AB$ are $C\pm(B-A)$, where $B-A=\left(-2,2\sqrt3\right)$ has length $4$. One of them is $C-(B-A)=(0,0)$, the centre of the hexagon, which is inside it; so
\[G=C+(B-A)=\left(-4,4\sqrt3\right).\]
Side $BC$ lies on the line $y=2\sqrt3$. The point $A+t(G-A)=\left(4-8t,\,4\sqrt3\,t\right)$ is on it when $t=\dfrac12$, so $N=\left(0,2\sqrt3\right)$, the midpoint of $BC$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=4;
pair A=s*dir(0),B=s*dir(60),C=s*dir(120),D=s*dir(180),Ep=s*dir(240),F=s*dir(300);
pair G=C+(B-A), N=extension(A,G,B,C);
draw(A--B--C--D--Ep--F--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
draw(circle(C,s),gray);
draw(C--G,blue); draw(A--G,red);
draw(arc(N,0.8,angle(A-N)*180/pi,0),deepgreen);
dot(G); dot(N);
label("$A$",A,E); label("$B$",B,NE); label("$C$",C,W); label("$D$",D,W); label("$E$",Ep,SW); label("$F$",F,SE);
label("$G$",G,NW); label("$N$",N,N);
\end{asy}

Now $\overrightarrow{NB}=(2,0)$ points horizontally and $\overrightarrow{NA}=\left(4,-2\sqrt3\right)$, so $\angle BNA$ is the angle below the horizontal of $\overrightarrow{NA}$:
\[\tan\angle BNA=\dfrac{2\sqrt3}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}},\qquad\angle BNA=\arctan\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\approx\boxed{40.9^\circ}.\]
