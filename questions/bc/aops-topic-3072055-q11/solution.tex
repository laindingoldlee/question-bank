The primes on the die are $2,3,5,7,11,13,17,19$, eight outcomes. A prime occurs with probability $\dfrac{8}{20}=\dfrac{2}{5}$. Independence gives $(\dfrac{2}{5})^2=\boxed{\dfrac{4}{25}}$.
