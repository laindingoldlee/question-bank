How many lattice points (points with integer coordinates) lie on the line segment from $(0,0)$ to $(500,900)$, not counting the endpoints? More generally, find a formula for the number of such points on the segment from $(0,0)$ to $(a,b)$, where $a$ and $b$ are positive integers.
