$x$ and $y$ are nonnegative integers with $x^2+y^2=313$. Checking $y=0,1,\dots,17$, the only solution is $\{x,y\}=\{12,13\}$ (e.g. $313-144=169=13^2$, and $313$ itself is not a square). A shortest route has $12$ steps in one direction and $13$ in the other, in any order, so the number of routes is
\[\dbinom{25}{12}=\boxed{5200300}.\]
