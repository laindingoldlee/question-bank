$A$ and $B$ are intersections in a grid of streets whose blocks are unit squares, and $AB=\sqrt{313}$. Let $x$ and $y$ be the side lengths of the rectangle with opposite vertices $A$ and $B$ and sides along the streets, chosen (if there is a choice) so that $|x-y|$ is as small as possible. How many shortest routes along the streets lead from $A$ to $B$?
