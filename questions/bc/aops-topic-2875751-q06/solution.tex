$2160$ feet is $720$ yards. We need $-16t^2+176t+240>720$, i.e. $16t^2-176t+480<0$, i.e. $t^2-11t+30=(t-5)(t-6)<0$. This holds for $5<t<6$, a duration of $\boxed{1}$ second.
