A student writes
\[\log_1 1+\log_1 1=\log_1 1=0=1=2=3=\cdots\]
and concludes that all numbers are equal. Find the error. In particular:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that $1^x=1$ for every real $x$, and explain why this means that $\log_1 1$ cannot be defined as a number.
\item[(b)] For which bases $b$ is $\log_b y$ defined, and is the first equation $\log_b1+\log_b1=\log_b1$ correct for those bases?
\end{enumerate}
