Multiplying out: $g(x)=x+xg(x)$, so $g(x)(1-x)=x$ and
\[
g(x)=\boxed{\dfrac{x}{1-x}},\qquad x\ne1 .
\]
For $x=1$ the equation would say $g=1+g$, which is impossible. (Check: $1+g=\dfrac{1}{1-x}$ and $\dfrac{g}{1+g}=x$.)
