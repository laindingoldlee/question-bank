Let the color counts be $r,b$ and put $n=r+b$. The probability of different colors is $\dfrac{7}{18}$, so
\[
\dfrac{2rb}{n(n-1)}=\dfrac{7}{18}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 36rb=7n(n-1).
\]
It follows that $9$ divides $n(n-1)$. Since consecutive integers are coprime, either $n$ or $n-1$ must be divisible by $9$. No $2\le n\le8$ works. For $n=9$, choose $r=2,b=7$. Then
\[
\dfrac{\binom22+\binom72}{\binom92}=\dfrac{22}{36}=\dfrac{11}{18}.
\]
Thus the minimum is $\boxed9$.
