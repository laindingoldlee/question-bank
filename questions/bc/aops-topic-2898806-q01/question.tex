Joey and Chloe and their daughter Zoe share a birthday. Joey is $1$ year older than Chloe, and Zoe is $1$ year old today. Today is the first of the $9$ birthdays on which Chloe's age is an integer multiple of Zoe's age. What is the sum of the digits of Joey's age the next time his age is a multiple of Zoe's age?
