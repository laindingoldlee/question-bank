Let Chloe be $c$ now. After $x$ years, $x+1\mid c+x$ iff $x+1\mid c-1$. So the birthdays correspond to divisors of $c-1$, and there are $9$ of them. Since $c-1$ is an adult's age minus one, the natural choice is $c-1=36$ (the next is $100$). So Chloe is $37$ and Joey is $38$.

Joey's age is a multiple of Zoe's iff $x+1\mid37$. After today ($x=0$), next is $x+1=37$: Joey is $74$. The digit sum is $\boxed{11}$.
