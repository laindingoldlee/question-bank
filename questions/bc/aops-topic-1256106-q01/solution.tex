Shift coordinates: with $u=x+2$ and $v=y-3$ the equation is $|u|+|v|=1$, a square with vertices $(\pm1,0)$ and $(0,\pm1)$ (a translation does not change lengths). Its diagonals join $(1,0)$ to $(-1,0)$ and $(0,1)$ to $(0,-1)$, so each has length $\boxed{2}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((-1.5,0)--(1.5,0),Arrow); draw((0,-1.5)--(0,1.5),Arrow);
draw((1,0)--(0,1)--(-1,0)--(0,-1)--cycle,blue+linewidth(1)); draw((-1,0)--(1,0),red); draw((0,-1)--(0,1),red);
label("$u$",(1.5,0),E); label("$v$",(0,1.5),N); label("$1$",(1,0),SE); label("$-1$",(-1,0),SW);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
