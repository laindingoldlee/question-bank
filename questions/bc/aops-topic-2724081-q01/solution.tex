The truncation with $ n $ nines is $ s_n=9\sum_{j=1}^n10^{-j}=1-10^{-n}$. Its difference from 1 is $ 10^{-n}$, which tends to 0. By the definition of the infinite decimal as this limit, $ 0.999\ldots=\lim s_n=\boxed1 $. No positive fixed gap remains, because any positive number exceeds $ 10^{-n}$ for sufficiently large $ n $.
