Take $A=10000$, $B=100$, $C=1$. Then $Ax+By+Cz=10000x+100y+z$; since $x,y,z$ are between $10$ and $99$, this six-digit number is just the digits of $x$, $y$, $z$ written one after another, so $x$, $y$, $z$ can be read off. $\boxed{A=10000,\ B=100,\ C=1}$ (many other choices work, e.g. any with $B>99C$ and $A>99B+99C$).
