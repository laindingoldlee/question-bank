Six balls are put into three boxes (boxes may be empty). Count the ways when
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] the balls are distinguishable and the boxes are distinguishable;
\item[(b)] neither balls nor boxes are distinguishable;
\item[(c)] the balls are distinguishable but the boxes are not;
\item[(d)] the balls are not distinguishable but the boxes are.
\end{enumerate}
