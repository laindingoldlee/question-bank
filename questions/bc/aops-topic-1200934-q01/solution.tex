(a) Each ball chooses a box: $3^6=729$.

(b) Only the multiset of box sizes matters: the partitions of $6$ into at most $3$ parts, namely $6$, $5+1$, $4+2$, $4+1+1$, $3+3$, $3+2+1$, $2+2+2$: $7$ ways.

(c) We split the $6$ balls into at most $3$ nonempty unlabelled groups: $S(6,1)+S(6,2)+S(6,3)=1+31+90=122$ (Stirling numbers of the second kind; e.g. $S(6,2)=2^5-1$).

(d) Stars and bars: solutions of $x_1+x_2+x_3=6$ in non-negative integers, $\dbinom82=28$.

$\boxed{729,\ 7,\ 122,\ 28}$.
