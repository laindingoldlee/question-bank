The expression is defined for $x\ne0,-1$. For such $x$ it equals $\dfrac x{x+1}$, which is $\ge0$ exactly when $x<-1$ or $x\ge0$; removing $x=0$ gives
\[\boxed{x<-1\ \text{ or }\ x>0}.\]
Note that $x=0$ is not a solution, even though the numerator is $0$ there, because the denominator is also $0$.
