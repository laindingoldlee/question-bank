Evenness requires $f(-x)=f(x)$ for every $x$; oddness requires $f(-x)=-f(x)$. Here $f(1)=1$ and $f(-1)=9$, so neither condition holds. The function is $\boxed{\text{neither}}$. Its symmetry about $x=2$ does not make it even; evenness specifically means symmetry about the vertical axis.
