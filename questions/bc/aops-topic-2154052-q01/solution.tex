By the difference of cubes,
\[x^3-27=x^3-3^3=(x-3)\left(x^2+3x+9\right).\]
So $x=3$, or $x^2+3x+9=0$, whose solutions are
\[x=\dfrac{-3\pm\sqrt{9-36}}{2}=\dfrac{-3\pm\sqrt{-27}}{2}=\dfrac{-3\pm3i\sqrt3}{2}.\]
The three solutions are
\[\boxed{x=3,\qquad x=\dfrac{-3+3i\sqrt3}{2},\qquad x=\dfrac{-3-3i\sqrt3}{2}}.\]
(They are $3$ times the three cube roots of unity $1$ and $\dfrac{-1\pm i\sqrt3}{2}$.)
