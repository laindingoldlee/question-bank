Three digits in base $2016$ means $2016^2\le n<2016^3$; two digits in base $2017$ means $2017\le n<2017^2$. Both hold exactly when $2016^2\le n<2017^2$, so
\[|S|=2017^2-2016^2=(2017-2016)(2017+2016)=\boxed{4033}.\]
