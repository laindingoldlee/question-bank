Since $q^0=1$, we have $(2pm^{-1})^{-4}=2^{-4}p^{-4}m^{4}$. Multiplying by $2m^{-1}p^3$ gives $2^{-3}p^{-1}m^{3}$, and dividing by $2pq^2$ gives
\[
2^{-4}p^{-2}m^{3}q^{-2}=\boxed{\dfrac{m^3}{16p^2q^2}}.
\]
