Each multiple-choice question can be answered in $5$ ways and each true-or-false question in $2$ ways, independently. By the multiplication principle the number of answer sheets is
\[
5^{15}\cdot2^{15}=(5\cdot2)^{15}=10^{15}=\boxed{1\times10^{15}} .
\]
