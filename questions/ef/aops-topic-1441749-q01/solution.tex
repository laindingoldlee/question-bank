Count the good three-digit numbers $\overline{d_1d_2d_3}$ ($d_1\ne0$).
\begin{itemize}
\item $d_1=d_2$ even: $d_1\in\{2,4,6,8\}$ and $d_3$ arbitrary, $4\cdot10=40$ numbers.
\item $d_2=d_3$ even: $d_2\in\{0,2,4,6,8\}$ and $d_1\in\{1,\dots,9\}$, $5\cdot9=45$ numbers.
\item Both: $d_1=d_2=d_3\in\{2,4,6,8\}$, $4$ numbers.
\end{itemize}
By inclusion--exclusion there are $40+45-4=81$ good numbers, so $900-81=\boxed{819}$ three-digit numbers are not good.
