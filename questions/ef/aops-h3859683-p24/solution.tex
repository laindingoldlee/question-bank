By the Chinese remainder theorem, count independently modulo $81$ and $25$ and multiply. For modulus $q$, form arrays $A_r=\#\{x:2x^3\equiv r\pmod q\}$ and $B_r=\#\{y:y^4\equiv r\pmod q\}$ for $0\le x,y<q$. The count is $\sum_r A_rB_r$. Direct modular cubing and fourth powers for the 81 and 25 residues give respectively 297 and 45. Thus the requested answer is $297\cdot45=\boxed{13365}$. Residue zero represents the endpoint 2025, so no endpoint is omitted.
