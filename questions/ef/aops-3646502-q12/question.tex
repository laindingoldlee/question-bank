Let $p, q, r$ be the roots of the cubic polynomial $2x^3-19x^2+59x-60$. A rectangular prism is constructed with $p$, $q$, $r$ as side lengths (in centimeters). Let $S$ be the set of points at most $1$ cm away from some point on the prism's surface. If $S$ encompasses a region of volume $\dfrac{a}{b} \pi + c$ cm$^3$, where $a$, $b$ and $c$ are integers where $a$ and $b$ are relatively prime, find $a + b + c$.



Clarification: S also includes regions inside the prism.
