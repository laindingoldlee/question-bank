The numbers $1,4,5,8,6,2,3,9,7$ are written in that order around a circle. A move consists of choosing three consecutive numbers $(a,b,c)$ around the circle and changing them to $(a+1,\,b-2,\,c+1)$. Is it possible, by a sequence of moves, to make all nine numbers equal to $5$?
