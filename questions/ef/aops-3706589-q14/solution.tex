Let $S$ be the total. The condition is $S\equiv2x\pmod{10}$ for every $x\in B$, so all members have the same residue modulo five. Among $1,\ldots,2016$, a residue class modulo five has at most $404$ members, ruling out $405$. For an example take $B=\{5,10,\ldots,2000\}$. It has $400$ members and sum $5\cdot\dfrac{400\cdot401}{2}=401000$, divisible by ten. Every $x$ is a multiple of five, so $2x\equiv0\equiv S\pmod{10}$.
