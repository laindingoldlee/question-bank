We have $9\le x\le9\cdot2026=18234$, and $x,y,z$ are positive multiples of $9$. For $x\le9999$ its digit sum is at most $36$; for $10000\le x\le18234$ it is at most $1+7+9+9+9=35$. Thus $y\le36$. The positive multiples of $9$ up to $36$ are $9,18,27,36$, all with digit sum $9$. Hence $\boxed{z=9}$ in every case, and such $N$ certainly exist.
