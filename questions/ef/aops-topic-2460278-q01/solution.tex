Let the digits be $d_1,\dots,d_{10}$ with $d_1\ge1$. Put $e_1=d_1-1\ge0$. Then
\[e_1+d_2+\cdots+d_{10}=3\]
with all ten numbers nonnegative. Every digit is then at most $4\le9$, so there is no extra restriction. By stars and bars the number of solutions is
\[\dbinom{3+9}{9}=\dbinom{12}{3}=\boxed{220}.\]
