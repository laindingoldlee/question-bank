Find all functions $f$ from the positive reals to the reals such that $f(1)=2008$,
\[|f(x)|\le x^2+1004^2\quad\text{for all }x>0,\]
and
\[f\left(x+y+\dfrac{1}{x}+\dfrac{1}{y}\right)=f\left(x+\dfrac{1}{y}\right)+f\left(y+\dfrac{1}{x}\right)\quad\text{for all }x,y>0 .\]
