In a school election, $20\%$ of the students vote. A teacher selects four different students at random and asks whether they voted.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Treating the school as very large, so that each selected student independently voted with probability $\dfrac15$, find the probability that exactly two of the four students voted.
\item[(b)] A student proposes the answer $\dfrac{\dbinom{0.2x}{2}\dbinom{0.8x}{2}}{\dbinom x4}$, where $x$ is the number of students in the school. Explain what this formula computes, evaluate it for $x=100$, and show that it tends to the answer of (a) as $x\to\infty$.
\end{enumerate}
