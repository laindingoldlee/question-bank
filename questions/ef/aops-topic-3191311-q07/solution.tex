The final digit is $0$ or $5$. If it is $0$, the first two digits sum to $15$, giving $690,780,870,960$, none divisible by $7$. If it is $5$, the candidates are $195,285,375,465,555,645,735,825,915$. Testing divisibility by $7$ leaves only $735=35\cdot21$. Thus the answer is $\boxed{735}$.
