If every absolute value were $1$, the sum of forty-nine odd integers would be odd and could not be zero. Thus at least one absolute value is at least $2$, giving squared sum at least $48+4=52$. Equality is attained by twenty-five $1$s, twenty-three $-1$s and one $-2$. Their sum is zero and their squared sum is $\boxed{52}$.
