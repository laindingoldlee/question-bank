Choose $B=(0,0),A=(0,3),C=(4,0)$. Then $G=(\dfrac{4}{3},1)$, so $AG=\sqrt{\dfrac{16}{9}+4}=\boxed{\dfrac{2\sqrt{13}}{3}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

\end{asy}
