Inside the cube root, $\dfrac{5\sqrt2}{8\sqrt2}=\dfrac58$, so
\[\dfrac{9\sqrt5}{2\sqrt3}\cdot\dfrac58=\dfrac{45\sqrt5}{16\sqrt3}=\dfrac{45\sqrt{15}}{48}=\dfrac{15\sqrt{15}}{16}=\dfrac{\left(\sqrt{15}\right)^3}{16}.\]
Hence
\[\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{\left(\sqrt{15}\right)^3}{16}}=\dfrac{\sqrt{15}}{\sqrt[3]{16}}=\dfrac{\sqrt{15}}{2\sqrt[3]2}=\dfrac{\sqrt{15}\,\sqrt[3]4}{2\sqrt[3]2\cdot\sqrt[3]4}=\dfrac{\sqrt{15}\,\sqrt[3]4}{4},\]
which has $a=15$, $b=4$, $c=4$ and $a+b+c=23$.

\textbf{No smaller sum.} Call the value $v=\dfrac{\sqrt{15}\sqrt[3]4}{4}\approx1.537$. If $\dfrac{\sqrt a\sqrt[3]b}{c}=v$, then raising to the sixth power gives $a^3b^2\cdot4^6=15^3\cdot4^2\cdot c^6$, that is, $a^3b^2\cdot2^8=15^3c^6$. The power of $2$ on the left is at least $8$, and on the right it is $6$ times the power of $2$ in $c$, so $4\mid c$.
\begin{itemize}
\item If $c=4$, then $a^3b^2=15^3\cdot2^4=2^4\cdot3^3\cdot5^3$. Comparing the exponents of $3$ and of $5$ (each equal to $3$ times the exponent in $a$ plus $2$ times that in $b$) forces $3$ and $5$ to divide $a$ once and $b$ not at all; for the prime $2$, $3i+2j=4$ forces $i=0$, $j=2$. So $a=15$ and $b=4$, the representation above.
\item If $c\ge8$, then $\sqrt a\sqrt[3]b=cv\ge8v>12$. But $a+b+c\le23$ would give $a,b\le15$, so $\sqrt a\sqrt[3]b\le\sqrt{15}\sqrt[3]{15}<4\cdot2.5=10$, a contradiction.
\end{itemize}
The least possible value is $a+b+c=\boxed{23}$.
