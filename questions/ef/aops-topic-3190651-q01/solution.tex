A travels $3.5$ units to the wall and $4$ more back to the table, totaling $\boxed{7.5}$ time units. B travels diagonally $3.5\sqrt2$ to the corner and $4\sqrt2$ back to the table, totaling $\boxed{7.5\sqrt2}$. Since $\sqrt2>1$, A arrives first.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw((-4.5,-4.5)--(4.5,-4.5)--(4.5,4.5)--(-4.5,4.5)--cycle);filldraw((-.5,-.5)--(.5,-.5)--(.5,.5)--(-.5,.5)--cycle,lightgray,black);draw((0,-1)--(0,-4.5),blue,Arrow);draw((1,1)--(4.5,4.5),red,Arrow);dot("$A$",(0,-1),W);dot("$B$",(1,1),NW);
\end{asy}
