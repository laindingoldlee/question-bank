A square room is $[-4.5,4.5]^2$ and its central table is $[-0.5,0.5]^2$. Walker A starts at $(0,-1)$ heading south, and B starts at $(1,1)$ heading northeast. Both move at unit speed. On first reaching the room boundary each reverses direction exactly and then walks until reaching the table. Who arrives first, and at what times?
