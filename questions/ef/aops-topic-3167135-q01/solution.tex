Every year from $2005$ through $2099$ contains a zero. Years $2100$ through $2110$ also contain a zero. The digit product of $2111$ is $2$, not a square, whereas that of $2112$ is $4$. Thus the first such year is $2112$, when the person turns $2112-2005=\boxed{107}$.
