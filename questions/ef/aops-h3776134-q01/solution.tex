Take $B=(0,0)$, $C=(17,0)$, and $A=(\dfrac{545}{34},h)$ with $h=\dfrac{105\sqrt{15}}{34}$, obtained by subtracting the two distance equations for $A$. The circumcircle has equation $x^2+y^2-17x+Dy=0$, where substituting $A$ gives $D=-\dfrac{289}{7\sqrt{15}}$. The minor arc $BC$ lies below the $x$-axis. Write $P=(t,-t)$ and $K=(t,0)$, with $t>0$. The circle equation gives $2t^2-(17+D)t=0$, so $t=\dfrac{17+D}{2}$. This is between $0$ and $17$ and locates the required point. The area ratio is $\dfrac{t^2}{17h}\approx\boxed{0.049}$.
