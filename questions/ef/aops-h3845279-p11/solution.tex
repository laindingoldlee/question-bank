Opposite vertices of a parallelogram have the same midpoint, so their drift sums are equal. Thus the missing drift is one of
\[
8+17-35=-10,\quad 8+35-17=26,\quad17+35-8=44.
\]
The largest candidate is attainable: the points $(4,4),(22,-5),(31,13),(13,22)$ form a square, because consecutive side vectors $(18,-9)$ and $(9,18)$ have equal lengths and zero dot product. Their drifts are $8,17,44,35$. Therefore the answer is $\boxed{44}$.
