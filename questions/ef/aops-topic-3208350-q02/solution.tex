The first two conditions say $N+4$ is a multiple of $63$, so $N=63t-4$. Modulo $13$, this gives $11t-4\equiv2$, or $11t\equiv6$. Since $11\cdot6\equiv1$, $t\equiv36\equiv10$. Thus $N\equiv626\pmod{819}$; the least positive solution is $\boxed{626}$.
