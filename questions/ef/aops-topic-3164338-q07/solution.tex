Choose the blue ordinate for each abscissa in order. For $x=1$ there are three choices 5, 6, 7. For $x=2$ the allowed set is 4 through 7, containing the previous choice, so three remain. The allowed sets for $x=3,4$ similarly grow by one each time, leaving three choices at each step. For $x=5,6,7$ all ordinates are allowed, and the three unused ones can be assigned in $3!$ ways. Therefore $\boxed{3^4\cdot3!=486}$.
