Subtract successive equalities to get $a-b=\dfrac{b-c}{bc}$, $b-c=\dfrac{c-a}{ac}$, and $c-a=\dfrac{a-b}{ab}$. Multiplying and cancelling the nonzero product $(a-b)(b-c)(c-a)$ gives $(abc)^2=1$. Both signs are possible: $(a,b,c)=(1,-\dfrac{1}{2},-2)$ has common value $-1$ and product $1$, while $(2,-1,\dfrac{1}{2})$ has common value $1$ and product $-1$. Hence $\boxed{abc\in\{-1,1\}}$.
