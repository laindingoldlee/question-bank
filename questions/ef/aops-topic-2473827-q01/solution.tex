Simplify each radical: $\sqrt{338}=\sqrt{169\cdot2}=13\sqrt2$, $\sqrt{288}=\sqrt{144\cdot2}=12\sqrt2$, $\sqrt{150}=\sqrt{25\cdot6}=5\sqrt6$ and $\sqrt{96}=\sqrt{16\cdot6}=4\sqrt6$. So
\[\dfrac{13\sqrt2}{12\sqrt2}+\dfrac{5\sqrt6}{4\sqrt6}=\dfrac{13}{12}+\dfrac54=\dfrac{13}{12}+\dfrac{15}{12}=\dfrac{28}{12}=\boxed{\dfrac73}.\]
