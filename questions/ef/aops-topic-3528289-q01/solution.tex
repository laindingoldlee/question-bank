Composition gives $f(f(x))=\dfrac{(a^2+bc)x+b(a+d)}{c(a+d)x+bc+d^2}$. Equality to $x$ on its infinite domain forces the polynomial identity $c(a+d)x^2+(d^2-a^2)x-b(a+d)=0$. Its quadratic coefficient is zero. Since $c\ne0$, $\boxed{a+d=0}$. The determinant condition ensures this is a genuine fractional-linear map rather than a constant with an empty composition domain.
