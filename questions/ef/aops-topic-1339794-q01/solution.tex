The non-prime faces are $1$, $4$ and $6$, so each die avoids a prime with probability $\dfrac{3}{6}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. The dice are independent, so the probability is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{4}}$.
