Both right-angle vertices lie on the circle with the common hypotenuse as diameter, so their distance is at most the hypotenuse $c$. We will attain $\dfrac{1443}{17}>84$, so only $85\le c<100$ need be checked. Testing $a^2+b^2=c^2$ with $1\le a\le b<c$ gives: for 85 the leg pairs are $(13,84),(36,77),(40,75),(51,68)$; for 87,89,90,91,95,97 there is respectively one pair $(60,63),(39,80),(54,72),(35,84),(57,76),(65,72)$; all other values in this range have none. This finite check can be reproduced by looping over $c=85,\ldots,99$ and $a=1,\ldots,\lfloor\dfrac{c}{\sqrt2}\rfloor$ and testing whether $c^2-a^2$ is a square. Thus only 85 allows noncongruent triangles. For sorted leg pairs $(a,b),(u,v)$ with common hypotenuse $c$, the maximum over reflected placements is $\dfrac{au+bv}{c}$: place the vertices at $(\dfrac{a^2}{c},\dfrac{ab}{c})$ and $(\dfrac{v^2}{c},-\dfrac{uv}{c})$ and expand their distance. This placement maximizes the chord because their smaller acute angles differ as little as possible. The six dot products for the four listed 85-pairs are $6936,6820,6375,7215,7072,7140$. The maximum is 7215, from $(36,77),(40,75)$, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{7215}{85}=\dfrac{1443}{17}}$.
