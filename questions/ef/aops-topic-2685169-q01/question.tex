Five different numbers are chosen from $1,2,\dots,7$, and only their product is announced.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that the product does not always determine whether the sum of the five numbers is odd or even.
\item[(b)] Find the only product for which the parity of the sum cannot be determined.
\end{enumerate}
