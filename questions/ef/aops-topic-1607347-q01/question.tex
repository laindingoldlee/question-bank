Two envelopes each contain a whole number of dollars, chosen independently and uniformly at random from $1$ to $100$. You are handed the first envelope and open it; you may accept or reject the money. Then you are handed the second envelope and open it; you may accept it only if it contains more than the amount you have already accepted (if you rejected the first envelope, you may always accept the second). You keep every amount you accept.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Show that the strategy ``accept the first envelope, and accept the second whenever allowed'' gives the greatest possible expected winnings.
\item Find the expected winnings under this strategy.
\end{enumerate}
