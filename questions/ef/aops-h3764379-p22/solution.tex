Let $BC=2a$. Use $B=(-a,0),C=(a,0),A=(0,a)$, so $D=(12-a,0)$ and $E=(a-16,0)$. Since $B,D,E,C$ occur in that order, $a>14$. The determinant and dot product of $\overrightarrow{AD},\overrightarrow{AE}$ give $\tan45^\circ=\dfrac{a(2a-28)}{28a-192}=1$. Thus $a^2-28a+96=0$, or $(a-4)(a-24)=0$. Only $a=24$ is admissible. The area is $\dfrac{1}{2}(2a)a=a^2=\boxed{576}$.
