By scaling, take $s=1$, with vertices $(0,0),(1,0),(1,1),(0,1)$. In the region $x,y\ge\dfrac{1}{2}$, the farthest vertex is $(0,0)$, so the common part there is $\dfrac{1}{2}\le x\le\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}\le y\le\sqrt{1-x^2}$. Four congruent parts give area $4\int_{\dfrac{1}{2}}^{\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}}(\sqrt{1-x^2}-\dfrac{1}{2})\,dx$. Using antiderivative $\dfrac{x\sqrt{1-x^2}+\arcsin x}{2}$ for the square root, the integral is $\dfrac{\pi}{12}-\dfrac{\sqrt3-1}{4}$. Restoring scale gives $\boxed{s^2(1+\dfrac{\pi}{3}-\sqrt3)}$.

draw((0,0)--(1,0)--(1,1)--(0,1)--cycle);draw(arc((0,0),1,0,90));draw(arc((1,0),1,90,180));draw(arc((1,1),1,180,270));draw(arc((0,1),1,270,360));draw((.5,0)--(.5,1),dashed);draw((0,.5)--(1,.5),dashed);label("common",(.5,.5));label("$1$",(.5,0),S);label("$(0,0)$",(0,0),SW);label("$(1,1)$",(1,1),NE);
\end{asy}
