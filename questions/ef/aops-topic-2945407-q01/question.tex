A cat starts at $(0,0)$. A friend sits at a lattice point chosen uniformly at random from those with $|x|,|y|\le10$. Every $5$ seconds the cat meows; each time, independently with probability $\dfrac12$, the friend answers, and then the cat moves $1$ unit up, down, left or right so as to decrease its distance to the friend (it always can while not there). What is the expected number of seconds until the cat reaches the friend?
