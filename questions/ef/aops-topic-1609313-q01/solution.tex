\textbf{The inner sum.}
\[
1+1+2+6+24+120+720+5040+40320+362880+3628800=4037914 .
\]
\textbf{Trailing zeros of $N!$.} The number of trailing zeros of $N!$ is the exponent of $5$ in $N!$ (there are more factors $2$ than $5$), which by Legendre's formula is $\sum_{i\ge1}\left\lfloor\dfrac {N}{5^i}\right\rfloor$. For $N=4037914$:
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccccccc}
5^i&5&25&125&625&3125&15625&78125&390625&1953125\\\hline
\left\lfloor\dfrac {N}{5^i}\right\rfloor&807582&161516&32303&6460&1292&258&51&10&2
\end{array}
\]
and $5^{10}>N$. The sum is
\[
807582+161516+32303+6460+1292+258+51+10+2=\boxed{1009474}.
\]
