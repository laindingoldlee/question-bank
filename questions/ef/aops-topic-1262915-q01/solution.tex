Multiply by $x$ (note $x\ne0$) and divide by $12$: $12+12x^2=25x$, i.e. $12x^2-25x+12=0$. This factors as $(3x-4)(4x-3)=0$, so $x=\boxed{\dfrac{4}{3}\text{ or }\dfrac{3}{4}}$.

The two roots are reciprocals, as expected: replacing $x$ by $\dfrac{1}{x}$ leaves $\dfrac {a}{x}+ax$ unchanged. So for $\dfrac {a}{x}+ax=z$ one can guess a root $t$ and immediately get the other as $\dfrac{1}{t}$; here $x+\dfrac{1}{x}=\dfrac{300}{144}=\dfrac{25}{12}=\dfrac{4}{3}+\dfrac{3}{4}$.
