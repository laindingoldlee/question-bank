The three winners form a set of $3$ of the $20$ people, and every such set is equally likely. There are $\dbinom{20}{3}=\dfrac{20\cdot19\cdot18}{6}=1140$ sets, and exactly one of them is \{Bobby and his two friends\}. So the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{1140}}$.

Drawing the prizes one at a time gives the same result: $\dfrac{3}{20}\cdot\dfrac{2}{19}\cdot\dfrac{1}{18}=\dfrac{6}{6840}=\dfrac{1}{1140}$ (the first prize must go to one of the three, the second to one of the remaining two, the third to the last one).
