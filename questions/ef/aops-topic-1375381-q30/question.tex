Bobby attends an event along with two friends. At the event there is a drawing for three door prizes. Each person in attendance has an equal chance of winning a prize, and no one can win more than one prize. If there are $20$ people at the event, including Bobby and his two friends, what is the probability that Bobby and both of his friends each win a prize? Express your answer as a common fraction.
