Make the leading digits as large as possible. Start with $4$, so the $4$'s occupy positions $1$ and $6$.

Position $2$: a $3$ would need its partner in position $6$, which is taken. A $2$ would put the other $2$ in position $5$; then the $3$'s must be in positions $3,7$ or $4,8$, and the two remaining places ($4,8$ or $3,7$) are not two apart, so the $1$'s cannot fit. So position $2$ holds $1$, and the other $1$ is in position $4$.

Position $3$: a $3$ there puts the other $3$ in position $7$, and the $2$'s fit in positions $5,8$. This gives $\boxed{41312432}$ (confirmed by exhaustive search).
