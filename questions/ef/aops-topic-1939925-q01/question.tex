One player secretly chooses three positive two-digit integers $x,y,z$. A second player chooses three integers $A,B,C$ and announces them. The first player then reveals only the value of $Ax+By+Cz$. The second player wins if he can then determine $x$, $y$ and $z$. Show that the second player can choose $A,B,C$ so that he always wins.
