Take $A=1$, $B=100$, $C=10000$. Then
\[
Ax+By+Cz=10000z+100y+x .
\]
Since $10\le x,y,z\le99$, this number is the six-digit number whose first two digits are $z$, middle two digits are $y$ and last two digits are $x$ (the blocks do not interfere, because each of $x,y$ is less than $100$). So $x$ is the value of the number modulo $100$, $y$ is the next two digits, and $z$ the first two digits. For example, $x=37$, $y=58$, $z=12$ give $125837$. Therefore the choice $\boxed{(A,B,C)=(1,100,10000)}$ always wins.
