$C$ lies on a line parallel to $AB$ at distance $2$. The angle $\angle ACB$ is largest when the circle through $A$ and $B$ is tangent to that line at $C$. With $A=(0,0)$, $B=(4,0)$, this circle is centred at $(2,0)$ with radius $2$, i.e. $AB$ is a diameter.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(4,0);draw(A--B);draw((-1,2)--(5,2),dashed);draw(circle((2,0),2),dotted);pair C=(2,2);draw(A--C--B);dot(C);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,N);
\end{asy}

So the maximum is $\boxed{90^\circ}$.
