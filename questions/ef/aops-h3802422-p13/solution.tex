A regular hexagon of side s comprises six equilateral triangles and has area $\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}s^2$. The required area is $\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}(2^2+6^2-4^2)=\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}\cdot24=\boxed{36\sqrt3}$.

\end{asy}
