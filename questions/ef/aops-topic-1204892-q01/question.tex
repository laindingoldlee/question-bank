Tyson starts with a point $a_1$. To get $a_{n+1}$ from $a_n$ he reflects $a_n$ in the line $x=n$ and then in the line $y=n$. If the sum of all four coordinates of $a_k$ and $a_{k+1}$ is $2016$, find $k$.
