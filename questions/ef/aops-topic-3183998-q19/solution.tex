Condition on the injective images of the five elements of $A$. For injectivity on all ten elements, the five images of $B$ must be precisely the unused five values. Among $10\cdot9\cdot8\cdot7\cdot6$ equally likely injective assignments for $B$, exactly $5!$ work. Thus the probability is $\dfrac{1}{\binom{10}{5}}=\dfrac{1}{252}$, and $m+n=\boxed{253}$.
