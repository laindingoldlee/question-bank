At a carnival, an ice cream costs $\$1.50$ less than a cotton candy. A tourist group buys $8$ cotton candies and $6$ ice creams, pays with a $\$50$ bill and receives $\$10$ in change. How much do $3$ cotton candies and $2$ ice creams cost?
