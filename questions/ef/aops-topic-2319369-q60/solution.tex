The revenue is $R=p(1000-2p)=-2p^2+1000p=125000-2(p-250)^2$, which is largest at $p=250$, inside the allowed range. So set the price at $\boxed{250\text{ dollars}}$, for a revenue of $250\cdot500=\boxed{125000}$ dollars.
