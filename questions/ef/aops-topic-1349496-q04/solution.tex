Write $f(x,y,z)=x^3y+y^3z+z^3x$ and $s=x+y+z$. We show $f\le\dfrac{27}{256}s^4$. The expression is cyclic, so we may assume $x$ is the largest of the three.

\textbf{Case $x\ge y\ge z$.} Then
\[
(x+z)^3y-f=3x^2yz+3xyz^2+yz^3-y^3z-z^3x=yz\left(3x^2-y^2\right)+xz^2(3y-z)+yz^3\ge0,
\]
because $x\ge y$ and $y\ge z$. By AM-GM applied to the four numbers $\dfrac{x+z}{3},\dfrac{x+z}{3},\dfrac{x+z}{3},y$,
\[
(x+z)^3y=27\left(\dfrac{x+z}{3}\right)^3y\le27\left(\dfrac{(x+z)+y}{4}\right)^4=\dfrac{27}{256}s^4 .
\]
\textbf{Case $x\ge z\ge y$.} A direct expansion gives the identity
\[
f(x,y,z)-f(x,z,y)=-(x-y)(y-z)(z-x)(x+y+z).
\]
Here $x-y\ge0$, $y-z\le0$, $z-x\le0$, so the right side is $\le0$ and $f(x,y,z)\le f(x,z,y)$. The triple $(x,z,y)$ falls under the first case, so $f(x,z,y)\le\dfrac{27}{256}s^4$.

Equality needs $z=0$ and $\dfrac{x}{3}=y$, i.e. $(x,y,z)=\left(\dfrac{3}{4}s,\dfrac{1}{4}s,0\right)$. With $s=\dfrac{1}{3}$ this is $\left(\dfrac{1}{4},\dfrac{1}{12},0\right)$, where $f=\dfrac{1}{64}\cdot\dfrac{1}{12}=\dfrac{1}{768}$, and indeed $\dfrac{27}{256}\cdot\dfrac{1}{81}=\dfrac{1}{768}$. So the maximum is $\dfrac{1}{768}$ and $a+b=\boxed{769}$.
