For lap length $L$ and speeds $u,v$, the overall average is $\dfrac{2L}{\dfrac{L}{u}+\dfrac{L}{v}}=\dfrac{2uv}{u+v}$. Equating it to $\dfrac{4}{5}u$ gives $10v=4u+4v$, or $\boxed{u:v=3:2}$.
