Let $f(\theta)=5\cos\theta-4\sin\theta$ for real $\theta$.
\begin{enumerate}\item Express $f$ as $R\cos(\theta+\alpha)$, with $R>0$ and $0<\alpha<\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. Give $R$ exactly and $\alpha$ to three decimal places.
\item Describe a vertical stretch followed by a translation that takes $y=\cos\theta$ to $y=f(\theta)$.
\item Find the range of $g(\theta)=\dfrac{90}{4+f(\theta)^2}$.\end{enumerate}
