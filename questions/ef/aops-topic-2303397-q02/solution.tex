A score of more than $15$ means at least $16$ correct answers, so at least $14$ of the $23$ guesses must be correct. If $K$ is the number of correct guesses,
\[P(K=k)=\dbinom{23}{k}\left(\dfrac15\right)^k\left(\dfrac45\right)^{23-k}=\dfrac{\dbinom{23}{k}4^{23-k}}{5^{23}}.\]
So the probability is
\[\sum_{k=14}^{23}\dfrac{\dbinom{23}{k}4^{23-k}}{5^{23}}=\dfrac{250821645805}{5^{23}}=\boxed{\dfrac{50164329161}{2384185791015625}}\approx2.10\times10^{-5}.\]
The term $k=14$ dominates: $\dbinom{23}{14}4^9=817190\cdot262144\approx2.14\times10^{11}$ of the numerator $\approx2.51\times10^{11}$. So the chance is about $1$ in $47500$.
