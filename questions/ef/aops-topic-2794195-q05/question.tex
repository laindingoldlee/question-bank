Right isosceles triangle $ABC$ has $AB=BC=2$. Reflect $A$ across $BC$ to $A^{\prime}$, $B$ across $AC$ to $B^{\prime}$, and $C$ across $AB$ to $C^{\prime}$. Find $[A^{\prime} B^{\prime} C^{\prime}]$.
