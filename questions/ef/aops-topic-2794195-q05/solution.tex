Use $B=(0,0),A=(2,0),C=(0,2)$. The reflected points are $A^{\prime}=(-2,0)$, $B^{\prime}=(2,2)$, $C^{\prime}=(0,-2)$. Vectors from $A^{\prime}$ are $(4,2),(2,-2)$, with determinant $-12$. Hence the area is $\boxed6$.
