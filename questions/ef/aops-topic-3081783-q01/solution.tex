Each step doubles the preceding term, giving $\boxed{a_n=2^{n-1}}$. Formally the formula gives $ a_1=2^0=1 $, and if it holds at $ n-1 $, then $ a_n=2\cdot2^{n-2}=2^{n-1}$. Induction proves it for all positive $ n $.
