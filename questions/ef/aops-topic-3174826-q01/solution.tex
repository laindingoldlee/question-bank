The components are $\mathbf a=(32,0)$ and $\mathbf b=(3\sqrt2,3\sqrt2)$. Therefore $|\mathbf a+\mathbf b|^2=(32+3\sqrt2)^2+(3\sqrt2)^2=1060+192\sqrt2$. The magnitude is $\boxed{\sqrt{1060+192\sqrt2}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair O=(0,0),A=(32,0),P=(32+3*sqrt(2),3*sqrt(2));draw(O--A,blue,Arrow);draw(A--P,red,Arrow);draw(O--P,dashed,Arrow);label("$\mathbf a$",A/2,S);label("$\mathbf b$",(A+P)/2,E);label("$O$",O,W);
\end{asy}
