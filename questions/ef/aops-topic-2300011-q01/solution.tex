\textbf{First gcd.} $7045000=1409\cdot5000$ and $21735234=1409\cdot15426$. Since $5000=2^3\cdot5^4$ and $15426=2\cdot3^2\cdot857$ have greatest common divisor $2$,
\[\gcd(7045000,21735234)=1409\cdot2=2818.\]
(Without guessing the factor $1409$, the Euclidean algorithm gives the same result: $21735234=3\cdot7045000+600234$, $7045000=11\cdot600234+442426$, $600234=442426+157808$, $442426=2\cdot157808+126810$, $157808=126810+30998$, $126810=4\cdot30998+2818$, $30998=11\cdot2818$.)

\textbf{Second gcd.} $2980000=2980\cdot1000$ and $35906020=2980\cdot12049$. Since $12049$ is odd and does not end in $0$ or $5$, $\gcd(1000,12049)=1$, so
\[\gcd(2980000,35906020)=2980.\]

\textbf{The lcm.} $2818=2\cdot1409$ and $2980=2^2\cdot5\cdot149$ have $\gcd=2$ ($1409$ is a prime), so
\[\operatorname{lcm}(2818,2980)=\dfrac{2818\cdot2980}{2}=2818\cdot1490=\boxed{4198820}.\]
