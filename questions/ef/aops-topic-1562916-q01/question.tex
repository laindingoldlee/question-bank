Air has a density of $1.3$ kg per cubic metre: $10\text{ m}^3$ of air weighs $13$ kg, and the weight of air is directly proportional to its volume. How much does the air in a room $4.2$ m long, $3.5$ m wide and $2.6$ m high weigh?
