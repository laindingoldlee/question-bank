The weight is proportional to the volume with constant of variation $\dfrac{13}{10}=1.3$ kg per $\text{m}^3$ (the density).

The room is a rectangular box:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real L=4.2,Wd=3.5,h=2.6;pair k=(0.5,0.32);
pair p0=(0,0),p1=(L,0),p2=(L,0)+Wd*k,p3=Wd*k;pair u=(0,h);
fill(p0--p1--p1+u--p0+u--cycle,gray(0.93));fill(p1--p2--p2+u--p1+u--cycle,gray(0.85));
draw(p0--p1--p2,linewidth(0.9));draw(p2--p3--p0,dashed);
draw(p0+u--p1+u--p2+u--p3+u--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(p0--p0+u,linewidth(0.9));draw(p1--p1+u,linewidth(0.9));draw(p2--p2+u,linewidth(0.9));draw(p3--p3+u,dashed);
label("$4.2$ m",(p0+p1)/2,S);label("$3.5$ m",(p1+p2)/2,SE);label("$2.6$ m",p0+u/2,W);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Its volume is
\[
4.2\cdot3.5\cdot2.6=14.7\cdot2.6=38.22\text{ m}^3 .
\]
Hence the air weighs
\[
1.3\cdot38.22=\boxed{49.686\text{ kg}}\approx 49.7\text{ kg}.
\]
