On a chessboard, a pawn moves one square forward, and a knight moves two squares in one direction and then one square sideways. Measuring each move as the straight-line distance between the centres of the starting and finishing squares, with the side of a square as the unit, find the ratio of the distance of one pawn move to the distance of one knight move.
