Let $\alpha$ be a root of $x^2-x-1$, so $\alpha^2=\alpha+1$. Multiplying repeatedly by $\alpha$ and reducing,
\[
\alpha^3=2\alpha+1,\quad\alpha^4=3\alpha+2,\quad\alpha^5=5\alpha+3,\quad\alpha^6=8\alpha+5,\quad\alpha^7=13\alpha+8
\]
(the coefficients are Fibonacci numbers: $\alpha^k=F_k\alpha+F_{k-1}$). Since $\alpha$ is a root of $mx^7+nx^6+1$,
\[
m(13\alpha+8)+n(8\alpha+5)+1=(13m+8n)\alpha+(8m+5n+1)=0 .
\]
The number $\alpha=\dfrac{1\pm\sqrt5}{2}$ is irrational, while both brackets are integers, so both brackets are $0$:
\[
13m+8n=0,\qquad 8m+5n=-1 .
\]
Multiplying the first by $5$ and the second by $8$ and subtracting gives $65m-64m=8$, so $m=\boxed{8}$, and then $n=-13$. (Conversely, with these values both roots of $x^2-x-1$ are roots of $8x^7-13x^6+1$, so $x^2-x-1$ divides it.)
