The writer types for 200 minutes, producing 9000 words. Deleting 9000 words at 50 per minute requires 180 minutes, so deletion starts 180 minutes before 7:20 PM, at $\boxed{4{:}20\text{ PM}}$. At that moment 900 words exist; the net depletion rate is 5 words per minute, so the text stays positive until the endpoint.
