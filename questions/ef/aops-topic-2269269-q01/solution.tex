By Legendre's formula, $15!=2^{11}\cdot3^{6}\cdot5^{3}\cdot7^{2}\cdot11\cdot13$ (for example the exponent of $2$ is $7+3+1=11$). A divisor $2^a3^b5^c7^d11^e13^f$ is a fourth power when all exponents are multiples of $4$: $a\in\{0,4,8\}$, $b\in\{0,4\}$, and $c=d=e=f=0$. So there are $3\cdot2=\boxed{6}$ such divisors.
