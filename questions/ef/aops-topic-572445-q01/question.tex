Find all pairs of integers $(n,x)$ with $n>5$ and $x>4$ such that
\[\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}=2^x-1,\]
that is, the $n$-th triangular number is one less than a power of $2$.

You may use the \emph{Ramanujan--Nagell theorem}: the only solutions of $2^k-7=m^2$ in positive integers $k,m$ are
\[(k,m)=(3,1),\ (4,3),\ (5,5),\ (7,11),\ (15,181).\]
