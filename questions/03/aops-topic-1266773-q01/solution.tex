Since $2500=50^2$, we look for $x^2+y^2=50^2$.

The solutions with $xy=0$ are $(\pm50,0)$ and $(0,\pm50)$: $4$ pairs.

For $x,y\ne0$, $(|x|,|y|,50)$ is a Pythagorean triple with hypotenuse $50=2\cdot5^2$. The primitive triples with hypotenuse dividing $50$ have hypotenuse $5$ or $25$: $(3,4,5)$ and $(7,24,25)$. Scaling gives $(30,40,50)$ and $(14,48,50)$, and no others. Each unordered pair $\{a,b\}$ with $a\ne b$ gives $2$ orders and $4$ sign choices, so $2\cdot 8=16$ pairs.

The total is $4+16=\boxed{20}$. (This agrees with the formula $4(d_1-d_3)$, where $d_1=5$ and $d_3=0$ count the divisors of $2500$ that are $1$ and $3$ modulo $4$.)
