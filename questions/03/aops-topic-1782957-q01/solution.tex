$6^{333}\cdot7^{222}\cdot8^{111}=2^{333}3^{333}\cdot7^{222}\cdot2^{333}=2^{666}\cdot3^{333}\cdot7^{222}$. It has $\boxed{3}$ distinct prime factors, and $666+333+222=\boxed{1221}$ prime factors counted with multiplicity.
