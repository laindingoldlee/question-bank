The patient weighs $\dfrac{143}{2.2}=65$ kg, so the dose is $650$ micrograms per minute, i.e. $650\cdot60=39000$ micrograms $=\boxed{39\text{ mg}}$ per hour. (The concentration, $0.5$ mg/mL, would give the pump rate $78$ mL/h, but it is not needed for the dose.)
