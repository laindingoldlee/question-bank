A box holds $13$ tabby cats and $15$ striped cats, and there are plenty of striped cats outside. Repeatedly take two cats out of the box at random. If they have different patterns, put the tabby back. If they have the same pattern, keep both out and put a striped cat (from outside) into the box. Continue until one cat remains in the box. What is its pattern?
