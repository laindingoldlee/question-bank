With $c$ correct, $w$ wrong and $s$ blank: $3c-w=40$ and $c+w+s=20$. Then $w=3c-40\ge0$ gives $c\ge14$, and $c+w=4c-40\le20$ gives $c\le15$. So $(c,s,w)=(14,4,2)$ or $(15,0,5)$: $\boxed{2}$ ways.
