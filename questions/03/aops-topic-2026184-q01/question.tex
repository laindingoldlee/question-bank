A disc of radius $r$ is shaded. A square is inscribed in its boundary circle and the inside of the square is left unshaded. A circle is inscribed in that square and the disc inside it is shaded; a square is inscribed in that circle and left unshaded; and this continues forever, alternating shaded discs and unshaded squares. What is the total shaded area, in terms of $r$?
