Testing small divisors of the constant term, $x=5$ and $x=-3$ are roots. Dividing by $(x-5)(x+3)=x^2-2x-15$,
\[x^4+6x^3-5344x^2+10506x+79695=\left(x^2-2x-15\right)\left(x^2+8x-5313\right),\]
and $x^2+8x-5313=(x+77)(x-69)$. The solutions are $\boxed{-77,\ -3,\ 5,\ 69}$.
