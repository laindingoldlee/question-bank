Exactly one digit must have exactly one factor of 2, so it is $2$ or $6$, and the other must be odd, one of $1,3,5,7,9$. Either digit may be in the tens place. The count is $2\cdot5\cdot2=\boxed{20}$. Zero is excluded because its product is divisible by 4.
