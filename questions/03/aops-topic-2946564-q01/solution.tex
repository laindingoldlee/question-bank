By the triangle inequality, $12-9<x<12+9$, so $4\le x\le20$: $17$ integers.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

\end{asy}

Scalene means all three sides differ, so $x\ne9$ and $x\ne12$. That leaves $\boxed{15}$ values. (The posted answer $17$ forgets the scalene condition.)
