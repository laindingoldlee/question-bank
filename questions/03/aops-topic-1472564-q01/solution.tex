Cutting a log into $6$ pieces takes $5$ cuts, so each cut takes $\dfrac{35}{5}=7$ minutes. Four pieces need $3$ cuts, which take $3\cdot7=\boxed{21}$ minutes. (The tempting answer $\dfrac{35}{6}\cdot4$ counts pieces instead of cuts.)
