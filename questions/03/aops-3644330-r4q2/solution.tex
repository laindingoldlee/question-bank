Assume nonnegative whole-dollar contributions. If Daniel pays $5d$ with $0\le d\le24$, Andrew can pay any integer from zero through $\lfloor\dfrac{(120-5d)}{2}\rfloor$; Eric pays the remainder. Thus the count is $\sum_{d=0}^{24}[\lfloor\dfrac{(120-5d)}{2}\rfloor+1]=\boxed{769}$. For even d these terms are $61,56,\ldots,1$, summing to 403; for odd d they are $58,53,\ldots,3$, summing to 366. Without whole-dollar restrictions for Andrew and Eric, infinitely many splits are possible.
