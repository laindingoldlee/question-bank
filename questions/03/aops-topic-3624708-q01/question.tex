A positive integer is perfect if its proper positive divisors sum to itself. For this exercise you may use that the only perfect numbers at most 10000 are $6,28,496,8128$. Call an integer jealous if it is one more or one less than a perfect number.\begin{enumerate}\item List all jealous positive integers below 10000.\item Prove that every even perfect number produces two distinct positive jealous integers.\item Are any integers in your list perfect?\item Explain why this finite list alone cannot justify an asymptotic formula for the count of jealous numbers.\end{enumerate}
