\begin{enumerate}\item The neighbors are $\boxed{5,7,27,29,495,497,8127,8129}$. Both are jealous by definition.\item None of the eight listed numbers occurs in the given perfect-number list, so none is perfect.\item A finite initial list provides no control over how many perfect numbers occur beyond the bound or how their neighbors may overlap.
