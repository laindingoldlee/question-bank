Pentagon $ABCDE$ is inscribed in a circle. Side $AB=24$, side $BC=32$, and the remaining three sides all have length $20$. The angle at vertex $B$ is a right angle. The area of $ABCDE$ is $a+b\sqrt c$, where $a$, $b$ and $c$ are integers and $c$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. What is $a+b+c$?
