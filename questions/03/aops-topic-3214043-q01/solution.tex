Let $a,b,c\ge0$ be the distances in the first, second and third hours. The conditions give $a+b=100$ and $b+c=100$, so $a+b+c=200-b\le200$. Equality is possible by traveling $100$ kilometers in each outer hour and stopping during the middle hour. Thus the maximum is $\boxed{200\text{ km}}$.
