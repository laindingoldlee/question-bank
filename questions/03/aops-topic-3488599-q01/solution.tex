Take $h_1=48u$. Then $h_2=10u$, $h_3=120u$, $h_4=3u$. The greatest height gives $120u=10$, so $u=\dfrac{1}{12}$. The least is $3u=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{4}\text{ foot}}$.
