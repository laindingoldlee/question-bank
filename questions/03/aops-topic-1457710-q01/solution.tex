Units digits of powers of $3$ cycle $3,9,7,1$; since $17\equiv1\pmod4$, $3^{17}$ ends in $3$. Powers of $7$ cycle $7,9,3,1$; since $23\equiv3\pmod4$, $7^{23}$ ends in $3$. The product ends in $3\cdot3=\boxed{9}$.
