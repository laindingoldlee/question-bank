A fair coin is flipped repeatedly, one flip at a time, and the flipping stops as soon as the number of heads so far is greater than the number of tails so far.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that the probability that the flipping ever stops is $1$.
\item[(b)] Prove that the expected number of flips until it stops is infinite.
\end{enumerate}
