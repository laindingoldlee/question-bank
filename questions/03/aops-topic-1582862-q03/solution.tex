\textbf{Distances.} The sound reaches Dmitri $5$ seconds after the flash and Tara $5+2=7$ seconds after it. So the strike is
\[
5\cdot1100=5500\text{ feet from Dmitri}\qquad\text{and}\qquad7\cdot1100=7700\text{ feet from Tara}.
\]
So it lies on the circle of radius $5500$ ft about Dmitri $D$ and on the circle of radius $7700$ ft about Tara $T$, where $DT=5280$ ft.

\textbf{The two circles meet in two points.} Since $7700-5500<5280<7700+5500$, the circles cross at two points, symmetric about the line $DT$. In coordinates with $D=(0,0)$ and $T=(5280,0)$ (in feet), subtracting the equations $x^2+y^2=5500^2$ and $(x-5280)^2+y^2=7700^2$ gives
\[
10560x-5280^2=5500^2-7700^2,\qquad x=\dfrac{5280^2-29{,}040{,}000}{10560}=-110 ,
\]
and then $y=\pm\sqrt{5500^2-110^2}\approx\pm5498.9$.

(Figure in thousands of feet.) So there are exactly \textbf{two possible locations} $L_1$, $L_2$: each is $5500$ ft from Dmitri and $7700$ ft from Tara; measured along the line from Tara to Dmitri they are $110$ ft beyond Dmitri, and they lie about $5499$ ft (just over a mile) to either side of that line.
