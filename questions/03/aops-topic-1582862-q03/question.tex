While talking on the phone to his friend Tara, Dmitri sees a lightning flash, and $5$ seconds later he hears the thunder. Two seconds after that, Tara, who lives $1$ mile ($5280$ feet) away, hears it. Sound travels at $1100$ feet per second, and the light of the flash arrives instantly. Assume that the lightning strikes the ground and that the ground is a flat plane. Describe all the possible locations of the lightning strike.
