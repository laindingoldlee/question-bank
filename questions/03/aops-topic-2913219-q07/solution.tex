Since $2^{20}=1048576\equiv76\pmod{100}$, a direct period calculation gives $2^{n+20}\equiv2^n\pmod{100}$ for $n\ge2$: the difference is $2^n(2^{20}-1)$, divisible by $4$ and $25$. Since $1073\equiv13\pmod{20}$, the last two digits agree with $2^{13}=8192$. Thus the answer is $9^2+2^2=\boxed{85}$.
