We have $(X+1)B=2025$, so $X+1$ is a divisor of $2025=3^4\cdot 5^2$ greater than $1$. The sum of all divisors is $(1+3+9+27+81)(1+5+25)=121\cdot 31=3751$, and there are $(4+1)(2+1)=15$ divisors. Summing $d-1$ over divisors $d>1$ gives $(3751-1)-(15-1)=\boxed{3736}$.
