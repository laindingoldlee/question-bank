Let $A$ be the adjacency matrix of the rook-move graph, and $J,I$ the all-ones and identity matrices. Each square has 14 neighbours. Two adjacent distinct squares have 6 common neighbours; two nonadjacent squares have 2. Thus $A^2=12I+4A+2J$, and $AJ=14J$. Hence $A^3=12A+4A^2+28J$. For the adjacent start and finish, the counts for lengths 1,2,3 are $1,6,12+24+28=64$. Their sum is $\boxed{71}$.
