Let $p_k=a^k+b^k+c^k$. Vieta gives $p_1=0$, $p_2=10$. The root identity $r^3=5r-3$ gives $p_3=-9$ and $p_5=5p_3-3p_2=-75$. The displayed sum is $\sum r^4(p_1-r)=-p_5=75$, so its absolute value is $\boxed{75}$.
