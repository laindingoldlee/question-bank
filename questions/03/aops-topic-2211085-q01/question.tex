Three circles of radius $1$ are pairwise externally tangent.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the radius $r$ of the small circle that fits in the gap between them and is tangent to all three, and the radius $R$ of the large circle that is tangent to all three and encloses them.
\item[(b)] Descartes' circle theorem states that if four circles are mutually tangent with ``curvatures'' $k_1,k_2,k_3,k_4$, then
\[(k_1+k_2+k_3+k_4)^2=2\left(k_1^2+k_2^2+k_3^2+k_4^2\right),\]
where the curvature of a circle of radius $\rho$ is $\dfrac{1}{\rho}$, except that a circle which contains the other three gets the curvature $-\dfrac{1}{\rho}$. Solve the equation for $k_4$ when $k_1=k_2=k_3=1$, and check that the two solutions correspond to the circles in (a). Show that the formula would fail for the enclosing circle if its curvature were taken as $+\dfrac{1}{R}$.
\end{enumerate}
