Let $a$ be a nonzero real number and let $x,y,z$ be nonzero real numbers with
\[x+y+z=a\qquad\text{and}\qquad\dfrac{1}{x}+\dfrac{1}{y}+\dfrac{1}{z}=\dfrac{1}{a} .\]
Find the value of $(x+y)(y+z)(z+x)$.
