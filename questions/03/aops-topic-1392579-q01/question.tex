Marcus and Al are playing rock-paper-scissors. The first person to win $10$ rounds does not have to do the dishes. The score is now $9$ to $7$ in favour of Marcus. Ties are ignored, and each counted round is equally likely to be won by either player. What is the probability that Marcus will not have to do the dishes? Express your answer as a common fraction.

(A student argues: Marcus wins in the cases W, LW, LLW and loses in the case LLL, so the answer is $\dfrac{3}{4}$. Find the error.)
