\textbf{(a)}
\[
a_{n+1}-\sqrt b=\dfrac{a_n^2+b}{2a_n}-\sqrt b=\dfrac{a_n^2-2a_n\sqrt b+b}{2a_n}=\dfrac{\left(a_n-\sqrt b\right)^2}{2a_n}.
\]

\textbf{(b)} All $a_n$ are positive (by induction, as averages of positive numbers). By (a), $a_{n+1}-\sqrt b\ge0$, so $a_n\ge\sqrt b$ for every $n\ge1$. Then for $n\ge1$, $2a_n\ge2\sqrt b$, and (a) gives
\[
0\le e_{n+1}=\dfrac{e_n^2}{2a_n}\le\dfrac{e_n^2}{2\sqrt b}.
\]
So each error is at most a constant times the square of the previous one (quadratic convergence). If $e_n\approx10^{-k}$, then $e_{n+1}$ is about $10^{-2k}$ times the constant $\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt b}$: the number of correct decimal places roughly doubles with every step.

\textbf{(c)}
\[
a_1=\dfrac{1}{2}\left(1+2\right)=\dfrac{3}{2},\qquad a_2=\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{3}{2}+\dfrac{4}{3}\right)=\dfrac{17}{12},\qquad a_3=\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{17}{12}+\dfrac{24}{17}\right)=\dfrac{577}{408}.
\]
So $\boxed{a_1=\dfrac{3}{2},\ a_2=\dfrac{17}{12},\ a_3=\dfrac{577}{408}}$. Their errors are about $0.086$, $0.0025$ and $0.0000021$, as (b) predicts ($\sqrt2=1.41421356\ldots$ and $\dfrac{577}{408}=1.41421568\ldots$).
