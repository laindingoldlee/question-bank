To approximate $\sqrt b$ (where $b>0$), start with a guess $a_0>0$ and repeat
\[
a_{n+1}=\dfrac{1}{2}\left(a_n+\dfrac{b}{a_n}\right).
\]
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $a_{n+1}-\sqrt b=\dfrac{\left(a_n-\sqrt b\right)^2}{2a_n}$.
\item[(b)] Deduce that $a_n\ge\sqrt b$ for all $n\ge1$, and that for $n\ge1$ the error $e_n=a_n-\sqrt b$ satisfies $0\le e_{n+1}\le\dfrac{e_n^2}{2\sqrt b}$. Explain why this means the number of correct digits roughly doubles at each step.
\item[(c)] For $b=2$ and $a_0=1$, compute $a_1$, $a_2$, $a_3$.
\end{enumerate}
