Mrs.\ Munch and Mrs.\ Crunch each bought the same number of identical boxes of cookies. Each woman took either $2$ or $3$ days to finish each of her boxes (one box at a time). Mrs.\ Munch took $43$ days to empty all her boxes, and Mrs.\ Crunch took $62$ days. How many boxes did each woman buy?
