The first $15$ odd numbers are $1,3,5,\dots,29$. Writing the sum forwards and backwards and adding term by term gives $15$ pairs, each adding up to $1+29=30$; so twice the sum is $15\cdot30=450$, and the sum is $\boxed{225}$. (In general the first $n$ odd numbers add up to $n^2$, and $15^2=225$.)
