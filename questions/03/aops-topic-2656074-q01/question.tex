A rabbit digs $100$ holes labelled $1$ to $100$. For $k=1,2,\dots,50$ in turn, he puts one carrot into every hole whose label is a multiple of $k$ (so for $k=1$ every hole gets a carrot, for $k=2$ the holes $2,4,\dots,100$ do, and so on). Just as he is about to start on the multiples of $51$, robbers take all the carrots he has not yet hidden. How many carrots are hidden in the holes?
