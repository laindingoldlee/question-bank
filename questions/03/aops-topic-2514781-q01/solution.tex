Since $2985=3\cdot5\cdot199$, a solution would satisfy $x^2\equiv14\equiv2\pmod3$. But every integer is $\equiv0,1,2\pmod3$, and then $x^2\equiv0,1,4\equiv1\pmod3$, respectively. So squares are $0$ or $1$ modulo $3$, never $2$, and there is no solution. $\blacksquare$
