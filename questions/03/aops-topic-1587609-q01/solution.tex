Each year the number of members is multiplied by $1.5=\dfrac{3}{2}$. After three years it is
\[
32\cdot\left(\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^3=32\cdot\dfrac{27}{8}=\boxed{\textbf{(C)}\ 108}.
\]
(The yearly counts are $48$, $72$, $108$. The sum $32+32\cdot1.5+32\cdot1.5^2=152$ adds up the memberships of different years, which is not what is asked.)
