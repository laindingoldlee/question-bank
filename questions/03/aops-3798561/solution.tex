If their occupied positions are $s_1<\cdots<s_5$, the nonadjacency condition is $s_{i+1}\ge s_i+2$. Define $t_i=s_i-(i-1)$. These are any five distinct increasing positions in $\{1,\ldots,10\}$, giving $\binom{10}5$ seat sets. Permuting the five people gives $5!$ orders. Hence there are $\binom{10}5\cdot5!=\boxed{30240}$ arrangements.
