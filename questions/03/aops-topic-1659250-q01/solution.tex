Let $\theta$ be the angle of elevation of the sun. The metre stick and its shadow form a right triangle with legs $1$ and $2$, so $\tan\theta=\dfrac{1}{2}$.

Look at the vertical plane through the centre $O$ of the sphere in the direction of the rays. The sphere appears as a circle of radius $r$ touching the ground at $P$, and the far end $T$ of the shadow is where the last ray grazing the sphere (touching it at $K$) meets the ground, with $PT=10$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(9cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real ta=0.5;real th=atan(ta);real r=10*(sqrt(5)-2);
pair P=(0,0),O=(0,r),T=(10,0);
pair d=(-cos(th),sin(th));
pair K=T+dot(O-T,d)*d;
filldraw(circle(O,r),palegray,linewidth(0.9));
draw((-3.5,0)--(17,0),linewidth(0.9));
draw(T--T+12.5*d,orange+linewidth(0.9));
draw(O--T,dashed);draw(O--P,dashed);draw(O--K,dashed);
pair s0=(14,0),s1=(14,1),s2=(16,0);
draw(s0--s1,linewidth(1.6));draw(s1--s1+2.6*d,orange);draw(s1--s2,orange+linewidth(0.9));
draw(arc(T,3.0,180-degrees(th),180),red);label("$\theta$",T+3.9*dir(180-degrees(th)/4),red+fontsize(8pt));
dot(O);dot(P);dot(T);
label("$O$",O,W);label("$P$",P,S);label("$T$",T,S);label("$K$",K,NE);
label("$r$",(O+P)/2,W);label("$10$",(5,0),S);
label("$1$",(s0+s1)/2,W);label("$2$",(s0+s2)/2,S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The ground line and the grazing ray are the two tangents from $T$ to the circle, so $O$ lies on the bisector of the angle $\theta$ between them. In the right triangle $OPT$ (right angle at $P$),
\[
\tan\dfrac{\theta}{2}=\dfrac{OP}{PT}=\dfrac{r}{10}.
\]
From $\tan\theta=\dfrac{1}{2}$ we get $\sin\theta=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt5}$ and $\cos\theta=\dfrac{2}{\sqrt5}$, so
\[
\tan\dfrac{\theta}{2}=\dfrac{\sin\theta}{1+\cos\theta}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt5+2}=\sqrt5-2 .
\]
Hence $r=10\left(\sqrt5-2\right)=\boxed{10\sqrt5-20}$ metres, about $2.36$ m.
