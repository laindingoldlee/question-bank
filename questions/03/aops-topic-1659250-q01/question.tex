A sphere and a metre stick stand on a horizontal field on a sunny day; the stick is perpendicular to the field. At a certain moment the shadow of the metre stick is $2$ metres long, and the shadow of the sphere reaches $10$ metres from the point where the sphere touches the ground. Find the radius of the sphere. (Assume all the sun's rays are parallel.)
