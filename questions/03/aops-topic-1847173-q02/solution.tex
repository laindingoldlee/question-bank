\textbf{(a)} Each painting goes to one of $3$ heirs, independently: $3^{10}=59049$ ways.

\textbf{(b)} Subtract the distributions in which some heir gets nothing, by inclusion--exclusion. The distributions that leave out a particular heir number $2^{10}$, and those that leave out two particular heirs number $1^{10}=1$. So the count is
\[
3^{10}-\dbinom31 2^{10}+\dbinom32 1^{10}=59049-3072+3=55980 .
\]
So the answers are $\boxed{59049\text{ and }55980}$.
