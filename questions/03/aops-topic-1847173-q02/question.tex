An art collector owns $10$ different original paintings and wants to leave them to three heirs. In how many ways can the paintings be distributed
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] if an heir may receive no painting;
\item[(b)] if every heir must receive at least one painting?
\end{enumerate}
