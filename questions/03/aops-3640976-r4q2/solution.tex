Let the four vectors from centroid E to the vertices have common squared length R squared and common pairwise dot product c. Their sum is zero, so dotting with one gives $R^2+3c=0$. Therefore $\cos\angle AEB=\dfrac{c}{R^2}=\boxed{-\dfrac{1}{3}}$.
