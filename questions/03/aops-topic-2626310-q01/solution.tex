Number the positions $1$ to $6$ and let $A_i$ ($i=1,2,3,4$) be the set of orders in which positions $i,i+1,i+2$ are increasing. We count the complement by inclusion-exclusion; if a set of positions must be increasing in a prescribed way, divide $6!$ accordingly.
\begin{itemize}
\item $|A_i|=\dfrac{6!}{3!}=120$, total $480$.
\item Pairs: $A_i\cap A_{i+1}$ forces $4$ consecutive increasing positions: $\dfrac{6!}{4!}=30$, three such pairs; $A_1\cap A_3$ and $A_2\cap A_4$ force $5$ increasing: $6$ each; $A_1\cap A_4$ forces two disjoint increasing triples: $\dfrac{6!}{3!\,3!}=20$. Total $90+12+20=122$.
\item Triples: $A_1A_2A_3$ and $A_2A_3A_4$ force $5$ increasing ($6$ each); $A_1A_2A_4$ and $A_1A_3A_4$ force all $6$ ($1$ each). Total $14$.
\item All four: $1$.
\end{itemize}
So $|A_1\cup\cdots\cup A_4|=480-122+14-1=371$, and the answer is $720-371=\boxed{349}$.
