Circle $O$ in the coordinate plane has its centre at the origin and has diameter $AB$. Square $ABCD$, with side $AB$, has area $212$. The line $13x+5y=9$ meets circle $O$ at $E$ and $F$, where $E$ is in quadrant II. The line $y=x$ is translated $5$ units to the left, and the translated line meets circle $O$ at the lattice points $G$ and $H$. Find the area of triangle $EGH$.
