For SIRI (letters I, I, R, S) the list begins IIRS, IISR, \textbf{IRIS}. For HIS the list is HIS, HSI, IHS, ISH, \textbf{SHI}, SIH. The answers are $\boxed{\text{IRIS}}$ and $\boxed{\text{SHI}}$.
