The expression is defined when $x\ne-1$, $y\ne2$ and $y\ne z$. Then $1+x=x+1$ cancels, and $\dfrac{y-z}{z-y}=-1$, so
\[
\dfrac{(1+x)(2+y)(y-z)}{2(2-y)(z-y)(x+1)}=-\dfrac{2+y}{2(2-y)}=\dfrac{2+y}{2(y-2)}=\boxed{\dfrac{y+2}{2(y-2)}} .
\]
Since $2(y-2)=2y-4$, the student's answer $\dfrac{2+y}{2y-4}$ is the same expression, so both answers are correct (for $x\ne-1$, $y\ne z$).
