\textbf{(a)} The arc is $\dfrac{1}{9}$ of the circle, so the whole circumference is $9\cdot12=108$ m, and the radius is
\[
r=\dfrac{108}{2\pi}\approx17.19\text{ m}\approx\boxed{17\text{ m}}.
\]
\textbf{(b)} The central angle is $\dfrac{360^\circ}{9}=40^\circ$. For the same angle, the arc length is proportional to the radius, so at half the distance the arc is about $\dfrac{12}{2}=6$ m long.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0);real R=3.2;
draw(arc(O,R,50,90),linewidth(1));draw(arc(O,R/2,50,90),linewidth(1));
draw(O--R*dir(50),dashed);draw(O--R*dir(90),dashed);
draw(arc(O,0.6,50,90));label("$40^\circ$",0.95*dir(70),fontsize(8pt));
for(int i=0;i<8;++i) dot(R*dir(52.5+5*i));
for(int i=0;i<4;++i) dot(R/2*dir(55+10*i),blue);
label("$12$ m",(R+0.15)*dir(70),dir(70));label("$r$",0.75*R*dir(50),SE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The eight desks occupy $12$ m, i.e.\ $1.5$ m per desk, so the shorter arc holds $\dfrac{6}{1.5}=\boxed{4}$ desks.
