The reading room of the Library of Congress has desks along arcs of concentric circles.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] An arc on the outermost circle with eight desks is about $12$ metres long and makes up $\dfrac{1}{9}$ of its circle. To the nearest metre, how far are these desks from the centre of the circle?
\item[(b)] How many desks, spaced in the same way, would fit along an arc with the same central angle but half as far from the centre?
\end{enumerate}
Use $3.14159$ for $\pi$.
