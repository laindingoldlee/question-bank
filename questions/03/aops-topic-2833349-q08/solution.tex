Jonathan's time is $\dfrac{4}{v}+\dfrac{4}{3v}+\dfrac{4}{9v}=\dfrac{52}{9v}$. Mary's is $\dfrac{3}{v}+\dfrac{3}{2v}+\dfrac{3}{4v}+\dfrac{3}{8v}=\dfrac{45}{8v}$. For equal distances, the speed ratio is the reciprocal time ratio: $\dfrac{52}{9}\cdot\dfrac{8}{45}=\dfrac{416}{405}$. Hence $\boxed{10000a+b=4160405}$.
