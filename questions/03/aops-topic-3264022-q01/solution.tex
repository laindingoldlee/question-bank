Let $t=2^x>0$, so $8^x=t^3$. Then $t^3+t-10=(t-2)(t^2+2t+5)=0$. Since $t^2+2t+5=(t+1)^2+4>0$, we have $t=2$. Therefore $\boxed{x=1}$.
