On planet $M$, creatures have $9$ fingers, so they do arithmetic in base $9$. The $M$-root of a positive integer is found by adding its base-$9$ digits, and if the result has more than one base-$9$ digit, adding its digits again, and so on until a single digit remains. How many numbers between $100_9$ and $1000_9$ inclusive have an $M$-root of $1$?
