Divide: $38792265\approx 10101\cdot3840.4$. Indeed
\[
10101\cdot3840=38400000+101\cdot3840=38400000+387840=38787840,
\]
and $38792265-38787840=4425$. The next multiple is $38787840+10101=38797941$, which is $38797941-38792265=5676$ away. Since $4425<5676$ (equivalently, $4425$ is less than half of $10101$), the nearest multiple is
\[
\boxed{38787840}=10101\cdot3840 .
\]
