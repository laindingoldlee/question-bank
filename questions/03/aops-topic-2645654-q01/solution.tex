By the binomial theorem,
\[(x+3)^n=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom nk3^{n-k}x^k.\]
The coefficient of $x^n$ is $1$. For $k<n$, the coefficient $\dbinom nk3^{n-k}$ contains the factor $3^{n-k}$ with $n-k\ge1$, so it is divisible by $3$ (and at least $3$). So the statement is $\boxed{\text{true}}$ for every $n\ge1$. $\blacksquare$
