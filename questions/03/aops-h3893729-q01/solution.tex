For $a\ge7$, $4a^2+3<2^{a+1}$. This holds at $a=7$, and induction follows because $4(a+1)^2+3<2(4a^2+3)$ for $a\ge3$. Therefore $(2^a)^2<b^2<(2^a+1)^2$, impossible. Checking $a=1,2,\ldots,6$ gives $b^2=12,36,104,324,1128,4244$; only $36$ and $324$ are squares. Thus $(a,b)=(2,6),(4,18)$, and the greatest sum is $\boxed{22}$.
