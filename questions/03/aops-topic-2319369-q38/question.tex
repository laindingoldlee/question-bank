A transit (an angle-measuring instrument) is $200$ feet from the base of a building, and a man stands on top of the building. The angles of elevation from the transit to the top and to the bottom of the man (the man's head and feet) are $45^\circ$ and $44^\circ$. How tall is the man? (Ignore the height of the transit.)
