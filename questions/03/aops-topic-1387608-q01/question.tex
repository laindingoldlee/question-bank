Steve has a calculator that adds $6$ to any number entered. Jordan has a calculator that squares any number entered. Alice gives Steve a number $a$; Steve enters it, gives the result to Jordan, who enters that, and Jordan gives the result to Alice. Adam does the same with a number $b$. Alice and Adam get back the same number, and $a\ne b$. What is $a+b$?
