The seven distinct seventh roots of unity are $1,\zeta,\ldots,\zeta^6$. Factoring and cancelling $x-1$ gives $\prod_{k=1}^{6}(x-\zeta^k)=1+x+x^2+\cdots+x^6$. Substitute $x=1$ into this polynomial identity to obtain $\boxed{7}$. Cancellation is justified as a polynomial identity before the substitution, so no division by zero is involved.
