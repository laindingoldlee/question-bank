Let the car drop Harry after $x$ miles, and let Dick have walked $y$ miles when Tom picks him up. From the start until the pickup, the car drives $x$ miles forward and $x-y$ miles back while Dick walks $y$ miles, so
\[\dfrac{2x-y}{25}=\dfrac{y}{5}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 2x-y=5y\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x=3y .\]
Harry rides $x$ and walks $100-x$; Dick walks $y$ and rides $100-y$. They arrive together, so
\[\dfrac{x}{25}+\dfrac{100-x}{5}=\dfrac {y}{5}+\dfrac{100-y}{25}.\]
Multiplying by $25$: $x+500-5x=5y+100-y$, so $400=4x+4y$ and $x+y=100$. With $x=3y$ this gives $y=25$ and $x=75$.

The trip takes Harry $\dfrac{75}{25}+\dfrac{25}{5}=3+5=8$ hours (Dick: $\dfrac{25}{5}+\dfrac{75}{25}=8$ hours as well; Tom arrives with Dick). The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(D)}\ 8}$.
