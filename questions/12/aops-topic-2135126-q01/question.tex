An integer $n$ is called \emph{even} if $n=2k$ for some integer $k$, and \emph{odd} if $n=2k+1$ for some integer $k$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Decide whether $-7$ and $-10$ are odd or even, and explain why every integer, positive, negative or zero, is either odd or even but not both.
\item[(b)] How many positive divisors does $24$ have, and how many of them are odd and how many even?
\item[(c)] How many divisors does $24$ have if negative divisors are also counted, and how many of these are odd and how many even?
\end{enumerate}
