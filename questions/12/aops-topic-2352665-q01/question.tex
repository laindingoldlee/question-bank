Consider all permutations of the $26$ letters of the English alphabet that start with $z$. In how many of them is the number of letters between $z$ and $y$ less than the number of letters between $y$ and $x$?

$\textbf{(A) }6\cdot23!\qquad\textbf{(B) }6\cdot24!\qquad\textbf{(C) }156\cdot23!\qquad\textbf{(D) }156\cdot24!$
