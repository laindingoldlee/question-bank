\textbf{(c)}: for $x>0$, $\dfrac{3x^{\dfrac{3}{2}}}{\sqrt{x}}=3x^{\dfrac{3}{2}-\dfrac{1}{2}}=3x$, so (c) equals the polynomial $x^2+3x$.

The others are not polynomials:
\begin{itemize}
\item (a) contains $-2x^{-2}$, a negative power of $x$; it is unbounded as $x\to0^+$, which no polynomial is.
\item (b) $\sqrt{2x}-1=\sqrt2\,x^{\dfrac{1}{2}}-1$ involves a fractional power. If it were a polynomial $p(x)$, then $(p(x)+1)^2=2x$ would force $p$ to have degree $\dfrac{1}{2}$, impossible.
\item (d) If $\dfrac{x-1}{x+1}=p(x)$ for all $x>0$, then $x-1=(x+1)p(x)$; comparing degrees, $p$ is a constant $c$, and $x-1=cx+c$ forces $c=1$ and $c=-1$ at once, a contradiction.
\end{itemize}
