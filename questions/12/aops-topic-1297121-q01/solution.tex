Suppose your hat were black. Each other prince would see one white and one black hat. Consider prince $X$: if his own hat were black, the third prince $Y$ would see two black hats and know at once that his own is white (only two black hats exist). Since $Y$ stays silent, $X$ would conclude that his own hat is white and announce it. So if your hat were black, one of them would have answered after a short time. As both remain silent, your hat is $\boxed{\text{white}}$.
