Three princes are shown $2$ black hats and $3$ white hats; each is blindfolded and given a hat, and the other two hats are hidden. When the blindfolds are removed, each prince sees the other two hats but not his own. You see two white hats. After some time, neither of the other (very clever) princes has announced his colour. What colour is your hat, and why?
