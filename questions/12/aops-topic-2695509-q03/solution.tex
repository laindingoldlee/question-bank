Let $x=\sqrt a$, $y=\sqrt b$, $z=\sqrt c$. Then $x^2+y^2+z^2=13^2-2\cdot47=75$, and $a+c=2b$ means $x^2+z^2=2y^2$. So $3y^2=75$ and $y=5$. Then $x+z=8$ and $xz=47-5\cdot8=7$, so $abc=(xyz)^2=(5\cdot7)^2=\boxed{1225}$.
