In the city of Random, the city chooses an integer uniformly at random from $1$ to $1000$, and Modnar independently chooses an integer uniformly at random from $1$ to $1000$. Modnar gains access to the city if his integer is within $20$ of the city's integer (a difference of at most $20$). Find the probability that Modnar gains access.
