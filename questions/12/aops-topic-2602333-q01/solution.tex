There are $1000^2=10^6$ equally likely pairs $(x,y)$. Count the pairs with $|x-y|\le20$: for a difference $0$ there are $1000$ pairs, and for each difference $d=1,\dots,20$ there are $1000-d$ pairs with $x-y=d$ and as many with $y-x=d$. The count is
\[1000+2\sum_{d=1}^{20}(1000-d)=1000+2(20000-210)=40580.\]
The probability is
\[\dfrac{40580}{10^6}=\boxed{\dfrac{2029}{50000}}=0.04058.\]
