Write them as $ 17u,17v $ with $\gcd(u,v)=1 $. Then $ uv=\dfrac{714}{17}=42 $. Both numbers are at least 100, so both $ u,v\ge6 $. Among factor pairs of 42, only $(6,7)$ meets this condition, and the entries are coprime. Thus the numbers are 102 and 119, with sum $\boxed{221}$.
