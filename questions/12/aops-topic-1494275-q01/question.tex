A bag holds $31$ letters, each of which is X, Y or Z. When one letter is drawn at random, the probability of drawing an X is $0.7$ times the probability of drawing a Y, and the probability of drawing a Z is $1.4$ times the probability of drawing a Y. How many Y's are in the bag?
