The probabilities are proportional to the numbers of letters, so the numbers of X's, Y's and Z's are in the ratio
\[
0.7:1:1.4=7:10:14 .
\]
These parts add to $31$, the number of letters, so there are exactly $7$ X's, $\boxed{10}$ Y's and $14$ Z's.
