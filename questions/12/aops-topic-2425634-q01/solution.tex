The $41$ plum trees have $40$ gaps of $7$ m between them, and there is a tree at each end, so the road is $40\cdot7=280$ m long. On the other side, $280\div5=56$ gaps of $5$ m need $56+1=\boxed{57}$ pine trees (one more than the number of gaps, because there is a tree at each end).
