Let $x=\overline{ABC}$ and $y=\overline{DEF}$. Then $4(1000x+y)=3(1000y+x)$, i.e. $3997x=2996y$. Since $3997=7\cdot571$ and $2996=7\cdot428$ (with $\gcd(571,428)=1$), this says $571x=428y$, so $x=428k$ and $y=571k$; three-digit values force $k=1$. Hence $\overline{ABCDEF}=\boxed{428571}$. Check: $4\cdot428571=1714284=3\cdot571428$.
