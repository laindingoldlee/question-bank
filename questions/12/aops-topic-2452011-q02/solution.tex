Glue $A$ and $H$ into a block, which can be $AH$ or $HA$ ($2$ ways). The block and $S$ can be arranged in $2$ orders. So there are $2\cdot2=\boxed{4}$ arrangements: $AHS$, $HAS$, $SAH$, $SHA$.
