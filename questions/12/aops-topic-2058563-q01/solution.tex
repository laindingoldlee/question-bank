Let $a_n$ be the number of spacy subsets of $\{1,\dots,n\}$. A spacy subset either does not contain $n$ (then it is a spacy subset of $\{1,\dots,n-1\}$) or contains $n$ (then it contains neither $n-1$ nor $n-2$, and the rest is a spacy subset of $\{1,\dots,n-3\}$). So
\[
a_n=a_{n-1}+a_{n-3}\qquad(n\ge4).
\]
Directly, $a_1=2$, $a_2=3$, $a_3=4$ (the empty set and the singletons). Then
\[
a_4=6,\ a_5=9,\ a_6=13,\ a_7=19,\ a_8=28,\ a_9=41,\ a_{10}=60,\ a_{11}=88,\ a_{12}=129 .
\]
The answer is $\boxed{129}$.
