We have $b=a+1$ and $c=a+3$. Clearing denominators in $\dfrac{1}{a}+\dfrac{2}{a+1}+\dfrac{1}{a+3}=1$:
\[(a+1)(a+3)+2a(a+3)+a(a+1)=a(a+1)(a+3),\]
\[4a^2+11a+3=a^3+4a^2+3a\quad\Longrightarrow\quad a^3-8a-3=0 .\]
An integer root divides $3$; $a=3$ works: $27-24-3=0$. Then $a^3-8a-3=(a-3)(a^2+3a+1)$, and $a^2+3a+1=0$ has no integer roots. So $a=3$, $b=4$, $c=6$ (check: $\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{2}{4}+\dfrac{1}{6}=1$), and
\[a+b+c=\boxed{13}.\]
Fast route in a contest: the denominators $a,a+1,a+3$ with sum of fractions $1$ suggest $\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{6}$, i.e.\ $a=3$.
