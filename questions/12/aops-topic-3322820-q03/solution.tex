The condition is $(x+2)(n+2)-xn=xn$, giving $(x-2)(n-2)=8$. Negative factor pairs cannot give both positive $x,n$: if both shifted values were negative they would both equal $-1$ at worst, producing product at most one. Thus $x-2$ is a positive divisor of $8$, so $x=3,4,6,10$. The sum is $\boxed{23}$.
