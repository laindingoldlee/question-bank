Put the first friend in some class. Of the remaining $179$ places, $11$ are in the first friend's class, and all places are equally likely for the second friend, so the probability that the second friend joins the first is $\dfrac{11}{179}$. Given this, the third friend has $178$ equally likely places left, $10$ of them in that class. Hence
\[P=\dfrac{11}{179}\cdot\dfrac{10}{178}=\dfrac{110}{31862}=\boxed{\dfrac{55}{15931}}.\]
