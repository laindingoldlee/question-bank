Let the entries be $\binom nk,\binom n{k+1},\binom n{k+2}$. Their consecutive ratios give $\dfrac{n-k}{k+1}=\dfrac{4}{3}$ and $\dfrac{n-k-1}{k+2}=\dfrac{5}{4}$. Thus $3n-7k=4$ and $4n-9k=14$. Solving yields $k=26,n=62$. These indices are valid, so such entries exist. Since the equations uniquely determine $n$, the least row is $\boxed{62}$.
