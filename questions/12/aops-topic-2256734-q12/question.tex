Bob needs to take at least $33$ individual socks out of the drawer (without looking) to guarantee one black sock for each foot. What is the remainder when $n$ is divided by $5$?

$\textbf{(A) }0\qquad\textbf{(B) }1\qquad\textbf{(C) }2\qquad\textbf{(D) }3\qquad\textbf{(E) }4$
