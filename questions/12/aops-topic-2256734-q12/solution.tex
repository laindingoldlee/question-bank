In the worst case Bob first takes all $14+10=24$ white and gray socks and all $n$ black socks for one foot, still without a black sock for the other foot; the next sock is then a black sock for the other foot. So the smallest number that guarantees success is $24+n+1=n+25$. Setting $n+25=33$ gives $n=8$, and $8$ leaves remainder $\boxed{\textbf{(D) }3}$ when divided by $5$.
