Since $100$ migs $=35$ jigs, $20$ migs $=\dfrac{35}{5}=7$ jigs. Since $28$ jigs $=25$ ligs, $1$ jig $=\dfrac{25}{28}$ lig, and $7$ jigs $=\dfrac{7\cdot25}{28}=\boxed{\dfrac{25}{4}}$ ligs.
