Let $n<52$ be the number of cards. Then $n\equiv2\pmod 3$ and $n\equiv2\pmod 5$, so $n-2$ is a multiple of $15$: $n\in\{2,17,32,47\}$. Of these, only $47$ leaves remainder $3$ on division by $4$ ($47=4\cdot11+3$; while $2$, $17$, $32$ leave $2$, $1$, $0$). So there are $\boxed{47}$ cards (and $5$ were lost).
