If two entries differ by at least 2, replacing them by $u+1,v-1$ increases their product since $(u+1)(v-1)-uv=v-u-1>0$. Therefore maximizing entries differ by at most 1 and must be 5,6,6,6. Product $\boxed{1080}$.
