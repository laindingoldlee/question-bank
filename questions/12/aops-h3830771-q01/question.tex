A row of $2025$ boxes is arranged in a line, some of which contain candies. It is known that among any three consecutive boxes, at least one of the three boxes does not contain candies. Santa Claus walked along the row and placed candies into some boxes. After this, it turned out that among any four consecutive boxes, at least three of them contain candies. Prove that Santa Claus placed candies in at least $337$ boxes.
