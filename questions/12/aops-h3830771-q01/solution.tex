Initially at most two of every three consecutive boxes contain candies. Partitioning all $2025$ boxes into $675$ triples shows that at least $675$ boxes are empty. List the initially empty boxes in order as $E_1,\ldots,E_b$, with $b\ge675$.
Two consecutive boxes on this list are at distance at most $3$: a larger gap would contain three consecutive initially nonempty boxes. After Santa's additions, two empty boxes cannot be at distance at most $3$, because some four-box interval would then contain at least two empty boxes. Thus no two consecutive members of the list can both stay empty.
At most $\lceil \dfrac{b}{2}\rceil$ listed boxes remain empty, so at least $\lfloor \dfrac{b}{2}\rfloor\ge\lfloor\dfrac{675}{2}\rfloor=\boxed{337}$ initially empty boxes must receive candies.
