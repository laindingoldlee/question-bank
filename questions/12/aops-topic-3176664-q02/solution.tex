Measure time in laps of the slow driver, calling it $t$. The relative lap counts are $23t$ and $8t$, both integers. Since $\gcd(23,8)=1$, the least positive possible $t$ is 1. The faster drivers have made 24 and 9 laps, giving $\boxed{33}$.
