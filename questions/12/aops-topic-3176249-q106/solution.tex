Only the four corner lattice points can be covered, except for measure-zero boundary cases involving other points. The centre must lie within distance $\dfrac{1}{2}$ of a corner. These four quarter-disks have disjoint interiors and total area $4\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}\pi(\dfrac{1}{2})^2=\dfrac{\pi}{4}$. Since the sampling square has area 1, the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{\pi}{4}}$.
