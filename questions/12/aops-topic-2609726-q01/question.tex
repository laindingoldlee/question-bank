A Rubik's cube has only finitely many positions. Starting from a position $P_0$, a fixed sequence of moves $M$ (for example, ``turn the middle row once, then turn the middle column once'') is performed over and over again, giving positions $P_0,P_1,P_2,\dots$, where $P_{k+1}$ is obtained from $P_k$ by performing $M$. Prove that $P_j=P_0$ for some $j\ge1$, i.e. the cube eventually returns to its starting position.
