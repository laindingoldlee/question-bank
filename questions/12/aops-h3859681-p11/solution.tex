If a special word has length $L$, it contains $12-L$ twos and $2L-12$ ones, so $6\le L\le12$. For each length, Burnside\textquotesingle s lemma for cyclic rotations gives $\dfrac{1}{L}\sum_{d\mid\gcd(L,12-L)}\varphi(d)\binom{\dfrac{L}{d}}{\dfrac{12-L}{d}}$ orbits. Indeed a rotation with cycles of length $d$ can fix a word only if its twos occupy whole cycles. Summing this explicit formula over the seven lengths gives $\boxed{31}$.
