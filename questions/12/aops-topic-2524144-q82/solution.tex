Being a square and a fifth power, every exponent in the prime factorisation of $y$ is divisible by $2$ and by $5$, hence by $10$. So $y$ is a tenth power of a number with three distinct odd prime factors, and the smallest such number is $3\cdot5\cdot7$. The least $y$ is $\boxed{105^{10}=3^{10}\cdot5^{10}\cdot7^{10}}$.
