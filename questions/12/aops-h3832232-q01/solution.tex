Let the third side be $c$. The law of cosines gives $c^2=5^2+8^2-2\cdot5\cdot8\cos60^\circ=89-40=49$, so $\boxed{c=7}$. If $\beta$ is opposite the side of length $8$, applying the same law gives $64=25+49-70\cos\beta$, hence $\boxed{\cos\beta=\dfrac{1}{7}}$.
