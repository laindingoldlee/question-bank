The number of selections is $\binom2x\binom3y$. The first factor is $1,2,1$, and the second is $1,3,3,1$. A product of $6$ requires $x=1$ and $y=1$ or $2$. Thus the possible totals are $2,3$, whose sum is $\boxed5$.
