Two cyclists start simultaneously from Berkeley and Stanford and ride toward the other campus at constant speeds. Each immediately turns around on reaching the other campus. They first meet $17.5$ miles from Berkeley and meet again, after both have turned, $10$ miles from Stanford. Find the distance between campuses.
