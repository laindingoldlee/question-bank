Let the distance be $D$. At the first meeting their combined travel is $D$; at the second it is $3D$. Their constant speeds therefore make each cyclist travel three times as far by the second meeting. The Berkeley cyclist has then covered $3(17.5)=52.5$ miles, consisting of the full distance $D$ plus $10$ miles back from Stanford. Thus $D+10=52.5$ and $\boxed{D=42.5\text{ miles}}$.
