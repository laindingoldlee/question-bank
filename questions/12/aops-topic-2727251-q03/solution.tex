The left side is $(1+a)(1+b)(1+c)-1$, so we need $(1+a)(1+b)(1+c)=100a+10b+c+1$.

If $b=c=9$ the left side is $100(1+a)$ and the right side is $100a+100$: always true. Otherwise $(1+b)(1+c)\le90$, so the left side is at most $90(1+a)$, while the right side is at least $100a+1$; then $90+90a\ge100a+1$ forces $a\le8$, and a direct check of the remaining cases (each $a$ from $1$ to $8$, comparing $(1+a)(1+b)(1+c)$ with $100a+10b+c+1$) finds no other solutions.

So the numbers are exactly $\boxed{199,299,399,\dots,999}$.
