Since $9^{16}=3^{32}$ and $27^6=3^{18}$,
\[9^{16}-27^6=3^{18}\left(3^{14}-1\right)=3^{18}\left(3^7-1\right)\left(3^7+1\right)=3^{18}\cdot2186\cdot2188 .\]
Now $2186=2\cdot1093$ and $2188=4\cdot547$, so
\[9^{16}-27^6=2^3\cdot3^{18}\cdot547\cdot1093 .\]
Both $547$ and $1093$ are prime: $547$ is not divisible by any prime up to $23$ (and $23^2=529<547<29^2$), and $1093$ is not divisible by any prime up to $31$ (and $33^2=1089<1093<34^2$); for example $1093=7\cdot156+1=11\cdot99+4=13\cdot84+1=31\cdot35+8$. The largest prime factor is $\boxed{1093}$.
