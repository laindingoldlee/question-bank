The three-digit squares are $n^2$ for $10\le n\le31$. Checking these $22$ cases gives the pairs\[100\leftrightarrow1,\quad121\leftrightarrow121,\quad144\leftrightarrow441,\quad169\leftrightarrow961,\quad400\leftrightarrow4,\quad484\leftrightarrow484,\quad676\leftrightarrow676,\quad900\leftrightarrow9.\]The other candidate squares $196,225,256,289,324,361,529,576,625,729,784,841$ reverse to $691,522,652,982,423,163,925,675,526,927,487,148$, none of which is a square (each lies strictly between consecutive squares). Thus the answer is $\boxed{100,121,144,169,400,441,484,676,900,961}$.
