A deck has $5$ kings, $5$ queens and $5$ jokers. Two bandits and Rex take turns drawing one card at random: first bandit, second bandit, Rex, then again first bandit, second bandit, Rex, and so on. After each draw the card is put back and the deck is shuffled. The first player to draw a queen wins. What is the probability that Rex wins?
