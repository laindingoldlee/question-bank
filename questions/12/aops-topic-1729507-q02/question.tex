A trapezoid has the property that the sum of each pair of opposite sides is $23$. The lengths of the legs differ by $7$, and the lengths of the bases differ by $17$. The area of the trapezoid can be written as a reduced fraction $\dfrac {x}{y}$ with $x>y$. Find $x-y$.
