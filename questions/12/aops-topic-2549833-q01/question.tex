Six dogs sit at the six corners of a hexagonal park with sides $112$ m, $112$ m, $115$ m, $124$ m, $160$ m and $77$ m. At the same moment, each dog starts running along the edge of the park, choosing clockwise or counterclockwise with probability $\dfrac12$ each, independently of the others. All dogs run at the same constant speed and never turn around. What is the probability that no two dogs ever collide?
