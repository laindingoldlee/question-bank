Up to rotation we may fix the baggage seat. The other five seats then form a row: seats $1,2,3,4,5$ in order around the table, where seats $1$ and $5$ are next to the baggage but not to each other. So we count arrangements of the five people in a row in which Marie and Prim are not adjacent and Paula and Minnie are not adjacent.

There are $5!=120$ arrangements in all. Marie and Prim are adjacent in $2\cdot4!=48$ of them (glue them into a block, in $2$ orders), and likewise Paula and Minnie in $48$. Both pairs are adjacent in $2\cdot2\cdot3!=24$. By inclusion-exclusion the number of good seatings is
\[
120-48-48+24=\boxed{48}.
\]
