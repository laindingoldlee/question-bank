Let $(x,y)$ be a point not on the non-positive $x$-axis, with polar coordinates $(r,\theta)$, where $r>0$ and $-\pi<\theta<\pi$. Show that $\theta=2\arctan\dfrac{y}{x+\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}$.
