With $a=\sqrt[3]2$ and $b=\sqrt[3]7$: $\sqrt[6]{16}=\sqrt[3]4=a^2$, $\sqrt[6]{196}=\sqrt[3]{14}=ab$ and $\sqrt[3]{49}=b^2$. So
\[\dfrac{5}{a^2+ab+b^2}=\dfrac{5(b-a)}{b^3-a^3}=\dfrac{5(b-a)}{5}=\boxed{\sqrt[3]7-\sqrt[3]2}.\]
