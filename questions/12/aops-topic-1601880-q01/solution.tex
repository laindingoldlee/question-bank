Write $n+32=a^2$ and $n+124=b^2$ with integers $a,b\ge0$. Then
\[
b^2-a^2=(b-a)(b+a)=92 .
\]
The factors $b-a$ and $b+a$ have the same parity (their sum $2b$ is even) and their product $92$ is even, so both are even. Since $92=2\cdot46$ is the only way to write $92$ as a product of two even numbers with $0<b-a\le b+a$, we get $b-a=2$, $b+a=46$, so $b=24$, $a=22$. Therefore
\[
n=22^2-32=484-32=\boxed{452},
\]
and indeed $452+124=576=24^2$.
