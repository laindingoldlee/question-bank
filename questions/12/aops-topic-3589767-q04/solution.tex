Write $N=10a+b$, where $1\le a\le9$ and $0\le b\le9$. Then $10a+b=3a+3b+8$, so $7a=2b+8$. Thus $a$ is even and $8\le7a\le26$. Only $a=2$ is possible, yielding $b=3$. The units digit is $\boxed3$, realized by $N=23$.
