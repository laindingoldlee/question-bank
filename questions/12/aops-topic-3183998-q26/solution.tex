After an even number of toggles the number of on switches is odd, so it is $1$ or $3$. Count sequences by the number on: from a state with $k$ on, $k$ of the three choices decrease the count and $3-k$ increase it. Starting with one on, the counts after successive moves are $(0:1,2:2)$, then $(1:7,3:2)$, then $(0:7,2:20)$, and finally $(1:61,3:20)$. Of $3^4=81$ equally likely sequences, $61$ finish with one on. Thus $m+n=\boxed{142}$.
