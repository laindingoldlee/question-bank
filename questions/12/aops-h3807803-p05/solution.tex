The diagonal $BD=6\sqrt2$. The points $E,F$ are the two equilateral vertices on opposite sides of $BD$. Thus the kite $BEDF$ consists of two equilateral triangles and has area
\[
2\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}(6\sqrt2)^2=36\sqrt3.
\]
Therefore the ratio is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3}}$.
