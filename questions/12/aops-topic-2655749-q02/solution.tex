Let $p(x,y)$ be the probability of ending on a vertical side when starting from the interior lattice point $(x,y)$.
\begin{itemize}
\item Reflection in the line $y=x$ swaps vertical and horizontal sides, so $p(x,y)=1-p(y,x)$. Hence $p(2,2)=p(1,1)=\dfrac12$.
\item Reflection in the line $y=2$ keeps vertical sides vertical, so $p(1,3)=p(1,1)=\dfrac12$.
\end{itemize}
From $(1,2)$ the first jump goes left (to the vertical side $x=0$), right to $(2,2)$, up to $(1,3)$ or down to $(1,1)$, each with probability $\dfrac14$:
\[p(1,2)=\dfrac14\cdot1+\dfrac14\cdot\dfrac12+\dfrac14\cdot\dfrac12+\dfrac14\cdot\dfrac12=\boxed{\dfrac58}.\]
