Answer yes or no and justify each assertion: (a) Every triangle's incircle radius is smaller than its circumcircle radius. (b) The smallest disk containing four distinct concyclic points is always their circumdisk. (c) Every triangle's orthocenter is interior. (d) There are positive integers $a,b,c\le100$ with $a^3+b^3=c^3$. (e) Every angle can be trisected with an unmarked straightedge and compass. (f) The three side midpoints and three altitude feet of a triangle lie on one circle.
