The first $n$ terms sum to $S_n=\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{3}(1-3^{-n})}{1-\dfrac{1}{3}}=\dfrac{1}{2}(1-3^{-n})$. As $n$ tends to infinity, $3^{-n}$ tends to zero. Thus the infinite sum is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$.
