The radius to the tangency point is perpendicular to the chord and bisects it. A half-chord is therefore $\sqrt{13^2-5^2}=12$. Thus $\boxed{AB=24}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair O=(0,0),A=(-12,5),B=(12,5),T=(0,5);draw(circle(O,13));draw(circle(O,5),blue);draw(A--B);draw(O--T^^O--B,dashed);label("$A$",A,W);label("$B$",B,E);label("$O$",O,S);label("$T$",T,N);label("$5$",(0,2.5),W);label("$13$",B/2,S);
\end{asy}
