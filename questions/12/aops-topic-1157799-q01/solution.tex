Let the first term be $a$ and the common difference $d$. The mean of the first $k$ terms of an arithmetic sequence is the mean of the first and $k$-th terms, $a+\dfrac{(k-1)d}{2}$. Hence
\[
a+2d=27,\qquad a+\dfrac{7}{2}d=39 .
\]
Subtracting, $\dfrac{3}{2}d=12$, so $d=8$ (and $a=11$: the terms are $11,19,27,35,43,51,59,67$, with means $27$ and $39$). The difference between the first and second terms is $|d|=\boxed{8}$.
