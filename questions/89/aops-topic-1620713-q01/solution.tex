$M$ is the midpoint of $BC$, so $BM=MC=4$. Let $\theta=\angle AMB$; then $\angle AMC=180^\circ-\theta$ and $\cos\angle AMC=-\cos\theta$.

By the law of cosines in triangles $ABM$ and $ACM$,
\[
AB^2=AM^2+BM^2-2\cdot AM\cdot BM\cos\theta,\qquad AC^2=AM^2+MC^2+2\cdot AM\cdot MC\cos\theta .
\]
Adding, the cosine terms cancel (since $BM=MC$):
\[
AB^2+AC^2=2AM^2+2BM^2=2\cdot16+2\cdot16=64 .
\]
Hence $AC^2=64-25=39$ and $AC=\boxed{\sqrt{39}}$.
