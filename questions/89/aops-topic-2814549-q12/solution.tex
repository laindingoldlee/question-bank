Count pairs (committee, $k$-person subcommittee) from $n$ people. Choosing a committee of size $r$ and then the subcommittee gives the left side. Alternatively choose the subcommittee ($\binom nk$), then decide for each of the other $n-k$ people whether they are on the committee ($2^{n-k}$). $\blacksquare$
