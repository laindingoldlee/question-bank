The product is $2^6\cdot(1\cdot2\cdot3\cdot4\cdot5\cdot6)=2^6\cdot720=2^6\cdot2^4\cdot3^2\cdot5=2^{10}\cdot3^2\cdot5$.

A factor $2^a3^b5^c$ is a perfect square exactly when $a$, $b$, $c$ are even. So $a\in\{0,2,4,6,8,10\}$ ($6$ choices), $b\in\{0,2\}$ ($2$ choices) and $c=0$ ($1$ choice), giving $6\cdot2\cdot1=\boxed{12}$ perfect-square factors (counting $1$).
