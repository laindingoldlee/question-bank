For an integer $n$, let $*(n)=\left\{n-2,\ n+2,\ 2n,\ \dfrac{n}{2}\right\}$. For example, $*(6)=\{4,8,12,3\}$. For how many distinct integers $n$ does $*(n)$ have exactly three distinct elements?
