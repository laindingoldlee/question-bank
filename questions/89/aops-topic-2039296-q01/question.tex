In triangle $ABC$, points $P$ and $Q$ lie on sides $AB$ and $AC$ with $\dfrac{AP}{AB}=\dfrac{AQ}{AC}=\dfrac15$. Segments $PC$ and $QB$ intersect at $R$. What is the ratio of the area of triangle $PQR$ to the area of triangle $ABC$? Express your answer as a common fraction.
