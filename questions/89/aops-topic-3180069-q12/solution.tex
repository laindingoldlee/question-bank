By the midpoint theorem, $E$ is the midpoint of $AC$, so $D$ is the centroid. Use $B=(0,0),A=(0,3),C=(4,0)$, valid by the Pythagorean theorem. The centroid is $D=(\dfrac{4}{3},1)$, so $AD=\sqrt{\dfrac{16}{9}+4}=\boxed{\dfrac{2\sqrt{13}}{3}}$.
