Put $u=5x+13y$. The first two equations say $u-z=100$, $(u-z)(u+z)=16000$, so $u=130,z=30$. The third equation reduces to $16u-2z=2020$ and adds nothing. Thus every real pair $5x+13y=130$ works. For example $(x,y,z)=(0,10,30)$ gives product zero, whereas $(13,5,30)$ gives 1950. The product is not determined.
