Two independent random walkers start at $(1,3)$ and $(2,2)$. Each step independently moves one unit north, south, east, or west with equal probability. A walker stops on reaching the boundary of the square $0\le x,y\le4$. The probability exactly one walker has stopped after at most three steps is $\dfrac{m}{n}$ in lowest terms. Find $10m+n$.
