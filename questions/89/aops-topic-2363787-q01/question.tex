An angle $\alpha$ (with $0^\circ<\alpha<180^\circ$) and two lengths $s$ and $h$ are given. Explain how to construct, with straightedge and compass, a triangle $ABC$ with $\angle BAC=\alpha$, semiperimeter $s$, and altitude from $A$ to $BC$ equal to $h$. Prove that the construction works, and show that such a triangle exists exactly when
\[h\le s\cdot\dfrac{1-\sin\dfrac{\alpha}{2}}{\cos\dfrac{\alpha}{2}}.\]
