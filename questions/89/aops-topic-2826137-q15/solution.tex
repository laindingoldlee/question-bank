Since $1011=3\cdot337$, its totient is $(3-1)(337-1)=672$. There are two complete residue blocks modulo $1011$ in $1,\ldots,2021$ for coprime residues, hence $1344$ selected integers. The involution $n\mapsto2022-n$ preserves coprimality and pairs the selected integers into $672$ pairs of sum $2022$. Therefore $\boxed{\dfrac{N}{2022}=672}$.
