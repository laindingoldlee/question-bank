A convex pentagon $ABCDE$ satisfies $AB=BC=CD=DE$, $\angle ABC=\angle CDE$, and $\angle EAB=\angle AED=\dfrac{1}{2}\angle BCD$. The lines $AB$ and $CD$ meet at $X$. If triangle $BCX$ has perimeter $18$ and area $11$, find the pentagon area. Choices: (A) $136$; (B) $137$; (C) $138$; (D) $139$; (E) $140$.
