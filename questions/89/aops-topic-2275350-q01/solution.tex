Factor: $49896=8\cdot6237=8\cdot81\cdot77=2^3\cdot3^4\cdot7\cdot11$.

A square divisor has even exponents, each at most the exponent in $49896$. The largest choice is $2^2$ (since $3$ is odd, use $2$), $3^4$, $7^0$ and $11^0$:
\[2^2\cdot3^4=4\cdot81=\boxed{324}.\]
(Indeed $49896=324\cdot154$, and $154=2\cdot7\cdot11$ has no square factor greater than $1$.)
