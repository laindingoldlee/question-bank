Eliminating $x$ gives $ab=3(a+b)-2$, i.e.
\[ab-3a-3b+9=7,\qquad (a-3)(b-3)=7.\]
Since $7$ is prime, $(a-3,b-3)$ is one of $(1,7)$, $(7,1)$, $(-1,-7)$, $(-7,-1)$, giving the four points
\[(4,10),\quad(10,4),\quad(2,-4),\quad(-4,2).\]
(Conversely each of these satisfies the conditions with $x=a+b$.) The six distances are
\[|(4,10)(10,4)|=|(2,-4)(-4,2)|=6\sqrt2,\qquad|(4,10)(-4,2)|=|(10,4)(2,-4)|=8\sqrt2,\]
\[|(4,10)(2,-4)|=|(10,4)(-4,2)|=\sqrt{2^2+14^2}=\sqrt{200}=10\sqrt2.\]
The largest distance is $\boxed{10\sqrt2}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
real f(real x){return 3+7/(x-3);}
draw((-6,0)--(12,0),Arrow(6)); draw((0,-6)--(0,12),Arrow(6));
label("$a$",(12,0),E); label("$b$",(0,12),N);
draw(graph(f,3.8,12),gray); draw(graph(f,-6,2.2),gray);
pair p1=(4,10), p2=(10,4), p3=(2,-4), p4=(-4,2);
draw(p1--p3,red+linewidth(0.9)); draw(p2--p4,red+linewidth(0.9));
draw(p1--p2--p3--p4--cycle,blue+dashed);
dot(p1);dot(p2);dot(p3);dot(p4);
label("$(4,10)$",p1,NE); label("$(10,4)$",p2,NE); label("$(2,-4)$",p3,SE); label("$(-4,2)$",p4,NW);
label("$10\sqrt2$",p1+0.3*(p3-p1),E,red);
\end{asy}

(The four points lie on the hyperbola $(a-3)(b-3)=7$, drawn in grey.)
