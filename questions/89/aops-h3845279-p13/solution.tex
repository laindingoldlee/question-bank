Expansion gives
\[
\dfrac{T(m+1)+T(m+3)}{T(m-1)}
=2+\dfrac{12m+14}{m(m-1)}.
\]
Integrality implies $m\mid14$ and $m-1\mid26$. The positive candidates from the first are $m=2,7,14$; the second retains only $2,14$. Since consecutive integers are coprime, the two divisibilities together suffice. Equivalently, direct evaluation gives $21$ for $m=2$ and $3$ for $m=14$. Thus the sum is $\boxed{16}$.
