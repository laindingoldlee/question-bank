Starting at home, Brenda walks $1$ block north, $2$ blocks east, $3$ blocks south, $4$ blocks west, and so on, each time turning $90^\circ$ to her right and walking one block further than the time before. After walking a total of $666$ blocks, how many blocks is Brenda from home (in a straight line)? Express your answer in simplest radical form.
