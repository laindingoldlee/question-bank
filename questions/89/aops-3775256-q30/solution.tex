Factor $(x-y)(x-2y)=8$. Set $u=x-y,v=x-2y$; then $y=u-v,x=2u-v$, so every ordered integer factor pair $uv=8$ gives exactly one solution. The pairs are $(1,8),(2,4),(4,2),(8,1)$ and their simultaneous negatives. The solutions are $(-6,-7),(0,-2),(6,2),(15,7)$ and their simultaneous negatives, for $\boxed8$ solutions.
