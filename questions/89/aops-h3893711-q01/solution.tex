Group $n$ by its integer cube root. For $1\le n\le7$, divisibility by 1 suffices: 7 choices. For $8\le n\le26$, divisibility by 2 is necessary and sufficient: 10 choices. For $27\le n\le63$, $n$ must be divisible by $\operatorname{lcm}(1,2,3)=6$: the six values $30,36,42,48,54,60$. For $64\le n\le100$, the required least common multiple is 12: the three values $72,84,96$. The total is $7+10+6+3=\boxed{26}$.
