A school wishes to use square tiles of artificial turf to cover an outdoor play area that measures $40$ feet by $72$ feet. Only whole tiles that are congruent squares of the largest possible size will be used.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] How many such square tiles are needed?
\item[(b)] A student claims the answer should be $6$, because the area can be covered by one $40\times40$ tile, one $32\times32$ tile and four $8\times8$ tiles. Is the claim correct?
\end{enumerate}
