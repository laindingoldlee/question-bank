Let $ABCD$ be a rhombus with side length $1$ such that $\angle ABC$ is obtuse. Let $\omega$ be the circle with diameter $AB$, and let $\omega$ meet $AD$ again at $E$. The circumcircle of triangle $EDC$ meets $\omega$ again at $F$. Let $X$ be the intersection of $AC$ and $BD$. Given that $FX\perp CD$, find the area of triangle $DEF$.
