Two swimmers start at the same moment at opposite ends of a $90$-foot pool and swim lengths of the pool back and forth, one at $3$ feet per second and the other at $2$ feet per second. They swim for $12$ minutes, losing no time at the turns. How many times do they pass each other (including any moment when both are at the same end at the same time)?
