With first term $a$ and difference $d$, $S_7=7(a+3d)=-42$ and $S_{21}=21(a+10d)=-714$, so $a+3d=-6$ and $a+10d=-34$. Hence $7d=-28$, $d=-4$, $a=6$, and
\[
S_{50}=\dfrac{50}{2}\bigl(2\cdot6+49\cdot(-4)\bigr)=25(12-196)=\boxed{-4600}.
\]
