Let $f$ be a function such that $f(0)=1$ and for positive integers $x$,

$$f(x)=\begin{cases}
0&\textrm{if }x\equiv 0\quad\pmod{3}\\
\dfrac{1}{3}f\left(\dfrac{x-1}{3}\right)&\textrm{if }x\equiv 1\quad\pmod{3}\\
\dfrac{1}{9}f\left(\dfrac{x-2}{3}\right)&\textrm{if }x\equiv 2\quad\pmod{3}
\end{cases}.$$Find $\sum^{\infty}_{n=1}f(n)$.
