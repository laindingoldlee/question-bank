Let the price be $p$ cents. James has $p-51$ cents and Steven has $p-45$ cents, so
\[(p-51)+(p-45)=p+2\iff p=98 .\]
Steven has $98-45=\boxed{53}$ cents. (James has $47$ cents, and $47+53=100=98+2$.)
