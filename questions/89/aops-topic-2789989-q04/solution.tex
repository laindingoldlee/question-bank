Number the positions $0,\dots,2018$ and join each position to the one $3$ places further on. Since $2019=3\cdot673$, this splits the trees into $3$ cycles of $673$ trees each. If no two oaks were exactly $3$ apart, no two oaks would be adjacent in any cycle, so each cycle (of odd length $673$) would hold at most $336$ oaks, at most $1008$ in total. But there are $1010$ oaks. $\blacksquare$
