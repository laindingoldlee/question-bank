A cave floods at a constant rate and would be completely flooded $70$ minutes from now. A scout fleet is $500$ units away and travels $200$ units every $10$ minutes; once it arrives, it immediately slows the flooding to $\dfrac{1}{100}$ of the cave per minute, but it cannot stop the flooding or retrieve the data chip inside. A cruiser fleet, which can stop the flooding and retrieve the chip, is $1000$ units away and travels $100$ units every $15$ minutes. Will the cruisers arrive before the cave is completely flooded?
