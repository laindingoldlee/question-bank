Measure the flooded part as a fraction of the cave. At first it floods at $\dfrac{1}{70}$ per minute.

The scouts travel at $20$ units per minute, so they arrive after $25$ minutes, when $\dfrac{25}{70}=\dfrac{5}{14}$ of the cave is flooded. The remaining $\dfrac{9}{14}$ then floods at $\dfrac{1}{100}$ per minute, taking $\dfrac{900}{14}\approx64.3$ minutes. So the cave is completely flooded about $25+64.3=89.3$ minutes from now.

The cruisers travel at $\dfrac{100}{15}=\dfrac{20}{3}$ units per minute, so they need $1000\cdot\dfrac{3}{20}=150$ minutes. Since $150>89.3$, $\boxed{\text{No}}$: the cave floods about $60.7$ minutes before the cruisers arrive.
