Complete the squares:
\[
9(x^2-4x+4)+4(y^2+6y+9)=-36+36+36\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad 9(x-2)^2+4(y+3)^2=36,
\]
that is,
\[
\dfrac{(x-2)^2}{4}+\dfrac{(y+3)^2}{9}=1 .
\]
This is an ellipse centred at $(2,-3)$ with semi-axes $a=2$ (horizontal) and $b=3$ (vertical).

dot(C);label("$(2,-3)$",C,S);

\end{asy}

An ellipse with semi-axes $a$ and $b$ is a circle of radius $a$ stretched by the factor $\dfrac {b}{a}$ in one direction, so its area is $\pi a^2\cdot\dfrac {b}{a}=\pi ab$. Here the area is $\pi\cdot2\cdot3=\boxed{6\pi}$.
