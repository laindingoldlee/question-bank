Use unit vectors $e,f$ along $AB,AC$, and write $e\cdot f=c$. A circle through the origin $A$ has equation $X\cdot X-u\cdot X=0$ for some vector $u$. Since $P=7e$ and $Q=5f$ lie on it, $u\cdot e=7$ and $u\cdot f=5$. Also $M=\dfrac{13e+12f}{2}$ lies on it, giving $\dfrac{313+312c}{4}=\dfrac{151}{2}$. Thus $312c=-11$, and $BC^2=313-312c=324$. Therefore $\boxed{BC=18}$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(220);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(13,0),C=(12*(-11/312),12*sqrt(1-(11/312)^2)); draw(A--B--C--cycle); label("$A$",A,(-1,-1)); label("$B$",B,(1,-1)); label("$C$",C,(0,1)); pair P=(7,0),Q=5*C/12,M=(B+C)/2;draw(P--M--Q--cycle,dashed);label("$P$",P,(0,-1));label("$Q$",Q,(-1,0));label("$M$",M,(1,1));
\end{asy}
