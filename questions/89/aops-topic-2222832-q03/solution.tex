In one hour Azula does $1$ problem, Zuko $\dfrac{1}{3}$ and Katara $\dfrac{1}{2}$ of a problem. Together they do
\[1+\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{6+2+3}{6}=\dfrac{11}{6}\]
problems per hour, so one problem takes $\boxed{\dfrac{6}{11}\text{ hour}}$, about $32.7$ minutes.
