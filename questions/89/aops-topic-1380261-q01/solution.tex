The vertices in this order go once around a convex quadrilateral. Cut it along the diagonal from $(1,1)$ to $(2006,2007)$ (figure not to scale):

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair P1=(1,3),P2=(1,1),P3=(2,1),P4=(7,8);
fill(P2--P3--P4--cycle,paleblue);fill(P2--P4--P1--cycle,palegreen);
draw(P1--P2--P3--P4--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(P2--P4,dashed);
dot(P1);dot(P2);dot(P3);dot(P4);
label("$(1,3)$",P1,W);label("$(1,1)$",P2,SW);label("$(2,1)$",P3,SE);label("$(2006,2007)$",P4,NE);
label("not to scale",(1.5,7.5),fontsize(8pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

\begin{itemize}
\item The triangle $(1,1),(2,1),(2006,2007)$ has a horizontal base of length $1$ and height $2007-1=2006$: area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot1\cdot2006=1003$.
\item The triangle $(1,1),(2006,2007),(1,3)$ has a vertical base of length $3-1=2$ on the line $x=1$ and height $2006-1=2005$: area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot2\cdot2005=2005$.
\end{itemize}
The area is $1003+2005=\boxed{3008}$. (The shoelace formula gives the same: $\dfrac{1}{2}\left|-2-1+2008+4011\right|=3008$.)
