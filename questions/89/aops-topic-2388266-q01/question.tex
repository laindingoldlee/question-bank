In triangle $ABC$, the internal bisector of angle $A$ and the median from $A$ meet $BC$ at two distinct points $L$ and $D$, respectively. The perpendicular from $B$ to line $AL$ meets $AL$ at $P$ and line $AD$ at $M$. The perpendicular from $C$ to line $AL$ meets $AL$ at $Q$ and line $AD$ at $N$. Prove that $LM\parallel AB$ and $LN\parallel AC$.
