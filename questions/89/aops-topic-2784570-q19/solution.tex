Let $k$ spades go to the friend forbidding clubs, so $3-k$ go to the friend forbidding hearts. The requirement of three cards per person then forces $k$ clubs to the friend forbidding hearts, $3-k$ clubs to the friend forbidding spades, and $k$ hearts to the friend forbidding spades. For each $k=0,1,2,3$, choosing the indicated $k$ cards independently from each suit gives $\binom3k^3$ deals. The total is $1+27+27+1=\boxed{56}$.
