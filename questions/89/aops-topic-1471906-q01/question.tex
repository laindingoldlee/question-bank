\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] In how many ways can $5$ people sit around a round table, if two seatings are the same when one is a rotation of the other?
\item[(b)] In how many ways can $5$ different beads be put on a bracelet, if two bracelets are the same when one can be rotated or flipped over to give the other?
\item[(c)] Explain why reflections are identified in (b) but not in (a).
\end{enumerate}
