Let the common ratio be $r>0$ (positive because all terms are positive). Then $a_8=a_4r^4$, so $r^4=\dfrac{800}{200}=4$ and $r^2=2$. Hence
\[
a_6=a_4r^2=200\cdot2=\boxed{400}.
\]
(Equivalently, $a_6$ is the geometric mean of $a_4$ and $a_8$: $\sqrt{200\cdot800}=400$.)
