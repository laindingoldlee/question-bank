An arrangement is described by listing the rings on the first finger from the base outwards, then a divider, then the rings on the second finger, a divider, the rings on the third finger, a divider, and the rings on the fourth finger. So arrangements correspond exactly to rows made of the $5$ different rings and $3$ identical dividers (empty fingers give adjacent dividers or a divider at an end). The number of such rows is
\[\dfrac{8!}{3!}=8\cdot7\cdot6\cdot5\cdot4=\boxed{6720}.\]

\emph{Another way.} Put the rings on one at a time. When $k$ rings are already on, the next ring can go on any finger in any position along it; a finger with $j$ rings offers $j+1$ positions, so there are $k+4$ positions in all. This gives $4\cdot5\cdot6\cdot7\cdot8=6720$ ways.
