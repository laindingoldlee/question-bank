Richard draws all the diagonals of a convex octagon and then erases all the sides of the octagon. A triangle is \emph{formed} if each of its three sides lies along one of the drawn diagonals (its vertices may be vertices of the octagon or intersection points of diagonals). What is the maximum possible number of triangles formed?
