Parity permits lengths 7 or 9 only. Seven-step paths use 3 left and 4 down steps: $\binom73=35$. A nine-step path has either 4 left,1 right,4 down or 3 left,5 down,1 up. Their multinomial counts are $\dfrac{9!}{4!1!4!}=630$ and $\dfrac{9!}{3!5!1!}=504$. Total $35+630+504=\boxed{1169}$.
