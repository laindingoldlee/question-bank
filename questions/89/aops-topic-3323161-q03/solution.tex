Take $a_0=2^9$ and $b=\dfrac{3}{2}$. Then $a_l=2^{9-l}3^l$. For $0\le l\le9$ this is an integer. For $l>9$ it equals $\dfrac{3^l}{2^{l-9}}$, whose denominator cannot cancel because the numerator is odd. This gives the required construction.
