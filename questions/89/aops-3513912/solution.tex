An odd-length consecutive sum is its length times its middle term. The first two conditions require a multiple of 35; every positive multiple of 35 has sufficiently large middle terms to make both representations positive. Such a number is a sum of three consecutive positive integers exactly when it is divisible by 3. Hence the count is $\lfloor\dfrac{10000}{35}\rfloor-\lfloor\dfrac{10000}{105}\rfloor=285-95=\boxed{190}$.
