Write the equal sides as $a,a$ and the remaining side as $23-2a$. Positivity requires $a\le11$, and the nontrivial triangle inequality is $2a>23-2a$, giving $a\ge6$. Each integer $a=6,7,8,9,10,11$ yields one valid noncongruent triangle. Hence there are $\boxed6$ triangles.

\end{asy}
