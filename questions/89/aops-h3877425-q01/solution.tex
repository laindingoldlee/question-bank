Since $18=2\cdot3^2$, every candidate is uniquely $p^2q$ for distinct primes $p,q$. Since $q\ge2$, $p^2\le100$, so $p$ is $2,3,5$, or $7$. The counts are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
p&q\le\lfloor\dfrac{200}{p^2}\rfloor&\text{number of primes }q\ne p\\\hline
2&50&14\\3&22&7\\5&8&3\\7&4&2
\end{array}
\]
Each number has a unique prime of exponent $2$, so no double counting occurs. The total is $\boxed{26}$.
