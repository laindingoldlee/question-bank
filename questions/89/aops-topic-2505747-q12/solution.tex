The perfect powers up to $50$ are $1,4,9,16,25,36,49$ (squares) and $8,27$ (cubes, not squares) and $32=2^5$; fourth powers ($1,16$) and sixth powers ($1$) are already listed. That is $10$ perfect powers, so $50-10=\boxed{40}$ integers are not perfect powers.
