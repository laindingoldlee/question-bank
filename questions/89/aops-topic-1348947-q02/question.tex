In triangle $ABC$, $AB=14$, $BC=15$ and $AC=13$. The altitudes $AD$, $BE$, $CF$ (so $DEF$ is the orthic triangle) meet at the orthocentre $H$. If $AH+BH+CH=\dfrac {a}{b}$ with $a$ and $b$ relatively prime positive integers, find $a+b$.
