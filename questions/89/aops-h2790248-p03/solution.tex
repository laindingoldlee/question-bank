Scale to $B=(0,0),A=(0,1),C=(1,0)$. The specified trisector from $A$ has equation $y=1-\sqrt3x$, while the trisectors from $B$ are $y=\sqrt3x$ and $y=\dfrac{x}{\sqrt3}$. Thus $D=(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{6},\dfrac{1}{2})$ and $E=(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4},\dfrac{1}{4})$. The distance formula gives $AD=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}$ and $DE=\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt3}$, proving the claim.

\end{asy}
