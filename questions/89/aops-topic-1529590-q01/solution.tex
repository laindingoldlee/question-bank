Since $5$ is prime, the product is a multiple of $5$ exactly when at least one die shows $5$. The probability that no die shows $5$ is $\left(\dfrac{5}{6}\right)^3=\dfrac{125}{216}$, so the answer is
\[
1-\dfrac{125}{216}=\boxed{\dfrac{91}{216}}.
\]
