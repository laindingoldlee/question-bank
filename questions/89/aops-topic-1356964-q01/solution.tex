(a) An element $x$ lies in $(A\cup B)\setminus(A\cap B)$ exactly when it is in at least one of the sets but not in both, i.e.\ in exactly one of them. Being in exactly one of them means $x\in A$ and $x\notin B$, or $x\in B$ and $x\notin A$, which is $x\in(A\setminus B)\cup(B\setminus A)$.

(b) $A\setminus B$ and $B\setminus A$ are disjoint, $|A\setminus B|=|A|-|A\cap B|$ and $|B\setminus A|=|B|-|A\cap B|$; add.

(c) $A\cap B=\{4,5,6\}$, so $A\triangle B=\boxed{\{1,2,3,7,8,9\}}$, with $6+6-2\cdot3=6$ elements as (b) predicts. In a Venn diagram of two circles it is the region inside exactly one circle.
