For sets $A$ and $B$, the set $(A\cup B)\setminus(A\cap B)$ is called the \emph{symmetric difference} of $A$ and $B$, written $A\triangle B$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $A\triangle B=(A\setminus B)\cup(B\setminus A)$.
\item[(b)] Prove that $|A\triangle B|=|A|+|B|-2|A\cap B|$ for finite sets.
\item[(c)] Find $A\triangle B$ for $A=\{1,2,3,4,5,6\}$ and $B=\{4,5,6,7,8,9\}$.
\end{enumerate}
