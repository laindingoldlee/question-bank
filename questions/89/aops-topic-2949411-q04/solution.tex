The hexagon area is $6\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}\cdot12^2=216\sqrt3$. Each requested piece has legs $3,3\sqrt3$ and area $\dfrac{9\sqrt3}{2}$. Thus at most 48 pieces fit. To attain 48, divide the hexagon into six equilateral triangles of side 12, divide each into four equilateral triangles of side 6, and bisect each small equilateral triangle along an altitude. This yields $6\cdot4\cdot2=\boxed{48}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair O=(0,0);for(int k=0;k<6;++k){pair A=6*dir(60*k),B=6*dir(60*(k+1));draw(O--A--B--O);pair P=(O+A)/2,Q=(A+B)/2,R=(O+B)/2;draw(P--Q--R--cycle);draw(O--(P+R)/2^^P--(A+Q)/2^^R--(Q+B)/2^^Q--(P+R)/2);}label("All small triangles bisected",(0,-7));
\end{asy}
