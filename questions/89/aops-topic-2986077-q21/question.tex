Let $a_1,a_2,\dots,a_{2022}$ be a permutation of $\{1,2,\dots,2022\}$ such that $|a_{2k}-a_{2k-1}|\in\{1,2\}$ for $k=1,2,\dots,1011$. If $N$ is the number of such permutations, find the largest $n$ such that $2^n\mid N$.

$\textbf{(A)}\ 1003\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ 1011\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ 1012\qquad\textbf{(D)}\ 2014\qquad\textbf{(E)}\ 2016$
