A row of $7$ seats has $6$ pairs of adjacent seats. Choose the pair for Jim and John ($6$ ways) and their order ($2$ ways). The other $4$ students go into $4$ of the remaining $5$ seats in order: $5\cdot4\cdot3\cdot2=120$ ways. The total is $6\cdot2\cdot120=\boxed{1440}$.
