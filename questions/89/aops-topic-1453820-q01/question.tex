A line through an external point $P$ touches a circle at $T$, and another line through $P$ meets the circle at $A$ and $B$, with $A$ between $P$ and $B$. Let $\overset{\frown}{TA}$ be the arc from $T$ to $A$ not containing $B$, and $\overset{\frown}{TB}$ the arc from $T$ to $B$ not containing $A$ (arcs measured in degrees). Prove that
\[
\angle TPB=\dfrac{\overset{\frown}{TB}-\overset{\frown}{TA}}{2},
\]
using only the inscribed angle theorem and the definition of a tangent (a line with exactly one point in common with the circle); in particular, do not assume the tangent-chord theorem.
