$1600=2^6\cdot5^2=2^4\cdot(2^2\cdot5^2)=16\cdot100$. So $\sqrt[4]{1600}=\sqrt[4]{16}\cdot\sqrt[4]{100}=2\sqrt{10}$. Check: $(2\sqrt{10})^4=16\cdot100$. Answer: $\boxed{2\sqrt{10}}$.
