Let $AB=x$ and $BE=y$, so $BC=2y$. Then $2x+2y=20$ gives $x+y=10$, and $x^2+y^2=81$. Hence $2xy=100-81=19$.

\end{asy}

The area is $AB\cdot BC=2xy=\boxed{19}$.
