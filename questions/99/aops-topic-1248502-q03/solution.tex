An identity $e$ satisfies $a\#e=a+ae+e=a$ for all $a$, i.e. $e(1+a)=0$ for all $a$, so $e=0$ (and $0\#a=a$ too). The inverse of $4$ is the number $b$ with $4\#b=e=0$: $4+4b+b=0$, so $b=\boxed{-\dfrac{4}{5}}$. (Check: $4\#\left(-\dfrac{4}{5}\right)=4-\dfrac{16}{5}-\dfrac{4}{5}=0$.)
