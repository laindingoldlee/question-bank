An integer has exactly three positive divisors precisely when it is the square of a prime. Thus $p+1=q^2$, so $p=(q-1)(q+1)$. For $p$ to be prime, $q-1=1$, yielding $q=2$ and $p=3$. Therefore there is $\boxed1$ such prime.
