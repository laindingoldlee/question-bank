Let the other sides be $b,c$. Area gives $bc\sin A=18$. The cosine rule gives $(b-c)^2=a^2-2bc(1-\cos A)=9-36\tan(\dfrac{A}{2})$, which is positive at $3^\circ$, so a non-acute triangle can exist. But for an acute triangle, $B,C<90^\circ$ and $B+C=177^\circ$, so both exceed $87^\circ$. By the sine rule its area is $\dfrac{a^2\sin B\sin C}{2\sin A}>\dfrac{9\cos^2 3^\circ}{2\sin3^\circ}>80$, contradicting 9. Hence no acute triangle satisfies the data.
