Because $f(x)-1=(x-1)^2$, induction gives $f^{\circ n}(x)-1=(x-1)^{2^n}$. Thus the number in question is $2018^{2^{2018}}=(2\cdot1009)^{2^{2018}}$. The number $1009$ is prime, as trial division by primes at most $31$ confirms. The greatest prime factor is $\boxed{1009}$.
