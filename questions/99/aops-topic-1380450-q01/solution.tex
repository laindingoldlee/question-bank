By the rational root theorem any integer root divides $6$. Trying $x=1$: $1-6+11-6=0$, so $x-1$ is a factor, and dividing,
\[
x^3-6x^2+11x-6=(x-1)(x^2-5x+6)=(x-1)(x-2)(x-3).
\]
The solutions are $\boxed{x=1,\ 2,\ 3}$.
