The sum is between $2$ and $10$, so it must be $3$, $6$ or $9$. Sum $3$: $(1,2),(2,1)$. Sum $6$: $(1,5),(2,4),(3,3),(4,2),(5,1)$. Sum $9$: $(4,5),(5,4)$. That is $9$ of the $25$ equally likely outcomes, so the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{9}{25}}$.
