Let Ann have $2a$. After she gives $a$ to Victor, everyone has $a$; so Victor had $0$ and every other child (including John) has $a$. Suppose there are $k$ children besides Ann and Victor. If Ann gives all $2a$ to John, he has $3a$, and the others have $0$ (Ann) $+0$ (Victor) $+(k-1)a$. So $3a=(k-1)a$, $k=4$, and there are $2+4=\boxed{6}$ children.
