The centre distance is $8-4=4$, equal to the small radius. Therefore the line through the large centre perpendicular to the centre line is tangent to the small circle. Its chord in the large circle is a diameter of length $16$. No chord exceeds a diameter, so the maximum is $\boxed{16}$.
