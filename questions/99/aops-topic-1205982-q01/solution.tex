Let $s=x+y+z$. Then $28s<28x+30y+31z=365<31s$ (strict because $y,z\ge1$ and $x\ge1$), so $11.8<s<13.04$, i.e. $s=12$ or $13$. If $s=13$, then $365=28\cdot13+2y+3z=364+2y+3z$, so $2y+3z=1$, impossible for positive $y,z$. Hence $s=\boxed{12}$, as in the calendar: for example $x=1$, $y=4$, $z=7$ (February, four $30$-day months, seven $31$-day months) gives $28+120+217=365$.
