Place $D$ at the origin, $A=(a,0)$ and $C=(-a,0)$, and write $B=(u,v)$ with $L=u^2+v^2$. Then $K=\dfrac{1}{3}B$ and $E=\dfrac{2}{3}B$. From $AK=AD=a$, we get $\dfrac{L}{9}-\dfrac{2au}{3}=0$, so $L=6au$. Consequently
\[AB^2=L-2au+a^2=\dfrac{2L}{3}+a^2,\]
\[CE^2=\dfrac{4L}{9}+\dfrac{4au}{3}+a^2=\dfrac{2L}{3}+a^2.\]
Therefore $CE=AB=\boxed{10}$.
