Count hands with a rank appearing at least three times:
\begin{itemize}
\item exactly three of a kind, other two of different ranks: $13\cdot\dbinom43\cdot\dbinom{12}{2}\cdot4^2=54912$;
\item full house: $13\cdot\dbinom43\cdot12\cdot\dbinom42=3744$;
\item four of a kind: $13\cdot48=624$.
\end{itemize}
The total is $59280$ out of $\dbinom{52}{5}=2598960$ hands, about $2.28\%$, i.e. $\boxed{2.3\%}$.
