Kevin proposes to Korn once a month. On his $n$-th proposal he succeeds with probability $\dfrac1{187^n}$ (that is, $\dfrac1{187}$ the first time, $\dfrac1{187^2}$ the second time, and so on). Show that Kevin never succeeds with probability at least $\dfrac{185}{186}$. What does this say about the expected time until he succeeds?
