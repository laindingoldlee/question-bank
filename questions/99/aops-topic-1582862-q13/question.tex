Astronaut Polly Hedra circles Earth once every $90$ minutes on a path directly above the equator. The diameter of Earth is about $8000$ miles. Ignoring the rotation of Earth, what distance along the equator does she pass directly over while eating a quick $15$-minute lunch? Give the answer to the nearest mile. Use $3.14159$ for $\pi$.
