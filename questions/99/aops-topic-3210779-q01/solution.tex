An edge-interior cube or a corner cube is painted; the six face-center cubes and the one interior cube are not. There are $12$ edge-interior cubes and $8$ corners, giving $\boxed{20}$. Equivalently $27-6-1=20$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair a=(0,0),v=(1.1,.8);for(int i=0;i<3;++i)for(int j=0;j<3;++j){pen p=(i==1&&j==1)?white:lightgray;filldraw((i,j)--(i+1,j)--(i+1,j+1)--(i,j+1)--cycle,p,black);}draw((0,3)--v+(0,3)--v+(3,3)--(3,3));draw((3,0)--v+(3,0)--v+(3,3));for(int i=1;i<3;++i){draw((i,3)--v+(i,3));draw((3,i)--v+(3,i));}label("front face",(1.5,-.4));
\end{asy}
