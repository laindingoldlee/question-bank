Let $T_1=100$ and $T_n=100^{T_{n-1}}$ for $n\ge2$, so that $T_n=100^{100^{\cdot^{\cdot^{\cdot^{100}}}}}$ is a tower of $n$ copies of $100$ (including the base). How many digits does $T_n$ have? Express your answer in terms of $T_{n-1}$ for $n\ge2$, and give the numbers of digits of $T_1$, $T_2$ and $T_3$.
