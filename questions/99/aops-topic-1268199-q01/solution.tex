For each $k\ge1$,
\[\dfrac{1}{k(k+1)}=\dfrac{(k+1)-k}{k(k+1)}=\dfrac{1}{k}-\dfrac{1}{k+1}.\]
Hence the sum telescopes:
\[\left(1-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)+\left(\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{3}\right)+\cdots+\left(\dfrac{1}{n}-\dfrac{1}{n+1}\right)=1-\dfrac{1}{n+1}=\dfrac{n}{n+1}.\qquad\blacksquare\]
