In hot weather, a homeowner places $20$ ice cubes, each with edge $2$ cm, in a container shaped like a rectangular box with internal dimensions $1$ dm, $6$ dm and $2$ dm. Assuming that the volume does not change when the ice melts, what fraction of the container does the melted water fill?
