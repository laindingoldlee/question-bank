No. A finite list does not specify a unique continuation. More formally, let $P$ be the interpolation polynomial of degree at most seven taking the listed values at $1,\ldots,8$. For any real $t$, the polynomial $P_t(x)=P(x)+\dfrac{t-P(9)}{8!}\prod_{j=1}^8(x-j)$ agrees at those eight indices and has $P_t(9)=t$. Thus every real next value is possible.
