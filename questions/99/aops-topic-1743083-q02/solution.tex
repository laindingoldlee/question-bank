The powers of $4$ are $4^k=2^{2k}$. We need $257\le 2^{2k}\le 262145$. Since $4^4=256<257$ and $4^9=2^{18}=262144\le262145<4^{10}$, the values are $k=5,6,7,8,9$: $\boxed{5}$ powers of $4$.
