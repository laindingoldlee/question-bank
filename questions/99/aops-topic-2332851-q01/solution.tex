The denominator is $\dfrac{8\cdot9}{2}=36=4\cdot9=4\cdot3\cdot3$. Cancelling $4$ and $3$ from the numerator and $3$ from the $6$:
\[\dfrac{1\cdot2\cdot3\cdot4\cdot5\cdot6\cdot7\cdot8}{36}=1\cdot2\cdot5\cdot2\cdot7\cdot8=1120.\]
The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(B) }1120}$.
