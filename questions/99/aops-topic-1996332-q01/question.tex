An equilateral triangle $ABC$ has a point $O$ inside it whose distances to the sides $AB$, $BC$ and $AC$ are $1$, $5$ and $12$, respectively. The circumcircle of the triangle is drawn, and the smallest square containing this circle is circumscribed about it. The area of the square minus the sum of the areas of the triangle and of the circle can be written as $x-y\sqrt z-w\pi$, where $x,y,z,w$ are positive integers and $z$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. Find $x+y+z+w$.
