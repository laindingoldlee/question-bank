First arrange $C,C,U,E$ in $\dfrac{4!}{2!}=12$ ways. Insert the three identical $S$ letters into three different gaps among the five surrounding/between-letter gaps; this gives $12\binom53=120$ arrangements with no adjacent $S$ letters.

Subtract those in which the two $C$ letters remain adjacent. Treat $CC$ as one block, arrange it with $U,E$ in $3!=6$ ways, and choose three of their four gaps for the $S$ letters. This contributes $6\binom43=24$. The answer is $\boxed{96}$.
