The letters $A,\ldots,J$ are distinct decimal digits satisfying $FBGA-ABAC=DCE$, where the first two numbers have four digits and the last three digits. Also $A<B<C<D$. Minimize the ten-digit number $ABCDEFGHIJ$, then find $ABCD+EFG+HI+J$, allowing a leading zero in the final extracted blocks.
