For each assignment, the unused digits must be assigned to $H,I,J$ increasingly to minimize the full number. Lexicographic minimization gives $(A,B,C,D,E,F,G,H,I,J)=(1,4,8,9,3,2,0,5,6,7)$. sol=[]
for A,B,C,D in permutations(range(1,10),4):
 if not A<B<C<D:continue
