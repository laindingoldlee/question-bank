Find all real $x>0$ such that the numbers $\dfrac x2$, $[x]$ and $\{x\}$ are the side lengths of a (non-degenerate) triangle. Here $[x]$ is the integer part of $x$ and $\{x\}=x-[x]$ is its fractional part.
