The interior angles of a hexagon add up to $(6-2)\cdot180^\circ=720^\circ$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair[] H={(0,0),(1.6,0),(2.2,1.039),(1.755,2.261),(0.693,2.546),(-0.498,1.858)};
draw(H[0]--H[1]--H[2]--H[3]--H[4]--H[5]--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
string[] a={"$x$","$120^\circ$","$130^\circ$","$125^\circ$","$135^\circ$","$105^\circ$"};
pair c=(0.95,1.28);
for(int k=0;k<6;++k){ label(a[k],H[k]+0.32*unit(c-H[k])); dot(H[k]); }
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The five given angles add up to $615^\circ$, so the sixth is $720^\circ-615^\circ=105^\circ$, and its digit sum is $1+0+5=\boxed{6}$.
