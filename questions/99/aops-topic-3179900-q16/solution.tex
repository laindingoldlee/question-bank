We have $N=123\sum_{j=0}^{332}1000^j$. Modulo 101, $1000\equiv-10$, whose powers cycle 1,$-10,-1,10$, summing to 0 over each four terms. The 333 terms consist of 83 full cycles and one extra 1. Thus $N\equiv123\equiv22$, and $\boxed{m=79}$.
