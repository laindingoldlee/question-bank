(Ceva's theorem.) In triangle $ABC$, points $D$, $E$, $F$ lie on the sides $BC$, $CA$, $AB$ (not at the vertices), and the segments $AD$, $BE$, $CF$ pass through a common point $P$. Prove that
\[
\dfrac{BD}{DC}\cdot\dfrac{CE}{EA}\cdot\dfrac{AF}{FB}=1 .
\]
