ENG and FRA are in matches $M_5$ and $M_6$, whose winners meet in a quarterfinal; they can only meet there, and do so iff both win their first matches: $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac12=\boxed{\dfrac14}$. (In particular they cannot meet in a semifinal.)
