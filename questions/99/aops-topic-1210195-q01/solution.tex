Let $u=\sqrt{x-b^2}\ge0$ and $v=\sqrt{x-a^2}\ge0$. Then $u-v=a-b\ne0$ and
\[u^2-v^2=a^2-b^2=(a-b)(a+b).\]
Dividing, $u+v=a+b$. Adding and subtracting, $u=a$ and $v=b$, so $x=a^2+b^2$. Check: $\sqrt{a^2}-\sqrt{b^2}=a-b$. The unique solution is $\boxed{x=a^2+b^2}$.
