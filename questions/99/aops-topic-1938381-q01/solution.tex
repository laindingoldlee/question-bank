Both expressions are limits of convergent processes, and both are positive. The continued fraction repeats itself, so $A=3+\dfrac{1}{A}$, i.e.\ $A^2-3A-1=0$ and $A=\dfrac{3+\sqrt{13}}{2}$ (the positive root). Similarly $B=\sqrt{3+B}$, so $B^2-B-3=0$ and $B=\dfrac{1+\sqrt{13}}{2}$. Hence $A-B=\dfrac{3+\sqrt{13}-1-\sqrt{13}}{2}=\boxed{1}$.
