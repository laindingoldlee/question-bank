Put $D=(0,0),A=(0,25),B=(-9,0),C=(16,0)$. For a common similarity parameter $t\in(0,1)$, we have $P=(-9t,25(1-t))$ and $Q=(16t,25(1-t))$. Perpendicularity gives $-144t^2+625(1-t)^2=0$. Positivity permits square roots: $25(1-t)=12t$, so $t=\dfrac{25}{37}$. Thus $PQ=25t=\dfrac{625}{37}$ and $111PQ=\boxed{1875}$.
