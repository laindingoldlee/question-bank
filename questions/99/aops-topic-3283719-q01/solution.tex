The triangle is right because $5^2+12^2=13^2$, so $A=30$. The equation becomes $2B-2=B+1$, hence $B=3$. Assigning three distinct numbered seats gives $C=8\cdot7\cdot6=\boxed{336}$.

\end{asy}
