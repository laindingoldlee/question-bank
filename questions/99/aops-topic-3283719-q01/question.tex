Let $A$ be the area of a triangle with sides $5,12,13$. Let $B$ solve $2B-2=\dfrac{10B+10}{A-20}$. How many ways $C$ can $B$ people be selected from eight distinct people and assigned to seats numbered $1$ through $B$?
