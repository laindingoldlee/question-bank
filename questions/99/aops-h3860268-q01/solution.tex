Use the black side length $s$ as a unit. Then $b=5s$, $r=20s$, and $L=60s$, so $3600s^2=2026$. The seven squares occupy
\[
2(20s)^2+2(5s)^2+3s^2=853s^2.
\]
Their interiors are disjoint, so the remaining area is
\[
(3600-853)s^2=\dfrac{2747\cdot2026}{3600}
=\boxed{\dfrac{2782711}{1800}\text{ cm}^2}.
\]
