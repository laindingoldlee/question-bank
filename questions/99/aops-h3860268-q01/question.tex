A square sheet has area $2026\text{ cm}^2$. Inside it lie seven smaller squares with disjoint interiors: two red squares, two blue squares, and three black squares. Their side lengths satisfy
\[
L=3r,\qquad r=4b,\qquad b=5s,
\]
where $L$ is the sheet's side length and $r,b,s$ are the red, blue, and black side lengths. Find the area of the part of the sheet outside all seven smaller squares.
