A cylindrical water hose is $2$ m long and has an internal diameter of $4$ cm. The wall of the hose is $0.5$ cm thick.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the external radius of the hose.
\item[(b)] Find the volume of material used to make the hose.
\item[(c)] Find the total surface area of the hose, counting the outer curved surface, the inner curved surface and the two ring-shaped ends.
\end{enumerate}
Give your answers in centimetres, in terms of $\pi$ where needed.
