Factor: $2004=2^2\cdot3\cdot167$, and $167$ is prime. One of the three numbers, say $z$, is a multiple of $167$. If $z\ge2\cdot167=334$, the sum exceeds $334$. If $z=167$, then $xy=12$, and $x+y$ is smallest when $x$ and $y$ are as close as possible: the factorisations $1\cdot12$, $2\cdot6$, $3\cdot4$ give sums $13$, $8$, $7$. So the minimum is $3+4+167=\boxed{174}$.
