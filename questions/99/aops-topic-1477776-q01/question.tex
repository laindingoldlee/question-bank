I have a bag with $6$ marbles numbered $1$ to $6$. Mathew has a bag with $12$ marbles numbered $1$ to $12$. Mathew chooses one marble from his bag and I choose two from mine, one after the other. In how many ways can we choose the marbles (where the order of my choices matters) so that the sum of the numbers on my marbles equals the number on his?
