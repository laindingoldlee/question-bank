Count my ordered choices first: I choose two different marbles in order in $6\cdot5=30$ ways. For each of them, the sum of my two numbers is between $1+2=3$ and $5+6=11$, which is a number on exactly one of Mathew's marbles. So each of my $30$ ordered choices goes with exactly one choice of Mathew's, and the number of ways is $\boxed{30}$.
