Alice, Bob and Cara start with the list of the first $1000$ positive integers. Alice removes every second number of the list (the 2nd, 4th, 6th, \dots), then Bob removes every third number of what is left, and then Cara removes every fifth number of what remains. These three steps together form one round, and three rounds are carried out in all. How many numbers are left?
