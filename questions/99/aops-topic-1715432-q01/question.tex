There are $12$ students at a party, $5$ of whom are girls and $7$ boys. In how many ways can the $12$ students be arranged in a row if
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] the $5$ girls must be together;
\item[(ii)] no two girls are adjacent;
\item[(iii)] between two particular boys $A$ and $B$ there are no boys but exactly $3$ girls?
\end{enumerate}
