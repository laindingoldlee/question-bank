At a prime with exponent $v$ in $n$, and exponents $a\le b$ in $2000,24$, the two sides have exponents $\min(v,b)$ and $\max(v,a)$. Equality holds exactly when $a\le v\le b$. Therefore $\gcd(2000,24)=8$ divides $n$ and $n$ divides $\operatorname{lcm}(2000,24)=6000$. Write $n=8d$ with $d\mid750=2\cdot3\cdot5^3$. There are $2\cdot2\cdot4=16$ divisors and their sum is $(1+2)(1+3)(1+5+25+125)=1872$. Thus $D=\dfrac{8\cdot1872}{16}=936$, and $\boxed{3D=2808}$.
