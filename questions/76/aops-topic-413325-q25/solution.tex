The display shows the hours $1$ to $12$ without a leading zero.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Hours $1$ to $9$} (times $h{:}mn$, three digits): a palindrome needs $n=h$, and the tens digit $m$ of the minutes can be $0,1,\dots,5$. That gives $9\cdot6=54$ times.
\item \textbf{Hours $10$, $11$, $12$} (four digits $1k{:}mn$): we need $m=k$ and $n=1$, giving $10{:}01$, $11{:}11$, $12{:}21$, which are $3$ times.
\end{itemize}
The total is $54+3=57$, answer $\boxed{\textbf{(A)}}$.
