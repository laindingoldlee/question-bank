We prove the contrapositive. If neither $x<5$ nor $y<5$, then $x\ge5$ and $y\ge5$, so $x+y\ge10$, i.e. it is not true that $x+y<10$. Hence $x+y<10$ forces $x<5$ or $y<5$. $\blacksquare$
