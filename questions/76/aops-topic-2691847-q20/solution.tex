Divide by $3$: $13x+50y=1$. Euclid: $50=3\cdot13+11$, $13=1\cdot11+2$, $11=5\cdot2+1$. Back-substituting,
\[1=11-5\cdot2=11-5(13-11)=6\cdot11-5\cdot13=6(50-3\cdot13)-5\cdot13=6\cdot50-23\cdot13.\]
So $(x,y)=\boxed{(-23,6)}$: $39(-23)+150\cdot6=-897+900=3$. All solutions are $x=-23+50t$, $y=6-13t$ for integers $t$.
