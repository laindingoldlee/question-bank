With $j$ jumps the total jump damage is $32-2^{5-j}$. If $j>5$, this is nonintegral and cannot combine with integer fireblast damage to give 100. For $j=0,1,2,3,4,5$ the jump totals are $0,16,24,28,30,31$. Only 0 and 30 leave a multiple of 5. These give 20 attacks, or 4 jumps plus 14 fireblasts, respectively. The minimum is $\boxed{18}$.
