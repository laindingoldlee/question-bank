Let $t=\sqrt{x+1}>0$. Then $6t^2-13t-6=0$, so $t=\dfrac{13\pm\sqrt{313}}{12}$. The minus sign is negative and must be discarded. Thus there is one root, $x=t^2-1=\boxed{\dfrac{169+13\sqrt{313}}{72}}$, which is also the requested sum. Keeping the negative t-root would introduce an extraneous value.
