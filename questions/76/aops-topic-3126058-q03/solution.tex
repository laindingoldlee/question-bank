The congruence is equivalent to $ k^2\equiv1\pmod3 $, hence to $ 3\nmid k $. Sum 1 through 299 and subtract the multiples of 3: $\dfrac{299\cdot300}{2}-3\dfrac{99\cdot100}{2}=44850-14850=\boxed{30000}$.
