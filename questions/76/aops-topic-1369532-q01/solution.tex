The denominators are $1\cdot2,\ 2\cdot3,\ 3\cdot4,\ 4\cdot5,\dots,49\cdot50$ (as $49\cdot50=2450$), and
\[
\dfrac{1}{k(k+1)}=\dfrac{1}{k}-\dfrac{1}{k+1}.
\]
So the sum telescopes:
\[
\left(1-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)+\left(\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{3}\right)+\cdots+\left(\dfrac{1}{49}-\dfrac{1}{50}\right)=1-\dfrac{1}{50}=\boxed{\dfrac{49}{50}} .
\]
