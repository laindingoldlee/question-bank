The polynomial $2x+3$ has degree $\boxed1$, and a nonzero constant such as $5$ has degree $\boxed0$. The degree is the greatest exponent with nonzero coefficient. The zero polynomial has no such exponent, so its degree is ordinarily left undefined (or assigned a special value under an explicitly stated convention).
