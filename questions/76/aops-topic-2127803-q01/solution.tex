Let $N$ be the number of coins. The conditions say $N\equiv-1$ modulo $3$, $4$ and $5$, so $N+1$ is divisible by $3$, $4$ and $5$, hence by $\operatorname{lcm}(3,4,5)=60$. With $100<N<150$ we need $101<N+1<151$, and the only multiple of $60$ there is $120$. So $N=\boxed{119}$.

Check: $119=3\cdot39+2=4\cdot29+3=5\cdot23+4$.
