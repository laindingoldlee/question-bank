Captain Hook found a chest of gold coins. If he carries them to his ship $3$ at a time, $2$ coins are left at the end; if he carries them $4$ at a time, $3$ are left; and if he carries them $5$ at a time, $4$ are left. How many coins are there, if there are more than $100$ but fewer than $150$?
