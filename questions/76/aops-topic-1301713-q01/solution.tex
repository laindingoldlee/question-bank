Scaling does not change percentages, so the scale $1:10$ is irrelevant. The torso and legs make up $85$ percent of the height in the ratio $5:7$, so the torso is
\[
85\cdot\dfrac{5}{12}=\dfrac{425}{12}\approx\boxed{35.4}\text{ percent}
\]
of the real height.
