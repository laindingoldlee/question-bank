In each round of an online game, $3$ of the $10$ players in a lobby are chosen uniformly at random to be the impostors, independently of the other rounds. You are one of the $10$ players. What is the expected number of rounds you need to play until you have been an impostor at least once (counting the round in which it first happens)? Prove your answer.
