Put $u=\overline{ab}$, $v=\overline{cd}$ and $t=u+v$. Then $100u+v=t^2$, so $99u=t(t-1)$. As $N$ has four digits, $32\le t\le99$. Coprimality of consecutive integers shows $t\equiv0$ or 1 modulo 9, and independently modulo 11. The four combined residue classes modulo 99 are $0,1,45,55$. In the range, $t=45,55,99$. These give $N=2025,3025,9801$, respectively, all satisfying the block equation. Distinct digits exclude 2025, leaving $\boxed{3025\text{ and }9801}$.
