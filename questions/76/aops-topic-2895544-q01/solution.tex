With $n$ teams, each has played between $0$ and $n-1$ games. A team with $0$ games and a team with $n-1$ games cannot both exist, since the latter has played everyone. So at most $n-1$ values occur among $n$ teams, and by pigeonhole two teams share a value. $\blacksquare$
