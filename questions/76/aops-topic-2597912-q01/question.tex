For a finite set $A$ of positive integers let $\sigma(A)$ be the sum of its elements, with $\sigma(\varnothing)=0$. Let $S$ be a subset of $\{1,2,\dots,25\}$ such that $\sigma(A)\ne\sigma(B)$ for any two different subsets $A$, $B$ of $S$. Find the largest possible value of $\sigma(S)$.
