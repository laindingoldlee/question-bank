Let $A$ be the starting point, $O$ the center, and $u$ the unit direction of the segment. Avoiding the disk requires $OA\cdot u\ge0$. If its length is $t$, the endpoint condition is $|OA+tu|^2=9$, or $t^2+2t(OA\cdot u)=8$. Thus $t^2\le8$, with equality when the segment is tangent to the inner circle at $A$. The maximum is $\boxed{2\sqrt2}$.
