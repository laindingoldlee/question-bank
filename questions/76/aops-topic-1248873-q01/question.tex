A bus has $13$ passenger seats.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] Eight passengers board at the terminal. In how many ways can they be seated?
\item[(ii)] At the first stop nobody gets off, and eight people are waiting to board, among them three friends who insist on travelling together (all three board or none). How many different groups of five of the waiting people can board to fill the remaining seats?
\end{enumerate}
