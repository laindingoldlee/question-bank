(i) The eight passengers take $8$ of the $13$ seats in order: $13\cdot12\cdots6=\dfrac{13!}{5!}=\boxed{51{,}891{,}840}$ ways.

(ii) Either all three friends board, together with $2$ of the other $5$ people: $\dbinom52=10$ groups; or none of them boards, and all $5$ others do: $1$ group. In total $\boxed{11}$ groups.
