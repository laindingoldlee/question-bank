We have $8^6=(2^3)^6=2^{18}=(2^6)^3=64^3$ and $5^9=(5^3)^3=125^3$. The cubes strictly between them are $k^3$ with $64<k<125$, that is $k=65,66,\dots,124$. There are $124-65+1=\boxed{60}$ of them.
