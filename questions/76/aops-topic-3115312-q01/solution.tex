Place the nine integers into three boxes according to their residue modulo $3$. One box contains at least three. Inside an interval of length $8$, a residue class can contain at most three integers, and any three in it must have consecutive offsets $0,3,6$. Thus those three form an arithmetic progression with common difference $3$.
