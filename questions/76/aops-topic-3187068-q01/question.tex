Four people A,B,C,D independently choose uniformly random rankings of four distinct songs. In that order each speaks for the song currently at the top of their own ranking. For each other person independently, with probability $\dfrac{1}{3}$ that song moves up one place in their ranking; with probability $\dfrac{2}{3}$ nothing changes. A song already on top stays there. After all four speeches, find the probability that at least two people have identical rankings. An exact finite algorithm is allowed.
