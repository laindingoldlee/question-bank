Of the $36$ equally likely ordered outcomes, $6$ have the two numbers equal. By symmetry the other $30$ split evenly: $15$ with the first number larger and $15$ with the second larger. So the favourable outcomes number $6+15=21$, and the probability is
\[
\dfrac{21}{36}=\boxed{\textbf{(D)}\ \dfrac{7}{12}}.
\]
