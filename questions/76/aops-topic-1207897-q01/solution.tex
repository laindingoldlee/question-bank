Every square is congruent to $0$, $1$ or $4$ modulo $8$ (an odd square is $1$; an even square $4k^2$ is $0$ or $4$). Adding three values from $\{0,1,4\}$ gives one of $0,1,2,3,4,5,6,8,9,12$, which are congruent to $0,1,2,3,4,5,6,0,1,4$ modulo $8$; the residue $7$ never occurs. So no number $8k+7$ is a sum of three squares, and there are infinitely many such numbers.
