A student tries to prove that $2k^2+1$ is never a perfect square for an integer $k>2$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that the claim is false by finding the least integer $k>2$ for which $2k^2+1$ is a perfect square.
\item[(b)] Prove that there are infinitely many positive integers $k$ for which $2k^2+1$ is a perfect square.
\end{enumerate}
