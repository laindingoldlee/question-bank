Condition on the four chosen vertices in circular order. There are three ways to partition them into two unordered pairs. Exactly one pairs opposite vertices and creates the two crossing diagonals; the other two pairings use noncrossing sides. Each pairing corresponds to the same number of ordered quadruples. Thus the probability is $\dfrac{1}{3}$, and $\boxed{ab=3}$.
