Write $h=P(HH)=P(TT)$ and $m=P(HT\text{ or }TH)$. The stated relation $h=3m$ and $2h+m=1$ give $h=\dfrac{3}{7}$, so the formal expectation over 84 trials is $\boxed{36}$. However independent weighted coins cannot meet these data: $P(HH)=P(TT)$ forces their head probabilities to sum to one, and their product is at most $\dfrac{1}{4}<\dfrac{3}{7}$. A dependent joint model can satisfy them, but an independent-coin model is inconsistent.
