Two odd primes sum to an even number greater than $2$, so one of $a,b$ is $2$. Write the primes as $2,p,p+2$ with odd $p$. If the singled-out prime is $r\ge3$, the other primes are at most $r+2$, so their average is at most $r+2$, whereas $4r^4-4>r+2$, impossible. Thus $r=2$, and $p+1=4\cdot2^4-4=60$. Hence $p=59$, with $61$ also prime, and the answer is $\boxed{59}$.
