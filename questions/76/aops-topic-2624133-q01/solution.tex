Both proportions are equivalent to the same product equation. Indeed,
\[\dfrac xy=\dfrac{x'}{y'}\iff xy'=x'y\]
(multiply by $yy'\ne0$, or divide by it), and
\[\dfrac{x}{x'}=\dfrac{y}{y'}\iff xy'=x'y\]
(multiply or divide by $x'y'\ne0$). Hence $x:y=x':y'\iff x:x'=y:y'$. $\blacksquare$
