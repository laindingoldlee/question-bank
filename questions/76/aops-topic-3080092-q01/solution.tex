Modulo $100$, the binomial theorem gives $11^{2020}=(1+10)^{2020}\equiv1+20200\equiv1$. Thus the expression is congruent to $38^2\equiv1444\equiv44\pmod{100}$. The last two digits are $\boxed{44}$.
