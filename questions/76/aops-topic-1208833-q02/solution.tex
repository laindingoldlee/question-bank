A given box stays unshaded with probability $\left(\dfrac{9}{10}\right)^{10}$. By linearity of expectation the expected number of shaded boxes is
\[10\left(1-\left(\dfrac{9}{10}\right)^{10}\right)=\boxed{\dfrac{6513215599}{1000000000}}\approx6.51.\]
