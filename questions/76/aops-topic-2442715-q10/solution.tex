A multiple of $25$ ends in $00$, $25$, $50$ or $75$. The product of the digits must be positive, so no digit is $0$, and the number ends in $25$ or $75$. The product of the digits must be divisible by $25=5^2$, and $5$ is prime, so at least two digits are $5$. The last two digits contain only one $5$, so the number has at least three digits, with another $5$ before the last two digits. The three-digit candidates are $525$ and $575$; the smaller is $\boxed{525}$, whose digit product is $5\cdot2\cdot5=50$, a positive multiple of $25$.
