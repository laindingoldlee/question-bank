Let $a_1,a_2$ be any positive integers and $a_{n+2}=a_{n+1}+a_n$ for $n\ge1$. Prove that
\[\lim_{n\to\infty}\dfrac{a_n}{a_{n+1}}=\phi=\dfrac{\sqrt5-1}{2}.\]
