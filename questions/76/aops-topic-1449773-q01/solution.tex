The product is between $4404$ and $4494$, and the three-digit factor is $\overline{ab4}$ with $a,b\ne4$, $a\ne0$.

\textbf{The last digit.} The product ends in $4$, and the first factor ends in $4$, so the second factor $\overline{ce}$ has last digit $e$ with $4e$ ending in $4$: $e=1$ or $e=6$.

\textbf{Size.} Since $100\le\overline{ab4}\le994$ and $4404\le\text{product}\le4494$, the second factor lies between $\dfrac{4404}{994}>4$ and $\dfrac{4494}{100}<45$, and its tens digit $c$ is not $4$; so $\overline{ce}\in\{11,16,21,26,31,36\}$.

For each one, the first factor ends in $4$ and equals the product divided by $\overline{ce}$, where the product is one of $4404,4414,\dots,4494$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $11$: $\overline{ab4}=\dfrac{\text{product}}{11}$ must be about $400$--$408$, so $a=4$, not allowed.
\item $16$: $\overline{ab4}$ would be about $276$--$280$, and $16\cdot274=4384$, $16\cdot284=4544$: no product of the form $44*4$.
\item $21$: $\overline{ab4}$ near $210$--$214$; $21\cdot214=4494$. Valid: $214\times21=4494$.
\item $26$: $\overline{ab4}$ near $170$--$172$; $26\cdot164=4264$, $26\cdot174=4524$: none.
\item $31$: $\overline{ab4}$ near $142$--$145$; $31\cdot144=4464$, but then $b=4$, not allowed.
\item $36$: $\overline{ab4}$ near $122$--$125$; $36\cdot124=4464$. Valid: $124\times36=4464$.
\end{itemize}
So there are exactly two solutions:
\[
\boxed{214\times21=4494\quad\text{and}\quad124\times36=4464}.
\]
(If a $*$ were allowed to be a $4$, the products $144\times31=4464$ and $404\times11=4444$ would also work.)
