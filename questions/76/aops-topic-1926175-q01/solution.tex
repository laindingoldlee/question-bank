This is an infinite geometric series with first term $7$ and common ratio $\dfrac{1}{2}$, and $\left|\dfrac{1}{2}\right|<1$. Its sum is
\[
x=\dfrac{7}{1-\dfrac{1}{2}}=\boxed{14}.
\]
(Indeed the partial sums are $7\left(2-\dfrac{1}{2^{n-1}}\right)$, which approach $14$.)
