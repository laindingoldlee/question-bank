Two leopard seals, Snap and Snarl, start $210$ m apart and swim towards each other, each at a constant $10$ km/h. A penguin, Gilly, starts at Snap and swims back and forth between the two seals until they meet. When going from Snap to Snarl, Gilly swims at $15$ km/h; when going from Snarl to Snap, at $20$ km/h. What total distance does Gilly swim before the seals meet?
