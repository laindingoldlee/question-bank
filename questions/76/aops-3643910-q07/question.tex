Kiran has two buckets, Bucket $A$ and Bucket $B$, each with lots of water, and a hose that can add water at a fixed rate to the buckets. Kiran punctures a hole into Bucket $A$ so it leaks out water at a constant rate into Bucket $B$. If Kiran uses the hose to add water to Bucket $A$, Bucket $A$ will first be holding $1$ more gallon of water $48$ seconds later. If he instead uses the hose to directly add water to Bucket $B$, Bucket $B$ will first be holding $1$ more gallon of water $16$ seconds later. How many seconds does it take for a gallon of water to leak from Bucket $A$ into Bucket $B$?
