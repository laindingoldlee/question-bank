For $n=2k$, $f(n)=\dfrac{k^2}{k(k+1)}=\dfrac{k}{k+1}$, whose distance from 1 is $\dfrac{1}{k+1}$. Thus even solutions begin at $k=9,n=18$. For $n=2k+1$, $f(n)=\dfrac{(k+1)^2}{k(k+1)}=\dfrac{k+1}{k}$, so odd solutions require $k\ge10$, starting at 21. The least is $\boxed{18}$.
