There are 25 primes at most 100. A single spin is nonprime with probability $\dfrac{75}{100}=\dfrac{3}{4}$. All $N$ spins avoid primes with probability $(\dfrac{3}{4})^N$. The complement is $\boxed{1-(\dfrac{3}{4})^N}$.
