Let the tens digit be $t$ and the ones digit $u$. Then $t=2u-1$ and $t=u+4$ with $4<u$. So $2u-1=u+4$, giving $u=5$ and $t=9$. The number is $\boxed{95}$ (check: $9=1\cdot5+4$).
