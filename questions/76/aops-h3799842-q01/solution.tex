The four removed corner triangles each have perpendicular legs that are $\dfrac{3}{5}$ of one rectangle side and $\dfrac{2}{5}$ of the other. Thus each has area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{3}{5}\cdot\dfrac{2}{5}=\dfrac{3}{25}$ of the rectangle. Together they occupy $\dfrac{12}{25}$, leaving $\dfrac{13}{25}$ for $PQRS$. The ratio is $\boxed{13:12}$.
