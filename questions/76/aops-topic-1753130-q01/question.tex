The dynamic pressure $q$ generated by a fluid moving with velocity $v$ is $q=\dfrac{1}{2}nv^2$, where $n$ is the constant density of the fluid. An engineer uses the formula to find the dynamic pressure of a fluid moving with velocity $v$ and of the same fluid moving with velocity $1.5v$. What is the ratio of the dynamic pressure of the faster fluid to that of the slower fluid?
