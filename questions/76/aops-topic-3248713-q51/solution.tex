Write $N=100k+67$, where $1\le k\le9$. Ending in $21$ in base three means $N\equiv7\pmod9$. Since $N\equiv k+4\pmod9$, we need $k\equiv3\pmod9$. Thus $k=3$ and $\boxed{N=367}$.
