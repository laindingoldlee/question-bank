A new user joins a forum with $0$ posts. She earns one upvote each time her total number of posts reaches a multiple of $10$, and her daily posting rate depends on her upvotes: on each day she makes $3+2u$ posts, where $u$ is the number of upvotes she has at the start of that day. On which day does her total first reach at least $1000$ posts?
