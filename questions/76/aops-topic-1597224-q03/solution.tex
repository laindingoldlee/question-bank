Group the terms so that each side is a difference of squares:
\[
(a+b+c+d)(a-b-c+d)=\bigl((a+d)+(b+c)\bigr)\bigl((a+d)-(b+c)\bigr)=(a+d)^2-(b+c)^2,
\]
\[
(a-b+c-d)(a+b-c-d)=\bigl((a-d)+(c-b)\bigr)\bigl((a-d)-(c-b)\bigr)=(a-d)^2-(c-b)^2 .
\]
So the condition says
\[
(a+d)^2-(a-d)^2=(b+c)^2-(c-b)^2 .
\]
Each side is again a difference of squares: $(a+d)^2-(a-d)^2=4ad$ and $(b+c)^2-(c-b)^2=4bc$. Hence $4ad=4bc$, i.e.\ $ad=bc$, and dividing by $bd\ne0$ gives
\[
\boxed{\dfrac {a}{b}=\dfrac {c}{d}}. \qquad\blacksquare
\]
