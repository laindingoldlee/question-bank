The side lengths imply $\angle ABC=90^\circ$, so $BD$ makes $45^\circ$ with both legs. The diameter-circle conditions give $\angle AXD=\angle CYD=90^\circ$. Therefore $X,Y$ are the orthogonal projections of $A,C$ onto $BD$.

Their distances along the same ray from $B$ are $BX=5\cos45^\circ=\dfrac{5}{\sqrt2}$ and $BY=12\cos45^\circ=\dfrac{12}{\sqrt2}$. Thus
\[
p=BY-BX=\dfrac{7}{\sqrt2},\qquad\boxed{2p^2=49}.
\]
