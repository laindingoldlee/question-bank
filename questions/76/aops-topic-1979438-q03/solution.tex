The semi-axes are half the axes: $a=1$ and $b=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Place the ellipse as $x^2+\dfrac{y^2}{\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^2}=1$, i.e.\ $x^2+4y^2=1$.

This ellipse is the unit circle squeezed vertically by the factor $\dfrac{1}{2}$, so its area is $\dfrac{1}{2}$ of the circle's area $\pi$, in general $\pi ab$. The area is $\pi\cdot1\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{\pi}{2}}$.
