Find the sum of all positive proper fractions $\dfrac {k}{d}$ (positive integers $k<d$) whose denominators satisfy $d\le35$. Equivalent fractions are counted separately (for example $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\dfrac{2}{4}$ are both included). Express your answer as a mixed number.
