For a fixed denominator $d\ge2$, the fractions are $\dfrac{1}{d},\dfrac{2}{d},\dots,\dfrac{d-1}{d}$, with sum
\[
\dfrac{1+2+\cdots+(d-1)}{d}=\dfrac{(d-1)d}{2d}=\dfrac{d-1}{2}.
\]
(Denominator $1$ gives no positive proper fraction.) Summing over $d=2,\dots,35$:
\[
\sum_{d=2}^{35}\dfrac{d-1}{2}=\dfrac{1+2+\cdots+34}{2}=\dfrac{34\cdot35}{4}=\dfrac{595}{2}=\boxed{297\dfrac{1}{2}}.
\]
