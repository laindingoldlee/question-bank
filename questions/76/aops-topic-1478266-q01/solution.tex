Since $100=9\cdot11+1$, we have $100\equiv1\pmod{11}$, so $100^k\equiv1$ for every $k$. Hence
\[
N\equiv\underbrace{1+1+\cdots+1}_{n}=n\pmod{11},
\]
and $11\mid N$ exactly when $11\mid n$. The least such $n$ is $\boxed{11}$.
