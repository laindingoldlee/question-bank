Player A chooses a digit $x$ and forms the four-digit number $\overline{100x}$ (with digits $1,0,0,x$). Then player B chooses a digit $y$. Now A looks at the numbers
\[\overline{100yx},\quad\overline{100yyx},\quad\overline{100yyyx},\quad\dots,\]
obtained by inserting one, two, three, $\dots$ copies of the digit $y$ between $100$ and $x$. Player A wins if at least one of these numbers has no common divisor greater than $1$ with $\overline{100x}$. For which digits $x$ can A be sure of winning, whatever digit $y$ B chooses?
