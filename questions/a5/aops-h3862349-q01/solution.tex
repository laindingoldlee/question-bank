Write $b=AC=\sqrt6$ and $c=AB=\sqrt{202}$. Set $A=(0,0)$, $B=(c,0)$, $C=(\dfrac{b}{2},\sqrt3\dfrac{b}{2})$. The circumcenter is
\[
O=\left(\dfrac{c}{2},\dfrac{2b-c}{2\sqrt3}\right).
\]
Rotating $A-O$ through $\pm120^\circ$ about $O$ gives
\[
Y=\left(c-\dfrac{b}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt3b}{2}\right),\qquad
X=\left(\dfrac{b+c}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt3(b-c)}{2}\right).
\]
These are the equilateral-triangle vertices in the required order.

The opposite sides $BY$ and $CX$ are parallel, with lengths $b$ and $c$. Their perpendicular separation is $\dfrac{\sqrt 3(c-b)}{2}$. Hence the quadrilateral is a trapezoid of area
\[
\dfrac{b+c}{2}\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3(c-b)}{2}
=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}(c^2-b^2)=49\sqrt3.
\]
Therefore $\boxed{m+n=52}$.
