Hui is an avid reader. She bought a copy of the best seller \emph{Math is Beautiful}. On the first day, she read $\dfrac15$ of the pages plus $12$ more, and on the second day she read $\dfrac14$ of the remaining pages plus $15$ more. On the third day she read $\dfrac13$ of the remaining pages plus $18$ more. She then realizes she has $62$ pages left, which she finishes the next day. How many pages are in this book?

$\textbf{(A) }120\qquad\textbf{(B) }180\qquad\textbf{(C) }240\qquad\textbf{(D) }300\qquad\textbf{(E) }360$
