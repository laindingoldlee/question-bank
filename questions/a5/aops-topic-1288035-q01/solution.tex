The units digit of a product depends only on the units digits of the factors. The units digits of $7^1,7^2,7^3,7^4$ are $7,9,3,1$. Since $7^4$ ends in $1$, multiplying by $7^4$ does not change the units digit, so $7^{n+4}$ has the same units digit as $7^n$. Hence the units digit of $7^n$ is $1,7,9,3$ according as $n\equiv0,1,2,3\pmod4$; the ``$10$'' in the statement $7^n\equiv\cdots\pmod{10}$ is because we want the units digit, and the ``$4$'' is the length of the cycle.

Now find $7^7\bmod4$: $7\equiv-1\pmod4$, so $7^7\equiv(-1)^7=-1\equiv3\pmod4$. Therefore $7^{7^7}$ has the same units digit as $7^3=343$, namely $\boxed{3}$.
