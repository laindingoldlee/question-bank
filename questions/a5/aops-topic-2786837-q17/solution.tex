With $p$ pens and $10-p$ pencils, the probability of one of each (in either order) is
\[2\cdot\dfrac p{10}\cdot\dfrac{10-p}{10}=\dfrac{21}{50}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad p(10-p)=21,\]
so $p^2-10p+21=(p-3)(p-7)=0$. The number of pens is $\boxed{3\text{ or }7}$.
