The answer is $\boxed{1729}$:
\[
1729=1^3+12^3=9^3+10^3 .
\]
A systematic check of all sums $a^3+b^3$ with $1\le a\le b\le12$ (any sum below $1729$ uses cubes below $1729$, so $b\le12$) shows that no smaller number has two representations.
