Jimmy is selling lemonade. He has $200$ cups of lemonade, and he will sell them all by the end of the day. Being the ethically dubious individual he is, Jimmy intends to dilute a few of the cups of lemonade with water to conserve resources. Jimmy sells each cup for $\$4$. It costs him $\$ 1$ to make a diluted cup of lemonade, and it costs him $\$2.75$ to make a cup of normal lemonade. What is the minimum number of diluted cups Jimmy must sell to make a profit of over $\$400$?
