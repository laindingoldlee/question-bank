If new constant symbols may be assigned meanings, for every positive integer $ M $ one can declare a single symbol to mean $ M $. Its one-symbol expression is finite and uses at most ten symbols. Since positive integers are unbounded, this interpretation has no greatest value. To obtain a well-defined finite search one must fix a finite alphabet, its operations and semantics, and a length-counting convention in advance. Under those restrictions at most ten symbols give only finitely many strings.
