A ship travelling at $20$ nautical miles per hour takes $25$ minutes to turn through $18^\circ$, turning at a constant rate. If it keeps turning in this way at this speed until it has gone around a full circle, what is the area, in square nautical miles, of this circle? Express your answer in terms of $\pi$.
