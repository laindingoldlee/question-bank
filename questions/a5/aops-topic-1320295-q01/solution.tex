After the first gift, Jie has $x-12$ and David $x+12=2(x-12)-6$, so $x=42$: Jie $30$, David $54$, Ben $42$.

After Ben gives $y$ to David: $54+y=(42-y)+20$, so $y=4$: David $58$, Ben $38$.

After Ben gives $4$ to Jie: Jie $34$, Ben $34$, which agrees with the last condition. David has $\boxed{58}$ buttons.
