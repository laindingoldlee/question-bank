Jie, David and Ben each have $x$ buttons. Jie gives $12$ buttons to David, who then has $6$ fewer than twice as many as Jie. Ben then gives $y$ buttons to David, who then has $20$ more than Ben. Ben then gives $y$ more buttons to Jie, who then has the same number as Ben. How many buttons does David have now?
