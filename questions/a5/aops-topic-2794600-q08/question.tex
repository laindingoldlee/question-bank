A traveler goes from $(0,0)$ to $(6,6)$ using unit right or up steps. At any of $(1,5),(2,4),(4,2)$, they may instead teleport to either of the other two points. At most one teleport may be used; afterward the machines vanish. How many distinct journeys are possible?
