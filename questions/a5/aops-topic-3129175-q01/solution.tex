Until either seat 1 or seat 100 is chosen by a displaced passenger, those two seats remain equally likely choices whenever random seating occurs. If seat 1 is chosen first, the displacement chain ends and passenger 100 eventually gets seat 100. If seat 100 is chosen first, passenger 100 cannot get it. One of the two must eventually be chosen, and exchanging their labels preserves every random-choice probability before that event. The two outcomes therefore have equal probability, so the answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$.
