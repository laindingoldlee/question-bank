A die has the shape of a regular octahedron with every edge of length $1$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] A number is written on each of the $6$ vertices, and each of the $8$ faces is given the sum of the numbers at its three vertices. Is it possible for the faces to receive the numbers $1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8$ (in some order) if
  \begin{enumerate}
  \item[(i)] the vertex numbers may be any rational numbers?
  \item[(ii)] the vertex numbers must be integers?
  \end{enumerate}
\item[(b)] The die rests on a face on a horizontal table. How high above the table is the opposite (top) face?
\end{enumerate}
