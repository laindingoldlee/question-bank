Multiply both sides by $E$: $T(M-a)=EF$. Divide by $T$ (allowed since $T\ne0$): $M-a=\dfrac{EF}{T}$. Hence
\[
\boxed{a=M-\dfrac{EF}{T}}.
\]
Check: substituting gives $\dfrac{T}{E}\cdot\dfrac{EF}{T}=F$.
