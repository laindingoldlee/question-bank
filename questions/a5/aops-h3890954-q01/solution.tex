Since $AM$ is a median, $[ABM]=[ACM]$. The triangles $ABE$ and $ACE$ occupy the same fraction $\dfrac{AE}{AM}$ of those respective areas, so $[ABE]=[ACE]$. Taking ratios to $[ADE]$ gives
\[\dfrac{AB}{AD}=\dfrac{[ABE]}{[ADE]}=\dfrac{[ACE]}{[ADE]}=\dfrac{CE}{DE}.\]
The first ratio uses bases on $AB$ and the last uses bases on $CD$. Since $DE=AD>0$, the equality reduces to $CE=AB$.

\end{asy}
