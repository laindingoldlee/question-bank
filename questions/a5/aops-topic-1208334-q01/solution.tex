We need $2x+3y+1=0$ and $3x-2y-1=0$. Solving $2x+3y=-1$, $3x-2y=1$: multiplying by $2$ and $3$ and adding gives $13x=1$, so $x=\dfrac{1}{13}$ and $y=\dfrac{3x-1}{2}=-\dfrac{5}{13}$. Hence $x+y=\boxed{-\dfrac{4}{13}}$.
