A flagpole is $10$ feet tall. There are $3$ different types of $2$-foot flags and $2$ different types of $1$-foot flags, with an unlimited supply of each type. Flags are stacked along the pole with no gaps so that they exactly fill the $10$ feet. The order of the flags matters; flags of the same type are indistinguishable, while flags of different types are distinguishable. In how many ways can this be done?
