Let $a_n$ be the number of ways to fill a pole of length $n$ feet. Look at the top flag. If it is a $1$-foot flag ($2$ choices of type), the rest is a filling of length $n-1$; if it is a $2$-foot flag ($3$ choices), the rest is a filling of length $n-2$. Hence
\[a_n=2a_{n-1}+3a_{n-2}\qquad(n\ge 2),\]
with $a_0=1$ (the empty filling) and $a_1=2$. Then
\[a_2=7,\ a_3=20,\ a_4=61,\ a_5=182,\ a_6=547,\ a_7=1640,\ a_8=4921,\ a_9=14762,\ a_{10}=44287 .\]
(Indeed the characteristic roots are $3$ and $-1$, and $a_n=\dfrac{3^{n+1}+(-1)^n}{4}$; for $n=10$ this gives $\dfrac{177147+1}{4}=44287$.)

The number of arrangements is $\boxed{44287}$.
