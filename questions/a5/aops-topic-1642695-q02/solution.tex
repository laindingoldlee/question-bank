Let an orange cost $p$ cents and an apple $q$ cents:
\[
6p+5q=240,\qquad 5p+6q=255 .
\]
Adding, $11p+11q=495$, so $p+q=45$. Hence $3$ apples and $3$ oranges cost $3(p+q)=135$ cents, that is
\[
\boxed{\$1.35}.
\]
(Subtracting the equations gives $q-p=15$, so an orange costs $15$ cents and an apple $30$ cents; check: $90+150=240$ and $75+180=255$.)
