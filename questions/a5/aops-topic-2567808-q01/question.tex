A textbook claims that
\[\dfrac{\sin(180^\circ+\theta)\cos(90^\circ+\theta)\tan(270^\circ-\theta)\cot(360^\circ-\theta)}{\sin(360^\circ-\theta)\cos(360^\circ+\theta)\csc(-\theta)\cot(270^\circ+\theta)}=1\]
for every $\theta$ for which both sides are defined.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Simplify the left-hand side and show that the claim is false.
\item[(b)] Prove that the identity becomes true if $\cot(270^\circ+\theta)$ in the denominator is replaced by $\sin(270^\circ+\theta)$.
\end{enumerate}
