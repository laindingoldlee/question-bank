Keep the whole-number factor $2^1=2$ in front and combine the two fractional powers under one cube root:
\[
2^{\dfrac{1}{3}}\cdot3^{\dfrac{2}{3}}=\sqrt[3]{2}\cdot\sqrt[3]{3^2}=\sqrt[3]{2\cdot9}=\sqrt[3]{18}.
\]
Hence
\[
\dfrac{2\cdot\sqrt[3]{18}}{5}=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{5}\sqrt[3]{18}}.
\]
