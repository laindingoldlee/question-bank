Each condition says $N\equiv-1$ modulo the divisor, so $N+1$ is divisible by $5,6,7$ and $8$, hence by $\operatorname{lcm}(5,6,7,8)=840$. The smallest positive $N$ is $840-1=\boxed{839}$.
