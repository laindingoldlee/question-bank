Completing the square gives $f(x)=a\left(x+\dfrac{b}{2a}\right)^2+c-\dfrac{b^2}{4a}$. Therefore $\boxed{h=-\dfrac{b}{2a},\quad k=c-\dfrac{b^2}{4a}}$. At $x=h$ the squared term is zero; the graph is symmetric about $x=h$ and has vertex $(h,k)$, a minimum if $a>0$ and maximum if $a<0$. Both forms describe the same quadratic function.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
import graph;real f(real x){return (x-1)^2-2;}draw((-2,0)--(4,0),Arrow);draw((0,-3)--(0,5),Arrow);draw(graph(f,-1.5,3.5),blue);draw((1,-3)--(1,5),dashed);dot("$(h,k)$",(1,-2),S);label("example: $a>0$",(2.5,4));
\end{asy}
