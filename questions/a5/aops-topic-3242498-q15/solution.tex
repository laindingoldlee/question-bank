Put $u=|x|\ge0$. We need $3u^2-10u+3=3$ or $-3$. The first gives $u=0,\dfrac{10}{3}$; the second gives $u=\dfrac{5\pm\sqrt7}{3}$, both positive. The zero value gives one $x$, while each of the three positive values gives two. Total $\boxed7$, choice D.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath"); size(230);
real f(real x){return abs(3*x*x-10*abs(x)+3);} path g;for(int k=0;k<=400;++k){real x=-3.7+7.4*k/400;g=g--(x,f(x));}draw(g,blue);draw((-3.8,3)--(3.8,3),red);draw((-3.8,0)--(3.8,0),Arrow);draw((0,-0.3)--(0,8),Arrow);label("$y=3$",(3.8,3),E);label("$x$",(3.8,0),E);label("$y$",(0,8),N);
\end{asy}
