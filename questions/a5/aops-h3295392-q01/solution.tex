Write $AP=t$, so $PD=20-t$. Triangles $APB$ and $CPD$ are right triangles, as is $BPC$ by hypothesis. For right-triangle legs $u,v$ and hypotenuse $w$, the inradius is $\dfrac{(u+v-w)}{2}$. Consequently
\[
r_1=\dfrac{8+t-BP}{2},\quad
r_2=\dfrac{BP+CP-20}{2},\quad
r_3=\dfrac{8+20-t-CP}{2}.
\]
The unknown lengths cancel when added, leaving $\boxed{r_1+r_2+r_3=8}$.
