Divisors pair up as $d\leftrightarrow\dfrac nd$ with one of each pair at most $\sqrt n$, so $\tau(n)\le2\sqrt n$. For $n\ge8$, $n-2\sqrt n\ge\dfrac34n$ (since $\sqrt n\ge\dfrac83$), so $\dfrac n{n-\tau(n)}\le\dfrac43<4$. For $n=3,\dots,7$ the values are $3,4,\dfrac53,3,\dfrac75$. The maximum is $\boxed{4}$, at $n=4$.
