Let $a,b>0$ with $a\ne1$, and let $n$ be a real number. Prove that
\[\log_a\left(b^n\right)=n\log_ab .\]
A textbook proof begins: ``Let $w=\log_a b^n$ and $x=\log_ab$. We want to show that $w=nx$.'' Explain why showing $w=nx$ proves the statement, and complete the proof.
