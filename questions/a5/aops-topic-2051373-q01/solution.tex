Showing $w=nx$ is enough because $w$ is, by definition, the left side $\log_a\left(b^n\right)$, while $nx=n\log_ab$ is the right side.

\textbf{Proof.} Since $x=\log_ab$, we have $a^x=b$. Raising to the power $n$ and using $(a^x)^n=a^{xn}$,
\[b^n=\left(a^x\right)^n=a^{nx}.\]
By the definition of the logarithm, $a^{nx}=b^n$ means $\log_a\left(b^n\right)=nx$. Hence $w=nx$, that is, $\log_a\left(b^n\right)=n\log_ab$. $\blacksquare$

(The key step is the exponent law $(a^x)^n=a^{nx}$; restating what has to be shown, as the poster did, is not yet a proof.)
