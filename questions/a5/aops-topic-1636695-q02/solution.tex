Each year the population is multiplied by $1-0.001=0.999$. From $2015$ to $2021$ there are $6$ years, so the population will be
\[
127{,}000{,}000\times0.999^6\approx127{,}000{,}000\times0.994015\approx\boxed{126{,}239{,}902}.
\]
