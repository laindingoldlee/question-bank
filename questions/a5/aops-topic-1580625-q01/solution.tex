For $x\ge10$, $x!$ contains the factors $2$, $5$ and $10$, so it is divisible by $100$ and does not affect the last two digits. Hence only $0!$ to $9!$ matter:
\[
1+1+2+6+24+120+720+5040+40320+362880=409114 .
\]
So the last two digits are $\boxed{14}$.
