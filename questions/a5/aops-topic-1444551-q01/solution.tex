Count the digits $1$ in the page numbers.
\begin{itemize}
\item From $1$ to $999$ (as three-digit strings $000$ to $999$): each of the three positions shows a $1$ in $100$ of the $1000$ strings, so $300$ ones.
\item From $1000$ to $1999$: $1000$ ones in the thousands place, plus $300$ in the last three places. Total so far $1600$.
\item From $2000$ to $2999$: $300$. Total $1900$.
\item From $3000$ to $3099$: $10$ in the tens place ($3010$--$3019$) and $10$ in the units place: $20$. Total $1920$.
\item From $3100$ onwards every page has a $1$ in the hundreds place. From $3100$ to $3169$: $70$ (hundreds) $+\,10$ (tens, $3110$--$3119$) $+\,7$ (units: $3101,3111,\dots,3161$) $=87$. Total $2007$.
\item Page $3170$ adds $1$ (total $2008$), and page $3171$ adds $2$ (total $2010$).
\end{itemize}
The next page, $3172$, would bring the total to $2011$. So the encyclopedia has $\boxed{3171}$ pages.
