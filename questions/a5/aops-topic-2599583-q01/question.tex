Three cards are numbered $1$, $2$, $3$ and shuffled. John draws a card, then his opponent draws one of the two remaining cards; John wins the game if his card is higher. If John wins the first game he receives $\$10{,}000$; if he loses he receives nothing. After each win he may stop and keep his money, or play another game (with a reshuffled deck) as ``double or nothing'': a win doubles his money, a loss ends everything with nothing. Show that John wins each game with probability $\dfrac12$, and find his expected winnings when he decides to stop after his $k$-th win (for any $k\ge1$). What does this say about the expected value of the game?
