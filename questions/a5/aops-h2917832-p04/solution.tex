Assign each pencil the ordered pair consisting of its first drawer and second drawer. There are $jk$ possible pairs. With $jk+1$ pencils, the pigeonhole principle gives two with the same pair, as required.

With only $jk$ pencils, give each a different ordered pair and distribute according to these coordinates. No two then share a drawer on both occasions. Therefore the sharp minimum is $\boxed{jk+1}$.
