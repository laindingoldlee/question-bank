The Purple Plant Garden Store sells grass seed in ten-pound bags and fifteen-pound bags. Yesterday half of the grass seed they had (by weight) was in ten-pound bags. This morning the store received a shipment of $27$ more ten-pound bags, and now they have twice as many ten-pound bags as fifteen-pound bags. Find the total weight in pounds of grass seed the store now has.
