Each intersection is determined by at least one pair of lines, so $M\le\binom42=6$. Four lines with no parallel pair and no three concurrent attain six. Four concurrent distinct lines have exactly one intersection, the least positive count. Thus $\boxed{M-m=6-1=5}$.
