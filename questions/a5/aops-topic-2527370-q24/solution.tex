$2$ sops $=6$ lops, so $1$ sop $=3$ lops and $9$ lops $=3$ sops $=1$ top. Since $3$ tops $=4$ pops, $1$ top $=\dfrac43$ pops. So $9$ lops equal $\boxed{1\dfrac13}$ pops.
