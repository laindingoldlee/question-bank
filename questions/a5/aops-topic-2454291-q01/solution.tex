The quadruple $(8,9,12,17)$ works because $ 64+81+144=289 $. For every positive integer $ t $, multiplying all entries by $ t $ preserves the equation, giving $\boxed{(a,b,c,d)=(8t,9t,12t,17t)}$. Distinct $ t $ give infinitely many solutions.
