With vertex $(3,4)$ the function is $y=a(x-3)^2+4$ (this rules out (C) and (D), whose vertices are at $x=-3$). At $x=0$: $9a+4=22$, so $a=2$. The answer is \textbf{(B)} $y=2(x-3)^2+4$.
