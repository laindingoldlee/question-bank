Let $\phi=\dfrac{1+\sqrt5}{2}$ be the golden ratio, and let $F_0=0$, $F_1=1$, $F_{k+1}=F_k+F_{k-1}$ be the Fibonacci numbers. Find a formula for $\phi^n$ ($n$ a positive integer) in the form $a+b\phi$ with integers $a,b$, and prove it.
