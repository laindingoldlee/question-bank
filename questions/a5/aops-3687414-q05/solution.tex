Translate the vertex to the origin, obtaining $Y=X^2$. The squared distance of $(t,t^2)$ from the origin is $t^2+t^4$, strictly increasing in $|t|$. Equal triangle sides from the vertex therefore force the other vertices to be $(t,t^2)$ and $(-t,t^2)$, $t>0$. The base is $2t$ and altitude $t^2$, so equilateral geometry gives $t^2=\sqrt3t$. Hence $t=\sqrt3$, side $2\sqrt3$, perimeter $\boxed{6\sqrt3}$.

\end{asy}
