Write $n=2^a m$ with $m$ odd. Then $\tau(n)+\tau(2n)=(a+1)\tau(m)+(a+2)\tau(m)=(2a+3)\tau(m)=18$. Since $2a+3$ is odd and at least $3$, it is $3$ or $9$.

$2a+3=9$: $a=3$, $\tau(m)=2$, so $n=8p$ with $p$ an odd prime, $8p\le100$: $p\in\{3,5,7,11\}$, $4$ values.

$2a+3=3$: $a=0$, $\tau(m)=6$, $m$ odd, $m\le100$: $m=p^5$ (none) or $p^2q$: $9\cdot5,9\cdot7,9\cdot11,25\cdot3$, i.e. $45,63,99,75$, $4$ values.

Total $\boxed{8}$.
