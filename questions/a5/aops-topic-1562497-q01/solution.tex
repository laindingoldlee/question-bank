The two conditions say
\[
a+c=2b\qquad\text{and}\qquad ac=b^2 .
\]
Then
\[
(a-c)^2=(a+c)^2-4ac=4b^2-4b^2=0 ,
\]
so $a=c$, and then $2b=a+c=2a$ gives $b=a$. Hence $a=b=c$. $\blacksquare$

Conversely, any constant triple $a=b=c\ne0$ works, with common difference $d=0$ and common ratio $r=1$. So the answer to ``can three numbers be in both an arithmetic and a geometric sequence?'' is: yes, but only constant (nonzero) sequences.
