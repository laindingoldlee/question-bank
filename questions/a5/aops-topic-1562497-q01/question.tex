Three nonzero real numbers $a$, $b$, $c$ form both an arithmetic sequence and a geometric sequence, that is, $c-b=b-a$ and $\dfrac {c}{b}=\dfrac {b}{a}$. Prove that $a=b=c$. (So such triples exist, but only with common difference $0$ and common ratio $1$.)
