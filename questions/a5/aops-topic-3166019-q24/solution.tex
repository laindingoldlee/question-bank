Let $b,c$ be the numbers of three-point and five-point tricks. The total is $12+b+3c$, where $b,c\ge0$ and $b+c\le6$. For $c=0,1,2,3,4,5,6$, the possible totals form intervals $12$–$18$, $15$–$20$, $18$–$22$, $21$–$24$, $24$–$26$, $27$–$28$, and the singleton $30$. Their union is every integer from $12$ through $28$, plus $30$. The sum is $\dfrac{(12+28)17}{2}+30=\boxed{370}$.
