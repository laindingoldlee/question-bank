First count four ones in a binary string of length eight with no run of three. If there are $k$ runs of ones, their lengths are $1$ or $2$ and total four. For $k=2,3,4$, the run-length choices number $1,3,1$. Distributing the four zeros with at least one between runs gives respectively $\binom 52,\binom 53,\binom 54$, so the count is $10+30+5=45$. Subtract strings with both endpoints one. Their zero end-gaps vanish; for $k=2,3,4$ the internal positive zero-gaps give $\binom 30,\binom 31,\binom 32$, respectively. Thus there are $1+9+3=13$ forbidden endpoint strings. The required count is $45-13=\boxed{32}$.
