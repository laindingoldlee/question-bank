Arrange the six non-E letters G,G,S,A,T,R in $\dfrac{6!}{2!}=360$ ways. Insert the three Es into three distinct gaps among seven, giving $360\binom73=12600$ arrangements with no adjacent Es. Among these, to have adjacent Gs, arrange the GG block and four other letters in $5!=120$ ways and insert the Es into three of the six gaps outside the GG block: $120\binom63=2400$. Subtract to get $\boxed{10200}$.
