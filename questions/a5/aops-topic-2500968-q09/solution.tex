Each interior angle of the hexagon is $120^\circ$. In triangle $ABC$, $AB=BC$ and $\angle B=120^\circ$, so $\angle BCA=30^\circ$ and
\[\angle ACD=120^\circ-30^\circ=90^\circ.\]
The lines $AC$ and $DE$ meet outside the hexagon at a point $P$ beyond $C$ on ray $AC$ and beyond $D$ on ray $ED$. In triangle $PCD$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $\angle PCD=180^\circ-\angle ACD=90^\circ$;
\item $\angle CDP=180^\circ-\angle CDE=180^\circ-120^\circ=60^\circ$.
\end{itemize}
Hence $\angle CPD=180^\circ-90^\circ-60^\circ=30^\circ$, and since $C$ lies on segment $AP$, $\angle APD=\angle CPD=\boxed{30^\circ}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair[] V; for(int k=0;k<6;++k) V.push(dir(60*k));
pair A=V[0],B=V[1],C=V[2],D=V[3],Ee=V[4],F=V[5];
pair P=extension(A,C,D,Ee);
draw(A--B--C--D--Ee--F--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
draw(A--P,blue); draw(Ee--P,blue);
real s=0.12;
draw(C+s*unit(D-C)--C+s*unit(D-C)+s*unit(A-C)--C+s*unit(A-C));
draw(C--D,linewidth(0.8));
draw(arc(D,0.25,degrees(C-D),degrees(P-D)),orange); label("$60^\circ$",D+0.45*dir((degrees(C-D)+degrees(P-D))/2),orange+fontsize(8pt));
draw(arc(P,0.4,degrees(D-P),degrees(A-P)),red); label("$30^\circ$",P+0.65*dir((degrees(D-P)+degrees(A-P))/2),red+fontsize(8pt));
dot(P);
label("$A$",A,E);label("$B$",B,NE);label("$C$",C,N);label("$D$",D,dir(200));label("$E$",Ee,SW);label("$F$",F,SE);label("$P$",P,NW);
\end{asy}

(With centre $(0,0)$ and $A=(1,0)$, one finds $P=\left(-2,\sqrt3\right)$, confirming the position of $P$.)
