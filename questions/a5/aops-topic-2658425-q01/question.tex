Find all real solutions of
\[(4x-3)\sqrt[5]{9x+5}=18,\]
where $\sqrt[5]{\ }$ is the real fifth root (defined for every real number). Show that there are exactly two real solutions; find the one that is an integer, and locate the other between two consecutive integers.
