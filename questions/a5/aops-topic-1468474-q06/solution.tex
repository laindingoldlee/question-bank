Only the pattern of $B$'s and $G$'s matters, and all $\dbinom73=35$ patterns are equally likely. There are $6$ pairs of adjacent seats. For a fixed pair, the probability that it holds one boy and one girl is
\[
2\cdot\dfrac{4}{7}\cdot\dfrac{3}{6}=\dfrac{4}{7} .
\]
By linearity of expectation the average number of meeting points is $6\cdot\dfrac{4}{7}=\boxed{\dfrac{24}{7}}$.
