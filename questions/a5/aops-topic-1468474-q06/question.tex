Four boys and three girls will be seated in a row. When a boy is next to a girl, we call this a ``meeting point.'' For example, $BBBBGGG$ has one meeting point and $BGBGBGB$ has six. Given all of the possible seating arrangements for these seven kids, what is the average number of meeting points per seating arrangement? Express your answer as a common fraction.
