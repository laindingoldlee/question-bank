Factor out $44^{133}$:
\[
44^{133}+44^{134}+44^{135}=44^{133}\left(1+44+44^2\right)=44^{133}\cdot1981 .
\]
Now $44=2^2\cdot11$ and $1981=7\cdot283$. The number $283$ is prime: it is not divisible by $2,3,5,7,11,13$, and $17^2=289>283$. So the prime factors are $2,7,11,283$, and the greatest is $\boxed{283}$.
