Let $n>1$ be odd. Finitely many regular $n$-gons (possibly of different sizes) are placed in the plane so that no two of them overlap. A bug starts at one of the polygons and may travel from a polygon to another one only if their perimeters share a line segment. After some time the bug is back at the polygon it started from. Prove that the number $k$ of moves it made is even.
