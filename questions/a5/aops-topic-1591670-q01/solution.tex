Write each mixed number as an improper fraction and pull out the square roots of the numerators and of the perfect-square parts of the denominators:
\[
\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{2}}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2},\qquad
\sqrt{12\dfrac{1}{2}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{25}{2}}=\dfrac{5}{\sqrt2}=\dfrac{5\sqrt2}{2},
\]
\[
\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{8}}=\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt2}=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{4},\qquad
\sqrt{1\dfrac{1}{8}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{9}{8}}=\dfrac{3}{2\sqrt2}=\dfrac{3\sqrt2}{4}.
\]
All four terms are rational multiples of $\sqrt2$, so they add:
\[
\left(\dfrac{2}{4}+\dfrac{10}{4}+\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{3}{4}\right)\sqrt2=\dfrac{16}{4}\sqrt2=\boxed{4\sqrt2}.
\]
