The distance sum is minimized at a median. Pairing points equidistant from the middle gives minimum $m^2$ for $n=2m$ and $m(m+1)$ for $n=2m+1$, namely $\left\lfloor\dfrac{n^2}{4}\right\rfloor$. At $n=66$ this is $1089$, and at $n=67$ it is $33\cdot34=1122$. Since the minimum increases with $n$, the answer is $\boxed{67}$.
