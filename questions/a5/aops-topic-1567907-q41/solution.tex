The points $(2,2)$ and $(2,-2)$ lie on the circle, since $2^2+2^2=8$. The radii to them point along the lines $y=x$ and $y=-x$, which are perpendicular, so the chord subtends a central angle of $90^\circ$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real r=2*sqrt(2);
fill(arc((0,0),r,-45,45)--cycle,lightblue);
draw(circle((0,0),r),linewidth(1));
draw((2,2)--(2,-2),red+linewidth(1));
draw((0,0)--(2,2),dashed);draw((0,0)--(2,-2),dashed);
real q=0.3;pair u=unit((2,2)),v=unit((2,-2));draw(q*u--q*(u+v)--q*v);
draw((-3.3,0)--(3.6,0),gray,Arrow(TeXHead));draw((0,-3.3)--(0,3.4),gray,Arrow(TeXHead));
dot((2,2));dot((2,-2));
label("$(2,2)$",(2,2),NE);label("$(2,-2)$",(2,-2),SE);label("$O$",(0,0),SW);
label("$2\sqrt{2}$",(1,1),NW);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The smaller region (shaded) is a quarter of the disc minus the right triangle with legs $2\sqrt2$:
\[
\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot\pi\left(2\sqrt2\right)^2-\dfrac{1}{2}\left(2\sqrt2\right)^2=2\pi-4 .
\]
The disc has area $8\pi$, so the larger region has area $8\pi-(2\pi-4)=6\pi+4$. Thus $a=6$, $b=4$ and
\[
ab=\boxed{24}.
\]
