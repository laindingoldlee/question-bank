A popsicle is a solid right circular cylinder of height $8$ inches and radius $3$ inches with a solid hemisphere of radius $3$ inches on top. Roehl eats the popsicle at a rate of $4\pi$ cubic inches per minute, while it melts (and drips away) at a rate of $2\pi$ cubic inches per minute. He never eats the melted parts. After how many minutes is there nothing left for him to eat?
