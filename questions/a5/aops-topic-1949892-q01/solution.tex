The volume is the cylinder plus the hemisphere:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real r=3,h=8,k=0.3;
draw(ellipse((0,0),r,r*k));
draw((-r,0)--(-r,h));draw((r,0)--(r,h));
draw(shift((0,h))*scale(r,r*k)*arc((0,0),1,180,360));draw(shift((0,h))*scale(r,r*k)*arc((0,0),1,0,180),dashed);
draw(arc((0,h),r,0,180));
draw((0,0)--(r,0),blue);label("$3$",(r/2,0),S,blue);
draw((r+0.6,0)--(r+0.6,h),Arrows(TeXHead));label("$8$",(r+0.6,h/2),E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

\[
\pi\cdot3^2\cdot8+\dfrac{2}{3}\pi\cdot3^3=72\pi+18\pi=90\pi\text{ cubic inches}.
\]
Eating and melting together remove $4\pi+2\pi=6\pi$ cubic inches per minute, so the popsicle is gone after
\[
\dfrac{90\pi}{6\pi}=\boxed{15}\text{ minutes}.
\]
