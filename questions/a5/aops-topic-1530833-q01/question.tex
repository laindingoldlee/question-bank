Kaleana shows her test score to Quay, Marty and Shana, but the others keep theirs hidden. Quay thinks, ``At least two of us have the same score.'' Marty thinks, ``I didn't get the lowest score.'' Shana thinks, ``I didn't get the highest score.'' Each of them knows only their own score and Kaleana's, and each thought is a sure conclusion from that information. List the scores from lowest to highest for Marty ($M$), Quay ($Q$) and Shana ($S$).
