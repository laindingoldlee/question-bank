The multiples of $225$ are $225k$. We need $1000<225k<5000$, i.e. $4.4\ldots<k<22.2\ldots$, so $k=5,6,\dots,22$ (from $225\cdot5=1125$ to $225\cdot22=4950$). That is $22-5+1=\boxed{18}$ integers. (Neither $1000$ nor $5000$ is a multiple of $225$, so it does not matter whether ``between'' includes them.)
