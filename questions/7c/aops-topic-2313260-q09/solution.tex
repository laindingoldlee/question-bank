The number $2p-104=2(p-52)$ is even, and the only even prime is $2$. So $2p-104=2$, giving $p=53$. Indeed $53$ and $2\cdot53+1=107$ are prime, and the father weighs $2$ pounds. So $p=53$ is the only possibility, and the sum is $\boxed{53}$.
