Parallel lines $\ell_1$ and $\ell_2$ are a distance $d$ apart, and points $A$ and $B$ lie on $\ell_1$ with $AB>d$. How many isosceles triangles have two of their vertices at $A$ and $B$ and the third vertex on $\ell_2$? Show that the answer is $5$, except for one special value of $d$, and find the number in that case.
