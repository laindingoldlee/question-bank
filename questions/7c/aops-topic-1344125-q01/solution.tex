From $x+\dfrac{1}{y}=y+\dfrac{1}{z}$ we get $x-y=\dfrac{1}{z}-\dfrac{1}{y}=\dfrac{y-z}{yz}$. In the same way
\[
y-z=\dfrac{z-x}{zx},\qquad z-x=\dfrac{x-y}{xy}.
\]
Multiplying the three equations:
\[
(x-y)(y-z)(z-x)=\dfrac{(y-z)(z-x)(x-y)}{x^2y^2z^2}.
\]
The numbers are different, so $(x-y)(y-z)(z-x)\ne0$ and we may cancel it: $x^2y^2z^2=\boxed{1}$.

Such numbers exist: $x=1$, $y=-\dfrac{1}{2}$, $z=-2$ give $x+\dfrac{1}{y}=y+\dfrac{1}{z}=z+\dfrac{1}{x}=-1$, and $x^2y^2z^2=1$.
