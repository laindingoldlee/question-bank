You come to a mountain with two paths: one leads to the other side, and the other gets you lost for ever. Two twins stand there; both know which path is which, but one of them always lies and the other always tells the truth, and you do not know which twin is which. You may ask one yes-or-no question to one of the twins. What question lets you find the right path? Justify your answer.
