Write $a=30k+19$ for an integer $k$. Then
\[3a=90k+57=10(9k+5)+7,\]
so $3a\equiv7\pmod{10}$. $\blacksquare$

(This is the precise form of the poster's idea: $30k$ ends in $0$, so for $a\ge0$ the last digit of $a$ is $9$, and the last digit of $3a$ is that of $3\cdot9=27$, namely $7$. Working with $a=30k+19$ also covers negative $a$.)
