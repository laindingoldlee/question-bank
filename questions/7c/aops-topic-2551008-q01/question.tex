Positive quantities $x$, $y$, $z$ are related so that $x$ is determined by $y$ and $z$. Suppose that when $z$ is held fixed, $x$ is directly proportional to $y$, and when $y$ is held fixed, $x$ is inversely proportional to $z$. Prove that $\dfrac{xz}{y}$ is constant, i.e. $x=\dfrac{ky}{z}$ for some constant $k$.
