On the first $\$30{,}000$ the tax is $0.04\cdot10{,}000+0.05\cdot20{,}000=400+1000=\$1400$. The remaining $\$3200-\$1400=\$1800$ is $6\%$ of the income above $\$30{,}000$, which is therefore $\dfrac{1800}{0.06}=\$30{,}000$. The income was $\boxed{\$60{,}000}$.
