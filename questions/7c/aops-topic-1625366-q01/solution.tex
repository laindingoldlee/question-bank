A number is divisible by $3$ exactly when the sum of its digits is. Every rearrangement of $3255$ has the same digits, with sum $3+2+5+5=15$, which is a multiple of $3$. So every rearrangement is a multiple of $3$, and the answer is $\boxed{\text{no}}$.
