The total of the five numbers is $46$. Since $46-e$ is divisible by $4$, $e$ is $10$ or $14$. If $e=10$, the other four sum to $36$, and divisibility of $a+b+c$ by $3$ forces $d$ divisible by $3$, impossible. Thus $e=14$. The other four sum to $32$, so $d\equiv2\pmod3$, forcing $d=11$. The remaining numbers are $4,7,10$; for $a+b$ to be even, it must be $4+10$, leaving $c=7$. Hence $c+2d+3e=7+22+42=\boxed{71}$.
