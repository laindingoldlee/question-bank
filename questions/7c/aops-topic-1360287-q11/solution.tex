Downstream her speed is $r+c=4$ mph and upstream $r-c=\dfrac{4}{2}=2$ mph, where $r$ is her rowing speed in still water and $c$ the current. Subtracting, $2c=2$, so the current is $\boxed{1}$ mile per hour (and $r=3$).
