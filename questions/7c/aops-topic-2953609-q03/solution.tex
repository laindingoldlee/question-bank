Measure time in minutes after 6:00. His arrival $X$ is uniform on $[-15,30]$ and my time $Y$ is uniform on $[0,60]$. He sees me iff $X\le Y\le X+5$. For fixed $Y$, the valid $X$ form an interval of length $5$ if $0\le Y\le30$, length $35-Y$ if $30\le Y\le35$, and $0$ afterwards. So
\[P(\text{seen})=\dfrac{30\cdot5+\dfrac12\cdot5\cdot5}{45\cdot60}=\dfrac{162.5}{2700}=\dfrac{13}{216}.\]
Then $P(\text{die})=\dfrac{13}{216}\cdot\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac9{10}=\dfrac{13}{480}$ and $P(\text{survive})=\boxed{\dfrac{467}{480}}$.
