A death eater arrives on Cherrison Street at a uniformly random time between 5:45 PM and 6:30 PM and stays exactly $5$ minutes. I pass through the street at a single, uniformly random instant between 6:00 PM and 7:00 PM, independently. If he sees me, he devours my soul with probability $50\%$, and if my soul is devoured I die with probability $90\%$. What is the probability that I survive?
