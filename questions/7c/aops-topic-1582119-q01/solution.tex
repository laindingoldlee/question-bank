An equilateral triangle of side $s$ has area $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}s^2$, so
\[
\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}s^2=16\sqrt3\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad s^2=64,\quad s=8 .
\]
Let $O$ be the centre and $M$ the midpoint of side $BC$. In an equilateral triangle the centre lies on the altitude $AM$, one third of the way up from the side, because $O$ is also the centroid. The altitude is $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\cdot8=4\sqrt3$, so the apothem is
\[
OM=\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot4\sqrt3=\dfrac{4\sqrt3}{3}.
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0),C=(8,0),A=(4,4*sqrt(3)),O=(4,4/sqrt(3)),M=(4,0);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(O--M,red+linewidth(1));
real s=0.35; draw((4,s)--(4+s,s)--(4+s,0));
dot(O);
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$M$",M,S);label("$O$",O,W);
label("$4$",(2,0),S);label("$4$",(6,0),S);
label("$\dfrac{4\sqrt{3}}{3}$",(4,2/sqrt(3)),E,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Check with the area: the three triangles $OBC$, $OCA$, $OAB$ each have base $8$ and height $OM$, so the area is $3\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot8\cdot OM=12\,OM=12\cdot\dfrac{4\sqrt3}{3}=16\sqrt3$, as required.

The apothem is $\boxed{\dfrac{4\sqrt3}{3}}$ units.
