Let $O=1\cdot3\cdots1985$ and $E=2\cdot4\cdots1986=\displaystyle\prod_{k=1}^{993}2k$. For each $k$,
\[2k=1987-(1987-2k)\equiv-(1987-2k)\pmod{1987},\]
and as $k$ runs from $1$ to $993$, the number $1987-2k$ runs through $1985,1983,\dots,3,1$, i.e. exactly through the factors of $O$. Multiplying the $993$ congruences,
\[E\equiv(-1)^{993}\,O=-O\pmod{1987},\]
so $O+E\equiv0\pmod{1987}$. $\blacksquare$

(The argument does not use that $1987$ is prime: the same works for any odd modulus $m=2n+1$ with the products of the odd numbers up to $2n-1$ and the even numbers up to $2n$, as long as $n$ is odd.)
