Choose the three seats used by $A,B,Z$ in $\binom{11}{3}$ ways. The middle chosen seat must hold $Z$, and $A,B$ occupy the other two in two orders. Arrange the other eight people in $8!$ ways. The count is
\[2\binom{11}{3}8!=330\cdot8!=\boxed{13305600}.\]
Equivalently, two of the six relative orders of $A,B,Z$ have $Z$ in the middle, so the count is $\dfrac{11!}{3}$.
