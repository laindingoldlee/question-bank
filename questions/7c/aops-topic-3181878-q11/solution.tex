The second remainder implies $A>2020$. If $A<B$, division of $A$ by $B$ would leave remainder $A=1$, a contradiction. Thus $A>B$, making the second remainder equal to $B$, so $B=2020$. Now $A=2020k+1<10000$ with $k=1,2,3,4$. The candidates 2021, 4041, 6061, 8081 have digit sums 5, 9, 13, 17. Hence $A=8081$ and $\boxed{A+B=10101}$.
