Bob can build a toy car in $7$ days, and Jill can build the same toy car in $4$ days. Bob works alone on the first day, and from the second day on Jill works with him (each at their own constant rate). How long does it take to build the toy car? Give the exact time, and also round it up to the next whole number of days.
