The function is $(x-1)^2$, so its minimum is the square of the distance from $1$ to the interval. The interval must be entirely to one side of $1$ and its nearer endpoint must be distance $2$ away. On the right, $a=3$. On the left, $a+1=-1$, so $a=-2$. Both intervals have minimum $4$. Thus $\boxed{a=-2\text{ or }3}$.
