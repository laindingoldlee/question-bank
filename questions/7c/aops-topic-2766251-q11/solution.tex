The factor with odd denominator $k$ is $\sqrt{\dfrac{k+2}{k}}$. The product telescopes to $\sqrt{\dfrac{5}{3}\cdot\dfrac{7}{5}\cdots\dfrac{75}{73}}=\sqrt{\dfrac{75}{3}}=\boxed5$.
