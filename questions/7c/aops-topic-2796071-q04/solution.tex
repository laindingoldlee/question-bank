Let $n^2+689=m^2$ with $m>n>0$. Then $(m-n)(m+n)=689=13\cdot53$, and both factors are positive with $m-n<m+n$ and the same parity (both odd here).

$m-n=1$, $m+n=689$ gives $n=344$; $m-n=13$, $m+n=53$ gives $n=20$.

So $n=\boxed{20\text{ or }344}$.
