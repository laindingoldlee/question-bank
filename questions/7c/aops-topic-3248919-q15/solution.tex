The homogeneous system in $x,y$ has a nonzero solution only if its determinant is zero:
\[20^2-(24-z)^2=0.\]
Thus $z=4$ or $44$. Both occur with nonzero variables: use $x=-y\ne0$ for $z=4$, or $x=y\ne0$ for $z=44$. The sum is $\boxed{48}$.
