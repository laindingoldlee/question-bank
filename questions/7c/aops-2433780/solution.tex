Set $a=x-1,b=y-9,c=z-17$. Then $a+b+c=9$ and $0\le a,b,c\le7$. Without upper bounds there are $\binom{11}2=55$ solutions. If $a\ge8$, subtracting $8$ leaves a nonnegative sum of $1$, with $3$ solutions; likewise for $b$ and $c$. Two entries cannot both exceed $7$ because their sum would exceed $9$. Therefore there are $55-3\cdot3=\boxed{46}$ solutions.
