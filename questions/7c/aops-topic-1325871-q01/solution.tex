Line up $n$ people. Count pairs by the person further left: the first person pairs with $n-1$ people to the right, the second with $n-2$, and so on, down to $1$ pair for the second-to-last person. Hence
\[
\dbinom n2=(n-1)+(n-2)+\dots+1=T_{n-1}.
\]
Algebraically, $\dbinom n2=\dfrac{n(n-1)}{2}=T_{n-1}$. $\blacksquare$
