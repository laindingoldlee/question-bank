A student solves $x^2+16x+4=0$ as follows:
\[
x^2+16x+64=60,\quad (x+8)^2-\left(\sqrt{60}\right)^2=0,\quad \left(x+8+\sqrt{60}\right)\left(x+8-\sqrt{60}\right)=0,
\]
so $x=-8\pm2\sqrt{15}$. The student believes this method works whenever $b>c$ for $x^2+bx+c=0$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that the method gives the real roots of $x^2+bx+c=0$ exactly when $b^2\ge4c$, and that it reproduces the quadratic formula.
\item[(b)] Show by examples that the condition $b>c$ is neither necessary nor sufficient.
\end{enumerate}
