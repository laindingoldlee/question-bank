Let $s(n)$ denote the sum of the digits of $n$. Estimate the smallest positive integer $N$ such that among the list \[s(1^2), s(2^2), ..., s(N^2),\]there are 2025 more odd numbers than even numbers. An estimate of $E$ will earn a score of the closest integer to $10 ( min ( \dfrac{N}{E}, \dfrac{E}{N}))^2$.
