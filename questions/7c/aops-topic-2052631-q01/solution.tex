Put the cube in coordinates with $A=(0,0,0)$, $B=(1,0,0)$, $C=(1,1,0)$, $D=(0,1,0)$, and $F=(1,0,1)$ directly above $B$. The center of face $ABCD$ is $X=\left(\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{1}{2},0\right)$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0), B=(3,0), C=(4.2,1.2), D=(1.2,1.2), up=(0,3);
pair EE=A+up, F=B+up, G=C+up, H=D+up;
pair X=(A+C)/2;
draw(A--B--C);draw(C--D--A,dashed);
draw(EE--F--G--H--cycle);
draw(A--EE);draw(B--F);draw(C--G);draw(D--H,dashed);
draw(A--C,gray+dashed);draw(B--D,gray+dashed);
draw(F--X,red+linewidth(0.9));
draw(B--X,blue+dashed);
dot(X);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,E);label("$D$",D,NW);
label("$E$",EE,W);label("$F$",F,SE);label("$G$",G,NE);label("$H$",H,NW);
label("$X$",X,S);
label("$1$",(B+F)/2,E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Segment $BX$ lies in the bottom face and $BF$ is perpendicular to that face, so triangle $FBX$ has a right angle at $B$. Here $BX$ is half the diagonal of a unit square, $BX=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$, and $BF=1$. By the Pythagorean theorem,
\[FX^2=BX^2+BF^2=\dfrac{1}{2}+1=\dfrac{3}{2},\qquad FX=\sqrt{\dfrac{3}{2}}=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt6}{2}}.\]
(This equals $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{\sqrt2}$, so that form of the answer is also correct.)
