Each side of one square meets the boundary of the other convex square in at most two points, so there are at most eight isolated intersections. A centred square and a congruent square rotated $45^\circ$ attain eight. Thus the maximum finite number is $\boxed8$. If overlapping sides count all their common points, infinitely many are possible; the usual question counts isolated crossings.
