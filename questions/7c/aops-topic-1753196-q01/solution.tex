The product is nonzero exactly when no digit is $0$: then each of the five digits is one of $1,\dots,9$, giving $9^5=59049$ numbers. Among these the product is odd exactly when all digits are odd ($1,3,5,7,9$): $5^5=3125$ numbers. By complementary counting the answer is $59049-3125=\boxed{55924}$.
