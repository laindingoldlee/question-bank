Use $A=(0,0),D=(6,0),F=(6,6),C=(0,6)$. For $B=(x,y)$, the right angle gives $x^2+y^2=6y$, while $BC=2$ gives $x^2+(y-6)^2=4$. Subtracting yields $y=\dfrac{16}{3}$ and $x=\dfrac{4\sqrt2}{3}$, choosing the interior point.
The congruent exterior triangle gives $E=(6+\dfrac{4\sqrt2}{3},\dfrac{16}{3})$. Thus $AD$ and $BE$ are parallel horizontal segments of length $6$, separated by height $\dfrac{16}{3}$. The area is
\[
\boxed{6\cdot\dfrac{16}{3}=32}.
\]

\end{asy}
