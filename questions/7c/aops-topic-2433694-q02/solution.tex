There are $34-5+1=30$ numbers. The palindromes among them are the one-digit numbers $5,6,7,8,9$ and the two-digit numbers $11,22,33$; the odd ones are $5,7,9,11,33$. The probability is $\dfrac{5}{30}=\boxed{\dfrac16}$.
