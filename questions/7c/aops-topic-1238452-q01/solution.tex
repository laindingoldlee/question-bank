Write $0$ to $1999$ as four-digit strings $0000$--$1999$. In the last three places each digit $0$--$9$ appears $200$ times per place, contributing $3\cdot200\cdot45=27000$; the thousands digit is $1$ for $1000$ numbers: $1000$. So $0$--$1999$ give $28000$. The numbers $2000$--$2016$ have digit sums $2,3,\dots,11$ for $2000$--$2009$ (total $65$) and $3,4,\dots,9$ for $2010$--$2016$ (total $42$). The total is $28000+65+42=\boxed{28107}$.
