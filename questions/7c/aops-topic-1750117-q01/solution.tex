Let $d=\gcd(a,a+n)$. Then $d\mid a$ and $d\mid a+n$, so $d$ divides their difference $(a+n)-a=n$. For $n=1$ this says $d\mid1$, and since $d$ is positive, $\gcd(a,a+1)=1$. $\blacksquare$
