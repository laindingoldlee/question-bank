The sum is $15$, so the mean is $\dfrac{15}{9}=\dfrac{5}{3}$. In increasing order the values are $0,0,1,1,2,2,3,3,3$, making the median $2$. The most frequent value is $3$, so it is the mode. Thus $\dfrac{5}{3}<2<3$, and the answer is $\boxed{\text{C}}$.
