Convert the terminating decimals: $1.385=\dfrac{277}{200}$ and $2.576=\dfrac{322}{125}$. The product is $\dfrac{277\cdot322}{200\cdot125\cdot3}=\boxed{\dfrac{44597}{37500}}$.
