The first circle has centre $O_1=(9,5)$ and radius $2.5$; the second has centre $O_2=(-6,-3)$ and radius $7$. The distance between the centres is
\[
\sqrt{15^2+8^2}=\sqrt{289}=17 .
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair C1=(9,5), C2=(-6,-3);
pair u=unit(C1-C2), v=rotate(90)*u;
draw(circle(C1,2.5),blue); draw(circle(C2,7),blue);
draw(C2--C2+7*u,dashed); draw(C1-2.5*u--C1,dashed);
draw(C2+7*u--C1-2.5*u,red+linewidth(1));
dot(C1);dot(C2);dot(C2+7*u);dot(C1-2.5*u);
label("$O_1$",C1,NE); label("$O_2(-6,-3)$",C2,S);
label("$7$",C2+3.5*u,v); label("$2.5$",C1-1.25*u,-v,fontsize(8pt));
label("$7.5$",C2+(7+3.75)*u,v,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Since $17>2.5+7$, the circles lie outside each other, and the shortest distance between them is measured along the line of centres:
\[
17-7-2.5=\boxed{7.5}.
\]
