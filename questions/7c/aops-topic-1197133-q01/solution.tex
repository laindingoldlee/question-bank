We need the least $t\ge5$ with
\[t\equiv3\pmod{11},\qquad t\equiv5\pmod{13},\qquad t\equiv1\pmod 9.\]
From the first two: $t=3+11k$ with $11k\equiv2\pmod{13}$. Since $11\equiv-2$, this is $-2k\equiv2$, so $k\equiv-1\equiv12\pmod{13}$ and $t\equiv3+132=135\pmod{143}$.

Now $t=135+143m\equiv1\pmod9$: $135\equiv0$ and $143\equiv8\equiv-1\pmod 9$, so $-m\equiv1$, $m\equiv8\pmod9$, and $t=135+143\cdot8=1279$. Since $11$, $13$, $9$ are pairwise coprime this is the unique solution modulo $1287$, and it is at least $5$. The lights first blink together at second $\boxed{1279}$.
