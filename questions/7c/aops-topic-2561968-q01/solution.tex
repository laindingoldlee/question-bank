The area is
\[(x+2)(7-x)=-x^2+5x+14=\dfrac{81}{4}-\left(x-\dfrac52\right)^2,\]
which is largest, $\boxed{\dfrac{81}{4}}$, when $x=\dfrac52$ (a square of side $\dfrac92$). Equivalently, the two sides have constant sum $9$, so their product is at most $\left(\dfrac92\right)^2$ by AM--GM.
