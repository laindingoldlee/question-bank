Extend $BD$ to meet $AC$ at $E$. The exterior angle of triangle $ABE$ gives $\angle BEC=\angle A+15^\circ$, and the exterior angle of triangle $DEC$ gives $\angle BDC=\angle BEC+21^\circ$. So $\angle BDC=\angle A+15^\circ+21^\circ$, and $\angle A=123^\circ-36^\circ=\boxed{87^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0),C=(5,0),A=(2,3),D=(2.3,1);pair E=extension(B,D,A,C);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(B--E,dashed);draw(D--C);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,S);label("$E$",E,NE);
\end{asy}
