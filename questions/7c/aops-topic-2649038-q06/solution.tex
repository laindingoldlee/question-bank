Let the path be $d$ miles long. Jill takes $\dfrac d5+\dfrac d8=\dfrac{13d}{40}$ hours. Jack takes $\dfrac d4=\dfrac{10d}{40}$ hours to go up, so he must come down in $\dfrac{3d}{40}$ hours, at speed $\dfrac{40}{3}\approx13.3$ mph. To the nearest mile per hour, $\boxed{13}$.
