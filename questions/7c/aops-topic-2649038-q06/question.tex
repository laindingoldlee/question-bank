Jack and Jill walk up a hill at $4$ and $5$ miles per hour respectively, starting together. Immediately after reaching the top, each comes back down the same way. Jill comes down at $8$ miles per hour. At what speed, to the nearest mile per hour, must Jack come down so that he reaches the bottom at the same time as Jill?
