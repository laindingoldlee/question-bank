There are $12$ men on an island. Eleven of them have the same weight, but one has a different weight; it is not known whether he is heavier or lighter. A seesaw can compare the total weights of two groups of men, showing which group is heavier or that they balance, and it may be used only three times. Show how to find the man with the different weight and to decide whether he is heavier or lighter than the others.
