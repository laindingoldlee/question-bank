For a positive integer $m$, let $\varphi(m)$ be the number of integers $k$ with $1\le k\le m$ and $\gcd(k,m)=1$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove Euler's theorem: if $a$ is an integer with $\gcd(a,m)=1$, then $a^{\varphi(m)}\equiv1\pmod m$.
\item[(b)] Show by an example that the condition $\gcd(a,m)=1$ cannot be dropped.
\end{enumerate}
