Bezout's identity says the condition is exactly $\gcd(x,y)=1$. The integer displacements from $(2,-3)$ are $(\pm5,0),(0,\pm5),(\pm3,\pm4),(\pm4,\pm3)$. Testing the resulting twelve points leaves $(-1,-7),(-1,1),(5,-7),(5,1),(7,-3)$. Hence the answer is $\boxed5$.

\end{asy}
