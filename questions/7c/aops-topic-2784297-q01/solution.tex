Any common divisor of $a$ and $a+1$ divides $(a+1)-a=1$, so $\gcd(a,a+1)=1$.

Suppose a prime $p$ divides both $a^{n+1}$ and $(a+1)^n$. A prime dividing a power divides the base, so $p\mid a$ and $p\mid a+1$, hence $p\mid1$, which is impossible. So $a^{n+1}$ and $(a+1)^n$ have no common prime factor, and $\gcd\bigl(a^{n+1},(a+1)^n\bigr)=1=\gcd(a,a+1)$. $\blacksquare$
