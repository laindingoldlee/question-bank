Use a sum of cubes: $81^3+64^3=(81+64)(81^2-81\cdot64+64^2)=145\cdot5473$. Factoring gives $145=5\cdot29$ and $5473=13\cdot421$. The number 421 is prime: no prime among $2,3,5,7,11,13,17,19$, all primes below its square root, divides it. Hence the largest prime factor is $\boxed{421}$.
