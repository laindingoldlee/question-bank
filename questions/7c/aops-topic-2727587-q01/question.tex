Functions $f:\mathbb R^+\to\mathbb R^+$ and $g:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ satisfy, for all admissible $a,b$,
\[f\left(g\left(a^4+4b^4\right)\right)=\left(a^2+2b^2+2ab\right)\left(a^2+2b^2-2ab\right)\quad\text{and}\quad f(a)=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\dfrac{a^{k+1}}{(2a)^k}.\]
Find $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{x}g(n)$ for a positive integer $x$.
