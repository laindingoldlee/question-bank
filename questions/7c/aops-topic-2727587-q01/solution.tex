For $a>0$, $f(a)=a\sum_{k\ge1}\left(\dfrac12\right)^k=a$. By the Sophie Germain identity the right side of the first equation is $a^4+4b^4$. So $g(t)=t$ for every $t=a^4+4b^4>0$, which covers all positive reals. Hence
\[\sum_{n=1}^{x}g(n)=1+2+\cdots+x=\boxed{\dfrac{x(x+1)}{2}}.\]
(The post's second condition, a denesting identity, is consistent with this and not needed.)
