Two pairs of vertices of a regular decagon are chosen at random: first a pair of distinct vertices, then a pair of two other distinct vertices, all choices equally likely, so that four different vertices are used. Each pair is joined by a segment. What is the probability that the two segments intersect inside the decagon?
