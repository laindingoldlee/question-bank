We have $2^m=n^2-1=(n-1)(n+1)$, so $n-1$ and $n+1$ are both powers of $2$. They differ by $2$, and the only powers of $2$ differing by $2$ are $2$ and $4$ (if the smaller is at least $4$, the larger is at least $8$; and $1$, $3$ fails). So $n-1=2$, $n=3$, and $2^m=8$, $m=3$. The only solution is $(m,n)=(3,3)$, so the sum is $10\cdot3+3=\boxed{33}$.
