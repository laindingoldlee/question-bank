Each draw is red with probability $\dfrac59$ and green with probability $\dfrac49$, independently. A particular sequence with $7$ reds and $2$ greens (for example RRRRRRRGG) has probability $\left(\dfrac59\right)^7\left(\dfrac49\right)^2$. The positions of the $2$ greens among the $9$ draws can be chosen in $\dbinom92=\dbinom97=36$ ways, and these sequences are disjoint events. So
\[P=\dbinom97\left(\dfrac59\right)^7\left(\dfrac49\right)^2=\dfrac{36\cdot5^7\cdot16}{9^9}=\boxed{\dfrac{5000000}{43046721}}\approx0.116.\]
The student's answer is the probability that the first seven draws are red, ignoring both the two green draws (the factor $\left(\dfrac49\right)^2$) and the $36$ possible orders (the factor $\dbinom97$).
