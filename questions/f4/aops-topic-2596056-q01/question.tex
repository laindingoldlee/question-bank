John has $4$ green balls and $5$ red balls in a bag. He draws one ball at random and puts it back, $9$ times in all. What is the probability that he draws exactly $7$ red and $2$ green balls? A student answers $\left(\dfrac59\right)^7$; explain what is wrong with this.
