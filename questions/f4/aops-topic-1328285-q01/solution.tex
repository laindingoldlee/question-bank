For positive numbers, $\dfrac {a}{b}$ is largest when $a$ is large and $b$ is small, and smallest when $a$ is small and $b$ is large. So
\[
\dfrac{3}{5}<\dfrac {a}{b}<\dfrac{4}{4}=1 .
\]
Every value in between is attained (fix $a$ and vary $b$ continuously). The possible values form the open interval $\boxed{\left(\dfrac{3}{5},\,1\right)}$.
