Use $\cos^2t=\dfrac{1+\cos2t}{2}$. The doubled-angle cosine sum is
\[
\cos34^\circ+\cos(120^\circ-34^\circ)+\cos(120^\circ+34^\circ)
=\cos34^\circ+2\cos120^\circ\cos34^\circ=0.
\]
The three squares sum to $\dfrac{3}{2}$, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{7}{2}}$.
