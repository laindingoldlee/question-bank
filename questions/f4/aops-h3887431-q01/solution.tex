(i) Each position has three choices, giving $\boxed{3^n}$.

(ii) Each position is 1 or 2, giving $\boxed{2^n}$.

(iii) There are $2^n$ strings with no zero. For exactly one zero, choose its position in $n$ ways and fill the others in $2^{n-1}$ ways. The answer is $\boxed{2^n+n2^{n-1}}$.

(iv) Begin with the all-2 string. There is one with neither exceptional symbol, $n$ with one zero only, $n$ with one one only, and $n(n-1)$ with both, choosing distinct ordered positions. The answer is $\boxed{1+2n+n(n-1)=n^2+n+1}$.
