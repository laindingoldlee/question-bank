These are the derangements of $4$ objects. By inclusion--exclusion,
\[4!-\dbinom41 3!+\dbinom42 2!-\dbinom43 1!+\dbinom44 0!=24-24+12-4+1=\boxed{9}.\]
The guess $4!-1=23$ subtracts only the arrangement in which \emph{everyone} is in the forbidden seat, whereas any arrangement with \emph{at least one} person in a forbidden seat must be removed.
