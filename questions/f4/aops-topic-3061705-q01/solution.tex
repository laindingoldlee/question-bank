Here are valid constructions, with each repeated 2 coming from a different card: $59=\dfrac{P(4,2)}{.2}-0!$; $73=\dfrac{(4+0!)!!}{.2}-2$; $75=\dfrac{\left(\dfrac{20}{4}\right)!!}{.2}$; $77=(4!!)!!\cdot.20+.2$; $90=P((4.0)!!+2,2)$; $91=\binom{(4!!-0!)\cdot2}{2}$; $92=\dfrac{\binom{4!}{2}}{2+0!}$; $94=(4!\cdot2-0!)\cdot2$; $99=\dfrac{4}{(.2)^2}-0!$. To verify the less immediate values, $5!!=15$, $4!!=8$, $8!!=384$, $P(10,2)=90$, $\binom{14}{2}=91$, and $\binom{24}{2}=276$. The remaining evaluations then follow by direct arithmetic.
