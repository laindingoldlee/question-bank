Use each digit card $2,0,2,4$ exactly once in each expression to obtain the targets $59,73,75,77,90,91,92,94,99$. Concatenation, decimal points, arithmetic, powers, factorials, double factorials, permutations $P(n,r)$ and combinations $\binom nr$ are allowed. For a nonnegative integer $n$, $n!!$ multiplies the positive integers at most $n$ having its parity, with $0!!=1$.
