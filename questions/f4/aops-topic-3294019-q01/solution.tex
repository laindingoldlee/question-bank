Pithon wins on a turn preceded by a whole number of all-head rounds and then Matt's head. Thus the probability is $\sum_{k=0}^\infty(\dfrac{2}{3})^{3k}\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot\dfrac{1}{3}=\dfrac{\dfrac{2}{9}}{1-\dfrac{8}{27}}=\boxed{\dfrac{6}{19}}$.
