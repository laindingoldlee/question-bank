A room has eight switches, each of which controls a different light. Initially exactly five of the lights are on. Three people enter the room, one after the other. Each person independently flips one switch chosen at random and then leaves. What is the probability that after the third person has left, exactly six of the lights are on? Express your answer as a common fraction.
