Each flip changes the number of lights that are on by $\pm1$: if $k$ lights are on, the next flip turns one off with probability $\dfrac {k}{8}$ and turns one on with probability $\dfrac{8-k}{8}$. To go from $5$ to $6$ in three flips we need two ``on'' flips and one ``off'' flip:
\begin{itemize}
\item on, on, off: $5\to6\to7\to6$ with probability $\dfrac{3}{8}\cdot\dfrac{2}{8}\cdot\dfrac{7}{8}=\dfrac{42}{512}$;
\item on, off, on: $5\to6\to5\to6$ with probability $\dfrac{3}{8}\cdot\dfrac{6}{8}\cdot\dfrac{3}{8}=\dfrac{54}{512}$;
\item off, on, on: $5\to4\to5\to6$ with probability $\dfrac{5}{8}\cdot\dfrac{4}{8}\cdot\dfrac{3}{8}=\dfrac{60}{512}$.
\end{itemize}
The total is $\dfrac{156}{512}=\boxed{\dfrac{39}{128}}$.
