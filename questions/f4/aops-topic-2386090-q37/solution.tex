Add $1$ to both sides and factor:
\[xy+x+y+1=(x+1)(y+1)=124=2^2\cdot31.\]
Both factors are at least $2$, so $(x+1,y+1)$ is one of $(2,62)$, $(4,31)$, $(31,4)$, $(62,2)$. Hence
\[(x,y)=\boxed{(1,61),\ (3,30),\ (30,3),\ (61,1)}.\]
