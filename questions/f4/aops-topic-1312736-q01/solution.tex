$A$ contains the factors $2$ and $5$, so $A$ is a multiple of $10$ and ends in $0$. Every prime in $B$ is odd, so $B$ is odd and $5B$ is an odd multiple of $5$, ending in $5$. Hence $A+5B$ ends in $\boxed{5}$.
