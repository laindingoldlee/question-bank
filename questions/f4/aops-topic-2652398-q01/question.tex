In the figure, $\overline{US}$ and $\overline{UT}$ are line segments each of length $2$, and $m\angle TUS=60^\circ$. Arcs $TR$ and $SR$ are each one-sixth of a circle with radius $2$, and they bend inward, into the region. What is the area of the region shown, bounded by $\overline{UT}$, arc $TR$, arc $RS$ and $\overline{SU}$?

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair U=(0,0),T=2*dir(120),Sx=2*dir(60);pair R=T+Sx;
pair X1=T+R-Sx,X2=Sx+R-T;
draw(U--T,linewidth(0.8));draw(U--Sx,linewidth(0.8));
draw(arc(X1,2,-60,0),linewidth(0.8));draw(arc(X2,2,180,240),linewidth(0.8));
label("$U$",U,S);label("$T$",T,W);label("$S$",Sx,E);label("$R$",R,N);label("$60^\circ$",(0,0.62),fontsize(7pt));
label("$2$",(U+T)/2,SW);label("$2$",(U+Sx)/2,SE);
\end{asy}
