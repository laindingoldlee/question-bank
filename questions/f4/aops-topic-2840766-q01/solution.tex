For $x=1,2,3,4$ the sums are $1,3,9,33$, giving the solutions $(1,1)$ and $(3,3)$.

For $x\ge4$, every term from $5!$ on is divisible by $5$, so the sum is $\equiv33\equiv3\pmod5$. But squares are $0,1,4\pmod5$. So there are no more solutions, and the answer is $\boxed{(x,y)=(1,1),(3,3)}$.
