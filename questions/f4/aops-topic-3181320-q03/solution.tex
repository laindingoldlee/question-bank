Since $b_n$ is arithmetic, subtracting $n-1$ makes $a_n$ arithmetic as well. A sequence both arithmetic and geometric has $a_1+a_3=2a_2$ and $a_2^2=a_1a_3$, so $(a_1-a_2)^2=0$ and it is constant. Here it cannot be the zero sequence because $a_{2019}=2022-2018=4$. Therefore $a_{2022}=a_1=4$, and the difference is $\boxed0$.
