Write the initial number as $2k$. Both the $k$ surviving teams and the $k$ disqualified teams played every pair within their groups, giving $k(k-1)$ matches. If each disqualified team played $j$ surviving teams, $0\le j\le k$, the remaining matches total $kj$. Thus $77=k(k-1+j)$, so $k$ divides 77. The choices are $1,7,11,77$. Values at least 11 give too many internal matches; $k=1$ gives at most one match. For $k=7$, $j=5$ is possible (choose any regular five-neighbour bipartite schedule). Hence the initial count is $\boxed{14}$.
