First count the patterns of men (M) and women (W). The men form blocks between women. A block of $3$ or more men has a man with no woman beside him, and a block of $2$ men at an end of the line has an end man whose only neighbour is a man. So blocks have size $1$ or $2$, and size-$2$ blocks are not at the ends.

The $7$ women create $8$ gaps: $6$ inner gaps and $2$ end gaps, each holding at most one block. With $j$ blocks of size $2$ (in inner gaps) and $5-2j$ single men:
\[j=0:\ \dbinom85=56,\qquad j=1:\ 6\dbinom73=210,\qquad j=2:\ \dbinom62\dbinom61=90.\]
That gives $356$ patterns. Arranging the men and women within a pattern gives $5!\cdot7!$ orders each, so the answer is
\[356\cdot120\cdot5040=\boxed{215{,}308{,}800}.\]
