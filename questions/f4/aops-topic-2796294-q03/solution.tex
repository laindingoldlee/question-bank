The halving step cannot be first. Let the moves before and after it be taken from $\{2,3,4\}$.

\emph{Halving last.} After moves $a,b,c$ the marks are $a$, $a+b$, $9$, and the midpoint of $a+b$ and $9$, i.e. $9-\dfrac c2$. The product $a(a+b)\cdot9\cdot\left(9-\dfrac c2\right)$ is largest with $c=2$ and then $a=4$, $b=3$: $4\cdot7\cdot9\cdot8=2016$. (The other orders give $1512,1620,945,810,630$.)

\emph{Halving third.} The marks are $a$, $a+b$, $a+\dfrac b2$, $a+\dfrac b2+c$. Here $a\le4$, $a+b\le7$, $a+\dfrac b2\le 6$ and $a+\dfrac b2+c=9-\dfrac b2\le8$, so the product is at most $4\cdot7\cdot6\cdot8=1344$.

\emph{Halving second.} The marks are $a$, $\dfrac a2$, $\dfrac a2+b$, $\dfrac a2+b+c$, with product at most $4\cdot2\cdot6\cdot9=432$.

The maximum is $\boxed{2016}$ (moves $4,3,2$, then halve).
