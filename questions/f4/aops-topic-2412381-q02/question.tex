A die is shaped like a regular tetrahedron; when it is rolled, one vertex points straight up. Three of the four vertices are marked with a dot and the fourth is unmarked, and the die is weighted so that the unmarked vertex points up $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of the time. If the die is rolled $3^5$ times, what is the expected number of rolls on which a dotted vertex points up?
