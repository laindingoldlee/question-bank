On each roll a dotted vertex points up with probability $1-\dfrac34=\dfrac14$. By linearity of expectation, over $3^5=243$ rolls the expected number of such rolls is
\[243\cdot\dfrac14=\boxed{\dfrac{243}{4}}=60.75.\]
