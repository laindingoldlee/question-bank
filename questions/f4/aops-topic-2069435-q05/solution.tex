We have $CAT+DOG=100(C+D)+10(A+O)+(T+G)$ with six distinct digits. To maximise, give the hundreds digits the two largest digits, then the tens digits the next two, then the units digits the next two: $\{C,D\}=\{9,8\}$, $\{A,O\}=\{7,6\}$, $\{T,G\}=\{5,4\}$. Any other choice can be improved by swapping a smaller digit in a higher place with a larger digit in a lower place. The maximum is
\[
100\cdot17+10\cdot13+9=\boxed{1839},
\]
for example $975+864$.
