Write $x+y=\dfrac k{35}$, $x-y=\dfrac k{210}$, $xy=\dfrac k{12}$. Then $x=\dfrac12\left(\dfrac k{35}+\dfrac k{210}\right)=\dfrac k{60}$ and $y=\dfrac12\left(\dfrac k{35}-\dfrac k{210}\right)=\dfrac k{84}$. So $xy=\dfrac{k^2}{5040}=\dfrac k{12}$, giving $k=420$ (as $k\ne0$), and $\boxed{x=7,\ y=5}$. Check: $12:2:35=\dfrac1{35}:\dfrac1{210}:\dfrac1{12}$ after multiplying the latter by $420$.
