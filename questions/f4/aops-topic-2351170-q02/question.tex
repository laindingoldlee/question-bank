In an acute triangle $ABC$, the segments $AA_1$, $BB_1$, $CC_1$ are the altitudes ($A_1$ on $BC$, $B_1$ on $CA$, $C_1$ on $AB$). Points $E$ and $F$ lie on $BB_1$ and $CC_1$ respectively, so that $\angle AEC=90^\circ$ and $\angle AFB=90^\circ$. Prove that $AE=AF$.
