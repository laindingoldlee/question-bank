Andy and Beth play a game. Andy thinks of two numbers $a$ and $b$ and tells Beth the ordered pair $(a,b)$. Beth must find a different ordered pair of numbers $(c,d)$ such that $a+b=c+d$, $ab=cd$ and $a\ne d$. Beth wins if she completes this task; otherwise Andy wins. Prove that Andy always wins.
