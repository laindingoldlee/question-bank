Let the large triangle have side $n=49$. There are three possible pairs of grid directions for the sides of a parallelogram; rotation makes their counts and perimeter distributions identical. Fix one pair, described by two unit directions at $60^\circ$.

Let the parallelogram side lengths be positive integers $p,q$, and let its near corner have nonnegative grid coordinates $i,j$. It fits exactly when $i+j+p+q\le n$. Introduce the nonnegative slack $s=n-i-j-p-q$. Then
\[
(p-1)+(q-1)+i+j+s=n-2.
\]
This is a bijection with five-part nonnegative compositions of $n-2$. By symmetry, the average of each part is $\dfrac{(n-2)}{5}$. Thus the average of $p+q$ is $2+\dfrac{2(n-2)}{5}$, and the average perimeter is
\[
2\left(2+\dfrac{2(n-2)}{5}\right)=\dfrac{4n+12}{5}.
\]
The other two orientations have the same average, so at $n=49$ the result is $\dfrac{208}{5}$. Therefore $\boxed{m+n=213}$ for the reduced numerator and denominator requested.
