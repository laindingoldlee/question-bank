Let Korinth be $K$. Korinth was $18$ exactly $K-18$ years ago, when Marin was $18-(K-18)=36-K$. So $K=2(36-K)$ and $K=\boxed{24}$. (Check: $6$ years ago Korinth was $18$ and Marin $12$; $24=2\cdot12$.)
