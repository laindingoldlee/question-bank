A coin lands heads with an unknown probability $p$. Bob knows nothing about $p$, so he models it as a number chosen uniformly at random from the interval $[0,1]$; given $p$, the flips are independent and each lands heads with probability $p$. He flips the coin twice and it lands heads both times. What is the probability that the third flip is also heads?
