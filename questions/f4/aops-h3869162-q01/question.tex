A $6\times6$ grid is filled with the numbers $1,2,\ldots,36$, one number in each square. Read the grid from left to right, top to bottom as a list of $36$ numbers. A move consists of swapping two consecutive numbers in this list. What is the maximum possible value, over all starting arrangements, of the minimum number of moves needed to transform the grid into the following arrangement?

\[
\begin{array}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 5 & 6\\
\hline
7 & 8 & 9 & 10 & 11 & 12\\
\hline
13 & 14 & 15 & 16 & 17 & 18\\
\hline
19 & 20 & 21 & 22 & 23 & 24\\
\hline
25 & 26 & 27 & 28 & 29 & 30\\
\hline
31 & 32 & 33 & 34 & 35 & 36\\
\hline
\end{array}
\]
