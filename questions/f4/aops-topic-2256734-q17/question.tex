The sequence $1,0,2,1,3,2,4,3,5,4,6,5,\ldots$ is formed so that the $n$th term is one less than the term before it if $n$ is even, and two more than the term before it if $n$ is odd. A second sequence $0,2,6,12,20,30,\ldots$ is formed by multiplying consecutive pairs: its $n$th term is the product of the $(2n-1)$th and $2n$th terms of the first sequence. What is the $2020$th term of the second sequence divided by $2020$?

$\textbf{(A) }2018\qquad\textbf{(B) }2019\qquad\textbf{(C) }2020\qquad\textbf{(D) }2021\qquad\textbf{(E) }2022$
