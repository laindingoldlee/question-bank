By induction, the $(2n-1)$th term of the first sequence is $n$ and the $2n$th term is $n-1$: indeed the $(2n)$th term is $n-1$ (one less), and the $(2n+1)$th is $n-1+2=n+1$. So the $n$th term of the second sequence is $n(n-1)$, and the $2020$th term is $2020\cdot2019$. Divided by $2020$ this is $\boxed{\textbf{(B) }2019}$.
