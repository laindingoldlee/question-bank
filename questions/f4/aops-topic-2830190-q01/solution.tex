The step $\sqrt a\sqrt b=\sqrt{ab}$ is valid for $a,b\ge0$ but not for negative numbers, so the second equality is the error. Correctly, $\sqrt{-1}\cdot\sqrt{-1}=i\cdot i=-1$.

$-1$ has two square roots, $i$ and $-i$. The notation $\sqrt{-1}$ denotes the \emph{principal} square root, which by convention is $i$. Square-root identities that hold for nonnegative reals need not hold for principal complex roots.
