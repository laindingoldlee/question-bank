For $n=11$, when $m$ is odd the number is divisible by 5 since $14\equiv-1$ and $11\equiv1\pmod5$. When $m$ is even it is divisible by 3 since $14\equiv-1$ and $11\equiv2\pmod3$. The numbers exceed these divisors, including $m=0$, which gives 12. The smaller positive integers coprime to 14 are $1,3,5,9$; they fail at $m=0,1,1,1$, yielding primes $2,17,19,23$. Thus $\boxed{n=11}$.
