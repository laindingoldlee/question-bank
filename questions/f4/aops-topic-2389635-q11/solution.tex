\textbf{Hemisphere.} Its curved surface is half of a sphere's surface: $\dfrac12\cdot4\pi\cdot5^2=50\pi$ square metres, costing $5\cdot50\pi=250\pi$ dollars.

\textbf{Cone.} The slant height is $\sqrt{5^2+12^2}=13$, so the curved surface is $\pi\cdot5\cdot13=65\pi$ square metres, costing $8\cdot65\pi=520\pi$ dollars.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real r=2.5;
draw(arc((0,0),r,0,180),linewidth(0.9));
draw((-r,0)--(0,-6)--(r,0),linewidth(0.9));
draw(xscale(r)*yscale(0.4*r)*arc((0,0),1,180,360),linewidth(0.6));
draw(xscale(r)*yscale(0.4*r)*arc((0,0),1,0,180),dashed);
draw((0,0)--(r,0),blue); label("$5$",(0.75,0),S,blue);
draw((0,0)--(0,-6),dashed); label("$12$",(0,-3.2),W);
label("$13$",(r/2,-3),E);
label("hemisphere",(0,1.6)); label("cone",(0.9,-1.9));
\end{asy}

The total cost is $250\pi+520\pi=\boxed{770\pi}$ dollars, about $\$2419.03$.
