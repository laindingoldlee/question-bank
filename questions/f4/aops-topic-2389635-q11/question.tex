A carpeting company charges $\$5$ per square metre for normal seaweed carpet and $\$8$ per square metre for premium seaweed carpet. Mr. Herm's shell-house is a hemisphere of radius $5$ metres sitting on the base of a cone of radius $5$ metres and height $12$ metres (there is no wall between the two parts). How much will it cost to carpet the curved surface of the hemisphere with normal seaweed and the curved surface of the cone with premium seaweed?
