The five terms are $a_3+12,\ a_3+6,\ a_3,\ a_3-6,\ a_3-12$, so their sum is $5a_3=k^2$. Hence $5\mid k$; write $k=5m$, so $a_3=5m^2$ and $a_5=5m^2-12$.

The condition $k>a_5$ is $5m>5m^2-12$, i.e. $5m^2-5m-12<0$. This holds for $m=1$ ($-12<0$) and $m=2$ ($-2<0$) but fails for $m=3$ ($18>0$) and every larger $m$. The largest choice is $m=2$, giving $k=10$, $a_3=20$, $a_5=8<10$, and
\[
a_2=a_3+6=\boxed{26}.
\]
