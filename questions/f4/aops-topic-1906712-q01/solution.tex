The average is $\dfrac{61}{162}$. Since $61$ is a bit more than a third of $162$ ($\dfrac{162}{3}=54$) and less than half of it, the average is between $0.33$ and $0.5$; more precisely $\dfrac{61}{162}\approx\dfrac{60}{160}=0.375$, and in fact $\dfrac{61}{162}\approx0.377$. The closest choice is $\boxed{\textbf{(B) }0.38}$.
