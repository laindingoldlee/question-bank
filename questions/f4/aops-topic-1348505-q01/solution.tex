Let the second container hold $s$ gallons at the start, so the first holds $2s$. Afterwards they hold $2s-3$ and $s-2$ gallons, and
\[
2s-3=4.5(s-2)\iff 2s-3=4.5s-9\iff 2.5s=6\iff s=2.4 .
\]
So the containers held $2.4$ and $4.8$ gallons, together $\boxed{7.2}$ gallons. Check: afterwards they hold $1.8$ and $0.4$ gallons, and $1.8=4.5\cdot0.4$.

(The phrase ``the second container had $4.5$ times less milk than the first'' means that the first had $4.5$ times as much as the second, which is how it is written in the equation.)
