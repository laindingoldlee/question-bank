Choose the digits from left to right. The hundreds digit can be any of $1,\dots,9$: $9$ choices. The tens digit can be any digit except the hundreds digit: $9$ choices (now $0$ is allowed). The units digit must differ from both: $8$ choices. The total is $9\cdot9\cdot8=\boxed{648}$.
