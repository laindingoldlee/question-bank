The degree of a monomial is the sum of the exponents of all its variables. Find the degree of each monomial:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $x^3y^3$;
\item[(b)] $-4x^2yz^5$;
\item[(c)] $7$.
\end{enumerate}
