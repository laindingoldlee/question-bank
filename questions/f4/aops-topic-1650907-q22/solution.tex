The numbers $1,2,\dots,250$ are equally spaced, so their mean is the average of the first and last: $\dfrac{1+250}{2}=\boxed{125.5}$. (Indeed $\dfrac{1}{250}\cdot\dfrac{250\cdot251}{2}=125.5$.)
