For $0\le x<1$ let
\[
f(x)=(1+x)\left(1+x^8\right)\left(1+x^{64}\right)\cdots=\prod_{k\ge0}\left(1+x^{8^k}\right).
\]
Compute
\[
\dfrac{1}{f^{-1}\left(\dfrac{5}{3\,f\left(\dfrac{2}{5}\right)f\left(\dfrac{4}{25}\right)}\right)} .
\]
