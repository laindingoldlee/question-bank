A cyclic trapezoid is isosceles, so its diagonals are equal and symmetry gives $AE=BE=3$, $CE=DE=7$ (with the two bases $AB\parallel CD$). Both perpendicular diagonals therefore have length 10. The area is half their product, $\boxed{50}$.
\begin{asy}
import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
pair A=(-3,3),B=(3,3),C=(7,-7),D=(-7,-7),E=(0,0);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C^^B--D,blue);label("$A$",A,(-1,1));label("$B$",B,(1,1));label("$C$",C,(1,-1));label("$D$",D,(-1,-1));label("$E$",E,(1,0));label("$3$",A/2,(-1,1));label("$7$",D/2,(-1,0));
\end{asy}
