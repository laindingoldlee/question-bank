One and a half adults eat $1.5$ pies in $1.5$ days, so they eat $1$ pie per day. Hence one adult eats $\dfrac{1}{1.5}=\dfrac{2}{3}$ of a pie per day.

Twelve adults for $3$ days eat
\[
12\cdot3\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}=\boxed{24}\text{ pies}.
\]
