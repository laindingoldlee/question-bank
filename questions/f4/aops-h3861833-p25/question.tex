Let a partition of sequecne be beautiful if the sequence is partitioned into contiguous blocks that are cyclic rotations of $[1,2,\dots,k]$ where $k$ is a positive integer. For example, $[2,3,1,1,2]$ is beautiful as it can be partitioned into $[2,3,1]$ and $[1,2]$. Find the sum of the number of beautiful partitions over all sequences of length $9$.
