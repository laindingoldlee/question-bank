Count sequence-and-partition pairs. A block of length $k$ has exactly $k$ possible cyclic rotations of $[1,\ldots,k]$. Thus if $A_n$ is the total for length $n$, $A_0=1$ and $A_n=\sum_{k=1}^n kA_{n-k}$ by choosing the first block. This gives $A_1=1,A_2=3$, and subtraction of consecutive recurrences yields $A_n=3A_{n-1}-A_{n-2}$ for $n\ge3$. The values through $n=9$ are $1,3,8,21,55,144,377,987,2584$. Hence $\boxed{2584}$.
