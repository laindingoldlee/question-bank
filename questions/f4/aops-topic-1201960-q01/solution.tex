$\dfrac{1}{34}=\dfrac{1}{2\cdot17}$; the factor $2$ only causes a pre-period of one digit, and the period equals the multiplicative order of $10$ modulo $17$. Since $10^2\equiv15$, $10^4\equiv15^2\equiv4$, $10^8\equiv16\equiv-1$, the order is $16$. Indeed $\dfrac{1}{34}=0.0\overline{2941176470588235}$, so the period is $\boxed{16}$.
