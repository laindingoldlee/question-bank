Cauchy--Schwarz gives $(a+b+c+d)(\dfrac{1}{a}+\dfrac{1}{b}+\dfrac{1}{c}+\dfrac{1}{d})\ge(1+1+1+1)^2=16$. Thus the integer sum is at least $\lceil\dfrac{160}{7}\rceil=23$. Equality in this integer bound is attained by $(a,b,c,d)=(5,6,6,6)$, whose reciprocal sum is $\dfrac{1}{5}+\dfrac{3}{6}=\dfrac{7}{10}$. Therefore the minimum is $\boxed{23}$.
