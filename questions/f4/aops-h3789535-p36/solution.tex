The square has side $8$, so $DE=23-8=15$. The perpendicular distance from $B$ to line $DE$ is $8$. Thus triangle $DBE$ has area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot15\cdot8=60$. Adding the square area gives $\boxed{124}$.
