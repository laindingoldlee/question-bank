The thousands carry forces $ R=1 $. In the units column, $ C+A $ ends in $ C $, forcing $ A=0 $ and no carry. The tens column gives $ I+1=M+10e $ with $ e=0 $ or 1. If $ e=1 $, then $ I=9,M=0 $, contradicting distinctness from $ A $; hence $ e=0 $ and $ M=I+1 $. The hundreds column now gives $ 1+T=10 $, so $ T=9 $. Therefore $ I $ can be 2,3,4,5,6,7, six choices, and $ M $ is determined. For each, $ C $ can be any of the five unused digits. The total is $ 6\cdot5=\boxed{30}$.
