Box seats: $\dfrac{1}{5}\cdot24000=4800$, leaving $24000-4800=19200$ seats. Reserved seats: $\dfrac{5}{8}\cdot19200=12000$. The rest are general admission:
\[
19200-12000=\boxed{7200}.
\]
(Equivalently, general admission is $\dfrac{4}{5}\cdot\dfrac{3}{8}=\dfrac{3}{10}$ of all seats.)
