Let $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be continuous. A continuous path $\gamma(t)=(x(t),y(t))$, $0\le t\le1$, starts at a point below the graph of $f$ ($y(0)<f(x(0))$) and ends at a point above it ($y(1)>f(x(1))$). Prove that the path meets the graph of $f$.
