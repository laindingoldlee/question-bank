There are $9!=362880$ ways to hand out the meals. The ways in which nobody gets the right meal are the derangements of $9$ objects:
\[D_9=9!\sum_{k=0}^{9}\dfrac{(-1)^k}{k!}=133496.\]
So at least one person gets the right meal in $362880-133496=\boxed{229384}$ ways.
