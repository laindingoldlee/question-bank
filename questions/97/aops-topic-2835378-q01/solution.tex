For positive bases, $\left(\dfrac ab\right)^r=\dfrac{a^r}{b^r}$, and since $a^r=b^r>0$ this quotient is $1$. $\blacksquare$

(Positivity is needed: with negative bases and non-integer $r$ the powers may not be real numbers.)
