Two non-vertical lines in the coordinate plane, with slopes $m_1$ and $m_2$, intersect, and $\theta$ is the smaller angle between them ($\theta=90^\circ$ if they are perpendicular).
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that the lines are perpendicular exactly when $m_1m_2=-1$, and that otherwise
\[\tan\theta=\left|\dfrac{m_1-m_2}{1+m_1m_2}\right| .\]
\item[(b)] For perpendicular lines the product of the slopes is always $-1$. If $\theta<90^\circ$, is the product $m_1m_2$ determined by $\theta$? Prove your answer.
\end{enumerate}
