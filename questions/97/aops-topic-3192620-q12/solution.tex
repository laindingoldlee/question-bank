With $f$ failures and $p$ passes, the smallest deficit below 60 from the failures is $1+\cdots+f=f\dfrac{f+1}{2}$, and the largest surplus from the passes is $40+39+\cdots+(41-p)=p\dfrac{81-p}{2}$. Thus $f(f+1)\le p(81-p)$. For $p=1$ this allows at most $f=8$. For $p\ge2$, if $f\ge8p$, then $f(f+1)\ge8p(8p+1)>p(81-p)$ because $65p>73$. Hence the ratio cannot exceed 8. Scores 52 through 59 together with 100 give total mean greater than 60 and ratio 8, so $\boxed8$.
