For each of the first 2024 segments, a length of 0.9 creates one break before the next segment, while length 1.1 creates none. The last segment cannot create a break within the 2025 segments. Thus the chain count is $1+X$, where $X\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(2024,0.3)$. At most 50 chains means $X\le 49$, giving\[\boxed{\sum_{j=0}^{49}\binom{2024}{j}\left(\dfrac{3}{10}\right)^j\left(\dfrac{7}{10}\right)^{2024-j}}.\]
