At each of 2025 equally spaced points on a line, draw a forward segment whose length is independently 0.9 with probability 0.3 and 1.1 with probability 0.7. Adjacent starting points are distance 1 apart. A chain is a maximal group of consecutively overlapping segments. Find the probability that there are at most 50 chains.
