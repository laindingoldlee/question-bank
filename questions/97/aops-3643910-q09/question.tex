$\bullet$ The second card from the top is moved to the top.

$\bullet$  The third card from the top is moved to the top.

$\bullet$  The fifth card from the top is moved to the top.

$\bullet$  The seventh card from the top is moved to the top.

What is the least number of rounds of shuffling that would return the deck to its original order?
