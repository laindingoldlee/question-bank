A positive integer divides $a$ and $\gcd(b,c)$ exactly when it divides all three of $a,b,c$. The same condition characterizes the divisors common to $\gcd(a,b)$ and $c$. Thus both gcd expressions are the greatest positive integer dividing all three numbers and are equal.
