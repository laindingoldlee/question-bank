If the total taken is $k$, choose the takers in $\binom4k$ ways; the gifts must total $13+k$. The gift generating polynomial is $(1+x+x^2+x^3)^8=\dfrac{(1-x^4)^8}{(1-x)^8}$. Thus the number is\[\sum_{k=0}^4\binom4k\sum_{j=0}^{\lfloor\dfrac{13+k}{4}\rfloor}(-1)^j\binom8j\binom{20+k-4j}{7}=\boxed{84372}.\]The inner sum is also inclusion--exclusion for eight nonnegative gift amounts bounded by three: subtract arrangements where a selected amount is at least four.
