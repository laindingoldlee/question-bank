The series lasts exactly six games and the Tigers win when the Tigers win game $6$ and exactly $3$ of the first $5$ games:
\[
P(\text{Tigers in }6)=\dbinom53\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^5\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{10}{64}.
\]
By symmetry, $P(\text{Giants in }6)=\dfrac{10}{64}$ as well, so $P(\text{exactly six games})=\dfrac{20}{64}$ and
\[
P(\text{Tigers win}\mid\text{six games})=\dfrac{\dfrac{10}{64}}{\dfrac{20}{64}}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}.
\]
(Without the condition, the probability that the Tigers win in exactly six games would be $\dfrac{10}{64}=\dfrac{5}{32}$.)
