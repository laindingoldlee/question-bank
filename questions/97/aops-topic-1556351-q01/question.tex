The World Series is a series of baseball games that continues until one team has won four games. The Tigers and the Giants are playing, and each team has probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$ of winning each game, independently. Given that exactly six games are played, what is the probability that the Tigers win the World Series? Express your answer as a common fraction.
