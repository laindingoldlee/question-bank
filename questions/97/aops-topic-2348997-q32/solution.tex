Let the first (smallest) term be $a$ and the difference $d\ge1$. Then $10a+45d=2020$, i.e. $2a+9d=404$. So $d$ is even, $d=2m$, and $a=202-9m$. To make $a$ small, take $m$ as large as possible with $a\ge1$: $m=22$ gives $a=4$ (and $d=44$; check: $40+45\cdot44=2020$). The answer is $\boxed{4}$.
