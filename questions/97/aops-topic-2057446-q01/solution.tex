Put $n=2^{k-1}$: $a_{2^k}=2^{k-1}a_{2^{k-1}}$ for $k\ge1$. Iterating from $a_{2^0}=a_1=1$,
\[
a_{2^{100}}=2^{99}\cdot2^{98}\cdots2^{1}\cdot2^{0}\cdot a_1=2^{0+1+\dots+99}=2^{\dfrac{99\cdot100}{2}}=\boxed{2^{4950}}.
\]
