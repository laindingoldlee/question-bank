The expression is the limit of $a_1=\sqrt6$, $a_{k+1}=\sqrt{6+a_k}$. By induction $0<a_k<3$ (since $a_k<3$ gives $a_{k+1}<\sqrt9=3$) and $a_{k+1}>a_k$ (since $a_{k+1}^2-a_k^2=6+a_k-a_k^2=(3-a_k)(2+a_k)>0$). An increasing bounded sequence converges, say to $L>0$, and then $L=\sqrt{6+L}$, so $L^2-L-6=(L-3)(L+2)=0$. Hence the value is $\boxed{3}$.
