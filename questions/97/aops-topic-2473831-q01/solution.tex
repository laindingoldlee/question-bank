Let the number be $x$. Subtracting $-72$ means adding $72$, so
\[7x+6=\dfrac x3+72.\]
Multiplying by $3$: $21x+18=x+216$, so $20x=198$ and $x=\boxed{9.9}$. (Check: $7\cdot9.9+6=75.3$ and $9.9\div3+72=3.3+72=75.3$.)
