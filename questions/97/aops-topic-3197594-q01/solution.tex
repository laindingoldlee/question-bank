Their difference is both $9(c-b)$ and $\dfrac{2n+1}{3}$, so $2n+1=27(c-b)$. The three-digit condition gives $9\le n\le31$, hence $19\le2n+1\le63$. Its being an odd multiple of $27$ forces $2n+1=27$, so $n=13$. Thus $\overline{abc}=169+9=\boxed{178}$, and the reversed final digits give $187=169+18$, as required.
