If the ratios were equal, cross-multiplication would give $p_1p_4=p_2p_3$. Thus the prime $p_1$ would divide $p_2p_3$, forcing it to divide $p_2$ or $p_3$. A prime dividing another prime equals it, contradicting distinctness.
