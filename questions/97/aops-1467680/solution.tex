Factor the denominators: $x^2-1=(x-1)(x+1)$ and $x^2+2x+1=(x+1)^2$. Take each irreducible factor to the largest exponent present. The monic least common denominator is $\boxed{(x-1)(x+1)^2}$, with $x\ne1,-1$. Multiplying the first fraction by $\dfrac{x+1}{x+1}$ and the second by $\dfrac{x-1}{x-1}$ puts both over it.
