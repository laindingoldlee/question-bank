Put $u=a+c$ and $v=b+d$. Then $(a-1)(c-1)=v-u+1$ and $(b-1)(d-1)=u-v+1$. These nonnegative integers sum to $2$. If both are $1$, all four digits equal $2$, giving one solution. If they are $0,2$, the second pair is $(b,d)=(2,3)$ or $(3,2)$; then $a+c=6$ and one of $a,c$ is $1$, so $(a,c)=(1,5)$ or $(5,1)$, giving four. Reversing the products gives four more. Total: $\boxed9$.
