A word made from the letters of TEXAS uses distinct letters from $\{T,E,X,A,S\}$. For it also to be made from the letters of TENNESSEE, every letter must occur in TENNESSEE, whose letters are T, E, N, S. So the word uses distinct letters from $\{T,E,S\}$, and every such word works (TENNESSEE has at least one T, one E and one S).

The number of words with distinct letters from a $3$-letter set is
\[3+3\cdot2+3\cdot2\cdot1=3+6+6=\boxed{15}\]
(words of length $1$, $2$ and $3$).
