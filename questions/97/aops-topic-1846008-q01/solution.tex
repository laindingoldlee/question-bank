The temperature rises by $34-27=7$ degrees. The expansion is proportional to the length and to the temperature rise:
\[
\Delta L=45\times1.17\times10^{-5}\times7=3.6855\times10^{-3}\text{ m}.
\]
To $2$ significant figures the space needed is $\boxed{3.7\times10^{-3}\text{ m}}$, i.e.\ about $3.7$ mm. (The value $3.69\times10^{-3}$ m is the same result to $3$ significant figures. The value $1.17\times10^{-5}\times7=8.19\times10^{-5}$ is the expansion of one metre of track; it must still be multiplied by the $45$ m length.)
