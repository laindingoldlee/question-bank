An engineer lays a railway track made of alloy steel, $45$ m long at a temperature of $27^\circ$C. For each degree Celsius of temperature rise, the steel expands by $1.17\times10^{-5}$ of its length. How much expansion space is needed so that the track does not bend when the temperature rises to $34^\circ$C? Give the answer in metres, correct to $2$ significant figures.
