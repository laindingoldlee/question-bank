Expanding both sides,
\[(a^2+b^2)(c^2+d^2)-(ac+bd)^2=a^2c^2+a^2d^2+b^2c^2+b^2d^2-a^2c^2-2abcd-b^2d^2=a^2d^2-2abcd+b^2c^2,\]
so
\[(a^2+b^2)(c^2+d^2)-(ac+bd)^2=(ad-bc)^2\ge0 .\]
This proves the inequality. Equality holds exactly when $ad=bc$, that is, when the vectors $(a,b)$ and $(c,d)$ are proportional (one is a scalar multiple of the other). $\blacksquare$
