Multiply by $4$ and complete the squares:
\[
(2x-1)^2+(2y-1)^2=4x^2-4x+1+4y^2-4y+1=8+2=10 .
\]
Here $2x-1$ and $2y-1$ are odd, and the odd squares not exceeding $10$ are $1$ and $9$. So $\{(2x-1)^2,(2y-1)^2\}=\{1,9\}$.

$(2x-1)^2=1$ gives $x\in\{0,1\}$, and $(2x-1)^2=9$ gives $x\in\{-1,2\}$; the same holds for $y$. Hence the solutions are the $\boxed{8}$ pairs
\[
(0,-1),\ (0,2),\ (1,-1),\ (1,2),\ (-1,0),\ (2,0),\ (-1,1),\ (2,1).
\]
