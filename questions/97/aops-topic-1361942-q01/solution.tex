By Vieta's formulas, $a$, $b$, $c$ are exactly the three roots (with multiplicity) of
\[
t^3-2t^2-7t+14=0 .
\]
Factor by grouping: $t^2(t-2)-7(t-2)=(t-2)(t^2-7)$, so the roots are $2$, $\sqrt7$ and $-\sqrt7$. Therefore $(a,b,c)$ is any of the $\boxed{6}$ arrangements of $\boxed{2,\ \sqrt7,\ -\sqrt7}$. Check: $2+\sqrt7-\sqrt7=2$, $2\sqrt7-2\sqrt7-7=-7$, $2\cdot(-7)=-14$.
