Let $D_t(x,y)$ count paths of $t$ steps that reach $(x,y)$ without having visited $(3,3)$. Start with $D_0(0,0)=1$ and pass each count to its four neighbors, except that arrivals at $(3,3)$ are recorded as first hits and removed. There are 20 first hits at time six and 480 first hits at time eight; parity rules out other times up to eight. Thus the probability is $\dfrac{20}{4^6}+\dfrac{480}{4^8}=\dfrac{25}{2048}$ and $\boxed{a+b=2073}$.
