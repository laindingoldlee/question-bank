For $0\le n<3^8$, write eight ternary digits, padding with zeroes. Each pair $u+3v$ becomes one base-nine digit, so its contribution to the digit-sum difference is $(u+3v)-(u+v)=2v$. The four higher-position trits in the pairs must sum to $2$, in $\binom53=10$ ways; the bound of $2$ per trit causes no exclusions. The four remaining trits are arbitrary, giving $3^4=81$ choices. The endpoint $6561=3^8=9^4$ has equal digit sums and does not count. Total $10\cdot81=\boxed{810}$.
