To guarantee 15 correct answers, at most 10 of the 100 words may be unknown, since a quiz could include all unknown words first. Learning 90 words suffices: any set of 25 contains at least $25-10=15$ known words. Learning only 89 leaves 11 unknown words, and a quiz consisting of those 11 plus 14 known words gives only 14 correct answers. Therefore the minimum is $\boxed{90}$.
