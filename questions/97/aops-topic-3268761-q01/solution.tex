Let $u_j$ be Tom's probability of ending with all three when he currently has $j$. A Tom win reduces his tokens, so $u_0=0,u_3=1$, $u_1=\dfrac{1}{3}u_2$, and $u_2=\dfrac{2}{3}u_1+\dfrac{1}{3}$. Solving gives $\boxed{u_1=\dfrac{1}{7}}$. Jane ends with all three with probability $\boxed{\dfrac{6}{7}}$.
