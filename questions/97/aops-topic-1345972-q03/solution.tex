Let $E_k$ be the expected number of flips to go from $k$ to $k+1$ for the first time. To reach $10$ from $0$ the frog must pass $1,2,\dots,9$ in order, so the answer is $E_0+E_1+\cdots+E_9$.

At $0$: each flip moves the frog to $1$ with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and otherwise leaves it at $0$, so $E_0=2$.

At $k\ge1$: after one flip the frog is at $k+1$ (done) with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, or at $k-1$, from where it needs $E_{k-1}$ flips to return to $k$ and then $E_k$ more. So
\[
E_k=1+\dfrac{1}{2}\left(E_{k-1}+E_k\right)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad E_k=E_{k-1}+2 .
\]
Hence $E_k=2k+2$ and the expected total is
\[
\sum_{k=0}^{9}(2k+2)=2\cdot45+20=\boxed{110}.
\]
(In general the answer for target $n$ is $n(n+1)$.)
