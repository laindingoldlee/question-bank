A quadrilateral $ABCD$ has right angles at the two adjacent vertices $A$ and $B$. Prove that
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $ABCD$ has a pair of parallel sides;
\item[(b)] if $ABCD$ is a parallelogram, then it is a rectangle.
\end{enumerate}
