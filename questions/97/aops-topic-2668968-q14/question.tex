Prove that a sphere of radius $r$ has surface area $4\pi r^2$. (You may use the formula $S=\displaystyle\int_a^b2\pi y\sqrt{1+\left(y'\right)^2}\,dx$ for the area of the surface obtained by rotating the graph of $y(x)\ge0$, $a\le x\le b$, about the $x$-axis.)
