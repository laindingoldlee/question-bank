The terms are $t_2=\dfrac{956}{3}$ and $t_4=\dfrac{3824}{27}$. Thus $r^2=\dfrac{t_4}{t_2}=\dfrac{4}{9}$, giving $r=\pm\dfrac{2}{3}$, both with magnitude less than one. For $r=\dfrac{2}{3}$, the first term is $478$ and the sum $\dfrac{478}{1-\dfrac{2}{3}}=1434$. For $r=-\dfrac{2}{3}$, the first term is $-478$ and the sum $\dfrac{-478}{1+\dfrac{2}{3}}=-\dfrac{1434}{5}$. Therefore the possible sums are $\boxed{1434,\ -\dfrac{1434}{5}}$.
