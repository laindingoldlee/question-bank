If $v\ge0$ litres are added, the concentration is $t=\dfrac{20+0.7v}{50+v}$. Solving gives $v=\dfrac{50t-20}{0.7-t}$. At $t=0.5$ this is $\boxed{25}$ litres. A weighted average of 0.4 and 0.7 belongs to $[0.4,0.7)$ for finite $v\ge0$; therefore neither 100 nor 200 percent is attainable. The algebraic negative-volume values at those targets have no physical meaning.
