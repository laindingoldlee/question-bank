The least common denominator of two monomials $k_1a^{p_1}b^{q_1}$ and $k_2a^{p_2}b^{q_2}$ is $\operatorname{lcm}(k_1,k_2)\,a^{\max(p_1,p_2)}b^{\max(q_1,q_2)}$. Write $F$, $G$, $H$ for the three denominators.

\textbf{(a)} The powers of $b$ satisfy $\max(q_F,q_G)=2$, $\max(q_G,q_H)=2$ and $\max(q_F,q_H)=1$. The last condition gives $q_F\le1$ and $q_H\le1$, so the first gives $q_G=2$. \textbf{George's} denominator has the highest power of $b$, namely $b^2$.

\textbf{(b)} The constants satisfy $\operatorname{lcm}(k_F,k_G)=10$, $\operatorname{lcm}(k_G,k_H)=20$, $\operatorname{lcm}(k_F,k_H)=4$. From the last, $k_H\mid4$ and $5\nmid k_F,k_H$. Since $k_G\mid10$, $k_G$ has at most one factor $2$, so $\operatorname{lcm}(k_G,k_H)=20$ forces $4\mid k_H$, so $k_H=4$, and it forces $5\mid k_G$, so $k_G\in\{5,10\}$. Also $k_F$ divides both $10$ and $4$, so $k_F\le2$. Hence \textbf{George's} constant ($5$ or $10$) is the largest.

\textbf{(c)} The common denominator of all three is the least common multiple of the three pairwise ones:
\[
\operatorname{lcm}\left(10ab^2,\ 20a^3b^2,\ 4a^3b\right)=\boxed{20a^3b^2}.
\]
