Fiona, George and Henry each think of a different fraction whose denominator is a monomial $ka^pb^q$ with $k$ a positive integer and $p,q$ nonnegative integers. The simplest (least) common denominator of Fiona's and George's fractions is $10ab^2$. The simplest common denominator of George's and Henry's fractions is $20a^3b^2$. The simplest common denominator of Fiona's and Henry's fractions is $4a^3b$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Whose fraction has the highest power of $b$ in its denominator? What is that power?
\item[(b)] Whose denominator has the largest constant $k$?
\item[(c)] What is the simplest common denominator of all three fractions?
\end{enumerate}
