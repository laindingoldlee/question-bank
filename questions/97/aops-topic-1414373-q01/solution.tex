By the binomial theorem
\[
(4x^3+4)^7=\sum_{k=0}^{7}\dbinom7k4^k4^{7-k}x^{3k}=4^7\sum_{k=0}^{7}\dbinom7kx^{3k},
\]
so there are $8$ terms, in $x^0,x^3,\dots,x^{21}$, all with nonzero coefficients. Their sum is the value at $x=1$, namely $8^7$. The average is
\[
\dfrac{8^7}{8}=8^6=\boxed{262144}.
\]
