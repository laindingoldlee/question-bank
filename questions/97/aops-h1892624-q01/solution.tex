Let the common logarithm of these powers be $L$. Then $\log u=\dfrac{L}{40}$ and $\log w=\dfrac{L}{60}$. Since $v^2=uw$,
\[\log v=\dfrac{\log u+\log w}{2}=\dfrac{L}{48}.\]
Here $L\ne0$, because $L=0$ would force $u=w=1$, contrary to strict inequality. The equality $v^n=u^{40}$ now gives $n\dfrac{L}{48}=L$, hence $\boxed{n=48}$.
