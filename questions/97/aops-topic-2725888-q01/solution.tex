$thanksgiving=thanks\cdot giv\cdot ing$, so $ing=\dfrac{81285120}{720\cdot504}=224$. Then $i,n,g$ are the roots of $u^3-19u^2+116u-224=(u-4)(u-7)(u-8)$, so $\{i,n,g\}=\{4,7,8\}$.

$int=32$ forces $\{i,n\}=\{4,8\}$, $t=1$ and $g=7$. Then $giv=504$ gives $iv=72$; $i=4$ would give $v=18$ and then $than=24$ needs $ha=3$ with $h,a$ different from $t=1$, impossible. So $i=8$, $n=4$, $v=9$.

Now $than=24$ gives $ha=6$; $things=2688$ gives $hs\cdot224=2688$, so $hs=12$; $thanks=720$ gives $ks=30$. With all values distinct and different from $1,4,7,8,9$: $h=2$, $a=3$, $s=6$, $k=5$ ($h=3$ would force $s=4=n$). Hence
\[think=1\cdot2\cdot8\cdot4\cdot5=\boxed{320}.\]
