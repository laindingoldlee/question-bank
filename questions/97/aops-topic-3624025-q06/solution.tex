Let $y=x^2+7x$. Then $y(y+7)+12=(y+3)(y+4)=0$. Thus $x^2+7x+3=0$ or $x^2+7x+4=0$. The quadratic formula gives all four real solutions:\[\boxed{x=\dfrac{-7\pm\sqrt{37}}{2},\quad x=\dfrac{-7\pm\sqrt{33}}{2}}.\]Both discriminants are positive, and every listed value satisfies one factor, hence the original equation.
