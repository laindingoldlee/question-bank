First bring the equation to the form $ax^2+bx+c=0$: $6x^2+12x+12=0$. The sum of the roots is $-\dfrac{12}{6}=-2$ and the product is $\dfrac{12}{6}=2$, so the requested number is $(-2)\cdot2=\boxed{-4}$. (Using $15$ instead of $12$ for the constant term is the trap.)
