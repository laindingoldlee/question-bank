The $k$-digit repunit is $\dfrac{10^k-1}{9}$. Hence the sum is $\dfrac{1}{9}\left(\dfrac{10(10^8-1)}{9}-8\right)=\boxed{12345678}$. Equivalently, column addition gives digits $1,2,\ldots,8$ without carrying.
