$400$ glops $=400\cdot\dfrac{6}{5}=480$ blops, and $1$ blop $=2$ slops, so the cow costs $200$ blops. The change is $480-200=\boxed{280\text{ blops}}$, which is $280\cdot\dfrac{5}{6}=233\dfrac{1}{3}$ glops.
