In each cell, selecting a triangle amounts to selecting a horizontal boundary and a vertical boundary meeting at its right-angle corner; these two choices are independent. For each of the two left--right cell pairs, the left/right edge choices must avoid both triangles using their shared vertical edge, leaving three of four possibilities. These choices give $3^2=9$. Independently, the two bottom--top pairs each have three allowed choices of bottom/top edge, giving another $9$. Thus the number of placements is $9\cdot9=\boxed{81}$.
