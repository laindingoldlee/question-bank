Cards $1,\ldots,6$ each have a front and back. Initially only cards $1$ and $6$ show their backs. A die result $k$ flips all cards numbered at most $k$ when $k$ is odd, and all cards numbered at least $k$ when $k$ is even. Let $f(n)$ count ordered sequences of $2n$ die results that leave all cards front up. Find $f(n)$ for positive integers $n$.
