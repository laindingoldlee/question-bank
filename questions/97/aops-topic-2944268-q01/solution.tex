The quarterly rate is $\dfrac54\%=0.0125$ and there are $3$ quarters. So
\[A=3200(1.0125)^3=3200\cdot1.037970703125=3321.50625\approx\text{Rs }3321.51,\]
and the compound interest is $\boxed{\text{Rs }121.51}$.
