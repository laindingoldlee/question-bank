A sum of money is divided among Allan, Bill and Carol as follows. First Allan receives \$1 plus one-third of the money that is left after that \$1 is taken. Then Bill receives \$6 plus one-third of the money that remains after that \$6 is taken. Carol receives the rest, which is \$40. How much does Bill receive?
