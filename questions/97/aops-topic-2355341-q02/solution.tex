Your friend does half the project in $\dfrac62=3$ days, and you do your half in $\dfrac{10}{2}=5$ days. The project is complete when both halves are done, after $\max(3,5)=5$ days. The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(A)}\ 5}$.
