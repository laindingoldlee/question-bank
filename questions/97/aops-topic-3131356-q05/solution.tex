The case $ a=1 $ is impossible. For each $ a=2,\ldots,9 $, the least eligible $ b $ is $\lceil\dfrac{1000}{a}\rceil $, and the resulting sums are 502,337,254,205,173,150,133,121. The minimum is $\boxed{121}$, attained by $ a=9,b=112 $, whose product is 1008.
