The ball first falls $100$ ft. After that, every bounce height $h$ is travelled twice, up and down. The bounce heights are $75,\ 75\cdot\dfrac{3}{4},\ 75\cdot\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^2,\dots$, a geometric series with sum
\[
\dfrac{75}{1-\dfrac{3}{4}}=300 .
\]
So the total distance is
\[
100+2\cdot300=\boxed{700\text{ ft}}.
\]
(Equivalently, the downward distances are $100,75,\dots$ with sum $\dfrac{100}{1-\dfrac{3}{4}}=400$, and the upward distances are $75,\dots$ with sum $300$; together $700$.)
