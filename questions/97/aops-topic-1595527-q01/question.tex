A rubber ball is dropped from a building $100$ ft tall. Each time it bounces, it rises to three quarters of its previous height: after the first bounce it rises to $75$ ft, after the second to $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of $75$ ft, and so on forever. What is the total distance the ball travels?
