Realise the tetrahedron by $(x,y,z),(x,-y,-z),(-x,y,-z),(-x,-y,z)$. The given opposite edge pairs give $y^2+z^2=\dfrac{25}{4}$, $x^2+z^2=\dfrac{49}{4}$, $x^2+y^2=16$, hence $x^2=11,y^2=5,z^2=\dfrac{5}{4}$. Its circumradius is $R=\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2}=\dfrac{\sqrt{69}}{2}$. Each face has sides 5,7,8 and Heron area $10\sqrt3$, so total area is $40\sqrt3$. Volume is $\dfrac{8xyz}{3}=\dfrac{20\sqrt{11}}{3}$, giving inradius $r=\dfrac{3V}{S}=\dfrac{\sqrt{33}}{6}$. The product is $\dfrac{\sqrt{253}}{4}$, hence $\boxed{a+b+c=258}$.
