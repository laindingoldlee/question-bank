After the first card, the second has $12\cdot3=36$ acceptable choices among $51$. The third has $11\cdot2=22$ among $50$, and the fourth $10$ among $49$. The probability is $\dfrac{36\cdot22\cdot10}{51\cdot50\cdot49}=\boxed{\dfrac{264}{4165}}$.
