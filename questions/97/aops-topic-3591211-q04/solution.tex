Your original choice contains the car with probability $\dfrac{1}{3}$. In that case switching loses. With probability $\dfrac{2}{3}$ your first choice is a goat; the host must then reveal the other goat, and switching wins. Therefore switching wins with probability $\boxed{\dfrac{2}{3}}$, compared with $\dfrac{1}{3}$ for staying, so you should switch.
