Choose finite sets $A,B,C$ with sizes $a,b,c$. First choosing the pair from $A\times B$ and then the last coordinate counts the triples as $(ab)c$. First choosing the first coordinate and then the pair from $B\times C$ counts the same triples as $a(bc)$. Hence the two products are equal.
