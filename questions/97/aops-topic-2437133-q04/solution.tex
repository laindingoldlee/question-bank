Leaving remainder $1$ on division by both $7$ and $8$ means that $N-1$ is divisible by $7$ and by $8$, hence by $\operatorname{lcm}(7,8)=56$. So $N$ is one of $1,57,113,169,225,\dots$. Their remainders on division by $5$ are $1,2,3,4,0$, so the least one divisible by $5$ is $\boxed{225}$. (Check: $225=5\cdot45=7\cdot32+1=8\cdot28+1$.)
