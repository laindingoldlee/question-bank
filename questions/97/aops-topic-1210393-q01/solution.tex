The month numbers $1,\dots,12$ that are cubes are $1$ and $8$ (January and August). Both months have $31$ days, and the cube day numbers are $1$, $8$ and $27$. So there are $2\cdot3=\boxed{6}$ dates: January 1, 8 and 27, and August 1, 8 and 27.
