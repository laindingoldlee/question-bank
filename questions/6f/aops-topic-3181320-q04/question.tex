Andrew, Damian, Hans, and Michael are counting numbers. Andrew starts with $1$, Damian counts the next three numbers, Hans two, then Michael six. After this, the cycle repeats forever, but instead Andrew counts $5$ numbers on all his other turns. The game ends when $10,001$ is said. How many numbers did Michael say?
