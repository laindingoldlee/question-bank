Divisibility by $5$ forces the final digit $5$. For length $L$ and $k$ sevens, digit sum is $5L+2k$. For $L=1,2,3,4$ no allowed sum is divisible by $9$: the possibilities are respectively $5$; $10,12$; $15,17,19$; and $20,22,24,26$. For $L=5$, the first suitable sum is $27$, requiring exactly one $7$. To minimize the number, place it as far right as possible before the final $5$, giving $\boxed{55575}$.
