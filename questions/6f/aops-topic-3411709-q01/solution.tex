If $N=ab$ is composite with $1<a\le b$, then $a\le\sqrt N$ and some prime divisor of $a$ is also a divisor of $N$. Thus a composite has a prime divisor at most its square root. For $97$, only $2,3,5,7$ need testing because $\sqrt{97}<10$. None divides $97$, so $\boxed{97\text{ is prime}}$.
