The grid is formed by $6$ horizontal and $6$ vertical lines. A rectangle is determined by choosing $2$ of the horizontal lines (its top and bottom) and $2$ of the vertical lines (its left and right sides), and every such choice gives a rectangle. Hence the number of rectangles is
\[\dbinom62\dbinom62=15\cdot15=\boxed{225}.\]
