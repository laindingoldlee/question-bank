The hypotenuse is the longest side, $5$, so the legs are $3$ and $4$ (indeed $3^2+4^2=5^2$). The area is $\dfrac12\cdot3\cdot4=\boxed{6}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0), B=(4,0), C=(0,3);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw((0.3,0)--(0.3,0.3)--(0,0.3));
label("$4$",(2,0),S); label("$3$",(0,1.5),W); label("$5$",(2,1.5),NE);
\end{asy}
