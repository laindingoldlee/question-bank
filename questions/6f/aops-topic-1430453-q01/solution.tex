At height $y$ ($0\le y\le4$) the base region extends from $x=-\sqrt{8y}$ to $x=\sqrt{8y}$, so the base of the rectangular cross-section is $2\sqrt{8y}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
real f(real x){return x^2/8;}
real y0=2, x0=sqrt(8*y0);
fill((-x0,y0-0.12)--(x0,y0-0.12)--(x0,y0+0.12)--(-x0,y0+0.12)--cycle,palered);
draw(graph(f,-sqrt(32),sqrt(32)),blue+linewidth(0.9));
draw((-sqrt(32),4)--(sqrt(32),4),blue+linewidth(0.9));
draw((-6.6,0)--(6.6,0),Arrow(TeXHead)); draw((0,-0.4)--(0,5.4),Arrow(TeXHead));
label("$x$",(6.6,0),E); label("$y$",(0,5.4),N);
draw((-x0,y0)--(x0,y0),red+linewidth(0.9));
dot((-x0,y0),red); dot((x0,y0),red);
label("$y=4$",(sqrt(32),4),E,blue); label("$x^2=8y$",(-5,f(-5)),W,blue);
draw((x0,0)--(x0,y0),red+dotted); draw((-x0,0)--(-x0,y0),red+dotted);
label("$\sqrt{8y}$",(x0,0),S,red); label("$-\sqrt{8y}$",(-x0,0),S,red);
label("$2\sqrt{8y}$",(x0/2,y0),N,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Its height is twice that, $4\sqrt{8y}$, so the cross-sectional area is
\[
A(y)=2\sqrt{8y}\cdot4\sqrt{8y}=8\cdot8y=64y .
\]
The volume is
\[
V=\int_0^4 64y\,dy=32y^2\Big|_0^4=\boxed{512}.
\]
