Clearing the nonzero denominators reduces the equation to $ ad^2+cb^2=0 $. Set $ b=1,d=2,a=t,c=-4t $. This makes $ 4t-4t=0 $ for every integer $ t $. Taking $ t=1,2,3,4,5 $ gives $(1,1,-4,2),(2,1,-8,2),(3,1,-12,2),(4,1,-16,2),(5,1,-20,2)$. Indeed each side of the original equation is $-t $.
