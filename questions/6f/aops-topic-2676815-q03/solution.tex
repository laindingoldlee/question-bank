The numbers $1,\dots,9$ split into the pairs $\{1,9\},\{2,8\},\{3,7\},\{4,6\}$ and the single number $5$. She can take at most one number from each pair, i.e. at most $4$ numbers, so the fifth must be $\boxed{5}$.
