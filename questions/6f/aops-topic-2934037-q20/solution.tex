Take $A=(0,0),B=(25,0)$. Subtracting the distance equations gives $C=(9,12)$ and $D=(\dfrac{576}{25},\dfrac{168}{25})$. Ray $AC$ lies on $y=\dfrac{4}{3}x$, and ray $BD$ on $y=\dfrac{24}{7}(25-x)$. Their intersection is $E=(18,24)$, lying on both rays. Thus $EA=30$ and $EB=\sqrt{7^2+24^2}=25$, so $\boxed{55}$.
