You are bitten by a poisonous snake, and the only cure comes from a male frog of a certain species. Each frog of this species is male with probability $60\%$, independently of the others. In front of you is one such frog of unknown sex. Behind you are two such frogs, and from the croak you hear you know that at least one of these two is female (but nothing more). You can go to only one place: the single frog, or the pair (where you survive if at least one of the two is male). What is the probability of surviving in each case, and which should you choose?
