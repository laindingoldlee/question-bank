Each person sends the picture only on the day after receiving it, to two new people, so the number of new receivers doubles each day: $5$ on Monday, $10$ on Tuesday, $20$ on Wednesday, $40$ on Thursday. Including Alexandra, the number of people who have seen the picture is
\[\text{Mon: }1+5=6,\quad\text{Tue: }16,\quad\text{Wed: }36,\quad\text{Thu: }76>75.\]
So the answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(B)}\ \text{Thursday}}$.

(Forgetting to count Alexandra gives $5,15,35,75$, and $75$ is not greater than $75$, which leads to the wrong answer Friday.)
