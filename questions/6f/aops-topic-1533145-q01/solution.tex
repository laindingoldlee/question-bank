Each of the four terms omits exactly one variable: $bcd$ omits $a$, $acd$ omits $b$, $abd$ omits $c$, $abc$ omits $d$. By the multinomial theorem, choosing the terms $bcd,acd,abd,abc$ respectively $k_a,k_b,k_c,k_d$ times ($k_a+k_b+k_c+k_d=10$) gives
\[
\dfrac{10!}{k_a!\,k_b!\,k_c!\,k_d!}\,a^{10-k_a}b^{10-k_b}c^{10-k_c}d^{10-k_d}.
\]
For $a^8b^4c^9d^9$ we need $k_a=2$, $k_b=6$, $k_c=1$, $k_d=1$, which indeed add up to $10$. So
\[
N=\dfrac{10!}{2!\,6!\,1!\,1!}=\dfrac{3628800}{1440}=2520 ,
\]
and the sum of its digits is $2+5+2+0=\boxed{9}$.
