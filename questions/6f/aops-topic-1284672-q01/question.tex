Two jars each contain $90$ balls: $b$ black, $w$ white and $r$ red, the same numbers in both jars, with $b,w,r$ positive integers and $b<w$. One ball is drawn at random from each jar. Let $x$, $y$, $z$ be the probabilities that both balls are black, both white, and both red. If $z=x+y$, find all possibilities for $(b,w,r)$.
