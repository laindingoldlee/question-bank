$x=\left(\dfrac {b}{90}\right)^2$, $y=\left(\dfrac {w}{90}\right)^2$, $z=\left(\dfrac {r}{90}\right)^2$, so $z=x+y$ means $r^2=b^2+w^2$ with $b+w+r=90$. So $(b,w,r)$ is a Pythagorean triple with perimeter $90$.

Every Pythagorean triple is $k(m^2-n^2,2mn,m^2+n^2)$ with perimeter $2km(m+n)=90$, i.e. $km(m+n)=45$ with $m>n\ge1$, $\gcd(m,n)=1$, $m-n$ odd. The possibilities for $m(m+n)$ dividing $45$ with $m<m+n<2m$ are $m(m+n)=5\cdot9=45$ ($m=5,n=4$, $k=1$) and $m(m+n)=3\cdot5=15$ ($m=3,n=2$, $k=3$). They give $(9,40,41)$ and $3\cdot(5,12,13)=(15,36,39)$. So
\[
\boxed{(b,w,r)=(9,40,41)\text{ or }(15,36,39)}.
\]
