$p^2-q^2=(p-q)(p+q)=1488=2^4\cdot3\cdot31$. Both primes are odd (if $q=2$, $p^2=1492$ is not a square), so $p-q$ and $p+q$ are both even. Since $p<50$, $p+q<100$, and $p+q>\sqrt{1488}>38$. The even factor pairs with $p+q$ between $39$ and $99$ are $(p-q,p+q)=(24,62)$, giving $p=43$, $q=19$, both prime. So the primes are $\boxed{43\text{ and }19}$.
