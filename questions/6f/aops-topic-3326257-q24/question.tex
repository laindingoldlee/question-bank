A journey starts at $(0,0)$, touches the line $y=3$, reaches $(8,-5)$, then touches the line $x=6$, and finishes at $(16,-10)$. Find its minimum length and express it as $a+\sqrt b$ with integers $a,b$; then find $a+b$.
