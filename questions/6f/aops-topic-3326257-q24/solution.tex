The fixed intermediate point separates the minimization into two independent pieces. Reflect $(0,0)$ across $y=3$ to $(0,6)$. The first piece has minimum length $\sqrt{8^2+11^2}=\sqrt{185}$, achieved by the straight segment to $(8,-5)$ folded across the line. Reflect $(8,-5)$ across $x=6$ to $(4,-5)$. The second piece has minimum length $\sqrt{12^2+5^2}=13$. Both straight segments cross their reflecting lines, so both bounds are attained. The answer is $13+\sqrt{185}$ and $\boxed{a+b=198}$.
