\emph{Lower bound.} In a weighing each weight is on the object's pan, on the other pan, or not used, so the measured mass is $\sum\varepsilon_iw_i$ with $\varepsilon_i\in\{-1,0,1\}$. There are $3^k$ sign choices; they come in pairs $\pm$ together with the all-zero choice, so at most $\dfrac{3^k-1}{2}$ different positive masses can be measured. For $k=7$ this is $1093<2009$, so $k\ge8$.

\emph{Construction for $k=8$.} Take the weights $1,3,9,\dots,3^7$. Every integer from $1$ to $\dfrac{3^8-1}{2}=3280$ has a balanced-ternary representation $\sum\varepsilon_i3^i$ with digits $\varepsilon_i\in\{-1,0,1\}$ (start from the ordinary base-$3$ representation of $m+3280$, whose digits $d_i\in\{0,1,2\}$ give $\varepsilon_i=d_i-1$). Put the weights with $\varepsilon_i=1$ on the pan opposite the object and those with $\varepsilon_i=-1$ on the object's pan. This covers $1$ to $2009$.

So the least $k$ is $\boxed{8}$.
