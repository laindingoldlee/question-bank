Let the distance be $d$ miles. Monday's trip took $\dfrac{d}{40}$ hours and Tuesday's $\dfrac{d}{45}$ hours, and the difference is $2$ minutes $=\dfrac{1}{30}$ hour:
\[\dfrac {d}{40}-\dfrac {d}{45}=\dfrac{1}{30}\iff\dfrac{45d-40d}{1800}=\dfrac{1}{30}\iff\dfrac{d}{360}=\dfrac{1}{30}\iff d=\boxed{12}.\]
(Check: $12$ miles take $18$ minutes at $40$ mph and $16$ minutes at $45$ mph.)
