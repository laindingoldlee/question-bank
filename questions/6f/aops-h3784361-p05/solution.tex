Set $A=(0,0),B=(50,0),O=(25,0)$. The circle equation is $x^2+y^2=50x$. The chord lengths give $C=(18,24)$ and $D=(\dfrac{98}{25},\dfrac{336}{25})$. Thus line $AD$ is $y=\dfrac{24x}{7}$, and line $OC$ is $(x,y)=(25-7t,24t)$. They meet at $F=(\dfrac{25}{2},\dfrac{300}{7})$. The midpoint of $AO$ is $(\dfrac{25}{2},0)$, so the median line is vertical. Its upper intersection with the circle is $Z=(\dfrac{25}{2},\dfrac{25\sqrt3}{2})$. Hence
\[
BZ=\sqrt{(\dfrac{75}{2})^2+(\dfrac{25\sqrt3}{2})^2}=\boxed{25\sqrt3}.
\]

\end{asy}
