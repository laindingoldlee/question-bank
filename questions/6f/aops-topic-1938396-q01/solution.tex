A digit sum of $9$ makes the number divisible by $9$, hence by $3$, so a Susanian number is one with digit sum $9$ that is divisible by $5$, i.e.\ ends in $0$ or $5$.

Ending in $0$: the other digits sum to $9$: $90$, $180$, $270$, $360$, $450$ ($5$ numbers below $500$).
Ending in $5$: the other digits sum to $4$: $45$, $135$, $225$, $315$, $405$ ($5$ numbers).

So there are $\boxed{10}$ Susanian integers less than $500$.
