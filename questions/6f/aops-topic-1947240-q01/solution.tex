Let $f$ tickets sell at the full price $p$ dollars and $140-f$ at $\dfrac {p}{2}$. Then
\[
fp+(140-f)\dfrac {p}{2}=2001\quad\Longrightarrow\quad p(f+140)=4002 .
\]
Since $0\le f\le140$, the factor $f+140$ is a divisor of $4002=2\cdot3\cdot23\cdot29$ between $140$ and $280$. The divisors of $4002$ near that range are $138=2\cdot3\cdot23$, $174=2\cdot3\cdot29$ and $667=23\cdot29$, so $f+140=174$: $f=34$ and $p=\dfrac{4002}{174}=23$.

The full-price tickets raise $34\cdot23=\boxed{\textbf{(A) }\$782}$. (Check: $106$ half-price tickets at $\$11.50$ raise $\$1219$, and $782+1219=2001$.)
