Michelle and Audrey both start walking at noon. Michelle walks from $P$ to $Q$, back to $P$, and so on back and forth, at a constant speed of $3$ miles per hour. Audrey walks from $Q$ to $P$, back to $Q$, and so on, at a constant speed of $2$ miles per hour. They pass each other for the first time at 1 P.M., $3$ miles from $P$ and $2$ miles from $Q$. At what time will they pass each other for the second time?
