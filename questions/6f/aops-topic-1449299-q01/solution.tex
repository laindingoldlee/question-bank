Let $a,b,c$ be the numbers of $\$1$, $\$5$ and $\$10$ notes, with $a+b+c=5$. There are $\dbinom{7}{2}=21$ such choices. They all give different totals: the total is
\[
a+5b+10c=5+4b+9c\qquad(a=5-b-c),
\]
and if $4b+9c=4b'+9c'$ with $0\le b,b',c,c'\le5$, then $9(c-c')=4(b'-b)$, so $4\mid c-c'$ and $9\mid b'-b$; with $|b'-b|\le5$ this forces $b=b'$ and then $c=c'$. So there are $\boxed{21}$ possible values.
