Maths Boulevard has eight houses. On one side of the street are houses $1,3,5,7$, and directly opposite them are houses $2,4,6,8$ respectively. A postman starts at house $1$, delivers to each of the other houses exactly once, and finally returns to house $1$. The house numbers he visits must alternate odd, even, odd, even, \dots, and he may never go from a house straight to the house directly opposite it. How many different delivery sequences are possible?
