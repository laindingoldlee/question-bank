Hank builds an increasing sequence of positive integers as follows. The first term is $1$ and the second term is $2$. Each subsequent term is the smallest integer greater than the previous term that does \emph{not} form a three-term arithmetic sequence with any two earlier terms. The first five terms are $1,2,4,5,10$. How many of the first $729$ positive integers are terms of Hank's sequence?
