Working from left to right:
\[
9(-8)=-72,\qquad -72\div2=-36,\qquad -36\cdot(-3)=\boxed{108}.
\]
(If the juxtaposition $2(-3)$ were treated as a single factor evaluated first, one would get $-72\div(-6)=12$; this is why such expressions are better written with explicit brackets.)
