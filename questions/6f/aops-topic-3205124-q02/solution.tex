There are seven groups in stage one. They yield $21$ humans, no cats and $13+7=20$ dogs. Stage two adds $4\cdot20=80$ rats. Thus the counts are $\boxed{21\text{ humans},\ 0\text{ cats},\ 20\text{ dogs},\ 80\text{ rats}}$.
