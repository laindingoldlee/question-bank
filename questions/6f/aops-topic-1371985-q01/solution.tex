After both gifts Cindy has $C-12+10=C-2$ and Elaine has $E+12-10=E+2$, where $C+E=60$. So
\[
C-2=3(E+2)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad C=3E+8,
\]
and $3E+8+E=60$ gives $E=13$, $C=47$. Cindy had $\boxed{\$47}$ and Elaine had $\boxed{\$13}$. Check: afterwards they have $45$ and $15$, and $45=3\cdot15$.
