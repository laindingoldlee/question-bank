We need $n^2<10^7$, i.e. $n<\sqrt{10^7}=1000\sqrt{10}\approx3162.28$. Indeed $3162^2=9998244<10^7$ and $3163^2=10004569>10^7$. So $n=1,2,\dots,3162$, and the answer is $\boxed{3162}$.
