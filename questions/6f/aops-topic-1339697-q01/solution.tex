Stacked exponents are evaluated from the top down, so $a^{b^c}$ means $a^{\left(b^c\right)}$. (The other reading would be pointless to write as a tower, since $\left(a^b\right)^c=a^{bc}$.)

Here $2^{3^2}=2^{9}=\boxed{512}$, while $\left(2^3\right)^2=8^2=2^6=\boxed{64}$. They differ, so the convention matters.
