Using their common altitude to $AB$, $\dfrac{[ADC]}{[ABC]}=\dfrac{AD}{AB}=\dfrac{1}{4}$. Similarity of $ACD$ and $ABC$ gives $AC^2=AD\cdot AB$, hence $\dfrac{AC}{AB}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore $\cos A=\dfrac{1}{2}$ and
\[
\boxed{\angle A=60^\circ,\quad\angle B=30^\circ,\quad\angle C=90^\circ}.
\]

\end{asy}
