Let the first two digits be $a\ge1$ and $b$. The digits are
\[
a,\ b,\ a+b,\ a+2b,\ 2a+3b,\ 3a+5b,
\]
and all must be at most $9$. The last condition $3a+5b\le9$ forces $a\le3$, and with $a=3$ it forces $b=0$. So the largest first digit is $3$, then $b=0$, and the number is $\boxed{303369}$.
