In triangle $ABC$, points $X$, $Y$ and $N$ lie on the sides $AB$, $AC$ and $BC$ respectively, with $\angle XNY=90^\circ$ and $XY\parallel BC$. The lengths $XN$, $YN$, $XY$, $BC$, in this order, form an arithmetic sequence with common difference $5$. Find the area of triangle $ABC$.
