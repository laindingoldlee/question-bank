Dr.\ Yan drives at a constant speed along a long straight road beside a railway line. Trains run in both directions along the line, all at the same constant speed, and in each direction they leave the terminal at equal time intervals. Every $3$ minutes Dr.\ Yan meets a train coming towards him, and every $6$ minutes a train travelling in his direction overtakes him. How often does a train leave the terminal?
