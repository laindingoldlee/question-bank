Continuing: $1,1,2,3,5,8,5,5,2,7,1,8,\ 1,1,\dots$. Terms $13$ and $14$ equal terms $1$ and $2$, and each term depends only on the two before it, so the sequence has period $12$.

Since $2020=12\cdot168+4$, the $2020$th term equals the $4$th: $\boxed{3}$.
