\emph{Algebra.} The $k$th odd number is $2k-1$, so $\sum_{k=1}^n(2k-1)=2\cdot\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}-n=n^2$.

\emph{Picture.} A $k\times k$ square of dots becomes a $(k+1)\times(k+1)$ square by adding an L-shaped border of $2k+1$ dots, the next odd number. Starting from one dot, the odd numbers build up the squares $1,4,9,16,\dots$

For $n=40$ the sum is $40^2=\boxed{1600}$.
