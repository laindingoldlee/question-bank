I have noted down six of the seven digits of Erich's seven-digit phone number, in the correct order, but I have missed out one digit. I know neither which digit I missed nor where it was. (The first digit of the number may be $0$.)
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What is the largest number of different numbers I may have to try in order to be sure of reaching the right one?
\item[(b)] A student argues: ``the missing digit can be in any of $7$ places and can be any of $10$ digits, so the answer is $7\cdot10=70$.'' Explain why this is wrong.
\end{enumerate}
