The equation is $x^2-x+2=0$. Its discriminant is $1-8=-7<0$, so $x$ is one of two non-real complex numbers, and $x^2=x-2$. Then
\[
x^3=x\cdot x^2=x^2-2x=(x-2)-2x=-x-2,
\]
so
\[
y=x^2+x^3=(x-2)+(-x-2)=\boxed{-4}
\]
for both roots. This is the only possible value.
