An infinite staircase has a lowest step of height $2$ feet, and each step has half the height of the step below it. A bug stands on the lowest step. Every second it jumps up $1$, $2$ or $3$ steps, each with probability $\dfrac13$, independently, and it continues forever. Let $n$ be the expected value of the sum of the heights of all the steps on which the bug stands, including the lowest step. If $n=\dfrac ab$ in lowest terms, find $a+b$.
