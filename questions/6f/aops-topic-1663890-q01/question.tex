Let $P$ be a point inside an equilateral triangle $ABC$, and let $E$, $D$, $F$ be the feet of the perpendiculars from $P$ to $AB$, $AC$, $BC$ respectively. Are the quadrilaterals $AEPD$ and $BFPE$ always similar? Prove your answer.
