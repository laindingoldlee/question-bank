There are $16^2$ choices for $P_2$, $15$ choices for $P_1$ in its column, and $15\cdot 14$ ordered choices for $P_3,P_4$ in its row. Each configuration has probability $p^3(1-p)$. By linearity, $E[X]=806400p^3(1-p)$. The derivative is $806400p^2(3-4p)$, so the maximum on $[0,1]$ occurs at $p=\dfrac{3}{4}$. Its value is $806400\cdot \dfrac{27}{256}=\boxed{85050}$.
