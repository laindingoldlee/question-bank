Let $u=2x-1$. The left side is $3x\cdot u-4\cdot u$, and by the distributive law $3x\cdot u-4\cdot u=(3x-4)u$. Substituting back, it equals $(3x-4)(2x-1)$. (Check by expanding both sides: each equals $6x^2-11x+4$.)
