Let $\angle C=\gamma$; then $\angle B=90^\circ-\gamma$. Since $DA=DC$, triangle $ADC$ is isosceles and $\angle DAC=\angle DCA=\gamma$. Hence
\[\angle DAB=90^\circ-\gamma=\angle B=\angle DBA,\]
so triangle $ABD$ is isosceles too, with $DB=DA$. Therefore $DB=DA=DC$, and $D$ is the midpoint of $BC$.

Since $DE\parallel AB$, triangles $CDE$ and $CBA$ are similar (they share the angle at $C$, and corresponding angles at $E$ and $A$ are equal), with ratio $\dfrac{CD}{CB}=\dfrac12$. So $E$ is the midpoint of $AC$ and
\[DE=\dfrac12AB=\boxed{3}.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(0,6),C=(8,0),D=(4,3),Ep=(4,0);
void tk(pair P,pair Q){pair M=(P+Q)/2; pair u=unit(rotate(90)*(Q-P)); draw(M-0.22*u--M+0.22*u);}
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
draw(A--D,blue); draw(D--Ep,red+linewidth(1));
draw((0,0.35)--(0.35,0.35)--(0.35,0));
draw((4,0.35)--(4.35,0.35)--(4.35,0));
tk(A,D); tk(B,D); tk(D,C);
dot(D);
label("$A$",A,SW); label("$B$",B,N); label("$C$",C,SE); label("$D$",D,NE); label("$E$",Ep,S);
label("$6$",(0,3),W); label("$3$",(4,1.5),E,red);
\end{asy}

(The sketch uses $AC=8$; the answer does not depend on $AC$.)
