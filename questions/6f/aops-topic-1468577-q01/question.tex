The points $0$, $1$ and $i$ of the complex plane form a right triangle with the right angle at $0$. A student computes the hypotenuse with the Pythagorean theorem as follows: ``the legs are $1$ and $i$, so the hypotenuse squared is $1^2+i^2=1-1=0$, so the hypotenuse has length $0$.'' Find the error, and find the correct length of the hypotenuse.
