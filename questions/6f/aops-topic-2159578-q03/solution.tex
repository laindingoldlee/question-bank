When a wheel rolls without slipping, one revolution moves the car forward by the circumference of the wheel, $\pi\cdot20=20\pi$ inches.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

draw((-1,1)--(1,1),blue); label("diameter $20$ in",(0,2),N,blue);
dot((0,0),red); dot((2*pi,0),red);
draw(circle((2*pi,1),1),gray(0.6)+dashed);
draw((-1.3,0)--(2*pi+1.5,0),linewidth(0.8));
draw((0,-0.35)--(2*pi,-0.35),Arrows(5));
label("one revolution: $20\pi$ in",(pi,-0.35),S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The speed is $60$ miles per hour, which is $1$ mile per minute, or
\[\dfrac{5280\cdot12}{60}=1056\text{ inches per second}.\]
So the number of revolutions per second is
\[\dfrac{1056}{20\pi}=\boxed{\dfrac{264}{5\pi}}\approx16.8 .\]
