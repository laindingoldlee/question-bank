How many $4$-digit numbers have their last digit equal to the sum of their first two digits? A student lists the possible first two digits for each last digit as follows, and asks why the pairs $01$, $02$, $03$, \dots\ are not included:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{c|l}
last digit & first two digits\\ \hline
$1$ & $10$, $01$\\
$2$ & $11$, $20$, $02$\\
$3$ & $12$, $21$, $30$, $03$
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Explain which pairs are not allowed and why, and find the count.
