We need $5a$ to leave remainder $1$ on division by $11$, i.e.\ $5a\in\{1,12,23,34,45,\dots\}$. The first multiple of $5$ in this list is $45=5\cdot9$. (Equivalently, $5\cdot(-2)=-10\equiv1\pmod{11}$, so $a\equiv-2\equiv9$.) Since $5$ is invertible modulo $11$, the solutions are exactly $a\equiv9\pmod{11}$, and the smallest positive one is $a=\boxed{9}$.
