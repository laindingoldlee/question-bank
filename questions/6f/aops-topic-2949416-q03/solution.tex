The alternating digit sums differ by $A-B$, so divisibility by 11 and the digit bounds force $A=B$. Divisibility by 9 requires $12+A+B$ to be divisible by 9, hence $A+B=6$ or 15. Since $A+B=2A$ is even, it equals $\boxed6$.
