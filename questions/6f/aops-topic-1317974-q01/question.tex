Events $A$, $B$, $C$ satisfy $P(A\cap B\cap C)=0.1$, $P(A\cap B)=0.3$, $P(A\cap C)=0.2$, $P(B\cap C)=0.4$ and $P(A)=P(B)=P(C)$. Suppose also that at least one of the three events always occurs. What is $P(A)$?
