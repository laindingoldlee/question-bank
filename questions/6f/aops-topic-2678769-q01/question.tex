A student claims to prove that $i^i=1$:
\[\left(i^i\right)^2=i^{-1},\qquad i^{-1}=\dfrac1i,\qquad\left(\dfrac1i\right)^2=-1^2=1,\]
``and taking the sixth root of one we get $1$.'' Identify the errors in this argument, and find the principal value of $i^i$, defined as $e^{i\operatorname{Log}i}$ with $\operatorname{Log}i=\dfrac{i\pi}{2}$.
