A restaurant menu has $6$ items: fries, pumpkin pie, onion rings, chicken nuggets, hamburgers and cheeseburgers. You buy $4$ items, and you may order the same item more than once (for example, two orders of fries). How many different combinations of $4$ items are possible? How many are possible if the $4$ items must all be different?
