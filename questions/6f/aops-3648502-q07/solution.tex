Let $t=AB$. Moving the reference point from $A$ to $B$ adds $t$ to the distance to $A$ and subtracts $t$ from each of the four distances to $B,C,D,E$, with the self-distance zero included. Thus the total decreases by $3t$. Therefore $3t=92-50=42$ and $\boxed{AB=14}$.
