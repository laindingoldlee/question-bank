\textbf{(a)} $\dfrac{\ \dfrac11\ }{3}=\dfrac{1}{3}$, because $\dfrac11=1$ and $1\div3=\dfrac13$.

$\dfrac{1}{\ \dfrac13\ }=1\times\dfrac31=3$: dividing by a fraction is multiplying by its reciprocal. (Equivalently, $3$ thirds make $1$, so $\dfrac13$ fits into $1$ three times.)

\textbf{(b)} From left to right, $1\div1\div3=(1\div1)\div3=1\div3=\boxed{\dfrac13}$.

\textbf{(c)} A mixed number means a sum: $1\dfrac13=1+\dfrac13=\boxed{\dfrac43}$. It involves no division by $\dfrac13$ and no division of $1$ by $1$, so it is different from both $\dfrac13$ and $3$.
