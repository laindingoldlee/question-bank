Let $k$ be a positive integer and $n=2^k$, and consider the binomial coefficients
\[\dbinom n1,\ \dbinom n2,\ \dots,\ \dbinom n{n-1}.\]
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that all of them are even.
\item[(b)] Find, with proof, how many of them are divisible by $4$.
\end{enumerate}
