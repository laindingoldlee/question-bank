Let the side be $s$. Triangle $BAM$ has base $AB=s$ and height $s$, so its area is $\dfrac{s^2}{2}$. Since $N$ is the midpoint of $AM$, triangles $BMN$ and $BNA$ have equal areas. Thus $2025=\dfrac{s^2}{4}$ and $\boxed{s=90}$.
