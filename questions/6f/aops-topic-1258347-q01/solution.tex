By Vieta's formulas, the product of the roots of $x^2+bx+c$ is $c$ and their sum is $-b$.

The first student had the correct constant term, so $c=6\cdot1=6$. The second had the correct middle term, so $-b=2+3=5$. The correct equation is
\[x^2-5x+6=0=(x-2)(x-3),\]
whose solutions are $\boxed{2\text{ and }3}$. (They happen to coincide with the second student's answer, because the second student's miscopied constant was also $6$.)
