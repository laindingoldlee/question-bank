A number is congruent modulo $9$ to its digit sum, and the digit sum of the whole string is the sum of the digit sums of $1,2,\dots,2010$, each of which is congruent to the number itself. So the number is congruent to
\[
1+2+\cdots+2010=\dfrac{2010\cdot2011}{2}=1005\cdot2011\equiv6\cdot4=24\equiv\boxed{6}\pmod9 .
\]
