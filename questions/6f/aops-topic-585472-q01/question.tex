Ryan's letter weighs $\dfrac{9}{10}$ oz. Stamps weigh $\dfrac{1}{30}$ oz each. The cost of mailing the first ounce, or fraction thereof, is $37$ cents, and each additional ounce or fraction thereof costs $23$ cents. Ryan has only $1$-cent stamps, and the weight of the stamps counts towards the weight of the letter. What is the least number of stamps he can use to pay exactly the postage? Assume the letter has no physical constraints.
