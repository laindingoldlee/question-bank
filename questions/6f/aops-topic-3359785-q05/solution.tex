For $7\nmid k$, the power depends on $k\bmod7$ and the exponent modulo $6$, since $k^6\equiv1\pmod7$. Hence the summand is periodic with period $42$; multiples of $7$ contribute zero. In each full block of $42$, fix an exponent residue modulo $6$. As the base runs over the six nonzero residues, its powers sum to $0$ modulo $7$ for exponents $1,\ldots,5$, and to $6$ for exponent $0$. Thus a full block contributes $6$. There are $96$ full blocks and $15$ further terms. The latter residues, for $k=1,\ldots,15$, are $1,4,3,1,3,1,0,1,2,1,2,2,6,0,1$, summing to $28\equiv0$. Therefore the total is $96\cdot6\equiv\boxed2\pmod7$.
