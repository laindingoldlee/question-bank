A digital clock shows times from 0:00 to 23:59, with hours written without a leading zero and minutes always with two digits (it does not show seconds). A time is palindromic if its digits, read without the colon, read the same forwards and backwards; for example 4:04, 0:00, 0:50 and 12:21. How many palindromic times occur in a $24$-hour day?
