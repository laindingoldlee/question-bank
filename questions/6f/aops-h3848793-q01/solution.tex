The maximum is $M=\min(9,\lfloor\dfrac{N}{5}\rfloor)$: use five blue cells per majority row, then distribute extras. With $r$ majority rows, at most $9r+4(9-r)=36+5r$ cells can be blue, so the minimum is
\[
m=\max\left(0,\left\lceil\dfrac{N-36}{5}\right\rceil\right).
\]
Both bounds can be attained by filling rows up to their allowed capacities. Solving $M-m=7$ gives
\[
N\in\{35,36,40,41,45,46\}.
\]
Indeed for $N\le36$ we need $\lfloor\dfrac{N}{5}\rfloor=7$, giving $35,36$; for $37\le N\le44$, only $40,41$ give difference $7$; for $N\ge45$, $M=9$ and $m=2$ exactly at $45,46$. The sum is $\boxed{243}$.
