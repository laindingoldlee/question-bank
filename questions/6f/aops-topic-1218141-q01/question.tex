In poker, a \emph{two-pair} hand consists of two cards of one rank, two cards of a second rank, and a fifth card of a third rank (for example $2,2,3,3,Q$, but not $2,2,3,3,3$). Five cards are dealt at random from a standard $52$-card deck. What is the probability of being dealt a two-pair? Express your answer to the nearest percent.
