There are $\dbinom{52}{5}=2{,}598{,}960$ equally likely hands. For a two-pair, choose the two paired ranks in $\dbinom{13}{2}=78$ ways, two suits for each pair in $\dbinom42^2=36$ ways, and the fifth card from the $44$ cards of the other $11$ ranks:
\[78\cdot36\cdot44=123{,}552.\]
The probability is
\[\dfrac{123552}{2598960}=\dfrac{198}{4165}\approx 0.0475,\]
which is $\boxed{5\%}$ to the nearest percent. (A common error is to choose the two pair ranks in order, $13\cdot12$ ways, which double counts and gives about $10\%$.)
