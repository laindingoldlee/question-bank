Every square is $0$ or $1$ modulo $4$: $(2k)^2=4k^2$ and $(2k+1)^2=4(k^2+k)+1$. Hence $x^2+y^2+1$ is congruent to $1$, $2$ or $3$ modulo $4$, never $0$. $\blacksquare$
