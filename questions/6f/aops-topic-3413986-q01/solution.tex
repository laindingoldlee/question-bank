The least nonnegative residues modulo $13$ are $1,2,1,0,1,2,1,1,1,0$, whose sum is $10$. Since $400\equiv10\pmod{13}$, any successful subset must include every number with nonzero residue: any smaller residue sum is between $0$ and $9$ and cannot work. But those required numbers already sum to $487>400$. Thus $\boxed{\text{no such subset exists}}$.
