Call the numbers $a$ and $b$. Then
\[(a-b)^2=a^2+b^2-2ab=244-240=4,\qquad (a+b)^2=a^2+b^2+2ab=484,\]
so $|a-b|=2$ and $a+b=\pm22$. With $a+b=22$ the numbers are $10$ and $12$; with $a+b=-22$ they are $-10$ and $-12$. Both pairs satisfy the first condition:
\[\left|a^3-b^3\right|=|a-b|\left(a^2+ab+b^2\right)=2(244+120)=728 .\]
So the two numbers are $\boxed{10\text{ and }12}$ or $\boxed{-10\text{ and }-12}$. (The condition on the cubes is automatically satisfied once the other two hold; if the numbers are required to be positive, they are $10$ and $12$.)
