Let $x$ be the largest real number less than $10$ satisfying
\[x+\lfloor x\rfloor^2=x^2.\]
Then $x$ can be written as $\dfrac{a+\sqrt b}{c}$, where $a$, $b$, $c$ are positive integers and $\gcd(b,c)=1$. Find $a+b+c$.
