John has $28$ chips of $\$100$ and $112$ chips of $\$20$. Let him bet $b$ chips: $0.1b$ of $\$100$ and $0.9b$ of $\$20$. He keeps $140-b$ chips, of which $112-0.9b$ are $\$20$ chips, so
\[
112-0.9b=0.7(140-b)=98-0.7b\iff0.2b=14\iff b=70 .
\]
He bet $7$ chips of $\$100$ and $63$ chips of $\$20$, worth $700+1260=\boxed{\$1960}$.
