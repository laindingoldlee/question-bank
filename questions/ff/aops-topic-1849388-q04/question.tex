John is playing poker with $140$ chips; $20\%$ of them are $\$100$ chips and $80\%$ are $\$20$ chips. He bets some chips, $10\%$ of which are $\$100$ chips, placing them at the centre of the table. Afterwards $70\%$ of John's remaining chips are $\$20$ chips. How much money did he bet?
