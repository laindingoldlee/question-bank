Let $g$ be the greatest common divisor. It divides $2002+2=2004$ and $2002^2+2$. Modulo $2004$ we have $2002\equiv-2$, so $2002^2+2\equiv4+2=6$; hence $g\mid\gcd(2004,6)=6$.

Conversely, $6$ divides every term: $2002\equiv4\pmod6$ and $4^k\equiv4\pmod6$ for all $k\ge1$ (as $4\cdot4=16\equiv4$), so $2002^k+2\equiv4+2\equiv0\pmod6$. Therefore $g=\boxed{6}$.
