The two-digit primes less than $40$ are $11,13,17,19,23,29,31,37$. Their digit differences and sums are:
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccccccc}
p & 11 & 13 & 17 & 19 & 23 & 29 & 31 & 37\\ \hline
\text{difference} & 0 & 2 & 6 & 8 & 1 & 7 & 2 & 4\\
\text{sum} & 2 & 4 & 8 & 10 & 5 & 11 & 4 & 10
\end{array}
\]
Both are prime only for $29$ (difference $7$, sum $11$). So there is $\boxed{1}$ such prime.
