A number with at most three digits has digit sum at most $9+9+9=27<33$, so the number has at least four digits. Among four-digit numbers, a smaller number has a smaller first digit (and then smaller following digits). The last three digits add up to at most $27$, so the first digit is at least $33-27=6$. With first digit $6$ the other three digits must add up to $27$, so they are all $9$. The answer is $\boxed{6999}$.
