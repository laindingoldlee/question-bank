An isosceles trapezoid $ABCD$ has bases $BC$ and $AD$, with $A=(0,0)$, $B=(2a,2b)$ and $C=(2a+c,2b)$, where $a$, $b$, $c$ are positive, and the fourth vertex $D$ lies on the $x$-axis. The coordinates of $D$ are $(ra+sb+tc,0)$, where $r$, $s$, $t$ are constants (the same for all such trapezoids). What is the value of $r+s+t$?
