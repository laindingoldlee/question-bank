An isosceles trapezoid is symmetric about the perpendicular bisector of its bases. The upper base $BC$ runs from $x=2a$ to $x=2a+c$, so the axis of symmetry is the vertical line $x=2a+\dfrac {c}{2}$. The vertex $D$ is the mirror image of $A=(0,0)$ in this line:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real a=1.1,bb=1.3,c=3.4;
pair A=(0,0),B=(2a,2bb),C=(2a+c,2bb),D=(4a+c,0);
draw((-0.6,0)--(4a+c+0.8,0),gray,Arrow(TeXHead));draw((0,-0.5)--(0,2bb+0.9),gray,Arrow(TeXHead));
label("$x$",(4a+c+0.8,0),E,gray);label("$y$",(0,2bb+0.9),N,gray);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
real xm=2a+c/2;draw((xm,-0.35)--(xm,2bb),red+dashed);
label("$A(0,0)$",A,SW);label("$B(2a,2b)$",B,N);label("$C(2a+c,2b)$",C,N);label("$D$",D,S);
draw((2a,0)--B,blue+dotted);draw((2a+c,0)--C,blue+dotted);
label("$2a$",(a,0),S,blue);label("$2a$",(3a+c,0),S,blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

\[
D=\left(2\left(2a+\dfrac {c}{2}\right)-0,\ 0\right)=(4a+c,0).
\]
(Equivalently, the leg $AB$ goes $2a$ to the right of $A$, so by symmetry the leg $CD$ ends $2a$ to the right of $C$'s foot $(2a+c,0)$.) Since this must hold for all positive $a,b,c$, we get $r=4$, $s=0$, $t=1$, and
\[
r+s+t=\boxed{5}.
\]
