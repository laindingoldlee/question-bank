For the standard maximal corner-anchored equilateral triangle in a unit square, the other vertices are $(1,2-\sqrt3)$ and $(2-\sqrt3,1)$, so its side squared is $8-4\sqrt3$. A square resting on one side of an equilateral triangle of side $l$ has side $\dfrac{l\sqrt3}{(2+\sqrt3)}$ by similar triangles. Thus each square area is multiplied by $r=(8-4\sqrt3)\cdot\dfrac{3}{(2+\sqrt3)^2}=312-180\sqrt3$. The sum is $\dfrac{1}{(1-r)}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{(180\sqrt3-311)}}$.

\end{asy}
