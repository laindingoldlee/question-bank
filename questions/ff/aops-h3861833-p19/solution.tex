The path contains exactly five up steps and ends with the fifth. Each of the five gaps before an up step contains zero, one, or two right steps. The initial gap is arbitrary, giving three choices. Among the four internal gaps, two consecutive zero gaps are forbidden because they create three consecutive up steps. Let $T_j$ count length-$j$ words in $\{0,1,2\}$ with no consecutive zeros. Then $T_0=1,T_1=3$, and $T_j=2T_{j-1}+2T_{j-2}$, by separating words ending in a nonzero symbol from those ending in zero. This gives $T_2=8,T_3=22,T_4=60$. Thus the answer is $3T_4=\boxed{180}$.
