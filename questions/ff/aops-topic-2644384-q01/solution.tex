Let $N=3^{200}$. Each of the $N$ factors of $N!$ is at most $N$, so $N!<N^N=3^{200\cdot3^{200}}$.

Next, $200!=200\cdot(199\cdot198\cdots4)\cdot6>200\cdot4^{196}$. Since $\left(\dfrac43\right)^4=\dfrac{256}{81}>3$, we have $\left(\dfrac43\right)^{196}>3^{49}>3^4$, so $4^{196}>3^{200}$. Hence $200!>200\cdot3^{200}$, and
\[3^{200!}>3^{200\cdot3^{200}}>\left(3^{200}\right)!.\]
So $\boxed{3^{200!}}$ is bigger. $\blacksquare$
