A point is visible exactly when $\gcd(|x|,|y|)=1$: otherwise division by the gcd gives an intervening tree, whereas a primitive direction has no earlier lattice point. In the positive quadrant, there are $1+2\sum_{k=2}^{10}\varphi(k)=1+2(1+2+2+4+2+6+4+6+4)=63$ coprime ordered pairs. Four quadrants give 252, and the four nearest axis points give 256. There are $21^2-1=440$ trees in all, so the fraction is $\boxed{\dfrac{32}{55}}$.
