A book says that ``the measure of a central angle is equal to the measure of its intercepted arc.''
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the lengths of the arcs intercepted by a $60^\circ$ central angle in a circle of radius $3$ and in a circle of radius $6$. Does the length of an arc depend only on its central angle?
\item[(b)] For which radius $r$ is the length of the arc intercepted by a central angle of $n^\circ$ equal to $n$ units, for every $n$?
\end{enumerate}
