Draw a right triangle with angle $B$, opposite leg $4$ and adjacent leg $3$. By the Pythagorean theorem its hypotenuse is $\sqrt{9+16}=5$. So $\sin B=\dfrac45$ and $\csc B=\boxed{\dfrac54}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0),C=(3,0),A=(3,4);draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
real s=0.35;draw(C+(-s,0)--C+(-s,s)--C+(0,s));
label("$B$",B,SW);label("$3$",(B+C)/2,S);label("$4$",(A+C)/2,E);label("$5$",(A+B)/2,NW);
\end{asy}

(Without the condition that $B$ is acute, $B$ could also lie in the third quadrant, where $\csc B=-\dfrac54$.)
