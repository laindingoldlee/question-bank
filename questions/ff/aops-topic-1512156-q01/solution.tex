Let the bucket hold $p$ potatoes and measure time in minutes. The total times are equal:
\[
120+1.5p=150+p\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad0.5p=30\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad p=\boxed{60}.
\]
(Check: Monday $120+90=210$ minutes, Tuesday $150+60=210$ minutes.)
