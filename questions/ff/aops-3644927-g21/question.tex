On the number line, a man initially stands on the origin. Each second, if he is currently on $k$ where $k < 2024$, he can move to any positive integer $n$ where $k < n \le 2023$ with a $2^{k-n}$ chance and can move to $2024$ with a $2^{k-2023}$ chance. When he reaches $2024$, he stops moving. What is the probability that he ever is on $2017$?
