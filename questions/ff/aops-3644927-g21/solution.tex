Let $h_j$ be the probability of ever visiting $j<2024$. Then $h_0=1$ and $h_j=\sum_{k=0}^{j-1}h_k2^{k-j}$. In particular $h_1=\dfrac{1}{2}$, and separating the final term gives $h_{j+1}=\dfrac{h_j}{2}+\dfrac{h_j}{2}=h_j$. Thus every positive intermediate state has visit probability $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$, including 2017.
