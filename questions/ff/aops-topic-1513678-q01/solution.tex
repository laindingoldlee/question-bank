For $n\ne0$,
\[
A=\dfrac{2n-15}{n}=2-\dfrac{15}{n},
\]
so $A$ is an integer exactly when $n$ divides $15$. The integer divisors of $15$ are $\pm1,\pm3,\pm5,\pm15$. They come in pairs $n,-n$, so their sum is $\boxed{0}$.
