There are $13$ pirates, all with different amounts of gold. In one move, any group of between $2$ and $12$ of the pirates may pool their gold and share it out equally among themselves. A student claims that it is impossible to make all $13$ pirates end up with the same amount of gold. Show that the claim is false.
