Write $AD=DC=a$ and $BD=b$. The angle-bisector theorem gives $\dfrac{A E}{E B} = \dfrac{a}{b}$ and $\dfrac{C F}{F B} = \dfrac{a}{b}$. Therefore $\dfrac{B E}{B A} = \dfrac{B F}{B C} = \dfrac{b}{a+b}$, so $EF\parallel AC$ and $E F = \dfrac{2 a b}{a+b}$. The homothety centred at $B$ sends $D$ to $P$ with scale $\dfrac{b}{a+b}$, giving $D P = b - \dfrac{b^2}{a+b} = \dfrac{a b}{a+b}$. Hence $\boxed{\dfrac{E F}{D P} = 2}$.
