Factor out the smallest powers. On the left, $34\cdot3^{x-2}=102\cdot3^{x-3}$, so the left side is $(102-2)\,3^{x-3}=100\cdot3^{x-3}$. On the right, $0.8\cdot10^{x-2}=8\cdot10^{x-3}$, so the right side is $9\cdot10^{x-3}$. Hence
\[100\cdot3^{x-3}=9\cdot10^{x-3}\iff\left(\dfrac{10}{3}\right)^{x-3}=\dfrac{100}{9}=\left(\dfrac{10}{3}\right)^2.\]
Since $t\mapsto\left(\dfrac{10}{3}\right)^t$ is strictly increasing (so one-to-one), $x-3=2$ and $x=\boxed{5}$.
