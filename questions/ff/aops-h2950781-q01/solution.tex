Scale to $B=(0,0),C=(2,0),A=(\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2})$. Write $P=(\dfrac{t}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt3 t}{2})$ with $0<t<1$. Then $Q=(2-\dfrac{3t}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt3 t}{2})$, and the isosceles condition with the larger base $BR$ gives $R=(2-t,0)$. Thus
\[[BPQR]=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}t(4-3t),\qquad [ABC]=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}.\]
Completing the square yields $t(4-3t)=\dfrac{4}{3}-3(t-\dfrac{2}{3})^2\le\dfrac{4}{3}$. Consequently
\[\dfrac{2[ABC]}{[BPQR]}=\dfrac{4}{t(4-3t)}\ge\boxed3,\]
with equality at $t=\dfrac{2}{3}$, a permitted configuration.

\end{asy}
