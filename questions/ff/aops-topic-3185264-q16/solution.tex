After eight unit moves, reachable lattice points have Manhattan distance at most $8$ from the origin and even parity. Conversely, any such point is reached by a shortest path followed by back-and-forth pairs. For distance $d>0$, there are $4d$ points. Hence the count is $1+4(2+4+6+8)=\boxed{81}$.
