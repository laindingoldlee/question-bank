Alice starts with three bottles and Bob with two. Independently at each step a coin is heads with probability $\dfrac{2}{3}$. On heads Alice gives Bob one bottle; on tails Bob gives Alice one. Stop when someone has no bottles. (a) Find the probability Bob loses all his bottles. (b) Find the expected number of flips.
