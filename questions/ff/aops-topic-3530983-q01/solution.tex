Let $u_i$ be Bob's probability of reaching zero when he currently has $i$ bottles. Then $u_0=1,u_5=0$, and $u_i=\dfrac{2}{3}u_{i+1}+\dfrac{1}{3}u_{i-1}$. Solving the recurrence gives $u_i=\dfrac{2^{-i}-2^{-5}}{1-2^{-5}}$, so $\boxed{u_2=\dfrac{7}{31}}$. For the expected duration $e_i$, the recurrence is $e_i=1+\dfrac{2}{3}e_{i+1}+\dfrac{1}{3}e_{i-1}$ with $e_0=e_5=0$. Its solution is $e_i=\dfrac{480}{31}(1-2^{-i})-3i$, as direct substitution and the two boundary values verify. Hence $\boxed{e_2=\dfrac{174}{31}}$. Absorption has finite expectation: from any interior state, four consecutive heads force absorption, an event of probability at least $(\dfrac{2}{3})^4$, so the number of four-step blocks is bounded by a geometric waiting time.
