All rockets take the same travel time, so arrivals remain one hour apart. By the final rocket's arrival, the robot teams have worked for $47,46,\ldots,1$ hours. Their pooled work is $\dfrac{47\cdot48}{2}=1128$ units. Since $5\cdot196=980$ and $6\cdot196=1176$, the number of completed bases is $\boxed5$.
