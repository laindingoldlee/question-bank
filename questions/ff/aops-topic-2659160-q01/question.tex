Rectangles of sizes $2\times3$, $4\times5$ and $6\times7$ are available in unlimited supply. They are to tile a $23\times8$ rectangle exactly (no gaps, no overlaps; pieces may be rotated, with their sides parallel to the sides of the big rectangle), using at least one rectangle of each size. What is the smallest possible number of rectangles?
