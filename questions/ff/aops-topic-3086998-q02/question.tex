You start with 3 tokens. Each play costs 1 token. Independently, a wheel instructs you to roll a sum of 7 with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, an even sum with probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$, or an odd sum with probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$. You roll two fair dice and receive 5 tokens if you succeed. Play until you have no tokens. Is the expected time to ruin finite?
