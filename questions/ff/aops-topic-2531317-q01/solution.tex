The opponent fails to escape exactly when he does not resign at the end of any of the $8$ cycles. Given that he has not resigned before, he does not resign at the end of cycle $n$ with probability $1-\dfrac{1}{10-n}=\dfrac{9-n}{10-n}$. The product telescopes:
\[\prod_{n=1}^{8}\dfrac{9-n}{10-n}=\dfrac89\cdot\dfrac78\cdot\dfrac67\cdots\dfrac12=\dfrac19.\]
So he escapes with probability $1-\dfrac19=\boxed{\dfrac89}$.
