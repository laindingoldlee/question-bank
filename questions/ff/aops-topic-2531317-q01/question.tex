An illusion traps an opponent in a loop of cycles. At the end of the $n$-th cycle ($n=1,2,\dots,8$), an opponent who has not yet resigned resigns, and so escapes, with probability $\dfrac{1}{10-n}$. An opponent who has not resigned after $8$ cycles dies of exhaustion. What is the probability that the opponent escapes?
