Let $n$ be a nonnegative integer.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove the multinomial expansion
\[(x+y+z)^n=\sum_{\substack{a+b+c=n\\ a,b,c\ge0}}\dfrac{n!}{a!\,b!\,c!}\,x^ay^bz^c,\]
where the sum is over all ordered triples of nonnegative integers with $a+b+c=n$.
\item[(b)] How many terms does the expansion have after like terms are collected?
\item[(c)] Find the coefficient of $x^2y^3z$ in $(x+y+z)^6$.
\end{enumerate}
