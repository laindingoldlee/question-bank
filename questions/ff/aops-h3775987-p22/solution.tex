At the highest binary position where $m,n$ differ, $m$ has $0$ and $n$ has $1$. Let $r$ be the number of lower positions, where $0\le r\le6$. The common higher prefix has $2^{6-r}$ choices. If the lower tails have $a,b$ ones respectively, we need $a>b+1$. Thus the tail count is $T_r=\sum_{a>b+1}\binom ra\binom rb$. For $r=0,\ldots,6$, these counts are $0,0,1,7,37,176,794$. Summing over the first differing position gives $\sum_{r=0}^6 2^{6-r}T_r=16+56+148+352+794=\boxed{1366}$.
