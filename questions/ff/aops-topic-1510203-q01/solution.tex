Both square roots must be of nonnegative numbers: $3-2x\ge0$, i.e.\ $x\le\dfrac{3}{2}$, and $1-x\ge0$, i.e.\ $x\le1$. Both hold exactly when $x\le1$. The domain is $\boxed{(-\infty,1]}$.
