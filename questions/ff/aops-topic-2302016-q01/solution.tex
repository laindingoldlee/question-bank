We work modulo $100$. Since $7^2=49$ and $7^4=49^2=2401$,
\[7^4\equiv1\pmod{100}.\]
Therefore $7^{52}=\left(7^4\right)^{13}\equiv1^{13}=1\pmod{100}$, and the last two digits are $\boxed{01}$.
