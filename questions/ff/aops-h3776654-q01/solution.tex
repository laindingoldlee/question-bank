One answer is $\boxed{38459}$. Its digit sum is $29$, its digit product is $3\cdot8\cdot4\cdot5\cdot9=4320$, and $38459=67\cdot574+1$. Its reversal is $95483$, different from itself. Trial division by every prime at most $\sqrt{38459}<197$, $\sqrt{95483}<310$, and $\sqrt{38461}<197$, respectively, verifies that $38459,95483,38461$ are prime. Thus every condition holds. This example question does not require uniqueness.
