Let there be $m$ boys and $f$ girls. Ethan's classmates are $m-1$ boys and $f$ girls, so $m-1=f$. Olivia's are $m$ boys and $f-1$ girls, so $f-1=\dfrac34m$. Then $m-2=\dfrac34m$, so $m=8$, $f=7$, and the class has $\boxed{15}$ students.
