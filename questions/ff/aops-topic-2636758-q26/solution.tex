$n(n-1)=2\dbinom n2$, and by the hockey-stick identity $\displaystyle\sum_{n=2}^{90}\dbinom n2=\dbinom{91}{3}$. So the sum is
\[2\dbinom{91}{3}=2\cdot\dfrac{91\cdot90\cdot89}{6}=\boxed{242970}.\]
