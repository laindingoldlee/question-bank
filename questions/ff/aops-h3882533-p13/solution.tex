Let $v=\overrightarrow{MN}$ and let $w$ be a vector parallel to the bases with length $\dfrac{(AB+CD)}{2}$. The two diagonal vectors are $v+w$ and $v-w$, in a suitable orientation. Thus
\[
|v|^2=361,\qquad |w|^2=\dfrac{24^2+23^2}{2}-361=\dfrac{383}{2},
\qquad v\cdot w=\dfrac{24^2-23^2}{4}=\dfrac{47}{4}.
\]
The trapezoid area is $|v\times w|$, so its square is
\[
361\cdot\dfrac{383}{2}-\left(\dfrac{47}{4}\right)^2
=\dfrac{1103895}{16}.
\]
This gives $a=1103895,b=4$, which satisfy the required normalization. Hence the answer is $\boxed{899}$.
