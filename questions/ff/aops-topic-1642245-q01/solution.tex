Let there be $w\ge1$ white and $k\ge1$ black dogs: $4w+9k=48$. Then $9k=48-4w$ is a multiple of $4$, so $k$ is a multiple of $4$, and $9k<48$ gives $k\le5$. Hence $k=4$ and $4w=48-36=12$, $w=3$:
\[
\boxed{3\text{ white dogs and }4\text{ black dogs}}.
\]
Check: $12+36=48$. (Without the condition ``at least one of each'' there would also be $12$ white dogs and no black dogs.)
