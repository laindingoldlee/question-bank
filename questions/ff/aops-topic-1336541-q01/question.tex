Let $T(N)=\dfrac{N(N+1)}{2}$ be the $N$th triangular number.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $T(N)$ is a positive integer for every positive integer $N$.
\item[(b)] Check that $\sqrt{T(N)}$ is an integer for $N=1$, $8$ and $49$.
\item[(c)] Prove that $\sqrt{T(N)}$ is an integer for infinitely many positive integers $N$.
\end{enumerate}
