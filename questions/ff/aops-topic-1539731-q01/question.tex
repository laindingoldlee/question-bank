Jessica has two necklaces, each made of $200$ beads in a circle: $100$ white and $100$ black. She wants to lay the first necklace on top of the second, bead on bead, so that as many beads as possible lie on a bead of the same colour. She may rotate the first necklace and turn it over. What is the largest number of matched beads that she can be sure to obtain, whatever the orders of the beads on the two necklaces?
