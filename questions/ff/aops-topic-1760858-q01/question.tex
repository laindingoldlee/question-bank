Consider the lattice points $(x,y)$ with $x,y$ integers, $0\le x\le7$ and $0\le y\le7$. Two points are selected at random from this set, independently, each point equally likely, and the two points need not be distinct. What is the probability that the triangle (possibly degenerate) formed by the two points and $(0,0)$ has integer area (possibly zero)? Express your answer as a decimal to the nearest thousandth.
