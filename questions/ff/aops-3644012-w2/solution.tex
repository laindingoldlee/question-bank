The midpoint P of the hypotenuse is five from each of A,B,C, so both triangles are isosceles and $BP=5$. For uniqueness, parameterise P along the hypotenuse and test the three possible equal-side conditions in each smaller triangle. The only compatible interior choice is $AP=BP=CP=5$; alternatives $AB=AP$ or $BC=CP$ do not satisfy an isosceles condition in the other triangle. Hence $\boxed{BP=5}$.

\end{asy}
