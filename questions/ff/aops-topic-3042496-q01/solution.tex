Cancel the positive factor $x$ after expanding the left side, obtaining $(x+1)(x+2)(x+3)=(x-1)!$. For $x=1,2,3,4,5,6$, the left sides are 24,60,120,210,336,504, while the right sides are 1,1,2,6,24,120, so none works. At $x=7$, both sides equal 720. Hence the least value is $\boxed7$.
