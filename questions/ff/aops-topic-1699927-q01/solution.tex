There are $12$ face cards and $40$ other cards. The number of $3$-card hands with exactly $2$ face cards is $\dbinom{12}{2}\dbinom{40}{1}=66\cdot40=2640$, out of $\dbinom{52}{3}=22100$ hands. The probability is
\[
\dfrac{2640}{22100}=\boxed{\dfrac{132}{1105}}\approx0.119 .
\]
