Set $h(t)=8$:
\[
-16t^2+40t+3=8\ \Longleftrightarrow\ 16t^2-40t+5=0 .
\]
By the quadratic formula,
\[
t=\dfrac{40\pm\sqrt{1600-320}}{32}=\dfrac{40\pm\sqrt{1280}}{32}=\dfrac{5\pm2\sqrt5}{4}.
\]
Since $\sqrt5\approx2.23607$, the two times are $t\approx\dfrac{0.52786}{4}\approx0.13$ and $t\approx\dfrac{9.47214}{4}\approx2.37$. The ball is $8$ feet high on the way up at $\boxed{t\approx0.13\text{ s}}$ and on the way down at $\boxed{t\approx2.37\text{ s}}$. (Both are before the ball lands, which happens when $h(t)=0$, at $t\approx2.57$.)
