Let $u(n)$ be the units digit of a positive integer $n$. Define a sequence by $k_1=2019$ and $k_i=u\left(2019^{k_{i-1}}\right)$ for $i>1$. What is $u\left(2019^{k_1+k_2+\cdots+k_{2019}}\right)$?
