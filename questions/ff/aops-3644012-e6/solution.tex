Use $A=(0,0),E=(1,0),D=(0,1),F=(1,1),C=(2,1)$. At height $0\le y\le1$, the arc AF has coordinate $x=\sqrt{1-(y-1)^2}$, and arc EC has coordinate $x=1+\sqrt{1-(y-1)^2}$. The horizontal width between them is always one. Therefore the bounded region has area $\int_0^1 1\,dy=\boxed1$.
