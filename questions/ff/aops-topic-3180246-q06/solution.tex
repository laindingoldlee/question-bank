If the absent digit is $d$, the combined digit sum is $45-d$. Modulo 9 this equals $11044\equiv1$, so $d\equiv8\pmod9$. Since $1\le d\le9$, $d=8$. Existence is confirmed by $3415+7629=11044$. Thus $\boxed8$.
