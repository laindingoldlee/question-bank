Triangle $PNQ$ has base $PQ=|p-q|$ on the $x$-axis and height $n$, so
\[\dfrac12|p-q|\,n=12,\qquad\text{i.e.}\qquad|p-q|\,n=24.\]
Also $n\mid15$. So $n$ is a positive common divisor of $15$ and $24$: $n=1$ or $n=3$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $n=1$: $p=-15$ and $|q+15|=24$, so $q=9$ or $q=-39$, giving $pqn=-135$ or $585$.
\item $n=3$: $p=-5$ and $|q+5|=8$, so $q=3$ or $q=-13$, giving $pqn=-45$ or $195$.
\end{itemize}

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real u=0.35;
pair T(real x,real y){return (u*x,u*y);}
draw(T(-15,0)--T(5,0),Arrow(6)); draw(T(0,-1)--T(0,5),Arrow(6));
label("$x$",T(5,0),E); label("$y$",T(0,5),N);
pair N3=T(0,3),P3=T(-5,0),Q3=T(3,0),Q4=T(-13,0);
fill(P3--N3--Q3--cycle,rgb(0.8,0.88,1));
draw(P3--N3--Q3,blue); draw(Q4--N3,red+dashed);
dot(N3); dot(P3); dot(Q3); dot(Q4);
label("$N(0,3)$",N3,NE,fontsize(8pt)); label("$P(-5,0)$",P3,S,fontsize(8pt)); label("$Q(3,0)$",Q3,S,fontsize(8pt)); label("$Q'(-13,0)$",Q4,S,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}

(The sketch shows the case $n=3$, with both positions of $Q$.) The possible values are
\[\boxed{pqn\in\{-135,\ -45,\ 195,\ 585\}}.\]
