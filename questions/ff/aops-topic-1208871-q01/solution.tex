The two numbers are the roots of $t^2-12t+9=0$ (by Vieta, a quadratic with these roots has sum $12$ and product $9$), namely $6\pm3\sqrt3$. Their difference is $6\sqrt3$. More directly,
\[(x-y)^2=(x+y)^2-4xy=144-36=108,\qquad |x-y|=\sqrt{108}=\boxed{6\sqrt3}.\]
