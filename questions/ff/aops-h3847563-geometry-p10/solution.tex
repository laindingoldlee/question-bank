For an $n$-gon the interior angle sum is $(n-2)180^\circ$. Each exterior turning angle supplements its interior angle, so their sum is $n180^\circ-(n-2)180^\circ=\boxed{360^\circ}$.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair[] P={(0,0),(3,0),(4,2),(2,4),(-1,2)};for(int i=0;i<5;++i){pair A=P[i],B=P[(i+1)%5];draw(A--B);draw(B--B+.4*(B-A),dashed);}label("convex polygon",(1.5,1.5));
\end{asy}
