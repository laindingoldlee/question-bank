SANTA has $5$ letters, with A appearing twice and S, N, T once each. Arranging $5$ letters gives $5!$ orders, but swapping the two A's gives the same word, so each word is counted $2!$ times:
\[
\dfrac{5!}{2!}=\dfrac{120}{2}=\boxed{60}.
\]
