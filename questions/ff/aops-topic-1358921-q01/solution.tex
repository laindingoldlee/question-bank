The last toss is the first toss with at least one head. Every toss is a fresh, independent toss, so the outcome of the last toss is distributed like one toss \emph{conditioned} on showing at least one head. Of the $8$ equally likely outcomes, $7$ have at least one head and $1$ of them is HHH. Hence
\[
P(\text{HHH on the last toss})=\dfrac{1}{7}.
\]
Formally, the last toss is toss $k$ with probability $\left(\dfrac{1}{8}\right)^{k-1}\dfrac{7}{8}$, and then HHH has probability $\dfrac{1}{7}$, so the answer is $\sum_k\left(\dfrac{1}{8}\right)^{k-1}\dfrac{1}{8}=\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{8}}{1-\dfrac{1}{8}}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{7}}$. The answer $\dfrac{1}{8}$ is wrong: it ignores that the outcome TTT never occurs on the last toss.
