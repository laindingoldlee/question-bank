You toss a fair nickel, a fair dime and a fair quarter at the same time. If you get at least one head, you stop; otherwise you toss all three coins again, and so on. When you toss for the last time, what is the probability that all three coins show heads? Express your answer as a common fraction. (A student answers $\dfrac{1}{8}$. Is that right?)
