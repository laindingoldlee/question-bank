Write the equation as $x^5+0\cdot x^4+ax^3+bx^2+dx-c=0$. A quintic has five roots (with multiplicity); let the fifth be $t$. By Vieta's formulas the sum of the roots is minus the coefficient of $x^4$:
\[
1+2+4+6+t=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad t=-13 .
\]
The product of the roots is $(-1)^5$ times the constant term, i.e.\ $-(-c)=c$:
\[
c=1\cdot2\cdot4\cdot6\cdot(-13)=-624,
\]
so $\dfrac{c}{156}=\boxed{-4}$.

(Here $a$, $b$, $d$ are then determined by the other Vieta relations; for instance $d=e_4$, the sum of the products of the roots four at a time, $=48-13\cdot92=-1148$.)
