The perpendicular diagonals divide a rhombus into four right triangles. Their total area is half the product of the diagonals: $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot14\cdot48=\boxed{336}$, choice (b).
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(-24,0),B=(0,7),C=(24,0),D=(0,-7);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C,dashed);draw(B--D,dashed);label("$A$",A,W);label("$B$",B,N);label("$C$",C,E);label("$D$",D,S);label("$48$",(10,0),S);label("$14$",(0,3),E);
\end{asy}
