Write an $L$-digit integer as leading digit $a\ge1$ followed by $L-1$ digits. Its value is at least $a10^{L-1}$, whereas its digit product is at most $a9^{L-1}$. Since $L\ge2$, the latter is strictly smaller. Thus $\boxed{\text{no such integer exists}}$.
