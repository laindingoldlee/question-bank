The two middle sorted outcomes must be $(5,5)$ or $(4,6)$. For $(5,5)$, the sorted form is $(a,5,5,d)$ with $a\le5,d\ge5$. The four cases $a<5,d=5$; $a<5,d=6$; $a=5,d=5$; $a=5,d=6$ give $16,48,1,4$ ordered outcomes, totalling 69. For $(4,6)$, the form is $(a,4,6,6)$: $a=1,2,3$ gives 36 orders and $a=4$ gives 6, totalling 42. Thus the probability is $\dfrac{111}{6^4}=\boxed{\dfrac{37}{432}}$.
