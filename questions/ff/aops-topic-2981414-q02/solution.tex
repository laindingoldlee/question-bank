Arc length equals radius times the central angle in radians: $\dfrac32\pi=9\theta$, so $\theta=\dfrac\pi6$, i.e. $\angle BAC=\boxed{30^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw(circle((0,0),1));pair B=dir(10),C=dir(40);draw(B--(0,0)--C);draw(arc((0,0),1,10,40),linewidth(1.5));
dot((0,0));label("$A$",(0,0),W);label("$B$",B,E);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$9$",B/2,S);
\end{asy}
