The angle at $O$ between two rays is $7^\circ$. Points $A_1,A_2,A_3,\dots$ are placed alternately on the two rays, each farther from $O$ than the previous point on the same ray, so that
\[
OA_1=A_1A_2=A_2A_3=A_3A_4=\cdots .
\]
What is the greatest possible number of segments in the sequence $OA_1, A_1A_2, A_2A_3,\dots$?

(A) $11$\quad (B) $12$\quad (C) $13$\quad (D) $14$\quad (E) infinitely many
