Sets $A$, $B$ and $C$ have $x$, $y$ and $z$ elements respectively.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Explain why $x$, $y$ and $z$ alone do not determine the number of elements of $A\cup B\cup C$.
\item[(b)] Prove the inclusion-exclusion formula
\[|A\cup B\cup C|=x+y+z-|A\cap B|-|A\cap C|-|B\cap C|+|A\cap B\cap C| .\]
\end{enumerate}
