On a circular track, every other jogger is both in front of Heather and behind her (going around the loop either way). So if there are $n$ joggers other than Heather, there are $n$ joggers in front of her and $n$ behind her, and the total number on the track is $n+1$. The condition reads
\[\dfrac{n}{5}+\dfrac{5n}{6}=n+1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \dfrac{31n}{30}=n+1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad n=30.\]
So the number of joggers on the track is
\[\boxed{31}.\]
