Assign equal masses $ m $ to $ A,C $, so $ M $ has mass $ 2m $. The ratio $ BP:PM=3:2 $ requires mass at $ B $ to be $\dfrac{4}{3}m $. Thus $ BD:DC=m:\dfrac{4}{3}m=3:4 $. By the angle-bisector theorem, $ AB:AC=3:4 $, giving $\boxed{AB=\dfrac{27}{4}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(0,27/4),C=(9,0),M=(4.5,0),P=(2.7,2.7),D=(27/7,27/7);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--D^^B--M,blue);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,N);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$M$",M,S);label("$P$",P,W);label("$D$",D,NE);
\end{asy}
