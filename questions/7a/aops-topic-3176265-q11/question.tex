Fix points $A,B$ with $AB=1$. For any point $X$ not on line $AB$, let $f(X)$ be the circumcenter of $ABX$. Count points $X$ for which all iterates through $f^{2019}(X)$ are defined and $f^{2019}(X)=X$.
