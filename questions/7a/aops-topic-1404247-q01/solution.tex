Let the middle number be $11m$. The numbers are $11(m-5),\dots,11(m+5)$, and their sum is $11\cdot11m=121m$. We need $121m<1000$, so $m\le8$ (as $121\cdot8=968<1000<1089=121\cdot9$). The largest number is $11(m+5)\le11\cdot13=\boxed{143}$, attained by $33,44,\dots,143$ (sum $968$).
