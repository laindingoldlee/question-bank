Each term is $\dfrac{1}{k(k+1)}=\dfrac{1}{k}-\dfrac{1}{k+1}$ for $k=3,4,\dots,27$. The sum telescopes:
\[
\left(\dfrac{1}{3}-\dfrac{1}{4}\right)+\left(\dfrac{1}{4}-\dfrac{1}{5}\right)+\cdots+\left(\dfrac{1}{27}-\dfrac{1}{28}\right)=\dfrac{1}{3}-\dfrac{1}{28}=\boxed{\dfrac{25}{84}}.
\]
