The integer 10,999,999 lies in the interval and ends in six 9 s. Any number ending in seven 9 s is congruent to 9,999,999 modulo 10,000,000; the two nearest possibilities are 9,999,999 and 19,999,999, both outside the interval. More terminal 9 s would also imply seven. Hence $\boxed6$.
