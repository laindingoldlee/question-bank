A solid cube is painted yellow on one pair of opposite faces, blue on another pair and black on the third pair. It is then cut into $36$ smaller cubes: $32$ of them are of one size and the other $4$ are larger, all of the same size, and none of the larger cubes has a blue face.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] How many of the $36$ cubes have at least one blue face?
\item[(b)] How many have exactly one painted face?
\item[(c)] How many have at least one black face?
\item[(d)] How many have at least one yellow or blue face?
\end{enumerate}
