The $k$th term is $1+3+\cdots+3^{k-1}=\dfrac{3^k-1}{2}$. So the sum is
\[\sum_{k=1}^{n}\dfrac{3^k-1}{2}=\dfrac12\left(\dfrac{3^{n+1}-3}{2}-n\right)=\boxed{\dfrac{3^{n+1}-2n-3}{4}}.\]
Check: $n=2$ gives $\dfrac{27-4-3}{4}=5=1+4$.
