Inductively $0\le a_n<5$, since $a_n<5$ implies $a_{n+1}<\sqrt{25}=5$. The sequence is increasing: $a_1>a_0$, and the increasing square-root function preserves each successive comparison. It is therefore bounded and monotone, so has a limit $L\ge0$. Continuity gives $L=\sqrt{20+L}$, hence $(L-5)(L+4)=0$. The nonnegative root is $\boxed5$.
