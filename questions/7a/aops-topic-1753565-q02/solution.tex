For odd $a=2k+1$, $a^2=4k(k+1)+1\equiv1\pmod8$, because $k(k+1)$ is even. Hence $a^2+3\equiv4\pmod8$, so $4\mid a^2+3$, and $a^2+7\equiv0\pmod8$. Therefore $4\cdot8=32$ divides the product. $\blacksquare$
