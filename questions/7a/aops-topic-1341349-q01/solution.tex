Both legs are multiples of $700007$:
\[
4900049=7\cdot700007,\qquad 6300063=9\cdot700007 .
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(9,0),C=(0,7);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(1));
real s=0.5; draw((s,0)--(s,s)--(0,s));
label("$6300063$",(4.5,0),S);label("$4900049$",(0,3.5),W);label("$c$",(4.5,3.5),NE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

By the Pythagorean theorem the hypotenuse is
\[
c=\sqrt{7^2\cdot700007^2+9^2\cdot700007^2}=700007\sqrt{49+81}=\boxed{700007\sqrt{130}}.
\]
($130=2\cdot5\cdot13$ has no square factor, so this is in simplest form.)
