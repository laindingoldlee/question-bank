There are $300$ multiples of $25$ and $7200$ non-multiples. A multiple of $25$ is a multiple of $30$ exactly when it is a multiple of $150$: there are $50$ of these. There are $250$ multiples of $30$ in all, so $250-50=200$ of them are not multiples of $25$. Hence
\[P=\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac{50}{300}+\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac{200}{7200}=\dfrac1{12}+\dfrac1{72}=\boxed{\dfrac7{72}}.\]
