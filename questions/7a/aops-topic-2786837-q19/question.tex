A strange random number generator always returns a positive integer from $1$ to $7500$ inclusive. Half of the time it returns a uniformly random multiple of $25$ in this range, and the other half of the time it returns a uniformly random integer in this range that is not a multiple of $25$. What is the probability that the number returned is a multiple of $30$?
