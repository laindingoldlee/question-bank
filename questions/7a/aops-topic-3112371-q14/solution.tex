The volume ratio gives an upper bound $\dfrac{5\cdot5\cdot8}{8\cdot5\cdot0.05}=100$. Stack the sheets with their $8\times5$ faces aligned to the corresponding box face. One hundred thicknesses total $5$ inches, so the bound is attained: $\boxed{100}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw((0,0)--(4,0)--(4,2.5)--(0,2.5)--cycle);draw((0,2.5)--(0.7,3.2)--(4.7,3.2)--(4,2.5));draw((4,0)--(4.7,0.7)--(4.7,3.2));for(int j=1;j<10;++j)draw((0,j/4)--(4,j/4),gray);label("$8$",(2,0),S);label("$5$",(0,1.25),W);label("stack height $5$",(4.8,2),E);
\end{asy}
