Three congruent right circular cones stand upright on a plane. Each has base radius $1$ and slant height $2$, and their bases are pairwise externally tangent. A small sphere sits in the central gap, tangent to the plane and externally tangent to the lateral surface of every cone. Its center lies vertically above the center of the triangle of cone axes. Find its radius.
