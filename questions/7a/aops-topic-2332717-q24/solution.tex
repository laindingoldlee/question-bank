Write $a=20a'$ etc.; then $\gcd(a',b',c')=1$ and $\operatorname{lcm}(a',b',c')=12=2^2\cdot3$. Handle each prime separately.

For $2$: exponents in $\{0,1,2\}$ with minimum $0$ and maximum $2$: $3^3-2\cdot2^3+1^3=12$ triples (inclusion-exclusion). For $3$: exponents in $\{0,1\}$ with minimum $0$ and maximum $1$: $2^3-2=6$. Total $12\cdot6=72$. \textbf{(E)}
