$T_n=\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$, so with $m=k^2$ we need $m(m+1)=1332=36\cdot37$. Since $m(m+1)$ increases with $m$, $m=36$ is the only positive solution, and $k^2=36$ gives $k=\boxed{6}$.
