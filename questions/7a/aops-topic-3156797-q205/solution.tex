Assume $ a\le b $ to avoid swapping legs. Test every $ 1\le a\le b<c<100 $ against the Pythagorean equation. This checks all allowed triangles and finds
\begin{verbatim}
T=[(a+b+c,a,b,c) for a in range(1,100)
   for b in range(a,100) for c in range(b+1,100)
   if a*a+b*b==c*c]
assert max(T)==(234,65,72,97)
\end{verbatim}
Therefore the maximum is $\boxed{234}$, attained by $(65,72,97)$; indeed $ 4225+5184=9409 $.
