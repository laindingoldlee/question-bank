The fractional part $r=x-\lfloor x\rfloor$ lies in $[0,1)$. It is zero exactly for integer $x$. In that case $\lceil r\rceil=0$ and $f(x)=1$. Otherwise $0<r<1$, so $\lceil r\rceil=1$ and $f(x)=0$.
