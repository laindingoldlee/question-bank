If the old rectangle is $L$ by $W$, the new one is $1.1L$ by $0.9W$, with area $(1.1)(0.9)LW=0.99LW$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw(box((0,0),(5,3)),linewidth(0.8));
draw(box((0,0),(5.5,2.7)),blue+dashed+linewidth(0.8));
label("$L$",(2.5,3),N); label("$W$",(0,1.5),W);
label("$1.1L$",(2.75,0),S,blue); label("$0.9W$",(5.5,1.35),E,blue);
\end{asy}

So the new area is $\boxed{99\%}$ of the old area, answer $\textbf{(B)}$.
