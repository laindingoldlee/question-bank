In an acute triangle $ABC$, let $D$ be the foot from $A$ to $BC$, let $E$ be the foot from $B$ to $CA$, and put $H=AD\cap BE$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Prove $A,B,D,E$ are concyclic.
\item Prove $E,H,D,C$ are concyclic.
\item Deduce $\angle HCD=\angle HAB$.
\item If $CH$ meets $AB$ at $F$, prove $CF\perp AB$.
\item Find the orthocenter of triangle $ABH$.
\item Prove $\angle AHB+\angle ACB=180^\circ$. Are $A,H,B,C$ concyclic?
\end{enumerate}
