For each course the customer either skips it or picks one of its dishes: $7$ options for the appetizer, $9$ for the main meal and $6$ for dessert. This gives $7\cdot9\cdot6=378$ combinations, one of which skips everything. So there are $378-1=\boxed{377}$ meals.

Check by cases: one course $6+8+5=19$; two courses $6\cdot8+6\cdot5+8\cdot5=118$; three courses $6\cdot8\cdot5=240$; and $19+118+240=377$.
