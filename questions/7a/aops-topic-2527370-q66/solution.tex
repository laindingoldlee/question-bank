Since $384=2^7\cdot3$, we have $384^4=2^{28}\cdot3^4$.

\textbf{(a)} The odd divisors are $3^k$ with $0\le k\le4$; the greatest is $3^4=\boxed{81}$.

\textbf{(b)} There are $29\cdot5=145$ divisors, of which $5$ are odd, so the probability is $\dfrac5{145}=\boxed{\dfrac1{29}}$.
