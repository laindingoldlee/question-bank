If Billy removes $x$ and Crystal removes $y$, then $b=45-x$ and $c=45-y$, so $b-c=y-x$. We need $x\equiv y\pmod4$. The numbers $1$ to $9$ fall into residue classes modulo $4$ of sizes
\[
\{1,5,9\}:3,\qquad\{2,6\}:2,\qquad\{3,7\}:2,\qquad\{4,8\}:2 .
\]
Of the $81$ equally likely pairs $(x,y)$, the favourable ones number $3^2+2^2+2^2+2^2=21$, so the probability is $\dfrac{21}{81}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{27}}$.
