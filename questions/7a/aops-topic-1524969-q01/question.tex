Billy and Crystal each have a bag containing $9$ balls numbered from $1$ to $9$. Each of them removes one ball, at random, from their own bag. Let $b$ be the sum of the numbers on the $8$ balls remaining in Billy's bag and $c$ the sum of the numbers on the $8$ balls remaining in Crystal's bag. Determine the probability that $b$ and $c$ differ by a multiple of $4$.
