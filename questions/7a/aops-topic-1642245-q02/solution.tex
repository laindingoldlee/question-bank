We need positive integers $w,k$ with $5w+7k=140$. Since $5w$ and $140$ are multiples of $5$, so is $7k$, hence $k$ is a multiple of $5$; also $7k<140$, so $k\in\{5,10,15\}$:
\[
k=5:\ w=21,\qquad k=10:\ w=14,\qquad k=15:\ w=7 .
\]
So there are $\boxed{3}$ ways: $21$ white and $5$ black, $14$ white and $10$ black, or $7$ white and $15$ black. (If a colour may be absent, the extra solutions $w=28,k=0$ and $w=0,k=20$ give $5$ ways.)
