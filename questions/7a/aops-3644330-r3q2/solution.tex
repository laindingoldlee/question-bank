Measure arrivals in minutes after nine. Alex is uniform on $[0,150]$ and Bob on $[60,120]$. For any Bob time $b$, overlap requires Alex’s time in $[b-30,b+30]$, an interval of length 60 fully inside Alex’s range. Thus the probability is $\dfrac{60}{150}=\dfrac{2}{5}$, giving $p+q=\boxed7$.
