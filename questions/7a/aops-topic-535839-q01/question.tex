Before new equipment was installed, Factory A filtered out $80\%$ of its pollutants and Factory B filtered out $72\%$ of its pollutants. After the new scrubber system was installed, both factories filter out $99.5\%$ of their pollutants. Factory A is four times as large as Factory B: without any filters it would produce four times as much pollution as Factory B. Otherwise the factories are the same. Let $\dfrac {a}{b}$, in lowest terms, be the ratio of the total volume of unfiltered pollutants from both factories \emph{after} the installation to the total volume \emph{before} the installation. Find $a+b$.
