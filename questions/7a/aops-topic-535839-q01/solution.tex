Let Factory B produce $V$ units of pollutants before filtering; Factory A produces $4V$.

\textbf{Before.} A lets through $20\%$ of $4V$, which is $0.8V$, and B lets through $28\%$ of $V$, which is $0.28V$. In total $1.08V$. (So A emitted about $\dfrac{0.8}{0.28}\approx2.9$ times as much as B, the ``nearly three times'' of the story.)

\textbf{After.} Both let through $0.5\%$, so together $0.005\cdot5V=0.025V$.

The ratio is
\[
\dfrac{0.025V}{1.08V}=\dfrac{25}{1080}=\dfrac{5}{216},
\]
which is in lowest terms ($216=2^3\cdot3^3$). Hence $a+b=5+216=\boxed{221}$.
