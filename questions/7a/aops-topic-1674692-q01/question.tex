Rebecca runs a quarter-mile each week. In each of the last $6$ weeks her time has decreased by $2\%$ (of the previous week's time), and she can now run a quarter-mile in $66$ seconds. What was her time six weeks ago? Round your answer to the nearest tenth of a second, and find it without a calculator.
