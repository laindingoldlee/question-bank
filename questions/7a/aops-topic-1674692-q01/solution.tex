If her time six weeks ago was $x$ seconds, then $x(0.98)^6=66$, so $x=\dfrac{66}{0.98^6}$.

\textbf{Compute $0.98^6$ by the binomial theorem.}
\[
(1-0.02)^6=1-6(0.02)+15(0.02)^2-20(0.02)^3+15(0.02)^4-\cdots=1-0.12+0.006-0.00016+0.0000024-\cdots,
\]
so $0.98^6\approx0.885842$ (the omitted terms are below $10^{-7}$).

\textbf{Divide.} $\dfrac{66}{0.885842}$: since $0.885842\cdot74.5=65.9952\ldots$ and $0.885842\cdot74.6=66.0838\ldots$, the quotient lies between $74.5$ and $74.6$, and $66-65.9952=0.0048$ is far less than half of $0.0886$, so $x=74.505\ldots$. Her time was about $\boxed{74.5}$ seconds.
