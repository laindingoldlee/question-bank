Let Inessa have $5k$ plants and Ralph $6k$. Half of Inessa's plants is $\dfrac{5k}{2}$, so
\[
6k+\dfrac{5k}{2}=\dfrac{17k}{2}=850\quad\Longrightarrow\quad k=100 .
\]
Ralph started with $6\cdot100=\boxed{600}$ plants (Inessa had $500$ and moved $250$).
