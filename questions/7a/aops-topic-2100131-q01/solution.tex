Number the columns $1$ to $7$ from the right and let $c_k$ be the carry out of column $k$. Three digits and a carry add up to at most $29$, so every carry is $0$, $1$ or $2$. There are ten letters, so every digit is used exactly once.

\textbf{Column 1:} $L+N+Y=Y+10c_1$, so $L+N=10c_1$. As $L\ne N$, $L+N$ is neither $0$ nor $20$, so $L+N=10$ and $c_1=1$.

\textbf{Column 2:} $E+A+L+1=A+10c_2$, so $E+L+1=10c_2$ with $1\le E+L+1\le18$. Hence $E+L=9$ and $c_2=1$.

\textbf{Columns 5 and 6:} $C+I+E+c_4=I+10c_5$ and $I+V+c_5=V+10c_6$, that is,
\[C+E+c_4=10c_5,\qquad I+c_5=10c_6 .\]
Since $C+E+c_4\le17+2$, $c_5\le1$; and $c_5=0$ would force $C=E=0$. So $c_5=1$, then $I+1=10c_6$ gives $I=9$, $c_6=1$, and $C+E+c_4=10$.

\textbf{Column 7:} $M+c_6=E$ with no carry out, so $E=M+1$.

\textbf{Column 3:} $A+I+I+c_2=H+10c_3$, that is, $A+19=H+10c_3$. If $c_3=1$ then $H=A+9$, so $A=0$ and $H=9=I$, impossible. So $c_3=2$ and $H=A-1$.

\textbf{Column 4:} $H+V+M+c_3=L+10c_4$, that is, $(A-1)+V+M+2=L+10c_4$.

Everything is now expressed through $M$ and $c_4$:
\[E=M+1,\quad L=9-E=8-M,\quad N=10-L=M+2,\quad C=10-E-c_4=9-M-c_4,\quad A+V=7-2M+10c_4 .\]
The digits $M$, $M+1$, $M+2$, $8-M$ must be distinct and different from $9$, so $M\le6$, $M\ne3$ and $M\ne4$; and $A+V\le8+7=15$ because $9$ is taken. Check the remaining values of $M$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $M=0$: $L=8$ and $C=9-c_4$; $C\ne9,8$ forces $c_4=2$, but then $A+V=27$. Impossible.
\item $M=1$: $E=2$, $N=3$, $L=7$, $C=8-c_4$. Here $c_4=1$ gives $C=7=L$ and $c_4=2$ gives $A+V=25$, so $c_4=0$, $C=8$ and $A+V=5$. The unused digits are $0,4,5,6$; $A+V=5$ forces $\{A,V\}=\{0,5\}$, and $A=0$ would make $H=-1$. So $A=5$, $V=0$, $H=4$ and $Y=6$.
\item $M=2$: $E=3$, $N=4$, $L=6$, $C=7-c_4$. Here $c_4=1$ gives $C=6=L$ and $c_4=2$ gives $A+V=23$, so $C=7$ and $A+V=3$; but no two of the unused digits $0,1,5,8$ add up to $3$.
\item $M=5$: $L=3$, $C=4-c_4$, $A+V=10c_4-3$, so $c_4\ge1$; $c_4=1$ gives $C=3=L$ and $c_4=2$ gives $A+V=17$. Impossible.
\item $M=6$: $L=2$, $C=3-c_4$, $A+V=10c_4-5$, so $c_4\ge1$; $c_4=1$ gives $C=2=L$, and $c_4=2$ gives $A+V=15$, but the unused digits are $0,3,4,5$.
\end{itemize}
So the unique solution is
\[\boxed{M=1,\ I=9,\ C=8,\ H=4,\ A=5,\ E=2,\ L=7,\ V=0,\ N=3,\ Y=6},\]
that is, $1984527+090953+21976=2097456$.
