In the addition below, each letter stands for a digit, and different letters stand for different digits. The letters $M$, $V$ and $E$, which begin numbers, are allowed to be $0$.
\[\begin{array}{cccccccc}
 & M & I & C & H & A & E & L\\
 &   & V & I & V & I & A & N\\
+&   &   & E & M & I & L & Y\\ \hline
 & E & V & I & L & H & A & Y
\end{array}\]
Find the unique solution, that is, the digits $M,I,C,H,A,E,L,V,N,Y$.
