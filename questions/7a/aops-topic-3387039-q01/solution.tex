For each prime, equality of exponents gives $2v_p(x)=3v_p(y)$, hence $v_p(x)=3k_p$ and $v_p(y)=2k_p$. Thus $x=t^3,y=t^2$ for an integer $t\ge2$. The sum increases with $t$, so its least value is $2^3+2^2=\boxed{12}$.
