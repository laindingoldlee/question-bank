Arrange the $5$ people and $2$ identical dividers in a row: the people before the first divider form the line at booth $1$, those between the dividers the line at booth $2$, and the rest the line at booth $3$. This is a bijection. There are $5!$ orders of the people and $\dbinom72$ ways to place the dividers among the $7$ positions, so the answer is $120\cdot21=\boxed{2520}$.
