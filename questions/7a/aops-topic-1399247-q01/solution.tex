The remainder on division by a quadratic has degree less than $2$, so it is a \emph{linear} polynomial $r(x)=ax+b$, not a constant; the student's mistake was to assume $r$ is a number. Write
\[
x^4+x^3=(x^2-1)q(x)+ax+b .
\]
At $x=1$: $2=a+b$. At $x=-1$: $0=-a+b$. So $a=b=1$, and the remainder is $\boxed{x+1}$.

Check: $x^4+x^3-(x+1)=(x+1)(x^3-1)=(x+1)(x-1)(x^2+x+1)=(x^2-1)(x^2+x+1)$.
