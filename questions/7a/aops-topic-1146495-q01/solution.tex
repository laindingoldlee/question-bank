\textbf{(a)} Each even square is $(2k)^2=4k^2$, so
\[2^2+4^2+\cdots+(2n)^2=4\left(1^2+\cdots+n^2\right)=4\cdot\dfrac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6}=\boxed{\dfrac{2n(n+1)(2n+1)}{3}}.\]

\textbf{(b)} The odd squares up to $(2n-1)^2$ are all the squares up to $(2n)^2$ minus the even ones:
\begin{align*}
1^2+3^2+\cdots+(2n-1)^2&=\dfrac{2n(2n+1)(4n+1)}{6}-\dfrac{2n(n+1)(2n+1)}{3}\\
&=\dfrac{2n(2n+1)\left[(4n+1)-2(n+1)\right]}{6}=\boxed{\dfrac{n(2n-1)(2n+1)}{3}}.
\end{align*}
Check with $n=2$: $4+16=20=\dfrac{2\cdot2\cdot3\cdot5}{3}$ and $1+9=10=\dfrac{2\cdot3\cdot5}{3}$.
