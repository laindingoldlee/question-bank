Change of base gives $\log_{\dfrac{1}{j}} x = - \log_j x$. Thus the denominator is the negative of the numerator. All terms in the numerator have the same nonzero sign, so it cannot vanish. Hence $R_N=-1$ for every $N$, and the limit is $\boxed{-1}$. This is a ratio of partial sums; the two separate infinite series do not converge, so one should not call their quotient an ordinary quotient of infinite sums.
