Compare with squares of two-decimal numbers:
\[
0.07^2=0.0049<0.005<0.0064=0.08^2 .
\]
Since squaring is increasing for positive numbers, $0.07<\sqrt{0.005}<0.08$. So $\sqrt{0.005}=0.07\ldots$ and the hundredths digit is $\boxed{7}$.

(Equivalently, $\sqrt{0.005}=\sqrt{\dfrac{50}{10000}}=\dfrac{\sqrt{50}}{100}$ and $7<\sqrt{50}<8$; in fact $\sqrt{0.005}\approx0.0707$.)
