Write $z=a+bi$. Then $a^2-b^2=0$ and $2ab=-1$. Thus $b=-a$, giving $a^2=\dfrac{1}{2}$. The two roots are $\boxed{\pm\dfrac{1-i}{\sqrt2}}$. The principal square root has nonnegative real part and is $\boxed{\dfrac{1-i}{\sqrt2}}$.
