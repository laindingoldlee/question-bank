Anna runs $\dfrac{1}{6}$ lap per minute and Boo $\dfrac{1}{8}$ lap per minute, so Anna gains $\dfrac{1}{6}-\dfrac{1}{8}=\dfrac{1}{24}$ lap per minute. She passes Boo each time her lead reaches a whole number of laps, that is at $t=24,48,72,96$ minutes (the start, $t=0$, is not a pass). The next pass would be at $t=120>100$. So Anna passes Boo $\boxed{4}$ times.
