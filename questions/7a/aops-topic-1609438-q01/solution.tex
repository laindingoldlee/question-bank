A decrease of $20\%$ multiplies the price by $0.8$, and an increase of $30\%$ multiplies it by $1.3$. The final price is
\[
d\cdot0.8\cdot1.3=1.04\,d,
\]
so the price increased by $\boxed{4\%}$ in total (not $30\%-20\%=10\%$, because the $30\%$ is taken of the reduced price).
