A library's book drop is broken: every third book put into it (the $3$rd, $6$th, $9$th, \dots) is shredded. Someone puts $30$ different books into the drop, one at a time. The shredded books are then replaced by identical new copies, all $30$ books are shuffled into a uniformly random order, and they are put into the drop again, one at a time. What is the probability that exactly the same books are shredded the second time?
