Let $e_1=x+y+z=1$. Squaring gives $e_2=xy+yz+zx=\dfrac{1-2}{2}=-\dfrac{1}{2}$. The cubic identity gives $3=e_1^3-3e_1e_2+3e_3$, so $e_3=xyz=\dfrac{1}{6}$. Each of $x,y,z$ is a root of $t^3-t^2-\dfrac{t}{2}-\dfrac{1}{6}=0$. Writing $p_k=x^k+y^k+z^k$, it follows that $p_k=p_{k-1}+\dfrac{p_{k-2}}{2}+\dfrac{p_{k-3}}{6}$ for $k\ge4$. Thus $p_4=3+1+\dfrac{1}{6}=\dfrac{25}{6}$ and $p_5=\dfrac{25}{6}+\dfrac{3}{2}+\dfrac{2}{6}=\boxed6$.
