Order them as $a<b<c<d$. Every number appears in exactly three of the six pairs, so
\[
3(a+b+c+d)=16+19+20+21+22+25=123,\qquad a+b+c+d=41 .
\]
The smallest sum is $a+b=16$ and the next is $a+c=19$; the largest is $c+d=25$ and the next is $b+d=22$. The two middle sums $20$ and $21$ are $a+d$ and $b+c$ in some order (they add to $41$).

Subtracting, $c-b=(a+c)-(a+b)=3$.
\begin{itemize}
\item If $b+c=21$: $b=9$, $c=12$, then $a=16-9=7$ and $d=25-12=13$. Check: $a+d=20$, $b+d=22$. This works.
\item If $b+c=20$: $c-b=3$ gives $b=8.5$, not an integer.
\end{itemize}
So the integers are $\boxed{7,\ 9,\ 12,\ 13}$; indeed the pair sums are $16,19,20,21,22,25$.
