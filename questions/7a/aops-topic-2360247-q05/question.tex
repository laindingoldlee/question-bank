On each repetition of his workout, John chooses with equal probability either to roll a fair six-sided die or to flip two fair coins. If he rolls the die, he does as many push-ups as the number on the top face; if he flips the coins, he does $3$ push-ups for each head. What is the expected number of push-ups he does in one repetition?
