Three independent fair dice have respectively $a_1,a_2,a_3$ faces, where $a_1=2$ and $a_n=(n+1)a_{n-1}+\lfloor\dfrac{a_{n-1}}{2}\rfloor$ for $n\ge2$. Bob fixes one face prediction for each die before rolling. Find the probability all predictions are correct.
