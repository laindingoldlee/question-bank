Multiplying by $(ab)^2=9$ gives $a^3+b^3=648$. Put $s=a+b$. Then $s^3-9s=648$, so $(s-9)(s^2+9s+72)=0$. The second factor has discriminant $81-288<0$, so $s=9$. Thus $a^2+b^2=s^2-2ab=81-6=\boxed{75}$. The roots of $t^2-9t+3$ are real and satisfy the conditions.
