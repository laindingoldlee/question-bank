If the first digit is $0$ modulo three, all digits are from $\{0,3,6,9\}$. The leading digit has three choices and the remaining six have four each, giving $3\cdot4^6$. Otherwise residues alternate between one and two modulo three; either starting residue gives three choices per digit, contributing $2\cdot3^7$. The total is $12288+4374=\boxed{16662}$.
