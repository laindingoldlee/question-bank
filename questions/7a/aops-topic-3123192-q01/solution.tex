Zero cannot occur, so choose $1\le a<b<c\le9$. The sum of the hundreds digits is $\sum_{a=1}^7a\binom{9-a}{2}=210$. The tens-digit sum is $\sum_{b=2}^8b(b-1)(9-b)=420$. The units-digit sum is $\sum_{c=3}^9c\binom{c-1}{2}=630$. Therefore the required sum is $100(210)+10(420)+630=\boxed{25830}$.
