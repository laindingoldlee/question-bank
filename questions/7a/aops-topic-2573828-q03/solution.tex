\textbf{(a)} $\dfrac{\dbinom{10}{5}}{2^{10}}=\dfrac{252}{1024}=\boxed{\dfrac{63}{256}}$.

\textbf{(b)} $\boxed{\dfrac{\dbinom{100}{50}}{2^{100}}}\approx0.0796$.

\textbf{(c)} Let $n=2m$ and $P_m=\dfrac{\dbinom{2m}{m}}{4^m}$. Since $\dbinom{2m}{m}=\dfrac{(2m)!}{m!\,m!}$,
\[P_m=\dfrac{1\cdot3\cdot5\cdots(2m-1)}{2\cdot4\cdot6\cdots(2m)}=\prod_{k=1}^{m}\dfrac{2k-1}{2k}.\]
For each $k$, $\dfrac{2k-1}{2k}<\dfrac{2k}{2k+1}$, so
\[P_m^2<\prod_{k=1}^{m}\dfrac{2k-1}{2k}\cdot\prod_{k=1}^{m}\dfrac{2k}{2k+1}=\dfrac{1}{2m+1}.\]
Hence $0<P_m<\dfrac1{\sqrt{2m+1}}\to0$, and the limit is $\boxed{0}$. (More precisely $P_m\approx\dfrac1{\sqrt{\pi m}}$.)
