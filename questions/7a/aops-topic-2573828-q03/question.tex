A fair coin is flipped repeatedly.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Marty flips $10$ coins. What is the probability that exactly $5$ land heads?
\item[(b)] Marty flips $100$ coins. What is the probability that exactly $50$ land heads?
\item[(c)] Marty flips $n$ coins, $n$ even. Find the limit, as $n\to\infty$, of the probability that exactly $\dfrac n2$ land heads, and prove your answer.
\end{enumerate}
