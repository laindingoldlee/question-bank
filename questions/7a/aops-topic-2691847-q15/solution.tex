Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral are supplementary: $2x+80=180$, so $x=\boxed{50}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(2.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=dir(200),B=dir(110),C=dir(10),D=dir(-70);
draw(unitcircle,gray);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
label("$A$",A,W);label("$B$",B,N);label("$C$",C,E);label("$D$",D,S);
\end{asy}
