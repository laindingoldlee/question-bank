We need $5x+2y=69$ with $x,y\ge0$ integers. Since $2y$ is even and $69$ is odd, $5x$ is odd, so $x$ is odd. Also $5x\le69$ gives $x\le13$. Each odd $x\in\{1,3,5,7,9,11,13\}$ gives a nonnegative integer $y=\dfrac{69-5x}{2}$ (namely $32,27,22,17,12,7,2$). So there are $\boxed{7}$ ways.
