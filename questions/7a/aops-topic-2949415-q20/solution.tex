Each digit contributes its place value times $|a_i-n_i|+|b_i-n_i|$. The triangle inequality is an equality exactly when $n_i$ lies between the two corresponding digits. Aligning $02021$ with $12345$ gives $2,1,4,3,5$ choices at successive places. Higher digits must be zero. Thus there are $2\cdot1\cdot4\cdot3\cdot5=\boxed{120}$ minimizers.
