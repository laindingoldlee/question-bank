Write $a=gx,b=gy$ with $\gcd(x,y)=1$ and $1\le y\le x$. Then $\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=gxy$, so the equation reduces to $g^2x^2y=g^3y$, giving $g=x^2$. Hence $a=x^3,b=x^2y$, with $1\le x\le12$. For each $x$, there are $\phi(x)$ admissible values of $y$, taking $\phi(1)=1$. These counts for 1 through 12 are $1,1,2,2,4,2,6,4,6,4,10,4$, whose sum is $\boxed{46}$.
