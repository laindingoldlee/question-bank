Jonathan has some coins, each a penny, nickel, dime, quarter or half-dollar. He notices that he cannot make change for a nickel, a dime, a quarter, a half-dollar or a dollar; that is, no collection of his coins worth less than the given coin each has a total value of exactly $5$, $10$, $25$ or $50$ cents, and no collection of his coins has a total value of exactly $100$ cents. What is the largest amount of money his coins can be worth?
