The complex numbers $\mathbb C$ consist of $a+bi$ with real $a,b$ and $i^2=-1$. A complex number is real exactly when $b=0$. For example $2+3i$ is complex but not real. To specify that $x$ may be any complex number, write $x\in\mathbb C$.
