Write $m=2^a u,n=2^b v$, where $u,v$ are positive odd integers. If $2a\ne4b$, dividing the sum by its smaller power of 2 leaves an odd integer greater than 1, impossible for a power of 2. Hence $a=2b$ and $m^2+n^4=2^{4b}(u^2+v^4)$. Since $u^2+v^4\equiv2\pmod4$, it must equal 2, so $u=v=1$. Every solution is therefore $(m,n)=(4^b,2^b)$. The bounds hold exactly for $b=0,\ldots,7$, since $4^7=16384$ and $4^8>20000$. There are $\boxed8$ ordered pairs.
