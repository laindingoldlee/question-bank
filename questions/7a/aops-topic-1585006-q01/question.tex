Plates come in three colours: red, blue and yellow. Prove that the number of ways to arrange $n$ plates in a row (each plate one of the three colours) so that the number of red plates is even is $\dfrac{3^n+1}{2}$. Show also that this equals $2+\dfrac{3\left(3^{n-1}-1\right)}{2}$.
