Take $P=(0,0),Q=(2,0),R=(0,2\sqrt3)$, so $M=(1,0)$. The line $QR$ has slope $-\sqrt3$, hence $PL$ is $y=\dfrac{x}{\sqrt3}$. The median is $y=2\sqrt3(1-x)$. Solving gives $F=\left(\dfrac{6}{7},\dfrac{2\sqrt3}{7}\right)$, so
\[
\boxed{PF=\sqrt{\dfrac{36+12}{49}}=\dfrac{4\sqrt3}{7}}.
\]
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair P=(0,0),Q=(2,0),R=(0,2*sqrt(3)),M=(1,0),L=(1.5,sqrt(3)/2),F=(6./7,2*sqrt(3)/7);draw(P--Q--R--cycle);draw(P--L,blue);draw(R--M,red);label("$P$",P,(-1,-1));label("$Q$",Q,(1,-1));label("$R$",R,(0,1));label("$M$",M,(0,-1));label("$L$",L,(1,1));label("$F$",F,(-1,0));
\end{asy}
