Let $S=\sum_{k=1}^{12}k2^{k-1}$. Then $2S=\sum_{k=1}^{12}k2^k=\sum_{k=2}^{13}(k-1)2^{k-1}$, and
\[2S-S=12\cdot2^{12}-\sum_{k=1}^{12}2^{k-1}=12\cdot4096-(4096-1)=11\cdot4096+1.\]
So $S=\boxed{45057}$.
