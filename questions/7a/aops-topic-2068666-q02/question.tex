A teacher gives her students a paper square. The first student cuts the square into two pieces with one straight cut that does not pass through any corner of the paper. The second student cuts one of the two pieces with one straight cut not passing through any corner of that piece, and so on. After ten students have made their cuts there are eleven pieces: seven triangles, two quadrilaterals, one pentagon, and one more piece. How many sides does the remaining piece have?
