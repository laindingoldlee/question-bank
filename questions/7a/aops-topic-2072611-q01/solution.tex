Since $y\ge1$ and $z\ge1$, we have $y+\dfrac{1}{z}\ge1+\dfrac{1}{z}>1$, so $0<\dfrac{1}{y+\dfrac{1}{z}}<1$. Hence $x$ is the integer part of $\dfrac{10}{7}$, i.e.\ $x=1$, and
\[
\dfrac{1}{y+\dfrac{1}{z}}=\dfrac{3}{7},\qquad y+\dfrac{1}{z}=\dfrac{7}{3}=2+\dfrac{1}{3} .
\]
If $z=1$ then $y=\dfrac{4}{3}$, not an integer. If $z\ge2$ then $0<\dfrac{1}{z}\le\dfrac{1}{2}<1$, so $y$ is the integer part of $\dfrac{7}{3}$, giving $y=2$ and $\dfrac{1}{z}=\dfrac{1}{3}$, $z=3$.

The unique solution is $\boxed{(x,y,z)=(1,2,3)}$.
