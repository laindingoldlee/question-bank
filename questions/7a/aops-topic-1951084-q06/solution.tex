\(P=1\cdot 3\cdot 5\cdots 199=\dfrac{200!}{2\cdot 4\cdots 200}=\dfrac{200!}{2^{100}\cdot 100!}\). By Legendre's formula, the exponent of \(3\) in \(200!\) is \(66+22+7+2=97\) and in \(100!\) is \(33+11+3+1=48\). So \(k=97-48=\boxed{49}\).
