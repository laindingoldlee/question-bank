There are $120$ blocks. Each block is made of one of $2$ materials, has one of $3$ colours, one of $4$ sizes and one of $5$ shapes, and no two blocks agree in all four properties. Two blocks are taken at random. What is the probability that they have exactly two of the four properties the same?
