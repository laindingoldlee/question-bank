Adding $1$ and factoring gives $(x+1)(y+1)(z+1)=2015=5\cdot13\cdot31$. Each of the three distinct prime factors can be assigned independently to one of the three positive factors. This produces $3^3=\boxed{27}$ ordered triples, and every solution is obtained once.
