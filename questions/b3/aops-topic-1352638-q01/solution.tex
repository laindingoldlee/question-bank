If $x=0$ then $y^2=0$, giving $(0,0)$. Now let $x\ne0$ and view the equation as a quadratic in $y$:
\[
y^2-3x^2y+2x^2=0,\qquad y=\dfrac{3x^2\pm\sqrt{9x^4-8x^2}}{2}=\dfrac{3x^2\pm|x|\sqrt{9x^2-8}}{2}.
\]
For $y$ to be an integer, $9x^2-8$ must be a perfect square, say $9x^2-8=m^2$ with $m\ge0$. Then $(3|x|-m)(3|x|+m)=8$. The two factors have the same parity and the second is positive, so both are even: $3|x|-m=2$, $3|x|+m=4$. This gives $|x|=1$, $m=1$.

For $x=\pm1$ the equation becomes $y^2-3y+2=0$, i.e. $(y-1)(y-2)=0$, so $y=1$ or $y=2$. The solutions are
\[
\boxed{(0,0),\ (1,1),\ (-1,1),\ (1,2),\ (-1,2)}.
\]
