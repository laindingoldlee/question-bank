Let the trapezoid be $ABCD$ with bases $AB=a>CD=b$ and altitude $3$. By symmetry, $C$ projects onto $AB$ at distance $\dfrac{a+b}2$ from $A$. So in the right triangle formed by the diagonal $AC$, $\tan\theta=3\div\dfrac{a+b}2=\dfrac6{a+b}=\dfrac14$, giving $\dfrac{a+b}2=12$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(16,0),C=(12,3),D=(4,3);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C,dashed);draw(D--(4,0),dotted);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$3$",(4,1.5),W,fontsize(8pt));label("$12$",(6,0),S,fontsize(8pt));label("$\theta$",(2.2,0.25),fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}

The area is $\dfrac{a+b}2\cdot3=36$, answer $\boxed{\text{d}}$. (The figure shows $a=16$, $b=8$; the area does not depend on the individual bases.)
