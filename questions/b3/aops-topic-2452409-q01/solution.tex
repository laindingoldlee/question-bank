\textbf{No three marked points are collinear.} All marked points lie on the boundary of the square. A line meets the boundary of a convex polygon either in at most two points or in a segment lying along one side. A line through three marked points would therefore contain a side of the square; but each side carries only two marked points, and the remaining marked points are not on the line of that side. So every choice of $3$ of the $8$ points gives a triangle.

\textbf{Count.} The number of triangles is $\dbinom83=\boxed{56}$.

\textbf{The student's count.} It counts ordered choices of (base, third vertex), and each triangle arises $3$ times, once for each of its sides taken as the base. So $168$ counts every triangle three times; the correct answer is $\dfrac{168}{3}=56$.
