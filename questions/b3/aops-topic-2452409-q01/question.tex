On each side of a square, two points are marked that divide the side into three equal parts ($8$ points in all; the corners of the square are not marked). How many triangles have all three vertices among the $8$ marked points?

A student argues: ``Choose two of the $8$ points as the base, in $\dbinom82$ ways, and then one of the remaining $6$ points as the third vertex, so the answer is $\dbinom82\cdot6=168$.'' Is this correct?
