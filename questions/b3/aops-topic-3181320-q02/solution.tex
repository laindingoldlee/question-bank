There are $\binom{10}{3}=120$ equally likely selections of slips. The product must have a factor $3$, so at least one of the three slips labeled $3$ is needed; it must also have an even factor, so at least one of the six even slips is needed. Excluding selections missing these categories gives $120-\binom73-\binom43=81$ (their overlap is impossible, since only one slip is labeled $1$). Hence the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{27}{40}}$.
