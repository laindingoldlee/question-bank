A bucket holds at most $670$ mL of water, and Jessica has poured a whole number of millilitres into it. The amount is a three-digit prime number. Its tens digit minus its units digit equals $6$. One of its digits is even and the other two are odd. None of the digits $4,5,6,8$ appears in it, and its units digit is $2$ or $3$. How much water is in the bucket?
