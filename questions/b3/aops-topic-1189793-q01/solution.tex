Unwinding the recursion, $a_n$ is the sum of the increments:
\[
a_n=1+\sum_{k=2}^{n}(3k-2)=\sum_{k=1}^{n}(3k-2)=3\cdot\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}-2n=\boxed{\dfrac{n(3n-1)}{2}}.
\]
Check: $a_1=1$, $a_2=5$, $a_3=12$, $a_4=22$ (the pentagonal numbers), and $\dfrac{n(3n-1)}{2}-\dfrac{(n-1)(3n-4)}{2}=\dfrac{6n-4}{2}=3n-2$. (A proof by induction uses exactly this last identity.)
