Look at the terms in pairs: $(0,2)$, $(6,10)$, $(24,30)$, $(60,x)$.

The first terms of the pairs, $0,6,24,60$, are $1^3-1$, $2^3-2$, $3^3-3$, $4^3-4$, and the second terms $2,10,30$ are $1^3+1$, $2^3+2$, $3^3+3$. So the $k$-th pair is
\[
k^3-k,\ k^3+k\qquad(k=1,2,3,\dots).
\]
(Equivalently, the terms are $k(k-1)(k+1)$ and $k(k^2+1)$.) The next term is the second term of the fourth pair:
\[
x=4^3+4=\boxed{68}.
\]
(As with any "next term" question, a finite list does not force the continuation; $68$ is the answer given by this simple rule, which fits all seven terms.)
