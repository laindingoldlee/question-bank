Fifty-five players each choose an integer from 1 through 10. A player choosing $ j $ receives score $\dfrac{j}{n_j}$, where $ n_j $ is the number choosing $ j $. Show that assigning exactly $ j $ players to each integer $ j $ gives a configuration in which no single player can improve by changing their choice.
