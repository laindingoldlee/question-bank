The counts sum to $ 1+\cdots+10=55 $, so the assignment is possible. Every player initially scores 1. A player moving to a different integer $ k $ makes its count $ k+1 $, obtaining $\dfrac{k}{k+1}<1 $. Thus no unilateral move improves a score, proving the claimed equilibrium.
