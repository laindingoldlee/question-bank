Let $n=\overline{FH}$, so $10\le n\le99$. Then $\overline{MFH}=100M+n$, and the condition says $n^2=100M+n$, i.e. $100\mid n(n-1)$ and $n^2<1000$, so $n\le31$.

Since $n$ and $n-1$ are coprime, $25$ divides one of them, so $n\in\{25,26\}$ in this range. Also $4\mid n(n-1)$: $26\cdot25=650$ is not a multiple of $4$, but $25\cdot24=600$ is. So $n=25$ and $25^2=625$: $M=6$, $F=2$, $H=5$, and
\[M+F+H=\boxed{13}.\]
