Treat the parents, the four aunts and uncles, and Muhammad-Ibrahim as blocks. The two neighbours of the Muhammad-Ibrahim block must be the parent block and the aunt-uncle block, in either order. Combine these three into a chain. Together with the four distinct children, there are five circular units, arranged in $4!$ ways up to rotation. The chain has two block orders; its internal orders contribute $2!$ for the parents, $4!$ for aunts and uncles, and $2!$ for Muhammad and Ibrahim. Divide by two for reflection, which fixes no arrangement of distinct people. The count is $\dfrac{4!\cdot2\cdot2!\cdot4!\cdot2!}{2}=\boxed{2304}$.
