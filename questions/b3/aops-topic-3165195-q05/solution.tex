Subtract the four corner right triangles from the square. Their areas at $A,B,C,D$ are respectively $\dfrac{1}{2}$, $\dfrac{1}{2}(4)(3)=6$, $\dfrac{1}{2}(2)(2)=2$, and $\dfrac{1}{2}(3)(4)=6$. Thus the quadrilateral area is $25-\dfrac{1}{2}-6-2-6=\dfrac{21}{2}$, giving $\boxed{m+n=23}$.

\end{asy}
