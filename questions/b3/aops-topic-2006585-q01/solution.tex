Suppose, for contradiction, that $\dfrac {a}{p}+\dfrac {b}{q}=1$. Multiplying by $pq$ gives
\[
aq+bp=pq,\qquad\text{so}\qquad aq=p(q-b).
\]
Thus $p$ divides $aq$. Since $p$ is prime, $p\mid a$ or $p\mid q$. But $q$ is a prime different from $p$, so $p\nmid q$; hence $p\mid a$. This is impossible because $0<a<p$. Therefore $\dfrac {a}{p}+\dfrac {b}{q}\ne1$.
