Arrange the $18$ non-multiples ($18!$ ways). They create $19$ gaps; choose $9$ distinct gaps for the multiples of $3$ ($\dbinom{19}9$) and order them ($9!$). The total is
\[18!\cdot\dbinom{19}9\cdot9!=\boxed{\dfrac{18!\,19!}{10!}}.\]
