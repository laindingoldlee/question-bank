A contractor plans to plant $1800$ trees in a certain number of days, planting the same number each day. During the first third of the planned time, $120$ fewer trees than planned were planted in total. To meet the target, every remaining day $20$ more trees than the planned daily number were planted, and the work was finished one day earlier than planned. How many trees did the contractor plan to plant each day?
