Mr.\ Emptyset's digital clock never shows the digit $0$. It advances once every minute, but whenever the next time would contain a $0$, it jumps ahead to the next time with no $0$ in it. For example, one minute after $11{:}59$ it shows $12{:}11$. The clock shows $5{:}25$ now. What will it show $60$ minutes later?
