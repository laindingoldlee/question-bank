A positive integer $N$ is written as $N=10a+b$, where $b$ is its last digit and $a$ is the number formed by the other digits. Prove that $7\mid N$ if and only if $7\mid a+5b$. (So, for example, to test $532$ one tests $53+5\cdot2=63$, which is divisible by $7$; hence $532$ is divisible by $7$.)
