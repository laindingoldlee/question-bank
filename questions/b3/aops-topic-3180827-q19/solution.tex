The replacement satisfies $(ab+a+b)+1=(a+1)(b+1)$. Thus the product of one more than each board entry is invariant, and the final value is always $2023!-1$. The number of its trailing nines is exactly the trailing-zero count of $2023!$, namely $\lfloor\dfrac{2023}{5}\rfloor+\lfloor\dfrac{2023}{25}\rfloor+\lfloor\dfrac{2023}{125}\rfloor+\lfloor\dfrac{2023}{625}\rfloor=404+80+16+3=\boxed{503}$.
