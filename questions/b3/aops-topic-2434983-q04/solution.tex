Call the value $x$ (the periodic continued fraction converges, and $x>6$). The pattern repeats after two levels, so
\[x=6+\dfrac{1}{2+\dfrac1x}=6+\dfrac{x}{2x+1}.\]
Then $x(2x+1)=6(2x+1)+x$, i.e. $2x^2-12x-6=0$, or $x^2-6x-3=0$. So $x=3\pm2\sqrt3$, and since $x>6$, $x=\boxed{3+2\sqrt3}$.
