The first case is right. The error is in the second case: from $0<-4x-5<1$ we get $5<-4x<6$, and dividing by $-4$ reverses the inequalities:
\[
-\dfrac{3}{2}<x<-\dfrac{5}{4} .
\]
(The value $-\dfrac{5}{6}$ is wrong; also, $x$ near $-\dfrac{5}{6}$ does not satisfy $4x+5<0$, the condition of this case.)

The union of the cases is $-\dfrac{3}{2}<x<-\dfrac{5}{4}$ or $-\dfrac{5}{4}<x<-1$:
\[
\boxed{-\dfrac{3}{2}<x<-1,\ x\ne-\dfrac{5}{4}} .
\]
Check: $|4x+5|<1$ means $4x+5$ is within $1$ of $0$, i.e.\ $x$ is within $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of $-\dfrac{5}{4}$; and $|4x+5|>0$ excludes $x=-\dfrac{5}{4}$ itself.
