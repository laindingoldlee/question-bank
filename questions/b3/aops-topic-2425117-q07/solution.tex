Let the triangle be $ABC$ with centre $G$. The distance from the centre of an equilateral triangle with side $s$ to a vertex is $\dfrac{s}{\sqrt3}$, so $GA=\dfrac{4\sqrt3}{\sqrt3}=4$. The sector at $A$ has radius $\dfrac{4\sqrt3}{2}=2\sqrt3$.

The line $AG$ bisects the $60^\circ$ angle at $A$, so it passes through the middle $T$ of the arc at $A$, with $AT=2\sqrt3$. The small circle touches the arc at $T$ from the outside, so its radius is
\[GT=GA-AT=\boxed{4-2\sqrt3}\approx0.536.\]
By symmetry the same circle touches the other two arcs at their middles.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real a=4*sqrt(3); pair A=(0,0), B=(a,0), C=(a/2,6), G=(a/2,2); real rr=2*sqrt(3), r=4-2*sqrt(3);
fill(A--arc(A,rr,0,60)--cycle,rgb(0.85,0.9,1)); fill(B--arc(B,rr,120,180)--cycle,rgb(0.85,0.9,1)); fill(C--arc(C,rr,240,300)--cycle,rgb(0.85,0.9,1));
draw(arc(A,rr,0,60),blue); draw(arc(B,rr,120,180),blue); draw(arc(C,rr,240,300),blue);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
draw(circle(G,r),red+linewidth(0.9));
draw(A--G,dashed);
pair T=A+rr*dir(30);
dot(G); dot(T); dot((A+B)/2); dot((B+C)/2); dot((A+C)/2);
label("$A$",A,SW); label("$B$",B,SE); label("$C$",C,N); label("$G$",G,E); label("$T$",T,W,fontsize(8pt));
label("$2\sqrt3$",A+rr/2*dir(30),NW,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}
