An equilateral triangle has side length $4\sqrt3$. At each vertex there is a $60^\circ$ sector of a circle whose centre is that vertex, whose two straight edges lie along the two sides of the triangle at that vertex, and whose radius is half a side, so that each arc reaches the midpoints of two sides and neighbouring arcs touch there. A circle centred at the centre of the triangle touches each of the three arcs at the middle of the arc. What is the radius of this circle?
