Let the legs be $a\le b$ and hypotenuse $c$. Its altitudes are $a,b,\dfrac{ab}{c}$, with the last strictly smaller than either leg. Thus $a=5$ and $\dfrac{5b}{\sqrt{25+b^2}}=4$. Squaring gives $9b^2=400$, so $b=\dfrac{20}{3}$. The area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot5\cdot\dfrac{20}{3}=\boxed{\dfrac{50}{3}}$.
