A product of two numbers is a multiple of the prime $5$ exactly when at least one factor is. There are $\dbinom{200}{2}=19900$ pairs, and $\dbinom{160}{2}=12720$ pairs with neither number a multiple of $5$ (there are $160$ such numbers). So $19900-12720=\boxed{7180}$ products are multiples of $5$.
