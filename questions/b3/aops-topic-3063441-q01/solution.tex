The last tile has length 1 or 2. Removing a last tile of length 1 leaves a tiling of $n$ cells, counted by $f(n)$. Removing a last tile of length 2 leaves $n-1$ cells, counted by $f(n-1)$. These cases are disjoint and exhaustive, so their counts add. There is one empty tiling and one tiling of a single cell, giving the stated initial values.
