In square $ABCD$ with $AB=1$ (vertices in order), point $M$ is the midpoint of $AB$, point $T$ is on $BC$ with $TB=2TC$, and point $H$ is on $CD$ with $HC=3HD$. Let $E$ be the intersection of segments $AH$ and $TD$. What is the area of triangle $MTE$?

(a) $\dfrac{4}{13}$\quad (b) $\dfrac{25}{78}$\quad (c) $\dfrac{1}{3}$\quad (d) $\dfrac{9}{26}$\quad (e) $\dfrac{5}{16}$
