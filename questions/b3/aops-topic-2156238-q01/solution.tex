For any positive integer $n$,
\[\dfrac{n\cdot n-n}{(n-1)(n-1)+(n-1)}=\dfrac{n(n-1)}{(n-1)n}=1 .\]
With $n=1999$, $2000$ and $2001$ this gives $a=b=c=1$, so $abc=\boxed{1}$.
