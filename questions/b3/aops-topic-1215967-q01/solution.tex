In one day John does $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of the job and the two together do the whole job, so Charlie does $1-\dfrac{1}{3}=\dfrac{2}{3}$ of the job per day. Alone he needs $\dfrac{1}{\dfrac{2}{3}}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{2}\ \text{days}}$.
