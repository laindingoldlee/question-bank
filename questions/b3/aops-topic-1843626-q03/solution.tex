Put an S at each end. The remaining $8$ letters in the middle are S, T, T, T, I, I, A, C (one S, three T's, two I's, one A, one C). They can be arranged in
\[
\dfrac{8!}{3!\,2!}=\dfrac{40320}{12}=\boxed{3360}
\]
ways.
