Each free throw is made with probability $\dfrac45$. He makes all three with probability $\left(\dfrac45\right)^3=\dfrac{64}{125}$, and exactly two with probability $\dbinom32\left(\dfrac45\right)^2\cdot\dfrac15=\dfrac{48}{125}$ (the miss can be any of the three throws). So the probability is
\[\dfrac{64}{125}+\dfrac{48}{125}=\boxed{\dfrac{112}{125}}.\]
