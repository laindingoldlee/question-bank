Dividing $k^i=k^{i-1}+k^{i-2}$ by $k^{i-2}\ne0$ gives $k^2=k+1$, so the golden numbers are exactly $\varphi=\dfrac{1+\sqrt5}{2}$ and $\psi=\dfrac{1-\sqrt5}{2}$ (and conversely both satisfy the relation with $i=2$).

The numbers $n$ and $n+\sqrt b$ are both golden and differ by $\sqrt b>0$, so they are different: $n=\psi$ and $n+\sqrt b=\varphi$. Hence $\sqrt b=\varphi-\psi=\sqrt5$, so $b=5$, and $\dfrac{a-\sqrt5}{c}=\dfrac{1-\sqrt5}{2}$. Comparing the irrational parts gives $c=2$, and then $a=1$.

Therefore
\[
\left(10\cdot5+2+\dfrac{5}{10}\right)(1+5+2)=52.5\cdot8=\boxed{420}.
\]
