Natural numbers here include $0$. A nonzero real number $k$ is called \emph{golden} if $k^i=k^{i-1}+k^{i-2}$ for some integer $i\ge2$. There are natural numbers $a$, $b$, $c$ with $b>0$ and $c>0$ such that both $n=\dfrac{a-\sqrt b}{c}$ and $n+\sqrt b$ are golden. Find $a$, $b$, $c$, and then compute
\[
\left(10b+c+\dfrac{b}{10}\right)(a+b+c).
\]
