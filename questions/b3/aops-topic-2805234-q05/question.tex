There are $2014$ stones in a pile. Player $A$ first takes between $1$ and $30$ stones. Then $B$ takes $1$ or $2$ stones, $A$ takes $2$ or $3$, $B$ takes $3$ or $4$, $A$ takes $4$ or $5$, and so on: the $j$th move after $A$'s first move takes $j$ or $j+1$ stones, with $B$ making the odd-numbered moves. Whoever takes the last stone wins. If the next player cannot legally move (fewer stones remain than required) the game is a draw. Who wins with correct play?
