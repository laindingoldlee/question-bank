\textbf{$A$ wins.} First $A$ takes $30$ stones, leaving $1984$.

Group the later moves in rounds: in round $i$ ($i=1,2,\dots$), $B$ takes $2i-1$ or $2i$ stones, then $A$ takes $2i$ or $2i+1$. $A$ answers $2i-1$ with $2i+1$ and answers $2i$ with $2i$, so each round removes exactly $4i$ stones. After $r$ rounds, $4(1+2+\cdots+r)=2r(r+1)$ stones have gone.

Since $2\cdot31\cdot32=1984$, the stones run out exactly at the end of round $31$, on $A$'s move. We must also check that every move is legal: before $B$'s move in round $i\le31$ there are $1984-2(i-1)i\ge 4i$ stones, enough for $B$'s move and $A$'s reply. So $A$ takes the last stone. $\blacksquare$
