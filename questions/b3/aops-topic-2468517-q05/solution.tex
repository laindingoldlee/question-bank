Both $AB$ and $ED$ are perpendicular to $BC$, so $ED\parallel AB$. Triangles $CDE$ and $CBA$ share the angle at $C$ and have right angles at $D$ and $B$, so they are similar, with ratio $\dfrac{CD}{CB}=\dfrac12$ because $D$ is the midpoint of $BC$. Hence $ED=\dfrac12AB$ (in fact $ED$ is the midline of the triangle and $E$ is the midpoint of $AC$), and
\[\dfrac{AB}{ED}=\boxed{2}.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0), C=(6,0), A=(0,4), D=(3,0), Ep=(3,2);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
draw(Ep--D,blue+linewidth(0.9));
real s=0.3;
draw((s,0)--(s,s)--(0,s));
draw(D+(-s,0)--D+(-s,s)--D+(0,s));
draw((1.5,-0.12)--(1.5,0.12)); draw((1.4,-0.12)--(1.4,0.12));
draw((4.5,-0.12)--(4.5,0.12)); draw((4.6,-0.12)--(4.6,0.12));
dot(D); dot(Ep);
label("$A$",A,N); label("$B$",B,SW); label("$C$",C,SE); label("$D$",D,S); label("$E$",Ep,NE);
\end{asy}
