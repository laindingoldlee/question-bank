One diamond is worth $\dfrac{100}{10}=10$ gems. Since $120=20\cdot6$ gold coins, $120$ gold coins are worth $20\cdot25=500$ gems, which is $\dfrac{500}{10}=50$ diamonds. You have $10$ diamonds, so you need $50-10=40$ more. The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(B)}\ 40}$.
