Among $a,\dots,m$ the only vowels are $a,e,i$ ($o$ and $u$ come later), so there are $3$ vowels and $10$ consonants. Fill the restricted positions first:
\begin{itemize}
\item $3$rd letter: $3$ vowels; $5$th letter: $2$ remaining vowels;
\item $1$st letter: $10$ consonants (no conflict with the vowels already used);
\item $2$nd and $4$th letters: any of the $13-3=10$ unused letters, then $9$.
\end{itemize}
The total is $3\cdot2\cdot10\cdot10\cdot9=\boxed{5400}$.

No casework is needed, because the vowel positions and the consonant position draw from disjoint sets of letters. The product $10\times10\times3\times9\times2$ is the same count written in position order, so it is correct.
