How many five-letter strings can be written using only the first $13$ letters of the alphabet ($a$ to $m$), with no letter repeated, such that the $3$rd and $5$th letters are vowels and the $1$st letter is a consonant? (The vowels are $a,e,i,o,u$; all other letters are consonants.)
