Two opposite corner squares (the top left and the bottom right) are removed from a $4\times4$ board of unit squares. Prove that the remaining $14$ squares cannot be cut into $7$ dominoes, where a domino is a $1\times2$ rectangle made of two neighbouring unit squares.
