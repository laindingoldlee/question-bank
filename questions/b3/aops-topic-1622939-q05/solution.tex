Place the numbers $1,\dots,19$ around a circle in order. Then ``$x$ is a follower of $y$'' means exactly that
\[
x-y\equiv1,2,\dots,9\pmod{19},
\]
i.e. $x$ is one of the $9$ numbers reached from $y$ by going $1$ to $9$ steps forward around the circle. (Indeed, $y-x\in[10,18]$ gives $x-y\in[-18,-10]$, which is $1$ to $9$ modulo $19$.)

Choose $z$ first ($19$ ways). Then $y=z+j$ and $x=z+i$ (modulo $19$) with $1\le i,j\le9$, since both follow $z$. The condition that $x$ follows $y$ is $i-j\in\{1,\dots,9\}$ modulo $19$; because $-8\le i-j\le8$, this means $i>j$. There are $\dbinom92=36$ choices of $j<i$ in $\{1,\dots,9\}$. The number of ordered triples is
\[
19\cdot36=\boxed{684}.
\]
