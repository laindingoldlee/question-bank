For $b$ blue and $r$ red draws before the green, the number of colour sequences is $\binom{b+r}r$. Sum over $0\le b\le46$ and $0\le r\le3$. The hockey-stick identity gives $\sum_{r=0}^3\binom{47+r}{r+1}=\binom{51}4-1=\boxed{249899}$. Each sequence ends with the green, so there is no extra ordering factor.
