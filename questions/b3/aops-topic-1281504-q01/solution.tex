The units digits of $2^1,2^2,2^3,2^4,\dots$ cycle through $2,4,8,6$ with period $4$. Since $2016$ is divisible by $4$, the units digit of $2^{2016}$ is the same as that of $2^4$, namely $\boxed{6}$.
