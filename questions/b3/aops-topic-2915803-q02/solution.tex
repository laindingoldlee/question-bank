All four sides of a rhombus are equal, so the perimeter is $4(5)=\boxed{20}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(-4,0),B=(0,3),C=(4,0),D=(0,-3);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C,dashed);draw(B--D,dashed);label("$A$",A,W);label("$B$",B,N);label("$C$",C,E);label("$D$",D,S);
\end{asy}
