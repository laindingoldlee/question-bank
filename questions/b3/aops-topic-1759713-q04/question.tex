An oceanographer tags every dolphin that she sees during one week: at the end of the week she has tagged $1000$ dolphins. One month later she examines $2000$ dolphins and finds that $400$ of them are tagged. Assuming that each time she saw a random sample of the local dolphin population, estimate the number of dolphins in the local population.
