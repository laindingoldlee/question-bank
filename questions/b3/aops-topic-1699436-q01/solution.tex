Since $11\equiv2\pmod3$, the congruence is $2x\equiv1\pmod3$. Multiply by $2$ (the inverse of $2$ modulo $3$, since $2\cdot2=4\equiv1$): $x\equiv2\pmod3$. Check: $11\cdot2=22=3\cdot7+1$. So the solutions are $\boxed{x\equiv2\pmod 3}$, i.e.\ $x=3k+2$ for integers $k$.
