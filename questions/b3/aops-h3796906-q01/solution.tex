The equal legs force the fourth side to repeat one of the given lengths. Equal legs of length $L$ with unequal bases $u,v$ exist exactly when $L>\dfrac{|u-v|}{2}$, by dropping perpendiculars from the shorter base. Legs $1$ fail because $1<5$. Legs $10$ work since $10>\dfrac{19}{2}$, and legs $20$ work since $20>\dfrac{9}{2}$. Thus the required sum is $\boxed{30}$.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

pair A=(0,0),B=(20,0),C=(10.5,3.122498999199199),D=(9.5,3.122498999199199);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);label("$20$",(A+B)/2,S);label("$1$",(C+D)/2,N);label("$10$",(A+D)/2,W);label("$10$",(B+C)/2,E);label("A",A,SW);label("B",B,SE);label("C",C,NE);label("D",D,NW);
\end{asy}
