Let $N$ be the original number. The good apples number $0.87N$, and after selling $75\%$ of them he has $0.25\cdot0.87N=0.2175N$ left. So $0.2175N=261$ and $N=\boxed{1200}$. (Check: $1044$ good apples, $261$ left after selling $783$.)
