A student writes $x=2^{2^{2^{\cdot^{\cdot^{\cdot}}}}}$ and argues: ``Then $x^2=2^{2^{2^{\cdots}}}$ as well, so $\dfrac{x^2}{x}=1$ and $x=1$.'' Find the error, and decide whether the infinite tower has a finite value.
