Infinity is not an ordinary real number to which the cancellation law applies. In the extended nonnegative reals, both sides equal $\infty$, but subtracting $\infty$ is undefined. Addition of $\infty$ is not injective, so equality after that operation cannot imply equality before it. Hence the argument gives no contradiction to ordinary arithmetic.
