Let each drive $m$ miles. Ray uses $\dfrac{m}{40}$ gallons and Tom uses $\dfrac{m}{10}$ gallons, so together they drive $2m$ miles on
\[\dfrac{m}{40}+\dfrac{m}{10}=\dfrac{m}{40}+\dfrac{4m}{40}=\dfrac{m}{8}\text{ gallons}.\]
The combined rate is $\dfrac{2m}{\dfrac {m}{8}}=\boxed{16}$ miles per gallon. (Not the average $25$: the combined rate is the harmonic mean of $40$ and $10$, because equal \emph{distances}, not equal amounts of fuel, are driven.)
