\textbf{(a)} For $ax^3+bx^2+cx+d$ with roots $r,s,t$, we have $a(x-r)(x-s)(x-t)=ax^3-a(r+s+t)x^2+a(rs+st+tr)x-a\,rst$. Comparing coefficients (Vieta's formulas):
\[r+s+t=-\dfrac {b}{a}=-\dfrac{1}{3},\qquad rs+st+tr=\dfrac {c}{a}=\dfrac{4}{3},\qquad rst=-\dfrac {d}{a}=-\dfrac{2}{3} .\]

\textbf{(b)}
\[r^2+s^2+t^2=(r+s+t)^2-2(rs+st+tr)=\dfrac{1}{9}-\dfrac{8}{3}=\boxed{-\dfrac{23}{9}},\qquad \dfrac{1}{r}+\dfrac{1}{s}+\dfrac{1}{t}=\dfrac{rs+st+tr}{rst}=\dfrac{\dfrac{4}{3}}{-\dfrac{2}{3}}=\boxed{-2}.\]

\textbf{(c)} If $r,s,t$ were all real, $r^2+s^2+t^2$ would be at least $0$, but it equals $-\dfrac{23}{9}$. So at least two roots are non-real (they are complex conjugates; the cubic has one real root).
