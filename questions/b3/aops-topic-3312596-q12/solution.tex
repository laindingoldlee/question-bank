Let $M,F$ be the costs of buying milk or fruit tea for the entire club. The first bill gives $\dfrac{1}{2}(M+F)=15.84$, so $M+F=31.68$. The second is $\dfrac{2}{3}M+\dfrac{1}{3}F=16.92$. The requested bill is the complementary combination $\dfrac{1}{3}M+\dfrac{2}{3}F=31.68-16.92=\boxed{14.76\text{ dollars}}$.
