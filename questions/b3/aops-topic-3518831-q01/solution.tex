Let the distance be $L$, running speed $k$, and escalator speed $e$. Then $k-e=\dfrac{L}{30}$ and $k+e=\dfrac{L}{6}$. Adding gives $2k=\dfrac{L}{5}$, so $k=\dfrac{L}{10}$. The stationary-stair time is $\dfrac{L}{k}=\boxed{10\text{ seconds}}$.
