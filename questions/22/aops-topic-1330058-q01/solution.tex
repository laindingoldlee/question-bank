Factor $m^6+n^6=(m^2+n^2)(m^4-m^2n^2+n^4)$ and note $m^4-m^2n^2+n^4=(m^2-n^2)^2+(mn)^2$. The Brahmagupta-Fibonacci identity $(a^2+b^2)(c^2+d^2)=(ac-bd)^2+(ad+bc)^2$ with $a=m$, $b=n$, $c=m^2-n^2$, $d=mn$ gives
\[
\boxed{m^6+n^6=\left(m^3-2mn^2\right)^2+\left(2m^2n-n^3\right)^2}.
\]
Check by expanding: $m^6-4m^4n^2+4m^2n^4+4m^4n^2-4m^2n^4+n^6=m^6+n^6$. The squares are new: $(m^3-2mn^2)^2=m^6$ would need $m^3-2mn^2=\pm m^3$, i.e. $n=0$ or $m=n$, both excluded. For example $1^6+2^6=65=7^2+4^2$.
