Fix the position of team $2$. Team $3$ must be in the other quarter of the same half, giving $8$ of the $31$ available positions. Team $1$ must be in the opposite half, otherwise it eliminates one of these two before they meet; after placing team $3$, there are $16$ such positions out of $30$. These conditions are also sufficient. Thus the probability is $\dfrac{8}{31}\cdot\dfrac{16}{30}=\boxed{\dfrac{64}{465}}$.
