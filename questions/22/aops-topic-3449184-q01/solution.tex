Clearing denominators gives $24x^2+25x-47=-8ax^2+(16-3a)x-47$. Equality for infinitely many $x$ forces equal coefficients, so $-8a=24$ and $a=-3$. This also gives $16-3a=25$, verifying the identity. Thus $\boxed{-3}$, choice (B).
