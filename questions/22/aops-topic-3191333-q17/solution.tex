The equations become $2A+B=4k$ and $6A+2B=13k$. Subtracting twice the first from the second gives $2A=5k$, then $B=-k$. Thus $(A,B,k)=(5t,-2t,2t)$ for positive integer $t$. Their gcd is $t$, so $t=1$. Hence $A+B+k=5-2+2=\boxed5$.
