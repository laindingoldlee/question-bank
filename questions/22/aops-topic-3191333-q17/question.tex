Integers $A,B,k$ satisfy $k>0$ and $\gcd(A,B,k)=1$. Define $x\#y=\dfrac{Ax+By}{kxy}$ for nonzero $x,y$. If $8\#4=\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $3\#1=\dfrac{13}{6}$, find $A+B+k$.
