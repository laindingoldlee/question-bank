The closed curve $x=5\cos\theta$, $y=3\sin\theta-\sin2\theta$, $0\le\theta<2\pi$, encloses a region. Rotate this entire region through $\pi$ radians about the $x$-axis.
\begin{enumerate}\item Show that its solid of revolution has volume $V=5\pi\int_\alpha^\beta\sin^3\theta(3-2\cos\theta)^2\,d\theta$, identifying $\alpha,\beta$.
\item Use $u=\cos\theta$ to evaluate $V$ exactly.\end{enumerate}
