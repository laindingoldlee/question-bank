At 8:30 the minute hand points at the $6$, i.e.\ $180^\circ$ from the $12$. The hour hand moves $30^\circ$ per hour, so it is at $8.5\cdot30^\circ=255^\circ$, halfway between the $8$ and the $9$. The angle between them is $255^\circ-180^\circ=\boxed{75^\circ}$.
