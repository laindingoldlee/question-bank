Use the identity $(A+B)^2-(A-B)^2=4AB$ (a difference of squares: $\bigl((A+B)-(A-B)\bigr)\bigl((A+B)+(A-B)\bigr)=2B\cdot2A$). Then
\[
4AB=(\sqrt8)^2-(\sqrt5)^2=8-5=3,\qquad AB=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{4}}.
\]
