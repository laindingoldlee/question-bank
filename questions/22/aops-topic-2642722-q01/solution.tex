If $d\mid1$, then $1=dk$ for some integer $k$. Neither $d$ nor $k$ is $0$, so $|d|\ge1$ and $|k|\ge1$, while $|d|\cdot|k|=1$. Hence $|d|=1$, i.e. $d=1$ or $d=-1$. $\blacksquare$
