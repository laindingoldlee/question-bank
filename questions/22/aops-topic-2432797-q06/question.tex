A flower shop sells bouquets of petunias wrapped in plastic. A small bouquet with $6$ flowers costs $\$5.30$ and a large bouquet with $10$ flowers costs $\$8.70$. Each flower costs the same, and the plastic wrap is the same for every bouquet. What is the price of a medium bouquet with $9$ flowers?
