Let a flower cost $f$ dollars and the wrap $w$ dollars. Then $6f+w=5.30$ and $10f+w=8.70$. Subtracting, $4f=3.40$, so $f=0.85$ and $w=5.30-5.10=0.20$. A medium bouquet costs $9\cdot0.85+0.20=\boxed{\$7.85}$.
