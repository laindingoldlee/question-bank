Let $u=\sqrt[3]{9}$, so $u^3=9$. Using $a^3-b^3=(a-b)(a^2+ab+b^2)$ with $a=2$, $b=u$,
\[
(2-u)(4+2u+u^2)=8-u^3=8-9=-1.
\]
Also $u^2=\sqrt[3]{81}=3\sqrt[3]{3}$. Multiplying numerator and denominator by $4+2u+u^2$,
\[
\dfrac{6x+1-\pi}{2-\sqrt[3]9}=\dfrac{(6x+1-\pi)(4+2\sqrt[3]9+3\sqrt[3]3)}{-1}=\boxed{(\pi-6x-1)\left(4+2\sqrt[3]{9}+3\sqrt[3]{3}\right)}.
\]
