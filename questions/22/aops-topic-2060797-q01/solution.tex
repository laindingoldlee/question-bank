Without the snake there are $\dbinom94=126$ routes ($5$ hops right and $4$ up in some order). The routes through $(2,2)$ consist of a route to $(2,2)$, in $\dbinom42=6$ ways, followed by a route from $(2,2)$ to $(5,4)$ ($3$ right and $2$ up), in $\dbinom52=10$ ways: $60$ routes. So the number of routes avoiding the snake is
\[126-60=\boxed{66}.\]
