Since $P,Q,R$ are the roots and the leading coefficient is $4$,
\[
4x^3-17x^2+23x-11=4(x-P)(x-Q)(x-R).
\]
Comparing constant terms, $-11=-4PQR$, so
\[
PQR=\boxed{\dfrac{11}{4}} .
\]
(Vieta's formulas: for $ax^3+bx^2+cx+d$ the product of the roots is $-\dfrac {d}{a}$. The value $\dfrac{23}{4}$ is $PQ+QR+RP$, and $\dfrac{17}{4}$ is $P+Q+R$.)
