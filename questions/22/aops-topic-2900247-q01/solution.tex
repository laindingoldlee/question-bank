Count multiples in the window $n,\dots,n+10$ of $11$ integers.
\begin{itemize}
\item Five multiples of $2$ (not six) iff $n$ is odd.
\item Three multiples of $3$ (not four) iff $n\equiv1\pmod3$.
\item Three multiples of $5$ (not two) iff $n\equiv0\pmod5$, namely $n,n+5,n+10$.
\item One multiple of $7$ (not two) iff the first multiple of $7$ is at offset $4$, $5$ or $6$, i.e. $n\equiv1,2,3\pmod7$.
\end{itemize}
The first three conditions give $n\equiv25\pmod{30}$: $25,55,85,\dots$, which are $4,6,1\pmod7$. The least is $n=\boxed{85}$.
