For $x>0$, $x^x\le x$ is equivalent to $x^{x-1}\le1$, i.e. $(x-1)\ln x\le0$. But $x-1$ and $\ln x$ always have the same sign (both negative for $x<1$, both zero at $x=1$, both positive for $x>1$), so $(x-1)\ln x\ge0$, with equality only at $x=1$.

Hence the only solution is $\boxed{x=1}$, and then $x^x(x-1)=0\le1$.
