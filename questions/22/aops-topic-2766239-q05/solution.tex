The first four are $2=1^2+1^2$, $5=1^2+2^2$, $13=2^2+3^2$, $17=1^2+4^2$. The intervening primes 3,7,11 are 3 modulo 4 and cannot be sums of two squares, since squares are 0 or 1 modulo 4. The sum is $\boxed{37}$.
