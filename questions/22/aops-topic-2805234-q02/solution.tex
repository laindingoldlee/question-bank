Let the catches be $F$ and $S$. Then $F-x=S+x$ and $F+x=2(S-x)$. From the first, $F=S+2x$; substituting, $S+3x=2S-2x$, so $S=5x$ and $F=7x$. The father's catch is $\dfrac{7x-5x}{5x}=\boxed{40\%}$ larger.
