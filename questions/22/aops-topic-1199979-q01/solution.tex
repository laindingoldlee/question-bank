Let the hexagon have side $s$; its circumradius is also $s$. The midpoints of the sides lie at distance $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}s$ from the centre, and midpoints of alternate sides are $120^\circ$ apart, so the triangle has side $\sqrt3\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}s=\dfrac{3}{2}s$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair[] H; for(int k=0;k<6;++k) H.push(4*dir(60*k)); draw(H[0]--H[1]--H[2]--H[3]--H[4]--H[5]--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); draw(circle((0,0),4),gray);
pair M1=(H[0]+H[1])/2,M2=(H[2]+H[3])/2,M3=(H[4]+H[5])/2; draw(M1--M2--M3--cycle,blue+linewidth(0.9)); draw((0,0)--H[0],dashed); label("$R$",(2,0),S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Thus $\dfrac{3}{2}s=6$, $s=4$, and the circle has area $\pi\cdot4^2=\boxed{16\pi}$.
