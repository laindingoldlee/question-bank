By the binomial theorem, $11^8=(10+1)^8=\sum_{j=0}^{8}\dbinom8j10^j$, so the ``digits'' before carrying are the numbers of row $8$ of Pascal's triangle,
\[
1,\ 8,\ 28,\ 56,\ 70,\ 56,\ 28,\ 8,\ 1 ,
\]
read from the highest power of $10$ to the lowest. Carry from the right: the units digit is $1$; then $8$; then $28$ gives digit $8$, carry $2$; $56+2=58$ gives $8$, carry $5$; $70+5=75$ gives $5$, carry $7$; $56+7=63$ gives $3$, carry $6$; $28+6=34$ gives $4$, carry $3$; $8+3=11$ gives $1$, carry $1$; $1+1=2$. Hence
\[
11^8=214\,358\,881,
\]
and its digit sum is $2+1+4+3+5+8+8+8+1=\boxed{40}$. (Check: $11^8\equiv2^8=256\equiv4\pmod9$ and $40\equiv4\pmod9$.)
