The pentagon's interior angle $BAE$ is $108^\circ$. Ray $AP$ is $60^\circ$ from $AB$ towards $AE$. Ray $AQ$ is $60^\circ$ from $AE$ towards $AB$, hence $108^\circ-60^\circ=48^\circ$ from $AB$. Their smaller separation is $\boxed{60^\circ-48^\circ=12^\circ}$.
