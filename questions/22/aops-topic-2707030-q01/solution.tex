Since $3\cdot5=15\equiv1\pmod7$, the first congruence gives $x\equiv25\equiv4\pmod7$. Since $7\cdot13=91\equiv1\pmod{15}$, the second gives $x\equiv65\equiv5\pmod{15}$. Write $x=5+15t$; then $5+t\equiv4\pmod7$, so $t\equiv6$ and $x=95$. The solution is $\boxed{x\equiv95\pmod{105}}$ (check: $3\cdot95=285=40\cdot7+5$ and $7\cdot95=665=44\cdot15+5$).
