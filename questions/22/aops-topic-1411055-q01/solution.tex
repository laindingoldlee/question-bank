Let the actual boiling point be $T$. Her value is $4.5\%$ too high, so
\[
125.4=1.045\,T\quad\Longrightarrow\quad T=\dfrac{125.4}{1.045}=\boxed{120^\circ\text{F}}.
\]
(Check: $\dfrac{125.4-120}{120}=\dfrac{5.4}{120}=0.045$.)
