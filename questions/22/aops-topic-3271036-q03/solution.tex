The double-angle identities give $\sin2\theta=2\cdot\dfrac{3}{5}\cdot\dfrac{4}{5}=\boxed{\dfrac{24}{25}}$ and $\cos2\theta=\dfrac{16}{25}-\dfrac{9}{25}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{25}}$. Their quotient is $\boxed{\tan2\theta=\dfrac{24}{7}}$.
