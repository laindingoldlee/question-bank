Using each of the numbers $2$, $3$, $4$ and $5$ exactly once, together with plus signs and one equals sign (no other symbols, and digits may not be placed side by side to form larger numbers), write a true equation. Show that, apart from the order of the terms and of the two sides, there is only one way to do it.
