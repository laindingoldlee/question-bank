The two sides of the equation have equal sums and together contain all four numbers, whose total is $2+3+4+5=14$. So each side adds up to $7$.

No single number equals $7$, so neither side can consist of one number; hence each side has exactly two numbers. The pairs with sum $7$ are $\{2,5\}$ and $\{3,4\}$ (the other pairs give $5,6,8,9$). So the equation must be
\[\boxed{2+5=3+4},\]
up to the order of the terms and of the two sides.
