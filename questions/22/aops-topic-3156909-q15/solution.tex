The characteristic roots are 2 and $-1 $, and the initial data give $ S_n=\dfrac{7}{3}\,2^{n-1}+\dfrac{2}{3}(-1)^{n-1}$. Both resulting series converge absolutely. Therefore the sum is $\dfrac{7}{9}\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{2}{3}}+\dfrac{2}{9}\dfrac{1}{1+\dfrac{1}{3}}=\dfrac{7}{3}+\dfrac{1}{6}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{2}}$.
