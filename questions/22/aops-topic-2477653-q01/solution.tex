Since $4\equiv 1\pmod 3$, a number has the same residue modulo $3$ as its digit sum. Choose the first seven digits arbitrarily, in $3^7$ ways. Among the possible last digits $1,2,3$, exactly one makes the sum divisible by $3$, since these represent all three residues. Thus the answer is $3^7=\boxed{2187}$.
