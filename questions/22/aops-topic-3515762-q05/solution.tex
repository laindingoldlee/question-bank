Write each such integer as $5m$, with $1\le m\le199$. We count multiples of $2,3,7,11$ by inclusion-exclusion. The single counts sum to $99+66+28+18=211$. The pair counts sum to $33+14+9+9+6+2=73$. The triple counts sum to $4+3+1+0=8$, and there are no multiples of $2\cdot3\cdot7\cdot11=462$ in the range. Thus the answer is $211-73+8=\boxed{146}$.
