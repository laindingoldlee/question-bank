The leading digit of a sum of two five-digit numbers must be one, so $T=1$. Let $c_1,c_2,c_3,c_4$ be successive carries from the units through thousands columns. The column equations are $1+R=P+10c_1$, $N+E+c_1=I+10c_2$, $A+1+c_2=N+10c_3$, $L+A+c_3=R+10c_4$, and $P+W+c_4=U+10$. Since $R=9$ would force the leading $P=0$, $c_1=0$ and $P=R+1$. This reduces the search: try $R\in\{2,3,4,5,6,7,8\}$; try each unused $A$ and $c_2\in\{0,1\}$; the hundreds equation determines $N,c_3$. Try unused $E$ and check the tens equation for $I$. Then try $c_4\in\{0,1\}$, obtain $L$ from the thousands equation, and use the remaining digits for $W,U$. Reject repeats and zero leading digits at every step. The sole surviving row is $(R,P,A,c_2,N,c_3,E,I,c_4,L,W,U)=(7,8,4,1,6,0,9,5,0,3,2,0)$. It gives $\boxed{83461+24197=107658}$; the displayed column equations verify every carry.
