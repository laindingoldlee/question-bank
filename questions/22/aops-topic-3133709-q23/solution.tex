Let counts be $a,b\ge0$. From $11a+17b=227$, reduction modulo 11 gives $6b\equiv7$, hence $b\equiv3\pmod{11}$. As $0\le b\le13$, $b=3$, giving $a=16$. The total is $\boxed{19}$.
