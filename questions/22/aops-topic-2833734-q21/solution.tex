Write the original progression as $ a,a+d,a+2d $, where $ a,d $ are positive integers. The geometric condition is $(a+d-1)^2=a(a+2d-1)$, which simplifies to $ a=(d-1)^2 $. Thus the terms are $(d-1)^2,d^2-d+1,d^2+1 $. Positivity and the upper bound give $ 2\le d\le5 $. The four triples are $(1,3,5),(4,7,10),(9,13,17),(16,21,26)$. Exactly two entries are prime in the first and third, giving sums $\boxed{9\text{ and }39}$.
