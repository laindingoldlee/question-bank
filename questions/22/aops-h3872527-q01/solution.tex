The first-half average is $\dfrac{100}{10}=10$ meters per second. To average 20 meters per second over 200 meters would require total time $\dfrac{200}{20}=10$ seconds. Those 10 seconds have already been used. Every finite positive speed over the second half takes additional positive time, making the overall average strictly less than 20. Thus $\boxed{\text{no finite speed can achieve this}}$.
