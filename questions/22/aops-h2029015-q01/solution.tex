The lines $AD,BE$ have equations $y=2-2x$ and $y=\dfrac{1}{5}(x+1)$. Hence $T=\left(\dfrac{9}{11},\dfrac{4}{11}\right)$. The parallel to $CD$ is $y=x-\dfrac{5}{11}$. Intersecting it with $AB$, whose equation is $y=\dfrac{1}{2}(x+1)$, and $CE$, whose equation is $y=\dfrac{3}{2}x-1$, gives
\[
X=\left(\dfrac{21}{11},\dfrac{16}{11}\right),\qquad
Y=\left(\dfrac{12}{11},\dfrac{7}{11}\right).
\]
The point $U=\left(\dfrac{12}{11},\dfrac{16}{11}\right)$ is at distance $\dfrac{9}{11}$ from each of $A,X,Y$, so it is their circumcenter. Furthermore
\[
OU=\dfrac{20}{11}=1+\dfrac{9}{11},\qquad
A=\dfrac{11}{20}U.
\]
Thus the centers and $A$ are collinear and the center distance is the sum of the radii. The circles are externally tangent at $\boxed A$.
