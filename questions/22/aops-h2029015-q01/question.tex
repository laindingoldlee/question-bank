The points
\[
A=\left(\dfrac{3}{5},\dfrac{4}{5}\right),\ B=(-1,0),\ C=(0,-1),\ D=(1,0),\
E=\left(\dfrac{12}{13},\dfrac{5}{13}\right)
\]
lie on the unit circle. Let $T=BE\cap AD$. The line through $T$ parallel to $CD$ meets $AB,CE$ at $X,Y$. Find $T,X,Y$ and prove that the circle through $A,X,Y$ is tangent to the unit circle.
