Reflection in the horizontal line $y=2000$ keeps the $x$-coordinate and moves the point to the same distance on the other side of the line. The point is $2012-2000=12$ units above the line, so its image is $12$ units below:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((-3,0)--(3,0),blue+linewidth(0.9)); label("$y=2000$",(3,0),E,blue);
pair P=(0,1.2), Q=(0,-1.2);
draw(P--Q,red+dashed); dot(P); dot(Q);
draw((0.15,0)--(0.15,0.15)--(0,0.15));
label("$(1000,2012)$",P,N); label("$(1000,1988)$",Q,S);
label("$12$",(0,0.6),E); label("$12$",(0,-0.6),E);
\end{asy}
