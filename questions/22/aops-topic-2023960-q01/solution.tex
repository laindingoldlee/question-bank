The sum is the total distance from $x$ to the $11$ points $2^0<2^1<\dots<2^{10}$ on the number line. Pair the points from the outside in: $(2^0,2^{10})$, $(2^1,2^9)$, \dots, $(2^4,2^6)$, leaving the middle point $2^5$. For each pair $(p,q)$, $|x-p|+|x-q|\ge q-p$, with equality iff $p\le x\le q$; and $|x-2^5|\ge0$ with equality iff $x=2^5$. All these equalities hold simultaneously exactly when $x=2^5$ (the median), so the minimum is attained only at
\[
x=\boxed{32}.
\]
