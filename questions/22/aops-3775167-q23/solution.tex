Let $K=21\cdot22\cdot23\cdot24=255024$. Factor the number as $(20!)^3(1+K^3)$. The first factor contributes $12$ factors of $5$. Also $1+K^3=(K+1)(K^2-K+1)$; $K+1=255025=25\cdot10201$ has exactly two factors of $5$, while the other factor is $3\pmod5$. The power of $2$ in $(20!)^3$ exceeds $14$. Thus there are $\boxed{14}$ trailing zeros.
