In a game, $10$ players are in a lobby: $2$ are impostors and $8$ are crewmates. The impostors never kill. At each meeting exactly one of the remaining players is voted out, each remaining player being equally likely. The crewmates win as soon as both impostors have been voted out; the impostors win as soon as the number of impostors remaining is at least the number of crewmates remaining. What is the probability that the impostors win?
