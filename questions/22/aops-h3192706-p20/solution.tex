The eight centres lie on a circle of radius R+1. Adjacent centres are two apart, so their consecutive central angles are equal: each is the same minor angle or its reflex counterpart, and a reflex gap would make the sum of the eight gaps exceed 360 degrees. Hence each gap is 45 degrees. Half of a centre triangle gives $(R+1)\sin22.5^\circ=1$, so
\[R=\dfrac{1}{\sin22.5^\circ}-1=\boxed{\sqrt{4+2\sqrt2}-1}.\]
