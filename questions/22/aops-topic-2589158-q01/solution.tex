We have
\[\dfrac{2021}{2^{2021}}=\dfrac{2021\cdot5^{2021}}{10^{2021}}.\]
The integer $N=2021\cdot5^{2021}$ ends in the digit $5$, so it is not divisible by $10$, and it has fewer than $2021$ digits, since $N<10^4\cdot5^{2021}<10^{4+1415}$. So the decimal is $0.00\ldots0N$ with exactly $2021$ digits after the point, and its last three digits are the last three digits of $N$.

Modulo $1000$: $5^3=125$, $5^4=625$, $5^5=3125\equiv125$, and in general $5^k\equiv125$ for odd $k\ge3$. So $5^{2021}\equiv125$ and
\[N\equiv2021\cdot125=252625\equiv625\pmod{1000}.\]
The decimal ends in $\ldots625$, and its third digit from the end is $\boxed{6}$.
