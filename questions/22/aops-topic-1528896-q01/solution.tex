By 9:30 Ivan has ridden $1.5$ hours, i.e.\ $12\cdot1.5=18$ miles, so $40-18=22$ miles separate them. From then on the gap closes at $12+16=28$ miles per hour, so they meet after
\[
\dfrac{22}{28}=\dfrac{11}{14}\text{ hour}=\dfrac{660}{14}=47\dfrac{1}{7}\text{ minutes}.
\]
They meet at $47\dfrac{1}{7}$ minutes after 9:30, i.e.\ at $10{:}17\dfrac{1}{7}$ a.m., which is $\boxed{10{:}17\text{ a.m.}}$ to the nearest minute.

(Check: Ivan has ridden $12\cdot\left(1.5+\dfrac{11}{14}\right)=\dfrac{192}{7}$ miles and Kate $16\cdot\dfrac{11}{14}=\dfrac{88}{7}$ miles, a total of $\dfrac{280}{7}=40$.)
