Let $a,x,y$ be positive real numbers with $a\ne1$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $\log_a x\cdot\log_a y=\log_a\!\left(x^{\log_a y}\right)$.
\item[(b)] Deduce that $x^{\log_a y}=y^{\log_a x}$, and use this to evaluate $9^{\log_3 5}$ and $8^{\log_2 7}\cdot 7^{-\log_2 8}$.
\end{enumerate}
