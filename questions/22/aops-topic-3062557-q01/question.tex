Consider a recursive merging procedure with 16 storage slots. It builds a tile 4 by placing it in an empty slot. To build a tile $2^k$ for $k>2$, it first recursively completes a tile $2^{k-1}$, keeps that tile in one slot, recursively completes another, and merges them. No child construction may be interleaved with another. Starting empty, what is the largest single tile this procedure can build?
