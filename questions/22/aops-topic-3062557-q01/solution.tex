Let $s_k$ be the number of slots required by this procedure to build $2^k$. A tile 4 needs one slot, so $s_2=1$. For a larger tile, its first child needs $s_{k-1}$ slots. Keeping that completed child occupies one slot while the second child needs another $s_{k-1}$ slots. Thus $s_k=s_{k-1}+1$, giving $s_k=k-1$ by induction. Sixteen slots therefore permit $k-1\le16$, with equality attainable by the specified recursion. The largest tile is $\boxed{2^{17}=131072}$.
