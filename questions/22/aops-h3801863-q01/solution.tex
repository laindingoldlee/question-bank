Every integer satisfies $k^5\equiv k\pmod{10}$: this follows modulo two immediately and modulo five from Fermat's theorem (including multiples of five). Thus the digits repeat $1,2,\ldots,9,0$, with block sum $45$. If $N=10q+r$, $0\le r<10$, the sum is $\boxed{45q+\dfrac{r(r+1)}{2}}$. For $N=25$ it is $90+15=\boxed{105}$.
