With $n$ people, counts range from 0 to $n-1$. But 0 and $n-1$ cannot both occur: someone friends with everyone would be a friend of an alleged isolated person. Therefore there are at most $n-1$ possible counts among $n$ people. The pigeonhole principle gives two equal counts.
