For positive integers $n\ge2$, define $g(n)$ to be one more than the largest proper divisor of $n$. For example, $g(35)=8$, since the proper divisors of $35$ are $1$, $5$ and $7$. For how many $n$ in the range $2\le n\le100$ is $g(g(n))=2$?
