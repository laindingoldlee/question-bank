For each sequence, decide whether it is arithmetic, geometric or neither, and give the common difference or ratio when there is one.
\begin{enumerate}
\item $3,9,27,81,243,\dots$
\item $2,4,16,256,\dots$
\item $1,5,9,13,17,\dots$
\item $12,24,48,96,\dots$
\item The balances at the end of each year of a bank account that earns $3\%$ interest per year, compounded yearly, with no deposits or withdrawals.
\end{enumerate}
