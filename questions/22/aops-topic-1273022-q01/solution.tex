Measure grass in cow-days. Let the field start with $G$ and grow $g$ per day. Then $G+90g=48\cdot90=4320$ and $G+30g=120\cdot30=3600$, so $g=12$ and $G=3240$. In $16$ days the cows must eat $3240+16\cdot12=3432$, i.e. $\dfrac{3432}{16}=214.5$ cows' worth; so $\boxed{215}$ cows are needed (with $214$ cows it would take slightly longer than $16$ days).
