Subtract the first two equations: $(2+c)(a-b)=0 $. Positivity gives $ a=b $, and cyclically all three are equal. Then $ 2a=1+a^2 $, so $(a-1)^2=0 $. The unique triple is $\boxed{(1,1,1)}$.
