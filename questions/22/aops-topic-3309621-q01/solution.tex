A triangle corresponds to its three positive cyclic gaps between selected vertices, summing to $10$. Rotations and reflections permute these three gaps arbitrarily. Conversely, congruent triangles have the same circumradius and an isometry carrying their unique circumcircle to itself; hence their vertex-gap triples agree up to rotation or reversal. Thus count unordered positive triples summing to $10$:
\[(1,1,8),(1,2,7),(1,3,6),(1,4,5),(2,2,6),(2,3,5),(2,4,4),(3,3,4).\]
There are $\boxed8$.
