Order them $ a<b<c $. The no-consecutive condition corresponds bijectively to choosing $ a<b-1<c-2 $ from 1 through 8. Thus there are $\binom83=56 $ favorable subsets among $\binom{10}{3}=120 $. The probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{7}{15}}$.
