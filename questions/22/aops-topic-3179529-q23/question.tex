Distinct nonzero digits $a,b,c$ satisfy $\overline{abc}\,\overline{cb}=100x^2+1$ and $\overline{acb}\,\overline{bc}=100y^2+1$ for integers $x,y$. Find the least possible $\overline{abbc}$.
