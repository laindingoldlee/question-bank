Factor $2019=3\cdot673$, with $673$ prime. The totient is $(3-1)(673-1)=1344$, giving probability $\dfrac{1344}{2019}=\boxed{\dfrac{448}{673}}$. Coprimality repeats every $2019$ integers, so this same fraction is the natural density.
