We need $1+2x\ge0$ and $\sqrt{1+2x}\ne1$, i.e. $x\ge-\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $x\ne0$. Rationalise: $\dfrac{2x}{1-\sqrt{1+2x}}=\dfrac{2x\left(1+\sqrt{1+2x}\right)}{1-(1+2x)}=-\left(1+\sqrt{1+2x}\right)$. Hence the left side is
\[
\left(1+\sqrt{1+2x}\right)^2=2+2x+2\sqrt{1+2x},
\]
and the inequality becomes $2\sqrt{1+2x}<7$, i.e. $1+2x<\dfrac{49}{4}$, i.e. $x<\dfrac{45}{8}$. Together with the domain,
\[
x\in\boxed{\left[-\dfrac{1}{2},0\right)\cup\left(0,\dfrac{45}{8}\right)}.
\]
