The first $n$ terms have sum $S_n=1-2^{-n}$, either by the geometric-sum formula or by subtracting $\dfrac{1}{2}S_n$ from $S_n$. Since $2^{-n}\to0$, the infinite sum is $\boxed1$. A unit rectangle can also be successively divided into pieces of areas $\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{1}{4},\ldots$ with a remainder of area $2^{-n}$.
\begin{asy}
import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
draw((0,0)--(8,0)--(8,2)--(0,2)--cycle);for(int k=1;k<=4;++k){real x=8*(1-2.0^(-k));draw((x,0)--(x,2));}label("$\dfrac{1}{2}$",(2,1));label("$\dfrac{1}{4}$",(5,1));label("$\dfrac{1}{8}$",(6.5,1));label("$\cdots$",(7.5,1));
\end{asy}
