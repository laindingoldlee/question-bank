Nour drove from the Dead Sea up to Amman, and her altitude increased at a constant rate. When she began driving, her altitude was $400$ metres below sea level. When she arrived in Amman $2$ hours later, her altitude was $1000$ metres above sea level. Let $A(t)$ be Nour's altitude relative to sea level, in metres, $t$ hours after she began driving. Write a formula for $A(t)$.
