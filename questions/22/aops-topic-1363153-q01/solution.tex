Because the altitude increases at a constant rate, $A$ is a linear function of $t$. At $t=0$ the altitude is $-400$ m and at $t=2$ it is $1000$ m, so the rate is
\[
\dfrac{1000-(-400)}{2-0}=700\text{ metres per hour}.
\]
Hence $\boxed{A(t)=700t-400}$ for $0\le t\le2$. Check: $A(2)=1400-400=1000$.
