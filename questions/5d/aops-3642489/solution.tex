Every term is positive. Also the sum is bounded by all adjacent-reciprocal terms through 100: $s<\sum_{j=1}^{99}\dfrac{1}{j(j+1)}=\sum_{j=1}^{99}(\dfrac{1}{j}-\dfrac{1}{(j+1)})=\dfrac{99}{100}<1$. Thus $0<s<1$, so it is not an integer. The strict comparison holds because omitted terms such as $\dfrac{1}{(2\cdot3)}$ are positive.
