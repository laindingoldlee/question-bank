Divisibility by $3$ requires the number of $4$s to be a multiple of three. There are at most four digits, so there must be exactly three $4$s. The possible numbers are $444,4044,4404,4440$. All are divisible by $4$ because their last two digits are $44,04,$ or $40$. Their sum is $\boxed{13332}$.
