Treat MATH as a block. The remaining M,A,T,E are distinct, giving $5!=120$ arrangements. Treat HEAT as a block; the remaining M,M,A,T give $\dfrac{5!}{2!}=60$. To contain both blocks, their unique H must overlap, producing MATHEAT plus the remaining M. This gives only MMATHEAT and MATHEATM. Inclusion--exclusion therefore gives $120+60-2=\boxed{178}$.
