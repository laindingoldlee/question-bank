Every day Bertha patrols the perimeter of a bay, which is $1200$ yards long. Once a month, during her patrol, she also travels $600$ yards straight out to sea and $600$ yards back, and then finishes the perimeter. How many feet does Bertha travel in the six years from 1 January 2021 to 31 December 2026? (The only leap year in this period is 2024.)
