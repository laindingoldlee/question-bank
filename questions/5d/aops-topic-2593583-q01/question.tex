On the planet Glogg, every inhabitant is either a \emph{header}, with $3$ heads and the usual $2$ arms and $2$ legs, or a \emph{legger}, with $1$ head, $3$ arms and $3$ legs. A group of Gloggians has $15$ heads and $48$ limbs (arms and legs) in total. How many headers and how many leggers are there?
