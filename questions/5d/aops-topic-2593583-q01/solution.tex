A header has $4$ limbs and a legger has $6$. With $h$ headers and $\ell$ leggers,
\[3h+\ell=15,\qquad4h+6\ell=48.\]
From the first equation $\ell=15-3h$; then $4h+90-18h=48$, so $14h=42$ and $h=3$, $\ell=6$. There are $\boxed{3\text{ headers and }6\text{ leggers}}$ (check: $9+6=15$ heads and $12+36=48$ limbs).
