Move $k$ has length $2^{k-1}$. The horizontal moves are the odd-numbered ones, alternating right and left: $+1,-4,+16,\dots$; the vertical moves are the even-numbered ones, alternating up and down: $+2,-8,+32,\dots$. After $13$ moves (seven horizontal and six vertical), these are geometric series with ratio $-4$:
\[x=1-4+16-\cdots+4096=\sum_{j=0}^{6}(-4)^j=\dfrac{1-(-4)^7}{1-(-4)}=\dfrac{16385}{5}=3277,\]
\[y=2-8+32-\cdots-2048=2\sum_{j=0}^{5}(-4)^j=2\cdot\dfrac{1-(-4)^6}{5}=2\cdot\dfrac{-4095}{5}=-1638.\]
Bob ends at $\boxed{(3277,-1638)}$.
