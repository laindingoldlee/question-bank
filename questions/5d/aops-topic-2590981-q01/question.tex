Bob the ant starts at the point $(0,0)$. His first move is $1$ unit to the right, to $(1,0)$. His second move is $2$ units up, his third move $4$ units left and his fourth move $8$ units down. He continues in this way: each move is twice as long as the previous one, and the directions cycle right, up, left, down. Where is Bob after his $13$th move?
