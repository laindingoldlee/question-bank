The path follows equal sides with constant exterior angle $30^\circ$, forming a regular twelve-gon. It first closes after $\dfrac{360}{30}=12$ sides. For a direct check, a partial sum of unit direction vectors is a geometric series, zero only when its number of terms is a multiple of twelve. Thus the first travelled distance is $\boxed{12\text{ m}}$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

pair p=(0,0);for(int i=0;i<12;++i){pair q=p+dir(30i);draw(p--q,Arrow);p=q;}dot((0,0));label("Start",(0,0),(0,-1));
\end{asy}
