Four warm-up questions.
\begin{enumerate}
\item A square is inscribed in a circle. At how many points do they intersect?
\item $f$ is an invertible function with $f(3)=4$. What is $f^{-1}(4)$?
\item Prove that for every point $T$ on a circle there is a line through $T$ tangent to the circle.
\item Find the prime factorisation of $4$.
\end{enumerate}
