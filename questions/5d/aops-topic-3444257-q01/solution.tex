The digit count is $9+180+3(N-99)=3N-108$. Put $m=N-36$, with $64\le m\le963$. The divisibility requires $6m\mid N(N+1)=m^2+73m+1332$, hence $m\mid1332=2^2\cdot3^2\cdot37$. Its divisors in the required range are $74,111,148,222,333,444,666$. The corresponding values of $m+73+\dfrac{1332}{m}$ are $165,196,230,301,410,520,741$, none divisible by $6$. Therefore the answer is $\boxed{\text{no}}$.
