Such a label is $12k$ with $1\le k\le30$, and no divisor 13 through 20 may divide it. The restrictions exclude multiples of 13,7,5,4,17,3,19 in the value of $k$, respectively;20 adds the already excluded multiples of 5. Among 1 through 30 the remaining values are $1,2,11,22,23,29$, giving card labels $12,24,132,264,276,348$. Thus there are $\boxed6$ cards.
