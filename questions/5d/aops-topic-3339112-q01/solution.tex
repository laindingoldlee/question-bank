Encode play as $1$ and no play as $0$. We count strings avoiding $000$ and $1111$. Let $Z_j(n)$ count valid length-$n$ strings ending in exactly $j$ zeros ($j=1,2$), and $O_j(n)$ count those ending in $j$ ones ($j=1,2,3$). Then $Z_1(n+1)=\sum O_j(n)$, $Z_2(n+1)=Z_1(n)$, $O_1(n+1)=Z_1(n)+Z_2(n)$, $O_2(n+1)=O_1(n)$, $O_3(n+1)=O_2(n)$. Start with $Z_1(1)=O_1(1)=1$ and other entries zero. Total counts for lengths $1$ through $10$ are $2,4,7,12,21,36,63,109,189,328$. Thus $\boxed{328}$.
