No maximizing partition uses $1$, because merging it with another part increases the product. A part $k\ge5$ can be replaced by $3$ and $k-3$ to increase its contribution, since $3(k-3)>k$. Replace $4$ by $2+2$ with no change. Three $2$'s should then be replaced by two $3$'s, increasing $8$ to $9$.
Thus a maximum uses $3$'s and at most two $2$'s. Since $2026\equiv1\pmod3$, it requires two $2$'s and $674$ threes:
\[
2026=2+2+674\cdot3,\qquad \boxed{4\cdot3^{674}}.
\]
