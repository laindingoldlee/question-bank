A bus takes $2$ minutes to travel between successive stops and waits $1$ minute at each stop. Zia takes $5$ minutes to walk between stops and boards only at a stop. Whenever she reaches a stop, she waits if the bus is at the preceding stop or has already left it; otherwise she walks to the next stop. At time zero Zia leaves stop $0$ and the bus simultaneously leaves stop $-3$, both moving forward. When does Zia board?
