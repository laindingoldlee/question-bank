The bus arrives at stops $-2,-1,0,1,2,3$ at times $2,5,8,11,14,17$. Zia reaches stop $1$ at time $5$; the bus is only at stop $-1$, so she continues. She reaches stop $2$ at time $10$; the bus has not yet reached preceding stop $1$, so she continues again. She reaches stop $3$ at time $15$; the bus has reached and is departing preceding stop $2$, so she waits. It arrives at stop $3$ at time $\boxed{17}$ minutes.
