One-digit numbers: all $9$ are palindromes. Two-digit palindromes are $\overline{aa}$: $9$. Three-digit palindromes are $\overline{aba}$ with $a\ne0$: $9\cdot10=90$. In total $9+9+90=\boxed{\textbf{(d)}\ 108}$.
