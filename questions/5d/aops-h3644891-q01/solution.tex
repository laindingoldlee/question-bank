Put $d=10a+b$. The integer is $1001d+100c$, so $d\mid100c$. Since $13\mid1001$ and $0\le c\le9$, failure of divisibility by $13$ means $c\ne0$. For each $c=1,\ldots,9$, list the two-digit divisors $d$ of $100c$ and maximize $2s(d)+c$. The maxima in that order are $15,16,27,20,19,30,27,24,33$. Thus the largest sum is $\boxed{33}$, attained at $d=75,c=9$, giving $75975=75\cdot1013$, which is not divisible by $13$.
