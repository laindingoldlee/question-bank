Guess a middle integer of the current possible interval, then retain only the indicated half if incorrect. From an interval of size $m$, either incorrect reply leaves at most $\lfloor\dfrac{m}{2}\rfloor$ possibilities. After six incorrect replies there are at most $\lfloor\dfrac{100}{64}\rfloor=1$ possibilities; guess that remaining number on the seventh try.
