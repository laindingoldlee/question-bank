The mean is $\dfrac{48+2x}{5}$. The median depends on where $x$ lies.
\begin{itemize}
\item $x\le10$: the sorted list is $x,x,10,12,26$ and the median is $10$. Then $48+2x=50$, so $x=1$, which is allowed.
\item $10\le x\le26$: the two copies of $x$ occupy two of the three middle places, so the median is $x$. Then $48+2x=5x$, so $x=16$, which is allowed.
\item $x\ge26$: the sorted list is $10,12,26,x,x$ and the median is $26$. Then $48+2x=130$, so $x=41$, which is allowed.
\end{itemize}
The possible values are $1$, $16$ and $41$, with sum $\boxed{58}$.
