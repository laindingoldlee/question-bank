Let $b$ be an integer with $10\le b\le20$, and let $x,y,z$ be digits from $0$ to $9$. Suppose that in base $b$,
\[0.x\overline{yz}_{\,b}=\dfrac{19}{60},\]
where the block $yz$ repeats forever. Find all possible three-digit strings $\overline{xyz}$.
