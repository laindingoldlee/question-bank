The average of an arithmetic progression is the average of its first and last terms. Let the terms be $a_1,\dots,a_n$ with difference $d$. Originally the average is $\dfrac{a_1+a_n}{2}$; afterwards the list runs from $a_{12}$ to $a_{n-25}$, with average $\dfrac{a_{12}+a_{n-25}}{2}$. The decrease is
\[\dfrac{(a_1-a_{12})+(a_n-a_{n-25})}{2}=\dfrac{-11d+25d}{2}=7d=2019,\]
so $d=\boxed{\dfrac{2019}{7}}$, whatever the number of terms and the first term.
