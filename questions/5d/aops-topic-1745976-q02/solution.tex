A term $x^2yz^3$ arises by choosing $3x$ from two of the six factors, $4y$ from one and $2z$ from three. The number of ways to choose which factors is $\dfrac{6!}{2!\,1!\,3!}=60$, and each choice contributes $3^2\cdot4\cdot2^3=288$. The coefficient is $60\cdot288=\boxed{17280}$.
