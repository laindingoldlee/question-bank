Unit direction vectors along the two rays are
\[
u=\dfrac{(1,\alpha)}{\sqrt{1+\alpha^2}},\qquad
v=\dfrac{(1,\beta)}{\sqrt{1+\beta^2}}.
\]
Their sum bisects the angle because it has equal scalar product with both unit vectors. Thus
\[
k=\dfrac{\alpha\sqrt{1+\beta^2}+\beta\sqrt{1+\alpha^2}}{\sqrt{1+\beta^2}+\sqrt{1+\alpha^2}}
=\dfrac{\alpha\beta-1+\sqrt{(1+\alpha^2)(1+\beta^2)}}{\alpha+\beta}.
\]
For $\alpha=2,\beta=5$, this is $\boxed{\dfrac{(9+\sqrt{130})}{7}}$.

\end{asy}
