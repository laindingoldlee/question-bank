Cauchy--Schwarz applied to $(x,\sqrt{2024-x^2})$ and $(\sqrt{2024-y^2},y)$ gives the stated left side at most $2024$. Equality forces these vectors equal, as both have norm $\sqrt{2024}$ and nonnegative coordinates. Hence $x^2+y^2=2024$. A second Cauchy--Schwarz inequality gives $x+7y\le\sqrt{50(x^2+y^2)}=\sqrt{101200}=20\sqrt{253}$. Equality occurs with $y=7x>0$, consistent with the first condition. Thus $\boxed{a+b=273}$.
