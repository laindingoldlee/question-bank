A ``degenerate triangle'' $ABC$ has its three vertices on one line, with $C$ on segment $AB$, $BC=a$, $CA=b$ and $AB=c=a+b$ ($a,b>0$). Use Heron's formula
\[
[ABC]=\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)},\qquad s=\dfrac{a+b+c}{2},
\]
to find its area, and confirm the result with the formula $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\text{base}\cdot\text{height}$.
