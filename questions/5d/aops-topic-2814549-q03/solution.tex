Count committees of $k$ from $n$ with a chosen spokesperson. Choose the committee, then the spokesperson: $k\binom nk$. Choose the spokesperson first ($n$ ways), then the other $k-1$ members from the remaining $n-1$: $n\binom{n-1}{k-1}$. $\blacksquare$
