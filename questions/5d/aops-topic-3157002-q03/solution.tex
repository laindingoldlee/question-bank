If the side is $ s $, then $ BP=\dfrac{s}{21}$ and $ BQ=\dfrac{s}{18}$. The right triangle has area $\dfrac{s^2}{756}$, so the ratio is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{756}}$.

pair A=(0,0),B=(21,0),C=(21,21),D=(0,21),P=(20,0),Q=(21,21/18);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);filldraw(P--B--Q--cycle,lightgray);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$P$",P,S);label("$Q$",Q,E);
\end{asy}
