The odd part must contain primes $3$ and $5$. Its divisor-count factors multiply to $21$, so the two exponents must be $6,2$ in some order; no third prime can occur since $21$ has only two nontrivial factors. The smaller odd part is $3^6 5^2=18225$, rather than $3^2 5^6$. The least power of two required is $2^1$. Hence $x=2\cdot18225$ and $\boxed{\sqrt{\dfrac{x}{2}}=135}$.
