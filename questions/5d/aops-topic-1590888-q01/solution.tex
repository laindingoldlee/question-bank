Combine the terms from the left, using the difference of squares at each step.
\[
\dfrac{1}{x-1}-\dfrac{1}{x+1}=\dfrac{(x+1)-(x-1)}{x^2-1}=\dfrac{2}{x^2-1},
\]
\[
\dfrac{2}{x^2-1}-\dfrac{2}{x^2+1}=\dfrac{2\left(x^2+1\right)-2\left(x^2-1\right)}{x^4-1}=\dfrac{4}{x^4-1},
\]
\[
\dfrac{4}{x^4-1}-\dfrac{4}{x^4+1}=\dfrac{8}{x^8-1},\qquad
\dfrac{8}{x^8-1}-\dfrac{8}{x^8+1}=\dfrac{16}{x^{16}-1}.
\]
So the expression equals
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{16}{x^{16}-1}}\qquad(x\ne\pm1).
\]
