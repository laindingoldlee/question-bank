If the side length is $s>0$, its area is $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}s^2$ and perimeter is $3s$. Thus $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}s^2=9s$, giving $\boxed{s=12\sqrt3}$.
\begin{asy}
import geometry;
usepackage("amsmath");
size(260);
pair A=(0,0),B=(2,0),C=(1,sqrt(3)); draw(A--B--C--cycle); label("$A$",A,SW); label("$B$",B,SE); label("$C$",C,N); label("$s$",(A+B)/2,S);
\end{asy}
