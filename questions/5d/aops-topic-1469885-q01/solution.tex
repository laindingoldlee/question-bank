The order does not matter and the $3$ sodas must be different, so this is a choice of a $3$-element subset of $8$: $\dbinom83=\dfrac{8\cdot7\cdot6}{6}=\boxed{56}$.

(Compare the next problem, where the same item may be bought several times; then stars and bars is needed instead. Use $\dbinom nk$ when the $k$ chosen objects must be different, and stars and bars, $\dbinom{n+k-1}{k}$, when repeats are allowed and order does not matter.)
