There are $\binom62=15$ segments and $\binom{15}5=3003$ ways to colour five of them. Count the colourings that contain a red triangle and subtract.

For a triangle $T$ (one of $\binom63=20$), the colourings containing all three sides of $T$ choose the other $2$ red segments from the remaining $12$: $\binom{12}2=66$ ways.

Five segments can contain two triangles only if the triangles share a side (otherwise they need $6$ segments); then the five segments are exactly the union. Each segment lies in $4$ triangles, so the number of pairs of triangles sharing a side is $15\binom42=90$, and each pair is contained in exactly one colouring. Five segments never contain three triangles: three triangles have at least $6$ distinct sides between them (two of them already use $5$, and a third triangle inside those $5$ segments would have to be one of the two).

By inclusion--exclusion, the number of colourings with a red triangle is $20\cdot66-90=1230$, so the number of red-triangle-free colourings is
\[
3003-1230=\boxed{1773}.
\]
