Emily draws six dots on a piece of paper, no three on a straight line, and draws a segment joining each pair of dots. She then colours five of these segments red. Her colouring is \emph{red-triangle-free} if for every set of three of the six dots, at least one of the three segments joining them is not red. In how many ways can Emily colour five segments so that the colouring is red-triangle-free?
