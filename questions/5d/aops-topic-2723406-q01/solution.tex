Since $BA=BC$, $B$ is equidistant from $A$ and $C$, so it lies on the perpendicular bisector of $AC$. Likewise $DA=DC$ puts $D$ on that perpendicular bisector. The points $B$ and $D$ are distinct, so line $BD$ is the perpendicular bisector of $AC$, and $AC\perp BD$. $\blacksquare$

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(-1,0),C=(1,0),B=(0,1.2),D=(0,-0.7);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.8));draw(A--C);draw(B--D,dashed);
draw((0,0)--(0.1,0)--(0.1,0.1)--(0,0.1));
label("$A$",A,W);label("$B$",B,N);label("$C$",C,E);label("$D$",D,S);
\end{asy}
