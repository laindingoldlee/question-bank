For cube edge $a$, the surface points nearest the center are face centers, at distance $\dfrac{a}{2}$, and the farthest are vertices, at distance $\dfrac{\sqrt3a}{2}$. Thus $\dfrac{a}{2}\ge2$ and $\dfrac{\sqrt3a}{2}\le4$, or $4\le a\le\dfrac{8}{\sqrt3}$. This interval is nonempty, so the maximum is $\boxed{\dfrac{8}{\sqrt3}=\dfrac{8\sqrt3}{3}}$.

\end{asy}
