$P=2+4+\dots+60=2\cdot\dfrac{30\cdot31}{2}=930$. The odd numbers $1,3,\dots,33$ are the first $17$ odd numbers, with sum $17^2=289$, so $Q=289-1=288$. By the difference of squares,
\[
P^2-Q^2=(930-288)(930+288)=642\cdot1218=\boxed{781956}.
\]
