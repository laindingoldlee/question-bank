Find the smallest positive integer solution $(A,B,C)$ to this linked system. A right triangle has hypotenuse-square area $2B$ and one leg-square area $B$; $A$ is its other leg length rounded up. The integer $B$ is the digit sum of $C$. Finally, $C=a_A$, where $a_0=1,a_1=2$, and $a_n=3a_{n-1}-a_{n-2}$ for $n\ge2$. Minimize $A$.
