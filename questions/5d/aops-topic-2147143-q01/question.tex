Prove or disprove each statement.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Every odd integer can be written as a sum of two or more consecutive integers.
\item[(b)] For every integer $k\ge2$, every odd integer can be written as a sum of $k$ consecutive integers.
\end{enumerate}
