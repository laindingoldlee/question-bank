Let $M$ and $N$ be points on the diagonals $AC$ and $CE$, respectively, of the regular hexagon $ABCDEF$, such that
\[
\dfrac{AM}{AC}=\dfrac{CN}{CE}=r .
\]
Determine $r$ so that the points $B$, $M$ and $N$ are collinear.
