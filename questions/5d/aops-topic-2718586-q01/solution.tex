\textbf{(b)} Each draw is white with probability $\dfrac13$, independently:
\[\sum_{k=0}^{2}\dbinom6k\left(\dfrac13\right)^k\left(\dfrac23\right)^{6-k}=\dfrac{64+6\cdot32+15\cdot16}{729}=\boxed{\dfrac{496}{729}}.\]
