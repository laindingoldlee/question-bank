The middle row gives magic sum $3+5+7=15$. The top row gives $8+x+6=15$, so $x=1$; the bottom row gives $4+y+2=15$, so $y=9$. Thus $\boxed{x+y=10}$. The other columns and diagonals also sum to $15$.
