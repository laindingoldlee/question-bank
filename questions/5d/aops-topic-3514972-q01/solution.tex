Give each person two marbles first. The remaining counts satisfy $x_1+\cdots+x_{12}=60$ with $0\le x_i\le12$. Inclusion-exclusion on coordinates at least $13$ gives\[\sum_{j=0}^{4}(-1)^j\binom{12}{j}\binom{71-13j}{11}=\boxed{469831301446}.\]For a chosen set of $j$ violating coordinates, subtract $13$ from each and apply stars and bars to the remaining sum $60-13j$. Five violations are impossible because $65>60$.
