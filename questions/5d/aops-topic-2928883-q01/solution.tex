$2^i=e^{i\ln2}=\cos(\ln2)+i\sin(\ln2)\approx0.769+0.639i$. And $i=\exp\left(\dfrac{i\pi}2\right)$ (principal argument), so $i^i=\exp\left(i\cdot\dfrac{i\pi}2\right)=\boxed{\exp\left(-\dfrac\pi2\right)}\approx0.208$, a real number.
