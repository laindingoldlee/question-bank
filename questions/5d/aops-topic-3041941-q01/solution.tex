The digit sum is an integer, so the number must be a perfect square. The two-digit squares are $16,25,36,49,64,81$. Their digit sums are respectively $7,7,9,13,10,9$, whereas their square roots are $4,5,6,7,8,9$. Only $\boxed{81}$ satisfies the condition.
