Complete the square in $x$ and in $y$:
\[
\left(x^2+16x+64\right)+\left(y^2-24y+144\right)=-159+64+144,
\]
\[
(x+8)^2+(y-12)^2=49 .
\]
So the circle has centre $(-8,12)$ and radius $\sqrt{49}=7$, and its diameter is $\boxed{14}$.

dot(O);label("$(-8,12)$",O,N);
draw((-15,3.6)--(-1,3.6),red,Arrows(TeXHead));draw((-15,3)--(-15,12),red+dotted);draw((-1,3)--(-1,12),red+dotted);label("$14$",(-8,3.6),S,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
