For arbitrary positive starting values $u,v$, the recurrence produces $u,v,\dfrac{v+1}{u},\dfrac{u+v+1}{uv},\dfrac{u+1}{v},u,v,\ldots$. Each equality follows by substitution, and positivity keeps denominators nonzero. Thus the sequence repeats every five terms. Since $1997\equiv2\pmod5$, $a_{1997}=a_2=\boxed{1828}$.
