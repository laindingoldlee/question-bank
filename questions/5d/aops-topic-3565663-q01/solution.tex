After $n$ replacements the milk fraction is $(\dfrac{9}{10})^n$. It must be less than $\dfrac{1}{2}$. At $n=6$ the fraction is $0.531441>0.5$; at $n=7$ it is $0.4782969<0.5$. Since it decreases at every replacement, the least number is $\boxed{7}$.
