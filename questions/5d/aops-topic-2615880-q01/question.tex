Shopkeeper Rubab is lazy: he ignores paisa and rounds every total price down to a whole number of taka ($100$ paisa $=1$ taka). For example, a total of $11$ taka $30$ paisa is calculated as $11$ taka. By his calculation, five chocolates cost $13$ taka and six chocolates cost $16$ taka. All chocolates have the same price, which is a whole number of paisa. How many different prices can one chocolate have?
