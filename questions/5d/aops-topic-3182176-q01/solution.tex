Choose remainders in the allowed digit set: $-15=(-4)\cdot4+1$, $4=(-4)(-1)+0$, $-1=(-4)\cdot1+3$, and $1=(-4)\cdot0+1$. Reading remainders backwards gives $\boxed{(1301)_{-4}}$. Check: $(-4)^3+3(-4)^2+1=-64+48+1=-15$.
