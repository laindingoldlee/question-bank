Allen lives in building $A$ and Ben lives in building $B$, at opposite ends of a straight street. At the same moment Allen sets off towards $B$ and Ben sets off towards $A$, each walking at his own constant speed; on reaching the far end, each immediately turns around and walks back towards his own building. They meet for the first time $300$ m from Allen's building $A$. When they meet for the second time they are walking towards each other, and this meeting is $400$ m from Ben's building $B$. How long is the street, and who walks faster, Allen or Ben?
