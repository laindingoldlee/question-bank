Factor the difference of cubes $x^6-y^6=\left(x^2\right)^3-\left(y^2\right)^3$:
\[\dfrac{x^6-y^6}{x^2-y^2}=x^4+x^2y^2+y^4=\left(x^4+y^4\right)+(xy)^2=81+729=\boxed{810}.\]

\emph{Remark.} Such numbers cannot both be real: for real $x,y$ we would have $x^4+y^4\ge2x^2y^2=1458>81$. They do exist as complex numbers: $x^4$ and $y^4$ must be the roots of $t^2-81t+3^{12}=0$, which are distinct, non-real complex numbers; choosing $x$ with $x^4$ equal to one root and $y^2=\dfrac{729}{x^2}$ gives $y^4$ equal to the other root, $(xy)^2=729$, and $x^2\ne y^2$ because $x^4\ne y^4$.
