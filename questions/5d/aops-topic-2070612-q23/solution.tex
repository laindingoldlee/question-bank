\(\overline{AB}+\overline{BC}+\overline{CA}=(10A+B)+(10B+C)+(10C+A)=11(A+B+C)=88\), so \(A+B+C=8\). For nonnegative integers with fixed sum, the product is largest when the numbers are as equal as possible. (If two differ by at least \(2\), moving one unit from the larger to the smaller does not decrease the product.) So the maximum is at \(\{3,3,2\}\), with product \(\boxed{\textbf{(A) } 18}\).
