By symmetry the blue ball is equally likely to be in each of the $6$ positions of the drawing order, so the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{6}}$. (Directly: $\dfrac{5}{6}\cdot\dfrac{1}{5}=\dfrac{1}{6}$.)
