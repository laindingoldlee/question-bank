In base $2i$ (the quater-imaginary system), a numeral $d_nd_{n-1}\dots d_0$ with digits $d_k\in\{0,1,2,3\}$ stands for $\sum d_k(2i)^k$.

(a) Find the complex number represented by $1031_{2i}$.

(b) Write $15$ in base $2i$.
