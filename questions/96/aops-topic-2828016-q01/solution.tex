The powers of $2i$ are $1,\ 2i,\ -4,\ -8i,\ 16,\dots$

(a) $1\cdot(-8i)+0\cdot(-4)+3\cdot2i+1=\boxed{1-2i}$.

(b) Real numbers use only even positions, i.e. base $(2i)^2=-4$. Write $15=d_0+d_2(-4)+d_4(16)$: take $d_4=1$, then $-1=d_0-4d_2$ gives $d_2=1$, $d_0=3$. Check: $16-4+3=15$. So $15=\boxed{10103_{2i}}$.
