Write the line as $y=m(x+2)$. Intersections satisfy $x^2-mx-2m=0$. Distinct real intersections require $m\ne0$, so Vieta gives $\left|\dfrac{x_1x_2}{x_1+x_2}\right|=\left|\dfrac{-2m}{m}\right|=\boxed2$.

\end{asy}
