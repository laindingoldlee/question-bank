Zero errors have probability $(\dfrac{9}{10})^{10}$, and exactly one has probability $10(\dfrac{1}{10})(\dfrac{9}{10})^9$. Their sum is $\boxed{\dfrac{19\cdot9^9}{10^{10}}=0.7360989291}$.
