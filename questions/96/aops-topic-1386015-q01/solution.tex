Since the circles touch externally, $O_1O_2=25+16=41$. The radii $O_1A$ and $O_2B$ are perpendicular to the tangent line, so $O_1ABO_2$ is a right trapezoid. Let $H$ be the point of $O_1A$ with $O_1H=25-16=9$ (so $HA=16$); then $HABO_2$ is a rectangle, so $HO_2=AB$ and $HO_2\perp O_1A$.

\begin{asy}

pair O1=(0,25),O2=(40,16),A=(0,0),B=(40,0),H=(0,16);

label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

In right triangle $O_1HO_2$,
\[
AB=HO_2=\sqrt{41^2-9^2}=\sqrt{1600}=\boxed{40}\text{ cm}.
\]
(In general $AB=2\sqrt{r_1r_2}=2\sqrt{25\cdot16}$.)
