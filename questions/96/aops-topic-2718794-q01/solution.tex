Collecting terms, $F(x)=-x^3+7x^2-2x+1$. Both have degree $3$, so the quotient is the ratio of leading coefficients, $Q(x)=\boxed{-1}$, and the remainder is $F(x)+H(x)=\boxed{6x^2-2x+2}$.
