Only the last three digits of the product matter, and they depend only on the last three digits of the factors:
\[89998\cdot78887\equiv998\cdot887\equiv(-2)\cdot887=-1774\equiv226\pmod{1000}.\]
So the hundreds digit is $\boxed{2}$. (Indeed $89998\cdot78887=(90000-2)\cdot78887=7099830000-157774=7099672226$.)
