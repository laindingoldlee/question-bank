If completion takes $t$ days, the amounts done give $\dfrac{t-8}{36}+\dfrac{t-12}{54}+\dfrac{t}{72}=1$. Multiplying by 216 gives $6(t-8)+4(t-12)+3t=216$, hence $13t=312$. Thus $\boxed{t=24}$ days. The other durations 16 and 12 days are positive, so the solution satisfies the schedule.
