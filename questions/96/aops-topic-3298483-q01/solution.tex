To avoid adjacent dictionaries, the type pattern must be dictionary--other--dictionary--other--dictionary. There are $3!2!$ such arrangements out of $5!$. Therefore the desired probability is $1-\dfrac{3!2!}{5!}=\boxed{\dfrac{9}{10}}$.
