The equilateral side length $s$ satisfies $\dfrac{s^2\sqrt3}{4}=\sqrt3$, so $s=2$ and circumradius $\dfrac{2}{\sqrt3}$. The arc from $A$ to $C$ through the point opposite $B$ has central angle $120^\circ$. Its length is $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\cdot\dfrac{2}{\sqrt3}=\boxed{\dfrac{4\pi\sqrt3}{9}}$.
\begin{asy}
import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
pair A=dir(120),B=(1,0),C=dir(240),R=(-1,0);draw(unitcircle);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(B--R,dashed);draw(arc((0,0),1,120,240),blue+linewidth(1.5));label("$A$",A,(-1,1));label("$B$",B,(1,0));label("$C$",C,(-1,-1));label("$R$",R,(-1,0));
\end{asy}
