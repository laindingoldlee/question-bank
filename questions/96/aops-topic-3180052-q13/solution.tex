Every ten consecutive integers contain exactly two multiples of 5. They contain two multiples of 7 precisely when $m\equiv0,5,6\pmod7$. The first 98 starting values include each residue 14 times;99 and 100 have residues 1 and 2. Hence the count is $3\cdot14=\boxed{42}$.
