The dimensions $x,y,z$ satisfy $x+y+z=14$, $xy+yz+zx=59$ and $xyz=70$, so they are the roots of $t^3-14t^2+59t-70=(t-2)(t-5)(t-7)$. The box is $\boxed{2\times5\times7}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import three;currentprojection=orthographic(4,3,2);draw(scale(2,5,7)*unitcube,white,black);
\end{asy}
