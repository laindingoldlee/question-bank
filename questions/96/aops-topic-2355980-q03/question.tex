The numbers $M,A,S,H$ satisfy
\[M+A+S+H=10,\qquad M+A=3,\qquad S+A=5.\]
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that these equations do not determine the value of $H$.
\item[(b)] Show that they do determine $H-A$, and find it.
\item[(c)] If $M,A,S,H$ are positive integers, what are the possible values of $H$?
\end{enumerate}
