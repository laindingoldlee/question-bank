From the first two equations, $S+H=10-3=7$, so $H=7-S=7-(5-A)=A+2$.

\textbf{(a)} The value of $A$ is free: for example $(M,A,S,H)=(3,0,5,2)$ and $(2,1,4,3)$ both satisfy all three equations, with different values of $H$. Three equations in four unknowns do not determine $H$.

\textbf{(b)} $H=A+2$ for every solution, so $H-A=\boxed{2}$.

\textbf{(c)} $M+A=3$ with positive integers forces $A=1$ or $A=2$ (then $M=2$ or $1$, and $S=5-A=4$ or $3$). So $H=A+2$ is $\boxed{3\text{ or }4}$: the solutions are $(2,1,4,3)$ and $(1,2,3,4)$.
