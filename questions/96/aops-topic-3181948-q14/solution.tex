Let $t=x^2-9x$ and $u=t+14$. Pairing factors with roots summing to 9 reduces the equation to $(t+8)(t+14)(t+20)=-t-64$, hence $u^3-35u+50=(u-5)(u^2+5u-10)=0$. Its three possible values are $5,\dfrac{-5+\sqrt{65}}{2},\dfrac{-5-\sqrt{65}}{2}$. The associated quadratic in $x$ has discriminant $25+4u$. The first two give positive discriminants; the third gives $15-2\sqrt{65}<0$ and no real roots. Each valid quadratic has root product $t=u-14$, so the product of all four real roots is $(-9)\cdot\dfrac{-33+\sqrt{65}}{2}=\dfrac{297-9\sqrt{65}}{2}$. Thus $\boxed{a+b+c+d=373}$.
