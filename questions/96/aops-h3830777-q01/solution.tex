If $k$ students score at least $3$, the total score is at most $5k+2(35-k)=70+3k$. Since the actual total is greater than $140$, we need $70+3k>140$, so $k\ge24$. This bound is attained by giving $24$ students $5$ points and the other $11$ students $2$ points: the total is $142>140$. Thus the minimum is $\boxed{24}$.
