The first circle has radius $R$ equal to half the diagonal, $R=\dfrac{\sqrt{A^2+B^2}}{2}$, and the second has radius $r=\dfrac {A}{2}$ (half the distance between the longer sides).

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real A=2, B=4;
pair O=(0,0);
draw(circle(O,sqrt(A^2+B^2)/2));
fill(circle(O,sqrt(A^2+B^2)/2),paleblue); fill(circle(O,A/2),white);
draw(circle(O,sqrt(A^2+B^2)/2)); draw(circle(O,A/2));
draw(box((-B/2,-A/2),(B/2,A/2)),linewidth(1));
dot(O); label("$O$",O,SW);
draw(O--(B/2,A/2),dashed); label("$R$",(0.68*B/2,0.68*A/2),NW);
draw(O--(0,A/2),dashed); label("$r$",(0,A/4),W);
label("$B$",(0,-A/2),S); label("$A$",(B/2,0),W);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The area between them is
\[
\pi\cdot\dfrac{A^2+B^2}{4}-\pi\cdot\dfrac{A^2}{4}=\dfrac{\pi B^2}{4}=4\pi ,
\]
so $B^2=16$ and $B=\boxed{4}$. (The answer does not depend on $A$.)
