A rectangle has side lengths $A$ and $B$ with $B>A$, and centre $O$. One circle with centre $O$ passes through the four vertices of the rectangle, and a second circle with centre $O$ is tangent to the two longer sides (of length $B$). The region between the two circles has area $4\pi$. Find $B$.
