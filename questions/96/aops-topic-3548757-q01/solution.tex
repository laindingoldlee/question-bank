First reserve one apple and two oranges per person. There remain 13 apples and 5 oranges, each freely distributed. Stars and bars gives $\binom{19}6$ apple distributions and $\binom{11}6$ orange distributions. Their independent product is $27132\cdot 462=\boxed{12534984}$.
