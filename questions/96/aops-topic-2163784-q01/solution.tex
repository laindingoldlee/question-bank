For a prime $p$, Legendre's formula gives the exponent of $p$ in $\dbinom{200}{100}=\dfrac{200!}{100!\,100!}$ as
\[v_p=\sum_{k\ge1}\left(\left\lfloor\dfrac{200}{p^k}\right\rfloor-2\left\lfloor\dfrac{100}{p^k}\right\rfloor\right).\]
Test the three-digit numbers from the top.
\begin{itemize}
\item $999=3^3\cdot37$: $v_3=(66-66)+(22-22)+(7-6)+(2-2)=1$, so $27$ does not divide $\dbinom{200}{100}$.
\item $998=2\cdot499$ and $997$ (a prime): the primes $499$ and $997$ exceed $200$, so they do not divide $200!$.
\item $996=2^2\cdot3\cdot83$: $v_{83}=\left\lfloor\dfrac{200}{83}\right\rfloor-2\left\lfloor\dfrac{100}{83}\right\rfloor=2-2=0$, so $83$ does not divide it.
\item $995=5\cdot199$: $v_{199}=1-0=1$ and $v_5=(40-40)+(8-8)+(1-0)=1$, so $5\cdot199$ divides $\dbinom{200}{100}$.
\end{itemize}
The answer is $\boxed{995}$.
