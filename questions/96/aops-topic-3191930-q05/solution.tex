The reverse triangle inequality gives at least $30-18=12$, attained on the same ray from the third person. Thus $\boxed{12}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair O=(0,0),A=(18,0),B=(30,0);draw(O--B);dot(O);dot(A);dot(B);label("$O$",O,S);label("$A$",A,S);label("$B$",B,S);label("$18$",(O+A)/2,N);label("$12$",(A+B)/2,N);
\end{asy}
