Let the integer be $n$. The product of $n$ and $4\cdot\dfrac{1}{n}$ is $4$, so
\[
n+\dfrac{4}{n}=4\ \Longrightarrow\ n^2-4n+4=0\ \Longrightarrow\ (n-2)^2=0,
\]
and $n=\boxed{2}$. Check: $2+2=4=2\cdot2$.
