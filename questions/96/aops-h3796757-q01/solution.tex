At $x=-2$, the value is $0^{12}=0$. Otherwise the base is positive. A positive real power equals $1$ precisely when the base is $1$ or the exponent is $0$. The former gives $(x+2)^2=1$, hence $x=-3,-1$. The latter gives $(x-1)(x-2)=0$, hence $x=1,2$. All four satisfy the equation, so the answer is $\boxed{\{-3,-1,1,2\}}$.
