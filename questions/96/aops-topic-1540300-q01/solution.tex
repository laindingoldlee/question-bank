\textbf{Without $0$.} The positive multiples of $3$ below $100$ are $3\cdot1,\dots,3\cdot33$: there are $33$. Those that are also multiples of $5$ are the multiples of $15$: $15\cdot1,\dots,15\cdot6$, that is $6$ numbers. This leaves $33-6=27$.

\textbf{With $0$.} Now $0$ is a multiple of $3$, so there are $34$ multiples of $3$; but $0$ is also a multiple of $5$ (and of $15$), so there are $7$ multiples of $15$. This leaves $34-7=27$.

Either way the answer is $\boxed{27}$: the number $0$ is added to both counts and removed again, because it is a multiple of $5$ and so is excluded anyway.
