Jar A starts with $12$ buttons and ends with $\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot12=8$, so $4$ buttons were moved: $2$ red and $2$ blue. Now Jar A has $2$ red and $6$ blue buttons, and Jar B has $2$ red and $2$ blue. The two draws are independent, so
\[
P(\text{both red})=\dfrac{2}{8}\cdot\dfrac{2}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{8}}.
\]
