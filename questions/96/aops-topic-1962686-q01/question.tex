A box contains $4$ chocolates and $1$ fruit chew. Clark and Chloe take turns drawing a treat out of the box at random, without replacement, and whoever draws the fruit chew wins. Clark draws first. What is the probability that Chloe wins? Express your answer as a common fraction.
