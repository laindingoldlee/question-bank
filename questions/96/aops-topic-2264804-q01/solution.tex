Let the numbers be $a\ge b$ and $d=\gcd(a,b)$. Since $d$ divides both $a$ and $b$, it divides $a-b$.

If $a>b$, then $a-b$ is a positive multiple of $d$, so $a-b\ge d$, contradicting $d>a-b$. Hence $a=b$. Conversely, if $a=b=n$, then $\gcd(n,n)=n>0=a-b$.

So the pairs are exactly the pairs of \textbf{equal numbers} $\boxed{(n,n)}$, for every positive integer $n$.
