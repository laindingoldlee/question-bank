In right triangle $ABC$ the right angle is at $B$ and $AB=BC=14$. Point $E$ lies on segment $BA$ and point $D$ lies on segment $BC$ with $BE=BD=6$. Segments $AD$ and $CE$ meet at $F$. Find the area of triangle $AFC$.

(A student gets $56$ by assuming that $F$ lies $\dfrac{4}{7}$ of the way from $B$ along the median to the midpoint of $AC$. Is this right?)
