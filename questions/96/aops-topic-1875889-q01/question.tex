Prove the SSS congruence criterion: if triangles $ABC$ and $A'B'C'$ satisfy $AB=A'B'$, $BC=B'C'$ and $CA=C'A'$, then the triangles are congruent. You may use the SAS criterion, the possibility of copying an angle, and the fact that the base angles of an isosceles triangle are equal.
