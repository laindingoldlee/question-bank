Since $AB=BC$, triangle $ABC$ has equal angles at $A,C$. Let $\angle ABC=\beta$. Then $2\cdot\dfrac{5}{2}\beta+\beta=180^\circ$, so $\beta=30^\circ$ and $\angle ACB=75^\circ$. Vertically opposite angle $DCE$ is also $75^\circ$. Since $CD=CE$, its base angles are $\dfrac{180^\circ-75^\circ}{2}=52.5^\circ$. Hence $x=10\cdot52.5=\boxed{525}$.
