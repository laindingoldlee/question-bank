The $k$th bracket is $3k+3$, with sign $+$ for odd $k$ and $-$ for even $k$, for $k=1,\dots,98$. Pairing $k=2j-1$ with $k=2j$ gives $(6j)-(6j+3)=-3$, and there are $49$ pairs: $-147$. \textbf{(B)}
