Let $0^\circ<\alpha<90^\circ$ and $0^\circ<\beta<90^\circ$. On the unit circle centred at the origin $O$, let $A=(\cos\alpha,\sin\alpha)$ and $B=(\cos\beta,-\sin\beta)$, and let segment $AB$ meet the $x$-axis at $X$. By computing the area of triangle $AOB$ in two ways (once with the formula $\dfrac{1}{2}\,OA\cdot OB\sin\angle AOB$, and once as $[AOX]+[BOX]$), prove that
\[\sin(\alpha+\beta)=\sin\alpha\cos\beta+\cos\alpha\sin\beta.\]
