Let $n=2006$. Then $(n+1)^3-(n-1)^3=\left(n^3+3n^2+3n+1\right)-\left(n^3-3n^2+3n-1\right)=6n^2+2$. So the expression equals $6n^2+2-6n^2=\boxed{2}$.
