The initial color swap is equally likely to reduce the red count to $2018$ or increase it to $2020$. Therefore the probability is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{2018}{4038}+\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{2020}{4038}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$.
