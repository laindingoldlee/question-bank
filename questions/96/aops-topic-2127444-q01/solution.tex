A shortest route consists of $5$ steps right and $6$ steps up in some order, so there are $\dbinom{11}{5}=462$ routes in all.

A route through $(2,3)$ consists of a route from $(0,0)$ to $(2,3)$, in $\dbinom{5}{2}=10$ ways, followed by a route from $(2,3)$ to $(5,6)$ (three steps right and three up), in $\dbinom{6}{3}=20$ ways. So $10\cdot20=200$ routes pass through the construction site, and the number of allowed routes is
\[462-200=\boxed{262}.\]
