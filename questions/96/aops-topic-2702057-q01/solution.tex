$-x^3+7x^2-15x+100=100-x\left(x^2-7x+15\right)$, and $x^2-7x+15$ has discriminant $49-60<0$, so it is positive. Hence for $x\ge0$ the value is at most $100$, attained at $x=0$. The maximum is $\boxed{100}$.
