With sides $l$ and $w$, $lw=2l+2w$, i.e. $(l-2)(w-2)=4$. The positive factorisations give $\{l-2,w-2\}=\{1,4\}$ or $\{2,2\}$, i.e. $6\times3$ or the square $4\times4$. (If $l-2$ and $w-2$ were both negative, one of $l,w$ would be at most $0$.)

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

So the rectangle is $6\times3$, and its perimeter is $\boxed{18}$ inches (equal to its area).
