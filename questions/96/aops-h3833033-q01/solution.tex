Partition the upper-left $6\times6$ portion into nine disjoint $2\times2$ blocks. In each block, at least two of its four fruits are identical by the pigeonhole principle. Any two cells of a $2\times2$ block share a side or corner, so remove such a pair. The blocks are disjoint, so these nine moves remove $\boxed{18}$ fruits without interfering with each other.
