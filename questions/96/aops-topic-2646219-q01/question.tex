For a complex number $w$, let $\sqrt w$ denote the principal square root (for $w\ge0$ it is the nonnegative root, and in general $\operatorname{Re}\sqrt w\ge0$). Prove that a complex number $z$ satisfies $|z|=\sqrt{z^2}$ if and only if $z$ is real.
