Let an ordinary plough do $o$ fields per day and a tractor $t=ko$. Then $6(3o+2t)=1$, so $o=\dfrac{1}{6(3+2k)}$. The times are $\dfrac{1}{9o}=\dfrac{2(3+2k)}{3}$ for nine ordinary ploughs and $\dfrac{1}{3ko}=\dfrac{2(3+2k)}{k}$ for three tractors, so
\[
\dfrac{2(3+2k)}{3}-\dfrac{2(3+2k)}{k}=5\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\dfrac{2(2k+3)(k-3)}{3k}=5\quad\Longrightarrow\quad4k^2-21k-18=0 .
\]
So $(k-6)(4k+3)=0$ and $k=\boxed{6}$: a tractor plough does $6$ times the work of an ordinary one. (Check: $o=\dfrac{1}{90}$; nine ordinary ploughs take $10$ days and three tractors take $5$ days.)
