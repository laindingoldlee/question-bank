The equation splits into $x^2-20x-1=20$ and $x^2-20x-1=-20$. The first is $(x-21)(x+1)=0$; the second is $(x-1)(x-19)=0$. Each quadratic has two real roots, so there are four solutions: $\boxed{x\in\{-1,1,19,21\}}$.
