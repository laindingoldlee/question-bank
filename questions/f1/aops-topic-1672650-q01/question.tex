Using the digits $6,5,5,2,5,2$ in this order, make $100$. You may join adjacent digits into multi-digit numbers (such as $65$ or $552$) and combine the resulting numbers, which keep their order, with $+$, $-$, $\times$, $\div$ and parentheses.
