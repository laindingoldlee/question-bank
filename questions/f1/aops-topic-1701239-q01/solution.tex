Track the amount of alcohol $a_k$ (in gallons) after $k$ procedures; the volume stays $100$ gallons. Draining $25$ gallons removes a quarter of the alcohol, and the $25$ gallons added contain $0.2\cdot25=5$ gallons of alcohol, so $a_{k+1}=\dfrac{3}{4}a_k+5$, with $a_0=50$:
\[
a_1=37.5+5=42.5,\qquad a_2=31.875+5=36.875,\qquad a_3=27.65625+5=32.65625 .
\]
(The procedure is done three times in all: once, then twice more.) So the final solution is $32.65625\%$ alcohol, which is $\boxed{33}$ to the nearest whole number.

Alternatively, the excess over $20\%$ shrinks by the factor $\dfrac{3}{4}$ each time: $a_3-20=(50-20)\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^3=\dfrac{810}{64}=12.65625$.
