Let $S=a+b+c+d$. Adding the equations gives $6S=12$, hence $S=2$. Each equation now reads $S+2a=4$, $S+2b=-2$, $S+2c=10$, or $S+2d=0$. Thus $\boxed{(a,b,c,d)=(1,-2,4,-1)}$, whose entries sum to $2$ as required.
