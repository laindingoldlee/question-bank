For three real numbers the sum of pairwise absolute differences is twice the range. Thus the smallest digit is $m$ and the largest is $m+5$, where $m=0,1,2,3,4$. For fixed $m$, ordered triples drawn from the six digits $m,\ldots,m+5$ and containing both endpoints number $6^3-2\cdot5^3+4^3=30$ by inclusion-exclusion. For $m=0$, remove triples starting with zero: their last two positions must include $5$, giving $6^2-5^2=11$. The total is $\boxed{5\cdot30-11=139}$.
