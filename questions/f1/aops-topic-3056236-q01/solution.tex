If the initial count is $ N $, the remainder is $\dfrac{8}{27}N $. Since each removal uses whole candies, $ N $ must be a multiple of 27: successive divisibility by 3 forces this. Thus the final count is a positive multiple of 8 and cannot be 12. The least possible remainder is $\boxed8 $, obtained from $\boxed{27}$ candies by the sequence $ 27\to18\to12\to8 $.
