A photon starts between a wall (on its left) and the first of $2047$ parallel ``magical'' mirrors, and moves to the right, perpendicular to the mirrors. To the right of the last mirror is a detector. Whenever the photon hits a mirror, from either side, it passes through with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and is reflected straight back with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, independently of everything else. If the photon hits the wall it is destroyed; if it passes the last mirror it reaches the detector. What is the probability that the detector detects the photon? Express your answer as a common fraction.
