There are $2^4=16$ equally likely sequences. Exactly three heads occur in $\dbinom43=4$ sequences and four heads in $1$ sequence, so the probability is
\[
\dfrac{4+1}{16}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{16}}.
\]
