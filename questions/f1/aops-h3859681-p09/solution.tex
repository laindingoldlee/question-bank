For a candidate value $n$, rearrangement gives $(3a-n)(3b-n)=n^2$. Both factors are positive because $n=\dfrac{3ab}{a+b}<3\min(a,b)$. Thus admissibility is equivalent to a divisor $d<n$ of $n^2$ for which both $d+n$ and $\dfrac{n^2}{d}+n$ are divisible by three; then $a=\dfrac{d+n}{3}$ and $b=\dfrac{\dfrac{n^2}{d}+n}{3}$ are distinct positive integers. Testing these divisors for $1\le n<100$ rejects exactly $1,3,7,13,19,31,37,43,49,61,67,73,79,91,97$. Therefore $99-15=\boxed{84}$ values qualify.
