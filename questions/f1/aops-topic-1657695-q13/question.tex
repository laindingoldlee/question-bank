Let $P(x)=(x-3)(x-9)$, and let $G_n(x)$ denote $P$ applied $n$ times: $G_1(x)=P(x)$, $G_2(x)=P(P(x))$, $G_3(x)=P(P(P(x)))$, and so forth. What is the sum of the roots of $G_{10}(x)$, counted with multiplicity?
