$G_n$ has degree $2^n$. Its roots are the solutions of $P(x)=y$ as $y$ runs over the $2^{n-1}$ roots of $G_{n-1}$ (with multiplicity), because $G_n(x)=G_{n-1}(P(x))$. For each such $y$, the equation $x^2-12x+27-y=0$ has two roots with sum $12$. So the sum of the roots of $G_n$ is $12\cdot2^{n-1}$, and for $n=10$ it is $12\cdot512=\boxed{6144}$.
