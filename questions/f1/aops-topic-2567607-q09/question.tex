Four basketball teams have distinct, fixed skill levels, and whenever two teams play, the more skilled team always wins. The manager does not know the skill levels. Every day, he chooses two of the four teams uniformly at random (independently of earlier days) to play each other and records the result; two teams may play each other more than once. What is the expected number of days until the manager can determine with certainty the order of the four teams' skill levels?
