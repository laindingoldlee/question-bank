The $n$-th factor ($n\ge2$) is $1-\dfrac{1}{n^2}=\dfrac{(n-1)(n+1)}{n\cdot n}$. The partial product up to $n=N$ telescopes:
\[
\prod_{n=2}^{N}\dfrac{n-1}{n}\cdot\prod_{n=2}^{N}\dfrac{n+1}{n}=\dfrac{1}{N}\cdot\dfrac{N+1}{2}=\dfrac{N+1}{2N}\to\dfrac{1}{2} .
\]
So the infinite product equals $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$.
