The remainder of $x^2$ on division by $9$ depends only on the remainder of $x$. Squaring $0,1,\dots,8$ gives the remainders
\[0,\ 1,\ 4,\ 0,\ 7,\ 7,\ 0,\ 4,\ 1,\]
so $x^2\equiv7\pmod 9$ exactly when $x\equiv4$ or $x\equiv5\pmod9$. (Because $9$ is not prime, factoring $x^2-16$ would not give this directly: $9$ could split between the two factors.) The numbers greater than $23$ in these classes are $31,32,40,41,\dots$, so the least is $\boxed{31}$. Check: $31^2=961=9\cdot106+7$.
