Write $d=\overline{AB}$. The number is $2000+d$, so d divides it exactly when d divides 2000. Also $26\le d\le 99$. Since $2000=2^4\cdot 5^3$, its divisors in this interval are 40,50,80. Their sum is $\boxed{170}$.
