In both cases every remaining card has an even number: either one of $2,4,\dots,18$, or twice a whole number.
\begin{itemize}
\item If the first card is odd, the sum is odd $+$ even $=$ odd, whatever the second card is.
\item If the first card is even, the sum is even $+$ even $=$ even.
\end{itemize}
So the sum is odd exactly when the first card is odd, which has probability $\dfrac{5}{10}=\boxed{\dfrac12}$.
