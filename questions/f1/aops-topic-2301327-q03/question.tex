RetroTurtle has a deck of $10$ cards labelled $1$ through $10$ and draws one card at random. If its number is odd, the $9$ remaining cards are relabelled with the even numbers $2,4,\dots,18$; if its number is even, the number on each of the $9$ remaining cards is doubled. He then draws a second card at random from the $9$ remaining cards. What is the probability that the sum of the two numbers is odd?
