The numbers in the set



\[
S=\{5,6,7,\ldots,n\}
\]

are each colored either red or blue.



If the product of any two numbers in \(S\) with the same color is also in \(S\), then that product has the opposite color.



If the square of any number in \(S\) is also in \(S\), then that square has the opposite color.



What is the greatest possible value of \(n\)?



\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{a)}\ 624 & \text{b)}\ 719 \\
\text{c)}\ 1224 & \text{d)}\ 4095 \\
\text{e)}\ \text{None}
\end{array}
\]
