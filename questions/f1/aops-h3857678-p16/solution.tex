The maximum is $\boxed{3124}$, hence choice E. To color through 3124, color $5,\ldots,24$ and $625,\ldots,3124$ red, and $25,\ldots,624$ blue. A product of two small red numbers lies from 25 to 576 and is blue. A product involving a large red number exceeds 3124. A product of two blue numbers, when in range, is at least 625 and is red. This includes squares. For impossibility at 3125, assume 5 is red. Then 25 is blue and 625 is red. Since $5\cdot125=625$, 125 must be blue. But $25\cdot125=3125$ forces 3125 red, while $5\cdot625=3125$ forces it blue, a contradiction.
