The event that every roll is at most four is contained in the event that every roll is at most five. Subtracting their probabilities leaves exactly the outcomes with every roll at most five and at least one five. That is precisely maximum five, even if several dice tie at five. Independence gives $\boxed{\left(\dfrac{5}{6}\right)^n-\left(\dfrac{4}{6}\right)^n}$.
