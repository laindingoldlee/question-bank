Since the right side is positive, $a^b>b^a$, so $a\ne b$. If one of the numbers is $1$: $a=1$ gives $1-b=1+b$, impossible; $b=1$ gives $a-1=a+1$, impossible. So $a,b\ge2$.

For $2\le a<b$ we usually have $a^b>b^a$ except in the cases $(2,3)$ and $(2,4)$: $2^3-3^2=-1$ and $2^4-4^2=0$, neither works. Try $a=2$: $2^b-b^2=b+2$ gives $b=5$: $32-25=7$, which works. For $b=6,7,8,9$ the left side is $28,79,192,431$ while the right is at most $11$.
For $a=3$: $b=4,5,\dots$ give $3^b-b^3=17,118,\dots$ against $a+b\le12$, and these differences grow; for $a\ge4$, $a^b-b^a\ge 4^5-5^4=399>18$.
If $a>b\ge2$ then $a^b<b^a$ except $(a,b)=(3,2)$, where $9-8=1\ne5$.

Thus $(a,b)=(2,5)$ and $ab=\boxed{10}$.
