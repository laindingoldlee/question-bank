For one-way distance $d$, total distance is $2d$ and total time is $\dfrac{d}{30}+\dfrac{d}{7}$. Thus the average speed is $\dfrac{2}{\dfrac{1}{30}+\dfrac{1}{7}}=\boxed{\dfrac{420}{37}}$ miles per hour.
