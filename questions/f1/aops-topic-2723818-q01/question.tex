For real $n$, let
\[f(n)=\sum\dfrac{25!}{a!\,b!\,c!}\,4^c,\]
over all ordered triples $(a,b,c)$ of nonnegative integers with $a+b+c=25$ and $6a+1.5b=n$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] If $\dfrac{f(135)}{f(139.5)}=\dfrac mn$ in lowest terms, find $m(n+1)$.
\item[(b)] Interpret $f(n)$ as a count of dice outcomes.
\item[(c)] Prove that $\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{100}f(1.5j)=6^{25}$.
\end{enumerate}
