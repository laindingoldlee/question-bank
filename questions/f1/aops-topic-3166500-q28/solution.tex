Let the successive arcs $AB,BC,CP,PD,DA$ have measures $a,b,t,t,e$; the middle two are equal because $PC=PD$. The given inscribed angles give $b+2t=274^\circ$ and $e+2t=218^\circ$, so $a=2t-132^\circ$. Positivity of all arcs implies $66^\circ<t<109^\circ$. Intersecting chords give $\angle BIC=\dfrac{b+e}{2}=246^\circ-2t$, hence $\angle CID=2t-66^\circ$. Since $I,C,D$ lie on the circle centered at $P$, whose central angle $\angle CPD=180^\circ-t$, the inscribed angle $\angle CID$ is either $90^\circ-\dfrac{t}{2}$ or $90^\circ+\dfrac{t}{2}$. The first choice gives $t=\dfrac{312}{5}^\circ<66^\circ$, impossible. The second gives $t=104^\circ$. Thus $\boxed{\angle BIC=38^\circ}$.
