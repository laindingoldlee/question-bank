Bob starts at the origin and takes $10$ steps, each of length $1$, every step in a direction chosen uniformly at random from those allowed, independently of the other steps.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] On a number line, each step is to the left or to the right. What is the probability that Bob ends at the origin?
\item[(b)] In the coordinate plane, each step is up, down, left or right. What is the probability that Bob ends at the origin?
\item[(c)] In space, each step is in one of the six directions $\pm x$, $\pm y$, $\pm z$. What is the probability that Bob ends at the origin?
\end{enumerate}
