Isosceles triangle $ABD$ has base angles 80 degrees. Isosceles triangle $BCD$ has base angles 20 degrees and vertex angle 140 degrees. Since the quadrilateral is convex, its angles at $B,D$ each add the corresponding triangle angles. Hence $\boxed{\angle ABC=100^\circ,\angle BCD=140^\circ,\angle CDA=100^\circ}$.

\end{asy}
