For $n\ge0$,
\[\dfrac{n+1}{2}-\sqrt n=\dfrac{n-2\sqrt n+1}{2}=\dfrac{\left(\sqrt n-1\right)^2}{2}\ge0,\]
so $\sqrt n\le\dfrac{n+1}{2}$. Equality holds exactly when $\sqrt n=1$, i.e. $\boxed{n=1}$. (This is the AM-GM inequality for the numbers $n$ and $1$: $\sqrt{n\cdot1}\le\dfrac{n+1}{2}$.) $\blacksquare$
