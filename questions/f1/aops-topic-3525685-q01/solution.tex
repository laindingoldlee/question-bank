Choose a point $A$. If its unit disk contains at least $13$ points, we are done. Otherwise at least $13$ of the points lie outside that disk. Call this set $S$. For any distinct $P,Q\in S$, both $AP$ and $AQ$ exceed $1$, so the hypothesis on $A,P,Q$ forces $PQ<1$. Hence a unit disk centred at any point of $S$ contains every point of $S$, at least $13$. 
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
import geometry;
size(260);
pair A=(-1.4,0),P=(1.2,0),Q=(1.7,.35),R=(1.4,-.45);draw(circle(A,1));draw(circle(P,1),blue);dot(A);dot(P);dot(Q);dot(R);label("$A$",A,W);label("$P$",P,W);label("$Q$",Q,NE);label("$R$",R,S);label("$S$",(1.7,.8));draw(A--P^^A--Q,dashed);
\end{asy}
