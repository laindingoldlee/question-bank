Differentiation gives $y'=3(x+1)(x+3)$, so the stationary points are $(-3,k)$ and $(-1,k-4)$. Since $y''=6x+12$, the first is a local maximum and the second a local minimum. A cubic with positive leading coefficient crosses the axis three times exactly when the maximum is positive and the minimum is negative. Thus $k>0$ and $k-4<0$, giving $\boxed{0<k<4}$. At either endpoint a repeated root prevents three distinct roots.
