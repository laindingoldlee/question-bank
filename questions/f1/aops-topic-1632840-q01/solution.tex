Clear denominators. Multiplying the first equation by $35$:
\[
5(1-3a)=70-7(3b-1)\iff5-15a=77-21b\iff21b-15a=72\iff7b-5a=24 .
\]
Multiplying the second by $11$:
\[
11b=99-3a-b\iff3a+12b=99\iff a+4b=33 .
\]
From the second, $a=33-4b$; substituting, $7b-5(33-4b)=24$, so $27b=189$ and $b=7$, $a=33-28=5$. The solution is $\boxed{a=5,\ b=7}$. (Check: $\dfrac{1-15}{7}=-2=2-\dfrac{20}{5}$ and $9-\dfrac{15+7}{11}=7$.)
