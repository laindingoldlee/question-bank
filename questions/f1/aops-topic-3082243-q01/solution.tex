Let the convergent positive denominator be $ S=\sum_{n\ge1}\dfrac{1}{n^3}$. The even-index terms total $\sum_{n\ge1}\dfrac{1}{(2n)^3}=\dfrac{S}{8}$. Therefore the odd-index terms total $ S-\dfrac{S}{8}=\dfrac{7S}{8}$, and the ratio is $\boxed{\dfrac{7}{8}}$. Convergence follows, for example, by comparison with the convergent integral of $ x^{-3}$ on $[1,\infty)$.
