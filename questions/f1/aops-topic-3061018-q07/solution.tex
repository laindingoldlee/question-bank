Let the common perimeter be $P$. The hexagon has side $\dfrac{P}{6}$ and area $\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}\left(\dfrac{P}{6}\right)^2=\dfrac{\sqrt3P^2}{24}$. The square has area $\left(\dfrac{P}{4}\right)^2=\dfrac{P^2}{16}$. Their ratio is $\boxed{\dfrac{2\sqrt3}{3}}$.
