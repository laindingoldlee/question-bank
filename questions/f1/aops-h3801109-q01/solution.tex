Put $u=x^2-3$. The two bases are $1-x+u$ and $1+x+2u$. Since the fifth-power function is strictly increasing, for $u>0$ their fifth-power sum is greater than $(1-x)^5+(1+x)^5=2+20x^2+10x^4$, which is greater than $152$ when $x^2>3$. For $u<0$ the sum is smaller than that same expression, which is smaller than $152$ when $x^2<3$. Consequently equality requires $u=0$. At $x^2=3$, the expression is indeed $2+60+90=152$. Thus $\boxed{x=\pm\sqrt3}$.
