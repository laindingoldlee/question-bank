Let the sides be $a,b$. The first increase gives $0.10a+0.05b=0.08(a+b)$, so $2a=3b$. The second relative increase is $\dfrac{0.05a+0.10b}{a+b}=\dfrac{0.05\cdot3+0.10\cdot2}{5}=0.07$. Hence the perimeter increases by $\boxed{7\%}$.
