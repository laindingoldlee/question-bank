A line segment intersects the boundary of a convex polygon in at most two points unless it overlaps an edge. Each of the first quadrilateral's four edges therefore contributes at most two points, for an upper bound of eight. A square and another square rotated $45^\circ$ attain eight, as in the sketch. Thus the maximum is $\boxed8$.

\end{asy}
