$p=\dfrac{\binom{1232}{616}}{2^{1232}}$. For the central binomial coefficient, Wallis-type estimates give
\[
\dfrac{2^{2n}}{\dbinom{2n}{n}}=\sqrt{\pi n}\left(1+\dfrac{1}{8n}+O\!\left(\dfrac{1}{n^2}\right)\right).
\]
With $n=616$: $\pi n=616\pi\approx1935.22$, so $\sqrt{\pi n}\approx43.9911$, and the correction factor $1+\dfrac{1}{4928}\approx1.000203$ brings this to about $44.0000$. (This is why $1232$ was chosen: $616\pi\approx44^2-0.8$.) An exact computation gives $\dfrac{1}{p}\approx44.000075$, so the answer is $\boxed{44}$.
