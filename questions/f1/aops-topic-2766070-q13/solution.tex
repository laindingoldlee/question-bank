Each hexagon has area $\dfrac{3\sqrt3}2\cdot2^2=6\sqrt3$ and apothem (inradius) $\sqrt3$. Their intersection is a regular dodecagon. Its sides lie on the twelve side-lines of the two hexagons, all at distance $\sqrt3$ from the centre, so the dodecagon has inradius $\sqrt3$. A regular $12$-gon with inradius $r$ has area $12r^2\tan15^\circ=12\cdot3(2-\sqrt3)=72-36\sqrt3$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair[] P,Q;for(int k=0;k<6;++k){P.push(2*dir(60*k));Q.push(2*dir(60*k+30));}
path h1=P[0]--P[1]--P[2]--P[3]--P[4]--P[5]--cycle, h2=Q[0]--Q[1]--Q[2]--Q[3]--Q[4]--Q[5]--cycle;
fill(h1,rgb(0.85,0.9,1));fill(h2,rgb(0.85,0.9,1));
path dd;for(int k=0;k<12;++k) dd=dd--(sqrt(3)/Cos(15))*dir(15+30*k);dd=dd--cycle;fill(dd,rgb(0.6,0.72,0.95));draw(h1);draw(h2,dashed);draw(circle((0,0),sqrt(3)),dotted);
dot((0,0));draw((0,0)--(sqrt(3)*dir(0)),Arrow(3));label("$\sqrt3$",(0.85,0),S,fontsize(8pt));label("$2$",(P[0]+P[1])/2,NE,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}

The union has area
\[2\cdot6\sqrt3-(72-36\sqrt3)=\boxed{48\sqrt3-72}\approx11.14.\]
(If ``total area'' meant simply the sum of the two areas, the answer would be $12\sqrt3$.)
