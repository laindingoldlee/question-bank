The function $f(x)=2^x+5^x$ is the sum of two strictly increasing functions, so it is strictly increasing and takes the value $40$ at most once. Since
\[f(2)=4+25=29<40<133=8+125=f(3),\]
and $f$ is continuous, the equation has exactly one real solution $x_0$, with $\boxed{2<x_0<3}$.

The equation mixes two different bases, so it cannot be solved by taking logarithms of both sides; we narrow the solution down numerically:
\[f(2.21)\approx4.627+35.05=39.68,\qquad f(2.215)\approx4.643+35.33=39.98,\qquad f(2.22)\approx4.659+35.62=40.28.\]
So $2.215<x_0<2.22$, and to two decimal places $x_0\approx\boxed{2.22}$ (more precisely $x_0\approx2.2154$).
