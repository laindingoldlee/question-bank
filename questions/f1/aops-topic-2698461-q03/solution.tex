Let $w_n$ be the number of ways to climb $n$ steps; the last move covers $1$, $2$ or $3$ steps, so $w_n=w_{n-1}+w_{n-2}+w_{n-3}$, with $w_0=1$, $w_1=1$, $w_2=2$. Continuing:
\[4,7,13,24,44,81,149,274,504,927,1705,3136,5768,10609,19513,35890,66012.\]
