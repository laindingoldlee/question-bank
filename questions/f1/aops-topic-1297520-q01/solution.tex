Count zeros place by place. Among $d$-digit numbers, a non-leading place is $0$ in $\dfrac{1}{10}$ of them.
\begin{itemize}
\item $2$-digit ($90$ numbers): units $0$: $9$.
\item $3$-digit ($900$): tens and units: $2\cdot90=180$.
\item $4$-digit ($9000$): hundreds, tens, units: $3\cdot900=2700$.
\end{itemize}
Total: $9+180+2700=\boxed{2889}$.
