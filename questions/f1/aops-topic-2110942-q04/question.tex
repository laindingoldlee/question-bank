Triangle $ABC$ has vertices $A(0,5)$, $B(12,0)$ and $C(0,0)$ in the coordinate plane. The image of triangle $ABC$ under a clockwise rotation about the origin is triangle $A'B'C'$, with $A'=(3,4)$. What fraction of the area of triangle $A'B'C'$ lies below the $x$-axis? Express your answer as a common fraction.
