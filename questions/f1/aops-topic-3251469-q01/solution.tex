Continuity implies $3=\sqrt[3]{z+9}$, so $z=18$. For this value, $0\le u_n\le3$ by induction. The map $t\mapsto\sqrt[3]{18+3t}$ increases, so $u_1>u_0$ implies the sequence is increasing. Its limit $L\ge0$ exists and satisfies $L^3-3L-18=(L-3)(L^2+3L+6)=0$. The quadratic has negative discriminant, so $L=3$. Hence $\boxed{z=18}$.
