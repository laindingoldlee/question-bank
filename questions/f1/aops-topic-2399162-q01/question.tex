Find the arrangement $d_1d_2\cdots d_9$ of the digits $1,2,\dots,9$ (each used once) such that, for every $k=1,2,\dots,9$, the number formed by the first $k$ digits is divisible by $k$. Show that there is only one such arrangement. (For example, $123456789$ fails: $12$ is divisible by $2$ and $123$ by $3$, but $1234$ is not divisible by $4$.)
