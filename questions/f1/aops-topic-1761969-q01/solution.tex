In order the data are $1,5,5,5,8,10$. The mode is $5$ (it occurs three times). With six values the median is the mean of the third and fourth, $\dfrac{5+5}{2}=5$. The mean is $\dfrac{1+5+5+5+8+10}{6}=\dfrac{34}{6}=\dfrac{17}{3}$. So the mode is $\boxed{5}$, the median is $\boxed{5}$ and the mean is $\boxed{\dfrac{17}{3}}$.
