A student claims that for positive integers $n$, $k$ with $k<n$ and every positive integer $P$,
\[
\dfrac{(n+k)^{P-1}-\displaystyle\sum_{a=0}^{P-1}\left(\dbinom{P-1}{a}-1\right)n^{P-(a+1)}k^{a}}{n^{P}}=1-\left(\dfrac{k}{n}\right)^{P}.
\]
(For a fair die, $n=6$ and $k=5$, the right side is the probability of at least one six in $P$ rolls.) Simplify the left side, and determine exactly for which $n$ and $k$ the claim is true for every $P$.
