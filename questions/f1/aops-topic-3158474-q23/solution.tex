The value $c=a+b-2016$ is forced. Its positivity requires $b\ge2017-a$; the condition $c\le b$ follows from $a\le2016$. Thus $1009\le a\le2016$, and for each such $a$ there are $a-(2017-a)+1=2a-2016$ choices of $b$. Summing $2+4+\cdots+2016$ gives $1008\cdot1009=\boxed{1017072}$.
