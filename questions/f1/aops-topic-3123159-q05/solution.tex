The equation is $(10-n)a=(10n-1)b$. For fixed $n$, put $g=\gcd(10-n,10n-1)$. Then $a$ is a positive multiple of $\dfrac{10n-1}{g}$. For $n=2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9$, these least possible values of $a$ are respectively $19,29,13,49,59,23,79,89$, all greater than $9$. Thus no digit $a$ is possible.
