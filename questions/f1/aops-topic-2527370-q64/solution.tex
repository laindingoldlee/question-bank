By AM-GM applied to $2x^2$ and $3y^2$,
\[36=2x^2+3y^2\ge2\sqrt{6x^2y^2}=2\sqrt6\,|xy|,\]
so $xy\le\dfrac{36}{2\sqrt6}=3\sqrt6$. Equality needs $2x^2=3y^2=18$, i.e. $x=3$, $y=\sqrt6$ (with $xy>0$), which is on the curve. The maximum is $\boxed{3\sqrt6}$.

In the sketch, the hyperbola $xy=3\sqrt6$ touches the ellipse at $(3,\sqrt6)$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
draw((-5,0)--(6.3,0),Arrow(TeXHead));draw((0,-4)--(0,4.8),Arrow(TeXHead));
draw(scale(sqrt(18),sqrt(12))*unitcircle,linewidth(0.8));
real h(real x){return 3*sqrt(6)/x;}
draw(graph(h,1.65,6),red+dashed);
pair Q=(3,sqrt(6));dot(Q);
label("$(3,\sqrt6)$",Q,NE,fontsize(9pt));
label("$xy=3\sqrt6$",(4.5,h(4.5)),NE,red+fontsize(8pt));
label("$2x^2+3y^2=36$",(-3.2,-3.0),SW,fontsize(8pt));
label("$x$",(6.3,0),E);label("$y$",(0,4.8),N);
\end{asy}
