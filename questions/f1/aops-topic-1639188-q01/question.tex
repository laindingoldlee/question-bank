Quadrilateral $ABCD$ is inscribed in a circle, with its vertices in the order $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ around the circle. Two consecutive angles are $\angle ABC=70^\circ$ and $\angle BCD=80^\circ$, and the arc $DA$ cut off by side $DA$ (the arc not containing $B$ and $C$) measures $88^\circ$. Find the measure of the arc $BC$ cut off by the opposite side $BC$.
