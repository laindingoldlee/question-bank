An inscribed angle is half of the arc it intercepts. Write $\overset{\frown}{AB}$, $\overset{\frown}{BC}$, $\overset{\frown}{CD}$, $\overset{\frown}{DA}$ for the four arcs cut off by the sides; they add up to $360^\circ$.

$\angle ABC$ intercepts the arc $ADC$, so $\overset{\frown}{CD}+\overset{\frown}{DA}=2\cdot70^\circ=140^\circ$ and $\overset{\frown}{CD}=140^\circ-88^\circ=52^\circ$.

$\angle BCD$ intercepts the arc $BAD$, so $\overset{\frown}{AB}+\overset{\frown}{DA}=2\cdot80^\circ=160^\circ$ and $\overset{\frown}{AB}=160^\circ-88^\circ=72^\circ$.

Therefore
\[
\overset{\frown}{BC}=360^\circ-88^\circ-52^\circ-72^\circ=\boxed{148^\circ}.
\]
(Check: $\angle CDA=\dfrac{72^\circ+148^\circ}{2}=110^\circ=180^\circ-70^\circ$ and $\angle DAB=\dfrac{148^\circ+52^\circ}{2}=100^\circ=180^\circ-80^\circ$, as they must be in a cyclic quadrilateral.)

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0);real r=2.2;
pair A=r*dir(110),B=r*dir(182),C=r*dir(330),D=r*dir(22);
draw(circle(O,r),gray);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(arc(O,r,22,110),red+linewidth(1.6));
real a1=degrees(C-B),d1=degrees(A-B)-a1;d1=d1-360*floor((d1+180)/360);
real a2=degrees(D-C),d2=degrees(B-C)-a2;d2=d2-360*floor((d2+180)/360);
draw(arc(B,0.45,a1,a1+d1),blue);draw(arc(C,0.45,a2,a2+d2),blue);
label("$70^\circ$",B+0.85*dir(a1+d1/2),blue);
label("$80^\circ$",C+0.85*dir(a2+d2/2),blue);
label("$A$",A,dir(110));label("$B$",B,dir(182));label("$C$",C,dir(330));label("$D$",D,dir(22));
label("$88^\circ$",(r+0.4)*dir(66),red);label("$72^\circ$",(r+0.4)*dir(146));label("$148^\circ$",(r+0.45)*dir(256));label("$52^\circ$",(r+0.4)*dir(356));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

(The figure is drawn to scale.)
