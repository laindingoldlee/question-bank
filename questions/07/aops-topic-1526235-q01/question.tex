In triangle $ABC$, $\angle C=90^\circ$, $\angle A=30^\circ$ and $\angle B=60^\circ$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of the hypotenuse $AB$. Using only the segment $CM$ (no other auxiliary lines), prove that $BC=\dfrac{1}{2}AB$, and deduce that $AC=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}AB$.
