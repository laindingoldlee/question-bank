Eddie is at $(0,0)$ on the coordinate plane and wants to get to $(2,10)$. If he is at $(x,y)$, he can move to one of $(x+2,y)$, $(x-2,y)$, $(x+1,y+1)$, $(x-1,y+1)$. He does not revisit any points along his path and his $x$ and $y$ coordinates are always greater than or equal to $0$ and less than or equal to $2$ and $10$ respectively. Find the number of ways Eddie can get to $(2,10)$.
