Every reached lattice point has even $x+y$. Thus at even heights the possible positions have $x=0,2$, and at odd heights the only position has $x=1$. At an even height one may either cross horizontally once or not cross; crossing twice would revisit a point. At each odd height except the final transition, the next even endpoint can be chosen in two ways. Starting at height zero gives two horizontal choices. At heights $2,4,6,8$, the incoming endpoint and horizontal choice give four possibilities each. From height $9$, one may go directly to $(2,10)$ or reach $(0,10)$ and cross, giving two final choices. Hence $2\cdot4^4\cdot2=\boxed{1024}$.
