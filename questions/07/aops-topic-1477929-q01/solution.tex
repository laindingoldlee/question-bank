The primes less than $10$ are $2,3,5,7$, and $x\ne y$. We need $10x+y$ prime as well; $y=2$ or $y=5$ makes $10x+y$ divisible by $2$ or $5$, so $y\in\{3,7\}$. The possibilities are:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
x & y & 10x+y & m=xy(10x+y)\\\hline
2 & 3 & 23 & 138\\
2 & 7 & 27\ (\text{not prime}) & -\\
3 & 7 & 37 & 777\\
5 & 3 & 53 & 795\\
5 & 7 & 57=3\cdot19 & -\\
7 & 3 & 73 & 1533\ (\text{four digits})
\end{array}
\]
The largest three-digit value is $\boxed{795}=5\cdot3\cdot53$.
