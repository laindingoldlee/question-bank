Let there be $r$ red marbles and $t$ marbles in total. Removing one red marble leaves $r-1$ red among $t-1$ marbles, so $7(r-1)=t-1$, that is $t=7r-6$. Removing two blue marbles leaves $r$ red among $t-2$, so $5r=t-2$.

Substituting, $5r=7r-8$, so $r=4$ and $t=7\cdot4-6=22$. Check: removing a red leaves $3$ red of $21$, which is $\dfrac{1}{7}$; removing two blue leaves $4$ red of $20$, which is $\dfrac{1}{5}$.

There were $\boxed{22}$ marbles originally.
