Two balls move back and forth between the ends of a unit segment, reflecting instantly at the ends and maintaining constant speeds. They start at opposite ends and take $6$ and $9$ seconds respectively to cross the segment. How many times do they pass one another strictly inside the segment during the first $12$ minutes $19$ seconds? Do not count simultaneous contacts at an endpoint.
