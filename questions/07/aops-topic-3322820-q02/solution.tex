Unfold reflection by using the triangular wave $T(u)$ of period $2$, equal to $u$ on $[0,1]$ and $2-u$ on $[1,2]$. The positions are $T(\dfrac{t}{6})$ and $T(1+\dfrac{t}{9})$. Equality means the two arguments are congruent or opposite modulo $2$. These give $t=18(2j+1)$ or $t=\dfrac{18}{5}(2j+1)$; the first list is contained in the second. Up to $739$ seconds, the latter list has $j=0,1,\ldots,102$, or $103$ meetings. Endpoint meetings occur when $j\equiv2\pmod5$, giving $21$ such meetings. Removing them leaves $\boxed{82}$ interior crossings. If endpoint meetings were counted, the answer would instead be $103$.
