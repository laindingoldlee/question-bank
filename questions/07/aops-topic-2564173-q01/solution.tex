\textbf{(b)} A monic quadratic with roots $a$ and $b$ is
\[(x-a)(x-b)=\boxed{x^2-(a+b)x+ab}.\]
\textbf{(a)} Here $a+b=6$ and $ab=(3+i)(3-i)=9-i^2=10$, so the polynomial is $\boxed{x^2-6x+10}$.
