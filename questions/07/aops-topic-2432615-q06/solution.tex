Place the $12$ uncoloured $0$'s in a row (one way, as they are identical). They create $13$ gaps (before, between and after them). No two coloured $0$'s are adjacent exactly when they go into $5$ different gaps: choose the gaps in $\dbinom{13}{5}=1287$ ways and put the five distinct colours into them in $5!=120$ orders. The number of arrangements is
\[1287\cdot120=\boxed{154440}.\]
