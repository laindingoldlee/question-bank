The triangle area is $25\sqrt3$. Each circle removes a $60^\circ$ sector of area $\dfrac{\pi}{6}$, and these sectors do not overlap. Thus the area is $\boxed{25\sqrt3-\dfrac{\pi}{2}}$. The original coefficient sum is $25+3+2=30$.

usepackage("amsmath");

pair A=(0,0),B=(10,0),C=(5,5*sqrt(3));draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(circle(A,1));draw(circle(B,1));draw(circle(C,1));label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,N);
\end{asy}
