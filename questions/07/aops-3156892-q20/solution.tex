The first six selected cells are a uniformly random six-element subset. A six-element set is line-free exactly when its three missing cells form one of the two full diagonals: this follows by requiring a missing cell in every row and column, then checking the six permutation patterns against both diagonals. Two patterns work. No seven-element set is line-free, since two missing cells cannot cover all three rows. A line-free six-set automatically has no earlier completed line. Hence the required probability is $\dfrac{2}{\binom96}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{42}}$.
