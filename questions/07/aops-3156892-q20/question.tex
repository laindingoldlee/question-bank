In blindfolded solitary tic-tac-toe, a player starts with a blank $3$-by-$3$ tic-tac-toe board. On each turn, he randomly places an "$X$" in one of the open spaces on the board. The game ends when the player gets $3$ $X$s in a row, in a column, or in a diagonal as per normal tic-tac-toe rules. (Note that only $X$s are used, not $O$s). What fraction of games will run the maximum $7$ amount of moves?
