A sum of three distinct powers of two has exactly three $1$ digits in its binary representation. The positive squares up to $100$ have binary representations\[1,\ 100,\ 1001,\ 10000,\ 11001,\ 100100,\ 110001,\ 1000000,\ 1010001,\ 1100100.\] Exactly four have three $1$ digits:\[25=16+8+1,\quad49=32+16+1,\quad81=64+16+1,\quad100=64+32+4.\] Zero cannot be a sum of three positive powers. The answer is $\boxed4$.
