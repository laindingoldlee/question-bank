At $60$ miles per hour the train covers $1$ mile per minute, so in $3$ minutes it moves $3$ miles. Between the moment the front enters and the moment the tail leaves, the front of the train travels the length of the tunnel plus the length of the train. So
\[
L+1=3\quad\Longrightarrow\quad L=\boxed{2\text{ miles}}.
\]
