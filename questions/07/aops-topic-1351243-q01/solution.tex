Look for two numbers with product $10\cdot(-6)=-60$ and sum $-7$: they are $-12$ and $5$. So
\[
10x^2-7x-6=10x^2-12x+5x-6=2x(5x-6)+(5x-6)=(5x-6)(2x+1).
\]
The solutions are $x=\boxed{\dfrac{6}{5}}$ and $x=\boxed{-\dfrac{1}{2}}$. (The quadratic formula agrees: $x=\dfrac{7\pm\sqrt{49+240}}{20}=\dfrac{7\pm17}{20}$.)
