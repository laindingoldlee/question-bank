The boundary consists of the circle arc from $(-6\sqrt3,6)$ through the lower-left side to $(6,-6\sqrt3)$, plus segments through $(0,6),(6,0)$. Decompose at the origin. The sector angle is $150^\circ$, giving area $60\pi$; the three remaining triangles have areas $18\sqrt3,18,18\sqrt3$. Therefore the region has area $\boxed{60\pi+36\sqrt3+18}$.
