Try small integer roots: $x=-1$ gives $-1-1+2=0$, so by the factor theorem $x+1$ is a factor. Dividing,
\[x^3+x+2=(x+1)\left(x^2-x+2\right).\]
The quadratic $x^2-x+2$ has discriminant $1-8=-7<0$, so it has no real roots and cannot be factored further over the reals. The answer is $\boxed{(x+1)(x^2-x+2)}$.
