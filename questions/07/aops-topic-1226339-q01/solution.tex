Colourings without red use colours from the other $4$: $4$ single colours and $\dbinom42=6$ pairs, $10$ in all. Those including green: green alone, or green with one of the $3$ others: $4$. The probability is $\dfrac{4}{10}=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{5}}$.
