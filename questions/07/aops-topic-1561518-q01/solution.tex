Draw $\theta$ in a right triangle with hypotenuse $1$, so the legs are $\cos\theta$ and $\sin\theta$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=(sqrt(5)-1)/2, c=sqrt(s);
pair O=(0,0),P=(c,0),Q=(c,s);
draw(O--P--Q--cycle,linewidth(1));
real m=0.06; draw(P+(-m,0)--P+(-m,m)--P+(0,m));
draw(arc(O,0.2,0,degrees(atan2(s,c))),red);
label("$\theta$",0.28*dir(degrees(atan2(s,c))/2),red);
label("$1$",(O+Q)/2,NW);label("$\cos\theta$",(O+P)/2,S);label("$\sin\theta$",(P+Q)/2,E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Then $\tan\theta=\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$, and the condition $\cos\theta=\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$ becomes $\cos^2\theta=\sin\theta$. With $s=\sin\theta$ and $\cos^2\theta=1-s^2$:
\[
1-s^2=s\iff s^2+s-1=0\iff s=\dfrac{-1\pm\sqrt5}{2}.
\]
Since $0<s<1$, we get
\[
\sin\theta=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt5-1}{2}}\approx0.618 .
\]
(The figure is drawn to scale for this angle, $\theta\approx38.2^\circ$.)
