Samantha does $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of the program per hour and Sarah $\dfrac{1}{5}$, so together they do $\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{5}=\dfrac{8}{15}$ per hour. The time is $\dfrac{15}{8}=1.875$ hours (1 hour 52.5 minutes), which rounds to $\boxed{2}$ hours.
