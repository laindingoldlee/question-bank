The ``grandma'' of the problem is the mother: she is the grandmother of the family in the photo.

Treat each couple as a block. Then $4$ units are arranged in a row: the mother and three couple blocks. The mother must not be at an end, so she is in position $2$ or $3$ of the four units: $2$ ways. The three couple blocks fill the other positions in $3!=6$ ways. Each couple can sit in $2$ orders, giving $2^3=8$ ways. In total
\[
2\cdot6\cdot8=\boxed{96}
\]
seating arrangements. (Checked by listing all $7!$ orders.)
