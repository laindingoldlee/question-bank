Count ordered triples up to the six permutations of the positions. The identity fixes $\binom{419}{2}=87571 $ triples. Each transposition fixes triples with two specified coordinates equal: $ 2x+z=420 $ gives $ 209 $ choices. Each of the two 3-cycles fixes only $(140,140,140)$. Burnside's lemma gives $\dfrac{87571+3(209)+2}{6}=\boxed{14700}$.
