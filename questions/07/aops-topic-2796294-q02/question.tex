Container $A$ is a right rectangular prism with a $4$ in by $6$ in base and height $6$ in; container $B$ has a $3$ in by $3$ in square base and height $6$ in. Container $A$ holds water to a depth of $5$ in and container $B$ to a depth of $2$ in. Water is pumped from $A$ into $B$ until the depths are equal. What is the common depth? Give a mixed number.
