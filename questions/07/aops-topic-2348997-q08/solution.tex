$\sqrt[3]{y^{18}}=y^6=289$, so $|y|^3=\sqrt{289}=17$. Then $\sigma(2)=\left(y^2\right)^{\dfrac32}=\left(\sqrt{y^2}\right)^3=|y|^3=\boxed{17}$ (whatever the sign of $y$).
