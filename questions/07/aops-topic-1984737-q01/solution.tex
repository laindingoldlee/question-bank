\textbf{(a)} A listed number $\overline{201d}$ has digits $2,0$ (even) and $1$ (odd), so $d$ must be odd with $3+d=12$: $d=9$. So the only listed number from $2010$ to $2019$ is $2019$. Numbers from $2000$ to $2009$ repeat the digit $0$. Hence no listed number lies in $[2013,2018]$, while $2019$ is on the list (digits distinct, sum $12$, even $2,0$ and odd $1,9$). The closest number is $\boxed{2019}$, at distance $3$.

\textbf{(b)} By (a), the listed numbers below $2016$ are exactly those starting with $1$. Then the other three digits are distinct, differ from $1$, sum to $11$, and are two even digits and one odd digit $o\in\{3,5,7,9\}$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $o=3$: even pair sum $8$: $\{0,8\},\{2,6\}$;
\item $o=5$: even pair sum $6$: $\{0,6\},\{2,4\}$;
\item $o=7$: even pair sum $4$: $\{0,4\}$;
\item $o=9$: even pair sum $2$: $\{0,2\}$.
\end{itemize}
That is $6$ digit sets, each arranged in $3!=6$ ways after the leading $1$, so $36$ numbers. For one set the six arrangements use each digit twice in each place, so they sum to $2\cdot11\cdot111=2442$. The total is
\[
36\cdot1000+6\cdot2442=36000+14652=\boxed{50652}.
\]
