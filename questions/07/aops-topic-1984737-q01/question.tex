Carl made a list of all four-digit numbers with four distinct digits such that the sum of the digits is $12$ and exactly two of the digits are even and two are odd (zero is even, and no number starts with $0$). For example, $2703$ is on the list.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Which number on Carl's list is closest to $2016$?
\item[(b)] Find the sum of all numbers on Carl's list that are less than $2016$.
\end{enumerate}
