Taking $m=0$, $n=1$: $x^0\cdot x=x^{1}=x$. Dividing by $x\ne0$ gives $x^0=1$.

Taking $m=-1$, $n=1$: $x^{-1}\cdot x=x^0=1$. Dividing by $x$ gives $x^{-1}=\dfrac1x$. $\blacksquare$

(So the definition $x^{-1}=\dfrac1x$ is the only one consistent with the exponent laws.)
