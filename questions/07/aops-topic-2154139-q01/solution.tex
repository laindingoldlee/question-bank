Both lines pass through the origin. The line $y=\dfrac{4}{3}x$ has direction $(3,4)$, of length $5$, and $y=\dfrac{12}{5}x$ has direction $(5,12)$, of length $13$. The unit vectors
\[\mathbf u=\left(\dfrac{3}{5},\dfrac{4}{5}\right),\qquad \mathbf v=\left(\dfrac{5}{13},\dfrac{12}{13}\right)\]
make an acute angle (their dot product $\dfrac{15+48}{65}$ is positive), and since they have equal lengths, $\mathbf u+\mathbf v$ is the diagonal of a rhombus and bisects the angle between them:
\[\mathbf u+\mathbf v=\left(\dfrac{39+25}{65},\dfrac{52+60}{65}\right)=\left(\dfrac{64}{65},\dfrac{112}{65}\right).\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
pair O=(0,0);
draw((-0.3,0)--(5,0),Arrow(6)); draw((0,-0.3)--(0,5.6),Arrow(6));
label("$x$",(5,0),E); label("$y$",(0,5.6),N);
pair U=(3,4)/5, V=(5,12)/13;
pair W=unit(U+V);
draw(O--4.2*U,blue+linewidth(0.9)); draw(O--5.5*V,red+linewidth(0.9));
draw(O--4.9*W,deepgreen+dashed+linewidth(0.9));
draw(2*U--2*(U+V)--2*V,gray+dotted);
draw(O--2*U,blue+linewidth(1.5),Arrow(6)); draw(O--2*V,red+linewidth(1.5),Arrow(6));
draw(O--2*(U+V),deepgreen+linewidth(1.2),Arrow(6));
label("$y=\dfrac{4}{3}x$",4.2*U,E,blue);
label("$y=\dfrac{12}{5}x$",5.5*V,W,red);
label("$y=\dfrac{7}{4}x$",4.9*W,E,deepgreen);
dot(O); label("$O$",O,SW);
label("$\mathbf{u}$",1.1*U,SE,blue); label("$\mathbf{v}$",1.1*V,NW,red);
label("$\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}$",2*(U+V),NW,deepgreen);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline

So the bisector of the acute angle has slope $\dfrac{112}{64}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{4}}$. (The other bisector, of the obtuse angle, is perpendicular to it and has slope $-\dfrac{4}{7}$.)

\emph{Check.} If $\tan\alpha=\dfrac{4}{3}$, $\tan\beta=\dfrac{12}{5}$ and $\tan\theta=\dfrac{7}{4}$, then
\[\tan(\theta-\alpha)=\dfrac{\dfrac{7}{4}-\dfrac{4}{3}}{1+\dfrac{7}{4}\cdot\dfrac{4}{3}}=\dfrac{\dfrac{5}{12}}{\dfrac{10}{3}}=\dfrac{1}{8},\qquad \tan(\beta-\theta)=\dfrac{\dfrac{12}{5}-\dfrac{7}{4}}{1+\dfrac{12}{5}\cdot\dfrac{7}{4}}=\dfrac{\dfrac{13}{20}}{\dfrac{26}{5}}=\dfrac{1}{8},\]
so the line $y=\dfrac{7}{4}x$ makes equal angles with the two given lines.
