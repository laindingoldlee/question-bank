Three people pay separately for a meal. A full bottle of wine costs $\$24$. A buys three-quarters of a bottle. B's orange juice costs one-sixth as much as A's wine; C's water is free. A's main dish costs three times the combined cost of A's and B's drinks. B's main dish costs $\$34$, and B's side costs one-third of a full wine bottle. A's bread costs half the combined cost of B's drink and side. C has $\$14$ beef, four mozzarella sticks at $\$1.20$ each, and a side costing half as much as the beef. A's dessert costs $\$6$. B pays one-quarter of a $\$40$ shared dessert. C's dessert costs $\$4$. Find the amounts paid by A, B, and C.
