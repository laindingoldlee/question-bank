The equation factors as $(x+3)(x+7)=0$, so $x=-3$ or $x=-7$, and the average is $\dfrac{-3+(-7)}{2}=\boxed{-5}$. (By Vieta's formulas the sum of the roots is $-10$, so the average is $-5$ without solving.)
