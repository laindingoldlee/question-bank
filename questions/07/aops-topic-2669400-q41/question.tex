For a positive integer $n$, let $p_n$ be the least prime that does not divide $n$. Prove that $\dfrac{p_n}{n}\to0$ as $n\to\infty$. (You may use Bertrand's postulate: for every real $x\ge1$ there is a prime in $(x,2x]$.)
