Since $\dfrac{1}{n}$ must be positive, $n$ is a positive integer, and then taking reciprocals reverses the inequalities:
\[
\dfrac{1998}{98}<n<\dfrac{1998}{19}.
\]
Now $\dfrac{1998}{98}=20.38\ldots$ and $\dfrac{1998}{19}=105.15\ldots$, so $n=21,22,\dots,105$. That is $105-21+1=\boxed{85}$ integers.
