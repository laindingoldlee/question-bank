The digit count is $1+\lfloor2021\log_{10}(2021!)\rfloor$. For $n=2021$, let $L=(n+\dfrac{1}{2})\ln n-n+\dfrac{1}{2}\ln(2\pi)$. Stirling's explicit bounds $L+\dfrac{1}{12n+1}<\ln(n!)<L+\dfrac{1}{12n}$ place $2021\log_{10}(2021!)$ strictly between $11731687.26288$ and $11731687.26290$. Hence the digit count is $\boxed{11731688}$. The error bounds are much smaller than the distance to an integer, certifying the floor.
