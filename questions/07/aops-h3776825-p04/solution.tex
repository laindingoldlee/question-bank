Every pair of tetrahedron vertices is joined by an edge of length $1$. Any path between two points has length at least their straight-line distance, which here is $1$. Walking along the edge attains the bound, so the answer is $\boxed1$.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

pair A=(0,0),B=(3,0),C=(1,1),D=(1.5,3);draw(A--B--D--A);draw(A--C--B,dashed);draw(C--D);draw(A--B,blue+1.5);label("A",A,SW);label("B",B,SE);label("C",C,W);label("D",D,N);label("1",(A+B)/2,S);
\end{asy}
