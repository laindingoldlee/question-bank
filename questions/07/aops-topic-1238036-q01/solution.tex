For each term,
\[\dfrac{1}{(k-2)k(k+2)}=\dfrac{1}{4}\left(\dfrac{1}{(k-2)k}-\dfrac{1}{k(k+2)}\right),\]
since the bracket equals $\dfrac{(k+2)-(k-2)}{(k-2)k(k+2)}$. The sum telescopes over $k=3,5,\dots,13$:
\[\dfrac{1}{4}\left(\dfrac{1}{1\cdot3}-\dfrac{1}{13\cdot15}\right)=\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot\dfrac{65-1}{195}=\boxed{\dfrac{16}{195}}.\]
