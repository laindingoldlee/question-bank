The final chocolate consists of $81$ unit squares. Each cut increases the number of pieces by one, so there are $80$ cuts. Across all cuts, every internal unit edge of the final grid is cut exactly once. Their total length is $8\cdot9+8\cdot9=144$. Thus the average cut length is $\dfrac{144}{80}=\boxed{\dfrac{9}{5}}$.
