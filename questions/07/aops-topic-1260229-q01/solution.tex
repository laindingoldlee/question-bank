Use the common denominator $2x(x+2)$ in both the numerator and the denominator:
\[\dfrac{x-2}{2x}+\dfrac{1}{x+2}=\dfrac{(x-2)(x+2)+2x}{2x(x+2)}=\dfrac{x^2+2x-4}{2x(x+2)},\]
\[\dfrac{3}{2}-\dfrac{6}{x(x+2)}=\dfrac{3x(x+2)-12}{2x(x+2)}=\dfrac{3\left(x^2+2x-4\right)}{2x(x+2)}.\]
The quotient is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{3}}$ (for all $x$ where the expression is defined: $x\ne0,-2$ and $x^2+2x\ne4$).
