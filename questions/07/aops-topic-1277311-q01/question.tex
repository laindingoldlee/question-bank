You stand on the first rung of a ladder with $1000$ rungs. Each turn a fair die is rolled: on $1$ or $2$ you move down one rung, and on $3$ to $6$ you move up one rung. Moving down from the first rung means you fall and lose, and moving up from the $1000$th rung means you escape. What is the probability that you escape?
