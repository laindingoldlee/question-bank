Count the beginning of a run of at least 21 heads. It starts at position 1 with probability $2^{-21}$, or at one of positions 2 through 20 with a preceding tail and 21 heads, each probability $2^{-22}$. Two such distinct runs cannot fit in 40 tosses, so these events are disjoint. Thus Sam's chance is $2^{-21}+19\cdot2^{-22}=\boxed{\dfrac{21}{4194304}}$.
