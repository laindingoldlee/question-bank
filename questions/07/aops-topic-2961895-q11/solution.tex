Let $g_0,\dots,g_6$ be the numbers of adults before the first child, between consecutive children, and after the last. Then $g_0+\cdots+g_6=18$ with $g_1,\dots,g_5\ge3$. Setting $g_i'=g_i-3$ for $1\le i\le5$ leaves $3$ adults distributed freely into $7$ gaps: $\binom{3+6}6=84$. \textbf{(C)}
