For numbers $x$ and $y$ define
\[x*y=\dfrac{3x^3y+3x^2y^2+xy^3+45}{(x+1)^3+(y+1)^3-60}.\]
The expression $2021*2020*2019*\cdots*3*2$ is evaluated from left to right, that is, as $\left(\cdots\left((2021*2020)*2019\right)*\cdots*3\right)*2$. Its value is $\dfrac pq$ with $p$ and $q$ relatively prime positive integers. Find $q$.
