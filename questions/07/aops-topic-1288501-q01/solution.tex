Let $t=\dfrac {a}{b}$; then $\dfrac{(a+b)^2}{ab}=\dfrac {a}{b}+2+\dfrac {b}{a}=t+\dfrac{1}{t}+2$. The ratio $t$ lies in $\left[\dfrac{1}{2},2\right]$. The function $t+\dfrac{1}{t}$ decreases on $\left[\dfrac{1}{2},1\right]$ and increases on $[1,2]$, so it is largest at the endpoints, where it equals $\dfrac{5}{2}$. Hence
\[
k=\dfrac{5}{2}+2=\boxed{\dfrac{9}{2}},
\]
attained at $(a,b)=(2,1)$ or $(1,2)$.
