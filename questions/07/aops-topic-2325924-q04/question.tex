Two brothers secretly split $20$ identical pieces of candy between them in the dark, and all $21$ possible splits ($0$ and $20$, $1$ and $19$, \dots, $20$ and $0$) are equally likely. If one brother gets $2$ or more pieces more than the other, they fight and get caught. What is the probability that they get caught?

$\textbf{(A) }\dfrac{20}{21}\qquad\textbf{(B) }\dfrac67\qquad\textbf{(C) }\dfrac{73}{100}\qquad\textbf{(D) }\dfrac{15}{16}\qquad\textbf{(E) }\dfrac17$
