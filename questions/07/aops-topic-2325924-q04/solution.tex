If one brother gets $k$ pieces, the difference is $|20-2k|$, which is even. So the difference is less than $2$ only when it is $0$, i.e. for the split $10$ and $10$. They get caught in the other $20$ of the $21$ equally likely splits: the probability is $\boxed{\textbf{(A) }\dfrac{20}{21}}$.
