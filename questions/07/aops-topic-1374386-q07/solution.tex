Let there be $b$ blue marbles originally. After adding $5$ blue marbles there are $b+5$ blue among $b+16$ marbles, and we need
\[
\dfrac{b+5}{b+16}>\dfrac{7}{10}\iff10b+50>7b+112\iff3b>62\iff b>\dfrac{62}{3}.
\]
So $b\ge21$, and the bag originally held at least $21+11=\boxed{32}$ marbles. (Check: $\dfrac{26}{37}\approx70.3\%$, while $b=20$ gives $\dfrac{25}{36}\approx69.4\%$.)
