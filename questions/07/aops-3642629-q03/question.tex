A piece of graph paper extends forever in all directions. Each square contains  one of the four digits $1$, $2$, $3$, or $4$. Each digit is used at least once. Two squares are called neighbors if they share a side. A square is called balanced if its digit is equal to the number of different digits in its four neighbors. (In the example shown, the blue square is balanced but the orange one is not. Both have three different digits as neighbors.) Is it possible that all squares can be balanced?
