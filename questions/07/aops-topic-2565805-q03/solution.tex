Since $729=3^6$,
\[\left(729^{\dfrac15}\right)^{\dfrac12}=729^{\dfrac1{10}}=3^{\dfrac6{10}}=3^{\dfrac35}=\sqrt[5]{27}.\]
If $\sqrt[a]{b}=3^{\dfrac35}$ with positive integers $a,b$, then $b^5=3^{3a}$, so $b$ is a power of $3$, say $b=3^e$, and $5e=3a$. Hence $5\mid a$, so $a\ge5$, and $a=5$ works with $b=27$. Therefore $a+b=\boxed{32}$.
