Consecutive triples starting at $2,3,4,5$ have products $24,60,120,210$. A number $N$ is a product of consecutive integers exactly when $4N+1$ is an odd square. The first three give $97,241,481$, not squares; the fourth gives $841=29^2$, so $210=14\cdot15$. Triple products increase with their first term, proving the least is $\boxed{210}$.
