Conditional on $N=n$, the sum has expectation $n+\dfrac{7}{2}n=\dfrac{9}{2}n$. Since $\mathbb E[N]=\dfrac{7}{2}$, the unconditional expectation is $\dfrac{9}{2}\cdot\dfrac{7}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{63}{4}}$.
