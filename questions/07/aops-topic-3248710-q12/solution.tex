If $n>1$, its divisor sum is at least $n+1$, so $n\le35$. Also $n=1$ has sum one. Exact checking of $1$ through $35$ gives only $22$, whose divisors are $1,2,11,22$ and sum $36$.
\begin{verbatim}
print([n for n in range(1,36)
       if sum(d for d in range(1,n+1) if n%d==0)==36])
# [22]
\end{verbatim}
Thus $\boxed{n=22}$.
