By the formula for binomial coefficients,
\[
\dbinom{n}{n-1}=\dfrac{n!}{(n-1)!\,1!}=n .
\]
Combinatorially: choosing $n-1$ of $n$ objects is the same as choosing the single object to leave out, which can be done in $n$ ways. So the identity always holds, not just ``most of the time''.
