Each year moves the weekday of 25 December forward by $1$ day, or by $2$ if the year that has just started is a leap year (its 29 February comes before Christmas). Going back from 2021 (Saturday): 2020 Friday (2021 is not a leap year), 2019 Wednesday (2020 is a leap year), 2018 Tuesday, 2017 Monday, 2016 Sunday, 2015 Friday (2016 is a leap year), 2014 Thursday, 2013 Wednesday, 2012 Tuesday, 2011 Sunday (2012 is a leap year), 2010 Saturday. The year is 2010, with digit sum $\boxed{3}$.
