Let there be $c$ cats and $p$ pigeons. Then $c+p=7$ and $4c+2p=22$. Subtracting twice the first equation from the second gives $2c=8$, so $c=4$ and $p=3$: $\boxed{4\text{ cats and }3\text{ pigeons}}$. (Check: $16+6=22$ feet.)
