Kenny, Abby and Ned play pickleball for an afternoon. Each game is between two of them, and the winner of each game stays on to play the person who sat out that game. At the end, Abby has played the last seven games in a row. Kenny tallies the games played: Kenny played $8$ games, Abby played $12$ games and Ned played $14$ games. Who won the fourth game, and against whom?
