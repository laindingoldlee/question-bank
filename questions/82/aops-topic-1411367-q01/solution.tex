From $2a+2b=10$ we get $a+b=5$, with $a,b>0$. By the AM-GM inequality,
\[
\sqrt{ab}\le\dfrac{a+b}{2}=\dfrac{5}{2},\qquad\text{so}\qquad ab\le\dfrac{25}{4}=6.25 .
\]
Equality in AM-GM holds exactly when $a=b$, i.e.\ for the $2.5$ cm by $2.5$ cm square (dashed).

Without AM-GM: $ab=\left(\dfrac{a+b}{2}\right)^2-\left(\dfrac{a-b}{2}\right)^2=6.25-\left(\dfrac{a-b}{2}\right)^2\le6.25$. $\blacksquare$
