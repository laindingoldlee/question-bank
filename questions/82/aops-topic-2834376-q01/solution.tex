The ratio is $35:21:15$, so the areas are $210$, $126$ and $90$. An equilateral triangle of side $s$ has area $\dfrac{\sqrt3}4s^2$, so $s^2=\dfrac{4A}{\sqrt3}$: the largest has $s^2=280\sqrt3$ and the smallest $s^2=120\sqrt3$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s1=sqrt(280*sqrt(3)),s2=sqrt(120*sqrt(3));
draw((0,0)--(s1,0)--s1*dir(60)--cycle);draw((s1+3,0)--(s1+3+s2,0)--(s1+3,0)+s2*dir(60)--cycle);
label("area $210$",(s1/2,s1*0.25));label("area $90$",(s1+3+s2/2,s2*0.25));
\end{asy}

The perimeters sum to $3\left(\sqrt{280\sqrt3}+\sqrt{120\sqrt3}\right)=\boxed{6\sqrt[4]{3}\left(\sqrt{70}+\sqrt{30}\right)}\approx152.0$.
