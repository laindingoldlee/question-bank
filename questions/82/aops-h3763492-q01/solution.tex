In the prime factorization, the divisor-sum factor for an odd prime $p$ is $1+p+\cdots+p^e$, whose parity is that of $e+1$. The factor for the prime $2$ is always odd. Hence the divisor sum is odd exactly when every odd prime exponent is even, equivalently when $N$ is a square or twice a square. The next square after $2025=45^2$ is $2116=46^2$; the smallest twice-square above $2025$ is $2\cdot32^2=2048$. The answer is $\boxed{2048}$.
