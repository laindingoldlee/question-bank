The condition is $2\lfloor x\rfloor=\{x\}+x=\lfloor x\rfloor+2\{x\}$, i.e. $\lfloor x\rfloor=2\{x\}$. Since $0\le2\{x\}<2$ and $\lfloor x\rfloor$ is an integer, either $\lfloor x\rfloor=0=\{x\}$, giving $x=0$, or $\lfloor x\rfloor=1$, $\{x\}=\dfrac12$, giving $x=\dfrac32$. The sum is $\dfrac32$. Answer $\boxed{\textbf{(C)}\ \dfrac32}$.
