At each stage the fractional tail is strictly between zero and one, so the integer part is forced. The Euclidean divisions are $268=1\cdot187+81$, $187=2\cdot81+25$, $81=3\cdot25+6$, and $25=4\cdot6+1$. Thus $a=1,b=2,c=3,d=4,e+1=6$. Hence $a+b+c+d+e=\boxed{15}$.
