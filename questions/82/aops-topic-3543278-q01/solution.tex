Factor the left side as $\dfrac{4-\pi}{4} x^2$. Since $4-\pi>0$, dividing gives $x^2=\dfrac{4(3+3\sqrt 3-2\pi)}{4-\pi}$. The numerator inside the square root is positive (for example $\sqrt 3>\dfrac{5}{3}$ and $\pi<\dfrac{22}{7}$ imply it exceeds $\dfrac{12}{7}$). Therefore the two solutions are $\boxed{x=\pm 2\sqrt{\dfrac{3+3\sqrt 3-2\pi}{4-\pi}}}$.
