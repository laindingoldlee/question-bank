There are $4\cdot3=12$ such numbers:
\[
13,\ 15,\ 17,\ 31,\ 35,\ 37,\ 51,\ 53,\ 57,\ 71,\ 73,\ 75 .
\]
Those ending in $5$ ($15,35,75$) are multiples of $5$, $51=3\cdot17$ and $57=3\cdot19$ are composite, and the rest, $13,17,31,37,53,71,73$, are prime. So $\boxed{7}$ of them are prime.
