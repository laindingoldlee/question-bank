A baseball team gets exactly $5$ hits in a $9$-inning game, every hit is a double, and each hit independently falls in an inning chosen uniformly at random. A run scores only when a double is hit while a runner is already on second base, and runners left on base when an inning ends do not carry over. What is the probability that the team scores no runs?
