The booster's impulse is $12{,}000{,}000\cdot120=1{,}440{,}000{,}000=1.44\times10^9$ newton-seconds.

The maximum impulse of a type $k$ motor is $2.5\cdot2^{k-1}$, and for $k\ge2$ its range is from $2.5\cdot2^{k-2}$ up to $2.5\cdot2^{k-1}$. Dividing by $2.5$, we need
\[
2^{k-2}<\dfrac{1.44\times10^9}{2.5}=576{,}000{,}000\le2^{k-1}.
\]
By hand: $2^{10}=1024$, $2^{20}=1024^2=1{,}048{,}576$, and $2^{30}=1{,}048{,}576\cdot1024=1{,}073{,}741{,}824$, so $2^{29}=536{,}870{,}912$. Since $2^{29}<576{,}000{,}000\le2^{30}$, we get $k-1=30$, i.e.\ type $\boxed{31}$.

(A quick estimate: $2^{10}\approx10^3$ gives $2^{30}\approx10^9$, and $5.76\times10^8$ lies between $2^{29}\approx5.4\times10^8$ and $2^{30}$.)
