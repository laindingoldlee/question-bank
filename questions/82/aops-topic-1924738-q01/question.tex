Model rocket motors are rated by their total impulse, where impulse equals force (in newtons) multiplied by time (in seconds). A type $1$ motor has impulse up to $2.5$ newton-seconds, a type $2$ motor from $2.5$ up to $5$ newton-seconds, and so on, each motor type having a maximum impulse twice that of the previous type. The Space Shuttle Solid Rocket Booster produces a force of $12$ million newtons for $120$ seconds. To what type of model rocket motor would this correspond? Solve without a calculator.
