For a cut coordinate $X$ uniform on $[0,12]$, the smaller length $\min(X,12-X)$ is uniform on $[0,6]$. The smallest of the eight volumes is the product of these three independent smaller lengths. Thus it equals $216UVW$, where $U,V,W$ are independent uniform variables on $[0,1]$.

For $0<t<1$, conditioning on one variable gives
\[
F_2(t)=\Pr(UV<t)=t+\int_t^1\dfrac{t}{u}\,du=t(1-\ln t).
\]
Conditioning once more,
\[
F_3(t)=t+\int_t^1F_2(\dfrac{t}{u})\,du
=t\left(1-\ln t+\dfrac{(\ln t)^2}{2}\right).
\]
Here $t=\dfrac{27}{216}=\dfrac{1}{8}$, so the probability is
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{1}{8}\left(1+\ln8+\dfrac{(\ln8)^2}{2}\right)}.
\]
The strict versus nonstrict inequality does not change the probability because equality has probability zero.
