A test has 30 Sprint questions worth one point each and 8 Target questions worth two points each. A student independently answers each Sprint question correctly with probability $\dfrac{4}{5}$ and each Target question with probability $\dfrac{3}{5}$. Find the probability of a total score at least $ 40 $.
