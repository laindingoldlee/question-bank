From the $6$th term to the $13$th term the common difference $d$ is added $13-6=7$ times, so
\[7d=5\dfrac{5}{8}-\dfrac{3}{8}=\dfrac{45}{8}-\dfrac{3}{8}=\dfrac{42}{8}=\dfrac{21}{4},\qquad d=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{4}}.\]
