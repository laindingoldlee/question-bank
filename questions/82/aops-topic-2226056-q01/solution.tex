$g(x)=f(x+1)$ is defined exactly when $x+1$ lies in the domain of $f$:
\[-1<x+1<1\iff-2<x<0 .\]
So the domain of $g$ is $\boxed{-2<x<0}$, which is not the same as the domain of $f$: the graph of $g$ is the graph of $f$ shifted $1$ unit to the left.
