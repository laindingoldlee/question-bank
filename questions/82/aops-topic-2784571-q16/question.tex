Let $P$ be a point outside a circle $\omega$ with centre $O$. Let $A,B$ be points on $\omega$ such that $PB$ is tangent to $\omega$ and $PA=AB$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of $AB$. Given $OM=1$ and $PB=3$, the value of $AB^2$ is $\dfrac mn$ for relatively prime positive integers $m,n$. Find $m+n$.
