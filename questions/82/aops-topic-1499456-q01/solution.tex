Let the width be $w$ feet. The garden together with the border is a square of side $5+2w$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.8cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real w=1.405125; real L=5+2*w;
fill((0,0)--(L,0)--(L,L)--(0,L)--cycle,gray(0.82));
fill((w,w)--(w+5,w)--(w+5,w+5)--(w,w+5)--cycle,palegreen);
draw((0,0)--(L,0)--(L,L)--(0,L)--cycle,linewidth(1)); draw((w,w)--(w+5,w)--(w+5,w+5)--(w,w+5)--cycle,linewidth(1));
label("garden",(L/2,L/2+0.5)); label("$5\times5$",(L/2,L/2-0.4));
draw((w+5,L/2)--(L,L/2),Arrows(TeXHead)); label("$w$",((w+5+L)/2,L/2),N);
draw((0,-0.5)--(L,-0.5),Arrows(TeXHead)); label("$5+2w$",(L/2,-0.5),S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The border is the large square minus the garden, so
\[
(5+2w)^2-25=36,\qquad (5+2w)^2=61 .
\]
Since $5+2w>0$, we get $5+2w=\sqrt{61}$ and
\[
w=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt{61}-5}{2}\text{ feet}}\approx1.41\text{ feet}.
\]
(The negative square root would give a negative width.)
