After buying $100$, each exchange followed by eating decreases the wrapper count by $3$. The maximum number of exchanges is $\left\lfloor\dfrac{100-1}{3}\right\rfloor=33$, leaving one wrapper. Thus the maximum total is $\boxed{133}$.
