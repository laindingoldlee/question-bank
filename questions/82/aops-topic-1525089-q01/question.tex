A known solution finds the smallest positive $k$ with $63\mid 2^{39}+k$ by using $2^6\equiv1\pmod{63}$: then $2^{39}=\left(2^6\right)^6\cdot2^3\equiv8$, so $k=63-8=55$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Explain why no power $2^m$ with $m\ge1$ is congruent to $1$ modulo $24$, so this method cannot be copied directly for the modulus $24$.
\item[(b)] Find the smallest positive integer $k$ such that $2^{100}+k$ is divisible by $24$.
\end{enumerate}
