A function $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ satisfies $f(x+y)=f(x)+f(y)$ for all real numbers $x$ and $y$. Let $c=f(1)$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $f(q)=cq$ for every rational number $q$.
\item[(b)] Prove that if, in addition, $f$ is continuous, then $f(x)=cx$ for every real number $x$.
\end{enumerate}
