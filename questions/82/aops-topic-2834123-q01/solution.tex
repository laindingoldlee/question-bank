Write each number as $2y_i+1$ with $y_i\ge0$. Then $\sum(2y_i+1)=18$ gives $\sum y_i=6$. Multisets of six nonnegative integers with sum $6$ correspond to partitions of $6$ into at most $6$ parts, i.e. all partitions of $6$. There are $p(6)=\boxed{11}$ of them.
