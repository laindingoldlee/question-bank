The units digit of a product depends only on the units digits of the factors. The numbers used end in $2,4,6,8$, and each of these endings occurs $10$ times ($2,12,\dots,92$, and so on). Since $2\cdot4\cdot6\cdot8=384$ ends in $4$, the product has the same units digit as $4^{10}$. The units digits of the powers of $4$ repeat $4,6,4,6,\dots$ (odd powers end in $4$, even powers in $6$), so the answer is $\boxed{6}$.
