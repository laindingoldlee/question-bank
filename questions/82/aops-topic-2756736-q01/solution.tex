By the law of cosines, $BC^2=25+64-2\cdot5\cdot8\cdot\dfrac12=49$, so $BC=7$. The median formula (Apollonius) gives
\[AM^2=\dfrac{2AB^2+2AC^2-BC^2}4=\dfrac{50+128-49}4=\dfrac{129}4,\]
so $AM=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt{129}}2}$.
\begin{asy}

pair A=(0,0),B=5*dir(60),C=(8,0);pair M=(B+C)/2;draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--M,dashed);dot(M);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,N);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$M$",M,NE);label("$5$",(A+B)/2,NW);label("$8$",(4,0),S);label("$60^\circ$",A+1.15*dir(42),fontsize(7pt));
\end{asy}

(Equivalently, with vectors, $\overrightarrow{AM}=\dfrac12(\overrightarrow{AB}+\overrightarrow{AC})$ and $AM^2=\dfrac14(25+64+40)=\dfrac{129}4$.)
