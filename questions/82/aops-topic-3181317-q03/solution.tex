Put $A=(0,0),B=(w,0),C=(w,2w),D=(0,2w)$. Then $E=(\dfrac{w}{2},2w),F=(\dfrac{w}{4},w),G=(w,w),H=(w,\dfrac{w}{2})$. The shoelace formula gives $[EFHG]=\dfrac{9}{16}w^2=9$, so $w=4$. Hence $CH=\dfrac{3}{2}w=\boxed6$.
