In the complement graph, each person has exactly two neighbours. A finite simple graph with every degree $2$ is a disjoint union of cycles of length at least $3$. With six labelled vertices, the possibilities are one $6$-cycle or two $3$-cycles.

The first type has $\dfrac{(6-1)!}{2}=60$ possibilities. The second has $\dfrac{\binom63}{2}=10$ partitions into two triangles. Complementation is a bijection, so the answer is $\boxed{70}$.
