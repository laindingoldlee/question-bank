Each square loses $\dfrac{1}{4}$ inch on each of two opposite sides, so the visible part is $3-2\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{5}{2}$ inches on a side. The quilt is $5$ feet $=60$ inches on a side, so each side has $60\div\dfrac{5}{2}=24$ squares, and she needs $24^2=\boxed{576}$ squares.
