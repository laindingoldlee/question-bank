CONSTELLATION has $13$ letters: the vowels A, E, I, O, O and the consonants C, L, L, N, N, S, T, T.

Choose the $5$ positions occupied by vowels: $\dbinom{13}{5}=1287$ ways. The vowels must fill these positions in the order A, E, I, O, O, so there is only $1$ way to place them. The $8$ consonants, with L, N and T each appearing twice, fill the other $8$ positions in
\[\dfrac{8!}{2!\,2!\,2!}=5040\]
ways. Hence the number of arrangements is
\[1287\cdot5040=\boxed{6486480}.\]

(Check: all arrangements number $\dfrac{13!}{2!\,2!\,2!\,2!}=389188800$, and the vowels A, E, I, O, O can appear in $\dfrac{5!}{2!}=60$ different orders, equally often; $\dfrac{389188800}{60}=6486480$.)
