Each round multiplies the number by a factor whose expected value is $\dfrac12\cdot2+\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac54$, independently of the other rounds. The expected value of a product of independent factors is the product of the expected values, so the answer is $\left(\dfrac54\right)^5=\boxed{\dfrac{3125}{1024}}$.
