A reflection preserves lengths and therefore areas. Triangle $ABC$ has a right angle at $A$ with legs $AB=3$ and $AC=4$, so its area, and that of $A'B'C'$, is $\dfrac12\cdot3\cdot4=\boxed{6}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,9),B=(3,9),C=(0,5);
draw((-0.5,4)--(4,4),gray,Arrow(4));draw((0,3.6)--(0,10),gray,Arrow(4));
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
label("$A$",A,NW);label("$B$",B,NE);label("$C$",C,W);label("$3$",(A+B)/2,N,fontsize(8pt));label("$4$",(A+C)/2,W,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}
