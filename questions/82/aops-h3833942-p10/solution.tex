Assume a fair coin. Ignore initial tails, and begin a cycle just after a head. Brandon wins during the ensuing head run with probability $u=2^{-2024}$. Otherwise a tail ends that run. Ben then wins if the next $2023$ flips are tails, with probability $v=2^{-2023}$. If this fails, a head starts a new cycle. If $p$ is Ben\textquotesingle s eventual winning probability, $p=(1-u)v+(1-u)(1-v)p$. Thus $p=\dfrac{(1-u)v}{u+(1-u)v}=\dfrac{2^{2024}-1}{3\cdot2^{2023}-1}$. In this reduced form, $a=2024,b=3,c=2023$, so $\boxed{a+b+c=4050}$.
