The least common multiple takes each prime to the larger of its two exponents:
\[
\operatorname{lcm}\left(2^2\cdot3^3,\ 3^4\cdot5^3\right)=2^2\cdot3^4\cdot5^3=4\cdot81\cdot125=\boxed{40500}.
\]
