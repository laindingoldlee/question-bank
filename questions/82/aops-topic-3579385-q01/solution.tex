Put $g(n)=f(n)+1$. The given rule becomes $g(ab)=g(a)g(b)$ for coprime factors and $g(p^e)=e+1$. Thus if $N=\prod p_i^{e_i}$, then $f(N)+1=\prod(e_i+1)$, the divisor-count formula. We require this product to be $21$. The possible nonzero exponent multisets are $(20)$ or $(6,2)$. Their smallest realizations use the smallest primes, with the larger exponent on the smaller prime: $2^{20}$ and $2^6\cdot3^2=576$. The latter is smaller, so the units digit is $\boxed{6}$, choice (D).
