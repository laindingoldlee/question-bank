A function on positive integers satisfies $f(1)=0$, $f(p^n)=n$ for prime $p$ and positive $n$, and $f(ab)=f(a)f(b)+f(a)+f(b)$ whenever $a,b$ are coprime. If $N$ is the smallest positive integer with $f(N)=20$, find its units digit. Choices: (A) $0$; (B) $2$; (C) $4$; (D) $6$; (E) $8$.
