Fix a position and a digit. The other two positions can be filled in $8\cdot7=56$ ways. Thus every digit occurs $56$ times in each position, and the total is $(1+\cdots+9)\cdot56\cdot(100+10+1)=45\cdot56\cdot111=\boxed{279720}$.
