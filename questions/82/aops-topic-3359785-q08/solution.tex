Wilson's theorem gives $102!\equiv-1\pmod{103}$. For $0\le k\le102$, the binomial coefficients satisfy $\binom{102}{k}\equiv(-1)^k\pmod{103}$, so $k!(102-k)!\equiv(-1)^{k+1}$. Include $0!=1$ in the product and pair the whole product with its reverse. Its square is therefore $\prod_{k=0}^{102}(-1)^{k+1}=(-1)^{5356}=\boxed1\pmod{103}$.
