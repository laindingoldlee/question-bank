Each sum is an arithmetic series: (number of terms) times (average of first and last term). The top has $3n-2$ terms and the bottom $5n-4$ terms, so
\[\dfrac{3+\cdots+3n}{5+\cdots+5n}=\dfrac{(3n-2)\cdot\dfrac{3+3n}2}{(5n-4)\cdot\dfrac{5+5n}2}=\dfrac{3(3n-2)(n+1)}{5(5n-4)(n+1)}=\dfrac{3(3n-2)}{5(5n-4)}.\]
Setting this equal to $\dfrac4{11}$: $33(3n-2)=20(5n-4)$, i.e. $99n-66=100n-80$, so $n=\boxed{14}$.
