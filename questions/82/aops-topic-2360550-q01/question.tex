A pentagonal prism has two pentagonal faces and five rectangular faces. A \emph{face diagonal} joins two vertices of the same face that are not joined by an edge, and a \emph{space diagonal} joins two vertices that are not on a common face. How many face diagonals and how many space diagonals does the prism have?
