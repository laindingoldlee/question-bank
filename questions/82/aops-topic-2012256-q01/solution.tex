Since \(18^3=5832>5256\), both primes are at most \(17\).

If one prime were \(2\), the other cube would be \(5256-8=5248\), which lies strictly between \(17^3=4913\) and \(18^3\), so it is not a cube. Hence both primes are odd.

The larger prime \(p\) satisfies \(p^3>\dfrac{5256}{2}=2628>13^3\), so \(14\le p\le17\), and the only prime in this range is \(p=17\), and the other cube is \(5256-4913=343=7^3\).

The primes are \(\boxed{7\text{ and }17}\).
