Let $H_n,J_n,T_n$ count valid length-$n$ strings ending respectively in exactly one head, exactly two heads, and one tail. Then
\[H_{n+1}=T_n,\qquad J_{n+1}=H_n,\qquad T_{n+1}=H_n+J_n,\]
with $(H_1,J_1,T_1)=(1,0,1)$. The successive triples are
\[(1,1,1),(1,1,2),(2,1,2),(2,2,3),(3,2,4),(4,3,5),(5,4,7).\]
Thus there are $5+4+7=16$ valid eight-toss strings among $2^8$, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{16}}$.
