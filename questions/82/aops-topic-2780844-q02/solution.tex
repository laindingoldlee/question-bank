$\dfrac{60}n$ is a whole number exactly when $n$ is a positive divisor of $60$ ($n=0$ is not allowed). Since $60=2^2\cdot3\cdot5$ has $(2+1)(1+1)(1+1)=12$ divisors, namely $1,2,3,4,5,6,10,12,15,20,30,60$, there are $\boxed{12}$ values. (``In simplest form'' does not restrict anything: the value of the fraction is the same either way.)
