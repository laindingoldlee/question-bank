Expand both sides carefully: $4-(x+2)=4-x-2=2-x$ and $-3(x+4)=-3x-12$. So
\[
2-x<-3x-12\iff 2x<-14\iff \boxed{x<-7}.
\]
Check with $x=-8$: the left side is $4-(-6)=10$ and the right side is $-3(-4)=12$, and $10<12$. With $x=-7$ both sides equal $9$, so $-7$ itself is excluded. (A common slip is $4-(x+2)=4-x+2$, which leads to the wrong answer $x<-9$.)
