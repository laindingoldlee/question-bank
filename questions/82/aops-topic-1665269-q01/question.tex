Two towns $A$ and $B$ lie to the north of a straight road that runs east-west. The points of the road nearest to $A$ and to $B$ are $C$ and $D$, with $AC=10$ km, $BD=20$ km and $CD=40$ km. The towns are connected neither to the road nor to each other. Two new straight roads are to be built, one from each town to the existing road, so that the route from $A$ to $B$ along the new roads and the existing road is as short as possible. Find the length of this shortest route.
