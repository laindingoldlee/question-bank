A jeweller has four small bars that look like gold. Exactly one is counterfeit: its weight differs slightly from that of a genuine bar, but it is not known whether it is heavier or lighter. The three genuine bars weigh the same. Using a balance scale without weights, what is the minimum number of weighings that guarantees identifying the counterfeit bar?
