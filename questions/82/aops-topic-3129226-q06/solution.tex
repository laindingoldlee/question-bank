For even $n$, the sum is 2 modulo 4, so it has at most one factor of 2 and hence at most one trailing zero. For odd $n\ge3$, it is $1+3=4$ modulo 8, since $3^n\equiv3\pmod8$ and the even powers are divisible by 8. Thus it has exactly two factors of 2, allowing at most two trailing zeros. For $n=1$ the sum is 10. Finally, $n=3$ gives $1+8+27+64=100$, attaining $\boxed2$ trailing zeros.
