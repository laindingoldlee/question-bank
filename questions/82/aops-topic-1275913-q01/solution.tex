$P(\text{all red})=\dfrac{1}{32}$, and then the drawn ball is certainly red. By symmetry each ball drawn is red with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. By Bayes' theorem,
\[
P(\text{all red}\mid\text{red drawn})=\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{32}\cdot1}{\dfrac{1}{2}}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{16}}.
\]
