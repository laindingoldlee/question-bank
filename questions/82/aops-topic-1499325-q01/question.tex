A circle $(\mathcal C)$ with centre $O$ and radius $R$ and a chord $BC$ of it are fixed. A point $A$ moves on the circle (with $A\ne B$, $A\ne C$), and $G$ is the centroid of triangle $ABC$. Let $G'$ be the reflection of $G$ in the line $AB$. Prove that $G'$ always lies on a fixed circle, and determine the centre and the radius of that circle.
