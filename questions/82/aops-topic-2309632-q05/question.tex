When a convenience store got a new owner, the old sodas were replaced by new ones that were $20\%$ larger, and the price of a new soda was $20\%$ less than the price of an old soda. What is the ratio of the cost per ounce of the old soda to the cost per ounce of the new soda? Express your answer as a common fraction.
