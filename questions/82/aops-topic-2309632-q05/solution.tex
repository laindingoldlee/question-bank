If an old soda has $V$ ounces and costs $P$, a new soda has $1.2V$ ounces and costs $0.8P$. The costs per ounce are $\dfrac PV$ and $\dfrac{0.8P}{1.2V}=\dfrac23\cdot\dfrac PV$. So the ratio of old to new is
\[\dfrac{\dfrac PV}{\dfrac23\cdot\dfrac PV}=\boxed{\dfrac32}.\]
