An architect designs a high-rise building with a positive number $N$ of suites, all of them on full stories. The suites must fit either with exactly $14$ suites on every story or, in an alternative layout, with exactly $12$ suites on every story. What is the least possible number of stories in the layout with $14$ suites per story? (Why is the answer not $0$?)
