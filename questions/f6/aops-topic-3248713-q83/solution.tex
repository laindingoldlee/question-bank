Let the cube roots be $a,b$. Their product is the real cube root of $324-325=-1$, so $ab=-1$. Thus $u=a+b$ satisfies $u^3=a^3+b^3+3abu=36-3u$. Factoring gives $(u-3)(u^2+3u+12)=0$. The quadratic has negative discriminant $9-48$, so the only real possibility is $\boxed3$.
