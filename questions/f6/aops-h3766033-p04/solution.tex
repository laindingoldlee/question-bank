Yes. Pair the $1012$ odd-indexed cards among themselves, and pair the $1012$ even-indexed cards among themselves. Whenever the first player takes a card, the second takes its paired mate. Such a mate is always available. Each receives exactly one card from each pair, hence exactly $506$ odd squares and $506$ even squares. Odd squares are $1$ modulo $4$ and even squares $0$, so the first player\textquotesingle s sum is $506\equiv2\pmod4$.
