Seven lines are drawn in the plane. There are two pairs of parallel lines, but not all four of these lines are parallel. The other three lines, which are not parallel to any of the first four lines (or to each other), pass through a single point, and there are no other points at which more than two of the lines meet. Into how many regions do these seven lines divide the plane?
