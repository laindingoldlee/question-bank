Take $A=(0,0)$, $B=(10,0)$, $C=(10,16)$, $D=(0,16)$. Then $K=(8,0)$, $L=(10,2)$, $M=(2,16)$, $N=(0,14)$. Central symmetry about $(5,8)$ exchanges $K,M$ and $L,N$, so $O=(5,8)$. The area $[KBLO]$ is $[KBO]+[BLO]=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot2\cdot8+\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot2\cdot5=13$. The opposite quadrilateral has equal area, so the sum is $\boxed{26}$.

\end{asy}
