By the binomial theorem the coefficient of $x^k$ is $\dbinom{2000}{k}a^kb^{2000-k}$. Equal coefficients of $x^2$ and $x^3$ mean
\[
\dbinom{2000}{2}a^2b^{1998}=\dbinom{2000}{3}a^3b^{1997}\iff\dbinom{2000}{2}b=\dbinom{2000}{3}a .
\]
Since $\dbinom{2000}{3}=\dbinom{2000}{2}\cdot\dfrac{1998}{3}=666\dbinom{2000}{2}$, this says $b=666a$. As $\gcd(a,b)=\gcd(a,666a)=a$ must be $1$, we get $a=1$, $b=666$ and $a+b=\boxed{667}$.
