Since $y>0$, the ratio $\dfrac{x}{y}$ is largest when $x$ is large and $y$ is small, and smallest when $x$ is small and $y$ is large:
\[
\dfrac{x}{y}<\dfrac{5}{y}<\dfrac{5}{6},\qquad \dfrac{x}{y}>\dfrac{2}{y}>\dfrac{2}{9}.
\]
Conversely every value in between occurs. For a fixed $y$ the ratio takes every value in the interval $\left(\dfrac{2}{y},\dfrac{5}{y}\right)$; as $y$ runs over $(6,9)$ these intervals overlap (each has its left end below $\dfrac{1}{3}$ and right end above $\dfrac{5}{9}$), and their union is $\left(\dfrac{2}{9},\dfrac{5}{6}\right)$. So the possible values are exactly
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{2}{9}<\dfrac{x}{y}<\dfrac{5}{6}} .
\]
