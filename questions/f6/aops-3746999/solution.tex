Standardise: $Z = \dfrac{X-60}{15}$ has the standard normal distribution, and $X>75$ is equivalent to $Z>1$. Hence $\boxed{\Pr(X>75)=1-\Phi(1)\approx0.1587}$, about $15.87\%$. This is the tail above one standard deviation, not one quarter of the distribution. Equivalently it is $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} \int_1^\infty e^{- \dfrac{t^2}{2}} \, d t$.
