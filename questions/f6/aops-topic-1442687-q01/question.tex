The \emph{roots} of a quadratic polynomial $x^2+px+q$ are the solutions of $x^2+px+q=0$, that is, the values of $x$ that make the polynomial equal to $0$. Suppose $x^2+px+q=(x+r)(x+s)$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the roots in terms of $r$ and $s$.
\item[(b)] Express the sum and the product of the roots in terms of $r$ and $s$, and in terms of $p$ and $q$.
\item[(c)] Apply this to $x^2+5x+6$.
\end{enumerate}
