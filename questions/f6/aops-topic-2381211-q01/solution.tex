The numbers are $\dfrac{1000+2}{2}=501$ and $\dfrac{1000-2}{2}=499$. Since $5+0+1=6$ is divisible by $3$, $501=3\cdot167$ is composite. (And $499$ is prime: it is not divisible by $2,3,5,7,11,13,17,19$, and $23^2>499$.) The composite number is $\boxed{501}$.
