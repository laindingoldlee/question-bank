Use the difference of cubes: $7999999999=2000^3-1=(2000-1)(2000^2+2000+1)=1999\cdot4002001$. To certify primality, trial division of $1999$ by primes at most $44$ leaves no zero remainder; trial division of $4002001$ by primes at most $2000$ also leaves none. These bounds suffice because a composite integer has a prime divisor no greater than its square root. Thus the prime factors are $\boxed{1999\text{ and }4002001}$.
