Squaring a sum gives $(a+b)^2=a^2+2ab+b^2 $, not $ a^2+b^2 $. The missing cross term is nonzero for positive $ a,b $. Here $ c=\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=\boxed5 $, whereas $ a+b=7 $.
