Let $(a_1,a_2,\dots,a_{100})$ be a permutation of $(1,2,\dots,100)$. A salesman's item has initial price $p_0=1$ dollar. On day $i$ ($1\le i\le100$) he raises the price by $a_i\%$, so that $p_i=p_{i-1}+\dfrac{a_i}{100}p_{i-1}$. For which permutation is the final price $p_{100}$ greatest?
