It suffices to exclude multiples of $2,3,5,7$, since every other one-digit divisor has one of these prime factors. Inclusion--exclusion on integers 100 through 999 gives $\sum_{S\subseteq\{2,3,5,7\}}(-1)^{|S|}(\lfloor\dfrac{999}{\prod S}\rfloor-\lfloor\dfrac{99}{\prod S}\rfloor)=\boxed{206}$, where the empty product is one. This formula counts each integer once exactly when none of the four primes divides it.
