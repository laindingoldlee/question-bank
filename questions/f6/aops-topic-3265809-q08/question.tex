Four houses in a row have distinct colours yellow, blue, green, and red. Their residents A,B,C,D each have one distinct pet: hamster, bird, cat, or dog. The cup is held by one resident. The yellow house does not hold it; B has the hamster; D lives in blue; the bird is not in green; A has the dog, is allergic to cats, and lives at the right end; C lives next to green but not in red; the dog is not next to the cat. The cat sees the cup in the immediately neighbouring house to its left. (a) Can the holder be determined uniquely? (b) Would knowing the complete left-to-right colour order determine the holder?
