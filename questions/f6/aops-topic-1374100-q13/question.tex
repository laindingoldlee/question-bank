In a town, every couple keeps having children until they have a girl, and then stops, so the possible families are $G$, $BG$, $BBG$, $BBBG$, \dots. Each child is a boy or a girl with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, independently. In the long run (over a very large number of families), what fraction of the children are boys? Express your answer as a common fraction.
