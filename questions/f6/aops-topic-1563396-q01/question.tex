The points $A$, $B$, $C$ are $\left(a\cos t_i,\ a\sin t_i\right)$ for $i=1,2,3$, where $a>0$, and they are distinct. Suppose
\[
\cos(t_1-t_2)+\cos(t_2-t_3)+\cos(t_3-t_1)=-\dfrac{3}{2} .
\]
Prove that triangle $ABC$ is equilateral.
