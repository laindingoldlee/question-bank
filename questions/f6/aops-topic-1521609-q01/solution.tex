Since $2^6=64=63+1$, we have $2^6\equiv1\pmod{63}$. Then
\[
2^{39}=\left(2^6\right)^6\cdot2^3\equiv1^6\cdot8=8\pmod{63}.
\]
So $2^{39}+k$ is divisible by $63$ exactly when $k\equiv-8\equiv55\pmod{63}$, and the smallest positive $k$ is $\boxed{55}$.
