Because $F$ lies on the perpendicular bisector of $AB$, $FA=FB$. The corresponding fact for $AC$ gives $FA=FC$. Thus $FA=FB=FC$, so the circle centred at $F$ with radius $FA$ passes through all three vertices. Hence $F$ is the circumcentre.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(-2,0),B=(2,0),C=(1,3),F=(0,1),M=(A+B)/2,N=(A+C)/2;draw(circle(F,sqrt(5)));draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw((0,-1.5)--(0,4),blue+dashed);draw(N-2*unit((3,-3))--N+2*unit((3,-3)),red+dashed);draw(F--A);draw(F--B);draw(F--C);label("A",A,SW);label("B",B,SE);label("C",C,(0,1));label("F",F,W);label("M",M,S);label("N",N,NW);
\end{asy}
