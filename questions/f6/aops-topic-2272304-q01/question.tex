Two sailboats, each travelling at a constant speed, start from the same port and travel in straight lines whose directions differ by $60^\circ$. One boat leaves one hour after the other and travels $5$ km per hour faster than the boat that left first. Two hours after the first boat left, they are exactly $10$ km apart. What is the speed of the slower boat?
