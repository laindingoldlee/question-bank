We show $ w>2011 $. For $|x|\le2 $, $ w-2011=(x^2+\dfrac{x}{2}-2)^2+1-\dfrac{x^2}{4}$ is positive in the interior, and also positive at both endpoints. For $ x\ge2 $, write $ w-2011=x^2(x^2-4)+x(x^2-2)+5>0 $. For $ x=-2-u $ with $ u\ge0 $, expansion gives $ w-2011=u^4+7u^3+14u^2+6u+1>0 $. Thus no integer at most 2011 is possible. At $ x=1 $, $ w=2012 $, so the minimum attainable integer is $\boxed{2012}$.
