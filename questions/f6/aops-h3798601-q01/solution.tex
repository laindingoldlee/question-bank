The original area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot8\cdot8=32$. Each medial triangle is similar to its parent with length scale $\dfrac{1}{2}$, hence area scale $\dfrac{1}{4}$. Three repetitions give area $32(\dfrac{1}{4})^3=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$.

\end{asy}
