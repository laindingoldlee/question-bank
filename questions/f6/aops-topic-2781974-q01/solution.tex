We have $u^n+u^{n-1}v=u^{n-1}(u+v)$. Since $\gcd(u,c)=1$, no prime factor of $c$ divides $u$, so $\gcd(u^{n-1},c)=1$. From $c\mid u^{n-1}(u+v)$ and $\gcd(c,u^{n-1})=1$ it follows that $c\mid u+v$ (Euclid's lemma, generalised). $\blacksquare$
