Subtract each digit from 9. The five deficits are nonnegative and sum to 5, giving $\binom94=126$ possibilities by stars and bars. Every deficit is at most 5, so every digit is at least 4; in particular no leading-zero restriction removes any. There are 90000 five-digit integers, so the probability is $\dfrac{126}{90000}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{5000}}$.
