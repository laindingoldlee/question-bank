A bug walks on the second hand of a clock. The second hand ticks once each second, turning $6^\circ$ each time. In each second, during the first half second the hand is stationary and the bug walks $1$ mm outward along it; during the second half second the bug stands still while the hand ticks, carrying the bug along a circular arc. The bug starts at the centre of the clock and this continues for exactly $40$ seconds (ending with the $40$th tick). The total distance travelled by the bug is $\dfrac{a+b\pi}{c}$ mm, where $a$, $b$, $c$ are positive integers with $\gcd(a,b,c)=1$. Find $a+b+c$.
