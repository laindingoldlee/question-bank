A $4$-digit palindrome is $\overline{abba}=1001a+110b$ with $1\le a\le9$ and $0\le b\le9$; there are $90$ of them.
\begin{itemize}
\item It is divisible by $2$ exactly when its last digit $a$ is even: $a\in\{2,4,6,8\}$, giving $4\cdot10=40$ palindromes.
\item It is divisible by $3$ exactly when its digit sum $2a+2b$ is, i.e. when $3\mid a+b$. For each $a$ the number of digits $b$ with $b\equiv-a\pmod3$ is $4$ (if $3\mid a$) or $3$ (otherwise), giving $3\cdot4+6\cdot3=30$ palindromes.
\item It is divisible by both when $a$ is even and $3\mid a+b$: for $a=2,4,6,8$ there are $3,3,4,3$ choices of $b$, so $13$ palindromes.
\end{itemize}
Divisible by exactly one of $2$ and $3$: $40+30-2\cdot13=\boxed{44}$.
