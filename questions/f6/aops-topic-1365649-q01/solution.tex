Write the number as $\overline{abcd}$ with $1\le a\le9$ and $0\le b,c,d\le9$. Put $a'=a-1$, so we count solutions of
\[
a'+b+c+d=16,\qquad 0\le a'\le8,\quad 0\le b,c,d\le9 .
\]
\textbf{Stars and bars without the upper bounds:} $\dbinom{16+3}{3}=\dbinom{19}{3}=969$.

\textbf{Subtract the solutions that break a bound} (complementary counting with inclusion-exclusion):
\begin{itemize}
\item $a'\ge9$: put $a''=a'-9$, then $a''+b+c+d=7$: $\dbinom{10}{3}=120$ solutions.
\item $b\ge10$: then the remaining sum is $6$: $\dbinom93=84$ solutions; the same for $c$ and for $d$.
\item Two bounds broken at once would need a sum of at least $19>16$, impossible.
\end{itemize}
So the count is $969-120-3\cdot84=\boxed{597}$.
