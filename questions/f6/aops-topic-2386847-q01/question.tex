Let $r$ be the remainder when $40!+k$ is divided by $41$, and let $r_1$ be the remainder when $11^{41}+18^{41}$ is divided by $41$. If $|r_1-r|=4$, find all possible integers $k$; in particular, list those with $0\le k\le40$.
