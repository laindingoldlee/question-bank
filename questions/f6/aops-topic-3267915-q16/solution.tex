The integer polynomial $p(x)-q(x)$ vanishes at $1$, so it is $(x-1)h(x)$ with integer-coefficient $h$. Thus its value at $2023$ is divisible by $2022$. The choice $p(x)=x-1,q(x)=0$ gives exactly $2022$, showing no larger universal divisor is possible. Answer $\boxed{2022}$.
