Every integer leaves one of the $2021$ remainders $0,1,\dots,2020$ when divided by $2021$. Since $H$ has $2022>2021$ elements, by the pigeonhole principle two different elements $x\ne y$ of $H$ leave the same remainder. Then $2021\mid x-y$, and $x-y\ne0$. $\blacksquare$
