These are exactly the invertible residues modulo 1000. Pair each residue with its inverse; every pair contributes one, leaving only solutions of $x^2\equiv1\pmod{1000}$. Modulo 125 there are two such residues, $\pm1$; modulo eight all four odd residues work. The Chinese remainder theorem therefore gives eight self-inverse residues. They pair as x and -x, with four distinct pairs; each pair contributes $-x^2\equiv-1$. Their total product is $(-1)^4=1$. Hence the required remainder is $\boxed1$.
