Let $b_n=\dfrac{9}{a_n}$. The reciprocal recurrence becomes $b_{n+1}=b_n+b_{n-1}$, with $b_1=10,b_2=72$. The successive values through $b_{14}$ are $10,72,82,154,236,390,626,1016,1642,2658,4300,6958,11258,18216$. Since $9\cdot2024=18216$, the answer is $\boxed{k=14}$. All values increase from the second onward, proving uniqueness.
