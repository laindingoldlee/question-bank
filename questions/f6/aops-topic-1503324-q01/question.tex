In trapezoid $PQRS$, $PQ\parallel RS$ and $\dfrac{PQ}{RS}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Let $H$ be the midpoint of $RS$, $G$ the intersection of $PH$ and $SQ$, and $X$ the intersection of $PR$ and $SQ$. Show that $PX\cdot QX=XR\cdot XG$.
