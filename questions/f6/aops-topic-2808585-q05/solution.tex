$BC=13$. The midpoint of the hypotenuse is the circumcenter, so $AM=BM=\dfrac{13}2$. The perimeter is $5+\dfrac{13}2+\dfrac{13}2=\boxed{18}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

pair A=(0,0),B=(0,5),C=(12,0);pair M=(B+C)/2;draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--M,dashed);draw(A+(0.6,0)--A+(0.6,0.6)--A+(0,0.6));
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,N);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$M$",M,NE);
\end{asy}
