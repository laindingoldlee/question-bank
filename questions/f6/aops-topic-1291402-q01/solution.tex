A card violates the claim exactly when one side is even and the other side is not prime. So we must turn every card that could hide such a pair:
\begin{itemize}
\item $2$ and $6$ are even: their backs must be checked for primality.
\item $2015=5\cdot13\cdot31$ is not prime: its back must be checked for being even.
\item $5$ and $7$ are odd primes: a prime back is fine whatever is on the other side, and an even number on the back would have a prime ($5$ or $7$) opposite, which satisfies the claim.
\end{itemize}
So $3$ cards must be turned: $\boxed{\textbf{(C)}}$. (Note $6$ is also not prime, but it is turned anyway because it is even.)
