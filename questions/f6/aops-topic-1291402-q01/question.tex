Stephen has $5$ cards, each with a number on each side. He claims: ``If a card has an even number on one side, then it has a prime number on the other side.'' The visible faces show $2$, $5$, $6$, $7$ and $2015$. What is the least number of cards that must be turned over to check whether the claim is true?

$\textbf{(A) }1\qquad\textbf{(B) }2\qquad\textbf{(C) }3\qquad\textbf{(D) }4\qquad\textbf{(E) }5$
