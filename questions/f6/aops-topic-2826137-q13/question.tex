A regular hexagon is inscribed in a circle and circumscribed about a concentric circle of radius $1$. The area inside the larger circle but outside the hexagon is $\dfrac{a\pi}{b}-c\sqrt d$, where the positive integers satisfy $\gcd(a,b)=1$ and $d$ is squarefree. Find $a+b+c+d$.
