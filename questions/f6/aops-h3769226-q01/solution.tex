Joining the midpoints of an equilateral triangle divides it into four equilateral triangles. Replacing any one current triangular piece this way increases the piece count by $3$. Perform this subdivision four times, each time within the piece at the same original vertex. The counts are $1,4,7,10,13$. Thus the final dissection has exactly thirteen equilateral pieces.
