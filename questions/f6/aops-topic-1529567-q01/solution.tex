Every term of the expansion is a monomial $a^ib^jc^kd^l$ with nonnegative integers $i+j+k+l=10$, and every such monomial occurs with a positive coefficient $\dfrac{10!}{i!\,j!\,k!\,l!}$, so none cancel. By stars and bars the number of such exponent quadruples is
\[
\dbinom{10+3}{3}=\dbinom{13}{3}=\boxed{286}.
\]
