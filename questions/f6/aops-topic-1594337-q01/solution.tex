"Between $100$ and $1000$" excludes $100=10^2$. The perfect squares strictly between $100$ and $1000$ are
\[
11^2=121,\ 12^2,\ \dots,\ 31^2=961,
\]
since $31^2=961<1000<1024=32^2$. That is $31-11+1=21$ squares. A square is even exactly when its root is even, and the even roots are $12,14,\dots,30$: $10$ of them. So the percentage is
\[
\dfrac{10}{21}\cdot100\%\approx\boxed{47.6\%}.
\]
(Counting $100=10^2$ as well would give $11$ even squares out of $22$, which is $50\%$; that is where the answer $50\%$ in the post comes from.)
