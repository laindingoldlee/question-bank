Let $x$ gallons of the $90\%$ stock be used, so $270-x$ gallons of the $30\%$ stock are used. Counting sodium:
\[
0.9x+0.3(270-x)=0.5\cdot270\iff 0.6x+81=135\iff 0.6x=54\iff x=90 .
\]
He needs $\boxed{90}$ gallons of the $90\%$ stock (and $180$ gallons of the $30\%$ stock).
