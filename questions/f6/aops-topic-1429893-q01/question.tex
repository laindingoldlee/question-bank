My uncle "simplifies" $\dfrac{16}{64}$ by cancelling the two sixes, which gives $\dfrac{1}{4}$, and this happens to be correct. Besides $\dfrac{16}{64}$, find all fractions $\dfrac{\overline{ab}}{\overline{bc}}$, where $\overline{ab}$ and $\overline{bc}$ are two-digit numbers with nonzero digits $a,b,c$, that are equal to $\dfrac{a}{c}$, excluding the trivial cases with $a=b=c$ (such as $\dfrac{11}{11}$).
