Write each odd positive integer as $x_i=2y_i-1$ with $y_i$ a positive integer. The equation becomes
\[
2(y_1+y_2+y_3+y_4)-4=98,\qquad y_1+y_2+y_3+y_4=51 .
\]
By stars and bars, the number of ordered quadruples of positive integers with sum $51$ is
\[
\binom{50}{3}=\dfrac{50\cdot49\cdot48}{6}=\boxed{19600}.
\]
