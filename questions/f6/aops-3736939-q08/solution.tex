The positive powers of 6 modulo 50 cycle as $6,36,16,46,26$, then return to 6. The cycle repeats because multiplication by 6 carries each displayed residue to the next. Every tower $T_h$ is $1\pmod5$, since its base is $6\equiv1\pmod5$. Thus the exponent $T_{99}$ selects the first entry of the five-term cycle, giving $\boxed6$.
