A parent normally drives at constant speed to school, arrives at 2:30 pm, and returns immediately with a child, reaching home at 3:30 pm. One day the child begins walking home at 2:00 pm. The parent leaves at the usual time, meets the child, immediately turns around, and reaches home at 3:10 pm. For how long had the child walked?
