Real numbers x, y, z satisfy the system of equations \[\dfrac{x^2}{y^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{z^2} +\dfrac{z^2}{x^2} =\dfrac{y}{x}+\dfrac{z}{y}+\dfrac{x}{z} \]\[\dfrac{x^3}{y}+\dfrac{2y^2}{z}+\dfrac{4z}{y^2} +\dfrac{4y}{x^3} = 59\]The maximum possible value of $x+y+z$ can be expressed in the form $\dfrac{a+b\sqrt{c}}{2}$ , where $a, b, c$ are positive integers, and c is square-free. Find $a + b + c$.
