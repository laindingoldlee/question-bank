Let $u=\dfrac{x}{y},v=\dfrac{y}{z},w=\dfrac{z}{x}$. The first equation says $u^2+v^2+w^2=uv+vw+wu$, so $(u-v)^2+(v-w)^2+(w-u)^2=0$. Thus $u=v=w$, and $uvw=1$ forces each to be 1. Hence $x=y=z=t\ne0$. The second equation becomes $t^2+2t+\dfrac{4}{t}+\dfrac{4}{t^2}=59$. With $U=t+\dfrac{2}{t}$ this is $U^2+2U-63=0$, giving $U=7$ or $-9$. The largest possible $t$ is $\dfrac{7+\sqrt{41}}{2}$, so the largest sum is $\dfrac{21+3\sqrt{41}}{2}$. Therefore $\boxed{a+b+c=65}$.
