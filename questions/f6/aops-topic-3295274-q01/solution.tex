The horizontal and initial vertical velocity components are equal, say $u$. Thus $ut=500$ and $ut-4.9t^2=25$. Subtracting gives $4.9t^2=475$, so $\boxed{t=\dfrac{5\sqrt{190}}{7}\text{ seconds}\approx9.85\text{ seconds}}$. A positive initial speed exists since $u=\dfrac{500}{t}>0$.
