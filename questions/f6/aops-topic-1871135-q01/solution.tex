Let $t=2x$, so $x=\dfrac {t}{2}$ runs over all positive numbers as $t$ does. Then
\[
f(t)=\dfrac{\dfrac {t}{2}}{2+\dfrac {t}{2}}=\dfrac{t}{4+t}\qquad(t>0).
\]
Hence $2f(x)=\boxed{\dfrac{2x}{4+x}}$ for $x>0$. (Check at $x=2$: $f(2)=f(2\cdot1)=\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $\dfrac{2}{4+2}=\dfrac{1}{3}$.)
