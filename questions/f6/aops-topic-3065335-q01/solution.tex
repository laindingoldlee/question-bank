Jake cannot win the game, since ending with $1000$ points would require five more wins than losses. Thus Jake loses all $500$ points. If he loses $L$ rounds, $500+100(3-L)=0$, giving $L=8$. Consequently the game lasts exactly $3+8=\boxed{11}$ rounds. It is attainable by three Jake wins followed by eight Ryan wins, without earlier ruin.
