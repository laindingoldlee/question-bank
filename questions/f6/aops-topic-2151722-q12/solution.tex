Squaring gives $G+\sqrt G=9$. Put $u=\sqrt G\ge0$; then $u^2+u-9=0$, so
\[u=\dfrac{-1\pm\sqrt{37}}{2},\]
and since $u\ge0$, $u=\dfrac{-1+\sqrt{37}}{2}$. Hence
\[G=u^2=\dfrac{1-2\sqrt{37}+37}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{19-\sqrt{37}}{2}}\approx6.459 .\]
Check: then $G+\sqrt G=u^2+u=9$, and $\sqrt9=3$.
