Label the two groups 0 and 1 each month. After $m$ months, each student has a binary word of length $m$. Two students have been separated exactly when their words differ, so all 16 words must be distinct. Thus $2^m\ge16$ and $m\ge4$. Assign the students the 16 different four-bit words and use their successive bits to form the monthly groups. This attains $\boxed4$ months.
