By the binomial theorem $(a+b)^{9001}=\sum_{k=0}^{9001}\binom{9001}{k}a^{9001-k}b^k$, and the sum of the coefficients is the value at $a=b=1$: $(1+1)^{9001}=2^{9001}$. So $n=\boxed{9001}$.
