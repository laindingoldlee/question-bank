An ant crawls along the surface of a $3\times3\times3$ cube (made of unit cubes) from one corner to the opposite corner. It must travel along exactly $9$ unit segments, each lying on an edge of the cube or on a face of the cube, separating two $1\times1$ squares. How many such routes are there?
