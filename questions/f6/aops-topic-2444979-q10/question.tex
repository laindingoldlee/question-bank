Bloggy has $1200$ treats. He chooses a positive divisor $n$ of $1200$ at random, each of the positive divisors of $1200$ being equally likely, and arranges the treats in $n$ rows of $\dfrac{1200}{n}$ treats each. Then, for each $x=1,2,\dots,n$, he eats all the treats in the $x$-th row with probability $\dfrac1x$. What is the expected number of treats he eats, rounded to the nearest integer?
