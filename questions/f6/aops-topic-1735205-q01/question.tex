Claudia and Mary Ann each choose a set of ten consecutive positive integers. They each compute the greatest common factor of each pair of distinct numbers in their set, so that each has a list of $45$ numbers. If $C$ is the sum of Claudia's $45$ greatest common factors, and $M$ is the sum of Mary Ann's $45$ greatest common factors, what is the greatest possible value of $C-M$?
