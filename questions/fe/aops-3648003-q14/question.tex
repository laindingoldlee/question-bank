Let $\vartriangle ABC$ have sides $AB = 3$, $BC = 5$, and $CA = 7$. Circle $\omega_1$ passes through point $B$ and is tangent to AC at point $A$, and circle $\omega_2$ passes through $B$ and is tangent to $AC$ at point $C$. Let $O_1$ and $O_2$ be the centers of $\omega_1$ and $\omega_2$ respectively. If the area of quadrilateral $AO_1O_2C$ can be expressed as $\dfrac{a}{b\sqrt{c}}$ where $a$ and $b$ are relatively prime positive integers and $c$ is square-free, find $a + b + c$.
