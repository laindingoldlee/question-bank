Subtract $A$ to obtain vectors $(4,1)$ and $(2,4)$. The triangle area is half the absolute determinant:
\[\dfrac{1}{2}|4\cdot4-1\cdot2|=\boxed7.\]
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
for(int i=0;i<=5;++i){draw((i,0)--(i,5),gray);draw((0,i)--(5,i),gray);}pair A=(.5,.5),B=(4.5,1.5),C=(2.5,4.5);filldraw(A--B--C--cycle,lightblue,blue);label("$A$",A,(-1,-1));label("$B$",B,(1,0));label("$C$",C,(0,1));
\end{asy}
