Let $p(x)=ax^2+bx+c$ with $a\ne0$ and discriminant $b^2-4ac=0$. Show that $p(x)=a(x-r)^2$ for $r=-\dfrac{b}{2a}$, and use this to explain how a quadratic with discriminant $0$ can have only one solution even though "every quadratic has two roots".
