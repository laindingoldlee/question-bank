Let $F=\underbrace{44\cdots4}_{94}$. Then the product is
\[
F\cdot\left(10^{94}-1\right)=F\cdot10^{94}-F .
\]
Subtracting $F$ from $F$ followed by $94$ zeros: the last $94$ digits become $10^{94}-F=\underbrace{55\cdots5}_{93}6$, with a borrow of $1$ from the upper half, which becomes $F-1=\underbrace{44\cdots4}_{93}3$. So
\[
\underbrace{99\cdots9}_{94}\times\underbrace{44\cdots4}_{94}=\underbrace{44\cdots4}_{93}3\,\underbrace{55\cdots5}_{93}6 .
\]
(For example $99\times44=4356$ and $999\times444=443556$.) The digit sum is
\[
93\cdot4+3+93\cdot5+6=93\cdot9+9=94\cdot9=\boxed{846}.
\]
The problem is about place value and digit sums, and the small cases show the pattern.
