Let $p(x)=x^3-4x^2+5x-2$. The coefficients add up to $1-4+5-2=0$, so $p(1)=0$ and $x-1$ is a factor (factor theorem). Dividing,
\[
p(x)=(x-1)\left(x^2-3x+2\right)=(x-1)(x-1)(x-2).
\]
Hence
\[
x^3-4x^2+5x-2=\boxed{(x-1)^2(x-2)} .
\]
Check: $(x^2-2x+1)(x-2)=x^3-2x^2-2x^2+4x+x-2=x^3-4x^2+5x-2$.
