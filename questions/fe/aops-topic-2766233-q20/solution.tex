Let $v_i$ be expected visits from state $i$, counting an initial visit if $i=0$. Then $v_{10}=0$, $v_0=1+v_1$, and $v_i=\dfrac{v_{i-1}+v_{i+1}}{2}$ for $1\le i\le9$. The interior recurrence makes $v_i$ linear; the boundary conditions give $v_i=10-i$. Hence $\boxed{v_3=7}$. Absorption has finite expectation because from any state there is a uniformly positive probability of reaching 10 within ten moves.
