An angle $\theta$ is chosen uniformly at random from the interval $0\le\theta\le\dfrac{\pi}{2}$. Given $\theta$, an event $A$ then happens with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{\theta}{2\pi}$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find $P(A)$.
\item[(b)] Explain why the shortcut ``replace $\theta$ by its average value $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$'' gives the right answer here, and show by an example that the shortcut fails when the conditional probability is not a linear function of $\theta$.
\end{enumerate}
