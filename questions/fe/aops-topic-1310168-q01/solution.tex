The profit is
\[
P(x)=x^2+100x-(240x+500)=x^2-140x-500 .
\]
We need $x^2-140x-500\ge10000$, i.e. $x^2-140x-10500\ge0$. The roots of $x^2-140x-10500=0$ are
\[
x=70\pm\sqrt{4900+10500}=70\pm\sqrt{15400}\approx70\pm124.1 ,
\]
so for positive $x$ the condition is $x\ge70+\sqrt{15400}\approx194.1$. Checking integers: $P(194)=9976<10000$ and $P(195)=10225\ge10000$. So the company must make and sell at least $\boxed{195}$ units.
