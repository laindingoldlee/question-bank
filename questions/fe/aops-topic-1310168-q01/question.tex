A company finds that when $x$ units of a product are made and sold, its revenue is $x^2+100x$ dollars and its cost is $240x+500$ dollars. What is the least number of units it must produce and sell to make a profit of at least $\$10{,}000$? (Profit $=$ revenue $-$ cost.)
