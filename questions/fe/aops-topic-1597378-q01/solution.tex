Let $E$ be the expected number of flips from the start (or just after a tail), and $E_1$ the expected number still needed just after a head that follows a tail or the start.
\begin{itemize}
\item From the start: one flip, then with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$ we are in state ``one head'' and with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$ back at the start: $E=1+\dfrac{1}{2}E_1+\dfrac{1}{2}E$.
\item After one head: one flip, then with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$ we are done and with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$ (a tail) back at the start: $E_1=1+\dfrac{1}{2}E$.
\end{itemize}
Substituting, $E=1+\dfrac{1}{2}\left(1+\dfrac{1}{2}E\right)+\dfrac{1}{2}E=\dfrac{3}{2}+\dfrac{3}{4}E$, so $\dfrac{1}{4}E=\dfrac{3}{2}$ and $E=\boxed{6}$.
