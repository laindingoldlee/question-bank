A circle with a radius of $2$ units has its centre at $(0,0)$. A circle with a radius of $7$ units has its centre at $(15,0)$. A line tangent to both circles intersects the $x$-axis at $(x,0)$ to the right of the origin. What is the value of $x$? Express your answer as a common fraction.
