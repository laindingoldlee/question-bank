$ABCDEF$ is a regular hexagon with side $1$. A circle of radius $r_1$ is inscribed in it. Then a regular hexagon is inscribed in this circle, and a circle of radius $r_2$ is inscribed in the second hexagon. Continuing in this way, alternating between hexagons and circles, find $r_{10}$, the radius of the tenth circle.
