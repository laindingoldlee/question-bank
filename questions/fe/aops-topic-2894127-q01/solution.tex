Pair the numbers so each pair sums to a square: $(1,8),(2,14),(3,13),(4,12),(5,11),(6,10),(7,9)$. Alice makes the $1$st, $3$rd, $\dots$, $11$th erasure and Bob the $2$nd, $\dots$, $12$th. Whenever Alice erases a number, Bob erases its partner. Then after each of Bob's moves only whole pairs remain, so the final two numbers are a pair, whose sum is a square. $\blacksquare$
