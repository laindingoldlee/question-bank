Let $x=\sqrt[3]{49}+\sqrt[3]{7}$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find a monic cubic polynomial with integer coefficients that has $x$ as a root.
\item[(b)] Find the other two roots of this polynomial. Is $\sqrt[3]{49}-\sqrt[3]{7}$ a root?
\end{enumerate}
