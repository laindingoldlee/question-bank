$10^2+24^2=676=26^2$, so the triangle has a right angle, and the hypotenuse is a diameter (Thales).
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(-13,0),B=(13,0),C=(-13+100/26,240/26);draw(circle((0,0),13));draw(A--B--C--cycle);dot((0,0));
label("$26$",(0,0),S);label("$10$",(A+C)/2,W);label("$24$",(B+C)/2,NE);label("$O$",(0,0),N);
\end{asy}

The radius is $13$ cm and the area is $\boxed{169\pi\text{ cm}^2}$.
