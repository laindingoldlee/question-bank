The $k$th term is $a_k=\dfrac{10^{k+1}-7}{3}$ (since $3a_k+7=10^{k+1}$). The first seven terms
\[
31,\ 331,\ 3331,\ 33331,\ 333331,\ 3333331,\ 33333331
\]
are all prime (this can be checked by trial division by the primes up to the square root, about $5774$ for the last one).

To find a factor of a later term, look for a small prime $p$ with $10^{k+1}\equiv7\pmod p$; then $p\mid3a_k$, and $p\mid a_k$ if $p\neq3$. For $p=17$ the powers of $10$ are
\[
10,\ 15,\ 14,\ 4,\ 6,\ 9,\ 5,\ 16,\ 7\pmod{17}
\]
for exponents $1$ to $9$. So $10^9\equiv7\pmod{17}$, and $17$ divides $a_8$:
\[
333333331=17\cdot19607843 .
\]
Hence the first term that is not prime is $\boxed{333333331}$ (eight threes followed by a one).
