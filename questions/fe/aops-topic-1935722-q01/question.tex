A student notices that $3+4+5$, $5+12+13$ and $20+21+29$ are all even, and conjectures that $a+b+c$ is even for every Pythagorean triple, that is, for all positive integers $a,b,c$ with $a^2+b^2=c^2$. Prove the conjecture.
