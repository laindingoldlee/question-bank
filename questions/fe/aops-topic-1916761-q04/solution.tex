The full sum is $\dfrac{150\cdot151}{2}=11325$. The multiples of $3$ up to $150$ sum to $3(1+\cdots+50)=3825$, the multiples of $7$ to $7(1+\cdots+21)=1617$, and the multiples of $21$ (counted twice) to $21(1+\cdots+7)=588$. By inclusion-exclusion the removed numbers sum to $3825+1617-588=4854$, and the result is $11325-4854=\boxed{6471}$.
