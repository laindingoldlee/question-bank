There are $8^3=512$ unit cubes and $6\cdot512$ equally likely cube-face outcomes. The large cube has $6\cdot8^2=384$ painted unit faces, so there are $3072-384=2688$ unpainted-bottom outcomes. The completely unpainted cubes form a $6^3=216$ interior block, each contributing six such outcomes. Thus the conditional probability is $\dfrac{216\cdot6}{2688}=\boxed{\dfrac{27}{56}}$.
