Let the bill counts be positive integers $a,b,c$. Dividing the total equation by ten gives $2a+5b+10c=80$, so $b$ is even. Write $b=2t$ with $t\ge1$. Then $a=40-5(t+c)>0$, so $t+c\le7$. For each $c=1,\ldots,6$, the number of positive $t$ is $7-c$. Their sum is $6+5+4+3+2+1=\boxed{21}$.
