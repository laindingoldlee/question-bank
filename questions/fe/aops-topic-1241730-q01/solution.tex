A sum of $k\ge2$ consecutive positive integers starting at $m$ is $\dfrac{k(2m+k-1)}{2}$; the factors $k$ and $2m+k-1$ have opposite parity and $2m+k-1>k$. So $N$ is such a sum iff $2N$ has an odd factor $>1$ paired appropriately, which happens iff $N$ has an odd divisor greater than $1$; the numbers that cannot be written are exactly the powers of $2$. Among the five numbers only $512=2^9$ is a power of $2$, so the answer is $\boxed{1}$.
