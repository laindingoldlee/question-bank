Ten brothers are to receive fractions $P_1>\cdots>P_{10}>0$ of an inheritance of $1023$ camels, with $P_1<\dfrac{1}{2}$. Their shares are not whole numbers. After one camel is borrowed, applying the same fractions to the enlarged herd leaves exactly two camels: one to return and one as a reward. Give a possible list of fractions.
