For the enlarged total $1024$, use allocations $511,256,128,64,32,16,8,4,2,1$. They are strictly decreasing, all positive, and sum to $1022$. Divide each by $1024$ to obtain $\boxed{(P_1,\ldots,P_{10})=\dfrac{1}{1024}(511,256,128,64,32,16,8,4,2,1)}$. The largest is below one-half, and the two leftover camels have the required uses. Each fraction has a nontrivial power of two as reduced denominator, so multiplying it by the odd original total $1023$ gives a noninteger share.
