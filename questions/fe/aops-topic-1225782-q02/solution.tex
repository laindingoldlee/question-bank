The quarter stays in the bag with probability $\dfrac{5}{9}$. It moves to the urn with probability $\dfrac{4}{9}$; the urn then has $16$ coins, and the quarter is among the $3$ drawn back with probability $\dfrac{3}{16}$. Total:
\[\dfrac{5}{9}+\dfrac{4}{9}\cdot\dfrac{3}{16}=\dfrac{5}{9}+\dfrac{1}{12}=\boxed{\dfrac{23}{36}}.\]
