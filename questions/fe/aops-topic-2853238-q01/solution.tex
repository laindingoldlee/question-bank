Let $n$ want neither, so $2n$ want both. Then $40=31+2n+n$, giving $n=3$ and $6$ wanting both. With $R$ and $G$ the numbers wanting each game, $R+G-2\cdot6=31$, so $R+G=43$; with $R-G=13$ this gives $R=\boxed{28}$.
