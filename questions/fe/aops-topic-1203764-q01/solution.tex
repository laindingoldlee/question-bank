Winning with four heads means you win right after the third tail is followed by $HHHH$, without having won earlier. Track the stages: before the first tail, the first flip is $H$ (win with $1$ head) or $T$. So reaching the "one tail" stage has probability $\dfrac12$.

In the stage with $t$ tails you win if the next $t+1$ flips are all heads; otherwise a tail appears first. The tail comes after $0,1,\dots,t$ heads, so the probability of moving to the next stage is $1-\dfrac1{2^{t+1}}$.

Probability of winning with exactly $4$ heads $=\dfrac12\cdot\left(1-\dfrac14\right)\left(1-\dfrac18\right)\cdot\dfrac1{16}=\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac34\cdot\dfrac78\cdot\dfrac1{16}=\boxed{\dfrac{21}{1024}}$.
