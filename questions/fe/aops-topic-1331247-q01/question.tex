Find the error in the following ``proof'' that $-1=1$.
\[
(-1)(-1)=1\cdot1\ \Rightarrow\ (-1)^2=1^2\ \Rightarrow\ \sqrt{(-1)^2}=\sqrt{1^2}\ \Rightarrow\ \bigl((-1)^2\bigr)^{\dfrac{1}{2}}=\bigl(1^2\bigr)^{\dfrac{1}{2}}\ \Rightarrow\ (-1)^{2\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}}=1^{2\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}}\ \Rightarrow\ -1=1 .
\]
