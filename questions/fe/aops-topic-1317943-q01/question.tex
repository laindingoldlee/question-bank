Jay chases a girl who is $100$ meters ahead of him. The girl runs at $3$ m/s and Jay at $5$ m/s. A dog runs back and forth between them at $10$ m/s, turning instantly whenever it reaches either of them. How many meters does the dog run before Jay catches the girl?
