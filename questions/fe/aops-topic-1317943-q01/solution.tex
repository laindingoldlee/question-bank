Jay gains $2$ m/s on the girl, so he catches her after $\dfrac{100}{2}=50$ s. The dog runs the whole time at $10$ m/s, so it covers $10\cdot50=\boxed{500\text{ m}}$. (There is no need to sum the infinitely many back-and-forth legs.)
