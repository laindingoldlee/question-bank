The two groups share $k-1$ people. When $k\ge2$, a person in their overlap can connect the two common names. But the first induction step is $k=1$: the two singleton groups have no person in common. Knowing that each singleton has one name gives no reason their names agree.

For example, a group consisting of Alice and Bob defeats this step. Thus the implication from the case $1$ to the case $2$ is unproved and false; the induction cannot begin.
