A student wants to find a closed form for sequences satisfying
\[a_n=3a_{n-1}a_{n-3}+a_{n-4}\qquad(n\ge5)\]
by the characteristic-polynomial method used for recurrences such as $a_n=k_1a_{n-1}+k_2a_{n-2}$, and writes the ``characteristic equation'' $c^4=3c^4+1$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that no geometric sequence $a_n=c^n$ with $c\ne0$ satisfies this recurrence for all $n\ge5$.
\item[(b)] Find two sequences satisfying the recurrence whose sum does not satisfy it.
\item[(c)] Explain why the method works for $a_n=k_1a_{n-1}+k_2a_{n-2}$ but not here.
\end{enumerate}
