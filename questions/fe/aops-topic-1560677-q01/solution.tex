\textbf{(a)} For $|r|<1$ the sum is $S=\dfrac{a}{1-r}$. Solving for $a$:
\[
a=S(1-r),\qquad\text{and the $n$-th term is } ar^{n-1}=S(1-r)r^{n-1}.
\]

\textbf{(b)} $a=12\left(1-\dfrac{1}{3}\right)=8$, so the series is $8+\dfrac{8}{3}+\dfrac{8}{9}+\cdots$, with first three terms $\boxed{8,\ \dfrac{8}{3},\ \dfrac{8}{9}}$. Check: $\dfrac{8}{1-\dfrac{1}{3}}=12$.
