An infinite geometric series has common ratio $r$ with $|r|<1$ and sum $S$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Express the first term $a$ and the $n$-th term in terms of $S$ and $r$.
\item[(b)] Find the first three terms of the infinite geometric series with sum $12$ and common ratio $\dfrac{1}{3}$.
\end{enumerate}
