Let $s_n$ count length-$n$ sequences with no two consecutive heads. Such a sequence ends in T (after any valid sequence of length $n-1$) or in TH (after one of length $n-2$), so $s_n=s_{n-1}+s_{n-2}$ with $s_1=2$, $s_2=3$. This gives $5,8,13,21,34,55,89,144$, so $s_{10}=144$, and the probability is $\dfrac{144}{1024}=\boxed{\dfrac9{64}}$.
