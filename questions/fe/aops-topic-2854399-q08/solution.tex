The $30$th ring comes after $29$ gaps, totalling $1+\dfrac32+\cdots+\left(\dfrac32\right)^{28}=\dfrac{\left(\dfrac32\right)^{29}-1}{\dfrac32-1}=2\left(\left(\dfrac32\right)^{29}-1\right)\approx255666.08$ hours. That is about $10652.75$ days, and $10652=7\cdot1521+5$, so it is $5$ days and about $18$ hours after the start of a Sunday: $\boxed{\text{Friday}}$ (around 6 p.m.).
