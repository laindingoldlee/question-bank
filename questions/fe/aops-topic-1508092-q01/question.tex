A standard $52$-card deck (no jokers) is shuffled and dealt out completely, $26$ cards to Joe and $26$ cards to Jeff. A player ``has a four-of-a-kind'' if his hand contains all four cards of some rank.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What is the probability that Joe has a four-of-a-kind?
\item[(b)] What is the probability that Jeff has a four-of-a-kind?
\item[(c)] What is the probability that both have a four-of-a-kind?
\item[(d)] What is the probability that neither has a four-of-a-kind (the game is drawn)?
\end{enumerate}
Give each answer as an exact expression and as a decimal to four places.
