The outer boundary of the swept region is the 5-by 6 rectangle with each unit corner square replaced by a quarter-disk. Its enclosed area is $30-4(1-\dfrac{\pi}{4})=26+\pi$. The unswept inner region is a 1-by 2 rectangle, because the centre path is inset another unit on each side. Subtracting gives $24+\pi$, hence $\boxed{a+b=25}$.
