Five piles initially contain $42,70,105,462,2009$ stones. One may replace a pair $(a,b)$ by $(a,a+b)$ or by $(a,|a-b|)$. One may also replace any $k$ chosen piles whose total is $kn$ by $k$ piles of $n$ stones. Empty piles are allowed and $1\le k\le5$. Can the five piles all be made to contain $2008$ stones? Can they all be made to contain $2009$?
