The probabilities of choosing $A$ and being cured, and of choosing $B$ and being cured, are $\dfrac{8}{25}\cdot\dfrac{3}{4}=\dfrac{12}{50}$ and $\dfrac{17}{25}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{17}{50}$. Hence the total cure probability is $\dfrac{29}{50}$. Conditioning on a cure yields $\dfrac{\dfrac{12}{50}}{\dfrac{29}{50}}=\boxed{\dfrac{12}{29}}$.
