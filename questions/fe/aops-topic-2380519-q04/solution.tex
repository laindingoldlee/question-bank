Since $2016=2^5\cdot3^2\cdot7=32\cdot63=16\cdot126$, look for $63$ and $126$ near powers:
\[\boxed{2016=2^5\times\left(4^3-1\right)}\qquad\text{and}\qquad\boxed{2016=2^4\times\left(5^3+1\right)}.\]
Check: $2^5=32$, $4^3-1=63$ and $32\cdot63=2016$; $2^4=16$, $5^3+1=126$ and $16\cdot126=2016$. Each expression uses each of $1,2,3,4,5$ exactly once.
