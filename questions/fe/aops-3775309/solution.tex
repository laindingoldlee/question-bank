If a unit segment is supplied, draw two rays from $O$. Mark $OU=1,OX=x$ on one and $OV=x$ on the other. Draw through $X$ the parallel to $UV$, meeting the second ray at $W$. Similar triangles give $\dfrac{O W}{O V} = \dfrac{O X}{O U} = x$, so $OW=x^2$. Without a specified unit, the request has no scale-independent meaning: dilating every input by a factor $c$ dilates every straightedge-and-compass construction by $c$, whereas squaring a numerical length would scale the desired output by $c^2$. Thus a unit or another reference length is essential.
