We need $10^{20}\le B^{17}<10^{21}$.

\begin{itemize}
\item $15^{17}<10^{20}$: dividing both sides by $5^{17}$, this says $3^{17}<2^{20}\cdot5^3=131072000$, which is true because $3^{17}=129140163$. So every $B\le15$ gives at most $20$ digits, and $B\ge16$.
\item $18^{17}>10^{21}$: we have $18^{17}=18\cdot324^8=18\cdot3.24^8\cdot10^{16}$, and $3.24^2=10.4976>10$, so $3.24^8>10^4$ and $18^{17}>18\cdot10^{20}>10^{21}$. So every $B\ge18$ gives at least $22$ digits, and $B\le17$.
\end{itemize}
Thus $B=16$ or $B=17$. The units digit of $17^{17}$ is that of $7^{17}$; the units digits of powers of $7$ cycle $7,9,3,1$, and $17\equiv1\pmod4$, so it is $7$. The units digit of $16^{17}$ is $6$ (every power of a number ending in $6$ ends in $6$). Hence $B=\boxed{16}$; indeed $16^{17}=2^{68}=295147905179352825856$ has $21$ digits.
