If $X\le14$, a two-digit multiple of 5 could only be 10, which is neither odd nor square, so at least two statements would fail. More directly, among $X\le14$, no value satisfies four of the conditions. Thus the false statement is $X\le14$. A two-digit odd square divisible by 5 must be $\boxed{25}$, and it indeed satisfies the other four statements.
