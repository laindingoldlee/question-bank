$13$ children sit around a table. Boys lie to girls and tell the truth to boys; girls lie to boys and tell the truth to girls. One child tells the child on the right "Most of us are boys"; that child tells the next "Most of us are girls"; and so on, alternating, until the $13$th child tells the first "Most of us are boys". How many boys are at the table?
