Write the palindrome as $\overline{abba}=1001a+110b$, with $1\le a\le9$ and $0\le b\le9$. Since $1001$ is divisible by $7$ and $110\equiv5$, divisibility requires $b\equiv0\pmod7$, giving $b=0$ or $7$. Each combines with nine values of $a$, yielding $\boxed{18}$.
