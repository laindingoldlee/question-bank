A draw is determined by the numbers $r,g,u\ge0$ of red, green and blue balls, with $r+g+u=6$; since each colour has at least $6$ balls, every such triple is possible. By stars and bars (arrange $6$ stars and $2$ bars in a row), the number of nonnegative solutions is
\[\dbinom{6+2}{2}=\dbinom82=\boxed{28}.\]
