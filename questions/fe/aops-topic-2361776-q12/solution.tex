The circumference is $2\pi r=8\pi$, so $r=4$, and the area is $\pi r^2=\boxed{16\pi}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(2.8cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0); draw(circle(O,4)); draw(O--4*dir(30),blue); dot(O); label("$O$",O,S); label("$r=4$",2*dir(30),NW,blue); label("circumference $8\pi$",(0,-4),S,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}
