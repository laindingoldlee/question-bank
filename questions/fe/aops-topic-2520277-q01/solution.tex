A $5$-digit palindrome has the form $\overline{abcba}$ with $a\in\{1,\dots,9\}$ and $b,c\in\{0,\dots,9\}$. Its number of even digits is
\[2\cdot[a\text{ even}]+2\cdot[b\text{ even}]+[c\text{ even}],\]
which is odd exactly when $c$ is even. So $a$ has $9$ choices, $b$ has $10$, and $c\in\{0,2,4,6,8\}$ has $5$, giving $9\cdot10\cdot5=\boxed{450}$ nice numbers.
