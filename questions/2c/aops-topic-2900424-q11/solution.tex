The sum is even, so $p=2$ and $q+r=2a-2<38$ is even with $q\ge3$. An even number with four divisors is $8$ or $2\cdot$(odd prime): $6,8,10,14,22,26,34$. We need $q+r$ with odd primes $3\le q<r$: $6=3+3$ fails, while $8=3+5$, $10=3+7$, $14=3+11$, $22=3+19$, $26=3+23$ and $34=3+31$ work. Then $a=\dfrac{q+r+2}2$ is $5,6,8,12,14,18$, with sum $\boxed{63}$.
