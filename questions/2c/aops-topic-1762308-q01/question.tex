An absent-minded cashier switched the dollars and the cents when he cashed a cheque for Mr.\ Truck, giving him dollars instead of cents and cents instead of dollars. On his way home Mr.\ Truck dropped a $5$-cent coin, and then he found that he had exactly twice the amount of the original cheque. What was the amount of the cheque?
