Every ordering of the four socks is equally likely, and by symmetry every sock is equally likely to be the fourth one removed. Two of the four socks are gray, so the probability is
\[
\dfrac{2}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}.
\]
Check by listing colour patterns: the $\dbinom42=6$ equally likely positions of the two gray socks are $\{1,2\},\{1,3\},\{1,4\},\{2,3\},\{2,4\},\{3,4\}$, and $3$ of them contain position $4$.

(More generally, with $2$ brown and $g$ gray socks the answer is $\dfrac{g}{g+2}$, whatever the position asked about.)
