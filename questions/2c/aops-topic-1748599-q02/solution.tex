With Melissa the record was $41$--$23$: $\dfrac{41}{64}=64.0625\%$. Without her the team went $(54-41)$--$(34-23)=13$--$11$: $\dfrac{13}{24}\approx54.1667\%$. The difference is about $9.896$ percentage points, so the answer is $\boxed{10}$.
