The Bobcats had a win-loss record of $41$--$23$ before their star player, Melissa, got injured. Melissa did not play in any more games, and the Bobcats finished the season with an overall win-loss record of $54$--$34$. What is the absolute difference, in percentage points, between the Bobcats' winning percentages with and without Melissa? Express your answer to the nearest whole number.
