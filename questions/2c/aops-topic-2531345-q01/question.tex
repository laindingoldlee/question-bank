Every day a dog arrives in the games room at a time chosen uniformly at random between 12:00 and 1:00, waits there for $20$ minutes, and then leaves. Independently, a person arrives at a uniformly random time between 12:00 and 1:00 with a game disc, and puts it away again after $20$ minutes. The dog gets to play if both are in the room at the same time. Assuming different days are independent, what is the probability that over two days the dog gets to play at least once?
