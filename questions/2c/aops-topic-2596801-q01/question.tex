A puzzle book claims: ``For every integer $x$, the sum of the digits of $9x$ is $9$.''
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that the claim is false.
\item[(b)] Prove the correct statement: for every positive integer $x$, the digit sum of $9x$ is a positive multiple of $9$; and if, starting from $9x$, we repeatedly replace a number by its digit sum, we eventually reach $9$.
\end{enumerate}
