Put $t=\sqrt x>0$. The expression is $t^{-4}+t$, whose derivative is $1-4t^{-5}$. It decreases until $t=4^{\dfrac{1}{5}}$ and increases afterward, so this is the global minimum. Its value is $4^{-\dfrac{4}{5}}+4^{\dfrac{1}{5}}=\dfrac{5}{4^{\dfrac{4}{5}}}=\dfrac{5}{2^{\dfrac{8}{5}}}$. Thus $(a,b,c,d)=(5,2,8,5)$ and the sum is $\boxed{20}$.
