By noon the freight train has travelled for $3$ hours, so it is $24\cdot3=72$ miles ahead. After that the passenger train gains on it at $60-24=36$ miles per hour, so it catches up after
\[
\dfrac{72}{36}=2\text{ hours},
\]
at 2 p.m. By then the passenger train has gone $60\cdot2=\boxed{120\text{ miles}}$ from Boston. (Check: the freight train has travelled $24\cdot5=120$ miles in $5$ hours.)
