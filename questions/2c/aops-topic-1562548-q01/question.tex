At 9 a.m.\ a freight train leaves Boston for New York, travelling at an average speed of $24$ miles per hour. At noon a passenger train leaves Boston on the same route, travelling at an average speed of $60$ miles per hour. How far from Boston does the passenger train pass the freight train?
