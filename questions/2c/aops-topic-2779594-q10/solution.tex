There are $6!$ assignments to fixed faces and 24 cube rotations. No nonidentity rotation fixes a coloring with all colors distinct, so the count is $\dfrac{6!}{24}=\boxed{30}$.
A cube has24 rotations: choose the face sent to the top in6 ways, then choose one of its4 adjacent faces to face forward. These choices determine the rotation.
