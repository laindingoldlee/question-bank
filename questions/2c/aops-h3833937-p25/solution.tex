Place $B=(0,0),C=(14,0),A=(9,12)$; these coordinates give the specified three side lengths. The altitudes are $x=9$ and $y=\dfrac{5}{12}x$, so $H=(9,\dfrac{15}{4})$. The circumcircle has equation $x^2+y^2-14x-\dfrac{33}{4}y=0$. Intersecting with $x=9$ gives $X=(9,-\dfrac{15}{4})$. Intersecting with $y=\dfrac{5}{12}x$ gives, besides $B$, $Y=(\dfrac{2511}{169},\dfrac{4185}{676})$. Therefore $XY=\sqrt{(\dfrac{990}{169})^2+(\dfrac{1680}{169})^2}=\dfrac{1950}{169}=\dfrac{150}{13}$, and $\boxed{a+b=163}$.

\end{asy}
