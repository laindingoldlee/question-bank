$A$ and $B$ travel around a circular track at uniform speeds in opposite directions, starting at the same time from diametrically opposite points. They meet for the first time after $B$ has travelled $100$ yards, and they meet a second time $60$ yards before $A$ completes one lap. What is the circumference of the track?
