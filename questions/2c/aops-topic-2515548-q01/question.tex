Let $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$, $f(x)=2\cos\left|x-\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right|$. The points $A=(-\pi,0)$ and $B=\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2},y\right)$ lie on the graph of $f$. Find the equation of the line $\ell$ through $A$ and $B$.
