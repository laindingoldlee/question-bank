A lorry left Town $M$ for Town $N$ at 7:30 a.m., travelling at a constant $65$ km/h for the whole journey. A van left Town $M$ for Town $N$ along the same route at 9:30 a.m., travelling at a constant $95$ km/h. The van overtook the lorry before either reached Town $N$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] At what time did the van pass the lorry?
\item[(b)] How far from Town $M$ had the lorry travelled when they passed each other?
\end{enumerate}
