\textbf{(a)} By 9:30 a.m. the lorry had driven for $2$ hours, so it was $2\cdot65=130$ km ahead of the van. After that the van gains on the lorry at $95-65=30$ km/h, so it closes the gap after
\[
\dfrac{130}{30}=\dfrac{13}{3}\text{ hours}=4\text{ hours }20\text{ minutes}.
\]
The van passed the lorry at 9:30 a.m. plus 4 h 20 min, that is, at $\boxed{\text{1:50 p.m.}}$

\textbf{(b)} By then the lorry had driven for $6$ hours $20$ minutes, $\dfrac{19}{3}$ hours, so it had covered
\[
65\cdot\dfrac{19}{3}=\dfrac{1235}{3}=\boxed{411\dfrac{2}{3}\text{ km}}\approx 411.7\text{ km}.
\]
Check: the van drove $\dfrac{13}{3}$ hours at $95$ km/h, also $\dfrac{1235}{3}$ km.
