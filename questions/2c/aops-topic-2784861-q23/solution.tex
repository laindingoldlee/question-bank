Two queries suffice, because the desired sum is $\dfrac{P(1)-P(-1)}{2}$. One query at any $ t $ cannot suffice: for any degree 2017 polynomial $ P $, the polynomial $ Q(x)=P(x)+(x-t)$ has the same value at $ t $ and still degree 2017, but its odd-coefficient sum is larger by 1. Thus indistinguishable answers exist after one query, and the minimum is $\boxed2 $.
