Yes. A polynomial is an expression $a_nx^n+\cdots+a_1x+a_0$ with constant coefficients and nonnegative integer exponents; $P(x)=5=5x^0$ is of this form, with $a_0=5$ and all other coefficients $0$.

The degree is the largest exponent with a nonzero coefficient. Here that is the exponent $0$ (the term $5x^0$), so $P$ has degree $\boxed{0}$, not $1$; a polynomial of degree $1$ would need a nonzero $x$ term, as in $5x$. (Every nonzero constant polynomial has degree $0$; the zero polynomial is usually given no degree, or degree $-\infty$.)
