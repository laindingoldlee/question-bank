One pound of meat with bones costs $\$7.80$. One pound of boneless meat costs $\$9.00$, and one pound of bones (without meat) costs $\$1.50$. Assuming the price of meat with bones is the price of its meat plus the price of its bones, what percentage of the weight of meat with bones is bone?
