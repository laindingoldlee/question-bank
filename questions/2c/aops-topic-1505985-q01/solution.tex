Let a pound of meat with bones consist of $w$ pounds of bone and $1-w$ pounds of meat. Then
\[
9(1-w)+1.5w=7.80\quad\Longrightarrow\quad9-7.5w=7.8\quad\Longrightarrow\quad w=\dfrac{1.2}{7.5}=0.16 .
\]
So $\boxed{16\%}$ of the weight is bone. (Check: $0.84\cdot9+0.16\cdot1.5=7.56+0.24=7.80$.)
