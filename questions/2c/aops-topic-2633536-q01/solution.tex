The set of $4$ empty spaces is equally likely to be any of the $\dbinom{16}4=1820$ sets. Auntie Em cannot park exactly when no two empty spaces are adjacent. To count these, place the $12$ cars in a row; they create $13$ gaps (including the two ends), and the $4$ empty spaces must go into $4$ different gaps: $\dbinom{13}4=715$ ways. So
\[P(\text{she can park})=1-\dfrac{715}{1820}=1-\dfrac{11}{28}=\boxed{\textbf{(E) }\dfrac{17}{28}}.\]
