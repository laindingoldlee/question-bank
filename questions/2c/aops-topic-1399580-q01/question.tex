A student says that a triangle whose vertices are lattice points (points with integer coordinates) has area $1223442.25$, and is puzzled that Pick's theorem then gives a non-integer number of interior lattice points. Prove that the area of every triangle whose vertices are lattice points is an integer multiple of $\dfrac{1}{2}$, and conclude that the student's area must be wrong.
