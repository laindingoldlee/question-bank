If $n$ ends in exactly $k$ nines, then $S(n+1)=S(n)-9k+1$. Both are multiples of $5$ only if $9k-1\equiv0\pmod5$, i.e. $k\equiv4\pmod5$. So $n$ ends in at least four nines: $n=\overline{m9999}$ with last non-nine part $m$ and $S(n)=S(m)+36\equiv0\pmod5$, i.e. $S(m)\equiv4\pmod5$. The smallest choice is $m=4$, giving $n=\boxed{49999}$ (digit sums $40$ and $5$).
