Factor: $x^2+8x+15=(x+3)(x+5)$.

\textbf{Divisible by $2$:} $x+3$ and $x+5$ have the same parity, so the product is even exactly when $x$ is odd.

\textbf{Divisible by $5$:} $5$ is prime, so $5$ must divide $x+3$ or $x+5$, i.e.\ $x\equiv2$ or $x\equiv0\pmod5$.

Combining, $x$ odd with $x\equiv0$ or $2\pmod 5$ means $x\equiv5$ or $x\equiv7\pmod{10}$. Each residue occurs $10$ times in $1,\dots,100$, so there are $\boxed{20}$ such integers.
