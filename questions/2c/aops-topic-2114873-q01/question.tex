Sam and Ted bake cookies, each working at a constant rate. Sam works $6$ times as fast as Ted, and together they can make $190$ cookies in $3$ hours. They start baking together, but after $130$ minutes Ted has to leave, and Sam continues alone until they have $1600$ cookies in total. For how many minutes does Sam work alone? Round your answer to the nearest minute.
