Together they make $190$ cookies in $180$ minutes, that is, $\dfrac{19}{18}$ cookies per minute. Sam does $\dfrac{6}{7}$ of this work, so Sam's rate is
\[\dfrac{6}{7}\cdot\dfrac{19}{18}=\dfrac{19}{21}\text{ cookies per minute}.\]
In the first $130$ minutes they make $130\cdot\dfrac{19}{18}=\dfrac{1235}{9}\approx137.2$ cookies, so $1600-\dfrac{1235}{9}=\dfrac{13165}{9}$ cookies remain. Sam alone needs
\[\dfrac{13165}{9}\div\dfrac{19}{21}=\dfrac{13165\cdot21}{9\cdot19}=\dfrac{92155}{57}\approx1616.75\]
minutes, which rounds to $\boxed{1617}$ minutes (about $27$ hours).
