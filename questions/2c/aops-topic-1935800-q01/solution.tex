Suppose $45=a+(a+1)+\cdots+(a+k-1)$ with $k\ge2$ terms and $a\ge1$. Then $\dfrac{k(2a+k-1)}{2}=45$, i.e.\ $k(2a+k-1)=90$. The two factors $k$ and $2a+k-1$ have opposite parity and $2a+k-1>k$. The factorizations of $90$ as $k\cdot m$ with $2\le k<m$ and $k,m$ of opposite parity are
\[
2\cdot45,\quad3\cdot30,\quad5\cdot18,\quad6\cdot15,\quad9\cdot10,
\]
giving $22+23$, $14+15+16$, $7+\cdots+11$, $5+\cdots+10$ and $1+\cdots+9$. There are $\boxed{5}$ ways.
