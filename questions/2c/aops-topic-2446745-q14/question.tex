Adam and Bob play a game with three fair six-sided dice. Adam rolls first: if the sum of his three numbers is a multiple of $3$, he wins. If not, Bob rolls the three dice: if his sum is a multiple of $5$, he wins. If neither has won, Adam rolls again, and so on, alternating until someone wins. What is the probability that Adam wins?
