Let $B, C,$ and $D$ be points in the plane such that $BC = 25$, $BD = 39$, and $CD = 40$. For a certain point $A$ on the segment $CD$, let $\omega_1$ be the circumcircle of $\triangle ABC$ and $\omega_2$ be the circumcircle of $\triangle ABD$. The tangent line to $\omega_1$ at $C$ and the tangent line to $\omega_2$ at $D$ intersect at a point $P$. Given that $P$ lies on the perpendicular bisector of segment $CD$, find the length of $PC$.
