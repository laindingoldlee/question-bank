No: $\boxed0$. Consecutive edge directions differ by 45 degrees. Rotate the first edge to horizontal and call the ordered lengths $a_0,\ldots,a_7$. Horizontal closure gives $(a_0-a_4)+\dfrac{a_1-a_3-a_5+a_7}{\sqrt2}=0$. Since all lengths are integers and $\sqrt2$ is irrational, $a_0=a_4$. These two opposite sides would have equal lengths, contradicting that all eight lengths differ.

usepackage("amsmath");

for(int k=0;k<8;++k)draw(3*dir(22.5+45*k)--3*dir(22.5+45*(k+1)));label("integer opposite horizontal sides must agree",(0,-3.7));
\end{asy}
