The first player starts by adding $ 8 $. Whenever the second adds $ a $, the first adds $ 12-a $, which lies in $ 1,\ldots,11 $. Thus after the first player's turns the totals are $ 8,20,32,44,56 $. The second player cannot reach the next such target in one move because it is $ 12 $ away. Therefore the first player can force a win.
