Subtract twice the second equation from the first: the cubes cancel and $x-2y=18$. Then $x-2y-8=10$, and the second equation gives $1000+y=1000$, so $y=0$ and $x=18$.

Check: $2\cdot10^3+18=2018$ and $10^3+0=1000$. The unique solution is $\boxed{(x,y)=(18,0)}$.
