$\lfloor\log_2m\rfloor$ increases only when $m$ reaches a power of $2$. Going from $2k$ to $2k+1$ never reaches a power of $2$ (for $k\ge1$, $2k+1$ is odd and greater than $1$). So each difference is $0$, and the answer is $\boxed{0}$.
