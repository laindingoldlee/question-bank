Bob has six cards. Three of them say ``distinguishable'' and can be told apart from one another (call them $D_1$, $D_2$, $D_3$); the other three say ``indistinguishable'' and look exactly alike. Bob draws cards one at a time, at random and without replacement. After his first two cards:
\begin{itemize}
\item if both are distinguishable cards, or both are indistinguishable cards, he draws $3$ more cards;
\item if he has one of each kind, he draws $2$ more cards.
\end{itemize}
A \emph{draw order} is the sequence of cards Bob draws, where $D_1$, $D_2$, $D_3$ are told apart but the indistinguishable cards are not. How many different draw orders are possible?
