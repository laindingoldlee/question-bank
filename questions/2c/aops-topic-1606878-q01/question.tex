For a positive integer $n$, let $\sigma(n)$ be the sum of the positive divisors of $n$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that the sum of the reciprocals of the positive divisors of $n$ is $\dfrac{\sigma(n)}{n}$.
\item[(b)] Find the sum of the reciprocals of the positive divisors of $1000$.
\item[(c)] Show that this sum equals $2$ exactly when $n$ is a perfect number (that is, $\sigma(n)=2n$), and check it for $n=28$.
\end{enumerate}
