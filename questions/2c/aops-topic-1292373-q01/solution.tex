The order of the cards does not matter: with $W$ wins and $L=90-W$ losses the final amount is $100\cdot1.5^W\cdot0.5^{90-W}=100\cdot\dfrac{3^W}{2^{90}}$. The $10$ undrawn cards decide $W$: if $j$ WIN cards are left out, $W=60-j$, with probability $\dfrac{\binom{60}{j}\binom{40}{10-j}}{\binom{100}{10}}$. So
\[
E=\dfrac{100}{2^{90}\binom{100}{10}}\sum_{j=0}^{10}\dbinom{60}{j}\dbinom{40}{10-j}3^{60-j}\approx\boxed{18.07\text{ dollars}}.
\]
(The expected money is far below $100$ because a $50$ percent gain followed by a $50$ percent loss leaves only $75$ percent.)
