A pile of $100$ face-down cards has $40$ marked LOSE and $60$ marked WIN. You draw $90$ cards at random without replacement. Each WIN card increases your money by $50$ percent and each LOSE card decreases it by $50$ percent. Starting with $100$ dollars, what is the expected amount of money at the end?
