The condition $x\equiv0\pmod 2$ follows from $x\equiv0\pmod4$. The condition $x\equiv4\pmod6$ means $x$ is even and $x\equiv1\pmod3$; both already follow from $x\equiv0\pmod4$ and $x\equiv1\pmod9$. So the system is equivalent to
\[x\equiv0\pmod4,\qquad x\equiv1\pmod9.\]
The numbers $\equiv1\pmod9$ are $1,10,19,28,\dots$, and the first multiple of $4$ among them is $28$. Since $\gcd(4,9)=1$, the solutions are $x\equiv28\pmod{36}$.

The birth year is $x-1$, between $2001$ and $2100$, so $2002\le x\le2101$. Since $2008=36\cdot55+28$, the possible values are $x=2008,2044,2080$ (the next one, $2116$, is too large). The birth year could be $\boxed{2007,\ 2043\text{ or }2079}$.
