If the solutions are $r$ and $s$, then by Vieta's formulas $rs=6$, so
\[
r^2s^2=(rs)^2=6^2=\boxed{36}.
\]
(The solutions $\dfrac{-13\pm\sqrt{145}}{2}$ are real, but this is not needed.)

Note: the answer $9$ would correspond to the equation $2x^2+13x+6=0$, where $rs=\dfrac{6}{2}=3$.
