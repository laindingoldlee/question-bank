To maximize a positive weighted sum with distinct digits, assign larger digits to larger weights; swapping a smaller digit on a larger weight strictly increases it. Use $A=9,M=8,T=7,I=6$, and $E,K=5,4$ in either order. The maximum is $99009+80008+7700+600+50+40=\boxed{187407}$.
