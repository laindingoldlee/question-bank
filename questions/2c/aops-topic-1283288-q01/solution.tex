Let $ABCD$ have long side $AB=33+12=45$ and $AD=29$. Put $A=(0,0)$, $B=(45,0)$, $D=(u,h)$ with $h>0$. The diagonals meet at the centre $O=\left(\dfrac{45+u}{2},\dfrac {h}{2}\right)$, whose foot on $AB$ is $P=\left(\dfrac{45+u}{2},0\right)$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s=0.2){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
pair A=(0,0),B=(45,0),Dp=(21,20),C=B+Dp; pair O=(A+C)/2, P=(O.x,0);
draw(A--B--C--Dp--cycle); draw(A--C,gray); draw(B--Dp,gray); draw(O--P,red); draw(ra(P,(1,0),(0,1),2)); dot(O);
label("$A$",A,SW); label("$B$",B,SE); label("$C$",C,NE); label("$D$",Dp,NW); label("$O$",O,N); label("$P$",P,S);
label("$33$",(A+P)/2,S); label("$12$",(P+B)/2,S); label("$29$",(A+Dp)/2,NW);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

With $AP=33$ we get $u=21$ (the other order, $AP=12$, gives $u=-21$ and the same answer). Then $h^2=29^2-21^2=400$, so $h=20$, and the area is $AB\cdot h=45\cdot20=\boxed{900\text{ cm}^2}$.
