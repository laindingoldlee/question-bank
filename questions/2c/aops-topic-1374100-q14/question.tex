In $\triangle ABC$, points $D$, $E$ and $F$ lie on $BC$, $AC$ and $AB$ respectively, and $AD$, $BE$, $CF$ meet at a common point $G$. If $AE:EC=2:5$ and $AF:FB=8:17$, then
\[
\dfrac{AG}{GD}\cdot\dfrac{BG}{GE}\cdot\dfrac{CG}{GF}=\dfrac{m}{n}
\]
for relatively prime positive integers $m$ and $n$. Find $m+n$.
