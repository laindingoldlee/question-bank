Let $ABC$ be a triangle with $\angle BAC=90^\circ$. Point $D$ lies on line $AC$ with $\angle DBC=90^\circ$, and $E$ lies on line $AB$ with $\angle ECB=90^\circ$. Point $Y$ lies on line $DE$ with $AY\perp BC$, and $X$ lies on line $BC$ with $AX\perp DE$. Prove that $XY=BC$.
