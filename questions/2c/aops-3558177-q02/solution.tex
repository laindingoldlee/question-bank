Only opposite faces may share a colour. Suppose exactly $e$ of the three opposite pairs are monochromatic. Then $6-e$ distinct colours are needed, and there are $\binom3e(5)_{6-e}$ colourings, where $(5)_k=5\cdot4\cdots(5-k+1)$ and is zero for $k>5$. The sum over $e=0,1,2,3$ is $0+360+360+60=780$. Thus the probability is $\dfrac{780}{5^6}=\dfrac{156}{3125}\approx\boxed{0.050}$.
