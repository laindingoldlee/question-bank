Writing $t=\sqrt x\ge0$, the inequality becomes $2t^2-4t+C=2(t-1)^2+C-2\ge0$. Its minimum occurs at $t=1$. Thus $C\ge2$ is necessary and sufficient, and the least constant is $\boxed{2}$, with equality at $\boxed{x=1}$.
