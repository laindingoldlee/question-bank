Order the cut positions as $0<X<Y<7$. The ordered pair is uniform on the triangle $0<X<Y<7$, of area $\dfrac{49}{2}$. The three pieces form a triangle exactly when each is shorter than $\dfrac{7}{2}$, that is $X<\dfrac{7}{2}$, $Y>\dfrac{7}{2}$, and $Y-X<\dfrac{7}{2}$. These inequalities cut out a triangle with legs $\dfrac{7}{2}$ and area $\dfrac{49}{8}$. The probability is therefore $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{4}}$. Boundary equality has probability zero.

\end{asy}
