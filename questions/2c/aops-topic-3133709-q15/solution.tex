Both are 3--4--5 right triangles, with inradius $\dfrac{3+4-5}{2}=1$. Using $A=(0,0),B=(3,0),C=(3,4),D=(0,4)$, their incenters are $(2,1)$ and $(2,3)$. The distance is $\boxed2$.

\end{asy}
