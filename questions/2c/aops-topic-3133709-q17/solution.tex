The diagonal $AC=2\sqrt2$ is a diameter, so the radius is $\sqrt2$ and semicircle area is $\pi$. The square’s intersection with that half-plane is triangle $ABC$, area 2, entirely inside the semicircle because the disk is convex and contains its three vertices. Hence the requested area is $\boxed{\pi-2}$.

\end{asy}
