Take $E=(0,0)$, $A=(-12,0)$, $B=(12,0)$, and $C=(u,v)$ with $v>0$. Then $u^2+v^2=729$ and $D=\left(\dfrac{u+12}{2},\dfrac{v}{2}\right)$. The median condition gives
\[
\left(\dfrac{u}{2}+18\right)^2+\dfrac{v^2}{4}=324,
\]
so $u=-\dfrac{81}{8}$ and $v=\dfrac{27\sqrt{55}}{8}$.

Intersecting chords through $E$ give $EC\cdot EF=EA\cdot EB=144$. Since $EC=27$, $EF=\dfrac{16}{3}$; $F$ lies on the opposite ray from $C$. Hence the height of $F$ below $AB$ is
\[
\dfrac{EF}{EC}v=\dfrac{16}{81}\cdot\dfrac{27\sqrt{55}}{8}=\dfrac{2\sqrt{55}}{3}.
\]
Thus $[AFB]=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot24\cdot\dfrac{2\sqrt{55}}{3}=\boxed{8\sqrt{55}}$.

\end{asy}
