Divide by $x^2+2x-3$:
\[
x^4+7x^3+8x^2-13x+15=\left(x^2+2x-3\right)\left(x^2+5x+1\right)+18 .
\]
(Check: $\left(x^2+2x-3\right)\left(x^2+5x+1\right)=x^4+7x^3+8x^2-13x-3$.) Since $x^2+2x-3=0$, the value is $\boxed{18}$. (Equivalently, $x=1$ or $x=-3$, and both give $18$.)
