A problem asks to write $\sqrt{54+\sqrt{180}}$ in the form $c+\sqrt d$ with positive integers $c$ and $d$, and to find $c+d$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that no positive integers $c$ and $d$ satisfy $\sqrt{54+\sqrt{180}}=c+\sqrt d$.
\item[(b)] Find the positive integers $c$ and $d$ with $\sqrt{14+\sqrt{180}}=c+\sqrt d$, and find $c+d$.
\end{enumerate}
