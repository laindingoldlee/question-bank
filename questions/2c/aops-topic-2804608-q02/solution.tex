Let there be $n\ge2$ towns. Each town is connected to between $0$ and $n-1$ others. Values $0$ and $n-1$ cannot both occur (a town connected to all others rules out an isolated town). So the $n$ degrees take at most $n-1$ values, and by the pigeonhole principle two coincide. $\blacksquare$
