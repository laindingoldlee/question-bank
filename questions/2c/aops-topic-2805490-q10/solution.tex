The inscribed angle $O$ is 75 degrees, so $N=105^\circ-M$. The sine rule gives $\sin N=2\sin M$. Expanding yields $\sin105^\circ\cos M=(2+\cos105^\circ)\sin M$. Thus $\tan M=\dfrac{\sin105^\circ}{2+\cos105^\circ}$, giving $M\approx29.0^\circ$, hence $\boxed{29^\circ}$. The range $0<M<105^\circ$ and positive tangent select the acute solution.

\end{asy}
