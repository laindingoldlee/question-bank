In the \emph{arithmetic game}, a positive integer $n$ is given, and the player chooses how many copies of the polynomial $n$ to start with. A move takes two of the current polynomials (in the variable $n$) and replaces them by their sum, difference, product or quotient, provided the result is again a polynomial in $n$; a difference or quotient of two polynomials of the \emph{same degree} is not allowed. The player wins by ending with the single polynomial $6$ (the constant), with nothing left over. Prove that the player can always win.
