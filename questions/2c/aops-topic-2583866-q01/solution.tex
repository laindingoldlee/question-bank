If $D=k^2$, its positive square root is the integer $k$. Conversely suppose $\sqrt D=\dfrac{a}{b}$ with coprime positive integers $a,b$. Then $a^2=Db^2$. Any prime dividing $b$ would divide $a^2$ and hence $a$, contradicting coprimality. Thus $b=1$ and $D=a^2$.
