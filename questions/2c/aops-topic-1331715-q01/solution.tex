Per hour, Al does $\dfrac{1}{6}$ of the job, Bob $\dfrac{1}{4}$ and Chuck $\dfrac{1}{3}$.
\begin{itemize}
\item Noon to 1:00: Al alone does $\dfrac{1}{6}=\dfrac{4}{24}$.
\item 1:00 to 1:30: Al and Bob do $\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{1}{6}+\dfrac{1}{4}\right)=\dfrac{5}{24}$.
\end{itemize}
By 1:30 PM, $\dfrac{9}{24}=\dfrac{3}{8}$ of the job is done and $\dfrac{5}{8}$ remains. All three together work at $\dfrac{1}{6}+\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{3}=\dfrac{3}{4}$ job per hour, so they need $\dfrac{5}{8}\div\dfrac{3}{4}=\dfrac{5}{6}$ hour $=50$ minutes. They finish at $\boxed{\text{2:20 PM}}$.
