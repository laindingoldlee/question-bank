Put square $1$ at the origin with unit squares at integer points. The $k$-th layer is the ring of squares at ``distance'' $k-1$ (all squares $(x,y)$ with $\max(|x|,|y|)=k-1$), which contains $(2k-1)^2-(2k-3)^2=8(k-1)$ squares. The first $k$ layers fill a $(2k-1)\times(2k-1)$ square, so the $k$-th layer ends with $(2k-1)^2$.

From the figure, each layer ends at the upper-left corner $(-(k-1),\,k-1)$ (for example $9$ at $(-1,1)$), the next layer starts directly to its left, runs down the left side, then right along the bottom, up the right side, and left along the top.

\textbf{(a)} The third layer ends with $5^2=\boxed{25}$.

\textbf{(b)} The fourth layer runs from $26$ to $49$ and lies at distance $3$. It starts at $26=(-3,2)$, then goes down the left side: $27=(-3,1)$, $28=(-3,0)$, $29=(-3,-1)$, $30=(-3,-2)$, $31=(-3,-3)$, and then right along the bottom: $32=(-2,-3)$, $33=(-1,-3)$. So the triangle for $33$ has horizontal side $\boxed{1\text{ cm}}$ and vertical side $\boxed{3\text{ cm}}$.

\textbf{(c)} Since $44^2<2019<45^2=2025$ and $45=2\cdot23-1$, square $2019$ is in layer $23$, at distance $22$. That layer ends with $2025$ at the upper-left corner $(-22,22)$, reached by moving left along the top row $y=22$. Going back $6$ steps from $2025$ along the top row, $2019$ is at $(-22+6,22)=(-16,22)$ (the top row from $(22,22)$ to $(-22,22)$ has $45$ squares, so this is still on it). The triangle for $2019$ has horizontal side $\boxed{16\text{ cm}}$ and vertical side $\boxed{22\text{ cm}}$.
