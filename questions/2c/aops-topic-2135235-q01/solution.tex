Write the numbers as $31x$ and $31y$ with $\gcd(x,y)=1$. Their least common multiple is then $31xy$, so $31xy=217$ and $xy=7$. With $x$ and $y$ coprime positive integers, $\{x,y\}=\{1,7\}$, so the numbers are $\boxed{31\text{ and }217}$. (Check: $\gcd(31,217)=31$ and $\operatorname{lcm}(31,217)=217$, since $217=7\cdot31$.)
