MERRYCHRISTMAS has $14$ letters: R appears $3$ times, M twice, S twice, and E, Y, C, H, I, T, A once each ($3+2+2+7=14$). The number of arrangements is
\[
\dfrac{14!}{3!\,2!\,2!}=\dfrac{87178291200}{24}=\boxed{3632428800}.
\]
