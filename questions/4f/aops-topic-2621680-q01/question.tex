Let $p$ be a prime and let $a$ be an integer not divisible by $p$. Note that $a$ may be smaller or larger than $p$, or negative. Prove that $a^{p-1}\equiv1\pmod p$, and explain why the condition $p\nmid a$ cannot be dropped.
