The perpendicular vectors $(a-2,a-4)$ and $(a+8,2a+5)$ have zero dot product: $(a-2)(a+8)+(a-4)(2a+5)=3(a+4)(a-3)=0 $. Thus $ a=-4 $. The perpendicular legs are then $ PQ=\sqrt{(-6)^2+(-8)^2}=10 $ and $ PR=\sqrt{4^2+(-3)^2}=5 $. The area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot10\cdot5=\boxed{25}$.
