A number is divisible by $5$, $7$, and $72=8\cdot9$ exactly when it is divisible by $\operatorname{lcm}(5,7,72)=2520$. The eight-digit number lies between $19720000$ and $19729999$. Since
\[
2520\cdot7825=19719000<19720000\le 2520\cdot7826=19721520,
\]
and $2520\cdot7830=19731600>19729999$, the multiples of $2520$ in the range are
\[
2520\cdot7826,\ 2520\cdot7827,\ 2520\cdot7828,\ 2520\cdot7829,
\]
that is,
\[\boxed{19721520,\ 19724040,\ 19726560,\ 19729080}.\]
