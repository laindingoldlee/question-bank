Let $P_1,P_2,\dots,P_n$ be the vertices of a polygon, listed in order around its boundary, with $P_i=(x_i,y_i)$, and put $P_{n+1}=P_1$. The shoelace sum is
\[
S\left(P_1,P_2,\dots,P_n\right)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(x_iy_{i+1}-x_{i+1}y_i\right),
\]
and the shoelace formula says that the area of the polygon is $\dfrac{1}{2}|S|$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that starting the list at a different vertex does not change $S$.
\item[(b)] Prove that listing the vertices in the opposite direction changes $S$ into $-S$.
\item[(c)] Compute $S$ for the triangle with vertices $(0,0)$, $(4,0)$, $(1,3)$ listed counterclockwise and listed clockwise, and find its area.
\end{enumerate}
