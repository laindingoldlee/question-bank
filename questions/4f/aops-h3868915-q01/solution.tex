Choose $2n$ distinct primes $p_1,\ldots,p_{2n}$. The moduli $p_{2i-1}p_{2i}$ are pairwise coprime. The Chinese remainder theorem gives an integer $X$ with $X\equiv-i\pmod{p_{2i-1}p_{2i}}$ for each $1\le i\le n$. Adding a sufficiently large multiple of the product of these moduli makes $X$ positive. Each $X+i$ is divisible by two distinct primes, so cannot be a prime power. These are the required consecutive positive integers.
