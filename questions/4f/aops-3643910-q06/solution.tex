Pad numbers below 1000 to three digits. All units-five occurrences are erased. A five in either the tens or hundreds position survives exactly when the units digit is one of $1,2,3,4,6,7,8,9$. The other free digit has ten choices. Thus each of those two positions contributes 80, for a total $\boxed{160}$. The number 1000 contributes none.
