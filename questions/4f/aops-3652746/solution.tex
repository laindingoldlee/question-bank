Deterministic dry weather and deterministic rainy weather are different admissible models; an unspecified travel speed can also make the deadline feasible or impossible regardless of harvest. Replacing random quantities by expected values does not in general give the probability of exceeding a cost or meeting a deadline. Thus the requested probability is underdetermined.
