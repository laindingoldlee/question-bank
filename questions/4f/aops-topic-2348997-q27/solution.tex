$31.25\left(1-\dfrac x{100}\right)^2=20$, so $\left(1-\dfrac x{100}\right)^2=\dfrac{20}{31.25}=0.64$. Since $1-\dfrac{x}{100}>0$, it equals $0.8$, and $x=\boxed{20}$.
