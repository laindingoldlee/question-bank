The discriminant is $4a^2-4b$, so we need $a^2>b$. For each $a$, count $b\ne a$ with $b<a^2$: $a=0$: none; $a=1$: $b=0$ ($1$); $a=2$: $b\in\{0,1,3\}$ ($3$); $a=3$: $b\in\{0,\dots,8\}\setminus\{3\}$ ($8$); $a\ge4$: every $b\ne a$ works ($9$ each, $6\cdot9=54$). Total $1+3+8+54=66$ out of $90$ ordered pairs. The probability is $\dfrac{66}{90}=\boxed{\dfrac{11}{15}}$.
