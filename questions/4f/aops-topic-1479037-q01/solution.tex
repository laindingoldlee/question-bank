Let $B$, $S$ and $G$ be the total masses of the black, silver and golden weights. All the weights together weigh $1+2+\cdots+17=153$ grams, and $S=B+32$.

The ten black weights weigh at least $1+2+\cdots+10=55$ grams, so $S=B+32\ge87$. But six weights weigh at most $12+13+\cdots+17=87$ grams. Hence $S=87$ exactly, so the silver weights are $12,13,\dots,17$, and $B=55$, so the black weights are $1,2,\dots,10$. The only weight left is
\[
G=153-87-55=\boxed{11}\text{ grams}.
\]
