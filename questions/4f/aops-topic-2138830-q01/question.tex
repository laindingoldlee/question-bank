Albert, Bernard and Cheryl became friends with Denise, and they wanted to know when her birthday is. Denise gave them a list of $20$ possible dates:
\[\begin{array}{llll}
\text{17 Feb 2001} & \text{16 Mar 2002} & \text{13 Jan 2003} & \text{19 Jan 2004}\\
\text{13 Mar 2001} & \text{15 Apr 2002} & \text{16 Feb 2003} & \text{18 Feb 2004}\\
\text{13 Apr 2001} & \text{14 May 2002} & \text{14 Mar 2003} & \text{19 May 2004}\\
\text{15 May 2001} & \text{12 Jun 2002} & \text{11 Apr 2003} & \text{14 Jul 2004}\\
\text{17 Jun 2001} & \text{16 Aug 2002} & \text{16 Jul 2003} & \text{18 Aug 2004}
\end{array}\]
Denise then told Albert, Bernard and Cheryl separately the month, the day and the year of her birthday, respectively. The following conversation ensues; everyone hears every statement, and each sentence takes into account everything said before it.
\begin{itemize}
\item Albert: I don't know when Denise's birthday is, but I know that Bernard does not know.
\item Bernard: I still don't know when Denise's birthday is, but I know that Cheryl still does not know.
\item Cheryl: I still don't know when Denise's birthday is, but I know that Albert still does not know.
\item Albert: Now I know when Denise's birthday is.
\item Bernard: Now I know too.
\item Cheryl: Me too.
\end{itemize}
When is Denise's birthday?
