Albert knows the month, Bernard the day and Cheryl the year. We remove dates step by step.

\textbf{Albert's first statement.} Every month appears at least twice, so Albert cannot know. In the full list the days $12$ (12 Jun 2002) and $11$ (11 Apr 2003) appear only once, so Bernard would know if the day were $12$ or $11$. Albert is sure Bernard does not know, so the month is neither June nor April. Fifteen dates remain:
\[\begin{array}{ll}
\text{Jan: } 13\ (2003),\ 19\ (2004) & \text{Feb: } 17\ (2001),\ 16\ (2003),\ 18\ (2004)\\
\text{Mar: } 13\ (2001),\ 16\ (2002),\ 14\ (2003) & \text{May: } 15\ (2001),\ 14\ (2002),\ 19\ (2004)\\
\text{Jul: } 16\ (2003),\ 14\ (2004) & \text{Aug: } 16\ (2002),\ 18\ (2004)
\end{array}\]

\textbf{Bernard's statement.} ``I still don't know'': among the fifteen dates the days $15$ and $17$ appear once, so the day is not $15$ or $17$; this removes 15 May 2001 and 17 Feb 2001. Now the year $2001$ appears only once (13 Mar 2001), so Cheryl would know if the year were $2001$. Bernard is sure she does not, so his day is not $13$; this removes 13 Mar 2001 and 13 Jan 2003. Eleven dates remain:
\[\begin{array}{l}
2002\text{: 16 Mar, 14 May, 16 Aug}\\
2003\text{: 16 Feb, 14 Mar, 16 Jul}\\
2004\text{: 19 Jan, 18 Feb, 19 May, 14 Jul, 18 Aug}
\end{array}\]

\textbf{Cheryl's statement.} Every year still appears at least three times, so she does not know. Among the eleven dates January appears only once (19 Jan 2004), so Albert would know if the month were January. Cheryl is sure he does not, so the year is not $2004$. Six dates remain: 16 Mar 2002, 14 May 2002, 16 Aug 2002, 16 Feb 2003, 14 Mar 2003, 16 Jul 2003.

\textbf{Albert now knows.} His month must appear once among these six. March appears twice, so the month is May, August, February or July: 14 May 2002, 16 Aug 2002, 16 Feb 2003, 16 Jul 2003.

\textbf{Bernard now knows.} His day must appear once among these four. The day $16$ appears three times, so the day is $14$, and the date is 14 May 2002.

\textbf{Cheryl now knows too}, consistently. Denise's birthday is $\boxed{\text{14 May 2002}}$.
