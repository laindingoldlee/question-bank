Let $AB$ be a chord of a circle with centre $O$ that is not a diameter, and let $M$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $O$ to line $AB$ (the segment $OM$ is the apothem of the chord). Prove that $M$ is the midpoint of $AB$, so that line $OM$ is the perpendicular bisector of $AB$.
