The gcd is $\gcd(z,24)$. If $x=1$, then $y=1$ and $z=2$, but $\gcd(2,24)=2$, a contradiction. For $x\ge2$, the geometric series gives $y=\dfrac{1}{x-1}$. Hence $z=\lfloor x-1+\dfrac{1}{x-1}\rfloor$. For $x=2$, this gives $y=1,z=2$, which works. For $x\ge3$, it gives $z=x-1$, but $x=\gcd(x-1,24)$ would require $x\mid x-1$, impossible. Thus $\boxed{(x,y,z)=(2,1,2)}$.
