Find the positive integer $x$, positive real $y$, and positive integer $z$ satisfying all three conditions: $x=\gcd(z,7z+24)$; if $x=1$ then $y=1$, otherwise $y=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}x^{-k}$; and $z=\left\lfloor\dfrac{y^2+1}{y}\right\rfloor$.
