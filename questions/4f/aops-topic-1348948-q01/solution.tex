Let Sheri be $s$ years old and Terry $t$ years old now. The two conditions are
\[
s+20=t,\qquad t+10=2(s+10).
\]
Substituting the first into the second: $s+30=2s+20$, so $s=10$ and $t=30$.

Check: in $20$ years Sheri will be $30$, Terry's present age; in $10$ years Terry will be $40=2\cdot20$, twice Sheri's age then. So Sheri is $\boxed{10}$ and Terry is $\boxed{30}$. A negative age such as $-60$ comes from setting up the second equation as $t+10=2s+10$ or similar; both ages must be shifted by $10$ years.
