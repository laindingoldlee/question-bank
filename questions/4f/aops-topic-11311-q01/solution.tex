If the price is raised by $x$ cents, he sells $400-16x$ peaches at $15+x$ cents, for an income of
\[
I(x)=(15+x)(400-16x)=-16x^2+160x+6000=-16(x-5)^2+6400 .
\]
This is largest at $x=5$, so the best price is $\boxed{20\text{ cents}}$, giving $320$ peaches and an income of $6400$ cents ($\$64$) per day.
