A merchant has determined that if the selling price of peaches is $15$ cents each, he will sell $400$ of them each day. He has also determined that for each cent the price is increased, he will sell $16$ fewer peaches each day. At what price, in cents, should he sell each peach to maximise his daily income from peaches?
