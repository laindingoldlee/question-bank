$(5x-1)^2=0$ forces $5x-1=0$; both signs in $\pm(5x-1)=0$ give the same equation. The only solution is the double root $\boxed{x=\dfrac15}$. Check: $x=-\dfrac15$ gives $1+2+1=4\ne0$.
