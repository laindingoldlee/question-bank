An equilateral triangle $ABC$ has side length $2$. A square $PQRS$ (vertices in this order) has $P$ on $AB$, $Q$ on $BC$, and $R$ and $S$ on $AC$. The points move so that $P$, $Q$, $R$ always remain on the sides $AB$, $BC$, $CA$ respectively and $PQRS$ is always a square, while $S$ moves from $AC$ to $AB$ through the interior of the triangle. Show that $S$ moves along a segment parallel to $BC$, and find its distance from $BC$.
