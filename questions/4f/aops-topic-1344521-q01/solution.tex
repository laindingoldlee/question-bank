Let the small stable's ponies (including Rein Beau) be worth $S$ in total and the large stable's $L$, in dollars. After the move, the small stable has $2$ ponies worth $S-250000$ and the large stable $4$ ponies worth $L+250000$:
\[
\dfrac{S-250000}{2}=\dfrac {S}{3}+10000\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 3S-750000=2S+60000\quad\Longrightarrow\quad S=810000,
\]
\[
\dfrac{L+250000}{4}=\dfrac {L}{3}+10000\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 3L+750000=4L+120000\quad\Longrightarrow\quad L=630000 .
\]
The total is $810000+630000=\boxed{\$1{,}440{,}000}$.
