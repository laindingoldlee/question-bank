Mr Gallop has two stables, each of which initially houses three ponies. His prize pony, Rein Beau, worth $\$250{,}000$, usually spends the day in the small stable. One day it wanders into the large stable, and Mr Gallop finds that the average value of the ponies in each stable has risen by $\$10{,}000$. What is the total value of all six ponies?
