A solid papaya has the shape of an ellipsoid with semi-axes $\dfrac{3}{\pi}$, $4$ and $5$ inches. It is to be reshaped into a hollow cubical box whose walls are $1$ inch thick (on all six faces), using exactly the same volume of material as the ellipsoid. What is the outer edge length of the box, in inches?
