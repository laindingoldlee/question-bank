Try each suspect as the criminal.
\begin{itemize}
\item Alice: the criminal lies, so ``Bob is lying'' is false and Bob tells the truth; but then Chad did it, a contradiction.
\item Bob: Bob lies, while Chad (``I didn't''), Alice (``Bob is lying'') and Dan (``Bob did it'') all tell the truth: only one liar, not two.
\item Chad: Chad lies, Bob tells the truth, and Alice and Dan lie: three liars.
\item Dan: Dan lies, Bob (``It was Chad'') lies, Chad tells the truth, and Alice (``Bob is lying'') tells the truth: exactly two liars, including the criminal.
\end{itemize}
Only Dan works: $\boxed{\textbf{(D) }\text{Dan}}$.
