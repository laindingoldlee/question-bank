Detective Holmes interviews four suspects. Exactly two of them lie and two tell the truth. There is exactly one criminal, and the criminal lies.
\begin{itemize}
\item Bob: ``It was Chad!''
\item Chad: ``I didn't do it.''
\item Alice: ``Bob is lying.''
\item Dan: ``Then Bob did it.''
\end{itemize}
Who is the criminal?
