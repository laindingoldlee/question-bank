The second ray makes an acute angle $180^\circ-145^\circ=35^\circ$ with a horizontal line. Since the rays are on opposite sides of the leftward horizontal ray, their angle is $40^\circ+35^\circ=\boxed{75^\circ}$.

\end{asy}
