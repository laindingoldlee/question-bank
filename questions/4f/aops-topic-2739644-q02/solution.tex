Expanding: $(x^3+6x^2+11x+6)+(x^3+15x^2+74x+120)=2x^3+21x^2+85x+126$. Try $x=-\dfrac72$: $2\left(-\dfrac{343}8\right)+21\cdot\dfrac{49}4-\dfrac{595}2+126=-\dfrac{343}4+\dfrac{1029}4-\dfrac{1190}4+\dfrac{504}4=0$. Dividing by $2x+7$ gives $x^2+7x+18$, whose discriminant $49-72<0$. So the factorisation is $\boxed{(2x+7)(x^2+7x+18)}$.
