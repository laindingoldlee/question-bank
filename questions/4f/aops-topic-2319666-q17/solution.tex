By linearity of expectation, the answer is $8$ times the probability that a fixed target is hit. The archers hit this target with probabilities $\dfrac{0.65}{8}$, $\dfrac{0.55}{8}$ and $\dfrac18$ (choose it, then hit it), independently, so it is missed by all three with probability
\[\left(1-\dfrac{0.65}{8}\right)\left(1-\dfrac{0.55}{8}\right)\left(1-\dfrac18\right)=\dfrac{147}{160}\cdot\dfrac{149}{160}\cdot\dfrac78=\dfrac{153321}{204800}.\]
The expected number of targets hit is
\[8\left(1-\dfrac{153321}{204800}\right)=\dfrac{8\cdot51479}{204800}=\boxed{\dfrac{51479}{25600}}\approx2.011.\]
