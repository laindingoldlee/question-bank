The vowels are $A,A,E$ and the consonants are $H,R,M,B$. Arrange the four distinct consonants in $4!=24$ ways; they create $5$ gaps (including the two ends). The three vowels must go into three different gaps: $\dbinom53=10$ choices, and the vowels $A,A,E$ can be ordered in $\dfrac{3!}{2!}=3$ ways. The total is
\[
24\cdot10\cdot3=\boxed{720}.
\]
