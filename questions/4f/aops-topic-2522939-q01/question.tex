Leo guesses a real number chosen uniformly at random between $1$ and $2$. The correct answer is a real number chosen uniformly at random between $1$ and $3$, independently of his guess. His answer is marked correct if it is within $10\%$ of the correct answer. What is the probability that he is marked correct?
