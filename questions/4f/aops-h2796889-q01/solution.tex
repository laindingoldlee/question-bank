The prime 2 cannot belong to such a progression: if $2,p,q$ were the increasing terms, $q=2p-2$ would be an even prime greater than 2, impossible. Thus each side is at least the corresponding member of $3,5,7$. These primes are in arithmetic progression and satisfy the triangle inequality, so they give the least perimeter $L=15$. Opposite the side 7, the law of cosines gives
\[\cos a^\circ=\dfrac{3^2+5^2-7^2}{2\cdot3\cdot5}=-\dfrac{1}{2}.\]
Hence $a=120$ and $\dfrac{a}{L}=\boxed8$.
