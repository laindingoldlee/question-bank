The first choice has 8 possibilities. Removed slices always form one contiguous circular block. Until only one slice remains, each new person may choose either end of the remaining block. There are six such two-way choices, followed by the forced final slice. Thus $8\cdot2^6=\boxed{512}$.

\end{asy}
