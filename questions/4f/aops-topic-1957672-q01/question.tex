In an election in which every voter voted for one of two candidates, the votes went to the two candidates in the ratio $8:5$. A total of $3{,}109{,}600$ people voted. How many votes did the first candidate (the one with the larger share) receive? Also explain what is wrong with the proportion $\dfrac{5}{8}=\dfrac{x}{3{,}109{,}600}$ for the number $x$ of the second candidate's votes.
