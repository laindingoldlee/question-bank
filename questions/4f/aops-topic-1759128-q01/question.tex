Squidward's clarinet is a solid right circular cylinder with radius $4$ inches and length $16$ inches. He drops it into his bath, and it comes to rest completely horizontal, with the water surface $2$ inches above the lowest line of the clarinet. What volume of water, in cubic inches, does the clarinet displace?
