Put $n=2017$, so $2018=n+1$ and $2016=n-1$. The number is
\[
(n+1)^4-6n^2-12(n-1)-9=\left(n^4+4n^3+6n^2+4n+1\right)-6n^2-12n+12-9=n^4+4n^3-8n+4 .
\]
This is a square:
\[
\left(n^2+2n-2\right)^2=n^4+4n^3+4n^2-4n^2-8n+4=n^4+4n^3-8n+4 .
\]
Therefore
\[
2018^4-6\cdot2017^2-12\cdot2016-9=\left(2017^2+2\cdot2017-2\right)^2=4072321^2,
\]
a perfect square. $\blacksquare$
