There are ten teams in a baseball league, and each team plays every other team exactly once. In each game, the winner gets $5$ points and the loser gets $0$ points; if the game is a draw, both teams get $2$ points. At the end of the season, the total number of points given out was $210$. How many games ended in a draw?

$\textbf{(A)}\ 9\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ 12\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ 15\qquad\textbf{(D)}\ 18\qquad\textbf{(E)}\ 21$
