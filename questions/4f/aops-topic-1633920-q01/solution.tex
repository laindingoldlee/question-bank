There are $\dbinom{10}{2}=45$ games. A decided game gives out $5$ points and a draw gives out $4$, so if $d$ games are draws the total is
\[
5(45-d)+4d=225-d=210 ,
\]
and $d=\boxed{\textbf{(C)}\ 15}$.
