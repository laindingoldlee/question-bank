Let $t=\sqrt[3]3$, so $t^2=\sqrt[3]9$ and $t^3=3$. The denominator is $3^2-3t+t^2$, which has the form $a^2-ab+b^2$ with $a=3$ and $b=t$. Since $(a+b)\left(a^2-ab+b^2\right)=a^3+b^3$, we multiply the numerator and denominator by $3+t$:
\[\dfrac{4(3+t)}{3^3+t^3}=\dfrac{4\left(3+\sqrt[3]3\right)}{27+3}=\dfrac{2\left(3+\sqrt[3]3\right)}{15}=\boxed{\dfrac{6+2\sqrt[3]3}{15}}.\]
