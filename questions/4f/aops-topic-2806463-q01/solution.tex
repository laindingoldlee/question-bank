Let $B$ take $b$ days and $A$ take $b+20$. After the changes, $A$ takes $\dfrac{b+20}{1.25}=0.8(b+20)$ days and $B$ takes $b\div\dfrac23=1.5b$ days. So $0.8b+16=1.5b-5$, giving $b=30$, and $A$ takes $\boxed{50}$ days.
