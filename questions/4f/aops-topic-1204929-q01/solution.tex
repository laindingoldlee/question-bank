The number has at most $4$ digits and must contain $666$ as a block that is not part of $6666$.
\begin{itemize}
\item Three digits: only $666$.
\item Four digits $\overline{666x}$ with $x\ne6$: $9$ numbers.
\item Four digits $\overline{x666}$ with $x\ne0,6$: $8$ numbers.
\end{itemize}
Total: $1+9+8=\boxed{18}$.
