Call a positive integer \emph{beastly} if its decimal representation contains a block of exactly three consecutive $6$s and no longer block of $6$s (for example $3666$ and $66696$ are beastly, but $6606$, $666666$ and $66662$ are not). How many positive integers less than $10000$ are beastly?
