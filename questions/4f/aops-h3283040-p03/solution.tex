There are eight consonants C,M,B,N,T,R,C,S, with C repeated twice, and five vowels O,I,A,O,I. Arrange the consonants in $\dfrac{8!}{2!}$ ways. Their nine gaps must hold the five vowels in distinct gaps, chosen in $\binom 95$ ways. The vowel arrangements number $\dfrac{5!}{2!2!}=30$. The total is $\boxed{\dfrac{8!}{2!}\binom 95\dfrac{5!}{2!2!}=76204800}$.
