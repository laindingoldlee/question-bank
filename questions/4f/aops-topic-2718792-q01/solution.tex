The remainder has the form $ax+b$, and $F(x)=(x^2-1)Q(x)+ax+b$. Putting $x=1$ and $x=-1$: $a+b=F(1)=-1$ and $-a+b=F(-1)=11$. So $b=5$, $a=-6$, and the remainder is $\boxed{-6x+5}$.
