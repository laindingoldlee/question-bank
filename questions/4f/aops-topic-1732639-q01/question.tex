Take two different natural numbers $m$ and $n$. Multiply each by the next two natural numbers, getting $n(n+1)(n+2)$ and $m(m+1)(m+2)$. Subtract the smaller product from the larger and divide by the positive difference of $m$ and $n$. Finally add the product $mn$. Prove that the result is a product of two consecutive integers.
