A line through the centroid $G$ of triangle $ABC$, not passing through $A$, meets line $AB$ at $M$ and line $AC$ at $N$. Measure lengths along line $AB$ with sign, positive in the direction from $A$ to $B$, and lengths along line $AC$ positive in the direction from $A$ to $C$ (so, for example, $MB=AB-AM$ even when $M$ is outside segment $AB$). Prove that
\[AN\cdot MB+AM\cdot NC=AM\cdot AN.\]
