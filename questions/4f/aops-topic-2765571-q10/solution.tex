The failure probability is $(\dfrac{4}{5})^n$. At $n=3$ it is $\dfrac{64}{125}>\dfrac{1}{2}$, while at $n=4$ it is $\dfrac{256}{625}<\dfrac{1}{2}$. As this probability decreases with $n$, the answer is $\boxed4$.
