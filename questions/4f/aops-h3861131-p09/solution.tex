Assume real terms. The repeated initial pair makes the sequence periodic with period dividing $2025$. If any term is zero, recurrence forces all later terms zero, and periodicity forces every term zero. Otherwise let $u_i=\log|n_i|$. Then $u_{i+2}=u_i+u_{i+1}$. Its general form is $A\phi^i+B\psi^i$, where $\phi=\dfrac{1+\sqrt5}{2}>1$ and $\psi=\dfrac{1-\sqrt5}{2}$ has absolute value less than one. Bounded periodicity forces $A=0$, and a convergent periodic sequence must be constant, forcing $B=0$. Hence every $n_i$ is $1$ or $-1$. Any two initial signs give a sign sequence of period dividing three, so all four choices satisfy the required period. Including the zero sequence gives $\boxed5$.
