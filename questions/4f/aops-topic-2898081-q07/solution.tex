$n^2\equiv4\pmod8$. Odd squares are $\equiv1$, multiples of $4$ have squares $\equiv0$, and $n\equiv2\pmod4$ gives $n^2=4(\text{odd})^2\equiv4$. So $\boxed{n\equiv2\pmod4}$.
