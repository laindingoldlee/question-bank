The denominators are the triangular numbers $1+2+\cdots+k=\dfrac{k(k+1)}{2}$, and
\[\dfrac{2}{k(k+1)}=2\left(\dfrac1k-\dfrac{1}{k+1}\right).\]
So the coefficient of $x$ telescopes:
\[\sum_{k=1}^{4041}\dfrac{2}{k(k+1)}=2\left(1-\dfrac{1}{4042}\right)=\dfrac{4041}{2021}.\]
The equation becomes $\dfrac{4041}{2021}x=4041$, so $x=\boxed{2021}$.
