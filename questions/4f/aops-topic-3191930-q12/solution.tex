If Druv runs distance $d$, Sylvia runs $2d$. The right triangle gives $9+d^2=4d^2$, hence $\boxed{d=\sqrt3}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair D=(0,0),Spt=(3,0),M=(0,sqrt(3));draw(D--Spt--M--cycle);label("$D$",D,SW);label("$S$",Spt,SE);label("$M$",M,N);label("$3$",(D+Spt)/2,S);
\end{asy}
