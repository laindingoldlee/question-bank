\textbf{Shoelace formula.} The terms $x_iy_{i+1}-x_{i+1}y_i$ are
\[
1\cdot(-1)-3\cdot1=-4,\quad 3\cdot2-6\cdot(-1)=12,\quad 6\cdot6-5\cdot2=26,\quad 5\cdot5-2\cdot6=13,\quad 2\cdot1-1\cdot5=-3,
\]
with sum $44$, so the area is $\dfrac{44}{2}=\boxed{22}$.

\textbf{Check by subtraction.} The pentagon lies in the dashed rectangle $[1,6]\times[-1,6]$ of area $5\cdot7=35$. Outside the pentagon there are three right triangles, below $(1,1)$--$(3,-1)$ (legs $2,2$, area $2$), below $(3,-1)$--$(6,2)$ (legs $3,3$, area $4.5$) and beside $(6,2)$--$(5,6)$ (legs $1,4$, area $2$), and the quadrilateral with vertices $(1,1)$, $(2,5)$, $(5,6)$, $(1,6)$, which splits along $x=2$ into a trapezoid of area $\dfrac{5+1}{2}=3$ and a triangle of area $\dfrac{3\cdot1}{2}=1.5$. So the area is $35-(2+4.5+2+3+1.5)=22$.
