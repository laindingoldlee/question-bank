Choose a longest side $BC$ as horizontal base of length $b$. The altitude from $A$ meets its interior, so use coordinates $B=(0,0),C=(b,0),A=(t,h)$ with $0<t<b$. Let $M,N$ be midpoints of $AB,AC$, and $P=(t,\dfrac{h}{2})$ on $MN$. Cut along $MN$ and $AP$, obtaining the lower trapezoid and triangles $AMP,ANP$. Rotate $AMP$ by $180^\circ$ about $M$, sending $A$ to $B$ and $P$ to $(0,\dfrac{h}{2})$. Rotate $ANP$ similarly about $N$, sending $A$ to $C$ and $P$ to $(b,\dfrac{h}{2})$. These exactly fill the two missing corners of the rectangle $[0,b]\times[0,\dfrac{h}{2}]$.
