Order the pencil counts $a_1\ge\cdots\ge a_n$. The largest six sum to at most $41$, so $a_6\le6$, and every later count is also at most $6$. Hence
\[
153\le41+6(n-6)=6n+5,
\]
giving $n\ge25$. This is attainable with three students receiving $7$ pencils and twenty-two receiving $6$: the total is $153$, and even the largest six counts sum to $39\le41$. Therefore the minimum is $\boxed{25}$.
