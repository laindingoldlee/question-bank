Choose which $3$ of the $5$ rolls are sixes ($\dbinom53=10$ ways). Each such pattern has probability $\left(\dfrac{1}{6}\right)^3\left(\dfrac{5}{6}\right)^2$. Hence
\[
P=10\cdot\dfrac{1}{216}\cdot\dfrac{25}{36}=\dfrac{250}{7776}=\boxed{\dfrac{125}{3888}}.
\]
