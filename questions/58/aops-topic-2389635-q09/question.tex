A small fish cage consists of a $5$ inch by $6$ inch by $8$ inch glass tank surrounded by iron bars. The bars lie on the outside surface of the tank, parallel to its edges, and are spaced at $1$-inch intervals all around the tank, forming a square grid on every face (the edges of the tank are also covered by bars). What is the total length of the iron bars?
