An $8\times8$ checkerboard has side length $16$ inches, so each small square is $2$ inches by $2$ inches. Coin A has circumference $1.2\pi$ inches and coin B has circumference $1.6\pi$ inches. Both coins are dropped on the board so that the centre of each coin is uniformly distributed over the board, independently of the other coin. What is the probability that coin A does not touch any line of the board (including the border lines), but coin B does touch at least one line?
