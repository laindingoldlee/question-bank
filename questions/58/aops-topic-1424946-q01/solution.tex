Only the last three digits of each product matter, so work modulo $1000$:
\[
12^2=144,\quad 12^3=1728\equiv728,\quad 12^4\equiv728\cdot12=8736\equiv736,
\]
\[
12^5\equiv736\cdot12=8832\equiv832,\quad 12^6\equiv832\cdot12=9984\equiv984,\quad 12^7\equiv984\cdot12=11808\equiv808 .
\]
The last three digits are $\boxed{808}$ (indeed $12^7=35831808$).
