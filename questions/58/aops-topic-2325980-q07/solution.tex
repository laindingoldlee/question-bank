$x\equiv3\pmod6$ means $x=6k+3$, which is odd. $x\equiv4\pmod8$ means $x=8j+4$, which is even. No integer is both odd and even, so the system has $\boxed{\text{no solution}}$. (The Chinese remainder theorem does not apply because the moduli $6$ and $8$ are not coprime, and the two congruences disagree modulo $\gcd(6,8)=2$.)
