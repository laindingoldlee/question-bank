Let $E_k$ be the remaining expected days when $k$ people know. From three, the last person is missed with probability $(\dfrac{2}{3})^3$, so $E_3=1+(\dfrac{8}{27})E_3=\dfrac{27}{19}$. From two, no new person hears with probability $\dfrac{1}{9}$, exactly one with probability $\dfrac{6}{9}$, and both with probability $\dfrac{2}{9}$. Thus $E_2=1+\dfrac{E_2}{9}+\dfrac{6E_3}{9}$. Including the compulsory first day gives $1+E_2=\dfrac{485}{152}$. Hence the answer is $\boxed{637}$.
