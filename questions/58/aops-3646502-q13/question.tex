In a room with $4$ people, one person knows a secret message. On the first day, that person tells someone else the secret message. On every succeeding day, each person who knows the secret message randomly picks another person to tell (that person may or may not know the secret message already). The expected number of days until everyone knows the secret message can be expressed as $\dfrac{a}{b}$ , where $a$ and $b$ are relatively prime. Find $a + b$.
