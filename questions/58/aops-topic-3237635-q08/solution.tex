Expansion cancels the two rational terms and leaves $xy+4=0$, so the curve is $xy=-4$. For lattice points in the specified region, the product is $-1,-2,-3$, or $-4$. For product $-k$, each positive divisor $d\mid k$ gives two points $(d,-\dfrac{k}{d})$ and $(-d,\dfrac{k}{d})$. Thus the count is
\[2\bigl(\tau(1)+\tau(2)+\tau(3)+\tau(4)\bigr)=2(1+2+2+3)=\boxed{16}.\]

\end{asy}
