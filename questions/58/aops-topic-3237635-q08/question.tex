For nonzero $x,y$, simplify the curve equation
\[(x+\dfrac{2y}{x})(y+\dfrac{2x}{y})=\dfrac{2x^2}{y}+\dfrac{2y^2}{x}.\]
How many lattice points $(a,b)$ satisfy $-4\le ab\le-1$, lying between this curve and the axes in the opposite-sign quadrants?
