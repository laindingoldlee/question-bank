Each happy monkey needs at least two non-orange fruits, of which there are 90. Thus at most 45 monkeys can be happy. Achieve 45 by giving 5 monkeys pear-banana-orange,15 monkeys pear-peach-orange, and 25 monkeys banana-peach-orange. This uses 20 pears,30 bananas,40 peaches,45 oranges. Hence the maximum is $\boxed{45}$.
