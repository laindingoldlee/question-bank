Let $\angle DAB=\angle CBA=\theta$ (the trapezoid is isosceles). Triangle $ADC$ is isosceles, so $\angle DAC=\angle DCA$, and $\angle DCA=\angle CAB$ (alternate angles, $DC\parallel AB$). Hence $AC$ bisects $\angle A$ and $\angle CAB=\dfrac\theta2$. Triangle $ABC$ has $AB=AC$, so $\angle ACB=\angle ABC=\theta$. Its angle sum gives $\dfrac\theta2+2\theta=180^\circ$, so $\theta=72^\circ$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

pair A=(0,0),B=(1+2*Cos(72),0),C=(1+Cos(72),Sin(72)),D=(Cos(72),Sin(72));draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C,dashed);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);
\end{asy}

The angles are $\boxed{\angle A=\angle B=72^\circ,\ \angle C=\angle D=108^\circ}$.
