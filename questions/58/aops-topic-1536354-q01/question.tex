Triangle $ABC$ has a right angle at $C$ and $\angle A=15^\circ$, so $\angle B=75^\circ$. Let $c=AB$ be the hypotenuse.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that the altitude from $C$ to the hypotenuse has length $\dfrac {c}{4}$.
\item[(b)] Find the legs $BC$ and $AC$ in terms of $c$, in simplest radical form.
\end{enumerate}
