(a) Every attainable quantity is divisible by 4, so arbitrarily large integers are impossible; no largest exists. (b) After division by 4, attainable numbers are $2a+3b$ for nonnegative integers $a,b$. Every integer at least 2 is representable: even ones use 2 s, odd ones use a 3 and then 2 s. The only positive exception is 1, corresponding to $\boxed4$ nuggets.
