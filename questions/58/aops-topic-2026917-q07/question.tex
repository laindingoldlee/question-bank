Every student in Jarvis' school takes $6$ English tests, each worth $100$ points. On his first $5$ tests Jarvis scored $42$, $38$, $64$, $36$, and $60$. His mom will buy him ice cream if his average over all $6$ tests is at least $55$. What is the minimum percent Jarvis must score on the last test to get ice cream?
