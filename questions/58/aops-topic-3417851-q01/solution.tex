Put $S=x+y+z$. Adding the equations gives $2S^2=18$, hence $S=3$ or $-3$. For $S=3$, the pair sums are $x+y=5$, $y+z=2$, $z+x=-1$, giving $(x,y,z)=(1,4,-2)$. For $S=-3$, all pair sums reverse signs, yielding $(-1,-4,2)$. Both triples satisfy the original equations, so these are all solutions.
