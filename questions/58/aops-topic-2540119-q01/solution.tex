In $t$ seconds the three of them read $\dfrac t{20}+\dfrac t{45}+\dfrac t{30}$ pages. With the common denominator $180$,
\[\dfrac{9t+4t+6t}{180}=\dfrac{19t}{180}=760,\qquad t=\dfrac{760\cdot180}{19}=40\cdot180=7200.\]
(Alice reads $360$ pages, Bob $160$ and Chandra $240$.) The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(E) } 7200}$.
