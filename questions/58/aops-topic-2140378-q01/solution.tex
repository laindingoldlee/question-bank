The positive multiples of $101$ up to $2020=101\cdot20$ are $101k$ for $k=1,2,\dots,20$. Since $101k=100k+k$, the units digit of $101k$ is the units digit of $k$, which is even exactly when $k$ is even. There are $\boxed{10}$ even values of $k$ from $1$ to $20$.
