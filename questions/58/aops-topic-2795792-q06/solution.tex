The sum of two two-digit numbers is less than $200$, so $\overline{EF}\cdot\overline{EG}<200$ forces $E=1$, and $\overline{1F}\cdot\overline{1G}\ge10\cdot12=120$. Try $\overline{EF}\cdot\overline{EG}=10\cdot12=120$, using $1,0,2$. We need two two-digit numbers from the remaining digits $3,\dots,9$ summing to $120$: $34+86=120$. So
\[\boxed{34+86=10\cdot12}\]
works ($A=3,B=4,C=8,D=6,E=1,F=0,G=2$).
