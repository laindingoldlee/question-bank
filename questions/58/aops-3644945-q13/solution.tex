Put $CD=x$. The right triangle gives $AD^2=x^2+16$. Tangent-secant power gives $AD^2=DC\cdot DB=x(x+9)$. Equating yields $9x=16$, hence $\boxed{x = \dfrac{16}{9}}$.

import graph;

\end{asy}
