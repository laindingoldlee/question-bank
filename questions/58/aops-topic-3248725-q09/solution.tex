All terms are nonnegative, so the iterated sums equal the limit of increasing finite rectangular sums and may be interchanged. Thus $\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}\sum_{k=2}^{\infty}k^{-2j}=\sum_{k=2}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{k^2-1}$. Using $\dfrac{1}{k^2-1}=\dfrac{1}{2}(\dfrac{1}{k-1}-\dfrac{1}{k+1})$, the partial sum through $k=N$ is $\dfrac{1}{2}(1+\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{N}-\dfrac{1}{N+1})$. The limit is $\boxed{\dfrac{3}{4}}$.
