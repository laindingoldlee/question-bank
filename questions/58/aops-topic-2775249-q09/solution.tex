Factoring gives $(3x-4)(y+1)=11$. The possible first factors are $1,-1,11,-11$, but $3x-4\equiv2\pmod3$ permits only-1 and 11. They give $(x,y)=(1,-12),(5,0)$. The requested total is $1-12+5+0=\boxed{-6}$.
