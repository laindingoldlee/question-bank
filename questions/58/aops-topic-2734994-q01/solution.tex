$2^{2^2}=2^4=16$, and by coincidence $\left(2^2\right)^2=4^2=16$ too. But $3^{3^3}=3^{27}=7625597484987$, whereas $\left(3^3\right)^3=3^9=19683$. So in general $a^{b^c}=a^{(b^c)}$ differs from $\left(a^b\right)^c=a^{bc}$; for $2^{2^2}$ they agree because $2^2=2\cdot2$.
