Count ways to choose a committee of $r$ people from $n$, and a subcommittee of $k$ of them. Committee first, then subcommittee: $\binom nr\binom rk$. Subcommittee first ($\binom nk$), then the other $r-k$ committee members from the remaining $n-k$: $\binom nk\binom{n-k}{r-k}$. $\blacksquare$
