Using $\tan15^\circ=2-\sqrt3$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] show that $\sin15^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt{2-\sqrt3}}{2}$ and write this in the form $\dfrac{\sqrt a-\sqrt b}{c}$;
\item[(b)] simplify $\cos15^\circ=\dfrac{\left(2+\sqrt3\right)\sqrt{2-\sqrt3}}{2}$;
\item[(c)] find $\csc15^\circ$, $\sec15^\circ$, $\cot15^\circ$, $\csc75^\circ$, $\sec75^\circ$ and $\cot75^\circ$ in simplest radical form.
\end{enumerate}
