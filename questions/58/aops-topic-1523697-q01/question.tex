A drink is made by mixing a $20\%$ grape juice (by volume) with a $50\%$ orange juice; the rest of each is water. The drink must contain exactly $24$ litres of pure juice, and its juice content must be between $25\%$ and $37.5\%$, inclusive. Find the least and the greatest amounts of the $20\%$ grape juice that can be used.
