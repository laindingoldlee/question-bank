$C$, $M$ and $E$ are distinct digits, with $C\ne0$ and $E\ne0$, and each letter stands for the same digit everywhere it appears. If
\[
CMEC+EMM=CEEC
\]
(where $CMEC$, $EMM$, $CEEC$ are numbers written in base $10$), what is $M^2\cdot E^2\cdot C^2$?
