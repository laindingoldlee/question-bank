Write the equation with place values:
\[
(1000C+100M+10E+C)+(100E+10M+M)=1000C+100E+10E+C .
\]
Cancelling $1001C+10E+100E$ from both sides leaves $100M+11M=0$, that is, $111M=0$, so $M=0$. (Column by column: the units give $C+M\equiv C$, so $M=0$ with no carry, and the other columns then agree for every $C$ and $E$.)

Hence $M^2E^2C^2=\boxed{0}$, whatever the digits $C$ and $E$ are; for example $1021+200=1221$.
