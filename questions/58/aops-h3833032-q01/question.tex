Samia and Alikhan study in class $6A$. It is known that every student in class $6A$ (including Samia and Alikhan) has exactly one of the following two hobbies: either collecting Lego or playing football. It is also known that Samia plays football. Samia noticed that the number of her classmates who play football is exactly $7$ times the number of her classmates who collect Lego. Alikhan noticed that the number of his classmates who play football is $18$ greater than the number of his classmates who collect Lego. How many students are there in this class altogether? Find all possible answers and prove that there are no others.
