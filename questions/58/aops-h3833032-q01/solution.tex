Let $F$ and $L$ be the numbers who play football and collect Lego. Since Samia plays football, she excludes herself when counting classmates, giving $F-1=7L$.
If Alikhan plays football, his observation gives $(F-1)-L=18$, hence $6L=18$, $L=3$ and $F=22$. If Alikhan collects Lego, his observation gives $F-(L-1)=18$; substituting $F=7L+1$ gives $6L=16$, impossible for integer $L$. Therefore there are exactly $\boxed{25}$ students, and the first case satisfies both observations.
