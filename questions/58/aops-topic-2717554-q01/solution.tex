Consider the $n+1$ numbers $1,11,111,\dots,\underbrace{1\cdots1}_{n+1}$. Two of them leave the same remainder mod $n$ (pigeonhole principle), say those with $i<j$ ones. Their difference
\[\underbrace{1\cdots1}_{j-i}\underbrace{0\cdots0}_{i}\]
is a positive multiple of $n$ using only the digits $0$ and $1$. $\blacksquare$
