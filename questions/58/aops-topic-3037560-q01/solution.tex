For the first, $ 9!+7!=7!(9\cdot8+1)=73\cdot7!$. The number 73 is prime (none of 2,3,5,7 divides it), so the largest prime factor is $\boxed{73}$. For the second, factor out $ 6!$: \[10(6!+8!)+10!=6!\left[10(1+8\cdot7)+10\cdot9\cdot8\cdot7\right]=6!\cdot5610.\]Now $ 5610=2\cdot3\cdot5\cdot11\cdot17 $, while all prime factors of $ 6!$ are at most 5. The largest is therefore $\boxed{17}$.
