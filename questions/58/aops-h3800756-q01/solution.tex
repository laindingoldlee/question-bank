Scale the side to one and use $A=(1,0),B=(\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}),D=(-1,0),E=(-\dfrac{1}{2},-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2})$. Then $M=(\dfrac{3}{4},\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4})$. On segment $ME$, height zero occurs one-third of the way from $M$ to $E$, giving $O=(\dfrac{1}{3},0)$. Thus $OD=\dfrac{4}{3}$ and the height from $M$ is $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}$, so $[OMD]=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{6}$. The hexagon has area $6\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}=\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}$. The ratio is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{9}}$.
