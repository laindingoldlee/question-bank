With straightedge and compass, starting from a segment of length $1$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] construct a segment of length $\sqrt2$;
\item[(b)] construct a segment of length $\dfrac{1+\sqrt5}{2}$;
\item[(c)] show how to construct a segment of length $\sqrt a$ from a segment of length $a$.
\end{enumerate}
(Together with the constructions of sums, differences, products and quotients, this shows that any length built from $1$ by $+$, $-$, $\times$, $\div$ and square roots can be constructed.)
