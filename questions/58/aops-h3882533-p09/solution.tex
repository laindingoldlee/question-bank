By a cube symmetry, take the triangle vertices to be $(36,0,0),(0,36,0),(0,0,36)$. Such a coordinate choice covers all equilateral vertex triangles: their sides must be face diagonals, since cube edges form no triangle and each vertex has only one body-diagonal partner. The plane is $x+y+z=36$.

The isolated vertex is $(0,0,0)$, and the other four are $(36,36,0),(36,0,36),(0,36,36),(36,36,36)$. Therefore $M=(12,12,12)$ and $G=(27,27,27)$, giving $\boxed{MG^2=3(15)^2=675}$.
