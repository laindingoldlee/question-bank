The solutions of \(x^2+bx+c=0\) are \(p\) and \(q\). The solutions of \(21x^2-10x+1=0\) are \(\dfrac{1}{p}\) and \(\dfrac{1}{q}\). If \(b\) and \(c\) are relatively prime integers, find \(b+c\).
