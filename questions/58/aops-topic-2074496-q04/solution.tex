Factor: \(21x^2-10x+1=(3x-1)(7x-1)\), with roots \(\dfrac{1}{3}\) and \(\dfrac{1}{7}\). Hence \(\{p,q\}=\{3,7\}\), and since \(x^2+bx+c\) is monic, \(x^2+bx+c=(x-3)(x-7)=x^2-10x+21\). So \(b=-10\), \(c=21\) (indeed coprime), and
\[
b+c=\boxed{11}.
\]
