By 3:00 p.m. Gene has painted $\dfrac{2}{12}=\dfrac{1}{6}$ of the fence, leaving $\dfrac{5}{6}$. Together they paint $\dfrac{1}{8}+\dfrac{1}{12}=\dfrac{5}{24}$ of the fence per hour, so the rest takes $\dfrac{5}{6}\div\dfrac{5}{24}=4$ hours. They finish at $\boxed{7{:}00\text{ p.m.}}$
