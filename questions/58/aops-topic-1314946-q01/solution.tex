$441_b=4b^2+4b+1=(2b+1)^2$, so $n=2b+1$ (taking $n>0$). Then
\[
351_b=3b^2+5b+1=(2b-1)^2=4b^2-4b+1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad b^2-9b=0,
\]
so $b=9$ (and $b>5$ is needed for the digit $5$). Hence $\boxed{b=9,\ n=19}$. Check: $441_9=361=19^2$ and $351_9=289=17^2$.
