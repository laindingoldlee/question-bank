\textbf{(a)} $1^2-1=0$, $2^2-2=2$, $3^2-3=6$, $4^2-4=12$: the first four terms are $\boxed{0,\ 2,\ 6,\ 12}$.

\textbf{(b)} $10^2-10=\boxed{90}$.

(Since $n^2-n=n(n-1)$, each term is the product of two consecutive integers; the differences $2,4,6,\dots$ grow by $2$.)
