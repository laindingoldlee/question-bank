Initially the hour hand leads by 120 degrees. The minute hand gains $\dfrac{11}{2}$ degrees per minute, so first reaches a 10-degree gap after gaining 110 degrees. Time is $\dfrac{110}{\dfrac{11}{2}}=\boxed{20}$ minutes.
