Completing the square gives $N=5((a+2r)^2+2r^2)$. Since $5\mid N$, its repeated digit must be five. For $k$ digits, dividing by five gives $(a+2r)^2+2r^2=11\cdots1$. If $k\ge3$, the right side is seven modulo eight; but a square plus twice a square can only be $0,1,2,3,4,6$ modulo eight. If $k=1$, the right side is one, below the minimum eleven. For $k=2$, the equation equals eleven, achieved only by $r=1$, $a+2r=3$. Hence $\boxed{(a,r)=(1,1)}$, giving $N=55$.
