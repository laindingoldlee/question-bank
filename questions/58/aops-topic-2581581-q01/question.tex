A line $l$ and points $A$, $B$ on the same side of $l$ are given in the plane. Construct, with ruler and compass, a point $C$ such that the line $l$ meets segment $AC$ at $M$ and segment $BC$ at $N$, where $BM$ is an altitude and $AN$ is a median of triangle $ABC$. When does a solution exist?
