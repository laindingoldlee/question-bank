\emph{Base case} $n=1$: $1=\dbinom22$.

\emph{Inductive step.} Suppose $1+2+\cdots+n=\dbinom{n+1}2=\dfrac{n(n+1)}2$. Then
\[1+2+\cdots+n+(n+1)=\dfrac{n(n+1)}2+(n+1)=\dfrac{(n+1)(n+2)}2=\dbinom{n+2}2,\]
which is the statement for $n+1$. By induction it holds for every positive integer $n$. $\blacksquare$
