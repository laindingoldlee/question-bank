A wishing well hears a wish with probability $\dfrac16$. If it hears the wish, it takes it into consideration with probability $29\%$; a wish that is not considered is not granted. A wish is good or bad with probability $50\%$ each. A good wish that is considered is always granted; a bad wish that is considered is granted (for a noble reason) with probability $11\%$. What is the probability that a wish is granted?
