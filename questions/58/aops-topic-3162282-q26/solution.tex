Take $ B=(0,0),C=(14,0),A=(5,12)$. Heron's formula gives area 84 and semiperimeter 21, so the inradius is 4. Tangent lengths give $ BD=BE=6 $, and hence $ I=(6,4)$. The two midpoints are $(3,0)$ and $\dfrac{3}{13}A=(\dfrac{15}{13},\dfrac{36}{13})$. Their line is $ y=\dfrac{9}{2}-\dfrac{3}{2}x $. The horizontal line through $ A $ is $ y=12 $, giving $ X=(-5,12)$. Therefore $\boxed{XI=\sqrt{185}}$.
