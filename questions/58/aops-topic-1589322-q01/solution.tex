\textbf{Prime factorisation.} $2084=4\cdot521$, and $521$ is prime: it is not divisible by $2,3,5,7,11,13,17$ or $19$, and $23^2=529>521$. So $2084=2^2\cdot521$ has exactly three prime factors counted with multiplicity.

\textbf{Uniqueness.} If $2084=uvw$ with $u,v,w>1$, each factor contains at least one prime factor. There are only three prime factors in total, so each factor is a single prime, and the factors must be $2$, $2$ and $521$ in some order. Hence
\[
2084=\boxed{2\cdot2\cdot521}
\]
is the only way, apart from order. (If factors equal to $1$ were allowed, there would be other ways, such as $1\cdot4\cdot521$ or $1\cdot1\cdot2084$.)
