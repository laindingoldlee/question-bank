Condition on the subset size $k$. If $k=2r$ or $2r+1$, the statistic is the $r$th smallest element. For a uniformly random $k$-subset of $\{1,\ldots,10\}$, the expected $j$th smallest element is $11\dfrac{j}{(k+1)}$. One proof uses the $k+1$ gaps before, between, and after the selected elements: these gaps are symmetric and total $10-k$, giving equal expected gap sizes.

There are $2^{10}-1-10=1013$ eligible subsets. Consequently
\[
E=\dfrac{1}{1013}\sum_{k=2}^{10}\binom{10}{k}\dfrac{11\lfloor \dfrac{k}{2}\rfloor}{k+1}
=\dfrac{4097}{1013}.
\]
The fraction is reduced, so $m+n=5110$ and the requested remainder is $\boxed{110}$.
