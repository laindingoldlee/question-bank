For each subset $S$ of $\{1,\,2,\,3,\,\ldots,\,10\}$ with at least $2$ elements, let $n(S)$ denote the largest element of $S$ less than the median of $S$. If $S$ is chosen uniformly at random from all subsets of $\{1,\,2,\,3,\,\ldots,\,10\}$ with at least $2$ elements, the expected value of $n(S)$ can be written as $\dfrac{m}{n}$ for relatively prime positive integers $m$ and $n$. Find the remainder when $m+n$ is divided by $1000$.
