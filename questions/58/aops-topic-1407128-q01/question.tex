In triangle $ABC$ let $a=BC$, $b=CA$, $c=AB$, and let $I$ be the incentre (the intersection of the angle bisectors). Prove that, in coordinates (or as position vectors),
\[
I=\dfrac{aA+bB+cC}{a+b+c}.
\]
