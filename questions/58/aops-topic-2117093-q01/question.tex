Jefferson and his five pals share a box of $30$ fries, all of different lengths. To decide who gets their only coke, they draw fries from the box one at a time without replacement, and whoever draws the longest fry gets the coke. Jefferson arrives late. Before he sits down, his five pals take turns around the table, each drawing one fry per turn, until each of them has drawn $3$ fries. Then Jefferson sits down, and the six of them continue taking turns, Jefferson first and then his five pals in the same order as before, each drawing one fry per turn, until someone draws the longest fry. What is the probability that Jefferson gets the coke?
