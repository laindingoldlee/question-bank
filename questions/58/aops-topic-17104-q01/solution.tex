The floor is an $m\times n$ rectangle with $mn=30$, and its perimeter is $2(m+n)$. For a fixed product, $m+n$ is largest when the factors are farthest apart: the factor pairs $(1,30),(2,15),(3,10),(5,6)$ have sums $31,17,13,11$. The greatest perimeter is $2\cdot31=\boxed{62}$ feet, for a $1$ by $30$ floor.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(9cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
for(int i=0;i<30;++i) draw(shift(i,0)*unitsquare,gray);
draw((0,0)--(30,0)--(30,1)--(0,1)--cycle,blue+linewidth(1));
for(int i=0;i<6;++i) for(int j=0;j<5;++j) draw(shift(i,-7+j)*unitsquare,gray);
draw((0,-7)--(6,-7)--(6,-2)--(0,-2)--cycle,red+linewidth(1));
label("$1\times30$: perimeter $62$",(15,1),N,blue);
label("$5\times6$: perimeter $22$",(6,-4.5),E,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
