A right triangle inscribed in a circle has a diameter as its hypotenuse. Opposite vertices of the $2n$-gon have the same colour precisely when $n$ is even. Thus $n$ is even. Each colour then has $\dfrac{n}{2}$ diameter pairs, and for each pair the third vertex can be any of its other $n-2$ vertices. Each right triangle has a unique hypotenuse, so the count is $2(\dfrac{n}{2})(n-2)=n(n-2)$. Hence $(n-1)^2=2025$, giving $\boxed{n=46}$.
