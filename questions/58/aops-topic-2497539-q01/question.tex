Consider arrangements of all the letters of TOILETPAPER in a row, where letters that are the same are indistinguishable.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] How many arrangements have the two T's not next to each other and the two P's not next to each other?
\item[(b)] How many arrangements have no T next to a P?
\end{enumerate}
