The projection is $\dfrac{13a+1}{17}(4,1)$. Both coordinates are integers exactly when $13a+1\equiv0\pmod {17}$. The inverse of $13$ is $4$, so $a\equiv13\pmod {17}$. The values are $13+17j$ for $0\le j\le118$, giving $\boxed{119}$.

\end{asy}
