Of the $6!=720$ orders, swapping a wife with her husband is a bijection, so for each couple exactly half of the orders have the wife ahead; the three conditions are independent in this sense, giving $\dfrac{720}{2^3}=\boxed{90}$. (Equivalently: choose the positions of couple $1$ in $\dbinom62$ ways, couple $2$ in $\dbinom42$, couple $3$ in $\dbinom22$, and the order within each couple is forced: $15\cdot6\cdot1=90$.)
