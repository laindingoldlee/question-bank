The sum of all six digits is $21$, so divisibility by $3$ requires omitting either $3$ or $6$. If $3$ is omitted, there are three even choices for the last digit, and $4!$ orders for the rest: $72$. If $6$ is omitted, there are two even choices, giving $48$. The total is $\boxed{120}$.
