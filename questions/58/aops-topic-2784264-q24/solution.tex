Power of a point gives $PC=\dfrac{PA^2}{PB}=2\sqrt2$. Since $PA\perp AC$, $AC=\sqrt{PC^2-PA^2}=2$, and the radius is 1. Coordinates $A=(0,0),C=(2,0),P=(0,2)$ give $B=(1,1)$. The part of the upper semicircle outside the triangle is a quarter-circle segment of area $\dfrac{\pi}{4}-\dfrac{1}{2}$; the entire lower semicircle is also outside. The requested area is therefore $\boxed{\dfrac{3\pi}{4}-\dfrac{1}{2}}$. (The original coefficient sum is $3+4+1+2=10$.)
