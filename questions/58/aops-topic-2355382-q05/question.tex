A family of four drives $600$ miles to their grandparents' house. At miles $100$, $200$, $300$, $400$ and $500$ they stop at a rest station for $5$ minutes per person. At every $50$th mile that is not a multiple of $100$ (miles $50$, $150$, $\dots$, $550$) they stop for gas, which takes $15$ minutes. They start at $60$ miles per hour, and after every second rest station their speed drops by $10$ miles per hour because of traffic; otherwise their speed is constant and they make no other stops. They leave home at $6{:}20$ a.m.\ Eastern Time, and their grandparents live in the Central Time zone, one hour behind Eastern Time. At what time, in Central Time, do they arrive?
