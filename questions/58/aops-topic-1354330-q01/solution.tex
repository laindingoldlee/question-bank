We need $2\pi r=\pi r^2$ with $r>0$. Dividing by $\pi r$ gives $r=\boxed{2}$. Check: circumference $4\pi$, area $4\pi$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw(circle((0,0),2),blue+linewidth(0.9)); dot((0,0));
draw((0,0)--(2,0),dashed); label("$r=2$",(1,0.3),N);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
