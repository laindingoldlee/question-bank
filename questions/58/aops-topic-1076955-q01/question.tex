Five points $A,B,C,D,E$ lie on a line in this order. Taking the points in pairs gives $10$ distances, which listed in increasing order are
\[
16,\ 23,\ 37,\ 39,\ 53,\ 60,\ 69,\ 76,\ 92,\ 129 .
\]
If $AB<DE$, determine $(AB,BC,CD,DE)$.
