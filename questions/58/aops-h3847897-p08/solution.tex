The cubic is $(x+1)^3$, so the expression is $(x+1)^{x+3}$ on its domain. A positive base gives one either when the base is one ($x=0$) or the exponent is zero; the latter yields $x=-3$ and is valid in the original expression. A negative base of magnitude other than one can give one only at exponent zero. Base $-1$ would mean $x=-2$, but the total exponent is one, giving $-1$. Base zero is undefined here. The solutions are $0,-3$, with sum $\boxed{-3}$.
