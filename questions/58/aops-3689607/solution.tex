The common base $AB$ gives $[AOB]:[ADB]=1:3$. Using bases $BO,BD$ on the same line instead shows $BO:BD=1:3$, hence $BO:OD=1:2$. Similar triangles $BOC$ and $DOA$ then give $CO:OA=1:2$. Triangles $COD,AOD$ share base $OD$, and their altitudes are proportional to $CO,OA$, so $[COD]=\dfrac{6}{2}=\boxed3$.

\end{asy}
