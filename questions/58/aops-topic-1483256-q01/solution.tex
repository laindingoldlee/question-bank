Factor $60=2^2\cdot3\cdot5$. A positive integer is a perfect cube exactly when every exponent in its prime factorisation is a multiple of $3$. The exponents of $2$, $3$, $5$ in $60x$ must therefore be raised to at least $3$, and $x$ needs no other primes. The smallest choice is
\[
x=2\cdot3^2\cdot5^2=\boxed{450},
\]
giving $60x=27000=30^3$.
