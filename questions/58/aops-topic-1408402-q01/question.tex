Twins Taylor and Tyler were born on 05/02/07 (May 2, 2007). This date is called a \emph{sum date} because the month plus the day equals the two-digit year: $05+02=07$. How many years of the 21st century (2001 to 2100) have a sum date in every one of the twelve months?
