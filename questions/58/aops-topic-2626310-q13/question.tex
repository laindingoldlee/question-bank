Let $x_1,x_2,\dots,x_{150}$ be a sequence of real numbers with $x_i\in\left\{\sqrt2+1,\ \sqrt2-1\right\}$ for all $i$. For how many integers $S$ with $1\le S\le1000$ does there exist such a sequence with
\[\sum_{i=1}^{149}x_ix_{i+1}=S?\]
