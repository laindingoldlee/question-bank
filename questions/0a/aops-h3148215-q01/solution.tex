Fix $1$ on top and $2$ on bottom. The four other labels have $4!$ cyclic arrangements around the side faces, identified by the four rotations about this axis, giving $\dfrac{4!}{4}=6$ numbered cubes. Each of the three opposite-face pairs independently receives either colour, giving $2^3=8$ colourings. Distinct face labels leave no extra symmetry to identify these choices. Thus there are $6\cdot8=\boxed{48}$ designs.
