Since $A\cap B\subseteq B$, $p\le\dfrac23$. By inclusion--exclusion, $p=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cup B)\ge\dfrac34+\dfrac23-1=\dfrac{5}{12}$.

Both bounds occur. Take a number uniform in $[0,1]$ and $A=\left[0,\dfrac34\right]$. With $B=\left[0,\dfrac23\right]$ we get $p=\dfrac23$; with $B=\left[\dfrac13,1\right]$ we get $A\cap B=\left[\dfrac13,\dfrac34\right]$ and $p=\dfrac{5}{12}$. So the smallest interval is $\left[\dfrac{5}{12},\dfrac23\right]$, answer $\boxed{\textbf{(E)}}$.
