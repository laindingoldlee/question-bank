The probability of event $A$ is $\dfrac34$ and the probability of event $B$ is $\dfrac23$. With only this information, let $p$ be the probability that both $A$ and $B$ occur. What is the smallest interval that must contain $p$?

$\textbf{(A) }\left[\dfrac{1}{12},\dfrac23\right]\qquad\textbf{(B) }\left[\dfrac12,\dfrac23\right]\qquad\textbf{(C) }\left[\dfrac{1}{12},\dfrac12\right]\qquad\textbf{(D) }\left[\dfrac{5}{12},\dfrac12\right]\qquad\textbf{(E) }\left[\dfrac{5}{12},\dfrac23\right]$
