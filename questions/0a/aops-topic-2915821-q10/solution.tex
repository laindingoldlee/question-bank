If the primes end in 2 or 5, only the primes 2 or 5 themselves qualify, so no distinct pair exists. For units digit 1, the smallest distinct pair is 11 and 31, already too large; for 7 it is 7 and 17, also too large; for 9 it is 19 and 29. For units digit 3, the prime 3 paired with 13 or 23 gives 39 or 69, while the next such prime 43 gives a three-digit product. Two primes both at least 13 are also too large. Hence there are $\boxed{2}$ numbers, 39 and 69.
