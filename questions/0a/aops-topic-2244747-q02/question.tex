The ellipse $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1$, where $a>b>0$, has foci $F_1$ and $F_2$. The segment $F_1F_2$ is a diameter of a circle. The absolute difference between the area of the ellipse and the area of the circle is $11\pi$. How many ordered pairs $(a,b)$ of positive integers are possible?
