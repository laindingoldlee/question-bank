Define the \emph{juiciness} of a permutation $p_1p_2\cdots p_n$ of the first $n$ positive integers to be
\[\sum_{j=1}^{n-1}\left|p_{j+1}-p_j\right| .\]
Let $m_k$ be the average juiciness of all permutations of the first $k$ positive integers, and let $v_k$ be the maximum possible juiciness of such a permutation. Find the remainder when $m_{2020}+v_{2020}$ is divided by $1000$.

$\textbf{(A)}\ 332 \qquad\textbf{(B)}\ 374 \qquad\textbf{(C)}\ 679 \qquad\textbf{(D)}\ 721 \qquad\textbf{(E)}\ 932$
