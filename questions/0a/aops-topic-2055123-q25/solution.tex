\textbf{Average.} For each $j$, the pair $(p_j,p_{j+1})$ over a random permutation is a uniformly random ordered pair of distinct numbers from $1,\dots,k$. Now
\[\sum_{x<y}(y-x)=\sum_{d=1}^{k-1}d(k-d)=\dfrac{k(k-1)(k+1)}{6},\]
so the average of $|p_{j+1}-p_j|$ is $\dfrac{2}{k(k-1)}\cdot\dfrac{k(k-1)(k+1)}{6}=\dfrac{k+1}{3}$. By linearity, $m_k=\dfrac{(k-1)(k+1)}{3}$, and
\[m_{2020}=\dfrac{2019\cdot2021}{3}=673\cdot2021=1360133 .\]

\textbf{Maximum.} Write $|p_{j+1}-p_j|$ as (larger) $-$ (smaller). Then the juiciness equals $\sum c_ip_i$, where each of the two end entries has coefficient $\pm1$, each interior entry has coefficient $2$, $0$, or $-2$, and the coefficients sum to $0$. The positive coefficients add to $k-1$, which is odd for $k=2m$; so exactly one end has $+1$ and the other $-1$, and the positive coefficients are $m-1$ twos and one $1$ (the negative ones likewise), with no zeros. By the rearrangement principle the sum is largest when the twos sit on the largest numbers and the $-2$'s on the smallest:
\[v_{2m}\le2\sum_{i=m+2}^{2m}i+(m+1)-m-2\sum_{i=1}^{m-1}i=(m-1)(3m+2)+1-(m-1)m=2m^2-1 .\]
This is attained by $m,\,2m,\,1,\,2m-1,\,2,\,2m-2,\,\dots,\,m-1,\,m+1$ (for example $2,4,1,3$ has juiciness $7$). So $v_k=\dfrac{k^2}{2}-1$ for even $k$, and $v_{2020}=2040199$.

Therefore $m_{2020}+v_{2020}=1360133+2040199=3400332$, with remainder $\boxed{\textbf{(A)}\ 332}$.
