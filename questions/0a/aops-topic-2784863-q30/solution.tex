The value 119 is impossible: reducing $ 13x+11y=119 $ modulo 11 gives $ 2x\equiv9 $, so $ x\equiv10\pmod{11}$, forcing $ 13x\ge130>119 $. For any integer $ N\ge120 $, choose $ x\in\{0,1,\ldots,10\}$ with $ 13x\equiv N\pmod{11}$. Then $ N-13x\ge-10 $ and is divisible by 11, so it is nonnegative. Thus every such $ N $ is representable, and $ n=119 $. Since $ n+1=120=2^3\cdot3\cdot5 $, its divisor count is $ 4\cdot2\cdot2=\boxed{16}$.
