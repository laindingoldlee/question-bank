Real numbers $x,y,z$ satisfy
\[
\dfrac{x}{y+z}+\dfrac{y}{z+x}+\dfrac{z}{x+y}=1
\]
(all denominators nonzero). Evaluate
\[
\dfrac{x^2}{y+z}+\dfrac{y^2}{z+x}+\dfrac{z^2}{x+y}.
\]
