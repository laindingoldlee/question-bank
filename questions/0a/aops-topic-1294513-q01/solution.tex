\textbf{Bound.} Suppose the marked points lie in $r$ different rows (horizontal lines) and $c$ different columns. In a row containing $m$ marked points there are at most $m-1$ horizontal adjacent pairs, so there are at most $100-r$ horizontal pairs in all; similarly at most $100-c$ vertical pairs. The points fit in an $r\times c$ array, so $rc\ge100$ and $r+c\ge2\sqrt{rc}\ge20$. Hence the number of adjacent pairs is at most $200-(r+c)\le180$.

\textbf{Construction.} A $10\times10$ square of points has $10\cdot9$ horizontal and $10\cdot9$ vertical pairs:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
for(int i=0;i<10;++i) for(int j=0;j<10;++j) dot((i,j),blue);
for(int i=0;i<9;++i) for(int j=0;j<10;++j){ draw((i,j)--(i+1,j),gray); draw((j,i)--(j,i+1),gray);}
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

So the maximum is $\boxed{180}$.
