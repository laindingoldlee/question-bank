All $8!$ orders are equally likely. Exactly two people stand between Goby and Bogy when their positions are $i$ and $i+3$ for some $i=1,\dots,5$: that is $5$ pairs of positions, $2$ ways to place Goby and Bogy in them, and $6!$ ways to arrange the others. So the probability is
\[
\dfrac{5\cdot2\cdot6!}{8!}=\dfrac{10}{56}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{28}} .
\]
