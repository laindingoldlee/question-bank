The intersections are the real roots of $h=f-g$, a polynomial of degree at most $2$.

If $h$ has degree $2$: $0$, $1$ or $2$ roots. If degree $1$: exactly $1$. If $h$ is a nonzero constant: $0$. If $h=0$, i.e. $f=g$: infinitely many. All cases occur (e.g. $x^2$ against $x^2+1$, $2x^2$, $x^2+x$, $2x^2-1$, $x^2$). So the possibilities are $\boxed{0,\ 1,\ 2,\ \text{or infinitely many}}$.
