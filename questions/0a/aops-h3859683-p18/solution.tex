If $p$ has degree $d>1$, then $p(p(x))$ has degree $d^2$, greater than the degree at most $d$ of the right side, impossible. For constant $p(x)=c$, the equation gives $c=6c+2025$, so $c=-405$. For $p(x)=ax+b$ with $a\ne 0$, comparison gives $a^2=14a$, hence $a=14$, and $(a+1)b=6b+2025$, hence $b=225$. Thus $|p(1)|$ is $405$ or $239$, whose sum is $\boxed{644}$.
