Five people have $5,4,3,2,1$ eggs; Emily has $1$. Repeatedly, two of the people who still have eggs are paired at random and play: at each fair coin flip one of them takes an egg from the other, the richer one on heads and the poorer one on tails, until one of the two has no eggs; that person is out. This continues until one person has all $15$ eggs. What is the probability that Emily ends with all $15$ eggs?
