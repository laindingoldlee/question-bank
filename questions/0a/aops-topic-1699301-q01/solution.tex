The maximum of two numbers is at least their average, so
\[
M\ge\dfrac{1009a^2+2018b+1009b^2+2018a}{2}=\dfrac{1009}{2}\left(a^2+2a+b^2+2b\right)=\dfrac{1009}{2}\left((a+1)^2+(b+1)^2-2\right)\ge-1009 .
\]
Equality requires $a=b=-1$, and then both numbers equal $1009-2018=-1009$, so $M=-1009$ is attained. The smallest possible value is $\boxed{-1009}$.
