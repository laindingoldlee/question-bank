$ARST$ is a right trapezoid. Draw the segment from $A$ parallel to $RS$; it meets $TS$ at a point at height $12$, forming a right triangle with horizontal leg $24$ and hypotenuse $AT=26$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s=0.2){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
pair R=(0,0),Sp=(24,0),A=(0,12),T=(24,22); draw(R--Sp,black); draw(R--A,blue); draw(Sp--T,red); draw(A--T); draw(A--(24,12),gray+dashed);
draw(ra(R,(1,0),(0,1),1.2)); draw(ra(Sp,(-1,0),(0,1),1.2));
label("$R$",R,SW); label("$S$",Sp,SE); label("$A$",A,W); label("$T$",T,NE); label("$24$",(12,0),S); label("$12$",(0,6),W); label("$26$",(A+T)/2,NW); label("$10$",(24,17),E,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Its vertical leg is $\sqrt{26^2-24^2}=10$, so $TS=12+10=\boxed{22}$.
