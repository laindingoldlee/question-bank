Let $y=x+\dfrac{1}{x}$. Then $(y-17)^2=y+17$, i.e. $y^2-35y+272=0$, with roots $y=\dfrac{35\pm\sqrt{137}}{2}$, both greater than $2$. For each such $y$, the equation $x^2-yx+1=0$ has two real roots (discriminant $y^2-4>0$) whose sum is $y$. So the sum of all real $x$ is the sum of the two values of $y$, which is $\boxed{35}$.
