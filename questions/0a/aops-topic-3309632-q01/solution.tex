Subtract two adjacent four-term sums: entries four positions apart have equal residues modulo $6$. The residues of the numbers are three $0$s, two $2$s, and two $4$s. Positions $(1,5),(2,6),(3,7)$ must be equal-residue pairs. Position $4$ must be the remaining residue $0$, and the three pairs use residues $0,2,4$ once each, in $3!$ ways. Every window then sums to $0+0+2+4\equiv0$. Arrange the actual numbers in their residue positions in $3!2!2!$ ways. Total $\boxed{3!\,3!\,2!\,2!=144}$.
