Each day uses one pair of students, and no pair may be used twice, so the number of days is at most the number of pairs:
\[\binom42=6 .\]
Six days are possible: with students $A,B,C,D$, use the pairs $AB$, $AC$, $AD$, $BC$, $BD$, $CD$ on six different days. The answer is $\boxed{6}$.
