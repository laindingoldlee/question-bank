At least one prime equals $19$; assume $p=19$. Then $qr=19+q+r$, or $(q-1)(r-1)=20$. Up to order, the factor pairs are $(1,20),(2,10),(4,5)$, giving $(q,r)=(2,21),(3,11),(5,6)$. Only $(3,11)$ consists of primes. Hence the sum is $19+3+11=\boxed{33}$.
