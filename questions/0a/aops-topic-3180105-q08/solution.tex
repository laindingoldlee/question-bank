Rotate the face 1 to the top;6 is then underneath. Rotate around the vertical axis to place 2 in front;5 is forced behind. The remaining pair 3, 4 can occupy the left and right faces in either order. These two arrangements are not related by a rotation fixing 1 on top and 2 in front. Thus $\boxed2$.

\end{asy}
