Every one of the ten factors contributes exactly two prime factors counted with multiplicity. Multiplying merely adds prime exponents, so their sum is always $10\cdot2=\boxed{20}$, independent of which feasible set maximizes the product. Feasible sets exist: $6,10,14,15,21,22,26,33,39,1838$ sum to 2024, and $1838=2\cdot919$ with 919 prime. There are finitely many positive-integer sets with the stated sum, so a maximum is attained.
