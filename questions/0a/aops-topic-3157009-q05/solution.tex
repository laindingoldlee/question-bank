Unequal powers yield unique binary numbers with exactly two one-bits. Both exponents lie in 0 through 10, and even the largest distinct pair sums to $ 1024+512=1536\le2015 $, giving $\binom{11}{2}=55 $ numbers. Equal powers yield $ 2,4,\ldots,1024 $, ten more. The two classes have different binary one-bit counts, so the total is $\boxed{65}$.
