First $2^{20}-2^{18}=4\cdot2^{18}-2^{18}=3\cdot2^{18}$, so
\[
2^{20}-2^{18}-3=3\left(2^{18}-1\right).
\]
By the difference of squares and the sum and difference of cubes,
\[
2^{18}-1=(2^9+1)(2^9-1),\qquad 2^9+1=(2^3+1)(2^6-2^3+1)=9\cdot57,\qquad 2^9-1=(2^3-1)(2^6+2^3+1)=7\cdot73 .
\]
Since $57=3\cdot19$,
\[
2^{20}-2^{18}-3=3\cdot9\cdot3\cdot19\cdot7\cdot73=3^4\cdot7\cdot19\cdot73 .
\]
The number of divisors is $(4+1)(1+1)(1+1)(1+1)=\boxed{40}$.
