Extend $AD$ through $D$ to $A'$ with $DA'=DA$. The circle centred at $D$ through $A$ also contains $H$ and has diameter $AA'$. Thus $AH\perp HA'$. Since $AH\perp BM$ and $H$ lies on $BM$, points $B,M,H,A'$ are collinear. Triangles $MDA'$ and $MCB$ are similar because $DA'\parallel CB$ and their other sides lie on the same two lines. Therefore $\dfrac{AD}{18}=\dfrac{DA'}{CB}=\dfrac{MD}{MC}=\dfrac{11}{9}$, giving $\boxed{AD=22}$.
