Three people take afternoon naps.
\begin{itemize}
\item Brien takes two naps of $1$ hour each, starting at $2\!:\!00$ and at $5\!:\!00$.
\item Jill takes three naps of $30$ minutes each: the first starts at $1\!:\!45$, the second starts three hours after the start of her first nap, and the third starts $45$ minutes after the start of her second nap.
\item Bob takes two naps of $90$ minutes each: the first starts $5$ minutes after Jill falls asleep for the first time, and the second starts $10$ minutes after Brien falls asleep for the second time.
\end{itemize}
For how many minutes in total are all three of them asleep at the same time?
