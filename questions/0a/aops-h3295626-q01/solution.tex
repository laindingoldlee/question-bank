The center $O$ lies on the perpendicular bisector through $C,D$. If the radius is $r$, the distance $OC=r-6$. The right triangle with legs $AC=12$, $OC=r-6$ gives
\[
12^2+(r-6)^2=r^2.
\]
Thus $144+36=12r$, and the radius is $\boxed{15\text{ cm}}$.

\end{asy}
