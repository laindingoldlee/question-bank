Multiply the harmonic sum by $p!$. For $1\le k<p$, the integer $\dfrac{p!}{k}$ is divisible by $p$, whereas $\dfrac{p!}{p}=(p-1)!$ is not divisible by $p$. Hence the resulting integer is nonzero modulo $p$. If the harmonic sum were an integer, its product with $p!$ would be divisible by $p$, a contradiction.
