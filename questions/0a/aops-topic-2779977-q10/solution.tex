Swapping $ y,z $ reverses the sign of every paired contribution, so $ f(x,z,y)=-f(x,y,z)$. Pair the six permutations by keeping their first entry fixed and swapping the remaining two. Each pair sums to zero, so the total is $\boxed0 $.
