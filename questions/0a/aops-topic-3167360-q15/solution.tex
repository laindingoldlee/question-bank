Let $u=\sqrt[3]x,v=\sqrt[3]y$. Then $u+v=2$ and $20=u^3+v^3=8-6uv$, so $uv=-2$. Thus $u,v=1\pm\sqrt3$. Cubing gives $x,y=10\pm6\sqrt3$, and the maximum is $\boxed{10+6\sqrt3}$.
