The three-digit squares between $100$ and $199$ are $100,121,144,169,196$. We need two of them whose last two digits are reverses of each other, with $a<b$. Among $00,21,44,69,96$ the only such pair with distinct digits is $69$ and $96$. So $\overline{1ab}=169=13^2$ and $\overline{1ba}=196=14^2$, giving $\boxed{a=6,\ b=9}$.
