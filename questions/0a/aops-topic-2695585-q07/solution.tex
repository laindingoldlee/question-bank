$(x-88)(y+66)=23333-88\cdot66=17525=5^2\cdot701$. Since $y+66>0$, $x-88=d>0$, and $y+66=\dfrac{17525}d>66$ forces $d\le265$, so $d\in\{1,5,25\}$. The solutions are $\boxed{(x,y)=(89,17459),\ (93,3439),\ (113,635)}$.
