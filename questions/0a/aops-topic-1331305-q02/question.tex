There are $1000$ people in a food fight, numbered $1,2,\dots,1000$. Whenever person $A$ hits person $B$, person $B$ also hits person $A$ (in revenge), and nobody hits the same person twice or hits themselves. A student claims that it is possible for every person $n$ to have hit exactly $n$ different people. Show that this is impossible.
