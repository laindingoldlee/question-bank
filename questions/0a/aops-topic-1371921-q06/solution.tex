The minute hand turns $6^\circ$ per minute and the hour hand $0.5^\circ$ per minute, so the minute hand gains $5.5^\circ$ per minute. At 2:00 the minute hand is $60^\circ$ behind the hour hand. The angle between them first shrinks to $0^\circ$ and then grows; it is next $60^\circ$ when the minute hand is $60^\circ$ ahead, i.e.\ after it has gained $120^\circ$:
\[
t=\dfrac{120}{5.5}=\dfrac{240}{11}\text{ minutes}=\dfrac{14400}{11}\text{ seconds}\approx1309.09\text{ seconds}.
\]
To the nearest whole number, $\boxed{1309}$ seconds (at about 2:21:49).
