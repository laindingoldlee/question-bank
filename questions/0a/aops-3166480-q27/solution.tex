Let $W(n)$ mean the current player can force a win. Set $W(0)=\cdots=W(6)=\mathrm{false}$. For larger $n$, $W(n)$ holds exactly when at least one legal predecessor $n-7,n-13$ is losing. Evaluating this recurrence in increasing order gives the losing positions in $180,\ldots,199$ as $180,181,182,183,184,185,186$. The largest is therefore $\boxed{186}$. This finite computation is reproducible by setting each entry to $(n\ge7\text{ and }\neg W(n-7))\text{ or }(n\ge13\text{ and }\neg W(n-13))$; every entry uses only earlier certified entries.
