Factor: $450=2\cdot3^2\cdot5^2=2\cdot15^2$. Hence
\[
\sqrt[4]{450}=\sqrt[4]{15^2}\cdot\sqrt[4]{2}=\boxed{\sqrt{15}\cdot\sqrt[4]{2}}
\]
(using $\sqrt[4]{15^2}=\sqrt{15}$), so $m=15$, $n=2$.

As a single fourth root, $\sqrt[4]{450}$ is already simplified in the usual sense: $450$ has no factor that is a perfect fourth power other than $1$ (each prime appears at most twice), so nothing can be taken outside the fourth root as an integer. The form $\sqrt{15}\,\sqrt[4]2$ is an equivalent mixed form; which one counts as "simplest" is a matter of convention, and contest answers normally use $\sqrt[4]{450}$ or ask for a specific form.
