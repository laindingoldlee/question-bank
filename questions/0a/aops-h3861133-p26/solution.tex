As a quadratic in $x$, the equation is $x^2+(2y-100)x+y^2-y=0$. Integer roots require $2500-99y=z^2$ for an integer $z\ge0$, and then $x=50-y\pm z$. Since $y<-100$, we need $z\ge112$. The congruence $z^2\equiv25\pmod{99}$ gives $z\equiv5,49,50,94\pmod{99}$ by combining residues modulo nine and eleven. The least allowable $z$ is $148$, giving $y=-196$ and the larger root $x=394$. Hence $\boxed{(x_2,y)=(394,-196)}$.
