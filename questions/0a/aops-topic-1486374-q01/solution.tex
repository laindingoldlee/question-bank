Note $343=7^3$ and $2^{147}=8^{49}=(1+7)^{49}$. By the binomial theorem,
\[
(1+7)^{49}=1+\dbinom{49}{1}7+\dbinom{49}{2}7^2+\sum_{j\ge3}\dbinom{49}{j}7^j .
\]
Every term with $j\ge3$ is divisible by $7^3$. Also $\dbinom{49}{1}\cdot7=49\cdot7=7^3$ and $\dbinom{49}{2}7^2=\dfrac{49\cdot48}{2}\cdot49=7^4\cdot24$. Hence
\[
2^{147}-1=(1+7)^{49}-1
\]
is a sum of multiples of $7^3$, so $343\mid 2^{147}-1$. $\blacksquare$

(In fact, by the same expansion, $2^{147}-1$ is divisible by $7^3$ but not by $7^4$.)
