Each full turn moves the circle forward by its circumference $2\pi r$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real r=0.6; draw((-0.5,0)--(5.2,0)); draw(circle((0.2,r),r),blue); draw(circle((4.4,r),r),blue+dashed);
draw((0.2,r)--(0.2,0),red); label("$r$",(0.2,r/2),E,red); draw((0.2,-0.25)--(4.4,-0.25),Arrows); label("$8$ m after $8$ turns",(2.3,-0.25),S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

So $8\cdot2\pi r=8$, giving $r=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2\pi}\text{ m}}\approx0.159$ m.
