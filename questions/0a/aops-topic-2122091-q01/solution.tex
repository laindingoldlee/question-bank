Let $t=\sqrt{4x+8y-z}\ge0$, so that $z=4x+8y-t^2$. Substituting for $z$ on the left,
\[2x^2+y^2-4x-8y+t^2=2t-19,\]
that is,
\[\left(2x^2-4x\right)+\left(y^2-8y\right)+\left(t^2-2t\right)+19=0 .\]
Completing the squares,
\[2(x-1)^2-2+(y-4)^2-16+(t-1)^2-1+19=0,\qquad\text{so}\qquad 2(x-1)^2+(y-4)^2+(t-1)^2=0 .\]
A sum of nonnegative terms is $0$ only if each term is $0$, so $x=1$, $y=4$ and $t=1$. Then $z=4+32-1=35$ and
\[x+y+z=1+4+35=\boxed{40}.\]
(Check: $4x+8y-z=1$, and both sides equal $2+16-35=-17=2\cdot1-19$.)
