Call the sum $T$. The terms are $2^{26-k}3^k$ for $k=0,1,\dots,26$, a geometric progression with ratio $\dfrac{3}{2}$. Using $a^{n+1}-b^{n+1}=(a-b)(a^n+a^{n-1}b+\cdots+b^n)$ with $a=3$, $b=2$, $n=26$:
\[
T=\dfrac{3^{27}-2^{27}}{3-2}=3^{27}-2^{27}.
\]
Since $3^{27}=7625597484987$ and $2^{27}=134217728$,
\[
T=\boxed{3^{27}-2^{27}=7625463267259}.
\]
