Answer these scoring questions for a mathematical game.
\begin{enumerate}
\item There are $138$ integer guesses whose sum is $6838$. Round their mean to the nearest integer. A guess earns $\max(0,5-|\text{guess}-\text{rounded mean}|)$ points. What does a guess of $47$ earn?
\item Of $138$ respondents, $81,29,28$ choose options A, B, C respectively. A earns $1$ point; B earns $3$ points only if fewer than $25\%$ choose B; C earns $5$ only if fewer than $7.5\%$ choose C. Find the three scores.
\item A pot of $20$ points is shared equally by $17$ people. Compare each share with the expected score from a $20\%$ chance of winning $5$ points and otherwise winning zero.
\item In rock-paper-scissors, $34$ choose rock, $73$ paper, and $31$ scissors. A choice scores the number it beats minus the number that beats it, with a minimum score of $-5$. Find the score for each choice.
\item Choose three of eight options uniformly. One specified option awards $3$ points if chosen and another specified option awards $5$ if chosen. Find the expected total.
\item Exactly one of ten options is correct, and a correct answer earns $3$ points. Find the expected score of a uniform random guess.
\item The $138$ players have a total of $2000$ points before a final question. Round their mean. A guess earns $\max(0,8-|\text{guess}-\text{rounded mean}|)$ points. What does a guess of $12$ earn?
\end{enumerate}
