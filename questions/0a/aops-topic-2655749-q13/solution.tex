Check the faces at $A$:
\[AB^2+AC^2=4+9=13=BC^2,\quad AB^2+AD^2=4+16=20=BD^2,\quad AC^2+AD^2=9+16=25=CD^2.\]
By the converse of the Pythagorean theorem, the angles $BAC$, $BAD$ and $CAD$ are all right angles, so $AB$, $AC$, $AD$ are mutually perpendicular. Taking $ABC$ as the base (area $\dfrac12\cdot2\cdot3=3$) and $AD=4$ as the height,
\[V=\dfrac13\cdot3\cdot4=\boxed{4}.\]

dot(Ap);label("$A$",(0.3,0.3),NE);label("$B$",Bp,SW);label("$C$",Cp,E);label("$D$",Dp,N);

\end{asy}
