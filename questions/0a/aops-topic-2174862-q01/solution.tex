Since $a^3+b^3=(a+b)^3-3ab(a+b)=343-21ab$, we want $ab$ as small as possible. The possible products are $1\cdot6=6$, $2\cdot5=10$, $3\cdot4=12$, so the minimum is $6$ and the maximum of $a^3+b^3$ is
\[343-21\cdot6=\boxed{217}\qquad(\text{at }\{a,b\}=\{1,6\}).\]
