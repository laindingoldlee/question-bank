Xavier and Yaxley go to a restaurant that sells only pizza slices, at $2$ dollars each, and chocolate eclairs, at $3$ dollars each. When they enter, they have $112$ dollars together, and the square of Xavier's money (in dollars) is twice Yaxley's money. When they leave, the square of Yaxley's money is twice Xavier's money. They bought the same number of pizza slices, and Xavier bought nothing else. What is the greatest number of eclairs Yaxley could have bought?
