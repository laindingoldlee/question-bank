An equilateral triangle inscribed in a circle of radius $x$ has side $x\sqrt3$, so its area is $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}\left(x\sqrt3\right)^2=\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{4}x^2$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real x=1.6; pair O=(0,0),A=x*dir(90),B=x*dir(210),C=x*dir(330);
fill(A--B--C--cycle,lightblue); draw(circle(O,x)); draw(A--B--C--cycle,blue); draw(O--C,red);
label("$x$",(O+C)/2,N,red); label("$A$",A,N); label("$B$",B,SW); label("$C$",C,SE); dot("$O$",O,W);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The circle has area $\pi x^2$, so the ratio is
\[
\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{4\pi}\approx0.4135,
\]
that is, $\boxed{\dfrac{75\sqrt3}{\pi}\%\approx41.3\%}$. The given area $18$ only fixes $x$ (with $x^2=\dfrac{24}{\sqrt3}=8\sqrt3$) and does not affect the percentage.
