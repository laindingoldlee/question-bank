A racetrack has two straightaways of $100$ metres and two semicircular ends with diameter $40$ metres. A racecar completes $4$ laps in $6$ minutes. What is its average speed, in metres per minute, to the nearest metre per minute? Use $3.14159$ for $\pi$.
