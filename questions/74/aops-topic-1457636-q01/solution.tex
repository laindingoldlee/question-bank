Let the solutions be $r_1,r_2$. By Vieta, $r_1+r_2=-8$ and $r_1r_2=k$, so
\[
|r_1-r_2|=\sqrt{(r_1+r_2)^2-4r_1r_2}=\sqrt{64-4k},\qquad r_1^2+r_2^2=(r_1+r_2)^2-2r_1r_2=64-2k .
\]
Put $u=\sqrt{64-4k}\ge0$, so $k=\dfrac{64-u^2}{4}$ and $64-2k=32+\dfrac{u^2}{2}$. The condition $10u=32+\dfrac{u^2}{2}$ becomes $u^2-20u+64=0$, so $u=4$ or $u=16$, giving
\[
k=\dfrac{64-16}{4}=12\qquad\text{or}\qquad k=\dfrac{64-256}{4}=-48 .
\]
Both give two real solutions ($x=-2,-6$ and $x=4,-12$; check: $10\cdot4=40=4+36$ and $10\cdot16=160=16+144$). The sum is $12+(-48)=\boxed{-36}$.
