A round passes without a winner with probability $q=(\dfrac{11}{12})^3(\dfrac{7}{12})=\dfrac{9317}{20736}$. The eventual probability is $w(1+q+q^2+\cdots)=\dfrac{w}{1-q}=\dfrac{6655}{11419}$.
