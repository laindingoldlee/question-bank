The altitude to the side $14$ splits it into $5$ and $9$, with height $12$ (right triangles $5$-$12$-$13$ and $9$-$12$-$15$). So the area is $\dfrac12\cdot14\cdot12=84$. The semiperimeter is $21$, and area $=rs$ gives $r=\dfrac{84}{21}=\boxed{4}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0),C=(14,0),A=(5,12);pair I=(14*A+15*B+13*C)/42;
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.8));draw(circle(I,4));draw(A--(5,0),dashed);dot(I);
label("$13$",(A+B)/2,NW,fontsize(8pt));label("$15$",(A+C)/2,NE,fontsize(8pt));label("$14$",(B+C)/2,S,fontsize(8pt));label("$12$",(5,6),W,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}
