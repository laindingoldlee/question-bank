The base 67 is coprime to 100, and direct squaring gives $67^{20}\equiv1\pmod{100}$. The outer exponent is $E=65^K$, where $K$ is a positive odd integer. Modulo 20, $65\equiv5$ and every positive power of 5 is congruent to 5. Thus $E\equiv5\pmod{20}$ and the answer is $67^5\pmod{100}$. Since $67^2\equiv89$ and $67^4\equiv21$, this gives $21\cdot67=1407\equiv\boxed7\pmod{100}$.
