A quadratic $ax^2+bx+c$ has two distinct real roots exactly when its discriminant is positive. Here
\[
\Delta=(2p)^2-4\cdot3\cdot4=4p^2-48>0\iff p^2>12\iff |p|>2\sqrt3 .
\]
So $\boxed{p<-2\sqrt3\ \text{or}\ p>2\sqrt3}$. (If a repeated root counted as ``two real roots'', the condition would be $|p|\ge2\sqrt3$.) Note that the answer is a range of values, not a single value.
