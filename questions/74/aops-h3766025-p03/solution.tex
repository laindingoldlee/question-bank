Every displayed exponent on a variable is odd, and $t^{2k+1}\equiv t\pmod3$ for any integer $t$. Thus the second left side is congruent to $a+b+c+d+e\equiv2^{2022}\equiv1\pmod3$. But $20222021\equiv2\pmod3$, so its odd power is $2$ modulo $3$. This contradiction proves that there are $\boxed{\text{no solutions}}$.
