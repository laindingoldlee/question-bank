Exactly three divisors force the integer to be $p^2$ for a prime $p$: the divisor-count formula must be $3$. But a square has units digit among $0,1,4,5,6,9$, never $3$. Therefore $\boxed{\text{no such integer exists}}$, regardless of the palindrome condition.
