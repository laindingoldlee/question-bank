Temporarily distinguish the dice. Choose the two contacting face values in $6^2$ ways and their relative quarter-turn orientation in four ways, giving $144$ configurations. Forgetting the distinction identifies each with its swapped version. A swapped configuration is fixed precisely when the face values agree; for each of the six common values all four orientations are fixed (the swap reverses the normal as well as exchanging the two face frames). Thus there are $24$ fixed configurations. Burnside\textquotesingle s lemma gives $\dfrac{144+24}{2} = \boxed{84}$. Equivalently, unequal face pairs give $\binom62\cdot4=60$, and equal pairs give $6\cdot4=24$.
