For numbers $a,b$, the difference is $(a+b)^2-|a-b|^2=(a+b)^2-(a-b)^2=4ab$. Thus division by $\boxed4$ always gives $ab$. A pair with nonzero product, such as $a=b=1$, also shows no other constant can work.
