$\dfrac ab+\dfrac cd=\dfrac{ad+bc}{bd}$ is an integer, so $b\mid ad+bc$, hence $b\mid ad$. As $\gcd(a,b)=1$, $b\mid d$. Symmetrically $d\mid bc$ gives $d\mid b$. Therefore $\boxed{|b|=|d|}$, i.e. $b=\pm d$. (Conversely, $\dfrac12+\dfrac12=1$ shows this can happen.)
