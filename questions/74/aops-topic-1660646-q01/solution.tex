\textbf{As grouping.} $a\div b$ asks how many copies of $b$ make $a$. Each whole unit contains two halves, so $25$ units contain $25\cdot2=50$ halves. Hence $25\div0.5=50$. (Dividing by a number less than $1$ gives a result larger than the dividend, because the pieces are smaller than $1$.)

\textbf{Without decimals.} Multiplying the dividend and the divisor by the same nonzero number does not change the quotient, so
\[
25\div0.5=\dfrac{25}{0.5}=\dfrac{250}{5}=\boxed{50}.
\]
Check: $50\cdot0.5=25$. (In $4\div2=2$ the same reading works: two groups of $2$ make $4$.)
