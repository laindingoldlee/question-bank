We split according to the first card.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{First card the king of hearts} ($1$ way). The second card is one of the other $3$ kings. Of the $50$ cards left, $4$ are queens, so the third card has $46$ choices. Total $3\cdot46=138$.
\item \textbf{First card the queen of hearts} ($1$ way). The second card is any of the $4$ kings. Of the $50$ cards left, $3$ are queens, so the third card has $47$ choices. Total $4\cdot47=188$.
\item \textbf{First card one of the other $11$ hearts.} The second card is any of the $4$ kings, and of the $50$ cards left $4$ are queens, so the third card has $46$ choices. Total $11\cdot4\cdot46=2024$.
\end{itemize}
Altogether there are $138+188+2024=\boxed{2350}$ such draws.
