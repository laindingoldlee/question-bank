Twelve-place repetition means $\dfrac{1}{n}=\dfrac{K}{10^{12}-1}$ for an integer $K$, so $n$ must divide $10^{12}-1$. Conversely, each divisor $n>1$ gives such a repeating block, padded with leading zeros when needed. Factor $10^{12}-1=(10^6-1)(10^6+1)=3^3\cdot7\cdot11\cdot13\cdot37\cdot101\cdot9901$. There are $(3+1)2^6=256$ positive divisors. Excluding $n=1$ leaves $\boxed{255}$.
