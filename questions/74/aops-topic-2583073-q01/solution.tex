Let $y=3^x>0$. The equation becomes
\[243y+\dfrac{2}{y^2}=1467\qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad 243y^3-1467y^2+2=0.\]
By the rational root theorem, any rational root has the form $\dfrac{p}{q}$ with $p\mid2$ and $q\mid243=3^5$. Also $1467=9+1458=3^2+2\cdot3^6$, which suggests $y=\dfrac1{27}$; indeed then $243y=9$ and $\dfrac2{y^2}=1458$. Dividing by $27y-1$ gives the factorisation
\[243y^3-1467y^2+2=(27y-1)\left(9y^2-54y-2\right).\]
The quadratic factor has roots $y=\dfrac{54\pm\sqrt{54^2+72}}{18}=3\pm\dfrac{\sqrt{83}}{3}$. Only $3+\dfrac{\sqrt{83}}3=\dfrac{9+\sqrt{83}}{3}$ is positive.

So $3^x=\dfrac1{27}$ or $3^x=\dfrac{9+\sqrt{83}}3$, that is,
\[\boxed{x=-3\quad\text{or}\quad x=\log_3\left(9+\sqrt{83}\right)-1\approx1.637.}\]
Check for $x=-3$: $3^2+2\cdot3^6=9+1458=1467$.
