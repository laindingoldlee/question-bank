Let $p_k$ be H's winning probability when holding $k$ tokens. Then $p_0=0$, $p_4=1$, and $p_k=\dfrac{p_{k-1}+p_{k+1}}{2}$ for $1\le k\le3$. Thus successive differences are equal, giving $p_k=\dfrac{k}{4}$. Initially $k=3$, so the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{3}{4}}$.
