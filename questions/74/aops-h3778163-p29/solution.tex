The left side exceeds $7$, so $y\ge3$. Negative $x$ would put it strictly between $7$ and $8$, impossible. At $x=0$ we get $(0,3)$. For $x\ge1$, reduction modulo $3$ forces $y$ even; modulo $8$ then forces $x$ even. Write $x=2k,y=2m$ and factor
\[
(2^m-3^k)(2^m+3^k)=7.
\]
The positive integer factors must be $1,7$, giving $2^m=4$ and $3^k=3$. Thus the other solution is $(2,4)$, and the largest sum is $\boxed6$.
