In the vertex form $f(x)=a(x-h)^2+k$ the vertex is $(h,k)$. Here $(x-2)^2\ge0$ with equality only at $x=2$, so the minimum value $6$ is reached at $x=2$; the vertex is $(2,6)$ and its $x$-coordinate is $\boxed{2}$.
