The terms are $\dfrac{2}{k(k+1)}=2(\dfrac{1}{k}-\dfrac{1}{k+1})$ for $k=1,\ldots,6$. Telescoping gives $2(1-\dfrac{1}{7})=\boxed{\dfrac{12}{7}}$.
