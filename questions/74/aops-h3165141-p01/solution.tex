Measure grass in cow-days. Let initial grass be $G$ and daily growth $g$. The two depletion experiments give $G+10g=1000$ and $G+8g=100\cdot5+120\cdot3=860$. Subtracting gives $g=70$, then $G=300$. At most $70$ cows can be supported forever: at that count growth matches consumption and the positive initial stock persists, while any larger count eventually exhausts it. Thus $\boxed{70}$.
