For $ n=2020,2021,\ldots,2025 $, the digit sums are 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, so each has $ g(n)=1 $. But 2026 has digit sum 10 and then 1, so $ g(2026)=2 $. The first transition is therefore at $\boxed{n=2025}$.
