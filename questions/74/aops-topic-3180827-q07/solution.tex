Each square side is at most 3. Three or fewer square areas from $\{1,4,9\}$ cannot total 15: if 9 occurs, the remaining 6 cannot be expressed using at most two of 1 or 4, and without 9 the sum is at most 12. Four squares do work: one 3-by 3 square, one 2-by 2 square, and two 1-by 1 squares. Thus $\boxed4$.
