Write the odd integers as $2a+1$ and $2b+1$ with integers $a,b$. Then
\[(2a+1)(2b+1)=4ab+2a+2b+1=2(2ab+a+b)+1\]
is odd, and
\[(2a+1)+(2b+1)=2(a+b+1)\]
is even. $\blacksquare$
