Place $B=(0,0)$, $C=(14,0)$. From $AB=13$, $AC=15$, the vertex is $A=(5,12)$. Write $D=(14+d,0)$ with $d=CD>0$. Line $AD$ has slope $-\dfrac{12}{9+d}$, so the two lines perpendicular to it have slope $\dfrac{9+d}{12}$. On the vertical line $x=5$, their heights are $\dfrac{5(9+d)}{12}$ and $-\dfrac{9(9+d)}{12}$. Therefore $H_1H_2=\dfrac{14(9+d)}{12}=1001$, giving $9+d=858$ and $\boxed{CD=849}$.
