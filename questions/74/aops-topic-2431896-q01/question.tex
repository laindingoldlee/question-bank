A sequence is defined by its first term $a_1$ and the rule $a_{i+1}=x\,a_i+n$ for $i\ge1$, where $x$ and $n$ are constants.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find a closed formula for $a_i$ when $x\ne1$, and when $x=1$.
\item[(b)] For $x\ne1$, show that $a_i-\dfrac{n}{1-x}$ is a geometric sequence.
\end{enumerate}
