\textbf{(a)} Unrolling the rule,
\[a_i=a_1x^{i-1}+n\left(x^{i-2}+x^{i-3}+\cdots+x+1\right),\]
which is proved by induction: it holds for $i=1$ (empty sum), and if it holds for $i$ then $x\,a_i+n=a_1x^i+n\left(x^{i-1}+\cdots+x\right)+n$, the formula for $i+1$. Summing the geometric series,
\[\boxed{a_i=a_1x^{i-1}+n\cdot\dfrac{x^{i-1}-1}{x-1}\quad(x\ne1),\qquad a_i=a_1+(i-1)n\quad(x=1).}\]
\textbf{(b)} Let $c=\dfrac{n}{1-x}$, so that $c=xc+n$. Subtracting this from $a_{i+1}=xa_i+n$ gives
\[a_{i+1}-c=x\,(a_i-c),\]
so $a_i-c$ is geometric with ratio $x$: $a_i=c+(a_1-c)x^{i-1}$, which is the formula of (a) rearranged.
