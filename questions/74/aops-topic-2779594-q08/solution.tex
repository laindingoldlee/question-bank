Let the sides be $h,s$. Six equilateral triangles give hexagon area $\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}h^2=s^2$. Therefore $\left(\dfrac{h}{s}\right)^2=\dfrac{2}{3\sqrt3}=\boxed{\dfrac{2\sqrt3}{9}}$.
