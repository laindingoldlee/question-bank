There are $2015$ squares in a row, and a pawn stands on the middle square. Two players alternate moves. On the $k$-th move of the game ($k=1,2,3,\dots$), the player whose turn it is moves the pawn exactly $2^{k-1}$ squares to the left or to the right, staying on the board. So the first player moves $1$ square, the second player $2$ squares, the first player $4$ squares, and so on. A player who cannot move loses. Which player has a winning strategy?
