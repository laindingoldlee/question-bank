Ten good batteries and three dead batteries are mixed up. Jack tests them one by one, in random order, without replacement. Before testing the fifth battery he realises that he does not remember whether the first battery he tested was good or dead; all he remembers is that the second, third and fourth batteries he tested were all good. Given this information, what is the probability that the first battery was also good?
