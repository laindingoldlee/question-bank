First, $x^2-13x+36=(x-4)(x-9)$, so the roots of $t^2-13t+36=0$ are $t=4$ and $t=9$.

The second equation is the first one with $t=7x+1$ in place of the variable. So it holds exactly when $7x+1=4$ or $7x+1=9$:
\[
7x+1=4\ \Longrightarrow\ x=\dfrac{3}{7},\qquad 7x+1=9\ \Longrightarrow\ x=\dfrac{8}{7} .
\]
The solutions are $\boxed{x=\dfrac{3}{7}\text{ and }x=\dfrac{8}{7}}$.
