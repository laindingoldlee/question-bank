Harry has a magic cube. To open it, he must write integers on each of the $8$ corners, $12$ edges, and $6$ faces. The cube will unlock if

$\bullet$  the number on each edge is the sum of the two corners it connects,

$\bullet$  the number on each face is the sum of the four corners around it, and

$\bullet$  the sum of all $8+12+6 = 26$ numbers is $2024$.

Can Harry unlock the cube?
