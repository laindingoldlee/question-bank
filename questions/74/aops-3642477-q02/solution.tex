Let S be the sum of the eight corner integers. In the grand total, each corner is counted once directly, three times through its incident edges, and three times through its incident faces. Thus the total must be $7S$. But $2024\equiv1\pmod7$, so it cannot equal $7S$ for integer S. Therefore Harry cannot unlock the cube.
