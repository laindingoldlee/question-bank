There are 36 equally likely ordered pairs. For (a), the favourable pairs are $(1,0),(2,1),(3,2),(4,3),(5,4)$, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{5}{36}}$. For (b), their five reversals also qualify, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{5}{18}}$.
