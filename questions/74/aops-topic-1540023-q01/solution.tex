All $\dbinom{52}{5}=2598960$ hands are equally likely. A hand with exactly two aces consists of $2$ of the $4$ aces and $3$ of the $48$ other cards:
\[
\dbinom42\dbinom{48}{3}=6\cdot17296=103776 .
\]
So the probability is
\[
\dfrac{103776}{2598960}=\boxed{\dfrac{2162}{54145}}\approx0.0399 .
\]
