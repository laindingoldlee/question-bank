Let the digits be $a,b,c,d,e$, with $a\ge1$. Put $u=a-1\ge0$. Then $u+b+c+d+e=8$. Every nonnegative solution automatically respects digit bounds, because each variable is at most $8$, so $a\le9$. Stars and bars gives $\boxed{\binom{12}4=495}$. Defining the shifted variable explicitly ensures that the new sum is not mistakenly treated as an equation in the original digits.
