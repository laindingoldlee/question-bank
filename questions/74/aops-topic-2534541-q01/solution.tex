Let $x$ be the given number and $u=\dfrac{\sqrt[3]3}{\sqrt[3]2}=\sqrt[3]{\dfrac32}$. Dividing the numerator and the denominator by $\sqrt[3]2$,
\[x=\dfrac{3-u}{u-1}.\]
Then $x(u-1)=3-u$, so $u(x+1)=x+3$. Here $x+1\ne0$ (otherwise $0=2$), so $u=\dfrac{x+3}{x+1}$. Cubing and using $u^3=\dfrac32$:
\[2(x+3)^3=3(x+1)^3,\]
\[2x^3+18x^2+54x+54=3x^3+9x^2+9x+3,\]
\[x^3-9x^2-45x-51=0.\]
So $x$ is a root of the cubic. $\blacksquare$ (Numerically $x\approx12.82$.)
