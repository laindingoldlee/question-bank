Let $ABC$ be a right triangle with $\angle B=90^\circ$, and let $BH$ be the altitude to the hypotenuse $AC$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that no such triangle satisfies $AH=CH+AB$.
\item[(b)] Find the angles of the triangle if instead $CH=AH+AB$.
\end{enumerate}
