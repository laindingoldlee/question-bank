The remainder is a regular octahedron. Its six vertices are the original edge midpoints. Four original faces leave central triangles, and four cuts create four more triangular faces, totaling 8. Each edge belongs to two triangular faces, so there are $\dfrac{3(8)}{2}=12$ edges. The sum is $6+12+8=\boxed{26}$.
