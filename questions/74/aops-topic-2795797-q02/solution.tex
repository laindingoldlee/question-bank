Each mixed pair has one man and one woman, so the number of men sitting with women equals the number of women sitting with men: $\dfrac25M=\dfrac23W$, i.e. $M=\dfrac53W$. With $W=3t$ and $M=5t$, the fraction of men is $\dfrac{5t}{8t}=\boxed{\dfrac58}$.
