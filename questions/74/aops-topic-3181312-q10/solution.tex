At each clearing, some untouched area remains and has the deepest snow, so all seven cleared patches are distinct. The total snowfall is $400\cdot\dfrac{7}{6}=\dfrac{1400}{3}$ cubic feet. At hour $k$, the removed patch has depth $\dfrac{k}{6}$ feet, giving total removal $\dfrac{25}{6}(1+\cdots+7)=\dfrac{350}{3}$. The remaining volume is $\boxed{350\text{ cubic feet}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw((0,0)--(8,0)--(8,8)--(0,8)--cycle);for(int i=0;i<7;++i){int c=i%4,r=quotient(i,4);filldraw((2*c,2*r)--(2*c+2,2*r)--(2*c+2,2*r+2)--(2*c,2*r+2)--cycle,lightgray);label(string(i+1),(2*c+1,2*r+1));}label("seven distinct cleared patches",(4,-.7));
\end{asy}
