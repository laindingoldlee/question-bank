Let $\alpha,\beta$ be the roots of $t^2-3t+1$, so $\alpha\beta=1$. Since $2025$ is odd, squaring permutes the nontrivial $2025$th roots. Therefore
\[
\prod_{z\in S}(z^4-3z^2+1)
=\prod_{z\in S}(z-\alpha)(z-\beta)
=\dfrac{(\alpha^{2025}-1)(\beta^{2025}-1)}{(\alpha-1)(\beta-1)}
=\alpha^{2025}+\beta^{2025}-2.
\]
This is positive. Set $L_n=\alpha^n+\beta^n$. Then $L_0=2$, $L_1=3$, and $L_{n+1}=3L_n-L_{n-1}$. Iterating this integer recurrence modulo $1000$ through $n=2025$ gives $L_{2025}\equiv498$. It can also be evaluated quickly with
\[
L_{2n}=L_n^2-2,\qquad L_{2n+1}=L_nL_{n+1}-3.
\]
Thus $\boxed{P\equiv496\pmod{1000}}$.
