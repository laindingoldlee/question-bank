Let $p_k=x^k+y^k+z^k$. From the first two sums, $xy+yz+zx=\dfrac{1-3}{2}=-1$. Newton's third identity gives $xyz=1$. Thus each variable is a root of $t^3-t^2-t-1$, and
\[
p_k=p_{k-1}+p_{k-2}+p_{k-3}\qquad(k\ge4).
\]
Starting from $p_1=1,p_2=3,p_3=7$, the successive values are
\[
p_4=11,\ p_5=21,\ p_6=39,\ p_7=71,\ p_8=131,\ p_9=241,\ p_{10}=443,\ p_{11}=815.
\]
Thus the reduced fraction is $\dfrac{815}{1}$, and $\boxed{m+n\equiv16\pmod{100}}$.
