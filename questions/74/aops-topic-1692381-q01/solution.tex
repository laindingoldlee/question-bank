The rise is $125-62=63$ feet and the run is $7.5$ yards $=22.5$ feet (the units must match). So
\[
\text{slope}=\dfrac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}}=\dfrac{63}{22.5}=\boxed{2.8}.
\]

(Figure not to scale: the horizontal direction is stretched.)

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real k=2.5;
pair P=(0,62),R=(k*22.5,125),Cn=(k*22.5,62);
draw((-12,0)--(k*22.5+44,0),linewidth(0.8));
draw(P--R,linewidth(1.2));draw(P--Cn,dashed);draw(Cn--R,dashed);
draw((0,0)--P,gray);draw((k*22.5,0)--Cn,gray);
real q=3;draw(Cn+(-q,0)--Cn+(-q,q)--Cn+(0,q));
draw((-7,0)--(-7,62),Arrows(TeXHead));label("$62$ ft",(-7,31),W);
draw((k*22.5+36,0)--(k*22.5+36,125),Arrows(TeXHead));label("$125$ ft",(k*22.5+36,62.5),E);
label("run $22.5$ ft",(P+Cn)/2,S);label("\shortstack[l]{rise\\ $63$ ft}",(Cn+R)/2,E);label("roof",(P+R)/2,NW);
label("ground",(k*11,0),S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
