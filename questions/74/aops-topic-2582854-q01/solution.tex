Expanding gives
\[9x^7+7x^5+24x^4-49x^2+14,\]
and $9+7+24-49+14=\boxed{5}$.

A quicker way: the sum of the coefficients of a polynomial $P(x)$ is $P(1)$. Here $P(1)=3(3+8-7)+7(1-7+5)=12-7=5$. Leaving out the constant term would give the wrong value $-9$.
