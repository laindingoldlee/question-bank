In pentagon $ABCDE$, $\angle EAB=60^\circ$ and all the other interior angles measure $120^\circ$. If $AB=AE=2$ units and $BC=CD=DE=1$ unit, what is the ratio of the area of triangle $ACD$ to the area of pentagon $ABCDE$? Express your answer as a common fraction.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s3=sqrt(3);
pair A=(0,0),B=(2,0),C=(2.5,s3/2),D=(2,s3),Ep=(1,s3);
draw(A--B--C--D--Ep--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(arc(A,0.35,0,60)); label("$60^\circ$",A+0.6*dir(30),fontsize(8pt));
label("$A$",A,SW); label("$B$",B,SE); label("$C$",C,E); label("$D$",D,NE); label("$E$",Ep,NW);
label("$2$",(A+B)/2,S); label("$2$",(A+Ep)/2,W); label("$1$",(B+C)/2,SE); label("$1$",(C+D)/2,NE); label("$1$",(D+Ep)/2,N);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
