Extend $AB$ and $AE$ to $X$ and $Y$ with $AX=AY=3$. The angles at $B$, $C$, $D$, $E$ are $120^\circ$, so triangles $BXC$ and $DYE$ have two $60^\circ$ angles: they are equilateral with side $1$, and $C$, $D$ lie on $XY$ with $XC=CD=DY=1$. So $AXY$ is an equilateral triangle of side $3$, and the pentagon is $AXY$ with two equilateral triangles of side $1$ removed.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s3=sqrt(3);
pair A=(0,0),B=(2,0),C=(2.5,s3/2),D=(2,s3),Ep=(1,s3),X=(3,0),Y=(1.5,1.5*s3);
fill(A--C--D--cycle,lightblue);
draw(B--X--C,dashed); draw(D--Y--Ep,dashed);
draw(A--B--C--D--Ep--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(A--C,blue); draw(A--D,blue);
label("$A$",A,SW); label("$B$",B,S); label("$C$",C,E); label("$D$",D,E); label("$E$",Ep,W);
label("$X$",X,SE); label("$Y$",Y,N);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

In units of $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}$ (the area of an equilateral triangle of side $1$), $[AXY]=9$ and
\[
[ABCDE]=9-1-1=7 .
\]
Triangle $ACD$ has base $CD=1$ on line $XY$ and the same apex $A$ as triangle $AXY$, whose base is $XY=3$, so $[ACD]=\dfrac{1}{3}[AXY]=3$. The ratio is
\[
\dfrac{[ACD]}{[ABCDE]}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{7}}.
\]
