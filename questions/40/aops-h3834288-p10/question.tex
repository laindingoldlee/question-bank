Anika and Angela are playing a game of tic-tac-toe. They take turns drawing shapes in a $3\times 3$ grid. Anika goes first and draws an $\mathsf{X}$ each turn and Angela draws an $\mathsf{O}$ each turn. The first player who gets three of their shapes in a line (vertical, horizontal, diagonal) wins the game. Suppose that Anika won the game. Compute the remainder of the number of possible final configurations of the $3\times 3$ grid divided by $1000$.
