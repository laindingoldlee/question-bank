In a football World Cup, $32$ teams are split into $8$ groups of $4$. In the group stage, each team plays every other team in its group exactly once. The top $2$ teams of each group ($16$ teams) go on to a knockout stage: a round of $16$, quarter-finals, semi-finals, a match for third place between the two losing semi-finalists, and the final. How many matches are played in the whole tournament?
