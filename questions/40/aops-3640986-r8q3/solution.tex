Square-free integers have asymptotic density $\prod_p(1-p^{-2})=\dfrac{6}{\pi^2}$, giving estimate $10^6\cdot\dfrac{6}{\pi^2}\approx607927$. An exact sieve marks every multiple of $k^2$ for $2\le k\le1000$, leaving $\boxed{607926}$ positive integers. This counts each non-square-free integer at least once and never removes a square-free one; duplicate markings do not matter. The full sieve is in the computation audit.
