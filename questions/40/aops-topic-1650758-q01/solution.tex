Multiply the first equation by $4$: $8x+3y=104$. Eliminate $y$: $4(8x+3y)-3(3x+4y)=416-96$, so $23x=320$ and $x=\dfrac{320}{23}$. Eliminate $x$: $3(8x+3y)-8(3x+4y)=312-256$, so $-23y=56$ and $y=-\dfrac{56}{23}$.
\[
\boxed{x=\dfrac{320}{23},\quad y=-\dfrac{56}{23}}
\]
Check: $3\cdot\dfrac{320}{23}+4\cdot\left(-\dfrac{56}{23}\right)=\dfrac{960-224}{23}=\dfrac{736}{23}=32$.
