The diagonal is a side of both pieces. The hexagon uses $5$ sides of the octagon, leaving $3$ octagon sides for the other piece, which with the diagonal has $\boxed{4}$ sides.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair[] V;for(int k=0;k<8;++k) V.push(dir(22.5+45k));draw(V[0]--V[1]--V[2]--V[3]--V[4]--V[5]--V[6]--V[7]--cycle);draw(V[0]--V[5],dashed);label("$6$ sides",(-0.25,0.2));label("$4$ sides",(0.55,-0.5));
\end{asy}
