A triangle $T$ is inscribed in a circle with centre $O$. The circle and the triangle are dilated with centre $O$ and scale factor $2$, giving a triangle $T_1$ inscribed in the larger circle. Then this circle and $T_1$ are dilated again with centre $O$ and scale factor $2$, giving a triangle $T_2$. Prove or disprove: the centroids (centres of mass) of $T$, $T_1$ and $T_2$ are collinear.
