The initial purchase gives $10000$ bars and wrappers. Each exchange followed by eating reduces the number of wrappers by $3$. Exchanges can continue precisely while at least four remain. Thus the maximum number of exchanges is $\left\lfloor\dfrac{10000-1}{3}\right\rfloor=3333$, leaving one wrapper. The total is $\boxed{13333}$.
