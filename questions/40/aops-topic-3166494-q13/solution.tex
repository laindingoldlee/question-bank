Fifteen heads occur before the fourth tail exactly when the first eighteen flips contain at most three tails. Thus the probability is $\dfrac{\binom{18}{0}+\binom{18}{1}+\binom{18}{2}+\binom{18}{3}}{2^{18}}=\dfrac{1+18+153+816}{262144}=\boxed{\dfrac{247}{65536}}$.
