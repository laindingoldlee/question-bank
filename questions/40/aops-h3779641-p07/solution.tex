A regular n-gon has angle $180-\dfrac{360}{n}$ degrees. This is integral exactly when n divides 360. Factor $360=2^3\cdot3^2\cdot5$, giving $4\cdot3\cdot2=24$ divisors. Exclude n=1 and n=2, which are not polygons, leaving $\boxed{22}$.
