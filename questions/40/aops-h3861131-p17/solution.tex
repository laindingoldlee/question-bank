Let $E_r$ be the mean additional rolls when $r$ is still needed, with $E_0=0$. For $1\le r\le6$, rejected overshoots give $E_r=1+\dfrac{1}{6}\sum_{i=1}^rE_{r-i}+\dfrac{6-r}{6}E_r$, hence $E_r=\dfrac{6+\sum_{j=0}^{r-1}E_j}{r}=6$. For larger $r$, no roll is rejected, so $E_r=1+\dfrac{1}{6}\sum_{i=1}^6E_{r-i}$. Therefore $E_7=7$, $E_8=\dfrac{43}{6}$, and $E_9=1+\dfrac{4\cdot6+7+\dfrac{43}{6}}{6}=\boxed{\dfrac{265}{36}}$.
