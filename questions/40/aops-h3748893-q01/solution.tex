A strict majority needs $\lfloor \dfrac{n}{2}\rfloor+1$ votes. The proposer therefore needs $\lfloor \dfrac{n}{2}\rfloor$ other yes votes, costing at least that many dollars. Give one dollar to exactly those members and zero to the others. This attains the upper bound $\boxed{\$\,\bigl(100{,}000{,}000-\lfloor n/2\rfloor\bigr)}$.
