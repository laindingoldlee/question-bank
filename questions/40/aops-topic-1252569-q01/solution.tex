In cents, the prices $a,b,c,d$ satisfy $a+b+c+d=711$ and $\dfrac{abcd}{100^4}=7.11$, i.e. $abcd=711\cdot10^6=2^6\cdot3^2\cdot5^6\cdot79$.

One price must be a multiple of the prime $79$: $79k$ with $79k<711$, so $k\le8$. Writing that price as $316=4\cdot79$ (the case that works), the other three satisfy $a+b+c=395$ and $abc=\dfrac{711\cdot10^6}{316}=2^4\cdot3^2\cdot5^6=2250000$. Searching the divisors of $2250000$ with sum $395$ gives $120+125+150=395$ and $120\cdot125\cdot150=2250000$. A systematic search (over all multiples $79k$ and all factorisations) shows this is the only solution:
\[\boxed{\$1.20,\ \$1.25,\ \$1.50,\ \$3.16}.\]
