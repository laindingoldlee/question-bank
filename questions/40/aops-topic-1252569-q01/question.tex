A customer buys $4$ items at a 7-Eleven store. The cashier multiplies the four prices (in dollars) and gets $\$7.11$. When the customer points out that the prices should be added, the cashier adds them and again gets $\$7.11$. Assuming each price is a whole number of cents, what are the four prices?
