By place value, $111(A+B+C)=1110A+D$, so
\[D=111(A+B+C)-1110A=111(B+C-9A).\]
Since $0\le D\le9$ and $D$ is a multiple of $111$, $D=0$ and $B+C=9A$. As $B$ and $C$ are different digits, $B+C\le17$, so $A=1$ and $B+C=9$. For example $111+222+777=1110$. Therefore $A+B+C+D=1+9+0=\boxed{10}$.
