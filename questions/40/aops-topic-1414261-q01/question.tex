A bowl contains $10$ jelly beans, of which $3$ are black and $7$ are yellow. Alphonse eats the jelly beans one at a time, each time picking a jelly bean at random. What is the probability that the third jelly bean he eats is the second black one he eats? Express your answer as a common fraction in lowest terms.
