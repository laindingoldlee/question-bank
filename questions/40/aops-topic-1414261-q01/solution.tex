The third jelly bean is the second black one exactly when among the first two there is exactly one black, and the third is black.

The first two contain exactly one black with probability
\[
\dfrac{3}{10}\cdot\dfrac{7}{9}+\dfrac{7}{10}\cdot\dfrac{3}{9}=\dfrac{42}{90}=\dfrac{7}{15}.
\]
Then $8$ beans remain, $2$ of them black, so the third is black with probability $\dfrac{2}{8}=\dfrac{1}{4}$. The answer is
\[
\dfrac{7}{15}\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{60}}.
\]
