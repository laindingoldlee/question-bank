Squaring and adding: $25+144+120(\sin A\cos B+\cos A\sin B)=229$, so $\sin(A+B)=\dfrac12$ and $\sin C=\dfrac12$, i.e. $C=30^\circ$ or $150^\circ$.

If $C=150^\circ$, then $A<30^\circ$, so $5\sin A+12\cos B<\dfrac52+12<15$, a contradiction. Hence $C=\boxed{30^\circ}$.
