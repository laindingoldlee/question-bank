Put $e_2=xy+yz+zx$ and $e_3=xyz$. The reciprocal condition gives $e_2=7e_3$, while the cube sum gives
\[
7=7^3-3\cdot7e_2+3e_3=343-144e_3.
\]
Hence $e_3=\dfrac{7}{3}$ and $e_2=\dfrac{49}{3}$.

(a) For real variables,
\[
(x-y)^2+(y-z)^2+(z-x)^2=2(7^2-3e_2)=0.
\]
Thus all three would equal $\dfrac{7}{3}$, whose product is not $\dfrac{7}{3}$. This is a contradiction.

(b) For the reciprocal variables the elementary symmetric sums are
\[
7,\qquad\dfrac{x+y+z}{xyz}=3,\qquad\dfrac{1}{xyz}=\dfrac{3}{7}.
\]
Their cube sum is consequently
\[
7^3-3\cdot7\cdot3+3\cdot\dfrac{3}{7}=\boxed{\dfrac{1969}{7}}.
\]
A complex triple does exist: the roots of $t^3-7t^2+\dfrac{49}{3}t-\dfrac{7}{3}$ have the required symmetric sums and are nonzero.
