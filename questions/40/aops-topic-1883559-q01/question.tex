Rita the painter rolls a fair six-sided die that has $3$ red faces, $2$ yellow faces and $1$ blue face. She rolls the die twice and mixes the two colours that come up. Purple is obtained exactly when one roll is red and the other is blue. What is the probability that she mixes purple?
