Triangle inequalities require $8<c<50$. If $c\le29$, obtuseness requires $29^2>21^2+c^2$, or $c^2<400$, giving 9 through 19 (11 choices). If $c>29$, it requires $c^2>21^2+29^2=1282$, giving 36 through 49 (14 choices). Thus the total is $\boxed{25}$.
