With eight numbers, the median is the average of the $4$th and $5$th smallest, $s_4$ and $s_5$, and $s_4\le13.5\le s_5$. So at least four numbers are at most $13.5$ and at least four are at least $13.5$.
\begin{itemize}
\item Of $10,14,18,22$ only $10$ is at most $13.5$, so at least three of the $x$-terms are, in particular $x+9\le13.5$, i.e. $x\le4.5$.
\item Only three of $10,14,18,22$ are at least $13.5$, so $x+12\ge13.5$, i.e. $x\ge1.5$.
\end{itemize}
Then $x+9\ge10.5>10$, so the four smallest numbers are $10,x+3,x+6,x+9$ and $s_4=x+9$, while $s_5=\min(x+12,14)$.
\begin{itemize}
\item If $x+12\le14$: $(x+9)+(x+12)=27$ gives $x=3$, but then $x+12=15>14$, a contradiction.
\item If $x+12>14$: $(x+9)+14=27$ gives $x=4$, which is consistent.
\end{itemize}
So $x=4$ and the list is $7,10,10,13,14,16,18,22$. The unique mode is $10$ and the range is $22-7=15$, so the answer is $10+15=\boxed{25}$.
