Since $D$ lies on segment $AC$, we have $AC=7$. Also $\cos A=\dfrac{AD}{AB}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Projecting $AC$ onto $AB$ gives $AE=AC\cos A=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{2}}$.

\end{asy}
