Switching wins exactly when the initial choice was a goat: the host must then reveal the other goat and leave the car unopened. The initial choice is a goat with probability $\dfrac{2}{3}$. Thus switching wins with probability $\boxed{\dfrac{2}{3}}$, compared with $\dfrac{1}{3}$ for staying, so switching is better.
