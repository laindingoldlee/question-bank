The triangle has area $\sqrt{21\cdot8\cdot7\cdot6}=84$, so the height to the side $14$ is $h=12$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0),C=(14,0),A=(5,12);
fill((2.5,0)--(9.5,0)--(9.5,6)--(2.5,6)--cycle,gray(0.8));draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw((2.5,0)--(2.5,6)--(9.5,6)--(9.5,0));
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$14$",(7,0),S);label("$13$",(A+B)/2,NW);label("$15$",(A+C)/2,NE);
draw(A--(5,0),dotted);label("$12$",(5,9),E);
\end{asy}

A rectangle of height $y$ standing on that side has its top corners on the other two sides, where the triangle's width is $14\left(1-\dfrac y{12}\right)$ (similar triangles). Its area is $14y\left(1-\dfrac y{12}\right)$, maximised at $y=6$: $14\cdot6\cdot\dfrac12=42$, half the triangle. The probability of missing it is $\dfrac{84-42}{84}=\boxed{\dfrac12}$.
