Put $CD=b$ and let the height of the trapezoid be $h$. Then $[ABCD]=\dfrac{1}{2}(3b+b)h=2bh$. Because $E$ is halfway from $D$ to $B$, its distance from line $CD$ is $\dfrac{h}{2}$. Hence $[CDE]=\dfrac{1}{2}b\cdot\dfrac{h}{2}=\dfrac{bh}{4}$. The ratio is $\boxed{n=8}$.

\end{asy}
