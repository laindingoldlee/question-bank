A convex hexagon $ABCDEF$ has all six sides equal. Suppose that
\[
\angle A+\angle C+\angle E=\angle B+\angle D+\angle F.
\]
Prove that $\angle A=\angle D$, $\angle B=\angle E$ and $\angle C=\angle F$.
