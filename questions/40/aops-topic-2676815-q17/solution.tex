\emph{Two sides equal to $21$.} The third side $b$ can be any integer from $1$ to $21$ (the triangle inequality $b<42$ holds): $21$ triangles, including the equilateral one.

\emph{Base $21$ with equal sides $a<21$.} The triangle inequality needs $2a>21$, so $11\le a\le20$: $10$ triangles.

The total is $\boxed{31}$.
