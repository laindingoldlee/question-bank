Multiply numerator and denominator by the conjugate $3+i$ of the denominator:
\[\dfrac{3+i}{3-i}=\dfrac{(3+i)^2}{(3-i)(3+i)}=\dfrac{9+6i+i^2}{9-i^2}=\dfrac{8+6i}{10}=\boxed{\dfrac{4}{5}+\dfrac{3}{5}i}.\]
(Check: $\left(\dfrac{4}{5}+\dfrac{3}{5}i\right)(3-i)=\dfrac{12}{5}-\dfrac{4}{5}i+\dfrac{9}{5}i+\dfrac{3}{5}=3+i$.)
