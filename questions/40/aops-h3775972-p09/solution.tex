Write each allowed number uniquely as $q$ times one of $1,2,5,10$, where $q$ is squarefree and has no factors $2,5$. The unit digit of $q$ is uniform among $1,3,7,9$: holding all other prime choices fixed, freely including the primes $3$ and $19$ multiplies that digit by $1,3,9,7$, respectively. For a given digit $u$, the four unit digits are $u$, the residue of $2u$ modulo $10$, $5$, and $0$. Their averages for $u=1,3,7,9$ are $2,\dfrac{7}{2},4,\dfrac{11}{2}$. Averaging gives $\boxed{\dfrac{15}{4}}$.
