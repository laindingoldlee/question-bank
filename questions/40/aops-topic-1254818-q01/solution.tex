Expanding, $(x+y)^4+(x-y)^4=2x^4+12x^2y^2+2y^4$, so the left side is
\[2x^4+4x^2y^2+2y^4=2\left(x^2+y^2\right)^2.\]
The inequality becomes $\left(x^2+y^2\right)^2\le2^{1336}$, i.e. $x^2+y^2\le2^{668}$: a disc of radius $2^{334}$. Its area is $\boxed{2^{668}\pi}$.
