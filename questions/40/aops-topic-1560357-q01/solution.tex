Cancel $n!$: $\dfrac{(n+7)!}{n!}=(n+7)(n+6)(n+5)(n+4)(n+3)(n+2)(n+1)$, so the left side is
\[
(n+7)(n+5)(n+3)(n+2)(n+1).
\]
The right side is $\dfrac{362880}{3}=120960=14\cdot12\cdot10\cdot9\cdot8$, which is the value of the left side at $n=7$. The left side is strictly increasing in $n$, so $n=\boxed{7}$ is the only solution.
