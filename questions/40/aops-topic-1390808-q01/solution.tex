Olivia's speed is $\dfrac{12}{5}\div\dfrac{2}{3}=\dfrac{12}{5}\cdot\dfrac{3}{2}=\dfrac{18}{5}=3.6$ km per hour, and Ryan's is $\dfrac{9}{2}\div\dfrac{3}{2}=3$ km per hour. Olivia walked faster, and
\[
\dfrac{3.6}{3}=\boxed{\dfrac{6}{5}}
\]
so she walked $1.2$ times as fast as Ryan.
