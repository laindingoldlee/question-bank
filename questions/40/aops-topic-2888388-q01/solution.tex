Write $C$ for answer (1). Answer (3) is $B=\sqrt3\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}4C^2=\dfrac{3C^2}4$, which must be a whole number, so $C$ is even. Answer (2) is $A=\dbinom B2$, and $C$ is the number of trailing zeros of $(A+10)!$.

$C=0$: $B=A=0$, but $10!$ ends in $2$ zeros. $C=2$: $B=3$, $A=3$, and $13!$ ends in $2$ zeros, which is consistent. For $C\ge4$, $A\ge66$ and $(A+10)!$ has far more than $C$ zeros (e.g. $C=4$ gives $76!$ with $18$), and the gap only grows. So the answers are $\boxed{(1)\ 2,\ (2)\ 3,\ (3)\ 3}$.
