Three linked problems: (1) Let $A$ be the answer to (2); how many zeros end $(A+10)!$? (2) Let $B$ be the answer to (3); in how many ways can $2$ of $B$ books be chosen? (3) Let $C$ be the answer to (1); what is $\sqrt3$ times the area of an equilateral triangle of side $C$? Find the three answers.
