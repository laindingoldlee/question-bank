Relative to the first train, the second train moves at $22+18=40$ m per second. From the moment the fronts meet until the backs pass each other, the second train must move, relative to the first, a distance equal to the sum of the two lengths, $125+115=240$ m. This takes
\[\dfrac{240}{40}=\boxed{6}\text{ seconds}.\]
