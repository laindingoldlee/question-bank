Let $x_1,x_2,\dots,x_n$ be positive real numbers with arithmetic mean $A=\dfrac{x_1+x_2+\cdots+x_n}{n}$. Prove, without calculus, that
\[
x_1x_2\cdots x_n\le A^n ,
\]
with equality only when all the numbers are equal.
