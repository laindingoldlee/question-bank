The glass is the region between two concentric spheres (the cross-section through the centre is shown).

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
fill(circle((0,0),5)^^circle((0,0),3),evenodd+paleblue);
draw(circle((0,0),5),linewidth(1)); draw(circle((0,0),3),linewidth(0.9));
draw((0,0)--5*dir(30),blue); draw((0,0)--3*dir(150),red);
label("$5$",4.1*dir(30),NW,blue); label("$3$",1.5*dir(150),N,red); dot((0,0));
label("glass",4*dir(-90));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Its volume is the volume of the outer ball minus that of the empty centre:
\[\dfrac{4}{3}\pi\cdot5^3-\dfrac{4}{3}\pi\cdot3^3=\dfrac{4}{3}\pi(125-27)=\boxed{\dfrac{392\pi}{3}}\approx410.5\text{ cubic inches}.\]
