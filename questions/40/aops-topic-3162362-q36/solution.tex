Compute the integer product exactly and compare against consecutive powers of ten. This avoids rounding logarithms near an integer boundary:
\begin{verbatim}
from math import factorial,prod
N=prod(factorial(j) for j in range(1,21))*prod(factorial(j) for j in range(1,18))
assert 10**260<=N<10**261
\end{verbatim}
A positive integer in $[10^{260},10^{261})$ has $\boxed{261}$ digits.
