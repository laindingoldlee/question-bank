Let each single-subject count be $k$, the exactly-two counts for geometry and number theory, geometry and algebra, number theory and algebra be $a,b,c$, and the all-three count be $t$. Subtracting subject totals gives $c=b+10$ and $a=b-10$. The geometry equation becomes $k+2b+t=40$. Nonnegativity implies $10\le b\le20$. Both endpoints are attainable: take $t=0$, $k=40-2b$, $a=b-10,c=b+10$. Thus the difference is $\boxed{10}$.
