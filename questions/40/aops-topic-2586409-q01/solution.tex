Replacing $x$ by $\dfrac1x$ gives $3f\!\left(\dfrac1x\right)-2f(x)=\dfrac1x$. Solving the two linear equations for $f(x)$: $9f(x)-4f(x)=3x+\dfrac2x$, so $f(x)=\dfrac{3x+\dfrac2x}5$. Hence $f(4)=\dfrac{12+\dfrac12}5=\boxed{\dfrac52}$.
