An increase of $55\dfrac{5}{9}\%=\dfrac{500}{9}\%=\dfrac{5}{9}$ multiplies the length by $1+\dfrac{5}{9}=\dfrac{14}{9}$. A decrease of $14\dfrac{2}{7}\%=\dfrac{100}{7}\%=\dfrac{1}{7}$ multiplies the width by $1-\dfrac{1}{7}=\dfrac{6}{7}$. So the area is multiplied by
\[
\dfrac{14}{9}\cdot\dfrac{6}{7}=\dfrac{84}{63}=\dfrac{4}{3},
\]
an increase of $\dfrac{1}{3}$, i.e.\ $\boxed{33\dfrac{1}{3}\%}$.
