A fish starts at a point $O$ in open water. It swims $3$ metres in a straight line in a uniformly random direction (in space), rests, then swims $4$ metres in a new, independent, uniformly random direction. Predators wait at every point at distance $5$ metres from $O$. What is the probability that the fish completes both swims without reaching a predator?
