The equation factors: $x^2-3x-40=(x-8)(x+5)$, so $x=\boxed{8}$ or $x=\boxed{-5}$. Factoring is the most efficient method, because $-40$ has the factor pair $-8$, $5$ with sum $-3$, which is quick to spot; the quadratic formula would also work but takes longer.
