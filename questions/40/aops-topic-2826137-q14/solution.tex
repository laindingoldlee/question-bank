Treat $C,D$ as a block with two internal orders. There are $2\cdot8!$ circular arrangements of this block and the other eight people. Those also having $A,B$ adjacent are $2\cdot2\cdot7!$. Subtraction gives $2\cdot8!-4\cdot7!=\boxed{60480}$.
