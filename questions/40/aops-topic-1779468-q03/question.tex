On day $1$ you have $100$ dollars. On day $2$ you earn $1$ dollar, on day $3$ you lose $2$ dollars, on day $4$ you earn $2$ dollars, on day $5$ you lose $3$ dollars, on day $6$ you earn $3$ dollars, and so on (on day $2k$ you earn $k$ dollars and on day $2k+1$ you lose $k+1$ dollars). On which day do you first run out of money?
