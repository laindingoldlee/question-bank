Work from the inside out. First, $-\left(-2q^2+3pq+7\right)=2q^2-3pq-7$, so the large bracket is
\[
-8q^2-6pq-q+6+2q^2-3pq-7=-6q^2-9pq-q-1 .
\]
Subtracting it changes every sign:
\[
2p^2+q^2-3p+4+6q^2+9pq+q+1=\boxed{2p^2+9pq+7q^2-3p+q+5}.
\]
