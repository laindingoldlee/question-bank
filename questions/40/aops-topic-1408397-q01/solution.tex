For one student the number of tails is $0,1,2,3$ with probabilities $\dfrac{1}{8},\dfrac{3}{8},\dfrac{3}{8},\dfrac{1}{8}$. The students are independent, so
\[
P=\left(\dfrac{1}{8}\right)^3+\left(\dfrac{3}{8}\right)^3+\left(\dfrac{3}{8}\right)^3+\left(\dfrac{1}{8}\right)^3=\dfrac{1+27+27+1}{512}=\dfrac{56}{512}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{64}}.
\]
