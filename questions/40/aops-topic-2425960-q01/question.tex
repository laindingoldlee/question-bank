Let $n$ be a positive integer with $d(n)$ positive divisors.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that the product of all the positive divisors of $n$ is $n^{\dfrac{d(n)}{2}}$.
\item[(b)] Use this to find the product of all the positive divisors of $36$.
\end{enumerate}
