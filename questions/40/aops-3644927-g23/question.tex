A frog is on a number line. It starts at $0$, and each minute it has an equally likely chance to jump to any integer between $c + 1$ and $n$ inclusive, where $c$ is the number it currently sits on and $n$ is an arbitrary constant positive number. Let $E$ be the expected number of minutes until the frog lands on $n$. Find the minimum $n$ such that $E > 3$.
