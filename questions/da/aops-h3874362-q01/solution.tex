A circle of radius $r$ tangent to the two rays of a $60^\circ$ angle has its center $2r$ from the vertex. For adjacent radii $r>r'$, external tangency gives $2r-2r'=r+r'$, so $r'=\dfrac{r}{3}$. Their areas therefore have ratio $\dfrac{1}{9}$, and the first area is $32\pi$.

The combined area of the first $N$ circles is
\[
32\pi\dfrac{1-9^{-N}}{1-\dfrac{1}{9}}=36\pi(1-9^{-N}).
\]
Exceeding $35999\dfrac{\pi}{1000}$ is equivalent to $9^N>36000$. Since $9^4=6561$ and $9^5=59049$, the least value is $\boxed5$.
