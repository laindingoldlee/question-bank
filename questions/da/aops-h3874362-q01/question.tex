Inside a $60^\circ$ angle, a circle $C_1$ of radius $4\sqrt2$ is tangent to both rays of the angle.

For every $n\ge1$, circle $C_{n+1}$ is tangent to both rays and externally tangent to $C_n$, and lies between $C_n$ and the vertex of the angle.

Find the least positive integer $N$ such that the combined area of $C_1,C_2,\ldots,C_N$ is greater than

\[
\dfrac{35999\pi}{1000}.
\]
