The first two terms give $S>1+\dfrac{1}{8}=1.125$, and the first three give $S>\dfrac{251}{216}>1.15$. Since $x^{-3}$ is decreasing, $\sum_{k=4}^\infty k^{-3}<\int_3^\infty x^{-3}\,dx=\dfrac{1}{18}$. Thus $1.15<S<\dfrac{251}{216}+\dfrac{1}{18}=\dfrac{263}{216}<1.25$. Therefore $S$ rounds to $\boxed{1.2}$.
