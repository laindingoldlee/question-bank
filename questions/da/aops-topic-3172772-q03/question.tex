Let $a\uparrow\uparrow n$ denote a right-associated tower of $n$ copies of $a$, and define $a\uparrow\uparrow\uparrow1=a$, $a\uparrow\uparrow\uparrow(n+1)=a\uparrow\uparrow(a\uparrow\uparrow\uparrow n)$. Simplify $\dfrac{3\uparrow\uparrow6}{3\uparrow\uparrow\uparrow2}$ as a single power of $3$.
