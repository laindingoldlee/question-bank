The denominator is $3\uparrow\uparrow3=3^{3^3}=3^{27}$. The numerator is $3^{3\uparrow\uparrow5}$. Using the quotient rule for powers gives $\boxed{3^{(3\uparrow\uparrow5)-27}}$.
