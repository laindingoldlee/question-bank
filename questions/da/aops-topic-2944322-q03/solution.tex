Taking reciprocals, $\dfrac1{a_{n+1}}=\dfrac1{a_n}+n$. So $\dfrac1{a_n}=1+(1+2+\cdots+(n-1))=1+\dfrac{n(n-1)}2$. Then $a_{2017}=\dfrac1{1+2017\cdot1008}=\boxed{\dfrac1{2033137}}$.
