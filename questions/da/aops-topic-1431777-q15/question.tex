Ms. Spice tells her class that she is thinking of a positive common fraction. The product of the numerator and denominator is $60$. When she adds $1$ to the numerator and divides the denominator by $2$, the resulting fraction is equal to $1$. What common fraction is Ms. Spice thinking of? Solve it algebraically, without listing factor pairs.
