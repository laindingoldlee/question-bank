A circle with centre $(h,k)$ and radius $r$ has equation $(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2$. Writing $y+4=y-(-4)$ and $25=5^2$, the centre is $\boxed{(9,-4)}$ and the radius is $\boxed{5}$.

To sketch it, plot the centre and the four points $5$ units to the left, right, above and below it: $(4,-4)$, $(14,-4)$, $(9,1)$, $(9,-9)$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
draw((-1,0)--(15.5,0),Arrow(TeXHead)); draw((0,-10.5)--(0,2.5),Arrow(TeXHead));
label("$x$",(15.5,0),E); label("$y$",(0,2.5),N);
pair O=(9,-4);
draw(circle(O,5),blue+linewidth(1));
draw(O--(14,-4),red); label("$5$",(11.5,-4),N,red);
dot(O); label("$(9,-4)$",O,SW,fontsize(8pt));
dot((4,-4)); dot((14,-4)); dot((9,1)); dot((9,-9));
label("$(4,-4)$",(4,-4),W,fontsize(8pt)); label("$(14,-4)$",(14,-4),E,fontsize(8pt));
label("$(9,1)$",(9,1),N,fontsize(8pt)); label("$(9,-9)$",(9,-9),S,fontsize(8pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

(The circle crosses the $x$-axis where $(x-9)^2=25-16=9$, at $x=6$ and $x=12$, and does not meet the $y$-axis.)
