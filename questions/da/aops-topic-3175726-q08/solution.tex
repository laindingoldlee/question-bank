Adding the conjugate sixth powers cancels odd radical terms: $(\sqrt7+\sqrt3)^6+(\sqrt7-\sqrt3)^6=2(343+2205+945+27)=7040$. Also $0<\sqrt7-\sqrt3<1$, because $\sqrt7<\sqrt3+1$. Thus its sixth power is between zero and one, making the requested number strictly between $7039$ and $7040$. The answer is $\boxed{7039}$.
