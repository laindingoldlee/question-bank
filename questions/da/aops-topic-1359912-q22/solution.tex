There are $\dbinom{10}{3}=120$ equally likely sets of three marbles. All red: $\dbinom33=1$; all yellow: $\dbinom53=10$; all blue: impossible ($2<3$). The probability is $\dfrac{1+10}{120}=\boxed{\dfrac{11}{120}}$.
