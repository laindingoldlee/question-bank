The areas form a geometric series with first term $16\pi$ and ratio $r^2$, so
\[\dfrac{16\pi}{1-r^2}=32\pi\ \Longrightarrow\ r^2=\dfrac12\ \Longrightarrow\ r=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}.\]
So $a+b=\boxed{4}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real r=1/sqrt(2);real x=0,R=4;
for(int i=0;i<7;++i){draw(circle((x,0),R),linewidth(0.7));real Rn=R*r;x+=R+Rn;R=Rn;}
label("$4$",(0,0)--(0,4),E,fontsize(8pt));draw((0,0)--(0,4));
\end{asy}
