An infinite ``caterpillar'' consists of circles whose radii form a geometric sequence: the largest has radius $4$ cm, and each circle's radius is a fixed ratio $r<1$ times the radius of the next larger one. The total area of all the circles is $32\pi$ cm$^2$. If $r=\dfrac{\sqrt a}{b}$ in simplest form, find $a+b$.
