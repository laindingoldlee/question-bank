There are $\binom{54}2=1431$ equally likely pairs. Exactly one joker: $2\cdot52=104$ pairs. No joker and two royals: $\binom{12}2=66$ pairs. The two events are disjoint, so the probability is
\[\dfrac{104+66}{1431}=\boxed{\dfrac{170}{1431}}.\]
