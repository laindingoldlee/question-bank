Children play in a rectangular schoolyard $ABCD$ with $AB=16$ yards and $BC=12$ yards. They start at the point $S$ on wall $AB$ that is $4$ yards from $A$. They must run and touch each of the other three walls $BC$, $CD$ and $DA$ (in any order), and then return to $S$. What is the length of the shortest possible route, and why is it the shortest?
