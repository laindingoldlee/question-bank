Venus has $30$ numbers, none of which is a whole number. She colours each of them red or blue. On Monday she rounds every red number up and every blue number down (to the nearest integer) and adds the results, getting $2018$. On Tuesday she rounds every red number down and every blue number up, and gets $2014$. On Wednesday she rounds every number down. What sum does she get on Wednesday?
