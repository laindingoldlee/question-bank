Let $y$ dollars be invested at $7\%$, so $2y$ dollars are invested at $6.5\%$. Then
\[
0.065\cdot2y+0.07y=0.2y=800\quad\Longrightarrow\quad y=4000 .
\]
He invested $\boxed{8000\text{ dollars at }6.5\%\text{ and }4000\text{ dollars at }7\%}$. Check: $520+280=800$.
