The square has area $x^2$. The circle has radius $x$, so its area is $\pi x^2$. The ratio is
\[\dfrac{x^2}{\pi x^2}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{\pi}},\]
which does not depend on $x$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((0,0)--(2,0)--(2,2)--(0,2)--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
label("$x$",(1,0),S); label("$x$",(2,1),E);
pair O=(5.2,1);
draw(circle(O,2),linewidth(0.8));
draw((O.x-2,O.y)--(O.x+2,O.y),red);
dot(O);
label("$2x$",(O.x+0.9,O.y),N,red);
label("area $x^2$",(1,1));
label("area $\pi x^2$",(O.x,O.y-0.9));
\end{asy}
