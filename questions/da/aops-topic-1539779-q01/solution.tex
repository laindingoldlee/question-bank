Let Ellen be $e$ years old now, so Anthony is $e+5$. Seven years ago their ages added to
\[
(e-7)+(e+5-7)=23\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad 2e-9=23\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad e=16 .
\]
So Anthony is $21$ now and will be $30$ in $\boxed{9}$ years.
