There are $\dbinom{26}{2}=325$ equally likely pairs of chairs. Numbering the chairs $1$ to $26$, the pairs with exactly one chair between them are $\{i,i+2\}$ for $i=1,\dots,24$, so there are $24$ of them. The probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{24}{325}}$.
