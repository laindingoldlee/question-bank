Let $ABCD$ be a rectangle with length $AB=l$ and width $BC=w$, where $l\ne w$. The angle bisectors of $\angle DAB$ and $\angle ABC$ meet at $P$, the angle bisectors of $\angle ABC$ and $\angle BCD$ meet at $Q$, the angle bisectors of $\angle BCD$ and $\angle CDA$ meet at $R$, and the angle bisectors of $\angle CDA$ and $\angle DAB$ meet at $S$. If $PQ=2019$, compute $|l-w|$.
