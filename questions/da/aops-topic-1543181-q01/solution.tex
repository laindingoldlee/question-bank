Both triangles are right triangles: $18$-$24$-$30$ is $6$ times the $3$-$4$-$5$ triple, and $14$-$48$-$50$ is $2$ times the $7$-$24$-$25$ triple. (Check: $18^2+24^2=900=30^2$ and $14^2+48^2=2500=50^2$.) So the longest side is the hypotenuse and the other two sides are the legs.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(9.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A1=(0,0),B1=(24,0),C1=(0,18);
pair A2=(30,0),B2=(78,0),C2=(30,14);
draw(A1--B1--C1--cycle,linewidth(1));draw(A2--B2--C2--cycle,linewidth(1));
real s=1.4; draw((s,0)--(s,s)--(0,s)); draw(A2+(s,0)--A2+(s,s)--A2+(0,s));
label("$24$",(A1+B1)/2,S);label("$18$",(A1+C1)/2,W);label("$30$",(B1+C1)/2,NE);
label("$48$",(A2+B2)/2,S);label("$14$",(A2+C2)/2,W);label("$50$",(B2+C2)/2,NE);
label("$216$",(8,6));label("$336$",(46,4.5));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The areas are
\[
\dfrac{18\cdot24}{2}=216\qquad\text{and}\qquad\dfrac{14\cdot48}{2}=336 .
\]
Their ratio is $216:336$; dividing by $24$, it is $\boxed{9:14}$.
