A website shuts down for maintenance for $10$ consecutive minutes at some time between 10:00 and 11:00 (the whole shutdown lies within that hour, but its start may be random). You visit the website at a time chosen uniformly at random between 10:00 and 11:00, independently of the shutdown. What is the probability that the website is shut down when you visit?
