An equilateral triangle sits on a table in a starting position with its vertices labelled. On each move it is either reflected over the vertical axis through its top vertex (switching the two bottom vertices), or rotated $120^\circ$ clockwise about its centre. How many different sequences of five moves return the triangle to its starting position?
