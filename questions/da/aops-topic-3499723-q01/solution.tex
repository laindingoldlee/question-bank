Let the occupied positions be $a<b<c$ with gaps at least two. The transformation $(a,b,c)\mapsto(a,b-1,c-2)$ is a bijection to three-element subsets of $\{1,\ldots,8\}$. There are $\binom83$ occupied-seat sets and $3!$ assignments to the friends. The answer is $\binom83\cdot3!=\boxed{336}$.
