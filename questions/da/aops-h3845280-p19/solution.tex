Choose the two internal horizontal grid levels from $1,2,3,4,5$, giving $\binom52=10$ possibilities. At each of the four chosen levels (including bottom and top), the left endpoint of its length-two base can have any integer horizontal coordinate from $0$ to $4$, giving $5^4$ possibilities. Each pair of adjacent bases uniquely determines a parallelogram, and its slanted edges stay in the square because their coordinates interpolate between endpoints in the square. Thus
\[
\boxed{\binom52\,5^4=6250}.
\]
