From the second equation $6y=13-7x$, so $12xy=2x(13-7x)=-4$, i.e. $7x^2-13x-2=0$, i.e. $(7x+1)(x-2)=0$. With $x>0$, $x=2$ and $y=\dfrac{13-14}6=-\dfrac16$. Answer $\boxed{\left(2,-\dfrac16\right)}$.
