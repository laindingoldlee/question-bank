Since $\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom nk=2^n$, the numerator is $2^{100}-1$ and the denominator is $2^{50}-1$ (the terms $k=0$ are missing). By the difference of squares,
\[\dfrac{2^{100}-1}{2^{50}-1}=\dfrac{\left(2^{50}-1\right)\left(2^{50}+1\right)}{2^{50}-1}=\boxed{2^{50}+1}.\]
