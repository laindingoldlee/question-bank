\textbf{(a)} For $x=0,1,-1$ we get $0,1,-1$, which are not prime, and for $x\le-2$ the power is negative. For $x\ge2$, $x^{213}=x\cdot x^{212}$ has the divisor $x$ with $1<x<x^{213}$, so it is composite. $\blacksquare$

\textbf{(b)} Take $x=2^{\dfrac{1}{213}}$; then $x^{213}=2$, which is prime. $\blacksquare$

So whether such an $x$ exists depends on the domain: $\boxed{\text{no integer, but some real}}$ $x$ works.
