The area is $20\cdot18=360$ square feet. A square yard is $3\cdot3=9$ square feet, so the area is
\[\dfrac{360}{9}=\boxed{40}\text{ square yards}.\]
(Equivalently, $\dfrac{20}{3}\text{ yd}\cdot6\text{ yd}=40\text{ yd}^2$.)

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((0,0)--(20,0)--(20,18)--(0,18)--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
for(int i=1;i<=6;++i) draw((3i,0)--(3i,18),gray(0.85));
for(int j=1;j<=5;++j) draw((0,3j)--(20,3j),gray(0.85));
draw((0,0)--(20,0)--(20,18)--(0,18)--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
label("$20$ ft $=\dfrac{20}{3}$ yd",(10,0),S);
label("$18$ ft $=6$ yd",(20,9),E);
label("$3$ ft",(1.5,18),N,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}
