Number the days so that day $1$ is the Monday. By the end of day $n$ she has eaten $\dfrac{11n}{10}$ mints and has received $2018+(n-1)=2017+n$. She can only run out when what she has eaten reaches what she has received. At the end of day $n$ this means
\[\dfrac{11n}{10}\ge2017+n\iff n\ge20170,\]
and since within a day the supply stays fixed while eating is gradual, she first runs out exactly at the end of day $20170$. Day $20170$ is $20169=7\cdot2881+2$ days after Monday, so it is a $\boxed{\text{Wednesday}}$.
