Two ants start at $(0,0)$ and $(5,0)$ and walk to their colonies at $(5,5)$ and $(0,5)$, respectively. They move only along the grid lines $x=c$ and $y=c$ ($c$ an integer), along shortest routes, both at the same constant speed and starting at the same time; each ant chooses one of its shortest routes uniformly at random, independently of the other. If they are ever at the same point at the same time, they collide. What is the probability that they reach their colonies without colliding?
