There are $19$ handholds between neighbours. For any one of them, the two people are a uniformly random pair of the $20$ children, so the probability that it joins a girl and a boy is
\[\dfrac{13\cdot7}{\dbinom{20}{2}}=\dfrac{91}{190}.\]
By linearity of expectation, $E[s]=19\cdot\dfrac{91}{190}=\boxed{\dfrac{91}{10}}=9.1$.
