A warden lines up $10$ prisoners in single file, all facing forward, so that each prisoner sees the hats of everyone in front of him (the prisoner at the back sees all the others; the one at the front sees nobody). Each prisoner gets a black or a white hat and cannot see his own. Starting from the back, each prisoner in turn says aloud a guess for the colour of his own hat; everyone hears each guess and whether it was right. The prisoners may agree on a strategy beforehand. Prove that there is a strategy which guarantees that at most one prisoner guesses wrongly.
