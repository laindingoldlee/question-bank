For odd $n$, $5^n-3^n\equiv2\pmod4$, so the valuation is $1$. For even $n=2^su$ with odd $u$ and $s\ge1$, the odd-power quotient shows $v_2(5^n-3^n)=v_2(5^{2^s}-3^{2^s})$. Initially $5^2-3^2=16$ has valuation $4$; every subsequent squaring introduces a factor $5^{2^j}+3^{2^j}\equiv2\pmod8$ and adds one. Thus $v_2(5^n-3^n)=3+v_2(n)$.

For $n=2,4,6,8,10$, these values are $4,5,4,6,4$; all other factors are $1$. Their product is $\boxed{1920}$.
