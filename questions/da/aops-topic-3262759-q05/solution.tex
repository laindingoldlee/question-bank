For an opponent sum $s$ between $1$ and $12$, C wins with conditional probability $\dfrac{12-s}{12}$. Therefore the overall probability is $\dfrac{12-E(s)}{12}$. A has expected sum $2(\dfrac{7}{2})=7$, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{5}{12}}$. B has expected sum $3(\dfrac{5}{2})=\dfrac{15}{2}$, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{3}{8}}$.
