The semiperimeter is $21$, so the tangent length $BD=21-15=6$. The angle bisector theorem gives $BK=14\cdot\dfrac{13}{28}=\dfrac{13}{2}$, hence $DK=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Heron's formula gives area $84$, so the inradius $ID=\dfrac{84}{21}=4$. Therefore $[KID]=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot4=\boxed1$.
