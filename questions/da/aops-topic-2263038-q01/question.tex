A company plans to make its cans of beans smaller. A can is a cylinder of height $h$ inches and radius $r$ inches, with volume $V=\pi r^2h$ cubic inches. Which of the following changes gives the can with the smallest volume?

\textbf{(a)} Keep $h$ the same and reduce $r$ by $20\%$.

\textbf{(b)} Keep $r$ the same and reduce $h$ by $20\%$.

\textbf{(c)} Reduce $h$ by $10\%$ and reduce $r$ by $10\%$.

\textbf{(d)} Increase $h$ by $10\%$ and reduce $r$ by $30\%$.

\textbf{(e)} All of the above give the same volume.
