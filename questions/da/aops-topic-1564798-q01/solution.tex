Since $6$ is the arithmetic mean of $1$ and $11$, the property gives $a_6=\sqrt{a_1a_{11}}$, so
\[
a_{11}=\dfrac{a_6^2}{a_1}=\dfrac{36}{1}=\boxed{36}.
\]
Such a sequence exists: $a_n=6^{\dfrac{n-1}{5}}$ is geometric, and for every geometric sequence of positive terms $\sqrt{a_pa_q}=a_{\dfrac{p+q}{2}}$ when $\dfrac{p+q}{2}$ is an integer.
