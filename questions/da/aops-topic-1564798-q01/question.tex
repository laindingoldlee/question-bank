A sequence of positive numbers $a_1,a_2,a_3,\dots$ has the property that whenever $r$ is the arithmetic mean of $p$ and $q$, the number $a_r$ is the geometric mean of $a_p$ and $a_q$. If $a_1=1$ and $a_6=6$, find $a_{11}$.
