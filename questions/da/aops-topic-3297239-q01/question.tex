(a) For coprime positive integers $a,b$, prove the simultaneous congruences $x\equiv r\pmod a$, $x\equiv s\pmod b$ have a unique solution modulo $ab$. (b) Show that $\gcd(6,10,15)=1$ alone does not ensure arbitrary congruences modulo $6,10,15$ are compatible.
