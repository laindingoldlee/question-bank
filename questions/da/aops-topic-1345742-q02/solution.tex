Divide by $5^p$: $\left(\dfrac{3}{5}\right)^p+\left(\dfrac{2}{5}\right)^p=1$. The left side is strictly decreasing in $p$, so there is at most one solution, and $p=1$ works ($\dfrac{3}{5}+\dfrac{2}{5}=1$). So $\boxed{p=1}$.
