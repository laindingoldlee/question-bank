Modulo 16 the number is 0. Euler's theorem gives $2^{500}\equiv1\pmod{625}$ because $\varphi(625)=500$, hence $2^{1000}\equiv1\pmod{625}$. Write its residue modulo 10000 as $625k+1$, with $0\le k\le15$. Since $625\equiv1\pmod{16}$, divisibility by 16 requires $k=15$. Thus the final four digits are $\boxed{9376}$.
