Let $E$ be the exponent. We claim that $E$ is even. If all $2019$ factors $a_1+a_2,\ a_2+a_3,\ \dots,\ a_{2019}+a_1$ were odd, then any two cyclically consecutive numbers $a_i,a_{i+1}$ would have opposite parities. Going once around the cycle $a_1\to a_2\to\cdots\to a_{2019}\to a_1$ makes $2019$ changes of parity, an odd number, so $a_1$ would have the opposite parity to itself, which is impossible. Hence some factor is even and $E$ is even.

Write $E=2m$ with $m\ge1$. Then $4^E=16^m$, and every positive power of $16$ ends in $6$ (since $6\cdot6=36$ ends in $6$). So $4^E-1$ ends in $\boxed{5}$.
