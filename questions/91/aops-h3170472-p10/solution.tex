Use seven students labeled $1,\ldots,7$. Consider the seven triples
\[
123,\quad145,\quad167,\quad246,\quad257,\quad347,\quad356.
\]
Take their complements as the seven four-person groups. The triples are the lines of the following explicit incidence design: direct checking of the displayed list shows that any two triples share exactly one label. Their union therefore has size five, so the intersection of their complementary groups has size $7-5=2$.
Each student occurs in three of the triples, and therefore is absent from three of the complementary groups. In particular, no student is in every group, so no pair is together in every group. This proves all the requested properties.
