Let the original price be $p$ dollars. The sale price is $0.6p$, and with the tax Tim paid $1.1\cdot0.6p=0.66p$. So
\[
0.66p=79.20\quad\Longrightarrow\quad p=\dfrac{79.20}{0.66}=120 .
\]
The original price was $\boxed{\$120}$. Check: $40\%$ off gives $\$72$, and $10\%$ tax on $\$72$ is $\$7.20$, for a total of $\$79.20$.
