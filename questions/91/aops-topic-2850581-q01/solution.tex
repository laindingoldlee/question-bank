The primes are $2,3,5$. Let $N(d)=\left\lfloor\dfrac{2019}d\right\rfloor$. Numbers divisible by at least the pair $\{2,3\}$, $\{2,5\}$, $\{3,5\}$ number $N(6)=336$, $N(10)=201$, $N(15)=134$; each number divisible by all three ($N(30)=67$) is counted in all three pairs. So the count for exactly two is
\[336+201+134-3\cdot67=\boxed{470}.\]
