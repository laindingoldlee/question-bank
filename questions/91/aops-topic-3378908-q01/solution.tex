Factoring the numerator gives $317!316!(1+317+317\cdot318)=317!316!\cdot318^2$. Similarly the denominator is $314!313!\cdot315^2$. Their ratio simplifies to $314\cdot316^2\cdot317\cdot318^2=2^7\cdot3^2\cdot53^2\cdot79^2\cdot157\cdot317$. The listed factors $53,79,157,317$ are prime, as trial division by primes up to their square roots confirms. Thus the divisor count is $(7+1)(2+1)^3(1+1)^2=\boxed{864}$.
