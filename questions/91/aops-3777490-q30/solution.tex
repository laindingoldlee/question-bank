The difference between the two sides is $(a-\dfrac{b}{2})^2+(d-\dfrac{c}{2})^2+\dfrac{3}{4}(b^2+c^2)-pbc$. Since $b^2+c^2\ge2|bc|$, this is nonnegative when $|p|\le\dfrac{3}{2}$. Necessity follows by setting $a = \dfrac{b}{2} , d = \dfrac{c}{2}$ and $b=c=1$ or $b=-c=1$, which force $p\le\dfrac{3}{2}$ and $p\ge-\dfrac{3}{2}$. Thus $\boxed{-\dfrac{3}{2}\le p\le\dfrac{3}{2}}$.
