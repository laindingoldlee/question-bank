Let the point be $x\in[2,8]$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $x$ is closer to $4$ than to $2$ exactly when $x>3$ (the midpoint of $2$ and $4$).
\item $x$ is closer to $4$ than to $8$ exactly when $x<6$ (the midpoint of $4$ and $8$).
\end{itemize}
So the favourable points form the interval $(3,6)$, of length $3$, out of the total length $6$. The probability is $\dfrac{3}{6}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$.
