A student solved the inequality $(2-7x)^{-1}\ge5$ as follows:
\[\dfrac{1}{2-7x}\ge5\ \Longrightarrow\ 1\ge5(2-7x)\ \Longrightarrow\ 1\ge10-35x\ \Longrightarrow\ x\ge\dfrac{9}{35}.\]
Find the error and solve the inequality correctly.
