Before the winning turn, every turn must consist of one non-six. Each complete pair of turns has probability $(\dfrac{5}{6})^2$. Richard's winning pair of sixes has probability $\dfrac{1}{36}$. Sum over the number of preceding complete pairs: $\dfrac{1}{36}\sum_{k\ge0}(\dfrac{25}{36})^k=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{11}}$.
