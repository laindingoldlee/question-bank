Richard and Tracy alternate turns, with Richard first. On a turn, a fair die is rolled; $1$ through $5$ end the turn, while a six allows another roll. Two consecutive sixes within a turn win. What is the probability Richard wins and the two winning sixes are the first sixes rolled by either player?
