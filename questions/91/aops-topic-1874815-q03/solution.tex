Since $10001=10^4+1$, the product is $1020304050\times10^4+1020304050$:
\[
\begin{array}{r}
10203040500000\\
+\quad1020304050\\\hline
10204060804050
\end{array}
\]
(Shifting by four places, the digit blocks overlap only where one of them has zeros, so no carrying occurs.) The product is $\boxed{10204060804050}$.
