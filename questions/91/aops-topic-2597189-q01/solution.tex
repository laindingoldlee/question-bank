$f'(x)=-6e^{3x}$. Setting $-6e^{3x}=-2$ gives $e^{3x}=\dfrac13$, so $x=-\dfrac{\ln3}{3}$. There $f(x)=-2\cdot\dfrac13+2=\dfrac43$. The tangent line is
\[y-\dfrac43=-2\left(x+\dfrac{\ln3}{3}\right),\qquad\text{i.e.}\qquad\boxed{y=-2x+\dfrac43-\dfrac{2\ln3}{3}}.\]
