In $\triangle ABC$, $AB=AC=10$. The bisector of $\angle A$ meets $\overline{BC}$ at $D$, and the bisector of $\angle C$ meets $\overline{AB}$ at $E$. If $AD=8$, find $\dfrac{AE}{BE}$.

$\textbf{(A)}\ \dfrac34$\qquad$\textbf{(B)}\ \dfrac56$\qquad$\textbf{(C)}\ 1$\qquad$\textbf{(D)}\ \dfrac43$\qquad$\textbf{(E)}\ \dfrac65$
