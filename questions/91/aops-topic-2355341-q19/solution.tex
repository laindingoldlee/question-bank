In the isosceles triangle, the bisector of the apex angle is also the median and the altitude, so $AD\perp BC$ and $BD=DC$. By Pythagoras in triangle $ABD$, $BD=\sqrt{10^2-8^2}=6$, so $BC=12$.

\end{asy}

By the angle bisector theorem in triangle $ABC$, the bisector from $C$ divides $AB$ in the ratio of the adjacent sides:
\[\dfrac{AE}{BE}=\dfrac{CA}{CB}=\dfrac{10}{12}=\dfrac56,\]
choice $\boxed{\textbf{(B)}\ \dfrac56}$.
