Multiply numerator and denominator by $\sqrt{10}-\sqrt6$. The denominator becomes $2(10-6)=8$, and the numerator
\[
(2+\sqrt6+\sqrt{10})(\sqrt{10}-\sqrt6)=2\sqrt{10}-2\sqrt6+(10-6)=4+2\sqrt{10}-2\sqrt6 .
\]
So the expression equals $\boxed{\dfrac{2+\sqrt{10}-\sqrt6}{4}}$. It cannot be simplified to a non-fraction just because the same symbols appear on top and bottom.
