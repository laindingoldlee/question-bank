To account for rotations, seat Audrey first; by rotating we may fix her seat, and then all remaining choices give distinct seatings.

\textbf{(a)} Fred must take the seat opposite Audrey (one way). The other $8$ kids fill the remaining $8$ seats in $8!=40320$ ways. Answer: $\boxed{40320}$.

\textbf{(b)} After Audrey and Fred are placed, Betty may take any of the $8$ remaining seats, and then Guang's seat (opposite Betty) is forced; it is free, because the seats of Audrey and Fred are opposite each other, so neither is opposite Betty. The remaining $6$ kids fill $6$ seats in $6!=720$ ways. Answer: $8\cdot720=\boxed{5760}$.
