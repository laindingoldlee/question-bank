The flips are independent and each shows heads with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, so
\[
P=\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{10}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{1024}}.
\]
(Equivalently, exactly one of the $2^{10}=1024$ equally likely sequences is all heads.)
