Putting knights on all 128 cells of one checkerboard color works, since every knight move changes color. For the upper bound, tile the board by 32 rectangles of size $ 2\times4 $. Within each block, pair the top-row cells in columns 1,2,3,4 with bottom-row cells in columns 3,4,1,2 respectively. Each pair consists of mutually attacking cells, so at most one knight lies in each of the four pairs. Thus each block holds at most 4, and the total is at most $ 32\cdot4=128 $. The maximum is $\boxed{128}$.
