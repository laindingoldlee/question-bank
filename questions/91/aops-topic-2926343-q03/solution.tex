Let $w_k$ be the winning probability from $k$: $w_0=0$, $w_4=1$, $w_k=0.6w_{k+1}+0.4w_{k-1}$. With $r=\dfrac{0.4}{0.6}=\dfrac23$, $w_k=\dfrac{1-r^k}{1-r^4}$, so
\[w_2=\dfrac{1-r^2}{1-r^4}=\dfrac1{1+r^2}=\dfrac1{1+\dfrac49}=\boxed{\dfrac9{13}}.\]
