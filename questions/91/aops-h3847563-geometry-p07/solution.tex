Let the angle between sides $a,b$ be $C$. Then $K=\dfrac{1}{2}ab\sin C$ and $\cos C=\dfrac{a^2+b^2-c^2}{2ab}$. Consequently $16K^2=4a^2b^2-(a^2+b^2-c^2)^2=((a+b)^2-c^2)(c^2-(a-b)^2)=(a+b+c)(a+b-c)(c+a-b)(c-a+b)=16s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)$. Taking the positive square root proves the formula for every nondegenerate triangle.

\end{asy}
