The longest diagonal is a diameter of the circumcircle, $2R$. Each side subtends $30^\circ$ at the centre, so $2=2R\sin15^\circ$ and $R=\dfrac1{\sin15^\circ}=\dfrac4{\sqrt6-\sqrt2}=\sqrt6+\sqrt2$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair[] V;for(int k=0;k<12;++k){V.push(dir(15+30*k));}for(int k=0;k<12;++k){draw(V[k]--V[(k+1)%12]);}draw(V[0]--V[6],linewidth(1));dot((0,0));
\end{asy}

The longest diagonal is $\boxed{2\left(\sqrt6+\sqrt2\right)}\approx7.73$.
