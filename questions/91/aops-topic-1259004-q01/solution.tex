A regular hexagon has $\dbinom62-6=9$ diagonals: $6$ short ones, which skip one vertex and have length $\sqrt3$ (they are sides of an equilateral triangle inscribed in the circumcircle of radius $1$), and $3$ long ones through the centre, of length $2$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair[] V; for(int k=0;k<6;++k) V[k]=dir(60*k);
draw(V[0]--V[1]--V[2]--V[3]--V[4]--V[5]--cycle,linewidth(1));
draw(V[0]--V[2]--V[4]--cycle,blue); draw(V[1]--V[3]--V[5]--cycle,blue);
draw(V[0]--V[3],red); draw(V[1]--V[4],red); draw(V[2]--V[5],red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The product is $\left(\sqrt3\right)^6\cdot2^3=27\cdot8=\boxed{216}$.
