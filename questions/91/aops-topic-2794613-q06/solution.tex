Expanding gives $ 7b^2+6b+3=3(b+5)^2+9(b+5)+4 $, or $ 4b^2-33b-121=(b-11)(4b+11)=0 $. Only $ b=11 $ is a valid positive integer base, and all digits are legal. Thus $ N=7\cdot121+6\cdot11+3=\boxed{916}$.
