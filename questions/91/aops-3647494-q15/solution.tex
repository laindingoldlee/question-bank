Let $a$ be the base apothem and $b$ its side. The $60^\circ$ face-base dihedral angle gives slant height $\dfrac{a}{\cos60^\circ}=2a$. The base consists of five triangles of area $\dfrac{ab}{2}$, so $\dfrac{ab}{2}=\dfrac{2025}{5}=405$. One lateral face has area $\dfrac{b(2a)}{2}=2\cdot405=\boxed{810}$.
