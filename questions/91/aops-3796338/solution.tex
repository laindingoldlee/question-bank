For the included angle $\theta=\angle BMC$, the area is $[BMC] = (\dfrac{1}{2}) 20 \cdot 26 \sin \theta \le 260$. Equality is attainable when $MB\perp MC$. Therefore the maximum is $\boxed{260}$.
