Group the denominator as $\left(2+\sqrt3\right)+\sqrt7$ and multiply the numerator and denominator by $\left(2+\sqrt3\right)-\sqrt7$:
\[\left(2+\sqrt3\right)^2-\left(\sqrt7\right)^2=\left(7+4\sqrt3\right)-7=4\sqrt3 .\]
So
\[\dfrac{4\sqrt3}{2+\sqrt3+\sqrt7}=\dfrac{4\sqrt3\left(2+\sqrt3-\sqrt7\right)}{4\sqrt3}=\boxed{2+\sqrt3-\sqrt7}.\]
