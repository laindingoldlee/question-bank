An ellipse has semi-axes $a$ and $b$, that is, it is the curve $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Using the area $\pi r^2$ of a circle, show that the area enclosed by the ellipse is $\pi ab$.
\item[(b)] Similarly, find the volume of the ellipsoid $\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}+\dfrac{z^2}{c^2}\le1$.
\end{enumerate}
