Scale so $A=(0,0),B=(1,0),C=(0,1)$, and put $r=\sqrt3$. The side-division ratios give $D = (\dfrac{2+r}{3+r} , \dfrac{1}{3+r})$ and $E = (\dfrac{r}{3+r} , \dfrac{3}{3+r})$. Ignoring the common positive denominator, the vectors $AD,AE$ have dot product $(2+r)r+3=6+2r$ and positive determinant $3(2+r)-r=6+2r$. Thus the sine and cosine of their angle are equal and positive, so $\boxed{\angle DAE=45^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
import graph;
usepackage("amsmath");
import geometry;
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(4,0),C=(0,4);real r=sqrt(3);pair D=4*((2+r)/(3+r),1/(3+r)),E=4*(r/(3+r),3/(3+r));draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(D--A--E);label("$A$",A,(-1,-1));label("$B$",B,(1,-1));label("$C$",C,(0,1));label("$D$",D,(1,1));label("$E$",E,(1,1));
\end{asy}
