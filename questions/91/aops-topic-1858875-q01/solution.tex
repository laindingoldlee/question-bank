Notice that $1000027=100^3+3^3$, a sum of cubes:
\[
1000027=(100+3)\left(100^2-100\cdot3+3^2\right)=103\cdot9709 .
\]
Also $9709=7\cdot1387=7\cdot19\cdot73$, and $103$ is prime (it is not divisible by $2,3,5,7$, and $11^2>103$). So
\[
1000027=7\cdot19\cdot73\cdot103,
\]
and its only three-digit prime factor is $\boxed{103}$.
