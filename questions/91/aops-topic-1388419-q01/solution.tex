\textbf{(a)} We need two numbers with product $3$ and sum $-4$: $-1$ and $-3$. So $x^2-4x+3=\boxed{(x-1)(x-3)}$.

\textbf{(b)} Put $u=zb$; the expression is $u^2+u-2$. Two numbers with product $-2$ and sum $1$ are $2$ and $-1$, so $u^2+u-2=(u+2)(u-1)$ and
\[
z^2b^2+zb-2=\boxed{(zb+2)(zb-1)}.
\]
Check by expanding: $(zb+2)(zb-1)=z^2b^2-zb+2zb-2=z^2b^2+zb-2$.
