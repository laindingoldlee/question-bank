Let $AC=b$ and $BC=a$. By Pythagoras in triangles $ACN$ and $BCM$:
\begin{asy}

\end{asy}

$b^2+\dfrac{a^2}4=361$ and $a^2+\dfrac{b^2}4=484$. Adding: $\dfrac54(a^2+b^2)=845$, so $AB^2=a^2+b^2=676$ and $AB=\boxed{26}$.
