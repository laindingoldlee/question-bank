From the fifth equation $U=L-I+A$, and the second gives $E=2A-I+1$. The sixth then gives $T=L-2I+A+1$. Substituting into the first yields $R=-I+2A-1$. The third gives $T=I+A+1$, so $L=3I$. The fourth now becomes $2I+2A-1=4A+7$, giving $A=I-4$. Consequently $T=2I-3$, $R=I-9$, $L=3I$, $U=3I-4$, and $E=I-7$. The last equation is $3I+9=-2I-2$, hence $I=-\dfrac{11}{5}$. All variables are therefore uniquely determined:
\[\boxed{(I,A,T,R,L,U,E)=-\dfrac{1}{5}(11,31,37,56,33,53,46)}.\]
Substitution verifies every equation. There is no positive-integer solution. All elimination steps were reversible, so there is no second real solution.
