Place the folded corner at $(0,0)$ and the square centre at $(\dfrac{s}{2} , \dfrac{s}{2})$. The crease is their perpendicular bisector, $x + y = \dfrac{s}{2}$, which cuts off a right triangle with legs $\dfrac{s}{2}$. Folding this triangle into the square reduces the outline area by $\dfrac{s^2}{8}$. Thus $\dfrac{s^2}{8}=8$, giving $\boxed{s=8\text{ cm}}$.
