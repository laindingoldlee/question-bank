Factorising,
\[35{,}335{,}872=2^6\cdot3^3\cdot11^2\cdot13^2 .\]
The possible ages are $11$, $12$, $13$, $14$. No age is $14$, since $7$ does not divide the product. The factors $11^2$ and $13^2$ must come from two students aged $11$ and two aged $13$ (no other allowed age is divisible by $11$ or $13$). The remaining three students have ages from $\{12\}$ with product $2^6\cdot3^3=1728=12^3$, so they are all $12$. The sum of the ages is
\[2\cdot11+3\cdot12+2\cdot13=\boxed{84}.\]
