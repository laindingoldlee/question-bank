There are $9\cdot12=108$ equiprobable ordered tile pairs. Matching pairs number $1\cdot6+5\cdot4+3\cdot2=32$, so (a) $\boxed{\dfrac{8}{27}}$. Red, green and blue matches score $21,27,15$. Thus (b) the expectation is $\dfrac{6(21)+20(27)+6(15)}{108}=\boxed7$ points per play. It is a long-run average score, not a probability. Conditional on scoring, the mean score is $7\div\dfrac{8}{27}=\dfrac{189}{8}$.
