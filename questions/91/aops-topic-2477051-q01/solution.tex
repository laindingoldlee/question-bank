The positive fourth powers are $1,16,81,256,625,1296,2401,\dots$.

If one of the four powers is at least $7^4=2401$, the sum is at least $2401+1+16+81=2499$.

Otherwise the four powers are chosen from $\{1,16,81,256,625,1296\}$, whose total is $2275$. Choosing four of these six means leaving out two, so the sum is $2275$ minus the sum of the two omitted powers. It exceeds $2018$ exactly when the omitted pair has sum less than $257$:
\[\{1,16\}:\ 17,\qquad\{1,81\}:\ 82,\qquad\{16,81\}:\ 97;\]
every other pair contains $256$ or a larger power together with a power at least $1$, so its sum is at least $257$. (The pair $\{1,256\}$ gives exactly $2018$.) These three choices give the sums $2258$, $2193$ and $2178$.

The smallest candidate is $2178<2499$, so the answer is
\[\boxed{2178}=1^4+4^4+5^4+6^4.\]
