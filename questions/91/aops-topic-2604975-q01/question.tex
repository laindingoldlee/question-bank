Simon's Favorite Factoring Trick rewrites $ab+ma+nb=R$ as $(a+n)(b+m)=R+mn$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show how to handle a coefficient on $ab$: for integers $k\ne0$, $m$, $n$ and $R$, prove that $kab+ma+nb=R$ is equivalent to $(ka+n)(kb+m)=kR+mn$.
\item[(b)] Use (a) to find all pairs of positive integers $(a,b)$ with $3ab+2a+4b=44$.
\end{enumerate}
