The medians $BN$ and $CM$ meet at the centroid $G$, which divides each in ratio $2:1$. So $BG=\dfrac23BN=\dfrac23CM=CG$ and $GN=\dfrac13BN=\dfrac13CM=GM$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,3),B=(-2,0),C=(2,0),M=(A+B)/2,N=(A+C)/2,G=(A+B+C)/3;
draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(C--M);draw(B--N);dot(G);label("$G$",G,S);
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$M$",M,W);label("$N$",N,E);
\end{asy}

Triangles $BGM$ and $CGN$ have $BG=CG$, $GM=GN$ and $\angle BGM=\angle CGN$ (vertical angles), so they are congruent (SAS) and $BM=CN$. Hence $AB=2BM=2CN=AC$. $\blacksquare$
