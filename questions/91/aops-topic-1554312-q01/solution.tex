Compute $\sigma(x)$ for $x=1,2,\dots,21$:
\[
1,3,4,7,6,12,8,15,13,18,12,28,14,24,24,31,18,39,20,42,32 .
\]
None of these is $36$. For $x=22=2\cdot11$,
\[
\sigma(22)=1+2+11+22=36 .
\]
So the answer is $\boxed{22}$. (In fact $22$ is the only positive integer with $\sigma(x)=36$.)
