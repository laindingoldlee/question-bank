Appending a $5$ before the final $7$ gives $N_{r+1}=10N_r-13$. Therefore
\[
9S=\sum_{r=0}^{98}(N_{r+1}-N_r+13)
=N_{99}-77+99\cdot13=N_{99}+1210.
\]
Thus $m=1210,n=9$ work. They are unique within the given bounds: comparing any other representation gives
\[
(n-9)N_{99}=9m-1210n.
\]
The right side has absolute value less than $4\,000\,000$, whereas $N_{99}>10^{100}$. Hence $n=9$, then $m=1210$. The answer is $\boxed{1219}$.
