Condition on the largest value $m$. A failed nondegenerate triangle has one side at least the sum of the other two; that largest side is unique. For a specified side $m$, the ordered positive pairs with sum at most $m$ number $\dfrac{m(m-1)}{2}$. Thus failures total $\dfrac{3\sum_{m=1}^6m(m-1)}{2}=105$. There are 216 ordered rolls, so the probability is $\dfrac{(216-105)}{216}=\boxed{\dfrac{37}{72}}$.
