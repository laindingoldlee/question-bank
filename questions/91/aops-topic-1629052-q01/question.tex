For real numbers, $ab=ba$ is one of the axioms, so it cannot be proved from the other rules of algebra. For the natural numbers $0,1,2,\dots$, however, multiplication can be \emph{defined} from addition by
\[
m\cdot0=0,\qquad m\cdot(n+1)=m\cdot n+m .
\]
Assume that addition is associative and commutative and that $m+0=m$. Prove from this definition that $m\cdot n=n\cdot m$ for all natural numbers $m$ and $n$.
