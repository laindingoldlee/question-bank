The domain requires integer $ x\ge4 $. Expanding $(x-2)!=(x-2)(x-3)(x-4)!$ and cancelling the positive common factors gives $ 1=(x-3)^2 $. Thus $ x=2 $ or 4 algebraically, but only 4 lies in the domain. Therefore $\boxed{x=4}$.
