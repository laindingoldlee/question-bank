Measure time in minutes after 12:00. Bob paints $\dfrac{1}{180}$ of the wall per minute and Anne $\dfrac{1}{240}$. Let the conflict start at time $t$. The whole job takes $145$ minutes: $t$ minutes together, $10$ minutes with nobody painting, and $135-t$ minutes of Bob alone. So
\[
t\left(\dfrac{1}{180}+\dfrac{1}{240}\right)+\dfrac{135-t}{180}=1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\dfrac{t}{240}+\dfrac{135}{180}=1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\dfrac {t}{240}=\dfrac{1}{4} ,
\]
so $t=60$. The conflict started at $\boxed{13\!:\!00}$.

(Check: in $60$ minutes together they paint $\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{7}{12}$ of the wall, and Bob paints the remaining $\dfrac{5}{12}$ in $\dfrac{5}{12}\cdot180=75$ minutes, from 13:10 to 14:25.)
