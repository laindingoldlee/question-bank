Bob can paint a wall in $3$ hours alone, and Anne can paint it in $4$ hours alone. They start painting together at 12:00. At some moment they have a conflict, and nobody paints for exactly $10$ minutes. After the conflict Anne leaves, and Bob paints the rest of the wall alone. The wall is finished at 14:25. At what time did the conflict start?
