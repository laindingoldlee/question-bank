Put $u=1-\cos x$, $v=2-2\cos(\dfrac{x}{2})$, and $w=4-4\cos(\dfrac{x}{4})$. Half-angle identities give $64\sin^2(\dfrac{x}{8})=8w$, $8w-w^2=4v$, $4v-v^2=2u$, and $2u-u^2=\sin^2x$. Applying these in succession reduces the expression to $\boxed{\sin^2x}$.

\end{asy}
