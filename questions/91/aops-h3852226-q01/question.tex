Consider the $11 \times 11$ grid of lattice points whose border forms a square with vertices at $(0, 0),(10, 0), (0, 10),$ and $(10, 10).$ Each lattice point in the grid is colored green or red. A unit square (a $1 \times 1$ square formed by four adjacent lattice points) is called $\emph{happy}$ if exactly two of its four vertices are green and the other two are red. How many ways are there to color the lattice points so that every unit square in the grid is $\emph{happy}$?
