Represent green and red by $1$ and $-1$. For two neighboring rows $u,v$, the happy-square condition is
\[
(u_j+v_j)+(u_{j+1}+v_{j+1})=0.
\]
Thus $u_j+v_j$ alternates between a number $t$ and $-t$, where $t\in\{-2,0,2\}$. If $t=0$, the two rows are opposite entry by entry. If $t=\pm2$, both rows are alternating.

If some row is not alternating, its neighbors must be its entrywise opposite; propagation shows that every column alternates. If no such row exists, every row alternates. Conversely either of these properties guarantees every square is happy.

There are $2^{11}$ colorings with all rows alternating, and $2^{11}$ with all columns alternating. Their intersection consists of the two checkerboards. Inclusion-exclusion gives
\[
\boxed{2^{11}+2^{11}-2=4094}.
\]
