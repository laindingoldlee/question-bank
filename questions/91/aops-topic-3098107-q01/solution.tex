Burnside’s lemma averages the numbers fixed by the six rotations. The identity fixes $\binom63=20$. Rotations by 1 or 5 positions fix none. Rotations by 2 or 4 positions have two cycles of length 3 and fix 2 arrangements each. A rotation by 3 has only cycles of length 2 and cannot preserve exactly three red beads. Thus the answer is $\dfrac{20+2+2}{6}=\boxed4$.
