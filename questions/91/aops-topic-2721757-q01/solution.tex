The second condition means $b^2=4a$, so $b$ is even, $b=2c$ and $a=c^2$. The first means $a^2<4b$, i.e. $c^4<8c$, so $c^3<8$ and $c=1$. Thus $(a,b)=(1,2)$ (check: $x^2+x+2$ has discriminant $-7$, and $x^2+2x+1=(x+1)^2$), and the answer is $\boxed{1}$.
