The positive integers whose digits are strictly increasing from left to right are listed in increasing order:
\[
1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9,12,13,14,\dots
\]
(so numbers such as $11$, $22$, $555$, $7778$ are not in the list). What is the $256$th number in the list?
