A number with strictly increasing digits cannot contain the digit $0$ (it could only be the leading digit). So such numbers correspond exactly to nonempty subsets of $\{1,2,\dots,9\}$: write the chosen digits in increasing order. The number of $k$-digit ones is $\dbinom9k$.

Counting by length (shorter numbers come first):
\[
\dbinom91+\dbinom92+\dbinom93+\dbinom94=9+36+84+126=255 .
\]
So the first $255$ numbers are those with at most $4$ digits, and the $256$th is the smallest $5$-digit number with strictly increasing digits, which is $\boxed{12345}$.
