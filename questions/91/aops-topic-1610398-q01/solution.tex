Let $n>1$ have prime factorisation $\prod p^{e_p}$. The square root, cube root and fourth root are integers exactly when every exponent $e_p$ is divisible by $2$, $3$ and $4$, i.e. by $\operatorname{lcm}(2,3,4)=12$. So $n=m^{12}$ for an integer $m\ge2$, and the smallest is
\[
2^{12}=\boxed{4096}
\]
(with $\sqrt{4096}=64$, $\sqrt[3]{4096}=16$, $\sqrt[4]{4096}=8$).
