The object, its shadow and the sun's ray through the top of the object form a right triangle with the angle $20^\circ$ at the tip of the shadow. If $L$ is the length of the shadow, then $\tan20^\circ=\dfrac2L$, so
\[L=\dfrac{2}{\tan20^\circ}\approx\boxed{5.49\text{ m}}.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real L=2/Tan(20);
pair P=(0,0),Q=(0,2),R=(L,0);
draw(P--Q,linewidth(1.5));draw(P--R,linewidth(0.8));draw(Q--R,dashed);
draw((0.25,0)--(0.25,0.25)--(0,0.25));
draw(arc(R,0.9,160,180));label("$20^\circ$",R+0.9*dir(170),W,fontsize(8pt));
label("$2$",(0,1),W);label("$L$",(L/2,0),S);label("sun's rays",(Q+R)/2,NE,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}
