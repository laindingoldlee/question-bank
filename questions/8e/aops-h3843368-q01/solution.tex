Write $n=\sum_{j=0}^r d_j10^j$, with $d_0d_r\ne0$. Before carries, the product coefficients are those of
\[
\left(\sum_{j=0}^r d_jX^j\right)
\left(\sum_{j=0}^r d_{r-j}X^j\right).
\]
This polynomial is symmetric under reversing its $2r+1$ coefficients.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Its central coefficient is $\sum_{j=0}^r d_j^2$. If any digit is at least $4$, this coefficient is at least $16$, so a carry is necessary.
\item Every coefficient is a dot product of two partial digit lists. Cauchy-Schwarz bounds it by $\sum d_j^2\le9$. Thus there are no carries, and the symmetric coefficient list is already the decimal digit list of the product. Its first and last coefficients are nonzero, so the product is a palindrome.
\item No: $\boxed{222\cdot222=49284}$, which is not a palindrome despite every digit being $2$.
\end{enumerate}
