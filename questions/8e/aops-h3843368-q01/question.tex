For a positive integer $n$ not divisible by $10$, let $R(n)$ reverse its decimal digits.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Prove that if a digit of $n$ is at least $4$, the usual multiplication of $nR(n)$ requires a carry.
\item Prove that if the sum of the squares of the digits of $n$ is at most $9$, then $nR(n)$ is a palindrome.
\item Is it true that having every digit at most $2$ always makes $nR(n)$ a palindrome? Prove or disprove.
\end{enumerate}
