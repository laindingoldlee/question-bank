A prime is positive, so $0<x<740$. Let $p=\dfrac{x^2}{740-x}$ be prime. Then $p(740-x)=x^2$, so $p\mid x^2$ and hence $p\mid x$; write $x=pk$ with $k\ge1$. Dividing $p(740-pk)=p^2k^2$ by $p$ gives $740=pk+pk^2$, i.e.
\[pk(k+1)=740=2^2\cdot5\cdot37.\]
So $k(k+1)$ is a divisor of $740$ that is a product of two consecutive integers. The divisors are $1,2,4,5,10,20,37,74,148,185,370,740$, and only $2=1\cdot2$ and $20=4\cdot5$ qualify. $k=1$ gives $p=370$, not prime; $k=4$ gives $p=37$, prime. Hence $x=pk=\boxed{148}$, and indeed $\dfrac{148^2}{592}=\dfrac{21904}{592}=37$.
