Place the base centre at the origin, with $A=(\sqrt3,0,0)$, $B=(-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2},\dfrac{3}{2},0)$, $C=(-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2},-\dfrac{3}{2},0)$ and $P=(x,y,z)$ with $z>0$. Subtracting squared distances gives $3\sqrt3x-3y=7$ and $3\sqrt3x+3y=16$, hence $x=\dfrac{23}{6\sqrt3},y=\dfrac{3}{2}$. From $PA^2=9$, $|P|^2=\dfrac{41}{3}$, and thus $z^2=\dfrac{176}{27}$. Any sphere through $A,B,C$ has centre $(0,0,t)$ and squared radius $3+t^2$. Equality of its distances to $A$ and $P$ gives $2tz=|P|^2-3=\dfrac{32}{3}$. Hence $t^2=\dfrac{(\dfrac{16}{3})^2}{\dfrac{176}{27}}=\dfrac{48}{11}$. Therefore $R^2=3+\dfrac{48}{11}=\dfrac{81}{11}$ and $\boxed{R=\dfrac{9}{\sqrt{11}}}$.
