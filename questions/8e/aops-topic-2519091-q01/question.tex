Prove that if $AB=AC$ in triangle $ABC$, then $\angle ABC=\angle ACB$.

A student gives this proof: ``Let the bisector of angle $A$ meet $BC$ at $Z$. Since $AB=AC$, $AZ=AZ$ and $\angle BAZ=\angle CAZ$, triangles $ABZ$ and $ACZ$ are congruent by SAS. So $\angle ABC=\angle ACB$, because they are corresponding angles.'' Is this proof correct? Explain.
