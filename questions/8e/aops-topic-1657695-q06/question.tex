A triangle in the Cartesian plane has vertices $(x,2)$, $(18,3x)$ and $(6,5x)$. Let $p(x)$ be the polynomial with $p(0)<0$ such that $\left|p(x)\right|$ is the area of the triangle for all real numbers $x$. What is the sum of the coefficients of $p(x)$?
