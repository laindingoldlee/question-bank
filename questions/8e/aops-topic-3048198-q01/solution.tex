All prime factors belong to $300=2^2\cdot3\cdot5^2$. For prime 2, the gcd forces both exponents in $a,b$ to be 2; the exponent in $c$ can be 0,1,2, giving three choices. For prime 3, both exponents in $a,b$ are 1 and that in $c$ can be 0 or 1, giving two choices. For prime 5, at least one of $a,b$ has exponent zero, so the lcm conditions force exponent 2 in $c$. The ordered exponent pairs in $a,b$ are $(0,0),(0,1),(0,2),(1,0),(2,0)$, giving five choices. Independent prime choices give $\boxed{3\cdot2\cdot5=30}$ triples.
