$24000=24\cdot1000=2^6\cdot3\cdot5^3$. The sum of divisors is multiplicative, so
\[\sigma(24000)=\left(1+2+\cdots+2^6\right)(1+3)\left(1+5+5^2+5^3\right)=127\cdot4\cdot156=\boxed{79248}.\]
