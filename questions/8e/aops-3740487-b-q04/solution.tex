The diagonals of a rhombus bisect each other at right angles. Their half-lengths are $7$ and $24$, so each side is $\sqrt{7^2+24^2}=25$. The perimeter is $4\cdot25=\boxed{100}$, choice D.
\begin{asy}
import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
pair A=(-24,0),B=(0,7),C=(24,0),D=(0,-7);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C^^B--D,dashed);label("$A$",A,(-1,0));label("$B$",B,(0,1));label("$C$",C,(1,0));label("$D$",D,(0,-1));label("24",(24/2,0),(0,-1));label("7",(0,7/2),(1,0));
\end{asy}
