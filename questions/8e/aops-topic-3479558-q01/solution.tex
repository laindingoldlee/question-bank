The new price is $\dfrac{3}{5}$ of the old price. Attendance must therefore be multiplied by $\dfrac{5}{3}$. The increase is $\dfrac{5}{3}-1=\dfrac{2}{3}=\boxed{66\dfrac{2}{3}\%}$.
