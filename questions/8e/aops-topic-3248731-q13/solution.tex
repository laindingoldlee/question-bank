Factor $20!+24!=20!(1+21\cdot22\cdot23\cdot24)=20!\cdot255025=20!\cdot5^2 101^2$. In $20!$, the prime exponents for $2,3,5,7$ are $18,8,4,2$; the other primes $11,13,17,19$ have exponent one. Thus the number of square divisors is $(\lfloor\dfrac{18}{2}\rfloor+1)(\lfloor\dfrac{8}{2}\rfloor+1)(\lfloor\dfrac{6}{2}\rfloor+1)(\lfloor\dfrac{2}{2}\rfloor+1)(\lfloor\dfrac{2}{2}\rfloor+1)=\boxed{800}$. A square divisor independently chooses an even exponent for each prime.
