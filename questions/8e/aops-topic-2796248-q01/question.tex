Catherine and Doris enter the first floor of a $101$-floor skyscraper and must reach a meeting on a certain floor. The elevator is at the top floor; it takes $1$ minute to come down to the first floor, and then $1$ second per floor to go up. Walking up the stairs takes $16$ seconds per floor. Doris walks while Catherine waits for the elevator and rides it up. They arrive at the same moment. On which floor is the meeting?
