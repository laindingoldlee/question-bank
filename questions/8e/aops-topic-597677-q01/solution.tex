The primes in increasing order are $2,3,5,7,\dots$. The pair $(2,4)$ fails because $4$ is not prime, and $(3,5)$ is a twin prime pair. So the smallest pair is $(3,5)$, with mean $\dfrac{3+5}{2}=\boxed{4}$.
