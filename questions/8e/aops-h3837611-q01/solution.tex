Let the centers be $O_1,O_2,O_3$, with $X$ on $O_2O_3$, $Y$ on $O_3O_1$, and $Z$ on $O_1O_2$. Write the center-triangle angles as $\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3$. The isosceles triangles $O_2XZ$ and $O_3XY$ give
\[
\angle O_2XZ=\dfrac{180^\circ-\alpha_2}{2},\qquad
\angle O_3XY=\dfrac{180^\circ-\alpha_3}{2}.
\]
Since $XO_2$ and $XO_3$ are opposite rays,
\[
\angle YXZ=180^\circ-\dfrac{180^\circ-\alpha_2}{2}
-\dfrac{180^\circ-\alpha_3}{2}
=90^\circ-\dfrac{\alpha_1}{2}<90^\circ.
\]
It is positive, and the other two angles obey the same formula. Thus right and obtuse triangles cannot be realized under external tangency.

For an equilateral triangle $XYZ$, construct an equilateral triangle $O_1O_2O_3$ having it as its medial triangle. Its sides are $2a$. Circles of radius $\boxed a$ centered at its vertices are pairwise externally tangent exactly at the side midpoints $X,Y,Z$, giving the requested construction.

\end{asy}
