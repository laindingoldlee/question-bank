Three circles of positive radii are pairwise externally tangent, with noncollinear centers. Let $X,Y,Z$ be their three tangency points.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Prove that triangle $XYZ$ is acute. Hence explain why arbitrary nondegenerate triangles cannot all arise in this way.
\item Show that every equilateral triangle of side length $a$ can arise, and determine the radii in a construction using three equal circles.
\end{enumerate}
