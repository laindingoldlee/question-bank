Triangle $ABC$ has vertices $A(x_1,y_1)$, $B(x_2,y_2)$ and $C(x_3,y_3)$. Prove that its three medians pass through the point
\[G=\left(\dfrac{x_1+x_2+x_3}{3},\ \dfrac{y_1+y_2+y_3}{3}\right),\]
and that $G$ divides each median in the ratio $2:1$ from the vertex.
