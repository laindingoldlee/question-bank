Let $y=\sqrt{x^2+18x+45}\ge0$. The equation becomes $y^2-15=2y$, i.e.\ $(y-5)(y+3)=0$, so $y=5$. Then $x^2+18x+45=25$, i.e.\ $x^2+18x+20=0$, whose discriminant $324-80>0$ gives two real roots, both satisfying the original equation. Their product is $\boxed{20}$.
