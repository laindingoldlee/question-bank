By the binomial theorem,
\[
9^{2004}=(10-1)^{2004}=\sum_{k=0}^{2004}\dbinom{2004}{k}10^k(-1)^{2004-k}.
\]
Every term with $k\ge2$ is a multiple of $100$, so
\[
9^{2004}\equiv1-2004\cdot10=1-20040\equiv1-40=-39\equiv61\pmod{100}.
\]
The last two digits are $6$ and $1$, and their sum is $\boxed{7}$.
