Group the terms in blocks of four: for $k=1,\dots,25$ the $k$-th block from the end is
\[
(4k)^2+(4k-1)^2-(4k-2)^2-(4k-3)^2 .
\]
By the difference of squares, $(4k)^2-(4k-2)^2=2(8k-2)$ and $(4k-1)^2-(4k-3)^2=2(8k-4)$, so the block equals $2(16k-6)=32k-12$. Hence
\[
N=\sum_{k=1}^{25}(32k-12)=32\cdot\dfrac{25\cdot26}{2}-12\cdot25=10400-300=10100 ,
\]
and the remainder on division by $1000$ is $\boxed{100}$.
