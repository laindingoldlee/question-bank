For every positive integer $k$,
\[k\cdot k!=(k+1)\cdot k!-k!=(k+1)!-k!.\]
So the sum telescopes:
\[\sum_{k=1}^{2001}k\cdot k!=(2!-1!)+(3!-2!)+\cdots+(2002!-2001!)=2002!-1 .\]
Hence $\boxed{2002!}$ is larger, by exactly $1$.
