Four points are chosen independently and uniformly at random inside a square.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What is the probability that the four points are the vertices of a convex quadrilateral? You may use the fact that the expected area of the triangle formed by three independent uniformly random points in a square of area $1$ is $\dfrac{11}{144}$.
\item[(b)] What is the probability that the four points lie on a circle (so that they are the vertices of a cyclic quadrilateral)?
\end{enumerate}
