The perimeter is $4+3+5=12$ parts, so one part is $\dfrac{18}{12}=1.5$ inches, and the sides are $\boxed{6,\ 4.5\text{ and }7.5}$ inches. (Since $4.5^2+6^2=7.5^2$, it is a right triangle.)

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0), B=(6,0), C=(6,4.5);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(B+(-0.35,0)--B+(-0.35,0.35)--B+(0,0.35));
label("$6$",(A+B)/2,S); label("$4.5$",(B+C)/2,E); label("$7.5$",(A+C)/2,NW);
\end{asy}
