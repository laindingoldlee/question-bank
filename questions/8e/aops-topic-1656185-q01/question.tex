In rectangle $ABCD$ with $AB=4$ and $BC=3$, $M$ is the midpoint of $\overline{CD}$. The segments $\overline{AC}$ and $\overline{BM}$ divide the interior of the rectangle into four regions $R_1$, $R_2$, $R_3$, $R_4$, whose areas are in decreasing order from $R_1$ to $R_4$. What is $[R_1]-[R_2]+[R_3]-[R_4]$? (Here $[R]$ denotes the area of $R$.)

(a) $2$\quad (b) $\dfrac{9}{4}$\quad (c) $\dfrac{5}{2}$\quad (d) $\dfrac{11}{4}$\quad (e) $3$\quad (f) $\dfrac{7}{2}$\quad (g) $4$
