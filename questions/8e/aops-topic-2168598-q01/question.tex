A student writes the following chain of equations and concludes that $10=9$:
\begin{align*}
2\sqrt1&=x &&\text{(1)}\\
2&=x-\sqrt1 &&\text{(2)}\\
4&=x^2-2x\sqrt1+1 &&\text{(3)}\\
4+2x\sqrt1&=x^2 &&\text{(4)}\\
10&=9 &&\text{(5)}
\end{align*}
Line (5) is obtained by substituting into line (4) the value of $x$ given by line (2). Exactly two of the steps from one line to the next are invalid. Find them, and explain what the correct versions of those steps give.
