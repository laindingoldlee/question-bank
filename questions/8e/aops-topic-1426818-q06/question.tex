Integers $a,b,c,d,e,x$ satisfy
\[
5^a+5^b+5^c+5^d+5^e=5^x .
\]
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $a=b=c=d=e$ and $x=a+1$.
\item[(b)] Explain why $x$ has no smallest possible value, and find the smallest possible value of $x$ if $a,b,c,d,e$ are required to be nonnegative integers.
\end{enumerate}
