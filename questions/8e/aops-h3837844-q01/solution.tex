Each element must belong to at least one of the three subsets, but may not belong to all three. Its membership pattern therefore has either one or two selected subsets, giving $\binom31+\binom32=6$ choices. The ten elements choose independently, yielding
\[
\boxed{6^{10}=60466176}.
\]
Pairwise intersections are allowed; only the intersection of all three is required to be empty.
