An eagle perched on top of a $9$ ft high pillar sees a snake on the ground $27$ ft from the bottom of the pillar. The snake slides in a straight line towards its hole at the bottom of the pillar. At that moment the eagle flies in a straight line to grab the snake. If the eagle and the snake move at equal speeds, at what distance from the bottom of the pillar does the eagle grab the snake?
