Divide by $3^y>0$:
\[
\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^y+\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^y=1 .
\]
The left side is a sum of two strictly decreasing functions of $y$, so it is strictly decreasing and takes the value $1$ at most once. Since $y=1$ works ($\dfrac{2}{3}+\dfrac{1}{3}=1$), the only solution is $\boxed{y=1}$.
