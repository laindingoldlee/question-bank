Let $a,b$ be complex numbers such that $a+b=a^2+b^2=\dfrac{2\sqrt3\,i}{3}$. Then $\left|\operatorname{Re}(a)\right|$ can be expressed in the form $\dfrac{\sqrt p}{q}$, where $p$ and $q$ are positive integers and $p$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. Find $p+q$.
