Sebastian is coloring a circular spinner with $4$ congruent sections. He randomly chooses one of four colors for each of the sections. If two or more adjacent sections have the same color, he fuses them and considers them as one section. (Sections meeting at only one point are not adjacent.) Suppose that the expected number of sections in the final colored spinner is equal to  $\dfrac{m}{n}$ for relatively prime positive integers $m$ and $n$. Compute $m + n$.
