Write the decimals as fractions: $9.5=\dfrac{19}{2}$ and $2.5=\dfrac{5}{2}$. Then
\[
9.5\cdot2.5\sqrt3=\dfrac{19}{2}\cdot\dfrac{5}{2}\sqrt3=\boxed{\dfrac{95\sqrt3}{4}}=23.75\sqrt3 .
\]
