Let the odd numbers be $a,a+2,a+4,a+6$ and the even numbers $e,\dots,e+8$. Then $4a+12=5e+20$, so $4a=5e+8$. Hence $4\mid5e$, i.e. $4\mid e$; write $e=4m$, so $a=5m+2$. Since $a$ is odd, $m$ is odd, and then $a\equiv7\pmod{10}$.

So the four odd numbers end in $7,9,1,3$ and never in $5$. The other choices do occur: $m=149$ gives $a=747$ (odd numbers $747,749,751,753$), and $m=185$ gives the even numbers $740,742,\dots,748$ (with $a=927$; check: $4\cdot927+12=3720=5\cdot740+20$). The number that can never occur is $\boxed{\textbf{(A) }745}$.
