Put $\alpha=45+\sqrt{2024}$ and $\beta=45-\sqrt{2024}$. Then $\alpha+\beta=90$, $\alpha\beta=1$, and $0<\beta<1$. The quantities $U_n=\alpha^n+\beta^n$ satisfy
\[
U_0=2,\quad U_1=90,\quad U_n=90U_{n-1}-U_{n-2},
\]
so they are integers. Since $\alpha^5=U_5-\beta^5$ and $0<\beta^5<1$, $N=U_5-1$.

Modulo $100$, the terms $U_0,\ldots,U_5$ are $2,90,98,30,2,50$. Consequently $\boxed{N\equiv49\pmod{100}}$.
