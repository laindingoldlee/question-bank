Mr.~N has $10$ rectangular blocks: one of size $2\times2\times2$, three of size $1\times1\times2$ and six of size $1\times1\times1$. He builds one larger solid rectangular box (cuboid) by fitting together some or all of these blocks, with no gaps or overlaps. What is the largest possible surface area of the box?
