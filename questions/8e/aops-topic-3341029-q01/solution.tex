The handshake graph is a simple graph in which every vertex has degree two, so each component is a cycle of length at least three. On six vertices the only possibilities are one six-cycle or two triangles. Six-cycles number $\dfrac{(6-1)!}{2}=60$, with division by two for reversing direction. Two triangles correspond to unordered splits into two sets of three, numbering $\dfrac{1}{2}\binom63=10$. Total $\boxed{70}$.
