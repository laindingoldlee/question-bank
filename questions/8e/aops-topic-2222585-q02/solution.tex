Let the speed be $v$ metres per second.

While the light is above the train, the train moves its own length $x$ past the light, so $x=10v$.

From the front entering to the back leaving, the front travels the tunnel length plus the train length, so $x+300=20v=2x$.

Hence $\boxed{x=300}$ metres (and $v=30$ metres per second).
