The terrible Thompson triplets get their hands on a $1500$-page book whose pages are numbered $1$ to $1500$. They rip out every page whose number is a multiple of $2$, every page whose number is a multiple of $3$, and every page whose number is a multiple of $7$. How many pages are left? (Treat each page number as a separate page.)
