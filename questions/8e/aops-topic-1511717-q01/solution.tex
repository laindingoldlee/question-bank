Press the key a few times starting from $5$:
\[
5\ \longrightarrow\ \dfrac{1}{1-5}=-\dfrac{1}{4}\ \longrightarrow\ \dfrac{1}{1+\dfrac{1}{4}}=\dfrac{4}{5}\ \longrightarrow\ \dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{4}{5}}=5 .
\]
The display returns to $5$ after every $3$ presses. Since $100=3\cdot33+1$, after $100$ presses it shows the same as after $1$ press, namely $-\dfrac{1}{4}=-0.25$. The answer is $\boxed{\text{(A)}\ -0.25}$.
