A special key on a calculator replaces the number $x$ currently displayed with $\dfrac{1}{1-x}$. For example, if the calculator is displaying $2$ and the special key is pressed, the calculator will display $-1$, since $\dfrac{1}{1-2}=-1$. Now suppose that the calculator is displaying $5$. After the special key is pressed $100$ times in a row, the calculator will display
\[
\text{(A)}\ -0.25\qquad\text{(B)}\ 0\qquad\text{(C)}\ 0.8\qquad\text{(D)}\ 1.25\qquad\text{(E)}\ 5
\]
