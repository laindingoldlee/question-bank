The number of trailing zeros is the exponent of $5$ in $241!$:
\[\left\lfloor\dfrac{241}{5}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{241}{25}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{241}{125}\right\rfloor=48+9+1=\boxed{58}.\]
