For each sum $s$ of the four dice, the $20$-sided die exceeds it in $20-s$ ways (for $4\le s\le19$). Summing $(20-s)\cdot N(s)$ over the distribution of four dice gives $\dfrac{979}{3240}$. So the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{979}{3240}}\approx0.302$.
