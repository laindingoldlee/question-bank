Define $y_n=ax_n+\dfrac{b}{2}$. The special value of $c$ makes
\[
y_{n+1}=a(ax_n^2+bx_n+c)+\dfrac{b}{2}
=\left(ax_n+\dfrac{b}{2}\right)^2=y_n^2.
\]
Therefore $y_n=y_1^{\,2^{n-1}}=(a\alpha+\dfrac{b}{2})^{2^{n-1}}$. Solving for $x_n$ gives
\[
\boxed{x_n=\dfrac{(a\alpha+\dfrac{b}{2})^{2^{n-1}}-\dfrac{b}{2}}{a}}.
\]
The transformation and initial value verify the formula for every $n\ge1$.
