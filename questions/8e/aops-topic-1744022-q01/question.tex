Unit cubes are stacked to form a cube of edge length $5$. A square hole with side length $1$ is made through the centre of each face, straight through the entire figure (so three perpendicular tunnels meet at the centre). The resulting solid is dipped in paint and then taken apart into its unit cubes. How many of the unit cubes have exactly two faces painted?
