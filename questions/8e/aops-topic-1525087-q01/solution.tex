Let $k$ of the numbers be odd.

\textbf{Parity.} The even numbers contribute an even amount, so the sum $2015$ has the parity of $k$: $k$ is odd.

\textbf{Bound.} The $k$ odd numbers are distinct, so their sum is at least $1+3+\cdots+(2k-1)=k^2$. If $k\ge45$, this is at least $2025>2015$, which is impossible. Since $k$ is odd, $k\le43$.

\textbf{Construction.} Take the odd numbers $1,3,\dots,85$, with sum $43^2=1849$, and the four even numbers $2,4,6,154$, with sum $166$. These $47$ numbers are distinct and their sum is $1849+166=2015$.

So at most $\boxed{43}$ of the numbers are odd.
