Alice travels $4+9+8=21$ meters and takes $21$ seconds. Her net displacement is nine meters west and twelve north, whose length is $\sqrt{9^2+12^2}=15$ meters. Bob therefore takes $\dfrac{15}{0.5}=30$ seconds. The difference is $\boxed9$ seconds.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(220);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair O=(0,0),P=(0,4),Q=(-9,4),R=(-9,12);draw(O--P--Q--R);draw(O--R,dashed);label("Start",O,(1,-1));label("Math",R,(0,1));label("$4$",(O+P)/2,(1,0));label("$9$",(P+Q)/2,(0,-1));label("$8$",(Q+R)/2,(-1,0));
\end{asy}
