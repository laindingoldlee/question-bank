Pair the variables as $A=a+d$, $B=b+e$, $C=c+f$. The quotient-and-remainder algorithm uniquely recovers $(a,d)$ from each nonnegative $A$ on division by $10$, and similarly recovers $(b,e)$ and $(c,f)$ on division by $100$ and $1000$. Thus this is a bijection with nonnegative triples $A+B+C=2024$. Stars and bars gives $\boxed{\binom{2026}{2}}$, choice A.
