Since $\phi\approx1.618033989>1.609344$, the approximation overestimates. Its relative error is $\dfrac{\phi-1.609344}{1.609344}\cdot100\%\approx\boxed{0.54\%}$. The approximate relation is kilometers approximately equal to $\phi$ times miles, not the reverse. This is a numerical coincidence between a chosen unit conversion and the algebraic constant.
