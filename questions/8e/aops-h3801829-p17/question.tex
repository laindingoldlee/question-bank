At a gathering of knights and knaves there were $13$ people. The first said: ``There are more than $1$ knave here,'' the second: ``There are more than $2$ knaves,'' the third: ``There are more than $3$ knaves,'' and so on, with the $13$th saying: ``There are more than $13$ knaves.'' How many knaves were there? (Knights always tell the truth, and knaves always lie.)
