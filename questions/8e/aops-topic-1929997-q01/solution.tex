Let the numbers of mangoes, watermelons, apples and pineapples be $m,w,a,p$. Put $m'=m-2\ge0$; then
\[
m'+w+a+p=20,\qquad 0\le p\le5 .
\]
For a fixed $p$, the number of nonnegative solutions of $m'+w+a=20-p$ is $\dbinom{22-p}{2}$ by stars and bars. Hence the total is
\[
\dbinom{22}{2}+\dbinom{21}{2}+\dbinom{20}{2}+\dbinom{19}{2}+\dbinom{18}{2}+\dbinom{17}{2}=231+210+190+171+153+136=\boxed{1091}.
\]
