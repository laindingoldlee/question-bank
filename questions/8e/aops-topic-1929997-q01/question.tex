Mr.\ Nash makes a fruit salad from a total of $22$ fruits chosen from mangoes, watermelons, apples and pineapples (fruits of the same kind are identical). He takes at least $2$ mangoes and at most $5$ pineapples. In how many ways can he choose the $22$ fruits?
