The post is $\sqrt{4^2+3^2}=5$ feet from the origin, and the dog is at most $10$ feet from the post. By the triangle inequality its distance from the origin is at most $5+10=\boxed{15}$ feet, attained at the point of the circle on the ray from the origin through the post, $(12,9)$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));

draw(circle((4,3),10),blue); dot((0,0)); dot((4,3)); dot((12,9));
draw((0,0)--(12,9),red); label("$O$",(0,0),SW); label("$(4,3)$",(4,3),SE); label("$(12,9)$",(12,9),NE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
