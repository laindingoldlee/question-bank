The quarter-circle sector has area $\dfrac{1}{4}\pi5^2=\dfrac{25\pi}{4}$. The right triangle formed by its two radii has area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot5\cdot5=\dfrac{25}{2}$. The circular segment area is their difference, $\boxed{\dfrac{25\pi}{4}-\dfrac{25}{2}}$.
