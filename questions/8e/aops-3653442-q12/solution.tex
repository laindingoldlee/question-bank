The hypotenuse is $AC=25$. Put $A=(0,0),C=(25,0)$. The equations $AD=7$ and $AD\perp DC$ give $D = (\dfrac{49}{25} , \pm \dfrac{168}{25})$. Similarly $E = (\dfrac{576}{25} , \pm \dfrac{168}{25})$. The horizontal separation is always $\dfrac{527}{25}$; choosing equal signs makes the vertical separation zero, attaining the lower bound. Thus $\boxed{D E_{\min} = \dfrac{527}{25}}$.
