Products of diagonally opposite areas are equal. Depending on which known area is opposite the missing one, the fourth area is $\dfrac{6\cdot8}{12}=4$, $\dfrac{6\cdot12}{8}=9$, or $\dfrac{8\cdot12}{6}=16$. Each positive assignment satisfying this relation is realizable by choosing row heights and column widths. Thus the sum is $\boxed{29}$.
