I roll two pairs of ordinary six-sided dice (four fair dice in all) and multiply the two numbers in each pair, which gives two products. For example, if one pair shows $5$ and $6$ and the other shows $3$ and $2$, the products are $30$ and $6$. What is the probability that the larger of the two products (or their common value, if they are equal) is a multiple of $6$?
