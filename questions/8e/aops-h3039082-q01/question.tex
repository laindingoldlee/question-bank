Define the Fermi exponent of a positive number $x$ to be the nearest integer to $\log_{10}x$. Exponent towers are evaluated from the top down. Find the Fermi exponents of (a) $3^{3^3}$, (b) $4^{4^4}$, and (c) $c$ satisfying $0.35=7.8(0.01)c$. You may use $\log_{10}3\approx0.477121$ and $\log_{10}4\approx0.602060$.
