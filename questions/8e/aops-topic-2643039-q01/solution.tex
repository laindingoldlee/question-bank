The map $(a,b,c)\mapsto(a,\,b+1,\,c+2)$ is a bijection from such triples to triples $x<y<z$ chosen from $\{1,2,\dots,7\}$ (its inverse subtracts $0,1,2$). So the count is $\dbinom73=\boxed{35}$.

(Equivalently, by stars and bars, a nondecreasing triple is a multiset of size $3$ from $5$ values: $\dbinom{5+3-1}{3}=35$.)
