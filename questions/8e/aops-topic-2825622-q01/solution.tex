$\dfrac1{k^2-1}=\dfrac12\left(\dfrac1{k-1}-\dfrac1{k+1}\right)$, so the sum telescopes:
\[\sum_{k=2}^{10}\dfrac1{k^2-1}=\dfrac12\left(1+\dfrac12-\dfrac1{10}-\dfrac1{11}\right)=\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac{110+55-11-10}{110}=\dfrac{144}{220}.\]
Multiplying by $110$ gives $\boxed{72}$.
