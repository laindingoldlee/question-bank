Let the poles be at $x=\pm\dfrac d2$ and the vertex at height $20$. The cable sags $30$ m, so it is $y=20+\dfrac{120}{d^2}x^2$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;real d=47.337,h=30;real f(real t){return 20+4*h*t^2/d^2;}
draw((-d/2-6,0)--(d/2+6,0));draw((-d/2,0)--(-d/2,50),linewidth(1.2));draw((d/2,0)--(d/2,50),linewidth(1.2));
draw(graph(f,-d/2,d/2));draw((0,0)--(0,20),dashed);label("$20$",(0,10),E);label("$50$",(d/2,25),E);
draw((-d/2,-4)--(d/2,-4),Arrows);label("$d$",(0,-4),S);label("cable $80$",(8,f(8)),SE);
\end{asy}

Its length is
\[L(d)=2\int_0^{d/2}\sqrt{1+\left(\dfrac{240x}{d^2}\right)^2}\,dx.\]
Substituting $x=\dfrac d2u$ gives $L(d)=d\displaystyle\int_0^1\sqrt{1+\dfrac{3600u^2}{d^2}}\,du$, which increases with $d$. Solving $L(d)=80$ numerically gives $d\approx\boxed{47.3\text{ m}}$. (Check: $L(47.3)\approx79.97$ and $L(47.4)\approx80.03$.)
