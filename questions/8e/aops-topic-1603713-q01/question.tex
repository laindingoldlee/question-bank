A coin $8$ units in diameter is tossed onto a $5$ by $5$ grid of squares, each of side length $10$ units. The coin is in a winning position if no part of it touches or crosses a grid line; otherwise it is in a losing position. Given that the coin lands in a random position with no part of it off the grid, what is the probability that it is in a winning position? Express your answer as a common fraction.
