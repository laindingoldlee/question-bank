Factor $1000=2^3 5^3$. Exactly three digits must be $5$, and all remaining digits belong to $\{1,2,4,8\}$. The two remaining digits multiply to $8$, so they are $1,8$ or $2,4$. Each multiset has $\dfrac{5!}{3!}=20$ arrangements, giving $\boxed{40}$.
