Factor $561=3\cdot11\cdot17$. Fermat gives $a^2\equiv1\pmod3$, $a^{10}\equiv1\pmod{11}$, and $a^{16}\equiv1\pmod{17}$. Since 2,10,16 all divide 560, the number $a^{560}-1$ is divisible by each of these distinct primes and hence by their product 561. This proves the required congruence despite compositeness.
