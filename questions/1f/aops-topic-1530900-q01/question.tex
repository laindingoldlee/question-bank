A large semicircle has radius $N+1$, where $N$ is a positive integer. Along its diameter, inside the large semicircle, sit $N$ small semicircles of radius $1$, side by side, each with its diameter on the diameter of the large semicircle (the figure shows $N=4$). The shaded region is the part of the large semicircle outside all the small semicircles. Find its area in terms of $N$.
