A \emph{degenerate triangle} is one whose three vertices lie on a line (so it has zero area); a \emph{non-degenerate triangle} has three non-collinear vertices.

Three segments have lengths $3$, $5$ and $x$, where $x>0$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] For which values of $x$ do they form a non-degenerate triangle?
\item[(b)] For which values of $x$ do they form a degenerate triangle?
\end{enumerate}
