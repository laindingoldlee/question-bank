Some students in a gym class are wearing blue jerseys and the rest are wearing red jerseys. There are exactly $25$ ways to pick a team of three players that includes at least one player wearing each color. Compute the number of students.

A student set up the equation $b\cdot r\cdot(b+r-2)=25\cdot3!$, where $b$ and $r$ are the numbers of blue and red students, and found that it has no solution in positive integers. Find the error, and solve the problem.
