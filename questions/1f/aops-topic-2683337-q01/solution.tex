$y=(x-2)^2+3$, so the vertex is $V=(2,3)$. The line is $2x-y-3=0$, and the distance from $V$ is
\[\dfrac{|2\cdot2-3-3|}{\sqrt{2^2+1^2}}=\dfrac{2}{\sqrt5}=\dfrac{2\sqrt5}{5},\]
answer $\boxed{\textbf{(B)}}$. The foot of the perpendicular is $\left(\dfrac{14}{5},\dfrac{13}{5}\right)$.

label("$V(2,3)$",V,W,fontsize(8pt));label("$y=2x-3$",(4,5),E,fontsize(8pt));label("$y=x^2-4x+7$",(4,f(4)),E,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}
