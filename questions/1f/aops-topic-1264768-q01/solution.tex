There are $\dfrac{240}{4}=60$ multiples of $4$ and $\dfrac{240}{6}=40$ multiples of $6$. The integers that are multiples of both are the multiples of $\operatorname{lcm}(4,6)=12$: $20$ of them. By inclusion--exclusion the answer is $60+40-20=\boxed{80}$.
