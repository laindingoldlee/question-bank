The centre-to-line distance is $\dfrac{|3-5-b|}{\sqrt2}=4$, so $b=-2\pm4\sqrt2$. Their sum is $\boxed{-4}$.
\begin{asy}
import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
pair O=(5,3);draw(circle(O,4));for(int s:new int[]{-1,1}){real b=-2+s*4*sqrt(2);draw((-2,-2+b)--(12,12+b),blue);}dot(O);label("$(5,3)$",O,(1,0));label("two parallel tangents",(5,-10));
\end{asy}
