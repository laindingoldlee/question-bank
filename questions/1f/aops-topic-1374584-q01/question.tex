Solve for $x$ in terms of the real parameter $a$:
\[
\dfrac{3a+1}{a+x}-\dfrac{a-1}{a-x}=\dfrac{2a(a^2-1)}{x^2-a^2}.
\]
State for which values of $a$ there is no solution, a unique solution, or infinitely many solutions.
