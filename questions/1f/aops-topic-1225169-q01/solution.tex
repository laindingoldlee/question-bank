The series $1+2+4+8+\cdots$ diverges: its partial sums $2^n-1$ grow without bound, so $S$ is not a real number. The steps ``$2S=\ldots$'' and ``$=S-1$'' treat $S$ as a number and use rules (multiplying termwise, subtracting a term) that are only valid for convergent series. A geometric series $1+r+r^2+\cdots$ equals $\dfrac{1}{1-r}$ only when $|r|<1$; with $r=2$ the formula gives $-1$, but that is not the sum.
