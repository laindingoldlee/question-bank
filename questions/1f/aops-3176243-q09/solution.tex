The secant along the pole has external length 32 and whole length $32+2r$. By power of a point, $32(32+2r)=40^2$, so $32+2r=50$ and $\boxed{r=9}$.
\begin{asy}
import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
pair P=(0,0),O=(0,41),T=(360/41,1600/41);draw(circle(O,9));draw(P--(0,32));draw(P--T,blue);draw((0,32)--(0,50),dashed);label("$32$",(0,16),(-1,0));label("$40$",T/2,(1,0));label("$r$",O,(1,0));
\end{asy}
