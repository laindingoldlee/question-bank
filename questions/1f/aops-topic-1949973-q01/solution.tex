Reflect the house $H=(4,1)$ in the river to $H'=(4,-1)$. For any point $R$ on the river, $HR=H'R$, so the trip $H\to R\to G$ has length $H'R+RG\ge H'G$, with equality when $R$ lies on segment $H'G$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair H=(4,1),G=(-6,9),Hp=(4,-1);
draw((-7.5,0)--(6,0),blue+linewidth(1));label("river $y=0$",(-7.5,0),N+E,blue);
pair R=(3,0);
draw(H--R--G,linewidth(1));draw(R--Hp,dashed);draw(H--Hp,dotted);
dot(H);dot(G);dot(Hp);dot(R);
label("$H(4,1)$",H,E);label("$G(-6,9)$",G,W);label("$H'(4,-1)$",Hp,E);label("$R$",R,SW);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Hence the minimum is
\[
H'G=\sqrt{(4-(-6))^2+(-1-9)^2}=\sqrt{200}=\boxed{10\sqrt2},
\]
attained with the stop at $R=(3,0)$, where $H'G$ crosses the river.
