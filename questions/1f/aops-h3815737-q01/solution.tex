A nonvertical line through $P$ is $y=m(x-20)$, or $mx-y-20m=0$. Its distance from the origin is $\dfrac{|20m|}{\sqrt{m^2+1}}$. Tangency requires this distance to equal $5$, giving $400m^2=25(m^2+1)$, so $m^2=\dfrac{1}{15}$. The vertical line $x=20$ does not meet the circle. Thus the two tangents are
\[\boxed{y=\dfrac{x-20}{\sqrt{15}}\quad\text{and}\quad y=-\dfrac{x-20}{\sqrt{15}}}.\]
Their contact points are $\left(\dfrac{5}{4},\pm\dfrac{5\sqrt{15}}{4}\right)$, and the radii to them are perpendicular to the respective tangents.
