Rearrangement factors the equation:
\[
(x-1)(x+1-4y)=0.
\]
If $x=1$, all $201$ allowed values of $y$ work. Otherwise $x=4y-1$, and the bound $-100\le x\le100$ gives $-24\le y\le25$, totaling $50$ values. None has $x=1$, since that would require $y=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Hence the answer is $\boxed{201+50=251}$.
