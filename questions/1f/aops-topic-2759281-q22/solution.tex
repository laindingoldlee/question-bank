Place $S=(0,0)$, $R=(4,0)$, $Q=(4,3)$, $P=(0,3)$. The fold line $PR$ is $3x+4y=12$, and $Q'$ is the reflection of $Q$ in it. Since $3\cdot4+4\cdot3-12=12$,
\[Q'=(4,3)-\dfrac{2\cdot12}{25}(3,4)=\left(\dfrac{28}{25},-\dfrac{21}{25}\right),\]
and $Q'S=\dfrac{\sqrt{28^2+21^2}}{25}=\dfrac{35}{25}=\dfrac75$.
\begin{asy}

pair P=(0,3),Q=(4,3),R=(4,0),S_=(0,0);pair Qp=(28/25,-21/25);

label("$P$",P,NW);label("$Q$",Q,NE);label("$R$",R,SE);label("$S$",S_,W);label("$Q'$",Qp,S);label("$\dfrac75$",(S_+Qp)/2,W,fontsize(8pt));label("$4$",(2,3),N,fontsize(8pt));label("$3$",(4,1.5),E,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}

Answer $\boxed{\textbf{(E)}\ \dfrac75}$. (Alternatively, $PRQ'S$ is an isosceles trapezoid with $SQ'\parallel PR$.)
