Factor out $30^2$: since $\dfrac{150}{13}=30\cdot\dfrac{5}{13}$,
\[
h^2=30^2-30^2\left(\dfrac{5}{13}\right)^2=30^2\left(1-\dfrac{25}{169}\right)=30^2\cdot\dfrac{144}{169}.
\]
So $h=30\cdot\dfrac{12}{13}=\boxed{\dfrac{360}{13}}$.

The idea: the numbers come from a right triangle similar to the $5$-$12$-$13$ triangle with hypotenuse $30$; the legs are $30\cdot\dfrac{5}{13}$ and $30\cdot\dfrac{12}{13}$. (Equivalently, $h^2=\left(30-\dfrac{150}{13}\right)\left(30+\dfrac{150}{13}\right)=\dfrac{240}{13}\cdot\dfrac{540}{13}=\dfrac{360^2}{13^2}$ by the difference of squares.)
