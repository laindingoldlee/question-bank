A quantity $x$ depends on positive quantities $w$, $y$ and $z$ in the following way: whenever $w$ and $z$ are held fixed, $x$ is directly proportional to $y$; whenever $w$ and $y$ are held fixed, $x$ is directly proportional to $z$; and whenever $y$ and $z$ are held fixed, $x$ is inversely proportional to $w$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $\dfrac{xw}{yz}$ has the same value for all $w,y,z$.
\item[(b)] Given that $x=4$ when $(w,y,z)=(6,8,5)$, find $x$ when $(w,y,z)=(4,10,9)$.
\end{enumerate}
