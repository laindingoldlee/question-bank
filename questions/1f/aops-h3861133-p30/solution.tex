Put $A=(0,0),B=(3,0),C=(0,4)$. Equal angles subtending the same circle chord give $\angle PAS=\angle PQS=60^\circ$ and $\angle QAR=\angle QPR=60^\circ$. Since $R,S$ lie in the first quadrant, ray $AR$ makes $30^\circ$ with $AB$ and $AS$ makes $60^\circ$. For a point $T$ on $BC$ lying at ray angle $\theta$, the section ratio is $\dfrac{BT}{CT}=\dfrac{3\tan\theta}{4}$. Hence the requested sum is $\dfrac{3}{4}(\tan30^\circ+\tan60^\circ)=\boxed{\sqrt3}$.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

\end{asy}
