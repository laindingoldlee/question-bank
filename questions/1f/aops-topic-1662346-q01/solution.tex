A product of positive integers is prime only if one factor is a prime and all the other factors are $1$. So exactly one die must show a prime ($2$, $3$ or $5$) and the other eleven must show $1$.

Choose the die showing the prime ($12$ ways) and its value ($3$ ways); each such outcome has probability $\left(\dfrac{1}{6}\right)^{12}$. Hence
\[
P=\dfrac{12\cdot3}{6^{12}}=\dfrac{36}{6^{12}}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{6^{10}}}.
\]
