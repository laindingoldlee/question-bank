Weighted Cauchy-Schwarz gives
\[
(4a+5b+6c)^2
\le(a^2+2b^2+3c^2)\left(16+\dfrac{25}{2}+\dfrac{36}{3}\right)
=36\cdot\dfrac{81}{2}=1458.
\]
Equality requires $(a,b,c)=\lambda(4,\dfrac{5}{2},2)$. Taking $\lambda=2\dfrac{\sqrt 2}{3}$ meets the constraint and keeps all variables positive. Thus the maximum is $\boxed{27\sqrt2}$.
