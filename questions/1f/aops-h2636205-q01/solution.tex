(a) The interval has length $30$. The positive part has length $10$ and the negative part length $20$; the single point $0$ has probability zero. A positive product requires matching nonzero signs, so
\[
\boxed{\left(\dfrac{10}{30}\right)^2+\left(\dfrac{20}{30}\right)^2=\dfrac{5}{9}}.
\]

(b) There are $31$ possible integers, including $0$. Ten are positive and twenty are negative. Thus
\[
\boxed{\left(\dfrac{10}{31}\right)^2+\left(\dfrac{20}{31}\right)^2=\dfrac{500}{961}}.
\]
In the continuous model probabilities use lengths, whereas in the discrete model they use counts of equally likely outcomes. Zero has positive probability only in the latter model.
