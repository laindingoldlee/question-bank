Each spin is blue with probability $\dfrac{3}{4}$ and red with probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$, independently. A particular sequence with $4$ blues and $1$ red, such as BBBBR, has probability $\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^4\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{81}{1024}$. The red spin can be any of the $5$ spins, so
\[
P(\text{exactly 4 blue})=\dbinom54\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^4\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)=\dfrac{5\cdot81}{1024}=\boxed{\dfrac{405}{1024}}\approx39.6\%.
\]
This is not too large. Blue is likely on each spin, so one expects about $5\cdot\dfrac{3}{4}=3.75$ blues, and $4$ blues is in fact the most likely number: the probabilities of $5$, $4$, $3$ blues are $\dfrac{243}{1024}$, $\dfrac{405}{1024}$ and $\dfrac{270}{1024}$.
