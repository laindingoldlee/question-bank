The conditions are SSS (three corresponding sides), SAS (two corresponding sides and their included angle), ASA (two corresponding angles and their included side), AAS (two corresponding angles and a corresponding nonincluded side), and hypotenuse-leg for right triangles. In each case the specified corresponding measurements must be equal. AAA determines only similarity; unrestricted SSA is not sufficient.

\end{asy}
