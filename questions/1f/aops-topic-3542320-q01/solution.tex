Adding the equations gives $32-2 xy=5$, hence $xy=\dfrac{27}{2}$. Subtracting the first equation from the second gives $8(x-y)=1$, so $(x-y)^2=\dfrac{1}{64}$. Therefore $x^2+y^2=2 xy+(x-y)^2=27+\dfrac{1}{64}$. The target equals $x^2 y^2-x^2-y^2+1=\dfrac{729}{4}-27-\dfrac{1}{64}+1=\boxed{\dfrac{9999}{64}}$.
