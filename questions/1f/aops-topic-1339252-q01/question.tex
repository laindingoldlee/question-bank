$n$ different people ($n\ge3$) are to be arranged around a circle.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] How many arrangements are there if two arrangements count as the same when one is a rotation of the other (as for seating at a round table)?
\item[(b)] How many are there if, in addition, an arrangement and its mirror image count as the same (as for beads on a bracelet that can be turned over)?
\item[(c)] Give both numbers for $n=6$.
\end{enumerate}
