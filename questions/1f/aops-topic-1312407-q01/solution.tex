Distances are nonnegative, so $b\le0$. By the distance formula,
\[
(b+9)^2+(5+4)^2=(-b)^2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad b^2+18b+81+81=b^2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad18b=-162,
\]
so $b=\boxed{-9}$. Check: the points $(-9,-4)$ and $(-9,5)$ are $9=-b$ apart.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((-10,0)--(1,0),gray,Arrow); draw((0,-5)--(0,6),gray,Arrow);
pair P=(-9,-4),Q=(-9,5); dot(P);dot(Q); draw(P--Q,red);
label("$(-9,-4)$",P,W); label("$(b,5)$",Q,W); label("$9$",(P+Q)/2,E,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

(The equation $81+b^2+81=b^2$ in the post comes from expanding $(b+9)^2$ as $b^2+81$, dropping the middle term $18b$.)
