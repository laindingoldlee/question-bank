The right angle is at $C$: $\angle C=90^\circ$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(2.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair C=(0,0),B=(10,0),A=(0,24);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(C+(1,0)--C+(1,1)--C+(0,1));
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,SW);label("$10$",(5,0),S);label("$24$",(0,12),W);label("$26$",(A+B)/2,NE);
\end{asy}

$\tan A=\dfrac{BC}{CA}=\dfrac{10}{24}=\dfrac5{12}$, so $\angle A=\arctan\dfrac5{12}\approx22.62^\circ$, and $\angle B=90^\circ-\angle A\approx67.38^\circ$. So the angles are about $\boxed{22.62^\circ,\ 67.38^\circ,\ 90^\circ}$.
