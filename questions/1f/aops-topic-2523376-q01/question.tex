If Jaiden starts writing his essay at $10{:}30$, he is certain to be admitted to a programme. Let $p_i$ be the probability that he is admitted if he procrastinates for $i$ minutes, so $p_0=1$. Suppose that $0<p_i\le1$ and
\[p_i+\dfrac{3}{p_i}=p_{i+1}+\dfrac{3}{p_{i+1}}-\dfrac1{20}\]
for all $i\ge0$. What is the largest whole number of minutes he can procrastinate and still have at least a $60\%$ chance of being admitted?
