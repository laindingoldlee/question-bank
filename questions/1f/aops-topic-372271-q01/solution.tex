The odd integers from $25$ to $49$ are $25,27,\dots,49$, which are $\dfrac{49-25}{2}+1=13$ numbers with average $\dfrac{25+49}{2}=37$. Their sum is $13\cdot37=\boxed{481}$.

(Alternatively, the sum of the first $m$ odd numbers is $m^2$, so the sum is $1+3+\cdots+49$ minus $1+3+\cdots+23$, that is $25^2-12^2=625-144=481$.)
