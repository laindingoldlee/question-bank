The maximum separation of two points in the box is its space diagonal, $\sqrt{8^2+9^2+12^2}=\boxed{17}$. It is attained by opposite vertices.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair U=(0,0),V=(4,0),T=(1,1.3);draw(U--V--V+T--T--cycle);draw((0,3)--(4,3)--(4,3)+T--(0,3)+T--cycle);for(pair p:new pair[]{U,V,V+T,T})draw(p--p+(0,3));draw(U--V+T+(0,3),blue);label("8",(2,0),S);label("9",V+T/2,E);label("12",(0,1.5),W);
\end{asy}
