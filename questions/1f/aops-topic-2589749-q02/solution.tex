One printer prints $\dfrac{60}{4\cdot12}=\dfrac54$ pages per minute. To print $200$ pages in $10$ minutes we need $20$ pages per minute, i.e. $\dfrac{20}{\dfrac54}=\boxed{16}$ printers.
