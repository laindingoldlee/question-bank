Write $c=gc_1$ and $m=gm_1$, where $\gcd(c_1,m_1)=1$. The original congruence means $gm_1\mid gc_1(a-b)$, equivalently $m_1\mid c_1(a-b)$. Coprimality makes this equivalent to $m_1\mid a-b$. Thus the reduced modulus is $m_1=\dfrac{m}{g}$. Cancellation keeps modulus $m$ when $g=1$. It need not do so otherwise: $2\cdot1\equiv2\cdot3\pmod4$, but $1\not\equiv3\pmod4$.
