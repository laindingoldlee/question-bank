For integers $a,b,c$ and positive integer $m$, prove $ca\equiv cb\pmod m$ if and only if $a\equiv b\pmod{\dfrac{m}{g}}$, where $g=\gcd(c,m)$. Explain when cancellation preserves the original modulus.
