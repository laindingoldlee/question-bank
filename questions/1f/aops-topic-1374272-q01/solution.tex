The sum of the numbers is $2\cdot40=80$. If the smaller is $y$, then $y+(y+42)=80$, so $y=19$. The numbers are $\boxed{61\text{ and }19}$. Check: $61-19=42$ and $\dfrac{61+19}{2}=40$.
