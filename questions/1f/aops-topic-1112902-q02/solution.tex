Put the rectangle as $A(0,0)$, $B(11,0)$, $C(11,8)$, $D(0,8)$ and fold $A$ onto $C$. The crease is the perpendicular bisector of $AC$; it meets $AB$ at $X=(x,0)$ with $XA=XC$:
\[
x^2=(11-x)^2+8^2\iff 22x=185\iff x=\dfrac{185}{22}.
\]
By symmetry about the centre, it meets $CD$ at $Y=\left(11-\dfrac{185}{22},8\right)=\left(\dfrac{57}{22},8\right)$.

The crease cuts the rectangle into two congruent trapezoids. The trapezoid $AXYD$ is reflected across the crease: $A$ goes to $C$, $X$ and $Y$ stay, and $D$ goes to a point $D'$ outside the rectangle. The folded figure is the pentagon $XBCD'Y$ (shaded), and the doubled part is triangle $XCY$ (darker), where the two layers overlap:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real a=185/22,c=57/22;
pair A=(0,0),B=(11,0),C=(11,8),D=(0,8);
pair X=(a,0),Y=(c,8);
pair d=unit(Y-X);
pair refl(pair p){ return X+2*dot(p-X,d)*d-(p-X); }
pair D2=refl(D);
fill(X--B--C--Y--cycle,paleblue);
fill(X--C--D2--Y--cycle,paleblue);
fill(X--C--Y--cycle,lightblue);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,dashed+gray(0.5));
draw(X--B--C--D2--Y--cycle,linewidth(1));
draw(X--Y,red+linewidth(0.9));
draw(X--C,linewidth(0.6));
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$D$",D,NW);
label("$C=A'$",C,E);label("$D'$",D2,N);
label("$X$",X,S);label("$Y$",Y,NW);
label("$11$",(5.5,-0.9));label("$8$",(-0.8,4));
label("crease",(a,0)+0.55*(Y-X),3*W,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The image of $AX$ is $CX$, which runs inside the fixed trapezoid $XBCY$, and the image of $AD$ is $CD'$, which leaves the rectangle; so the reflected trapezoid meets the fixed one exactly in triangle $XCY$. That triangle has base $YC=11-\dfrac{57}{22}=\dfrac{185}{22}$ on the line $y=8$ and height $8$, so its area is
\[
\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{185}{22}\cdot8=\dfrac{370}{11}.
\]
The folded figure is the union of two trapezoids of area $44$ each, overlapping in this triangle, so its area is
\[
88-\dfrac{370}{11}=\dfrac{598}{11}\approx\boxed{54.4}\text{ square inches}.
\]
