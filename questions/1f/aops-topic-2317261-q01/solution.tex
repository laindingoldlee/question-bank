After giving $2$ apples to each person, $18$ apples remain, and a sharing is a solution of
\[x+y+z=18\]
in nonnegative integers (the extra apples of Alice, Becky and Chris).

\textbf{The error.} $\dbinom{18}{3}$ counts the ways to choose $3$ of $18$ distinct objects. Here the apples are identical, and we are not choosing $3$ apples: we are splitting a row of $18$ apples into three (possibly empty) parts. For example, with only $2$ extra apples there are $6$ sharings ($2,0,0$ in $3$ orders and $1,1,0$ in $3$ orders), but $\dbinom23=0$.

\textbf{Stars and bars.} A splitting corresponds to a row of $18$ stars and $2$ bars: the stars before the first bar go to Alice, those between the bars to Becky, and those after the second bar to Chris. Choosing the positions of the $2$ bars among the $20$ symbols gives
\[\dbinom{20}{2}=190\]
ways. The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(C) }190}$.
