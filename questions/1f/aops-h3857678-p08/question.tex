Asli and Zehra play a game by making moves alternately. Asli first colors some unit squares of an \(n\times n\) chessboard red and the rest blue.



After that, the player whose turn it is chooses a unit square and changes its color. The same picture cannot appear twice, and the player who cannot make a move loses.



For \(n=11,22,34,100\), how many of these games can Asli guarantee winning?



\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{a)}\ 0 & \text{b)}\ 1 \\
\text{c)}\ 2 & \text{d)}\ 3 \\
\text{e)}\ 4
\end{array}
\]
