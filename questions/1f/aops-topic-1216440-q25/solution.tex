Write $3^{12}=27^4$ and $2^{10}=4\cdot 4^4$. Sophie Germain's identity $a^4+4b^4=(a^2+2b^2+2ab)(a^2+2b^2-2ab)$ with $a=27$, $b=4$ gives
\[3^{12}+2^{10}=(729+32+216)(729+32-216)=977\cdot545=977\cdot5\cdot109.\]
$977$ is prime (it is not divisible by any prime up to $31$, and $31^2>977$), so the greatest prime factor is $\boxed{977}$.
