\[
4\longmapsto7\longmapsto8\longmapsto15\longmapsto24
\longmapsto60\longmapsto168\longmapsto480.
\]
For example $Q(24)=(1+2+4+8)(1+3)=60$, $Q(60)=(1+2+4)(1+3)(1+5)=168$, and $Q(168)=(1+2+4+8)(1+3)(1+7)=480$. Hence the answer is $\boxed{480}$.
