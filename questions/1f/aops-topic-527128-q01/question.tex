At a school test the following problem was set: ``The hypotenuse of a right triangle is $12$ inches, and the height drawn to the hypotenuse is $7$ inches. Find the area of the triangle.'' Everyone in the class answered $42$ square inches, except the two best students. Explain why the two best students did not give this answer.
