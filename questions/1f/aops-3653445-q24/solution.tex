The literal conditions permit two values. Choose coordinates $P=(0,0)$, $R=(4,0)$, $A=(4,5)$, $D=(0,d)$, $S=(0,s)$. The circle through $D,S$ has equation $x^2+y^2+ux-(d+s)y+ds=0$. Substituting $A$ gives $4u=-41+5(d+s)-ds$. Let $C=(c,0)$, so $c^2+uc+ds=0$. Computing the projection $Q$ of $D$ on $AC$ with these identities shows that $PQ$ has slope $\dfrac{5-s}{4}$. Consequently $X=(4,5-s)$ and $AX=|s|$.
But $AS^2=4^2+(5-s)^2=121$, so $s=5\pm\sqrt{105}$. Thus the possible lengths are $\boxed{\sqrt{105}-5,\ \sqrt{105}+5}$. Both are feasible: for the negative choice take $d=5$; for the positive choice take $d=20$. In each case take $B,C$ as the two horizontal-axis intersections of the displayed circle, in the order making $ABCD$ convex. The resulting intersections are distinct and give nondegenerate convex quadrilaterals. An extra positional condition on $S$ would be needed to select one length.

\end{asy}
