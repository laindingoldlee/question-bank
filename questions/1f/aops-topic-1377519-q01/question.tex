Four birds eat four worms in four minutes. Assuming every bird eats at the same constant rate, how many minutes do $32$ birds take to eat $32$ worms? Justify the answer with rates, and explain why the proportion
\[
\dfrac{4\text{ birds}}{4\text{ worms}}\cdot4\text{ minutes}=\dfrac{32\text{ birds}}{32\text{ worms}}\cdot y\text{ minutes}
\]
gives the right answer.
