The radius $r$ of the sector becomes the slant height of the cone, and the arc $KJ$ becomes the circumference of the base. The arc has length $\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot2\pi r=\dfrac{\pi r}{2}$, so the base radius $a$ satisfies $2\pi a=\dfrac{\pi r}{2}$, i.e.\ $a=\dfrac {r}{4}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real r=2.4;
pair O=(0,0),K=(r,0),J=(0,r);
draw(O--K,linewidth(0.9));draw(O--J,linewidth(0.9));draw(arc(O,r,0,90),linewidth(0.9));
draw((0.3,0)--(0.3,0.3)--(0,0.3));
label("$O$",O,SW);label("$K$",K,SE);label("$J$",J,NW);label("$r$",(r/2,0),S);label("$r$",(0,r/2),W);
draw((r+0.4,r/2)--(r+1.4,r/2),Arrow(TeXHead));
real a=r/4, h=sqrt(r^2-a^2);
pair V=(r+2.4+a,r+0.1), c=(r+2.4+a,r+0.1-h);
draw(shift(c)*xscale(a)*yscale(0.12)*arc((0,0),1,180,360),linewidth(0.9));draw(shift(c)*xscale(a)*yscale(0.12)*arc((0,0),1,0,180),dashed);
draw(V--(c+(a,0)),linewidth(0.9));draw(V--(c-(a,0)),linewidth(0.9));
draw(V--c,dashed);draw(c--c+(a,0),dashed);
label("$O$",V,N);label("$r$",(V+c+(a,0))/2,E);
real xl=c.x-a-0.3; draw((xl,c.y)--(xl,V.y),Arrows(TeXHead));draw(V--(xl-0.08,V.y),dotted);draw(c-(a,0)--(xl-0.08,c.y),dotted);label("$h$",(xl,(c.y+V.y)/2),W);label("$\dfrac{r}{4}$",c+(a/2,-0.12),S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The height is $h=\sqrt{r^2-\dfrac{r^2}{16}}=\dfrac{r\sqrt{15}}{4}$, so the volume is
\[
V=\dfrac{1}{3}\pi a^2h=\dfrac{1}{3}\pi\cdot\dfrac{r^2}{16}\cdot\dfrac{r\sqrt{15}}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt{15}\,\pi r^3}{192}} .
\]
