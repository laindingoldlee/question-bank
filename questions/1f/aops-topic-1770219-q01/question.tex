A sector of a circle with central angle $90^\circ$ and radius $r$ has radii $OK$ and $OJ$, where $K$ and $J$ are points on the circle. The sector is cut out and curved so that $OK$ and $OJ$ coincide, forming the lateral surface of a cone. Find the volume of the cone in terms of $r$.
