The two $5$s must be adjacent at the peak. For each of $1,2,3,4$, independently choose whether $0,1,$ or $2$ copies lie to the left of that peak. The remaining copies go to its right, and their orders on each side are forced. These choices are distinct and describe every mountainous row, giving $\boxed{3^4=81}$.
