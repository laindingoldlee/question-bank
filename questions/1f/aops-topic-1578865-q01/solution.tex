\textbf{No.} The next case already fails: for $x=5$,
\[
2^5\cdot10+3=323=17\cdot19 .
\]
So $\boxed{323=17\cdot19}$ is a counterexample.

In fact infinitely many values are composite: since $2^8\equiv1\pmod{17}$ and $2^5\cdot10+3\equiv0\pmod{17}$, every $x=5+8j$ gives a multiple of $17$ (larger than $17$).
