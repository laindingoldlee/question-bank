From the area formula $10=\dfrac{1}{2} AB\cdot AC\sin A$ and $\sin A\le1$, we get $AB\cdot AC\ge20$. Equality requires $A=90^\circ$ and is attainable: put $B=(0,0),C=(10,0),A=(5-\sqrt{21},2)$. Its altitude is $2$, giving area $10$, and $(B-A)\cdot(C-A)=0$ since $(5-\sqrt{21})(5+\sqrt{21})=4$. Thus the minimum is $\boxed{20}$.
