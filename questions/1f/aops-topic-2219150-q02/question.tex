A ruler of length $12$ units has $11$ marks, at $1,2,\dots,11$ units from one end, so every whole length from $1$ to $12$ units can be measured as the distance between two of its marks or ends. What is the least number of marks (not counting the two ends) that a ruler of length $12$ needs so that every whole length from $1$ to $12$ can still be measured directly as the distance between two marks or ends? Give a ruler with that many marks.
