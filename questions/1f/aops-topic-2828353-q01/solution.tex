With $c$ and $d$ the weights, $5c+27d=1020$ and $12c+22d=2020$. Multiplying the first by $12$ and the second by $5$ and subtracting: $214d=2140$, so $d=10$ and $c=150$. Then $12c+27d=1800+270=\boxed{2070}$ pounds.
