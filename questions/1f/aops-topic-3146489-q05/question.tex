Anna and Bruno start biking together at speed $v$ miles per hour toward a mall $6$ miles away. After $1$ mile Anna turns back to school for a calculator, then goes to the mall, cycling at $v+4$ miles per hour throughout this detour. Bruno continues at speed $v$. They arrive together. Assuming no stopping time, find $v$.
