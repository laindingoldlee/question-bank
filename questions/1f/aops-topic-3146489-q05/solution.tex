After Anna turns, Bruno has $5$ miles remaining and Anna has $1+6=7$. Equality of their remaining times gives $\dfrac{7}{v+4}=\dfrac{5}{v}$. Therefore $7v=5v+20$, so $\boxed{v=10\text{ mph}}$.
