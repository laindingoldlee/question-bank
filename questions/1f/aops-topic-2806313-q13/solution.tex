Write the numbers $0$ to $2019$ as $4$-digit strings $0000$ to $2019$, count even digits, then remove the padding zeros and the string $0000$.

Strings $0000$ to $1999$: in each of the last three places every digit appears equally often, so even digits occur $3\cdot1000=3000$ times; the first digit is $0$ in $1000$ strings. Strings $2000$ to $2019$: first digit $2$ ($20$), second digit $0$ ($20$), third digit $0$ in $10$ strings, last digit even in $10$: $60$. Total $4060$.

Remove $0000$ ($4$ even digits) and the padding zeros of $1$ to $999$: $999$ in the first place, $99$ in the second (for $1$ to $99$) and $9$ in the third (for $1$ to $9$), $1107$ in all. The answer is $4060-4-1107=\boxed{2949}$.
