Before the first correction the labels are $6+5k$, so the first power of two reached is $16$: two forward jumps then a backward jump to $14$. From $14$, the first larger power of two congruent to $14$ modulo $5$ is $64$: ten forward jumps then a backward jump to $62$. From $62$, the next such power is $512$: ninety forward jumps then a backward jump to $510$. Powers of two have residues $2,4,3,1$ modulo $5$, never $0$, so there are no more corrections. After $f$ forward jumps and three backward jumps the unwrapped position is $6+5f-6=5f$. Thus the first passage beyond $2017$ occurs at $f=404$. Including the three backward jumps gives $\boxed{407}$.
