The vertices of a regular $2017$-gon are numbered consecutively. A flea starts at vertex $6$. Each forward jump advances five vertices clockwise. Whenever it lands on a power-of-two label, it immediately makes a separate backward jump of two vertices. Count all forward and backward jumps up to and including the first forward jump that passes vertex $1$.
