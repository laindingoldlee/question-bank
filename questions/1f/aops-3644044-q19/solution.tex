Triangle $BCD$ has area $20\cdot\dfrac{99}{2}=990$. Since $A,X,C$ are collinear and $AX=2CX$, their perpendicular distances to line $BD$ are in ratio two. Hence $[ABD]=2[CBD]$. Adding the two triangles gives quadrilateral area $3\cdot990=\boxed{2970}$.

import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
pair B=(0,0),C=(4,0),D=(4,6),X=(2,3),A=X+2*(X-C);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C^^B--D,dashed);label("$A$",A,(-1,1));label("$B$",B,(-1,-1));label("$C$",C,(1,-1));label("$D$",D,(1,1));label("$X$",X,(1,0));
\end{asy}
