Triangle $MEW$ is right at $E$, with $EW=9,MW=15$. Thus $ME=\sqrt{225-81}=12$, giving area 54. The rectangle area is 135, so the ratio is $\boxed{\dfrac{2}{5}}$.

usepackage("amsmath");

pair A=(0,0),X=(9,0),Ee=(9,15),Ww=(0,15),M=(9,3);draw(A--X--Ee--Ww--cycle);draw(M--Ww);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$X$",X,SE);label("$E$",Ee,NE);label("$W$",Ww,NW);label("$M$",M,E);
\end{asy}
