Let $X$ and $Y$ be positive numbers, and suppose that the infinite continued fraction
\[N=X+\dfrac{Y}{X+\dfrac{Y}{X+\dfrac{Y}{X+\cdots}}}\]
converges to a positive number $N$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that $N^2-XN-Y=0$, and deduce that $N=\dfrac{X+\sqrt{X^2+4Y}}{2}$.
\item[(b)] Evaluate $N$ for $X=Y=1$ and for $X=2$, $Y=3$.
\end{enumerate}
