The equation $0=9x^8-28x^6+\cdots$ has degree $8$ and is written with decreasing exponents, so it has no $x^7$ term; the next coefficient after $-28x^6$ (that of $x^5$) is negative. The equation has $7$ equal roots, and its eighth root is different from them. Find the eighth root.
