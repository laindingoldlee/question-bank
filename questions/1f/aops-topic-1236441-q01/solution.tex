John runs $h-d$ metres while Don runs $h$, so John's speed is $\dfrac{h-d}{h}$ times Don's. In the second race Don runs $h+d$ metres. In that time John runs $(h+d)\cdot\dfrac{h-d}{h}=h-\dfrac{d^2}{h}<h$ metres, so Don wins, and when Don finishes John is $\boxed{\dfrac{d^2}{h}}$ metres from the finish.
