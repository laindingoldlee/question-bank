$2013$ guests are at a ball; some pairs are friends. In minute $1$, everyone with no friends among the remaining humans turns into a mouse; in minute $2$, everyone with exactly one human friend; in minute $k$, everyone with exactly $k-1$ human friends. What is the largest possible number of humans left after $2013$ minutes?
