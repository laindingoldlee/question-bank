The roots of $x^2-18x-319$ are $9\pm\sqrt{81+319}=9\pm20$, i.e. $-11$ and $29$. So the inequality holds for $-11<x<29$, i.e. for the integers $-10,-9,\dots,28$. Their sum is $11+12+\cdots+28=\boxed{351}$ (the terms from $-10$ to $10$ cancel).
