Take $2x$ adults and $3x$ children. The literate adults are $60\%$ of $2x$, i.e.\ $1.2x$, and the literate children are $85\%$ of $3x$, i.e.\ $2.55x$. So the literate fraction of the population is
\[
\dfrac{1.2x+2.55x}{5x}=\dfrac{3.75}{5}=0.75=\boxed{75\%}.
\]
