The intersection $A\cap B$ is a $3$-element subset of $\{2,3,4,5,6\}$, the elements both sets may contain: $\dbinom{5}{3}=10$ choices. Then decide the remaining elements independently:
\begin{itemize}
\item each of the other two elements of $\{2,\dots,6\}$ is in $A$ only, in $B$ only, or in neither (not in both): $3^2=9$ ways;
\item the element $1$ is in $A$ or not: $2$ ways;
\item each of $7$ and $8$ is in $B$ or not: $2^2=4$ ways.
\end{itemize}
The total is $10\cdot9\cdot2\cdot4=\boxed{720}$.
