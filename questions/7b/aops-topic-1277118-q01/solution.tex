A girl must have at least two sisters, so there are at least $3$ girls. A boy must have at least one brother, so there are at least $2$ boys. With $3$ girls and $2$ boys every girl has $2$ sisters and $2$ brothers, and every boy has $1$ brother and $3$ sisters. The minimum is $\boxed{5}$.
