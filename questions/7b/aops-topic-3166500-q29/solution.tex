Divisor pairing gives $\sum_{d\mid n}\dfrac{1}{d}=\dfrac{\sigma(n)}{n}$, so the product is $\dfrac{\sigma(n)^2}{n}$. Since $\sigma(n)$ is a positive integer, this is a rational square exactly when $n$ is a rational square; an integer with rational square root is an integer square by prime factorization. Thus the qualifying values are $1^2,2^2,\ldots,44^2$. Their sum is $\dfrac{44\cdot45\cdot89}{6}=\boxed{29370}$.
