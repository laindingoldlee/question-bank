We need $32\le n\le99$. The last digit of $n^2$ is even, so $n$ is even. The first digit of $n^2$ is $2$, $4$, $6$ or $8$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $2000\le n^2<3000$: $45\le n\le54$; even $n$: $46,48,50,52,54$ with squares $2116,2304,2500,2704,2916$; each has an odd digit.
\item $4000\le n^2<5000$: $64\le n\le70$; even $n$: $64,66,68,70$ with squares $4096,4356,4624,4900$; only $4624$ works.
\item $6000\le n^2<7000$: $78\le n\le83$; even $n$: $78,80,82$ with squares $6084,6400,6724$; $6084$ and $6400$ work.
\item $8000\le n^2<9000$: $90\le n\le94$; even $n$: $90,92,94$ with squares $8100,8464,8836$; only $8464$ works.
\end{itemize}
So $n\in\boxed{\{68,\ 78,\ 80,\ 92\}}$, with squares $4624$, $6084$, $6400$, $8464$.
