Larry writes a list of numbers that has average $14$, median $21$ and mode $11$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Moe creates a list by adding $12$ to each number in Larry's list. What are the average, median and mode of Moe's list?
\item[(b)] Curly creates a list by multiplying each number in Larry's list by $2$. What are the average, median and mode of Curly's list?
\end{enumerate}
