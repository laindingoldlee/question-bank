Use $A=(0,0),B=(1,0),C=(\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2})$, and set $F=(f,0)$. Since $EF$ is vertical, $E=(f,\sqrt3f)$. Writing $D=(1-\dfrac{t}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt3t}{2})$, the condition $FD\perp BC$ gives $t=\dfrac{1-f}{2}$. The condition $DE\perp CA$ then gives $5f-1=1-f$, hence $f=\dfrac{1}{3}$. Consequently $E=(\dfrac{1}{3},\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3})$ and $D=(\dfrac{5}{6},\dfrac{\sqrt3}{6})$. The inner triangle is equilateral with side $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3}$, so its area is $\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt3}{12}}$.
