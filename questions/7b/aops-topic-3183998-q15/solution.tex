Let the cylinder radius be $r$, hence its height is $2r$. Similarity in an axial cross-section gives $\dfrac{r}{5}=\dfrac{12-2r}{12}$. Therefore $12r=60-10r$ and $\boxed{r=\dfrac{30}{11}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
real r=30/11;draw((-5,0)--(0,12)--(5,0)--cycle);draw((-r,0)--(r,0)--(r,2*r)--(-r,2*r)--cycle);draw((0,0)--(0,12),dashed);label("$12$",(0,9),E);label("$r$",(r/2,2*r),N);label("$2r$",(r,r),E);label("Axial section",(0,-1));
\end{asy}
