Let $N=6n$ with $n\ge2$. Then $N$, $3n$, $2n$, $n$ and $1$ are divisors of $N$, and they are all different: $6n>3n>2n>n\ge2>1$. Hence the sum of the divisors of $N$ is at least
\[
6n+3n+2n+n+1=12n+1>12n=2N .
\]
So every multiple of $6$ greater than $6$ is $\boxed{\text{abundant}}$. (For $N=6$ the sum is exactly $1+2+3+6=12=2N$: $6$ is perfect.) $\blacksquare$
