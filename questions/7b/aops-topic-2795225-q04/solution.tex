The number 550 has only one other valid three-digit permutation,505, so one relative is attainable. Zero relatives would require all three digits equal, or just one nonzero digit and two zeros. Three equal digits cannot sum to 10, and a single nonzero digit cannot be 10. In every other case at least two distinct permutations have nonzero first digits. Thus the minimum is $\boxed1$.
