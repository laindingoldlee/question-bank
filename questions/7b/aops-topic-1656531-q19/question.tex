Real numbers $a$ and $b$ are chosen from the interval $[-1,1]$. Which of the following could \emph{not} be a real root of the polynomial $\left(a^2+ab-b^2\right)x^2+3x-1$? (More than one choice may be correct.)

(a) $\dfrac{1}{2}$\quad (b) $\dfrac{5}{3}$\quad (c) $\dfrac{8}{3}$\quad (d) $3$\quad (e) $-18$
