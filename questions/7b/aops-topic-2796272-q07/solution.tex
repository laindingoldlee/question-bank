Erika's lap takes $100$ s. If Frank walked the opposite way they would meet more often than once per $100$ s, so he walks the same way, and between consecutive passings Erika gains exactly one lap on him. The passings are $160$ s apart, so with lap length $L$:
\[(v_E-v_F)\cdot160=L,\qquad v_E=\dfrac L{100}\ \Rightarrow\ v_F=\dfrac L{100}-\dfrac L{160}=\dfrac{3L}{800}.\]
The ratio is $\dfrac{v_F}{v_E}=\boxed{\dfrac38}$.
