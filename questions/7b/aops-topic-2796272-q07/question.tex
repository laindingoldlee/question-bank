Erika runs laps of a circular track at constant speed. She passes a trash can at $6{:}28{:}50$ and again on her next lap at $6{:}30{:}30$. At that moment Frank starts walking around the track at constant speed. Erika passes Frank for the first time at $6{:}31{:}10$ and for the second time at $6{:}33{:}50$. Find the ratio of Frank's speed to Erika's.
