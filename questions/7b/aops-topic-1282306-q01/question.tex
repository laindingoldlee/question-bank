For a real number $x$ and a positive integer $y$, let
\[
F(x,y)=x+\dfrac{y}{10^{\lfloor\log_{10}y\rfloor+1}} .
\]
Find $F(1,4)$, $F(19,34)$ and $F(177777,34343434343)$, find $F(a,7)$ for every integer $a$, and show that $F(x,10^ny)=F(x,y)$ for every nonnegative integer $n$.
