Let $x$ dollars go into bonds and $10000-x$ into stocks. The income condition is
\[
0.045x+0.025(10000-x)\ge300\iff 250+0.02x\ge300\iff x\ge2500 .
\]
So she must invest at least $\boxed{\$2500}$ in bonds, and so at most $\$7500$ in stocks. (Any split with between $\$2500$ and $\$10000$ in bonds works.)
