Mrs.\ Chavis will invest $\$10000$ in stocks and bonds. The bonds pay $4.5\%$ annual interest and the stocks pay $2.5\%$. She wants her total annual income to be at least $\$300$. What is the least amount she can invest in bonds? (Equivalently, what is the most she can invest in stocks?)
