If $p\ne3$, its residue modulo 3 is 1 or 2. In the first case $p+14$ is a multiple of 3 greater than 3; in the second $p+10$ is. Thus $p=3$ is necessary, and $3,13,17$ are indeed prime. Hence $\boxed{p=3}$.
