Factor out the smaller power:
\[
5^{31}-5^{29}=5^{29}\left(5^2-1\right)=24\cdot5^{29}=24\cdot25\cdot5^{27}=600\cdot5^{27}=100\cdot\left(6\cdot5^{27}\right).
\]
Since $6\cdot5^{27}$ is an integer, $100$ divides $5^{31}-5^{29}$. $\blacksquare$
