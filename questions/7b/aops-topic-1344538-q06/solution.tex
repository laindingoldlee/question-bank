Put $C=(0,0)$, $B=(24,0)$, $A=(24,24)$, $D=(0,24)$. The arc $BD$ has centre $C$ and radius $24$. Let the semicircle have radius $r$, so its centre is $O=(24-r,24)$ on side $AD$ and $AE=2r$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair C=(0,0), B=(24,0), A=(24,24), D=(0,24);
real r=6; pair O=(24-r,24), Ep=(24-2r,24);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(arc(C,24,0,90),blue+linewidth(0.9));
draw(arc(O,r,180,360),red+linewidth(0.9));
pair T=C+24*unit(O-C);
draw(C--O,gray+dashed);
dot(O);dot(T);dot(Ep);
label("$A$",A,NE); label("$B$",B,SE); label("$C$",C,SW); label("$D$",D,NW); label("$E$",Ep,N); label("$O$",O,N); label("$T$",T,dir(225));
label("$24$",(12,0),S); label("$24$",(C+T)/2+1.6*dir(135),gray); label("$r$",(O+T)/2+1.0*dir(-45),gray);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The semicircle lies outside the quarter circle and touches it, so the distance between the centres is the sum of the radii, $CO=24+r$ (the point of tangency $T$ lies on segment $CO$):
\[
(24-r)^2+24^2=(24+r)^2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 576=96r\quad\Longrightarrow\quad r=6 .
\]
So $AE=2r=\boxed{12\text{ cm}}$.
