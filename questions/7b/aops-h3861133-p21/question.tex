Find the number of ordered quadruples of nonnegative integers $w,x,y,z$ such that

$$\left\lfloor\dfrac{2025}{10^k}\right\rfloor=\left\lfloor\dfrac{w}{10^k}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{x}{10^k}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{y}{10^k}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{z}{10^k}\right\rfloor$$holds for all nonnegative integers $k$.
