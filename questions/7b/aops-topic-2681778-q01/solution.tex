After $n$ days Sarah has $30n$ cents and Josh has $1+2+\cdots+n=\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$ cents. Setting them equal with $n>0$: $\dfrac{n+1}{2}=30$, so $n=\boxed{59}$ days (each has saved $1770$ cents).
