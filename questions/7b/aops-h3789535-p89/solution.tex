Nonreal roots of a real polynomial occur with their conjugates, so $6-7i$ is also a root. These five numbers are distinct. No conjugate of $6+\sqrt7$ is forced when coefficients are merely real. The polynomial $(x-67)(x-\dfrac{6}{7})(x-6-\sqrt7)((x-6)^2+49)$ has real coefficients and exactly the required degree. Hence $\boxed5$.
