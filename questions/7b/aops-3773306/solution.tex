Digit sums are subadditive: $S(u+v)\le S(u)+S(v)$, since every carry reduces the sum by 9. Multiplication by powers of 10 does not affect digit sum, so $S(f)\le S(400)+S(625)+S(784)=4+13+19=36$. Also $f\equiv400+625+784=1809\equiv0\pmod9$, so its positive digit sum must be one of $9,18,27,36$. All four occur. Removing an irrelevant common factor of 100, exponent triples $(0,1,1),(0,0,0),(0,0,1),(0,1,3)$ give respectively $141300,1809,79425,784062900$, whose digit sums are $9,18,27,36$. Adding 1 to every exponent makes the original exponents positive without changing the sums. Thus their sum is $\boxed{90}$.
