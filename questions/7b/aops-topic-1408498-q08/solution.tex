There are $10^3=1000$ codes in all. The forbidden codes are:
\begin{itemize}
\item agreeing with $023$ in all three positions: $1$ code;
\item agreeing in exactly two positions: choose the position that differs ($3$ ways) and its new digit ($9$ ways): $27$ codes;
\item obtained by swapping two digits: $203$, $320$, $032$. These agree with $023$ in exactly one position, so they are not already counted.
\end{itemize}
So Reckha has $1000-1-27-3=\boxed{969}$ codes.
