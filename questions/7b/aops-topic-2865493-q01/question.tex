Point $P$ is inside triangle $ABC$, and its projections onto $BC$, $CA$, $AB$ are $D$, $E$, $F$ respectively. Prove that if
\[AF\cdot BD\cdot CE=BF\cdot AE\cdot CD\quad\text{and}\quad\dfrac1{AF}+\dfrac1{BD}+\dfrac1{CE}=\dfrac1{BF}+\dfrac1{AE}+\dfrac1{CD},\]
then the sets of lengths $\{AF,BD,CE\}$ and $\{BF,AE,CD\}$ are equal (as multisets).
