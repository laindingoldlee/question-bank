Since $\overline{abc}$ is divisible by five, $c=0$ or $5$. A four-digit arithmetic-progression set must be $\{s,s+t,s+2t,s+3t\}$ with $t=1,2,3$ and $0\le s\le9-3t$. For each such set, place $0$ or $5$ in the third position and test the remaining arrangements for $17\mid\overline{bcd}$, requiring $a,b\ne0$.
