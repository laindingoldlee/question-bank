Four friends play a game in which heads count $1$ and tails count $0$, and dice show their usual values.
\begin{itemize}
\item Abe flips a fair coin $3$ times; the sum of his flips is $a$.
\item Brenda rolls a standard six-sided die once; the result is $b$.
\item Carl rolls once a special weighted six-sided die with faces $1,2,\ldots,6$, where the probability of each face is proportional to its value; the result is $c$.
\item Dane flips $3$ times a weighted coin that lands heads with probability $\dfrac{1}{3}$; the sum of his flips is $d$.
\end{itemize}
What is the probability that $a+b+c+d$ is less than $17$? Express your answer as a common fraction.
