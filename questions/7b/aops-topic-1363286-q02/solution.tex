The face $k$ of Carl's die has probability $\dfrac{k}{1+2+\cdots+6}=\dfrac{k}{21}$. The largest possible values are $a=3$, $b=6$, $c=6$, $d=3$, with total $18$, so it is easiest to find the probability of the complement, $a+b+c+d\ge17$.

The single-value probabilities we need are
\[
P(a=3)=\dfrac{1}{8},\ P(a=2)=\dfrac{3}{8},\quad P(b=6)=P(b=5)=\dfrac{1}{6},\quad P(c=6)=\dfrac{6}{21},\ P(c=5)=\dfrac{5}{21},\quad P(d=3)=\dfrac{1}{27},\ P(d=2)=\dfrac{6}{27}.
\]
Write every probability over the common denominator $8\cdot6\cdot21\cdot27=27216$.

\textbf{Total $18$.} All four take their maximum: $1\cdot1\cdot6\cdot1=6$ parts out of $27216$.

\textbf{Total $17$.} Exactly one of the four is one below its maximum and the other three are at their maximum:
\begin{itemize}
\item $a=2$: $3\cdot1\cdot6\cdot1=18$;
\item $b=5$: $1\cdot1\cdot6\cdot1=6$;
\item $c=5$: $1\cdot1\cdot5\cdot1=5$;
\item $d=2$: $1\cdot1\cdot6\cdot6=36$.
\end{itemize}
That is $65$ parts. So $P(a+b+c+d\ge17)=\dfrac{6+65}{27216}=\dfrac{71}{27216}$, and
\[
P(a+b+c+d<17)=1-\dfrac{71}{27216}=\boxed{\dfrac{27145}{27216}}.
\]
