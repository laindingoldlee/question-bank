Scale so that $AB=CD=5$; then $DN=1$ and $NC=4$. Extend $AM$ to meet line $DC$ at $T$.

\emph{Point $S$.} Since $NC\parallel AB$, triangles $SNC$ and $SBA$ are similar, so
\[\dfrac{NS}{SB}=\dfrac{NC}{AB}=\dfrac{4}{5},\qquad\text{so } NS=\dfrac{4}{9}\,NB .\]

\emph{Point $R$.} Triangles $MAB$ and $MTC$ are congruent ($MB=MC$, vertical angles at $M$, and $\angle ABM=\angle TCM=90^\circ$), so $CT=AB=5$ and $NT=NC+CT=9$. Since $NT\parallel AB$, triangles $RBA$ and $RNT$ are similar, so
\[\dfrac{BR}{RN}=\dfrac{AB}{NT}=\dfrac{5}{9},\qquad\text{so } RB=\dfrac{5}{14}\,NB .\]

Hence $SR=NB\left(1-\dfrac{4}{9}-\dfrac{5}{14}\right)=\dfrac{126-56-45}{126}\,NB=\dfrac{25}{126}\,NB$, and
\[NS:SR:RB=\dfrac{56}{126}:\dfrac{25}{126}:\dfrac{45}{126}=56:25:45 .\]
Since $\gcd(56,25,45)=1$, the least possible value of $x+y+z$ is $56+25+45=\boxed{126}$.
