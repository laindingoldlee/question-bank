In rectangle $ABCD$, point $M$ is the midpoint of side $BC$, and point $N$ lies on side $CD$ with $DN:NC=1:4$. Segment $BN$ meets $AM$ at $R$ and $AC$ at $S$. If $NS:SR:RB=x:y:z$, where $x$, $y$ and $z$ are positive integers, what is the least possible value of $x+y+z$?
