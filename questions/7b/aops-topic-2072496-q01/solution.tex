Let the numbers be \(u\ge v\). Then
\[
(u-v)^2=(u+v)^2-4uv=8-4=4,
\]
so \(u-v=2\). With \(u+v=2\sqrt2\) this gives \(u=\sqrt2+1\) and \(v=\sqrt2-1\). Check: \((\sqrt2+1)(\sqrt2-1)=2-1=1\). The numbers are
\[
\boxed{\sqrt2+1\ \text{and}\ \sqrt2-1}.
\]
