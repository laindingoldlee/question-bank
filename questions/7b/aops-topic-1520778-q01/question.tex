Three children go on an $84$ km trip. Each can walk at $5$ km/h or ride a bicycle at $20$ km/h, but they have only two bicycles among them. A bicycle can carry only one rider at a time, and bicycles may be left at the roadside for someone else to pick up. What is the least time, in hours, needed for all three children to finish the trip?
