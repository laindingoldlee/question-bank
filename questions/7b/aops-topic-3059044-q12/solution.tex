The outputs are $2^k\bmod1000$ for $k\ge1$. The first two,2 and 4, are not divisible by 8, whereas all later ones are. For $k\ge3$, equality of two residues is equivalent to equality modulo 125, since both are 0 modulo 8. The order of 2 modulo 125 is 100: it divides $\phi(125)=100$, is divisible by the order 4 modulo 5, and $2^{20}\equiv76\ne1\pmod{125}$, while $2^{100}\equiv1$. The divisors of 100 divisible by 4 are 4,20,100, and the 20 test excludes the first two. Thus there are 100 distinct periodic outputs after the first two, giving $\boxed{102}$.
