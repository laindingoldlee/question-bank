Adding $ 12+9 $ counts the five overlap members twice, so the union has $ 12+9-5=\boxed{16}$ students. The remaining $ 20-16=\boxed4 $ are in neither. The three disjoint club regions contain $ 7,5,4 $ students, respectively.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw((-3,-2)--(3,-2)--(3,2)--(-3,2)--cycle);draw(circle((-0.8,0),1.3)^^circle((0.8,0),1.3));label("$7$",(-1.2,0));label("$5$",(0,0));label("$4$",(1.2,0));label("$A$",(-1,1.5));label("$B$",(1,1.5));label("neither: $4$",(0,-1.7));
\end{asy}
