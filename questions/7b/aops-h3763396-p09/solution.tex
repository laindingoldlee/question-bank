A square ending in $0$ must end in $00$, impossible when each digit is used exactly once. A square cannot end in $2$ or $3$, so it ends in $5$, and hence its last two digits are $25$. The remaining leading digits are $3,0$, forcing the four-digit square to be $3025$. Since $3025=55^2$, the required digit sum is $5+5=\boxed{10}$.
