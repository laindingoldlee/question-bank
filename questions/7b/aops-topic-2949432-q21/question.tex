For integers $x,y$, define $a_0=a_1=1$ and let $a_{n+2}$ be the remainder of $xa_{n+1}+ya_n$ modulo $15$. Let $t$ be the least positive integer with $a_t=a_{t+1}=1$ (or $t=0$ if none exists). Find the maximum of $t$ over all pairs $(x,y)$.
