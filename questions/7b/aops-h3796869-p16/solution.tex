Let $AD=a$, $BC=b$, and $AB=h=a+b$. Choose $A=(0,0),B=(0,h),D=(a,0),C=(b,h)$. The midpoint is $E=(\dfrac{h}{2},\dfrac{h}{2})$, so $BE^2=\dfrac{h^2}{2}=400$. The trapezoid area is $\dfrac{(a+b)h}{2}=\dfrac{h^2}{2}=\boxed{400}$.
