A numeral beginning with $X$ is between $10$ and $39$, or between $40$ and $49$ (prefix $XL$), or between $90$ and $99$ (prefix $XC$). The units suffixes of lengths $0,1,2,3,4$ occur $1,2,4,2,1$ times, respectively: the length-three suffixes are $III,VII$, the length-two suffixes are $II,IV,VI,IX$, and the length-four suffix is $VIII$. Prefix $X$ contributes one numeral; $XX$ contributes two; $XXX$ contributes four; and $XL,XC$ contribute two each. Thus the count is $1+2+4+2+2=\boxed{11}$. The values are $18,23,27,32,34,36,39,43,47,93,97$.
