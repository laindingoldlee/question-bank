There are $160-54-37-45=24$ purple marbles. The draws are independent, so the probability is
\[\dfrac{45}{160}\cdot\dfrac{37}{160}\cdot\dfrac{24}{160}=\dfrac{39960}{4096000}=\boxed{\dfrac{999}{102400}}.\]
(Here $39960=2^3\cdot3^3\cdot5\cdot37$ and $4096000=2^{15}\cdot5^3$ have greatest common divisor $40$.)
