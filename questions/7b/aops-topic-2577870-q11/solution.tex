The characteristic equation is $7r^2+r-8=(r-1)(7r+8)=0$, giving $a_n=A+B(-\dfrac{8}{7})^n$. The initial values imply $A+B=4$ and $A+(\dfrac{64}{49})B=1$, hence $A=\dfrac{69}{5},B=-\dfrac{49}{5}$. Consequently $a_7=\dfrac{69}{5}+(\dfrac{49}{5})(\dfrac{8}{7})^7=\boxed{\dfrac{651367}{16807}}$.
