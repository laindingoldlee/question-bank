A delivery drone starts at the origin $S(0,0,0)$ and wants to reach its destination $T(3,3,1)$ by moving only $1$ unit at a time in the positive $x$, $y$, or $z$ directions along the 3D grid lines. The drone must pass through exactly one of the two charging stations located at $P(1,1,0)$ and $Q(2,2,1)$. How many different routes can the drone take?
