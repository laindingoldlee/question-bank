The units digit in base $6$ is the remainder modulo $6$. Since $235=6\cdot39+1\equiv1$ and $57=6\cdot9+3\equiv3\pmod6$, the product is $\equiv1\cdot3=3\pmod6$. The units digit is $\boxed{3}$. (Indeed $235\cdot57=13395=6\cdot2232+3$.)
