Bradley is driving at a constant speed. When he passes his school, he notices that in $20$ minutes he will be exactly $\dfrac14$ of the way to his destination, and in $45$ minutes he will be exactly $\dfrac13$ of the way to his destination. Find the number of minutes it takes Bradley to reach his destination from the point where he passes his school.
