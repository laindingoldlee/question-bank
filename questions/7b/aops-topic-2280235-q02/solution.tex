Split the middle terms:
\[x^4+2021x^2+2020x+2021=(x^4+x^3+x^2)-(x^3+x^2+x)+2021(x^2+x+1).\]
(Expanding the right side gives $x^4+(1-1+2021)x^2+(-1+2021)x+2021$.) Each group contains $x^2+x+1$, so
\[x^4+2021x^2+2020x+2021=\boxed{(x^2+x+1)(x^2-x+2021)}.\]
Neither quadratic factors further over the reals (their discriminants $-3$ and $1-4\cdot2021$ are negative).
