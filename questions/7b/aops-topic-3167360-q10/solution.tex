Choose the block indices $i,j,k$ of the selected L,M,T. Their order in the string is correct precisely when $1\le i\le j\le k\le6$. These weakly increasing triples are multisets of size 3 from 6 values, counted by $\binom{6+3-1}3=\boxed{56}$.
