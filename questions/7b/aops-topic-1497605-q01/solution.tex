\textbf{(a)} We have $118-25=93=7\cdot13+2$, so $13$ does not divide $93$. In terms of remainders, $118=9\cdot13+1$ and $25=1\cdot13+12$, which leave remainders $1$ and $12$. So the statement is $\boxed{\text{false}}$. True statements are, for example, $118\equiv1\pmod{13}$ and $118\equiv27\pmod{13}$.

\textbf{(b)} We need $m\mid118-25=93=3\cdot31$. The divisors of $93$ greater than $1$ are
\[
\boxed{m=3,\ 31,\ 93}.
\]
