The statement $a\equiv b\pmod m$ means that $m$ divides $a-b$, i.e.\ $a$ and $b$ leave the same remainder on division by $m$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Is $118\equiv25\pmod{13}$ true?
\item[(b)] Find all integers $m>1$ for which $118\equiv25\pmod m$.
\end{enumerate}
