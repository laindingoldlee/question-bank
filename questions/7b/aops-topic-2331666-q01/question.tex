Ten players, including you, start a game in which $2$ of the $10$ players become Impostors. They are chosen as follows: the game picks one of the $10$ players uniformly at random, and the picked player becomes an Impostor with probability $10\%$ (if the picked player is already an Impostor, nothing happens). This is repeated until there are $2$ Impostors. What is the probability that you are an Impostor?
