Before $10{:}00$, the hour is $04$ through $09$. No triple starting in the first position works, and a triple starting in the second position would require minutes $56,67,78,89,$ or larger; the only valid such option is the excluded starting time $04{:}56$. In hour $10$, the first match is the final triple $012$ in $10{:}12$. Thus $\boxed{10{:}12}$.
