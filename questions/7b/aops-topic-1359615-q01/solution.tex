Label the hexagon $P_0P_1P_2P_3P_4P_5$ with side $1$ and centre $O$, with $P_1P_2$ and $P_4P_5$ the two parallel sides containing sides of the rectangle. Since the leftover pieces are triangles, the rectangle is exactly $P_1P_2P_4P_5$ (a narrower rectangle would leave pentagons), and the triangles are $P_2P_3P_4$ and $P_5P_0P_1$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair[] H; for(int i=0;i<6;++i) H.push(dir(60*i));
fill(H[1]--H[2]--H[4]--H[5]--cycle,palegreen);
fill(H[2]--H[3]--H[4]--cycle,paleblue); fill(H[5]--H[0]--H[1]--cycle,paleblue);
draw(H[0]--H[1]--H[2]--H[3]--H[4]--H[5]--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(H[1]--H[5],linewidth(0.8)); draw(H[2]--H[4],linewidth(0.8));
draw((0,0)--H[5],red+dashed); draw((0,0)--H[1],red+dashed); dot((0,0));
label("$O$",(0,0),W);
label("$P_0$",H[0],E); label("$P_1$",H[1],NE); label("$P_2$",H[2],NW); label("$P_3$",H[3],W); label("$P_4$",H[4],SW); label("$P_5$",H[5],SE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Triangle $P_5P_0P_1$ has base $P_1P_5=\sqrt3$ and height $1-\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{1}{2}$, so area $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}$. Equivalently, it is half of the rhombus $OP_5P_0P_1$, which consists of two of the six equilateral triangles of the hexagon (dashed: $OP_1$ and $OP_5$); so each triangle is $\dfrac{1}{6}$ of the hexagon, and the two together are $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of it. The rectangle is the remaining $\dfrac{2}{3}$. The ratio is
\[
\dfrac{\dfrac{2}{3}}{\dfrac{1}{3}}=\boxed{2}.
\]
