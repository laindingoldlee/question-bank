A region has the shape of a regular hexagon. One faction controls a rectangle inside it that passes through the centre: two opposite sides of the rectangle lie along two opposite (parallel) sides of the hexagon, and its other two sides run across the hexagon, perpendicular to those sides. The other faction controls the rest of the hexagon, which consists of exactly two triangles. What is the ratio of the area of the rectangle to the total area of the two triangles?
