Emily's Sporting Goods sold $661$ frisbees, some at $10$ dollars and the rest at $12$ dollars. When they recorded how many frisbees were sold at each price, they accidentally swapped the two numbers, and so they thought they had made $378$ dollars more than they actually did. How many frisbees were actually sold at $10$ dollars?
