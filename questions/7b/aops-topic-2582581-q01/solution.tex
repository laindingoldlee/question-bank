Let $x$ frisbees be sold at $10$ dollars and $y$ at $12$ dollars, so $x+y=661$. The actual revenue is $10x+12y$ and the recorded revenue is $10y+12x$. The difference is
\[(12x+10y)-(10x+12y)=2(x-y)=378,\]
so $x-y=189$. With $x+y=661$ this gives $x=\boxed{425}$ (and $y=236$).
