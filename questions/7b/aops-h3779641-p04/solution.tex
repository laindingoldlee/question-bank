AB is opposite thirty degrees, so the special right-triangle ratios give AC=4 and $BC=2\sqrt3$. Their product is $2\cdot4\cdot2\sqrt3=\boxed{16\sqrt3}$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair B=(0,0),A=(0,2),C=(2sqrt(3),0);draw(A--B--C--cycle);label("$A$",A,(-1,1));label("$B$",B,(-1,-1));label("$C:30^\circ$",C,(1,0));label("$2$",(0,1),(-1,0));label("$4$",(A+C)/2,(0,1));
\end{asy}
