Clearing denominators (with $y\ne1$ and $y\ne0$):
\[5(x-2)=3(y-1)\iff5x-3y=7,\qquad3(x-1)=2y\iff3x-2y=3.\]
Multiplying the first equation by $2$ and the second by $3$ gives $10x-6y=14$ and $9x-6y=9$; subtracting, $x=5$, and then $y=\dfrac{3x-3}{2}=6$. Both denominators are nonzero ($y-1=5$, $y=6$), and indeed $\dfrac{3}{5}=\dfrac35$ and $\dfrac46=\dfrac23$. So $xy=\boxed{30}$.
