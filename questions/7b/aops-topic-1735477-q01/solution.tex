Let there be $n$ sparrows. The first condition says $n>5$. In the second arrangement $n$ is even (two on each occupied feeder) and at most $4$ feeders are occupied, so $n\le8$. The even numbers with $5<n\le8$ are $6$ and $8$, and both work: $6$ sparrows leave $1$ flying, or fill $3$ feeders leaving $2$ empty; $8$ sparrows leave $3$ flying, or fill $4$ feeders leaving $1$ empty. So there are $\boxed{6\text{ or }8}$ sparrows.
