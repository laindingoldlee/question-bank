The language of Thironos has exactly four words: Gmontlet, Inor, Nam and Tonse. A sentence is a sequence of $1$ to $6$ words, in which words may be repeated, subject to two rules: Gmontlet may never stand next to Inor, and every Inor must stand next to at least one Nam. How many sentences are there?
