A satellite in geostationary orbit stays over the same spot on Earth: it completes one orbit in the same time that Earth rotates once about its axis, $23.93$ hours. The satellite's orbit is a circle of radius $4.23\times10^7$ m. Calculate the satellite's orbital speed in metres per second, to the nearest metre per second. Use $3.14159$ for $\pi$.
