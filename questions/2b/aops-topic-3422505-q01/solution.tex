Four consecutive positive integers starting at $k$ sum to $4k+6$. Hence $T_n=\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$ must be at least $10$ and congruent to $2$ modulo $4$. Checking residues modulo $8$ in $n(n+1)\equiv4\pmod8$ gives exactly $n\equiv3$ or $4\pmod8$. Among $1,\ldots,2023$, each residue occurs $253$ times. Only $n=3$ fails the lower bound, since $T_3=6$; all later candidates have triangular sum at least $10$. Thus the count is $253+253-1=\boxed{505}$.
