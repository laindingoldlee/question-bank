$53$ is impossible: in $7a+10b=53$, reducing modulo $7$ gives $3b\equiv4$, hence $b\equiv6\pmod7$, forcing $10b\ge60$. The seven consecutive values $54,\ldots,60$ are representable as $7\cdot2+10\cdot4$, $7\cdot5+10\cdot2$, $7\cdot8$, $7+10\cdot5$, $7\cdot4+10\cdot3$, $7\cdot7+10$, and $10\cdot6$. Adding $7$ repeatedly represents every larger integer. Thus the answer is $\boxed{53}$.
