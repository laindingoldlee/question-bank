The number with $k$ digits all equal to $6$ is $\dfrac{6}{9}\left(10^k-1\right)=\dfrac{2}{3}\left(10^k-1\right)$. Hence
\[
A=\dfrac{2}{3}\sum_{k=1}^{n}\left(10^k-1\right)=\dfrac{2}{3}\left(\dfrac{10^{n+1}-10}{9}-n\right)=\boxed{\dfrac{2\left(10^{n+1}-9n-10\right)}{27}}.
\]
Check: $n=1$ gives $\dfrac{2\cdot81}{27}=6$, and $n=2$ gives $\dfrac{2\cdot972}{27}=72=6+66$.
