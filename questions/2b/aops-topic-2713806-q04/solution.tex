Given $k$, the probability is $\dfrac1{\binom k2}=\dfrac2{k(k-1)}=2\left(\dfrac1{k-1}-\dfrac1k\right)$. Averaging over $k=2,\dots,10$ telescopes: $\dfrac19\cdot2\left(1-\dfrac1{10}\right)=\boxed{\dfrac15}$.
