\textbf{Setting up the equation.} Fix one team. Its division has $3$ other teams, and it plays each of them $N$ games: $3N$ division games. The other division has $4$ teams, and it plays each of them $M$ games: $4M$ games. So
\[3N+4M=76 .\]
(The coefficient $3$ is the number of division opponents, $\dbinom31=3$; a team is involved in only the games against those three opponents, not in all $\dbinom42$ pairings of its division.)

\textbf{Solving.} Since $N>2M$, we get $76=3N+4M>6M+4M=10M$, so $M<7.6$; with $M>4$ this leaves $M\in\{5,6,7\}$. Also $3N=76-4M$ must be divisible by $3$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $M=5$: $76-20=56$, not divisible by $3$;
\item $M=6$: $76-24=52$, not divisible by $3$;
\item $M=7$: $76-28=48$, so $N=16$, and indeed $16>14$.
\end{itemize}
So $N=16$, $M=7$, and a team plays $3N=48$ games within its division: $\boxed{\textbf{(B)}\ 48}$.
