The angle $ADC$ subtends the major arc $AC$, so it is $120^\circ$. By the cosine rule, $1 = A D^2 + D C^2 - 2 A D \cdot D C \cos 120^\circ = A D^2 + D C^2 + \dfrac{1}{26}$. Thus the answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{25}{26}}$.
