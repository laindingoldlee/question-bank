The arc of a semicircle of radius $R$ has length $\pi R$, so $\pi R=20\pi$ and $R=20$; the small radius is $r=10$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real R=2, r=1;
fill(arc((0,0),R,0,180)--cycle,lightgray);
fill(arc((r,0),r,0,180)--cycle,white);
draw(arc((0,0),R,0,180)--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(arc((r,0),r,0,180),linewidth(0.9));
dot((0,0)); dot((r,0));
label("$O$",(0,0),S); label("$P$",(r,0),S);
draw((0,0)--R*dir(135),red); label("$20$",R/2*dir(135),SW,red);
draw((r,0)--(r,0)+r*dir(60),blue); label("$10$",(r,0)+r/2*dir(60),E,blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The shaded area is
\[
\dfrac{1}{2}\pi R^2-\dfrac{1}{2}\pi r^2=\dfrac{1}{2}\pi(400-100)=\boxed{150\pi}.
\]
(The position of the small semicircle does not matter, as long as it lies inside the big one; the figure shows it with its diameter from the centre $O$ to the end of the big diameter.)
