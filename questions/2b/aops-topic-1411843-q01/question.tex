A drawing consists of a big semicircle and a small semicircle inside it, with the diameter of the small one lying on the diameter of the big one. The curved arc of the bigger semicircle has length $20\pi$, and the ratio of the radius of the bigger semicircle to the radius of the smaller one is $2:1$. What is the area of the region inside the bigger semicircle but outside the smaller one? Give your answer in terms of $\pi$.
