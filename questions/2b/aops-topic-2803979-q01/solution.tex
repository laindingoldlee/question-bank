With $\alpha=1+2\sqrt2$: $\alpha^2=9+4\sqrt2$, $\alpha^6=(9+4\sqrt2)^3=1593+1100\sqrt2$, and $\alpha^{12}=(1593+1100\sqrt2)^2=4957649+3504600\sqrt2$. (The conjugate trick does not help here, since $|1-2\sqrt2|>1$.)

(a) $1.41421<\sqrt2<1.41422$ gives $1555.63<1100\sqrt2<1555.65$, so $\alpha^6\approx3148.6$ and the nearest integer is $\boxed{3149}$.

(b) $1.4142135<\sqrt2<1.4142136$ gives $4956252.6<3504600\sqrt2<4956253.0$, so $9913901.6<\alpha^{12}<9913902.0$ and the nearest integer is $\boxed{9913902}$.
