Factor:
\[
2010=a^2-b^2+c^2-d^2=(a-b)(a+b)+(c-d)(c+d).
\]
Since $a-b\ge1$ and $c-d\ge1$, the right side is at least $(a+b)+(c+d)=2010$, with equality only if $a-b=1$ and $c-d=1$. So $b=a-1$ and $d=c-1$, and the sum condition becomes $2a+2c-2=2010$, i.e.\ $a+c=1006$.

Conversely, any $a,c$ with $a+c=1006$, $b=a-1$, $d=c-1$ work provided $b>c$ and $d\ge1$: that is, $a-1>c=1006-a$, so $a\ge504$, and $c\ge2$, so $a\le1004$. Hence $a$ can be any integer from $504$ to $1004$, which gives $\boxed{501}$ values.
