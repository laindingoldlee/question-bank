The angles of $ABC$ are 36, 72, 72 degrees. In $BCD$, the angles at $B,C,D$ are 36, 72, 72, so $BD=BC=30$. In $ABD$, the angles at $A,B$ are both 36, so $AD=BD=\boxed{30}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair B=(0,0),C=(30,0),A=extension(B,dir(72),C,C+dir(108)),D=extension(B,dir(36),A,C);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(B--D);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,E);
\end{asy}
