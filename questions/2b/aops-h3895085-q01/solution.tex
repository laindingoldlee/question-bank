Number the eight $01$ pairs from $1$ to $8$. If the three chosen $1$s come from pairs $b,c,e$ and the chosen $0$s from pairs $a,d$, their order spells $01101$ exactly when $a\le b<c<d\le e$. The six nonnegative integers $a-1,b-a,c-b-1,d-c-1,e-d,8-e$ sum to $5$, and conversely each such six-tuple specifies one subsequence. Stars and bars therefore gives $\binom{10}{5}=252$. The product of its digits is $2\cdot5\cdot2=\boxed{20}$.
