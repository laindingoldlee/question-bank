If $p$ divides both numbers, it divides their difference $(n^3+5)-(n^3+3)=2$, so $p=2$. Conversely, $p=2$ works: for $n=1$ the numbers are $4$ and $6$, both even (any odd $n$ works). So there is exactly $\boxed{1}$ such prime.
