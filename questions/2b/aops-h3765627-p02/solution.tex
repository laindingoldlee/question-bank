The hundredths column gives $2A\equiv0\pmod{10}$, so $A=0$ or 5. Multiplying the whole equation by 100 and simplifying gives $100C+102A=90B$. If $A=0$, then $10C=9B$, impossible for nonzero digits $B,C$. Thus $A=5$, and $10C+51=9B$. Reduction modulo 9 gives $C=3$, then $B=9$. Hence $\boxed{A=5,B=9,C=3}$; indeed $3.95+5.55=9.5$.
