Factor $Q(x)=(x^2+1)(x^2+x+1)$. The first factor divides $x^4-1$ and the second divides $x^3-1$, so modulo these factors $x^{2025}$ is respectively $x$ and 1.

Seek a cubic remainder of the form $R(x)=x+(x^2+1)(ax+b)$, ensuring the first condition. Modulo $x^2+x+1$, use $x^2\equiv-x-1$ to get
\[R(x)\equiv a+(a+1-b)x.\]
Setting this equal to 1 gives $a=1,b=2$. The two factors are coprime, since their difference is $x$ and neither vanishes at 0; matching both residues therefore matches the residue modulo their product. Thus
\[R(x)=x^3+2x^2+2x+2,\qquad R(3)=27+18+6+2=\boxed{53}.\]
