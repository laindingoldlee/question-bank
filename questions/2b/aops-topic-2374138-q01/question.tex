The $n$th triangular number is $T_n=\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$ (with $T_0=0$), and the $n$th pentagonal number is $P_n=\dfrac{n(3n-1)}{2}$ (so $P_1,P_2,P_3,\dots=1,5,12,\dots$). Prove that $P_n=3T_{n-1}+n$ for every positive integer $n$.
