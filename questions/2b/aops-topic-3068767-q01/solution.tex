An even integer has the form $2k$, and $2k\le x$ is equivalent to $k\le\dfrac{x}{2}$. Its largest value is therefore $\boxed{2\left\lfloor\dfrac{x}{2}\right\rfloor}$. An odd integer has the form $2k+1$, giving $\boxed{2\left\lfloor\dfrac{x-1}{2}\right\rfloor+1}$. These formulas also hold for negative $x$.
