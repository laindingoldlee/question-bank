If the common border width is $d$, the hole has dimensions $10-2d$ and $6-2d$. Its area equation gives $(10-2d)(6-2d)=12$, or $d^2-8d+12=0$. The roots are 2 and 6, but only $0<d<3$ is geometrically possible. Thus $\boxed{2\text{ cm}}$, choice D.
