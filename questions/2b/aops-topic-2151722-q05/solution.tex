From October 8, 2015 to October 8, 2019 there are four years, one of which contains February 29, 2016, so that span is $4\cdot365+1=1461$ days.

From October 8, 2019 to June 16, 2020 the days are: $23$ more in October, then $30$ (November), $31$ (December), $31$ (January), $29$ (February 2020, a leap year), $31$ (March), $30$ (April), $31$ (May) and $16$ (June), a total of
\[23+30+31+31+29+31+30+31+16=252 .\]
So June 16, 2020 is $1461+252=1713$ days after October 8, 2015. Counting both end days adds one more day:
\[1713+1=\boxed{1714}\text{ days}.\]
