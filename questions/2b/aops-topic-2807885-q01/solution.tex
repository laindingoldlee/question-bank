Write the sum $S$ forwards and backwards and add term by term:
\[2S=\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\big[(a+kd)+(a+(n-1-k)d)\big]=n\big(2a+(n-1)d\big).\]
So $S=\dfrac n2\big(2a+(n-1)d\big)$, i.e. $n$ times the average of the first and last terms. $\blacksquare$
