Write the denominator over the common denominator $vwxyz$:
\[\dfrac1v+\dfrac1w+\dfrac1x+\dfrac1y+\dfrac1z=\dfrac{wxyz+vxyz+vwyz+vwxz+vwxy}{vwxyz}.\]
Dividing $5$ by this fraction means multiplying by its reciprocal:
\[\dfrac{5}{\dfrac1v+\dfrac1w+\dfrac1x+\dfrac1y+\dfrac1z}=\boxed{\dfrac{5vwxyz}{wxyz+vxyz+vwyz+vwxz+vwxy}}.\]
Each term of the denominator is the product of four of the five numbers (the one missing from $vwxyz$). This quantity is the \emph{harmonic mean} of $v,w,x,y,z$. For example, for $v=w=x=y=z=t$ it equals $\dfrac{5t^5}{5t^4}=t$.
