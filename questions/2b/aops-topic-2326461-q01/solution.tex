From the first equation $y=\dfrac23x$, and from the second $z=\dfrac32x$. Then the third gives
\[x+\dfrac23x+\dfrac32x=\dfrac{19}{6}x=1,\qquad x=\dfrac{6}{19},\]
so $y=\dfrac{4}{19}$ and $z=\dfrac{9}{19}$. The solution is $\boxed{(x,y,z)=\left(\dfrac{6}{19},\dfrac{4}{19},\dfrac{9}{19}\right)}$.
