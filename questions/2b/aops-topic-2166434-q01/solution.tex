``Inversely proportional with constant $k$'' means $a_{n+1}=\dfrac{k}{a_n}$, i.e. $a_na_{n+1}=k$ for every $n$. From $a_1a_2=2\cdot5$ we get $k=10$. Then
\[a_3=\dfrac{10}{5}=2,\quad a_4=\dfrac{10}{2}=5,\quad a_5=2,\ \dots\]
In fact $a_{n+1}a_{n+2}=k=a_na_{n+1}$, so $a_{n+2}=a_n$ and the terms alternate $2,5,2,5,\dots$ and every even-numbered term is $5$. The $12$th term is $\boxed{5}$.
