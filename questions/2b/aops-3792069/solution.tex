If indices $i<j$ are swapped, $p(x)-q(x)=(a_i-a_j)x^i(1-x^{j-i})$. The root $1$ always occurs; $0$ occurs exactly when $i>0$; and $-1$ occurs exactly when $j-i$ is even. There are no other real roots. Linearity of expectation gives $1+\dfrac{\binom{100}2}{\binom{101}2}+\dfrac{\binom{51}2+\binom{50}2}{\binom{101}2}=1+\dfrac{99}{101}+\dfrac{50}{101}=\boxed{\dfrac{250}{101}}$.
