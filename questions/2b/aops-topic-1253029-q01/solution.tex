Since $CT\parallel BD$, triangles $ACT$ and $ABD$ are similar with ratio $\dfrac{AT}{AD}=\dfrac{12}{18}=\dfrac{2}{3}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(10.5,0),D=(4,7); pair C=A+(2/3)*(B-A), T=A+(2/3)*(D-A);
draw(A--B--D--cycle,linewidth(1)); draw(C--T,blue);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$D$",D,N);label("$C$",C,S);label("$T$",T,W);
label("$7$",(C+B)/2,S); label("$12$",(A+T)/2,W); label("$6$",(T+D)/2,W); label("$18$",(C+T)/2,E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

So $BD=\dfrac{3}{2}CT=27$, and $AC=\dfrac{2}{3}AB$ means $BC=\dfrac{1}{3}AB=7$, so $AB=21$. The perimeter is $21+18+27=\boxed{66}$.
