For a fixed year, the first four digits DDMM must be the reverse of the year YYYY. The remaining years 2023 through 2029 would require days $32,42,52,62,72,82,92$, all invalid. For 2030, the reversed four digits are 0302, yielding the valid date $\boxed{03.02.2030}$. There is at most one palindromic date per year, so this is the next one.
