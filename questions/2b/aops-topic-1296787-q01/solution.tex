Whether or not order matters, the probability is the fraction of $4$-element digit sets that are consecutive (each set gives the same number, $24$, of codes). The consecutive sets are $\{1,2,3,4\},\dots,\{6,7,8,9\}$: $6$ of the $\dbinom94=126$ sets. The probability is $\dfrac{6}{126}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{21}}$.
