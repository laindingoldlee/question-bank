A lab has three full vials: a $1$ oz vial of grape juice, a $2$ oz vial of sulfuric acid and a $3$ oz vial of water, and three empty vials of $1$, $2$ and $3$ oz. With no other equipment (liquid can only be poured from one vial into another until the first is empty or the second is full), make three full vials, one of each size, each containing the three liquids in the same ratio.
