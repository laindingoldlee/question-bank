The equilateral triangle has circumradius $R=2\sqrt3$ and inradius $r=\sqrt3$. As it rotates continuously through 60 degrees, its three vertices sweep three disjoint 60-degree arcs. In those angular sectors the swept region reaches radius $R$, contributing total area $\pi \dfrac{R^2}{2}=6\pi$. The three remaining angular gaps each have width 60 degrees. By symmetry, each gap has area $r^2\int_{\dfrac{\pi}{6}}^{\dfrac{\pi}{3}}\sec^2u\,du=r^2(\sqrt3-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3})=2\sqrt3$. Thus the swept area is $6\pi+6\sqrt3=6\pi+\sqrt{108}$, giving $\boxed{a+b=114}$.
