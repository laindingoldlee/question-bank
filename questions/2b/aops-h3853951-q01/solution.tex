A point $(x,y,z)$ is on the sphere exactly when its distance from $(0,0,0)$ is $1$. By the three-dimensional distance formula,
\[
\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2}=1,\qquad \boxed{x^2+y^2+z^2=1}.
\]
The equation describes the surface; the inequality $x^2+y^2+z^2\le1$ would describe the solid ball.
