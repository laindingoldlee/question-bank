Take $O$ as the origin and denote the radius vectors by $a,b,c$. The vector $h=a+b+c$ is the orthocenter: $(h-a)\cdot(b-c)=(b+c)\cdot(b-c)=|b|^2-|c|^2=0$, and likewise for the other altitudes. Thus $|h|^2=3\cdot12^2+2(a\cdot b+b\cdot c+c\cdot a)$. Independent uniform points have mean vector zero, so each cross term has expectation zero. Hence $\boxed{\mathbb E(OH^2)=432}$.
