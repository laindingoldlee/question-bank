$BEEKEEPER$ has five $E$'s and the distinct letters $B,K,P,R$. Arrange $B,K,P,R$ in $4!=24$ ways; this leaves $5$ gaps (ends included), and the five $E$'s must occupy all five gaps, one each. So there are $\boxed{24}$ arrangements.
