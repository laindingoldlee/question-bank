A triangular board is cut into $100$ small triangular cells by lines parallel to its sides. Two cells that share a side are called neighbors. In each cell there is a grasshopper. All at once, every grasshopper hops from its cell to a neighboring cell; this happens $9$ times. Prove that afterwards at least $10$ cells are empty.
