Om and Madhav forget what time they agreed to meet. Om arrives at a uniformly random time between 9:30 am and 11:00 am, and independently Madhav arrives at a uniformly random time between 10:00 am and 12:00 pm. What is the probability that they arrive within one minute of each other? Express your answer as a common fraction.
