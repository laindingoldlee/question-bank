$x^2+x-20=(x+5)(x-4)$, and a length is positive, so $x=4$. The sides $4,18,20$ do form a triangle, since $4+18>20$. Its semiperimeter is $s=\dfrac{4+18+20}{2}=21$, and Heron's formula gives
\[\text{Area}=\sqrt{21(21-4)(21-18)(21-20)}=\sqrt{21\cdot17\cdot3\cdot1}=\sqrt{1071}=\boxed{3\sqrt{119}}.\]
