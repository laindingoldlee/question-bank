At a relay event, Rob and Kat decide to run a total of $52$ laps combined. Rob begins running at a constant rate of $3$ minutes per lap. After $36$ minutes, Kat begins running at a constant rate of $2$ minutes per lap, while Rob keeps running. How many more minutes will the two have to run, after Kat starts, to reach their goal?
