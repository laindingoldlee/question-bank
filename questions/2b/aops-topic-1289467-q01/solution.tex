Suppose $n$ is composite, so $n=ab$ with $1<a\le b<n$. If $a>\sqrt n$, then also $b>\sqrt n$, and $ab>n$, a contradiction. So $a\le\sqrt n$, and $a$ is a divisor with $1<a\le\sqrt n$. By contraposition, if no such divisor exists then $n$ is prime. $\blacksquare$
