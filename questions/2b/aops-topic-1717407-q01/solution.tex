The sides and diagonals together are all $15$ segments joining two of the six vertices. Look at the five segments from $A$. By the pigeonhole principle at least three of them have the same colour, say $AX$, $AY$, $AZ$ are blue.
\begin{itemize}
\item If one of $XY$, $YZ$, $ZX$ is blue, say $XY$, then $AXY$ has three blue sides.
\item Otherwise $XY$, $YZ$, $ZX$ are all yellow, and $XYZ$ has three yellow sides.
\end{itemize}
In either case there is a monochromatic triangle. $\blacksquare$
