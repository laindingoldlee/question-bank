Compute $3^{50}$ exactly by repeated squaring:
\[
3^{5}=243,\quad3^{10}=59049,\quad3^{20}=59049^2=3486784401,\quad3^{25}=3^{20}\cdot3^5=847288609443,
\]
\[
3^{50}=\left(3^{25}\right)^2=847288609443^2=717897987691852588770249 .
\]
The digits from the left are $7,1,7,8,9,7,\dots$, so the sixth digit is $\boxed{7}$. (There is no real shortcut for a digit in the middle of a power; the leading digits can be estimated with logarithms, $50\log_{10}3\approx23.856$, which shows that $3^{50}$ has $24$ digits and begins with $7$, but the sixth digit needs more precision than such estimates give by hand.)
