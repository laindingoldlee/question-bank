Let $R$ be the perpendicular foot from $E$ to line $AB$. A point on an internal angle bisector has equal perpendicular distances to its sides, by congruence of the two right triangles with common hypotenuse. Since $E$ lies on the bisector of $\angle DAB$, $EP=ER$. Since it also lies on the bisector of $\angle ABC$, $EQ=ER$. Therefore $\boxed{EP=EQ}$.
