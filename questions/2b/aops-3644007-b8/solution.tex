Any path must cross the equatorial square. The two opposite apex heights above and below its plane are $\sqrt2$. The nearest equatorial boundary point to the axis is an edge midpoint, one unit from the axis. Thus the straight spatial distance from each apex to any possible crossing is at least $\sqrt{2+1}=\sqrt3$. The total is at least $2\sqrt3$, attained by the two face altitudes meeting at an equatorial edge midpoint. Therefore the shortest surface route is $\boxed{2\sqrt3}$.
