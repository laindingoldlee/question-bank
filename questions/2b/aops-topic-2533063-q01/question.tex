The integers from $1$ to $5005$ are listed as follows. First, all the odd integers are listed in increasing order; next, all the integers divisible by $2$ but not by $4$, in increasing order; next, all the integers divisible by $4$ but not by $8$, in increasing order; and so on, until every integer from $1$ to $5005$ has been listed.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What is the last integer listed?
\item[(b)] What is the $2000$th integer listed?
\end{enumerate}
