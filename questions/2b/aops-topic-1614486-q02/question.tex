\textbf{Euclidean algorithm.} Let $a$ and $b$ be positive integers and write $a=bq+r$ with integers $q$ and $0\le r<b$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $\gcd(a,b)=\gcd(b,r)$ (where $\gcd(b,0)=b$).
\item[(b)] Explain why repeating this step (replace $(a,b)$ by $(b,r)$ until the remainder is $0$) must stop, and why the last nonzero remainder is $\gcd(a,b)$.
\end{enumerate}
