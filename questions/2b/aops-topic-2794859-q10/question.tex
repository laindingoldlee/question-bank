Triangle $ABC$ has circumcircle. Let $M$ be the midpoint of the arc $BAC$, and let the angle bisector from $A$ meet $BC$ at $N$. Line $MN$ meets the circle again at $P$. If $PB=3$, $AB=5$, $AC=7$, find $PC$.
