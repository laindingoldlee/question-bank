The step ``$a\cdot0=0\Rightarrow0\div0=a$'' assumes $0\div0$ is a well-defined number. Division $p\div q$ means the unique $r$ with $rq=p$. For $q=0$, $p=0$, every $r$ works, so there is no unique value and $0\div0$ is undefined. $\boxed{0\div0\text{ is undefined}}$.
