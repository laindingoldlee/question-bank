Factor $6!$ from the numerator and $5!$ from the denominator:
\[
\dfrac{6!\,(10\cdot9\cdot8\cdot7-8\cdot7+1)}{5!\,(9\cdot8\cdot7\cdot6-7\cdot6+1)}=\dfrac{6\,(5040-56+1)}{3024-42+1}=\dfrac{6\cdot4985}{2983}=\boxed{\dfrac{29910}{2983}}.
\]
This is in lowest terms: $29910=2\cdot3\cdot5\cdot997$ and $2983=19\cdot157$ share no prime factor.
