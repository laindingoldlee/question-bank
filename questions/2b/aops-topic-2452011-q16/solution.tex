Walking from home to school takes $45$ minutes (6:30 to 7:15), and running takes $30$ minutes (7:00 to 7:30). The distance is the same in both directions, so walking home takes $45$ minutes and running back takes $30$ minutes: $\boxed{75\text{ minutes}}$ in total (ignoring the time spent at home).
