On a normal school day, Ash leaves home at 6:30 AM and walks to school at his constant walking speed, arriving at 7:15 AM. Today he leaves home at 7:00 AM and runs to school at his constant running speed, arriving at 7:30 AM. At school he realises that he has forgotten his bow, so he walks back home at his walking speed to get it, and then runs back to school at his running speed. How long does this round trip take?
