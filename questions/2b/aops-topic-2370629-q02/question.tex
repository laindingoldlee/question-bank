A school offers $7$ different maths classes, $9$ different science classes, $8$ different English classes and $4$ different foreign-language classes. A student chooses a set of different classes, taking at least $4$ and at most $8$ classes in total, including at least $2$ maths classes. How many different sets of classes are possible?
