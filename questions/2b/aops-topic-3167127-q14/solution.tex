The combined numerator is $n^4-2n^3+2n^2-2n+1=(n-1)^2(n^2+1)$, while $n^4-1=(n-1)(n+1)(n^2+1)$. Each summand reduces to $\dfrac{n-1}{n+1}=1-\dfrac{2}{n+1}$. Thus the sum is $5-2(\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{5}+\dfrac{1}{6}+\dfrac{1}{7})=\boxed{\dfrac{197}{70}}$.
