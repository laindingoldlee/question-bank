$\sqrt{20}+2=2\left(\sqrt5+1\right)$, so the sum is $\dfrac{4+3}{2\left(\sqrt5+1\right)}=\dfrac{7}{2\left(\sqrt5+1\right)}=\dfrac{7\left(\sqrt5-1\right)}{2\cdot4}=\boxed{\dfrac{7\sqrt5-7}{8}}$.
