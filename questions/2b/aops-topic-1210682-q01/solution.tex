Units: $8+6+c$ ends in $8$, so $c\equiv4\pmod{10}$, $c=4$, carry $1$. Tens: $7+b+5+1=13+b$ ends in $3$, so $b=0$, carry $1$. Hundreds: $a+6+1+1=a+8$ must be $10+d$ (to give the thousands digit $1$), so $d=a-2$.

With $a,b,c,d$ different even digits, $b=0$ and $c=4$ rule out $a=2$ ($d=0$), $a=4$, and $a=6$ ($d=4$). So $a=\boxed{8}$ (and $d=6$): $878+606+154=1638$.
