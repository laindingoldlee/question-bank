The conditional equation is $\dfrac{4p^3(1-p)}{4p^3(1-p)+p^4}=\dfrac{4}{9}$. Its denominator is positive, so $p>0$. Simplifying gives $9(1-p)=4-3p$, hence $p=\dfrac{5}{6}$. Exactly two heads has probability $\binom42(\dfrac{5}{6})^2(\dfrac{1}{6})^2=\boxed{\dfrac{25}{216}}$.
