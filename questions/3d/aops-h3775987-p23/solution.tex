Put the rectangle in the plane with $A=(0,0)$, $B=(a,0)$, $C=(a,b)$, $D=(0,b)$, and let the apex have perpendicular projection $(x,y)$ and height $h>0$. Since $ab=6$, the first and third face areas give $a^2(y^2+h^2)=400$ and $a^2((b-y)^2+h^2)=484$. Subtracting yields $ay=-4$. The fourth and second faces similarly give $b^2(x^2+h^2)=784$ and $b^2((a-x)^2+h^2)=676$, yielding $bx=12$. Therefore $a^2h^2=400-16=384$ and $b^2h^2=784-144=640$. Dividing gives $\dfrac{a^2}{b^2}=\dfrac{3}{5}$, so $\boxed{\dfrac{AB}{BC}=\dfrac{\sqrt{15}}{5}}$. The computed projection lies outside the rectangle, which is permitted for an oblique pyramid.
