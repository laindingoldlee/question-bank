Let $W(1)=0$, interpreting the player who receives 1 as having already lost. For $n>1$, $W(n)=1$ exactly when some $d\in\{2,\ldots,9\}$ divides $n$ and has $W (\dfrac{n}{d}) = 0$. Evaluate in increasing order of $n$; every needed quotient is smaller, so induction proves the recurrence gives the exact winning status. Applying it to $1\le n\le160$ and retaining three-digit integers with only the allowed prime factors gives first losing value $\boxed{160}$. Its permitted quotients are $80,40,32,20$, all winning; every earlier admissible three-digit starting value is winning.
