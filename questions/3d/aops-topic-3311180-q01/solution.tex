Let $s_j(n)$ be the probability of no run of four after $n$ presses, with the final success-run having length $j\in\{0,1,2,3\}$. Initially $(s_0,s_1,s_2,s_3)=(1,0,0,0)$. With $p=\dfrac{1}{28}$, update by $s_0(n+1)=(1-p)\sum_{j=0}^3s_j(n)$ and $s_j(n+1)=p\,s_{j-1}(n)$ for $j=1,2,3$. The desired probability is $1-\sum s_j(n)$. Iteration, or powering the associated four-by-four matrix, gives $P_{441826}=0.4999996560668826557\ldots$ and $P_{441827}=0.5000004404832300280\ldots$. These are separated from $\dfrac{1}{2}$ by more than $3\cdot10^{-7}$; fifty-digit arithmetic suffices to resolve the comparison. The event probability is nondecreasing in $n$, so the least value is $\boxed{441827}$.
