The five-digit sum is at most 11097, so $d=1$. To reach 10000, the four-digit number must have $a=9$. Let units, tens, and hundreds carries be $u,v,w$. Thousands give $9+w=10+f$. Since $w$ is 1 or 2 and $f\ne d=1$, we obtain $w=1,f=0$. Hundreds give $b+1+v=10$, while tens give $c+1+u=10v$. Thus $v=1,b=8,c+u=9$. Units give $9+2e=1+10u$. If $u=1$, then $e=1$, forbidden; hence $u=2,e=6,c=7$. The solution is $\boxed{(9,8,7,1,6,0)}$, checked by $9879+116+16=10011$.
