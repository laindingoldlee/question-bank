Let Bob's age last year be $10x+y$, so the daughter was $10y+x$ and the wife was $10x+y-2$. Ten years from now is eleven years after last year, so
\[10x+y-2+11=2\left(10y+x+11\right)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad8x=19y+13.\]
Modulo $8$: $3y+5\equiv0$, so $3y\equiv3$ and $y\equiv1\pmod8$, i.e. $y=1$ or $y=9$. For $y=9$, $x=\dfrac{184}8=23$ is not a digit. For $y=1$, $x=4$.

So last year Bob was $41$ and his daughter $14$. Now Bob is $42$, his wife $40$ and his daughter $15$ (check: in ten years $50=2\cdot25$). The sum is $42+40+15=\boxed{97}$.
