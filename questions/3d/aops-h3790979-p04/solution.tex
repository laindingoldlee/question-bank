An $n$-gon has interior-angle sum $(n-2)180^\circ$. The bound gives $n\le2+\dfrac{2020}{180}<14$, so the greatest integer is $\boxed{13}$. A convex thirteen-gon exists and has angle sum $1980^\circ$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

\end{asy}
