The square diagonal is a diameter, and its length is $\sqrt{4^2+4^2}=\boxed{4\sqrt2}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw(circle((2,2),2*sqrt(2)));draw((0,0)--(4,0)--(4,4)--(0,4)--cycle);draw((0,0)--(4,4),blue);label("$4$",(2,0),S);
\end{asy}
