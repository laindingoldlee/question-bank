For a nonzero product the month must be $11$ or $12$; all other two-digit month strings contain zero. The largest day-digit product is $2\cdot9=18$, attained on day $29$. December contributes month product $1\cdot2=2$, larger than November\textquotesingle s $1$. Thus December $29$ gives the maximum $18\cdot2=\boxed{36}$.
