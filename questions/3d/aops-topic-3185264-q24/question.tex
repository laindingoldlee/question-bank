Grand-Ovich the ant is at a vertex of a regular hexagon and he moves to one of the adjacent vertices every minute with equal probability. Let the probability that after $8$ minutes he will have returned to the starting vertex at least once be the common fraction $\dfrac{a}{b}$ in lowest terms. What is $a + b$?
