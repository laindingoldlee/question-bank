If $s_5(n)$ is the sum of base-$5$ digits, Legendre's formula gives $4v_5(n!)=n-s_5(n)$. Thus the divisibility condition is $s_5(n)\le3$. Since $2021=(31041)_5$, the largest allowable number is $(30000)_5=3\cdot625=\boxed{1875}$: any larger number at most $2021$ has leading digit $3$ and at least one additional nonzero digit.
