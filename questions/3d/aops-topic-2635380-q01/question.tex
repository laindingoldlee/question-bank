For a positive integer $n$, let $A_n$ be the string obtained by writing all $26^n$ strings of $n$ lowercase letters one after another in alphabetical order. For example,
\[A_1=\text{abcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz}\qquad\text{and}\qquad A_2=\text{aaabac}\dots\text{azbabbbc}\dots\text{zyzz}.\]
Let $f(n)$ be the number of times the word ``mathicorn'' occurs as a block of consecutive letters in $A_n$. Evaluate $\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{9}f(i)$.
