Let $a$ pounds of the $\$2.10$ coffee and $b$ pounds of the $\$1.90$ coffee be used. Then
\[
a+b=300,\qquad 2.10a+1.90b=2.00\cdot300=600 .
\]
Substituting $b=300-a$: $2.10a+570-1.90a=600$, so $0.20a=30$ and $a=150$. He must mix $\boxed{150\text{ pounds of each grade}}$. (This makes sense: $\$2.00$ is exactly halfway between the two prices.)
