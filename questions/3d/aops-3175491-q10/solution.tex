For $c=\dfrac{ab-1}{a+b-2}$, subtract one to get $c-1=\dfrac{(a-1)(b-1)}{a+b-2}$, hence $\dfrac{1}{c-1}=\dfrac{1}{a-1}+\dfrac{1}{b-1}$. All numbers remain above one. Thus the sum of transformed values is invariant, and the final value satisfies $\dfrac{1}{C-1}=\sum_{n=1}^{10}2^{-n}=\dfrac{1023}{1024}$. Therefore $C=\dfrac{2047}{1023}$ for every operation order; in particular its minimum has $\boxed{p+q=3070}$.
