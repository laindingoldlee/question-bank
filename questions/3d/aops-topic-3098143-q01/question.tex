Independently choose integers $m$ uniformly from 1 through 100 and $n$ uniformly from 101 through 200. A bag starts with $m$ red and $n$ green marbles. Repeatedly replace two marbles of the same colour by one red marble, or two of different colours by one green marble, until one remains. What is the probability the last marble is red?
