The number of green marbles changes by either 0 or $-2$, so its parity is invariant. The final marble is red exactly when the initial green count $n$ is even. Exactly 50 of the 100 equally likely choices of $n$ are even. Thus the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$, or 50 percent, independent of $m$ and of the replacement choices.
