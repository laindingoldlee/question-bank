\textbf{(a)} Every element counted on either side lies in $A\cup B\cup C$, so it suffices to check that each element $x$ of $A\cup B\cup C$ contributes exactly $1$ to the right side. Suppose $x$ lies in exactly $r$ of the three sets, $r\in\{1,2,3\}$. Then $x$ is counted $r$ times in $|A|+|B|+|C|$, $\dbinom r2$ times in the pairwise intersections and $\dbinom r3$ times in the triple intersection, so it contributes
\[r-\dbinom r2+\dbinom r3=\begin{cases}1-0+0=1,&r=1,\\2-1+0=1,&r=2,\\3-3+1=1,&r=3.\end{cases}\]
So the right side counts every element of $A\cup B\cup C$ exactly once. $\blacksquare$

\textbf{(b)} Let $A$, $B$, $C$ be the multiples of $2$, $3$, $5$ in $\{1,\dots,100\}$. Intersections are multiples of $6$, $15$, $10$ and $30$. So
\[|A\cup B\cup C|=50+33+20-16-6-10+3=\boxed{74}.\]
