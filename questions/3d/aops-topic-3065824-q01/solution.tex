When $r>0$, the numerator product is $(n-r+1)(n-r+2)\cdots n=\dfrac{n!}{(n-r)!}$ and the denominator product is $r!$. Their quotient is $\dfrac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}=\binom nr$. For $r=0$, both sides are 1 by definition.
