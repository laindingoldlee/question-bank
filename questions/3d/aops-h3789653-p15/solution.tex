Put $AB=AC=s$ and $CD=t$, with $0<t<s$. The cosine law in $ABC$ gives $\cos A=1-\dfrac{25}{2s^2}$. Applying it in $ABD$ gives
\[BD^2=s^2+(s-t)^2-2s(s-t)\cos A=t^2+\dfrac{25(s-t)}{s}.\]
The condition $BD^2=st$ becomes $(s-t)(\dfrac{25}{s}-t)=0$. Since $D$ is interior, $t=\dfrac{25}{s}$ and $BD^2=st=25$. Hence $\boxed{BD=5}$.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

\end{asy}
