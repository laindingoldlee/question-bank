The parity of the total sum is invariant, because replacing $a+b$ by $|a-b|$ changes it by an even number. Initially the sum is $1012\cdot2025$, which is even, so the final entry is even. Initially 1012 entries are odd. Selecting two odds reduces this count by 2; an odd and an even leaves it unchanged, and two evens also leave it unchanged. Thus the number of odd-odd selections is $\dfrac{1012}{2}=\boxed{506}$.
