\textbf{Answer:} each has $\boxed{\dfrac{2^{2017}-2}{3}}$ candies.

Let $N=2^{2017}$ and call one execution of Steps 1 and 2 a round. If all three numbers are even, Step 1 does nothing, and if the numbers are $x,x,y$ in clockwise order (two equal numbers followed by a different one), Step 2 gives
\[
\left(\dfrac{x+y}{2},\ x,\ \dfrac{x+y}{2}\right)
\]
(the first student keeps $\dfrac{x}{2}$ and receives $\dfrac{y}{2}$, the second keeps $\dfrac{x}{2}$ and receives $\dfrac{x}{2}$, the third keeps $\dfrac{y}{2}$ and receives $\dfrac{x}{2}$). Read cyclically, this is again ``two equal numbers followed by a different one'' (now starting from the third student). So the state always consists of a value $X_t$ held by two students and a value $Y_t$ held by one, with
\[
X_{t+1}=\dfrac{X_t+Y_t}{2},\qquad Y_{t+1}=X_t,
\]
as long as all values are even. At the start $X_0=0$, $Y_0=N$.

\textbf{Claim.} For $0\le t\le2017$,
\[
X_t=2^{2017-t}\cdot\dfrac{2^t-(-1)^t}{3},\qquad Y_t=2^{2017-t}\cdot\dfrac{2^t+2(-1)^t}{3}.
\]
This holds for $t=0$ ($X_0=0$, $Y_0=N$). If it holds for $t<2017$, then all values are even (they contain the factor $2^{2017-t}$ and the fractions are integers), so Step 1 does nothing and
\[
X_{t+1}=2^{2017-t-1}\cdot\dfrac{2^{t+1}+(-1)^t}{3}=2^{2016-t}\cdot\dfrac{2^{t+1}-(-1)^{t+1}}{3},\qquad Y_{t+1}=X_t=2^{2016-t}\cdot\dfrac{2^{t+1}+2(-1)^{t+1}}{3},
\]
as claimed. Also $X_t-Y_t=-2^{2017-t}(-1)^t\ne0$, so the procedure does not stop before round $2017$.

After round $2017$ (an odd number), two students have $X=\dfrac{2^{2017}+1}{3}$, which is odd, and one has $Y=\dfrac{2^{2017}-2}{3}$, which is even. In the next round, Step 1 makes the two odd students discard one candy each, and all three have $\dfrac{2^{2017}-2}{3}$; Step 2 then changes nothing (each gives away half and receives half of an equal amount). Now the numbers are equal and the procedure ends with $\dfrac{2^{2017}-2}{3}$ candies each.
