Three students $A$, $B$, $C$ sit around a round table in that clockwise order. They share candies by repeating the following two steps:
\begin{itemize}
\item Step 1: each student with an odd number of candies discards one candy; a student with an even number does nothing.
\item Step 2: each student passes half of the candies he has after Step 1 to the next student clockwise.
\end{itemize}
The two steps are repeated until the three students have the same number of candies, and then the procedure ends. At the start $A$ has $2^{2017}$ candies and $B$ and $C$ have none. How many candies does each student have when the procedure ends?
