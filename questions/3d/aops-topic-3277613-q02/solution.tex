After $n$ meals the success probability is $1-(\dfrac{99}{100})^n$. Thus $n\ge\dfrac{\log(\dfrac{1}{2})}{\log(\dfrac{99}{100})}=68.967\ldots$, so $n=69$. The cost is $69(4.45)=\boxed{\$307.05}$.
