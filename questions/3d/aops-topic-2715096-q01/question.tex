Trapezoid $ABCD$ has $AB\parallel CD$ and $\angle B=\angle C=90^\circ$. Point $E$ on $BC$ has $AB=CE=a$, $BE=CD=b$ and $AE=DE=c$. Show that $\angle AED=90^\circ$, and use the trapezoid's area to prove $a^2+b^2=c^2$.
