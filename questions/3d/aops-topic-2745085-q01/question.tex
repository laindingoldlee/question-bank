A positive integer is squarefree if it is not divisible by the square of any prime. Find the sum of the reciprocals of the squares of all squarefree positive integers, given that $\displaystyle\sum_{n\ge1}\dfrac1{n^2}=\dfrac{\pi^2}{6}$ and $\displaystyle\sum_{n\ge1}\dfrac1{n^4}=\dfrac{\pi^4}{90}$.
