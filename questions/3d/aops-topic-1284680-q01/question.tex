A fly sits at the centre of a clock face on its hour hand at 12:00 noon. For the next $14$ hours it walks along the hour hand out to the tip, for the following $14$ hours it walks back to the centre, and it repeats this forever. When is the first time after noon that the fly is back at the centre while the hour hand points exactly at $4$?
