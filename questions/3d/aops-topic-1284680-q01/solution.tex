The fly is at the centre exactly at times $28k$ hours after noon, $k=0,1,2,\dots$. The hour hand points exactly at $4$ at $4$ o'clock, that is, at times $t$ hours after noon with $t\equiv4\pmod{12}$. So we need
\[
28k\equiv4\pmod{12}\ \Longleftrightarrow\ 4k\equiv4\pmod{12}\ \Longleftrightarrow\ k\equiv1\pmod3 .
\]
The least positive $k$ is $k=1$, i.e.\ $28$ hours after noon. That is $\boxed{4{:}00\text{ p.m. the next day}}$ (noon plus $24$ hours is noon the next day, plus $4$ hours).
