All four exponents are multiples of $8$, so compare the eighth roots:
\[\left(17^{16}\right)^{\dfrac{1}{8}}=17^2=289,\quad\left(3^{48}\right)^{\dfrac{1}{8}}=3^6=729,\quad\left(2^{72}\right)^{\dfrac{1}{8}}=2^9=512,\quad\left(7^{24}\right)^{\dfrac{1}{8}}=7^3=343 .\]
Since $t\mapsto t^8$ is increasing for $t>0$, the order of the four numbers is the order of these values, and the largest is $\boxed{3^{48}}$.
