Let the numbers be $x$ and $x+3$. Then
\[
x^2+(x+3)^2=117\iff2x^2+6x-108=0\iff x^2+3x-54=0\iff(x+9)(x-6)=0 .
\]
Since $x>0$, $x=6$, and the larger number is $\boxed{9}$. (Check: $36+81=117$.)
