In many countries the binomial coefficient $\dbinom nk$ (the number of ways to choose $k$ objects from $n$) is written $C_n^k$, with the size $n$ of the whole set as the lower index. For a positive integer $n$, evaluate $\dbinom{1}{2n}$ and $C_{2n}^{1}$, and explain why they are different.
