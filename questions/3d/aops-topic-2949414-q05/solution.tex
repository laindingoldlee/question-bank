Order the counts $a<b<c<d$. If $a=0$, the pairs $(b,c)$ are $(1,2),(1,3),(1,4),(1,5),(2,3),(2,4),(2,5),(3,4)$, with $d=13-b-c$, giving 8. If $a=1$, the remaining possibilities are $(b,c,d)=(2,3,7),(2,4,6),(3,4,5)$, giving 3. If $a\ge2$, the sum is at least $2+3+4+5=14$, impossible. Total $\boxed{11}$.
