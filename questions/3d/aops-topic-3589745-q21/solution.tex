We have $(a-b)^2=2ab$ and $(a+b)^2=6ab$. Hence the square of the required ratio is $\dfrac{1}{3}$. Both $a-b$ and $a+b$ are negative, so the ratio is positive: $\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3}}$.
