The quotient must have four digits, so it is between $1000$ and $1111$ and starts with $1$. Thus $N$ ends with $1$. Its quotient's last digit $c$ satisfies $9c\equiv1\pmod{10}$, giving $c=9$. Write the quotient as $1009+100a+10b$, with $a,b$ digits. Its reverse is $9001+100b+10a$. Equating this with nine times the quotient gives $80+890a-10b=0$, so $b=8+89a$. As $0\le b\le9$, this forces $a=0,b=8$. The quotient is $1089$, and $N=\boxed{9801}$. Indeed $9\cdot1089=9801$.
