A picometer is a trillionth of a meter, and the diameter of a hydrogen atom is $240$ picometers. Annabelle's height increased by $1$ inch during a non-leap year, and $1$ inch equals $2.54$ centimeters. On average, by how many hydrogen-atom diameters did Annabelle's height increase each second? Express your answer as a decimal to the nearest tenth. (A calculator may be used.)
