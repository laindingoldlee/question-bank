The total is $645$, so each should have $\dfrac{645}{3}=215$. Kyle needs $15$ more, Jacob already has $215$, and Chris has $15$ too many. So $\boxed{\text{Chris gives }15\text{ pieces to Kyle}}$; Jacob gives and gets nothing. At least $15$ pieces must change hands, since Kyle must receive $15$.
