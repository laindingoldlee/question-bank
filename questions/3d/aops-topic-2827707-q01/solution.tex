Work backwards. Undoing a round: the winner halves, and each loser adds back the winner's gain.

Before round $4$ (winner $A$): $A=40$, the others $80+40=120$.

Before round $3$ (winner $B$, who had $120$): $B=60$, $A=40+60=100$, $C=120+60=180$.

Before round $2$ (winner $C$, who had $180$): $C=90$, $A=190$, $B=150$.

So after round $1$ the scores were $190,150,90$, and the highest was $\boxed{190}$. (Since all final scores are equal, the labelling of the winners does not matter.)
