Peter pours $4$ ounces of water into one bottle and $4$ ounces of honey into a second bottle; each bottle holds $8$ ounces. He pours half of the contents of the first bottle into the second and mixes it well. Then he pours half of the contents of the second bottle back into the first. What fraction of the contents of the first bottle is now honey?
