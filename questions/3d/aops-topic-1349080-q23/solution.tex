After the first pour, the first bottle has $2$ oz of water and the second has $2$ oz of water and $4$ oz of honey ($6$ oz). Half of the well-mixed second bottle is $3$ oz: $1$ oz of water and $2$ oz of honey. The first bottle then holds $3$ oz of water and $2$ oz of honey, so the fraction that is honey is $\boxed{\dfrac{2}{5}}$.
