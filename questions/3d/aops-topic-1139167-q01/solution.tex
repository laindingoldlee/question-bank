\textbf{Xentos.} There are only two white hats. If Xentos saw two white hats on Appino and Tenedos, he would know his own hat is grey. Since he does not know, Appino and Tenedos are \emph{not both} white.

\textbf{Appino.} Appino heard this and sees Tenedos' hat. If Tenedos' hat were white, Appino would conclude that his own hat cannot also be white, so it is grey, and he would know. Since Appino does not know, Tenedos' hat is not white.

\textbf{Tenedos.} Tenedos follows the same reasoning, so he knows his hat is grey without seeing any hat.

Tenedos' hat is $\boxed{\text{grey}}$.
