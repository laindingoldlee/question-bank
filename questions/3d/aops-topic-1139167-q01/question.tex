Hermos seats three friends in a line, one behind the other: Xentos at the back sees both Appino and Tenedos, Appino in the middle sees only Tenedos, and Tenedos at the front sees no one. Hermos shows them five hats, three grey and two white, puts one hat on each man's head, and hides the other two. Each man knows all of this, and each reasons perfectly.

Xentos, asked the color of his own hat, says he does not know. Appino then also says he does not know. Tenedos then announces that he is sure of the color of his own hat. What color is Tenedos' hat, and how does he know?
