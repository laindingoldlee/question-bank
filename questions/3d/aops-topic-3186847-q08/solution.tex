If hexagon side length is s, triangle side length is 2s. The hexagon consists of six equilateral triangles of side s, and the larger triangle has the area of four such triangles. The ratio is $\dfrac{6}{4}=\dfrac{3}{2}$, so $a+b=\boxed 5$.
\begin{asy}
import geometry; size(220);
for(int i=0;i<6;++i){draw(dir(60*i)--dir(60*(i+1)));draw((0,0)--dir(60*i),dashed);}draw((3,0)--(5,0)--(4,sqrt(3))--cycle);label("$s$",(0.75,0.45),E);label("$2s$",(4,0),S);
\end{asy}
