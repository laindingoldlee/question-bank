The odd-position and even-position digit sums each lie between $5$ and $15$. Their difference must be a multiple of $11$, hence zero. The total digit sum is even and a multiple of $9$ between $10$ and $30$, so it is $18$. Each parity group has sum $9$. Subtracting $1$ from its five digits gives entries in $\{0,1,2\}$ summing to $4$. Stars and bars with exclusion of entries at least $3$ gives $\binom84-5\binom54=45$ choices. The two groups are independent, giving $\boxed{2025}$.
