A positive integer $A$ has $23$ million digits and its first digit is $5$; a positive integer $B$ has $24$ million digits and its first digit is $9$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item How many digits does $AB$ have?
\item What can its first digit be?
\item Is its second digit determined?
\end{enumerate}
