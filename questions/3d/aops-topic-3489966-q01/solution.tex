(a) Treat the girls as one block. Arrange eight objects and then the three girls: $8!3!=\boxed{241920}$. (b) Arrange the boys in $7!$ ways. There are six internal gaps between them; only these gaps keep boys at both ends. Choose three gaps and assign the girls in $3!$ ways: $7!\binom63 3!=\boxed{604800}$.
