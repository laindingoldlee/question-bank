A remainder of $9$ on division by $n$ requires $n>9$ and $n\mid 2009-9=2000$. Since $2000=2^4\cdot5^3$ has $(4+1)(3+1)=20$ positive divisors, and those not exceeding $9$ are $1,2,4,5,8$, the number of valid $n$ is $20-5=\boxed{15}$.
