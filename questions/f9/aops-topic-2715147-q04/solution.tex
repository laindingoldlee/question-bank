$h(t)=-(t-8)^2+1000$, so the maximum ($1000$ ft) is at $t=\boxed{8}$ s. It reaches the ground when $(t-8)^2=1000$, i.e. $t=8+10\sqrt{10}\approx\boxed{39.6}$ s (the other root is negative).
