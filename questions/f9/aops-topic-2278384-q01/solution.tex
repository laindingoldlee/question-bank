By the definition,
\[3^{2i}=e^{2i\ln3}=\cos(2\ln3)+i\sin(2\ln3).\]
Since $2\ln3=\ln9\approx2.197$ radians, this is
\[3^{2i}=\boxed{\cos(\ln9)+i\sin(\ln9)}\approx-0.586+0.810i,\]
and $\left|3^{2i}\right|=\sqrt{\cos^2(\ln9)+\sin^2(\ln9)}=\boxed{1}$: raising a positive number to a purely imaginary power gives a point on the unit circle.
