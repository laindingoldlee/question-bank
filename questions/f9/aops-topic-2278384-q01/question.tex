For a positive real number $a$ and a complex number $z$, define $a^z=e^{z\ln a}$, where $\ln a$ is the real natural logarithm and $e^{i\theta}=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta$. Write $3^{2i}$ in the form $p+qi$ with real $p,q$, and find $\left|3^{2i}\right|$.
