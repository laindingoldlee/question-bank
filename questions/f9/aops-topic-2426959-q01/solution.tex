Place $D$ first: $2$ ways (place $3$ or $4$). Then $C$ goes into one of the places $2,3,4$ not taken by $D$: $2$ ways. Finally $A$ and $B$ fill the remaining two places in $2$ ways. The total is $2\cdot2\cdot2=\boxed{8}$. Listing them: with $D$ third, $ACDB$, $BCDA$, $ABDC$, $BADC$; with $D$ fourth, $ACBD$, $BCAD$, $ABCD$, $BACD$.
