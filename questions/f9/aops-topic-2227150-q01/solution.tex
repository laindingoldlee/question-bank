Multiplying out, $2(a+1)(b+1)=3ab$, i.e. $ab-2a-2b-2=0$, which factors as
\[(a-2)(b-2)=6 .\]
If $a=1$, then $1+\dfrac{1}{a}=2>\dfrac{3}{2}$, impossible; so $a\ge2$, and $a=2$ gives $0=6$. Hence $a-2\ge1$ and $b-2>a-2$, so $(a-2,b-2)\in\{(1,6),(2,3)\}$, i.e. $(a,b)=(3,8)$ or $(4,5)$. (Check: $\dfrac{4}{3}\cdot\dfrac{9}{8}=\dfrac{3}{2}$ and $\dfrac{5}{4}\cdot\dfrac{6}{5}=\dfrac{3}{2}$.) The sum of the possible values of $a$ is $3+4=\boxed{7}$.
