By the binomial theorem, $(x+2)^8=\sum_{k=0}^{8}\dbinom8k x^k2^{8-k}$. The $x^5$ term has $k=5$:
\[
\dbinom85x^5\cdot2^3=56\cdot8\,x^5=448x^5 .
\]
The coefficient is $\boxed{448}$.
