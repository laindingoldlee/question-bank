First convert to base ten:
\[
1337_9=1\cdot9^3+3\cdot9^2+3\cdot9+7=729+243+27+7=1006 .
\]
Now $1006=3\cdot256+238$ and $238=14\cdot16+14$. With the digit $E$ for fourteen,
\[
1006=3\cdot16^2+14\cdot16+14=3EE_{16},
\]
answer \textbf{(A)}. (Choice (B), $1006$, is the base-ten value, not the base-sixteen numeral.)
