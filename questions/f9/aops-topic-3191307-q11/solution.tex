Two groups contain one woman and two men; the third contains two women and one man. Choose the pair of women in $\binom42=6$ ways and its man in $5$ ways. The remaining two distinct women identify the other groups; choose the two men for one of them in $\binom42=6$ ways, and the final two men are forced. Thus there are $6\cdot5\cdot6=\boxed{180}$ partitions.
