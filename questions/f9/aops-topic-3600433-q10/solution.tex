Write the digits as $a,b,c,d$, with $1\le a\le 9$ and $0\le b,c,d\le 9$. Let $s=b+d$. Since $1\le a+c\le 18$ and $a+c=s^2$, only $s=1,2,3,4$ are possible. The numbers of pairs $(b,d)$ are $2,3,4,5$ respectively. The numbers of pairs $(a,c)$ with sums $1,4,9,16$ are $1,4,9,3$ respectively. Thus the total is $2\cdot 1+3\cdot 4+4\cdot 9+5\cdot 3=\boxed{65}$.
