Let $f(x)=\sqrt[3]{8x^3-7x^2-4x-2}$. As $x\to\infty$, the graph of $f$ approaches the line $y=ax+b$, in the sense that $f(x)-(ax+b)\to0$. What is $a+b$? Express your answer as a common fraction.
