Clearing denominators, $9y+9x=4xy$, so $16xy-36x-36y=0$ and
\[(4x-9)(4y-9)=81.\]
Since $x,y\ge1$, both factors are at least $-5$. If both were negative, their product would be at most $5\cdot5=25<81$; so both are positive divisors of $81$, that is, among $1,3,9,27,81$. Also $4x-9\equiv3\pmod4$, and of these divisors only $3$ and $27$ are $\equiv3\pmod 4$. So $(4x-9,4y-9)=(3,27)$ or $(27,3)$, giving $(x,y)=(3,9)$ or $(9,3)$. Check: $\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{1}{9}=\dfrac{4}{9}$.

Hence the only possible value is $x+y=\boxed{12}$. The list of possible values is just $\{12\}$: its median and mean are $12$ and its range is $0$, so the requested sum is $12+12+0=\boxed{24}$.
