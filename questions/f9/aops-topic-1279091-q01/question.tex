A brick measuring $22$ cm by $10$ cm by $5$ cm is laid flat (on its $22$ cm by $10$ cm face) on the bottom of a container with a square base of side $25$ cm. Before the brick was added the container was $6\%$ full of water; with the brick in place, exactly half of the brick is under water. What is the capacity of the container in litres?
