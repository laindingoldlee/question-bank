The $n$th term of a sequence is $a_n=(-1)^{n+1}(3n+2)$. A student computes $a_1+a_2+\cdots+a_{100}$ by adding the odd-numbered terms, $5+11+\cdots+299=7600$, and the even-numbered terms, $8+14+\cdots+302$, which he evaluates as $\dfrac{400\cdot50}{2}=10000$, and then subtracts to get $-2400$. Find his error and the correct value of the sum.
