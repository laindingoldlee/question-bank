The even-numbered terms $8,14,\dots,302$ are $50$ terms with average $\dfrac{8+302}{2}=155$, so they add up to $50\cdot155=7750$, not $10000$ (the student used $400$ instead of $8+302=310$). The odd-numbered part is right: $50\cdot\dfrac{5+299}{2}=7600$.

So the sum is $7600-7750=\boxed{-150}$. Quicker: pair the terms, $a_{2k-1}+a_{2k}=(6k-1)-(6k+2)=-3$, and $50$ pairs give $-150$.
