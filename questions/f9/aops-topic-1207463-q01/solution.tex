The pairwise intersections are: $2x+y=8$ and $2x-5y=20$ give $(5,-2)$; $2x+y=8$ and $x+y=10$ give $(-2,12)$; $2x-5y=20$ and $x+y=10$ give $(10,0)$.

usepackage("amsmath");

draw((-3,14)--(6,-4),gray); draw((-3,-5.2)--(12,0.8),gray); draw((-3,13)--(12,-2),gray);
dot(P);dot(Q);dot(R); label("$(5,-2)$",P,S); label("$(-2,12)$",Q,W); label("$(10,0)$",R,E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

By the shoelace formula the triangle has area
\[\dfrac{1}{2}\left|5(12-0)+(-2)(0-(-2))+10(-2-12)\right|=\dfrac{1}{2}|60-4-140|=\boxed{42}.\]
