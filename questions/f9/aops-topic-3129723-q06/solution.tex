Let $c_k$ count connected graphs on $k$ labelled vertices. We have $c_1=1,c_2=1,c_3=4$. Counting graphs by the component containing a specified vertex gives $c_4=2^6-\left(\binom30c_1\,2^3+\binom31c_2\,2^1+\binom32c_3\right)=64-(8+6+12)=38$. A graph with two components on five vertices has component sizes 1 and 4, or 2 and 3. These contribute $5c_4=190$ and $\binom52c_2c_3=40$, respectively. Therefore the answer is $\boxed{230}$.
