Water is $\dfrac{0.02}{0.06}=\dfrac{1}{3}$ of the solution. So $0.48$ liters of solution contain $\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot0.48=\boxed{0.16}$ liters of water (and $0.32$ liters of chemical A).
