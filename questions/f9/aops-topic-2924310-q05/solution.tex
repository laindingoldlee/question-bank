There are $9$ one-digit, $9$ two-digit, $90$ three-digit and $90$ four-digit palindromes: $198$ in all. Five-digit palindromes $\overline{abcba}$ are ordered by $\overline{abc}$ from $100$. The $1000$th overall is the $802$nd of these, with $\overline{abc}=100+801=901$: $\boxed{90109}$.
