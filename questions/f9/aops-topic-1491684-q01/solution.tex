The number ends in $40$, so it is divisible by $4$ and by $5$:
\[
100340=4\cdot25085=4\cdot5\cdot5017 .
\]
Trial division of $5017$ by the primes up to $\sqrt{5017}<71$: it is not divisible by $3$ (digit sum $13$), $7$, $11$, $13$, $17$, $19$, $23$, but $5017=29\cdot173$. And $173$ is prime, since it is not divisible by $2$, $3$, $5$, $7$, $11$ or $13$ (and $13^2<173<14^2$). So
\[
100340=\boxed{2^2\cdot5\cdot29\cdot173},
\]
and it has $(2+1)(1+1)(1+1)(1+1)=\boxed{24}$ positive divisors.
