The circumference is $1+2+3=6$, so the radius is $R=\dfrac{6}{2\pi}=\dfrac{3}{\pi}$, and the arcs subtend central angles proportional to their lengths:
\[
\dfrac{1}{6}\cdot360^\circ=60^\circ,\qquad\dfrac{2}{6}\cdot360^\circ=120^\circ,\qquad\dfrac{3}{6}\cdot360^\circ=180^\circ.
\]
Call the triangle $PQT$, with arc $PT=1$, $TQ=2$ and $QP=3$ (not containing $T$).

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real R=3/pi;
pair O=(0,0);
pair P=R*dir(90),Q=R*dir(-90),T=R*dir(30);
draw(circle(O,R),gray);
draw(arc(O,R,-90,30),red+linewidth(1.2));draw(arc(O,R,30,90),blue+linewidth(1.2));draw(arc(O,R,90,270),deepgreen+linewidth(1.2));
draw(P--Q--T--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
dot(O);label("$O$",O,W);
label("$1$",1.15*R*dir(60),blue);label("$2$",1.15*R*dir(-30),red);label("$3$",1.12*R*dir(180),deepgreen);
draw(O--T,dashed);
label("$P$",P,N);label("$Q$",Q,S);label("$T$",T,NE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The arc of length $3$ is a semicircle, so $PQ$ is a diameter and, by the inscribed angle theorem, $\angle PTQ=90^\circ$. The chord $PT$ subtends $60^\circ$ at $O$, so triangle $OPT$ is equilateral and $PT=R$; then $TQ=\sqrt{(2R)^2-R^2}=R\sqrt3$. The area is
\[
\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot R\cdot R\sqrt3=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\cdot\dfrac{9}{\pi^2}=\boxed{\dfrac{9\sqrt3}{2\pi^2}}.
\]
