$A$ is not the right angle (as $\sin A\ne1$), and $\sin A=\dfrac{12}{13}$ means the side opposite $A$ and the hypotenuse are in ratio $12:13$; by Pythagoras the sides are $5k,12k,13k$. For a right triangle the inradius is $\dfrac{\text{leg}+\text{leg}-\text{hypotenuse}}{2}=\dfrac{5k+12k-13k}{2}=2k$. So $2k=1$, and the hypotenuse is $13k=\boxed{\dfrac{13}{2}}$.
