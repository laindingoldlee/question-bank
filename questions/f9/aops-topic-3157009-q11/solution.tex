There are no one-digit examples and one two-digit example, $(17)_8=15 $. A three-digit example has 17 in the first two places, giving $(170)_8 $ through $(177)_8 $, eight choices; or in the last two places, giving $(117)_8,(217)_8,\ldots,(717)_8 $, seven choices. The largest of these is $(717)_8=463\le500 $, and the two types cannot overlap. Since $ 500<8^3 $, there are no longer representations. Total $\boxed{16}$.
