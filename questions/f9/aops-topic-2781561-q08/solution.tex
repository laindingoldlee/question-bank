Let the original counts be $ A,M $. Equality after exchange gives $\dfrac{2}{3}A+\dfrac{1}{4}M=\dfrac{1}{3}A+\dfrac{3}{4}M $, hence $ 2A=3M $. Together with $ A-M=18 $, this gives $ M=36,A=54 $. Their total is $\boxed{90}$.
