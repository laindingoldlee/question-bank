\textbf{All arrangements.} Fix Ben's seat to remove rotations; the other $5$ people can be arranged in the remaining seats in $5!=120$ ways.

\textbf{Arrangements with Zayn next to Anna.} Glue Zayn and Anna into one block. With Ben fixed, the block and the other three people fill the remaining places around the table in $4!=24$ ways, and the block can be ordered in $2$ ways (Zayn on the left or the right): $48$ arrangements.

\textbf{Answer.} By complementary counting there are $120-48=\boxed{72}$ arrangements.
