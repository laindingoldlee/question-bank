The eight entries sum to 16, so their mean is 2. In sorted order they are $0,0,1,2,3,3,3,4$; the median is $\dfrac{2+3}{2}=\dfrac{5}{2}$. The mode is 3, which occurs three times. The sum is $2+\dfrac{5}{2}+3=\boxed{\dfrac{15}{2}=7.5}$, choice C.
