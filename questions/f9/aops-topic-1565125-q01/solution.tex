An assortment is determined by the numbers $a,b,c\ge0$ of cookies of the three kinds, with $a+b+c=12$. By stars and bars ($12$ stars and $2$ bars in a row of $14$ symbols), the number of solutions is
\[
\dbinom{14}{2}=\boxed{91}.
\]
