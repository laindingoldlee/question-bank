Both triangles have sides 5, 12, 13 and inradius $\dfrac{5+12-13}{2}=2 $. With rectangle corners $(0,0),(12,0),(12,5),(0,5)$ and diagonal from $(0,5)$ to $(12,0)$, the incentres are $(2,2)$ and $(10,3)$. Their distance is $\sqrt{8^2+1^2}=\boxed{\sqrt{65}}$.

usepackage("amsmath");

\end{asy}
