The $k$th term ($k=0,1,\dots,502$) is $(-1)^kf(k)$ with $f(k)=(4k+1)(4k+3)=16k^2+16k+3$. Then
\[
f(k+1)-f(k)=16(2k+1)+16=32k+32 .
\]
Pair the terms $k=2j$ and $k=2j+1$ for $j=0,1,\dots,250$; this uses $k=0$ to $501$ and leaves the last term $k=502$, which is $+2009\cdot2011$. Each pair gives
\[
f(2j)-f(2j+1)=-(64j+32).
\]
Hence the sum is
\[
-\sum_{j=0}^{250}(64j+32)+2009\cdot2011=-\left(64\cdot\dfrac{250\cdot251}{2}+32\cdot251\right)+4040099=-2016032+4040099=\boxed{2024067}.
\]
