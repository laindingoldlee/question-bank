Arrange the digits $2,3,4,5,6$ and $7$ in a $2$ by $3$ array, one digit per cell, so that the product of the two $3$-digit numbers formed by the rows (read left to right) equals the product of the three $2$-digit numbers formed by the columns (read top to bottom). What is the sum of the two $3$-digit numbers in the rows?
