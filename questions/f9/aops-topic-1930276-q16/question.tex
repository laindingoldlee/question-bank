A $10\times10\times10$ cube is made of $1000$ unit cubes. A grasshopper sits at the centre $O$ of one of the corner unit cubes. In one jump it can move to the centre of any unit cube that shares a face with the cube it is in, provided the jump increases its distance from $O$. In how many ways can the grasshopper reach the unit cube at the opposite corner?
