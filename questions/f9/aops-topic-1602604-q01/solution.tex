Let $a=BC$, $b=AC$, $c=AB$.

\textbf{The hypotenuse.} In triangle $AMC$, $O$ and $N$ are the midpoints of $AM$ and $AC$, so $ON$ is a midline: $ON=\dfrac{1}{2}MC$. In a right triangle the median to the hypotenuse is half the hypotenuse, so $MC=\dfrac {c}{2}$. Hence $c=4\cdot ON=50$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

pair C=(0,0),A=(48,0),B=(0,14);
pair M=(A+B)/2,Np=(A+C)/2,O=(A+M)/2;
fill(M--Np--C--B--cycle,paleyellow);

draw(O--Np,red+linewidth(1));
real q=1.4;draw((q,0)--(q,q)--(0,q));
dot(M);dot(Np);dot(O);
label("$C$",C,SW);label("$A$",A,SE);label("$B$",B,NW);label("$M$",M,N);label("$N$",Np,S);label("$O$",O,NE);

\textbf{The legs.} Then $a+b=112-50=62$ and $a^2+b^2=50^2=2500$, so $2ab=62^2-2500=1344$ and $ab=672$. (The legs are $14$ and $48$.) The area of triangle $ABC$ is $\dfrac{ab}{2}=336$.

\textbf{The quadrilateral.} $MN$ is a midline of triangle $ABC$, so triangle $AMN$ is similar to triangle $ABC$ with ratio $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and has area $\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot336=84$. Therefore
\[
[MNCB]=336-84=\boxed{252}.
\]
