A student claims that for every $x$,
\[
\dfrac{1}{x}+\dfrac{1}{x^2}+\dfrac{1}{x^3}+\cdots=\dfrac{1}{x+1}.
\]
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show by a counterexample that the claim is false.
\item[(b)] For which real $x$ does the series $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{x^n}$ converge, and what is its sum?
\item[(c)] Find a similar series whose sum really is $\dfrac{1}{x+1}$ (for suitable $x$).
\end{enumerate}
