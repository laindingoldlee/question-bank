Every summand must be $1$, $11$, or $111$, since $1111>1000$. Let their counts be $c,b,a_0$, respectively. Then
\[
111a_0+11b+c=1000,\qquad
n=3a_0+2b+c=1000-9(12a_0+b).
\]
Here $0\le a_0\le9$ and $0\le b\le\lfloor\dfrac{1000-111a_0}{11}\rfloor$. Thus the possible integers $t=12a_0+b$ lie in these intervals:
\[
[0,90],[12,92],[24,94],[36,96],[48,98],
[60,100],[72,102],[84,104],[96,106],\{108\}.
\]
Their union is $\{0,1,\ldots,106,108\}$, containing $108$ integers. Each gives a distinct positive length and a valid nonnegative value of $c$. Therefore $a=108$ and the requested digit sum is $\boxed9$.
