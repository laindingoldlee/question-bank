Since $\gcd(5,6)=1$, integer sides in the ratio $5:6$ are $AC=5t$ and $AB=6t$ for a positive integer $t$. Let $\angle A=30^\circ$ and drop the altitude $CH$ to $AB$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s=0.2){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
pair A=(0,0), B=(12,0), C=10*dir(30), H=(C.x,0);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); draw(C--H,red+dashed); draw(ra(H,(1,0),(0,1),0.6),red);
draw(arc(A,1.6,0,30)); label("$30^\circ$",A+2.3*dir(15),fontsize(8pt));
dot(H);
label("$A$",A,SW); label("$B$",B,SE); label("$C$",C,N); label("$H$",H,S);
label("$5t$",(A+C)/2,NW);
draw((0,-1.1)--(12,-1.1),Arrows(5)); draw((0,-0.4)--(0,-1.5),gray); draw((12,-0.4)--(12,-1.5),gray);
label("$6t$",(6,-1.1),S);
label("$\dfrac{5t}{2}$",(C+H)/2,E,red+fontsize(8pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Triangle $AHC$ is a $30$-$60$-$90$ triangle, so the leg opposite the $30^\circ$ angle is half the hypotenuse: $CH=\dfrac{5t}{2}$. (Without memorising this: reflecting $AHC$ in $AH$ gives an equilateral triangle of side $5t$, and $CH$ is half of one of its sides.) Hence
\[
[ABC]=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot6t\cdot\dfrac{5t}{2}=\dfrac{15t^2}{2}.
\]
This is an integer exactly when $t$ is even. The least even $t$ is $2$ (sides $10$ and $12$), giving the minimum integer area $\dfrac{15\cdot4}{2}=\boxed{30}$.
