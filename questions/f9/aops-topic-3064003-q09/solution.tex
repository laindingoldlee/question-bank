Choose the sole first-place runner in 4 ways. If Robert's group has size 2, choose his companion in 3 ways; the last two runners can tie or finish in either order, giving 3 possibilities. If his group has size 3, choose its other two members in $\binom32=3$ ways and the last runner is determined. If his group has size 4, there is 1 possibility. Therefore the total is $4(3\cdot3+3+1)=\boxed{52}$.
