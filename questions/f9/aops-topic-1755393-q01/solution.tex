Together they do $\dfrac{1}{30}$ of the work per day, so in $18$ days they do $\dfrac{18}{30}=\dfrac{3}{5}$ of it. The remaining $\dfrac{2}{5}$ takes $B$ alone $20$ days, so $B$ does $\dfrac{2}{5\cdot20}=\dfrac{1}{50}$ of the work per day. Then $A$ does $\dfrac{1}{30}-\dfrac{1}{50}=\dfrac{5-3}{150}=\dfrac{1}{75}$ per day. Alone, $\boxed{A\text{ needs }75\text{ days and }B\text{ needs }50\text{ days}}$.
