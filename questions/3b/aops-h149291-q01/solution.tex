At $(u,\dfrac{u^2}{4a})$ the tangent is $y=\dfrac{u}{2a}x-\dfrac{u^2}{4a}$. It passes through $P$ exactly when
\[
\boxed{u^2-2x_0u+4ay_0=0}.
\]
The discriminant is $4(x_0^2-4ay_0)>0$, so there are two distinct contacts. At either contact, $u^2=4ay$; substituting in the quadratic gives
\[
\boxed{x_0x=2a(y+y_0)}.
\]
Thus this line contains both contact points and is their chord.
