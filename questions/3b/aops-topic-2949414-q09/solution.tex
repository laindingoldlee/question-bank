The common gcd of all three is $\gcd(21,10,11)=1$. For a prime with exponents $u\le v\le w$ across the three numbers, the exponent in the product of pairwise gcds is $2u+v$, while in $\dfrac{abc}{\operatorname{lcm}(a,b,c)}$ it is $u+v$. Dividing the pairwise-gcd product by the common gcd therefore gives the desired ratio. Here it is $21\cdot10\cdot11=\boxed{2310}$.
