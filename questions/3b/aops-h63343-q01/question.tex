A $6$-by-$7$ rectangle is divided by one segment into a triangle $U_2$ and a trapezoid $V_2$, with $U_1\sim U_2$ and $V_1\sim V_2$. Find the least area of $U_1$ as a reduced fraction $\dfrac{m}{n}$, and report $m+n$.
