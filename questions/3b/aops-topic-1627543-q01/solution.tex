The annual income is
\[
1100\cdot\dfrac{r}{100}+900\cdot\dfrac{r+1}{100}=11r+9r+9=20r+9 .
\]
Setting $20r+9=99$ gives $r=\boxed{4.5}$. (Check: $4.5\%$ of $\$1100$ is $\$49.50$ and $5.5\%$ of $\$900$ is $\$49.50$, total $\$99$.)
