There are $\binom{64}{2}=2016$ unordered square pairs. (a) Every knight pair lies on opposite corners of a $2\times3$ or $3\times2$ rectangle. There are $7\cdot6$ of each orientation and two pairs per rectangle, giving 168 attacking pairs and probability $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{12}}$. (b) In each diagonal direction the lengths are $1,2,\ldots,7,8,7,\ldots,1$. The pair count in both directions is $2\left(2\sum_{k=1}^7\binom k2+\binom82\right)=2(112+28)=280$, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{5}{36}}$.
