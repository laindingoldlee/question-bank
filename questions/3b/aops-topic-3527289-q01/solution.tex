With $k$ copies of digit $2$, its value is $2\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}9^j=\dfrac{9^k-1}{4}$ and it has $2k-1$ digits. Since $221_3=25$, divisibility is equivalent to $9^k\equiv1\pmod{100}$. The residues for $k=1,\ldots,10$ are $9,81,29,61,49,41,69,21,89,1$; hence the order is ten. The valid $k$ are $10,20,30,\ldots$, and the fifth is $50$, giving $\boxed{99}$ digits.
