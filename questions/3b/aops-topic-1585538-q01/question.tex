Can a $66\times62$ rectangle be tiled by $1\times12$ rectangular tiles? A student argues: ``$66\cdot62=4092=12\cdot341$, so the area is a multiple of $12$, and hence it is possible.'' Find the error in this argument and decide the question with proof.
