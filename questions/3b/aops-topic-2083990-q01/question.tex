On Mars, one in five Martians is a compulsive liar who always lies, and the rest always tell the truth. It rains $30\%$ of the time. Three randomly chosen Martians, each independently a liar with probability $\dfrac{1}{5}$ (and independently of the weather), all tell astronaut Sue that it is raining. What is the probability that it is actually raining?
