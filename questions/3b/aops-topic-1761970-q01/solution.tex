The total is $12\cdot2=24$ cubic feet. One cubic yard is $3\cdot3\cdot3=27$ cubic feet, so the mulch is $\dfrac{24}{27}=\boxed{\dfrac{8}{9}}$ of a cubic yard. (Using $3$ instead of $27$ cubic feet per cubic yard is the usual mistake.)
