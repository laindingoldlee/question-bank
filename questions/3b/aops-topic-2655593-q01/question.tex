Determine the value of
\[\sqrt{\left(\sum_{h=1}^{3}h\right)\left(\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{i^3}+\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{(j+k)^3}\right)}.\]
You may use the fact that $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\dfrac1{n^2}=\dfrac{\pi^2}{6}$.
