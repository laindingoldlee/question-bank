The points lie on the circle with centre $C=(3,-4)$ and radius $5$; note that the origin $O$ is on this circle. For any point $P$ on the circle, the triangle inequality gives $OP\le OC+CP=5+5=10$, with equality exactly when $P$ is on ray $OC$ beyond $C$, i.e. $P=(6,-8)$, the point diametrically opposite $O$. This is a lattice point, so the maximum of $x^2+y^2=OP^2$ is $\boxed{100}$.

label("$O$",O,NW);label("$(3,-4)$",C,E,fontsize(8pt));label("$(6,-8)$",P,SE,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}
