The larger interval $\dfrac{3}{7}<\dfrac{\alpha}{\beta}<\dfrac{7}{16}$ contains the stated interval. The endpoints satisfy $7\cdot7-3\cdot16=1$. For any fraction strictly between them, the positive integers $7\alpha-3\beta$ and $7\beta-16\alpha$ satisfy
\[
\beta=16(7\alpha-3\beta)+7(7\beta-16\alpha)\ge16+7=23.
\]
The fraction $\dfrac{10}{23}$ lies in the required interval, since $16\cdot23=368<370=37\cdot10$ and $16\cdot10=160<161=7\cdot23$. Thus the minimum is $\boxed{23}$.
