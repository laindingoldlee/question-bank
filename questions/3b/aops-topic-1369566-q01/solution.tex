Choosing $x^a$, $x^b$, $x^c$ from the three factors gives $x^{a+b+c}$, so the coefficient of $x^9$ is the number of ordered triples $(a,b,c)$ with $1\le a,b,c\le6$ and $a+b+c=9$ (the number of ways three dice show a total of $9$).

Put $a'=a-1$ and so on: we need $a'+b'+c'=6$ with $0\le a',b',c'\le5$. Stars and bars gives $\dbinom{8}{2}=28$ solutions without the upper bound; the ones with some variable equal to $6$ are the $3$ triples $(6,0,0)$, $(0,6,0)$, $(0,0,6)$. So the coefficient is $28-3=\boxed{25}$.
