For $j=1,10$, the two favorable counts are 3 and 1, giving ratio 3. For $j=2,9$, they are 4 and 1, giving ratio 4. For every $j=3,4,\ldots,8$, they are 5 and 2, giving ratio $\dfrac{5}{2}$. Thus the minimum is $\boxed{\dfrac{5}{2}}$, attained by $\boxed{j=3,4,5,6,7,8}$.
