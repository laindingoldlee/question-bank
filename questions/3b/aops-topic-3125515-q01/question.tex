Mark chooses an integer uniformly from 1 through 10. John chooses a fixed integer $j$ in the same range. Let $A$ be the event that Mark's number is within 2 of $j$, and $B$ the event that its distance from $j$ is exactly 2. Find all $j$ minimizing $\dfrac{\Pr(A)}{\Pr(B)}$.
