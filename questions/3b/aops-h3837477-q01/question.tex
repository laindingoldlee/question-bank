Let $F_1=F_2=1$ and $F_{n+2}=F_{n+1}+F_n$. Put $\varphi=\dfrac{1+\sqrt5}{2}$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Disprove $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{F_n}=4-\varphi$.
\item Prove that this ordinary reciprocal series converges and its sum lies strictly between $3$ and $4$.
\item Prove the corrected sparse-series identity $\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{F_{2^k}}=4-\varphi$.
\end{enumerate}
