The first three terms of a sequence are $2,4,16$. The next term is not determined by these three terms alone.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the fourth term if each term after the first is the square of the previous term.
\item[(b)] Find the fourth term if each term after the first is $2$ raised to the power of the previous term.
\item[(c)] Find the quadratic polynomial $p$ with $p(1)=2$, $p(2)=4$, $p(3)=16$, and compute $p(4)$.
\item[(d)] Prove that for every real number $c$ there is a polynomial $q$ of degree at most $3$ with $q(1)=2$, $q(2)=4$, $q(3)=16$ and $q(4)=c$.
\end{enumerate}
