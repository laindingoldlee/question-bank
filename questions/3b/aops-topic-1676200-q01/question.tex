Suppose that $P_1P_2\ldots P_{325}$ is a regular $325$-sided polygon. Determine the number of incongruent triangles of the form $P_iP_jP_k$, where $i$, $j$ and $k$ are distinct integers between $1$ and $325$, inclusive.
