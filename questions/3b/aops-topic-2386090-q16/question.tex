For real $\theta$, $\operatorname{cis}\theta=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta$, and Euler's formula says $\operatorname{cis}\theta=e^{i\theta}$. Using $e^{i\theta}$ as the definition of $\operatorname{cis}\theta$ for complex $\theta$ as well, evaluate $\operatorname{cis} i$.
