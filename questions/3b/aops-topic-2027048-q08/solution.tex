Each number is odd with probability $\dfrac{3}{5}$ and even with probability $\dfrac{2}{5}$, independently. The product $xz$ is odd only when both $x$ and $z$ are odd, so
\[P(xz\text{ odd})=\dfrac{9}{25},\qquad P(xz\text{ even})=\dfrac{16}{25}.\]
The sum $xz+y$ is even exactly when $xz$ and $y$ have the same parity:
\[
P=\dfrac{16}{25}\cdot\dfrac{2}{5}+\dfrac{9}{25}\cdot\dfrac{3}{5}=\dfrac{32+27}{125}=\boxed{\dfrac{59}{125}}.
\]
