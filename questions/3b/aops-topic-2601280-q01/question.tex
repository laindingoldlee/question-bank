The region of the plane satisfying
\[x\ge\dfrac y2+7,\qquad y\ge\dfrac x2+7,\qquad x\le18\dfrac13,\qquad y\le18\dfrac13\]
has area $\dfrac mn$ for relatively prime positive integers $m$ and $n$. Find $m+n$.
