Adding gives $x^2+y^2-6x+8y+25=0$, or $(x-3)^2+(y+4)^2=0$. Both squares are nonnegative, so $(x,y)=(3,-4)$. Substitution gives $-9+48-15-28+4=0$ and $18-32-3-4+21=0$, so $\boxed{(3,-4)}$ is the unique solution.
