Four football teams play a championship in which each team plays every other team once. A win is worth $3$ points, a draw $1$ point to each team, and a loss $0$ points. The champion gets $9$ points, the runner-up $6$ points and the third-placed team $3$ points. How many points does the fourth-placed team get?
