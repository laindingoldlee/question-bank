Temporarily label repeated copies. A letter appearing $m$ times has $m$ forbidden positions for each of its $m$ copies. The number of ways to force $j$ disjoint forbidden assignments for this letter is $\binom mj^2j!$: choose the copies, choose the positions, and match them.
Thus, if $r_k$ is the coefficient of $z^k$ in
\[
R(z)=(1+z)^{4}(1+4z+2z^2)^{1},
\]
inclusion--exclusion gives $\sum_{k=0}^{6}(-1)^kr_k(6-k)!$ labeled derangements. Every distinct letter arrangement is counted 2 times, the product of the multiplicity factorials. Hence the answer is
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}\sum_{k=0}^{6}(-1)^k[z^k]R(z)\,(6-k)!=84}.
\]
This formula excludes matching letter types, not merely matching labels on identical copies.
