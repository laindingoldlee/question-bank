The triangle is right at $B$. Put $A=(0,0),B=(3,0),C=(3,4)$ and $X=(t,0)$ with $t\ge0$. The equation is $t^2=(t-3)^2+16$, so $6t=25$. Hence $t=\dfrac{25}{6}>3$, and $\boxed{XB=\dfrac{7}{6}}$. No point on segment $AB$ can work, since there $AX\le3$ but $XC\ge4$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(3,0),C=(3,4),X=(25/6,0);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(B--X--C,dashed);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,S);label("$C$",C,N);label("$X$",X,SE);label("$3$",(1.5,0),S);label("$4$",(3,2),W);label("$5$",(A+C)/2,NW);
\end{asy}
