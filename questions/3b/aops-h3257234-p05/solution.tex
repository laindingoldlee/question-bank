Every integer $k\le99$ has a multiple in the interval $102,\ldots,200$, which contains $99$ consecutive integers; $100$ divides $200$. Thus all integers through $100$ already divide the denominator. The only omitted integer not accounted for is the prime $101$, and none of $102,\ldots,200$ is divisible by it. Adjoining $101$ multiplies the least common multiple by $101$. The ratio is $\boxed{101}$.
