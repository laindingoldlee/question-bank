The unit-circle centres form an equilateral triangle of side 2. The small-circle centre is its centre, at distance $\dfrac{2}{\sqrt3}$ from each vertex. Hence the radius is $\dfrac{2}{\sqrt3}-1=\boxed{\dfrac{2\sqrt3-3}{3}}$. In the source form $\dfrac{a\sqrt b-\sqrt c}{d}$, the parameters are $(2,3,9,3)$, whose sum is 17.
