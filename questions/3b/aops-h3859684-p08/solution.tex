In every row and column, precisely one cell contains a factor $2$, and precisely one contains a factor $3$. The cells containing $2$ form a permutation matrix, as do those containing $3$, independently. Put $6$ at overlaps, $2$ or $3$ at the remaining selected cells, and $1$ elsewhere. This construction is reversible, and each row and column automatically has at most two nonunit entries. Hence the number is $(6!)^2=\boxed{518400}$.
