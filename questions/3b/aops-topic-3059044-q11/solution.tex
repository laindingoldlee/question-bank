Inductively $1\le u_j\le j+1$, since the comparison values satisfy $\sqrt{1+j(j+2)}=j+1$. Write $e_j=(j+1)-u_j$. Rationalizing gives $e_j=\dfrac{j e_{j+1}}{j+1+u_j}\le\dfrac{j}{j+2}e_{j+1}$. At the tail $e_{n+1}=n+1$. Therefore $0\le3-u_2\le(n+1)\prod_{j=2}^n\dfrac{j}{j+2}=\dfrac{6}{n+2}\to0$. Thus the explicitly defined nested radical converges to 3. An infinite radical representation does not imply irrationality; $3=\dfrac{3}{1}$ is rational.
