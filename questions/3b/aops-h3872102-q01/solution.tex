Adjacent entries must alternate parity. Let $O(n)$ and $E(n)$ count such lists of sum $n$ ending in an odd and an even number, respectively. A list ending in an odd $j$ is either the one-term list $[j]$, or an even-ending list of sum $n-j$ followed by $j$. Thus
\[
O(n)=[n\text{ odd}]+\sum_{\substack{1\le j<n\\j\text{ odd}}}E(n-j),
\quad
E(n)=[n\text{ even}]+\sum_{\substack{1\le j<n\\j\text{ even}}}O(n-j).
\]
The bracket denotes $1$ when its condition holds and $0$ otherwise. Starting from empty sums gives
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrrr}
n&1&2&3&4&5&6&7&8\\\hline
O(n)&1&0&2&1&3&4&5&10\\
E(n)&0&1&1&1&3&2&6&6
\end{array}
\]
Therefore the answer is $\boxed{O(8)+E(8)=16}$.
