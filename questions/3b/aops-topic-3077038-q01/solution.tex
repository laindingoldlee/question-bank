For side lengths $a,b,c$, the sum is $abc+2(ab+bc+ca)+4(a+b+c)$. Adding $8$ yields $(a+2)(b+2)(c+2)=6006=2\cdot3\cdot7\cdot11\cdot13$. Thus we partition five distinct prime factors into three unordered nonempty groups, each with product at least $3$. There are $\dfrac{3^5-3\cdot2^5+3}{6}=25$ partitions into three nonempty groups. The forbidden partitions have the prime $2$ alone; the remaining four primes split into two nonempty groups in $\dfrac{2^4-2}{2}=7$ ways. Hence there are $\boxed{18}$ prisms.
