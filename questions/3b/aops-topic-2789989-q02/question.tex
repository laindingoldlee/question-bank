The numbers $1$ to $100$ fill a $10\times10$ board row by row ($1,\dots,10$ in the first row, $11,\dots,20$ in the second, and so on). Ten rooks are placed with no two in the same row or column. What is the largest possible sum of the numbers under the rooks?
