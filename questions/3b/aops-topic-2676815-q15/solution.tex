The decade has $10\cdot365+3=3653$ days (2020, 2024 and 2028 are leap years), i.e. $521$ weeks and $6$ days. The $521$ full weeks contain $521$ Wednesdays, and the $6$ extra days run from Wednesday to Monday and contain one more. The answer is $\boxed{522}$.
