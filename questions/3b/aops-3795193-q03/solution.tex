Certified bounds are $3.141592653<\pi<3.141592654$ and $2.718281828<e<2.718281829$. For example, the latter follows from $\sum_{j=0}^{13}\dfrac{1}{j!}$ and the tail bound $\sum_{j=14}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{j!}<\dfrac{15}{14\cdot14!}$. The former follows from Machin\textquotesingle s identity $\pi = 16 \arctan (\dfrac{1}{5}) - 4 \arctan (\dfrac{1}{239})$ with alternating-series remainder bounds, using eight and three terms respectively. Dividing these positive rational bounds and subtracting from $\dfrac{7}{6}$ gives, by cross-multiplication, $\dfrac{1}{92}<\dfrac{7}{6}-\dfrac{\pi}{e}<\dfrac{1}{91}$. In particular the difference is positive, so $\dfrac{\pi}{e}<\dfrac{7}{6}$.
