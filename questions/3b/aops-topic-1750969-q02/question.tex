Amy rolls a standard six-sided die and tosses a fair coin at the same time. If the coin shows heads, she multiplies the number on the die by $2$; otherwise she divides it by $2$. What is the expected value of the resulting number? Express your answer as a decimal rounded to the nearest thousandth.
