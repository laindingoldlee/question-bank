Use $A=(0,0),B=(12,0),C=(12,5),D=(0,5)$. Then $E=(12,\dfrac{5}{2})$ and $F=(6,5)$. Intersecting $BD:y=5-\dfrac{5}{12}x$ with $AE:y=\dfrac{5}{24}x$ and $AF:y=\dfrac{5}{6}x$ gives $M=(8,\dfrac{5}{3})$ and $N=(4,\dfrac{10}{3})$. Hence $MN=\sqrt{4^2+(\dfrac{5}{3})^2}=\boxed{\dfrac{13}{3}\text{ cm}}$.
