\textbf{Claim:} it is impossible. \textbf{Evidence:} Compared with a perfect score of 150, each blank loses 4.5 points and each incorrect answer loses 6. If $b,w$ are their counts, a score of 142.5 would require $4.5b+6w=7.5$, or $3b+4w=5$. \textbf{Reasoning:} If $w\ge2$, the left side exceeds 5. For $w=0$ or $w=1$, it requires $3b=5$ or $3b=1$, neither possible for an integer $b$. Hence no answer pattern gives that score.
