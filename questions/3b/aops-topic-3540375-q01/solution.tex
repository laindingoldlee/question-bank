With T explicitly defined, the first expression is $1+2+3+7+100=\boxed{113}$. Conventional ellipsis indicates all consecutive integers from 1 through 100, whose sum is $\dfrac{100\cdot 101}{2}=\boxed{5050}$. A defined number and a notation for omitted terms have different meanings.
