Two numbers have equal fractional parts exactly when their difference is an integer. Here the difference is $2x$, so $x$ is a half-integer. The floor condition is $9\le(x-3)^2-3<10$, or $12\le(x-3)^2<13$. The only half-integers with absolute value between $\sqrt{12}$ and $\sqrt{13}$ are $\pm\dfrac{7}{2}$. Hence $x=\dfrac{13}{2}$ or $-\dfrac{1}{2}$. Their squared sum is $\dfrac{169+1}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{85}{2}}$.
