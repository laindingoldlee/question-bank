Mr.\ Bash must decide whether to solve a problem by bashing or by thinking. If he bashes, the number of cases is equally likely to be $2$, $3$, $4$ or $5$, and each case takes a time uniformly distributed between $3$ and $6$ minutes, independently of the other cases and of the number of cases. If he thinks instead, it takes a time uniformly distributed between $5$ and $10$ minutes. On average, which method is faster, and by how many minutes?
