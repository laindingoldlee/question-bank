A uniform distribution on $[p,q]$ has mean $\dfrac{p+q}{2}$. So one case takes $\dfrac{3+6}{2}=4.5$ minutes on average, thinking takes $\dfrac{5+10}{2}=7.5$ minutes on average, and the number of cases $N$ has mean $\dfrac{2+3+4+5}{4}=3.5$.

Given $N=n$, the expected bashing time is $4.5n$ (linearity of expectation). Averaging over $n$:
\[E(\text{bashing time})=\dfrac14\left(4.5\cdot2+4.5\cdot3+4.5\cdot4+4.5\cdot5\right)=4.5\cdot3.5=15.75\text{ minutes}.\]
So bashing is \emph{not} faster: on average, thinking is faster by
\[15.75-7.5=\boxed{8.25\text{ minutes}}.\]
