Let $M$ students like maths and $S$ like science. The students who like both are counted in two ways:
\[\dfrac{1}{2}M=\dfrac{1}{3}S .\]
Hence $3M=2S$, and $M:S=\boxed{2:3}$. (For example, $20$ like maths, $30$ like science and $10$ like both.)
