We have $5^2=25\equiv-1\pmod{13}$, so $5^4\equiv1\pmod{13}$. Since $37=4\cdot9+1$, $5^{37}\equiv5$, and the remainder is $\boxed5$. Here the exponent is reduced modulo 4, the period of powers, not modulo 13. In fact $37\equiv11\pmod{13}$, not 1. As a counterexample to reducing modulo 13, $5^{13}\equiv5\not\equiv1=5^0\pmod{13}$.
