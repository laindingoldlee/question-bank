Victor's first four portions are $\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{1}{8},\dfrac{1}{32},\dfrac{1}{128}$. After eight halvings the remainder is $\dfrac{1}{256}$. His total is $\dfrac{128+32+8+2+1}{256}=\boxed{\dfrac{171}{256}}$.
