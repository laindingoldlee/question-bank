The radii are $4$ and $2$ feet, so the ring has area $\pi\cdot4^2-\pi\cdot2^2=16\pi-4\pi=\boxed{12\pi}$ square feet.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
filldraw(circle((0,0),4),mediumgray,linewidth(0.8));
filldraw(circle((0,0),2),white,linewidth(0.8));
dot((0,0));draw((0,0)--(4,0),dashed);draw((0,0)--2*dir(120),dashed);
label("$4$",(3,0),S,fontsize(9pt));label("$2$",dir(120),W,fontsize(9pt));
\end{asy}
