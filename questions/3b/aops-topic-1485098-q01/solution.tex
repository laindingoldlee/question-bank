Let the total distance be $2d$ km. The two halves take $\dfrac{d}{80}$ and $\dfrac{d}{120}$ hours, in total
\[
\dfrac{d}{80}+\dfrac{d}{120}=\dfrac{3d+2d}{240}=\dfrac{d}{48}\text{ hours}.
\]
So the average speed is
\[
\dfrac{2d}{\dfrac{d}{48}}=\boxed{96}\text{ km per hour}.
\]
This is the harmonic mean of $80$ and $120$, not their ordinary average $100$: he spends more time at the lower speed.
