Let $a$ and $b$ be positive integers with $\gcd(a,b)=d$. Prove that $\gcd\left(\dfrac {a}{d},\dfrac {b}{d}\right)=1$.

Is the following argument a proof? ``By the definition of gcd, $d$ is the most that $a$ and $b$ have in common. So $\dfrac {a}{d}$ and $\dfrac {b}{d}$ have all of the common stuff removed, and their gcd is $1$.
