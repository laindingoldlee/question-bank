$ITEST=I\cdot E\cdot S\cdot T^2=2006=2\cdot17\cdot59$ is squarefree, so $T^2$ divides it only if $T=1$. Then $IES=2006$ with $I,E,S$ distinct and different from $1$, so $\{I,E,S\}=\{2,17,59\}$ (a product of three integers greater than $1$ equal to a product of three primes uses exactly those primes). The sum is $2+17+59+1+1+2006=\boxed{2086}$, choice $\textbf{(A)}$; it is the only possible value, hence the largest.
