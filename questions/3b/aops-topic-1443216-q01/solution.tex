Let Richville have $R\ge1$ people and Poorville $P\ge1$ people. Their total wealths are $150000R$ and $20000P$, so the average in Mediumville is
\[
\dfrac{150000R+20000P}{R+P}=20000+\dfrac{130000R}{R+P}>20000,
\]
because $R\ge1$. So \textbf{no}: the combined average is strictly between the two averages. It can be made as close to $\$20000$ as we like by making Poorville much larger than Richville, but it never equals $\$20000$.
