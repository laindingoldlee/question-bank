The alternating sum is $H_{55}-H_{27}=\sum_{j=28}^{55}\dfrac{1}{j}$. Write $D=\prod_{j=28}^{55}j$ and numerator $N=\sum_{j=28}^{55}\dfrac{D}{j}$. The exponent of three in $D$ is $(18-9)+(6-3)+(2-1)=13$. Among denominators $28,\ldots,55$, only $54$ contains $3^3$; all others have at most $3^2$. Thus $\dfrac{D}{54}$ has exactly ten factors of three, while every other term of $N$ has at least eleven. Factoring $3^{10}$ leaves one term not divisible by three plus terms divisible by three, so $N$ has exactly ten factors of three. Therefore $\boxed{k=10}$.
