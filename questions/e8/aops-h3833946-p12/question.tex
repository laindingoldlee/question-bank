Jonathan and Nathan are playing a card game. Each turn, the player draws one card from a standard $52$-card deck plus $2$ jokers. If the player draws a King or Queen, they win immediately, but if they draw a Joker, they lose instantly. Jonathan goes first, then they alternate turns. The probability that Jonathan wins the game can be expressed as $\dfrac{a}{b}$, where $a$ and $b$ are relatively prime positive integers. What is $a+b$?
