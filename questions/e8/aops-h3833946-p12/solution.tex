Assume draws are without replacement. The game ends at the first of the ten decisive cards (eight Kings/Queens and two Jokers). If its position is odd, Jonathan wins with probability $\dfrac{4}{5}$; if even, he wins with probability $\dfrac{1}{5}$. The probability that the first decisive position is $t$ is $\dfrac{\binom{54-t}{9}}{\binom{54}{10}}$. Summing over odd $t=1,3,\ldots,45$ and weighting gives $\dfrac{2358082}{4446435}$. Thus $\boxed{a+b=6804517}$. The denominator counts all ten-position subsets of the shuffled deck, and the numerator fixes their smallest element.
