Each square pen has area four and side two. In a compact $2\times2$ layout, the outer fence is 16 metres and the two internal dividing fences total eight, so $\boxed{24}$ metres suffice. This is the minimum: of the 32 metres of separate pen perimeters, at most four full shared sides can be merged, saving eight metres. Other layouts can need more fencing.
