Write a point of the hyperbola as $X=(ct,\dfrac{c}{t})$, $t\ne0$. The diameter-circle criterion is $(X-P)\cdot(X-Q)=0$. After dividing by $c^2$, this becomes $(t-p)(t-q)\left(1+\dfrac{1}{t^2pq}\right)=0$. Since $pq<0$, the two additional parameters are $t=\pm\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{-pq}}$, giving $\boxed{(\dfrac{c}{\sqrt{-pq}},c\sqrt{-pq})}$ and its negative. They are distinct, and the two extra assumptions ensure neither parameter equals $p$ or $q$.

\end{asy}
