Let $s_n=x^n+y^n$. Since $x,y$ are roots of $t^2-5t+7=0$, the sums obey $s_n=5s_{n-1}-7s_{n-2}$. Starting with $s_0=2,s_1=5$, we get $s_2=11$, $s_3=20$, $s_4=23$, and $s_5=115-140=\boxed{-25}$. Complex numbers are necessary because the quadratic has discriminant $25-28=-3$.
