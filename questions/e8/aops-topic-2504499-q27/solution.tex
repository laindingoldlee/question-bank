$r=\dfrac{5b+7}{b^2-1}$ and $3r=1+\dfrac6{b^2-1}=\dfrac{b^2+5}{b^2-1}$. So $15b+21=b^2+5$, $(b-16)(b+1)=0$, $b=16$, and $r=\dfrac{87}{255}=\boxed{\dfrac{29}{85}}$.
