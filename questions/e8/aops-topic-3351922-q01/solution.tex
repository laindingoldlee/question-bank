Let the two cube roots be $a,b$, so $a^3+b^3=50$ and $ab=\sqrt[3]{625-500}=5$. For $s=a+b$, $s^3=50+15s$. Thus $(s-5)(s^2+5s+10)=0$. The quadratic has discriminant $-15$, so the only real root is $\boxed5$.
