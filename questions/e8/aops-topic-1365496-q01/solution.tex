Let the side of the square be $s$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(8cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=36/sqrt(10);
pair A=(0,0), B=(s,0), C=(s,s), D=(0,s);
pair F=(0,s/3), G=(s/4,s/4), Ep=(-2*s,s);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(A--C,gray); draw(D--Ep,gray+dashed);
draw(B--Ep,red);
dot(F);dot(G);dot(Ep);
label("$A$",A,SW); label("$B$",B,SE); label("$C$",C,NE); label("$D$",D,N); label("$E$",Ep,NW);
label("$F$",F,SW); label("$G$",G,SE);
label("$9$",(B+G)/2,NE,red); label("$3$",(G+F)/2,N,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Since $AF\parallel BC$, triangles $GAF$ and $GCB$ are similar (angles at $G$ are vertical and $\angle GAF=\angle GCB$). So
\[
\dfrac{AF}{CB}=\dfrac{GF}{GB}=\dfrac{3}{9}=\dfrac{1}{3},\qquad AF=\dfrac {s}{3},\quad FD=\dfrac{2s}{3}.
\]
Since $DE\parallel AB$, triangles $FDE$ and $FAB$ are similar, so
\[
\dfrac{FE}{FB}=\dfrac{FD}{FA}=\dfrac{\dfrac{2s}{3}}{\dfrac {s}{3}}=2 .
\]
With $FB=FG+GB=12$, we get $EF=\boxed{24}$.
