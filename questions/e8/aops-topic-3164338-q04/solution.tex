The ring ending at radius $k$ has area $\pi(2k-1)$ and score $11-k$. Even scores occur for odd $k=1,3,5,7,9$, with total area $(1+5+9+13+17)\pi=45\pi$. Thus a shot is even with probability $\dfrac{9}{20}$ and odd with $\dfrac{11}{20}$. A sum is even when both parities agree, giving $(\dfrac{9}{20})^2+(\dfrac{11}{20})^2=\boxed{\dfrac{101}{200}}$.
