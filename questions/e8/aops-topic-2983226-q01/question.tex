Circles $\omega_1$ and $\omega_2$ are both centred at $O$ with radii $6\sqrt3$ and $12$. Point $P$ lies on $\omega_2$ and point $Q$ on $\omega_1$ such that line $PQ$ is tangent to $\omega_1$ at $Q$. The bisector of $\angle OPQ$ meets $\omega_1$ at two points. If $x$ is the distance between them, find $x^2$.
