To be sure of a green, one must exhaust the non-greens: $r+b=16$. Similarly $b+g=17$. To be sure of all three colours, one must exhaust the two largest colour groups: their sum is $19$. Since $r+b$ and $b+g$ are $16$ and $17$, the two largest must be $r$ and $g$, with $r+g=19$. Adding, $2(r+b+g)=52$, so there are $\boxed{26}$ marbles ($r=9$, $b=7$, $g=10$).
