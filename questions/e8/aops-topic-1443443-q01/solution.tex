The one-digit numbers $1$--$9$ use $9$ digits and the two-digit numbers $10$--$99$ use $90\cdot2=180$ digits, $189$ in all. That leaves $1000-189=811$ digits among the three-digit numbers. Since $811=3\cdot270+1$, the numbers $100$ through $369$ use the next $810$ digits, and the $1000$th digit is the first digit of $370$, namely $\boxed{3}$.
