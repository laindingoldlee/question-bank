Each month the balance is multiplied by $r=1+\dfrac{0.058}{12}=1.004833\ldots$, so after $m$ months it is $3000r^m$. We need the least integer $m$ with
\[
3000r^m\ge60000\iff r^m\ge20\iff m\ge\dfrac{\ln20}{\ln r}.
\]
Now $\ln20=2.995732\ldots$ and $\ln r=0.0048217\ldots$, so $\dfrac{\ln20}{\ln r}=621.30\ldots$. Hence the investment is still below $\$60{,}000$ after $621$ months ($3000r^{621}\approx\$59{,}914$) and first reaches it after $\boxed{622}$ months ($3000r^{622}\approx\$60{,}204$), about $51$ years and $10$ months.
