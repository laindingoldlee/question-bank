If $k=1$ or $n-1$, then $n=6$, giving $(6,1),(6,5)$. If $2\le k\le n-2$, then $\binom nk\ge\binom n2$. For $n\ge5$ this is at least 10; for $n=4$, $k=2$ gives 6. No smaller $n$ works. Thus exactly $\boxed3$ pairs occur.
