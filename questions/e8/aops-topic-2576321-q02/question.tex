A deck has $n$ cards, each with a red side and a black side. The deck is shuffled so that some cards lie black side up and the others red side up. A blind man is told truthfully that exactly $k$ cards are black side up ($0\le k\le n$), but he is not told $n$, and he cannot see or feel which side of a card is up. Describe how he can split the deck into two piles containing the same number of black-side-up cards, and prove that your method works. (He may turn cards over.)
