Let the common length be $t$, and let the rectangle have width $L$ and height $H$. Use coordinates
\[
E=(0,0),\ F=(L,0),\ G=(L,H),\ H_0=(0,H).
\]
(The symbol $H_0$ denotes vertex $H$.) Then
\[
A=(t,0),\quad B=(L,t),\quad C=(L-t,H),\quad D=(0,H-t),
\]
with $0\le t\le\min(L,H)$. Since $AB=BC$,
\[
(L-t)^2+t^2=t^2+(H-t)^2.
\]
Both $L-t$ and $H-t$ are nonnegative, so $L-t=H-t$, hence $L=H$. The rectangle is therefore a square.

\end{asy}
