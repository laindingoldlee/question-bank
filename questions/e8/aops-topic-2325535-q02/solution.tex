Since $\dfrac5{98}=\dfrac1{10}\cdot\dfrac{25}{49}$ and long division gives $\dfrac{25}{49}=0.\overline{510204081632653061224489795918367346938775}$ (a block of $42$ digits),
\[\dfrac5{98}=0.0\overline{510204081632653061224489795918367346938775}.\]
In the block, the digit in position $i$ and the digit in position $i+21$ add up to $9$ for every $i=1,\dots,21$:
\[510204081632653061224+489795918367346938775=999999999999999999999.\]
So the block has digit sum $21\cdot9=189$. (The first half has sum $5+1+0+2+0+4+0+8+1+6+3+2+6+5+3+0+6+1+2+2+4=61$ and the second half $128$.)

The first digit is $0$, and the remaining $69419=42\cdot1652+35$ digits are $1652$ complete blocks followed by the first $35$ digits of the block. The last $7$ digits of the block are $6,9,3,8,7,7,5$, with sum $45$, so its first $35$ digits have sum $189-45=144$. The total is
\[0+1652\cdot189+144=312228+144=\boxed{312372}.\]
