There are $5$ different pairs of gloves, and the left and right gloves are distinguishable ($10$ different gloves in all). Four gloves are selected.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] In how many ways can the four gloves be chosen so that they include at least one complete pair?
\item[(b)] In how many ways can they be chosen so that they include exactly one complete pair?
\end{enumerate}
