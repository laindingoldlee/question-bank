The vertical strip has area $1\cdot3=3$ and the horizontal strip has area $4\cdot1=4$. Their overlap is a unit square, counted in both strips. Thus the union has area $3+4-1=6$. The rectangle has area $12$, so the probability is
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{6}{12}=\dfrac{1}{2}},\qquad \boxed{a+b=3}.
\]
