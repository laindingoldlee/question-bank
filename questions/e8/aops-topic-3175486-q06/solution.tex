If $a_n$ is the end-of-day count, $a_n=\dfrac{a_{n-1}+2}{2}$, so $a_n-2=\dfrac{1}{2}(a_{n-1}-2)$. Therefore $a_n=2+\dfrac{2048}{2^n}$. Setting this equal to six gives $2^n=512=2^9$. The first such day is $\boxed9$; the earlier counts are larger and integral.
