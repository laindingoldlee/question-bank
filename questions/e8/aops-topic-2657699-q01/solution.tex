If the integers are $a,a+1,\dots,a+n-1$ with $a\ge1$, their sum is $\dfrac{n(2a+n-1)}2=2010$, so
\[n(2a+n-1)=4020=2^2\cdot3\cdot5\cdot67.\]
Since $a\ge1$, the second factor exceeds $n$, so $n^2<4020$ and $n\le63$. The divisors of $4020$ up to $63$ are $1,2,3,4,5,6,10,12,15,20,30,60$. For $n=60$ the cofactor is $67$, and $2a+59=67$ gives $a=4$: indeed $4+5+\cdots+63=30\cdot67=2010$. So the largest value is $\boxed{60}$.
