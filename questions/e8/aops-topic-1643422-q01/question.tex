A fish tank in the shape of a rectangular prism holds $10$ gallons. A second tank has the same shape (it is similar to the first, with all dimensions multiplied by the same factor $k$) and holds $\dfrac{1}{4}$ as many gallons. Find $k$, and say how the side lengths of the new tank compare with those of the old one. Can the side lengths be found exactly from the given information alone?
