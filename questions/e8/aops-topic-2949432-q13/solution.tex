Let $u_n$ be the number of valid length-$n$ strings (first digit nonzero) ending in $0$ or $3$, and $v_n$ the number ending in $1$; by symmetry the same number end in $2$. Appending a digit gives
\[u_{n+1}=2(u_n+2v_n),\qquad v_{n+1}=u_n+v_n.\]
Starting from $(u_1,v_1)=(1,1)$: $(6,2),(20,8),(72,28),(256,100),(912,356)$. The total for $n=6$ is $u_6+2v_6=912+712=\boxed{1624}$.
