Label the points $P_1,P_2,\dots,P_n$. Count each chord at its endpoint with the smaller label: $P_1$ is the smaller endpoint of $n-1$ chords, $P_2$ of $n-2$ chords (to $P_3,\dots,P_n$), and so on, down to $P_{n-1}$, which is the smaller endpoint of $1$ chord. So the number of chords is
\[(n-1)+(n-2)+\cdots+2+1=\dbinom n2=\dfrac{n(n-1)}{2},\]
the sum of the first $\boxed{n-1}$ positive integers.
