Today I was muted in a chat room. On any day following a day on which I was muted, I am muted with probability $\dfrac12$; on any day following a day on which I was not muted, I am muted with probability $\dfrac15$. As $k$ grows, the probability that I am muted $k$ days from now converges to $\dfrac mn$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $m+n$.
