The relative angular speed is $6-\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{11}{2}$ degrees per minute. Opposition occurs at times $t=\dfrac{180+360k}{\dfrac{11}{2}}=\dfrac{360+720k}{11}$ minutes. Exactly $k=0,1,\ldots,21$ give $0\le t<1440$. The answer is $\boxed{22}$.
