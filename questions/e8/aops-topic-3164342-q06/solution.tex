For rolls $a,b$, the condition is $(a+1)(b+1)\equiv1\pmod3$. Thus either both $a,b$ are 0 modulo 3 or both are 1 modulo 3. The first die has two faces of each kind; the second has six multiples of 3 and seven numbers congruent to 1. There are $2\cdot6+2\cdot7=26$ favourable outcomes among 120, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{13}{60}}$.
