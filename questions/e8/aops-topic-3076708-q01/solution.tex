The laws are $a+b=b+a$, $(a+b)+c=a+(b+c)$, and $a+0=a$ for all real $a,b,c$. They are themselves among the field axioms: they are starting assumptions specifying the structure. One can prove that a particular construction of the real numbers satisfies them, but that requires specifying the construction and its earlier definitions. Without such a foundation, an attempted derivation merely restates an assumption.
