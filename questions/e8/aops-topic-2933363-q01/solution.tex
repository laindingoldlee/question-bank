Each song is liked by one of the sets $\varnothing,\{A\},\{B\},\{J\},\{A,B\},\{A,J\},\{B,J\}$. We need each of the three pair-sets to be used by at least one song.

\emph{Three songs take the three pairs, the fourth song anything else.} If the fourth song is not a pair, choose which song it is ($4$), its type ($4$ options), and arrange the pairs on the other three songs ($3!$): $4\cdot4\cdot6=96$.

\emph{The fourth song is also a pair.} Then the multiset of types is $\{p,p,q,r\}$: choose the doubled pair ($3$) and arrange ($\dfrac{4!}{2!}=12$): $36$.

Total $96+36=\boxed{132}$. (The posted attempt missed songs liked by nobody or by one girl, and overcounted.)
