Emma stands at the point $(0,0)$ and walks to $(10,10)$ in unit steps, each step going up or to the right. Her path may not pass through any of the points $(1,1)$, $(2,2)$, $(3,3)$, $(4,4)$. Let $W$ be the number of such paths. Find the sum of the distinct prime factors of $W$.
