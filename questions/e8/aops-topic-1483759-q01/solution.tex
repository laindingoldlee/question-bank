There are $\dbinom{20}{10}=184756$ paths in all. Count the bad ones by the first forbidden point they pass through. Let $g_k$ be the number of paths from $(0,0)$ to $P_k=(k,k)$ that avoid $P_1,\dots,P_{k-1}$:
\[
\begin{aligned}
g_1&=\dbinom21=2,\\
g_2&=\dbinom42-g_1\dbinom21=6-4=2,\\
g_3&=\dbinom63-g_1\dbinom42-g_2\dbinom21=20-12-4=4,\\
g_4&=\dbinom84-g_1\dbinom63-g_2\dbinom42-g_3\dbinom21=70-40-12-8=10 .
\end{aligned}
\]
After its first forbidden point $P_k$, a bad path continues freely to $(10,10)$ in $\dbinom{20-2k}{10-k}$ ways. So the number of bad paths is
\[
2\dbinom{18}{9}+2\dbinom{16}{8}+4\dbinom{14}{7}+10\dbinom{12}{6}=97240+25740+13728+9240=145948,
\]
and
\[
W=184756-145948=38808=2^3\cdot3^2\cdot7^2\cdot11 .
\]
The sum of the distinct prime factors is $2+3+7+11=\boxed{23}$.
