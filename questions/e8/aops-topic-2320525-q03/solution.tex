A mixture is a subset of the $n$ colours with at least two elements. There are $2^n$ subsets in all; removing the empty set and the $n$ one-element subsets leaves
\[2^n-n-1\]
mixtures. This increases with $n$, and
\[n=8:\ 2^8-8-1=247<500,\qquad n=9:\ 2^9-9-1=502>500.\]
So the greatest possible number of colours is $\boxed{8}$.
