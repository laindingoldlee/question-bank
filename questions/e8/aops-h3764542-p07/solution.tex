Each piece has area 3, so the side length of a square tiled along unit grid lines must be divisible by 3. A side-3 square would require three pieces, but each L-triomino can cover at most one of its four corner cells, so this is impossible. A side-6 square can be partitioned into six $2\times3$ rectangles, each tiled by two L-triominoes. Thus the minimum number of pieces is $\boxed{12}$.
