Let the contact point be $T$ and put $DN=x$. Since $AM=\dfrac{\sqrt5}{2}$, the tangent length is $MT=\sqrt{AM^2-1}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. The segment $ND$ is the other tangent from $N$, so $NT=ND=x$. The contact point lies on $MN$, giving $MN=x+\dfrac{1}{2}$.
In coordinates $A=(0,0)$, $M=(1,\dfrac{1}{2})$, $N=(x,1)$, the distance formula gives
\[
\left(x+\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^2=(1-x)^2+\dfrac{1}{4}.
\]
Hence $3x=1$, and $\boxed{MN=\dfrac{5}{6}}$.
