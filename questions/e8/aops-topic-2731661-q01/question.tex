Three friends put cents, nickels and dimes into a piggy bank. In week one the numbers of cents, nickels and dimes were in ratio $1:2:3$; in week two, $4:2:0$. Both weeks had the same total number of coins, and \$4.95 was added in all. How much money in cents was added?
