Let each week have $6k$ coins. Then there are $k+4k=5k$ cents, $2k+2k=4k$ nickels and $3k$ dimes, worth $5k+20k+30k=55k$ cents. Solving $55k=495$ gives $k=9$, so the cents are worth $\boxed{45\text{ cents}}$.
