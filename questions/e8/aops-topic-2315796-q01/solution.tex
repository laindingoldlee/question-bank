Since $818=2\cdot409$ and $409$ is prime (it is not divisible by $2,3,5,7,11,13,17,19$, and $23^2>409$), we have $818^5=2^5\cdot409^5$. A positive divisor is $2^a409^b$ with $0\le a,b\le5$.
\begin{itemize}
\item Squares: $a,b$ even, $a,b\in\{0,2,4\}$: $3\cdot3=9$.
\item Cubes: $a,b\in\{0,3\}$: $2\cdot2=4$.
\item Both (sixth powers): $a,b\in\{0\}$: $1$.
\end{itemize}
By inclusion-exclusion the number is $9+4-1=\boxed{12}$.
