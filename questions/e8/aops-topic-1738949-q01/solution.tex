Suppose integers $x$ and $y\ne0$ satisfy $3x^{10}=1991\,y^{10}$. Then $x\ne0$ too. Look at the exponent of the prime $3$ in both sides. Write $3^p\parallel x$ and $3^q\parallel y$ (the exact powers of $3$ dividing $x$ and $y$). Since $1991=11\cdot181$ is not divisible by $3$, the exponent of $3$ is $10p+1$ on the left and $10q$ on the right. But $10p+1\equiv1\pmod{10}$ and $10q\equiv0\pmod{10}$, so they cannot be equal, contradicting unique prime factorisation. Hence there are no integer solutions. $\blacksquare$
