Let $ABC$ be any triangle, and let $m_1$, $m_2$, $m_3$ be the midpoints of $\overline{AB}$, $\overline{BC}$ and $\overline{AC}$, respectively. Prove or disprove: triangle $m_1m_2m_3$ is similar to triangle $ABC$.
