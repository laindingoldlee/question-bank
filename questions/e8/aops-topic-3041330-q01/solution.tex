The three numbers have different residues modulo $3$, so one is divisible by $3$. As it is prime, it equals $3$. Thus $n$ could only be $3,1$, or $-1$. Positivity eliminates $-1$, and $1$ is not prime. Finally, $3,5,7$ are all prime, so $\boxed{n=3}$ is the unique solution.
