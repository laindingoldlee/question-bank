Since $\dfrac{1}{49}=\dfrac{0.02}{1-0.02}$, the geometric series gives
\[\dfrac{1}{49}=0.02+0.02^2+0.02^3+\cdots=\sum_{k\ge1}\dfrac{2^k}{10^{2k}}=0.02+0.0004+0.000008+0.00000016+\cdots .\]
The $19$th digit after the decimal point is the units digit of $\left\lfloor\dfrac{10^{19}}{49}\right\rfloor$, and
\[\dfrac{10^{19}}{49}=\sum_{k\ge1}2^k\cdot10^{19-2k}.\]
The terms with $k\le8$ are multiples of $1000$. The term $k=9$ is $2^9\cdot10=5120$. The remaining terms add up to
\[\sum_{k\ge10}2^k\cdot10^{19-2k}=\dfrac{2^{10}}{10}\left(1+0.02+0.02^2+\cdots\right)=\dfrac{102.4}{0.98}=104.48\ldots .\]
So $\left\lfloor\dfrac{10^{19}}{49}\right\rfloor$ ends in the last three digits of $5120+104=5224$, namely $224$, and the $19$th digit is $\boxed{4}$.

(Indeed $\dfrac{1}{49}=0.020408163265306122\underline{4}48979\ldots$: the powers of $2$ appear two digits apart until the carries take over.)
