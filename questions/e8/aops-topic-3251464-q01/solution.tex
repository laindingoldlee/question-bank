The rational-root equation is $361a+266b+196c=0$. Reduction modulo $14$ forces $14\mid a$, and modulo $19$ forces $19\mid c$. Write $a=14k,c=19l$ with nonzero integers $k,l$. Then $b=-19k-14l$, and $a^2+b^2+c^2=557(k^2+l^2)+532kl=291(k^2+l^2)+266(k+l)^2\ge582$. Equality is attained by $k=1,l=-1$, giving $(a,b,c)=(14,-5,-19)$. Its discriminant is $1089=33^2$, and the specified root is $\dfrac{5+33}{28}=\dfrac{19}{14}$. Thus the minimum is $\boxed{582}$.
