A true-false test has $10$ questions. You did not study, so you guess on every question; each guess is correct with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, independently of the others. If you answer fewer than $8$ questions correctly (a score below $80\%$), you retake the test, again guessing, and keep the higher of your two scores; otherwise you keep your first score. What is the expected number of correct answers in the score you keep? Give the exact value and the corresponding percentage to the nearest tenth.
