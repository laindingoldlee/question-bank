Let $a\ge1$ be an integer and $a^2<N<(a+1)^2$. Define $t=\dfrac{N-a^2}{2a+1}$ and $x_0=a+t$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Prove $x_0<\sqrt N$.
\item For $x>0$, define $T(x)=\dfrac{1}{2}\left(x+\dfrac{N}{x}\right)$. Prove $T(x)-\sqrt N=\dfrac{(x-\sqrt N)^2}{2x}$.
\item Apply this iteration twice from $x_0$ for $N=48$, and determine $\sqrt{48}$ to five decimal places.
\end{enumerate}
