Modulo $6$: $43\equiv1$ and $33\equiv3$, so $43^3\cdot33^2\equiv1\cdot9\equiv\boxed{3}$.

The error: cancelling changes the divisor. After cancelling, one is dividing $43^3\cdot33\cdot11$ by $2$, and the remainder modulo $2$ tells nothing directly about the remainder modulo $6$. (In general, if $N=3M$, then the remainder of $N$ modulo $6$ is $3$ times the remainder of $M$ modulo $2$; here $M$ is odd, giving $3$.)
