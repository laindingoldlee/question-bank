Let $\ell$ be the least common multiple of two distinct integers between $1$ and $100$ (inclusive), and let $g$ be their greatest common divisor. What is the maximum possible value of $\left\lfloor\dfrac{\ell g+\ell+g}{\ell^2+g^2}\right\rfloor$?
