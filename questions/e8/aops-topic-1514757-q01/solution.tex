For every $k$,
\[
k(k+1)(k+2)(k+3)-(k-1)k(k+1)(k+2)=k(k+1)(k+2)\bigl[(k+3)-(k-1)\bigr]=4k(k+1)(k+2).
\]
So the sum telescopes:
\[
\sum_{k=1}^{20}k(k+1)(k+2)=\dfrac{1}{4}\sum_{k=1}^{20}\Bigl[k(k+1)(k+2)(k+3)-(k-1)k(k+1)(k+2)\Bigr]=\dfrac{20\cdot21\cdot22\cdot23}{4}=\boxed{53130}.
\]
