Work modulo $10$. Every power $5^k$ with $k\ge1$ ends in $5$. The factorial $100!$ and the power $50^{20}$ end in $0$. Powers of $2$ have units digits cycling $2,4,8,6$ with period $4$, and $500$ is a multiple of $4$, so $2^{500}$ ends in $6$. Hence
\[
N\equiv5+0-6+0=-1\equiv9\pmod{10}.
\]
($N$ is positive since $5^{8200}>2^{500}$.) So the units digit is $9$, and $N$ ends with $\boxed{0}$ zeros.
