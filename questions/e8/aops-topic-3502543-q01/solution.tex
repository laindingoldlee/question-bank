Charlie must lie outside the interval between Alice and Bob. First put Charlie to their right, and let the gap between Alice and Bob be $d$. If their leftmost seat is $i$, Charlie can use seats $i+2d+1,\ldots,10$, giving $10-i-2d$ choices. For $d=1,2,3,4$ the totals over $i$ are $28,15,6,1$, respectively, summing to $50$. There are two orders of Alice and Bob and two sides for Charlie. The remaining seven people can be arranged in $7!$ ways. The answer is $50\cdot2\cdot2\cdot7!=\boxed{1008000}$.
