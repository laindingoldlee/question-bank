Consider the infinite sequence $a_n$ consisting of only the positive integers $3$ and $4$, such that $a_1=3$, and $a_n$ is equal to the number of $4$'s between the $n^{\textrm{th}}$ and $(n+1)^{\textrm{st}}$ $3$ in the sequence. For example, the next four terms are

$$a_2=4,\quad a_3=4,\quad a_4=4,\quad a_5=3.$$Find the numbers of $3$'s in the first $2640$ terms of the sequence.
