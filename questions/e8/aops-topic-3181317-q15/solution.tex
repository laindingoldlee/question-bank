Place $A=(0,0),B=(6,0),C=(0,8)$. The circumcircle has equation $x^2+y^2-6x-8y=0$. The bisector is $y=x$, so its second circle intersection is $D=(7,7)$. Hence $CD=\sqrt{7^2+(-1)^2}=\boxed{5\sqrt2}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(6,0),C=(0,8),D=(7,7);draw(circle((3,4),5));draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--D--C);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NW);label("$D$",D,NE);
\end{asy}
