Square $ABCD$ has side length $AB=6$. Inside the square, a quarter-circle of radius $6$ centred at $A$ is drawn from $B$ to $D$, and a quarter-circle of radius $6$ centred at $C$ is drawn from $B$ to $D$. Find the area of the region of the square that lies inside both quarter-circles.
