\begin{enumerate}
\item Count partitions of the eight labelled balls into one, two or three nonempty groups. There is one one-group partition. Two groups give $\dfrac{2^8-2}{2}=127$. Three groups give $\dfrac{3^8-3\cdot2^8+3}{6}=966$ by inclusion-exclusion followed by removing the $3!$ group labels. The total is $\boxed{1094}$.
\item The occupancies solve $x_1+x_2+x_3=8$ in nonnegative integers. Stars and bars gives $\binom{10}{2}=\boxed{45}$.
\item Each labelled ball independently chooses one of three labelled boxes, giving $3^8=\boxed{6561}$.
\end{enumerate}
