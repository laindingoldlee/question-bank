Let the fractions be $x$ and $y$ with $xy=\dfrac{14}{15}$ and $\dfrac {x}{y}=\dfrac{35}{24}$. Multiplying the two equations,
\[
x^2=\dfrac{14}{15}\cdot\dfrac{35}{24}=\dfrac{490}{360}=\dfrac{49}{36},
\]
so $x=\dfrac{7}{6}$ (as $x>0$), and $y=\dfrac{14}{15}\div\dfrac{7}{6}=\dfrac{14}{15}\cdot\dfrac{6}{7}=\dfrac{4}{5}$. Since $\dfrac {x}{y}>1$, $x$ is the greater one: the answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{7}{6}}$. Check: $\dfrac{7}{6}\cdot\dfrac{4}{5}=\dfrac{28}{30}=\dfrac{14}{15}$ and $\dfrac{7}{6}\div\dfrac{4}{5}=\dfrac{35}{24}$.
