Square $ABCD$ has side length $10$. Point $M$ is the midpoint of diagonal $AC$. A circle with centre $C$ and radius $CM$ meets side $BC$ at $E$. Segment $ED$ meets diagonal $AC$ at $F$. Find the area of the region bounded by the minor arc $EM$ of the circle and the segments $EF$ and $MF$, exactly and rounded to the nearest hundredth.
