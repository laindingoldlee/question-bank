The $k$-th multiple is $19k$, so $N=19^9\cdot(1\cdot2\cdots9)=19^9\cdot9!$. The factor $9!$ contains $2\cdot5=10$, so $N$ is a multiple of $10$ and its last digit is $\boxed{0}$. (Directly: $38\times95$ is part of the product and $38\times95=3610$.)
