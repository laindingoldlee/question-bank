$(a+bi)^2=a^2-b^2+2abi=i$ gives $a^2=b^2$ and $2ab=1$. So $a=b$ and $2a^2=1$, i.e. $a=b=\pm\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$. The square roots are $\boxed{\pm\left(\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}+\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}i\right)}$.
