In right triangle $BCD$ (right angle at $C$), $CD=AB=6$ and $\tan\angle DBC=\dfrac{CD}{BC}$, so $BC=\dfrac{6}{\tan30^\circ}=6\sqrt3$. Then $AD=6\sqrt3$, $AE=4\sqrt3$ and $ED=2\sqrt3$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=3^0.5;
pair B=(0,0),C=(6*s,0),D=(6*s,6),A=(0,6);
pair Ep=A+(2/3)*(D-A);
pair K=extension(C,Ep,B,D);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(B--D,linewidth(0.7));draw(C--Ep,linewidth(0.7));
draw(Ep--K,red+linewidth(1.4));
draw(arc(B,1.4,0,30),linewidth(0.5));label("$30^\circ$",B+2.1*dir(13));
dot(Ep);dot(K);
label("$A$",A,NW);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,NE);
label("$E$",Ep,N);label("$K$",K,dir(-120));
label("$6$",(0,3),W);label("$4\sqrt{3}$",(A+Ep)/2,N);label("$2\sqrt{3}$",(Ep+D)/2,N);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

In right triangle $EDC$, $EC=\sqrt{ED^2+DC^2}=\sqrt{12+36}=4\sqrt3$.

Since $ED\parallel BC$, triangles $KED$ and $KCB$ are similar with ratio $\dfrac{ED}{BC}=\dfrac{2\sqrt3}{6\sqrt3}=\dfrac{1}{3}$. So $EK:KC=1:3$, and
\[
EK=\dfrac{1}{4}\,EC=\boxed{\sqrt3}.
\]
