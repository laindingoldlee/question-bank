Choose which $7$ questions are correct in $\binom{10}{7}=120$ ways; each such pattern has probability $(0.9)^7(0.1)^3$. Hence
\[
P=\binom{10}{7}(0.9)^7(0.1)^3=\dfrac{120\cdot9^7}{10^{10}}=\dfrac{573956280}{10^{10}}=\dfrac{3^{15}}{250000000}=\boxed{0.057395628}\approx5.74\%.
\]
