Put $s=x+y+z$, $q=xy+yz+zx$, and $r=xyz$. Clearing denominators and adding the equations gives $q=s$. Also
\[
(3-2x)(3-2y)(3-2z)=\dfrac{y}{z}\dfrac{z}{x}\dfrac{x}{y}=1,
\]
so $3s+4r=13$ after using $q=s$.

Multiplying the cleared equations $2xz+y=3z$, $2xy+z=3x$, and $2yz+x=3y$ by $y,z,x$, respectively, and adding gives
\[
x^2+y^2+z^2=3q-6r.
\]
Therefore $s^2=5s-6r$, and elimination of $r$ yields
\[
2s^2-19s+39=0,\qquad s=3\ \text{or}\ \dfrac{13}{2}.
\]
If $s=3$, then $q=3$ and $\sum(x-1)^2=s^2-2q-2s+3=0$, forcing $x=y=z=1$, contrary to distinctness. Hence $\boxed{x+y+z=\dfrac{13}{2}}$.
