Six indistinguishable red marbles and six indistinguishable blue marbles are arranged in a line so that every red marble is next to at least one red marble and every blue marble is next to at least one blue marble.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] How many such arrangements are possible?
\item[(b)] Generalise: find a formula for the number of such arrangements of $n$ red and $n$ blue marbles ($n\ge2$).
\end{enumerate}
