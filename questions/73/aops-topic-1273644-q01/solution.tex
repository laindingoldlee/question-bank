With first term $a$ and difference $d$: $10a+45d=160$ and $10a+145d=340$. Subtracting, $100d=180$, so $d=\dfrac{9}{5}$; then $10a=160-81=79$, so $a=\boxed{\dfrac{79}{10}}$.
