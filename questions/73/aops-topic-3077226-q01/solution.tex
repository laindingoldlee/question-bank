Factor $462=2\cdot3\cdot7\cdot11$. One complete block of $462$ consecutive integers contains $\varphi(462)=462(\dfrac{1}{2})(\dfrac{2}{3})(\dfrac{6}{7})(\dfrac{10}{11})=120$ coprime integers. The remaining integers $463$ through $528$ correspond to residues $1$ through $66$. Their coprime residues are $1,5,13,17,19,23,25,29,31,37,41,43,47,53,59,61,65$, seventeen in all. Thus the answer is $\boxed{137}$. The scaled-floor expression happens to agree here, but is not a general rule: among $1,\ldots,5$ two integers are coprime to $6$, whereas $\lfloor\varphi(6)\cdot\dfrac{5}{6}\rfloor=1$.
