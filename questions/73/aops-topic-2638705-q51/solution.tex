The separate means are $\dfrac{1+8}{2}=\dfrac{9}{2}$ and $\dfrac{1+6}{2}=\dfrac{7}{2}$. (a) Linearity gives $\dfrac{9}{2}+\dfrac{7}{2}=\boxed8$. (b) Independence gives the product of means, $\dfrac{9}{2}\cdot\dfrac{7}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{63}{4}}$.
