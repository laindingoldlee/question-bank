Five spheres, each of radius $6$, are placed inside a pyramid with a square base. Four of them rest on the base in a square formation, each touching two of the others; the fifth rests on top of these four, touching all of them. Each lateral face of the pyramid touches the top sphere and the two bottom spheres next to that face. Find the total surface area of the pyramid, base included.
