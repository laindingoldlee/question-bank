By Vieta's formulas, $x$, $y$, $z$ are the roots of
\[t^3-3t^2-12t-4=0 .\]
Trying small integers, $t=-2$ works: $-8-12+24-4=0$. Dividing,
\[t^3-3t^2-12t-4=(t+2)(t^2-5t-2),\]
and $t^2-5t-2=0$ gives $t=\dfrac{5\pm\sqrt{33}}{2}$. Since $\dfrac{5-\sqrt{33}}{2}\approx-0.37$, the order is
\[x=-2,\qquad y=\dfrac{5-\sqrt{33}}{2},\qquad z=\dfrac{5+\sqrt{33}}{2}.\]
So $a+b+c=5+33+2=40$, answer $\boxed{\textbf{(E) } 40}$.
