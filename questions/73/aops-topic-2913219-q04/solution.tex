Since $840=2^3\cdot3\cdot5\cdot7$, it has $4\cdot2\cdot2\cdot2=32$ positive divisors. Divisors avoiding $3$ and $7$ can use only $2^a5^b$ with $0\le a\le3$, $0\le b\le1$, giving eight. The probability is $\dfrac{8}{32}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{4}}$.
