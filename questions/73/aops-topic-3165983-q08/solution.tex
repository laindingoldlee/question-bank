The horizontal separation is six feet and the vertical separation is $14-6=8$ feet. By Pythagoras the distance is $\sqrt{6^2+8^2}=\boxed{10}$ feet.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw((0,0)--(0,14));draw((0,0)--(6,0)--(6,6));draw((0,14)--(6,6),blue);draw((0,6)--(6,6),dashed);label("spider",(0,14),N);label("head",(6,6),E);label("$6$",(3,0),S);label("$8$",(0,10),W);
\end{asy}
