Ties have probability $0$. Sort the numbers as $y_1<y_2<y_3<y_4$. The sorted $4$-tuple is uniformly distributed on the region $0\le y_1\le y_2\le y_3\le y_4\le9$, whose volume is $\dfrac{9^4}{4!}$. The intervals are disjoint exactly when $y_{i+1}-y_i>1$ for $i=1,2,3$.

Substitute $z_i=y_i-(i-1)$. This is a translation in each coordinate (volume preserving), and it maps the disjoint region onto $0\le z_1<z_2<z_3<z_4\le6$, of volume $\dfrac{6^4}{4!}$. Hence the probability is
\[
\dfrac{6^4}{9^4}=\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^4=\dfrac{16}{81},
\]
and $m+n=16+81=\boxed{97}$, answer $\textbf{(C)}$.
