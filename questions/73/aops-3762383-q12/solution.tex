Before absorption, either all numbers agree (state 0) or two agree and the third differs by $d\ge1$ (state $d$, with sign irrelevant). Write expectations $E_d$. Enumerating the eight coin outcomes gives $E_0 = \dfrac{4}{3} + E_1$, $4E_1=8+E_0+E_2$, and $6E_d=8+E_{d-1}+E_{d+1}$ for $d\ge2$. The bounded solution of the last recurrence is $E_d=2+A(3-2\sqrt2)^{d-1}$. Boundedness follows since from every nonabsorbed state there is a uniform positive probability of absorption within two steps. Substituting the first two equations gives $A = \dfrac{4 \sqrt{2}}{3}$. Therefore $\boxed{E_0 = \dfrac{10+4\sqrt2}{3}}$.
