Exactly three equal has 6 choices for the repeated value, 4 choices for the exceptional die, and 5 choices for its different value, giving 120 outcomes. All four equal gives 6 more. These cases are disjoint, so $\boxed{\dfrac{126}{6^4}=\dfrac{7}{72}}$.
