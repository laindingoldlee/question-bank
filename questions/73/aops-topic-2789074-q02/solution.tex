Let correct and incorrect counts be $c,w$. Then $8c-5w=13$ and $c+w\le20$. Modulo 5 gives $c\equiv1\pmod5$, so write $c=1+5k$, $w=8k-1$. Nonnegative $w$ requires $k\ge1$, while $c+w=13k\le20$ forces $k=1$. Thus $c=6,w=7$, and $\boxed{13}$ were attempted.
