Let $P(x)=(x-3)(x-5)(x-7)(x-9)$. The equation is $P\prime(x)-2014P(x)=0$, with no pole being a root. Its leading coefficients are $-2014$ and $4+2014(24)$, so Vieta gives root sum $\boxed{24+\dfrac{2}{1007}}$. There are four real roots: the reciprocal sum decreases continuously from positive infinity to negative infinity between consecutive poles, giving one in each of the three interior intervals, and decreases from positive infinity to zero to the right of 9, giving the fourth.
