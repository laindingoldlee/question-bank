The conditioning event has $2^7-\binom70-\binom71-\binom72=99$ outcomes. Before the third tail, the head runs occupy three gaps. If there are three heads, exactly one gap has length three and the others zero: three choices. The third tail is flip six, and the last flip is arbitrary, giving six outcomes. If there are four heads, the gap lengths must be $3,1,0$ in any order, giving six outcomes, with the third tail at flip seven. No other head count can work. Thus the probability is $\dfrac{12}{99}=\boxed{\dfrac{4}{33}}$.
