Let the distance be $D$ miles. At $50$ mph the trip takes $\dfrac{D}{50}$ hours, and at $30$ mph it takes $\dfrac{D}{30}$ hours, which is $2$ hours longer:
\[
\dfrac{D}{30}-\dfrac{D}{50}=2\iff \dfrac{5D-3D}{150}=2\iff D=150 .
\]
The planned trip takes $\dfrac{150}{50}=3$ hours, so Santa was supposed to arrive at $\boxed{\text{11:00 AM}}$ (he actually arrived at 1:00 PM).
