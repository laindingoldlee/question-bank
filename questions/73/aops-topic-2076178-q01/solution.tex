The difference is the tail
\[
R_k=\sum_{n=k+1}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{n!}=\dfrac{1}{(k+1)!}\left(1+\dfrac{1}{k+2}+\dfrac{1}{(k+2)(k+3)}+\cdots\right),
\]
a sum of positive terms, so \(R_k>0\). Each factor \(k+2,k+3,\dots\) is at least \(k+2\), so the bracket is at most the geometric series
\[
1+\dfrac{1}{k+2}+\dfrac{1}{(k+2)^2}+\cdots=\dfrac{k+2}{k+1},
\]
with strict inequality from the third term on. Hence
\[
R_k<\dfrac{k+2}{(k+1)\,(k+1)!}=\dfrac{k+2}{(k+1)^2}\cdot\dfrac{1}{k!}.
\]
Finally \(\dfrac{k+2}{(k+1)^2}<\dfrac{1}{k}\iff k^2+2k<k^2+2k+1\), which is true. So \(0<R_k<\dfrac{1}{k\cdot k!}\). \(\blacksquare\)
