Using \(e=\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{n!}\), prove that for every positive integer \(k\),
\[
0<e-\sum_{n=0}^{k}\dfrac{1}{n!}<\dfrac{1}{k\cdot k!}.
\]
(So, although the partial sums have no simple closed form, they approximate \(e\) extremely quickly.)
