The common difference is $7$, so the $200$th term is $5+199\cdot7=1398$. The sum of an arithmetic sequence is the number of terms times the average of the first and last terms:
\[
200\cdot\dfrac{5+1398}{2}=100\cdot1403=\boxed{140300}.
\]
