Let there be $D$ dogs and $C$ cats. Those who think they are cats are $20\%$ of the dogs and $80\%$ of the cats:
\[0.2D+0.8C=0.32(D+C)\ \Longrightarrow\ 0.48C=0.12D\ \Longrightarrow\ D=4C.\]
With $D=C+180$ we get $3C=180$, so $C=60$ and $D=\boxed{240}$.
