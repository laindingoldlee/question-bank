Each day a dog wants exactly one of three activities: a walk (probability $50\%$), a ride in the back of the truck ($25\%$) or playing ball ($25\%$). Independently, the owner chooses two of the three activities at random, each pair being equally likely, and does them with the dog. Different days are independent. What is the probability that the dog gets what it wants on at least $5$ days of a $7$-day week?
