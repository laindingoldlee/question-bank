Each activity is among the owner's two choices with probability $\dfrac{2}{3}$ (two of the three pairs contain it). So on any day, whatever the dog wants,
\[P(\text{dog gets its wish})=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}+\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}+\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}=\dfrac{2}{3} .\]
The number $X$ of good days in a week is binomial with $n=7$ and $p=\dfrac{2}{3}$:
\[P(X\ge5)=\dfrac{\dbinom75 2^5+\dbinom76 2^6+\dbinom77 2^7}{3^7}=\dfrac{672+448+128}{2187}=\dfrac{1248}{2187}=\boxed{\dfrac{416}{729}}.\]
