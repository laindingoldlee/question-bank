A customer has three coupons, only one of which may be used: Coupon 1 gives $10\%$ off a listed price of at least \$50; Coupon 2 gives \$20 off a listed price of at least \$100; Coupon 3 gives $18\%$ off the amount by which the listed price exceeds \$100. For which listed price does Coupon 1 give a greater reduction than \emph{each} of the other two coupons?

$\textbf{(A)}\ \$179.95\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ \$199.95\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ \$219.95\qquad\textbf{(D)}\ \$239.95\qquad\textbf{(E)}\ \$259.95$
