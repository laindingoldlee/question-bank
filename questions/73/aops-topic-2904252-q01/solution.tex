For a price $x>100$, the reductions are $0.1x$, $20$ and $0.18(x-100)$. Coupon 1 must beat both (``greater than either'' means greater than whichever you compare it with). So $0.1x>20$ gives $x>200$, and $0.1x>0.18x-18$ gives $x<225$. Only $\boxed{\textbf{(C)}\ \$219.95}$ satisfies both.
