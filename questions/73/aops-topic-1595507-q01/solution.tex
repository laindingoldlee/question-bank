Rewrite the equation as $v^2+3v-1=0$. It does not factor over the integers, so use the quadratic formula with $a=1$, $b=3$, $c=-1$:
\[
v=\dfrac{-3\pm\sqrt{3^2-4\cdot1\cdot(-1)}}{2}=\dfrac{-3\pm\sqrt{13}}{2}.
\]
(Equivalently, completing the square: $\left(v+\dfrac{3}{2}\right)^2=1+\dfrac{9}{4}=\dfrac{13}{4}$.) So
\[
\boxed{v=\dfrac{-3+\sqrt{13}}{2}\quad\text{or}\quad v=\dfrac{-3-\sqrt{13}}{2}}.
\]
