Subtract $n+k$ to obtain the equivalent condition $n+k\mid n!-n$. For $n=1$ or $2$, this difference is zero, so every positive $k$ works. For $n\ge3$, it is positive. Thus choose any positive divisor $d>n$ of $n!-n$ and set $k=d-n$; every such choice works, and every solution arises this way. The complete answer is $\boxed{n\in\{1,2\}:k\ge1;\quad n\ge3:k=d-n,\ d\mid n!-n,\ d>n}$.
