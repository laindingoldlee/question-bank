With $m,n$ odd: $m+3n$ and $3m-n$ are odd $\pm$ odd, hence even; $3m^2+3n^2$ is odd $+$ odd, even; $nm+3$ is odd $+$ odd, even, so its square is even. But $3mn$ is a product of odd numbers, hence odd. Answer $\boxed{\textbf{(E)}\ 3mn}$.
