Point $W$ lies in or on the rectangle with vertices $(-5,5),(-5,0),(5,0),(5,5)$, and point $Z$ lies in or on the circle with centre $(1,10)$ and radius $2.4$. The product of the minimum and maximum possible values of $WZ$ can be written as $s+r\sqrt q$ with $q$ squarefree. Compute $r\cdot q\cdot s$.
