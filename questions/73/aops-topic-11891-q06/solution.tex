A number is divisible by $4$ exactly when the number formed by its last two digits is. Two-digit endings with distinct digits from $\{2,4,5,6,7\}$ that are multiples of $4$:
\[
24,\ 52,\ 56,\ 64,\ 72,\ 76
\]
(the other even candidates $26,42,46,54,62,74$ are not multiples of $4$). For each ending, the first two digits are chosen in order from the remaining $3$ digits in $3\cdot2=6$ ways. The total is $6\cdot6=\boxed{36}$.
