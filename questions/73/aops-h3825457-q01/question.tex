Let $n=2k+1$ people, with $k\ge1$, each choose red or blue. Red always guarantees survival; blue survives exactly when a strict majority choose blue. The other $2k$ people independently choose blue with probability $p$. You wish to maximize the expected total number of survivors. Derive a criterion for choosing blue, prove there is one switching probability in $(0,1)$, and determine its limit as $k\to\infty$.
