Find the two smallest positive real numbers $x$ (in radians) such that
\[\tan\left(\dfrac{\pi}{4}+x\right)=9\tan\left(\dfrac{\pi}{4}-x\right),\]
and show that their sum is $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
