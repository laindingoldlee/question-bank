A boat travels between City $A$ and City $B$ along a river. It takes the boat $2$ hours to go from City $A$ to City $B$ (downstream) and $3$ hours to return from City $B$ to City $A$ (upstream). If the speed of the water is $2$ km per hour, what is the speed of the boat in still water, in km per hour?
