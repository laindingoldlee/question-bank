Let the speed of the boat in still water be $v$ km per hour. Downstream its speed is $v+2$ and upstream it is $v-2$. The distance is the same both ways, so
\[2(v+2)=3(v-2)\iff 2v+4=3v-6\iff v=\boxed{10}.\]
(Check: downstream $2\cdot12=24$ km, upstream $3\cdot8=24$ km.)
