Take a right triangle with legs a,b and hypotenuse c. Arrange four congruent copies inside a square of side a+b, with their right angles at the outer corners. The remaining central figure is a square: all its sides have length c, and each angle is $180^\circ$ minus the two acute triangle angles, hence $90^\circ$. Comparing areas gives $(a+b)^2=4(\dfrac{ab}{2})+c^2$. Cancelling 2ab yields $\boxed{a^2+b^2=c^2}$.
