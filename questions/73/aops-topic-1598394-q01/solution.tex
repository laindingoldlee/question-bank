Count all arrangements and subtract those in which the two letters are adjacent (complementary counting).

There are $9!$ arrangements in all. To count those with the two letters together, glue them into one block: the block and the other $7$ letters can be arranged in $8!$ ways, and the block itself in $2$ orders, giving $2\cdot8!$. Hence the number of arrangements is
\[
9!-2\cdot8!=8!\,(9-2)=7\cdot40320=\boxed{282240}.
\]
