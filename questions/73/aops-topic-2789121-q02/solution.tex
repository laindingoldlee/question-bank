The three triangle vertices have only two available colours, so two share a colour. Every pair of vertices is exactly 1 foot apart, proving the claim.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(2,0),C=(1,sqrt(3));draw(A--B--C--cycle);dot(A);dot(B);dot(C);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,N);label("$1$ foot",(1,0),S);
\end{asy}
