While waiting for his Expii class, Gunga begins drawing a square of side length $1$, then a circle of radius $1$, then a square of side length $2$, then a circle of radius $2$, and so on. In general, for each positive integer $k$, he draws a square of side length $k$ followed by a circle of radius $k$. He continues this process until the total area of all the shapes he has drawn exceeds $5000$. How many shapes does Gunga draw in total?
