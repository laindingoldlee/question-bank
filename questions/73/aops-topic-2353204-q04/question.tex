Jericho arranges nine cupcakes in a row: two identical yellow cupcakes, one purple, one blue, one pink, and four other cupcakes, all of different colours from each other and from these. The two yellow cupcakes must not be next to each other, the purple cupcake must be at one of the two ends of the row, and there must be exactly one cupcake between the blue and the pink cupcakes. He then puts all of his $60$ identical sprinkles on the cupcakes, at least $5$ on each cupcake. How many different displays are possible? (A display is determined by the colour of the cupcake in each position and the number of sprinkles on each cupcake.)
