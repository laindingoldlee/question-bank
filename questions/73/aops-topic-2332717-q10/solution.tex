Let there be $n$ students. Those liking at least one sport are $\dfrac{11}{12}n$, so both $=\dfrac7{12}n+\dfrac8{12}n-\dfrac{11}{12}n=\dfrac13n$. Since $\dfrac7{12}n$ is an integer, $12\mid n$, and the minimum is $\dfrac{12}{3}=4$. \textbf{(D)}
