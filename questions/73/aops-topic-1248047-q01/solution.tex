Whatever number I chose, each roll shows it with probability $\dfrac{1}{n}$, independently. The number of rolls is geometric with success probability $\dfrac{1}{n}$, so its expectation is $\boxed{n}$. (It is larger than $\dfrac{n+1}{2}$ because the die may repeat numbers.)
