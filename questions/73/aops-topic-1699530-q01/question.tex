Line $m$ is the perpendicular bisector of both $\overline{AB}$ and $\overline{CD}$. Which of the following must be true, and why (or why not)?
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $AB=CD$.
\item[(b)] $\overline{AB}\parallel\overline{CD}$.
\item[(c)] $AC=BD$.
\end{enumerate}
In (c), does it matter whether $A$ and $C$ are on the same side of $m$?
