The real numbers $x$, $b$, $c$ satisfy $x+b=2c$ and $x>b$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Explain why the value of $c$ cannot be determined from this information.
\item[(b)] Prove that $b<c<x$.
\end{enumerate}
