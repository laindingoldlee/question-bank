\textbf{(a)} The data allow different values of $c$: $(x,b,c)=(2,0,1)$ and $(x,b,c)=(4,0,2)$ both satisfy $x+b=2c$ and $x>b$, with $c=1$ and $c=2$. So $c$ is not determined.

\textbf{(b)} The condition says $c=\dfrac{x+b}{2}$, the average of $x$ and $b$. Then
\[
c-b=\dfrac{x-b}{2}>0\qquad\text{and}\qquad x-c=\dfrac{x-b}{2}>0,
\]
so $b<c<x$: the average of two different numbers lies strictly between them. $\blacksquare$
