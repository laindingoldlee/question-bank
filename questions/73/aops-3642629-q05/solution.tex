Sort Mark's five allocations as $m_1\le\cdots\le m_5$. Lily can prevent him capturing three castles exactly when she can match his three cheapest allocations, so a guarantee requires $m_1+m_2+m_3\ge11$. If Mark had at most 18 soldiers, the sum of his three smallest allocations would be at most $\lfloor3\cdot\dfrac{18}{5}\rfloor=10$, so this cannot work. With allocations $3,4,4,4,4$, every three cost at least 11 to block. Lily has only ten, so Mark wins at least three. The minimum is $\boxed{19}$.
