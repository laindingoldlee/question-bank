In any round with $n$ players, the sum of received-pointer counts is $n$. Modulo two, that sum equals the number $o$ of players receiving odd counts. Hence $o\equiv n\pmod2$, and the number of survivors $n-o$ is even. After every round there are therefore an even number of survivors, never one. Starting from five, a sole winner is impossible and its probability is $\boxed0$, regardless of how recipients are chosen. An empty group or a nonterminating process gives no sole winner.
