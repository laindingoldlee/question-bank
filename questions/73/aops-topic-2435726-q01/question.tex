Let $P_1P_2\cdots P_n$ be a convex polygon whose vertices $P_i=(x_i,y_i)$ are listed in counterclockwise order, and put $P_{n+1}=P_1$. Prove the shoelace formula: the area of the polygon is
\[\dfrac12\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left(x_iy_{i+1}-x_{i+1}y_i\right).\]
