$\angle A=\angle C=x$ and $\angle B=\angle D=180-x$. So $x^2-(180-x)^2=360x-32400=720$, giving $x=\boxed{92^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(2.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(4,0),D=1.5*dir(92),C=B+D;draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);
\end{asy}
