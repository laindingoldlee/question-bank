Elimination adds the equations to give $2x=6$, so $x=3,y=2$. Substitution uses $y=5-x$ in the second equation, giving $x-(5-x)=1$ and the same pair $\boxed{(3,2)}$. Both methods preserve the equations and lead to the same solution.
