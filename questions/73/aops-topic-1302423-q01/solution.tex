Write the number as $\overline{abc}$ with $a\ne0$ and $c$ even. Exactly two distinct digits means exactly one of the patterns $a=b\ne c$, $a=c\ne b$, $b=c\ne a$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $a=b\ne c$: if $a$ is even ($4$ choices) then $c$ is one of the other $4$ even digits; if $a$ is odd ($5$ choices), $c$ is any of $5$ even digits: $16+25=41$.
\item $a=c\ne b$: $a$ even and nonzero ($4$), $b\ne a$ ($9$): $36$.
\item $b=c\ne a$: if $c=0$, $a$ has $9$ choices; if $c\in\{2,4,6,8\}$, $a$ has $8$ choices: $9+32=41$.
\end{itemize}
The total is $41+36+41=\boxed{118}$.
