In cube $ABCDEFGH$, face $ABCD$ is the bottom face and $E,F,G,H$ are directly above $A,B,C,D$ respectively, so $C$ and $E$ are opposite vertices. Points $J$ and $I$ are the midpoints of edges $FB$ and $HD$. The plane through $E$, $J$, $C$, $I$ cuts the cube into two pieces. What fraction of the volume of the cube is the piece $EFGHICJ$ (the piece containing the top face $EFGH$)?
