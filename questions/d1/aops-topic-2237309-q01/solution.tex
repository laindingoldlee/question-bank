Write everything as a power of $5$: $125=5^3$ and $25^3=(5^2)^3=5^6$. Then
\[5^3=\dfrac{5^6}{5^x}=5^{6-x},\]
so $6-x=3$ and $x=\boxed{3}$. (Check: $\dfrac{25^3}{5^3}=\dfrac{15625}{125}=125$.)
