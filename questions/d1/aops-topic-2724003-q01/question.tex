Prove that (a) $\left(x^m\right)^2\cdot\left(\dfrac1{x^{-n}}\right)^2=x^{2(m+n)}$ and (b) $y^m\cdot\dfrac{y}{y^{-n}}=y^{m+n+1}$, for nonzero $x,y$ and integers $m,n$.
