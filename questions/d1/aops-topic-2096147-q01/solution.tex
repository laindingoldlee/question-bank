By Vieta's formulas, $p+q=-5$ and $pq=3$. Then
\[p^2+q^2=(p+q)^2-2pq=25-6=19,\qquad p^2q^2=(pq)^2=9 .\]
Applying Vieta's formulas to $x^2+bx+c$, whose roots are $p^2$ and $q^2$: $b=-(p^2+q^2)=-19$ and $c=p^2q^2=9$. So $b+c=\boxed{-10}$.
