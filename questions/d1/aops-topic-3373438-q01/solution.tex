Divide quantities by $6$, reducing to packs of $6$ and $7$. The value $29$ cannot be written $6a+7b$: modulo $6$, $b\equiv5$, forcing $7b\ge35>29$. On the other hand $30,31,32,33,34,35$ equal $6\cdot5$, $6\cdot4+7$, $6\cdot3+14$, $6\cdot2+21$, $6+28$, $35$. Adding packs of $6$ produces every larger integer. Thus the answer is $6\cdot29=\boxed{174}$ pencils.
