The solutions $x=u$ and $x=v$ of the quadratic equation $rx^2+sx+t=0$ are the reciprocals of the solutions of the quadratic equation
\[(2+a)x^2+5x+(2-a)=0,\]
where $a$ is an integer with $a\ne\pm2$. If $r$, $s$ and $t$ are integers whose greatest common factor is $1$ and $s>0$, what is the value of $r+s+t$?
