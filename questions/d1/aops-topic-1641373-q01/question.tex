For how many integers $n$ with $15<n<1500$ is it true that $3A_n$ is a perfect square and $23_n$ is a perfect cube? (Here $3A_n$ is the two-digit base-$n$ numeral with digits $3$ and $A$, where the digit $A$ has value $10$.)
