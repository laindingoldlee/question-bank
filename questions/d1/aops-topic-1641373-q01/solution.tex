In base $n$, $3A_n=3n+10$ and $23_n=2n+3$.

\textbf{The cube condition.} For $15<n<1500$, $2n+3$ is an odd number with $35\le2n+3\le3001$. The odd cubes in this range are $5^3=125$, $7^3=343$, $9^3=729$, $11^3=1331$, $13^3=2197$ (the next, $15^3=3375$, is too big, and $3^3=27$ is too small). They give
\[
n=61,\ 170,\ 363,\ 664,\ 1097 .
\]

\textbf{The square condition.} For these $n$,
\[
3n+10=193,\ 520,\ 1099,\ 2002,\ 3301 .
\]
None of these is a square: $13^2<193<14^2$, $22^2<520<23^2$, $33^2=1089<1099<34^2$, $44^2<2002<45^2=2025$, $57^2<3301<58^2$.

So there are $\boxed{0}$ such integers $n$.
