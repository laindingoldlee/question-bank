Take $A=(0,0),B=(3,0),C=(3,6),D=(0,6)$. The crease is the perpendicular bisector of $BD$: $(x-3)^2+y^2=x^2+(y-6)^2$, or $y=\dfrac{x}{2}+\dfrac{9}{4}$. Hence $E=(0,\dfrac{9}{4})$, $F=(3,\dfrac{15}{4})$. The distance is $\sqrt{3^2+(\dfrac{3}{2})^2}=\boxed{\dfrac{3\sqrt5}{2}}$.

\end{asy}
