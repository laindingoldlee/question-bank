From left to right: $\dfrac{2m^{-2}}{n^0}=2m^{-2}$; dividing by $\dfrac{m^8}{4n^3}$ gives $8m^{-10}n^3$; multiplying by $\dfrac{7}{14}=\dfrac{1}{2}$ gives $4m^{-10}n^3$; multiplying by $\dfrac{2}{m^{-2}}=2m^2$ gives $8m^{-8}n^3=\boxed{\dfrac{8n^3}{m^8}}$.
