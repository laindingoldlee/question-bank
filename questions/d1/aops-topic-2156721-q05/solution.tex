There are $40$ multiples of $5$, $\left\lfloor\dfrac{200}{7}\right\rfloor=28$ multiples of $7$ and $\left\lfloor\dfrac{200}{35}\right\rfloor=5$ multiples of $35$. The answer is $40+28-5=\boxed{63}$.
