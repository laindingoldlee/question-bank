We need $a+b+c=f(1)$, and $x+3=1$ when $x=-2$:
\[
a+b+c=f(1)=3(-2)^2+7(-2)+4=12-14+4=\boxed{2}.
\]
(Indeed, substituting $x-3$ for $x$ gives $f(x)=3(x-3)^2+7(x-3)+4=3x^2-11x+10$, and $3-11+10=2$.)
