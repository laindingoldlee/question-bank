Alice and Bob write down numbers every minute, in unison. At minute $0$ Alice writes $-120$ and Bob writes $-14$. Every minute after that, Alice writes the number $8$ greater than her previous number, and Bob writes the number $3$ greater than his previous number. At the first minute at which one of them writes a number greater than or equal to $300$, by how much does that person's number exceed the other person's number?
