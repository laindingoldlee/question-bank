$\left(i\sqrt2\right)^3=i^3\cdot2\sqrt2=\boxed{-2i\sqrt2}$.

The rule $\sqrt a\sqrt b=\sqrt{ab}$ holds for $a,b\ge0$ but fails for negative numbers: for example $\sqrt{-1}\sqrt{-1}=i^2=-1$, while $\sqrt{(-1)(-1)}=1$. Combining the square roots of negative numbers is exactly the invalid step.
