Two students compute the same product of a two-digit number (the multiplicand) and a three-digit number (the multiplier). One misreads the multiplier's hundreds digit $2$ as $3$ and gets an answer $3800$ too large. The other misreads the multiplicand's tens digit $3$ as $6$ and gets an answer $6030$ too large. What is the correct product?
