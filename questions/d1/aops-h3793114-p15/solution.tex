The equal sides give equal base angles, so $\angle DEF=\angle EFD=68^\circ$. The angle sum gives $\angle EDF=180^\circ-136^\circ=\boxed{44^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair E=(-1,0),F=(1,0),D=(0,tan(68*pi/180));draw(D--E--F--cycle);label("D",D,N);label("E",E,SW);label("F",F,SE);label("$68^\circ$",(-.55,.2));
\end{asy}
