A car drives from station \(A\) to station \(B\), stopping at \(B\), and then drives from \(B\) to station \(C\), stopping at \(C\). It starts from rest at \(A\), and its speed changes continuously. On the trip from \(A\) to \(B\) its top speed is \(22\) km/h, and on the trip from \(B\) to \(C\) its top speed is \(60\) km/h. What is the least possible number of moments at which the car's speed is exactly \(58\) km/h?
