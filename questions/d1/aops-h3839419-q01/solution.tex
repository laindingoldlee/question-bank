Complete the squares:
\[
(x-8)^2+(y-8)^2+(z-8)^2=192.
\]
If three squares sum to a multiple of $4$, each is divisible by $4$, since every square is $0$ or $1$ modulo $4$. Thus all three bases are even. Applying this observation successively to sums $192,48,12$ shows that $x-8,y-8,z-8$ are all divisible by $8$.

Write these bases as $8u,8v,8w$. Then $u^2+v^2+w^2=3$. The only squares at most $3$ are $0,1$, so all three squares equal $1$. Consequently each of $x,y,z$ is either $0$ or $16$. All eight choices work, and the complete answer is $\boxed{\{0,16\}^3}$.
