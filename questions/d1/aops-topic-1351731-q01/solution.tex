Let the dimensions be $x,y,z$ with $xyz=2016=2^5\cdot3^2\cdot7$. By the AM-GM inequality,
\[
x+y+z\ge3\sqrt[3]{xyz}=3\sqrt[3]{2016}.
\]
Now $\left(\dfrac{37}{3}\right)^3=\dfrac{50653}{27}<1877<2016$, so $3\sqrt[3]{2016}>37$, and since $x+y+z$ is an integer, $x+y+z\ge38$. The sum $38$ is attained: $12\cdot12\cdot14=2016$ and $12+12+14=38$. The least possible sum is $\boxed{38}$.
