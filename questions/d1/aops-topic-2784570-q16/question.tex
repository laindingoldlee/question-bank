For each positive integer $n$, count ordered tuples $(a_1,\ldots,a_n)$ of pairwise distinct elements of $\{1,2,\ldots,25\}$ such that $i^2$ divides $a_i$ for every $i$. Also give the total count over all possible positive lengths.
