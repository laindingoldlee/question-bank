Since \(n^2+3n+2=(n+1)(n+2)\),
\[
\dfrac{1}{n^2+3n+2}=\dfrac{1}{n+1}-\dfrac{1}{n+2}.
\]
The partial sums telescope:
\[
S_N=\sum_{n=0}^{N}\left(\dfrac{1}{n+1}-\dfrac{1}{n+2}\right)=1-\dfrac{1}{N+2}.
\]
As \(N\to\infty\), \(S_N\to1\). So the series converges, and its value is \(\boxed{1}\).
