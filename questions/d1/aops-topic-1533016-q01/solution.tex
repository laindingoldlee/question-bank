Worker $n$ does $\dfrac{1}{n}$ of the job per hour. The first $k$ workers together finish in under $20$ minutes $=\dfrac{1}{3}$ hour exactly when they do more than $3$ jobs per hour, that is, when
\[
H_k=1+\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{3}+\cdots+\dfrac{1}{k}>3 .
\]
Using more workers can only help, and the best $k$ workers are workers $1$ to $k$, so we need the least $k$ with $H_k>3$. We have
\[
H_{10}=\dfrac{7381}{2520}\approx2.929<3,\qquad H_{11}=H_{10}+\dfrac{1}{11}=\dfrac{83711}{27720}\approx3.020>3 .
\]
So the least number of workers is $\boxed{11}$.
