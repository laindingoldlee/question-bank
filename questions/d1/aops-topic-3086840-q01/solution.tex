$29=1\cdot3^3+0\cdot3^2+0\cdot3^1+2\cdot3^0$, so $\boxed{29=(1002)_3}$. The zeros reserve the $3^2$ and $3^1$ place values; omitting them would produce $(12)_3=5$, a different number.
