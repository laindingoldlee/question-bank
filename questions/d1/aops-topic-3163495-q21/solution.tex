Use $B=(0,0),A=(4,0),C=(-3\sqrt2,3\sqrt2)$. The trisectors have equations $y=x$ and $x=0$. The line $AC$ has equation $3\sqrt2\,x+(4+3\sqrt2)y=12\sqrt2$. Hence $F=(\dfrac{12\sqrt2}{4+6\sqrt2},\dfrac{12\sqrt2}{4+6\sqrt2})$ and $G=(0,\dfrac{12\sqrt2}{4+3\sqrt2})$. Their determinant gives $[BFG]=\dfrac{144}{(4+6\sqrt2)(4+3\sqrt2)}=\boxed{\dfrac{468-324\sqrt2}{7}}$.

\end{asy}
