For $m\ge n$, subtracting $a^{m-n}(a^n-1)$ from $a^m-1$ gives $a^{m-n}-1$. Thus $\gcd(a^m-1,a^n-1)=\gcd(a^{m-n}-1,a^n-1)$. Repeating the Euclidean algorithm on the exponents eventually reaches $(d,0)$, where $d=\gcd(m,n)$. The final gcd is $\gcd(a^d-1,0)=a^d-1$.
