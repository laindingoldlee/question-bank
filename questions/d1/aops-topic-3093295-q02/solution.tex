Cauchy–Schwarz gives $\sum a_i^2\ge\dfrac{1}{n}$ and $\sum\dfrac{1}{a_i}\ge n^2$. Applying it again gives $\sum\dfrac{1}{a_i^2}\ge\dfrac{(\sum\dfrac{1}{a_i})^2}{n}\ge n^3$. Expanding, the target sum is at least $\dfrac{1}{n}+2n+n^3=\dfrac{(n^2+1)^2}{n}$. Equality holds precisely when every $a_i=\dfrac{1}{n}$.
