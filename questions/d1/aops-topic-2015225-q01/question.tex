On Monday, Crystal drives from San Antonio to Dallas at a constant speed. On her return trip on Tuesday, she initially drives $10\%$ faster than her Monday speed. However, during the last part of her trip, she runs into traffic and has to drive $30\%$ slower than her Monday speed. As a result, her Tuesday trip takes exactly as long as her Monday trip. What fraction of the distance of her Tuesday trip does she travel at the faster speed? Express your answer as a common fraction.
