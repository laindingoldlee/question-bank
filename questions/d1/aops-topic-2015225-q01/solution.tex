Take the distance to be $1$ and the Monday speed to be $1$, so Monday's trip takes time $1$. Let $f$ be the fraction of Tuesday's distance driven at speed $1.1$; the rest, $1-f$, is driven at speed $0.7$. Equal times give
\[
\dfrac{f}{1.1}+\dfrac{1-f}{0.7}=1 .
\]
Multiplying by $7.7$: $7f+11(1-f)=7.7$, so $4f=3.3$ and
\[
f=\boxed{\dfrac{33}{40}}.
\]
