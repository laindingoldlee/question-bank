Homer chooses from $2,4,6,8,10$ and Jethro from the $10$ odd numbers $1,3,\dots,19$, so there are $50$ equally likely pairs. For Homer's $2,4,6,8,10$, Jethro's number is larger for $9,8,7,6,5$ choices respectively, a total of $35$. The probability is $\dfrac{35}{50}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{10}}$.
