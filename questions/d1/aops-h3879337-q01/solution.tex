By Burnside's lemma, average the numbers of colourings fixed by the three rotations. The identity fixes $3^{n^2}$ colourings.

For $n=3$, the center is a grid vertex, so a nonidentity rotation fixes no individual cell. The nine cells form three 3-cycles, and each such rotation fixes $3^3$ colourings. The count is
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{3^9+2\cdot3^3}{3}=6579}.
\]
For $n=4$, exactly one central cell is fixed; the other fifteen form five 3-cycles. Each nonidentity rotation fixes $3^{1+5}$ colourings. The count is
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{3^{16}+2\cdot3^6}{3}=14349393}.
\]

\end{asy}
