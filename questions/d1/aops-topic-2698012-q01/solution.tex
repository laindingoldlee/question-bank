No. Since $abc=1$, $ab+bc+ca=\dfrac1a+\dfrac1b+\dfrac1c$. Take $a=b=\dfrac12$, $c=4$: then $ab+bc+ca=\dfrac14+2+2=\dfrac{17}4$, but $a+b+c=5$. So the inequality can fail; it holds for some triples (e.g. $a=b=2$, $c=\dfrac14$) and fails for others.
