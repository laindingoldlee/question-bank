Diagonal $AR$ of regular pentagon $PALIR$ is a side of regular pentagon $MAREB$, and quadrilateral $LIRA$ lies inside both pentagons. The side length of $PALIR$ is $PA=PR=2$ m. Let $\varphi=\dfrac{1+\sqrt5}{2}$ (the golden ratio). You may use that $RA=2\varphi$ m and that triangle $RAP$ has area $\sqrt{2+\varphi}$ m$^2$. The area of the heptagon $MALIREB$ can be written as $\sqrt{m+n\varphi}$ m$^2$ with integers $m$ and $n$. Find $m+2n$.
