The square has side $\sqrt{49}=7$ m. A diagonal $AC$ of square $ABCD$ is the hypotenuse of the right triangle $ABC$ with legs $7$ and $7$, so by the Pythagorean theorem
\[
AC=\sqrt{7^2+7^2}=\sqrt{98}=7\sqrt2 .
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0), B=(7,0), C=(7,7), D=(0,7);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(1));
draw(A--C,blue+linewidth(1));
draw((6.3,0)--(6.3,0.7)--(7,0.7));
label("$A$",A,SW); label("$B$",B,SE); label("$C$",C,NE); label("$D$",D,NW);
label("$7$",(A+B)/2,S); label("$7$",(B+C)/2,E);
label("$7\sqrt{2}$",(A+C)/2+(-0.3,0.3),NW,blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

So that diagonal of the parallelogram is $\boxed{7\sqrt2\text{ m}}\approx9.90$ m. (The other diagonal of the parallelogram is not determined by the data.)
