A basketball team has eight games next month and wins each game with probability $\dfrac{2}{3}$, independently of the other games.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What is the probability that the team has a winning streak of at least four games at some point during the month?
\item[(b)] A student used inclusion-exclusion and got
\[5\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^4-4\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^5+3\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^6-2\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^7+\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^8=\dfrac{4240}{6561},\]
reasoning that there are $5$, $4$, $3$, $2$, $1$ ways to win $4$, $5$, $6$, $7$, $8$ games in a row. What is wrong with this computation?
\end{enumerate}
