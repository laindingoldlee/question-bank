The distance is measured along the perpendicular from the point to the line. Write the line as $5x-y+7=0$; the distance from $(x_0,y_0)$ to $ax+by+c=0$ is $\dfrac{|ax_0+by_0+c|}{\sqrt{a^2+b^2}}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
pair Pt=(2,3);pair F=(-9/13,7-45/13);
draw((-2.3,0)--(3,0),Arrow(TeXHead));draw((0,-1)--(0,8.6),Arrow(TeXHead));
label("$x$",(3,0),E);label("$y$",(0,8.6),N);
draw((-1.6,-1)--(0.32,8.6),linewidth(0.9)+blue);
label("$y=5x+7$",(0.32,8.6),E,blue);
draw(Pt--F,dashed+red);
pair u=unit(Pt-F),v=unit((1,5));real r=0.25;draw(F+r*u--F+r*(u+v)--F+r*v,linewidth(0.6));
dot(Pt);dot(F);
label("$(2,3)$",Pt,E);label("$F$",F,W);
label("$d$",(Pt+F)/2,S,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Here
\[
d=\dfrac{|5\cdot2-3+7|}{\sqrt{5^2+1^2}}=\dfrac{14}{\sqrt{26}}=\boxed{\dfrac{7\sqrt{26}}{13}}\approx2.75 .
\]
Check with the foot of the perpendicular: the line through $(2,3)$ with slope $-\dfrac{1}{5}$ meets $y=5x+7$ at $F=\left(-\dfrac{9}{13},\dfrac{46}{13}\right)$, and $\left(2+\dfrac{9}{13}\right)^2+\left(3-\dfrac{46}{13}\right)^2=\dfrac{35^2+7^2}{169}=\dfrac{1274}{169}=\dfrac{98}{13}=\left(\dfrac{7\sqrt{26}}{13}\right)^2$.
