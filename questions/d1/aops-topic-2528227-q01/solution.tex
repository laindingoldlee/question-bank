Since $21^8=441^4$ and $131^5=131\cdot131^4$, it is enough to show that $\left(\dfrac{441}{131}\right)^4<131$.

First, $131\cdot3.37=441.47>441$, so $\dfrac{441}{131}<3.37$. Next, $3.37^2=11.3569<11.36$ and $11.36^2=129.0496<131$. Hence
\[\left(\dfrac{441}{131}\right)^4<3.37^4<11.36^2<131,\]
so $441^4<131^5$, i.e. $21^8<131^5$. $\blacksquare$

(The inequality is tight: $131^5=38579489651$ and $21^8=37822859361$, a ratio of about $1.02$.)
