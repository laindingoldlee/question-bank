Each such number uses all five digits exactly once, so its digit sum is $0+1+4+6+9=20\equiv2\pmod3$; hence every one of them leaves remainder $2$ on division by $3$.

The leading digit cannot be $0$, so there are $4$ choices for it and $4!=24$ arrangements of the rest: $96$ numbers. The sum of the remainders is $96\cdot2=\boxed{192}$.
