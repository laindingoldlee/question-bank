The first two conditions are $n\equiv-2\pmod{35}$, so $n=35k-2$. Modulo $9$, $-k-2\equiv1$, giving $k\equiv6\pmod9$. Thus $n\equiv208\pmod{315}$ and the least positive value is $\boxed{208}$.
