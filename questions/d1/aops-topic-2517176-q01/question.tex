Prove that the sets
\[A=\left\{\dfrac{3n+2}{3n+7}:n\in\mathbb N\right\}\qquad\text{and}\qquad B=\left\{\dfrac{3k+1}{3k+4}:k\in\mathbb N\right\}\]
are disjoint (whether or not $0\in\mathbb N$).
