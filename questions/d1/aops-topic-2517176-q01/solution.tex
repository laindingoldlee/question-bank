Suppose a number lies in both sets: $\dfrac{3n+2}{3n+7}=\dfrac{3k+1}{3k+4}$ for some nonnegative integers $n,k$. The denominators are positive, so we may cross-multiply:
\[(3n+2)(3k+4)=(3k+1)(3n+7).\]
Expanding, $9nk+12n+6k+8=9nk+21k+3n+7$, i.e.
\[9n+1=15k.\]
The right side is divisible by $3$, but the left side leaves remainder $1$ when divided by $3$. This contradiction shows that $A\cap B=\varnothing$. $\blacksquare$
