For each $k\ge1$, the terms with denominators $2^k,2^k+1,\ldots,2^{k+1}-1$ number $2^k$, and each is greater than $\dfrac{1}{2^{k+1}}$. Their sum is therefore greater than $\dfrac{1}{2}$. The partial sums containing $m$ complete such blocks exceed $\dfrac{m}{2}$, which is unbounded as $m$ grows. Hence the series diverges to $+\infty$.
