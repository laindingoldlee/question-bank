Find the error in the following ``proof'' that $2=1$.

Let $a=b$, where $b\ne0$. Multiplying both sides by $a$ gives $a^2=ab$. Subtracting $b^2$ from both sides gives $a^2-b^2=ab-b^2$. Factoring, $(a+b)(a-b)=b(a-b)$. Cancelling $a-b$ gives $a+b=b$. Since $a=b$, this says $2b=b$, and dividing by $b$ gives $2=1$.
