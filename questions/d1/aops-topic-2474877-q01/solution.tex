The steps up to $(a+b)(a-b)=b(a-b)$ are correct: both sides equal $0$, because $a-b=0$.

The error is the cancellation of $a-b$. Cancelling means dividing both sides by $a-b$, and $a-b=0$, so this is a division by zero. From $XZ=YZ$ one may conclude $X=Y$ only when $Z\ne0$; when $Z=0$ the equation holds for all $X$ and $Y$ and says nothing about them. (The last division by $b$ is fine, since $b\ne0$.)

So the error is $\boxed{\text{the division by }a-b=0}$.
