Write $M=m+22$ and its chosen divisors as $\dfrac{M}{u},\dfrac{M}{v}$. Both are proper divisors, so $u,v\ge2$. They cannot both equal two, since their sum would be $M$, not $M-22$. Thus $\dfrac{1}{u}+\dfrac{1}{v}\le\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{3}=\dfrac{5}{6}$, giving $M-22\le\dfrac{5}{6}M$ and $m\le110$. Equality holds for $M=132$, using divisors 66 and 44. The same reasoning gives $n\le120$, attained with divisors 72 and 48 of 144. Hence $\boxed{m+n=230}$, choice C.
