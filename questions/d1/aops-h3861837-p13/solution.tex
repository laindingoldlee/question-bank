At least two draws are required. If those agree, stop; otherwise consider the state of two distinct latest books. The next draw stops the process with probability $\dfrac{1}{6}+\dfrac{4}{6}=\dfrac{5}{6}$. With probability $\dfrac{1}{6}$ it equals the earlier of the two, leaving the same state. Expected further draws from that state are $D=1+\dfrac{D}{6}=\dfrac{6}{5}$. The initial expectation is $2+\dfrac{5}{6}D=\boxed{3}$.
