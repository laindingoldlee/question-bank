Take $B=(0,0),A=(1,0),C=(\dfrac{\sqrt3-1}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt3+1}{2})$. Because the circle passes through the origin, write its equation as $x^2+y^2-x-(3\sqrt3-4)y=0$; substitution of $A,C$ verifies it. Its centre is $O=(\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{3\sqrt3-4}{2})$. The tangents at $A$ and $C$ have equations $(A-O)\cdot D=\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $(C-O)\cdot D=1$, respectively. Solving these two linear equations gives $D=(\dfrac{3+2\sqrt3}{2},\dfrac{2+\sqrt3}{2})$. Hence $\dfrac{D_y}{D_x}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}$, and both coordinates are positive. Therefore $\boxed{\angle ABD=30^\circ}$.
