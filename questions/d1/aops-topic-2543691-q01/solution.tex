Suppose such an assignment $f$ exists. Let $m$ be a positive integer and consider the $(m+1)^n$ points of the cube $\{0,1,\dots,m\}^n$. Each of them can be reached from the origin by at most $nm$ steps between neighbours (increase one coordinate at a time). Each step changes the assigned tuple by $\pm1$ in one entry, so for each point $P$ of the cube, $f(P)-f(\mathbf 0)$ is an integer vector whose entries lie between $-nm$ and $nm$. There are at most $(2nm+1)^k$ such vectors. Since $f$ takes different values at different points,
\[(m+1)^n\le(2nm+1)^k\le\left(2n(m+1)\right)^k,\qquad\text{so}\qquad(m+1)^{n-k}\le(2n)^k.\]
But $n-k\ge1$, so the left side is unbounded as $m$ grows, while the right side is fixed. This contradiction shows that no such assignment exists. $\blacksquare$
