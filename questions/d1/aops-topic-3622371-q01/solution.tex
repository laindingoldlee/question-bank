The odd primes are $3,5,7$. Trial 1 avoids them with probability $(\dfrac{7}{10})^6$. Conditional on avoidance so far, the excluded label is always one of the seven non-odd-prime labels. Therefore each subsequent trial has six allowed non-odd-prime labels among nine allowed labels, and avoids odd primes with probability $(\dfrac{6}{9})^6=(\dfrac{2}{3})^6$. Multiplying conditional probabilities, total avoidance has probability $(\dfrac{7}{10})^6(\dfrac{2}{3})^{18}$. The requested probability is $\boxed{1-(\dfrac{7}{10})^6(\dfrac{2}{3})^{18}}$.
