In trial 1, six labels are independently and uniformly chosen from $1,2,\ldots,10$. One of the six positions is chosen uniformly to designate an excluded label. In each of trials 2, 3, and 4, six new independent uniform labels are chosen from $1,2,\ldots,10$ except the preceding trial's excluded label, and one position is again chosen uniformly as the next excluded label. What is the probability that an odd prime occurs among the 24 labels?
