At the end of last week, a baseball player's batting average (hits divided by at-bats) was $0.256$. One week later, after getting $12$ hits in $35$ at-bats, the player's average is $0.275$. How many hits and how many at-bats does the player now have for the season?

A student wrote $\dfrac{256x+12}{1000x+35}=\dfrac{3}{8}$. Explain what is wrong with this equation.
