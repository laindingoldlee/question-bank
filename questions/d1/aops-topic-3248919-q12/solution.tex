Let the outer side be $L$ and the smaller square side be $b$. Then $A$ has area $L(L-b)=80$ and $C$ has area $b(L-b)=30$. Their ratio gives $L=\dfrac{8}{3}b$. Consequently $30=b(\dfrac{8}{3}b-b)=\dfrac{5}{3}b^2$, so $\boxed{[B]=18}$.
