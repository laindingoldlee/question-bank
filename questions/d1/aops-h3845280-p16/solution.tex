Factoring gives $(x-y)(x+y+1)=304=2^4\cdot19$. Let $u=x-y>0$, $v=x+y+1$. Then $v>u+1$ and $u,v$ have opposite parity; conversely such a factor pair gives $x=\dfrac{u+v-1}{2}$, $y=\dfrac{v-u-1}{2}$.

The factor pairs with $u<v$ are $(1,304),(2,152),(4,76),(8,38),(16,19)$. Only the first and last have opposite parity, and both yield positive $y$. The corresponding $x$ values are $152$ and $17$. Their sum is $\boxed{169}$.
