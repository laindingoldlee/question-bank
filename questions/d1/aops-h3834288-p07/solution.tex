Every path visits $4047$ lattice points, including the endpoints. At each fixed step, reflecting paths across $y=x$ swaps $x,y$ and preserves the uniform path distribution. The two collection probabilities add to $\dfrac{x}{x+y}+\dfrac{y}{x+y}=1$, so the mean at every step is $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Linearity of expectation gives $\dfrac{4047}{2}$, hence $\boxed{a+b=4049}$.
