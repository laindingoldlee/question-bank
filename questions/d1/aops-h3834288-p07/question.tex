David is collecting coins in the coordinate plane. At any point $(x,y)$, he collects a coin with probability $\dfrac{x}{x+y}$ and moves to either $(x+1,y)$ or $(x,y+1)$. If he takes any route from $(1,1)$ to $(2024, 2024)$ with equal probability, the expected number of coins he will collect on his journey can be expressed as $\dfrac{a}{b}$, where $a$ and $b$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $a+b$. (Note: he can collect coins at both his start and end points)
