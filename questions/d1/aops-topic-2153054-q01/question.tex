A circle of radius $r$ rolls without slipping around the outside of a fixed circle of radius $R=nr$, where $n$ is a positive integer. It starts at the top of the fixed circle and goes once around it, back to its starting position.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] How many complete rotations does the rolling circle make?
\item[(b)] A student argues: ``The fixed circle has circumference $2\pi nr$ and the rolling circle $2\pi r$, so the rolling circle rotates $n$ times.'' What is wrong with this argument?
\end{enumerate}
