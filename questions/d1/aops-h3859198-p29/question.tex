Let \(ABC\) be an isosceles triangle with



\[
AB=AC,\qquad \angle BAC=36^\circ.
\]

A point \(P\) inside the triangle satisfies



\[
\angle APB=108^\circ,\qquad \angle APC=90^\circ.
\]

If \(D=AP\cap BC\), find



\[
\dfrac{CD}{BD}.
\]

\[
\textbf{(A)}\ \sqrt2
\qquad
\textbf{(B)}\ 2
\qquad
\textbf{(C)}\ \sqrt5
\qquad
\textbf{(D)}\ 3
\qquad
\textbf{(E)}\ \text{None of these}
\]
