Let $\theta=\angle BAP$, so $\angle PAC=36^\circ-\theta$. The sine rule in $ABP,ACP$, together with $AB=AC$, gives $\sin(72^\circ-\theta)=\sin72^\circ\sin(54^\circ+\theta)$. Expanding these sines and using the half-angle identities for $36^\circ,72^\circ$ simplifies to $\tan\theta=\dfrac{\sin36^\circ}{2+\cos36^\circ}$. Hence $\sin(36^\circ-\theta)=2\sin\theta$. Comparing areas $ABD,ACD$ with equal sides $AB,AC$ now gives $\dfrac{CD}{BD}=\dfrac{\sin(36^\circ-\theta)}{\sin\theta}=\boxed{2}$, choice B.
