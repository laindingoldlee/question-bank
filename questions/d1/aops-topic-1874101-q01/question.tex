Suppose $f(4)=8$. Then the point $(2,8)$ is on the graph of $y=f(2x)$, and the point $(5,8)$ is on the graph of $y=f(2x-6)$, so the point found on the second graph is $3$ units to the right of the point found on the first. Which of the following is true? Explain your answer.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] A mistake was made.
\item[(b)] It is a coincidence: not every point on $y=f(2x-6)$ is $3$ units to the right of a point on $y=f(2x)$.
\item[(c)] The graph of $y=f(2x-6)$ is the graph of $y=f(2x)$ shifted $3$ units to the right.
\end{enumerate}
Why is the shift $3$ and not $6$?
