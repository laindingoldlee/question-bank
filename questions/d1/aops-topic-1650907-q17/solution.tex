Write $40=p+q$ with primes $p\le q$, so $p\le20$. Check each prime $p\le20$:
\[
40-2=38,\ 40-3=37,\ 40-5=35,\ 40-7=33,\ 40-11=29,\ 40-13=27,\ 40-17=23,\ 40-19=21 .
\]
The values of $q$ that are prime are $37$, $29$ and $23$. So $40=3+37=11+29=17+23$, in $\boxed{3}$ ways.
