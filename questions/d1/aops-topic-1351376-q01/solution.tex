After $36$ games, twice as many wins as losses means wins $=\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot36=24$ and losses $=12$. They already have $5$ wins, so they need $24-5=\boxed{19}$ wins in the next $30$ games. (Then they lose $11$ of them, and $1+11=12$ losses in all.)
