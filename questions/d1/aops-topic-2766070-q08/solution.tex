A four-digit palindrome is $\overline{abba}=1001a+110b=11(91a+10b)$, so all $9\cdot10=90$ of them are multiples of $11$. The four-digit multiples of $11$ run from $1001=11\cdot91$ to $9999=11\cdot909$: there are $909-90=819$. The probability is $\dfrac{90}{819}=\boxed{\dfrac{10}{91}}$.
