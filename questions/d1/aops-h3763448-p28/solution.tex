The coefficient determinant is $k^2-1$, so the solution is unique exactly when $k\ne1,-1$. Subtracting the equations then gives $(k-1)(x-y)=0$, hence $x=y$, and substituting gives $\boxed{x=y=\dfrac{1}{k+1}}$. At $k=1$ there are infinitely many solutions $x+y=1$; at $k=-1$ adding the equations gives $0=2$, so there are none.
