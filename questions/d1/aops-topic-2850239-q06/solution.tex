At $x=5$, with $u=y^2$: $390625u^3-u-625=0$. The cubic $h(u)=390625u^3-u-625$ has $h(0)<0$, and for $u>0$ it decreases only while $u<\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3\cdot390625}}$ (where $h$ is still negative) and then increases to infinity, so it has exactly one positive root $u_0$. The points are $(5,\pm\sqrt{u_0})$.

The curve is symmetric under $y\mapsto-y$ (only $y^2$ and $y^6$ appear). Reflecting in the $x$-axis maps the tangent at $(5,\sqrt{u_0})$ to the tangent at $(5,-\sqrt{u_0})$ and negates its slope. (Explicitly, implicit differentiation gives $y'=-\dfrac{4x^3-8x^7y^6}{2y-6x^8y^5}$, which is odd in $y$.) So the sum of the slopes is $\boxed{0}$.
