Right triangle $ABC$ has $AB=2$ and hypotenuse $AC=4$. Let $D$ be the midpoint of $BC$. Let $BE$ be the altitude to line $AD$ in triangle $ABD$, and $DF$ the altitude to line $AC$ in triangle $ADC$. Write $BE^2-DF^2=\dfrac{a}{b}$ in lowest terms and find $a+b$.
