Use $B=(0,0),A=(2,0),C=(0,2\sqrt3),D=(0,\sqrt3)$. Both triangles $ABD,ADC$ have area $\sqrt3$. Since $AD=\sqrt7$ and $AC=4$, their altitudes are $BE=\dfrac{2\sqrt3}{\sqrt7}$ and $DF=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}$. Hence $BE^2-DF^2=\dfrac{12}{7}-\dfrac{3}{4}=\dfrac{27}{28}$ and $\boxed{a+b=55}$.

\end{asy}
