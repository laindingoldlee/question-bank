A student says: ``Negative numbers are not real, because you can have $1$ of something or $0$ of something, but not $-1$ of something.''
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Let $a$ and $b$ be whole numbers ($0,1,2,\dots$) with $a<b$. Show that the equation $b+x=a$ has no solution $x$ among the whole numbers, but has exactly one solution among the integers, and that this solution is negative. Solve $5+x=3$.
\item[(b)] Explain what is wrong with the student's argument.
\end{enumerate}
