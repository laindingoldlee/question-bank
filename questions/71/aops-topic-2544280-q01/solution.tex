\textbf{(a)} $243_{(-7)}=2\cdot49+4\cdot(-7)+3=98-28+3=\boxed{73}$.

\textbf{(b)} Divide repeatedly by $-2$, always choosing the remainder $0$ or $1$; the remainders are the digits from right to left:
\[10=(-2)(-5)+0,\quad-5=(-2)\cdot3+1,\quad3=(-2)(-1)+1,\quad-1=(-2)\cdot1+1,\quad1=(-2)\cdot0+1,\]
so $10=\boxed{11110_{(-2)}}$. Check: $16-8+4-2+0=10$. Similarly
\[-10=(-2)\cdot5+0,\quad5=(-2)(-2)+1,\quad-2=(-2)\cdot1+0,\quad1=(-2)\cdot0+1,\]
so $-10=\boxed{1010_{(-2)}}$. Check: $-8+0-2+0=-10$. (In a negative base, negative numbers need no minus sign.)
