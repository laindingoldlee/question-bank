Real coefficients force conjugate roots $3-i$ and $5+2i$. Since the polynomial is monic and quartic, $f(x)=((x-3)^2+1)((x-5)^2+4)$. Therefore $f(0)=10\cdot29=290$ and $f(1)=5\cdot20=100$, giving $\boxed{390}$.
