Lucas the pigeon has $n$ peanuts. When he puts them into piles of $11$, there are $2$ left over, and he eats those $2$. He then puts the remaining peanuts into piles of $7$, there are $6$ left over, and he eats those $6$ as well. The number of peanuts he has left is a positive perfect square. What is the smallest possible value of $n$?
