Using $\log_4u+\log_4v=\log_4(uv)$,
\[
\log_45+\log_425+\log_4100=\log_4(5\cdot25\cdot100)=\log_4 12500 .
\]
Since $12500=2^2\cdot5^5$ and $\log_4t=\dfrac{\log_2t}{2}$,
\[
\log_412500=\dfrac{\log_2\left(2^2\cdot5^5\right)}{2}=\dfrac{2+5\log_25}{2}=\boxed{1+\dfrac{5}{2}\log_25}\approx6.805 .
\]
