Divide: with $t=1+i$, $\dfrac{t^{10}-1}{t^6-1}=\dfrac{5}{2}$, i.e. $2t^{10}-5t^6+3=0$. The left ratio increases from $\dfrac{10}{6}$ (as $t\to1$) to infinity for $t>1$, so there is exactly one root $t>1$; there is no closed form, and numerically $t\approx1.18148$. Hence
\[i\approx0.1815=18.15\%,\qquad C=\dfrac{800}{t^6-1}\approx\boxed{465.14}.\]
