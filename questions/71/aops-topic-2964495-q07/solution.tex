At the end of $t$ full seconds there are $1000-9t$ leaves. After $110$ seconds there are $10$; during the $111$th second the raking removes these $10$ before the next leaf falls. So the yard is first empty after $\boxed{111}$ seconds (not $\dfrac{1000}9\approx112$).
