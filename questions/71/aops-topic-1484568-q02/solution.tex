A four-digit number with a digit appearing exactly three times has digits $d,d,d,e$ with $e\ne d$. At least one digit is $0$.
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{The repeated digit is $0$:} the number has three zeros, and the first digit is the other digit, so it is $e000$ with $e=1,\dots,9$: $9$ numbers.
\item \emph{The single digit is $0$:} the repeated digit $d$ is one of $1,\dots,9$, and the $0$ is in one of the last three places (it cannot lead): $9\cdot3=27$ numbers.
\end{itemize}
The total is $9+27=\boxed{36}$.
