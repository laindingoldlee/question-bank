In 2016 the allowed day after month 4 is 9; the other possible month 9 would require a square day strictly between 9 and 16, which does not exist. The next square year suffix is 25. In that year the earliest eligible month is 1 and earliest eligible day is 4. Thus the next date is $\boxed{\text{January 4, 2025}}$, with components $(1,4,25)$.
