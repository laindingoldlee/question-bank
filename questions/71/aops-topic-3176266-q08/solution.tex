Put $BE=x$, so $AE=2-x$. Pythagoras in right triangle $DAE$ gives $DE^2=4+(2-x)^2$. The given relation gives $DE=2+x$. Squaring and simplifying yields $8-4x+x^2=4+4x+x^2$, so $x=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$. This lies on the required segment.
