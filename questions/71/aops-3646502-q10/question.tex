Let $ABCD$ be a square with side length $12$. A circle with radius $5$ is drawn inside of the square such that it is tangent to $\overline{AB}$ and $\overline{BC}$. A line passing through $D$ and tangent to the circle intersects $\overline{BC}$ at $X$. The area of $\vartriangle CDX$ can be expressed as $p - q\sqrt{r}$, where $p$, $q$, and $r$ are positive integers and $r$ is not divisible by any perfect square. Find $p + q + r$.
