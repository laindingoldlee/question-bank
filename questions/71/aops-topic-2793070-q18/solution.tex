Adding the equations gives $2x^2-xy-\sqrt3xz=0$. Since $x>0$, $2x=y+\sqrt3z$. Substituting $x=\dfrac{y+\sqrt3z}{2}$ into the first equation gives $\dfrac{3}{4}(y^2+z^2)=z^2$, so $3y^2=z^2$. Positivity gives $\dfrac{y}{z}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}}$. This is attainable, for example with $(x,y,z)=(2,1,\sqrt3)$, and is in fact constant.
