The outer base lines are perpendicular to the bisectors of their respective apex angles. Those bisectors differ in direction by $2\theta$, so the acute angle between the bases is $2\theta$ (because $0<2\theta<90^\circ$). It is supplementary to the given obtuse angle and hence equals sixty degrees. Therefore $\boxed{\theta=30^\circ}$.
