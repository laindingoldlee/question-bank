Let there be $n$ marbles. The numbers of red and blue marbles are $\dfrac{3n}{13}$ and $\dfrac{2n}{5}$, which are integers, so $65\mid n$. Since $0<n<100$, $n=65$: there are $15$ red, $26$ blue and $65-15-26=24$ green marbles. Then
\[
P(\text{green, then red})=\dfrac{24}{65}\cdot\dfrac{15}{64}=\dfrac{360}{4160}=\boxed{\dfrac{9}{104}}.
\]
