A bowl contains only red, blue and green marbles. The probability of selecting a red marble from the bowl is $\dfrac{3}{13}$ and the probability of selecting a blue marble is $\dfrac{2}{5}$. There are fewer than $100$ marbles in the bowl. Two marbles are selected at random, one after the other, without replacement. What is the probability that the first is green and the second is red? Express your answer as a common fraction.
