Let $S=a+b+c$, $Q=ab+bc+ca$, $P=abc$. The reciprocal equations give $P=S$ and $Q=\dfrac{25}{9}S$. The cubic-sum identity yields $S^3-\dfrac{25}{3}S^2+3S=25$, or $(3S-25)(S^2+3)=0$. Because $S$ is real, $S=\dfrac{25}{3}$, hence $m+n=\boxed{28}$.
