For digits $a,b,c$ with $a\ne0$, call the three-digit number $\overline{abc}=100a+10b+c$ \emph{clock-perfect} if
\begin{itemize}
\item it can be shown on a $12$-hour clock as the time $a{:}bc$ (ignoring the colon; hours and minutes only), and
\item it is a perfect square.
\end{itemize}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] List the ten smallest clock-perfect numbers.
\item[(b)] How many of the perfect squares $1^2,2^2,\dots,30^2$ are clock-perfect?
\item[(c)] During one $24$-hour day, for how many minutes in total does a $12$-hour clock show a clock-perfect number?
\end{enumerate}
