Real numbers $a$ and $b$ satisfy $a^2+ab+b^2=1$, and $t=ab-a^2-b^2$.

A student argues: ``Adding the equations gives $2ab=1+t$, and combining them gives $(a+b)^2=\dfrac{3+t}{2}$, so $t\ge-3$. Also $1-t=(a-b)^2+(a+b)^2\ge0$, so $t\le1$. Hence $-3\le t\le 1$.''

Find the error in this argument, and determine the exact set of possible values of $t$.
