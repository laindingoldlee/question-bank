Write $k=3a$ and $\ell=3b$ with $\gcd(a,b)=1$. Then
\[
\operatorname{lcm}[k,\ell]=\dfrac{k\ell}{\gcd(k,\ell)}=\dfrac{9ab}{3}=3ab .
\]
Since $k\ge1000$, $a\ge\dfrac{1000}{3}$, so $a\ge334$, and similarly $b\ge334$. Also $a\ne b$ (otherwise $\gcd(a,b)=a>1$). So one of them is at least $334$ and the other at least $335$, giving $ab\ge334\cdot335$. This is attained by $a=334$, $b=335$, which are coprime (consecutive integers). Then $k=1002$, $\ell=1005$, $\gcd(1002,1005)=3$, and
\[
\operatorname{lcm}[k,\ell]=3\cdot334\cdot335=\boxed{335670}.
\]
