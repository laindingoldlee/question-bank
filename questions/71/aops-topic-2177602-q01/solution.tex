Rewrite the equation as $x^{2017}-1=2x-2$, that is,
\[(x-1)\left(x^{2016}+x^{2015}+\cdots+x+1\right)=2(x-1).\]
Since $x\ne1$, we may divide by $x-1$:
\[x^{2016}+x^{2015}+\cdots+x+1=\boxed{2}.\]
(Such $x$ exist: $x^{2017}-2x+1$ has degree $2017$, and $x=1$ is only a simple root because the derivative there is $2017-2\ne0$.)
