Each exterior angle is 10 degrees. Their sum is 360 degrees, so there are $\boxed{36}$ sides.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
path poly=dir(0);for(int k=1;k<36;++k)poly=poly--dir(10*k);poly=poly--cycle;draw(poly);pair V=dir(0);draw(V--(V+1.8*(V-dir(-10))),dashed);label("$170^\circ$",(.75,0),W);label("36 equal sides",(0,0));
\end{asy}
