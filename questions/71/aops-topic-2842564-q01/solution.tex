Rearrange to $ yx^2-x+y-1=0 $. For $ y\ne0 $, a real $ x $ exists exactly when the discriminant $ 1-4y(y-1)$ is nonnegative, giving $\dfrac{1-\sqrt2}{2}\le y\le\dfrac{1+\sqrt2}{2}$. The value $ y=0 $ also occurs at $ x=-1 $. Both endpoints are nonzero and give a real double root, so they are attained. Their sum is $\boxed1 $.
