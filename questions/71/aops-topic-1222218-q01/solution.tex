At $4{:}00$ the hour hand is $120^\circ$ ahead. The minute hand moves $6^\circ$ per minute and the hour hand $0.5^\circ$, so the gap closes at $5.5^\circ$ per minute. It closes in $\dfrac{120}{5.5}=\boxed{\dfrac{240}{11}}=21\dfrac{9}{11}$ minutes.
