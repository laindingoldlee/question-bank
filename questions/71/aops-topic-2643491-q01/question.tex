Jaber writes the positive integers in a new way: the numbers $1$ to $10$ are written $1,2,10,11,12,20,21,22,100,101$, and he continues in the same way, using only the digits $0$, $1$ and $2$. In this notation, what is the difference $12201-212$? Give the answer both in ordinary (base-ten) notation and in Jaber's notation.
