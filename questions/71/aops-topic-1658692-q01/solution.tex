A combination is a triple $(x,y,z)$ with $0\le x,y,z\le5$, at least one entry $0$, and at most one entry $\ge3$. Count by the number of zero entries.

\textbf{Three zeros:} $(0,0,0)$, $1$ combination.

\textbf{Exactly two zeros:} choose the nonzero path ($3$ ways) and its count ($5$ ways): $15$ combinations. (One entry $\ge3$ is allowed.)

\textbf{Exactly one zero:} choose the zero path ($3$ ways); the other two entries are in $\{1,\dots,5\}$ with at most one of them $\ge3$. Of the $25$ ordered pairs, the $3\cdot3=9$ with both $\ge3$ are forbidden, leaving $16$. This gives $3\cdot16=48$ combinations.

In total $1+15+48=\boxed{64}$.
