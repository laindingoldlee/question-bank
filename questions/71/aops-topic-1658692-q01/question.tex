A tower in a game has three upgrade paths. On each path it can receive from $0$ to $5$ upgrades. It may not have upgrades on all three paths, and if it has $3$ or more upgrades on one path, it may not have $3$ or more upgrades on any other path. How many upgrade combinations (triples of upgrade counts) are possible?
