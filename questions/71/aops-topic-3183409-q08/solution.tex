Write the palindrome $abcdcba$. The digits $a,b,c$ must be distinct, $a\ne0$, and $d$ must differ from all three. Increasing the four-digit prefix gives the seven smallest eligible prefixes 1027, 1037, 1047, 1057, 1067, 1072, 1073. To see completeness, the smallest possible initial pair is 10; then $c=2,3,4,5,6$ forces $d=7$, while $c=7$ permits $d=2,3,\ldots$. Therefore the answer is $\boxed{1073701}$.
