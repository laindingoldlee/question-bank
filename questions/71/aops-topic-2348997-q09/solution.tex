Either $x^2-20x-1=20$, i.e. $x^2-20x-21=(x-21)(x+1)=0$, or $x^2-20x-1=-20$, i.e. $x^2-20x+19=(x-1)(x-19)=0$. The four values $21,-1,1,19$ are distinct, and their sum is $\boxed{40}$.
