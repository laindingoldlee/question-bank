A pie is served to $p$ people in turn ($p,d>1$ integers). Each person takes a fraction of the \emph{remaining} pie chosen uniformly at random from $\left(0,\dfrac1d\right)$, independently of the others. What fraction of the original pie is expected to have been eaten at the end, in terms of $p$ and $d$?
