For $ n\ge1 $, $(10^n-1)^2=10^{2n}-2\cdot10^n+1 $ has $ n-1 $ nines, then an 8, then $ n-1 $ zeroes, then a 1. The digit sum is $ 9(n-1)+8+1=9n $. At $ n=2017 $, this is $\boxed{18153}$.
