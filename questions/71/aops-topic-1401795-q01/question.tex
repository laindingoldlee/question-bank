The integers $2,2,5,5,8,9$ are written on six cards. Any number (at least one) of the six cards is chosen, and the sum of the integers on these cards is found. For example, the integers $1$ and $30$ cannot be obtained as such sums. How many of the integers from $1$ to $31$ cannot be obtained?
