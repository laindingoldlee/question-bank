Use $B=(-3,0),C=(3,0),A=(0,\sqrt{15})$, so $M=(-1,0)$. The circumcenter is $O=(0,k)$, where equality of distances gives $9+k^2=(\sqrt{15}-k)^2$, hence $k=\dfrac{\sqrt{15}}{5}$. Its radius is $R=\dfrac{4\sqrt{15}}{5}$.

The indicated arc is below $BC$. A circle tangent to $BC$ and touching that arc must lie below the line, so its center is $X=(-1,-r)$. Internal tangency gives $OX=R-r$, hence
\[
1+(r+k)^2=(R-r)^2
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
2r(R+k)=R^2-k^2-1=8.
\]
Since $R+k=\sqrt{15}$,
\[
\boxed{r=\dfrac{4}{\sqrt{15}}=\dfrac{4\sqrt{15}}{15}}.
\]

\end{asy}
