A scale of $1:500{,}000$ means $1$ cm on the map represents $500{,}000$ cm. So the actual distance is
\[
18\cdot500{,}000=9{,}000{,}000\text{ cm}=90{,}000\text{ m}=\boxed{90\text{ km}} .
\]
