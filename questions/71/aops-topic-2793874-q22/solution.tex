After every even-denominator transfer $\dfrac{1}{2k}$ from cup 1, the cups each contain half the milk. This holds for $k=1$. The following transfer gives cup 1 $\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{2(2k+1)}=\dfrac{k+1}{2k+1}$. The next transfer of the fraction $\dfrac{1}{2k+2}$ multiplies that amount by $\dfrac{2k+1}{2k+2}$, restoring half. For the last transfer $2k+1=101$, cup 1 has $\boxed{\dfrac{51}{101}}$ of the initial milk.
