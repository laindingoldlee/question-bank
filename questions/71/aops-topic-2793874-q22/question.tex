Cup 1 initially contains one unit of milk and cup 2 is empty. Transfer half of cup 1 to cup 2, then one-third of cup 2 to cup 1, then one-fourth of cup 1 to cup 2, continuing alternately through transferring $\dfrac{1}{101}$ of cup 2 to cup 1. How much milk is then in cup 1?
