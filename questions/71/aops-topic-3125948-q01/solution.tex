Let $t=\sqrt{x}\ge0$. Then $t^2+15t-4=0$, so $t=\dfrac{-15+\sqrt{241}}{2}$; the other root is negative. Squaring gives $\boxed{x=\dfrac{233-15\sqrt{241}}{2}}$. The chosen $t$ is positive because $241>225$, and substituting verifies the original equation.
