Let the yield before be $y$ tons per acre; after, it is $y+4$. Then
\[
320(y+4)=400y+640\iff320y+1280=400y+640\iff80y=640,
\]
so $y=8$. Before the new methods $\boxed{8}$ tons were collected per acre, and after them $\boxed{12}$ tons. (Check: $320\cdot12=3840=400\cdot8+640$.)
