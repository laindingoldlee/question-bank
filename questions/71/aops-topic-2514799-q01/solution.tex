$10!=2^8\cdot3^4\cdot5^2\cdot7$ (the exponent of $2$ is $5+2+1=8$, of $3$ is $3+1=4$, of $5$ is $2$). Also $\log_{10}5=\log_{10}\dfrac{10}{2}=1-a$. So
\[\log_{10}(10!)=8a+4b+2(1-a)+c=\boxed{2+6a+4b+c}.\]
