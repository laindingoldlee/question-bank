Only S and A can supply mirrored pairs, at most one pair each. A one-letter palindrome has six choices. With exactly one mirrored pair, choose S or A: an S pair permits no center or any of six remaining letter types, giving seven; an A pair permits no center or any of five remaining types, giving six. With both mirrored pairs, their order in the left half has two choices, and the center is absent or is one of S,U,Y,B,K, giving six choices. Thus the total is $6+7+6+2\cdot6=\boxed{31}$.
