The perfect squares among $1,\dots,5$ are $1$ and $4$, so the coin is flipped with probability $\dfrac{2}{5}$. Then it shows heads with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. The probability is $\dfrac{2}{5}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{5}}$.
