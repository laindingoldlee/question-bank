Any three sphere points lie in a closed hemisphere. If the first two radius vectors are linearly independent, choose a nonzero normal perpendicular to both and orient it so that its dot product with the third is nonnegative. If they are dependent, choose any nonzero vector perpendicular to them, again orienting it to make the third dot product nonnegative. The hemisphere defined by nonnegative dot product contains all three, including boundary points. Thus every triple qualifies, giving $\boxed{\binom{100}3=161700}$.
