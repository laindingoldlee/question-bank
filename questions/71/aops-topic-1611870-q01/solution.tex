The square of a binomial $(p+q)^2=p^2+2pq+q^2$ has two square terms and a middle term $2pq$. Here $25a^2=(5a)^2$ and $\dfrac{1}{4}b^2=\left(\dfrac {b}{2}\right)^2$, so with $p=5a$ and $q=\pm\dfrac {b}{2}$,
\[
\left(5a\pm\dfrac {b}{2}\right)^2=25a^2\pm5ab+\dfrac{1}{4}b^2 .
\]
So $\boxed{M=5ab\ \text{or}\ M=-5ab}$.

No other monomial works: if one of the given terms were the middle term $2pq$, the remaining square term $M$ would have to be $q^2$ with $2pq=25a^2$ and $p^2=\dfrac{1}{4}b^2$ (or the same with the roles of $25a^2$ and $\dfrac{1}{4}b^2$ exchanged), which forces $q=\pm\dfrac{25a^2}{b}$ (or $\pm\dfrac{b^2}{20a}$), not a polynomial.
