Put $a=1+x,b=1+y,c=1+z$. Adding the first two equations and $3$ gives $(a+b+c)(\dfrac{1}{a}+\dfrac{1}{b}+\dfrac{1}{c})=\dfrac{10}{3}+\dfrac{41}{12}+3=\dfrac{39}{4}$. Since the reciprocal sum is $\dfrac{9}{5}$, $a+b+c=(\dfrac{39}{4})(\dfrac{5}{9})=\dfrac{65}{12}$. Therefore $x+y+z=\dfrac{65}{12}-3=\dfrac{29}{12}$ and $\boxed{m+n=41}$.
