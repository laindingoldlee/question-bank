The number of trailing zeros is the exponent of $10$, which is the smaller of the exponents of $2$ and $5$; there are far more factors $2$ than $5$, so we count factors of $5$ (Legendre's formula):
\[
\begin{aligned}
100!&:\ 20+4=24,\\
200!&:\ 40+8+1=49,\\
300!&:\ 60+12+2=74.
\end{aligned}
\]
The total is $24+49+74=\boxed{147}$.
