Write $N=\sum_{i=0}^r a_i240^i$, where $0\le a_i<240$. Since $240\equiv1\pmod{239}$, we have $N\equiv\sum a_i\pmod{239}$. Also $1434=6\cdot239$ and $\gcd(6,239)=1$, so divisibility by both factors is equivalent to divisibility by their product. Finally $22944=95\cdot240+144$, with digit sum $239$, and $22944$ is divisible by $6$. Hence it is divisible by $1434$ (indeed the quotient is $16$).
