The double-angle identity gives $\tan^2t\tan2t=\tan2t-2\tan t$. Divide this identity by $2^r$ with $t=2^r\alpha$ and sum. All intermediate tangent terms cancel, leaving $\boxed{f_n(\alpha)=2^{-n}\tan(2^{n+1}\alpha)-2\tan\alpha}$. This holds whenever all the original tangents are defined. In particular, $f_4 (\dfrac{\pi}{3}) = - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{16} - 2 \sqrt{3} = \boxed{- \dfrac{33 \sqrt{3}}{16}}$.
