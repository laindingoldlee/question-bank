Insert a digit into one of the five gaps around 2012. There are $5\cdot10-1=49$ valid insertion operations, excluding a leading zero. The only duplications occur when the inserted digit equals an adjacent original digit: inserting 2 on either side of the first 2;0 on either side of 0;1 on either side of 1; or 2 on either side of the last 2. These are four disjoint duplicate pairs, so the number of distinct integers is $49-4=\boxed{45}$.
