Let $N$ be the number of apples. Then $N-7$ is a positive multiple of $4$, of $5$ and of $6$, hence a positive multiple of $\operatorname{lcm}(4,5,6)=60$. The least possibility is $N-7=60$, so $N=\boxed{67}$. (Then she has $15$, $12$ or $10$ friends' worth of apples to hand out respectively.)
