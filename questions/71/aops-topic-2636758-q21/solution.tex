The longest side is the hypotenuse. $m^2+(m+17)^2=(m+25)^2$ gives $m^2-16m-336=0$, i.e. $(m-28)(m+12)=0$, so $m=28$ (the triangle $28,45,53$). Similarly $n^2+(n+23)^2=(n+32)^2$ gives $n^2-18n-495=(n-33)(n+15)=0$, so $n=33$ (the triangle $33,56,65$). Then $\left\lfloor\dfrac{61}{3}\right\rfloor=\boxed{20}$.
