Let $m$ be the positive integer for which the triangle with side lengths $m$, $m+17$, $m+25$ has a right angle, and let $n$ be the positive integer for which the triangle with side lengths $n$, $n+23$, $n+32$ has a right angle. Find $\left\lfloor\dfrac{m+n}{3}\right\rfloor$.
