Let $D=3\cdot5\cdot7\cdots99$, which is not divisible by the prime $101$. The sum of all terms except the last is $\dfrac{K}{D}$ for some integer $K$. If the whole sum were the integer $N$, then $101K+102D=101DN$. Reducing modulo $101$ gives $D\equiv0\pmod{101}$, a contradiction. Hence the sum is not an integer.
