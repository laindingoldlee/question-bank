Let $E_n$ be the expectation with $n$ balls remaining, one blue. The probability a draw misses blue is $\dfrac{n-3}{n}$, and then exactly one red ball is removed. Therefore $E_3=1$ and $E_n=1+\dfrac{n-3}{n}E_{n-1}$. Inductively $E_n=\dfrac{n+1}{4}$: substituting $E_{n-1}=\dfrac{n}{4}$ gives $1+\dfrac{n-3}{4}=\dfrac{n+1}{4}$. Initially $n=16$, so $\boxed{E_{16}=\dfrac{17}{4}}$.
