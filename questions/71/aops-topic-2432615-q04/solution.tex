The functions are only defined at positive integers $x$ (because of $\varphi$) for which the denominator $1728-\varphi(x)^3$ is not zero, i.e. $\varphi(x)\ne12$.

At such $x$ the first two terms of $f$ and $g$ are the same numbers, and $\varphi(x+24)=\varphi(|x|+24)$ because $x>0$. So $f(x)=g(x)$ exactly when
\[26x^3-34x^2+18x+1=25x^3+17x^2-668x+1177\iff x^3-51x^2+686x-1176=0.\]
Trying divisors of $1176$, $x=2$ is a root, and
\[x^3-51x^2+686x-1176=(x-2)\left(x^2-49x+588\right)=(x-2)(x-21)(x-28).\]
But $\varphi(21)=\varphi(3)\varphi(7)=2\cdot6=12$ and $\varphi(28)=\varphi(4)\varphi(7)=2\cdot6=12$, so at $x=21$ and $x=28$ the denominator $1728-12^3$ is $0$ and $f$, $g$ are undefined there. At $x=2$ everything is defined ($\varphi(2)=1$, $2!!=2$), and $f(2)=g(2)$.

So the graphs meet only at $x=2$, and the answer is $\boxed{2}$ (not $28$).
