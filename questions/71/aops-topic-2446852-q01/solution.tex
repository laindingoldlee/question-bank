\textbf{(a)} $(n+1)^3=(n+1)\left(n^2+2n+1\right)=n^3+2n^2+n+n^2+2n+1=n^3+3n^2+3n+1$. $\blacksquare$

\textbf{(b)} With $n=17$: $18^3=4913+3\cdot289+3\cdot17+1=4913+867+51+1=\boxed{5832}$.

With $n=18$: $19^3=5832+3\cdot324+3\cdot18+1=5832+972+54+1=\boxed{6859}$.

\textbf{(c)} The four terms are correct, but they were added wrongly: $5832+972=6804$, $6804+54=6858$ and $6858+1=6859$, not $6853$. (Check: $19^3=361\cdot19=6859$.)
