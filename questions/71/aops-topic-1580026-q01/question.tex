You have an unlimited supply of $1\times1$ squares. Show that for every positive integer $n$ you can form a square of area $n$ by cutting $n$ of these unit squares along straight lines and rearranging the pieces without overlap. (For example, two unit squares can be cut and rearranged into a square of side $\sqrt2$.)
