The minimum is $m-\dfrac{9}{4}$ at $x=-\dfrac{3}{2}$. Tangency requires $m-\dfrac{9}{4}=-3$, so $\boxed{m=-\dfrac{3}{4}}$.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

\end{asy}
