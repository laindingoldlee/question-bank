Group the terms with $b^2$ and the terms with $c^2$:
\[-b^2\left(a^2-2ab+b^2\right)+c^2\left(a^2-2ab+b^2\right)=(a-b)^2\left(c^2-b^2\right).\]
So the expression factors as
\[\boxed{(a-b)^2(c-b)(c+b)}.\]
