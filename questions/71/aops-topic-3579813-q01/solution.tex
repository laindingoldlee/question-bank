The overbar denotes complex conjugation: it changes the sign of the imaginary part. Thus $\overline z=\boxed{3+4i}$, $z+\overline z=\boxed{6}$, and $z\overline z=(3-4i)(3+4i)=9+16=\boxed{25}$.
