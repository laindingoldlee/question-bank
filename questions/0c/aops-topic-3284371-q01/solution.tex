Substitution gives $4a+9b=5$ and $8a+3b=7$. Doubling the first and subtracting the second yields $15b=3$, so $b=\dfrac{1}{5}$ and $a=\dfrac{4}{5}$. Hence $\boxed{a+b=1}$.
