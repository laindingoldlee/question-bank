Two players play a game $2160$ times. Each time, Sam rolls two red dice and one blue die. If the product of the red numbers divided by the blue number is an integer, Cat gives Sam $1$ cookie; otherwise Sam gives Cat $2$ cookies. What is the expected net number of cookies Sam owes Cat after all $2160$ games?
