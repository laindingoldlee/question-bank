Let $p$ be the probability that the blue number $c$ divides the product $ab$ of the red numbers. Counting pairs $(a,b)$ for each $c$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $c=1$: all $36$.
\item $c=2$: $ab$ even: $36-9=27$.
\item $c=3$: $3\mid a$ or $3\mid b$: $36-16=20$.
\item $c=4$: both even ($9$) or one of them is $4$ with the other odd ($2\cdot3=6$): $15$.
\item $c=5$: $36-25=11$.
\item $c=6$: $ab$ divisible by $2$ and $3$: $36-9-16+4=15$.
\end{itemize}
So $p=\dfrac{36+27+20+15+11+15}{216}=\dfrac{124}{216}=\dfrac{31}{54}$.

Each game Sam's expected net payment to Cat is $2(1-p)-p=2-3p=2-\dfrac{31}{18}=\dfrac5{18}$. Over $2160$ games: $2160\cdot\dfrac5{18}=\boxed{600}$ cookies.
