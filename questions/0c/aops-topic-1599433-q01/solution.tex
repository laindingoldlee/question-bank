Let the rectangle be $ABCD$ with $AB=CD=m$, $BC=DA=n$, and rotate about the diagonal $AC$, of length $d=\sqrt{m^2+n^2}$. The solid is swept out by the triangles $ABC$ and $ACD$, which lie on opposite sides of $AC$. Rotating $ACD$ gives the same solid as rotating its mirror image $ACD'$ in the line $AC$, which lies on the same side as $B$. So the solid is obtained by rotating the union of triangles $ABC$ and $AD'C$, and its radius at each point of $AC$ is the larger of the two triangles' heights there.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real m=4,n=3,d=5,h=m*n/d;
pair A=(0,0),C=(d,0),B=(m*m/d,h),D=(n*n/d,-h),Dp=(n*n/d,h),M=(d/2,h*d*d/(2*m*m));
fill(A--Dp--M--B--C--(B.x,-h)--(d/2,-M.y)--(n*n/d,-h)--cycle,paleblue);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(1));
draw((-0.4,0)--(d+0.4,0),dashed);
draw(A--Dp--C,gray+dashed);
draw(A--Dp--M--B--C,blue+linewidth(1.1));
draw(A--(n*n/d,-h)--(d/2,-M.y)--(B.x,-h)--C,blue+dashed);
dot(Dp,gray);dot(M,blue);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$B$",B,N);label("$D$",D,S);label("$D'$",Dp,N,gray);label("$M$",M,N,blue);
label("$m$",(A+B)/2,SE);label("$n$",(B+C)/2,NE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

\textbf{The profile.} Both triangles have height $h=\dfrac{mn}{d}$ over $AC$. The foot of $B$ is at distance $\dfrac{m^2}{d}$ from $A$ and the foot of $D'$ at distance $\dfrac{n^2}{d}\le\dfrac {d}{2}$ (as $AD'=n$). The two triangles are mirror images in the perpendicular bisector of $AC$, so their sides $D'C$ and $AB$ cross at the point $M$ above the midpoint of $AC$. On the half from $A$ to the midpoint the profile is $A\to D'\to M$: it rises to $h$ and then falls along $D'C$. At the midpoint, $D'C$ has height
\[
r=h\cdot\dfrac{\dfrac {d}{2}}{d-\dfrac{n^2}{d}}=h\cdot\dfrac{d^2}{2(d^2-n^2)}=h\cdot\dfrac{d^2}{2m^2}=\dfrac{nd}{2m}.
\]
\textbf{Volume of half the solid.} It is a cone of radius $h$ and height $\dfrac{n^2}{d}$ together with a frustum of radii $h$ and $r$ and height $t=\dfrac {d}{2}-\dfrac{n^2}{d}=\dfrac{m^2-n^2}{2d}$:
\[
\dfrac{V}{2}=\dfrac{\pi}{3}h^2\cdot\dfrac{n^2}{d}+\dfrac{\pi t}{3}\left(h^2+hr+r^2\right).
\]
Here
\[
h^2+hr+r^2=n^2\left(\dfrac{m^2}{d^2}+\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{d^2}{4m^2}\right)=\dfrac{n^2\left(4m^4+2m^2d^2+d^4\right)}{4m^2d^2}=\dfrac{n^2\left(7m^4+4m^2n^2+n^4\right)}{4m^2d^2},
\]
using $d^2=m^2+n^2$. Hence
\[
V=\dfrac{2\pi n^2}{3d^3}\left(m^2n^2+\dfrac{(m^2-n^2)(7m^4+4m^2n^2+n^4)}{8m^2}\right)=\dfrac{\pi n^2\left(7m^6+5m^4n^2-3m^2n^4-n^6\right)}{12m^2d^3}.
\]
The bracket vanishes at $m^2=-n^2$, so it has the factor $m^2+n^2=d^2$: $7m^6+5m^4n^2-3m^2n^4-n^6=(m^2+n^2)(7m^4-2m^2n^2-n^4)$. Therefore
\[
V=\boxed{\dfrac{\pi n^2\left(7m^4-2m^2n^2-n^4\right)}{12m^2\sqrt{m^2+n^2}}}\qquad(m\ge n).
\]
\textbf{Check.} For a square, $m=n$: $V=\dfrac{4\pi n^6}{12n^2\cdot\sqrt2\,n}=\dfrac{\pi n^3}{3\sqrt2}$, which is the double cone of radius $\dfrac{n}{\sqrt2}$ and total height $n\sqrt2$, as it should be.
