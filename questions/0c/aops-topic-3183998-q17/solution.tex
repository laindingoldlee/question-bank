Let the one-way distance be $D$ and walking speed $v$. The bicycle plan takes $\dfrac{2rD}{100v}+\dfrac{2D}{(\dfrac{5}{2})v}$, including the initial walk and return. Walking the whole round trip takes $\dfrac{2D}{v}$. Comparison gives $\dfrac{r}{50}+\dfrac{4}{5}\le2$, or $r\le60$. The largest value is $\boxed{60}$.
