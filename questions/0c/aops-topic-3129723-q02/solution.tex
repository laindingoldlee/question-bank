There are $8!$ unrestricted circular arrangements. For a specified forbidden pair, treating it as a block gives $2\cdot7!$ arrangements. When both forbidden pairs are adjacent, there are two blocks with two internal orders each, giving $4\cdot6!$. Inclusion–exclusion gives $8!-4\cdot7!+4\cdot6!=\boxed{23040}$.
