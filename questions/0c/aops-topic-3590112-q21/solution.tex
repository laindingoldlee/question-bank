The polynomial $Q(x)=xP(x)-1$ has degree at most $2016$ and roots $1,\ldots,2016$, so $Q(x)=c\prod_{k=1}^{2016}(x-k)$. At $x=0$, $-1=c\,2016!$, hence $c=-\dfrac{1}{2016!}$. At $2017$, the product is $2016!$, giving $2017P(2017)-1=-1$. Thus $\boxed{P(2017)=0}$.
