Let $ABCD$ be a cyclic quadrilateral (vertices in this order on its circumcircle $\omega$) with $AD<BC$. The lines $AB$ and $CD$ meet at $E$, the tangents to $\omega$ at $A$ and $D$ meet at $G$, and the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ meet at $O$. Prove that $E$, $G$ and $O$ are collinear.
