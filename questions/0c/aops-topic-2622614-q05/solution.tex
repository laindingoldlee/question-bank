A fraction in lowest terms has a terminating decimal exactly when its denominator has no prime factors other than $2$ and $5$. Since $42=2\cdot3\cdot7$, $\dfrac{k}{42}$ terminates exactly when the factors $3$ and $7$ cancel, i.e. when $21\mid k$. This happens for $k=21$ ($\dfrac12=0.5$) and $k=42$ ($1$). So $42-2=\boxed{40}$ of the fractions are repeating decimals.
