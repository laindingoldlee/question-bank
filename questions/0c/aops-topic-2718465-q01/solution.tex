Combining like terms:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $A-B-C=\boxed{10x^3+8x^2-8x}$;
\item[(b)] $A-C=\boxed{14x^3+6x^2-7x}$;
\item[(c)] $A+B-C=\boxed{18x^3+4x^2-6x}$;
\item[(d)] $C+A=\boxed{4x^3+6x^2-3x}$;
\item[(e)] $B-C=\boxed{9x^3-2x^2-x}$.
\end{enumerate}
