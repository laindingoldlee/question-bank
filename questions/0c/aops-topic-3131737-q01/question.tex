A jar contains 50 cookies. Exactly 15 are plain, containing neither oatmeal nor chocolate. Every other cookie contains oatmeal, chocolate, or both. There are 13 oatmeal cookies and 23 chocolate cookies. How many contain chocolate but no oatmeal?
