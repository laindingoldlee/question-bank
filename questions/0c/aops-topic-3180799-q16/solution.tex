After $n$ days the totals are $4n^2-3n$ and $2^n-1$. For $n=1,\ldots,7$ they are respectively $(1,10,27,52,85,126,175)$ and $(1,3,7,15,31,63,127)$, so no strict exceedance has occurred. At $n=8$, the second total 255 exceeds 232. The eighth day is the following $\boxed{\text{Monday}}$.
