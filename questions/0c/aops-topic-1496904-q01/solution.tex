Let $M=10a+b$ with digits $1\le a\le9$ and $0\le b\le9$. Then
\[
(10a+b)-(10b+a)=9(a-b).
\]
We need $9(a-b)$ to be a positive cube, with $1\le a-b\le9$, so it lies between $9$ and $81$. The only cube in this range that is a multiple of $9$ is $27$: the cubes there are $27$ and $64$, and $64$ is not divisible by $9$. So $a-b=3$, which gives
\[
M=30,\ 41,\ 52,\ 63,\ 74,\ 85,\ 96 ,
\]
and there are $\boxed{7}$ such numbers.
