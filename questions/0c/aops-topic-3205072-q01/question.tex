A ball of radius $2$ rolls along three smoothly joined semicircular track arcs of radii $100,60,80$. Relative to the ball, the first and third arcs curve inward, so its center follows radii reduced by $2$; the middle curves outward, so the radius increases by $2$. Find the total distance traveled by the center.
