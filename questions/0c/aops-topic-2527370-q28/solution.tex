Measure arrival times in hours after 7:00, so $(x,y)$ is uniform in the unit square. They meet exactly when $|x-y|\le\dfrac13$. The complement consists of two right triangles with legs $1-\dfrac13=\dfrac23$, of total area $2\cdot\dfrac12\cdot\left(\dfrac23\right)^2=\dfrac49$. So the probability of meeting is $1-\dfrac49=\boxed{\dfrac59}$.
