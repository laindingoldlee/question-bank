Since $b+\dfrac{1}{c}>1$, the fractional term lies strictly between $0$ and $1$, so $a=3$. Inverting $\dfrac{7}{50}=\dfrac{1}{b+\dfrac{1}{c}}$ gives $b+\dfrac{1}{c}=\dfrac{50}{7}=7+\dfrac{1}{7}$. If $c=1$ the left side is an integer, impossible. Thus $c>1$, $b=7$, and $c=7$. The sum is $\boxed{17}$, choice (c).
