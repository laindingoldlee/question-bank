Let there be $t$ turkeys and $c$ cows. Then $t+c=98$ and $2t+4c=212$. Subtracting twice the first equation from the second gives $2c=16$, so $c=8$ and $t=\boxed{90}$. (Check: $2\cdot90+4\cdot8=212$.)
