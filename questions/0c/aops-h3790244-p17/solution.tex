The mirror line is $y=x+3$. Reflection across it sends $(x,y)$ to $(X,Y)=(y-3,x+3)$. For $y=\dfrac{x}{2}+5$, this gives $X=\dfrac{x}{2}+2$, $Y=x+3$, hence $Y=2X-1$. Thus $a=2$, $b=-1$, and $\boxed{a+b=1}$.
