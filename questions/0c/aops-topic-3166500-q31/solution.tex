Since $P$ is monic, the product is $P(i)P(-i)$. Grouping coefficients according to exponent modulo four gives real part $(101+97+\cdots+1)-(99+95+\cdots+3)=1326-1275=51$, and imaginary coefficient $(100+96+\cdots+4)-(98+94+\cdots+2)=1300-1250=50$. Thus $P(i)=51+50i$ and $P(-i)=51-50i$, yielding $51^2+50^2=\boxed{5101}$.
