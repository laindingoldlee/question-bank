Let the ages be $w$ and $a$, so $w^2+a=209$ and $a^2+w=183$. Subtracting,
\[
w^2-a^2-(w-a)=26\quad\Longrightarrow\quad(w-a)(w+a-1)=26 .
\]
Since $w^2<209$ we have $w\le14$, and $a^2<183$ gives $a\le13$, so $w+a-1\le26$. The factor pairs of $26$ with $w-a>0$ (clearly $w>a$, since $w^2+a>a^2+w$ forces it for whole numbers) are $1\cdot26$ and $2\cdot13$. The pair $w-a=1$, $w+a=27$ gives $w=14$, $a=13$, which works: $196+13=209$ and $169+14=183$. The pair $w-a=2$, $w+a=14$ gives $w=8$, $a=6$, but $64+6\ne209$. So the sum of their ages is $\boxed{27}$.
