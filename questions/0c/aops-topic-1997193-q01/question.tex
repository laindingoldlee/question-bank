Anna has a bowl containing two square tiles, one with side length $2$ cm and the other with side length $3$ cm. She randomly chooses a tile from the bowl, where the probability of choosing a tile is proportional to its area. What is the expected value of the area of the tile she chooses? Express your answer as a common fraction.
