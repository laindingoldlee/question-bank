All iterates lie in $[0,\sqrt x]$, since $\sqrt x<x$. The map $f(t)=\sqrt{x-t}$ reverses order, so $f\circ f$ preserves it. Consequently the even subsequence increases from $0$ and the odd subsequence decreases from $\sqrt x$, with every even term at most every odd term. Their limits $u\le v$ satisfy $u^2=x-v$ and $v^2=x-u$. Subtraction gives $(u-v)(u+v-1)=0$. If $u+v=1$, then $x=u^2+v=u^2-u+1\le1$ for $0\le u\le1$, contradicting $x>1$. Thus $u=v=L$, where $L^2+L=x$. The nonnegative root is $\boxed{L=\dfrac{\sqrt{1+4x}-1}{2}}$. At $x=1$ the specified truncations alternate between $0$ and $1$, so a domain condition is necessary.
