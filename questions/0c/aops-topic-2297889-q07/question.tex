Lines $\ell$ and $m$ are parallel, and a transversal $t$ meets $\ell$ at $P$ and $m$ at $Q$. Let $X$ and $Y$ be points of $t$ outside the strip between the lines, with $P$ between $X$ and $Q$ and with $Q$ between $P$ and $Y$, and let $A$ on $\ell$ and $B$ on $m$ lie on opposite sides of $t$. Using the Corresponding Angles Theorem and the Vertical Angles Theorem, prove the Alternate Exterior Angles Theorem: $\angle XPA\cong\angle YQB$.
