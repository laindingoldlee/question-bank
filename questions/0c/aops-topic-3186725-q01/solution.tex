Euler's formula for a convex polyhedron is $V-E+F=2$. Thus $V=2+678-305=\boxed{375}$. The convexity assumption supplies the topology needed for this formula. The sketch below illustrates a polyhedral surface, not the specific $305$-face solid.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(3,0),C=(3,2),D=(0,2),v=(1,1);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw((A+v)--(B+v)--(C+v)--(D+v)--cycle);draw(A--(A+v)^^B--(B+v)^^C--(C+v)^^D--(D+v));label("schematic only",(2,-.5));
\end{asy}
