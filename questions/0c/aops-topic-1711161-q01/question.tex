It rained on exactly $10$ days during Tricia's vacation. On each rainy day it rained either in the morning or in the afternoon, but not both. There were exactly $13$ mornings when it did not rain and exactly $17$ afternoons when it did not rain. How many days did Tricia's vacation last?
