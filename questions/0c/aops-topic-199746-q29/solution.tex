The condition says $x^2-5x=-7$. Since $(x^2-5x)^2=x^4-10x^3+25x^2$,
\[
x^4-10x^3+25x^2-9=(x^2-5x)^2-9=49-9=\boxed{40}.
\]
(The value is the same for both roots of $x^2-5x+7=0$; they are not real, but the identity holds for them too.)
