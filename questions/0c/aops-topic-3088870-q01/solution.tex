Measure minutes after noon. The sample rectangle is $0\le a\le90$, $30\le b\le120$, with area 8100. Meeting requires $a-30\le b\le a+20$. For $a<10$ no arrival of B works; for $10\le a\le60$ the permitted vertical length is $a-10$; for $60\le a\le90$ it is 50. Thus the favourable area is $\dfrac{1}{2}(50)(50)+(30)(50)=2750$, and the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{55}{162}}$.
