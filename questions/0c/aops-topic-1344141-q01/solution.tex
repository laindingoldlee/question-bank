When the second ship leaves, the first is $40\cdot\dfrac{5}{4}=50$ miles ahead. The gap closes at $60-40=20$ mph, so the second ship catches up after $\dfrac{50}{20}=2.5$ hours of its own travel, that is
\[
\boxed{3.75\text{ hours (3 h 45 min) after the first ship leaves}},
\]
at a distance of $60\cdot2.5=\boxed{150\text{ miles}}$ from the port. Check: the first ship has travelled $40\cdot3.75=150$ miles.
