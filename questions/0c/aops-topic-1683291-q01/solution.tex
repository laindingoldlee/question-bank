Since $\gcd(8,9)=1$, a number is divisible by both $8$ and $9$ exactly when it is divisible by $72$. The positive multiples of $72$ below $360=5\cdot72$ are $72,144,216,288$, so there are $\boxed{4}$.

(Without the word ``positive'' the question would have infinitely many answers, since $0,-72,-144,\dots$ also work; the intended meaning is positive integers.)
