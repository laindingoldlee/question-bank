By the Pythagorean theorem its hypotenuse is $\sqrt{9^2+12^2}=15$. The midpoint of the hypotenuse is equidistant from the three vertices, so it is the circumcentre and the radius is half the hypotenuse: $\boxed{\dfrac{15}{2}}$.
