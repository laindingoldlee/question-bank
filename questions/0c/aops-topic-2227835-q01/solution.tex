First solve $x\equiv3\pmod{13}$ and $x\equiv2\pmod7$: $x=13k+3$ with $13k+3\equiv-k+3\equiv2\pmod7$, so $k\equiv1\pmod 7$ and $x\equiv16\pmod{91}$.

Next $x=91m+16\equiv1\pmod6$: $91\equiv1$ and $16\equiv4\pmod6$, so $m+4\equiv1$, $m\equiv3\pmod6$, and $x\equiv91\cdot3+16=289\pmod{546}$.

(Check: $289=6\cdot48+1=7\cdot41+2=13\cdot22+3$.) The solutions are $289+546j$ for $j\ge0$, and $289+546j<6996$ means $j\le\dfrac{6707}{546}\approx12.3$. So $j=0,1,\ldots,12$, and there are $\boxed{13}$ such integers.
