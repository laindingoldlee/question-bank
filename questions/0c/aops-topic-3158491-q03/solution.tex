Put $ BC=t>0 $, so $ AC=\sqrt{400+t^2}$. Equating area formulas gives $ 20t=12\sqrt{400+t^2}$. Squaring and simplifying yields $ 256t^2=57600 $, so $ t=15 $. The area is $\dfrac{1}{2}(20)(15)=\boxed{150}$.
