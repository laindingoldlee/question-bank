The larger circle has circumference $2\pi\cdot205=410\pi$. (The square fits inside it, since half its diagonal is $100\sqrt2<205$.) The smaller circle has radius $100$, so the part of the square outside it has area
\[200^2-\pi\cdot100^2=40000-10000\pi.\]
The product is
\[410\pi\left(40000-10000\pi\right)=\boxed{4100000\pi(4-\pi)}\approx1.106\times10^7.\]
