A cube has edge length $12$. On each of its six faces, draw the square of side $10$ that has the same centre as the face and sides parallel to the face's edges. The convex polyhedron whose vertices are the $24$ vertices of these six squares has $6$ square faces (the drawn squares), $12$ rectangular faces and $8$ triangular faces; it looks like a rhombicuboctahedron, except that its rectangular faces are not squares. The ratio of the surface area of this polyhedron to the surface area of the cube can be written as
\[\dfrac{a+b\sqrt3+c\sqrt2}{d},\]
where $a,b,c,d$ are positive integers with no common factor greater than $1$. Find $a+b+c+d$.
