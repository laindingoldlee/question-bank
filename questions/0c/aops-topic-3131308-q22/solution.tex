For prime $p$, the defining equation reads $f(p)=\dfrac{f(p)}{p}+2p-2$, so $f(p)=2p$. Thus in general $f(n)=2f(\dfrac{n}{p})+2p-2$. Since $2022=2\cdot3\cdot337$, with $337$ prime (trial division by primes up to $\sqrt{337}<19$ suffices), we get $f(337)=674$, $f(1011)=2(674)+4=1352$, and $f(2022)=2(1352)+2=\boxed{2706}$.
