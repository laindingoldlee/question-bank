Treat the empty chair as a sixth distinct object. There are $6!=720$ unrestricted arrangements. When Dan and Evan are adjacent, regard them as one block with two internal orders, giving $2\cdot5!=240$ forbidden arrangements. Therefore the count is $\boxed{480}$.
