Ten people stand in a line. The first person receives an apple with probability $1$. After someone receives an apple, the next person's probability is $\dfrac15$ lower than that person's; after someone does not receive one, the next person's probability resets to $1$. (So the probabilities run $1,\dfrac45,\dfrac35,\dfrac25,\dfrac15,0$ along a streak of apples.) Find the expected number of apples given out.
