By linearity, the expectation is the sum over people of the probability that person $i$ gets an apple. Tracking the distribution of each person's probability (a short calculation, done exactly) gives these probabilities for persons $1$ to $10$:
\[1,\ \dfrac45,\ \dfrac{17}{25},\ \dfrac{84}{125},\ \dfrac{449}{625},\ \dfrac{92}{125},\ \dfrac{2237}{3125},\ \dfrac{11028}{15625},\ \dfrac{55853}{78125},\ \dfrac{280604}{390625}.\]
For example person $3$: either person $2$ got an apple (probability $\dfrac45$, then $\dfrac35$) or not (probability $\dfrac15$, then $1$), so $\dfrac45\cdot\dfrac35+\dfrac15=\dfrac{17}{25}$. The sum is
\[\boxed{\dfrac{2914569}{390625}}\approx7.46.\]
