Let $AH$ be the altitude from $A$ to line $BC$. If $H\ne B$, triangle $AHB$ is right-angled at $H$, so $AB$ is its hypotenuse and $AB>AH$. If $H=B$, the angle at $B$ is right and $AB=AH$. The same holds for $AC$. $\blacksquare$
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(1,3),B=(0,0),C=(4,0),H=(1,0);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--H,dashed);draw((1.25,0)--(1.25,0.25)--(1,0.25));
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$H$",H,S);
\end{asy}
