Let $H$ be the foot of the altitude. In right triangle $BPH$, $\angle BPH=90^\circ-\dfrac B2$, so $\angle APB=180^\circ-\angle BPH=90^\circ+\dfrac B2=105^\circ$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0),C=(5,0);pair A=extension(B,B+dir(30),C,C+dir(130));pair H=(A.x,0);pair P=extension(A,H,B,B+dir(15));draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--H,dashed);draw(B--P);dot(P);
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$H$",H,S);label("$P$",P,NW);
\end{asy}

Hence $\angle B=\boxed{30^\circ}$ and $\angle A=180^\circ-30^\circ-50^\circ=\boxed{100^\circ}$. (Both base angles are acute, so $H$ lies on segment $BC$ as assumed.)
