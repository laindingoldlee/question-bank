From $\pi r^2=40\pi$ we get $r^2=40$ and $r=\sqrt{40}=2\sqrt{10}$. The circumference is $2\pi r=\boxed{4\sqrt{10}\,\pi}\approx39.7$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real r=sqrt(40);
fill(circle((0,0),r),rgb(0.85,0.92,1)); draw(circle((0,0),r),linewidth(0.8));
dot((0,0)); draw((0,0)--r*dir(30),red); label("$2\sqrt{10}$",0.5*r*dir(30),NW,red);
label("area $40\pi$",(0,-2.5));
\end{asy}
