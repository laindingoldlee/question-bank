We look for a multiple of $11a+2b$ that agrees with $18a+5b$ modulo $19$. Since $12\cdot11=132=114+18$ and $12\cdot2=24=19+5$,
\[
12(11a+2b)=132a+24b=(18a+5b)+19(6a+b).
\]
Hence
\[
18a+5b=12(11a+2b)-19(6a+b).
\]
If $19\mid 11a+2b$, both terms on the right are multiples of $19$, so $19\mid18a+5b$. $\blacksquare$
