Let there be $n$ members, each paying $p$ dollars, so the car cost $np$. The two conditions say
\[
(n+4)(p-10)=np\quad\text{and}\quad(n-6)(p+20)=np .
\]
They simplify to $4p-10n=40$ and $20n-6p=120$, i.e.\ $2p=5n+20$ and $3p=10n-60$. Then $3(5n+20)=2(10n-60)$, so $15n+60=20n-120$, $n=36$ and $p=100$. The car cost $36\cdot100=\boxed{3600}$ dollars. Check: $40\cdot90=3600$ and $30\cdot120=3600$.
