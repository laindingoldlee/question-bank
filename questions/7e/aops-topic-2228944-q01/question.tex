Let $F_1=F_2=1$ and $F_{n+1}=F_n+F_{n-1}$ be the Fibonacci numbers. Prove that for every positive integer $n$,
\[F_n\equiv n\cdot3^{\,n-1}\pmod5,\]
and deduce that $5\mid F_n$ exactly when $5\mid n$, and that the remainders of $F_n$ on division by $5$ repeat with period $20$.
