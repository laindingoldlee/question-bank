Each small centre lies within distance 1 of the large centre. Two centres must be at least 2 apart, and the triangle inequality shows this can occur only when they are opposite points on that unit-radius centre locus. A third point cannot be opposite to both. Two disks centred at $(\pm1,0)$ attain the bound, so $\boxed2$.
