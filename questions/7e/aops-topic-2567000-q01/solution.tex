The sphere has centre at the origin and radius $r=\sqrt8=2\sqrt2$. So the volume is
\[\dfrac43\pi r^3=\dfrac43\pi\left(2\sqrt2\right)^3=\dfrac43\pi\cdot16\sqrt2=\boxed{\dfrac{64\sqrt2\,\pi}{3}}.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real r=2*sqrt(2);
draw(circle((0,0),r),linewidth(0.8));
draw(yscale(0.3)*arc((0,0),r,180,360),gray);
draw(yscale(0.3)*arc((0,0),r,0,180),gray+dashed);
draw((0,0)--(r,0),blue);dot((0,0));
label("$2\sqrt2$",(r/2,0),N,blue+fontsize(8pt));label("$O$",(0,0),SW,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}
