Let the numbers be $a\le b\le c\le d$ with sum $16$ and $b+c=6$. The unique mode $2$ must occur at least twice. If $b=c=2$ the median would be $2$; so $a=b=2$, hence $c=4$ and $d=16-8=8$. The range is $8-2=6$. Answer $\boxed{\textbf{(C)}\ 6}$.
