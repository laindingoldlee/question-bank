The following problem was posed with the answer choices $\dfrac{\sqrt5}{10}$, $\dfrac{\sqrt{15}}{10}$, $\dfrac{1}{2}$, $\dfrac{4\sqrt{15}}{25}$ and $1$: \emph{positive real numbers $x,y,z$ satisfy}
\[\sqrt{5xy}=x+y+1,\qquad\sqrt{4yz}=y+z,\qquad\sqrt{3xz}=x+z-1;\]
\emph{determine $\dfrac{1}{x}+\dfrac{1}{y}$.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that any solution in positive real numbers would have to satisfy $y=z$, $x+y=4+\sqrt{15}$ and $xy=8+2\sqrt{15}$.
\item[(b)] Deduce that the system has no solution in positive real numbers.
\end{enumerate}
