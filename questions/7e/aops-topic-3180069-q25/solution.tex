Use the sum of cubes with $14^2=196$: $14^6+27=(196+3)(196^2-3\cdot196+9)=199\cdot37837=199\cdot157\cdot241$. The primes at most $\sqrt{241}<16$ are 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, and direct division shows none divides 157,199, or 241 (only primes below each individual square root need checking). Thus these three factors are prime, and their sum is $157+199+241=\boxed{597}$.
