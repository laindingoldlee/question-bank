Such an $N$ is $2^i+2^j$ with $0\le j<i\le7$ (since $N\le255<2^8$). Modulo $3$, $2\equiv-1$, so $2^k\equiv1$ for even $k$ and $2^k\equiv-1$ for odd $k$. Hence $2^i+2^j\equiv0\pmod3$ exactly when one exponent is even and the other is odd (two even exponents give $2$, two odd give $-2\equiv1$).

Among $0,1,\dots,7$ there are $4$ even and $4$ odd exponents, so the number of such $N$ is $4\cdot4=\boxed{16}$.
