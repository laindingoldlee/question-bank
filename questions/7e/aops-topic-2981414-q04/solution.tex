Let the radius be $r$. Your distance to the centre is $r+9$, and the tangent segment is perpendicular to the radius at the point of tangency $T$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0),Y=(-2.6,0);real r=1.4;draw(circle(O,r));pair T=r*dir(degrees(acos(r/2.6)));
draw(Y--O,dashed);draw(Y--T);draw(O--T);dot(Y);dot(O);dot(T);
label("you",Y,W);label("$O$",O,E);label("$T$",T,N);label("$9$",(Y+(-r,0))/2,S);label("$r$",(O+(-r,0))/2,S);label("$51$",(Y+T)/2,NW);
\end{asy}

So $(r+9)^2=r^2+51^2$: $18r+81=2601$, $r=\boxed{140}$ m.
