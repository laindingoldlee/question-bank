Let $f_n$ count allowed strings of $n$ delivery decisions. Strings ending in delivery contribute $f_{n-1}$; those ending in omission must end in delivery-omission and contribute $f_{n-2}$. Hence $f_n=f_{n-1}+f_{n-2}$, with $f_0=1,f_1=2$. The successive values are $1,2,3,5,8,13,21,34,55$, giving $\boxed{f_8=55}$.
