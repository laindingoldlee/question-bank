Put $DC=t$, $BD=2t$. Stewart's theorem yields $9^2(2t)+12^2t=3t(6^2+2t^2)$. Dividing by $3t$ gives $102=36+2t^2$, so $t^2=33$. Therefore $\boxed{BC=3\sqrt{33}}$.
