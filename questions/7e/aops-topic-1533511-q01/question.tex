$ABCD$ is a convex cyclic quadrilateral in which the lines $AB$ and $CD$ are perpendicular. If $AB=a$, $BC=b$, $CD=c$ and $DA=d$, prove that
\[
(ab+cd)^2+(ad+bc)^2=\left(b^2-d^2\right)^2 .
\]
