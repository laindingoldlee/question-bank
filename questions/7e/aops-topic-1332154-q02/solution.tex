In a geometric sequence the middle term $m$ of three consecutive terms satisfies $\dfrac{m}{2^2}=\dfrac{2^4}{m}$, so $m^2=2^2\cdot2^4=2^6$ and $m=\pm2^3$. The positive term is $\boxed{8}$ (common ratio $2$). Without the word ``positive'' the sequence $4,-8,16$ (ratio $-2$) would also work.
