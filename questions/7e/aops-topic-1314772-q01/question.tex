A city has a rectangular grid of roads: $10$ streets numbered $1$ to $10$ running east-west and $16$ avenues numbered $1$ to $16$ running north-south. All streets end at First and Sixteenth Avenues, and all avenues end at First and Tenth Streets. A rectangular park is bounded by Sixth and Eighth Streets and by Fourth and Twelfth Avenues; the roads bounding the park are unbroken, but no road goes through the park. At every corner where roads meet, a crosswalk is painted across each road that leaves that corner; so a four-way crossing has $4$ crosswalks and a corner such as First Street and First Avenue has $2$. How many crosswalks are painted?
