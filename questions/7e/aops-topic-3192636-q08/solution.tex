Rationalization gives $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{n+1}+\sqrt n}<\dfrac{1}{20}$, or denominator greater than 20. At $n=99$ the denominator is less than 20; at $n=100$ it exceeds 20. The denominator increases with $n$, so the answer is $\boxed{100}$.
