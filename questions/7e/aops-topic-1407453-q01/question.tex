Prove that for every even positive integer $n$,
\[
n^2-(n-1)^2+(n-2)^2-(n-3)^2+\cdots+4^2-3^2+2^2-1^2=\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}.
\]
Does the same formula hold for odd $n$ if the sum is taken as $n^2-(n-1)^2+\cdots-2^2+1^2$?
