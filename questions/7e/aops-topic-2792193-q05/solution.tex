$\boxed{\text{Yes}}$. Take $1,2,3,6,12,24,\dots,3\cdot2^{2011}$, that is $1,2$ and $3\cdot2^k$ for $k=0,\dots,2011$: $2014$ numbers. Their sum is $3+3(2^{2012}-1)=3\cdot2^{2012}$, which is divisible by $1$, $2$ and every $3\cdot2^k$ with $k\le2011$. (Each new term equals the sum of all previous ones, which keeps the property.)
