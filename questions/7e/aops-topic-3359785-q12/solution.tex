The linear condition is $x+2y+4z=18$. AM-GM gives $x+2y+4z\ge3\sqrt[3]{8xyz}=18$. Equality is forced, so $x=2y=4z=6$. Hence $(x,y,z)=(6,3,\dfrac{3}{2})$ is the sole positive solution and $\boxed{x+y+z=\dfrac{21}{2}}$.
