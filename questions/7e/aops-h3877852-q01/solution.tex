Let $T,S,M,B,C\in\{0,1\}$ indicate guilt. For each of the $32$ possible assignments evaluate the statement pairs $(1-C,S)$, $(1-M,1-C)$, $(C,1-T)$, $(M,S)$, $(B,1-T)$. Keep an assignment exactly when the two entries differ precisely for the innocent suspects. The unique surviving assignment is $(T,S,M,B,C)=(0,1,1,1,1)$. To verify it, Terry says false/true; Steve false/false; Matt true/true; Ben true/true; Carl true/true. Hence $\boxed{\text{Steve, Matt, Ben, and Carl are guilty}}$. The exhaustive procedure checks both necessity and sufficiency.
