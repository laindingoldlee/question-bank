$\mathcal P$ is the line $y=1-\dfrac x2$ and $\mathcal Q$ is an ellipse. Substituting,
\[\dfrac{x^2}{4}+\left(1-\dfrac x2\right)^2=1\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \dfrac{x^2}{2}-x=0\ \Longleftrightarrow\ x=0\ \text{or}\ x=2,\]
giving the points $(0,1)$ and $(2,0)$. The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(C) } 2}$.
