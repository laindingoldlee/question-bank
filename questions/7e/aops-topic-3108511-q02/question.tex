For positive integers $ x,y,z $, consider those values of $\dfrac{\operatorname{lcm}(x,y)+\operatorname{lcm}(y,z)}{\operatorname{lcm}(x,z)}$ that are integers. Find the greatest common divisor of all such integer values.
