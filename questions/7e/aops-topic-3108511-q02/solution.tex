Let the powers of $ 2 $ dividing $ x,y,z $ be $ a,b,c $, and exchange $ x,z $ if needed so $ a\le c $. If $\max(a,b)<c $, the two numerator terms have unequal powers of $ 2 $, and their sum has valuation $\max(a,b)<c $; thus the quotient cannot be an integer. Otherwise $\max(a,b)=\max(c,b)\ge c $, so both numerator terms have the same valuation at least $ c $, and their sum has valuation at least $ c+1 $. Every integer quotient is therefore even. The choice $ x=y=z=1 $ gives $ 2 $, proving the gcd is $\boxed2 $.
