For $n=1,2,3$, the left sides are $10,44,120$ and right sides $1,4,27$, so none works. For $n=4$, both sides equal $256$. If $n\ge5$, then $n^n\ge n^4$. Also $n^3>4n^2$ and $n^2\ge3n+4$, giving $n^3>3n^2+3n+4$; multiply by $n$ to get $n^4>3n^3+3n^2+4n$. Hence no larger $n$ works, and $\boxed{n=4}$.
