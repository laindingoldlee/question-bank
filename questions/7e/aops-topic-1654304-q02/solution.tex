With $5$ flips, either there are at least $3$ tails or at least $3$ heads, never both. By symmetry between heads and tails these two events have the same probability, and together they have probability $1$, so
\[
P(\text{at least 3 tails})=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}.
\]
Directly: $\dfrac{\dbinom53+\dbinom54+\dbinom55}{2^5}=\dfrac{10+5+1}{32}=\dfrac{16}{32}$.
