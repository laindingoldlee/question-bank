The sphere condition gives $R=2r$. Thus the torus volume is $\dfrac{3\pi^2r^3}{4}$, while the sphere volume is $\dfrac{4\pi r^3}{3}$. Dividing gives $\boxed{\dfrac{16}{9\pi}}$.
