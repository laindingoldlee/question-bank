A number has three digits in base $5$ exactly when $5^2\le n<5^3$, i.e.\ $25\le n\le124$. That is $124-25+1=\boxed{100}$ numbers. (Equivalently: $4$ choices for the leading digit and $5$ for each of the other two, $4\cdot5\cdot5=100$.)
