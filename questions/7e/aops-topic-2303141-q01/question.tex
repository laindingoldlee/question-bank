Each cell of a grid is coloured red or blue. A \emph{monochromatic rectangle} consists of two rows and two columns such that the four cells where they cross all have the same colour (these are the four corner cells of a rectangle with sides along the grid lines).
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that every colouring of a $5\times5$ grid contains a monochromatic rectangle.
\item[(b)] Show that a grid with $4$ rows and $6$ columns can be coloured with no monochromatic rectangle.
\end{enumerate}
