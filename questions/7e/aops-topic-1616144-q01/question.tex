I have $2$ gallons of orange paint, $2$ gallons of purple paint, $1$ gallon of yellow paint and $1$ gallon of blue paint. I use $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of the orange paint, $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of the purple paint, $\dfrac{1}{2}$ of the yellow paint and $\dfrac{5}{7}$ of the blue paint. How many gallons of paint are left over? Express your answer as a mixed number.
