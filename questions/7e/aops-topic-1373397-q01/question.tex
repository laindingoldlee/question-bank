Find the minimum of $x^2+y^2+z^2$ for positive reals $x,y,z$ with $xyz=1$.

A posted solution reads: ``By AM-GM, $x^2+y^2+z^2\ge3\sqrt[3]{x^2y^2z^2}=\sqrt[3]{(xyz)^2}=1$, with equality when $x=y=z=1$.'' Find the error and give the correct minimum.
