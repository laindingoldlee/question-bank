An integer $x$ is chosen uniformly at random from $\{1,2,\dots,3000\}$. Given that $x$ is divisible by $3$, what is the probability that $x$ is divisible by $15$? Explain why the answer is the same for $\{1,2,\dots,N\}$ whenever $N$ is a multiple of $15$.
