By Vieta, $r_1+r_2=-\dfrac ba$ and $r_1r_2=\dfrac ca\ne0$. So $\dfrac1{r_1}+\dfrac1{r_2}=\dfrac{r_1+r_2}{r_1r_2}=-\dfrac bc$. (Equivalently, $\dfrac1{r_i}$ are the roots of the reversed polynomial $cx^2+bx+a$.) $\blacksquare$
