Among the $2014$ numbers $0,1,\dots,2013$ there are $1007$ even and $1007$ odd numbers, so each chosen number is odd with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, independently. The product is odd only when all three numbers are odd, which has probability $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^3=\dfrac{1}{8}$. Hence the product is even with probability
\[
1-\dfrac{1}{8}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{8}}.
\]
