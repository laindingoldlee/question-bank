Bob can clean the garage in $9$ hours less than his brother Paul. Working together, they can do the job in $20$ hours. How long would it take each of them to do the job alone?

(A student lets Paul's time be $p$ hours, writes $\dfrac{1}{p}+\dfrac{1}{p-9}=\dfrac{1}{20}$ and arrives at $p^2-49p+180=0$. Are these steps right?)
