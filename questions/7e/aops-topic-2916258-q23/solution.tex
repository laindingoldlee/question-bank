For $n\ge1$, $r_{n+1}=r_n^2+r_n$, so $\dfrac{r_{n+1}}{r_n^2}=1+\dfrac1{r_n}$. With $r_1=1$, $r_2=2$, $r_3=6$, $r_4=42$, and so on, the sum is $2022+\sum_{n=1}^{2022}\dfrac1{r_n}$. Now $\sum\dfrac1{r_n}=1+\dfrac12+\dfrac16+\dfrac1{42}+\cdots$ lies strictly between $1$ and $2$: the terms from $\dfrac1{r_3}$ on sum to less than $\dfrac16\left(1+\dfrac17+\dfrac1{49}+\cdots\right)<\dfrac15$. So the sum is in $(2023,2024)$ and the answer is $\boxed{2023}$.
