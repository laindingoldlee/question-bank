Define $r_0=1$ and $r_n=r_{n-1}^2+r_{n-2}^2+\cdots+r_0^2$ for $n\ge1$. Find the greatest integer less than $\dfrac{r_2}{r_1^2}+\dfrac{r_3}{r_2^2}+\cdots+\dfrac{r_{2023}}{r_{2022}^2}$.
