Vieta gives $r+s+t=7$, so $r+s=7-t$. If $P(x)=x^3-7x^2+3x+2$, then the monic polynomial
\[
Q(x)=-P(7-x)=x^3-14x^2+52x-23
\]
has $7-t$ as a root. The original cubic has no rational root: its only candidates are $\pm1,\pm2$, and none works. It is therefore irreducible over the rationals, as is its invertible affine transform $Q$. This proves uniqueness of the monic rational cubic. Thus $\boxed{A+B+C=-14+52-23=15}$.
