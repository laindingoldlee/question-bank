If the prime exponents are e, each e+1 divides $1729=7\cdot13\cdot19$. Every divisor of 1729 is one modulo six, so all exponents are multiples of six and the number is a perfect sixth power. Sharpness follows from $2^63^{12}5^{18}$, which has $7\cdot13\cdot19$ divisors and exponent gcd six. Thus the largest universal n is $\boxed6$.
