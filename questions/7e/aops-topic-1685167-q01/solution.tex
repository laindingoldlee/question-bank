The numbers of Pokeballs on days $1$ to $6$ are $16,28,40,52,64,76$, an arithmetic sequence with sum
\[
\dfrac{6(16+76)}{2}=276 .
\]
At a $30\%$ success rate he catches $0.3\cdot276=82.8$, i.e.\ about $\boxed{83}$ Pokemon by the end of day $6$.
