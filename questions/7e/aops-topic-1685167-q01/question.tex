Each day Pikachu buys $y=12x+4$ Pokeballs on day $x$ ($x=1,2,3,\dots$) and uses all of them that day. Exactly $30\%$ of the Pokeballs used on any day catch a Pokemon, on average. How many Pokemon, in total over days $1$ to $6$, does he expect to catch? Round to the nearest whole number.
