$AB=DC=12$, so $AE=12-4=8$, and the right triangle $AED$ gives $AD=\sqrt{8^2+6^2}=10$; hence $BC=10$.

\begin{asy}

path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s=0.2){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
pair A=(0,0),B=(12,0),Dp=(8,6),C=B+Dp; pair Ep=(8,0); pair F=B+dot(Dp-B,unit(C-B))*unit(C-B);
draw(A--B--C--Dp--cycle); draw(Dp--Ep,red); draw(Dp--F,blue); draw(ra(Ep,(1,0),(0,1),0.8)); draw(ra(F,unit(Dp-F),unit(C-F),0.8));
label("$A$",A,SW); label("$B$",B,S); label("$C$",C,NE); label("$D$",Dp,NW); label("$E$",Ep,S); label("$F$",F,2*E+N);
label("$6$",(Dp+Ep)/2,W,red); label("$4$",(Ep+B)/2,S); label("$12$",(Dp+C)/2,N);

\end{asy}

Computing the area in two ways, $AB\cdot DE=BC\cdot DF$, so $12\cdot6=10\cdot DF$ and $DF=\boxed{7.2}$, answer $\textbf{(C)}$.
