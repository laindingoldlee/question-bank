Bob is $8$ blocks away from his car, and each block is $\dfrac{5}{16}$ of a mile. He is carrying a full container holding $\dfrac78$ cup of hot apple cider, and he spills one drop at the end of every $10$ minutes of walking, where a drop is $\dfrac16$ of a fluid ounce. He walks at $2.5$ miles per hour. How much cider, in cups, does he have left when he reaches his car? ($1$ cup $=8$ fluid ounces.)
