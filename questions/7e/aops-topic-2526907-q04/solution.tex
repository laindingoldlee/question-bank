Write $h(x)=e^{g(x)}$ with $g(x)=x\ln x$. Then $g'(x)=\ln x+1$, which is negative for $0<x<\dfrac1e$ and positive for $x>\dfrac1e$. So $g$ decreases and then increases, with minimum $g\left(\dfrac1e\right)=-\dfrac1e$. Also $g(x)\to\infty$ as $x\to\infty$. Since $g$ is continuous, it takes every value in $\left[-\dfrac1e,\infty\right)$ and no smaller value. As $e^y$ is increasing and continuous, the range of $h$ is
\[\boxed{\left[e^{-\dfrac1e},\ \infty\right)}\approx[0.692,\infty).\]
