Write $p_k$ for the probability of being late on day $k$, given what happened before.

\textbf{Day 2.} With probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$ she is late on day 1, and then the probability for day 2 is $2\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. With probability $\dfrac{3}{4}$ she is on time on day 1, and then the probability for day 2 is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{1}{8}$.

\textbf{Day 3.} Follow the four possible histories of days 1 and 2:
\begin{itemize}
\item late, late: probability $\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{1}{8}$; then $p_3=2\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=1$;
\item late, on time: probability $\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{1}{8}$; then $p_3=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{1}{4}$;
\item on time, late: probability $\dfrac{3}{4}\cdot\dfrac{1}{8}=\dfrac{3}{32}$; then $p_3=2\cdot\dfrac{1}{8}=\dfrac{1}{4}$;
\item on time, on time: probability $\dfrac{3}{4}\cdot\dfrac{7}{8}=\dfrac{21}{32}$; then $p_3=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{1}{8}=\dfrac{1}{16}$.
\end{itemize}
Hence
\[
P(\text{late on day 3})=\dfrac{1}{8}\cdot1+\dfrac{1}{8}\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{3}{32}\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{21}{32}\cdot\dfrac{1}{16}=\dfrac{64+16+12+21}{512}=\boxed{\dfrac{113}{512}}.
\]
