For every $d\mid n$, the quotient is the integer geometric sum $1+3^d+3^{2 d}+\cdots+3^{n-d}$, whose parity is the parity of its number of terms, $\dfrac{n}{d}$. As d ranges over divisors, so does $\dfrac{n}{d}$. Hence the parity of f equals the parity of the number of odd divisors of n. Write $n=2^a m$ with m odd; its odd divisors are the divisors of m, whose count is odd exactly when m is a square, by pairing complementary divisors. Such n are precisely the perfect squares and twice perfect squares, with the two families disjoint. Their count is $\lfloor\sqrt{2023}\rfloor+\lfloor\sqrt{\dfrac{2023}{2}}\rfloor=44+31=\boxed{75}$.
