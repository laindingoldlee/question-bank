Cup A contains $600$ grams of salt solution with concentration $40\%$, and cup B contains $400$ grams of salt solution with concentration $20\%$. The same mass of solution is poured from A into B and from B into A at the same time. How many grams should be exchanged so that the two cups end up with the same concentration?
