A person owns $11$ different books: $3$ on Roman history, $4$ on Greek myths, $3$ on Persian history and $1$ other book. In how many ways can they arrange $9$ of the books in a row on a shelf if the shelf must contain at least $2$ of the Roman history books and at least $1$ of the Greek myth books?
