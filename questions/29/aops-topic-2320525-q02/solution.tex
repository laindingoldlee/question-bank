Choosing which $9$ books go on the shelf is the same as choosing the $2$ books that are left out, and each choice of $9$ books can be arranged in $9!$ orders.

The Greek myth condition is automatic: there are $4$ Greek myth books and only $2$ are left out. The Roman history condition fails exactly when at least $2$ of the $3$ Roman history books are left out, that is, when the $2$ books left out are both Roman history books: $\dbinom32=3$ choices. So the number of allowed choices of the two books left out is
\[\dbinom{11}{2}-\dbinom32=55-3=52,\]
and the number of arrangements is
\[52\cdot9!=52\cdot362880=\boxed{18869760}.\]
