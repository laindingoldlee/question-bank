Write $a=du,b=dv$ with $\gcd(u,v)=1$. Then the condition is $\gcd(u-v,u+v)=1$. Any common divisor divides $2u$ and $2v$, hence divides $2$. If $u,v$ are both odd, this gcd is $2$; if they have opposite parity, it is $1$. Thus the condition holds exactly when $a,b$ have different powers of $2$ in their prime factorizations.

Among $1,\ldots,100$, the counts with exact powers $2^0,2^1,\ldots,2^6$ are $50,25,13,6,3,2,1$. The excluded pairs have equal powers, so the answer is
\[
\boxed{100^2-(50^2+25^2+13^2+6^2+3^2+2^2+1^2)=6656}.
\]
