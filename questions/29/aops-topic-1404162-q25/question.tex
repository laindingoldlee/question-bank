A circle is inscribed in triangle $ABC$ with $AC=5$, $AB=6$ and $BC=3$. A segment $DE$, with $D$ on side $AB$ and $E$ on side $AC$, is tangent to the circle (between the circle and $A$). What is the perimeter of triangle $ADE$?

(A) $7$\quad (B) $4$\quad (C) $9$\quad (D) $6$\quad (E) $8$
