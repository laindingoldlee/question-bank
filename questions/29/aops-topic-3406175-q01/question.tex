A discussion starts with one post on day $0$. On each subsequent day, if the last post was $n$ days ago, a new post appears with probability $2^{-n}$; otherwise no post appears. At most one appears per day, and the stated probabilities hold conditional on all earlier history. Find the probability of at least six total posts by the end of day $15$, to the nearest tenth of a percent.
