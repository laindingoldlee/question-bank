Let $D_t(c,g)$ be the probability that after day $t$ there have been $c$ additional posts and the age used for the next day is $g$. Start at $D_0(0,1)=1$. From state $(c,g)$ move to $(c+1,1)$ with probability $2^{-g}$ and to $(c,g+1)$ with probability $1-2^{-g}$. These disjoint transitions account for every history. After fifteen updates, sum states with $c\ge5$. Exact rational arithmetic gives $\dfrac{212834576891532577543}{1180591620717411303424}=0.180277898942\ldots$, so the requested answer is $\boxed{18.0\%}$.
