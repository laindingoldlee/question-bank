Let $O$ be the centre. The segments from $O$ to the six vertices cut the hexagon into six congruent equilateral triangles; call the area of each one $1$, so the hexagon has area $6$.

$ABOF$ is a rhombus made of two of these triangles (all its sides equal the side of the hexagon), and its diagonal $FB$ cuts it into two congruent halves, so $[ABF]=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot2=1$. In the same way $[BCD]=1$, using the rhombus $BCDO$. The rest of the hexagon is the quadrilateral $BDEF$, with area $6-1-1=4$. The ratio is
\[
\dfrac{1+1}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}.
\]
