Let $n$ be a positive integer and let $a_1,a_2,\dots,a_{n+2}$ be distinct positive integers.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove or disprove: $n$ always divides
\[A=(a_1-a_2)(a_2-a_3)\cdots(a_{n+1}-a_{n+2}).\]
\item[(b)] Prove that $n$ always divides the product of all the differences $a_i-a_j$ with $1\le i<j\le n+2$.
\end{enumerate}
