Let $t=x+\dfrac{1}{x}$, so $t>0$ and $x^2+\dfrac{1}{x^2}=t^2-2$. The equation becomes
\[t^2-2+t+1=89,\qquad t^2+t-90=0,\qquad (t-9)(t+10)=0,\]
so $t=9$. (Such $x$ exists, since $x+\dfrac{1}{x}=9$ has the positive root $x=\dfrac{9+\sqrt{77}}{2}$.) Then $x^2+\dfrac{1}{x^2}=81-2=79$ and
\[x^4+\dfrac{1}{x^4}=79^2-2=6239 ,\]
which leaves remainder $\boxed{239}$ on division by $1000$.
