$2013$ has digit sum $6$, so it is divisible by $3$: $2013=3\cdot671$. The alternating digit sum of $671$ is $6-7+1=0$, so $671=11\cdot61$, and $61$ is prime. Hence $2013=3\cdot11\cdot61$ and the sum is $3+11+61=\boxed{75}$.
