The sum of all nine digits is $45$. Since the left side is $9(a+c)+45-h-i$, equality gives $11h+2i=45+9(a+c)$. Modulo $9$, this implies $h+i\equiv0\pmod9$. The distinct nonzero digits have sum between $3$ and $17$, so $h+i=9$.
The left side is consequently $36+9(a+c)\ge36+9(1+2)=63$. The right side is a two-digit multiple of $9$ with nonzero digits summing to $9$. Thus only $63,72,81$ remain. All occur:
\[14+25+7+8+9=63,\quad14+35+6+8+9=72,\quad24+35+6+7+9=81.\]
The answer is $\boxed{63,72,81}$.
