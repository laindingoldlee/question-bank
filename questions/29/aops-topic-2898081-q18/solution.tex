Minor arc $AB$ is $135^\circ$, and since $BC$ is a diameter, the arc $AC$ not containing $B$ is $180^\circ-135^\circ=45^\circ$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(2.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0),B=dir(200),C=-B,A=dir(200+135);draw(circle(O,1));draw(B--C);draw(B--A--C);label("$B$",B,W);label("$C$",C,E);label("$A$",A,S);dot(O);
\end{asy}

The inscribed angle $\angle ABC$ subtends arc $AC$, so $\angle ABC=\boxed{22.5^\circ}$.
