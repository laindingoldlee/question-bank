Four individual registrations cost $12$, so a full team saves $1$. A team of at most three costs $11$ and never improves on individual registration. Thus use $16$ full teams and three individual entries: $16\cdot11+3\cdot3=\boxed{185}$ dollars. No arrangement can save more than $\lfloor\dfrac{67}{4}\rfloor=16$ dollars from the individual cost $201$.
