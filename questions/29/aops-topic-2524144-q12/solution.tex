Completing the square, $f(x)=\left(x-\dfrac52\right)^2-\dfrac14$. The vertex $x=\dfrac52$ lies in $[-4,8]$, so the minimum is $-\dfrac14$. The maximum is at the endpoint farther from $\dfrac52$, namely $x=-4$: $f(-4)=16+20+6=42$ (while $f(8)=30$). As $f$ is continuous, every value in between is taken. The range is $\boxed{\left[-\dfrac14,\,42\right]}$.
