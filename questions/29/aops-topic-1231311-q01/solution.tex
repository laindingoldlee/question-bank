Count by the smallest part $c$: then $b$ runs from $c$ to $\left\lfloor\dfrac{18-c}{2}\right\rfloor$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $c=1$: $b=1,\dots,8$: $8$; \quad $c=2$: $b=2,\dots,8$: $7$; \quad $c=3$: $b=3,\dots,7$: $5$;
\item $c=4$: $b=4,\dots,7$: $4$; \quad $c=5$: $b=5,6$: $2$; \quad $c=6$: $b=6$: $1$.
\end{itemize}
Total $\boxed{27}$. In general, the number of partitions of $n$ into exactly $3$ positive parts is the integer nearest to $\dfrac{n^2}{12}$ (here $\dfrac{324}{12}=27$); for more parts there is no equally simple closed form, but the counts $p_k(n)$ satisfy the recursion $p_k(n)=p_{k-1}(n-1)+p_k(n-k)$.
