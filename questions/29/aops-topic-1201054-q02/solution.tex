Let the numbers in order be $a\le b\le m\le d\le e$, with median $m$. To make the mean exceed the median as much as possible, take $a=b=1$, $m=1$ and $d=e=9$: mean $\dfrac{21}{5}$, median $1$, difference $\dfrac{16}{5}$. In general the mean minus the median is $\dfrac{a+b+d+e-4m}{5}\le\dfrac{2m+18-4m}{5}\le\dfrac{16}{5}$ (using $a,b\le m$ and $d,e\le9$, then $m\ge1$); the case mean below median is symmetric ($m=9$, $d=e=9$, $a=b=1$). So the greatest difference is $\boxed{\dfrac{16}{5}}$.
