Subtracting $16$ from both sides gives $x=28-16=\boxed{12}$. In general, subtract $b$ and then divide both sides by nonzero $a$, obtaining $\boxed{x=\dfrac{c-b}{a}}$. The apparent sign change is shorthand for subtracting the same additive term from both sides; moving a multiplicative factor instead requires division.
