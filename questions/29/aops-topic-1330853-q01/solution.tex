Since $AB\parallel CD$, triangles $ABO$ and $CDO$ are similar (alternate angles), with ratio $k=\dfrac{AO}{OC}=\dfrac{BO}{OD}$, so $\dfrac{S_1}{S_2}=k^2$ and $k=\sqrt{\dfrac{S_1}{S_2}}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(7,0),C=(5,4),D=(1.5,4);
pair O=extension(A,C,B,D);
fill(A--B--O--cycle,paleblue); fill(C--D--O--cycle,palered);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(A--C); draw(B--D);
dot(O);
label("$A$",A,SW); label("$B$",B,SE); label("$C$",C,NE); label("$D$",D,NW); label("$O$",O,2*E);
label("$S_1$",(A+B+O)/3); label("$S_2$",(C+D+O)/3+(0,0.15));
label("$T$",(A+D+O)/3); label("$T$",(B+C+O)/3);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Triangles $AOD$ and $COD$ have the same height from $D$ to line $AC$, so
\[
[AOD]=\dfrac{AO}{OC}\,[COD]=kS_2=\sqrt{S_1S_2} .
\]
Similarly $[BOC]=\dfrac{BO}{OD}[COD]=\sqrt{S_1S_2}$. (These are the two triangles labelled $T$.) Hence the area of the trapezoid is
\[
S_1+S_2+2\sqrt{S_1S_2}=\boxed{\left(\sqrt{S_1}+\sqrt{S_2}\right)^2}.
\]
