A courtyard has the shape of a parallelogram $ABCD$. Poles $AA'$, $BB'$, $CC'$, $DD'$ stand perpendicular to the ground at the corners, with heights $AA'=68$, $BB'=75$, $CC'=112$ and $DD'=133$ centimetres. Find the distance between the midpoints of $A'C'$ and $B'D'$.
