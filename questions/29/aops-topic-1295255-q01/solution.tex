The diagonals of a parallelogram bisect each other at a point $O$. The midpoint of $A'C'$ lies directly above the midpoint of $AC$, which is $O$, at height $\dfrac{68+112}{2}=90$; the midpoint of $B'D'$ lies directly above the midpoint of $BD$, also $O$, at height $\dfrac{75+133}{2}=104$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(6,0),C=(8,3),D=(2,3),O=(A+C)/2;
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); draw(A--C,gray+dashed); draw(B--D,gray+dashed);
real s=0.035; pair A1=A+(0,68*s),B1=B+(0,75*s),C1=C+(0,112*s),D1=D+(0,133*s);
draw(A--A1); draw(B--B1); draw(C--C1); draw(D--D1); draw(A1--C1,blue); draw(B1--D1,red);
dot((A1+C1)/2,blue); dot((B1+D1)/2,red); dot(O);
label("$A$",A,SW); label("$B$",B,SE); label("$C$",C,E); label("$D$",D,W); label("$A'$",A1,W); label("$C'$",C1,E); label("$B'$",B1,E); label("$D'$",D1,N);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

So the two midpoints are on the same vertical line, $\boxed{14}$ cm apart.
