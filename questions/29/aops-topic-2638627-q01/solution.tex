The polynomial $f(x)=x^6-10x^5+29x^4-4x^3+ax^2-bx-c$ is nonnegative and vanishes at exactly three points $p,q,r$. At each of them $f$ does not change sign, so each is a root of even multiplicity, at least $2$. As $\deg f=6$, each has multiplicity exactly $2$ and
\[f(x)=(x-p)^2(x-q)^2(x-r)^2=\left(x^3+ux^2+vx+w\right)^2,\]
where $x^3+ux^2+vx+w=(x-p)(x-q)(x-r)$. Expanding, the coefficients of $x^5,x^4,x^3$ give
\[2u=-10,\qquad u^2+2v=29,\qquad2w+2uv=-4,\]
so $u=-5$, $v=2$, $w=8$, and
\[x^3-5x^2+2x+8=(x+1)(x-2)(x-4).\]
The intersection points are $-1,2,4$, and the largest is $\boxed{\textbf{(A) }4}$.
