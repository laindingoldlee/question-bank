Let the sum be $S$. The fixed digits sum to $17$. Divisibility by $11$ gives $S\equiv17\equiv6\pmod{11}$, and divisibility by $9$ gives $S\equiv-17\equiv1\pmod9$. Since $0\le S\le45$, the first condition allows $6,17,28,39$; only $28$ is $1$ modulo $9$. Hence $\boxed{28}$. A digit choice such as $(A,B,C,D,E)=(9,9,9,1,0)$ shows attainability.
