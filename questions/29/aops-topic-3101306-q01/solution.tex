The inner quotient $\dfrac{y}{0}$ is undefined for every real $y$, including $y=0$. Therefore the outer quotient is also undefined. The rule $\dfrac{x}{\dfrac{a}{b}}=\dfrac{xb}{a}$ requires $b\ne0$ and $a\ne0$; applying it when $b=0$ discards a required domain condition. Thus the given expression is $\boxed{\text{undefined}}$.
