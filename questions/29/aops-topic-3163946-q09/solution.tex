The expression under the outer radical is $(\sqrt{x+1}+\sqrt x)^2$, so $f(x)=\sqrt{x+1}+\sqrt x$. Rationalizing gives $\dfrac{1}{f(n)}=\sqrt{n+1}-\sqrt n$. The sum telescopes to $\sqrt{25}-\sqrt1=\boxed4$.
