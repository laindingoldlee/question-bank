$|w+rz|^2=|w|^2+r^2|z|^2+2r\operatorname{Re}(w\bar z)$, and $c^2=a^2+b^2+2\operatorname{Re}(w\bar z)$. Hence
\[|w+rz|=\boxed{\sqrt{a^2+r^2b^2+r\left(c^2-a^2-b^2\right)}}.\]
