A canoeist paddled upstream for $2$ hours, then downstream for $3$ hours. The current flows at $2$ mph, and she paddles at a constant speed relative to the water. When she stopped, she found she was $20$ miles downstream from her starting point. How many hours will it take her to paddle back to her starting point?
