Let her speed in still water be $v$ mph. Upstream she moves at $v-2$ mph and downstream at $v+2$ mph, so her final position is
\[3(v+2)-2(v-2)=v+10\]
miles downstream of the start. Hence $v+10=20$ and $v=10$. Going back upstream she moves at $10-2=8$ mph, so the $20$ miles take
\[\dfrac{20}{8}=\boxed{2.5\text{ hours}}\]
(2 hours 30 minutes). The answer $5\dfrac{1}{2}$ hours proposed in the post is not correct.
