List the sums by size.
\begin{itemize}
\item One element: $1,3,5,7$; the primes are $3,5,7$: $3$ subsets.
\item Two elements: the sums of two odd numbers are even and at least $4$, so none is prime.
\item Three elements: $1+3+5=9$, $1+3+7=11$, $1+5+7=13$, $3+5+7=15$; the primes are $11$ and $13$: $2$ subsets.
\item All four: $16$, not prime.
\end{itemize}
The answer is $3+2=\boxed{5}$.
