For an integer with at least three digits define it smae to be the integer formed by swapping the second and third digit. For example, the smae of $12345$ is $13245$. Let $n$ be a $5$-digit positive integer, and let $m$ be its smae. Suppose $n$ is the unique such $5$-digit integer whose prime factorization is the first three digits of $m$ times the last three digits of $m$. Find $n$.
