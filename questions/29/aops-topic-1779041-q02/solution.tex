Write $z=x+yi$. Then $z-\overline z=2yi$, so $|z-\overline z|=2|y|$. The condition says $z$ lies on the circle with centre $i$ and radius $2$, so $y$ ranges over $[1-2,\,1+2]=[-1,3]$. Hence $|y|\le3$, with $|y|=3$ at $z=3i$, and the maximum is $\boxed{6}$.
