Amy and Rob play a game with a fair $10$-sided die whose faces are numbered $1$ through $10$. On each roll, Rob earns $3$ points if the number rolled is prime, and Amy earns $2$ points if the number rolled is even (both earn points when a $2$ is rolled). After $4$ rolls, what is the probability that Amy has more points than Rob? Express your answer as a percent to the nearest hundredth.
