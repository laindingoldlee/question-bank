An ice cream with a flavour, a topping and a cone is determined by one choice of each, so by the multiplication principle there are
\[5\cdot6\cdot8=\boxed{240}\]
of them. The information that he skips the topping three quarters of the time does not affect how many such ice creams are possible. (Without a topping there would be $5\cdot8=40$ more.)
