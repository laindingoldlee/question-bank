Write the six numbers as $2k+1,2k+3,\dots,2k+11$ with $k\ge0$. Their sum is
\[
6\cdot2k+(1+3+5+7+9+11)=12k+36=12(k+3),
\]
so $12$ always divides the sum. No larger number must: the sums for $k=0$ and $k=1$ are $36$ and $48$, and $\gcd(36,48)=12$. The answer is $\boxed{12}$.
