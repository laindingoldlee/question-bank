Take a grape of mass $100$ g: $80$ g of water and $20$ g of solids. Drying removes only water, so the raisin still has $20$ g of solids, which make up $80\%$ of it. The raisin's mass is $\dfrac{20}{0.8}=25$ g, of which $25-20=5$ g is water. The fraction of the water that remains is
\[
\dfrac{5}{80}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{16}}.
\]
