Choose two distinct points on each axis. The two segments joining them in reversed endpoint order intersect once in the positive quadrant; the other pairing does not. Conversely each interior intersection uniquely determines these four endpoints. No three segments concur, so nothing is counted twice. Thus the number is $\binom{10}{2}\binom{20}{2}=45\cdot190=\boxed{8550}$.
