The curves $x^2+y^2=1$ and $(x^2+y^2)^2=16x^2y^2$ intersect in eight points. The area of their convex octagon is $a+\sqrt b$, where $a,b$ are positive integers and $b$ is squarefree. Find $2a^2+b^2$.
