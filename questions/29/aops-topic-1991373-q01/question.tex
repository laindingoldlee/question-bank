Four angles around a point $O$ have measures $x,y,z,t$ (in degrees, all positive, with $x+y+z+t=360$). The numbers $x,y,z$ are directly proportional to $x+y$, $y+z$, $z+x$, that is,
\[
\dfrac{x}{x+y}=\dfrac{y}{y+z}=\dfrac{z}{z+x},
\]
and the numbers $y,z,t$ are inversely proportional to $y+z$, $z+t$, $t+y$, that is,
\[
y(y+z)=z(z+t)=t(t+y).
\]
Determine the four angles.
