Choose $A=(0,0),B=(20,0),C=(20,26),T=(0,6)$. The vector $TC=(20,20)$ makes $45^\circ$ with the positive horizontal direction; $TA$ points vertically downward. Thus the interior angle $ATC$ is $135^\circ$, and its bisector $TM$ points at $-22.5^\circ$ to the horizontal. It meets $AB$ at horizontal distance $6\cot22.5^\circ=6(1+\sqrt2)<20$, so $M$ is indeed on the segment. At $M$, the ray $MB$ points right and $MT$ points at $157.5^\circ$. Hence $\boxed{\angle BMT=157.5^\circ}$.
