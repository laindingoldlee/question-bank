The characteristic equation $t^2=2t+3$ has roots $3$ and $-1$, so $a_n=A\cdot3^n+B(-1)^n$. From $a_0=A+B=2$ and $a_1=3A-B=2$ we get $A=B=1$, so $a_n=3^n+(-1)^n$ and
\[a_8=3^8+1=\boxed{6562}.\]
(Directly: $2,2,10,26,82,242,730,2186,6562$.)
