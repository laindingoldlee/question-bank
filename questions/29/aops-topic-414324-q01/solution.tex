Height on bounce $n$ is $h_n=3(\dfrac{2}{3})^n$. We have $h_4=\dfrac{16}{27}>\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $h_5=\dfrac{32}{81}<\dfrac{1}{2}$. Heights decrease, so the first qualifying bounce is $\boxed5$, choice (C).
