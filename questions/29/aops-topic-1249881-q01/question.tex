A test has $100$ multiple-choice questions, each with $4$ choices of which exactly one is correct. You want to get every question wrong. On each question whose answer you know, you choose a wrong option; on each other question you choose one of the $4$ options at random (independently, each with probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$).
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What percent of the questions must you know to be \emph{certain} of getting all $100$ wrong?
\item[(b)] What is the smallest percent you must know for the probability of getting all $100$ wrong to be at least $\dfrac{1}{2}$?
\end{enumerate}
