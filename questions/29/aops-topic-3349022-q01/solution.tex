For the first six primes, the product plus one is $2\cdot3\cdot5\cdot7\cdot11\cdot13+1=30031=59\cdot509$, so it is composite. However, none of the primes included in the product can divide the new number, because division by each leaves remainder one. Thus it always has a prime factor not among those used, but the new number itself need not be prime.
