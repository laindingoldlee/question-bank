The distances between centres are the sums of radii: $1+2=3$, $1+3=4$, $2+3=5$. A $3$-$4$-$5$ triangle is right-angled.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(3,0),C=(0,4);draw(circle(A,1));draw(circle(B,2));draw(circle(C,3));draw(A--B--C--cycle);dot(A);dot(B);dot(C);
label("$3$",(1.5,0),S);label("$4$",(0,2),W);label("$5$",(1.5,2),NE);
\end{asy}

Its area is $\dfrac12\cdot3\cdot4=\boxed{6}$.
