Clearly $a\ne0$. Taking reciprocals, $\dfrac{a^2+a+1}{a}=6$, that is $a+\dfrac{1}{a}=5$. Squaring, $a^2+2+\dfrac{1}{a^2}=25$, so $a^2+\dfrac{1}{a^2}=23$. Then
\[\dfrac{a^4+a^2+1}{a^2}=a^2+1+\dfrac{1}{a^2}=24,\qquad\text{so}\qquad \dfrac{a^2}{a^4+a^2+1}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{24}}.\]
