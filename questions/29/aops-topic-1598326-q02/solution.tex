Let the middle digit be $m$. The first digit can be any of $1,\dots,m-1$ (it is not $0$), giving $m-1$ choices, and the last digit any of $0,\dots,m-1$, giving $m$ choices. So $m\ge2$ and the count is
\[
\sum_{m=2}^{9}(m-1)m=1\cdot2+2\cdot3+\dots+8\cdot9=2+6+12+20+30+42+56+72=\boxed{240}.
\]
