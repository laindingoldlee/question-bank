B prime is the antipode of B, so $\angle BCB^{\prime}=90^\circ$. Heron gives $\Delta=6\sqrt6$, and $R=5\cdot6\cdot\dfrac{7}{(4\Delta)}=\dfrac{35}{(4\sqrt6)}$. Hence $B^{\prime}C^2=(2R)^2-7^2=\dfrac{1225}{24}-49=\dfrac{49}{24}$. Therefore $\boxed{B^{\prime}C=\dfrac{7\sqrt6}{12}}$.
