The final two digits must be $00,25,50,$ or $75$. (a) For each ending, there are $7$ choices for the leading digit and $8$ each for the next two. The total is $4\cdot7\cdot8^2=\boxed{1792}$. (b) The ending $00$ is excluded. Endings $25$ and $75$ each leave five nonzero leading choices, then five and four choices, contributing $100$ each. Ending $50$ leaves six possible leading digits, then five and four choices, contributing $120$. The answer is $\boxed{320}$.
