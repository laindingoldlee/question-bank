We need $7^{2011}$ modulo $100$. Since $7^2=49$ and $7^4=2401\equiv1\pmod{100}$, and $2011=4\cdot502+3$,
\[7^{2011}=\left(7^4\right)^{502}\cdot7^3\equiv7^3=343\equiv43\pmod{100}.\]
The last two digits are $43$, so the tens digit is $\boxed{4}$.
