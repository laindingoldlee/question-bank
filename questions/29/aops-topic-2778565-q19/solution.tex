Write the original fraction as $\dfrac{2k}{7k}$ with $k\ge1$. Then $18(2k-X)=5(7k-X)$ gives $k=13X$. Thus $X$ is positive and at least 1. Taking $X=1,k=13$ yields $\dfrac{26}{91}$ originally and $\dfrac{25}{90}=\dfrac{5}{18}$ after subtraction. Hence $\boxed1$.
