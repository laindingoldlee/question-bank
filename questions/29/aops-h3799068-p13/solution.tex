Sort the 23 classmates's candy counts as $a_1\le\cdots\le a_{23}$. The first sixteen sum to at least 100, forcing $a_{16}\ge7$. Therefore the total distributed is at least $100+7\cdot7=149$. This bound is attained by giving six candies to twelve classmates and seven to eleven: the sixteen smallest counts sum to $12\cdot6+4\cdot7=100$. Daniyar can keep $200-149=\boxed{51}$ candies.
