The center displacement resolves into tangent-parallel length $L$ and perpendicular difference $18-3=15$. Pythagoras gives $L=\sqrt{25^2-15^2}=\boxed{20}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair O=(0,0),P=(25,0),u=(-.6,.8),A=3*u,B=P+18*u;draw(circle(O,3));draw(circle(P,18));draw(A--B,blue);draw(O--P,dashed);draw(O--A);draw(P--B);label("$3$",(O+A)/2,W);label("$18$",(P+B)/2,E);
\end{asy}
