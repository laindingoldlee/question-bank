Let $T_i=a_i+a_{i+1}+a_{i+2}$, with indices modulo $18$. Their sum is $3(1+\cdots+18)=513$, so their average is $28.5$. (a) No, since all sums would be at least $30$. (b) Yes: use the cyclic order $1,9,17,2,11,15,3,12,13,5,10,14,6,7,16,4,8,18$. The consecutive triple sums are $27,28,30,28,29,30,28,30,28,29,30,27,29,27,28,30,27,28$, all in the required interval. (c) No. If every $T_i\le29$, set $d_i=29-T_i\ge0$, so $\sum d_i=522-513=9$. Then $a_{i+3}-a_i=d_i-d_{i+1}$, giving $\sum|a_{i+3}-a_i|\le2\sum d_i=18$. But the step-three indices split into three cycles of six distinct integers. Each cycle has range at least $5$ and total variation at least twice its range, so the same sum is at least $3\cdot10=30$. This contradiction excludes (c).
