There are $\binom{10}{2}=45$ equally likely unordered pairs of individual socks. Eight are rejected: either of two brown socks with any of four green or black socks. Thus $37$ accepted pairs remain. Exactly five are matching pairs, one per colour. Repeated rejection and resampling gives the uniform distribution on the accepted pairs, so the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{37-5}{37}=\dfrac{32}{37}}$.
