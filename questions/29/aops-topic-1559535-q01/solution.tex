At a constant rate $v$, the extra $q$ yards take $\dfrac {q}{v}$ extra seconds. So $\dfrac {q}{v}=\dfrac {q}{8}$, which gives $v=\boxed{8}$ yards per second. (Equivalently, $\dfrac {y}{t}=\dfrac{y+q}{t+\dfrac {q}{8}}$ forces $\dfrac {y}{t}=8$.) The values of $y$ and $t$ are not needed beyond $y=8t$.
