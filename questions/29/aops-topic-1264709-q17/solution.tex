A block of four repeating digits in base $8$ gives
\[0.\overline{1337}_8=\dfrac{1337_8}{8^4-1}.\]
Now $1337_8=1\cdot512+3\cdot64+3\cdot8+7=735$ and $8^4-1=4095$. Since $735=3\cdot5\cdot7^2$ and $4095=3^2\cdot5\cdot7\cdot13$, the greatest common divisor is $105$, and
\[\dfrac{735}{4095}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{39}}.\]
