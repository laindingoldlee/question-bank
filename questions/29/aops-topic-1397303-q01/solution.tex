If $\gcd(a,b)=13$, then $13\mid a$ and $13\mid b$, so $13^2=169$ divides $ab$. But
\[
897=3\cdot13\cdot23
\]
is divisible by $13$ only once, so $169\nmid897$. Hence no positive integers satisfy both conditions.

The formula $\gcd(a,b)\cdot\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=ab$ is true, but it only gives the lcm of numbers that exist. Here it gives $69$, which is not even a multiple of $13$, while any common multiple of $a$ and $b$ would have to be. (For example, $a=13$, $b=69$ has $ab=897$, but $\gcd(13,69)=1$.)
