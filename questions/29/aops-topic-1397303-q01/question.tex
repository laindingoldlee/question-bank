A student is asked: ``Two positive integers $a$ and $b$ satisfy $\gcd(a,b)=13$ and $ab=897$. Find $\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)$.'' The student uses $\gcd(a,b)\cdot\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=ab$ and answers $\dfrac{897}{13}=69$.
Show that in fact no such integers $a$ and $b$ exist, so the question has no answer.
