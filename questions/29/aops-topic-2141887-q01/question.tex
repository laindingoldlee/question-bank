Lines $q$ and $r$ in the coordinate plane are perpendicular, and neither is vertical. Let $x$ be the product of their slopes, and let $C$ be the point $(x,x)$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the coordinates of $C$.
\item[(b)] Find the coordinates of the image of $C$ under a rotation of $270^\circ$ clockwise about the origin.
\item[(c)] Prove that the product of the slopes of two perpendicular, non-vertical lines is always the same number.
\end{enumerate}
