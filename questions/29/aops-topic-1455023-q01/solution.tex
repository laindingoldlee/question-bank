We need $x\ne5$ and $x\ne\dfrac{5}{2}$. Since $10-4x=2(5-2x)$, multiply both sides by $2(5-x)(5-2x)$:
\[
2(3x-6)(5-2x)+(11-2x)(5-x)=7(5-x)(5-2x).
\]
The left side is $(-12x^2+54x-60)+(2x^2-21x+55)=-10x^2+33x-5$, and the right side is $14x^2-105x+175$. So
\[
24x^2-138x+180=0\iff4x^2-23x+30=0\iff(x-2)(4x-15)=0 .
\]
Both roots are allowed, so $x=\boxed{2}$ or $x=\boxed{\dfrac{15}{4}}$. Check: for $x=2$, $0+\dfrac{7}{2}=\dfrac{7}{2}$; for $x=\dfrac{15}{4}$, $\dfrac{21}{5}-\dfrac{7}{10}=\dfrac{7}{2}$.
