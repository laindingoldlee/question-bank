If the side is $s$, the Pythagorean theorem gives $s^2+s^2=\left(2\sqrt2\right)^2=8$, so the area is $s^2=\boxed{4}$. (Equivalently, the area of a square is half the square of its diagonal.)

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(2.6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(2,0),C=(2,2),D=(0,2);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.8)); draw(A--C,dashed);
label("$s$",(A+B)/2,S); label("$s$",(B+C)/2,E);
label("$2\sqrt2$",(A+C)/2,NW);
real m=0.18; draw((m,0)--(m,m)--(0,m));
\end{asy}
