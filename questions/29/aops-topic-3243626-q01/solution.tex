Let $X\sim\operatorname{Bin}(2,\dfrac{1}{2})$ and $Y\sim\operatorname{Bin}(10,\dfrac{1}{10})$, independent. The probability is $\sum_{a=0}^2\binom2a(\dfrac{1}{2})^2\sum_{b=\max(0,4-a)}^{10}\binom{10}{b}(\dfrac{1}{10})^b(\dfrac{9}{10})^{10-b}=0.104269480525\ldots$. Thus the requested value is $\boxed{0.104}$.
