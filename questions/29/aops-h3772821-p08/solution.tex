The sides of lengths $2$ and $9$ cannot belong to the same triangle with diagonal $4$, since $2+4<9$. In the triangle containing side $2$, isoscelesness forces sides $(2,4,4)$: $(2,2,4)$ would be degenerate. The other triangle must have sides $(9,9,4)$ because $(9,4,4)$ fails the triangle inequality. Omitting the shared diagonal, the quadrilateral perimeter is $2+4+9+9=\boxed{24}$.
