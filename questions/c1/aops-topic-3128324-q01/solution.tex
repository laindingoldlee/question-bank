Expand the first cube: it contains each $r_i^3$ once, each term $r_i^2r_j$ with $i\ne j$ three times, and each product of three distinct variables six times. The middle product before multiplying by $-3$ contains each $r_i^2r_j$ once and each distinct triple three times. Hence those coefficients cancel as $3-3=0$ and $6-9+3=0$, leaving exactly the sum of cubes. This is Newton's third power-sum identity and holds for every $n\ge1$.
