For real numbers $r_1,\ldots,r_n$, prove $\displaystyle\sum_i r_i^3=(\sum_i r_i)^3-3(\sum_i r_i)(\sum_{i<j}r_ir_j)+3\sum_{i<j<k}r_ir_jr_k$. Empty sums are 0.
