Consider the parabola $y=x^2$ and an ellipse in the same plane.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that they meet in at most $4$ points.
\item[(b)] Show that each of $0,1,2,3,4$ intersection points is possible.
\end{enumerate}
