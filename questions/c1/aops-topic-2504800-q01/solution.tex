Let $a=\sqrt[3]{x}$ and $b=\sqrt[3]{y}$. Then $a-b=2$ and $a^3-b^3=12$. Since
\[a^3-b^3=(a-b)^3+3ab(a-b)=8+6ab,\]
we get $8+6ab=12$, so $ab=\dfrac23$. Therefore
\[xy=(ab)^3=\boxed{\dfrac{8}{27}}.\]
(Such numbers exist: $b=-1+\sqrt{\dfrac53}$ and $a=b+2$ satisfy $a-b=2$ and $ab=\dfrac23$.)
