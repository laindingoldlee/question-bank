To reach $n$, the required increases are $n-20,n-201,n-2016$. The first increase cannot exceed the sum of the other two, so $n-20\le2n-2217$, giving $n\ge2197$. This value is attainable: increment the first two entries together 1996 times, then the first and third together 181 times. All become $\boxed{2197}$.
