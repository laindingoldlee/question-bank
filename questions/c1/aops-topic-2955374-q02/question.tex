A sequence of five nonzero real numbers $a,b,c,d,e$ has the property that each term plus its neighbouring term(s) equals $15$ or $25$; that is, each of $a+b$, $a+b+c$, $b+c+d$, $c+d+e$, $d+e$ is $15$ or $25$. Find $a+b+c+d+e$.
