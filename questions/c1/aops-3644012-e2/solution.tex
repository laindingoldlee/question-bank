An inscribed angle subtending a diameter is right. Hence $\boxed{\angle ATW=90^\circ}$; equidistance is not needed.
\begin{asy}
import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
pair A=(-1,0),W=(1,0),T=(0,1);draw(circle((0,0),1));draw(A--T--W--cycle);label("$A$",A,(-1,0));label("$W$",W,(1,0));label("$T$",T,(0,1));
\end{asy}
