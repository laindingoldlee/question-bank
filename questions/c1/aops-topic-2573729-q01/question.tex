For a real number $x$, the symbol $\lfloor x\rfloor$ (the floor of $x$) denotes the greatest integer less than or equal to $x$. Compute $\lfloor3.7\rfloor$, $\lfloor-3.7\rfloor$, $\lfloor5\rfloor$ and $\left\lfloor\sqrt{50}\right\rfloor$, and explain why $\lfloor x\rfloor=x$ exactly when $x$ is an integer.
