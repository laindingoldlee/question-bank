The number $cd^4$ has $(1+1)(4+1)=10$ divisors, so we need $10\mid cd^4$, i.e. both $2$ and $5$ divide $cd^4$. Since only the primes $c$ and $d$ occur, $\{c,d\}=\{2,5\}$. The numbers are $5\cdot2^4=80$ and $2\cdot5^4=1250$, i.e. $\boxed{80\text{ and }1250}$ (with $f=8$ and $125$).
