Work modulo $3$. Since $2\equiv-1$ and $5\equiv-1\pmod 3$,
\[
2^{2010}\equiv(-1)^{2010}=1,\qquad 5^{2011}\equiv(-1)^{2011}=-1\pmod 3,
\]
so $A\equiv1-1=0\pmod3$. Thus $3$ divides $A$, and $A>3$, so $A$ has the proper divisor $3$ and is $\boxed{\text{not prime}}$.
