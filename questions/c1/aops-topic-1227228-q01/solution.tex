This is the number of derangements of $4$ objects: $D_4=4!\left(1-1+\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{6}+\dfrac{1}{24}\right)=24-24+12-4+1=\boxed{9}$. (Directly: $3$ ways to pair the bosses into two swaps, plus $3!=6$ four-cycles.)
