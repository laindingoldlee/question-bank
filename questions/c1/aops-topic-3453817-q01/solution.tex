Since $91=7\cdot13$ and $P,S$ are distinct nonzero digits, they are $1,7$ in either order. The middle digits sum to $13$ and exclude $1,7$. Their four-element set must include $0$, since otherwise the least sum is $2+3+4+5=14$. Enumerating triples from $2,3,4,5,6,8,9$ gives precisely $\{0,2,3,8\},\{0,2,5,6\},\{0,3,4,6\}$. Each has $4!$ orders, giving $3\cdot24\cdot2=\boxed{144}$.
