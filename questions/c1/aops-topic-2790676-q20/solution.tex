Two consecutive replacements multiply both original entries by 2, possibly interchanging them: $(a,b)\mapsto(a+b,|a-b|)\mapsto(2\max(a,b),2\min(a,b))$. After the first step the entries are 12 and 2. After 1008 further pairs of steps, both are multiplied by $ 2^{1008}$. Their product is $ 24\cdot2^{2016}=3\cdot2^{2019}$, with $(2019+1)(1+1)=\boxed{4040}$ positive divisors.
