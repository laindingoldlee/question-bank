The statement is \textbf{false}. Write
\[100!=1\cdot2\cdot3\cdots100=2\cdot3\cdot4\cdots100,\]
a product of $99$ factors, each at least $2$, and most of them larger than $2$. Hence $100!>2^{99}$ (already $3\cdot4>2\cdot2$, and every other factor is at least $2$). So $2^{99}<100!$. $\blacksquare$
