Unit cubes form a completely filled rectangular stack on an impenetrable floor. One red cube lies in the bottom layer, and all others are blue. A person can expose a face of the red cube by removing a face-connected tunnel of blue cubes from outside the stack. What is the fewest blue cubes possible if every such tunnel must remove at least five cubes?
