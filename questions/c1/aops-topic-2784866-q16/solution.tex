There must be at least five cubes between the red cube and each of the four vertical sides, forcing both horizontal dimensions to be at least $ 5+1+5=11 $. At least five cubes must lie above it, forcing height at least 6. Thus there are at least $ 11\cdot11\cdot6-1=725 $ blue cubes. The fully filled $ 11\times11\times6 $ stack with the red cube horizontally centered on the floor attains the bound: the shortest face-connected route to it from any accessible exterior side passes through five blue cubes. Therefore the answer is $\boxed{725}$.
