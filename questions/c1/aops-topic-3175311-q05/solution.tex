Let $m=\min(w,x,y)$. Divide the first equation by $3^m$. The right side is a power of $3$, and the left side has one, two, or three terms congruent to $1$ modulo $3$, according to how many exponents attain the minimum. It cannot be a positive power of $3$ unless all three attain it; nor can it equal $1$, since the sum is at least $3$. Hence $w=x=y=m$ and $z=m+1$. The sum condition gives $4m+1=2017$, so the unique quadruple is $\boxed{(504,504,504,505)}$.
