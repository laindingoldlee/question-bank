Let $g(x)=f(x)+14$. Then $g$ is a cubic with $g(1)=g(2)=0$, so $g(x)=(x-1)(x-2)(ax+b)$. From $g(3)=4$ and $g(4)=18$:
\[2(3a+b)=4,\qquad6(4a+b)=18,\]
so $3a+b=2$, $4a+b=3$, giving $a=1$, $b=-1$. Hence $g(x)=(x-1)^2(x-2)=x^3-4x^2+5x-2$ and
\[\boxed{f(x)=x^3-4x^2+5x-16}.\]
(Check: $f(3)=27-36+15-16=-10$ and $f(4)=64-64+20-16=4$.)
