By the binomial theorem with $i^2=-1$,
\[
(1+i)^{20}=\sum_{k=0}^{20}\dbinom{20}{k}i^k .
\]
The even-$k$ terms are real, with $i^{2j}=(-1)^j$, and the odd-$k$ terms are imaginary. So the requested sum is the real part of $(1+i)^{20}$:
\[
\dbinom{20}{0}-\dbinom{20}{2}+\dbinom{20}{4}-\cdots+\dbinom{20}{20}=\operatorname{Re}(1+i)^{20}.
\]
Since $(1+i)^2=2i$, we get $(1+i)^{20}=(2i)^{10}=2^{10}i^{10}=1024\cdot(-1)=-1024$. Hence the sum is $\boxed{-1024}$. (The last term is $+\dbinom{20}{20}$ because $20=2\cdot10$ and $(-1)^{10}=1$.)
