First, $7^4=2401\equiv401\pmod{1000}$. Then
\[7^{20}=\left(7^4\right)^5\equiv401^5=(1+400)^5\pmod{1000}.\]
By the binomial theorem, $(1+400)^5=1+5\cdot400+\binom52\cdot400^2+\cdots$, and every term from $400^2=160000$ on is a multiple of $1000$. So
\[7^{20}\equiv1+2000\equiv1\pmod{1000}.\]
Since $343=20\cdot17+3$,
\[7^{343}=\left(7^{20}\right)^{17}\cdot7^3\equiv1\cdot343\pmod{1000}.\]
The remainder is $\boxed{343}$.
