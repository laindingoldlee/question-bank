A strictly increasing number cannot contain the digit $0$ (it could not be the first digit, and no digit after the first is smaller than it). So a strictly increasing number with $k$ digits corresponds to a $k$-element subset of $\{1,2,\dots,9\}$, written in increasing order, and there are $\dbinom9k$ of them.

Numbers with fewer digits are smaller, so the list starts with all numbers of at most $4$ digits:
\[
\dbinom91+\dbinom92+\dbinom93+\dbinom94=9+36+84+126=255 .
\]
Hence the $256$th number is the smallest strictly increasing $5$-digit number, $\boxed{12345}$.
