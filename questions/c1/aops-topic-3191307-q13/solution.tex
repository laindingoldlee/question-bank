There are six constant triples. For positive difference $1$, the increasing triples are $123,234,345,456$; for difference $2$, they are $135,246$. These six nonconstant triples each have six orders. Thus the probability is $\dfrac{6+6\cdot6}{216}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{36}}$.
