Both players take $30$ attempts, so Candace also makes $25$ baskets. Her first-half total $a$ satisfies $\dfrac{a}{12}<\dfrac{15}{20}$, hence $a\le8$. Her second-half total $b$ is less than $18$, hence $b\le17$. Since $a+b=25$, both bounds must be equalities: $a=8,b=17$. The difference is $\boxed9$.
