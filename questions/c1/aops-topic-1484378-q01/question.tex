Let $p_1=2$, $p_2=3$, $p_3=5$, $\dots$ be the primes in order, and let $a_n=p_1p_2\cdots p_n$. Is $a_n+1$ always prime? Also explain why $a_n+1$ always has a prime factor different from $p_1,\dots,p_n$.
