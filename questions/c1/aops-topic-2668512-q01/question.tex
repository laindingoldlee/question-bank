Joe has $k$ playing cards labelled with the distinct integers $1$ to $k$. He first puts down card $k$. Then he puts down the cards $k-1$, $k-2$, \dots, $1$ in this order, each to the right of the cards already down; but whenever the leftmost card on the table is a multiple of the card just placed, those two cards swap places. For example, for $k=4$ the row develops as
\[4;\quad4,3;\quad4,3,2\to2,3,4;\quad2,3,4,1\to1,3,4,2.\]
If $k=999$, what is the rightmost card after all the cards have been placed?
