Only a swap changes the leftmost card. Suppose the leftmost card is $L$. The cards placed next are smaller than $L$, in decreasing order, so the first one that divides $L$ is the largest proper divisor of $L$; it swaps with $L$, becomes the new leftmost card, and $L$ moves to the right end. So the leftmost card runs through
\[999\to333\to111\to37,\]
each the largest proper divisor of the previous one. The largest proper divisor of the prime $37$ is $1$. When card $1$ (the last card) is placed, it divides $37$, so they swap and $37$ ends up in the rightmost position. The answer is $\boxed{37}$.

(In general the final rightmost card is the largest prime factor of $k$, for $k\ge2$.)
