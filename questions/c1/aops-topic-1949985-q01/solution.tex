Cutting off a corner tetrahedron of edge $6$ removes a corner triangle of side $6$ from each of the three faces meeting at that vertex, and exposes a new equilateral triangle of side $6$. So each original face, an equilateral triangle of side $18$, becomes a regular hexagon of side $6$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=18;pair A=(0,0),B=(s,0),C=s*dir(60);
pair p(real i,real j){return A+i*(B-A)/3+j*(C-A)/3;}
fill(p(0,0)--p(1,0)--p(0,1)--cycle,palegray);
fill(p(2,0)--p(3,0)--p(2,1)--cycle,palegray);
fill(p(0,2)--p(1,2)--p(0,3)--cycle,palegray);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(1));
draw(p(1,0)--p(2,0)--p(2,1)--p(1,2)--p(0,2)--p(0,1)--cycle,blue+linewidth(1.2));
draw(p(1,0)--p(0,1),dashed);draw(p(2,0)--p(2,1),dashed);draw(p(0,2)--p(1,2),dashed);
label("$6$",(p(0,0)+p(1,0))/2,S);label("$6$",(p(1,0)+p(2,0))/2,S);label("$6$",(p(2,0)+p(3,0))/2,S);
label("cut off",(p(0,0)+p(1,0)+p(0,1))/3+(0,-0.3),fontsize(7pt));
label("hexagon",(p(1,1)),blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

An equilateral triangle of side $6$ has area $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}\cdot36=9\sqrt3$, and the triangle of side $18$ is made of $9$ of them. Each hexagon is $9-3=6$ of them, area $54\sqrt3$. The surface consists of $4$ hexagons and $4$ new triangles:
\[
4\cdot54\sqrt3+4\cdot9\sqrt3=216\sqrt3+36\sqrt3=\boxed{252\sqrt3}.
\]
