Write $p=\dfrac{1}{4}$ and $q=\dfrac{3}{4}$. Summing over complete unsuccessful cycles, the probabilities that Robert and Luke leave first are respectively $\dfrac{p}{1-q^3}=\dfrac{16}{37}$ and $\dfrac{q^2p}{1-q^3}=\dfrac{9}{37}$. After Robert leaves, Miguel rolls next and beats Luke with probability $\dfrac{p}{1-q^2}=\dfrac{4}{7}$. After Luke leaves, Robert rolls next, so Miguel beats him with probability $\dfrac{qp}{1-q^2}=\dfrac{3}{7}$. These disjoint cases give $\dfrac{16}{37}\dfrac{4}{7}+\dfrac{9}{37}\dfrac{3}{7}=\boxed{\dfrac{13}{37}}$.
