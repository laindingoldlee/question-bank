He was born in the year $x^2-x$. We need $1801\le x^2-x\le1850$ (and he must be alive in year $x^2$). Now $42^2-42=1722$, $43^2-43=1806$ and $44^2-44=1892$, and $x^2-x$ increases with $x$, so $x=43$: he was born in $\boxed{1806}$ and was $43$ in $1849=43^2$.
