Let $\lfloor x\rfloor=-k-1$. The equation requires $k\ge 0$ and $x=-2\sqrt k$, since the floor is negative. For this to have the stated floor,\[-k-1\le-2\sqrt k<-k,\quad\text{or}\quad k<2\sqrt k\le k+1.\]The right inequality always holds because $(\sqrt k-1)^2\ge 0$. The left inequality requires $0<k<4$. Thus $k=1,2,3$, giving $x=-2,-2\sqrt 2,-2\sqrt 3$. There are $\boxed 3$ solutions.
