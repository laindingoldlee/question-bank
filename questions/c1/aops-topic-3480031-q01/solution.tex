After the first removal there are $2022-\lfloor\dfrac{2022}{6}\rfloor=1685$. After the second, $1685-\lfloor\dfrac{1685}{5}\rfloor=1348$. After the third, $1348-\lfloor\dfrac{1348}{4}\rfloor=\boxed{1011}$. Renumbering after each stage is essential.
