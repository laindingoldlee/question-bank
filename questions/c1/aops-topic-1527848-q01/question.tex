Let $n$ be a positive integer. Define a sequence by $a_1=n$ and $a_m=\left\lfloor\dfrac{a_{m-1}}{3}\right\rfloor$ for $m\ge2$; the sequence stops when it reaches $0$. The number $n$ is called \emph{lucky} if no term of the sequence other than the final $0$ is divisible by $3$. Determine the number of lucky numbers less than or equal to $1000$.
