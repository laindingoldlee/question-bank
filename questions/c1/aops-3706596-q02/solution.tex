The second equation gives $x\le7$. If $y\ge0$, it gives $x=7$, making the first equation force $y=-\dfrac{12}{5}$, a contradiction. Thus $y<0$ and $x=7+2y$. If $x<0$, the first equation gives $y=\dfrac{2}{5}$, also impossible. Hence $x\ge0$, so $2x+5y=2$. Solving with $x-2y=7$ gives $y=-\dfrac{4}{3}$, $x=\dfrac{13}{3}$. Their sum is three, and the answer is $\boxed{2019}$.
