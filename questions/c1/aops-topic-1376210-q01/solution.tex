Let $u=\sqrt[3]{n+\sqrt{n^2+8}}$ and $v=\sqrt[3]{n-\sqrt{n^2+8}}$ (real cube roots), so $u+v=8$. Then
\[
u^3+v^3=2n,\qquad uv=\sqrt[3]{\left(n+\sqrt{n^2+8}\right)\left(n-\sqrt{n^2+8}\right)}=\sqrt[3]{n^2-(n^2+8)}=\sqrt[3]{-8}=-2 .
\]
Cube $u+v=8$:
\[
(u+v)^3=u^3+v^3+3uv(u+v)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad512=2n+3(-2)(8)=2n-48,
\]
so $n=\boxed{280}$.

Check: with $n=280$, $u$ and $v$ are the roots of $t^2-8t-2=0$, i.e. $4\pm3\sqrt2$, and indeed $(4+3\sqrt2)^3=64+144\sqrt2+216+54\sqrt2=280+198\sqrt2$ with $198\sqrt2=\sqrt{78408}=\sqrt{280^2+8}$.
