Changing the first plus to minus gives $6\cdot9-6+9=57$. To check uniqueness: replacing the multiplication gives $30,12,$ or $\dfrac{47}{3}$; replacing the first plus by multiplication or division gives $333$ or $18$; replacing the last plus gives $51,108,$ or $\dfrac{164}{3}$. Only the stated change gives $57$. The original value is $54+6+9=\boxed{69}$.
