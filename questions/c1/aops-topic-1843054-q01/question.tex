In trapezoid $ABCD$ the sides $AB$ and $CD$ are parallel and have different lengths. Let $AB=a$, $BC=c$, $CD=b$ and $DA=d$ (so $a,c,b,d$ are consecutive sides). Prove that
\[
|d-c|<|b-a| .
\]
