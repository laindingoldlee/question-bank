Let $ s=a+b $. The equation becomes $ s^2-s=600 $, or $(s-25)(s+24)=0 $. Positivity forces $ s=25 $. The choices $ a=1,\ldots,24 $, each with $ b=25-a $, give $\boxed{24}$ ordered pairs.
