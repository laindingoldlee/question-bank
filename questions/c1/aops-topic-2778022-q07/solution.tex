Let the unit prices be $ p,q,r $. Add the two equations to obtain $ 20p+20q+20r=20 $, so $\boxed{p+q+r=1}$ dollar. The conditions are consistent with positive prices: taking all three unit prices to be one-third of a dollar gives the stated purchase totals.
