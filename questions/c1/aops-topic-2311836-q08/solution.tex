Since $7^4=2401$, we have $7^{2005}=7\cdot7^{2004}=7\cdot2401^{501}$. By the binomial theorem,
\[2401^{501}=(1+2400)^{501}=1+501\cdot2400+\dbinom{501}{2}2400^2+\cdots.\]
Every term from the third on contains $2400^2$, a multiple of $1000$. And $501\cdot2400=1202400\equiv400\pmod{1000}$. So $2401^{501}\equiv401\pmod{1000}$ and
\[7^{2005}\equiv7\cdot401=2807\equiv807\pmod{1000}.\]
The last three digits are $\boxed{807}$.
