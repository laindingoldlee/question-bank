Rectangle $ABCD$ is symmetric about the $y$-axis. Points $A$ and $B$ lie on the parabola $y=x^2$, and points $C$ and $D$ lie on $y=-3x^2+g$, above $A$ and $B$. If $AB=6$ and the area of the rectangle is $6$ times the length of $AB$, find $g$.
