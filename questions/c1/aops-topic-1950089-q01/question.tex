For a positive integer $m$ not ending in $0$, let $\operatorname{rev}(m)$ be the number with the digits of $m$ in reverse order. For example $12^2=144$ and $\operatorname{rev}(144)=441=21^2$. Find all two-digit numbers $n$ with nonzero units digit such that $\operatorname{rev}(n^2)=\operatorname{rev}(n)^2$.
