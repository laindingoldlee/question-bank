$10\%$ of the Math and AI 4 Girls Competition’s contestants like dogs. $60\%$ of them like rabbits. $90\%$ of them like cats. Liking each of these animals is independent. That means, for example, that whether or not you like dogs does not affect whether you like cats. If we choose a random contestant:

a. What is the probability of this contestant liking cats and dogs, but not rabbits?

b. What is the most likely outcome of this contestant’s preferences? As in, which animals does she like, and which does she not like?
