$696969=69\cdot10101$, and $69=3\cdot23$. Further $10101=3\cdot3367$, $3367=7\cdot481$ and $481=13\cdot37$. Hence
\[696969=\boxed{3^2\cdot7\cdot13\cdot23\cdot37}.\]
(Check: $9\cdot7=63$, $63\cdot13=819$, $819\cdot23=18837$, $18837\cdot37=696969$.)
