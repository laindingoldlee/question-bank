Since $2020=2^2\cdot5\cdot101$, it has $(2+1)(1+1)(1+1)=12$ factors. Count those with at most $3$ factors:
\begin{itemize}
\item $1$ has $1$ factor;
\item the primes $2$, $5$, $101$ have $2$ factors each;
\item a number with exactly $3$ factors is the square of a prime; the only such factor of $2020$ is $4$.
\end{itemize}
That is $5$ factors, so $12-5=7$ factors of $2020$ have more than $3$ factors (namely $10$, $20$, $202$, $404$, $505$, $1010$, $2020$). The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(B) }7}$.
