\begin{enumerate}
\item The cube has $V=3^3=\boxed{27}$ and $S=6\cdot3^2=\boxed{54}$.
\item The cylinder has $V=\pi r^2h=\boxed{36\pi}$. Its two circular ends contribute $18\pi$ and its lateral surface contributes $2\pi rh=24\pi$, giving $S=\boxed{42\pi}$.
\item The sphere has $V=\dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3=\boxed{36\pi}$ and $S=4\pi r^2=\boxed{36\pi}$.
\end{enumerate}
