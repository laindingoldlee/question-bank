After $7$ minutes the runner has completed $7$ full laps and is back at the start: displacement $\boxed{0}$. After $7.5$ minutes the runner is diametrically opposite the start, so the displacement is the diameter $\dfrac{100}\pi\approx31.8$ m.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(2.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw(circle((0,0),1));pair S0=(0,-1),H=(0,1);dot(S0);dot(H);draw(S0--H,dashed);label("start",S0,S);label("after $7.5$ min",H,N);
\end{asy}
