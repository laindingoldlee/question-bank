The first factor is $x^{3}z^{-5}$, the second is $x^{-5}y^{5}z^{-8}$ and the third is $x^6$. Adding exponents: $x^{3-5+6}y^5z^{-5-8}=x^4y^5z^{-13}=\boxed{\dfrac{x^4y^5}{z^{13}}}$.
