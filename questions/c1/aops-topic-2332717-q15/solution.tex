The intervals are $\dfrac{60}{72}=\dfrac56$ s and $\dfrac{60}{96}=\dfrac58$ s. We need the smallest $t$ that is a multiple of both: $t=\dfrac56a=\dfrac58b$, i.e. $4a=3b$, smallest $a=3$, so $t=\dfrac52$ s. (Equivalently, $\gcd(72,96)=24$ common beeps per minute.) \textbf{(A)}
