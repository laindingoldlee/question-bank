Each integer occurs in exactly half of the subsets: adding or removing that integer pairs subsets that contain it with those that do not. Thus the average sum is $\dfrac{1}{2}\sum_{k=1}^{2017}k=\dfrac{2017\cdot2018}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{2035153}{2}}$.
