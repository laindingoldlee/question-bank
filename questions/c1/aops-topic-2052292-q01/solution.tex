We claim $a_n=\dfrac{2^n-(-1)^n}{3}$. This holds for $n=1$ ($\dfrac{2+1}{3}=1$) and $n=2$ ($\dfrac{4-1}{3}=1$), and if it holds for $n$ and $n+1$ then
\[a_{n+1}+2a_n=\dfrac{2^{n+1}+(-1)^{n}+2\cdot2^n-2(-1)^n}{3}=\dfrac{2^{n+2}-(-1)^{n+2}}{3},\]
so by induction it holds for all $n$. (The characteristic equation $r^2=r+2$ has roots $2$ and $-1$.)

The sequence is increasing, with $a_{14}=\dfrac{16384-1}{3}=5461<10000$ and $a_{15}=\dfrac{32768+1}{3}=10923>10000$. The least $k$ is $\boxed{15}$.
