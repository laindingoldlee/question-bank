Suppose the series winner wins $m$ games and the loser wins $k$ games, where $0\le k\le m-1$. The winner must win the last game, and the earlier $m-1+k$ games can be split between the teams in $\dbinom{m-1+k}{k}$ ways. Summing over $k$ with the hockey-stick identity,
\[\sum_{k=0}^{m-1}\dbinom{m-1+k}{k}=\dbinom{2m-1}{m-1}.\]
With $2$ choices of winner, the number of outcomes is $2\dbinom{2m-1}{m-1}=\dbinom{2m}{m}$. So
\[m=2:\ \boxed{6},\qquad m=3:\ \boxed{20},\qquad m=4:\ \boxed{70}.\]
(For example, for $m=2$ the outcomes are AA, ABA, BAA, BB, BAB, ABB.)
