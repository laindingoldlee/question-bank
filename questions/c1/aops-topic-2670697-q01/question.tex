In a play-off between two teams, the winner is the first team to win $m$ games. An \emph{outcome} is the sequence of winners of the games actually played. How many different outcomes are there for a ``$2$ out of $3$'' play-off ($m=2$), a ``$3$ out of $5$'' play-off ($m=3$) and a ``$4$ out of $7$'' play-off ($m=4$)?
