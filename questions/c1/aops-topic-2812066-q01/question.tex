A cube of edge $8$ cm is painted red on the outside and cut into $512$ unit cubes. One unit cube is chosen at random and rolled, so that each of its faces is equally likely to land on the bottom. Given that the bottom face is unpainted, what is the probability that the whole unit cube is unpainted?
