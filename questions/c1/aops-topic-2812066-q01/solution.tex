Each of the $6\cdot512=3072$ (cube, face) pairs is equally likely. Painted faces: $6\cdot64=384$, so $2688$ outcomes have an unpainted bottom. Of these, the $6^3=216$ interior cubes contribute $216\cdot6=1296$. The probability is $\dfrac{1296}{2688}=\boxed{\dfrac{27}{56}}$.
