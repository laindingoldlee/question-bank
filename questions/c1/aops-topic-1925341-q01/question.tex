In triangle $ABC$, $AB=4$, $AC=3$, $BC=5$ (so the right angle is at $A$). Point $D$ on $BC$ is chosen so that triangles $ACD$ and $ABD$ have equal perimeters. What is the area of triangle $ABD$? Explain in particular why the area is $\dfrac{2\cdot6}{5}$.

$\textbf{(A)}\ \dfrac{3}{4}\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ \dfrac{3}{2}\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ 2\qquad\textbf{(D)}\ \dfrac{12}{5}\qquad\textbf{(E)}\ \dfrac{5}{2}$
