Packs of 18 add no new possibilities because $18=3\cdot6$. If $49=6a+11b$, reduction modulo 6 forces $b\equiv5\pmod6$, impossible with $11b\le49$. Meanwhile $50=4\cdot11+6$, $51=3\cdot11+18$, $52=2\cdot11+30$, $53=11+42$, $54=9\cdot6$, and $55=5\cdot11$. Adding multiples of 6 now represents every number at least 50. The largest impossible total is $\boxed{49}$.
