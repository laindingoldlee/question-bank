A city's streets form a grid, drawn on the coordinate plane with the south-west corner at the origin; one unit is one block. A mathematician walks from $(0,0)$ to $(5,8)$ using only shortest routes (each step one block north or one block east). The block from $(2,4)$ to $(2,5)$ is closed. How many routes can he take?
