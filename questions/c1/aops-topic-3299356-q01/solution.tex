If the selected numbers are $a<b<c$ with gaps at least $2$, map them to $a<b-1<c-2$, an arbitrary three-element subset of $1,\ldots,6$. This is a bijection, with inverse adding $0,1,2$ to the ordered entries. Hence $\boxed{\binom63=20}$.
