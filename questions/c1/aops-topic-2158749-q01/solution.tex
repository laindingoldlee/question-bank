\textbf{Method 1 (small representatives).} Since $16\equiv-3\pmod{19}$, we need $-3x\equiv1$, that is, $3x\equiv-1\equiv18\pmod{19}$. As $\gcd(3,19)=1$ we may divide by $3$: $x\equiv6\pmod{19}$.

\textbf{Method 2 (Euclidean algorithm).} $19=1\cdot16+3$ and $16=5\cdot3+1$. Working backwards,
\[1=16-5\cdot3=16-5(19-16)=6\cdot16-5\cdot19,\]
so $6\cdot16\equiv1\pmod{19}$.

Either way the inverse is $\boxed{6}$; check: $16\cdot6=96=5\cdot19+1$.
