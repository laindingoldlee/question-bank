By the factor theorem, $x+4$ divides the polynomial exactly when the value at $x=-4$ is $0$:
\[
(-4)^3+6(-4)^2+k(-4)+12=-64+96-4k+12=44-4k=0,
\]
so $k=\boxed{11}$. Check: $x^3+6x^2+11x+12=(x+4)(x^2+2x+3)$.
