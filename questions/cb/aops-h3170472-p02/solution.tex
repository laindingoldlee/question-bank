Each game awards $2$ or $3$ points, so
$2\binom n2\le21\le3\binom n2$. The only possible triangular number in $[7,10.5]$ is $10$, so $n=5$. Ten draws would give $20$ total points, hence there is exactly one decisive game. Its winner has $3$ points from that game and one from each of its other three games, for $p=6$. All other teams have $3$ or $4$ points. Therefore $\boxed{n+p=11}$.
