Let $n$ be the number of nickels and $q$ the number of quarters. In cents,
\[
n+q=15,\qquad 5n+25q=275 .
\]
Dividing the second equation by $5$ gives $n+5q=55$; subtracting the first, $4q=40$, so $q=10$ and $n=\boxed{5}$. Check: $5\cdot5+10\cdot25=25+250=275$ cents.
