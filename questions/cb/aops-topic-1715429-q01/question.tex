A triangular prism has height $1$, and its two bases are isosceles triangles with side lengths $10,10,6$. Let $T$ be the apex (the vertex between the two sides of length $10$) of one base, and let $M'$ be the midpoint of the side of length $6$ of the \emph{other} base. Find the distance $TM'$.
