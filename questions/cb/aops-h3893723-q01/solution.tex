After every even number of steps, the coordinate sum is even. The coordinates have the same parity, and the restriction forces both to be even. Hence every pair of consecutive steps is east-east or north-north; a mixed pair would end at a forbidden point. Conversely, such pairs are allowed. The last step must be east, so the first 18 steps end at $(6,12)$ and consist of three east-east pairs and six north-north pairs. Their order can be chosen in $\binom93=\boxed{84}$ ways.
