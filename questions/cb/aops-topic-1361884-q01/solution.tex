Subtract $4x$ from both sides: $5x-4x=-3$, so $\boxed{x=-3}$. Check: $5(-3)=-15$ and $4(-3)-3=-12-3=-15$.

The student's error is a sign slip: moving $-3$ (or $4x$) across the equals sign without changing its sign gives $x=3$. When a term moves to the other side, its sign changes.
