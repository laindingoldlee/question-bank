Three cards are drawn at random, without replacement, from a standard $52$-card deck. Each card has a value: an ace is $1$, the cards $2$ to $10$ have their face values, and a jack, queen and king are $11$, $12$ and $13$. What is the probability that the three values add up to $16$?
