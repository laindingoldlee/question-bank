Use the Euclidean algorithm on $34$ and $23$:
\[34=1\cdot23+11,\qquad23=2\cdot11+1 .\]
Working backwards, $1=23-2\cdot11=23-2(34-23)=3\cdot23-2\cdot34$. So $3\cdot23\equiv1\pmod{34}$ (indeed $69=2\cdot34+1$), and multiplying the congruence by $3$ gives
\[x\equiv\boxed{3}\pmod{34}.\]
