The letters are $M,A,T,I,T,A$: the vowels are $A,A,I$ and the consonants are $M,T,T$. The first and last letters are two of the three vowels, so the ordered pair (first letter, last letter) is one of
\[(A,A),\qquad (A,I),\qquad (I,A).\]
In each case the four middle letters are the remaining letters, which always include the two $T$'s and otherwise distinct letters:
\begin{itemize}
\item $(A,A)$: the middle letters are $M,T,I,T$;
\item $(A,I)$ and $(I,A)$: the middle letters are $M,T,T,A$.
\end{itemize}
Four letters with exactly one repeated pair can be arranged in $\dfrac{4!}{2!}=12$ ways. So the number of anagrams is
\[3\cdot12=\boxed{36}.\]
