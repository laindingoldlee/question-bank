If the sequence has $n\ge2$ terms, then $(n-1)d=1296-216=1080$, so $d$ can be any positive divisor of $1080=2^3\cdot3^3\cdot5$. The even divisors are $2e$ with $e$ a divisor of $540=2^2\cdot3^3\cdot5$, so their sum is
\[
2\,\sigma(540)=2(1+2+4)(1+3+9+27)(1+5)=2\cdot7\cdot40\cdot6=3360 .
\]
The remainder on division by $100$ is $\boxed{\textbf{(d)}\ 60}$.
