$ad-bc$ is odd exactly when one of $ad$ and $bc$ is odd and the other is even. A product of two numbers from $\{0,1,2,3\}$ is odd only when both are odd: $2\cdot2=4$ of the $16$ ordered pairs, leaving $12$ even pairs. Hence the count is
\[4\cdot12+12\cdot4=\boxed{96}.\]
