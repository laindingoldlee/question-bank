When leaned against a vertical structure, a straight ladder can be used safely if its top is no more than $4$ feet above the base of the structure for every foot that the bottom of the ladder is away from the base. How high can a $22$-foot ladder safely reach up a vertical structure? Express your answer as a decimal to the nearest tenth.
