Let the foot of the ladder be $d$ feet from the base of the structure and the top $h$ feet up. The ladder, the wall and the ground form a right triangle, so $h^2+d^2=22^2=484$. The safety rule says $h\le4d$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.2cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s=0.2){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
real d=22/sqrt(17), h=88/sqrt(17);
pair O=(0,0), T=(0,h), F=(d,0);
draw((-1.2,0)--(d+2,0),linewidth(0.8)); draw(O--(0,h+2),linewidth(1.2));
draw(F--T,blue+linewidth(1.2));
draw(ra(O,(1,0),(0,1),0.8));
dot(O);dot(T);dot(F);
label("ladder $22$",(F+T)/2,NE,blue);
label("$h$",(0,h/2),W); label("$d$",(d/2,0),S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Moving the foot closer to the wall raises the top, so the highest safe position is the one where the rule is exactly met, $h=4d$. Then
\[
(4d)^2+d^2=484\ \Longrightarrow\ 17d^2=484\ \Longrightarrow\ d=\dfrac{22}{\sqrt{17}},\qquad h=\dfrac{88}{\sqrt{17}}\approx21.34 .
\]
So the ladder can safely reach $\boxed{21.3}$ feet. (Any steeper position has $h>4d$ and is unsafe; any flatter one has a smaller $h$.)
