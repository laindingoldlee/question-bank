$k=8$ gives $81k=2^3\cdot 3^4$ and $(3+1)(4+1)=20$ divisors. To rule out smaller k, check $k=1,2,3,4,5,6,7$: the respective divisor counts of $81k$ are $5,10,6,15,10,12,10$. Therefore the smallest is $\boxed 8$.
