Set $y=0$ in the rule. An associative product of $x+1$ zeros under $\nabla$ equals $x\star0=x$. Therefore 20 is a product of 21 zeros, and 25 a product of 26 zeros. Associativity makes $20\nabla25$ a product of 47 zeros, equal to $46\star0=\boxed{46}$. No assumption about the operation on arbitrary unequal real inputs is needed.
