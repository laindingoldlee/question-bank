A character starts with 1000 health. Independently choose integers $n$ uniformly from 1 through 100 and $m$ uniformly from 101 through 200. First subtract $n$ health, then subtract $-\lfloor\dfrac{(n-m)^3}{420}\rfloor$ health. The character survives exactly when final health is positive. Find the survival probability.
