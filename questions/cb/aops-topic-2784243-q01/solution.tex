Since $a+1\equiv1\pmod a$, $(a+1)^n\equiv1^n=1\pmod a$. As $a\ge2$, the remainder is $\boxed{1}$. (By the binomial theorem, every term of $(a+1)^n$ except the final $1$ is a multiple of $a$. For $a=1$ every remainder is $0$.)
