Write $p(x)=ax^3+bx^2+cx+4$. Then
\[
a+b+c=6,\qquad -a+b-c=-2,\qquad 8a+4b+2c=22 .
\]
Adding the first two gives $b=2$, so $a+c=4$ and $4a+c=7$; hence $a=1$ and $c=3$. So $p(x)=x^3+2x^2+3x+4$ and
\[
p(3)=27+18+9+4=\boxed{58}.
\]
