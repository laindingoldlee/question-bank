The semiperimeter is $21$, so Heron's formula gives area $\sqrt{21\cdot8\cdot7\cdot6}=84$. The circumradius is $R=\dfrac{13\cdot14\cdot15}{4\cdot84}=\dfrac{65}{8}$. Since all three distances to the circumcenter equal $R$, their sum is $\dfrac{195}{8}$ and its floor is $\boxed{24}$.

\end{asy}
