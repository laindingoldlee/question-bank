Numbers $\equiv3\pmod4$: $3,7,11,\dots$; the first that is $\equiv1\pmod3$ is $7$. By the Chinese remainder theorem the zany numbers are exactly those $\equiv7\pmod{12}$: $7,19,31,43,55$, with sum $5\cdot31=\boxed{155}$.
