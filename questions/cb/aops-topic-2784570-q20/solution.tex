The characteristic polynomial factors as $(t-1)(t-2)(t-4)$. The expression $a_n=1+2\cdot2^n+4^n$ satisfies the recurrence and all three initial values, so agrees with the sequence by induction. Thus $a_{16}=2^{32}+2^{17}+1$, giving $\boxed{32+17+1=50}$.
