By Pascal's rule, $\dbinom x3+\dbinom x2=\dbinom{x+1}3=\dfrac{(x+1)x(x-1)}{6}$. So the equation is
\[
\dfrac{(x+1)x(x-1)}{6}=15(x-1)(x+1).
\]
For $x\ge3$ the factor $(x-1)(x+1)$ is positive, so we may divide by it: $\dfrac {x}{6}=15$, so $x=\boxed{90}$.

Check: $\dbinom{91}{3}=\dfrac{91\cdot90\cdot89}{6}=121485$ and $15\left(90^2-1\right)=15\cdot8099=121485$.
