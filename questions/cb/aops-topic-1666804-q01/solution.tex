The number $2^{1024}-1$ is odd, so $2$ is not a factor. For any $m\ge1$, $a-1$ divides $a^m-1$. Since $2^{1024}=4^{512}=16^{256}$:
\[
4-1=3\ \Big|\ 4^{512}-1,\qquad 16-1=15\ \Big|\ 16^{256}-1 .
\]
So $3$ and $5$ are prime factors, and they are the two smallest primes other than $2$. The product is $3\cdot5=\boxed{15}$.
