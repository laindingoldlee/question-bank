Swap the order (all terms are positive):
\[\sum_{k\ge1}\dfrac k{2^k}\sum_{n\ge k+1}\dfrac1{2^n}=\sum_{k\ge1}\dfrac k{2^k}\cdot\dfrac1{2^k}=\sum_{k\ge1}\dfrac k{4^k}=\dfrac{\dfrac14}{\left(1-\dfrac14\right)^2}=\boxed{\dfrac49}.\]
