Each removed corner is an isosceles right triangle with legs $x$. The resulting horizontal or vertical octagon sides have length $5-2x$, while the slanted sides have length $x\sqrt2$. Regularity gives $5-2x=x\sqrt2$, so $x=\dfrac{5}{2+\sqrt2}$. The four removed triangles have total area
\[4\cdot\dfrac{x^2}{2}=2x^2=25(3-2\sqrt2)\approx4.2893.\]
Rounded to the nearest integer, this is $\boxed4$.
