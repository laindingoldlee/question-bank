Compute $f(1)=4$, $f(2)=3$, and $f(3)=\dfrac{16}{3}$. For $n\ge4$, we have $f(n)>\dfrac{2n!}{n}=2(n-1)!\ge12$. Thus $8$ is not in the image of $f$ on the positive integers, so $f^{-1}(8)$ is undefined. Regardless of whether one establishes injectivity on the entire domain, no inverse on its image can be evaluated at 8. Hence $\boxed{z(8)+z(4)\text{ is undefined}}$ as posed.
