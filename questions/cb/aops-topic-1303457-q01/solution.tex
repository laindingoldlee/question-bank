Choose the $3$ people who get wrong letters: $\dbinom73=35$ ways. The other $4$ get their own letters, and the $3$ chosen letters must be deranged among their $3$ owners: $D_3=2$ ways. The total is $35\cdot2=\boxed{70}$.
