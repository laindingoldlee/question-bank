Write $u=r_1^{\dfrac{1}{1024}}>0$, so $r_2^{\dfrac{1}{1024}}=u^{-1}$. Their sum is an integer $m>2$, because the roots are distinct. Starting with $s_0=m$, define $s_{j+1}=s_j^2-2$. Then $s_j=u^{2^j}+u^{-2^j}$, and $a=s_{10}$. These expressions increase with $m\ge3$, so the least $a$ comes from $m=3$. The residues modulo $100$ for $s_0,\ldots,s_{10}$ are $3,7,47,7,47,7,47,7,47,7,47$. Thus $\boxed{47}$.
