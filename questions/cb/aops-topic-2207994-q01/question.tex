Alice, Benedict, Calvin, Dennis and Evelyn attended a party. At the end of the night, each of them randomly grabbed a left shoe and a right shoe: the five left shoes are handed out by a uniformly random permutation, and, independently, so are the five right shoes. What is the probability that each person left with exactly one of their own shoes?
