How many $5$-digit positive integers consist of two pairs of equal nonzero digits together with one further digit? That is, two different nonzero digits each appear exactly twice, and the fifth digit (which may be $0$) is different from both. For example, $30399$ and $86844$ count, but $12876$, $11237$, $90000$ and $20000$ do not.
