Choose the two pair digits from $1,\dots,9$: $\binom{9}{2}=36$ ways. Choose the fifth digit $r$ from the $8$ remaining digits.

The five digits can be arranged in $\dfrac{5!}{2!\,2!}=30$ ways. If $r\neq0$ (7 choices), all $30$ arrangements are valid. If $r=0$, the arrangements with $0$ in front, $\dfrac{4!}{2!\,2!}=6$ of them, must be removed, leaving $24$. The total is
\[
36\cdot\left(7\cdot30+24\right)=36\cdot234=\boxed{8424}.
\]
