Let $s$ be the top-$5$ sum. Then $\dfrac s5=\dfrac{1720-s}{15}+12$, which gives $s=475$. So ranks $6$--$10$ sum to $445$. Being distinct, the smallest of them is at most $87$ (as $91+90+89+88+87=445$). Ranks $11$--$19$ are then at most $86,85,\dots,78$, summing to at most $738$, so the $20$th score is at least $800-738=62$.

This is achievable: top five $97,96,95,94,93$ (sum $475$), then $91,\dots,87$, then $86,\dots,78$, then $62$. Answer $\boxed{62}$.
