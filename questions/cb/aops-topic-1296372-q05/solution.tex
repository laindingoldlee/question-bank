Let the numbers be $a_1\le a_2\le a_3\le a_4\le a_5$, with sum $500$. Removing $a_3$ leaves sum $4\cdot105=420$, so $a_3=80$. The new median is $\dfrac{a_2+a_4}{2}=75$, so $a_2+a_4=150$. Then $a_1+a_5=420-150=270$, so $a_5=270-a_1\le269$. This is attained with $a_1=1$, $a_2=70$, $a_3=80$, $a_4=80$, $a_5=269$ (sum $500$, sorted, and $a_2+a_4=150$). The maximum is $\boxed{269}$.
