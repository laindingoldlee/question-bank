If the palindromes are $x$ and $y$, then
\[\dfrac{1}{x}+\dfrac{1}{y}=\dfrac{x+y}{xy}=\dfrac{x+y}{27331},\]
so we need $x+y$, which does require factoring $27331$. Since $27331$ is odd, not divisible by $3$ (digit sum $16$) or $5$, try $3$-digit palindromes near $\sqrt{27331}\approx165$: $27331=151\cdot181$, and both $151$ and $181$ are prime palindromes. As both are prime, this is the only way to write $27331$ as a product of two numbers greater than $1$, so $\{x,y\}=\{151,181\}$ and
\[\dfrac{1}{151}+\dfrac{1}{181}=\dfrac{332}{27331}=\boxed{\dfrac{332}{27331}}.\]
