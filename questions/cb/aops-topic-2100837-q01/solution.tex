There are $10+30+20+15=75$ marbles, and because of the replacement the two draws are independent.

\textbf{(a)} On each draw, $10+30=40$ of the $75$ marbles are red or white, a probability of $\dfrac{40}{75}=\dfrac{8}{15}$. So both draws are red or white with probability
\[\left(\dfrac{8}{15}\right)^2=\boxed{\dfrac{64}{225}}.\]

\textbf{(b)} A draw is not blue with probability $\dfrac{55}{75}=\dfrac{11}{15}$, so by complementary counting
\[P(\text{at least one blue})=1-\left(\dfrac{11}{15}\right)^2=1-\dfrac{121}{225}=\boxed{\dfrac{104}{225}}.\]
