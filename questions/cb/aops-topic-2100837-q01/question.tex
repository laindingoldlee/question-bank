A box contains $10$ red, $30$ white, $20$ blue and $15$ orange marbles. Two marbles are drawn one after the other, and the first marble is put back before the second is drawn. Find the probability that
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] each of the two marbles is red or white (so the two marbles are both red, both white, or one red and one white);
\item[(b)] at least one of the two marbles is blue.
\end{enumerate}
