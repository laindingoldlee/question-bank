The roof of a car measures $5$ feet by $8$ feet and is to be covered with rectangular shingles measuring $8$ inches by $14$ inches. The shingles are laid in a rectangular grid of rows and columns, all in the same orientation, and neighbouring shingles (side by side or in neighbouring rows) must overlap by at least $2$ inches; no overlap is needed at the outer edge of the roof. What is the minimum number of shingles needed?
