A cube has four painted faces exactly when two of its six faces touch other cubes. In the one-layer $7\times2$ slab, only the four corner cubes have two neighbours. Every other cube has three neighbours. Thus $\boxed4$ cubes have four painted faces, choice \textbf{A}.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair p(real x,real y,real z){return (x+.5y,z+.35y);}for(int x=0;x<=7;++x){draw(p(x,0,1)--p(x,2,1));draw(p(x,0,0)--p(x,0,1));}for(int y=0;y<=2;++y)draw(p(0,y,1)--p(7,y,1));draw(p(0,0,0)--p(7,0,0)--p(7,2,0)--p(7,2,1));draw(p(7,1,0)--p(7,1,1));label("$7$ cubes",p(3.5,0,0),(0,-1));label("$2$ cubes",p(7,1,1),(1,1));
\end{asy}
