Writing the volumes as $s,m,l$ and the barrel as $V$, equality gives $6s+3m+l=2s+m+3l$, so $l=2s+m$. Therefore $V=2s+m+3l=4l$. Exactly $\boxed4$ large buckets fill it.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
draw(box((0,0),(4,2)));for(int i=1;i<4;++i)draw((i,0)--(i,2),dashed);for(int i=0;i<4;++i)label("$l$",(i+.5,1));label("$V=4l$",(2,2.3));
\end{asy}
