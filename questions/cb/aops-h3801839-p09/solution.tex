Because $AM$ bisects $\angle DAN$, reflection in $AM$ exchanges rays $AD$ and $AN$. The perpendicular projection of $M$ on $AD$ is $D$ (since $CD\perp AD$), and its projection on $AN$ is $E$. Reflection therefore exchanges $D,E$, giving $AD=AE$. In isosceles triangle $ADE$, the angle at $A$ is $36^\circ$, so $\angle ADE=72^\circ$. Since $DN\perp DA$, $\angle EDN=90^\circ-72^\circ=\boxed{18^\circ}$.
