Points $P$, $Q$, $R$, $S$ are the midpoints of $\overline{AB}$, $\overline{BD}$, $\overline{DC}$, $\overline{CA}$ respectively, and the diagonals $\overline{AC}$ and $\overline{BD}$ meet at $O$. The area of triangle $AOB$ is $2019$ and the area of triangle $COD$ is $2020$. What is the area of quadrilateral $PQRS$?
