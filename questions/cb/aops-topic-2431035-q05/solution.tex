The $25$ numbers form an arithmetic sequence with common difference $2$, so their mean equals the middle (13th) number. The mean is $\dfrac{10000}{25}=400$, so the 13th number is $400$, and the largest, $12$ steps of $2$ later, is
\[400+12\cdot2=\boxed{424}.\]
