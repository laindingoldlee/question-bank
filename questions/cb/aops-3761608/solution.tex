Where the right side is defined, $\tan x + \sec x = \dfrac{1+\sin x}{\cos x} = \tan (45^\circ + \dfrac{x}{2})$. Equality of tangents gives $16 x = 45 + \dfrac{x}{2} + 180 k$, so $x=\dfrac{90+360k}{31}$ for integers $k$, subject to the original domain. For $k<0$ these are negative, and $k=0$ gives $\dfrac{90}{31}$ degrees, at which both sides are defined. Hence $\boxed{x=\dfrac{90}{31}^\circ}$.
