If the $18$ horizontal steps form $k$ runs of length at least two, subtracting one per run leaves a composition of $18-k$ into $k$ positive parts. Its count is $c_k=\binom{17-k}{k-1}$ for $1\le k\le9$, otherwise zero. Horizontal and vertical runs alternate. With equal numbers $k$ of each there are $2c_k^2$ choices, accounting for the first direction; with counts differing by one there are $2c_kc_{k+1}$. Hence the count is $2\sum_{k=1}^9(c_k^2+c_kc_{k+1})$. The values $c_1,\ldots,c_9$ are $1,15,91,286,495,462,210,36,1$, and substitution gives $\boxed{2192902}$.
