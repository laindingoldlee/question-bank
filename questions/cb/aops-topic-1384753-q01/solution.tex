The smallest possible total is $5+1+1+1+1=9$ and the largest is $10+4+3+2+1=20$. Every total in between is possible: start from $9$ beads and add white beads one at a time up to $10$ white ($14$ beads), then black up to $4$ ($17$), then blue up to $3$ ($19$), then the second yellow ($20$). So the possible totals are $9,10,\dots,20$: $\boxed{12}$ numbers.
