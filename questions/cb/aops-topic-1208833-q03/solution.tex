A given box is chosen $j$ times with the binomial probability $\dbinom{10}{j}\left(\dfrac{1}{10}\right)^j\left(\dfrac{9}{10}\right)^{10-j}$. It is chosen $0$ or $1$ times with probability
\[\left(\dfrac{9}{10}\right)^{10}+10\cdot\dfrac{1}{10}\left(\dfrac{9}{10}\right)^9=\left(\dfrac{9}{10}\right)^{10}+\left(\dfrac{9}{10}\right)^9.\]
By linearity, the expected number of boxes chosen at least twice is
\[10\left(1-\left(\dfrac{9}{10}\right)^{10}-\left(\dfrac{9}{10}\right)^{9}\right)=\boxed{\dfrac{2639010709}{1000000000}}\approx2.64.\]
