Let $c$ be the number of correct answers and $w$ the number of wrong ones, with $c,w\ge0$ and $c+w\le26$. Then
\[
7c-3w=76 .
\]
Reducing modulo $3$: $7c\equiv c\equiv76\equiv1\pmod3$. Also $7c=76+3w\ge76$, so $c\ge11$. The candidates are $c=13,16,19,\dots$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $c=13$: $w=\dfrac{91-76}{3}=5$, and $c+w=18\le26$. This works (with $8$ omitted).
\item $c=16$: $w=12$ and $c+w=28>26$; larger $c$ make $c+w$ even bigger.
\end{itemize}
So Daniel answered $\boxed{13}$ questions correctly.
