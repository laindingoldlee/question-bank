Conditioned on Bob's hand, the remaining seven spades occupy a uniform seven-element subset of the $39$ card positions in the other hands. Bob fails to have a strict lead if another player has six or seven spades. These three failure events are disjoint, since only seven spades remain.

For one specified opponent, the number of such subsets is
$\binom{13}{6}\binom{26}{1}+\binom{13}{7}$. Thus the desired probability is
\[
1-\dfrac{3\left(\binom{13}{6}26+\binom{13}{7}\right)}{\binom{39}{7}}
=\dfrac{11843}{11951}.
\]
The numerator and denominator are coprime, and $11951=17\cdot19\cdot37$. Therefore the answer is $\boxed{17+19+37=73}$.
