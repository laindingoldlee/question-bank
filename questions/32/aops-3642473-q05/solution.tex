Let M be the largest chest count and use the sum of squared counts as a nonnegative integer potential. Since ten distinct integers all at most 104 sum to at most $104+103+\cdots+95=995$, total 1000 forces $M\ge105$. One move changes the potential by $(M-9)^2-M^2+\sum_{a\ne M}[(a+1)^2-a^2]=2090-20M\le-10$. Thus it strictly decreases while all counts remain distinct. A nonnegative integer cannot decrease forever, so eventually two counts coincide and Jim must stop.
