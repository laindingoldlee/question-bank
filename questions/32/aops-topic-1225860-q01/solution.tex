Let $a=\sqrt[4]{47-x}$, $b=\sqrt[4]x$, $p=ab$. Then $a+b=3$, $a^4+b^4=47$. Now $a^2+b^2=9-2p$ and $a^4+b^4=(9-2p)^2-2p^2$, so $2p^2-36p+81=47$, i.e. $p^2-18p+17=0$, $p=1$ or $17$. Since $p\le\left(\dfrac{a+b}{2}\right)^2=\dfrac{9}{4}$, $p=1$. Then
\[x^2-47x=-x(47-x)=-(ab)^4=\boxed{-1}.\]
