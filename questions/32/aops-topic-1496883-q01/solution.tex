\textbf{Yes.} Write the contents as (4-litre bucket, 9-litre bucket).
\[
(0,9)\to(4,5)\to(0,5)\to(4,1)\to(0,1)\to(1,0)\to(1,9)\to(4,6).
\]
In words: fill the 9-litre bucket. Pour from it into the 4-litre bucket and empty the small bucket, twice; this leaves $1$ litre in the big bucket. Pour this litre into the empty small bucket. Fill the big bucket again and pour from it until the small bucket is full, which takes $3$ litres. Exactly $\boxed{6}$ litres remain in the 9-litre bucket. (This uses $6=2\cdot9-3\cdot4$.)
