No root is zero. The reciprocals $u_i=\dfrac{1}{r_i}$ satisfy $u^3+2u+3=0$, obtained by dividing the original equation by $r_i^3$. Their sum is zero by Vieta. Summing $u_i^3=-2u_i-3$ over the three roots therefore gives $\boxed{-9}$.
