The only composite numbers on a die are $4$ and $6$; the faces $1$, $2$, $3$, $5$ are not composite ($1$ is neither prime nor composite). The sequences with no composite roll use only these $4$ faces, so there are $4^3=64$ of them. By complementary counting, the number of sequences with at least one composite roll is
\[216-64=\boxed{152}.\]
