Triangles $ABF$ and $AFE$ share the height from $A$ to line $BE$, so $\dfrac{BF}{FE}=\dfrac{15}{10}=\dfrac{3}{2}$. Also $[ABE]=25$, and triangles $ABE$, $ABC$ share the height from $B$ to line $AC$, so $\dfrac{AE}{AC}=\dfrac{25}{40}=\dfrac{5}{8}$, i.e. $AE:EC=5:3$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(8,0),C=(2.2,6);
pair Ee=A+5/8*(C-A); pair D=B+15/31*(C-B);
pair F=extension(B,Ee,A,D);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); draw(B--Ee,blue); draw(A--D,blue);
dot(D);dot(Ee);dot(F);
label("$A$",A,SW); label("$B$",B,SE); label("$C$",C,N); label("$D$",D,NE); label("$E$",Ee,W); label("$F$",F,N);
label("$15$",(A+B+F)/3); label("$10$",(A+F+Ee)/3+(-0.15,0));
label("$?$",(B+D+F)/3,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

\textbf{Mass points.} Put masses $m_A=3$, $m_C=5$ so that $E$ (with $AE:EC=5:3$) is their balance point, $m_E=8$. On $BE$ we need $m_B\cdot BF=m_E\cdot FE$, so $m_B=8\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}=\dfrac{16}{3}$. Then $D$ on $BC$ has $\dfrac{BD}{DC}=\dfrac{m_C}{m_B}=\dfrac{15}{16}$, and $\dfrac{AF}{FD}=\dfrac{m_B+m_C}{m_A}=\dfrac{31}{9}$.

\textbf{Areas.} $[ABD]=\dfrac{BD}{BC}[ABC]=\dfrac{15}{31}\cdot40=\dfrac{600}{31}$. Triangle $BDF$ is the part of $ABD$ cut off by $F$, with $\dfrac{FD}{AD}=\dfrac{9}{40}$, so
\[
[BDF]=\dfrac{9}{40}\cdot\dfrac{600}{31}=\boxed{\dfrac{135}{31}}.
\]
Check: $[ABF]=\dfrac{31}{40}[ABD]=\dfrac{31}{40}\cdot\dfrac{600}{31}=15$, as given.
