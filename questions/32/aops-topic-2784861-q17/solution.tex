Let original Algebra II and I counts be $ A,B $. Equality gives $\dfrac{5}{7}A+\dfrac{1}{3}B=\dfrac{2}{7}A+\dfrac{2}{3}B $, hence $ 9A=7B $. Therefore $ A=7t,B=9t $ for a positive integer $ t $. The minimum $ t=1 $ permits integer transfers 2 and 3, yielding total $\boxed{16}$.
