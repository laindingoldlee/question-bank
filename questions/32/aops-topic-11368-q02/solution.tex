Let the length be $L$ and diagonal $D$. Then $\dfrac {L}{2}=\dfrac {D}{L}$, so $L^2=2D$, and $D^2=L^2+4=2D+4$. Hence $D^2-2D-4=0$ and $D=\boxed{1+\sqrt5}$ (the positive root).

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real D=1+sqrt(5); real L=sqrt(2D); draw((0,0)--(L,0)--(L,2)--(0,2)--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); draw((0,0)--(L,2),blue); label("$L$",(L/2,0),S); label("$2$",(L,1),E); label("$D$",(L/2,1),NW);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
