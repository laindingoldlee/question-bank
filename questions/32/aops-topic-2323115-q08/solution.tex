Adding the first two equations: $y+4z=7$. Subtracting twice the first from the third: $-y-z=-1$, so $y+z=1$. Subtracting, $3z=6$, so $z=2$ and $y=-1$. Then $x=9+2y-3z=9-2-6=1$. The solution is $\boxed{(x,y,z)=(1,-1,2)}$. (Check in the third equation: $2+5+10=17$.)
