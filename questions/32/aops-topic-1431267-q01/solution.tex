Let the car be worth $c$ dollars. A full year's pay is worth $8000+c$, so $7$ months' pay is worth $\dfrac{7}{12}(8000+c)$, and this equals what he received, $1600+c$:
\[
\dfrac{7}{12}(8000+c)=1600+c\ \Longrightarrow\ 56000+7c=19200+12c\ \Longrightarrow\ 5c=36800 .
\]
So the car is worth $\boxed{\$7360}$. (Check: $\dfrac{7}{12}\cdot15360=8960=1600+7360$.)
