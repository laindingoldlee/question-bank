Every $2$ hours only $20\%$ remains. Of the first dose, $500\cdot0.2=100$ mg remain at 4:00 pm and $100\cdot0.2=20$ mg at 6:00 pm. Then the second dose brings the total to $520$ mg at 6:00 pm, and at 8:00 pm $520\cdot0.2=\boxed{104}$ mg remain.

(The poster added the second dose at 6:00 pm to the $100$ mg present at 4:00 pm, skipping the clearance between 4:00 and 6:00 pm, and so got $24$.)
