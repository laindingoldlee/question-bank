If the geometric sequence has $k+1$ terms, then $xy^k=2023=7\cdot17^2$ with $k\ge2$. Every prime in $y$ must therefore have exponent at least two in $2023$, forcing $y=17$, $k=2$, and $x=7$. The arithmetic terms are $7+17j$. Since $2023\equiv0\pmod{17}$, the largest integer at most $2023$ congruent to $7$ is $\boxed{2013}$.
