Since $4\cdot\overline{AB}<100$, we have $\overline{AB}\le24$, so $A\in\{1,2\}$. The units digit of $4B$ is $A$, which must be even, so $A=2$. Then $4B$ ends in $2$: $B=3$ or $B=8$. $\overline{AB}=28$ gives $112$, too large; $\overline{AB}=23$ gives $92$, so $C=9$.

$A+B+C=2+3+9=\boxed{14}$.
