Let $AC=a>0$. Triangle $CMN$ is right with one acute angle $45^\circ$, so $CN=CM=\dfrac{a}{2}$. Thus $BC=1195+\dfrac{a}{2}$. Pythagoras gives $a^2+(1195+\dfrac{a}{2})^2=2390^2$. Multiplying by $4$ and factoring gives $5(a-1434)(a+2390)=0$. The positive solution is $\boxed{AC=1434}$.
