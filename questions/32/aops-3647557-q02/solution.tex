The two branches map bijectively onto the opposite components of $D$, so the inverse exists on $D$. Applying $f$ shows the equation is equivalent to $f(f(x))=x$. For $x\le0$, the composition is $-x^2$, giving $x=0,-1$. For $x\ge1$, it is $1+(x-1)^2$, giving $x=1,2$. All are in the appropriate domains, so $\boxed{-1,0,1,2}$.
