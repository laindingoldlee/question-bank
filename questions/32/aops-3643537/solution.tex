After separating sign and integer part, use long division on $\dfrac{a}{b}$ with $0\le a<b$. Every remainder belongs to the finite set $\{0,1,\ldots,b-1\}$. A zero remainder ends the expansion. Otherwise some nonzero remainder repeats. The next digit and remainder are determined uniquely by multiplying the current remainder by ten and dividing by $b$, so from that point the entire subsequent process repeats. Hence the decimal is eventually periodic.
