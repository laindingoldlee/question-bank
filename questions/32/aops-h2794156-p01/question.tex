Six animals sit at a round table. Each is a knight, who always tells the truth, or a liar, who always lies. At least one is known to be a knight. Asked how many of their two neighbors are knights, all six give the same answer. Knowing this, an observer still cannot determine the number of knights. One guest says, \emph{It is my birthday}, and a neighbor says, \emph{Yes, it is that guest's birthday}. Now the observer can determine the number of knights. How many are there?
