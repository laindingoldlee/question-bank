There are $3$ one-digit, $9$ two-digit, $27$ three-digit and $81$ four-digit favourite numbers, and $3+9+27+81=120$. So the first $120$ favourite numbers are exactly those with at most four digits.

A number is congruent to its digit sum modulo $3$, and the digits $1,3,4$ are congruent to $1,0,1$. Among the $3^k$ favourite numbers with $k$ digits, each position shows each of the three digits $3^{k-1}$ times, so the digit residues of all of them add up to
\[k\cdot3^{k-1}\cdot(1+0+1)=2k\cdot3^{k-1}.\]
Adding over $k=1,2,3,4$:
\[2+12+54+216=284\equiv2\pmod 3 .\]
The remainder is $\boxed{2}$.
