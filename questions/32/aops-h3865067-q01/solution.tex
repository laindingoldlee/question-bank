Temporarily label identical copies. The multiplicities of $M,I,S,P$ are $1,4,4,2$. For a letter with multiplicity $m$, its forbidden-position block is $m$ by $m$. The number of ways to force $k$ nonconflicting labelled letters into forbidden positions in this block is $\binom mk^2k!$. Thus define
\[
R_m(z)=\sum_{k=0}^m\binom mk^2k!z^k,\qquad
R(z)=R_1(z)R_4(z)^2R_2(z)=\sum r_kz^k.
\]
Inclusion-exclusion gives $\sum_{k=0}^{11}(-1)^kr_k(11-k)!$ labelled arrangements. Explicitly,
\[
R_1=1+z,\quad R_2=1+4z+2z^2,\quad
R_4=1+16z+72z^2+96z^3+24z^4.
\]
Their product has coefficients
\[
1,37,566,4690,23248,71888,140448,172128,
128832,55872,12672,1152.
\]
The alternating factorial sum is $746496$. Each unlabelled arrangement has $4!4!2!=1152$ labellings, all satisfying the same restrictions. Therefore the answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{746496}{1152}=648}$.
