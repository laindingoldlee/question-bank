Model the seven diagonals of each slope as fourteen binary toggle variables. A cell changes exactly when its two diagonal variables differ. The incidence graph connecting diagonals through cells has two connected components, corresponding to the two checkerboard colors. A toggle assignment does nothing exactly when all diagonal variables are equal within each component, giving four assignments in the kernel. Thus the $2^{14}$ assignments produce $\dfrac{2^{14}}{4}=\boxed{4096}$ distinct configurations.
