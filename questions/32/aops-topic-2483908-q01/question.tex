Finitely many ants are placed at distinct points of a straight rope $10$ metres long. Each ant starts walking along the rope towards one of its ends at $1$ metre per second. Whenever two ants meet, both instantly reverse direction and continue at the same speed. An ant that reaches an end of the rope falls off. Prove that after at most $10$ seconds all the ants have fallen off.
