Pair the factors of $300!$ as $k(301-k)$ for $k=1,\dots,150$. Each pair satisfies $k(301-k)\ge300$, because $k(301-k)-300=(k-1)(300-k)\ge0$. Hence
\[
300!\ge300^{150}=90000^{150}>10000^{150}=100^{300}. \qquad\blacksquare
\]
(The post's claim $300!<100^{300}$ is false.)
