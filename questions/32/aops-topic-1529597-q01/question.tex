Alex and Bob ride along a circular path whose circumference is $15$ km. They start at the same time from diametrically opposite points of the path. Alex rides at a constant speed of $35$ km per hour clockwise, and Bob at a constant speed of $25$ km per hour counterclockwise. They both ride for $3$ hours. How many times do they meet?
