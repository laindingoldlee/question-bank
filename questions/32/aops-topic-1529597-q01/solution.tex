They ride in opposite directions, so the distance between them measured along the path in Alex's direction of travel shrinks at $35+25=60$ km per hour.
\begin{itemize}
\item At the start they are half a lap apart, $7.5$ km, so they first meet after $\dfrac{7.5}{60}=\dfrac{1}{8}$ hour.
\item After each meeting they must together cover a full lap, $15$ km, before meeting again, which takes $\dfrac{15}{60}=\dfrac{1}{4}$ hour.
\end{itemize}
So they meet at times $\dfrac{1}{8}+\dfrac {k}{4}$ hours for $k=0,1,2,\dots$. The condition $\dfrac{1}{8}+\dfrac {k}{4}\le3$ gives $k\le11.5$, so $k=0,1,\dots,11$, and they meet $\boxed{12}$ times.
