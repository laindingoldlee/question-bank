Since $b^2=ac$, the first equation is $a\left(x+\dfrac {b}{a}\right)^2=0$, with the double root $x=-\dfrac {b}{a}$. Substituting in the second: $d\dfrac{b^2}{a^2}-2e\dfrac {b}{a}+f=0$. Divide by $\dfrac{b^2}{a}=c$:
\[\dfrac {d}{a}-\dfrac{2e}{b}+\dfrac {f}{c}=0,\quad\text{i.e.}\quad\dfrac {d}{a}+\dfrac {f}{c}=2\cdot\dfrac {e}{b}.\]
So they are in arithmetic progression: $\boxed{\text{(A)}}$.
