Both pentagons are built on the same side of their bases, and $\overrightarrow{AD}$ translates $ABEFG$ onto $DCHIJ$. So $I=F+\overrightarrow{AD}$, and with unit side length $\overrightarrow{EI}=\overrightarrow{EF}+\overrightarrow{AD}$.

Taking $\overrightarrow{AB}$ as direction $0^\circ$, the sides $\overrightarrow{BE}$ and $\overrightarrow{EF}$ have directions $72^\circ$ and $144^\circ$, and $\overrightarrow{AD}$ has direction $90^\circ$. Two unit vectors at $144^\circ$ and $90^\circ$ add up to a vector at $117^\circ$, so $\overrightarrow{EI}$ points at $117^\circ$.

Triangle $ABE$ is isosceles ($AB=BE$) with apex angle $108^\circ$, so $\overrightarrow{EA}$ points at $72^\circ+180^\circ-36^\circ=216^\circ$. Hence $\angle AEI=216^\circ-117^\circ=\boxed{99^\circ}$.

pair A=(0,0),B=(1,0),C=(1,1),D=(0,1);
pair Ep=B+dir(72),F=Ep+dir(144),G=F+dir(216);pair H=C+dir(72),I=H+dir(144),J=I+dir(216);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,gray);draw(A--B--Ep--F--G--cycle,linewidth(0.8));draw(D--C--H--I--J--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
draw(A--Ep--I,dashed);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,SW);label("$D$",D,SE);label("$E$",Ep,E);label("$F$",F,NW);label("$G$",G,W);label("$H$",H,E);label("$I$",I,N);label("$J$",J,W);
\end{asy}
