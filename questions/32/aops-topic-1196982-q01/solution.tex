$4^{\dfrac{3}{2}}=\left(\sqrt4\right)^3=8$, a constant. A constant is a polynomial (of degree $0$), so $\boxed{\text{yes}}$: it is the constant polynomial $8$. (Fractional exponents on a \emph{variable}, as in $x^{\dfrac{3}{2}}$, are what make an expression not a polynomial.)
