\emph{Method.} He takes any $10$ cards to form the first pile, leaves the other $42$ cards as the second pile, and then turns over every card of the first pile.

\emph{Proof.} Suppose that, before the turning, the first pile contains $j$ face-up cards ($0\le j\le10$). Then the second pile contains the other $10-j$ face-up cards. The first pile consists of $j$ face-up and $10-j$ face-down cards; after every card in it is turned over, it has exactly $10-j$ face-up cards. So both piles now contain $10-j$ face-up cards. $\blacksquare$
