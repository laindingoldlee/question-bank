A blind man is handed a deck of $52$ cards and told that exactly $10$ of them are face up. He cannot tell by touch which cards are face up, but he may turn cards over. How can he divide the cards into two piles, not necessarily of equal size, so that both piles contain the same number of face-up cards? Prove that your method works.
