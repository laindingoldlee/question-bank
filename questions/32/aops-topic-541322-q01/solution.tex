From $i+r^2=11$ with $i\le3$: $r=3$, $i=2$ (since $r=2$ gives $i=7>3$ and $r\le3$). Then $c=1$, $e=4$, $l=5$, and indeed $(c+l)^2=36$ and the digits are distinct. So $c\cdot i\cdot r\cdot e-i\cdot l=24-10=\boxed{14}$.
