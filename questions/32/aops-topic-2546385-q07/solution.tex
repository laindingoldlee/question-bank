Imagine all $5$ rounds are played; he wins the match exactly when he wins at least $3$ of them. With $p=0.84$ and $q=0.16$,
\[P=\binom53p^3q^2+\binom54p^4q+p^5\approx0.1518+0.3983+0.4182=0.9682.\]
So the probability is about $\boxed{97\%}$.
