Since $A\ge1$ and the digits strictly increase, all three digits are among $1,\dots,9$ and distinct. Conversely, any $3$ distinct digits from $1$ to $9$ can be written in increasing order in exactly one way. So the count is $\dbinom93=\boxed{84}$.
