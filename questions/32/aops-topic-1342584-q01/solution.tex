Long division gives $\dfrac{5}{13}=0.\overline{384615}$: the remainders $5,11,6,8,2,7$ cycle back to $5$ after six steps, so the digits $3,8,4,6,1,5$ repeat with period $6$.

Since $25=6\cdot4+1$, the $25$th digit is the first digit of the block, namely $\boxed{3}$.
