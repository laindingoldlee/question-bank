Each patient is either healthy or infected, so there are $2^6=64$ outcomes in all. The outcomes in which none of Al, Betty, Carl is infected leave only the other $3$ patients free: $2^3=8$ outcomes. By complementary counting the answer is $64-8=\boxed{56}$.
