If the diagonal segment lengths are $a,c$ and $b,d$ with common sine factor $\dfrac{s}{2}$, the four successive areas are $\dfrac{abs}{2},\dfrac{bcs}{2},\dfrac{cds}{2},\dfrac{das}{2}$. Opposite products are equal. Depending on which of 1,2,3 is opposite $x$, this gives $x=6,\dfrac{3}{2},\dfrac{2}{3}$. All are realisable by choosing positive diagonal segment ratios and scaling. Their sum is $\dfrac{49}{6}$, so $\boxed{p+q=55}$.
