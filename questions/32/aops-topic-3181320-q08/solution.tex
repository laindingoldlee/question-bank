There are $25$ usable letters, including five vowels. An adjacent ordered pair of distinct vowels has $5\cdot4=20$ choices, so either specified adjacent pair is forbidden in $20\cdot25=500$ words. Both adjacent pairs are forbidden in $5\cdot4\cdot4=80$ words. Inclusion--exclusion leaves $25^3-500-500+80=\boxed{14705}$.
