Let $v$ be the biking speed and $w$ the running speed (km per hour). Then
\[\dfrac{20}{v}+\dfrac{10}{w}=2.84,\qquad\dfrac{20}{v+2}+\dfrac{10}{w}=2.7,\]
since $2$ hours $42$ minutes is $2.7$ hours. Subtracting,
\[\dfrac{20}{v}-\dfrac{20}{v+2}=\dfrac{40}{v(v+2)}=0.14,\qquad\text{so}\qquad v^2+2v=\dfrac{2000}{7}.\]
Hence $v=-1+\sqrt{1+\dfrac{2000}{7}}=-1+\sqrt{\dfrac{2007}{7}}\approx15.933$ (taking the positive root). Then
\[\dfrac{10}{w}=2.84-\dfrac{20}{15.933}\approx2.84-1.2553=1.5847,\qquad w\approx6.310,\]
so Richard ran at about $\boxed{6.3}$ km per hour.
