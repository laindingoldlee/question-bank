A race consists of a $20$ kilometre bike ride followed by a $10$ kilometre run. Richard finished both parts in $2.84$ hours. If he had biked $2$ km per hour faster (running at the same speed), he would have finished in $2$ hours $42$ minutes. To the nearest tenth of a kilometre per hour, find Richard's running speed.
