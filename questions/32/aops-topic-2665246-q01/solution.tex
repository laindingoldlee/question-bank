\textbf{(a)} For $p=3,5,7,11,13,17,19$ we get $f(p)=23,47,79,167,223,359,439$, all prime (each has no prime factor up to its square root). But $f(23)=529+92+2=623=7\cdot89$. So the answer is $\boxed{23}$.

\textbf{(b)} If $x$ is even, $f(x)$ is even and greater than $2$, so it is composite. The odd composites in order are $9,15,21,25,\dots$, and
\[f(9)=119=7\cdot17,\qquad f(15)=287=7\cdot41,\qquad f(21)=527=17\cdot31,\qquad f(25)=727.\]
$727$ is prime (it is not divisible by $2,3,5,7,11,13,17,19,23$, and $27^2>727$). So the answer is $\boxed{25}$.
