Let $f(x)=x^2+4x+2$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the smallest prime $p>2$ for which $f(p)$ is not prime.
\item[(b)] Find the smallest composite positive integer $x$ for which $f(x)$ is prime.
\end{enumerate}
