The three prices are $10000$, $12500$ and $15000$. The three highest prices are now $12500$, $15000$ and the new price $p$, in geometric sequence, so
\[p=\dfrac{15000^2}{12500}=\boxed{18000\text{ dollars}}\]
(the ratio is $\dfrac65$).
