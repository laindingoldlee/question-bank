Three cars are for sale, and their prices form an arithmetic sequence. The cheapest costs $10000$ dollars and the most expensive $15000$ dollars. A fourth car, more expensive than the other three, is then added, and the three highest prices now form a geometric sequence. What is the price of the new car?
