\textbf{(a)} The board has $9$ horizontal and $9$ vertical grid lines. A rectangle is determined by choosing $2$ of the vertical lines and $2$ of the horizontal lines, so there are $\dbinom92^2=36^2=\boxed{1296}$ rectangles.

\textbf{(b)} A $k\times k$ square can be placed in $9-k$ positions horizontally and $9-k$ vertically, so there are $(9-k)^2$ of them. In total
\[8^2+7^2+\cdots+1^2=\dfrac{8\cdot9\cdot17}{6}=\boxed{204}\]
squares.
