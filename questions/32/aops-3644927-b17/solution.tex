The parallelogram law gives the sum of squared diagonals as twice the sum of squared adjacent sides. Thus $AC^2+BD^2=2(4^2+7^2)=\boxed{130}$.
\begin{asy}
import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(4,0),C=B+7*dir(105),D=7*dir(105);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C^^B--D,dashed);label("$A$",A,(-1,-1));label("$B$",B,(1,-1));label("$C$",C,(1,1));label("$D$",D,(-1,1));
\end{asy}
