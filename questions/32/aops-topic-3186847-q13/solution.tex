The altitude AM is $\sqrt{2^2-1^2}=\sqrt 3$, and P lies on the circle centered at M with radius 1. The reverse triangle inequality gives $AP\ge AM-MP=\sqrt 3-1$, attained when P lies between M and A. Hence $a+b=3+1=\boxed 4$.
\begin{asy}
import geometry; size(220);
pair B=(-1,0),C=(1,0),A=(0,sqrt(3)),M=(0,0),P=(0,1);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(circle(M,1));draw(A--M,dashed);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$M$",M,S);label("$P$",P,E);
\end{asy}
