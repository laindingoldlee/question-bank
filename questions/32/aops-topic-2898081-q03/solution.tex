By QM--AM, $\sqrt{a^2+b^2+c^2}\ge\dfrac{|a|+|b|+|c|}{\sqrt3}$, and similarly for the other three roots. Each variable appears in three of the four roots, so the left side is at least $\dfrac{3(|a|+|b|+|c|+|d|)}{\sqrt3}=\sqrt3(|a|+|b|+|c|+|d|)\ge\sqrt3(a+b+c+d)$. $\blacksquare$
