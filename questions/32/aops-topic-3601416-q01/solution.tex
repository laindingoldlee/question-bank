Take $C=D=0$ and $E=2$. Then $600(A+B)=177000$, so $A+B=295$. Both $(A,B,C,D,E)=(295,0,0,0,2)$ and $(294,1,0,0,2)$ satisfy the equation and differ. Hence the solution is not unique. In fact these fixed $C,D,E$ alone give $296$ possible ordered pairs $(A,B)$.
