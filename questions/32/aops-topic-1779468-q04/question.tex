You pick a number from $1$ to $6$ and then roll two fair six-sided dice. If your number does not show on either die, you lose $1$ dollar; if it shows on exactly one die, you win $2$ dollars; if it shows on both dice, you win $5$ dollars. Find your expected winnings per game. Is the game favourable, unfavourable or fair?
