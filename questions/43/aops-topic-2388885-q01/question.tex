A seller of dogwater scams each purchase with probability $60\%$, independently of the other purchases: a purchase that is not a scam gives the buyer one bottle of dogwater, and a scam gives nothing. A buyer plans to buy at most $10$ times, but buys again only if he was not scammed on his previous purchase. What is the expected number of bottles he receives?
