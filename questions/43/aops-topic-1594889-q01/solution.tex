Let the train have length $L$ feet, so its speed is $\dfrac {L}{3}$ feet per second. From the moment its front reaches the station until its end passes the far end, the front travels the length of the station plus the length of the train, $336+L$ feet. So
\[
\dfrac{336+L}{10}=\dfrac {L}{3}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 1008+3L=10L\quad\Longrightarrow\quad L=\boxed{144}.
\]
Check: the speed is $48$ ft/s, and $\dfrac{336+144}{48}=10$ seconds.
