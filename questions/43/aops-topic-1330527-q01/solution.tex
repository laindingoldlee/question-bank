We use the minus sign. First, every integer $m$ that is odd or divisible by $4$ is a difference of two squares:
\[
2k+1=(k+1)^2-k^2,\qquad 4k=(k+1)^2-(k-1)^2 .
\]
If $n\not\equiv2\pmod4$, take $z=0$ and write $n=x^2-y^2$. If $n\equiv2\pmod4$, take $z=1$: then $n-1$ is odd, so $n-1=x^2-y^2$ and $n=x^2-y^2+1^3$. Either way $n$ has the required form. $\blacksquare$
