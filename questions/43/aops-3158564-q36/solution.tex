If all other submissions sum to $S_0$, submitting $n$ gives total $S=S_0+n$. The expected score is $\boxed{\dfrac{n}{S_0+n}\lfloor\min(K,\dfrac{10000}{S_0+n})\rfloor}$. Without $S_0$ and the secret $K$, a numerical optimal submission is not determined. The formula includes both the chance of selection and the rounded prize, so maximizing selection probability alone is not sufficient.
