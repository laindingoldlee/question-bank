Parameterise the diagonal by $(at,bt,ct)$ for $0<t<1$. It crosses $a-1$, $b-1$, and $c-1$ internal coordinate planes in the three directions. Two crossings cannot occur simultaneously: for instance $\dfrac{i}{a}=\dfrac{j}{b}$ with $0<i<a$, $0<j<b$ would contradict $\gcd(a,b)=1$. Therefore every crossing enters exactly one new unit cube, so the number is
\[
1+(a-1)+(b-1)+(c-1)=a+b+c-2.
\]
Equating this to $159$ gives $\boxed{a+b+c=161}$.
