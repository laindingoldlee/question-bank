The equation is equivalent to
\[\dfrac{20}{17}=a+\dfrac{cd+1}{bcd+b+d}=a+\dfrac{1}{b+\dfrac{1}{c+\dfrac{1}{d}}}.\]
The final fraction is strictly between 0 and 1, so $a=1$. Taking a reciprocal of the remainder gives $\dfrac{17}{3}=b+\dfrac{1}{c+\dfrac{1}{d}}$. The final fraction is in $(0,1]$, forcing $b=5$. Thus $c+\dfrac{1}{d}=\dfrac{3}{2}$, giving $c=1,d=2$. Consequently $a^2+b^2+c^2+d^2=1+25+1+4=\boxed{31}$.
