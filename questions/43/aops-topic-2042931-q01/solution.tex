The number of trailing zeros in base \(12\) is the largest \(k\) with \(12^k=2^{2k}3^k\) dividing \(58!\).

By Legendre's formula,
\[
v_2(58!)=\left\lfloor\dfrac{58}{2}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{58}{4}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{58}{8}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{58}{16}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{58}{32}\right\rfloor=29+14+7+3+1=54,
\]
\[
v_3(58!)=\left\lfloor\dfrac{58}{3}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{58}{9}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{58}{27}\right\rfloor=19+6+2=27 .
\]
We need \(2k\le54\) and \(k\le27\), so \(k=27\). Thus \(58!\) ends in \(\boxed{27}\) zeros in base \(12\).
