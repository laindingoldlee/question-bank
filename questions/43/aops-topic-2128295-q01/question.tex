Let $x$ be a positive real number that is not an integer, and let $y=\lfloor x\rfloor\cdot\lceil x\rceil$. Suppose $y\ne0$. Define
\[\sqrt{y+\sqrt{y+\sqrt{y+\cdots}}}\qquad\text{and}\qquad\sqrt{y-\sqrt{y-\sqrt{y-\cdots}}}\]
as the limits of the sequences $a_1=\sqrt y$, $a_{k+1}=\sqrt{y+a_k}$ and $b_1=\sqrt y$, $b_{k+1}=\sqrt{y-b_k}$. Prove that
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $\sqrt{y+\sqrt{y+\sqrt{y+\cdots}}}$ is an integer;
\item[(b)] $\sqrt{y-\sqrt{y-\sqrt{y-\cdots}}}$ is an integer;
\item[(c)] $\sqrt{y+\sqrt{y+\cdots}}-\sqrt{y-\sqrt{y-\cdots}}=1$.
\end{enumerate}
