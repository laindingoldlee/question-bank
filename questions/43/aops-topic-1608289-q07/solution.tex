Lay down the seven red cards first. They create $8$ gaps: one before the first card, one between each pair of neighbours and one after the last card. No two black cards are adjacent exactly when the three black cards go into three different gaps, and since cards of each colour are identical, an arrangement is determined by the set of three gaps used. So the number of arrangements is
\[
\dbinom83=\boxed{56}.
\]
