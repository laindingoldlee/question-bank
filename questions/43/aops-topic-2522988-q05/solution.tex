Since $P$ is quadratic and $x^2-4x+4=(x-2)^2$ is quadratic, the quotient is the constant $a$:
\[P(x)=a(x-2)^2-15x+81.\]
By the remainder theorem, $P(-1)=42$: $9a+15+81=42$, so $a=-6$. Then
\[P(x)=-6\left(x^2-4x+4\right)-15x+81=-6x^2+9x+57,\]
and $a+b+c=P(1)=-6+9+57=\boxed{60}$.
