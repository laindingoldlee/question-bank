Let $x=\sqrt a$, $y=\sqrt b$, $z=\sqrt c$. Then
\[
a+b+c-\sqrt{ab}-\sqrt{bc}-\sqrt{ca}=x^2+y^2+z^2-xy-yz-zx=\dfrac{1}{2}\left[(x-y)^2+(y-z)^2+(z-x)^2\right]\ge0,
\]
with equality iff $a=b=c$. $\blacksquare$
