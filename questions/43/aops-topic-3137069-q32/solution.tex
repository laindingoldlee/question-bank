Let $ E_i $ be the event that positions $ i $ through $ i+3 $ are all different, for $ i=1,2,3 $. Each has $ 4!\cdot4^2=384 $ strings. Adjacent intersections each have $ 4!\cdot4=96 $, since the fifth character is forced to equal the first. For $ E_1\cap E_3 $, after fixing the first four letters, positions 5 and 6 must be the first two letters in either order, giving 48. The triple intersection forces positions 5 and 6 in their original order, giving 24. Inclusion-exclusion yields $ 3\cdot384-96-96-48+24=\boxed{936}$.
