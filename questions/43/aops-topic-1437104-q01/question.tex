A PE class has $12$ students, $6$ girls and $6$ boys. The coach has $4$ jerseys in each of $3$ colours to mark $3$ teams of $4$ for a soccer tournament. If the coach wants at least one girl and at least one boy on each team, in how many ways can he give out the jerseys? (Jerseys of the same colour are indistinguishable.)
