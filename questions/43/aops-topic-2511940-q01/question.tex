There are $5$ different pairs of gloves, and left and right gloves are distinguishable, so there are $10$ gloves in all. In how many ways can $4$ of the gloves be selected so that some $2$ of the $4$ form a pair? Count the selections directly (without complementary counting), and explain why a direct count giving $120$ is incomplete.
