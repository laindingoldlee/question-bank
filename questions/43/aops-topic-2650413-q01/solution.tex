A total of $k$ eggs can be laid in $\dbinom{10}{k}$ ways (choose the $k$ laying days). The values $\dbinom{10}{k}$ for $k=0,1,\dots,5$ are $1,10,45,120,210,252$, and $\dbinom{10}{k}=\dbinom{10}{10-k}$. So $\dbinom{10}{k}=120$ exactly for $k=3$ and $k=7$, and the least total is $\boxed{3}$.
