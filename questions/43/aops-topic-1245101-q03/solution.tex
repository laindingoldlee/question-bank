ORDERED has the letters O, R, R, D, D, E, E. Glue one R, E, D into a block RED; with the remaining O, R, D, E (all different) we arrange $5$ distinct objects: $5!=120$. An arrangement containing RED twice (RED RED with O somewhere) is counted twice; there are $\dfrac{3!}{2!}=3$ of those. Hence $120-3=\boxed{117}$.
