Choose $A=(0,0),C=(c,0),D=(0,d),B=(c,-d)$ with $c,d>0$. Projection onto $CD$ gives $H = (\dfrac{c d^2}{c^2+d^2} , \dfrac{c^2 d}{c^2+d^2})$. The circle through $A,D,B$ is $x^2 + y^2 - (\dfrac{c^2+2d^2}{c}) x - d y = 0$. Its tangent at $D$ meets the x-axis at $P = (- \dfrac{c d^2}{c^2+2d^2} , 0)$. For each ordered vector pair $(BP,BA)$ and $(BD,BH)$, direct determinant-over-dot-product calculation gives $- \dfrac{c d^3}{c^4+4c^2d^2+2d^4}$. Both dot products are positive, so the two smaller angles are acute and have equal tangents. Hence $\boxed{\angle PBA=\angle DBH}$.

\end{asy}
