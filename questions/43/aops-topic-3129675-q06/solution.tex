The nonzero right sides force $x,y,z\ne0$. Taking reciprocals and setting $u=\dfrac{1}{x},v=\dfrac{1}{y},w=\dfrac{1}{z}$ gives $u+v=\dfrac{3}{8}$, $v+w=\dfrac{5}{12}$, $u+w=\dfrac{7}{24}$. Addition of the first and third minus the second gives $2u=\dfrac{1}{4}$, so $u=\dfrac{1}{8}$. Then $v=\dfrac{1}{4},w=\dfrac{1}{6}$. Thus $\boxed{(x,y,z)=(8,4,6)}$, which satisfies all three original equations and has nonzero denominators.
