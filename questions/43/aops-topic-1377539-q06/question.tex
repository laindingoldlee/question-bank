When adding using the standard method, it is sometimes necessary to compose, or ``carry,'' a ten, a hundred, or a thousand. For example, when we add $15$ to $16$, we get $11$ ones and compose a ten. For how many ordered pairs $(a,b)$ of three-digit numbers $a$ and $b$ does the addition $a+b$ require at least one digit to be carried?

(A student computes $900^2-55\cdot55\cdot45$. Is that right?)
