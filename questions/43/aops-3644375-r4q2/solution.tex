Bisect the isosceles triangle from its apex. The half-base is $2\cos36^\circ$, so $BC=4\cos36^\circ=1+\sqrt5$. Thus $a+b=\boxed6$.
\begin{asy}
import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
pair B=(-2*cos(pi/5),0),C=(2*cos(pi/5),0),A=(0,2*sin(pi/5));draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--(0,0),dashed);label("$2$",(A+B)/2,(-1,1));label("$2$",(A+C)/2,(1,1));label("$36^\circ$",B+(.35,.13));
\end{asy}
