Check $81,\ldots,89$: $81=3^4$, $82=2\cdot41$, $84$, $86$, $88$ are even, $85=5\cdot17$, $87=3\cdot29$. The primes are $83$ and $89$ (neither is divisible by $2,3,5,7$, and $\sqrt{89}<10$). Their sum is $\boxed{172}$.
