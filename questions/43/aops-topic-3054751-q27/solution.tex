There are six outfits. Assigning four different outfits to four labelled students gives $6\cdot5\cdot4\cdot3=360=2^3 3^2 5$ possibilities per delegation. Across 56 independent delegations the total is $360^{56}=2^{168}3^{112}5^{56}$. Therefore $\boxed{a+b+c=336}$.
