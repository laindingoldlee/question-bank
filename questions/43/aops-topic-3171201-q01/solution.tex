Over the reals, the initial equation has no solution because $(x+\dfrac{1}{2})^2+\dfrac{3}{4}>0$. Over $\mathbb C$, the equation has roots $\boxed{\dfrac{-1+i\sqrt3}{2},\dfrac{-1-i\sqrt3}{2}}$. They do satisfy $x^3=1$, but that equation has three complex solutions, not only $1$. The invalid inference is $x^3=1\Rightarrow x=1$ when working over $\mathbb C$.
