Taking reciprocals: $\dfrac1a+\dfrac1b=3$, $\dfrac1b+\dfrac1c=4$, $\dfrac1c+\dfrac1a=5$. Adding, $\dfrac1a+\dfrac1b+\dfrac1c=6$. Dividing numerator and denominator by $abc$, $\dfrac{24abc}{ab+bc+ca}=\dfrac{24}{\dfrac1a+\dfrac1b+\dfrac1c}=\dfrac{24}6=\boxed{4}$.
