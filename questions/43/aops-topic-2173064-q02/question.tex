Consider rectangle $ABCD$. Let $E$ be the midpoint of side $AD$ and let $F$ be the midpoint of $ED$. Let $G$ be the intersection of line $CE$ with line $AB$, and let $H$ be the intersection of line $BF$ with line $CD$. The ratio of the area of triangle $BCG$ to the area of triangle $BCH$ can be written as $\dfrac {m}{n}$ in lowest terms. Compute $10m+10n+mn$.
