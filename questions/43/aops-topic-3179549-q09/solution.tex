The altitude is $3r$ and the incenter is $r$ above the base. The first intersection from $C$ is the top of the incircle, $2r$ above the base. Thus $MN=2r$, $CN=r$, and the ratio is $\boxed2$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair C=(0,3),A=(-sqrt(3),0),Bp=(sqrt(3),0),M=(0,0),Np=(0,2);draw(A--Bp--C--cycle);draw(circle((0,1),1));draw(C--M,dashed);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",Bp,SE);label("$C$",C,N);label("$M$",M,S);label("$N$",Np,E);
\end{asy}
