If $f$ is constant, $f=c$, then $2c+2x^2=c+8x+5$ fails. If $\deg f=n\ge1$, the right side has degree $2n$ and the left side degree at most $\max(n,2)$, so $2n\le2$ and $n=1$.

Put $f=ax+b$. Then $2ax+2b+2x^2=a(x^2-2x+4)+b+8x+5$. Comparing coefficients: from $x^2$, $a=2$; from $x$, $2a=-2a+8$, which holds; from the constant term, $2b=4a+b+5$, so $b=13$. Therefore
\[\boxed{f(x)=2x+13}.\]
Check: $2(2x+13)+2x^2=2x^2+4x+26$ and $2(x^2-2x+4)+13+8x+5=2x^2+4x+26$.
