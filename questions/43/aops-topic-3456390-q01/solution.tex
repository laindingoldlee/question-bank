All terms lie in $[1,2]$, and terms from $a_3$ onward lie in $[\dfrac{3}{2},2]$. Put $\phi=\dfrac{1+\sqrt5}{2}$, so $\phi=1+\dfrac{1}{\phi}$. Then $|a_{n+1}-\phi|=\dfrac{|a_n-\phi|}{a_n\phi}\le\dfrac{2}{3\phi}|a_n-\phi|$ for $n\ge3$. The constant is less than one, so iteration makes the error tend to zero. The limit is $\boxed{\dfrac{1+\sqrt5}{2}}$.
