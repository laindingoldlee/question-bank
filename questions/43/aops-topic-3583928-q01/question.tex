For a positive integer $n$, label $2n$ cyclically ordered points by residues modulo $2n$. Alice chooses $1\le d\le n$. Bob must partition the points into pairs whose indices differ by $d$ modulo $2n$. For which $n$ can Alice choose $d$ making this impossible?
