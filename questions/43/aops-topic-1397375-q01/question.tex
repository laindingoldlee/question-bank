A robot walks once around a regular $k$-gon, turning by the same angle at each corner.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Explain why the robot turns by $\dfrac{360^\circ}{k}$ at each corner, and deduce that each interior angle of a regular $k$-gon is $180^\circ-\dfrac{360^\circ}{k}$.
\item[(b)] Show that this agrees with the formula $180^\circ(k-2)$ for the sum of the interior angles.
\item[(c)] Find the interior angle of a regular $360$-gon.
\end{enumerate}
