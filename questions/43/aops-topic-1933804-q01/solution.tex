Both sides of $|A|=|B|$ are nonnegative, so it is equivalent to $A^2=B^2$, i.e.\ $(A-B)(A+B)=0$, i.e.\ $A=B$ or $A=-B$.

\textbf{Case $x^2-7=x-5$:} $x^2-x-2=0$, $(x-2)(x+1)=0$, so $x=2$ or $x=-1$.

\textbf{Case $x^2-7=-(x-5)$:} $x^2+x-12=0$, $(x+4)(x-3)=0$, so $x=-4$ or $x=3$.

By the equivalence all four values are solutions (e.g.\ $x=-4$: $|-9|=9=|16-7|$). The solutions are $\boxed{x=-4,\ -1,\ 2,\ 3}$.
