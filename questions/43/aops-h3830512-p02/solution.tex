Interchange the finite sums: $S(n)=\sum_{k=1}^n(n-k+1)\lfloor\sqrt{k}\rfloor$. Group terms between consecutive squares, where the floor is constant, or use the exact recurrences $Q(n)=Q(n-1)+\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor$ and $S(n)=S(n-1)+Q(n)$. Evaluation gives $S(36)=1943$ and $S(37)=2080$. Every increment is positive, so the least qualifying integer is $\boxed{37}$.
