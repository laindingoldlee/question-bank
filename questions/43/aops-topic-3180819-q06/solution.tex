Since $64=2^6$, write $a=2^k$ with positive integer $k$ and $kb=6$. The divisor pairs $(k,b)=(1,6),(2,3),(3,2),(6,1)$ give sums $8,7,10,65$. The minimum is $\boxed7$, attained by $(a,b)=(4,3)$.
