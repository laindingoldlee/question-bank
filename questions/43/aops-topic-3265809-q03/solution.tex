If M's solo time is $t>0$, their combined rate is $\dfrac{1}{5}+\dfrac{1}{t}$. Finishing within two hours requires $\dfrac{1}{5}+\dfrac{1}{t}\ge\dfrac{1}{2}$, or $\dfrac{1}{t}\ge\dfrac{3}{10}$. Thus $t\le\dfrac{10}{3}$, with equality attainable by proportional division of the work. The greatest solo time is $\boxed{\dfrac{10}{3}\text{ hours}}$.
