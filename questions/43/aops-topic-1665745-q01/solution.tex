The factorial applies to the number just before it, so $3!^2$ means $(3!)^2$. Since $3!=3\cdot2\cdot1=6$,
\[
3!^2=6^2=\boxed{36}.
\]
(It is not $(3^2)!=9!=362880$; to mean that, one must write $\left(3^2\right)!$.)
