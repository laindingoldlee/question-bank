Factor the second equation: $(a-b)(a+b)=51$. Since $a+b=15$, $a-b=\dfrac{17}{5}$. Adding and subtracting the two linear equations gives $a=\dfrac{15+\dfrac{17}{5}}{2}=\dfrac{46}{5}=9.2$ and $b=\dfrac{15-\dfrac{17}{5}}{2}=\dfrac{29}{5}=5.8$. Their sum is $15$ and their squared difference is $(\dfrac{17}{5})15=51$, verifying both values.
