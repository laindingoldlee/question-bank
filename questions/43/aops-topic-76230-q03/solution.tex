Write the numbers $0,1,\dots,9999$ with four digits each, padding with leading zeros (this does not change digit sums). Each of the $4$ positions runs through every digit $0,\dots,9$ exactly $1000$ times, so the sum of all digits is
\[
4\cdot1000\cdot(0+1+\dots+9)=4\cdot1000\cdot45=180000 .
\]
Adding the digit sum $1$ of $10{,}000$ gives $\boxed{180001}$. (The answer $49995$ suggested in the post is wrong.)
