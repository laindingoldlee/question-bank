$A=\left(-\dfrac{2k}{k-1},0\right)$ and $B=(0,2k)$. Since $k>1$, $A$ is on the negative $x$-axis, so the base is $OA=\dfrac{2k}{k-1}$, a positive length.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((-7,0)--(2,0),Arrow);draw((0,-1)--(0,7),Arrow);pair A=(-6,0),B=(0,6);draw((-6.5,-0.5)--(0.5,6.5));
fill((0,0)--A--B--cycle,lightgray);label("$A$",A,S);label("$B$",B,E);label("$O$",(0,0),SE);
\end{asy}

Then $\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac{2k}{k-1}\cdot2k=9$, so $2k^2=9k-9$, i.e. $(2k-3)(k-3)=0$, and $k=\boxed{\dfrac32\text{ or }3}$. Using the signed coordinate $-\dfrac{2k}{k-1}$ as a length gives a negative area; that was the error.
