The $24$ arrangements of $0,5,1,2$ are distinct; the $6$ beginning with $5$ exceed $5000$, and the other $18$ (e.g. $0125=125$) are valid room numbers. Of the $24$ arrangements of $4,9,1,3$, those at most $5000$ begin with $1$, $3$ or $4$: $18$. No number is counted twice. So $36$ rooms qualify, and the probability is $\dfrac{36}{5000}=\boxed{\dfrac9{1250}}$.
