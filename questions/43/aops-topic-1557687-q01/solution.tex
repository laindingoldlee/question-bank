Every minute of the day shows one of the $24\cdot60=1440$ displays, so count the displays with no $5$ and no $6$ (complementary counting).
\begin{itemize}
\item Hours: among $00$--$23$, those containing a $5$ or $6$ are $05$, $06$, $15$, $16$; that leaves $20$ hours.
\item Minutes: the tens digit is $0$--$5$, so it must be $0$--$4$ ($5$ choices); the units digit avoids $5$ and $6$ ($8$ choices): $40$ minutes.
\end{itemize}
So $20\cdot40=800$ displays have no $5$ or $6$, and the fraction with at least one is
\[
\dfrac{1440-800}{1440}=\dfrac{640}{1440}=\boxed{\dfrac{4}{9}}.
\]
