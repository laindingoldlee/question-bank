Place $B=(0,0)$, $C=(28,0)$. The distance equations give $A=(8,15)$. The half-angle formula gives the slope of the bisector as $\dfrac{15}{17+8}=\dfrac{3}{5}$. Thus $P=(8,\dfrac{24}{5})$, and $BP=\sqrt{64+\dfrac{576}{25}}=\dfrac{8\sqrt{34}}{5}$. The answer is $8+34+5=\boxed{47}$.
