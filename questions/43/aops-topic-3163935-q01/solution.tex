A line intersects the boundary of a convex square in at most two points, unless it overlaps a side, which is excluded. Three triangle sides therefore give at most 6 intersections. The displayed construction attains two crossings per side, with all six distinct and no parallel sides. Thus $\boxed6$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw((-1,-1)--(1,-1)--(1,1)--(-1,1)--cycle);pair A=rotate(10)*(0,1.5),B=rotate(10)*(1.5,-.75),C=rotate(10)*(-1.5,-.75);draw(A--B--C--cycle,blue);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,E);label("$C$",C,W);
\end{asy}
