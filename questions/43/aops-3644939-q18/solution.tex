After $k$ zeros, $N=2^k3^45^{k+2}$. The sum of its even divisors is $(2^{k+1}-2)(1+3+3^2+3^3+3^4)(1+5+\cdots+5^{k+2})=\dfrac{2(2^k-1)121(5^{k+3}-1)}{4}$. Divisibility by five requires $k$ a multiple of four. Then $k+3$ is odd, so the final factor is not divisible by three. For $k=4,8$, $2^k-1$ has exactly one factor of three; for $k=12$, it has two. Thus the first multiple of four satisfying divisibility by nine as well is $\boxed{12}$.
