There are $n$ kids around a pile of pencils. The first kid takes $1$ pencil and then one fifth of the remaining pencils. Then the second kid takes $1$ pencil and then one fifth of the remaining pencils, and so on, until all $n$ kids have had a turn. No pencil is ever broken, so every amount taken is a whole number; pencils may be left in the pile at the end. Prove that the smallest possible number of pencils in the original pile is $5^n-4$.
