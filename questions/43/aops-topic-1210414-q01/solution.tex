Numbers below $100$ that leave remainder $10$ on division by $11$: $10,21,32,43,54,65,76,87,98$. Of these only $87$ ends in $7$ (remainder $7$ on division by $10$), and $87=6\cdot14+3$ leaves remainder $3$ on division by $6$. So she has $\boxed{87}$ disks.
