Let the universe $U$ be the set of letters of the alphabet, $A=\{a,b,d,f,g\}$, and let $B\subseteq U$ satisfy $A\cap B=\{b,d\}$. Which of the following could be $B$?

(a) $\{b,d,g\}$\qquad (b) $\{b,d,k\}$\qquad (c) $\{b,d,5,e\}$\qquad (d) $\{a,f,g\}$
