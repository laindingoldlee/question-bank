The fraction is already reduced, and its denominator is $2^{4034}5^{2018}$. A reduced denominator $2^a5^b$ requires exactly $\max(a,b)$ decimal places, since that is the least $k$ for which it divides $10^k$. Here the answer is $\boxed{4034}$. Multiplying numerator and denominator by $5^{2016}$ produces denominator $10^{4034}$ and a numerator not divisible by ten, confirming that no trailing decimal zeroes can be removed.
