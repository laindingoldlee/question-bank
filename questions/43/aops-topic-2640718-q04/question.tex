In a middle-school mentoring program, a number of the sixth graders are paired with a ninth-grade student as a buddy. No ninth grader is assigned more than one sixth-grade buddy. If $\dfrac13$ of all the ninth graders are paired with $\dfrac25$ of all the sixth graders, what fraction of the total number of sixth and ninth graders have a buddy?
