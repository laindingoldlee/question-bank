Let there be $N$ ninth graders and $S$ sixth graders. The pairs are one-to-one, so $\dfrac N3=\dfrac{2S}5$, i.e. $N=\dfrac{6S}5$. The number of students with a buddy is $2\cdot\dfrac{2S}{5}=\dfrac{4S}5$, out of $S+\dfrac{6S}5=\dfrac{11S}5$. The fraction is $\boxed{\dfrac4{11}}$.
