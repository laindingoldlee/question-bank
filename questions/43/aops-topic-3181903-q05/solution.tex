The $k$th line can intersect the preceding lines in at most $k-1$ points, dividing it into at most $k$ pieces and adding at most $k$ regions. No parallel lines and no triple concurrence attain this bound. Thus the maximum is $1+1+2+3+4=\boxed{11}$.

\end{asy}
