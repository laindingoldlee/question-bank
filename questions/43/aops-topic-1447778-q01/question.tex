A \emph{diagonal} of a polygon is a segment joining two vertices that are not adjacent.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find a formula for the number of diagonals of a convex polygon with $n$ sides.
\item[(b)] How many diagonals does a chiliagon (a polygon with $1000$ sides) have?
\end{enumerate}
