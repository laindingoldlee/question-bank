Since $3^3=27<50<64=4^3$, a cube of an integer lies strictly between $-50$ and $50$ exactly when $-3\le n\le3$ (as $(-n)^3=-n^3$). That is $\boxed{7}$ integers: $-3,-2,-1,0,1,2,3$. (Neither $-50$ nor $50$ is a cube, so it does not matter whether the endpoints are included.)
