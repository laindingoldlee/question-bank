Let $t=|x|\ge0$. Since $x^2=t^2$, the equation becomes $t^2-t-5=0$, so $t=\dfrac{1\pm\sqrt{21}}{2}$. Only the positive root is allowed, $t=\dfrac{1+\sqrt{21}}{2}$. Hence
\[x=\boxed{\pm\dfrac{1+\sqrt{21}}{2}}.\]
