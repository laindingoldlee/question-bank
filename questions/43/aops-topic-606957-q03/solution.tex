The first two lines meet at $(0,-10)$. The line $y=4$ meets them at $x=42$ and $x=-12$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair P=(0,-10),Q=(42,4),R=(-12,4); filldraw(P--Q--R--cycle,lightblue,blue); draw((-15,0)--(45,0),gray,Arrow); draw((0,-12)--(0,7),gray,Arrow);
label("$(0,-10)$",P,S); label("$(42,4)$",Q,NE); label("$(-12,4)$",R,NW);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The triangle has base $42-(-12)=54$ on $y=4$ and height $4-(-10)=14$, so its area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot54\cdot14=\boxed{378}$.
