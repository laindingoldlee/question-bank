The sum is $128$ and there are $13$ entries, so the mean is $\boxed{\dfrac{128}{13}}$. In increasing order they are $1,2,4,6,6,7,9,10,11,15,18,19,20$; the seventh is the median $\boxed9$. The range is $20-1=\boxed{19}$, and the unique mode is $\boxed6$.
