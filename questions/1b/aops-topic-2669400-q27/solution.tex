The centre $(h,k)$ is equidistant from the three points. From the first two,
\[(h+3)^2+(k+4)^2=(h-3)^2+(k+6)^2\iff12h-4k=20\iff3h-k=5,\]
and from the last two,
\[(h-3)^2+(k+6)^2=(h-11)^2+(k+2)^2\iff16h+8k=80\iff2h+k=10.\]
So $h=3$, $k=4$. The radius is the distance from $(3,4)$ to $(3,-6)$, namely $10$. Centre $\boxed{(3,4)}$, radius $\boxed{10}$.
