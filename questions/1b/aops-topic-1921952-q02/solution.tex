The surface consists of three parts (the top of the cylinder and the flat face of the hemisphere are glued together and are not on the surface):

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real r=5,hh=10,k=2.2;
path el(real y){return shift(0,y)*xscale(r)*yscale(k)*unitcircle;}
draw(arc((0,hh),r,0,180),linewidth(1));
draw((-r,0)--(-r,hh),linewidth(1));draw((r,0)--(r,hh),linewidth(1));
draw(shift(0,0)*xscale(r)*yscale(k)*arc((0,0),1,180,360),linewidth(1));
draw(shift(0,0)*xscale(r)*yscale(k)*arc((0,0),1,0,180),dashed);
draw(shift(0,hh)*xscale(r)*yscale(k)*arc((0,0),1,180,360),gray+dashed);
draw(shift(0,hh)*xscale(r)*yscale(k)*arc((0,0),1,0,180),gray+dashed);
draw((0,0)--(r,0),linewidth(0.7));dot((0,0));label("$5$",(2,0),S);
draw((r+1.5,0)--(r+1.5,hh),Arrows(4));label("$10$",(r+1.5,hh/2),E);
draw((0,hh)--(0,hh+r),dotted);label("$5$",(0,hh+3.8),E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

\begin{itemize}
\item the curved surface of the hemisphere: half of $4\pi r^2$, that is $2\pi\cdot5^2=50\pi$;
\item the lateral surface of the cylinder: $2\pi rh=2\pi\cdot5\cdot10=100\pi$;
\item the bottom disc of the cylinder: $\pi\cdot5^2=25\pi$.
\end{itemize}
The total surface area is $50\pi+100\pi+25\pi=\boxed{175\pi}$.
