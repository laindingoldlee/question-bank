The octagon consists of $8$ triangles with two sides $4$ and an angle of $45^\circ$ at $O$, so its area is
\[8\cdot\dfrac12\cdot4\cdot4\cdot\sin45^\circ=32\sqrt2.\]
The circle has area $16\pi$, so the required area is $\boxed{16\pi-32\sqrt2}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw(unitcircle,linewidth(0.7));pair[] V;for(int i=0;i<8;++i)V[i]=dir(22.5+45i);
for(int i=0;i<8;++i)draw(V[i]--V[(i+1)%8],linewidth(0.8));
fill(V[0]--arc((0,0),1,22.5,67.5)--cycle,lightgray);
draw((0,0)--V[0],dashed);draw((0,0)--V[1],dashed);dot((0,0));
label("$O$",(0,0),S);label("$4$",V[0]/2,SE,fontsize(8pt));label("$45^\circ$",(0,0),dir(45)*3.5,fontsize(7pt));
\end{asy}
