The cross-section is roughly a circle of circumference $766$ cm, so its radius is
\[
r=\dfrac{766}{2\pi}\approx\dfrac{766}{6.283}\approx121.9\text{ cm}.
\]
Each year adds about $0.5$ cm to the radius, so the number of rings is about
\[
\dfrac{121.9}{0.5}\approx244 .
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
for(int k=1;k<=8;++k) draw(circle((0,0),k*0.5),k==8?black+linewidth(1):gray+linewidth(0.5));
draw((0,0)--(4,0),red+linewidth(0.9));label("$r\approx122$ cm",(4,0),E,red);
label("rings $0.5$ cm thick",(0,-4.1),S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
