Two ellipses have at most four isolated intersections, a line and ellipse at most two, and two lines at most one. The bound is $4\binom32+2\cdot3\cdot3+\binom32=12+18+3=\boxed{33}$. Three suitably rotated ellipses near a common centre can meet pairwise four times; three generic secants near that centre each cross every ellipse twice and meet one another at distinct points. A small perturbation avoids triple coincidences and attains the bound.
\begin{asy}
import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
for(int i=0;i<3;++i)draw(rotate(60*i)*ellipse((0,0),3,1),blue);draw((-3.5,.15)--(3.5,.15));draw((-3.5,-1.8)--(3.5,1.7));draw((-.3,-3.5)--(.4,3.5));label("three ellipses and three secants",(0,-4));
\end{asy}
