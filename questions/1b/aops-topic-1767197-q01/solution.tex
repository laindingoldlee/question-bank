The equation is $1-\dfrac{1}{1-x}=\dfrac{1}{1-x}$ with $x\ne1$. Then $1=\dfrac{2}{1-x}$, so $1-x=2$ and $x=\boxed{-1}$. Check: $\dfrac{1}{1-(-1)}=\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $1-\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{1}{2}$.
