Place $A=(0,0),B=(1,0),D=(0,1),C=(1,1)$. Tangency to the two side segments puts the center at $(r,r)$, with $0<r\le1$. Passing through $C$ gives $2(1-r)^2=r^2$, whose roots are $2\pm\sqrt2$. Only $r=2-\sqrt2$ has tangency points on the segments. Hence the area is $\boxed{\pi(6-4\sqrt2)}$. 
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(220);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
real r=2-sqrt(2);pair A=(0,0),B=(1,0),C=(1,1),D=(0,1),O=(r,r);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(circle(O,r));draw(O--(r,0),dashed);label("$A$",A,(-1,-1));label("$B$",B,(1,-1));label("$C$",C,(1,1));label("$D$",D,(-1,1));label("$O$",O);
\end{asy}
