Arc $MULD$ is a semicircle with centre $A$ (so $MD$ is a diameter, and $U$, $L$ lie on the arc). Points $H$, $U$, $L$, $K$ lie on a line $\ell$, with $MH\perp\ell$ and $DK\perp\ell$. Prove that
\[
HU^2+UK^2=HL^2+LK^2 .
\]
