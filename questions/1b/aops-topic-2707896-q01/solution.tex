The first digit is at least $1$ and the digits never decrease, so no digit is $0$. A spooky number is determined by the multiset of its four digits from $\{1,\dots,9\}$ (written in increasing order). By stars and bars there are $\dbinom{9+4-1}{4}=\dbinom{12}{4}=\boxed{495}$.
