A man writes $1$ on the first line of his paper, then writes $2$ and $3$ on the second line, $4$, $5$ and $6$ on the third, and continues so that on line $n$ he writes the $n$ integers following the last integer on line $n-1$. What is the sum of the first and last integers on line $17$?
