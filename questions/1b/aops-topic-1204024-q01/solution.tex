The frame takes $10$ cm from each side, so the mirror is $(80-20)$ by $(60-20)$, i.e. $60$ by $40$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
filldraw((0,0)--(80,0)--(80,60)--(0,60)--cycle,lightgray,black); filldraw((10,10)--(70,10)--(70,50)--(10,50)--cycle,lightblue,black);
label("$80$",(40,0),S); label("$60$",(0,30),W); label("$10$",(75,30)); label("mirror",(40,30));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

its area is $\boxed{2400}$ square centimetres.
