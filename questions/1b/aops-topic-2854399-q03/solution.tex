$2x^2+5x+2=(2x+1)(x+2)$, so the intercepts are $-2$ and $-\dfrac12$; the greater is $\left(-\dfrac12,0\right)$. At $x=7$, $y=98+35+2=135$. The distance is
\[\sqrt{\left(\dfrac{15}2\right)^2+135^2}=\dfrac{15}{2}\sqrt{1+18^2}=\dfrac{15\sqrt{325}}2=\boxed{\dfrac{75\sqrt{13}}{2}}.\]
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;real f(real x){return (2x^2+5x+2)/20;}draw(graph(f,-3,7.2));draw((-3.5,0)--(8,0),gray);draw((0,-0.5)--(0,7.5),gray);draw((7,0)--(7,7.5),dotted);pair Q=(7,f(7)),R=(-0.5,0);draw(R--Q,dashed);dot(Q);dot(R);label("$Q$",Q,E);label("$\left(-\dfrac12,0\right)$",R,S);label("$x=7$",(7,7.5),N);
\end{asy}
