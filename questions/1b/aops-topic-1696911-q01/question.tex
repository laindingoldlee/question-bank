In square $ABCD$ (vertices in counterclockwise order), $P$ is a point inside the square such that $AP=\sqrt5$, $BP=\sqrt2$ and $CP=1$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find $\angle BPC$.
\item[(b)] Find the side length of the square.
\end{enumerate}
