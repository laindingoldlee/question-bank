Michael has ten cards labelled with the numbers $1,2,\dots,10$. He shuffles them, lays them face down in a row in random order, and turns over the leftmost card. Whenever he turns over a card with the number $n$ on it, he goes to the $n$th card from the left: if that card is face down, he turns it over and continues in the same way; otherwise he stops. What is the expected number of cards still face down when he stops?

$\textbf{(A) } \dfrac{7}{2} \qquad \textbf{(B) } 4 \qquad \textbf{(C) } \dfrac{9}{2} \qquad \textbf{(D) } 5 \qquad \textbf{(E) } \dfrac{11}{2}$
