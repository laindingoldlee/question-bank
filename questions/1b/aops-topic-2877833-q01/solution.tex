The step $\sqrt{(-1)(-1)}=\sqrt{-1}\cdot\sqrt{-1}$ is invalid. The rule $\sqrt{ab}=\sqrt a\sqrt b$ holds for $a,b\ge0$ but not for negative $a,b$ with principal square roots. Complex arithmetic (adding and multiplying) is fine; only this square-root rule fails.
