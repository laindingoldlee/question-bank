Let the Florentine statue be $f$ years old, so the Roman statue is $3f$ years old. In $100$ years:
\[
3f+100=2(f+100)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad f=100 .
\]
So the Roman statue is $3f=\boxed{300}$ years old. Check: in $100$ years the ages are $400$ and $200$. (An answer of $-100$ comes from forgetting to add $100$ to both ages.)
