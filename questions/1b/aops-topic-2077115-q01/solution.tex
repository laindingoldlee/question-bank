The ratio of consecutive known terms is \(\dfrac{1}{8}\div\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{1}{2}\), so the common ratio is \(\dfrac{1}{2}\). Then \(x=1\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{1}{2}\), and indeed \(\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{1}{4}\). So \(x=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}\).
