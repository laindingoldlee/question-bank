Write the quantities in the 12-,5-,8-litre containers in that order. A valid sequence is $(12,0,0)\to(4,0,8)\to(4,5,3)\to(9,0,3)\to(9,3,0)\to(1,3,8)\to(1,5,6)\to(6,0,6)$. Each arrow pours from one container into another until one empties or the other fills. The final quantities give the two requested portions.
