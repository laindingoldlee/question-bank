A palindrome with $2n$ digits is determined by its first $n$ digits, where the first digit is $1$--$9$ and the others are $0$--$9$. So $p(2n)=9\cdot10^{n-1}$, and
\[\prod_{n=2}^{50}p(2n)=9^{49}\cdot10^{1+2+\cdots+49}=9^{49}\cdot10^{1225}.\]
Since $9^a\cdot10^b=3^{2a}2^b5^b$, the exponents $a$ and $b$ are determined by the prime factorisation: $a=49$ and $b=1225$. So $a+b=\boxed{1274}$.
