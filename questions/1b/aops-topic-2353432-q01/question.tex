For a positive integer $n$, let $p(n)$ be the number of palindromes with $n$ digits (a palindrome reads the same forwards and backwards, and its first digit is not $0$). If
\[\prod_{n=2}^{50}p(2n)=9^a\cdot10^b\]
for nonnegative integers $a$ and $b$, find $a+b$.
