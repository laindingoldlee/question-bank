The square has side 8 and diagonal $8\sqrt2$, so the outer circle has radius $4\sqrt2$. Its area outside the square is $32\pi-64$. Add the inner circle\textquotesingle s area $16\pi$ to obtain $\boxed{48\pi-64}$.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

\end{asy}
