Heron gives $[ABC]=6\sqrt6$. Use signed distances to the three side lines, positive on the interior side. They obey $5d_{AB}+6d_{AC}+7d_{BC}=2[ABC]=12\sqrt6$, by signed area decomposition. The four intersections of the offset lines have $d_{AB}=\pm2$ and $d_{AC}=\pm3$, so their distances to $BC$ are $\dfrac{|12\sqrt6\pm10\pm18|}{7}$. Since $12\sqrt6>28$, all four numerators are positive. Summing cancels the signed terms and gives $\boxed{\dfrac{48\sqrt6}{7}}$.

\end{asy}
