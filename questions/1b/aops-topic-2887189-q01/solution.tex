By definition $a^{-t}=\dfrac1{a^t}$, and $\dfrac1{a^t}=\left(\dfrac1a\right)^t$ since $a^t\left(\dfrac1a\right)^t=\left(a\cdot\dfrac1a\right)^t=1$. With $a=\dfrac32$ and $\dfrac1a=\dfrac23$, $\left(\dfrac32\right)^{-\dfrac32}=\left(\dfrac23\right)^{\dfrac32}$. $\blacksquare$
