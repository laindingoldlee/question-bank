Substitute $y=9-x$, obtaining $3x^2+4x(9-x)=128$, or $x^2-36x+128=0$. Factoring gives $(x-4)(x-32)=0$. Both yield integer partners: $(x,y)=(4,5)$ and $(32,-23)$. Thus $\boxed{x\in\{4,32\}}$.
