A term comes from choosing $n^5$ from $a$ factors, $n$ from $b$ and $1$ from $c$, with $a+b+c=9$ and $5a+b=36$. Then $b=36-5a\le9-a$ forces $a\ge7$, and $a\le7$ (as $5\cdot8>36$), so $a=7$, $b=1$, $c=1$. The coefficient is $\dfrac{9!}{7!\,1!\,1!}=\boxed{72}$.
