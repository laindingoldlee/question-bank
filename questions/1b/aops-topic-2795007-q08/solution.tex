Consider where the five odd numbers go. In a row of three cells, at most two can hold odd numbers, and two only if they are the two end cells. Five odd numbers in three rows need row counts $2,2,1$ in some order. Two rows with two odd numbers each cannot be adjacent (their end cells would be vertically adjacent), so the top and bottom rows each contain odd numbers in both corners, and the middle row contains exactly one odd number, which cannot be in an end column (it would lie below a corner). So the odd numbers occupy exactly the four corners and the centre, and this placement is valid.

So the odd numbers fill the corners and centre in $5!$ ways and the evens fill the other cells in $4!$ ways: $120\cdot24=\boxed{2880}$.
