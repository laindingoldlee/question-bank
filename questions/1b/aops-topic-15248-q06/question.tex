Walter has $5$ bottles of juice; each bottle contains either cranberry juice or orange juice, and he has at least two bottles of each kind. Every bottle contains at least $12$ oz and at most $20$ oz of juice, and altogether he has $80$ oz. One of his orange-juice bottles contains $16$ oz. What is the largest possible ratio of the total amount of cranberry juice to the total amount of orange juice? Express your answer as a common fraction.
