Let the largest integer be $N\ge10$. The product is divisible by $N^2$ exactly when $N\mid(N-1)(N-2)\cdots(N-9)$. Modulo $N$, this product is $(-1)^9 9!$, so the condition is simply $N\mid9!$. Now $9!=2^7 3^4 5\cdot7$ has $8\cdot5\cdot2\cdot2=160$ positive divisors. All integers $1$ through $9$ are among them and must be excluded. Hence the number of sets is $\boxed{151}$.
