The first term is $a_1=2$ and the common difference is $3$, so $a_n=2+3(n-1)$.
\[
a_{20}=2+3\cdot19=\boxed{59}.
\]
The $14$th term is $a_{14}=2+3\cdot13=41$, and the sum of the first $14$ terms is the number of terms times the average of the first and last terms:
\[
S_{14}=\dfrac{14\,(2+41)}{2}=7\cdot43=\boxed{301}.
\]
