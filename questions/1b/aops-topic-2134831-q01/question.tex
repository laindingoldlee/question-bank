A $3\times4\times5$ rectangular prism is built from $60$ unit cubes. Counting the whole prism and the $60$ unit cubes themselves, how many rectangular prisms are formed by the unit cubes inside it (that is, how many rectangular prisms are unions of some of the $60$ cubes)?
