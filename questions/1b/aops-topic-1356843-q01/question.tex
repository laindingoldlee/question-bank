The \emph{dihedral angle} along an edge of a polyhedron is the angle between the two faces that meet at that edge, measured in a plane perpendicular to the edge. Find the cosine of the dihedral angle of a regular tetrahedron, and the angle to the nearest tenth of a degree. (For comparison, the dihedral angle of a cube is $90^\circ$.)
