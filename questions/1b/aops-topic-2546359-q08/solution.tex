$4=2^2$, $27=3^3$, $25=5^2$, $343=7^3$: the consecutive primes are alternately squared and cubed. The next primes are $11$, $13$, $17$, so the next three terms are
\[11^2=121,\qquad13^3=2197,\qquad17^2=289,\]
i.e. $\boxed{121,\ 2197,\ 289}$.
