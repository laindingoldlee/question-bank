There are $100^3$ ordered positive triples before the triangle restrictions. A failure such as $x+y\le z$ can occur for at most one designated largest coordinate, so the three failure types are disjoint. For fixed $z$, the number of positive pairs with $x+y\le z$ is $\binom z2$. Hence the valid count is $100^3-3\sum_{z=1}^{100}\binom z2=1000000-3\binom{101}3=\boxed{500050}$.
