Consider one coin. On lap $1$ it is flipped with probability $1$, so after lap $1$ it is tails. On lap $2$ it is flipped with probability $\dfrac12$, so after lap $2$ it is heads with probability $\dfrac12$.

Suppose that after some lap $k-1\ge2$ the coin is heads with probability $\dfrac12$. On lap $k$, independently of its state, it is flipped with probability $\dfrac1k$, so after lap $k$ it is heads with probability
\[\dfrac12\left(1-\dfrac1k\right)+\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac1k=\dfrac12.\]
By induction, after lap $2019$ each coin is heads with probability $\dfrac12$. By linearity of expectation, the expected number of heads is
\[2018\cdot\dfrac12=\boxed{1009}.\]
