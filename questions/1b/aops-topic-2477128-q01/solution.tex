For $y>0$, $\log y$ is by definition the exponent $t$ with $10^t=y$. Hence $10^{\log y}=y$ for every $y>0$, and $\log y$ is undefined for $y\le0$. With $y=3a-1$:
\[10^{\log(3a-1)}=\boxed{3a-1},\qquad\text{defined exactly when }3a-1>0,\ \text{i.e. }a>\dfrac13.\]
