By Vieta's formulas, $x_1+x_2+x_3+x_4=0$ (there is no $x^3$ term) and $x_1x_2x_3x_4=-2009$, so $x_3x_4=-\dfrac{2009}{49}=-41$. Let $s=x_1+x_2$; then $x_3+x_4=-s$, and the polynomial factors as
\[\left(x^2-sx+49\right)\left(x^2+sx-41\right)=x^4+\left(8-s^2\right)x^2+90s\,x-2009 .\]
(The $x^3$ terms cancel, the $x$ terms give $41s+49s=90s$, and the $x^2$ terms give $-41-s^2+49$.) Comparing coefficients of $x$: $90s=90$, so $s=1$. Then
\[k=8-s^2=\boxed{7}.\]
(Check: $\left(x^2-x+49\right)\left(x^2+x-41\right)=x^4+7x^2+90x-2009$.)
