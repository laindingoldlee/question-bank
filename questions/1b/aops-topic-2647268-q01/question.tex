Let $n$ be a positive integer. There are $\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$ marks, each with a black side and a white side, arranged in an equilateral triangle with rows of $1,2,\dots,n$ marks. Initially every mark shows its black side. An operation consists of choosing a line parallel to one of the sides of the triangle and flipping every mark on that line. A configuration is \emph{admissible} if it can be obtained from the initial one by finitely many operations; for an admissible configuration $C$, let $f(C)$ be the least number of operations needed to obtain $C$. Find the maximum value of $f(C)$ over all admissible configurations $C$.
