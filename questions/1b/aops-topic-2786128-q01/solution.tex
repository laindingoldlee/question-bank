Take an equilateral triangle $ABC$ with side $2$ and drop the altitude $AD$. By symmetry $D$ is the midpoint of $BC$ and $AD$ bisects $\angle A$, so triangle $ABD$ has angles $30^\circ$ at $A$, $60^\circ$ at $B$ and $90^\circ$ at $D$, with $AB=2$ and $BD=1$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0),C=(2,0),A=(1,sqrt(3)),D=(1,0);
draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--D,dashed);draw((1,0)--(0.88,0)--(0.88,0.12)--(1,0.12));
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,S);
label("$2$",(A+B)/2,NW);label("$1$",(B+D)/2,S);label("$30^\circ$",A+(-0.17,-0.62),fontsize(8pt));label("$60^\circ$",B+(0.33,0.13));
\end{asy}

In the right triangle $ABD$, the side opposite the $30^\circ$ angle is $BD$, so
\[\sin30^\circ=\dfrac{BD}{AB}=\boxed{\dfrac12}.\]
