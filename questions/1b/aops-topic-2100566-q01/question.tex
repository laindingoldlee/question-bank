A tree grows at a rate of $1$ centimetre per day, and while it is raining it grows faster, at $1\dfrac{1}{2}$ centimetres per day. The tree is $0$ centimetres tall at midnight at the start of March $1$ and grows throughout March, which has $31$ days. It rains for $5\%$ of March. How tall is the tree at the end of March?
