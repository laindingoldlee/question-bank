In triangle $ABC$,\[
AB = 13, \qquad BC = 14, \qquad CA = 15.
\]Point $D$ lies on $\overline{BC}$ such that $\overline{AD}$ bisects $\angle BAC$. Point $E$ lies on $\overline{AC}$ such that $AE : EC = 13 : 14$. Lines $AD$ and $BE$ intersect at $P$. The line $AP$ intersects the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$ again at $X$.

The length of segment $AX$ can be written in the form $m\sqrt{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $m+n$.
