The side inequality implies $60<x<90$. Since $AE\perp BC$, $\angle CAE=90^\circ-x^\circ$. Also $\angle CAD=\dfrac{x}{2}{}^\circ$. Because $x>60$, the angle between these rays is $\angle DAE=\left(\dfrac{3x}{2}-90\right)^\circ$. Thus $a=\dfrac{3}{2}$, $b=90$, and $\boxed{ab=135}$.
