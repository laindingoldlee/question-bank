An ant that left $2k$ seconds earlier has a lead equal to $\dfrac{2k}{100}$ of the route. Victor gains at rate $\dfrac{1}{65}-\dfrac{1}{100}=\dfrac{7}{1300}$ routes per second. Catch time is therefore $\dfrac{26k}{7}$ seconds. This is below $65$ precisely when $k<17.5$, so $k=1,\ldots,17$ are passed. Later ants never get ahead, and earlier ones have already arrived or arrive before he can catch them. Thus $\boxed{17}$.
