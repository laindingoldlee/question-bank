Count ordered triples $(a,b,c)$ with $1\le a,b,c\le6$ and $a+b+c=11$. For each first die $a$, the other two must sum to $11-a$; the number of ways two dice make a sum $s$ is $6-|s-7|$:
\[a=1,\dots,6:\quad s=10,9,8,7,6,5\ \text{give}\ 3+4+5+6+5+4=27.\]
The probability is $\dfrac{27}{216}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{8}}$.
