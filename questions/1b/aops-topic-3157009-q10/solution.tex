AM--GM gives $ 5x+3y\ge2\sqrt{15xy}=20\sqrt{15}$. Equality occurs when $ 5x=3y $, with $ x=\sqrt{60}$ and $ y=\sqrt{\dfrac{500}{3}}$, both positive and satisfying the product. Thus the minimum is $\boxed{20\sqrt{15}}$.
