Pairing each divisor $d$ with $\dfrac{2520}{d}$ gives reciprocal sum $\dfrac{\sigma(2520)}{2520}$. Since $2520=2^33^25\cdot7$, its divisor sum is $15\cdot13\cdot6\cdot8=9360$. Thus the fraction is $\dfrac{26}{7}$ and $\boxed{a+b=33}$.
