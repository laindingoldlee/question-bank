The radius $OQ$ is perpendicular to the tangent $PQ$, so $OQ^2=PO^2-PQ^2=169-64=105$. Thus $\boxed{r=\sqrt{105}}$. 
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(220);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
real r=sqrt(105);pair O=(0,0),P=(13,0),Q=(105/13,8*sqrt(105)/13);draw(circle(O,r));draw(O--P--Q--O);label("$O$",O,(-1,0));label("$P$",P,(1,0));label("$Q$",Q,(0,1));
\end{asy}
