Positive real numbers $a,b,c$ satisfy
\[\dfrac{\sqrt a}{a}+\dfrac{\sqrt b}{b}+\dfrac{\sqrt c}{c}=10.\]
Find the minimum or the maximum, whichever exists, of $3\left(\dfrac1a+\dfrac1b+\dfrac1c\right)$.
