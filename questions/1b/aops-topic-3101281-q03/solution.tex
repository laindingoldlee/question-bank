Fix Alice's slot. Bob occupies each of the other $255$ slots with equal probability. They meet in round $r$ when Bob lies in the opposite half of Alice's $2^r$-slot sub-bracket, giving $2^{r-1}$ possibilities. Alice then plays $r-1$ earlier matches. Therefore
\[E=\dfrac{\sum_{j=0}^7j2^j}{255}=\dfrac{0+2+8+24+64+160+384+896}{255}=\boxed{\dfrac{1538}{255}}.\]
