Relative to the passenger, the faster train moves at $63-49=14$ miles per hour, which is
\[14\cdot\dfrac{5280}{3600}=\dfrac{308}{15}\ \text{feet per second}.\]
In $4.5$ seconds the whole length of the train passes him, so the train is $\dfrac{308}{15}\cdot4.5=\boxed{92.4}$ feet long.
