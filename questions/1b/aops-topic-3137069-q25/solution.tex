Choose the digits, whose maximum is necessarily the peak. If 0 is absent, choose five of 1 through 9, then assign each of the four nonmaximum digits to the increasing or decreasing side. Both sides must be nonempty, giving $\binom95(2^4-2)=1764 $. If 0 is present, it must lie on the decreasing side, since otherwise it is a leading zero. Choose four nonzero digits; among the remaining three nonmaximum positive digits, at least one must lie left of the peak. This gives $\binom94(2^3-1)=882 $. Each assignment fixes the ordering uniquely, for a total $\boxed{2646}$.
