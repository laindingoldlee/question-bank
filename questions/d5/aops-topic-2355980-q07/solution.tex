Number the places $1$ (front) to $5$ (back). Bob is in place $3$. Caroline is behind Bob, so she is in place $4$ or $5$, and Alex is behind Caroline, so Caroline is in place $4$ and Alex in place $5$. Then Debby and Eddie take places $1$ and $2$, and since Eddie is behind Debby, $\boxed{\text{Debby}}$ is first. (The order is Debby, Eddie, Bob, Caroline, Alex; it is the same whether ``behind'' means directly behind or anywhere behind.)
