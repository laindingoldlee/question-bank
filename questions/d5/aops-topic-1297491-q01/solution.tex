The sum is odd, so the set contains $2$ (a sum of four odd primes is even). The other three are distinct odd primes adding to $43$. Listing by the smallest:
\[
3+11+29,\ 3+17+23,\ 5+7+31,\ 7+13+23,\ 7+17+19,\ 11+13+19 .
\]
(For example, with smallest $3$ we need two primes adding to $40$: $11+29$, $17+23$; with $5$, two adding to $38$: $7+31$; with $7$, two greater than $7$ adding to $36$: $13+23$, $17+19$; with $11$, two adding to $32$: $13+19$; with $13$ or more, none.) There are $\boxed{6}$ sets.
