A school has $1000$ lockers and $1000$ students. Student $1$ opens every locker; then, for $k=2,\dots,1000$, student $k$ toggles every $k$th locker (opening it if closed, closing it if open). Afterwards the principal closes every locker left open. How many lockers does she close?
