In $\triangle ABC$, points $D$, $E$, $F$ lie on sides $\overline{BC}$, $\overline{AC}$, $\overline{AB}$ respectively, and the segments $\overline{AD}$, $\overline{BE}$, $\overline{CF}$ pass through a common point. Suppose that $\angle ADE=\angle ADF$, $AD=12$, $AB=14$ and $AC=17$. The area of $\triangle ABC$ can be written as $\sqrt a+\sqrt b$ for positive integers $a$ and $b$. Find $a+b$.
