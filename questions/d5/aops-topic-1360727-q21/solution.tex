An assortment is determined by the numbers $c,o,p\ge0$ of each kind with $c+o+p=6$ (cookies of one kind are indistinguishable, and there are enough of each). Represent it as a row of $6$ stars and $2$ bars, the bars separating the three kinds; for example $**|*|***$ means $c=2$, $o=1$, $p=3$. Each arrangement of $8$ symbols with $2$ bars gives exactly one assortment, so the number is
\[
\dbinom{8}{2}=28 .
\]
The formula with $n=3$ kinds and $k=6$ cookies gives $\dbinom{3+6-1}{6}=\dbinom{8}{6}=28$ as well, since $\dbinom{8}{6}=\dbinom{8}{2}$: choosing the $6$ positions of the stars is the same as choosing the $2$ positions of the bars. The student's set-up is right; only the evaluation went wrong. The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(D) }28}$.
