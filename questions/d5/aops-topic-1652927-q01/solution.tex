Let Lila be $L$ years old now, so Sophia is $L+8$. Twenty years ago:
\[
L+8-20=3(L-20)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad L-12=3L-60\quad\Longrightarrow\quad L=24 .
\]
So $\boxed{\text{Lila is }24\text{ and Sophia is }32}$. Check: twenty years ago they were $4$ and $12$, and $12=3\cdot4$.
