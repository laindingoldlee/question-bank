The Rockets had $6995$ possessions during the first $70$ basketball games of the season. With those possessions, the team made $1019$ baskets worth three points each, $1797$ baskets worth two points each and $1421$ baskets worth one point each. If only one, two or three points are awarded for making baskets, on average, during those $70$ games, how many points did the Rockets score per $100$ possessions? Express your answer as a decimal to the nearest thousandth.
