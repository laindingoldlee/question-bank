A point $A$ lies outside a circle $\omega$ with centre $O$ and radius greater than $1$. Line $AO$ meets $\omega$ at $B$, the intersection point nearer to $A$, and $AB=1$. A line through $A$ meets $\omega$ at $C$ and $D$, with $C$ nearer to $A$, such that $CD=2$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of $CD$, and suppose $OM=1$. The tangent from $A$ to $\omega$ touches $\omega$ at $E$. Find $AE^2$.

$\textbf{(A) } 2\sqrt2+1 \qquad \textbf{(B) } 2\sqrt3+1 \qquad \textbf{(C) } \sqrt3+1 \qquad \textbf{(D) } 6 \qquad \textbf{(E) } 5+2\sqrt2$
