By Heron's formula with $s=\dfrac{11+15+16}{2}=21$, the area is $\sqrt{21\cdot10\cdot6\cdot5}=\sqrt{6300}=30\sqrt7$. The altitude to the side $15$ is $h=\dfrac{2\cdot30\sqrt7}{15}=\boxed{4\sqrt7}$ inches.

\begin{asy}

draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.8));draw(A--H,dashed);draw((3,0.7)--(3.7,0.7)--(3.7,0));
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$H$",H,S);
label("$11$",(A+B)/2,W,fontsize(8pt));label("$16$",(A+C)/2,NE,fontsize(8pt));label("$15$",(9,0),S,fontsize(8pt));label("$h$",(3,5.3),E,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}
