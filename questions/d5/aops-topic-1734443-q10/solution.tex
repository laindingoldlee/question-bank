Let the edge be $s$. The foot of the height is the centre of the base triangle, at distance $\dfrac{s}{\sqrt3}$ from each base vertex, so by Pythagoras the height is
\[
h=\sqrt{s^2-\dfrac{s^2}{3}}=s\sqrt{\dfrac{2}{3}}.
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=3*sqrt(2),h=2*sqrt(3);
pair pr(real x,real y,real z){return (x+0.6*y,0.3*y+z);}
pair P1=pr(0,0,0),P2=pr(s,0,0),P3=pr(s/2,s*sqrt(3)/2,0),G=pr(s/2,s*sqrt(3)/6,h),O=pr(s/2,s*sqrt(3)/6,0);
draw(P1--P3,dashed);draw(P1--P2--P3,linewidth(0.9));draw(P1--G--P2,linewidth(0.9));draw(P3--G,linewidth(0.9));
draw(G--O,blue+dashed);dot(O,blue);
label("$h$",(G+O)/2,E,blue);label("$s$",(P1+P2)/2,S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

With $h=2\sqrt3$ we get $s=2\sqrt3\cdot\sqrt{\dfrac{3}{2}}=3\sqrt2$. The volume is
\[
V=\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}s^2\cdot h=\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}\cdot18\cdot2\sqrt3=\boxed{9}.
\]
