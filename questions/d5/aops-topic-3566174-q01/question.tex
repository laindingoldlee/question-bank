A full jar initially contains coffee. A fraction $f$ is removed and replaced by cocoa. After mixing thoroughly, the same fraction is removed and replaced by cocoa again. The final coffee:cocoa ratio is $16:9$. Find the ratio of the amount of cocoa after the first replacement to that after the second.
