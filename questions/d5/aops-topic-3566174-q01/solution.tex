The remaining coffee fraction is $(1-f)^2=\dfrac{16}{25}$. Since $0\le f\le1$, $1-f=\dfrac{4}{5}$ and $f=\dfrac{1}{5}$. Cocoa occupies fractions $\dfrac{1}{5}$ and $\dfrac{9}{25}$ after the two steps. Their ratio is $\boxed{5:9}$.
