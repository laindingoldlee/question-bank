Sort all ten rows, then sort column $6$. Let $x$ be the entry in row $5$, column $6$ afterward. Five of the original sixth-column entries are at most $x$. Each belonged to a sorted row with at least six entries at most that entry, giving at least $5\cdot 6=30$ numbers at most $x$. Thus $x\ge 30$. Six original sixth-column entries are at least $x$, each with five entries in its row at least that large. Hence at least $6\cdot 5=30$ numbers are at least $x$, so $101-x\ge 30$ and $x\le 71$. Sorting the column changes locations but not these counts.
