The numbers 1 through 100 are placed arbitrarily in a 10-by-10 array. You may request eleven operations, each sorting a row increasingly left to right or a column increasingly top to bottom. You cannot see the array. Give a strategy and a cell guaranteed to contain a number from 30 through 71 inclusive.
