No digit can be zero. A one-digit number cannot have product $ 24 $. The possible two-digit multisets are $\{3,8\}$ and $\{4,6\}$, with sums $ 11,10 $, so neither works. The three-digit multisets with product $ 24 $ are $\{1,3,8\},\{1,4,6\},\{2,2,6\},\{2,3,4\}$. Only the first and last have sums divisible by $ 3 $. Each has three distinct digits and therefore $ 3!=6 $ permutations, giving $\boxed{12}$.
