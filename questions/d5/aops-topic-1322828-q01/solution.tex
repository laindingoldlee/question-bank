\textbf{Case 1: $12$ is not chosen.} Then we choose $5$ non-adjacent numbers from the line $1,\dots,11$. Choosing $k$ non-adjacent items from $n$ in a row can be done in $\dbinom{n-k+1}{k}$ ways, so this gives $\dbinom75=21$.

\textbf{Case 2: $12$ is chosen.} Then $1$ and $11$ are excluded, and we choose $4$ non-adjacent numbers from $2,\dots,10$ ($9$ in a row): $\dbinom64=15$ ways.

The total is $21+15=\boxed{36}$, answer (B).
