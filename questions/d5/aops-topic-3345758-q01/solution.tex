For $0\le a\le500$, the winning interval is $0\le b\le\sqrt{a+500}$, with increasing length. For $500\le a\le9500$, its length is $\sqrt{a+500}-\sqrt{a-500}=\dfrac{1000}{\sqrt{a+500}+\sqrt{a-500}}$, which decreases with $a$. For $a\ge9500$, the length is $100-\sqrt{a-500}$, also decreasing. Hence the maximum occurs at $\boxed{a=500}$. The probability is $\dfrac{\sqrt{1000}}{100}=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt{10}}{10}}$.
