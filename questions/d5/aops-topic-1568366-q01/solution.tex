Let $x$ dollars be invested at $8\%$. Then $x+1500$ dollars are invested at $9\%$ and $3x-500$ dollars at $10\%$, and
\[
0.08x+0.09(x+1500)+0.10(3x-500)=1965 .
\]
This gives $0.47x+135-50=1965$, so $0.47x=1880$ and $x=4000$. Hence the amounts are
\[
\boxed{\$4000\text{ at }8\%,\quad \$5500\text{ at }9\%,\quad \$11500\text{ at }10\%}.
\]
Check: $320+495+1150=1965$.
