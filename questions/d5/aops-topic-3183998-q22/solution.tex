The required interval is $\dfrac{711}{1000}\le\dfrac{m}{n}<\dfrac{712}{1000}$, lying strictly between $\dfrac{27}{38}$ and $\dfrac{5}{7}$. For any fraction strictly between these latter two, the positive integers $38m-27n$ and $5n-7m$ are at least one. The identity $n=7(38m-27n)+38(5n-7m)$ then gives $n\ge45$. This bound is attained by $\dfrac{32}{45}=0.71111\ldots$. Thus $\boxed{45}$.
