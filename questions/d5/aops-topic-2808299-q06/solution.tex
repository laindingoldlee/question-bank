By the sine rule, $\dfrac{x}{\sin(\dfrac{\pi x}{180})}=\dfrac{y}{\sin(\dfrac{\pi y}{180})}=\dfrac{z}{\sin(\dfrac{\pi z}{180})}$. The function $\dfrac{t}{\sin t}$ is strictly increasing for $0<t<\pi$: its derivative has numerator $\sin t-t\cos t$, whose derivative is $t\sin t>0$ and whose limit at 0 is 0. Thus $x=y=z$. The angles sum to 180, giving sides 60. The area is $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}60^2=\boxed{900\sqrt3}$.
