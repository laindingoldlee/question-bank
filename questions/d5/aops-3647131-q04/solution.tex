Divisibility of the six-digit decimal string by three requires three ones, because the leading digit is one and the last digit zero. Binary divisibility by three requires the alternating sum of its bits to be zero modulo three. With exactly three ones, all must occupy positions of the same parity. The leading and final digits select $101010$. Its base-six value is $6^5+6^3+6=\boxed{7998}$.
