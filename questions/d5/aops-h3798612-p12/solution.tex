The equality $\angle BAC=\angle BCA=\theta$ gives $AB=BC=8$. In an isosceles triangle, the median from the apex also bisects its angle, so $\angle MBC=90^\circ-\theta$. Since this equals $\theta$, $\theta=45^\circ$ and $\angle B=90^\circ$. The hypotenuse is $8\sqrt2$ and its midpoint is at half that distance from the right-angle vertex. Thus $\boxed{BM=4\sqrt2}$.
