Divisibility by 9 requires digit sum a multiple of 9. Divisibility by 11 requires the difference of sums in alternating positions to be a multiple of 11. For at most 8 digits, the only possible positive digit sum is 9; the alternating difference is odd and has absolute value at most 9, so cannot be a multiple of 11. For 9 digits the only digit sums are 9 (all 1 s) or 18 (all 2 s); their alternating differences are 1 and 2, respectively. For 10 digits, the digit sum must be 18, so there are eight 2 s and two 1 s. The alternating sums must both be 9, forcing one 1 in an odd position and one in an even position. To minimize the number, put them in the first two positions. The answer is $\boxed{1122222222}$, whose digit sum is 18 and alternating sum is 0.
