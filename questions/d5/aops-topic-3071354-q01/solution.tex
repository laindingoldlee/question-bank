Substituting $x=0$ gives $B=D$. Substituting $x=1$ then gives $A+B=C+D$, hence $A=C$. In the example the equality holds only at $x=\dfrac{3}{5}$, not for every real $x$. An identity and an equation with one particular solution have different hypotheses.
