Someone remembers the following ``theorem'': if $x^n-y^n\equiv0\pmod{p^k}$, then $n\equiv0\pmod{p^k}$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that this statement is false.
\item[(b)] Prove the following correct statement, a basic case of the \emph{lifting the exponent lemma}: let $p$ be a prime and let $x\ne y$ be integers with $p\mid x-y$ and $p\nmid x$. Then for every positive integer $n$,
\[p\ \Big|\ \dfrac{x^n-y^n}{x-y}\quad\text{if and only if}\quad p\mid n.\]
\end{enumerate}
