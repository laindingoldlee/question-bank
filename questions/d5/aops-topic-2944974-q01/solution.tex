Expanding gives $3a_n-6a_{n+1}=a_na_{n+1}$. Dividing by $a_na_{n+1}$ (the terms are nonzero, as the formula below shows), with $b_n=\dfrac1{a_n}$ we get $3b_{n+1}-6b_n=1$, i.e. $b_{n+1}+\dfrac13=2\left(b_n+\dfrac13\right)$. Since $b_0=\dfrac13$, $b_n=\dfrac{2^{n+1}-1}3$. Then
\[\sum_{i=0}^{2022}b_i=\dfrac{(2^{2024}-2)-2023}3=\dfrac{2^{2024}-2025}3=\dfrac{2^{2024}-2022}3-1,\]
so $x=\boxed{2024}$.
