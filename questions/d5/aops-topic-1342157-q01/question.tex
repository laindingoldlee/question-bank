Let $a=1^3+2^3+\dots+n^3$, $b=1^5+2^5+\dots+n^5$ and $c=1^7+2^7+\dots+n^7$. Prove that
\[
(a+6b+3c)^2+(7a+13b+4c)^2=(7a+14b+5c)^2 .
\]
You may use the formulas $a=T^2$, $b=\dfrac{T^2(4T-1)}{3}$ and $c=\dfrac{T^2\left(6T^2-4T+1\right)}{3}$, where $T=\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$.
