Coins of $1$, $6$ and $36$ cents exist. Josh has $99$ coins of $36$ cents. He may exchange a $36$-cent coin for six $6$-cent coins, or a $6$-cent coin for six $1$-cent coins. Can he ever have exactly $500$ coins?
