We have
\[
\overline{abba}=1000a+100b+10b+a=1001a+110b=11(91a+10b),
\]
so it is divisible by the prime $11$, and $11$ must be one of the three consecutive primes. The candidates are
\[
5\cdot7\cdot11=385,\qquad 7\cdot11\cdot13=1001,\qquad 11\cdot13\cdot17=2431 .
\]
Only $1001$ is a four-digit number of the form $\overline{abba}$ (with $a=1$, $b=0$). So the number is $\boxed{1001}$.
