A person counts 12 initially visible sheep at a constant rate of 4 per hour. At each completed hour, two new uncounted sheep appear if he has not already finished. He stops immediately upon counting the last currently uncounted sheep; completion takes precedence over a simultaneous arrival. When does he stop?
