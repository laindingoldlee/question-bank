Bob has $60$ apricots and throws all of them at his neighbour's house, one handful at a time. Each handful contains $1$, $2$ or $3$ apricots, and he takes exactly $24$ handfuls. He writes down the number of apricots in each handful, in order (so $3,1,2$ is different from $1,3,2$). How many different lists could he have written down?
