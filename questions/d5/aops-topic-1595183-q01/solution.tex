The perfect squares in the range are $1^2,2^2,\dots,k^2$ with $k^2\le2013^6$, i.e.\ $k\le2013^3$. So $S=2013^3$.

The perfect cubes are $1^3,2^3,\dots,k^3$ with $k^3\le2013^6$, i.e.\ $k\le2013^2$. So $Q=2013^2$.

Hence
\[
\boxed{S=2013\,Q}.
\]
