The opposite side has midpoint $\left(\dfrac{4+10}{2},\dfrac{2+8}{2}\right)=(7,5)$. The slope from $(3,10)$ to $(7,5)$ is $\dfrac{5-10}{7-3}=-\dfrac{5}{4}$, so the line is $y-10=-\dfrac{5}{4}(x-3)$, i.e.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(3,10),B=(4,2),C=(10,8),M=(7,5); draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); draw((1,12.5)--(11,0),blue);
dot(M); label("$(3,10)$",A,NW); label("$(4,2)$",B,SW); label("$(10,8)$",C,E); label("$(7,5)$",M,SE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
