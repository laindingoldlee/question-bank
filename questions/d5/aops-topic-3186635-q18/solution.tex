Vieta gives $r+s+t=11$, $rs+st+tr=36$, and $rst=36$, so $\dfrac{1}{r}+\dfrac{1}{s}+\dfrac{1}{t}=1$. Also $r^2+s^2+t^2=121-72=49$. The requested expression is $(r^2+s^2+t^2)(\dfrac{1}{r}+\dfrac{1}{s}+\dfrac{1}{t})-(r+s+t)=49-11=\boxed{38}$.
