Trailing zeros count factors of $10=2\cdot5$. Each factorial has at least as many factors of $2$ as of $5$, so count only factors of $5$. They number $0 + 1 + \lfloor \dfrac{719}{5} \rfloor + \lfloor \dfrac{719}{25} \rfloor + \lfloor \dfrac{719}{125} \rfloor + \lfloor \dfrac{719}{625} \rfloor = 1 + 143 + 28 + 5 + 1 = \boxed{178}$. Each multiple of $25$ contributes its second factor of $5$, and similarly for the higher powers.
