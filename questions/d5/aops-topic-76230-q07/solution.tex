If two of the numbers differ by $2$ or more, say $a\ge b+2$, replacing them by $a-1$ and $b+1$ keeps the sum and increases the product, because $(a-1)(b+1)=ab+a-b-1>ab$. So in a maximal choice any two numbers differ by at most $1$. Since $92=5\cdot18+2$, the numbers are $18,18,18,19,19$, and the greatest product is
\[
18^3\cdot19^2=5832\cdot361=\boxed{2105352}.
\]
(The answer $2046528$ suggested in the post is wrong.)
