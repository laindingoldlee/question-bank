On a $6\times6$ board, six white pawns start in row 1 and six black pawns in row 6, one per column. White starts; players alternate moving one pawn forward one cell toward the other side. Pawns cannot capture or enter occupied cells. The game ends when the active player has no move. How many terminal positions are possible?
