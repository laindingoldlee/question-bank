Write $n=\prod p^{e_p}$. It is a square, a cube and a fourth power exactly when every exponent $e_p$ is divisible by $2$, $3$ and $4$, that is, by $\operatorname{lcm}(2,3,4)=12$. So $n$ is a twelfth power, and the smallest one greater than $1$ is
\[
2^{12}=\boxed{4096}.
\]
