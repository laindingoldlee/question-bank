Expanding the product of six factors $(x+y+z)$, the term $x^2y^2z^2$ arises by choosing $x$ from $2$ of the factors and $y$ from $2$ of the remaining $4$; the last $2$ give $z$. The coefficient is
\[\binom62\binom42=15\cdot6=\dfrac{6!}{2!\,2!\,2!}=\boxed{90}.\]
