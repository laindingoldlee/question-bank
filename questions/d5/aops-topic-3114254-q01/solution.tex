The water cone is similar to the full cone with scale factor $\dfrac{7.5}{10}=\dfrac{3}{4}$. Its radius is $4\cdot\dfrac{3}{4}=3$ cm, not $6$ cm: $6$ is its diameter. Therefore $V=\dfrac{1}{3}\pi\cdot3^2\cdot7.5=22.5\pi\approx70.69$, giving $\boxed{71\text{ cm}^3}$.

\end{asy}
