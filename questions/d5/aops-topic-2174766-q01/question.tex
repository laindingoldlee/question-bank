Find constants $A$ and $B$ such that
\[\dfrac{x+7}{x^2-2x-35}=\dfrac{A}{x-7}+\dfrac{B}{x+5}\]
for all $x\ne7,-5$. A student multiplies both sides by $x^2-2x-35$ to get $x+7=A(x+5)+B(x-7)$ and then substitutes $x=7$ and $x=-5$. Is this valid, even though the original equation is not defined at $x=7$ and $x=-5$?
