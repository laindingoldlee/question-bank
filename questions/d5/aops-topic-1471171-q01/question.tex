Pump $A$ can fill a tank of water in $4$ hours. Pump $B$ can fill the same tank in $6$ hours. Both pumps are started at $8{:}00$ a.m. to fill the same empty tank. An hour later, pump $B$ breaks down; it takes one hour to repair and is then restarted. When will the tank be full? (Round your answer to the nearest minute.)
