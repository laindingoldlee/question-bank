Pump $A$ fills $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of the tank per hour and pump $B$ fills $\dfrac{1}{6}$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $8{:}00$--$9{:}00$, both pumps: $\dfrac{1}{4}+\dfrac{1}{6}=\dfrac{5}{12}$ of the tank.
\item $9{:}00$--$10{:}00$, pump $A$ only: $\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{3}{12}$; total $\dfrac{8}{12}=\dfrac{2}{3}$.
\item From $10{:}00$, both pumps again fill the remaining $\dfrac{1}{3}$ at $\dfrac{5}{12}$ per hour, which takes $\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{12}{5}=\dfrac{4}{5}$ hour $=48$ minutes.
\end{itemize}
The tank is full at $\boxed{10{:}48\text{ a.m.}}$
