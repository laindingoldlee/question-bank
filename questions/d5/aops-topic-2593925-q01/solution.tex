Joining the centre $O$ to the eight vertices cuts the octagon into $8$ congruent isosceles triangles with two sides $1$ and apex angle $\dfrac{360^\circ}{8}=45^\circ$.

In one of them, $OAB$, drop the perpendicular $BH$ to $OA$. Triangle $OHB$ is a $45^\circ$--$45^\circ$--$90^\circ$ triangle with hypotenuse $OB=1$, so its height is $BH=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$. Hence
\[[OAB]=\dfrac12\cdot OA\cdot BH=\dfrac12\cdot1\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt2}2=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{4},\]
and the octagon has area $8\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt2}4=\boxed{2\sqrt2}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0);pair[] V;for(int k=0;k<8;++k)V.push(dir(45*k));
fill(O--V[0]--V[1]--cycle,lightgray);
draw(V[0]--V[1]--V[2]--V[3]--V[4]--V[5]--V[6]--V[7]--cycle,linewidth(0.8));draw(unitcircle,gray);
draw(O--V[0]);draw(O--V[1]);pair H=(V[1].x,0);draw(V[1]--H,dashed);
real s=0.06;draw(H+(-s,0)--H+(-s,s)--H+(0,s));
dot(O);label("$O$",O,W);label("$A$",V[0],E);label("$B$",V[1],NE);label("$H$",H,S);
label("$1$",V[1]/2,NW,fontsize(8pt));label("$45^\circ$",O,(3,1),fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}
