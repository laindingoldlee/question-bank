When $n=1$, the player to move wins immediately, giving $11$ pairs. For $n\ge2$, a position is winning exactly when $m+n$ is odd. This follows by induction on the total, allowing $m=0$: an odd-total position can move to an even-total position with at least two red marbles (if $n=2$, remove blue; otherwise remove red), whereas every move from an even-total position either leaves an odd-total position with at least two reds or leaves one red, both winning for the next player. There are five even $n$ from $2$ to $11$, each with six odd choices of $m$, and five odd $n\ge3$, each with five even choices. Total $11+30+25=\boxed{66}$.
