The whole right triangle has $CA=\sqrt{6^2+4^2}=2\sqrt{13}$. Since $D^{\prime} C=3$ and $BC=1+3=4$, similarity of triangles $CA^{\prime} D^{\prime}$ and $CAB$ gives $\dfrac{CA^{\prime}}{CA}=\dfrac{3}{4}$. Hence $\boxed{CA^{\prime}=\dfrac{3\sqrt{13}}{2}}$.
