There are $5\cdot9=45$ allowed two-digit numbers from $50$ through $99$, giving $90$ digits. The desired position is therefore the $1927$th digit among the allowed three-digit numbers. Since $1927=642\cdot3+1$, it is the first digit of the $643$rd such number. There are $8\cdot9^2=648$ allowed three-digit numbers in total. The final nine are $990,991,992,994,995,996,997,998,999$, so the $643$rd is $994$. Its first digit is $\boxed9$.
