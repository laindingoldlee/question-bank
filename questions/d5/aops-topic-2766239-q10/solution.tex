The discriminants in $p$ must be integer squares $u^2=169+20c$ and $v^2=169-20c$, with $u>13$ and $v\ge0$. Thus $u^2+v^2=338$, so $14\le u\le18$. The remainders $338-u^2$ are 142,113,82,49,14; only 49 is a square, at $u=17,v=7$. Hence $c=6$. It works because the factors are $(5p+3q)(p+2q)$ and $(5p-2q)(p+3q)$. Thus $\boxed6$.
