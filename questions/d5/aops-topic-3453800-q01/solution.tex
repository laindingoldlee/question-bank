Place $B=(0,0)$, $C=(28,0)$, $A=(0,21)$. If the square side is $s$, then $E=(s,s)$ lies on $\dfrac{x}{28}+\dfrac{y}{21}=1$, giving $s=12$. Thus $G=(6,6)$. The original area is 294, while $[ABG]=\dfrac{1}{2}(21)(6)=63$ and $[BCG]=\dfrac{1}{2}(28)(6)=84$. Subtracting gives $[AGC]=294-63-84=\boxed{147}$.
\begin{asy}
import geometry; usepackage("amsmath"); size(240);
pair A=(0,21),B=(0,0),C=(28,0),D=(12,0),Epoint=(12,12),F=(0,12),G=(6,6);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(B--D--Epoint--F--cycle,blue);draw(D--F^^A--G^^C--G,dashed);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,S);label("$E$",Epoint,NE);label("$F$",F,W);label("$G$",G,N);
\end{asy}
