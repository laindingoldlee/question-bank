Tomas writes two three-digit positive base-ten integers (without leading zeros) on a blackboard, one of which is three times the other. Ben sees the board but assumes the two integers are written in base $16$. Under this assumption he converts both integers to base ten and divides the larger by the smaller. What is the difference between the largest and smallest possible values of Ben's result? Express your answer as a common fraction.
