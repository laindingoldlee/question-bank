Joy takes a contest and reaches problem $10$. She arrives at the test in one of two states of mind, hazy or clear, and the probability that she is hazy is half the probability that she is clear. If she is hazy she has a $90\%$ chance of getting stuck on problem $10$; if she is clear, a $20\%$ chance. What is the probability that Joy does not get stuck on problem $10$?

(a) $\dfrac{3}{10}$\quad (b) $\dfrac{11}{30}$\quad (c) $\dfrac{13}{30}$\quad (d) $\dfrac{11}{20}$\quad (e) $\dfrac{17}{30}$\quad (f) $\dfrac{19}{30}$\quad (g) $\dfrac{7}{10}$
