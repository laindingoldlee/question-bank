$P(\text{hazy})+P(\text{clear})=1$ and $P(\text{hazy})=\dfrac{1}{2}P(\text{clear})$, so $P(\text{hazy})=\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $P(\text{clear})=\dfrac{2}{3}$. By the law of total probability,
\[
P(\text{not stuck})=\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{1}{10}+\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot\dfrac{8}{10}=\dfrac{1}{30}+\dfrac{16}{30}=\boxed{\textbf{(e)}\ \dfrac{17}{30}} .
\]
