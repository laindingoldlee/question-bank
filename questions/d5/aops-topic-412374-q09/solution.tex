By inclusion--exclusion, since beds $B$ and $C$ share no plants (so no plant is in all three beds),
\[
500+450+350-50-100=1150 .
\]
Each shared plant was counted twice in $500+450+350$ and is subtracted once. The total is $\boxed{\textbf{(C)}\ 1150}$.
