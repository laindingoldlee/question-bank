A mathematical reservoir initially contains $200$ units of a substance and decays exponentially with half-life five hours. At selected instants, exactly $200$ units may be added. Require the amount always to remain between $100$ and $300$ units. Determine the first addition time and all subsequent gaps for an indefinitely maintained schedule.
