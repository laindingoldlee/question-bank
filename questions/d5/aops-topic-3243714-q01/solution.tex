Just before each addition the amount must be at most $100$ to avoid exceeding $300$ afterwards, and at least $100$ by the lower bound. Thus additions must occur precisely at level $100$. The initial $200$ decays to $100$ after $\boxed5$ hours. Every addition then raises it to $300$, which reaches $100$ after $300\cdot2^{-\dfrac{t}{5}}=100$, or $\boxed{t=5\log_2 3}$ hours. These threshold times uniquely maintain the bounds indefinitely.
