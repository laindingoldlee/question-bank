Count $100$ to $599$ (neither $100$ nor $600$ contains a $7$). No hundreds digit is $7$. For each hundreds digit $1$ to $5$, the last two digits contain a $7$ in $100-9\cdot9=19$ cases. The total is $5\cdot19=\boxed{95}$.
