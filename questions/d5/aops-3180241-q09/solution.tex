Three distinct noncollinear points have a circumcircle. A repeated point would lie on a circle with the other fixed point, so it cannot meet the stated no-circle condition. Thus $(x,y)$ lies on both lines through the two fixed pairs. They are $y=\dfrac{5}{3}x$ and $y=17+\dfrac{1}{8}x$. Solving gives $x=\dfrac{408}{37}$, $y=\dfrac{680}{37}$, and $x-y=-\dfrac{272}{37}$. Therefore $\boxed{p+q=309}$.
