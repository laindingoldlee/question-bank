B's possible sums $0,3,6,9,12$ have counts $1,4,6,4,1$ out of $16$. A's matching dice-sum counts are $0,2,5,4,1$. Thus ties have probability
\[\boxed{\dfrac{1(0)+4(2)+6(5)+4(4)+1(1)}{16\cdot36}=\dfrac{55}{576}}.\]
The numbers of dice outcomes below those five sums are $0,1,10,26,35$. Thus B wins with probability
\[\boxed{\dfrac{1(0)+4(1)+6(10)+4(26)+1(35)}{576}=\dfrac{203}{576}}.\]
