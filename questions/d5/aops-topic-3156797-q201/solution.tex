Multiplying by $ x-1 $ gives $ x^7-1=0 $. The value $ x=1 $ does not solve the original equation, whose value there is seven. Conversely every other seventh root of unity does solve it. Thus $\boxed{x=e^{\dfrac{2\pi i k}{7}},\ k=1,2,\ldots,6}$. These are all nonreal.
