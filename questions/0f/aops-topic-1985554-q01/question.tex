A projectile fired vertically upward with initial velocity $120$ ft per second has height $s=120t-16t^2$ feet after $t$ seconds.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] After how many seconds does the projectile first reach a height of $216$ feet?
\item[(b)] Can the projectile reach $900$ feet? Justify your answer.
\end{enumerate}
