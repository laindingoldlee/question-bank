Because $34=18+16$ and $24\sqrt2=2(3\sqrt2)(4)$, the radicand is $(3\sqrt2-4)^2$. The principal square root is $|3\sqrt2-4|$. Since $18>16$, $3\sqrt2>4$, giving $\boxed{3\sqrt2-4}$.
