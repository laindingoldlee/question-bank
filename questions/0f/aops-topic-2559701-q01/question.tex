For positive integers $n$ and $r\le n$, let $P(n,r)=\dfrac{n!}{(n-r)!}$ be the number of ordered selections of $r$ objects from $n$ distinct objects. Let $n$, $k$, $j$ be positive integers with $k+j\le n$. Simplify $P(n,k)\cdot P(n-k,j)$ and explain the result by counting.
