Call a set of four students a candidate set if every member names exactly the other three. The actual failing group is a candidate set. Two candidate sets cannot intersect without being equal: a shared student\textquotesingle s list, together with that student, determines the entire set. Thus distinct candidate sets are disjoint and cover a multiple of four students. Since $2019$ is not divisible by four, at least one student lies outside every candidate set. This student cannot belong to the actual failing group and is certainly not failing.
