Let the airline route counts be $a,b,c$. After any airline closes, the remaining graph on 15 vertices must be connected, so $a+b,a+c,b+c\ge14$. Summing gives $a+b+c\ge21$. Attain 21 with seven triangles sharing one central city and otherwise disjoint: assign one edge of each triangle to each airline. Removing any one color leaves each triangle connected to the center. Thus the minimum is $\boxed{21}$.

\end{asy}
