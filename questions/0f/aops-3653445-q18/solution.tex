Assume Ella is behind each door with equal probability, independently of the uniformly chosen host. Conditional on Ella being left, the observed middle-pickle event has probability $\dfrac{1+\dfrac{1}{2}+1}{3}=\dfrac{5}{6}$. Conditional on Ella being right, it has probability $\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{2}+0}{3}=\dfrac{1}{3}$. Ella in the middle is incompatible with the observation. A pickle on the left means Ella on the right, so Bayes's rule gives $\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{3}}{\dfrac{5}{6}+\dfrac{1}{3}}=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{7}}$.
