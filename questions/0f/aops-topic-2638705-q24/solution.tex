For $A=0,1,2,3,4,5$, the permissible $B$ values are $1$ through $5$: even the largest product $35\cdot57=1995$ works, while $30\cdot67=2010$ already fails. For $A=6,7,8,9$, the permissible values are $1$ through $4$, since $39\cdot47=1833\le2000$ but $36\cdot57=2052>2000$. Therefore the count is $6\cdot5+4\cdot4=\boxed{46}$.
