Consider the system
\[
2x^2=y+4,\qquad 2y^2=x+7 .
\]
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that the system has exactly four real solutions $(x,y)$.
\item[(b)] Find all solutions in rational numbers.
\end{enumerate}
