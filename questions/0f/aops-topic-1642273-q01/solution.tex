Expand and collect terms.

First equation: $-2a+2b+16=3b+21$, so $-2a-b=5$.

Second equation: $5a+5=-b-9$, so $5a+b=-14$.

Adding the two equations eliminates $b$: $3a=-9$, so $a=-3$, and then $b=-2a-5=1$. So
\[
\boxed{a=-3,\ b=1}.
\]
