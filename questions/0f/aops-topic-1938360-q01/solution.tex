Chaining the comparisons, $3<4<1<2$ and $5<4$. So $3$ and $5$ are both below $4$ (in some order), and $1$ and $2$ are above it. Whatever the order of $3$ and $5$, the order is $\{3,5\}<4<1<2$, and the middle (third) number is $\boxed{4}$.
