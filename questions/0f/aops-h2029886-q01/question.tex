In a convex quadrilateral $ABCD$, perpendicular diagonals $AC,BD$ intersect at $E$. A point $P$ on $AD$ satisfies $EP=EC$. The circle through $B,C,D$ meets $AD$ again at $Q\ne D$. The circle through $A$, tangent to line $EP$ at $P$, meets $AC$ again at $R\ne A$. All these points and circles are well-defined. If $B,Q,R$ are collinear, prove $\angle BCD=90^\circ$.
