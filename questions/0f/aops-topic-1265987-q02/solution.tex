The result is $2(200-n)$ with $10\le n\le99$. It is largest when $n$ is smallest, $n=10$: $2\cdot190=\boxed{380}$.

If negative integers were allowed, $n=-99$ would give $2\cdot299=598$; the contest intends ``two-digit integer'' to mean a positive one, which is why its answer is $380$.
