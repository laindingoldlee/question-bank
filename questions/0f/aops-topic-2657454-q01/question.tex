There are $11$ trees $t_1,t_2,\dots,t_{11}$, and $s(k)$ is the number of squirrels on tree $t_k$. At $12$ pm, $s(1)=s(2)=\cdots=s(11)=1$. At $1$ pm, $s(1)$ becomes the sum of all eleven values at $12$ pm (so $s(1)=11$), the other trees being unchanged. At $2$ pm, $s(2)$ becomes the sum of all eleven values at $1$ pm (so $s(2)=21$). In general, at $k$ pm the value $s(k)$ becomes the total at $(k-1)$ pm, and the other trees are unchanged. How many squirrels are on tree $t_{11}$ at $11$ pm?
