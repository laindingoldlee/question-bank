Let $T_k$ be the total number of squirrels just after $k$ pm, so $T_0=11$. Tree $t_k$ still has its original $1$ squirrel just before $k$ pm, and then gets $T_{k-1}$, so
\[T_k=T_{k-1}-1+T_{k-1}=2T_{k-1}-1,\qquad\text{i.e.}\qquad T_k-1=2(T_{k-1}-1).\]
Hence $T_k=1+10\cdot2^k$ (check: $T_1=21$, $T_2=41$). At $11$ pm, $s(11)=T_{10}=1+10\cdot1024=\boxed{10241}$.
