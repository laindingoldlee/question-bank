Each person gets $1$ club sandwich ($3$ slices) and $2$ regular sandwiches ($2\cdot2=4$ slices), so $3+4=7$ slices per person, or $7n$ slices for $n$ people. The Dagwood adds $5$ slices. The deli needs $\boxed{7n+5}$ slices of bread.
