A deli receives a large order for sandwiches for a company picnic. The company wants one club sandwich and two regular sandwiches for each person, and also one extra Dagwood sandwich. A club sandwich has $3$ slices of bread, a regular sandwich has $2$ slices and a Dagwood has $5$ slices. If $n$ people are going to the picnic, how many slices of bread does the deli need? Write your answer as a simplified expression in which the variable $n$ appears exactly once.
