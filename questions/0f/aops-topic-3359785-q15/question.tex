A game starts at throwing speed $50$ mph. At speed $v$, a successful throw earns $\dfrac{v}{10}$ points and has probability $\min(1,\dfrac{140-v}{100})$. After success the speed rises by $10$ mph, and after failure it falls by $10$ mph. Conditional on the speeds, throws use independent random outcomes. If the expected total after three throws is $\dfrac{m}{n}$ in lowest terms, find $n$.
