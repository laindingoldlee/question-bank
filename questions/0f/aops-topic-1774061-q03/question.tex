An unfair die with faces numbered $1$ to $6$ has the property that the probability of rolling each number is proportional to that number. You roll this unfair die twice and a fair die twice. What is the probability that the sum of the two rolls of the unfair die is greater than the sum of the two rolls of the fair die?
