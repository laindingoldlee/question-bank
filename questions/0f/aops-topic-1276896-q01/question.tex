A teacher whispers a positive integer to each of Anna ($A$), Brett ($B$) and Chris ($C$). Each knows only their own number, and all know that $A+B+C=14$. Anna says, "I know that Brett and Chris have different numbers." Then Brett says, "I already knew that all three of our numbers are different." Finally Chris says, "Now I know all three numbers." Find $ABC$.
