Divide the numbers and subtract the exponents of each variable:
\[
\dfrac{x^2y}{2yx^{-2}}=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot x^{2-(-2)}\cdot y^{1-1}=\dfrac{1}{2}x^4y^0 .
\]
So $\boxed{A=\dfrac{1}{2},\ m=4,\ n=0}$, i.e.\ the expression equals $\dfrac{1}{2}x^4$.
