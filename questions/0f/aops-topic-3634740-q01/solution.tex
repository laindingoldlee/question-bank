There are $\binom{78}{2}=3003$ equally likely pairs. Choose the two suits in $\binom 42=6$ ways and one of the four face cards from each suit in $4^2$ ways. The probability is $\dfrac{6\cdot 16}{3003}=\boxed{\dfrac{32}{1001}}$.
