Let the circle have centre $O$ and radius $R$. A chord of length $4$ subtends a central angle $2\theta$ and a chord of length $10$ subtends $2\varphi$, where
\[
\sin\theta=\dfrac{2}{R},\qquad \sin\varphi=\dfrac{5}{R}.
\]
The six central angles fill the full turn: $2\cdot2\theta+4\cdot2\varphi=360^\circ$, so $\theta+2\varphi=90^\circ$. (The order of the sides does not matter: reordering the chords around the circle keeps the same circle. With the order $4,10,10,4,10,10$, the chords $AB,BC,CD$ span exactly a semicircle, so $AD$ is a diameter.)

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real R=1+sqrt(51);
real t=2*aSin(2/R), u=2*aSin(5/R);
pair P0=R*dir(180);
pair P1=R*dir(180-t), P2=R*dir(180-t-u), P3=R*dir(180-t-2*u);
pair P4=R*dir(180-2*t-2*u), P5=R*dir(180-2*t-3*u);
draw(circle((0,0),R),gray);
draw(P0--P1--P2--P3--P4--P5--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(P0--P3,red+dashed);
dot((0,0)); label("$O$",(0,0),S);
label("$A$",P0,W); label("$B$",P1,NW); label("$C$",P2,N); label("$D$",P3,E);
label("$4$",(P0+P1)/2,E); label("$10$",(P1+P2)/2,SE); label("$10$",(P2+P3)/2,SW);
label("$4$",(P3+P4)/2,W); label("$10$",(P4+P5)/2,NW); label("$10$",(P5+P0)/2,NE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

From $\theta=90^\circ-2\varphi$,
\[
\sin\theta=\cos2\varphi=1-2\sin^2\varphi
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \dfrac{2}{R}=1-\dfrac{50}{R^2}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad R^2-2R-50=0 .
\]
The positive root is $R=1+\sqrt{51}$, so the area of the circle is
\[
\pi R^2=\pi\left(1+\sqrt{51}\right)^2=\boxed{\left(52+2\sqrt{51}\right)\pi}\approx208.2 .
\]
