Regroup the six fractions in reciprocal pairs:
\[\dfrac{a+b}{c}+\dfrac{a+c}{b}+\dfrac{b+c}{a}=\left(\dfrac {a}{b}+\dfrac {b}{a}\right)+\left(\dfrac {a}{c}+\dfrac {c}{a}\right)+\left(\dfrac {b}{c}+\dfrac {c}{b}\right).\]
For positive $x$, AM-GM gives $x+\dfrac{1}{x}\ge2\sqrt{x\cdot\dfrac{1}{x}}=2$, with equality only for $x=1$ (equivalently $x+\dfrac{1}{x}-2=\dfrac{(x-1)^2}{x}\ge0$). Applying this to $\dfrac {a}{b}$, $\dfrac {a}{c}$ and $\dfrac {b}{c}$, the expression is at least $2+2+2=6$, and it equals $6$ when $a=b=c$ (each fraction is then $2$). The minimum value is $\boxed{6}$.
