Let $Z$ walk $r$ steps per minute, so $A$ walks $2r$, and let the escalator move $e$ steps per minute. $A$ is on the escalator for $\dfrac{27}{2r}$ minutes and $Z$ for $\dfrac{18}{r}$ minutes. Each boy covers the $n$ visible steps, partly by walking and partly carried by the escalator:
\[
n=27+e\cdot\dfrac{27}{2r}=18+e\cdot\dfrac{18}{r}.
\]
Subtracting, $9=\dfrac{9e}{2r}$, so $\dfrac {e}{r}=2$. Then $n=18+18\cdot2=\boxed{54}$, answer $\textbf{(B)}$. (Check: $27+27=54$.)
