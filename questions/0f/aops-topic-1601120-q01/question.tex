The two-digit positive integers $x$ and $y$ are such that $x$ is obtained by reversing the digits of $y$. Also $x^2=y^2+k$, where $k$ is a positive integer that has exactly $24$ positive divisors and exactly $3$ distinct prime factors. What is the greatest possible value of $k$?
