The terms are $4k-2$ for $k=1,2,\dots$, and $4k-2=3098$ gives $k=775$. The sum of this arithmetic series is
\[
\dfrac{775\,(2+3098)}{2}=775\cdot1550=\boxed{1201250}.
\]
