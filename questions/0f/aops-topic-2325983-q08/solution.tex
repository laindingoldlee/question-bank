Micah spent $1$ hour $30$ minutes $=90$ minutes; Lewis spent $1$ hour $40$ minutes $=100$ minutes. So $\boxed{\text{Lewis spent }10\text{ minutes longer}}$. Micah's rate was $\dfrac{150}{90}=\dfrac53\approx1.67$ houses per minute and Lewis's was $\dfrac{140}{100}=1.4$ houses per minute, so $\boxed{\text{Micah was faster}}$.
