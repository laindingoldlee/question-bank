Since $CH\perp AB$, triangle $AHC$ is right at $H$. Its hypotenuse is $AC$, whose midpoint is $M$ because $BM$ is a median. The midpoint of a right triangle\textquotesingle s hypotenuse is equidistant from its three vertices, so $MH=\dfrac{AC}{2}$. Thus $\boxed{AC=20\text{ cm}}$.

\end{asy}
