We need nonnegative integers $a,b$ with $2a+3b=2021$. Then $3b$ is odd, so $b$ is odd, and $3b\le2021$ gives $b\le673$. Every odd $b$ with $1\le b\le673$ gives $a=\dfrac{2021-3b}{2}$, a nonnegative integer (at least $1$). So there are as many ways as odd numbers from $1$ to $673$, namely $\boxed{337}$.
