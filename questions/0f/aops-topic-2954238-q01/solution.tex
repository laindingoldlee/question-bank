Let $a=3^{256}$, so $a^4=3^{1024}=3^{4^5}$. Since $5^6-1=15624=4\cdot3906$, we have $4^{5^6}=4\cdot\left(4^{3906}\right)^4=4b^4$ with $b=4^{3906}=2^{7812}$. By the Sophie Germain identity,
\[a^4+4b^4=(a^2+2b^2+2ab)(a^2+2b^2-2ab)=\bigl((a+b)^2+b^2\bigr)\bigl((a-b)^2+b^2\bigr).\]
Both factors are at least $b^2=2^{15624}>2^{10\cdot1562}=1024^{1562}>10^{4686}>10^{2009}$. $\blacksquare$
