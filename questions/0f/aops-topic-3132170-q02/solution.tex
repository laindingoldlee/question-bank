The integer $24=2^3\cdot3$ has $(3+1)(1+1)=8$ divisors. To rule out smaller numbers, three distinct prime factors already give a number at least 30. A number with one prime factor needs exponent at least 6, giving at least 64. With two prime factors, exponents $(1,1)$ give only four divisors and $(2,1)$ only six. The first remaining possibilities have exponents at least $(3,1)$, giving at least $2^3\cdot3=24$, or $(2,2)$, giving at least 36. Thus $\boxed{24}$.
