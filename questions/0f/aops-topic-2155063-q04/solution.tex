At the end of each night the snail is $1$ foot higher than the night before, so after $d-1$ full days and nights it is $d-1$ feet up. On day $d$ it climbs to $d-1+3=d+2$ feet. It reaches the top $10$ feet when $d+2\ge10$, that is, on day $\boxed{8}$. (After the $7$th night it is at $7$ feet, and on day $8$ it climbs to $10$ feet before it can slide back.)
