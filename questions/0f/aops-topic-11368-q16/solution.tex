At 4:00 the hour hand is $120^\circ$ ahead of the minute hand. The minute hand gains $5.5^\circ$ per minute, and the hands are first perpendicular when the gap has shrunk to $90^\circ$, after gaining $30^\circ$:
\[t=\dfrac{30}{5.5}=\dfrac{60}{11}\ \text{min}=5\ \text{min}\ 27.27\ \text{s}.\]
So the time is $\boxed{4{:}05{:}27}$.
