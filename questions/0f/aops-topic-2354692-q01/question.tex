Ling Ling usually practises the violin for $40$ minutes a day. Starting on the Monday of the week before a competition, which is on Sunday, Ling Ling practises $20\%$ longer each day than on the day before: on Monday $20\%$ longer than the usual $40$ minutes, on Tuesday $20\%$ longer than on Monday, and so on. Ling Ling's cousin, Lung Lung, practises each day $\dfrac14$ as long as Ling Ling does that day. How long does Lung Lung practise on Saturday?
