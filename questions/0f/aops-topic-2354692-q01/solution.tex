Saturday is the sixth day of increases, so on Saturday Ling Ling practises $40\cdot1.2^6$ minutes, and Lung Lung practises
\[\dfrac14\cdot40\cdot1.2^6=10\cdot\left(\dfrac65\right)^6=10\cdot\dfrac{46656}{15625}=\boxed{\dfrac{93312}{3125}\text{ minutes}}=29.85984\text{ minutes},\]
about $30$ minutes.
