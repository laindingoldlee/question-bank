Rearrangement gives $(b^2-2026a)(c-2026)=2026^2a$. Both factors are positive. Thus for each trial $a$, inspect positive divisors $d$ of $2026^2a$ and ask whether $2026a+d$ is a square. For $a=1,2$, none is; this requires only the divisors of $2^2 1013^2$ and $2^3 1013^2$, respectively, obtainable by their prime-exponent choices. For $a=3$, the divisor $d=6$ gives $b^2=6078+6=6084=78^2$, and $c = 2026 + 2026^2 \cdot \dfrac{3}{6} = 2054364$. Hence the least value is $\boxed3$.
