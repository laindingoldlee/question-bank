Triangles $ABD$ and $DEF$ are isosceles right triangles with right angles at $B$ and $E$. Points $A$, $D$, $F$ and $C$ are collinear in this order, and points $B$, $E$ and $C$ are collinear, with $B$ and $E$ on the same side of line $AC$. If $AB=BD=4$ and $DE=EF=2$, find $AC$. Also justify the following ``infinite'' method: repeating the construction with triangles half as large each time fills segment $AC$, so $AC=4\sqrt2+2\sqrt2+\sqrt2+\cdots$.
