Raymond and the cello must occupy adjacent rear seats: a front-row pair would put either Raymond or the cello in the driver’s seat. There are two adjacent rear pairs and two orders within each. The three remaining people occupy the remaining seats in $3!$ ways. Total: $2\cdot2\cdot6=\boxed{24}$.
