Let $f$ be a function defined on the set of positive rational numbers with the property that $f(a\cdot b)=f(a)+f(b)$ for all positive rational numbers $a$ and $b$. Suppose also that $f(p)=p$ for every prime number $p$. For which of the numbers
\[\dfrac{17}{32},\qquad\dfrac{11}{16},\qquad\dfrac79,\qquad\dfrac76,\qquad\dfrac{25}{11}\]
is $f(x)<0$?
