Triangle $ABC$ is equilateral, since it is isosceles with apex angle $60^\circ$. Thus $AC=5$ and its area is $\dfrac{25\sqrt3}{4}$. Triangle $ACD$ has sides $5,5,6$; its altitude to the side of length six is $\sqrt{25-9}=4$, so its area is $12$. The diagonal partitions the convex quadrilateral, giving $k=\dfrac{25}{4}$ and $\boxed{100k=625}$.
