Starting on May $1$, Marquinho collects $10$ kg of aluminium cans every day. On April $30$ the recycling price was $\$1.60$ per kilogram, and during May the price falls by $\$0.04$ per kilogram each day, so on May $n$ it is $1.60-0.04n$ dollars per kilogram. Because of shipping costs he must sell all the cans he has collected at one time, at the end of some day in May (after collecting that day's cans). On which day should he sell to get the greatest revenue, and what is that revenue?
