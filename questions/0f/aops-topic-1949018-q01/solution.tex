If he sells at the end of May $n$ ($1\le n\le31$), he has $10n$ kg and the price is $1.60-0.04n$ dollars per kilogram, so the revenue is
\[
R(n)=10n(1.60-0.04n)=16n-0.4n^2=0.4\,n(40-n)=160-0.4(n-20)^2 .
\]
This is largest when $n=20$, giving $R(20)=160$ dollars. He should sell on \boxed{\text{May }20}, for revenue $\$160$.
