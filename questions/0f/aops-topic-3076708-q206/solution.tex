The left side is a list of $b+c$ copies of $a$. Split it after its first $b$ terms. The first group sums to $ab$ and the second to $ac$, so associativity of addition gives $a(b+c)=ab+ac$. Empty groups cover the cases $b=0$ or $c=0$. For arbitrary real $b,c$, distributivity is instead part of the field axioms.
