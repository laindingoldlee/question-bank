After $n$ days F has $2(\dfrac{5}{4})^n$ and S has $50+5n$. F's day-$n$ gain is $\dfrac{1}{2}(\dfrac{5}{4})^{n-1}$. For it to exceed $5$, we need $(\dfrac{5}{4})^{n-1}>10$. Exact integer comparisons give $(\dfrac{5}{4})^{10}<10<(\dfrac{5}{4})^{11}$, so (a) $\boxed{12}$.
For (b), direct comparison gives $2(\dfrac{5}{4})^{19}<145$ and $2(\dfrac{5}{4})^{20}>150$. Before day $12$, F's daily gain is smaller, so the initial gap cannot close; thereafter the difference between the balances increases strictly. The consecutive comparisons at $19,20$ therefore show that the first day is $\boxed{20}$.
