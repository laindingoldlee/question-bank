The altitude to the base $18$ bisects it, so the height is $\sqrt{15^2-9^2}=12$. Let the rectangle have height $y$ and width $x$. The part of the triangle above the rectangle is similar to the whole triangle, with height $12-y$, so
\[
\dfrac{x}{18}=\dfrac{12-y}{12}\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad x=\dfrac{3}{2}(12-y).
\]

The area is the quadratic
\[
A(y)=xy=\dfrac{3}{2}\,y(12-y)=-\dfrac{3}{2}\left(y^2-12y\right)=54-\dfrac{3}{2}(y-6)^2 ,
\]
which is largest at $y=6$, $x=9$. The largest area is $\boxed{54}$, half the area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot18\cdot12=108$ of the triangle.
