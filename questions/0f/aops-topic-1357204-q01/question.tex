A baseball team is equally likely to win or not win each game, independently. Four of its games are chosen at random.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What is the probability that exactly $2$ of them are won?
\item[(b)] What is the probability that exactly $3$ of them are won?
\item[(c)] Give a general formula for the probability that exactly $k$ of $n$ games are won.
\end{enumerate}
