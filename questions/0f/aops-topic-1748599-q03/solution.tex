If Tyler scored $T$, the mean is $\dfrac{86+72+61+93+T}{5}=\dfrac{312+T}{5}$. Setting it equal to $T$ gives $312+T=5T$, so $T=78$. Then the scores in order are $61,72,78,86,93$, whose median is indeed $78$. Tyler scored $\boxed{78}$.
