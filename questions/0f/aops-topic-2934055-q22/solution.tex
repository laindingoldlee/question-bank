Comparing $ab=c+d$ and $ac=b+d$ gives $(a+1)(b-c)=0$, with analogous identities for any shared variable. If no entry is-1, these identities force all four equal, contrary to the hypothesis. Thus one entry is-1. Pairing it with each other entry shows the sum of the other three is 0. Their pairwise products then equal the remaining entry minus 1. If these three were all equal they would all be 0, but $0\ne-1$. Therefore comparing identities among them forces another entry to be-1. The remaining entries $u,v$ satisfy $u+v=1$ and $uv=-2$, giving $u,v=-1,2$. Thus $\boxed{(-1,-1,-1,2)}$, readily checked.
