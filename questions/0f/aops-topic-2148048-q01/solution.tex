Each term splits as
\[\dfrac{1}{k(k+2)}=\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{1}{k}-\dfrac{1}{k+2}\right).\]
Adding for $k=1,\dots,8$, every $\dfrac{1}{k}$ with $3\le k\le8$ appears once with each sign and cancels, leaving
\[\dfrac{1}{2}\left(1+\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{9}-\dfrac{1}{10}\right)=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{90+45-10-9}{90}=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{116}{90}=\boxed{\dfrac{29}{45}}.\]
