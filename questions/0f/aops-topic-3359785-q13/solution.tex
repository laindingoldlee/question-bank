Put $u=AX,v=BX,w=CX$. The cosine rule at $X$ gives $AB^2=u^2+v^2+uv$ and the analogous two identities. Their sum is $AB^2+BC^2+CA^2=2(u^2+v^2+w^2)+uv+vw+wu$. Adding the three triangle areas gives $[ABC]=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}(uv+vw+wu)$. Therefore\[(u+v+w)^2=\dfrac{AB^2+BC^2+CA^2+4\sqrt3[ABC]}{2}.\]Now $BC^2=4^2+6^2-2\cdot4\cdot6\cos30^\circ=52-24\sqrt3$ and $[ABC]=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot4\cdot6\sin30^\circ=6$. Substitution cancels the radicals and gives $\boxed{52}$.
