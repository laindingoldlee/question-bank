PROBABILISTIC has $13$ letters: three I's, two B's, and the eight distinct letters P, R, O, A, L, S, T, C.

First arrange the $10$ letters other than the I's. Because of the two B's there are
\[
\dfrac{10!}{2!}=1814400
\]
such arrangements. These $10$ letters create $11$ gaps: one before the first letter, one after the last, and $9$ between neighbouring letters. No two I's are adjacent exactly when the I's go into three \emph{different} gaps (one I per gap), and since the I's are identical, a choice of $3$ of the $11$ gaps determines their placement. Conversely, in any arrangement with no two I's adjacent, deleting the I's leaves an arrangement of the other $10$ letters with the I's in distinct gaps. So the count is
\[
\dfrac{10!}{2!}\cdot\dbinom{11}{3}=1814400\cdot165=\boxed{299376000}.
\]
