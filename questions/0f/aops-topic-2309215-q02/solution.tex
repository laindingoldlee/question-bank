First choose Group $1$ in $\dbinom{32}{5}=201376$ ways, then choose Group $2$ from the remaining $27$ students in $\dbinom{27}{6}=296010$ ways. The number of ways is
\[\dbinom{32}{5}\dbinom{27}{6}=201376\cdot296010=\boxed{59609309760}.\]
