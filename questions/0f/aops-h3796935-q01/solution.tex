Divisibility by nine requires a positive multiple of nine ones, hence at least nine ones. Divisibility by four requires the last two digits to be 00, since the other possible endings 01,10,11 are not divisible by four. Thus at least eleven digits are needed. With eleven digits, all the first nine must be ones and the last two zeros. The number $\boxed{11111111100}$ has digit sum nine and ends in 00, so is divisible by both nine and four, hence by 36. It is therefore minimal.
