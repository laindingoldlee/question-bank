Choose $n$ distinct primes $p_0,\ldots,p_{n-1}$. Their powers are pairwise coprime. The Chinese remainder theorem supplies $N\equiv-i\pmod{p_i^n}$ for all $0\le i<n$. Add a sufficiently large multiple of $\prod_i p_i^n$ to make $N>0$. Then $N+i$ is divisible by $p_i^n$, with $p_i>1$, as required.
