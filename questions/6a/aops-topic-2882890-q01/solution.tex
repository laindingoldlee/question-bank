With the \$500 book, we need \$300 more: three \$100 books ($\dbinom53=10$) or one \$200 and one \$100 ($3\cdot5=15$), giving $25$. Without it: three \$200 and two \$100 ($1\cdot10=10$), or two \$200 and four \$100 ($3\cdot5=15$), giving $25$ (one \$200 would need six \$100 books). The total is $\boxed{50}$.
