For each prime $p\le25$, its exponent in $a$ is $e_p=\sum_{j=20}^{25}\sum_{r\ge1}\lfloor\dfrac{j}{p^r}\rfloor$. This gives $e_2=118,e_3=55,e_5=26,e_7=17,e_{11}=10,e_{13}=6,e_{17}=6,e_{19}=6,e_{23}=3$. Thus $n=118$ and $m=2^{\lfloor\dfrac{118}{26}\rfloor}3^{\lfloor\dfrac{55}{26}\rfloor}5^{\lfloor\dfrac{26}{26}\rfloor}=2^4 3^2 5=720$. Hence $\boxed{m+n=838}$.
