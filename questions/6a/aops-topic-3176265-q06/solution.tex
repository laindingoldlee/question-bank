Project the square vertices onto a unit normal to the lines. After reflecting or exchanging square sides, their ordered projections are $0,a,b,a+b$, with $0<a<b$ and $a^2+b^2=1$. Equal consecutive gaps give $a=b-a$, so $b=2a$. Thus $5a^2=1$, and the spacing is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{\sqrt5}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw((0,0)--(1,0)--(1,1)--(0,1)--cycle);for(int k=0;k<4;++k)draw((-.4,(k+.4)/2)--(1.4,(k-1.4)/2),blue);label("unit square",(.5,-.7));
\end{asy}
