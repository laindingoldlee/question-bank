From $3a=4b$ we get $a=\dfrac{4}{3}b$, and from $5b=6c$ we get $c=\dfrac{5}{6}b$. Then $a+b=\dfrac{7}{3}b$ and
\[
\dfrac{c}{a+b}=\dfrac{\dfrac{5}{6}b}{\dfrac{7}{3}b}=\dfrac{5}{6}\cdot\dfrac{3}{7}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{14}} .
\]
