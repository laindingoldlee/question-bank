Use $B=(0,0),A=(3,0),C=(0,4)$. The altitude is $y=\dfrac{3}{4}x$, and the perpendicular bisector is $x=\dfrac{3}{2}$. Hence $K=(\dfrac{3}{2},\dfrac{9}{8})$. The circumcenter is the hypotenuse midpoint $O=(\dfrac{3}{2},2)$, giving $\boxed{KO=\dfrac{7}{8}}$.

\end{asy}
