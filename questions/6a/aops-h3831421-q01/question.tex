(a) Is it true that for all positive integers $a,b,n,k$ with $n\mid a+b$, one has $n^{\dfrac{a+b}{n}}\mid a^{nk}-(-1)^n b^{nk}$? Give a counterexample if not.
(b) Prove the corrected statement
\[
n^2\mid a^{nk}-(-1)^{nk}b^{nk}\qquad\text{whenever }n\mid a+b.
\]
(c) Find the remainder of $6^{273}+8^{273}$ modulo $49$.
