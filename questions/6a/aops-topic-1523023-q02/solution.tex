The mean of the five numbers is $\dfrac {K}{3}$, so their sum is $\dfrac{5K}{3}$. Removing the median leaves a sum of $\dfrac{5K}{3}-K=\dfrac{2K}{3}$ for four numbers, whose mean is $\dfrac{2K}{3}\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac {K}{6}$. So the answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{6}}$.

(Such sets exist, for example $-10,-8,3,10,10$: the median is $3$ and the mean is $1$.)
