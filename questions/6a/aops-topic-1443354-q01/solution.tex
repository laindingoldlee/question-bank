The terms are $3k$ for $k=2,3,\dots,57$, so there are $57-2+1=56$ terms. The sum is the number of terms times the average of the first and last:
\[
56\cdot\dfrac{6+171}{2}=28\cdot177=\boxed{4956}.
\]
