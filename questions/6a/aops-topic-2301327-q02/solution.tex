Write the cards as $2a$ and $2b$ with $a\ne b$ in $\{1,2,\dots,10\}$. The product is $4ab$, which is at least $32$ exactly when $ab\ge8$. Count the complementary pairs $\{a,b\}$ with $ab<8$, that is, $ab\le7$:
\[\{1,2\},\{1,3\},\{1,4\},\{1,5\},\{1,6\},\{1,7\},\{2,3\},\]
which is $7$ pairs ($\{2,4\}$ already gives $8$). Out of $\dbinom{10}{2}=45$ equally likely pairs, $45-7=38$ work, so the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{38}{45}}$.
