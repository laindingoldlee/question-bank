Let $f$ be a real-valued function on positive integers, and define $F(x,y)=f(x)+f(y)$. Suppose $F(n,n)=n$ for every positive integer $n$. Let $s$ and $p$ be the sum and product, respectively, of all possible values of $F(12,34)$. Find $sp$.
