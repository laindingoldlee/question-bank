The value of the following sum \[{2024 \choose 0}+{2024  \choose 4}+{2024  \choose 8}+ ... +{2024  \choose 4k}+ ... +{2024  \choose 2024}\]can be expressed as $2^x + 2^y$, where $x$ and $y$ are positive integers. Find $x + y$.



Note: The notation ${n \choose k}$ expresses the number of ways to choose $k$ objects from a group of $n$. For example, ${5  \choose 2}= 10$ because there are $10$ ways to choose $2$ objects from a group of $5$. Additionally, ${n  \choose 0}= 1$ for any $n$.
