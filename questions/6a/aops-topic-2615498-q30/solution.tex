Adding, $f_1+f_2+f_3+f_4=x^4+10x^3+35x^2+50x=(x+1)(x+2)(x+3)(x+4)-24$. Now $(i+1)(i+2)(i+3)(i+4)=24\binom{i+4}{4}$, so by the hockey-stick identity
\[\sum_{i=1}^{10}(i+1)(i+2)(i+3)(i+4)=24\left(\binom{15}{5}-\binom55\right)=24\cdot3002=72048.\]
The double sum is $72048-240=71808$, and $\dfrac{71808}{384}=\boxed{187}$.
