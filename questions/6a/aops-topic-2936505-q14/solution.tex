With probability one, the four points are distinct. For their cyclic order, the chord joining $A$ to $B$ crosses $CD$ exactly when the two pairs alternate. Fix $A$ first around the circle. Among the six orders of the other three labels, two alternate, so the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{3}}$.

usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw(unitcircle);pair A=dir(15),C=dir(105),B=dir(190),D=dir(285);draw(A--B^^C--D);label("$A$",A,E);label("$B$",B,W);label("$C$",C,N);label("$D$",D,S);
\end{asy}
