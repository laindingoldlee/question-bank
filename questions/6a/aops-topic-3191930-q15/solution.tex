The angle is $180-\dfrac{360}{n}$. It is an even integer exactly when $\dfrac{180}{n}$ is an integer, so $n$ must divide 180. Since $180=2^2 3^2 5$, it has 18 divisors. Remove 1 and 2 to get $\boxed{16}$.

usepackage("amsmath");

path p;for(int k=0;k<6;++k)p=p--dir(60*k);draw(p--cycle);label("$120^\circ$",(.55,0));
\end{asy}
