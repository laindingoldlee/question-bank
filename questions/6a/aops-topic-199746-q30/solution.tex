The songs last $30,60,\dots,300$ seconds, and the favourite lasts $210$ seconds. Use the complement: she hears all of the favourite within the first $270$ seconds exactly when it starts at time at most $270-210=60$ seconds. The time before it is the total length of the songs played before it, so this happens when
\begin{itemize}
\item the favourite is first: probability $\dfrac{1}{10}$;
\item the $30$-second song is first and the favourite second: $\dfrac{1}{10}\cdot\dfrac{1}{9}=\dfrac{1}{90}$;
\item the $60$-second song is first and the favourite second: $\dfrac{1}{90}$.
\end{itemize}
(Two songs before it last at least $30+60=90$ seconds, too long.) So the complement has probability $\dfrac{9+1+1}{90}=\dfrac{11}{90}$, and the answer is
\[
1-\dfrac{11}{90}=\boxed{\dfrac{79}{90}}.
\]
