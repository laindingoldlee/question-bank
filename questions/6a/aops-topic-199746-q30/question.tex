An o-Pod MP3 player stores and plays entire songs. Celeste has $10$ songs stored on her o-Pod, all of different lengths. When the songs are ordered by length, the shortest song is $30$ seconds long and each subsequent song is $30$ seconds longer than the previous one. Her favourite song is $3$ minutes, $30$ seconds long. The o-Pod plays all the songs in random order (all orders equally likely) before repeating any song. What is the probability that she hears the first $4$ minutes, $30$ seconds of music (there are no pauses between songs) without hearing every second of her favourite song? Express your answer as a common fraction.
