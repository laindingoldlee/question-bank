Modulo 13, $20\equiv7$. We have $7^6\equiv-1$ and $7^4\equiv9$, so its multiplicative order is 12: neither 6 nor 4 can contain the order, excluding every proper divisor of 12. Thus its powers give all 12 nonzero residues. Spirited integers are exactly the positive integers not divisible by 13. There are $2012-\left\lfloor\dfrac{2012}{13}\right\rfloor=2012-154=\boxed{1858}$.
