For fixed $a$, the other two variables have sum $3-a$ and product $x-a(3-a)$. Real values $b,c$ exist exactly when their quadratic discriminant is nonnegative:
\[
(3-a)^2-4(x-3a+a^2)\ge0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
(a-1)^2\le4-\dfrac{4x}{3}.
\]
Thus feasibility requires $x\le3$, and the full interval of possible $a$ has width $4\sqrt{1-\dfrac{x}{3}}$. Requiring this width to equal $8$ gives $x=-9$. At the two endpoints $a=-3,5$, the choices $b=c=3,-1$, respectively, confirm attainment. Therefore $\boxed{50+N=41}$.
