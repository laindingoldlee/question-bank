Each side is $\sqrt{6^2+12^2}=6\sqrt5$, and area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot12\cdot24=144$. Area equals inradius times semiperimeter, so $r=\dfrac{144}{12\sqrt5}=\dfrac{12}{\sqrt5}$. Then $r^2=\dfrac{144}{5}$ and $\boxed{149}$.
