Since $\ln e=1$, a blue egg weighs $1$ kilogram. The truck holds $95$ eggs. With $y$ yellow and $b$ blue eggs,
\[
y+b=95,\qquad 3y+b=255 .
\]
Subtracting, $2y=160$, so $y=80$ and $b=15$. The probability of a blue egg is
\[
\dfrac{15}{95}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{19}} .
\]
