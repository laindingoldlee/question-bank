The Easter Bunny's truck holds $190$ eggs when full, and it is now exactly half full. Each egg is yellow or blue. Each yellow egg weighs $3$ kilograms and each blue egg weighs $\ln e$ kilograms, and the eggs in the truck weigh $255$ kilograms in total. An egg is chosen at random from the truck. What is the probability that it is blue?
