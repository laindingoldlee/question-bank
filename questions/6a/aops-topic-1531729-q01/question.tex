Two lines $\ell$ and $m$ intersect at a point $O$ and form four angles. Consider the statement: ``If two of the four angles are congruent, then the lines are perpendicular.''
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Explain why the statement is false as written.
\item[(b)] Insert one word so that it becomes true, and prove the corrected statement.
\end{enumerate}
