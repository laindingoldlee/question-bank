For integer $n\ge0$, expected profit is $P(n)=100000(1-10^{-n})-1000n$, including $P(0)=0$. Adding one team changes it by $P(n+1)-P(n)=90000\cdot10^{-n}-1000$. This is positive for $n=0,1$ and negative for every $n\ge2$. Therefore the unique maximum occurs at $\boxed{n=2}$, with expected profit $99000-2000=97000$ dollars.
