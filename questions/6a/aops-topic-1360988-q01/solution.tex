Look for $\left(m+n\sqrt2\right)^2=m^2+2n^2+2mn\sqrt2=17+12\sqrt2$ with positive integers $m,n$: we need $mn=6$ and $m^2+2n^2=17$. Trying the factor pairs of $6$, $(m,n)=(3,2)$ gives $9+8=17$. So
\[
17+12\sqrt2=\left(3+2\sqrt2\right)^2,\qquad \sqrt{17+12\sqrt2}=\boxed{3+2\sqrt2},
\]
taking the positive square root since $3+2\sqrt2>0$.
