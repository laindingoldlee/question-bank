The possible sums are $1,4,9,16,25$. For digits $a,b,c$, put $u=a-1$, so $0\le u\le8$ and $0\le b,c\le9$. Stars and bars with upper-bound exclusions gives respectively $1,10,45,66,6$ solutions. For example, sum 16 means $u+b+c=15$, giving $\binom{17}2-\binom82-2\binom72=66$. Sum 25 can be counted by deficits from 999 summing 2, giving $\binom42=6$. The total is $\boxed{128}$.
