The right side is $111d=3\cdot37\cdot d$, which is positive. Since $37$ is prime, it divides $10a+b$ or $10c+b$; the equation is symmetric in $a$ and $c$, so say $37\mid10a+b$. As $0<10a+b<100$, we have $10a+b=37$ or $74$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $10a+b=37$: then $b=7$ and $37(10c+7)=111d$, so $10c+7=3d\le27$. Of the numbers $7,17,27$, only $27$ is a multiple of $3$. So $c=2$ and $d=9$.
\item $10a+b=74$: then $b=4$ and $74(10c+4)=111d$, so $20c+8=3d\le27$. This forces $c=0$ and $3d=8$, which is impossible.
\end{itemize}
So the two factors are $37$ and $27$ (in some order), $37\cdot27=999$, and
\[a+b+c+d=3+7+2+9=\boxed{21}.\]
