A circle has centre $A$. A diameter of the circle is extended beyond its endpoint $B$ to a point $T$ outside the circle. A chord $RS$ is extended beyond $S$ to the same point $T$, so that $T$, $S$, $R$ lie on a line in this order. The measure of angle $RAS$ is $74^\circ$ and the measure of angle $RTB$ is $28^\circ$. What is the measure of minor arc $BR$, in degrees?
