Let Max's speed be $v$ and total time $t$. Equal distances give $3v(12)+\dfrac{v}{3}(t-12)=vt$. Divide by $v$ and simplify: $36+\dfrac{t-12}{3}=t$, hence $t=48$. Thus $\boxed{48\text{ minutes}}$.
