A uniform wooden cylinder of radius $4$ cm and height $23$ cm is hollowed out to create a tube of uniform thickness $0.5$ cm. The density of wood is $0.75$ grams per cubic centimetre. The ratio between the mass of the tube and the mass of the original cylinder can be expressed as $\dfrac{a}{b}$ , where $a$ and $b$ are relatively prime. Find $a + b$.
