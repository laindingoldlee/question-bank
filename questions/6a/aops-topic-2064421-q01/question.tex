Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle with altitudes $AD$, $BE$, $CF$ ($D$ on $BC$, $E$ on $CA$, $F$ on $AB$). Let $K$ and $L$ be the incentres of triangles $BFD$ and $CDE$, and let $P$ and $Q$ be the circumcentres of triangles $ABK$ and $ACL$. Prove that $PQ\parallel KL$.
