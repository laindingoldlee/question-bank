Let $a_1,a_2,\dots,a_n$ be positive integers, with least common multiple $L$ and greatest common divisor $G$. Prove that
\[
L\ \ge\ \dfrac{a_1+a_2+\cdots+a_n}{n}\ \ge\ G,
\]
and that equality holds in either inequality only when $a_1=a_2=\cdots=a_n$.
