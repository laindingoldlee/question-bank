The units digits of $7^1,7^2,7^3,7^4,\dots$ are $7,9,3,1$, repeating with period $4$. Since $2018\equiv2\pmod4$, the digit written in $2018$ is $9$ and in $2019$ it is $3$. These are adjacent and $|9-3|=6\ge4$. So the condition is first met in $2019$, after $\boxed{1}$ year.
