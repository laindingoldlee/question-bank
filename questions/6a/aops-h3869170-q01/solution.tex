For $x=2\cos\theta$, induction using the double-angle identity gives $P_n(x)=2\cos(2^n\theta)$. Therefore the $1024$ distinct roots are
\[
r_j=2\cos\left(\dfrac{(2j+1)\pi}{2048}\right),\qquad 0\le j<1024.
\]
The polynomial has degree $1024$, so this is its full root set. Using $4\cos^2t=2+2\cos2t$,
\[
b=2048+2\sum_{j=0}^{1023}\cos\left(\dfrac{(2j+1)\pi}{1024}\right)=2048.
\]
The final cosine sum is the real part of a geometric sum of equally spaced roots of unity and is zero. Hence $\boxed{a+b=1024+2048=3072}$.
