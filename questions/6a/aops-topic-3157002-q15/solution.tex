For $|x|<1 $, twice differentiating the geometric series gives $\sum_{n\ge1}n(n+1)x^n=\dfrac{2x}{(1-x)^3}$. Substituting $ x=\dfrac{1}{2}$ and dividing by 2 gives $\boxed4 $. Alternatively, summing triangular numbers by their contributions yields $\sum_{n\ge1}\sum_{j=1}^n j2^{-n}=2\sum_{j\ge1}j2^{-j}=4 $.
