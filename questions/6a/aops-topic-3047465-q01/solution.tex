Write $a=3+x,b=3+y,c=3+z$ with $x,y,z\ge0$. The left side minus the right side expands to $9x+6y+3z+7xy+5xz+3yz+3xyz$. Every term is nonnegative, proving the inequality. Equality holds exactly when $x=y=z=0$, that is, $a=b=c=3$.
