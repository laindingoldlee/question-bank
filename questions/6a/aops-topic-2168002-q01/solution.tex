Write everything in terms of powers of $2$ and $3$:
\[8^{\left(9^{10}\right)}=\left(2^3\right)^{\left(9^{10}\right)}=2^{3\cdot9^{10}}=2^{3\cdot3^{20}}=2^{\left(3^{21}\right)} .\]
Two powers of $2$ are equal only when the exponents are equal, so $3^x=3^{21}$ and $x=\boxed{21}$.
