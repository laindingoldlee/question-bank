By Vieta's formulas, the quadratic has root sum $-\dfrac{9}{3}=-3$ and root product $-\dfrac{16}{3}$. The cubic $2x^3+ax^2+6x-b$ has root sum $-\dfrac {a}{2}$ and root product $-\dfrac{-b}{2}=\dfrac {b}{2}$. So
\[-\dfrac {a}{2}=-3,\qquad\dfrac {b}{2}=-\dfrac{16}{3},\]
giving $a=6$ and $b=-\dfrac{32}{3}$. Hence
\[17a+35b=102-\dfrac{1120}{3}=\boxed{-\dfrac{814}{3}}.\]
