Since $5=6-1$,
\[
555555_6=5(6^5+6^4+\dots+1)=6^6-1 .
\]
Factor as a difference of squares and then as a difference and a sum of cubes:
\[
6^6-1=(6^3-1)(6^3+1)=(6-1)(6^2+6+1)(6+1)(6^2-6+1)=5\cdot43\cdot7\cdot31 .
\]
So $555555_6=46655$ and its prime factors are $\boxed{5,\ 7,\ 31,\ 43}$.
