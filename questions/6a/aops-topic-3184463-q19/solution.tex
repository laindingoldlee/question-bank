A base 6 trailing zero requires one factor 2 and one factor 3. We have $v_2(12!)=6+3+1=10$ and $v_3(12!)=4+1=5$. Thus the largest power of 6 dividing the factorial is $6^5$, giving $\boxed5$ zeroes.
