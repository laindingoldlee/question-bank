A parabola is the set of points that are equidistant from a fixed point (the focus) and a fixed line (the directrix). Show that the parabola with focus $(h,k+p)$ and directrix $y=k-p$, where $p\ne0$, has equation
\[(x-h)^2=4p(y-k),\]
and explain the roles of $h$, $k$ and $p$, in particular where the factor $4p$ comes from.
