Since $8!=8\cdot7!$,
\[
8!-7!=8\cdot7!-1\cdot7!=(8-1)\cdot7!=7\cdot7!=7\cdot5040=\boxed{35280}.
\]
Factoring out the common factor $7!$ works exactly like $8a-a=7a$ with $a=7!$.
