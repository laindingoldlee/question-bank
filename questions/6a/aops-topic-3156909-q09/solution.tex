The alternating digit sum must be 0, because its absolute value is below 11. Thus the two odd-position digits sum to 5, as do the even-position digits. The pairs are $\{1,4\},\{2,3\}$. Choose which pair occupies odd positions in 2 ways and arrange each pair in $ 2!$ ways, giving $\boxed8 $.
