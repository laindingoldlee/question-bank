Exactly three fixed coats can be chosen in $\binom63 $ ways, and the other three can be deranged in $ 2 $ ways. Exactly four fixed coats give $\binom64 $ possibilities; exactly five is impossible; all six gives one. Thus the probability is $\dfrac{\binom63\cdot2+\binom64+1}{6!}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{90}}$.
