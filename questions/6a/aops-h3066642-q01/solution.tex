For a factored polynomial, $\sum_\xi\dfrac{1}{z-\xi}=\dfrac{P'(z)}{P(z)}$, where the sum is over its roots. Also $\dfrac{1}{1-\xi^2}=\dfrac{1}{2}(\dfrac{1}{1-\xi}+\dfrac{1}{1+\xi})$. Therefore the requested sum is
\[\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{P'(1)}{P(1)}-\dfrac{P'(-1)}{P(-1)}\right).\]
Here $P(1)=2023$, $P'(1)=-2019$, $P(-1)=-2023$, and $P'(-1)=6069$. Thus the sum is $\dfrac{1}{2}(-\dfrac{2019}{2023}+3)=\boxed{\dfrac{2025}{2023}}$. The nonzero values $P(\pm1)$ also show that every denominator is defined.
