A last digit of one requires $n$ coprime to ten. For $n\ge4$, $n!$ is divisible by four, so every such $n$ works by the four-cycle of units digits. There are $202\cdot4+2=810$ integers at most 2024 coprime to ten. One works, but three fails because $3^{3!}=3^6$ ends in nine. Therefore the count is $\boxed{809}$.
