Scale and place the circle at $x^2+y^2=1$, with $E=(0,-1)$, $C=(1,1)$, and $D=(-1,1)$. Points on $EC$ are $(t,-1+2t)$. The circle equation gives $t^2+(-1+2t)^2=1$, or $t(5t-4)=0$. The second intersection therefore has $t=\dfrac{4}{5}$, so $EP=\dfrac{4}{5}EC$. By symmetry $EQ=\dfrac{4}{5}ED$. The triangles share their included angle at $E$, so their areas are in the ratio $\dfrac{4}{5}\cdot\dfrac{4}{5}=\boxed{16:25}$.

\end{asy}
