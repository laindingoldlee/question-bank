Box A has side $6$ and surface area $216$. The spheres have total volume $27\cdot\dfrac{4}{3}\pi=36\pi$, so box B has side $(36\pi)^{\dfrac{1}{3}}$ and surface area $6(36\pi)^{\dfrac{2}{3}}$. The difference is $216-6(36\pi)^{\dfrac{2}{3}}\approx\boxed{75.7}$ square centimetres. Box B is defined by volume alone; it need not contain the spheres.
