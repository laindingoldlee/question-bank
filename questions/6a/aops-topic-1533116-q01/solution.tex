\textbf{The terms are odd.} If $x$ were even, every term would be even and the sum of cubes would be divisible by $8$; but $-1197$ is odd. So the terms are consecutive odd integers.

\textbf{Cancellation.} Let $L=x+2n$ be the last term. If $L\ge-x$, the terms from $x$ to $-x$ cancel in pairs ($k^3+(-k)^3=0$) and the remaining terms are positive, so the sum would be $\ge0$. Hence $L<-x$. If $L>0$, the terms from $-L$ to $L$ cancel and what is left is the block $x,x+2,\dots,-L-2$ of negative odd numbers; if $L<0$, the whole sequence is such a block. Either way, $1197$ is a sum of cubes of consecutive positive odd numbers ending at $-x$.

\textbf{Finding the block.} The cubes of the odd numbers are $1,27,125,343,729,1331,\dots$, and the sums of consecutive ones ending at $9$ are $729$, $1072$, $1197$, $1224$, $1225$, while any block ending at $7$ or below sums to at most $496$, and any block ending at $11$ or more is at least $1331$. So the block is $5,7,9$: the negative block is $-9,-7,-5$, and $x=-9$.

\textbf{The two sequences.} Either the sequence is just $-9,-7,-5$ (then $n=2$), or it continues and $-L-2=-5$, i.e.\ $L=3$: the sequence $-9,-7,\dots,1,3$, for which $-9+2n=3$ gives $n=6$. Since $n>3$, the answer is $n=\boxed{6}$.

(Check: $(-9)^3+(-7)^3+(-5)^3+(-3)^3+(-1)^3+1^3+3^3=-729-343-125=-1197$.)
