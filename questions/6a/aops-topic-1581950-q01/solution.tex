Following the definition, multiply $n$, $n-3$, $n-6$, $\dots$ while the factors stay positive:
\[
10!!!=10\cdot7\cdot4\cdot1=\boxed{280},\qquad 9!!!=9\cdot6\cdot3=\boxed{162}.
\]
(The triple factorial is not $\bigl((n!)!\bigr)!$; the three exclamation marks mean "step down by $3$", just as $n!!$ means $n(n-2)(n-4)\cdots$.)
