The triple factorial of a positive integer $n$ is
\[
n!!!=n(n-3)(n-6)\cdots,
\]
the product of the positive integers not exceeding $n$ that leave the same remainder as $n$ when divided by $3$. (For example, $8!!!=8\cdot5\cdot2=80$.) Compute $10!!!$ and $9!!!$.
