A video camera's battery lasts $2$ hours if it is used only for recording and $3$ hours if it is used only for playing back; in each mode it drains at a constant rate. You want to record a video and then play back the whole video, all on one fully charged battery.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What is the greatest length of video, in minutes, that you can record and then play back?
\item[(b)] A student argues: ``The battery is used at $\dfrac{1}{2}$ battery per hour while recording and $\dfrac{1}{3}$ battery per hour while playing back. The harmonic mean of $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $\dfrac{1}{3}$ is $\dfrac{2}{5}$, so one battery lasts $\dfrac{5}{2}$ hours, half of which is recording: $75$ minutes.'' Find the error.
\item[(c)] Explain why the harmonic mean of $2$ and $3$ (hours per battery) does give the correct answer.
\end{enumerate}
