Any chord of the smaller circle has length at most its diameter, $8$. This bound is attainable: take its centre at $(0,0)$ and the larger centre at $(0,4\sqrt3)$. The points $(\pm4,0)$ lie on both circles because $4^2+(4\sqrt3)^2=8^2$. Thus the maximum is $\boxed8$.
\begin{asy}
import graph;
usepackage("amsmath");
import geometry;
size(7cm);
pair O=(0,0),P=(0,4sqrt(3)),A=(-4,0),B=(4,0);draw(circle(O,4));draw(circle(P,8));draw(A--B);dot(O);dot(P);label("$O$",O,(0,-1));label("$P$",P,(0,1));label("$A$",A,(-1,0));label("$B$",B,(1,0));
\end{asy}
