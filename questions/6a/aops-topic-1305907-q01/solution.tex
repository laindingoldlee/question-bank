The step $-1\cdot\sqrt{-1}=\sqrt{(-1)^2\cdot(-1)}$ is wrong. The rule $c\sqrt x=\sqrt{c^2x}$ holds only for $c\ge0$, because $\sqrt{c^2x}=|c|\sqrt x$ (for real $c$ and the principal square root). Here $c=-1$, so $\sqrt{(-1)^2(-1)}=|-1|\sqrt{-1}=i$, while $-1\cdot\sqrt{-1}=-i$. A negative factor cannot be moved inside a square root, so $-i\ne i$.
