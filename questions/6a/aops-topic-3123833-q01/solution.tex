In each block of six consecutive integers, exactly two have residues $1$ or $5$ modulo $6$. Divide thirteen consecutive integers into two six-term blocks and one leftover term. At most $2+2+1=5$ qualify. The block $1,\ldots,13$ attains this with $1,5,7,11,13$. Thus the maximum is $\boxed5$. Zero is divisible by both $2$ and $3$.
