A six-digit sequence is generated by choosing each digit independently and uniformly from $0,1,2,3,4,5$. A digit $n$ ``wins'' if it appears exactly $n$ times in the sequence (so $0$ wins if it does not appear, and $1$ wins if it appears exactly once). What is the probability that $0$, $1$ and $2$ win and no other digit wins? Express your answer as a common fraction.
