(a) Extract the coefficient of $x^{100}$ from $(1-x)^{100}(1+x)^{100}=(1-x^2)^{100}$. The left side gives the stated sum, and the right side gives $(-1)^{50}\binom{100}{50}$. Thus the answer is $\boxed{\binom{100}{50}}$. (b) Set $R_r=\dfrac{\binom{50+r}r}{\binom{50}r}$. Factorials give $\dfrac{R_r}{R_{r-1}}=\dfrac{50+r}{51-r}$, hence $R_r-R_{r-1}=R_r\dfrac{2r-1}{50+r}$. The sum telescopes to $R_{50}-R_0=\boxed{\binom{100}{50}-1}$.
