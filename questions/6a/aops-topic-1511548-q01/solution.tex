Let a potato patty cost $p$ dollars and a hot dog $h$ dollars. Then $p+h=6.30$ and $5p+12h=46.55$. Subtracting $5(p+h)=31.50$ gives $7h=15.05$, so $h=2.15$ and $p=4.15$.

Suppose the Archimedes team bought $a$ patties and $b$ hot dogs and paid $P$ dollars. In cents,
\[
415a+215b=100P\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad 83a+43b=20P .
\]
Modulo $5$: $83a+43b\equiv3(a+b)$, so $5\mid a+b$. Modulo $4$: $83a+43b\equiv3(a+b)$, so $4\mid a+b$. Hence $a+b=20k$ for a positive integer $k$, and then
\[
20P=43(a+b)+40a=20(43k+2a),\qquad P=43k+2a,\quad 0\le a\le20k .
\]
So $P\ge43$. With $k=1$, $P$ runs over the odd numbers $43,45,\dots,83$; with $k\ge2$, $P\ge86$. The palindromes $1,\dots,9,11,22,33$ are below $43$, and $44$ is even, so neither is possible. The next palindrome, $55$, works with $k=1$, $a=6$, $b=14$:
\[
6\cdot4.15+14\cdot2.15=24.90+30.10=55.00 .
\]
The least palindrome is $\boxed{55}$.
