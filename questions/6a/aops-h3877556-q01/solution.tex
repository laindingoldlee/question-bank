For a sequence with $k\le12$ losses, the reflection principle gives
\[
\binom{25}{k}-\binom{25}{k-1}
\]
valid sequences, with $\binom{25}{-1}=0$. Indeed, reflecting the initial segment through the first point where losses exceed wins maps the invalid sequences bijectively to sequences with $k-1$ losses. Summing over $k=0,\ldots,12$ telescopes:
\[
N=\binom{25}{12}=5200300=2^2 5^2\cdot7\cdot17\cdot19\cdot23.
\]
Thus the divisor count is $\boxed{3\cdot3\cdot2^4=144}$.
