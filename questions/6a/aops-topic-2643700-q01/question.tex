Let $a_1=\sqrt2$ and $a_{n+1}=\left(\sqrt2\right)^{a_n}$ for $n\ge1$, so that $a_n$ is a tower of $n$ copies of $\sqrt2$. The infinite tower $\sqrt2^{\sqrt2^{\sqrt2^{\cdots}}}$ is defined as $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}a_n$. Show that the limit exists and find its value.
