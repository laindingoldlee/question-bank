If $89=13a+15b+17c$, reduction modulo 13 gives $b+2c\equiv12\pmod{13}$. But $0\le b+2c\le\dfrac{2(15b+17c)}{17}\le\dfrac{178}{17}<11$, impossible. Every number 90 through 102 is achievable: respectively $6\cdot15$, $7\cdot13$, $5\cdot15+17$, $6\cdot13+15$, $4\cdot15+2\cdot17$, $6\cdot13+17$, $3\cdot15+3\cdot17$, $5\cdot13+15+17$, $2\cdot15+4\cdot17$, $5\cdot13+2\cdot17$, $15+5\cdot17$, $4\cdot13+15+2\cdot17$, and $6\cdot17$. Adding 13 repeatedly represents every larger integer. Therefore $\boxed{89}$.
