Place $E=(0,0)$ and $A=(1,0)$. The region $R$ is a $120^\circ$ sector of radius $\sqrt 3$, so its area is $\pi$. The circle centered at A with radius $\sqrt 3$ meets the sector rays at $B=(-\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt 3}{2})$ and $C=(-\dfrac{1}{2},-\dfrac{\sqrt 3}{2})$. The desired intersection consists of triangle EBC and the circular segment bounded by chord BC and the left arc of that circle. The triangle area is $\dfrac{\sqrt 3}{4}$. At A, chord BC subtends $60^\circ$, so the segment area is $\dfrac{\pi}{2}-\dfrac{3\sqrt 3}{4}$. The total is $\dfrac{\pi}{2}-\dfrac{\sqrt 3}{2}$. Dividing by $\pi$ gives $\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{\sqrt 3}{2\pi}$, and $\boxed{(a,b,c)=(\dfrac{1}{2},3,2)}$.

\end{asy}
