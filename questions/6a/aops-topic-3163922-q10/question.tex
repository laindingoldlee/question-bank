Let $EA=1$. Region $R$ consists of points $P$ with $\angle PEA\ge 120^\circ$ and $PE<\sqrt 3$. A point $X$ is chosen uniformly by area from $R$. Express $P(AX<\sqrt 3)$ as $a-\dfrac{\sqrt b}{c\pi}$, where $b$ is squarefree and $a$ is rational. Find $(a,b,c)$.
