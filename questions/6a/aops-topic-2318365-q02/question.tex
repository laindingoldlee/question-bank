I have $5$ books to read. On a given day, I read none of them with probability $\dfrac{1}{10}$, exactly one with probability $\dfrac{2}{20}$, exactly two with probability $\dfrac{3}{20}$, exactly three with probability $\dfrac{4}{20}$, and exactly four with probability $\dfrac{5}{20}$. Otherwise I read all five. What is the probability that I read all five books?
