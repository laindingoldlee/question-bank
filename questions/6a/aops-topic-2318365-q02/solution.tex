The given probabilities add up to
\[\dfrac{2}{20}+\dfrac{2}{20}+\dfrac{3}{20}+\dfrac{4}{20}+\dfrac{5}{20}=\dfrac{16}{20},\]
so the probability of reading all five is
\[1-\dfrac{16}{20}=\dfrac{4}{20}=\boxed{\dfrac15}.\]
