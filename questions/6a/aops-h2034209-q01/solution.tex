The circle is $x^2+y^2-6x+2y=0$, with center $(3,-1)$ and radius $\sqrt{10}$. On $AB$, write $(x,y)=(2t,4t)$; substitution gives $20t^2-4t=0$, so $E=\left(\dfrac{2}{5},\dfrac{4}{5}\right)$.

The lengths $AC=4\sqrt2$ and $AB=2\sqrt5$ give, on the specified rays,
\[
K=\left(\dfrac{8\sqrt{10}}{5},\dfrac{4\sqrt{10}}{5}\right),\qquad
L=\left(6-\dfrac{7\sqrt{10}}{5},\dfrac{\sqrt{10}}{5}\right).
\]
Set $O=(3,\sqrt{10}-1)$. Direct substitution shows
\[
OA^2=OK^2=OL^2=36-10\sqrt{10}.
\]
The three vertices are noncollinear, so this equal-distance point is their circumcenter. Finally its distance from $(3,-1)$ is exactly $\sqrt{10}$, proving $\boxed{O\in\omega}$.
