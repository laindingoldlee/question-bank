Take $B=(0,0)$, $C=(14,0)$ and $A=(5,12)$; these coordinates give the required side lengths. Then $D=(7,0)$. The acute angles at $B,C$ satisfy $\tan B=\dfrac{12}{5}$ and $\tan C=\dfrac{4}{3}$. Therefore the slope of $BE$ is
\[
\tan(B-C)=\dfrac{\dfrac{12}{5}-\dfrac{4}{3}}{1+\dfrac{12}{5}\dfrac{4}{3}}=\dfrac{16}{63}.
\]
Write $E=A+t(D-A)=(5+2t,12-12t)$. Its slope condition gives $63(12-12t)=16(5+2t)$, so $t=\dfrac{169}{197}$. Since $AD=2\sqrt{37}$, the answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{338\sqrt{37}}{197}}$.
