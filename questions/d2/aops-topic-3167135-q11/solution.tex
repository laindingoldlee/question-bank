Since $AD\perp CD$, triangle $ACD$ gives $\angle DAE=90^\circ-24^\circ=66^\circ$. Triangle $ADE$ is isosceles with $AE=AD$, so $\angle AED=\dfrac{180^\circ-66^\circ}{2}=57^\circ$. Because $A,E,C$ are collinear in that order, $\angle CED=180^\circ-57^\circ=\boxed{123^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
real w=1/tan(24*pi/180);pair A=(0,0),Bp=(w,0),C=(w,1),D=(0,1),Ep=dir(24);draw(A--Bp--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C);draw(D--Ep,blue);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",Bp,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$E$",Ep,S);
\end{asy}
