\textbf{(b)} Factor out $a^n$: $a^{n+1}-a^n=a^n\cdot a-a^n\cdot1=(a-1)a^n$.

\textbf{(a)} With $a=3$, $n=4$: $3^5=3\cdot3^4$ is three copies of $3^4$, so going from $3^4$ up to $3^5$ adds two more copies of $3^4$. That is, $3^5-3^4=2\cdot3^4=2\cdot81=\boxed{162}$.
