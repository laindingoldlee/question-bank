A professor computes $3^5-3^4$ mentally, saying ``between $3^4$ and $3^5$ there are two times $3^4$''.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Explain what he means and compute $3^5-3^4$.
\item[(b)] Prove that $a^{n+1}-a^n=(a-1)a^n$ for every number $a$ and positive integer $n$.
\end{enumerate}
