A troll tries to start an online argument at most once per day.
\begin{itemize}
\item On Mondays and Tuesdays he makes an attempt with probability $80\%$. An attempt succeeds with probability $80\%$ on a Monday and $30\%$ on a Tuesday.
\item On Wednesdays he never makes an attempt.
\item On Thursdays, Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays he makes an attempt with probability $30\%$, and an attempt succeeds with probability $20\%$.
\end{itemize}
What happens on different days is independent. During one week, from Monday to Sunday, what is the probability that on some two consecutive days of that week he successfully starts an argument on both days?
