Repeated difference of squares yields $3^{1024}-1=(3-1)(3+1)\prod_{j=1}^{9}(3^{2^j}+1)$. The first two factors contribute $1+2=3$ factors of $2$. For $j\ge1$, $3^{2^j}\equiv1\pmod8$, so $3^{2^j}+1\equiv2\pmod8$ contributes exactly one. Therefore $m=3+9=\boxed{12}$.
