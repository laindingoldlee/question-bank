Five suspects, Mr.\ Ashfield, Ms.\ Barlow, Mr.\ Caswell, Dr.\ Denton and Mr.\ Everett, are investigated for a crime. Exactly two of them are guilty and the other three are innocent. It is known that:
\begin{itemize}
\item if Mr.\ Ashfield or Mr.\ Everett (or both) is guilty, then Ms.\ Barlow is guilty;
\item if Mr.\ Caswell is innocent, then Ms.\ Barlow is innocent;
\item if Dr.\ Denton is guilty and Mr.\ Ashfield is innocent, then Mr.\ Caswell is innocent.
\end{itemize}
Who are the two guilty suspects?
