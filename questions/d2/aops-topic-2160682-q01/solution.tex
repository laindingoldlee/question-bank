\textbf{Case 1: Barlow is guilty.} By the second fact (in contrapositive form), Caswell is guilty too. That makes two guilty suspects, so Ashfield, Denton and Everett are innocent. Check: the first fact holds because Ashfield and Everett are innocent, the second because Caswell is guilty, and the third because Denton is innocent. So this works.

\textbf{Case 2: Barlow is innocent.} By the first fact, Ashfield and Everett are innocent. The two guilty suspects must then be Caswell and Denton. But then Denton is guilty and Ashfield is innocent, so by the third fact Caswell is innocent, a contradiction.

So the guilty suspects are \boxed{\text{Ms.\ Barlow and Mr.\ Caswell}}.
