A positive integer has exactly two divisors precisely when it is prime. Thus exactly one die must show a prime ($2,3,$ or $5$), and the other two must show $1$. There are $3\cdot3=9$ favourable ordered outcomes among $6^3=216$. The probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{24}}$.
