Adam and Becky are building a house. Becky works twice as fast as Adam, and they both work at constant speeds for the same amount of time each day. They plan to finish in $6$ days. However, after $2$ days their friend Charlie also helps, and because of this they finish in just $5$ days in total. What fraction of the house did Adam build?
