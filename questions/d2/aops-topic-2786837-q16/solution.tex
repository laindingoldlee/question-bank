Let Adam build $a$ of the house per day, so Becky builds $2a$. Together they would finish in $6$ days, so $6\cdot3a=1$ and $a=\dfrac1{18}$. Adam works on all $5$ days, so he builds $5a=\boxed{\dfrac5{18}}$ of the house. (Charlie builds the rest, $1-15a=\dfrac16$, in the last $3$ days.)
