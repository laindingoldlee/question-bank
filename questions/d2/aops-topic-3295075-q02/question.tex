Let $ABC$ be acute, with circumcircle $\omega$. The altitudes from $A,B$ meet the circle again at $D,E$. Reflect $D$ in the midpoint of $BC$ to get $D^\prime$. Suppose $C,D^\prime,E$ are collinear. Prove $\angle BAC=2\angle ACB$.
