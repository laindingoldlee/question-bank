Choose all 8 cells in rows 1,3,5, numbering rows from bottom. In each chosen cell draw the diagonal from its lower-left to upper-right corner. Within one row, successive diagonals have endpoints on opposite horizontal grid lines at their shared vertical boundary, so they do not meet. Chosen rows are separated by an unchosen row, so their diagonals also are disjoint. This gives $3\cdot8=\boxed{24}$ diagonals.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
for(int x=0;x<=8;++x)draw((x,0)--(x,5),gray);for(int y=0;y<=5;++y)draw((0,y)--(8,y),gray);for(int y=0;y<5;y+=2)for(int x=0;x<8;++x)draw((x,y)--(x+1,y+1),blue+1);label("$8$ columns",(4,-.4));label("$5$ rows",(-.4,2.5),W);
\end{asy}
