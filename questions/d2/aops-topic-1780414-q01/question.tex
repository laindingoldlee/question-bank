A magic box contains $n=3p+1$ balls, where $p$ is a positive integer. Each time we take $1$ ball out of the box, $2$ more balls vanish from the box (if fewer than $2$ balls remain, all of them vanish). How many times can we take a ball out of the box? Express the answer in terms of $p$.
