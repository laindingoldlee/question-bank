Two trains move towards each other on parallel straight tracks. One travels at $120$ miles per hour and is $\dfrac{1}{2}$ mile long. The other travels at $20$ miles per hour and is $\dfrac{1}{12}$ mile long. $S$ seconds after the fronts of the trains pass each other, their ends pass. Find $S$.
