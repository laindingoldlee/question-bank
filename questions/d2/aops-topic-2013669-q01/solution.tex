Relative to the slower train, the faster train moves at $120+20=140$ miles per hour. From the moment the fronts meet until the moment the ends meet, the trains must slide past each other by the sum of their lengths:
\[
\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{12}=\dfrac{7}{12}\text{ mile}.
\]
This takes
\[
\dfrac{7}{12}\div140=\dfrac{1}{240}\text{ hour}=\dfrac{3600}{240}\text{ s}=\boxed{15}\text{ seconds}.
\]
