Cross-multiply:
\[
-23(5x-3)=2(22x+28)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad -115x+69=44x+56\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 13=159x .
\]
So $x=\boxed{\dfrac{13}{159}}$. (Check: both sides equal $-\dfrac{206}{159}$.)
