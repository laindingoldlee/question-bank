Work backwards. Let $R$ be the number of apples before Joe eats. He eats $\dfrac R6-1$, leaving $R-\dfrac R6+1=\dfrac56R+1=6$, so $R=6$ (and Joe eats $0$ apples). Before Mary took $\dfrac14$, there were $\dfrac43\cdot6=8$ apples, and before Bob took one there were $\boxed{9}$. (Check: $9\to8\to6\to6$.)
