The five players\' relative orders are uniformly random. Under the condition, Bill, Colette, Dan must occur in that order. Choose their three rank positions in $\binom{5}{3}=10$ ways; the other two players can be ordered in two ways for each. Colette has median rank $3$ precisely when Bill has rank $1$ or $2$ and Dan has rank $4$ or $5$, giving four rank selections. Thus the conditional probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{4}{10}=\dfrac{2}{5}}$.
