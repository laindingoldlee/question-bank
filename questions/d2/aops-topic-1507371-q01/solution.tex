Expanding, $(x+y)^2+(x-y)^2=2(x^2+y^2)$, so $x^2+y^2=5$. Next, with $u=(x+y)^2$ and $v=(x-y)^2$,
\[
u^2+v^2=(u+v)^2-2uv=100-2\bigl((x+y)(x-y)\bigr)^2=100-2(x^2-y^2)^2=98,
\]
so $(x^2-y^2)^2=1$. Then
\[
4x^2y^2=(x^2+y^2)^2-(x^2-y^2)^2=25-1=24,
\]
so $x^2y^2=6$ and, as $x,y>0$, $xy=\boxed{\sqrt6}$.

Such numbers exist: $x^2,y^2=3,2$ (from $x^2+y^2=5$, $x^2-y^2=\pm1$), e.g.\ $x=\sqrt3$, $y=\sqrt2$.
