The Jones family has $5$ boys and $3$ girls, and $2$ of the girls are twins. In how many ways can the eight of them be seated in a row of $8$ chairs if the twins insist on sitting next to each other and their other sister refuses to sit next to either of her sisters?

(One solution gives $2!\cdot2!\cdot5!\cdot5!$ ways, placing the twins only at an end of the row. Is that right?)
