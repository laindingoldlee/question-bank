Chain the conversion factors so that each unit cancels:
\[77\text{ pups}\cdot\dfrac{57\text{ pops}}{2\text{ pups}}\cdot\dfrac{32\text{ pips}}{3\text{ pops}}\cdot\dfrac{5\text{ peps}}{11\text{ pips}}\cdot\dfrac{3\text{ paps}}{2\text{ peps}}=\dfrac{77\cdot57\cdot32\cdot5\cdot3}{2\cdot3\cdot11\cdot2}\text{ paps}=\boxed{15960}\text{ paps}.\]
