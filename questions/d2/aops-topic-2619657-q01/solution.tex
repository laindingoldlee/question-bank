Rearranging, $11n^2=7\left(m^2-1\right)$, so $7\mid11n^2$. Since $7$ is prime and $7\nmid11$, we get $7\mid n^2$, and then $7\mid n$ (again because $7$ is prime). $\blacksquare$ (Solutions exist, for example $m=1$, $n=0$.)
