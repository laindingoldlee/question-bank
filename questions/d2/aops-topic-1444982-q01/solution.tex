Call a number \emph{squareful} if it is divisible by the square of an integer greater than $1$ (equivalently, by the square of a prime). Among three consecutive numbers, at most one is a multiple of $4$, and a number that is $2$ mod $4$ is squareful only if it has an odd square factor.

List the squareful numbers from $2010$ on. Multiples of $4$: $2012,2016,2020,2024$. Multiples of $9$: $2016,2025$. Multiples of $25$: $2025$. Multiples of $49$: $2009$, then $2058$. Multiples of $121$: $2057$. Multiples of $169$: $2028$. Multiples of $289=17^2$: $2023$. Larger prime squares $p^2\le2025$ ($p=19,23,29,31,37,41,43$) have multiples $1805,2166$; $1587,2116$; $1682$; $1922$; $1369$; $1681$; $1849$, none in $[2010,2025]$. So the squareful numbers in $[2010,2025]$ are
\[
2012,\ 2016,\ 2020,\ 2023,\ 2024,\ 2025 .
\]
The first three consecutive ones are $\boxed{2023,\ 2024,\ 2025}$: $2023=7\cdot17^2$, $2024=4\cdot506$, $2025=45^2$.
