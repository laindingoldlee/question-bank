There are $4+\dbinom42=10$ possible sets of places. By the pigeonhole principle, some set was chosen by at least $\left\lceil\dfrac{101}{10}\right\rceil=11$ students. With $11$ students on one set and $10$ on each of the others, $N=11$. The answer is $\boxed{11}$.
