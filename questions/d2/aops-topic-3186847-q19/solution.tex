The congruences are $n\equiv 1\pmod 3$, $n\equiv 0\pmod 4$, $n\equiv 4\pmod 5$, $n\equiv 3\pmod 7$. The first and third combine to $n\equiv 4\pmod{15}$; combining with divisibility by 4 gives $n\equiv 4\pmod{60}$. Write $n=60k+4$. Modulo 7 this gives $4k+4\equiv 3$, so $4k\equiv 6$ and $k\equiv 5\pmod 7$. Thus $n\equiv 304\pmod{420}$, with least positive value $\boxed{304}$.
