Draw the V-shaped graph $y=|x+1|$, with vertex $(-1,0)$, and the horizontal line $y=4$. They meet where $x+1=\pm4$, i.e. at $x=-5$ and $x=3$. The graph of $|x+1|$ is on or above the line exactly to the left of $x=-5$ and to the right of $x=3$ (including these points).

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
real f(real x){return abs(x+1);}
draw((-7.4,0)--(5.6,0),Arrow(5)); draw((0,-0.8)--(0,7),Arrow(5));
label("$x$",(5.6,0),E); label("$y$",(0,7),N);
draw(graph(f,-7,5),blue+linewidth(0.9));
draw((-7.2,4)--(5.3,4),red+linewidth(0.9));
draw((-7.2,0)--(-5,0),green+linewidth(2.5)); draw((3,0)--(5.1,0),green+linewidth(2.5));
draw((-5,4)--(-5,0),dashed); draw((3,4)--(3,0),dashed);
dot((-5,4)); dot((3,4)); dot((-5,0)); dot((3,0));
label("$-5$",(-5,0),S); label("$3$",(3,0),S); label("$-1$",(-1,0),S,fontsize(8pt));
label("$y=4$",(5.3,4),E,red); label("$y=|x+1|$",(-7.1,6),W,blue);
\end{asy}

So the solution is $\boxed{x\le-5\ \text{or}\ x\ge3}$, i.e. $(-\infty,-5]\cup[3,\infty)$ (thick on the $x$-axis).
