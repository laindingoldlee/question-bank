The half-life condition is $\lambda^{5700}=\dfrac{1}{2}$, so
\[
\lambda=2^{-\dfrac{1}{5700}}=0.9998784026\ldots,
\]
which rounds to $\boxed{0.999878}$. Use this exact expression, rather than its rounded value, for subsequent computations.
The first item's amount is $15\cdot2^{-\dfrac{3250}{5700}}=10.1030\ldots$, or $\boxed{10.1\text{ grams}}$.
For the second item,
\[
\dfrac{18}{25}=2^{-\dfrac{t}{5700}},\qquad
t=\dfrac{5700\ln(\dfrac{18}{25})}{\ln(\dfrac{1}{2})}
=2701.4077\ldots.
\]
Thus its age is $\boxed{2700\text{ years}}$ to the nearest hundred.
