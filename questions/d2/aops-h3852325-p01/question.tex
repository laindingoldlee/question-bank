Carbon-14 dating is modeled by $N=k\lambda^t$, where $k$ grams is the initial amount, $N$ grams remains after $t$ years, and $0<\lambda<1$. Its half-life is $5700$ years.
\begin{enumerate}\item Show that $\lambda=0.999878$ to six decimal places.
\item An item initially contained $15$ grams and is $3250$ years old. Find the predicted amount remaining, to three significant figures.
\item Another item initially contained $25$ grams and now contains $18$ grams. Find its age to the nearest $100$ years, using algebra.\end{enumerate}
