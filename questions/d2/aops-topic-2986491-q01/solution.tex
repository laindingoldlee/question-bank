He needs exactly $5$ shots iff the $5$th shot is his $3$rd hit: exactly $2$ hits among the first $4$ shots, then a hit. So the probability is
\[\binom42p^2(1-p)^2\cdot p=\boxed{6p^3(1-p)^2}.\]
