(After Sam Kean, \emph{Caesar's Last Breath}.) Suppose all $300$ inmates of Alcatraz escape and spread out over the United States so that each one, independently, ends up in a given place with probability proportional to its population. Take the population of the United States to be $325$ million, and suppose your hometown has $100{,}000$ people.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What is the expected number of fugitives in your hometown?
\item[(b)] What is the probability that at least one fugitive ends up in your hometown? Give a decimal to three significant figures.
\end{enumerate}
