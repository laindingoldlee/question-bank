The first two remain unchanged when $x,y$ are swapped. The last two are sums of all square terms and all unordered pair products, so any permutation merely reorders their summands. But $x-y$ changes to $y-x$, and these are not identically equal (take $x=1,y=0$). Thus every listed expression except $\boxed{x-y}$ is symmetric.
