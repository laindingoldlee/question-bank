Fabian has a deck of $25$ cards. Each card shows one of the integers $1,2,3,4,5$ printed in one of $5$ colours (red, orange, green, blue, violet), and each number--colour combination appears on exactly one card. Fabian draws $4$ cards at random without replacement. What is the probability that exactly $2$ different numbers and exactly $3$ different colours appear on his four cards?
