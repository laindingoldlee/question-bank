Substitute $a=t+4$. Expanding,
\[f(t+4)=-2t^4+12t^2-18=-2\left(t^2-3\right)^2.\]
So $f\le0$, with equality exactly when $t=\pm\sqrt3$, i.e. $a=4\pm\sqrt3$. The sum is $\boxed{8}$.
