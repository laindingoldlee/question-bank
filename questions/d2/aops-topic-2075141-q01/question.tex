Richard runs a blivet-making factory. Of the blivets he makes, $20\%$ are defective. Each time a defective blivet is tested, it fails with probability $10\%$, independently of all other tests; good (non-defective) blivets never fail. Richard takes a blivet from the assembly line and tests it repeatedly, and it passes every test. What is the least number of passed tests after which Richard can be at least $95\%$ sure that the blivet is good?
