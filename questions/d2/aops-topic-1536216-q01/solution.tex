\textbf{Order.} The husband is first or last ($2$ ways), and the other three can be called in $3!=6$ orders: $12$ orders.

\textbf{Names.} The husband (one person, called by either of two names) has $2$ possible names, the son $3$, the daughter-in-law $2$ and the granddaughter $2$: $2\cdot3\cdot2\cdot2=24$ choices.

Altogether there are $12\cdot24=\boxed{288}$ ways, answer $\textbf{D}$.
