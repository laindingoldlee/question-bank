Grandma calls her husband, son, daughter-in-law and granddaughter to dinner one at a time. She calls her husband either first or last, and the other three in any order. She calls her husband ``Hon'' or ``Don''; her son ``Rick'', ``Rich'' or ``Richard''; her daughter-in-law ``Fran'' or ``Francine''; and her granddaughter ``Suzanne'' or ``Zan''. In how many different ways could grandma call the four of them to dinner?

$\textbf{A.}\ 96\qquad\textbf{B.}\ 144\qquad\textbf{C.}\ 192\qquad\textbf{D.}\ 288\qquad\textbf{E.}\ 576$
