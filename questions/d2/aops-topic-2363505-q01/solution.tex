All $\dbinom72=21$ pairs are equally likely, and $\dbinom32=3$ of them consist of two green apples. The probability is $\dfrac{3}{21}=\boxed{\dfrac17}$. (Equivalently, $\dfrac37\cdot\dfrac26=\dfrac17$.)
