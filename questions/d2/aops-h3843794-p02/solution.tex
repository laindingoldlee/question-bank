For any two distinct specified labels, their clockwise separation is uniform on $1,\ldots,n-1$ spaces, with mean $\dfrac{n}{2}$. There are $n$ legs, including the return from $n$ to $1$. By linearity of expectation, the expected total distance is $n\cdot\dfrac{n}{2}$ spaces. One full rotation is $n$ spaces, so the expected number of rotations is
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{n}{2}}.
\]
The actual total is an integer number of rotations because the ant ends where it started; its expectation need not be integral.
