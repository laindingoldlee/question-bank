First $12^2=144$ and $12^3=144\cdot12=1728$. Then
\[
12^6=\left(12^3\right)^2=1728^2=(1700+28)^2=1700^2+2\cdot1700\cdot28+28^2=2890000+95200+784 .
\]
So $12^6=\boxed{2985984}$.

Check another way: $12^6=2^{12}\cdot3^6=4096\cdot729=4096\cdot700+4096\cdot29=2867200+118784=2985984$.
