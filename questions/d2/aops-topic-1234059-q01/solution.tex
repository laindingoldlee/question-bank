$7$ cannot be made: $7$, $7-5=2$ are not multiples of $3$. But $8=3+5$, $9=3\cdot3$, $10=5\cdot2$, and adding $3$s to these gives every larger number. The answer is $\boxed{7}=3\cdot5-3-5$.
