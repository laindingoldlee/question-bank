The cosine rule in triangle $ABI$ gives $AI^2=26^2+20^2-2\cdot26\cdot20\cos30^\circ=1076-520\sqrt3$. Therefore $\boxed{AI=\sqrt{1076-520\sqrt3}}$. The quantity is positive since it is the squared third side of the constructed triangle.

\end{asy}
