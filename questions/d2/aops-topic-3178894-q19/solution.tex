Let $f_n$ count valid length-$n$ strings. A final blue follows any length-$n-1$ string, and a final red is either alone or follows a blue after a valid length-$n-2$ string. Thus $f_n=f_{n-1}+f_{n-2}$, with $f_0=1,f_1=2$. The values through $n=6$ are 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, giving $\boxed{21}$.
