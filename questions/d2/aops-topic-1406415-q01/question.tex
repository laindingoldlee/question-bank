A fair coin is flipped $10$ times. What is the probability of getting heads in at least $6$ of the $10$ flips? (A solution says: by symmetry the probability $p$ of at least $6$ heads equals the probability of at least $6$ tails, and the only other possibility is exactly $5$ heads and $5$ tails, so $\dfrac{63}{256}+2p=1$. Explain why there are no other possibilities.)
