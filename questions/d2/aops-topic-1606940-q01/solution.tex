Substitute $y=3x+2$ into the first equation:
\[
x=4(3x+2)+2=12x+10\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad x=-\dfrac{10}{11}.
\]
Then $y=3\left(-\dfrac{10}{11}\right)+2=-\dfrac{30}{11}+\dfrac{22}{11}=-\dfrac{8}{11}$. The two lines have different slopes, so this is the only intersection:
\[
\boxed{(x,y)=\left(-\dfrac{10}{11},-\dfrac{8}{11}\right)}.
\]
Check: $4\left(-\dfrac{8}{11}\right)+2=-\dfrac{32}{11}+\dfrac{22}{11}=-\dfrac{10}{11}$.
