A solution of $a+b+c+d+e=n$ in nonnegative integers corresponds to a row of $n$ stars and $4$ bars: the stars before the first bar are $a$, those between the first and second bars are $b$, and so on, and the stars after the last bar are $e$. For example, with $n=7$,
\[
\star\star\,|\,|\,\star\,|\,\star\star\star\,|\,\star \quad\longleftrightarrow\quad (a,b,c,d,e)=(2,0,1,3,1).
\]
Every arrangement gives exactly one solution and vice versa. A row of $n+4$ symbols is determined by choosing which $4$ positions hold bars, so there are $\binom{n+4}{4}$ solutions.
\begin{itemize}
\item $n=7$: $\binom{11}{4}=\boxed{330}$.
\item $n=2$: $\binom{6}{4}=\binom{6}{2}=\boxed{15}$ (choosing the $4$ bar positions is the same as choosing the $2$ star positions).
\end{itemize}
