Keep counts according to the final run: $w,q_1,q_2,n_1,n_2,n_3$. Appending a letter updates them to $(q_1+q_2+n_1+n_2+n_3,\ w+n_1+n_2+n_3,\ q_1,\ w+q_1+q_2,\ n_1,\ n_2)$. This accounts for all allowed continuations and excludes precisely the forbidden run lengths. Start at length 1 with $(1,1,0,1,0,0)$. Successive total counts for lengths 1 through 8 are $3,8,21,54,140,362,937,2425$. At length 8 the state vector is $(676,610,236,588,227,88)$, giving $\boxed{2425}$.
