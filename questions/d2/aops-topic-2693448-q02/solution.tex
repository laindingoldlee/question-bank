Let $c$, $b$, $x$ be the numbers in only computer, only biology, and both. Then $c+b+x=25$, $c+b-1=x$ and $x=c+1$. The first two give $2x+1=25$, so $x=\boxed{12}$ (with $c=11$, $b=2$).
