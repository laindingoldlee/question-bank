A river has straight, parallel banks. Masadca walks along one bank from point $A$ to point $B$, taking $20$ seconds at $0.75$ m per second. Point $C$ is on the opposite bank, with $BC$ perpendicular to the banks, and $\angle BAC=57^\circ$. Masadca has a plank of wood $20$ m long and wants to lay it across the river from $B$ to $C$. Is the plank long enough? If not, by how much is it too short? Round to the nearest hundredth of a metre.
