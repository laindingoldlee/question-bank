Use $r=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$ and the angle $\theta$ with $\cos\theta=\dfrac {x}{r}$, $\sin\theta=\dfrac {y}{r}$, choosing the quadrant from the signs of $x,y$:
\[\begin{array}{c|c||c|c}
\text{point}&(r,\theta)&\text{point}&(r,\theta)\\\hline
(-6,8)&(10,\ 126.9^\circ)&(8,-15)&(17,\ 298.1^\circ)\\
(0,-4)&(4,\ 270^\circ)&(2.4,1.8)&(3,\ 36.9^\circ)\\
(-5,-5)&(7.1,\ 225^\circ)&(-7\sqrt2,7\sqrt2)&(14,\ 135^\circ)\\
(3,-3)&(4.2,\ 315^\circ)&(-4.2,-3.6)&(5.5,\ 220.6^\circ)\\
(-\sqrt3,1)&(2,\ 150^\circ)&(5.7,-0.6)&(5.7,\ 354.0^\circ)\\
(-9,9\sqrt3)&(18,\ 120^\circ)&(-3.5,-10.5)&(11.1,\ 251.6^\circ)
\end{array}\]
For example, for $(-6,8)$: $r=10$, and $\theta=180^\circ-\arctan\dfrac{8}{6}\approx180^\circ-53.1^\circ=126.9^\circ$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((-8,0)--(4,0),gray,Arrow); draw((0,-1)--(0,10),gray,Arrow); draw((0,0)--(-6,8),blue+linewidth(0.9)); dot((-6,8));
draw(arc((0,0),2,0,126.87),red,Arrow); label("$(-6,8)$",(-6,8),NW); label("$r=10$",(-3,4),SW); label("$126.9^\circ$",(1.5,2.2),NE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
