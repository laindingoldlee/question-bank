License plates consist of four letters followed by two digits. How many plates have exactly one letter appearing exactly twice among the four letters (the other two letters different from it and from each other) and two different digits? Explain why the count $26\cdot25\cdot24\cdot\dfrac{4!}{2!}$ for the letters is twice too large.
