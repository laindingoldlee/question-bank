Choose the repeated letter ($26$ ways) and the set of two other letters ($\binom{25}{2}=300$ ways). Arrange the multiset $\{L,L,M,N\}$ in $\dfrac{4!}{2!}=12$ ways. Letters: $26\cdot300\cdot12=93600$. Digits: $10\cdot9=90$. Total $93600\cdot90=\boxed{8{,}424{,}000}$.

The product $25\cdot24$ counts the two single letters as an \emph{ordered} pair, but the arrangement factor $\dfrac{4!}{2!}$ already puts them in every order. Each plate is therefore counted twice.
