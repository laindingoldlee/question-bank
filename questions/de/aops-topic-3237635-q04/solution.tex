Since $e,i,v\ge2$ and $live=12$, the product $eiv$ must be $12$ and $l=1$, so $e,i,v$ are $2,2,3$ in some order. But $ei$ divides $8$, forcing $e=i=2,v=3$. Then the second equation becomes $12tw=12$, so $t=w=1$. The first gives $gh=2$, hence $g+h=3$. Therefore the requested sum is $\boxed{2+3+2+1+1+3+1=13}$.
