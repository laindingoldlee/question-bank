Numerator: $1-\dfrac{1}{2}+\dots+\dfrac{1}{19}=\left(1+\dfrac{1}{2}+\dots+\dfrac{1}{19}\right)-2\left(\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{4}+\dots+\dfrac{1}{18}\right)=\dfrac{1}{10}+\dfrac{1}{11}+\dots+\dfrac{1}{19}$.

Denominator: the $k$th term is $\dfrac{1}{k+(k+18)}=\dfrac{1}{2(k+9)}$ for $k=1,\dots,10$, so the denominator is $\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{1}{10}+\dots+\dfrac{1}{19}\right)$.

The ratio is $\boxed{2}$.
