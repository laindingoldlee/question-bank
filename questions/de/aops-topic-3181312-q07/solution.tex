Let counts be $q,d,n,p$. Then $q=d+n,p=\dfrac{d}{2}$ and $q+d+n+p=9$, giving $5d+4n=18$. Since $d$ is even and nonnegative, $d=0$ would make $n$ nonintegral, while $d\ge4$ is too large. Thus $d=2,n=2,q=4,p=1$. These coins total $100+20+10+1=131$, so the nickel count is $\boxed2$.
