Segment $SE$ joins $S(3,15)$ and $E(-9,-1)$. It is rotated $270^\circ$ clockwise about $E$, and the new position of $S$ is called $O$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the area of triangle $SEO$.
\item[(b)] Find the sum of all six coordinates of $S$, $E$ and $O$.
\end{enumerate}
