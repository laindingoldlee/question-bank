\textbf{The value.} $2017_{-10}=2\cdot(-10)^3+0\cdot(-10)^2+1\cdot(-10)+7=-2000-10+7=-2003$.

\textbf{Conversion to base $-8$.} Repeatedly divide by $-8$, always choosing a remainder $r\in\{0,1,\dots,7\}$ (so $n=-8q+r$):
\[
\begin{aligned}
-2003&=(-8)\cdot251+5, && r=5,\\
251&=(-8)\cdot(-31)+3, && r=3,\\
-31&=(-8)\cdot4+1, && r=1,\\
4&=(-8)\cdot0+4, && r=4.
\end{aligned}
\]
Reading the remainders from last to first, $-2003=\boxed{4135_{-8}}$.

Check: $4\cdot(-8)^3+1\cdot(-8)^2+3\cdot(-8)+5=-2048+64-24+5=-2003$.
