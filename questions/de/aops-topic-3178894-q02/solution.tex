If the other side is $s$, equal perimeters give $6(2s)=ns$. Since $s>0$, $\boxed{n=12}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
path h,p;for(int k=0;k<6;++k)h=h--2*dir(60*k);for(int k=0;k<12;++k)p=p--((6,0)+dir(30*k)/(2*sin(pi/12)));draw(h--cycle^^p--cycle);label("hexagon: side $2s$",(0,-3));label("12-gon: side $s$",(6,-3));
\end{asy}
