All three roots must be defined, so $1\le x\le7$. Rewrite the equation as $\sqrt{2x+10}=\sqrt{7-x}+\sqrt{2x-2}$ (both sides nonnegative) and square:
\[
2x+10=(7-x)+(2x-2)+2\sqrt{(7-x)(2x-2)}\iff x+5=2\sqrt{(7-x)(2x-2)} .
\]
Both sides are nonnegative, so squaring again is reversible:
\[
x^2+10x+25=4(-2x^2+16x-14)\iff 9x^2-54x+81=0\iff (x-3)^2=0 .
\]
So $x=\boxed{3}$, and it checks: $\sqrt{16}-\sqrt4=4-2=2=\sqrt4$.
