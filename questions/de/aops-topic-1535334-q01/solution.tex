William walked $12-8=4$ miles more, and this took the extra $40$ minutes. So the common speed is $4$ miles per $\dfrac{2}{3}$ hour, that is $6$ miles per hour, and William's $12$ miles took
\[
\dfrac{12}{6}=\boxed{2\text{ hours}}.
\]
(Eliot's hike took $\dfrac{8}{6}$ hours $=80$ minutes, which is indeed $40$ minutes less.)
