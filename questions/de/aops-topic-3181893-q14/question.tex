Triangle $ABC$ has sides $AB=24,BC=25,CA=26$ and circumcentre $O$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of $AB$. Choose $K\in BC,L\in CA$ with $BK<KC,CL<LA$ and $OM=OK=OL$. Express the area of $MLK$ as $\dfrac{a\sqrt b}{c}$ in reduced form, with squarefree $b$, and find $a+b+c$.
