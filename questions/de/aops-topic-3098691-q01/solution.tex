The identity $Q^2-A^2=\dfrac{(a-b)^2}{4}\ge0$ gives $Q\ge A$, since both are positive. Also $A^2-G^2=\dfrac{(a-b)^2}{4}\ge0$, so $A\ge G$. Finally $H=\dfrac{G^2}{A}\le G$. Equality anywhere requires $a=b$, and then all four means are equal.
