For positive reals $a,b$, define the quadratic, arithmetic, geometric and harmonic means by $Q=\sqrt{\dfrac{a^2+b^2}{2}}$, $A=\dfrac{a+b}{2}$, $G=\sqrt{ab}$ and $H=\dfrac{2ab}{a+b}$. Prove $Q\ge A\ge G\ge H$ and identify equality.
