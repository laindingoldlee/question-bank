Ann sits at a table of size $m$ with probability $\dfrac{m}{21}$. Given her seat, exactly $m-3$ of the remaining $20$ seats are at that table and not adjacent to her: exclude her own seat and the two neighbors. Therefore
\[
\boxed{\Pr=\sum_{m=6}^8\dfrac{m}{21}\dfrac{m-3}{20}
=\dfrac{18+28+40}{420}=\dfrac{43}{210}}.
\]
