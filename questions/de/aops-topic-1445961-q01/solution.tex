The area of a rectangle is length times width. The picture can be divided into $3$ rows of $4$ unit squares (each $1$ cm by $1$ cm):

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((0,0)--(4,0)--(4,3)--(0,3)--cycle,linewidth(1));
for(int i=1;i<4;++i) draw((i,0)--(i,3),gray+dashed);
for(int j=1;j<3;++j) draw((0,j)--(4,j),gray+dashed);
label("$4$ cm",(2,0),S); label("$3$ cm",(0,1.5),W);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

so its area is $4\cdot3=\boxed{12}$ square centimetres.
