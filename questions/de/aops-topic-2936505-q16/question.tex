Let $S_n$ be the sum of reciprocals of the integers between $1$ and $n$ inclusive. Find a triple $(a, b, c)$ of positive integers such that $S_{2017} \cdot  S_{2017} - S_{2016} \cdot S_{2018} = \dfrac{S_a+S_b}{c}$ .
