Write $H=S_{2017}$. Then $S_{2016}=H-\dfrac{1}{2017}$ and $S_{2018}=H+\dfrac{1}{2018}$. Subtracting their product from $H^2$ gives $\dfrac{H+1}{2017\cdot2018}=\dfrac{S_{2017}+S_1}{4070306}$. Thus one triple is $\boxed{(2017,1,4070306)}$.
