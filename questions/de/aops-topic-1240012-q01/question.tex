Consider $(a+c-b)x^2+2cx+(b+c-a)=0$, where $a,b,c$ are distinct real numbers and $a+c-b\ne0$. Suppose both roots are rational. Which of the following must be true?
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(A)] $a$, $b$ and $c$ are rational
\item[(B)] $\dfrac{c}{a-b}$ is rational
\item[(C)] $\dfrac{b}{c-a}$ is rational
\item[(D)] $\dfrac{a}{b-c}$ is rational
\end{enumerate}
