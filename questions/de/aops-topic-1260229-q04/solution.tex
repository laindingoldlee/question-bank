Numerator: $\dfrac{(x+2)+(x-1)}{(x-1)(x+2)}=\dfrac{2x+1}{(x-1)(x+2)}$. Denominator: $\dfrac{2(x-1)-(x+1)}{(x+1)(x-1)}=\dfrac{x-3}{(x+1)(x-1)}$. Dividing, the factors $x-1$ cancel:
\[\boxed{\dfrac{(2x+1)(x+1)}{(x+2)(x-3)}},\qquad x\ne\pm1,-2,3.\]
