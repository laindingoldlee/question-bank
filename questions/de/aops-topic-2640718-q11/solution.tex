Normally the walk takes $\dfrac13$ hour $=20$ minutes. Today the first half mile took $\dfrac14$ hour $=15$ minutes, leaving $5$ minutes $=\dfrac1{12}$ hour for the last half mile. The speed needed is $\dfrac12\div\dfrac1{12}=\boxed{6}$ miles per hour.
