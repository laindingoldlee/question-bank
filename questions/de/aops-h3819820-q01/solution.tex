The median from $C$ divides the triangle into two equal-area triangles, so $[ACM]=\dfrac{2025}{2}$. The centroid divides that median in the ratio $CG:GM=2:1$. Triangles $AMG$ and $AMC$ share base $AM$, and the perpendicular heights from points on $MC$ are proportional to their distances from $M$. Thus
\[[AMG]=\dfrac{MG}{MC}[AMC]=\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{2025}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{675}{2}}.\]
