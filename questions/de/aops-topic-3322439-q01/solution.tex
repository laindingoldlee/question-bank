If the circle radius is $r$, the outer square side is $2r$ and area $4r^2$. The inner square diagonal is $2r$, so its area is $\dfrac{(2r)^2}{2}=2r^2$. The ratio is $\boxed{1:2}$.
\begin{asy}
import geometry;
usepackage("amsmath");
size(270);
draw((-1,-1)--(1,-1)--(1,1)--(-1,1)--cycle);draw(circle((0,0),1));draw((1,0)--(0,1)--(-1,0)--(0,-1)--cycle,blue);label("outer square",(0,-1),S);label("inner square",(0,0));
\end{asy}
