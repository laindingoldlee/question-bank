There are four groups and four special students, and no group may contain two of them, so each group contains exactly one special student. A grouping is therefore determined by choosing, for each special student, the two other students who join her group. Going through the special students in a fixed order, this can be done in
\[\dbinom82\dbinom62\dbinom42\dbinom22=28\cdot15\cdot6\cdot1=\boxed{2520}\]
ways. (The groups are told apart by their special students, so no division by $4!$ is needed.)
