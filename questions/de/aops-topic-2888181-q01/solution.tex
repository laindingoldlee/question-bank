By the change-of-base chain, the product telescopes to $\log_{F(0)}F(N)=\log_2(10N+2)$. The equation becomes $\log_2\log_2(10N+2)=12$, so $10N+2=2^{4096}$. But $2^{4096}$ ends in $6$ (powers $2^{4k}$ end in $6$), so $2^{4096}-2$ ends in $4$ and is not divisible by $10$. Hence $\boxed{\text{no such }N\text{ exists}}$.
