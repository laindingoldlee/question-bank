It suffices to test primes $p\le15$, namely 2,3,5,7,11,13. Their complements are 28,27,25,23,19,17; only the final three are prime. The distinct products are $7\cdot23=161$, $11\cdot19=209$, and $13\cdot17=221$. Their sum is $\boxed{591}$.
