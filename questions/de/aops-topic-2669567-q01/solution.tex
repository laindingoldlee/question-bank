\emph{First sum.} For $|x|\le1$, $\arctan x=\displaystyle\sum_{n\ge0}\dfrac{(-1)^nx^{2n+1}}{2n+1}$. With $x=\dfrac1{\sqrt3}$ we have $x^{2n+1}=\dfrac{1}{3^{n+\dfrac12}}$, so the first sum is $\arctan\dfrac1{\sqrt3}=\dfrac\pi6$, and the first term is $\dfrac{54}{\pi}\cdot\dfrac\pi6=9$.

\emph{Second sum.} $\dfrac{1}{n^2-1}=\dfrac12\left(\dfrac1{n-1}-\dfrac1{n+1}\right)$, so the sum telescopes:
\[\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{n^2-1}=\dfrac12\left(1+\dfrac12\right)=\dfrac34,\]
and the second term is $\dfrac{40}{3}\cdot\dfrac34=10$.

\emph{Third sum.} It is the Taylor series of $\sin x$ at $x=\dfrac{7\pi}{6}$, so it equals $\sin\dfrac{7\pi}{6}=-\dfrac12$, and the third term is $-100\cdot\left(-\dfrac12\right)=50$.

The total is $9+10+50=\boxed{69}$.
