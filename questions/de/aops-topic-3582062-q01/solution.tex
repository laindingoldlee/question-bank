Each quotient is $1$ or $-1$. If $k$ of the three integers are positive, the sum is $k-(3-k)=2k-3$. For $k=0,1,2,3$, this gives $-3,-1,1,3$, all attainable. Hence there are $\boxed{4}$ values.
