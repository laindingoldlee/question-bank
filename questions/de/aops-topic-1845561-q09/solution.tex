\textbf{A way to get $33$.} Buy $25$ chocolates; their $25$ wrappers give $6$ free chocolates with $1$ wrapper left over. Now there are $6+1=7$ wrappers, giving $1$ chocolate with $3$ left over. Then $3+1=4$ wrappers give $1$ more chocolate. In total $25+6+1+1=33$.

\textbf{No more is possible.} Suppose you get $N$ chocolates in total, $F$ of them free, so $N=25+F$. Each free chocolate uses up $4$ wrappers, and the wrapper of the last chocolate you obtain is never exchanged (nothing is obtained after it). So $4F\le N-1=24+F$, i.e. $F\le8$ and $N\le33$.

The answer is $\boxed{33}$.
