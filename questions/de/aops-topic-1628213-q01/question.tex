\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
x&-2&-1&0&1&2\\\hline
y&\dfrac{1}{8}&\dfrac{1}{4}&\dfrac{1}{2}&1&2
\end{array}
\]
Find an exponential rule $y=ab^x$ that fits all five values.
