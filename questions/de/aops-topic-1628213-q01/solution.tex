When $x$ increases by $1$, $y$ doubles every time ($\dfrac{1}{8}\to\dfrac{1}{4}\to\dfrac{1}{2}\to1\to2$), so $b=2$. At $x=0$, $y=a=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Hence
\[
\boxed{y=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot2^x=2^{x-1}},
\]
and indeed $2^{-3}=\dfrac{1}{8}$, $2^{-2}=\dfrac{1}{4}$, $2^{-1}=\dfrac{1}{2}$, $2^0=1$, $2^1=2$.
