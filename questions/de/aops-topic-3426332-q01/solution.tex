The term indexed by $k\ge2$ is $\dfrac{2}{k(k+1)}=2\left(\dfrac{1}{k}-\dfrac{1}{k+1}\right)$. The partial sum through index $N$ is therefore $2\left(\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{N+1}\right)=1-\dfrac{2}{N+1}$. Its limit is $\boxed1$.
