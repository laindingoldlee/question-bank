Take $A=(0,0)$, $B=(1,0)$, $C=(1,1)$, $D=(0,1)$, $E=(t,1)$ and $F=(s,1-s)$. The possible angle directions and the $45^\circ$ difference give $\dfrac{1-s}{s}=\dfrac{1-t}{1+t}$, hence $s=\dfrac{1+t}{2}$. The equality $CF=CE$ gives $(s-1)^2+s^2=(1-t)^2$, which reduces to $t^2-4t+1=0$. The root in $[0,1]$ is $t=2-\sqrt3$. Thus $AE=\sqrt{1+t^2}=\sqrt{8-4\sqrt3}=\boxed{\sqrt6-\sqrt2}$. The other possible orientation of a $45^\circ$ angle would require the endpoint pair $E=C,F=D$, which fails $CF=CE$.
