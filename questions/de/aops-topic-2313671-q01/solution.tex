When the son leaves, the father has driven for $1$ hour, so he is $48$ km ahead. Relative to the father, the son moves forward at $72-48=24$ km per hour, so the gap of $48$ km closes in
\[\dfrac{48}{24}=\boxed{2\text{ hours}}.\]
(Check: in $2$ hours the son drives $144$ km, and in $3$ hours the father drives $3\cdot48=144$ km.) So the son catches up $2$ hours after he leaves, which is $3$ hours after the father left.
