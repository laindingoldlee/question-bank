One hundred mathematicians stand in a line, each wearing a black or a white hat. Each can see the hats of everyone in front of him but not his own hat or the hats behind him; so the person at the back sees $99$ hats and the person at the front sees none. Starting from the back, each person in turn says one word, ``black'' or ``white'', which everyone hears. A person who names the colour of his own hat survives. Before the hats are placed, they may agree on a strategy. Find a strategy that guarantees that at least $99$ of them survive, and explain why no strategy can guarantee that all $100$ survive.
