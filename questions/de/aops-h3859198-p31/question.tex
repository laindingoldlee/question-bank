A sequence \(a_1,a_2,\ldots\) of real numbers satisfies



\[
a_1=1
\]

and



\[
3na_{n+1}^2+(4n+4)a_n^2=(7n+3)a_na_{n+1}
\]

for every positive integer \(n\). Find the smallest possible value of \(a_{100}\).



\[
\textbf{(A)}\ 100
\qquad
\textbf{(B)}\ \dfrac{1600}{27}
\qquad
\textbf{(C)}\ \dfrac{729}{16}
\qquad
\textbf{(D)}\ \dfrac{1143}{32}
\qquad
\textbf{(E)}\ \text{None of these}
\]
