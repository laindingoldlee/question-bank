Since $a_1>0$, induction and the factored recurrence $(3a_{n+1}-4a_n)(na_{n+1}-(n+1)a_n)=0$ show each ratio is either $\dfrac{4}{3}$ or $\dfrac{n+1}{n}$. Every ratio is positive, so minimize their product by independently choosing the smaller one. Use $\dfrac{4}{3}$ for $n=1,2,3$ and $\dfrac{n+1}{n}$ thereafter. The resulting minimum is $(\dfrac{4}{3})^3\dfrac{100}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{1600}{27}}$, choice B.
