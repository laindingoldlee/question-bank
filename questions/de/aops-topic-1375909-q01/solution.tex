Let the common difference be $d$ and the number of terms $k+1$, so $56=20+kd$ and $kd=36$ with $k\ge1$. Since $56>20$, $d$ is a positive integer divisor of $36$, and each such $d$ gives exactly one sequence (with $k=\dfrac{36}{d}$). Since $36=2^2\cdot3^2$ has $(2+1)(2+1)=9$ positive divisors, there are $\boxed{9}$ sequences (from $d=1$: $20,21,\dots,56$, to $d=36$: $20,56$).
