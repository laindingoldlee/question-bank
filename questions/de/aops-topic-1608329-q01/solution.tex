There are $20^3=8000$ equally likely ordered outcomes $(a,b,c)$. We need the number of solutions of $a+b+c=17$ in positive integers; every such solution automatically has $a,b,c\le15\le20$, so the upper limit of the spinner plays no role.

By stars and bars, the positive solutions of $a+b+c=17$ correspond to choosing $2$ of the $16$ gaps between $17$ stars, so there are $\dbinom{16}{2}=120$ of them. The probability is
\[
\dfrac{120}{8000}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{200}}.
\]
