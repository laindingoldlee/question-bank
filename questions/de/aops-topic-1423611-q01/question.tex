A revolver has $6$ chambers, and $2$ of them, chosen at random, contain a bullet. You spin the cylinder and have time to pull the trigger exactly three times, firing three different chambers in turn, before a velociraptor reaches you. Each bullet that is fired stops the velociraptor with probability $0.69$, independently of the others; an empty chamber does nothing. If the velociraptor is not stopped, you are eaten. What is the probability that you are not eaten?
