The three chambers that are fired form a random set of $3$ of the $6$ chambers, so the number $X$ of bullets among them is hypergeometric:
\[
P(X=0)=\dfrac{\binom{4}{3}}{\binom{6}{3}}=\dfrac{4}{20}=\dfrac{1}{5},\qquad
P(X=1)=\dfrac{\binom{2}{1}\binom{4}{2}}{20}=\dfrac{3}{5},\qquad
P(X=2)=\dfrac{\binom{4}{1}}{20}=\dfrac{1}{5}.
\]
You are eaten exactly when every fired bullet misses. A bullet misses with probability $0.31$, so
\[
P(\text{eaten})=\dfrac{1}{5}\cdot1+\dfrac{3}{5}\cdot0.31+\dfrac{1}{5}\cdot0.31^2=0.2+0.186+0.01922=0.40522 .
\]
Hence
\[
P(\text{not eaten})=1-0.40522=\boxed{0.59478}=\dfrac{29739}{50000}.
\]
