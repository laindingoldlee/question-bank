Since $s>0$ and the product is positive, $s-t$ is a positive integer, and $s-t<s$ because $t>0$. As $29$ is prime, its only factorisation into positive integers with the first factor larger is $29=29\cdot1$, so $s=29$ and $s-t=1$, i.e. $t=28$. Hence $s+t=\boxed{57}$.
