To get into your account at a bank in Wonderland you must dial a $7$-digit password (each digit is one of $0,1,\dots,9$) on the phone. The bank disconnects the call as soon as you dial a wrong digit, and you may call again as often as you like. Describe a strategy, and find the smallest number of calls that guarantees getting into the account in the worst case (counting the final, successful call). Explain.
