For $n<5000$ let $N(d)=\left\lfloor\dfrac{4999}{d}\right\rfloor$. By inclusion--exclusion, the number divisible by at least one of $3,5,7$ is
\[N(3)+N(5)+N(7)-N(15)-N(21)-N(35)+N(105)=1666+999+714-333-238-142+47=2713.\]
Every multiple of $35$ is among these, and there are $N(35)=142$ of them. So the answer is $2713-142=\boxed{2571}$.
