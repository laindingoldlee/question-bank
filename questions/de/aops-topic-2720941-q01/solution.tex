The sum of the first $n$ odd numbers is $1+3+\cdots+(2n-1)=n^2$. By AM--GM,
\[\sqrt[n]{1\cdot3\cdot5\cdots(2n-1)}\le\dfrac{1+3+\cdots+(2n-1)}{n}=\dfrac{n^2}{n}=n,\]
and raising to the $n$th power gives $1\cdot3\cdots(2n-1)\le n^n$. $\blacksquare$ (Equality holds only for $n=1$.)
