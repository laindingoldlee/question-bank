Multiply both sides by $19!$. The left side becomes $\displaystyle\sum_{k=2}^{9}\binom{19}{k}$ and the right side becomes $19N$.

Since $\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{19}\binom{19}{k}=2^{19}$ and $\binom{19}{k}=\binom{19}{19-k}$, we get $\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{9}\binom{19}{k}=2^{18}$. Hence
\[19N=2^{18}-\binom{19}{0}-\binom{19}{1}=262144-20=262124,\]
so $N=13796$. The greatest integer less than $\dfrac N{100}=137.96$ is $\boxed{137}$.
