A rectangular vegetable bed is bordered by $n$ wooden beams, each $1$ yard long, laid end to end without cutting, so that each side of the rectangle is a whole number of yards. The largest possible area of the bed is $16$ square yards. What is the largest possible area with $n+6$ beams?
