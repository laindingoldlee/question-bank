For each positive integer $i$, let $f(i)$ be its largest perfect-square divisor. How many positive integers $n<1024$ make $Q_n=n!\prod_{i=1}^{n}f(i)^{-\dfrac{3}{2}}$ an integer divisible by $4$ but not by $8$?
