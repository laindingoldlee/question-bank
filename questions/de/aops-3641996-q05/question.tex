Circles $P$ and $Q$ have diameters $AB$ and $CD$, respectively, and they share a common tangent $AD$. Circle $M$ is constructed, concentric to circle $P$, to pass through point $D$. Circle $N$ is constructed, concentric to circle $Q$, to pass through point $A$. Given that $AB = 4CD$, the positive difference in area between circles $M$ and $N$ can be expressed as an integer multiple, $k$, of the area of circle $Q$. Find the value of $k$.
