Let circle Q have radius r, so P has radius 4r, and let the common tangent segment AD have length t. The radii to tangency points are perpendicular to AD, giving $R_M^2=16r^2+t^2$ and $R_N^2=r^2+t^2$. Their area difference is $15\pi r^2$, exactly $\boxed{15}$ times the area of Q.

import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),D=(10,0),P=(0,4),Q=(10,1);draw(A--D);draw(circle(P,4));draw(circle(Q,1));draw(P--D^^Q--A,dashed);label("$A$",A,(0,-1));label("$D$",D,(0,-1));label("$P$",P,(0,1));label("$Q$",Q,(0,1));
\end{asy}
