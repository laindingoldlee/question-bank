Let $s=a+c$. The sum is $101s+20b$. If $s<10$, its units digit is odd only when $s$ is odd, but then the tens digit $2b$ modulo $10$ is even, impossible. Hence $10\le s\le17$. The units digit forces $s$ odd, so $s=11,13,15,17$ and the carry to the tens column is one. The tens digit of $2b+1$ is odd. Put $t=\left\lfloor\dfrac{2b+1}{10}\right\rfloor$, which is $0$ or $1$. The hundreds digit comes from $s+t$, and must be odd; because $s$ is odd, $t=0$ is necessary, so $b\le4$. Since $c<b\le4$ and $a\le9$, $s\le12$, leaving $s=11$. With $a+c=11$ and $c<b\le4$, the possible pairs $(a,c)$ are $(9,2)$ or $(8,3)$. They yield $(a,b,c)=(9,3,2),(9,4,2),(8,4,3)$. Direct sums are $1171,1191,1191$, respectively. Thus the numbers are $\boxed{843,932,942}$.
