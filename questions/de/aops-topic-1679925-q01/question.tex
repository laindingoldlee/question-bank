Claire jogs at $6$ miles per hour and Megan at $5$ miles per hour. They start together at their campsite and jog to an outpost $18$ miles away. When Claire gets there, she immediately turns around and heads back towards Megan. How many miles from the outpost are they when they meet? Express your answer as a mixed number.
