Until they meet, Claire has run to the outpost and back a distance $x$, while Megan is still $x$ miles short of it. So together they have covered $18+x+18-x=36$ miles, at a combined $11$ miles per hour: they meet after $\dfrac{36}{11}$ hours. In that time Megan has run $5\cdot\dfrac{36}{11}=\dfrac{180}{11}$ miles, so they are
\[
18-\dfrac{180}{11}=\dfrac{18}{11}=\boxed{1\dfrac{7}{11}\text{ miles}}
\]
from the outpost. (Check: Claire runs $6\cdot\dfrac{36}{11}=\dfrac{216}{11}=18+\dfrac{18}{11}$ miles.)
