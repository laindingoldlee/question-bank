The first sequence consists of the numbers $\equiv2\pmod3$ from $2$ to $599$; the second of the numbers $\equiv1\pmod4$ from $5$ to $801$. Since $5$ is in both and $\operatorname{lcm}(3,4)=12$, the numbers in both residue classes are those $\equiv5\pmod{12}$. They must also lie between $5$ and $599$: these are $5,17,29,\dots,593$, and there are $\dfrac{593-5}{12}+1=\boxed{50}$ of them.
