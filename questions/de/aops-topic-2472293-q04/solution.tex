We have $\dfrac1{n^{-1}}=\dfrac{1}{\ \dfrac1n\ }=n$ and $1^{-n}=1$, so $\dfrac{n}{1^{-n}}=n$. Hence the expression equals
\[n+\dfrac1n+n+n=\boxed{3n+\dfrac1n}=\dfrac{3n^2+1}{n}.\]
