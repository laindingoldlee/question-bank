Assume $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{n^2}=\dfrac{\pi^2}{6}$. (a) Find $\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\dfrac{1}{(2k+1)^2}$. (b) The first $N$ terms of $4\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\dfrac{(-1)^k}{2k+1}$ approximate $\pi$ with error at most $\dfrac{4}{2N+1}$. What is the smallest positive integer $N$ for which this bound is strictly less than $0.001$?
