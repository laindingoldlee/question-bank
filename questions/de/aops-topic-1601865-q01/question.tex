A ball is thrown in a straight line from a point $P$ strictly inside a convex polygon and bounces off the wall it hits according to the law of reflection (angle of incidence equals angle of reflection). Is there a convex polygon and a point $P$ inside it such that, wherever on the walls the ball is aimed, it never comes back through $P$ after exactly one bounce? Prove your answer.
