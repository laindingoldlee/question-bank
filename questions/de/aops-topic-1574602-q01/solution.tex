Record the total distance travelled at each bounce. The first fall is $50$ ft. After the $k$-th hit the ball rises to $\dfrac{50}{2^k}$ ft and falls the same distance before the next hit, adding $2\cdot\dfrac{50}{2^k}$ ft:
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
\text{hit} & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 5\\ \hline
\text{distance at the hit (ft)} & 50 & 50+50=100 & 100+25=125 & 125+12.5=137.5 & 137.5+6.25=143.75
\end{array}
\]
At $140$ feet the ball has passed its $4$th hit ($137.5$ ft) but not its $5$th ($143.75$ ft): it is in the air, $2.5$ ft above the ground on its way up. So it has hit the ground $\boxed{4}$ times.
