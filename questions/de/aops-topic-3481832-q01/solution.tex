For $n$ teams the total is $\binom n2=\dfrac{n(n-1)}{2}$, increasing with $n$. But $\binom{64}{2}=2016<2024<2080=\binom{65}{2}$. Therefore $\boxed{\text{no}}$ integer number of teams works.
