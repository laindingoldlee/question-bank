You have ten pigeons to give away. Your first friend takes as many as she likes. You then give the rest to your other friends. If you give each of the other friends $3$ pigeons, $5$ pigeons are left over; if you give each of them $5$ pigeons, $3$ are left over. How many pigeons did your first friend take?
