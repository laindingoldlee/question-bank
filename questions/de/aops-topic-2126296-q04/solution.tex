Let $k$ be the number of other friends and $R$ the number of pigeons left after the first friend has chosen. Then
\[R=3k+5\qquad\text{and}\qquad R=5k+3 .\]
Subtracting, $2k=2$, so $k=1$ and $R=8$. The first friend took $10-8=\boxed{2}$ pigeons.

(Check: with one other friend and $8$ pigeons, giving $3$ leaves $5$ and giving $5$ leaves $3$.)
