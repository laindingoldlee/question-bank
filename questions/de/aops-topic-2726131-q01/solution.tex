The probability that all five numbers are different is $\dfrac{7\cdot6\cdot5\cdot4\cdot3}{7^5}=\dfrac{2520}{16807}=\dfrac{360}{2401}$. So the answer is $1-\dfrac{360}{2401}=\boxed{\dfrac{2041}{2401}}$.
