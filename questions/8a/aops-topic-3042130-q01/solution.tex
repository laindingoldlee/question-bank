The first fall takes $\sqrt{\dfrac{20}{9.8}}=\dfrac{10}{7}$ seconds. The successive rebound heights are $10p,10p^2,\ldots$, so their one-way travel times are $\dfrac{10}{7}\sqrt p,\dfrac{10}{7}p,\ldots$. Each rebound contributes an upward and a downward trip. Since $\sqrt p<1$, the geometric series gives\[T=\dfrac{10}{7}\left(1+2\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}p^{\dfrac{k}{2}}\right)=\boxed{\dfrac{10}{7}\dfrac{1+\sqrt p}{1-\sqrt p}\text{ seconds}}.\] For $p=0$, only the initial fall contributes.
