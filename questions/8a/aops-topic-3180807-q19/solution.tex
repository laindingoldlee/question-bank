Rearrangement gives $(3x-2p)(3y-p)=3p^2$. Also $3x(3y-p)=p(p+6y)>0$, so both factors are positive. If $p\ne3$, neither factor is divisible by $3$, contradicting their product. Thus $p=3$, and the equation becomes $(x-2)(y-1)=3$. Its positive factor pairs give $(x,y)=(3,4)$ or $(5,2)$. The corresponding sums are $34$ and $38$, so the maximum is $\boxed{38}$.
