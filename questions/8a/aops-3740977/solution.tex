Use barycentric masses at the vertices. The ratio $AP:PD=1:1$ gives $m_A=m_B+m_C$, and $BP:PE=3:1$ gives $m_A+m_C=3m_B$. Hence $m_A:m_B:m_C=2:1:1$, so $CP:PF=3:1$. Since $CF=20$, $CP=15$. Put $P$ at the origin and write the vertex vectors as $a,b,c$. Then $2a+b+c=0$, $|a|=6,|b|=9,|c|=15$. Squaring $c=-2a-b$ gives $225=144+81+4a\cdot b$, so $a\perp b$. The doubled triangle area is $|(b-a)\times(c-a)|=4|a\times b|=216$. Therefore $\boxed{[ABC]=108}$.
