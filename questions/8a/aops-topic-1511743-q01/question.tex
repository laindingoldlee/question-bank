There are $5$ red, $3$ white and $2$ blue marbles in a bag. If two marbles are selected at random (without replacement), what is the probability that both are red?

(a) $\dfrac{2}{9}$\qquad (b) $\dfrac{1}{4}$\qquad (c) $\dfrac{1}{3}$\qquad (d) $\dfrac{1}{2}$

One student reasoned: ``the first marble has a $\dfrac{1}{5}$ chance of being red and the second a $\dfrac{4}{9}$ chance, so the answer is $\dfrac{4}{45}$, which is not a choice.'' Find the error.
