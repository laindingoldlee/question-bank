James walks along a street at a constant speed. Buses run in both directions along the street, all at the same constant speed, which is greater than James's speed; in each direction they leave the terminal at the end of the street at equal time intervals, the same interval for both directions. Every $12$ minutes a bus travelling in the same direction as James overtakes him, and every $4$ minutes a bus travelling in the opposite direction passes him. How many minutes long is the interval between buses?
