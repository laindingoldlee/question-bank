Positivity and $f(1)^2=f(1)$ imply $f(1)=1$. Injectivity forces $f(3),f(37)$ to be distinct integers at least $2$. Since $999=3^3\cdot37$, $f(999)=f(3)^3f(37)$. If $f(3)=2$, the least product is $2^3\cdot3=24$; if $f(3)\ge3$, the product is at least $3^3\cdot2=54$. The value $24$ is achievable: permute primes by sending $3\mapsto2$, $37\mapsto3$, $2\mapsto37$, and fixing all others, then extend multiplicatively. Unique factorisation makes this extension injective. Thus the minimum is $\boxed{24}$.
