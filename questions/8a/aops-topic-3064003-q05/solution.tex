For any particular non-king, its relative position among itself and the four kings is uniform among five positions. It comes before all four kings with probability $\dfrac{1}{5}$. Let an indicator record this event for each of the 16 non-kings. By linearity of expectation, the required mean is $16\cdot\dfrac{1}{5}=\boxed{\dfrac{16}{5}}$.
