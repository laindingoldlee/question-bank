Let the numbers be $x$ and $y$. Then $6(x+4)=5(y+4)$ and $2(x-4)=y-4$. The second gives $y=2x-4$; substituting, $6x+24=10x$, so $x=6$ and $y=8$. The numbers are $\boxed{6\text{ and }8}$ (check: $10:12=5:6$ and $2:4=1:2$).
