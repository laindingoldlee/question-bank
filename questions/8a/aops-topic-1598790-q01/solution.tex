If $a$ is uniform on $[-1,1]$, then $u=|a|$ is uniform on $[0,1]$: for $0\le s\le1$, $P(|a|\le s)=\dfrac{2s}{2}=s$. So $u=|a|$, $v=|b|$, $w=|c|$ are independent and uniform on $[0,1]$, and the point $(u,v,w)$ is uniform in the unit cube.

The complementary event $u+v+w<\dfrac{1}{2}$ is the corner tetrahedron with three perpendicular edges of length $\dfrac{1}{2}$, of volume
\[
\dfrac{1}{6}\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^3=\dfrac{1}{48}.
\]
The cube has volume $1$, so
\[
P\left(|a|+|b|+|c|\ge\dfrac{1}{2}\right)=1-\dfrac{1}{48}=\boxed{\dfrac{47}{48}}.
\]
