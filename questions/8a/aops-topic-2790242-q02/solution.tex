Divide by $5^n$: we need $f(n)=\left(\dfrac35\right)^n+\left(\dfrac45\right)^n=1$. Since both bases lie in $(0,1)$, $f$ is strictly decreasing in $n$. Also $f(2)=\dfrac{9}{25}+\dfrac{16}{25}=1$. Hence $f(n)>1$ for $n<2$ and $f(n)<1$ for $n>2$, and the only solution is $\boxed{n=2}$. (This covers negative $n$ and $n=0$ too.)
