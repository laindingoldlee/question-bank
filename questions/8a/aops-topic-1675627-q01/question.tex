Let $p(n)$ denote the number of partitions of the positive integer $n$ (ways of writing $n$ as a sum of positive integers, disregarding order), and let $p(n,m)$ denote the number of partitions of $n$ into exactly $m$ parts. Prove that $p(n)=p(2n,n)$.
