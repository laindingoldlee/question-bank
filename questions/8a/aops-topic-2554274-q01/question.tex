The numbers $1,2,\dots,100$ are written in a row. In the first round, every $9$th number in the row is removed (the numbers in positions $9,18,27,\dots$). In the next round every $8$th number of the remaining row is removed, then every $7$th, every $6$th, and so on, the last round removing every $2$nd number. What is the sum of the numbers that remain at the end?
