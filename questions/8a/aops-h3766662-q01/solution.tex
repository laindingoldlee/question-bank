The two factors $x-y,x+y$ have the same parity and multiply to $2024$. They must both be even. Put $u=\dfrac{x-y}{2}$, $v=\dfrac{x+y}{2}$, giving $uv=506=2\cdot11\cdot23$ with $u<v$. Its factor pairs are $(1,506),(2,253),(11,46),(22,23)$, each producing positive $x=u+v,y=v-u$. Thus there are $\boxed4$ representations.
