For altitude $h$, the side is $\dfrac{2h}{\sqrt3}$ and the area is $\dfrac{h^2}{\sqrt3}$. So $h^2=72\sqrt3=36\sqrt{12}$ and $h=\boxed{6\sqrt[4]{12}}\approx11.17$.
