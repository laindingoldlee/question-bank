The number must be a multiple of $\operatorname{lcm}(1,2,3,4,5,6)=60$. A multiple $60k$ divides $720$ exactly when $k$ divides $12$. The three-digit candidates are $120,180,240,360,720$; only $\boxed{120}$ has digit sum $3$.
