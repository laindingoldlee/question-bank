Multiplicativity gives $f(2^r)=2^r$. Fix $n$ and for each positive $k$ choose $r$ with $2^r\le n^k<2^{r+1}$. Monotonicity gives $2^r\le f(n^k)=f(n)^k<2^{r+1}$. Thus $\dfrac{1}{2} < (\dfrac{f (n)}{n})^k < 2$ for every $k$. This is impossible if $\dfrac{f (n)}{n}$ is greater or less than $1$, so $f(n)=n$. Therefore the sum is $1+\cdots+20=\boxed{210}$.
