The digits are $2,0,1,2$. They can be arranged in $\dfrac{4!}{2!}=12$ different orders. The arrangements starting with $0$ are not $4$-digit numbers; there are $\dfrac{3!}{2!}=3$ of them (the digits $2,1,2$ after the $0$). Every other arrangement starts with $1$ or $2$ and is greater than $1000$ (the smallest is $1022$). So there are $12-3=9$ such numbers: the answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(D)}\ 9}$.
