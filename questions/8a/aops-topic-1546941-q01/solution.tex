The square has side $\sqrt8=2\sqrt2$. All four vertices lie on the circle, and the angle subtended by a diagonal at a vertex is a right angle, so the diagonal of the square is a diameter of the circle.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real q=sqrt(2);
draw(circle((0,0),2),linewidth(1));
draw((-q,-q)--(q,-q)--(q,q)--(-q,q)--cycle,linewidth(1));
draw((-q,-q)--(q,q),dashed+blue);
dot((0,0));label("$O$",(0,0),SE);
label("$4$",(0.5,0.5),2*NW,blue);
label("$2\sqrt{2}$",(0,-q),N);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The diagonal is $2\sqrt2\cdot\sqrt2=4$, so the radius is $2$, and the area of the circle is
\[
\pi\cdot2^2=\boxed{4\pi}\text{ square units}.
\]
