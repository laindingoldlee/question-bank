Start with a square of side length $1$. A square of side length $\dfrac12$ is drawn with its sides parallel to those of the first square and its centre at the midpoint of the top side of the first square, so that this side bisects it. Then a square of side length $\dfrac14$ is drawn in the same way, centred at the midpoint of the top side of the square of side $\dfrac12$, and so on without end, each square having half the side length of the previous one. What is the area of the whole figure? Express your answer as a common fraction.
