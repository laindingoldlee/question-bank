Events $A$, $B$ and $C$ are independent, and their probabilities $p(A)$, $p(B)$, $p(C)$ are the three roots of a cubic polynomial $P(x)$ with leading coefficient $1$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that the probability that at least one of $A$, $B$, $C$ occurs is $1-P(1)$.
\item[(b)] Find this probability when $P(x)=x^3-\dfrac{13}{12}x^2+\dfrac38x-\dfrac1{24}$.
\end{enumerate}
