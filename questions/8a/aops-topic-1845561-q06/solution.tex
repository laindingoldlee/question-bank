A factor has the form $2^x3^y5^z$ with $0\le x\le6$, $0\le y\le2$, $0\le z\le3$. Divisibility by $9$ forces $y=2$ ($1$ choice); not being divisible by $25$ forces $z\in\{0,1\}$ ($2$ choices); $x$ is free ($7$ choices). The count is $7\cdot1\cdot2=\boxed{14}$.
