Put $m=f(k)$. Then $k$ divides the positive integer $m(m-1)$, so $k\le m(m-1)$. If $m\le32$, this gives $\dfrac{m}{k}\ge\dfrac{1}{m-1}\ge\dfrac{1}{31}$. If $m\ge33$, then $\dfrac{m}{k}\ge\dfrac{33}{1000}>\dfrac{1}{31}$. The bound is attained at $k=992=32\cdot31$: $32$ is admissible, while for $2\le m<32$ the positive product $m(m-1)<992$ cannot be divisible by $992$. Thus $f(992)=32$ and the minimum is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{31}}$.
