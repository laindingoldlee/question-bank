Write the integers increasingly as $ a<b<c<d $. The smallest and largest sums give $ a+b=23,c+d=35 $, so their total is 58. The sum of all six pair sums is three times this,174. The five listed values sum to 145, so the repeated value is 29. Also $ a+c=26 $ and $ b+d=32 $, leaving $ a+d=b+c=29 $. Solving $ a+b=23,a+c=26,b+c=29 $ yields $ a=10,b=13,c=16 $, and $ d=19 $. The largest is $\boxed{19}$.
