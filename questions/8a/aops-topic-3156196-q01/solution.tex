Try a new choice for each still-unsolved question at each check. After three checks, any unsolved question has exactly one untried choice, which must be correct. Two checks cannot guarantee success: for even one question both tried choices may be wrong, leaving two indistinguishable possibilities. Hence $\boxed3 $, choice A.
