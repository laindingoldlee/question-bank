A quiz has four questions with four choices each. A check tells which currently selected answers are correct, without revealing the right choice for an incorrect answer. Checks inspect all four questions at once. What is the smallest number of checks that guarantees you can then select all answers correctly, without a final confirmation check? Choices: (A) $ 3 $, (B) $ 4 $, (C) $ 12 $, (D) $ 16 $, (E) $ 64 $.
