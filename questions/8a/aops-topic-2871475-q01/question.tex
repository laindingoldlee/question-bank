A desert is $800$ miles wide, and there is unlimited fuel at one edge. A truck's tank holds enough fuel for $500$ miles, and the truck can leave any amount of fuel in caches anywhere in the desert and collect it later. What is the least total distance the truck must drive to get from the fuelled edge to the other side?
