Let $u=\dfrac{1}{x}$ and $v=\dfrac{1}{y}$. Then $u+v=1$ and $2u+3v=4$. Subtracting twice the first equation from the second gives $v=2$, and then $u=-1$. So $x=\dfrac{1}{u}=-1$ and $y=\dfrac{1}{v}=\dfrac{1}{2}$:
\[
\boxed{(x,y)=\left(-1,\dfrac{1}{2}\right)}.
\]
Check: $-1+2=1$ and $-2+6=4$.
