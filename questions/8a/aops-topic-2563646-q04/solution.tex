Since $11\cdot9=99<100\le110=11\cdot10$ and $11\cdot81=891\le900<902=11\cdot82$, the multiples are $11\cdot10,11\cdot11,\dots,11\cdot81$. There are $81-10+1=\boxed{72}$ of them.
