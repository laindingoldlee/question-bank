There are $21$ students in a $10$-minute class. During the class each student sleeps for exactly one minute (one unbroken interval of length $1$ minute, inside the class time, at a time that may be different for different students). Consider the following ``proof'' that at some moment at least $3$ students are asleep:

\emph{``Two students sleep for the first minute, two during the second, and so on, for a total of $20$ students sleeping for a minute during the $10$ minute class. The remaining student must sleep as well, so there must be three students sleeping during the same minute.''}

Explain why this proof is wrong, and give a correct proof.
