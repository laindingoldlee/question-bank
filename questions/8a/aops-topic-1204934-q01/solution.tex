The pattern of vowel and consonant positions is uniformly random: the $3$ vowel positions form a uniformly random $3$-subset of the $9$ positions. So we need the probability that $3$ random positions out of $9$ are pairwise non-adjacent. The number of non-adjacent $3$-subsets of $9$ positions is $\dbinom{9-3+1}{3}=\dbinom73=35$, out of $\dbinom93=84$. The probability is $\dfrac{35}{84}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{12}}$.
