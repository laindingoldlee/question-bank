The divisor factors as $(x-1)(x+1)(x-2)$. A remainder $R$ has degree at most 2 and must satisfy $R(-1)=0$, $R(1)=54$, and $R(2)=M$, where $M=2^{100}+27\cdot2^{37}+29\cdot2^{10}-3$. Start with $27(x+1)$, which has the correct values at $\pm1$. Adding $a(x^2-1)$ preserves those values. At 2, it becomes $81+3a$, so $a=\dfrac{M-81}{3}$. Therefore\[\boxed{R(x)=\dfrac{M-81}{3}(x^2-1)+27(x+1)}.\]This polynomial has degree at most 2 and agrees with $P$ at all three distinct roots of the divisor, so the divisor divides $P-R$ and the remainder is verified.
