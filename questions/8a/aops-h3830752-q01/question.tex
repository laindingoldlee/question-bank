Nursultan wrote a natural number $X$ on the board and asked each of his seven students to state one property of this number. Here are the answers he heard.

$\bullet$ Ali: ``The number $X$ is at least 30 but not greater than 130.''

$\bullet$ Adilzhan: ``The number $X$ is not a prime number.''

$\bullet$ Sanzhar: ``The last digit of the number $X$ is not equal to 5.''

$\bullet$ Eldar: ``The number $X$ is odd.''

$\bullet$ Arsen: ``The number $X$ is not a perfect square of a natural number.''

$\bullet$ Alimzhan: ``The sum of the digits of the number $X$ is even.''

$\bullet$ Aldinash: ``Not all digits in the representation of the number $X$ are the same.''

It is known that none of the students made a mistake. What values can $X$ take? List all possible answers (in this problem, you may simply list the answers).
