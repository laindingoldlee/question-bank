Use $2^n=2\cdot2^{n-1}$ and $(a^b)^c=a^{bc}$:
\[
16^{2^{n-1}}=\left(4^2\right)^{2^{n-1}}=4^{2\cdot2^{n-1}}=4^{2^n},\qquad 4^{2^{n-1}}=\left(2^2\right)^{2^{n-1}}=2^{2\cdot2^{n-1}}=2^{2^n}.
\]
Adding the two equalities gives the identity. $\blacksquare$
