Parentheses first: $2\cdot5\div4=\dfrac52$ and $2-4=-2$. Then the power $3^2=9$, then the product $-2\cdot3=-6$. Finally, from left to right,
\[\dfrac52+9-6+3-2=\boxed{\dfrac{13}{2}}.\]
