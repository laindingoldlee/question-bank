By Vieta, $a+b=-5$ and $ab=3$, so both roots are negative. Then $\sqrt a=i\sqrt{-a}$ and $\sqrt b=i\sqrt{-b}$, so
\[\sqrt a\,\sqrt b=i^2\sqrt{(-a)(-b)}=-\sqrt{ab}=-\sqrt3.\]
Therefore
\[\left(\sqrt a+\sqrt b\right)^2=a+b+2\sqrt a\sqrt b=\boxed{-5-2\sqrt3}.\]
(The tempting answer $-5+2\sqrt3$ uses $\sqrt a\sqrt b=\sqrt{ab}$, which is false when $a$ and $b$ are both negative.)
