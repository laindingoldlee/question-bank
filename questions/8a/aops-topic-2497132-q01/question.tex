Find all four-digit numbers $\overline{abcd}$ such that
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] $a$, $c$ and $d$ are prime numbers;
\item[(ii)] $\dfrac{a}{b+2}\le1\le\dfrac{c}{b+2}$;
\item[(iii)] $\dfrac{c+5}{d}\le1$.
\end{enumerate}
