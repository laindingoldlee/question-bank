Alice and Bob alternately play with $n\ge1$ stones, Alice first. On each turn a fair die with labels $1,1,2,2,3,3$ is rolled. The player chooses to remove at least one and at most the roll (and at most the remaining stones). Taking the last stone wins. Under optimal play: (a) find Alice's win probability for $n=2$; (b) find the least $n$ favouring Bob; (c) determine all $n$ favouring Bob.
