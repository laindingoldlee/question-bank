The positive root is $ a_n=1+2\sqrt{a_{n-1}-1}$: when $ a_{n-1}\ge2 $, the other root is negative. For $ 1<t<5 $, $ t<1+2\sqrt{t-1}<5 $, since $(t-1)^2<4(t-1)$. Thus the sequence increases and remains below 5. Its limit $ L $ exists and satisfies $ L=1+2\sqrt{L-1}$, yielding $ L=1 $ or 5; since $ L\ge2 $, it is 5. Any $ k<5 $ is eventually exceeded, while 5 is always a strict upper bound. The answer is $\boxed5 $.
