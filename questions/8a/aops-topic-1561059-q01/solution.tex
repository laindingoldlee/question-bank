Let $\gamma$ be the angle between the sides $a$ and $b$. Then
\[
\text{Area}=\dfrac{1}{2}ab\sin\gamma\le\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot1\cdot2\cdot1=1 .
\]
Equality needs $a=1$, $b=2$ and $\gamma=90^\circ$. Then $c=\sqrt{1^2+2^2}=\sqrt5\approx2.24$, which does satisfy $2\le c\le3$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair Cc=(0,0),A=(2,0),B=(0,1);
draw(Cc--A--B--cycle,linewidth(1));
real s=0.12; draw((s,0)--(s,s)--(0,s));
label("$b=2$",(Cc+A)/2,S);label("$a=1$",(Cc+B)/2,W);label("$c=\sqrt5$",(A+B)/2,NE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

So the maximum area is $\boxed{1}$, answer $\textbf{(A)}$.
