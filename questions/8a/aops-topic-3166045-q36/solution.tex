Use $\log_2\binom{1000}{500}=\dfrac{\ln(1000!)-2\ln(500!)}{\ln2}$. Applying the explicit Stirling bounds $L_n+\dfrac{1}{12n+1}<\ln(n!)<L_n+\dfrac{1}{12n}$, where $L_n=(n+\dfrac{1}{2})\ln n-n+\dfrac{1}{2}\ln(2\pi)$, bounds this expression between $994.690998$ and $994.691001$. It therefore rounds to $\boxed{994.691}$.
