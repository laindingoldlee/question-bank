Line $m$ has equation $6x-3y=7$. Line $n$ is perpendicular to $m$ and passes through the point $(6,2)$. Line $k$ has slope $5$ and $y$-intercept $1$. What is the $x$-coordinate of the point where $n$ and $k$ intersect? Express your answer as a common fraction.
