By definition, $x=\dfrac ab$ is the number with $bx=a$, and $y=\dfrac cd$ is the number with $dy=c$. Then
\[(bd)(xy)=(bx)(dy)=ac,\]
so $xy$ is the number which multiplied by $bd$ gives $ac$, that is, $xy=\dfrac{ac}{bd}$. $\blacksquare$
