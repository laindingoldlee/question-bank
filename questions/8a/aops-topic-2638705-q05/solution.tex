Completing squares gives $3(x-2)^2+(y+\dfrac{5}{2})^2-\dfrac{13}{4}$. The square terms are nonnegative and vanish at $x=2,y=-\dfrac{5}{2}$. Thus the minimum is $\boxed{-\dfrac{13}{4}}$.
