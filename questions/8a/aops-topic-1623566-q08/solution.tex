$\left(2^2\right)^{5713}=4^{5713}$. The units digits of the powers of $4$ alternate: $4^1=4$, $4^2=16$, $4^3=64$, \dots, giving $4$ for odd exponents and $6$ for even exponents (since $4\cdot4=16$ ends in $6$ and $6\cdot4=24$ ends in $4$). The exponent $5713$ is odd, so the units digit is $\boxed{4}$.
