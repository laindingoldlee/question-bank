Moving along the diagonal, the line enters a new tile each time it crosses a vertical or horizontal grid line. It crosses $17-1=16$ interior vertical lines and $10-1=9$ interior horizontal lines, and it never crosses both at once at an interior lattice point, since $\gcd(10,17)=1$. Starting in one tile and entering a new tile at each of the $16+9=25$ crossings gives $1+25=\boxed{26}$ tiles. (In general the answer is $m+n-\gcd(m,n)$.)
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
for(int i=0;i<=17;++i) draw((i,0)--(i,10),gray(0.7));
for(int j=0;j<=10;++j) draw((0,j)--(17,j),gray(0.7));
draw((0,0)--(17,10),red+linewidth(1)); draw((0,0)--(17,0)--(17,10)--(0,10)--cycle);
label("$17$",(8.5,0),S); label("$10$",(0,5),W);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
