In a right triangle $ABC$, $AB=1$, $\angle A=90^\circ$, and $\angle B=60^\circ$. Draw the semicircle with diameter $BC$ containing $A$. Let $M,N$ be the midpoints of the arcs $AC,AB$ on this semicircle. Find the area of $ANM$.
