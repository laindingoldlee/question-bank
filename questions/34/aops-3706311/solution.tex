Mary immediately wins with probability $\dfrac{4}{6}$, immediately loses with probability $\dfrac{1}{6}$, and ties with probability $\dfrac{1}{6}$. After a tie both players restart symmetrically, so Mary then wins with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Thus the probability is $\dfrac{4}{6}+\dfrac{1}{6}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{4}}$. The value $\dfrac{2}{3}$ counts only an immediate win. If Mark instead kept his two and only Mary rerolled ties, a different rule would give $\dfrac{4}{5}$.
