Let $a$ be the probability if someone other than six starts, and $b$ if six starts. A nondouble roll sends play to six in $10$ ordered outcomes, and to another player in $20$. Thus $a=\dfrac{10b+20a}{36}$. If six starts, there is additionally a $\dfrac{6}{36}$ immediate win, so $b=\dfrac{1}{6}+a$. Substitution gives $36a=10(a+\dfrac{1}{6})+20a$, hence $a=\dfrac{5}{18}$ and $b=\dfrac{4}{9}$. The probability of never rolling doubles is zero. Therefore the answers are $\boxed{\dfrac{5}{18}}$ for an initial player other than six and $\boxed{\dfrac{4}{9}}$ for six.
