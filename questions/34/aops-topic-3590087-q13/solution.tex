Read the occupancy counts as base-$4$ digits, box $1$ being the units digit. Incrementing the first digit not equal to $3$ and resetting preceding digits to zero is exactly addition of $1$ in base $4$. Thus the occupancies after $2016$ turns are the digits of $2016=1\cdot4^5+3\cdot4^4+3\cdot4^3+2\cdot4^2$, namely $(133200)_4$. Their sum is $1+3+3+2=\boxed9$ balls.
