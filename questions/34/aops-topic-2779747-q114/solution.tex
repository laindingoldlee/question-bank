Let $ p_k $ be the probability A obtains $ k $ more points before B's first point, starting just before A's attempt. For $ k=1 $, success can occur immediately with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$; if both miss, probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$, the state repeats. Thus $ p_1=\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{4}p_1 $, yielding $ p_1=\dfrac{2}{3}$. For $ k\ge2 $, A scoring and B missing contributes $\dfrac{1}{4}p_{k-1}$, while both missing contributes $\dfrac{1}{4}p_k $. Thus $ p_k=\dfrac{1}{3}p_{k-1}$. Consequently $ p_5=\dfrac{2}{3}(\dfrac{1}{3})^4=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{243}}$.
