For positive edge lengths $a,b,c$, $a^2+b^2+c^2=d^2$, $ab+bc+ca=\dfrac{S}{2}$, and $abc=V$. Therefore $a+b+c=\sqrt{d^2+S}$. Expanding $(x-a)(x-b)(x-c)$ yields $\boxed{x^3-\sqrt{d^2+S}\,x^2+\dfrac{S}{2}x-V=0}$.
