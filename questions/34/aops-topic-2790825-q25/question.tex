Let $S$ be the set of positive integers less than $3^{2019}$ whose base-$3$ representation uses only the digits $0$ and $1$. Find the sum of the squares of the elements of $S$, in the form $a^b(c^d-1)(e^f-1)$ where $a,c,e$ are not perfect powers.
