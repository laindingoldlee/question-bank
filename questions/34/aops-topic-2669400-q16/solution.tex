Let $r,s$ be the common roots; they are nonzero since the constant terms are nonzero. Subtracting the equations, $r,s$ satisfy $(a-b)x^2-3x-2=0$; we need $a\ne b$, as otherwise this has only one root. Taking $4\times$(first) $-3\times$(second) gives $x^3+(4a-3b)x^2+2x=0$, so $r,s$ are roots of $x^2+(4a-3b)x+2$; hence $rs=2$. From the first quadratic, $rs=-\dfrac{2}{a-b}$ and $r+s=\dfrac{3}{a-b}$, so $a-b=-1$ and $r+s=-3$. Thus $r,s$ are the roots of $x^2+3x+2$, namely $-1$ and $-2$.

Substituting $x=-1$ into the first equation: $-1+a-11+6=0$, so $a=6$, and $b=a+1=7$. Indeed
\[x^3+6x^2+11x+6=(x+1)(x+2)(x+3),\qquad x^3+7x^2+14x+8=(x+1)(x+2)(x+4).\]
So $\boxed{a=6,\ b=7}$.
