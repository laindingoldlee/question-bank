Let $\omega$ be a nonreal cube root of unity. Then $r^2+r+1=(r-\omega)(r-\omega^2)$. Since $f$ is monic of even degree, the numerator product is $f(\omega)f(\omega^2)$. Modulo $x^2+x+1$, the polynomial reduces to $4x-9$, so this product is $(4\omega-9)(4\omega^2-9)=16-36(-1)+81=133$. By Vieta, $abcd=-4$, and no root is zero. The requested value is $\boxed{-\dfrac{133}{4}}$.
