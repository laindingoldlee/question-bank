The line of centres $O_1O_2$ is the perpendicular bisector of the common chord $PQ$; let it meet $PQ$ at $H$, so $PH=12$. By Pythagoras, $O_1H=\sqrt{13^2-12^2}=5$ and $O_2H=\sqrt{15^2-12^2}=9$.

If the centres are on opposite sides of the chord, $O_1O_2=5+9=14$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O1=(0,0),O2=(14,0),P=(5,12),Q=(5,-12);
draw(circle(O1,13)); draw(circle(O2,15));
draw(P--Q,red+linewidth(0.9)); draw(O1--O2,blue);
draw(O1--P,gray+dashed); draw(O2--P,gray+dashed);
dot(O1);dot(O2);dot(P);dot(Q);dot((5,0));
label("$O_1$",O1,W); label("$O_2$",O2,E); label("$P$",P,N); label("$Q$",Q,S); label("$H$",(5,0),SE);
label("$5$",(2.5,0),N,blue); label("$9$",(9.5,0),N,blue);
label("$13$",(3.0,4.0)); label("$15$",(7.6,4.0));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

If they are on the same side, $O_1O_2=9-5=4$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O1=(0,0),O2=(4,0),P=(-5,12),Q=(-5,-12);
draw(circle(O1,13)); draw(circle(O2,15));
draw(P--Q,red+linewidth(0.9)); draw((-5,0)--O2,blue);
draw(O1--P,gray+dashed); draw(O2--P,gray+dashed);
dot(O1);dot(O2);dot(P);dot(Q);dot((-5,0));
label("$O_1$",O1,S); label("$O_2$",O2,S); label("$P$",P,N); label("$Q$",Q,S); label("$H$",(-5,0),W);
label("$13$",(-3.0,2.6)); label("$15$",(1.3,7.0));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

So the possible distances are $14$ and $4$, and among the choices the answer is $\boxed{\text{(D) } 4}$.
