Assign the letters $A$ to $Z$ the numbers $26$ down to $1$ ($A=26$, $B=25$, \dots, $Z=1$). The value of ``MATH ROCKS'' is $M+A+T+H+R+O+C+K+S$. George instead assigns the letters $A$ to $Z$ the numbers $1$ to $26$ ($A=1$, \dots, $Z=26$). What is the absolute difference between the correct value and George's value?
