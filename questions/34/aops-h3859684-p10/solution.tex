Let the base angle be $\beta$, so the apex angle is $180^\circ-2\beta$. In triangle $APQ$, the sine rule yields $\sin3\beta=-\dfrac{11}{17}\sin\beta$. The triple-angle identity gives $\sin^2\beta=\dfrac{31}{34}$ and hence $\cos A=\dfrac{14}{17}$. If $AB=AC=L$, the cosine rule gives $CP^2=L^2-28L+289=(L-14)^2+93$. Thus $(CP-L+14)(CP+L-14)=93$. With $L\ge17$, the positive factor pairs $(1,93),(3,31)$ give respectively $(CP,L)=(47,60),(17,28)$. Both have $P,Q$ on the indicated sides, so the sum is $\boxed{64}$.
