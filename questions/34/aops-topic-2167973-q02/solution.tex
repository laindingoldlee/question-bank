Here $x$ can be $0,1,\dots,8$. For a fixed $x$ we need $y+z\le17-2x$, that is, $y+z+w=17-2x$ with a slack $w\ge0$, which has $\dbinom{17-2x+2}{2}=\dbinom{19-2x}{2}$ solutions. Summing over $x$,
\[\dbinom{19}{2}+\dbinom{17}{2}+\dbinom{15}{2}+\cdots+\dbinom{3}{2}=171+136+105+78+55+36+21+10+3=\boxed{615}.\]
