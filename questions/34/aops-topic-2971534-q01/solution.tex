The drink contains $1.35$ quarts of orange juice. Adding $x$ quarts: $\dfrac{1.35+x}{3+x}=0.6$, so $1.35+x=1.8+0.6x$, $0.4x=0.45$, and $x=\boxed{1.125}$ quarts. (Adding $0.45$ quarts would give only $\dfrac{1.8}{3.45}\approx52\%$.)
