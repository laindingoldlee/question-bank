Each congruence says that $x+1$ is divisible by the modulus: $x+1\equiv0$ modulo $4$, $5$ and $6$. So $x+1$ is a multiple of $\operatorname{lcm}(4,5,6)=60$, and conversely every such $x$ works. The solutions are $\boxed{x\equiv59\pmod{60}}$.
