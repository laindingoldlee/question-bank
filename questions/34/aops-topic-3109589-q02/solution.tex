The digit $2$ requires $x\ge3$. The equation becomes $2^{2x+1}=x^4+x^3+2x+2=(x+1)(x^3+2)$. At $x=3$, the left side is $128$ and the right side is $116$. As $x$ increases by one, the left side multiplies by $4$. For $x\ge3$, the right-side ratio is $\dfrac{x+2}{x+1}\cdot\dfrac{(x+1)^3+2}{x^3+2}<\dfrac{5}{4}\left(\dfrac{4}{3}\right)^3=\dfrac{80}{27}<4$. Thus the left side remains strictly larger by induction. There are no integer bases satisfying the equation.
