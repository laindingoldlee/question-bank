The exterior angles are distinct positive integers summing 360. Therefore $\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}\le360$, giving $n\le26$, since $\dfrac{26\cdot27}{2}=351$ and $\dfrac{27\cdot28}{2}=378$. For existence take exterior angles 1 through 25 and 35, totaling 360. A convex polygon with these angles can be constructed by taking tangents to a unit circle with successive outward-normal angle gaps equal to these values. Each gap is less than 180, and successive tangent intersections yield positive side lengths, so the circle is inscribed in a bounded convex 26-gon. Thus $\boxed{26}$.
