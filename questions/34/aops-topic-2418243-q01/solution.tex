Doubling ten times, $2^{10}=1024$. By the rule for a power of a power,
\[1024^2=\left(2^{10}\right)^2=2^{10}\cdot2^{10}=2^{10+10}=2^{20}.\]
So
\[2^{20}=1024^2=(1000+24)^2=1000000+2\cdot24000+576=\boxed{1048576}.\]
