They are all at the start at times that are common multiples of $44=2^2\cdot11$, $16=2^4$ and $24=2^3\cdot3$. The first such time is $\operatorname{lcm}(44,16,24)=2^4\cdot3\cdot11=\boxed{528}$ seconds.
