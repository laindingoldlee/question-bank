First, $37038\cdot26=962988$. Let $x=962989$. Then
\[
37038\cdot962990\cdot26=962988\cdot962990=(x-1)(x+1)=x^2-1 .
\]
So the expression is $x^2-1369814121$. Now $1369814121=37011^2$, because
\[
37011^2=37000^2+2\cdot37000\cdot11+11^2=1369000000+814000+121 .
\]
Hence, by the difference of squares,
\[
x^2-37011^2=(x-37011)(x+37011)=925978\cdot1000000=\boxed{925978000000},
\]
using $x+37011=962989+37011=1000000$.
