The bunny and porcupine portions are each $280$ mL; the meerkat portion is $480$ mL. Every mixture is therefore $760$ mL. Alex drinks $6\cdot760=4560$ mL, which is $\dfrac{4560}{1500}\cdot100\%=304\%$ of one blender capacity. Sam drinks $4\cdot760=3040$ mL, which is $\dfrac{3040}{1400}\cdot100\%=\dfrac{1520}{7}\%=217\dfrac{1}{7}\%$. Thus Alex has the higher percentage.
