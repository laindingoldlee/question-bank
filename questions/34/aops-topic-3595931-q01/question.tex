A bunny portion is $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of $840$ mL, a porcupine portion is $\dfrac{2}{7}$ of $980$ mL, and a meerkat portion is $\dfrac{2}{5}$ of $1200$ mL. Over four Sundays, Alex drinks three mixtures of one porcupine portion and one meerkat portion on each of two Sundays. Sam drinks one mixture of one bunny portion and one meerkat portion each Sunday. Express each total as a percentage of their blender capacity, $1500$ mL for Alex and $1400$ mL for Sam. Who has the higher percentage?
