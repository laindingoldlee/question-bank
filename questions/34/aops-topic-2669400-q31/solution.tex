The interior angle of triangle $ABC$ at $B$ is $180^\circ-\alpha$, so by the law of cosines
\[AC^2=64+25-80\cos\left(180^\circ-\alpha\right)=89+80\cos\alpha.\]
$AC<7$ means $89+80\cos\alpha<49$, i.e. $\cos\alpha<-\dfrac12$, i.e. $\alpha>120^\circ$. The probability is $\dfrac{180-120}{180}=\boxed{\dfrac13}$. (If $\alpha$ is read as the angle $ABC$ instead, the condition is $\alpha<60^\circ$ and the probability is again $\dfrac13$.)
