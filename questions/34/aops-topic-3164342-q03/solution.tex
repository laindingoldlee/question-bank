Remove one month of each length, contributing 113 days. For a residual total $r<210$, the number $b$ of 36-day months is at most 5. Modulo 7, $r\equiv b$, so any two representations have the same $b$. Their remaining equation is $35a+42c$ equal, or $5a+6c$ equal. Distinct nonnegative solutions differ by $(\Delta a,\Delta c)=(6t,-5t)$, requiring a residual total at least 210. Thus smaller totals cannot be ambiguous. At $r=210$, six 35-day months or five 42-day months have different counts. Restoring the mandatory months gives $\boxed{n=323}$.
