Let $T$ toy-making and $G$ gift-wrapping elves take the quiz. Then $\dfrac{2}{3}T=\dfrac{3}{4}G$, so $8T=9G$. Since $\gcd(8,9)=1$, $T=9k$ and $G=8k$ for a positive integer $k$ (then $\dfrac{2}{3}T=6k$ is a whole number). The least total is $9+8=\boxed{17}$.
