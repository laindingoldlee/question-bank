The squared distance between the given vertices is $2^2+1^2+2^2=9$. In a cube with side $s$, two distinct vertices are separated by $s$, $s\sqrt2$, or $s\sqrt3$. Thus $s^2$ can be $9,\dfrac{9}{2},3$, and the corresponding surface areas are $54,27,18$. All three types occur in cubes, so the sum is $\boxed{99}$.
