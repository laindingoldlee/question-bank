Let $P(z)=\prod_i(z-z_i)=z^4+az^3+bz^2+cz+d$. The hypothesis says $z_iP\prime(z_i)-4=0$. Thus the degree-four polynomial $zP\prime(z)-4$ equals $4P(z)$, since both have the same four distinct roots and the same leading coefficient. Comparing coefficients yields $3a=4a,2b=4b,c=4c,-4=4d$, so $P(z)=z^4-1$. Its roots are $1,-1,i,-i$. Their sum is $\boxed0$, product $\boxed{-1}$, and sum of squares $\boxed0$.
