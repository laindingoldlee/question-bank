The water level must drop to $10-4=6$ feet, i.e. by $3$ feet.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair d=(1.6,1.0);real L=6,H=2.5,w1=2.25,w2=1.5;
fill((0,0)--(L,0)--(L,w1)--(0,w1)--cycle,opacity(0.25)+lightblue);
fill((0,w1)--(L,w1)--(L,w1)+d--(0,w1)+d--cycle,opacity(0.25)+lightblue);
draw((0,0)--(L,0)--(L,H)--(0,H)--cycle);draw((L,0)--(L,0)+d--(L,H)+d--(L,H));draw((0,H)--(0,H)+d--(L,H)+d);
draw((0,0)+d--(L,0)+d,dashed);draw((0,0)--(0,0)+d,dashed);draw((0,0)+d--(0,H)+d,dashed);
draw((0,w1)--(L,w1),blue);draw((0,w2)--(L,w2),blue+dashed);
label("$60$",(L/2,0),S);label("$40$",(L,0)+d/2,SE);label("$10$",(L,H/2)+d,E);
label("now $9$",(0,w1),W,blue);label("target $6$",(0,w2),W,blue);
\end{asy}

 That removes $60\cdot40\cdot3=7200$ cubic feet. The pumps remove $5\cdot30=150$ cubic feet per minute, so it takes $\dfrac{7200}{150}=\boxed{48}$ minutes.
