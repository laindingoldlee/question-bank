A fish can jump $4$ feet out of the water. A pool is $60$ feet long, $40$ feet wide and $10$ feet deep, and it is filled with water to a depth of $9$ feet. Five pumps each drain $30$ cubic feet of water per minute. For how many minutes must all five pumps run so that the water surface is at least $4$ feet below the top of the pool (so the fish cannot jump out)?
