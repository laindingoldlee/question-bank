Each trailing zero comes from a factor $10=2\cdot5$, and factors of $2$ are more plentiful than factors of $5$, so we count the factors of $5$ in $314!$:
\[
\left\lfloor\dfrac{314}{5}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{314}{25}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{314}{125}\right\rfloor=62+12+2=76
\]
(and $625>314$). So $314!$ has
\[\boxed{76}\]
trailing zeros.
