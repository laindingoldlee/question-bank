Pairing terms, $10^{2j}-10^{2j-1}=9\cdot10^{2j-1}$, so $x=\overline{9090\ldots90}$ with $n$ nines and $S(x)=9n$. We need $9n+S(9n)=2025$. Since $S(9n)$ is a multiple of $9$ and at most $27$ here, $9n\in\{1998,2007,2016\}$. Checking: $1998+27=2025$ works ($n=222$); $2007+9=2016$ fails; $2016+9=2025$ works ($n=224$). The average is $\boxed{223}$.
