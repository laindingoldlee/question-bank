The number of desks $n$ has no factorisation $n=ab$ with $a,b\ge2$, so $n$ is prime. We look for primes between $5745$ and $5772$; since $\sqrt{5772}<76$, it is enough to test the primes up to $73$.

The even numbers and the multiples of $3$ and $5$ are out, which leaves
\[5747,\ 5749,\ 5753,\ 5759,\ 5761,\ 5767,\ 5771 .\]
Six of these are composite:
\[5747=7\cdot821,\quad5753=11\cdot523,\quad5759=13\cdot443,\quad5761=7\cdot823,\quad5767=73\cdot79,\quad5771=29\cdot199 .\]
Dividing $5749$ by $7,11,13,\ldots,73$ always leaves a remainder, so $5749$ is prime. Bob has $\boxed{5749}$ desks.
