If the run has length $k\ge2$ and begins at $a\ge1$, then $k(2a+k-1)=690$. The bound $k(k+1)\le690$ gives $k\le25$. Testing divisors of $690$ in this range and requiring $a=\dfrac{345}{k}-\dfrac{k-1}{2}$ to be a positive integer gives $k=2,3,5,6,10,15,23$. Therefore there are $\boxed7$ representations.
