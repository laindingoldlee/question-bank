Use the complement. There are $4!=24$ arrangements in all. An arrangement with no drawer in its correct slot is a derangement of $4$ objects. By inclusion--exclusion the number of derangements is
\[D_4=4!\left(1-\dfrac{1}{1!}+\dfrac{1}{2!}-\dfrac{1}{3!}+\dfrac{1}{4!}\right)=24-24+12-4+1=9.\]
So the number of arrangements with at least one drawer in place is
\[24-9=\boxed{15}.\]
