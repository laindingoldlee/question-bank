Since $127=9\cdot13+10$, we need $2N+10\equiv0$, i.e.\ $2N\equiv3\pmod{13}$. Because $2\cdot7=14\equiv1\pmod{13}$, multiply by $7$: $N\equiv21\equiv8\pmod{13}$. The least positive integer is $N=\boxed{8}$; indeed $2\cdot8+127=143=11\cdot13$.

(The posted answer $\dfrac{3}{2}$ treats $N$ as any positive number: $2N+127=130$ gives $N=\dfrac{3}{2}$. For an integer $N$ the answer is $8$.)
