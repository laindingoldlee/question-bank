A semicircle of radius $1$ contains a smaller semicircle whose diameter endpoints lie on the larger arc, whose diameter is parallel to the larger diameter, and whose arc is tangent to the larger diameter. Repeat this construction indefinitely inside each new semicircle. Find the sum of all semicircle areas, including the first.
