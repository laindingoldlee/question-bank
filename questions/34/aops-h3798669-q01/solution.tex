The eight-move sequence $1,2,3,6,12,24,25,50,51$ reaches the target. For a lower bound, define a positive integer's binary weight as its number of binary digits plus its number of ones, minus two. Doubling increases this weight by exactly one. Adding one either adds a new one without a carry, or replaces trailing ones by zeros and increments the preceding bit; in every case the weight increases by at most one, including the all-ones carry case. The initial weight is zero. Since $51=(110011)_2$ has six digits and four ones, its weight is eight. Thus at least eight moves are necessary, and $\boxed8$ is optimal.
