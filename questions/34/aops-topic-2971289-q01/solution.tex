Extend $CE$ to meet $AB$ at $C'$. Since $AE$ bisects $\angle CAC'$ and is perpendicular to $CC'$, triangle $ACC'$ is isosceles with $AC'=AC=26$ and $E$ the midpoint of $CC'$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(8cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(30,0);real c=26;real cosA=(30^2+26^2-28^2)/(2*30*26);pair C=26*(cosA,sqrt(1-cosA^2));
pair D=B+(15/28)*(C-B);pair Ep=A+dot(C-A,unit(D-A))*unit(D-A);pair Cp=(26,0);
pair F=extension(Ep,Ep+(1,0),A,C),G=extension(Ep,Ep+(1,0),B,C);
draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--D);draw(C--Cp,dashed);draw(F--G);dot(Ep);dot(D);dot(G);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,N);label("$D$",D,NE);label("$E$",Ep,S);label("$F$",F,W);label("$G$",G,SE);label("$C'$",Cp,S);
\end{asy}

The line through $E$ parallel to $C'B$ (that is, to $AB$) bisects the sides of triangle $CC'B$, so it meets $CB$ at its midpoint: $CG=14$. By the angle bisector theorem $\dfrac{CD}{DB}=\dfrac{26}{30}$, so $CD=28\cdot\dfrac{26}{56}=13$. Therefore $DG=14-13=\boxed{1}$.
