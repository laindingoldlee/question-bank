Sharing among himself and $10$ friends means sharing among $11$ people, and among himself and $12$ friends means $13$ people. So
\[x\equiv2\pmod{11},\qquad x\equiv1\pmod{13}.\]
The numbers $\equiv1\pmod{13}$ are $1,14,27,40,53,66,79,\dots$, with remainders $1,3,5,7,9,0,2$ on division by $11$. The first one with remainder $2$ is $79=7\cdot11+2=6\cdot13+1$. By the Chinese remainder theorem all solutions are $79+143k$, so the smallest is $\boxed{79}$.
