The sum is $T=\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{20}(17-k)\dbinom{20}{k}$. Since $\dbinom{20}{k}=\dbinom{20}{20-k}$, reversing the order of summation gives
\[T=\sum_{k=0}^{20}\bigl(17-(20-k)\bigr)\dbinom{20}{k}=\sum_{k=0}^{20}(k-3)\dbinom{20}{k}.\]
Adding the two expressions,
\[2T=\sum_{k=0}^{20}14\dbinom{20}{k}=14\cdot2^{20},\]
so $T=7\cdot2^{20}=\boxed{7340032}$.

(Alternatively, $\sum k\dbinom{20}{k}=20\cdot2^{19}$, so $T=17\cdot2^{20}-20\cdot2^{19}=14\cdot2^{19}$.)
