How many ordered pairs of positive integers $(a,b)$ satisfy $\gcd(a,b)=8$ and $\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=120$? How many ordered pairs of nonzero integers satisfy the same conditions when gcd and lcm are defined to be positive?
