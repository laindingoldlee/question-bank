For positive integers, write $a=8u,b=8v$ with $\gcd(u,v)=1$. Since $ab=\gcd(a,b)\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)$, we have $uv=15$. The ordered coprime factor pairs are $(1,15),(3,5),(5,3),(15,1)$, giving $\boxed{4}$ positive pairs. Each absolute-value pair permits four independent sign choices, giving $\boxed{16}$ pairs of nonzero integers.
