Let the width be $w$ and the length $2w$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((0,0)--(2,0)--(2,1)--(0,1)--cycle,linewidth(1)); draw((0,0)--(2,1),blue);
label("$2w$",(1,0),S); label("$w$",(2,0.5),E); label("$30$",(1,0.5),NW);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

By the Pythagorean theorem $w^2+4w^2=900$, so $w=\sqrt{180}=6\sqrt5$. The perimeter is $2(w+2w)=6w=\boxed{36\sqrt5}$ cm.
