The base angles are $40^\circ$. Scale $AB=AC=1$, and put $B=(0,0)$ with $BC$ horizontal. Then $D=(1,0)$ and $A=(\cos40^\circ,\sin40^\circ)$. Since $\angle EBC=10^\circ$, the sine rule in $ABE$ gives
\[
BE=\dfrac{\sin100^\circ}{\sin50^\circ}=2\sin40^\circ.
\]
Thus $E=(2\sin40^\circ\cos10^\circ,2\sin40^\circ\sin10^\circ)$. The sine-difference identity gives
\[
2\sin40^\circ\cos10^\circ-1
=2\sin10^\circ\cos40^\circ.
\]
Consequently
\[
E-D=2\sin10^\circ(\cos40^\circ,\sin40^\circ).
\]
The ray $DE$ makes an angle of $40^\circ$ with the positive horizontal ray $DC$, so $\boxed{\angle CDE=40^\circ}$.

\end{asy}
