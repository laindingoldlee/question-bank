Let $a$ and $b$ be integers such that the quadratic $f(x)=x^2+ax+b+1$ has two roots that are positive integers. Factorise $a^2+b^2$ as a product of two integers in terms of the roots, and deduce that $a^2+b^2$ is not a prime number.
