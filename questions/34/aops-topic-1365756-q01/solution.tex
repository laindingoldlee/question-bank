Let the roots be the positive integers $x_1$ and $x_2$. By Vieta's formulas
\[
x_1+x_2=-a,\qquad x_1x_2=b+1 .
\]
Hence
\[
a^2+b^2=(x_1+x_2)^2+(x_1x_2-1)^2=x_1^2+2x_1x_2+x_2^2+x_1^2x_2^2-2x_1x_2+1
\]
\[
=x_1^2x_2^2+x_1^2+x_2^2+1=\boxed{\left(x_1^2+1\right)\left(x_2^2+1\right)}.
\]
Since $x_1,x_2\ge1$, both factors are at least $2$, so $a^2+b^2$ is a product of two integers greater than $1$ and is composite. For example, roots $1$ and $2$ give $f(x)=x^2-3x+2$, so $a=-3$, $b=1$ and $a^2+b^2=10=2\cdot5$.
