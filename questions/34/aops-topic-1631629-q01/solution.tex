One square mile is $5280^2=27{,}878{,}400$ square feet, so one acre is $\dfrac{27{,}878{,}400}{640}=43{,}560$ square feet, and $20$ acres is $871{,}200$ square feet. The seed needed is
\[
\dfrac{871{,}200}{100}\cdot3=26{,}136\text{ pounds},
\]
which costs $26{,}136\cdot\$0.50=\boxed{\$13{,}068.00}$.
