Winter rye seed costs $50$ cents per pound. Three pounds of seed are needed for every $100$ square feet. There are $640$ acres in one square mile (and $5280$ feet in a mile). How many dollars will it cost to seed a $20$-acre field? Express your answer to the nearest hundredth.
