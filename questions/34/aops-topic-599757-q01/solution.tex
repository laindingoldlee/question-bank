Both numbers divide $36$, so they come from $\{1,2,3,4,6,9,12,18,36\}$. The only two of these with sum $30$ are $12$ and $18$, and indeed $\operatorname{lcm}(12,18)=36$. The numbers are $\boxed{12\text{ and }18}$.
