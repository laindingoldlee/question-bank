Let $|A|=|B|=x$. When we add $|A|+|B|=2x$, every element of the union is counted once, except the $1001$ elements of the intersection, which lie in both sets and are counted twice. So
\[
|A\cup B|=|A|+|B|-|A\cap B|\quad\Longrightarrow\quad2007=2x-1001\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x=1504 .
\]
The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(C) }1504}$.

Alternatively, by regions of the Venn diagram: the parts of $A$ and of $B$ outside the intersection have the same size $y$, and $y+1001+y=2007$ gives $y=503$, so $|A|=503+1001=1504$. (The ``$2x$'' is the sum of the sizes of the two sets, not the size of either one.)
