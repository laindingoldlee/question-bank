Let the two integer quotients be $u,v$. Their ratio is $u:v=(\dfrac{2}{3}):(\dfrac{4}{5})=5:6$. Hence $u=5k,v=6k$ for positive integer $k$, giving $\dfrac{m}{n}=\dfrac{2}{15k}$. The greatest occurs at $k=1$, so $m=2,n=15$ and $\boxed{m+n=17}$.
