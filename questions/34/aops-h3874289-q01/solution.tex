Radii $AY=AM$ make triangle $AYM$ isosceles. Its base angles are both $56^\circ$, so $\angle YAM=180^\circ-112^\circ=\boxed{68^\circ}$. 
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(220);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(0,0),M=dir(-34),Y=dir(34);draw(circle(A,1));draw(A--M--Y--A);label("$A$",A,(-1,0));label("$M$",M,(1,-1));label("$Y$",Y,(1,1));
\end{asy}
