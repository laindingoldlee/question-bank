The conditioning region is the unit square minus two right triangles of leg $\dfrac{3}{4}$, so it has area $1-\dfrac{9}{16}=\dfrac{7}{16}$. Its portion in the top-right half-size square has area $\dfrac{1}{4}-2\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}(\dfrac{1}{4})^2=\dfrac{3}{16}$. The conditional probability is their ratio, $\boxed{\dfrac{3}{7}}$.
