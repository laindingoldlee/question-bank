The first four terms of a pattern are
\[
\text{ABBA},\quad\text{AABBBAA},\quad\text{AAABBBBAAA},\quad\text{AAAABBBBBAAAA}.
\]
The pattern continues, with the A's and B's increasing as shown.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Describe the $n$th term.
\item[(b)] Find the ratio of the number of B's to the total number of letters in the $n$th term.
\item[(c)] Which term has exactly $35\%$ B's?
\item[(d)] Is there a term in which exactly $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of the letters are B's?
\end{enumerate}
