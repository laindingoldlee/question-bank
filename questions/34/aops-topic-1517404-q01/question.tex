For a nonzero complex number $w$ and a real number $r\ne0$, define the principal root
\[
\sqrt[r]{w}=\exp\!\left(\dfrac{\ln|w|+i\operatorname{Arg}w}{r}\right),
\]
where $\operatorname{Arg}w$ is the argument of $w$ in $(-\pi,\pi]$. (For $w>0$ this is the usual positive real root.) Prove that for all positive integers $x$ and $y$,
\[
\sqrt[x]{-y}=\sqrt[x]{y}\cdot\sqrt[\dfrac {x}{2}]{i}.
\]
