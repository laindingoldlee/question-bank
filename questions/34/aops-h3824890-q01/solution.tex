A subset is outside $B$ exactly when it contains $1$ and omits $2$. There are $2^5=32$ such subsets, so $|B|=128-32=96$.

Only prime-sum sets outside $B$ remain to be added. They are $\{1\}\cup U$, where $U\subseteq\{3,4,5,6,7\}$ and $1+\sum U$ is prime. Exhaustively grouping by size, the qualifying $U$ are
\[
\begin{array}{c|l}
|U|&U\\\hline
1&\{4\},\{6\}\\
2&\{3,7\},\{4,6\},\{5,7\}\\
3&\{3,4,5\},\{3,6,7\},\{4,5,7\},\{5,6,7\}\\
4&\{3,4,5,6\},\{4,5,6,7\}
\end{array}
\]
The empty and full sets fail. There are $11$ additional sets, giving $\boxed{107}$.
