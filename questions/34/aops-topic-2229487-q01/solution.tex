$6$ dogs catch $6$ frogs in $6$ minutes, so together they catch $1$ frog per minute, and each dog catches $\dfrac{1}{6}$ frog per minute. To catch $100$ frogs in $50$ minutes the dogs must catch $2$ frogs per minute, which needs
\[2\div\dfrac{1}{6}=2\cdot6=\boxed{12}\text{ dogs}.\]
