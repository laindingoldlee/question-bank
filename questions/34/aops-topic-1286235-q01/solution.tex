By the choice of $k$, $2^k<n<2^{k+1}$ (equality $n=2^{k+1}$ is impossible since $n$ is not a power of $2$, and $2^{k+1}<n$ would contradict the maximality of $k$). If $2^k\mid n$, then $n=m\cdot2^k$ with $1<m<2$, which no integer $m$ satisfies. So $2^k\nmid n$. $\blacksquare$
