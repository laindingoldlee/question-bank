How many pairs of parallel edges, such as $\overline{AB}$ and $\overline{GH}$ or $\overline{EH}$ and $\overline{FG}$, does the cube below have?

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair P(real x,real y,real z){return (x+0.55*y,z+0.42*y);}
draw(P(0,0,0)--P(1,0,0)--P(1,0,1)--P(0,0,1)--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
draw(P(1,0,0)--P(1,1,0)--P(1,1,1)--P(1,0,1),linewidth(0.8));
draw(P(0,0,1)--P(0,1,1)--P(1,1,1),linewidth(0.8));
draw(P(0,0,0)--P(0,1,0)--P(1,1,0),dashed);draw(P(0,1,0)--P(0,1,1),dashed);
label("$D$",P(0,0,0),SW);label("$A$",P(0,0,1),NW);label("$H$",P(0,1,0),W);label("$E$",P(0,1,1),N);
label("$C$",P(1,0,0),S);label("$B$",P(1,0,1),NW);label("$G$",P(1,1,0),E);label("$F$",P(1,1,1),NE);
\end{asy}

$\textbf{(A) } 6 \qquad \textbf{(B) } 12 \qquad \textbf{(C) } 18 \qquad \textbf{(D) } 24 \qquad \textbf{(E) } 36$

(A student argued: ``$\overline{AB}$ is parallel to $3$ other edges, and then $\overline{EF}$ gives $2$ new pairs, so there are $5$ pairs in each direction and $15$ in all.'' Find the error.)
