Divide coefficients and subtract exponents:
\[
\dfrac{6a^2b}{3a^2b^3}=\dfrac{6}{3}\cdot a^{2-2}\cdot b^{1-3}=2b^{-2}=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{b^2}}.
\]
The student's $\dfrac{12a^2}{b}$ is the same expression as the book's $12a^2b^{-1}$ (since $b^{-1}=\dfrac{1}{b}$), but neither equals the given quotient: for $a=b=1$ the quotient is $2$, not $12$. The $a^2$ cancels and the coefficient is $\dfrac{6}{3}=2$.
