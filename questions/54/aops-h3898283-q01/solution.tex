Scale $AB=1$. Triangle $ABE$ has angles $50^\circ,30^\circ,100^\circ$, so $AE=\dfrac{\sin30^\circ}{\sin100^\circ}=\dfrac{1}{2\sin80^\circ}$. Triangle $ABD$ has angles $30^\circ,70^\circ,80^\circ$, so $AD=\dfrac{\sin70^\circ}{\sin80^\circ}=2AE\cos20^\circ$. Also $\angle DAE=20^\circ$. The cosine rule gives $DE^2=AD^2+AE^2-2AD\cdot AE\cos20^\circ=AE^2$. Hence triangle $ADE$ is isosceles, with $\angle AED=140^\circ$. Since ray $EB$ lies between $EA$ and $ED$, $\angle DEB=140^\circ-100^\circ=\boxed{40^\circ}$.
