If the death count is $d$, rounding requires $2.005\le\dfrac{233}{d}<2.015$. Thus $\dfrac{233}{2.015}<d\le\dfrac{233}{2.005}$, or approximately $115.633<d\le116.209$. The only integer is $\boxed{116}$; indeed $\dfrac{233}{116}\approx2.00862$ rounds to $2.01$.
