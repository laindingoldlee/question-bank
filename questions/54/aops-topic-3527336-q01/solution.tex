The first two statements are equivalent by subtracting $a$, and Fermat's little theorem makes them valid for every integer $a$. If $p\nmid a$, the residue $a$ has a multiplicative inverse, so cancelling it gives $a^{p-1}\equiv1\pmod p$. If $p\mid a$, cancellation is invalid and the last congruence is false, since its left side is zero. Thus the third form requires $\gcd(a,p)=1$.
