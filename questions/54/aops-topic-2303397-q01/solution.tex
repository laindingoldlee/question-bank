In each flip, independently of the past, the class goes to MATHCOUNTS (heads, kept) with probability $\dfrac12\cdot0.7=0.35$, goes to AMC (tails) with probability $0.5$, and there is a reflip with probability $\dfrac12\cdot0.3=0.15$, after which everything starts over. If $p$ is the probability of MATHCOUNTS, then
\[p=0.35+0.15\,p,\qquad\text{so}\qquad p=\dfrac{0.35}{0.85}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{17}}\approx0.412.\]
