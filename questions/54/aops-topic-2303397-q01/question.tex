A teacher flips a fair coin to choose a competition: heads means MATHCOUNTS and tails means AMC. Whenever the coin shows heads, she reflips it with probability $30\%$; whenever it shows tails, she keeps the result. She stops as soon as she keeps a result. What is the probability that the class goes to MATHCOUNTS?
