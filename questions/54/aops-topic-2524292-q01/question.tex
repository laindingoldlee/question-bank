Some time in $2016$, a person says:

``Everything I have said or will say before $2017$ will be a lie on January $1$, $2017$.''

Call this statement $S$, and assume that every statement the person makes has a definite truth value (true or false) on January $1$, $2017$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $S$ is not true on January $1$, $2017$.
\item[(b)] Prove that if the person makes, before $2017$, at least one other statement that is true on January $1$, $2017$, then $S$ is consistently false, that is, a lie.
\item[(c)] Prove that if every other statement the person makes before $2017$ is false on January $1$, $2017$, then $S$ can be neither true nor false, so it is a paradox.
\end{enumerate}
So was the statement true, a lie, or a paradox?
