The arc $BE$ of radius $6$ has length $2\pi$, so the angle of the sector is $\angle BAE=\dfrac{2\pi}{6}=\dfrac{\pi}{3}=60^\circ$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(6,0),C=(6,-6),D=(0,-6);
pair Ep=6*dir(60),G=6*dir(150),F=Ep+G;
fill(arc(A,6,0,60)--A--cycle,palegreen);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); draw(A--Ep--F--G--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(arc(A,6,0,60),deepgreen+linewidth(0.9));
draw(D--G,red+linewidth(0.9));
draw(arc(A,1.0,150,270),blue); label("$120^\circ$",1.35*dir(210),blue+fontsize(8pt));
draw(arc(A,1.4,0,60)); label("$60^\circ$",1.9*dir(30),fontsize(8pt));
draw(arc(D,1.1,0,120),red); label("$120^\circ$",D+1.9*dir(40),red+fontsize(8pt));
dot(A);dot(B);dot(C);dot(D);dot(Ep);dot(F);dot(G);
label("$A$",A,dir(-30)+dir(0)*0.3);label("$B$",B,NE);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,SW);
label("$E$",Ep,NE);label("$F$",F,N);label("$G$",G,W);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Around the point $A$ we have, without overlaps, the right angle $DAB$ of the first square, the $60^\circ$ sector $BAE$ and the right angle $EAG$ of the second square. The remaining angle is
\[\angle GAD=360^\circ-90^\circ-60^\circ-90^\circ=120^\circ .\]
Triangle $GAD$ is isosceles with $AG=AD=6$, so
\[\angle ADG=\dfrac{180^\circ-120^\circ}{2}=30^\circ .\]
The points $G$ and $C$ lie on opposite sides of line $AD$ (the square $ABCD$ is on one side and $G$ on the other), so ray $DA$ lies inside angle $GDC$ and
\[\angle GDC=\angle GDA+\angle ADC=30^\circ+90^\circ=\boxed{120^\circ}.\]
