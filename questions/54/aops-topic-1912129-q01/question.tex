You stand in front of two doors, $A$ and $B$. One of them leads to heaven and the other to hell, but you do not know which. Two all-knowing cats guard the doors: one always tells the truth and the other always lies, and you do not know which cat is which. The cats understand any yes-or-no question, but they answer only with the words ``Meow'' and ``Nya''; one of these words means ``yes'' and the other means ``no'', but you do not know which. You may ask as many questions as you like, one at a time, each to a cat of your choice; a question that would lead to a paradox may not be asked.

Is it possible to find out which door leads to heaven? If so, what is the least number of questions needed, and which questions should you ask?
