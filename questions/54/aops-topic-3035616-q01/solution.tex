No. If their common value is $k$, the three equations become $(1-k)a+(1+k)b=0$, $(1-2k)b+(1+2k)c=0$, and $(1+3k)a+(1-3k)c=0$. Their coefficient determinant is $(1-k)(1-2k)(1-3k)+(1+k)(1+2k)(1+3k)=2+22k^2>0$. Thus the homogeneous system has only $a=b=c=0$, contrary to distinctness and the nonzero denominators. The requested conditions are impossible over the reals.
