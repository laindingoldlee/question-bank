Let Rekha be $r$ years old now; then Mrs Goel is $r+27$. In $8$ years their ages are $r+8$ and $r+35$, so
\[
r+35=2(r+8)\ \Longrightarrow\ r+35=2r+16\ \Longrightarrow\ r=19 .
\]
Rekha is $\boxed{19}$ and Mrs Goel is $\boxed{46}$. Check: in $8$ years they will be $27$ and $54$, and $54=2\cdot27$.
