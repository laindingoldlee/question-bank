Let $a\ne0$ and $r$ be real numbers, and consider the infinite geometric series $a+ar+ar^2+\cdots$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that if $|r|<1$, the series converges and its sum is $\dfrac{a}{1-r}$.
\item[(b)] Prove that if $|r|\ge1$, the series diverges.
\end{enumerate}
