There are $7$ months with $31$ days, $4$ with $30$ and $1$ with $28$. By Legendre, $v_2(31!)=15+7+3+1=26$, $v_2(30!)=15+7+3+1=26$, $v_2(28!)=14+7+3+1=25$. Total $7\cdot26+4\cdot26+25=\boxed{311}$.
