Triangle $ABC$ has side lengths $a=BC$, $b=CA$, $c=AB$ and incentre $I$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that, as position vectors, $I=\dfrac{aA+bB+cC}{a+b+c}$.
\item[(b)] Prove that for every point $X$ in the plane of the triangle,
\[a\cdot XA^2+b\cdot XB^2+c\cdot XC^2=a\cdot IA^2+b\cdot IB^2+c\cdot IC^2+(a+b+c)\,XI^2 .\]
\item[(c)] For which point $X$ is $a\cdot XA^2+b\cdot XB^2+c\cdot XC^2$ smallest? Prove that the smallest value is $abc$.
\end{enumerate}
