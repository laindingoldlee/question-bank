(a) Put $r=\dfrac{a}{b}>0$. Then $r=1+\dfrac{1}{r}$, so $r^2-r-1=0$, giving $\phi=\dfrac{1+\sqrt5}{2}$. (b) Let $\psi=\dfrac{1-\sqrt5}{2}$. Since both numbers satisfy $t^2=t+1$, induction from the first two terms gives $F_n=\dfrac{\phi^n-\psi^n}{\sqrt5}$. Consequently $\dfrac{F_{n+1}}{F_n}=\phi\dfrac{1-\left(\dfrac{\psi}{\phi}\right)^{n+1}}{1-\left(\dfrac{\psi}{\phi}\right)^n}\longrightarrow\boxed\phi$ because $|\psi|<\phi$.
