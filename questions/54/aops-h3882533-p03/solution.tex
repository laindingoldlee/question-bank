Lusen can receive only three Hi-Chews, because the other nine friends need at least nine in total. The remaining ten Hi-Chews are distributed positively among those nine friends in $\binom98=9$ ways. The other two candy types have $\binom{11}{9}=55$ and $\binom{14}{9}=2002$ positive distributions. The choices are independent, so the total is $9\cdot55\cdot2002=990990$, giving remainder $\boxed{990}$.
