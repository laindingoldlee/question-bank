Voldemort has $12$ hairbrushes, all of different colours, and has forgotten where he keeps them. Each hairbrush is in one of $165$ possible places, and the red hairbrush is equally likely to be in any of them. Voldemort checks the places one at a time in a random order, never checking a place twice. What is the probability that he finds the red hairbrush in the $144$th place he checks?
