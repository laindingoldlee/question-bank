Choose the Maserati and Bentley recipients in $3\cdot2=6$ ways. Exactly two heirs must receive two cars. Among the two special recipients, let $k$ receive a second car. If $k=0$, choose the two double recipients from the other three in $3$ ways and distribute the five remaining cars in $\dfrac{5!}{2!2!}=30$ ways. If $k=1$, choose the special and nonspecial double recipients in $2\cdot3=6$ ways, with $\dfrac{5!}{2!}=60$ assignments. If $k=2$, the five remaining cars go one to each heir, in $5!=120$ ways. Total: $6(3\cdot30+6\cdot60+120)=\boxed{3420}$.
