\textbf{Division.} $2x^3-4x^2-85x+66=(x+6)(2x^2-16x+11)$ (check: $-6$ is a root, since $-432-144+510+66=0$). So $A,B$ are the roots of $2x^2-16x+11$, and by Vieta $AB=\boxed{\dfrac{11}{2}}$.

\textbf{Without dividing.} The cubic's roots are $-6,A,B$, with product $-\dfrac{66}{2}=-33$ by Vieta. So $-6AB=-33$ and $AB=\dfrac{11}{2}$.
