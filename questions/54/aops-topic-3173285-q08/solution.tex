The fixed point solves $k=\dfrac{9}{10}k+\dfrac{2}{5}$, giving $k=4$. Subtracting it yields $x_{n+1}-4=\dfrac{9}{10}(x_n-4)$. Since $x_0-4=1$, induction gives $x_n=4+(\dfrac{9}{10})^n$. Therefore $\boxed{a+b+k=9+10+4=23}$.
