Let $n\ge5$ be an integer. Find the largest integer $k$ (in terms of $n$) such that there exists a convex $n$-gon $A_1A_2\cdots A_n$ for which exactly $k$ of the quadrilaterals $A_iA_{i+1}A_{i+2}A_{i+3}$ ($i=1,\dots,n$, indices mod $n$) have an inscribed circle.
