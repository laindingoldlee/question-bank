Let the total be $T$. In the first sharing the parts are $\dfrac{15T}{36},\dfrac{12T}{36},\dfrac{9T}{36}$, and in the second $\dfrac{14T}{36},\dfrac{12T}{36},\dfrac{10T}{36}$. Only Julian gains, by $\dfrac{T}{36}$. So $\dfrac{T}{36}=40$ and $T=1440$. They received $\boxed{600,\ 480\text{ and }360}$ candies (John, Carl, Julian).
