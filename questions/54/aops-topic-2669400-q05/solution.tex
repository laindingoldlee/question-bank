Solve $y=\dfrac{4x-7}{3x-2}$ for $x$: $3xy-2y=4x-7$, so $x(3y-4)=2y-7$ and
\[f^{-1}(x)=\boxed{\dfrac{2x-7}{3x-4}}.\]
Since $f(x)=\dfrac43-\dfrac{13}{3(3x-2)}$ never equals $\dfrac43$, the range of $f$ is $\mathbb R\setminus\left\{\dfrac43\right\}$, and this is the domain of $f^{-1}$. The range of $f^{-1}$ is the domain of $f$, $\mathbb R\setminus\left\{\dfrac23\right\}$.
