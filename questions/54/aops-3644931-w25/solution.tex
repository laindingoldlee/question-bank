By symmetry consider $0\le k\le20$. For $k\ge10$, both coordinates are nonnegative, so $f(k)=k$. For $0\le k\le10$, the coordinate $x$ is uniform on $[k-10,10]$ and $y=k-x$. Integrating the two absolute values gives $f(k)=\dfrac{[(10-k)^2+100]}{(20-k)}=-k+\dfrac{200}{(20-k)}$. Its derivative vanishes at $20-k=10\sqrt2$, and the second derivative is positive. The minimum is therefore $\boxed{20\sqrt2-20}$, attained at $k=\pm(20-10\sqrt2)$.
