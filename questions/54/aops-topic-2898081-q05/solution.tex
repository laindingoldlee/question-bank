Let $g=\sqrt[3]{abc}$. By AM--GM, $a+b+c\ge3g$, so the left side is at most $\dfrac3{6g}=\dfrac1{2g}$. The right side is $\dfrac16\left(\dfrac1a+\dfrac1b+\dfrac1c\right)\ge\dfrac16\cdot\dfrac3g=\dfrac1{2g}$, again by AM--GM. $\blacksquare$ Equality holds iff $a=b=c$.
