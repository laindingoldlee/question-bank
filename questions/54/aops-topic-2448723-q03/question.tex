Twenty-five circles of radius $3$ inches are packed in a square in a $5\times5$ arrangement: each circle is tangent to its neighbours in its row and in its column, and the circles in the outer rows and columns are tangent to the sides of the square. What is the ratio of the total area of the circles to the area of the part of the square not covered by the circles?

$\textbf{(A) }\dfrac{2\pi}{12-\pi}\qquad\textbf{(B) }\dfrac{250\pi}{\pi-250}\qquad\textbf{(C) }\dfrac{\pi}{4-\pi}\qquad\textbf{(D) }\dfrac{4\pi}{3\pi-16}\qquad\textbf{(E) }\dfrac{\pi}{5-\pi}$
