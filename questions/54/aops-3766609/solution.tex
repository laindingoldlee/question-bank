If $r$ litres of mixture remain, they contain $\dfrac{3}{5}r$ litres of blue. The final mixture may contain only $2$ litres of blue, and adding paint cannot remove blue, so $\dfrac{3}{5}r\le2$, or $r\le\dfrac{10}{3}$. This upper bound is achievable: keep $\dfrac{10}{3}$ litres, containing $2$ litres blue and $\dfrac{4}{3}$ litres yellow, and add $\dfrac{5}{3}$ litres of yellow. The least discard is $5-\dfrac{10}{3}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{3}\text{ litres}}$.
