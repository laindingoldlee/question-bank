Since $x=0$ is not a root, divide by $x$: $x+\dfrac{1}{x}=5$. Expanding the cube,
\[
\left(x+\dfrac{1}{x}\right)^3=x^3+\dfrac{1}{x^3}+3\left(x+\dfrac{1}{x}\right),
\]
so $x^3+\dfrac{1}{x^3}=5^3-3\cdot5=\boxed{110}$.
