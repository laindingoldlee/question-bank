Moving $9$ points counterclockwise on a $12$-point circle lands on the same point as moving $3$ points clockwise. So after $k$ turns Alice is $5k$ and Bob $3k$ points clockwise from the start (counted modulo $12$). They meet when $5k\equiv3k\pmod{12}$, that is, when $12\mid 2k$, i.e. $6\mid k$. The first time is after $\boxed{6}$ turns, answer $\textbf{(A)}$. (Check: $30\equiv18\equiv6\pmod{12}$, so both are at point $6$.)
