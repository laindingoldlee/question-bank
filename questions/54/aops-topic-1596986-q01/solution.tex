Let $A$, $C$, $I$ be the sets of students taking algebra, advanced computer and industrial technology, and let $t$ be the number taking all three. Exactly $400-164=236$ students take at least one course. By inclusion--exclusion,
\[
|A\cup C\cup I|=|A|+|C|+|I|-|A\cap C|-|A\cap I|-|C\cap I|+|A\cap C\cap I|,
\]
so
\[
236=117+109+114-70-34-29+t=207+t .
\]
Hence $t=\boxed{29}$.

(Check: the regions of the Venn diagram are then all nonnegative. Algebra and computer only: $70-29=41$; algebra and technology only: $34-29=5$; computer and technology only: $0$; only algebra $117-41-5-29=42$; only computer $109-41-0-29=39$; only technology $114-5-0-29=80$; and $42+39+80+41+5+0+29=236$.)
