Combining the factored denominators gives
\[
\dfrac{A(x-4)+(x+1)}{(x-2)(x+1)(x-4)}.
\]
For this to stay bounded near $2$, the numerator at $2$ must vanish: $-2A+3=0$. Hence $A=\dfrac{3}{2}$ is necessary. For this value the expression simplifies to $\dfrac{5}{2(x+1)(x-4)}$. On $1<x<3$, $(x+1)(4-x)>4$, so its absolute value is less than $\dfrac{5}{8}<1999$. Therefore $\boxed{A=\dfrac{3}{2}}$.
