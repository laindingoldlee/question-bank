Let $T$ be the first zapping minute. Since minute $30$ has conditional success probability one, $1\le T\le30$. Surviving the first $k$ minutes has probability $\Pr(T>k)=\prod_{j=1}^k(1-\dfrac{j}{30})=\dfrac{29!}{(29-k)!30^k}$ for $0\le k\le29$. The tail-sum formula therefore gives $\boxed{\mathbb E[T]=\sum_{k=0}^{29}\dfrac{29!}{(29-k)!30^k}\approx6.549460831}$. This is about $6$ minutes $33$ seconds, so the expected clock time is approximately 6:06:33 pm. The formula follows by writing $T=\sum_{k=0}^{29}\mathbf1_{T>k}$ and taking expectations.
