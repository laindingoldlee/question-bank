A fly is alive at 6:00 pm. Conditional on surviving, at minute $n=1,2,\ldots,30$ after 6:00 it is zapped with probability $\dfrac{n}{30}$. No zapping occurs between integer minutes. Find the expected number of minutes until it is zapped, and approximate the expected clock time.
