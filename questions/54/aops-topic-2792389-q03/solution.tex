Alex first puts plus between 1 and 2. Pair the remaining gaps on the two sides of each of 3,5,7,9. Whenever Vicky fills one gap in a pair, Alex puts multiplication in its mate. Each such odd number is then multiplied by at least one adjacent even number, so every product term after the initial 1 is even. The first 1 remains its own term. The final expression is odd, so $\boxed{\text{Alex wins}}$.
