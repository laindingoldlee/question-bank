Two hikers on a mountain sight two consecutive mile markers on a straight, level road that runs directly away from them in the plane below. The angles of depression of the two markers are $45^\circ$ and $15^\circ$. How many feet above the plane of the road are the hikers? Give the exact value and the value to the nearest foot ($1$ mile $=5280$ feet).
