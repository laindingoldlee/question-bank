Let $ABCD$ be a trapezoid with $AB\parallel CD$ such that $AC\perp BC$ and $BD\perp AD$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $ABCD$ is an isosceles trapezoid.
\item[(b)] Is the converse true, that is, does every isosceles trapezoid $ABCD$ with bases $AB$ and $CD$ satisfy $AC\perp BC$?
\end{enumerate}
