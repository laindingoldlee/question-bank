For a positive integer $x$ let $s(x)$ denote the sum of its decimal digits. Let
\[
S=\{\,s(2x) : x \text{ a positive integer with } s(x)=2016\,\}.
\]
Prove that $S$ is the set of all multiples of $9$ between $405$ and $4032$ inclusive.
