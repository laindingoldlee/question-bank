For $n\ge2$, exactly $n-1$ ordered positive pairs $(i,j)$ have $i+j=n$. Hence the sum is $\sum_{i,j\ge1}2^{-i-j}=(\sum_{i\ge1}2^{-i})^2=1^2=\boxed1$. Rearranging is valid because all terms are nonnegative (or by absolute convergence).
