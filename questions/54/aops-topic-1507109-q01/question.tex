In quadrilateral $ABCD$, points $M$, $N$, $P$, $Q$ lie on the sides $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, $DA$ respectively, with
\[
\dfrac{MA}{MB}=\dfrac{PD}{PC}=a\qquad\text{and}\qquad\dfrac{QA}{QD}=\dfrac{NB}{NC}=b .
\]
The segments $MP$ and $NQ$ meet at $L$. Prove that $\dfrac{LQ}{LN}=a$ and $\dfrac{LM}{LP}=b$.
