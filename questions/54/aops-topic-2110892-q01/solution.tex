There are $9\cdot10^5=900000$ six-digit numbers. Let $A_d$ be the set of six-digit numbers with no digit $d$; we want the numbers in none of $A_1,A_2,A_3$. Count by choosing the leading digit (which cannot be $0$) and then the other five digits:
\begin{itemize}
\item $|A_1|=8\cdot9^5=472392$, and the same for $A_2$ and $A_3$;
\item $|A_1\cap A_2|=7\cdot8^5=229376$, and the same for the other two pairs;
\item $|A_1\cap A_2\cap A_3|=6\cdot7^5=100842$.
\end{itemize}
By inclusion-exclusion, the count is
\[900000-3\cdot472392+3\cdot229376-100842=900000-1417176+688128-100842=\boxed{70110}.\]
