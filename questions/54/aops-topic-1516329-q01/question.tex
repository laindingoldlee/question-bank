A chess club has $8$ girls and $6$ boys. Two members, Zig and Zag, are fraternal twins of different genders. If a team of $3$ girls and $3$ boys is randomly selected for the district championship, what is the probability that exactly one of the twins is on the team? Express your answer as a common fraction.
