One twin is a girl and the other a boy. The $3$ girls and the $3$ boys are chosen independently, each choice equally likely.
\begin{itemize}
\item The girl twin is chosen with probability $\dfrac{\binom72}{\binom83}=\dfrac{21}{56}=\dfrac{3}{8}$ (equivalently, $3$ of the $8$ girls are chosen).
\item The boy twin is chosen with probability $\dfrac{\binom52}{\binom63}=\dfrac{10}{20}=\dfrac{1}{2}$.
\end{itemize}
Exactly one twin is on the team with probability
\[
\dfrac{3}{8}\cdot\left(1-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)+\left(1-\dfrac{3}{8}\right)\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{3}{16}+\dfrac{5}{16}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}.
\]
