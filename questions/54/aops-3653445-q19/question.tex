Positive integers $L,M,T$ are defined mutually as follows. On a fully occupied toroidal $2L\times2L$ board, half the pieces are rooks and half bishops; $M$ is the greatest number of ordered pairs with one-way attack, with pieces blocking beyond adjacent occupied squares. In a regular pentagon $DZHAO$, reflect $D$ in $HA$ to $R$; if $[ZHAO]=M$, define $T=[DZHAO]+[ORZ]$. Finally $L$ counts integers $1\le k\le \dfrac{T}{6}$ with $301\mid3^k-1$. Find $L,M,T$.
