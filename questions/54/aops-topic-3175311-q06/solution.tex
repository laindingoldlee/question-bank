Write $AD=h$. The horizontal displacement from $A$ to $E$ is $3$, so $h^2+3^2=6^2$, giving $h^2=27$. Hence $AC^2=6^2+h^2=63$, and $AC=\boxed{3\sqrt7}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),Bp=(6,0),C=(6,3*sqrt(3)),D=(0,3*sqrt(3)),Ep=(3,3*sqrt(3));draw(A--Bp--C--D--cycle);draw(A--Ep^^A--C,blue);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",Bp,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$E$",Ep,N);
\end{asy}
