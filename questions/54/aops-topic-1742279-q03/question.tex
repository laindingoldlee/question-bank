Exploding $200$ lb of TNT produces an explosion of $400$ kabloowies, and the strength of an explosion is proportional to the amount of TNT. A sheet of paper can withstand $0.5$ kabloowies, a sheet of metal is $400$ times as strong as a sheet of paper, and a wall is equivalent to $400$ sheets of metal. How much TNT is needed to produce an explosion as strong as the wall can withstand?
