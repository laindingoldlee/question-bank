Any particular sequence with $c$ heads and $n-c$ tails has probability $p^c(1-p)^{n-c}$. There are $\binom nc$ such sequences (choose the positions of the heads), and they are mutually exclusive, so
\[P=\boxed{\binom nc p^c(1-p)^{n-c}}.\]
The expression $p^c(1-p)^{n-c}$ alone is the probability of one specific order, e.g. all heads first.
