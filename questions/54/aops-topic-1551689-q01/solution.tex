Kirsten and I close the gap at $4+4=8$ miles per hour, so we meet after
\[
\dfrac{1}{2}\div8=\dfrac{1}{16}\text{ hour}.
\]
Potter runs at $9$ miles per hour during all of that time, so he runs
\[
9\cdot\dfrac{1}{16}=\boxed{\dfrac{9}{16}}\text{ mile}.
\]
(There is no need to add up the infinitely many back-and-forth legs.)
