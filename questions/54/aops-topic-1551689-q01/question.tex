I was walking my dog Potter when I saw my daughter Kirsten in the distance, $\dfrac{1}{2}$ mile away. We walked towards each other, both at $4$ miles per hour. Meanwhile Potter ran to Kirsten, then back to me, then back to her, and so on, continually running at $9$ miles per hour, until Kirsten and I met. How many miles did Potter run? Express your answer as a common fraction.
