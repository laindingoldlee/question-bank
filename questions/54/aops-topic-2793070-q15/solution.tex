Let $\alpha,\beta,\gamma$ be the minor half-central angles for chords $6,7,8$. Each lies between $30^\circ$ and $60^\circ$, so each is less than the sum of the other two. Thus none of these three consecutive arcs can be major: choosing a major arc would make their total exceed $360^\circ$. Also $\sin\alpha=\dfrac{3}{5}$, $\sin\gamma=\dfrac{4}{5}$, so $\alpha+\gamma=90^\circ$. The three known arcs total $180^\circ+2\beta>180^\circ$, leaving a minor arc $DA$ with half-angle $90^\circ-\beta$. Hence $DA=10\cos\beta=10\sqrt{1-\dfrac{49}{100}}=\boxed{\sqrt{51}}$.

\end{asy}
