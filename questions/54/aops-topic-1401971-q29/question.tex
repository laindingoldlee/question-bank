A four-by-four grid consists of $16$ points, spaced $1$ unit apart horizontally and vertically. Two distinct points $P$ and $Q$ are selected at random from this grid. What is the probability that the distance between $P$ and $Q$ is less than $3$? Express your answer as a common fraction.
