There are $\binom{16}{2}=120$ pairs of points. If the coordinate differences are $d_x,d_y\in\{0,1,2,3\}$, the distance is $\sqrt{d_x^2+d_y^2}$. Since $d_x^2+d_y^2\le2^2+2^2=8<9$ whenever both differences are at most $2$, while a difference of $3$ already gives distance at least $3$, the distance is at least $3$ exactly when $d_x=3$ or $d_y=3$.

Count those pairs by complementary counting: pairs with $d_x=3$ use one point in column $0$ and one in column $3$, $4\cdot4=16$ pairs; likewise $16$ pairs with $d_y=3$; and the pairs with both $d_x=3$ and $d_y=3$ are the $2$ pairs of opposite corners. So $16+16-2=30$ pairs have distance at least $3$, and $120-30=90$ pairs have distance less than $3$. The probability is
\[
\dfrac{90}{120}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{4}} .
\]
