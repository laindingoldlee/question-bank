Let the counts be $c>t>s\ge0$, so $4c+3t+6s=24$. Modulo 3, $c$ is a multiple of 3. Since $c\le6$, it is 3 or 6. If $c=6$, the other counts vanish, contradicting $t>s$. Thus $c=3$, and $3t+6s=12$ with $t<3$ forces $t=2,s=1$. Hence $\boxed3$ cars.
