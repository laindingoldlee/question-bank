Multiply by the nonzero denominator and complete the square:
\[\sum_{i=1}^{2025}(a_i-1012)^2=2025\cdot1012^2.\]
Thus $(a_1-1012)^2\le2025\cdot1012^2$ and $a_1\le1012+45\cdot1012=46552$. Equality holds by taking $a_1=46552$ and every other entry equal to 1012; their sum is positive, so the original quotient is defined and equals 2024. The maximum is $\boxed{46552}$.
