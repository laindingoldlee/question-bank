Rosie and her sister Lucy each have $9$ class periods next quarter, and neither has seen her schedule. Rosie learns that she has math in period $4$ and English in some later period; assume her English period is equally likely to be any of periods $5$ to $9$. Lucy's English period is equally likely to be any of her $9$ periods, independently of Rosie's. The probability that the sisters have English in the same period is $\dfrac xy$ in lowest terms. Find $y-x$.
