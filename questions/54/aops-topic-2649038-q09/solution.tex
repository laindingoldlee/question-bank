Whatever Rosie's English period $r\in\{5,\dots,9\}$ is, Lucy's English period equals $r$ with probability $\dfrac19$. So the probability is
\[\sum_{r=5}^{9}\dfrac15\cdot\dfrac19=\dfrac19,\]
and $y-x=\boxed{8}$. (The information about Rosie's schedule does not change the answer.)
