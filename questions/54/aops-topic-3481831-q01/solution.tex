The retained fraction is $(1-\dfrac{1}{2})(1-\dfrac{1}{3})(1-\dfrac{1}{6})=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot\dfrac{5}{6}=\dfrac{5}{18}$. Thus the initial number is $10\div\dfrac{5}{18}=\boxed{36}$. The successive remainders $18,12,10$ verify the result.
