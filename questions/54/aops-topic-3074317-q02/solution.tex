For every $k\ge1$, $c(2k)c(2k+2)=c(k)c(k+1)$, while $c(2k+1)c(2k+3)=(-1)^{k+k+1}c(k)c(k+1)=-c(k)c(k+1)$. Thus the terms indexed $2k,2k+1$ cancel. Pairing indices $2,3$ through $2012,2013$ leaves only $c(1)c(3)$. Since $c(3)=(-1)^1c(1)=-1$, the sum is $\boxed{-1}$.
