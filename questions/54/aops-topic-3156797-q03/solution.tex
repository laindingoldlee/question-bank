The first two conditions combine as $ x=68+77t $: indeed $ 68 $ has the required residues, and the coprime moduli give period $ 77 $. The last condition becomes $ 3+12t\equiv8\pmod{13}$, so $ t\equiv8 $. Therefore $ x\equiv684\pmod{1001}$ and the least positive value is $\boxed{684}$.
