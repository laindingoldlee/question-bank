(a) $4^2=\boxed{16}$. (b) $3\cdot5=\boxed{15}$. (c) $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot6\cdot4=\boxed{12}$. (d) The semiperimeter is $21$, so Heron gives $\sqrt{21\cdot8\cdot7\cdot6}=\boxed{84}$. Finally Pythagoras gives $\sqrt{5^2+12^2}=\boxed{13}$.
