$2024=11\cdot184$ is the $184$th number. Rows $1$ to $r$ contain $\dfrac{r(r+1)}{2}$ numbers; $\dfrac{18\cdot19}{2}=171<184\le190=\dfrac{19\cdot20}{2}$. So $2024$ is in row $\boxed{19}$, in position $184-171=\boxed{13}$.
