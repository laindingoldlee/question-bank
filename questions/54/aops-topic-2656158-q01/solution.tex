Since $a$ is a positive integer, $a\ge1$ and $3a-1\ge2>0$. Dividing the inequality $(3a-1)(a-b)\ge0$ by the positive number $3a-1$ keeps its direction, so $a-b\ge0$, i.e. $a\ge b$. $\blacksquare$

(The integrality of $a$ is what makes $3a-1$ positive: for the real numbers $a=\dfrac16$, $b=1$ the product is positive but $a<b$.)
