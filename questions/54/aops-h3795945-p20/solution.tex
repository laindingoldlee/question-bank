Since $1000=8\cdot125$ and consecutive numbers are coprime, either $n$ or $n+1$ is divisible by $125$. Below $375$, the positive possibilities are $124,125,249,250$. None has $8\mid n(n+1)$, as direct reduction modulo $8$ shows. But $375\cdot376$ is divisible by both $125$ and $8$. Therefore the least value is $\boxed{375}$.
