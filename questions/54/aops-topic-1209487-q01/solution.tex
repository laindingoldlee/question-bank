Every term of the expansion of $(x+y+xy)^5$ is a product of five factors, each equal to $x$, $y$ or $xy$; so its total degree in $x$ and $y$ is at least $5$. Multiplying by a term of $(w+x+y+z)^5$ cannot lower the degree in $x$ and $y$. But $xyz$ has degree only $2$ in $x$ and $y$. So no term equals $xyz$, and the coefficient is $\boxed{0}$.
