The typed number consists of the digits $2,0,0,6$ in this order together with two $1$'s placed somewhere among them. Choosing the $2$ positions (out of $6$) for the $1$'s determines the number, and different choices give different numbers (the $1$'s are the only digits equal to $1$). The leading digit is then $1$ or $2$, never $0$, so every choice is a genuine six-digit number. The count is $\dbinom62=\boxed{15}$.
