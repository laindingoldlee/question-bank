Vieta gives $a+b+c=6$, $ab+bc+ca=9$, $abc=23$. (i) Reverse coefficients and divide by the leading coefficient: the answer is $\boxed{x^3-\dfrac{9}{23}x^2+\dfrac{6}{23}x-\dfrac{1}{23}}$. It is $-\dfrac{1}{23}x^3P(\dfrac{1}{x})$. (ii) Substitute $x+2$ into $P$ to obtain $\boxed{x^3-3x-21}$. (iii) The squared-root sum is $36-18=18$, the pairwise squared-product sum is $9^2-2\cdot6\cdot23=-195$, and their product is $23^2=529$. Hence the answer is $\boxed{x^3-18x^2-195x-529}$.
