The hands of a clock move in the wrong way: the minute hand moves as the hour hand should (one turn in $12$ hours), and the hour hand moves as the minute hand should (one turn per hour). At six o'clock in the evening the clock shows the correct time. The next day, shortly after seven o'clock in the morning, it shows the correct time again. How many minutes after seven o'clock does this happen?
