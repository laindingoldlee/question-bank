For $s=1,2,3,4,5$, feasible counts (small,large) are respectively $(59,0),(27,1),(15,2),(8,3),(3,4)$. If $s=6$, then $6a+13b=59$ requires $b\equiv5\pmod6$, while $0\le b\le4$. This is impossible. Thus the least impossible price is $\boxed6$ dollars.
