The dog gains on the fox at $750-720=30$ meters per minute, and the gap is $60$ meters, so it catches the fox after
\[
\dfrac{60}{30}=\boxed{2}\text{ minutes}.
\]
Check: in $2$ minutes the dog runs $1500$ m and the fox $1440$ m, and $1440+60=1500$.
