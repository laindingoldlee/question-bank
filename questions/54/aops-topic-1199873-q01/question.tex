In triangle $ABC$ the angle bisectors $BD$ and $CE$ meet at $O$, and $BD\cdot CE=2\,BO\cdot OC$. Let $H$ be the point of $BC$ with $OH\perp BC$. Prove that $AB\cdot AC=2\,HB\cdot HC$.
