Move everything to the left and complete the squares:
\[
a^2-a=\left(a-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^2-\dfrac{1}{4},\qquad 3b^2-b=3\left(b-\dfrac{1}{6}\right)^2-\dfrac{1}{12},
\]
\[
\dfrac{c^2}{2}-c=\dfrac{1}{2}(c-1)^2-\dfrac{1}{2},\qquad \dfrac{3d^2}{2}-d=\dfrac{3}{2}\left(d-\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^2-\dfrac{1}{6} .
\]
The constants add up to $-\dfrac{1}{4}-\dfrac{1}{12}-\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{6}=-1$, so the equation becomes
\[
\left(a-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^2+3\left(b-\dfrac{1}{6}\right)^2+\dfrac{1}{2}(c-1)^2+\dfrac{3}{2}\left(d-\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^2=0 .
\]
A sum of nonnegative terms is $0$ only if every term is $0$, so $a=\dfrac{1}{2}$, $b=\dfrac{1}{6}$, $c=1$, $d=\dfrac{1}{3}$, and
\[
1000a+100b+10c+d=500+\dfrac{50}{3}+10+\dfrac{1}{3}=\boxed{527}.
\]
