An $8\times8$ square is cut into four pieces. A horizontal cut splits it into an $8\times3$ rectangle and an $8\times5$ rectangle. The $8\times3$ rectangle is cut along a diagonal into two right triangles with legs $3$ and $8$. The $8\times5$ rectangle is cut into two congruent right trapezoids, each with parallel sides $3$ and $5$ and height $5$. The four pieces seem to rearrange into a $5\times13$ rectangle, which would mean $64=65$. Explain the paradox: show that the pieces do not fill the $5\times13$ rectangle exactly, and find the area of the region they leave uncovered.
