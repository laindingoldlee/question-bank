A selection is described by how many balls $x_i\in\{0,1,2\}$ of colour $C_i$ it contains, with $x_1+\cdots+x_6=6$. Let $k$ be the number of colours with $x_i=2$; then $6-2k$ colours have $x_i=1$ and the rest have $x_i=0$, so $0\le k\le3$. Choose the $k$ doubled colours and then the $6-2k$ single colours among the other $6-k$:
\[
\sum_{k=0}^{3}\dbinom6k\dbinom{6-k}{6-2k}=1\cdot1+6\cdot5+15\cdot6+20\cdot1=1+30+90+20=\boxed{141}.
\]
(Equivalently, $141$ is the coefficient of $x^6$ in $\left(1+x+x^2\right)^6$.)
