The intersection abscissas solve $x^2-8x-4n=0$, so $ac=-4n$. Also $bd=\dfrac{a^2c^2}{16}=81$, giving $ac=\pm36$ and $n=\mp9$. Real intersections require $64+16n\ge0$, excluding $n=-9$. Thus $ac=-36$, $n=9$ and $(ac)(-n)=\boxed{324}$.
