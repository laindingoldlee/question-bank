Write $\alpha=a+bi$ and $\gamma=c+di$. Then
\[f(1)=(4+a+c)+(1+b+d)i,\qquad f(i)=-(4+i)+(ai-b)+(c+di)=(c-b-4)+(a+d-1)i.\]
Both are real exactly when $b=-1-d$ and $a=1-d$. Then
\[|\alpha|+|\gamma|=\sqrt{(1-d)^2+(1+d)^2}+\sqrt{c^2+d^2}=\sqrt{2+2d^2}+\sqrt{c^2+d^2}\ge\sqrt2,\]
with equality for $c=d=0$, i.e. $\alpha=1-i$ and $\gamma=0$. The minimum is $\boxed{\textbf{(C) }\sqrt2}$.
