Subtract $(2x+1)(2x)=4x^2+2x$ from the numerator, leaving $-6x+1$. Subtract $(2x+1)(-3)=-6x-3$, leaving $4$. Thus $\boxed{\dfrac{4x^2-4x+1}{2x+1}=2x-3+\dfrac{4}{2x+1}}$, where $x\ne-\dfrac{1}{2}$. Only common factors can be cancelled; individual terms inside sums cannot be cancelled separately.
