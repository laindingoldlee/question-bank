Children stand in a circle. Counting the next child as first and never making a complete extra lap, Marco is sixth clockwise from Fabian, Julio is eighth counterclockwise from Fabian, and Julio is fourth clockwise from Marco. Clockwise from Julio, Fabian is encountered before Marco. How many children are there?
