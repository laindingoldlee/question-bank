Since $8=2^3$, group the binary digits in threes from the right: $110\ 111\ 101\ 001$. Each group is one octal digit: $110_2=6$, $111_2=7$, $101_2=5$, $001_2=1$. So $x=6751_8$ and the leftmost digit is $\boxed{6}$.
