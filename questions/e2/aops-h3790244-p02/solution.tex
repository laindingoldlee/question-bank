An equilateral triangle of side $s$ has area $\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}s^2$. Thus $a=2$, $b=4$, and $ab=8$. The new area is $16\sqrt3$. Since $27^2=729<768=(16\sqrt3)^2<784=28^2$, its integer part is $\boxed{27}$.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

\end{asy}
