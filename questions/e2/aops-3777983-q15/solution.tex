The centre heights are 5 and 7, so their vertical separation is 2. Pythagoras gives horizontal separation $\sqrt{13^2-2^2}=\boxed{\sqrt{165}}$.
\begin{asy}
import graph;
usepackage("amsmath");
import geometry;
size(7cm);
pair I=(0,5),J=(sqrt(165),7);draw((-6,0)--(21,0));draw(circle(I,5));draw(circle(J,7));draw(I--J,dashed);draw(I--(sqrt(165),5)--J,dotted);label("$I$",I,(0,1));label("$J$",J,(0,1));label("13",(I+J)/2,(0,1));
\end{asy}
