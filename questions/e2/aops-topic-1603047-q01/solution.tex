The first three statements are true: each side of $4(5-5)=5(5-5)$ equals $0$, so this line just says $0=0$.

The error is the next step, ``cancelling'' the common factor $(5-5)$. Cancelling a factor means dividing both sides by it, and $5-5=0$; division by $0$ is not allowed. The rule $ac=bc\Rightarrow a=b$ holds only when $c\ne0$: for $c=0$ the equation $a\cdot0=b\cdot0$ is true for \emph{all} $a$ and $b$, so it says nothing about whether $a=b$. Hence $4=5$ does not follow, and neither does $2+2=5$.
