Find the error in the following ``proof'' that $2+2=5$:
\[
20-20=25-25\ \Longrightarrow\ 5\cdot4-5\cdot4=5\cdot5-5\cdot5\ \Longrightarrow\ 4(5-5)=5(5-5)\ \Longrightarrow\ 4=5\ \Longrightarrow\ 2+2=5 .
\]
