Let the original rectangle be $l$ by $w$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

label("$l$",(2.5,0),S);label("$w$",(0,1.5),W);
label("$l+2$",(3.5,5),N);label("$w+2$",(7,2.5),E);
label("original",(2.5,1.5));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The new area is
\[
(l+2)(w+2)=lw+2l+2w+4=\underbrace{lw}_{\text{area}}+\underbrace{2(l+w)}_{\text{perimeter}}+4 .
\]
So the new area always exceeds the sum of the original area and perimeter by $\boxed{4}$, whatever $l$ and $w$ are.
