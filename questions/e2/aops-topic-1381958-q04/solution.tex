Someone who is $k$ years old in the year $k^2$ was born in the year $k^2-k=k(k-1)$, which increases with $k$:
\[
42\cdot41=1722,\qquad 43\cdot42=1806,\qquad 44\cdot43=1892,\qquad 45\cdot44=1980 .
\]
The only value in $1801$--$1850$ is $1806$ (so $x=43$, and he was $43$ in $1849$), and the only value in $1851$--$1900$ is $1892$ (so $y=44$, and she was $44$ in $1936$). They were born $1892-1806=\boxed{86}$ years apart.
