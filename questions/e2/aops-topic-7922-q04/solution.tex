Every real $u$ can be written as $u=2x+1$ with $x=\dfrac{u-1}{2}$, so
\[f(u)=4\left(\dfrac{u-1}{2}\right)^2+14\cdot\dfrac{u-1}{2}=(u-1)^2+7(u-1)=u^2+5u-6=(u+6)(u-1).\]
The roots of $f(x)=0$ are $-6$ and $1$, with sum $\boxed{-5}$ (as also given by Vieta's formulas).
