Sort the chosen numbers as $x\le y\le z$. Every number in the list from $2$ on is the sum of the two numbers just before it: $2=1+1$, $3=1+2$, $5=2+3$, and so on. If $z$ is at position $k$ of the list, then $x$ and $y$ come from earlier positions, so $x+y$ is at most the sum of the two entries just before $z$, which equals $z$. (The value $z=1$ is impossible, as there are only two cards marked $1$.) So always $x+y\le z$ and the triangle inequality fails.

Hence no choice gives a non-degenerate triangle, and the probability is $\boxed{0}$.
