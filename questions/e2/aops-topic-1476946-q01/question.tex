With no wind, a plane flying at a constant airspeed makes the round trip from $A$ to $B$ and back in $10$ hours. If the wind blows from $A$ toward $B$ at $k$ km/h, the round trip takes $25$ minutes longer. If $A$ and $B$ are $1000$ km apart, find $k$.
