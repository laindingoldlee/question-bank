Let there be $n$ small islands and $k$ completed bridges to the large island. Then $\binom n2+k=28$, with $1\le k\le2n$. For $n\le6$, the total is at most $\binom62+12=27$; for $n\ge8$, the small-island bridges alone number at least 28. Thus $n=7$, with $k=7$, is the only possibility. There are $\boxed8$ islands in all.
