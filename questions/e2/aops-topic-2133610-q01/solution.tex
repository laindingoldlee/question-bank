Since $360=2^3\cdot3^2\cdot5$, every positive divisor has the form $2^a3^b5^c$ with $0\le a\le3$, $0\le b\le2$ and $0\le c\le1$, and different exponent choices give different divisors. So the number of divisors is
\[(3+1)(2+1)(1+1)=\boxed{24}.\]
