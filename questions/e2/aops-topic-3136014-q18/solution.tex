Since $x^3+x=x(x^2+1)$, the condition is $x^2+1\mid1000$. The divisors of 1000 are $1,2,4,5,8,10,20,25,40,50,100,125,200,250,500,1000$. Among these, subtracting 1 gives a square only for $1,2,5,10,50$, corresponding to $|x|=0,1,2,3,7$. Therefore the greatest is $\boxed7$.
