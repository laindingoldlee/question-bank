\textbf{(a)} The powers of $-2$ are $1,-2,4,-8,16,-32,\dots$. Reading $110110$ from the right, the digits $1$ sit at the places $(-2)^1,(-2)^2,(-2)^4,(-2)^5$, so the value is
\[
-2+4+16-32=\boxed{-14}.
\]
\textbf{(b)} Repeatedly divide by $-2$, choosing the remainder $r\in\{0,1\}$ so that $n=-2q+r$:
\[
\begin{array}{rcl}
2020&=&-2\cdot(-1010)+0\\
-1010&=&-2\cdot505+0\\
505&=&-2\cdot(-252)+1\\
-252&=&-2\cdot126+0\\
126&=&-2\cdot(-63)+0\\
-63&=&-2\cdot32+1\\
32&=&-2\cdot(-16)+0\\
-16&=&-2\cdot8+0\\
8&=&-2\cdot(-4)+0\\
-4&=&-2\cdot2+0\\
2&=&-2\cdot(-1)+0\\
-1&=&-2\cdot1+1\\
1&=&-2\cdot0+1
\end{array}
\]
Reading the remainders from bottom to top gives $2020=\boxed{1100000100100_{-2}}$. Check: $4096-2048-32+4=2020$.
