The first character must be one of the letters $A,M,C$ ($3$ choices), and the last must be one of the digits $1,0$ ($2$ choices). The remaining $3$ characters fill the middle three places in $3!=6$ ways. The total is $3\cdot2\cdot6=\boxed{36}$.
