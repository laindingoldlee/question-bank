Let $f(k)$ be the number of Star Wars stories in hours $k,\dots,k+11$, for $k=1,\dots,13$. Moving the window by one hour changes $f$ by at most $1$. Also $f(1)+f(13)=12$ (the two windows are complementary), so one of them is at least $6$ and the other at most $6$. By this "discrete intermediate value" argument some window has $f(k)=6$, i.e. $6$ stories of each kind. $\blacksquare$
