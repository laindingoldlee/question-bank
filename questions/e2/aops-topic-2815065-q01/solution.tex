Among the $8!$ arrangements, swapping the positions of $1$ and $2$ is a bijection between those with $1$ left of $2$ and those with $1$ right of $2$; the same holds for each of the four independent pairs. So the answer is $\dfrac{8!}{2^4}=\boxed{2520}$.
