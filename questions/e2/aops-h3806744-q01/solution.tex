Put $B=(0,0)$, $C=(a,0)$, $D=(m,0)$, and $A=(x,h)$. Then
\[b^2=(x-a)^2+h^2,\quad c^2=x^2+h^2,\quad d^2=(x-m)^2+h^2.\]
Consequently
\[\begin{aligned}mb^2+nc^2&=(m+n)(x^2+h^2)-2max+ma^2\\&=a\bigl((x-m)^2+h^2+m(a-m)\bigr)\\&=a(d^2+mn),\end{aligned}\]
as required. The coordinate $x$ is unrestricted, so the calculation also covers obtuse triangles.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(1.5,3),B=(0,0),C=(5,0),D=(2,0);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--D,blue);label("$A(x,h)$",A,(0,1));label("$B$",B,(-1,-1));label("$C$",C,(1,-1));label("$D$",D,(0,-1));label("$c$",(A+B)/2,(-1,0));label("$b$",(A+C)/2,(1,0));label("$d$",(A+D)/2,(1,0));label("$m$",(B+D)/2,(0,-1));label("$n$",(D+C)/2,(0,-1));
\end{asy}
