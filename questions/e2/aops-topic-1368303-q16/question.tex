A circle of radius $1$ is tangent to a circle of radius $2$. The sides of $\triangle ABC$ are tangent to the circles: $AB$ and $AC$ are tangent to both circles, $BC$ is tangent to the circle of radius $2$, and the circle of radius $1$ lies between the larger circle and $A$. The sides $\overline{AB}$ and $\overline{AC}$ are congruent. What is the area of $\triangle ABC$?

$\textbf{(A) }\dfrac{35}{2}\qquad\textbf{(B) }15\sqrt2\qquad\textbf{(C) }\dfrac{64}{3}\qquad\textbf{(D) }16\sqrt2\qquad\textbf{(E) }24$
