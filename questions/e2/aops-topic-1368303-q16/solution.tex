By symmetry both centres lie on the altitude from $A$. Put the foot of the altitude at the origin, so the larger circle has centre $O_1=(0,2)$, the smaller has centre $O_2=(0,5)$ (the circles are externally tangent, so $O_1O_2=3$), and $A=(0,h)$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s=0.2){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
pair ft(pair P,pair A,pair B){return A+dot(P-A,unit(B-A))*unit(B-A);}
real t=2*sqrt(2);
pair A=(0,8),B=(-t,0),C=(t,0),O1=(0,2),O2=(0,5);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(circle(O1,2),blue); draw(circle(O2,1),blue);
draw(A--(0,0),gray+dashed);
pair T1=ft(O1,A,C), T2=ft(O2,A,C);
draw(O1--T1,red); draw(O2--T2,red);
draw(ra(T1,A-T1,O1-T1,0.3),red); draw(ra(T2,A-T2,O2-T2,0.3),red);
dot(O1); dot(O2);
label("$A$",A,N); label("$B$",B,SW); label("$C$",C,SE);
label("$O_1$",O1,W); label("$O_2$",O2,W);
label("$2$",(O1+T1)/2,S,red); label("$1$",(O2+T2)/2,S,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The radii to the points of tangency on $AC$ are perpendicular to $AC$, so the two right triangles $AO_2T_2$ and $AO_1T_1$ are similar:
\[\dfrac{1}{AO_2}=\dfrac{2}{AO_1}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \dfrac{1}{h-5}=\dfrac{2}{h-2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad h=8 .\]
So $AO_1=6$, and the half-angle $\theta$ at $A$ has $\sin\theta=\dfrac{2}{6}=\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $\tan\theta=\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt2}$. Half the base is $8\tan\theta=\dfrac{8}{2\sqrt2}=2\sqrt2$, so $BC=4\sqrt2$ and
\[[ABC]=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot4\sqrt2\cdot8=\boxed{\textbf{(D) }16\sqrt2}.\]
