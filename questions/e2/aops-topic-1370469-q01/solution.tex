Count corners of faces. The faces have $4a+5a=9a$ corners in total.
\begin{itemize}
\item Each edge lies on exactly two faces, so counting the sides of all faces counts each edge twice: $E=\dfrac{9a}{2}$.
\item Each vertex lies on exactly three faces, so $V=\dfrac{9a}{3}=3a$.
\end{itemize}
Nothing is wrong with $E=4.5a$: it only shows that $a$ must be even for $E$ to be a whole number. Euler's formula $V-E+F=2$ gives
\[
3a-\dfrac{9a}{2}+2a=2\iff\dfrac {a}{2}=2\iff a=4,
\]
which is even, as it must be. So the polyhedron has $\boxed{8}$ faces ($4$ quadrilaterals and $4$ pentagons), with $V=12$ and $E=18$.

Such a polyhedron exists: start with a cube and cut off (bevel) two edges that do not share a vertex and are not parallel, say the front top edge and the back right vertical edge. Bevelling an edge replaces it by a new quadrilateral face and adds one side to each of the two faces at its ends, and keeps three faces at every vertex. Here the left, right, top and bottom faces each gain a side and become pentagons, while the front and back faces stay quadrilaterals, and the two new faces are quadrilaterals.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real c=0.3;
pair pj(triple p){return (p.x+0.42*p.y, p.z+0.3*p.y);}
triple b1=(0,0,0),b2=(1,0,0),b3=(1,1-c,0),b4=(1-c,1,0),b5=(0,1,0);
triple t1=(0,c,1),t2=(1,c,1),t3=(1,1-c,1),t4=(1-c,1,1),t5=(0,1,1);
triple f1=(0,0,1-c),f2=(1,0,1-c);
fill(pj(b1)--pj(b2)--pj(f2)--pj(f1)--cycle,gray(0.88));
fill(pj(f1)--pj(f2)--pj(t2)--pj(t1)--cycle,gray(0.88));
fill(pj(t1)--pj(t2)--pj(t3)--pj(t4)--pj(t5)--cycle,paleblue);
fill(pj(b2)--pj(b3)--pj(t3)--pj(t2)--pj(f2)--cycle,paleblue);
pen v=linewidth(0.9), h=dashed+gray;
draw(pj(b1)--pj(b2),v); draw(pj(b2)--pj(b3),v); draw(pj(b3)--pj(b4),h); draw(pj(b4)--pj(b5),h); draw(pj(b5)--pj(b1),h);
draw(pj(b1)--pj(f1),v); draw(pj(b2)--pj(f2),v); draw(pj(b3)--pj(t3),v); draw(pj(b4)--pj(t4),h); draw(pj(b5)--pj(t5),h);
draw(pj(t1)--pj(t2)--pj(t3)--pj(t4)--pj(t5)--cycle,v);
draw(pj(f1)--pj(f2),v); draw(pj(f1)--pj(t1),v); draw(pj(f2)--pj(t2),v);
label("$4$",pj((0.78,0,0.2)),fontsize(9pt)); label("$4$",pj((0.5,c/2,1-c/2)),fontsize(8pt));
label("$5$",pj((0.5,0.5,1)),fontsize(9pt)); label("$5$",pj((1,0.5,0.45)),fontsize(9pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

(The visible faces are labelled with their numbers of sides; hidden edges are dashed.)
