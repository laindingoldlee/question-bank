For $0\le x\le1$ a function satisfies $f\left(\dfrac x{x+1}\right)=\dfrac{f(x)}2$ and $f(1-x)=1-f(x)$. Find $f\left(\dfrac23\right)+f\left(\dfrac25\right)+f\left(\dfrac27\right)+\cdots+f\left(\dfrac2{2n+1}\right)+\cdots$.
