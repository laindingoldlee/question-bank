Applying the first rule to $x=\dfrac2{2n+1}$ gives $\dfrac x{x+1}=\dfrac2{2n+3}$, so each term is half the previous one and the sum is $2f\left(\dfrac23\right)$.

From $f\left(\dfrac12\right)=1-f\left(\dfrac12\right)$, $f\left(\dfrac12\right)=\dfrac12$; then $f\left(\dfrac13\right)=\dfrac12f\left(\dfrac12\right)=\dfrac14$ and $f\left(\dfrac23\right)=1-\dfrac14=\dfrac34$. The sum is $\boxed{\dfrac32}$.
