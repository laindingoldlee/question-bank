Centre the cube at the origin with coordinates between -6 and six. Being closer to the origin than vertex $(\pm6,\pm6,\pm6)$ is equivalent to the corresponding signed sum of coordinates being less than nine. All eight inequalities combine to $|x|+|y|+|z|<9$. The full octahedron has volume $4\cdot\dfrac{9^3}{3}=972$. Its six portions beyond the cube faces are disjoint pyramids, each of volume $\int_6^9 2(9-x)^2\,dx=18$. Therefore the required volume is $972-6\cdot18=\boxed{864}$.

\end{asy}
