For $1\le k\le2023$, take $a_k=3^{2023-k}4^{k-1}$. Each is a positive integer and $a_{k+1}=\dfrac{4}{3}a_k>a_k$, so they are distinct. For $k<2023$, put $t=3^{2022-k}4^{k-1}$, an integer; then $a_k=3t,a_{k+1}=4t$, and $a_k^2+a_{k+1}^2=(5t)^2$. This proves the construction.
