\emph{No real roots.} Write the polynomial as
\[x^4\left(5x^2+103x+531\right)+x^2\left(28901x^2+395819x+2000000\right)+\left(3948192x^2+78192834x+1938581923\right).\]
Each quadratic has a positive leading coefficient and a negative discriminant:
\[103^2-4\cdot5\cdot531=-11,\qquad395819^2-4\cdot28901\cdot2000000<1.6\cdot10^{11}-2.3\cdot10^{11}<0,\]
\[78192834^2-4\cdot3948192\cdot1938581923<6.2\cdot10^{15}-3.0\cdot10^{16}<0.\]
So all three quadratics are positive for every real $x$, and the polynomial is positive on $\mathbb R$.

\emph{The sum.} All six roots are non-real. By Vieta their sum is $-\dfrac{103}{5}$, which is real, so the sum of their real parts is $\boxed{-\dfrac{103}{5}}$.
