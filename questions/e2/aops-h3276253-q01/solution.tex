Write the integers increasingly as $a<b<c<d<e$. The conditions give
\[
b=37-a,\quad e=119-a,\quad c+d=164+a.
\]
The ordering is equivalent to
\[
1\le a\le18,\qquad 46+2a\le c\le\left\lfloor\dfrac{163+a}{2}\right\rfloor.
\]
Indeed, the lower bound on $c$ comes from $d<e$ and also implies $b<c$; the upper bound gives $c<d$. Thus every set is uniquely
\[
\boxed{\{a,\ 37-a,\ c,\ 164+a-c,\ 119-a\}}
\]
with the indicated integer parameters. The number is
\[
\sum_{a=1}^{18}\left(\left\lfloor\dfrac{163+a}{2}\right\rfloor-45-2a\right)=\boxed{396}.
\]
The source conditions therefore do not specify just one set.
