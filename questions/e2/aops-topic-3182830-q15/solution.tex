Place $A=(0,0)$ and $B=(14,0)$. The side lengths give $C=(9,12)$ and area $84$. The semiperimeter is $21$, hence the inradius is $4$ and $AX=21-13=8$. Thus $X=(8,0)$, $I=(8,4)$ and $Y=(8,8)$. Line $AC$ is $y=\dfrac{4}{3}x$, so $Z=(8,\dfrac{32}{3})$. Therefore $[AYZ]=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot8\cdot\left(\dfrac{32}{3}-8\right)=\dfrac{32}{3}$ and $m+n=\boxed{35}$.
