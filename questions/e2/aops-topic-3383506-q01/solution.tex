The unobstructed side contributes a semicircle of radius $14$. Wrapping around the nearer and farther corners leaves lengths $14-2=12$ and $14-8=6$, each contributing an exterior quarter-circle. From the nearer corner the rope can also wrap around the next corner, leaving $14-2-10=2$, adding another quarter-circle. No other corner can be reached along the shed. These four regions have disjoint interiors. Thus the area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\pi14^2+\dfrac{1}{4}\pi(12^2+6^2+2^2)=\boxed{144\pi}$ square meters, so $n=144$.
