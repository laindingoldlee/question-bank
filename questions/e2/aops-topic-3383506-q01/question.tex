A cow is tethered outside a square shed of side $10$ meters. The tether point lies on one side, $2$ meters from one corner, and the rope is $14$ meters long. The rope can wrap around corners but cannot pass through the shed. Find the reachable grazing area, expressed as $n\pi$.
