For $n=2$ the ratio is $\dfrac{d(2)}{2}=\dfrac{2}{2}=1$.

For $n\ge3$, every divisor of $n$ is one of $1,2,\dots,n$, but $n-1$ is not a divisor: $n-1\ge2$ and $n=(n-1)+1$ leaves remainder $1$ on division by $n-1$. So $d(n)\le n-1$ and $\dfrac{d(n)}{n}\le\dfrac{n-1}{n}<1$.

Hence the largest composite ratio is $1$, attained only at $\boxed{n=2}$.
