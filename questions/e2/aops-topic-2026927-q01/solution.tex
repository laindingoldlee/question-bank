On day $n$ the factory produces $1+2+\dots+n=T_n=\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}=\binom{n+1}{2}$ chocolates. The total over $12$ days is, by the hockey-stick identity,
\[
\sum_{n=1}^{12}\binom{n+1}{2}=\binom{14}{3}=\dfrac{14\cdot13\cdot12}{6}=\boxed{364}.
\]
(Check by adding: $1+3+6+10+15+21+28+36+45+55+66+78=364$.)
