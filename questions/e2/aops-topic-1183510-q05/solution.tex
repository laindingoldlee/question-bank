The pair sums $(a+b,\ c+d)$ come from splitting $\{1,2,3,4\}$ into two pairs, so they are $(3,7)$, $(4,6)$ or $(5,5)$ in some order. The candidates are
\[3^7=2187,\quad 7^3=343,\quad 4^6=4096,\quad 6^4=1296,\quad 5^5=3125 .\]
The greatest is $4^6=\boxed{4096}$, for example with $a=1$, $b=3$, $c=2$, $d=4$.
