Let $AM=x$, so $MB=7-x$. Pythagoras in the two right triangles gives
\[
CM^2=32-x^2=25-(7-x)^2 .
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s=0.2){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
pair A=(0,0),B=(7,0),C=(4,4),M=(4,0);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); draw(C--M,red+dashed); draw(ra(M,C-M,B-M,0.35));
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,N);label("$M$",M,S);
label("$4\sqrt2$",(A+C)/2,NW); label("$5$",(B+C)/2,NE); label("$x$",(A+M)/2,S); label("$7-x$",(M+B)/2,S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

So $32-x^2=25-49+14x-x^2$, $14x=56$, $x=4$, and $CM=\sqrt{32-16}=\boxed{4}$.
