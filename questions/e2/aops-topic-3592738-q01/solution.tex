Put $p=xy$. The power-sum identity gives $x^5+y^5=(x+y)^5-5p(x+y)^3+5p^2(x+y)=1024-320p+20p^2$. Thus $p^2-16p+28=0$, so $p=2$ or $14$. Since real $x,y$ with sum $4$ satisfy $xy\le4$, only $p=2$ is possible. The roots of $t^2-4t+2=0$ are $2\pm\sqrt2$. Hence the two ordered pairs are $\boxed{(2+\sqrt2,2-\sqrt2),(2-\sqrt2,2+\sqrt2)}$.
