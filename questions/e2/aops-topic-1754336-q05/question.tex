A circle is inscribed in a rhombus whose diagonals are $2$ feet and $4$ feet long. What is the area, in square feet, of the region of the rhombus that is outside the circular region? Express your answer as a decimal to the nearest tenth.

A student argues: the rhombus has area $4$, which equals the total area of $4$ congruent right triangles with height $r$ and base $2\sqrt5$, so $4=4\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot r\cdot2\sqrt5$, $r^2=\dfrac{1}{5}$, and the answer is $4-\dfrac{\pi}{5}\approx3.4$. Find the error.
