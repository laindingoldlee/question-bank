Write $127=nq_1+r_1$, $324=nq_2+r_2$, $411=nq_3+r_3$ with $0\le r_i<n$. Adding,
\[862=n(q_1+q_2+q_3)+12,\qquad\text{so}\qquad n\mid850=2\cdot5^2\cdot17 .\]
The divisors of $850$ in decreasing order are $850,425,170,85,50,34,25,17,\dots$. Check the remainder sums:
\begin{itemize}
\item $n=850$ or $425$: $127+324+411=862$;
\item $n=170$: $127+154+71=352$;\quad $n=85$: $42+69+71=182$;\quad $n=50$: $27+24+11=62$;
\item $n=34$: $25+18+3=46$;\quad $n=25$: $2+24+11=37$;
\item $n=17$: $127=7\cdot17+8$, $324=19\cdot17+1$, $411=24\cdot17+3$, and $8+1+3=12$.
\end{itemize}
So the largest possible $n$ is $\boxed{17}$. (The only other $n$ that works is $10$.)
