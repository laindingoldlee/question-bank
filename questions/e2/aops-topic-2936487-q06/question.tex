A bouquet originally contains 50\% more magnolias than myosotis and costs 50 dollars. Halving the magnolia count and multiplying the myosotis count by 1.5 leaves the price unchanged. What would it cost to multiply the original magnolia count by 1.5 and halve the original myosotis count?
