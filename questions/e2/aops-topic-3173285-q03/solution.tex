At $x=1$, the left side is $10$, so $x\ne1$. Multiplying by $x-1$ gives $x^{10}-1=0$. The only real possibilities are $x=1$ and $x=-1$; the first is excluded, while the second makes the ten alternating terms cancel. Therefore $\boxed{x=-1}$.
