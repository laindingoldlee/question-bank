A house contains $c$ coins, where $1\le c\le10000$, and $k\ge2$ kids visit it. The kids arrive one at a time. Each kid, believing he is the first to arrive, takes exactly $\dfrac1k$ of the coins remaining in the house at that moment; coins cannot be split. (Some coins may remain after all $k$ kids have come.) For how many values of $c$ is this possible for at least one $k\ge2$?
