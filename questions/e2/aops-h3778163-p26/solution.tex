There are $5\binom62 4!=1800$ labeled-face colorings. A rotation preserving a coloring must fix each of the four uniquely colored faces individually, so it must be the identity. Thus every orbit under the $24$ cube rotations has size $24$, and the answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{1800}{24}=75}$.
