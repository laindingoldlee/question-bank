We have $(x+y)^2=(x-y)^2+4xy=49+32=81$, so $x+y=9$ or $x+y=-9$. Hence
\[
x^2-y^2=(x-y)(x+y)=\pm63 .
\]
Both occur: $(x,y)=(8,1)$ gives $63$, and $(x,y)=(-1,-8)$ gives $-63$. So $x^2-y^2$ is $\boxed{63\ \text{or}\ -63}$.
