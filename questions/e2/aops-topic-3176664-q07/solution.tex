Use $A=(0,0),B=(30,0),C=(30,30),D=(0,30)$. The circle centre is $(15,30-5\sqrt3)$ and radius $10\sqrt3$. At $x=0$ or 30, its vertical displacement from the centre is $\sqrt{300-225}=5\sqrt3$. The lower intersections therefore have height $30-10\sqrt3$, giving rectangle area $30(30-10\sqrt3)=900-300\sqrt3$. Hence $\boxed{a+b+c=1203}$.
