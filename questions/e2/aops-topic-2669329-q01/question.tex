Let $0<a<b$ and $p\ge0$. Without using the fundamental theorem of calculus, prove that the area under the curve $y=x^p$ between $x=a$ and $x=b$ is
\[\dfrac{b^{p+1}-a^{p+1}}{p+1}.\]
You may use the limit $\displaystyle\lim_{r\to1}\dfrac{r^q-1}{r-1}=q$ for real $q>0$.
