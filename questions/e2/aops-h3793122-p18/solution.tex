Every liar has exactly one liar neighbor, so liars occur in blocks of two and all must falsely claim both neighbors are liars. Truthful blocks have size one or two; a larger block would contain someone with no liar neighbors who could truthfully make neither stated claim. Let there be $u$ singleton truthful blocks and $v$ double truthful blocks. The number of liar blocks is $u+v$. Counting people gives $3u+4v=30$, while counting the eighteen both-liars statements gives $3u+2v=18$. Thus $v=6,u=2$, and the truthful population is $u+2v=\boxed{14}$.
