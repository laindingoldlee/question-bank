First compute $51^8$ by repeated squaring:
\[51^2=2601,\qquad 51^4=2601^2=6765201,\qquad 51^8=6765201^2=45767944570401.\]
Multiplying the equation by $4$ and dividing by $9$,
\[z=\dfrac{51^8-9.9}{36}=\dfrac{45767944570391.1}{36}.\]
Since $36\cdot1271331793621=45767944570356$ and $45767944570391.1-45767944570356=35.1=36\cdot0.975$,
\[z=\boxed{1271331793621.975}=\dfrac{50853271744879}{40}.\]
