Complete the squares:
\[
(x+2)^2+(y+3)^2=4+9-9=4,\qquad (x-7)^2+(y-9)^2=49+81-129=1 .
\]
So the first circle has centre $(-2,-3)$ and radius $2$, and the second has centre $(7,9)$ and radius $1$. The distance between the centres is
\[
\sqrt{(7+2)^2+(9+3)^2}=\sqrt{81+144}=15 .
\]
Since $15>2+1$, the circles are outside each other, and the shortest distance between them is measured along the line of centres:
\[
15-2-1=\boxed{12}.
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(-2,-3),B=(7,9);
draw((-4.8,0)--(10.5,0),gray,Arrow(4)); draw((0,-5.5)--(0,11),gray,Arrow(4));
label("$x$",(10.5,0),S,gray); label("$y$",(0,11),W,gray);
draw(circle(A,2),linewidth(0.9)); draw(circle(B,1),linewidth(0.9));
pair P=A+2*unit(B-A), Q=B-unit(B-A);
draw(A--P,blue+dashed); draw(Q--B,blue+dashed); draw(P--Q,red+linewidth(1.1));
dot(A);dot(B);dot(P,red);dot(Q,red);
label("$(-2,-3)$",A-(0.7,2),S); label("$(7,9)$",B+(1,0),E);
label("$12$",(P+Q)/2,SE,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
