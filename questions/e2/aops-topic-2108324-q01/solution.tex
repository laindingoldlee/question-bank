Ryan's distance along the trail is
\[5\cdot\dfrac{45}{60}+3\cdot\dfrac{80}{60}+1.5\cdot\dfrac{20}{60}=3.75+4+0.5=8.25\text{ miles}.\]
With a constant grade, the height gained is proportional to the distance walked along the trail (the trail and its height profile form similar right triangles). So his height above the valley floor is
\[10079\cdot\dfrac{8.25}{10.7}=\dfrac{83151.75}{10.7}\approx7771.2\text{ feet},\]
and the Clif bar falls about $\boxed{7771}$ feet.
