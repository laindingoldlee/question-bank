Mount Whitney rises $10{,}079$ feet above the valley floor, and the trail from the valley floor to its summit is $10.7$ miles long. Assume that the trail climbs at a constant grade all the way. Ryan starts at the bottom of the trail and hikes up it at $5$ mph for $45$ minutes, then at $3$ mph for $80$ minutes, and then at $1.5$ mph for $20$ minutes. Then he stops for lunch, and one of his Clif bars falls over a cliff, straight down to the valley floor. How far does the Clif bar fall? Round your answer to the nearest foot.
