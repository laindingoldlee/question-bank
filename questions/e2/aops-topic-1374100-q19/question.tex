A bug starts at the origin of the coordinate plane. In each move it chooses a lattice point and moves to it along a straight segment that passes through no other lattice point (for example, it cannot move directly from $(-1,-1)$ to $(1,1)$). After a sequence of moves it has travelled a total distance of exactly $6$ units and is at a lattice point $(x,y)$ with $|x|+|y|=4$. How many such sequences of moves are possible?

(You may use the fact that if $\sqrt{n_1}+\sqrt{n_2}+\cdots+\sqrt{n_k}$ is an integer for positive integers $n_i$, then every $\sqrt{n_i}$ is an integer.)
