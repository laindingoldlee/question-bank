Since $p>5$ is prime, it is odd and not divisible by $3$ or $5$. Hence $p^2\equiv1\pmod8$ and $p^2\equiv1\pmod3$, so $p^2\equiv1\pmod{24}$. Among $1,25,49,73,97$ modulo $120$, only $1$ and $49$ are congruent to a nonzero square ($1$ or $4$) modulo $5$. Since the remainder is not $1$, it is $\boxed{49}$.
