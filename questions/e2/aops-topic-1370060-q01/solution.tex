Split $2a^2$ as $a^2+a^2$ and group:
\[2a^2+b^2-2a+2ab+1=(a^2+2ab+b^2)+(a^2-2a+1)=(a+b)^2+(a-1)^2 .\]
A sum of two squares of real numbers is $0$ only when both are $0$, so $a-1=0$ and $a+b=0$. Hence $\boxed{a=1,\ b=-1}$, and these values do satisfy the equation.
