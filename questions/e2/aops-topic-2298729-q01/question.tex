A dishonest dealer uses faulty weights. When buying, he receives $x\%$ more goods than he pays for; when selling, he charges his customers for $x\%$ more goods than he actually gives them. He buys and sells at the same price per unit of weight. Show that his gain, as a percentage of his outlay, is $\left(2x+\dfrac{x^2}{100}\right)\%$.
