Divisibility by $3$ makes each adjacent digit sum a multiple of $3$. Thus the digit residues alternate $r,-r$ modulo $3$. They cannot all be $0$, since only $9,6,3,0$ are available, fewer than six distinct digits. Therefore the digits must be the six nonzero-residue digits $8,7,5,4,2,1$ in descending order. They alternate residues as required, giving $\boxed{875421}$.
