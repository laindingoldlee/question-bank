We need $x\ne0$. Bring everything to one side:
\[
\dfrac{15}{x}-x-2>0\iff\dfrac{15-2x-x^2}{x}>0\iff\dfrac{-(x+5)(x-3)}{x}>0\iff\dfrac{(x+5)(x-3)}{x}<0 .
\]
The expression $\dfrac{(x+5)(x-3)}{x}$ changes sign only at $x=-5$, $0$, $3$:
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccc}
 & x<-5 & -5<x<0 & 0<x<3 & x>3\\\hline
\text{sign} & - & + & - & +
\end{array}
\]
So the solution set is
\[
\boxed{x<-5\ \text{ or }\ 0<x<3}.
\]
(Multiplying the inequality by $x$ without considering its sign would lose the part $x<-5$.)
