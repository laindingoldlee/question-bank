Pyro, Pyra, and Bob have distinct types: type 1 always tells the truth, type 2 always lies, and type 3 alternates truth and falsehood on successive statements, starting either way. They say, in order:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Pyro: Pyra is the liar.
\item Pyra: Pyro is the alternator.
\item Bob: I am the alternator.
\item Pyro: I am the alternator.
\end{enumerate}
If their respective type numbers are $A,B,C$, find $100A+10B+C$.
