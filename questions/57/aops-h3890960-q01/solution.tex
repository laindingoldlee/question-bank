Write $AB=c,AC=b$ and put the internal bisector on the positive $x$-axis:
\[A=(0,0),\quad B=(\dfrac{c}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt3c}{2}),\quad
C=(\dfrac{b}{2},-\dfrac{\sqrt3b}{2}),\quad P=(b+c,0).\]
Then
\[PB^2=(b+\dfrac{c}{2})^2+\dfrac{3c^2}{4}=b^2+bc+c^2,\]
and similarly $PC^2=b^2+bc+c^2$. Finally,
\[BC^2=\dfrac{(b-c)^2}{4}+\dfrac{3(b+c)^2}{4}=b^2+bc+c^2.\]
All three side lengths are equal and positive, so $PBC$ is equilateral.

\end{asy}
