The inequality is unchanged when $x$ is replaced by $-x$ or $y$ by $-y$, so the region is symmetric about both axes. In the first quadrant it is $x+y\le4$ with $x,y\ge0$: a right triangle with legs $4$, of area $8$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((-5.2,0)--(5.2,0),Arrow(6));draw((0,-5.2)--(0,5.2),Arrow(6));
path d=(4,0)--(0,4)--(-4,0)--(0,-4)--cycle;
fill(d,rgb(0.85,0.92,1));draw(d,linewidth(1));
label("$x$",(5.2,0),E);label("$y$",(0,5.2),N);
label("$4$",(4,0),SE);label("$-4$",(-4,0),SW);label("$4$",(0,4),NE);label("$-4$",(0,-4),SE);
label("$x+y=4$",(2,2),NE,fontsize(9pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

So the region is the square with vertices $(\pm4,0)$, $(0,\pm4)$, and its area is $4\cdot8=\boxed{32}$ (equivalently, a square with diagonals $8$ has area $\dfrac{8\cdot8}{2}=32$).
