Put $a_1=a$, $a_2=b$, with $0<a<b$. Iterating gives $a_7=5a+8b=120$. Modulo 5, $b=5k$, whence $a=24-8k$. Positivity gives $k=1$ or 2. The first gives $(a,b)=(16,5)$, violating increase; the second gives $(8,10)$ and works. Therefore $a_8=8a+13b=64+130=\boxed{194}$.
