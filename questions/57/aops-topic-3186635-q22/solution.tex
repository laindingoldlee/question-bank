Scale lengths so $PC=4$, $PA=5$, and $CD=6$, hence $PD=10$. Power of $P$ gives $PB=\dfrac{PC\cdot PD}{PA}=8$. Use independent unit direction vectors $u,v$ along the two secants, so $A=5u$, $B=8u$, $C=4v$, $D=10v$. Write $X=A+t(D-A)=B+s(C-B)$. Comparing coefficients gives $5(1-t)=8(1-s)$ and $10t=4s$, so $t=\dfrac{1}{5}$. Thus $XD=4XA$. The triangles $BXD$ and $BXA$ share altitude from $B$ to $AD$, giving area ratio $4=\dfrac{4}{1}$, and $m+n=\boxed5$.

\end{asy}
