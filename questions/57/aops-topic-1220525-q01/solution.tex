$f(x)=3(x-5)^2+1$. With $t=|x-5|\ge0$: $h=3t^2-2t+1=3\left(t-\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^2+\dfrac{2}{3}$. The minimum is $\boxed{\dfrac{2}{3}}$, at $t=\dfrac{1}{3}$, i.e. $x=5\pm\dfrac{1}{3}$.
