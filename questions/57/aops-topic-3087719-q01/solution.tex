Put $n=\lfloor x\rfloor\ne0$, so $x=\dfrac{2002n}{2003}$. The floor condition $n\le x<n+1$ becomes $0\le-\dfrac{n}{2003}<1$. Thus $-2003<n\le0$; excluding 0 leaves $n=-2002,-2001,\ldots,-1$. The expression for $x$ increases with $n$, so its minimum is $\boxed{-\dfrac{2002^2}{2003}}$, attained at $n=-2002$.
