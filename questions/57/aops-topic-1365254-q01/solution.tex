Move everything to one side: $x^3-3x^2+(a+2)x-a=0$. Substituting $x=1$ gives $1-3+a+2-a=0$, so $x=1$ is a root and
\[
x^3-3x^2+(a+2)x-a=(x-1)\left(x^2-2x+a\right).
\]
The quadratic factor has roots $1\pm\sqrt{1-a}$; for three distinct real roots we need $a<1$. Then
\[
x_1=1-\sqrt{1-a}<x_2=1<x_3=1+\sqrt{1-a},
\]
so $x_1+x_3=2$ and
\[
x_3^2-x_1^2=(x_3-x_1)(x_3+x_1)=2(x_3-x_1).
\]
Therefore
\[
4x_1-x_1^2+x_3^2=4x_1+2x_3-2x_1=2(x_1+x_3)=\boxed{4},
\]
for every admissible $a$.
