The perfect squares in the list are $k^2$ for positive integers $k$ with $k^2\le n$, i.e.\ $k\le\sqrt n$. The positive integers $k\le\sqrt n$ are $1,2,\dots,\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor$, so the count is
\[
\boxed{\lfloor\sqrt n\rfloor}.
\]
For example, from $1$ to $50$ there are $\lfloor\sqrt{50}\rfloor=7$ squares: $1,4,9,16,25,36,49$.
