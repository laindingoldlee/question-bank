Bob has seven shirts, one of each colour of the rainbow: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo and violet. Each morning he puts on one of them at random, each with probability $\dfrac{1}{7}$. On a day when he wears red he always sings in the shower; on a day when he wears indigo he never does; and on a day when he wears any of the other five colours he sings in the shower with probability $65\%$. What is the probability that on a randomly chosen day Bob sings in the shower?
