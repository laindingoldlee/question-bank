The relation $f^{30}=\mathrm{id}$ makes $f$ a permutation, and the second condition says its order is exactly 30. Its cycle lengths divide 30 and are at most 10, hence belong to $1,2,3,5,6,10$. A factor 5 requires a 5- or 10-cycle. A 10-cycle leaves no room for a factor 3, so there must be a 5-cycle. Among the remaining five elements, factors 3 and 2 require one 3-cycle and one 2-cycle. Thus the cycle type is exactly $(5,3,2)$. The number is $\dfrac{10!}{5\cdot3\cdot2}=\boxed{120960}$.
