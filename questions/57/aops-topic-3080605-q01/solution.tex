In increasing order the data are $12,12,14,14,15,15,17,19,23,24,26,26,27,27,27$. The eighth value gives median $19$. The lower and upper halves each contain seven values, with medians $14,26$. Thus the five-number summary is $\boxed{(12,14,19,26,27)}$. Draw the box from $14$ to $26$, its median line at $19$, and whiskers to $12,27$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
