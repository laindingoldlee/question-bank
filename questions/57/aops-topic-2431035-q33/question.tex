Let points $A=(0,0)$, $B=(1,2)$, $C=(3,3)$ and $D=(4,0)$. Quadrilateral $ABCD$ is cut into equal-area pieces by a line passing through $A$. This line intersects $\overline{CD}$ at the point $\left(\dfrac{p}{q},\dfrac{r}{s}\right)$, where these fractions are in lowest terms. What is $p+q+r+s$?

$\textbf{(A) }54\qquad\textbf{(B) }58\qquad\textbf{(C) }62\qquad\textbf{(D) }70\qquad\textbf{(E) }75$
