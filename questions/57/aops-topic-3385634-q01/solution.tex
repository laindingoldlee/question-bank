Write $m=111a,n=31b$ with $a,b>0$. Modulo $31$, $18a\equiv2$. Multiplying by $19$, the inverse of $18$, gives $a\equiv7\pmod{31}$. Since $111a<2017$, we have $1\le a\le18$, so $a=7$. Thus $m=777,n=1240$, and $\boxed{n-m=463}$.
