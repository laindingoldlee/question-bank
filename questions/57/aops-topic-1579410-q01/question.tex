Let $p$ be a prime and $n$ a positive integer, and let $s_p(n)$ be the sum of the digits of $n$ written in base $p$. Prove that the exponent of $p$ in the prime factorisation of $n!$ equals
\[
\dfrac{n-s_p(n)}{p-1}.
\]
(For example, $10=1010_2$ has $s_2(10)=2$, and $10!$ contains $2^{\dfrac{10-2}{1}}=2^8$.)
