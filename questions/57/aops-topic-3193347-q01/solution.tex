Let $AB=AC=s$. The cosine rule gives $100=2s^2-2s^2\cos120^\circ=3s^2$, so $s=\dfrac{10}{\sqrt3}$. The required distance is $s\sin120^\circ=\dfrac{10}{\sqrt3}\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}=\boxed5$. The perpendicular foot lies on the extension of $AC$, not necessarily its segment.

usepackage("amsmath");

pair A=(0,0),C=(4,0),B=4dir(120),H=(-2,0);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(H--A,dashed);draw(B--H,blue);label("$A$",A,S);label("$B$",B,N);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$H$",H,S);label("$120^\circ$",.8dir(60));
\end{asy}
