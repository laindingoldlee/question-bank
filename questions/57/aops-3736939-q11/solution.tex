Write the tower as $6^E$, where $E=7^F$ and $F=6^G$. The remaining tower makes $G\ge2$, so $F$ is divisible by 4. Hence $E\equiv2^F\equiv1\pmod5$. Also $E\ge4$, so $6^E$ is divisible by 16. Modulo 25, the binomial theorem gives $6^E=(1+5)^E\equiv1+5E\equiv6$. We therefore seek $r\equiv0\pmod{16}$ and $r\equiv6\pmod{25}$. Writing $r=16t$ gives $t\equiv16\pmod{25}$, since $16\cdot16=256\equiv6$. The least nonnegative solution is $\boxed{256}$.
