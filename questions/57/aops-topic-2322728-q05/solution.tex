Each of the other three people shakes hands with the infected person exactly once, and becomes infected with probability $\dfrac15$ (handshakes among the healthy people transmit nothing, since nobody else is contagious). By linearity of expectation the expected number of new infections is
\[3\cdot\dfrac15=\boxed{\dfrac35=0.6}.\]
(Counting the first person as well, the expected number of infected people is $1.6$.)
