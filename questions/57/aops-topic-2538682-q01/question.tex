A $6$-problem olympiad has $3$ algebra problems and $3$ number theory problems. Addison solves each algebra problem with probability $\dfrac45$ and each number theory problem with probability $\dfrac13$, independently of each other. She qualifies for the next round if she solves at least $3$ problems. What is the probability that Addison does not qualify?
