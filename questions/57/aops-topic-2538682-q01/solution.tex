Let $A$ and $T$ be the numbers of algebra and number theory problems she solves. Then
\[P(A=0,1,2,3)=\dfrac{1}{125},\ \dfrac{12}{125},\ \dfrac{48}{125},\ \dfrac{64}{125},\qquad P(T=0,1,2,3)=\dfrac{8}{27},\ \dfrac{12}{27},\ \dfrac{6}{27},\ \dfrac{1}{27}.\]
She fails when $A+T\le2$:
\[P=P(A=0)P(T\le2)+P(A=1)P(T\le1)+P(A=2)P(T=0)=\dfrac{1\cdot26+12\cdot20+48\cdot8}{125\cdot27}=\dfrac{650}{3375}=\boxed{\dfrac{26}{135}}.\]
