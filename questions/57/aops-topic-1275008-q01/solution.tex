A number is divisible by $11$ exactly when the sum $S_1$ of its digits in odd positions minus the sum $S_2$ of its digits in even positions is divisible by $11$. Here $S_1+S_2=24$, so $S_1-S_2=24-2S_2$, with $S_2$ the sum of two of the digits, between $5$ and $15$. Then $24-2S_2$ ranges from $-6$ to $14$ and is a multiple of $11$ only when it is $0$, i.e. $S_2=12$. The only pair summing to $12$ is $\{4,8\}$.

So the $4$ and $8$ occupy the two even positions ($2$ ways) and $2,3,7$ the odd positions ($3!=6$ ways): $12$ of the $5!=120$ arrangements. The probability is $\dfrac{12}{120}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{10}}$.
