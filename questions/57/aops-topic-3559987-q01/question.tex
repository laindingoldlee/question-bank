A $7$-column, $6$-row gravity board is filled by alternating red and yellow pieces, with either color allowed to start. Play does not stop on four in a row. There are $21$ pieces of each color. Explain why $\binom{42}{21}$ overcounts reachable full boards by constructing an unreachable balanced board, and give a consequent upper bound.
