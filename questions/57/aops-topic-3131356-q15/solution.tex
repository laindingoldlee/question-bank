Condition on the five selected labels and rank them 1 through 5. Every two-element subset of these ranks is equally likely to belong to Patrick. Patrick must receive rank 5. His other rank may be 4, leaving Kevin 1, 2, 3, or 3, leaving Kevin 1, 2, 4. Ranks 1 or 2 fail the second comparison. Thus exactly 2 of $\binom52=10 $ assignments work, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{5}}$.
