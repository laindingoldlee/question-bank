Adding $ c+d<e+a $ and $ e+a<b+c $ gives $ d<b $. Adding $ a+b<c+d $ and $ c+d<e+a $ gives $ b<e $. Adding $ e+a<b+c $ and $ b+c<d+e $ gives $ a<d $. Thus $ a<d<b<e $. The first inequality also gives $ c>a+b-d>a $, while the last gives $ c<e+d-b<e $. Therefore $\boxed{a\text{ is smallest and }e\text{ is largest}}$.
