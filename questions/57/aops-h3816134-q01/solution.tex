Factor $2016=32\cdot9\cdot7$. Put $E=10^{100}$. Since $7^4\equiv1\pmod{32}$ and $4\mid E$, the remainder modulo $32$ is $1$. Since $7^3\equiv1\pmod9$ and $E\equiv1\pmod3$, the remainder modulo $9$ is $7$. Modulo $7$ it is $0$. The latter two conditions combine as $x\equiv7\pmod{63}$. Write $x=7+63k$; modulo $32$ this gives $7-k\equiv1$, so $k\equiv6$. The least nonnegative solution is $\boxed{x=385}$.
