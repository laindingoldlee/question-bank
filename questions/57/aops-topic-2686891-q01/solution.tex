$\dfrac1n$ terminates exactly when $n$ has no prime factors other than $2$ and $5$, i.e. $n=2^i5^j$. Those up to $100$ are
\begin{itemize}
\item $j=0$: $1,2,4,8,16,32,64$ ($7$ numbers);
\item $j=1$: $5,10,20,40,80$ ($5$ numbers);
\item $j=2$: $25,50,100$ ($3$ numbers);
\end{itemize}
and $125>100$. The total is $\boxed{15}$ (counting $\dfrac11=1$).
