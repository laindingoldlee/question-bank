For $x=2$: $\dfrac12+\dfrac13+\dfrac14=\dfrac{6+4+3}{12}=\dfrac{13}{12}$, so $x=2$ is a solution. The left side is strictly decreasing for $x>0$ (each term decreases as $x$ grows), so it takes the value $\dfrac{13}{12}$ at most once. (Indeed, at $x=1$ it is $\dfrac{11}{6}>\dfrac{13}{12}$, and for $x\ge3$ it is at most $\dfrac13+\dfrac14+\dfrac15=\dfrac{47}{60}<\dfrac{13}{12}$.) So exactly $\boxed{1}$ positive integer works, namely $x=2$.
