Let $\phi(n)$ be Euler's totient function. Find the fourth-smallest four-digit integer $n=\overline{abcd}$ such that $\phi(n)=\overline{ab}\cdot\overline{cd}$, interpreting a suffix such as $04$ as $4$. A finite exhaustive search with a reproducible algorithm is permitted.
