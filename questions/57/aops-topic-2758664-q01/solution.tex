A number with exactly $4$ divisors is $p^3$ or $pq$ for distinct primes $p,q$. Even ones are $2^3=8$ and $2q$ with $q$ an odd prime. $2q<99$ means $q\le47$, and there are $14$ odd primes up to $47$ ($3,5,7,\dots,43,47$). The total is $1+14=\boxed{15}$.
