A $2016$-powerful integer has digit sum $2016$, which is divisible by $3$. By the divisibility rule for $3$, the integer itself is divisible by $3$. It is also far larger than $3$ (a digit sum of $2016$ needs at least $224$ digits), so it is not prime. Hence $p=0$, and the remainder is $\boxed{0}$.

(The condition on the ones, hundreds and thousands digits is a red herring; such integers do exist, for example the number formed by $222$ nines followed by the digits $1908$, whose thousands, hundreds and ones digits give $1\cdot9\cdot8=72$ and whose digit sum is $1998+18=2016$; but none of them is prime.)
