There are $10$ people, and each of them has exactly $3$ books, so there are $30$ books in total. Each book has a title, and different books may have the same title. For any two of the $10$ people, one of them has a book with the same title as a book of the other. Let $x$ be the number of books carrying the most common title. Find, with proof, the smallest possible value of $x$.
