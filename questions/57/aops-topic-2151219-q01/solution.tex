Complete the square using $x^4-2x^2y^2+y^4=\left(x^2-y^2\right)^2$:
\[x^4+y^4-3x^2y^2=\left(x^2-y^2\right)^2-x^2y^2=\boxed{\left(x^2-xy-y^2\right)\left(x^2+xy-y^2\right)}.\]
Neither factor splits further over the integers: as quadratics in $x$ they have discriminant $5y^2$, which is not a perfect square for $y\ne0$.
