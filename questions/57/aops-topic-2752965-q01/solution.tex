No. Every positive integer is congruent to its digit sum modulo $9$, so $d_x(N)\equiv N\pmod9$ for all $x$. Since $2022=3\cdot674$, the number $2022^{2022}$ is divisible by $3^{2022}$, hence by $9$. So every iterate $d_x\!\left(2022^{2022}\right)$ is a positive multiple of $9$ and can never equal $1$. (The iterates decrease until they reach the digital root $9$, and then stay at $9$.)
