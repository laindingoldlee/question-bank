There are $\binom{12}6=924$ total paths. Those through $(3,3)$ number $\binom63^2=400$. Those through $(2,1)$ number $\binom32\binom94=378$. Those through both, necessarily visiting $(2,1)$ first, number $\binom32\binom31\binom63=180$. Inclusion-exclusion gives $924-400-378+180=\boxed{326}$.
