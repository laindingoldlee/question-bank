The improper divisors of a positive integer $m>1$ are $1$ and $m$; its other positive divisors are called proper. For a natural number $n\ge2$, let $S_i$ be the sum of the improper divisors and $S_p$ the sum of the proper divisors of $2^n$. Prove that $3\mid S_i$ if and only if $3\mid S_p$.
