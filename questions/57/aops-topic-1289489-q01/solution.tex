The primes up to $25$ are $2,3,5,7,11,13,17,19,23$: nine primes. Since $\gcd(m,n)=1$, each prime power $p^{e}$ exactly dividing $25!$ goes entirely into $m$ or entirely into $n$, which gives $2^9=512$ ordered pairs $(m,n)$ with $mn=25!$. Since $25!$ is not a perfect square, $m\ne n$ in every pair, and the pairs come in pairs $(m,n),(n,m)$. Exactly half have $m<n$, so the answer is $\dfrac{512}{2}=\boxed{256}$.
