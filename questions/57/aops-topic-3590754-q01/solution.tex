Let $p$ be the smallest prime divisor of $n$. It is the smallest nontrivial divisor, and the largest proper divisor is $\dfrac{n}{p}$. Thus $\dfrac{n}{p}=7p$, or $n=7p^2$. For $p$ to remain the smallest prime factor, $p\le7$. Hence $p=2,3,5,7$, giving $\boxed{n=28,63,175,343}$. Each has nontrivial divisor endpoints $p,7p$ and meets the condition.
