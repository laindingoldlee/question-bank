Group $k^2-(k+1)^2=-(2k+1)$ for $k=6,8,\dots,2014$, and add $2016^2$:
\[2016^2-\sum_{j=3}^{1007}(4j+1)=2016^2-\left(4\cdot\dfrac{(3+1007)\cdot1005}{2}+1005\right)=4064256-2031105=2033151.\]
The remainder is $\boxed{51}$.
