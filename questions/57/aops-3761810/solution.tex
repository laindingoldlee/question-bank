Rationalising gives $f(x)=\dfrac{(x-45)(x+45)(\sqrt{5x}+15)}{5(x-45)}=\dfrac{(x+45)(\sqrt{5x}+15)}{5}$ for $x\ne45$. At $x=44.99999$, both positive factors are less than $90$ and $30$, so $f(x)<540$. They exceed $89.99$ and $29.99$, giving $f (x) > 89.99 \cdot \dfrac{29.99}{5} > 539$. Therefore $\boxed{\lfloor f(x)\rfloor=539}$.
