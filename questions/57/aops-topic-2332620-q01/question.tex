Claire starts walking to the polling station at a random time between 6:00 pm and 7:00 pm, all times being equally likely. Her walk takes between $15$ and $30$ minutes, all durations in this range being equally likely, independently of the starting time. The polls close at 7:00 pm, and her vote counts if she arrives before 7:00 pm. What is the probability that her vote counts?
