Let her start $T$ minutes after 6:00 pm and walk for $W$ minutes, so $(T,W)$ is uniform on the rectangle $0\le T\le60$, $15\le W\le30$. Her vote counts when $T+W<60$. For a fixed $W$ this has probability $\dfrac{60-W}{60}$, which is linear in $W$, so averaging over $W$ (whose mean is $22.5$) gives
\[\dfrac{60-22.5}{60}=\dfrac{37.5}{60}=\boxed{\dfrac58}.\]
(Equivalently, the good part of the $60\times15$ rectangle is a trapezoid with parallel sides $45$ and $30$ and width $15$, of area $562.5$, and $\dfrac{562.5}{900}=\dfrac58$.)
