\textbf{Combine the last two conditions.} $n\equiv10\pmod{11}$ and $n\equiv10\pmod{13}$ mean that $n-10$ is divisible by $11$ and by $13$, hence by $143$: $n=10+143k$.

\textbf{The condition mod $7$.} Since $143=140+3\equiv3$ and $10\equiv3\pmod7$, we need $3+3k\equiv5$, i.e.\ $3k\equiv2\pmod7$. Multiplying by $5$ (as $3\cdot5=15\equiv1$) gives $k\equiv10\equiv3\pmod7$. So $k=3+7j$ and
\[
n=10+143(3+7j)=439+1001j .
\]

\textbf{Largest below $2010$.} The values are $439$, $1440$, $2441$, \dots, so the answer is $\boxed{1440}$.

Check: $1440=7\cdot205+5=11\cdot130+10=13\cdot110+10$.
