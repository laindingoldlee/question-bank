Three circles with radii $r_1$, $r_2$ and $r_3$ are each externally tangent to the other two. A fourth circle lies in the gap enclosed by the three circles and is externally tangent to all three. Find its radius $r$ in terms of $r_1$, $r_2$ and $r_3$.
