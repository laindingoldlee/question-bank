Let $S$ be the displacement of one such walk. A frog starting at $i$ reaches 0 with probability $\Pr(S=-i)$. Linearity gives expectation $\sum_{i=-1011}^{1011}\Pr(S=-i)=1$, because these are all possible displacements. Thus $\boxed1$.
