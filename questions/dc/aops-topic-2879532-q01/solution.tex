A number with exactly $8$ divisors has the form $p^7$, $p^3q$ or $pqr$ (distinct primes $p,q,r$). Among four-digit numbers: $p^7$ gives only $3^7=2187$; $p^3q$ gives $268$; $pqr$ gives $1665$. The total is $1+268+1665=\boxed{1934}$.
