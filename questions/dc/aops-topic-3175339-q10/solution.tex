The entries $1,2$ must be adjacent, because nothing is smaller than $1$. Delete $1$ and relabel $2,\ldots,10$ as $1,\ldots,9$. The same property holds in the resulting permutation. Conversely, insert the deleted $1$ immediately before or after the original $2$: either choice preserves every higher consecutive-pair condition, since the inserted entry is smaller than all of them. Thus the count doubles with each increase in size. Starting with one permutation of size one gives $2^9=\boxed{512}$.
