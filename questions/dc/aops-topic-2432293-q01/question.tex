Bob wants to join the points $A=(0,6)$ and $B=(10,5)$ with copper wire, and also to join each of these two points by wire to one common point $P$ on the $x$-axis, which he may choose. Lengths are in feet, and two feet of copper wire cost $10$ dollars. What is the least amount of money Bob needs to spend? Round your answer to the nearest dollar.
