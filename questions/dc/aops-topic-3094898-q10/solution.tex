Schedule (i) takes $\dfrac{2}{\dfrac{2}{7}}=7$ hours. After 6 hours of schedule (ii), Alice has finished and Bob has completed $\dfrac{6}{7}$ of his wall. Their collaborative rate finishes the remaining $\dfrac{1}{7}$ in $\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{7}}{\dfrac{2}{7}}=\dfrac{1}{2}$ hour. Thus schedule (ii) takes $\dfrac{13}{2}$ hours and saves $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}\text{ hour}}$.
