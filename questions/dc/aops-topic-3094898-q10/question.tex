Alice alone builds a wall in 6 hours, and Bob alone in 7 hours. When collaborating, their total rate is only $\dfrac{2}{7}$ wall per hour. Compare two schedules for two identical walls: (i) collaborate on both walls; (ii) work separately until Alice finishes, then collaborate on Bob’s unfinished wall. How much time does schedule (ii) save?
