There are $25$ usable letters. Old plates: $25^2\cdot10^4$; new plates: $25^3\cdot10^3$. The ratio is $\dfrac{25^2\cdot10^4}{25^3\cdot10^3}=\dfrac{10}{25}=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{5}}$.
