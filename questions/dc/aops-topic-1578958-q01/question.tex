\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Every point of the plane is coloured red or blue. Prove that there are two points of the same colour at distance exactly $1$ km.
\item[(b)] Now the plane is divided into $1\text{ m}\times1\text{ m}$ squares by a square grid, and each square is coloured red or blue. The distance between two squares is the distance between their centres. Prove or disprove: there must be two squares of the same colour at distance exactly $1$ km.
\end{enumerate}
