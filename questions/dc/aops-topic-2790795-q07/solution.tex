Repeatedly halving any nonminimum count must reach a smaller count in the set, eventually 5. Thus every count is 5 times a power of 2, and no exponent may be skipped below an included exponent. The five counts are 5, 10, 20, 40, 80, so the largest is $\boxed{80}$.
