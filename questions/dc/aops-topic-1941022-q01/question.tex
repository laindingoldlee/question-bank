A parallelogram has diagonals of lengths $d_1$ and $d_2$ that meet at an angle $\theta$. Prove that its area is $\dfrac{1}{2}d_1d_2\sin\theta$. Then find the area of a parallelogram whose diagonals have lengths $10$ and $8$ and meet at an angle of $30^\circ$.
