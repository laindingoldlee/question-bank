Consider the numbers
\[\textbf{(A) }10^{2000}\qquad\textbf{(B) }10^{4000}\qquad\textbf{(C) }10^{5802}\qquad\textbf{(D) }10^{8000}\qquad\textbf{(E) }10^{10000}.\]
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Which of them is closest to $2020!$ on a logarithmic scale (that is, has the smallest ratio $\dfrac{\text{larger}}{\text{smaller}}$ with $2020!$)?
\item[(b)] Which of them is closest to $2020!$ in the usual sense (smallest absolute difference)?
\end{enumerate}
You may use Stirling's formula $n!=\sqrt{2\pi n}\left(\dfrac ne\right)^ne^{\theta}$ with $0<\theta<\dfrac1{12n}$.
