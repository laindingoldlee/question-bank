After a $20\%$ discount the price is $80\%$ of the original price $P$:
\[0.8P=192,\qquad P=\dfrac{192}{0.8}=\boxed{\$240}.\]
(Check: $20\%$ of $\$240$ is $\$48$, and $240-48=192$.)
