We need $400\mid n(n+1)$. Since consecutive integers are coprime, the factors 16 and 25 must each divide one of $n,n+1$. The four combinations give residues $n\equiv0,175,224,399\pmod{400}$. For example $n\equiv-1\pmod{16}$ and $n\equiv0\pmod{25}$ give 175. The least positive among these is $\boxed{175}$.
