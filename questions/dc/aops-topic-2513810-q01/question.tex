In a game, Players $1$ through $5$ take turns rolling a standard six-sided die, beginning with Player $1$. If a six is rolled, the player who rolled it wins. If any other number is rolled, the die is given to the player whose number was rolled (even if that is the player who just rolled), and that player rolls next. What is the probability that Player $1$ wins? Explain why it is enough to use one unknown for ``another player holds the die'', whoever that player is.
