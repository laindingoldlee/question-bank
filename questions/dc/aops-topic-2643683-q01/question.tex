Let $ABCD$ be a rhombus whose diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ meet at $X$. A line meets side $BC$ at $E$ and side $AD$ at $F$. Line $EF$ meets line $AB$ at $G$ (on the extension beyond $A$), line $CD$ at $H$ (on the extension beyond $C$), diagonal $AC$ at $Y$ (between $X$ and $A$), and diagonal $BD$ at $Z$ (between $X$ and $B$). Given that $GA=8$, $HC=16$, $XY=4$ and $XZ=3$, find the side length of $ABCD$.
