Let $p_0$ be the success probability when the preceding attempt was not a Steve mistake, and $p_1$ when it was. Conditioning on the next attempt gives $p_0=\dfrac{2}{9}p_1+\dfrac{6}{9}p_0$ and $p_1=\dfrac{2}{9}+\dfrac{6}{9}p_0$. The first equation yields $p_0=\dfrac{2}{3}p_1$; substituting into the second gives $p_1=\dfrac{2}{5}$, and hence $\boxed{p_0=\dfrac{4}{15}}$. A perfect performance terminates with no later opportunity, explaining its zero contribution.
