A distribution is a solution of $x_1+\dots+x_{15}=20$ in nonnegative integers, where $x_i$ is the number of balls in basket $i$. By stars and bars (arrange $20$ stars and $14$ bars in a row), the number is
\[
\dbinom{34}{14}=\boxed{1391975640}.
\]
