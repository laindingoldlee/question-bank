There are $99-32+1=68$ four-digit squares. Write $N=k^2$, $32\le k\le99$. Since $68=4\cdot17$ divides $k^2$, $34$ divides $k$. Thus $k=34$ or 68, giving 1156 and 4624. Their digit sums are 13 and 16 respectively. Only 13 is prime, so $\boxed{1156}$.
