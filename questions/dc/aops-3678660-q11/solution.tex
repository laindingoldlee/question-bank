Bob and Fred have the same truth value, the opposite of Alice's. If Alice is lying, Bob and Fred are the two mathematicians. Alice ate the cookie; Charlie's claim is false, Dave's conjunction is false, and Emily's claim is false because the culprit Alice is a machine. This works. If Alice is truthful, Bob and Fred are machines, and Dave has the same truth value as Emily. If both Dave and Emily were truthful there would already be three mathematicians. Thus both are machines, forcing Charlie to be the other mathematician. But then Charlie ate the cookie, making Emily truthful, a contradiction. Hence $\boxed{\text{Bob and Fred are mathematicians; Alice ate the cookie}}$, with all others machines.
