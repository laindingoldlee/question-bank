Multiply numerator and denominator by the conjugate $\sqrt2-1$; the denominator becomes $(\sqrt2+1)(\sqrt2-1)=2-1=1$:
\[
\dfrac{7-\sqrt2}{1+\sqrt2}=(7-\sqrt2)(\sqrt2-1)=7\sqrt2-7-2+\sqrt2=\boxed{8\sqrt2-9}.
\]
Check: $(8\sqrt2-9)(1+\sqrt2)=8\sqrt2+16-9-9\sqrt2=7-\sqrt2$.
