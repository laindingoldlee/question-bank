Geoff the frog starts at the origin of the coordinate plane. Each move is either one unit to the right ($R$) or one unit up ($U$). Every up move must be immediately followed by a right move, except that the last move of the sequence may be an up move. How many different sequences of moves take Geoff from the origin to the point $(9,5)$?
