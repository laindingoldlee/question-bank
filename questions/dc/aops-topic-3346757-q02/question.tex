On an $8\times8$ chessboard, start at h 1 and each minute choose uniformly among horizontally or vertically adjacent squares. Let $A$ be the probability of being at a 1 after $60$ moves and $B$ the probability of being at a 8. Find $\dfrac{A}{B}$.
