Direct each match edge from winner to loser. Starting at a player, repeatedly choose someone who defeated the current player. Eventually a vertex repeats, producing a directed cycle. Choose a shortest cycle $v_1\to v_2\to\cdots\to v_k\to v_1$. It has length at least 3. If $k>3$, the match between $v_1$ and $v_3$ gives either $v_3\to v_1$, producing a 3-cycle, or $v_1\to v_3$, allowing $v_2$ to be omitted. Either gives a shorter cycle, a contradiction. Thus a 3-cycle exists.
