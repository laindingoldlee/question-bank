A monument in the shape of a right triangle sits on a rectangular pedestal that is $5$ metres high and $11$ metres long. The longest side of the triangular monument measures $61$ metres, and its base, which lies along the top of the pedestal, has the same length as the pedestal. How high above the ground is the top of the monument?
