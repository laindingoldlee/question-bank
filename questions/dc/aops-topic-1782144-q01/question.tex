Gina lays square tiles on a floor. She begins with $1$ tile in a corner of the room. She adds $3$ tiles to form a $2\times2$ square of tiles, then $5$ tiles to form a $3\times3$ square, and so on, until the whole floor is covered. The last time, she adds $25$ tiles. What is the size of the floor, in tiles?
