Let the shelves hold $s_1,\dots,s_5$. Then
\[93=(s_1+s_2)+(s_3+s_4)+s_5=37+33+s_5,\]
so $s_5=23$ and $s_4=41-23=\boxed{18}$. (The others are $s_3=15$, $s_2=20$, $s_1=17$, and $17+20+15+18+23=93$.)
