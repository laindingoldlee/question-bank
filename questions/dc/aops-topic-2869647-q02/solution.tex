Each of the $13$ guests gives on average $1\cdot\dfrac34+2\cdot\dfrac3{16}+3\cdot\dfrac1{16}=\dfrac{12+6+3}{16}=\dfrac{21}{16}$ presents. By linearity of expectation the total is $13\cdot\dfrac{21}{16}=\boxed{\dfrac{273}{16}}$.
