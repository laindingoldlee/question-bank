Maya has $14$ guests at her party. Her brother gives no present. Each of the other $13$ guests independently gives $1$ present with probability $\dfrac34$, $2$ presents with probability $\dfrac3{16}$ and $3$ presents with probability $\dfrac1{16}$. What is the expected number of presents Maya receives?
