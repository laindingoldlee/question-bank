$16\%=\dfrac{16}{100}$, so
\[\sqrt{16\%}=\sqrt{\dfrac{16}{100}}=\dfrac4{10}=\boxed{0.4=40\%}.\]
(It is not $4\%$: the square root applies to the number $0.16$, not just to the $16$.)
