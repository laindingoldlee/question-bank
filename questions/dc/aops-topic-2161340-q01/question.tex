Xavier and Yoni each have a whole number of marbles. Xavier's number of marbles is a multiple of $12$ that is less than $150$. Yoni's number of marbles is a factor of $159$ that is greater than $10$. Together they have $149$ marbles. One of their $149$ marbles is chosen at random. What is the probability that it is Xavier's?
