Since $159=3\cdot53$, its factors are $1,3,53,159$, and those greater than $10$ are $53$ and $159$. Yoni cannot have $159$ marbles, since together they have only $149$, so Yoni has $53$. Then Xavier has $149-53=96=12\cdot8$, which is indeed a multiple of $12$ less than $150$.

The probability that a randomly chosen marble is Xavier's is $\boxed{\dfrac{96}{149}}$.
