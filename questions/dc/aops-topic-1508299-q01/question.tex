Prove that
\[
\dfrac{4}{1}-\dfrac{4}{3}+\dfrac{4}{5}-\dfrac{4}{7}+\cdots=\pi,
\]
that is, that the partial sums $S_n=\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\dfrac{4(-1)^k}{2k+1}$ tend to $\pi$ as $n\to\infty$. (You may use calculus.)
