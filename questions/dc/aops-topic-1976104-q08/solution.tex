The volume is
\[
\dfrac{4}{3}\pi r^3=\dfrac{4}{3}\pi\cdot\dfrac{15625}{8}=\boxed{\dfrac{15625\pi}{6}}\text{ ft}^3\approx8181\text{ ft}^3 .
\]
