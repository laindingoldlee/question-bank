Let the units carry be $u$ and the tens carry be $v$. The tens equation is $C+2+u=8+10v$. Its left side is at most 12, so $v=0$ and $C=6-u$. The hundreds equation gives $A=C+B$. If $u=0$, the units equation yields $6+2B=8$, giving $B=1,C=6,A=7$. If $u=1$, it yields $5+2B=18$, impossible for integer $B$. Thus $667+121=788$ is the solution, and $ABC=7\cdot1\cdot6=\boxed{42}$.
