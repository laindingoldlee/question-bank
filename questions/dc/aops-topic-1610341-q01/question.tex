Ron and Martin play a game with a bowl containing $39$ marbles. The players take turns removing $1$, $2$, $3$ or $4$ marbles from the bowl. The player who removes the last marble loses. Ron moves first. How many marbles should he remove on his first move to guarantee that he wins? Give a rigorous justification.
