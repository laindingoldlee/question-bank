The word has $11$ letters; the two $O$'s are alike and the other $9$ letters are all different. Every choice of the two positions for the $O$'s can be completed by the other letters in the same number of ways ($9!$), so the pair of positions of the $O$'s is equally likely to be any of the $\dbinom{11}{2}=55$ pairs. Exactly $10$ of these pairs are adjacent positions. So the probability that the $O$'s are not adjacent is
\[1-\dfrac{10}{55}=\boxed{\dfrac{9}{11}}.\]
