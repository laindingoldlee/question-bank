Classify by the number $k$ of on bulbs. For $k=1,2$ the off bulbs cannot fit into the $k$ cyclic gaps with at most two per gap. For $k=3$, the three off-gap lengths sum to $4$ with each at most $2$; there are $\binom62-3\binom32=6$ ordered gap triples. Choosing a marked on bulb gives $7\cdot6$, and forgetting the mark divides by $3$, giving $14$. For $k=4$, the gap lengths sum to $3$; $\binom63-4=16$ choices give $\dfrac{7\cdot16}{4}=28$ configurations. For $k=5,6,7$, every configuration works, giving $21,7,1$. The answer is $\boxed{71}$.
