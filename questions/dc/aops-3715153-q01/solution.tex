Similarity gives $\dfrac{AE}{AC}=\dfrac{AD}{AB}=\dfrac{4}{10}$. Thus $AC=\dfrac{25}{2}$ and $EC=AC-AE=\boxed{\dfrac{15}{2}\text{ cm}}$.
