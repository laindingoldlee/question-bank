With $N$ bricks, their rates are $\dfrac {N}{9}$ and $\dfrac {N}{10}$, so together they lay $\dfrac {N}{9}+\dfrac {N}{10}-10$ bricks per hour, and $5\left(\dfrac{19N}{90}-10\right)=N$. This gives $\dfrac{19N}{18}-50=N$, so $\dfrac {N}{18}=50$ and $N=\boxed{900}$.
