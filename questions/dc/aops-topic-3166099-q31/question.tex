In triangle $ABC$, take $D$ beyond $B$ on ray $CB$ with $BD=AB$, and $E$ beyond $C$ on ray $AC$ with $CE=BC$. Let $L\ne A$ be the second intersection of line $AD$ with the circumcircle. The exterior angle bisector at $C$ meets line $AD$ at $K$, with $AK=AB+BC+CA$. Given $B,E,L$ are collinear, find $\angle CAB$.
