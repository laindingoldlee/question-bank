A grandfather and his grandson have the same birthday. At this year's celebration the grandfather notices that his age is a multiple of his grandson's age, and that this will also be true on each of the next five birthdays. The grandfather is younger than $120$ and the grandson is at least $1$ year old. How old are they now?
