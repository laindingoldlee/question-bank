Let $e_1$ and $e_3$ be the numbers ordering exactly one and exactly three items. Then $e_1+e_3=10$, and counting orders, $e_1+3e_3=7+5+4=16$. Subtracting, $2e_3=6$, so $\boxed{3}$ people order all three. (Inclusion--exclusion gives the same: $10=16-3e_3+e_3$.)
