A code works as follows. The first time a letter appears in a message, it is replaced by the letter $1$ place to its right in the alphabet (with $A$ to the right of $Z$). The second time the same letter appears, it is replaced by the letter $1+2$ places to the right, the third time $1+2+3$ places, and so on. For example, ``banana'' becomes ``cbodqg''. What letter replaces the last $s$ in the message ``Lee's sis is a Mississippi miss, Chriss!''?
