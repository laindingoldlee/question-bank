If there are $W$ workers, $T$ years, and $N$ tunnels, then $WT=100000N$. Thus (a) $T=40$ years; (b) $N=\dfrac{1}{5}$ of a tunnel; (c) $T=40$ years; (d) $W=400000$. Fractional tunnel completion is permitted in the work model.
