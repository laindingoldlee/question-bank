By the remainder theorem, the remainder when $p(x)$ is divided by $x-3$ is $p(3)=-2$; so (D) must be true.

The others need not be true: for example $p(x)=-2$ (a constant polynomial) has $p(3)=-2$, but $p(5)$, $p(2)$ and $p(-2)$ are all $-2\ne0$, so none of $x-5$, $x-2$, $x+2$ is a factor. The answer is $\boxed{\text{(D)}}$.
