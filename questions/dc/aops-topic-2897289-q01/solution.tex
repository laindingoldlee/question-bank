At the first meeting the ferries have jointly covered one width $W$, and the ferry from shore $P$ has gone $720$ yards. At the second meeting they have jointly covered $3W$ (the equal waits do not matter). So the ferry from $P$ has gone $3\cdot720=2160$ yards, which is $W+400$. Hence $W=\boxed{1760}$ yards.
