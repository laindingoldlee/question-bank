The progression equation is $2(x^2-1)=-x+x^3$, or $(x-2)(x-1)(x+1)=0$. The roots are 2,1,-1. The common difference is $x^2+x-1$, negative only at-1 among these. Thus the permitted cubes are 8 and 1, summing to $\boxed9$.
