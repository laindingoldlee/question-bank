Take two of the spheres, with radii $r_1,r_2$, centres $O_1,O_2$ and points of contact $T_1,T_2$ with the plane. The radii $O_1T_1$ and $O_2T_2$ are perpendicular to the plane, so $O_1T_1T_2O_2$ is a right trapezoid with $O_1O_2=r_1+r_2$ (the spheres are externally tangent) and parallel sides $r_1,r_2$. With $d=T_1T_2$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s=0.2){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
real r1=15/4, r2=12/5, d=6;
pair T1=(0,0), T2=(d,0), C1=(0,r1), C2=(d,r2);
draw((-4.5,0)--(11,0),linewidth(0.9));
draw(circle(C1,r1),blue); draw(circle(C2,r2),blue);
draw(C1--C2,red); draw(C1--T1,dashed); draw(C2--T2,dashed);
pair K=(d,r1); draw(C2--K,gray+dashed); draw(C1--K,gray+dashed);
draw(ra(T1,(1,0),(0,1),0.35)); draw(ra(T2,(-1,0),(0,1),0.35));
dot(C1);dot(C2);dot(T1);dot(T2);
label("$T_1$",T1,S); label("$T_2$",T2,S); label("$O_1$",C1,W); label("$O_2$",C2,E);
label("$r_1$",(0,r1/2),W); label("$r_2$",(d,r2/2),E);
label("$r_1+r_2$",(C1+C2)/2,SW,red,Fill(white)); label("$r_1-r_2$",(d,3.3),E,gray+fontsize(7pt));
label("$d$",(d/2,0),S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

\[
d^2=(r_1+r_2)^2-(r_1-r_2)^2=4r_1r_2,\qquad\text{so}\qquad d=2\sqrt{r_1r_2}.
\]
Let the spheres at the ends of the side $10$ have radii $p,q$, at the ends of the side $8$ radii $q,s$, and at the ends of the side $6$ radii $s,p$ (the sphere with radius $q$ sits at the vertex where the sides $10$ and $8$ meet). Then
\[
pq=25,\qquad qs=16,\qquad sp=9 .
\]
Multiplying, $(pqs)^2=3600$, so $pqs=60$, and
\[
s=\dfrac{60}{25}=\dfrac{12}{5},\qquad p=\dfrac{60}{16}=\dfrac{15}{4},\qquad q=\dfrac{60}{9}=\dfrac{20}{3}.
\]
The radii are $\boxed{\dfrac{15}{4},\ \dfrac{12}{5},\ \dfrac{20}{3}}$: the sphere at the vertex opposite the side $10$ has radius $\dfrac{12}{5}$, the one opposite the side $8$ has $\dfrac{15}{4}$, and the one opposite the side $6$ (between the sides $8$ and $10$) has $\dfrac{20}{3}$. (The figure shows the vertical cross-section through the spheres of radii $\dfrac{15}{4}$ and $\dfrac{12}{5}$, whose points of contact are $6$ apart.)
