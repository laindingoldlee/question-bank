There are two coins on a table that look identical. One is fair, and the other comes up heads with probability $\dfrac35$. You pick one of them at random and flip it twice, getting tails and then heads. You may then either keep this coin or switch to the other coin, and you flip the coin you now hold once more. Finally you must say which coin is the unfair one. If you choose whether to switch, and your final guess, so as to maximise your chance of being right, what is the probability that you identify the unfair coin correctly? Express the answer as a common fraction.
