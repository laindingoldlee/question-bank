Let $M$ be the midpoint of $BC$. The locus circle lies in the perpendicular-bisector plane of $BC$, has center $M$, and radius $r=5\sqrt3$. Also $AM=12$, and $A$ lies in that plane. The chord of contact $EF$ is perpendicular to $AM$, with its midpoint distance $\dfrac{r^2}{AM}=\dfrac{25}{4}$ from $M$. Thus $EF^2=4(75-\dfrac{625}{16})=\dfrac{575}{4}$, and $\boxed{m+n=579}$.

\end{asy}
