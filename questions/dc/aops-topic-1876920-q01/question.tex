Define $x\#y=x+2y$ and $x\,@\,y=x-y$. Solve the system
\[
(x\#y)\,@\,z=4,\qquad\left[x+\left(x\#\dfrac {y}{2}\right)\right]@\,(-z)=-2,\qquad(x\#y)\,@\,(-z)=2
\]
for $x$, $y$ and $z$.
