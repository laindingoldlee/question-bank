There are $11\cdot11=121$ equally likely ordered pairs $(a,b)$. Rewrite the condition:
\[a+b<ab\iff ab-a-b+1>1\iff (a-1)(b-1)>1 .\]
Put $u=a-1$ and $v=b-1$, so $u,v\in\{-6,\ldots,4\}$ and we need $uv>1$, i.e.\ $uv\ge2$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $u,v>0$: $u,v\in\{1,2,3,4\}$, $16$ pairs, minus $(1,1)$: $15$.
\item $u,v<0$: $u,v\in\{-6,\ldots,-1\}$, $36$ pairs, minus $(-1,-1)$: $35$.
\end{itemize}
(If $u$ or $v$ is $0$, or they have opposite signs, then $uv\le0$.) That is $50$ pairs, so the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{50}{121}}$.
