The double-headed coin contributes likelihood $\dfrac{1}{3}$, while the fair coins together contribute $\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot\dfrac{1}{64}$. Bayes' theorem gives $\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{3}}{\dfrac{1}{3}+\dfrac{2}{3\cdot64}}=\dfrac{32}{33}$. Thus $\boxed{65}$.
