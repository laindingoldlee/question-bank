$m$ minutes after 1:00 the minute hand is at $6m$ degrees (from 12) and the hour hand at $30+\dfrac {m}{2}$ degrees. They are opposite when $6m-\left(30+\dfrac {m}{2}\right)=180$, i.e. $\dfrac{11m}{2}=210$, $m=\dfrac{420}{11}=38\dfrac{2}{11}$. The time is $\boxed{1{:}38\dfrac{2}{11}}$ (about 1:38:11).
