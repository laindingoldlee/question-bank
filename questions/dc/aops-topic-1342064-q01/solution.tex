Work in radians, so $a+b+c=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ with $a,b,c\in\left[0,\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right]$. On this interval $\sin$ is concave, so its graph lies below its tangent line at $x=\dfrac{\pi}{6}$:
\[
\sin x\le\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\left(x-\dfrac{\pi}{6}\right)\qquad\left(0\le x\le\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right).
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
real f(real x){return sin(x);}
real g(real x){return 0.5+sqrt(3)/2*(x-pi/6);}
draw(graph(f,0,pi/2),blue+linewidth(0.9));
draw(graph(g,-0.05,1.2),red+dashed);
draw((-0.1,0)--(1.8,0),Arrow(4)); draw((0,-0.45)--(0,1.3),Arrow(4));
dot((pi/6,0.5));
draw((pi/6,0)--(pi/6,0.5),gray+dotted);
label("$30^\circ$",(pi/6,0),S); label("$90^\circ$",(pi/2,0),S);
label("$y=\sin x$",(1.38,0.87),S,blue); label("tangent at $30^\circ$",(0.75,g(0.75)),NW,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Adding this for $x=a,b,c$:
\[
\sin a+\sin b+\sin c\le\dfrac{3}{2}+\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\left(a+b+c-\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)=\dfrac{3}{2} .
\]
Equality holds for $a=b=c=30^\circ$, where $\sin a+\sin b+\sin c=3\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}$. So the maximum is $\boxed{\dfrac{3}{2}}$.
