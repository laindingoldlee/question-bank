The route graph is connected and has no cycle: a cycle would give two different paths between its vertices. Thus it is a tree. Choose a root and color each city by the parity of its distance in edges from the root. Every edge joins opposite parities; otherwise the root paths and that edge would create a cycle. Therefore each color class contains no adjacent pair. The larger class has at least $\lceil\dfrac{n}{2}\rceil$ cities and is the required selection.
