The total number remains $600$. Also every difference between two type-counts is invariant modulo $3$: a collision changes counts by a permutation of $(-1,-1,2)$, whose coordinates are equal modulo $3$.

Initially the difference between the counts of $x$ and $y$ is $-100\equiv2\pmod3$. In any one-type state the three counts are a permutation of $(600,0,0)$, so every difference is $0\pmod3$. This contradicts the invariant. Therefore $\boxed{\text{No}}$.
