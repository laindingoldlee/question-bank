Rearrange: $mn-8m-9n+72=66$, i.e. $(m-9)(n-8)=66$. For the largest $m$ take $m-9=66$ and $n-8=1$, giving $m=\boxed{75}$ (with $n=9$: $600+81=681=675+6$). Negative factor pairs give $m<9$.
