Kelly wants a circular table such that $12$ chairs, each $16$ inches wide, can be placed around it with at least $8$ inches between neighbouring chairs (all widths measured along the edge of the table).
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What is the smallest possible diameter of the table, to the nearest tenth of an inch?
\item[(b)] Will a table of this size fit in a $12$-foot by $14$-foot dining room?
\end{enumerate}
Use $3.14159$ for $\pi$.
