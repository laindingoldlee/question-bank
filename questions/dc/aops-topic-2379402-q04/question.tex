Harry and Kate are $2500$ metres apart. They start walking towards each other at the same moment, Harry at $40$ metres per minute and Kate at $60$ metres per minute. At the same moment Kate's dog starts running back and forth between them at $120$ metres per minute, turning instantly whenever it reaches one of them. How far has the dog run when Harry and Kate meet?
