Harry and Kate approach each other at $40+60=100$ metres per minute, so they meet after $\dfrac{2500}{100}=25$ minutes. The dog runs at $120$ metres per minute the whole time, so it runs $120\cdot25=\boxed{3000\text{ m}}$. (There is no need to add up the individual trips.)
