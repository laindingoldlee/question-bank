There are four steps, with exactly two independent choices at each step. Each sequence of choices determines one path, even when different paths end at the same T. Thus the count is $2^4=\boxed{16}$. 
\begin{asy}
size(7cm);
string[] L={"C","O","U","N","T"};for(int r=0;r<5;++r){for(int j=0;j<=r;++j){pair P=(2*j-r,-r);label(L[r],P);if(r<4){draw((P+(-.15,-.15))--(P+(-.85,-.85)),gray);draw((P+(.15,-.15))--(P+(.85,-.85)),gray);}}}
\end{asy}
