Work backwards: $5\cdot12=60$; $60+5=65$; dividing by $\dfrac12$ means doubling, so undo it by halving: $\dfrac{65}2$; subtract $7$: $\dfrac{51}2$; divide by $3$: $\boxed{\dfrac{17}{2}}$. (Check: $3\cdot8.5+7=32.5$, doubled $65$, minus $5$ is $60$, divided by $12$ is $5$.)
