Let $m$ and $n$ be natural numbers with $m=n^2$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(1)] Must $m$ be a perfect square?
\item[(2)] Can $m$ be irrational?
\item[(3)] Check your answers for $n=3$.
\end{enumerate}
