The sum is even exactly when both numbers are even or both are odd. These two cases are exclusive, and the spins are independent, so
\[
P(\text{even sum})=\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}+\dfrac{3}{4}\cdot\dfrac{1}{3}=\dfrac{1}{6}+\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{5}{12},
\]
answer $\boxed{\textbf{(D)}}$. (Choice (E), $\dfrac{4}{9}$, does not fit these probabilities.)
