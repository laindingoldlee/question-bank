Two wheels are spun, and the two resulting numbers are added. On the first wheel, the pointer lands on an even number with probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$ and on an odd number with probability $\dfrac{3}{4}$. On the second wheel, it lands on an even number with probability $\dfrac{2}{3}$ and on an odd number with probability $\dfrac{1}{3}$. The two spins are independent. The probability that the sum is even is

$\textbf{(A)}\ \dfrac{1}{6}\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ \dfrac{1}{4}\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ \dfrac{1}{3}\qquad\textbf{(D)}\ \dfrac{5}{12}\qquad\textbf{(E)}\ \dfrac{4}{9}$
