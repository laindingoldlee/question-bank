The integer is $\boxed{67}$, since it is prime and $1+67=68$. To verify uniqueness, any such $n$ satisfies $n<68$. For each prime power $p^e$ dividing $n$, its divisor sum $1+p+\cdots+p^e$ must divide $68$. Among prime powers at most $67$, the only such sums are $4$ from $3$ and $68$ from $67$. The factor $4$ cannot be supplemented by a prime-power sum $17$, since none exists. Thus only $n=67$ works.
