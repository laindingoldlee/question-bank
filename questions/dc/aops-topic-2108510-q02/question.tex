Let $G$ be the centroid of triangle $ABC$, and let $a=BC$, $b=CA$ and $c=AB$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that for every point $P$ in the plane of the triangle,
\[PA^2+PB^2+PC^2=GA^2+GB^2+GC^2+3PG^2 .\]
\item[(b)] Prove that $GA^2+GB^2+GC^2=\dfrac{a^2+b^2+c^2}{3}$.
\item[(c)] Deduce that $PA^2+PB^2+PC^2$ is smallest when $P=G$, and find this smallest value for a triangle with side lengths $5$, $5$ and $6$.
\end{enumerate}
