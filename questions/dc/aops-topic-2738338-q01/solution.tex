Since $37^2=1369<1442<1444=38^2$ and $44^2=1936<2020<2025=45^2$, the integers are $-37,-36,\dots,44$. The terms from $-37$ to $37$ cancel, leaving $38+39+\cdots+44=7\cdot41=\boxed{287}$.
