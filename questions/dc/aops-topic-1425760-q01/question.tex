Johnny walks to school every day at $12$ mph. Every day a train, travelling at a constant speed parallel to Johnny's route and in the same direction, passes him exactly when he reaches school; the train keeps the same timetable every day. One day Johnny leaves home $4$ minutes $10$ seconds late, and that day the train passes him when he is still one mile from school. How fast is the train travelling?
