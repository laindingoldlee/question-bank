A divisor is $2^a3^b5^c7^d$, with $0\le a\le2,0\le b\le3,0\le c\le5,0\le d\le7$, giving $3\cdot4\cdot6\cdot8=576$ divisors. A remainder 1 divisor must have $a=0$; then its remainder is $(-1)^{b+d}$. Exactly half of the $4\cdot8$ pairs $(b,d)$ have even sum, while $c$ has six choices. Thus there are 96 favourable divisors, and the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{6}}$.
