$AB=AD$ gives $\angle ADB=65^\circ$ and $\angle A=50^\circ$. Since $BD$ bisects $\angle ADC$, $\angle BDC=65^\circ$ and $\angle D=130^\circ$. Next, $\angle B=65^\circ+35^\circ=100^\circ$, and in triangle $BCD$, $\angle C=180^\circ-35^\circ-65^\circ=80^\circ$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0),D=(4,0);pair A=extension(B,B+dir(65),D,D+dir(115));pair C=extension(B,B+dir(-35),D,D+dir(180+65));draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(B--D,dashed);
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,W);label("$C$",C,S);label("$D$",D,E);
\end{asy}

Answer: $\boxed{\angle A=50^\circ,\ \angle B=100^\circ,\ \angle C=80^\circ,\ \angle D=130^\circ}$.
