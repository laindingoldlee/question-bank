Fix $z$ and count solutions of $3x+2y=30-5z$ with $x,y\ge1$; $x$ must have the parity of $30-5z$.
\[\begin{array}{c|ccccc}z&1&2&3&4&5\\\hline 3x+2y&25&20&15&10&5\\ x&1,3,5,7&2,4,6&1,3&2&1\\ \#&4&3&2&1&1\end{array}\]
(For example with $z=1$, $x=7$ gives $y=2$, while $x=9$ would give $y<1$.) $z\ge6$ is impossible. The total is $4+3+2+1+1=\boxed{11}$.
