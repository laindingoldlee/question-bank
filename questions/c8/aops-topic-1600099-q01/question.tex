A dog is tied by a leash of length $37$ meters to a corner of a building whose floor plan is a regular octagon with side length $20$ meters; the dog stays outside the building. Later a square fence is built around the octagon: the octagon is in the exact middle of the square, four sides of the octagon are parallel to the sides of the square, and each side of the square is $30$ meters from the nearest side of the octagon. Let $A$ be the area of the region the dog could reach before the fence was built, and $B$ the area it can reach afterwards (inside the fence). Find $B-A$ to the nearest tenth of a square meter.
