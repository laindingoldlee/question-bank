Since the towns have equal numbers of voters, the fraction for all four towns is the average of the four fractions:
\[\dfrac14\left(\dfrac35+\dfrac15+\dfrac45+\dfrac55\right)=\dfrac14\cdot\dfrac{13}{5}=\boxed{\dfrac{13}{20}},\]
that is, $65\%$. (Equivalently, splitting each town's $5$ votes in proportion gives $3+1+4+5=13$ of the $20$ electoral votes.)
