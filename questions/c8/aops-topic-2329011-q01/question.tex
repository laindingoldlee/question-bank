In a made-up version of an election, Pennsylvania's $20$ electoral votes are split equally among four towns, Philadelphia, Pittsburgh, Harrisburg and Scranton, each worth $5$ votes, and the four towns have the same number of voters. Joe Biden wins $\dfrac35$ of the voters of Philadelphia, $\dfrac15$ of Pittsburgh, $\dfrac45$ of Harrisburg and all of the voters of Scranton. What fraction of all the voters in the four towns support Biden?
