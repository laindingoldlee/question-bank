Triangle $ABC$ has $AB=20$, $BC=17$ and $AC=12$, and $\omega$ is its circumcircle. Point $P$ lies on the minor arc $BC$ of $\omega$, and $K$ is the foot of the perpendicular from $P$ to $BC$, with $BK=PK$. Find the ratio of the area of triangle $BPK$ to the area of triangle $ABC$, as a decimal to the nearest thousandth.
