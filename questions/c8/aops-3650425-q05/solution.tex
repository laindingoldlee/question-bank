Let half the base be $u$ and altitude $h$. Then $uh=120$ and $u^2+h^2=289$. Thus $(u+h)^2=529$ and $u+h=23$, giving roots $u,h=8,15$. Since $2u>20$, $u=15$ and the base is $\boxed{30}$.
