Since $2240=2\cdot1111+18$,
\[
\dfrac{2240}{1111}=2+\dfrac{18}{1111}=2+\dfrac{162}{9999}=2.\overline{0162}.
\]
The digits after the decimal point repeat with period $4$: $n_1=0$, $n_2=1$, $n_3=6$, $n_4=2$, and $n_k$ depends only on $k$ modulo $4$. So
\[
n_{16}=n_4=2,\quad n_{13}=n_1=0,\quad n_{10}=n_2=1,\quad n_7=n_3=6,
\]
and $1000n_{16}+100n_{13}+10n_{10}+n_7=2000+0+10+6=\boxed{2016}$.
