Alice and Bob alternate turns, Alice first. Independently on each turn, Alice obtains a head with probability $\dfrac{2}{3}$ and a six with probability $\dfrac{1}{6}$; Bob obtains a head with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$ and a six with probability $\dfrac{2}{3}$. All random choices are independent. Stop when two consecutive turns both produce a head and a six. Count a round when its Alice turn starts, even if the game stops before Bob moves. Find the expected number of rounds.
