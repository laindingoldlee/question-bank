Call a turn successful if it produces both outcomes. Alice and Bob have success probabilities $\alpha=\dfrac{1}{9}$ and $\beta=\dfrac{1}{3}$. Let $F_0,F_1$ be the expected additional rounds at the start of Alice\textquotesingle s turn, according as the preceding Bob turn was unsuccessful or successful; initially the state is $0$. Conditioning on the coming round gives $F_0=1+(1-\beta)F_0+(1-\alpha)\beta F_1$ and $F_1=1+(1-\alpha)((1-\beta)F_0+\beta F_1)$. Thus $F_0-\dfrac{8}{9}F_1=3$ and $19F_1=27+16F_0$. Solving gives $F_1=\dfrac{675}{43}$ and $\boxed{F_0=\dfrac{729}{43}}$. These expectations are finite because every fresh two-turn block has a fixed positive probability of two successes.
