The denominators are $1\cdot2$, $2\cdot3$, $3\cdot4$, $4\cdot5$, $5\cdot6$, $6\cdot7$, and $\dfrac{1}{k(k+1)}=\dfrac{1}{k}-\dfrac{1}{k+1}$. So the sum telescopes:
\[
\left(1-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)+\left(\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{3}\right)+\cdots+\left(\dfrac{1}{6}-\dfrac{1}{7}\right)=1-\dfrac{1}{7}=\boxed{\dfrac{6}{7}} .
\]
