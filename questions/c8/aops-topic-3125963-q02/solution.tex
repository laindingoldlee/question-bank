For every solution, expansion gives $(m+n-o)^2-m^2-n^2=2p-o^2$. The identity therefore holds exactly when $2p=o^2$. For integer parameters this means $o=2t,p=2t^2$ for some integer $t$. In particular every integer solution then has $m^2+n^2$ a perfect square.
