Snape's cloak is an isosceles trapezoid with bases $2$ feet and $5$ feet and height $6$ feet. It gets jammed in an industrial shredder, and when it comes out it has several triangular holes, which do not overlap. Each hole is a triangle whose side lengths are whole numbers of inches and whose perimeter is $16$ inches, and no two holes are congruent.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that the cloak has at most $5$ holes. (The original version of this problem said that there were $6$ holes.)
\item[(b)] If the cloak has $5$ holes, what is the area of the remaining cloak, in square inches?
\end{enumerate}
