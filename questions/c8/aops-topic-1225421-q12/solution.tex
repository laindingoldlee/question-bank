The four areas are positive with sum $1$, so by AM-GM their product is at most $\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^4=\dfrac{1}{256}$. Equality is achieved by the two lines joining the midpoints of opposite sides, which cut the square into four squares of area $\dfrac{1}{4}$. The answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{256}}$.
