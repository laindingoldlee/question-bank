Since $a+b=\dfrac{50-4b}{3}+b=\dfrac{50-b}{3}$, the sum is largest when $b$ is smallest. We need $3\mid50-4b$, i.e. $b\equiv2\pmod3$, so the smallest $b$ is $2$, giving $a=14$ and $a+b=\boxed{16}$. (The pairs are $(14,2),(10,5),(6,8),(2,11)$ with sums $16,15,14,13$.)
