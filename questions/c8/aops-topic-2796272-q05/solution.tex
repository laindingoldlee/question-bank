The pieces have $3\cdot2017+5\cdot2016=6051+10080=16131$ sides in all. The chain has $4033$ pieces and hence $4032$ joins, and each join hides $2$ sides.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real R=1/sin(pi/5);pair[] V;for(int k=0;k<5;++k) V.push(R*dir(90+72k));draw(V[0]--V[1]--V[2]--V[3]--V[4]--cycle);
for(int i : new int[]{1,3}){pair m=(V[i]+V[(i+1)%5])/2;pair apex=m+sqrt(3)*unit(m);draw(V[i]--apex--V[(i+1)%5]);}
label("$\cdots$",(2.6,-0.6));label("$\cdots$",(-2.6,-0.6));
\end{asy}

So the polygon has $16131-2\cdot4032=8067$ sides of length $2$, and its perimeter is $\boxed{16134}$ inches.
