Marissa cuts $2016$ regular pentagons and $2017$ equilateral triangles, all with side $2$ inches. She joins them in a chain, alternating triangle, pentagon, triangle, $\dots$, triangle, each piece sharing a full side with the previous one, with no overlaps and no other sides touching. What is the perimeter of the resulting polygon?
