Triangle $ABC$ has vertices $A=(0,0)$, $B=(2b,2c)$ and $C=(2a,0)$, where $a\ne0$ and $c\ne0$. Using coordinates, prove that the three medians of the triangle pass through one point $G$ (the centroid), and that $G$ divides each median in the ratio $2:1$ from the vertex.
