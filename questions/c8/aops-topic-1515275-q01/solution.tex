The midpoints of the sides are
\[
D=(a+b,\ c)\ \text{of }BC,\qquad E=(a,\ 0)\ \text{of }AC,\qquad F=(b,\ c)\ \text{of }AB .
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(2,4),C=(6,0);
pair D=(B+C)/2,E=(A+C)/2,F=(A+B)/2,G=(A+B+C)/3;
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(1));
draw(A--D,blue);draw(B--E,blue);draw(C--F,blue);
dot(D);dot(E);dot(F);dot(G,red);
label("$A(0,0)$",A,SW);label("$B(2b,2c)$",B,N);label("$C(2a,0)$",C,SE);
label("$D$",D,NE);label("$E$",E,S);label("$F$",F,NW);label("$G$",G,2*dir(245),red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Let $G=\left(\dfrac{2a+2b}{3},\dfrac{2c}{3}\right)$. The point two thirds of the way from a vertex $V$ to a point $W$ is $V+\dfrac{2}{3}(W-V)$. For each median:
\[
A+\dfrac{2}{3}(D-A)=\left(\dfrac{2(a+b)}{3},\ \dfrac{2c}{3}\right)=G,
\]
\[
B+\dfrac{2}{3}(E-B)=\left(2b+\dfrac{2}{3}(a-2b),\ 2c-\dfrac{4c}{3}\right)=\left(\dfrac{2a+2b}{3},\ \dfrac{2c}{3}\right)=G,
\]
\[
C+\dfrac{2}{3}(F-C)=\left(2a+\dfrac{2}{3}(b-2a),\ \dfrac{2c}{3}\right)=\left(\dfrac{2a+2b}{3},\ \dfrac{2c}{3}\right)=G .
\]
So $G$ lies on all three medians $AD$, $BE$, $CF$, and on each it is two thirds of the way from the vertex, i.e.\ $AG:GD=BG:GE=CG:GF=2:1$. $\blacksquare$

(This avoids solving for the intersection of two lines. If one does use line equations, the median $BE$ has slope $\dfrac{2c-0}{2b-a}$ when $2b\ne a$ and passes through $E=(a,0)$, so its equation is $y=\dfrac{2c}{2b-a}(x-a)$; the post's version, with ``$-a$'' added after $\dfrac{2c}{a-2b}x$, has both the slope sign and the intercept wrong.)
