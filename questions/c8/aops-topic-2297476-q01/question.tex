\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Let $a\ne0$. By completing the square, show that $y=ax^2+bx+c$ can be written in the form $y=a(x-h)^2+k$, and find $h$ and $k$ in terms of $a$, $b$, $c$. Explain why the graph is a parabola with vertex $(h,k)$ that opens upward if $a>0$ and downward if $a<0$.
\item[(b)] Explain in the same way why the graph of $x=ay^2+by+c$ (with $a\ne0$) is a parabola that opens to the right if $a>0$ and to the left if $a<0$, and find its vertex.
\item[(c)] For each of the parabolas $y=2x^2-12x+13$ and $x=-y^2+4y+1$, find the vertex and the direction in which it opens.
\end{enumerate}
