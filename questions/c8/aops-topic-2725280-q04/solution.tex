Let $N=10k$. Then $10k\equiv1\pmod3$ gives $k\equiv1\pmod3$, and $10k\equiv5\pmod{11}$, i.e. $-k\equiv5$, gives $k\equiv6\pmod{11}$. So $k\equiv28\pmod{33}$ and $N\equiv280\pmod{330}$. The least such $N$ above $500$ is $\boxed{610}$.
