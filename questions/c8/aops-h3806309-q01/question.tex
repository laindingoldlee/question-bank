Two players alternately fill a nonempty contiguous segment of unfilled cells in a single row or column of an $N\times M$ board. No filled cell may be used again. Normally the last player to move wins. Prove that the second player can force a win when $N,M$ are both even, and the first player can force a win when at least one is odd. In the misere version the last player to move loses; classify the winner on a $1\times M$ board.
