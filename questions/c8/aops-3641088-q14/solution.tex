The 8-15-17 triangle is right at A. Radii to the tangency points X on AB and Z on AC are perpendicular to those sides, so the minor central angle XIZ is $90^\circ$. Y lies on the other incircle arc. The inscribed angle XYZ is half the minor arc angle, hence $\boxed{45^\circ}$.

import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(8,0),C=(0,15),I=(3,3),X=(3,0),Z=(0,3),Y=(96.0/17,75.0/17);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(circle(I,3));draw(X--Y--Z--cycle,blue);label("$A$",A,(-1,-1));label("$B$",B,(1,-1));label("$C$",C,(0,1));label("$X$",X,(0,-1));label("$Y$",Y,(1,1));label("$Z$",Z,(-1,0));
\end{asy}
