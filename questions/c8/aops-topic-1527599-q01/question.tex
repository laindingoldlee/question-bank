(Pascal's theorem.) Let $A,B,C,D,E,F$ be six distinct points on a circle. Suppose the lines $AB$ and $DE$ meet at $P$, the lines $BC$ and $EF$ meet at $Q$, and the lines $CD$ and $FA$ meet at $R$, and that the lines $AB$, $CD$, $EF$ form a triangle. Prove that $P$, $Q$, $R$ are collinear, using Menelaus' theorem and the power of a point.
