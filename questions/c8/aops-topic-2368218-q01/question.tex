A buffalo gives $5$ litres of milk a day, a cow $2$ litres and a goat $\dfrac14$ litre. A farmer has $20$ animals in all, with at least one of each kind, and together they give $40$ litres of milk a day. How many buffaloes, cows and goats does the farmer have?
