Three bus drivers went to a grocery store. The first bought $4$ sausages, a bottle of cola and $10$ pieces of bread, and paid $16.90$. The second bought $3$ sausages, a bottle of cola and $7$ pieces of bread, and paid $12.60$. The third bought $2$ sausages, $2$ bottles of cola and $2$ pieces of bread. How much did the third driver pay?
