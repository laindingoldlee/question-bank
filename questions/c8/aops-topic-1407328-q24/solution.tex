Since $6033=3\cdot2011$ and $\left(2011\sqrt3\right)^2=3\cdot2011^2$,
\[
x^3+3\cdot2011x^2+3\cdot2011^2x+2011^3=(x+2011)^3 .
\]
For an integer $x$, $(x+2011)^3=n^6=\left(n^2\right)^3$ holds exactly when $x+2011=n^2$, because cubing is one-to-one on the reals. So $x+2011$ must be a perfect square. The smallest square larger than $2011$ is $45^2=2025$ ($44^2=1936$), so the smallest positive $x$ is $2025-2011=\boxed{14}$.
