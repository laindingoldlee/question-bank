In triangle $ABC$, points $D$, $E$, $F$ lie on sides $BC$, $CA$, $AB$ respectively, with
\[\dfrac{AF}{AB}=\dfrac{BD}{BC}=\dfrac{CE}{CA}=\dfrac25 .\]
Then points $G$, $H$, $I$ lie on sides $EF$, $FD$, $DE$ of triangle $DEF$ respectively, with
\[\dfrac{GE}{EF}=\dfrac13,\qquad\dfrac{FH}{FD}=\dfrac14,\qquad\dfrac{DI}{DE}=\dfrac34 .\]
Find $\dfrac{[GHI]}{[ABC]}$, where $[XYZ]$ denotes the area of triangle $XYZ$.
