More heads than tails in six tosses means 4,5,or 6 heads. By the binomial formula, the probability is\[\binom64\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^4\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^2+\binom65\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^5\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)+\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^6=\dfrac{1215+1458+729}{4096}=\boxed{\dfrac{1701}{2048}}.\]This is choice E.
