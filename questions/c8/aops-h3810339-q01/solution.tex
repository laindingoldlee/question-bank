Let $M,P,C$ be the events that all books of the indicated subject are consecutive. Among $7!=5040$ orders,
\[
|M|=5!3!=720,\quad |P|=|C|=6!2!=1440.
\]
Treating each indicated subject as a block gives
\[
|M\cap P|=|M\cap C|=4!3!2!=288,\quad |P\cap C|=5!2!2!=480,
\]
and $|M\cap P\cap C|=3!3!2!2!=144$. Inclusion--exclusion therefore gives
\[
5040-(720+1440+1440)+(288+288+480)-144=\boxed{2352}.
\]
