Each nonzero digit occurs six times in the thousands position, contributing $ 6(1+2+7)1000=60000 $. In any lower position each nonzero digit occurs four times: choose either other nonzero leading digit, then order the remaining two digits. Zero contributes nothing. Thus lower positions contribute $ 4(1+2+7)(100+10+1)=4440 $. The sum is $\boxed{64440}$.
