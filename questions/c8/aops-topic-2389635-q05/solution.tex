Put the launch point at the origin, with $x$ the horizontal distance and $y$ the height. The vertex of the parabola is $(15,112.5)$, so
\[y=112.5-a(x-15)^2\]
for some $a>0$. It passes through $(0,0)$: $0=112.5-225a$, so $a=\dfrac12$. At $x=20$:
\[y=112.5-\dfrac12\cdot5^2=112.5-12.5=\boxed{100\text{ metres}}.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
real s=0.25;
real f(real x){return s*(112.5-0.5*(x-15)^2);}
draw((-1,0)--(35,0),Arrow(6)); draw((0,-1.5)--(0,33),Arrow(6));
label("$x$",(35,0),E); label("$y$",(0,33),N);
draw(graph(f,0,30),blue+linewidth(0.9));
dot((0,0)); dot((15,s*112.5)); dot((20,s*100),red);
draw((15,0)--(15,s*112.5),dashed); draw((20,0)--(20,s*100),red+dashed);
label("$(15,\,112.5)$",(15,s*112.5),N); label("$(20,\,100)$",(20,s*100),NE,red);
label("$15$",(15,0),S); label("$20$",(20,0),S); label("$30$",(30,0),S); label("$0$",(0,0),SW);
\end{asy}

(By symmetry the fish lands $30$ metres from the beach.)
