A mock test has $10$ problems. A correct answer earns $5$ points, a wrong answer earns $-2$ points, and a problem left blank earns $1$ point.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] For how many combinations of the numbers of correct, wrong and blank answers is the total score exactly $28$?
\item[(b)] In how many ways can the $10$ problems be marked correct, wrong or blank so that the total score is $28$?
\end{enumerate}
