Square $ABCD$ has vertices $A(0,0)$, $B(4,0)$, $C(4,4)$ and $D(0,4)$. A point $P$ in the interior of square $ABCD$ is chosen and reflected over the line containing segment $CD$ to point $Q$. Point $Q$ is then reflected over the line containing segment $BC$ to point $R$. What are the coordinates of the midpoint of segment $PR$? Express your answer as an ordered pair.
