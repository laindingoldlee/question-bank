Factor the sum as $70!(1+71+71\cdot72)=70!\cdot5184=70!\cdot2^6\cdot3^4$. The exponent of $3$ in $70!$ is $\lfloor\dfrac{70}{3}\rfloor+\lfloor\dfrac{70}{9}\rfloor+\lfloor\dfrac{70}{27}\rfloor=23+7+2=32$. Adding four gives $n=\boxed{36}$.
