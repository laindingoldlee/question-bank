The $52$ cards in a deck are numbered $1,2,\dots,52$. Alex, Blair, Corey and Dylan each pick a card from the deck without replacement, each card equally likely. The two people with the lower-numbered cards form one team and the two with the higher-numbered cards form the other. Let $p(a)$ be the probability that Alex and Dylan are on the same team, given that Alex picks one of the cards $a$ and $a+9$ and Dylan picks the other. Find the minimum value of $p(a)$ for which $p(a)\ge\dfrac{1}{2}$.
