Blair and Corey take two of the other $50$ cards, $\dbinom{50}2=1225$ equally likely pairs. Alex and Dylan are together if both of Blair's and Corey's cards are below $a$ or both are above $a+9$:
\[
p(a)=\dfrac{\dbinom{a-1}2+\dbinom{43-a}2}{1225}.
\]
This is symmetric about $a=22$ and decreases towards the middle. We need $(a-1)(a-2)+(43-a)(42-a)\ge1225$. At $a=8$: $42+35\cdot34=1232\ge1225$; at $a=9$: $56+34\cdot33=1178<1225$. So the smallest admissible value occurs at $a=8$ (and symmetrically $a=36$):
\[
p(8)=\dfrac{\dbinom72+\dbinom{35}2}{1225}=\dfrac{21+595}{1225}=\dfrac{616}{1225}=\boxed{\dfrac{88}{175}}.
\]
(In the original problem the answer is asked as $m+n=88+175=263$.)
