We have $2^{62}=4^{31}=3n+1$, so $2^{62}\equiv1\pmod n$. Also $n-1=\dfrac{4(4^{30}-1)}{3}$. Since $2^5\equiv1\pmod{31}$, $4^{30}\equiv1\pmod{31}$; as $3$ is coprime to $31$, $31\mid n-1$. The latter is also even, so $62\mid n-1$. Therefore $2^{n-1}$ is a power of $2^{62}$ and is congruent to $\boxed1\pmod n$.
