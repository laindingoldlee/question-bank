The two parts take $\dfrac{30}{40}=\dfrac{3}{4}$ hour and $\dfrac{75}{50}=\dfrac{3}{2}$ hours, a total of $\dfrac{9}{4}$ hours for $105$ miles. The average speed is total distance over total time:
\[
\dfrac{105}{\dfrac{9}{4}}=\dfrac{420}{9}=\boxed{\dfrac{140}{3}}\approx46.7\text{ miles per hour}.
\]
(It is not the average $45$ of the two speeds, because the parts take different times.)
