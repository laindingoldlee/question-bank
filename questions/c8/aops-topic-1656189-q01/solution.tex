$8!=40320=20\cdot2016$, so $2016$ divides $n!$ for every $n\ge8$. Hence
\[
L\equiv1!+2!+\cdots+7!=1+2+6+24+120+720+5040=5913\pmod{2016},
\]
and $5913-2\cdot2016=1881$. The remainder is $1881$, with digit sum $1+8+8+1=\boxed{\textbf{(f)}\ 18}$.
