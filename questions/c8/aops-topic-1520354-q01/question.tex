In how many ways can $1{,}000{,}000$ be written as a product of three positive integers, if the order of the factors does not matter? A student argued: ``$1{,}000{,}000=2^6\cdot5^6$; the $2$'s can be split among the three factors in $7$ ways, $(0,0,6),(0,1,5),(0,2,4),(0,3,3),(1,1,4),(1,2,3),(2,2,2)$, and the $5$'s in $\dbinom82=28$ ways, so the answer is $7\cdot28=196$.'' Find the correct answer and explain the error.
