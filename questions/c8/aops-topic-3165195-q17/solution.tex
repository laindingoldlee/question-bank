The zero signed sum implies that the absolute-value sum is twice the sum of the positive terms, hence even. Thus $n^2$, and therefore $n$, must be even. Conversely, let $n=2m$. Choose the $m$ positive distinct integers $m+1,m+3,\ldots,3m-1$ and their negatives. Their positive sum is $m\cdot\dfrac{(m+1)+(3m-1)}{2}=2m^2$, so the absolute-value sum is $4m^2=n^2$ and the signed sum is zero. Every positive even integer works. The count below 1000000 is $\boxed{499999}$.
