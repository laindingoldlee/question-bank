Let the radius be $r$ and the height $h$ (in cm).
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real r=2, h=2;
path bot=ellipse((0,0),r,0.45);
path top=ellipse((0,h),r,0.45);
fill(top,paleblue);
draw(scale(r,0.45)*arc((0,0),1,180,360));
draw(scale(r,0.45)*arc((0,0),1,0,180),dashed);
draw(top);
draw((-r,0)--(-r,h)); draw((r,0)--(r,h));
draw((0,0)--(r,0),blue); label("$r$",(r/2,0),S,blue);
draw((r+0.35,0)--(r+0.35,h),Arrows(4)); label("$h$",(r+0.35,h/2),E);
label("open top",(0,h+0.45),N);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The volume condition is $\pi r^2h=1000\pi$, so $h=\dfrac{1000}{r^2}$. The material is the base plus the side:
\[
A=\pi r^2+2\pi rh=\pi r^2+\dfrac{2000\pi}{r}=\pi\left(r^2+\dfrac{1000}{r}+\dfrac{1000}{r}\right).
\]
By AM-GM on the three positive terms,
\[
r^2+\dfrac{1000}{r}+\dfrac{1000}{r}\ge 3\sqrt[3]{r^2\cdot\dfrac{1000}{r}\cdot\dfrac{1000}{r}}=3\sqrt[3]{10^6}=300,
\]
with equality when $r^2=\dfrac{1000}{r}$, that is $r=10$ (and then $h=10$). Hence the minimum amount of material is
\[\boxed{300\pi}\ \text{cm}^2.\]
