There are $4!=24$ such numbers. In each of the four positions, each digit appears in $\dfrac{24}{4}=6$ of the numbers (fix the digit in that position and arrange the other three in $3!=6$ ways). So the digits in each position add up to $6\cdot(6+7+8+9)=6\cdot30=180$, and the total is
\[
180\cdot(1000+100+10+1)=180\cdot1111=\boxed{199980}.
\]
(Equivalently, the $24$ numbers pair up into $12$ pairs adding to $6666+9999=16665$, each digit $d$ being matched with $15-d$: $12\cdot16665=199980$.)
