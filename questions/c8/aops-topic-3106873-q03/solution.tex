The numerator counts five-element subsets of a set split into groups of sizes $ 6 $ and $ 12 $, according to how many elements come from the first group. It is therefore $\binom{18}{5}$. The ratio is $\dfrac{18-4}{5}=\boxed{\dfrac{14}{5}}$.
