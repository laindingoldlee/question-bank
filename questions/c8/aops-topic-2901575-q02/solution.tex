If both legs were even, the hypotenuse would be even too, contradicting primitivity. If both legs were odd, then $a^2+b^2\equiv2\pmod4$, but a square is congruent only to $0$ or $1$ modulo $4$. Thus exactly one leg is odd and one is even. Their squared sum is odd, so $c$ is odd.
