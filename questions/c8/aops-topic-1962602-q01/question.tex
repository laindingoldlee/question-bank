Seven jars hold a total of $250$ candies. They are lined up from left to right from fullest to emptiest, and no two jars hold the same number of candies. The leftmost jar has $72$ candies and the rightmost jar has $13$. What is the positive difference between the greatest and the least number of candies that could be in the second jar from the left?
