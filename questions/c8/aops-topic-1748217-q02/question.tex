Kevin and Allen start at the same place and walk at the same constant speed. Kevin walks $8$ m north, turns $18^\circ$ counterclockwise, walks another $8$ m, turns $18^\circ$ counterclockwise again, and so on. At the same time Allen walks $6$ m south, turns $15^\circ$ counterclockwise, walks another $6$ m, and so on. How far must Kevin walk before he meets Allen again?
