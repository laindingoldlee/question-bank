Put the pole at coordinate 0 and the person at 20 feet, with shadows extending positively. The person's shadow is $[20,34]$. For an overlap of 9 feet, the pole shadow ends at 29. Similar triangles give height ratio $\dfrac{29}{14}$, so the pole height is $70\cdot\dfrac{29}{14}=\boxed{145\text{ inches}}$.
