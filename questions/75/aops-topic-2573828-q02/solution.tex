Alice wins on her first flip with probability $\dfrac13$. Otherwise both miss (probability $\dfrac23\cdot\dfrac23=\dfrac49$) and the game restarts. So if $P$ is her winning probability, $P=\dfrac13+\dfrac49P$, giving $P=\boxed{\dfrac35}$.
