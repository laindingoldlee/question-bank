A number with $6$ divisors is $p^5$ or $p^2q$ ($p,q$ distinct primes). Using primes $\ge5$, the candidates are $5^5$, $5^2\cdot7=175$ and $7^2\cdot5=245$. The least is $\boxed{175}$.
