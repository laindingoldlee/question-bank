Each of the five sides lies on a line and meets a circle at at most two points, giving an upper bound of $10$. For attainment, take a regular pentagon centered at the circle center, with apothem less than the circle radius and circumradius greater than it. Every side then crosses the circle twice in its interior, and all ten intersection points are distinct. Thus the answer is $\boxed{10}$.

\end{asy}
