First arrange the $5$ non-physics books: $5!=120$ ways. They create $6$ gaps (before the first book, between books, after the last):
\[
\_\ X\ \_\ X\ \_\ X\ \_\ X\ \_\ X\ \_
\]
No two physics books are adjacent exactly when they go into $4$ different gaps. Choosing the gaps for the $4$ distinct physics books in order gives $6\cdot5\cdot4\cdot3=360$ ways. Total:
\[
120\cdot360=\boxed{43200}.
\]
