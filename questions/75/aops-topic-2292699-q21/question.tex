Okta can stay in the sun for $16$ minutes before getting sunburned. If he uses a sunscreen with SPF $20$, he can stay in the sun $20$ times as long before getting sunburned, or $20\cdot16=320$ minutes. This morning, he spent $9$ minutes in the sun before putting on sunscreen. If he then applies SPF $20$ sunscreen, how many more minutes can he stay in the sun before getting burned?
