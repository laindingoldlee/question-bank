Five numbers in $[-1,1]$ have four consecutive gaps totaling at most $2$, so their smallest gap is at most $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Thus the sum is at most $\dfrac{3}{2}$. Equality is achieved by the points $(-1,-\dfrac{1}{2},0),(-\dfrac{1}{2},-1,\dfrac{1}{2}),(0,\dfrac{1}{2},-1),(\dfrac{1}{2},1,1),(1,0,-\dfrac{1}{2})$. Each lies on the surface, and each coordinate list is a permutation of $-1,-\dfrac{1}{2},0,\dfrac{1}{2},1$. The maximum is $\boxed{\dfrac{3}{2}}$.
