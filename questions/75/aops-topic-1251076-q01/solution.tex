Look at parities: odd $+$ odd $=$ even, odd $+$ even $=$ odd, even $+$ odd $=$ odd. Starting from (odd, odd), the parities run
\[\text{odd, odd, even, odd, odd, even},\dots\]
and since each term depends only on the previous two, the pattern repeats with period $3$. By induction, $F_n$ is even exactly when $3\mid n$. Among $F_1,\dots,F_{3k}$ there are $k$ even terms ($F_3,F_6,\dots,F_{3k}$) and $2k$ odd ones. $\blacksquare$
