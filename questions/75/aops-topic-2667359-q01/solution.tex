By Vieta's formulas $a+b+c=0$. From $f(0)=c$ we get $c=-21$, and indeed $(-21)^3-883(-21)-9282=-9261+18543-9282=0$. Then $a+b=21$, and $f(-1)=a-b+c=26$ gives $a-b=47$. So $a=34$ and $b=-13$. (Check: $x^3-883x-9282=(x+21)(x-34)(x+13)$.) Hence
\[f(x)=34x^2-13x-21=(x-1)(34x+21),\]
whose roots are $\boxed{1\ \text{and}\ -\dfrac{21}{34}}$.
