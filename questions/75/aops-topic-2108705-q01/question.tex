A work crew is laying a pipeline with pipe that has an inside diameter of $40$ cm. They run out of pipe $40$ m from their goal, but they have plenty of straws, each $15$ cm long with an inside diameter of $0.5$ cm. They plan to attach many lines of straws to the end of the pipe, each line made of straws joined end to end and reaching all the way to the goal, so that the lines together carry as much water as the pipe would. Assume that water flows at the same speed in the straws as in the pipe (ignoring friction and turbulence), so that the amount of water carried is proportional to the total cross-sectional area, and that straws cannot be cut. What is the least number of straws needed?
