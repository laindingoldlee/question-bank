\textbf{Number of lines.} Cross-sectional areas scale with the square of the diameter. The pipe's diameter is $\dfrac{40}{0.5}=80$ times a straw's, so its cross-section is $80^2=6400$ times as large.

So at least $6400$ lines of straws are needed.

\textbf{Straws per line.} Each line must cover $40$ m $=4000$ cm. Since $\dfrac{4000}{15}=266\dfrac{2}{3}$, $266$ straws reach only $3990$ cm, and each line needs $267$ straws.

Hence the least number of straws is
\[6400\cdot267=\boxed{1708800}.\]
