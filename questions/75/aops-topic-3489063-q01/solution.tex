Put $u=4b-3$ and $v=3a+4$. Then $uv=-12$, with $u\equiv1\pmod4$ and $v\equiv1\pmod3$. The divisors of $-12$ satisfying the first condition are $u=1,-3$. They yield $v=-12,4$ respectively, and only $v=4$ satisfies the second condition. Therefore $b=0,a=0$, and the unique solution is $\boxed{(0,0)}$.
