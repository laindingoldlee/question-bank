Let $v_2(n)$ denote the exponent of $2$ in $n$. The reduced denominator is odd exactly when $v_2(a)\ge v_2(b)$. The counts for valuations $0,1,\ldots,9$ are $256,128,64,32,16,8,4,2,1,1$. Thus the number of pairs with equal valuations is $\sum_{j=0}^{8}4^j+1=87382$. By symmetry, half the unequal pairs have the desired order. The favorable count is $\dfrac{512^2+87382}{2}=174763$, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{174763}{262144}}$.
