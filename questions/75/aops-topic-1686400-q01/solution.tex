There are $9\cdot10^5=900000$ six-digit numbers. Group them by their first five digits; each group consists of the ten numbers ending in $0,1,\dots,9$. Within a group the digit sums are ten consecutive integers, so exactly five are even. Hence exactly half of all six-digit numbers have an even digit sum: $\dfrac{900000}{2}=\boxed{450000}$.
