There are $\dbinom{15}{5}=3003$ arrangements in all (choose the positions of the black marbles).

\textbf{No two black marbles adjacent.} Place the $10$ white marbles in a row; they create $11$ gaps (including both ends). Putting at most one black marble in each gap gives $\dbinom{11}{5}=462$ arrangements.

So the number with at least two adjacent black marbles is $3003-462=\boxed{2541}$.
