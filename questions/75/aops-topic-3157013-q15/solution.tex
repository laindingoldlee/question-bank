Multiply by $ b $ and $ a $ respectively: $ ab+1=\dfrac{b}{2015}=2016^2a $. Thus $ a=\dfrac{b}{2015\cdot2016^2}$. Substitution into the second equation gives $ b^2-2016^2b+2015\cdot2016^2=0 $, factoring as $(b-2016)(b-2015\cdot2016)=0 $. Both yield nonzero $ a $ and satisfy the originals. Hence $\boxed{b=2016\text{ or }4062240}$.
