Let O be the diagonals' intersection. The common region is triangle ABO. Its base is one side s and its altitude is half a side, so its area is $\dfrac{s^2}{4}$. Thus $\dfrac{a}{b}=\dfrac{1}{4}$ and $a+b=\boxed 5$.
\begin{asy}
import geometry; size(220);
pair A=(0,0),B=(2,0),C=(2,2),D=(0,2),O=(1,1);fill(A--B--O--cycle,lightgray);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C);draw(B--D);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$O$",O,N);
\end{asy}
