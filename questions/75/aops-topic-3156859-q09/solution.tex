Consecutive centres have distance $ 2^{i+1}-2^i=2^i $. By the triangle inequality, centre separation is at most $ 1+2+\cdots+512=1023 $. Thus $ AB\le1+1023+1024=2048 $. Place all centres on one line at coordinate $ 2^i-1 $, so all circles share the leftmost point $-1 $. Choose $ A=-1 $ and $ B=2047 $ to attain $\boxed{2048}$.
