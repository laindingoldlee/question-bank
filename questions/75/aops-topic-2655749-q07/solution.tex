Let each half of the trail have length $d$. Until they meet, Chantal has spent
\[\dfrac d4+\dfrac d2+\dfrac d3=\dfrac{13d}{12}\text{ hours},\]
and in that time Jean walked the distance $d$. So Jean's average speed is $d\div\dfrac{13d}{12}=\boxed{\dfrac{12}{13}}$ miles per hour.
