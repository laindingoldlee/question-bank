For a positive integer $n$, let $S=\{1,2,\ldots,10\}$. A sequence $(x_1,\ldots,x_n)\in S^n$ is palindromic if $x_i=x_{n+1-i}$ for every $i$. Find $\displaystyle\sum_{\text{palindromic }x}\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}10^kx_{k+1}$.
