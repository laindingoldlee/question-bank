\textbf{A tangent line bound.} For every $t\ge0$,
\[\dfrac{1}{t^2+1}\ge1-\dfrac {t}{2},\]
because
\[\dfrac{1}{t^2+1}-1+\dfrac {t}{2}=\dfrac{2-2\left(t^2+1\right)+t\left(t^2+1\right)}{2\left(t^2+1\right)}=\dfrac{t^3-2t^2+t}{2\left(t^2+1\right)}=\dfrac{t(t-1)^2}{2\left(t^2+1\right)}\ge0 .\]
(The line $1-\dfrac {t}{2}$ is the tangent to $y=\dfrac{1}{t^2+1}$ at $t=1$.)

\textbf{Summing.} Applying this to each $x_i$ and adding,
\[\sum_{i=1}^{n}\dfrac{1}{x_i^2+1}\ge n-\dfrac{x_1+\cdots+x_n}{2}=n-\dfrac {n}{2}=\dfrac {n}{2} .\]
Equality holds when every $x_i=1$, which satisfies the condition and gives $n\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}$. So the minimum is $\boxed{\dfrac {n}{2}}$.
