Let $n$ be a positive integer, and let $x_1,x_2,\dots,x_n$ be nonnegative real numbers with $x_1+x_2+\cdots+x_n=n$. Find the minimum value of
\[\dfrac{1}{x_1^2+1}+\dfrac{1}{x_2^2+1}+\cdots+\dfrac{1}{x_n^2+1}.\]
