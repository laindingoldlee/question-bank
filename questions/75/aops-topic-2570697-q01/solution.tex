We have $a+b=3$ and $a^2+b^2=4$, so
\[ab=\dfrac{(a+b)^2-\left(a^2+b^2\right)}2=\dfrac{9-4}2=\dfrac52.\]
Then
\[a^3+b^3=(a+b)^3-3ab(a+b)=27-\dfrac{45}2=\dfrac92,\]
so $x=\dfrac92-2=\boxed{\dfrac52}$, not the ``pattern'' value $3$.

(Here $a,b$ are the roots of $t^2-3t+\dfrac52=0$, namely $\dfrac{3\pm i}2$; there are no real such numbers.)
