Every year a witch makes $50$ candy apples, one for each of the $50$ children who visit her. For $50$ candy apples she buys $50$ apples for $\$100$, $100$ bottles of caramel sauce for $\$200$, $50$ bottles of sprinkles for $\$50$ and $5$ bottles of whipped cream for $\$15$. This year the number of children will be $80\%$ lower. If she buys ingredients in proportion to the number of children, at the same prices per item, how many of each item does she buy, and how much does she spend?
