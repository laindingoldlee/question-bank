A student claims that for all positive integers $n$ and $z$,
\[
n^z+n^{z-1}+\dots+n^2+n=\dfrac{n^{z+1}-1}{2}.
\]
Show that the claim is false, and find and prove the correct formula for the sum when $n\ge2$.
