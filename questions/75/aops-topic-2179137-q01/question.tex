The hockey stick identity states that for integers $0\le r\le n$,
\[\dbinom rr+\dbinom{r+1}r+\cdots+\dbinom nr=\dbinom{n+1}{r+1}.\]
By writing $k^3$ in terms of binomial coefficients, use this identity to find a closed form for $1^3+2^3+\cdots+n^3$.
