The left side is unchanged when $x$ is replaced by $2-x$, since $(2-x)-1=1-x$ and $1-(2-x)=x-1$. So if such an $f$ existed, then for every $x$
\[
x=f(x-1)+f(1-x)=f\bigl((2-x)-1\bigr)+f\bigl(1-(2-x)\bigr)=2-x,
\]
which fails for $x=0$. Concretely, $x=0$ gives $f(-1)+f(1)=0$, while $x=2$ gives $f(1)+f(-1)=2$, a contradiction. Hence no such function exists. $\blacksquare$
