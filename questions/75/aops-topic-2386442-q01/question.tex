Iris plays a game on a $5\times5$ board. Her piece starts on the bottom-left square $X$ and must reach the top-right square $Y$. Each move takes the piece any positive number of squares straight up or any positive number of squares straight to the right (staying on the board). Moving two squares up in a single move is different from moving two squares up in two moves. How many different sequences of moves take the piece from $X$ to $Y$?
