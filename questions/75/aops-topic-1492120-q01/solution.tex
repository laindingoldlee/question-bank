Use the complement. He wins neither with probability
\[
\left(1-\dfrac{2}{3}\right)\left(1-\dfrac{1}{10}\right)=\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot\dfrac{9}{10}=\dfrac{3}{10},
\]
by independence. So he wins at least one with probability $1-\dfrac{3}{10}=\boxed{\dfrac{7}{10}}$.
