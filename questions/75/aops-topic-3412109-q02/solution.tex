If the final digit is $0$, the first three positions have $4\cdot3\cdot2=24$ choices. If the final digit is $6$ or $8$, the leading digit has three nonzero choices, and the two middle positions have $3\cdot2$ choices, giving $18$ per ending. Thus the total is $24+2\cdot18=\boxed{60}$.
