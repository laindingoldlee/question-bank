A regular pentagon $ABCDE$ has consecutive vertices $A=(-2,-4)$, $B=(2,-4)$ and lies below line $AB$. The arc of $y=-x^2$ from $A$ to $B$ bows outward from the pentagon. Rotate this arc about the pentagon center by multiples of $72^\circ$ to replace all five sides by congruent outward parabolic arcs. Find the area enclosed by the resulting curve, rounded to two decimal places.
