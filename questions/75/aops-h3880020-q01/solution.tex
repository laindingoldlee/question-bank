The side length is $4$, and the pentagon apothem is $2\cot36^\circ$. Thus its area is $\dfrac{1}{2}(20)(2\cot36^\circ)=20\cot36^\circ$. The parabolic segment between $AB:y=-4$ and $y=-x^2$ has area
\[
\int_{-2}^{2}(4-x^2)\,dx=\dfrac{32}{3}.
\]
The five outward segments lie in the five disjoint exterior radial sectors of the pentagon, so their areas add without overlap. Consequently the enclosed area is
\[
20\cot36^\circ+\dfrac{160}{3}\approx80.86097174,
\]
which rounds to $\boxed{80.86}$ square units.
