Let $H=\dfrac{\sum_{k=1}^{2023}1}{k}$ and $Q(x)=x^2P(x)-(x^3+x^2+x+1)$. This vanishes at 1 through 2023, so $Q(x)=(Ax+B)\prod_{k=1}^{2023}(x-k)$. Values and derivatives at zero give $B=\dfrac{1}{2023!}$ and $A=\dfrac{(H+1)}{2023!}$. Consequently $L=2024^3+2024^2+2024+1+2024H+2025$, which is positive. But the stipulated expression $1-\dfrac{(c^a-1)}{b}$ is negative whenever integers satisfy $2<a<b<c$. Therefore no such parameters exist, and a+b+c is not defined by the source.
