A museum has $100$ floors with one egg on each floor; the higher the floor, the more valuable the egg. You may steal one egg, but you must drop it from its floor into your truck, so it has to survive the fall. All eggs have the same (unknown) strength: there is a floor $h$, with $0\le h\le100$, such that an egg dropped from floor $k$ survives exactly when $k\le h$. You have two worthless fake eggs of the same strength; a fake egg that survives a drop can be reused, one that breaks cannot. What is the least number of test drops that guarantees you can determine $h$ (and so steal the most valuable egg that survives its drop)?
