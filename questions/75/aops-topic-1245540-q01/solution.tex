There are $\dbinom52=10$ intersection points. A triple of them forms a triangle unless it is collinear. Each of the $5$ lines contains $4$ intersection points (one with each other line), giving $\dbinom43=4$ collinear triples per line; no other triple is collinear (three intersection points not all on one of the lines would require further coincidences, which general position excludes). Hence the number of triangles is
\[\dbinom{10}{3}-5\cdot4=120-20=\boxed{100}.\]
