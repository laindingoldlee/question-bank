Forty men can finish a piece of work in $26$ days. How many men are needed to finish it in $16$ days? A student writes: ``$1$ man can finish the work in $40\cdot26=1040$ days, so $\dfrac{1040\text{ days}}{1\text{ man}}=\dfrac{16\text{ days}}{x\text{ men}}$, giving $x=\dfrac{16}{1040}$.'' Find the correct answer and explain the error.
