Since $10^5\le N<10^6$, its fourth power has $d=21,22,23,$ or 24 digits. Its digit sum is 5d. Fourth powers modulo nine are $0,1,4,7$, whereas $5d$ for these four d has residues $6,2,7,3$. Therefore only d=23 is possible, giving digit sum $\boxed{115}$. The condition is attainable: $316384^4=10019776859647362727936$ has 23 digits and digit sum 115.
