The Hinge Theorem says: if two sides of one triangle are equal to two sides of another triangle, then the triangle with the larger included angle has the larger third side. Its converse also holds.

In triangle $ABC$ with $AB>AC$, let $M$ be the midpoint of $BC$. Prove that $\angle AMB>\angle AMC$, and deduce that $\angle AMB$ is obtuse.
