Let there be $n$ people. Since $\dfrac{2n}{5}$ and $\dfrac{3n}{4}$ are whole numbers, $n$ is a multiple of $20$. At most $n$ people wear a hat or a glove, so by inclusion-exclusion the number wearing both is at least
\[\dfrac{2n}{5}+\dfrac{3n}{4}-n=\dfrac{3n}{20}\ge\dfrac{3\cdot20}{20}=3.\]
This is attained with $n=20$: $8$ people wear gloves, $15$ wear hats, $3$ wear both and nobody wears neither ($8+15-3=20$). The minimum is $\boxed{3}$.
