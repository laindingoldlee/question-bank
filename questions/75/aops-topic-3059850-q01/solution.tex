The midpoint segment has half the base length and lies at half the triangle height. If the triangle base and height are $ b,h $, the rectangle area is $\dfrac{b}{2}\cdot\dfrac{h}{2}=\dfrac{bh}{4}$, half the triangle area $\dfrac{bh}{2}$. Hence the triangle area is $\boxed{34\text{ cm}^2}$.

usepackage("amsmath");

pair A=(0,4),B=(-3,0),C=(3,0),M=(A+B)/2,Nn=(A+C)/2;draw(A--B--C--cycle);filldraw((-1.5,0)--(1.5,0)--Nn--M--cycle,lightgray);label("$M$",M,W);label("$N$",Nn,E);label("$17$",(0,1));
\end{asy}
