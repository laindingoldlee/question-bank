Ten guppies in Vida's tank are $5,5,8,8,8,9,10,11,11$ and $33$ weeks old. A guppy of age $n$ weeks is added to her tank. The sum of the mean, the median and the unique mode of the eleven guppies' ages equals their range. What is the sum of all possible integer values of $n$?
