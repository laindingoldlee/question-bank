The angles around the vertex must sum to $360^\circ$:
\[90+k\left(180-\dfrac{360}{n}\right)=360.\]
Thus $n=\dfrac{4k}{2k-3}$. The denominator must be positive, so $k\ge2$. Since $n\ge3$, $4k\ge6k-9$, giving $k\le4$. Testing $k=2,3,4$ gives $n=8,4,\dfrac{16}{5}$ respectively. The integer solutions are $\boxed{(n,k)=(8,2),(4,3)}$. Both are realizable locally: one square with two octagons, or four squares.
