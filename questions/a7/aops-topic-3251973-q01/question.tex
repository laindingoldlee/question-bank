Use $C=\dfrac{5}{9}(F-32)$ and $F=\dfrac{9}{5}C+32$, rounding to the nearest integer after each conversion. (a) Give two integer Fahrenheit temperatures that change after a round trip through Celsius. (b) Classify all such Fahrenheit integers. (c) Classify Celsius integers that change after the reverse round trip. Which starting scale avoids this effect?
