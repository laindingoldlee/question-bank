Let the fraction of the problems solved together that were answered correctly be $t$ (the same for both girls, since they solved them together). Each half counts equally, so Chloe's overall percentage is the average of $80\%$ and $t$:
\[\dfrac{80+t}{2}=88\quad\Longrightarrow\quad t=96.\]
Zoe's overall percentage is $\dfrac{90+96}{2}=\boxed{\textbf{(C) }93}$.
