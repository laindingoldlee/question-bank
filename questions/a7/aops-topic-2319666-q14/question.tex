Andrew, Beth and Carter start in Boston, Beijing and Moscow, respectively. Every night at midnight each of them teleports to a city chosen uniformly at random, independently of the others, from the list Boston, Beijing, Buenos Aires, London, Sydney, Moscow, Shanghai, Tokyo, excluding the city they are currently in. The first teleport is at midnight starting 17 September 2008 and the last at midnight starting 7 August 2009, and after each teleport, for each of the three pairs of people, we count $1$ if the two are in the same city. What is the expected value of the total count?
