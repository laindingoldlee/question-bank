Both are positive ninth powers: $33^{54}=(33^6)^9$ and $63^{45}=(63^5)^9$. Exact multiplication gives $33^6=1291467969>992436543=63^5$. Raising positive numbers to the ninth power preserves order, so $\boxed{33^{54}>63^{45}}$.
