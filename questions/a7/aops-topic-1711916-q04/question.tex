The number $2019$ can be written as a sum of consecutive integers (positive, zero or negative) in increasing order in several ways, for example $1009+1010=2019$ and $(-2018)+(-2017)+\cdots+2018+2019=2019$. Including these two examples, in how many ways can $2019$ be written as the sum of at least two consecutive integers in increasing order?
