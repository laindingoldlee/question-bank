Write $A=t\left(2s^3-t^3\right)$ and $B=s\left(s^3-2t^3\right)$, and let $p=s^3$, $q=t^3$. Then
\[A^3+B^3=q(2p-q)^3+p(p-2q)^3=p^4+2p^3q-2pq^3-q^4=(p+q)^3(p-q),\]
as both sides expand to the same polynomial. So
\[A^3+B^3=\left(s^3+t^3\right)^3\left(s^3-t^3\right)=(s-t)(s+t)^3\left(s^2-st+t^2\right)^3\left(s^2+st+t^2\right).\]
Dividing by $\left(s^3+t^3\right)^3$:
\[\left(\dfrac{A}{s^3+t^3}\right)^3+\left(\dfrac{B}{s^3+t^3}\right)^3=s^3-t^3.\]
Take $s=3$, $t=2$: $s^3-t^3=19$, $s^3+t^3=35$, $A=2\cdot27\cdot2-16=92$ and $B=81-2\cdot3\cdot8=33$. Hence
\[19=\left(\dfrac{92}{35}\right)^3+\left(\dfrac{33}{35}\right)^3,\]
a sum of cubes of two positive rationals. $\blacksquare$
