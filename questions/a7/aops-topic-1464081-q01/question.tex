Suppose $a\equiv b\pmod{x}$ and $c\equiv d\pmod{x}$, where $c$ divides $a$ and $d$ divides $b$, so that $\dfrac{a}{c}$ and $\dfrac{b}{d}$ are integers.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show by an example that $\dfrac{a}{c}\equiv\dfrac{b}{d}\pmod{x}$ need not hold.
\item[(b)] Prove that it does hold when $\gcd(c,x)=1$.
\end{enumerate}
