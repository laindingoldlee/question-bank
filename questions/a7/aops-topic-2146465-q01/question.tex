Town $B$ is $25$ km due east of town $A$. A traveller starts at $A$ and walks $20$ km due north, while a second traveller starts at $B$ and walks $50$ km due south. What is the bearing of the second traveller from the first (measured clockwise from north)? Give your answer to three decimal places.
