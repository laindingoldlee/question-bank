For a positive integer $n$, compute $2\tau(n)+3$, where $\tau(n)$ is its number of positive divisors, and let $s$ be the sum of the digits of the result. If $s$ is prime, set $f(n)=s+1$; otherwise set $f(n)=\dfrac{s}{p}$, where $p$ is the smallest prime factor of $s$. Starting at $n=1$, apply $f$ exactly $10^{12}$ times. Find the final value.
