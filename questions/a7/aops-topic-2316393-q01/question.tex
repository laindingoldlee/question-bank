Sam and Delilah are reading different books. Today each of them read one chapter of their book, and each read more than one page. They read the same number of pages, but the sum of the page numbers of the chapter Sam read was $880$, and the sum of the page numbers of the chapter Delilah read was $1008$. How many pages did Sam read today?
