A chapter of $k$ pages numbered $a,a+1,\dots,a+k-1$ has page-number sum $\dfrac{k(2a+k-1)}{2}$. So, with $k\ge2$ the common number of pages,
\[k(2a+k-1)=1760,\qquad k(2b+k-1)=2016,\]
where $a$ and $b$ are the first pages of the two chapters.

The factors $k$ and $2a+k-1$ have opposite parity. If $k$ were odd, it would be an odd common divisor of $1760=2^5\cdot5\cdot11$ and $2016=2^5\cdot3^2\cdot7$, so $k=1$, which is excluded. So $k$ is even and $2a+k-1$ is odd; hence $k$ contains the full factor $2^5$ of $1760$, and also of $2016$. Since $\gcd(1760,2016)=32$, $k=32$.

Check: $2a+31=\dfrac{1760}{32}=55$ gives $a=12$ (pages $12$ to $43$), and $2b+31=\dfrac{2016}{32}=63$ gives $b=16$ (pages $16$ to $47$). So Sam read $\boxed{32}$ pages.
