Sort the entries as $x_1\le\cdots\le x_{2023}$. Removing the two largest forces $x_{1011}=m$, and removing the two smallest forces $x_{1013}=m$. Hence all three positions 1011, 1012, 1013 equal $m$, so there are at most 2021 distinct values. Attain this with 1010 distinct values below $m$, three copies of $m$, and 1010 distinct values above $m$. After any two removals, at most 1010 values are below or above $m$, so the new median is still $m$. Thus $\boxed{2021}$.
