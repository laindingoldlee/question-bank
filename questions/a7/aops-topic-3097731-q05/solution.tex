For each $k\ge0$, the block from $2^k+1$ through $2^{k+1}$ has $2^k$ terms, each at least $2^{-(k+1)}$. Its sum is at least $\dfrac{1}{2}$. After $m$ such blocks, the partial sum is at least $1+\dfrac{m}{2}$, which is unbounded. Hence the harmonic series diverges.
