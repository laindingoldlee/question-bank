There are $\lfloor\dfrac{2023}{20}\rfloor=101$ multiples of 20 and $\lfloor\dfrac{2023}{23}\rfloor=87$ of 23. Their intersection consists of the $\lfloor\dfrac{2023}{460}\rfloor=4$ multiples of 460. Inclusion--exclusion gives $101+87-4=\boxed{184}$.
