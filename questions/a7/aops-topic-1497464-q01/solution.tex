After $d$ days the money has doubled $d$ times, so you have $5\cdot2^d$ cents. After $22$ days:
\[
5\cdot2^{22}=5\cdot4{,}194{,}304=20{,}971{,}520\text{ cents}=\boxed{\$209{,}715.20}.
\]
(The value $\$104{,}857.60=5\cdot2^{21}$ cents is the amount after $21$ doublings, i.e.\ on day $22$ if day $1$ is the day you start with $5$ cents.)
