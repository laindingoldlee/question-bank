A regular octahedron has edge length $1$. An ant starts at vertex $A$ and traverses three edges. Its first edge is chosen uniformly; on every later move it chooses uniformly among edges other than the one it just traversed. Find the probability that its final Euclidean distance from $A$ is greater than $1$.
