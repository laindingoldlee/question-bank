The digit set must include $5$: otherwise it is $\{1,3,7,9\}$ with sum $20$, inconsistent with divisibility by $3$. Divisibility by $5$ forces $Z=5$. A set containing $9$ must have digit sum divisible by $9$; among choices of two other odd digits from $\{1,3,7\}$, only $\{1,3\}$ gives sum $18$. Without $9$, the three other digits must be $1,3,7$, whose total with $5$ is $16$, again invalid. Thus the digits are $1,3,5,9$ with last digit $5$. All permutations of the first three are divisible by $1,3,5,9$ because their digit sum is $18$. To maximize $W-X+Y-5$, put the smallest remaining digit $1$ in $X$. This yields $3+9-1-5=\boxed6$, attained by $3195$ or $9135$.
