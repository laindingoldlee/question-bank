Triangle $EAT$ has legs 3, 4, so $ET=5$ and area 6. Triangle $SET$ has perpendicular legs 2 and 5, hence area 5. They lie on opposite sides of diagonal $ET$ by convexity, giving total $\boxed{11}$.
