Put $T=xy+yz+xz$. Multiplying the three equations by their denominators and rearranging gives $T=x+y=2(y+z)=2(x+z)$. The last equality yields $x=y$; the first and last then yield $z=0$. Therefore $T=x^2=2x$, giving $x=0$ or $2$. The zero case makes denominators vanish. The remaining solution is $\boxed{(x,y,z)=(2,2,0)}$, which satisfies all three original equations.
