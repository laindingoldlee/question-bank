By Heron's formula ($s=24$) the triangle has area $\sqrt{24\cdot14\cdot7\cdot3}=84$, so the height to the side $21$ is $8$.

For a rectangle with one side on a side of length $a$ with height $h$, a rectangle of height $x$ has width $a\left(1-\dfrac xh\right)$ by similar triangles, so its area $ax\left(1-\dfrac xh\right)$ is maximised at $x=\dfrac h2$, giving $\dfrac{ah}4$, half the triangle's area.

The same bound holds for every side, so the largest area is $\dfrac{84}2=\boxed{42}$.
