Factor $75600=756\cdot100=2^2\cdot3^3\cdot7\cdot2^2\cdot5^2=2^4\cdot3^3\cdot5^2\cdot7$. The product $75600B$ is a fourth power exactly when every prime exponent in it is a multiple of $4$. The least $B$ supplies $3^1$, $5^2$ and $7^3$:
\[
B=3\cdot5^2\cdot7^3=3\cdot25\cdot343=\boxed{25725},
\]
and then $75600B=2^4\cdot3^4\cdot5^4\cdot7^4=210^4$.
