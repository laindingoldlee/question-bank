Boys and girls must alternate. Seat the boys first: up to rotation, five people can be arranged around the table in $4!=24$ ways. Once the boys are seated, the rotation is used up and the five gaps between them are distinguishable; the girls fill them in $5!=120$ ways. The total is $24\cdot120=\boxed{2880}$.
