Write $s=p+q$, so $n=s(s+1)\le2025$, hence $s\le44$. If $p=q$, then $s=2p$ and every prime $p\le22$ works, giving $n=20,42,110,210,506,702,1190,1482$. If $p\ne q$, neither prime divides $p+q$, so both divide $s+1$. Consequently $pq\le p+q+1$, or $(p-1)(q-1)\le2$. Distinct primes force $\{p,q\}=\{2,3\}$, giving the additional $n=30$.
