The overall factor is $(1+\dfrac{p}{100})(1-\dfrac{p}{100})=1-\dfrac{p^2}{10000}=\dfrac{84}{100}$. Thus $p^2=1600$, and the nonnegative percentage is $\boxed{40\%}$. If clearing denominators from $\dfrac{(100+p)(100-p)}{10000}=\dfrac{84}{100}$, the right side becomes 8400, not 84.
