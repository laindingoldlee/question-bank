Inscribed angles on the same arc are equal: $\angle CAB=\angle CDB$ (both subtend arc $CB$), i.e. $\angle PAC=\angle PDB$. Also $\angle APC=\angle DPB$ (vertical angles).
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0),A=dir(150),B=dir(-20),C=dir(60),D=dir(230);pair P=extension(A,B,C,D);draw(circle(O,1));draw(A--B);draw(C--D);draw(A--C,dashed);draw(D--B,dashed);
dot(P);label("$A$",A,W);label("$B$",B,E);label("$C$",C,N);label("$D$",D,SW);label("$P$",P,S);
\end{asy}

So triangles $PAC$ and $PDB$ are similar, and $\dfrac{PA}{PD}=\dfrac{PC}{PB}$, i.e. $PA\cdot PB=PC\cdot PD$. (For a point outside the circle, the same similar-triangle argument with secants $PAB$, $PCD$ gives the same identity.) $\blacksquare$
