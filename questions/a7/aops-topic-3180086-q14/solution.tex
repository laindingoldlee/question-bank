Let $t_n$ count length-$n$ sequences ending at the target. There are $4^n$ total length-$n$ sequences, and the next pass reaches the target exactly from a non-target holder. Hence $t_{n+1}=4^n-t_n$, with $t_0=0$. This gives $t_1=1,t_2=3,t_3=13,t_4=51,t_5=205$. Therefore $\boxed{205}$.
