Let $w$ and $b$ be his numbers of white and black socks. Each day uses one sock of each colour, so after $d$ days he has used $d$ white and $d$ black socks. Hence
\[
\dfrac{1}{5}w=d=\dfrac{5}{8}b .
\]
So $w=5d$ and $b=\dfrac{8d}{5}$; since $b$ is an integer, $d$ is a multiple of $5$, say $d=5k$. Then $w=25k$ and $b=8k$, and he has used $d=5k$ white socks and $d=5k$ black socks (the same number $5k$, because both colours are used one per day). The fraction used is
\[
\dfrac{5k+5k}{25k+8k}=\boxed{\dfrac{10}{33}},
\]
which does not depend on $k$.
