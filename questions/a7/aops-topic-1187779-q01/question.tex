Bobo the clown always wears one white sock and one black sock. He changes his socks every day, and at the end of several days he finds that he has used exactly $\dfrac{1}{5}$ of his white socks and $\dfrac{5}{8}$ of his black socks. What fraction of all of his socks has he used? Explain why the numbers of used white and used black socks are the same multiple of the same number.
