Points $V,W,X,Y,Z$ lie on a circle in that order. In triangle $VWY$, the altitude from $Y$ to $VW$ bisects the altitude from $W$ to $VY$. Also $VY\parallel WX$, $VW\parallel ZY$, $\angle WXY-\angle ZVW=30^\circ$ and $VW=26$. The area of the isosceles trapezoid $VWXY$ can be written as $a\sqrt b$ with $b$ squarefree. Find $a+b$.
