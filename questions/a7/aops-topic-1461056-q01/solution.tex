Let $a=2016$ and $b=2017$, so $4033=a+b$ and $b-a=1$. Combining fractions,
\[
\dfrac{1}{b-a}-\dfrac{1}{b+a}=\dfrac{2a}{b^2-a^2},\qquad
\dfrac{2a}{b^2-a^2}-\dfrac{2a}{b^2+a^2}=\dfrac{4a^3}{b^4-a^4},\qquad
\dfrac{4a^3}{b^4-a^4}-\dfrac{4a^3}{b^4+a^4}=\dfrac{8a^7}{b^8-a^8}.
\]
Adding these three equations, the middle terms cancel:
\[
\dfrac{1}{b-a}=\dfrac{1}{a+b}+\dfrac{2a}{a^2+b^2}+\dfrac{4a^3}{a^4+b^4}+\dfrac{8a^7}{b^8-a^8}.
\]
The last term equals $-\dfrac{8a^7}{a^8-b^8}$, so the given expression is exactly the right-hand side, and its value is $\dfrac{1}{b-a}=\dfrac{1}{1}=\boxed{1}$.
