There are $\binom94=126$ paths. A path through $(2,3)$ is composed of $\binom52=10$ initial choices and $\binom42=6$ final choices. Thus the probability is $\dfrac{60}{126}=\dfrac{10}{21}$, giving $\boxed{31}$.
