If the multiplicities are $c_1,\ldots,c_{10}$, the permutation count is $\dfrac{83!}{c_1!\cdots c_{10}!}$. The prime $83$ divides the numerator exactly once. Unless some $c_i=83$, every denominator factorial is free of that prime, so the quotient is divisible by $83$. Thus all ratings coincide. Their gcd equals that common rating, so it must be $1$. The only total is $83$, and the requested sum is $\boxed{83}$.
