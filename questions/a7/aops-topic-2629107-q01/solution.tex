After the first term, the denominators are $1\cdot3,\ 3\cdot5,\ 5\cdot7,\dots,19\cdot21$, and
\[\dfrac1{(2k-1)(2k+1)}=\dfrac12\left(\dfrac1{2k-1}-\dfrac1{2k+1}\right).\]
So
\[\sum_{k=1}^{10}\dfrac1{(2k-1)(2k+1)}=\dfrac12\left(1-\dfrac1{21}\right)=\dfrac{10}{21},\]
and the whole sum is $1+\dfrac{10}{21}=\boxed{\dfrac{31}{21}}$.
