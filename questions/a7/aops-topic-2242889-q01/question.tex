Emma arranges all of her square unit tiles into a rectangle. (For example, a $5$ by $7$ rectangle uses $35$ tiles and is considered the same as a $7$ by $5$ rectangle.) Emma can form exactly ten different such rectangles, each using all of her tiles. What is the least number of tiles Emma could have?
