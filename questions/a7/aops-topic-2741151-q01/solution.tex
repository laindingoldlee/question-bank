Every integer is $6k$, $6k\pm1$, $6k\pm2$ or $6k+3$. Numbers of the form $6k$ and $6k\pm2$ are even, and those of the form $6k+3$ are divisible by $3$. A prime $p>3$ is neither, so $p=6k\pm1$, with $k\ge1$ since $p\ge5$. $\blacksquare$ (The converse is false: $25=6\cdot4+1$.)
