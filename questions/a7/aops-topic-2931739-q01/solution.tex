Write $k=d\cdot R$ with $1\le d\le9$ and $R=\underbrace{11\ldots1}_{99}$. Since $10\equiv-1\pmod{11}$, $R\equiv\sum_{i=0}^{98}(-1)^i=1\pmod{11}$, so $11\nmid R$, and $11\nmid d$. As $1001=7\cdot11\cdot13$, the prime $11$ divides $N$ exactly once. So $N$ is $\boxed{\text{never a perfect square}}$.
