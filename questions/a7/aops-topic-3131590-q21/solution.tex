Draw an arrow from each eliminated player to their eliminator. Elimination times strictly increase along arrows, so there are no cycles among these four arrows, and every chain ends at the survivor. They therefore form a labelled tree rooted at that survivor. Conversely every rooted labelled tree is attainable by successively eliminating leaves, with each parent taking the child's paper. The final distribution determines this tree uniquely. There are $5^{5-2}=125$ labelled trees by the Pr\"ufer-sequence bijection and five choices of root, giving $\boxed{625}$.
