Five labelled players each start with a paper bearing their own name taped to their back. An active player eliminates another active player by removing that paper. Eliminated players take no further turns, but keep the papers they have already removed. The sole survivor removes their own paper last. Count the distinct final distributions of papers among the five players, ignoring elimination order.
