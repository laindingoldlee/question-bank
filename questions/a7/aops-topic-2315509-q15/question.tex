In triangle $ABC$ with side lengths $13$, $14$ and $15$, it is given that $AB=14$. The incircle of triangle $ABC$ touches $AB$ at $E$. Let $H$ be the reflection of $E$ over the incentre $I$. The midpoint of side $AB$ is $F$, and line $CF$ meets $EH$ at $G$. Compute the ratio $\dfrac{HG}{GE}$.
