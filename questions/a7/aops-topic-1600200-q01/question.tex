A rectangle is inscribed in a triangle with sides $13$, $14$ and $15$ so that one side of the rectangle lies along the side of length $14$ and the other two vertices lie on the other two sides. Find the largest possible area of the rectangle, and show that in general the largest such rectangle has half the area of the triangle.
