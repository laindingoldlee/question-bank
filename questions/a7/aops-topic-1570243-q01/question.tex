There are $n$ points in a plane. Each pair of them is joined by a line (extended indefinitely in both directions). Suppose no two of these lines are parallel or coincide, and no three of them pass through the same point, except at the $n$ given points. Find the number of points of intersection of the lines other than the $n$ given points.
