The dimensions are $78,48,48$ inches. A small cube of edge $s$ satisfies $6s^2=24$, so $s=2$ inches. An aligned arrangement fits $39\cdot24\cdot24=\boxed{22464}$ cubes. This also equals the volume ratio, so no larger number can fit.
