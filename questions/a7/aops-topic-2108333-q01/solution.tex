Let the mother and daughter now be $m$ and $d$ years old, and let $k=m-d$ be their age difference, which is the mother's age when her daughter was born.

The mother was $d$ years old $k$ years ago; the daughter was then $d-k$, so $d-k=10$.

The daughter will be $m$ years old in $k$ years; the mother will then be $m+k$, so $m+k=88$, that is, $d+2k=88$.

Subtracting the equations gives $3k=78$, so $k=26$, $d=36$ and $m=62$.

\textbf{(a)} The mother was $\boxed{26}$.

\textbf{(b)} The mother is $\boxed{62}$ and the daughter is $\boxed{36}$.

Check: $26$ years ago the mother was $36$ and the daughter $10$; in $26$ years the daughter will be $62$ and the mother $88$.
