A mother says to her daughter: ``When I was your age, you were $10$. When you are my age, I will be $88$.''
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] How old was the mother when her daughter was born?
\item[(b)] How old are the mother and the daughter now?
\end{enumerate}
