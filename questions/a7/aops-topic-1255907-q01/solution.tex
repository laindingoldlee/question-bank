Let the number be $n$ with $10\le n\le99$. Then $n+90=100+(n-10)$, where $0\le n-10\le89$. So $n+90$ is a three-digit number whose first digit is $1$ and whose last two digits form $n-10$ (with a leading zero if $n-10<10$). Crossing out the $1$ leaves $n-10$, and adding $\sqrt[3]{1000}=10$ gives back $n$. $\blacksquare$
