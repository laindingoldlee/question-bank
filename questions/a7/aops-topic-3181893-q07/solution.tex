There are 400 total pairs. The excluded count is $\sum_{a=1}^{15}\lfloor\dfrac{15}{a}\rfloor=15+7+5+3+3+2+2+8=45$, where the last 8 accounts for $a=8,\ldots,15$. Therefore $\boxed{355}$.
