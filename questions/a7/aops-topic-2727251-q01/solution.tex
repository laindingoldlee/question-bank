The equation $a+b+c+abc=100a+10b+c$ becomes $abc=99a+9b$, i.e. $a(bc-99)=9b$. Since $bc\le81<99$, the left side is negative (as $a\ge1$) while the right side is nonnegative. So there are \textbf{no} such numbers.
