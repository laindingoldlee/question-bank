Photographers often choose a shutter speed, in seconds, equal to the reciprocal of the effective focal length of the lens in millimeters. Jackie shoots at a focal length of $80$ mm, Clarise at $200$ mm and Sage at $400$ mm, and they all apply this reciprocal rule. What focal length corresponds to the sum of their shutter speeds?
