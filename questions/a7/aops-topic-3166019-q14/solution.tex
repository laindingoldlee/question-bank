Because $260=2^2\cdot5\cdot13$, all such $n$ have form $65k^2$ with $k\ge1$. They increase with $k$, so the desired one is $65\cdot2020^2$. Since $2020=2^2\cdot5\cdot101$, its factorization is $2^4\cdot5^3\cdot13\cdot101^2$. The requested sum is $2+5+13+101+4+3+1+2=\boxed{131}$.
