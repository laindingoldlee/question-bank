Two triangles are placed in the plane so that they overlap, and together they cover a region bounded by a polygon (the outline of their union).
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that this polygon can have $10$ sides, by giving an example.
\item[(b)] Prove that it can never have more than $12$ sides.
\end{enumerate}
