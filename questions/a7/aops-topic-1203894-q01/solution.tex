Write $n=2^km$ with $m$ odd. The odd divisors are the divisors of $m$, $d(m)$ of them, and the even divisors number $k\,d(m)$. These are equal exactly when $k=1$, i.e. $n\equiv2\pmod4$. Such $n\le100$ are $2,6,10,\dots,98$: $\boxed{25}$ numbers.
