$a$ divides both $42$ and $30$, so $a\mid6$; it is even because $\gcd(a,b)=2$. Since $3\mid b$, $a$ cannot be $6$, hence $a=2$. Now $ab=\gcd(a,b)\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=60$, so $b=30$. To have $\operatorname{lcm}(2,c)=42$, $c$ is $21$ or $42$; the condition $\gcd(30,c)=3$ selects $21$. Therefore $abc=2\cdot30\cdot21=\boxed{1260}$.
