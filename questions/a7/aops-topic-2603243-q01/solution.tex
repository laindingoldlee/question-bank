Since $\csc^2\theta-\cot^2\theta=1$,
\[\left(\csc\theta+\cot\theta\right)\left(\csc\theta-\cot\theta\right)=1,\]
so $\csc\theta-\cot\theta=\boxed{2}$.

Such a $\theta$ exists: adding and subtracting the two values gives $\csc\theta=\dfrac54$ and $\cot\theta=-\dfrac34$, i.e. $\sin\theta=\dfrac45$ and $\cos\theta=\cot\theta\sin\theta=-\dfrac35$, an angle in the second quadrant.

pair P=(-0.6,0.8);draw((0,0)--P,linewidth(0.8));draw(P--(-0.6,0),dashed);
draw(arc((0,0),0.25,0,degrees(P)),Arrow(3));
dot(P);label("$\left(-\dfrac35,\dfrac45\right)$",P,NW,fontsize(8pt));label("$\theta$",(0.18,0.2),NE,fontsize(8pt));
label("$1$",P/2,NE,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}
