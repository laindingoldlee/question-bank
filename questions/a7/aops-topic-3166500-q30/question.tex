For positive reals define $f(x,y)=\dfrac{x}{y}+\dfrac{y}{x}$. Suppose $f(a,b)=7$, $f(b,c)=5$, $f(c,d)=3$, and $f(d,e)=2$. Find the sum of all distinct possible values of $f(e,a)$.
