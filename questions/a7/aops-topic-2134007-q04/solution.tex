Let the triangle be $ABC$ with $AB=13$, $AC=12$, $BC=14$, and let $I$ be the centre and $r$ the radius of the inscribed circle.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real ax=221/28; pair B=(0,0), C=(14,0), A=(ax,sqrt(169-ax^2));
pair I=(14*A+12*B+13*C)/39; real r=I.y;
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(circle(I,r),blue+linewidth(0.9));
pair F=(I.x,0);
draw(I--F,red+linewidth(0.9));
draw(F+(0,0.6)--F+(0.6,0.6)--F+(0.6,0),red);
dot(A); dot(B); dot(C); dot(I);
label("$A$",A,N); label("$B$",B,SW); label("$C$",C,SE); label("$I$",I,NW);
label("$13$",(A+B)/2,NW); label("$12$",(A+C)/2,NE); label("$14$",(B+C)/2+(-2.5,0),S);
label("$r$",(I+F)/2,W,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The semiperimeter is $s=\dfrac{12+13+14}{2}=\dfrac{39}{2}$, so by Heron's formula the area is
\[K=\sqrt{\dfrac{39}{2}\cdot\dfrac{15}{2}\cdot\dfrac{13}{2}\cdot\dfrac{11}{2}}=\dfrac{\sqrt{3^2\cdot5\cdot11\cdot13^2}}{4}=\dfrac{39\sqrt{55}}{4}.\]
Joining $I$ to the vertices splits the triangle into three triangles with heights $r$ on the three sides, so $K=\dfrac {r}{2}(AB+BC+CA)=rs$. Hence
\[r=\dfrac {K}{s}=\dfrac{39\sqrt{55}}{4}\cdot\dfrac{2}{39}=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt{55}}{2}}\approx3.71 .\]
