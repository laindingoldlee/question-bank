Let $x$ be the age in question, so in the year $x^2$ he was $x$ years old and was born in $x^2-x$. The year $x^2$ was no later than $1864$, so $x\le43$ (as $44^2=1936$). Also $x$ cannot be much smaller than $43$: for $x=42$ he would have been born in $1764-42=1722$ and would be $142$ years old in $1864$, which is impossible, and smaller $x$ is worse still. So $x=43$, the year was $43^2=1849$, and De Morgan was born in
\[1849-43=\boxed{1806},\]
making him $58$ in $1864$.
