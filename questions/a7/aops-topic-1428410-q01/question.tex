Let $A$ and $B$ be sets and let $g:B\to A$ be a function.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that there is at most one function $f:A\to B$ whose inverse is $g$, that is, with $g(f(a))=a$ for all $a\in A$ and $f(g(b))=b$ for all $b\in B$.
\item[(b)] Show that such an $f$ exists if and only if $g$ is a bijection, and that then $f=g^{-1}$.
\end{enumerate}
