Let $x$ liters be the $75\%$ solution. Then $0.75x+0.15(3-x)=0.40\cdot3$, so $0.60x=0.75$ and $x=1.25$. Therefore use $\boxed{1.75\text{ L at }15\%,\ 1.25\text{ L at }75\%}$.
