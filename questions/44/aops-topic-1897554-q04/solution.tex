If a divisor $d$ of $1002036$ were a multiple of $5$, then $5\mid d$ and $d\mid1002036$ would give $5\mid1002036$. But $1002036$ ends in $6$, so it is not a multiple of $5$. Hence the answer is $\boxed{0}$. (In fact $1002036=2^2\cdot3\cdot7\cdot79\cdot151$ has $48$ divisors, none of them a multiple of $5$.)
