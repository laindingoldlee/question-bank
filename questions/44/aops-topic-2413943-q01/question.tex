A parabola $y=ax^2+bx+c$ (with $a\ne0$) meets the $x$-axis at $x=p$ and $x=q$ and the $y$-axis at $y=k$, where $p$, $q$ and $k$ are nonzero numbers.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the equation of the parabola in terms of $p$, $q$ and $k$.
\item[(b)] Find the equation of the parabola with $x$-intercepts $-1$ and $3$ and $y$-intercept $-6$.
\end{enumerate}
