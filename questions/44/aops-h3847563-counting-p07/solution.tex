Let $H_i,T_j$ be expected additional tosses when the current terminal run is $i$ heads or $j$ tails, for $i=1,2$ and $j=1,2,3$. First-step conditioning gives $H_2=1+\dfrac{1}{2}T_1$, $H_1=1+\dfrac{1}{2}H_2+\dfrac{1}{2}T_1$, $T_3=1+\dfrac{1}{2}H_1$, $T_2=1+\dfrac{1}{2}H_1+\dfrac{1}{2}T_3$, and $T_1=1+\dfrac{1}{2}H_1+\dfrac{1}{2}T_2$. Solving yields $H_1=\dfrac{90}{11}$ and $T_1=\dfrac{98}{11}$. From the initial empty run, $E=1+\dfrac{1}{2}H_1+\dfrac{1}{2}T_1=\boxed{\dfrac{105}{11}}$.
