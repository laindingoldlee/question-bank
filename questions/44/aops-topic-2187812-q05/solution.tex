Factor out $x$:
\[2x^3-x^2+x=x\left(2x^2-x+1\right).\]
The quadratic $2x^2-x+1$ has discriminant $(-1)^2-4\cdot2\cdot1=-7<0$, so it has no real roots at all, and in particular no rational ones. Hence the only rational root (indeed the only real root) is $\boxed{x=0}$.
