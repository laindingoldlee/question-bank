Five boxes always suffice. Use a next-fit procedure: put packages into the current box until the next one does not fit, then open another box. Every closed box has weight greater than $20$ kg, because the rejected package weighs less than $10$ kg. If six boxes were needed, the first five closed boxes would already weigh more than $100$ kg, impossible.
Four boxes need not suffice: take thirteen packages of weight $\dfrac{100}{13}$ kg. Each is under $10$ kg, but four together weigh $\dfrac{400}{13}>30$ kg, so a box holds at most three packages. Thirteen packages then require five boxes. Therefore the guarantee is $\boxed5$.
