With $a$ rooms at $\$180$ and $b$ at $\$125$: $a+b=160$ and $180a+125b=24125$. Then $55a=24125-20000=4125$, so $a=75$ and $b=85$: $\boxed{75\text{ at }\$180,\ 85\text{ at }\$125}$.
