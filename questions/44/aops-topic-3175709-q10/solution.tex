The runner-up is the best of the eight players in the half opposite rank one. Those eight are a uniform subset of the other fifteen. Subtract one from their ranks to obtain a subset of $\{1,\ldots,15\}$. Its nine gaps, including the two end gaps, are exchangeable and sum to seven missing positions, so the expected minimum is $1+\dfrac{7}{9}=\dfrac{16}{9}$. Restore one to the rank to get $\dfrac{25}{9}$; hence $\boxed{m+n=34}$.
