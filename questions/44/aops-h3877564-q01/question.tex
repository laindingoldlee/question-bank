For positive integers $x,y$, let $S_{x,y}=\{ax+by:a,b\text{ are nonnegative integers},\ a+b>0\}$. Let $f(x,y)$ be its natural density among positive integers. If $i$ is uniform on $\{1,\ldots,144\}$ and $\mathbb E[f(i,144)]=\dfrac{m}{n}$ in lowest terms, find the sum of the distinct prime factors of $mn$.
