Let $g=\gcd(x,y)$. Every represented number is divisible by $g$, and every sufficiently large multiple of $g$ is represented: after dividing by $g$, multiples of $\dfrac{x}{g}$ give all residue classes modulo $\dfrac{y}{g}$, and adding enough copies of $\dfrac{y}{g}$ reaches every sufficiently large integer in each class. Thus $f(x,y)=\dfrac{1}{g}$.

Grouping the choices of $i$ by $d=\gcd(i,144)$ gives
\[
E=\dfrac{1}{144}\sum_{d\mid144}\dfrac{\phi(\dfrac{144}{d})}{d}
=\dfrac{1}{144^2}\sum_{r\mid144}r\phi(r).
\]
Since $144=2^4 3^2$, multiplicativity yields
\[
E=\dfrac{(1+2+8+32+128)(1+6+54)}{20736}
=\dfrac{1159}{2304}=\dfrac{19\cdot61}{2^8 3^2}.
\]
The requested sum is $\boxed{2+3+19+61=85}$.
