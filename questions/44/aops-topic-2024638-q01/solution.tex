A collection is determined by how many times each of the six faces appears: nonnegative integers $c_1,\dots,c_6$ with $c_1+\cdots+c_6=4$. By stars and bars the number of solutions is
\[
\dbinom{4+6-1}{6-1}=\dbinom95=\boxed{126}.
\]
