The difference is the tail starting at 43. Since $\dfrac{1}{n(n+1)}=\dfrac{1}{n}-\dfrac{1}{n+1}$, the partial tail through $N$ is $2021(\dfrac{1}{43}-\dfrac{1}{N+1})$. Letting $N\to\infty$ gives $\dfrac{2021}{43}=\boxed{47}$.
