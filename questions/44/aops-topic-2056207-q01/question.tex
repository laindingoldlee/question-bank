Six sprinters run a 100-meter dash. Ties are allowed in the final standings; for example, one possible order of finish is: Runner $6$ wins, Runners $2$ and $5$ tie for second, and Runners $1$, $3$, $4$ tie for last. How many different finishing orders are possible?
