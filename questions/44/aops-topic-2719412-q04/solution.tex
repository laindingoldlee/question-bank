Multiply the three equations. Expanding,
\[\left(x+\dfrac1y\right)\left(y+\dfrac1z\right)\left(z+\dfrac1x\right)=xyz+\dfrac1{xyz}+\left(x+\dfrac1y\right)+\left(y+\dfrac1z\right)+\left(z+\dfrac1x\right).\]
With $t=xyz$: $4\cdot1\cdot\dfrac73=t+\dfrac1t+4+1+\dfrac73$, so $t+\dfrac1t=2$ and $t=\boxed{1}$. (Indeed $x=\dfrac32$, $y=\dfrac25$, $z=\dfrac53$.)
