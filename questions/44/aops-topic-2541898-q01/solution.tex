$840=2^3\cdot3\cdot5\cdot7$ has $(3+1)(1+1)(1+1)(1+1)=32$ positive factors, of which exactly $4$ (namely $2,3,5,7$) are prime. The probability is $\dfrac4{32}=\boxed{\dfrac18}$.
