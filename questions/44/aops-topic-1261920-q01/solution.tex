Multiply two of the equations and divide by the third:
\[a^2=\dfrac{(ab)(ac)}{bc}=\dfrac{40}{5}=8,\quad b^2=\dfrac{(ab)(bc)}{ac}=\dfrac{20}{10}=2,\quad c^2=\dfrac{(bc)(ac)}{ab}=\dfrac{50}{4}=\dfrac{25}{2}.\]
So $a^2+b^2+c^2=8+2+\dfrac{25}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{45}{2}}$.

The attempted solution added $ab+bc+ac$ and wrote the result as $2a+2b+2c$; but $ab+bc+ac$ is a sum of \emph{products}, not $2(a+b+c)$, so that step is invalid.
