Let $S=\overline{ab}+\overline{ac}$, where $\overline{ab}$ and $\overline{ac}$ are distinct two-digit prime numbers (with the same tens digit $a$) such that $10\mid\overline{ab}^{\,2}+\overline{ac}^{\,2}$. Determine the minimum and the maximum value of $S$.
