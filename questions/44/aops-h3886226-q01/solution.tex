Replace every $C$ by $AB$. The resulting relations on $A,B$ are $AA=A$, $BB=B$, and $ABAB=AB$. Orient these three rules toward shorter words. Every reduction terminates. The only overlapping reductions to check occur in
\[
AAA,\quad BBB,\quad AABAB,\quad ABABB,\quad ABABAB.
\]
In each case either initial reduction leads, after further reductions, to the same word. Disjoint reductions commute. It follows by induction on word length that the final irreducible word is independent of the reduction choices.

An irreducible word alternates letters and contains no $ABAB$. The complete list is
\[
\text{empty},\ A,\ B,\ AB,\ BA,\ ABA,\ BAB,\ BABA.
\]
Each relation preserves its unique reduced word, so these eight forms are inequivalent and exhaust the classes. The answer is $\boxed8$.

The empty-string class is a singleton; every nonempty class is infinite because a letter can be duplicated arbitrarily often.
