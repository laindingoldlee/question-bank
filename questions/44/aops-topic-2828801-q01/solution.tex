For $0\le t\le1$, $\dfrac1{1+t^2}=\sum_{n=0}^{N}(-1)^nt^{2n}+\dfrac{(-1)^{N+1}t^{2N+2}}{1+t^2}$. Integrating from $0$ to $1$:
\[\dfrac\pi4=\sum_{n=0}^{N}\dfrac{(-1)^n}{2n+1}+R_N,\qquad|R_N|\le\int_0^1t^{2N+2}\,dt=\dfrac1{2N+3}\to0.\]
So $\sum_{n\ge0}\dfrac{(-1)^n}{2n+1}=\dfrac\pi4$ (Leibniz), and the given sum is $8\cdot\dfrac\pi4=\boxed{2\pi}$.
