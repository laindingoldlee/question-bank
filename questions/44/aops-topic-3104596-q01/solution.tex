The two legs are $ 8 $, so the triangle area is $\dfrac{8\cdot8}{2}=32 $. Its inradius is $\dfrac{8+8-8\sqrt2}{2}=8-4\sqrt2 $. Subtracting the disk area gives $\boxed{32-\pi(8-4\sqrt2)^2}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(8,0),C=(0,8);real r=8-4*sqrt(2);filldraw(A--B--C--cycle,lightgray);filldraw(circle((r,r),r),white);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NW);label("$8\sqrt2$",(4,4),NE);label("$I$",(r,r));
\end{asy}
