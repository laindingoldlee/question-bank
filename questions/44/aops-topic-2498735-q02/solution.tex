Let the legs be $AD=BC=L$.

In $APQR\sim ABCD$ with ratio $k=\dfrac{AP}{AB}$, the side $PQ$ corresponds to $BC$, so $PQ=kL$.

In $BCQP\sim ABCD$, the side $BC$ corresponds to $AB$, so the ratio is $m=\dfrac{BC}{AB}=\dfrac{L}{10}$. Then $QP$ corresponds to $CD$ and $PB$ corresponds to $DA$:
\[QP=8m=\dfrac{8L}{10},\qquad PB=mL=\dfrac{L^2}{10}.\]
Comparing the two expressions for $PQ$: $kL=\dfrac{8L}{10}$, so $k=\dfrac45$ and $AP=8$. Hence $PB=10-8=2$, so $\dfrac{L^2}{10}=2$ and $L^2=20$.

The legs of $ABCD$ overhang the shorter base by $\dfrac{10-8}{2}=1$ on each side, so the height is $\sqrt{L^2-1}=\sqrt{19}$, and the area is
\[\dfrac{10+8}{2}\cdot\sqrt{19}=\boxed{9\sqrt{19}}.\]
\emph{Check.} With $A=(0,0)$, $B=(10,0)$, $C=\left(9,\sqrt{19}\right)$, $D=\left(1,\sqrt{19}\right)$, take $P=(8,0)$, $Q=\left(7.2,\,0.8\sqrt{19}\right)$, $R=\left(0.8,\,0.8\sqrt{19}\right)$. Then $APQR$ is $ABCD$ scaled by $0.8$ from $A$, and $BCQP$ has parallel sides $BC=\sqrt{20}$ and $QP=0.8\sqrt{20}$ and legs $CQ=PB=2$, which is $ABCD$ scaled by $\dfrac{\sqrt{20}}{10}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real h=sqrt(19);
pair A=(0,0), B=(10,0), C=(9,h), D=(1,h);
pair P=(8,0), Q=(7.2,0.8*h), R=(0.8,0.8*h);
fill(A--P--Q--R--cycle,rgb(0.85,0.9,1)); fill(B--C--Q--P--cycle,rgb(1,0.88,0.85));
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(P--Q--R); draw(Q--C);
dot(P);dot(Q);dot(R);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$P$",P,S);label("$Q$",Q,N);label("$R$",R,W);
label("$8$",(A+P)/2,S,fontsize(8pt)); label("$2$",(P+B)/2,S,fontsize(8pt)); label("$8$",(D+C)/2,N,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}
