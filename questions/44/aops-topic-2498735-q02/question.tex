In isosceles trapezoid $ABCD$, sides $AB$ and $DC$ are parallel, $AB=10$ and $CD=8$. Point $P$ lies on side $AB$, point $R$ on side $AD$, and point $Q$ inside the trapezoid, such that trapezoids $APQR$ and $BCQP$ are both similar to trapezoid $ABCD$, with vertices corresponding in the order listed ($A,P,Q,R$ and $B,C,Q,P$ correspond to $A,B,C,D$). What is the area of trapezoid $ABCD$? Express your answer in simplest radical form.
