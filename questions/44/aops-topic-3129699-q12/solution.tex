The exponent of 7 in $100!$ is $\lfloor\dfrac{100}{7}\rfloor+\lfloor\dfrac{100}{49}\rfloor=14+2=16$. Since 49 is $7^2$, the number of trailing zeros is $\lfloor\dfrac{16}{2}\rfloor=\boxed8$.
