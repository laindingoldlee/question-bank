Since $LN\parallel AC$ and $CL$ bisects angle C, triangle $CLN$ has equal angles at C and L, so $CN=LN=24$. Similarly, $AM$ bisects angle A and $KM\parallel AC$, so triangle $AKM$ is isosceles and $AK=KM=24$. The parallel cut $KN$ gives $\dfrac{AK}{AB}=\dfrac{CN}{CB}$, hence $AB=BC$. With angle B equal to 60 degrees, triangle ABC is equilateral. Triangle BKN is also equilateral, so $BK=KN=36$. Consequently $AC=AB=AK+BK=24+36=\boxed{60}$.
