Let $b<c$ be positive integers. Prove that the largest integer $a$ with $\left\lfloor\dfrac ab\right\rfloor=\left\lfloor\dfrac ac\right\rfloor$ is
\[a=b\left\lfloor\dfrac{c-1}{c-b}\right\rfloor-1.\]
