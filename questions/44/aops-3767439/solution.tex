For $k=1$ the group is trivial, so $f(1)=1$. For $k=2025$ only the whole-deck cyclic shift is possible, giving $f(2025)=2025$. For $2\le k<2025$, consecutive $k$-cycles generate the alternating group when $k$ is odd and the symmetric group when $k$ is even. To see the key step, the product of the cycle on positions $i,\ldots,i+k-1$ and the inverse of the next cycle is the 3-cycle $(i,i+1,i+k)$. Conjugating these by the available cycles generates all 3-cycles, hence the alternating group; an even-length cycle is an odd permutation, giving the full symmetric group in that case. Therefore the four distinct values are $1 , 2025 , \dfrac{2025 !}{2} , 2025 !$. Both factorial expressions are divisible by 1000, so the required remainder is $\boxed{26}$.
