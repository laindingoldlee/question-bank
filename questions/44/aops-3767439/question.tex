You have a deck of $2025$ cards. For some fixed $1 \leq k \leq 2025$, let $f(k)$ be the number of possible arrangements of the $2025$ cards you can achieve via shuffling this deck by performing some finite number of $k$-swaps, where a $k$-swap consists of taking $k$ cards at consecutive positions in the deck and placing the card that was originally the bottommost card among those $k$ cards on the top of the $k-1$ other cards. Let $S$ be the sum of all distinct possible values of $f(k)$ over the range $1 \leq k \leq 2025.$ Find the remainder when $S$ is divided by $1000$.
