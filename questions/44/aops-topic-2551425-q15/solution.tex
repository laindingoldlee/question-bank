The last digits of powers of $7$ cycle $7,9,3,1$ with period $4$, and $2401\equiv1\pmod4$, so $7^{2401}$ ends in $7$. The last digits of powers of $2$ cycle $2,4,8,6$, and $8192\equiv0\pmod4$, so $2^{8192}$ ends in $6$. The sum is $7+6=\boxed{13}$, answer $\textbf{(E)}$.
