A cow stands on vertex $A$ of trapezoid $ABCD$. Each minute, the cow moves to one of the two vertices adjacent to its current vertex (along a side of the trapezoid), each with probability $\dfrac12$. What is the probability that after $1001$ minutes the cow is on vertex $D$?

$\textbf{(A) }\dfrac14\qquad\textbf{(B) }\dfrac27\qquad\textbf{(C) }\dfrac13\qquad\textbf{(D) }\dfrac25\qquad\textbf{(E) }\dfrac12$
