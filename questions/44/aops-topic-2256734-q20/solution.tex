The vertices form a cycle $A-B-C-D-A$. Colour $A$ and $C$ white and $B$ and $D$ black; every move changes the colour, so after an odd number of moves, such as $1001$, the cow is on $B$ or $D$. Reflecting the cycle in the line through $A$ and $C$ swaps $B$ and $D$ and maps every walk from $A$ to a walk from $A$ with the same probability, so the cow is on $B$ and on $D$ with equal probabilities. Hence the probability of $D$ is $\boxed{\textbf{(E) }\dfrac12}$.
