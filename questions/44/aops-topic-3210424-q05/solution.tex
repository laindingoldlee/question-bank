On a left page Iris finishes at minute 4; Ivy has $\dfrac{1}{5}$ of the page left, taking $\dfrac{1}{3}$ extra minute at triple speed. Thus each left page takes $\dfrac{13}{3}$ minutes. On a right page Ivy finishes at minute 3; Iris has $\dfrac{1}{4}$ left, again taking $\dfrac{1}{3}$ minute, for $\dfrac{10}{3}$ total. Hence total time is $120(\dfrac{13}{3}+\dfrac{10}{3})=\boxed{920\text{ minutes}}$.
