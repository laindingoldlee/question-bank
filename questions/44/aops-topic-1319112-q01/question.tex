On the $120$-mile drive from St. Louis to Columbia, a driver averaged $64$ miles per hour from St. Louis to Wentzville and then $74$ miles per hour from Wentzville to Columbia. Wentzville is one third of the way from St. Louis to Columbia. What was the driver's average speed for the whole drive? Round to the nearest tenth of a mile per hour.
