The first leg is $40$ miles and takes $\dfrac{40}{64}=\dfrac{5}{8}$ hour; the second leg is $80$ miles and takes $\dfrac{80}{74}=\dfrac{40}{37}$ hours. The total time is
\[
\dfrac{5}{8}+\dfrac{40}{37}=\dfrac{185+320}{296}=\dfrac{505}{296}\text{ hours},
\]
so the average speed is
\[
\dfrac{120}{\dfrac{505}{296}}=\dfrac{120\cdot296}{505}=\dfrac{7104}{101}\approx70.34,
\]
i.e. about $\boxed{70.3}$ miles per hour. (Not the average of $64$ and $74$: the average speed is total distance over total time.)
