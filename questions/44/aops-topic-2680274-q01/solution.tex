Let the number be $k^2$ with $k\ge1$. Since $3\mid k^2$, $3\mid k$, and since $\dfrac{k^2}3$ must be odd, $k$ is odd. So $k$ is an odd multiple of $3$ with $k^2<1000$, i.e. $k\le31$: $k=3,9,15,21,27$. The numbers are $9,81,225,441,729$ (each is $3$ times $3,27,75,147,243$, all odd). There are $\boxed{5}$.
