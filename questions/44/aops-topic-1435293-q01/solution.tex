Let a null last $u$ seconds and a nill $v$ seconds. Then
\[
3u+18v=60,\qquad 7u+2v=120 .
\]
Multiply the second equation by $9$: $63u+18v=1080$. Subtract the first: $60u=1020$, so $u=17$, and then $2v=120-119=1$, so $v=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore
\[
9u+6v=153+3=\boxed{156}\text{ seconds}.
\]
