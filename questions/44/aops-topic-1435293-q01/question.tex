An ancient stone uses a different system of time units, the \emph{null} and the \emph{nill}. It is known that $3$ nulls and $18$ nills together last one minute, and $7$ nulls and $2$ nills together last two minutes. How many seconds do $9$ nulls and $6$ nills last?
