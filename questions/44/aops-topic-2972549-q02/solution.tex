$n\equiv4\pmod9$ already implies $n\equiv1\pmod3$. So we need $n\equiv4\pmod 9$ and $n\equiv2\pmod5$. Checking $4,13,22$: $22\equiv2\pmod5$. By the Chinese remainder theorem the solutions are $n\equiv22\pmod{45}$, and the least is $\boxed{22}$.
