Since $21=3\cdot7$, $n^2\equiv1\pmod{21}$ means $n^2\equiv1$ modulo both $3$ and $7$. Modulo a prime $p$, $n^2-1=(n-1)(n+1)\equiv0$ forces $n\equiv\pm1$. So $n\equiv\pm1\pmod3$ and $n\equiv\pm1\pmod7$, and by the Chinese remainder theorem each of the four sign choices gives one residue modulo $21$:
\[n\equiv\boxed{1,\ 8,\ 13,\ 20}\pmod{21}.\]
