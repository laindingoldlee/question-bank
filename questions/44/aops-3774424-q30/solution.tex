Take successive edge directions $0^\circ,60^\circ,120^\circ,180^\circ,240^\circ,300^\circ$. If the final two lengths are $e,f$, closure gives $e+f=18$ and $f-e=14$, hence $e=2,f=16$. Coordinates of the vertices are $(0,0),(6,0),(10,4\sqrt3),(5,9\sqrt3),(-7,9\sqrt3),(-8,8\sqrt3)$. Shoelace gives area $\dfrac{1}{2}(254-34)\sqrt3=\boxed{110\sqrt3}$.
