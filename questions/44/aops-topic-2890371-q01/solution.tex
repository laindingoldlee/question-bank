$(1+\sqrt2)^n=\displaystyle\sum_j\dbinom nj(\sqrt2)^j$. Adding $(1-\sqrt2)^n$ cancels the odd $j$ and doubles the even ones, where $(\sqrt2)^{2k}=2^k$. So the sum is $\boxed{\dfrac{(1+\sqrt2)^n+(1-\sqrt2)^n}2}$.
