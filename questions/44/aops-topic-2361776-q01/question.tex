The lines $AB$ and $AC$ are both tangent to a circle at the same point $A$ of the circle (where $B\ne A$ and $C\ne A$). Prove that $B$, $A$ and $C$ are collinear. You may use the fact that a tangent line is perpendicular to the radius at the point of tangency.
