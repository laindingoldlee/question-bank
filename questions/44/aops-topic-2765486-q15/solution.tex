The gcd is $\gcd(n+6,(2n+6)-2(n+6))=\gcd(n,6)$. It equals 1 exactly when $n\equiv1$ or 5 modulo 6. There are 336 full blocks of six through 2016, plus 2017 in residue 1. Total $\boxed{673}$.
