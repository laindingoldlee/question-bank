$2026=2\cdot1013$. Trial division by primes at most $\sqrt{1013}<32$ shows that $1013$ is prime. The divisors are therefore $1,2,1013,2026$. Since $2!$ divides $2026$ but $3!$ does not, the largest $k$ is $2$. A factorial divisible by $1013$ must reach $m\ge1013$; at $m=1013$ it also contains a factor two. Thus the least $m$ is $1013$.
