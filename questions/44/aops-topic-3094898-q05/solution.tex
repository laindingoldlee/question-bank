For a fixed denominator $b$, the numerator has $\lfloor\dfrac{8}{b}\rfloor$ choices. Their total is $8+4+2+2+1+1+1+1=20$. There are 64 equally likely ordered pairs, so the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{5}{16}}$.
