(a) If all last digits were different, they would be $0,1,\ldots,9$. Their sum is $45$, so the total would end in $5$, contrary to $229$.

(b) Sort the counts as $a_1\le\cdots\le a_{10}$. Suppose no four work. For any sorted four positive counts, only the inequality involving the largest can fail, so
\[
a_i\ge a_{i-1}+a_{i-2}+a_{i-3}\quad(4\le i\le10).
\]
Starting from $a_1,a_2,a_3\ge1$, this gives componentwise lower bounds
\[
1,1,1,3,5,9,17,31,57,105.
\]
Their sum is $230$, contradicting the available total $229$. Therefore the required four students exist.
