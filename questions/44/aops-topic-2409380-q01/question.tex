Let $p_1<p_2<p_3<\cdots$ be the primes, and for each positive integer $n$ let $f(n)$ be the smallest composite number that is divisible by none of $p_1,\dots,p_n$. Prove that $f(n)$ is a perfect square for every $n$; in fact $f(n)=p_{n+1}^2$.
