The coefficients $1,8,28,56,70,56,28,8,1$ are the binomial coefficients $\dbinom8k$, so by the binomial theorem $f(x)=(x+1)^8$. Hence
\[f(2)-f(1)=3^8-2^8=\left(3^4-2^4\right)\left(3^4+2^4\right)=65\cdot97,\]
and
\[\dfrac{f(2)-f(1)}{65}=\boxed{97}.\]
