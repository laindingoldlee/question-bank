There are $10^4$ digit strings, including leading zeros. A triple repetition in the first three positions occurs in $100$ strings, and likewise in the last three; their overlap is the ten constant strings, so $190$ have a triple. There are $100$ strings of the form $abab$. Among them, only the ten constant strings also have a triple. Thus $190+100-10=280$ strings are forbidden, leaving $10000-280=\boxed{9720}$.
