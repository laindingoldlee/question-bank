Add $5$ and factor: $(x+1)(7y+5)=178=2\cdot89$. Since $x+1\ge2$ and $7y+5\ge12$, the only possible factor allocation is $x+1=2$, $7y+5=89$. Thus $\boxed{(x,y)=(1,12)}$, and both are non-prime under the stated definition.
