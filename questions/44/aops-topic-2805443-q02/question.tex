There are $998$ items (candies and chocolate bars) and $499$ bags, with exactly two items in each bag. Ann packed them so that exactly half of the candies share a bag with a chocolate bar. Can Helen repack the same items into the same bags so that exactly half of the chocolate bars share a bag with a candy?
