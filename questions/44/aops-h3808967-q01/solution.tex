For each prime $p$, write $e_p$ for its exponent in $n$. The conditions on the primes $2,3,5$ are
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc|c}
p&e_p\bmod2&e_p\bmod3&e_p\bmod5&e_p\bmod30\\ \hline
2&1&0&0&15\\
3&0&1&0&10\\
5&0&0&1&6
\end{array}
\]
For every other prime, the exponent is divisible by $2,3,5$, hence by $30$. The Chinese remainder theorem makes these residue conditions necessary and sufficient. Therefore
\[
\boxed{n=2^{15}3^{10}5^6t^{30}\quad(t=1,2,3,\ldots)}.
\]
The smallest value is $\boxed{2^{15}3^{10}5^6}$.
