If the integer $n$ has $d$ digits, the new value is $5\cdot10^{d+1}+10n+5$. Thus $9n+5\cdot10^{d+1}=518054$. If $d\le3$, then the left side is at most $9\cdot999+50000=58991$, too small. If $d\ge5$, the power-of-ten term alone is at least $5000000$, too large. Hence $d=4$, and $9n=18054$, giving $\boxed{n=2006}$. Indeed $520065-2006=518059$.
