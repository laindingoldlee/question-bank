Choose which die shows the $1$ ($15$ ways); it shows $1$ with probability $\dfrac{1}{6}$ and each other die avoids $1$ with probability $\dfrac{5}{6}$:
\[15\cdot\dfrac{1}{6}\cdot\left(\dfrac{5}{6}\right)^{14}=\dfrac{15\cdot5^{14}}{6^{15}}=\boxed{\dfrac{5^{15}}{2\cdot6^{14}}}\approx0.194.\]
