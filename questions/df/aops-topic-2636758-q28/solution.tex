The last three base-$4$ digits encode $7^{177}\bmod 64$. Now $7^2=49$, $7^4=2401\equiv33$ and $7^8\equiv33^2=1089\equiv1\pmod{64}$. Since $177=8\cdot22+1$, $7^{177}\equiv7\pmod{64}$, and $7=0\cdot16+1\cdot4+3$. The last three digits are $\boxed{013}$.
