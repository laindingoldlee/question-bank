$56=2^3\cdot7$. The exponent of $7$ in $99!$ is $\left\lfloor\dfrac{99}{7}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{99}{49}\right\rfloor=14+2=16$. The exponent of $2$ is $49+24+12+6+3+1=95$, allowing $\left\lfloor\dfrac{95}{3}\right\rfloor=31$ factors of $8$. The limit is the $7$s: $k=\boxed{16}$. (The poster's $13$ misses the extra factor of $7$ in each of $49$ and $98$.)
