The condition says $m$ and $n$ have exactly four digits in base $7$: $343\le m,n\le2400$. With $n=2010-m$: $n\ge343$ gives $m\le1667$, and $m>n$ gives $m\ge1006$ (both bounds within range). So $m$ runs from $1006$ to $1667$: $\boxed{662}$ values.
