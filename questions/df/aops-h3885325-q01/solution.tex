The positive denominators sum to $x$. Cauchy--Schwarz gives $\dfrac{x^2}{y-x}+\dfrac{y^2}{z-y}+\dfrac{z^2}{2x-z}\ge\dfrac{(x+y+z)^2}{x}$, and the hypothesis forces equality. Thus $\dfrac{x}{y-x}=\dfrac{y}{z-y}=\dfrac{z}{2x-z}$. The first equality gives $xz=y^2$; the second gives $2xy=z^2$. Put $r=\dfrac{y}{x}>1$. Then $\dfrac{z}{x}=r^2$ and $2r=r^4$, so $r^3=2$. Therefore $\boxed{\dfrac{y}{x}=\sqrt[3]{2}}$. Since $1<r<r^2<2$, the required inequalities are consistent.
