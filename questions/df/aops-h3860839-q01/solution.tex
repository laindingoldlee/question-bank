Since $D$ is the midpoint of $BC$, the added condition gives $DB=DC=DE$. Thus $D$ lies on the perpendicular bisector of $BE$. In the right triangle $ABE$, the midpoint of hypotenuse $AE$ is equidistant from $B,E$, and the perpendicular bisector of $BE$ meets line $AE$ at that unique midpoint. Therefore $D$ is also the midpoint of $AE$.

The diagonals $AE$ and $BC$ of quadrilateral $ABEC$ now bisect each other, so it is a parallelogram. Since $AB\perp BE$, it is a rectangle, giving $\angle BAC=90^\circ$. The median divides that angle in the ratio $1:2$, hence $\boxed{\angle BAD=30^\circ}$.
