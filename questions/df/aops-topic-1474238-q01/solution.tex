\textbf{No.} Squaring,
\[
\left(3\sqrt2+\sqrt6\right)^2=18+6+2\cdot3\sqrt{12}=24+12\sqrt3 .
\]
If $3\sqrt2+\sqrt6=k\sqrt n$, its square $k^2n$ would be rational, but $24+12\sqrt3$ is irrational because $\sqrt3$ is. So the sum cannot be combined into one radical term. The most one can do is factor it, for example $3\sqrt2+\sqrt6=\sqrt2\left(3+\sqrt3\right)=\sqrt6\left(\sqrt3+1\right)$.

Numerically, $3\sqrt2+\sqrt6\approx4.2426+2.4495=\boxed{6.69}$.
