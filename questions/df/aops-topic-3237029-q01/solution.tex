Total minutes range from 500000 to 1000000. Dividing by 60 gives $\boxed{\dfrac{25000}{3}\text{ to }\dfrac{50000}{3}\text{ hours}}$. Dividing instead by 1440 gives $\boxed{\dfrac{3125}{9}\text{ to }\dfrac{6250}{9}\text{ days}}$.
