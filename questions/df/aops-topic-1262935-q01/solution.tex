The deals cost $\$1.80$, $\$2.50$ and $\$3$ per hamburger, so use as many $5$-for-$\$9$ deals as possible. Five of them cost $\$45$ for $25$ hamburgers, and the remaining $\$3$ buys one more: $26$. With $4$ deals ($\$36$, $20$ burgers) the remaining $\$12$ buys at most $4$ more (two $2$-for-$\$5$ deals), giving only $24$; fewer deals are worse still. The maximum is $\boxed{26}$.
