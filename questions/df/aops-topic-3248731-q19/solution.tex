After $m$ moves, the reachable bottom points form a consecutive interval of length $2024-m$. At most $m$ points have been blocked, even after noon, so stopping requires $2024-m\le m$, or $m\ge1012$. This can occur: the walker always moves toward the left edge, and at the first $1012$ noons block the leftmost $1012$ bottom points, one each. After move $1012$, exactly those points are reachable, so the walker stops at that noon. Thus the sharp lower bound is $\boxed{1012}$.
