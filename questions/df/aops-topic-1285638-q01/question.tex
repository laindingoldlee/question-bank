Mr. Banana puts $1$ gram of filling inside a $5$ cm by $5$ cm square dumpling skin. Today he puts $2$ grams of filling inside each skin. Assuming the new dumplings are exactly similar in shape to the old ones (with the filling filling the same proportion of the inside), how big are the new square skins?
