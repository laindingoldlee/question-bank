If the moves occur $a,b$ times respectively, then $1+4a-2b=15$, or $b=2a-7\ge0$. Thus $a\ge4$ and the number of days is $a+b=3a-7\ge5$. The sequence $1,5,9,13,17,15$ is feasible, so $\boxed5$ days suffice and are necessary.
