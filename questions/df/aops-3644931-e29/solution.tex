An area bound requires at least $2025^2=4100625$ stickers. A triangular-lattice covering by unit disks has centre spacing $\sqrt3$ and fundamental area $\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}$, giving bulk density $\dfrac{2\pi}{(3\sqrt3)}\approx1.2092$. Thus the natural large-radius estimate is $[\dfrac{2\pi}{(3\sqrt3)}]2025^2$, about $4.96$ million stickers. A rigorous finite upper bound follows by retaining lattice centres within 2026 of the table centre: these cover the table, and their disjoint hexagonal cells lie within radius 2027. Their number is at most $\lfloor\dfrac{2\pi(2027)^2}{(3\sqrt3)}\rfloor$. Neither this construction nor the area lower bound proves the exact optimum.
