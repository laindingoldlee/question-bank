The minor segment has sector area $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$ minus triangle area $\sqrt3$, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{4\pi}{3}-\sqrt3}$. The other region has the remaining area $\boxed{\dfrac{8\pi}{3}+\sqrt3}$.
