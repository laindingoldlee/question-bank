The line $y=mx+b$ is tangent to the circle $x^2+y^2=r^2$, where $r>0$; that is, the line and the circle have exactly one common point $T$. Let $O$ be the origin. Prove that
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $r^2\left(1+m^2\right)=b^2$;
\item[(b)] $T=\left(-\dfrac{r^2m}{b},\ \dfrac{r^2}{b}\right)$;
\item[(c)] the tangent line is perpendicular to the radius $OT$.
\end{enumerate}
