Trapezoid $ABCD$ has $AB\parallel CD$. Point $P$ is on side $CD$ such that $\angle APB=90^\circ$. Given that $\angle APD=\angle ADP$ and $\angle BPC=\angle BCP$, prove that the distance between the midpoints of $AB$ and $CD$ is $\dfrac{AB}{2}$.
