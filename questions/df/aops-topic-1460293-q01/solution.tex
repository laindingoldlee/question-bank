Let the digits be $h,t,u$ with $h\in\{1,\dots,9\}$ and $h\ge3t$. Count by the second digit $t$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $t=0$: $h=1,\dots,9$, $9$ choices;
\item $t=1$: $h=3,\dots,9$, $7$ choices;
\item $t=2$: $h=6,\dots,9$, $4$ choices;
\item $t=3$: $h=9$, $1$ choice;
\item $t\ge4$: impossible, as $3t\ge12$.
\end{itemize}
That is $9+7+4+1=21$ choices of $(h,t)$, and the last digit is arbitrary ($10$ choices): $\boxed{210}$ numbers.
