We have $n^4-20n^2+4=(n^2-4n-2)(n^2+4n-2)$. For $|n|=1,2,3,4$ the values are respectively $-15,-60,-95,-60$, which are not positive composite integers. For $n=0$ the value is $4$. For $|n|\ge 5$, put $m=|n|$. Both integer factors $m^2-4m-2$ and $m^2+4m-2$ exceed $1$ (the smaller is at least $3$). Thus the expression is positive composite exactly when $n=0$ or $|n|\ge 5$. The original unrestricted claim is false.
