The area equation gives $\sin B=\dfrac{72}{90}=\dfrac{4}{5}$. As $B$ is obtuse, $\cos B=-\dfrac{3}{5}$. Thus $AC^2=100+81-180(-\dfrac{3}{5})=289$, giving $\boxed{17}$.
