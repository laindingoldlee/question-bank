The numbers $1,\ldots,6$ have residue multiplicities $(1,2,2,1)$ modulo four. Let $v_t(r)$ count sums with residue $r$, starting from $v_0=(1,0,0,0)$ and updating $v_{t+1}(r)=\sum_{j=1}^6v_t(r-j\bmod4)$. Six updates give $v_6(0)=11664$ among $46656$ outcomes. Thus the probability is $\boxed{0.250}$.
