There are $26^4$ words in all. The words with four different letters number $26\cdot25\cdot24\cdot23$. So the fraction with a repeated letter is
\[
1-\dfrac{26\cdot25\cdot24\cdot23}{26^4}=1-\dfrac{25\cdot24\cdot23}{26^3}=1-\dfrac{13800}{17576}=1-\dfrac{1725}{2197}=\boxed{\dfrac{472}{2197}}.
\]
