Let the first train travel at $v$ mph, so the second travels at $v+72$ mph. By 10:00 PM the first train has travelled for $5$ hours and the second for $2$ hours, and at that moment they have covered the same distance:
\[
5v=2(v+72)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 3v=144\quad\Longrightarrow\quad v=48 .
\]
So the first train travels at $\boxed{48\text{ mph}}$ and the second at $\boxed{120\text{ mph}}$. Check: $5\cdot48=240=2\cdot120$ miles.
