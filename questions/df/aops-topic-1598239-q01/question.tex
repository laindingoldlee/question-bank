Edna enters a room with $1000$ bottles lined up in a row from left to right. One bottle contains a tasteless magic potion. All bottles to the left of the potion contain tasteless water, and all bottles to the right of it contain a bitter poison. Edna can drink from at most two bottles of poison: as soon as she has drunk from a second poisoned bottle she becomes sick and cannot drink anything else. She may drink from any other bottles as often as she likes. What is the minimum number of bottles from which Edna may need to drink to be sure to identify the bottle containing the potion, wherever it is?
