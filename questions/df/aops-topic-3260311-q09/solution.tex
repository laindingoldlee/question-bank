The conditions modulo $6,4$ give $x\equiv8\pmod{12}$. Write $x=8+12k$. Modulo $5$ this becomes $3+2k\equiv3$, so $k\equiv0\pmod5$. Thus $x\equiv8\pmod{60}$ and the least positive solution is $\boxed8$.
