An even number must end in $2$. The other two digits are an ordered choice from $\{3,5,7\}$: $3\cdot2=6$ numbers. By symmetry each of $3,5,7$ appears equally often in the hundreds and tens places, average $5$. The mean is $500+50+2=\boxed{552}$.
