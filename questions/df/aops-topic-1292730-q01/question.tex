Polynomials $P$ and $Q$ have difference $R=P-Q$ with $R(10)=6$, $R(9)=7$, $R(8)=8$.
(a) Show that there are infinitely many such pairs $(P,Q)$.
(b) If $Q(x)=x^2+x+1$ and $P$ is quadratic, find $P$.
