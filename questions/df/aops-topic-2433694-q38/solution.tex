We have $n=100q+r=99q+(q+r)$, and $11\mid99q$. So $q+r$ is divisible by $11$ exactly when $n$ is divisible by $11$.

The $5$-digit multiples of $11$ are $11k$ with $11k\ge10000$ and $11k\le99999$, i.e. $910\le k\le9090$ (since $11\cdot909=9999$ and $11\cdot9091=100001$). There are $9090-910+1=8181$ of them: the answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(B)}\ 8181}$.
