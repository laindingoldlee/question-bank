By the difference of squares, $(f+g)^2-(f-g)^2=\bigl((f+g)+(f-g)\bigr)\bigl((f+g)-(f-g)\bigr)=4fg$. So the equation is $f(x)g(x)=0$. Now $f(x)=(x-1)(x-5)$ and $g(x)=(x-3)(x-4)$, so the solutions are $x=1,3,4,5$, and their sum is $\boxed{13}$.
