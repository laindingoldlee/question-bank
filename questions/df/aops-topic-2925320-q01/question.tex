A chess player wins a game with probability $0.8$ if it finishes before class, and each game (independently) runs into class time with probability $0.4$, in which case he loses. Games are independent. Find the probability that he wins (a) $1$ of $1$ game; (b) at least $2$ of $3$; (c) at least $7$ of $10$; (d) at least $k$ of $n$.
