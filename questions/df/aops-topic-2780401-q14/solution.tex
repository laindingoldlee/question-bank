The triangle inequality for the six vectors $(a_i,i)$ gives $\sum\sqrt{a_i^2+i^2}\ge\sqrt{(\sum a_i)^2+(\sum i)^2}=\sqrt{20^2+21^2}=29 $. Equality holds when all vectors are positive multiples of one another, namely $ a_i=\dfrac{20i}{21}$. These are nonnegative and sum to 20. Thus the minimum is $\boxed{29}$.
