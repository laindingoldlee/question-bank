Use $A=(0,0)$, $B=(s,0)$, $C=(s,s)$, and $P=(x,y)$. Subtracting squared-distance equations gives $x=\dfrac{s^2+12}{2s}$ and $y=\dfrac{s^2-4}{2s}$. Substitution into $x^2+y^2=16$, with $t=s^2$, yields $(t+12)^2+(t-4)^2=64t$, or $(t-4)(t-20)=0$. If $t=4$, then $x=4>s=2$, contradicting interior placement. For $t=20$, both coordinates are strictly between $0$ and $s$, so the area is $\boxed{20}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
real s=sqrt(20);pair A=(0,0),Bp=(s,0),C=(s,s),D=(0,s),P=(16/s,8/s);draw(A--Bp--C--D--cycle);draw(A--P--Bp,blue);draw(P--C,blue);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",Bp,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$P$",P,W);
\end{asy}
