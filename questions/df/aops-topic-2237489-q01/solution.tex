We need
\[2n^2-2<n^2+216n-18\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad n^2-216n+16<0 .\]
The roots of $n^2-216n+16=0$ are $108\pm\sqrt{108^2-16}=108\pm\sqrt{11648}$. Since $107^2=11449<11648<11664=108^2$, the larger root lies between $215$ and $216$, and the smaller root lies between $0$ and $1$. The inequality holds between the roots, so the positive integers that work are $1,2,\ldots,215$.

Check: for $n=215$, $n^2-216n+16=-215+16<0$; for $n=216$ it is $16>0$. The answer is $\boxed{215}$.
