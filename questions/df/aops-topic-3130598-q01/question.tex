People occupy the integer lattice of seats. The person at $(0,0)$ certainly sings; the one at $(-1,0)$ certainly does not. Everyone else sings independently with probability $\dfrac{1}{3}$. Find the probability that four consecutive singers in a horizontal, vertical, or 45-degree diagonal line include $(0,0)$.
