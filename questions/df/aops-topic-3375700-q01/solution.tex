Labor per cake costs $\dfrac{10\cdot6}{60}=1$ dollar, giving total cost $2.60$. The marked price is $2.60\cdot1.75=4.55$ dollars. After discount it is $4.55\cdot0.80=\boxed{3.64\text{ dollars}}$.
