The third equation is $8x=-9$, the vertical line $x=-\dfrac98$. Write the other two lines as
\[\ell_1:\ y=\dfrac{200-2x}{9},\qquad \ell_2:\ y=\dfrac{3-13x}{2}.\]
\textbf{The triangle.} The three lines meet pairwise at
\begin{itemize}
\item $\ell_1\cap\ell_2$: from $10x+\dfrac{45(3-13x)}2=1000$ we get $-565x=1865$, so $x=-\dfrac{373}{113}\approx-3.30$, $y=\dfrac{2594}{113}\approx22.96$;
\item $\ell_1$ and $x=-\dfrac98$: $y=\dfrac{809}{36}\approx22.47$;
\item $\ell_2$ and $x=-\dfrac98$: $y=\dfrac{141}{16}\approx8.81$.
\end{itemize}
So the region is the triangle with these vertices: the points with $-\dfrac{373}{113}\le x\le-\dfrac98$ and $\dfrac{3-13x}2\le y\le\dfrac{200-2x}9$ (for these $x$ the line $\ell_2$ lies below $\ell_1$, as at $x=-\dfrac98$).

\textbf{Counting.} The integers $x$ with $-3.30\le x\le-1.125$ are $x=-3$ and $x=-2$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $x=-3$: $21\le y\le\dfrac{206}{9}\approx22.9$, so $y=21,22$: $2$ points (the point $(-3,21)$ lies on $\ell_2$).
\item $x=-2$: $14.5\le y\le\dfrac{204}{9}\approx22.7$, so $y=15,16,\dots,22$: $8$ points.
\end{itemize}
In total there are $\boxed{10}$ lattice points.
