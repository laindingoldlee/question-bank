The expression is $(2x+\dfrac{3}{x})^3$. AM--GM gives $2x+\dfrac{3}{x}\ge2\sqrt6$, with equality when $2x=\dfrac{3}{x}$, or $x=\sqrt{\dfrac{3}{2}}$. Cubing gives the attained minimum $\boxed{48\sqrt6}$. AM--GM on the four displayed terms would require them all equal. The first two being equal forces $x^2=\dfrac{9}{2}$, whereas the middle two being equal forces $x^2=\dfrac{3}{2}$; these cannot both hold. That bound is valid but not sharp.
