The final active set may contain $k=0,1,2,$ or $3$ originally benched players, giving $\binom{11}{k}^2$ sets for each $k$. All are attainable. For $k=3$, make three separate swaps. For $k=1$, make one swap and a cancelling pair. For $k=2$, insert an intermediate bench player before replacing that player by one desired newcomer, then make the second desired replacement. For $k=0$, replace an original by bench player $A$, replace $A$ by another bench player $B$, and replace $B$ by the original. Thus the count is $1+121+3025+27225=\boxed{30372}$.
