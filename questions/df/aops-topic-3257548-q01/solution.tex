Each nonzero digit must appear at least twice to form $11,22,\ldots,99$, requiring at least $18$ digits. A zero is also needed, for example to form $10$, so at least $19$ digits are necessary. The $19$-digit number $1234567891234567890$ works: choose any nonzero tens digit from the first block and any nonzero units digit from the second, or the final zero as units. Single-digit numbers also occur. Therefore the smallest possible integer has $\boxed{19}$ digits.
