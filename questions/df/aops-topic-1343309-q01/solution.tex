The square root requires $x\ge0$. Completing the square,
\[
x^2-5x+12=\left(x-\dfrac{5}{2}\right)^2+\dfrac{23}{4}>0
\]
for every real $x$, and $2\sqrt{3x}\ge0$. So the left-hand side is always positive, and the equation has $\boxed{\text{no real roots}}$.
