Factor as $2^9(1+2^{90})$. Since $2^{10}=1024\equiv-1\pmod{25}$, $2^{90}\equiv-1\pmod{25}$, so the second factor is divisible by $25$. The first is divisible by $4$. As $4$ and $25$ are coprime, the whole number is divisible by $100$.
