Each weighing has at most three outcomes, so three weighings distinguish at most $ 3^3=27<32 $ possibilities; at least four are needed. Four suffice by splitting candidates into groups as equally as possible and comparing two equal-sized groups. First compare 11 against 11, leaving at most 11 candidates. Subsequent balanced three-way splits reduce this to at most 4, then 2, then 1. Thus the answer is $\boxed4 $.
