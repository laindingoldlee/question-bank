Expanding shows
\[(x+3)(y-2)(z+6)=xyz+6xy-2xz+3yz-12x+18y-6z-36,\]
so the equation is $(x+3)(y-2)(z+6)=53$, a prime. For positive integers, $x+3\ge4$ and $z+6\ge7$, so $(x+3)(z+6)$ is a positive divisor of $53$ that is a product of two integers each at least $4$. The only positive divisors are $1$ and $53$, and neither is such a product ($53$ is prime). Hence there are $\boxed{0}$ triples.
