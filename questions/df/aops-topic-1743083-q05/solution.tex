Numbers $\equiv7\pmod{11}$: $7,18,29,40,\dots$; of these, $\equiv5\pmod7$ first at $40$ ($40=35+5$). So $n\equiv40\pmod{77}$: $40,117,194,\dots$; the first $\equiv2\pmod5$ is $117$. By the Chinese remainder theorem the solutions are $n\equiv117\pmod{385}$. The three-digit ones are $117$, $502$, $887$ (the next is $1272$): $\boxed{3}$ integers.
