Dewey buys soda in $12$-ounce cans that cost $\$1$ each. Peppar buys soda in $20$-ounce bottles that cost $\$1.25$ each. If Dewey and Peppar buy the same volume of soda in a week, then Peppar pays $P\%$ less than Dewey. What is the value of $P$?
