Compare the prices per ounce: Dewey pays $\dfrac{1}{12}$ dollar per ounce and Peppar $\dfrac{1.25}{20}=\dfrac{1}{16}$ dollar per ounce. For the same volume, Peppar pays
\[
\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{16}}{\dfrac{1}{12}}=\dfrac{12}{16}=\dfrac{3}{4}
\]
of what Dewey pays, that is, $25\%$ less. So $P=\boxed{25}$. (For example, for $240$ ounces Dewey pays $\$20$ and Peppar $\$15$.)
