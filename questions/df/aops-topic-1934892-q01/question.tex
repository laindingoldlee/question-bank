Let $S$ be a set of positive integers, none of which has a prime factor greater than $20$. The greatest common factor of any two distinct elements of $S$ is greater than $1$ and is not divisible by the square of any prime. What is the greatest possible number of elements of $S$? (A student found $135$ elements: all $2^7=128$ squarefree even numbers built from the primes up to $19$, plus $7$ numbers each containing one square of an odd prime. Find the missing element and prove the maximum.)
