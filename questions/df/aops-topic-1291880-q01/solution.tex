By definition, $-(-x)$ is the number that added to $-x$ gives $0$. Then
\[
-(-x)=-(-x)+0=-(-x)+\bigl((-x)+x\bigr)=\bigl(-(-x)+(-x)\bigr)+x=0+x=x,
\]
using, in order, the identity $0$, the fact that $(-x)+x=0$, associativity, the definition of $-(-x)$, and the identity again. $\blacksquare$ (Alternatively: $x$ itself satisfies $x+(-x)=0$, and additive inverses are unique.)
