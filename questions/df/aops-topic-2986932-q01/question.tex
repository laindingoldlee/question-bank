Consider $5$-tuples $(a_1,\dots,a_5)$ of integers from $1$ to $5$ (repetition allowed). The tuple is \emph{perfect} if for any three distinct indices $i,j,k$, the numbers $a_i,a_j,a_k$ do not form an arithmetic progression in any order (a constant triple counts as an arithmetic progression). How many perfect $5$-tuples are there?
