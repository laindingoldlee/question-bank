An oddly shaped test tube consists of a hemisphere of radius $2$ cm attached to the bottom of a cylindrical tube of radius $2$ cm and height $10$ cm, with glass of negligible thickness. Then $50$ mL of liquid is poured into the tube, and the tube is tilted $15^\circ$ from the vertical (rotated about its bottom). The surface of the liquid is an ellipse. What is its area, in square centimetres? Express your answer as a decimal to the nearest thousandth.
