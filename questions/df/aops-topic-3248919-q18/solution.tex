Track the longest suffix that is a prefix of a target pattern. Let $p_P,p_{PO},p_O,p_{OO}$ be the win probabilities from those suffixes. From $OO$, the next $P$ loses, and further $O$s retain $OO$, so $p_{OO}=0$. From $PO$, the next $P$ wins and the next $O$ reaches $OO$, so $p_{PO}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. From $P$, a $P$ retains that state and $O$ reaches $PO$, giving $p_P=\dfrac{1}{2}p_P+\dfrac{1}{2}p_{PO}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. From $O$, a $P$ reaches $P$ and $O$ reaches $OO$, so $p_O=\dfrac{1}{4}$. Initially the probability is $\dfrac{1}{2}p_P+\dfrac{1}{2}p_O=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{8}}$.
