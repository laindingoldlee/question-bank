Write the angular coordinates as $0\le\theta\le\pi$ and $\pi\le\phi\le2\pi$. Their joint distribution is uniform on a square of area $\pi^2$. With $\delta=\phi-\theta$, the distance from the origin to their chord line is $2|\cos(\dfrac{\delta}{2})|$. This exceeds $1$ exactly when
\[
\delta<\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\quad\hbox{or}\quad\delta>\dfrac{4\pi}{3}.
\]
Within the parameter square these are two right triangles, each with legs $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$. Their combined area is $\dfrac{4\pi^2}{9}$, so the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{4}{9}}$. Tangencies have probability zero and do not affect the result.
