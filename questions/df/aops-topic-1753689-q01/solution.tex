Write $x^4-10x^2+16=0$ as $(x^2-2)(x^2-8)=0$, so the roots are $\pm\sqrt2$ and $\pm2\sqrt2$. Two distinct roots with positive product have the same sign and different absolute values (a root and itself is excluded): $\left\{\sqrt2,2\sqrt2\right\}$ or $\left\{-\sqrt2,-2\sqrt2\right\}$. In both cases $pq=\sqrt2\cdot2\sqrt2=\boxed{4}$.
