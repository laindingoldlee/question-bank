Use cube coordinates $[0,2]^3$ and normalize the plane $ax+by+cz=d$ so that $a^2+b^2+c^2=3$. Its signed expressions at vertices must be in $\{-3,-1,1,3\}$. Across an edge, the difference is $2a,2b$, or $2c$, so $a,b,c$ are integers. Their squared sum forces $|a|=|b|=|c|=1$. Reflect coordinates as necessary to reduce to $x+y+z=t$.

The vertex sums are $0,2,4,6$. The conditions $|t|,|6-t|\in\{1,3\}$ force $t=3$, and the other vertices then have the required distances too. The section vertices are
\[(0,1,2),(0,2,1),(1,2,0),(2,1,0),(2,0,1),(1,0,2).\]
Consecutive sides have length $\sqrt2$ and interior angle $120^\circ$, so this is a regular hexagon. Its area is $6\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}(\sqrt2)^2=\boxed{3\sqrt3}$, the sole possible value.

\end{asy}
