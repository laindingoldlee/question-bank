The row count $r$ can be any positive divisor of $36=2^2\cdot 3^2$, with column count $\dfrac{36}{r}$ determined. The number is $(2+1)(2+1)=\boxed 9$. They are $(1,36),(2,18),(3,12),(4,9),(6,6),(9,4),(12,3),(18,2),(36,1)$.
