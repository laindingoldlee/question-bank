The equation is $50x^2-10x+11i=0$. By the quadratic formula,
\[x=\dfrac{10\pm\sqrt{\Delta}}{100},\qquad\Delta=10^2-4\cdot50\cdot11i=100-2200i=100(1-22i),\]
so $\sqrt\Delta=10w$, where $w$ is a square root of $1-22i$.

\textbf{The square root.} Write $w=p-qi$ with $p,q$ real. Then $w^2=p^2-q^2-2pqi=1-22i$ gives $p^2-q^2=1$ and $pq=11$, and $|w|^2=|1-22i|$ gives $p^2+q^2=\sqrt{1+484}=\sqrt{485}$. So
\[p=\sqrt{\dfrac{\sqrt{485}+1}{2}}\approx3.3928,\qquad q=\sqrt{\dfrac{\sqrt{485}-1}{2}}\approx3.2421\]
(both positive, since $pq=11>0$).

\textbf{The roots.} $x=\dfrac{10\pm10(p-qi)}{100}=\dfrac{1\pm(p-qi)}{10}$:
\[\boxed{x_1=\dfrac{1+p}{10}-\dfrac{q}{10}\,i\approx0.4393-0.3242i,\qquad x_2=\dfrac{1-p}{10}+\dfrac{q}{10}\,i\approx-0.2393+0.3242i}\]
with $p,q$ as above. (Check: $x_1+x_2=\dfrac15=\dfrac{10}{50}$ and $x_1x_2=\dfrac{1-w^2}{100}=\dfrac{22i}{100}=\dfrac{11i}{50}$, as Vieta's formulas require.)
