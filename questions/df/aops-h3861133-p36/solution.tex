The sine addition identity gives $P_0(x)=1,P_1(x)=2x$, and $P_n(x)=2xP_{n-1}(x)-P_{n-2}(x)$. Thus at $x=2$, generate integers $u_0=1,u_1=4,u_n=4u_{n-1}-u_{n-2}$. Summing $\dfrac{u_i}{u_{2i}}$ for $1\le i\le49$ gives $0.3647159222024971326\ldots$. To control the tail, let $r=2-\sqrt3$. The characteristic-root formula gives $\dfrac{u_i}{u_{2i}}=r^i\dfrac{1-r^{2i+2}}{1-r^{4i+2}}<2r^i$. The omitted tail is less than $\dfrac{2r^{50}}{1-r}<10^{-27}$. A reliable estimate is therefore $\boxed{0.364715922202497}$.
