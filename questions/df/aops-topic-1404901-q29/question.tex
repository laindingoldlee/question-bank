An oval is built from four arcs of circles: the arcs on the left and right are congruent, and so are the arcs at the top and bottom, and consecutive arcs are tangent where they meet. The oval has a horizontal and a vertical line of symmetry, and it fits exactly in a $4\times8$ rectangle (touching all four sides). The radius of the small (left and right) arcs is $1$. What is the radius of the big arcs?

(A) $6$\quad (B) $6.5$\quad (C) $7$\quad (D) $7.5$\quad (E) $8$
