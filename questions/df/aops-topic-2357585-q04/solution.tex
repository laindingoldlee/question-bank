Since $39=3\cdot13$, the exponent of $39$ in $2018!$ is the smaller of the exponents of $3$ and of $13$. By Legendre's formula, the exponent of $13$ is
\[\left\lfloor\dfrac{2018}{13}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{2018}{169}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{2018}{2197}\right\rfloor=155+11+0=166,\]
and the exponent of $3$ is $672+224+74+24+8+2=1004$, which is larger. So the highest power is $\boxed{39^{166}}$.
