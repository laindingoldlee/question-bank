Let $X$ be a player with the most wins, and let $Y$ be someone who beat $X$ (exists since $X$ lost a game). If some player $Z$ beaten by $X$ beat $Y$, then $Y,X,Z$ form the required cycle ($Y$ beats $X$, $X$ beats $Z$, $Z$ beats $Y$). Otherwise $Y$ beat every player that $X$ beat, and also $X$, so $Y$ has more wins than $X$, a contradiction. $\blacksquare$
