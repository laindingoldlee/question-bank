Put $A=(-6,0),C=(6,0),B=(0,6\phi),D=(0,-6\phi)$. The circumcentre of $ABD$ is $(3\phi,0)$, so the second intersection with $AC$ is $P=(6\phi^2,0)$. Power of C, or direct substitution of line CD into the circle, gives $Q=(6+\dfrac{12}{\sqrt5},\dfrac{12\phi}{\sqrt5})$. Shoelace on $P,Q,B,C$ gives area $\dfrac{36(\phi^3+\phi)}{\sqrt5}=54+18\sqrt5$, using $\phi^2=\phi+1$. Hence $a+b=\boxed{72}$.
