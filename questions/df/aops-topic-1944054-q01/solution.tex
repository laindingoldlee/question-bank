Expanding, the left side is $a+ab+b+ab=a+b+2ab$ and the right side is $c+d+2cd$. So
\[
a+b+2ab=c+d+2cd,\qquad\text{hence}\qquad a+b-(c+d)=2(cd-ab)
\]
is even. Then $a+b+c+d=\bigl(a+b-(c+d)\bigr)+2(c+d)$ is a sum of two even numbers, so it is even. $\blacksquare$
