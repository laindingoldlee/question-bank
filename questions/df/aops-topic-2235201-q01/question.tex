Ryan rides in a hot-air balloon that is tethered by a $2500$ ft cable to an anchor point on the ground, and the balloon starts at the anchor point. Every five seconds the balloon rises $24$ ft and the wind blows it $7$ ft forward (always in the same direction), until the cable is taut; then the balloon stops moving. A rope is attached to the midpoint of the cable and tied to the point on the ground directly beneath the balloon. How long is the rope?
