Ian's Pomeranian had a litter of $7$ puppies. Their birth weights, in ounces, are whole numbers with these statistics:
\begin{itemize}
\item the median is $14$;
\item the mean is $15$;
\item the mode is $14$ (and $14$ is the only mode);
\item the range is $8$.
\end{itemize}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Give one possible list of the seven weights.
\item[(b)] How many different lists (ignoring the order of the puppies) are possible?
\end{enumerate}
