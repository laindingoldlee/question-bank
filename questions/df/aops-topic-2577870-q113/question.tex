Convert $1\le N\le4$ integers from 0 through 15 to four-bit binary strings, retaining leading zeros. Paco and Diana alternate, Paco first. Paco may choose a string currently beginning with 1; Diana may choose one beginning with 0. A move deletes any nonempty subset of positions in one eligible string, preserving the order of undeleted digits. Empty strings disappear. A player with no eligible string passes. The player who deletes the final remaining digit loses. Design an exact algorithm to determine the winner under optimal play, and output the original four-bit strings.
