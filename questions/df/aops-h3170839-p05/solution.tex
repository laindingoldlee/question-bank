Partition the interval into $91$ blocks of $11$ consecutive integers. In each block, the edges joining numbers with differences $4$ or $7$ form an 11-cycle: modulo $11$ its cyclic order is
\[
0,4,8,1,5,9,2,6,10,3,7,0.
\]
An independent set in an odd cycle of length $11$ has at most $5$ vertices: each selected vertex needs a distinct unselected successor. Hence $|S|\le91\cdot5=455$.
For equality, take all numbers congruent to $0,2,5,8,$ or $10$ modulo $11$. No two of these residues differ by $\pm4$ modulo $11$, so actual differences $4$ or $7$ are excluded even between blocks. Each residue occurs $91$ times. Thus the maximum is $\boxed{455}$.

\end{asy}
