Triangle $ABC$ has a right angle at $C$, with legs $BC=a$ and $CA=b$. Let $CD$ be the altitude to the hypotenuse $AB$, and let $BM$ be the median to the leg $CA$ (so $M$ is the midpoint of $CA$). The altitude $CD$ meets the median $BM$ at $X$. Find the ratio $BX:XM$ in terms of $a$ and $b$.
