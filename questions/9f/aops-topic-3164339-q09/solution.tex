Write $BC=AD=L$. Since $BE\parallel AD$, angle bisection makes triangle $ABE$ isosceles, with $BE=AB=9$, so $EC=L-9$. Using unit vectors $u$ along $AD$ and $v$ along $AB$, we have $D=Lu$, $B=9v$, $E=9u+9v$. At the intersection $P=tE=B+s(D-B)$, coefficient comparison gives $9t=Ls$ and $t=1-s$, hence $t=\dfrac{L}{L+9}$. Therefore $EP=16(1-t)=\dfrac{144}{L+9}$. The condition gives $(L-9)(L+9)=144$, so $L^2=225$ and $\boxed{BC=15}$.
