The total number of arrangements is $\dfrac{12!}{3!4!5!}$. First arrange the seven non-birches in $\dfrac{7!}{3!4!}$ ways. Their eight gaps, including the two ends, may each receive at most one birch, so choose five of them in $\binom85$ ways. The probability is
\[
\dfrac{\dfrac{7!}{3!4!}\binom85}{\dfrac{12!}{3!4!5!}}
=\dfrac{\binom85}{\binom{12}{5}}
=\dfrac{56}{792}=\dfrac{7}{99}.
\]
Thus $\boxed{m+n=106}$.
