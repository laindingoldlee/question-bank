In the square of side 2000, the excluded region $|x-y|>1800 $ consists of two right triangles of leg 200. Its total area is $ 200^2 $. The probability is $ 1-\dfrac{200^2}{2000^2}=\boxed{\dfrac{99}{100}}$.
