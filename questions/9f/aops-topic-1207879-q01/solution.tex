$5^{12}=(5^6)^2=(5^4)^3$ is both; $4^{11}=(2^{11})^2$ and $3^{10}=(3^5)^2$ are squares; $2^9=(2^3)^3$ is a cube. In $6^{13}=2^{13}3^{13}$ the exponent $13$ is neither even nor a multiple of $3$, so it is neither. Answer $\boxed{\textbf{(A)}}$.
