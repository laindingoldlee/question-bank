The sum equals $111(a+b+c)=3\cdot37(a+b+c)$. Since $3\le a+b+c\le27$, the factor $a+b+c$ is not divisible by $37$. Thus the exponent of $37$ in the sum is exactly one, whereas every prime exponent of a perfect square is even. It cannot be a perfect square.
