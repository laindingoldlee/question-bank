Going trick-or-treating implies I get the most candy. By the contrapositive of the fourth statement, I do not invite my best friend, so I invite my sister and she comes. By the contrapositive of the third statement, I do not wear my funny costume. Continuing backwards through the contrapositives: my friend did not dare me to wear a serious costume, so he was not scared, so I do not go to a haunted house.

Hence (e) must be true. (a), (c) and (d) are false, and (b) is not implied by anything. Answer: $\boxed{\text{(e)}}$.
