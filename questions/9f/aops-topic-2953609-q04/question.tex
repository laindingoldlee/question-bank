The following statements are all true.
\begin{itemize}
\item If I do not invite my best friend, I invite my sister.
\item If I go trick-or-treating, I get the most candy.
\item If I wear my funny costume, my sister refuses to come.
\item If I invite my best friend, I do not get the most candy.
\item If my best friend dares me to wear a serious costume, I wear my funny costume.
\item If I go to a haunted house, my best friend gets scared.
\item If my best friend gets scared, he dares me to wear a serious costume.
\end{itemize}
Given that I go trick-or-treating, which of the following must be true? (a) I go to a haunted house. (b) My best friend dares me to wear a funny costume. (c) I invite my best friend. (d) I do not get the most candy. (e) I do not go to a haunted house.
