Let $x=\log_ab$, so $a^x=b$. Taking $\log_c$ of both sides and using $\log_c(a^x)=x\log_ca$,
\[x\log_ca=\log_cb.\]
Since $a\ne1$, $\log_ca\ne0$, and dividing gives $\log_ab=x=\dfrac{\log_cb}{\log_ca}$. $\blacksquare$

With $c=2$: $\log_832=\dfrac{\log_232}{\log_28}=\boxed{\dfrac53}$. (Check: $8^{\dfrac53}=2^5=32$.)
