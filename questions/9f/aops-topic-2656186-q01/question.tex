State and prove the change-of-base formula for logarithms: for positive numbers $a,b,c$ with $a\ne1$ and $c\ne1$,
\[\log_ab=\dfrac{\log_cb}{\log_ca}.\]
Then use it to evaluate $\log_8 32$.
