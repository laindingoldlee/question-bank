Four consecutive odd integers can be written $2k-3$, $2k-1$, $2k+1$, $2k+3$ for an integer $k$; their sum is $8k$. Conversely every multiple $8k$ is such a sum. Now $16=8\cdot2$, $40=8\cdot5$, $72=8\cdot9$ and $200=8\cdot25$ (for instance $16=1+3+5+7$), but $100$ is not a multiple of $8$. The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(D) }100}$.
