A hungry unicorn eats $6$ bowls of food, and each bowl contains $1000$ calories. One hour of moderate labour burns $755$ calories. How long must the unicorn work to burn off all the calories she ate? Give the answer in hours and minutes, rounded up to the next whole minute.
