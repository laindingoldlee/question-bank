Take $B=(0,0),A=(0,6),C=(8,0)$. Then $D=(6,0)$ and $E=(4,3)$. Lines $AD$ and $BE$ are $y=6-x$ and $y=\dfrac{3}{4}x$, so $F=(\dfrac{24}{7},\dfrac{18}{7})$. The distance formula gives $AF=\dfrac{24\sqrt2}{7}$ and $FE=\dfrac{5}{7}$. Hence $\dfrac{AF}{FE}=\dfrac{24\sqrt2}{5}$ and $\boxed{a+b+c=31}$.
