Let the quantities for Twilight, Fluttershy, Pinkie, and Rarity be $t,f,p,r$. Rainbow gets the remainder $40-t-f-p-r$, which must be positive and odd. Rarity can take $1,3,9,27$. When $p=2$, all four choices of $r$ are allowed, and $t+f$ must be even, giving $2\cdot4=8$ possibilities. When $p=14$, only $r=1,3,9$ are small enough, again with $t+f$ even, giving $6$. When $p=15$, the same three values of $r$ work, with $t+f$ odd, giving $6$. Total: $\boxed{20}$.
