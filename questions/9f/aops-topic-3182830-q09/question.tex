This year, the Apple family has harvested so many apples that they cannot sell them all! Applejack decides to make $40$ glasses of apple cider to give to her friends. If Twilight and Fluttershy each want $1$ or $2$ glasses; Pinkie Pie wants either $2$, $14$, or $15$ glasses; Rarity wants an amount of glasses that is a power of three; and Rainbow Dash wants any odd number of glasses, then how many ways can Applejack give her apple cider to her friends?

Note: $1$ is considered to be a power of $3$.
