On each weekday a student has homework with probability $\dfrac35$, and on Saturday and on Sunday with probability $\dfrac16$. On any day it rains with probability $\dfrac27$. Whenever the student has homework, he finishes it on time with probability $q$. All these events are independent. The student plays outside on a day exactly when it does not rain and he either has no homework or finishes it on time. A day of the week is chosen at random. What is the smallest $q$ for which the probability that the student plays outside on that day is at least $\dfrac12$?
