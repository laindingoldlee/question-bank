On a weekday the probability of being free to play (no homework, or homework finished) is $\dfrac25+\dfrac35q$; on a weekend day it is $\dfrac56+\dfrac16q$. Choosing the day at random ($5$ weekdays out of $7$) and requiring no rain,
\[P=\dfrac57\left[\dfrac57\left(\dfrac25+\dfrac35q\right)+\dfrac27\left(\dfrac56+\dfrac16q\right)\right]=\dfrac57\cdot\dfrac{(6+9q)+(5+q)}{21}=\dfrac{5(11+10q)}{147}.\]
Then $P\ge\dfrac12\iff10(11+10q)\ge147\iff q\ge\dfrac{37}{100}$. The smallest such $q$ is $\boxed{\dfrac{37}{100}}$.
