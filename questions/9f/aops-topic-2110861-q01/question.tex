A school with $100$ students offers French and Spanish. Twice as many students take French as take Spanish. Three times as many students take both languages as take neither. The number of students taking both languages is even, and fewer than $10$ students take neither. How many students take Spanish?

(Explain also why $30$, $34$ and $38$ are not possible answers.)
