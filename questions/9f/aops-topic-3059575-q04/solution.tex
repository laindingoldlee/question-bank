The logarithm domain is $ x+y>0 $. Monotonicity gives $ 1+x^2+y^2\le10(x+y)$ for $ S_1 $, or $(x-5)^2+(y-5)^2\le49 $. Its minimum $ x+y $ is $ 10-7\sqrt2>0 $, so the domain does not cut the disk. Similarly, $ S_2 $ is $(x-50)^2+(y-50)^2\le4998 $, whose minimum sum $ 100-\sqrt{9996}>0 $ is also positive. The areas are $ 49\pi $ and $ 4998\pi $, giving $\boxed{102:1}$.
