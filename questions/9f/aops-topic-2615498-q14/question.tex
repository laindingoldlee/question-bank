Let $a,b$ be positive integers of the same parity with fixed sum $c=a+b$. Prove that, over all such pairs, the maximum minus the minimum of $\displaystyle\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}\dfrac{a^i+b^i}{c^i}$ equals $\dfrac{(c-2)^2}{c-1}$.
