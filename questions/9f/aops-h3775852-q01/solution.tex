Use $A=(0,0),B=(75,0),C=(75,100),D=(0,100)$. If $AE\parallel DC$, equal legs force $E=(75,0)$ or $(-75,0)$; the first gives a crossed quadrilateral and the second a parallelogram, so neither is allowed. Thus $ED\parallel AC$. Write $E=(-75t,100-100t)$. Equal legs $AE=DC=75$ give $25t^2-32t+7=0$, so $t=1$ or $\dfrac{7}{25}$. The first again gives a parallelogram. The valid point is $E=(-21,72)$, yielding a convex trapezoid. The pentagon consists of the rectangle and triangle $ADE$, so its area is $75\cdot100+\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot100\cdot21=\boxed{8550}$.
