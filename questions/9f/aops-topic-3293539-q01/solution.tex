In the reference frame of the water, the cap is stationary. The rower moves away from it for $15$ minutes at a constant speed and then returns at the same speed, taking another $15$ minutes. Thus the cap drifts $2$ km in half an hour. The current speed is $\boxed{4\text{ km per hour}}$.
