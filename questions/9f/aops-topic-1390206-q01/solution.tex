The first digit can be any of $1,\dots,9$ except $7$ ($8$ choices), the last digit any of $0,\dots,9$ except $7$ ($9$ choices), and the five middle digits are free ($10^5$ choices). The count is $8\cdot9\cdot10^5=\boxed{7200000}$.
