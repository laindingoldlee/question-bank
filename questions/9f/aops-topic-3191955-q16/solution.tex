Place $C=(0,0)$, $A=(36,0)$, $B=(0,15)$. The hypotenuse has equation $5x+12y=180$. A semicircle of radius $r$ has center $(r,0)$. Its distance to that line is $\dfrac{180-5r}{13}$, which must equal $r$. Thus $180=18r$ and $r=\boxed{10}$. Its diameter $20$ fits on $CA$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair C=(0,0),A=(36,0),Bp=(0,15),O=(10,0);draw(C--A--Bp--cycle);draw(arc(O,10,0,180));dot(O);label("$C$",C,SW);label("$A$",A,SE);label("$B$",Bp,N);label("$O$",O,S);label("$X$",(20,0),S);
\end{asy}
