Let the first term be $a$ and common difference $d$. Then $2a+d=9$, while the eight-term sum is $4(2a+7d)=108$, so $2a+7d=27$. Subtracting gives $6d=18$, hence $d=3$ and $a=3$. The terms are $\boxed{3,6,9,12,15,18,21,24}$.
