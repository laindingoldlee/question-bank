A student claims: ``If $a^5$ is a natural number, then $a$ is also a natural number.''
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that the claim is false for real numbers $a$.
\item[(b)] Prove that the claim is true if $a$ is a rational number.
\end{enumerate}
