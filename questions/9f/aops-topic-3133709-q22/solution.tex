All terms lie in $[4,7]$: if $4\le s_n\le7$, then $4+\dfrac{12}{s_n}$ also lies there. Moreover $|s_{n+1}-6|=\dfrac{2}{s_n}|s_n-6|\le\dfrac{1}{2}|s_n-6|$. Thus the distance from 6 tends to 0, so $S=6$ and $4+\dfrac{12}{S}=\boxed6$.
