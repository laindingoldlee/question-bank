The right side is defined only for $z\ne0$, and then $\dfrac{iz}{z}=i$. The equation would say $\dfrac{i-1}{2}=i$, that is, $-\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{2}i=0+1\cdot i$; comparing real parts gives $-\dfrac{1}{2}=0$, which is false. So \textbf{there is no such $z$}.
