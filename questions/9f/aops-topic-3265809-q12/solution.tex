A regular hexagon is six equilateral triangles, so its area is $6(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4})(2^2)=6\sqrt3$. The total prism height is $8+2=10$, hence the volume is $\boxed{60\sqrt3\text{ cubic inches}}$.
