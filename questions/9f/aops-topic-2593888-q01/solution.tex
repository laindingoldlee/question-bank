Using $\sum_{n=1}^{10}n^2=\dfrac{10\cdot11\cdot21}{6}=385$ and $\sum_{n=1}^{10}n=55$,
\[\sum_{n=1}^{10}\left(n^2+3n+2\right)=385+3\cdot55+2\cdot10=385+165+20=\boxed{570}.\]
(Check: the terms are $(n+1)(n+2)=6,12,20,\dots,132$, and $6+12+20+30+42+56+72+90+110+132=570$.)
