Twenty-five dots are arranged in a $5\times5$ square array, with equal distances between horizontally or vertically adjacent dots.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
for(int i=0;i<5;++i)for(int j=0;j<5;++j)dot((i,j));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

How many non-congruent isosceles trapezoids have all four vertices among these dots? Here a trapezoid has \emph{exactly one} pair of parallel sides (so rectangles are not counted).
