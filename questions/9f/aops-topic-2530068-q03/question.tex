Two dogs, Max and Rudy, race the same distance of $D$ miles from the same start. Max starts $10$ seconds before Rudy; he runs at $15$ miles per hour for the first three quarters of the race and at $8$ miles per hour for the last quarter. Rudy runs at $12$ miles per hour, but at the halfway point she stops for $15$ seconds before continuing at $12$ miles per hour, and at the three-quarter point she stops for $5$ seconds and then runs the last quarter at $18$ miles per hour. All changes of speed are instantaneous. For which values of $D$ does Rudy win?
