Measure time in hours from Max's start; $10$, $15$ and $5$ seconds are $\dfrac{10}{3600}$, $\dfrac{15}{3600}$ and $\dfrac{5}{3600}$ hours.

Max finishes at
\[\dfrac{\dfrac34D}{15}+\dfrac{\dfrac14D}{8}=\dfrac{D}{20}+\dfrac{D}{32}=\dfrac{13D}{160}.\]
Rudy finishes at
\[\dfrac{30}{3600}+\dfrac{\dfrac12D}{12}+\dfrac{\dfrac14D}{12}+\dfrac{\dfrac14D}{18}=\dfrac{1}{120}+\dfrac{D}{24}+\dfrac{D}{48}+\dfrac{D}{72}=\dfrac{1}{120}+\dfrac{11D}{144}.\]
Rudy wins exactly when
\[\dfrac{1}{120}+\dfrac{11D}{144}<\dfrac{13D}{160}\iff\dfrac{1}{120}<\left(\dfrac{13}{160}-\dfrac{11}{144}\right)D=\dfrac{7D}{1440}\iff D>\dfrac{12}{7}.\]
So Rudy wins if the race is longer than $\boxed{\dfrac{12}{7}\text{ miles}}$ (about $1.71$ miles); for exactly $\dfrac{12}{7}$ miles they tie, and for shorter races Max wins.
