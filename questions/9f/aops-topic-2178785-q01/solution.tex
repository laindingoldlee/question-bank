The conditions say $a+b=2c$ and $c+d=2b$. Subtracting the first equation from the second,
\[(c+d)-(a+b)=2b-2c\quad\Longrightarrow\quad (d-a)-(b-c)=2(b-c)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad d-a=3(b-c).\]
So $b-c=\dfrac{60}{3}=\boxed{20}$.

The conditions can be met: for example $a=0$, $b=40$, $c=20$, $d=60$ ($a<b$, $c<d$, the average of $0$ and $40$ is $20$, and the average of $20$ and $60$ is $40$).
