Let $f(x)=A_0+A_1x+A_2x^2$. Then $f(1)+f(-1)-2f(0)=2A_2$. Each of $f(1),f(-1),f(0)$ lies in $[-1,2]$, so
\[2A_2\le2+2-2(-1)=6\quad\text{and}\quad2A_2\ge-1-1-2\cdot2=-6.\]
Hence $-3\le A_2\le3$. $\blacksquare$ (Both bounds are sharp: $f=2-3x^2$ and $f=3x^2-1$.)
