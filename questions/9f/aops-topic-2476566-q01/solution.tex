\textbf{(a)} Measure the position of each hand in degrees clockwise from the $12$.
\begin{itemize}
\item The minute hand turns $360^\circ$ in $60$ minutes, so it is at $6m$ degrees.
\item The hour hand turns $30^\circ$ per hour, i.e. $0.5^\circ$ per minute, so after $h$ hours and $m$ minutes it is at $30h+0.5m$ degrees.
\end{itemize}
Both positions lie in $[0,360)$, since $30h+0.5m<330+30=360$ and $6m<360$. The two hands split the full turn into two angles: the difference of the positions, $\left|(30h+0.5m)-6m\right|=|30h-5.5m|=\theta$, and $360^\circ-\theta$. (Since both positions are in $[0,360)$, we have $0\le\theta<360$.) The angle between the hands is the smaller of the two, which is $\theta$ when $\theta\le180^\circ$ and $360^\circ-\theta$ otherwise. $\blacksquare$

So the bare formula $|30h-5.5m|$ can give the reflex angle: at $9{:}00$ it gives $270^\circ$, while the hands are $90^\circ$ apart.

\textbf{(b)} At $9{:}40$: $\theta=|270-220|=50$, so the angle is $\boxed{50^\circ}$.

At $2{:}50$: $\theta=|60-275|=215>180$, so the angle is $360^\circ-215^\circ=\boxed{145^\circ}$.
