For odd $m$, taking $x=2$ shows $m\mid3$, so $m=1$ or $3$. For $2^k$ with $k\ge1$, taking $x=3$ gives $2^k\mid8$, hence $k\le3$. Conversely, every odd $x$ has $(x-1)(x+1)$ divisible by $8$, and every $x$ coprime to $3$ has square $1$ modulo $3$. Thus every divisor of $24$ works, and these are exactly $\boxed{1,2,3,4,6,8,12,24}$.
