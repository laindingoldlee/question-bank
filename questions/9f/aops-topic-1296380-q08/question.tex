An east-west road runs through Acorn, Centerville and Midland in that order, and a north-south road runs from Centerville to Drake. The distances from Acorn to Centerville, from Centerville to Midland and from Centerville to Drake are each $60$ km. At noon Aaron starts at Acorn and bicycles east at $17$ km/h, Michael starts at Midland and bicycles west at $7$ km/h, and David starts at Drake and bicycles at a constant speed in a straight line across an open field. All three arrive at the same moment at a point on the road between Centerville and Midland. How many kilometres does David bicycle?
