Put Centerville at $0$ on the east-west road, Acorn at $-60$ and Midland at $60$. Aaron and Michael meet when $-60+17t=60-7t$, i.e. $t=5$ hours, at the point $25$ km east of Centerville. David rides from Drake, $60$ km north of Centerville, straight to that point, a distance of $\sqrt{25^2+60^2}=\boxed{65}$ km.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((-60,0)--(60,0)); draw((0,0)--(0,60)); dot((-60,0)); dot((60,0)); dot((0,60)); dot((0,0)); dot((25,0),red);
label("Acorn",(-60,0),S); label("Centerville",(0,0),S); label("Midland",(60,0),S); label("Drake",(0,60),N); draw((0,60)--(25,0),red+dashed); label("$65$",(12.5,30),E,red);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
