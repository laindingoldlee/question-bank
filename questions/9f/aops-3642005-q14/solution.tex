Alice’s final quarter takes five seconds, so her crowded trip takes 20 seconds and Bob’s 15. For length L, normal speed is $\dfrac{L}{15}$ and escalator speed is $\dfrac{L}{20}-\dfrac{L}{60}=\dfrac{L}{30}$. Unobstructed Alice moves at $\dfrac{L}{10}$, taking ten seconds. She beats Bob by $\boxed5$ seconds.
