Put $a=x(y+z)$, $b=y(x+z)$, and $c=z(x+y)$. The equations are $ab=165$, $ac=88$, $bc=120$, so positivity gives $a=\sqrt{\dfrac{165\cdot88}{120}}=11$, $b=15$, $c=8$. Solving $xy+xz=11$, $xy+yz=15$, $xz+yz=8$ gives $xy=9$, $xz=2$, $yz=6$. Therefore $x^2=\dfrac{9\cdot2}{6}=3$, $y^2=\dfrac{9\cdot6}{2}=27$, and $z^2=\dfrac{2\cdot6}{9}=\dfrac{4}{3}$. Their sum is $\boxed{\dfrac{94}{3}}$.
