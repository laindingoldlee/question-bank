For an ordered pair of digits from $1,2,3,4$, the counts by sum $2,\ldots,8$ are $w=(1,2,3,4,3,2,1)$. We need three strictly increasing pair sums, so the answer is $\sum_{i<j<k}w_iw_jw_k$. Here $\sum w_i=16$, $\sum w_i^2=44$, and $\sum w_i^3=136$. Expanding $(\sum w_i)^3$ and grouping repeated indices gives the required sum as $\dfrac{[16^3-3(16)(44)+2(136)]}{6}=\boxed{376}$.
