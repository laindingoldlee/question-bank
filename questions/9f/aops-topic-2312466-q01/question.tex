A carnival game uses a sack with $42$ blue balls and $27$ red balls. A player repeatedly picks a ball at random from the sack and earns a prize worth $10$ cents for each pick, including the last one. A blue ball is returned to the sack and the player picks again; when a red ball is picked, the game ends. How much money, in dollars, does the player win on average?
