A student claims that $\sqrt[3]{x}=\sqrt{\sqrt{x}}$ and $\sqrt[4]{x}=\sqrt{\sqrt{\sqrt{x}}}$ for every $x\ge0$, ``and so on''. Show that the first claim is false by giving a counterexample, determine for which $x\ge0$ it does hold, and give the correct value of a square root taken $k$ times in succession.
