The last digits of $7^1,7^2,7^3,7^4$ are $7,9,3,1$, and since $7^4\equiv1\pmod{10}$ they repeat with period $4$. As $2021=4\cdot505+1$, the last digit of $7^{2021}$ is the same as that of $7^1$, namely $\boxed{7}$.
