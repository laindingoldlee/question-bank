Let $f(x)=\dfrac{\ln x}{x^2}$ for $x>0$. Show that $f(a)=f(b)$ does not imply $a=b$ in general, but that it does when $a,b\in(0,\sqrt e\,]$ (or when $a,b\in[\sqrt e,\infty)$).
