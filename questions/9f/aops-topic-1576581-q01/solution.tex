\textbf{Corners.} The line $2x+3y=21$ meets the axes at $(10.5,0)$ and $(0,7)$; the line $5x+2y=25$ meets them at $(5,0)$ and $(0,12.5)$. The two lines meet where
\[
4x+6y=42,\quad 15x+6y=75\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 11x=33,\ x=3,\ y=5 .
\]
Near the $x$-axis the lower line is $5x+2y=25$, and near the $y$-axis it is $2x+3y=21$, so the region is the quadrilateral with vertices $O(0,0)$, $(5,0)$, $(3,5)$, $(0,7)$:

\textbf{Area.} Split it along the dashed diagonal from $O$ to $(3,5)$. The triangle with vertices $O$, $(5,0)$, $(3,5)$ has base $5$ on the $x$-axis and height $5$, area $\dfrac{25}{2}$. The triangle with vertices $O$, $(0,7)$, $(3,5)$ has base $7$ on the $y$-axis and height $3$ (the $x$-coordinate of $(3,5)$), area $\dfrac{21}{2}$. Hence the area is
\[
\dfrac{25}{2}+\dfrac{21}{2}=\boxed{23}.
\]
