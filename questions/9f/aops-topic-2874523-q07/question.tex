There are 100 buildings in a 10-by-10 array. A spaceship will crush a horizontal or vertical segment of five adjacent buildings. You may put any nonnegative integer number of cups on each roof and will hear only the total number crushed. Give a placement that allows you to determine every crushed building, and state its total number of cups. Minimizing the total is not required.
