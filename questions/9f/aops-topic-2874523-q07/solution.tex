Number the buildings $0,1,\ldots,99$ and put $2^j$ cups on building $j$. The binary expansion of the number of broken cups has a $1$ exactly in the positions corresponding to crushed buildings, because every building is used at most once. Thus the entire set is identified. The total number placed is $1+2+\cdots+2^{99}=\boxed{2^{100}-1}$.
