Becky gains $\dfrac{1}{76}-\dfrac{1}{88}=\dfrac{3}{1672}$ laps per second. She first overtakes Alex after $\dfrac{1672}{3}$ seconds, when Alex has run $\dfrac{1672}{3\cdot88}=\dfrac{19}{3}$ laps. Hence he has finished $\boxed6$ complete laps.
