Jan wants to mail a ring to Maria. In their country anything sent by mail is stolen unless it is inside a box locked with a padlock; a locked box arrives safely, but nobody can open a padlock without its key, and a box can carry several padlocks at once. Jan and Maria each have plenty of padlocks and the keys to their own padlocks, but neither has a key to any of the other's padlocks, and keys sent by mail are stolen. How can Jan get the ring safely to Maria?
