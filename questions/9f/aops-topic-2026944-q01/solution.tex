By Vieta's formulas (with $a\ne0$),
\[
b+c=-\dfrac {b}{a},\qquad bc=\dfrac {c}{a}.
\]
Since $c\ne0$, the second gives $b=\dfrac{1}{a}$, so $a=\dfrac{1}{b}$ (and $b\ne0$). Then the first gives $b+c=-b^2$, i.e. $c=-b^2-b$. Therefore
\[
ac=\dfrac{-b^2-b}{b}=\boxed{-b-1}.
\]
(Check: for $b=1$, $a=1$, $c=-2$, the quadratic $x^2+x-2=(x-1)(x+2)$ has roots $1=b$ and $-2=c$.)
