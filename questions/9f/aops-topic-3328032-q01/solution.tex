In the row order $(P,Q)=(T,T),(T,F),(F,T),(F,F)$, the truth values of $P\Rightarrow Q$ are $T,F,T,T$; those of $(\neg P)\Rightarrow Q$ are $T,T,T,F$. Both are true exactly when $Q$ is true. An implication with a false hypothesis is true by the definition of material implication; this does not assert its conclusion independently.
