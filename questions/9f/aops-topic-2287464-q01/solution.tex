From $a=0.6b=\dfrac35b$ we get $5a=3b$. Since $\gcd(3,5)=1$, $3\mid a$; writing $a=3k$ gives $b=5k$. Then $\gcd(a,b)=\gcd(3k,5k)=k$, so $k=7$, $a=21$, $b=35$, and $a+b=\boxed{56}$.
