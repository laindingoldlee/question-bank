The sum of all digits is $28$. The difference between odd-position and even-position sums must be divisible by $11$. It is even and lies between $-8$ and $16$, so it must be zero. The three even-position digits must sum to $14$. Their possible sets are $\{1,6,7\},\{2,5,7\},\{3,4,7\},\{3,5,6\}$. Each allows $3!4!$ arrangements, giving $\boxed{4(3!)(4!)=576}$.
