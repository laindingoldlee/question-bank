In triangle $AOC$, the angles at $A,C$ are $\dfrac{A}{2},\dfrac{C}{2}$. Hence $\angle AOC=180^\circ-\dfrac{A+C}{2}=90^\circ+\dfrac{B}{2}>90^\circ$. It is always obtuse, so $\boxed{\text{no}}$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(5,0),C=(2,3);real a=length(B-C),b=length(A-C),c=length(A-B);pair O=(a*A+b*B+c*C)/(a+b+c);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--O--C,blue);dot(O);label("A",A,SW);label("B",B,SE);label("C",C,N);label("O",O,E);
\end{asy}
