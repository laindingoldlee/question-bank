A $24$-hour clock shows the correct time again when it has gained a whole $24$ hours $=86\,400$ seconds. At $90$ seconds per hour this takes $\dfrac{86\,400}{90}=960$ hours, which is $\dfrac{960}{24}=\boxed{40}$ days.
