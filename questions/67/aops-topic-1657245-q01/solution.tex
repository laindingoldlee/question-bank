For $n\ge5$, $n!$ contains the factors $3$ and $5$, so $15\mid n!$. Hence only the first four terms matter:
\[
1!+2!+3!+4!=1+2+6+24=33=2\cdot15+3 .
\]
The remainder is $\boxed{3}$.
