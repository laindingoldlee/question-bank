Each student's initials are of the form ``XX'' for a single letter X, and no two students share initials, so the $26$ students use the $26$ letters once each. The vowels are A, E, I, O, U, Y: $6$ letters. The probability is
\[
\dfrac{6}{26}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{13}} .
\]
