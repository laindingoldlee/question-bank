By symmetry take $a\ge b\ge c\ge0$. Since $a=(b-c)^2$ and $b=(a-c)^2$, we have $\sqrt a=b-c\le a-c=\sqrt b$. Together with $a\ge b$, this forces $a=b$. Then $c=(a-b)^2=0$ and $a=b^2=a^2$, so $a=0$ or $1$. The possible triples are $(0,0,0)$ and permutations of $(1,1,0)$. The greatest sum is $\boxed2$.
