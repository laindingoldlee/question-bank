For $ n=1,\ldots,10 $, stars and bars gives $\binom{23-2n}{2}$ choices for $ k,l,m $. Reverse the order of summation to obtain \[\sum_{j=1}^{10}\binom{2j+1}{2}=\sum_{j=1}^{10}(2j^2+j)=2\dfrac{10\cdot11\cdot21}{6}+\dfrac{10\cdot11}{2}=\boxed{825}.\]No larger $ n $ is possible because the other three entries are positive.
