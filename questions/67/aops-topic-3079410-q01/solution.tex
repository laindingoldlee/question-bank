Set $ t=8x-1 $. Multiplying by $ 64 $ after substitution yields $ t^3-27t-90=0 $. Let $ r=\sqrt[3]{81}+\sqrt[3]9 $. The product of the two cube roots is $ 9 $, so $ r^3=81+9+27r $, verifying that $ r $ is a root. Factoring gives $(t-r)(t^2+rt+r^2-27)$. Since $ r>4+2=6 $, the quadratic discriminant $ 108-3r^2 $ is negative. Thus there is only one real root, giving $\boxed{x=\dfrac{1+3\sqrt[3]3+\sqrt[3]9}{8}}$.
