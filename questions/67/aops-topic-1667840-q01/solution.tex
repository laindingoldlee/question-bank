\textbf{Side $AB$.} A semicircle of radius $r$ has area $\dfrac{1}{2}\pi r^2$. From $\dfrac{1}{2}\pi r^2=8\pi$ we get $r^2=16$, $r=4$, so $AB=8$.

\textbf{Side $AC$.} The arc of a semicircle of radius $R$ is half of the full circumference $2\pi R$, so its length is $\pi R$. (This is the point that confuses many readers: the arc length is not the radius, but it is $\pi$ times the radius.) From $\pi R=8.5\pi$ we get $R=8.5$, so $AC=17$.

\textbf{Side $BC$.} Since $\angle B=90^\circ$, $AC$ is the hypotenuse and
\[
BC=\sqrt{17^2-8^2}=\sqrt{225}=15 .
\]
The semicircle on $BC$ has radius $\dfrac{15}{2}=7.5$: $\boxed{\textbf{(B)}\ 7.5}$.
