The left side is $a^{\dfrac25}$, and the right side is $\left(a^{xy+\dfrac{xy}3}\right)^z=a^{\dfrac{4xyz}{3}}$. Equating exponents, $\dfrac{4xyz}{3}=\dfrac25$, so $xyz=\boxed{\dfrac3{10}}$.
