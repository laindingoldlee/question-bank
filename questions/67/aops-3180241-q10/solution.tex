Fix Alfred at position zero and write the other five as $b,c,d,e,f$, with their letter ranks compared in the usual order. There are $5!=120$ seatings. A forbidden increasing block cannot cross the descent into Alfred. Thus the only bad events are $E_0:A<b<c<d$, $E_1:b<c<d<e$, $E_2:c<d<e<f$. Their sizes are $\binom53 2!=20$, $\binom54=5$, and $\binom54=5$. Pairwise intersection sizes are $5,1,1$, respectively for $(E_0,E_1),(E_0,E_2),(E_1,E_2)$; the triple intersection has size one. Inclusion--exclusion gives $24$ bad seatings, so $\boxed{96}$ good ones.
