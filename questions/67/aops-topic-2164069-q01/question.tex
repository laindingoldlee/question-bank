Let $D$, $A$ and $E$ be points in the plane such that $\angle DAE$ is acute. Points $B$ and $C$ are chosen on rays $AD$ and $AE$, respectively, so that triangle $ABC$ is acute. Let $AX$, $BY$ and $CZ$ be the altitudes of triangle $ABC$, with $X$ on $BC$, $Y$ on $CA$ and $Z$ on $AB$. Prove that, as $B$ and $C$ vary, the measure of $\angle YXZ$ stays the same. (In fact $\angle YXZ=180^\circ-2\angle DAE$.)
