Two distinct points are selected at random from the nine-point set
\[
S=\{(1,1),(1,2),(1,3),(2,1),(2,2),(2,3),(3,1),(3,2),(3,3)\}.
\]
What is the probability that their midpoint also belongs to $S$? Express your answer as a common fraction.
