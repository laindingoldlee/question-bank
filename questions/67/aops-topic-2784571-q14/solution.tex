A sequence is determined by its first letter and the lengths of its runs, which form a composition of $8$. Let $c(n)$ be the number of compositions of $n$ into parts from $\{1,2,3,4\}$. Then $c(0)=1$ and $c(n)=c(n-1)+c(n-2)+c(n-3)+c(n-4)$, giving
\[c(1),\dots,c(8)=1,2,4,8,15,29,56,108.\]
There are $2\cdot108=216$ good sequences, and the probability is $\dfrac{216}{256}=\dfrac{27}{32}$, so $p+q=\boxed{59}$.
