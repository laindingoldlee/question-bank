Charlie chooses $10$ different integers from $1$ to $20$ inclusive, no two of which sum to $21$. Henry chooses $12$ different integers from $1$ to $24$ inclusive, all different from Charlie's, no two of which sum to $25$. In how many ways can Charlie and Henry choose their numbers? (Only the sets of numbers matter, not the order.)
