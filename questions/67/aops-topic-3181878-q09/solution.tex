The triangles share their included angle at $A$, so their area ratio is $\dfrac{AD\cdot AE}{AB\cdot AC}=\dfrac{20}{120}=\dfrac{1}{6}$. Thus the quadrilateral has five times the small triangle area, giving $[ADE]=\dfrac{40}{5}=\boxed8$.
