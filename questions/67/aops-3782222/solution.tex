Put $t=\sqrt{a^2-b}\ge0$. The roots are $a\pm t$ and $-a\pm t$. Depending on their ordering, the specified determinant is $0$ or $\pm4at$. Thus $at=26$, and $a^2(a^2-b)=676$. Since $a^2-b$ is an integer, $a^2\mid676=2^2 13^2$, giving $a\in\{1,2,13,26\}$. Positivity of $b = a^2 - \dfrac{676}{a^2}$ eliminates $1,2$; the remaining values are $165,675$. With $a=13,t=2$, an appropriate root ordering gives determinant $104$. The minimum is therefore $\boxed{165}$.
