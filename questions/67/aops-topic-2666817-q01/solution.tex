Up to $1000$ there are $\left\lfloor\dfrac{1000}{4}\right\rfloor=250$ multiples of $4$ and $\left\lfloor\dfrac{1000}{6}\right\rfloor=166$ multiples of $6$; the numbers that are multiples of both are the multiples of $\operatorname{lcm}(4,6)=12$, of which there are $83$. By inclusion--exclusion, $250+166-83=333$ numbers are multiples of $4$ or $6$.

Every multiple of $24$ is among them, and there are $\left\lfloor\dfrac{1000}{24}\right\rfloor=41$ of these. The answer is $333-41=\boxed{292}$.
