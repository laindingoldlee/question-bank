Let Todd' s still-water time be $t>10$. Current-assisted times are $\dfrac{20t}{(t+20)}$ and $\dfrac{20(t-10)}{(t+10)}$. Their difference is $\dfrac{4000}{((t+20)(t+10))}=2$, giving $t^2+30t-1800=(t-30)(t+60)=0$. Thus $t=30$ and Todd' s race time is $20\cdot\dfrac{30}{50}=\boxed{12\text{ seconds}}$.
