The mistake is in step 1: $(x+3)^2$ is not $x^2+9$. Squaring a sum gives a middle term:
\[
(x+3)^2=x^2+2\cdot3\cdot x+9=x^2+6x+9 .
\]
(For instance, at $x=1$: $(1+3)^2=16$, but $1^2+9=10$.) The expansion $4(x^2+7x+12)=4x^2+28x+48$ is correct. So
\[
(x+3)^2+4(x^2+7x+12)+5x^4=x^2+6x+9+4x^2+28x+48+5x^4=\boxed{5x^4+5x^2+34x+57}.
\]
