A student simplifies $(x+3)^2+4(x^2+7x+12)+5x^4$ as follows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $x^2+9+4x^2+28x+48+5x^4$,
\item $5x^4+5x^2+28x+57$.
\end{enumerate}
Find the mistake and give the correct simplified expression.
