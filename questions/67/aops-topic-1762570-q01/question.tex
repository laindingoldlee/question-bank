Let $ABCD$ be a square of side length $1$, and let $P$ be a variable point on side $\overline{CD}$. Let $Q$ be the point where the bisector of $\angle APB$ meets $\overline{AB}$. As $P$ moves along $\overline{CD}$, the set of possible locations of $Q$ is a line segment. Find the length of this segment.
