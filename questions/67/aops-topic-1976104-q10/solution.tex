The circle has area $\pi\cdot4^2=16\pi$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((0,0)--(10.05,0)--(10.05,5)--(0,5)--cycle,linewidth(1));
label("$x$",(5.03,0),S);label("$5$",(10.05,2.5),E);
draw(circle((16,2.5),4),linewidth(1));
draw((16,2.5)--(20,2.5),blue);dot((16,2.5));label("$4$",(18,2.5),N,blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

So $5x=16\pi$ and $x=\dfrac{16\pi}{5}\approx\dfrac{50.265}{5}=10.053$. To the nearest tenth, $x=\boxed{10.1}$ inches.
