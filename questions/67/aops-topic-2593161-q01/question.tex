Let $w=r(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)$ with $r>0$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that for every complex number $z=s(\cos\varphi+i\sin\varphi)$,
\[wz=rs\bigl(\cos(\theta+\varphi)+i\sin(\theta+\varphi)\bigr).\]
\item[(b)] Deduce that, in the complex plane, multiplication by $w$ is a rotation about $0$ through the angle $\theta$ followed by a dilation with centre $0$ and factor $r$.
\item[(c)] Illustrate with $w=1+i$ and $z=2+i$.
\end{enumerate}
