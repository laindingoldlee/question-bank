Let the numbers be $n,n+1,n+2,n+3,n+4$ with $n\ge1$. Their sum is
\[5n+10=5(n+2).\]
This is a multiple of $5$, and since $n+2\ge3$ it is at least $15$, so it is a multiple of $5$ larger than $5$. Such a number has the divisor $5$ with $1<5<5(n+2)$, so it is not prime. (If $0$ is counted as a natural number, the case $n=0$ gives $10$, also not prime.) $\blacksquare$
