If the smallest central angle is $x$, the angles are $x,2x,4x,8x$, so $15x=360^\circ$ and $x=24^\circ$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real[] a={24,48,96,192};real t=0;pair O=(0,0);
draw(unitcircle);
for(int i=0;i<4;++i){draw(O--dir(t));label("$"+string(a[i])+"^\circ$",0.6*dir(t+a[i]/2),fontsize(8pt));t+=a[i];}
dot(O);
\end{asy}

Answer $\boxed{\textbf{(D)}\ 24}$.
