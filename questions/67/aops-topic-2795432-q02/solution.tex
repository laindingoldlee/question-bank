In the five rows from bottom to top, put the leftmost $2,3,3,4,3$ squares in the first piece. They total 15; the complement also has 15. Both pieces are connected. The first piece touches 10 units of the outside boundary:5 on the left,2 on the bottom,3 on top. The other touches 12. The common cut has length $5+1+0+1+1=8$. Thus their perimeters are 18 and 20, differing by 2.

\end{asy}
