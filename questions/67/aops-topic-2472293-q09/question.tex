Let $a$ and $b$ be real numbers. The four roots of
\[x^4-(a+16i)x^3+(147+306i)x^2+(238-1610i)x-(2000-bi)\]
are Gaussian integers (complex numbers whose real and imaginary parts are integers), and in the complex plane they are the vertices of a parallelogram. Find $a$ and $b$.
