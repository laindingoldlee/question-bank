Since $450=2\cdot3^2\cdot5^2$, a factor of $450$ is a multiple of $3$ exactly when it equals $3k$ with $k$ a factor of $\dfrac{450}{3}=150=2\cdot3\cdot5^2$. The number $150$ has $(1+1)(1+1)(2+1)=12$ factors, so there are $12$ such factors $3k$.

The factors of $150$ pair up as $k$ and $\dfrac{150}{k}$, so their product is $150^{6}$ (six pairs, each with product $150$). Hence
\[
\prod 3k=3^{12}\cdot150^6=(9\cdot150)^6=\boxed{1350^6}.
\]
Equivalently, the twelve numbers $3k$ pair up as $3k$ and $3\cdot\dfrac{150}{k}$, each pair having product $9\cdot150=1350$; there are $6$ pairs.
