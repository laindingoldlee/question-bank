A given coin survives three rounds with probability $(\dfrac{1}{2})^3=\dfrac{1}{8}$. Survival events are independent across the four coins. The game lasts longer than three rounds exactly when at least one survives, with probability $1-(\dfrac{7}{8})^4=\boxed{\dfrac{1695}{4096}}$.
