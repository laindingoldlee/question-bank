Since $2013=3\cdot11\cdot61$, its three-digit divisors are $183$ and $671$. For prefix $183$, a multiple of thirteen beginning with $3$ and using new digits in its last two positions must be checked against the two-digit condition; none works. For prefix $671$, the three-digit multiple starts with $1$. Testing $104,117,130,143,156,169,182,195$ and excluding repeated digits leaves the successful extensions $143$ followed by $39$ and $195$ followed by $52$. Thus the only six-digit prefixes are $671439$ and $671952$.
In each case the four unused digits may be permuted freely in the last four positions, giving $4!$ choices. Hence the answer is $\boxed{2\cdot4!=48}$.
