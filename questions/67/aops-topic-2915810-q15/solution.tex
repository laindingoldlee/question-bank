The difference $b-a$ lies between 1 and 29 and cannot equal $b$, so it must equal the selected number $a$. Thus $b=2a$. The sum $3a$ exceeds both selected numbers and must be greater than 30, so $a\ge11$. The least choice is $\boxed{(a,b)=(11,22)}$, with sum 33 and difference 11.
