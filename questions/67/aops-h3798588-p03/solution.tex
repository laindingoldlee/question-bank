Let $x_i$ be one for a knight and zero for a knave. Truthfulness requires $x_i=x_{i-1}+x_{i+1}\pmod2$, hence $x_{i+3}=x_i$. The allowed three-term repeating blocks are $000,011,101,110$. Since $2025=3\cdot675$, a nonzero block gives two knights per three people, attaining the maximum $\boxed{1350}$. Repeating knight, knight, knave verifies every statement.
