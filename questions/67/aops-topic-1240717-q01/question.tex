Let $T(n)$ be the number of ways to choose three distinct numbers from $\{1,2,\dots,n\}$ that are the side lengths of a (non-degenerate) triangle. For example $T(5)=3$: $\{2,3,4\}$, $\{2,4,5\}$, $\{3,4,5\}$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find a recursion for $T(n)$.
\item[(b)] Find a closed formula for $T(n)$.
\end{enumerate}
