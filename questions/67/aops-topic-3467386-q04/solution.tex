The original paper volume is $\pi h(R^2-r^2)$ and the new volume is $\pi h(R^2-1.21r^2)$. To keep volume divided by price unchanged, price must scale by the same ratio. The fractional reduction is $\dfrac{0.21r^2}{R^2-r^2}$, so the percentage reduction is $\boxed{\dfrac{21r^2}{R^2-r^2}\%}$.
