Place the right triangle at $A=(0,0),B=(4,0),C=(0,3)$. Its median lengths are $\dfrac{5}{2},\sqrt{13},\dfrac{\sqrt{73}}{2}$. Vectors $u=(-4,\dfrac{3}{2})$ and $v=(2,-3)$ have the latter two lengths, while $|u+v|=\dfrac{5}{2}$. They therefore form two sides of the median triangle. Its area is $\dfrac{1}{2}|(-4)(-3)-2\cdot\dfrac{3}{2}|=\boxed{\dfrac{9}{2}}$.

\end{asy}
