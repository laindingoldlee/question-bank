Let $p_k$ be the probability of reaching displacement $k$, with $p_0=1$ and $p_k=0$ for $k<0$. Conditioning on the last step gives $p_k=\dfrac{p_{k-1}+p_{k-2}+p_{k-3}}{3}$. Successive values are $p_1=\dfrac{1}{3},p_2=\dfrac{4}{9},p_3=\dfrac{16}{27},p_4=\dfrac{37}{81},p_5=\dfrac{121}{243}$. The fraction is reduced, so $m+n=\boxed{364}$.
