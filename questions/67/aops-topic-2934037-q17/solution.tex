Writing $N=100M+11O$ and $T=10S+E$, the gcd equals $\gcd(N,100N+T)=\gcd(N,T)\le T\le99$. A gcd of 99 would require $11\mid N$, but $N\equiv M\pmod{11}$ and $1\le M\le9$. Thus it is at most 98. The choice $N=588,T=98$ attains 98, since $588=6\cdot98$. Hence $\boxed{98}$.
