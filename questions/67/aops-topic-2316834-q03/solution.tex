$27=3^3$, $64=4^3$, $x^5=x^3\cdot x^2$ and $y^9=\left(y^3\right)^3$, so
\[\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{27x^5}{64y^9}}=\dfrac{3x\sqrt[3]{x^2}}{4y^3}.\]
The answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{3x\sqrt[3]{x^2}}{4y^3}}$.
