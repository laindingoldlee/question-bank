Since $x+520=(x+50)+470$, the number $x+50$ divides $x+520$ exactly when it divides $470=2\cdot5\cdot47$. For positive $x$ we need a divisor of $470$ greater than $50$; the divisors of $470$ are
\[
1,\ 2,\ 5,\ 10,\ 47,\ 94,\ 235,\ 470,
\]
and the smallest one above $50$ is $94$. So $x+50=94$ and $x=\boxed{44}$. Check: $\dfrac{564}{94}=6$.
