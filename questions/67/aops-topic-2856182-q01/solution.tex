After $t\le10$ further attempts she has made all of them, and the percentage is $\dfrac{45+t}{100+t}$. We need $45+t\ge0.48(100+t)$, i.e. $0.52t\ge3$, i.e. $t\ge5.77$. So $t=\boxed{6}$ attempts (all made): $\dfrac{51}{106}\approx48.1\%$, while $t=5$ gives $\dfrac{50}{105}\approx47.6\%$.
