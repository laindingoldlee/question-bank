The included angles $\theta,\phi$ between sides $a,b$ have equal sine because the areas agree. They are unequal since $c\ne d$, so they are supplementary. Their cosines are opposites, and adding the cosine-rule equations gives $c^2+d^2=2(a^2+b^2)$. Therefore $\boxed{a^2+b^2=1012}$.
