The blue fractions are $\dfrac{1}{5}$, $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{1}{5}+\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{3}{5}$, and $\dfrac{3}{5}\cdot\dfrac{3}{5}+\dfrac{2}{5}=\dfrac{19}{25}$. For one unit of each shade, total blue is $\dfrac{39}{25}$ and total yellow is $ 3-\dfrac{39}{25}=\dfrac{36}{25}$. Thus blue:yellow is $\boxed{13:12}$.
