$240=2^4\cdot3\cdot5$. A positive factor has the form $2^a3^b5^c$ with $0\le a\le4$, $0\le b\le1$, $0\le c\le1$, and different choices give different factors. So there are $(4+1)(1+1)(1+1)=\boxed{20}$ factors.
