The digit count of a positive integer $N$ is $\lfloor\log_{10}N\rfloor+1$. Since $\log_{10}2=0.3010299956\ldots$, we have $69<230\log_{10}2<70$. Thus $10^{69}<2^{230}<10^{70}$, and it has $\boxed{70}$ digits. The bounds can be certified using $0.3010299956<\log_{10}2<0.3010299957$, far from an integer threshold.
