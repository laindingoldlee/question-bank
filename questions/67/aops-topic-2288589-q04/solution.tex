Let the medians meet at the centroid $G$. The centroid divides each median in the ratio $2:1$ from the vertex, so
\[MG=CG=\dfrac23\cdot12=8,\qquad GV=GU=4.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair G=(0,0), M=(-8,0), C=(0,-8), A=(8,8), V=(A+C)/2, U=(A+M)/2;
draw(A--M--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(M--V,blue); draw(C--U,red);
draw((0.9,0)--(0.9,0.9)--(0,0.9));
dot(G); dot(V); dot(U);
label("$A$",A,NE); label("$M$",M,W); label("$C$",C,S); label("$G$",G,SE);
label("$V$",V,E); label("$U$",U,NW);
label("$8$",(-4,0),S,blue); label("$4$",(2,0),S,blue);
label("$8$",(0,-4),E,red); label("$4$",(0,2),W,red);
\end{asy}

Since $MV\perp CU$, triangle $GMC$ has a right angle at $G$, so its area is $\dfrac12\cdot8\cdot8=32$.

The three triangles $GMC$, $GCA$ and $GAM$ have equal areas. (For example, $GV$ is a median of triangle $GAC$, and $MV$ is a median of $MAC$; so $[MCV]=[MAV]$ and $[GCV]=[GAV]$, which gives $[GMC]=[GMA]$. In the same way $[GMC]=[GCA]$.) Hence
\[[AMC]=3[GMC]=3\cdot32=\boxed{96}.\]
(Check in coordinates: with $G=(0,0)$, $M=(-8,0)$ and $C=(0,-8)$, the conditions give $A=(8,8)$; then $AM=AC=\sqrt{320}$ and the shoelace formula gives area $96$.)
