The other factor is a monic quadratic: $x^4+rx+s=(x^2+4x+6)(x^2+ax+b)$. Expanding,
\[
x^4+(a+4)x^3+(b+4a+6)x^2+(4b+6a)x+6b .
\]
The $x^3$ and $x^2$ coefficients must vanish: $a=-4$ and $b=-4a-6=10$. Then $r=4b+6a=40-24=16$ and $s=6b=60$, so $r+s=\boxed{76}$.
