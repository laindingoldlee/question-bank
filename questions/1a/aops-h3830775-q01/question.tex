In Nuria's bookcase there are 3 shelves. After Nuria took one book from the first shelf and her sister Amali took two books from the second shelf, it turned out that the number of books on these two shelves became equal. Moreover, the number of books on the first shelf is now divisible by $11$, and the number of books on the second shelf is now divisible by $7$. How many books were on each of the three shelves before the girls took the books, if it is known that initially there were $527$ books in total? List all possible cases and prove that there are no others.
