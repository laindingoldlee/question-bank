Let the common number remaining on the first two shelves be $m$. Since it is divisible by both $11$ and $7$, write $m=77k$ with integer $k\ge0$. The original counts are $77k+1$, $77k+2$, and $527-(154k+3)=524-154k$. Positivity of the third count requires $k=0,1,2,3$. Thus the complete list is
\[\boxed{(1,2,524),(78,79,370),(155,156,216),(232,233,62)}.\]
The case $k=0$ is valid: both first shelves become empty, and zero is divisible by $7$ and $11$. Every listed triple sums to $527$ and satisfies the removal conditions.
