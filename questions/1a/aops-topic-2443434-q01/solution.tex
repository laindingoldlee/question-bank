Since $1000=2^3\cdot5^3$, every divisor is $2^i5^j$ with $0\le i,j\le3$, and expanding the product below gives each divisor exactly once:
\[\sigma(1000)=\left(1+2+4+8\right)\left(1+5+25+125\right)=15\cdot156=\boxed{2340}.\]
