A circle tangent to the $x$-axis and passing through points above it has centre $(h,r)$ and radius $r$. Then
\[(h-1)^2+(r-9)^2=r^2,\qquad(h-8)^2+(r-8)^2=r^2,\]
i.e. $h^2-2h-18r+82=0$ and $h^2-16h-16r+128=0$. Subtracting gives $r=7h-23$, and then $h^2-128h+496=0$, so $h=4$ or $h=124$, with $r=5$ or $r=845$.

\begin{asy}

draw(circle((4,5),5),blue); dot(P); dot(Q); dot((4,5)); label("$(1,9)$",P,NW); label("$(8,8)$",Q,NE); draw((4,5)--(4,0),dashed); label("$r=5$",(4,2.5),E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The sum of the radii is $5+845=\boxed{850}$. (Alternatively, by Vieta the two values of $h$ sum to $128$, so the radii sum to $7\cdot128-46=850$.)
