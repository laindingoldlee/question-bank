Since $4032=n(n-1)$ is a product of consecutive integers and $\sqrt{4032}\approx63.5$, try $64\cdot63=4032$. So $n^2-n-4032=(n-64)(n+63)$ and $\boxed{n=64\text{ or }n=-63}$.
