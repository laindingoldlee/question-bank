The first $N$ factors have product $2^{\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{4}+\cdots+\dfrac{1}{2^N}}=2^{1-2^{-N}}$. As $N\to\infty$, the exponent tends to 1, so the product tends to $\boxed2$.
