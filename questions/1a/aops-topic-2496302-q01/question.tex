Let $n\ge2$ be an integer and let $d$ be a positive divisor of $n-1$. Prove that a positive integer $N$ is divisible by $d$ if and only if the sum of its digits in base $n$ is divisible by $d$. (For $n=10$ and $d=3$ or $9$ this is the familiar divisibility test.)
