Subtract $1$ and invert to obtain $L+\dfrac{1}{M+\dfrac{1}{T}}=\dfrac{43}{36}=1+\dfrac{7}{36}$. Since $M,T\ge1$, the fractional part is strictly between $0$ and $1$, so $L=1$. Inverting the remainder gives $M+\dfrac{1}{T}=\dfrac{36}{7}=5+\dfrac{1}{7}$. Thus $M=5,T=7$, and $\boxed{(1,5,7)}$.
