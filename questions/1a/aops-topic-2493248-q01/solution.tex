\textbf{(a)} Subtract $b$ and divide by $2$: $x=\boxed{\dfrac{10-b}{2}}$.

\textbf{(b)} The left side is $-\dfrac{2x}{4c}=-\dfrac{x}{2c}$. Multiplying $-\dfrac{x}{2c}=25$ by $-2c$ gives $x=\boxed{-50c}$.

\textbf{(c)} From (a), $b=10-2x$, and from (b), $x=-50c$, so $b=\boxed{10+100c}$.
