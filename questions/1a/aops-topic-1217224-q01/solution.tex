There are $\dbinom93=84$ equally likely sets of sleepers. Exactly two from the same country means a pair from one country and the third sleeper from a different country:
\[\underbrace{\dbinom22\cdot7}_{\text{Mexico}}+\underbrace{\dbinom32\cdot6}_{\text{Canada}}+\underbrace{\dbinom42\cdot5}_{\text{USA}}=7+18+30=55.\]
So the probability is $\dfrac{55}{84}$ (already in lowest terms), and $m+n=\boxed{139}$.
