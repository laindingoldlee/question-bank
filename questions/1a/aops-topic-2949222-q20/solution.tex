The monic quartic $Q(x)=P(x)-[(x-1)^3+1]$ vanishes at 3,5,9,17, so $Q(x)=(x-3)(x-5)(x-9)(x-17)$. At 1 this yields $P(1)-1=2\cdot4\cdot8\cdot16=1024$. Thus $\boxed{P(1)=1025}$.
