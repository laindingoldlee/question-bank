The square labels among $1,\dots,10$ are $1$, $4$ and $9$. Bob ate two fish with square labels, and only one of them ($9$) is among $6,\dots,10$. So at least one of the fish $1$ or $4$, which are mine, was eaten. The answer is \textbf{no}.
