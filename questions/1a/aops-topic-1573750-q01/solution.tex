At each vertex the interior angle $I$ and the exterior angle $X$ are supplementary: $I+X=180^\circ$.

\textbf{Case $I-X=140^\circ$.} Then $I=160^\circ$ and $X=20^\circ$. The exterior angles of a convex polygon add up to $360^\circ$, and in a regular polygon they are all equal, so the number of sides is $n=\dfrac{360^\circ}{20^\circ}=18$.

\textbf{Case $X-I=140^\circ$.} Then $X=160^\circ$, and $n=\dfrac{360}{160}$ is not an integer, so this case is impossible.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real R=10;
pair[] V; for(int i=-3;i<=3;++i) V.push(R*dir(90+20*i));
for(int i=0;i<6;++i) draw(V[i]--V[i+1],linewidth(0.9));
draw(V[0]--R*dir(90-80),gray+dotted);draw(V[6]--R*dir(90+80),gray+dotted);
pair Vt=V[3],Vp=V[2],Vn=V[4];
pair ext=Vt+4.8*unit(Vt-Vp);
draw(Vt--ext,dashed);
draw(arc(Vt,1.0,degrees(Vn-Vt),degrees(Vp-Vt)+360),blue);
label("$160^\circ$",Vt+1.6*dir(270),blue+fontsize(9pt));
draw(arc(Vt,2.4,degrees(ext-Vt),degrees(Vn-Vt)),red);
pair m20=Vt+2.4*dir(180);draw(m20--Vt+(-3.6,2.2),gray);label("$20^\circ$",Vt+(-3.6,2.2),W,red+fontsize(9pt));
dot(Vt);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Hence the polygon has $\boxed{18}$ sides. (Check: the interior angle of a regular $18$-gon is $\dfrac{(18-2)\cdot180^\circ}{18}=160^\circ$.)
