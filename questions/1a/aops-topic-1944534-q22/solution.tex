Since $2019=13\cdot155+4$, we have $2019\equiv4\pmod{13}$, and the powers $4^1,\dots,4^6$ are
\[
4,\ 3,\ 12,\ 9,\ 10,\ 1\pmod{13}.
\]
Their total is $39\equiv0\pmod{13}$. Choosing $4$ elements is the same as leaving out $2$, and the sum of the chosen four is divisible by $13$ exactly when the sum of the two omitted residues is. The pairs summing to a multiple of $13$ are $\{4,9\}$, $\{3,10\}$, $\{12,1\}$: $3$ of the $\binom62=15$ pairs. The probability is $\dfrac{3}{15}=\dfrac{1}{5}$, so $m+n=\boxed{\textbf{(A) }6}$.
