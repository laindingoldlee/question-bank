Let $s=\sqrt[3]{4}\,\sqrt2$. Find the combined volume of the following solids, in all of which every equilateral face has side length $s$:
\begin{itemize}
\item two regular tetrahedra;
\item two tetrahedra whose base is an equilateral triangle and whose other three faces are right isosceles triangles;
\item one square pyramid whose four lateral faces are equilateral triangles;
\item one tetrahedron with two equilateral faces and two right isosceles faces.
\end{itemize}
