At $x=1$ the polynomial is $1-10+35-50+24=0$, so
\[x^4-10x^3+35x^2-50x+24=(x-1)(x^3-9x^2+26x-24).\]
For the cubic, any integer root divides $24$. At $x=2$: $8-36+52-24=0$, so
\[x^3-9x^2+26x-24=(x-2)(x^2-7x+12)=(x-2)(x-3)(x-4).\]
Hence the polynomial is $(x-1)(x-2)(x-3)(x-4)$ and the roots are $\boxed{1,2,3,4}$.
