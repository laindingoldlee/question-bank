The numbers $1,2,3$ cannot be written, nor can $6$ (it is not $4$, $5$ or $7$, and any sum of two of them is at least $8$). But $7=7$, $8=4+4$, $9=4+5$ and $10=5+5$ can, and adding $4$'s then gives every integer $n\ge7$. So the answer is $\boxed{6}$.

(For two coprime numbers $p,q$ the answer is $pq-p-q$, but for three or more numbers there is no comparably simple formula; one finds a run of consecutive representable numbers as long as the smallest generator, as here.)
