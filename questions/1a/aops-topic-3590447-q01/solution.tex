Put $u=\sqrt a$, $v=\sqrt b$. Then $u+v=5$ and $u^3+v^3=20$. The identity $u^3+v^3=(u+v)^3-3uv(u+v)$ gives $20=125-15uv$, hence $uv=7$. But $(u-v)^2=(u+v)^2-4uv=25-28=-3$, impossible for real $u,v$. Thus $\boxed{\text{no such real numbers exist}}$.
