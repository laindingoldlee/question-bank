Complete the square: $ 3x^2+6x+9=3((x+1)^2+2)$. Thus one factorization is $\boxed{(3x+3-3i\sqrt2)(x+1+i\sqrt2)}$. The original quadratic is $ 3((x+1)^2+2)>0 $ for every real $ x $, so it has no real root. A product of real linear factors would have real roots, ruling out real $ p,q $.
