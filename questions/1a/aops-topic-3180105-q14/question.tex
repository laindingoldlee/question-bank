Two players alternate turns. At the start of each turn the player chooses a fixed heads probability between $\dfrac{1}{4}$ and $\dfrac{3}{4}$, then flips until the first head. If its flip number within that turn is odd, that player wins immediately; otherwise the turn passes. The first player starts. Under optimal play, write the second player's winning probability as $\dfrac{a}{b}$ in lowest terms and find $ab$.
