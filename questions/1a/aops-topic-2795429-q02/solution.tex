Put $BC=d$. Moving the reference point from $B$ to $C$ increases the distances to $A$ and $B$ by $d$ each, and decreases those to $C,D,E$ by $d$ each. Thus the total decreases by $d$. Hence $d=20-18=\boxed2$ miles.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair[] P={(0,0),(1,0),(3,0),(4,0),(6,0)};string[] names={"A","B","C","D","E"};draw(P[0]--P[4]);for(int i=0;i<5;++i){dot(P[i]);label("$"+names[i]+"$",P[i],N);}label("$d$",(2,0),S);
\end{asy}
