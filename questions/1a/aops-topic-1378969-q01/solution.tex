By Burnside's lemma, the number of distinct colourings is the average, over the symmetries, of the number of colourings fixed by a symmetry. A colouring is fixed by a symmetry exactly when it is constant on each cycle of faces, so a symmetry whose action on the faces has $c$ cycles fixes $4^c$ colourings.

\textbf{Rotations ($24$).}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{l|c|c|c}
rotation & number & face cycles & fixed\\ \hline
identity & $1$ & $6$ & $4096$\\
$\pm90^\circ$ about a face axis & $6$ & $3$ & $6\cdot64=384$\\
$180^\circ$ about a face axis & $3$ & $4$ & $3\cdot256=768$\\
$\pm120^\circ$ about a vertex axis & $8$ & $2$ & $8\cdot16=128$\\
$180^\circ$ about an edge axis & $6$ & $3$ & $6\cdot64=384$
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The total is $5760$, so with rotations only there are $\dfrac{5760}{24}=240$ colourings.

\textbf{Improper symmetries ($24$ more).} Each is a rotation followed by the central inversion.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{l|c|c|c}
symmetry & number & face cycles & fixed\\ \hline
central inversion & $1$ & $3$ & $64$\\
reflection in a plane parallel to two faces & $3$ & $5$ & $3\cdot1024=3072$\\
reflection in a diagonal plane & $6$ & $4$ & $6\cdot256=1536$\\
$90^\circ$ rotation-reflection & $6$ & $2$ & $6\cdot16=96$\\
$60^\circ$ rotation-reflection & $8$ & $1$ & $8\cdot4=32$
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
These add to $4800$. With all $48$ symmetries the number of colourings is
\[
\dfrac{5760+4800}{48}=\dfrac{10560}{48}=\boxed{220}.
\]
(Only rotations: $240$.)
