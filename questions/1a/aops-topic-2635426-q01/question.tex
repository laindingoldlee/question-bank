Let $ABCD$ be a trapezoid with $AB\parallel CD$ and $AB>CD$, and let $A=\angle DAB$ and $B=\angle ABC$ satisfy
\[\sin A\cos B+\cos A\sin B=1.\]
Prove that the distance between the midpoints of $AB$ and $CD$ is $\dfrac{AB-CD}{2}$.
