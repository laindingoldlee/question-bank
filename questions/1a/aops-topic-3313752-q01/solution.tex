Let $E$ be the expectation from the start, $H_1,H_2$ the remaining expectations after one or two consecutive heads, and $T_1,T_2,T_3$ those after one, two, or three consecutive tails. First-step equations give $H_2=1+\dfrac{1}{2}T_1$ and $H_1=1+\dfrac{1}{2}H_2+\dfrac{1}{2}T_1=\dfrac{3}{2}+\dfrac{3}{4}T_1$. Similarly, $T_3=1+\dfrac{1}{2}H_1$, $T_2=1+\dfrac{1}{2}T_3+\dfrac{1}{2}H_1$, and $T_1=1+\dfrac{1}{2}T_2+\dfrac{1}{2}H_1=\dfrac{7}{4}+\dfrac{7}{8}H_1$. Solving gives $H_1=\dfrac{90}{11}$ and $T_1=\dfrac{98}{11}$. Hence $E=1+\dfrac{1}{2}H_1+\dfrac{1}{2}T_1=\boxed{\dfrac{105}{11}}$. The expectations are finite because every block of four future flips has at least probability $\dfrac{1}{16}$ of all tails and thus absorption.
