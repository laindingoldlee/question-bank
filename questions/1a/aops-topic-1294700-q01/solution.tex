$\dfrac {k}{2016}$ is in lowest terms exactly when $\gcd(k,2016)=1$. Since $2016=2^5\cdot3^2\cdot7$, there are $\varphi(2016)=2016\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot\dfrac{6}{7}=\boxed{576}$ of them. They pair up as $\dfrac {k}{2016}$ and $\dfrac{2016-k}{2016}$ (both in lowest terms, never equal), each pair summing to $1$, so their sum is $\dfrac{576}{2}=\boxed{288}$.
