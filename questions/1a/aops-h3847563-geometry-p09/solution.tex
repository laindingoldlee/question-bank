A square is a rectangle because all its angles are right, a rhombus because all sides are equal, and a parallelogram because opposite sides are parallel. It is also a kite under the inclusive definition requiring two pairs of equal adjacent sides. Under the definition of trapezoid as having at least one parallel side pair, it is a trapezoid too; under the exactly-one-pair convention it is not.
