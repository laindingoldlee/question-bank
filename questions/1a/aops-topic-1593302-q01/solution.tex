Write $f(x)=a(x-3)^2-4$ with $a\ne0$. Then
\[
g(x)=2f(x-4)=2\left(a(x-4-3)^2-4\right)=2a(x-7)^2-8 .
\]
So the graph of $g$ is a parabola with vertex $(7,-8)$: replacing $x$ by $x-4$ shifts the graph $4$ units to the right, and multiplying by $2$ doubles every $y$-coordinate. Hence
\[
h+k=7+(-8)=\boxed{-1}.
\]
