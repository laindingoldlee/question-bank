The difference of the four-digit numbers $\overline{aabb}$ and $\overline{bbaa}$ (where $a>b$, and $b\ne0$ so that $\overline{bbaa}$ is a four-digit number) is a perfect square greater than $2000$ and less than $5000$. Find all possible pairs of digits $(a,b)$.
