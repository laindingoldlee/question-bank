We have $\overline{aabb}=1100a+11b$ and $\overline{bbaa}=1100b+11a$, so
\[
\overline{aabb}-\overline{bbaa}=1089(a-b)=33^2(a-b).
\]
This is a perfect square exactly when $a-b$ is a perfect square. Also $2000<1089(a-b)<5000$ gives $a-b\in\{2,3,4\}$. So $a-b=4$, and the difference is $4356=66^2$. With $b\ge1$ and $a=b+4\le9$, the possible pairs are
\[
\boxed{(a,b)=(5,1),\ (6,2),\ (7,3),\ (8,4),\ (9,5)}.
\]
