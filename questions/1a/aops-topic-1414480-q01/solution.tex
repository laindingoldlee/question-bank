The general term is
\[
\dbinom6r(2x^2)^{6-r}\left(\dfrac{1}{2x}\right)^r=\dbinom6r2^{6-2r}x^{12-3r}.
\]
The power of $x$ depends only on $r$, so the constant coefficient does not change which term is constant: the condition is still $(x^2)^{6-r}(x^{-1})^r=x^0$, that is, $12-3r=0$ and $r=4$. The constant term is
\[
\dbinom64\cdot2^{-2}=\dfrac{15}{4}.
\]
So the constant term is $\boxed{\dfrac{15}{4}}$; the coefficients $2$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}$ only change its value, not its position.
