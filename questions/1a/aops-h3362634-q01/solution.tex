Let $A',B',C'$ be the midpoints of $BC,CA,AB$. Since $A'B'$ is parallel to $AB$ and its oriented vector is $-\dfrac{1}{2}$ that of $AB$, the homothety taking $A'$ to $A$ and $B'$ to $B$ has scale factor $-2$. Its center $G$ lies on both $AA'$ and $BB'$. Consequently
\[
A-G=-2(A'-G),\qquad B-G=-2(B'-G).
\]
Using $A'=\dfrac{B+C}{2}$ in the first relation gives
\[
G=\dfrac{A+B+C}{3}.
\]
Since $C'=\dfrac{A+B}{2}$, this also gives $C-G=-2(C'-G)$, so $G$ lies on the third median. The negative factor places $G$ between each vertex and opposite midpoint, with vertex distance twice midpoint distance. Hence all three ratios are $\boxed{2:1}$.
