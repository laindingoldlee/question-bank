By inclusion-exclusion, the probability of no fixed point is $\sum_{j=0}^{2016}\dfrac{(-1)^j}{j!}$. Thus the desired probability is $\boxed{1-\sum_{j=0}^{2016}\dfrac{(-1)^j}{j!}}$. This alternating partial sum differs from $ e^{-1}$ by less than $\dfrac{1}{2017!}$, so the probability is $\boxed{0.632121}$ to six decimal places.
