The first congruence says $343\equiv200\pmod n$, that is, $n\mid143=11\cdot13$. The second says $2000\equiv999\pmod n$, that is, $n\mid1001=7\cdot11\cdot13$. Both hold exactly when $n$ divides $\gcd(143,1001)=143$. The divisors of $143$ greater than $1$ are $11$, $13$ and $143$, and their sum is
\[11+13+143=\boxed{167}.\]
