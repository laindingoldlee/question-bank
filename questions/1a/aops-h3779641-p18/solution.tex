If the equal radius is r, the centre distance condition gives $(16-2r)^2+(18-2r)^2=4r^2$, or $(r-5)(r-29)=0$. Only r=5 fits the rectangle. Let the third radius be s; its centre is $(16-s,s)$, and the lower-left centre is (5,5). Tangency gives $(11-s)^2+(s-5)^2=(s+5)^2$, simplifying to $s^2-42s+121=0$. Thus $s=21\pm8\sqrt5$. Only the smaller root fits, so $\boxed{s=21-8\sqrt5}$. At this radius its distance to the upper-right centre exceeds 5+s, so the other disjointness condition is met.
