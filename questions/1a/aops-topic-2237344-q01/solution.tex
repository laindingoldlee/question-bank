There are $20\%\cdot10=2$ cool planets and $8$ others. The aliens visit no cool planet exactly when all three planets come from the $8$ others, so
\[P(\text{no cool planet})=\dfrac{\dbinom83}{\dbinom{10}3}=\dfrac{56}{120}=\dfrac{7}{15},\]
and
\[P(\text{at least one cool planet})=1-\dfrac{7}{15}=\boxed{\dfrac{8}{15}}.\]
