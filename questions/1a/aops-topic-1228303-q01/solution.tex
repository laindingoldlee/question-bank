Regroup: $a^2(b+c)+b^2(c+a)+c^2(a+b)=ab(a+b)+bc(b+c)+ca(c+a)$. Each term $xy(x+y)$ is even (if $x,y$ are both odd, $x+y$ is even). So the expression is even, and since each term is at least $2$, it is at least $6$. An even number at least $6$ is not prime. $\blacksquare$

(If $0$ is allowed, $(a,b,c)=(0,1,1)$ and its permutations give the prime $2$, and these are the only ones.)
