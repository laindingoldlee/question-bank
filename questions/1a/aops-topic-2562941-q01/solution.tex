Adding $1$ to both sides of the recurrence gives $a_{n+1}+1=2\left(a_n+1\right)$. So the numbers $a_n+1$ form a geometric sequence with first term $3$ and ratio $2$: $a_n+1=3\cdot2^{n-1}$. Hence
\[\boxed{a_n=3\cdot2^{n-1}-1}.\]
Check: $2,5,11,23,47$ for $n=1,\dots,5$.
