First, $1.5<\varphi<\sqrt3$: indeed $\varphi>\dfrac{3}{2}\iff\sqrt5>2$, and $\varphi<\sqrt3\iff1+\sqrt5<2\sqrt3\iff6+2\sqrt5<12\iff\sqrt5<3$ (squaring positive numbers).

So $\varphi$ is closer to $\sqrt3$ exactly when $\sqrt3-\varphi<\varphi-\dfrac{3}{2}$, that is, when
\[\sqrt3+\dfrac{3}{2}<1+\sqrt5\iff\sqrt3+\dfrac{1}{2}<\sqrt5.\]
Both sides are positive, so we may square:
\[3+\sqrt3+\dfrac{1}{4}<5\iff\sqrt3<\dfrac{7}{4}\iff3<\dfrac{49}{16}\iff48<49,\]
which is true. Hence $\varphi$ is closer to $\boxed{\sqrt3}$, but only just: $\varphi\approx1.6180$, so the distances are about $0.1180$ to $1.5$ and $0.1140$ to $\sqrt3$. The comparison comes down to $48<49$, which is why it is hard to decide by rough estimates.
