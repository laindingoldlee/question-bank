Opposite vertices $A,C$ form a diameter. Thus $\angle AEC=90^\circ$ and $AC=\sqrt{6^2+8^2}=10$. Put the circle centre at the origin with $A=(5,0)$, $B=(0,5)$, $C=(-5,0)$, $D=(0,-5)$. Writing $E=(u,v)$ on the minor arc $AB$, the distance equations give $u=\dfrac{7}{5}$, $v=\dfrac{24}{5}$. Hence $DE^2=(\dfrac{7}{5})^2+(\dfrac{49}{5})^2=98$, so $\boxed{DE=7\sqrt2}$ kilometres.

import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
pair A=(5,0),B=(0,5),C=(-5,0),D=(0,-5),E=(1.4,4.8);draw(circle((0,0),5));draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--E--C^^D--E,blue);label("$A$",A,(1,0));label("$B$",B,(-1,1));label("$C$",C,(-1,0));label("$D$",D,(0,-1));label("$E$",E,(1,1));label("$6$",(A+E)/2,(1,0));label("$8$",(C+E)/2,(-1,1));
\end{asy}
