(a) $(f\circ g)(x)=f(g(x))=f(x^2)=\boxed{x^2+1}$. (Note the order: $g$ is applied first. The other composition is $(g\circ f)(x)=(x+1)^2$, which is different.)

Then
(b) $(-2)^2+1=\boxed{5}$; (c) $0^2+1=\boxed{1}$; (d) $5^2+1=\boxed{26}$; (e) $10^2+1=\boxed{101}$.
