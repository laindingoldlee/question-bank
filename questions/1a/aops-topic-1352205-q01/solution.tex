The first present can get any of the $8$ papers, the second any of the remaining $7$, and so on. By the multiplication principle the number of ways is
\[
8!=8\cdot7\cdot6\cdot5\cdot4\cdot3\cdot2\cdot1=\boxed{40320}.
\]
