Write the eligible numbers as $n_k=\dfrac{k(k+1)}{2}-1=\dfrac{(k-1)(k+2)}{2}$ for $k\ge2$. Their probabilities sum to
\[
\sum_{k=2}^{\infty}\dfrac{4}{(k-1)k(k+1)(k+2)}
=\dfrac{4}{3}\sum_{k=2}^{\infty}\left(\dfrac{1}{(k-1)k(k+1)}-\dfrac{1}{k(k+1)(k+2)}\right).
\]
The finite partial sums telescope; the last term tends to zero. The probability is therefore $\dfrac{4}{3}\cdot\dfrac{1}{6}=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{9}}$.
