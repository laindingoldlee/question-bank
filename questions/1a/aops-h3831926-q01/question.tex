Ford has an unfair random number generator that generates positive integers. When the generator is run, each positive integer n comes up with probability $\dfrac{1}{n(n +1)}$ . For example, the probability of generating an $8$ is $\dfrac{1}{72}$. Ford calls a positive integer ``interdimensional'' if it is one less than a triangular number. (The first few interdimensional numbers are $2$, $5$, $9$, $14$, \ldots{}) What is the probability that Ford's random number generator shows an interdimensional number when run? Express your answer as a common fraction.
