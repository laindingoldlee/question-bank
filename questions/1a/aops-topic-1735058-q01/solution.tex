The trains approach each other at $20+40=60$ mph, so they meet after $\dfrac{300}{60}=5$ hours. The fly flies at $70$ mph the whole time, so it travels $70\cdot5=\boxed{350}$ miles. (There is no need to add up the infinitely many back-and-forth trips.)
