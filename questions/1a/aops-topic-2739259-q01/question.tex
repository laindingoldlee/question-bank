The radius of circle $A$ is $\dfrac13$ of the radius of circle $B$. Circle $A$ rolls without slipping around the outside of circle $B$ and returns to its starting point. How many complete rotations does circle $A$ make?

$\textbf{(A)}\ \dfrac32\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ 3\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ \dfrac92\qquad\textbf{(D)}\ 6\qquad\textbf{(E)}\ 9$
