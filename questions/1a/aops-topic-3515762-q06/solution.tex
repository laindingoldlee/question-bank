Equating exponents gives $x+2y=\dfrac{7}{2}$. Since $(x-2y)^2\ge0$, $8xy\le(x+2y)^2=\dfrac{49}{4}$. Therefore $xy\le\dfrac{49}{32}$, with equality at $x=\dfrac{7}{4},y=\dfrac{7}{8}$. The maximum is $\boxed{\dfrac{49}{32}}$.
