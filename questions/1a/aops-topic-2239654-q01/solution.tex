Let the roots be $r$ and $s$ (they are non-real, since the discriminant $9-560$ is negative, but Vieta's formulas still hold). For $ax^2+bx+c$ with $a=-7$, $b=3$, $c=-20$:
\[r+s=-\dfrac {b}{a}=\dfrac{3}{7},\qquad rs=\dfrac {c}{a}=\dfrac{20}{7}.\]
Hence
\[\dfrac{1}{r}+\dfrac{1}{s}=\dfrac{r+s}{rs}=\dfrac{3}{7}\cdot\dfrac{7}{20}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{20}}.\]
