The \emph{comboity} of a word is the number of letters it shares with the word COMBO, counted with multiplicity but never more often than they occur in COMBO: it is the sum, over the letters C, O, M, B, of the smaller of the number of times the letter occurs in the word and the number of times it occurs in COMBO (once for C, M, B and twice for O). For example, BOW has comboity $2$, BOOK has comboity $3$ and BOOOK also has comboity $3$. A $4$-letter word is any string of $4$ letters from the $26$-letter alphabet, repetitions allowed. What is the expected comboity of a $4$-letter word chosen uniformly at random?
