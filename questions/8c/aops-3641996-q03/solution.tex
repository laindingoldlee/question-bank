Five identical two-sock combinations are possible if some colour has at least ten socks, or if two colours each have at least five. To avoid this, every count is at most nine and at most one exceeds four. The greatest possible avoiding draw has counts $9,4,4,4,3,1$, totalling 25, and is feasible. Any 26 drawn socks exceed this bound, so the guarantee threshold is $\boxed{26}$.
