Let $\theta$ be the angle between the two sides of length $20$. The area is
\[
[\triangle]=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot20\cdot20\sin\theta=200\sin\theta .
\]

\textbf{(a)} Since $\sin\theta\le1$ with equality for $\theta=90^\circ$, the maximum area is $\boxed{200}$, reached when the two equal sides are perpendicular (the base is then $20\sqrt2$).

\textbf{(b)} The original triangle has height $\sqrt{20^2-12^2}=16$, so its area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot24\cdot16=192$. The area is $192$ when $\sin\theta=\dfrac{192}{200}=\dfrac{24}{25}$. The original triangle has the acute angle with $\cos\theta=\dfrac{7}{25}$; the other moment is the obtuse angle with $\cos\theta=-\dfrac{7}{25}$. By the law of cosines the base is then
\[
\sqrt{20^2+20^2-2\cdot20\cdot20\cdot\left(-\dfrac{7}{25}\right)}=\sqrt{800+224}=\sqrt{1024}=\boxed{32}.
\]
Without trigonometry: cut the original triangle along its height into two right triangles with legs $12$ and $16$; putting them together along the legs of length $12$ instead gives an isosceles triangle with sides $20,20$, height $12$ and base $2\cdot16=32$, with the same area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot32\cdot12=192$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(12.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real r=sqrt(2);
pair A1=(-12,0),B1=(12,0),C1=(0,16);
pair A2=(26-10*r,0),B2=(26+10*r,0),C2=(26,10*r);
pair A3=(42+10*r-16,0),B3=(42+10*r+16,0),C3=(42+10*r,12);
draw(A1--B1--C1--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); draw(A2--B2--C2--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); draw(A3--B3--C3--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(C1--(0,0),gray+dashed); draw(C3--(42+10*r,0),gray+dashed);
path sq(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
draw(sq(C2,A2-C2,B2-C2,1.6));
draw(sq((0,0),B1,C1,1.2)); draw(sq((42+10*r,0),(1,0),(0,1),1.2));
label("$20$",(A1+C1)/2,NW); label("$20$",(B1+C1)/2,NE); label("$24$",(-6,0),S); label("$16$",(0,8),W,gray);
label("$20$",(A2+C2)/2,NW); label("$20$",(B2+C2)/2,NE); label("$20\sqrt{2}$",(A2+B2)/2,S);
label("$20$",(A3+C3)/2,NW); label("$20$",(B3+C3)/2,NE); label("$32$",(42+10*r-8,0),S); label("$12$",(42+10*r,6),E,gray);
label("area $192$",(0,-5)); label("area $200$",(26,-5)); label("area $192$",(42+10*r,-5));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
