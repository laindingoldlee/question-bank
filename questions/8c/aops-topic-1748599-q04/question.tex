At Randolf's furniture store, a standard breakfast table costs $\$1000$. Karthik wants to buy the luxury version, which costs $35\%$ more than the standard one, and he has a coupon for a $15\%$ discount on his purchase. After a $6\%$ sales tax is applied to the discounted price, how much will Karthik pay for the luxury table?
