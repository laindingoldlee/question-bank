Per dog, a rottweiler gets $\dfrac{75}{48}$ and a dachshund gets $\dfrac{25}{52}$ (in units of total food per total dogs). The ratio is $\dfrac{75\cdot52}{48\cdot25}=\dfrac{3900}{1200}=3.25$, so a rottweiler gets $225\%$ more, answer $\boxed{\text{d}}$.
