Place values give $1004A+200L+30M=2014$. The leading digit $A$ is positive and at most $2$. If $A=2$, the remaining value is $6$, impossible; hence $A=1$. Dividing the remainder equation by $10$ gives $20L+3M=101$. Modulo $20$, this requires $3M\equiv1$, and among digits only $M=7$ works. Then $L=4$, with distinct nonzero leading digits. Thus $\boxed{M=7}$.
