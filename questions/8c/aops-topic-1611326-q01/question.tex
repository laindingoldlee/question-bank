Solve the right triangle $ABC$, with the right angle at $C$ (that is, find all its sides and angles), given that the altitude to the hypotenuse is $h=2$ and the radius of the excircle lying in the right angle (the excircle tangent to the hypotenuse $AB$ and to the extensions of the legs beyond $A$ and $B$) is $r'=2+2\sqrt2$.
