A $10\times10$ board has its $5\times5$ corner square coloured green and the remaining $75$ cells coloured red. The board is cut along grid lines into pieces (polygons, convex or not). Let $n$ be the number of pieces in which the number of red cells is three times the number of green cells. Find the maximum possible value of $n$.
