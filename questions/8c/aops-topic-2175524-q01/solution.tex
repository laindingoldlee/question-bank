\textbf{$N$ is a Fibonacci number.} Give each step a weight: a step right increases $2x+y$ by $2$ and a step up increases it by $1$. A path from $(0,0)$ ends on the line $2x+y=2020$ exactly when the weights of its steps add up to $2020$, and the path is determined by its sequence of steps. So $N$ is the number of ordered ways to write $2020$ as a sum of $1$s and $2$s.

Let $c_n$ be the number of ordered ways to write $n$ as a sum of $1$s and $2$s. Then $c_1=1$, $c_2=2$, and $c_n=c_{n-1}+c_{n-2}$ (according to whether the last summand is $1$ or $2$). With the Fibonacci numbers $F_1=F_2=1$, $F_{n+1}=F_n+F_{n-1}$, this gives $c_n=F_{n+1}$, so
\[N=c_{2020}=F_{2021}.\]
(Counting by endpoints gives the same thing: $N=\sum_{k=0}^{1010}\dbinom{2020-k}{k}$, the sum along a shallow diagonal of Pascal's triangle.)

\textbf{Fibonacci numbers mod $11$.} Reducing $F_1,F_2,\ldots$ modulo $11$:
\[1,1,2,3,5,8,2,10,1,0,1,1,\ldots\]
Since $F_{11}\equiv F_{12}\equiv1$, the sequence repeats with period $10$. As $2021\equiv1\pmod{10}$, $F_{2021}\equiv F_1=1\pmod{11}$. The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(B)}\ 1}$.
