Let $x$ be an integer and $y$ a non-zero number such that $\dfrac {x}{y}$ and $\dfrac{x+1}{y}$ are both integers. Prove that if $y$ is an integer, then $y=1$ or $y=-1$. (So the claim ``$y$ cannot be an integer'' is false as stated.)
