Connecting the midpoints divides off four corner right triangles, each with legs half the original side and therefore area one-eighth of the original square. The inner square has the remaining fraction $1-4\cdot\dfrac{1}{8}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Its area is $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}\text{ m}^2}$.
