Write $\angle B=\beta$, $\angle C=\gamma$, so $\beta+\gamma=30^\circ$. Because $PA=PB$, triangle $ABP$ is isosceles and $\angle BAP=\beta$. Similarly $\angle QAC=\gamma$. Thus the rays occur in the order $AB,AP,AQ,AC$, giving $\angle PAQ=120^\circ$ and $B,P,Q,C$ in that order.

Put $u=AP=BP$ and $v=AQ=CQ$. Then $u+v=BC-PQ=77$. The cosine rule in $APQ$ gives
\[
73^2=u^2+v^2+uv=(u+v)^2-uv,
\]
so $uv=77^2-73^2=600$. Therefore
\[
[APQ]=\dfrac{1}{2}uv\sin120^\circ=\boxed{150\sqrt3}.
\]

real u=(77-sqrt(3529))/2,v=77-u,w=73,t=(u*u+w*w-v*v)/(2*w),h=sqrt(u*u-t*t);pair B=(0,0),C=(150,0),P=(u,0),Q=(u+w,0),A=(u+t,h);draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(A--P,blue);draw(A--Q,blue);pair M=(A+B)/2,N=(A+C)/2;draw(M--P,dashed);draw(N--Q,dashed);label("$A$",A,(0,1));label("$B$",B,(-1,-1));label("$C$",C,(1,-1));label("$P$",P,(0,-1));label("$Q$",Q,(0,-1));label("$73$",(P+Q)/2,(0,-1));
\end{asy}
