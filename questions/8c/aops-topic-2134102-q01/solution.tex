Take the three parts separately.
\begin{itemize}
\item In $(x+6)^7$ the $x^3$ term is $\dbinom73x^3\,6^4$, with coefficient $35\cdot1296=45360$.
\item $(2x+3)^2$ has degree $2$, so it contributes $0$.
\item In $(4x+3)^3$ the $x^3$ term is $(4x)^3=64x^3$.
\end{itemize}
The coefficient is $45360+0+64=\boxed{45424}$.
