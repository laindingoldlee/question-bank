The three lengths must be positive and satisfy the triangle inequality (each side less than the sum of the other two):
\[
\begin{aligned}
2x+(3x+7)&>6x-5 &&\Longleftrightarrow\ x<12,\\
2x+(6x-5)&>3x+7 &&\Longleftrightarrow\ x>\dfrac{12}{5},\\
(3x+7)+(6x-5)&>2x &&\Longleftrightarrow\ x>-\dfrac{2}{7} .
\end{aligned}
\]
For $x>\dfrac{12}{5}$ all three lengths are positive. So $\dfrac{12}{5}<x<12$, and the integers are $x=3,4,\dots,11$: $\boxed{9}$ values. For example $x=3$ gives the triangle with sides $6$, $16$, $13$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0),C=(13,0); real xa=(16^2-6^2+13^2)/(2*13); pair A=(xa,sqrt(16^2-xa^2));
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
label("$6x-5=13$",(B+C)/2,S); label("$2x=6$",(A+C)/2+(0.5,0),E); label("$3x+7=16$",(A+B)/2,NW);
dot(A);dot(B);dot(C);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
