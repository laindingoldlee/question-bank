Choose $A=(0,0)$, $B=(25,0)$, $C=(25,16)$, $D=(0,16)$, so $E=(5,0)$. Since $EB=20$ and $BF=20$, if $F=(t,16)$ then $(25-t)^2+16^2=20^2$. The root on $CD$ is $t=13$. Thus
\[
EF=\sqrt{(13-5)^2+16^2}=\boxed{8\sqrt5}.
\]
The remaining kite vertex is consistent: $G=(0,\dfrac{25}{2})$ satisfies $GE=GF$ and lies on $AD$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(25,0),C=(25,16),D=(0,16),E=(5,0),F=(13,16),G=(0,12.5);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);filldraw(E--B--F--G--cycle,lightblue+opacity(.3),blue);draw(E--F,red);label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,NE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$E$",E,(0,-1));label("$F$",F,(0,1));label("$G$",G,(-1,0));
\end{asy}
