Let $\mathbb N=\{1,2,3,\dots\}$,
\[A=\{2n-3:n\in\mathbb N\}\qquad\text{and}\qquad B=\{801-9m:m\in\mathbb N\}.\]
Prove that $A\cap B=\{801-18k:k\in\mathbb N,\ k\le44\}$. How many elements does $A\cap B$ have?
