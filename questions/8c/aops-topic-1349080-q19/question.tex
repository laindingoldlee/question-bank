Seven lamps labelled $A$ through $G$ stand in a row, each with its own switch. Lamps $A,C,E,G$ are on and the others are off. Ava flips the switches one at a time in the order $A,B,C,D,E,F,G,A,B,C,\dots$, each flip changing its lamp from on to off or from off to on. Which lamps are on after $2011$ flips?
