Since $2011=7\cdot287+2$, Ava makes $287$ complete passes and then flips $A$ and $B$ once more. Each complete pass flips every lamp once, and $287$ is odd, so after the complete passes every lamp is reversed: $B,D,F$ are on and $A,C,E,G$ are off. The last two flips turn $A$ on and $B$ off. The lamps that are on are $\boxed{A,\ D,\ F}$.
