Since $985^{998}<1000^{998}=10^{2994}$, its first digit sum is at most $9\cdot2994=26946$. The next sum is at most 45, the third at most 12, and the fourth at most 9. All are positive and preserve the residue modulo 9. Now $985\equiv4\pmod9$ and $4^{998}\equiv4^2\equiv7\pmod9$, since 4 has period 3. The only positive integer at most 9 with this residue is $\boxed7$.
