Bart takes a $31$-question test, answering each question correctly with probability $\dfrac12$, independently. He advances to the next round if he answers at least $24$ questions correctly. The next round has $21$ questions, again each answered correctly with probability $\dfrac12$ independently, and he advances again if he answers at least $16$ correctly. The final round has $10$ questions, with the same chances, and if he answers at least $8$ correctly he wins a trip to the International Space Station. What is the probability that Bart wins the trip?
