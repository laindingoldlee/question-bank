The square root is increasing, so we must minimise $a+\sqrt b$. Since $a\ge4$ and $\sqrt b\ge2$, with $a\ne b$ the candidates using the two smallest numbers are
\[
a=4,\ b=5:\ 4+\sqrt5\approx6.24,\qquad a=5,\ b=4:\ 5+\sqrt4=7 .
\]
Any other choice has $a\ge5$ and $b\ge4$ with one of them larger, or $a=4$ and $b\ge6$, and is larger than $4+\sqrt5$. So the minimum is at $a=4$, $b=5$, and $a-b=\boxed{-1}$.
