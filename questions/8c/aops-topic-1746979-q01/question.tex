Let $a,b,c,d,e$ be constants, with $b\ne c$ and $a\ne0$, in the system
\[
ax+by=d,\qquad ax+cy=e .
\]
A student thinks the system might have no solution. Prove that it always has exactly one solution $(x,y)$, and find it.
