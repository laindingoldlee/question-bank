Cauchy-Schwarz gives
\[
\dfrac{a+b}{c}+\dfrac{b+c}{a}+\dfrac{c+a}{b}
=(a+b+c)\left(\dfrac{1}{a}+\dfrac{1}{b}+\dfrac{1}{c}\right)-3\ge6.
\]
Since $\lfloor t\rfloor>t-1$ for every real $t$, the displayed integer sum is strictly greater than $6-3=3$ and hence at least $4$. Equality is attained at $(a,b,c)=(4,4,3)$, giving floors $2,1,1$. Thus the minimum is $\boxed4$.
