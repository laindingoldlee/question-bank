Define $\{p_n\}_{n=0}^{\infty} \subset \mathbb{N}$ and $\{q_n\}_{n=0}^{\infty} \subset \mathbb{N}$ to be sequences of natural numbers as follows:

$p_0=q_0=1$; For all $n\in\mathbb{N}$, $q_n$ is the smallest natural number such that there exists a natural number $p_n$ with $\gcd(p_n,q_n)=1$ satisfying

\[
\dfrac{p_{n-1}}{q_{n-1}}<\dfrac{p_n}{q_n}<\sqrt{2}.
\]

Find $q_3$.
