Compute only modulo $10$. The units digits through the first zero are
\[
1,1,2,3,5,8,3,1,4,5,9,4,3,7,1,8,9,7,6,5,1,6,7,3,0.
\]
Each is the sum of the preceding two, reduced modulo $10$. By the nineteenth term all nonzero digits have appeared, while zero first appears at the twenty-fifth term. Thus the answer is $\boxed0$, choice $\boxed{\text{A}}$.
