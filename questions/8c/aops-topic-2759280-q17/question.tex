Six circles are drawn with centres at the vertices of a regular hexagon, each passing through the centre of the hexagon. Thirteen intersection points result: the centre, the six vertices, and six further points. The six further points form another hexagon. Its area is how many times the area of the original hexagon?

$\textbf{(A)}\ 2\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ \dfrac94\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ 3\qquad\textbf{(D)}\ \dfrac{16}9\qquad\textbf{(E)}\ 4$
