The minute hand gains on the hour hand at $1-\dfrac{1}{12}=\dfrac{11}{12}$ revolution per hour. To gain one complete revolution takes $\boxed{\dfrac{12}{11}\text{ hours}}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
draw(unitcircle);for(int k=0;k<12;++k)label(string(k==0?12:k),1.2*dir(90-30*k));draw((0,0)--.9*dir(90-360/11),blue);draw((0,0)--.6*dir(90-360/11),red+linewidth(2));dot((0,0));
\end{asy}
