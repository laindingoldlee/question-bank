Some positive integers are written on a blackboard. A move erases two numbers $a,b$ such that neither divides the other and replaces them with $\gcd(a,b)$ and $\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)$. Prove that the process always stops, and that the final set of numbers does not depend on the choice of moves.
