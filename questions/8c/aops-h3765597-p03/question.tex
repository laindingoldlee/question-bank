Maksat and Daulet each deposit $30000$ tenge in a bank for one year. Maksat chooses the plan ``Confidence'': the deposit increases by $20\%$ over a year, and upon withdrawal the bank takes a $10\%$ commission. Daulet chooses the plan ``Reliability'': the deposit increases by $40\%$ over a year, and upon withdrawal the bank takes a $20\%$ commission. Who earns a greater annual profit, and by how much? If the profits are equal, choose $0$.
