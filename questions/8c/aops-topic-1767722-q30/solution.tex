Place $A=(0,0)$, $B=(6,0)$, $C=(0,8)$. Then $\overrightarrow{BC}=(-6,8)$, and the perpendicular vector of the same length pointing away from $A$ is $(8,6)$. So the square has vertices $D=C+(8,6)=(8,14)$ and $E=B+(8,6)=(14,6)$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(6,0),C=(0,8),D=(8,14),Ee=(14,6);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));draw(B--C--D--Ee--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(A--D,blue);draw(A--Ee,blue);draw(D--Ee,blue+linewidth(0.9));
draw((0.6,0)--(0.6,0.6)--(0,0.6));
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,S);label("$C$",C,W);label("$D$",D,N);label("$E$",Ee,E);
label("$6$",(A+B)/2,S);label("$8$",(A+C)/2,W);label("$10$",C+0.3*(B-C),SW);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The area of triangle $ADE$ is
\[
\dfrac{1}{2}\left|x_Dy_E-x_Ey_D\right|=\dfrac{1}{2}\left|8\cdot6-14\cdot14\right|=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot148=\boxed{74}.
\]
Without coordinates: the distance from $A$ to line $DE$ equals the distance from $A$ to line $BC$ (the altitude $\dfrac{6\cdot8}{10}=4.8$) plus the side $10$ of the square, so $[ADE]=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot10\cdot14.8=74$.
