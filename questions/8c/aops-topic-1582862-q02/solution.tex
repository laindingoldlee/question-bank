The merry-go-round makes $\dfrac{30}{10}=3$ revolutions per minute. Audrey moves on a circle of radius $8$ m and Anna on a circle of radius $8-3=5$ m:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0);
draw(circle(O,8),linewidth(0.9));draw(circle(O,5),linewidth(0.9));
dot(O);dot((8,0));dot((5,0));
draw(O--(8,0));label("$5$",(2.5,0),S);label("$3$",(6.5,0),S);
label("Anna",(2.7,0.95),fontsize(8pt));label("Audrey",(8,0),E,fontsize(8pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

\textbf{Audrey:} $3\cdot2\pi\cdot8=48\pi\approx150.80$ m per minute, about $\boxed{151\text{ m per minute}}$.

\textbf{Anna:} $3\cdot2\pi\cdot5=30\pi\approx94.25$ m per minute, about $\boxed{94\text{ m per minute}}$.

(Both make the same number of turns, but Audrey's circle is longer, so she is faster.)
