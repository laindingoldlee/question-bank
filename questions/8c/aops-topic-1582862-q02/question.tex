Audrey rides a merry-go-round on a horse at its outer edge, $8$ metres from the centre. Her sister Anna sits in the inner ring of horses, $3$ metres closer to the centre than Audrey. In $10$ minutes they go around $30$ times. To the nearest metre per minute, what is the average speed of each sister? Use $3.14159$ for $\pi$.
