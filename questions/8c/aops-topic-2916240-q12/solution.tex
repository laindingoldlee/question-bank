If their car counts are $j,n\ge0$, then $11n-6j=1$. Modulo 6, $-n\equiv1$, so $n\equiv5\pmod6$. The least possible $n$ is 5, attained with $j=9$. Nash earns $\boxed{55\text{ dollars}}$.
