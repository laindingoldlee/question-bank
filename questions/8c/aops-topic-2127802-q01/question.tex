An ant crawls along the edge of a circular disc of radius $5$ metres at a constant speed of $5\pi$ centimetres per minute. Each time it completes a quarter of the circle, it gets tired and sleeps for $3$ minutes before going on. The ant starts at time $0$ and stops the moment it completes $3$ full rounds of the circle. How many minutes does the trip take?
