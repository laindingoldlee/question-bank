The circumference is $2\pi\cdot5=10\pi$ metres $=1000\pi$ cm, so a quarter of the circle is $250\pi$ cm, which takes
\[\dfrac{250\pi}{5\pi}=50\text{ minutes}.\]

\begin{asy}

pair[] Q={dir(0),dir(90),dir(180),dir(270)};

label("nap",dir(90),N,red+fontsize(8pt)); label("nap",dir(180),W,red+fontsize(8pt)); label("nap",dir(270),S,red+fontsize(8pt));

Three rounds are $12$ quarters, so the ant crawls for $12\cdot50=600$ minutes. It naps after each of the first $11$ quarters, but not after the $12$th, because the trip ends as soon as the third round is complete. The naps take $11\cdot3=33$ minutes, so the whole trip takes
\[600+33=\boxed{633}\text{ minutes}.\]
(Counting a nap after the last quarter as well would give $636$ minutes; the fencepost count of $11$ gaps between $12$ quarters is the point of the problem.)
