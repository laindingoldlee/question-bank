\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] An $l\times w$ rectangle, where $l$ and $w$ are positive integers, is divided into $1\times1$ squares. Through how many of the squares does a diagonal of the rectangle pass (that is, how many squares contain points of the diagonal in their interior)?
\item[(b)] An $l\times w\times d$ rectangular box, where $l,w,d$ are positive integers, is divided into $1\times1\times1$ cubes. Through how many of the cubes does a space diagonal of the box pass?
\end{enumerate}
Give the answers in terms of greatest common divisors.
