Starting from a whole number, a move either adds $1$, subtracts $1$ (from a positive number), or reverses the order of the digits (leading zeros are dropped, so reversing $790$ gives $97$ and reversing $500$ gives $5$). We want to get from $500$ to $83$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What is the least number of moves needed if at most one of the moves is a reversal?
\item[(b)] Find a way to get from $500$ to $83$ in only $14$ moves.
\end{enumerate}
