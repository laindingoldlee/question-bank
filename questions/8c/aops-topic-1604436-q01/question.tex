A donut has the shape of a regular octagonal prism of height $6$ whose base edges have length $3$, with a cylindrical hole of radius $3$ and height $6$ cut through the middle (the axis of the hole is the axis of the prism). What is the volume of the donut? Give the exact value and a decimal approximation to the nearest tenth.
