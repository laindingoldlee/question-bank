\textbf{The octagonal base.} A regular octagon of side $s$ is a square of side $s+2\cdot\dfrac{s}{\sqrt2}=s\left(1+\sqrt2\right)$ with four isosceles right triangles of legs $\dfrac{s}{\sqrt2}$ cut from its corners, so its area is
\[
s^2\left(1+\sqrt2\right)^2-4\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{s^2}{2}=s^2\left(3+2\sqrt2-1\right)=2\left(1+\sqrt2\right)s^2 .
\]
With $s=3$ the base has area $18\left(1+\sqrt2\right)$, and the prism has volume $6\cdot18\left(1+\sqrt2\right)=108\left(1+\sqrt2\right)$.

\textbf{The hole fits.} The distance $\rho$ from the centre of the octagon to each side is half the width of the square above, $\rho=\dfrac{3\left(1+\sqrt2\right)}{2}\approx3.62>3$, so the hole of radius $3$ lies inside the prism (it does not touch the sides, so it is not literally inscribed):

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=3; real R=s/(2*sin(pi/8)); real rin=R*cos(pi/8);
pair[] V; for(int k=0;k<8;++k) V[k]=R*dir(22.5+45*k);
path oct=V[0]--V[1]--V[2]--V[3]--V[4]--V[5]--V[6]--V[7]--cycle;
fill(oct,palegray); filldraw(circle((0,0),3),white,linewidth(0.9)); draw(oct,linewidth(0.9));
dot((0,0)); draw((0,0)--3*dir(200),blue); label("$3$",1.5*dir(200),dir(110),blue);
draw((0,0)--rin*dir(0),red+dashed); label("$\rho$",(1.6,0),N,red);
label("$3$",(V[1]+V[2])/2,N);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

\textbf{The volume.} The hole has volume $\pi\cdot3^2\cdot6=54\pi$, so the donut has volume
\[
\boxed{108+108\sqrt2-54\pi}\approx91.1 .
\]
