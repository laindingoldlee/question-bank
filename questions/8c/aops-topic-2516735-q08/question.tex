A certain glue sticks to only $60\%$ of the objects in my house: each object, independently, is one it sticks to with probability $\dfrac35$. Two objects stay stuck together only if the glue sticks to both of them. If I choose two objects at random and glue them together, what is the probability that they stay stuck together?
