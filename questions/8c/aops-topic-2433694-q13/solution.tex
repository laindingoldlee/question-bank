Keiko gets $0$ or $1$ head, each with probability $\dfrac12$. Ephraim gets $0$ heads with probability $\dfrac14$ and $1$ head with probability $\dfrac24=\dfrac12$. The tosses are independent, so
\[P(\text{same})=\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac14+\dfrac12\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac38.\]
The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(B)}\ \dfrac38}$.
