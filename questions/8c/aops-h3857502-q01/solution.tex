The absolute sine values repeat every $45$ terms, because increasing $k$ by $45$ adds $180^\circ$. There are $45$ complete blocks.
The sine-product identity
\[
\left|\prod_{j=0}^{m-1}\sin\left(\theta+\dfrac{j\pi}{m}\right)\right|
=2^{1-m}|\sin(m\theta)|
\]
follows by factoring $z^m-1$ over its roots of unity and taking absolute values, with $z=e^{2i\theta}$. Taking $m=45$ and $\theta=1^\circ$ gives
\[
\prod_{j=0}^{44}|\sin(4j+1)^\circ|
=2^{-44}\sin45^\circ=2^{-\dfrac{89}{2}}.
\]
Thus each block contributes $\log_4(2^{\dfrac{89}{2}})=\dfrac{89}{4}$, and the entire sum is $45\cdot\dfrac{89}{4}=1001.25$. Therefore $N=1001$, whose remainder modulo $1000$ is $\boxed{1}$.
