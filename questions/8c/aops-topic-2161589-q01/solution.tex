\textbf{(a)} $i^1=i$, $i^2=-1$, $i^3=i^2\cdot i=-i$ and $i^4=\left(i^2\right)^2=1$. Since $i^4=1$, the powers repeat with period $4$: $i^{n+4}=i^n\cdot i^4=i^n$. So $i^n$ is $i,-1,-i,1$ according as $n$ leaves remainder $1,2,3,0$ on division by $4$.

\textbf{(b)} Expanding and using $i^2=-1$,
\[3(3-i)+i(i+6)=9-3i+i^2+6i=9-3i-1+6i=\boxed{8+3i}.\]
