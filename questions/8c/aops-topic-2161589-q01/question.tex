Let $i$ be the imaginary unit, so $i^2=-1$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find $i^1$, $i^2$, $i^3$ and $i^4$, and explain how to find $i^n$ for any positive integer $n$.
\item[(b)] Simplify $3(3-i)+i(i+6)$.
\end{enumerate}
