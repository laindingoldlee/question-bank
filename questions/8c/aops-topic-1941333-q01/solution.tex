Let $z=a+bi$ with $a,b$ real. Then $z^2=a^2-b^2+2abi$, so
\[
a^2-b^2=21,\qquad 2ab=-20 .
\]
Also $|z|^2=|z^2|=\sqrt{21^2+20^2}=29$, so $a^2+b^2=29$. Adding and subtracting, $a^2=25$ and $b^2=4$. Since $ab=-10<0$, $a$ and $b$ have opposite signs: $(a,b)=(5,-2)$ or $(-5,2)$. So $z=\boxed{\pm(5-2i)}$. Check: $(5-2i)^2=25-20i+4i^2=21-20i$.
