Let $k>l\ge1$ be integers, and let $a_1,\dots,a_k$ and $b_1,\dots,b_l$ be real numbers such that $a_i>b_j$ for every $i$ and every $j$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show by an example that $a_1+a_2+\cdots+a_k>b_1+b_2+\cdots+b_l$ need not hold.
\item[(b)] Prove that it does hold if, in addition, all the $a_i$ are nonnegative.
\end{enumerate}
