$\boxed{\text{No}}$. Take $f(t)=-t$, $x=1$, $y=-1$: then $f(x)=-1=y$ and $f(y)=1=x$, but $x\ne y$. (It is true when $f$ is strictly increasing: if $x<y$ then $y=f(x)<f(y)=x$, a contradiction.)
