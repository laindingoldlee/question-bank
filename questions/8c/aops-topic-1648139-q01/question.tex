Points $A$ and $B$ are opposite corners of a $3$ by $3$ grid of $1$ by $1$ squares, with $A$ at the top left and $B$ at the bottom right. A step is a move of one unit along a grid line. How many $6$-step paths are there from $A$ to $B$ if each step goes either one unit to the right or one unit down?
