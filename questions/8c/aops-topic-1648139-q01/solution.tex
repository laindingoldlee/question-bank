Every such path makes exactly $3$ steps to the right and $3$ steps down, in some order, and every arrangement of $3$ R's and $3$ D's gives a different valid path that stays on the grid. The number of paths is the number of ways to choose which $3$ of the $6$ steps go right:
\[
\dbinom63=\boxed{20}.
\]
