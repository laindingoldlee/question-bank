Modulo $8$, the even exponent gives residue $1$. Modulo $125$, Euler's theorem permits reducing the exponent modulo $100$, giving exponent $90$. Repeated squaring yields
\[
7^2\equiv49,\quad7^4\equiv26,\quad7^8\equiv51,\quad
7^{16}\equiv101,\quad7^{32}\equiv76,\quad7^{64}\equiv26\pmod{125}.
\]
Thus $7^{90}=7^{64+16+8+2}\equiv26\cdot101\cdot51\cdot49\equiv124\pmod{125}$.
The residues $124+125k$ for $0\le k\le7$ are $1$ modulo $8$ exactly when $4+5k\equiv1\pmod8$, namely $k=1$. The last three digits are $\boxed{249}$.
