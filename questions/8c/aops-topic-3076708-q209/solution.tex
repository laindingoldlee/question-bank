First distributivity shows $u(-v)=-(uv)$ because $uv+u(-v)=u(v-v)=0$. Commutativity gives the analogous identity $(-u)v=-(uv)$. Applying these twice yields $(-a)(-b)=-((-a)b)=-(-(ab))=ab$.
