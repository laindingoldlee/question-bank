At time $0$, player $P$ stands at the origin. Player $C$ is at $(10,0)$ and walks east at $1$ unit per hour; player $Q$ is at $(0,-7)$ and walks south at $\dfrac32$ units per hour; player $B$ is at $(0,5)$ and walks east at $2$ units per hour. Player $P$ can walk in any direction at $\dfrac52$ units per hour, and wants to get within $1$ unit of one of the other three as soon as possible. Which player can $P$ reach soonest, and after how many hours?
