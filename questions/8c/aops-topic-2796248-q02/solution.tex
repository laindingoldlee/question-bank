Write $x=10a+b$ with $a,b\in\{1,\dots,9\}$ (since $y$ has two digits, $b\ne0$). Then $z=11(a+b)$, with $2\le a+b\le18$. For $z$ to be a perfect square, $11\mid a+b$ is needed, so $a+b=11$ and $z=121$.

The pairs $(a,b)$ with $a+b=11$ are $(2,9),(3,8),\dots,(9,2)$: $\boxed{8}$ values of $x$.
