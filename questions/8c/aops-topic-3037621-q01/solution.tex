Subtract the fifth equation from the fourth to get $ A=2 $. Then $ B+C=29 $, so the first equation gives $ D+E=38 $. The third gives $ E-D=4 $, yielding $ E=21,D=17 $. The fourth now gives $ B-C=-3 $; together with their sum 29 this gives $ B=13,C=16 $. Thus $\boxed{(A,B,C,D,E)=(2,13,16,17,21)}$. These values are distinct and substitution verifies every equation.
