Any adjacent ordered pair is uniformly distributed among the $30$ ordered pairs of distinct numbers. For one pair, the sum of squared differences over unordered pairs is $\sum_{h=1}^5(6-h)h^2=105$. Thus the average squared difference is $\dfrac{2\cdot105}{30}=7$. There are five terms, so linearity of expectation gives $\boxed{35}$.
