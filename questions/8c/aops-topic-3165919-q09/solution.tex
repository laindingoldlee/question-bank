Use $O=(0,0)$, $A=(1,0)$, $B=(0,1)$, $M=(\dfrac{1}{2},0)$, $N=(0,\dfrac{1}{2})$. Lines $AN,BM$ have equations $x+2y=1$ and $2x+y=1$, so $X=(\dfrac{1}{3},\dfrac{1}{3})$. The quarter-disk area is $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$, and the removed triangles $OAX,OBX$ each have area $\dfrac{1}{6}$. The answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{\pi}{4}-\dfrac{1}{3}}$.
