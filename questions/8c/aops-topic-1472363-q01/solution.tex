Factor with the difference of squares and the sum and difference of cubes:
\[
x^6-1=(x^3-1)(x^3+1)=(x-1)(x^2+x+1)(x+1)(x^2-x+1).
\]
The quadratic formula gives the roots of $x^2+x+1$ as $\dfrac{-1\pm i\sqrt3}{2}$ and of $x^2-x+1$ as $\dfrac{1\pm i\sqrt3}{2}$. So the six roots are
\[
\boxed{x=1,\ -1,\ \dfrac{1}{2}\pm\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\,i,\ -\dfrac{1}{2}\pm\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\,i}
\]
These are the sixth roots of unity $\cos(60^\circ k)+i\sin(60^\circ k)$, $k=0,1,\dots,5$; only $\pm1$ are real.
