Two numbers have product $9$ and sum $12$. What is the positive difference between them? A student argued: from $(a+b)^2=144$ and $2ab=18$ we get $a^2+b^2=126$; then $(a+b)(a-b)=126$, so $12(a-b)=126$ and $a-b=\dfrac{21}{2}$. Find the correct difference and the error in this argument.
