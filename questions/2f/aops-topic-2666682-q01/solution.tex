Let $z_n=x_n+iy_n$. Then
\[\left(1+i\sqrt3\right)z_{n-1}=x_{n-1}-\sqrt3y_{n-1}+i\left(\sqrt3x_{n-1}+y_{n-1}\right)=z_n.\]
Since $z_1=1$ and $1+i\sqrt3=2\left(\cos60^\circ+i\sin60^\circ\right)$, De Moivre's theorem gives
\[z_{49}=\left(1+i\sqrt3\right)^{48}=2^{48}\left(\cos2880^\circ+i\sin2880^\circ\right)=2^{48},\]
because $2880^\circ=8\cdot360^\circ$. Hence $x_{49}=\boxed{2^{48}=281474976710656}$ and $y_{49}=\boxed{0}$.
