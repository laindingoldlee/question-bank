Moving everything to one side, $(x+2)\left[(x+3)-(x+17)\right]=0$, i.e. $-14(x+2)=0$. So $x=\boxed{-2}$ is the only solution. (Dividing by $x+2$ would give the impossible $x+3=x+17$ and lose this solution.)
