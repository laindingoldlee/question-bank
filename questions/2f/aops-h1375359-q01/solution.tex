The radius to a point of tangency is perpendicular to the tangent, so $OPT$ is right at $T$. Hence $PT=\sqrt{10^2-6^2}=\boxed8$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair O=(0,0),T=(3.6,4.8),P=(10,0);draw(circle(O,6));draw(O--P--T--O);label("O",O,SW);label("T",T,N);label("P",P,E);label("6",T/2,NW);label("10",(5,0),S);
\end{asy}
