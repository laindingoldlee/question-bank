Let the integers be $n$ and $n+1$. Then
\[(n+1)^3-n^3=3n^2+3n+1,\]
and the condition is
\[3n^2+3n+1=5n(n+1)-111=5n^2+5n-111.\]
This simplifies to $2n^2+2n-112=0$, i.e. $n^2+n-56=(n+8)(n-7)=0$. Since $n>0$, $n=7$, and the integers are $7$ and $8$. (Check: $512-343=169=5\cdot56-111$.) Their sum is
\[\boxed{15}.\]
