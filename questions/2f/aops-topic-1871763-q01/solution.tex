\textbf{(I)} Each of the five people independently chooses one of $7$ floors, so there are $7^5=16807$ ways.

\textbf{(II)} The first person has $7$ floors to choose from, the second $6$ remaining floors, and so on: $7\cdot6\cdot5\cdot4\cdot3=2520$ ways.

The answers are $\boxed{16807\text{ and }2520}$.
