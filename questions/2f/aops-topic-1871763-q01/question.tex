Five people enter a lift on the ground floor of a building with $7$ floors above the ground floor. Each of them leaves the lift at one of the floors $1,2,\dots,7$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(I)] In how many ways can the five people leave the lift?
\item[(II)] In how many ways can they leave if no two of them leave at the same floor?
\end{enumerate}
