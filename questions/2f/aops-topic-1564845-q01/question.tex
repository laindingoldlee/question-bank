The cookies in a jar contain a total of $1000$ chocolate chips. All but one of the cookies contain the same number of chips; the remaining cookie contains one more chip than each of the others. The number of cookies in the jar is between one dozen and three dozen. What is the sum of the number of cookies in the jar and the number of chips in the cookie with the extra chip?
