Let there be $n$ cookies, $n-1$ of them with $c$ chips and one with $c+1$. Then $nc+1=1000$, so $nc=999=3^3\cdot37$. The divisors of $999$ are $1,3,9,27,37,111,333,999$, and the only one between $12$ and $36$ is $n=27$, giving $c=37$. The special cookie has $38$ chips, and the answer is $27+38=\boxed{65}$.
