Between any two consecutive cats there must be at least one dog. With $1001$ cats this needs at least $1000$ dogs, and there are exactly $1000$. So the pattern must be cat, dog, cat, dog, $\dots$, cat: cats in the odd cages and dogs in the even cages.

The cats can be arranged in their cages in $1001!$ ways and the dogs in $1000!$ ways, for $\boxed{1001!\cdot1000!}$ arrangements.
