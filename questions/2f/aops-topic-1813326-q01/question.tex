Two cars leave cities $A$ and $B$ at the same time, driving towards each other at constant speeds, and they meet after $5$ hours. The car from $A$ is $10$ km per hour slower than the car from $B$. If instead the car from $A$ had left $4.5$ hours before the car from $B$, they would have met $150$ km from $B$. Find the distance between $A$ and $B$.
