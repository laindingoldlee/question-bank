Put $x=AB=CD$ and $y=BC$. The angle-bisector theorem gives
\[
\dfrac{y-x}{x}=\dfrac{x}{2007},\qquad
y=x+\dfrac{x^2}{2007}.
\]
Thus $2007=3^2\cdot223$ divides $x^2$, so $x=669t$ for a positive integer $t$. Then $y=223t(t+3)$.

The inequality $x+2007>y$ implies $x<2007$, so $t=1$ or $2$. At $t=1$, the proposed sides include $x=669,y=892$, whose sum is less than $2007$, so no triangle exists. At $t=2$, $x=1338,y=2230$, and all triangle inequalities hold. Therefore
\[
\boxed{AB=1338,\qquad BC=2230}.
\]
