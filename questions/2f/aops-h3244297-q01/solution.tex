Since $2004=2^2\cdot3\cdot167$, the required exponent is
\[
\min\left(\left\lfloor\dfrac{v_2(2004!)}{2}\right\rfloor,v_3(2004!),v_{167}(2004!)\right).
\]
The last valuation is $\lfloor\dfrac{2004}{167}\rfloor=12$, since $167^2>2004$. The first two valuations are already at least $1002$ and $668$ respectively from their first terms alone. Hence the minimum is $\boxed{12}$.
