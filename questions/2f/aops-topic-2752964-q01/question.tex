A student argues: ``Since $632\equiv2\pmod{10}$, it follows that $632\equiv2\pmod 2$ and $632\equiv2\pmod5$. But $632\equiv0\pmod2$, so something is wrong.'' Is there a contradiction? Explain, and give the least nonnegative residues of $632$ modulo $2$, $5$ and $10$.
