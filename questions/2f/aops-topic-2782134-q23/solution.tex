The loss is $\sum_{n=1}^{48}(n+1)=\dfrac{48\cdot49}{2}+48=1224 $. A run of $ w $ wins gains $\sum_{n=1}^w(2n+1)=w^2+2w $. Thus $(w+1)^2=1225=35^2 $, giving $\boxed{w=34}$.
