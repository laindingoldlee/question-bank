A square metal plate of side length $2$ feet and negligible thickness rests on a table top parallel to the ground, and a point source of light is suspended $2$ feet above the centre of the plate. While one edge of the plate remains on the table, the opposite edge is lifted until the plate makes a $45^\circ$ angle with the table top. What is the area, in square feet, of the shadow cast by the plate on the table top?

$\textbf{(A) }6\sqrt2\qquad\textbf{(B) }6+3\sqrt2\qquad\textbf{(C) }5+4\sqrt2\qquad\textbf{(D) }8\sqrt2\qquad\textbf{(E) }6+4\sqrt2$
