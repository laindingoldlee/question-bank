Set up coordinates with the table as the plane $z=0$, the plate originally at $-1\le x\le1$, $-1\le y\le1$, and the light at $L=(0,0,2)$. The edge $x=-1$ stays on the table and the plate turns about it, so the lifted edge goes to $x=-1+\sqrt2$, $z=\sqrt2$, $-1\le y\le1$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=sqrt(2);
pair L=(0,2), H=(-1,0), T=(-1+s,s), Sh=(s,0);
draw((-1.8,0)--(2.2,0),linewidth(0.9));
draw(H--T,linewidth(2));
draw(L--Sh,red+dashed); draw(L--H,red+dashed);
draw((0,0)--L,gray+dotted);
dot(L); dot(Sh); dot(T); dot(H);
draw(arc(H,0.35,0,45));
label("$45^\circ$",H+0.55*dir(22),fontsize(8pt));
label("light",L,N); label("$2$",(0,0.45),E,gray);
label("$2$",(H+T)/2,SE);
label("$x=-1$",H,S); label("$x=\sqrt2$",Sh,S,red);
label("$(\sqrt2-1,\sqrt2)$",T,E,fontsize(8pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

A point $(p,q,h)$ with $h<2$ casts its shadow where the ray from $L$ through it meets $z=0$; by similar triangles this is $\left(\dfrac{2p}{2-h},\dfrac{2q}{2-h},0\right)$.
\begin{itemize}
\item The edge on the table ($h=0$) is its own shadow: the segment $x=-1$, $-1\le y\le1$, of length $2$.
\item The lifted edge has $h=\sqrt2$ and $\dfrac{2}{2-\sqrt2}=2+\sqrt2$, so its shadow is at $x=(2+\sqrt2)(\sqrt2-1)=\sqrt2$, with $-(2+\sqrt2)\le y\le2+\sqrt2$: length $4+2\sqrt2$.
\end{itemize}
The plate is flat, so its shadow is the quadrilateral spanned by these two parallel segments: a trapezoid with parallel sides $2$ and $4+2\sqrt2$ and height $\sqrt2-(-1)=1+\sqrt2$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real s=sqrt(2), w=2+s;
filldraw((-1,-1)--(s,-w)--(s,w)--(-1,1)--cycle,gray(0.85),black);
draw((-1,-1)--(-1+s,-1)--(-1+s,1)--(-1,1),blue);
label("$2$",(-1,0),W); label("$4+2\sqrt2$",(s,0),E);
draw((-1,-w-0.4)--(s,-w-0.4),Arrows(4)); label("$1+\sqrt2$",((-1+s)/2,-w-0.4),S);
label("plate",(-1+s/2,0),fontsize(8pt)+blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

\[
\text{Area}=\dfrac{2+4+2\sqrt2}{2}\,(1+\sqrt2)=(3+\sqrt2)(1+\sqrt2)=3+3\sqrt2+\sqrt2+2=\boxed{\textbf{(C) }5+4\sqrt2}.
\]
(The value $2+\sqrt2$ obtained by working only in the side view is wrong: the side view gives the depth $1+\sqrt2$ of the shadow, but the shadow also widens from $2$ to $4+2\sqrt2$ across the table.)
