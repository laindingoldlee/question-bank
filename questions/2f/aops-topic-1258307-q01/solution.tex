We need a number $N$ such that \emph{every} basket of $N$ fruits meets one of the conditions. A basket with $7$ apples, $5$ bananas and $8$ oranges ($20$ fruits) meets none of them, so $N\ge21$. With $21$ fruits, if there were at most $7$ apples, $5$ bananas and $8$ oranges, there would be at most $20$ fruits; so one condition holds. The answer is $\boxed{21}$.

A basket of $8$ fruits \emph{can} satisfy a condition (for example $6$ bananas), but it does not \emph{guarantee} one, which is what the question asks.
