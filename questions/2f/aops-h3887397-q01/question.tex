A snail starts at $M=(-5,-4)$ on the graph of
\[
f(x)=\begin{cases}x+1,&x<-2,\\-1-\sqrt{4-x^2},&-2\le x\le2,\\x-2,&2<x\le3.\end{cases}
\]
It must follow a continuous path along the graph. Let $N$ be the reachable point with largest $x$-coordinate. Find the shortest distance the snail must travel from $M$ to $N$, to the nearest tenth.
