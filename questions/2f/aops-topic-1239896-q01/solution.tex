Let $A_i$ be the set of arrangements in which person $i$ stays in place. An intersection of $k$ of these sets fixes $k$ people and has $(n-k)!$ arrangements. By inclusion--exclusion,
\[D_n=n!-\dbinom n1(n-1)!+\dbinom n2(n-2)!-\cdots=\boxed{n!\sum_{k=0}^n\dfrac{(-1)^k}{k!}}.\]
(So $D_1,D_2,\dots=0,1,2,9,44,265,\dots$; also $D_n=(n-1)(D_{n-1}+D_{n-2})$, and $D_n$ is the integer nearest $\dfrac{n!}{e}$.)
