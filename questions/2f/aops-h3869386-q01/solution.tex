Tangent-secant power from $B$ gives $BA\cdot BE=BD^2$, so $AB=\dfrac{81}{3}=27$. Similarly $CA\cdot CF=CD^2$ gives $AC=\dfrac{256}{8}=32$. With $BC=25$, Heron's formula gives
\[
[ABC]=\sqrt{42\cdot15\cdot10\cdot17}=30\sqrt{119}.
\]
Also $AE=27-3=24$ and $AF=32-8=24$. Triangles $AEF$ and $ABC$ share angle $A$, so their area ratio is
\[
\dfrac{[AEF]}{[ABC]}=\dfrac{24\cdot24}{27\cdot32}=\dfrac{2}{3}.
\]
Therefore $[BCFE]=\dfrac{[ABC]}{3}=10\sqrt{119}$, giving $\boxed{m+n=129}$.
