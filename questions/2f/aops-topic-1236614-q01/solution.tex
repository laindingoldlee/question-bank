By the rational root theorem a rational root must be $\pm1$; but $1-3-1=-3$ and $-1+3-1=1$. So there are $\boxed{0}$ rational roots: (A). (All three roots are real and irrational, namely $2\cos20^\circ$, $2\cos140^\circ$, $2\cos260^\circ$.)
