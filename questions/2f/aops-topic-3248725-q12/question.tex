For each of two square sheets $A,B$, repeat four times: fold the bottom side to the top side, then the right side to the left side. Cut all layers of folded sheet $A$ along its top-left to bottom-right diagonal; cut all layers of folded sheet $B$ along the other diagonal. Unfold all pieces. How many pieces result in total?
