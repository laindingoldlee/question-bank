The three means give
\[a+b+c=3\cdot8=24,\qquad abc=5^3=125,\qquad \dfrac{3}{\dfrac{1}{a}+\dfrac{1}{b}+\dfrac{1}{c}}=3,\ \text{ so }\ \dfrac{1}{a}+\dfrac{1}{b}+\dfrac{1}{c}=1 .\]
Since $\dfrac{1}{a}+\dfrac{1}{b}+\dfrac{1}{c}=\dfrac{ab+bc+ca}{abc}$, we get $ab+bc+ca=abc=125$. Therefore
\[a^2+b^2+c^2=(a+b+c)^2-2(ab+bc+ca)=576-250=\boxed{326}.\]
(Such numbers exist: they are the roots of $t^3-24t^2+125t-125$, which is $-23$, $37$, $25$, $-275$, $775$ at $t=1,2,5,10,20$, so it has three positive real roots.)
