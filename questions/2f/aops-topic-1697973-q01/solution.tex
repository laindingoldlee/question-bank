The probability of landing on $k$ is $ck$ for a constant $c$. The probabilities add to $1$, so $c(1+2+\cdots+9)=45c=1$ and $c=\dfrac{1}{45}$. The odd labels have total $1+3+5+7+9=25$, so the probability is $\dfrac{25}{45}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{9}}$.
