The nine regions on a spinner are numbered with the positive integers $1$ through $9$, and the probability of the spinner landing on a given region is proportional to the label for that region. For example, since $4\times2=8$, the probability of landing on $8$ is twice the probability of landing on $4$. What is the probability that this spinner will land on an odd number? Express your answer as a common fraction.
