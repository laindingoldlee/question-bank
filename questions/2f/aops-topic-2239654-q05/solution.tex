This is the Fibonacci sequence shifted by one place: $F_1,F_2,F_3,\ldots=0,1,1,2,3,5,\ldots$. Mod $5$ the terms are
\[0,1,1,2,3,0,3,3,1,4,0,4,4,3,2,0,2,2,4,1,\quad0,1,\ldots\]
After $20$ terms the pair $0,1$ returns ($F_{21}\equiv0$, $F_{22}\equiv1$), and since each term depends only on the two before it, the remainders repeat with period $20$. As $999=20\cdot49+19$,
\[F_{999}\equiv F_{19}\equiv\boxed{4}\pmod5 .\]
(Indeed $F_{19}=2584$ in this indexing.)
