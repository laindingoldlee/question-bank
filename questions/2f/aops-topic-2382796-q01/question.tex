A positive integer is \emph{cute} if it equals the sum of its digits plus the product of its digits. For example, $19$ is cute since $(1+9)+(1\cdot9)=19$, but $543$ is not, since $(5+4+3)+(5\cdot4\cdot3)\ne543$. How many cute positive integers are there?
