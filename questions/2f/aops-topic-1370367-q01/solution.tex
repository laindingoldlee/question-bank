Both statements are true.

(a) If $n^2$ ends in $007$, then $n^2=1000k+7$, and since $1000=8\cdot125$ is divisible by $8$, $n^2\equiv7\pmod8$. But squares are $\equiv0,1$ or $4\pmod8$: an even $n=2m$ gives $n^2=4m^2\equiv0$ or $4$, and an odd $n=2m+1$ gives $n^2=4m(m+1)+1\equiv1\pmod8$, because $m(m+1)$ is even. So no square ends in $007$.

(b) If $n^2$ ends in $005$, then $n^2\equiv5\pmod8$ in the same way, which is again impossible. (Alternatively: $n^2$ is divisible by $5$, so $n=5m$ and $n^2=25m^2$, whose last two digits are $00$, $25$, $50$ or $75$, never $05$.)

So no perfect square ends in $007$ or in $005$. $\blacksquare$
