$(n-1)\left(n^2+1\right)=n^3-n^2+n-1$. With $\sum n^3=\left(\dfrac{40\cdot41}{2}\right)^2=672400$, $\sum n^2=\dfrac{40\cdot41\cdot81}{6}=22140$ and $\sum n=820$, the sum is
\[672400-22140+820-40=\boxed{651040}.\]
