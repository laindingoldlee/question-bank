The height from $A$ to $BC$ is $7\sin75^\circ$, so the area is $\dfrac12\cdot7\cdot12\cdot\sin75^\circ=42\sin75^\circ$. By the angle-addition formula,
\[\sin75^\circ=\sin\left(45^\circ+30^\circ\right)=\sin45^\circ\cos30^\circ+\cos45^\circ\sin30^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}+\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\cdot\dfrac12=\dfrac{\sqrt6+\sqrt2}{4}.\]
So the area is
\[42\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt6+\sqrt2}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{21\left(\sqrt6+\sqrt2\right)}{2}}\approx40.57.\]
