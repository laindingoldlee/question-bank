A binary string of length $s$ contains exactly $n$ ones, where $0\le n\le s$. Count its contiguous substrings that contain at least one $1$ (substrings in different positions count as different, so there are $\dfrac{s(s+1)}{2}$ substrings in all). Find, in terms of $s$ and $n$, the largest possible value of this count.
