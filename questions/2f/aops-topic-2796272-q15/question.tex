A $5\times5$ array of light bulbs starts all off. Fernando flips every bulb in two chosen rows, then every bulb in two chosen columns, then every bulb on one of the two main diagonals. If $L$ and $M$ are the least and greatest possible numbers of bulbs that end up on, find $L\cdot M$.
