After the rows and columns, bulb $(i,j)$ is on iff exactly one of "row $i$ chosen" and "column $j$ chosen" holds, so $2\cdot5+2\cdot5-2\cdot2\cdot2=12$ bulbs are on. On the chosen diagonal, a bulb is on iff its row or its column (but not both) was chosen; the number $k$ of such bulbs is $|R\triangle C'|\in\{0,2,4\}$, where $R$ is the set of chosen rows and $C'$ the rows meeting the chosen columns on that diagonal.

Flipping the diagonal turns $k$ bulbs off and $5-k$ on: the final count is $12-k+(5-k)=17-2k\in\{17,13,9\}$. So $L=9$, $M=17$ and $LM=\boxed{153}$.
