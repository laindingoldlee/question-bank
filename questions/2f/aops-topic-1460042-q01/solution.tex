Let $S=\sqrt{4+\sqrt7}-\sqrt{4-\sqrt7}$. It is positive, since $4+\sqrt7>4-\sqrt7$. Squaring,
\[
S^2=(4+\sqrt7)+(4-\sqrt7)-2\sqrt{(4+\sqrt7)(4-\sqrt7)}=8-2\sqrt{16-7}=8-6=2,
\]
so $S=\boxed{\sqrt2}$.

(Alternatively, $4\pm\sqrt7=\dfrac{8\pm2\sqrt7}{2}=\dfrac{(\sqrt7\pm1)^2}{2}$, so $\sqrt{4\pm\sqrt7}=\dfrac{\sqrt7\pm1}{\sqrt2}$, and the difference is $\dfrac{2}{\sqrt2}=\sqrt2$.)
