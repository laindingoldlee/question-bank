There are $ 16 $ ordered pairs of digits differing by $ 2 $, from the eight unordered pairs $\{0,2\},\ldots,\{7,9\}$. Exclude $(0,2)$ because the leading digit cannot be zero, leaving $ 15 $. For each, the middle positions have $ 8 $ and $ 7 $ choices. Thus there are $ 15\cdot8\cdot7=\boxed{840}$.
