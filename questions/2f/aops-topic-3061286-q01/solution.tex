Put $ k=\dfrac{n}{3}$. Then $ k $ is an integer and $ 3n=9k $. Both are four-digit exactly when $ 1000\le k\le9999 $ and $ 1000\le9k\le9999 $. Combining gives $ 1000\le k\le1111 $, yielding $\boxed{112}$ values of $ n=3k $.
