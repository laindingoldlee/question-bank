AM--GM on $t$ and $2017$ copies of $1$ gives $u\ge t$. Applying the same argument at the next two stages gives $v\ge u$ and $f(t)\ge v$, hence $f(t)\ge t$. Therefore $f (a) + f (b) + f (c) \ge a + b + c \ge 3 (abc)^{\dfrac{1}{3}} = 3$. Equality holds for $a=b=c=1$, since every intermediate value is then $1$. The minimum is $\boxed3$.
