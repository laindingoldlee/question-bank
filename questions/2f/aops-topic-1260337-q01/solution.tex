The products under the roots are equal: $12\cdot27=324=4\cdot81$ (both are $2^2\cdot3^4$), so both radicands equal $324\cdot16=5184$. The common value is $\sqrt{5184}=\sqrt{2^2\cdot3^4\cdot2^4}=2^3\cdot3^2=\boxed{72}$.
