Group play into pairs of points while the score is tied. A wins the pair with probability $\dfrac{49}{100}$, B wins with $\dfrac{9}{100}$, and a split pair restores the tie with probability $\dfrac{42}{100}$. If $p$ is A's eventual win probability, $p=\dfrac{49}{100}+\dfrac{42}{100}p$. Therefore $\boxed{p=\dfrac{49}{58}}$. The probability of endless split pairs is 0.
