Every handshake involves a different pair of people, so there are at most as many handshakes as pairs:
\[
\dbinom{23}{2}=\dfrac{23\cdot22}{2}=253 .
\]
This is attained when every pair shakes hands once; then each person shakes $22$ hands, which is allowed. The maximum is $\boxed{253}$.
