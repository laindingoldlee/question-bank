Put $t=x^2$ and divide by $12$. The polynomial factors as $500t^6+25t^5+5t^3+t-4=(25t^4+5t^2+1)(20t^2+t-4)$. The first factor is positive for real $t$, so $20t^2+t-4=0$. Its unique nonnegative root is $t=\dfrac{\sqrt{321}-1}{40}$. Thus $x=\sqrt{\dfrac{\sqrt{321}-\sqrt1}{40}}$, giving $\boxed{321+1+40=362}$. The original even polynomial also has the negative of this root; specifying positivity removes that ambiguity.
