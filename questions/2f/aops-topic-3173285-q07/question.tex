The positive real root of $6000x^{12}+300x^{10}+60x^6+12x^2-48=0$ has the form $\sqrt{\dfrac{\sqrt m-\sqrt n}{k}}$, where $m,n,k$ are positive integers, $m>n$, and $\gcd(m^2,n^2,k)=1$. Find $m+n+k$ using the representation obtained by simplifying the equation.
