$N$ needs at least $\left\lceil\dfrac{2020}{9}\right\rceil=225$ digits, since each digit is at most $9$. Among $225$-digit numbers with digit sum $2020$, the smallest puts as little as possible in front: the last $224$ digits contribute at most $9\cdot224=2016$, so the first digit is at least $4$, and $N=4\underbrace{99\dots9}_{224}$ works. The first digit is $\boxed{4}$.
