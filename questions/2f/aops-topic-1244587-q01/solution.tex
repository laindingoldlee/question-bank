The vertices: $x=6$ and $y=x+9$ give $(6,15)$; $x=6$ and $x+2y=-6$ give $(6,-6)$; $y=x+9$ and $x+2y=-6$ give $3x+18=-6$, so $(-8,1)$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((-10,0)--(8,0),gray,Arrow); draw((0,-8)--(0,17),gray,Arrow);
pair P=(6,15),Q=(6,-6),R=(-8,1);
fill(P--Q--R--cycle,lightblue); draw(P--Q--R--cycle,blue);
label("$(6,15)$",P,E); label("$(6,-6)$",Q,E); label("$(-8,1)$",R,W);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The side on $x=6$ has length $21$, and the opposite vertex is $14$ units from that line, so the area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot21\cdot14=\boxed{147}$.
