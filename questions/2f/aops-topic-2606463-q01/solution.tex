Equal remainders mean $b$ divides the differences $216-73=143=11\cdot13$ and $227-216=11$. So $b\mid11$, and as $b>1$, $b=11$. Check: $73$, $216$ and $227$ all leave remainder $7$ when divided by $11$. Finally $108=9\cdot11+9$, so the remainder is $\boxed{9}$.
