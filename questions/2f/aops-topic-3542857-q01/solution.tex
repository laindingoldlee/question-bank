(a) All three must close, giving $0.9^3=\boxed{0.729}$. (b) Failure requires all three open, so the probability is $1-0.1^3=\boxed{0.999}$. (c) A must close and at least one of B,C must close. Independence gives $0.9(1-0.1^2)=\boxed{0.891}$.
