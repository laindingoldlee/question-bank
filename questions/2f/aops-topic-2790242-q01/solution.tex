One valid packing is
\[\{2,7,9\}\ (18\text{ lb}),\qquad\{3,4,10\}\ (17\text{ lb}),\qquad\{5,6,8\}\ (19\text{ lb}).\]
Each weighs less than $20$ pounds. Within each suitcase no weight divides another: $2\nmid7$, $2\nmid9$, $7\nmid9$; $3\nmid4$, $3\nmid10$, $4\nmid10$; $5\nmid6$, $5\nmid8$, $6\nmid8$.

(How to find it: the total is $54$, so the three loads are close to $18$. The weight $2$ divides $4,6,8,10$, so its companions come from $\{3,5,7,9\}$; $3$ divides $6,9$ and $5$ divides $10$. Trying $2$ with $7,9$ quickly leads to the packing above; in fact it is the only one.)
