A bar of length $ m $ has $ 2^{m-1}$ end-removal orders, because each of the first $ m-1 $ removals offers two ends and the last is forced. Choose the five positions in the combined eight-step sequence assigned to the longer bar, then choose each bar's internal order. The result is $\binom85\cdot2^4\cdot2^2=\boxed{3584}$.
