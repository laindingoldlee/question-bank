In the sequence $3,\ 13,\ 1113,\ 3113,\ 2123,\ 112213,\ \dots$ each term describes the previous one by stating, for each digit that occurs, in increasing order of the digit, how many times it occurs followed by the digit (for example $3113$ contains two $1$s and two $3$s, so the next term is $2123$). Verify that the given terms follow this rule and find the next term.
