Divide repeatedly by $5$: $2121=5\cdot424+1$, $424=5\cdot84+4$, $84=5\cdot16+4$, $16=5\cdot3+1$, $3=5\cdot0+3$. Reading the remainders upward gives $\boxed{31441_5}$. Check: $3\cdot625+1\cdot125+4\cdot25+4\cdot5+1=2121$.
