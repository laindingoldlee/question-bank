Let $x,y,z$ count friends receiving apple--mango, apple--guava, and mango--guava respectively. Then $x+y=3$, $x+z=4$, $y+z=5$. Adding the first two and subtracting the third gives $2x=2$, so $(x,y,z)=(1,2,3)$. Assign these pair types to the friends in $\dfrac{6!}{1!2!3!}=\boxed{60}$ ways.
