First reduce the 6-pile to 3, leaving sizes $(3,5)$ after sorting. This is a losing position for the player to move: any move either leaves a zero pile, equal piles, or one of $(1,5),(2,5),(1,3),(2,3),(3,4),(2,4)$. Zero or equal piles can be emptied at once. Each listed unequal pair can be moved to $(1,2)$: reduce the larger pile when possible, reduce 3 to 1 in $(2,3)$, and subtract 2 from both in $(3,4)$. From $(1,2)$ every move leaves either a zero pile or equal piles, which the opponent empties. This gives a complete winning response strategy for the first player.
