The original $N$ positions are $2,6$, so the two new $N$ positions must be chosen from the other five positions, in $\binom52=10$ ways.

For any such choice, arrange the five distinct remaining letters in the five remaining positions. Exactly three of these letters still have their own original position available; the other two original positions are occupied by $N$. Inclusion-exclusion on the three forbidden fixed positions gives
\[
5!-\binom31 4!+\binom32 3!-2!=120-72+18-2=64.
\]
Therefore the answer is $\boxed{10\cdot64=640}$.
