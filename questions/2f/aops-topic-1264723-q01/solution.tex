Let $k=\left\lceil\dfrac{x}{30}-\dfrac{1}{10}\right\rceil$, an integer. Then $x=37+23k$, and the definition of the ceiling requires
\[k-1<\dfrac{37+23k}{30}-\dfrac{1}{10}\le k\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad 30k-30<34+23k\le 30k.\]
The left inequality gives $7k<64$, so $k\le 9$; the right gives $7k\ge34$, so $k\ge5$. Each $k\in\{5,6,7,8,9\}$ works, giving
\[x\in\{152,\ 175,\ 198,\ 221,\ 244\}.\]
