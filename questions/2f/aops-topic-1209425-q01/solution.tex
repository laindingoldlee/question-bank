Choose the boys in $\dbinom63=20$ ways and the girls in $\dbinom93=84$ ways, then arrange the six chosen students in $6!=720$ orders:
\[20\cdot84\cdot720=\boxed{1{,}209{,}600}.\]
(This confirms the poster's method.)
