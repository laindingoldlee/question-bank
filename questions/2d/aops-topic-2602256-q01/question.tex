A Pythagorean triple $(a,b,c)$ of positive integers with $a^2+b^2=c^2$ is called primitive if $\gcd(a,b,c)=1$. Prove that in a primitive triple exactly one of $a$, $b$ is even, and that the primitive triples with $b$ even are exactly
\[a=m^2-n^2,\qquad b=2mn,\qquad c=m^2+n^2,\]
where $m>n\ge1$ are relatively prime integers of opposite parity.
