Seven unit circles have centers at the origin and the six vertices of a regular hexagon of side 2. Add two more unit circles externally tangent to adjacent pairs of outer circles, placing their centers beyond two opposite hexagon edges. Find the area of the smallest circle enclosing all nine circles.
