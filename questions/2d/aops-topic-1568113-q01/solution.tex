The terms are
\[
3,\ 3,\ 6,\ 9,\ 15,\ 24,\ 39,\ 63,\ 102,\ 165,\ 267,\ \dots
\]
The first term greater than $200$ is $\boxed{267}$.

\textbf{A shortcut.} The sequence is $3$ times the Fibonacci sequence $1,1,2,3,5,8,\dots$ (both satisfy the same rule, and the first two terms are in ratio $3$). So we want the first Fibonacci number greater than $\dfrac{200}{3}\approx66.7$, which is $89$, and the answer is $3\cdot89=267$. No sum of Fibonacci numbers is needed.
