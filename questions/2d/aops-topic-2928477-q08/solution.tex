After $k$ intervals, the unknown fraction is $(\dfrac{4}{5})^k$. More than $\dfrac{5}{7}$ is known exactly when $(\dfrac{4}{5})^k<\dfrac{2}{7}$. At $k=5$, $\dfrac{1024}{3125}>\dfrac{2}{7}$ because $7168>6250$. At $k=6$, $\dfrac{4096}{15625}<\dfrac{2}{7}$ because $28672<31250$. The unknown fraction decreases each interval, so six is the first successful number of intervals. The time is $6\cdot20=\boxed{120\text{ seconds}}$.
