Both denominators are nonzero, so we may cross-multiply:
\[
(a+b)(c-a)=(c+a)(a-b).
\]
Expanding,
\[
ac-a^2+bc-ab=ac-bc+a^2-ab .
\]
Cancelling $ac$ and $-ab$ from both sides leaves $-a^2+bc=a^2-bc$, i.e.\ $2bc=2a^2$, so
\[
a^2=bc .
\]
Since $a$ and $c$ are nonzero, dividing $a\cdot a=b\cdot c$ by $a\cdot c$ gives $\boxed{\dfrac {a}{c}=\dfrac {b}{a}}$, a proportion in which $a$ appears twice. $\blacksquare$
