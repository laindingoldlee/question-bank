The nonzero numbers $a$, $b$, $c$ satisfy $a\ne b$, $a\ne c$ and
\[
\dfrac{a+b}{a-b}=\dfrac{c+a}{c-a}.
\]
Prove that $a$ is the mean proportional of $b$ and $c$, that is, $\dfrac {b}{a}=\dfrac {a}{c}$ (equivalently $a^2=bc$).
