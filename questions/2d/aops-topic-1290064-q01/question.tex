Let $O$ be the centre of a circle, and let $A$, $B$ and $C$ be points on the circle such that $\angle AOC=60^\circ$ and $B$ lies on arc $AC$. Let $M$, $N$, $P$ and $Q$ be the midpoints of $OA$, $AB$, $BC$ and $CO$, respectively. Prove that $MP\perp NQ$.
