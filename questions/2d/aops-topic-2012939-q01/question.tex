Ying lives on a tiny planet with $4$ cities, each $100$ miles from each of the others. She drives from her home city Viavesta to the city Havennew. From then on, at each city she chooses one of the other three cities uniformly at random (possibly Viavesta) and drives there. She makes $8$ trips in total (including the first trip from Viavesta to Havennew), and then stops in the city where the $8$th trip ends. Let $p$ be the probability that she stops in Viavesta and $q$ the probability that she stops in Havennew. Find $p+q$.
