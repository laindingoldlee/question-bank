Triangle $ABC$ has medians $AD$, $BF$ and $CE$ (so $D$, $E$, $F$ are the midpoints of $BC$, $AB$, $CA$) meeting at the centroid $O$. The centroids of triangles $AFO$, $FOC$, $COD$, $DOB$, $BOE$ and $EOA$ are the vertices of a hexagon. Find the ratio of the area of this hexagon to the area of triangle $ABC$.
