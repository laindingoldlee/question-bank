Let $n=2017$ and $t=10^n$. The number is
\[
t^3-3t^2+3t-1=(t-1)^3 .
\]
Write it as
\[
t^3-3t^2+3t-1=(t-3)\,t^2+(3t-1).
\]
Here $t-3=10^n-3=\underbrace{99\ldots9}_{n-1}7$ has $n$ digits, and $3t-1=2\underbrace{99\ldots9}_{n}$ has $n+1$ digits, which is less than $t^2=10^{2n}$. So the decimal representation is the digits of $t-3$ followed by the $2n$-digit block $0\ldots0\,2\,9\ldots9$ (that is, $n-1$ zeros, a $2$, then $n$ nines):
\[
\underbrace{99\ldots9}_{n-1}7\,\underbrace{00\ldots0}_{n-1}2\,\underbrace{99\ldots9}_{n}.
\]
(For $n=3$: $999^3=997\,002\,999$.) The number of nines is $(n-1)+n=2n-1=\boxed{4033}$.
