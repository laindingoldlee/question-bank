$m=10^{999}-1$, so $m^2=10^{1998}-2\cdot10^{999}+1$. Now $10^{1998}-2\cdot10^{999}=\underbrace{99\ldots9}_{998}8\underbrace{00\ldots0}_{999}$, and adding $1$ gives
\[
m^2=\underbrace{99\ldots9}_{998}8\underbrace{00\ldots0}_{998}1 .
\]
(For example $99^2=9801$, $999^2=998001$.) The digit sum is $998\cdot9+8+1=\boxed{8991}$.
