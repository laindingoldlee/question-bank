The surface area is $4\pi3^2=36\pi$, giving 36 pieces. For $k$ men the least possible total is $1+\cdots+k=\dfrac{k(k+1)}{2}$. This is 36 when $k=8$ and 45 when $k=9$. The distribution 1 through 8 uses all pieces, so the maximum is $\boxed8$.
