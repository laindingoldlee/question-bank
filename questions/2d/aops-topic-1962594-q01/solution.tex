Five touching circles of diameter $2$ cm in a row span $5\cdot 2=10$ cm, so the cookie has diameter $10$ cm and radius $5$ cm.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw(circle((0,0),5),linewidth(1));
for(int i=-2;i<=2;++i) filldraw(circle((2*i,0),1),gray(0.55),black);
draw((-5,-1.6)--(5,-1.6),Arrows(TeXHead));
label("$10$",(0,-1.6),S);
filldraw(circle((-1,2.6),1),gray(0.55),black);filldraw(circle((1.5,-3),1),gray(0.55),black);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The cookie has area $25\pi$, and the $15$ chips have total area $15\cdot\pi\cdot1^2=15\pi$. The ratio is
\[
\dfrac{15\pi}{25\pi}=\boxed{\dfrac{3}{5}}.
\]
