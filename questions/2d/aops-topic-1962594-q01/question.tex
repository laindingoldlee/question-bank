Exactly $5$ round chocolate chips of radius $1$ cm fit side by side along a diameter of a round cookie, touching each other, with the two end chips touching the edge of the cookie. You put $15$ such chips on each cookie (without overlapping). What is the ratio of the total area of the chips to the area of the cookie?
