Thirty is impossible: with zero, one or two copies of 13, the remaining totals 30,17,4 are not nonnegative combinations of 7 and 11. All integers from 31 through 37 are representable: $31=7+11+13$, $32=3\cdot7+11$, $33=3\cdot11$, $34=3\cdot7+13$, $35=5\cdot7$, $36=2\cdot7+2\cdot11$, $37=2\cdot13+11$. Adding sevens covers every larger integer. Thus the largest excluded number is $\boxed{30}$.
