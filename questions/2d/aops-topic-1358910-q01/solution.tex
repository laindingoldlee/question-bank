Since $2^3=8=7+1$, we have $2^3\equiv1\pmod 7$. As $87=3\cdot29$,
\[
2^{87}=\left(2^3\right)^{29}\equiv1^{29}=1\pmod 7 .
\]
So $2^{87}+3\equiv1+3=4\pmod7$, and the remainder is $\boxed{4}$.

(One can also see it without congruences: $2^{87}-1=\left(2^3\right)^{29}-1$ is divisible by $2^3-1=7$, because $x-1$ divides $x^{29}-1$.)
