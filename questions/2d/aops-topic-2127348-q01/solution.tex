We have $(p+qi)^2=p^2-q^2+2pq\,i$. Comparing real and imaginary parts with $i=0+1\cdot i$:
\[p^2-q^2=0,\qquad 2pq=1 .\]
Since $p,q>0$, the first equation gives $p=q$, and then $2p^2=1$, so $p=q=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$. Therefore
\[p+q=\boxed{\sqrt2}.\]
(Check: $\left(\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}+\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}i\right)^2=\dfrac{1}{2}-\dfrac{1}{2}+2\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\,i=i$.)
