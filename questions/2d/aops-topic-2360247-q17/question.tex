$A$ and $B$ are finite sets with no common element and $|A\cup B|=12$, where $|S|$ denotes the number of elements of $S$. A set $C$ is a subset of $B$ with $|C|=3$ and $|C\cup A|=8$. Find $|A|$ and $|B|$.
