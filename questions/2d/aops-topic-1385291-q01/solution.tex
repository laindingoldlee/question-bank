In the first $45$ minutes $=\dfrac{3}{4}$ hour the first ship covers $45\cdot\dfrac{3}{4}=33.75$ miles, leaving $162-33.75=128.25$ miles between the ships. Then they approach each other at $45+36=81$ mph, which takes $\dfrac{128.25}{81}=\dfrac{19}{12}$ hours $=1$ hour $35$ minutes. In total the meeting is
\[
\dfrac{3}{4}+\dfrac{19}{12}=\dfrac{7}{3}\ \text{hours}=\boxed{2\text{ hours }20\text{ minutes}}
\]
after the first ship left. (Check: the first ship has gone $105$ miles and the second $36\cdot\dfrac{19}{12}=57$ miles, and $105+57=162$.)
