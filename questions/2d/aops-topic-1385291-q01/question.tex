The distance between ports $M$ and $N$ is $162$ miles. A ship left port $M$ at a speed of $45$ mph, heading for $N$. After $45$ minutes, another ship left port $N$ at a speed of $36$ mph, heading towards the first ship. How long after the departure of the first ship will the two ships meet?
