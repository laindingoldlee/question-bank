In convex pentagon $ABCDE$, the perpendicular bisector of side $AB$ passes through the vertex $D$ and meets $AB$ at $P$. The pentagon is symmetric with respect to line $DP$, and line $BC$ is perpendicular to line $AE$. If $BC=4$, $AB=16$ and $DP=7$, find all possible values of the area of the pentagon.
