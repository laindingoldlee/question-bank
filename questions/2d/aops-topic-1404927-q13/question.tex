A $2\times2\times2$ cube is built from four transparent white $1\times1\times1$ cubes and four opaque black $1\times1\times1$ cubes, placed so that the big cube is non-transparent: it is not possible to see through it from top to bottom, from front to back, or from left to right (along any row of small cubes). What is the least number of black cubes needed to make a $3\times3\times3$ cube built from $27$ small white and black cubes non-transparent in the same sense?

(A) $6$\quad (B) $9$\quad (C) $10$\quad (D) $12$\quad (E) $18$
