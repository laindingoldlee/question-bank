The square is $(f(x))^2=(x+1)^2=\boxed{x^2+2x+1}$. The composition is $f(f(x))=(x+1)+1=\boxed{x+2}$. The notation $f^2$ can denote a pointwise square or a second iterate depending on convention. Writing $(f(x))^2$ or $f\circ f$ explicitly avoids ambiguity.
