For a positive integer $n$, let $\tau(n)$ be the number of positive divisors of $n$ and let $p(n)$ be the product of all positive divisors of $n$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $p(n)=n^{\dfrac{\tau(n)}{2}}$.
\item[(b)] Deduce a formula for the product of the proper divisors of $n$ (all positive divisors except $n$ itself), and check both formulas for $n=12$.
\end{enumerate}
