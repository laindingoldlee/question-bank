Someone notices on a calculator that $\pi\approx\dfrac{\sqrt{987}}{10}$. Using only the bounds $3.14159<\pi<3.14160$ and hand calculation, prove that $\dfrac{\sqrt{987}}{10}$ is a better approximation to $\pi$ than $\dfrac{22}{7}$.
