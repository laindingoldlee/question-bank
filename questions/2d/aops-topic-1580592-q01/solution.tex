The job requires $md$ worker-days of work. With $m+r$ workers it takes
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{md}{m+r}}\ \text{days}.
\]
(For example, if $4$ workers take $6$ days, the job is $24$ worker-days, and $4+2=6$ workers take $\dfrac{24}{6}=4$ days.)
