A triangle inscribed in a circle is right exactly when one side is a diameter. There are three diameter pairs and four choices of the third vertex for each. Thus $\boxed{12}$ right triangles occur.
\begin{asy}
import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);
string[] names={"A","B","C","D","E","F"};pair[] V;for(int i=0;i<6;++i){V.push(2*dir(60*i));label("$"+names[i]+"$",V[i],unit(V[i]));}draw(circle((0,0),2));draw(V[0]--V[3]--V[1]--cycle,blue);label("diameter gives a right angle",(0,-3));
\end{asy}
