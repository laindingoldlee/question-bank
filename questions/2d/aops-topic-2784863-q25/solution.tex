Repeated difference of squares gives $(1-t)\prod_{j=0}^{N}(1+t^{2^j})=1-t^{2^{N+1}}$. For $ t=\dfrac{2}{3}$, the final power tends to zero. Hence the product tends to $\dfrac{1}{1-\dfrac{2}{3}}=\boxed3 $.
