Subtracting gives $x-y=4$. Put $x=y+4$ in the second equation to obtain $y^2+5y-6=(y-1)(y+6)=0$. This gives pairs $(5,1)$ and $(-2,-6)$, with nonzero denominators and ratios 5 and $\dfrac{1}{3}$. Their sum is $\boxed{\dfrac{16}{3}}$.
