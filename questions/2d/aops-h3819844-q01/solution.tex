Write the diameter endpoints as $C_1\pm u$ and $C_2\pm v$, with $|u|=|v|=1$, and put $w=C_2-C_1$. A common point of the arcs is at distance $1$ from each center, so $|w|\le2$. The scalar triple product formula for tetrahedron volume gives
\[
V=\dfrac{2}{3}|w\cdot(u\times v)|
\le\dfrac{2}{3}|w|\,|u\times v|\le\dfrac{4}{3}.
\]
To attain equality, choose endpoints $(\pm1,0,1)$ and $(0,\pm1,-1)$. Take the semicircle in the plane $y=0$ with center $(0,0,1)$ that passes through the origin, and the semicircle in $x=0$ with center $(0,0,-1)$ that also passes through the origin. They meet at the origin, and $u=(1,0,0),v=(0,1,0),w=(0,0,-2)$ make the bound an equality. Therefore $\boxed{V_{\max}=\dfrac{4}{3}}$.
