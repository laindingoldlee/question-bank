Let $s_2(n)$ be the number of ones in the binary expansion of $n$. Summing $\lfloor \dfrac{n}{2} \rfloor + \lfloor \dfrac{n}{4} \rfloor + \cdots$ gives $v_2(n!)=n-s_2(n)$. Thus we minimize $100-s_2(a)-s_2(b)$. Alternatively, $v_2(a!b!)=v_2(100!)-v_2\binom{100}a=97-v_2\binom{100}a$. In binary addition $a+b=1100100_2$, carries can occur only at positions $0$ through $5$, so at most six carries occur. Each carry reduces the digit sum by one, giving $s_2(a)+s_2(b)\le s_2(100)+6=9$. Equality occurs at $a=37=100101_2$, $b=63=111111_2$, whose digit sums are $3$ and $6$. Hence the required minimum is $\boxed{91}$.
