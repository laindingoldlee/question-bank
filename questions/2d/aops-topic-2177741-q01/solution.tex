First,
\[x^2+\dfrac{1}{x^2}=\left(x-\dfrac{1}{x}\right)^2+2=n^2+2,\]
\[x^3-\dfrac{1}{x^3}=\left(x-\dfrac{1}{x}\right)\left(x^2+1+\dfrac{1}{x^2}\right)=n\left(n^2+3\right)=n^3+3n .\]
Multiplying these,
\[\left(x^2+\dfrac{1}{x^2}\right)\left(x^3-\dfrac{1}{x^3}\right)=x^5-\dfrac{1}{x}+x-\dfrac{1}{x^5}=\left(x^5-\dfrac{1}{x^5}\right)+\left(x-\dfrac{1}{x}\right).\]
Hence
\[x^5-\dfrac{1}{x^5}=\left(n^2+2\right)\left(n^3+3n\right)-n=\boxed{n^5+5n^3+5n}.\]
