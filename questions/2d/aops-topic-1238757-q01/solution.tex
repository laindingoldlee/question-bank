Let $a_N$ be the number. A good subset of $\{1,\dots,N\}$ either omits $N$ (then it is a good subset of $\{1,\dots,N-1\}$) or contains $N$ (then it omits $N-1$, and the rest is a good subset of $\{1,\dots,N-2\}$). So $a_N=a_{N-1}+a_{N-2}$ with $a_1=2$, $a_2=3$. Hence $a_N=\boxed{F_{N+2}}$, where $F_1=F_2=1$ are the Fibonacci numbers (e.g. $a_3=5$, $a_4=8$). Equivalently $a_N=\sum_k\dbinom{N-k+1}{k}$, counting subsets of size $k$.
