Let $n$ and $k$ be non-negative integers. Prove that for every real number $\alpha$,
\[
\sum_{j=0}^{k}\dbinom{n+j}{j}\alpha^j=\sum_{j=0}^{k}\dbinom{n+k+1}{n+1+j}(1-\alpha)^j\alpha^{k-j}.
\]
Show that the case $\alpha=1$ is the hockey-stick identity, and deduce that $\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}\dbinom{n+j}{j}\alpha^j=\dfrac{1}{(1-\alpha)^{n+1}}$ for $0\le\alpha<1$.
