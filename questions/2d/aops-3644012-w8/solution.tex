The centroid vectors satisfy $G_A=\dfrac{(I+B+C)}{3}$, and similarly. Thus $G_B-G_A=\dfrac{(A-B)}{3}$ and all corresponding sides scale by one third. The centroid triangle area is one ninth of ABC, independent of I. Heron gives $[ABC]=84$, so the answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{28}{3}}$.

import graph;import geometry;usepackage("amsmath");size(7cm);

\end{asy}
