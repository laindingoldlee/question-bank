$\vartriangle ABC$ is a triangle with $AB = 13$, $BC = 14$, $CA = 15$. Let $I$ be the incenter of $ABC$, $G_A$ be the centroid of  $\vartriangle IBC$, $G_B$ be the centroid of  $\vartriangle ICA$, and $G_C$ be the centroid of  $\vartriangle IAB$. What is the area of triangle  $\vartriangle G_AG_BG_C$? (The incenter is the intersection point of the three angle bisectors and the centroid is the intersection point of the three medians).
