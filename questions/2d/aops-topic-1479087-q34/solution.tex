Simplify each radical: $\sqrt{75}=5\sqrt3$, $\sqrt{48}=4\sqrt3$ and $\sqrt{192}=8\sqrt3$. So
\[
\sqrt{75}+\dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{48}-\sqrt{192}=5\sqrt3+2\sqrt3-8\sqrt3=-\sqrt3\approx\boxed{-1.732}.
\]
