Rationalising, $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt k+\sqrt{k+1}}=\dfrac{\sqrt{k+1}-\sqrt k}{(k+1)-k}=\sqrt{k+1}-\sqrt k$. The sum telescopes:
\[
\sum_{k=1}^{9999}\left(\sqrt{k+1}-\sqrt k\right)=\sqrt{10000}-\sqrt1=100-1=\boxed{99}.
\]
