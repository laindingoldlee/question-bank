Fix the middle letter. If it is A or Z, exactly one of the 26 letters is forbidden in either end position, giving $25^2$ choices. For any of the other 24 letters, exactly two are forbidden, giving $24^2$. The total is $2\cdot25^2+24\cdot24^2=\boxed{15074}$.
