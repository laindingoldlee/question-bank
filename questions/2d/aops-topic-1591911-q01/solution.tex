Suppose $g$ green marbles are added. Then there are $5+g$ green marbles out of $12+g$, and we need
\[
\dfrac{5+g}{12+g}=\dfrac{3}{4}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad 20+4g=36+3g\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad g=16 .
\]
Equivalently: the $7$ non-green marbles must be the remaining $25\%$, so there must be $28$ marbles in all, $21$ of them green. So $\boxed{16}$ green marbles must be added.
