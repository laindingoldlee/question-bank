The sun rises at 5:00 AM and sets at 5:00 PM today. Assume that the sun moves across the sky at a constant angular rate along a half circle from the eastern horizon through the point directly overhead (at noon) to the western horizon. At a certain moment a vertical tree $5\sqrt{3}$ feet tall casts a shadow $5$ feet long on level ground. At which times today could this be?
