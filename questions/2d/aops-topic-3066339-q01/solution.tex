The coefficient from the first parenthesis is $3\cdot50=150$. In the second, the coefficient of $x^{50}$ is $-51$, since exponent $k<100$ has coefficient $-(101-k)$. Multiplication by $-2$ contributes $102$. The total is $\boxed{252}$.
