Scale $BC=1$ and put $A=(0,0),B=(2,0)$, with $C$ above $AB$. Since $E$ lies between $A,D$, the direction of $EC$ is $60^\circ$. The bisected angle between $CA$ and the downward vertical $CD$ then gives $\angle CAB=30^\circ$. Write $AC=t$, so $C=(\dfrac{\sqrt3t}{2},\dfrac{t}{2})$. The condition $BC=1$ gives $t^2-2\sqrt3t+4=1$, or $(t-\sqrt3)^2=0$. Thus $C=(\dfrac{3}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2})$ and $E=(1,0)$.
The lines through $E,C$ and through $B$ parallel to $AC$ are $y=\sqrt3(x-1)$ and $y=\dfrac{x-2}{\sqrt3}$. Their intersection is $F=(\dfrac{1}{2},-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2})$. Vectors $FA=(-\dfrac{1}{2},\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2})$ and $FC=(1,\sqrt3)$ have directions $120^\circ$ and $60^\circ$, so $\boxed{\angle AFC=60^\circ}$.

\end{asy}
