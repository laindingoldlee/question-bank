There are $4!=24$ orders in all. In those with the Steelie and the Tiger together, glue them into a block, which can be ordered in $2$ ways; the block and the other two marbles can be arranged in $3!=6$ ways, giving $2\cdot6=12$ orders. So $24-12=12$ orders keep them apart. The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(C) }12}$.
