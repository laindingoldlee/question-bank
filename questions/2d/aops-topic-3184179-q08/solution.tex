Let $t$ buy all three. The total 210 item-category purchases is at most $3t+2(100-t)=200+t$, so $t\ge10$. This is attainable with 10 buying all three, 30 only oranges and apples, 40 only oranges and bananas, and 20 only apples and bananas. Thus $\boxed{10}$.
