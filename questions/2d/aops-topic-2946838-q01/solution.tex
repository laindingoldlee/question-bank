Let the diagonals $AC$ and $BD$ meet at $P$ with $AC\perp BD$. They cut the quadrilateral into four right triangles with right angles at $P$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(-2,0),C=(3,0),B=(0,2),D=(0,-1.5);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(A--C);draw(B--D);draw((0.25,0)--(0.25,0.25)--(0,0.25));
label("$A$",A,W);label("$B$",B,N);label("$C$",C,E);label("$D$",D,S);label("$P$",(0,0),SW);
\end{asy}

\[[ABCD]=\dfrac12\bigl(AP\cdot BP+BP\cdot CP+CP\cdot DP+DP\cdot AP\bigr)=\dfrac12(AP+CP)(BP+DP)=\dfrac12\,AC\cdot BD.\ \blacksquare\]
