Reflect $(0,4)$ in the $x$-axis to $(0,-4)$ and $(18,4)$ in the line $y=6$ to $(18,8)$. For any path of the given form, replacing the first and last legs by their mirror images does not change the length, so its length equals that of a broken line from $(0,-4)$ to $(18,8)$ through the same two points, which is at least
\[\sqrt{18^2+12^2}=\sqrt{468}=6\sqrt{13}.\]
Equality holds for the straight segment, which meets $y=0$ at $(6,0)$ and $y=6$ at $(15,6)$, in the required order. The shortest length is $\boxed{6\sqrt{13}}$.

usepackage("amsmath");

defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair P0=(0,4),P3=(18,4),R0=(0,-4),R3=(18,8),X1=(6,0),X2=(15,6);
draw((-1,0)--(19,0),gray);draw((-1,6)--(19,6),gray);
draw(P0--X1--X2--P3,linewidth(0.8));draw(R0--X1,dashed);draw(X2--R3,dashed);
dot(P0);dot(P3);dot(R0);dot(R3);dot(X1);dot(X2);
label("$(0,4)$",P0,W,fontsize(7pt));label("$(18,4)$",P3,E,fontsize(7pt));label("$(0,-4)$",R0,W,fontsize(7pt));label("$(18,8)$",R3,E,fontsize(7pt));
label("$y=0$",(19,0),E,fontsize(7pt));label("$y=6$",(19,6),E,fontsize(7pt));
\end{asy}
