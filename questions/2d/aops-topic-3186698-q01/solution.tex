The semiperimeter is $21$, giving area $K=\sqrt{21\cdot8\cdot7\cdot6}=\boxed{84}$. From $K=\dfrac{1}{2}bh$ with $b=14$, the altitude is $h=\dfrac{168}{14}=\boxed{12}$. Both formulas always apply to a nondegenerate triangle; Heron is convenient when three sides rather than a height are known.
