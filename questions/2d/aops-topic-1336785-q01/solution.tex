\textbf{(a)} The integers are $-101,-100,\dots,102,103$. The numbers $-101,\dots,101$ cancel in pairs ($k$ with $-k$, and $0$), leaving $102+103=\boxed{205}$.

\textbf{(b)} The integers $-10,\dots,12$ include $0$, so the product is $\boxed{0}$.

(If ``between'' included the endpoints, the answers would be $103+104=207$ and $0$.)
