A father divides $83$ diamonds among his $5$ sons according to the following rules.
\begin{enumerate}
\item No diamond is cut, and every diamond is given out.
\item No two sons receive the same number of diamonds.
\item The differences between the numbers received by two sons are all different (no two pairs of sons have the same difference).
\item Any three sons together receive more than half of the $83$ diamonds.
\end{enumerate}
Find all possible ways to divide the diamonds, i.e.\ all possible sets of five amounts.
