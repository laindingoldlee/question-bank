The value factors as $5b^3+6b^2+5b+4=(b+1)(5b^2+b+4)$. Both factors exceed 1. Their gcd divides 8, because substituting $b=-1$ into the second gives 8. If the product is a prime power, both factors must therefore be powers of 2. Write $b+1=2^u$, with $u\ge3$. The other factor is $5\cdot2^{2u}-9\cdot2^u+8$. If $u\ge4$, this is 8 modulo 16, so being a power of 2 would force it to equal 8, impossible. For $u=3$, $b=7$ and the other factor is 256, giving $5654_7=8\cdot256=2048$. Thus the sum is $\boxed{2048}$.
