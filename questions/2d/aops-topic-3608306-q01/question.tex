To bisect an angle with vertex $A$, mark points $X,Y$ on its two rays with $AX=AY$. Draw equal-radius arcs centred at $X,Y$ that meet at a point $Z$ inside the angle, with $Z\ne A$. Prove that ray $AZ$ bisects the angle.
