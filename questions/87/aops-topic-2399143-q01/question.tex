A rectangular prism $ABCD$-$EFGH$ (with $ABCD$ the bottom face and $E,F,G,H$ directly above $A,B,C,D$) has volume $32\text{ cm}^3$ and height $AE=2\text{ cm}$; the dimensions of its base are not given. Consider the tetrahedron with base $BEG$ and apex $F$. The maximum possible distance from $F$ to the plane $BEG$ can be written as $\dfrac{a\sqrt b}{c}$, where $a,b,c$ are positive integers, $b$ is not divisible by the square of a prime, and $\gcd(a,c)=1$. Find $a\cdot b\cdot c$.
