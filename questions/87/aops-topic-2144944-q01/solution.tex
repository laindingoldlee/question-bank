Computing the terms,
\[1,\ 1,\ 3,\ 5,\ 11,\ 21,\ 43,\ 85,\ 171,\ 341,\ 683,\ 1365,\ \dots\]
so $a_{11}=683\le1000<a_{12}=1365$. The sequence is increasing from $a_2$ on (each term adds a positive amount to the previous one), so the answer is $k=\boxed{12}$.

(In fact $a_n=\dfrac{2^n-(-1)^n}{3}$: this formula gives $a_1=a_2=1$ and satisfies the recurrence, since $2^{n+2}=2^{n+1}+2\cdot2^n$ and $(-1)^{n+2}=(-1)^{n+1}+2(-1)^n$. So $a_{12}=\dfrac{4095}{3}=1365$.)
