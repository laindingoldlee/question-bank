Multiplying by $5^2$ shifts the base-$5$ point two places: $25x=31.\overline{31}_{\,5}=31_5+x$. Since $31_5=3\cdot5+1=16$,
\[
24x=16,\qquad x=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{3}}.
\]
Check: $\dfrac{2}{3}=\dfrac{16}{24}=16\left(\dfrac{1}{25}+\dfrac{1}{625}+\cdots\right)$.
