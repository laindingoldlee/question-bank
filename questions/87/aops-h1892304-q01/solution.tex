A chosen pentagon misses the centre exactly when its five vertices lie in an open semicircle. Since $17$ is odd, no chosen pair is antipodal. Every such five-set has a unique first point followed clockwise by its other four points within the next eight positions. Thus the number missing the centre is $17\binom84$. The desired probability is $1-\dfrac{17\binom84}{\binom{17}{5}}=\boxed{\dfrac{21}{26}}$. Uniqueness follows because the complementary circular gap then exceeds a semicircle and there can be only one such gap.

\end{asy}
