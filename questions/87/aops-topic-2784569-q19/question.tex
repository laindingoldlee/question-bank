For a positive distance $d$, an integer count $n$ may equal either $\lfloor\dfrac{1000}{d}\rfloor$ or $\lceil\dfrac{1000}{d}\rceil$. Suppose the width of the smallest open interval containing all possible $d$ for the observed $n$ is $\dfrac{2000}{16899}$. Find $n$.
