Writing $ k=pqr $, there are eight divisors, with sum $(p+1)(q+1)(r+1)=528=2^4\cdot3\cdot11 $. If all three primes were odd, all three factors would be even. The factor containing 11 would have to be at least 44:11 plus 1 cannot produce an odd prime except among even multiples 44,132,264,528. But the other two even factors are at least 4 each, making the product at least 704, impossible. Thus one prime is 2. The other two factors multiply to 176. Its even factor pairs with factors at least 4 are $(4,44)$ and $(8,22)$; only the first yields two primes after subtracting 1. Therefore the primes are 2, 3, 43 and $\boxed{k=258}$.
