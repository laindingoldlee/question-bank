The breakpoints are $x=-10$ and $x=-\dfrac{3}{2}$. For $x\le-10$, the equation becomes $-8-\dfrac{x}{2}=0$, giving $x=-16$. For $-10\le x\le-\dfrac{3}{2}$, it becomes $2+\dfrac{x}{2}=0$, giving $x=-4$. For $x\ge-\dfrac{3}{2}$, it becomes $8+\dfrac{9x}{2}=0$, whose candidate $-\dfrac{16}{9}$ is outside that interval. Thus there are $\boxed2$ real solutions.
