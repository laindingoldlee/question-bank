The cubes in $A$ are $(7k-1)^3$ for $k=1,2,\dots,n$. Since $7k-1\equiv-1\pmod7$, each cube satisfies $(7k-1)^3\equiv(-1)^3=-1\pmod7$. So
\[A\equiv n\cdot(-1)+15n=14n\equiv0\pmod7.\ \blacksquare\]
