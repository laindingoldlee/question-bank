Since $\sqrt{25}=5$ and $\sqrt{81}=9$,
\[
f(5)=\pi\cdot\dfrac{5}{5}=\pi,\qquad g(3)=2\pi\cdot\dfrac{9}{3}=6\pi .
\]
Both are positive, so $|f(5)|=\boxed{\pi}$ and $|g(3)|=\boxed{6\pi}$.
