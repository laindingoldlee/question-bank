The cosine rule in $APB$ gives $\cos\angle APB=\dfrac{8^2+4^2-6^2}{2\cdot8\cdot4}=\dfrac{11}{16}$. Since $PB,PD$ point in opposite directions, $\cos\angle APD=-\dfrac{11}{16}$. Thus $AD^2=8^2+6^2-2\cdot8\cdot6(-\dfrac{11}{16})=166$, and $\boxed{AD=\sqrt{166}}$.
