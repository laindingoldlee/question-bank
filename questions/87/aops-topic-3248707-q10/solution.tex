For $x\ge2$, only exponents $2,3,5,7$ matter, since $2^{11}>514$. There are $22$ squares, including $1$. The eight cubes are $1,8,27,64,125,216,343,512$; only $1,64$ are squares, so six are new. Fifth powers add $32,243$ beyond $1$, and seventh powers add $128$ beyond $1$. These added values are distinct and are not earlier powers by their prime exponents. Thus the total is $\boxed{22+6+2+1=31}$.
