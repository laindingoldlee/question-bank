Let the circle have radius $r$. The diagonal of the inscribed square is a diameter, so it equals $2r$, and the square's side is $s=\dfrac{2r}{\sqrt2}=\sqrt2\,r$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0); draw(circle(O,1),blue); draw(dir(45)--dir(135)--dir(225)--dir(315)--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(dir(225)--dir(45),red+dashed); dot(O); label("$O$",O,SE);
label("$2r$",(0.25,0.25),SE,red); label("$s$",(dir(225)+dir(315))/2,S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The square has area $s^2=2r^2$ and the circle has area $\pi r^2$, so the ratio is $\boxed{\dfrac{2}{\pi}}\approx0.637$.
