Check $n=70,71,\dots$: $70+4=74$ is not prime; $71$ and $73$ are prime, not composite; $72+4=76$ and $74+4=78$ are not prime; $75=3\cdot5^2$ is composite and $75+4=79$ is prime. So the answer is $\boxed{75}$.
