Four chess players — Nurzhan, Akylzhan, Dastan, and Bakhyt — played a round-robin tournament (each pair played one game). A win gives $1$ point, a draw $0.5$, and a loss $0$. The first-place player scored $3$ points, and the last-place player scored $0.5$ points. How many different score distributions among these players are possible? (Distributions that differ by permuting the players' names are considered different.)
