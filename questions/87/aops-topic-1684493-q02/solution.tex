Each pair of points determines a line, and since no three points are collinear, different pairs give different lines (a line containing two different pairs would contain at least three of the points). So the number of lines is the number of pairs,
\[
\binom62=\dfrac{6\cdot5}{2}=\boxed{15}.
\]
