There is no uniform distribution on all positive integers. Under the usual interpretation of independent uniform choices from $1,\ldots,N$ followed by $N\to\infty$, the reduced numerator is even exactly when $v_2(p)>v_2(q)$. The limiting probability is $\sum_{j\ge0}\Pr(v_2(q)=j)\Pr(v_2(p)>j)=\sum_{j\ge0}2^{-(j+1)}2^{-(j+1)}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{3}}$.
