Let $q=ab+bc+ca$.

\textbf{Step 1.} Squaring $a+b+c=0$ gives $a^2+b^2+c^2+2q=0$, so $a^2+b^2+c^2=-2q$, and the left side equals $4q^2$.

\textbf{Step 2.} Squaring $q$:
\[
q^2=a^2b^2+b^2c^2+c^2a^2+2abc(a+b+c)=a^2b^2+b^2c^2+c^2a^2 .
\]
\textbf{Step 3.} Then
\[
a^4+b^4+c^4=\left(a^2+b^2+c^2\right)^2-2\left(a^2b^2+b^2c^2+c^2a^2\right)=4q^2-2q^2=2q^2 .
\]
So $2(a^4+b^4+c^4)=4q^2=(a^2+b^2+c^2)^2$. $\blacksquare$
