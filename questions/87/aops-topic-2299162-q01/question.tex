A fair coin is flipped $n$ times, and $p_n$ is the probability of getting exactly $16$ heads (so $p_n=0$ for $n<16$). A student's mother says that with more flips an outcome becomes more likely, and the student wonders whether $p_n$ approaches $1$ as $n$ grows.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] For which $n$ is $p_n$ largest, and what is its largest value?
\item[(b)] Show that $p_n$ approaches $0$ as $n$ grows.
\end{enumerate}
