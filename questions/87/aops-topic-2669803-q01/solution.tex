Let $m$ be the mean and $d_i=x_i-m$. Then $\sum d_i=0$ and $\sum|d_i|=5\cdot5=25$. So the positive $d_i$ add up to $\dfrac{25}{2}$ and the negative ones add up to $-\dfrac{25}{2}$. The range is
\[\max d_i-\min d_i\le\dfrac{25}{2}+\dfrac{25}{2}=25,\]
since the largest $d_i$ is at most the sum of the positive ones and the smallest is at least the sum of the negative ones. The list $-12.5,0,0,0,12.5$ has mean $0$, mean absolute deviation $\dfrac{25}{5}=5$, and range $25$. The largest possible range is $\boxed{25}$.
