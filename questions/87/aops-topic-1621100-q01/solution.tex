After $10$ games he had won $\dfrac{4}{5}\cdot10=8$ games. After $10+15=25$ games he had won $52\%$ of $25$, that is $0.52\cdot25=\boxed{13}$ games in total. (So he won only $5$ of the last $15$ games.)
