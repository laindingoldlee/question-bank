Four cyclists each ride round a different circular track; the four tracks all pass through a common point, the centre, and each track is $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of a mile round. They ride at $6$, $9$, $12$ and $15$ miles per hour, all starting from the centre at twelve o'clock. They agree to ride until all four are together at the centre for the fourth time (not counting the start). At what time do they stop?
