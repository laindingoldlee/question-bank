Complete the squares: $a^2-14a+b^2-4b=11$ becomes
\[
(a-7)^2+(b-2)^2=64 .
\]
Put $u=a-7$ and $v=b-2$, so $u^2+v^2=64$ and $5a+12b=59+5u+12v$. By the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality,
\[
5u+12v\le\sqrt{5^2+12^2}\,\sqrt{u^2+v^2}=13\cdot8=104,
\]
with equality when $(u,v)$ is a positive multiple of $(5,12)$, namely $(u,v)=\left(\dfrac{40}{13},\dfrac{96}{13}\right)$. So the maximum is $59+104=\boxed{163}$.
