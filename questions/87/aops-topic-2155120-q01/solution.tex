Subtracting the second expression from the first:
\[x^3-y^3-20(x-y)=0\iff(x-y)\left(x^2+xy+y^2-20\right)=0 .\]
Since $x\ne y$, $x^2+xy+y^2=20$. In the same way $y^2+yz+z^2=20$ and $z^2+zx+x^2=20$.

Subtracting the second of these from the first: $x^2+xy-yz-z^2=0$, that is, $(x-z)(x+y+z)=0$. Since $x\ne z$,
\[x+y+z=0 .\]
Adding the three equations $x^2+xy+y^2=20$ and so on gives
\[2\left(x^2+y^2+z^2\right)+(xy+yz+zx)=60 .\]
From $(x+y+z)^2=0$ we get $xy+yz+zx=-\dfrac{1}{2}\left(x^2+y^2+z^2\right)$, so $\dfrac{3}{2}\left(x^2+y^2+z^2\right)=60$ and
\[x^2+y^2+z^2=\boxed{40}.\]

\emph{Such numbers exist.} Let $x,y,z$ be the roots of $t^3-20t-20=0$. Its values at $t=-4,-2,0,5$ are $-4,12,-20,5$, so it has three distinct real roots. Their sum is $0$, so $20y+20z=-20x$ and $x^3+20y+20z=x^3-20x=20$, and similarly for the other two expressions.
