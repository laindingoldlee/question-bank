No. For $n\ge1$ every term satisfies $\sqrt n\ge1$, so the partial sums satisfy
\[\sum_{n=0}^{N}\sqrt n\ge N .\]
They grow without bound, so the series diverges (to $+\infty$) and has no finite sum. In general, a series can converge only if its terms tend to $0$, and here they tend to infinity.
