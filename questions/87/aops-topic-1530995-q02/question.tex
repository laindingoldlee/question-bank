Twelve balls numbered $1$ to $12$ are in a bin. In how many ways can $3$ balls be drawn, in order, from the bin, if
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] each ball remains outside the bin after it is drawn;
\item[(b)] each ball is placed back into the bin immediately after it is drawn;
\item[(c)] the first ball is replaced after it is drawn, but the second ball remains outside the bin?
\end{enumerate}
