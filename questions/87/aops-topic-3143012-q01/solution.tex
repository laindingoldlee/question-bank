If the men are all isolated, choose three of the four gaps around WWW, giving 4 gender patterns. If there is a block of two men, it must be flanked by women. The six possible patterns are WMMWMW, WMMWWM, MWMMWW, WMWMMW, MWWMMW, and WWMMWM. A block of three men fails. Thus there are 10 patterns, each allowing $3!3!$ assignments of people. Total: $\boxed{360}$.
