Describe and sketch the set of all points $(x,y)$ in the plane satisfying
\[
\dfrac{xy}{2}=\sqrt{\left(\dfrac{x+y+\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}}{2}\right)\left(\dfrac{x-y+\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}}{2}\right)\left(\dfrac{y-x+\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}}{2}\right)\left(\dfrac{x+y-\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}{2}\right)} .
\]
