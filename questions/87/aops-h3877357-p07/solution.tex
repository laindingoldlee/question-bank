Within either group, equal chords have equal intervening arcs: a major intervening arc is impossible because the other three equal chords would already force total arc greater than $360^\circ$. If the two arc sizes are $\alpha,\beta$, then $4\alpha+4\beta=360^\circ$. The arc from $D$ to $F$ through $E$ has size $90^\circ$. The other arc has size $270^\circ$, so $\angle DEF=\boxed{135^\circ}$.
