The scales at Jan's house only work if two people weigh themselves at the same time. Four people in his house weigh themselves in pairs, one weighing for each of the six pairs, obtaining $89$, $105$, $112$, $113$, $120$ and $136$ kg. What is the difference between the weights of the heaviest and the lightest person?
