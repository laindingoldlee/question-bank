Intersecting chords give $EA\cdot EB=EC\cdot ED=12$, so $EA=6$. Put $E=(0,0),A=(-6,0),B=(2,0),C=(0,3),D=(0,-4)$. The circle center lies on both chord perpendicular bisectors, hence is $O=(-2,-\dfrac{1}{2})$. Therefore $r^2=OB^2=4^2+(\dfrac{1}{2})^2=\dfrac{65}{4}$, giving $\boxed{m+n=69}$.

\end{asy}
