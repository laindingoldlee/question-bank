In right pyramid $PABCD$, $ABCD$ is a square of side $2$, and the distance from $P$ to the centre $O$ of the square is $1$. A plane through $A$ meets edges $PB$, $PC$, $PD$ at $B'$, $C'$, $D'$. If $\dfrac{B'B}{PB}=\dfrac14$ and $\dfrac{D'D}{PD}=\dfrac15$, find $\dfrac{C'C}{PC}$. A proposed solution weights the heights of $B'$ and $D'$ in the ratio $3:4$ and gets $\dfrac{16}{43}$; find the error.
