In base $b$: $13_b=b+3$, $11_b=b+1$, $10_b=b$, $12_b=b+2$, $14_b=b+4$, and the one-digit numerals are themselves. The row sums are
\[
(b+3)+1+(b+1)=2b+5,\qquad 3+b+(b+2)=2b+5,\qquad 4+(b+4)+2=b+10 .
\]
Equal sums require $2b+5=b+10$, so $b=\boxed{5}$ (the digit $4$ is allowed in base $5$). Check the columns, $(b+3)+3+4=b+10$, $1+b+(b+4)=2b+5$, $(b+1)+(b+2)+2=2b+5$, and the diagonals, $(b+3)+b+2=2b+5$ and $(b+1)+b+4=2b+5$; for $b=5$ all equal $15$. In base ten the square is
\[
\begin{array}{ccc}8&1&6\\3&5&7\\4&9&2\end{array},
\]
the classical $3\times3$ magic square.
