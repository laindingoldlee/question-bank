A rectangular prism has a base of length $L$ and width $W$ and height $H$, where $L$, $W$, $H$ are positive integers. Suppose
\[
H=\dfrac{LW}{2},\qquad L \text{ is a perfect cube other than } 1,\qquad \sqrt H=2\sqrt6 .
\]
Find the length of a space diagonal of the prism.
