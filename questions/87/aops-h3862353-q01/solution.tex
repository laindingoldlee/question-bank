Let $R=\sqrt{(a_{n-1}-1)^2+8}$. The numerator is $R^2-1$, so cancellation gives
\[
a_n=1+\sqrt{(a_{n-1}-1)^2+8}.
\]
Thus $(a_n-1)^2=(a_{n-1}-1)^2+8$. Since $a_0=0$, induction yields $a_n=1+\sqrt{8n+1}$ for $n\ge1$.

This is an integer exactly when $8n+1=(2k+1)^2$, or $n=\dfrac{k(k+1)}{2}$. The positive triangular numbers at most $1000$ correspond to $k=1,\ldots,44$, since $44\cdot\dfrac{45}{2}=990$ and $45\cdot\dfrac{46}{2}=1035$. The count is $\boxed{44}$.
