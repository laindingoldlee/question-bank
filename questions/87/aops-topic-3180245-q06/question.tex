A 30-day November starts on Friday. A writer has 6 free hours each weekday, 12 on Saturday, 9 on Sunday. Homework takes 2 hours daily, a Wednesday activity takes 1.5 more hours, and a Friday activity takes 3 more. All remaining time is spent writing. Find the constant words-per-minute rate needed for 50000 words, rounded to the nearest integer.
