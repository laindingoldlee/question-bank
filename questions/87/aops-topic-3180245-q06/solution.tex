Available writing hours per week are 4 each on Monday, Tuesday, Thursday;2.5 on Wednesday;1 on Friday;10 on Saturday;7 on Sunday. Their sum is 32.5. The month contains four full weeks plus a Friday and Saturday, giving $4\cdot32.5+1+10=141$ hours, or 8460 minutes. The required rate is $\dfrac{50000}{8460}\approx5.91$, which rounds to $\boxed6$ words per minute.
