This is a trapezoid whose parallel vertical sides have lengths 10 and 20 and whose distance between them is 10. Its area is $\dfrac{10+20}{2}\cdot10=\boxed{150}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
filldraw((10,0)--(20,0)--(20,20)--(10,10)--cycle,lightgray);label("(10,0)",(10,0),S);label("(20,0)",(20,0),S);label("(20,20)",(20,20),N);label("(10,10)",(10,10),W);
\end{asy}
