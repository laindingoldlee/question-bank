Rationalise each term: $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt k+\sqrt{k+1}}=\dfrac{\sqrt{k+1}-\sqrt k}{(k+1)-k}=\sqrt{k+1}-\sqrt k$. The sum telescopes:
\[\left(\sqrt6-\sqrt5\right)+\left(\sqrt7-\sqrt6\right)+\cdots+\left(\sqrt{20}-\sqrt{19}\right)=\sqrt{20}-\sqrt5=2\sqrt5-\sqrt5=\boxed{\sqrt5}.\]
