Use $T,L,M$ for always truthful, always lying and unrestricted. Fix the alternating second answers as yes at even positions and no at odd positions; rotations may later identify outcomes. For a candidate cyclic word $a_0\ldots a_9$, let $u_i$ mean at least one neighbour is $T$, and $v_i$ mean both are $T$. At a $T$ position require $u_i$ true and $v_i$ equal to the given second answer; at an $L$ position require $u_i$ false and $v_i$ opposite the second answer. An $M$ position imposes neither restriction.
These tests give a short exhaustive procedure: enumerate the $3^{10}$ words, reject those missing a type, apply the two local tests at every position, then identify each surviving word with its least rotation or reversed rotation. Exactly two representatives remain:
\[\boxed{LMLMMTTTMM,\qquad LMMMMTTTMM}.\]
For verification, the three consecutive truth-tellers occupy positions 5,6,7 in these representatives; their second answers are no, yes, no. The liar at position 0, and the optional liar at position 2, have no truthful neighbour and falsely answer yes to both questions. All other answers can be made by the unrestricted people. The enumeration is exhaustive because the local tests are necessary and sufficient for each speaker.
