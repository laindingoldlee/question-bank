Choose $a,b,c$ independently and uniformly from $[250,317]$. The complex roots of $x^3-ax^2+bx-c$ are $p,q,r$, counted with multiplicity. If the probability that $\dfrac{1}{p}+\dfrac{1}{q}+\dfrac{1}{r}>\dfrac{317}{300}$ is written $\dfrac{m}{67^2 n}$, determine $\dfrac{m}{n}$.
