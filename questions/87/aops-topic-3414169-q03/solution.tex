Vieta gives $\dfrac{1}{p}+\dfrac{1}{q}+\dfrac{1}{r}=\dfrac{b}{c}$; no root is zero because $c>0$. In the $bc$ square the favorable region has $250\le c<300$ and $\dfrac{317c}{300}<b\le317$. It is a right triangle of base $50$ and height $317-\dfrac{317\cdot250}{300}=\dfrac{317}{6}$. Its area is $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot50\cdot\dfrac{317}{6}=\dfrac{7925}{6}$. Dividing by the square area $67^2$ shows $\boxed{\dfrac{m}{n}=\dfrac{7925}{6}}$.
