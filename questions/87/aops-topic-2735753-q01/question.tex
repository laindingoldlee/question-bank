In a single-elimination tournament with $2^n$ players ($n\ge1$), each player who loses cheers against the player who beat him. If that player later loses, he cheers for the new winner, then against the next, and so on, alternating along the chain of winners. A player is \emph{happy} if he ends up cheering for the champion; the champion counts as happy too. Prove that exactly $2^{n-1}$ players are happy.
