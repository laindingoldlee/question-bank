A number has exactly $4$ divisors when it is $p^3$ or $pq$ for distinct primes $p$, $q$. Check the numbers ending in $9$ in increasing order:
\begin{itemize}
\item $9=3^2$ has $3$ divisors;
\item $19$ and $29$ are prime ($2$ divisors);
\item $39=3\cdot13$ has exactly the $4$ divisors $1,3,13,39$.
\end{itemize}
The answer is $\boxed{39}$.
