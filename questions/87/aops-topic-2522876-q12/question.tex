There are $12$ cupcakes on a tray. Each day, independently of the other days, John eats exactly one of the remaining cupcakes with probability $\dfrac18$, and eats none with probability $\dfrac78$. What is the expected number of days until he has eaten all the cupcakes (counting the day on which he eats the last one)?
