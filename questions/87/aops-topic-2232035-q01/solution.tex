Let the dog's speed in still water be $d$ and the speed of the current be $w$. Upstream the current slows the dog and downstream it helps:
\[d-w=3,\qquad d+w=4 .\]
Subtracting, $2w=1$, so the current flows at $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}\text{ mile per hour}}$ (and the dog swims at $\dfrac{7}{2}$ miles per hour in still water).
