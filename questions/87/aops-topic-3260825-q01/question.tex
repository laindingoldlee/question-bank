A fictional monthly budget includes $20000$ dollars for a car, $500$ dollars per day for another expense over a $30$-day month, and $100000$ dollars for travel. (a) Find the total. (b) If monthly income must be the sum of two nonnegative integer squares, find the smallest such income covering the budget. An exact finite search may be used.
