The thousands digit $d$ is at least $5$. The other three digits $x,y,z$ are digits (between $0$ and $9$) with $x+y+z=d\le9$, so the upper limit $9$ never matters, and by stars and bars the number of choices is $\dbinom{d+2}{2}$. Every such number is at least $5005>5000$. Summing over $d=5,\dots,9$:
\[
\dbinom72+\dbinom82+\dbinom92+\dbinom{10}{2}+\dbinom{11}{2}=21+28+36+45+55=\boxed{185}.
\]
(A hand list of digit patterns easily goes wrong: patterns such as $4220$ have thousands digit $4$ and must not be counted, and the multiplicities must count arrangements of the last three digits only.)
