The roots have product $-1$ and sum $\dfrac1y-y$, which suggests $-y$ and $\dfrac1y$. Indeed
\[(x+y)\left(x-\dfrac1y\right)=x^2-\dfrac xy+xy-1=x^2+\left(y-\dfrac1y\right)x-1.\]
So $\boxed{x=-y\ \text{ or }\ x=\dfrac1y}$.
