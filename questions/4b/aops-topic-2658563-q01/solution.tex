Since $9999999999=10^{10}-1$,
\[2222222222\times9999999999=22222222220000000000-2222222222=22222222217777777778.\]
The product consists of nine $2$s, one $1$, nine $7$s and a final $8$. Its even digits are the nine $2$s and the $8$, so there are $\boxed{10}$ even digits.
