Let $u=x+23$. Then $x+1=u-22$ and $(x+1)^2=u^2-44u+484$, so
\[
\dfrac{(x+1)^2}{x+23}=u-44+\dfrac{484}{u}.
\]
This is an integer exactly when $u\mid484$. The largest divisor is $u=484$, giving $x=\boxed{461}$ (and indeed $\dfrac{462^2}{484}=441$).
