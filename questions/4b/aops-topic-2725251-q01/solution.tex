In a round with $g$ girl--girl games, $30-2g$ girls play boys, so there are $30-2g$ mixed games, and the remaining $70-(30-2g)=40+2g$ boys form $20+g$ boy--boy games. Summing over three rounds, the number of boy--boy games is $3\cdot20+21=\boxed{81}$.
