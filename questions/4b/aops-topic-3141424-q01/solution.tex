$2019=3\cdot673$. None of the primes 2,3,5,7,11,13,17,19,23 (all primes at most $\sqrt{673}$) divides 673, so it is prime. The divisors are 1,3,673,2019. Their median is $\dfrac{3+673}{2}=\boxed{338}$.
