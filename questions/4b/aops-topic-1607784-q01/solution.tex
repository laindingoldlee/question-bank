The numbers that can be displayed are exactly those of the form $17a+19b$ with integers $a,b\ge0$.

\textbf{$287$ cannot be displayed.} If $17a+19b=287$, reduce modulo $17$: $19\equiv2$ and $287=16\cdot17+15\equiv15$, so $2b\equiv15\equiv32\pmod{17}$, i.e.\ $b\equiv16\pmod{17}$. Then $b\ge16$ and $19b\ge304>287$, which is impossible.

\textbf{Every $n\ge288$ can be displayed.} The numbers $19b$ for $b=0,1,\dots,16$ leave all $17$ different remainders modulo $17$ (as $19\equiv2$ and $2$ is invertible modulo $17$). Choose $b\le16$ with $19b\equiv n\pmod{17}$. Then $n-19b$ is a multiple of $17$, and $n-19b\ge288-304=-16>-17$, so $n-19b\ge0$; write $n-19b=17a$ with $a\ge0$.

Hence the greatest number that cannot be displayed is $\boxed{287}$ (in agreement with the formula $17\cdot19-17-19=287$ for the largest number not of the form $17a+19b$).
