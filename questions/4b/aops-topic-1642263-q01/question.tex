A farmer pays fruit pickers $\$a$ for the first day, $\$(a+d)$ for the second day, $\$(a+2d)$ for the third day, and so on, throughout a $30$-day season. A picker who works all $30$ days earns $\$40.75$ on the final day. Write an equation in $a$ and $d$ that expresses this information.
