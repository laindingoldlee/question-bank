Use $D=(0,0,0)$, $A=(0,0,2)$, $B=(2,0,0)$, $C=(-1,\sqrt3,0)$. Equal distances from the circumcenter $O$ to $D,A,B$ force $O_z=1$, $O_x=1$. Equality with $C$ gives $-O_x+\sqrt3O_y=2$, hence $O_y=\sqrt3$. Thus $R=OD=\sqrt{1+3+1}=\boxed{\sqrt5}$.

usepackage("amsmath");

pair D=(0,0),A=(0,2),Bp=(2,-.5),C=(-1,-1);draw(A--Bp--C--cycle);draw(D--A);draw(D--Bp,dashed);draw(D--C,dashed);label("$D$",D,W);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",Bp,E);label("$C$",C,SW);label("$2$",(0,1),W);
\end{asy}
