A piggy bank contains only pennies and nickels, and $\dfrac27$ of the coins are nickels. If $84$ pennies are removed, then $\dfrac13$ of the remaining coins are pennies. Suppose there are initially $p$ pennies and $n$ nickels.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Write an equation expressing that $\dfrac27$ of the coins are nickels.
\item[(b)] Write a second equation describing the situation after $84$ pennies are removed.
\item[(c)] How many nickels are in the piggy bank?
\end{enumerate}
