A line divides the disc into two equal areas exactly when it passes through the centre $O$. For $P=O$ there are infinitely many such lines with rational slope. For $P\ne O$ the only such line is $OP$, whose slope $\dfrac {y}{x}$ is rational unless $x=0$ (vertical line, no slope).

There are $81$ lattice points with $x^2+y^2\le25$ (for $x=0,\pm1,\pm2,\pm3,\pm4,\pm5$ the numbers of $y$ are $11,9,9,9,7,1$, so $11+2(9+9+9+7+1)=81$). Remove the $11$ with $x=0$ (which include $O$): $\boxed{70}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw(scale(5)*unitcircle,blue); for(int x=-5;x<=5;++x) for(int y=-5;y<=5;++y) if(x*x+y*y<=25) dot((x,y),(x==0?red:black)+linewidth(2.5));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
