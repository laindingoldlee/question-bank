Choose one team in $\dbinom{10}{3}=120$ ways and the other from the remaining $7$ students in $\dbinom73=35$ ways. Every pair of teams is produced twice this way (either team can be chosen first), so the number of ways is
\[\dfrac{120\cdot35}{2}=\boxed{2100}.\]
(If the teams were labelled, say ``red'' and ``blue'', the answer would be $4200$; forgetting to divide by $2$ is the usual source of a mismatch.)
