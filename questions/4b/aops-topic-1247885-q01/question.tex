A propane tank is a cylinder $10$ feet long with a hemisphere of radius $r$ attached at each end. The company's standard tank has diameter $6$ feet. It wants a new tank, of the same shape with the same $10$-foot cylinder, with twice the capacity. What should the radius of the new tank be? Give the answer to the nearest hundredth of a foot.
