Let the radii be $r,s$ and the centre distance be $d>r+s$. The closest points are on the line of centres between the centres, at distance $d-r-s=20$. The farthest points are on the same line on the outward sides, at distance $d+r+s=26$. Subtracting gives $2(r+s)=6$. Hence the sum of circumferences is $2\pi(r+s)=\boxed{6\pi}$.
