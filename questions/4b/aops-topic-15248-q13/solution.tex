There are $\dbinom62=15$ pairs. Two numbers from $1$ to $6$ share a prime factor only if they share $2$ or $3$ ($5$ divides only one of them). Pairs sharing $2$: $\{2,4\},\{2,6\},\{4,6\}$; pairs sharing $3$: $\{3,6\}$. That is $4$ pairs, so $11$ pairs have no common prime factor (pairs with $1$ count, since $1$ has no prime factors). The probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{11}{15}}$.
