The $100$th prime is $541$. Group the primes by hundreds:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lcc}
range & number of primes & sum\\ \hline
$2$--$97$ & $25$ & $1060$\\
$101$--$199$ & $21$ & $3167$\\
$211$--$293$ & $16$ & $4048$\\
$307$--$397$ & $16$ & $5612$\\
$401$--$499$ & $17$ & $7649$\\
$503,509,521,523,541$ & $5$ & $2597$
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The counts add to $25+21+16+16+17+5=100$, and the sum is $1060+3167+4048+5612+7649+2597=\boxed{24133}$.
