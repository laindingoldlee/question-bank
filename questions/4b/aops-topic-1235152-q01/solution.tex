The height to the side $6$ is $\sqrt{25-9}=4$, so the area is $K=12$. Then $R=\dfrac{abc}{4K}=\dfrac{5\cdot5\cdot6}{48}=\boxed{\dfrac{25}{8}}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(-3,0),B=(3,0),C=(0,4),O=(0,4-25/8);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(1)); draw(circle(O,25/8),blue); draw(C--(0,0),dashed); dot(O);
label("$6$",(0,0),S); label("$5$",(A+C)/2,NW); label("$5$",(B+C)/2,NE); label("$4$",(0,2),W);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
