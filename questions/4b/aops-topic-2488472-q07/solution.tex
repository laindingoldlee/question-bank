The circumference is $C=\pi d$, so $\pi d=6\pi$ and $d=\boxed{6}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw(circle((0,0),3),linewidth(0.8));
draw((-3,0)--(3,0),red+linewidth(0.9)); dot((0,0));
label("$d=6$",(1.5,0),N,red);
\end{asy}
