If the list has $n$ numbers, $\dfrac{S+25}{n+1}=\dfrac Sn+1$. Multiplying by $n(n+1)$: $nS+25n=nS+S+n^2+n$, so $S=24n-n^2=144-(n-12)^2$. The maximum is $144$ at $n=12$, with digit sum $9$. \textbf{(D)}
