Alex bikes between home and school every day along the same route. It takes him $20$ minutes to bike to school and only $15$ minutes to bike back. If his average biking pace for the whole round trip is $7$ minutes per mile, how many miles long is the trip from home to school? Express your answer as a decimal to the nearest tenth.
