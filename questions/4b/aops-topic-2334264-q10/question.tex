Call a point $Q$ \emph{good} if it is strictly inside both circles ($OQ<2020$) or inside neither of them ($OQ\ge2021$; a point on a circle is not inside it). Find the smallest possible distance $OP$ such that for every positive real number $r$ there is a good point $Q$ at distance $r$ from $P$.
