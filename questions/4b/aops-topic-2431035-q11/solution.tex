Number the days of 2017 from $1$ (January 1) to $365$ (2017 is not a leap year). The grandchildren call on day $n$ exactly when $3\mid n$, $4\mid n$ or $5\mid n$, respectively. By inclusion-exclusion, the number of days with at least one call is
\[\left\lfloor\dfrac{365}{3}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{365}{4}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{365}{5}\right\rfloor-\left\lfloor\dfrac{365}{12}\right\rfloor-\left\lfloor\dfrac{365}{15}\right\rfloor-\left\lfloor\dfrac{365}{20}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{365}{60}\right\rfloor\]
\[=121+91+73-30-24-18+6=219.\]
So she received no call on $365-219=\boxed{146}$ days.
