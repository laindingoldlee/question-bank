Let $\Phi$ be the standard normal cumulative distribution function. Standardizing gives
\[
\Pr(G>174)=1-\Phi(-0.4)=\Phi(0.4)=\boxed{0.6554\text{ (approximately)}}.
\]
Next,
\[
\Phi\left(\dfrac{k-180}{15}\right)=\Phi(-0.4)-0.3196=0.0249782584\ldots.
\]
Taking the inverse normal cumulative distribution gives $k=150.594958\ldots$, hence $\boxed{k=150.6\text{ kg}}$.
Finally symmetry of $\Phi$ gives
\[
\Phi\left(\dfrac{180-w}{15}\right)=\Phi\left(\dfrac{w-216}{30}\right).
\]
Since $\Phi$ is strictly increasing, $2(180-w)=w-216$, so $\boxed{w=192\text{ kg}}$ and $\boxed{p=\Phi(-0.8)\approx0.2119}$.
