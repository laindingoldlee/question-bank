Gorilla weights $G$ are normally distributed with mean $180$ kg and standard deviation $15$ kg.
\begin{enumerate}\item Find $\Pr(G>174)$.
\item Find $k$ to one decimal place if $\Pr(k<G<174)=0.3196$.
\item Buffalo weights $B$ are normally distributed with mean $216$ kg and standard deviation $30$ kg. If $\Pr(G>w)=\Pr(B<w)=p$, find $w$ and $p$.\end{enumerate}
