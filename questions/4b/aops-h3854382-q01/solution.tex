For a fixed $b$, the equation $a(a+1)=b(b+1)(b+2)$ is equivalent to
\[
(2a+1)^2=4b(b+1)(b+2)+1.
\]
For $b=1,\ldots,10$, the values on the right are
\[
25,\ 97,\ 241,\ 481,\ 841,\ 1345,\ 2017,\ 2881,\ 3961,\ 5281.
\]
The only squares are $25=5^2$ and $841=29^2$. This is checked by comparing each other value with its consecutive bounding squares. Thus $(a,b)=(2,1)$ or $(14,5)$, giving
\[
\boxed{N=6\text{ or }210}.
\]
