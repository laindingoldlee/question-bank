A problem asks for the smallest possible value of the sum of $\operatorname{lcm}(S)$ over all nonempty subsets $S$ of a list of $26$ positive integers $a,b,c,\dots,z$ (one for each letter of the alphabet) satisfying
\[\gcd(a,b)=1,\quad\gcd(b,c)=4,\quad\gcd(c,d)=9,\quad\gcd(d,e)=16,\quad\dots,\quad\gcd(y,z)=625;\]
that is, the greatest common divisor of the $k$-th and $(k+1)$-th numbers is $k^2$ for $k=1,2,\dots,25$. Prove that no positive integers satisfy these conditions, so the problem has no answer.
