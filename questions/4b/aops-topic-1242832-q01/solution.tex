The $k$th term is $2^{2-k}$, so the product of the first $n$ terms is
\[2^{\sum_{k=1}^n(2-k)}=2^{2n-\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}}=2^{-\dfrac{n(n-3)}{2}}.\]
For $n=1,2,3$ it is $2,2,1$, and from then on the exponent $-\dfrac{n(n-3)}{2}$ decreases without bound. So the products tend to $\boxed{0}$.
