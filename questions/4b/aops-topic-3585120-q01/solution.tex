(a) The simultaneous congruences give $C\equiv69\pmod{88}$, hence $C=69$ in the stated range. The trial expression is $563\equiv58\pmod{101}$, so it fails. (b) Write $s=X+Y+Z$ and substitute $Z=s-X-Y$. The condition becomes $65X+70Y-79s\equiv69\pmod{101}$, with $1\le X\le s-2$ and $1\le Y\le s-X-1$. Enumerating these finite ranges for $s=3,4,5,6,7,8$ gives no solution; for $s=9$ it gives $(X,Y,Z)=(1,3,5)$. Directly, $87+276-395=-32\equiv69$. Thus the least sum is $\boxed{9+69=78}$.
