Let $C$ be an integer with $0\le C\le100$, $C\equiv5\pmod8$ and $C\equiv3\pmod{11}$. (a) Find $C$ and check whether $3\cdot87+5\cdot92-2\cdot79$ has remainder $C$ modulo $101$. (b) Find the smallest $X+Y+Z+C$ for positive integers $X,Y,Z$ satisfying $87X+92Y-79Z\equiv C\pmod{101}$.
