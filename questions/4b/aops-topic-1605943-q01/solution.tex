The walls in order around the house are front, left, back, right; front and back are opposite (not adjacent), and so are left and right. Split by whether the two opposite walls front and back match.

\textbf{Front and back have the same finish.} Choose it in $4$ ways. Left and right are each adjacent to both front and back, so each can be any of the other $3$ finishes, independently (left and right are not adjacent): $4\cdot3\cdot3=36$ ways.

\textbf{Front and back have different finishes.} Choose them in $4\cdot3=12$ ways. Left and right must each avoid both of these, so each has $2$ choices: $12\cdot2\cdot2=48$ ways.

Total: $36+48=\boxed{84}$. (This is the number of proper colourings of a $4$-cycle with $4$ colours, $(4-1)^4+(4-1)=84$.)
