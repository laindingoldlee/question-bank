Let $e_1,e_2,e_3$ be the numbers of students in exactly one, exactly two and all three sports. Counting students,
\[
e_1+e_2+e_3=427 .
\]
Adding the three team counts counts each student once per sport played:
\[
e_1+2e_2+3e_3=128+291+318=737 .
\]
Subtracting the first equation from the second gives $e_2+2e_3=310$, and with $e_3=36$ we get $e_2=310-72=\boxed{238}$. (Then $e_1=427-238-36=153\ge0$, so the data are consistent.)
