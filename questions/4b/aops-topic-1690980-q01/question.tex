At Mallard High School there are three intramural sports leagues: football, basketball and baseball. There are $427$ students participating in these sports: $128$ play on football teams, $291$ play on basketball teams, and $318$ play on baseball teams. If exactly $36$ students participate in all three sports, how many students participate in exactly two of the sports?
