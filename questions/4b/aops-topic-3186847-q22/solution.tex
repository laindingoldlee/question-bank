Write $x=k+t$ with integer $k\ge 0$, $0\le t<1$. Then $f(x)=k^2+2kt$ ranges over $[k^2,k^2+2k)$. The integers 2000 through 2023 all lie in $[44^2,44^2+88)$, so $k=44$ and each m determines $x=\dfrac{m+1936}{88}$. Therefore $N=\dfrac{\dfrac{24(2000+2023)}{2}+24\cdot 1936}{88}=\dfrac{94740}{88}$, whose floor is $\boxed{1076}$.
