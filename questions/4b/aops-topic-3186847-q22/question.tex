For $x>0$, let $f(x)=\lfloor x\rfloor(x+\{x\})$, where $\{x\}=x-\lfloor x\rfloor$. There are 24 values of x for which f(x) is an integer from 2000 to 2023. Let their sum be N. Find $\lfloor N\rfloor$.
