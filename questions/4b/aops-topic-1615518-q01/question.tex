A \emph{derangement} of $n$ objects is a permutation in which no object stays in its original position; let $D_n$ be the number of derangements of $n$ objects.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $D_n=n!\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n}\dfrac{(-1)^k}{k!}$.
\item[(b)] Prove that for every $n\ge1$, $D_n$ is the integer closest to $\dfrac{n!}{e}$.
\item[(c)] Deduce that the probability that a random permutation of $n$ objects is a derangement tends to $\dfrac{1}{e}$ as $n\to\infty$.
\end{enumerate}
