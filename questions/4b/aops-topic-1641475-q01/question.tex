\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] What is the volume of a sphere whose diameter is $20$?
\item[(b)] Compare it with the volume of the smallest cylinder that contains the sphere (radius $10$, height $20$). Which is larger, and what fraction of the cylinder does the sphere fill?
\end{enumerate}
