Let the triangle have side $t$ and the hexagon side $h$. The hexagon is made of six equilateral triangles of side $h$, so
\[
\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}t^2=6\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}h^2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad t^2=6h^2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad t=\sqrt6\,h .
\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real h=1; real t=sqrt(6)*h;
pair A=(0,0),B=(t,0),C=(t/2,t*sqrt(3)/2); draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); label("$t$",(A+B)/2,S);
path hx=shift(t+1.6,0.9)*scale(h)*polygon(6); draw(hx,linewidth(0.9)); label("$h$",(t+1.6,0.9)+h*(dir(0)+dir(60))/2,NE);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The ratio of perimeters is $\dfrac{3t}{6h}=\dfrac{3\sqrt6\,h}{6h}=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt6}{2}}\approx1.22$.
