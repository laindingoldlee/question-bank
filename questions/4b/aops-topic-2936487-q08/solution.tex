Factor $143=11\cdot13$. Every integer 144 through 153 is divisible by neither 11 nor 13, so is coprime to 143. A common multiple would then be at least $143(144)=20592>10000$. But 154 shares factor 11 with 143, and their least common multiple is $2\cdot7\cdot11\cdot13=2002<10000$. Therefore $\boxed{154}$.
