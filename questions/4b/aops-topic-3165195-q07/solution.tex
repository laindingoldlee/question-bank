The original area is $\dfrac{1}{2}(15)(20)=150$, so the altitude to the 25-unit hypotenuse is $\dfrac{2\cdot150}{25}=12$. The hypotenuse segments are $\dfrac{15^2}{25}=9$ and $\dfrac{20^2}{25}=16$, by similarity. The two areas are $\dfrac{1}{2}(12)(9)=54$ and $\dfrac{1}{2}(12)(16)=96$. Their difference is $\boxed{42}$.

\end{asy}
