\(59\equiv3\pmod7\). By Fermat's little theorem \(3^6\equiv1\pmod 7\), and \(28=6\cdot4+4\), so
\[
59^{28}\equiv3^{28}\equiv3^4=81\equiv4\pmod7 .
\]
The remainder is \(\boxed{4}\).
