Put $F(k)=\lfloor \dfrac{k}{4}\rfloor+\lfloor \dfrac{k}{5}\rfloor+\lfloor \dfrac{k}{6}\rfloor$. Then $F(60q+r)=37q+F(r)$ for $0\le r<60$.

On $0\le r<60$, $F$ starts at zero and increases exactly at the positive multiples of $4,5,$ or $6$. Inclusion-exclusion counts these increase points as
\[
14+11+9-2-4-1=27.
\]
Thus this block has $28$ distinct values, all between $F(0)=0$ and $F(59)=34$. Different blocks have disjoint ranges, separated by shifts of $37$.

Since $1000=27\cdot37+1$, the blocks $q=0,\ldots,26$ each contribute all $28$ values. The block $q=27$ contributes only $37\cdot27=999$, and later blocks contribute none. Therefore the answer is $\boxed{27\cdot28+1=757}$.
