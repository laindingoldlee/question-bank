INDIANA has the letters I, I, N, N, D, A, A. We glue I and N into blocks and use inclusion-exclusion.

\textbf{At least one IN, counted with multiplicity.} Glue one I and one N into a block IN; the objects are IN, I, N, D, A, A, with $\dfrac{6!}{2!}=360$ arrangements. An arrangement with two IN blocks is counted twice here (once for each block chosen).

\textbf{Two IN blocks.} The objects are IN, IN, D, A, A, with $\dfrac{5!}{2!\,2!}=30$ arrangements.

So the number with at least one IN is $360-30=\boxed{330}$, out of $\dfrac{7!}{2!\,2!\,2!}=630$ arrangements in all.
