The goat is the only item that conflicts with both others, so it must travel first and be the one brought back.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Take the goat across; return alone.
\item Take the lion across; bring the goat back.
\item Take the hay across (leaving the goat on the start bank alone); return alone.
\item Take the goat across.
\end{enumerate}
At no time are the lion and goat, or the goat and hay, left alone together, so this works, using $7$ crossings. (The roles of the lion and the hay in steps 2 and 3 can be swapped.)
