\textbf{(a)} The range is $2.5-0.5=\boxed{2.0}$.

\textbf{(b)} The standard deviation measures the typical distance of the data from the mean: it is the square root of the average squared deviation. The mean is $\dfrac{9}{6}=1.5$. The deviations are $-1,\ 0.5,\ 1,\ 0,\ -0.5,\ 0$, with squares $1,\ 0.25,\ 1,\ 0,\ 0.25,\ 0$, summing to $2.5$. The variance is $\dfrac{2.5}{6}\approx0.4167$, so the standard deviation is
\[\sigma=\sqrt{\dfrac{2.5}{6}}\approx\boxed{0.65}.\]
(The \emph{sample} standard deviation divides by $6-1=5$ instead and gives $\sqrt{0.5}\approx0.71$.)
