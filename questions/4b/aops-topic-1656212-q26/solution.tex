The number of trailing zeros of $2018!$ is the exponent of $5$:
\[
\left\lfloor\dfrac{2018}{5}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{2018}{25}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{2018}{125}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{2018}{625}\right\rfloor=403+80+16+3=502\ge250 .
\]
So the rightmost $250$ digits are all $0$, and their sum is $\boxed{\textbf{(a)}\ 0}$.
