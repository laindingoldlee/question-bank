A circle tangent to both axes has centre $(\pm r,\pm r)$ and radius $r$. The point $(-8,-1)$ is in the third quadrant and the circle does not cross the axes, so the centre is $(-r,-r)$. The circle passes through $(-8,-1)$ when
\[
(r-8)^2+(r-1)^2=r^2\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad r^2-18r+65=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad(r-5)(r-13)=0 .
\]
So the centres are $O_1=(-5,-5)$ and $O_2=(-13,-13)$.

Their distance is $\sqrt{8^2+8^2}=\boxed{8\sqrt2}$.
