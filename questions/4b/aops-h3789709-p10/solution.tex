The six numbers sum to $75$. When adding the three side sums, each vertex is counted twice and each midpoint once. Therefore $3S=75+V$, where $V$ is the sum of the three vertex numbers. Since $V\le13+14+15=42$, $S\le39$. Place $13,14,15$ at the vertices, and $12,10,11$ on the respective sides joining $(13,14),(14,15),(15,13)$. Every side then sums to $39$, so the maximum is $\boxed{39}$.
