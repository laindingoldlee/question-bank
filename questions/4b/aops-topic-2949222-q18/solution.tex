Substitute $B=10000-A$. The expression becomes $9999A+10000$, with $1\le A\le9999$. The finite digit check below counts every pair once:\begin{verbatim}
ans=0
for A in range(1,10000):
 s=str(9999*A+10000)
 ans+=len(s)==len(set(s))
print(ans) # 384
\end{verbatim}
The answer is $\boxed{384}$.
