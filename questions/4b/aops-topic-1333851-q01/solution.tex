Let $R$ be the event that every rank appears and $U$ the event that every suit appears. There are $\dbinom{25}{10}=3268760$ hands.

By inclusion-exclusion over the missing ranks (a hand missing $k$ given ranks is chosen from $25-5k$ cards),
\[
|R|=\sum_{k=0}^{5}(-1)^k\dbinom5k\dbinom{25-5k}{10}=3268760-5\cdot184756+10\cdot3003-10\cdot1=2375000,
\]
and $|U|=|R|$ by symmetry. A hand missing $i$ given ranks and $j$ given suits is chosen from $(5-i)(5-j)$ cards, so
\[
|R\cap U|=\sum_{i=0}^{5}\sum_{j=0}^{5}(-1)^{i+j}\dbinom5i\dbinom5j\dbinom{(5-i)(5-j)}{10}=1674840 .
\]
Hence $|R\cup U|=2\cdot2375000-1674840=3075160$, and the probability is
\[
\dfrac{3075160}{3268760}=\boxed{\dfrac{6989}{7429}}\approx0.941 .
\]
