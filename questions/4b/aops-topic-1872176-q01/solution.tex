Each trailing zero comes from a factor $10=2\cdot5$, and $420!$ has many more factors $2$ than factors $5$, so we count the factors $5$ (Legendre's formula):
\[
\left\lfloor\dfrac{420}{5}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{420}{25}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{420}{125}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{420}{625}\right\rfloor=84+16+3+0=\boxed{103}.
\]
