First, $(-a)b=-(ab)$: indeed $ab+(-a)b=(a+(-a))b=0\cdot b=0$, so $(-a)b$ is the additive inverse of $ab$.

Now
\[
(-a)(-b)+(-a)b=(-a)\bigl((-b)+b\bigr)=(-a)\cdot0=0,
\]
so $(-a)(-b)$ is the additive inverse of $(-a)b=-(ab)$. The additive inverse of $-(ab)$ is $ab$, and inverses are unique, so $(-a)(-b)=ab$. In particular a negative number times a negative number is positive: if $a,b>0$ then $(-a)(-b)=ab>0$. $\blacksquare$
