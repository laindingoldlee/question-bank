Put $C=(0,0),A=(a,0),B=(0,b)$ and $AB=c$. The angle-bisector theorem gives $CL=\dfrac{ab}{b+c}$. The midpoint of $BL$ is $(\dfrac{ab}{2(b+c)},\dfrac{b}{2})$. Since it lies on the altitude from $C$, whose equation is $y=\dfrac{a}{b} x$, we get $b(b+c)=a^2=c^2-b^2$. Thus $(c-2b)(c+b)=0$, and $c=2b$. The right triangle therefore has $\sin\angle BAC=\dfrac{b}{c}=\dfrac{1}{2}$, giving $\boxed{30^\circ}$.

\end{asy}
