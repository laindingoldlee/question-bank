Alex published a book a year ago. He paid the author $\$20{,}000$, and printing each copy cost $\$15$. Each copy sold for $\$30$, and Alex paid the bookstore $30\%$ of the sale price of each copy sold. Assume he printed exactly the copies that were sold. If Alex made a profit of at least $10\%$ of his costs (author's fee plus printing) at the end of the year, what is the least possible number of books sold?
