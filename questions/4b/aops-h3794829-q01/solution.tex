Each digit occurs twice in the tens place and twice in the units place, so the sum is $22(a+b+c)$. Equating this to $100b+10c+a$ gives $7a+4c=26b$. Modulo $2$, $a$ is even; modulo $7$, $4c\equiv5b$. Also $b\le3$, since $7a+4c\le99$. For $b=1,2,3$, solving with $a\in\{2,4,6,8\}$ gives respectively $(a,c)=(2,3),(4,6),(6,9)$. Thus $\boxed{(a,b,c)=(2,1,3),(4,2,6),(6,3,9)}$.
