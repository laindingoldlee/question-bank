List the factorisations of $100$ into three distinct factors in increasing order: $1\cdot2\cdot50$, $1\cdot4\cdot25$, $1\cdot5\cdot20$ and $2\cdot5\cdot10$. (With $a\ge3$ we would need $a^3<100$ and $a\mid100$, so $a=4$, and then $bc=25$ with $4<b<c$ is impossible.) Answer $\boxed{\textbf{(E)}\ 4}$.
