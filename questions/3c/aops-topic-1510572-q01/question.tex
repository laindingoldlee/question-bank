\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $ab+bc+ca$ cannot be written as a product of two non-constant polynomials in $a,b,c$ (with real or complex coefficients).
\item[(b)] Prove the same for $abc+bcd+cda+dab$, as a polynomial in $a,b,c,d$.
\end{enumerate}
