We need the number of solutions of $a+b+c=100$ in positive integers, where $a$, $b$, $c$ are the numbers of fish above, through and below the pipe. Put one fish in each group first; then $97$ fish remain to be shared out with no restriction, that is, $a'+b'+c'=97$ in nonnegative integers. By stars and bars the number of solutions is
\[
\dbinom{97+2}{2}=\dbinom{99}{2}=\dfrac{99\cdot98}{2}=\boxed{4851}.
\]
