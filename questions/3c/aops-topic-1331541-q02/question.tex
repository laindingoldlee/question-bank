A school of $100$ fish comes to a very wide horizontal pipe. To reach the food on the other side, each fish swims above the pipe, through the pipe or below the pipe. If we do not distinguish between individual fish, in how many ways can the school be split into three groups, one for each of the three routes, with at least one fish in each group?
