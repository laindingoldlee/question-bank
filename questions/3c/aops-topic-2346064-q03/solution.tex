The garden has area $6\cdot8=48$ square feet (it is made of $48$ squares of $1$ square foot each):

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
for(int i=0;i<=8;++i) draw((i,0)--(i,6),gray(0.7));
for(int j=0;j<=6;++j) draw((0,j)--(8,j),gray(0.7));
draw((0,0)--(8,0)--(8,6)--(0,6)--cycle,linewidth(1));
label("$8$ ft",(4,0),S); label("$6$ ft",(8,3),E);
\end{asy}

She plants $4\cdot48=192$ plants and expects $10\cdot192=1920$ strawberries. The answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(D) }1920}$.
