Write $u=a^2$, $v=b^2$, $w=c^2$, and let the area be $K$. The condition is $u+2v+3w=12$. Heron's identity gives
\[
16K^2=Q=2uv+2vw+2wu-u^2-v^2-w^2.
\]
The following sum-of-squares identity provides a sharp bound:
\[
(u+2v+3w)^2-11Q
=\dfrac{(12u-9v-8w)^2+11(3v-4w)^2}{12}\ge0.
\]
Thus $176K^2\le144$, so $K\le\dfrac{3}{\sqrt{11}}$.

Equality requires $u:v:w=5:4:3$. Combined with the given sum, this gives
\[
(a^2,b^2,c^2)=\left(\dfrac{30}{11},\dfrac{24}{11},\dfrac{18}{11}\right).
\]
These lengths form a nondegenerate triangle and attain the bound. Therefore the maximum area is $\boxed{3\dfrac{\sqrt{11}}{11}}$.
