If $c$ answers are correct and $w$ wrong, with $c,w\ge0$ and $c+w\le100$, the score is $3c-w$. All scores lie between $-100$ (all wrong) and $300$ (all correct), a range of $401$ integers.

For a fixed $c$, the score takes every value from $3c-(100-c)=4c-100$ up to $3c$. The intervals $[4c-100,\,3c]$ for consecutive $c$ overlap or touch as long as $4(c+1)-100\le3c+1$, i.e. $c\le97$. So every integer from $-100$ up to $3\cdot97=291$ is reached, and also $c=98$ gives $292,293,294$, $c=99$ gives $296,297$ and $c=100$ gives $300$.

The unreachable values are therefore exactly $295$, $298$ and $299$. (For example $299=3c-w$ needs $c=100$, $w=1$, which is $101$ questions.) The number of possible scores is
\[
401-3=\boxed{398}.
\]
