Two days complete $\dfrac{1}{7}+\dfrac{1}{8}=\dfrac{15}{56}$ of the job. After six days, $\dfrac{45}{56}$ is done. On day 7, $A$ completes another $\dfrac{8}{56}$, leaving $\dfrac{3}{56}$. Worker $B$ needs $\dfrac{\dfrac{3}{56}}{\dfrac{1}{8}}=\dfrac{3}{7}$ day for that remainder. The total is $\boxed{\dfrac{52}{7}\text{ days}}$.
