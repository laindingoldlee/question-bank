Among the $2700$ digits, the hundreds digits are $1$-$9$ ($100$ times each) and the tens and units digits are $0$-$9$ ($90$ times each per place).

Squares: $0,1,4,9$ have one digit; $16,25,36,49,64,81$ have two. The ones written come from $1\to1$, $4\to16$ and $9\to81$, each contributing one ``$1$''.

Total digits written: hundreds place: $100(3\cdot1+6\cdot2)=1500$; tens and units: $2\cdot90(4\cdot1+6\cdot2)=2880$. Total $4380$.

Ones written: digits $1,4,9$ occur $300$ times in the hundreds place and $2\cdot90\cdot3=540$ times elsewhere: $840$.

The proportion is $\dfrac{840}{4380}=\dfrac{14}{73}$, so $m+n=\boxed{87}$.
