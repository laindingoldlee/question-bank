Jeremy wrote all the three-digit integers from $100$ to $999$ on a blackboard. Then Allison erased each of the $2700$ digits and replaced it with its square ($1\to1$, $2\to4$, $3\to9$, $4\to16$, and so on). The proportion of all the digits Allison wrote that were ones is $\dfrac {m}{n}$ in lowest terms. Find $m+n$.
