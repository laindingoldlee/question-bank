Every prime other than $2$ is odd. If both primes were odd, their sum would be even and at least $3+3=6$, so it would be an even number greater than $2$, hence not prime. Therefore at least one of the two primes is even, and the only even prime is $2$. $\blacksquare$

(For example $2+3=5$ and $2+5=7$; the other prime $p$ must be such that $p+2$ is also prime.)
