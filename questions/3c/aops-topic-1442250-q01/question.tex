Two candidates are running for president, each having $5$ supporters, and each supporter supports only one candidate. In the election, each supporter independently has a $50\%$ chance to vote for the candidate they support and a $50\%$ chance to be lazy and not vote. What is the probability that the election is a tie? (Both candidates having no votes also counts as a tie.)
