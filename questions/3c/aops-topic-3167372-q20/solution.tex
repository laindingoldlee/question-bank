Write $f(x)=ax^4+bx^3+cx^2+dx+2$. The values at $\pm1$ give $a+c=\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $b+d=\dfrac{3}{2}$. Equality at $\pm2$ gives $b=-\dfrac{1}{2}$, hence $d=2$. Substituting $c=\dfrac{1}{2}-a$ yields $f(2)=12a+4$ and $f(3)=72a-1$. Their equality gives $a=\dfrac{1}{12}$, $c=\dfrac{5}{12}$. Therefore $f(4)=\dfrac{256+80}{12}-32+8+2=\boxed6$.
