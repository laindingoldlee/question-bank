The differences have moduli $2 | \sin (\dfrac{\theta_k}{2}) |$ and $2|\sin\theta_k|$, respectively. For every $t>0$, $|\sin t|<t$, whereas both sides vanish at $t=0$. Thus each first summand is at most $\theta_k$ and each second at most $2\theta_k$. At least one $\theta_k$ is positive, since their sum is $2\pi$, so each total inequality is strict.
