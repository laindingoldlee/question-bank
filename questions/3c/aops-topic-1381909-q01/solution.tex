Since $2x<1000$, $x\le499$, so the hundreds digit $a$ of $x$ is at most $4$. With tens digit $b$ and units digit $c$, $b+c=19-a\ge15$, so $b,c\ge6$.

When a number is doubled, the digit sum doubles and then drops by $9$ for every carry. Doubling a digit produces a carry exactly when the digit is at least $5$. Here $b$ and $c$ are at least $6$ and $a\le4$, so there are exactly two carries, and
\[
S(2x)=2\cdot19-2\cdot9=\boxed{20}.
\]
(For example $x=496$: $2x=992$ with digit sum $20$.)
