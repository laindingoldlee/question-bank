Let $T_p=1+2+\cdots+p=\dfrac{p(p+1)}{2}$. Then
\[T_{p+20}-T_p=(p+1)+(p+2)+\cdots+(p+20)=20p+210=10(2p+21),\]
which is a multiple of $10$. So $T_{p+20}$ and $T_p$ have the same last digit: $a_{p+20}=a_p$, and the sequence $a_p$ repeats with period $20$.

The first $20$ triangular numbers are $1,3,6,10,15,21,28,36,45,55,66,78,91,105,120,136,153,171,190,210$, so $a_1,\dots,a_{20}$ are
\[1,3,6,0,5,1,8,6,5,5,6,8,1,5,0,6,3,1,0,0,\]
with sum $70$.

Since $2017=20\cdot100+17$, the sum consists of $100$ full periods and then $a_1,\dots,a_{17}$, whose sum is $70-(a_{18}+a_{19}+a_{20})=70-1=69$. Therefore
\[a_1+\cdots+a_{2017}=100\cdot70+69=7069,\]
and the remainder on division by $1000$ is $\boxed{69}$.
