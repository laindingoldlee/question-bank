Let $ABC$ be a triangle, and let $D$, $E$, $F$ be points on the sides $BC$, $CA$, $AB$ such that $AD$, $BE$, $CF$ meet at a point $X$. The ratios $\dfrac{BD}{DC}$, $\dfrac{CE}{EA}$, $\dfrac{AF}{FB}$ all lie in the interval $\left[\dfrac15,5\right]$, and
\[\dfrac{BD}{DC}+\dfrac{CE}{EA}+\dfrac{AF}{FB}=\dfrac{31}{5}.\]
Then $\dfrac{AX}{XD}+\dfrac{BX}{XE}+\dfrac{CX}{XF}=\dfrac mn$ for relatively prime positive integers $m$ and $n$. Find $m+n$.
