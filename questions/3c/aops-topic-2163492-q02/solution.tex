Let the width be $w$ metres, so the length is $w+3$. The perimeter is $2(w+w+3)=4w+6$ and the area is $w(w+3)$. Setting them equal,
\[w^2+3w=4w+6\iff w^2-w-6=0\iff(w-3)(w+2)=0 .\]
Since $w>0$, $w=3$. The rectangle is $\boxed{3\text{ m}\times6\text{ m}}$ (perimeter $18$, area $18$).
