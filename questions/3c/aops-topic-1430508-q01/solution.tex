Each step subtracts $10$, so $h$ is an arithmetic sequence with first term $-75$ and common difference $-10$:
\[
h(n)=-75-10(n-1)=\boxed{-10n-65}.
\]
(Check: $h(1)=-75$, and $h(n)-h(n-1)=-10$.)
