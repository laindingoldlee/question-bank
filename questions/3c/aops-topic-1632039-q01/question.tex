Billy can mow a lawn in $6$ hours and paint a roof in $2$ hours. Joel can mow the lawn in $8$ hours and paint the roof in $4$ hours. Elton can mow the lawn in $4$ hours and paint the roof in $4$ hours.

Billy and Elton begin painting the roof while Joel begins mowing the lawn. When Billy and Elton finish painting the roof, Elton goes inside to play the piano while Billy helps Joel finish mowing the lawn. Assuming everyone works at a constant rate, how long does it take, from the start, until both the roof is painted and the lawn is mowed?
