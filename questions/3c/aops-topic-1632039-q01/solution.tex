Measure work in ``jobs per hour''.

\textbf{The roof.} Billy paints $\dfrac{1}{2}$ of the roof per hour and Elton $\dfrac{1}{4}$, so together they paint $\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{3}{4}$ of the roof per hour and finish it in $\dfrac{4}{3}$ hours.

\textbf{The lawn so far.} Joel mows $\dfrac{1}{8}$ of the lawn per hour, so after $\dfrac{4}{3}$ hours he has mowed $\dfrac{1}{8}\cdot\dfrac{4}{3}=\dfrac{1}{6}$ of it, leaving $\dfrac{5}{6}$.

\textbf{The rest of the lawn.} Billy and Joel together mow $\dfrac{1}{6}+\dfrac{1}{8}=\dfrac{7}{24}$ of the lawn per hour, so the remaining $\dfrac{5}{6}$ takes
\[
\dfrac{5}{6}\div\dfrac{7}{24}=\dfrac{5}{6}\cdot\dfrac{24}{7}=\dfrac{20}{7}\text{ hours}.
\]

\textbf{Total.}
\[
\dfrac{4}{3}+\dfrac{20}{7}=\dfrac{28+60}{21}=\boxed{\dfrac{88}{21}\text{ hours}}\approx 4\text{ hours }11\text{ minutes}.
\]
