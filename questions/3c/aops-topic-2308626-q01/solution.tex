We work modulo $100$. Since $3^5=243\equiv43$,
\[3^{10}\equiv43^2=1849\equiv49,\qquad 3^{20}\equiv49^2=2401\equiv1\pmod{100}.\]
As $1993=20\cdot99+13$,
\[3^{1993}=\left(3^{20}\right)^{99}\cdot3^{13}\equiv3^{13}=3^{10}\cdot3^3\equiv49\cdot27=1323\equiv23\pmod{100}.\]
The last two digits are $\boxed{23}$.
