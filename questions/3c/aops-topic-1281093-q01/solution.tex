As fractions of the original height, after $t$ hours the candles have heights $1-\dfrac {t}{4}$ and $1-\dfrac {t}{3}$. We need $1-\dfrac {t}{4}=2\left(1-\dfrac {t}{3}\right)$, i.e. $\dfrac{2t}{3}-\dfrac {t}{4}=1$, so $\dfrac{5t}{12}=1$ and $t=\dfrac{12}{5}$ hours $=\boxed{2\text{ hours }24\text{ minutes}}$. (Then the heights are $\dfrac{2}{5}$ and $\dfrac{1}{5}$.)
