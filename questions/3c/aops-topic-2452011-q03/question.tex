A cake is made of $22$ solid cylinders. The cylinder with radius $k$ has height $(23-k)^2$, for $k=1,2,\dots,22$; that is, the radii $1,2,3,\dots,22$ go with the heights $22^2,21^2,20^2,\dots,1^2$ respectively. What is the total volume of the cake?
