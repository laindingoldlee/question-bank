Let $A$ be Marty's eventual win probability immediately after a Marty win, and $B$ that probability after a Molly win. Then $A=\dfrac{3}{4}+\dfrac{1}{4}B$ and $B=\dfrac{3}{4}A$. Solving gives $A=\dfrac{12}{13},B=\dfrac{9}{13}$. Before any game the probability is $\dfrac{3}{4}A+\dfrac{1}{4}B=\boxed{\dfrac{45}{52}}$.
