$\left(\dfrac{\sqrt6-\sqrt2}{2}\right)^2=\dfrac{6+2-2\sqrt{12}}{4}=\dfrac{8-4\sqrt3}{4}=2-\sqrt3$, and $\dfrac{\sqrt6-\sqrt2}{2}>0$. So $\sqrt{2-\sqrt3}=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt6-\sqrt2}{2}}$.
