Put $ k=\lfloor r\rfloor $ and $ t=r-k\in[0,1)$. The 73 summands are each $ k $ or $ k+1 $, so $ 73k\le546\le73(k+1)$. Thus $ k=7 $, and exactly $ 546-73(7)=35 $ terms must equal eight. Since the terms increase with $ j $, these are exactly $ j=57,58,\ldots,91 $. Therefore $ t+\dfrac{56}{100}<1\le t+\dfrac{57}{100}$, or $ 0.43\le t<0.44 $. Hence $\boxed{\lfloor100r\rfloor=743}$.
