Points $M$, $N$, $P$ satisfy $\overrightarrow{MP}=2\,\overrightarrow{MN}$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that if $M\ne N$, then the vectors $\overrightarrow{NM}$ and $\overrightarrow{NP}$ are different.
\item[(b)] Show that the condition $M\ne N$ cannot be dropped.
\end{enumerate}
