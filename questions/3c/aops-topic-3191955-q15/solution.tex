Factor the denominator and subtract $1$: the expression is $1+\dfrac{16(n+3)}{(n+1)(n+3)}=1+\dfrac{16}{n+1}$. Thus $n+1$ is a divisor of $16$ exceeding $1$, giving $n=1,3,7,15$. Their sum is $\boxed{26}$.
