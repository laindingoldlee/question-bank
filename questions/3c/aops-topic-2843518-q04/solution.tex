Factor $n^4-3n^2+9=(n^2+3n+3)(n^2-3n+3)$. Both factors are positive integers, since each equals a square plus $\dfrac{3}{4}$. For the product to be prime, one factor must be 1. Solving $n^2\pm3n+3=1$ gives $n=\pm1,\pm2$. The resulting prime values are $\boxed{7,13}$.
