Leading binary zeros do not affect the bit count. Enumerate bit counts $k=1,\ldots,8$: choose $k$ positions from $0,\ldots,7$, form $N$ as the sum of the corresponding powers of two, retain $100\le N\le255$, and test $k\mid N$. This enumerates each permitted integer once. For weight $1$ the valid numbers are $128$. For weight $2$ the valid numbers are $130,132,136,144,160,192$. For weight $3$ the valid numbers are $138,162,168$. For weight $4$ the valid numbers are $108,116,120,156,172,180,184,204,212,216,228,232,240$. For weight $5$ the valid numbers are $110,115,155,185,205,220,230$. For weight $6$ the valid numbers are $126,222,246,252$. The resulting counts sum to $\boxed{34}$.
