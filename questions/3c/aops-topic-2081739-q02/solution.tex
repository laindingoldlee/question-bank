After my first serving \(\dfrac{2}{3}\) of the cake is left. Becky eats \(\dfrac{1}{5}\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}=\dfrac{2}{15}\), leaving \(\dfrac{8}{15}\). Jim eats half, leaving \(\dfrac{4}{15}\), which is my second serving. The ratio is
\[
\dfrac{1}{3}\div\dfrac{4}{15}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{4}}.
\]
