\textbf{Finding the formula.} Using $1^3+2^3+\cdots+m^3=\left(\dfrac{m(m+1)}{2}\right)^2$, take all cubes up to $(2n)^3$ and remove the even ones, $(2k)^3=8k^3$:
\[\sum_{k=1}^{n}(2k-1)^3=\left(\dfrac{2n(2n+1)}{2}\right)^2-8\left(\dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}\right)^2=n^2(2n+1)^2-2n^2(n+1)^2=n^2\left(2n^2-1\right).\]
So the formula is
\[1^3+3^3+\cdots+(2n-1)^3=\boxed{n^2\left(2n^2-1\right)}.\]

\textbf{Proof by induction.} For $n=1$ both sides equal $1$. Suppose the formula holds for $n$. Then
\[1^3+\cdots+(2n-1)^3+(2n+1)^3=n^2\left(2n^2-1\right)+(2n+1)^3=2n^4+8n^3+11n^2+6n+1,\]
and
\[(n+1)^2\left(2(n+1)^2-1\right)=\left(n^2+2n+1\right)\left(2n^2+4n+1\right)=2n^4+8n^3+11n^2+6n+1 .\]
These agree, so the formula holds for $n+1$, and by induction for every positive integer $n$. (Check: $1+27+125=153=9\cdot17$ for $n=3$.)
