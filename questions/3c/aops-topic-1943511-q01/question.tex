Two players play the following game. There are two piles of stones, with $p$ and $q$ stones ($p,q\ge0$ integers, not both $0$). The players move alternately; a move is either
\begin{itemize}
\item take any positive number of stones from one pile, or
\item take the same positive number of stones from both piles.
\end{itemize}
The player who takes the last stone (leaving both piles empty) wins. Let $\varphi=\dfrac{1+\sqrt5}{2}$. Prove that the second player has a winning strategy exactly when
\[
\{p,q\}=\{\lfloor k\varphi\rfloor,\ \lfloor k\varphi^2\rfloor\}\quad\text{for some positive integer }k,
\]
and describe the winning strategy in every other case.
