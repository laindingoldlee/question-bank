Since $35\mid n$, the divisors 1, 5, 7 are below 35. Neither 2 nor 3 can divide $n$: otherwise respectively 1, 2, 5, 7, 10, 14 or 1, 3, 5, 7, 15, 21 already give six smaller divisors. With 2 and 3 excluded, the only additional possible divisors below 35 are 25 and primes 11, 13, 17, 19, 23, 29, 31. Exactly two such additions are required. Using 25 and the smallest prime 11 forces $n\ge\operatorname{lcm}(35,25,11)=1925$; using two primes forces $n\ge35\cdot11\cdot13=5005$. The number 1925 has the first six divisors 1, 5, 7, 11, 25, 35. Therefore $\boxed{1925}$.
