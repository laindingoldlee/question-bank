Write $s=a+b+c$. AM--GM gives $abc\le(\dfrac{s}{3})^3$. Therefore $7s\le\dfrac{s^3}{27}$, and positivity yields $s^2\ge189$. Hence $s\ge3\sqrt{21}$. Equality is attained at $a=b=c=\sqrt{21}$, which satisfies the condition. The minimum is $\boxed{3\sqrt{21}}$.
