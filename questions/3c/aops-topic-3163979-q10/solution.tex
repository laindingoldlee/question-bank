Put $AM=BM=BC=s$, $CM=t$, and $AC=u$. The median-length formula gives $u^2+s^2=2t^2+2s^2$, so $s^2=u^2-2t^2$. In triangle $AMC$, the law of cosines at $C$ gives $s^2=t^2+u^2-tu$, since $\cos60^\circ=\dfrac{1}{2}$. Equating yields $3t^2=tu$. Since $t>0$, $u=3t$ and $\boxed{\dfrac{CM}{AC}=\dfrac{1}{3}}$.
