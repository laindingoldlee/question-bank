Let a pencil cost $p$. The first purchase costs $8p+2(2p)=12p$, so the initial money was $60p$ and the remainder is $48p$. The next purchase costs $\dfrac{5}{8}(48p)=30p$. At $2p$ per marker, she buys $\boxed{15}$ additional markers.
