For a fixed $n$, any three copies of $3^j$ in a representation can be replaced by one $3^{j+1}$, reducing the number of summands. Repeating gives the base-three expansion, so the minimum count is its digit sum. If $n<729=3^6$, there are at most six ternary digits, with sum at most $12$, attained by $728=(222222)_3$. If $729\le n<1000$, the leading digit is $1$ and the remaining part is at most $270$. Its $3^5$ digit is at most $1$ and the other five digits at most $2$, giving total digit sum at most $1+1+10=12$. Hence the rabbit can force exactly $\boxed{12}$.
