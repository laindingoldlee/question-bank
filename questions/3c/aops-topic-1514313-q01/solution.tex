The circle is $(x-8)^2+(y-9)^2=100$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $x$-axis ($y=0$): $(x-8)^2=100-81=19$, so $x=8\pm\sqrt{19}$: two points. (The distance $9$ from the centre to the $x$-axis is less than the radius.)
\item $y$-axis ($x=0$): $(y-9)^2=100-64=36$, so $y=3$ or $y=15$: two points.
\end{itemize}
