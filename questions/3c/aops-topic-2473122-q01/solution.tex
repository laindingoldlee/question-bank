On the line $y=kx+c$ (the line $y=kx$ is the case $c=0$) we have $y_1=kx_1+c$ and $y_2=kx_2+c$, so $y_1-y_2=k(x_1-x_2)$. By the distance formula,
\[\sqrt{(x_1-x_2)^2+(y_1-y_2)^2}=\sqrt{(x_1-x_2)^2+k^2(x_1-x_2)^2}=\sqrt{(x_1-x_2)^2\left(1+k^2\right)}=\boxed{|x_1-x_2|\sqrt{k^2+1}}.\]
Geometrically, the segment is the hypotenuse of a right triangle with a horizontal leg $|x_1-x_2|$ and a vertical leg $|k|\,|x_1-x_2|$, because the slope is the ratio of the vertical change to the horizontal change.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real k=0.6;
draw((-0.4,0)--(5.4,0),Arrow(6)); draw((0,-0.4)--(0,3.6),Arrow(6));
label("$x$",(5.4,0),E); label("$y$",(0,3.6),N);
draw((-0.4,-0.24)--(5.4,3.24),blue+linewidth(0.9));
pair P1=(1.8,1.08), P2=(4.5,2.7), Q=(4.5,1.08);
draw(P1--Q--P2,dashed);
draw(Q+(-0.18,0)--Q+(-0.18,0.18)--Q+(0,0.18));
draw(P1--P2,red+linewidth(1.1));
dot(P1); dot(P2);
label("$(x_1,y_1)$",P1,NW); label("$(x_2,y_2)$",P2,NW);
label("$|x_2-x_1|$",(P1+Q)/2,S);
label("$|k|\,|x_2-x_1|$",(Q+P2)/2,E);
label("$y=kx$",(5.4,3.24),E,blue);
\end{asy}
