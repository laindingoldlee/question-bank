The points $(x_1,y_1)$ and $(x_2,y_2)$ lie on the line $y=kx$. Show that the distance between them is
\[|x_1-x_2|\sqrt{k^2+1}.\]
Show also that the same formula holds when the two points lie on any line $y=kx+c$.
