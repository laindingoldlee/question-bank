Sam and Sally each have a pocketful of U.S. coins (pennies, nickels, dimes, quarters). Sam has $49$ cents and Sally has $43$ cents. Neither has enough of any one kind of coin to exchange them for a coin of larger value; for example, neither has five pennies to exchange for a nickel. What is the least possible total number of coins they have together?
