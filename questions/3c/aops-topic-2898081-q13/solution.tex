The region is the sector $AOB$ together with triangle $BOC$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(2.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0),A=dir(90),B=dir(0),C=dir(-135);draw(circle(O,1),gray);fill(O--arc(O,1,90,0)--B--C--cycle,lightgray);draw(O--A);draw(O--C);draw(B--C);draw(arc(O,1,90,0));
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,E);label("$C$",C,SW);label("$O$",O,NW);
\end{asy}

The sector has area $\dfrac14\cdot\pi\cdot4=\pi$ and the triangle $\dfrac12\cdot2\cdot2\sin135^\circ=\sqrt2$. Total $\boxed{\pi+\sqrt2}$.
