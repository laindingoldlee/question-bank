Let $\theta=\dfrac{2\pi}{7}$ and $u=2\cos\theta$. Summing the seventh roots of unity gives $1+2\cos\theta+2\cos2\theta+2\cos3\theta=0$. Expressing the latter cosines in $u$ gives $u^3+u^2-2u-1=0$. The coefficient signs change once, so Descartes\textquotesingle s rule gives at most one positive root; $u>0$ is that root. Hence $10 \arccos (\dfrac{\alpha}{2}) = 10 \theta = \dfrac{20\pi}{7} \approx 8.976$, whose nearest integer is $\boxed9$.
