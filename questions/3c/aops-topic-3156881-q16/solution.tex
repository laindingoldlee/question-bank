Since $ 98=2\cdot7^2 $, $\phi(98)=42 $. Pair each coprime integer $ a $ with $ 98-a $; the pairs sum to 98, giving $\alpha=\dfrac{98\cdot42}{2}$. The sum of all positive integers below 98 is $\dfrac{98\cdot97}{2}$, so $\beta=\dfrac{98(97-42)}{2}$. Cancelling gives $\boxed{\dfrac{42}{55}}$.
