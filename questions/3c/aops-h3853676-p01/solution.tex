A cube of side $s$ has space diagonal $s\sqrt3$, so its circumsphere has radius $\dfrac{s\sqrt3}{2}$. For the largest cube the radius is $2\sqrt3$ and the volume is $\dfrac{4}{3}\pi(2\sqrt3)^3=32\pi\sqrt3$. Halving the side divides volume by eight. Thus the sum is $\dfrac{32\pi\sqrt3}{1-\dfrac{1}{8}}=\boxed{\dfrac{256\pi\sqrt3}{7}}$.
