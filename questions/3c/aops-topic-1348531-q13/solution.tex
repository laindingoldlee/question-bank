A perfect square divisible by $5$ is divisible by $25$, so the lists have $(5u)^2$ and $(5v)^2$ people with $25(u^2+v^2)=1850$, that is, $u^2+v^2=74$. Checking $v=1,\dots,8$: $74-v^2$ is $73,70,65,58,49,38,25,10$, and the only squares are $49$ and $25$. So $\{u,v\}=\{5,7\}$, and the lists have $625$ and $1225$ people. Naughty to nice is
\[
\dfrac{625}{1225}=\boxed{\dfrac{25}{49}}.
\]
