Since $1375=5^3\cdot11=25\cdot55$,
\[
\sqrt{\dfrac{1375}{9}}=\dfrac{\sqrt{25\cdot55}}{3}=\boxed{\dfrac{5\sqrt{55}}{3}}\approx12.3603308 .
\]
This is \textbf{not} a fraction of two integers, because $\sqrt{55}$ is irrational: if $\sqrt{55}=\dfrac {p}{q}$ in lowest terms, then $p^2=55q^2$, so $5\mid p^2$, hence $5\mid p$; writing $p=5p_1$ gives $5p_1^2=11q^2$, so $5\mid q$, contradicting lowest terms. Consequently $\dfrac{5\sqrt{55}}{3}$ is irrational as well (otherwise $\sqrt{55}=\dfrac{3}{5}\cdot\dfrac{5\sqrt{55}}{3}$ would be rational).

The calculator's $\dfrac{86683}{7013}=12.36033081\ldots$ is only a close rational \emph{approximation}: the calculator converts its $10$-digit decimal value to the simplest fraction that agrees with it to the displayed precision. Indeed $3\cdot86683=260049$ and $5\cdot7013=35065$, and $\left(\dfrac{260049}{35065}\right)^2\ne55$ since $260049^2=67625482401$ while $55\cdot35065^2=67625482375$.
