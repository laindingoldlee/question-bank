$BE$ bisects $\angle ABC$ in triangle $ABC$, so by the angle bisector theorem $\dfrac{AE}{EC}=\dfrac{AB}{BC}$, i.e. $\dfrac36=\dfrac4{BC}$ and $BC=8$.

$CF$ bisects $\angle ACD$ in triangle $ACD$, so $\dfrac{AF}{FD}=\dfrac{AC}{CD}=\dfrac9{10}$, giving $FD=\dfrac{10}9$ and $AD=\dfrac{19}9$.

The perimeter is $4+8+10+\dfrac{19}9=\boxed{\dfrac{217}{9}}$. (Such a convex quadrilateral exists: the triangles $ABC$ with sides $4,8,9$ and $ACD$ with sides $9,10,\dfrac{19}9$ can be placed on opposite sides of $AC$.)
