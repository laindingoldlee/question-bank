The given win probability simplifies to $\dfrac{a}{a+b}$. Conditional on the first opponent being $j=1,2,3$, compare using the weak hand first with using it in the final. Average the final-round win chance over the winner of the other pair. These two probabilities are respectively $(\dfrac{128}{315},\dfrac{44}{125})$, $(\dfrac{11}{35},\dfrac{14}{45})$, and $(\dfrac{64}{225},\dfrac{20}{63})$. The optimal choices are weak hand first against 1 or 2, and strong hand first against 3. Averaging the three maxima gives $\dfrac{109}{315}$, hence $\boxed{424}$. Each first opponent is equally likely.
