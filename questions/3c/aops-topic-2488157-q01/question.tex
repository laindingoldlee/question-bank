The Fibonacci numbers are defined by $F_0=0$, $F_1=1$ and $F_n=F_{n-1}+F_{n-2}$ for $n\ge2$. Prove that $F_{2k+1}$ is a sum of two squares of nonnegative integers for every nonnegative integer $k$.
