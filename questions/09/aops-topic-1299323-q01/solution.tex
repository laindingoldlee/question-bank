With exactly one blue, one red and no green among three marbles, the third must be yellow. Counting unordered selections, there are $6\cdot4\cdot4=96$ favourable sets out of $\dbinom{16}{3}=560$. The probability is $\dfrac{96}{560}=\boxed{\dfrac{6}{35}}$.
