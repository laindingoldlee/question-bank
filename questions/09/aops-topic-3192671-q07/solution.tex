The base $EF=2$ and the altitude from $A$ is $\sqrt{6^2-3^2}=3\sqrt3$. Thus the area is $\dfrac{1}{2}(2)(3\sqrt3)=\boxed{3\sqrt3}$.

usepackage("amsmath");

\end{asy}
