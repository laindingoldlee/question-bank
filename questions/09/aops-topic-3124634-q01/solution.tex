For fixed $x$, the positive $y^2$ term makes the expression unbounded above as $|y|\to\infty$, so the unrestricted game has no finite maximizing choice. Under the restriction, the convex quadratic in $y$ attains its maximum at an endpoint. The maximum is $2x^2+|x|-3$, minimized uniquely at $x=0$, where it is $\boxed{-3}$. Thus the repaired game has $\boxed{x=0}$.
