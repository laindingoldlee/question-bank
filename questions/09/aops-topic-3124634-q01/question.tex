First Goose chooses real $x$, then Duck chooses real $y$. Goose wants to minimize and Duck maximize $xy+2x^2+y^2-4$. Does Duck have a finite maximizing choice? If instead $-1\le y\le1$, find Goose's optimal $x$ and the resulting worst-case value.
