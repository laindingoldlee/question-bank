The $30$ gallons of mix contain $0.2\cdot30=6$ gallons of lemon juice. After adding $x$ gallons of pure juice, there are $6+x$ gallons of juice in $30+x$ gallons of blend, so
\[\dfrac{6+x}{30+x}=\dfrac{1}{4}\iff24+4x=30+x\iff x=\boxed{2}.\]
Check: $8$ gallons of juice in $32$ gallons is $25\%$.

(Another view: the non-juice part, $24$ gallons, does not change, and it must be $75\%$ of the blend, so the blend is $32$ gallons and $2$ gallons are added.)
