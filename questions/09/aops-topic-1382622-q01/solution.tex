Work backwards. If Auggie enters a store with $h$ dollars, he spends $\dfrac{h}{2}+1$ and leaves with $r=\dfrac{h}{2}-1$, so $h=2(r+1)$.

He leaves the fifth store with $r=0$. Going backwards through the stores:
\[
0\ \to\ 2(0+1)=2\ \to\ 2(2+1)=6\ \to\ 2(6+1)=14\ \to\ 2(14+1)=30\ \to\ 2(30+1)=62 .
\]
So he entered the first store with $\boxed{62}$ dollars. Check: $62\to30\to14\to6\to2\to0$, spending $32,16,8,4,2$.
