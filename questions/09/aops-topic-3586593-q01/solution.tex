The consecutive differences are $x^2$ and $(x-10)^2$. Since both $x$ and $x-10$ are positive, taking square roots gives $\dfrac{x}{x-10}=\dfrac{7}{5}$. Hence $5x=7x-70$, giving $\boxed{x=35}$.
