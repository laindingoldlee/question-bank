For $\overline{abcde}$, $11\mid(a+c+e)-(b+d)$. With total $15$ (odd), the difference is odd and in $[-15,15]$, so it is $\pm11$. That forces $(a+c+e,\ b+d)=(13,2)$ or $(2,13)$. To be smallest, take $a=1$. The case $a+c+e=2$ needs $c+e=1$ and $b+d=13$, with smallest option $b=4$, $d=9$, $c=0$, $e=1$: $14091$. The case $a+c+e=13$ with $a=1$ needs $c+e=12$ and $b+d=2$; the smallest is $b=0$, $c=3$, $d=2$, $e=9$: $10329$. The answer is $\boxed{10329}$.
