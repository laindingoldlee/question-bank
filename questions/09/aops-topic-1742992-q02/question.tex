A farmer builds a rectangular pig pen against a long barn, which serves as one side; the other three sides use $42$ feet of fencing. If the pen must have area $200$ square feet, what is the length of each of the two sides perpendicular to the barn? Give the smaller of the two possible answers, to the nearest tenth of a foot.
