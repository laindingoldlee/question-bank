The new labels 7 and 6 occur at old positions 4 and 5, because $\sigma(4)=7$ and $\sigma(5)=6$. The new label 15 occurs at old position 8, because $\sigma(8)=15$. Thus the time is 4:40. The hour hand is at $4\cdot30+40\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=140$ degrees clockwise from 12, and the minute hand at 240 degrees. The smaller angle is $\boxed{100^\circ}$.

\end{asy}
