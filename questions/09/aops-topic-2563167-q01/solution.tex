Two different lines meet in at most one point, so there are at most $\dbinom92=36$ intersection points. This is attained when no two lines are parallel and no three pass through a common point; for example, take the lines $y=kx+k^2$ for $k=1,\dots,9$: lines $k\ne l$ meet at $\left(-(k+l),\,-kl\right)$, and the pair $(k+l,kl)$ determines $\{k,l\}$, so all $36$ points are different. The answer is $\boxed{36}$.
