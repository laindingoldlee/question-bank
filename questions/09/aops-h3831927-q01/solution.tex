Write the tangent line as $y=mx+30$. Its distance from the semicircle centre $(15,0)$ is $15$, so $\dfrac{|15m+30|}{\sqrt{m^2+1}}=15$. Squaring and simplifying gives $(m+2)^2=m^2+1$, hence $m=-\dfrac{3}{4}$. The line is $3x+4y=120$, at distance $24$ from $O$. A radius perpendicular to a chord bisects it, so $BP=2\sqrt{30^2-24^2}=\boxed{36}$. The second intersection has positive coordinates, so the chord lies in the quarter disk.
