Factor $1434=2\cdot3\cdot239$, where 239 is prime (none of 2,3,5,7,11,13 divides it). The totient formula gives $\phi(1434)=1434\left(1-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)\left(1-\dfrac{1}{3}\right)\left(1-\dfrac{1}{239}\right)=1\cdot2\cdot238=476=14\cdot34$.
