Since $(a-1)(a+1)=a^2-1$, the line is
\[y=a^2x-2a^2+2=a^2(x-2)+2.\]
A point $(x,y)$ lies on the line for every real $a$ exactly when $a^2(x-2)=y-2$ for all $a$. Taking $a=0$ gives $y-2=0$, and then taking $a=1$ gives $x-2=0$. Conversely, the point $(2,2)$ satisfies $2=a^2\cdot0+2$ for every $a$.

Hence every line of the family passes through the single common point
\[\boxed{(2,2)}.\]
