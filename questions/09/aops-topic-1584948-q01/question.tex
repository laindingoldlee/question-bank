A chord $AB$ of a circle with centre $O$ and radius $10$ subtends a central angle $\angle AOB=120^\circ$. Find the length of the chord. More generally, show that a chord subtending a central angle $\theta$ in a circle of radius $r$ has length $2r\sin\dfrac{\theta}{2}$.
