Let $O$ be the circumcentre of $ABC$. It lies on the perpendicular bisectors of the sides, so $\angle ODB=\angle OEB=90^\circ$; hence $D$ and $E$ lie on the circle with diameter $OB$, which is therefore the circumcircle of $BDE$. Likewise $\angle OEC=\angle OFC=90^\circ$, so the circumcircle of $CEF$ is the circle with diameter $OC$. Both circles pass through $O$ and $E$, and $O\ne E$ (the triangle has no right angle), so $X=O$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(5,12),B=(0,0),C=(14,0);pair D=(A+B)/2,Ee=(B+C)/2,F=(A+C)/2,O=(7,33/8);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
draw(circle((B+O)/2,abs(O-B)/2),gray);draw(circle((C+O)/2,abs(O-C)/2),gray);
draw(O--D,dashed);draw(O--Ee,dashed);draw(O--F,dashed);
dot(D);dot(Ee);dot(F);dot(O);
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,NW);label("$E$",Ee,S);label("$F$",F,NE);label("$X=O$",O,2*dir(-20));
\end{asy}

Thus $XA+XB+XC=3R$. The area of the $13$-$14$-$15$ triangle is $84$ (the altitude to $BC$ is $12$), so
\[R=\dfrac{abc}{4K}=\dfrac{13\cdot14\cdot15}{4\cdot84}=\dfrac{65}{8},\qquad XA+XB+XC=\boxed{\dfrac{195}{8}}.\]
