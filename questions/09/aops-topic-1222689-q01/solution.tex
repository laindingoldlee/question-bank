For each year $y\in\{17,18,19,20\}$ count dates with $md=y$ or $m+d=y$ (both cannot hold for positive $m,d$).
\begin{itemize}
\item Sums: $m+d=y$ with $1\le m\le12$ gives $d=y-m$ between $5$ and $19$, always a valid day: $12$ dates each year.
\item Products: $17$: $1\cdot17$ ($1$ date); $18$: $1\cdot18$, $2\cdot9$, $3\cdot6$, $6\cdot3$, $9\cdot2$ ($5$); $19$: $1\cdot19$ ($1$); $20$: $1\cdot20$, $2\cdot10$, $4\cdot5$, $5\cdot4$, $10\cdot2$ ($5$).
\end{itemize}
Total $4\cdot12+1+5+1+5=\boxed{60}$.
