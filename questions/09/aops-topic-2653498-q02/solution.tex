Squaring, $a+b+2\sqrt{ab}=4+\sqrt7$, so $2\sqrt{ab}=r+\sqrt7$ with $r=4-a-b$ rational. Squaring again,
\[4ab=r^2+7+2r\sqrt7.\]
If $r\ne0$, then $\sqrt7=\dfrac{4ab-r^2-7}{2r}$ would be rational, which is false. So $r=0$: $a+b=4$ and $4ab=7$. Then $a$ and $b$ are the roots of $t^2-4t+\dfrac74=0$, namely $t=\dfrac{4\pm3}{2}$, i.e. $\dfrac72$ and $\dfrac12$.

Conversely, $\left(\sqrt{\dfrac72}+\sqrt{\dfrac12}\right)^2=4+2\sqrt{\dfrac74}=4+\sqrt7$, and both sides of the original equation are positive. So the solutions are
\[\boxed{(a,b)=\left(\dfrac72,\dfrac12\right)\text{ or }\left(\dfrac12,\dfrac72\right)}.\]
