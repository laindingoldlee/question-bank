Factor $N=2^6\cdot3\cdot11^2\cdot23^2$. Hence count $2^i3^j11^k23^\ell<6072$, with $0\le i\le6$, $0\le j\le1$, $0\le k,\ell\le2$. Enumerating these 126 exponent choices gives $\boxed{70}$.
