Since $a_n=10^n-1$, the sum is $(10+10^2+\cdots+10^{1000})-1000$. The parenthesized number has one in positions $10^1$ through $10^{1000}$ and zero in its units position. Subtracting 1000 simply changes its thousands digit from one to zero, without borrowing. Thus 999 ones remain, and the digit sum is $\boxed{999}$.
