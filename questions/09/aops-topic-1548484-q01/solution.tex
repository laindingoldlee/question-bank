Work from the inside out:
\[
\begin{aligned}
x-2(x-2)&=x-2x+4=-x+4,\\
x-2(-x+4)&=x+2x-8=3x-8,\\
x-2(3x-8)&=x-6x+16=-5x+16,\\
-2(-5x+16)&=10x-32 .
\end{aligned}
\]
So $a=10$, $b=-32$ and $a+b=\boxed{-22}$. (Check: $a+b$ is the value of the expression at $x=1$. Working outwards, the brackets are $1-2=-1$, then $1-2(-1)=3$, then $1-2\cdot3=-5$, then $1-2(-5)=11$, and finally $-2\cdot11=-22$.)
