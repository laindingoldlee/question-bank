$ABCD$ is a trapezoid with $AB\parallel DC$, $AB=a$ and $DC=b$, and $M$ and $N$ are the midpoints of the legs $AD$ and $BC$. Prove that
\[\overrightarrow{MN}=\dfrac{a+b}{2a}\,\overrightarrow{AB}=\dfrac{a+b}{2b}\,\overrightarrow{DC}.\]
