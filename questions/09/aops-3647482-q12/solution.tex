The relative angular speed of the minute hand with respect to the hour hand is constant. Over 12 hours, the relative angle completes 11 full turns, so a uniformly random time makes the directed relative angle uniform on $[0,360^\circ)$. The smaller angle is obtuse exactly when the directed angle lies between $90^\circ$ and $270^\circ$, excluding the single straight-angle instant at $180^\circ$, which has probability zero. The interval has half the full angular measure. Thus the probability is $\boxed{50\%}$.
