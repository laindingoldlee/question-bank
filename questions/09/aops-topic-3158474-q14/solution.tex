For one-digit numbers the sum is $1+\cdots+9=45$. For two-digit numbers, independently summing tens and units contributions gives $(1+\cdots+9)(0+\cdots+9)=45^2$. Three-digit numbers similarly contribute $45^3$, and $f(1000)=0$. Hence the total is $45+2025+91125=\boxed{93195}$.
