Let the pack counts be $a,b,c\ge0$. Then $3a+4b+9c=14$, so $c\le1$ and $b\equiv2\pmod3$. Since $b\le3$, $b=2$. Thus $a+3c=2$, forcing $a=2,c=0$. The total is $2(4)+2(6)=\boxed{20}$.
