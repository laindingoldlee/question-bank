Since $3^2+4^2=5^2$, the angle at $A$ is a right angle. The point $G$, where two angle bisectors meet, is the incentre, and the inradius of a right triangle is $r=\dfrac{3+4-5}{2}=1$; so $G$ is at distance $1$ from both legs $AB$ and $AC$.

By the angle bisector theorem,
\[\dfrac{AE}{EC}=\dfrac{AB}{BC}=\dfrac{3}{5}\ \Longrightarrow\ AE=\dfrac{3}{8}\cdot4=\dfrac{3}{2},\qquad\dfrac{AF}{FB}=\dfrac{AC}{BC}=\dfrac{4}{5}\ \Longrightarrow\ AF=\dfrac{4}{9}\cdot3=\dfrac{4}{3} .\]

Split the quadrilateral along $AG$. Triangle $AFG$ has base $AF=\dfrac{4}{3}$ on $AB$ and height $1$; triangle $AEG$ has base $AE=\dfrac{3}{2}$ on $AC$ and height $1$. Hence
\[[AEGF]=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{4}{3}\cdot1+\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{3}{2}\cdot1=\dfrac{2}{3}+\dfrac{3}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{17}{12}}.\]
