Proceed by induction. For $n=1$, give the whole cake to the sole person. For $n>1$, ask each person to mark a leftmost initial interval of value $\dfrac{1}{n}$. Give the interval ending at the leftmost of these marks to its marker, breaking ties arbitrarily. Every other person values that interval at most $\dfrac{1}{n}$, hence values the remaining interval at least $\dfrac{n-1}{n}$. Apply the inductive procedure to the remaining $n-1$ people and their normalized valuations of the remainder. Each receives at least $\dfrac{1}{n-1}$ of their remainder value, which is at least $\dfrac{1}{n}$ of the original cake. The chosen person receives exactly $\dfrac{1}{n}$, completing the proof.
