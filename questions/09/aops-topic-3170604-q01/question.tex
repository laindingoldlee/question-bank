Model a cake as an interval. Each of $n$ people assigns it a nonatomic, additive value of $1$, and every intermediate value can be attained by moving a cut continuously. Give a procedure dividing the cake into $n$ pieces so each person values their own piece at least $\dfrac{1}{n}$.
