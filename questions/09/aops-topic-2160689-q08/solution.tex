The diagonals of a rhombus are perpendicular and bisect each other, so they cut the rhombus into four right triangles with legs $3$ and $5$. The area is
\[4\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot3\cdot5=\dfrac{6\cdot10}{2}=\boxed{30}.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(-5,0), B=(0,-3), C=(5,0), D=(0,3);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(A--C,dashed); draw(B--D,dashed);
draw((0,0)--(0.5,0)--(0.5,0.5)--(0,0.5));
label("$10$",(-2.5,0),S); label("$6$",(0,-1.5),E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
