Factor:
\[
n^3-1=(n-1)\left(n^2+n+1\right).
\]
For $n\ge2$ both factors are positive integers and $n^2+n+1\ge7>1$. If $n^3-1$ is prime, the smaller factor must be $1$, so $n-1=1$, $n=2$, and $n^3-1=7$, which is prime. For $n=1$, $n^3-1=0$ is not prime. So $7$ is the only such prime. $\blacksquare$
