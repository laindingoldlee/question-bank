The monic degree-five polynomial $x^5-P(x)$ has roots $1,2,3,4,5$, so it equals $\prod_{j=1}^5(x-j)$. Substituting $x=6$ gives $P(6)=6^5-5!=7776-120=\boxed{7656}$.
