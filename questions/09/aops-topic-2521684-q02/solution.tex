We need $2(a+b)=ab$, i.e. $ab-2a-2b+4=4$, i.e. $(a-2)(b-2)=4$. Since $a,b\ge1$, $a-2,b-2\ge-1$, so both factors are positive (a negative factor would be $-1$, forcing the other to be $-4<-1$). So $\{a-2,b-2\}$ is $\{1,4\}$ or $\{2,2\}$; the second gives $a=b$. Hence $\{a,b\}=\{3,6\}$, and the difference is $\boxed{3}$.
