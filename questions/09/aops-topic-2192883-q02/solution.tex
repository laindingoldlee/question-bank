Let $C=(34,68,16)$. Then
\[P(t)-C=(966-100t,\ 432-50t,\ 884-90t),\]
so the square of the distance is
\[D(t)^2=(966-100t)^2+(432-50t)^2+(884-90t)^2=20600t^2-395520t+\left(966^2+432^2+884^2\right).\]
Here $100^2+50^2+90^2=20600$ and $2(966\cdot100+432\cdot50+884\cdot90)=2\cdot197760=395520$. The distance is smallest when $D(t)^2$ is, and a quadratic $\alpha t^2-\beta t+\gamma$ with $\alpha>0$ is smallest at its vertex $t=\dfrac{\beta}{2\alpha}$ (complete the square: $\alpha\left(t-\dfrac{\beta}{2\alpha}\right)^2+\gamma-\dfrac{\beta^2}{4\alpha}$). So
\[t=\dfrac{395520}{2\cdot20600}=\dfrac{197760}{20600}=\boxed{9.6}.\]
\emph{Check.} $P(9.6)=(40,20,36)$ and $P(9.6)-C=(6,-48,20)$, which is perpendicular to the direction of flight: $6\cdot(-100)+(-48)(-50)+20\cdot(-90)=-600+2400-1800=0$. The closest point is the foot of the perpendicular from $C$ to the flight path (sketch, not to scale); the minimum distance is $\sqrt{6^2+48^2+20^2}=\sqrt{2740}\approx52.3$.
