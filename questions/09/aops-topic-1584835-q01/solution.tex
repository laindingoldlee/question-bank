Let the price be $x$ cents. The tip $\dfrac{24}{100}x=\dfrac{6x}{25}$ cents is a whole number, and $\gcd(6,25)=1$, so $25\mid x$: the price is a multiple of $25$ cents.

The total is $1.24x$ cents, with $250<1.24x<300$, so
\[
201.6\ldots<x<241.9\ldots .
\]
The only multiple of $25$ in this range is $x=225$. So the slice cost $\boxed{\$2.25}$ (tip $54$ cents, total $\$2.79$).
