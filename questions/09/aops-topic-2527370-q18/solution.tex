Completing the squares,
\[\left(x^2-6x+9\right)+\left(y^2+4y+4\right)=21-2+9+4\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad(x-3)^2+(y+2)^2=32.\]
So the centre is $(3,-2)$, the radius is $\sqrt{32}$ and the area is $32\pi$. Hence $h+k+z=3-2+32=\boxed{33}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");

defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((-3.5,0)--(10,0),Arrow(TeXHead));draw((0,-8.5)--(0,4.5),Arrow(TeXHead));
pair C=(3,-2); real r=sqrt(32);
draw(circle(C,r),linewidth(0.8));dot(C);
draw(C--C+r*dir(-40),dashed);
label("$(3,-2)$",C,W,fontsize(9pt));label("$\sqrt{32}$",C+0.5*r*dir(-40),NE,fontsize(9pt));
label("$x$",(10,0),E);label("$y$",(0,4.5),N);
\end{asy}
