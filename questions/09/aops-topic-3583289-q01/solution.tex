Write $R(x)=ax^2+bx+c$. Substitution at the roots $0,1,-1$ of the divisor gives $c=0$, $a+b=5050$, and $a-b=100-99+\cdots+2-1=50$. Hence $a=2550$, $b=2500$, and $R(-2)=4(2550)-2(2500)=\boxed{5200}$.
