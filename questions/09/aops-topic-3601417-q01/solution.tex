Choose the front wall in $4$ ways. If the back has the same finish, the left and right each have $3$ choices, giving $4\cdot9=36$. If the back differs, there are $3$ choices for it and $2$ choices for each side, giving $4\cdot3\cdot4=48$. The total is $\boxed{84}$.
