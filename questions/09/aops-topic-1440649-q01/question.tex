Let $a$ and $b$ be positive integers such that
\[
a\mid b^2,\quad b^2\mid a^3,\quad a^3\mid b^4,\quad b^4\mid a^5,\quad\dots
\]
that is, $a^{2k-1}\mid b^{2k}$ and $b^{2k}\mid a^{2k+1}$ for every $k\ge1$. Prove that $a=b$.
