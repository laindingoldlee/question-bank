A barrel contains $2017$ white balls and $2016$ black balls. Repeatedly, two balls are taken out without looking. If they have the same colour, both are left out and one black ball (from an outside supply) is put into the barrel. If they have different colours, the white ball is put back into the barrel and the black ball is left out. This continues until only one ball is left in the barrel. What colour is the last ball?
