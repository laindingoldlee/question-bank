The quadratic $ax^2+bx+c$ ($a\ne0$) has a root in common with $x^2-x-1$. Must $ax^2+bx+c$ be a constant multiple of $x^2-x-1$?
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that the answer is yes when $a$, $b$, $c$ are rational.
\item[(b)] Show by an example that the answer is no when $a$, $b$, $c$ may be any real numbers.
\end{enumerate}
