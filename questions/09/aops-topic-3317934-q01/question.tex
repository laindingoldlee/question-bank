A four-layer cube pyramid consists of unit cubes with lower-left-bottom coordinates $(i,j,k)$ where $i,j,k$ are nonnegative integers and $i+j+k\le3$. All exposed faces, including the underside, are painted. (a) How many cubes are in the bottom layer? (b) How many cubes have exactly three painted faces? (c) How many have exactly one?
