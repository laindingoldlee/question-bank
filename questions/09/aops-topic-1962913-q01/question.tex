In a $16\times16$ board of unit squares, $10$ unit squares are coloured red. Then, step by step, every unit square that has at least two red neighbours (squares sharing a side with it) is also coloured red. Is it possible that, after finitely many steps, the whole board becomes red? Prove your answer.
