Tiles are numbered 0 through $N+1$. A ball visits multiples of $k$ starting from 0 on the first attempt, and positions $N+1-jk$ on the second, stopping when it leaves the interval. Given $N\ge0$, $0\le x\le N+1$, and $k\ge1$, decide whether either attempt visits tile $x$, counting starting tiles as visited.
