Consider the following proposed system with positive integers $A,B,C$. (i) $AC=495$. (ii) An election has $3A-1$ ballots, and the winner has exactly one more vote than the loser; $B$ is the number of pairs of the winner’s ballots. (iii) $B=7920(C-1)$, corresponding to three laps around a square of side $C-1$ furlongs, with 660 feet per furlong. Can all three conditions hold? Prove your answer.
