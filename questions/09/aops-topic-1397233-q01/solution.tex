There are $\binom{100}{2}=4950$ equally likely pairs, and $\binom{50}{2}=1225$ pairs of each genre. The probability is
\[
\dfrac{2\cdot1225}{4950}=\boxed{\dfrac{49}{99}} .
\]
(Equivalently: whatever the first book is, $49$ of the remaining $99$ books match it.)
