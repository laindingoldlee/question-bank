Encode birches by one and lindens by zero, numbering the exceptional birch as position zero. At every other position the truth condition is $x_i=x_{i-1}+x_{i+1}\pmod2$. Therefore, starting with $x_0=1,x_1=b$, the sequence along the other positions repeats the block $(1,b,1+b)$ modulo two. Position 160 must equal position zero; since $160\equiv1\pmod3$, this forces $b=1$. Thus the pattern is $(1,1,0)$ repeated 53 times, followed by one extra one. At the exceptional position both neighbours are birches, so its statement is false as required. Every other statement has the required truth value. There are $2\cdot53+1=\boxed{107}$ birches, and the recurrence shows no larger count is possible.
