Without Jim, Tom and Huck would need $4+5=9$ hours, so together they paint $\dfrac{1}{9}$ of the fence per hour. In the $6$ hours they actually worked they painted $\dfrac{6}{9}=\dfrac{2}{3}$, so Jim painted $\dfrac{1}{3}$ in $2$ hours. Alone he would take $\boxed{6\text{ hours}}$.
