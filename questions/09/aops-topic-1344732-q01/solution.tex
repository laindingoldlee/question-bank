Let the first term be $a$ and the common difference $d$, so the $n$-th term is $a+(n-1)d$. Then
\[
a+4d=10,\qquad a+31d=-83 .
\]
Subtracting, $27d=-93$, so $d=-\dfrac{93}{27}=-\dfrac{31}{9}$. (The poster's $\dfrac{31}{9}$ has the wrong sign: the terms decrease from $10$ to $-83$.) Then
\[
a=10-4d=10+\dfrac{124}{9}=\dfrac{214}{9}.
\]
So the first term is $\boxed{\dfrac{214}{9}}$ and the common difference is $\boxed{-\dfrac{31}{9}}$. (Check: $\dfrac{214}{9}+31\cdot\left(-\dfrac{31}{9}\right)=\dfrac{214-961}{9}=-83$.)
