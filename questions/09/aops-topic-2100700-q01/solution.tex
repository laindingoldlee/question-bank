Say there are $100N$ trees, so $N$ of them are not eucalyptus. The plan does not touch these $N$ trees, and afterwards they make up $100\%-98\%=2\%$ of the trees. So $N$ is $2\%$ of the new total, which is therefore $\dfrac{N}{0.02}=50N$.

The plan removes $100N-50N=50N$ trees, which is $\boxed{50\%}$ of the existing trees.

(So, far from cutting down only a few trees, the plan removes half of them: the share of other trees doubles from $1\%$ to $2\%$ only if the total is halved.)
