Octo the Octopus has a machine that squares the number of fish he has to eat. Suppose that Octo dumps a positive integer number of fish, $x$, into his machine at lunch. Then, as the machine is squaring the number of fish, Octo catches four more fish. When the machine finishes its work, Octo realizes that he can split all the fish equally into his eight tentacles. Later, at dinner, when Octo is simultaneously reading and eating, he tosses the same number of fish he did at lunch,$x$, into the machine. When the machine has done its work, Octo uses his six tentacles (two of them are holding a book) to grab all the fish. Once again, he can evenly distribute the fish into all six tentacles. If the total number of fish he ate that day for lunch and dinner was less than $400$, what is the probability that the number of fish he ate was greater than $100$?

$\textbf{(A)}~0\qquad\textbf{(B)}~\dfrac{1}{2}\qquad\textbf{(C)}~\dfrac{1}{3}\qquad\textbf{(D)}~\dfrac{1}{4}\qquad\textbf{(E)}~1\qquad$
