$\boxed{48}$ works, with divisors 12,16,24,48. For $n<40$, any two-digit divisor corresponds to a distinct complementary divisor $\dfrac{n}{d}<4$, allowing at most three. For $40\le n<48$, at least four two-digit divisors would require all complementary divisors 1,2,3,4, because $\dfrac{n}{d}<4.8$. Then 12 divides $n$, but no integer 40 through 47 is divisible by 12. Thus nothing smaller works.
