Let $M$ and $P$ be the ages, so $M+P^2=7308$ and $P+M^2=6974$. Subtracting,
\[
P^2-M^2-(P-M)=334\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad (P-M)(P+M-1)=334=2\cdot167 ,
\]
with $167$ prime. The second factor is positive, so $P-M$ is a positive divisor of $334$, and $P-M<P+M-1$. So $(P-M,\,P+M-1)$ is $(1,334)$ or $(2,167)$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $(1,334)$: $P+M=335$, $P=168$, $M=167$; but then $M+P^2>7308$. Rejected.
\item $(2,167)$: $P+M=168$, $P=85$, $M=83$; check: $83+85^2=83+7225=7308$ and $85+83^2=85+6889=6974$.
\end{itemize}
So the sum of their ages is $\boxed{168}$.
