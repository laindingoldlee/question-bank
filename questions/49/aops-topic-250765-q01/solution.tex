A counterexample must have a vowel and an odd number. Among the visible cards, only $3$ is an odd number; its reverse might be a vowel. The even-number cards cannot violate the claim, and the consonant cards cannot violate it regardless of their reverse numbers. Thus the card to turn is $\boxed3$.
