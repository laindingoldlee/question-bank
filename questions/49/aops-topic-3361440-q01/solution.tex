Each path consists of three $x$-steps, five $y$-steps, and two $z$-steps. Thus the number is $\dfrac{10!}{3!5!2!}=\boxed{2520}$. Alternatively arrange the $x,y$ steps in $\binom83=56$ ways, then place the two $z$-steps among nine gaps allowing both in the same gap. Stars and bars gives $\binom{10}{2}=45$, not $\binom92$, so the total is $56\cdot45=2520$.
