Let $A_1A_2A_3A_4A_5A_6$ be a hexagon inscribed in a circle of radius $r$, and let $M_i$ be the midpoint of $\overline{A_iA_{i+1}}$ (with $A_7=A_1$). Suppose that hexagon $M_1M_2M_3M_4M_5M_6$ can also be inscribed in a circle. If $A_1A_2=A_3A_4=5$ and $A_5A_6=23$, then $r^2=\dfrac mn$ with $m,n$ relatively prime positive integers. Find $m+n-1$.
