The exponent $\binom cd$ is at least $2$. From $36=2^2 3^2$, its only representation as an integer base raised to an integer exponent at least $2$ is $6^2$. Thus $\binom cd=2$, forcing $(c,d)=(2,1)$. The solutions to $\binom ab=6$ are $(a,b)=(6,1),(6,5),(4,2)$. Indeed for $2\le b\le a-2$, the binomial coefficient is at least $\binom a2$, so $a\le4$; the endpoint cases give $a=6$. The three sums are $10,14,9$, whose sum is $\boxed{33}$.
