Ignore the decimal points first: $30.450=\dfrac{30450}{1000}$ and $6.5=\dfrac{65}{10}$, so
\[
30.450\times6.5=\dfrac{30450\times65}{1000\times10}=\dfrac{1979250}{10000}=197.925 .
\]
Here $30450\times65=30450\times60+30450\times5=1827000+152250=1979250$.

In general, multiply the numbers as whole numbers and then place the decimal point so that the answer has as many digits after the point as the two factors together: $30.450$ has $3$ and $6.5$ has $1$, so the product has $4$, giving $197.9250=\boxed{197.925}$. (A quick estimate, $30\times6.5=195$, confirms the size.)
