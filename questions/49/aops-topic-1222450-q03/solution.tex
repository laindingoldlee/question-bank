Let the smoking rate be $2p$ among men with headaches and $p$ among those without. By Bayes,
\[P(H\mid S)=\dfrac{0.25\cdot2p}{0.25\cdot2p+0.75\cdot p}=\dfrac{0.5}{1.25}=\dfrac{2}{5}:\quad\boxed{\text{(c)}}.\]
