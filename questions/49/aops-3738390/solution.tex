In one diagonal direction, lengths are $1,2,\ldots,7,8,7,\ldots,2,1$. A diagonal containing $j$ squares contributes $\binom j3$ triples. Thus one direction contributes $2\sum_{j=3}^7\binom j3+\binom83=2\binom84+56=196$. Here the sum identity follows by counting four-element subsets according to their largest element. The other diagonal direction contributes equally, and no triple of distinct points lies on both a slope-1 and a slope-minus-1 line. The answer is $\boxed{392}$.
