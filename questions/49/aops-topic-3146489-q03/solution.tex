Write their ages as $a^2>b^2$ with $1\le b<a\le9$. Then $(a-b)(a+b)=33$. The positive factor pairs are $(1,33)$ and $(3,11)$. The first gives $a=17$, too large; the second gives $a=7,b=4$. Thus the ages are $49$ and $16$, with sum $\boxed{65}$.
