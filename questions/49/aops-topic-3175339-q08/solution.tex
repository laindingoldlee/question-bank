Cauchy--Schwarz gives $(2a+3b+6c)^2\le(4+9+36)(a^2+b^2+c^2)=49\cdot63$. The stated value attains equality, so $(a,b,c)$ is proportional to $(2,3,6)$. Therefore $\dfrac{a}{c}=\dfrac{1}{3}$, $\dfrac{a}{b}=\dfrac{2}{3}$, and the answer is $\boxed{(\dfrac{1}{3})^{\dfrac{2}{3}}=\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{1}{9}}}$.
