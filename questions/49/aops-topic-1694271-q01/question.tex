Six semicircles, each of radius $r$, are constructed inside a regular hexagon of side length $s$, one on each side (each semicircle's diameter lies along a side, centred at the side's midpoint), so that each semicircle is tangent to the two adjacent ones. What is the ratio of $r$ to $s$? Express your answer in simplest radical form.
