Adding and subtracting the constraints gives $x_1+x_3=\dfrac{(a+b)}{2}$ and $x_2+x_4=\dfrac{(a-b)}{2}$. For a fixed sum, two squares are minimized when the two numbers are equal. Thus
\[
\sum x_i^2\ge\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{a+b}{2}\right)^2+
\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{a-b}{2}\right)^2
=\boxed{\dfrac{a^2+b^2}{4}}.
\]
Equality occurs at $x_1=x_3=\dfrac{(a+b)}{4}$ and $x_2=x_4=\dfrac{(a-b)}{4}$, so the bound is attained.
