One way to pack a $100$ by $100$ square with $10{,}000$ circles, each of diameter $1$, is to put them in $100$ rows with $100$ circles in each row. Suppose instead that the circles are packed in rows parallel to one side of the square, with each circle tangent to its neighbours in its row, each row shifted half a diameter relative to the rows next to it, and neighbouring rows touching, so that the centres of any three mutually tangent circles form an equilateral triangle. What is the largest number of additional circles that can be packed into the square in this way?
