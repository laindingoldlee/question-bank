Let the total number of liars be $L$, and put $K=2023-L$. For a knight in position $i$, if $u$ liars precede them, then $K-(i-1-u)-1=K-i+u$ knights follow. Truth requires $u>K-i+u$, or $i>K$. For a liar at position $i$, the number of knights following is $K-(i-1-u)=K-i+1+u$; falsity requires $u\le K-i+1+u$, or $i\le K+1$. Hence positions $1$ through $K$ are liars, positions $K+2$ onward are knights, and position $K+1$ may be either. Thus $L=K$ or $K+1$. Since $L+K=2023$ is odd, $L=K+1$, giving $\boxed{L=1012}$. The arrangement of $1012$ liars followed by $1011$ knights satisfies all statements.
