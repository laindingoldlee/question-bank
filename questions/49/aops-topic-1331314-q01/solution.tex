Let $p=a+b+c$, $q=ab+bc+ca$, $r=abc$. Then $(a+b)(b+c)(c+a)=pq-r$. By AM-GM, $p\cdot q\ge3\sqrt[3]{abc}\cdot3\sqrt[3]{(abc)^2}=9r$, so $r\le\dfrac{pq}{9}$ and
\[
1=pq-r\ge pq-\dfrac{pq}{9}=\dfrac{8}{9}\,pq .
\]
Also $p^2=a^2+b^2+c^2+2q\ge3q$, so $p\ge\sqrt{3q}$. Hence $\dfrac{9}{8}\ge pq\ge\sqrt3\,q^{\dfrac{3}{2}}$, and squaring, $q^3\le\dfrac{81}{64\cdot3}=\dfrac{27}{64}$, so $q\le\dfrac{3}{4}$, i.e. $4(ab+bc+ca)\le3$. Equality holds at $a=b=c=\dfrac{1}{2}$. $\blacksquare$
