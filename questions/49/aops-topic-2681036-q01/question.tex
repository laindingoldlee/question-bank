The price $p$ of a ball was raised by $10\%$, and then, because nobody bought it, the new price was lowered by $10\%$. Is the final price $p'$ equal to $p$? Someone answered: ``Yes, because $p'=p+10\%-10\%=p$.'' Find the error and compute $p'$.
