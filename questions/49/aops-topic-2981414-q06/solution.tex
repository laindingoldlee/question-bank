\begin{enumerate}
\item The radius is the distance from the centre to the line: $12-8=4$. So $\boxed{(x-9)^2+(y-12)^2=16}$.
\item The centre is the midpoint $(9,13)$; the radius squared is $(9-7)^2+(13-14)^2=5$. So $\boxed{(x-9)^2+(y-13)^2=5}$.
\end{enumerate}
