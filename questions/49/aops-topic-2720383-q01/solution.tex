Let $P=1\cdot3\cdot5\cdots2019$. It is odd and divisible by $25$, so $P\bmod100$ is $25$ or $75$. Mod $4$, each factor is $\pm1$; the factors $\equiv3\pmod4$ are $3,7,\dots,2019$, of which there are $\dfrac{2019-3}{4}+1=505$, an odd number. So $P\equiv(-1)^{505}\equiv3\pmod4$. Of $25$ and $75$, only $75\equiv3\pmod4$. The last two digits are $75$, with sum $\boxed{12}$.
