Since $1000=2^3\cdot5^3$, a positive integer is relatively prime to $1000$ exactly when it is divisible by neither $2$ nor $5$. Among $1,2,\dots,2000$, by inclusion-exclusion,
\[\dfrac{2000}{2}+\dfrac{2000}{5}-\dfrac{2000}{10}=1000+400-200=1200\]
are divisible by $2$ or $5$, so $2000-1200=800$ are relatively prime to $1000$. The number $2000$ itself is not among them, so there are $\boxed{800}$ positive integers less than $2000$ relatively prime to $1000$.

(Equivalently, whether $k$ is relatively prime to $1000$ depends only on $k$ modulo $1000$, so each of the two blocks $1$--$1000$ and $1001$--$2000$ contains $\varphi(1000)=400$ such numbers.)
