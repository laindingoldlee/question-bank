The special side $a$ satisfies $a=(42-a)-12$, so $a=15$. All sides lie in an interval of width 2, and their sum is 42. If 15 were not the largest, the other two would be at least 13 and one would exceed 15, making the sum exceed 43; more directly, the other two sum 27 and each is at least 13, so they are 13 and 14 because their minimum must be 13 to differ by 2 from 15. Thus the sides are 13,14,15. Heron gives $\sqrt{21\cdot8\cdot7\cdot6}=\boxed{84}$.
