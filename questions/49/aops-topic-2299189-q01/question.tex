Find all pairs of integers $(x,y)$ with $x^2-2y^2=1$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that if $(x,y)$ is a solution, then so is $(3x+4y,\ 2x+3y)$, and deduce that there are infinitely many solutions in positive integers.
\item[(b)] Prove that every solution in positive integers is obtained from $(1,0)$ by applying the map in (a) repeatedly, and describe all integer solutions.
\end{enumerate}
