Exactly one of the three mirrored pairs must be unequal. If the outer pair is unequal, its first digit has $9$ choices and its last digit $9$ different choices; the two equal inner pairs give $10^2$ choices, for $8100$. If a specified inner pair is unequal, the outer matching pair has $9$ choices, the other matching pair $10$, and the unequal ordered pair $10\cdot9$, again $8100$. Summing the three cases gives $\boxed{24300}$. A mismatched outer pair can always be repaired by changing the last digit to the nonzero first digit.
