The possible square sums are 4, 9, 16. Sums 4 and 16 have one outcome each. For sum 9, subtract 1 from each roll to count $y_1+\cdots+y_4=5$ with $0\le y_i\le3$. Stars and bars and exclusion of a variable at least 4 give $\binom83-4\binom43=56-16=40$; two variables cannot both exceed 3. Thus the probability is $\dfrac{1+40+1}{4^4}=\boxed{\dfrac{21}{128}}$.
