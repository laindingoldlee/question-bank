A ladybug is in the center of a cube with side length $4$, such that it is distance $2$ away from every face of the cube. The ladybug wants to touch four faces of the cube and return to its original position (the ladybug "touches" a face of the cube if it is at any interior or boundary point of the face). If $M$ is the minimum distance the ladybug needs to travel, find $M^2$.
