Since $BH\perp AC$ and $CH\perp AB$, angles $HBC=90^\circ-C$ and $HCB=90^\circ-B$. Therefore $BHC=180^\circ-((90^\circ-C)+(90^\circ-B))=B+C=\boxed{100^\circ}$.
