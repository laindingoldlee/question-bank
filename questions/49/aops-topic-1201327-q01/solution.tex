$\dfrac {a}{b}=\dfrac {b}{a}$ with $a,b\ne0$ means $a^2=b^2$, i.e. $b=\pm a$. For each of the $10$ choices of $a$ there are $2$ choices of $b$, so there are $\boxed{20}$ ordered pairs.
