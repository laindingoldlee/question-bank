Modulo $3$, the numbers are congruent to $p,p+1,p+2$, so one is divisible by $3$. Since it must be prime, it must equal $3$. The values $p+10,p+14$ exceed $3$, hence $p=3$. Then $13$ and $17$ are prime, so $\boxed{p=3}$ is the unique solution.
