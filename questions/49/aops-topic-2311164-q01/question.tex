``Engineer's induction'' means checking a statement for the first few values of $n$ and concluding that it holds for all $n$. A student checks that $n^2-3n+2=0$ for $n=1$ and $n=2$, and concludes that $n^2-3n+2=0$ for every positive integer $n$. Show that the conclusion is false, find all positive integers $n$ with $n^2-3n+2=0$, and explain what is missing compared with a proof by mathematical induction.
