The $12$ letters of THRIFTYPIANO are written on $12$ tiles. Three of the tiles are chosen at random (all $\dbinom{12}{3}$ choices equally likely), and we count the number of different $3$-letter strings that can be formed by arranging the chosen tiles in a row (tiles with the same letter are indistinguishable). Find the expected number of strings.
