Multiplying by $-1$ reverses both inequalities: $0<\dfrac{1}{x}\le1$.

From $\dfrac{1}{x}>0$ we get $x>0$. Then multiplying $\dfrac{1}{x}\le1$ by the positive number $x$ gives $1\le x$. Conversely, every $x\ge1$ gives $0<\dfrac{1}{x}\le1$. So the solution set is
\[
\boxed{x\ge1}.
\]
