Adding pair rates counts each worker twice. The combined rate is $\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{1}{5}+\dfrac{1}{7}+\dfrac{1}{4}\right)=\dfrac{83}{280}$. The time is $\dfrac{280}{83}$ days, giving $\boxed{363}$.
