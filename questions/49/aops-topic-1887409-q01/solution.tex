Use $a$ three-dollar bills and $b$ four-dollar bills, with $a,b\ge0$ and $3a+4b=25$. Then $25-4b$ must be a nonnegative multiple of $3$. For $b=0,1,\dots,6$, $25-4b=25,21,17,13,9,5,1$, and the multiples of $3$ are $21$ ($b=1$, $a=7$) and $9$ ($b=4$, $a=3$). So there are $\boxed{2}$ combinations: seven $\$3$ bills and one $\$4$ bill, or three $\$3$ bills and four $\$4$ bills.
