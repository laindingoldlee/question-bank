Add 1 to complete a product: $(x+1)(y+1)=12 $. Both factors are at least 2. The ordered factor pairs are $(2,6),(3,4),(4,3),(6,2)$, giving $\boxed{(x,y)=(1,5),(2,3),(3,2),(5,1)}$. More generally, an expression $ xy+ax+by $ becomes $(x+b)(y+a)$ after adding $ ab $.
