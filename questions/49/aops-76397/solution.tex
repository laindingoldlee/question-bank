The mode is always $2$ and the mean is $\dfrac{25+x}{7}$. If $x\le2$, the median is $2$, so two of the three statistics coincide, impossible for a nonconstant arithmetic progression. For $2<x<4$, the median is $x$ and the mean exceeds $x$. Hence $2x=2+\dfrac{25+x}{7}$, giving $x=3$. For $x\ge4$, the median is $4$ and the mean exceeds $4$, so $\dfrac{25+x}{7}=6$, giving $x=17$. Both work; their sum is $\boxed{20}$.
