Standardise: for women, $z=\dfrac{74-64.3}{2.55}\approx3.80$; for men, $z=\dfrac{80-69.62}{2.81}\approx3.69$. The upper tail of the standard normal is larger at the smaller $z$: $P(Z\ge3.69)\approx1.1\cdot10^{-4}$ versus $P(Z\ge3.80)\approx7.1\cdot10^{-5}$. Both are multiplied by $\dfrac12$, so meeting $\boxed{\text{a man of at least }80\text{ in}}$ is more likely.
