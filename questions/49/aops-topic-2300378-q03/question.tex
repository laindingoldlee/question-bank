The Fibonacci numbers are defined by $F_0=0$, $F_1=1$ and $F_{n+2}=F_{n+1}+F_n$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Show that infinitely many Fibonacci numbers are multiples of $50$, so there is no largest one.
\item[(b)] Find the smallest positive Fibonacci number that is a multiple of $50$.
\end{enumerate}
