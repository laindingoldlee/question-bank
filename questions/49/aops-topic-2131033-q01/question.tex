Ron wants to ask Hermione to the Yule Ball, and so does Viktor Krum. Viktor asks her with probability $\dfrac{3}{4}$ and Ron asks her with probability $\dfrac{7}{9}$, independently. If at least one of them asks, Hermione goes to the ball with someone who asked. If nobody asks, she goes with Luna Lovegood with probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$, with Ginny Weasley with probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$, and otherwise she goes with no one. What is the probability that Hermione goes with no one?
