Hermione goes with no one exactly when nobody asks her \emph{and} she then goes with neither Luna nor Ginny. By independence,
\[P(\text{nobody asks})=\left(1-\dfrac{3}{4}\right)\left(1-\dfrac{7}{9}\right)=\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot\dfrac{2}{9}=\dfrac{1}{18}.\]
Given that nobody asks, she goes with no one with probability $1-\dfrac{1}{4}-\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{1}{2}$. So the probability is
\[\dfrac{1}{18}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{36}}.\]
