Find
\[
\left\lfloor\dfrac{1}{8}\right\rfloor+\left\lceil\dfrac{2}{8}\right\rceil+\left\lfloor\dfrac{3}{8}\right\rfloor+\left\lceil\dfrac{4}{8}\right\rceil+\cdots+\left\lfloor\dfrac{99}{8}\right\rfloor+\left\lceil\dfrac{100}{8}\right\rceil,
\]
where odd numerators get the floor and even numerators get the ceiling.
