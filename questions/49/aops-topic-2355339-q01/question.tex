Sarah rolls a fair $n$-sided die with faces $1,2,\dots,n$, and $x$ is the number it shows. If $x=n$ (which happens with probability $\dfrac1n$), the result of the roll is $n$; otherwise the result is $2x$, twice the number shown. Sarah computed the expected result for $n=6$, $10$ and $20$ and got $n$ each time. Prove that the expected result is $n$ for every positive integer $n$.
