Let each half of the road have length $d$ (speeds per minute). Going: $\dfrac {d}{a}+\dfrac {d}{b}=23$. Returning: $10a+10b=2d$, so $d=5(a+b)$. Substituting,
\[
\dfrac{5(a+b)}{a}+\dfrac{5(a+b)}{b}=23\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 5\left(1+\dfrac {b}{a}\right)+5\left(\dfrac {a}{b}+1\right)=23\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \dfrac {a}{b}+\dfrac {b}{a}=\dfrac{13}{5}.
\]
With $t=\dfrac {a}{b}$ this is $5t^2-13t+5=0$, whose discriminant $169-100=69$ is positive; both roots are positive (their product is $1$ and their sum is positive), so both are possible. By Vieta's formulas the sum of the possible values is $\boxed{\textbf{(D)}\ \dfrac{13}{5}}$.
