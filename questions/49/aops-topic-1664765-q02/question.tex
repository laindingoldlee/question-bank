Ali rides his bike from home to school. He rides the first half of the way at speed $a$ and the second half at speed $b$, and the trip takes $23$ minutes. On the way back, along the same road, he rides at speed $a$ for $10$ minutes and then at speed $b$ for $10$ minutes, and arrives home. Find the sum of all possible values of $\dfrac {a}{b}$.

$\textbf{(A)}\ \dfrac{33}{20}\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ \dfrac{28}{13}\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ \dfrac{23}{10}\qquad\textbf{(D)}\ \dfrac{13}{5}\qquad\textbf{(E)}\ \dfrac{23}{7}$
