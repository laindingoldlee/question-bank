Let $M$ be the largest. If $M\ge10$, the four other distinct integers are at least $\lfloor \dfrac{M}{2}\rfloor+1,\ldots,\lfloor \dfrac{M}{2}\rfloor+4$, so the total is at least $6+7+8+9+10=40$, too large. If $M\le8$, even the largest five choices sum to at most $4+5+6+7+8=30$. Thus $M=\boxed{9}$, achieved by $5,6,7,8,9$.
