Let $f(k)$ denote the $k^{\text{th}}$ smallest positive integer that is not a perfect square. For example, $f(1)=2$, $f(2)=3$, and $f(3)=5$. Let $n$ be the positive integer that satisfies

$$f^{2024}(n)=2024^2+1$$where $f^{2024}$ denotes the function $f$ composed $2024$ times. Find the remainder when $n$ is divided by $1000$.
