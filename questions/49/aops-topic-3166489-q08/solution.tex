Choose $A=(0,0)$, $O=(-2,0)$, $P=(3,0)$. The equilateral triangle vertices are $B=(-3,\sqrt3)$ and $C=(-3,-\sqrt3)$; its side length is $2\sqrt3$. The extensions add half this length, giving $D=(-\dfrac{9}{2},\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2})$ and $E=(-\dfrac{9}{2},-\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2})$. Hence $DE=3\sqrt3$, and the perpendicular distance from $P$ to line $DE$ is $\dfrac{15}{2}$. The area is $\boxed{\dfrac{45\sqrt3}{4}}$.

\end{asy}
