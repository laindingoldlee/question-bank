By the shoelace formula the area is
\[\dfrac{1}{2}\left|(-3)(-3-0)+(-2)(0-1)+x(1-(-3))\right|=\dfrac{1}{2}|11+4x|.\]
Setting this equal to $7.5$: $|11+4x|=15$, so $x=1$ or $x=-\dfrac{13}{2}$; with $x>0$, $x=\boxed{1}$.
