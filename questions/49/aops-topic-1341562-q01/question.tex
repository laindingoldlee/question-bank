An urn contains $1$ white ball, $2$ red balls and $3$ green balls. Three balls are drawn at random without replacement. Find the probability that
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] exactly one of the drawn balls is not red;
\item[(b)] at least one of the drawn balls is not green;
\item[(c)] the three drawn balls are not all of the same colour.
\end{enumerate}
