Treat the six balls as distinct; there are $\dbinom63=20$ equally likely draws.

(a) Exactly one ball is not red means two balls are red and one is not: $\dbinom22\dbinom41=4$ draws. The probability is $\dfrac{4}{20}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{5}}$.

(b) The complement is ``all three are green'', which happens in $\dbinom33=1$ draw. The probability is $1-\dfrac{1}{20}=\boxed{\dfrac{19}{20}}$.

(c) Three balls of the same colour are only possible for green (there are only $1$ white and $2$ red balls), so again the complement has probability $\dfrac{1}{20}$ and the answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{19}{20}}$.
