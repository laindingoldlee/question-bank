A right triangle has the following properties: the length of its shortest side is an odd positive integer $n$, and the lengths of its other two sides are consecutive integers. Prove or disprove that the area of this triangle is $\dfrac{n-1}{4}$ times its perimeter. (For example, the triangles $3,4,5$, $5,12,13$ and $7,24,25$ have area equal to $\dfrac{2}{4}$, $\dfrac{4}{4}$ and $\dfrac{6}{4}$ times the perimeter.)
