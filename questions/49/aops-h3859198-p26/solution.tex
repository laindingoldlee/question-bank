Complete the square to obtain $(a+17)^2-b^2=289-N$. An integer is a difference of two integer squares exactly when it is odd or divisible by four. Sufficiency follows from $2k+1=(k+1)^2-k^2$ and $4k=(k+1)^2-(k-1)^2$; necessity follows modulo four. The five values of $289-N$ are $190,189,188,187,186$, of which 189,188,187 qualify. Thus $\boxed{3}$ choices, answer C.
