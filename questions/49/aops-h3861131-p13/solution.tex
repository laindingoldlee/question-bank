At least eighteen of the 35 sophomores occupy one color class; move the other at most seventeen to vacant cells in that same class. Each move is one swap with a freshman, and no two cells of one color are edge-adjacent. For sharpness, initially fill a 5 by 7 rectangle with the sophomores. Its cells can be partitioned into seventeen adjacent pairs and one singleton, so any independent subset has at most eighteen members. At least seventeen sophomores must therefore change cells.
