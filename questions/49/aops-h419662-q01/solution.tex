Take the squares as $[0,4]\times[0,4]$ and $[2,6]\times[2,6]$. Their overlap has area $2^2=4$, so their union has area $16+16-4=28$. The two boundary intersection points are $(2,4)$ and $(4,2)$, at distance $2\sqrt2$. The circle therefore has radius $\sqrt2$ and area $2\pi$. It lies inside the union, giving shaded area $\boxed{28-2\pi}$, choice D.
