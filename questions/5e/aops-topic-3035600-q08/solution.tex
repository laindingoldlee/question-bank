Consider $c=(\sqrt2)^{\sqrt2}$. If $c$ is rational, take $a=b=\sqrt2$. If $c$ is irrational, take $a=c,b=\sqrt2$; then $a^b=((\sqrt2)^{\sqrt2})^{\sqrt2}=(\sqrt2)^2=2$ is rational. In either case the two bases and exponents chosen are irrational and positive. This proof establishes existence without needing to decide which case holds.
