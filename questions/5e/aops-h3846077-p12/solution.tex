Factor the polynomial:
\[
x^3-5x^2-22x+56=(x-7)(x-2)(x+4).
\]
Over the field modulo $p$, its roots are exactly the residues of $7,2,-4$. Fewer than three distinct residues occur precisely when $p$ divides one of their pairwise differences $5,11,6$. The primes are $\boxed{2,3,5,11}$, so the requested count is $\boxed4$.
