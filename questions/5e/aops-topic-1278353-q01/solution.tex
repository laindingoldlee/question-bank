With $N$ people: children $\dfrac{3}{8}N$, adults $\dfrac{5}{8}N$, women $\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot\dfrac{5}{8}N=\dfrac{5}{32}N$. Then $\dfrac{12}{32}N-\dfrac{5}{32}N=\dfrac{7}{32}N=140$, so $N=\boxed{640}$.
