There are two urns containing candy. One urn contains two pieces: one green and one red. The other urn contains one green and three red pieces. You pick a piece of candy at random from each urn and eat the two chosen candies. If you ate exactly one piece of green candy, you then draw another piece of candy at random from the urn that still contains a green piece, and eat that. What is the probability that you ate two pieces of green candy? Express your answer as a common fraction.
