In Nicomachus's pattern, $n^3$ is the sum of the next $n$ odd numbers after those used for $1^3,\dots,(n-1)^3$. (Indeed the $n$ odd numbers centred at $n^2$, namely $n^2-n+1,\dots,n^2+n-1$, add up to $n\cdot n^2=n^3$, and the last one for $n$ is followed by the first one for $n+1$: $(n^2+n-1)+2=(n+1)^2-(n+1)+1$.)

So $1^3+2^3+\cdots+8^3$ is the sum of the first $1+2+\cdots+8=36$ odd numbers, and by the Pythagorean pattern that sum is $36^2$. Hence
\[1^3+2^3+\cdots+8^3=36^2,\qquad s=\boxed{36}.\]
(Check: $1+8+27+64+125+216+343+512=1296=36^2$.)
