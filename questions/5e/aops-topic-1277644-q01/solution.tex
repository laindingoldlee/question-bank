For a third vertex $(x,y)$ the area is $\dfrac{|27y-14x|}{2}$. The integer $27y-14x$ cannot be $0$ for a genuine triangle, so the area is at least $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Since $\gcd(27,14)=1$, the value $1$ is attained: $27\cdot1-14\cdot2=-1$, i.e. the point $(2,1)$.

The minimum area is $\boxed{0.50}$.
