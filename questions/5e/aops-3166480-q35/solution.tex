The solutions are generated from $(x,y)=(1,1)$ by $(x,y)\mapsto(2x+y,3x+2y)$. To prove completeness, for any solution with $x>1$, $\sqrt{3x^2-2}=y$ lies between $\dfrac{3}{2}x$ and $2x$. The inverse map $(x,y)\mapsto(2x-y,2y-3x)$ therefore gives a smaller positive solution of the same equation, until $(1,1)$ is reached. (The inequalities follow directly for $x\ge3$; $x=2$ is not a solution.) Hence no solutions are omitted. Starting $x_1=1,x_2=3$, the recurrence is $x_{n+2}=4x_{n+1}-x_n$. Iterating with integers and comparing successive terms to $2^{75}=37778931862957161709568$ gives forty terms below the bound, with $x_{40}=15953372374843566659921$ and $x_{41}=59538796254981950751153$ straddling it. Therefore $\boxed{40}$.
