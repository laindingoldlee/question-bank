Treat $2^3$ as one quantity, say $t$. Then the expression is $t-3t=1\cdot t-3\cdot t=(1-3)t=-2t$ by the distributive law (just as $a-3a=-2a$). So
\[
2^3-3\left(2^3\right)=-2\left(2^3\right)=-2\cdot8=\boxed{-16}.
\]
Check: $8-24=-16$.
