Bearings are measured clockwise from north. The velocity of $Q$ points equally east and south, so its bearing is $\boxed{135^\circ}$.
The positions are $15t\mathbf i$ and $20t\mathbf i+(200-20t)\mathbf j$; subtraction gives
$\boxed{\overrightarrow{PQ}=5t\mathbf i+(200-20t)\mathbf j}$.
At $t=10$, this is $50\mathbf i$, so $P$ lies due west of $Q$, giving $\boxed{270^\circ}$.

Northeast requires equal positive components: $5t=200-20t$, hence $t=8$. Both components are $40$, and the distance is $\boxed{40\sqrt2\text{ metres}}$.
Finally
\[
(5t)^2+(200-20t)^2=200^2
\iff 425t^2-8000t=0.
\]
Thus $\boxed{t=0\text{ or }t=\dfrac{320}{17}\text{ seconds}}$.
