Let $\mathbf i,\mathbf j$ be unit vectors east and north. Boats $P,Q$ have constant velocities $15\mathbf i$ and $20\mathbf i-20\mathbf j$ metres per second. At $t=0$, their positions are $0$ and $200\mathbf j$ metres.
\begin{enumerate}\item Find the bearing of $Q$'s velocity.
\item Show that $\overrightarrow{PQ}=5t\mathbf i+(200-20t)\mathbf j$.
\item Find the bearing of $P$ from $Q$ when $t=10$.
\item Find their distance when $Q$ is northeast of $P$.
\item Find all nonnegative times when they are $200$ metres apart.\end{enumerate}
