You are going to buy $6$ donuts. There are $3$ flavours: chocolate, jelly and glazed. The store has an unlimited supply of chocolate and jelly donuts but only $3$ glazed donuts left. Donuts of the same flavour are identical. How many different selections of $6$ donuts can you buy?
