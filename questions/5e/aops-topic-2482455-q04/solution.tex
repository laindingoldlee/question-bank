Cancellation simplifies the numerator to $a^8+b^8+c^8+d^8+e^8+f^8+g^8+h^8$. By AM--GM, its average is at least $\sqrt[8]{a^8b^8c^8d^8e^8f^8g^8h^8}=abcdefgh$. Thus the expression is at most 0, attained when all eight variables are equal. The maximum is $\boxed0$.
