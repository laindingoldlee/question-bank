Let $S=\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{23}\dfrac{2k-1}{2^k}$. Then $\dfrac {S}{2}=\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{23}\dfrac{2k-1}{2^{k+1}}=\sum_{k=2}^{24}\dfrac{2k-3}{2^{k}}$, and subtracting,
\[
S-\dfrac {S}{2}=\dfrac{1}{2}+\sum_{k=2}^{23}\dfrac{2}{2^k}-\dfrac{45}{2^{24}}=\dfrac{1}{2}+\left(1-\dfrac{1}{2^{22}}\right)-\dfrac{45}{2^{24}} .
\]
So $\dfrac {S}{2}=\dfrac{3}{2}-\dfrac{4}{2^{24}}-\dfrac{45}{2^{24}}=\dfrac{3}{2}-\dfrac{49}{2^{24}}$ and
\[
S=3-\dfrac{49}{2^{23}}=\boxed{\dfrac{25165775}{8388608}}\approx2.99999416 .
\]
