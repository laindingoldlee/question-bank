Fix the number $t$ of thick layers. There are $6-t$ ways to split the remaining $5-t$ layers into medium and light counts. For any such split, the category order is forced and the individual juice choices give $4^t3^{5-t}$ arrangements. Thus the count is $\sum_{t=0}^{5}(6-t)4^t3^{5-t}=1458+1620+1728+1728+1536+1024=\boxed{9094}$.
