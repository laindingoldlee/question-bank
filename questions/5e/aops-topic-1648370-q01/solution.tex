The distance home is speed $\times$ time $=45\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=22.5$ miles. At $45-x$ miles per hour the same distance takes
\[
\boxed{y=\dfrac{22.5}{45-x}=\dfrac{45}{2(45-x)}}\ \text{hours}
\]
(or $\dfrac{1350}{45-x}$ minutes). This makes sense for $0\le x<45$: at $x=45$ he would not move at all. For example, $x=15$ gives $y=\dfrac{22.5}{30}=\dfrac{3}{4}$ hour, that is $45$ minutes.
