The equations are not independent: adding the first and third gives $a+b+c+d=24$, and adding the second and fourth gives the same thing. So we expect infinitely many solutions.

Let $a=t$. The first equation gives $b=11-t$, the second $c=10-b=t-1$, and the fourth $d=14-t$. The third equation then holds automatically: $c+d=(t-1)+(14-t)=13$. So the solutions are exactly
\[\boxed{(a,b,c,d)=(t,\ 11-t,\ t-1,\ 14-t)\quad\text{for any real }t},\]
for example $(5,6,4,9)$ and $(0,11,-1,14)$.
