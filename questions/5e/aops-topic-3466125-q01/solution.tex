The negative-exponent rule gives $a^{-b}=\dfrac{1}{a^b}=\left(\dfrac{1}{a}\right)^b$: invert the base and make the exponent positive. Thus $\dfrac{3^{-2}}{x^{-7}}=\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{9}}{\dfrac{1}{x^7}}=\boxed{\dfrac{x^7}{9}}$. The condition $x\ne0$ is necessary.
