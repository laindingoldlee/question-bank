Let there be $n$ nickels, $d$ dimes and $q$ quarters. Then
\[
n+d+q=30,\qquad 5n+10d+25q=500 \;\Longrightarrow\; n+2d+5q=100 .
\]
Subtracting the first equation gives $d+4q=70$, so $d=70-4q$ and $n=30-d-q=3q-40$. We need $d\ge0$ and $n\ge0$: $q\le17$ and $q\ge\dfrac{40}{3}$, so $q\in\{14,15,16,17\}$:
\[
(n,d,q)=(2,14,14),\ (5,10,15),\ (8,6,16),\ (11,2,17).
\]
There are $\boxed{4}$ combinations.
