The fifth jump has endpoints $(4m,0)$ and $(5m,0)$. Since the apple has positive height, $4m<30<5m$, giving $6<m<7.5$. Hence $m=7$.

Write the first arc as $y=kx(7-x)$ for $0\le x\le7$, with $k>0$. The fifth is $y=k(x-28)(35-x)$, so $4=k(2)(5)$ and $k=\dfrac{2}{5}$. Therefore
\[
\boxed{m=7,\qquad y=\dfrac{2}{5}x(7-x)\quad(0\le x\le7)}.
\]
