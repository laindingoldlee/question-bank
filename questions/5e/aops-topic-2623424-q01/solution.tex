One person digs $\dfrac{5}{10\cdot3}=\dfrac16$ of a hole per hour. So $n$ people working $d$ hours dig $m=\dfrac{nd}{6}$ holes. When $d=3$ this gives $m=\dfrac n2$, i.e.
\[\boxed{n=2m}.\]
So yes: $n$ is directly proportional to $m$, with constant $2$. Twice as many holes in the same time need twice as many people.
