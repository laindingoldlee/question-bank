If $a<b<c$ with $b\ge a+2$ and $c\ge b+2$, then $5\ge c\ge a+4$, which forces $a=1$, $b=3$, $c=5$. (In general, $a<b-1<c-2$ are three distinct numbers from $\{1,2,3\}$, giving $\binom33=1$.) The answer is $\boxed{1}$.
