For one-digit $x$ the quotient is $1$, not prime. For two-digit $x$ we check in order:
\[
\dfrac{10}{1}=10,\ \dfrac{11}{2},\ \dfrac{12}{3}=4,\ \dfrac{13}{4},\ \dfrac{14}{5},\ \dfrac{15}{6},\ \dfrac{16}{7},\ \dfrac{17}{8},\ \dfrac{18}{9}=2 .
\]
None of $10$ to $17$ gives a prime, and $18$ gives $2$. The answer is $\boxed{18}$.
