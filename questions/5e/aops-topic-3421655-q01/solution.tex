The polynomial is monic of degree $100$. The coefficient of $x^{99}$ is $-100\cdot3=-300$, unaffected by the lower-degree terms $-4x+3$. Vieta's formula says the root sum is the negative of this coefficient, namely $\boxed{300}$.
