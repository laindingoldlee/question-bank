Rewrite the equation as $m^2-p^2=n^2-2017$, i.e.\ $(m-p)(m+p)=n^2-2017$. Try $m=p+1$: then $m^2-p^2=2p+1$, so we need
\[
2p+1=n^2-2017,\qquad p=\dfrac{n^2-2018}{2}.
\]
This is a positive integer when $n$ is even and $n^2>2018$. So take $n=2k$ with $k\ge23$: then
\[
n=2k,\qquad p=2k^2-1009,\qquad m=2k^2-1008
\]
are positive integers, and
\[
n^2+p^2-m^2=4k^2-\left(m-p\right)\left(m+p\right)=4k^2-\left(4k^2-2017\right)=2017 .
\]
Different values of $k$ give different solutions, so there are infinitely many. (For example, $k=23$ gives $46^2+49^2-50^2=2116+2401-2500=2017$.) $\blacksquare$
