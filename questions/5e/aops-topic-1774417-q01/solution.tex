Let $r$ kg remain in each basket. The first basket kept $\dfrac{5}{6}$ of its apples, the second $\dfrac{10}{13}$ and the third $\dfrac{2}{3}$, so they held $\dfrac{6}{5}r$, $\dfrac{13}{10}r$ and $\dfrac{3}{2}r$ kg. Then
\[
\left(\dfrac{6}{5}+\dfrac{13}{10}+\dfrac{3}{2}\right)r=4r=2000,\qquad r=500 .
\]
The baskets held $\boxed{600,\ 650\text{ and }750\text{ kg}}$. (Check: $500=\dfrac{5}{6}\cdot600=\dfrac{10}{13}\cdot650=\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot750$.)
