There were $2000$ kg of apples in three baskets in total. After $\dfrac{1}{6}$ of the apples in the first basket, $\dfrac{3}{13}$ of those in the second basket and $\dfrac{1}{3}$ of those in the third basket were sold, the same mass of apples remained in each basket. How many kilograms of apples were in each basket at first?
