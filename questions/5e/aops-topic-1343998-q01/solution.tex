Complete the square in each variable:
\[
a^2-2a+1+b^2-2b+1=2\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad (a-1)^2+(b-1)^2=2 .
\]

\textbf{Integer solutions.} Two integer squares with sum $2$ must both equal $1$, so $a-1=\pm1$ and $b-1=\pm1$. This gives exactly four solutions:
\[
\boxed{(a,b)=(0,0),\ (0,2),\ (2,0),\ (2,2)} .
\]

\textbf{Real solutions.} The real solutions are all points $(a,b)$ on the circle with centre $(1,1)$ and radius $\sqrt2$, that is $a=1+\sqrt2\cos t$, $b=1+\sqrt2\sin t$ for real $t$; there are infinitely many, for example $\left(1+\sqrt2,1\right)$.
