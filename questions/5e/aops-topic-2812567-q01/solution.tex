The total is $36$, so the condition is $2L\le36-L$, i.e. $L=a_1+a_2+a_3+a_4\le12$. Count $4$-element subsets of $\{1,\dots,8\}$ with sum at most $12$: the minimum is $1+2+3+4=10$, and the subsets are $\{1,2,3,4\}$ (sum $10$), $\{1,2,3,5\}$ ($11$), $\{1,2,3,6\}$, $\{1,2,4,5\}$ ($12$). That is $4$ subsets, and each gives $4!\cdot4!=576$ permutations, for $4\cdot576=\boxed{2304}$.
