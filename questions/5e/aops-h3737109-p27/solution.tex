If the lengths 2 and 5 occur in the same rectangle, it is $2\times5$, and the other has area 30. Its unordered dimensions are $(1,30),(2,15),(3,10),(5,6)$, giving four pairs. Otherwise write the rectangles as $(2,a),(5,b)$, with $2a+5b=40$. Positive solutions are $(a,b)=(15,2),(10,4),(5,6)$. The first and third were counted already; the middle adds one. Thus there are $\boxed5$ unordered pairs of rectangles.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

\end{asy}
