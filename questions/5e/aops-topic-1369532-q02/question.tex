Five men can excavate $5$ tons of gravel in $5$ hours, and $4$ men drink $4$ quarts of water in $4$ hours. Assuming every man works and drinks at a constant rate, how many quarts of water will the men drink while they excavate $1$ ton of gravel? Express your answer as a common fraction.
