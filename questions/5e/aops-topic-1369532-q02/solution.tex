Measure everything in \emph{man-hours}. Excavating $5$ tons takes $5\cdot5=25$ man-hours, so $1$ ton takes $5$ man-hours. Drinking $4$ quarts takes $4\cdot4=16$ man-hours, so the men drink $\dfrac{4}{16}=\dfrac{1}{4}$ quart per man-hour.

So while $1$ ton is excavated, the men drink $5\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{4}}$ quarts. This does not depend on how many men do the work: more men finish sooner but drink at a higher combined rate.
