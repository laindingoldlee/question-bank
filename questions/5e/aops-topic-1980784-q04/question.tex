On Tuesdays, Ben walks from his home, at one end of Main Street, to the grocery store at the other end. On the way he passes the post office and then the police station, in that order. His home is $7.5$ blocks from the police station. The post office is $6$ blocks from the grocery store and $3.5$ blocks from the police station. Ben can ride his bicycle at $3.5$ blocks per hour and can walk at $0.5$ block per hour. What is the positive difference, in minutes, between the time he takes to walk from home to the police station and the time he takes to bike from home to the police station?
