If $5\mid a$, then $a^4\equiv0$. Otherwise $a\equiv\pm1$ or $\pm2\pmod5$, so $a^2\equiv1$ or $4\equiv-1$, and $a^4=(a^2)^2\equiv1\pmod5$. (This is also Fermat's little theorem for $p=5$.) $\blacksquare$
