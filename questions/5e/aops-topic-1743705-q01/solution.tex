The people not dancing are Roger, Anne, Peter and Mary, so the dancers are Henry, Louis, Elizabeth and Jeanne, in two couples. Henry's wife is dancing with Elizabeth's husband, who is not Henry, so Henry's wife is not Elizabeth; being a dancer, she is Jeanne. Jeanne's partner is then Louis, so Louis is Elizabeth's husband. The remaining men, Peter and Roger, are married to Mary and Anne, and Anne's husband is not Peter. So Roger's wife is $\boxed{\textbf{(D)}\ \text{Anne}}$.
