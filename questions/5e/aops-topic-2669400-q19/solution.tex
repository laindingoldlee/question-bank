Let $z=x+iy$. Then $z^2=x^2-y^2+2xyi$ and, for $z\ne0$, $\dfrac1z=\dfrac{x-yi}{x^2+y^2}$. So $A$ and $B$ together say (with $z\ne0$)
\[z^2-\dfrac1z=\left(x^2-y^2-\dfrac{x}{x^2+y^2}\right)+\left(2xy+\dfrac{y}{x^2+y^2}\right)i=3i,\]
i.e. $z^3-3iz-1=0$ with $z\ne0$.

Also $z^3=x^3-3xy^2+\left(3x^2y-y^3\right)i$ and $-3iz=3y-3xi$, so
\[z^3-3iz=\left(x^3-3xy^2+3y\right)+\left(3x^2y-y^3-3x\right)i.\]
So $C$ and $D$ together say $z^3-3iz=1$. This equation has no solution $z=0$, so both intersections are the set of roots of $z^3-3iz-1=0$. $\blacksquare$
