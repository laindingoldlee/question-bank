Curves $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ in the plane are defined by
\[A:\ x^2-y^2=\dfrac{x}{x^2+y^2},\qquad B:\ 2xy+\dfrac{y}{x^2+y^2}=3,\]
\[C:\ x^3-3xy^2+3y=1,\qquad D:\ 3x^2y-3x-y^3=0.\]
Prove that the intersection of $A$ and $B$ is the same as the intersection of $C$ and $D$.
