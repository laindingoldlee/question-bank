She walks $2$ km in $30+10=40$ minutes, i.e. $\dfrac23$ hour. Her average speed is $\dfrac{2}{\dfrac23}=3$ km per hour: the answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(A)}\ 3}$. (Averaging the two speeds, $2$ and $6$ km per hour, would wrongly give $4$.)
