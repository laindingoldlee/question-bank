Each day Dulce eats $\dfrac{10}{11}$ of a mint and then receives $1$ mint, so her supply never decreases over a full day. More precisely, at time $t$ days after the start (with $k=\lfloor t\rfloor$ cousin's mints received so far) she has
\[
2018+k-\dfrac{10}{11}t\ \ge\ 2018+k-\dfrac{10}{11}(k+1)=2018+\dfrac{k-10}{11}>0
\]
mints left. So she \textbf{never} finishes the mints: there is no such day. (The supply grows by $\dfrac{1}{11}$ of a mint per day on average.)
