Timmy will be allowed to go to a concert if he wins two games in a row in a three-game chess tournament against his parents. He chooses whether to play his mother or his father first, and his opponents then alternate (mother--father--mother or father--mother--father). He beats his mother with probability $m$ and his father with probability $f$, where $0<m<f<1$, and the games are independent. Which parent should Timmy play first to maximise his chance of going to the concert, and why?
