The inner expression $\left|x^2-2\right|+1$ is positive, so the equation is $\left|x^2-2\right|=4$, i.e. $x^2=6$ or $x^2=-2$. The real solutions are $\pm\sqrt6$, with sum $\boxed{0}$.
