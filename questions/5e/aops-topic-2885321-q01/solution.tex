Let $d=10^m-n$, so $0<d<10^m-10^{m-1}<10^m$ and $r=\dfrac d{10^m}\in(0,1)$. The sum is a geometric series with first term $\dfrac1{10^m}$ and ratio $r$: $\dfrac{\dfrac1{10^m}}{1-\dfrac d{10^m}}=\dfrac1{10^m-d}=\dfrac1n$. $\blacksquare$
