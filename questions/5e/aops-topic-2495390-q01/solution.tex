\textbf{(a)} The mean is $\dfrac{4+7+7+9+13}{5}=\dfrac{40}{5}=8$. The distances from the mean are
\[|4-8|=4,\quad|7-8|=1,\quad|7-8|=1,\quad|9-8|=1,\quad|13-8|=5,\]
with sum $12$. So the MAD is $\dfrac{12}{5}=\boxed{2.4}$.

\textbf{(b)} In the second list every number equals the mean, so every distance is $0$ and its MAD is $0$: the list has no spread at all. The numbers of the first list are on average $2.4$ away from their mean. The MAD measures how spread out a list is around its mean, which the mean alone does not show.
