In triangle $ABC$, $\angle C$ is a right angle and $CD$ is the altitude to the hypotenuse $AB$. Find the measures of $\angle CDB$, $\angle CBD$, $\angle BCD$, $\angle CDA$, $\angle CAD$ and $\angle ACD$ when
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $\angle A=20^\circ$;
\item[(b)] $\angle A=65^\circ$;
\item[(c)] $\angle A=a$ (in terms of $a$, where $0^\circ<a<90^\circ$).
\end{enumerate}
