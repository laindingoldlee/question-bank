Write $N=1000a+100b+10c+d$ and $S=a+b+c+d$, with $a\ge1$. Then $2N-121S=1879a+79b-101c-119d$. For distinct digits $c,d$, the largest possible value of $101c+119d$ is $101\cdot8+119\cdot9=1879$. Meanwhile $1879a+79b\ge1879$. Thus $2N\ge121S$, or $\dfrac{N}{S}\ge\dfrac{121}{2}$. Equality occurs for $a=1,b=0,c=8,d=9$, giving $N=1089$ and $S=18$. The minimum is $\boxed{\dfrac{121}{2}}$.
