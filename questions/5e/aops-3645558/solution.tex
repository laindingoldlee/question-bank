Let the units and tens carries be $u,v$. Units give $2A=D+10u$; tens give $A+B+u=A+10v$, so $B+u=10v$. If $u=0$, then $B=v=0$ and the hundreds column makes $D=0$, violating distinctness. Hence $u=1,B=9,v=1$. The hundreds column $9+1=10$ gives $D=0$ and carry one; units then force $A=5$, and thousands give $C=6$. Thus $\boxed{(A,B,C,D)=(5,9,6,0)}$, with $55+5995=6050$.
