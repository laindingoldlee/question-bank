Two unit circles with distinct centres meet exactly when their centres are at distance at most $2$. Adjacent vertices are distance $2$ apart; vertices two or three steps apart are at distances $2\sqrt3$ and $4$. Thus exactly the six adjacent pairs give tangency. The probability is $\dfrac{6}{\binom62}=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{5}}$.
