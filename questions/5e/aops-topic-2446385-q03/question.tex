Bobby cooks for his parents on every odd-numbered day of the year 2021 (the $1$st, $3$rd, $5$th, \dots\ day of the year), and also on these holidays: New Year's Day (1 January), Easter (4 April 2021), Independence Day (4 July), Halloween (31 October), Thanksgiving (25 November 2021) and Christmas (25 December). On how many days of 2021 did Bobby cook for his parents? (2021 was not a leap year.)
