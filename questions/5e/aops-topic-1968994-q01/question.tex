In the coordinate plane, the points $(0,0)$, $(20,0)$, $(20,19)$ and $(0,19)$ are the vertices of a rectangle. A ball of negligible size starts at $(0,0)$ and moves towards the point $(2,1)$; every second it travels a distance equal to the distance from $(0,0)$ to $(2,1)$. When it hits a side of the rectangle it bounces off by the law of reflection; when it hits a corner it comes straight back along the direction it came from. How many times does the ball hit a corner during the first $2019$ seconds?
