Record each way by the lengths of Fido's steps. These lengths are positive integers, each at most $3$, adding to $5$, and the number of stairs he steps on equals the number of steps he takes, so he must take at least $3$ steps.

The ordered lists of $1$s, $2$s and $3$s with sum $5$ are:
\begin{itemize}
\item two steps: $2+3$, $3+2$;
\item three steps: $1+1+3$, $1+3+1$, $3+1+1$, $1+2+2$, $2+1+2$, $2+2+1$;
\item four steps: $1+1+1+2$ in $4$ orders;
\item five steps: $1+1+1+1+1$.
\end{itemize}
Discarding the two-step climbs (he would step on only two stairs), there are $6+4+1=\boxed{11}$ ways.
