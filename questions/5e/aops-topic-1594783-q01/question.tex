Tom and Jerry share a chain of sausages joined end to end. They take turns, Tom first, biting through one of the remaining connections; after a bite, the biter eats every single sausage that has just come loose (a sausage is loose when none of its connections remains). Each of them tries to eat more sausages than the other. Who succeeds
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] for a chain of $7$ sausages;
\item[(b)] for a chain of $101$ sausages?
\end{enumerate}
