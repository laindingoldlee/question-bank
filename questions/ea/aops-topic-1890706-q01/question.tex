Carmichael plays a game with six cards numbered $1$ to $6$. He shuffles the cards and then deals them face up, one at a time. As he deals each card, he also counts aloud from $1$ to $6$. If, at any time, the number on the card dealt is the same as the number spoken, he loses the game. What is the probability that Carmichael wins? Express your answer as a common fraction.
