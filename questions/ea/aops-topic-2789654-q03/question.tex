Eight unit cubes form a $2\times2\times2$ cube. Every face of every small cube is red or blue. One-third of the $48$ small faces are blue, and two-thirds of the $24$ visible faces of the assembled cube are blue. Prove that the small cubes can be reassembled with every exterior face red.
