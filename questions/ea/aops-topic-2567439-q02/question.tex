Sigma wants to expand $(x+a)(x+b)$, where $a$ and $b$ are integers and $a=9$. By mistake he writes $7$ in place of this $9$, and so works with $(x+7)(x+b)$. He then finds that the vertex of the parabola $y=(x+9)(x+b)$ is at distance $\sqrt{122}$ from the vertex of the parabola $y=(x+7)(x+b)$. Find all possible values of the sum of the roots of the correct quadratic $(x+9)(x+b)$.
