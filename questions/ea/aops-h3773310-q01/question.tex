Suppose pairwise distinct positive integers $a,b,c$ have the property that all three numbers
\[
a^2+b+2c,\quad b^2+c+2a,\quad c^2+a+2b
\]
are perfect squares. Prove that if one such ordered triple exists, at least three such ordered triples exist. If the pairwise-distinct condition is removed, give one positive integer solution.
