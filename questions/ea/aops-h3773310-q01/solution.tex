Replacing $(a,b,c)$ by $(b,c,a)$ merely cycles the three displayed expressions, preserving the condition. Repeating gives $(c,a,b)$. These three ordered triples are distinct because $a,b,c$ are pairwise distinct. Thus a claim that exactly one such ordered triple exists cannot be true.
