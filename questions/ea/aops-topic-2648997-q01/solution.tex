The point $(x,y)$ is uniform in the unit square, of area $1$. The region $x+y>\dfrac32$ is the triangle with vertices $\left(\dfrac12,1\right)$, $(1,1)$, $\left(1,\dfrac12\right)$, with legs $\dfrac12$, so its area is $\dfrac18$. The probability is $\boxed{\dfrac18}$.
