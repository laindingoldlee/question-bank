The leading term has positive coefficient and even degree. More explicitly, $ q(x)=x^4\left(3-\dfrac{5}{x}-\dfrac{2}{x^2}+\dfrac{1}{x^3}-\dfrac{18}{x^4}\right)$, whose parenthesis approaches $ 3 $ as $ x $ tends to either infinity. Thus $\boxed{q(x)\to+\infty\text{ as }x\to\pm\infty}$.
