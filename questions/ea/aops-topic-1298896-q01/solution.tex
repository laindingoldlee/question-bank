Volume: $20.0\cdot8.5\cdot6.0=1020$ cm$^3$. Mass: $1020\cdot19.3=19686$ g $=\dfrac{19686}{28.35}\approx694.4$ oz. Value: $694.4\cdot356\approx\boxed{247{,}000\text{ dollars}}$ (about $2.47\times10^5$ dollars). (Gold is in practice priced per troy ounce, $31.10$ g; that would give about $225{,}000$ dollars.)
