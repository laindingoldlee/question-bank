Let the digits be $h$ (hundreds), $t$ (tens) and $u$ (ones). Then $t=u+5$ and $h=t-8$. Since $h\ge1$ (a three-digit number), $t\ge9$, so $t=9$. Then $h=1$ and $u=4$, and the number is $\boxed{194}$.
