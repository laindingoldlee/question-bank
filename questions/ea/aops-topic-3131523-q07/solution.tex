An equilateral triangle’s circumradius is side divided by $\sqrt3$, hence $OB=3$. The tangent is perpendicular to $OB$, so $OD=\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=\boxed5$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair O=(0,0),B=(3,0),A=3*dir(120),C=3*dir(240),D=(3,4);draw(circle(O,3));draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(O--B--D--O,blue);label("$O$",O,W);label("$B$",B,E);label("$A$",A,NW);label("$C$",C,SW);label("$D$",D,N);
\end{asy}
