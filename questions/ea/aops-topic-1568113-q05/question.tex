Let $K$ be the set of positive integers $n$ such that either $n$ is divisible by neither $3$ nor $4$, or $n$ is divisible by $5$. (So $15$, $20$ and $60$ are in $K$, but $6$ and $8$ are not.) Find the $79$th smallest member of $K$.
