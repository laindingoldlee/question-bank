Two cyclists start simultaneously from Berkeley and Stanford, ride at constant speeds to the other campus, then immediately return along the same road. Their first meeting is $17.5$ miles from Berkeley, and their next meeting is $10$ miles from Stanford. Both cyclists have reached the opposite campus and turned around before their second meeting. Find the distance between campuses.
