Two hackers want to draw the letters of ``I'm Bored!'' (only the letters count, not the apostrophe, the space or the exclamation mark). One hacker draws $0.25$ letter per minute and the other $0.4$ letter per minute. Working together at the same time, and sharing the work freely, how many minutes do they need?
