On a 25-question test with five choices per question, a correct answer earns $6$ points and a wrong answer earns $0$. Each answer is guessed independently and uniformly. Write the probability of a score above $5$ as $1-\left(\dfrac{b}{c}\right)^d$, where $b,c$ are coprime positive integers chosen as small as possible. Find the greatest prime factor of $b+c+d$. Choices: (A) $3$, (B) $7$, (C) $11$, (D) $17$, (E) $19$.
