A score above five requires at least one correct answer. Its probability is $1-\left(\dfrac{4}{5}\right)^{25}$. The smallest reduced base is $\dfrac{4}{5}$ (the exponent $25$ on the prime $5$ and exponent $50$ on $2$ have greatest common divisor $25$). Hence $b+c+d=4+5+25=34$, whose greatest prime factor is $\boxed{17}$, choice D.
