Let $c_i$ be the number of copies of $i$. Then $c_1+\cdots+c_8=6$ with all $c_i\ge0$. Stars and bars gives $\binom{13}{7}=\boxed{1716}$. Dividing $8^6$ by $6!$ fails because different multisets have different numbers of orderings.
