Let $E_k$ be the expected number of remaining minutes when he is $k$ metres from the left end ($0\le k\le10$). Then $E_0=0$, $E_{10}=1+E_9$, and
\[E_k=1+\dfrac12E_{k-1}+\dfrac12E_{k+1}\qquad(1\le k\le9).\]
Let $D_k=E_k-E_{k-1}$ for $1\le k\le10$. The middle equations say $D_{k+1}=D_k-2$, and the last says $D_{10}=1$. Hence $D_k=1+2(10-k)=21-2k$, and
\[E_3=D_1+D_2+D_3=19+17+15=\boxed{51}.\]
(In general, starting $k$ metres from the left end of a bridge of length $N$, the expected time is $k(2N-k)$.)
