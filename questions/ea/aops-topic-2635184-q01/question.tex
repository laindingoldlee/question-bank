Mr.~X is on a bridge $10$ metres long, $3$ metres from its left end. Every minute he moves $1$ metre to the left or $1$ metre to the right, each with probability $\dfrac12$, except that he refuses to leave the bridge at the right end: whenever he is at the right end, on the next minute he moves $1$ metre left. What is the expected number of minutes until he leaves the bridge at the left end?
