Write $y=px$ for $p=2,3,5$. For each $p$, $x$ runs through the divisors of $\dfrac{3600}{p}$ and the contribution is $(p-1)\sigma(\dfrac{3600}{p})$. Thus the total is $\sigma(1800)+2\sigma(1200)+4\sigma(720)=6045+7688+9672=\boxed{23405}$, using the multiplicative divisor-sum formula on $3600=2^4 3^2 5^2$.
