Put $P=(0,0),O=(1,0),Q=(2,0),A=(0,t)$ with $t\ne0$. The nontrivial intersections are $A\prime=(\dfrac{2t^2}{t^2+4},\dfrac{4t}{t^2+4})$ and $A\prime\prime=(\dfrac{t^2}{t^2+1},\dfrac{t}{t^2+1})$, obtained by substituting the two lines into their diameter-circle equations. Their joining line meets the horizontal axis at $R=(\dfrac{2}{3},0)$. Consequently $RA\prime^2=\dfrac{16(t^2+1)}{9(t^2+4)}<\dfrac{16}{9}$, tending to $\dfrac{16}{9}$ as $|t|\to\infty$. Thus the least strict upper bound is $k=\dfrac{4}{3}$, giving $\boxed{\lfloor1000k\rfloor=1333}$.
