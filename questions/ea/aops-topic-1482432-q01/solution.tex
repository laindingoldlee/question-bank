List the multiples $2015k$ and check the digits:
\[
\begin{array}{llll}
2015, & 4030, & 6045, & 8060,\\
10075, & 12090, & 14105, & 16120,\\
18135, & 20150, & 22165, & 24180,\\
26195, & 28210, & 30225, & 32240,\\
34255, & 36270, & 38285, & 40300,\\
42315, & 44330 . &&
\end{array}
\]
For $k=1,\dots,21$ the digits are not in non-increasing order (in each case some digit is larger than the one before it: for example $2015$ has $0<1$, $40300$ has $0<3$, $42315$ has $1<3$). For $k=22$ we get $44330$, whose digits $4\ge4\ge3\ge3\ge0$ are non-increasing. So the answer is $\boxed{44330}$.
