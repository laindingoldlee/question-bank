A unique mode is needed, so $x$ or $y$ repeats a value. Let $M_o$, $M_e$, $M_n$ be the mode, median and mean.

The mode is less than the median. The largest $y$ comes from taking $x=11$ (mode $11$) and $y\ge15$: the sorted list is $11,11,13,15,17,y$ (or with $y$ among them), the median is $\dfrac{13+15}{2}=14$, and the arithmetic condition requires the mean to be $14+3=17$, so $\dfrac{67+y}{6}=17$, $y=35$. Check: $35\ge17$, mode $11$, median $14$, mean $17$. \checkmark

The other choices give smaller $y$ (for example a mode of $13$ forces the median to be at most $14$ and the mean at most $15$, so $y\le23$), and a search over all integer pairs confirms the maximum. The greatest $y$ is $\boxed{35}$.
