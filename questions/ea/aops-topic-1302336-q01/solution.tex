Divisibility by $4$ depends on the last two digits. The two-digit endings made of distinct digits from $1$ to $5$ that are multiples of $4$ are $12,24,32,52$. For each, the other three digits can be arranged in $3!=6$ ways, so there are $4\cdot6=\boxed{24}$.
