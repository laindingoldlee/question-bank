Collecting powers gives $R_N=x^{\sum_{k=1}^N \dfrac{k}{(k+1)!}}$. Since $\dfrac{k}{(k+1)!}=\dfrac{1}{k!}-\dfrac{1}{(k+1)!}$, the exponent telescopes to $1-\dfrac{1}{(N+1)!}$. It tends to $1$, so by continuity the limit is $\boxed{x}$.
