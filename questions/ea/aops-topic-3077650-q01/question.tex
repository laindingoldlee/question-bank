A mass of $10^{15}$ pounds is divided into $10^{18}$ pieces of type A and $10^{100}$ pieces of type B. Each type-B piece weighs $10^{-99}$ times a type-A piece. If a type-B piece weighs $N$ pounds, find the integer nearest to $\log_{10}N$.
