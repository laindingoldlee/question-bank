A type-A piece weighs $10^{99}N$. Thus $10^{18}10^{99}N+10^{100}N=10^{15}$, giving $N=\dfrac{10^{-102}}{1+10^{-17}}$. Since $1<1+10^{-17}<\sqrt{10}$, we have $-102.5<\log_{10}N<-102$. Therefore the nearest integer is $\boxed{-102}$.
