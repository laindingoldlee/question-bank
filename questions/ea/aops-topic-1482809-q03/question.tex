Five triples $(a_1,b_1,c_1),\ (a_2,b_2,c_2),\ \dots,\ (a_5,b_5,c_5)$ are given, and in each triple at least one member has property $Q$. Find the largest integer $m$ for which one can always conclude that at least $m$ of the $a$'s, or at least $m$ of the $b$'s, or at least $m$ of the $c$'s have property $Q$.
