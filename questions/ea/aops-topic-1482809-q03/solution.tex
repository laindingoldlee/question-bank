From each triple pick one member with property $Q$. These $5$ picks are labelled $a$, $b$ or $c$, and there are only $3$ labels, so by the pigeonhole principle some label occurs at least $\left\lceil\dfrac{5}{3}\right\rceil=2$ times. So at least $2$ of the $a$'s, or $2$ of the $b$'s, or $2$ of the $c$'s have property $Q$.

We cannot conclude $3$: if exactly $a_1,a_2,b_3,b_4,c_5$ have property $Q$, every triple contains one of them, but each letter has property $Q$ at most twice. Hence $m=\boxed{2}$.
