The general term is
\[\dbinom8k\left(\dfrac{x^2}{2}\right)^{8-k}\left(-\dfrac{2}{x}\right)^k=\dbinom8k\dfrac{(-2)^k}{2^{8-k}}\,x^{16-3k}.\]
We need $16-3k=7$, so $k=3$, and the coefficient is
\[\dbinom83\cdot\dfrac{(-2)^3}{2^5}=56\cdot\dfrac{-8}{32}=\boxed{-14}.\]
