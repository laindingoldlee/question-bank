A solution must have $5-x^2\ge0$, hence $|x|\le\sqrt5$. Squaring yields
\[x^4-10x^2+x+20=(x^2-x-4)(x^2+x-5)=0.\]
The first factor has roots $\dfrac{1\pm\sqrt{17}}{2}$, and the second has roots $\dfrac{-1\pm\sqrt{21}}{2}$. Of these, $\dfrac{1+\sqrt{17}}{2}>\sqrt5$ and $\dfrac{-1-\sqrt{21}}{2}<-\sqrt5$ are excluded. The remaining two lie in $[-\sqrt5,\sqrt5]$, so $5-x^2\ge0$ and the squared equation implies the original one. Therefore
\[\boxed{x=\dfrac{1-\sqrt{17}}{2}\quad\text{or}\quad x=\dfrac{-1+\sqrt{21}}{2}}.\]
