By the addition formula, $\cos2x=\cos^2x-\sin^2x$, and $\sin^2x=1-\cos^2x$, so $\cos2x=2\cos^2x-1$. Rearranging gives $\cos^2x=\dfrac{1+\cos2x}{2}$. $\blacksquare$ (So $\cos^2x$ is a cosine wave of period $\pi$ oscillating between $0$ and $1$.)

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
import graph;
real f(real x){return cos(x)^2;}
draw(graph(f,-pi,pi),linewidth(0.8));
draw((-3.4,0)--(3.5,0),gray,Arrow(4));draw((0,-0.2)--(0,1.3),gray,Arrow(4));
label("$y=\cos^2x=\dfrac{1+\cos2x}{2}$",(0,1),NE,fontsize(7pt));
\end{asy}
