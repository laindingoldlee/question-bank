Let $e_1=a+b+c$, $e_2=ab+bc+ca$, $e_3=abc$, and $p_k=a^k+b^k+c^k$. Then
\[
e_1=2,\qquad e_2=\dfrac{e_1^2-p_2}{2}=\dfrac{1}{2}.
\]
Newton's third identity gives $p_3-e_1p_2+e_2p_1-3e_3=0$, so $e_3=-\dfrac{1}{3}$. Each root satisfies $t^3=e_1t^2-e_2t+e_3$, hence
\[
p_4=2p_3-\dfrac{1}{2}p_2-\dfrac{1}{3}p_1=\dfrac{35}{6},
\]
\[
p_5=2p_4-\dfrac{1}{2}p_3-\dfrac{1}{3}p_2=\dfrac{26}{3}.
\]
This fraction is reduced, giving $\boxed{P+Q=29}$.
