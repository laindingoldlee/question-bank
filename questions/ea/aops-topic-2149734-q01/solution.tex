Trying $a_n=r^n$ gives $r^2=4r+6$, the characteristic equation $r^2-4r-6=0$, with roots
\[r=2\pm\sqrt{10}.\]
The roots are distinct, so $a_n=A\left(2+\sqrt{10}\right)^n+B\left(2-\sqrt{10}\right)^n$ for constants $A,B$. From $a_0=0$ we get $B=-A$, and from $a_1=1$ we get $A\left(\left(2+\sqrt{10}\right)-\left(2-\sqrt{10}\right)\right)=2\sqrt{10}\,A=1$. So $A=\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{10}}$ and
\[\boxed{a_n=\dfrac{\left(2+\sqrt{10}\right)^n-\left(2-\sqrt{10}\right)^n}{2\sqrt{10}}}.\]
Check: the formula gives $a_2=\dfrac{\left(2+\sqrt{10}\right)^2-\left(2-\sqrt{10}\right)^2}{2\sqrt{10}}=\dfrac{8\sqrt{10}}{2\sqrt{10}}=4$, and the recurrence gives $a_2=4\cdot1+6\cdot0=4$.
