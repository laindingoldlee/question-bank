\textbf{(c)} Let $S=a+(a+d)+\cdots+\ell$ with $n$ terms. Writing the sum forwards and backwards,
\[
\begin{aligned}
S&=a+(a+d)+\cdots+(\ell-d)+\ell,\\
S&=\ell+(\ell-d)+\cdots+(a+d)+a .
\end{aligned}
\]
Adding term by term, each of the $n$ columns sums to $a+\ell$, so $2S=n(a+\ell)$ and
\[
S=\dfrac{n(a+\ell)}{2},
\]
the number of terms times the average of the first and last terms.

\textbf{(a)} Here $n=10000$, $a=1$, $\ell=10000$: $S=\dfrac{10000\cdot10001}{2}=\boxed{50005000}$.

\textbf{(b)} The terms are $4\cdot1,4\cdot2,\dots,4\cdot25$, so $n=25$ and $S=\dfrac{25(4+100)}{2}=\boxed{1300}$.
