Let Sky be $s$ years old now. Marvin was $s$ years old $33-s$ years ago, and Sky was then $s-(33-s)=2s-33$. So
\[33=3(2s-33),\qquad 6s=132,\qquad s=\boxed{22}.\]
Check: $11$ years ago Marvin was $22$ (Sky's age now) and Sky was $11$, and $33=3\cdot11$.
