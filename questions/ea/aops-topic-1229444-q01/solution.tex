The medians meet at the centroid $G$, which divides each median in the ratio $2:1$. So $AG=\dfrac{2}{3}AL=\dfrac{2}{3}BM=BG$ and $GL=\dfrac{1}{3}AL=\dfrac{1}{3}BM=GM$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(6,0),C=(3,4.5); pair L=(B+C)/2,M=(C+A)/2,G=(A+B+C)/3;
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(1)); draw(A--L,blue); draw(B--M,blue);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,N);label("$L$",L,NE);label("$M$",M,NW);label("$G$",G,S);dot(G);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

In triangles $AGM$ and $BGL$: $AG=BG$, $GM=GL$, and $\angle AGM=\angle BGL$ (vertical angles, since $M$ is on ray $BG$ beyond $G$ and $L$ on ray $AG$ beyond $G$). By SAS they are congruent, so $AM=BL$. Hence $AC=2AM=2BL=BC$. $\blacksquare$
