The angles are distinct positive integers. To maximize largest minus smallest, take the smallest $1^\circ$ and the middle at least $2^\circ$, so the largest is at most $177^\circ$. The difference is at most $176^\circ$, attained by angles $1^\circ,2^\circ,177^\circ$. Thus the answer is $\boxed{176^\circ}$.
