Factor out $12!$:
\[
12!+14!=12!\,(1+13\cdot14)=12!\cdot183=12!\cdot3\cdot61 .
\]
The primes dividing $12!$ are $2,3,5,7,11$, and $61$ is prime, so the largest prime factor is $\boxed{61}$.
