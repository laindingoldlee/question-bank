The probability that Evan laughs at Mr.\ Rushano's joke, given that the joke is funny, is $\dfrac{2}{5}$. The probability that Evan does not laugh, given that the joke is not funny, is $\dfrac{1}{3}$. The probability that Evan laughs or the joke is funny (or both) is $\dfrac{3}{4}$. What is the probability that Evan laughs and the joke is funny?

(a) $\dfrac{1}{10}$\quad (b) $\dfrac{3}{40}$\quad (c) $\dfrac{1}{15}$\quad (d) $\dfrac{1}{20}$
