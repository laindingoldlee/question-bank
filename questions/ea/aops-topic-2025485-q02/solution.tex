Let $F$ be the event that the joke is funny and $L$ the event that Evan laughs, and let $f=P(F)$. Then
\[
P(L\cap F)=\dfrac{2}{5} f,\qquad P(L\cap F^c)=P(L\mid F^c)P(F^c)=\left(1-\dfrac{1}{3}\right)(1-f)=\dfrac{2}{3}(1-f).
\]
The event ``$L$ or $F$'' is the disjoint union of $F$ and $L\cap F^c$, so
\[
\dfrac{3}{4}=f+\dfrac{2}{3}(1-f)=\dfrac{2}{3}+\dfrac {f}{3},
\]
giving $f=\dfrac{1}{4}$. Therefore
\[
P(L\cap F)=\dfrac{2}{5}\cdot\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{1}{10},
\]
and the answer is \boxed{\text{(a) }\dfrac{1}{10}}.
