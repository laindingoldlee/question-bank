$\dfrac1{T_k}=\dfrac2{k(k+1)}=2\left(\dfrac1k-\dfrac1{k+1}\right)$, so $\sum_{k\ge1}\dfrac1{T_k}=2$ and the tail is $\dfrac54\cdot2=\dfrac52$. The total is $\boxed{\dfrac{23}2}$.
