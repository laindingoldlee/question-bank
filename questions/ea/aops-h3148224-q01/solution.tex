The identity
\[
u^3+v^3+w^3-3uvw
=\dfrac{1}{2}(u+v+w)\bigl((u-v)^2+(v-w)^2+(w-u)^2\bigr)
\]
shows that either $p_1+p_2+p_3=0$ or $p_1=p_2=p_3$.
Modulo $2$, $p_1$ and $p_3$ have the same parity, while $p_2$ is odd because $c$ is odd. Thus their sum is odd and cannot be zero. Hence
\[
p_2-p_1=7+3a+b=0,\qquad
p_3-p_2=19+5a+b=0.
\]
Subtracting gives $a=-6$, then $b=11$. Therefore $p_1=p_2=c+6$, and
\[
p_2+2p_1-3p_0=3(c+6)-3c=\boxed{18}.
\]
