The first $N$ terms sum to $S_N=\dfrac{N(N+1)}{2}$, which tends to $+\infty$. Hence the series diverges and has no finite ordinary sum. In particular its ordinary sum is not $-\dfrac{1}{12}$; that number can arise from a different regularization convention, not convergence of these partial sums.
