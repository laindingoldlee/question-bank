In triangle $ABC$, $AB=27$, $BC=23$ and $CA=34$. Points $X$ on $AB$ and $Y$ on $AC$ satisfy $BX=16$ and $CY=7$. Let $O$ be the circumcentre of triangle $BXY$. Then $CO^2=\dfrac mn$ in lowest terms. Find $m+n$.
