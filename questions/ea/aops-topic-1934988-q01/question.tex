An equilateral triangle has sides of length $8$. An equilateral triangle with sides of length $4$ is cut off at the top by one straight cut, leaving an isosceles trapezoid. What is the ratio of the area of the smaller triangle to the area of the trapezoid? Express your answer as a common fraction.
