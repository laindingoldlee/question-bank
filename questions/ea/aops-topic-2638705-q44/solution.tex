Put $a=\dfrac{1}{x},b=\dfrac{1}{y}$. The equation becomes $ab+3a-2b=18$, or $(a-2)(b+3)=12$. Since $b+3\ge4$, the possible values of $b+3$ are $4,6,12$, giving $(a,b)=(5,1),(4,3),(3,9)$. These give three distinct reciprocal pairs, so the answer is $\boxed3$.
