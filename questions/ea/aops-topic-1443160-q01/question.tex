A club has $n$ boys and $n$ girls.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] In how many ways can a committee of $n$ members be chosen from the $2n$ club members?
\item[(b)] By counting the committees in (a) according to the number $k$ of boys on them, prove that
\[
\dbinom{n}{0}^2+\dbinom{n}{1}^2+\cdots+\dbinom{n}{n}^2=\dbinom{2n}{n}.
\]
\end{enumerate}
