By the midsegment theorem, each side of triangle $MNK$ is half of the side of $ABC$ parallel to it.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0), C=(4.8,0);
real x=(2.4^2-3.2^2+4.8^2)/(2*4.8); pair A=(x,sqrt(2.4^2-x^2));
pair M=(B+C)/2, Nn=(A+C)/2, K=(A+B)/2;
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); draw(M--Nn--K--cycle,red);
dot(M);dot(Nn);dot(K);
label("$A$",A,N); label("$B$",B,SW); label("$C$",C,SE);
label("$M$",M,S); label("$N$",Nn,NE); label("$K$",K,NW);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

So the perimeter of $ABC$ is $2\cdot5.2=10.4$. With sides $3k$, $4k$, $6k$ we get $13k=10.4$, so $k=0.8$ and the sides are
\[
\boxed{2.4,\ 3.2,\ 4.8}.
\]
