We need $x\equiv1\pmod7$, $x\equiv2\pmod9$ and $x\equiv3\pmod{11}$.

From the second condition, $x=2+9t$. Then $2+9t\equiv2+2t\equiv1\pmod7$, so $2t\equiv-1\equiv6\pmod7$ and $t\equiv3\pmod7$. Thus $t=3+7u$ and $x=29+63u$.

Now $29+63u\equiv7+8u\equiv3\pmod{11}$, so $8u\equiv-4\equiv7\pmod{11}$. Since $8\cdot7=56\equiv1\pmod{11}$, the inverse of $8$ is $7$, and $u\equiv49\equiv5\pmod{11}$.

Hence $x\equiv29+63\cdot5=344\pmod{693}$, and the smallest positive solution is $\boxed{344}$. (Check: $344=7\cdot49+1=9\cdot38+2=11\cdot31+3$.)
