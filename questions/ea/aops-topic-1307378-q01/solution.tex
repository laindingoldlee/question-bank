$2016=2^5\cdot3^2\cdot7$, so $2016^{2016}=2^{10080}\cdot3^{4032}\cdot7^{2016}$. Its divisors are $2^a3^b7^c$ with $a\le10080$, $b\le4032$, $c\le2016$, and such a divisor has $(a+1)(b+1)(c+1)$ divisors. We need ordered triples of positive integers $(a+1,b+1,c+1)$ with product $2016$; each factor is then at most $2016$, so the exponent bounds hold automatically.

Distribute the prime powers of $2016$ among three ordered factors: $2^5$ in $\dbinom{7}{2}=21$ ways, $3^2$ in $\dbinom42=6$ ways, $7^1$ in $3$ ways. The count is $21\cdot6\cdot3=\boxed{378}$.
