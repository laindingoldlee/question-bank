Since $1001=1000+1$,
\[
715\times1001=715\times1000+715=715000+715=\boxed{715715}.
\]
The secret: for any three-digit number $N$ with digits $a,b,c$, we have $N\times1001=1000N+N$. The number $1000N$ is the three digits $abc$ followed by three zeros, and since $N<1000$, adding $N$ simply fills the three zero places with $a,b,c$, with no carrying. So the product is the six-digit number $abcabc$: the boy just writes the number twice.
