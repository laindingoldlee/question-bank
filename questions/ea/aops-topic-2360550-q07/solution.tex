By the quadratic formula,
\[z=\dfrac{-1\pm\sqrt{1-4}}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{-1\pm i\sqrt3}{2}}.\]
(These are the two non-real cube roots of $1$: multiplying $z^2+z+1=0$ by $z-1$ gives $z^3=1$.)
