There are $8\cdot8=64$ equally likely ordered outcomes. The sums greater than $12$ are
\[
13:\ (5,8),(6,7),(7,6),(8,5);\quad 14:\ (6,8),(7,7),(8,6);\quad 15:\ (7,8),(8,7);\quad 16:\ (8,8),
\]
which is $4+3+2+1=10$ outcomes. The probability is $\dfrac{10}{64}=\boxed{\dfrac{5}{32}}$.
