In triangle $AFE$, $FA=FE=s$ and $\angle AFE=120^\circ$, so $AE=s\sqrt3$ (law of cosines: $AE^2=s^2+s^2+s^2=3s^2$). Thus $s=\sqrt3$ and the perimeter is $\boxed{6\sqrt3}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(2.8cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair[] V;for(int k=0;k<6;++k)V[k]=dir(120+60*k);
string[] L={"A","B","C","D","E","F"};
draw(V[0]--V[1]--V[2]--V[3]--V[4]--V[5]--cycle,linewidth(0.8));draw(V[0]--V[4],dashed);
for(int k=0;k<6;++k)label("$"+L[k]+"$",V[k],V[k]);label("$3$",(V[0]+V[4])/2,NE,fontsize(8pt));
\end{asy}
