Let $\omega=\cos\dfrac{2\pi}{7}+i\sin\dfrac{2\pi}{7}$. By De Moivre's theorem $\omega^k=\cos\dfrac{2\pi k}{7}+i\sin\dfrac{2\pi k}{7}$, so the numbers $\omega,\omega^2,\dots,\omega^7=1$ are the seven vertices of a regular heptagon on the unit circle, and the sum in the problem is the real part of
\[
s=\omega+\omega^2+\cdots+\omega^7 .
\]
Since $\omega^7=1$,
\[
\omega s=\omega^2+\omega^3+\cdots+\omega^7+\omega^8=\omega^2+\cdots+\omega^7+\omega=s ,
\]
so $(\omega-1)s=0$. As $\omega\ne1$, we get $s=0$. Taking real parts, $\displaystyle\sum_{k=1}^{7}\cos\dfrac{2\pi k}{7}=0$. $\blacksquare$

For the second part, the $k=7$ term is $\cos2\pi=1$, and $\cos\dfrac{2\pi(7-k)}{7}=\cos\dfrac{2\pi k}{7}$ pairs the terms $k=1,6$, $k=2,5$ and $k=3,4$. So
\[
0=1+2\left(\cos\dfrac{2\pi}{7}+\cos\dfrac{4\pi}{7}+\cos\dfrac{6\pi}{7}\right),\qquad \cos\dfrac{2\pi}{7}+\cos\dfrac{4\pi}{7}+\cos\dfrac{6\pi}{7}=\boxed{-\dfrac{1}{2}}.
\]
Geometrically, the vertices of a regular polygon centred at the origin add up to $0$ (rotating the set by $\dfrac{2\pi}{7}$ about the origin leaves it unchanged, so it also leaves the sum unchanged), and the cosines are the $x$-coordinates of the vertices.
