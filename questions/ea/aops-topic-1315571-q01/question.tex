Steph Hurry drinks one Powerade at each practice. There are four flavours of $1$-gallon Powerade and three flavours of $2$-gallon Powerade, with an unlimited supply of each. He must drink a $2$-gallon Powerade at his third practice. In how many ways can he drink exactly $6$ gallons in total, if the order matters?
