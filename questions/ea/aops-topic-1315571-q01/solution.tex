A way is a sequence of $1$- and $2$-gallon drinks adding to $6$ gallons with the third drink $2$ gallons; each $1$-gallon drink has $4$ flavours and each $2$-gallon drink $3$. Split by the first two drinks:
\begin{itemize}
\item $1,1$ ($16$ flavour choices), then the $2$-gallon third drink ($3$), then $2$ more gallons: $1+1$ ($16$) or $2$ ($3$): $16\cdot3\cdot19=912$.
\item $1,2$ or $2,1$ ($12$ each, $24$ total), third drink ($3$), then $1$ gallon ($4$): $24\cdot3\cdot4=288$.
\item $2,2$ ($9$), third drink ($3$), and nothing more: $27$.
\end{itemize}
The total is $912+288+27=\boxed{1227}$.
