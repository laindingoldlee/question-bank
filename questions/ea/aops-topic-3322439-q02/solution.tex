Place the right angle at $(0,0)$ and the other triangle vertices at $(L,0),(0,L)$. The hypotenuse is $x+y=L$. If the small square side is $s$, its opposite vertex is $(s,s)$, so $2s=L$. The area ratio is $\dfrac{s^2}{L^2}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{4}}$.

import geometry;
usepackage("amsmath");

draw((0,0)--(2,0)--(2,2)--(0,2)--cycle);draw((0,2)--(2,0));draw((0,0)--(1,0)--(1,1)--(0,1)--cycle,blue);label("$L$",(1,0),S);label("$s$",(.5,1),N);
\end{asy}
