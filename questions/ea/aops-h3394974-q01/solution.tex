Write $BD=2t$, $DC=t$, $AB=u$, and $AC=v$. The length condition gives $u+2t=v+t$, so $v-u=t>0$. Pythagoras then gives
\[
u^2+v^2=(3t)^2=9(v-u)^2.
\]
For $r=\dfrac{v}{u}>1$, this becomes $4r^2-9r+4=0$, so
\[
r=\dfrac{9+\sqrt{17}}{8}.
\]
The other root is less than one and is excluded. Thus $\boxed{m+n+p=9+8+17=34}$.

\end{asy}
