In the SmallState Lottery, three white balls are drawn at random from twenty balls numbered $1$ through $20$, and a blue SuperBall is drawn at random from ten balls numbered $21$ through $30$. When you buy a ticket, you select three different numbers from $1$ to $20$ and one number from $21$ to $30$. To win a prize, the numbers on your ticket must match at least two of the white balls or must match the SuperBall. If you buy a ticket, what is the probability that you win a prize? Express your answer as a common fraction.
