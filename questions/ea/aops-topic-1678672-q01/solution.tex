The white draw and the SuperBall draw are independent.

\textbf{White balls.} All $\dbinom{20}{3}=1140$ sets of three white balls are equally likely. Your three numbers match exactly two of them in $\dbinom32\dbinom{17}{1}=51$ cases and all three in $1$ case, so
\[
P(\text{at least two white matches})=\dfrac{52}{1140}=\dfrac{13}{285}.
\]

\textbf{SuperBall.} $P(\text{SuperBall matches})=\dfrac{1}{10}$.

\textbf{Combine (complement).} You lose only if both fail:
\[
P(\text{win})=1-\left(1-\dfrac{13}{285}\right)\left(1-\dfrac{1}{10}\right)=1-\dfrac{272}{285}\cdot\dfrac{9}{10}=1-\dfrac{2448}{2850}=\dfrac{402}{2850}=\boxed{\dfrac{67}{475}}.
\]
