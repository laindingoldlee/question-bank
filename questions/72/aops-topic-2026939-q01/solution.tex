Circle $A$ has radius $6$. Let the tangent line meet circle $A$ at $P$ and $Q$, touch circle $B$ at $T$, and let $\theta=\angle POQ$ with $0<\theta\le\pi$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0);
real R=6, r=3;
pair P=R*dir(30), Q=R*dir(150);
fill(arc(O,R,30,150)--cycle,paleblue);
draw(circle(O,R),linewidth(0.9)); draw(circle(O,r),blue);
draw(P--Q,linewidth(0.9));
draw(O--P,gray); draw(O--Q,gray); draw(O--(0,3),red);
draw((0,3)+(0.3,0)--(0,3)+(0.3,-0.3)--(0,3)+(0,-0.3));
dot(O); dot(P); dot(Q); dot((0,3));
label("$O$",O,S); label("$P$",P,E); label("$Q$",Q,W); label("$T$",(0,3),(1.3,-1));
label("$6$",(O+P)/2,SE); label("$3$",(0,1.6),W,red);
label("$120^\circ$",(1.0,0.9));
label("$B$",r*dir(240),NE,blue); label("$A$",R*dir(240),SW);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The smaller piece is the circular segment, with area
\[
\dfrac{\theta}{2}\cdot6^2-\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot6^2\sin\theta=18(\theta-\sin\theta).
\]
The function $\theta-\sin\theta$ is strictly increasing (its derivative $1-\cos\theta$ is positive on $(0,\pi]$), so the equation $18(\theta-\sin\theta)=12\pi-9\sqrt3$ has at most one solution, and $\theta=\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$ works: $18\cdot\dfrac{2\pi}{3}-18\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}=12\pi-9\sqrt3$.

The radius $OT$ of circle $B$ is perpendicular to the chord and bisects $\angle POQ$, so
\[
OT=6\cos\dfrac{\theta}{2}=6\cos60^\circ=\boxed{3}.
\]
