Two concentric circles $A$ and $B$ are given; circle $A$ has area $36\pi$ and is larger than circle $B$. A line that is tangent to circle $B$ cuts off a region of area $12\pi-9\sqrt3$ from circle $A$ (the smaller of the two pieces). Compute the radius of circle $B$.
