Write the time as $10a+c$ with tens digit $a\in\{0,1,\dots,5\}$ and units digit $c\in\{0,1,\dots,9\}$. All $60$ pairs $(a,c)$ are equally likely, so $a$ and $c$ are each uniformly distributed. By linearity of expectation,
\[E[a+c]=E[a]+E[c]=\dfrac{0+5}{2}+\dfrac{0+9}{2}=2.5+4.5=\boxed{7}.\]
