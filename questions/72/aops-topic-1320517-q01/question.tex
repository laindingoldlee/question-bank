Let $a$ and $c$ be different positive real numbers, and let $x,y,q,z$ be real numbers with $a^x=c^q$ and $c^y=a^z$. Prove that $xy=qz$.

A student argues: ``Multiplying $a^x=c^q$ and $c^y=a^z$ gives $a^xc^y=a^zc^q$; since the bases are equal, $xy=qz$.'' Explain what is wrong with this argument.
