Let the integers be $a\le b\le c\le d\le e$. Each integer appears in $4$ of the ten sums, so
\[
4(a+b+c+d+e)=110+112+113+114+115+116+117+118+120+121=1156,
\]
and $a+b+c+d+e=289$. The smallest sum is $a+b=110$ and the largest is $d+e=121$, so $c=289-231=58$. The second smallest sum is $a+c=112$, so $a=54$ and $b=56$; the second largest is $c+e=120$, so $e=62$ and $d=59$.

Check: the pairwise sums of $54,56,58,59,62$ are $110,112,113,116,114,115,118,117,120,121$, which are exactly the given sums. The largest integer is $\boxed{62}$.
