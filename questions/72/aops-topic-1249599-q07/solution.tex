Each factor is $\dfrac{n!}{(n-1)!}=n$, so the product is $1\cdot2\cdots10=10!=\boxed{3628800}$. (Alternatively the product telescopes: $\dfrac{1!}{0!}\cdot\dfrac{2!}{1!}\cdots\dfrac{10!}{9!}=\dfrac{10!}{0!}$.)
