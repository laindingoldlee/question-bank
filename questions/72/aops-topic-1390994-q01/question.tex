I have $8$ cookies, all with different designs.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] In how many ways can I give them to my $3$ (distinguishable) friends, if a friend may receive no cookies?
\item[(b)] In how many ways can I put them into $3$ identical (indistinguishable) boxes, if boxes may be empty?
\end{enumerate}
