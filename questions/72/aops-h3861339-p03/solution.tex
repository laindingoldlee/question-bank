Suppose at most three block sums exceeded $100$. Choose three blocks containing all such blocks. The other thirteen would have total at most $1300$. The selected three contain twelve numbers, whose sum is at most that of the twelve largest numbers:
\[
53+54+\cdots+64=\dfrac{12(53+64)}{2}=702.
\]
The whole board would then sum to at most $2002$. But its actual sum is $1+\cdots+64=2080$, a contradiction. Therefore at least four block sums exceed $100$ in every arrangement. Manu can always win, so $\boxed{\text{Fede cannot prevent a win}}$.
