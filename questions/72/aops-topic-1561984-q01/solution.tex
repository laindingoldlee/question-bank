Let the car travel for $t$ hours; then the truck travelled $t+2$ hours. The distances satisfy
\[
50t=40(t+2)+10\iff 10t=90\iff t=9 .
\]
Check: the car goes $450$ miles and the truck $40\cdot11=440$ miles. The car travelled $\boxed{9\text{ hours}}$.
