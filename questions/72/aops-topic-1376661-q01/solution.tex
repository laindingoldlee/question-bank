Expand $(1-a)(1-b)(1-c)$:
\[
(1-a)(1-b)(1-c)=1-(a+b+c)+(ab+bc+ca)-abc=ab+bc+ca-abc,
\]
using $a+b+c=1$. The three numbers $1-a,1-b,1-c$ are positive (each of $a,b,c$ is less than $1$) and their sum is $3-(a+b+c)=2$. By AM-GM,
\[
(1-a)(1-b)(1-c)\le\left(\dfrac{(1-a)+(1-b)+(1-c)}{3}\right)^3=\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^3=\dfrac{8}{27}.
\]
So $ab+bc+ca-abc\le\dfrac{8}{27}$, with equality exactly when $1-a=1-b=1-c$, i.e. $\boxed{a=b=c=\dfrac{1}{3}}$.
