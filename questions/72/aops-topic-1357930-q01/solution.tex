The first digit can be $1,2,3$ or $4$ ($4$ choices; it cannot be $0$), and each of the other four digits can be $0,1,2,3$ or $4$ ($5$ choices each). So there are
\[
4\cdot5^4=\boxed{2500}
\]
such integers. The student's $768=3\cdot4^4$ is wrong: it forgets that $0$ and $4$ are allowed digits (it uses only the digits $1,2,3$ for the first place and $0,1,2,3$ for the others, or a similar off-by-one count).
