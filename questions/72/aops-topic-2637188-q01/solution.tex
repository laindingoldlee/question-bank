An adult uses $\dfrac{1}{19}$ of a section and a child $\dfrac{1}{21}$. With $k$ adults and $k$ children,
\[\dfrac{k}{19}+\dfrac{k}{21}=N\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad40k=399N.\]
Since $\gcd(40,399)=1$, $40\mid N$. The least value is $N=\boxed{40}$, with $k=399$.
