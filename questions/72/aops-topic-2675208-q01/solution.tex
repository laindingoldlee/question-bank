The statement fails only for a card with a star and a square.
\begin{itemize}
\item Card 3 shows a star: its other side must be checked for a triangle.
\item Card 2 shows a square: if its other side were a star, the statement would fail, so it must be checked.
\item Card 1 (circle) and card 4 (triangle) cannot violate the statement whatever is on the back.
\end{itemize}
So exactly $\boxed{\text{cards 2 and 3}}$ must be turned over.
