Each card has either a circle or a star on one side, and either a triangle or a square on the other side. Four cards lie on a table showing: card 1, a circle; card 2, a square; card 3, a star; card 4, a triangle. Which cards must be turned over to check the statement ``every card with a star on it also has a triangle on it''?
