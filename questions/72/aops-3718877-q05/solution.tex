By the sine law, $\dfrac{BC}{\sin A}=\dfrac{10}{\dfrac{1}{2}}=20$. Since $AB=20$, we get $\sin C=\dfrac{AB}{20}=1$, so $C=90^\circ$ is forced. There is only one congruence class, with $AC=\sqrt{20^2-10^2}=10\sqrt3$. Its area, hence also its expected area, is $\boxed{50\sqrt3}$. Uniform selection of all translated or rotated embedded triangles would not itself define a finite uniform measure, which is why the congruence-class interpretation is specified.
