Put $m=S(n)$. The smallest positive integer with digit sum $20$ is $299$: two digits cannot suffice, and the smallest possible hundreds digit is $2$, forcing the other digits to be $9$ and $9$. Thus $S(n)\ge299$.
A number with $d$ digits has digit sum at most $9d$, so $d\ge\lceil\dfrac{299}{9}\rceil=34$. A number consisting of a $2$ followed by $33$ nines has digit sum $299$ and satisfies the requirement. Hence the answer is $\boxed{34}$.
