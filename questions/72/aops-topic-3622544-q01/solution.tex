Substitution into the digit sum gives $2y+1=13$, so $y=6$. Then $x+z=7$ and $xz=12$, so $x,z$ are the roots of $t^2-7t+12=(t-3)(t-4)$. Since $x<z$, $(x,z)=(3,4)$. The integer is $\boxed{364}$.
