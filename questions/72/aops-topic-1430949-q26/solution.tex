Let the sides be $l$ and $w$, so $2(l+w)=20$, i.e. $l+w=10$, and by Pythagoras $d^2=l^2+w^2$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real l=7,w=3;
draw((0,0)--(l,0)--(l,w)--(0,w)--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw((0,0)--(l,w),blue);
label("$l$",(l/2,0),S); label("$w$",(l,w/2),E); label("$d$",(l/2,w/2),NW,blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Then
\[
100=(l+w)^2=l^2+w^2+2lw=d^2+2A,
\]
so $A=50-\dfrac{1}{2}d^2$. Thus $m=50$, $n=\dfrac{1}{2}$, and $mn=\boxed{25}$.
