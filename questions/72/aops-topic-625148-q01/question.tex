A singer has memorized $12$ different songs. Each time he performs, he sings three different songs from these, and a performance is determined by which three songs he sings (the order does not matter). How many different performances can he give?

$\textbf{(A)}\ 440\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ 12\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ 110\qquad\textbf{(D)}\ 220$
