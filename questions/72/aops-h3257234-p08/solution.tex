Equality of tangents gives integers $m,n$ with
\[
11x=34^\circ+180^\circ m,\qquad19x=21^\circ+180^\circ n.
\]
Multiplying the first equation by $19$ and the second by $11$, then subtracting, yields $0=415+180(19m-11n)$. This is impossible because $180$ does not divide $415$. Thus $\boxed{\text{no real }x\text{ exists}}$, and no value of $\tan(5x)$ can follow from these conditions.

\end{asy}
