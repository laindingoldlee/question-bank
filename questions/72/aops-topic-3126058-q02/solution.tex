The sum of all 2023 roots of unity is 0, so $\sum_{S}z=-1 $. Conjugation permutes $ S $, giving the same sum for conjugates; hence (a) is $\boxed{-2}$. Every root has modulus 1, so $\overline z=z^{-1}$ and the summand in (b) is $ iz^{-2}$. Since $\gcd(2,2023)=1 $, raising to power $-2 $ permutes the nontrivial roots. Their sum remains $-1 $, giving (b) $\boxed{-i}$.
