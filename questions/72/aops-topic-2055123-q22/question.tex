In a video game level, the player must defeat a boss, which initially spawns as a single Generation $0$ boss. After the player defeats a Generation $k$ boss with $k<2020$, with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$ the game spawns one Generation $k+1$ boss and with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$ it spawns two Generation $k+1$ bosses, independently for each defeated boss. No boss of Generation greater than $2020$ ever spawns, and the level is completed when all Generation $2020$ bosses are defeated. The expected total number of bosses that spawn during the level (including the initial Generation $0$ boss) can be written as $\dfrac{m}{2^n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are positive integers and $m$ is odd. Find the remainder when $m+n$ is divided by $1000$.

$\textbf{(A)}\ 071 \qquad\textbf{(B)}\ 072 \qquad\textbf{(C)}\ 325 \qquad\textbf{(D)}\ 845 \qquad\textbf{(E)}\ 846$
