The units digit of a number in base $4$ is its remainder when divided by $4$. Every integer is congruent to $0,1,2$ or $3$ modulo $4$, and
\[
0^2=0,\quad1^2=1,\quad2^2=4\equiv0,\quad3^2=9\equiv1\pmod4 .
\]
So a perfect square ends in $\boxed{0\text{ or }1}$ in base $4$, and both occur ($4=10_4$, $1=1_4$).
