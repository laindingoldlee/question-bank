Surface area scales with the square of the edge, so the edges are in the ratio $\sqrt9=3$; volume scales with the cube of the edge, so the volumes are in the ratio $3^3=27$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
void cub(pair o, real s, pen p){
 pair d=(0.45*s,0.3*s);
 draw(o--o+(s,0)--o+(s,s)--o+(0,s)--cycle,p);
 draw(o+(0,s)--o+(0,s)+d--o+(s,s)+d--o+(s,s),p);
 draw(o+(s,0)--o+(s,0)+d--o+(s,s)+d,p);
 draw(o--o+d--o+(0,s)+d,dashed+p); draw(o+d--o+(s,0)+d,dashed+p);}
cub((0,0),1,blue); cub((2,0),3,red);
label("$s$",(0.5,0),S,blue); label("$3s$",(3.5,0),S,red);
label("volume $2$",(0.6,1.6),N,blue); label("volume $54$",(3.8,4),N,red);
\end{asy}

The smaller cube has volume $\dfrac{54}{27}=\boxed{\textbf{(B) }2}$.
