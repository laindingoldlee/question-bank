The number with $k$ ones is $R_k=\dfrac{10^k-1}{9}$, and $41\mid R_k$ exactly when $41\mid10^k-1$ (as $\gcd(41,9)=1$). Modulo $41$: $10^2\equiv18$, $10^3\equiv180\equiv16$, $10^4\equiv160\equiv37$, $10^5\equiv370\equiv1$. So the least $k$ is $5$ (the order of $10$ is $5$), and the answer is $\boxed{11111}=41\cdot271$.
