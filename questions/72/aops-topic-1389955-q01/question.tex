Cora has five balls: two red, two blue and one yellow; balls of the same colour look identical. She has two containers, one red and one green. Each ball is placed in one of the two containers at random, independently and with equal probability. What is the probability that the two red balls are alone in the red container (that is, the red container holds exactly the two red balls)? Express your answer as a common fraction.
