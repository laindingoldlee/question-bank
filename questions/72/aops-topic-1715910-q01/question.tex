A seller has watermelons, melons and young corn cobs, $239$ pieces in total, with at least one of each kind. One buyer bought $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of all the watermelons, $\dfrac{3}{5}$ of all the melons and $\dfrac{5}{7}$ of all the corn cobs. A second buyer bought $\dfrac{1}{13}$ of all the watermelons, $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of all the melons and $\dfrac{1}{5}$ of all the corn cobs. How many watermelons, melons and corn cobs did the seller have at the beginning, and how many pieces in total did the second buyer buy?
