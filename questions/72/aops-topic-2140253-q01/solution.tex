Let $w_n$ be the number of such words of length $n$. A good word of length $n\ge3$ either ends in $b$, and then its first $n-1$ letters form any good word, or ends in $a$, and then the letter before is $b$ and the first $n-2$ letters form any good word. So
\[w_n=w_{n-1}+w_{n-2}.\]
Since $w_1=2$ ($a$, $b$) and $w_2=3$ ($ab$, $ba$, $bb$), we get
\[w_3=5,\quad w_4=8,\quad w_5=13,\quad w_6=21,\quad w_7=\boxed{34}.\]
(Check by counting the $a$'s: with $k$ letters $a$, no two adjacent, there are $\dbinom{8-k}{k}$ words, and $1+7+15+10+1=34$.)
