Derik's bike has a problem: after every $528$ feet that he rides, its wheels come off, and it takes him exactly one minute to put them back on, during which he does not move. He must ride $10$ miles to a barbecue, and he must arrive within two hours or his pizza will be cold. He rides at a constant speed whenever he is moving, and once he arrives he does not need to put the wheels back on. What is the minimum speed, in miles per hour, at which he must ride? ($1$ mile $=5280$ feet.)
