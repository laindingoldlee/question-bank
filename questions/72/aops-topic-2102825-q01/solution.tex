Ten miles is $52800$ feet, which is exactly $100$ stretches of $528$ feet. The wheels come off at the end of each stretch; the last time is at the barbecue itself, where no repair is needed. So he makes $99$ repairs on the way, taking $99$ minutes. That leaves at most $120-99=21$ minutes, or $\dfrac{21}{60}=\dfrac{7}{20}$ hour, for riding $10$ miles, so his speed must be at least
\[\dfrac{10}{\dfrac{7}{20}}=\dfrac{200}{7}\approx28.6\text{ miles per hour}.\]
The minimum speed is $\boxed{\dfrac{200}{7}}$ miles per hour. (If he also had to refit the wheels on arrival, he would have only $20$ minutes and would need $30$ miles per hour.)
