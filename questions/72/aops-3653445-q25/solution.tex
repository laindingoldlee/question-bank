With $n$ submissions the score is at most $\lfloor\dfrac{30}{n}\rfloor$, attained when one submitted integer equals the total number of submissions $N$. If the other teams'submission count is known as $S_0$, submit just $S_0+1$: then $n=1,N=S_0+1,d=0$ and the maximum 30 points is attained. Without information about $S_0$, no uniquely optimal prediction follows without a probability model.
