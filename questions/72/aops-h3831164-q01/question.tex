A quadratic $az^2+bz+c=0$ with real coefficients and $a\ne0$ has nonreal roots $z_1,z_2$ satisfying
\[
\left|2z_1-\dfrac{1}{9}\right|=|z_1-z_2|,\qquad |z_1|=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt k}.
\]
Let $w$ be either root of $cw^2+bw+a=0$. How many positive integers $k$ allow exactly nine complex numbers $z_3$ such that $\operatorname{Im}z_3$ is an integer, $z_3-w$ is purely imaginary, and $|z_3|\le|w|$?
