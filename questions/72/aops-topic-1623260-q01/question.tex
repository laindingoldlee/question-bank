Deb's $16$-ounce mug is filled with cocoa powder, and Harold's $16$-ounce mug is empty. Deb pours one-third of the cocoa powder from her mug into Harold's, then Harold fills his mug to the top with milk. Harold stirs his mixture, then pours enough back into Deb's mug to fill it. Find the number of ounces of cocoa powder now in Deb's mug. Express your answer as a mixed number.
