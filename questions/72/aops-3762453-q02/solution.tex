Take $A$ as the origin, write vertex vectors $b,c$, and let $u$ be a unit vector along $\ell$. The projection vectors are $(b\cdot u)u$ and $(c\cdot u)u$. The two specified lines have equations $X\cdot c=(b\cdot u)(c\cdot u)$ and $X\cdot b=(b\cdot u)(c\cdot u)$. Because $b,c$ are independent, these have a unique intersection. Subtracting gives $X\cdot(c-b)=0$, exactly the equation of the altitude line $AD$.

import graph;

pair A=(0,0),B=(-3,-3),C=(4,-3),u=dir(20),Bp=dot(B,u)*u,Cp=dot(C,u)*u;draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(-4u--5u);draw(B--Bp,dashed);draw(C--Cp,dashed);draw(A--(0,-4),dotted);label("$A$",A,(0,1));label("$B$",B,(-1,-1));label("$C$",C,(1,-1));label("$B^{\prime}$",Bp,(0,1));label("$C^{\prime}$",Cp,(0,1));
\end{asy}
