For positive integers, $\gcd(a,b)\cdot\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=ab$, so $31n=527$ and $n=17$. Since $31$ and $17$ are distinct primes, $\gcd=1$ and $\operatorname{lcm}=527$, and the sum is $\boxed{\textbf{(C) }528}$.
