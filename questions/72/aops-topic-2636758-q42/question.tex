A semicircle is placed inside a semicircle of radius $12$ so that the arc of the smaller one is tangent to the diameter of the larger one and the endpoints of the smaller one's diameter lie on the arc of the larger one. A rectangle whose width $W$ is half its length $L$ is inscribed in the smaller semicircle (one side on its diameter, the other two vertices on its arc). Among all such configurations, the area of the rectangle is as large as possible. What is $W+L$?
