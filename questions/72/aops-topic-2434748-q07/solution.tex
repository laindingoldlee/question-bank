With $p$ pennies, $1\le p\le2019$, the amount is $p+5(2020-p)=10100-4p$ cents. It is greatest for $p=1$ ($10096$ cents) and least for $p=2019$ ($2024$ cents). The difference is $4\cdot2018=8072$, so the answer is $\boxed{\textbf{(C) }8072}$.
