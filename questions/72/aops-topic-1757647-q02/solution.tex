Factor: $x^2-4x-45=(x-9)(x+5)$. The product is $0$ at $x=-5$ and $x=9$; it is negative when the factors have opposite signs, i.e.\ for $-5<x<9$, and positive for $x<-5$ or $x>9$. Including the zeros (because of $\le$), the solution is $\boxed{-5\le x\le9}$, i.e.\ $[-5,9]$.
