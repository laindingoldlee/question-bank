Let $s=5x+12y$. The conditions are $9s\le800\le11s$, i.e. $73\le s\le88$.

Since $x+y=\dfrac{s-7y}5$, the sum is largest when $y$ is small and $s$ large: $y=1$ needs $5x\le76$, so $x\le15$, and $(x,y)=(15,1)$ gives $s=87$ and $x+y=\boxed{16}$. (Larger $y$ only lowers $x+y$, as $x+y\le\dfrac{88-7y}5$.)

The sum is smallest when $y$ is large: $x\ge1$ forces $12y\le s-5\le83$, so $y\le6$; $y=6$, $x=1$ gives $s=77$ and $x+y=\boxed{7}$. With $y\le5$, $x\ge\dfrac{73-60}5$, so $x\ge3$ and $x+y\ge8$.
