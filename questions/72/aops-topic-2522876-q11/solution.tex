As $x$ ranges over the reals, $\left\lfloor\dfrac x2\right\rfloor$ takes every integer value $m$ (for example at $x=2m$), so $2\left\lfloor\dfrac x2\right\rfloor$ takes every even integer value. Likewise $3\left\lceil\dfrac y3\right\rceil$ takes every multiple of $3$ (at $y=3n$).

Every integer $k$ is an even integer plus a multiple of $3$: $k=2(-k)+3k$. Taking $x=-2k$ and $y=3k$ gives
\[2\left\lfloor\dfrac{-2k}{2}\right\rfloor+3\left\lceil\dfrac{3k}{3}\right\rceil=-2k+3k=k.\]
So every integer $k$ works, and the integers with $1<k<2021$ are $2,3,\dots,2020$: there are $\boxed{2019}$ of them.
