Triangle $ABC$ is equilateral. Three circles of equal radius, centred at $P$, $Q$ and $R$ inside the triangle, are each tangent to the other two. The circle centred at $P$ is tangent to sides $AB$ and $AC$, the circle centred at $Q$ to sides $AC$ and $BC$, and the circle centred at $R$ to sides $AB$ and $BC$. If $\dfrac{[ABC]}{[PQR]}=a+b\sqrt3$ with integers $a,b$, find $a+b$. (Here $[\,\cdot\,]$ denotes area.)
