Modulo 58, $3^5=243\equiv11$, so $3^{10}\equiv121\equiv5$. Also $7^2\equiv-9$, $7^4\equiv23$, and $7^5\equiv45\equiv-13$, hence $7^{10}\equiv169\equiv53$. The sum is $1+5+53=59\equiv\boxed1$.
