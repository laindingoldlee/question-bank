Since $300=2^2\cdot3\cdot5^2$, both $m$ and $n$ divide $300$: $m=2^{a}3^{b}5^{c}$ and $n=2^{a'}3^{b'}5^{c'}$, and the condition is
\[\max(a,a')=2,\qquad\max(b,b')=1,\qquad\max(c,c')=2.\]
For the prime $2$: pairs of exponents from $\{0,1,2\}$ with maximum $2$ number $3^2-2^2=5$. For $3$: $2^2-1^2=3$. For $5$: again $5$. The choices are independent, so there are $5\cdot3\cdot5=\boxed{75}$ ordered pairs: exactly the pairs of divisors of $300$ such that each of $2^2$, $3$, $5^2$ divides at least one of $m$, $n$ to that full power.
