Factor $6767=67\cdot101$. We compute the needed residues of $E=T_9$. For every tower of height at least 2, its exponent is divisible by 4, so its units digit is 6. Hence $T_8\equiv6\pmod{10}$. Modulo 11, Fermat gives $E=8^{T_8}\equiv8^6\equiv3$; $E$ is even, so $E\equiv14\pmod{22}$. Direct squaring gives $8^{22}\equiv1\pmod{67}$ and $8^{14}\equiv24$, so $T_{10}\equiv24\pmod{67}$. To work modulo 101, first compute $E\pmod{100}$. The powers of 8 modulo 20 cycle $8,4,12,16$, so $T_8\equiv16\pmod{20}$ because its exponent $T_7$ is divisible by 4. Modulo 25, $8^{20}\equiv1$, so $E\equiv8^{16}\equiv6\pmod{25}$. Also $E\equiv0\pmod4$, yielding $E\equiv56\pmod{100}$. Fermat now gives $T_{10}\equiv8^{56}\equiv52\pmod{101}$. Finally $1163=17\cdot67+24=11\cdot101+52$, and $0\le1163<6767$. By the Chinese remainder theorem, the answer is $\boxed{1163}$.
