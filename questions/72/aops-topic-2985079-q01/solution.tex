The two $L$s must avoid positions $1$ and $3$, so they occupy two of positions $2,4,5$: three choices.
\begin{itemize}
\item $L$s in $2,4$: $I,A,C$ fill positions $1,3,5$ with $C\ne5$: $3!-2!=4$.
\item $L$s in $2,5$: $I,A,C$ fill $1,3,4$ with $A\ne4$: $4$.
\item $L$s in $4,5$: $I,A,C$ fill $1,2,3$ with $I\ne2$: $4$.
\end{itemize}
Total $\boxed{12}$.
