Let $a=x-1$, $b=y-2$, $c=z-3$. Then $a+b+c=0$, and the identity $a^3+b^3+c^3-3abc=(a+b+c)(\cdots)$ gives $a^3+b^3+c^3=3abc$. So $abc=0$; say $c=0$ (the other cases are the same). Then $b=-a$, and since $2013$ is odd, $a^{2013}+(-a)^{2013}+0=0$. Hence $F=\boxed{0}$.
