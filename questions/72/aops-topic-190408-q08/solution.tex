The geometric condition is $a^2=4b$, so $a$ is even and $b=\dfrac{a^2}{4}$ must be a digit: $a\in\{2,4,6\}$ gives $b\in\{1,4,9\}$ ($a=8$ gives $16$; $a=0$ is not allowed as a leading digit). The arithmetic condition is $c=\dfrac{b+5}{2}$, so $b$ must be odd: $b=1$ ($c=3$) or $b=9$ ($c=7$). The numbers are $213$ and $697$, and the greatest is $\boxed{697}$.
