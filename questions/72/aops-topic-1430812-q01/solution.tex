The second hand makes one rotation in $1$ minute. The hour hand makes one rotation in $12$ hours (not $1$ hour; that is the minute hand), which is $12\cdot60=720$ minutes. So the second hand is $\boxed{\textbf{(E)}\ 720}$ times as fast.

(The poster's $60$ compares the second hand with the minute hand.)
