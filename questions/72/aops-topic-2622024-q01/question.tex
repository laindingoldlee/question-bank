Let $n$ be a positive integer with $n\le100$. The $101$ numbers
\[0\cdot n\bmod101,\quad1\cdot n\bmod101,\quad2\cdot n\bmod101,\quad\dots,\quad100\cdot n\bmod101\]
are written in a row, where $a\bmod101$ denotes the remainder of $a$ on division by $101$ (a number from $0$ to $100$). How many pairs of neighbouring numbers in this row have the left number bigger than the right one? Give the answer in terms of $n$.
