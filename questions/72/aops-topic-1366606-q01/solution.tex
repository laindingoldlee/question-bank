The parabola $h=-16t^2+35t$ opens downward, so its maximum is at the vertex $t=\dfrac{35}{32}$:
\[h_{\max}=-16\left(\dfrac{35}{32}\right)^2+35\cdot\dfrac{35}{32}=\dfrac{35^2}{64}=\dfrac{1225}{64}\approx19.1\text{ ft}.\]
For $0\le h\le h_{\max}$ the equation $-16t^2+35t=h$ has a real solution $t\ge0$, and for $h>h_{\max}$ it has none. (Equivalently, the discriminant of $16t^2-35t+h=0$ is $1225-64h$, which is $\ge0$ exactly when $h\le\dfrac{1225}{64}$.) So among the choices only $\boxed{\text{A. }h=16\text{ ft}}$ is possible; it is reached at $t=\dfrac{35\pm\sqrt{201}}{32}$, i.e.\ about $0.65$ s and $1.54$ s.
