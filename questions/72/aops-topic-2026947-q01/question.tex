For a positive integer $n$, let $f(n)$ be the product of the digits of $n$, and let $g(n)$ be the one-digit number obtained by applying $f$ repeatedly until a one-digit number is reached (for a one-digit $n$, $g(n)=n$). For example, $g(673)=g(126)=g(12)=2$, $g(10)=0$, and $g(1)=1$. Find the number of integers $k$ from $1$ to $100$, inclusive, such that $g(k)=0$.
