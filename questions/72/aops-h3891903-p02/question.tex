For $f(x)=x^2$, answer the following.
\begin{enumerate}
\item State where $f$ is increasing and decreasing.
\item Compute its average rates of change on $[-2,-1],[-1,0],[0,1]$.
\item For $h\ne0$, simplify $\dfrac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}$, and use the limit as $h\to0$ to find $f'(x)$.
\item Determine its concavity. At $x=0$, does it have a zero, a stationary point, or an inflection point?
\end{enumerate}
