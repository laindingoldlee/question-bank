Use the complement. $P(0\text{ heads})=\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^4=\dfrac{1}{81}$ and $P(1\text{ head})=4\cdot\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right)^3=\dfrac{8}{81}$. So $P(\text{at least }2)=1-\dfrac{9}{81}=\boxed{\dfrac{8}{9}}$.
