Label four watermelons $A,B,C,D$. Weigh the four triples $ABC,ABD,ACD,BCD$. Adding these readings counts each of the four weights three times, so divide their sum by three to obtain the total of these four. Split the other six watermelons into two triples and weigh each once. Add those two readings to the first total. This uses exactly $\boxed6$ weighings and determines the full weight.
