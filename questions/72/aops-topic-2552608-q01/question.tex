Four mutually exclusive events have probabilities $\dfrac1x$, $\dfrac1y$, $\dfrac1z$ and $\dfrac1w$, where
\begin{itemize}
\item $x$ is the sum of the roots of $2t^2-729t+540=0$;
\item $y$ is the smallest positive integer that can be written as the sum of two positive cubes in two different ways;
\item $z$ is the number of ordered pairs of integers $(p,q)$ with $\dfrac1p+\dfrac1q=\dfrac1{12}$;
\item $w$ is the third prime number.
\end{itemize}
What is the probability that none of the four events occurs?
