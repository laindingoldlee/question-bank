The sum is $r(1+2+\cdots+100)=5050r$. Since $5050=2\cdot5^2\cdot101$, the product $5050r$ is a perfect square exactly when $2\cdot101=202$ divides $r$ and $\dfrac{r}{202}$ is a perfect square. The smallest such $r$ is $202$ (giving $5050\cdot202=1010^2$), and its digit sum is $2+0+2=\boxed{4}$.
