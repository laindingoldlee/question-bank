For the largest number, choose the largest possible leading digits successively. A leading $9$ leaves product $40$; the next-largest possible digit dividing this is $8$, leaving $5$. The remaining digits are maximized as $5,1,1$, giving $M=98511$. For the smallest, a leading $1$ is possible, followed by $0$. With three places left and product $360$, the next digit cannot be below $5$, since two remaining digits have product at most $81$ and $\dfrac{360}{4}>81$. Choosing $5$ leaves $72$, whose two decimal factors are $8,9$, so $N=10589$. The difference is $87922$, whose digit-product is $8\cdot7\cdot9\cdot2\cdot2=\boxed{2016}$.
