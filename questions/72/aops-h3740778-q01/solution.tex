For every real $t$,
\[
t^4-t^3-t+1=(t-1)^2(t^2+t+1)\ge0,
\]
with equality only at $t=1$, since $t^2+t+1=(t+\dfrac{1}{2})^2+\dfrac{3}{4}>0$. Summing this inequality over the $2006$ variables, the two hypotheses make the total exactly zero. Every nonnegative summand must vanish. Thus the unique solution is $\boxed{x_1=\cdots=x_{2006}=1}$.
