Look for positive numbers $a$, $b$ with $\left(\sqrt a+\sqrt b\right)^2=a+b+2\sqrt{ab}=11+2\sqrt{18}$, i.e. $a+b=11$ and $ab=18$. These are the roots of $t^2-11t+18=0$, namely $9$ and $2$. Indeed
\[\left(3+\sqrt2\right)^2=9+6\sqrt2+2=11+6\sqrt2=11+2\sqrt{18},\]
and $3+\sqrt2>0$, so
\[\sqrt{11+2\sqrt{18}}=\boxed{3+\sqrt2}.\]
