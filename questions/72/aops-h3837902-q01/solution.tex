For distinct indices $i,j,k$, we have $z_i+z_j=-z_k$, so
\[
r^2=|z_i+z_j|^2=2r^2+2\operatorname{Re}(z_i\overline{z_j}).
\]
Thus $\operatorname{Re}(z_i\overline{z_j})=-\dfrac{r^2}{2}$, and
\[
|z_i-z_j|^2=2r^2-2\operatorname{Re}(z_i\overline{z_j})=3r^2.
\]
All three side lengths are $\sqrt3r>0$, proving a nondegenerate equilateral triangle.
