A room has the shape of a regular hexagon with side length $20$ m. There are $19$ students in the room (points inside or on the boundary of the hexagon), and no three of them lie on one straight line. Prove that some $4$ of the students are the vertices of a quadrilateral with area less than $100\sqrt3\ \text{m}^2$.
