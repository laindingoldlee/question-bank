The product is $(1\cdot2\cdot3\cdot4\cdot5)^{300}=120^{300}$. Since $120$ is divisible by $10$, $120^{300}$ is divisible by $10^{300}$, in particular by $1000$. The last three digits are $\boxed{000}$.
