\emph{Lateral surfaces:} $2\pi\cdot2\cdot2+2\pi\cdot3\cdot2+2\pi\cdot4\cdot2=36\pi$.

\emph{Upward-facing surfaces:} the top of $A$ ($\pi\cdot2^2$), the exposed ring on top of $B$ ($\pi(3^2-2^2)$) and the exposed ring on top of $C$ ($\pi(4^2-3^2)$). Together they make up exactly a disc of radius $4$, i.e. $16\pi$.

draw((-3,2)--(-3,4)); draw((3,2)--(3,4)); draw(shift(0,4)*xscale(3)*yscale(3*s)*unitcircle);
