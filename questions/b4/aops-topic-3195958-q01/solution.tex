The semiperimeter is $\dfrac{3s}{2}$, so Heron gives $K=\sqrt{\dfrac{3s}{2}(\dfrac{s}{2})^3}=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}s^2}$. The equilateral formula is exactly Heron specialized to equal sides; it saves repeated substitution.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair A=(0,0),B=(4,0),C=(2,2sqrt(3));draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(C--(2,0),dashed);label("$s$",(2,0),S);label("$s$",(1,sqrt(3)),NW);label("$s$",(3,sqrt(3)),NE);
\end{asy}
