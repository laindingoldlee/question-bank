Each friend was born on a Tuesday with probability $\dfrac{1}{7}$, independently (Amy's own birthday is irrelevant). Choosing which two of the three friends were born on a Tuesday, the probability is
\[\dbinom32\left(\dfrac{1}{7}\right)^2\cdot\dfrac{6}{7}=3\cdot\dfrac{1}{49}\cdot\dfrac{6}{7}=\boxed{\dfrac{18}{343}}.\]
