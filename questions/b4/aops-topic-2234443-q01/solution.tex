\textbf{$y$ is a multiple of $5$.} Modulo $5$, $3y^4\equiv0$, so $5\mid y$. Since $3y^4<2020$, we have $y^4<674<10^4$, so the positive multiple of $5$ must be $y=5$, with $3y^4=1875$.

\textbf{Then} $5x^3+10z=145$, i.e. $x^3+2z=29$. So $x^3$ is odd and less than $29$: $x=1$ or $x=3$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $x=1$: $2z=28$, $z=14$.
\item $x=3$: $2z=2$, $z=1$.
\end{itemize}
The solutions are $\boxed{(x,y,z)=(1,5,14)\text{ and }(3,5,1)}$. Check: $5+1875+140=2020$ and $135+1875+10=2020$.
