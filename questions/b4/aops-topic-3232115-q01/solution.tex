Divide by 4 to get $5x+6y=506$. Modulo 6, $-x\equiv2$, so $x\equiv4\pmod6$. Positivity gives $1\le x\le100$, hence $x=4,10,\ldots,100$, seventeen values. Each gives a positive integer $y=\dfrac{506-5x}{6}$. The answer is $\boxed{17}$.
