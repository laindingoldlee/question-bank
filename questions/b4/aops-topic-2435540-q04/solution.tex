Since $60^4=12{,}960{,}000$ and $70^4=24{,}010{,}000$, we have $60<n<70$. The last digit of $n^4$ is $1$, so $n$ is odd and not a multiple of $5$: $n\in\{61,63,67,69\}$. The digit sum of $22667121$ is $27$, so $9\mid n^4$, hence $3\mid n$ and $n\in\{63,69\}$. As $63^4=3969^2=15{,}752{,}961$ is too small, $n=69$. Indeed $69^2=4761$ and $4761^2=22{,}667{,}121$.

The sum of the digits of $n$ is $6+9=\boxed{15}$.
