Complete the squares:
\[3x^2+ax+3y^2-ay+a^2=3\left(x+\dfrac {a}{6}\right)^2+3\left(y-\dfrac {a}{6}\right)^2+a^2-\dfrac{a^2}{12}-\dfrac{a^2}{12}.\]
The squares are at least $0$ and both vanish at $x=-\dfrac {a}{6}$, $y=\dfrac {a}{6}$, so the minimum value is $\dfrac{5a^2}{6}$. The product of the first four primes is $2\cdot3\cdot5\cdot7=210$, so
\[\dfrac{5a^2}{6}=210,\qquad a^2=252,\qquad a=\boxed{\pm6\sqrt7}.\]
