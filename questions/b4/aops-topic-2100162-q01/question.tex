In triangle $ABC$ let $a=BC$, $b=CA$ and $c=AB$, and let $AM$, $BN$ and $CP$ be the angle bisectors, with $M$ on $BC$, $N$ on $CA$ and $P$ on $AB$. Writing $[XYZ]$ for the area of triangle $XYZ$, prove that
\[\dfrac{[MNP]}{[ABC]}=\dfrac{2abc}{(a+b)(b+c)(c+a)} .\]
