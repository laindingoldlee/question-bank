All cells of an $8\times8$ table are initially white. Ann and Bob play the following game. First Ann paints $n$ of the cells red. Then Bob chooses $4$ rows and $4$ columns of the table and paints all cells in them black. Ann wins if at least one red cell is left. Find the least value of $n$ for which Ann can win no matter how Bob plays.
