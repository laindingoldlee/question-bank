Subtracting the second equation from the first gives $2b-c=0$, so $c=2b$. Then the third equation becomes $2a+2b-2b=262$, so $a=131$. The first equation now gives $b+c=0$, i.e. $3b=0$, so $b=0$ and $c=0$. The solution is $\boxed{(a,b,c)=(131,0,0)}$, which satisfies all three equations.
