The characteristic equation is $r^2-2r+5=0$, with distinct roots $1+2i$ and $1-2i$. Each geometric sequence with one of these ratios satisfies the recurrence. The expression $\boxed{a_n=\dfrac{(4+i)(1+2i)^{n-1}+(4-i)(1-2i)^{n-1}}{4}}$ has initial values $2$ and $1$, as direct substitution shows, and is real because its two numerator terms are conjugates. It therefore satisfies the recurrence and the initial conditions. These determine the sequence uniquely, proving the formula.
