Let $A$ be a four-digit integer and $n$ a positive integer. When the first (leftmost) and the third digits of $A$ are each increased by $n$, and the second and the fourth digits are each decreased by $n$, all four results are again digits and the new four-digit number equals $nA$. Find the sum of the digits of $A$.
