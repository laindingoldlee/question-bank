Look for $\sqrt{55-10\sqrt{10}}=\sqrt m-\sqrt k$ with $m>k>0$. Squaring, $m+k-2\sqrt{mk}=55-2\sqrt{250}$, so it suffices that $m+k=55$ and $mk=250$. Then $m,k$ are the roots of $t^2-55t+250=0$, namely $50$ and $5$. Since $\sqrt{50}>\sqrt5$, the difference is positive and
\[
\sqrt{55-10\sqrt{10}}=\sqrt{50}-\sqrt5=\boxed{5\sqrt2-\sqrt5}.
\]
Check: $(5\sqrt2-\sqrt5)^2=50+5-10\sqrt{10}$. (The poster's attempts with $(-5,1)$ and $(-1,5)$ fail because $m$ and $k$ need sum $55$ and product $250$.)
