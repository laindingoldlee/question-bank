A $99$-year-old man has been fed every day since birth from a vat that initially held $1000$ litres of potion. On the day he was born he drank $\dfrac12$ of the vat's contents, and on each day $n>1$ he drank $\dfrac1{n+1}$ of the amount he drank on day $n-1$. To the nearest integer, how many litres will he have drunk by his $100$th birthday? (No calculator.)
