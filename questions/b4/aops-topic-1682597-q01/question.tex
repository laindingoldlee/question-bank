Twenty-five people of different heights stand in a $5\times5$ grid of squares, one person in each square. In each row, take the shortest person; let Ana be the tallest of these five people. In each column, take the tallest person; let Bev be the shortest of these five people. Assuming Ana and Bev are not the same person, who is taller, Ana or Bev? Prove that your answer is always correct.
