Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle with $AB>AC$. Let $D$ be the point on side $AB$ such that $\angle ACD=\angle CBD$. Let $E$ be the midpoint of $BD$ and let $S$ be the circumcentre of triangle $BCD$. Prove that $A$, $E$, $S$, $C$ lie on a circle.
