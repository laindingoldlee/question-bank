A 64-question quiz has four choices per question and one correct answer each. After every attempt, each response is marked correct or incorrect. A student remembers the feedback and may change answers on later attempts. What minimum number of attempts guarantees a perfect submitted quiz, and what is $k$ if that number is $2^{2^k}$?
