Test a new choice on each still-incorrect question and keep known correct choices. After at most four attempts all questions are correct. Fewer cannot guarantee a perfect submission: on even one question, the first three distinct guesses can all be wrong. Hence the guaranteed minimum is 4, and $4=2^{2^1}$ gives $\boxed{k=1}$.
