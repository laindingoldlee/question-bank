Since $x\ne y$, the numbers $x$ and $y$ are the two roots of $t^2-t-1=0$, so by Vieta's formulas $x+y=1$ and $xy=-1$. Then
\[
x^3+y^3=(x+y)^3-3xy(x+y)=1-3(-1)(1)=\boxed{4}.
\]
(Alternatively, $x^3=x\cdot x^2=x^2+x=2x+1$ and likewise $y^3=2y+1$, so $x^3+y^3=2(x+y)+2=4$.)
