Take $l:y=0$, $A=(0,7)$, $B=(3,3)$; the horizontal separation is three by Pythagoras. A circle tangent to $l$ at $(s,0)$ has center $(s,r)$. Its passages through $A,B$ give $s^2+49=14r$ and $(3-s)^2+9=6r$. Eliminating $r$ yields $4s^2-42s-21=0$. The distance between the roots is $\dfrac{\sqrt{2100}}{4}=\boxed{\dfrac{5\sqrt{21}}{2}}$. Both radii are positive, so both circles exist.

\end{asy}
