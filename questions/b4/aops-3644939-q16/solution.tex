Put the square at $[0,2]^2$. The circle centre is $(r,r)$, and passing through $(2,2)$ gives $2(2-r)^2=r^2$. Tangency to the side segments selects $r=4-2\sqrt2$. The parts outside the square are two congruent disjoint circular caps. The distance from the centre to either far side is $d=2-r=2\sqrt2-2$, with $\dfrac{d}{r}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}$. Each cap has area $\pi \dfrac{r^2}{4}-d^2$. Thus overlap is $\pi \dfrac{r^2}{2}+2d^2=(12-8\sqrt2)\pi+24-16\sqrt2$. The requested signed coefficient sum is $12-8+2+24-16+2=\boxed{16}$.
