Square $ABCD$ has side length $2$. A circle is drawn such that it passes through $C$ and is tangent to sides $\overline{AB}$ and $\overline{AD}$. The area of the overlapping region covered by both the circle and square can be written as $(a+b\sqrt{c})\pi +d+e\sqrt{f}$, where $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$, $e$ and $f$ are all integers, and $c$ and $f$ are square-free (they are not divisible by any perfect square greater than $1$). Find $a+b+c+d+e+f$.
