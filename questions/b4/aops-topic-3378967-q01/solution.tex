If the first term is $a$ and length is $k$, then $63=\dfrac{k(2a+k-1)}{2}$, so $k\mid126$ and $a=\dfrac{\dfrac{126}{k}-k+1}{2}$. Also $\dfrac{k(k+1)}{2}\le63$, so $k\le10$. The divisors of $126$ in this range are $1,2,3,6,7,9$. Each gives a positive integer first term, respectively $63,31,20,8,6,3$. Thus there are $\boxed6$ sums.
