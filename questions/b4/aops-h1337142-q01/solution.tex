Write $x=AB,y=BC,z=CA$. Bringing the right side to the left and completing squares gives
\[
\dfrac{2}{29}(29x-28y-12z)^2+\dfrac{3}{29}(3y-7z)^2=0.
\]
Both terms must vanish. Thus $y=\dfrac{7}{3}z$ and $x=\dfrac{8}{3}z$, so $AB:BC:CA=8:7:3$. These are valid triangle side ratios. The cosine rule gives
\[
\cos A=\dfrac{8^2+3^2-7^2}{2\cdot8\cdot3}=\dfrac{1}{2}.
\]
Therefore $\boxed{\angle BAC=60^\circ}$.

\end{asy}
