Let $a$, $b$, $c$ be nonzero integers such that both
\[
\dfrac{a}{b}+\dfrac{b}{c}+\dfrac{c}{a}\qquad\text{and}\qquad\dfrac{a}{c}+\dfrac{c}{b}+\dfrac{b}{a}
\]
are integers. Prove that $|a|=|b|=|c|$.
