Player 1 has $7$ pennies and Player 2 has $9$ pennies. In each round a fair coin decides the winner of the round (each player wins with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$), and the loser gives one penny to the winner. The game ends when one player has all $16$ pennies. What is the probability that Player 2 wins?
