A disc of radius $1$ cm is placed on a large rectangular grid with cells $5$ cm by $3$ cm. Its center is uniform modulo one cell, and boundary events have probability zero.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Show that the probability the entire disc lies in one cell is $\dfrac{1}{5}$.
\item Fifteen students each make $20$ throws. Their prize counts satisfy $\sum x=61$, $\sum x^2=295$. Find the population standard deviation of these counts.
\item Twelve more students each make $20$ throws. Their prize counts have mean $3.5$ and population standard deviation $2$. Find the combined mean and population standard deviation.
\item The estimated probability that a disc covers a grid corner is $0.2216$. Explain how to use this to estimate $\pi$, and give the estimate.
\end{enumerate}
