Three litres of water are poured at random into $5$ buckets, so that the amounts $(x_1,\dots,x_5)$ with $x_i\ge0$ and $x_1+\cdots+x_5=3$ are uniformly distributed over all such $5$-tuples of real numbers. What is the probability that no bucket contains more than $1$ litre?
