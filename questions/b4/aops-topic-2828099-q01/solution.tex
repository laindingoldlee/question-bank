Scale by $\dfrac13$: the shares $y_i=\dfrac{x_i}3$ are uniform on the simplex $y_i\ge0$, $\sum y_i=1$, and we need every $y_i\le\dfrac13$.

For a uniform point of this $4$-dimensional simplex, the event $y_{i_1},\dots,y_{i_k}>t$ (with $kt<1$) has probability $(1-kt)^4$, because subtracting $t$ from those coordinates maps the event onto a copy of the simplex scaled by $1-kt$. By inclusion-exclusion with $t=\dfrac13$,
\[P=\sum_{k=0}^{2}(-1)^k\binom5k\left(1-\dfrac k3\right)^4=1-5\cdot\dfrac{16}{81}+10\cdot\dfrac1{81}=\dfrac{81-80+10}{81}=\boxed{\dfrac{11}{81}}.\]
(Terms with $k\ge3$ vanish, since three buckets cannot each hold more than $1$ litre.)
