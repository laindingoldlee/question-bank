If $N=ab$ with $1<a\le b$, then $a\le\sqrt N$, so a composite number has a prime divisor at most its square root. Here $11<\sqrt{131}<12$. None of $2,3,5,7,11$ divides $131$ (remainders $1,2,1,5,10$), so $\boxed{131\text{ is prime}}$. The upper bound is inclusive: primes whose square equals $N$ must also be tested.
