From either nonrainy state, the next day is nonrainy with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{4}=\dfrac{3}{4}$. Conditional on Saturday being nonrainy, Sunday again has probability $\dfrac{3}{4}$ of no rain. Thus the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac{3}{4}\cdot\dfrac{3}{4}=\dfrac{9}{16}}$. The relative probabilities of sunny and cloudy on Friday do not matter.
