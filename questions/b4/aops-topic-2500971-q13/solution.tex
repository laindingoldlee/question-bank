The volume is preserved. The sphere has volume
\[\dfrac43\pi\cdot28^3,\]
so the edge $s$ of the cube satisfies $s^3=\dfrac43\pi\cdot28^3$, and
\[s=28\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{4\pi}{3}}=\boxed{\dfrac{28}{3}\sqrt[3]{36\pi}\text{ cm}}\approx45.1\text{ cm}.\]
(We used $\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{4\pi}{3}}=\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{36\pi}{27}}=\dfrac13\sqrt[3]{36\pi}$.)

Because of the factor $\pi$, the edge cannot be written as $m\sqrt[3]{n}$ with integers $m$ and $n$.
