Let $T$ be the stopping time. At most six flips are needed. For $k=0,1,2$, $P(T>k)=1$. For $k=3,4,5$, survival means fewer than three tails and fewer than four heads, giving probabilities $\dfrac{7}{8}$, $\dfrac{10}{16}$, and $\dfrac{10}{32}$ respectively. The tail-sum formula gives $E(T)=3+\dfrac{7}{8}+\dfrac{10}{16}+\dfrac{10}{32}=\boxed{\dfrac{77}{16}}$.
