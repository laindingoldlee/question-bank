Let $x=[BCF]$, and split $AEFD$ by $AF$ into $u=[AEF]$ and $v=[AFD]$, so $u+v=\dfrac{204}{13}$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair B=(0,0),C=(10,0),F=(4,3);
pair Ep=F+0.8*(F-C),D=F+0.6*(F-B);
pair A=extension(B,Ep,C,D);
fill(A--Ep--F--D--cycle,paleblue);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(1));
draw(B--D,linewidth(0.9));draw(C--Ep,linewidth(0.9));
draw(A--F,dashed);
dot(F);
label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,NE);label("$E$",Ep,W);label("$F$",F,S);
label("$4$",(B+Ep+F)/3);label("$x$",(B+C+F)/3);label("$3$",(C+D+F)/3);
label("$u$",(A+Ep+F)/3,blue);label("$v$",(A+F+D)/3,blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Triangles with the same apex and bases on one line have areas in the ratio of the bases.
\begin{itemize}
\item On line $EC$, with apex $B$ and with apex $A$: $\dfrac{EF}{FC}=\dfrac{[BEF]}{[BCF]}=\dfrac{4}{x}$ and $\dfrac{EF}{FC}=\dfrac{[AEF]}{[ACF]}=\dfrac{u}{v+3}$.
\item On line $BD$, with apex $C$ and with apex $A$: $\dfrac{DF}{FB}=\dfrac{[CDF]}{[CBF]}=\dfrac{3}{x}$ and $\dfrac{DF}{FB}=\dfrac{[ADF]}{[ABF]}=\dfrac{v}{u+4}$.
\end{itemize}
Hence $ux=4v+12$ and $vx=3u+12$. Solving this linear system for $u$ and $v$,
\[
u=\dfrac{12x+48}{x^2-12},\qquad v=\dfrac{12x+36}{x^2-12},\qquad u+v=\dfrac{24x+84}{x^2-12}.
\]
Setting $u+v=\dfrac{204}{13}$:
\[
13(24x+84)=204\left(x^2-12\right)\iff 204x^2-312x-3540=0\iff 17x^2-26x-295=0 .
\]
The discriminant is $26^2+4\cdot17\cdot295=20736=144^2$, so $x=\dfrac{26\pm144}{34}$, and the positive root is $x=\dfrac{170}{34}=\boxed{5}$.

(Check: $u=\dfrac{108}{13}$, $v=\dfrac{96}{13}$, $u+v=\dfrac{204}{13}$; and $\dfrac{u}{v+3}=\dfrac{108}{135}=\dfrac{4}{5}$, $\dfrac{v}{u+4}=\dfrac{96}{160}=\dfrac{3}{5}$.)
