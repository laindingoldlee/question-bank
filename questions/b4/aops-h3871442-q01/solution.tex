A product is odd only when both factors are odd, so the three odd numbers must occupy nonadjacent positions. Arrange the four even numbers in $4!$ ways. They create five gaps, including both ends. Choose three gaps for the odd numbers in $\binom53$ ways and order those numbers in $3!$ ways. The total is
\[
\boxed{4!\binom53 3!=1440}.
\]
