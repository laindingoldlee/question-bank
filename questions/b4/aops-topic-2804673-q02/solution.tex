A repunit with at least two digits is $\equiv11\equiv3\pmod4$ (its last two digits are $11$), while squares are $\equiv0$ or $1\pmod4$, so $n^2+m^2\equiv0,1,2\pmod4$. And $1$ itself is not a sum of two positive squares. So $\boxed{\text{no}}$.
