Heron gives area 84 and circumradius $R=13\cdot14\cdot\dfrac{15}{(4\cdot84)}=\dfrac{65}{8}$. The perpendicular centre-to-chord distances for sides 13,14,15 are $\dfrac{39}{8},\dfrac{33}{8},\dfrac{25}{8}$. A circle in the larger disk segment, tangent to its chord, has radius at most $\dfrac{(R+d)}{2}$: along the chord normal the available width is $R+d$, and equality occurs with its centre on that normal. Thus the three maximal radii are $\dfrac{13}{2},\dfrac{49}{8},\dfrac{45}{8}$. No mutual non-overlap condition is imposed, so these maxima may be chosen independently. The product of areas is $\pi^3(\dfrac{28665}{128})^2=\dfrac{821682225\pi^3}{16384}$, giving $m+n=\boxed{821698609}$.
