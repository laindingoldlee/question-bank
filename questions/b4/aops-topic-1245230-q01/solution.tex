$A$ improves $\dfrac{42}{7}=6$ ranks per day and $B$ improves $\dfrac{77}{7}=11$ per day, so $B$ gains $5$ ranks per day on $A$. $B$ is $41-28=13$ ranks behind, so they are level after $\dfrac{13}{5}=2.6$ days, and $B$ is ahead from then on: after $\boxed{2.6}$ days, i.e. during the third day. (At that time $A$ is at rank $28-15.6=12.4$, so neither has reached rank $1$.)
