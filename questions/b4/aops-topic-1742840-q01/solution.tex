If there were four different colours, one pencil of each would form a group of four with no two the same colour; so there are at most three colours. If some colour had four or more pencils, four of them plus any fifth pencil would be a group of five with at least four of the same colour; so each colour has at most three pencils. With nine pencils, at most three colours and at most three per colour, there are exactly three colours with three pencils each. Blue is one of them, so there are $\boxed{3}$ blue pencils.
