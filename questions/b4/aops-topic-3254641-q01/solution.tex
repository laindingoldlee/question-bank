The hundreds digit may be any of $1,\ldots,9$: its doubled result can occupy one or two leading places correctly. Each lower digit must be at most four, so that its doubled value occupies exactly one place and produces no carry. These conditions suffice, giving $9\cdot5\cdot5=225$ numbers.
To see necessity, concatenating a two-digit doubled lower digit shifts every preceding positive prefix an extra decimal place. Compared with the usual weighted sum of doubled digits, this strictly increases the result, and all other contributions are nonnegative; it cannot be cancelled. Hence $\boxed{225}$.
