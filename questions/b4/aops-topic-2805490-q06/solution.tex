Let the side be $s$, with the south gate $(0,0)$, north gate $(0,s)$, outpost $(0,-42)$, and observer $(315,s+42)$. Collinearity with corner $(\dfrac{s}{2},0)$ gives $\dfrac{42}{\dfrac{s}{2}}=\dfrac{s+84}{315}$. Thus $s(s+84)=26460$, or $(s-126)(s+210)=0$. The positive solution is $\boxed{126\text{ metres}}$.
