Each of the corner triangles $AQR,BRP,CPQ$ has area $\dfrac{mn}{(m+n)^2}$ times the whole: its two sides along sides of $ABC$ have scale factors $\dfrac{m}{m+n}$ and $\dfrac{n}{m+n}$. Subtracting these three areas leaves $\dfrac{[PQR]}{[ABC]}=1-\dfrac{3mn}{(m+n)^2}=\dfrac{m^2-mn+n^2}{(m+n)^2}=\boxed{\dfrac{m^3+n^3}{(m+n)^3}}$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(1,3),B=(0,0),C=(5,0),P=(B+2C)/3,Q=(C+2A)/3,R=(A+2B)/3;draw(A--B--C--cycle);filldraw(P--Q--R--cycle,lightblue,blue);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,SW);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$P$",P,S);label("$Q$",Q,NE);label("$R$",R,W);
\end{asy}
