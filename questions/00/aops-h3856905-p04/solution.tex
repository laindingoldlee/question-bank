Triangles $ABC$ and $CDF$ are each similar to the large triangle. Let $t=\dfrac{AC}{AF}$; their linear scales are $t$ and $1-t$. Thus $\left(\dfrac{1-t}{t}\right)^2=2025$, giving $t=\dfrac{1}{46}$. If $[ABC]=1$, the large triangle has area $46^2=2116$ and $CDF$ has area 2025. The remainder is $2116-2025-1=90$, so the ratio is $\boxed{90:1}$. The sketch is schematic.
