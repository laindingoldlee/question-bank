Both numbers are even, so $a$ and $b$ are even: $a,b\in\{2,4,6,8\}$. Both are multiples of $3$: $2a+b\equiv0$ and $a+2b\equiv0\pmod3$; subtracting, $a\equiv b\pmod3$, and then $2a+b\equiv3a\equiv0$ holds automatically. Among $2,4,6,8$ the residues mod $3$ are $2,1,0,2$, so the pairs are $(2,2),(2,8),(8,2),(8,8),(4,4),(6,6)$, with $a+b\in\{4,10,16,8,12\}$. That is $\boxed{5}$ values.
