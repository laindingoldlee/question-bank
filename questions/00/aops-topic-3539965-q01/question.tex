Arrange the digits 1,2,3,4,5,6 once each. Read the first position as a one-digit block, positions 2–3 as a two-digit block, and positions 4–6 as a three-digit block. Require the blocks to be divisible by 1,2,3 respectively. How many arrangements are possible?
