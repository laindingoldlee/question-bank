Each residue class mod $3$ has $4$ numbers. The sum is $\equiv0$ iff the three residues are equal ($3\binom43=12$ ways) or all different ($4^3=64$ ways). The probability is $\dfrac{76}{\binom{12}3}=\dfrac{76}{220}=\boxed{\dfrac{19}{55}}$.
