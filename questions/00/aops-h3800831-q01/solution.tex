The sum is divisible by nine because its digit contributions are congruent to $1+\cdots+9=45$ modulo nine. The smallest possible total is $100(1+2+3)+10(4+5+6)+(7+8+9)=774$; the largest is $100(7+8+9)+10(4+5+6)+(1+2+3)=2556$. The only multiple of nine ending in $65$ between these bounds is $1665$ (successive possibilities differ by $900$). It is attainable, for example $147+582+936=1665$. Thus $\boxed{1665}$.
