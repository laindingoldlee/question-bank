Let $n$ be a positive integer, and let $A_1,A_2,\dots,A_n$ be points on a circle. For $1\le i<j\le n$ draw the segment $A_iA_j$, and let $S$ be the set of all these segments. What is the maximum possible number of points, other than the $A_i$ themselves, at which two segments of $S$ intersect?
