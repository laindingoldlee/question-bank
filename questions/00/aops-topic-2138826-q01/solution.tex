As a quadratic in $x^4$,
\[x^8-12x^4-64=\left(x^4-16\right)\left(x^4+4\right).\]
\textbf{First factor.} $x^4-16=\left(x^2-4\right)\left(x^2+4\right)=(x-2)(x+2)\left(x^2+4\right)$.

\textbf{Second factor (Sophie Germain's identity).} Completing the square,
\[x^4+4=\left(x^4+4x^2+4\right)-4x^2=\left(x^2+2\right)^2-(2x)^2=\left(x^2+2x+2\right)\left(x^2-2x+2\right).\]
Hence
\[x^8-12x^4-64=\boxed{(x-2)(x+2)\left(x^2+4\right)\left(x^2+2x+2\right)\left(x^2-2x+2\right)}.\]
The three quadratics have negative discriminants ($-16$, $-4$, $-4$), so they have no real roots and cannot be factored further over the integers. (The common mistake is to stop at $x^4+4$, which looks irreducible but is not.)
