Write $n-86=a^2$, $n+86=b^2$ with $0\le a<b$. Then $(b-a)(b+a)=172$. The factors must have the same parity. Of the positive factor pairs $(1,172),(2,86),(4,43)$, only $(2,86)$ qualifies. It gives $b=44,a=42$, and $n=42^2+86=\boxed{1850}$.
