Rewrite as $4x^2-3x-225=0$. By the quadratic formula,
\[x=\dfrac{3\pm\sqrt{9+4\cdot4\cdot225}}{8}=\dfrac{3\pm\sqrt{3609}}{8}=\boxed{\dfrac{3\pm3\sqrt{401}}{8}},\]
since $3609=9\cdot401$.
