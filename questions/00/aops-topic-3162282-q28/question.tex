A unit circle centered at $ O $ is internally tangent at $ T $ to a circle of radius 2. Point $ R $ lies on the unit circle, and its projection $ N $ to $ OT $ has $ O\in NT $ and $ RN:NO=4:3 $. The second intersection of line $ TR $ with the larger circle is $ S $. The tangents from $ S $ to the unit circle touch at $ P,Q $. The tangent at $ R $ meets $ PQ $ at $ Z $. Find the area of $ ZRS $.
