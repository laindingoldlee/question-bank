If any of the integers were even, the product would be even. So all three are odd. The sum of two odd numbers is even, and adding a third odd number makes it odd. So the sum must be odd: $\boxed{\text{(A)}}$.

(It need not be positive or negative: $1+1+1=3$ and $(-1)+(-1)+(-1)=-3$ both have odd products.)
