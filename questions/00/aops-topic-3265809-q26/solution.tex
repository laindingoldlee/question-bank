Since values are integers, $|f(k)-k|\le1$. Such a permutation consists only of fixed points and disjoint swaps of adjacent integers. To prove this, consider the smallest remaining index: it either maps to itself, or maps to the next index, which must map back since no later index can reach the smallest one. Remove that singleton or pair and continue.
Let $a_n$ count permutations of $n$ indices. This gives $a_n=a_{n-1}+a_{n-2}$ with $a_0=a_1=1$. Hence $a_2=2,a_3=3,a_4=5,a_5=\boxed8$.
