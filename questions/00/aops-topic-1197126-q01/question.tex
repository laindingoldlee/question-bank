Magda claims that an isosceles trapezoid must be a parallelogram, because an isosceles trapezoid has a pair of congruent opposite sides. Using the definition of a trapezoid as a quadrilateral with exactly one pair of parallel sides, explain why Magda's argument is wrong and show that no isosceles trapezoid is a parallelogram.
