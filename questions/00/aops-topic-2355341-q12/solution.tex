There are $5!=120$ arrangements in all. In those where Eddie sits next to Thomas, treat the two as a block: the block and the other $3$ people can be arranged in $4!=24$ ways, and the block in $2$ orders, giving $48$. So the number of arrangements with Eddie not next to Thomas is $120-48=\boxed{72}$.
