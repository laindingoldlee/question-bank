The four ace positions are a uniform four-element subset of the 52 card positions. All must lie among this player's 13 positions, so the probability is $\dfrac{\binom{13}{4}}{\binom{52}{4}}=\dfrac{715}{270725}=\dfrac{11}{4165}$. Hence $\boxed{a=11}$.
