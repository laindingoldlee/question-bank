Note $(1+\sqrt3)^3=1+3\sqrt3+9+3\sqrt3=10+6\sqrt3$ and $(\sqrt3-1)^3=3\sqrt3-9+3\sqrt3-1=-10+6\sqrt3$. So the sum is $(1+\sqrt3)+(\sqrt3-1)=\boxed{2\sqrt3}$.

(Without guessing: if $s$ is the sum, then $s^3=12\sqrt3+3\sqrt[3]{(6\sqrt3)^2-100}\,s=12\sqrt3+3\sqrt[3]{8}\,s=12\sqrt3+6s$, and $s=2\sqrt3$ is the only real root of $s^3-6s-12\sqrt3=0$.)
