Let \(r_1,r_2,r_3,r_4\) be the roots of \(x^4-3x^3+2x^2-5x+7=0\). Let \(e_1=\sum r_i\), \(e_2=\sum_{i<j}r_ir_j\) and \(e_4=r_1r_2r_3r_4\). Compute \(\dfrac{e_1}{e_4}\cdot e_2\).
