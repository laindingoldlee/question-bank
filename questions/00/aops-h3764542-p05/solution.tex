Every row of a 4 by 5 rectangular patch has either two or three black squares. Consecutive rows are complementary, so each pair contains five black squares. The four rows contain $2\cdot5=\boxed{10}$ black squares.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
for(int x=0;x<5;++x)for(int y=0;y<4;++y)filldraw(box((x,y),(x+1,y+1)),(x+y)%2==0?gray:white);
\end{asy}
