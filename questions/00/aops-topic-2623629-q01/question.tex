In triangle $XYZ$, $\angle X=60^\circ$ and $\angle Y=45^\circ$. A circle with centre $P$ meets side $XY$ at points $A$ and $B$, side $YZ$ at points $C$ and $D$, and side $ZX$ at points $E$ and $F$. Suppose $AB=CD=EF$. Prove that $P$ is the incentre of triangle $XYZ$.
