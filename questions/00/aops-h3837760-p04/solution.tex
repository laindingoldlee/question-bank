The interior angle at the tether corner is $108^\circ$, leaving a $252^\circ$ sector of radius 25. Wrapping around either adjacent vertex uses ten feet and creates an additional $72^\circ$ sector of radius 15. Wrapping around the next vertex in either direction leaves five feet and adds another $72^\circ$ sector. These final two sectors meet only at the midpoint of the opposite side, since their centers are ten feet apart. No other overlaps have positive area. Thus the area is $\dfrac{252}{360}\pi25^2+2\dfrac{72}{360}\pi15^2+2\dfrac{72}{360}\pi5^2=\boxed{\dfrac{1075\pi}{2}}$.
