A multiple-choice test has $10$ questions: $5$ are worth $3$ points each, $3$ are worth $4$ points each and $2$ are worth $5$ points each, and a wrong answer scores $0$. Every question has the same number $n\ge2$ of answer choices, exactly one of which is correct. A student guesses every answer uniformly at random, independently of the other questions, and passes with a score of at least $22$ points. What is the greatest $n$ for which the probability of passing is at least $\dfrac15$?
