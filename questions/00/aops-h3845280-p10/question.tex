Let $\ell$ be a positive integer. Twenty cards are labeled with ten $1$'s, nine $3$'s, and one $\ell$. Let $M$ be the largest possible sum of the ten pair-products when the cards are partitioned into pairs. Separately, $\ell$ parallel lines and four additional distinct lines are drawn so their number of distinct intersections is as large as possible. If this maximum is also $M$, determine $\ell$ and $M$.
