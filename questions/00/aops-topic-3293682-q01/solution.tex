Each new square has half the preceding square's area. The part of a square left outside its inscribed circle is $1-\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ times the square area. These shaded regions have disjoint interiors, and their total is $(1-\dfrac{\pi}{4})\sum_{k=0}^\infty2^{-k}=\boxed{2-\dfrac{\pi}{2}}$. The nested limiting set has zero area, so its colouring does not affect the answer.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
fill((-1,-1)--(1,-1)--(1,1)--(-1,1)--cycle,lightgray);fill(circle((0,0),1),white);fill((0,-1)--(1,0)--(0,1)--(-1,0)--cycle,lightgray);fill(circle((0,0),1/sqrt(2)),white);draw((-1,-1)--(1,-1)--(1,1)--(-1,1)--cycle);draw(circle((0,0),1));draw((0,-1)--(1,0)--(0,1)--(-1,0)--cycle);draw(circle((0,0),1/sqrt(2)));label("first two stages",(0,1.4));
\end{asy}
