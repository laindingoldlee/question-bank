There are $\binom{150}{2}=11175$ equally likely pairs, and $\binom{50}{2}=1225$ pairs inside each of the three genres. The probability is
\[
\dfrac{3\cdot1225}{11175}=\boxed{\dfrac{49}{149}} .
\]
(Equivalently: whatever the first book is, $49$ of the remaining $149$ match it.)
