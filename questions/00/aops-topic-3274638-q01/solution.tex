Choose the full pair in $15$ ways. Select two different other pairs in $\binom{14}{2}$ ways, then one shoe from each in $2^2$ ways. No favourable selection is counted twice because its full pair is unique. Dividing by all $\binom{30}{4}$ selections gives $\boxed{\dfrac{15\binom{14}{2}4}{\binom{30}{4}}=\dfrac{52}{261}}$.
