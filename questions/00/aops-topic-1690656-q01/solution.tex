Factor $9000=2^3\cdot3^2\cdot5^3$. Each of $a,b,c$ is a divisor of $9000$, so write $a=2^{x_1}3^{y_1}5^{z_1}$, $b=2^{x_2}3^{y_2}5^{z_2}$, $c=2^{x_3}3^{y_3}5^{z_3}$ with nonnegative exponents. Then $abc=9000$ exactly when
\[
x_1+x_2+x_3=3,\qquad y_1+y_2+y_3=2,\qquad z_1+z_2+z_3=3,
\]
and the three equations can be solved independently. By stars and bars, $x_1+x_2+x_3=n$ has $\dbinom{n+2}{2}$ solutions in nonnegative integers. Hence the number of triples is
\[
\dbinom{5}{2}\dbinom{4}{2}\dbinom{5}{2}=10\cdot6\cdot10=\boxed{600}.
\]
(If negative integers were allowed, exactly zero or two of $a,b,c$ could be negative, multiplying the count by $4$.)
