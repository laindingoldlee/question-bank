Three minor great-circle sides of length $5\pi$ on a radius-ten sphere have central angles $90^\circ$. Their endpoints are three pairwise perpendicular radius directions, so the smaller spherical triangle is an octant. Its area is $\dfrac{1}{8}\cdot4\pi10^2=\boxed{50\pi}$. The complementary region has area $\boxed{350\pi}$.
