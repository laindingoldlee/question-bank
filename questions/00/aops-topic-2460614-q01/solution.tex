By definition, $\sqrt a$ (for $a\ge0$) denotes the \emph{nonnegative} number whose square is $a$. So $\sqrt{36}=6$ only; $-6$ is also a square root of $36$ in the wider sense, but it is written $-\sqrt{36}$. The equation $x^2=36$ has the two solutions $x=\pm\sqrt{36}=\boxed{\pm6}$.
