Trauma and Coma each start with $1000$ coins and take turns flipping a fair coin, Trauma first. If the flip is tails, the flipper takes all of the other player's coins and the game ends. If it is heads, the flipper takes half of the other player's coins (coins may be split into fractions) and play continues. What is the expected number of coins Trauma has when the game ends, and what is Trauma's expected gain?
