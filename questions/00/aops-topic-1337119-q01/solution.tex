The game ends at the first tails, and the player who flips it ends with all $2000$ coins. The heads flips only move coins around before that; they do not affect who ends up with everything. The game ends with probability $1$, since the chance of no tails in $k$ flips is $\dfrac{1}{2^k}\to0$.

Trauma flips on turns $1,3,5,\dots$, so the first tails is Trauma's with probability
\[
\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{8}+\dfrac{1}{32}+\cdots=\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{2}}{1-\dfrac{1}{4}}=\dfrac{2}{3} .
\]
So Trauma ends with $2000$ coins with probability $\dfrac{2}{3}$ and with $0$ otherwise. The expected final holding is $\dfrac{2}{3}\cdot2000=\boxed{\dfrac{4000}{3}}$ coins, and the expected gain is $\dfrac{4000}{3}-1000=\boxed{\dfrac{1000}{3}}$ coins (Coma's expected gain is $-\dfrac{1000}{3}$).
