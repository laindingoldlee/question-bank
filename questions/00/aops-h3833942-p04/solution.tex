When $k$ colors remain, there are $4k$ cards. A successful turn chooses one of the $k$ monochromatic four-card sets, among $\binom{4k}{4}$ possible sets. Its probability is $\dfrac{k}{\binom{4k}{4}}$ and the mean waiting time is $\dfrac{\binom{4k}{4}}{k}$. Summing for $k=4,3,2,1$ gives $\dfrac{1820}{4}+\dfrac{495}{3}+\dfrac{70}{2}+1=455+165+35+1=\boxed{656}$.
