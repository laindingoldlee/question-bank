Vieta gives $a+b+c=4$, $ab+bc+ca=2$, and $abc=7$. The common denominator is
\[
(1+ab)(1+bc)(1+ca)
=1+(ab+bc+ca)+abc(a+b+c)+(abc)^2=80,
\]
so all three original denominators are nonzero. The numerator is
\[
\sum_{\mathrm{cyc}}a(1+ab)(1+ac)
=(a+b+c)+\sum_{\mathrm{sym}}a^2b+abc(a^2+b^2+c^2).
\]
Now $\sum_{\mathrm{sym}}a^2b=4\cdot2-3\cdot7=-13$ and $a^2+b^2+c^2=16-4=12$. Hence the numerator is $4-13+7\cdot12=75$, giving
\[
\boxed{\dfrac{75}{80}=\dfrac{15}{16}}.
\]
