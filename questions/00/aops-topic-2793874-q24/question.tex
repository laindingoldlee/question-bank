An octopus starts with 8 legs. Each successful hit removes one leg and regrows three. After each successful hit, independently with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, it counterattacks, permanently halving the attacker's hit probability. Initial accuracy is 1. What is the probability the octopus has 14 legs after three attempted hits?
