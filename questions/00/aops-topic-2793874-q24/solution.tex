Fourteen legs requires all three attacks to hit. The first certainly hits. Let $C_1,C_2$ indicate counterattacks after the first and second hits. The conditional product of hit probabilities for the second and third attacks is $2^{-C_1}2^{-(C_1+C_2)}=4^{-C_1}2^{-C_2}$. Averaging the independent fair indicators gives $\dfrac{1+\dfrac{1}{4}}{2}\cdot\dfrac{1+\dfrac{1}{2}}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{15}{32}}$.
