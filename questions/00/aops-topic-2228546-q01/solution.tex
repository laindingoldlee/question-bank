Extending the positive nested radical increases its value, so $r_N$ is increasing. For a truncation define the tail $T_k=\sqrt{k+T_{k+1}}$, with $T_N=\sqrt N$. Backward induction gives $T_k\le k+1$: if $T_{k+1}\le k+2$, then $T_k\le\sqrt{2k+2}\le k+1$ for $k\ge1$. For $N\ge3$, this gives $T_3\le4$, then $T_2\le\sqrt6$ and $r_N\le\sqrt{1+\sqrt6}<2$. Smaller truncations also satisfy this bound. Hence the increasing sequence is bounded and converges. Its limit is at least $r_2=\sqrt{1+\sqrt2}>1$ and at most the displayed bound below $2$. The floor is therefore $\boxed1$.
