You reach two doors, door $1$ and door $2$. Exactly one of them leads out; the other leads to doom. Two trolls guard the doors, and both know which door leads out. One troll always lies and the other always tells the truth, but you do not know which is which. You may ask one question to one of the trolls, and then you must choose a door. What question guarantees that you choose the door that leads out? Prove that it works.
