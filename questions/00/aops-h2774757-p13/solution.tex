Write $a=BC,b=CA,c=AB$ and use $A,B,C$ for the angles. Right-triangle geometry gives $DE=c\cos C$ and $DF=b\cos B$. The angle between lines $EF$ and $BC$ is $|B-C|$, hence $PQ=a\cos(B-C)$ (positive for an acute triangle). By the sine rule,
\[
DE+DF=2R(\sin C\cos C+\sin B\cos B)
=2R\sin(B+C)\cos(B-C)=a\cos(B-C)=2018.
\]
Combining this with $DE-DF=4$ yields $\boxed{DE=1011}$.
