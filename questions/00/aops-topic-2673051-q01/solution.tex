The total is $150$. The other $10$ integers are at least $6$, so they sum to at least $60$, and the $20$ integers sum to at most $90$: their average is at most $\dfrac{90}{20}=4.5$. This is attained by ten $5$s, ten $4$s and ten $6$s. The answer is $\boxed{4.5}$.
