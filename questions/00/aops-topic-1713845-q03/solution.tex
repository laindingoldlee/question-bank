Write $y=kx$ and $z=\dfrac{c}{y}$. From $x=3$, $y=18$: $k=6$. From $y=18$, $z=5$: $c=90$. When $x=15$: $y=6\cdot15=90$ and $z=\dfrac{90}{90}=1$, so $y+z=\boxed{91}$.
