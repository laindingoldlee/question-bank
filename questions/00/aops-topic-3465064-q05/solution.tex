It suffices to inspect the 28 multiples of 7. In increasing order, their digit sums are $7,5,3,10,8,6,13,11,9,7,14,12,10,17,6,4,11,9,7,5,12,10,8,15,13,11,18,16$. The multiples whose digit sums are 7 or 14 are $7,70,77,133$. Their sum is $\boxed{287}$. This list exhausts the range because $7\cdot28=196$ and $7\cdot29>200$.
