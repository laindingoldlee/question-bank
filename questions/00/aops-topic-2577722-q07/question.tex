Ajax is in a $16$-team soccer tournament. In any game, Ajax wins with probability $\dfrac23$, except against two particular teams, against which Ajax wins with probability $\dfrac12$; there are no draws, and games are independent. For the first round the $16$ teams are split uniformly at random into $4$ groups of $4$, and each team plays each other team in its group twice. A team reaches the next round if it wins at least $3$ of its $6$ games. What is the probability that Ajax reaches the next round?
