The sum $x^2+y^2+z^2$ is $0$, $1$ or $2$, and each square is $0$ or $1$ (a square of absolute value at least $2$ is at least $4$). So each coordinate is $-1$, $0$ or $1$, and at most two coordinates are nonzero.
\begin{itemize}
\item Sum $0$: $(0,0,0)$, $1$ triple.
\item Sum $1$: one coordinate is $\pm1$: $3\cdot2=6$ triples.
\item Sum $2$: two coordinates are $\pm1$: $\dbinom{3}{2}\cdot2^2=12$ triples.
\end{itemize}
The total is $1+6+12=\boxed{19}$.
