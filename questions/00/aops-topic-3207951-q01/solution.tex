Order the group sizes as $a_1<\cdots<a_6$ and put $b_i=a_i-i$. Then $0\le b_1\le\cdots\le b_6$ and $\sum b_i=25-21=4$. These correspond to partitions of $4$, padded with zeros: $4$, $3+1$, $2+2$, $2+1+1$, and $1+1+1+1$. All use at most six parts and each reverses uniquely to a division. Thus there are $\boxed5$.
