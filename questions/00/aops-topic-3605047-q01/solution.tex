Count the complementary paths, those with heads never ahead. If the final number of heads is $h\le5$, reflection through the first forbidden step gives $\binom{11}{h}-\binom{11}{h-1}$ allowed paths, where $\binom{11}{-1}=0$. Indeed reflection bijects paths that first reach one more head than tail with all paths having $h-1$ heads. Summing over $h=0,\ldots,5$ telescopes to $\binom{11}{5}=462$. All $2^{11}=2048$ paths are equally likely. The desired probability is $1-\dfrac{462}{2048}=\boxed{\dfrac{793}{1024}}$.
