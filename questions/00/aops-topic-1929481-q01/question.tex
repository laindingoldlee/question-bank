Frosty the Snowman wants to make a small snowman friend from three snow spheres: a base, a torso and a head, with the torso no larger than the base and the head no larger than the torso. He has one spherical snowball of radius $6$ inches and wants to use all of its snow, and the radius of each of the three spheres must be a positive integer number of inches. Can Frosty do this? Find all possibilities.
