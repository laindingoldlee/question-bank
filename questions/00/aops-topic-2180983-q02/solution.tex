With $t$ teams there are $\dbinom t2$ pairs of teams, and each pair plays $6$ games, so
\[6\cdot\dfrac{t(t-1)}{2}=396\quad\Longrightarrow\quad t(t-1)=132=12\cdot11 .\]
Since $t(t-1)$ increases with $t$ for $t\ge1$, the only positive solution is $t=\boxed{12}$.
