Such an $n$ is divisible by $2,3,7$, so $n=2^a3^b7^c m$ with $a,b,c\ge1$ and $\tau(n)=(a+1)(b+1)(c+1)\tau(m)=42$. Each of the first three factors is at least $2$, and $42=2\cdot3\cdot7$ has only three prime factors, so $\tau(m)=1$ ($m=1$) and $\{a+1,b+1,c+1\}=\{2,3,7\}$. The $3!=6$ assignments of exponents $\{1,2,6\}$ give $6$ numbers. \textbf{(D)}
