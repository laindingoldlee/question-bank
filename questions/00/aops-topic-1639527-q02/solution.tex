Machine A makes $\dfrac{120}{40}=3$ bolts per second and Machine B makes $\dfrac{100}{20}=5$ bolts per second, so together they make $8$ bolts per second. They need
\[
\dfrac{200}{8}=\boxed{25}\text{ seconds}.
\]
