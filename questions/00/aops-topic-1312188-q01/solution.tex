Let the edges be $x,y,z$ with $xy=16$, $yz=20$, $zx=45$. Multiplying, $(xyz)^2=16\cdot20\cdot45=14400$, so the volume is $xyz=\boxed{120}$. (The edges themselves are $x=\dfrac{120}{20}=6$, $y=\dfrac{120}{45}=\dfrac{8}{3}$, $z=\dfrac{120}{16}=\dfrac{15}{2}$, not whole numbers, which is why guessing them fails.)

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
draw((0,0)--(4,0)--(4,2.5)--(0,2.5)--cycle); draw((4,0)--(5.5,1.2)--(5.5,3.7)--(4,2.5)); draw((0,2.5)--(1.5,3.7)--(5.5,3.7));
label("$xy=16$",(2,1.25)); label("$yz=20$",(4.75,1.85)); label("$xz=45$",(2.75,3.1),fontsize(8pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
