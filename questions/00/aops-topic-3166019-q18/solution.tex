The prime factorization is $2^{3a}3^{2b}$, so $(3a+1)(2b+1)=578=2\cdot17^2$. The odd factor $2b+1>1$ is either $17$ or $289$. The first gives $b=8$ and $3a+1=34$, so $a=11$. The second would give $3a+1=2$, impossible for positive integer $a$. Therefore $\boxed{ab=88}$.
