If the divisor count is odd, the integer is a square and the middle divisor is its square root. Thus this case gives $81$.
If the count is even, the middle divisors $u<v$ pair to the number $N=uv$ and satisfy $u+v=18$. Check $u=1,\ldots,8$:
\[\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrrr}u&1&2&3&4&5&6&7&8\\N=u(18-u)&17&32&45&56&65&72&77&80\\\text{actual median}&9&6&7&\dfrac{15}{2}&9&\dfrac{17}{2}&9&9\end{array}\]
The entries follow by listing each small integer's divisors; this exhausts all possible middle pairs. Thus the required numbers are $17,65,77,80,81$, summing to $\boxed{320}$.
