Count $k$-digit base-$4$ strings (first digit nonzero) with no run of length $3$ or more, by tracking the length of the final run. Let $u_k$ end in a run of $1$ and $v_k$ end in a run of $2$: $u_1=3,\ v_1=0$, and $u_{k+1}=3(u_k+v_k)$, $v_{k+1}=u_k$. This gives
\[(u,v):\ (3,0),\ (9,3),\ (36,9),\ (135,36),\ (513,135),\]
so the numbers of "good" strings of lengths $1$ to $5$ are $3,12,45,171,648$. The total numbers of length $k$ are $3\cdot4^{k-1}$: $3,12,48,192,768$. The differences give $0+0+3+21+120=\boxed{144}$.
