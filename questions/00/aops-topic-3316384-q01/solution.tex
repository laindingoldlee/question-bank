The positive values $7+30n$ begin $7,37,67,97,127,157,187$. The first six are prime by trial division up to their square roots, while $187=11\cdot17$, so $a=187$. The absolute-value sequence also includes positive numbers congruent to $23$ modulo 30: $23,53,83,113,143,\ldots$. The first four are prime and $143=11\cdot13$. Interleaving both residue classes shows $b=143$. Therefore $\gcd(187,143)=\boxed{11}$.
