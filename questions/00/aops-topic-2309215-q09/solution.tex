Since $\dfrac1x<\dfrac16$ and $\dfrac1y<\dfrac16$, both $x>6$ and $y>6$. Multiplying by $6xy$ gives $6y+6x=xy$, that is,
\[xy-6x-6y+36=36,\qquad (x-6)(y-6)=36.\]
Both factors are positive integers, so $x-6$ is a positive divisor $d$ of $36$ and $y-6=\dfrac{36}{d}$. The divisors are $1,2,3,4,6,9,12,18,36$, giving the $9$ pairs
\[\boxed{(7,42),\ (8,24),\ (9,18),\ (10,15),\ (12,12),\ (15,10),\ (18,9),\ (24,8),\ (42,7)}.\]
