The sample space is the unit square $[1,2]^2$. The line $x+y=3$ joins $(1,2)$ and $(2,1)$ and divides the square into two congruent triangles. The event is the lower-left triangle, so its area and probability are $\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}$. The boundary has probability zero.
