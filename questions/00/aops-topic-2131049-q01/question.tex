A traveller visits one of two families: the Potters with probability $\dfrac35$ or the Longbottoms with probability $\dfrac25$. If he visits the Potters, he finds Lily at home with probability $\dfrac{8}{10}$ and James at home with probability $\dfrac{9}{10}$. If he visits the Longbottoms, he finds Frank at home with probability $\dfrac{7}{10}$ and Alice at home with probability $\dfrac{7}{10}$. Within each family, whether one parent is at home does not affect whether the other is. What is the probability that he visits the Potters and finds both Lily and James at home?
