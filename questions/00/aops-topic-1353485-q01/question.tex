Five boys and five girls sit at $10$ equally spaced seats around a circle. Rotations of an arrangement are considered the same. In how many ways can they sit so that every boy sits directly opposite a girl?

A student argues: ``Pair each boy with a girl in $5\cdot5=25$ ways. Then the first boy has $10$ seats, the second $8$, then $6$, $4$, $2$; dividing by $10$ for rotations gives $25\cdot\dfrac{10\cdot8\cdot6\cdot4\cdot2}{10}=9600$.'' Is this right?
