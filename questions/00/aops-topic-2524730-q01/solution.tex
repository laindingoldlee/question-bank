The product is negative exactly when one factor is positive and the other negative. (The values $x=0$ or $y=0$ have probability $0$.) We have
\[P(x>0)=\dfrac{9}{14},\quad P(x<0)=\dfrac5{14},\quad P(y>0)=\dfrac{2}{10},\quad P(y<0)=\dfrac{8}{10}.\]
By independence,
\[P(xy<0)=\dfrac9{14}\cdot\dfrac{8}{10}+\dfrac5{14}\cdot\dfrac2{10}=\dfrac{72+10}{140}=\boxed{\dfrac{41}{70}}.\]
