Snow falls at a rate of $4$ inches per hour (in depth) on a road that is $2000$ feet long and $30$ feet wide, and that is clear of snow at the moment the snow starts. At that moment a snowplow, also $30$ feet wide, starts at one end of the road and drives to the other end at $1000$ feet per hour, removing all the snow it touches. (Ignore the length of the plow.) When the plow reaches the other end of the road, how many cubic feet of snow are on the road?
