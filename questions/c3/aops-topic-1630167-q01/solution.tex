There are $7!$ orderings of the seven digits, and those starting with $0$ are not $7$-digit integers. By symmetry, $0$ is first in $\dfrac{1}{7}$ of the orderings. So the count is $7!-6!=6\cdot6!=\boxed{4320}$.
