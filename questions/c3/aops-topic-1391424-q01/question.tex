Five of James' friends are sitting around a circular table to play a game of Fish. James chooses a place between two of his friends to pull up a chair and sit. Then the six people divide themselves into two disjoint teams, each team consisting of three consecutive players at the table. If the order in which the three members of a team sit does not matter, how many possible (unordered) pairs of teams are possible?
