The identity $\sin2t=2\sin t\cos t$ gives $2^{14}\prod_{k=1}^{14}\cos\dfrac{k\pi}{15}=\dfrac{\prod_{k=1}^{14}\sin\dfrac{2k\pi}{15}}{\prod_{k=1}^{14}\sin\dfrac{k\pi}{15}}$. Doubling permutes the nonzero residues modulo $15$. For $k=8,\ldots,14$, subtracting $\pi$ from $\dfrac{2 k \pi}{15}$ introduces a negative sign; there are seven such factors. Thus the ratio is $-1$, and the answer is $\boxed{-2^{-14}}$.
