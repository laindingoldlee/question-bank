Each day he gets
\[v(d)+s(d)=d^2+9d+20+d^3-d^2+d+1=d^3+10d+21\]
hundredths of a dollar. Over $d=1,\dots,7$,
\[\sum_{d=1}^{7}\left(d^3+10d+21\right)=(1+2+\cdots+7)^2+10\cdot28+7\cdot21=784+280+147=1211,\]
so he has made $\dfrac{1211}{100}=\boxed{\$12.11}$.
