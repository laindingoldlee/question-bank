Let the side length be $s$. The distances from $P$ to the parallel lines $AB,CD$ are $\dfrac{6}{s},\dfrac{30}{s}$. If $P$ lies between the lines, their sum is $s$, giving $s^2=36$. If it lies outside, their difference is $s$, giving $s^2=24$. Both are attainable, so the requested sum is $\boxed{60}$.
