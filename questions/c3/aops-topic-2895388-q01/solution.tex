Let the escalator move $r$ steps for each step the shorter man takes. Then the step count is $N=50+50r$ for the shorter man. The taller man takes $75$ steps in time of $25$ short-man steps, so $N=75+25r$. Equating, $r=1$ and $N=\boxed{100}$.
