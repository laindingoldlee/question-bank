At the tether corner, the house blocks one quadrant, leaving $\dfrac{3}{4}\pi16^2$. The rope can pass the adjacent corner $13$ feet away, leaving $3$ feet, which accesses one additional quarter-disk. It cannot reach the other adjacent corner $37$ feet away or wrap a second corner. The two accessible regions have disjoint interiors, so the area is $\dfrac{3}{4}\pi256+\dfrac{1}{4}\pi9=\boxed{\dfrac{777\pi}{4}}$ square feet.
