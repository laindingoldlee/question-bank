The product of two linear functions of $n$ is quadratic. The first differences are $2,12$, so the second difference is $10$. The next first difference would be $22$, giving $\boxed{40}$.
But the quadratic through these values is $5n^2-13n+12$, with discriminant $169-240=-71<0$. It cannot be a product of two nonconstant real linear factors, which would have real roots. If one factor were constant, the product would be at most linear. Hence $\boxed{\text{no such real sequences exist}}$.
