In one round the last colour put on a section is the colour of the last painter whose step divides the section number. After the first round, each later round paints the sections in the same order again, so it ends with the same colours. Hence the final colours are the colours after one round:
\begin{itemize}
\item yellow: multiples of $5$, namely $5,10$;
\item blue: multiples of $4$ that are not multiples of $5$, namely $4,8$;
\item green: multiples of $3$ that are not multiples of $4$ or $5$, namely $3,6,9$;
\item red: even numbers not yet listed, namely $2$;
\item section $1$ is never painted.
\end{itemize}
So $\boxed{2}$ sections (sections $4$ and $8$) are blue.
