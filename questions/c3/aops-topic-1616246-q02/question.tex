A flute has $10$ sections in a row, numbered $1$ to $10$. Ren paints with four colours in this order: he paints every $2$nd section red (sections $2,4,6,\dots$), then every $3$rd section green (sections $3,6,9$), then every $4$th section blue (sections $4,8$), and finally every $5$th section yellow (sections $5,10$). Painting a section covers its old colour. He then repeats this whole procedure, in the same order, $2018$ times in total. At the end, how many sections are blue?
