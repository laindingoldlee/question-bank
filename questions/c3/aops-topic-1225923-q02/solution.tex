For a quadratic, third differences vanish: $f(3)-3f(2)+3f(1)-f(0)=0$. Hence
\[f(3)=\cos^340^\circ-3\cos40^\circ\sin^240^\circ=\cos(3\cdot40^\circ)=\cos120^\circ=\boxed{-\dfrac{1}{2}},\]
using $\cos3\theta=\cos^3\theta-3\cos\theta\sin^2\theta$ (the real part of $(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)^3$).
