For each of 26 black cards, restrict attention to its relative order with the two jokers. The black card is equally likely to be first, middle, or last, so its probability of lying between them is $\dfrac{1}{3}$. By linearity, the expectation is $\boxed{\dfrac{26}{3}}$.
