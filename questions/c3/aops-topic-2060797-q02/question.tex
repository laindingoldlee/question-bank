Jack is riding his bicycle up a straight hill at a constant speed. When he is $20\%$ of the way up, an egg starts rolling down from the top of the hill; at that moment Jack turns around and rides back down, at a constant speed twice his uphill speed. Exactly $1$ minute later Jack reaches the bottom of the hill, at the same moment as the egg. The egg rolled $100$ feet, from the top of the hill to the bottom. At his uphill speed, how many minutes would it take Jack to ride up the whole hill?
