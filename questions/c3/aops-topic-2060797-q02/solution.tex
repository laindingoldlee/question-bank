The egg rolled from the top of the hill to the bottom, so the hill is $100$ feet long, and Jack turned around $20\%$ of the way up, that is, $20$ feet from the bottom.

He rode these $20$ feet downhill in $1$ minute, so his downhill speed is $20$ feet per minute, and his uphill speed is half of that, $10$ feet per minute. At $10$ feet per minute the whole $100$-foot hill takes
\[\dfrac{100}{10}=\boxed{10}\text{ minutes}.\]
(The egg's information is consistent but not needed beyond the length of the hill: it rolled $100$ feet in $1$ minute. Before turning around, Jack had ridden $20$ feet uphill in $2$ minutes; riding up the whole hill and back down would take $10+5=15$ minutes.)
