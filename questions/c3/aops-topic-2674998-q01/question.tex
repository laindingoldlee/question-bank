Let $f(s)$ be the area of the region of points $(x,y)$ satisfying $x\ge\dfrac y2+7$, $y\ge\dfrac x2+7$, $x\le s$ and $y\le s$. Suppose $f(s)=\dfrac mn$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers with $m+n=187$. Find the sum of all the numerators and denominators (in lowest terms) of all possible rational values of $s$.
