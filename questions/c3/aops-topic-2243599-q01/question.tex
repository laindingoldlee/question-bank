Four balls are placed into $6$ bins, each ball independently into a uniformly random bin.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Find the expected number of empty bins.
\item[(b)] A solution computes the probability that one particular bin is empty and multiplies it by $6$. Explain why this is correct even though at least two bins are always empty.
\end{enumerate}
