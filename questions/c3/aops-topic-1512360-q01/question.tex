Squares $ABTM$ and $ACRN$ are drawn outside triangle $ABC$ (vertices in this order, so $M$ and $N$ are the vertices of the squares adjacent to $A$). Let $AD$ be the median from $A$ in triangle $ABC$. Prove that $MN=2AD$ and that $MN\perp AD$.
