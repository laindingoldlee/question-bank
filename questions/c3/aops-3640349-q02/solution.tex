For n positive, $S(n)\ge n+1$ and $S(n+1)\ge n+2$ except the harmless n=1 case, so n is at most 46. Enumerating the divisors for these finitely many n gives the unique solution n=31, since $S(31)=32$ and $S(32)=63$. Therefore $S(n^2)=1+31+31^2=\boxed{993}$. The exact bounded search is included in the audit.
