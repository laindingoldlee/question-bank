A garden urn contains $18$ coloured beetles: $6$ red beetles numbered $1$ to $6$, and $12$ yellow beetles numbered $1$ to $12$. The beetles wander out of the urn one at a time in random order, and none goes back in. What is the probability that the numbers on the first four beetles to wander out are strictly increasing (each number is larger than the one before, so no number is repeated)? Give the answer as a fraction in lowest terms.
