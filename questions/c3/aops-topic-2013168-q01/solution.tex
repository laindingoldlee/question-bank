All $18\cdot17\cdot16\cdot15=73440$ ordered choices of the first four beetles are equally likely.

A favourable sequence is determined by choosing a set of $4$ distinct numbers (they must appear in increasing order) and then choosing which beetle carries each number. Numbers $1$ to $6$ are each carried by $2$ beetles (one red, one yellow), and numbers $7$ to $12$ by $1$ beetle. If exactly $k$ of the four chosen numbers are at most $6$, the number of favourable sequences is $\binom{6}{k}2^k\binom{6}{4-k}$:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
k & \binom6k 2^k\binom6{4-k}\\\hline
0 & 1\cdot1\cdot15=15\\
1 & 6\cdot2\cdot20=240\\
2 & 15\cdot4\cdot15=900\\
3 & 20\cdot8\cdot6=960\\
4 & 15\cdot16\cdot1=240
\end{array}
\]
The total is $15+240+900+960+240=2355=3\cdot5\cdot157$. Therefore the probability is
\[
\dfrac{2355}{73440}=\boxed{\dfrac{157}{4896}}=\dfrac{157}{2^5\cdot3^2\cdot17}.
\]
