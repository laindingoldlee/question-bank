Write $x=\omega(3+4i-x)$ with $\omega^6=1$. The value -1 is impossible, and the other five give $x=(3+4i)[\dfrac{1}{2}+(\dfrac{i}{2})\tan(\dfrac{\arg\omega}{2})]$. The possible tangent values are $0,\pm\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3},\pm\sqrt3$. The imaginary part is $2+(\dfrac{3}{2})$ times that tangent, maximised at $\sqrt3$. Thus the required root is $\boxed{(\dfrac{3}{2}-2\sqrt3)+i(2+\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2})}$.
