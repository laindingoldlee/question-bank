Let $a=\log_{225}x$ and $b=\log_{64}y$. Then $a+b=4$ and $\dfrac{1}{a}-\dfrac{1}{b}=1$, i.e. $b-a=ab$. Substituting $b=4-a$: $4-2a=4a-a^2$, so $a^2-6a+4=0$. Its two roots satisfy $a_1+a_2=6$, and then $b_1+b_2=8-6=2$. Hence
\[x_1x_2y_1y_2=225^{a_1+a_2}\cdot64^{b_1+b_2}=225^6\cdot64^2=15^{12}\cdot2^{12}=30^{12},\]
and the answer is $\boxed{12}$.
