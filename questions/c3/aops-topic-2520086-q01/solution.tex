$1050=2\cdot3\cdot5^2\cdot7$. A $5$-tuple is determined by how the exponent of each prime is split among the five factors. The single factors $2$, $3$ and $7$ each go to one of the $5$ numbers: $5$ ways each. The exponent $2$ of $5$ is split as $e_1+\cdots+e_5=2$ with $e_i\ge0$: by stars and bars $\dbinom{6}{4}=15$ ways. The total is
\[5\cdot5\cdot5\cdot15=\boxed{1875}.\]
