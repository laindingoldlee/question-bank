Six people must cross a river in a boat that can carry at most $170$ pounds at a time (any number of people, as long as their total weight is at most $170$ pounds). Their weights are: Lucky $100$, Tarune $100$, Grayson $40$, Whitnie $30$, Caroll $70$ and Vice $30$ pounds. The boat cannot cross without at least one person in it. What is the least number of crossings (counting each trip across the river, in either direction) needed to get all six people to the other side?
