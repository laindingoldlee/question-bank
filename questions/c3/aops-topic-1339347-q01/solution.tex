The product is $64\cdot32=2048$. To convert to base $5$, use the powers $5^0,\dots,5^4=1,5,25,125,625$ (as $5^5=3125>2048$):
\[
2048=3\cdot625+173,\quad173=1\cdot125+48,\quad48=1\cdot25+23,\quad23=4\cdot5+3 .
\]
So $2048=3\cdot5^4+1\cdot5^3+1\cdot5^2+4\cdot5+3=\boxed{31143_5}$.
