The $k$-th group is the triangular number $T_k=\dfrac{k(k+1)}{2}$. Using $\dbinom{k+1}{2}=T_k$ and the hockey-stick identity,
\[\sum_{k=1}^{n}\dbinom{k+1}{2}=\dbinom{n+2}{3}=\boxed{\dfrac{n(n+1)(n+2)}{6}}.\]
(Check: $n=3$ gives $1+3+6=10=\dfrac{3\cdot4\cdot5}{6}$.)
