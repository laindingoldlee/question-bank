For a positive integer $m$, let $r(m)$ be its digital root, obtained by repeatedly adding the digits until one digit remains (for example $325\to10\to1$, so $r(325)=1$).
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Prove that $r(ab)=r\bigl(r(a)\,r(b)\bigr)$ for all positive integers $a$ and $b$.
\item[(b)] Check this for $325\times675=219375$.
\item[(c)] If the digital roots agree, does that prove that a product was computed correctly?
\end{enumerate}
