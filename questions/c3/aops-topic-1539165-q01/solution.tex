The vowels are A, A, I and the consonants are F, C, L.

The first letter must be one of the $3$ consonants. The remaining $5$ letters are A, A, I and the other two consonants; with the two A's identical they can be arranged in $\dfrac{5!}{2!}=60$ ways. So there are $3\cdot60=\boxed{180}$ rearrangements.

Check by complement: in all there are $\dfrac{6!}{2!}=360$ arrangements. Starting with A, the remaining F, C, I, A, L are all different: $5!=120$. Starting with I, the rest F, A, C, A, L give $\dfrac{5!}{2!}=60$. So $360-120-60=180$.
