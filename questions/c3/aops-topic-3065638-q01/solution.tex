Factor $3689=7\cdot17\cdot31$. Since $2^3\equiv1\pmod7$, $2^8\equiv1\pmod{17}$, and $2^5\equiv1\pmod{31}$, we have $2^{120}\equiv1\pmod{3689}$. Let $E=8^{11}$, which is even. Modulo 120, $5^2=25$ and $25^2\equiv25$, so $5^E\equiv25$. The answer is consequently the remainder of $2^{25}$ on division by 3689, namely $\boxed{2977}$.
