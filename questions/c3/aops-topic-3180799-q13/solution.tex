Let the similarity ratio be $t$. Then $AE=13t,AF=12t,EF=5t$. The triangle perimeter is $30t$, and the trapezoid perimeter is $5t+5+25(1-t)=30-20t$. Equality gives $t=\dfrac{3}{5}$, so $\boxed{EF=3}$.
