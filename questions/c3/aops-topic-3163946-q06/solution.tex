After $n$ minutes, the number in box $k$ steps to the right is $\binom nk$, by choosing which of the $n$ cloning stages moved along that path, or by Pascal's recurrence. The largest counts in rows $0$ through $8$ do not exceed the central value $\binom84=70$. In row $9$, the central two counts are $\binom94=\binom95=126$, while the next largest is $84$. Thus the first boxes exceeding $100$ contain $\boxed{126}$ creatures.
