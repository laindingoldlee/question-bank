Let
\[
S=1+11+111+\cdots+\underbrace{11\ldots1}_{96\text{ ones}},
\]
the sum of the $96$ numbers whose decimal digits are all $1$, with $1,2,\dots,96$ digits. How many digits $1$ appear in the decimal representation of $S$?
