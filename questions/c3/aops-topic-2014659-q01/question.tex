A $2\times2$ square grid is made of four $1\times1$ squares, labelled $A$ (upper left), $B$ (upper right), $C$ (lower left) and $D$ (lower right). The four squares are to be painted so that $2$ are blue, $1$ is red and $1$ is green. In how many ways can this be done?
