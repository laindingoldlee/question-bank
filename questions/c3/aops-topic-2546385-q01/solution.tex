A fraction in lowest terms terminates exactly when its denominator has no prime factors other than $2$ and $5$. Since $30030=2\cdot3\cdot5\cdot7\cdot11\cdot13$, every allowed $x$ is divisible by $3$, $7$, $11$ and $13$. For $\dfrac nx$ to terminate, these primes must cancel, so $3\cdot7\cdot11\cdot13=3003$ must divide $n$.

The largest $7$-digit multiple of $3003$ is $3003\cdot3330=9999990$ (as $9999999=3003\cdot3330+9$). It works: with $x=30030\cdot512=15375360>n$,
\[\dfrac{9999990}{15375360}=\dfrac{3003\cdot3330}{3003\cdot5120}=\dfrac{333}{512}=0.650390625.\]
So $n=\boxed{9999990}$.
