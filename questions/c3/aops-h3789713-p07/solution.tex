For either prime $2$ or $3$, let the exponents in $m,n$ be $u,v$. We need $\min(3u,2v)=2$ and $\max(2u,3v)=4$. These force $v=1,u=2$. For prime $5$, the equations are $\min(3u,2v)=0$, $\max(2u,3v)=6$, giving $(u,v)=(3,0)$ or $(0,2)$. No other prime can occur. Hence exactly $\boxed2$ ordered pairs exist, namely $(4500,6)$ and $(36,150)$.
