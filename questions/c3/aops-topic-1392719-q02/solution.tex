Use mass points. Assign masses so that $D$ and $E$ are balance points:
\begin{itemize}
\item $AD:DC=2:1$ requires $m_A:m_C=1:2$;
\item $AE:EB=2:3$ requires $m_A:m_B=3:2$.
\end{itemize}
Take $m_A=3$, $m_B=2$, $m_C=6$ (written at the vertices in the figure). Then $m_D=m_A+m_C=9$ and $m_E=m_A+m_B=5$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(6cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(5,0),C=(1.5,3.2);
pair D=A+2/3*(C-A), Ep=A+2/5*(B-A);
pair K=extension(Ep,C,D,B);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(Ep--C,blue); draw(D--B,red);
dot(A);dot(B);dot(C);dot(D);dot(Ep);dot(K);
label("$A$",A,SW); label("$B$",B,SE); label("$C$",C,N);
label("$D$",D,W); label("$E$",Ep,S); label("$K$",K,NE);
label("$3$",A,2.2N,fontsize(9pt)); label("$6$",C,2.2E,fontsize(9pt)); label("$2$",B,2.2N,fontsize(9pt));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The point $K$ is the balance point of $D$ (mass $9$) and $B$ (mass $2$) on segment $DB$, so
\[
\dfrac{DK}{KB}=\dfrac{m_B}{m_D}=\boxed{\dfrac{2}{9}}.
\]
(As a check, on $EC$ we get $EK:KC=m_C:m_E=6:5$.) With coordinates $A=(0,0)$, $B=(5,0)$, $C=(0,3)$ one finds $D=(0,2)$, $E=(2,0)$ and $K=\left(\dfrac{10}{11},\dfrac{18}{11}\right)$, which divides $DB$ in the ratio $2:9$.
