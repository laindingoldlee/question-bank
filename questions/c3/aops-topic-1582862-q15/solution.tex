Let the tangents touch the circle at $A$ and $B$ and meet at $P$, and let $O$ be the centre. A tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of tangency, so $\angle OAP=\angle OBP=90^\circ$. The central angle $\angle AOB$ equals the arc, $116^\circ$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0);real r=1.6;
pair A=r*dir(58),B=r*dir(-58);
pair P=extension(A,A+rotate(90)*A,B,B+rotate(90)*B);
draw(circle(O,r),linewidth(0.9));
draw(P--A,linewidth(0.9));draw(P--B,linewidth(0.9));
draw(arc(O,r,-58,58),red+linewidth(1.6));
draw(O--A,dashed);draw(O--B,dashed);
real s=0.17;
pair u=unit(O-A),v=unit(P-A);draw(A+s*u--A+s*(u+v)--A+s*v);
u=unit(O-B);v=unit(P-B);draw(B+s*u--B+s*(u+v)--B+s*v);
draw(arc(O,0.35,-58,58));label("$116^\circ$",O+0.65*dir(0),fontsize(8pt));
draw(arc(P,0.5,180-32,180+32),blue);label("$x$",P+0.75*dir(180),blue);
dot(O);dot(A);dot(B);dot(P);
label("$O$",O,W);label("$A$",A,N);label("$B$",B,S);label("$P$",P,E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The angles of quadrilateral $OAPB$ add up to $360^\circ$, so
\[
\angle APB=360^\circ-90^\circ-90^\circ-116^\circ=\boxed{64^\circ}.
\]
(Equivalently, the angle between two tangents is half the difference of the intercepted arcs: $\dfrac{244^\circ-116^\circ}{2}=64^\circ$.)
