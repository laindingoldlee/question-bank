After $n$ years the simple interest is $12000\cdot0.25n=3000n$ and the compound interest is $12000(1.15^n-1)$. We need $1.15^n-1>\dfrac{n}{4}$:
\[
\begin{array}{c|cccccccc}
n&1&2&3&4&5&6&7&8\\ \hline
1.15^n-1&0.15&0.32&0.52&0.75&1.01&1.31&1.66&2.06\\
\dfrac{n}{4}&0.25&0.50&0.75&1.00&1.25&1.50&1.75&2.00
\end{array}
\]
(values rounded to two decimals; exactly, $1.15^7=2.66\ldots<2.75$ and $1.15^8=3.059\ldots>3$). The first year is $n=\boxed{8}$.
