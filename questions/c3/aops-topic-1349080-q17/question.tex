Papa Elf has $12{,}000$ Elvens to save. The Elven Bank pays $25\%$ simple interest per year, and the Golden Reindeer Bank pays $15\%$ interest per year, compounded yearly. After how many whole years will the interest earned at the Golden Reindeer Bank first be greater than the interest earned at the Elven Bank?
