Since $S(42-S)=441-(S-21)^2$, we need $S\le8$ or $S\ge34$. Replacing each face $d$ by $7-d$ exchanges these events. For $S\le8$, subtract $1$ from every die; the six nonnegative increments sum to $0,1,$ or $2$, with counts $1,6,\binom72=21$. There are $56$ favorable outcomes among $6^6=46656$, giving $\boxed{\dfrac{7}{5832}}$.
