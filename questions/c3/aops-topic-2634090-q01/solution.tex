Let $a=\dfrac{x}{3y}$, $b=\dfrac{y}{3z}$, $c=\dfrac{z}{3x}$; these are positive with $abc=\dfrac1{27}$. Expanding,
\[(2+a)(2+b)(2+c)=8+4(a+b+c)+2(ab+bc+ca)+abc.\]
By AM-GM, $a+b+c\ge3\sqrt[3]{abc}=1$ and $ab+bc+ca\ge3\sqrt[3]{(abc)^2}=\dfrac13$. Hence the product is at least
\[8+4+\dfrac23+\dfrac1{27}=\dfrac{343}{27}=\left(\dfrac73\right)^3,\]
with equality when $a=b=c=\dfrac13$, i.e. $x=y=z$. The minimum is $\boxed{\dfrac{343}{27}}$.

(Applying AM-GM to each factor separately, $2+a\ge3\sqrt[3]a$, only gives the lower bound $9$, which is not attained.)
