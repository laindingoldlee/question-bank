For a positive integer $x$, let $\#[x^2]$ denote the sum of the digits of $x^2$. For example, $\#[65^2]=\#[4225]=4+2+2+5=13$ and $\#[665^2]=\#[442225]=19$. The number $x$ consists of a certain number of $6$'s followed by a single $5$. Find $x$ if $\#[x^2]=49$.
