For a fixed order, the probability that all are glazed is $\dfrac{1}{2}$ (first person) times a factor for each of persons $2$ to $6$: $\dfrac{1}{4}$ if the person in front is a man and $\dfrac{3}{4}$ if a woman. The people in front of someone are exactly persons $1$ to $5$.
\begin{itemize}
\item If the last person is a man (probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$), persons $1$ to $5$ are $2$ men and $3$ women, giving $\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^2\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^3=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{27}{1024}$.
\item If the last person is a woman (probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$), persons $1$ to $5$ are $3$ men and $2$ women, giving $\dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^3\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^2=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{9}{1024}$.
\end{itemize}
Averaging,
\[
P=\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{27}{1024}+\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot\dfrac{9}{1024}=\dfrac{36}{4096}=\boxed{\dfrac{9}{1024}}.
\]
