Three men and three women line up in a uniformly random order to get donuts; each person gets either a chocolate or a glazed donut. The first person in line gets each kind with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Every later person's choice depends only on the person directly in front of them: a person standing behind a man gets chocolate with probability $\dfrac{3}{4}$ and glazed with probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$, while a person standing behind a woman gets chocolate with probability $\dfrac{1}{4}$ and glazed with probability $\dfrac{3}{4}$. What is the probability that all $6$ donuts are glazed?
