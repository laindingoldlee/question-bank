Multiply the first equation by $3$ and the second by $2$:
\[
30x-27y=24,\qquad 30x+42y=1 .
\]
Subtracting, $69y=-23$, so $y=-\dfrac{1}{3}$. Then $10x=8+9y=8-3=5$, so $x=\dfrac{1}{2}$. The solution is
\[
\boxed{(x,y)=\left(\dfrac{1}{2},-\dfrac{1}{3}\right)}.
\]
Check in the second equation: $21\cdot\left(-\dfrac{1}{3}\right)+15\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=-7+7.5=0.5$.
