The equation is $x^2-14x-19=0$. Its discriminant $196+76=272$ is positive, so it has two real roots, and by Vieta's formulas their sum is $\boxed{14}$. (They are $7\pm\sqrt{68}=7\pm2\sqrt{17}$.)
