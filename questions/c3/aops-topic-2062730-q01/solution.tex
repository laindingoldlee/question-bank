All \(\dbinom{20}{4}=4845\) choices are equally likely. Choosing \(2\) of the \(5\) marked and \(2\) of the \(15\) unmarked coins can be done in \(\dbinom52\dbinom{15}{2}=10\cdot 105=1050\) ways. The probability is
\[
\dfrac{1050}{4845}=\boxed{\dfrac{70}{323}}.
\]
