Two sides of a triangle have lengths $x$ and $y$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Describe all possible lengths $z$ of the third side.
\item[(b)] If $x$ and $y$ are positive integers, how many integer values can $z$ take?
\end{enumerate}
