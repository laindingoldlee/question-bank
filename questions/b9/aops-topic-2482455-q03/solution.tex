If Beth's problem time is $t$, her shoe time is $\dfrac{1}{t}$, so $t+\dfrac{1}{t}=9$. Squaring the rate makes Carl's problem time $t^2$; his shoe time is $\dfrac{1}{t^2}$. Thus his total is $(t+\dfrac{1}{t})^2-2=81-2=\boxed{79\text{ minutes}}$.
