For a nonnegative integer $n$, $f(n)$ is the remainder when $n$ is divided by $10$, i.e. the units digit of $n$. The units digits of $0!,1!,2!,3!,4!$ are $1,1,2,6,4$. For $i\ge5$, $i!$ is divisible by $2\cdot5=10$, so $f(i!)=0$. Hence the sum is
\[1+1+2+6+4=\boxed{14}.\]
