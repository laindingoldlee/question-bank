Let $x=0.08\overline3=0.08333\ldots$. Then $1000x=83.333\ldots$ and $100x=8.333\ldots$, so
\[900x=75,\qquad x=\dfrac{75}{900}=\boxed{\dfrac{1}{12}} .\]
(Check: $\dfrac{1}{12}=0.08333\ldots$.)
