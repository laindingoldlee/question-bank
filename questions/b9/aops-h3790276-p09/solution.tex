If each ordered pair exchanges $k$ letters in its specified direction, the total is $kn(n-1)=440$. Hence $n(n-1)\mid440$ and $n\le21$. A possible $n$ must divide $440$; among divisors at most $21$, the largest is $20$, which fails because $19\nmid440$. The next is $11$, which works since $11\cdot10\cdot4=440$. Thus the greatest group size is $\boxed{11}$.
