A circle of radius $r$ is inscribed in a right triangle. Find the perimeter of the triangle if:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $r=4$ cm and the hypotenuse is $24$ cm;
\item[(b)] the point of tangency on the hypotenuse divides it into segments of lengths $5$ and $12$.
\end{enumerate}
