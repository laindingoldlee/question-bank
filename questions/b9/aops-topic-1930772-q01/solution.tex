Both numbers must be even, so they are at most $998$ and $996$ (the two largest even numbers below $1000$), with sum at most $1994$. This pair works: $\gcd(998,996)=2\gcd(499,498)=2\cdot1=2$, since consecutive integers are coprime. So the numbers are $\boxed{996\text{ and }998}$.
