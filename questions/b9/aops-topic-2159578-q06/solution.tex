\textbf{Employees.} There are $24\cdot8=192$ employees, and each shakes hands with the $192-8=184$ employees of the other companies. Each handshake is counted by both people, so there are
\[\dfrac{192\cdot184}{2}=17664\]
employee handshakes. (Equivalently, $\dbinom{192}{2}-24\dbinom82=18336-672=17664$.)

\textbf{CEOs.} Any two of the $24$ CEOs shake hands: $\dbinom{24}{2}=276$ handshakes.

The total is $17664+276=\boxed{17940}$.
