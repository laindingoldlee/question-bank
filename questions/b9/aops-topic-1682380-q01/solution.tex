Treat Lisa and John as one block. The block and the other four students are $5$ objects, which can be ordered in $5!=120$ ways, and inside the block Lisa and John can stand in $2$ orders. So there are $120\cdot2=\boxed{240}$ ways.

The poster's answer $600$ is not right. (For comparison, all $6!=720$ orders minus the $240$ above gives $480$ orders in which they are \emph{not} adjacent.)
