From the corner, the rope can sweep through the $270^\circ$ outside the building. Since the rope ($6$ m) is shorter than the walls ($8$ m), it can never reach around another corner, so the grazing region is exactly three quarters of a disc of radius $6$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(5.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
fill((0,0)--arc((0,0),6,90,360)--cycle,palegreen);
fill(box((0,0),(8,8)),lightgray);draw(box((0,0),(8,8)),linewidth(1.1));
draw(arc((0,0),6,90,360),deepgreen+linewidth(0.9));
label("building",(4,4));
draw((0,0)--6*dir(300),red+linewidth(0.9));label("$6$",3*dir(300),NE,red);
dot((0,0));
draw(arc((0,0),1.3,90,360),deepgreen);label("$270^\circ$",2.4*dir(200),deepgreen);
label("$8$",(4,8),N);label("$8$",(8,4),E);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}
