Write $N=3^a m$ with $3\nmid m$. The ratio of the two divisor sums is $3+3^2+\cdots+3^a$, which equals 39 exactly for $a=3$. Since $(a+1)\tau(m)=16$, we have $\tau(m)=4$. Thus $m$ is a prime cube or a product of two different primes, neither involving 3. The smallest possibilities are $2^3=8$ and $2\cdot5=10$, so $m=8$ minimizes $N$. The answer is $\boxed{216}$.
