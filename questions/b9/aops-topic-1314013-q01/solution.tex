A number is coprime to $100=2^2\cdot5^2$ when it is odd and not a multiple of $5$. The odd composites up to $100$ not divisible by $5$ are those with smallest prime factor $3$, $7$ or more:
\[
9,21,27,33,39,49,51,57,63,69,77,81,87,91,93,99 .
\]
(Multiples of $3$: $9,21,27,33,39,51,57,63,69,81,87,93,99$; with smallest factor $7$: $49,77,91$.) Their sum is $\boxed{946}$.
