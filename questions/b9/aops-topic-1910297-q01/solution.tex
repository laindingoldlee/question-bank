Choose the cone ($2$ ways), the flavour ($10$ ways) and the topping, where ``no topping'' is a tenth option ($9+1=10$ ways). The total is $2\cdot10\cdot10=\boxed{200}$. (Alternatively: $2\cdot10\cdot9=180$ combinations with a topping plus $2\cdot10=20$ without one.)
