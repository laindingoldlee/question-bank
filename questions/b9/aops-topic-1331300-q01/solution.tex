Regroup the six fractions in reciprocal pairs:
\[
\left(\dfrac {x}{y}+\dfrac {y}{x}\right)+\left(\dfrac {y}{z}+\dfrac {z}{y}\right)+\left(\dfrac {x}{z}+\dfrac {z}{x}\right).
\]
For positive $t$, $t+\dfrac{1}{t}-2=\dfrac{(t-1)^2}{t}\ge0$, so each pair is at least $2$ and the sum is at least $6$, with equality exactly when $x=y=z$. (The argument works for all positive reals.) $\blacksquare$
