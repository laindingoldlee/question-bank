The initial gap is 36. Every two-year cycle reduces it by 4.5, so after $ k $ cycles the gap is $ 36-4.5k $. After eight cycles, in 2034, the temperatures are equal. The next odd-year step increases the gap by 4, so 2035 does not qualify; the following step makes it $-4.5 $, in 2036. Earlier even-year gaps are positive and their following odd-year gaps are larger. Thus the first year is $\boxed{2036}$.
