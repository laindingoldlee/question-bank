For positive integers $n$ and $k$ let $S_k(n)=1^k+2^k+\cdots+n^k$, and let $S_0(n)=n$. Prove that
\[(k+1)S_k(n)=(n+1)^{k+1}-1-\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}\dbinom{k+1}{j}S_j(n),\]
deduce that $S_k(n)$ is a polynomial in $n$ of degree $k+1$ with leading coefficient $\dfrac1{k+1}$, and use the formula to find $S_3(n)$.
