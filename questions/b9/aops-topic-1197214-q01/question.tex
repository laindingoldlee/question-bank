Isosceles triangle $ABC$ has a right angle at $C$. Point $P$ inside the triangle satisfies $PA=11$, $PB=7$ and $PC=6$. The legs $AC$ and $BC$ have length $s=\sqrt{a+b\sqrt2}$, where $a$ and $b$ are positive integers. Find $a+b$.
