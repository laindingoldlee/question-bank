The permitted unordered pairs are $\{1,2\},\{1,4\},\{2,3\},\{3,4\}$, forming a cycle $1,2,3,4,1$. Choose the first entry in four ways and the second among its two neighbours. Thereafter the unused neighbour is forced at each step, and this produces a valid permutation using all four numbers. The count is $4\cdot2=\boxed8$.
