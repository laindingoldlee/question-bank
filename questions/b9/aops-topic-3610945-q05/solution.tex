Measure B's displacement relative to A in units of 5 miles. Let $F_t(d,j)$ be the probability that after $t$ hours the displacement is $d$ and exactly $j$ whole hours were spent alongside A. Start with $F_0(0,0)=1$. For each state, a speed of 5 or 15 sends it to $(d-1,j)$ or $(d+1,j)$ with weight $0.1$; speed 10 sends it to $(d,j+1)$ if $d=0$, and to $(d,j)$ otherwise, with weight $0.8$. Iterating six times and summing $F_6(d,3)$ over all d gives $\boxed{\dfrac{1952}{15625}}=0.124928$. Equivalently enumerate the $3^6=729$ speed sequences. A sequence with z zero-displacement steps has weight $\dfrac{8^z}{10^6}$ and qualifies when exactly three of its zero steps begin at displacement zero. The valid integer weights sum to 124928, giving the same result.
