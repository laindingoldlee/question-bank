Cars A and B begin together. A travels at 10 miles per hour. At the beginning of each of six hours B independently chooses speed 5, 10, or 15 with probabilities 0.1, 0.8, and 0.1. What is the probability that B travels alongside A, at the same speed and position for the entire hour, in exactly three of the six hours?
