Since $\varphi(81)=54$ and both $16$ and $20$ are coprime to $81$, Euler's theorem gives $16^{54}\equiv20^{54}\equiv1\pmod{81}$. Hence
\[
16^{56}+20^{56}\equiv16^2+20^2=256+400=656=8\cdot81+8\equiv\boxed{8}\pmod{81}.
\]
