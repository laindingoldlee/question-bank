Let the return trip take $t$ hours; the outward trip took $t+2$ hours. The distances are equal:
\[
238t=218(t+2)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 20t=436\quad\Longrightarrow\quad t=\boxed{21.8\text{ hours}}.
\]
