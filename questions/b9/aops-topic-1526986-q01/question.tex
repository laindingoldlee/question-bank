In right triangle $ABC$, with the right angle at $B$, $AB=44$ cm and $BC=33$ cm. Point $D$ lies on side $BC$ so that $BD:DC=2:1$. The triangle is folded along a line so that vertex $A$ lands on point $D$; the fold line meets $AB$ at $F$ and $AC$ at $E$, and the folded figure is quadrilateral $BCEF$. What is the area of triangle $CDE$, in square centimetres?
