Adam must record $4$ pieces in a single take; if he messes up any piece, he must restart the whole recording. He messes up the four pieces with probabilities $\dfrac1{10}$, $\dfrac3{10}$, $\dfrac7{10}$ and $\dfrac9{10}$, independently, on every attempt. What is the expected number of recordings he makes?
