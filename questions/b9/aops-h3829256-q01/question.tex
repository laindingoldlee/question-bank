Let $a_1,\ldots,a_{2k}$ be real numbers.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Give explicit recursive formulas for $U_k,V_k$ such that
\[
\prod_{j=1}^k(a_{2j-1}^2+a_{2j}^2)=U_k^2+V_k^2,
\]
and prove the formula.
\item Verify the identity
\[
(a^2+b^2)(c^2+d^2)(e^2+f^2)
=(ace-bdf)^2+(acf+bde)^2+(ade-bcf)^2+(adf+bce)^2.
\]
\item Explain why a product of integers that are each sums of two integer squares is again a sum of two integer squares.
\end{enumerate}
