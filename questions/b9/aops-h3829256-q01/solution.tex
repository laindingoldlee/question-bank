\begin{enumerate}
\item Start with $U_0=1,V_0=0$ and define
\[
U_j=a_{2j-1}U_{j-1}-a_{2j}V_{j-1},\qquad
V_j=a_{2j}U_{j-1}+a_{2j-1}V_{j-1}.
\]
Expansion gives $U_j^2+V_j^2=(a_{2j-1}^2+a_{2j}^2)(U_{j-1}^2+V_{j-1}^2)$, because the mixed terms cancel. Induction proves the formula. Equivalently, $U_j+iV_j=\prod_{t=1}^j(a_{2t-1}+ia_{2t})$ and complex moduli multiply.
\item Expanding the four squares gives all eight products obtained by choosing one square from each parenthesis. Their four mixed terms are respectively $-2abcdef,+2abcdef,-2abcdef,+2abcdef$, which cancel. This proves the identity.
\item With integer inputs, the recursion uses only integer additions and multiplications, so every $U_j,V_j$ is an integer. The identity therefore gives the required integer-square representation constructively.
\end{enumerate}
