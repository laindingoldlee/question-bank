From the ratios, $a=4c$ and $b=2d$. Then
\[c^d=a^b=(4c)^{2d}=\left(16c^2\right)^d.\]
Both bases are positive and $d\ne0$, and $t\mapsto t^d$ is one-to-one on the positive reals, so $16c^2=c$. As $c\ne0$, $c=\dfrac1{16}$ and $\dfrac1c=\boxed{16}$.
