How many $4$-digit integers $\overline{abcd}$ have the property that both the two-digit number $\overline{ab}$ formed by the first two digits and the number $\overline{cd}$ formed by the last two digits are perfect squares? Here $\overline{cd}$ may start with $0$ (so $04$ means $4$ and $00$ means $0$), and $0$ counts as a perfect square.
