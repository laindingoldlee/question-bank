A cake shop offers $3$ cake bases costing \$10, \$15 and \$17, and $2$ frostings costing \$5 and \$7. There are also $10$ distinct toppings, each costing \$2. Maya chooses exactly one base, exactly one frosting and any set of toppings, and she spends exactly \$30. In how many ways can she choose her cake?
