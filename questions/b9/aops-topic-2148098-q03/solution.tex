The divisor is $16_8=14$.
\begin{itemize}
\item $22_8=18$; $18\div14=1$ remainder $4$. Quotient digit $1$.
\item Bring down $4$: $44_8=36$; $36\div14=2$ remainder $8=10_8$. Quotient digit $2$.
\item Bring down $6$: $106_8=70$; $70\div14=5$ remainder $0$. Quotient digit $5$.
\end{itemize}
So $2246_8\div16_8=\boxed{125_8}$ exactly. Check: $125_8=85$, $2246_8=1190=85\cdot14$.
