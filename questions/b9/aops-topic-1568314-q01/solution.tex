The job requires $6\cdot48=288$ worker-hours. Half the team is $3$ workers, who need
\[
\dfrac{288}{3}=\boxed{96}\text{ hours}.
\]
With fewer workers the job takes \emph{longer}: the time is inversely proportional to the number of workers, so halving the team doubles the time. (The tempting answer $24$ would be right for twice as many workers.)
