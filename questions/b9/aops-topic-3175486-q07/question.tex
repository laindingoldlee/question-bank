Triangle $ABC$ has $AB=5$, $AC=6$. Let $D,E,F$ be the midpoints of $BC,AC,AB$. Let $X$ be the perpendicular projection of $D$ onto line $EF$, and $Y=AX\cap BC$. Given $DY=1$, write $BC=\dfrac{p}{q}$ in lowest terms and find $p+q$.
