After $j$ distinct toys have been collected, the chance that the next box gives a new toy is $\dfrac{10-j}{10}$. The waiting time for that next new toy has expectation $\dfrac{10}{10-j}$. Linearity of expectation gives\[10\left(1+\dfrac{1}{2}+\cdots+\dfrac{1}{10}\right)=10\cdot\dfrac{7381}{2520}=\boxed{\dfrac{7381}{252}}\approx 29.29.\]Independence between these waiting times is not needed for adding their expectations.
