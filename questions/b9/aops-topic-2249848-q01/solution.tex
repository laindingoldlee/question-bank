The digits are $2,2,0,0$. They can be arranged in $\dfrac{4!}{2!\,2!}=6$ ways, but a four-digit number cannot start with $0$; the arrangements starting with $0$ are those of $2,2,0$ in the last three places, $3$ of them. That leaves $6-3=\boxed{3}$ numbers: $2002$, $2020$ and $2200$.
