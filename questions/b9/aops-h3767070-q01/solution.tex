Burnside\textquotesingle s lemma averages the numbers fixed by each rotation. The identity fixes $2^4=16$ colourings. Rotations by $90^\circ$ and $270^\circ$ each force all four beads equal, fixing $2$ each. A $180^\circ$ rotation requires opposite beads to agree and fixes $2^2=4$. The average is $\dfrac{16+2+4+2}{4}=\boxed6$.
