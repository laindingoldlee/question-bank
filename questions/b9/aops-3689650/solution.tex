If the side is $s$ and altitude $h$, joining the point to the vertices decomposes the area into three triangles. Therefore $\dfrac{sh}{2}=\dfrac{s(1+2+3)}{2}$, so $h=6$. Since $h=\dfrac{s\sqrt3}{2}$, the side is $\boxed{4\sqrt3}$.

\end{asy}
