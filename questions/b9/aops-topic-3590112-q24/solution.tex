Necessarily $x+x^{-1}=k$ is an integer. Solving $x^2-kx+1=0$ gives $x=\dfrac{k\pm\sqrt{k^2-4}}{2}$, with real roots exactly when $|k|\ge2$. Conversely, put $s_n=x^n+x^{-n}$. Then $s_0=2$, $s_1=k$, and $s_{n+1}=ks_n-s_{n-1}$, so induction makes every $s_n$ an integer. Thus all solutions are $\boxed{x=\dfrac{k\pm\sqrt{k^2-4}}{2},\quad k\in\mathbb Z,\ |k|\ge2}$.
