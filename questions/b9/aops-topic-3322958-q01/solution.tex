The other intersection with the $x$-axis is $B=(12,0)$, so $AB=8$. The equations for $C=(x,y)$ are $(x-8)^2+(y+3)^2=25$ and $(x-4)^2+y^2=64$. Subtracting gives $4x-3y=48$. Substitute $x=12+\dfrac{3}{4}y$ into the second equation: $\dfrac{25}{16}y^2+12y=0$. The root $y=0$ gives $B$; the other is $y=-\dfrac{192}{25}$, with $x=\dfrac{156}{25}$. Their sum is $-\dfrac{36}{25}=\boxed{-1.44}$.

\end{asy}
