One of three cats scratched a couch. Two of the cats lie and one tells the truth.
\begin{itemize}
\item Red Cat: ``I'm innocent! It was Blue Cat!''
\item Blue Cat: ``Yes, I scratched that couch.''
\item Green Cat: ``I saw Red Cat do it.''
\end{itemize}
Which cat scratched the couch?
