A circle has centre $O$ and radius $33$ cm, and $AB$ and $CD$ are perpendicular diameters. Circles are drawn on $AO$ and $OB$ as diameters. Two further circles have their centres on $CD$ and are tangent to all three of the circles already drawn. Find the radius of one of these two circles.
