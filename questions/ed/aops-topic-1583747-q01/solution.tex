Half of the $60$ students play basketball, so the Basketball team has $30$ players. The ratio $4:3:2$ then gives Mathletics $40$ and Soccer $20$.

Let $x$ be the number of students on exactly two teams. Count team memberships: the team sizes add to $40+30+20=90$, and in this sum a student on one team is counted once, a student on exactly two teams twice, and a student on all three teams three times. With $y$ students on exactly one team,
\[
y+x+8=60,\qquad y+2x+3\cdot8=90 .
\]
Subtracting, $x+16=30$, so $x=\boxed{14}$.

\emph{The error in the attempt $40+30+20-8-x=60$:} each student on exactly two teams is counted twice in $90$, so $x$ is the right correction for them, but each of the $8$ students on all three teams is counted three times and must be subtracted twice, not once. The correct equation is $90-x-2\cdot8=60$, giving $x=14$ again.
