Let $F$ be the total price of the $27$ free items and $P$ the total price of the other $33$. Since every free item is no more expensive than every paid item, their average prices satisfy $\dfrac{F}{27}\le \dfrac{P}{33}$. Hence
\[
\dfrac{F}{F+P}\le\dfrac{27}{60}=45\%.
\]
The bound is attained when all $60$ items have the same positive price. Thus the maximum is $\boxed{45\%}$, and it cannot be exceeded.
