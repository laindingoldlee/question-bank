With the wind blowing from Chicago towards Boston, the ground speed is $500+100=600$ mph on the way to Boston and $500-100=400$ mph on the way back. The times are
\[
\dfrac{1000}{600}=\dfrac{5}{3}\text{ h}\quad\text{and}\quad\dfrac{1000}{400}=\dfrac{5}{2}\text{ h},
\]
for a total of $\dfrac{5}{3}+\dfrac{5}{2}=\boxed{\dfrac{25}{6}\text{ hours}}$, i.e. $4$ hours $10$ minutes (longer than the $4$ hours with no wind).
