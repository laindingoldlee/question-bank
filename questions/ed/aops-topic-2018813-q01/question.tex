On a day with no wind, an airplane flies the $2000$-mile round trip from Chicago to Boston and back (so $1000$ miles each way) at a constant airspeed of $500$ mph. How long would the same round trip take if, during the whole flight, a $100$ mph wind blows in the direction from Chicago to Boston?
