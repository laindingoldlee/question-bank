At $h$ hours and $m$ minutes after twelve, the signed difference between the hands is $5.5m-30h$ degrees. During the digital hour $5$, this ranges from $-150^\circ$ to, but not including, $180^\circ$. It is never $\pm180^\circ$ there. In each of the other eleven displayed hours, exactly one opposite-hand time occurs; these are the eleven times $t=\dfrac{360+720k}{11}$ minutes after twelve for $k=0,\ldots,10$. Thus the forbidden displayed hour is $\boxed5$.
