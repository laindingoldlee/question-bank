Let $ABC$ be a triangle, and let $D$ and $E$ be points on the sides $\overline{AB}$ and $\overline{AC}$ (different from the vertices) such that $\dfrac{AD}{AE}=\dfrac{BD}{EC}$. Prove that $\overline{DE}$ is parallel to $\overline{BC}$.
