This is a geometric series with first term $5^{20}$, ratio $5$ and $81$ terms, so
\[
5^{20}+5^{21}+\dots+5^{100}=\dfrac{5^{101}-5^{20}}{5-1}=\boxed{\dfrac{5^{101}-5^{20}}{4}}=\dfrac{5^{20}\left(5^{81}-1\right)}{4}.
\]
