Centre the square at the origin. $P=(u,v)$ is in all four circles iff $(|u|+3)^2+(|v|+3)^2\le25$. In the quadrant $u,v\ge0$, this region has area $\displaystyle\int_0^1\left(\sqrt{25-(u+3)^2}-3\right)du=\dfrac{25}2\left(\arcsin\dfrac45-\arcsin\dfrac35\right)-3$.

Since $\arcsin\dfrac45-\arcsin\dfrac35=\arcsin\dfrac7{25}$, the region has total area $50\arcsin\dfrac7{25}-12$, and the probability is
\[\boxed{\dfrac{25\arcsin\dfrac7{25}-6}{18}}\approx0.0608.\]
