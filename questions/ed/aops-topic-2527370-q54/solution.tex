$9!+8!+7!=7!(72+8+1)=7!\cdot81$. Since $7!=2^4\cdot3^2\cdot5\cdot7$, we get $9!+8!+7!=2^4\cdot3^6\cdot5\cdot7$. The largest square divisor takes each prime to the largest even exponent: $2^4\cdot3^6$, whose square root is $2^2\cdot3^3=\boxed{108}$.
