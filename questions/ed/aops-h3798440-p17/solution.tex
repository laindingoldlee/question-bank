A selection lacking a boy and girl from different grades either contains only one sex or has all students from one grade. Indeed, once it contains both a boy and a girl, every boy must match every girl's grade. The one-sex totals are 33 boys and 35 girls; the grade totals are 19, 25, and 24. Thus a bad selection has at most 35 students, and choosing all girls attains that. One more guarantees the desired pair, so $\boxed{N=36}$.
