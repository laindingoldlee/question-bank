Since $2007=9\cdot223$, there are $223$ complete rounds. Every person receives one coin per round, hence $\boxed{223}$ coins each. With A numbered zero through I numbered eight, round $r$ starts at $-(r-1)$ modulo nine and ends at $-r$ modulo nine. For $r=223$, the last position is $-223\equiv2\pmod9$, namely $\boxed C$.
