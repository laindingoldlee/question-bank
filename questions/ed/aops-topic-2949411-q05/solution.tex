The composition $h=f\circ g$ is $h(x)=\dfrac{x}{x+1}$, so $\dfrac{1}{h(x)}=\dfrac{1}{x}+1$. There are 1009 copies of $h$ applied after the first $f(2019)=-\dfrac{1}{2017}$. Its reciprocal increases from $-2017$ by 1009 to $-1008$. Hence the result is $\boxed{-\dfrac{1}{1008}}$. All intermediate reciprocals remain negative and nonzero, so every operation is defined.
