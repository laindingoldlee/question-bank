Joining the centre $O$ to the nine vertices divides the nonagon into $9$ congruent isosceles triangles with two sides $7$ and apex angle $\dfrac{360^\circ}{9}=40^\circ$. Each has area $\dfrac12\cdot7\cdot7\sin40^\circ$, so the nonagon has area
\[9\cdot\dfrac{49}{2}\sin40^\circ=\boxed{\dfrac{441}{2}\sin40^\circ}\approx141.7.\]

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real R=7;
pair O=(0,0);
pair V(int k){return R*dir(90+40k);}
fill(O--V(0)--V(1)--cycle,rgb(0.8,0.88,1));
draw(circle(O,R),gray(0.5));
guide g; for(int k=0;k<9;++k) g=g--V(k); draw(g--cycle,linewidth(0.8));
draw(O--V(0)); draw(O--V(1));
draw(arc(O,1.3,90,130)); label("$40^\circ$",2.75*dir(110),fontsize(8pt));
dot(O); label("$O$",O,S);
label("$7$",V(0)/2,E);
\end{asy}
