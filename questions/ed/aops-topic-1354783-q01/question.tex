In how many ways can $8$ mutually non-attacking rooks be placed on a $9\times9$ chessboard (coloured in the usual way) so that all $8$ rooks stand on squares of the same colour? (Two rooks attack each other if they are in the same row or column. Placements that differ by a rotation or reflection of the board count as different.)
