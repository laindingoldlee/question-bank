If $q\in A\cap B$, then $q$ is prime and $q=n-p$ for some prime $p$, so $n=p+q$. Conversely such a representation makes $q\in A\cap B$. Examples are $6=3+3$, $8=3+5$, $10=3+7$, $12=5+7$. The assertion for every even $n>2$ is the strong Goldbach conjecture; this exercise establishes the equivalence and the listed finite cases rather than claiming a general proof.
