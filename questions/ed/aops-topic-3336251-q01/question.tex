For even $n>2$, let $A$ be the primes less than $n$ and $B=\{n-p:p\in A\}$. Prove that $A\cap B\ne\varnothing$ is equivalent to expressing $n$ as a sum of two primes. Verify the condition for $n=6,8,10,12$.
