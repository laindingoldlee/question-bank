Let the distance be $d$ km. Walking takes $\dfrac {d}{5}$ hours and the bus $\dfrac {d}{30}$ hours; the difference is $7+13=20$ minutes $=\dfrac{1}{3}$ hour. So $\dfrac {d}{5}-\dfrac {d}{30}=\dfrac {d}{6}=\dfrac{1}{3}$, and $d=\boxed{2\text{ km}}$. (Walking takes $24$ minutes, the bus $4$ minutes.)
