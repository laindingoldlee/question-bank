Put $t=|y|\ge0$, so $x=w+t$ and $2t^2+2wt+w^2-50=0$. If $|w|<\sqrt{50}$, the product of the two roots is negative, so exactly one root is positive, giving two points with opposite nonzero $y$. For $-10<w<-\sqrt{50}$ both roots are positive, giving four points; outside $[-10,\sqrt{50}]$ there are none. The boundary values $-10,-\sqrt{50},\sqrt{50}$ give respectively two, three, and one points, and have probability zero. Thus the desired interval has length $2\sqrt{50}$ out of $40$. The probability is $\dfrac{\sqrt2}{4}\approx\boxed{0.35}$.
