For $n=27=3^3$, we have $n^n=3^{81}$ and $n^{n^n}=(3^3)^{3^{81}}=3^{3^{82}}$. The function $n^{n^n}$ is strictly increasing for positive integers $n$, because both its base and exponent increase once $n\ge1$. Thus this solution is unique, and $\boxed{27}$.
