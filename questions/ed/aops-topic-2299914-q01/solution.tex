For every integer $k\ge0$,
\[k\cdot k!=(k+1)\cdot k!-k!=(k+1)!-k!.\]
With $k=i-1$ this says $(i-1)(i-1)!=i!-(i-1)!$. So the sum telescopes:
\[\sum_{i=1}^{n}(i-1)(i-1)!=\big(1!-0!\big)+\big(2!-1!\big)+\cdots+\big(n!-(n-1)!\big)=n!-0!=n!-1.\]
Adding $1$ gives $1+\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{n}(i-1)(i-1)!=n!$. $\blacksquare$
