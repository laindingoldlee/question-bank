A circle has centre $O=(m,n)$, and the point $B=(m+5,\,n+12)$ lies on the circle. Point $C$ lies inside the circle such that triangle $OBC$ is a right triangle and $OC$ is parallel to the $x$-axis. Line $l$ is tangent to the circle at $B$, and line $t$ is the line through $C$ parallel to $l$. Let $x$ be the distance between the parallel lines $l$ and $t$, and let $y$ be the slope of $l$. Find $xy$.
