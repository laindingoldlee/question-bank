Two boys and four girls are officers of the Math Club. For the yearbook photo, the photographer asks the six officers and the faculty sponsor to sit in a row of seven chairs, with the faculty sponsor in the middle chair and the two boys not next to one another. How many different seating arrangements are possible?

(A student computes $720-24\cdot4=624$. Find the error.)
