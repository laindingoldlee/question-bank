With the sponsor fixed in chair $4$, the six officers fill chairs $1,2,3,5,6,7$ in $6!=720$ ways. The boys are next to one another when they occupy one of the adjacent pairs of chairs $\{1,2\}$, $\{2,3\}$, $\{5,6\}$, $\{6,7\}$ (chairs $3$ and $5$ are separated by the sponsor): $4$ pairs, $2$ orders of the boys and $4!=24$ arrangements of the girls, giving $4\cdot2\cdot24=192$ arrangements. Hence the answer is
\[
720-192=\boxed{528}.
\]
The student's count of the bad arrangements, $24\cdot4$, forgets that the two boys can sit in either order within an adjacent pair, so it must be doubled.
