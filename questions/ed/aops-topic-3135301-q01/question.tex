A cube of side 3 inches has a centred square tunnel of side 1 inch drilled through each pair of opposite faces, parallel to the cube edges. Twenty-seven identical drilled cubes are assembled as a $3\times3\times3$ cube with all tunnels aligned. Find the total exposed surface area, including tunnel walls.
