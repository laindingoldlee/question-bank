A machine begins with 5 bears,3 dragons,22 salamanders. Each day a player removes four animals one at a time, always choosing a type currently most numerous. Every evening three animals are added to the currently least numerous type. Ties prefer bears, then dragons, then salamanders. Which type first reaches zero during a removal?
