Together they do
\[\dfrac18+\dfrac19+\dfrac1{10}+\dfrac1{11}+\dfrac1{12}=\dfrac{495+440+396+360+330}{3960}=\dfrac{2021}{3960}\]
of the job per hour (here $3960$ is the least common multiple of $8,9,10,11,12$). So the job takes $\dfrac{3960}{2021}$ hours. Since $2021=43\cdot47$ and $3960=2^3\cdot3^2\cdot5\cdot11$ have no common prime factor, the fraction is in lowest terms, and $b=\boxed{2021}$.
