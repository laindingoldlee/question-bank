Anna, Bella, Cathy, Dorothy and Elina can each do a certain job alone in $8$, $9$, $10$, $11$ and $12$ hours respectively. Working together at these rates, they finish the job in $\dfrac ab$ hours, where $a$ and $b$ are relatively prime positive integers. What is $b$?
