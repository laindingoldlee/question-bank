Let $ABCD$ be a square with side length $3$. Let $E$ be the point on side $AD$ with $AE=2$ and $DE=1$, and let $F$ be the point on side $AB$ such that triangle $CEF$ is right with hypotenuse $CF$. The value of $CF^2$ is $\dfrac mn$ for relatively prime positive integers $m,n$. Compute $m+n$.
