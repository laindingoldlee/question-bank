Let the edges be $a$, $b$, $c$, so the faces have areas $ab$, $bc$, $ac$, each appearing twice.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
real a=8, bb=6, c=12; pair dx=(1,0), dy=(0.45,0.32), dz=(0,1);
pair p(real x,real y,real z){return x*dx+y*dy+z*dz;}
draw(p(0,0,0)--p(a,0,0)--p(a,0,c)--p(0,0,c)--cycle,linewidth(0.9));
draw(p(0,0,c)--p(0,bb,c)--p(a,bb,c)--p(a,0,c),linewidth(0.9));
draw(p(a,0,0)--p(a,bb,0)--p(a,bb,c),linewidth(0.9));
draw(p(0,0,0)--p(0,bb,0)--p(a,bb,0),dashed); draw(p(0,bb,0)--p(0,bb,c),dashed);
label("$a$",p(a/2,0,0),S); label("$b$",p(a,bb/2,0),SE); label("$c$",p(0,0,c/2),W);
label("$ac$",p(a/2,0,c/2)); label("$ab$",p(a/2,bb/2,c)); label("$bc$",p(a,bb/2,c/2));
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

Label the edges so that $ab=48$, $bc=72$ and $ac=96$. Multiplying,
\[(abc)^2=48\cdot72\cdot96=331776=576^2,\qquad abc=576 .\]
Hence $c=\dfrac{576}{48}=12$, $a=\dfrac{576}{72}=8$ and $b=\dfrac{576}{96}=6$. The edges are $\boxed{6,\ 8\text{ and }12}$. (Check: $6\cdot8=48$, $6\cdot12=72$, $8\cdot12=96$.)
