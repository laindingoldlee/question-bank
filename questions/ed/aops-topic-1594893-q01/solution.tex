\textbf{Complementary counting.} It stays dry on a given day with probability $\dfrac{3}{4}$, so it stays dry on all five days with probability $\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^5=\dfrac{243}{1024}$. Hence
\[
P(\text{rain at least once})=1-\dfrac{243}{1024}=\boxed{\dfrac{781}{1024}}\approx0.763 .
\]

\textbf{Direct counting.} Add the probabilities of rain on exactly $k$ days, $k=1,\dots,5$:
\[
\sum_{k=1}^{5}\dbinom5k\left(\dfrac{1}{4}\right)^k\left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^{5-k}=\dfrac{5\cdot81+10\cdot27+10\cdot9+5\cdot3+1}{1024}=\dfrac{405+270+90+15+1}{1024}=\dfrac{781}{1024}.
\]
Both methods agree; the complementary count is much shorter.
