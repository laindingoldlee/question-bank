For $n=a^xb^y$, the integers in $1,\dots,n$ not coprime to $n$ are those divisible by $a$ or by $b$. By inclusion--exclusion,
\[\varphi(n)=n-\dfrac {n}{a}-\dfrac {n}{b}+\dfrac {n}{ab}=n\left(1-\dfrac{1}{a}\right)\left(1-\dfrac{1}{b}\right)=a^{x-1}b^{y-1}(a-1)(b-1).\]
(Counting up to $n$ or below $n$ is the same, since $n$ itself is not coprime to $n$.) The same formula with $x+1$, $y+1$ gives
\[\varphi\left(a^{x+1}b^{y+1}\right)=a^{x}b^{y}(a-1)(b-1)=ab\cdot a^{x-1}b^{y-1}(a-1)(b-1)=ab\,c.\qquad\blacksquare\]
