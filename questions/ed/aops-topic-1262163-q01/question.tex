Let $a$ and $b$ be distinct primes and $x,y$ positive integers, and let $c=\varphi\left(a^xb^y\right)$ be the number of positive integers less than $a^xb^y$ that are relatively prime to it. Prove that the number of positive integers less than $a^{x+1}b^{y+1}$ that are relatively prime to it is $abc$.
