For $x>2$, the coprime residues pair as $r,x-r$, so $m=\varphi(x)$ is even and at least $2$. At most half the integers up to an even $m$ are coprime to it, so $\varphi(m)\le\dfrac{m}{2}\le\dfrac{x-1}{2}$. The equation then implies $x-3\le\dfrac{x-1}{2}$, or $x\le5$. Direct checking $x=1,2,3,4,5$ leaves exactly $4,5$, since $\varphi(\varphi(4))=\varphi(2)=1$ and $\varphi(\varphi(5))=\varphi(4)=2$. Their sum is $\boxed9$.
