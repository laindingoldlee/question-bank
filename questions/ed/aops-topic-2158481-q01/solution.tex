The profit is $1-1.3=-0.3$ cents on each bloorg (a loss) and $5-4.1=0.9$ cents on each wobbler. Let each part of the ratio be $p$ coins.

Last year the mint made $4p$ bloorgs and $5p$ wobblers, for a profit of $4p(-0.3)+5p(0.9)=3.3p$ cents. This equals $\$330=33000$ cents, so $p=10000$.

This year it made $9p$ bloorgs and $6p$ wobblers, for a profit of
\[9p(-0.3)+6p(0.9)=2.7p=27000\text{ cents}=\boxed{\$270}.\]
