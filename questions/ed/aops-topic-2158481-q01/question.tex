A mint spends $1.3$ cents to make a bloorg, which is worth $1$ cent, and $4.1$ cents to make a wobbler, which is worth $5$ cents. The mint's profit on a coin is its value minus its cost. Last year the mint made bloorgs and wobblers in the ratio $4:5$ and made a total profit of $\$330$. This year it made them in the ratio $9:6$, with the same number of coins in each part of the ratio as last year. What was the mint's profit this year?
