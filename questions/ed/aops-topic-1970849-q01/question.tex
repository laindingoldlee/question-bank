Let $M$ be a point inside triangle $ABC$. The lines $AM$, $BM$, $CM$ meet $BC$, $CA$, $AB$ at $A_1$, $B_1$, $C_1$. Let $A_2$, $B_2$, $C_2$ be the midpoints of $B_1C_1$, $C_1A_1$, $A_1B_1$. The lines $MA_2$, $MB_2$, $MC_2$ meet $BC$, $CA$, $AB$ at $A_3$, $B_3$, $C_3$. Prove that the lines $AA_3$, $BB_3$, $CC_3$ are concurrent.
