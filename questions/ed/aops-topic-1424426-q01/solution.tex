The second hose runs from 4:00 P.M.\ to midnight, $8$ hours $=480$ minutes, supplying $8\cdot480=3840$ gallons. The first hose therefore supplied $7740-3840=3900$ gallons, which takes $\dfrac{3900}{5}=780$ minutes $=13$ hours. Starting at 9:00 A.M., it was turned off at $\boxed{\text{10:00 P.M.}}$ (which is after 4:00 P.M., as required).
