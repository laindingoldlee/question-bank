At 9:00 A.M.\ an empty water tank begins to be filled through a hose at $5$ gallons per minute. Seven hours later a second hose also starts to fill the tank, at $8$ gallons per minute. Some time later the first hose is turned off, but the second hose keeps running. At midnight the $7740$-gallon tank is exactly full. At what time was the first hose turned off?
