The geometric sum is $2^{101}-1$. The exact value is $2535301200456458802993406410751$, which lies between $10^{30}$ and $10^{31}$. Consequently it has $\boxed{31}$ digits. Alternatively, $2^{100}=1267650600228229401496703205376$ gives the same bounds after doubling and subtracting 1.
