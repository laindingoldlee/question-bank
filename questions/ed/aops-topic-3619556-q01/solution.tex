For any integer $n$ in the stated range, use $n$ copies of $\dfrac{5}{5}$ and add them: $\underbrace{\dfrac{5}{5}+\cdots+\dfrac{5}{5}}_{n\text{ terms}}=n$. This construction works for all $100$ requested integers.
