The exponential function has the power series
\[e^x=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\dfrac{x^n}{n!},\]
valid for every real $x$. Putting $x=1$ gives $\displaystyle\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\dfrac1{n!}=\boxed{e}\approx2.71828$.

(The series converges quickly: for $n\ge1$, $n!\ge2^{n-1}$, so the sum is at most $1+\left(1+\dfrac12+\dfrac14+\cdots\right)=3$, and its first three terms already give $2.5$.)
