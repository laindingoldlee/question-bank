Let current ages be $V,H,R$. The first statement gives $V-12=2(H-12)$, or $V=2H-12$. The second gives $H+7=13+\dfrac{V+7}{3}$, or $V=3H-25$. Thus $H=13,V=14$. Finally $R-3=(14-3)+(13-3)=21$, so $\boxed{R=24}$.
