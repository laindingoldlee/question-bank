Arrange the other three people in $(3-1)!=2$ circular orders. The three restricted people must occupy the three gaps, one in each, in $3!=6$ ways. This is necessary because each needs separation and sufficient because each gap is bounded by unrestricted people. The answer is $\boxed{12}$.
