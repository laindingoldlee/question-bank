Two balls with labels at least $10$ cannot be adjacent ($10\cdot10=100$). So the ``large'' balls (labels $\ge10$) are separated by ``small'' balls ($1$ to $9$). With at most $9$ small balls there are at most $9$ large ones, so at most $18$ balls.

$18$ is possible: around the circle place
\[1,\ 18,\ 2,\ 17,\ 3,\ 16,\ 4,\ 15,\ 5,\ 14,\ 6,\ 13,\ 7,\ 12,\ 8,\ 10,\ 9,\ 11\]
(and back to $1$). The neighbouring products are $18,36,34,51,48,64,60,75,70,84,78,91,84,96,80,90,99,11$, all less than $100$. The maximum is $\boxed{18}$.
