A normal vector to the line is $(3,4)$. The perpendicular foot from $ O=(7,5)$ is $ O-t(3,4)$. Substitution into the line gives $ 3(7-3t)+4(5-4t)=16 $, hence $ 41-25t=16 $ and $ t=1 $. Therefore the contact point is $\boxed{(4,1)}$.
\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(7cm);
pair O=(7,5),T=(4,1);draw(circle(O,5));draw((-1,4.75)--(9,-2.75));draw(O--T,blue);dot(T);label("$(7,5)$",O,N);label("$(4,1)$",T,SW);label("$3x+4y=16$",(-1,4.75),W);
\end{asy}
