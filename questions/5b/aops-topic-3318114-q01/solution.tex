Write $N=50k$, so its divisor count must be $k$. The candidate $1800=2^3 3^2 5^2$ has $(3+1)(2+1)(2+1)=36$ divisors and equals $50\cdot36$. To prove minimality, it suffices to check $k=1,2,\ldots,35$. Factoring the corresponding $50k$ gives divisor counts, in order,\[6,9,12,12,8,18,12,15,18,12,12,24,12,18,16,18,12,27,12,16,24,18,12,30,10,18,24,24,12,24,12,21,24,18,16.\]None equals its index. Hence $\boxed{N=1800}$. This finite list follows by increasing the exponent of $2$ and $5$ from the factorization $50=2\cdot5^2$, and multiplying one more than each prime exponent.
