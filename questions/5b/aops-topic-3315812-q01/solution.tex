A decimal integer is congruent modulo 9 to the sum of its digits, and concatenation is congruent to the sum of the concatenated integers. Thus $a_k\equiv1+3+\cdots+(2k-1)=k^2\pmod9$. This is zero exactly when $3\mid k$. The divisible terms have indices $3,6,9,\ldots$, so the twenty-third is $\boxed{m=69}$.
