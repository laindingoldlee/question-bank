Three cars leave town $A$ for town $B$ one after another, at equal time intervals, and each car travels at its own constant speed for the whole journey. All three reach $B$ at the same moment and continue to town $C$, which is $240$ miles beyond $B$. The first car arrives at $C$ one hour after the second car. The third car, on reaching $C$, immediately turns back towards $B$, and it meets the first car $80$ miles from $C$. What is the difference between the speeds of the first and the third car, in miles per hour?

$\textbf{(A)}\ 60\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ 20\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ 40\qquad\textbf{(D)}\ 80\qquad\textbf{(E)}\ 35$
