The person $P$ with $14$ handshakes shook with everyone, so the six people with $1$ handshake shook only with $P$. The person $Q$ with $8$ handshakes shook with $P$ and $7$ others, none of whom have $1$ handshake. These are exactly the remaining $7$ people: the five ``$2$--$4$'' people and two others $X,Y$.

$X,Y$ have degrees not in $\{1,2,3,4,8,14\}$, so each has at least $5$; they have $2$ each from $P,Q$ and need $3$ more among the other $6$ of these $7$ people. Counting the extra edges among the $7$: if $XY$ is an edge, at least $1+2+2=5$ edges; if not, at least $6$. So the degree sum is at least $6+14+8+7\cdot2+2\cdot5=52$.

This is attained: $X$ and $Y$ shake with each other and each with two different people of the group (group degrees $4,4,2,2,2$). The minimum is $\dfrac{52}2=\boxed{26}$.
