In a room of $15$ people, each shakes hands with a positive number of others, at most once per pair. Exactly $6$ people shook $1$ hand, exactly $5$ shook between $2$ and $4$ hands, exactly $1$ shook $8$ hands and exactly $1$ shook $14$ hands. What is the least possible number of handshakes?
