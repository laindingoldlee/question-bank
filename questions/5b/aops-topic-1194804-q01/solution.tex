Count the ways to write $21$ and $17$ as sums of $2$, $3$, $6$, $7$ (order irrelevant). Let $w(r)$ be the number of ways to write $r\ge0$ as $2a+3b$ with $a,b\ge0$; checking the possible $b$ (which must have the parity of $r$) gives
\[w(0)=1,\ w(1)=0,\ w(2)=w(3)=w(4)=w(5)=1,\ w(7)=1,\ w(8)=w(9)=w(10)=w(11)=2,\ w(14)=w(15)=w(17)=3,\ w(21)=4.\]
Now choose the number $c$ of $7$s and $d$ of $6$s and let $r$ be the rest.

\emph{Score $21$:} $c=0$: $r=21,15,9,3$ give $4+3+2+1=10$; $c=1$: $r=14,8,2$ give $3+2+1=6$; $c=2$: $r=7,1$ give $1+0=1$; $c=3$: $r=0$ gives $1$. Total $\boxed{18}$.

\emph{Score $17$:} $c=0$: $r=17,11,5$ give $3+2+1=6$; $c=1$: $r=10,4$ give $2+1=3$; $c=2$: $r=3$ gives $1$. Total $\boxed{10}$.

The two teams' scoring is independent, so there are $18\cdot10=\boxed{180}$ combinations for the game.
