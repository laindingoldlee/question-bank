In football a team can score $2$ points (safety), $3$ points (field goal), $6$ points (touchdown) or $7$ points (touchdown plus the extra point). A game ends $21$ to $17$. Counting only how many scores of each kind each team made (not their order), how many different combinations of scoring plays could have produced this final score?
