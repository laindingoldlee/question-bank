From an exterior point $A$, a tangent to a sphere touches it at $X$ with $AX=\sqrt{2017}$. A secant ray from $A$ meets the sphere at $Y,Z$ in that order, and $AY,AZ$ are positive integers. Across all configurations, find the sum of the distinct possible values of $AZ-AY$.
