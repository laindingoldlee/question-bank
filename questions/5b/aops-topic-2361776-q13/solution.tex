The segment is the sector $OAB$ minus the triangle $OAB$, where $O$ is the centre.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair O=(0,0), A=6*dir(30), B=6*dir(150);
fill(arc(O,6,30,150)--cycle,rgb(0.85,0.92,1));
draw(circle(O,6)); draw(A--B,linewidth(0.9)); draw(O--A,dashed); draw(O--B,dashed);
draw(arc(O,1,30,150),red); label("$120^\circ$",O+1.8*dir(90),red+fontsize(8pt));
dot(O); dot(A); dot(B); label("$O$",O,S); label("$A$",A,E); label("$B$",B,W); label("$6$",(O+A)/2,SE);
\end{asy}

The sector is $\dfrac{120}{360}=\dfrac13$ of the disc, with area $\dfrac13\cdot36\pi=12\pi$. The triangle has two sides $6$ with included angle $120^\circ$, so its area is $\dfrac12\cdot6\cdot6\sin120^\circ=9\sqrt3$. The segment has area
\[\boxed{12\pi-9\sqrt3}\approx22.11.\]
