If $A$ ended in $1$, then $A+51$ would end in $2$ and $A-38$ in $3$, neither a square. That would make two statements false, impossible. Thus statement 2 is false and the two square statements are true. Write $u^2=A+51$, $v^2=A-38$. Then $(u-v)(u+v)=89$, so primality gives $u-v=1$, $u+v=89$, hence $u=45,v=44$. Thus $A=2025-51=1974$ and its digit sum is $\boxed{21}$.
