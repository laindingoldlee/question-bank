A jar contains $45$ marbles, each green, blue or red. There are twice as many green marbles as blue marbles, and $5$ more red marbles than blue marbles. Marbles are added to the jar (none are removed) so that the ratio of red to blue to green becomes $1:2:1$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] Can this be done by adding marbles of only one colour?
\item[(b)] What is the smallest total number of marbles that must be added?
\end{enumerate}
