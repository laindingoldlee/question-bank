The region $|x|+|y|\le9$ is a square with vertices $(\pm9,0)$ and $(0,\pm9)$; its diagonals have length $18$, so its area is $\dfrac{18\cdot18}{2}=162$.

The condition $-5\le x\le4$ cuts off two triangles:
\begin{itemize}
\item the part with $x>4$: vertices $(9,0)$, $(4,5)$, $(4,-5)$, area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot10\cdot5=25$;
\item the part with $x<-5$: vertices $(-9,0)$, $(-5,4)$, $(-5,-4)$, area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot8\cdot4=16$.
\end{itemize}

\end{asy}

So the area is $162-25-16=\boxed{121}$.

A shortcut for similar questions: at abscissa $x$ the region has height $2(9-|x|)$, a linear function on each side of $0$, so the area is a sum of trapezoids: $\dfrac{(8+18)}{2}\cdot5+\dfrac{(18+10)}{2}\cdot4=65+56=121$.
