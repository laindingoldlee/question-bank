Write the numbers as $2^j$ for $j=-3,-2,-1,0,1,2,3$. A product is in the set exactly when the two distinct exponents sum between-3 and 3. There are $\binom 72=21$ pairs total. Sums above 3 occur for $(1,3),(2,3)$, and sums below-3 occur for $(-3,-1),(-3,-2)$. Excluding these four pairs leaves $\boxed{17}$.
