An odd divisor count forces a square. Write $n=\prod p_i^{2e_i}$. Its number of square divisors is $\prod(e_i+1)$; for this to be odd each $e_i$ must be even. Thus every positive exponent is at least $4$. There must be an odd, hence nonzero, number of distinct primes, and the smallest allowed prime is $3$. The least candidate is $3^4=\boxed{81}$; it has $5$ divisors, one distinct prime factor, and three square divisors.
