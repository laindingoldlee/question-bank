At a math dinner there are $15$ possible dishes: $5$ vegetable dishes, $4$ protein dishes, $3$ snacks (one of which is Mentos), $2$ drinks (one of which is Coke) and $1$ ice cream. A meal consists of $4$, $5$ or $6$ different dishes. Each meal must contain at least one vegetable dish and at most two protein dishes, and it may not contain both Coke and Mentos. How many possible meals are there?
