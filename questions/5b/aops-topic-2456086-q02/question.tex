Jonny and three friends eat at a restaurant, and the total bill is $\$120.60$. If Jonny had brought his money, the four of them would have split the bill equally. But Jonny forgot his money, so his three friends split the bill equally, and Jonny promised to pay each of them $\$5.60$ afterwards. Does Jonny pay more or less than he would have paid if he had brought his money, and by how much?
