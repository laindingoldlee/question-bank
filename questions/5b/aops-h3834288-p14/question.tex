Cindy and Zadie are playing a game. There are three piles of candies, $A$, $B$, $C$, each with $a$, $b$, $c$ candies respectively where $1\leq a,b,c\leq 8$. Cindy begins the game and each turn, a player chooses a pile and eats any positive number of candies in that pile. The player who eats the last remaining candy loses. What is the number of triples $(a,b,c)$ such that Cindy can guarantee a win no matter how Zadie plays?
