Group the two fractions with denominator $25$:
\[
\dfrac{8668-5533}{25}=\dfrac{3135}{25}=125+\dfrac{10}{25}=125+\dfrac{2}{5},\qquad \dfrac{4141}{9}=460+\dfrac{1}{9}
\]
(only the remainders matter: $3135=25\cdot125+10$ and $4141=9\cdot460+1$). The fractional parts add to $\dfrac{2}{5}+\dfrac{1}{9}=\dfrac{23}{45}$, which is less than $1$, so the fractional part of the result is $\boxed{\dfrac{23}{45}}$ (the whole number is $585\dfrac{23}{45}$).
