The left side is $(a-3b)^2+(pb-3c)^2$. Thus $a=3b$ and $c=\dfrac{p}{3}b$. Dividing the side lengths by $b>0$, the triangle inequalities become
\[
|3-1|<\dfrac{p}{3}<3+1,
\]
or $6<p<12$. Hence $p=7,8,9,10,11$, and the count is $\boxed5$.
