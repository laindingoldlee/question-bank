Determine all ordered pairs $(x,y)$ of positive integers that satisfy each equation:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $\dfrac{1}{x}+\dfrac{1}{y}=\dfrac{1}{4}$;
\item[(b)] $\dfrac{1}{x}+\dfrac{1}{y}=\dfrac{1}{3}$;
\item[(c)] $\dfrac{1}{x}+\dfrac{1}{y}=\dfrac{1}{2}$.
\end{enumerate}
