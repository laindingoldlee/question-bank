Let the digits be $a,b,c\ge1$. Without the upper digit bound, $a+b+c=12$ has $\binom{11}{2}=55$ positive solutions. A digit can exceed 9 only by being 10 while the other two are 1. There are three such solutions, one for each position, and no overlap. Thus the answer is $55-3=\boxed{52}$. This is choice A.
