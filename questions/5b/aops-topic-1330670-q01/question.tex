Positive two-digit integers $a<b<c<d$ form an arithmetic sequence, in that order, and the numbers of positive divisors $\tau(a),\tau(b),\tau(c),\tau(d)$ also form an arithmetic sequence (possibly constant). Find the maximum possible value of $a+b+c+d$.
