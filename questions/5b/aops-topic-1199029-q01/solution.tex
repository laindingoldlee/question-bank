A segment parallel to the bases that divides the legs in ratio $3:4$ (from $AB$) has length equal to the weighted average of the bases, $\dfrac{4\cdot AB+3\cdot CD}{7}$. To see this, draw the diagonal $AC$ meeting $EF$ at $G$: in triangle $ABC$, $GF=\dfrac{4}{7}AB=4$, and in triangle $ACD$, $EG=\dfrac{3}{7}CD=\dfrac{30}{7}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(1,3),B=(8,3),C=(10,0),D=(0,0); pair F=B+3/7*(C-B), Ee=A+3/7*(D-A);
draw(A--B--C--D--cycle,linewidth(0.9)); draw(Ee--F,blue); label("$A$",A,NW);label("$B$",B,NE);label("$C$",C,SE);label("$D$",D,SW);label("$E$",Ee,W);label("$F$",F,E);
label("$7$",(A+B)/2,N); label("$10$",(C+D)/2,S);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

So $EF=4+\dfrac{30}{7}=\boxed{\dfrac{58}{7}}$.
