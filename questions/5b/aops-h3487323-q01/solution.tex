Choose $A=(0,0),B=(8,0),C=(8,5),D=(0,5)$. Then $E=(2,0),F=(4,5)$. The lines $BD,EF$ have equations
\[
y=5-\dfrac{5}{8}x,\qquad y=\dfrac{5}{2}(x-2),
\]
so $G=\left(\dfrac{16}{5},3\right)$. Relative to the downward vertical ray $CB$, the horizontal and vertical displacements toward $G$ are $\dfrac{24}{5}$ and $2$. Therefore
\[
\tan\angle BCG=\dfrac{\dfrac{24}{5}}{2}=\dfrac{12}{5},\qquad\boxed{p+q=17}.
\]
