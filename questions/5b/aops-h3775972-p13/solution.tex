A line $y=mx+k$ is tangent to the first parabola exactly when $k=9-\dfrac{(m-4)^2}{4}$, and to the second exactly when $k=3-\dfrac{(m+8)^2}{16}$. Equating these gives $m^2-16m-32=0$, with two distinct real slopes. Using that relation in the first formula gives $k=-3-2m$. Thus both lines have form $y=m(x-2)-3$, so they meet at $\boxed{(2,-3)}$.

\end{asy}
