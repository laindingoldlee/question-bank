Real coefficients require the conjugate roots $ 2+4i $ and $ 3-5i $ as well. All four are distinct, so the monic polynomial is \[((x-2)^2+16)((x-3)^2+25)=(x^2-4x+20)(x^2-6x+34)=\boxed{x^4-10x^3+78x^2-256x+680}.\]
