Let $\theta$ be the angle at $A$, opposite the leg $BC=936$:

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(702,0),C=(702,936);
draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(1));
real z=55; draw(B+(-z,0)--B+(-z,z)--B+(0,z));
draw(arc(A,140,0,degrees(C-A)),red);
label("$\theta$",A+205*dir(degrees(C-A)/2),red);
label("$A$",A,SW);label("$B$",B,SE);label("$C$",C,N);
label("$702$",(A+B)/2,S);label("$936$",(B+C)/2,E);label("$1170$",(A+C)/2,NW);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

The sine is the opposite leg over the hypotenuse:
\[
\sin\theta=\dfrac{936}{1170}=\dfrac{4\cdot234}{5\cdot234}=\dfrac{4}{5}=\boxed{0.8}.
\]
(The triangle is a $3$-$4$-$5$ triangle scaled by $234$; check: $702^2+936^2=492804+876096=1368900=1170^2$.)
