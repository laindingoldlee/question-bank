Since $t_2>t_1$ and the exponential map is increasing, induction shows $t_{n+1}>t_n$. Also $t_1<2$, and $t_n\le2$ implies $t_{n+1}\le(\sqrt2)^2=2$. Hence the increasing bounded sequence converges to some $L\in[\sqrt2,2]$, satisfying $\log L=\dfrac{L}{2}\log2$. The function $h(t)=\log t-\dfrac{t}{2}\log2$ has derivative $\dfrac{1}{t}-\dfrac{1}{2}\log2>0$ on this interval. Since $h(2)=0$, the unique possible limit is $\boxed2$.
