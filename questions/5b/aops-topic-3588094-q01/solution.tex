Since $21=3\cdot7$, the exponent is the lesser of the exponents of $3$ and $7$. The exponent of $7$ is $\lfloor\dfrac{2025}{7}\rfloor+\lfloor\dfrac{2025}{49}\rfloor+\lfloor\dfrac{2025}{343}\rfloor=289+41+5=335$. The exponent of $3$ is $675+225+75+25+8+2=1010$. Thus $\boxed{w=335}$.
