The volume ratio is $\dfrac{27}{8}$, so the linear scale factor is $\sqrt[3]{\dfrac{27}{8}}=\dfrac{3}{2}$. Every edge and hence the sum of edges has this ratio. Therefore $\boxed{a+b=5}$.
