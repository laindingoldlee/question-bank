The product is $2^{\dfrac{2}{3}}3^{\dfrac{1}{2}}$. For its $n$ th power to be an integer, unique prime factorization requires $\dfrac{2n}{3}$ and $\dfrac{n}{2}$ to be integers. The least such $n$ is 6, yielding $m=2^4\cdot3^3=432$. Thus $\boxed{m+n=438}$.
