Casework on the number of sixes is quickest here:
\[\dbinom53\cdot5^2+\dbinom54\cdot5+\dbinom55=250+25+1=276\]
of the $6^5=7776$ outcomes. The probability is $\dfrac{276}{7776}=\boxed{\dfrac{23}{648}}$.
