The eight numbers sum to $8$. If one group sums to $s$, the expression is $s^2+(8-s)^2=32+2(s-4)^2$. A group of four cannot sum to $4$: with zero odd entries its sum is $10$, with four odd entries it is $-2$, and with two odd entries the possible odd-pair sums $-12,-10,-8,6,8,10$ cannot combine with an even-pair sum $0,2,4,6,8,10$ to make $4$. A group $(-7,-5,2,13)$ sums to $3$, so the minimum is $32+2=\boxed{34}$.
