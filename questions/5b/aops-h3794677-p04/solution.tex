Rearrange to $x^2-(y+1)^2=91$, so $(x-y-1)(x+y+1)=91$. Each signed divisor $d$ of $91$ specifies the ordered factors $d,\dfrac{91}{d}$. Both are odd, so $x=\dfrac{d+\dfrac{91}{d}}{2}$ and $y=\dfrac{\dfrac{91}{d}-d}{2}-1$ are integers. Conversely every solution gives one such divisor. Since $91=7\cdot13$ has four positive and four negative divisors, there are $\boxed8$ solutions.
