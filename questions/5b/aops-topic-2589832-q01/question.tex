Let $a>b>0$ and $c=\sqrt{a^2-b^2}$. Prove that for every point $P$ on the ellipse
\[\dfrac{x^2}{a^2}+\dfrac{y^2}{b^2}=1,\]
the distances $d_1$ and $d_2$ from $P$ to the foci $F_1=(-c,0)$ and $F_2=(c,0)$ satisfy $d_1+d_2=2a$.
