A point is chosen at random within the square in the coordinate plane whose vertices are $(0,0)$, $(2020,0)$, $(2020,2020)$ and $(0,2020)$. The probability that the point is within $d$ units of a lattice point is $\dfrac12$. (A point $(x,y)$ is a lattice point if $x$ and $y$ are both integers.) What is $d$ to the nearest tenth?
