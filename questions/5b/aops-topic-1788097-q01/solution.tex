The difference is $90(b-c)$, so $b\equiv c\pmod7$. Also $1000a+100b+10c+d\equiv6a+2b+3c+d\equiv6a+5b+d\pmod7$. Thus choose $a\in\{1,\ldots,9\}$ and $b,c\in\{0,\ldots,9\}$ with $c=b$ or $c=b\pm7$ when that remains a digit. Then choose every digit $d$ congruent to $a+2b$ modulo $7$. Equivalently, if $r$ is the residue in $\{0,\ldots,6\}$, use $d=r$ and also $d=r+7$ when $r\le2$. These conditions are necessary by the two congruences and sufficient by substitution, so they describe exactly all requested integers.
