Factor the left side as $(a+c)(b+d)$. For each divisor $u$ of $2016$, set $a+c=u$ and $b+d=\dfrac{2016}{u}$. There are $(u-1)(\dfrac{2016}{u}-1)$ ordered choices, with the formula correctly giving zero for a factor equal to one. Thus the count is
\[
\sum_{u\mid2016}\left(2017-u-\dfrac{2016}{u}\right)
=2017\tau(2016)-2\sigma(2016).
\]
Since $2016=2^5 3^2 7$, $\tau(2016)=36$ and $\sigma(2016)=63\cdot13\cdot8=6552$. The answer is
\[
\boxed{2017\cdot36-2\cdot6552=59508}.
\]
