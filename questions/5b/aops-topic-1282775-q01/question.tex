Two circles have centres $O_1,O_2$ and radii $r_1,r_2$. For a point $X$, the power of $X$ with respect to circle $i$ is $XO_i^2-r_i^2$. Find the locus of points $X$ for which the sum of the powers of $X$ with respect to the two circles equals a given constant $k$.
