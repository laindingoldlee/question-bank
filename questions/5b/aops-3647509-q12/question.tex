In an infinite grid of squares, each square is initially colored white. Oron paints $n$ squares red and $n$ squares blue such that:

$\bullet$ No square is painted both colors.

$\bullet$ Any two red squares are connected by a path of red squares. (Any two consecutive squares in the path must be vertically or horizontally adjacent. In particular, they may not be diagonally adjacent.)

$\bullet$ Any two blue squares are connected by a path of blue squares.

$\bullet$ No red square is adjacent to a white square.

What is the minimum possible value of $n$?
