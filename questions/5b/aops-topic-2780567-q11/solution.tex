Choose the floor sitter in 6 ways and arrange the remaining five cyclically in $ 4!=24 $ ways. The count is 144. Its prime factorization $ 2^4 3^2 $ shows that the only common exponent greater than 1 is 2, so $ 144=12^2 $. Thus $\boxed{x+y=14}$.
