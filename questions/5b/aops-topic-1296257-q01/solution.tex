Trying rational roots, $x=1$ works: $2+8-13+3=0$. Dividing, $2x^3+8x^2-13x+3=(x-1)(2x^2+10x-3)$, and the quadratic formula gives $x=\dfrac{-10\pm\sqrt{100+24}}{4}=\dfrac{-5\pm\sqrt{31}}{2}$. The solutions are $\boxed{x=1,\ \dfrac{-5+\sqrt{31}}{2},\ \dfrac{-5-\sqrt{31}}{2}}$.
