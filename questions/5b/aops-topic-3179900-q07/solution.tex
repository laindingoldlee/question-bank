Thirteen consecutive years contain either three or four multiples of 4. At most one can be a century year excluded from leap status, so there are between two and four leap days. Both extremes occur:1897--1909 has two leap years, while 2000--2012 has four. All ordinary-day counts agree, so the difference is $\boxed2$.
