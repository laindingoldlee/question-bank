Mathew wants to put numbers on the toy cars in his collection (he has more than $100$ of them). He buys stickers bearing the digits $0,1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8$; a $6$ can also be used upside down as a $9$. He has $20$ stickers of each digit, $180$ stickers altogether. If he numbers his cars $1,2,3,\dots$ in order, what is the first number that he cannot make?
