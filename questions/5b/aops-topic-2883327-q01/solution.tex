Let $p=\dfrac1x$, $q=\dfrac1y$, $r=\dfrac1z$. The equations say $\dfrac1r-1=q-p$, $\dfrac1p-1=r-q$ and $\dfrac1q-1=p-r$. The system is cyclic, so we may take $p$ to be the largest of the three.

If $p\ge q\ge r$: $q-p\le0$ gives $r\ge1$, and $p-r\ge0$ gives $q\le1$. So $1\le r\le q\le1$, i.e. $q=r=1$, and then $\dfrac1p=1$.

If $p\ge r\ge q$: $q-p\le0$ gives $r\ge1$, and $r-q\ge0$ gives $p\le1$. So $1\le r\le p\le1$, and then $\dfrac1r-1=0$ forces $q=p=1$.

Hence $x=y=z=1$, and the value is $\dfrac{1-1+1}{1+1+1}=\boxed{\dfrac13}$.
