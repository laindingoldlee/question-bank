Explain the difference between permutations and combinations by proving that the number $P(n,k)$ of ordered arrangements of $k$ objects chosen from $n$ distinct objects equals $k!$ times the number $\dbinom nk$ of unordered selections. Illustrate with $n=5$ and $k=3$.
