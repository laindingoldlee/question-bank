By the AM-GM inequality for the positive numbers $x^2$ and $\dfrac{1}{x^2}$,
\[x^2+\dfrac{1}{x^2}\ge2\sqrt{x^2\cdot\dfrac{1}{x^2}}=2,\]
with equality exactly when $x^2=\dfrac{1}{x^2}$, that is, $x=\pm1$. Hence
\[32x^2+\dfrac{32}{x^2}+32=32\left(x^2+\dfrac{1}{x^2}\right)+32\ge64+32=96,\]
with equality at $x=\pm1$. The minimum value is $\boxed{96}$.
