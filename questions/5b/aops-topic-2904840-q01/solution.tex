Write the list as $1,a,b,c,5$. Then $2a=1+b+2$, $2b=a+c+2$ and $2c=b+5+2$. From the first and third equations, $a=\dfrac{b+3}2$ and $c=\dfrac{b+7}2$. Substituting, $2b=b+5+2$, so $b=7$, $a=5$ and $c=7$. The list is $1,5,7,7,5$, and its sum is $\boxed{25}$.
