A clock face is a circle of radius $20$ cm. A circular disk of radius $10$ cm is externally tangent to the clock face at $12$ o'clock, and an arrow painted on the disk initially points vertically upward. The disk rolls without slipping clockwise around the outside of the clock face. At what point on the clock face will the disk be tangent when the arrow next points vertically upward?

$\textbf{(A)}\ \text{2 o'clock}\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ \text{3 o'clock}\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ \text{4 o'clock}\qquad\textbf{(D)}\ \text{6 o'clock}\qquad\textbf{(E)}\ \text{8 o'clock}$
