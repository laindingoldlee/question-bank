Ghetty the ant will starve unless he eats within the next $5$ seconds. He smells food $1.5$ inches away and walks straight toward it. He walks $1$ inch in the first second, $\dfrac13$ inch in the next second, $\dfrac19$ inch in the third second, and so on, each second covering a third of the previous second's distance. Does Ghetty reach the food in time?
