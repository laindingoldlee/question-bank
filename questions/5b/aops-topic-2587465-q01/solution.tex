In $5$ seconds he walks
\[1+\dfrac13+\dfrac19+\dfrac1{27}+\dfrac1{81}=\dfrac{81+27+9+3+1}{81}=\dfrac{121}{81}\]
inches, which is $\dfrac{3}{2}-\dfrac{121}{81}=\dfrac{1}{162}$ inch short of $1.5$ inches. So $\boxed{\text{no}}$, he does not reach the food in time.

In fact he never reaches it: after $n$ seconds he has walked $\dfrac{1-\left(\dfrac13\right)^n}{1-\dfrac13}=\dfrac32\left(1-\dfrac{1}{3^n}\right)<\dfrac32$ inches.
