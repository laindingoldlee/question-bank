Summing the increments gives
\[
f(m,n)=1+\dfrac{m(m-1)}{2}-\dfrac{n(n-1)}{2}.
\]
Thus $(p-q)(p+q-1)=4030=2\cdot5\cdot13\cdot31$. Put $d=p-q>0$. Since $q\ge1$, the second factor is $d+2q-1>d$, so $d<\sqrt{4030}<64$. The value $d=63$ is not a divisor of $4030$, while $d=62$ has complementary factor $65$. Solving $p-q=62$, $p+q-1=65$ gives $q=2,p=64$, both positive. Therefore the maximum is $\boxed{62}$.
