Let $e$ students have blue eyes, so $3e$ have blond hair. The $40-15=25$ students with at least one of the two features are counted by inclusion--exclusion:
\[
3e+e-3=25,\qquad 4e=28,\qquad e=\boxed{7}.
\]
(So $21$ are blond, $18$ blond only, $4$ blue-eyed only and $3$ both.)
