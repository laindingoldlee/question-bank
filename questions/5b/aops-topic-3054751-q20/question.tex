Line $p$ is $y=3x+5$. Line $t$ is perpendicular to it and passes through $(1,8)$. Let $A,B$ be the $x$- and $y$-intercepts of $p$, and let $C,D$ be the $y$- and $x$-intercepts of $t$. Find the area of the simple polygon with boundary $A\to B\to C\to D\to A$.
