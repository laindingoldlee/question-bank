Let $S$ be the sum. Put
\[p_1=1,\qquad p_2=b,\qquad p_3=ab,\qquad p_4=abd,\]
all positive, and note $abcd=1$. Rewrite each term (multiplying the numerator and denominator by $1$, $b$, $ab$, $abd$ respectively):
\[\dfrac{b}{1+b+ab}=\dfrac{p_2}{p_1+p_2+p_3},\qquad \dfrac{a}{1+a+ad}=\dfrac{ab}{b+ab+abd}=\dfrac{p_3}{p_2+p_3+p_4},\]
\[\dfrac{d}{1+d+cd}=\dfrac{abd}{ab+abd+abcd}=\dfrac{p_4}{p_3+p_4+p_1},\qquad \dfrac{c}{1+c+bc}=\dfrac{abcd}{abd+abcd+abcd\cdot b}=\dfrac{p_1}{p_4+p_1+p_2}.\]
So, with $T=p_1+p_2+p_3+p_4$,
\[S=\dfrac{p_2}{p_1+p_2+p_3}+\dfrac{p_3}{p_2+p_3+p_4}+\dfrac{p_4}{p_3+p_4+p_1}+\dfrac{p_1}{p_4+p_1+p_2}.\]

\textbf{Lower bound.} Each denominator is less than $T$, so $S>\dfrac{p_2+p_3+p_4+p_1}{T}=1$.

\textbf{Upper bound.} Dropping one term from each denominator makes each fraction larger:
\[\dfrac{p_2}{p_1+p_2+p_3}+\dfrac{p_3}{p_2+p_3+p_4}<\dfrac{p_2}{p_2+p_3}+\dfrac{p_3}{p_2+p_3}=1,\]
\[\dfrac{p_4}{p_3+p_4+p_1}+\dfrac{p_1}{p_4+p_1+p_2}<\dfrac{p_4}{p_4+p_1}+\dfrac{p_1}{p_4+p_1}=1.\]
Hence $S<2$.
