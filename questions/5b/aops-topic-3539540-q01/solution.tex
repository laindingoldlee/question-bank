The product is $\prod_{j=1}^{26}(j-x)$, independent of the permutation. It is a polynomial of even degree 26 with positive leading coefficient 1, so it tends to positive infinity as x tends to positive infinity. Thus it is unbounded above and $\boxed{\text{has no largest value}}$.
