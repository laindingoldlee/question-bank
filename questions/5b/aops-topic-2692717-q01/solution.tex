By the Pythagorean theorem the hypotenuse is $\sqrt{3^2+5^2}=\boxed{\sqrt{34}}$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(3cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
pair A=(0,0),B=(5,0),C=(0,3);draw(A--B--C--cycle,linewidth(0.8));draw((0.3,0)--(0.3,0.3)--(0,0.3));
label("$5$",(A+B)/2,S);label("$3$",(A+C)/2,W);label("$\sqrt{34}$",(B+C)/2,NE);
\end{asy}
