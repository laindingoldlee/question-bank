Since $20=4\cdot5$, the $20$-point ring adds nothing new: a total is obtainable exactly when it is $5a+9b$ with $a,b\ge0$.

For coprime $5$ and $9$, the obtainable totals are all numbers from $(5-1)(9-1)=32$ on, and the largest non-obtainable one is $5\cdot9-5-9=31$. To see it directly: $31-9b$ for $b=0,1,2,3$ gives $31,22,13,4$, none a multiple of $5$, so $31$ fails; and $32=5\cdot1+9\cdot3$, $33=5\cdot3+9\cdot2$, $34=5\cdot5+9$, $35=5\cdot7$, $36=9\cdot4$ are five consecutive obtainable totals, so adding $5$s gives everything beyond. The answer is $\boxed{31}$.
