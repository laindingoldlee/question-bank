$2015=5\cdot13\cdot31$ is coprime to $6$, so $a$, $b$, $c$ are coprime to $2$ and $3$. For any $n$ coprime to $6$, $n^2\equiv1\pmod{24}$: $n^2\equiv1\pmod8$ for odd $n$, and $n^2\equiv1\pmod3$ for $3\nmid n$. Hence
\[
A\equiv19+5+1890=1914=79\cdot24+18\equiv\boxed{18}\pmod{24}.
\]
