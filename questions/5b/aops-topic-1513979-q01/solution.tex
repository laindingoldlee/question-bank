An inequality $|u|<5$ means $-5<u<5$. With $u=|x+4|-7$:
\[
-5<|x+4|-7<5\iff 2<|x+4|<12 .
\]
Now solve the two conditions:
\begin{itemize}
\item $|x+4|<12\iff-12<x+4<12\iff-16<x<8$;
\item $|x+4|>2\iff x+4>2$ or $x+4<-2\iff x>-2$ or $x<-6$.
\end{itemize}
Both must hold, so
\[
\boxed{-16<x<-6\ \text{ or }\ -2<x<8}.
\]
(Check: $x=0$ gives $|4-7|=3<5$; $x=-4$ gives $|0-7|=7$, not less than $5$; $x=-16$ gives $|12-7|=5$, excluded.)
