Odd and even labels must be paired separately. On either group of four labels, represent a mixed pair by an edge of a simple graph and an equal pair by a loop. A label has four copies, so its degree among mixed edges is either $4$ without a loop or $2$ with a loop (a loop consumes two copies). Since there are only three other labels, degree $4$ is impossible. Every label therefore has one loop and exactly two mixed edges. A simple graph on four vertices with every degree $2$ is a four-cycle. There are $\dfrac{(4-1)!}{2}=3$ such cycles. The odd and even choices are independent, giving $\boxed9$ partitions.
