All stated denominators are nonzero. Cross-multiplication and the sum/difference-of-cubes identities give
\[
x^3+y^3=1+z^3,\qquad x^3-y^3=27-z^3.
\]
Adding yields $2x^3=28$, so $\boxed{x=\sqrt[3]{14}}$. This value is attainable: take $z=0$ and $y=-\sqrt[3]{13}$; the denominators are nonzero and the two factored identities recover the original equations.
