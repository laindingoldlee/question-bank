There are $9\cdot8\cdot7\cdot6\cdot5=15120$ assignments in total. For no two assigned spaces to be adjacent, the $5$ chosen spaces must be $1,3,5,7,9$ (the number of ways to choose $5$ non-adjacent spaces from $9$ is $\dbinom{9-5+1}{5}=1$), and the workers can be arranged in them in $5!=120$ ways. So the answer is $15120-120=\boxed{15000}$, confirming the poster's work.
