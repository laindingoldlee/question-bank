We need $(2x+10)^2=4(x-5)^2=\bigl(2(x-5)\bigr)^2$, so $2x+10=\pm(2x-10)$. The plus sign gives $10=-10$, impossible. The minus sign gives $2x+10=-2x+10$, so $x=\boxed{0}$. Check: $(10)^2=100=4\cdot(-5)^2$.
