For an ordered pair, the thousands digits can split $2$ in $3$ ways, the hundreds digits split $0$ in $1$ way, the tens digits split $1$ in $2$ ways, and the units digits split $3$ in $4$ ways. The total is $3\cdot1\cdot2\cdot4=24$. Because $2013$ is odd, the summands cannot be equal. Every unordered sum is counted exactly twice, giving $\boxed{12}$.
