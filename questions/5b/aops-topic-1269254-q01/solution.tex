Count the days from $1$ January $1999$ to $18$ July $2004$. The years $1999$, $2000$, $2001$, $2002$, $2003$ contain $365+366+365+365+365=1826$ days, bringing us to $1$ January $2004$. In $2004$ (a leap year), $18$ July is day $31+29+31+30+31+30+18=200$, so it is $199$ days after $1$ January $2004$.

The total is $1826+199=2025$ days, and $2025=7\cdot289+2$. So $1$ January $1999$ is $2$ weekdays before Sunday: it was a $\boxed{\text{Friday}}$.
