For $x\le-2$ or $x\ge2$, $x^4+x^3+x^2-(x+1)^2>1$, as follows from its derivative or direct monotonicity outside that interval. Hence $-2<x<2$. The left side $(x+1)^2$ is an integer $n$, so $n=0,\ldots,8$ and $x=-1+\sqrt n$; the other branch lies outside the interval except at $n=0$. Substitution gives $x^4+x^3+x^2=n^2+4n+1-(3n+3)\sqrt n$. Its floors for $n=0,\ldots,8$ are $1,0,0,1,3,5,9,14,20$. Only $n=5$ agrees with its floor. Thus $x=-1+\sqrt5$, so $\boxed{a+b=4}$.
