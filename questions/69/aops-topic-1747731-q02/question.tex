Three friends go to a concert. Ann arrives at a time chosen uniformly at random between 8:00 and 8:30, Ben between 8:10 and 8:40, and Cal between 8:20 and 8:30, independently. Each stays for exactly $5$ minutes and then leaves. What is the probability that all three of them see each other?
