The condition says $\tau(n)$ is a prime other than two. Therefore $n=p^{r-1}$ for primes $p$ and odd primes $r$. With $n<2025$, the possible exponents are $2,4,6,10$. For exponent two there are fourteen primes $p\le44$; for four, $p=2,3,5$; for six, $p=2,3$; and for ten, $p=2$. These values are distinct by unique factorization. Their total is $14+3+2+1=\boxed{20}$.
