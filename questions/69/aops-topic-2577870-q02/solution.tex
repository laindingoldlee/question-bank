The sunny expectation is $0.95(30)+0.03(25)+0.02(20)=29.65$ million. The rainy expectation is $0.50(30)+0.20(25)+0.15(20)+0.15(10)=24.5$ million. Weighting by weather gives $0.8(29.65)+0.2(24.5)=28.62$ million, or $\boxed{28,620,000\text{ dollars}}$.
