Square $ABCD$ has side length $8$. Point $E$ is the midpoint of $AB$, and $F$ is the midpoint of $ED$. A circle with centre $F$ has radius $FD$. Line $L$ is tangent to this circle at $D$. Line $M$ passes through $F$ and is parallel to $AB$. Lines $L$ and $M$ meet at $G$. What is the length of $GF$?
