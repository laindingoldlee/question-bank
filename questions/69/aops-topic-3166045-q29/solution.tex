There must be at least four people, and the degree sum $3n$ must be even, so $n$ is even. The candidates are $4,6,8,10$. Each works: put the people on an even cycle and connect every person additionally to the opposite person. These three neighbors are distinct for all these sizes. Thus the required product is $4\cdot6\cdot8\cdot10=\boxed{1920}$.
