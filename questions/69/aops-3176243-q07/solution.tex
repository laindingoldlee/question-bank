The smaller central angle $ADC$ is $120^\circ$. On the circle centred at $D$ with radius four, the chord $AC$ subtends an inscribed angle of $60^\circ$ from its major arc. The locus of points on the same side of $AC$ as $D$ that see $AC$ under $60^\circ$ is precisely that major arc. Since concavity places $B$ on this side, $B$ lies on the circle. Thus $\boxed{BD=4}$.
