By symmetry, the second ball drawn is equally likely to be any of the $5$ balls, so the probability is $\boxed{\dfrac25}$. (Directly: $\dfrac25\cdot\dfrac14+\dfrac35\cdot\dfrac24=\dfrac{2+6}{20}=\dfrac25$.)
