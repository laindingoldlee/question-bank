Normalize the initial total to one. Water remains $0.4$, so final energy drink totals $0.8$. If $a$ cherry and $c$ blueberry are added, $0.2+a+c=0.8$ and $0.2+a=c$. Solving gives $a=0.2,c=0.4$. The final cherry total is $0.4$, half of which was added, giving $\boxed{50\%}$.
The tea conditions are consistent: final total is $T=0.4+0.8+0.4T$, so $T=2$, with tea amount $0.8$. Before the last $0.4$ blueberry addition, total is $1.6$ and tea is exactly half.
