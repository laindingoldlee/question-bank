A mixture initially contains $40\%$ water, $20\%$ cherry energy drink, and $40\%$ iced tea. Cherry drink is added, then tea until tea makes up half the mixture, then blueberry drink. Finally total energy drink is twice the water amount, tea is again $40\%$ of the mixture, and cherry and blueberry amounts are equal. What percentage of the final cherry amount was added?
