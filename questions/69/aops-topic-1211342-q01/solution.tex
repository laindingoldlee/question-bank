\emph{Lower bound.} Remove the number $1$ from the circle; the other nine numbers, read around the circle starting just after $1$, split into three blocks of three consecutive numbers. These three blocks have total $2+3+\cdots+10=54$, so one of them has sum at least $\dfrac{54}{3}=18$. Hence the largest sum is at least $18$.

\emph{Construction.} The order $1,4,9,5,3,7,8,2,6,10$ (around the circle) has consecutive-triple sums
\[14,\ 18,\ 17,\ 15,\ 18,\ 17,\ 16,\ 18,\ 17,\ 15,\]
whose maximum is $18$. So the answer is $\boxed{18}$.
