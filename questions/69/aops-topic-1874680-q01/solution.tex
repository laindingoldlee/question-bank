Let the ages be $10a+b$ and $10b+a$. Both are two-digit numbers, so $a,b$ are digits from $1$ to $9$. The difference is
\[
|(10a+b)-(10b+a)|=9|a-b| .
\]
For a positive difference $a\ne b$, so $|a-b|\ge1$ and the difference is at least $9$. It equals $9$ for ages such as $21$ and $12$ (or $34$ and $43$). The answer is $\boxed{9}$.
