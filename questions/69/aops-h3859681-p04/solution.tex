The equilateral triangle has area $36\sqrt3$. Triangle $PBC$ has area $\dfrac{1}{2}\cdot12\cdot\sqrt3=6\sqrt3$, leaving $30\sqrt3$ for $PAB,PAC$. Splitting in the ratio $2:3$ gives $S=18\sqrt3$, hence $\boxed{S^2=972}$.

texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");

\end{asy}
