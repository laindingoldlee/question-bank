Elva got $93+275=368$ items, of which a fraction
\[\dfrac{93}{368}\approx0.253\]
were chocolate. One in four is $0.25$, so $\boxed{\text{yes, about one in four}}$ ($92$ of $368$ would be exactly one in four). The ratio of chocolate to non-chocolate items is $93:275$, about $1:3$, which is the same statement.
