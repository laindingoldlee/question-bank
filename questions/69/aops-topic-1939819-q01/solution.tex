Two numbers have an even sum exactly when they have the same parity. There are $99$ balls numbered $1$--$99$ and $1020$ balls numbered $1000$--$2019$, so $1119$ balls without three-digit numbers. The $900$ three-digit balls ($100$--$999$) are $450$ even and $450$ odd.

Drawing $1121$ balls is not enough: we might get all $1119$ non-three-digit balls plus one even and one odd three-digit ball. Drawing $1122$ balls guarantees at least $1122-1119=3$ three-digit balls, and by the pigeonhole principle two of them have the same parity, so their sum is even. The answer is $\boxed{1122}$.
