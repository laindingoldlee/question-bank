The quarter-disks have disjoint interiors and together have the area of one disk of radius $\dfrac{1}{2}$, namely $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$. Thus each tile has shaded area $1-\dfrac{\pi}{4}$. There are $80$ tiles, so the total shaded area is
\[
\boxed{80-20\pi\text{ square feet}}.
\]
