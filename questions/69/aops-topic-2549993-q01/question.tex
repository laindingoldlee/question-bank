Let $ABC$ be an equilateral triangle, and let circle $O$ be inscribed in it (tangent to all three sides). Altitude $AD$ meets circle $O$ at two points, $D$ and $E$. If $AE=1$, then $AB=a\sqrt b$, where $a\ne1$ and $b$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. Find $a+b$.
