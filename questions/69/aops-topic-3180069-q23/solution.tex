The denominator factors as $(n-1)(n+2)(n+3)$, and partial fractions give $\dfrac{1}{n-1}-\dfrac{2}{n+2}+\dfrac{1}{n+3}$. Summing through $N$ cancels the common harmonic terms. The surviving lower terms are $1+\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{3}-\dfrac{1}{4}$, while the upper terms tend to 0 as $N\to\infty$. Thus the series equals $\boxed{\dfrac{19}{12}}$.
