Let $f$ be continuous on $\mathbb R$ with $\int_t^{t+1}f(x)\,dx=0$ for all $t$. Let $g$ be continuously differentiable on $[a,b]$, $a<b$, with $\int_a^b g=\int_a^b g\prime=0$. Prove that $f$ is periodic, give a nonzero example, and decide whether $g$ must have two distinct zeros in (i) $[a,b]$, (ii) $(a,b)$.
