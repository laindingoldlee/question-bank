Two different positive integers less than $100$ are selected at random. What is the probability that both are perfect squares? A student argues that there are $9$ perfect squares among the $99$ numbers, so the answer is $\dfrac{9}{99}=\dfrac{1}{11}$. What is wrong with this argument?
