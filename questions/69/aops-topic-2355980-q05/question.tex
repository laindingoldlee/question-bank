Jill and Bill travel together to a show in Las Vegas and back. On the way they take a car to their local airport, spend time in the local airport, fly to Las Vegas and spend time in the Las Vegas airport, and then watch the show. On the way back they take a car to the Las Vegas airport, spend time in that airport, fly home, spend time in the local airport and take a car home. The probability of catching an illness is $2\%$ on each car trip, $0.5\%$ on each visit to an airport, $1\%$ on each flight and $5\%$ at the show, independently of each other. What is the probability that they catch the illness during the trip? Give the answer to the nearest tenth of a percent.
