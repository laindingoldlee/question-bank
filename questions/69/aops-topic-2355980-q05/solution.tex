The trip has $3$ car trips, $4$ airport visits, $2$ flights and $1$ show. They avoid the illness only if they avoid it at every one of these, which by independence has probability
\[0.98^3\cdot0.995^4\cdot0.99^2\cdot0.95\approx0.941192\cdot0.980150\cdot0.9801\cdot0.95\approx0.85894.\]
So the probability of catching it is about $1-0.85894=0.14106$, that is, $\boxed{14.1\%}$. (Adding the percentages, $3\cdot2+4\cdot0.5+2\cdot1+5=15\%$, overestimates it, because it counts the cases with several exposures more than once.)
