Let the total be $S$. Each entry satisfies $t^2+t=S$, so its possible values are $r$ and $-r-1$, where the maximum $r$ is nonnegative. From $r^2\le16r$, $0\le r\le16$. If $k$ entries equal $r$, summing gives $k(2r+1)=(r+1)(r+17)$. Multiplying by four shows $2r+1$ divides 33, since $4(r+1)(r+17)=(2r+1)(2r+35)+33$. Thus $r=0,1,5,16$, giving $k=17,12,12,17$. The constant tuples contribute two; the others contribute $\binom{17}{12}$ each. Total $\boxed{2+2\binom{17}{12}=12378}$.
