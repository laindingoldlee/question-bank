Let $x=0.\overline{d_1d_2\ldots d_k}$, with block $D=\overline{d_1d_2\ldots d_k}$. Multiplying by $10^k$ shifts the decimal point $k$ places:
\[10^kx=D+0.\overline{d_1d_2\ldots d_k}=D+x.\]
So $\left(10^k-1\right)x=D$ and $x=\dfrac{D}{10^k-1}$. For $k=1,2,3$ this gives $\dfrac a9$, $\dfrac{\overline{ab}}{99}$ and $\dfrac{\overline{abc}}{999}$. $\blacksquare$
