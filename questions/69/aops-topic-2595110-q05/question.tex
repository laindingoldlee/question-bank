Show that $0.\overline{a}=\dfrac a9$, $0.\overline{ab}=\dfrac{\overline{ab}}{99}$, $0.\overline{abc}=\dfrac{\overline{abc}}{999}$, and in general that a decimal whose $k$-digit block $D$ repeats forever from the decimal point equals $\dfrac{D}{10^k-1}$. (Here $\overline{ab}$ denotes the two-digit block $10a+b$, and so on.)
