The minute hand is 60 degrees clockwise from 12; the hour hand is $10\cdot30+10\cdot\dfrac{1}{2}=305$ degrees clockwise from 12. The smaller difference is $360-(305-60)=\boxed{115^\circ}$.

\end{asy}
