Three mutually externally tangent circles have radius $8$. Their centres form an equilateral triangle with centre $O$. Among equilateral triangles centred at $O$ whose sides are perpendicular to the three rays from $O$ to the circle centres, and whose interiors lie in the central gap, find the largest area.
