For $x>0$, $\dfrac {x}{18}+2x=\dfrac{37x}{18}$, and by AM-GM
\[\dfrac{19}{x}+\dfrac{37x}{18}\ge2\sqrt{\dfrac{19\cdot37}{18}}=2\sqrt{\dfrac{703}{18}}=\boxed{\dfrac{\sqrt{1406}}{3}}\approx12.50,\]
with equality at $x^2=\dfrac{19\cdot18}{37}$. Without the restriction there is no minimum: as $x\to0^-$ the expression tends to $-\infty$.
