The four jobs take $x$, $2x$, $2(2x)-7=4x-7$ and $x-5$ elves. So
\[
x+2x+(4x-7)+(x-5)=100\;\Longrightarrow\;8x=112\;\Longrightarrow\;x=14,
\]
and $4\cdot14-7=\boxed{49}$ elves wrap presents.
