Santa's $100$ elves are divided among four jobs, each elf doing exactly one job. Washing the sleigh takes $x$ elves. Cleaning the stables takes twice as many elves as washing the sleigh. Wrapping presents takes $7$ fewer than twice as many elves as cleaning the stables. Tagging presents takes $5$ fewer elves than washing the sleigh. How many elves wrap presents?
