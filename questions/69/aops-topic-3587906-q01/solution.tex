Uniform scaling does not change the ratio, so take $A=(0,0)$, $B=(1,0)$, $C=(1,1)$ and $D=(0,1)$. Put $t=\dfrac{1-\sqrt3}{2}$; the equilateral-triangle constructions give $E=(t,t)$ and $F=(t,1-t)$. Since $t^2=t+\dfrac{1}{2}$, the shoelace formula gives $[DCFE]=\dfrac{1}{2}|-1+(1-2t)+(2t^2-t)+t|=\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $[ABFE]=\dfrac{1}{2}|(1-t)+(2t^2-t)|=1$. Both named quadrilaterals are simple in the specified configuration. Thus the required ratio is $\boxed{1:2}$. Returning to the actual side length multiplies both areas by two, giving $1$ and $2$ square centimetres respectively.
\begin{asy}
size(7cm);real t=(1-sqrt(3))/2;pair A=(0,0),B=(1,0),C=(1,1),D=(0,1),E=(t,t),F=(t,1-t);draw(A--B--C--D--cycle);draw(B--E--D,dashed);draw(A--F--C,dashed);draw(E--F,blue);pair[] V={A,B,C,D,E,F};string[] N={"A","B","C","D","E","F"};for(int i=0;i<6;++i)label("$"+N[i]+"$",V[i],i==0?S:i==1?SE:i==2?NE:i==3?N:i==4?SW:NW);
\end{asy}
