Let $ABCD$ be a square of side $\sqrt2$ centimetres. Construct equilateral triangles $BDE$ and $ACF$ so that both $E$ and $F$ lie in the open half-plane bounded by $AD$ that does not contain $B$. Find the ratio of the area of quadrilateral $DCFE$ to that of quadrilateral $ABFE$.
