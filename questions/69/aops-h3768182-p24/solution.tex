Let the bisectors meet at $I$. In triangle $BIC$, $\angle IBC+\angle ICB=\dfrac{B+C}{2}=\dfrac{180^\circ-60^\circ}{2}=60^\circ$. Hence $\boxed{\angle BIC=120^\circ}$.
\begin{asy}
texpreamble("\usepackage{amsmath,amssymb}");
size(240);
defaultpen(fontsize(9pt));
pair A=(0,3*sqrt(3)),B=(-3,0),C=(3,0),I=(0,sqrt(3));draw(A--B--C--cycle);draw(B--I--C,blue);label("A",A,N);label("B",B,SW);label("C",C,SE);label("I",I,N);label("$60^\circ$",A+(0,-1));
\end{asy}
