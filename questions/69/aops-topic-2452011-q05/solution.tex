Out of every $100$ users, $25$ are Pikachu fans, and $0.85\cdot25=21.25$ of them are Ash fans. The other $75$ users are not Pikachu fans, and $0.30\cdot75=22.5$ of them are Ash fans. In total $21.25+22.5=43.75$ out of every $100$ users are Ash fans, which is $\boxed{43.75\%}$.
