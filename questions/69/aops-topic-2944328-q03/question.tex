Two ducks, Wat and Q, take a $30$-question test with $1022$ ducklings; question $n$ is worth $n$ points. Independently, Wat answers question $n$ correctly with probability $\dfrac1{n^2}$ and Q with probability $\dfrac1{n+1}$. Each duckling answers every question incorrectly. Before submitting, on every question where Wat and Q gave the same answer, all ducklings copy that answer. Assume that two wrong answers never coincide, so Wat and Q agree only when both are correct. Find the expected total of all $1024$ scores.
