On question $n$, Wat's expected points are $n\cdot\dfrac1{n^2}=\dfrac1n$ and Q's are $\dfrac n{n+1}$. Each duckling scores $n$ exactly when both ducks are correct, with probability $\dfrac1{n^2(n+1)}$, so its expected points are $\dfrac1{n(n+1)}$. Summing over $n=1,\dots,30$:
\[\sum\left(\dfrac1n+\dfrac n{n+1}\right)=H_{30}+30-(H_{31}-1)=31-\dfrac1{31},\qquad\sum\dfrac1{n(n+1)}=1-\dfrac1{31}=\dfrac{30}{31}.\]
The total is $\dfrac{960}{31}+1022\cdot\dfrac{30}{31}=\dfrac{960+30660}{31}=\boxed{1020}$.
