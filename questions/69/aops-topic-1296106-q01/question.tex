The formula $C=\dfrac{5}{9}(F-32)$ converts a Fahrenheit temperature $F$ to Celsius $C$. Which of these must be true?
\begin{enumerate}
\item[I.] An increase of $1$ degree Fahrenheit is an increase of $\dfrac{5}{9}$ degree Celsius.
\item[II.] An increase of $1$ degree Celsius is an increase of $1.8$ degrees Fahrenheit.
\item[III.] An increase of $\dfrac{5}{9}$ degree Fahrenheit is an increase of $1$ degree Celsius.
\end{enumerate}
