The formula is linear with slope $\dfrac{5}{9}$: a change $\Delta F$ gives $\Delta C=\dfrac{5}{9}\Delta F$, so $\Delta F=\dfrac{9}{5}\Delta C=1.8\Delta C$. Statement I is true ($\Delta F=1$). Statement II is true ($\Delta C=1$ gives $\Delta F=1.8$). Statement III is false: $\Delta F=\dfrac{5}{9}$ gives $\Delta C=\dfrac{25}{81}$. So $\boxed{\text{I and II only}}$.
