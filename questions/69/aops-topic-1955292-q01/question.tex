An ant walks along the edges of a cube. Each move takes it along one edge to an adjacent vertex, and it may reuse edges and vertices. Let $A$ and $G$ be opposite vertices of the cube (the ends of a space diagonal). In how many ways can the ant go from $A$ to $G$ in exactly $7$ moves? More generally, find the number of such walks of $n$ moves for every odd $n$.
