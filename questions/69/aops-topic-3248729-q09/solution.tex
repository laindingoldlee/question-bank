Write $a=A+\alpha,b=B+\beta$ where $A,B$ are integers and $0\le\alpha,\beta<1$. The first equation gives $A+B=2,\alpha=0.14$; the second gives the same integer sum and $\beta=0.72$. Thus $a+b=2+0.14+0.72=\boxed{2.86}$.
