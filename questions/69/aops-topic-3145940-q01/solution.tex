Factor $P(x)=a_n\prod_{i=1}^n(x-r_i)$. The coefficient of $x^{n-1}$ is $-a_n\sum r_i$, while the constant term is $a_n(-1)^n\prod r_i$. Comparing coefficients gives $\boxed{\sum r_i=-\dfrac{a_{n-1}}{a_n}}$ and $\boxed{\prod r_i=(-1)^n\dfrac{a_0}{a_n}}$.
