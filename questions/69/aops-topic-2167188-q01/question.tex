Let $n\ge4$. The number of ways to arrange $n$ distinct keys on a keychain (a ring), where two arrangements count as the same if one can be obtained from the other by a rotation or a reflection, can be written as
\[\prod_{i=a}^{\,n-b} i=a(a+1)\cdots(n-b)\]
for some positive integer constants $a$ and $b$ that do not depend on $n$. Find $a+b$.
