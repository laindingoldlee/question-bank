Let the numbers be $x$ and $y$. Then
\[
\left|\dfrac{1}{y}-\dfrac{1}{x}\right|=\dfrac{|x-y|}{xy}=\dfrac{2\sqrt5}{5}.
\]
(Such numbers exist: $x=\sqrt{10}+\sqrt5$, $y=\sqrt{10}-\sqrt5$ have difference $2\sqrt5$ and product $10-5=5$.) The answer is $\boxed{\dfrac{2\sqrt5}{5}}$.
