Two towers of heights $90$ m and $150$ m stand $100$ m apart on level ground. A straight horizontal bridge connects the towers at a height of $70$ m. A rope is stretched from the top of the shorter tower to the foot of the taller tower. Find the distance from the top of the shorter tower to the point where the rope crosses the bridge.
