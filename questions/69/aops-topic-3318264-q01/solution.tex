Count the complement, writing $n$ with four digits including leading zeros. No carry requires thousands and hundreds digits at most $1$, and tens and units digits at most $3$. There are $2\cdot2\cdot4\cdot4=64$ such four-digit strings. Every one is at most $1133$, hence within the upper bound; excluding $0000$ leaves $63$ positive integers. Thus the desired count is $2024-63=\boxed{1961}$.
