The next difference is $5!=120$, so the next term is $44+120=\boxed{164}$.

In general the $n$th term is $10$ plus the first $n-1$ differences:
\[a_n=10+\sum_{k=0}^{n-2}k!=10+0!+1!+\cdots+(n-2)!,\]
which gives $a_1=10$, $a_2=11$, $a_3=12$, $a_4=14$, $a_5=20$, $a_6=44$ and $a_7=164$.
