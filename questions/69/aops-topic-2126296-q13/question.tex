In the sequence
\[10,\ 11,\ 12,\ 14,\ 20,\ 44,\ \dots\]
the differences between consecutive terms are $1,1,2,6,24$, that is, $0!,1!,2!,3!,4!$. If this pattern continues, what is the next term? Find also a formula for the $n$th term.
