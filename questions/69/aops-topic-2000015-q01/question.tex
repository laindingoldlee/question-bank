A circular dartboard of radius $1$ is centred at the origin. At the start the bullseye is the whole board, a disc of radius $r_0=1$ centred at the origin. A player throws darts that land uniformly at random in the square $-1\le x\le1$, $-1\le y\le1$, independently. Each time a dart lands inside the current bullseye (a disc of radius $r$ centred at the origin), the player scores a point and the bullseye shrinks: if the dart lands at distance $d$ from the origin, the new bullseye is the disc centred at the origin whose diameter is the length of the chord of the current bullseye through the dart and perpendicular to the line from the origin to the dart; that is, its radius is $\sqrt{r^2-d^2}$. The game ends with the first dart that misses the bullseye. Find the expected score.
