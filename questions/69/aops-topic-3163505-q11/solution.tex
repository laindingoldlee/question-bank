For $n\ge2$, $8-6\sqrt n<0$, while the reciprocal of $\dfrac{80+6\sqrt n}{n}$ is positive. Thus such $n$ cannot work. For $n=1$, the two proposed reciprocal quantities are $2$ and $86$, whose product is not $1$. Hence $\boxed{\text{no positive integer }n\text{ exists}}$.
