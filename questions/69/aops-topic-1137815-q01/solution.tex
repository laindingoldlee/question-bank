The hypotenuse is $BC=10$ and the altitude from $A$ to $BC$ is $h=\dfrac{6\cdot8}{10}=\dfrac{24}{5}$. Let the square have side $s$. Its side opposite $BC$ is parallel to $BC$ at distance $s$, and it cuts off from the triangle a smaller triangle similar to $ABC$, with base $s$ and height $h-s$.

\begin{asy}
usepackage("amsmath");
size(4.5cm);
defaultpen(fontsize(10pt));
path ra(pair P,pair u,pair v,real s=0.2){return P+s*unit(u)--P+s*unit(u)+s*unit(v)--P+s*unit(v);}
pair A=(0,0),B=(8,0),C=(0,6); real s=120/37; real t=(4.8-s)/4.8; pair n=(0.6,0.8);
pair P=t*B, Q=t*C, Pf=P+s*n, Qf=Q+s*n;
draw(A--B--C--cycle); draw(P--Q--Qf--Pf--cycle,blue); draw(ra(A,(1,0),(0,1),0.5));
label("$A$",A,SW); label("$B$",B,SE); label("$C$",C,NW); label("$8$",(4,0),S); label("$6$",(0,3),W); label("$s$",(P+Q)/2,SW,blue);
label("\mbox{}",(min(currentpicture,user=true)+max(currentpicture,user=true))/2,black); // keeps the text after the figure black when LaTeX typesets the labels inline
\end{asy}

By similarity $\dfrac{s}{10}=\dfrac{h-s}{h}$, so $s\left(\dfrac{1}{10}+\dfrac{1}{h}\right)=1$, i.e. $s\left(\dfrac{1}{10}+\dfrac{5}{24}\right)=s\cdot\dfrac{37}{120}=1$. Hence $s=\boxed{\dfrac{120}{37}}$ cm.
