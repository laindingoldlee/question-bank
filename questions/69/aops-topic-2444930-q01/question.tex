Find the value of the infinite continued fraction
\[1+\dfrac{2}{3+\dfrac{4}{5+\dfrac{6}{7+\cdots}}},\]
defined as the limit of the finite continued fractions $C_n$ obtained by stopping at the number $2n+1$:
\[C_1=1+\dfrac23,\qquad C_2=1+\dfrac{2}{3+\dfrac45},\qquad C_3=1+\dfrac{2}{3+\dfrac{4}{5+\dfrac67}},\qquad\dots\]
