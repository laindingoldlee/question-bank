The letters are A, M, E (twice each) and Z, X, C, O (once each): $7$ distinct letters, three of which may appear twice. A $4$-letter string uses either four different letters, one letter twice and two others once, or two letters twice each.
\begin{itemize}
\item Four different letters: $7\cdot6\cdot5\cdot4=840$ strings.
\item One doubled letter (from A, M, E: $3$ ways) and two other different letters (from the remaining $6$: $\binom62=15$ ways), arranged in $\dfrac{4!}{2!}=12$ orders: $3\cdot15\cdot12=540$.
\item Two doubled letters ($\binom32=3$ ways), arranged in $\dfrac{4!}{2!\,2!}=6$ orders: $18$.
\end{itemize}
The total is $840+540+18=\boxed{1398}$.
