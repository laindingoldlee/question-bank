The sum consists of $1+2+\cdots+x=\dfrac{x(x+1)}2$ and $1+2+\cdots+(x-1)=\dfrac{(x-1)x}2$. Adding,
\[\dfrac{x(x+1)}2+\dfrac{x(x-1)}2=\dfrac{x\cdot2x}{2}=x^2.\ \blacksquare\]
In words: the sum of two consecutive triangular numbers is a perfect square. A picture shows it too: in an $x\times x$ grid of dots, the diagonals parallel to one diagonal contain $1,2,\dots,x-1,x,x-1,\dots,2,1$ dots.
