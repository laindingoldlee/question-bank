Let there be $o$ orang-utans, $g$ gorillas and $c$ chimpanzees (nonnegative integers):
\[
o+g+c=100,\qquad 4o+6g+c=200 .
\]
Subtracting gives $3o+5g=100$. To make $c=100-o-g$ as small as possible we make $o+g$ as large as possible. From $o=\dfrac{100-5g}{3}$,
\[
o+g=\dfrac{100-2g}{3},
\]
which is largest when $g$ is smallest. For $o$ to be an integer we need $100-5g\equiv0\pmod 3$, i.e.\ $g\equiv2\pmod3$, so the smallest choice is $g=2$, giving $o=30$ and
\[
c=100-30-2=\boxed{68}.
\]
(Check: $30+2+68=100$ and $120+12+68=200$.)
