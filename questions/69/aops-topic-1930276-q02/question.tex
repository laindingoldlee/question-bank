In trapezoid $ABCD$ with bases $AD\parallel BC$ and legs $AB$ and $CD$ not parallel, let $M$ and $N$ be the midpoints of the bases $AD$ and $BC$. Suppose that line $MN$ makes equal angles with lines $AB$ and $CD$. Prove that the trapezoid is isosceles, i.e.\ $AB=CD$.
