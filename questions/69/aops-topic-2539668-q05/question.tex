Let $f(x)=2x\sqrt{1-x^2}$. A triangle has sides $a$, $b$, $c$ with
\[\dfrac ax=\dfrac b{f(x)}=\dfrac c{f(f(x))}\]
for some $x$ with $0<x<\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$, and the angle opposite side $b$ is twice the angle opposite side $a$. Find the smallest angle of the triangle, and show that the angles of the triangle form a geometric sequence and that the triangle is obtuse.
