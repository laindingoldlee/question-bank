Place the numbers $10,11,12,13,14,15$ once each in six circles: one at each vertex of a triangle and one at the midpoint of each side. Each side's three numbers must have the same sum $S$. Find the greatest possible $S$. Choices: (A) $36$, (B) $37$, (C) $38$, (D) $39$, (E) $40$.
