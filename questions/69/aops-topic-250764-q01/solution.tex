The sum of all six numbers is $75$. Adding the three side sums counts each vertex twice and each midpoint once, so $3S=75+V$, where $V$ is the vertex sum. This is at most $13+14+15=42$, giving $S\le39$. Put $13,14,15$ at the vertices and $12,10,11$ between the pairs $(13,14),(14,15),(15,13)$, respectively. Each side then sums to $39$, attaining the bound. Thus $\boxed{39}$, choice (D).

\end{asy}
