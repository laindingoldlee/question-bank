A girl and a boy walk side by side at the same constant speed. The girl steps onto a moving walkway and keeps walking at her speed, while the boy walks alongside it. When the girl reaches the end of the walkway, the boy has covered only two fifths of its length. What is the ratio of the walkway's speed to their walking speed?
