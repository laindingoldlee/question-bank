Let the walking speed be $v$, the walkway's speed $w$ and its length $L$. The girl takes $\dfrac{L}{v+w}$, in which the boy covers $\dfrac{vL}{v+w}=\dfrac{2}{5}L$. So $5v=2v+2w$, $w=\dfrac{3}{2}v$, and the ratio is $\boxed{3:2}$.
