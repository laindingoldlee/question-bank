Let the individual participation probabilities be $x,y,z$. Independence gives $yz=\dfrac{1}{3}$, $xz=\dfrac{1}{4}$, and $xy=\dfrac{1}{5}$. Multiplying yields $(xyz)^2=\dfrac{1}{60}$, so $xyz=\dfrac{\sqrt{15}}{30}$. Therefore $p+q=\boxed{45}$.
