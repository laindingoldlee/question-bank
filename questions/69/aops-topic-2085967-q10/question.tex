Let \(a\ge\dfrac{3}{2}\) be a constant and \(f(x)=\sqrt{ax-3}+2\). For \(x\ge 2\), define
\[
g(x)=\begin{cases} f(x), & f(x)<f^{-1}(x),\\ f^{-1}(x), & f(x)\ge f^{-1}(x).\end{cases}
\]
For a positive integer \(n\), let \(h(n)\) be the number of intersection points of \(y=g(x)\) and \(y=x-n\). Suppose \(h(1)=h(3)<h(2)\). Then \(g(4)=\dfrac{q}{p}\), where \(p\) and \(q\) are relatively prime positive integers. Find \(p+q\).
